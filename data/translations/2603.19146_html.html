摘要Abstract

离散扩散模型是文本生成自回归方法的有希望的替代方案，但其解码方法仍未得到充分研究。自回归模型的标准解码方法（例如波束搜索）并不直接应用于迭代去噪，并且现有的扩散解码技术对批量分集提供有限的控制。为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了一种用于离散扩散的通用波束搜索框架，该框架可以并行生成候选对象并支持模块化波束选择目标。作为一个注重多样性的实例，我们建议Discrete diffusion models are promising alternatives to autoregressive approaches for text generation, yet their decoding methods remain understudied. Standard decoding methods for autoregressive models, such as beam search, do not directly apply to iterative denoising, and existing diffusion decoding techniques provide limited control over in-batch diversity. To bridge this gap, we introduce a generalized beam-search framework for discrete diffusion that generates candidates in parallel and supports modular beamselection objectives. As a diversity-focused instantiation, we proposeD5P4，它将选择步骤表述为行列式点过程上的 MAP 推理。 D5P4 利用可扩展的贪婪求解器，保持多 GPU 兼容性，并以接近零的计算开销实现模型概率和目标多样性之间的明确权衡。自由形式生成和问答实验表明，D5P4 在保持有竞争力的生成质量的同时，提高了强基线的多样性。[1], which formulates the selection step as MAP inference over a Determinantal Point Process. Leveraging a scalable greedy solver, D5P4 maintains multi-GPU compatibility and enables an explicit trade-off between model probability and target diversity with near-zero compute overhead. Experiments on free-form generation and question answering demonstrate that D5P4 improves diversity over strong baselines while maintaining competitive generation quality.[1]

2.相关工作2. Related Work

2.1.离散扩散语言模型2.1. Discrete diffusion language models

离散扩散模型起源于离散变量扩散过程的早期表述（SohlDickstein 等人，2015），后来通过多项扩散和基于 argmax 的流扩展到分类状态空间（Hoogeboom 等人，2021）。 D3PM（Austin 等人，2021）将离散扩散形式化为词汇元素上的结构化马尔可夫过程，产生统一的基于 ELBO 的训练目标并适应不同的腐败机制，包括均匀、高斯和吸收状态转换。吸收状态扩散是掩蔽扩散语言模型 (MDLM) 的基础，该模型利用连续扩散（Ho 等人，2020；Rombach 等人，2022）和基于变压器的架构（Peebles 和 Xie，2023）的进步来缩小与自回归模型的性能差距。萨胡等人。 (2024) 引入了一种简化且稳定的训练方法，而随后的工作表明，大规模 MDLM 可以在适当的缩放和微调机制下达到自回归性能 (von Rutte 等人，2025；Nie 等人，2024；Ye 等人，2025)。最近，LLaDA（Nie 等人，2025）等模型展示了竞争性的指令跟随和情境学习行为。 MDLM 的一个定义特征是并行解码，其中令牌是联合细化的，而不是从左到右生成的。虽然这将解码深度与序列长度解耦，但幼稚的并行细化通常会降低生成质量，因为令牌更新无法显式地建模令牌间依赖关系。因此，先前的工作通常将并行解码视为速度与质量的权衡。有几种方法通过推测执行或扩散特定的键值缓存来加速推理（Wu et al., 2025; Israel et al., 2025; Ma et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025），但主要关注吞吐量，而不是并行假设的联合质量或多样性。Discrete diffusion models originate from early formulations of diffusion processes over discrete variables (SohlDickstein et al., 2015), later extended to categorical state spaces via multinomial diffusion and argmax-based flows (Hoogeboom et al., 2021). D3PM (Austin et al., 2021) formalized discrete diffusion as a structured Markov process over vocabulary elements, yielding a unified ELBObased training objective and accommodating diverse corruption mechanisms, including uniform, Gaussian, and absorbing-state transitions. Absorbing-state diffusion underlies masked diffusion language models (MDLMs), which leverage advances in continuous diffusion (Ho et al., 2020; Rombach et al., 2022) and transformer-based architectures (Peebles & Xie, 2023) to narrow the performance gap with autoregressive models. Sahoo et al. (2024) introduce a simplified and stable training recipe, while subsequent work shows that large-scale MDLMs can approach autoregressive performance under suitable scaling and finetuning regimes (von Rutte et al.¨ , 2025; Nie et al., 2024; Ye et al., 2025). More recently, models such as LLaDA (Nie et al., 2025) demonstrate competitive instruction-following and in-context learning behavior. A defining characteristic of MDLMs is parallel decoding, where tokens are refined jointly rather than generated left-to-right. While this decouples decoding depth from sequence length, naive parallel refinement often degrades generation quality, as token updates fail to explicitly model inter-token dependencies. As a result, prior work typically frames parallel decoding as a speed–quality trade-off. Several approaches accelerate inference through speculative execution or diffusion-specific key–value caching (Wu et al., 2025; Israel et al., 2025; Ma et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025), but largely focus on throughput, not joint quality or diversity across parallel hypotheses.

2.3.多样性感知解码2.3. Diversity-aware decoding

最近对 ARM 中强化学习和监督微调 (SFT) 的分析（Dang 等人，2025b；Yue 等人，2025）观察到输出多样性持续减少，特别是在以推理为中心的环境中。李等人。 （2025）在 SFT 的背景下研究了这种现象，并在训练时提出了一种缓解策略，以提高覆盖率并有利于测试时间的扩展。这些发现表明，对齐和保真度的提高不一定伴随着更广泛的覆盖范围，从而进一步激发了明确的多样性感知解码机制。在扩散模型中，已知强的无分类器引导（CFG）会导致模式崩溃，这种效应也出现在文本的离散扩散中（Schiff 等人，2024）。现有的在连续扩散中恢复多样性的策略通常依赖于噪声注入（Sadat 等人，2024）或部分指导计划（Kynka¡anniemi 等人，2024），但没有解决设定级别的多样性。与我们的工作最密切相关的是，行列式波束搜索（Meister et al., 2021）使用 DPP 制定波束选择，通过对数行列式目标和基于字符串的相似性度量鼓励多样化但高分的假设。然而，这种方法本质上是从左到右的，并且不能自然地扩展到平行扩散轨迹。Recent analyses of reinforcement learning and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) in ARMs (Dang et al., 2025b; Yue et al., 2025) observe a consistent reduction in output diversity, particularly in reasoning-focused settings. Li et al. (2025) study this phenomenon in the context of SFT and propose a mitigation strategy at training time that improves coverage and benefits test-time scaling. These findings suggest that gains in alignment and fidelity are not necessarily accompanied by broader coverage, further motivating explicit diversity-aware decoding mechanisms. In diffusion models, strong classifier-free guidance (CFG) is known to induce mode collapse, an effect that also appears in discrete diffusion for text (Schiff et al., 2024). Existing strategies to recover diversity in continuous diffusion typically rely on noise injection (Sadat et al., 2024) or partial guidance schedules (Kynka¨anniemi et al.¨ , 2024), but do not address diversity at the set level. Most closely related to our work, Determinantal Beam Search (Meister et al., 2021) formulates beam selection using DPPs, encouraging diverse yet high-scoring hypotheses via a log-determinant objective and a string-based measure of similarity. However, this approach is inherently left-to-right and does not naturally extend to parallel diffusion trajectories.

六、结论6. Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 D5P4，一种用于离散扩散语言模型的解码框架，它将并行去噪与有原则的、多样性感知的选择结合起来。 D5P4 将波束选择作为分区决定点过程中的 MAP 推断，产生一种简单且可解释的机制，用于权衡模型隐含的质量和批量多样性。至关重要的是，这两个信号都是根据扩散模型本身（用于质量的序列级熵和用于语义相似性的隐藏状态表示）计算的，以避免在推理时依赖昂贵的外部评分器。In this work, we introduce D5P4, a decoding framework for discrete diffusion language models that couples parallel denoising with principled, diversity-aware selection. D5P4 casts beam selection as MAP inference in a Partition Determinantal Point Process, yielding a simple and interpretable mechanism for trading off model-implied quality and inbatch diversity. Crucially, both signals are computed from the diffusion model itself (sequence-level entropy for quality and hidden-state representations for semantic similarity) to avoid reliance on costly external scorers at inference time.

在使用 MDLM 的开放式生成和使用 LLADA 的问答中，D5P4 在不牺牲竞争质量的情况下提高了多样性，并且始终提供比广泛使用的替代方案（例如多样化波束搜索和基于温度的采样）更强的质量-多样性帕累托权衡。这些收益以最小的开销实现：我们的贪婪 MAP 求解器高效、多 GPU 友好，并且保留了并行解码的吞吐量优势。更广泛地说，D5P4 提出了一种扩展 LM 测试时间的实用途径，通过结构化候选选择而不是额外的模型调用来增加覆盖范围。未来的工作可以将该原则扩展到其他扩散公式和模式，并探索更丰富的内核设计或任务感知目标，同时保留相同的高效选择主干。Across open-ended generation with MDLM and question answering with LLADA, D5P4 improves diversity without sacrificing competitive quality, and consistently provides a stronger quality–diversity Pareto trade-off than widely used alternatives such as diverse beam search and temperaturebased sampling. These gains come with minimal overhead: our greedy MAP solver is efficient, multi-GPU friendly, and preserves the throughput advantages of parallel decoding. More broadly, D5P4 suggests a practical route to test-time scaling for diffusion LMs that increases coverage through structured candidate selection rather than additional model calls. Future work could extend this principle to other diffusion formulations and modalities, and explore richer kernel designs or task-aware objectives while retaining the same efficient selection backbone.

E. 缩放行为和计算效率E. Scaling Behavior and Computational Efficiency

在本节中，我们研究 D5P4 底层子集选择算法的缩放行为。我们按照主论文中使用的内核构造来构造合成 DPP 实例。具体来说，我们根据 MDLM 表示估计一阶和二阶统计量、样本嵌入和这些统计量的质量分数，并结合质量和相似性项构建加性 DPP 内核。In this section, we study the scaling behavior of the subset selection algorithms underlying D5P4. We construct synthetic DPP instances following the kernel construction used in the main paper. Concretely, we estimate first- and second-order statistics from MDLM representations, sample embeddings and quality scores from these statistics, and build additive DPP kernels combining quality and similarity terms.

我们评估跨方法的子行列式最大化。对于每个实例，我们报告：（i）标准化目标值，定义为所有方法中实现的标准化子决定因素，（ii）试验中每种方法的平均排名，以及（iii）优化步骤的执行时间。我们将组大小和组数从 4 更改为 64，这对应于增加对数组合复杂性，由 log(We evaluate subdeterminant maximization across methods. For each instance, we report: (i) the normalized objective value, defined as the achieved normalized subdeterminant across all methods, (ii) the average rank of each method across trials, and (iii) the execution time of the optimization step. We vary both the group size and the number of groups from 4 to 64, corresponding to increasing log combinatorial complexity, given by log(|S|) = log(组大小[组数] ).我们还扫描了控制成对排斥强度的相互作用参数。所有结果均是 500 多次独立试验的平均值。除了 DPPy 基线依赖于 NumPy 并在 CPU 上执行之外，所有方法都在 GPU 上实现。值得注意的是，DPPy 不支持横向分区约束，并且严格作为参考包含在内。我们还包括 Triton 优化的 D5P4 实现，以评估内核级加速的影响。) = log(group size[number of groups] ). Notably, DPPy does not support transversal partition constraints and is included strictly as a reference. 关于目标最大化，DPPy 和 Greedy Beam Search 的表现与随机基线相当。虽然多样性波束搜索在低复杂度下与我们的方法相匹配，但我们的方法在较高的组合复杂度下实现了卓越的性能。Regarding objective maximization, DPPy and Greedy Beam Search perform comparably to the random baseline. While Diverse Beam Search matches our method at low complexity, our approach achieves superior performance at higher combinatorial complexities.

Our standard GPU method scales more favorably than Diverse Beam Search, and our Triton-optimized variant further reduces execution time, underscoring the benefit of specialized kernels for large-scale subset selection.
