摘要Abstract

Looped Transformers 最近在机器学习领域获得了极大的关注，作为一种强大的基于注意力的架构范式，将迭代细化与循环参数共享相结合。该架构的变体在 ARC-AGI 和数独上显示出了有希望的结果，这两者都被认为需要多步骤推理。环形变压器产生了循环推理模型（RRM），例如层次推理模型（HRM）和微型递归模型（TRM）。尽管 RRM 取得了成功，但它并不能保证正确性：每次迭代都会预测完整的问题分配，而贪婪解码可能会产生违反约束的解决方案。 在这项工作中，我们重点关注 SE-RRM，这是一种 RRM 的符号等变实例，它对更大的问题规模表现出改进的外推能力。我们提出了一种神经符号方法“循环推理模型指导”（G-RRM），它将 SE-RRM 与符号求解器集成以解决约束满足问题。 SE-RRM 充当神经求解器，生成完整的解决方案建议并指导经典符号求解器，例如回溯或基于 SAT 的方法（如 Glucose 4.1 和 CaDiCaL 3.0.0），生成全局正确的解决方案。我们主要研究 G-RRM 的神经引导何时提高符号求解器的搜索效率。 我们的实验表明，G-RRM 的功效取决于两个条件：首先，问题实例必须具有广阔的组合搜索空间以暴露潜在的收益，其次，求解器架构必须能够动态覆盖其分支选择，以便在神经提示不完善时进行恢复。 当这些条件成立时，指导会将冲突计数中位数降至零，并显着加快挂钟速度：Looped Transformers have recently gained significant attention in machine learning as a powerful attention-based architectural paradigm integrating iterative refinement with recurrent parameter sharing. Variants of this architecture have shown promising results on ARC-AGI and Sudoku, both of which are considered to require multi-step reasoning. 1 简介Introduction

解决组合问题的循环变压器/循环推理模型。Looped Transformers/Recurrent Reasoning Models to Solve Combinatorial Problems.

由于其参数效率和通过递归状态更新执行迭代算法过程的固有能力，循环变压器Due to their parameter efficiency and inherent capability to execute iterative algorithmic procedures through recursive state updates, looped transformers(Liu2024)为解决复杂的组合问题提供了一个有前途的框架。但他们缺乏严格的逻辑验证能力offer a promising framework for solving complex combinatorial problems. However, they lack the capacity for rigorous logical verification(Renkhoff2024)，提出了如何有效地将其预测强度与符号方法的精确约束相结合的问题。在这个接口上，循环推理模型（RRM），例如层次推理模型（HRM）, raising the question of how to effectively pair their predictive strength with the exact constraints of symbolic methods. At this interface, Recurrent Reasoning Models (RRMs), such as Hierarchical Reasoning Models (HRMs)(wang2025hierarchical)和微型递归模型 (TRM)and Tiny Recursive Models (TRMs)(jolicoeur2025less)，已成为专门的环形变压器。尽管被称为推理模型，但这些模型并没有改变机器学习固有的归纳性质，因为它们不执行演绎逻辑。相反，他们应用学习的运算符来迭代更新内部状态。尽管如此，它们在严重受限的任务上表现出了出色的预测性能。这种高精度是通过可扩展的训练方法实现的，该方法利用各个循环迭代之间的中间监督和停止梯度操作来有效优化深度循环结构。因此，这种训练范式使 RRM 能够在组合领域取得强大的实证结果，例如数独谜题和来自 ARC-AGI 基准的抽象视觉推理任务, have emerged as specialized looped transformers. Despite being called reasoning models, these models do not alter the inherently inductive nature of machine learning, as they perform no deductive logic. Rather, they apply a learned operator to iteratively update an internal state. Nevertheless, they exhibit remarkable predictive performance on heavily constrained tasks. This high accuracy is unlocked by a scalable training methodology that leverages intermediate supervision and stop-gradient operations between individual loop iterations to efficiently optimize deeply looped structures. Consequently, this training paradigm has allowed RRMs to achieve strong empirical results across combinatorial domains, such as Sudoku puzzles and abstract visual reasoning tasks from the ARC-AGI benchmark(chollet2025arc).

布尔可满足性和现代 SAT 求解器。Boolean Satisfiability and Modern SAT Solvers.

组合问题传统上被编码为布尔可满足性 (SAT) 问题（理论计算机科学中的基础 NP 完全问题）的实例，以提供正确性的逻辑保证。在过去的几十年里，实用的 SAT 求解已经从纯粹的理论挑战发展成为一种工业级方法，由可以解决大型实例的算法、启发式方法和数据结构的各种进步驱动。Combinatorial problems are traditionally encoded as instances of the Boolean Satisfiability (SAT) problem, the foundational NP-complete problem in theoretical computer science, to provide logical guarantees of correctness. Over the past decades, practical SAT solving has evolved from a purely theoretical challenge into an industrial-grade methodology driven by various advancements in algorithms, heuristics and data structures that can solve large instances.

数独作为既定的组合基准。Sudoku as an Established Combinatorial Benchmark.

数独谜题是一种理想且广泛采用的组合基准。尽管概念简单，数独谜题却体现了受严格的非局部规则约束的严格约束满足问题 (CSP)。给定一个部分填充数字的网格，任务是将符号分配给所有剩余的单元格，同时确保每行、列和子网格仅包含每个符号一次。广义决策问题是 NP 完全问题Sudoku puzzles serve as an ideal and widely adopted combinatorial benchmark. Despite their conceptual simplicity, a Sudoku puzzle embodies a rigorous Constraint Satisfaction Problem (CSP) governed by strict, non-local rules. Given a grid partially filled with digits, the task is to assign symbols to all remaining cells while ensuring that each row, column, and subgrid contains every symbol exactly once. The generalized decision problem is NP-complete(yato2003complexity)，突出了其固有的计算难度。尽管规则简单，数独却给神经网络带来了巨大的挑战。纯粹的神经方法通常会生成局部合理但全局不一致的分配，并且很难推断出看不见的网格大小或符号集, highlighting its intrinsic computational difficulty. Despite its simple rules, Sudoku poses significant challenges for neural networks. Purely neural approaches often generate locally plausible but globally inconsistent assignments and struggle to extrapolate to unseen grid sizes or symbol sets(long2023large; seely2025sudoku; giannoulis2026teachingtransformers; vamsi2021deep)。我们的方法旨在通过将神经方法与精确求解器相结合来解决这个问题，这可以防止不一致的分配并保证正确性。. Our approach aims to solve this problem by combining neural approaches with exact solvers, which prevents inconsistent assignments and guarantees correctness.

为了形式化这些经验神经近似和精确演绎逻辑之间的相互作用，我们将我们的方法置于考茨的神经符号整合分类法中To formalize the interplay between these empirical neural approximations and exact deductive logic, we contextualize our approach within Kautz’s taxonomy of neural-symbolic integration(kautz2022third)。从架构上来说，将循环推理模型 (RRM) 的预测输出输入下游符号 SAT 求解器的管道与. Architecturally, a pipeline that feeds the predictive outputs of a recurrent reasoning model (RRM) into a downstream symbolic SAT solver most closely aligns with a神经 |象征性的Neuro | Symbolic系统（类型 3）。虽然考茨构建了一个完全集成的system (Type 3). 现有的神经符号框架在应用于硬约束任务时突出了这些局限性。例如，神经符号求解器Existing neuro-symbolic frameworks highlight these limitations when applied to hard constraint tasks. 相比之下，最近的架构创新缩小了这种经验性能差距。循环推理模型显着改进了纯神经数独求解，在极其困难的问题上实现了大约 70% 的求解率In contrast, recent architectural innovations have closed this empirical performance gap. Recurrent reasoning models have substantially advanced pure neural Sudoku solving, achieving solve rates of roughly 70% on extremely difficult9×99\times 9超过一半的数字缺失的谜题。在此进展的基础上，符号等变 RRM (SE-RRM) 进一步将完全解决率提高到 90% 以上，同时保持异常紧凑且可从小实例进行训练puzzles with more than half of the digits missing. Building on this progress, symbol-equivariant RRMs (SE-RRMs) further raise this fully solved rate to over 90%, while remaining exceptionally compact and trainable from small instances(freinschlag2026symbol).

循环推理模型可以在一次前向传递中提出难题的完整解决方案，但它不能证明它满足所有约束。符号求解器提供了对应的解决方案：它可以保证正确性，而无需事先了解有前途的解决方案。将子符号和符号组件耦合起来可以让 RRM 充当求解器搜索的先验。当网络正确时，求解器无需额外搜索即可验证分配；当错误时，求解器会依赖其自身的完整性保证，并可能利用搜索空间的有前途的子区域。A recurrent reasoning model can propose a full solution to a puzzle in a single forward pass, but it cannot certify it satisfies all constraints. A symbolic solver offers the counterpart: it guarantees correctness without prior knowledge of promising solutions. Coupling the sub-symbolic and symbolic components lets the RRM serve as a prior for the solver’s search. When the network is correct, the solver verifies an assignment with no extra search; when it is wrong, the solver falls back on its own completeness guarantees and potentially takes advantage of promising subareas of the search space.

我们表明，这种组合的好处取决于两个因素： (i) 问题的广泛搜索空间 主导一个实例的求解器的运行时间，并且 (ii) 求解器动态覆盖其 分支选择以利用神经模型的提示。因此，提示的准确性也决定了效益。We show that the benefit of this combination is dependent on two factors: (i) the problem’s expansive search space dominates the solver’s runtime for an instance, and (ii) the ability of the solver to dynamically overwrite its branching choices to exploit the hints of the neural model. Consequently, the accuracy of the hints also determines the benefit.

2.2 符号搜索和验证Symbolic Search and Verification

2.2.1 回溯搜索Backtracking Search

回溯使用深度优先搜索来探索搜索树中可能的解决方案，同时不断检查约束并在发生违规时撤消选择。对于数独，它从部分填充的谜题开始，并重复选择空单元格来填充。搜索树中的节点对应于部分填充的谜题，而边缘表示对先前空单元格的有效数字分配。Backtracking uses depth-first search to explore possible solutions in a search tree while continuously checking constraints and undoing choices when violations occur. For Sudoku, it starts from a partially filled puzzle and repeatedly selects empty cells to fill. Nodes in the search tree correspond to partially filled puzzles, while edges represent valid digit assignments to a previously empty cell.

每当算法达到空单元格没有有效数字可用的状态时，当前的部分解就无法扩展到完整解。在这种情况下，算法通过撤消最近的分配并探索替代边缘来回溯。重复此过程，直到找到完整的有效数独解决方案或已用尽所有可能的分配。Whenever the algorithm reaches a state in which an empty cell has no valid digit available, the current partial solution cannot be extended to a complete solution. In this case, the algorithm backtracks by undoing the most recent assignment and exploring alternative edges. This process is repeated until either a complete valid Sudoku solution is found or all possible assignments have been exhausted.

这种策略保证了完整性：如果存在有效的数独解决方案，回溯最终将通过系统地探索所有可行的分配来找到它。This strategy guarantees completeness: if a valid Sudoku solution exists, backtracking will eventually find it by systematically exploring all feasible assignments.

我们提出了 G-RRM，一种神经符号框架，其中符号等变循环推理模型（SE-RRM）指导精确符号求解器的搜索。 神经模型通过单次前向传递提供对候选分配的概率偏好，而符号求解器则强制执行全局约束并保证正确性。 与仅使用求解器相比，这种组合减少了有效搜索空间，并且仍然保留了符号算法的完整性；改进源自更高效的搜索。本质上，G-RRM 将高精度但未经验证的神经求解器转换为具有保证正确性的神经求解器。We propose G-RRM, a neuro-symbolic framework in which a symbol-equivariant recurrent reasoning model (SE-RRM) guides the search of exact symbolic solvers. The neural model provides probabilistic preferences over candidate assignments from a single forward pass, while a symbolic solver enforces global constraints and guarantees correctness. 5 局限性Limitations

我们的评估隔离了神经引导的影响，这给结果带来了一些限制。Our evaluation isolates the effect of neural guidance, which introduces some limitations to the results.

首先，所有报告的挂钟时间均指符号求解器。 SE-RRM 的推理时间不包括在内，因为它是单独预先计算的。合并推理时间会涉及批量大小、GPU 硬件和特定于实现的优化，从而使比较变得困难。我们这项工作的目标是隔离神经引导本身的影响，而不是最大化工程性能。因此，我们的 SE‑RRM 实施是一个简单的研究原型，并未充分利用所有可能的性能优化。First, all reported wall‑clock times refer to the symbolic solver. Inference time for SE‑RRM is not included, as it was precomputed separately. Incorporating inference time would entangle batch size, GPU hardware, and implementation‑specific optimizations, making comparisons difficult. Our objective in this work is to isolate the impact of neural guidance itself, rather than to maximize engineering performance. Accordingly, our SE‑RRM implementation is a straightforward research prototype and does not exploit the full range of possible performance optimizations.

其次，我们的研究仅限于数独。虽然数独是一个既定的组合基准，但我们并未在其他领域确认我们的结果。Second, our study is confined to Sudoku. While Sudoku is an established combinatorial benchmark, we do not confirm our results on other domains.
