摘要Abstract

图神经网络（GNN）擅长聚合邻居信息进行分类，但其性能受到图结构纠缠的阻碍，其中来自语义不相关邻居的虚假相关性污染了节点嵌入。 对于嵌入空间中类边界附近的节点来说，这一挑战最为严峻，其中放大的结构噪声模糊了决策边界并破坏了预测的稳定性。 现有的鲁棒 GNN 方法很大程度上统一处理所有节点，忽略边界漏洞。 在本文中，为了提高分类性能，我们通过将边界区域纠缠确定为主要瓶颈来解决图结构解缠问题，并提出Graph neural networks (GNNs) excel at aggregating neighbor information for classification, yet their performance is hindered by graph structural entanglement, where spurious correlations from semantically irrelevant neighbors contaminate node embeddings. This challenge is most acute for nodes near class boundaries in the embedding space, where amplified structural noise blurs decision boundaries and destabilizes predictions. Existing robust GNN methods largely treat all nodes uniformly, ignoring boundary vulnerabilities. 1 简介Introduction

图数据以复杂的拓扑和丰富的语义相互依赖性为特征，支撑着跨越社交网络的各种应用程序Graph data, characterized by intricate topologies and rich semantic interdependencies, underpins diverse applications spanning social networks(Lyu et al., 2024; Subramonian et al., 2024), 生物系统, biological systems(Zhang et al., 2025), 计算化学, computational chemistry(Li et al., 2025)和推荐系统, and recommendation systems(Zhang et al., 2024b; Wu et al., 2022)。 图神经网络（GNN）已成为通过聚合来自局部邻域的信息来构建节点嵌入来学习此类数据的主导范式。 然而，与图像等网格结构模态不同. Graph neural networks (GNNs) have emerged as the dominant paradigm for learning on such data by aggregating information from local neighborhoods to construct node embeddings. However, unlike grid-structured modalities such as images(Deng, 2012; Deng et al., 2009)，图表现出不规则的拓扑，其中邻域结构在节点之间显着变化，将噪声和不相关信息引入节点嵌入中，特别是在分类中。 这会导致虚假相关性，这种现象被称为图结构纠缠, graphs exhibit irregular topologies where neighborhood structures vary significantly across nodes, introducing noise and irrelevant information into node embeddings, particularly in classification. This leads to spurious correlations, a phenomenon referred to as graph structural entanglement(Zhu et al., 2020; Rusch et al., 2023)，这模糊了类别之间的决策边界，并使分类变得更加具有挑战性。, which blurs the decision boundaries between categories and renders classification notably more challenging.

However, these models often struggle to filter noisy or irrelevant signals introduced by the complex and diverse topological structures in real-world graphs. Transformer-based architectures in graph learning, such as Graphormer(Ying et al., 2021)，利用自注意力来捕获远程依赖关系，但它们经常忽略局部噪声并在大图上表现出较高的计算成本。, leverage self-attention to capture long-range dependencies, but they often overlook localized noise and exhibit high computational costs on large graphs.

对比表征学习。Contrastive Representation Learning.

班级引导对比学习已成为图表示学习的基石，其中有监督对比学习（SCL）等开创性工作Class‑guided contrastive learning has become a cornerstone of graph representation learning, with seminal works such as Supervised Contrastive Learning (SCL)(Khosla et al., 2020)，原型对比学习（PCL）, Prototypical Contrastive Learning (PCL)(Li et al., 2021)，和图ECL, and GraphECL(Xiao et al., 2024)。 这些方法通过采用一组聚类原型（或质心）作为锚点来近似全局特征分布，从而避免了详尽的成对比较，从而实现了计算效率和稳定的优化。 受基于原型范式的启发，BES采用基于中心的相似性来有效捕获结构纠缠，并进一步设计自适应预更新机制来选择性地过滤噪声拓扑结构。. These methods avoid exhaustive pairwise comparisons by employing a set of cluster prototypes (or centroids) as anchors to approximate global feature distributions, achieving computational efficiency and stable optimization. Motivated by the prototype-based paradigm, BES adopts center-based similarity to efficiently capture structural entanglement and further designs an adaptive pre-update mechanism to selectively filter noisy topological structures.

解缠结的图表示学习。Disentangled Graph Representation Learning.

图上的解缠结表示学习旨在将节点嵌入分解为独立因素（特征级解缠）或将与任务相关的子图从噪声中分离出来（结构级解缠）Disentangled representation learning on graphs aims to decompose node embeddings into independent factors (feature-level disentanglement) or separate task-relevant subgraphs from noise (structure-level disentanglement)(Ma et al., 2019)。 特征级纠缠主要是由 GNN 的共享模型参数引起的，现有方法引入多通道图编码器和正则化目标来鼓励分解的潜在表示. Feature-level entanglement is primarily caused by shared model parameters of GNNs, which existing methods introduce multi-channel graph encoders and regularization objectives to encourage factorized latent representations(Ma et al., 2019)。 然而，这些方法在消息传递过程中仍然忽略邻居噪声，导致跨节点干扰，限制了它们隔离纠缠源的能力。 其他工作侧重于通过识别与任务相关的子图或抑制虚假边缘来进行结构级解缠. However, these approaches still ignore neighbor noise during message passing, leading to cross-node interference that limits their ability to isolate the sources of entanglement. Other works focus on structure-level disentanglement by identifying task-relevant subgraphs or suppressing spurious edges(Miao et al., 2022)。 尽管特征编码和结构学习很有效，但它们通常是联合优化的，并且大多数现有方法缺乏特征级和结构级纠缠之间的原则性分离，这就激发了对显式控制特征耦合的框架的需求，以更好地研究结构引起的干扰。. Despite their effectiveness, feature encoding and structure learning are often optimized jointly, and most existing approaches lack a principled separation between feature-level and structure-level entanglement, motivating the need for frameworks that explicitly control feature coupling to better study structure-induced interference.

在这项工作中，我们通过揭示边界节点作为拓扑模糊性的主要来源来解决图结构纠缠的挑战，其中不相关的拓扑信号会恶化表示学习。 基于这一见解，我们引入了边界嵌入整形（BES），这是一个通用插件模块，利用自适应对比学习将预测结构信息与虚假相关性分开。 理论分析和实证结果证实，BES 有效地细化了决策边界，从而在标准基准上显着提高了性能。 此外，将 BES 扩展到低标签和自监督环境（其中类别引导信号稀缺或不存在）代表了将其适用性扩大到注释有限的大规模图形的有希望的途径。In this work, we tackled the challenge of graph structural entanglement by revealing boundary nodes as the principal sources of topological ambiguity, where irrelevant topological signals deteriorate representation learning. Building upon this insight, we introduced Boundary Embedding Shaping (BES), a general-purpose plug-in module that leverages adaptive contrastive learning to disentangle predictive structural information from spurious correlations. Theoretical analysis and empirical results confirm that BES effectively refines decision boundaries, leading to significant performance gains on standard benchmarks. Furthermore, extending BES to low-label and self-supervised settings, where class-guided signals are scarce or absent, represents a promising avenue for broadening its applicability to large-scale graphs with limited annotation.
