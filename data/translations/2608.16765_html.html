1. 简介Introduction

文本到图像的生成Text-to-image generation(44; 5)取得了巨大的成功，但仅靠文字往往不足以传达精确的视觉细节。 这种限制促进了基于参考的图像生成，它允许用户将输出基于用户提供的视觉内容。 一个特别具有挑战性的扩展是多参考生成，其中模型必须联合调节多个视觉元素——这是虚拟试穿、合影构图和多源创意设计等现实工作流程必不可少的功能。 最近的模型，都是专有的（GPT-Image-1.5has achieved remarkable success, yet text alone is often insufficient to convey precise visual details. This limitation has motivated reference-based image generation, which allows users to ground outputs in user-provided visual content. A particularly challenging extension is multi-reference generation, where models must jointly condition on multiple visual elements—a capability essential to real-world workflows such as virtual try-on, group photo composition, and multi-source creative design. Recent models, both proprietary (GPT-Image-1.5(36), 纳米香蕉2, Nano Banana 2(41)）和开源（OmniGen2) and open-source (OmniGen2(56)、Emu3.5, Emu3.5(3), Qwen-图像-编辑, Qwen-Image-Edit(55)），在遵循结合不同来源的实体、属性和样式的指令方面表现出了强大的能力。), have demonstrated strong capabilities in following instructions that combine entities, attributes, and styles from different sources.

Recent efforts like MultiBanana(37), MICON 工作台, MICON-Bench(57), 和 MacroBench, and MacroBench(2)开创了这个方向。 然而，虽然这些工作涵盖了更复杂的参考设置，但它们并没有从根本上重新思考底层的评估结构。 遵循早期生成和编辑基准的设计，他们仍然围绕预定义的组织测试用例have pioneered this direction. 3. TRACE-Bench

3.1. 概述Overview

5. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presentedTRACE-Bench，一个面向能力的多参考图像生成基准。 我们没有围绕预定义的任务类型组织评估，而是将多引用生成分解为四个原子运算符（Anchor、Disentangle、Apply、Compose）。 它们的组成结构是基准构建、操作员对齐评估和诊断树分析的统一基础。 对 9 个领先模型的评估表明，主要瓶颈在于属性解缠（, a capability-oriented benchmark for multi-reference image generation. Rather than organizing evaluation around predefined task types, we decompose multi-reference generation into four atomic operators (Anchor, Disentangle, Apply, Compose). Their compositional structure serves as the unified basis for benchmark construction, operator-aligned evaluation, and diagnostic tree analysis. Evaluation of 9 leading models reveals that the primary bottleneck lies in attribute disentanglement (gg) 和绑定 () and binding (⊕\oplus）而不是场景级合成（) rather than scene-level composition (CC），并且诊断树分析可以有效地将交叉引用干扰与独立能力缺陷分开。 未来，我们希望能力导向的表述不仅可以作为评估工具，还可以作为有针对性的模型改进的指南。), and that diagnostic tree analysis can effectively separate cross-reference interference from standalone capability deficits. In the future, we hope the capability-oriented formulation can serve not only as an evaluation tool but also as a guide for targeted model improvement.
