MARS：幅度感知排名统计MARS: Magnitude-Aware Rank Statistics

Muhammad Rajabinasab Afsaneh M. Nejad Arthur Zimek
摘要Abstract

机器学习模型的综合评估是确保它们按照预期稳健且一致地运行的关键。为了总结实验结果并选出获胜者，使用了临界差异（CD）图。标准 CD 图依赖于离散等级，忽略了模型之间性能差距的大小，从而提出了我们称之为量级盲性的问题。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了幅度感知排名统计（MARS），它将相对裕度系数作为离散排名的权重。该系数根据最佳表现者和最差表现者之间的距离进行排名，并使用动态投影来处理边界情况。随后计算 CD 值，MARS 可以更真实地统计模型性能差异，并更深入地了解方法在大量实验环境中的实际表现。Comprehensive evaluation of machine learning models is the key to make sure that they perform as robustly and consistently as desired. In order to summarize the experimental results and pick a winner, Critical Difference (CD) diagrams are used. Standard CD diagrams rely on discrete ranks, discarding the magnitude of performance gaps between models, raising an issue which we call magnitude-blindness. In order to address this issue, we propose Magnitude-Aware Rank Statistics (MARS) that incorporates a relative margin coefficient as a weight for the discrete ranks. This coefficient scales ranks based on the distance between the best and worst performers, with a dynamic projection to handle boundary cases. Followed by the calculation of a CD value, MARS results in a more realistic statistical representation of differences of model performances and more insights on how methods actually perform in vast and extensive experimental settings.

2 背景Background

机器学习模型的综合评估需要强大的统计测试来提供跨异构数据集的有效比较The comprehensive evaluation of machine learning models requires robust statistical tests to provide a valid comparison across heterogeneous datasets(Japkowicz and Boukouvalas, 2025)。此过程通常遵循两阶段的频率论流程：进行全局综合测试以检测组内的任何显着差异，然后进行事后配对分析以识别特定的获胜关系，通常通过关键差异（CD）图进行可视化。. 4.7 讨论Discussion

我们演示了六种场景，在这些场景中，标准排名统计数据完全或部分失效，无法提供有关评估结果的有效且现实的见解。另一方面，我们建议的幅度感知排名统计（MARS）成功地捕获了实验结果的重要属性，并为实验结果和比较提供了强大而可靠的见解。我们还表明，MARS 可以从有限的观测中获取更多信息，更好地反映性能状况，因为它在计算中纳入了性能指标值。We demonstrated six scenarios in which standard ranking statistics completely or partially breaks down and fails to provide valid and realistic insights on the evaluation results. Our suggested Magnitude-Aware Rank Statistics (MARS), on the other hand, successfully captures important properties of the experimental results and provides robust and reliable insights into the experimental results and comparisons. We also show that MARS can draw more information from limited observations, reflecting the landscape of the performance better, as it incorporates the performance metric values in the calculations.

值得注意的是，MARS对排名的频率仍然很敏感，并且随着包含更多观测数据，基于MARS的分析结果变得更加确定。正如所观察到的，MARS 本质上对性能指标值中极少数的异常观察具有鲁棒性，并为排名统计产生可靠且鲁棒的结果。 因此，作为未加权的标准排名统计数据，MARS 仍然容易受到用于性能测试的数据集选择的影响。选择具有可比性和合理难度的有意义的数据集仍然是执行测试的研究人员的责任。It is noteworthy that MARS is still sensitive to the frequency of the rankings, and as more observations are included, analytical results based on MARS become more certain. As observed, MARS is inherently robust against extremely few anomalous observations in the performance metric values, and yields reliable and robust results for the ranking statistics. Thus, as the unweighted standard ranking statistic, also MARS remains susceptible to the selection of datasets used for the performance test. Selecting meaningful datasets at a comparable and reasonable level of difficulty remains the responsibility of the researcher performing the test.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 MARS，一种感知量级的排名统计和关键差异，以促进更稳健和现实的模型评估。 传统的排名统计比之前经常遵循的错误程序具有强大的优势，即简单地对许多数据集的性能分数进行平均，其中性能分数实际上不具有可比性。不考虑绩效指标的规模，排名确实具有可比性。然而，传统排名统计量的盲目性又会导致综合评价结果得出有问题的、不正确的结论。我们展示了 MARS 如何帮助解决传统排名分析在不同边缘情况和场景中的问题，再次考虑尺度，但不是直接直接进行，而是通过使用相对分数进行内置归一化。In this paper, we proposed MARS, a magnitude-aware rank statistics and critical difference to facilitate more robust and realistic model evaluation. The traditional rank statistic has a strong advantage over a faulty procedure often followed earlier, namely simply averaging performance scores over many datasets, where the performance scores are actually not comparable. The ranks are indeed comparable, disregarding the scale of performance measures. However, the magnitude-blindness of the traditional rank statistic can again lead to derivation of problematic and incorrect conclusions about the comprehensive evaluation results. We showed how MARS can help fixing the issues of traditional rank analysis in different edge cases and scenarios in taking the scale into account again, however, not directly but with a built-in normalization by using relative scores.

还提供易于使用的软件包，可轻松进行 MARS 以及标准排名统计和分析，并生成关键差异 (CD) 图。An easy-to-use software package is also provided to easily conduct MARS, as well as the standard rank statistics and analysis, and to generate Critical Difference (CD) diagrams.

一般来说，MARS 能够提供更多信息，因为它直接考虑性能指标值。我们通过不同的场景观察到，MARS提供的图片往往包含更多的见解，并导致对整个评估和实验结果有更深入的理解。纳入绩效指标值并避免MARS in general is able to provide more information as it takes the performance metric values into account directly. We observed through different scenarios that the picture which MARS provides often includes more insights and leads to a deeper understanding of the entire evaluation and experimental results. The incorporation of performance metric values and avoiding量级失明magnitude-blindness有助于使实验和分析更加稳健，通常可以提供更全面的实验景观。helps making the experiments and analyses more robust, often offering a more comprehensive view of the experimental landscape.

最后的问题是标准的排名分析和统计是否应该完全被MARS取代。我们想声明并强调，有关实验和测试的许多决定都是研究人员的责任。选择合适的数据集、仔细评估竞争对手、选择评估框架以及其他决策，对于传达模型性能和实验结果中的正确信息非常重要。在本文中，我们提出了 MARS 并强调了使我们相信它是进行排名分析的正确且富有洞察力的方法的原因。使用 MARS 代替或与标准排名统计一起使用，或根本不使用它，是进行评估和测试的研究人员的决定和责任。The final question is whether the standard rank analysis and statistics should be entirely replaced by MARS or not. We would like to state and emphasize, that many decisions with regards to the experiments and the tests are the responsibility of the researcher. Selecting suitable datasets, carefully evaluating the competitors, choosing the evaluation framework, and other decisions as such, are very important for conveying the right message which lays within the performance of a model and the experimental results. In this paper, we proposed MARS and highlighted the reasons which make us believe it is a proper and insightful way of conducting rank analyses. Using MARS instead of or alongside with the standard rank statistics, or not using it at all, is the decision and the responsibility of the researcher conducting the evaluations and tests.
