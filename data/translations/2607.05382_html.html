6 结论Conclusion

核心问题不是如何构建一个知道一切的模型，而是如何构建一个知道它不知道的系统。这种分裂创建了一个特定于生成器的知识边界，我们已经证明，这个边界虽然很难先验地指定，但可以通过协同训练来发现。我们的证据表明，这种自我意识来自协同训练动态，而不仅仅是规模。The central question is not how to build a model that knows everything, but how to build a system that knows what it does not know. This split creates a generator-specific knowledge boundary, and we have shown that this boundary, though hard to specify a priori, is discoverable through co-training. Our evidence suggests that this self-awareness emerges from co-training dynamics, not from scale alone.

Online DPO serves a dual function: it pushes the generator’s boundary outward by internalizing stable visual knowledge, and it teaches the generator to use imperfect references without being corrupted by them. Rejection finetuning then recalibrates the reasoner to search only what the strengthened generator still cannot render. 我们故意采用最小的配方（一次 DPO 传递、一次 RFT 传递、一个 4B 生成器、一个 8B 推理器）以最小的有用规模验证该原理。随后的每次迭代都可以将边界进一步向外推，并进一步向内收紧搜索策略，从而收敛到只有真正的上下文知识才能触发搜索的状态。一个自然的反对意见是，下一代模型将大规模吸收更多知识，从而无需进行外部搜索。但是，训练数据是有限的，而世界是无界且不断运动的：没有任何模型，无论规模如何，都可以在训练截止后内化有关事件的知识，对于任何可行的数据集来说都太罕见的实体，或者跨社区和时间演变的文化知识。知识边界可能会随着规模的扩大而向外移动，但它不会消失；协同训练可以发现任何规模的任何给定生成器的位置。额外的迭代是否会锐化边界，较大的生成器是否会内化较小生成器中抵制参数化的知识，以及更丰富的奖励信号是否会加速最困难层的收益，这些都是定义清晰研究轨迹的开放性问题。Our deliberately minimal recipe (one DPO pass, one RFT pass, a 4B generator, an 8B reasoner) validates the principle at the smallest useful scale. Each subsequent iteration can push the boundary further outward and tighten the search policy further inward, converging toward a regime where only genuinely contextual knowledge triggers search. A natural objection is that next-generation models will absorb more knowledge at scale, making external search unnecessary. But training data is finite while the world is unbounded and in constant motion: no model, regardless of scale, can internalize knowledge about events after its training cutoff, entities too rare for any feasible dataset, or cultural knowledge that evolves across communities and time. The knowledge boundary may shift outward with scale, but it cannot disappear; co-training discovers where it lies for any given generator, at any scale. Whether additional iterations sharpen the boundary, whether larger generators internalize knowledge that resists parameterization in smaller ones, and whether richer reward signals accelerate gains on the hardest strata are open questions that define a clear research trajectory.

除了这些迫在眉睫的问题之外，这项工作还开辟了几个更广泛的研究方向。知识边界的缩放法则是什么：随着生成器规模的增加，可内部化的集合是否会在故障类别中均匀扩展，或者某些类别（例如文化特异性）是否会抵制内部化？是否可以在没有完全协同训练的情况下从模型内部预测边界，例如通过探测分类器或不确定性估计？内部化/上下文分离是否可以推广到视频、3D 和音乐生成等其他模式，这些模式也面临有限训练数据和无限制用户请求的相同结构问题？Beyond these immediate questions, this work opens several broader research directions. What is the scaling law of the knowledge boundary: does the internalizable set expand uniformly across failure categories as generator size increases, or do certain categories (e.g., cultural specificity) resist internalization? Can the boundary be predicted from model internals without full co-training, for instance via probing classifiers or uncertainty estimates? Does the internalizable/contextual split generalize to other modalities such as video, 3D, and music generation, which face the same structural problem of finite training data and unbounded user requests?

附录FAppendix F局限性和未来方向Limitations and future directions

我们的工作有意优先考虑深度洞察而不是详尽的优化，这种选择为未来的研究开辟了几个有希望的途径。Our work deliberately prioritizes depth of insight over exhaustive optimization, and this choice opens several promising avenues for future research.

迭代协同训练。Iterative co-training.

我们的配方由单个 DPO 传递和随后的单个 RFT 传递组成。 虽然这一最小协议已经产生了单调改进并验证了核心原理，但内化知识和情境知识之间的知识边界不太可能在一次迭代中完全解决。 多轮协同训练（生成器和推理器在几个周期中交替改进）可以逐渐锐化这一边界并释放额外的收益。 研究这种迭代循环中的收敛行为、收益递减和潜在的不稳定性是一个令人兴奋的方向。Our recipe consists of a single DPO pass followed by a single RFT pass. While this minimal protocol already produces monotonic improvement and validates the core principle, the knowledge boundary between internalizable and contextual knowledge is unlikely to be fully resolved in one iteration. Multi-round co-training—where the generator and reasoner alternate improvement over several cycles—could progressively sharpen this boundary and unlock additional gains. Investigating convergence behavior, diminishing returns, and potential instability in such iterative loops is an exciting direction.

发电机规模和架构。Generator scale and architecture.

我们的协同训练实验使用 4B 参数生成器 (Flux.2-Klein-4B) 和 7B 参数生成器 (Bagel)。 我们的最佳结果与前沿商业系统之间的巨大差距表明，更大或架构不同的生成器可能会内化更广泛的知识，改变知识边界，并可能改变需要搜索的提示的分布。 研究内部化/情境分裂如何在生成器尺度上演变，以及某些故障类别（例如，时间近期与文化特异性）是否表现出本质上不同的尺度行为，将加深对作为模型能力函数的知识边界的理解。Our co-training experiments use a 4B-parameter generator (Flux.2-Klein-4B) and 7B-parameter generator (Bagel). The substantial gap between our best result and frontier commercial systems suggests that larger or architecturally different generators may internalize a broader swath of knowledge, shifting the knowledge boundary and potentially changing the distribution of prompts that require search. Studying how the internalizable/contextual split evolves across generator scales—and whether certain failure categories (e.g., temporal-recent vs. cultural-specificity) exhibit qualitatively different scaling behavior—would deepen understanding of the knowledge boundary as a function of model capacity.

奖励信号。Reward signals.

我们当前的评估依赖于自动 VLM 判断 (Gemini-3-Flash)。 虽然它与人类评分具有很强的相关性（SpearmanOur current evaluation relies on an automated VLM judge (Gemini-3-Flash). The choice of DPO is intentional: it resists reward noise by selecting the best- and worst-ranked generations to construct preference pairs[32].
