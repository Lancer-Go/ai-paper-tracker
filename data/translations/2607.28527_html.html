Before execution, MANTA initializes a task-conditioned topology from prior structural experience. 简介Introduction

生物系统不仅在遇到环境之前进行适应，而且还通过与环境压力的持续相互作用来适应。现代人工智能系统正在经历类似的转变，从主要是离线优化到执行过程中的适应。大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地作为代理系统的组件运行，用于推理、使用工具、保留经验并与其他代理进行协调。这些系统不仅仅依赖于部署前的权重更新，还可以通过提示、演示、推理跟踪、记忆、工具、工作流程和协调协议在推理时进行调整。情境学习首先表明演示可以在推理过程中诱导任务适应Biological systems adapt not only before encountering their environments, but also through continued interaction with environmental pressures. Modern AI systems are undergoing a similar transition from predominantly offline optimization toward adaptation during execution. Large language models (LLMs) increasingly operate as components of agentic systems that reason, use tools, retain experience, and coordinate with other agents. Rather than relying only on pre-deployment weight updates, these systems can adapt at inference time through prompts, demonstrations, reasoning traces, memories, tools, workflows, and coordination protocols. In-context learning first showed that demonstrations can induce task adaptation during inference(Brown et al. 2020)，而思维链提示和ReAct则证明了中间推理和推理与行动轨迹的价值, while chain-of-thought prompting and ReAct demonstrated the value of intermediate reasoning and reasoning-and-acting trajectories(Wei et al. 2023; Yao et al. 2023b).

Most approaches, however, adapt individual agents rather than how multiple agents are organized and communicate.

多代理框架将复杂的任务分解为专门的角色和协作推理过程，使代理能够辩论、验证、聚合、实施、批评或协调以实现共同的目标Multi-agent frameworks decompose complex tasks across specialized roles and collaborative reasoning processes, allowing agents to debate, verify, aggregate, implement, critique, or coordinate toward a shared objective(Wu et al. 2023; Qian et al. 2024; Hong et al. 2024; Du et al. 2023)。它们的拓扑结构决定了谁与谁通信、如何路由信息、在何处进行验证以及如何改进中间输出。. Their topology determines who communicates with whom, how information is routed, where validation occurs, and how intermediate outputs are refined.

拓扑通常仍然在设计时进行优化，而不是在执行期间进行调整。现有方法联合优化提示和拓扑或自动化工作流程设计Topology is still typically optimized at design time rather than adapted during execution. Existing methods jointly optimize prompts and topologies or automate workflow design(Zhou et al. 2026; Zhang et al. 2025a; Hu et al. 2025; Shang et al. 2025)，但通常会在任务执行之前修复结果结构。因此，拓扑是否可以在线自我改进仍有待探索。, but generally fix the resulting structure before task execution. Whether topology can self-improve online therefore remains underexplored.

这就提出了一个核心问题：多智能体系统能否在解决每个单独任务的同时改进其通信拓扑？与依赖于训练、离线搜索或预执行优化的先前方法不同，这需要实例条件规划、中间协作跟踪的测试时拓扑突变以及拓扑经验的交叉运行传输，而无需更新模型权重。This raises a central question: Can a multi-agent system improve its communication topology while solving each individual task? Unlike prior approaches that rely on training, offline search, or pre-execution optimization, this requires instance-conditioned planning, test-time topology mutation from intermediate collaboration traces, and cross-run transfer of topology experience without updating model weights.

查询条件规划。Query-conditioned planning.

规划器接收任务和经验记忆，而无需访问基准标识或手工设计的拓扑。它分析任务要求和可能的流程风险，然后生成一个紧凑的计划，指定交互模式、代理数量以及任何可选的验证程序或嵌套组。确定性代码将此计划扩展为完整的拓扑，分配角色和上下文策略，并验证所有结构约束。The Planner receives the task and the experience memory without access to the benchmark identity or a hand-designed topology. It analyzes the task requirements and likely process risks, then produces a compact plan specifying the interaction pattern, agent count, and any optional verifier or nested groups. Deterministic code expands this plan into a complete topology, assigns roles and context policies, and validates all structural constraints.

转执行。Turn execution.

每个协作回合根据其组结构递归地执行拓扑。代理通过结构化中继数据包而不是原始转录本进行通信。这些数据包总结了他们的答案、证据、信心和未解决的问题。共享上下文控制器强制执行每个代理的可见性策略。在执行过程中，相同的工具调用会进行重复数据删除，而仅附加的证据分类账会保留跨分支生成的声明和支持证据。Each collaboration turn executes the topology recursively according to its group structure. Agents communicate through structured relay packets rather than raw transcripts. These packets summarize their answers, evidence, confidence, and unresolved issues. A shared-context controller enforces the visibility policy of every agent. By combining topology planning, trace-based auditing, targeted structural repair, and cross-run experience, MANTA adapts how agents communicate and validate information when the initial organization becomes insufficient. Our findings highlight topology as a distinct layer of self-improvement, where effective adaptation may involve rewiring communication, changing execution order, or adding validation rather than simply increasing the number of agents.
