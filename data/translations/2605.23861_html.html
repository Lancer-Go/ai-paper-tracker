利用基础模型进行因果生成建模Leveraging Foundation Models for Causal Generative Modeling

Aneesh Komanduri University of ArkansasFayettevilleArkansasUSA akomandu@uark.edu and Xintao Wu University of ArkansasFayettevilleArkansasUSA xintaowu@uark.edu
（2009 年 6 月 5 日）(5 June 2009)
摘要。Abstract.

因果生成模型对于开发能够进行反事实推理的可靠且透明的人工智能系统至关重要。虽然现有方法侧重于在生成模型的训练过程中整合因果约束，但它们通常缺乏统一的框架来利用预训练基础模型的零样本推理能力。我们引入了 FM-CGM，这是一种使用预训练基础模型进行端到端视觉因果推理的模块化框架。 FM-CGM 通过三个核心组件形式化因果管道：概念提取器、概念操纵器和反事实生成器。通过利用大型推理模型进行因果推理和文本到图像扩散模型进行生成，我们的方法可以实现零样本因果发现、干预和反事实生成。然后，我们开发了因果语义指导（CSG），这是一种基于交叉注意的机制，可确保语义干预传播到后代概念，同时保留不变区域。我们凭经验表明，我们的方法可以识别合理的因果结构，并且适合忠实的反事实图像生成。Causal generative modeling is essential for developing reliable and transparent AI systems capable of counterfactual reasoning. While existing approaches focus on integrating causal constraints during the training of generative models, they often lack a unified framework to leverage the zero-shot reasoning capabilities of pretrained foundation models. We introduce FM-CGM, a modular framework for end-to-end visual causal reasoning using pretrained foundation models. FM-CGM formalizes the causal pipeline through three core components: a concept extractor, a concept manipulator, and a counterfactual generator. By leveraging a large reasoning model for causal inference and a text-to-image diffusion model for generation, our approach enables zero-shot causal discovery, intervention, and counterfactual generation. We then develop Causal Semantic Guidance (CSG), a cross-attention-based mechanism that ensures semantic interventions propagate to descendant concepts while preserving invariant regions. We empirically show that our approach can identify plausible causal structures and is suitable for faithful counterfactual image generation.

1. 简介Introduction

因果生成模型Causal generative modeling(Komanduri et al., 2024b)在从高维数据中发现因果概念及其关系的背景下进行了研究has been studied both in the context of discovering causal concepts and their relationships from high-dimensional data(Scholkopf et al., 2021)以及对反事实场景的推理and reasoning about counterfactual scenarios(Ribeiro et al., 2025)。这个方向有助于提高人工智能系统的可靠性和透明度。因果生成模型的现有工作主要侧重于将因果关系直接整合到生成模型（例如 GAN）的训练过程中. This direction has contributed to improving the reliability and transparency of AI systems. Existing work in causal generative modeling primarily focuses on integrating causality directly into the training process of generative models such as GANs(Kocaoglu et al., 2018), VAE, VAEs(Pawlowski et al., 2020; Komanduri et al., 2024a)和扩散模型, and Diffusion models(Komanduri et al., 2024c)通过对生成因果因素之间的关系进行建模。一些作品还研究了使用最先进的扩散模型的反事实图像生成by modeling relationships among generative causal factors. Several works have also studied counterfactual image generation using state-of-the-art diffusion models(Sanchez and Tsaftaris, 2022; Spyrou et al., 2025)。最近，由于大型生成模型（例如大型语言模型（LLM）和大型推理模型）的推理能力，人们正在进行利用基础模型作为领域专家的力量来改进因果发现和推理的研究. Recently, due to the inference capabilities of large-scale generative models such as large language model (LLMs) and large reasoning models, there has been ongoing research utilizing the power of foundation models as domain experts to improve causal discovery and inference(Vashishtha et al., 2023)，以及因果代理框架来自动化科学发现中的任务, and causal agent frameworks to automate tasks in scientific discovery(Verma et al., 2025)。然而，没有一个统一的框架可以利用预训练基础模型的功能来发现生成因素、推理干预措施并生成反事实场景，即因果生成模型的完整流程。. However, there is no unified framework that leverages the capabilities of pretrained foundation models to discover generative factors, reason about interventions, and generate counterfactual scenarios, the complete pipeline of causal generative modeling.

在这项工作中，我们从预训练基础模型的角度提出了一种视觉因果推理的形式主义。我们开发In this work, we propose a formalism for visual causal reasoning from the perspective of pretrained foundation models. Specifically, we implement a practical method consisting of a reasoning VLM (Qwen3-VL) as the concept extractor and manipulator and text-to-image generative model (Stable Diffusion XL) as the counterfactual generator. To translate semantic interventions inferred by the concept manipulator into pixel-space counterfactual edits, we propose因果语义指导（CSG）causal semantic guidance (CSG)通过文本到图像扩散模型的文本调节机制运行的过程。具体来说，我们利用交叉注意力归因图来定位与每个概念及其后代相关的区域，并构建一个推理时间指导机制，该机制（a）放大对干预概念的编辑，（b）鼓励后代概念的一致变化，以及（c）抑制对非后代（不变）概念的意外变化。这会产生反事实图像，这些图像既在视觉上合理，又与预期的因果语义一致。a procedure that operates through the text-to-image diffusion model’s text-conditioning mechanism. This yields counterfactual images that are both visually plausible and aligned with the intended causal semantics.

7. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了基础模型驱动的因果生成模型，这是由预训练的基础模型组件组成的因果生成模型的抽象。然后，我们开发了一个框架，使用大型视觉语言模型进行概念提取和操作，并使用稳定扩散 XL 进行反事实生成。我们提出因果语义指导，这是一种在 LEDITS++ 上扩展的推理时间编辑方法，以确保在反事实生成的图像中充分表示对后代概念的干预和传播。定性和定量的实证评估表明，我们的框架有望利用基础模型的力量来模块化和自动化因果生成建模的过程。In this work, we propose Foundation Model Powered Causal Generative Model, an abstraction for causal generative modeling consisting of pretrained foundation model components. We then develop a framework using large vision-language models for concept extraction and manipulation and Stable Diffusion XL for counterfactual generation. We propose Causal Semantic Guidance, an inference-time editing approach extending on LEDITS++ to ensure interventions and propagation to descendant concepts are sufficiently represented in the counterfactually generated image. Qualitative and quantitative empirical evaluation shows that our framework is promising for modularizing and automating the process of causal generative modeling by utilizing the power of foundation models.
