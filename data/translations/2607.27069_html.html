多模态空间推理的视觉信用分配Visual Credit Assignment for Multimodal Spatial Reasoning

Feixiang Liu1,2, Qiang Qiu1, Lanbo Sun1,2, Nan Wei1, Huawei Shen1, Xueqi Cheng1
摘要Abstract

即使图像在无图像上下文之外几乎没有提供支持，封闭的是/否空间基准也可以奖励正确的答案。在固定的强制选择界面下，视觉信用审核（VCA）分离两个估计值：基准图像是否比纯文本和空白对照为模型声明的决策提供更多支持，以及模型是否对特定于关系的视觉证据做出响应。第一次审核无需培训和标签，也不需要翻转答案。应用标签产生依赖信用正确性（D-CC）；在正确的项目上，它等于相同控制黄金对齐的正增益，而预测对齐则将审计扩展到错误。在四个开放的 MLLM 和两个空间基准中，12.73-26.25% 的决策是正确的但未经认可。匹配的同分割图像排列将 D-CC 减少了 21.25–47.80 个点，每对 95% 的间隔都高于零。固定像素关系对比和Closed yes/no spatial benchmarks can reward a correct answer even when the image adds little support beyond no-image contexts. Under a fixed forced-choice interface, Visual Credit Audit (VCA) separates two estimands: whether the benchmark image gives the model’s declared decision more support than text-only and blank controls, and whether the model responds to relation-specific visual evidence. The first audit is training- and label-free and does not require an answer flip. Applying labels yields dependence-credited correctness (D-CC); on correct items, it equals same-control gold-aligned positive gain, while prediction alignment extends the audit to errors. Across four open MLLMs and two spatial benchmarks, 12.73–26.25% of decisions are correct yet uncredited. Matched same-split image permutation reduces D-CC by 21.25–47.80 points, with every paired 95% interval above zero. Fixed-pixel relation contrasts and a3×33{\times}3证据源阶乘显示了为什么空控件无法识别关系响应。在受控的正确但未认可的协议决策中，对关系逆转的响应范围为 81.57-100.00%，而 32.11% 汇总了更改答案。对 108 个几何兼容编辑的独立审核结果提供了有限的自然图像对应检查。因此，VCA 将基准测试的成功分解为正确性、附加图像支持和关系一致的响应。evidence-source factorial show why null controls cannot identify relation response. Among controlled correct-but-uncredited agreement decisions, response to relation reversal spans 81.57–100.00%, while 32.11% pooled change answer. Independently audited outcomes on 108 geometry-compatible edits provide a bounded natural-image correspondence check. VCA thereby decomposes benchmark success into correctness, additional image support, and relation-consistent response.

简介Introduction

空间推理是多模态系统的一项基本功能，它必须了解对象的位置以及它们之间的关系。现代 MLLM 将大规模视觉语言预训练与强大的指令遵循先验相结合Spatial reasoning is a basic capability for multimodal systems that must understand where objects are and how they relate. Modern MLLMs combine large-scale visual-language pretraining with strong instruction-following priors(Bai et al. 2023; Chen et al. 2024b; Bai and others 2025; Wang et al. 2025; Li et al. 2024a)，VSR和WhatsUp等空间基准评估它们是否可以回答涉及左/右、上/下以及其他参考系的关系问题, and spatial benchmarks such as VSR and WhatsUp evaluate whether they can answer relation questions involving left/right, above/below, and other reference frames(Liu et al. 2023a; Kamath et al. 2023; Rajabi and Kosecka 2024)。然而，封闭式是/否问题的正确性与增量视觉支持不同。模型可以对看似合理的查询（例如“电视在猫上方”）回答“是”，因为文本很熟悉，提示格式有偏差，或者数据集包含强对象关系先验。即使无图像控件至少同样强烈地支持相同的决定，答案也是正确的。. Yet correctness on a closed yes/no question is not the same as incremental visual support. A model can answer “yes” to a plausible query such as “the TV is above the cat” because the text is familiar, the prompt format is biased, or the dataset contains strong object-relation priors. The answer is then correct even when no-image controls support the same decision at least as strongly.

这种模糊性在我们的审计结果中显而易见。在 GSR-COCO 上，Qwen 的原始图像准确度达到 90.91，但所有示例中只有 78.18 是正确的，并且图像的支持比纯文本或空白控件更有力。对于 LLaVA，相应的值为 81.93 和 55.68。在消除无图像上下文必须支持相反答案的要求后，这些差距仍然存在。此外，当原始决策方向保持固定时，确定性同分割图像排列在所有八次运行中将 D-CC 减少了 21.25-47.80 个点，每个配对间隔都高于零。因此，核心问题不仅仅是空间精度低；在预先指定的控制下，即使没有基准图像支持优势，它也是成功的。This ambiguity is visible in our audit results. On GSR-COCO, Qwen reaches 90.91 original-image accuracy, but only 78.18 of all examples are both correct and more strongly supported by the image than by text-only or blank controls. For LLaVA, the corresponding values are 81.93 and 55.68. These gaps remain after removing the requirement that a no-image context must favor the opposite answer. Moreover, when the original decision direction is held fixed, a deterministic same-split image permutation reduces D-CC by 21.25–47.80 points across all eight runs, with every paired interval above zero. The core issue is therefore not simply low spatial accuracy; it is success without benchmark-image support advantage under the prespecified controls.

这种区别改变了基准解释。具有相似准确性的模型在支持合格的性能方面可能有所不同，并且总体成功可以隐藏改进是来自图像条件证据还是来自更强的答案先验。决策级审计可以测量这些差异，而无需重新训练评估的模型。它还揭示了第二个区别：没有边际图像优势本身并不意味着没有视觉反应。当文本和图像冗余地支持相同的答案时，删除图像可以保留支持，即使颠倒其关系会改变模型的分数。因此，空对照和关系反转图像回答了不同的识别问题。This distinction changes benchmark interpretation. Models with similar accuracy can differ in support-qualified performance, and aggregate success can hide whether improvements arise from image-conditioned evidence or from stronger answer priors. A decision-level audit makes these differences measurable without retraining the evaluated model. It also exposes a second distinction: no marginal image advantage does not by itself imply no visual response. When text and image redundantly support the same answer, removing the image can preserve support even though reversing its relation changes the model’s score. Null controls and relation-reversed images therefore answer different identification questions.

现有的工作暴露了相邻的问题，但没有直接将功劳分配给固定的空间决策。 VQA 去偏差和反事实评估减少了语言先验或在变化的条件下测试一致性Existing work exposes adjacent issues but does not directly assign credit to a fixed spatial decision. VQA debiasing and counterfactual evaluation reduce language priors or test consistency under changed conditions(Zhang et al. 2016; Agrawal et al. 2018; Niu et al. 2021; Zhang et al. 2024; Li et al. 2024b)。空间推理方法通过专门的数据、接地表示、3D 先验或视觉文本推理来改进底层模型. Spatial-reasoning methods improve the underlying model with specialized data, grounded representations, 3D priors, or visual-textual reasoning(Chen et al. 2024a; Ma et al. 2024; Liang et al. 2025; Xu et al. 2025)。幻觉和对比解码方法减少了图像不一致的输出. Hallucination and contrastive-decoding methods reduce image-inconsistent outputs(Rohrbach et al. 2018; Li et al. 2023; Leng et al. 2024; Yin et al. 2025)。这些线是互补的，但它们并没有询问特定的强制选择空间决策是否从其图像获得比匹配的无图像上下文更大的支持，同时在固定像素上保持关系一致。. These lines are complementary, but they do not ask whether a particular forced-choice spatial decision receives greater support from its image than from matched no-image contexts while remaining relation-consistent on fixed pixels.

我们使用We use视觉信用visual credit作为此操作支持审计的简写，而不是内部因果信用分配。信用被分配给特定的决策-背景对比，而不是潜在的机制或不受约束的生成行为。它提出了两个不同的问题。首先，相对于无图像对照，原始图像是否增加了对模型自身决策的支持？其次，相同的评分规则是否可以区分固定像素上的查询关系和错误替代项？将这些条件混为一谈会产生误导：决策可能对图像敏感但关系不一致，或者关系一致但缺乏相对图像支持优势。as shorthand for this operational support audit, not for internal causal credit assignment. Credit is assigned to a specified decision–context contrast, not to a latent mechanism or unconstrained generation behavior. It asks two distinct questions. First, does the original image increase support for the model’s own decision relative to no-image controls? Second, does the same scoring rule distinguish the queried relation from false alternatives on fixed pixels? Conflating these conditions is misleading: a decision may be image-sensitive but relation-inconsistent, or relation-consistent yet lack a relative image-support advantage.

VCA 的方法论贡献是一个二估计识别框架，而不是纯正确增益的新名称。它的支持轴将相同的双控制事件无标签地扩展到每个声明的决策上，保留支持优势的错误，并在应用标签后产生完整的准确性-信用分解。它的关系轴解决了空输入无法识别的另一个问题：固定像素关系分离和受控关系反转是否会改变模型的证据。匹配图像排列校准标题D-CC/C-U分解；经过审核的编辑提供有限的自然图像对应。VCA’s methodological contribution is a two-estimand identification framework, not a new name for a correct-only gain. Its support axis extends the same dual-control event label-free over every declared decision, preserving support-advantaged errors and yielding a complete accuracy–credit decomposition once labels are applied. Its relation axis addresses a different question that null inputs cannot identify: whether fixed-pixel relation separation and controlled relation reversal change the model’s evidence. Matched image permutation calibrates the headline D-CC/C-U decomposition; audited edits provide bounded natural-image correspondence.

相关工作Related Work

捷径学习和反事实 VQAShortcut Learning and Counterfactual VQA

视觉问答使图像条件推理可以大规模测量，但也揭示了正确答案可以遵循答案先验、数据集不平衡和仅问题相关性而不是视觉证据Visual question answering made image-conditioned reasoning measurable at scale, but also revealed that correct answers can follow answer priors, dataset imbalance, and question-only correlations rather than visual evidence(Goyal et al. 2017; Zhang et al. 2016; Agrawal et al. 2018)。去偏差、因果和反事实 VQA 方法会削弱语言先验分支、改变训练分布、估计直接和间接效果或扰乱图像问题条件以测试证据敏感性. Debiasing, causal, and counterfactual VQA methods weaken language-prior branches, change the training distribution, estimate direct and indirect effects, or perturb image-question conditions to test evidence sensitivity(Niu et al. 2021; Zhang et al. 2024; Li et al. 2024b)。这些工作确立了 VCA 背后的核心问题：准确性不足以显示证据的使用。 VCA 保持是/否项目固定，并询问其原始图像是否比没有足够视觉证据的匹配上下文为模型的空间决策提供更多支持。. These works establish the central concern behind VCA: accuracy is not enough to show evidence use. VCA keeps the yes/no item fixed and asks whether its original image gives the model’s spatial decision more support than matched contexts without sufficient visual evidence.

构图和空间关系基础Compositional and Spatial Relation Grounding

组合视觉语言评估测试模型是否绑定对象、属性、关系、常识和词序，而不是匹配粗略的图像文本统计数据Compositional vision-language evaluation tests whether models bind objects, attributes, relations, commonsense, and word order rather than matching coarse image-text statistics(Johnson et al. 2017; Thrush et al. 2022)。空间关系基准通过隔离关系词、参考框架以及左/右或上/下区别来加剧这个问题. Spatial-relation benchmarks sharpen this problem by isolating relation words, reference frames, and left/right or above/below distinctions(Liu et al. 2023a; Kamath et al. 2023; Rajabi and Kosecka 2024)。最近的方法通过专门的数据、接地表示、3D 先验、视觉文本推理或明确的空间提示来提高空间能力. Recent methods improve spatial ability through specialized data, grounded representations, 3D priors, visual-textual reasoning, or explicit spatial prompting(Chen et al. 2024a; Ogezi and Shi 2025; Ma et al. 2024; Liang et al. 2025; Xu et al. 2025)。这些工作主要询问模型在空间任务上是否变得更加准确或一致。相反，VCA 衡量的是当匹配的探针移除或替换相关视觉证据时观察到的成功是否仍然得到支持，而不是通过语言先验或图像不敏感行为来衡量。. These efforts primarily ask whether a model becomes more accurate or consistent on spatial tasks. VCA instead measures whether the observed success is still supported when matched probes remove or replace the relevant visual evidence, rather than by language priors or image-insensitive behavior.

在正确的项目上，VCA 的依赖事件在相同的控制下有意减少到与黄金对齐的正增益；贡献不是一个不同的仅正确公式。相反，VCA 在每个声明的决策上没有标签的情况下完成此事件，保留支持优势错误，并将生成的支持分解放置在单独标识的关系响应轴旁边。总下降不能为每个决策分配一个事件，纯黄金收益不能审计错误，纯翻转规则将减少的支持与相反答案的证据混为一谈。修复了多语言和以对为中心的检查测试跨答案表面的传输，而不是假设它。这种相对支持审核是对概率校准的补充，概率校准衡量置信度的可靠性On correct items, VCA’s dependence event intentionally reduces exactly to a gold-aligned positive gain under the same controls; the contribution is not a different correct-only formula. VCA instead completes this event without labels over every declared decision, retaining support-advantaged errors, and places the resulting support decomposition beside a separately identified relation-response axis. Aggregate drops cannot assign an event to each decision, gold-only gains cannot audit errors, and flip-only rules conflate reduced support with evidence for the opposite answer. Fixed multi-verbalizer and pair-centered checks test transport across answer surfaces rather than assuming it. This relative support audit is complementary to probabilistic calibration, which measures confidence reliability(Naeini et al. 2015; Guo et al. 2017).

问题设置和两轴审核Problem Setup and Two-Axis Audit

讨论Discussion

VCA 生成分层报告，而不是准确性的替代报告。准确性保证任务成功； D-CC 通过额外的图像支持来确定成功； PairCredit 测试图像特定的语义分离，而 Rel。VCA yields a layered report rather than one replacement for accuracy. Accuracy retains task success; D-CC identifies successes with additional image support; PairCredit tests image-specific semantic separation, while Rel.o当存在配对语句时，Joint 和 Joint 保留更严格的绝对检查。原始差距支持排名，而编辑后的结果检查语义转移。这些输出共同保留了正确性、相对支持度和关系辨别之间的区别。and Joint retain stricter absolute checks when paired statements exist. Raw gaps support ranking, while edited outcomes check semantic transfer. Together these outputs preserve the distinction between correctness, relative support, and relation discrimination.

方程中的四单元分解。The four-cell decomposition in Eq.3还给出了两种不同的诊断，而同样的准确性却无法做到这一点。 C-U 质量标记决策的正确性超出了所选控制下基准图像的支持。相反，支持优势错误质量标记了存在图像特定证据但映射到错误答案的决策。纯黄金收益必然会破坏第二个区别，因为它无法为错误的宣布决策提供证据。预测对齐保留它而不改变正确的项目事件。also gives two distinct diagnoses that the same accuracy cannot. C-U mass marks decisions whose correctness outruns support from the benchmark image under the chosen controls. Support-advantaged error mass instead marks decisions for which image-specific evidence is present but mapped to the wrong answer. A gold-only gain necessarily collapses this second distinction because it cannot credit evidence for an incorrect declared decision. Prediction alignment preserves it without altering the correct-item event.

在操作上，Accuracy 和 D-CC 将任务成功与支持合格的成功进行比较；非对角线单元区分无支持的正确性和有支持的错误；关系轴询问证据是否识别所查询的关系。这些级别共享缓存的边距，但保留单独的解释。 VCA 是准确性的事后伴侣，而不是拟合的替代分数。Operationally, Accuracy and D-CC compare task success with support-qualified success; the off-diagonal cells distinguish unsupported correctness from support-advantaged error; and the relation axis asks whether evidence identifies the queried relation. These levels share cached margins but retain separate interpretations. VCA is a post-hoc companion to accuracy, not a fitted replacement score.

纯文本和空白定义边际支持对比；交替控制测试其稳定性。阶乘提供了空控制无法识别的关系对立干预，区分了响应性和非响应性的未可信决策。其中立控制将关系证据与通用对象存在区分开来，而审计交换将这种区别与独立判断的自然图像编辑联系起来。Text-only and blank define the marginal support contrast; alternate controls test its stability. The factorial supplies the relation-opposite intervention that null controls cannot identify, separating responsive and nonresponsive uncredited decisions. Its neutral control distinguishes relation evidence from generic object presence, while audited swaps connect the distinction to independently judged natural-image edits.

至关重要的是，阶乘并没有取代 VCA 作为主要审计。它需要大多数自然基准无法提供的关系相反图像，而 VCA 使用基准输入和不带标签的与源无关的空控件。他们的角色是互补的：VCA 可以扩展以量化现有测试的边际信用；阶乘确定了为什么未经认可的决策仍然可以对愿景做出反应。不应将这两个估计值强行归为一个量。Crucially, the factorial does not replace VCA as the headline audit. It requires relation-opposite images that most natural benchmarks cannot supply, whereas VCA uses the benchmark input and source-agnostic null controls without labels. Their roles are complementary: VCA scales to quantify marginal credit on existing tests; the factorial identifies why an uncredited decision can still respond to vision. These two estimands should not be forced into one quantity.

两种设计选择使这种分解可审计。预测对齐锚定模型声明的决策，保留支持优势错误，而不是将它们折叠成黄金答案分数。超过纯文本和空白的最大值要求图像超过每个控件。交替空白、不匹配和编辑测试实施、正常图像充分性和语义转移，而无需重新定义标题事件。固定零阈值事件与连续排名共存，并且匹配图像校准确定总体优势特定于基准图像。Two design choices make this decomposition auditable. Prediction alignment anchors the model’s declared decision, retaining support-advantaged errors instead of folding them into a gold-answer score. The maximum over text-only and blank requires the image to exceed each control. Alternate blanks, mismatch, and edits test implementation, normal-image sufficiency, and semantic transfer without redefining the headline event. Fixed zero-threshold events coexist with continuous ranking, and matched image calibration establishes that the aggregate advantage is specific to the benchmark image.

局限性Limitations

VCA 衡量预先指定的控制下的相对支持，而不是内部因果路径或像素归因。其每个问题的比率是针对预先指定的强制选择界面定义的；固定的多语言器和以对为中心的分析评估等效答案表面上的传输。阶乘识别简单程序场景中受控关系变化的行为反应，而不是内部因果路径；自然基准编辑检查涵盖几何选择的子集。评估目标是跨两个空间基准和四个自回归 MLLM 的固定强制选择决策接口，而不是无约束的贪婪文本。开放式生成和其他推理领域需要特定于输出的支持定义。VCA measures relative support under prespecified controls, not an internal causal pathway or pixel attribution. Its per-question rates are defined for a prespecified forced-choice interface; fixed multi-verbalizer and pair-centered analyses evaluate transport across equivalent answer surfaces. The factorial identifies behavioral response to controlled relation changes in simple procedural scenes, not an internal causal path; the natural-benchmark edit check covers a geometry-selected subset. The evaluation target is a fixed forced-choice decision interface across two spatial benchmarks and four autoregressive MLLMs, not unconstrained greedy text. Open-ended generation and other reasoning domains require output-specific support definitions.

结论Conclusion

VCA 提供了一种无需训练的审计方法，可以检查空间决策是否获得图像的支持并区分固定像素上的关系。在四个 MLLM 和两个基准中，它量化了 12.73-26.25% 的正确但未可信的质量，这些质量隐藏在总精度中；匹配的图像排列确认了 21.25–47.80 点的原始图像 D-CC 过剩，所有配对间隔均为正值。识别边界和受控证据源因子表明为什么这种边际信用事件必须与关系响应分开：模型内对视觉反转的定向响应跨度为 81.57-100.00%，并且 97.80% 的汇总超过了对象保留中性控制。以对为中心的对比提高了答案表面的稳定性；审核编辑检查自然对应；缓存边距使所有输出无需重新训练即可重现。VCA provides a training-free audit of whether spatial decisions gain support from the image and distinguish relations on fixed pixels. Across four MLLMs and two benchmarks, it quantifies 12.73–26.25% correct-but-uncredited mass hidden by aggregate accuracy; matched image permutations confirm 21.25–47.80 points of original-image D-CC excess, with all paired intervals positive. An identification boundary and controlled evidence-source factorial show why this marginal-credit event must be separated from relation responsiveness: within-model directional response to visual reversal spans 81.57–100.00%, and 97.80% pooled exceed the object-preserving neutral control. Pair-centered contrasts improve answer-surface stability; audited edits check natural correspondence; cached margins make all outputs reproducible without retraining.
