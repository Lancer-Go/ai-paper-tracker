摘要Abstract

Transformer 推理受到自回归解码的限制，自回归解码通过令牌空间重复压缩丰富的隐藏计算，并使中间推理状态难以跨时间持续。我们引入具有时间中间层递归的 Transformers (TTransformer reasoning is limited by autoregressive decoding, which repeatedly compresses rich hidden computation through token space and makes it difficult for intermediate reasoning states to persist across time. We introduce Transformers with Temporal Middle-Layer Recurrence (T2MLR），一种基于变压器的潜在推理架构，它将前一个令牌的缓存中间层表示直接融合到当前令牌位置的较早层中，从而使抽象中间计算能够在解码步骤中持续存在，而推理开销很小。 通过自然语言预训练和多跳推理微调，TMLR), a transformers-based latent reasoning architecture that fuses a cached middle-layer representation from the previous token directly into an earlier layer of the current token position, enabling abstract intermediate computation to persist across decoding steps with little inference overhead. Across natural-language pretraining and multi-hop reasoning finetuning, T2MLR 始终优于数据和参数匹配的 Transformer 基线。此外，仅将递归应用于局部中间层块（少至网络的 20%）通常优于全层递归。重要的是，TMLR consistently outperforms data- and parameter-matched Transformer baselines. Moreover, applying recurrence to only a localized middle-layer block (as little as 20% of the network) often outperforms full-layer recurrence. Importantly, T2MLR 不需要从头开始预训练：将循环路径改造为现有的预训练 1.7B Transformer 并进行短暂微调，可显着改善数学推理，降低实际采用的障碍。这些结果表明，Transformers 中有效的潜在推理不需要像以前的工作那样在所有层上循环，而是可以从目标中间层递归中更强烈地出现。MLR does not require pretraining from scratch: retrofitting the recurrent pathway into an existing pretrained 1.7B Transformer and briefly finetuning substantially improves math reasoning, lowering the barrier to practical adoption. These results suggest that effective latent reasoning in Transformers does not require looping over all layers as in previous works, but can instead emerge more strongly from targeted middle-layer recurrence.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型的最新发展在推理、解决数学、物理和其他科学领域的复杂问题方面表现出了卓越的能力Recent developments in large language models have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in reasoning, solving complex problems in mathematics, physics, and other scientific domains(Wei et al., 2022; Cobbe et al., 2021; OpenAI, 2023)。其中许多任务需要多步骤推理，其中在产生最终答案之前必须迭代地细化中间抽象推理状态。 尽管取得了这些进步，底层的 Transformer 架构. Many of these tasks require multi-step reasoning in which intermediate abstract reasoning states must be iteratively refined before producing a final answer. Despite these advances, the underlying Transformer architecture(Vaswani et al., 2017)本质上仍然以令牌为中心：自回归生成在每个解码步骤中重复将丰富的高维潜在表示投影回令牌空间中的稀疏单热向量，然后将该离散表示用作下一个前向传递的唯一输入。这种重复的投影产生了一个信息瓶颈，限制了潜在的中间推理状态在时间上的持续性并影响未来令牌的计算。remains fundamentally token-centric: auto-regressive generation repeatedly projects rich, high-dimensional latent representations back into a sparse one-hot vector in the token space at each decoding step, and this discrete representation is then used as the sole input for the next forward pass. This repeated projection creates an information bottleneck that limits how latent intermediate reasoning states can persist across time and influence the computation for future tokens.

为了进一步提高变形金刚的推理能力，越来越多的工作To further improve the reasoning capability of Transformers, a growing line of work on潜在推理latent reasoning试图通过使计算能够持续存在于显式令牌级中间体之外的连续潜在空间中来放松这种时间约束。这些方法要么完全在连续空间中执行推理seeks to relax this temporal constraint by enabling computation to persist in continuous latent space beyond explicit token-level intermediates. Such approaches either perform reasoning entirely in continuous space(Hao et al., 2024; Shen et al., 2025)，或者通过形成由 post-softmax 分布引起的标记嵌入的线性组合来传播不确定性, or propagate uncertainty by forming linear combinations of token embeddings induced by post-softmax distributions(Zhang et al., 2025; Zhuang et al., 2025; Yue et al., 2025; Tang et al., 2026)。然而，这些方法通常对最终层或最终层之外的表示进行操作，并通过输入嵌入将循环信号馈送到下一个标记中。结果，循环信息被迫存在于中间层之外，而众所周知，抽象推理在中间层中更为突出. As a result, recurrent information is forced to live outside the middle layers, where abstract reasoning is known to occur more prominently(Tenney et al., 2019; Geva et al., 2021; Meng et al., 2022; Saunshi et al., 2024; Atanas and Liu, 2025).

与此同时，另一项工作试图通过沿深度维度扩展计算来扩展推理能力。这些方法在单个令牌的前向传递过程中多次循环变压器层，而不增加参数数量Concurrently, another line of work has attempted to scale reasoning capacity by extending computation along the depth dimension. 2.1 动机：中间层的影响和信息瓶颈Motivation: Effect of Middle Layers and the Information Bottleneck

最近的机制分析表明，中间层是 Transformer 中抽象推理的主要场所，而早期层专注于词汇和句法处理，而后期层则专门将表示投影到输出词汇上Recent mechanistic analyses reveal that intermediate layers serve as the primary locus of abstract reasoning in Transformers, whereas early layers focus on lexical and syntactic processing and late layers specialize in projecting representations onto the output vocabulary(Tenney et al., 2019; Geva et al., 2021; Meng et al., 2022; Saunshi et al., 2024; Atanas and Liu, 2025)。这表明最有价值的推理相关计算主要发生在中间层，并且这些层之后产生的表示编码了有关模型当前推理状态的丰富信息。. This suggests that the most valuable reasoning-related computation occurs mainly in the middle layers, and that representations produced after these layers encode rich information about the model’s current reasoning state.

然而，标准解码器 Transformer 中的自回归推理没有提供任何机制，可以在当前令牌的前向传递过程中直接利用为前一个令牌计算的中间表示：处理当前令牌的浅层无法直接访问在上一步中计算的更深层表示，即使这些表示已经在内存中可用。为了利用这些信息，模型必须通过再次遍历相同的深度并通过注意力间接检索来重建它，或者依赖于输入嵌入，这是一种高度压缩的表示，在到达更深的层之前必须通过非嵌入-解码-嵌入瓶颈。However, autoregressive inference in a standard decoder-only Transformer provides no mechanism for such intermediate representations computed for the previous token to be directly leveraged during the forward pass of the current token: shallow layers processing the current token cannot directly access deeper-layer representations computed at the previous step, even though these representations are already available in memory. To exploit this information, the model must either reconstruct it by traversing the same depth again and retrieving it indirectly via attention, or rely on the input embedding, a highly compressed representation that must pass through the unembed–decode–embed bottleneck before reaching deeper layers.
