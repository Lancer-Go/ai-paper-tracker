O-RAN 中的高级 AI 服务配置Advanced AI Service Provisioning in O-RAN
通过LLM引擎集成through LLM Engine Integration

Seyed Bagher Hashemi Natanzi,  Pranshav Gajjar, Bo Tang,  and Vijay K. Shah S. B. H. Natanzi and B. Tang are with the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Worcester Polytechnic Institute, Worcester, MA 01609 USA (e-mail: snatanzi@wpi.edu; btang1@wpi.edu).P. Gajjar and V. K. Shah are with the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC 27695 USA.This work was supported by NSF under Awards CNS-2120411 and CNS-2120442.
摘要Abstract

开放无线电接入网络 (O-RAN) 架构允许 AI 通过模块化 xApp 和 rApp 直接嵌入到 RAN 中，但创建这些收集数据、训练模型、编写代码和安全部署它们的应用程序仍然缓慢且主要是手动的。大型语言模型 (LLM) 提供强大的推理和代码生成功能，但不适合实时 RAN 控制所需的快速、确定性推理。我们提出了一种结合了两种优势的概念验证双脑架构：基于 LLM 的编排器将操作员意图转化为数据收集策略和部署代码，而自动化 ML 引擎 NeuralSmith 通过 API 按需训练轻量级分类器。我们描述架构和配置工作流程，分享来自容器化 O-RAN 5G SA 测试床的实用见解，并讨论开放研究方向。The Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN) architecture allows AI to be embedded directly into the RAN through modular xApps and rApps, yet creating these applications collecting data, training models, writing code, and deploying them safely remains slow and largely manual. Large Language Models (LLMs) offer strong reasoning and code-generation capabilities but are unsuited for the fast, deterministic inference required in real-time RAN control. We present a proof-of-concept Dual-Brain architecture that combines both strengths: an LLM-based orchestrator translates operator intents into data-collection policies and deployment code, while an automated ML engine, NeuralSmith, trains lightweight classifiers on demand via an API. We describe the architecture and provisioning workflow, share practical insights from a containerized O-RAN 5G SA testbed, and discuss open research directions.

I 简介Introduction

蜂窝网络已发展成为软件定义的、日益智能的系统，部署和操作的每一层都有望采用人工智能 (AI)Cellular networks have evolved into software-defined, increasingly intelligent systems, with Artificial Intelligence (AI) expected at every layer of deployment and operation[13]。 开放无线接入网络 (O-RAN). The Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN)[12]架构是实现这一愿景的关键推动者：通过分解基站并引入智能控制器，O-RAN 允许将第三方 AI 应用（xApp 和 rApp）直接部署到 RAN 中。 然而，虽然architecture is a key enabler of this vision: by disaggregating the base station and introducing intelligent controllers, O-RAN allows third-party AI applications (xApps and rApps) to be deployed directly into the RAN. However, while部署deployingAI应用变得越来越容易，AI applications is becoming easier,创造creating它们仍然是一个重大瓶颈。配置新的 xApp 涉及网络工程师、数据科学家、软件工程师和部署工程师之间的一系列手动交接，通常需要几天到几周的时间，这对于需要响应快速变化的网络条件的运营商来说太慢了。them remains a significant bottleneck. Provisioning a new xApp involves a chain of manual handoffs across network engineers, data scientists, software engineers, and deployment engineers, often taking days to weeks far too slow for operators needing to respond to rapidly changing network conditions.

大型语言模型（LLM）的快速发展引发了社区的疑问：LLM可以作为O-RAN内部的AI引擎吗？答案是微妙的。 LLM 擅长理解意图、推理系统和生成代码，但它们不适合实时数值推理：它们的延迟违反了近实时 RIC 的 10 毫秒预算，它们的输出是不确定的，并且它们的模型大小使得每个 xApp 部署不切实际。The rapid development of Large Language Models (LLMs) has led the community to ask: can LLMs serve as the AI engine inside O-RAN? The answer is nuanced. LLMs excel at understanding intents, reasoning about systems, and generating code, but they are unsuited for real-time numerical inference: their latency violates the Near-RT RIC’s 10 ms budget, their outputs are non-deterministic, and their model size makes per-xApp deployment impractical.

最近的工作已经开始将法学硕士集成到电信和 O-RAN 的多个层面。早期研究探索了电信领域的生成式人工智能Recent work has begun integrating LLMs into telecommunications and O-RAN at multiple layers. Early studies explored generative AI for telecom[2]，而 NetLLM, while NetLLM[14]使法学硕士适应网络任务，例如视口预测和比特率适应。更接近我们的领域，ORAN-Bench-13Kadapted LLMs to networking tasks such as viewport prediction and bitrate adaptation. Closer to our domain, ORAN-Bench-13K[4]和 ORANSight-2.0and ORANSight-2.0[5]针对 O-RAN 知识进行基准化和微调的 LLM，揭示了通用 LLM 与 RIC 中所需的领域推理之间的差距。在系统层面，Hermesbenchmarked and fine-tuned LLMs for O-RAN knowledge, exposing the gap between general-purpose LLMs and the domain reasoning required in the RIC. At the system level, Hermes[1]提出用于自主网络建模的链式 LLM 代理，LLM-xAppproposed chained LLM agents for autonomous network modeling, LLM-xApp[15]直接在近实时 RIC xApp 中部署了法学硕士，最近的愿景工作主张代理协调器取代 6G AI-RAN 中的狭隘预测人工智能deployed an LLM directly inside a Near-RT RIC xApp, and recent vision work argues for agentic orchestrators replacing narrow predictive AI in 6G AI-RAN[6].

本文的主要观点是法学硕士不应该The key insight of this article is that LLMs should not替换replace经典机器学习，但应该改为classical machine learning but should instead编排orchestrate它。我们提出了一种双脑架构，它将基于 LLM 的零接触编排代理 (ZTO-Agent) 与专用的 ML 引擎 NeuralSmith 配对it. We present a Dual-Brain architecture that pairs an LLM-based Zero-Touch Orchestration Agent (ZTO-Agent) with a dedicated ML engine, NeuralSmith[7]。与之前的工作不同，我们的架构将编排与实时执行解耦：LLM 在非 RT RIC 层处理语义任务（意图解析、代码合成），而专用分类器在近 RT RIC 处理 10 毫秒以下的控制决策。与自由格式代码生成不同，我们的模板约束合成消除了已部署控制逻辑中的幻觉风险。该论文描述了该架构，详细介绍了配置工作流程，分享了来自容器化 O-RAN 5G SA 测试床的实用见解，并讨论了开放研究方向。. Unlike prior work, our architecture decouples orchestration from real-time execution: the LLM handles semantic tasks (intent parsing, code synthesis) at the Non-RT RIC layer, while purpose-built classifiers handle sub-10 ms control decisions at the Near-RT RIC. Unlike free-form code generation, our template-constrained synthesis eliminates hallucination risk in deployed control logic. The paper describes the architecture, details the provisioning workflow, shares practical insights from a containerized O-RAN 5G SA testbed, and discusses open research directions.

Ⅱ-BII-B 供给缺口The Provisioning Gap

WG2 规范定义了概念性 AI/ML 工作流程，但保留了实施的可能性。在实践中，配置新的 xApp 涉及一系列手动交接：网络工程师提取 KPM，数据科学家训练模型，软件工程师编写 xApp，部署工程师将其推出。每次切换都会带来延迟和错误风险。这个分散的过程可能需要数小时到数天的时间，对于需要响应快速变化的网络条件的运营商来说太慢了。The WG2 specification defines a conceptual AI/ML workflow but leaves implementation open. In practice, provisioning a new xApp involves a chain of manual handoffs: a network engineer extracts KPMs, a data scientist trains a model, a software engineer writes the xApp, and a deployment engineer rolls it out. Each handoff introduces delay and risk of error. This fragmented process can take hours to days far too slow for operators needing to respond to rapidly changing network conditions.

    意图翻译：操作员用简单语言提交目标（例如，“Intent Translation: The operator submits a goal in plain language (e.g., “预测小区边缘拥塞并预留物理资源块 (PRB)predict cell-edge congestion and reserve Physical Resource Blocks (PRBs)”）。 ZTO-Agent 生成结构化规范：收集哪些指标、收集的粒度、如何标记数据以及需要哪些模型特征。”). V-A 什么效果好What Worked Well

关注点分离。Separation of concerns.在我们开发的早期，我们尝试使用 LLM 进行编排和推理，促使它根据原始遥测值对拥塞状态进行分类。结果很差：延迟不可接受，相同输入的输出不一致，并且模型偶尔会产生不存在的拥塞状态。将 LLM（思维）与 ML 引擎（计算）分离可以立即解决所有三个问题。Early in our development, we attempted to use the LLM for both orchestration and inference, prompting it to classify congestion states from raw telemetry values. The results were poor: latency was unacceptable, outputs were inconsistent across identical inputs, and the model occasionally hallucinated congestion states that did not exist. Separating the LLM (thinking) from the ML engine (computing) resolved all three issues immediately.

基于模板的代码合成。Template-based code synthesis.通过限制 LLM 填充预先验证的 Jinja2 模板而不是生成任意 xApp 代码，我们消除了基于 LLM 的代码生成的最危险的故障模式：到达实时网络的幻觉控制逻辑。我们的管道生成的每个 xApp 都会继承模板经过测试的正确性。这种方法直接满足了行业对强大的 AI Agent Harness 的需求；通过将 LLM 的输出限制在预定义的参数空间中，我们的架构强制执行结构护栏，确保自主代码生成遵守标准化的信号流和安全协议。By restricting the LLM to populating pre-verified Jinja2 templates rather than generating arbitrary xApp code, we eliminated the most dangerous failure mode of LLM-based code generation: hallucinated control logic reaching the live network. Every xApp produced by our pipeline inherits the template’s tested correctness. This approach directly addresses the industry’s requirement for a robust AI Agent Harness; by confining the LLM’s output to predefined parameter spaces, our architecture enforces a structural guardrail that ensures autonomous code generation adheres to standardized signaling flows and safety protocols.

Without this, the automated pipeline would risk producing accurate but too-slow models.

VI 研究方向Research Directions

双脑架构为未来的研究开辟了多种途径，我们将其分为四个主题，我们认为这些主题对于将 LLM 协调的 O-RAN 配置扩展到生产部署最为重要。The Dual-Brain architecture opens several avenues for future research, which we group into four themes that we believe are most consequential for scaling LLM-orchestrated O-RAN provisioning to production deployments.

从模拟到实际部署。From Simulation to Real Deployments.我们的原型在软件模拟的射频通道上运行，该通道忠实地执行编排接口，但掩盖了真实无线环境的可变性。弥合这一差距提出了两个相互关联的问题：如何通过域随机化和在线自适应等技术使 NeuralSmith 训练的分类器对信道衰落、干扰和硬件损伤具有鲁棒性；以及如何克服目前阻止在一个站点训练的模型推广到另一个站点的元数据碎片。社区将从标准化参考数据集（实际上是无线 MNIST）中受益匪浅，它可以捕获遥测和环境背景，从而实现可重复的模拟到真实的传输以及自主配置的 xApp 的跨供应商可移植性。Our prototype operates over a software-simulated RF channel, which faithfully exercises the orchestration interfaces but masks the variability of real wireless environments. Bridging this gap raises two coupled questions: how to make NeuralSmith-trained classifiers robust to channel fading, interference, and hardware impairments through techniques such as domain randomization and online adaptation; and how to overcome the metadata fragmentation that currently prevents models trained at one site from generalizing to another. The community would benefit substantially from standardized reference datasets, effectively an MNIST for wireless, that capture both telemetry and environmental context, enabling reproducible sim-to-real transfer and cross-vendor portability of autonomously provisioned xApps.

安全、信任和生命周期管理。Safety, Trust, and Lifecycle Management.自主生成的 xApp 会带来安全问题，而传统的手工控制逻辑则不会。三个方向显得尤为紧迫。首先，自主合成的 xApp 在部署前必须经过系统正确性、稳健性和对抗性测试，扩展了之前的 O-RAN AI 测试框架Autonomously generated xApps introduce safety concerns that classical hand-crafted control logic does not. Three directions appear particularly pressing. First, autonomously synthesized xApps must be subjected to systematic correctness, robustness, and adversarial testing before deployment, extending prior O-RAN AI testing frameworks[11]进入法学硕士精心策划的制度。其次，当多个自主配置的xApp共存时，它们的控制行为可能会发生冲突；例如，吞吐量提升型 xApp 与节能型 xApp 竞争同一 PRB 池。部署前冲突检测（可能通过对渲染模板进行静态分析或对共享参数执行保护带）是一项重要的保障措施。第三，运营商和监管机构需要了解每个自动生成的xApp的作用及其原因；整合可解释性报告，这些报告显示了分类器所依赖的特征以及法学硕士在综合过程中做出的决策，这对于生产采用至关重要。into the LLM-orchestrated regime. Second, when multiple autonomously provisioned xApps coexist, their control actions may conflict; for example, a throughput-boosting xApp competing with an energy-saving xApp for the same PRB pool. Pre-deployment conflict detection, possibly through static analysis of the rendered templates or guard-band enforcement on shared parameters, is an important safeguard. Third, operators and regulators need to understand what each automatically generated xApp does and why; integrating explainability reports that surface the features the classifier relied upon and the decisions the LLM made during synthesis is essential for production adoption.

闭环、信心感知编排。Closed-Loop, Confidence-Aware Orchestration.当前的管道是基于触发器的：操作员提交意图，构建并部署xApp，系统等待下一个意图。两个扩展将把它转变为一个持续的学习循环。 ZTO-Agent 可以监控实时 xApp KPI，并在检测到性能漂移时重新触发 NeuralSmith，从而无需操作员干预即可实现自我修复的 AI 服务。与此同时，编排层本身也将受益于校准的不确定性估计。作为 ZTO-Agent 核心的 LLM 可以在不明确的网络条件下产生过度自信的输出，以及基于 LLM 的自主控制的保形预测的最新工作The current pipeline is trigger-based: the operator submits an intent, an xApp is built and deployed, and the system waits for the next intent. Two extensions would transform this into a continuous learning loop. The ZTO-Agent could monitor live xApp KPIs and re-trigger NeuralSmith upon detecting performance drift, enabling self-healing AI services without operator intervention. In parallel, the orchestration layer itself would benefit from calibrated uncertainty estimation. LLMs at the heart of the ZTO-Agent can produce overconfident outputs under ambiguous network conditions, and recent work on conformal prediction for LLM-based autonomous control[3]提供了一种原则性的方法，将可靠性保证附加到意图解析和自动标记决策中。然后，低可信度的案例可以被标记为人工审查，从而缩小完全自治和操作员信任之间的差距。offers a principled way to attach reliability guarantees to intent parsing and auto-labeling decisions. Low-confidence cases could then be flagged for human review, bridging the gap between full autonomy and operator trust.

高效且可移植的 LLM 后端。Efficient and Portable LLM Backends.两个实际问题决定了双脑架构的部署范围。首先是计算成本：虽然 8B 参数 LLM 可以在配备 GPU 的非 RT RIC 基础设施上轻松运行，但较小的蜂窝基站和边缘部署将需要更轻的后端。对电信专用小语言模型、参数高效微调以及针对编排提示的结构化性质进行调整的推测性解码的研究可以大大减少这一障碍。第二个是可移植性：在特定于供应商的 E2 实现中推广 xApp 模板需要对 xApp 打包和 E2SM 配置文件协商进行更深入的标准化，以便无论底层 RIC 平台如何，相同的意图都能可靠地生成可互操作的 xApp。Two practical concerns determine how broadly the Dual-Brain architecture can be deployed. The first is computational cost: while an 8B-parameter LLM runs comfortably on GPU-equipped Non-RT RIC infrastructure, smaller cell sites and edge deployments will need lighter backends. Research on telecom-specialized small language models, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and speculative decoding tuned to the structured nature of orchestration prompts could substantially reduce this barrier. The second is portability: generalizing xApp templates across vendor-specific E2 implementations requires deeper standardization of xApp packaging and E2SM profile negotiation, so that the same intent reliably produces an interoperable xApp regardless of the underlying RIC platform.

七VII结论Conclusion

在 O-RAN 中创建和部署人工智能服务的过程绝大多数仍然是手动的。本文提出了一种双脑架构，它将 LLM 的编排功能与专用 ML 引擎的数值精度相结合，以实现整个配置生命周期的自动化。基于法学硕士的编排器处理意图翻译、数据管理和代码合成；而机器学习引擎 NeuralSmith 通过简单的 API 训练轻量级的确定性分类器。我们在容器化 O-RAN 测试台上构建系统的经验表明，该方法是实用的，可实现 384 毫秒的稳态编排延迟，同时满足近 RT RIC 的 10 毫秒以下推理约束。虽然我们的原型在单个下游任务上演示了端到端的管道，但无线部署和跨其他用例的验证仍然是未来的重要工作。该架构与 LLM 无关，保留了数据主权，并提供了一条在 6G 网络中实现自动化 AI 服务交付的可重复路径。The process of creating and deploying AI services in O-RAN remains overwhelmingly manual. This article has presented a Dual-Brain architecture that combines the orchestration capabilities of LLMs with the numerical precision of dedicated ML engines to automate the entire provisioning lifecycle. The LLM-based orchestrator handles intent translation, data curation, and code synthesis; while the ML engine, NeuralSmith, trains lightweight, deterministic classifiers via a simple API. Our experience building the system on a containerized O-RAN testbed shows that the approach is practical, achieving 384 ms steady-state orchestration latency while meeting the Near-RT RIC’s sub-10 ms inference constraint. While our prototype demonstrates the pipeline end-to-end on a single downstream task, validation in over-the-air deployments and across additional use cases remains important future work. The architecture is LLM-agnostic, preserves data sovereignty, and offers a reproducible path toward automated AI service delivery in 6G networks.
