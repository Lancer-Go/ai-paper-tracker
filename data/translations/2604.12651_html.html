摘要Abstract

知识图嵌入（KGE）模型在链接预测方面表现良好，但在处理看不见的实体、关系，尤其是文字方面表现不佳，限制了它们在动态异构图中的使用。相比之下，预训练的大型语言模型 (LLM) 通过提示可以有效地进行泛化。我们将链接预测重新表述为即时学习问题并引入Knowledge graph embedding (KGE) models perform well on link prediction but struggle with unseen entities, relations, and especially literals, limiting their use in dynamic, heterogeneous graphs. In contrast, pretrained large language models (LLMs) generalize effectively through prompting. We reformulate link prediction as a prompt learning problem and introduceRALP，它学习基于字符串的思想链 (CoT) 提示作为三元组的评分函数。通过 MIPRO 算法使用贝叶斯优化，, which learns string-based chain-of-thought (CoT) prompts as scoring functions for triples. Using Bayesian Optimization through MIPRO algorithm,RALP在没有梯度访问的情况下，从少于 30 个训练示例中识别有效提示。 据推断，identifies effective prompts from fewer than 30 training examples without gradient access. At inference,RALP预测缺失的实体、关系或整个三元组，并根据学习到的提示分配置信度分数。我们评估转导式、数值式和 OWL 实例检索基准。predicts missing entities, relations or whole triples and assigns confidence scores based on the learned prompt. 6 局限性Limitations

当提供给法学硕士的上下文信息包含极端异常值时，我们在数值链接预测设置中观察到的一个限制就会出现。在某些情况下，这些异常值与其他数值的分布存在显着偏差，可能会对模型的预测质量产生负面影响。例如，在 LitWD1K 数据集中，委内瑞拉的通货膨胀率为 16,988.4，比其他国家的平均值高出一个数量级。当输入上下文中包含此类异常值时，它们可能会扭曲区间预测的推断值范围。然而，这个问题可以通过简单的预处理技术来缓解，例如过滤或降低上下文中统计异常值的权重。One limitation we observed in the numerical link prediction setting arises when the contextual information provided to the LLM includes extreme outliers. In some cases, these outlier values deviate significantly from the distribution of other numerical values and can negatively impact the model’s prediction quality. For instance, in the LitWD1K dataset, Venezuela’s inflation rate is recorded as 16,988.4—an order of magnitude higher than the mean for other countries. When such outliers are included in the input context, they can distort the inferred value range for the interval prediction. This issue, however, can be mitigated through simple pre-processing techniques, such as filtering or down-weighting statistical outliers in the context.

另一个实际限制是输入上下文必须与人类可读的标签或描述保持一致，特别是当使用标识符（例如 QID 或 URI）引用实体或关系时。由于法学硕士主要接受自然语言培训，因此未能将这些标识符映射到描述性文本会降低他们对输入进行有效推理的能力。Another practical constraint is that input context must be aligned with human-readable labels or descriptions, especially when entities or relations are referenced using identifiers (e.g., QIDs or URIs). Since LLMs are primarily trained on natural language, failing to map these identifiers to descriptive text reduces their ability to reason effectively over the input.

最后，我们的方法依赖于大型预训练语言模型，这可能在推理时间和计算资源方面对可扩展性提出挑战。在处理大型图时，表示用于推理的上下文的令牌限制成为一个问题。然而，解决这个问题的方法是设计一个采样算法，选择最相关的三元组进入上下文。虽然我们的方法能够实现强大的零样本性能和泛化，但未来的工作可以探索将推理能力提炼或压缩为更轻量级模型的方法，以实现更广泛的部署。Lastly, our method relies on large pre-trained language models, which can present challenges for scalability in terms of inference time and computational resources. Token limitation into representing the context used for inference becomes an issue when dealing with large graphs. However, a solution to that would be to craft a sampling algorithm that selects the most relevant triples to go into the context. While our approach enables strong zero-shot performance and generalization, future work could explore ways to distill or compress the reasoning capabilities into lighter-weight models for broader deployment.
