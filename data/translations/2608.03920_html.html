等变音乐变压器Equivariant Music Transformer

摘要Abstract

即使音乐段落在时间上发生了变化或音高发生了变化，人类也能识别出它，这表明了表示空间中的等变性概念。然而，我们的分析表明，标准音乐变换器将这种时移或音调转换的输入映射到不相关的表示上：这些模型随着规模的扩大或训练时间的延长而变得越来越不等变。 这表明在标准音乐转换器中，额外的模型容量被分配用于记忆绝对模式而不是捕获共享的音乐结构。 在本文中，我们提出了等变音乐变换器（EMT），它通过联合优化下一个标记预测和辅助等方差正则化损失，通过自蒸馏来强制等方差。 我们发现额外的等方差损失充当有益的正则化器，同时改进下一个标记预测并产生等变潜在表示。 通过客观和主观评估，与数据增强、特征工程和最先进的 (SOTA) 基线相比，EMT 表现出了卓越的等方差性和生成能力。 更广泛地说，我们的研究结果表明，仅标准语言建模方法并不能捕获音乐的平移对称性，并且需要专门的归纳偏差来产生更好的音乐表示。代码、权重和演示可在线获取Humans recognize a musical passage even when it is shifted in time or transposed in pitch, indicating a notion of equivariance in the representation space. Our analysis, however, shows that standard music transformers map such time-shifted or pitch-transposed inputs onto uncorrelated representations: these models become progressively less equivariant as they scale in size or train longer. This suggests that in standard music transformers, additional model capacity is allocated to memorizing absolute patterns rather than capturing shared musical structures. In this paper, we propose the Equivariant Music Transformer (EMT), which enforces equivariance through self-distillation by jointly optimizing a next-token-prediction and an auxiliary equivariance regularization loss. We find that the additional equivariance loss acts as a beneficial regularizer, simultaneously improving next-token prediction and producing equivariant latent representations. Through both objective and subjective evaluations, EMT demonstrates superior equivariance and generative capability compared to data augmentation, feature engineering, and state-of-the-art (SOTA) baselines. More broadly, our findings reveal that standard language modeling methods alone do not capture music’s translational symmetries, and dedicated inductive biases are required to produce better music representations. 1 简介Introduction

人类可以毫不费力地识别音乐段落，无论它是否被移调到不同的调或在时间上移动。这是因为即使绝对音高或起始点发生变化，感知的音程也保持不变。在计算方面，这对应于等方差的概念Humans effortlessly recognize a musical passage regardless of whether it is transposed to a different key or shifted in time. This is because the perceived intervals remain unchanged even as the absolute pitches or onsets change. In computational terms, this corresponds to the notion of equivariance[1, 2]：输入的变换会产生可预测的、相应的输出变换。事实证明，结合等变归纳偏置在一系列 MIR 任务中是有效的: a transformation of the input produces a predictable, corresponding transformation of the output. Incorporating the equivariant inductive bias has proven effective across a range of MIR tasks[3, 4, 5, 6].

在符号音乐领域，现有方法要么通过输入级特征工程合并这种归纳偏差，例如，使用相对属性对符号音乐进行编码In the area of symbolic music, existing approaches either incorporate this inductive bias via input-level feature engineering, for instance, encoding symbolic music using relative attributes[7, 8, 9, 10, 11], 潜在空间修改, latent space modification[11, 7, 9, 12]或数据增强or data augmentation[13, 14]。然而，这些方法都没有在模型级别强制执行等变属性。. Yet, none of these approaches enforces the equivariant property at the model level.

在本文中，我们提出了等变音乐变压器（EMT），它通过辅助等方差正则化损失直接在生成变压器架构中强制执行等变，并与 标准的下一个令牌预测目标。我们假设修改模型的损失景观是比输入级特征工程更强的正则化信号，并且比数据增强更通用。In this paper, we propose the Equivariant Music Transformer (EMT), which directly enforces equivariance in a generative transformer architecture via an auxiliary equivariance regularization loss, jointly optimized with the standard next-token prediction objective. We hypothesize that modifying the model’s loss landscape is a stronger regularization signal than input-level feature engineering, and more generalizable than data augmentation.

据我们所知，这是第一个将等方差正则化损失纳入音乐生成变压器架构的工作。因此，我们的目标是回答以下研究问题： 1.辅助等方差损失是否充当下一个标记预测的有益正则化器，或者是否引入了降低生成性能的竞争梯度？ 2. EMT 和标准变压器的等方差在整个训练过程中如何演变？模型级正则化与数据增强和特征级等方差正则化相比如何？ 3. 最后，随着基于 Transformer 的符号音乐模型变得更大或训练时间更长，是否会出现等方差？To our best knowledge, this is the first work that incorporates an equivariance regularization loss to the generative transformer architecture for music. We hence aim to answer the following research questions: 1. Does the auxiliary equivariance loss act as a beneficial regularizer for next-token prediction, or does it introduce competing gradients that degrade generative performance? 2. How does the equivariance for both EMT and standard transformers evolve throughout training? And how does model-level regularization compare against data augmentation and feature-level equivariance regularization? 3. Finally, does equivariance emerge as transformer-based symbolic music models grow larger or train longer?

为了回答上述问题，我们测量了预期音乐变压器的等方差性To answer the above questions, we measured the equivariance of the Anticipatory Music Transformer[15]，一个 SOTA 符号音乐模型，以及我们自己的消融模型，仅使用下一个令牌预测损失进行训练。我们发现了一个显着的趋势：随着模型变得越来越大并且训练时间越来越长，它们的等变性逐渐降低。这表明标准变换器越来越多地分配表征和生成能力来记住绝对模式，将音乐等效输入（例如同一音乐段落的开始移位或音调转换版本）映射到潜在空间中遥远的、不相关的表征上。我们提出的 EMT 通过限制潜在空间将音乐转移的输入映射到相应转移的表示上来直接解决这个问题，确保模型的内部表示反映潜在的音乐结构。, a SOTA symbolic music model, and our own ablation model trained with the next-token-prediction loss only.
