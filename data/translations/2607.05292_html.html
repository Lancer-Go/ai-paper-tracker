1 简介Introduction

气候变化正在重塑大气状况，通过气象、大气化学和人类活动之间复杂的相互作用直接影响空气质量。随着气候条件的变化，温度、环流模式和极端事件频率的变化会影响空气污染物的形成、传输和扩散，给空气质量管理和预测带来新的挑战。空气质量仍然是一个主要的环境和公共卫生问题，影响着全世界数十亿人，并严重加重了呼吸系统和心血管疾病的负担Climate change is reshaping atmospheric conditions in ways that directly influence air quality through complex interactions between meteorology, atmospheric chemistry, and human activities. As climatic conditions evolve, changes in temperature, circulation patterns, and the frequency of extreme events can affect the formation, transport, and dispersion of air pollutants, creating new challenges for air-quality management and forecasting. Air quality remains a major environmental and public health concern, affecting billions of people worldwide and contributing significantly to respiratory and cardiovascular disease burdens[17]。因此，准确及时的预测对于支持公共卫生干预、为环境政策提供信息以及增强我们对地球系统过程的理解至关重要。 在这种背景下，基于机器学习的预测系统为改进污染事件的预测和为公共卫生决策提供信息提供了一条有前途的途径。. Accurate and timely predictions are therefore essential for supporting public health interventions, informing environmental policy, and enhancing our understanding of Earth system processes. In this context, machine-learning-based forecasting systems offer a promising pathway for improving the prediction of pollution episodes and informing public health decision-making.

用于科学预测和决策的机器学习（ML）系统的快速发展开创了环境预测的新时代The rapid development of machine learning (ML) systems for scientific prediction and decision-making has ushered in a new era for environmental forecasting[45, 60, 37]。在气象学领域，这种进步在基于人工智能的先进天气预报系统的出现中尤为明显，该系统在预测动态大气变量方面取得了显着的性能. In meteorology, this progress has been particularly evident in the emergence of advanced AI-based weather forecasting systems, which have achieved remarkable performance in predicting dynamical atmospheric variables[35, 34, 53, 28, 13, 24, 52, 26, 4, 7, 29, 6, 20]。然而，将这些成功从天气预报扩展到大气成分预测仍然具有挑战性。与传统天气预报不同，大气成分预测不仅必须考虑气象传输，还必须考虑排放、化学转化、沉积和人类活动，这使得大气成分预测变得更加复杂且计算要求更高. However, extending these successes from weather prediction to atmospheric-composition forecasting remains challenging. Unlike conventional weather forecasting, atmospheric-composition prediction must account not only for meteorological transport but also for emissions, chemical transformations, deposition, and human activities, making it substantially more complex and computationally demanding[5]。这一挑战对于直接影响人类健康和生态系统的空气污染尤为重要. This challenge is especially important for air pollution, which directly affects human health and ecosystems[38, 11].

在数据方面，空气质量监测和预报依赖两个具有互补特征的主要数据源。一方面，哥白尼大气监测服务（CAMS）等预报和分析数据提供了大气成分的空间完整且一致的估计，特别是在观测数据覆盖率较低或无法直接观测的大气污染物的情况下On the data side, air quality monitoring and forecasting rely on two main data sources with complementary characteristics. In contrast, the station measurements are spatially sparse and unevenly distributed, with higher densities in particular areas and limited coverage in rural or remote areas. 因此，关键的挑战在于连接这两个数据源，并将 CAMS 的空间完整性与地面站的精度相结合，以生成高分辨率、偏差校正的空气质量估计值。编码物理和环境特征的辅助变量可以在此过程中提供附加信息。结合高分辨率辅助信息使模型能够学习基于物理的关系，并将来自站点的点观测传播到粗 CAMS 网格中，从而提高降尺度性能。The key challenge, therefore, lies in bridging these two data sources and combining the spatial completeness of CAMS with the precision of ground stations to produce high-resolution, bias-corrected air-quality estimates. Auxiliary variables that encode physical and environmental characteristics can provide additional information in this process. Incorporating high-resolution side information enables the model to learn physically-grounded relationships, and to propagate point observations from stations into the coarse CAMS grids, thereby improving downscaling performance.

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 STARQ，一种用于偏差校正 PM 的站点引导降尺度框架We introduce STARQ, a station-guided downscaling framework for bias-corrected PM2.5对欧洲的估计。所提出的方法将 CAMS 的空间完整性与 OpenAQ 观测的点级精度相结合，同时结合了描述人类活动、土地覆盖、地形、气溶胶负荷和气象运输的辅助变量。通过使用高斯核插值和伪标签传播，该框架将稀疏站测量转换为密集监督，并在任意时间戳处实现与时间无关的空间校正。estimation over Europe. The proposed method combines the spatial completeness of CAMS with the point-level accuracy of OpenAQ observations, while incorporating auxiliary variables that describe human activity, land cover, topography, aerosol loading, and meteorological transport. By using Gaussian-kernel interpolation and pseudo-label propagation, the framework converts sparse station measurements into dense supervision and enables time-agnostic spatial correction at arbitrary timestamps.

仍然存在一些限制。监督质量取决于 OpenAQ 站测量的覆盖范围和可靠性，这些测量在空间上不均匀并且可能包含异常值。此外，当前的伪标签设计依赖于基于固定核的传播，这可能无法完全捕获不同气象条件下复杂的污染物传输。未来的工作将探索自适应和不确定性感知内核、更强的台站观测质量控制策略，以及对其他污染物、区域和极端污染事件的更广泛评估。Several limitations remain.
