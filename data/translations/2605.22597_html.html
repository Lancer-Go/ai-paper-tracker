MoSA：运动约束应力适应，通过学习残余各向异性来缓解连续动力学中的实模差距MoSA: Motion-constrained Stress Adaptation for Mitigating Real-to-Sim Gap in Continuum Dynamics via Learning Residual Anisotropy

Jiaxu Wang†    Junhao He    Jingkai Sun    Yi Gu    Yunyang Mo    Jiahang Cao    Qiang Zhang    Renjing Xu†
摘要Abstract

从视觉观察中学习现实世界的动态对于各个领域都至关重要。一种常见的策略是通过估计物理参数来校准模拟器，但准确性最终受到底层物理模型的限制，该模型通常假设材料是均匀且各向同性的。即使合理，现实世界的物体通常也会表现出轻微的各向异性和异质性。在近各向同性主干经过良好校准后，这些残余效应成为进一步缩小真实与模拟差距的关键瓶颈。尽管神经网络可以端到端地拟合动力学，但这种黑盒建模丢弃了强大的物理先验，导致数据效率低下和过度拟合。 因此，我们提出了 MoSA，一种运动约束应力适应框架，针对这些残余效应，进一步改进真实到模拟的动态学习。 MoSA 使用各向同性模型作为物理先验，并学习残余应力算子来捕获轻微的各向异性和异质性。它通过物理信息级联网络中的微平面约束重新分布逐步适应应力。我们通过监督变形场的时间和空间导数进一步施加运动约束。通过实验，我们学习到的动力学实现了卓越的准确性、泛化性和鲁棒性，同时学习了物理上有意义的残余各向异性。最后，我们在机器人操作设置中验证了 MoSA，表明更好的真实到模拟动力学建模可以转化为更可靠的模拟到真实转换。项目页面位于Learning real-world dynamics from visual observations is crucial for various domains. A common strategy is to calibrate simulators by estimating physical parameters, yet accuracy is ultimately bounded by the underlying physical models, which often assume materials are homogeneous and isotropic. Even if reasonable, real-world objects typically exhibit mild anisotropy and heterogeneity. After the near-isotropic backbone is well calibrated, these residual effects become the key bottleneck for further closing the real-to-sim gap. Although neural networks can fit dynamics end-to-end, such black-box modeling discards strong physical priors, leading to poor data efficiency and overfitting. Therefore, we propose MoSA, a motion-constrained stress adaptation framework that targets these residual effects to further improve real-to-sim dynamics learning. MoSA uses an isotropic model as a physics prior and learns residual stress operators to capture mild anisotropy and heterogeneity. It progressively adapts stresses via microplane-constrained redistribution in a physics-informed cascaded network. We further impose motion constraints by supervising temporal and spatial derivatives of the deformation field. Experimentally, our learned dynamics achieves superior accuracy, generalization, and robustness, while learning physically meaningful residual anisotropy. Finally, we validate MoSA in a robot manipulation setting, showing that better real-to-sim dynamics modeling translates into more reliable sim-to-real transfer. 1 简介Introduction

模拟不同材料的真实动力学对于图形、机器人和实体代理中的许多应用至关重要，也是构建现实世界的交互式数字孪生的关键要素。视觉观察广泛可用，可以自然地捕获丰富的变形和运动信号。因此，从视觉数据中学习现实世界的动态已成为一个活跃的研究方向。Simulating realistic dynamics of diverse materials is essential for many applications in graphics, robotics, and embodied agents, and it is a key ingredient for building interactive digital twins of the real world. Visual observations are widely available and naturally capture rich deformation and motion signals. As a result, learning real-world dynamics from visual data has become an active research direction.

流行的范例是用经典物理模型来表示动力学，并通过估计一小组物理参数来识别对象A prevailing paradigm is to represent dynamics with classical physical models and identify objects by estimating a small set of physical parameters(Cai et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023)。在实践中，大多数管道采用均质和各向同性材料模型，因为它们为许多日常物体提供了简单且通常有效的近似，并实现了稳定的可微分模拟。然而，这种近似很少是精确的：即使一个对象可以合理地视为均匀和各向同性，它通常在现实世界中表现出小但系统的各向异性和异质性. In practice, most pipelines adopt homogeneous and isotropic material models, since they provide a simple and often effective approximation for many everyday objects and enable stable differentiable simulation. However, this approximation is rarely exact: even when an object can be reasonably treated as homogeneous and isotropic, it typically exhibits small but systematic anisotropy and heterogeneity in the real world(Li et al., 2025; Fu et al., 2025)。这些偏差，我们称之为连续体中的残余效应，可能是由内部结构、逐渐磨损等引起的。因此，估计各向同性模型内的参数可以捕获主要骨干，但它通常无法解释这些残余效应，这成为进一步缩小真实与模拟差距的关键瓶颈。 另一种方法是使用神经网络端到端地学习动力学. These deviations, which we call residual effects in continuum, can arise from internal structure, gradual wear, etc. As a result, estimating parameters within an isotropic model can capture the dominant backbone, but it often fails to account for these residual effects, which become a key bottleneck for further closing the real-to-sim gap. An alternative is to learn dynamics end-to-end with neural networks(Mittal et al., 2025; Cao et al., 2025; Zhobro et al., 2025)。虽然这可以对缺失的物理现象进行建模，但直接拟合完整的动力学会丢弃强大的物理先验，并且不会将主要的各向同性主干与弱残余效应分开。网络必须重新学习主干网，同时捕获细微的残差，这往往会导致数据效率差、训练不稳定和过度拟合。. While this can model missing physics, directly fitting full dynamics discards strong physical priors and does not disentangle the dominant isotropic backbone from weak residual effects. The network must re-learn the backbone and capture subtle residuals at the same time, which often leads to poor data efficiency, unstable training, and overfitting.

受这一瓶颈的推动，我们采取了有针对性的建模方法：我们保持校准的近各向同性主干来解释主导行为，并仅学习由轻度各向异性和异质性产生的残余效应。具体来说，我们将各向同性本构定律视为物理先验，并引入结构化残余应力适应算子，逐步纠正先验应力响应并鼓励物理上一致的应力重新分布。这种设计保留了强大的物理归纳偏差，同时将学习能力分配给缺失的剩余动态。Motivated by this bottleneck, we take a targeted modeling approach: we keep a calibrated near-isotropic backbone to explain the dominant behavior, and learn only the residual effects that arise from mild anisotropy and heterogeneity. Concretely, we treat an isotropic constitutive law as a physics prior and introduce a structured residual stress adaptation operator that progressively corrects the prior stress response and encourage physically consistent stress redistribution. This design preserves strong physical inductive bias while allocating learning capacity to the missing residual dynamics.

从视觉中学习动态的另一个挑战是监督通常是间接的且缺乏约束。早期的尝试假设可以访问完整的 3D 几何轨迹Another challenge in learning dynamics from vision is that supervision is often indirect and underconstrained. Earlier attempts assume access to full 3D geometry trajectories(Ma et al., 2023; Jaques et al., 2019)，这在现实世界中很难获得。更新的方法, which is hard to obtain in real-world settings. More recent methods(Chen et al., 2025a)通过可微分模拟将视频与 3D 模拟连接起来connect videos to 3D simulation through differentiable simulation(Bolliger et al., 2025)和神经渲染and neural rendering(Kerbl et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024b)。然而，仅由图像重建驱动的优化仍然是病态的：不同的 3D 变形轨迹和材料响应可以产生相似的图像外观。. However, optimization driven only by image reconstruction remains ill-conditioned: different 3D deformation trajectories and material responses can produce similar image appearances.

为了缓解这种情况，我们添加了运动感知约束，比单独的像素重建提供更直接的监督。我们首先从输入视频中获得动态 3D 重建，这提供了有关对象如何随时间移动和变形的可靠线索。然后，我们使用这些线索来监督变形场的时间和空间导数。与我们的目标残余应力适应算子一起，产生了我们的框架 MoSA。To mitigate this, we add motion-aware constraints that provide more direct supervision than pixel reconstruction alone. We first obtain a dynamic 3D reconstruction from the input videos, which provides reliable cues about how the object moves and deforms over time. We then use these cues to supervise the temporal and spatial derivatives of the deformation field. Together with our targeted residual stress adaptation operator, this yields our framework, MoSA.

各向异性建模问题分析Problem Analysis about Anisotropic Modeling。 经典本构模型通常假设材料是均匀且各向同性的。在这些假设下，机械行为只需几个参数（例如杨氏模量和泊松比）即可描述，这大大简化了建模并实现了稳定的校准。然而，这种近似在现实世界中很少是准确的。即使一个物体在粗略水平上被合理地视为近各向同性，它通常也会由于内部结构、制造或磨损而表现出轻微的各向异性和空间异质性。在校准近各向同性的主干网之后，这些残余效应可以主导剩余的真实到模拟误差。 其中，各向异性通常比异质性更难表达和识别，因为它取决于方向响应而不仅仅是空间变化。因此，我们的方法保留了各向同性本构先验作为骨干，并学习两种效应的结构化残差校正，特别关注残差各向异性作为更具挑战性的组成部分。. Classic constitutive models often assume materials are homogeneous and isotropic. Under these assumptions, mechanical behavior can be described by only a few parameters (e.g., Young’s modulus and Poisson’s ratio), which greatly simplifies modeling and enables stable calibration. However, this approximation is rarely exact in the real world. Even when an object is reasonably treated as near-isotropic at a coarse level, it typically exhibits mild anisotropy and spatial heterogeneity due to internal structure, fabrication, or wear. After the near-isotropic backbone is calibrated, these residual effects can dominate the remaining real-to-sim error. Among them, anisotropy is generally harder to express and identify than heterogeneity, since it depends on directional responses rather than only spatial variation. Our method therefore preserves an isotropic constitutive prior as the backbone and learns structured residual corrections for both effects, with particular focus on residual anisotropy as the more challenging component.

5 结论Conclusion

总之，我们的结果表明，进一步缩小日常可变形物体的真实与模拟差距，与其说是重新学习占主导地位的各向同性骨干模型，不如说是捕捉由标准模拟器忽略的各向异性和异质性引起的温和但系统的连续残余效应。通过明确限制学习残余应力校正并加强运动提示的监督，我们的方法在动态指标和下游机器人操作方面始终实现更好的稳定性和转移。我们希望这项工作能够鼓励未来的动态学习方法将强大的物理骨干与有针对性的残差建模相结合，以实现可扩展的现实世界部署。In conclusion, our results indicate that further reducing the real-to-sim gap for everyday deformable objects is less about re-learning the dominant isotropic backbone model, and more about capturing the mild but systematic continuum residual effects caused by the anisotropy and heterogeneity that standard simulators ignore. By explicitly constraining learning to residual stress corrections and strengthening supervision with motion cues, our approach achieves consistently better stability and transfer in both dynamics metrics and downstream robot manipulation. We hope this work encourages future dynamics learning methods to combine strong physical backbones with targeted residual modeling for scalable real-world deployment.
