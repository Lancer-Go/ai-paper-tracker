摘要Abstract

复杂场景中对精确、可靠控制的需求不断增长，导致控制器的开发日益复杂，包括采用封闭箱模型的数据驱动方法和数学上严格但复杂的设计。这种复杂性凸显了对可解释控制的需求，这种控制可以为控制器行为提供人类可理解的见解。在本文中，提出了可解释的控制框架（XCF）以及支持算法和用户界面，以解释控制器如何确定其控制动作及其底层工作机制。这项工作的新颖贡献有三个：首先，XCF 旨在为闭环系统中的控制器提供与模型无关的解释，并且可以选择性地通过系统响应动态来完善局部解释。其次，基于所设计的框架，提出了一种新颖的解释方法，即控制系统的分层模糊模型不可知解释（HFMAE-C）。 HFMAE-C 采用模糊逻辑系统来近似控制器的行为和系统动态，通过 IF-THEN 规则提供样本、局部、域和宇宙级别的解释，揭示控制器的决策逻辑和显着值，量化系统状态对控制动作的贡献。第三，开发了由代理支持的大语言模型用户界面，以自动分析用户需求，选择合适的算法，将生成的解释解释为自然语言报告，并提供交互式咨询。Increasing demand for precise and reliable control in complex scenarios has led to the development of increasingly sophisticated controllers, including data-driven approaches employing closed box models and mathematically rigorous yet complex designs. This complexity highlights the needs for explainable control that can provide human-understandable insights into controller behavior. In this paper, an explainable control framework (XCF) along with supporting algorithms and user interface are proposed to explain how controllers determine their control actions and their underlying working mechanism. The novel contributions of this work are threefold: First, the XCF is designed to provide model-agnostic explanations for controllers in closed-loop systems and can optionally refine local explanations by system response dynamics. Second, a novel explanation method, hierarchical fuzzy model-agnostic explanation for control systems (HFMAE-C), is proposed based on the designed framework. The HFMAE-C employs a fuzzy logic system to approximate the controller’s behavior and system dynamics, providing sample, local, domain and universe level explanations via IF-THEN rules revealing the controller’s decision logic and salience values quantifying the contribution of system states to control actions. Third, a large language model agent-supported user interface is developed to automatically analyze user requirements, select appropriate algorithms, interpret the generated explanations to a natural language report, and provide interactive consultation.倒立摆系统和Turtlebot避障的案例研究通过模拟用户实验以及与主流可解释控制方法的定量比较证明了该方法的有效性。Case studies on inverted pendulum system and Turtlebot obstacle avoidance demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method through simulated user experiments and quantitative comparisons with mainstream explainable control approaches.

From traditional model-based control to data-driven approaches, the complexity of controllers grows significantly, making their internal decision-making process more difficult to interpret. 这种可解释性的挑战反映了人工智能 (AI) 领域面临的类似问题，其中强大但不透明的封闭盒模型引起了人们对信任和可靠性的担忧。为了解释模型决策，人工智能社区开发了事后可解释人工智能（XAI）This challenge of explainability mirrors a similar problem faced in the artificial intelligence (AI) field, where powerful but opaque closed box models raise concerns about trust and reliability. To explain model decisions, the AI community has developed post-hoc explainable AI (XAI)[19]，模型训练后生成解释的方法和工具的集合。其中，与模型无关的方法被广泛使用，因为它们仅根据模型输入和输出生成解释，而不需要访问模型的内部结构。代表性方法包括局部可解释的模型不可知解释（LIME）, a collection of methods and tools to generate explanations after model training. Among these, model-agnostic methods are widely used, as they generate explanations solely based on model inputs and outputs without requiring access to the model’s internal structure. Despite clear differences between these methods, they all aim to return (approximately) minimal conditions sufficient to alter predictions in some prespecified way[27, 28]。 结果解释应该准确、简单并且与询问代理相关. These techniques have been widely applied to domains such as computer vision and natural language processing, and hold potential for improving explainability in control systems.

尽管在可解释控制方面取得了令人鼓舞的进展，但现有方法仍然面临着限制其更广泛适用性的两个关键挑战：普遍性和可访问性。通用性是指一种方法能够泛化不同类型的控制系统和应用场景的能力，而不是针对特定领域或控制器架构进行定制。许多现有方法与特定系统结构紧密耦合，因此很难在其他控制环境中转移或重用它们。可访问性涉及用户（尤其是在控制理论或 XAI 方面没有深厚专业知识的用户）应用该方法并解释生成的解释的难易程度Despite the encouraging progress made in explainable control, existing methods still face two key challenges that limit their broader applicability: universality and accessibility. 三、III控制系统的分层模糊模型不可知解释 (HFMAE-C)Hierarchical Fuzzy Model-Agnostic Explanation for Control systems (HFMAE-C)

HFMAE-C 被提出作为一种解释算法，普遍能够解释闭环系统中给定控制器的行为。本节介绍分层解释结构，它将解释组织为样本、局部、领域和宇宙级别；相应的与模型无关的解释算法，为每个级别训练解释器；以及经过训练的解释器的解释界面，它提供系统状态的显着值和揭示控制器决策逻辑的 IF-THEN 规则。HFMAE-C is proposed as an explanation algorithm universally capable of interpreting the behavior of a given controller in a closed-loop system. This section introduces the hierarchical explanation structure, which organizes explanations into sample, local, domain, and universe levels; the corresponding model-agnostic explanation algorithms, which train explainers for each level; and the explanation interface of the trained explainer, which provides salience values for system states and IF-THEN rules revealing the controller’s decision logic.

Ⅲ-AIII-A 分层解释结构Hierarchical Explanation Structure

HFMAE-C 将控制器行为的解释组织成由四个级别组成的结构化层次结构：样本、本地、域和宇宙。每个级别对应于对控制器行为的不同分析规模。宇宙包含所有域，一个域包含多个局部，一个局部包含无数个样本。示例解释捕获了特定系统状态下的控制器决策。局部解释总结了给定系统状态实例周围的小邻域内的控制器行为。域解释概括了状态空间的更大区域，在该区域中观察到类似的行为，通常通过聚合多个局部解释来形成。宇宙解释提供了整个状态空间中控制器决策逻辑的全局摘要。HFMAE-C organizes the explanations of controller behavior into a structured hierarchy consisting of four levels: sample, local, domain, and universe. Each level corresponds to a different scale of analysis over the controller behavior. The universe includes all domains, a domain includes multiple localities, and a locality includes numerous samples. The sample explanation captures the controller decision at a specific system state. The local explanation summarizes the controller behavior within a small neighborhood around a given system state instance. The domain explanation generalizes over a larger region of the state space where similar behaviors are observed, typically formed by aggregating several local explanations. The universe explanation provides a global summary of the controller decision logic across the entire state space.

如 FMAE 的现有工作所示As shown in the existing work on FMAE[30]，这种层次结构既允许自下而上的聚合，也允许自上而下的解释简化，用户可以根据自己的需求在任何级别开始分析。在以下部分中，我们将描述如何在实际实现中构建不同级别的解释器：直接从全局数据训练通用解释器；当地的解释员针对特定的州实例进行了培训和完善；领域解释器是通过聚合多个本地解释器获得的。, such hierarchical structures allow both bottom-up aggregation and top-down simplification of explanations, and users can begin analysis at any level based on their needs. In the following sections, we describe how the explainers for different levels are constructed in a practical implementation: a univers explainer is trained directly from global data; local explainers are trained and refined for given state instances; and domain explainers are obtained by aggregating multiple local explainers.

HFMAE-C的解释界面提供了显着性值和IF-THEN规则。这两种形式对于人类用户来说都是可以理解的，但是它们紧凑且高度结构化的表示使得它们不太直观，特别是当涉及大量状态或规则时。为了使这些更易于使用，并利用以可以由法学硕士直接解释的自然语言形式表达的 IF-THEN 规则的独特优势，我们构建了一个由多个基于法学硕士的代理支持的用户界面。通过这个界面，用户可以用自然语言与系统进行交流，描述他们需要的解释范围，并收到以易于理解的形式编写的解释报告。可选组件还允许用户在需要时提出后续问题并获得系统诊断信息。The explanation interface of HFMAE-C provided the salience values and the IF-THEN rules. VI 结论与讨论Conclusion and Discussion

本文是最近会议出版物的延伸This paper is an extension of a recent conference publication[32]。我们提出了 XCF，它将模型无关的 XAI 技术系统地扩展到闭环控制系统，旨在解决可解释控制中普遍性和可访问性的挑战。通过将控制器视为一个封闭的盒子，XCF 可以对控制行为进行分层解释，而无需访问内部结构。作为该框架的具体实例，开发了 HFMAE-C，使用 FLS 来近似控制器行为，并通过 IF-THEN 规则和状态显着性值提供样本、局部、域和宇宙级解释。此外，引入了法学硕士代理支持的用户界面，以弥合技术解释和用户理解之间的差距，允许非专家用户指定解释要求并接收结构化、可访问的报告和交互式咨询。. We propose the XCF that systematically extends model-agnostic XAI techniques to closed-loop control systems, aiming to address the challenges of universality and accessibility in explainable control. By treating the controller as a closed box, XCF enables hierarchical explanations of control behavior without requiring access to internal structures. As a concrete instantiation of the framework, the HFMAE-C is developed, using FLSs to approximate controller behavior and provide sample, local, domain, and universe-level explanations through IF–THEN rules and state salience values. Furthermore, an LLM agent-supported user interface is introduced to bridge the gap between technical explanations and user understanding, allowing non-expert users to specify explanation requirements and receive structured, accessible reports and interactive consultation.两个案例研究表明，所提出的框架实现了较高的近似精度，同时提供了跨尺度的有意义且一致的解释。Two case studies demonstrated that the proposed framework achieves high approximation accuracy while offering meaningful and consistent explanations across scales.未来的工作将探索 LLM 和可解释控制之间的更深入集成，例如使用 LLM 进行更高级别的语义抽象和用户机器交互，允许用户推理跨操作域的控制器行为的相似性和差异。Future work will explore deeper integration between LLMs and explainable control, such as using LLMs for higher-level semantic abstraction and user-machine interaction, allowing users to reason about similarities and differences in controller behavior across operating domains.
