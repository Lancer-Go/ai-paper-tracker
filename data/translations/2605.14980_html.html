摘要Abstract

分析显微图像以提取生物对象特性（例如其形态组织、时间动态和种群密度）是各种生物医学研究的基础。然而，手动执行此操作既昂贵又耗时。尽管已经探索了基于深度学习的方法来自动化这一过程，但实践中显微镜分析设置的巨大多样性（包括生物对象类型、样品处理协议、成像设备和分析任务等的变化）常常导致它们无效。因此，这些方法通常需要针对不同的环境进行广泛的调整，然而，这可能会给实验室带来实际上不可持续的负担，迫使生物医学研究人员仍然普遍依赖手动分析，从而严重阻碍了生物医学研究进展的步伐。 这种情况迫切需要一种可靠且广泛适用的显微图像分析工具，但这种工具仍然缺失。为了解决这一差距，我们提出了第一个即用型显微镜图像分析框架 MicroscopyMatching，它可以在不同的显微镜分析设置中可靠地执行关键分析任务（包括分割、跟踪和计数）。从根本上不同的角度来看，MicroscopyMatching 将不同的显微图像分析任务重新表述为统一的匹配问题，通过利用预训练的潜在扩散模型的鲁棒匹配能力来有效地处理该问题。通过这种独特的设计，MicroscopyMatching 在 20 个基准数据集和来自 200 组不同的真实世界实验的图像数据集合中实现了始终如一的卓越性能，其性能甚至可以与人类专家相媲美，而现有方法经常无法提供可靠的结果。总之，这些结果使 MicroscopyMatching 成为一种实用且随时可用的工具，它将为现实环境中的各种生物医学研究带来巨大的好处。Analyzing microscopy images to extract biological object properties (e.g., their morphological organization, temporal dynamics, and population density) is fundamental to various biomedical research. Yet conducting this manually is costly and time-consuming. Though deep learning-based approaches have been explored to automate this process, the substantial diversity of microscopy analysis settings in practice (including variations of biological object types, sample processing protocols, imaging equipment, and analysis tasks, etc.) often renders them ineffective. As a result, these approaches typically require extensive adaptation for different settings, which, however, can impose burdens that are often practically unsustainable for laboratories, forcing biomedical researchers to still commonly rely on manual analysis, thereby severely bottlenecking the pace of biomedical research progress. This situation has created a pressing and long-standing need for a reliable and broadly applicable microscopy image analysis tool, yet such a tool is still missing. To address this gap, we present the first ready-to-use microscopy image analysis framework, MicroscopyMatching, that can reliably perform key analysis tasks (including segmentation, tracking, and counting) across diverse microscopy analysis settings. From a fundamentally different perspective, MicroscopyMatching reformulates diverse microscopy image analysis tasks as a unified matching problem, effectively handling this problem by exploiting the robust matching capability from pre-trained latent diffusion models. Through this distinctive design, MicroscopyMatching achieves consistently superior performance across 20 benchmark datasets and a collection of image data from 200 sets of diverse, real-world experiments, with performance even comparable to human experts, in contrast to existing methods frequently failing to provide reliable results. Together, these results establish MicroscopyMatching as a practical and ready-to-use tool, which shall bring substantial benefit to various biomedical research in real-world settings.

In this context, analyzing microscopy images to extract properties (e.g., morphological organization, temporal dynamics, and population density) of biological objects has become a fundamental component of biomedical research[caicedo2019nucleus, falk2019u, mavska2014benchmark]。这些分析支持广泛的生物医学研究，例如分析复杂组织中肿瘤细胞的形态和空间组织. These analyses support a broad range of biomedical investigations, such as profiling the morphology and spatial organization of tumor cells in complex tissues[romero2024neuroendocrine]，监测癌症进展过程中的细胞动态, monitoring cell dynamics during cancer progression[panagopoulos2025multigenerational]，并量化组织微环境中的免疫细胞群, and quantifying immune cell populations in tissue microenvironments[chi2025dietary]。然而，显微图像通常包含大量密集的生物对象，这使得手动提取生物对象属性（例如手动分割、跟踪（追踪）或计数目标生物对象）变得特别繁重和具有挑战性。 基于显微镜的实验的规模和普及进一步放大了这种负担：因为单个实验可能会生成数千张图像. However, microscopy images often contain large numbers of densely packed biological objects, making it particularly burdensome and challenging for manual extraction of biological object properties, such as manually segmenting, tracking (tracing), or counting target biological objects. This burden is further amplified by the scale and prevalence of microscopy-based experiments: as a single experiment may generate thousands of images[boutros2015microscopy]，并且此类实验在生物医学研究中广泛进行，手动分析变得极其劳动密集型和耗时，这甚至可能会分散研究人员在核心科学活动上的时间和精力，最终阻碍整体研究进展。, and such experiments are conducted widely across biomedical research studies, manual analysis becomes extremely labor-intensive and time-consuming, which could even divert researchers’ time and effort from core scientific activities, ultimately hindering overall research progress.

This situation has thus led to a long-standing and pressing need for a “ready-to-use” microscopy image analysis tool: one that works robustly across diverse microscopy analysis settings without additional training data annotation or machine learning model tuning, while consistently delivering reliable analysis results. However, such a tool remains largely unavailable to biomedical researchers.

为了填补这一关键空白，在本文中，我们的目标是开发这样一种实用的即用型工具，该工具可以在关键分析任务（包括分割、跟踪和计数）的不同实验设置中可靠地运行。为了实现这一目标，我们首先重新考虑：是什么使得现有方法对域转移如此敏感？我们发现根本原因在于这些任务的根本制定方式：现有方法通常将显微镜图像分析任务制定为高级对象理解问题，例如识别特定的生物对象、解释其形态特征以及在不同的成像条件下将它们与背景区分开来。这种高层次的理解本质上是针对特定领域的To fill this critical gap, in this paper, we aim to develop such a practically ready-to-use tool that can reliably operate across diverse experimental settings on key analysis tasks (including segmentation, tracking, and counting). To achieve this, we first reconsider: what makes existing methods so sensitive to domain shifts? We identify that the root cause lies in how these tasks are fundamentally formulated: Existing methods typically formulate microscopy image analysis tasks as high-level object understanding problems, such as recognizing specific biological objects, interpreting their morphological features, and distinguishing them from background across varying imaging conditions. Such high-level understandings are inherently domain-specific[stacke2020measuring]，因为学习到的特征和表示与特定的实验设置（特定的细胞类型、样品制备和成像设备）相关，从而在设置改变时导致性能下降和适应要求。 因此，这种限制促使我们从根本不同的角度重新制定任务。为此，我们观察到，尽管显微图像分析设置存在多样性，分割、跟踪和计数都可以被视为匹配问题：由于显微图像中的生物对象通常密集地出现相似且重复的视觉模式，因此可以通过“图像内”匹配来识别这些对象，或者通过在图像内指定一个样本来指示分析目标并将样本与图像的其余部分进行匹配，或者通过利用这些重复的模式来推断底层对象结构并在对象之间进行相互匹配。对象。 在这种重新表述下，分割可以被视为描绘图像中的匹配区域，跟踪可以被视为识别每个帧中的匹配区域并在连续帧中匹配它们，并且可以通过统计图像中的所有匹配实例来执行计数。至关重要的是，这种基于匹配的公式从根本上简化了问题。基于匹配的公式不需要对不同条件下的生物对象进行高层次的理解，而是依赖于更基本、较低级别的视觉比较：由于比较（无论是样本与目标对象之间还是图像内的相似对象之间）发生在同一图像（或图像序列）内，因此位于同一成像域内，因此问题本质上简化为“域内”低级比较，这比学习识别跨不同域的对象要简单得多。这种固有的简单性使得匹配从根本上对域变化具有鲁棒性，因此可以开发能够在不同实验设置中可靠运行的广泛适用的工具。因此，从这个角度来看，跨异构条件处理不同显微镜分析任务的看似具有挑战性的问题可以抽象为一个核心挑战：鲁棒匹配。这就给我们留下了一个关键问题：如何有效地实现这种鲁棒匹配？, as the learned features and representations are tied to particular experimental settings (specific cell types, sample preparations, and imaging equipment), thereby leading to performance degradation and adaptation requirements when settings change. This limitation thus motivates us to reformulate the tasks from a fundamentally different perspective. To this end, we observe that, despite the diversity in microscopy image analysis settings, segmentation, tracking, and counting can all be cast as matching problems: As biological objects in microscopy images typically appear densely with similar and repetitive visual patterns, these objects can be identified by “within-image” matching, either by specifying an exemplar within the image to indicate the analytical target and matching the exemplar to the rest of the image, or by exploiting these repetitive patterns to infer the underlying object structure and perform mutual matching among the objects. Under this reformulation, segmentation can be cast as delineating matched regions in the image, tracking can be viewed as identifying matched regions in each frame and matching them across successive frames, and counting can be performed by tallying all matched instances in the image. Critically, this matching-based formulation fundamentally simplifies the problem. Rather than requiring high-level understandings of biological objects across varying conditions, the matching-based formulation relies on a more basic, lower-level visual comparison: As the comparison (whether between an exemplar and target objects or among similar objects within the image) occurs within the same image (or image sequence) and therefore lies within the same imaging domain, the problem essentially reduces to “within-domain” low-level comparison, which is substantially simpler than learning to recognize objects across diverse domains. This inherent simplicity makes matching fundamentally robust to domain variations and therefore feasible to develop broadly-applicable tools that can reliably operate across diverse experimental settings. Hence, from this perspective, the seemingly challenging problem of handling diverse microscopy analysis tasks across heterogeneous conditions can be abstracted into a single core challenge: robust matching. This then leaves us with a key question: how can such robust matching be achieved effectively?

为了回答这个问题，我们从潜在扩散模型（LDM）中汲取灵感To answer this question, we draw inspiration from latent diffusion models (LDMs)[rombach2022high]，一类最初为高质量图像生成而开发的大型基础生成模型。我们发现 LDM 具有两个关键特性，使它们特别适合在显微图像中进行稳健匹配。首先，能够准确生成具有非常丰富的细粒度细节的图像表明LDM可以捕获密集的视觉结构，包括局部纹理、对象边界、形状和空间布局，这对于在显微图像中执行匹配非常有益。其次，除了图像合成能力之外，LDM 中的内部自注意力和交叉注意力还为细粒度匹配提供了自然机制。具体来说，在训练期间，自注意力层和交叉注意力层被显式优化，以在图像上下文中建立视觉对应关系，根据相同成像条件下的相似性关联视觉一致的区域。这使得 LDM 获得了“域内”匹配的基本能力：识别在相同成像条件下与给定图像块匹配的图像区域。通过对数十亿张不同图像进行超大规模训练，这种基本能力变得非常强大。正如我们之前的作品中所观察到的, a class of large foundational generative models originally developed for high-quality image generation. We identify that LDMs possess two key properties that make them particularly suitable for robust matching in microscopy images. First, being able to accurately generate images with very rich fine-grained details suggests that LDMs can capture dense visual structure, including local textures, object boundaries, shapes, and spatial layouts, which is highly beneficial for performing matching in microscopy images. Second, beyond image synthesis capability, the internal self- and cross-attention in LDMs also provide natural mechanisms for fine-grained matching. Specifically, during training, the self- and cross-attention layers are explicitly optimized to establish visual correspondences within an image context, associating visually consistent regions based on similarity under the same imaging conditions. This leads LDMs to acquire a fundamental capability of “within-domain” matching: identifying image regions that match a given image patch under the same imaging conditions. Through extremely large-scale training on billions of diverse images, this fundamental capability becomes highly robust. As observed in our previous works[hui2024class, peng_harnessing]，这样的 LDM 可以对具有不同条件的图像执行鲁棒匹配，包括训练期间未遇到的图像。, such LDMs can perform robust matching for images with diverse conditions, including those not encountered during training.

讨论Discussion

在本研究中，我们介绍了 MicroscopyMatching，这是一种用于实践中显微镜图像分析的即用型框架。与在高度多样化和不断发展的显微镜图像分析设置下经常失败的现有显微镜图像分析方法相比，我们表明 MicroscopyMatching 在不同的和以前未见过的实验设置中可靠地执行，而不需要为每个特定的显微镜实验场景进行模型适应的繁重工作。首先，在涵盖各种显微镜图像分析设置的 20 个不同基准数据集上，MicroscopyMatching 始终如一地提供卓越的性能。其次，除了标准基准之外，MicroscopyMatching 在来自不同现实世界实验的更加异构的显微镜图像数据集合上仍然保持稳健，在实际实验可变性下始终提供可靠的性能。第三，为了调查这种性能是否达到了具有实际意义的水平，我们进行了专家评级和人类模型一致性评估，这进一步证明了 MicroscopyMatching 实现了与人类注释者相当的性能。最后，一项用户研究表明，MicroscopyMatching 对于不同背景的生物医学研究人员来说非常实用且有帮助，进一步支持 MicroscopyMatching 作为显微镜图像分析的即用型工具。总而言之，这些互补评估维度的结果使 MicroscopyMatching 成为实践中显微镜图像分析的可靠且随时可用的框架。In the present study, we introduce MicroscopyMatching, a ready-to-use framework for microscopy image analysis in practice. In contrast to existing microscopy image analysis methods that frequently fail under the highly diverse and ever-evolving microscopy image analysis settings, we show that MicroscopyMatching performs reliably across diverse and previously unseen experimental settings, without requiring burdensome efforts of model adaptation for each specific microscopy experimental scenario. First, across 20 different benchmark datasets spanning a wide variety of microscopy image analysis settings, MicroscopyMatching consistently delivers superior performance. Second, beyond standard benchmarks, MicroscopyMatching remains robust on an even more heterogeneous collection of microscopy image data from diverse real-world experiments, consistently delivering reliable performance under practical experimental variability. Third, to investigate whether this performance reaches a practically meaningful level, we conducted expert rating and human-model agreement evaluation, which further demonstrate that MicroscopyMatching achieves performance comparable to human annotators. Finally, a user study shows that MicroscopyMatching is practically useful and helpful for biomedical researchers across diverse backgrounds, further supporting MicroscopyMatching as a ready-to-use tool for microscopy image analysis. Taken together, results across these complementary evaluation dimensions establish MicroscopyMatching as a reliable and ready-to-use framework for microscopy image analysis in practice.

尽管我们的 MicroscopyMatching 工具已被证明具有实际有效性，但仍存在一些限制，为未来的工作指明了方向。首先，在这项工作中，我们重点关注显微图像分析，这是一个至关重要的领域。然而，研究 MicroscopyMatching 的基本见解是否可以扩展到其他生物成像模式（例如 X 射线、磁共振和超声成像）也很有价值。其次，关于预训练LDM的选择，我们采用了Stable DiffusionDespite the demonstrated practical effectiveness of our MicroscopyMatching tool, there are several limitations that suggest directions for future work. First, in this work, we focus on microscopy image analysis, a domain of critical importance. However, it would also be valuable to investigate whether the underlying insights of MicroscopyMatching can be extended to other biological imaging modalities, such as X-ray, magnetic resonance, and ultrasound imaging. Second, regarding the choice of pre-trained LDM, we adopted Stable Diffusion[rombach2022high]，一个广泛使用且功能强大的模型，作为 MicroscopyMatching 的基础。虽然这种选择被证明非常有效，但 LDM 在 AI 领域的快速发展使得后续研究探索将更先进的 LDM 集成到框架中变得有意义和值得。最后，MicroscopyMatching 目前通过首先将非二维显微镜数据（例如体积电子显微镜图像）简化为二维表示来间接处理它们，这是生物医学分析流程中普遍采用的策略, a widely used and powerful model, as the foundation of MicroscopyMatching. While this choice proved very effective, the rapid evolution of LDMs in the AI field makes it meaningful and worthwhile for follow-up studies to explore the integration of more advanced LDMs into the framework. Extending MicroscopyMatching to directly handle three-dimensional data would also be valuable for future work.

Specifically, the AP metric is computed by matching the predicted mask of each instance to the ground-truth instance mask that has the highest intersection over union (IoU) with the predicted instance mask. Given an IoU threshold (e.g., 0.50), a predicted mask for an instance is considered a true positive (TP) if its IoU with the corresponding ground-truth mask is greater than the IoU threshold; otherwise, it is considered a false positive (FP). The ground-truth masks that have no matched predicted masks are considered as false negatives (FN). 跟踪。Tracking.我们使用成熟的细胞跟踪挑战赛中的跟踪指标来评估跟踪性能We evaluate the tracking performance using the tracking metrics in the well-established Cell Tracking Challenge[mavska2023cell]，即跟踪精度度量（TRA）、链接精度度量（LNK）和分割精度度量（SEG）。具体来说，TRA 通过联合评估对象检测和时间关联来量化整体跟踪性能。它是通过将预测对象轨迹的有向无环图与地面实况跟踪图进行比较来计算的，并对误报、漏检和不正确的时间链接等错误进行惩罚。 TRA 分数越高表明整体跟踪性能越好。 LNK 通过仅关注连续帧之间对象链接的正确性来评估时间关联性能。它比较了预测轨迹图和真实轨迹图的链接边缘，并反映了随着时间的推移跟踪对象的准确程度。 LNK 分数越高，表明随着时间的推移正确关联对象的性能越好。另一方面，SEG 通过比较基于 Jaccard 相似度指数的预测和地面真实对象掩模来评估分割质量，SEG 分数越高，反映在每帧中定位对象的性能越好。这些指标共同综合评估检测准确性、时间关联正确性和整体跟踪完整性。, i.e., the tracking accuracy measure (TRA), the linking accuracy measure (LNK), and the segmentation accuracy measure (SEG). Specifically, TRA quantifies the overall tracking performance by jointly evaluating object detection and temporal association. It is computed by comparing the directed acyclic graph of the predicted object trajectories with the ground-truth tracking graph, penalizing errors such as false positives, missed detections, and incorrect temporal links. The higher TRA score suggests better overall tracking performance. LNK evaluates the temporal association performance by focusing solely on the correctness of object linkages between consecutive frames. It compares the predicted and ground-truth trajectory graphs in terms of their linkage edge, and reflects how accurately objects are followed over time. The higher LNK score indicates the better performance of correctly associating objects over time. SEG, on the other hand, assesses the segmentation quality by comparing the predicted and ground-truth object masks based on Jaccard similarity index, with a higher SEG score reflecting better performance of localizing the objects in each frame. Together, these metrics comprehensively evaluate detection accuracy, temporal association correctness, and overall tracking integrity.

专家评级详情Details of expert rating

为了评估 MicroscopyMatching 预测的感知质量，我们进行了由八名生物医学专业人士参与的专家评级评估。对于三个任务（分割、跟踪和计数）中的每一个，从相应基准数据集的测试集中随机选择输入图像样本（图像或图像序列）的子集。对于每个输入图像样本，向每位专业人员提供输入图像数据本身以及两个相应的输出：一个由 MicroscopyMatching 生成，另一个通过人工注释获得。为了更清晰的可视化，以特定于任务的方式可视化输出：对于分割任务，通过将生成的掩模叠加在输入图像上来呈现输出；对于跟踪任务，轨迹显示在延时序列之上；对于计数任务，显示生成的密度图以及结果对象计数。To assess the perceived quality of MicroscopyMatching’s predictions, we conducted an expert rating evaluation involving eight biomedical professionals. For each of the three tasks (segmentation, tracking, and counting), a subset of input image samples (images or image sequences) was randomly selected from the test sets of the corresponding benchmark datasets. For each input image sample, each professional was presented with the input image data itself, together with two corresponding outputs: one generated by MicroscopyMatching and the other obtained via human annotation. For clearer visualization, outputs were visualized in a task-specific manner: for segmentation tasks, the outputs were presented by overlaying the generated masks on the input image; for tracking tasks, trajectories were shown on top of time-lapse sequences; and for counting tasks, the generated density map together with the resulting object count were displayed.

对于每个图像样本，两个结果（即人类注释和显微镜匹配预测）以随机顺序呈现，前者以随机顺序标记为“结果 1”，后者标记为“结果 2”。然后，每位专业人员对两个结果进行盲法比较，并按照反映其相对质量的 5 分李克特量表进行评分（1 =“结果 1”明显优于“结果 1”；2 =“结果 1”优于“结果 1”；3 =“结果 1”与“结果 2”质量相似；4 =“结果 1”较差；5 =“结果 1”明显较差）。汇总专家评分后，我们将每个分数转换为 MicroscopyMatching 的相对性能分数，范围从 -2 到 +2，其中 +2 表示 MicroscopyMatching 的预测比人工注释好得多，-2 表示相反。总共收集了 2,000 次配对比较。For each image sample, the two results (i.e., the human annotation and the MicroscopyMatching prediction) were presented in random order, with the former in the random order labeled as “result1” and the latter as “result2”. Each professional then performed a blinded comparison between the two results and assigned a score on a 5-point Likert scale reflecting their relative quality (1 = “result1” is clearly superior; 2 = “result1” is superior; 3 = “result1” is of similar quality to “result2”; 4 = “result1” is inferior; 5 = “result1” is clearly inferior). After aggregating the expert ratings, we converted each score into a relative performance score for MicroscopyMatching ranging from -2 to +2, where +2 indicates that MicroscopyMatching’s prediction is much better than the human annotation and -2 indicates the opposite. In total, 2,000 pairwise comparisons were collected.

人模一致性评估详情Details of human-model agreement evaluation

为了更细致地评估 MicroscopyMatching 的性能，我们还进行了人体模型一致性评估。具体来说，我们为五位注释者（数字健康实验室的成员）中的每一位提供了从不同的真实世界实验设置中收集的同一组显微镜图像样本（图像和图像序列），并要求他们独立注释每个样本。我们量化了注释者间的变异性，并将其与 MicroscopyMatching 与每个注释者的一致性进行了比较。在我们的评估中，我们使用平均 AP 作为分割指标，使用 TRA 作为跟踪指标，使用 MAE 作为计数指标。在各个任务中，MicroscopyMatching 与个体注释者的一致性（人类模型一致性）属于注释者间（人与人）变异性的范围，这表明 MicroscopyMatching 的性能与人类注释者的性能相当。To more meticulously assess MicroscopyMatching’s performance, we also conducted a human-model agreement evaluation. Specifically, we provided each of five annotators (members of a digital health laboratory) with the same set of microscopy image samples (images and image sequences) collected from diverse, real-world experimental settings and asked them to independently annotate each sample. We quantified inter-annotator variability and compared it with MicroscopyMatching’s agreement with each annotator. In our evaluation, we used mean AP as the metric for segmentation, TRA for tracking, and MAE for counting. Across tasks, MicroscopyMatching’s agreement with individual annotators (human-model agreement) falls within the range of inter-annotator (human-human) variability, indicating that MicroscopyMatching’s performance is comparable to that of human annotators.
