5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 IterCAD，这是一个统一的、绘图驱动的 CAD 代理框架，它将从头开始的合成和基于指令的编辑转变为与执行沙箱的闭环交互。通过利用具有尺寸约束的多视图工程图，IterCAD 为迭代缺陷诊断提供精确的物理锚点，克服了基于语法或点云的反馈的局限性。配备渐进式 SFT、几何感知 RL 和几何可行前缀掩码，我们的代理显着提高了代码可执行性和几何收敛性。使用整体指标（CD-TR 曲线和 AUC-TR）进行的综合评估表明，IterCAD 为稳健且可靠的 CAD 自动化设定了更严格的标准。In this work, we presented IterCAD, a unified, drawing-driven CAD agent framework that transforms from-scratch synthesis and instruction-based editing into a closed-loop interaction with an execution sandbox. By leveraging multi-view engineering drawings with dimensional constraints, IterCAD provides precise physical anchors for iterative defect diagnosis, overcoming the limitations of syntax- or point-cloud-based feedback. Equipped with progressive SFT, geometry-aware RL, and Geometry-Viable Prefix Masking, our agent significantly improves code executability and geometric convergence. Comprehensive evaluations using holistic metrics (CD-TR Curve and AUC-TR) demonstrate that IterCAD sets a more rigorous standard for robust and faithful CAD automation.

局限性Limitations

尽管取得了这些有希望的结果，但在未来的工作中仍有一些局限性有待解决。 首先，我们的研究主要集中在 CadQuery 程序和特定绘图标准中的单部件参数化建模，这代表了自动化工程绘图逆向工程的初步探索性步骤。过渡到其他专有绘图规范和大规模装配级协作设计是验证整个生态系统可扩展性的关键下一步。 其次，尽管我们的基准测试结合了高级建模功能，但它在很大程度上依赖于倒角距离等几何指标，而这些指标无法完全捕获高级机械语义和功能制造意图。因此，虽然合成模型可能满足基本空间尺寸，但 IterCAD 尚无法识别特定特征的基础工程实用性，例如键槽、轴承配合或螺纹规格。 最后，虽然代理可靠地提供可执行代码，但它优先考虑立即几何收敛而不是长期程序可维护性。这有时会导致次优的硬编码实现，缺乏人类协作和工程维护所必需的结构化、分层参数化设计树。Despite these promising results, several limitations remain to be addressed in future work. First, our research focuses primarily on single-part parametric modeling within the CadQuery program and specific drawing standards, representing an initial exploratory step toward automated engineering drawing reverse engineering. Transitioning to other proprietary drawing specifications and large-scale assembly-level collaborative design is a crucial next step in validating the scalability of the entire ecosystem. Second, although our benchmark incorporates advanced modeling features, it largely relies on geometric metrics like chamfer distance that fail to fully capture high-level mechanical semantics and functional manufacturing intents. Consequently, while the synthesized models may satisfy basic spatial dimensions, IterCAD cannot yet recognize the underlying engineering utilities of specific features, such as keyways, bearing fits, or thread specifications. Finally, while the agent reliably delivers executable code, it prioritizes immediate geometric convergence over long-term program maintainability. This occasionally results in suboptimal, hard-coded implementations that lack the structured, hierarchical parametric design trees essential for human collaboration and engineering maintenance.
