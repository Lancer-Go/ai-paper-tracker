1 简介Introduction

强化学习（RL）已成为大型语言模型（LLM）后培训的基础支柱，擅长培养特定领域模型的专业能力Reinforcement learning (RL) has established itself as a foundational pillar in large language model (LLM) post-training, excelling at cultivating specialized capabilities in domain-specific models[32, 48]。然而，在现实应用程序中提供多个专用模型通常在计算上是令人望而却步的，这就产生了对保留这些不同优势的单个统一模型的强烈需求。为了将多名专家整合为一名学生，多教师政策蒸馏（M-OPD）已成为一种有吸引力的范例；每个输入提示都会路由到相应的领域专家，该专家对学生策略生成的轨迹提供密集的每个令牌奖励监督. However, serving multiple specialized models in real-world applications is often computationally prohibitive, creating a strong demand for a single unified model that preserves these disparate strengths. To consolidate multiple specialists into a single student, multi-teacher on-policy distillation (M-OPD) has emerged as an appealing paradigm; each input prompt is routed to a corresponding domain expert, which provides dense per-token reward supervision over the trajectories generated by the student policy[24, 27, 4]。尽管在工业管道中越来越多地采用多教师蒸馏，但控制多领域能力集成的基本机制仍然知之甚少，研究界仍然缺乏开放且严格可重复的方法。. Despite the increasing adoption of multi-teacher distillation in industrial pipelines, the fundamental mechanisms governing multi-domain capability integration remain poorly understood, and the research community still lacks an open and rigorously reproducible recipe.

7 结论Conclusion

我们从头开始构建了一个完全开放的多教师策略蒸馏管道——混合域SFT， 三名领域 RL 教师，多教师 OPD，并用它来回答一个具体问题，即何时 路由已经没有错误了，是什么阻止了三个专家的能力被写入 一次进入同一组参数。 我们的实验将优化预算的分配确定为主要瓶颈。我们确定 造成不平衡的三个独立因素：代币数量不平等、奖励幅度不平等和过时 在反复的内部更新过程中获得奖励。 Open-MOPD的三种机制——We build from scratch a fully open multi-teacher on-policy distillation pipeline—mixed-domain SFT, three domain RL teachers, multi-teacher OPD—and use it to answer one concrete question, namely when routing is already error-free, what prevents the capabilities of three experts from being written into the same set of parameters at once. Our experiments identify the allocation of the optimization budget as the main bottleneck. We identify three separable contributors to imbalance: unequal token counts, unequal reward magnitudes, and outdated rewards during repeated inner updates.
