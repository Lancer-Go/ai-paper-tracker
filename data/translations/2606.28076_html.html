摘要。Abstract.

知识图问答（KGQA）旨在通过对结构化事实进行推理来回答自然语言问题。现有的多跳 KGQA 方法主要依赖于以主题为中心的扩展，面临两个关键挑战：搜索空间随着噪声混合类型路径的快速增长，检索到的路径可能无法满足复杂问题的语义约束。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 OPI，一种用于多跳 KGQA 的本体引导证据路径推理框架。 OPI 引入了以关系为中心的本体图来捕获关系的头尾类型约束，为答案端约束提供紧凑的接口。基于该本体图，OPI首先引入双向检索机制，将预测答案类型映射到兼容的最终跳关系，并将主题侧前缀扩展与答案侧最终跳匹配相结合，从而抑制噪声混合类型扩展。 OPI 进一步采用迭代细化策略来重新评估问题上下文下的检索路径和候选答案，过滤类型兼容但与问题无关的证据，以实现更可靠的答案预测。在 WebQSP、CWQ 和 MetaQA 上的实验表明，OPI 大大减少了搜索空间，与最强的先验结果相比，在 WebQSP 上将 Hit@1/F1 提高了 4.6/5.0 点，在 CWQ 上提高了 8.9/3.3 点，并且仅使用检索模块就在 MetaQA 上实现了接近饱和的 Hit@1。Knowledge graph question answering (KGQA) aims to answer natural-language questions by reasoning over structured facts. Existing multi-hop KGQA methods mainly rely on topic-centered expansion, which faces two key challenges: the search space rapidly grows with noisy mixed-type paths, and retrieved paths may fail to satisfy the semantic constraints of complex questions. To address these challenges, we propose OPI, an ontology-guided evidence path inference framework for multi-hop KGQA. OPI introduces a relation-centric ontology graph to capture the head-tail type constraints of relations, providing a compact interface for answer-side constraints. Based on this ontology graph, OPI first introduces a bidirectional retrieval mechanism by mapping the predicted answer type to compatible final-hop relations and combining topic-side prefix expansion with answer-side final-hop matching, thereby suppressing noisy mixed-type expansion. OPI further adopts an iterative refinement strategy to reassess retrieved paths and candidate answers under the question context, filtering type-compatible but question-irrelevant evidence for more reliable answer prediction. Experiments on WebQSP, CWQ, and MetaQA show that OPI substantially reduces the search space, improves Hit@1/F1 by 4.6/5.0 points on WebQSP and 8.9/3.3 points on CWQ over the strongest prior results, and achieves near-saturated Hit@1 on MetaQA with the retrieval module alone.

1. 简介Introduction

知识图问答（KGQA）旨在通过对知识图中的实体和关系进行推理来回答自然语言问题，并已应用于医疗保健等领域Knowledge graph question answering (KGQA) aims to answer natural-language questions by reasoning over entities and relations in a knowledge graph, and has been applied in domains such as healthcare(Frisoni et al., 2024), 农业, agriculture(Yang et al., 2024)、以及多媒体, and multimedia(Lee et al., 2024)。在大型语言模型 (LLM) 快速发展的推动下，最近的研究越来越多地将 LLM 与 KG 相结合，以支持问题理解、推理规划和基于图的答案生成. Driven by the rapid advances of large language models (LLMs), recent studies increasingly integrate LLMs with KGs to support question understanding, reasoning planning, and graph-grounded answer generation(Pan et al., 2024)。这些方法探索了多种策略，包括迭代图探索、关系路径规划和图约束解码，并且具有基于知识图谱的高级多跳推理. These methods explore diverse strategies, including iterative graph exploration, relation-path planning, and graph-constrained decoding, and have advanced multi-hop reasoning over KGs(Liu et al., 2025b; Wen et al., 2024; Sun et al., 2024; Luo et al., 2024, 2025; Tan et al., 2025; Mavromatis and Karypis, 2025)。然而，复杂的多跳 KGQA 在大规模 KG 上仍然具有挑战性，其中不断增加的规模、异质性和结构复杂性引入了巨大的搜索空间和模糊的推理证据. Nevertheless, complex multi-hop KGQA remains challenging over large-scale KGs, where increasing scale, heterogeneity, and structural complexity introduce large search spaces and ambiguous reasoning evidence(Dong, 2023; Ge et al., 2021; Rabbani et al., 2023).

多跳 KGQA 的常见范例是检索以主题实体为根的证据路径或子图，并使用它们来得出答案。为了提高检索效率，许多方法通过局部子图构建、迭代图探索和路径剪枝来控制扩展空间A common paradigm for multi-hop KGQA is to retrieve evidence paths or subgraphs rooted at the topic entity and use them to derive answers. To improve retrieval efficiency, many methods control the expansion space through local subgraph construction, iterative graph exploration, and path pruning(Wen et al., 2024; Sun et al., 2024; Tan et al., 2025)。为了提高推理质量，最近的方法进一步引入了语义信号，例如关系路径规划、图结构提示和基于LLM的证据推理. To improve reasoning quality, recent methods further introduce semantic signals such as relation-path planning, graph-structured prompting, and LLM-based evidence reasoning(Liu et al., 2025b; Luo et al., 2024, 2025; Mavromatis and Karypis, 2025)。尽管取得了这些进步，大多数检索过程仍然主要由以主题为中心的扩展驱动：它们从主题实体开始，逐步探索邻近的实体和关系。因此，检索通常会产生大量与预期答案类型不一致的结构上可达路径，同时还会引入无法满足问题复杂语义约束的虚假候选路径。. Despite these advances, most retrieval procedures are still primarily driven by topic-centered expansion: they start from the topic entity and progressively explore neighboring entities and relations. Consequently, retrieval often produces numerous structurally reachable paths that are inconsistent with the expected answer type, while also introducing spurious candidate paths that fail to satisfy the complex semantic constraints of the question.

3. 方法Method

3.1. 整体架构Overall Architecture

OPI 由三个紧密连接的模块组成：本体图构建、本体引导双向检索和迭代答案细化。它首先将原始知识图谱抽象为以关系为中心的本体图，其中每个关系都由类型级关系签名表示。基于此本体图，OPI 预测问题所隐含的答案类型，并将其映射到一组答案类型兼容的最终跳关系，从而实现将主题侧前缀扩展与答案侧最终跳匹配相结合的双向检索。然后，OPI 通过生成器-细化器循环来细化检索到的证据和候选答案，其中结构化反馈会迭代更新路径上下文和答案上下文。OPI consists of three tightly connected modules: ontology graph construction, ontology-guided bidirectional retrieval, and iterative answer refinement. It first abstracts the original knowledge graph into a relation-centric ontology graph, where each relation is represented by a type-level relation signature. Based on this ontology graph, OPI predicts the answer type implied by the question and maps it to a set of answer-type-compatible final-hop relations, enabling bidirectional retrieval that combines topic-side prefix expansion with answer-side final-hop matching. OPI then refines the retrieved evidence and candidate answers through a generator-refiner loop, where structured feedback iteratively updates both the path context and the answer context.Figure 3总结了 OPI 的主要推理流程。summarizes the main inference pipeline of OPI.

3.2. 本体图构建Ontology Graph Construction

在大规模知识图谱中，实体级图谱往往是密集且高度异构的。随着跳数的增加，不受约束的以主题为中心的扩展很容易产生大量嘈杂的证据路径，导致搜索空间快速增长。这促使我们构建知识图谱的紧凑类型级抽象，以便将重复的实体级事实概括为更稳定的关系类型模式。In large-scale knowledge graphs, the entity-level graph is often dense and highly heterogeneous. 3.4.3. 迭代修改和停止Iterative Revision and Stopping

OPI 采用基于两个信号的自适应停止策略：精化者置信度和答案稳定性。细化器输出离散的置信水平以及结构化的修正操作。当细化器指定最高置信度时，细化循环终止，表明当前答案得到检索到的证据的充分支持。我们使用这个最高置信水平作为保守的停止阈值，以避免在证据仍然不明确时过早终止。OPI adopts an adaptive stopping strategy based on two signals: refiner confidence and answer stability. The refiner outputs a discrete confidence level together with structured revision actions. The refinement loop terminates when the refiner assigns the highest confidence level, indicating that the current answer is sufficiently supported by the retrieved evidence. We use this highest confidence level as a conservative stopping threshold to avoid premature termination when the evidence remains ambiguous.

当优化后答案在连续迭代中保持不变时，OPI 也会停止。此稳定性标准捕获进一步细化不再改变最终预测的情况。如果满足任一停止条件，OPI 将返回当前优化轮次的答案。否则，更新的路径上下文、答案上下文、答案类型约束和细化反馈将传递到下一轮生成器。如果没有触发提前停止条件，OPI 将返回最后一轮优化的结果。OPI also stops when the post-refinement answer remains unchanged across consecutive iterations. This stability criterion captures cases where further refinement no longer changes the final prediction. If either stopping condition is satisfied, OPI returns the answer from the current refinement round. Otherwise, the updated path context, answer context, answer-type constraints, and refinement feedback are passed to the next generator round. If no early stopping condition is triggered, OPI returns the answer from the final refinement round.

总体而言，OPI 闭合了检索到的证据和最终答案预测之间的循环。生成器在基于图的检索上下文下提出答案假设，而细化器将当前答案状态转换为明确的修订操作。然后，路径和答案上下文将针对下一轮进行相应更新。这种自适应过程有助于抑制噪声分支并提高答案的可靠性，超越单次检索和读出。Overall, OPI closes the loop between retrieved evidence and final answer prediction. The generator proposes answer hypotheses under graph-grounded retrieval context, while the refiner converts the current answer state into explicit revision actions. The path and answer contexts are then updated accordingly for the next round. This adaptive process helps suppress noisy branches and improves answer reliability beyond single-pass retrieval-and-readout.

现有的多跳 KGQA 方法可以大致分为基于嵌入的方法、基于检索的方法、独立的 LLM 方法和 KG 增强的 LLM 方法。早期基于嵌入的方法，例如 KV-MemExisting multi-hop KGQA methods can be broadly grouped into embedding-based methods, retrieval-based methods, standalone LLM methods, and KG-enhanced LLM methods. 5.2. 本体论和类型级指导Ontology and Type-level Guidance

本体和模式信息提供了用于组织知识图中的实体、关系、类型和约束的高级语义结构。与实体级三元组相比，类型级抽象捕获更稳定的语义规律，例如预期的关系的头和尾类型。最近的研究探索了法学硕士的本体构建、增强和学习Ontology and schema information provide high-level semantic structures for organizing entities, relations, types, and constraints in knowledge graphs. Compared with entity-level triples, type-level abstractions capture more stable semantic regularities, such as the expected head and tail types of relations. Recent studies have explored LLMs for ontology construction, enhancement, and learning(Funk et al., 2023; Toro et al., 2024; Babaei Giglou et al., 2023)，以及从大型 RDF 资源或能力问题驱动的工作流程构建本体, as well as ontology construction from large RDF resources or capability-question-driven workflows(Chen and Zhao, 2018; Kommineni et al., 2024)。这些研究表明，本体构建和丰富可以为知识密集型任务提供有用的基础。. These studies show that ontology construction and enrichment can provide useful foundations for knowledge-intensive tasks.

在 KGQA 中，本体信息已被用来改善自然语言问题和图结构之间的语义对齐。例如，引入本体引导提示和逆向思维策略来加强多跳推理和泛化In KGQA, ontology information has been used to improve the semantic alignment between natural-language questions and graph structures. For example, ontology-guided prompting and reverse-thinking strategies have been introduced to strengthen multi-hop reasoning and generalization(Jiang et al., 2025b; Liu et al., 2025b)，而 OntoTune 通过本体驱动的自我训练将法学硕士与领域本体结合起来, while OntoTune aligns LLMs with domain ontologies through ontology-driven self-training(Liu et al., 2025c)。然而，大多数现有方法仅使用本体信息作为辅助信号，在大型异构检索空间中提供有限的基于图的约束。 Palantir 等工业系统进一步表明，本体可以作为连接数据、推理和下游操作的结构化接口. However, most existing methods use ontology information only as auxiliary signals, providing limited graph-grounded constraints in large heterogeneous retrieval spaces. Industrial systems such as Palantir further show that ontology can serve as a structured interface connecting data, reasoning, and downstream actions(Palantir, 2024)。 OPI 遵循这一动机，但专门针对多跳 KGQA：它从 KG 本身构建一个以关系为中心的本体图，并使用类型级签名将预测的答案类型映射到兼容的最终跳关系，从而集成类型级和实体级路径搜索。. OPI follows this motivation but specializes it for multi-hop KGQA: it constructs a relation-centric ontology graph from the KG itself and uses type-level signatures to map the predicted answer type to compatible final-hop relations, thereby integrating type-level and entity-level path search.

6. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 OPI，一种本体引导的多跳 KGQA 证据路径推理框架。 OPI 引入了以关系为中心的本体图，使答案端类型约束变得明确，并将主题端前缀扩展与答案端最终跳匹配相结合，以减少噪声路径爆炸。它还采用生成器-细化器循环来联合重新评估检索到的证据并回答假设，过滤类型兼容但与问题无关的候选者。 WebQSP、CWQ 和 MetaQA 上的实验表明，OPI 始终优于代表性的多跳 KGQA 方法。这些结果表明，OPI 为检索提供了一个紧凑且可重用的以关系为中心的本体图，同时有效缓解了多跳 KGQA 中的路径爆炸和语义错位。In this paper, we proposed OPI, an ontology-guided evidence path inference framework for multi-hop KGQA. OPI introduces a relation-centric ontology graph to make answer-side type constraints explicit, and combines topic-side prefix expansion with answer-side final-hop matching to reduce noisy path explosion. It further employs a generator-refiner loop to jointly reassess retrieved evidence and answer hypotheses, filtering type-compatible but question-irrelevant candidates. Experiments on WebQSP, CWQ, and MetaQA show that OPI consistently outperforms representative multi-hop KGQA methods. These results demonstrate that OPI provides a compact and reusable relation-centric ontology graph for retrieval, while effectively alleviating path explosion and semantic misalignment in multi-hop KGQA.

在未来的工作中，我们计划将 OPI 扩展到具有较弱或不太可靠的类型信息的知识图谱。一个关键方向是当实体类型丢失、有噪声或仅部分可用时构建和更新关系签名，以便本体引导的检索在具有明确定义模式的知识图谱之外仍然有效。这样的扩展将扩大 OPI 对开放域和动态发展的知识图的适用性。In future work, we plan to extend OPI to knowledge graphs with weaker or less reliable type information. A key direction is to construct and update relation signatures when entity types are missing, noisy, or only partially available, so that ontology-guided retrieval remains effective beyond KGs with well-defined schemas. Such an extension would broaden the applicability of OPI to open-domain and dynamically evolving knowledge graphs.
