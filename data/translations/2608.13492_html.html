AlayaWorld：交互式长视界世界建模 - 完整技术报告（v1.1）AlayaWorld: Interactive Long-Horizon World Modeling - Full Technical Report (v1.1)

AlayaWorld Team, Alaya Lab *
摘要Abstract

本报告介绍了AlayaWorld的改进版本。虽然主干架构、分块自回归生成方案和训练数据与之前的版本相比保持不变，但我们大幅修改了条件信号的表示方式和集成到模型中的方式。 新设计遵循一个简单的原则：条件信号应在潜在表示和时间结构方面尽可能与生成的内容匹配。为此，我们做出两项重大改变。首先，我们用流式 3D 点缓存渲染器替换之前基于深度扭曲的空间内存。其次，我们重新设计了调节管道，以便将视觉条件编码在相同的因果 VAE 潜在空间中，并且时间统计数据与生成视频的时间统计数据一致。 具体来说，新版本引入了六处修改：（1）用运动感知潜在调节代替静态帧图像调节； （2）将重新渲染的空间记忆因果编码为连续序列； (3)在像素空间中对齐时间记忆窗口； (4) 采用硬内存丢失，删除内存令牌而不是清零它们； (5) 统一跨训练和推理的VAE编码和解码协议； (6)移除相机AdaLN分支，使得视点控制完全通过重新渲染的空间条件来提供。This report presents an improved version of AlayaWorld. While the backbone architecture, chunk-wise autoregressive generation scheme, and training data remain unchanged from the previous release, we substantially revise how conditioning signals are represented and integrated into the model. The new design is guided by a simple principle: conditioning signals should match the generated content as closely as possible in both latent representation and temporal structure. To this end, we make two major changes. First, we replace the previous depth-warping-based spatial memory with a streaming 3D point-cache renderer. Second, we redesign the conditioning pipeline so that visual conditions are encoded in the same causal-VAE latent space, with temporal statistics consistent with those of the generated video. Concretely, the new version introduces six modifications: (1) replacing static-frame image conditioning with motion-aware latent conditioning; (2) causally encoding re-rendered spatial memory as a continuous sequence; (3) aligning the temporal-memory window in pixel space; (4) adopting hard memory dropout that removes memory tokens rather than zeroing them; (5) unifying the VAE encoding and decoding protocol across training and inference; and (6) removing the camera AdaLN branch, such that viewpoint control is provided entirely through the re-rendered spatial condition.

1 调节和记忆重新设计Conditioning and Memory Redesign

AlayaWorld 的早期版本以文本、时间记忆、空间记忆和显式相机控制分支为生成条件。 虽然这些信号提供了补充信息，但它们是通过不同的途径进行处理的：视觉条件通常是独立编码的，时间记忆并不总是与 VAE 的因果结构一致，并且相机轨迹通过 AdaLN 单独注入。 因此，模型看到的条件信号可能在时间结构、潜在表示或训练推理行为方面与生成的视频潜在不同。The previous version of AlayaWorld conditioned generation on text, temporal memory, spatial memory, and an explicit camera-control branch. While these signals provided complementary information, they were processed through different pathways: visual conditions were often encoded independently, temporal memory was not always aligned with the causal structure of the VAE, and camera trajectories were injected separately through AdaLN. As a result, the conditioning signals seen by the model could differ from the generated video latents in their temporal structure, latent representation, or train–inference behavior.

新版本围绕两个原则重新设计了调节管道。 首先，视觉条件应遵循与生成的视频相同的因果 VAE 编码结构。 其次，视点控制应该通过几何对齐的视觉条件来表达，而不是通过单独的调制分支来表达。 基于这些原则，我们提出以下六点改变。The new version redesigns the conditioning pipeline around two principles. First, visual conditions should follow the same causal-VAE encoding structure as the video being generated. Second, viewpoint control should be expressed through geometry-aligned visual conditions rather than through a separate modulation branch. Based on these principles, we introduce the following six changes.

1. 运动感知图像调节。1. Motion-aware image conditioning.

在之前的版本中，图像条件被编码为独立帧，产生潜在表示，而没有因果编码视频潜在中存在的时间上下文。 相反，我们构造一个九帧窗口，由条件帧及其前面的八帧组成（In the previous version, an image condition was encoded as an isolated frame, yielding a latent representation without the temporal context present in causally encoded video latents. We instead construct a nine-frame window consisting of the conditioning frame and its preceding eight frames (stride+1=9\text{stride}+1=9），用因果 VAE 对全窗口进行编码，并使用第二个潜在变量作为图像条件。 单个调节帧仅保留作为解码器前缀。 相同的九帧编码模式用于推理期间的块到块切换，因此图像调节和自回归延续用匹配的时间上下文表示。), encode the full window with the causal VAE, and use the second latent as the image condition. The single conditioning frame is retained only as a decoder prefix. The same nine-frame encoding pattern is used for chunk-to-chunk handoff during inference, so image conditioning and autoregressive continuation are represented with matched temporal context.

2. 流式 3D 空间记忆的因果编码。2. Causal encoding of a streaming 3D spatial memory.

我们替换了之前基于 DA3 的深度变形We replace the previous DA3-based depth warping1带有流式 3D 点缓存，使用 ViGeowith a streaming 3D point cache, using ViGeo3估计每像素 3D 几何形状。 在每个生成块之后，每像素 3D 点都会注册到持久缓存中，然后从下一个块的计划视点重新渲染。 为了在推出过程中保持几何一致性，相机轨迹使用前缀位移的成对中值比与缓存进行比例对齐，同时通过将针孔相机模型与点图进行最小二乘拟合，对每个剪辑估计一次相机内在参数，然后保持固定。 我们还改变了渲染空间条件的编码方式。 我们不是独立地对渲染帧进行编码，而是在前面添加一个真实的前缀帧并对结果序列进行因果编码。 然后丢弃前缀潜在变量，留下遵循与目标视频潜在变量相同的因果编码结构的空间记忆潜在变量。 与无效渲染区域相对应的标记将被删除而不是被屏蔽，从而保留与 FlashAttention 的兼容性。to estimate the per-pixel 3D geometry. After each generated chunk, per-pixel 3D points are registered into a persistent cache, which is then re-rendered from the planned viewpoint of the next chunk. To maintain geometric consistency over the rollout, the camera trajectory is scale-aligned to the cache using a pairwise-median ratio over prefix displacements, while camera intrinsics are estimated once per clip by least-squares fitting of a pinhole camera model to the point map and then kept fixed. We also change how the rendered spatial condition is encoded. Instead of encoding rendered frames independently, we prepend a real prefix frame and encode the resulting sequence causally. The prefix latent is then discarded, leaving spatial-memory latents that follow the same causal encoding structure as the target video latents. Tokens corresponding to invalid rendered regions are removed rather than masked, preserving compatibility with FlashAttention.

3. 像素对齐的时间记忆。3. Pixel-aligned temporal memory.

时间内存从 6 个潜在内存减少到 4 个，并通过在 VAE 编码之前在像素空间中对齐内存窗口来构建。 对于Temporal memory is reduced from six latents to four and is constructed by aligning the memory window in pixel space before VAE encoding. ForN=4N=4记忆潜伏，我们精确编码memory latents, we encode exactly1+(N−1)×8=251+(N-1)\times 8=25框架，恰好产生四个因果潜伏。 数据加载器和训练器使用相同的帧到潜在约定来确保一致的窗口边界。 这消除了先前可能泄漏到时间记忆上下文中的离一边界潜在因素。frames, producing exactly four causal latents. The data loader and trainer use the same frame-to-latent convention to ensure consistent window boundaries. This removes the off-by-one boundary latents that could previously leak into the temporal-memory context.

4.硬内存丢失。4. Hard memory dropout.

之前的内存丢失策略将内存标记归零，同时保留它们在序列中的位置。 尽管它们的值被删除，序列结构仍然揭示了内存标记是否存在。 我们将其替换为硬丢失，这会完全删除内存标记，从而改变实际的序列长度。 相应地，推理时的第一个推出步骤是在没有时间记忆的情况下执行的，与训练期间看到的无记忆情况相匹配。The previous memory-dropout strategy zeroed memory tokens while preserving their positions in the sequence. Although their values were removed, the sequence structure still revealed whether memory tokens were present. We replace this with hard dropout, which removes the memory tokens entirely and therefore changes the actual sequence length. Correspondingly, the first rollout step at inference is performed without temporal memory, matching the memory-free cases seen during training.

5. 跨训练和推理的统一 VAE 协议。5. A unified VAE protocol across training and inference.

我们标准化了整个训练、自回归部署和评估过程中使用的像素潜在接口。 块切换始终使用锚帧和相同的九帧因果编码窗口。 解码支持直接潜在延续和 RGB 解码-重新编码路径，而评估提供真实延续以及 VAE 所需的两个因果前缀潜在。 这从以前的版本中删除了几个特定于阶段的编码和解码约定，并减少了训练和自回归推理之间的差异。We standardize the pixel–latent interface used throughout training, autoregressive rollout, and evaluation. Chunk handoff consistently uses an anchor frame together with the same nine-frame causal encoding window. Decoding supports both direct latent continuation and an RGB decode–re-encode path, while evaluation supplies ground-truth continuations together with the two causal prefix latents required by the VAE. This removes several stage-specific encoding and decoding conventions from the previous version and reduces discrepancies between training and autoregressive inference.

6. 基于几何的相机控制。6. Geometry-based camera control.

专用相机 AdaLN 分支被删除。 相反，规划的摄像机轨迹用于确定重新渲染 3D 点缓存的视点，并且生成的空间条件为模型提供摄像机引导。 在因果 VAE 编码之后，视点信息通过与其他空间条件相同的视觉潜在表示来呈现。 这个直接 y 将摄像机控制与场景几何结构（包括比例、可见性和视差）耦合在一起，而不需要单独的摄像机调节侧通道。 文本调节继续指定场景语义，而渲染的空间记忆指定所需的视点。The dedicated camera AdaLN branch is removed. Instead, the planned camera trajectory is used to determine the viewpoint from which the 3D point cache is re-rendered, and the resulting spatial condition provides the camera guidance to the model. After causal-VAE encoding, viewpoint information is therefore presented through the same visual latent representation as other spatial conditions. This directl y couples camera control to scene geometry, including scale, visibility, and parallax, without requiring a separate camera-conditioning side channel. Text conditioning continues to specify scene semantics, while the rendered spatial memory specifies the desired viewpoint.

2 实验结果Experimental Results

2.1 定量结果Quantitative Results

表Table1报告 WBench 上的比较reports the comparison on the WBench2导航拆分，涵盖视频质量、设置、交互、一致性和物理指标的 158 个导航案例。阿赖耶世界在一致性基准测试中表现强劲，取得了 89.5 的最佳整体一致性得分。它还在视频质量方面保持竞争力，平均得分为 79.1，同时在交互式评估协议下保持稳定的导航性能。总体而言，结果表明阿赖耶世界在长视域交互生成过程中能够有效地保持视觉和几何一致性。navigation split, covering 158 navigation cases across Video Quality, Setting, Interaction, Consistency, and Physical metrics. AlayaWorld performs strongly on the Consistency benchmarks, achieving the best overall Consistency score of 89.5. It also remains competitive in Video Quality, with an average score of 79.1, while maintaining solid navigation performance under the interactive evaluation protocol. Overall, the results indicate that AlayaWorld is effective at preserving visual and geometric consistency during long-horizon interactive generation.

在视频质量方面，AlayaWorld 获得了 67.7 分的最佳成像分数，并且在美学质量方面以 62.6 分保持竞争力。尽管在闪烁、动态和平滑度等时间指标上不领先，但其视频质量总体得分为 79.1，接近最强的竞争方法。这些结果表明，AlayaWorld 在自回归交互过程中保持了较高的感知质量和图像保真度，同时为短期时间动态的进一步改进留下了空间。For Video Quality, AlayaWorld achieves the best Imaging score of 67.7 and remains competitive in Aesthetic quality with a score of 62.6. Its overall Video Quality score of 79.1 is close to the strongest competing methods, despite not leading on temporal metrics such as Flickering, Dynamic, and Smoothness. These results suggest that AlayaWorld maintains high perceptual quality and image fidelity during autoregressive interaction, while leaving room for further improvement in short-term temporal dynamics.

阿赖耶世界的主要优势在于一致性。它在背景一致性、透视一致性、主题一致性和几何一致性方面取得了最好的结果，在空间和门控空间一致性方面均排名第二。特别是，强大的透视和几何分数表明，当相机在环境中移动时，AlayaWorld 更好地保留了场景结构和视点相关的几何形状，而高背景和主题分数表明，先前观察到的视觉内容在扩展交互中保持稳定。总之，这些改进导致所有评估方法中总体一致性得分最高，验证了所提出的长视野生成空间和时间记忆机制的有效性。The main advantage of AlayaWorld is observed in Consistency. It achieves the best results in Background Consistency, Perspective Consistency, Subject Consistency, and Geometric Consistency, and ranks second in both Spatial and Gated Spatial Consistency. In particular, the strong Perspective and Geometric scores indicate that AlayaWorld better preserves scene structure and viewpoint-dependent geometry as the camera moves through the environment, while the high Background and Subject scores show that previously observed visual content remains stable over extended interactions. Together, these improvements lead to the highest overall Consistency score among all evaluated methods, validating the effectiveness of the proposed spatial and temporal memory mechanisms for long-horizon generation.

在交互方面，AlayaWorld 的导航得分为 79.9，展示了对导航控件的响应能力，尽管竞争方法表现更好。 它在场景和物理类别上的表现相对较弱，特别是在场景一致性和因果保真度方面。 这些结果表明，虽然AlayaWorld提供了强大的视觉持久性和几何稳定性，但改进环境级语义保存和物理交互建模仍然是未来的重要方向。For Interaction, AlayaWorld achieves a Navigation score of 79.9, demonstrating responsiveness to navigation controls, although competing methods perform better. Its performance on the Setting and Physical categories is comparatively weaker, particularly for Scene consistency and Causal Fidelity. These results suggest that while AlayaWorld provides strong visual persistence and geometric stability, improving environment-level semantic preservation and physical interaction modeling remains an important future direction.
