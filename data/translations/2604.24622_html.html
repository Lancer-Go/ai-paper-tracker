摘要。Abstract.

基于流的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）策略为动作生成提供了强大的表达能力，但存在根本性的低效率：需要多步推理才能从无信息的高斯噪声中恢复动作结构，导致实时约束下效率与质量的权衡较差。我们通过重新思考生成动作建模中起点的作用来解决这个问题。我们建议不要缩短采样轨迹Flow-based vision-language-action (VLA) policies offer strong expressivity for action generation, but suffer from a fundamental inefficiency: multi-step inference is required to recover action structure from uninformative Gaussian noise, leading to a poor efficiency–quality trade-off under real-time constraints. We address this issue by rethinking the role of the starting point in generative action modeling. Instead of shortening the sampling trajectory, we proposeCF-VLA，一种从粗到细的两阶段公式，将动作生成重组为粗略的初始化步骤，构造动作感知的起点，然后进行单步局部细化，以纠正残留错误。具体而言，粗略阶段学习端点速度的条件后验，以将高斯噪声转换为结构化初始化，而精细阶段则从该初始化执行固定时间细化。为了稳定训练，我们引入了一种逐步策略，首先学习受控粗预测器，然后执行联合优化。 CALVIN 和 LIBERO 上的实验表明，我们的方法在低 NFE（功能评估次数）制度下建立了强大的效率-性能前沿：它始终优于现有的 NFE=2 方法，匹配或超过 NFE=10, a coarse-to-fine two-stage formulation that restructures action generation into a coarse initialization step that constructs an action-aware starting point, followed by a single-step local refinement that corrects residual errors. 1. 简介Introduction

基于流的VLA策略Flow-based VLA policies(Intelligence et al., 2025; Kawaharazuka et al., 2025; Shao et al., 2025; Black et al., 2026)最近成为机器人操纵的一个有前途的方向， 使用诸如have recently emerged as a promising direction for robotic manipulation, with methods such asπ0.5\pi_{0.5}为多模式连续动作建模提供强大的表现力。 然而，它们的实际部署仍然受到根本性低效率的限制： 动作生成从无信息的高斯噪声开始，需要多个迭代细化步骤来恢复有意义的结构， 导致实时约束下效率与质量之间的权衡不佳。offering strong expressivity for modeling multimodal continuous actions. However, their practical deployment remains limited by a fundamental inefficiency: action generation starts from uninformative Gaussian noise and requires multiple iterative refinement steps to recover meaningful structure, leading to a poor efficiency–quality trade-off under real-time constraints.

对于这一限制的一个有用的观点来自于 Liu 等人最近的分析。A useful perspective on this limitation comes from the recent analysis of Liu et al.(Liu et al., 2025)，他们研究了基于流的扩散模型中的预言速度，并表明目标动力学呈现出两阶段结构：早期演化主要执行从高斯先验到相关模式的导航，而后期动力学逐渐由最近的数据样本主导，并专注于细化细粒度细节。这一观察结果有助于解释为什么大幅减少采样步骤数量会使高质量生成变得困难：较小的推理预算迫使全局传输和局部校正在相同的截断轨迹内竞争。与这一观点一致的是，最近在生成机器人控制方面的工作, who study oracle velocity in flow-based diffusion models and show that the target dynamics exhibit a two-stage structure: early-time evolution mainly performs navigation from the Gaussian prior toward relevant modes, whereas later-time dynamics become increasingly dominated by the nearest data sample and focus on refining fine-grained details. This observation helps explain why aggressively reducing the number of sampling steps can make high-quality generation difficult: a small inference budget forces global transport and local correction to compete within the same truncated trajectory. Consistent with this view, recent work in generative robotic control(Pan et al., 2026)表明将推理分为两个 MSE 监督阶段（粗略的大步更新，随后进行较小的修正细化）已经可以实现与基于流的策略相当的性能suggests that splitting inference into two MSE-supervised stages—a coarse, large-step update followed by a smaller corrective refinement—can already achieve performance on par with flow-based policiesπ0\pi_{0} (Black et al., 2026).

3.5. 逐步训练Stepwise Training

从粗到精的公式引入了不对称依赖性：精细阶段的有效性依赖于粗略初始化的质量。因此直接联合优化是不稳定的。The coarse-to-fine formulation introduces an asymmetric dependency: the effectiveness of the fine stage relies on the quality of the coarse initialization. Direct joint optimization is therefore unstable.

第一阶段：稳定。Phase I: Stabilization.我们首先在受控监督下训练端点表示，同时将细化分支暴露于通过在噪声和真实动作之间插值构建的稳定代理分布。此阶段尚未实现完整的从粗到细的机制，但确保两个分支都在条件良好的输入上运行。We first train the endpoint representation under controlled supervision, while exposing the refinement branch to a stable proxy distribution constructed by interpolating between noise and ground-truth actions. This phase does not yet realize the full coarse-to-fine mechanism, but ensures that both branches operate on well-conditioned inputs.

第二阶段：联合优化。Phase II: Joint optimization.一旦粗略输出变得足够结构化，我们就会转向完整目标。在此阶段，细化分支接收由学习的粗后验生成的输入，联合优化学习分布整形和局部恢复如何相互加强。Once the coarse outputs become sufficiently structured, we switch to the full objective. At this stage, the refinement branch receives inputs generated by the learned coarse posterior, and joint optimization learns how distribution shaping and local recovery reinforce each other.

这种逐步策略直接遵循问题的结构，而不是启发式训练技巧。This stepwise strategy follows directly from the structure of the problem, rather than being a heuristic training trick.

5. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 CF-VLA，这是一个从粗到细的框架，用于在视觉-语言-动作策略中高效地生成基于流程的动作。 通过明确地将全局对齐与局部细化分开，CF-VLA 将生成过程重组为由 AP 引导的初始化和单步校正组成的两步​​过程。We presented CF-VLA, a coarse-to-fine framework for efficient flow-based action generation in vision-language-action policies. By explicitly separating global alignment from local refinement, CF-VLA restructures the generation process into a two-step procedure consisting of AP-guided initialization and single-step correction.

在 CALVIN、LIBERO 和真实机器人基准测试中，CF-VLA 在性能和效率之间实现了强有力的平衡，表明高质量的动作生成不需要大量的迭代细化。 相反，我们的结果表明起点的结构在决定效率和鲁棒性方面起着核心作用。Across CALVIN, LIBERO, and real-robot benchmarks, CF-VLA achieves a strong balance between performance and efficiency, demonstrating that high-quality action generation does not require extensive iterative refinement. Instead, our results show that the structure of the starting point plays a central role in determining both efficiency and robustness.

更广泛地说，这项工作提出了生成控制视角的转变：根据全局对齐和局部校正的功能角色组织计算，而不是沿着采样轨迹均匀分配计算，会更有效。 我们希望这一原则能够指导未来可在现实世界中部署的低延迟 VLA 系统的设计。More broadly, this work suggests a shift in perspective for generative control: rather than allocating computation uniformly along a sampling trajectory, it is more effective to organize computation according to the functional roles of global alignment and local correction. We hope this principle can guide the design of future low-latency VLA systems that are deployable in the real world.
