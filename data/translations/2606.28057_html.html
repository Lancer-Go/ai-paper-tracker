摘要Abstract

语言模型 (LM) 使用随词汇量大小线性缩放的嵌入矩阵来表示标记。为了限制参数足迹，先前的工作建议将许多标记散列到仅编码器模型中的单个向量中。虽然这提供了参数效率，但多对一的冲突阻止了它在因果 LM 中的使用。在本文中，我们提出了 MULTIHASHFORMER，这是一种允许基于哈希的自回归的新框架。每个令牌都表示为一个唯一的哈希签名，即由多个独立哈希函数生成的离散哈希 ID 的短序列。哈希编码器将此签名压缩为单个潜在向量，以供 Transformer 解码器处理。然后，哈希解码器生成下一个令牌的哈希签名，然后将其映射回文本。我们在 100M、1B 和 3B 参数尺度上评估我们的方法，证明 MULTIHASHFORMER 在多个基准测试中始终优于标准 Transformer LM。此外，我们表明我们的模型可以在不进行任何修改的情况下以恒定的参数足迹处理多语言词汇扩展。Language models (LMs) represent tokens using embedding matrices that scale linearly with the vocabulary size. To constrain the parameter footprint, prior work proposes hashing many tokens into a single vector within encoder-only models. While this offers parameter efficiency, many-to-one collisions prevent its use in causal LMs. In this paper, we propose MULTIHASHFORMER, a new framework that allows hashbased autoregression. Each token is represented as a unique hash signature, a short sequence of discrete hash IDs, generated by multiple independent hash functions. A Hash Encoder compresses this signature into a single latent vector for processing by a Transformer decoder. Then, a Hash Decoder generates the hash signature of the next token, which is then mapped back to text. We evaluate our approach at the 100M, 1B and 3B parameter scales, demonstrating that MULTIHASHFORMER consistently outperforms standard Transformer LMs across multiple benchmarks. Furthermore, we show that our model handles multilingual vocabulary expansion with a constant parameter footprint without any modifications.1

语言模型依赖于随词汇量大小线性缩放的嵌入矩阵。虽然像 BPE (Sennrich et al., 2016) 和 SentencePiece (Kudo and Richardson, 2018) 这样的子词分词器可以缓解词汇量爆炸，但它们仍然受到固定的、数据派生的词汇的限制，而这些词汇很难处理罕见的或域外的单词。LMs rely on an embedding matrix that scales linearly with the vocabulary size. While subword tokenizers like BPE (Sennrich et al., 2016) and SentencePiece (Kudo and Richardson, 2018) mitigate vocabulary explosion, they remain constrained by a fixed, data-derived vocabulary that struggles with rare or out-of-domain words.

7 结论7 Conclusion

我们引入了 MULTIHASHFORMER，这是一个利用多哈希结构来绕过传统词汇瓶颈的生成框架。实证评估表明，它在核心功能和稀有词表示方面都提供了改进。此外，该框架无需额外参数即可实现无缝词汇扩展。We introduced MULTIHASHFORMER, a generative framework that leverages multi-hash structures to bypass traditional vocabulary bottlenecks. Empirical evaluation demonstrates that it offers improvements both in core capabilities and rare word representations. Furthermore, the framework enables seamless vocabulary expansion without requiring additional parameters.

Following the protocol of Cheng et al. (2024), 1B and 3B models were trained from scratch within a standard academic budget of 100B tokens. While MULTIHASHFROMER shows an upward performance trajectory with model size, exploring larger scales (e.g., 7B+ parameters) remains a valuable direction for institutions with access to the required computing resources.

单粒种子。Single seed.由于从头开始为 100B 代币预训练 1B 和 3B 参数模型的计算成本很高，因此在我们的学术基础设施限制下进行多次训练运行是令人望而却步的。因此，我们的经验结果基于每个配置的单个种子。然而，在 1B 和 3B 规模上观察到的一致性能提升证明了 MULTIHASHFORMER 框架的稳健性。这种一致性表明我们的哈希编码器和哈希解码器引入的改进源于归纳偏差而不是随机采样方差。此外，采用 Li 等人建立的超参数协议。 （2025）和程等人。 （2024），我们确保标准且可重复的培训动力。Due to the high computational cost of pre-training 1B and 3B parameter models from scratch for 100B tokens, conducting multiple training runs was prohibitive under our academic infrastructure constraints. Consequently, our empirical results are based on a single seed per configuration. However, the consistent performance gains observed across both the 1B and 3B scales demonstrate the robustness of the MULTIHASHFORMER framework. This consistency indicates that the improvements introduced by our Hash Encoder and Hash Decoder stem from inductive bias rather than random sampling variance. Furthermore, adopting the established hyperparameter protocols of Li et al. (2025) and Cheng et al. (2024), we ensure standard and reproducible training dynamics.
