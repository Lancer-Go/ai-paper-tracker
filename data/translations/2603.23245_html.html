摘要Abstract

我们研究神经常微分方程和随机微分方程（神经常微分方程和神经微分方程），以在基于模型的强化学习 (RL) 框架内对完全和部分观察的环境中的随机动力学进行建模。通过一系列模拟，我们表明神经 SDE 可以更有效地捕获过渡动态的固有随机性，从而在具有挑战性的场景中实现高性能策略并提高样本效率。我们利用神经 ODE 和 SDE 通过逆模型来有效地调整政策以适应环境动态的变化，只需要与新环境进行有限的交互。为了解决部分可观测性问题，我们引入了一种潜在的 SDE 模型，该模型在潜在空间中结合了 ODE 和 GAN 训练的随机分量。从该模型导出的策略提供了强大的基线，在随机连续控制基准中优于或匹配基于模型和无模型的通用方法。这项工作说明了动作条件潜在 SDE 在随机转换环境中的 RL 规划中的适用性。我们的代码可在以下位置获取：We investigate neural ordinary and stochastic differential equations (neural ODEs and SDEs) to model stochastic dynamics in fully and partially observed environments within a model-based reinforcement learning (RL) framework. Through a sequence of simulations, we show that neural SDEs more effectively capture transition dynamics’ inherent stochasticity, enabling high-performing policies with improved sample efficiency in challenging scenarios. We leverage neural ODEs and SDEs for efficient policy adaptation to changes in environment dynamics via inverse models, requiring only limited interactions with the new environment. To address partial observability, we introduce a latent SDE model that combines an ODE and a GAN-trained stochastic component in latent space. Policies derived from this model offer a strong baseline, outperforming or matching general model-based and model-free approaches across stochastic continuous-control benchmarks. This work illustrates the applicability of action-conditional latent SDEs for RL planning in environments with stochastic transitions. 1 简介Introduction

近年来，神经微分方程家族（神经DE）In recent years, the family of neural differential equations (neural DEs)(Chen et al., 2018; Rubanova et al., 2019; Li et al., 2020; Kidger et al., 2020; 2021)已成为动态系统建模的强大框架。这些模型的总体思想是使用神经网络来参数化系统动力学的导数，而状态演化则由数值微分方程求解器计算。这种将动力学建模与离散化方案解耦的方法可以提高模型的保真度，以捕获复杂的连续时间转变。因此，神经 DE 特别适合学习和表示强化学习 (RL) 中的过渡动态。have emerged as a powerful framework for modelling dynamical systems. The general idea of these models is to use neural networks to parameterise the derivatives of the system’s dynamics, while the state evolution is computed by a numerical differential equation solver. Such a way of decoupling the modelling of dynamics from the discretisation scheme leads to models with increased fidelity for capturing complex, continuous-time transitions. As a result, neural DEs are especially well-suited for learning and representing transition dynamics in reinforcement learning (RL).

最近的工作表明，积分神经常微分方程（神经 ODE）Recent work has demonstrated that integrating neural ordinary differential equations (neural ODEs)(Chen et al., 2018)和随机微分方程（神经 SDE）and stochastic differential equations (neural SDEs)Li et al. (2020); Kidger et al. (2021)到 RL 框架中可以改进连续时间动态的建模。在部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程 (POMDP) 和无模型强化学习中，循环神经常微分方程由于能够对非均匀时间序列进行建模，因此对不规则采样的观测结果表现出鲁棒性into RL frameworks can improve the modelling of continuous-time dynamics. In partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs) and model-free RL, recurrent neural ODEs have shown robustness to irregularly sampled observations, due to their capacity to model non-uniform time series(Zhao et al., 2023)。潜在神经常微分方程也被用作基于模型的强化学习中的数据驱动动力学模型，与无模型基线相比，提供了更高的样本效率. Latent neural ODEs have also been employed as data-driven dynamics models in model-based RL, providing higher sample efficiency compared to model-free baselines(Du et al., 2020)。此外，将神经常微分方程与控制理论约束相结合支持了连续时间域中安全稳定的强化学习的开发. Additionally, incorporating neural ODEs with control-theoretic constraints has supported the development of safe and stable RL in continuous-time domains(Zhao et al., 2025).

神经 SDE 通过联合建模系统动力学的确定性和随机成分来扩展神经 ODE 框架，从而允许不确定性感知的策略学习。最近的研究表明，基于离线模型的强化学习和神经 SDE，特别是在结合物理先验时，可以在低质量数据集上超越最先进的算法Neural SDEs extend the neural ODE framework by jointly modelling the deterministic and stochastic components of system dynamics, allowing for uncertainty-aware policy learning. Recent studies show that offline model-based RL with neural SDEs, particularly when incorporating physics priors, can outperform state-of-the-art algorithms on low-quality datasets(Koprulu et al., 2025)。物理约束的神经 SDE 明确表示模型不确定性，也被证明支持训练分布之外的泛化. Physics-constrained neural SDEs, which explicitly represent model uncertainty, have also been shown to support generalisation beyond the training distribution(Djeumou et al., 2023)。虽然原则上这两种神经 SDE 框架都可以应用于 POMDP，但迄今为止的实际演示主要集中在具有完全可观测性的环境上。. While both these neural SDE frameworks could, in principle, be applied to POMDPs, practical demonstrations to date have focused on environments with full observability.

尽管最近取得了进展，但潜在神经 SDE 在部分观察的随机强化学习环境中的应用仍未得到充分探索。缺乏在完全和部分观察的环境中结合受控潜在神经 ODE 和神经 SDE 的统一框架，并且尚未系统地评估潜在动态中的随机性建模以进行规划的实际好处。此外，在需要适应环境变化的环境中，对神经 ODE 和神经 SDE 的探索也很有限。Despite recent advances, the application of latent neural SDEs to partially observed, stochastic RL environments remains underexplored. Unified frameworks that combine controlled latent neural ODEs and neural SDEs in both fully and partially observed environments are lacking, and the practical benefits of modelling stochasticity in latent dynamics for planning have not been systematically assessed. Additionally, there has been limited exploration of neural ODEs and neural SDEs in settings that require adaptation to changes in the environment.

在这项工作中，我们首先通过采用逆动态方法展示了对环境配置变化的样本有效策略适应In this work, we first demonstrate sample-efficient policy adaptation to changes in environment configuration by employing an inverse dynamics approach(Christiano et al., 2016)基于神经 ODE 和 SDE 转换模型。然后，出于处理复杂随机环境中部分可观测性的需要，我们为基于模型的强化学习引入了一个统一的潜在 ODE/SDE 框架。该框架使用两阶段过程：使用潜在 ODE 学习平均潜在动态，并通过 GAN 训练的潜在 SDE 处理随机性。在随机连续控制任务的实验中，我们表明，在我们研究中最具挑战性的完全或部分观察环境中，与基于 ODE 的模型和无模型 SAC 基线相比，具有潜在神经 SDE 的基于模型的 RL 提高了样本效率。based on neural ODE and SDE transition models. We then introduce a unified latent ODE/SDE framework for model-based RL, motivated by the need to handle partial observability in complex, stochastic environments. The framework uses a two-phase procedure: the mean latent dynamics are learned using a latent ODE, and stochasticity is handled via a GAN-trained latent SDE. In experiments on stochastic continuous control tasks, we show that model-based RL with latent neural SDEs improves sample efficiency compared to both ODE-based models and a model-free SAC baseline in the most challenging fully or partially observed environment included in our study.

据我们所知，之前还没有针对部分观察的随机控制任务中的规划的动作条件潜在 SDE 进行过实证验证。我们的结果强调了潜在神经 SDE 在随机域中进行灵活、噪声感知规划的潜在好处。To our knowledge, there has been no prior empirical validation of action-conditioned latent SDEs for planning in partially observed stochastic control tasks. Our results highlight the potential benefit of latent neural SDEs for flexible, noise-aware planning in stochastic domains.

我们通过分阶段学习漂移和扩散组件来减轻基于 GAN 的 SDE 训练的不稳定性。首先，我们将漂移预训练为 ODE，并在状态空间中使用 MSE 或在潜在空间中使用 ELBO 进行优化。然后，我们冻结这种漂移，并在 GAN 风格的设置中与批评者一起训练扩散。基于 ODE 的模型被用作漂移，因为它凭经验捕获随机转换的平均动态，对应于 SDE 漂移函数。预训练漂移大大提高了扩散组件后续 GAN 式训练的稳定性。此外，我们发现忽略批评者输入中的动作可以进一步稳定更复杂的 Mujoco 任务中的 GAN 训练。We mitigate the instability of GAN-based SDE training by phasing the learning of the drift and diffusion components. First, we pre-train the drift as an ODE, optimized with MSE in the state space or ELBO in the latent space. We then freeze this drift and train the diffusion with a critic in a GAN-style setup. The ODE-based model is used as the drift since it empirically captures the mean dynamics of stochastic transitions, corresponding to the SDE drift function. Pretraining the drift substantially improves the stability of subsequent GAN-style training of the diffusion component. In addition, we found that ignoring actions in the critic input further stabilizes GAN training in the more complex Mujoco tasks.
