在声学场景分类中使用域适应的设备不变性Device Invariance using Domain Adaptation on Acoustic Scene Classification

Abhishek Dileep, Shubham Sharma, Padmanabhan Rajan
摘要Abstract

本文探讨了使用基于卷积神经网络 (CNN) 和基于变换器的特征表示进行声学场景分类时域适应技术的有效性。两种著名的域适应技术，即域对抗神经网络（也称为 DANN）和条件域对抗网络（也称为 CDAN）在各种域转换下进行评估。我们的研究表明，DANN 为两个特征提取器提供了相当一致的有效域适应。另一方面，CDAN 仅为基于 CNN 的特征提取器提供有效的域适应。该研究深入了解了领域适应方法可能需要如何根据底层特征表示进行定制。在 DCASE 2020 数据集上使用多个设备进行的实验评估支持了这些观察结果。This paper explores the effectiveness of domain adaptation techniques when using convolutional neural network (CNN)-based and transformer-based feature representations for acoustic scene classification. Two well-known domain adaptation techniques, namely domain adversarial neural network (also called DANN) and conditional domain adversarial network (also called CDAN) are evaluated under various domain shifts. Our study indicates that DANN provides effective domain adaptation fairly consistently for both feature extractors. On the other hand, CDAN provides effective domain adaptation only for CNN-based feature extractors. The study gives insights into how domain adaptation methods may need to be tailored to the underlying feature representation. Experimental evaluation with multiple devices on the DCASE 2020 dataset supports the observations.

I 简介Introduction

在过去的十年中，深度学习模型在分类任务方面取得了重大进展。在声学场景分类（ASC）中，任务是将单个标签（例如“公园”、“地铁站”、“公交车”等）分配给由音频信号表示的声学场景。标签代表记录信号的声学环境。声学场景和事件的检测和分类（DCASE）系列挑战Over the past decade, deep learning models have made significant progress in classification tasks. In acoustic scene classification (ASC), the task is to assign a single label (such as “park”, “metro station”, “bus” etc.) to an acoustic scene represented by an audio signal. The label represents the acoustic environment where the signal was recorded. The Detection and Classification of Acoustic Scenes and Events (DCASE) series of challenges111https://dcase.community/并取得了该领域的进步。卷积神经网络 (CNN) 和 Transformer 等深层架构带来了出色的分类性能have resulted in advancement in the field. Deep architectures such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs) as well as transformers have resulted in excellent classification performance[11], [12], [19], [10].

然而，当领域发生转移时，许多深度学习模型的泛化能力可能很差。这个问题在计算机视觉中已有详细记录。在这里，如果训练图像来自一个域（例如，正面图像），而测试图像来自不同的域（例如，斜向图像），则分类性能会下降。同样，对于音频数据，域转移会导致分类性能下降。例如，如果训练数据有成人的语音，但测试数据有儿童的语音，性能就会下降。同样，如果训练数据来自一个设备，而测试数据来自另一台设备，则会发生域转移。这已被证明会影响 ASC 的性能However, many deep learning models can be poor at generalization when there is a domain shift. This problem has been well documented in computer vision. Here, if the training images come from one domain (e.g., front-facing face images), and the test images come from a different domain (e.g., oblique-facing images), there is a drop in classification performance. Similarly, for audio data, a domain shift results in a drop in classification performance. For example, if the training data has speech from adults, but if the test data has speech from children, there will be a drop in performance. Similarly, a domain shift occurs if the training data comes from one device, and the test data comes from another device. This has been shown to affect performance in ASC[21], [8], [4], [23].

文献中已经提出了各种域适应方法来克服域转移Various methods for domain adaptation have been proposed in the literature to overcome domain shift[5]。这些方法包括基于实例的方法、基于特征的方法以及本质上是对抗性的方法。本文的目的是研究域适应方法在 ASC 背景下如何针对不同的特征表示（特别是 CNN 和 Transformer）执行。在我们的研究中，域转移由捕获设备的变化来表示（训练数据来自设备 X，测试数据来自设备 Y。）. These methods include methods which are instance-based, feature-based, and which are adversarial in nature. The purpose of this paper is to investigate how domain adaptation methods perform with respect to different feature representations, particularly CNNs and transformers, in the context of ASC. In our study, domain shift is represented by change in the capturing device (training data is from device X and test data is from device Y.)

本文的实验评估比较了两种著名的域适应方法的性能，即域对抗神经网络（也称为 DANN）和条件域对抗网络（也称为 CDAN）。我们的结果表明，可能需要根据底层特征表示来制定适应方法。我们通过在 DCASE 2020 ASC 数据集上使用多个设备进行大量实验来支持我们的假设。The experimental evaluation in this paper compares the performance of two well-known domain adaptation methods, namely domain adversarial neural network also known as DANN and conditional domain adversarial network also called as CDAN. Our results suggest that adaptation methods may need to be crafted depending upon the underlying feature representation. We support our hypothesis with extensive experiments with multiple devices on the DCASE 2020 dataset for ASC.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 自定义 CNN - 特征提取器Custom CNN - Feature Extractor

这是一个使用两层卷积神经网络 (CNN) 的自定义特征提取器，它获取输入频谱图并从中提取本地信息。该架构没有经过预先训练，而是从头开始训练的。第一个卷积层有 32 个内核大小为 7×7 的滤波器。之后，进行批量归一化、ReLU 激活、5×5 窗口的最大池化以及空间 dropout 来提高泛化能力。同样，第二个 CNN 层有 64 个滤波器，并添加了 dropout 和 max-pooling 层。然后，提取的特征图被展平为一维最终层，该层将用作学习的表示。此后，这在本文的其余部分中称为自定义 CNN。This is a custom feature extractor using a two-layer convolutional neural network (CNN) that takes input spectrograms and extracts local information from them. This architecture is not pre-trained and is trained from scratch. There are 32 filters with a kernel size of 7×7 in the first convolutional layer. After that, there is batch normalization, ReLU activation, max-pooling with a 5×5 window, and spatial dropout to improve generalization. Similarly, the second CNN layer has 64 filters and adds a dropout and max-pooling layer. The extracted feature maps are then flattened into a 1D final layer, which will be used as the learned representation. This is henceforth referred to as custom CNN in the rest of the paper.

在我们的实验中，源域是设备A，其他设备是目标域。具体来说，来自训练分区的设备 A 的数据用于训练源分类器，来自测试分区的设备 A 的数据用作可见测试数据（无域不匹配，同一设备）。来自训练分区的设备 C、B、S1、S3 和 S2 的数据用于使用 DANN 和 CDAN 进行域适应，以及来自设备 A 的数据仅使用源/目标域标签。来自设备 B、C、S1、S2 和 S3 的测试分区的数据用作未见的目标数据（域不匹配，设备与设备 A 不同）。因此，我们的无监督域适应设置使用源域的类标签，并使用源/目标域的域标签（目标域中不使用类标签）。因此，我们的域转移是通过在设备 A 上进行训练并在其他设备上进行测试来捕获的。因此记号In our experiments, the source domain is device A, and the other devices are target domains. Specifically, the data of device A from the train partition is used to train the source classifier and the data of device A from the test partition is used as the seen test data (no domain mismatch, same device.) The data of devices C, B, S1, S3 and S2 from the train partition is used for domain adaptation using DANN and CDAN, along with data from device A using only source/target domain labels. The data of the test partition from devices B, C, S1, S2 and S3 is used as the unseen target data (with domain mismatch, devices different from device A.) Our unsupervised domain adaptation setup thus uses class labels for the source domain and uses domain labels for the source/target domain (no class labels are used in the target domain.) Hence our domain shift is captured by training on device A and testing on the other devices. Thus the notationDev​A→Dev​X\textrm{Dev}A\rightarrow\textrm{Dev}X表示从设备 A 到设备 X 的域转移。represents a domain shift from device A to device X.

For the CNN models, we take 384 as the input for both DcaseNet and the custom CNN. Regarding DcaseNet, we use an adaptive average pooling layer to pool all the outputs from the CNN blocks. For the custom CNN, we have used a final flattening layer. For all the three feature extractors, a fully connected layer of 128 and an output layer for 10-class classification is used. V 讨论Discussion

我们的实验表明，域适应的有效方法取决于特征提取架构。的 结果还表明，DANN 改进了几乎所有考虑的特征提取器，包括预训练的 PaSST、DcaseNet 和自定义 CNN。 相比之下，CDAN 在基于 CNN 的模型上表现良好，但在基于 Transformer 的 PaSST 上表现不佳。 我们观察到，当使用 ViT 特征提取器进行训练并使用 CDAN 进行域适应时，分类器中会发生模式崩溃。 CDAN 严重依赖分类器提供的伪标签来调节鉴别器。 另一方面，由于有效感受野的高斯性质和局部归纳偏差，基于 CNN 特征的伪标签训练的分类器变化不大Our experiments indicate that effective methods for domain adaptation depend on the feature extraction architecture. The results also suggest that DANN improves on almost all feature extractors considered, including pre-trained PaSST, DcaseNet and custom CNN. In contrast, CDAN, which performs well for CNN-based models, fails on the transformer-based PaSST. We observed that mode collapse occurs in the classifier when trained with ViT feature extractor while using CDAN for domain adaptation. CDAN relies heavily on pseudo labels provided by the classifier for conditioning the discriminator. On the other hand, classifiers trained on pseudo labels from CNN-based features don’t vary much due to the Gaussian nature of the effective receptive field and local inductive bias[15]。 相比之下，ViT 模型包含更全局的归纳偏差. In contrast ViT models contain a more global inductive bias[24]，从而提供更多动态功能。初步研究揭示了这些特征的统计特性的差异。这些将是未来工作的主题。, hence providing more dynamic features. Preliminary studies reveal differences in the statistical properties of these features. These will be the subject of future work.

VI 结论Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们探索了具有不同特征提取架构的流行领域适应方法的功效。我们对基于 CNN 和 Transformer 的架构的实验揭示了一个重要的见解：域适应方法的性能取决于特征提取架构的性质。我们证明了丹恩 在几乎所有条件下都能很好地工作，提高所有架构的性能，表明它是一种稳健且可推广的域适应方法。同时，CDAN 似乎对基于 CNN 的特征提取器更有效，但无法适应基于 Transformer 的特征提取器（如 PaSST）。 这表明我们不能盲目地将基于 CNN 的域适应方法应用于基于 Transformer 的特征提取器，并且需要对每个上下文进行仔细评估。In this work, we have explored the efficacy of popular domain adaptation methods with different feature extraction architectures. Our experiments on CNN and transformer based architectures revealed an important insight: The performance of a domain adaptation methods is dependent on the nature of feature extraction architecture. We demonstrated that DANN works well in almost all conditions improving performance across all architectures, indicating that it is a robust and generalizable domain adaptation method. Meanwhile, CDAN seems to be more effective with CNN based feature extractors but fails to adapt with transformer based feature extractor like PaSST. This suggests that we cannot blindly apply CNN based domain adaptation methods on transformer based feature extractors and need to careful evaluated for each context.

我们的分析仅限于两种特定的适应方法和 DCASE 2020 数据集。结果可能无法推广到所有可能的对抗技术或声学环境。未来，我们希望更多地探索基于变压器的架构的领域适应动态，以及在领域适应的对抗训练过程中生成的特征的统计特性。Our analysis was confined to two specific adaptation methods and the DCASE 2020 dataset. The results may not generalize to all possible adversarial techniques or acoustic environments. In the future, we would like to explore more on the dynamics of domain adaptation with transformer based architectures and statistical properties of the features generated from these during adversarial training for domain adaptation.
