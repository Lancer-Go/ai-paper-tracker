相关工作Related Work

4D 雷达相机 3D 物体检测4D Radar-Camera 3D Object Detection

4D 雷达摄像头检测利用雷达几何形状和运动线索来加强 稀疏或模糊观察下的相机感知。基准例如 View-of-Delft、TJ4DRadSet 和 K-Radar 支持这一工作线4D radar-camera detection uses radar geometry and motion cues to strengthen camera perception under sparse or ambiguous observations. Benchmarks such as View-of-Delft, TJ4DRadSet, and K-Radar support this line of work(Palffy et al. 2022; Zheng et al. 2022; Paek et al. 2022)。早期的方法，包括 CenterFusion、CRAFT 和 RADIANT，将雷达证据附加到图像建议或对象中心. Early methods, including CenterFusion, CRAFT, and RADIANT, attach radar evidence to image proposals or object centers(Nabati and Qi 2021; Kim et al. 2023; Long et al. 2023)。后来的 BEV 方法，包括 RCFusion， RCBEVDet、LXL、SGDet3D 和 HGSFusion 将这两种模式提升为共享 BEV 场景级融合的特征. Later BEV methods, including RCFusion, RCBEVDet, LXL, SGDet3D, and HGSFusion, lift both modalities into shared BEV features for scene-level fusion(Zheng et al. 2023; Lin et al. 2024; Xiong et al. 2024; Bai et al. 2024; Gu et al. 2025)。 RaGS 和 R4Det 进一步强化了稀疏表示、深度推理和 时间建模. RaGS and R4Det further strengthen sparse representation, depth reasoning, and temporal modeling(Bai et al. 2026c; Xia et al. 2026)。最近的扩展还探索 局部全局雷达检测、稀疏到密集雷达学习、原始雷达张量 融合、雷达相机深度估计、跨视图实例感知，以及 协作感知. Recent extensions also explore local-global radar detection, sparse-to-dense radar learning, raw radar-tensor fusion, radar-camera depth estimation, cross-view instance awareness, and collaborative perception(Bai et al. 2025b, a; Guan et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2025; Bai et al. 2026a, b).

这些方法建立了强大的雷达摄像机检测基线并标记 从前视检测到 360 度全景检测的进展These methods establish strong radar-camera detection baselines and mark the progression from front-view detection to 360∘洞察力。然而， 他们的目标仍然以盒子为中心：融合雷达证据进行检测， 而密集的场景结构很少被维持为联合的可重用状态 对象和占用推理。perception. However, their objective remains box-centric: radar evidence is fused for detection, whereas dense scene structure is rarely maintained as a reusable state for joint object and occupancy reasoning.

Camera BEV and occupancy baselines such as MonoScene, VoxFormer, SurroundOcc, PanoOcc, and M-CONet from OpenOccupancy recover voxelized layout from multi-view imagery(Cao and De Charette 2022; Li et al. 2023; Wei et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2024, 2023; Huang et al. 2024)。 SOGDet 和 UniVision 进一步表明 占用上下文可以支持对象推理. We therefore extend ManTruckScenes with auditable occupancy labels and evaluate both datasets under one normalized radar-camera detection-and-occupancy protocol. Methodologically, we further differ from ordinary dual-head systems by propagating occupancy as an explicit scene state, together with Doppler motion cues, inside the shared representation rather than decoding it only at the output side.

结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了In this work, we presented4DR360∘，一个系统化的 360 度 4D 雷达摄像头框架, a systematic 4D radar-camera framework for 360∘多视角多任务感知。它的主要贡献是占用状态 推理，它将语义占用建模为持久的场景状态，而不是 比雷达相机表示学习的终端输出。 SBE 和 DTF 使这种状态在框架内和跨框架有用，允许前景证据， 密集的布局和雷达运动增强了通用的全场景表示。 这将占用率从辅助预测更改为界面 将对象检测与全面的场景理解结合起来。全高清场景实验 和 ManTruckScenes 在检测和占用预测方面表现出一致的增益， 而后者的入住出口提供了第二个基准合同 用于未来 4D 雷达相机多任务研究。未来的工作将集中于雷达感知的端到端或 VLA 研究。multi-view multi-task perception. Its main contribution is occupancy-state reasoning, which models semantic occupancy as a persistent scene state rather than a terminal output for radar-camera representation learning. SBE and DTF make this state useful within and across frames, allowing foreground evidence, dense layout, and radar motion to reinforce a common full-scene representation. This changes occupancy from an auxiliary prediction into the interface that couples object detection with comprehensive scene understanding. Experiments on OmniHD-Scenes and ManTruckScenes show consistent gains in detection and occupancy prediction, while the latter occupancy export provides a second benchmark contract for future 4D radar-camera multi-task studies. Future work will focus on radar-aware end-to-end or VLA research.
