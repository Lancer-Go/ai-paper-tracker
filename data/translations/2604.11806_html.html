检测多个代理踪迹中的安全违规行为Detecting Safety Violations Across Many Agent Traces

Adam Stein  Davis Brown11footnotemark: 1  Hamed Hassani  Mayur Naik  Eric Wong
University of Pennsylvania

摘要Abstract

为了识别安全违规行为，审核员经常搜索大量代理痕迹。这种搜索很困难，因为故障通常很少见、复杂，有时甚至是敌对性隐藏的，并且只有在一起分析多个痕迹时才能检测到。这些挑战出现在不同的环境中，例如滥用活动、秘密破坏、奖励黑客和即时注入。现有的方法在这里遇到困难有几个原因。每个跟踪判断会错过仅在跟踪中可见的故障，幼稚的代理审计无法扩展到大型跟踪集合，并且固定监视器对于意外行为很脆弱。我们引入了 Meerkat，它将聚类与代理搜索相结合，以发现自然语言中指定的违规行为。通过对有希望的区域进行结构化搜索和自适应调查，Meerkat 可以发现稀疏的故障，而无需依赖种子场景、固定工作流程或详尽的枚举。在滥用、错位和任务游戏设置方面，Meerkat 显着改进了对基线监视器的安全违规检测，发现了开发人员在顶级代理基准测试中普遍存在的作弊行为，并在 CyBench 上发现的奖励黑客示例比之前的审计多了近 4 倍。To identify safety violations, auditors often search over large sets of agent traces. This search is difficult because failures are often rare, complex, and sometimes even adversarially hidden and only detectable when multiple traces are analyzed together. These challenges arise in diverse settings such as misuse campaigns, covert sabotage, reward hacking, and prompt injection. Existing approaches struggle here for several reasons. Per-trace judges miss failures that only become visible across traces, naive agentic auditing does not scale to large trace collections, and fixed monitors are brittle to unanticipated behaviors. We introduce Meerkat, which combines clustering with agentic search to uncover violations specified in natural language. Through structured search and adaptive investigation of promising regions, Meerkat finds sparse failures without relying on seed scenarios, fixed workflows, or exhaustive enumeration. Across misuse, misalignment, and task gaming settings, Meerkat significantly improves detection of safety violations over baseline monitors, discovers widespread developer cheating on a top agent benchmark, and finds nearly 4x more examples of reward hacking on CyBench than previous audits.

1 简介Introduction

AI安全研究在监控和审计语言模型行为方面取得了真正的进展AI safety research has made real progress in monitoring and auditing language model behavior(Greenblatt et al., 2023; Marks et al., 2025; Baker et al., 2025)。但这些方法是针对可从单个代理跟踪中检测到的故障而设计的。然而，在实践中，安全违规的证据分布在许多痕迹中，并且通常没有单个痕迹足以确定明显的违规。例如，Anthropic 最近检测到一场大规模的网络间谍活动，该活动绕过了他们的安全过滤器，只有通过仔细的事后监控才被发现. But these methods are designed for failures detectable from individual agent traces. In practice, however, evidence of a safety violation is distributed across many traces, and often no single trace is sufficient to establish a clear violation. For example, Anthropic recently detected a large-scale cyber espionage campaign that bypassed their safety filters and was only uncovered through careful post-hoc monitoring(Anthropic, 2025)。在这种情况下，审计任务是确定整个跟踪存储库是否揭示了滥用、奖励黑客、破坏或其他安全违规行为。. In cases like this, the auditing task is to determine whether a repository of traces as a whole reveals misuse, reward hacking, sabotage, or some other safety violation.

由于三个原因，存储库级审计很困难。 相关证据可能分布在痕迹中，存储库可能包含数百或数千条大多是良性的痕迹，而不良痕迹可能会被敌意地伪装成普通用途。 图Repository-level auditing is difficult for three reasons. The relevant evidence may be distributed across traces, the repository may contain hundreds or thousands of mostly benign traces, and the bad traces may be adversarially disguised as ordinary use. This combination of distributed evidence, sparse search, and adversarial disguise is what makes repository-level auditing qualitatively different from ordinary per-trace monitoring.

现有的方法只能解决这个问题的一部分，但不能解决我们关心的全部问题。 其中一项工作是开发对各个痕迹的监控器，询问是否可以从模型的输入或输出中识别破坏、隐藏目标或其他不安全行为Existing approaches address parts of this problem, but not the full setting we care about. One line of work develops monitors over individual traces, asking whether sabotage, hidden goals, or other unsafe behavior can be identified from the input or output of the model(Marks et al., 2025; Kutasov et al., 2025; Kale et al., 2025; Cooper Stickland et al., 2025; Storf et al., 2026; Arike et al., 2026)。 另一种选择是定制数据分析. Another option is bespoke data analysis(Tamkin et al., 2024)，或者将一组选定的跟踪打包到一个 LLM 上下文中，并对该子集应用监视器, or packing a selected set of traces into one LLM context and applying a monitor over that subset(Brown et al., 2025)。 这些方法是有用的构建块，但它们没有提供一种通用方法来搜索大型存储库以查找难以单独识别但与许多其他痕迹一起变得明显的故障。. These approaches are useful building blocks, but they do not provide a general way to search large repositories for failures that are hard to identify in isolation but become apparent alongside many other traces.

这一差距促使我们研究跨痕迹集合的安全审计作为发现问题This gap motivates our study of safety auditing across collections of traces as the problem of finding违反证人集violating witness sets，在更大的跟踪存储库中，组合在一起的跟踪会导致安全故障。通过独立地对痕迹进行分类是不可能满足我们的安全目标的；相反，我们需要本地化哪些痕迹属于见证集。 正式而言，此设置涉及超属性, traces which taken together cause a safety failure, in a a larger repository of traces. It is not possible to satisfy our safety goal by classifying traces independently; instead, we need to localize which traces belong to a witness set. Formally, this setting concerns hyperproperties(Clarkson and Schneider, 2010)，因为安全性取决于踪迹集而不是孤立的踪迹。我们这里的重点是识别大型存储库中负责任的跟踪的实际任务。, since safety depends on sets of traces rather than traces in isolation. Our focus here is on the practical task of identifying the responsible traces in a large repository.

我们引入了 Meerkat，这是一种结合了集群和代理推理的代理搜索方法，用于在故障发生后识别故障。 Meerkat 首先将存储库组织成分层结构，以便可以比较相关的痕迹，并可以取消广泛的良性区域的优先级。 然后，它根据属性、存储库和集群结构构建提示和环境，并将它们提供给通用代理，该代理对存储库进行推理，提出违规见证人，并为每个跟踪分配一个分数，说明它属于至少一个此类见证人的可能性，以及总体判决。 因此，Meerkat 的目标是那些在任何一条痕迹中都不突出，但在许多痕迹中得到稀疏证据支持的故障。We introduce Meerkat, an agentic search approach for identifying failures after they have occurred that combines clustering and agent reasoning. Meerkat first organizes the repository into a hierarchical structure so related traces can be compared and broad benign regions can be deprioritized. It then builds a prompt and environment from the property, repository, and cluster structure, and gives those to a generic agent, which reasons over the repository, proposes violating witnesses, and assigns each trace a score for how likely it is to belong to at least one such witness, together with an overall verdict. Meerkat is therefore aimed at failures that do not stand out in any one trace, but are supported by sparse evidence across many traces.

我们在合成和真实环境中评估猫鼬。 基于分解攻击构建的标记合成基准We evaluate Meerkat in both synthetic and real settings. For real-world software and cybersecurity evaluations, Meerkat discovers developers cheating by hiding correct answers in scaffolds, and new instances of reward hacking.
