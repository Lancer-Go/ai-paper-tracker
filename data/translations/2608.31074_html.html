使用 YOLO 姿势估计和基于 CLIP 的语义评分进行实时视频异常检测Real-Time Video Anomaly Detection Using YOLO Pose Estimation and CLIP-Based Semantic Scoring

Vanodhya G. Warnasooriya1, Amir Hajian1, Watchara Ruangsang2, and Supavadee Aramvith1 Affiliation: 1Dept. of Electrical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok 10330, Thailand Affiliation: 2Media Technology Program, King Mongkut’s Univ. of Technology Thonburi, Bangkok 10150, Thailand Affiliation: {vanowarna, amirhajian85}@gmail.com, watchara.ruan@kmutt.ac.th, supavadee.a@chula.ac.th
摘要Abstract

我们提出了一种用于实时视频异常检测的轻量级两阶段框架。第一阶段采用 YOLO v11n-pose 来检测人员并在一次前向传递中提取 17 个骨骼关键点。第二阶段通过 CLIP ViT-B/32 对每个裁剪后的人物区域进行编码，并根据异常行为的预定义文本描述计算余弦相似度。这种架构消除了对光流、独立姿态估计器和基于密度的评分模块的需求。在香港中文大学大道、上海科技大学校园和朱拉隆功大学收集的自定义室内数据集上进行的实验表明，在 NVIDIA Titan XP GPU（3.36）上，端到端吞吐量约为 51 FPSWe propose a lightweight two-stage framework for real-time video anomaly detection. The first stage employs YOLO v11n-pose to detect persons and extract seventeen skeletal keypoints in a single forward pass. The second stage encodes each cropped person region through CLIP ViT-B/32 and computes cosine similarity against predefined textual descriptions of anomalous behaviors. This architecture eliminates the need for optical flow, standalone pose estimators, and density-based scoring modules. Experiments on CUHK Avenue, ShanghaiTech Campus, and a custom indoor dataset collected at Chulalongkorn University demonstrate an end-to-end throughput of approximately 51 FPS on an NVIDIA Titan XP GPU, a 3.36×\times比多特征基线加速，同时保持帧级 AUROC 值分别为 89.26%、70.26% 和 84.13%。speedup over the multi-feature baseline, while maintaining frame-level AUROC values of 89.26%, 70.26%, and 84.13%, respectively.

I 简介Introduction

闭路电视 (CCTV) 在城市和机构环境中的广泛部署推动了对智能视频分析的需求不断增长The widespread deployment of closed-circuit television (CCTV) across urban and institutional environments has driven growing demand for intelligent video analytics[1]。监控多个馈送的操作员很容易出现注意力疲劳，效率在几分钟内急剧下降. Human operators monitoring multiple feeds are susceptible to attention fatigue, with effectiveness declining sharply within minutes[2]，激励自动化系统检测跌倒、争吵和异常姿势。, motivating automated systems for detecting falls, altercations, and abnormal postures.

视频异常检测的先前工作涵盖基于重建的Prior work on video anomaly detection spans reconstruction-based[3, 4]，基于预测的, prediction-based[5]，以及基于属性的, and attribute-based[6]范式。赖斯和霍申paradigms. Reiss and Hoshen[6]通过将 AlphaPose、FlowNet2 和 CLIP 功能与 GMM/kNN 评分相结合，实现了很高的准确性，但多组件管道将吞吐量限制为大约 15 FPS。最近的两项发展使设计变得更简单：CLIPachieved strong accuracy by combining AlphaPose, FlowNet2, and CLIP features with GMM/kNN scoring, but the multi-component pipeline limits throughput to approximately 15 FPS. Two recent developments enable a simpler design: CLIP[7]通过自然语言提示提供零样本异常分类，无需特定于任务的异常标签，并且 YOLO v11n-poseprovides zero-shot anomaly classification via natural-language prompts without task-specific anomaly labels, and YOLO v11n-pose[8]在单次前向传递中通过十七个关键点回归执行人员检测。虽然VadCLIPperforms person detection with seventeen-keypoint regression in a single forward pass. Although VadCLIP[9]它还采用 CLIP 进行视频异常检测，它依赖于弱监督训练的可学习提示令牌，专为离线分析而不是实时部署而设计。also adapts CLIP for video anomaly detection, it relies on learnable prompt tokens trained with weak supervision and is designed for offline analysis rather than real-time deployment.

这项工作针对的是固定室内闭路电视环境中的人级异常（特别是跌倒、躺在地板上、打架和坐在地板上）。该系统设计用于部署在单个桌面级 GPU (NVIDIA Titan XP) 上，以在标准监控视频源上维持高于 30 FPS 的实时吞吐量。This work targets person-level anomalies (specifically falling, lying on the floor, fighting, and sitting on the floor) in fixed indoor CCTV environments. The system is designed for deployment on a single desktop-class GPU (NVIDIA Titan XP) to sustain real-time throughput above 30 FPS on standard surveillance video feeds.

在此基础上，我们提出了一个两阶段框架，其中 YOLO v11n-pose 处理检测，CLIP ViT-B/32 处理语义异常评分。我们的贡献如下：Building on these foundations, we present a two-stage framework in which YOLO v11n-pose handles detection and CLIP ViT-B/32 handles semantic anomaly scoring. Our contributions are as follows:

  • •

    简化的流程，用直接 CLIP 语义评分取代多特征密度估计，产生A streamlined pipeline that replaces multi-feature density estimation with direct CLIP semantic scoring, yielding a3.36×3.36\times吞吐量比之前的基线有所提高，同时保持可比较的准确性。throughput gain over the prior baseline while maintaining comparable accuracy.

  • •

    基于文本提示的分类机制，利用 CLIP 的零样本功能来识别和分类多种异常类型，而不需要标记的异常训练数据。A text-prompt-based classification mechanism that leverages CLIP’s zero-shot capability to identify and categorize multiple anomaly types without requiring labeled anomaly training data.

  • •

    对朱拉隆功大学的三个监控基准进行评估并部署在闭路电视直播中，确认了稳定的实时运行。Evaluation on three surveillance benchmarks and deployment on live CCTV feeds at Chulalongkorn University, confirming stable real-time operation.

Ganokratanaa et al.[10]提出了一种深度时空翻译网络（DSTN），它将背景去除帧与光流融合以进行像素级定位。莫赖斯等人。proposed a deep spatiotemporal translation network (DSTN) that fuses background-removed frames with optical flow for pixel-level localization. Morais et al.[11]引入了 MPED-RNN，它使用多时间尺度循环架构从骨架轨迹中学习正常运动规律，并检测与预测关节位置的偏差作为异常。赖斯和霍申introduced MPED-RNN, which learns normal motion regularity from skeleton trajectories using a multi-timescale recurrent architecture and detects anomalies as deviations from predicted joint positions. Reiss and Hoshen[6]将姿态 (AlphaPose)、速度 (FlowNet2) 和深度特征 (CLIP) 与 GMM/kNN 密度估计相结合，实现了很高的精度，但由于多组件管道，吞吐量仅为 15 FPS。combined pose (AlphaPose), velocity (FlowNet2), and deep features (CLIP) with GMM/kNN density estimation, achieving strong accuracy but at a throughput of only 15 FPS due to the multi-component pipeline.

最近的视觉语言模型提供了一个有前途的替代方案。剪辑Recent vision-language models offer a promising alternative. CLIP[7]通过对比预训练将图像和文本映射到共享嵌入空间，从而通过自然语言提示实现零样本分类。吴等人。maps images and text into a shared embedding space through contrastive pre-training, enabling zero-shot classification via natural-language prompts. Wu et al.[9]开发了 VadCLIP，它使用可学习的提示采用 CLIP 进行弱监督视频异常检测。森山等人。developed VadCLIP, which adapts CLIP for weakly supervised video anomaly detection using learnable prompts. Moriyama et al.[12]提出了一种语言引导的决策覆盖，该决策覆盖采用 CLIP 进行免重新训练的异常检测。然而，这些方法通常离线运行，并不是为实时部署而设计的。我们的工作与这些方法共享视觉语言模型的使用proposed a language-guided decision override that adapts CLIP for retraining-free anomaly detection. However, these methods typically operate offline and are not designed for real-time deployment. Our work shares the use of vision-language models with these approaches[6, 9, 12];贡献在于系统集成和架构简化，证明直接 CLIP 余弦相似度可以取代多分量密度估计来进行实时人员级异常检测，而不是引入全新的评分机制。; the contribution lies in system integration and architectural simplification, demonstrating that direct CLIP cosine similarity can replace multi-component density estimation for real-time person-level anomaly detection rather than introducing a fundamentally new scoring mechanism.

在检测方面，YOLO v11n-poseOn the detection side, YOLO v11n-pose[8]使用具有紧凑 C3K2 模块的跨阶段部分网络在单次前向传递中执行人员检测和 17 个关键点回归，为实时监控提供有利的精度与速度平衡。performs person detection and seventeen-keypoint regression in a single forward pass using Cross-Stage Partial Networks with compact C3K2 blocks, providing a favorable accuracy-speed balance for real-time surveillance.

IV 实验装置Experimental Setup

IV-A 数据集Datasets

我们评估三个数据集：We evaluate on three datasets:中大大道CUHK Avenue [13]（37 个视频，来自固定大学通道摄像机的 30,652 帧），(37 videos, 30,652 frames from a fixed university pathway camera),上海科技大学校区ShanghaiTech Campus [14]（437 个视频，来自 13 个不同校园场景的 317,398 帧），以及(437 videos, 317,398 frames from 13 diverse campus scenes), andCU室内异常CU Indoor Anomaly，一个由 40 个视频（17,798 帧；11,841 正常，5,957 异常）组成的自定义数据集，从朱拉隆功大学四栋建筑的闭路电视摄像机中收集，并针对四种异常类别（跌倒、躺着、坐在地板上、打架）进行了帧级注释。数据集分为 33 个训练视频和 7 个测试视频。, a custom dataset of 40 videos (17,798 frames; 11,841 normal, 5,957 anomalous) collected from CCTV cameras in four buildings at Chulalongkorn University, with frame-level annotations for four anomaly categories (falling, lying, sitting on floor, fighting). The dataset is partitioned into 33 training and 7 test videos.

IV-B 实施细节Implementation Details

YOLO v11n-pose 是根据 COCO 预训练的权重进行初始化的，并在来自 CU Indoor Anomaly 的 2,864 个训练图像（从 1,154 个手动标记的帧中增加）上进行了 500 个时期的微调（YOLO v11n-pose is initialized from COCO-pretrained weights and fine-tuned on 2,864 training images (augmented from 1,154 manually labeled frames) from CU Indoor Anomaly for 500 epochs (640×640640\times 640输入，批次 16，SGD，lr = 0.01）在 NVIDIA Titan XP GPU 上。对于CUHK Avenue和上海科技大学，使用相同的微调模型； COCO 预训练为不同场景中的人物检测提供了足够的泛化能力，CU 室内微调主要提高了室内姿势的边界框精度。 CLIP ViT-B/32 与预先训练的权重一起使用，无需微调；投入作物的大小调整为input, batch 16, SGD with lr = 0.01) on an NVIDIA Titan XP GPU. For CUHK Avenue and ShanghaiTech, the same fine-tuned model is used; COCO pretraining provides sufficient generalization for person detection across diverse scenes, and the CU Indoor fine-tuning primarily improves bounding-box precision for indoor poses. CLIP ViT-B/32 is used with pre-trained weights without fine-tuning; input crops are resized to224×224224\times 224遵循标准 CLIP 预处理。我们注意到，这项工作中的术语“零样本”特指基于 CLIP 的异常评分组件，它不需要标记的异常示例； YOLO 检测器受到人员定位的监督。following the standard CLIP preprocessing. We note that the term “zero-shot” in this work refers specifically to the CLIP-based anomaly scoring component, which requires no labeled anomaly examples; the YOLO detector is supervised for person localization.

IV-C 评估协议Evaluation Protocol

我们遵循异常检测文献中的既定做法，采用接收者操作特征曲线下的帧级面积（AUROC）作为主要评估指标We adopt the frame-level area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) as the primary evaluation metric, following established practice in the anomaly detection literature[6, 13, 14]。 AUROC 量化随机采样的异常帧比随机采样的正常帧获得更高异常分数的概率。对于 YOLO v11n-pose，我们还报告了在 CU 室内异常测试分区上评估的 IoU 阈值 0.5 (mAP@.5) 和 0.5:0.95 (mAP@.5:.95) 下的精度、召回率和平均精度。吞吐量以单个 NVIDIA Titan XP GPU 上每秒的端到端帧数来衡量，包括所有预处理、推理和后处理阶段。. AUROC quantifies the probability that a randomly sampled anomalous frame receives a higher anomaly score than a randomly sampled normal frame. For YOLO v11n-pose, we additionally report precision, recall, and mean average precision at IoU thresholds of 0.5 (mAP@.5) and 0.5:0.95 (mAP@.5:.95), evaluated on the CU Indoor Anomaly test partition. Throughput is measured as end-to-end frames per second on a single NVIDIA Titan XP GPU, inclusive of all preprocessing, inference, and post-processing stages.

VI 讨论Discussion

所提出的框架的主要优点是其架构简单。通过直接通过 CLIP 路由人物裁剪以实现文本图像相似性，该管道消除了光流提取 (FlowNet2)、独立骨架估计 (AlphaPose) 和迭代密度模型拟合 (GMM/kNN)，从而将系统减少为两个神经网络模型。这将吞吐量从 15.32 FPS 加速到 51.39 FPS，同时只需要一个 GPU 和两个模型检查点进行部署。The principal strength of the proposed framework is its architectural simplicity. By routing person crops directly through CLIP for text-image similarity, the pipeline eliminates optical flow extraction (FlowNet2), standalone skeleton estimation (AlphaPose), and iterative density model fitting (GMM/kNN), reducing the system to two neural network models. This accelerates throughput from 15.32 to 51.39 FPS while requiring only a single GPU and two model checkpoints for deployment.

文本提示机制提供了实用的灵活性：使系统适应新的监控环境只需更新提示集，无需重新训练。 CLIP 的零样本功能进一步能够泛化训练数据中缺少的异常类别，前提是它们可以用自然语言描述。该系统部署在朱拉隆功大学 Charoen Wisawakam 大楼 12 楼电梯区域的 5 个实时闭路电视信号上，实现了每个信号约 10 FPS 的稳定运行，包括头顶视频捕获、实时边界框覆盖以及异常分数图的并发更新。The text-prompt mechanism provides practical flexibility: adapting the system to a new surveillance environment requires only updating the prompt set, with no retraining. CLIP’s zero-shot capability further enables generalization to anomaly categories absent from the training data, provided they can be described in natural language. The system was deployed on five live CCTV feeds in the elevator area on the 12th floor of the Charoen Wisawakam Building at Chulalongkorn University, achieving stable operation at approximately 10 FPS per feed, including overhead video capture, real-time bounding-box overlays, and concurrent updates to anomaly score graphs.

然而，当前的系统是专门针对受控室内环境中的人员异常情况而设计的。不涉及人员的异常（例如车辆入侵）或四个固定提示未涵盖的开放式异常类型，在生产部署中需要补充检测模块。固定提示设计是为了零培训可部署性和可解释性而特意选择的：操作员无需机器学习专业知识即可检查和修改提示集。可学习的提示扩展，包括法学硕士生成的候选人提示，被推迟到未来的工作。However, the current system is designed specifically for person-level anomalies in controlled indoor settings. Anomalies that do not involve people, such as vehicle intrusion, or open-set anomaly types not covered by the four fixed prompts, would require complementary detection modules in a production deployment. The fixed-prompt design was chosen deliberately for zero-training deployability and interpretability: operators can inspect and modify the prompt set without machine-learning expertise. Learnable prompt expansion, including LLM-generated candidate prompts, is deferred to future work.

七VII结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种两阶段异常检测框架，将 YOLO v11n-pose 与 CLIP ViT-B/32 结合起来，取代了需要光流和密度估计的多组件管道。关键发现是通过 CLIP 的直接文本-图像相似度可以作为一种有效的评分机制，实现This paper presented a two-stage anomaly detection framework that couples YOLO v11n-pose with CLIP ViT-B/32, replacing multi-component pipelines that require optical flow and density estimation. The key finding is that direct text-image similarity via CLIP can serve as an effective scoring mechanism, achieving a3.36×3.36\times吞吐量增益 (51.39 FPS)，同时在既定基准上保持可比较的 AUROC，并提高目标室内数据集的性能 (84.13%)。朱拉隆功大学实时闭路电视直播的部署证实了每个直播的稳定运行速度超过 10 FPS。throughput gain (51.39 FPS) while maintaining comparable AUROC on established benchmarks and improving performance on the targeted indoor dataset (84.13%). Deployment on live CCTV feeds at Chulalongkorn University confirmed stable operation at over 10 FPS per feed.

当前的设计有局限性。固定的四个文本提示集限制了可检测的异常类别，如较低的上海科技大学校园 AUROC (70.26%) 所反映的，其中骑自行车和车辆入侵等异常情况不在提示覆盖范围内。此外，YOLO 检测器在室内场景上进行了微调，这可能会减少对明显不同环境的泛化。The current design has limitations. The fixed set of four text prompts constrains the detectable anomaly categories, as reflected in the lower ShanghaiTech Campus AUROC (70.26%) where anomalies such as cycling and vehicle intrusion fall outside prompt coverage. Additionally, the YOLO detector is fine-tuned on indoor scenes, which may reduce generalization to markedly different environments.

受 VadCLIP 的启发，未来的工作将通过使用可学习的提示扩展来解决这些限制Future work will address these limitations by using learnable prompt expansion, inspired by VadCLIP[9]，扩大异常覆盖范围；针对低光环境的光照稳健预处理；开发适合在资源受限的边缘设备上部署的统一端到端模型。, to broaden anomaly coverage; illumination-robust preprocessing for low-light environments; and developing a unified end-to-end model suitable for deployment on resource-constrained edge devices.
