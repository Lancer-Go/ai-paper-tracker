法力Mana：灵巧地操纵铰接工具: Dexterous Manipulation of Articulated Tools

摘要Abstract

由于需要协调内部自由度和丰富的接触交互，铰接式工具操作仍然是灵巧机器人技术的主要挑战。虽然之前的工作主要集中在刚性物体上，但由于其物理复杂性和学习功能操作策略的困难，铰接式工具的使用仍未得到充分探索。我们提出了 Mana（Manipulation Animator），这是一个通用的模拟真实框架，它将灵巧的操作重新解释为动画问题。受计算机动画的启发，Mana 采用从粗到细的流程，通过运动规划和强化学习将程序生成的抓取关键帧转换为操作轨迹。数据生成过程很大程度上是自动的，只需点击几下鼠标即可指定功能可供性（每个工具<1 分钟）。通过跨越不同尺度和关节类型的四种铰接工具，Mana 实现了抓取和手动操作的零次模拟到真实的转换，展示了灵巧铰接工具使用的可扩展方法。Articulated tool manipulation remains a major challenge in dexterous robotics due to the need to coordinate internal degrees of freedom and contact-rich interactions. While prior work has largely focused on rigid objects, articulated tool use remains underexplored because of its physical complexity and the difficulty of learning functional manipulation strategies. We present Mana (Manipulation Animator), a general sim-to-real framework that reinterprets dexterous manipulation as an animation problem. Inspired by computer animation, Mana employs a coarse-to-fine pipeline that transforms procedurally-generated grasp keyframes into manipulation trajectories through motion planning and reinforcement learning. The data generation process is largely automatic, requiring only a few mouse clicks to specify functional affordances (<1 minute per tool). Across four articulated tools spanning different scales and joint types, Mana achieves zero-shot sim-to-real transfer for both grasping and in-hand manipulation, demonstrating a scalable approach to dexterous articulated tool use.

1 简介Introduction

人类环境中许多最有用的工具不是刚性物体，而是铰接机构。它们要求用户不仅要抓住工具，还要驱动其内部自由度，同时保持与薄的移动手柄的稳定接触。此功能对于许多实际操作任务至关重要：用钳子夹起小物体、将衣夹夹在表面上、用钳子剪断电线或推动注射器柱塞来分配液体。尽管机器人抓取和刚体灵巧操作取得了快速进展Many of the most useful tools in human environments are not rigid objects, but articulated mechanisms. They require the user not only to grasp the tool, but also to actuate its internal degrees of freedom while maintaining stable contact with thin, moving handles. This capability is central to many practical manipulation tasks: picking up small objects with tongs, clipping a clothespin onto a surface, cutting a wire with pliers, or pushing a syringe plunger to dispense fluid. Despite rapid progress in robotic grasping and rigid-body dexterous manipulation[2, 27, 55, 24, 19]，用多指手自主抓取和操纵铰接工具仍然在很大程度上尚未解决。, autonomous grasping and manipulation of articulated tools with multi-fingered hands remains largely unsolved.

Forces that stably actuate the tool are not always aligned with the local surface normals, making the grasp prone to slipping. In contrast, forces aligned with the surface normals are less likely to slip, but can induce undesired accelerations or rotations of the tool body, ultimately destabilizing the grasp. This tradeoff becomes even harder as multiple fingers and the articulated tool form a tightly coupled dynamic system constrained by complex hand kinematics. Small execution errors at one finger can change contact locations, alter moment arms, and destabilize the entire interaction. Finally, many tools require substantial force: cutting a wire with pliers or pushing a syringe plunger can require force magnitudes large enough to amplify both slippage and dynamic instability. Successful tool use therefore requires choosing contact locations and force directions that balance manipulation effectiveness, grasp stability, friction, moment arms, and hand kinematic feasibility as the tool configuration changes over time.

3 法力数据系统Mana Data System

Mana 通过从粗到细的管道生成灵巧的铰接工具操作数据。如图所示Mana generates dexterous articulated-tool manipulation data through a coarse-to-fine pipeline. As illustrated in Fig.3，系统首先构建一组抓取关键帧，定义操作序列的骨架，包括预抓取状态、稳定抓取状态和手中驱动状态。然后，它使用适合每个阶段的最简单机制在这些关键帧之间生成轨迹：用于几何到达运动的运动规划和用于需要协调位置力控制的接触丰富过渡的强化学习。这种分解避免了端到端强化学习的探索负担，同时保留了功能工具使用所需的丰富接触行为。 Mana 的输入是一个工具网格，具有铰接关节模型和少量人工提供的功能可供性注释。输出是成功模拟操作轨迹的数据集，每个轨迹都包含手腕运动、手部关节命令、对象状态和语义阶段标签（例如抓取、打开和关闭）。这些轨迹随后用于训练第 2 节中描述的视觉运动策略。, the system first constructs a set of grasp keyframes that define the skeleton of the manipulation sequence, including pre-grasp states, stable grasp states, and in-hand actuation states. It then generates trajectories between these keyframes using the simplest mechanism appropriate for each phase: motion planning for geometric reaching motions and reinforcement learning for contact-rich transitions that require coordinated position-force control. This decomposition avoids the exploration burden of end-to-end RL while preserving the contact-rich behaviors needed for functional tool use. The input to Mana is a tool mesh with an articulated joint model and a small amount of human-provided functional affordance annotation. The output is a dataset of successful simulated manipulation trajectories, each containing wrist motion, hand joint commands, object state, and semantic phase labels such as grasping, opening, and closing. These trajectories are later used to train the visuomotor policy described in Sec.4.2.

3.1 抓取发生器Grasp Generator

抓取生成器将稀疏的功能可供性注释转换为一组密集的物理上合理的抓取和驱动状态。对于每个工具，用户在 3D 界面中标记标准工具姿势和网格的功能区域，例如钳子的手柄、钳子的臂或注射器的柱塞和针筒。标记过程很快，每个实例只需不到 1 分钟。我们相信这个过程可以通过最近的可供性模型进一步自动化The grasp generator converts sparse functional affordance annotations into a dense set of physically plausible grasp and actuation states. For each tool, the user marks the canonical tool pose and the functional regions of the mesh in a 3D interface, such as the handles of pliers, the arms of tongs, or the plunger and barrel of a syringe. The labeling process is fast and takes less than 1 minute for each instance. We believe the process can be further automated by recent affordance models[45]。然后，我们以 Lightning Grasp 管道为基础. Then, we build upon the Lightning Grasp pipeline[52]通过新的碰撞感知 IK 流程（附录 A.1），在存在环境障碍的情况下使用这些注释生成功能性抓握。给定注释，我们查询接触字段以在带注释的表面上构建接触域。然后，我们优化这些域内的指尖接触位置和手指关节位置。输出抓点经过过滤以确保 IsaacLab 模拟中的稳定性to generate functional grasps with these annotations in the presence of environmental obstacles via a new collision-aware IK process (Appendix A.1). Given the annotations, we query contact field to construct contact domains over the annotated surface. We then optimize fingertip contact locations within these domains and the finger joint positions. The output grasps are filtered for stability in IsaacLab simulation[29].

与刚性物体的抓取生成不同，铰接式工具操纵不仅需要稳定的抓取，还需要一系列抓取以供未来驱动。因此，关键帧规划器会跨功能接触区域和相关工具配置对一组密集的抓取状态进行采样。例如，对于注射器，它会采样支持稳定柱塞驱动的不同状态。这种状态空间的密集覆盖非常重要，因为铰接工具的使用对接触位置的毫米级变化高度敏感。生成的关键帧定义了 Mana 轨迹图的节点，包括预抓取、抓取和手动驱动关键帧。Unlike grasp generation for rigid objects, articulated-tool manipulation requires not only a stable grasp, but a chain of grasps for future actuation. Therefore, the keyframe planner samples a dense set of grasp states across the functional contact regions and across relevant tool configurations. For example, for a syringe, it samples diverse states that support stable plunger actuation. This dense coverage of state space is important because articulated tool use is highly sensitive to millimeter-scale changes in contact location. The resulting keyframes define the nodes of the Mana trajectory graph, including pre-grasp, grasp, and in-hand actuation keyframes.

4 机器人系统Robot System

Mana 数据系统会生成模拟轨迹，但现实世界中铰接工具的使用也很大程度上取决于硬件设计、感知和策略执行。我们的机器人系统由灵巧的操纵器平台和完全根据 Mana 生成的模拟数据训练的点云条件扩散策略组成。The Mana data system produces simulation trajectories, but real-world articulated tool use also depends critically on hardware design, perception, and policy execution. Our robot system consists of a dexterous manipulator platform and a point-cloud-conditioned diffusion policy trained entirely from Mana-generated simulation data.

4.1 灵巧机械手平台Dexterous Manipulator Platform

我们的平台使用配备 16 自由度 Allegro 手的 7 自由度 xArm7 机器人手臂。铰接式工具对手部提出了异常严格的几何和力要求。我们实验中的许多工具都有细手柄或窄接触面，物体厚度约为 1 厘米。标准的半球形刚性指尖通常会在这种几何形状上产生不稳定的点接触，导致在用力致动期间滑动或工具弹出。Our platform uses a 7-DoF xArm7 robot arm equipped with a 16-DoF Allegro hand. Articulated tools impose unusually strict geometric and force requirements on the hand. Many of the tools in our experiments have thin handles or narrow contact surfaces, with object thicknesses on the order of 1 cm. Standard hemispherical rigid fingertips often create unstable point contacts on such geometries, leading to slip or tool ejection during forceful actuation.

为了解决这一限制，我们设计了具有平坦、柔顺接触表面的定制指尖。当按压薄工具零件时，指尖轮廓增加了可用的接触面积，而柔软的硅胶层在工具表面周围被动变形。这种顺应性有助于补偿小的位姿误差，并在高压驱动期间更稳健地分布法向力。To address this limitation, we design custom fingertips with a flattened, compliant contact surface. The fingertip profile increases the available contact patch when pressing against thin tool parts, while the soft silicone layer passively deforms around the tool surface. This compliance helps compensate for small pose errors and distributes normal forces more robustly during high-pressure actuation.

6 局限性Limitations

我们在这项工作中发现了以下局限性。首先，由于电机强度不足（最大扭矩为 0.7 Nm，最长指连杆为 0.06-0.07 m），我们的系统无法处理所需力或激活阈值超过 10 N 的常见刚性工具使用情况（例如触发机构）。此外，我们专注于精确抓取，并没有像人类那样探索基于动力抓取的工具使用策略，因为 Allegro 手明显更大（2 倍人手大小），因此很难使用动力抓取来握住和驱动专为人手设计的细手柄。我们相信这些限制可以在未来的工作中通过改进硬件设计来解决。在感知方面，检测和响应遮挡下的滑动以及操纵非常小的工具仍然具有挑战性。这些任务需要高频触觉和力感应来解决指尖切向摩擦的细微变化。最后，我们使用手腕遥操作来组合不同的阶段来测试技能链的可行性，并且我们需要额外的策略来使技能执行在未来完全自主。We identify the following limitations in this work. First, due to insufficient motor strength (maximum torque of 0.7 Nm and a longest finger link of 0.06–0.07 m), our system cannot handle common stiff tool-use cases where the required force or activation threshold exceeds 10 N (e.g., trigger mechanisms). In addition, we focus on precision grasps and do not explore power-grasp-based tool use strategies as humans do, since the Allegro hand is significantly larger (2× human hand size), making it difficult to hold and actuate thin handles designed for human hands using power grasp. We believe these limitations can be addressed in future work with improved hardware design. On the perception side, detecting and responding to slip under occlusion, as well as manipulating very small tools, remains challenging. These tasks require high-frequency tactile and force sensing to resolve subtle changes in tangential friction at the fingertips. Finally, we used wrist teleoperation to combine different phases to test the feasibility of skill chaining, and we need additional policy to make the skill execution fully autonomous in the future.
