摘要Abstract

我们研究 on-policy self-distillation (OPSD)，其中语言模型通过沿其自己的 on-policy 轨迹蒸馏特权教师分布来提高其推理能力。尽管 OPSD 取得了性能提升，但我们发现教师和学生的反应之间存在一个常见但经常被忽视的不匹配：自我反思的教师反应可能会因反思引起的偏差和反应模板而改变，从而导致令牌级别监督的校准错误。为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了 OGLS-SD，一种以结果为导向的逻辑引导框架，利用可验证的结果奖励来对比成功和失败的政策轨迹并校准教师逻辑。通过 Logit 引导将结果级正确性与密集标记级指导相结合，OGLS-SD 稳定了自蒸馏，并提高了跨不同基准的标准 OPSD 和其他变体的推理性能。We study on-policy self-distillation (OPSD), where a language model improves its reasoning ability by distilling privileged teacher distributions along its own on-policy trajectories. Despite the performance gains of OPSD, we identify a common but often overlooked mismatch between teacher and student responses: self-reflected teacher responses can be shifted by reflection-induced bias and response templates, leading to miscalibrated token-level supervision. To mitigate this issue, we propose OGLS-SD, an outcome-guided logit-steering framework that leverages verifiable outcome rewards to contrast successful and failed on-policy trajectories and calibrate teacher logits. By combining outcome-level correctness with dense token-level guidance through logit steering, OGLS-SD stabilizes self-distillation and improves reasoning performance over standard OPSD and other variants across diverse benchmarks.

1 简介Introduction

随着大型语言模型（LLM）的快速进步，具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR；With the rapid progress of large language models (LLMs), reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR;Shao et al. 2024; Zhu et al. 2025; Zheng et al. 2025）已成为LLM后期培训期间提高推理能力的中心范例。然而，许多 RLVR 方法仅提供稀疏的序列级监督，其中采样轨迹中的所有标记都根据相同的结果奖励进行优化。这种粗略的监督使得细粒度的代币级信用分配变得困难，并可能导致学习效率低下) has become a central paradigm for improving reasoning ability during LLM post-training. However, many RLVR methods provide only sparse, sequence-level supervision, where all tokens in a sampled trajectory are optimized according to the same outcome reward. This coarse supervision makes fine-grained token-level credit assignment difficult and can lead to inefficient learning(Zhao et al., 2026).

为了解决这个限制，To address this limitation,在政策蒸馏on-policy distillation（OPD；(OPD;Agarwal et al. 2024）最近成为一种有前途的替代范式，其中学生模型利用其自己的策略生成来获得密集的代币级指导。与RLVR相比，OPD往往可以实现更快更强的改进) has recently emerged as a promising alternative paradigm, in which the student model leverages its own on-policy generations to obtain dense token-level guidance. Compared with RLVR, OPD can often achieve faster and stronger improvements(Agarwal et al., 2024; Lu and Lab, 2025; Gu et al., 2024; Ko et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026b)，并已在 MiMo 等工业培训后管道中采用或探索, and has been adopted or explored in industrial post-training pipelines such as MiMo(Team et al., 2026), Qwen3 (Yang et al., 2025),GLM5, GLM5(GLM-5-Team et al., 2026)和 DeepSeek-V4, and DeepSeek-V4(DeepSeek-AI, 2026).

在 OPD 成功的推动下，越来越多的工作利用了法学硕士的自我反思Motivated by the success of OPD, a growing line of work has leveraged LLMs’ self-reflection(Shinn et al., 2023)和自我判断and self-judgment(Zheng et al., 2023)能够在不依赖更大的教师模型的情况下实现自我提高。此设置，称为abilities to enable self-improvement without relying on a larger teacher model. This setting, referred to as策略自蒸馏on-policy self-distillation(OPSD)，消除了外部教师指导，从而可以与 RLVR 进行更直接的比较。在 OPSD 中，学生模型本身充当老师，通常通过在训练期间调节外部特权信息来提供密集的令牌级指导，而不需要单独的更强的模型(OPSD), removes external teacher guidance and thus enables a more direct comparison with RLVR. In OPSD, the student model itself serves as the teacher, typically by conditioning on external privileged information during training to provide dense token-level guidance without requiring a separate stronger model(Zhao et al., 2026; Hübotter et al., 2026)。尽管此类方法可以提高性能和训练效率，但最近的研究表明普通 OPSD 可能会出现训练崩溃和不稳定的问题. Although such methods can improve both performance and training efficiency, recent studies have reported that vanilla OPSD may suffer from training collapse and instability(Yang et al., 2026a; Li et al., 2026a; Kim et al., 2026)。这些观察结果激发了将 OPSD 与 RLVR 相结合的混合方法。然而，这种崩溃背后的机制仍未得到充分探索。因此，现有的混合方法很大程度上相当于 OPSD 损失和 GRPO 损失的直接组合，因此无法充分利用 OPSD 提供的细粒度令牌级信号。这自然会引发以下问题：. As a result, existing hybrid methods largely amount to a straightforward combination of the OPSD loss and the GRPO loss, and therefore fail to fully exploit the fine-grained token-level signals provided by OPSD. This naturally raises the following question:

在本文中，我们假设 OPSD 崩溃是由于“教师”和“学生”反应之间的分布不匹配引起的，即使两者都来自相同的语言模型。我们确定了这种不匹配的两个关键根源。首先，当教师通过对特定推理路径的单个输出进行自我反思来构建其响应时，由此产生的监督可能会集中在狭窄的输出分布上，从而可能会侵蚀学生的原始能力In this paper, we hypothesize that OPSD collapse arises from a distributional mismatch between the “teacher” and “student” responses, even though both are from the same language model. 6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 OGLS-SD，这是一种基于策略的自我蒸馏框架，它根据成功和失败的推出构建结果引导的 logit 转向。而不是直接从单一物质中蒸馏 特权教师分布，OGLS-SD 对以结果为条件的推出池中的教师逻辑进行平均，并将它们进行对比，以减少特定于解决方案的偏差和特权信息引起的过度自信。 数学推理基准实验表明，OGLS-SD 比标准 OPSD 有所改进，并导致更稳定的自蒸馏。In this paper, we propose OGLS-SD, an on-policy self-distillation framework that constructs outcome-guided logit steering from successful and failed rollouts. Instead of directly distilling from a single privileged teacher distribution, OGLS-SD averages teacher logits over outcome-conditioned rollout pools and contrasts them to reduce solution-specific bias and privileged-information-induced overconfidence. Experiments on mathematical reasoning benchmarks show that OGLS-SD improves over standard OPSD and leads to more stable self-distillation.
