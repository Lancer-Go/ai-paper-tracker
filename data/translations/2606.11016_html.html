对法学硕士决策的肤浅信念Superficial Beliefs in LLM Decision-Making

Gabriel Freedman & Francesca Toni
Department of Computing
Imperial College London
{g.freedman22,ft}@imperial.ac.uk
摘要Abstract

我们询问大型语言模型（LLM）在两个选项之间进行选择时是否只是模仿基本原理，或者它们的选择是否反映了系统性的底层决策结构。使用合成二元决策设置，其中模型在分级属性定义的配置文件之间进行选择，我们将模型认为最重要的属性与最能解释其在符合先前决策的行为模型下的选择的属性进行比较。行为模型可以很好地预测保留的选择，这表明模型行为与可见属性系统相关，而不是随机的。然而，直接自我报告和单独的基于分数的判断只能部分恢复行为推断的驾驶员。由此产生的图像既不是一种任意行为，也不是一种完全明确的信念——输出的结构足以支持预测，但明确的原因只能不完美地跟踪恢复的驾驶员。这种定性模式在提示顺序和采样扰动、替代行为模型、有针对性的遮挡分析和结构变化的决策设置中持续存在。我们将其解释为法学硕士决策中“肤浅信念”的证据：模型的行为就好像受属性上的概率局部优先级的指导，而对驱动其决策的属性只有有限的口头访问。We ask whether large language models (LLMs) merely imitate rationales when choosing between two options, or whether their choices reflect a systematic underlying decision structure. Using synthetic binary decision settings in which models choose between profiles defined by graded attributes, we compare the attribute a model says mattered most with the attribute that best explains its choice under a behavioural model fit to prior decisions. The behavioural model predicts held-out choices well, showing that model behaviour is systematically related to the visible attributes rather than being random. However, direct self-reports and a separate score-based judge recover the behaviourally inferred driver only partially. The resulting picture is neither one of arbitrary behaviour nor one of fully articulated belief - outputs are structured enough to support prediction, but explicit reasons track the recovered driver only imperfectly. This qualitative pattern persists across prompt-order and sampling perturbations, alternative behavioural models, targeted occlusion analyses, and structurally varied decision settings. We interpret this as evidence for “superficial belief” in LLM decision-making: models behave as if guided by probabilistic local priorities over attributes, while having only limited verbal access to the attributes that drive their decisions.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型是否仅仅模仿信仰语言，或者它们有时值得类似信仰的解释？最近的哲学著作使这个问题更加尖锐。从表面主义的角度来看，一个系统是否相信某件事取决于其外在行为和相关处置的稳定模式，而不是其底层架构Do large language models merely imitate the language of belief, or do they sometimes deserve belief-like interpretation? Recent philosophical work has sharpened this question. From a superficialist perspective, whether a system believes something depends on stable patterns in its outward behaviour and related dispositions rather than on its underlying architecture(Schwitzgebel, 2025)。在这种方法中，问题不是通过询问模型是由什么构成的来解决的，而是通过询问其言语和行为显示出什么样的稳定模式来解决。. On this approach, the issue is not settled by asking what a model is made of, but by asking what sort of stable pattern its words and actions display.

本文讨论了该问题的限制版本。我们不会问法学硕士是否有一般信仰。我们询问，在简单的决策环境中，他们的选择是否表现出足够强大的局部结构来支持弱形式的“肤浅信念”。如果模型以类似的方式对类似的属性差异做出反应，并且这些趋势会推广到新的案例，那么至少有一个表面上的案例可以将其决策视为由类似信念的优先级指导，而不是任意的输出。另一个问题是这些优先事项是否也可以通过明确的启发来获得。This paper takes up a restricted version of that question. We do not ask whether LLMs have beliefs in general. We ask whether, in simple decision settings, their choices exhibit a local structure strong enough to support a weak form of “superficial belief”. If models respond to similar attribute differences in similar ways, and those tendencies generalise to new cases, then there is at least a prima facie case for treating their decisions as guided by belief-like priorities rather than as arbitrary outputs. A further question is whether those priorities are also available through explicit elicitation.

这很重要，因为法学硕士不仅可以做出决定，而且可以证明这些决定的合理性。模型可以说明This matters because LLMs not only make decisions, but can justify them. A model can state the最重要的most important其决策的一个方面，其推理仅与实际推动该选择的因素松散相关aspect of its decision, with the reasoning only loosely connected to what actually drove the choice(Turpin et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2025)。我们的目的是直接比较这两种现象。我们构建了由四个分级属性描述的两个选项之间的二元决策基准。我们使用法学硕士在这些决策问题上所做的选择来拟合简单的行为模型。然后，我们将模型的预测（选项和驱动选择的属性）与通过提示法学硕士并使用基于分数的判断方法直接得出的预测进行比较。. Our aim is to compare these two phenomena directly. We construct a benchmark of binary decisions between two options described by four graded attributes. We use the choices made by the LLMs on these decision problems to fit simple behavioural models. We then compare the models’ predictions - both of the option and the attribute driving the choice - with those elicited directly by prompting the LLM and also using a score-based judge approach.

结果支持中间立场。行为模型可以很好地预测跨主题和模型设置的保留选择，这表明这些选择足够系统化，足以支持所揭示的局部结构。与此同时，属性层面的一致性要弱得多：模型命名的最重要的属性仅部分与重建的决策驱动因素一致。控制属性很少被选择，并且相同的定性模式在替代重建和结构变化的决策设置下经受住扰动、干预和鲁棒性检查。因此，整体情况既不是一种随机行为，也不是一种完全明确的信念。相反，我们认为，它与法学硕士决策中弱的、局部决策形式的肤浅信念是一致的。The results support a middle position. The behavioural model predicts held-out choices well across themes and model settings, indicating that the choices are systematic enough to support a revealed local structure. At the same time, agreement at the attribute level is much weaker: the attribute the model names as most important aligns with reconstructed decision drivers only partially. Control attributes are rarely selected, and the same qualitative pattern survives perturbation, intervention, and robustness checks under alternative reconstructions and structurally varied decision settings. The overall picture is therefore neither one of random behaviour nor one of fully articulated belief. Instead, we argue, it is consistent with a weak, decision-local form of superficial belief in LLM decision-making.

具体来说，我们做出了三点贡献。首先，我们引入了二元决策问题的综合基准，该基准是根据共享属性结构构建的，并跨多个主题（包括控制变体）进行实例化。其次，我们提出了一种适合法学硕士选择的简单行为模型的操作方法，以便重建属性上的局部决策优先级，然后将这些重建的优先级与两种明确的启发方法进行比较，Concretely, we make three contributions. First, we introduce a synthetic benchmark of binary decision problems, built from a shared attribute structure and instantiated across multiple themes, including control variants. Second, we propose an operational methodology that fits simple behavioural models to LLM choices in order to reconstruct local decision priorities over attributes, and then compares those reconstructed priorities on held-out problems against two explicit elicitation methods,直接回应direct response和and评分制裁判score-based judge（见图(see Figure1）。最后，通过三个主要主题、两个控制主题、四个模型系列和八个总设置，再加上扰动分析、替代行为重建和结构上不同的设置，我们发现保留的选择比驱动这些选择的属性更加一致。). Finally, across three main themes, two control themes, four model families, and eight total settings, together with perturbation analyses, alternative behavioural reconstructions, and a structurally different setting, we find that held-out choices are more aligned than the attributes driving those choices.

5 讨论和未来的工作Discussion and Future Work

我们的结果仅引起对肤浅信念的谨慎主张。第一个教训是，在我们确定的任务中，法学硕士的决定既不是任意的，也不是完全透明的。保留选择预测、低控制属性选择率、扰动证据、干预分析和结构不同的决策设置中的稳健性检查都表明有意义的局部决策结构。但这种结构最好被描述为概率性和局部性，而不是固定的隐性偏好：直接报告只能部分恢复所揭示的驱动因素，并且提示变量和重复样本之间的精确一致性并不常见。Our results invite only a cautious claim about superficial beliefs. A first lesson is that, in the tasks we identified, LLM decisions are neither arbitrary nor fully transparent. Held-out choice prediction, low control attribute selection rates, perturbation evidence, intervention analyses, and robustness checks in a structurally different decision setting all indicate a meaningful local decision structure. But that structure is better described as probabilistic and local than as a fixed implicit preference: direct reports recover the revealed driver only partially, and exact agreement across prompt variants and repeated samples is uncommon.

第二个教训是再现性和恢复率是分开的。基于（争论性）分数的判断比直接的 LLM 响应更具可重复性，但它并不能更准确地恢复行为模型目标。对于选择来说，情况显然更糟，而对于属性来说，情况大致相似。这不应被解读为对基于论证的方法的拒绝。在法官所基于的原始ArgLLM框架中，论证的要点是通过结构化的中间表示和形式聚合使决策更具可解释性和可争议性，而新的工作将这一想法扩展到更一般的决策支持和全球可争议性A second lesson is that reproducibility and recovery come apart. The (argumentative) score-based judge is more reproducible than the direct LLM responses, but it does not recover the behavioural model targets more accurately. For choices it is clearly worse, and for attributes it is broadly similar. This should not be read as a rejection of argumentation-based methods. In the original ArgLLM framework the judge is based on, the point of argumentation is to make decisions more explainable and contestable through structured intermediate representations and formal aggregation, and newer work extends this idea to more general decision support and global contestability(Freedman et al., 2025; Dejl et al., 2026)。有鉴于此，我们的基于分数的判断可能不是作为潜在优先事项的特权读出，而是作为稳定且有争议的决策框架。. In that light, our score-based judge may be useful less as a privileged readout of latent priorities than as a stable and contestable scaffold for decision-making.

因此，我们的研究结果仅支持一种薄弱的、局部决策形式的肤浅信念。从肤浅的观点来看，信念归因取决于外在行为的稳定模式，而不是隐藏的内部结构Our findings therefore support only a weak, decision-local form of superficial belief. On a superficialist view, belief attribution depends on stable patterns in outward behaviour rather than hidden internal architecture(Schwitzgebel, 2025)。我们的结果以有限的方式符合这一情况：在综合二元决策设置中，模型的行为就好像受可见属性的局部优先级引导，并且在结构不同的决策设置中仍然存在相同的定性分离。但证据仍然缺乏更有力的主张。优先级并不是完全不变的，它们只能在显式启发方法中部分恢复，并且我们没有表明它们与更广泛的世界模型承诺相关或泛化到一小类合成配对任务之外。从这个意义上说，结果更接近于弱行为倾向，而不是完全明确的信念状态，并且仍然缺乏更强的信念归因标准. Our results fit that picture in a limited way: within synthetic binary decision settings, models behave as if guided by local priorities over visible attributes, and the same qualitative dissociation persists in a structurally different decision setting. But the evidence still falls short of stronger claims. The priorities are not perfectly invariant, they are only partly recoverable in explicit elicitation methods, and we do not show that they connect to broader world-model commitments or generalise beyond a small class of synthetic pairwise tasks. In this sense, the results are closer to a weak behavioural disposition than to a fully articulated belief state, and they remain short of stronger standards for belief attribution(Herrmann and Levinstein, 2025).

研究范围仍然存在一些局限性，这表明了未来的工作。具体来说，尽管主要模式在（i）替代行为重建、（ii）额外干预设置和（iii）结构上不同的决策设置中幸存下来，但证据仍然是综合的，并且集中在二元概况比较任务上。因此，对更丰富、更少程式化的决策设置的更广泛的概括仍然是开放的。Some limitations remain concerning the scope of the study, pointing to future work. Specifically, although the main pattern survives (i) alternative behavioural reconstructions, (ii) additional intervention settings, and (iii) a structurally different decision setting, the evidence is still synthetic and centred on binary profile comparison tasks. Broader generalisation to richer and less stylised decision settings therefore remains open.
