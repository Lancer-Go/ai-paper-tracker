虚构世界构建：多代理法学硕士协作与分层上下文压缩和迭代审查Fictional Worldbuilding: Multi-Agent LLM Collaboration with Hierarchical Context Compression and Iterative Review

摘要。Abstract.

世界构建，即构建连贯的虚构世界，是游戏设计和文学创作的一项基本任务。大型语言模型（LLM）为自动化内容生成提供了新的可能性，但它们在世界构建中的应用面临三个挑战：随着构建过程线性增长的上下文爆炸、创意多样性和内容一致性之间的紧张关系以及缺乏自动化质量保证。Worldbuilding, the construction of coherent fictional worlds, is a foundational task in game design and literary creation. Large Language Models (LLMs) offer new possibilities for automated content generation, but their application to worldbuilding faces three challenges: context explosion that grows linearly with the building process, the tension between creative diversity and content consistency, and the absence of automated quality assurance.

本文介绍了 AutoWorldBuilder，这是一个多智能体协作系统，通过五个集成组件解决这些挑战：具有冲突检测功能的结构化概念网络；基于 DAG 的混合批处理调度程序，按语义局部性对任务进行分组；四层上下文压缩机制，实现约 90% 的令牌减少；由专门的审核员代理组成的迭代审核系统，可将提案通过率从 42% 提高到 85% 以上；以及支持零代码扩展和差异化温度配置的技能驱动代理架构。This paper presents AutoWorldBuilder, a multi-agent collaborative system that addresses these challenges through five integrated components: a structured concept network with conflict detection; a DAG-based hybrid batch scheduler that groups tasks by semantic locality; a four-layer context compression mechanism achieving approximately 90% token reduction; an iterative review system with specialized Auditor agents that improves proposal pass rates from 42% to over 85%; and a skill-driven agent architecture supporting zero-code extension with differentiated temperature configuration.

使用 GPT-OSS 120B 和 DeepSeek v3.2 作为 LLM 后端，针对 20 个不同的世界构建任务进行的两项实验显示出 95.0% 的成功率。该系统在 18-31 分钟内为每个世界生成了 56-103 个自洽概念，审核管道未检测到任何冲突。这里验证的架构模式，包括层预算压缩、语义局部调度以及生成和审查分离，可以转移到更广泛的知识密集型、多代理 LLM 应用程序。Two experiments across 20 diverse worldbuilding tasks, using GPT-OSS 120B and DeepSeek v3.2 as LLM backends, demonstrate a 95.0% success rate. The system generated 56–103 self-consistent concepts per world in 18–31 minutes, with no conflicts detected by the review pipeline. The architectural patterns validated here, including layer-as-budget compression, semantic-locality scheduling, and separation of generation and review, may transfer to the broader class of knowledge-intensive, multi-agent LLM applications.

A complete, self-consistent fictional world typically encompasses multiple dimensions, including geography, intelligent races, magic or technology systems, social organizations, historical events, and cultural customs, and contains dozens to hundreds of interrelated concepts. However, when directly applying a single LLM to complex worldbuilding tasks, we observed three fundamental technical challenges that constitute the core research problems of this paper.

研究Research(undefz, )已经发现了处理长上下文的法学硕士中的“迷失在中间”现象，其中模型对文档中间信息的召回准确性明显低于开头和结尾的信息。这意味着即使上下文窗口几乎无法容纳所有历史概念，模型也很难有效地利用这些信息。长上下文还会带来高昂的 API 调用成本和推理延迟，这使得它们对于需要数百次迭代的构建任务来说过于昂贵。has identified the “Lost in the Middle” phenomenon in LLMs processing long contexts, where the model’s recall accuracy for information in the middle of a document is significantly lower than for the beginning and end. This means that even if the context window could barely accommodate all historical concepts, the model would struggle to effectively utilize this information. Long contexts also incur high API call costs and inference latency, making them prohibitively expensive for construction tasks requiring hundreds of iterations.

现有方法，例如检索增强生成 (RAG)Existing approaches such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)(undefx, )通过检索相关段落来减轻上下文压力，但简单的关键字匹配无法捕获概念之间隐含的语义关联。例如，在生成“龙骑士训练场”时，系统需要了解其与“龙族”、“骑士团”、“魔法学院”等概念的关系，而不仅仅是检索包含“龙骑士”关键字的文档。mitigate context pressure by retrieving relevant passages, but simple keyword matching fails to capture implicit semantic associations between concepts. For example, when generating a “Dragon Knight Training Ground,” the system needs to understand its relationship to concepts such as “Dragon Clan,” “Knightly Orders,” and “Magic Academy,” not merely retrieve documents containing the keyword “Dragon Knight.”

挑战二：创意多样性与逻辑一致性之间的紧张关系Challenge 2: The Tension Between Creative Diversity and Logical Consistency

世界建设既需要创造性的多样性，又需要内部的一致性。多样性意味着每个精灵种族不应该仅仅是托尔金精灵的翻版；一致性意味着第一章中为魔法系统制定的规则必须在第十章中仍然适用。这两个目标本质上是矛盾的。Worldbuilding demands both creative diversity and internal consistency. Diversity means that each elven race should not be a mere copy of Tolkien’s elves; consistency means that the rules established for a magic system in Chapter 1 must still hold in Chapter 10. These two goals are inherently contradictory.

引入多个专门代理来并行生成内容是解决多样性问题的自然方法。然而，多智能体并行性带来了新的协调挑战。在实践中，我们观察到：（1）风格碎片化：科幻设计师的“量子传送门”和奇幻设计师的“魔法传送阵”共存于同一个世界； （2）概念冲突：地理设计师定义“精灵居住在北部森林”，而种族设计师定义“精灵王国位于南部沙漠”； (3)信息孤岛：智能体无法感知彼此的输出，导致概念重复或关键依赖缺失。Introducing multiple specialized agents to generate content in parallel is a natural approach to addressing diversity. However, multi-agent parallelism introduces new coordination challenges. In practice, we observed: (1) stylistic fragmentation: the sci-fi designer’s “Quantum Portal” and the fantasy designer’s “Magical Teleportation Array” coexist in the same world; (2) conceptual conflicts: the geography designer defines “elves inhabit the northern forests” while the race designer generates “the Elven Kingdom is located in the southern desert”; (3) information silos: agents cannot perceive each other’s output, leading to duplicated concepts or missing critical dependencies.

简单地提高生成温度可以增强多样性，但会放大一致性问题；降低温度可以提高稳定性，但会导致输出均匀。这就需要一种既能激发多样化创造力又能确保全球一致性的架构机制。Simply increasing the generation temperature can enhance diversity but amplifies consistency problems; decreasing temperature improves stability but results in homogeneous outputs. This necessitates an architectural mechanism that can both stimulate diverse creativity and ensure global consistency.

挑战 3：自动化质量检测和保证Challenge 3: Automated Quality Detection and Assurance

法学硕士生成内容的质量控制是一项公开的挑战。传统的“人工审核”在自动化施工场景中是不切实际的。如果系统在 18 分钟内生成 100 个概念，则对每个概念进行手动审核将需要数小时。然而，仅仅依靠LLM自我评估会遭受“自我认可偏差”Quality control of LLM-generated content is an open challenge. Traditional “manual review” is impractical in automated construction scenarios. If a system generates 100 concepts in 18 minutes, manual review of each would take hours. However, relying solely on LLM self-evaluation suffers from “Self-Approval Bias”(undefaa, )，即使存在明显的逻辑问题，模型也倾向于给自己的输出打高分。, where models tend to give high scores to their own outputs even when obvious logical problems exist.

世界构建还涉及多个专业领域的知识，单一模型无法具备所有相关的专家判断能力。冲突检测需要跨概念的全局视角。例如，“精灵寿命500岁”和“精灵王国成立仅100年”单独来看没有问题，但放在一起就构成了逻辑矛盾。Worldbuilding also involves knowledge from multiple specialized domains, and a single model cannot possess all relevant expert judgment capabilities. Conflict detection requires a cross-concept global perspective. For example, “elves live 500 years” and “the Elven Kingdom was established only 100 years ago” are individually unproblematic but constitute a logical contradiction when combined.

宪法人工智能等现有工作Existing work such as Constitutional AI(undefd, )通过预先设定的规则来约束生成行为，但世界构建的“规则”很难提前一一列举。奇幻世界的魔法规则和科幻世界的技术原理本质上是不同的，需要在构建过程中动态学习和检测。constrains generation behavior through preset rules, but the “rules” of worldbuilding are difficult to enumerate exhaustively in advance. The magical rules of a fantasy world and the technological principles of a science-fiction world are inherently different, requiring dynamic learning and detection during the construction process.

1.3. 研究目标Research Objectives

上述三个挑战是相互关联的：不适当的上下文管理加剧了一致性问题，不充分的一致性检测破坏了质量保证，质量保证机制本身可能会引入额外的上下文开销。因此，我们提出一个中心研究问题：The three challenges above are interrelated: improper context management exacerbates consistency problems, insufficient consistency detection undermines quality assurance, and quality assurance mechanisms themselves may introduce additional context overhead. We therefore pose a central research question:

我们如何设计一个多智能体协作系统，实现LLM驱动的自动化世界构建，同时确保生成质量和一致性？How can we design a multi-agent collaborative system that achieves LLM-driven automated worldbuilding while ensuring generation quality and consistency?

解决这个问题需要系统层面的创新，而不是对个别技术的渐进式改进。具体来说，我们确立以下研究目标：Solving this problem requires system-level innovation rather than incremental improvements to individual techniques. Specifically, we establish the following research objectives:

RO2并联协调RO2 Parallel Coordination：设计一个任务调度策略，既确保概念之间的依赖性完整性（子概念在其父概念之后生成），又最大化并行效率（独立任务同时执行），同时提供代理间信息隔离以防止风格污染。: Design a task scheduling strategy that both ensures dependency integrity between concepts (child concepts are generated after their parent concepts) and maximizes parallel efficiency (independent tasks execute concurrently), while providing inter-agent information isolation to prevent stylistic contamination.

RO3质量保证RO3 Quality Assurance：设计综合质量评分与领域专家评估相结合的多层审核机制，将概念通过率提高到可接受的水平（目标: Design a multi-layer review mechanism combining general quality scoring with domain-expert evaluation, improving concept pass rates to acceptable levels (target>>80%）无需人工干预，并确保最终输出中不会检测到冲突。80%) without human intervention, and ensuring that no conflicts are detected in the final output.

RO4系统可扩展性RO4 System Extensibility：设计灵活的多智能体架构，支持零代码添加新的专用智能体，以适应不同的世界构建类型（奇幻、科幻、历史等），为未来的研究提供可重用的技术基础。: Design a flexible multi-agent architecture supporting zero-code addition of new specialized agents to accommodate different worldbuilding genres (fantasy, sci-fi, historical, etc.), providing a reusable technical foundation for future research.

尽管世界构建是具体的测试平台，但这些目标解决的问题并非创意小说所独有。渐进的知识积累、多样性与一致性的紧张关系以及自动化的质量保证在代码生成、科学写作和教育内容制作中反复出现。因此，本文开发的方法不仅要评估其特定领域的性能，还要评估其向更广泛的知识密集型法学硕士应用程序的可转移性。Although worldbuilding is the concrete testbed, these objectives address problems that are not unique to creative fiction. Progressive knowledge accumulation, the diversity–consistency tension, and automated quality assurance recur in code generation, scientific writing, and educational content production. The methods developed in this paper are therefore evaluated not only for their domain-specific performance but also for their transferability to the broader class of knowledge-intensive LLM applications.

1.4. 研究贡献Research Contributions

基于这些研究目标，本文设计并实现了 AutoWorldBuilder，这是一个由法学硕士支持的多智能体协作世界构建系统。主要贡献如下：Building upon these research objectives, this paper designs and implements AutoWorldBuilder, a multi-agent collaborative worldbuilding system powered by LLMs. The main contributions are as follows:

贡献1：结构化概念网络模型。Contribution 1: Structured Concept Network Model.集成 ConceptNet 关系分类法Integrating the ConceptNet relation taxonomy(undefan, )以及SenticNet常识知识表示框架and the SenticNet commonsense knowledge representation framework(undeff, )，我们设计了一个专为虚构世界构建而设计的结构化概念网络。该模型定义了 16 种语义关系类型（涵盖六类：层次关系、定语关系、功能关系、基于事件的关系、因果关系和语义关系），并为每种类型定义了逆关系和传递性约束。基于该模型，我们设计了五类冲突检测算法：循环检测、一对多矛盾检测、概念定义冲突检测、时空设置冲突检测和文体冲突检测。我们注意到，在当前的实验中，关系解析模块尚未完全激活（参见第 6.4 节中的限制 1），因此概念网络存储没有边的节点，并且冲突检测对设计算法的子集进行操作。尽管如此，完整的正式模型仍然为知识存储和一致性维护提供了蓝图。除了世界构建之外，该模型还可以为任何必须在不断增长的生成实体之间保持关系一致性的 LLM 系统提供通用方法。, we designed a structured concept network tailored for fictional worldbuilding. This model defines 16 semantic relation types (spanning six categories: hierarchical, attributive, functional, event-based, causal, and semantic), with inverse relations and transitivity constraints defined for each type. Based on this model, we designed five categories of conflict detection algorithms: cycle detection, one-to-many contradiction detection, concept definition conflict detection, spatiotemporal setting conflict detection, and stylistic conflict detection. We note that in current experiments, the relation parsing module has not been fully activated (see Limitation 1 in Section 6.4), so the concept network stores nodes without edges and conflict detection operates on a subset of the designed algorithms. 限制5：单语言输入限制。Limitation 5: Single-Language Input Constraint.当前系统主要针对中文输入进行设计和测试，多语言支持有限。未来的工作应该验证英语和其他语言下的系统性能。The current system was primarily designed and tested for Chinese input, with limited multilingual support. Future work should verify system performance under English and other languages.

7. 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

7.1. 总结Summary

世界构建是一项既需要逻辑严密又需要想象力的创造性活动，是游戏设计、文学创作、影视制作等创意产业的基础性任务。传统的手动方法在效率、一致性和可扩展性方面面临着根本性挑战。本文通过一个中心研究问题来应对这些挑战：我们如何设计一个多智能体协作系统，实现 LLM 驱动的自动化世界构建，同时确保生成质量和一致性？Worldbuilding is a creative activity that demands both logical rigor and imagination, and a foundational task in creative industries including game design, literary creation, and film/television production. Traditional manual approaches face fundamental challenges in efficiency, consistency, and scalability. This paper approaches these challenges through a central research question: how can we design a multi-agent collaborative system that achieves LLM-driven automated worldbuilding while ensuring generation quality and consistency?

针对这个问题，本文设计并实现了 AutoWorldBuilder 系统，贡献了五项协同工作的技术创新。的Addressing this question, this paper designed and implemented the AutoWorldBuilder system, contributing five technical innovations that work in concert. The结构化概念网络模型Structured Concept Network Model为知识存储和一致性检测提供了形式化基础，定义了16种语义关系类型和5类冲突检测算法。的provides a formal foundation for knowledge storage and consistency detection, defining 16 semantic relation types and five categories of conflict detection algorithms. The基于DAG的混合批量任务调度策略DAG-Based Hybrid Batch Task Scheduling Strategy通过语义分组最大限度地提高并行效率，同时保持依赖性完整性，将迭代次数从 56–103（串行）减少到 6–7（批处理）。的maximizes parallel efficiency through semantic grouping while maintaining dependency integrity, reducing the number of iterations from 56–103 (serial) to 6–7 (batched). The四层上下文压缩机制Four-Layer Context Compression Mechanism通过 FAISS 语义检索和分层 token 预算分配，实现 89.9% 的压缩效率，将上下文需求从数万个 token 减少到数百个。的achieves 89.9% compression efficiency, reducing context requirements from tens of thousands of tokens to hundreds through FAISS semantic retrieval and layered token budget allocation. 两个对比实验（GPT-OSS 120B 和 DeepSeek v3.2，每个都有 20 个任务）跨不同的世界构建场景测试了系统设计。该系统总体成功率达到 95%，通过率分别为 85.5% 和 99.2%，平均构建时间分别为 18 和 31 分钟，上下文压缩效率为 89.9%。Two comparative experiments (GPT-OSS 120B and DeepSeek v3.2, each with 20 tasks) tested the system design across diverse worldbuilding scenarios. The system achieved an overall 95% success rate, with pass rates of 85.5% and 99.2% respectively, average build times of 18 and 31 minutes, and 89.9% context compression efficiency.

这些实验还产生了可能转移到其他领域的设计见解。当 LLM 输出不可预测时，在入口点检测和终止错误状态（例如循环依赖性检测）比尝试自动恢复更可靠。对于上下文管理来说，通过语义检索过滤相关信息的策略比扩展上下文窗口更实用。此外，与单一通用模型相比，协调分工工作的多个专业代理可以产生更具专业差异化的内容。These experiments also produced design insights that may transfer to other domains. When LLM outputs are unpredictable, detecting and terminating error states at the entry point (e.g., cyclic dependency detection) is more reliable than attempting automatic recovery. For context management, the strategy of filtering relevant information through semantic retrieval is more practical than expanding context windows. Additionally, multiple specialized agents working in coordinated division of labor can produce more professionally differentiated content than a single general-purpose model.

更广泛地说，这五个贡献（层作为预算上下文压缩、语义局部性 DAG 调度、生成和审查分离、具有冲突检测的结构化概念网络以及技能驱动的代理可扩展性）形成了知识密集型 LLM 应用程序的架构方法。必须尊重模块依赖性的代码生成管道、需要多维审查的法律文档系统以及平衡创造力与准确性的教育内容平台都面临着第 1.2 节中确定的相同三个挑战的变体。本文中测试的架构模式可以作为这些领域的起点。More broadly, the five contributions (layer-as-budget context compression, semantic-locality DAG scheduling, separation-of-generation-and-review, structured concept networks with conflict detection, and skill-driven agent extensibility) form an architectural approach for knowledge-intensive LLM applications. Code generation pipelines that must respect module dependencies, legal document systems that require multi-dimensional review, and educational content platforms that balance creativity with accuracy all face variants of the same three challenges identified in Section 1.2. The architectural patterns tested in this paper may serve as a starting point for these domains.

7.2. 未来的方向Future Directions

方向一：从概念集到知识图谱。Direction 1: From Concept Set to Knowledge Graph.利用已定义的16种语义关系类型实现概念之间的自动关系解析，支持基于路径的一致性检测和关系传递性推理，将概念集升级为完整的知识图谱。Leveraging the already-defined 16 semantic relation types to implement automatic relation parsing between concepts, supporting path-based consistency detection and relational transitivity reasoning, upgrading the concept set to a complete knowledge graph.

方向 2：增强审核员审核能力。Direction 2: Enhanced Auditor Review Capability.引入规则引擎编码常见问题模式、跨概念关联检测和修订建议生成，使 Auditor 机制更加有效。Introducing a rule engine encoding common problem patterns, cross-concept association detection, and revision suggestion generation, making the Auditor mechanism more effective.

方向三：从全自动生成到人机协同创作。Direction 3: From Fully Automated Generation to Human-AI Collaborative Creation.支持施工过程中的实时用户干预，包括接受/拒绝具体提案、修改概念定义、添加约束条件，将LLM生成能力与人类创造性判断相结合。Supporting real-time user intervention during construction, including accepting/rejecting specific proposals, modifying concept definitions, and adding constraint conditions, combining LLM generative capabilities with human creative judgment.

方向4：任务分解策略优化。Direction 4: Task Decomposition Strategy Optimization.改进分解提示，增加任务图预验证，引入失败案例的反馈学习，增强任务分解的鲁棒性。Improving decomposition prompts, adding task graph pre-validation, and introducing feedback learning from failure cases to enhance task decomposition robustness.

方向5：建立标准化评估基准。Direction 5: Building a Standardized Evaluation Benchmark.与游戏设计和创意写作领域专家合作，开发世界构建质量评估维度框架、标准化测试数据集和人工注释的参考样本。Collaborating with game design and creative writing domain experts to develop worldbuilding quality evaluation dimension frameworks, standardized test datasets, and human-annotated reference samples.

我们的实验表明，通过多智能体协作和分层上下文压缩，法学硕士可以在 30 分钟内构建包含 50 到 100 个自洽概念的世界构建草案。随着后续关系解析、Auditor增强、标准化评估等工作的进展，这一技术途径或可为游戏开发、文学创作等领域的世界构建设计提供实际帮助，让创作者能够专注于叙事和表达。Our experiments demonstrate that through multi-agent collaboration and hierarchical context compression, LLMs can construct a worldbuilding draft containing 50 to 100 self-consistent concepts in 30 minutes. As subsequent work on relation parsing, Auditor enhancement, and standardized evaluation progresses, this technical approach may provide practical assistance for worldbuilding design in game development, literary creation, and other domains, freeing creators to focus on narrative and expression.
