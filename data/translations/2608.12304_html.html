摘要Abstract

动态主逻辑 (DML) 通过将功能目标链接到底层结构元素来提供表示系统行为的分层框架。与故障树分析等基于事件的方法不同，DML 在统一的功能层次结构中捕获系统目标、功能依赖性和意外故障场景。然而，DML 构建通常依赖于专家对技术文档的解释，限制了复杂系统的可扩展性。本研究提出了一个框架，利用检索增强生成和大型语言模型作为支持工具，从系统描述及其知识图（KG-DML）表示形式自动构建 DML 模型。基于之前关于小规模系统的工作，目前的工作引入了 KG-DML 模型的自动化构建和评估，将范式扩展到更大、更复杂的系统。所提出的方法使用有针对性的检索来跨层次结构进行模型构建，保留层次依赖性和显式逻辑关系。由此产生的 KGDML 通过向上故障传播和向下依赖性跟踪来支持诊断推理、安全评估和基于图形的分析。该框架通过多级验证方法进行评估，该方法结合了特定层的精度和召回率、逻辑门一致性分析以及捕获元素级准确性和系统级结构完整性的聚合完整性度量。在退役沸水反应堆的低压冷却剂注入系统中的应用展示了重复运行中的一致重建，并突出了影响模型稳定性的因素。结果表明，自动化 KG-DML 构建可以将技术文档转换为可执行的功能模型，用于诊断和可靠性分析。Dynamic Master Logic (DML) provides a hierarchical framework for representing system behavior by linking functional objectives to underlying structural elements. Unlike event-based approaches such as Fault Tree Analysis, DML captures system objectives, functional dependencies, and unanticipated failure scenarios within a unified functional hierarchy. However, DML construction typically relies on expert interpretation of technical documentation, limiting scalability for complex systems. This study presents a framework for automated construction of DML models from system descriptions and their representation as Knowledge Graphs (KG-DML), leveraging Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Large Language Models as the enabling tools. Building upon prior work on small-scale systems, the present work introduces automated construction and evaluation of KG-DML models, extending the paradigm to substantially larger and more complex system. The proposed approach performs model construction across the hierarchy using targeted retrieval, preserving hierarchical dependencies and explicit logical relationships. The resulting KGDML supports diagnostic reasoning, safety assessment, and graph-based analysis through upward failure propagation and downward dependency tracing. The framework is evaluated through a multi-level validation methodology that combines layer-specific precision and recall, logical gate consistency analysis, and an aggregate integrity metric capturing both element-level accuracy and system-level structural completeness. Application to the Low-Pressure Coolant Injection system of a decommissioned Boiling Water Reactor demonstrates consistent reconstruction across repeated runs and highlights factors influencing model stability. The results show that automated KG-DML construction can transform technical documentation into executable functional models for diagnostic and reliability analysis.

一、简介1. Introduction

现代工程系统通常由跨多个抽象级别组织的许多交互组件组成。因此，准确地对这些系统进行建模需要能够解释复杂的交互和依赖关系的表示。事件树分析 (ETA) [1]、[2] 和故障树分析 (FTA) [3]、[4] 等传统诊断模型依赖于事件驱动的表示，其中通过预定义的因果序列来跟踪故障或启动事件。虽然对于已知故障有效，但事件驱动模型需要详尽的故障事件列表，因此当模型开发过程中未预见到故障序列时，它们本质上是不完整的。随着系统变得越来越复杂，仅使用基于事件的逻辑来跟踪故障路径也变得越来越不可行。Modern engineered systems are often composed of many interacting components organized across multiple levels of abstraction. Therefore, modeling these systems accurately requires representations that can account for complex interactions and dependencies. Traditional diagnostic models such as Event Tree Analysis (ETA) [1], [2] and Fault Tree Analysis (FTA) [3], [4] rely on event-driven representations in which faults or initiating events are traced through predefined causal sequences. While effective for known failures, event-driven models require exhaustive fault-event listings, making them inherently incomplete when failure sequences are not anticipated during model development. As systems grow in complexity, tracing failure pathways also becomes increasingly infeasible using event-based logic alone.

功能是通过组件之间的交互来实现的functions are realized through interactions among components1[5]。通过关注功能及其依赖性而不是单个事件，功能模型可以更清晰地表示组件行为在正常和异常条件下如何促进系统级目标[6]。[5]. By focusing on functions and their dependencies rather than individual events, functional models provide a clearer representation of how component behavior contributes to system-level objectives under both normal and abnormal conditions [6].

动态主逻辑 (DML) 由 Hu 和 Modarres [7] 提出，是一种功能建模框架，它通过将功能目标与底层结构链接起来的层次结构来表示系统行为。 DML 已应用于可靠性和安全性分析，以支持系统行为和故障机制的推理 [8]、[9]、[10]。然而，在实践中，构建 DML 模型在很大程度上依赖于手动、专家驱动的流程，其中开发人员分析系统描述和规范以解释系统行为并构建模型。这限制了可扩展性，并限制了对通过大量技术文档记录的复杂工程系统进行可靠推理所需的显式函数表示的使用。Dynamic Master Logic (DML), introduced by Hu and Modarres [7], is a functional modeling framework that represents system behavior through a hierarchy linking functional objectives to underlying structures. DML has been applied in reliability and safety analysis to support reasoning about system behavior and failure mechanisms [8], [9], [10]. In practice, however, constructing DML models has largely relied on manual, expert-driven processes, where developers analyze system descriptions and specifications to interpret system behavior and construct the model. This limits scalability and restricts the use of explicit functional representations needed for reliable reasoning over complex engineering systems documented through extensive technical documentation.

人工智能 (AI)，特别是大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展，为支持功能模型的构建创造了新的机会。大型语言模型在语义解释、文本摘要和生成等任务中非常有效[11]，这使得它们成为帮助工程师从技术文档构建功能模型的有前途的工具。然而，法学硕士也表现出重要的局限性，包括推理不一致、缺乏持久结构以及可能生成不受支持的信息[11]、[12]。最近的研究进一步强调了使语言模型能够明确地推理行为及其后果的挑战[13]。这种限制在依赖功能模型的工程诊断中尤其重要，其中准确的推理不仅需要识别相关信息，还需要理解控制系统行为的功能关系。如果没有显式的结构化表示，法学硕士无法可靠地推断哪些功能支持哪些目标或组件故障如何在系统中级联。动态主逻辑通过将目标、功能和依赖关系链接到显式结构化表示来解决此限制，该表示支持遍历并使 LLM 代理能够推理功能依赖关系和组件故障的后果，而不是仅仅依赖于广义语言模型知识。因此，准确的 DML 模型提供了明确的知识表示，可以在复杂的工程系统上实现基于 LLM 的辅助诊断推理。Recent advances in Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly Large Language Models (LLMs), create new opportunities to support the construction of functional models. Large language models are effective in tasks such as semantic interpretation, text summarization, and generation [11], making them promising tools to help engineers construct functional models from technical documentation. However, LLMs also exhibit important limitations, including inconsistent reasoning, lack of persistent structure, and the potential to generate unsupported information [11], [12]. Recent research has further highlighted the challenge of enabling language models to reason explicitly about actions and their consequences [13]. This limitation is particularly significant in engineering diagnostics that rely on functional models, where accurate reasoning requires not just recognizing relevant information but understanding the functional relationships that govern system behavior. Without an explicit structured representation, an LLM cannot reliably reason about which functions support which objectives or how a component failure cascades through the system. Dynamic Master Logic addresses this limitation by linking objectives, functions, and dependencies into an explicit structured representation that supports traversal and enables LLM agents to reason about functional dependencies and the consequences of component failures, rather than relying solely on generalized language model knowledge. Consequently, accurate DML models provide an explicit knowledge representation that enables grounded LLM-assisted diagnostic reasoning over complex engineering systems.

在我们之前的工作 [14] 中，我们探索了使用 LLM 为小型系统构建基于知识图 (KG) 的 DML 表示。该研究证明了生成能够支持法学硕士辅助诊断推理的显式功能表示的可行性，但没有解决大型文档密集型系统描述的可扩展构建问题。此外，评估框架相对简单，没有系统地评估层次一致性或结构完整性。随着系统复杂性的增加，这些限制激发了对可扩展且经过严格评估的方法的需求。更重要的是，可靠的诊断推理取决于准确且完整的 KG-DML 模型，该模型明确捕获功能目标、依赖性和故障传播。在实践中，构建此类模型依赖于专家的手动工作，限制了其可扩展性。In our prior work [14], we explored the use of LLMs to construct a Knowledge Graph (KG)-based DML representation for a small-scale system. That study demonstrated the feasibility of generating explicit functional representations capable of supporting LLM-assisted diagnostic reasoning, but did not address scalable construction from large and document-intensive system descriptions. In addition, the evaluation framework was relatively simple and did not systematically assess hierarchical consistency or structural completeness. As systems grow in complexity, these limitations motivate the need for a scalable and rigorously evaluated approach. More importantly, reliable diagnostic reasoning depends on accurate and complete KG-DML models that explicitly capture functional objectives, dependencies, and failure propagation. In practice, constructing such models has relied on manual expert effort, limiting their scalability.

The resulting executable KG-DML supports diagnostic reasoning, safety assessment, and resilience-oriented analysis through explicit representation of functional dependencies. This work makes three main contributions. First, it introduces an automated approach for constructing DML models from system documentation, leveraging retrieval-augmented LLMs as enabling tools, enabling functional decomposition and dependency identification from unstructured text. Second, it extends our previous small-scale approach to support construction for substantially larger, document-intensive systems. Third, it develops a structured, multi-level evaluation framework for assessing the constructed KG-DML, combining layer-level accuracy metrics with an integrity measure of structural completeness and consistency. The novelty of this work lies not in the individual use of established technologies such as RAG, LLMs, schema-constrained extraction, or graph databases, but in their integration into a coherent framework for automated DML construction from engineering documentation, together with an executable KG-DML representation for engineering safety applications and a principled evaluation methodology.

2. 背景2. Background

2.1.功能建模和动态主逻辑模型2.1. Functional Modeling and Dynamic Master Logic Model

传统的诊断方法，例如 ETA [1]、[2]、FTA [3]、[4] 和基于规则的专家系统 [16]、[17]，依赖于对离散事件和预定义故障序列进行建模。虽然对于已知的故障事件组合有效，但这些事件驱动的方法对于诊断目的通常是不完整的，因为无法诊断意外的序列，并且复杂性使得故障跟踪不切实际。相比之下，功能模型代表了可靠性分析的范式转变，从基于孤立事件的方法转向基于功能的全面评估。这种转变至关重要，因为系统知识正在Traditional diagnostic methods, such as ETA [1], [2], FTA [3], [4], and rule-based expert systems [16], [17], rely on modeling discrete events and predefined failure sequences. While effective for known faultevent combinations, these event-driven approaches are often incomplete for diagnostic purposes, as unanticipated sequences cannot be diagnosed, and complexity makes failure tracing impractical. In contrast, functional models represent a paradigm shift in reliability analysis, moving away from isolated event-based methods toward comprehensive, function-based evaluations. This shift is critical as system knowledge is

本质上是功能性的。传统上，工程系统是通过其操作目标、功能架构、操作和机械过程而不是相关的故障模式和事件来记录和概念化的。基于事件的方法（例如 FTA 和 ETA）代表了这种功能知识的下游抽象，并且作为因果推理的基础通常是不完整的。功能建模系统地捕获系统目标、功能及其层次依赖性，而不是枚举事件。通过表示预期的功能行为和组件贡献，功能模型可以通过与预期操作的偏差（例如，流量或信息丢失）而不是特定的故障事件来进行故障检测[18]。功能建模方法通过支持早期分析、通过系统构成元素的目的（功能）和任何功能依赖性来适应多故障和级联场景，从而提高完整性。通过关注功能关系而不是预定义的事件序列，该方法可以支持系统生命周期的多个阶段，包括设计阶段 FMEA、运行时诊断和假设分析 [19]。inherently functional. Engineering systems are traditionally documented and conceptualized through their operational objectives, functional architectures, operating and mechanistic processes, rather than their associated failure modes and events. Event-based methods such as FTA and ETA represent a downstream abstraction of this functional knowledge and are often incomplete as a basis for causal reasoning. Functional modeling systematically captures system goals, functions, and their hierarchical dependencies rather than enumerating events. By representing expected functional behavior and component contributions, functional models enable fault detection through deviations from intended operation (e.g., loss of flow or information) instead of specific failure events [18]. The functional modeling approach improves completeness by supporting early-stage analysis, accommodating multi-fault and cascading scenarios through the purpose (functions) of constitutive elements of the system and any functional dependencies. By focusing on functional relationships rather than predefined event sequences, the approach can support multiple stages of the system life cycle, including design-phase FMEA, runtime diagnosis, and what-if analysis [19].

基于这些原则，DML 提供了一个分层框架来表示系统目的和目标以及实现它们所需的功能。此外，该模型还提供了结构元素，例如实现这些功能的硬件操作、软件执行和人类行为。该模型历史上是通过应用目标树-成功树和主逻辑图（GTST-MLD）构建规则开发的[6]。在 GTST-MLD 中，较高级别的目标定义了为什么功能和结构是必要的，而较低级别的功能描述了如何通过硬件、软件和人为元素之间的交互来实现这些目标和功能。逻辑关系通过编码功能依赖关系的逻辑门结构明确表示，允许在统一的层次结构中对结构组成和因果关系进行建模[20]。动态主逻辑图 (DMLD) 或简称 DML 通过合并时间相关的、不确定的和演化的行为来扩展 GTST-MLD 基础，支持系统诊断、故障分析和可靠性评估的静态和动态推理 [7]。Building on these principles, DML provides a hierarchical framework for representing system objectives and goals, and the functions required to achieve them. Further, the model provides structural elements such as hardware operations, software executions, and human actions that realize those functions. The model is historically developed by applying the Goal Tree-Success Tree and Master Logic Diagram (GTST-MLD) construction rules [6]. In GTST-MLD, higher-level goals define why functions and structures are necessary, while lower-level functions describe how those goals and functions are achieved through interactions among hardware, software, and human elements. Logical relationships are represented explicitly through logic gate structures that encode functional dependencies, allowing structural composition and causal relationships to be modeled within a unified hierarchy [20]. The Dynamic Master Logic Diagram (DMLD) or simply DML extends the GTST-MLD foundation by incorporating timedependent, uncertain, and evolving behaviors, supporting both static and dynamic reasoning for system diagnosis, failure analysis, and reliability assessment [7].

最近，KG-DML 通过机器可读的表示形式扩展了 DML 的功能，这些表示形式支持计算推理和交互，同时保留了底层的 DML 结构。从基于事件的 FT/ET 方法，通过 GTST 和 GTST-MLD 的功能层次结构，到 DML 的动态推理，再到现在机器可读的 KG-DML，这一进展反映了人们为提高功能系统模型的完整性、可扩展性和计算可访问性而不断努力。More recently, KG-DML has extended DML’s capabilities through machine-readable representations that support computational reasoning and interaction while preserving the underlying DML structure. This progression from event-based FT/ET methods, through the functional hierarchy of GTST and GTST-MLD, to the dynamic reasoning of DML, and now to the machine-readable KG-DML, reflects a continuous effort to improve the completeness, scalability, and computational accessibility of functional system models.

2.2.法学硕士的优势和局限性2.2. LLMs Strengths and Limitations

大型语言模型已成为跨不同应用领域理解和生成自然语言的强大工具[33]、[34]。尽管有这些功能，但在考虑它们在系统建模中的使用时，仍存在一些相关的限制。当可用信息不完整或问题表述不明确时，可能会出现幻觉，导致生成看似合理但不正确的输出[35]，[36]。为了缓解这个问题，RAG 方法在推理过程中结合了外部知识源，将模型输出基于检索到的证据，并显着减少知识密集型任务中的幻觉 [37]、[38]。此外，对于需要一致的多步骤推理或显式验证的任务，性能可能会下降。这种限制促使基于 LLM 的代理与外部工具（包括预先编写的代码或函数、分析求解器、数据库和基于规则的系统）耦合，以在基于代理的框架中提供计算基础和验证[39]。另一个挑战是运行期间的输出变化[40]，特别是对于复杂的任务，这些任务通常通过特定领域的微调[41]和结构化提示设计来解决，以提高一致性和控制[42]。这项工作的目的并不是引入新技术来克服这些限制，而是应用文献中报告的既定缓解策略。Large language models have emerged as powerful tools for understanding and generating natural language across diverse application domains [33], [34]. Despite these capabilities, several limitations are relevant when considering their use in system modeling. Hallucination may occur when the available information is incomplete or when problem formulations are ambiguous, leading to the generation of outputs that are plausible but incorrect [35], [36]. To mitigate this issue, RAG methods incorporate external knowledge sources during inference, grounding model outputs in retrieved evidence and significantly reducing hallucination in knowledge-intensive tasks [37], [38]. In addition, performance can degrade for tasks that require consistent multi-step reasoning or explicit verification. This limitation has motivated the coupling of LLM-based agents with external tools, including prewritten code or functions, analytical solvers, databases, and rule-based systems, to provide computational grounding and validation in agentbased frameworks [39]. Another challenge is output variability across runs [40], particularly for complex tasks, which are commonly addressed through domain-specific fine-tuning [41] and structured prompt design to improve consistency and control [42]. This work does not aim to introduce new techniques to overcome these limitations, but rather it applies established mitigation strategies reported in the literature.

4.1.1.第一阶段：文档处理和嵌入4.1.1. Stage 1: Document Processing and Embedding

在嵌入和检索之前，对系统文档进行预处理。这减少了歧义并支持可靠的提取。组件标识符和标签被标准化为一致的格式，以防止对同一实体的引用不一致。缩写词在第一次出现时会被扩展和定义，以保持整个文档的清晰度。当首次引入标记组件时，标识符会补充简短的描述性上下文，例如功能角色或位置，以将每个标识符与其含义相关联。在嵌入过程中会保留此上下文，以便名称相似但角色不同的组件在检索过程中不太可能被混淆。在可能的情况下，代词和速记参考被解析为显式标识符，以保持文本片段的清晰度。总之，这些步骤使检索更加可靠，并通过将每个元素基于显式标识符而不是仅依赖于语义相似性来降低混淆相似命名组件的风险。Prior to embedding and retrieval, the system documentation is preprocessed. This reduces ambiguity and supports reliable extraction. Component identifiers and tags are normalized to a consistent format to prevent inconsistent references to the same entity. Abbreviations are expanded and defined at first occurrence to maintain clarity throughout the document. When tagged components are first introduced, identifiers are supplemented with brief descriptive context, such as functional role or location, to associate each identifier with its meaning. This context is retained during embedding so that components with similar names, but different roles are less likely to be conflated during retrieval. Where possible, pronouns and shorthand references are resolved to explicit identifiers to maintain clarity across text segments. Together, these steps make retrieval more reliable and reduce the risk of confusing similarly named components by grounding each element in explicit identifiers instead of relying only on semantic similarity.

第 2 阶段通过按顺序遍历 DML 层次结构来执行 DML 模型的结构化构建。每个层都是独立构建的，同时以前一层的结果为条件，而不是尝试在单个步骤中推断整个模型。图 4 说明了代表层的这一过程。在每一层的开始，模型的当前状态存储在主 JSON 表示中。该主 JSON 作为中间模型，包含早期阶段已验证和接受的所有节点和关系。与当前层相关的父元素直接从此主表示中读取，并用于约束提取任务的范围。为了管理提示长度并控制生成范围，每层的父元素都单独或小批量处理。批次对应于在单个模型调用中一起处理的固定数量的父元素。例如，一个批量大小独立地处理每个父元素，而较大的批量大小允许联合处理多个父元素。在给定层，通过生成其相应的子节点以及在 DML 层次结构中连接它们的逻辑关系来扩展每个父元素。评估部分检查了批量大小对模型质量的影响。Stage 2 performs the structured construction of the DML model by progressing sequentially through the DML hierarchy. Rather than attempting to infer the entire model in a single step, each layer is constructed independently while being conditioned on the results of previous layers. To manage prompt length and control the scope of generation, parent elements at each layer are processed either individually or in small batches. A batch corresponds to a fixed number of parent elements that are processed together in a single model invocation. For example, a batch size of one process each parent element independently, while larger batch sizes allow multiple parent elements to be processed jointly. At a given layer, each parent element is expanded by generating its corresponding child nodes and the logical relationships that connect them within the DML hierarchy. 4.2.评估框架4.2. Evaluation Framework

评估的目的是评估所构建的以 KG-DML 表示的 DML 模型的质量。由于该模型旨在通过遍历支持诊断推理，因此其有用性不仅取决于各个元素的正确性，还取决于 DML 层次结构中关系的完整性。因此，评估框架通过引入总体完整性评分来评估元素级准确性和系统级结构一致性。The objective of the evaluation is to assess the quality of the constructed DML model represented as KG-DML. Since the model is intended to support diagnostic reasoning through traversal, its usefulness depends not only on the correctness of individual elements, but also on the integrity of the relationships across the DML hierarchy. The evaluation framework therefore assesses element-level accuracy and system-level structural consistency by introducing an aggregate integrity score.

规范化之后，DML 层次结构的每一层都独立进行节点级评估。对于每一层，构建模型中的节点与参考模型（称为黄金模型）中的相应节点进行比较。每个预测节点被分为三类之一：正确识别、丢失或幻觉。如果在黄金模型中找到相应的匹配，则认为节点被正确识别。黄金模型中存在但构建的模型中不存在的节点被归类为缺失。相反，出现在构建的模型中但在黄金模型中没有相应匹配的节点被分类为幻觉。Following normalization, node-level evaluation is conducted independently for each layer of the DML hierarchy. For each layer, nodes in the constructed model are compared with the corresponding nodes in the reference model (referred to as the gold model). Each predicted node is classified into one of three categories: correctly identified, missing, or hallucinated. A node is considered correctly identified if a corresponding match is found in the gold model. Nodes that exist in the gold model but are absent from the constructed model are classified as missing. Conversely, nodes that appear in the constructed model but have no corresponding match in the gold model are classified as hallucinated.

这些分类用于计算每一层的精度和召回率。准确率反映了正确识别的节点的比例，而召回率则反映了成功恢复的参考节点的比例。据报道，𝐹2 分数更加强调召回率，反映了 DML 模型中缺失元素通常比包含额外元素更有害的事实。分别报告目标、功能、子功能、组件和成功条件的这些指标，可以深入了解不同抽象级别的提取性能如何变化。These classifications are used to compute precision and recall at each layer. Precision reflects the proportion of identified nodes that are correct, while recall reflects the proportion of reference nodes that are successfully recovered. An 𝐹2 score is also reported to place greater emphasis on recall, reflecting the fact that missing elements in a DML model are generally more detrimental than the inclusion of extra elements. Reporting these metrics separately for goals, functions, subfunctions, components, and success conditions provides insight into how extraction performance varies across different levels of abstraction.

4.2.2.关系结构和逻辑一致性4.2.2. Relationship Structure and Logical Consistency

除了节点识别之外，评估还检查节点之间的关系是否正确建立。对于参考模型中的每个父节点，将预期的子节点集与构建的 KG-DML 中生成的集进行比较。关系错误分为缺失链接、错误链接或额外链接。由于父节点和子节点之间的逻辑关系是通过每个层级的 AND/OR 门来表示的，因此针对 DML 结构的所有层都评估了门的准确性。门精度衡量连接父节点与其子节点的逻辑运算符是否与参考模型中的相应运算符匹配。门的正确性与节点和链路标识分开评估，因为即使存在正确的节点，不正确的逻辑运算符也可能改变元素对更高级别功能的贡献方式。链接评估仅考虑是否存在正确的父子关联，与逻辑结构无关。不同类型的错误会不同程度地影响KG-DML的实用性。在层次结构的较高级别发生的错误往往会向下游传播并影响模型的较大部分。因此，节点遗漏、关系错误和门不匹配将根据它们发生的 DML 层进行加权。当关系和门错误破坏从目标到成功条件的完整功能路径时，它们会受到更高的处罚。In addition to node identification, the evaluation examines whether relationships between nodes are correctly established. For each parent node in the reference model, the expected set of child nodes is compared with the set produced in the constructed KG-DML. Relationship errors are categorized as missing links, incorrect links, or extra links. Since logical relationships between parent and child nodes are represented through AND/OR gates at each hierarchical layer, gate accuracy is evaluated for all layers of the DML structure. Gate accuracy measures whether the logical operator connecting a parent node to its children matches the corresponding operator in the reference model. Gate correctness is evaluated separately from node and link identification, as an incorrect logical operator can alter how elements contribute to higher-level functions even when the correct nodes are present. Link evaluation considers only whether the correct parent-child associations are present, independent of logical structure. Different types of errors affect the usefulness of the KG-DML to different degrees. Errors occurring at higher levels of the hierarchy tend to propagate downstream and affect larger portions of the model. As a result, node omissions, relationship errors, and gate mismatches are weighted according to the DML layer at which they occur. Relationship and gate errors receive higher penalties when they disrupt complete functional paths from goals to success conditions.

4.3. Model Interaction

As shown in Figure 2, interaction with the constructed KG-DML is mediated by an LLM-based agent equipped with a set of predefined tools. These tools implement graph traversal and probabilistic reasoning procedures and are invoked by the LLM agent based on the user’s inferred intent. This interaction paradigm follows the general approach introduced in our previous work [14], in which LLMs serve as a coordination layer that interprets user queries and selects appropriate model-based operations, rather than functioning as autonomous reasoning engines.

In addition to tool-based reasoning, the interaction framework also supports general questions about the model structure. Such queries are handled using a graph-augmented retrieval mechanism (Graph-RAG), in which relevant subgraphs and graph segments are retrieved and summarized without invoking the tools.

4.3.1.向上传播4.3.1. Upward Propagation

向上传播是一种通过将成功概率从组件级别传播到更高级别的功能和目标节点来评估系统成功并识别受影响的上层节点的工具。概率语义和聚合规则遵循先前框架中建立的公式。当前工作中引入的主要区别是评估嵌套布尔门结构的能力，而不是将每个依赖性限制为单个平面门。当用户提出诸如“系统在当前条件下实现其目标的可能性有多大？”之类的问题时或者“特定的失败组件如何影响总体目标？”，LLM 代理调用向上传播工具。代理不直接计算概率；相反，它选择并执行在 KG-DML 上运行的工具。Upward propagation is a tool that evaluates system success and identifies impacted upper nodes by propagating success probability from the component level to higher-level functional and goal nodes. The probabilistic semantics and aggregation rules follow the formulation established in the prior framework. The primary difference introduced in the present work is the ability to evaluate nested Boolean gate structures, rather than restricting each dependency to a single flat gate. When a user poses a question such as “What is the likelihood that the system can achieve its objective under current conditions?” or “How does a specific failed component affect the overall goal?”, the LLM agent invokes the upward propagation tool. The agent does not compute probabilities directly; instead, it selects and executes the tool which operates on the KG-DML.

计算从组件和成功条件级别开始，其中每个组件都与操作状态（例如，工作、降级、失败）和一组成功条件相关联。与组件操作状态和成功条件相关联的概率可以从操作数据源导出，包括传感器测量、系统日志、检查或其他观察证据。这些值作为节点属性编码到 KG-DML 中，并表示交互时系统的当前或假定状态。每个成功条件定义了给定组件运行状态的满足条件概率。组件级成功概率是通过根据为组件定义的布尔逻辑对操作状态进行边缘化并组合成功条件来获得的。然后，这些组件的成功概率通过层次结构向上传播。The computation begins at the component and success condition level, where each component is associated with an operational state (e.g., working, degraded, failed) and a set of success conditions. The probabilities associated with component operational states and success conditions may be derived from operational data sources, including sensor measurements, system logs, inspections, or other observational evidence. These values are encoded into the KG-DML as node attributes and represent the current or assumed state of the system at the time of interaction. Each success condition defines conditional probabilities of satisfaction given the component’s operational state. Component-level success probabilities are obtained by marginalizing over operational states and combining success conditions according to the Boolean logic defined for the component. These component success probabilities are then propagated upward through the hierarchy.

由于向上传播直接对组件状态分配进行操作，因此组件之间的任何依赖关系本质上都会反映在这些输入中。如果多个组件由于共同原因而同时退化，则这种情况会直接以其指定的状态表示并通过模型传播。因此，在此模式下不需要额外的定性增强，因为输入条件中已经捕获了诸如共因故障 (CCF) 之类的依赖性影响。Since upward propagation operates directly on component state assignments, any dependencies among components are inherently reflected in these inputs. If multiple components are simultaneously degraded due to a shared cause, this condition is directly represented in their assigned states and propagated through the model. As a result, no additional qualitative augmentation is required in this mode, since dependency effects such as Common Cause Failures (CCFs) are already captured in the input conditions.

4.3.2.向下传播4.3.2. Downward Propagation

除了诊断解释之外，向下交互还支持询问如何实现特定目标、功能或子功能的查询。向下遍历逻辑遵循与先前建立的框架相同的结构原理，并在本工作中进行扩展以容纳嵌套的布尔子门。当用户提出诸如“如何实现这个目标？”之类的问题时或者“生成此函数的成功路径”，LLM 代理将意图解释为成功路径枚举的请求，并调用相应的向下遍历工具。在此模式下，该工具通过从查询节点向下遍历 KG-DML 及其依赖结构来生成最小成功路径集。遍历遵循模型中编码的布尔逻辑，包括嵌套（AND/OR）子门。对于联合依赖项 (AND)，成功路径是通过组合所需的子元素来构建的，而对于析取依赖项，则独立枚举替代路径。在较低级别，路径以组件的形式表示。所得到的成功路径表示足以实现所查询节点的结构上有效的元素组合。In addition to diagnostic explanations, downward interaction also supports queries that ask how a specific objective, function, or subfunction can be achieved. The downward traversal logic follows the same structural principles as the previously established framework, with the extension in this work to accommodate nested Boolean sub-gates. When a user poses questions such as “How can this goal be achieved?” or “Generate the success paths for this function,” the LLM agent interprets the intent as a request for success path enumeration and invokes the corresponding downward traversal tool. In this mode, the tool generates minimal success path-sets by traversing the KG-DML from the queried node downward through its dependency structure. The traversal respects the Boolean logic encoded in the model, including nested (AND/OR) sub-gates. For conjunctive dependencies (AND), success paths are constructed by combining the required child elements, whereas for disjunctive dependencies, alternative paths are enumerated independently. At lower levels, paths are expressed in terms of components. The resulting success paths represent structurally valid combinations of elements that are sufficient to achieve the queried node.

在向下遍历中，生成的成功和失败路径源自KG-DML的显式布尔逻辑结构，因此反映了组件之间的结构依赖关系。然而，共因故障可能由显式结构依赖性和隐式耦合机制引起，例如共享设计、环境、维护实践或模型结构中未明确表示的潜在共同根本原因。因此，当在替代成功路径中识别出多个相同或冗余组件时，交互层会通过强调可能同时影响这些组件的 CCF 的潜力来增强解释。因此，除了成功路径之外，解释还包括定性指示，即共同原因可能会同时使多个路径失效。这种增强不会修改底层逻辑结构，而是提供超出显式建模关系的依赖效应的部分表示。In downward traversal, the generated success and failure paths are derived from the explicit Boolean logic structure of the KG-DML and therefore reflect structural dependencies among components. Common cause failure, however, may arise from both explicit structural dependencies and implicit coupling mechanisms, such as shared design, environment, maintenance practices, or underlying common root causes that are not explicitly represented in the model structure. Accordingly, when multiple identical or redundant components are identified within alternative success paths, the interaction layer augments the explanation by highlighting the potential for a CCF that could simultaneously affect these components. In addition to the success paths, the explanation therefore includes a qualitative indication that a shared cause could invalidate multiple paths concurrently. This augmentation does not modify the underlying logical structure but provides a partial representation of dependency effects beyond explicitly modeled relationships.

CCF 的处理是片面的，因为它没有明确地建模 KG-DML 内的因果机制或依赖结构。相反，它用于标记组件之间是否存在潜在的共享漏洞，而无需更改布尔逻辑或路径生成过程。这些路径提供了对系统设计意图、冗余和功能替代方案的深入了解，并可用于支持规划、设计选项比较或假设分析。The treatment of CCF is partial, as it does not explicitly model the causal mechanisms or dependency structure within the KG-DML. Instead, it serves to flag the presence of potential shared vulnerabilities among components without altering the Boolean logic or path generation process. These paths provide insight into system design intent, redundancy, and functional alternatives, and can be used to support planning, comparison of design options, or what-if analysis.

除了向上和向下传播之外，交互框架还支持侧重于所构建的 KG-DML 结构属性的解释性查询。这些查询涉及元素在层次结构中如何相关或请求有关模型结构的摘要信息。例如，用户可能会询问特定功能如何与给定子功能相关，或者模型中总共定义了多少个功能或组件。In addition to upward and downward propagation, the interaction framework supports explanatory queries that focus on the structural properties of the constructed KG-DML. The generated Cypher statement is executed directly in the Neo4j database, and the resulting subgraph or aggregated output is returned to the LLM for interpretation and summarization into a natural language response. 7. 讨论7. Discussion

所提出的基于 LLM-KG 的诊断框架支持从系统文档中自动提取和构建功能模型，同时保持相对于参考模型的高结构准确性。与传统的专家驱动的开发流程相比，这种方法有可能减少构建 DML 模型所需的手动工作。在传统实践中，为复杂的工程系统构建 DML 模型通常需要几个月的手动工作。领域专家必须审查广泛的技术文档，确定系统目标和功能，提取组件关系，并将这些元素组装成连贯的层次结构。本研究中提出的框架通过利用 LLM 辅助提取和知识图谱表示简化了这一过程，将复杂系统的模型构建时间缩短到几天，同时保持逻辑一致性和诊断能力。The proposed LLM-KG-based diagnostic framework supports automated extraction and structuring of functional models from system documentation while maintaining high structural accuracy relative to the reference model. This approach has the potential to reduce the manual effort required for constructing DML models compared with traditional expert-driven development processes. In conventional practice, constructing a DML model for a complex engineered system often requires several months of manual effort. Domain experts must review extensive technical documentation, identify system goals and functions, extract component relationships, and assemble these elements into a coherent hierarchical structure. The framework presented in this study streamlines this process by leveraging LLM-assisted extraction and KG representation, reducing model construction time to a matter of days for complex systems while preserving logical consistency and diagnostic capability.

在组件层和成功条件层观察到与完美性能的微小偏差。尽管两个层的精确度仍然非常高，但召回率略有下降，导致 𝐹2 分数略有下降。由于 𝐹2 更加强调回忆，因此这种模式反映了层次结构的更详细级别上偶尔的遗漏。较低级别的元素数量更多，并且与特定的上下文描述联系更紧密，这使得它们对检索范围和批处理决策更加敏感。然而，变化的幅度是有限的，这些层的整体性能仍然很高。Small deviations from perfect performance are observed at the Component and Success Condition layers. Although precision remains very high for both layers, recall decreases slightly, leading to modest reductions in the 𝐹2 score. Since 𝐹2 places greater emphasis on recall, this pattern reflects occasional omissions at the more detailed levels of the hierarchy. Lower-level elements are more numerous and more closely tied to specific contextual descriptions, making them more sensitive to retrieval scope and batching decisions. Nevertheless, the magnitude of variation is limited, and overall performance at these layers remains high.

链路级评估进一步证实了所构建模型的结构保真度。对于目标-功能和功能-子功能关系，在所有运行中都正确识别了结构关联和逻辑运算符。这表明，一旦提取了更高级别的元素，它们的层次依赖性就会一致地重建。在 SubfunctionComponent 层观察到更大的可变性。虽然结构精度仍然很高，但召回率略有下降，并且门精度表现出可测量的变化。该层代表层次结构中结构密度和嵌套逻辑关系增加的点，这解释了观察到的敏感性。相比之下，ComponentSuccess Condition 关系显示出近乎完美的结构和逻辑一致性，表明一旦组件被识别，这些依赖关系就会稳定恢复。The link-level evaluation further confirms the structural fidelity of the constructed models. This layer represents the point in the hierarchy where structural density and nested logical relationships increase, which explains the observed sensitivity. In contrast, the ComponentSuccess Condition relationships show near-perfect structural and logical consistency, indicating the stable recovery of these dependencies once components are identified.

批量大小的分析突出了一个重要的实际考虑因素。当批量大小为 1 时获得最高平均完整性得分，并且随着批量大小的增加，性能逐渐下降。随着批量大小的增加，平均完整性得分逐渐下降，这表明同时扩展多个父节点会增加即时复杂性，并可能会在 LLM 输入中引入上下文竞争。这可能会导致分层提取过程中出现微小的遗漏或不一致。较大的批量大小要求模型同时扩展多个父元素，从而增加了提示的复杂性以及出现轻微不一致的可能性。较小的批量可以隔离每个膨胀步骤，这似乎可以促进更大的结构稳定性。尽管批量大小为 1 产生的平均完整性得分最高，但相对于批量大小为 5 的改进是有限的。将批量大小从 5 减少到 1，LLM 调用数量增加了大约五倍，所有构建层的调用从大约 32 次增加到 158 次。但是，由于较小的批量大小每次调用处理的父元素较少，因此每个单独的提示较短并且需要较少的输出标记，这部分抵消了总执行时间的增加。然而，相对于结构准确性的边际增益，API 使用和执行时间的累积开销仍然很大。批量大小为 5 提供了结构一致性和计算效率之间的平衡，保持高完整性分数，同时减少执行时间和 API 调用量。The analysis of batch size highlights an important practical consideration. The highest mean integrity score was obtained with a batch size of 1, and performance decreases gradually as the batch size increases. The gradual decline in mean integrity score with increasing batch size suggests that expanding multiple parent nodes simultaneously increases prompt complexity and may introduce competition for context within the LLM input. This can lead to minor omissions or inconsistencies during hierarchical extraction. Larger batch sizes require the model to expand multiple parent elements simultaneously, increasing prompt complexity and the potential for minor inconsistencies. Smaller batch sizes isolate each expansion step, which appears to promote greater structural stability. Although a batch size of 1 produced the highest mean integrity score, the improvement relative to a batch size of 5 was limited. Reducing the batch size from 5 to 1 increased the number of LLM invocations approximately fivefold, from roughly 32 to 158 calls across all construction layers. However, since smaller batch sizes process fewer parent elements per call, each individual prompt is shorter and requires fewer output tokens, which partially offsets the increase in total execution time. Nevertheless, the cumulative overhead in API usage and execution time remains substantial relative to the marginal gain in structural accuracy. A batch size of 5 provided a balance between structural consistency and computational efficiency, maintaining high integrity scores while reducing both execution time and API call volume.

应该指出的是，该分析仅关注计算成本和模型性能。此处并未明确量化自动化 DML 构建所带来的人工建模工作量的潜在减少，因为评估并未考虑人工成本。在实际应用中，这种人力成本的考虑可能会进一步影响配置的选择。It should be noted that this analysis focuses solely on computational cost and model performance. The potential reduction in human modeling effort enabled by automated DML construction is not explicitly quantified here, as the evaluation does not incorporate human labor costs. In practical applications, such human cost considerations may further influence the choice of configuration.

对惩罚尺度参数的敏感性分析表明，完整性得分以可预测的方式响应指数惩罚公式的变化。随着惩罚规模的增加，总分增加，变异性减少。此行为与参数的预期作用一致，该参数调节结构遗漏的影响。结果的相对顺序在参数值之间保持稳定，表明指标在检查范围内表现一致。The sensitivity analysis on the penalty scale parameter shows that the integrity score responds in a predictable manner to changes in the exponential penalty formulation. As the penalty scale increases, the overall score increases and variability decreases. This behavior is consistent with the intended role of the parameter, which modulates the impact of structural omissions. The relative ordering of results remains stable across parameter values, indicating that the metric behaves consistently within the examined range.

总体而言，结果表明该框架生成了结构连贯且可重现的 DML 表示。应该注意的是，KG-DML 的布尔结构在生成成功路径时假定组件之间是独立的。在实践中，CCF可能会引入相同组件或并置组件之间的依赖关系；在这项工作中，此类效应并未进行结构建模，而是通过交互过程中的定性注释进行传达。重复运行的变异性是有限的，偏差集中在层次结构中结构最密集的部分。评估还表明，完整性得分提供了模型质量的稳定总体衡量标准，同时对批处理选择和惩罚缩放决策保持敏感。尽管该框架加速了 DML 构建，但它的目的是支持而不是取代专家建模实践。生成的模型的最终审查和验证仍然是领域专家的责任，特别是对于详细的较低级别元素。Overall, the results indicate that the framework produces a structurally coherent and reproducible DML representation. Variability across repeated runs is limited, and deviations are concentrated in the most structurally dense portion of the hierarchy. The evaluation also demonstrates that the integrity score provides a stable aggregate measure of model quality while remaining sensitive to batching choices and penalty scaling decisions. Although the framework accelerates DML construction, it is intended to support rather than replace expert modeling practice. The final review and validation of the generated model remain the responsibility of domain experts, particularly for detailed lower-level elements.

本文提出的框架的主要贡献在于减少手动提取和构建工作，同时保留专家的监督和控制。在运行过程中观察到的高结构精度和低变异性可部分归因于提取过程的受限设计。通过模式基础、有界范围和结构化生成来限制法学硕士的自由度，该框架减少了歧义并减轻了与无约束知识图谱构建相关的常见故障模式。The principal contribution of the proposed framework in this paper lies in reducing manual extraction and structuring effort while preserving expert oversight and control. The high structural accuracy and low variability observed across runs can be attributed in part to the constrained design of the extraction process. By limiting the degrees of freedom of the LLM through schema grounding, bounded scope, and structured generation, the framework reduces ambiguity and mitigates common failure modes associated with unconstrained KG construction.

尽管所提出的框架是在单个系统上演示的，但它也可以应用于子系统级别。在实践中，大型系统通常被分解为更小的部分，并且可以使用相同的方法分别为每个子系统构建 KG-DML 模型。这项工作的贡献在于提供了一种从文档构建这些模型的一致方法。然后可以将这些子系统模型组合成代表整个系统的主模型，尽管需要额外的工作来处理子系统之间的依赖关系。Although the proposed framework is demonstrated on a single system, it can also be applied at the subsystem level. In practice, large systems are usually broken down into smaller parts, and the same approach could be used to construct KG-DML models for each of these subsystems separately. The contribution of this work is in providing a consistent way to build these models from documentation. These subsystem models could then be combined into a master model representing the full system, although additional work would be needed to handle dependencies across subsystems.

7.1.局限性7.1. Limitations

应该承认本研究的一些局限性。首先，评估是在单个案例研究系统上进行的。尽管所选系统在结构上并不平凡，并且代表了安全关键型工程系统，但该框架对更大或更复杂系统的通用性尚未得到检验。特别是，在分布在多个文档中的文档量增加、组件密度更高或嵌套逻辑结构更多的情况下，模型行为可能会引入额外的可变性，而这些可变性并未完全反映在当前结果中。Several limitations of the present study should be acknowledged. First, the evaluation is conducted on a single case study system. Although the selected system is structurally nontrivial and representative of safety-critical engineered systems, the generalizability of the framework to substantially larger or more complex systems has not yet been examined. In particular, model behavior under increased documentation volume distributed across multiple documents, higher component density, or more nested logical structures may introduce additional variability that is not fully reflected in the present results.

其次，评估框架强调节点、链路、逻辑门层面的结构一致性。虽然这种方法可以系统地评估层次对齐，但它不能直接评估功能等价性。结构相似性并不一定保证向上或向下传播期间相同的逻辑行为。基于最小割集或成功路径集的比较将提供更强的行为等效性测试。然而，即使完整的割集和路径集等价也不会构成基于 DML 的模型的完整功能验证，因为这些度量在结构组件级别上运行，并且不会捕获目标到功能和子功能的分层分解或故障如何通过该功能层次结构传播。因此，功能层的专家验证仍然是割集和路径集比较的必要补充。本研究未包括割集和路径集比较，但仍然是未来工作的重要方向。Second, the evaluation framework emphasizes structural consistency at the node, link, and logical gate levels. While this approach enables systematic assessment of hierarchical alignment, it does not directly evaluate functional equivalence. Structural similarity does not necessarily guarantee identical logical behavior during upward or downward propagation. A comparison based on minimal cut sets or success path sets would provide a stronger test of behavioral equivalence. However, even complete cut set and path set equivalence would not constitute full functional validation of a DML-based model, as these metrics operate at the structural component level and do not capture the hierarchical decomposition of objectives into functions and subfunctions or how failures propagate through that functional hierarchy. Expert validation of the functional layer would therefore remain a necessary complement to cut set and path set comparison. Cut set and path set comparison was not included in this study and remains an important direction for future work.

本研究评估总体框架，而不是单个实施组件的贡献。没有评估无检索基线，因为全上下文提取对于本研究中使用的系统文档来说是不可行的：其数量大大超过了实际的上下文窗口和生成限制，使得检索成为框架的结构要求，而不是针对替代方案进行基准测试的可配置设计选择。未来的工作应包括系统的消融研究和基线比较，检查检索增强、分层构建、提示策略、模型选择和参数设置的效果。此类分析将更详细地了解影响 KG-DML 构建质量和框架性能的因素。The present study evaluates the overall framework rather than the contribution of individual implementation components. A no-retrieval baseline was not evaluated, as full-context extraction is not feasible for the system documentation used in this study: its volume substantially exceeds practical contextwindow and generation limits, making retrieval a structural requirement of the framework rather than a configurable design choice to be benchmarked against an alternative. Future work should include systematic ablation studies and baseline comparisons examining the effects of retrieval augmentation, layerwise construction, prompting strategies, model selection, and parameter settings. Such analyses would provide a more detailed understanding of the factors influencing KG-DML construction quality and framework performance.

还值得注意的是，这种评估差距并不等同于一般基于法学硕士的知识图谱构建文献中常见的消融实践，其中模型从没有预定义结构目标的非结构化文本中提取任意实体和关系，因此可以与无约束或替代提取基线进行有意义的比较。相反，当前的框架针对特定的、模式约束的功能表示，其中 LLM 在预定义的 DML 内运行It is also worth noting that this evaluation gap is not equivalent to the ablation practices common in the general LLM-based KG construction literature, where models extract arbitrary entities and relationships from unstructured text without a predefined structural target and can therefore be meaningfully compared against unconstrained or alternative extraction baselines. The present framework instead targets a specific, schema-constrained functional representation, in which the LLM operates within a predefined DML

该框架还依赖于信息选择和解释的检索配置和模型行为。由于模型构建以基于嵌入的相似性和 top-k 证据检索为条件，因此块分割、检索排名或相似性阈值的变化可能会改变提供给语言模型的上下文证据。此外，该框架是使用单个大型语言模型配置进行评估的。性能可能会因替代模型架构或功能级别而异，并且模型之间的稳健性尚未得到系统检查。模型输出也可能对提示表述和指令结构敏感，这些在本研究中是固定的。The framework also depends on retrieval configuration and model behavior for information selection and interpretation. Consequently, the current framework does not capture shared failure mechanisms or dependency strengths required for quantitative CCF analysis.

虽然 DML 模型一旦构建就支持动态推理，但提取过程基于静态文档，因此会生成系统结构的快照。时间相关或上下文相关的行为没有在当前框架中明确建模，尽管这种动态可以通过诸如时间相关延迟门公式之类的扩展来合并。While the DML model supports dynamic reasoning once constructed, the extraction process is based on static documentation and therefore yields a snapshot of the system structure. Time-dependent or contextdependent behaviors are not explicitly modeled in the present framework, though such dynamics could be incorporated through extensions such as time-dependent delay gate formulations.

最后，尽管该框架是根据构建的 KGDML 的结构准确性（例如节点、链接和逻辑一致性指标）进行评估的，但本研究中并未正式量化人类建模工作量的减少。所提出的框架使模型构建过程的部分自动化，但是这在多大程度上减少了专家劳动力尚未得到系统测量。在本案例研究中，虽然该框架似乎将 DML 模型构建时间从几个月的专家工作缩短到几天，但这一估计是基于有限的实践经验，而不是基于受控比较。比较手动和自动 DML 构建的系统评估（包括专家时间和成本的指标）将为框架的实际影响提供更严格的评估。尽管预处理和标准化用于减轻歧义，但本研究并未系统地评估噪声、不完整或高度不一致的文档下的性能。在这种条件下评估稳健性仍然是未来工作的重要方向。Finally, although the framework is evaluated in terms of the structural accuracy of the constructed KGDML (e.g., node, link, and logical consistency metrics), reductions in human modeling effort are not formally quantified in this study. The proposed framework automates portions of the model construction process, but the extent to which this reduces expert labor has not been systematically measured. While the framework appears to reduce DML model construction time from several months of expert effort to a few days in the present case study, this estimate is based on limited practical experience rather than a controlled comparison. A systematic evaluation comparing manual and automated DML construction, including metrics for expert time and cost, would provide a more rigorous assessment of the framework’s practical impact. Although preprocessing and normalization are used to mitigate ambiguity, the present study does not systematically evaluate performance under noisy, incomplete, or highly inconsistent documentation. Assessing robustness under such conditions remains an important direction for future work.

Future extensions may incorporate explicit modeling of CCF by identifying coupling mechanisms among components, such as shared design features, co-location, maintenance dependencies, or exposure to common environmental conditions. Retrieval-augmented LLM methods could be used to infer such relationships from system documentation by detecting components subject to the same root causes, enabling a more complete representation of dependency effects within the KG-DML.

Further, alternative model architectures could reduce reliance on structured preprocessing. 八、结论8. Conclusions

本研究引入了一个框架，用于直接从系统文档构建 DML 模型并将其表示为 KG-DML 结构以支持诊断推理。通过将逐层检索与结构化生成和图形合成相结合，该方法将非结构化技术文本转换为可以存储、查询和计算分析的功能层次结构。目标不是取代专家建模，而是减少从文档中提取和组织系统结构所需的手动工作。This study introduced a framework for constructing DML models directly from system documentation and representing them as a KG-DML structure to support diagnostic reasoning. By combining layer-bylayer retrieval with structured generation and graph synthesis, the approach converts unstructured technical text into a functional hierarchy that can be stored, queried, and analyzed computationally. The objective was not to replace expert modeling, but to reduce the manual effort required to extract and organize system structure from documentation.

Higher-level goals and functions were identified without variation, while minor differences appeared at the Component and Subfunction-Component layers, where structural density and logical combinations are greater. The evaluation framework, including layer-level metrics and an integrity score that accounts for structural omissions, provided a systematic way to assess model completeness and logical consistency. Sensitivity analysis highlighted the trade-off between batching configuration and structural stability.

In this architecture, the language model interprets user queries and invokes predefined graph-based tools, while probabilistic and logical reasoning remains governed by the encoded model structure. The evaluation metrics reported include precision, recall, and the 𝐹2 score for both node-level and link-level results. In addition, gate accuracy is reported for link-level relationships and measures whether the logical operator assigned to each parent group matches the reference model. All metrics are bounded between 0 and 1, where a value of 1 indicates perfect performance.
