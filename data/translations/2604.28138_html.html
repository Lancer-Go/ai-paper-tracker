9. 结论Conclusion

代理沙箱会累积现有 C/R 机制无法实现的操作系统状态 捕获或捕获过于激进。根本原因是代理与操作系统之间的语义差距 这使得两层都没有有效恢复所需的信息。Agent sandboxes accumulate OS state that existing C/R mechanisms either fail to capture or capture too aggressively. The root cause is an agent–OS semantic gap that leaves neither layer with the information needed for efficient recovery.螃蟹Crab通过观察大多数代理轮次不产生任何结果来弥补这一差距 恢复相关状态，启用推断检查点的主机端运行时 来自操作系统可见效果的粒度，将 C/R 与 LLM 等待时间重叠，以及 跨同地沙箱安排检查点流量。关于外壳密集型和 代码修复工作负载，bridges this gap by observing that most agent turns produce no recovery-relevant state, enabling a host-side runtime that infers checkpoint granularity from OS-visible effects, overlaps C/R with LLM wait time, and schedules checkpoint traffic across co-located sandboxes. On shell-intensive and code-repair workloads,螃蟹Crab将恢复正确率从 8% 提高到 100%，检查站流量减少高达 87%，并保持在 1.9% 以内 无故障执行时间。更广泛地说，我们的结果表明操作系统可见 不透明工作负载的影响可以作为轻量级语义信号 自适应状态管理——适用于 C/R 之外的调度的原则， 资源隔离、代理沙箱安全审计。raises recovery correctness from 8% to 100%, cuts checkpoint traffic by up to 87%, and stays within 1.9% of fault-free execution time. More broadly, our results suggest that OS-visible effects of opaque workloads can serve as lightweight semantic signals for adaptive state management—a principle applicable beyond C/R to scheduling, resource isolation, and security auditing of agent sandboxes.
