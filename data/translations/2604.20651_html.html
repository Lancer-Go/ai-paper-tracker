摘要Abstract

了解在线话语的复杂动态取决于大规模的审议数据，由于限制性的可访问性政策、道德问题和不一致的数据质量，这种资源在交互式网络平台上仍然稀缺。在本文中，我们建议Understanding the intricate dynamics of online discourse depends on large-scale deliberation data, a resource that remains scarce across interactive web platforms due to restrictive accessibility policies, ethical concerns and inconsistent data quality. In this paper, we propose合唱团Chorus，一个代理框架，它协调具有行为一致角色的法学硕士驱动的参与者，以产生现实的审议讨论。每个参与者都由一个配备了不断发展的讨论记忆的自主代理控制，而参与时间则由基于原则的泊松过程的时间模型控制，该模型近似于真实用户的异构参与模式。该框架得到结构化工具使用的进一步支持，使参与者能够访问外部资源并促进与交互式网络平台的集成。该框架部署在, an agentic framework, which orchestrates LLM-powered actors with behaviorally consistent personas to generate realistic deliberation discussions. Each actor is governed by an autonomous agent equipped with memory of the evolving discussion, while participation timing is governed by a principled Poisson process-based temporal model, which approximates the heterogeneous engagement patterns of real users. The framework is further supported by structured tool usage, enabling actors to access external resources and facilitating integration with interactive web platforms. The framework was deployed on the故意的Deliberate平台由 30 位专家参与者从三个维度进行评估：内容真实性、讨论连贯性和分析实用性，确认platform and evaluated by 30 expert participants across three dimensions: content realism, discussion coherence and analytical utility, confirming合唱团Chorus作为生成适合在线话语分析的高质量审议数据的实用工具。as a practical tool for generating high-quality deliberation data suitable for online discourse analysis.

1 简介Introduction

审议数据涵盖在线参与性话语背景下的所有形式的结构化和非结构化记录，包括在旨在促进公民之间理性和包容性讨论的网络平台内生成的辩论交流、意见表达和互动响应。Deliberation data encompasses all forms of structured and unstructured records in the context of online participatory discourse, including argumentative exchanges, opinion expressions and interactive responses, generated within web platforms designed to facilitate rational and inclusive discussion among citizens(Behrendt et al., 2025)。这些数据构成了在线话语分析的关键资源，支撑着从研究审议动态和沟通模式到对自然语言处理流程进行基准测试等目标. Such data constitute a critical resource for online discourse analysis, underpinning objectives ranging from studying deliberation dynamics and communication patterns to benchmarking natural language processing pipelines(Lawrence and Reed, 2019; Steenbergen et al., 2003)。然而，获取足够规模的审议数据构成了重大挑战，平台访问限制、围绕用户隐私的道德考虑以及可用数据集质量不一致，加剧了这一挑战. However, the acquisition of deliberation data at sufficient scale poses a significant challenge, exacerbated by platform access restrictions, ethical considerations surrounding user privacy and inconsistent quality of available datasets(Argyle et al., 2023; Bisbee et al., 2023)。随着研究、政策制定和数字平台治理对大规模公共话语分析的需求不断增长，审议数据稀缺的问题变得越来越严重，这凸显了对能够生成高质量、现实且道德上健全的审议数据的替代方法的需求。. As the demand for large-scale public discourse analysis grows across research, policy-making and digital platform governance, the problem of deliberation data scarcity becomes increasingly acute, underscoring the need for alternative approaches capable of generating high-quality, realistic and ethically sound deliberation data.

尽管仿真驱动的方法在复杂交互设置建模方面表现出了卓越的能力Although simulation-driven approaches have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in modeling complex interactive settings(Park et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2024)，它们在审议数据生成中的应用仍然受到几个基本限制的限制。最值得注意的是，此类框架主要关注目标导向的任务，而不是真正的参与式交流所特有的开放式、多轮协商性话语特征, their application to deliberation data generation remains constrained by several fundamental limitations. Most notably, such frameworks exhibit a predominant focus on goal-directed tasks, rather than the open-ended, multi-turn deliberative discourse characteristic of authentic participatory exchanges(Liu et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2025)。结合用户角色的现有方法主要是为了安全测试和软件评估而开发的. Existing approaches incorporating user personas have been primarily developed for security testing and software evaluation(Lu et al., 2025; Mayr et al., 2025)，在产生持续的、上下文连贯的对话和模拟真实交互过程中观察到的新兴多用户动态方面仍然受到限制, remaining limited in producing sustained, contextually coherent conversations and emulating the emergent multi-user dynamics observed during authentic interaction(Casoria et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024)。此外，塑造互联网话语的异构时间模式在很大程度上没有得到解决，因为当前的解决方案缺乏对真实平台的不同参与率特征进行建模的原则性机制。这些缺点强调需要将行为保真度、时间现实性和情境意识结合起来的框架，以充分支持协商环境。. Furthermore, the heterogeneous temporal patterns shaping internet discourse are largely unaddressed, as current solutions lack principled mechanisms for modeling the diverse participation rates characteristic of real platforms. These shortcomings underscore the need for frameworks combining behavioral fidelity, temporal realism and contextual awareness to adequately support deliberative environments.

2 相关工作Related work

交互式网络平台的有效模拟框架的开发代表了两个基本研究方向的融合：（1）代理编排架构，它协调多个由法学硕士支持的代理，通过共享平台模拟真实的用户到用户交互；（2）基于角色的模拟策略，它生成行为真实的合成用户。第一个方向侧重于多智能体模拟，包括编排能力、协调机制和现实多用户交互所需的社会动态。第二个方向涉及行为多样化的代理角色的构建，包括数据驱动的行为基础的关键工作。在本节的其余部分中，我们对这些领域进行了全面的回顾，强调了它们的贡献和局限性，这些贡献和局限性推动了The development of effective simulation frameworks for interactive web platforms represents a confluence of two fundamental research directions: (1) agentic orchestration architectures, which coordinate multiple LLM-powered agents to simulate authentic user-to-user interactions through shared platforms and (2) persona-based simulation strategies, which generate behaviorally realistic synthetic users. The first direction focuses on multi-agent simulation, including orchestration capabilities, coordination mechanisms and the social dynamics necessary for realistic multi-user interaction. The second direction concerns the construction of behaviorally diverse agent personas, including critical work on data-driven behavioral grounding. In the rest of this section, we provide a comprehensive review across these areas, highlighting their contributions and limitations which motivate the development of合唱团Chorus.

Park et al. (2023)在《超人前传》中部署了 25 个由 LLM 驱动的智能体，每个智能体都配备了观察、记忆、反思和规划模块，展示了自动传播活动邀请、建立关系和协调小组活动等应急能力。该架构表明，基于记忆和规划机制的 LLM 驱动的代理可以产生可信的社交行为，而无需明确的脚本。然而，该框架仍然与真实的交互平台隔离， 生成的内容必须与正在进行的内容保持语义一致性 和动态发展的审议。deployed 25 LLM-powered agents in “Smallville”, each equipped with observation, memory with reflection and planning modules, demonstrating emergent capabilities such as autonomously spreading event invitations, forming relationships and coordinating group activities. The architecture established that LLM-powered agents grounded in memory and planning mechanisms can produce believable social behaviors without explicit scripting. However, the framework remains isolated from real interactive platforms, where generated content must maintain semantic coherence with an ongoing and dynamically evolving deliberation.

Sun et al. (2025)引入了 Persona-L，利用法学硕士和基于能力的框架来生成具有复杂需求的人的角色，证明仔细的提示策略可以将角色多样性扩展到典型的人口代表性之外。该方法生成综合用户配置文件，捕获能力、偏好和行为特征的细微组合，尽管它单独解决角色构建问题，而没有对多个并发角色如何在共享交互环境中共同塑造话语进行建模。introduced Persona-L, leveraging LLMs and an ability-based framework to generate personas of people with complex needs, demonstrating that careful prompting strategies can expand persona diversity beyond typical demographic representations. The methodology produces synthetic user profiles capturing nuanced combinations of abilities, preferences and behavioral characteristics, though it addresses persona construction in isolation without modeling how multiple concurrent personas collectively shape discourse within shared interactive environments.

Yang et al. (2024)使用 OASIS 进行大规模代理模拟，在模拟社交媒体平台上支持多达一百万个基于 LLM 的代理，复制信息传播、群体极化和羊群效应等现象。作者证明，更大的代理群体规模会增强群体动态和更现实的集体行为，将规模确立为模拟保真度的关键因素。尽管取得了这些进步，该框架仍然面向建模广泛的社会动态，而不是生成结构化参与平台所需的语义连贯、多轮协商的话语。scaled agentic simulation with OASIS, supporting up to one million LLM-based agents on simulated social media platforms, replicating phenomena including information spreading, group polarization and herd effects. The authors demonstrated that larger agent group scale leads to enhanced group dynamics and more realistic emergent collective behaviors, establishing scale as a critical factor in simulation fidelity. Despite these advances, the framework remains oriented toward modeling broad social dynamics rather than generating the semantically coherent, multi-turn deliberative discourse required for structured participatory platforms.

Li et al. (2024)证明与数据驱动的行为模式相比，基于人口统计的角色表现出有限的预测准确性，当引导法学硕士转向从实际用户响应数据提取的行为模式时，性能得到显着提高。这一发现促进了将经验行为基础整合到角色设计中，尽管这项工作并未扩展到对真实平台活动的时间参与动态或多方话语结构特征进行建模。demonstrated that demographic-based personas exhibit limited predictive accuracy compared to data-driven behavioral patterns, with substantial performance improvements when steering LLMs toward behavioral patterns extracted from actual user response data. This finding motivates the integration of empirical behavioral grounding into persona design, though the work does not extend to modeling temporal participation dynamics or multi-party discourse structures characteristic of real platform activity.

Casoria et al. (2025)发现模型审查会影响法学硕士生成的角色捕捉有争议或“负面”特征（例如低宜人性或高神经质）的能力，从而减少合成用户群体的行为多样性。这种限制与商议模拟尤其相关，其中怀疑的、逆向的或不参与的用户原型对于生成接近真实参与性话语的全部范围的讨论至关重要。found that model censorship impacts the ability of LLM-generated personas to capture controversial or “negative” traits such as low agreeableness or high neuroticism, reducing behavioral diversity in synthetic user populations. This limitation is particularly relevant for deliberation simulation, where skeptical, contrarian, or disengaged user archetypes are essential for generating discussions that approximate the full spectrum of authentic participatory discourse.

尽管这两个研究方向取得了进展，但它们对实际审议数据生成的适用性仍然有限。现有的代理框架在独立的虚拟环境中模拟社会动态，而无需与真实平台交互，而基于角色的方法增强了行为现实性，但没有模拟并发参与者如何随着时间的推移集体塑造话语。这两条工作线共同的另一个限制是缺乏原则性的时间建模，因为表征有机平台活动的参与模式和参与不对称仍然超出了当前方法的范围。Despite the advances reported across these two research directions, their applicability to realistic deliberation data generation remains limited. Existing agentic frameworks simulate social dynamics within self-contained virtual environments without interfacing with real platforms, while persona-based methods enhance behavioral realism but do not model how concurrent participants collectively shape discourse over time. A further limitation shared across both lines of work is the absence of principled temporal modeling, as the participation patterns and engagement asymmetries characterizing organic platform activity remain outside the scope of current approaches.合唱团Chorus通过在统一编排框架内的交互平台上直接部署角色驱动的代理，将行为一致性与参与动态的随机公式相结合，以生成真实的审议数据，从而展现真实用户交互的内容多样性和时间结构，从而弥补这些差距。bridges these gaps by deploying persona-driven agents directly on interactive platforms within a unified orchestration framework, coupling behavioral consistency with a stochastic formulation of participation dynamics to produce realistic deliberation data exhibiting both the content diversity and temporal structure of authentic user interaction.

5 结论和未来研究Conclusions and Future Research

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个基于代理的框架，名为In this work, we presented an agentic-based framework named合唱团Chorus，它协调具有行为一致角色的法学硕士支持的参与者，在交互式网络平台上生成真实的审议数据。该框架解决了在线话语分析中的一个关键瓶颈：限制性平台访问政策、围绕用户隐私的道德约束以及不一致的数据集质量造成的大规模审议数据的稀缺。通过将合成用户行为基于结构化角色、基于原则的泊松过程的时间模型和结构化工具的使用，, which orchestrates LLM-powered actors with behaviorally consistent personas to generate realistic deliberation data on interactive web platforms. The framework addresses a critical bottleneck in online discourse analysis: the scarcity of large-scale deliberation data imposed by restrictive platform access policies, ethical constraints surrounding user privacy and inconsistent dataset quality. By grounding synthetic user behavior in structured personas, a principled Poisson process-based temporal model and structured tool usage,合唱团Chorus为研究人员和平台运营商提供受控但丰富多样的审议数据源，适用于话语分析、平台演示和 NLP 管道评估。provides researchers and platform operators with a controlled yet richly diverse source of deliberation data suitable for discourse analysis, platform demonstration and NLP pipeline evaluation.

合唱团Chorus被部署在was deployed on the故意的Deliberate气候适应试点场景中的平台，在 20 分钟的模拟范围内实例化 10 个参与者，涵盖四种角色原型。该框架成功地产生了异质性讨论，展示了真实参与性话语的参与不对称性、话题多样性和互动模式特征。 30 名参与者的初步专家评估在内容真实性、分析实用性和讨论连贯性方面取得了令人鼓舞的结果，证实了这一点platform within a climate adaptation pilot scenario, instantiating 10 actors across four persona archetypes over a 20-minute simulation horizon. The framework successfully produced a heterogeneous discussion exhibiting the engagement asymmetry, topical diversity and interaction patterns characteristic of authentic participatory discourse. Preliminary expert evaluation across 30 participants yielded encouraging results relative to content realism, analytical utility and discussion coherence, confirming that合唱团Chorus-生成的数据构成了真实用户参与平台演示和分析管道验证的可靠替代品。-generated data constitutes a credible substitute for real user participation in platform demonstration and analytical pipeline validation.

当前评估的一个关键限制是缺乏单独框架组件贡献的消融研究。与更简单的基线进行比较，例如单个法学硕士整体生成完整的讨论，或者省略角色驱动的行为基础或基于泊松过程的时间建模的配置，将为每个设计决策的必要性提供直接的定量证据。解决这一差距是未来工作的重要方向。此外，当前的评估没有量化工具调用频率或其对内容质量的影响，并且完全依赖于 Claude Sonnet 4.5，而没有检查工具使用的经验贡献和对模型替换的敏感性。计划对工具使用模式和跨模型评估进行系统分析，作为该框架即将扩展的一部分。A key limitation of the current evaluation concerns the absence of ablation studies isolating the contribution of individual framework components. Comparisons against simpler baselines, such as a single LLM generating the complete discussion monolithically, or configurations omitting persona-driven behavioral grounding or Poisson process-based temporal modeling, would provide direct quantitative evidence for the necessity of each design decision. Addressing this gap constitutes an important direction for future work. Moreover, the current evaluation does not quantify tool invocation frequency or its effect on content quality and relies exclusively on Claude Sonnet 4.5, leaving the empirical contribution of tool usage and the sensitivity to model substitution unexamined. A systematic analysis of tool utilization patterns and cross-model evaluation are planned as part of the framework’s forthcoming extension.

在未来的工作中，我们打算扩展当前的功能In our future work, we intend to extend the current capabilities of合唱团Chorus通过参与对抗性模拟，其中专门的原型引入了破坏性行为，例如两极分化的言论和故意的错误信息，从而能够在受控条件下研究平台的弹性。第二个方向涉及自适应演员原型，其中行为策略根据不断变化的讨论状态进行迭代细化，超越静态角色参数化转向动态代理适应。最后，我们打算进行更大规模的验证研究，包括与真实审议数据的比较评估以及跨不同政策领域的部署，长期目标是建立by involving adversarial simulation, where specialized archetypes introduce disruptive behaviors such as polarizing rhetoric and deliberate misinformation, enabling the study of platform resilience under controlled conditions. A second direction concerns adaptive actor archetypes, wherein behavioral strategies are iteratively refined in response to the evolving discussion state, moving beyond static persona parameterization toward dynamic agent adaptation. Finally, we intend to conduct larger-scale validation studies involving comparative evaluation against real deliberation data and deployment across diverse policy domains, with the long-term goal of establishing合唱团Chorus作为参与式平台研究的强大基准测试工具。as a robust benchmarking tool for participatory platform research.
