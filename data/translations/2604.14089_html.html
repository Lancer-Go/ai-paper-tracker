UMI-3D：将通用操作界面从视觉受限扩展到 3D 空间感知UMI-3D: Extending Universal Manipulation Interface from Vision-Limited to 3D Spatial Perception

Ziming Wang1,2 1HKU, 2 USTC
https://umi-3d.github.io
摘要Abstract

我们提出了 UMI-3D，这是通用操作接口 (UMI) 的多模式扩展，用于在具体操作中进行稳健且可扩展的数据收集。虽然 UMI 可以实现便携式腕式数据采集，但它对单目视觉 SLAM 的依赖使其容易受到遮挡、动态场景和跟踪失败的影响，从而限制了其在现实环境中的适用性。 UMI-3D 通过引入紧密集成到腕戴式接口中的轻量级低成本 LiDAR 传感器来解决这些限制，从而使以 LiDAR 为中心的 SLAM 在具有挑战性的条件下能够进行精确的公制尺度姿态估计。我们进一步开发了硬件同步的多模态传感管道和统一的时空校准框架，将视觉观察与 LiDAR 点云对齐，生成一致的 3D 演示表示。尽管保持了原始的 2D 视觉运动政策制定，UMI-3D 显着提高了收集数据的质量和可靠性，这直接转化为增强的政策绩效。大量的现实世界实验表明，UMI-3D 不仅在标准操作任务上取得了很高的成功率，而且还能够学习对于原始仅视觉 UMI 设置具有挑战性或不可行的任务，包括大型可变形物体操作和铰接式物体操作。该系统支持用于数据采集、对齐、训练和部署的端到端管道，同时保留原始 UMI 的可移植性和可访问性。所有硬件和软件组件都是开源的，以促进大规模数据收集并加速实体智能的研究：We present UMI-3D, a multimodal extension of the Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI) for robust and scalable data collection in embodied manipulation. While UMI enables portable, wrist-mounted data acquisition, its reliance on monocular visual SLAM makes it vulnerable to occlusions, dynamic scenes, and tracking failures, limiting its applicability in real-world environments. UMI-3D addresses these limitations by introducing a lightweight and low-cost LiDAR sensor tightly integrated into the wrist-mounted interface, enabling LiDAR-centric SLAM with accurate metric-scale pose estimation under challenging conditions. We further develop a hardware-synchronized multimodal sensing pipeline and a unified spatiotemporal calibration framework that aligns visual observations with LiDAR point clouds, producing consistent 3D representations of demonstrations. Despite maintaining the original 2D visuomotor policy formulation, UMI-3D significantly improves the quality and reliability of collected data, which directly translates into enhanced policy performance. Extensive real-world experiments demonstrate that UMI-3D not only achieves high success rates on standard manipulation tasks, but also enables learning of tasks that are challenging or infeasible for the original vision-only UMI setup, including large deformable object manipulation and articulated object operation. The system supports an end-to-end pipeline for data acquisition, alignment, training, and deployment, while preserving the portability and accessibility of the original UMI. All hardware and software components are open-sourced to facilitate large-scale data collection and accelerate research in embodied intelligence:https://umi-3d.github.io.

I 简介Introduction

数据驱动的机器人学习的最新进展凸显了可扩展和高质量演示数据的重要性Recent advances in data-driven robot learning have highlighted the importance of scalable and high-quality demonstration data[4, 37, 2]。通用操纵接口 (UMI) 代表着朝着这一目标迈出的重要一步，通过腕部安装的传感实现低成本、便携式数据收集，有效地弥合了人类演示和机器人执行之间的体现差距. The Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI) represents a significant step toward this goal by enabling low-cost, portable data collection through wrist-mounted sensing, effectively bridging the embodiment gap between human demonstrations and robot execution[5]。通过利用视觉感知和手持操作，UMI 允许用户在现实环境中收集各种操作数据，而无需专门的基础设施，从而为超越传统实验室设置的具体数据采集开辟了新范例。. By leveraging visual perception and handheld operation, UMI allows users to collect diverse manipulation data in real-world environments without specialized infrastructure, opening a new paradigm for embodied data acquisition beyond traditional laboratory settings.

最近的研究进一步表明，这种数据收集范式的有效性不仅仅是经验性的，而且遵循可预测的扩展行为。特别是，先前关于机器人模仿学习中数据缩放定律的工作表明，随着高质量演示数据数量的增加，策略性能不断提高，遵循类似于语言和视觉模型中观察到的幂律趋势Recent studies have further revealed that the effectiveness of such data collection paradigms is not merely empirical, but follows predictable scaling behavior. In particular, prior work on data scaling laws in robotic imitation learning demonstrates that policy performance improves consistently with increasing amounts of high-quality demonstration data, following power-law trends analogous to those observed in language and vision models[15]。这些发现表明，收集大规模、多样化且一致的数据的能力是推进具身智能的主要瓶颈。最近的大规模研究进一步强化了这一观点，例如 GEN-0，它在超过 270,000 小时的现实世界交互数据上训练通用操纵策略，明确验证了机器人技术中的缩放定律假设. These findings indicate that the ability to collect large-scale, diverse, and well-aligned data is a primary bottleneck for advancing embodied intelligence. This perspective is further reinforced by recent large-scale efforts such as GEN-0, which trains generalist manipulation policies on over 270,000 hours of real-world interaction data, explicitly validating the scaling law hypothesis in robotics[27]。正如所证明的，增加预训练数据的规模会导致下游任务性能和泛化的可预测的改进，突出可扩展的数据采集系统在实现下一代具体基础模型方面的关键作用。总而言之，这些结果使 UMI 等系统不仅成为数据收集工具，而且成为扩展机器人学习的关键基础设施。. As demonstrated, increasing the scale of pretraining data leads to predictable improvements in downstream task performance and generalization, highlighting the critical role of scalable data acquisition systems in enabling next-generation embodied foundation models. Taken together, these results position systems like UMI not only as data collection tools, but as key infrastructure for scaling robot learning.

尽管 UMI 作为可扩展的数据收集接口非常有效，但它仍然继承了其依赖单目视觉 SLAM 进行姿态估计的基本局限性Despite its effectiveness as a scalable data collection interface, UMI still inherits fundamental limitations from its reliance on monocular visual SLAM for pose estimation[3]。正如原作中所承认的，该系统需要具有足够丰富视觉特征的环境来保持稳定的跟踪，并且可能在无纹理区域（例如空白墙壁或光线不足的场景）中失败. As acknowledged in the original work, the system requires environments with sufficiently rich visual features to maintain stable tracking, and may fail in textureless regions such as blank walls or poorly lit scenes[5]。更关键的是，后续大规模UMI数据采集的实证研究进一步揭示了视觉SLAM对遮挡和动态场景变化高度敏感。例如，在操作部分或完全遮挡摄像机视野的大型物体（例如打开门或抽屉）时，SLAM 系统可能会误解运动，甚至完全失去跟踪，从而限制可行任务的范围. More critically, subsequent empirical studies on large-scale UMI data collection further reveal that visual SLAM is highly sensitive to occlusions and dynamic scene changes. For instance, during the manipulation of large objects that partially or fully block the camera view, such as opening doors or drawers, the SLAM system may misinterpret motion or even lose tracking entirely, thereby limiting the range of feasible tasks[15]。同样，视觉特征不足或存在移动干扰物的环境可能会导致频繁的跟踪失败，需要仔细的场景设计或事后数据过滤。这些限制给扩大数据收集带来了根本性的压力。虽然大规模和多样化的数据对于学习通用策略至关重要，但视觉 SLAM 的可靠性对可捕获的环境、任务和交互施加了隐含的约束。结果，数据收集过程变得偏向于有利于视觉 SLAM 的场景，从而阻碍了鲁棒性并限制了实体学习系统的可扩展性，同时也增加了数据处理和管理的成本。这些限制表明，可扩展的体现数据收集的瓶颈不仅仅在于界面设计，而从根本上在于姿态估计的可靠性。. Similarly, environments with insufficient visual features or the presence of moving distractors can lead to frequent tracking failures, requiring careful scene design or post hoc data filtering. These limitations introduce a fundamental tension in scaling up data collection. While large-scale and diverse data are essential for learning generalizable policies, the reliability of visual SLAM imposes implicit constraints on the environments, tasks, and interactions that can be captured. As a result, the data collection process becomes biased toward scenarios that are favorable for visual SLAM, hindering robustness and limiting the scalability of embodied learning systems, while also increasing the cost of data processing and curation. These limitations suggest that the bottleneck of scalable embodied data collection does not lie merely in interface design, but fundamentally in the reliability of pose estimation.

在这项工作中，我们提出了 UMI-3D，这是 UMI 框架的以 LiDAR 为中心的扩展，它重新思考了 SLAM 在实体数据采集中的作用。所提出的系统的概述如图 1 所示。In this work, we propose UMI-3D, a LiDAR-centric extension of the UMI framework that rethinks the role of SLAM in embodied data acquisition. An overview of the proposed system is shown in Fig.1。我们没有将 SLAM 视为跟踪的辅助组件，而是将 SLAM 提升为确保度量一致、时间上对齐的感知-动作数据的核心机制。. Instead of treating SLAM as an auxiliary component for tracking, we elevate SLAM to a core mechanism for ensuring metric-consistent, temporally aligned perception-action data.

具体来说，UMI-3D 引入了一种轻型、腕式 LiDAR 视觉传感系统，具有紧密同步的多模态测量，可实现稳健且独立的姿态估计，而无需依赖外部基础设施或特定环境的假设。通过利用以 LiDAR 为中心的 SLAM，我们的系统在遮挡、无纹理区域、光照变化和动态交互下保持稳定，有效克服了视觉 SLAM 的基本限制。这种转变有两个重要的后果。首先，它显着提高了所收集数据的可靠性和质量，减少了环境管理和事后过滤的需要。其次，更重要的是，它扩展了数据收集的可行任务分配。以前在视觉 SLAM 下具有挑战性或不可行的任务，例如拉窗帘、开门和严重遮挡下的操作，现在都可以大规模收集。Specifically, UMI-3D introduces a lightweight, wrist-mounted LiDAR-visual sensing system with tightly synchronized multimodal measurements, enabling robust and self-contained pose estimation without reliance on external infrastructure or environment-specific assumptions. By leveraging LiDAR-centric SLAM, our system remains stable under occlusions, textureless regions, illumination variations, and dynamic interactions, effectively overcoming the fundamental limitations of visual SLAM. This shift has two important consequences. First, it significantly improves the reliability and quality of collected data, reducing the need for environment curation and post hoc filtering. Second, and more importantly, it expands the feasible task distribution for data collection. Tasks that were previously challenging or infeasible under visual SLAM, such as curtain pulling, door opening, and manipulation under severe occlusion, become consistently collectable at scale.

我们认为，从以视觉为中心的感知到几何感知的感知的转变对于扩展现实环境中的具体数据收集至关重要。从这个角度来看，SLAM 不仅充当定位模块，而且充当在统一度量空间中对齐感知和行动的基本机制，形成可扩展的体现学习系统的基础。We argue that this transition from vision-centric to geometry-aware perception is essential for scaling embodied data collection in real-world environments. In this view, SLAM serves not only as a localization module, but as a fundamental mechanism that aligns perception and action in a unified metric space, forming the foundation for scalable embodied learning systems.

II 相关作品Related Works

Ⅱ-AII-A UMI 变体和系统演进UMI Variants and System Evolution

UMI 式系统的有效性关键取决于用于末端执行器姿态估计的机制。如上所述，对单目视觉 SLAM 的依赖在鲁棒性和可扩展性方面引入了根本限制，特别是在遮挡、动态交互和特征稀疏环境下。为了应对这些挑战，最近的工作探索了类 UMI 系统的替代本地化策略，大致可分为两个方向。The effectiveness of UMI-style systems critically depends on the mechanism used for end-effector pose estimation. As discussed above, the reliance on monocular visual SLAM introduces fundamental limitations in robustness and scalability, particularly under occlusions, dynamic interactions, and feature-sparse environments. To address these challenges, recent works have explored alternative localization strategies for UMI-like systems, which can be broadly categorized into two directions.

第一个方向用外部跟踪系统完全取代视觉 SLAM。例如，基于红外跟踪的运动捕捉解决方案（例如 HTC Vive Tracker）可在受控环境中提供毫米级精度和稳健的姿态估计The first direction replaces visual SLAM entirely with external tracking systems. For example, motion capture solutions based on infrared tracking, such as HTC Vive Tracker, provide millimeter-level accuracy and robust pose estimation in controlled environments[18]。然而，这些系统需要基站等外部基础设施，这大大降低了便携性，增加了成本，并且与 UMI 的野外设计理念相矛盾。此外，在强光照、遮挡或安装空间有限的环境中，它们的性能可能会下降。. However, these systems require external infrastructure such as base stations, which significantly reduces portability, increases cost, and contradicts the in-the-wild design philosophy of UMI. Furthermore, their performance may degrade in environments with strong lighting, occlusions, or limited installation space.

第二个方向侧重于提高视觉 SLAM 范式的可用性和鲁棒性。一个代表性的例子是FastUMIThe second direction focuses on improving usability and robustness within the visual SLAM paradigm. A representative example is FastUMI[17]，它用现成的跟踪模块（例如英特尔实感 T265）取代了原始的 VIO 管道，从而降低了校准复杂性并实现了即插即用部署。同样，其他系统利用具有内置 SLAM 功能的消费设备，例如 Meta Quest 耳机, which replaces the original VIO pipeline with an off-the-shelf tracking module, such as the Intel RealSense T265, thereby reducing calibration complexity and enabling plug-and-play deployment. Similarly, other systems leverage consumer devices with built-in SLAM capabilities, such as Meta Quest headsets[32, 35]或 iPhone ARKitor iPhone ARKit[29, 13, 6, 9, 31]，提供集成姿态跟踪，无需自定义 SLAM 实现。这些方法降低了非专家用户在数据收集方面的障碍。, to provide integrated pose tracking without requiring custom SLAM implementations. These approaches lower the barrier for non-expert users in data collection.

然而，我们认为这些方法主要是提高工程便利性，而不是解决视觉 SLAM 的内在局限性。首先，商业 SLAM 模块通常是闭源的，并且作为黑匣子系统运行，限制了它们对不同任务、环境和传感配置的适应性。这限制了利用 SLAM 社区丰富的研究成果进行特定任务优化的能力。其次，将跟踪模块与观察传感器分离会导致时间不一致。在 FastUMI 等系统中However, we argue that these approaches primarily improve engineering convenience rather than addressing the intrinsic limitations of visual SLAM. First, commercial SLAM modules are typically closed-source and operate as black-box systems, limiting their adaptability to different tasks, environments, and sensing configurations. This restricts the ability to leverage the rich body of research in the SLAM community for task-specific optimization. Second, decoupling the tracking module from the observation sensor introduces temporal inconsistency. In systems such as FastUMI[17, 16]，姿态估计由专用跟踪设备（例如 T265）执行，而视觉观察由单独的相机（例如 GoPro）捕获。这破坏了观察和行动之间的时间一致性，可能会在动态操作任务中引入系统错误。相比之下，原始的 UMI 设计使用统一的传感源进行观察和 SLAM，确保严格的时间对齐。最后，视觉SLAM的局限性从根本上根源在于其对图像特征的依赖。虽然商业系统可以通过更好的工程和优化来提高鲁棒性，但它们无法完全克服无纹理环境、严重遮挡或动态干扰等挑战。, pose estimation is performed by a dedicated tracking device (e.g., T265), while visual observations are captured by a separate camera (e.g., GoPro). This disrupts the temporal alignment between observations and actions, potentially introducing systematic errors in dynamic manipulation tasks. In contrast, the original UMI design uses a unified sensing source for both observation and SLAM, ensuring strict temporal alignment. Finally, the limitations of visual SLAM are fundamentally rooted in its reliance on image features. While commercial systems may improve robustness through better engineering and optimization, they cannot fully overcome challenges such as textureless environments, severe occlusions, or dynamic disturbances.

这些观察结果表明，仅改进视觉 SLAM 不足以实现可靠且可扩展的野外操作数据收集。现有的 UMI 变体主要侧重于提高特定实现中的可用性或鲁棒性，但缺乏对具体数据收集的可靠姿态估计机制的原则性理解。These observations suggest that improving visual SLAM alone is insufficient for achieving reliable and scalable in-the-wild manipulation data collection. Existing UMI variants primarily focus on improving usability or robustness within specific implementations, yet lack a principled understanding of what constitutes a reliable pose estimation mechanism for embodied data collection.

为了弥补这一差距，我们从原则角度重新审视 UMI 式系统的设计。以第 2 节中的讨论为基础。 一、我们确定了一个设计良好的 UMI 系统应该满足的三个关键原则：To bridge this gap, we revisit the design of UMI-style systems from a principled perspective. Building upon the discussion in Sec. I, we identify three key principles that a well-designed UMI system should satisfy:

  1. 1.

    独立的姿态估计。Self-contained pose estimation.该系统应完全依赖安装在夹具上的传感器，从而无需外部基础设施并实现完全便携式部署。The system should rely exclusively on sensors mounted on the gripper, eliminating the need for external infrastructure and enabling fully portable deployment.

  2. 2.

    多模态传感中的时空一致性。Spatiotemporal consistency in multimodal sensing.多模态感知必须通过准确的时间同步和精确的空间校准保持严格的对准，确保跨传感器观测的一致性。Multimodal perception must maintain strict alignment through accurate time synchronization and precise spatial calibration, ensuring coherent cross-sensor observations.

  3. 3.

    定位精度的一致性。Consistency of localization accuracy.可靠的姿态估计系统应该在不同的环境、任务和交互条件下保持稳定一致的精度，而不是仅在有利或受限的场景下表现出强大的性能。在环境条件和任务动态无法控制的情况下，这种一致性对于确保大规模可靠的数据收集至关重要。A reliable pose estimation system should maintain stable and consistent accuracy across diverse environments, tasks, and interaction conditions, rather than exhibiting strong performance only under favorable or constrained scenarios. This consistency is critical for ensuring reliable data collection at scale, where environmental conditions and task dynamics cannot be controlled.

这些原则为评估现有 UMI 变体提供了统一的框架，并直接指导我们提出的系统的设计。特别是，它们推动了从以视觉为中心的定位向以激光雷达为中心的感知的转变，这构成了我们方法的基础。These principles provide a unified framework for evaluating existing UMI variants and directly guide the design of our proposed system. In particular, they motivate a shift from vision-centric localization toward LiDAR-centric perception, which forms the foundation of our approach.

Ⅱ-BII-B 从视觉 SLAM 到以激光雷达为中心的 SLAMFrom Visual SLAM to LiDAR-centric SLAM

SLAM 系统可大致分为基于视觉的范例和基于激光雷达的范例，这两种范例在过去几十年中基本上并行发展。视觉 SLAM 受益于低成本传感和丰富的语义信息，而 LiDAR SLAM 在充满挑战的环境中提供卓越的几何精度和鲁棒性。因此，这两种范式都已成为不同应用领域的主导方法。SLAM systems can be broadly categorized into vision-based and LiDAR-based paradigms, which have evolved largely in parallel over the past decades. While visual SLAM benefits from low-cost sensing and rich semantic information, LiDAR SLAM provides superior geometric accuracy and robustness in challenging environments. As a result, the two paradigms have each established themselves as dominant approaches across different application domains.

基于视觉的 SLAM 在过去几十年中经历了长足的发展，沿着方法论和系统级维度不断发展。从方法论的角度来看，现有的方法可以大致分为基于特征的方法Vision-based SLAM has undergone substantial development over the past decades, evolving along both methodological and system-level dimensions. From a methodological perspective, existing approaches can be broadly categorized into feature-based[20, 3, 22], 直接, direct[8]，和混合, and hybrid[10]方法，其不同之处在于是否利用几何对应或光度一致性来进行运动和结构估计。methods, which differ in whether geometric correspondences or photometric consistency are exploited for motion and structure estimation.

在系统层面，现代视觉SLAM框架已经从逐帧里程计逐步发展到基于关键帧的优化，并进一步通过闭环和长期数据关联实现全局一致的映射At the system level, modern visual SLAM frameworks have progressively advanced from frame-to-frame odometry to keyframe-based optimization, and further to globally consistent mapping through loop closure and long-term data association[20, 3]。这一演变显着提高了准确性和鲁棒性，实现了在各种场景下的可靠操作。为了解决纯视觉的固有局限性，例如尺度模糊和对视觉退化的敏感性，视觉惯性系统被引入，将惯性测量纳入紧密耦合的优化框架中，进一步提高运动估计的准确性和鲁棒性. This evolution has significantly improved accuracy and robustness, enabling reliable operation across a wide range of scenarios. To address the inherent limitations of pure vision, such as scale ambiguity and sensitivity to visual degradation, visual-inertial systems have been introduced to incorporate inertial measurements within tightly-coupled optimization frameworks, further enhancing motion estimation accuracy and robustness[22].

尽管取得了这些进步，视觉 SLAM 从根本上仍然受到其对图像形成的依赖的限制。在光照变化、运动模糊和纹理稀缺等具有挑战性的视觉条件下，其性能会下降Despite these advances, visual SLAM remains fundamentally constrained by its reliance on image formation. Its performance degrades under challenging visual conditions such as illumination changes, motion blur, and texture scarcity[22]，并且它通常缺乏直接的几何可观测性。此外，光度一致性和主要静态环境的隐含假设进一步限制了其在动态和复杂的现实场景中的适用性。这些固有的限制推动了以 LiDAR 为中心的 SLAM 系统的开发，该系统提供直接可靠的几何测量，并在感知退化和动态变化的环境中表现出卓越的鲁棒性, and it typically lacks direct geometric observability. Moreover, the implicit assumption of photometric consistency and predominantly static environments further limits its applicability in dynamic and complex real-world scenarios. These inherent limitations motivate the development of LiDAR-centric SLAM systems, which provide direct and reliable geometric measurements and exhibit superior robustness in perceptually degraded and dynamically evolving environments[30].

与基于视觉的方法相比，以 LiDAR 为中心的 SLAM 直接利用主动深度测量，提供准确且光照不变的几何观测In contrast to vision-based approaches, LiDAR-centric SLAM directly leverages active depth measurements, providing accurate and illumination-invariant geometric observations[36]。早期的 LiDAR SLAM 系统建立了一个结构化管道，将高频里程计与低频测绘分离，通常依赖于几何特征提取，例如用于扫描配准的边缘和平面基元. Early LiDAR SLAM systems established a structured pipeline that decouples high-frequency odometry from low-frequency mapping, typically relying on geometric feature extraction such as edge and planar primitives for scan registration[36, 26]。该设计可实现实时性能，同时通过分层优化保持低漂移。在此基础上，后续工作的重点是提高系统效率和稳健性。 LiDAR 惯性融合框架引入紧耦合状态估计来补偿运动失真并提高姿态精度. This design enables real-time performance while maintaining low drift through hierarchical optimization. Building upon this foundation, subsequent works have focused on improving system efficiency and robustness. LiDAR-inertial fusion frameworks introduce tightly-coupled state estimation to compensate for motion distortion and enhance pose accuracy[25, 30]，而最近的方法通过直接将原始点云注册到地图进一步消除了手工特征提取，提高了不同传感模式和环境的适应性, while recent methods further eliminate hand-crafted feature extraction by directly registering raw point clouds to maps, improving adaptability across different sensing modalities and environments[30]。除了系统设计之外，最近的进展还重新审视了地图表示。人们不再依赖原始点云图（原始点云图通常计算成本昂贵且缺乏不确定性建模），而是提出了更复杂的表示（例如体素化图和概率图），以实现高效的地图更新、稳健的扫描匹配以及测量不确定性的原则性处理. Beyond system design, recent advances have also revisited map representation. Instead of relying on raw point cloud maps, which are often computationally expensive and lack uncertainty modeling, more sophisticated representations such as voxelized and probabilistic maps have been proposed to enable efficient map updates, robust scan matching, and principled handling of measurement uncertainty[34].

总体而言，LiDAR SLAM 系统提供直接的几何可观测性、对感知退化的强大鲁棒性以及在大规模和动态环境中提高的可靠性。然而，尽管有这些优势，但与视觉传感器相比，激光雷达在机器人系统中的广泛采用历来受到其高成本、大外形尺寸和显着功耗的限制Overall, LiDAR SLAM systems provide direct geometric observability, strong robustness to perceptual degradation, and improved reliability in large-scale and dynamic environments. However, despite these advantages, the widespread adoption of LiDAR in robotic systems has historically been limited by its high cost, large form factor, and significant power consumption compared to vision sensors[12, 28]。传感器设计和制造的最新进展大大减少了这些障碍，实现了紧凑、低成本和节能的激光雷达系统. Recent advances in sensor design and manufacturing have substantially reduced these barriers, enabling compact, low-cost, and energy-efficient LiDAR systems[19, 24]。这种技术转变不仅缩小了激光雷达和视觉在部署限制方面的差距，使激光雷达成为基于视觉的方法的竞争性和补充性替代方案，而且还使轻型机器人平台上的新系统设计成为可能。特别是，将 LiDAR 直接集成到末端执行器变得可行，如 UMI-3D 所示，它可以在交互点实现几何感知。. This technological shift not only narrows the gap between LiDAR and vision in terms of deployment constraints, making LiDAR a competitive and complementary alternative to vision-based approaches, but also enables new system designs on lightweight robotic platforms. In particular, it becomes feasible to integrate LiDAR directly at the end-effector, as exemplified by UMI-3D, which enables geometric perception at the point of interaction.

Ⅲ-BIII-B SLAM 和数据处理管道SLAM and Data Processing Pipeline

VI 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

尽管取得了有希望的结果，UMI-3D 仍然存在一些局限性，这些局限性为未来研究指明了重要方向。Despite the promising results, UMI-3D still has several limitations that suggest important directions for future research.

硬件设计和人体工程学。Hardware design and ergonomics.当前的 UMI-3D 硬件由于 LiDAR 和支持组件的集成而带来了额外的重量，这可能会导致用户在长时间的数据收集过程中感到疲劳。未来的工作可以探索轻质材料、更紧凑的传感器集成以及改进的夹具和手柄设计，以增强长期操作的可用性和人体工程学。The current UMI-3D hardware introduces additional weight due to the integration of LiDAR and supporting components, which can lead to user fatigue during prolonged data collection. Future work could explore lightweight materials, more compact sensor integration, and improved gripper and handle design to enhance usability and ergonomics for long-duration operation.

单臂系统限制。Single-arm system limitation.UMI-3D 目前专注于单臂配置，这限制了其执行需要双手协调或复杂物体稳定的任务的能力。将系统扩展到双臂设置将显着扩大可实现的操纵任务的范围，并实现更丰富的交互形式。UMI-3D currently focuses on a single-arm configuration, which limits its ability to perform tasks that require bimanual coordination or complex object stabilization. Extending the system to a dual-arm setup would significantly expand the range of achievable manipulation tasks and enable richer forms of interaction.

3D 感知在政策学习中的利用有限。Limited utilization of 3D perception in policy learning.虽然 LiDAR 在提高 SLAM 鲁棒性和数据质量方面发挥着关键作用，但这项工作中学习的策略主要依赖于推理时的视觉观察。然而，系统在演示过程中固有地捕获同步的 3D 几何信息，包括对象结构、空间关系和交互动态。未来的一个重要方向是将此类 3D 信息直接纳入策略学习中，从而有可能实现更稳健和几何感知的操作。While LiDAR plays a critical role in improving SLAM robustness and data quality, the learned policies in this work rely primarily on visual observations at inference time. However, the system inherently captures synchronized 3D geometric information during demonstrations, including object structure, spatial relationships, and interaction dynamics. An important future direction is to incorporate such 3D information directly into policy learning, potentially enabling more robust and geometry-aware manipulation.

扩展到移动操作。Extension to mobile manipulation.UMI-3D的高保真数据采集能力为从固定基地操控扩展到移动操控场景提供了坚实的基础。未来的工作可以将运动和操纵集成到统一的数据到策略管道中，使机器人能够在更大、结构化程度较低的环境中运行，并显着扩大体现智能的范围。The high-fidelity data collection capability of UMI-3D provides a strong foundation for extending from fixed-base manipulation to mobile manipulation scenarios. Future work could integrate locomotion and manipulation into a unified data-to-policy pipeline, enabling robots to operate in larger and less structured environments and significantly expanding the scope of embodied intelligence.

总体而言，解决这些限制将进一步增强 UMI-3D 的可扩展性、可用性和通用性，并有助于弥合数据收集、感知和具体决策之间的差距。Overall, addressing these limitations will further enhance the scalability, usability, and generality of UMI-3D, and help bridge the gap between data collection, perception, and embodied decision-making.
