1 简介Introduction

研究和行业加速采用大型语言模型 (LLM) 和代理人工智能系统。仅 Google 一家报告称，到 2026 年中期，其产品每月处理的代币数量超过 3.2 万亿个，同比增长七倍Adoption of large language models (LLMs) and agentic AI systems has accelerated across research and industry. These systems are now widely adopted for software engineering[10], 科学发现, scientific discovery[8], 机器人技术, robotics[2]，以及特定领域的决策支持, and domain-specific decision support[71]。代理系统将法学硕士与外部工具、内存、检索、代码执行和可信求解器相结合，因此法学硕士充当规划者和协调者，而不是答案的唯一来源. Agentic systems combine LLMs with external tools, memory, retrieval, code execution, and trusted solvers so the LLM acts as planner and orchestrator rather than the sole source of answers[82, 68, 70, 78]。这种从单一模型调用到工具使用循环的转变让法学硕士能够参与多步骤的工程工作流程，而仅靠文本是不够的。代理系统可以分解问题、编排求解器和数据源、检查结果和修改计划，但这些技能几乎没有达到需要经过验证、物理基础计算的工程工作流程。. This shift from a single model call to a tool-use loop lets LLMs engage with multi-step engineering workflows where text alone is not enough. Agentic systems can decompose a problem, orchestrate solvers and data sources, inspect results, and revise plans, but these skills have barely reached engineering workflows that demand verified, physically grounded computation.

许多电力和能源系统任务与这种代理工作流程密切相关。例如，电网规划和运营将语言、数据、物理模型、优化和控制结合在一个过程中，操作员可以阅读市场手册、解析 SCADA 遥测数据、运行潮流求解器、检查热限制并编写报告。这些数据是异构的，将电网代码、NERC 可靠性标准、断电报告和供应商手册等文本工件与来自 SCADA 的数字流、天气预报、市场信号和资产记录混合在一起。计算堆栈也是混合的，涵盖预测模型、潮流和动态模拟器、经济调度 (ED) 和最优潮流 (OPF) 的优化求解器以及电压和频率调节控制器，每个都有自己的接口和假设。系统必须满足基尔霍夫定律、热和电压限制、斜坡率和安全裕度，违规会带来运营、经济或安全后果。可再生预测、分布式能源 (DER) 协调、电动汽车充电调度、电网规划、应急分析和需求响应都属于这种工作流程，其中 LLM 推理和代理系统可以充当将这些组件联系在一起的层。Many power and energy systems tasks align closely with such an agentic workflow. Grid planning and operation, for example, combines language, data, physical models, optimization, and control in one procedure, where an operator may read a market manual, parse SCADA telemetry, run a power-flow solver, check a thermal limit, and develop a report. The data is heterogeneous, mixing textual artifacts such as grid codes, NERC reliability standards, outage reports, and vendor manuals with numerical streams from SCADA, weather forecasts, market signals, and asset records. The computational stack is also mixed, spanning forecasting models, power-flow and dynamic simulators, optimization solvers for economic dispatch (ED) and optimal power flow (OPF), and controllers for voltage and frequency regulation, each with its own interface and assumptions. The system must satisfy Kirchhoff’s laws, thermal and voltage limits, ramp rates, and security margins, and a violation carries operational, economic, or safety consequences. Renewable forecasting, distributed energy resource (DER) coordination, EV charging scheduling, grid planning, contingency analysis, and demand response all sit in this kind of workflow, where LLM reasoning and agentic systems can act as the layer that ties these components together.

所有这三条生产线都有共同的故障模式。法学硕士产生幻觉，在没有底层系统基础的情况下生成数字上合理的答案All three lines share common failure modes. LLMs hallucinate, generating numerically plausible answers with no basis in underlying systems[35]，并且没有内置机制来执行物理定律，例如功率流方程、线路热限制或发电机斜坡率。因此，流畅的调度报告可以描述违反这些约束的时间表，而无需对其进行标记, and have no built-in mechanisms to enforce physical laws such as power flow equations, line thermal limits, or generator ramp rates. A fluent dispatch report can therefore describe a schedule violating these constraints without flagging it[30, 53]。 LLM 输出也很难通过检查来验证，因为正确的数字声明和错误的数字声明都是以相同的置信度和格式编写的，因此操作员在不重新运行可信工具的情况下无法判断可信度。这很重要，因为电网输出会影响安全关键的控制操作：错误报告的电压限制会影响调度决策，电动汽车交付不足的时间表会成为客户投诉和账单费用，而错过的意外事故可能会损坏设备或导致停电。因此，电力系统需要一种不同于开放式人工智能聊天机器人的部署原则，其中数字声明由可信工具生成，并在到达操作员之前通过明确的验证进行控制。. LLM outputs are also hard to verify by inspection, since a correct numerical claim and an incorrect one are written with the same confidence and format, so an operator cannot judge credibility without re-running the trusted tool. This matters because grid outputs feed safety-critical control actions: a misreported voltage limit propagates into a dispatch decision, an under-delivered EV schedule becomes customer complaints and bill charges, and a missed contingency violation can damage equipment or cause blackouts. Power systems therefore need a deployment principle distinct from open-ended AI chatbots, one in which numerical claims are produced by trusted tools and gated by explicit verification before they reach the operator.

1.1 相关作品Related works

自 2023 年以来，关于智能电网的 LLM 和代理人工智能的文献不断增长，目前涵盖四个方向：(i) 领域调查，(ii) 使用 LLM 作为预测模型的基于 LLM 的预测和时间序列建模，(iii) 将可信求解器包装在语言界面后面的工具增强代理，以及 (iv) 将 LLM 嵌入电网运营和 DER 闭环工作流程中的 LLM 驱动的规划、优化和运营代理。我们回顾每个领域的代表性作品，并讨论它们如何激发本教程。Literature on LLMs and agentic AI for smart grids have grown since 2023, and now spans four directions: (i) domain surveys, (ii) LLM-based forecasting and time-series modeling that use LLMs as prediction models, (iii) tool-augmented agents that wrap trusted solvers behind a language interface, and (iv) LLM-driven planning, optimization, and operations agents that embed LLMs in closed-loop workflows for grid operation and DERs. We review representative works in each and discuss how they motivate this tutorial.

领域调查。Domain surveys.第一个流评估当前跨电力系统应用的法学硕士的能力和局限性，从 OPF、EV 调度和潮流分析到检索增强问答，一致地识别流畅文本和物理基础计算之间的差距The first stream assesses the capabilities and limits of current LLMs across power-system applications, from OPF, EV scheduling, and power-flow analysis to retrieval-augmented question answering, consistently identifying a gap between fluent text and physically grounded computation[30, 53, 28, 32]。第二组构建面向任务的分类法，涵盖故障诊断、预测、控制、电网运营、市场和安全，并审查代理人工智能架构及其工具注入和治理风险. A second group builds task-oriented taxonomies spanning fault diagnosis, forecasting, control, grid operations, markets, and security, and reviews agentic AI architectures with their tool-injection and governance risks[3, 67, 52, 23, 31, 79]。这些研究确定了当前法学硕士可以做什么和不能做什么，但大多数研究都没有具体说明如何构建缩小能力与正确性差距的系统。. 2.3 基于求解器的设计规则The Solver-grounded Design Rule

In practice, there are several recurring bottlenecks in solver-grounded smart grid workflows: data preparation, model specification, tool-run configuration, failure detection, scenario iteration, and technical reporting and interpretation. 第三，验证本身就是一个瓶颈。Third, verification itself is a bottleneck.最终输出通常结合了数值结果、解释、诊断和建议。即使求解器调用正确，代理也可能误报值、忽略违规或夸大结果的有效性。因此，基于求解器的设计将三个功能分开：i)。法学硕士解释、计划、协调和解释，ii)。可信工具可提供数字证据，并且 iii)。验证门决定最终答案是否可以安全通信。案例研究量化了这些角色，其中 EVAgent 在 20 个基准日内产生零硬物理约束违规，而独立的 LLM 基线则违反了 50kW 站点上限。The final output often combines numerical results, explanations, diagnostics, and recommendations. Even when the solver call is correct, the agent can misreport a value, omit a violation, or overstate a result’s validity. A solver-grounded design therefore separates three functions: i). the LLM interprets, plans, orchestrates, and explains, ii). trusted tools produce the numerical evidence, and iii). a verification gate decides whether the final answer is safe to communicate. In power systems, CoT has been used within LLM assistants for dispatch operations[12].

A retriever first searches an external knowledge source for the passages most relevant to the query. Those passages are inserted into the prompt as context, so the model answers from the retrieved evidence rather than its parameters alone, often citing the passages used. In practice, source documents are split into self-contained chunks, retrieved with metadata filters such as jurisdiction, voltage level, or effective date, and reranked for relevance. A RAG system first retrieves the relevant document sections, such as the applicable voltage range, effective date, or operating condition. In power systems, RAG has grounded operational-reliability assessment in reliability documentation and operating procedures[11].

我们将 RAG 视为一种提示方法，因为它仅更改提供给模型的上下文，而不更改其参数或对外部计算的访问。当检索与工具执行、迭代规划和验证相结合时，生成的系统将变得代理，这就是本节的主题We treat RAG as a prompting method because it changes only the context provided to the model, not its parameters or its access to external computation. When retrieval is instead combined with tool execution, iterative planning, and verification, the resulting system becomes agentic, the subject of Section4.

4 智能电网代理系统Agentic Systems for Smart Grids

从提示系统转向代理系统，法学硕士不再局限于其输入上下文，而是协调外部工具、检查中间结果并决定下一步做什么。我们首先定义一个代理系统，并描述它如何通过 API 调用和工具使用对环境起作用。然后，我们介绍五种代表性架构：推理和行动（ReAct）、计划和行动、分层、自我反思和多代理系统。我们以每个架构都依赖的两个横切组件来结束：内存，它跨步骤保存状态，以及用于在实践中构建代理的框架。Moving from prompting to agentic systems, the LLM is no longer restricted to its input context but orchestrates external tools, inspects intermediate results, and decides what to do next. We first define an agentic system and describe how it acts on the environment through API calls and tool use. We then cover five representative architectures: Reasoning and Acting (ReAct), plan-and-act, hierarchical, self-reflection, and multi-agent systems. We close with two cross-cutting components every architecture relies on: memory, which preserves state across steps, and the frameworks used to build agents in practice.

4.2 操作：API 调用和工具使用Action: API Calls and Tool Use

工具的使用是基于求解器的智能电网工作流程的核心Tool use is central to solver-grounded smart grid workflows[82, 68]。原则是，每当任务需要实时数据、精确计算或外部文档时，代理就应该调用工具，而不是从文本模式推断答案。在电力系统中，此类工具包括数据 API、潮流模拟器、优化求解器和验证例程。法学硕士选择工具，制定其输入，解释输出，并如实报告结果，同时工具提供证据。实用的工具界面通过输入和输出模式、单元约定和失败消息使该契约变得明确，从而减少了常见错误，例如加载错误的案例或报告错误的单元。. The principle is that whenever a task requires real-time data, exact computation, or external documents, the agent should call a tool rather than infer the answer from text patterns. In power systems, such tools include data APIs, power flow simulators, optimization solvers, and verification routines. The LLM selects the tool, formulates its input, interprets the output, and reports the result faithfully, while the tool provides the evidence. A practical tool interface makes this contract explicit through input and output schemas, unit conventions, and failure messages, which reduce common errors such as loading the wrong case or reporting a wrong unit.

A system that produces fluent but unverified numbers can score well on standard language metrics while still issuing physically infeasible decisions, and existing evaluation efforts were not designed for this setting. 5.1 任务实用程序Task utility

任务效用根据问题本身来衡量答案的好坏，与答案的产生方式无关。这是我们在不涉及法学硕士或代理人的情况下分配的分数，对于仅法学硕士和基于求解器的变体报告相同，因此两者可以直接比较。对于回归和预测任务，我们报告针对真实情况的预测误差指标，例如平均绝对误差 (MAE)、均方根误差 (RMSE) 或平均绝对百分比误差。对于分类和检测子任务，我们报告基于准确性或重叠的指标，例如 F1 分数。对于约束优化，我们将实现的目标报告为相对于最佳求解器解决方案的最优性差距，并与任务完成指标（例如满足需求的比例）配对。对于诊断和补救，我们报告成功指标（例如修复率）和部分进度指标（例如违规减少）。这些系列涵盖了最常见的智能电网任务，并且它们之外的任务采用本机指标来实现其目标。任务效用是必要的，但还不够。独立的法学硕士可以通过插入最近的历史记录来最小化 MAE，同时仍然违反物理限制，这就是需要其余三组的原因。 有些任务是多目标的，因此单个标量是不够的。电动汽车调度以成本与未满足的能源进行权衡，潮流报告电压、流量和平衡误差。对于这些，任务效用是补充指标的一个小向量，而不是一个数字。Task utility measures how good an answer is on the problem’s own terms, independent of how it was produced. It is the score we would assign if no LLM or agent were involved, reported identically for the LLM-only and solver-grounded variants so the two are directly comparable. For regression and forecasting tasks, we report a prediction-error metric against ground truth, such as mean absolute error (MAE), root-mean-square error (RMSE), or mean absolute percentage error. For classification and detection subtasks, we report an accuracy- or overlap-based metric such as F1 score. For constrained optimization, we report the achieved objective as an optimality gap relative to the optimal solver solution, paired with a task-completion metric such as the fraction of demand served. For diagnosis and remediation, we report a success metric such as repair rate with a partial-progress metric such as violation reduction. These families cover the most common smart grid tasks, and a task outside them adopts the native metric for its objective. Task utility is necessary but not sufficient. A standalone LLM can minimize MAE by interpolating recent history while still violating physical constraints, which is why the remaining three groups are needed. Some tasks are multi-objective, so a single scalar does not suffice. EV scheduling trades cost against unmet energy, and power flow reports voltage, flow, and balance errors. For these, task utility is a small vector of complementary metrics rather than one number.

7 结论与讨论Conclusion and Discussions

本教程论文介绍了基于 LLM 的智能电网代理系统的基于实现的教程。它是为电力系统受众编写的，回顾了基本构建块：提示策略和代理架构及其内存、工具接口和编排框架。在此基础上我们提出This tutorial paper presents an implementation-grounded tutorial on LLM-based and agentic systems for smart grids. Written for a power-systems audience, it reviews fundamental building blocks: prompting strategies and agentic architectures, with their memory, tool interfaces, and orchestration frameworks. On this foundation we propose the基于求解器的设计规则solver-grounded design rule，指出只有当数值结果来自受信任的工具并通过显式验证时才会报告。引入了四组评估协议，涵盖任务实用性、基于求解器的正确性、忠实性和安全故障以及成本和延迟。我们在四个端到端案例研究中实例化了规则和协议：风电预测、电动汽车充电调度、潮流分析和应急诊断，每个案例都在相同的数据和指标上将纯法学硕士基线与其基于求解器的对应基线进行比较。出现了一致的模式。当 LLM 是接口并且可信工具是事实来源时，代理继承了该工具的正确性。当法学硕士被要求计算时，它不会。当不存在求解器时，如在预测中，验证将从物理可行性降级为输出级模式和范围检查。, stating that a numerical result is reported only when it originates from a trusted tool and passes explicit verification. A four-group evaluation protocol spanning task utility, solver-grounded correctness, faithfulness and safe failure, and cost and latency is introduced. We instantiate both the rule and the protocol in four end-to-end case studies: wind power forecasting, EV charging scheduling, power flow analysis, and contingency diagnosis, each comparing an LLM-only baseline against its solver-grounded counterpart on identical data and metrics. A consistent pattern emerged. When the LLM is the interface and a trusted tool is the source of truth, the agent inherits the tool’s correctness. When the LLM is asked to compute, it does not. When no solver exists, as in forecasting, verification degrades from physical feasibility to output-level schema and range checks.

这些发现存在三个局限性。首先，案例研究使用现成的前沿法学硕士，没有领域适应，因此比较衡量的是提示和架构，而不是微调。其次，它们涵盖了四个代表性任务，而不是完整的设计空间。实时控制、市场结算和保护协调保持开放。第三，该协议根据自己的基于求解器的对应系统审核每个系统，而不是直接比较两个代理。未来最重要的工作就是从这些差距中得出的。可重用的验证库可以在类型化接口后面公开每单元、功率平衡和收敛检查。修复策略可以在不削弱验证的情况下将安全故障路径转变为可恢复路径。可验证奖励微调Three limitations frame these findings. First, the case studies use off-the-shelf frontier LLMs without domain adaptation, so the comparisons measure prompting and architecture rather than fine-tuning. Second, they cover four representative tasks, not the full design space. Real-time control, market settlement, and protection coordination remain open. Third, the protocol audits each system against its own solver-grounded counterpart rather than comparing two agents head-to-head. The most consequential future work follows from these gaps. Reusable verification libraries could expose per-unit, power-balance, and convergence checks behind a typed interface. Repair policies could turn the safe-failure path into a recoverable one without weakening verification. Verifiable-reward fine-tuning[39]可以重用通过验证的轨迹来训练在公用设施网络内运行的较小工具调用代理，将操作数据保留在本地。这些都不能取代求解器。他们通过解析请求、检索上下文、对工具调用进行排序以及解释经过验证的结果来重新组织周围的工作。在我们看来，这些系统的操作影响不是来自新的数值能力，而是来自压缩操作员用自然语言提出的问题和控制系统可以采取行动的答案之间的工程时间。could reuse verification-passed trajectories to train smaller tool-calling agents that run inside utility networks, keeping operational data on premises. None of these replace solvers. They reorganize the work around them by parsing the request, retrieving context, sequencing tool calls, and explaining a verified result. The operational impact of these systems, in our reading, will come not from new numerical capability but from compressing the engineering hours between a question an operator asks in natural language and an answer a control system can act on.
