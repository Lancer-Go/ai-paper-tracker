RMISC：用于时间序列基础模型的大规模现实世界多元语料库RMISC: A Large-scale Real-world Multivariate Corpus for Time Series Foundation Models

Cheng Feng3,4   Shao-Qun Zhang1,2,4, 🖂 1State Key Laboratory of Novel Software Technology, Nanjing University, Nanjing, China

摘要Abstract

近年来，使用时间序列基础模型（TSFM）的多元建模出现了，它实现了先进的零样本泛化。现代多变量 TSFM 主要是在多变量合成数据上进行预训练的，这更容易扩展，但可能无法捕获现实世界时间序列中存在的复杂时间动态和跨变量关系。这就提出了一个关键问题：使用现实世界语料库训练的领先 TSFM 是否比使用合成数据训练的表现更好以及在多大程度上表现更好？为了回答这个问题，我们建立了 RMISC 语料库，这是一个相当大规模、高质量、可公开访问的真实世界的多元时间序列档案，其中包含约 200 个数据集和跨不同领域的 1420 亿个时间点。此外，我们在单变量、合成多变量和真实世界多变量数据上预训练了四个高级 TSFM，并在标准分布内和分布外基准上评估了它们的零样本泛化能力。实验结果表明，结合现实世界的多变量数据可以显着提高单变量和多变量 TSFM 的泛化性能。这些结果让我们更深入地了解现实世界的多元数据如何有助于开发更强大的 TSFM。Recent years have witnessed the emergence of multivariate modeling using time series foundation models (TSFMs), which achieve advanced zero-shot generalization. Modern multivariate TSFMs are predominantly pretrained on multivariate synthetic data, which is easier to scale but may fail to capture the complex temporal dynamics and cross-variable relationships present in real-world time series. This raises a key question: Whether and to what extent the leading TSFMs trained with the real-world corpus perform better than those trained with synthetic data? To answer this, we establish the RMISC corpus, a considerably large-scale, high-quality, openly accessible, real-world, and multivariate time series archive that contains around 200 datasets and 142 billion time points across diverse domains. Furthermore, we pretrain four advanced TSFMs on univariate, synthetic multivariate, and real-world multivariate data and evaluate their zero-shot generalization capabilities on standard in-distribution and out-of-distribution benchmarks. Experimental results show that incorporating real-world multivariate data predominantly improves the generalization performance for both univariate and multivariate TSFMs. These results provide a deeper understanding of how real-world multivariate data contributes to the development of stronger TSFMs.

1 简介Introduction

时间序列基础模型 (TSFM) 的最新进展显着重塑了时间序列分析的范式Recent advances in Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) have significantly remodeled the paradigm of time series analysis(Liang et al., 2024)。 TSFM 被输入到大规模和异构时间序列语料库中，可以直接兼容各种预测任务、频率分布和数据模态. Fed into large-scale and heterogeneous time series corpora, TSFMs can be directly compatible with diverse forecasting tasks, frequency distributions, and data modalities(Montet et al., 2025)具有卓越的零样本泛化能力，从而超越了传统的统计方法with remarkable zero-shot generalization capabilities, thus moving beyond traditional statistical methods(Hyndman and Athanasopoulos, 2018; Box and Jenkins, 1968)和深度学习模型and deep learning models(Hochreiter and Schmidhuber, 1997; Chung et al., 2014; Sen et al., 2019)为单个时间序列重复训练特定于任务的模型that repeatedly train task-specific models for individual time series(Challu et al., 2023; Lim et al., 2021)。近年来，开发人员已将 TSFM 广泛应用于工业传感等各个领域. In recent years, developers have widely applied TSFM to various fields, such as industrial sensing(Hector and Panjanathan, 2024)、财务评估, financial assessment(Sezer et al., 2020), 医疗保健监测, healthcare monitoring(Morid et al., 2023), 气候建模, climate modeling(Mudelsee, 2010)、能源管理, energy management(Hong and Fan, 2016)，以及交通预测, and traffic prediction(Li et al., 2018).

捕获跨变量信息是 TSFM 开发的基本主题之一Capturing the cross-variable information is one of the fundamental topics in the development of TSFMs(Ansari et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025b)，其建模流程如图所示, the modeling workflow of which is illustrated in Figure1。直观上，现实世界的时间序列很少是孤立观察的。一个目标变量通常伴随着多个相关协变量，其时间动态通常由复杂的跨变量依赖性决定. Intuitively, real-world time series are rarely observed in isolation; one target variable is usually accompanied by multiple related covariates, and its temporal dynamics are often shaped by complex cross-variable dependencies(Zhang and Yan, 2023)。例如，天气预报中的温度变化会受到降雨量和风速的影响。因此，多变量 TSFM 中的协变量建模有助于更准确的预测，因为辅助协变量和跨变量依赖性提供了超出目标历史记录的补充信号. For instance, temperature changes in weather forecasts are affected by rainfall and wind speed. Thus, covariate modeling in multivariate TSFMs contributes to more accurate forecasts as auxiliary covariates and cross-variable dependencies provide complementary signals beyond the target history alone(Lim et al., 2021; Zhang and Yan, 2023; Liu et al., 2024b)。然而，当前的多变量 TSFM 主要是在多变量合成数据上进行预训练的. However, current multivariate TSFMs are predominantly pretrained on multivariate synthetic data(Ansari et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025a);尽管易于使用和可扩展，但在捕获复杂的时间动态和跨变量关系方面，合成时间序列与现实时间序列之间仍然存在差距; despite the ease of use and scalability, there still exists a gap between synthetic and real-world time series in terms of capturing complex temporal dynamics and cross-variable relationships(Liu et al., 2025a; Li et al., 2025)。这就提出了一个关键问题：使用现实世界语料库训练的领先 TSFM 是否比使用合成数据训练的表现更好以及在多大程度上表现更好？. This raises a key question: Whether and to what extent do the leading TSFMs trained with the real-world corpus perform better than those trained with synthetic data?

Despite the scalability and flexibility of synthetic time series data, it is often constrained by the assumptions of the generation process and may fail to faithfully preserve real-world complex patterns and complex cross-variable dependencies(Liu et al., 2025a)。最近的证据进一步表明，在合成多元数据集上进行预训练并在标准基准上表现良好的 TSFM 可能仍然难以应对现实世界的时间动态. 最近的 TSFM 将现实世界的时间序列纳入模型开发中Recent TSFMs have incorporated real-world time series into model development(Woo et al., 2024; Cohen et al., 2026)，并且还引入了专用基准来评估现实多元预测场景下的模型, and dedicated benchmarks have also been introduced to evaluate models under realistic multivariate forecasting scenarios(Shchur et al., 2025; Aksu et al., 2024; Godahewa et al., 2021g);然而，现有的现实世界多元时间序列数据集在数量和质量上仍然有限，不足以完全支持大规模多元TSFM的预训练; however, existing real-world multivariate time series datasets remain limited in quantity and quality, insufficient to fully support the pretraining of large-scale multivariate TSFMs(Goswami et al., 2024)。此外，还需要根据多元真实时间序列数据构建测试床，用于综合评估多元TSFM的预训练和下游性能. Moreover, it is also necessary to build a testbed from multivariate real-world time series data, used to comprehensively evaluate the pretraining and downstream performance of multivariate TSFMs(Shchur et al., 2025).

Within each subdataset, time series data are sequentially partitioned into ordered Parquet files with consistent indexing.

Since RMISC is fully open-source and curated from publicly available real-world multivariate time series datasets, the metadata additionally records the original data source and license information for each sub-dataset. Furthermore, BibTeX citation files are provided whenever formal references are available.

3 实验Experiments

在本节中，我们通过实证证明了所提出的 RMISC 语料库的有效性。进行实验是为了回答以下问题：在 RMISC 语料库上预训练的领先 TSFM 在收敛、分布内 (ID) 和分布外 (OOD) 性能方面是否比在单变量和合成多变量数据上预训练的表现更好？In this section, we empirically demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed RMISC corpus. The experiments are performed to answer the question: Whether and to what extent do the leading TSFMs pretrained on the RMISC corpus perform better than those pretrained on univariate and synthetic multivariate data in terms of convergence, in-distribution (ID), and out-of-distribution (OOD) performance?

4 结论Conclusions

在本文中，我们提出了 RMISC 语料库，用于支持具有大规模和真实世界多元时间序列的预训练和基准测试 TSFM。在与现实世界的单变量和合成多元语料库的系统比较中，我们证实 RMISC 语料库提供了来自现实环境的有价值的多元信息，并且可以有效地补充现有的预训练数据。特别是，现实世界的单变量数据、合成多变量数据和 RMISC 的结合可以带来比目前广泛使用的预训练语料库更强大的零样本泛化能力。这些结果表明，我们提出的 RMISC 语料库为构建多元 TSFM 提供了有效的数据基础。In this paper, we proposed the RMISC corpus for supporting pretraining and benchmarking TSFMs with large-scale and real-world multivariate time series. In systematic comparisons with real-world univariate and synthetic multivariate corpora, we confirmed that the RMISC corpus provides valuable multivariate information from realistic contexts and can effectively complement existing pretraining data. In particular, the combination of real-world univariate data, synthetic multivariate data, and RMISC leads to more robust zero-shot generalization than the currently widely used pretraining corpus. These results suggest that our proposed RMISC corpus provides an effective data foundation for building multivariate TSFMs.
