1 简介Introduction

医学图像分割在现代医疗保健中发挥着至关重要的作用，为临床诊断、手术计划和治疗监测提供了显着的好处Medical image segmentation plays a crucial role in modern healthcare, offering significant benefits in clinical diagnosis, surgical planning, and treatment monitoring26。准确描绘解剖结构和病理区域可以做出更明智的临床决策，并有助于改善患者的治疗结果。然而，由于医学图像固有的复杂性和可变性，实现可靠的分割仍然极具挑战性。这些挑战主要来自两个主要方面。首先，医学图像在解剖结构的形状、大小和空间配置方面表现出相当大的多样性。例如，在皮肤病变分析中，毛发、血管和不均匀照明等成像伪影可能会模糊病变边界并混淆解释。此外，不规则的轮廓和不均匀的强度分布进一步降低了传统的基于形状或强度的模型的性能. Accurately delineating anatomical structures and pathological regions enables more informed clinical decision-making and contributes to improved patient outcomes. Nevertheless, achieving reliable segmentation remains highly challenging due to the inherent complexity and variability of medical images. These challenges arise primarily from two major aspects. First, medical images exhibit considerable diversity in the shape, size, and spatial configuration of anatomical structures. For instance, in skin lesion analysis, imaging artifacts such as hair, blood vessels, and uneven illumination can obscure lesion boundaries and confound interpretation. In addition, irregular contours and heterogeneous intensity distributions further degrade the performance of conventional shape- or intensity-based models45, 6。其次，图像分辨率对分割性能有显着影响。低分辨率扫描通常会出现边界清晰度差和精细解剖细节丢失的问题，而分辨率过高的图像可能会放大噪声并引入不稳定的特征响应。. Second, image resolution exerts a notable influence on segmentation performance. Low-resolution scans often suffer from poor boundary definition and loss of fine anatomical detail, whereas excessively high-resolution images may amplify noise and introduce unstable feature responses.

临床专家的手动描绘虽然准确度很高，但既费力又耗时，使其不适合大规模临床部署。为了缓解这种情况，过去几十年来开发了多种自动分割算法Manual delineation by clinical experts, though highly accurate, is both labor-intensive and time-consuming, rendering it impractical for large-scale clinical deployment. To mitigate this, a wide range of automatic segmentation algorithms have been developed over the past decades9, 10。早期的方法依赖于经典的图像处理技术，例如阈值处理. Early approaches relied on classical image processing techniques such as thresholding36和边缘检测and edge detection28。然而，当面对现实世界医学图像中存在的高解剖变异性和复杂的外观模式时，这些方法缺乏鲁棒性和普遍性。深度学习 (DL) 的最新进展，特别是卷积神经网络 (CNN) 的出现. However, these methods lacked robustness and generalizability when confronted with the high anatomical variability and complex appearance patterns present in real-world medical images. Recent advances in deep learning (DL), particularly the advent of convolutional neural networks (CNNs)29和变形金刚and Transformers12，彻底改变了医学图像分割，在不同的模式和解剖目标上取得了最先进的结果, have revolutionized medical image segmentation, achieving state-of-the-art results across diverse modalities and anatomical targets2, 48。尽管取得了成功，但这些模型通常需要大规模、完全注释的数据集才能获得最佳的准确性和泛化性。获取此类专家注释既昂贵又耗时，因为它需要特定领域的知识和细致的手动工作。. Despite their success, these models typically demand large-scale, fully annotated datasets to attain optimal accuracy and generalization. Acquiring such expert annotations is both costly and time-intensive, as it requires domain-specific knowledge and meticulous manual effort.

为了减轻对密集注释的依赖，弱监督和半监督分割策略被广泛探索，利用稀疏或噪声监督信号，例如图像级标签、边界框、涂鸦或点To alleviate the reliance on dense annotations, weakly and semi-supervised segmentation strategies have been widely explored, leveraging sparse or noisy supervisory signals such as image-level tags, bounding boxes, scribbles, or points3, 18, 44, 39, 46, 24。尽管这些方法大大降低了注释成本，但其有限的空间监督通常会导致边界描绘不准确和结构不一致，特别是在低对比度或容易出现伪影的区域。为了减轻这些缺点，一些研究结合了结构先验、对抗性形状正则化或一致性约束来提高空间一致性. Although these methods substantially reduce annotation costs, their limited spatial supervision often leads to inaccurate boundary delineation and structural inconsistencies, particularly in low-contrast or artifact-prone regions. 5 结论和未来的扩展Conclusion and Future Extensions

本文介绍了 PROMISE-Net，这是一种基于 PCCA 机制构建的即时感知医学图像分割框架。通过在编码器和解码器阶段分层嵌入提示条件调制，PROMISE-Net 使空间提示能够影响多个语义级别的特征表示，而不是仅限于后期融合。跨越四个异构基准（ISIC-2017、Kvasir-SEG、Kvasir-Instrument 和 CAMUS）的大量实验表明，与强卷积、基于变压器和提示驱动的基线相比，PROMISE-Net 在重叠精度、边界精度和假阴性减少方面提供了一致的改进。结果进一步证实了跨架构的稳健泛化（PROMISE-CThis paper introduced PROMISE-Net, a prompt-aware medical image segmentation framework built on the proposed PCCA mechanism. By embedding prompt-conditioned modulation hierarchically across encoder and decoder stages, PROMISE-Net enables spatial prompts to influence feature representations at multiple semantic levels rather than being restricted to late-stage fusion. Extensive experiments across four heterogeneous benchmarks (ISIC-2017, Kvasir-SEG, Kvasir-Instrument, and CAMUS) demonstrate that PROMISE-Net delivers consistent improvements in overlap accuracy, boundary precision, and false-negative reduction over strong convolutional, transformer-based, and prompt-driven baselines. The results further confirm robust generalization across architectures (PROMISE-CNN和承诺-Tand PROMISE-T变形者xformer）、解剖目标、成像方式、图像质量水平和标签空间复杂性（二元分割与多类分割），同时保持对观察者间变异和提示扰动的弹性。), anatomical targets, imaging modalities, image quality levels, and label-space complexity (binary vs. multi-class segmentation), while remaining resilient to inter-observer variability and prompt perturbations.

除了分割精度之外，PROMISE-Net 还显示出更高的解剖一致性和临床意义的可靠性，具体表现为边界误差减少、图像质量下降下的稳定性能以及下游心脏体积和功能测量的一致性提高。总的来说，这些发现将即时条件通道调制确立为交互式医学图像分割的有效且可推广的策略。Beyond segmentation accuracy, PROMISE-Net shows improved anatomical consistency and clinically meaningful reliability, as evidenced by reduced boundary errors, stable performance under degraded image quality, and improved agreement in downstream cardiac volumetric and functional measurements. Collectively, these findings establish prompt-conditioned channel modulation as an effective and generalizable strategy for interactive medical image segmentation.

虽然这项工作的重点是 2D 医学图像分割，但 PCCA 的模块化设计很容易支持几个重要的扩展。首先，分层提示调节机制可以自然地扩展到 3D 和 4D（时空）分割，从而能够对体积和动态数据（例如心动周期、胎儿超声心动图或内窥镜视频流）进行运动感知分析。其次，提示编码器可以通用化以支持多提示交互，包括边界框、点和涂鸦的组合，从而允许以最小的用户努力更好地控制模糊或重叠的解剖区域。While this work focuses on 2D medical image segmentation, the modular design of PCCA readily supports several important extensions. First, the hierarchical prompt-conditioning mechanism can be naturally extended to 3D and 4D (spatio-temporal) segmentation, enabling motion-aware analysis of volumetric and dynamic data such as cardiac cycles, fetal echocardiography, or endoscopic video streams. Second, the prompt encoder can be generalized to support multi-prompt interactions, including combinations of bounding boxes, points, and scribbles, allowing finer control over ambiguous or overlapping anatomical regions with minimal user effort.

此外，整合领域适应和持续学习策略将进一步增强跨机构的稳健性，促进跨扫描仪、中心和患者群体的部署。最后，将提示条件调制扩展到多模态设置，例如联合超声-MRI 或图像-临床元数据融合，代表了构建上下文感知和临床可扩展分割系统的有希望的方向。In addition, integrating domain adaptation and continual learning strategies would further strengthen cross-institutional robustness, facilitating deployment across scanners, centers, and patient populations. Finally, extending prompt-conditioned modulation to multimodal settings, such as joint ultrasound–MRI or image–clinical metadata fusion, represents a promising direction for building context-aware and clinically scalable segmentation systems.

总体而言，PROMISE-Net 为推进交互式、提示驱动和解剖学上可靠的分割提供了灵活的基础，并为更高维度、多模式和实时临床应用提供了明确的途径。Overall, PROMISE-Net provides a flexible foundation for advancing interactive, prompt-driven, and anatomically reliable segmentation, with clear pathways toward higher-dimensional, multimodal, and real-time clinical applications.
