摘要Abstract

可靠的天体姿态确定是航天器自主导航的关键要求，但传统的“迷失空间”(LIS) 算法常常面临高计算开销和对传感器引起的噪声敏感的问题。虽然深度学习已成为一种有前途的替代方案，但标准回归模型经常受到天球的非欧几里得拓扑以及赤经 (RA) 和赤纬 (Dec) 周期性边界条件的困扰。在本文中，我们提出Reliable celestial attitude determination is a critical requirement for autonomous spacecraft navigation, yet traditional ”Lost-in-Space” (LIS) algorithms often suffer from high computational overhead and sensitivity to sensor-induced noise. While deep learning has emerged as a promising alternative, standard regression models are often confounded by the non-Euclidean topology of the celestial sphere and by the periodic boundary conditions of Right Ascension (RA) and Declination (Dec). In this paper, we present星聚变Star-Fusion，一种多模态架构，将方向估计重新表述为离散拓扑分类任务。, a multi-modal architecture that reformulates orientation estimation as a discrete topological classification task.

我们的方法利用球形Our approach leverages sphericalKK- 表示将天球划分为聚类-Means clustering to partition the celestial sphere intoKK拓扑一致的区域，有效减轻坐标包裹伪影。所提出的架构采用三方融合策略：用于光度特征提取的 SwinV2-Tiny 变压器主干、用于空间接地的卷积热图分支以及用于几何锚定的基于坐标的 MLP。合成的实验评估topologically consistent regions, effectively mitigating coordinate wrapping artifacts. The proposed architecture employs a tripartite fusion strategy: a SwinV2-Tiny transformer backbone for photometric feature extraction, a convolutional heatmap branch for spatial grounding, and a coordinate-based MLP for geometric anchoring. Experimental evaluations on a synthetic依巴谷Hipparcos派生数据集表明 Star-Fusion 的 Top-1 准确率达到 93.4%，Top-3 准确率达到 97.8%。此外，该模型具有较高的计算效率，在资源有限的 COTS 硬件上保持 18.4 毫秒的推理延迟，使其成为下一代卫星星座中实时机载部署的可行候选者。-derived dataset demonstrate that Star-Fusion achieves a Top-1 accuracy of 93.4% and a Top-3 accuracy of 97.8%. Furthermore, the model exhibits high computational efficiency, maintaining an inference latency of 18.4 ms on resource-constrained COTS hardware, making it a viable candidate for real-time onboard deployment in next-generation satellite constellations.

1 简介Introduction

航天器姿态确定，即计算航天器相对于惯性参考系的方向的过程，是任务成功的基础。星跟踪器被广泛认为是执行此任务的最准确的传感器，因为它们通过观察遥远的恒星星座来提供绝对方向。然而，恒星识别（Star-ID）的经典方法通常涉及两个阶段的过程：恒星质心，然后针对大量星上目录进行子图匹配。算法如Spacecraft attitude determination, the process of calculating a vehicle’s orientation relative to an inertial reference frame, is fundamental to mission success. Star trackers are widely considered the most accurate sensors for this task, as they provide absolute orientation by observing distant stellar constellations. However, the classical approach to star identification (Star-ID) typically involves a two-stage process: star centroiding followed by subgraph matching against a massive onboard catalog. Algorithms such as金字塔Pyramid [1]和and网格Grid [2]在数学上是严格的，但在面对高动态机动、显着的传感器噪声或来自空间碎片的“假星”时，会难以应对“迷失在太空”的情况。are mathematically rigorous but struggle with the ”Lost-in-Space” scenario when faced with high-dynamic maneuvers, significant sensor noise, or ”false stars” from space debris.

1.1 动机和问题陈述Motivation and Problem Statement

深度表征学习的兴起为经典 Star-ID 提供了一种有前景的替代方案。通过将方向视为视觉任务，模型原则上可以学习对噪声不变的鲁棒特征。然而，现有的深度学习模型面临着一个重大障碍：The rise of Deep Representation Learning offers a promising alternative to classical Star-ID. By treating orientation as a vision task, models can, in principle, learn robust features invariant to noise. Nevertheless, existing deep learning models face a significant hurdle: the球面环绕问题spherical wrap-around problem。因为 RA 的范围是. Because RA ranges from0∘0^{\circ} to 360∘360^{\circ}，两个空间相邻的方向在坐标空间中可以在数值上远离。这些值的直接回归通常会导致训练不稳定，并且错误率接近, two spatially adjacent orientations can be numerically distant in the coordinate space. Direct regression of these values often leads to unstable training and high error rates near the0/3600/360边界。boundary.

此外，普通的视觉变形金刚（ViT）通常缺乏明确的“几何先验”。虽然它们擅长捕捉全局纹理，但它们本质上并不尊重定义星座的逐点几何关系。显然需要一种多模态架构，能够将 Transformer 的全局注意力与几何点云的局部精度融合在一起。Furthermore, vanilla Vision Transformers (ViTs) often lack an explicit ”geometric prior.” While they excel at capturing global textures, they do not inherently respect the point-wise geometric relationships that define a star constellation. There is a clear need for a multi-modal architecture that can fuse the global attention of transformers with the local precision of geometric point clouds.

9.1 未来的方向Future Directions

While this study establishes a robust foundation for discrete orientation, several avenues for future research remain.

  1. 1.

    由粗到细的细化：Coarse-to-Fine Refinement: We intend to implement a recursive regression head that utilizes the predicted class as a spatial prior to achieve sub-degree angular precision.

  2. 2.

    Temporal Consistency:利用循环神经网络（RNN）或时间变换器可以让模型利用卫星的轨道力学作为时间约束，进一步平滑方向转换。Leveraging Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) or Temporal Transformers could allow the model to utilize the satellite’s orbital mechanics as a temporal constraint, further smoothing orientation transitions.

  3. 3.

    在轨域适应：On-Orbit Domain Adaptation:未来的工作将探索自监督微调技术，使模型直接适应太空环境中的特定传感器噪声分布，确保长期任务的可靠性。Future work will explore self-supervised fine-tuning techniques to adapt the model to specific sensor noise profiles directly in the space environment, ensuring long-term mission reliability.
