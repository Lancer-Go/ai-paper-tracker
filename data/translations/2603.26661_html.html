GaussianGPT：迈向自回归 3D 高斯场景生成GaussianGPT: Towards Autoregressive 3D Gaussian Scene Generation

摘要Abstract

3D 生成建模的最新进展依赖于扩散或流量匹配公式。 相反，我们探索一种完全自回归的替代方案并引入Most recent advances in 3D generative modeling rely on diffusion or flow-matching formulations. We instead explore a fully autoregressive alternative and introduce高斯GPTGaussianGPT，一种基于 Transformer 的模型，可通过下一个标记预测直接生成 3D 高斯，从而促进完整的 3D 场景生成。 我们首先使用具有矢量量化功能的稀疏 3D 卷积自动编码器将高斯基元压缩为离散潜在网格。 使用具有 3D 旋转位置嵌入的因果转换器对生成的令牌进行序列化和建模，从而能够顺序生成空间结构和外观。 与整体细化场景的基于扩散的方法不同，我们的公式逐步构建场景，自然支持完成、覆盖、通过温度控制采样和灵活的生成范围。 该公式利用了自回归建模的组合归纳偏差和可扩展性，同时在与现代神经渲染管道兼容的显式表示上进行操作，将自回归变压器定位为可控和上下文感知 3D 生成的补充范例。, a transformer-based model that directly generates 3D Gaussians via next-token prediction, thus facilitating full 3D scene generation. We first compress Gaussian primitives into a discrete latent grid using a sparse 3D convolutional autoencoder with vector quantization. The resulting tokens are serialized and modeled using a causal transformer with 3D rotary positional embedding, enabling sequential generation of spatial structure and appearance. Unlike diffusion-based methods that refine scenes holistically, our formulation constructs scenes step-by-step, naturally supporting completion, outpainting, controllable sampling via temperature, and flexible generation horizons. This formulation leverages the compositional inductive biases and scalability of autoregressive modeling while operating on explicit representations compatible with modern neural rendering pipelines, positioning autoregressive transformers as a complementary paradigm for controllable and context-aware 3D generation.

1 简介Introduction

深度神经网络的最新进展已经实现了图像、视频、音频和语言的高质量合成。 将这些功能扩展到结构化 3D 场景生成是迈向沉浸式虚拟环境、具体人工智能、模拟和内容创建的关键一步。此类任务需要模型来推理连贯的空间布局而不是平面视觉信号。 虽然当前的 3D 生成方法通常依赖于整体去噪，但它们缺乏环境实际构建方式所需的灵活性：增量式。实际上，3D 场景很少是静态的；它们被逐步扩展、完成和编辑。这就迫切需要将 3D 空间视为结构化序列的模型，从而能够逐步构建。 因此，我们将 3D 生成视为一个自回归过程，其中通过预测以现有空间上下文为条件的新元素来迭代构建场景。Recent advances in deep neural networks have enabled high-quality synthesis across images, video, audio, and language. Extending these capabilities to structured 3D scene generation is a key step toward immersive virtual environments, embodied AI, simulation, and content creation. Such tasks require models to reason about coherent spatial layouts rather than flat visual signals. While current 3D generative approaches often rely on holistic denoising, they lack the flexibility required for the way environments are actually constructed: incrementally. In practice, 3D scenes are rarely static; they are progressively extended, completed, and edited. This creates a critical need for models that treat 3D space as a structured sequence, enabling step-by-step construction. Accordingly, we frame 3D generation as an autoregressive process in which the scene is iteratively built by predicting new elements conditioned on the existing spatial context.

尽管取得了快速进展，但由于 3D 数据固有的高维性和缺乏自然序列，生成结构化 3D 场景仍然很困难。 与 2D 图像不同，3D 环境需要联合捕获几何、外观和语义的表示，同时保持多视图一致性。 室内场景表现出很强的规律性和高度的可变性，要求全局结构连贯性（例如房间布局）和局部构图灵活性（包括不同的对象放置、几何形状和外观）之间的平衡。 此外，缺乏规范排序使得很难应用其他领域的成功自回归范例，因为模型需要将 3D 场景序列化为序列，同时保留空间依赖性。Despite rapid progress, generating structured 3D scenes remains difficult due to the inherent high dimensionality and lack of a natural sequence in 3D data. Unlike 2D images, 3D environments require representations that jointly capture geometry, appearance, and semantics while maintaining multi-view consistency. Indoor scenes exhibit both strong regularities and high variability, demanding a balance between global structural coherence (e.g., room layout) and local compositional flexibility, including varied object placement, geometry, and appearance. Furthermore, the lack of a canonical ordering makes it difficult to apply successful autoregressive paradigms from other domains, as models need to serialize the 3D scene into a sequence while preserving spatial dependencies.

最近最先进的方法Recent state-of-the-art approaches[xiang_native_2025, xiang_structured_2025, lan_gaussiananything_2025, roessle_l3dg_2024]主要依赖于在神经场、高斯原语或潜在场景表示上运行的扩散或基于流的模型。虽然这些方法实现了令人印象深刻的视觉保真度，但它们通常将生成制定为全局去噪或细化过程，这可能会使增量编辑和结构化完成变得不那么自然。相比之下，自回归 Transformer 在结构化序列建模和跨语言和视觉领域的可控生成方面表现出了强大的能力，但在结构化 3D 场景合成方面的探索相对较少。利用 3D 自回归建模需要能够实现有意义的标记化，同时保留空间结构和组合语义的表示。predominantly rely on diffusion or flow-based models operating over neural fields, Gaussian primitives, or latent scene representations. While these methods achieve impressive visual fidelity, they typically formulate generation as a global denoising or refinement process, which can make incremental editing and structured completion less natural. In contrast, autoregressive transformers have demonstrated strong capabilities for structured sequence modeling and controllable generation across language and vision domains, yet remain comparatively underexplored for structured 3D scene synthesis. Leveraging autoregressive modeling for 3D requires representations that enable meaningful tokenization while preserving spatial structure and compositional semantics.

We represent scenes as sequences of discrete tokens derived from vector-quantized 3D Gaussian primitives, enabling transformer-based models to incrementally generate, extend, and edit scenes through next-token prediction. This formulation combines explicit 3D representations with the compositional inductive biases of autoregressive transformers, providing a complementary alternative to diffusion-based paradigms.

2 相关作品Related Works

生成的 3D 表示。3D Representations for Generation.

3D 生成建模中的一个关键设计选择是底层的 3D 表示。 早期的生成方法主要使用离散或隐式几何公式来建模形状，包括基于体素的占用网格、有符号距离函数和点云A critical design choice in 3D generative modeling is the underlying 3D representation. Early generative approaches predominantly modeled shapes using discretized or implicit geometric formulations, including voxel-based occupancy grids, signed distance functions, and point clouds[wu_learning_2017, wu_3d_2015, park_deepsdf_2019, chou_diffusion-sdf_2023, shim_diffusion-based_2023, yang_pointflow_2019, achlioptas_learning_2018]。 网格构成了另一种显式表面表示，最近已被探索用于 3D 生成建模. Meshes constitute another explicit surface representation and have recently been explored for 3D generative modelling[siddiqui_meshgpt_2023, xiang_structured_2025, xiang_native_2025]。 引入神经辐射场 (NeRF). The introduction of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF)[mildenhall_nerf_2020]通过连续体积表示实现高保真 3D 生成建模enabled high-fidelity 3D generative modeling through continuous volumetric representations[niemeyer_giraffe_2021, muller_diffrf_2023, tang_volumediffusion_2024, poole_dreamfusion_2022]。 然而，基于 NeRF 的表示需要密集的体积采样，导致训练和推理成本较高，从而限制了可扩展性。 3D 高斯泼溅. However, NeRF-based representations require dense volumetric sampling, resulting in high training and inference costs that limit scalability. 3D Gaussian Splatting[kerbl_3d_2023]已成为 3D 生成建模的高效且富有表现力的替代方案，将高视觉质量与实时渲染相结合has emerged as an efficient and expressive alternative for 3D generative modelling, combining high visual quality with real-time rendering[chen_splatformer_2025, xiang_structured_2025, lan_gaussiananything_2025]。 然而，无约束的 3D 高斯集缺乏结构规律，这使得它们难以建模。 为了解决这个问题，提出了与空间网格对齐的结构化高斯参数化，以在保持渲染效率的同时引入规律性. However, unconstrained sets of 3D Gaussians lack structural regularity, which makes them difficult to model. To address this, structured Gaussian parameterizations aligned to spatial grids have been proposed to introduce regularity while preserving rendering efficiency[roessle_l3dg_2024]。 此外，我们不是直接生成结构化 3D 高斯函数，而是遵循潜在空间生成范式. Furthermore, rather than generating the structured 3D Gaussians directly, we follow the paradigm of latent-space generation[rombach_high-resolution_2022, gao_can3tok_2025]，已广泛应用于 3D 生成建模中，以提高稳定性、可扩展性和样本质量。, which has been widely adopted in 3D generative modeling to improve stability, scalability, and sample quality.

自回归 3D 生成。Autoregressive 3D Generation.

早期的 3D 建模生成方法主要基于变分自动编码器和生成对抗网络Early generative approaches for 3D modeling were largely based on variational autoencoders and generative adversarial networks[wu_3d_2015, wu_learning_2017, achlioptas_learning_2018, yang_pointflow_2019]。 最近，3D 生成建模的进步主要由扩散和流匹配范式驱动，这些范式通过迭代去噪或连续时间变换对复杂的数据分布进行建模，并在表示中实现强大的综合质量. More recently, advances in 3D generative modeling have been driven predominantly by diffusion and flow-matching paradigms, which model complex data distributions through iterative denoising or continuous-time transformations and achieve strong synthesis quality across representations[ho_denoising_2020, lipman_flow_2023, poole_dreamfusion_2022, chou_diffusion-sdf_2023, shim_diffusion-based_2023]。 并行自回归建模，因基于 Transformer 的序列架构（例如 GPT 样式模型）而流行. In parallel, autoregressive modeling, popularized by Transformer-based sequence architectures such as GPT-style models[vaswani_attention_2023, radford_language_2019, brown_language_2020]，通过将联合分布分解为一系列条件预测，在语言、视觉和多模态生成方面取得了显着的成功。 这种公式可以实现基于可能性的训练、渐进式生成和灵活的调节。 尽管在标记化领域取得了成功，但自回归建模在 3D 生成方面的探索相对较少。 现有的工作主要集中于离散几何表示的顺序建模，包括基于网格的生成, has achieved remarkable success in language, vision, and multimodal generation by factorizing joint distributions into a sequence of conditional predictions. This formulation enables likelihood-based training, progressive generation, and flexible conditioning. Despite its success in tokenized domains, autoregressive modeling remains comparatively underexplored for 3D generation. Existing works primarily focus on sequential modeling of discretized geometric representations, including mesh-based generation[siddiqui_meshgpt_2023, chen_meshanything_2024, hao_meshtron_2024]，分层体素或八叉树标记化, hierarchical voxel or octree tokenizations[ibing_octree_2021]，以及结构化几何标记建模, and structured geometric token modeling[chen_mar-3d_2025, han_var-3d_2026, zhang_g3pt_2024]。 相比之下，我们探索结构化高斯基元上的自回归生成，从而实现对象和整个场景的可扩展建模，同时避免基于扩散的方法的迭代采样过程特征。. In contrast, we explore autoregressive generation over structured Gaussian primitives, enabling scalable modeling of both objects and full scenes while avoiding the iterative sampling procedures characteristic of diffusion-based methods.

从对象到场景。From Objects to Scenes.

在对象层面，3D生成建模近年来取得了显着进展On object level, 3D generative modeling has seen remarkable progress in recent years[xiang_structured_2025, lan_gaussiananything_2025, xiang_native_2025, chen_text--3d_2024, tang_dreamgaussian_2024]。 相比之下，完整的 3D 场景生成仍然相对未得到充分探索。 场景带来了额外的挑战，包括大空间尺度、长距离空间依赖性、多对象组合性以及对连贯布局和物理合理性的需求。 现有方法以不同的方式应对这些挑战。 有些将场景生成分解为结构化布局预测，然后进行对象检索或对象级合成. In contrast, full 3D scene generation remains comparatively underexplored. Scenes introduce additional challenges, including large spatial scale, long-range spatial dependencies, multi-object compositionality, and the need for coherent layout and physical plausibility. Existing approaches address these challenges in different ways. Some decompose scene generation into structured layout prediction followed by object retrieval or object-level synthesis[tang_diffuscene_2024, paschalidou_atiss_2021, wang_sceneformer_2021]。 其他人利用预训练图像或视频生成模型的强大先验来指导 3D 优化或重建. Others leverage strong priors from pretrained image or video generative models to guide 3D optimization or reconstruction[schneider_worldexplorer_2025, hollein_text2room_2023]，或者依靠大规模基础模型来提供语义或结构监督, or rely on large-scale foundation models to provide semantic or structural supervision[li_worldgrow_2025, li_dreamscene_2025, zhou_dreamscene360_2024, feng_layoutgpt_2023]。 进一步的工作将场景生成制定为使用深度或多视图线索与高斯泼溅相结合的前馈重建，重点是重建而不是无条件生成建模. A further line of work formulates scene generation as feed-forward reconstruction using depth or multi-view cues combined with Gaussian splatting, focusing on reconstruction rather than unconditional generative modeling[lin_depth_2025, zhang_gs-lrm_2024, jiang_anysplat_2025, charatan_pixelsplat_2024, chen_mvsplat_2025, szymanowicz_flash3d_2025]。 虽然这些策略显示出有希望的结果，但它们通常依赖于外部先验、多阶段管道或优化过程。 相比之下，我们的目标是学习一个统一的自回归生成模型，该模型可以直接对 3D 场景进行建模，而不依赖于预训练的 2D 扩散或视频先验，从而在单个概率框架内实现可扩展的场景合成。. While these strategies demonstrate promising results, they often depend on external priors, multi-stage pipelines, or optimization-heavy procedures. In contrast, we aim to learn a unified autoregressive generative model that directly models 3D scenes without relying on pretrained 2D diffusion or video priors, enabling scalable scene synthesis within a single probabilistic framework.

3 方法论Methodology

我们的目标是使用 GPT 式转换器的无条件自回归生成来合成 3D 高斯场景。 由于 GPT 模型在离散标记序列上运行并且上下文范围有限，因此我们首先需要 3D 场景的紧凑且离散的表示。 为此，我们利用稀疏 3D 卷积自动编码器将高斯场景映射到离散潜在网格表示并忠实地重建它们（Our goal is to synthesize 3D Gaussian scenes using unconditional autoregressive generation with a GPT-style transformer. Since GPT models operate on discrete token sequences and have limited contextual scope, we first require a compact and discrete representation of 3D scenes. To this end, we leverage a sparse 3D convolutional autoencoder that maps Gaussian scenes to a discrete latent grid representation and faithfully reconstructs them (Sec.˜3.1）。 给定这种表示，我们将潜在网格序列化为令牌序列，并训练因果变换器来对网格占用和特征的联合分布进行建模。 我们详细介绍了自回归公式、架构设计、推理过程以及 3D 空间先验的纳入). Given this representation, we serialize the latent grids into token sequences and train a causal transformer to model the joint distribution of grid occupancy and features. We detail the autoregressive formulation, architectural design, inference procedure, and the incorporation of 3D spatial priors inSec.˜3.2.

3.1 通过稀疏 3D 潜在编码进行场景压缩Scene Compression via Sparse 3D Latent Encoding

为了实现基于标记的自回归建模，我们首先将连续的 3D 高斯场景转换为紧凑的离散潜在网格表示。 我们的压缩阶段包括 (i) 将高斯基元投影到稀疏 3D 特征网格中，(ii) 使用稀疏 3D 卷积自动编码器对该网格进行编码，以及 (iii) 通过矢量量化离散化潜在表示。 由此产生的量化潜在网格构成了后续自回归建模的基础。To enable token-based autoregressive modeling, we first transform a continuous 3D Gaussian scene into a compact discrete latent grid representation. Our compression stage consists of (i) projecting Gaussian primitives into a sparse 3D feature grid, (ii) encoding this grid using a sparse 3D convolutional autoencoder, and (iii) discretizing the latent representation via vector quantization. The resulting quantized latent grid forms the basis for subsequent autoregressive modeling.

稀疏 3D 特征网格。Sparse 3D Feature Grid.

遵循 3D 高斯泼溅Following 3D Gaussian Splatting[kerbl_3d_2023]，我们的输入是由一组高斯基元表示的场景。 每个图元都由一组连续属性定义：位置、不透明度、大小、旋转和颜色。 我们在世界坐标中定义一个网格，并根据它们的位置将高斯分配给相应的体素。 当体素中存在多个高斯分布时，我们用距体素中心的相对偏移量替换绝对位置，并随机进行子采样，以获得输入 3D 高斯场景。 对于每个体素，我们使用一组轻量级编码头对每个高斯特征进行编码，然后将结果连接成统一向量以获得稀疏输入特征网格。 对称地，在通过 3D CNN 后，一组按特征解码头将预测的每体素特征向量转换回单独的高斯属性。, our input is a scene represented by a set of Gaussian primitives. Each primitive is defined by a set of continuous attributes: position, opacity, size, rotation, and color. We define a grid in world coordinates and assign Gaussians to the corresponding voxels based on their positions. We replace absolute positions with relative offsets from the voxel centers and randomly subsample, when multiple Gaussians are present in a voxel, to obtain our input 3D Gaussian scene. For each voxel, we use a set of lightweight encoding heads to encode each Gaussian feature, then concatenate the results into a unified vector to obtain a sparse input feature grid. Symmetrically, after passing through the 3D CNN, a set of per-feature decoding heads converts the predicted per-voxel feature vectors back into individual Gaussian attributes.

稀疏 3D CNN 编码器-解码器。Sparse 3D CNN Encoder-Decoder.

输入特征网格由稀疏 3D 卷积编码器处理The input feature grid is processed by a sparse 3D convolutional encoderℰ\mathcal{E}和解码器and decoder𝒟\mathcal{D}关注 L3DGfollowing L3DG[roessle_l3dg_2024]。 编码器逐渐将网格下采样为紧凑的潜在表示，而解码器则重建体素级特征。 卷积设计保留了空间局部性和平移等方差，产生非常适合后续生成建模的结构化潜在特征。. The encoder progressively downsamples the grid to a compact latent representation, while the decoder reconstructs voxel-level features. The convolutional design preserves spatial locality and translation equivariance, yielding structured latent features well suited for subsequent generative modeling.

3.2 潜在 3D 网格的自回归建模Autoregressive Modeling of Latent 3D Grids

给定量化的潜在网格，我们的目标是使用基于序列的自回归模型对其占用和特征的联合分布进行建模。 因此，我们的第一步是将 3D 网格序列化为 1D 标记序列，之后我们可以训练因果变换器来对底层模式进行建模。 我们还通过分离位置和特征词汇并注入 3D 位置嵌入来为模型提供先验。Given the quantized latent grid, our goal is to model the joint distribution of its occupancy and features using a sequence-based autoregressive model. Our first step is therefore to serialize the 3D grid into a 1D token sequence, after which we can train a causal transformer to model the underlying patterns. We additionally provide priors to the model by separating position and feature vocabularies and injecting 3D positional embeddings.

3D 网格序列化。3D Grid Serialization.

我们使用简单且固定的方法将 3D 结构线性化为 1D 序列We linearize the 3D structure into a 1D sequence using a simple and fixedx​y​zxyz遍历顺序。 自从traversal order. Sincezz是最不重要的维度，对此的直观理解是我们每次都会迭代一个场景高度列is the least significant dimension, an intuitive understanding of this is that we iterate a scene-height column at every(x,y)(x,y)跳转到下一个之前的位置。 虽然此模式不保留 1D 序列中的 3D 局部性，但其优点在于其简单、可解释的排序（参见position before jumping to the next one. While this pattern does not preserve 3D locality in the 1D sequence, its advantages lie in its simple, interpretable ordering (seeSec.˜4.6).

给定体素的排序和相对位置索引，我们通过交错位置和特征标记来构建序列。 由于可能的序列随场景大小呈立方体增长，因此我们通过在块而不是整个场景上操作 GPT 模型来限制所需的上下文大小。 每个体素都被分配一个相对于当前网格块而不是其绝对位置的位置索引，使模型能够在本地操作并跨位置、场景和布局进行概括。Given the ordering and relative position indices of the voxels, we construct our sequence by interleaving position and feature tokens. Since the possible sequence grows cubically with scene size, we limit the required context size by operating our GPT model on chunks rather than entire scenes. Each voxel is assigned a position index relative to the current grid chunk rather than its absolute position, enabling the model to operate locally and generalize across positions, scenes, and layouts.

词汇设计。Vocabulary Design.

尽管我们的序列是由共享变压器主干处理的，但我们对位置和特征标记使用单独的词汇表。 遵循位置和特征标记的规则交替模式，我们在不同的位置和特征头之间交替。 位置头的任务是预测下一个占用的体素索引，特征头的任务是预测前一个位置的特征。 这种词汇和预测的明确分离将几何结构与外观建模分离，并防止空间和语义特征之间对共享索引的竞争。 顺便说一句，这种设计还允许我们自由控制块大小和相应的位置索引数量，而不管特征码本的大小。Although our sequences are processed by a shared transformer backbone, we employ separate vocabularies for position and feature tokens. Following the regular alternating pattern of position and feature tokens, we alternate between a distinct position and feature head. The task of the position head is to predict the next occupied voxel index, and the feature head predicts the feature at the preceding position. This explicit separation in vocabulary and predictions decouples geometric structure from appearance modeling and prevents competition between spatial and semantic features over shared indices. Incidentally, this design also allows us to freely control the chunk size and the corresponding number of position indices, irrespective of the feature codebook size.

3D 旋转位置编码。3D Rotary Positional Encoding.

GPT 样式模型中使用的 Transformer 将输入处理为一维标记序列，并且需要明确的位置概念。 在标准应用中，位置信息沿着这个一维索引注入，或者通过学习的绝对嵌入，或者通过相对编码，例如旋转位置嵌入（RoPE）Transformers, as used in GPT-style models, process inputs as a 1D token sequence and require an explicit notion of position. In standard applications, positional information is injected along this 1D index, either via learned absolute embeddings or via relative encodings such as rotary positional embeddings (RoPE)[su_roformer_2023]。 然而，在我们的设置中，令牌序列并不是本质上的一维信号：它是通过序列化稀疏 3D 潜在网格而获得的。 如果我们将标准一维位置编码直接应用于序列化顺序，则模型将主要学习序列邻近度的概念，这很难转化为空间邻近度。 特别是，靠近的体素. However, in our setting, the token sequence is not an inherently one-dimensional signal: it is obtained by serializing a sparse 3D latent grid. If we were to apply standard 1D positional encoding directly to the serialized order, the model would primarily learn notions of sequence proximity, which can translate poorly to spatial proximity. In particular, voxels that are close in(x,y,z)(x,y,z)在序列化中可能相距很远，反之亦然。may be far apart in the serialization, and vice versa.

为了注入显式的空间归纳偏差，我们使用 3D RoPE 对注意力机制内的实际体素坐标进行编码。 这样，注意力分数就成为标记之间相对空间偏移的函数，而不是序列偏移的函数。 该方法遵循最近在 3D 生成中使用坐标感知旋转嵌入的做法To inject an explicit spatial inductive bias, we encode actual voxel coordinates inside the attention mechanism using 3D RoPE. This way, the attention score becomes a function of the relative spatial offset between the tokens rather than the sequence offset. The approach follows recent uses of coordinate-aware rotary embeddings in 3D generation[xiang_native_2025]，使模型能够独立于序列化顺序来推理空间局部性。, enabling the model to reason about the spatial locality independent of the serialization order.

最后，我们的序列在位置和特征标记之间交替，两者都对应于相同的 3D 位置。 因此，在实践中，我们使用指示令牌类型的附加第四维来扩展旋转嵌入。 这种令牌型旋转组件有助于变压器进一步将几何形状与外观分开，同时在混合令牌流上保留单一统一的注意力公式。Finally, our sequence alternates between position and feature tokens, both of which correspond to the same 3D location. In practice, we therefore extend the rotary embeddings with an additional 4th dimension that indicates the token type. This token-type rotary component helps the transformer to further disentangle geometry from appearance while retaining a single unified attention formulation over the mixed token stream.

变压器架构。Transformer Architecture.

我们的仅解码器因果变压器架构遵循标准 GPT 公式，具有堆叠多头自注意力和前馈块。 我们的模型基于 GPT-2Our decoder-only causal transformer architecture follows the standard GPT formulation with stacked multi-head self-attention and feed-forward blocks. Our model is based on GPT-2[radford_language_2019]并建立在 nanochat 之上and builds on nanochat[nanochat]作为其技术支柱。 与普通的 GPT-2 管道相比，我们还采用了旋转位置嵌入（如上所述扩展到 3D）、查询键归一化、每层残差缩放和 Muon 优化器as its technical backbone. Compared to the vanilla GPT-2 pipeline, we additionally employ rotary positional embeddings (extended to 3D as described above), query–key normalization, per-layer residual scaling, and the Muon optimizer[henry_query-key_2020, modded_nanogpt_2024, jordan_muon_2024]。 虽然是 nanochat 主干默认配置的一部分，但我们不使用值嵌入或滑动窗口注意力. 场景生成和完成。Scene Generation and Completion.

在推理时，场景是通过根据先前生成的上下文对令牌进行采样而自动生成的。 从序列开始（BOS）令牌开始，转换器在预测位置令牌和特征令牌之间交替，直到产生序列结束（EOS）令牌。At inference time, scenes are generated autoregressively by sampling tokens conditioned on previously generated context. Starting from a begin-of-sequence (BOS) token, the transformer alternates between predicting position tokens and feature tokens until an end-of-sequence (EOS) token is produced.

自回归公式的一个关键优点是场景完成和无条件生成由相同的机制处理。 给定一个部分场景，我们只需序列化观察到的标记并将它们用作模型继续生成的前缀提示。 这使得转换器能够自然地推断缺失的几何形状和外观，同时保持与现有场景上下文完全一致。 同样的原理还可以实现超出固定训练块大小的大规模场景合成。 通过在先前生成的标记的滑动窗口上重复应用补全作为上下文，我们可以无限地超越场景。A key advantage of the autoregressive formulation is that scene completion and unconditional generation are handled by the same mechanism. Given a partial scene, we simply serialize the observed tokens and use them as a prefix prompt from which the model continues generation. This allows the transformer to naturally infer missing geometry and appearance while remaining fully consistent with the existing scene context. The same principle also enables large-scale scene synthesis beyond the fixed training chunk size. By repeatedly applying completions on a sliding window of previously generated tokens as context, we can infinitely outpaint the scene.

由于自回归生成的组合性质，我们可以进一步限制基于当前序列的预测。 具体来说，由于每个位置标记直接对应于当前块内的体素索引，因此我们可以屏蔽已经生成的位置，确保采样序列始终遵守排序约束。 此外，我们方法的顺序性质使得树搜索成为可能。 我们在通过对没有占用的列进行重新采样来生成更大的场景时使用此功能，从而创建更多占用和连接的场景。Due to the compositional nature of autoregressive generation, we can further limit predictions based on the current sequence. Specifically, since each position token directly corresponds to a voxel index within the current chunk, we can mask already generated locations, ensuring that the sampled sequence always respects ordering constraints. Additionally, the sequential nature of our approach enables tree search. We use this when generating larger scenes by resampling columns without occupancy, thereby creating more occupied and connected scenes.

5 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced高斯GPTGaussianGPT，一个完全自回归框架，用于直接在矢量量化高斯表示上运行 3D 场景生成和完成。 通过将稀疏 3D 潜在压缩与结构化标记化和 3D 感知变压器建模相结合，我们表明顺序下一个标记预测是基于扩散的结构化 3D 合成方法的可行且补充的替代方案。 与基于扩散的方法相比，我们的配方实现了多种新功能。 场景可以作为一系列离散空间决策逐步生成，从而允许对采样进行显式控制，对部分生成的几何图形进行因果推理，以及使用同一模型对完成和绘制进行无缝支持。, a fully autoregressive framework for 3D scene generation and completion operating directly on vector-quantized Gaussian representations. By combining sparse 3D latent compression with structured tokenization and 3D-aware transformer modeling, we show that sequential next-token prediction is a viable and complementary alternative to diffusion-based approaches for structured 3D synthesis. Compared to diffusion-based methods, our formulation enables several new capabilities. Scenes can be generated incrementally as a sequence of discrete spatial decisions, allowing explicit control over sampling, causal reasoning over partially generated geometry, and seamless support for completion and outpainting using the same model.

我们展示了 3D 形状合成中视觉质量的提高，并定性地展示了无条件生成、场景完成和大规模场景合成中生成的场景的多样性和保真度。 更广泛地说，我们的工作强调了将 3D 场景视为结构化标记序列的潜力，为可控和组合的 3D 生成开辟了新的机会。 未来的工作包括将框架扩展到现实世界的数据，其中不确定性感知建模变得至关重要，并且自回归方法特别适合。 我们还设想了其他有希望的途径来提高长期稳定性并将生成上下文扩展到固定空间块之外。We demonstrate improved visual quality in 3D shape synthesis and qualitatively showcase the diversity and fidelity of generated scenes across unconditional generation, scene completion, and large-scale scene synthesis. More broadly, our work highlights the potential of treating 3D scenes as structured token sequences, opening new opportunities for controllable and compositional 3D generation. Future work includes extending the framework to real-world data, where uncertainty-aware modeling becomes essential, and autoregressive approaches are particularly well-suited. We also imagine other promising avenues for improving long-horizon stability and extending the generation context beyond fixed spatial chunks.
