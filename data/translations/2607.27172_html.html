摘要。Abstract.

传统的搜索系统经过优化，可以检索与查询严格匹配的项目，通常优先考虑精确度而不是召回率。在电子商务市场，特别是杂货店，这种模式是有限制的，因为用户满意度和商业成果在很大程度上取决于替代品、补充品和主题相关商品的可发现性。在本文中，我们提出了一个可扩展的系统Traditional search systems are optimized to retrieve items that strictly match a query, often prioritizing precision over recall. In e-commerce marketplaces and particularly grocery, this paradigm is limiting, as user satisfaction and commercial outcomes depend heavily on the discoverability of substitute, complementary, and thematically related items. In this paper, we present a scalable system for发现增强搜索discovery-augmented search利用意图条件召回扩展。我们的方法生成隐式用户意图，以扩大候选者的召回率，同时保持相关性。that leverages intent-conditioned recall expansion. Our approach generates implicit user intents to expand candidate recall while maintaining relevance.

该系统通过两阶段混合架构解决生成检索的成本与质量权衡。首先，我们利用封闭权重大型语言模型（LLM）来最大限度地提高头部查询的可发现性。为了将这些优势扩展到尾部查询，我们引入了一种经过微调的小语言模型 (SLM)，通过 LoRA 适配器和师生蒸馏进行训练。我们使用严格的双重框架来评估系统：（a）根据人类对语义质量的偏好进行验证的法学硕士作为法官指标，以及（b）端到端会话级购买分析。结果表明，我们的方法提高了意图生成质量和下游检索效率，将查询流量的发现覆盖范围从约 60% 扩大到 80%，而教师模型的推理成本约为 30%，为大规模市场中的部署提供了可行的路径。 除了相关性增益之外，发现增强搜索还可以充当市场平衡机制，为长尾和新兴供应提供查询条件曝光的机会。The system addresses the cost-quality tradeoff of generative retrieval through a two-stage hybrid architecture. First, we leverage closed-weight large language models (LLMs) to maximize discoverability for head queries. To extend these benefits to tail queries, we then introduce a finetuned small language model (SLM), trained via LoRA adapters and teacher-student distillation. We evaluate the system using a rigorous dual framework: (a) LLM-as-a-judge metrics validated against human preferences for semantic quality, and (b) end-to-end session-level purchase analysis. Results demonstrate that our approach improves both intent generation quality and downstream retrieval effectiveness, extending discovery coverage from approximately 60% to 80% of query traffic at roughly 30% of the teacher model’s inference cost, offering a viable path for deployment in large-scale marketplaces. Beyond relevance gains, discovery-augmented search may serve as a marketplace-balancing mechanism, giving long-tail and emerging supply an opportunity for query-conditioned exposure.

1. 简介Introduction

搜索系统传统上是围绕以下原则设计的：Search systems have traditionally been designed around the principle of临时的ad hoc检索，其主要目标是返回与用户查询最直接匹配的结果。虽然查询重写和扩展等技术被广泛使用，但它们通常经过优化以解决词汇不匹配问题并提高显式查询的匹配准确性，而不是retrieval, with the primary goal of returning the most directly matching results for a user’s query. While techniques such as query rewriting and expansion are widely used, they are typically optimized to address vocabulary mismatch and improve matching accuracy for the explicit query, rather than拓宽broadening结果集的范围。the scope of the result set.

在杂货店和一般电子商务市场中，这种严格基于相关性的范例是不够的。用户购物时经常怀有超出明确关键词范围的潜在目标：寻找缺货商品的合理替代品、收集特定食谱的原料，或者寻找用例的补充物品。在这些场景中，诸如以下的关系In grocery and general e-commerce marketplaces, this strictly relevance-based paradigm is insufficient. Users frequently shop with latent objectives that extend beyond the explicit keyword: seeking reasonable substitutes for out-of-stock goods, gathering ingredients for a specific recipe, or finding complementary items for a use case. In these scenarios, relationships such as替代substitution（例如，tangerine 代表克莱门汀），(e.g., tangerine for clementine),互补性complementarity（例如面食和酱汁），以及(e.g., pasta and sauce), and专题协会thematic association（例如，海鲜拼盘和调味料）是用户目标的核心，但很少在单个查询中表达。因此，传统检索未能充分暴露目录中的大部分内容，而这些内容本来可以满足用户更广泛、隐含的意图，从而限制了收入潜力。在双边市场中，这种曝光不足也会不成比例地影响长尾和新兴供应，将发现视为除用户满意度之外的市场平衡问题。(e.g., seafood platter and seasoning) are central to the user’s goal but rarely expressed in a single query. Consequently, traditional retrieval under-exposes large portions of the catalog that could satisfy users’ broader, implicit intents, thereby limiting revenue potential. In a two-sided marketplace, this under-exposure also disproportionately affects long-tail and emerging supply, framing discovery as a marketplace-balancing concern in addition to a user-satisfaction one.

2. 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

查询理解和重写Query Understanding and Rewriting

查询重写和扩展技术Query rewriting and expansion techniques(Jones et al., 2006; Carpineto and Romano, 2012; Wang et al., 2023)是网络搜索的基础，可提高召回率和对词汇不匹配的鲁棒性。 在电子商务领域，用户经常重新表述搜索词，之前的工作利用这些细化模式来提高查询理解have been foundational in web search to improve recall and robustness to lexical mismatch. In the e-commerce domain, where users frequently reformulate search terms, prior work has leveraged these refinement patterns for improved query understanding(Hirsch et al., 2020)。 然而，这些方法通常侧重于释义、同义词和拼写变体，在用户的范围内有效地进行优化. However, these approaches typically focus on paraphrases, synonyms, and spelling variants, effectively optimizing within the bounds of the user’s明确的explicit意图。它们通常根据严格的相关性指标（例如 NDCG、MRR）进行评估，并且不考虑潜在意图扩展或对于杂货发现至关重要的互补性结构。intent. They are generally evaluated on strict relevance metrics (e.g., NDCG, MRR) and do not account for latent intent expansion or the complementarity structures that are critical for discovery for grocery.

互补性和共同购买模型Complementarity and Co-Purchase Modeling

推荐系统的先前工作广泛探索了使用共现统计、基于图的方法和基于会话的嵌入的互补项目预测Prior work in recommender systems has extensively explored complementary item prediction using co-occurrence statistics, graph-based methods, and session-based embeddings(McAuley et al., 2015; Barkan and Koenigstein, 2016; Ying et al., 2018; Nguyen et al., 2025)。 这些方法对于项目到项目的推荐表面（例如，“您可能也喜欢”）有效，但在查询条件检索设置中面临两个限制：它们通常与搜索索引分离并且缺乏查询条件逻辑，并且它们依赖于尾部查询、新兴供应和主题意图稀疏的历史协同交互信号（例如，. These methods are effective for item-to-item recommendation surfaces (e.g., ”You might also like”) but face two limitations in a query-conditioned retrieval setting: they are typically decoupled from the search index and lack query-conditional logic, and they rely on historical co-interaction signals that are sparse for tail queries, emerging supply, and thematic intents (e.g.,冰沙站smoothie station）这很少会单独从购买共现中显现出来。 我们的工作是互补的而不是竞争的：语言模型直接从查询中推断互补和主题关系，自然地扩展到共同购买图填充不足的长尾查询，并且它们的输出可以与可以获得密集参与数据的基于图的信号相结合。) that seldom surface from purchase co-occurrence alone. Our work is complementary rather than competing: language models infer complementary and thematic relationships directly from the query, extending naturally to long-tail queries where co-purchase graphs are underpopulated, and their outputs can be combined with graph-based signals where dense engagement data is available.

用于查询理解和检索的法学硕士LLMs for Query Understanding and Retrieval

法学硕士已证明在意图提取、分类和属性提取方面的功效LLMs have demonstrated efficacy in intent extraction, classification, and attribute extraction(Zhao et al., 2023; Çiftlikçi et al., 2025; Peng et al., 2024)。 此外，强化学习技术已应用于微调法学硕士以获得定制奖励信号. Furthermore, reinforcement learning techniques have been applied to fine-tune LLMs for customized reward signals(Agrawal et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2025)。 然而，在整个查询分布（尤其是长尾）中部署专有模型的成本仍然过高。 我们通过将推理能力转移到具有成本效益的模型来解决这个问题，从而实现可扩展的意图生成，而无需大量法学硕士的运营开销。. However, deploying proprietary models across the entire query distribution—particularly the long tail—remains cost-prohibitive. We address this by transferring reasoning capabilities to cost-efficient models, enabling scalable intent generation without the operational overhead of massive LLMs.

双边市场的发现Discovery in Two-Sided Marketplaces

双边市场中的搜索和排名必须共同满足消费者相关性以及主机、卖家和商家的供应方曝光度。之前在 Airbnb 上的制作工作Search and ranking in two-sided marketplaces must jointly satisfy consumer relevance and supply-side exposure for hosts, sellers, and merchants. Prior production work on Airbnb(Grbovic and Cheng, 2018; Abdool et al., 2020; Haldar et al., 2020)明确地权衡客人偏好与房东多样性和房源曝光度，并且方法论工作已将卖方评估正式化为与消费者方 A/B 测试不同的反事实问题explicitly trades off guest preferences against host diversity and listing exposure, and methodological work has formalized seller-side evaluation as a counterfactual problem distinct from consumer-side A/B testing(Ha-Thuc et al., 2020)。食品配送等多边环境进一步概括了不同供应商的目标. Multi-sided settings such as food delivery further generalize the objective across heterogeneous suppliers(Wang et al., 2022)。发现增强搜索对杂货市场的双方都有类似的影响：消费者可以获得他们不知道要查询的替代品和补充品，而零售商和品牌（尤其是长尾和新兴品牌）有机会获得超出精确匹配检索的查询条件曝光。因此，隐式意图生成作为一种市场平衡机制而不仅仅是一种相关性杠杆。. Discovery-augmented search has analogous implications for both sides of the grocery marketplace: consumers gain access to substitutes and complements they did not know to query for, and retailers and brands—particularly long-tail and emerging ones—have an opportunity to earn query-conditioned exposure beyond exact-match retrieval. Implicit intent generation thus operates as a marketplace-balancing mechanism rather than purely a relevance lever.

5. 部署经验教训和限制Deployment Lessons and Limitations

部署隐式意图生成并离线评估它会出现几种故障模式、操作选择和形成当前设计的公开警告。Deploying implicit intent generation and evaluating it offline surfaced several failure modes, operational choices, and open caveats that shaped the current design.

蒸馏上下文差距。Distillation context gap.

老师以丰富的离线元数据（品牌、属性、最近购买）为条件。当在没有元数据有效负载的情况下在请求时查询学生时，尾部查询的质量会悄然下降。我们通过在蒸馏集中包含元数据剥离示例的受控部分来缓解这种情况，这样学生就不会依赖于上下文有效负载。The teacher was conditioned on rich offline metadata (brands, attributes, recent purchases). When the student was queried at request time without that metadata payload, quality degraded silently for tail queries. We mitigate by including a controlled fraction of metadata-stripped examples in the distillation set so the student does not rely on the contextual payload.

解码的相干敏感性。Coherence sensitivity to decoding.

长尾政策。Long-tail policy.

为了最深的For the deepest∼\sim长尾的 20%，缓存的 LLM 和 SLM 都没有产生可靠有用的意图；我们选择放弃这些查询的轮播，而不是提供降级的结果，回退到仅检索。20% of the long tail, neither the cached LLM nor the SLM produced reliably useful intents; we elected to drop carousels for those queries rather than serve degraded results, falling back to retrieval-only.

维护。Maintenance.

两个必要的重复维护任务是随着查询分布和目录变化定期刷新头查询和尾查询注释，以及当教师更新或其行为漂移时重新提炼学生模型。Two necessary recurring maintenance tasks are periodically refreshing head- and tail-query annotations as the query distribution and catalog shift, and re-distilling the student model when the teacher is updated or its behavior drifts.

产生幻觉的品牌名称。Hallucinated brand names.

封闭式法学硕士有时会制造听起来合理但不存在的品牌（例如拼写错误或发明的自有品牌）。捏造的术语通常会在下游返回稀疏或空的检索结果，因此受影响的轮播对用户来说根本不可见，但我们目前不针对策划的品牌词汇应用预检索有效性检查。Closed-weight LLMs occasionally fabricate plausible-sounding but non-existent brands (e.g., misspellings or invented private labels). Fabricated terms typically return sparse or empty retrieval results downstream, so the affected carousel simply is not visible to the user, but we do not currently apply a pre-retrieval validity check against a curated brand vocabulary.

离线评估中的反事实偏差。Counterfactual bias in offline evaluation.

我们的端到端指标源自记录的会话数据，该数据本身是由当前的检索和排名系统决定的。排除完全匹配和排名前 10 的产品可以通过隔离用户购买的产品来减轻这种偏差Our end-to-end metric is derived from logged session data, which is itself shaped by the current retrieval and ranking system. 6. 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

我们提出了一个可扩展的框架，通过隐式意图生成来提高电子商务和杂货搜索中的商品可发现性。通过有效地将封闭权重 LLM 的推理能力与微调 SLM 的成本效率相结合，我们证明了可以在查询分布的长尾上实现广泛的召回扩展，而无需高昂的成本。We present a scalable framework for improving item discoverability in e-commerce and grocery search through implicit intent generation. By effectively combining the reasoning capabilities of closed-weight LLMs with the cost-efficiency of finetuned SLMs, we demonstrated that it is possible to achieve broad recall expansion across the long tail of the query distribution without prohibitive cost.

我们的结果表明，可发现性应被视为搜索系统中的首要目标，特别是在具有强互补结构的领域。查询条件召回扩展提供了一种灵活的机制来弥合精确检索和探索性推荐之间的差距，使结果锚定到用户的直接上下文。Our results suggest that discoverability should be treated as a first-class objective in search systems, particularly in domains characterized by strong complementary structures. Query-conditioned recall expansion provides a flexible mechanism to bridge the gap between precise retrieval and exploratory recommendation, keeping results anchored to the user’s immediate context.

部署中的两个元经验可能会推广到该系统之外。首先，将生成与检索分离，使 LLM 远离面向用户的热路径，并使缓存、后备和部署变得易于处理。其次，双重评估框架——基于会话的购买预测加上经过人工验证的法学硕士法官——为标准相关性指标低于信用探索的其他发现表面提供了模板。Two meta-lessons from deployment may generalize beyond this system. First, decoupling generation from retrieval kept the LLM out of the user-facing hot path and made caching, fallback, and rollout tractable. Second, the dual evaluation framework—session-derived purchase prediction plus a human-validated LLM judge—offers a template for other discovery surfaces where standard relevance metrics under-credit exploration.
