走向自主加速器设计：Towards Autonomous Accelerator Design:
使用 SECDA 生成 FPGA 加速器FPGA Accelerator Generation with SECDA

Vinamra Sharma Xingjian Fu Jude Haris José Cano {}^{\quad}
School of Computing Science, University of Glasgow, Scotland, UK 
{2805343s, 3065487f}@student.gla.ac.uk ,{jude.haris, jose.canoreyes,}@glasgow.ac.uk
摘要Abstract

为现代人工智能工作负载设计基于 FPGA 的加速器需要探索大型且复杂的硬件设计空间，其中涉及架构参数、数据流策略和内存层次结构，使得该过程非常耗时。 虽然 SECDA 等现有方法可以通过 SystemC 仿真和 FPGA 执行实现快速硬件-软件协同设计，但识别高效的加速器配置仍然是一个很大程度上需要手动操作的过程，需要广泛的领域知识。 SECDA-DSE 是一个将大型语言模型 (LLM) 集成到 SECDA 生态系统中的框架，用于指导基于 FPGA 的加速器的设计空间探索 (DSE)。它将用于生成候选架构的结构化 DSE Explorer 与 LLM Stack 相结合，LLM Stack 使用检索增强生成和思维链提示执行推理引导的探索，再加上用于迭代和强化细化的反馈循环。Designing FPGA-based accelerators for modern artificial intelligence workloads requires exploring a large and complex hardware design space that involves architectural parameters, data flow strategies, and memory hierarchies, making the process very time consuming. While existing methodologies such as SECDA enable rapid hardware-software co-design through SystemC simulation and FPGA execution, identifying efficient accelerator configurations remains a largely manual process requiring extensive domain knowledge. SECDA-DSE is a framework that integrates Large Language Models (LLMs) into the SECDA ecosystem to guide design space exploration (DSE) of FPGA-based accelerators. It combines a structured DSE Explorer for generating candidate architectures with an LLM Stack that performs reasoning-guided exploration using retrieval-augmented generation and chain-of-thought prompting, coupled with a feedback loop for iterative and reinforced refinement.

基于我们之前介绍 SECDA-DSE 的工作，本文通过生成三种加速器设计（包括逐元素向量乘法、2D 卷积和矩阵转置）以及在 FPGA 硬件上执行端到端执行来扩展其评估。 结果表明，SECDA-DSE 可以生成符合 SECDA 标准的加速器设计，并在 FPGA 硬件上成功综合和执行。此外，生成的设计捕获了计算并行性和数据移动之间特定于内核的权衡，突出了法学硕士引导的探索在适应不同工作负载的架构配置方面的潜力，同时减少了探索时间和对广泛的人类专业知识的需求。Building on our previous work introducing SECDA-DSE, this paper extends its evaluation by generating three accelerator designs, including element-wise vector multiplication, 2D convolution, and matrix transpose, and performing end-to-end execution on FPGA hardware. The results show that SECDA-DSE can generate SECDA-compliant accelerator designs that are successfully synthesized and executed on FPGA hardware. Furthermore, the generated designs capture kernel-specific trade-offs between compute parallelism and data movement, highlighting the potential of LLM-guided exploration to adapt architectural configurations across diverse workloads while reducing exploration time and the need for extensive human expertise.

I 简介Introduction

为现代人工智能 (AI) 工作负载设计基于 FPGA 的加速器需要在交互的架构选择中进行选择，包括计算并行性、平铺因子、内存层次结构、数据流策略和片上资源分配。这些选择共同影响延迟、吞吐量、带宽压力和 FPGA 资源利用率，使得加速器设计空间探索既昂贵又需要专业知识密集型Designing FPGA-based accelerators for modern artificial intelligence (AI) workloads requires selecting among interacting architectural choices, including compute parallelism, tiling factors, memory hierarchy organization, dataflow strategies, and on-chip resource allocation. These choices jointly affect latency, throughput, bandwidth pressure, and FPGA resource utilization, making accelerator design space exploration both expensive and expertise-intensive[14]。 尽管与固定功能 ASIC 相比，基于 FPGA 的加速器提供了灵活性和能效，但由于存在大量架构排列和优化权衡，识别高性能加速器配置仍然是一项重大挑战。. Although FPGA-based accelerators provide flexibility and energy efficiency compared to fixed-function ASICs, identifying performant accelerator configurations remains a significant challenge due to the large number of architectural permutations and optimization trade-offs.

现有方法，例如 SECDAExisting methodologies such as SECDA[6]以及 SECDA-TFLite 等工具包and toolkits like SECDA-TFLite[7]通过基于 SystemC 的仿真和 FPGA 执行实现快速硬件软件协同设计，从而显着降低与定制加速器设计相关的开发开销。它们为人工智能加速提供可重用的架构模板和自动化硬件流程。然而，探索最佳加速器配置的过程仍然需要大量的手动设计空间探索（DSE）、迭代调整以及专家驱动的硬件权衡推理。enable rapid hardware software co-design through SystemC-based simulation and FPGA execution, significantly reducing the development overhead associated with custom accelerator design. They provide reusable architectural templates and automated hardware flows for AI acceleration. However, the process of exploring optimal accelerator configurations still requires extensive manual design space exploration (DSE), iterative tuning, and expert-driven reasoning over hardware trade-offs.

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展展示了跨软件工程和系统领域的推理、代码生成和结构化问题解决的强大能力Recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated strong capabilities in reasoning, code generation, and structured problem solving across software engineering and systems domains[9, 2]。这些功能通过实现推理引导的设计细化和工作负载感知架构适应，创造了协助或自动化硬件加速器探索的机会。然而，将 LLM 应用于 FPGA 加速器 DSE 仍然相对未经探索，特别是在将 LLM 推理与结构化硬件探索流程相结合以及通过真实 FPGA 执行验证生成的加速器设计方面。. These capabilities create an opportunity to assist or automate hardware accelerator exploration by enabling reasoning-guided design refinement and workload-aware architectural adaptation. However, applying LLMs to FPGA accelerator DSE remains relatively unexplored, particularly in combining LLM reasoning with structured hardware exploration flows and validating generated accelerator designs through real FPGA execution.

在我们之前的工作中In our previous work[17]，我们介绍了, we introducedSECDA-DSE，一个框架，将 LLM 引导推理集成到 FPGA 加速器 DSE 的 SECDA 生态系统中，并使用高级仿真评估单个加速器设计。 SECDA-DSE 结合了结构化的 DSE Explorer（负责生成和评估候选硬件加速器配置）和 LLM 堆栈（使用检索增强生成 (RAG)、思想链 (CoT) 提示和参数高效微调来执行推理引导的细化）。该框架包含一个反馈驱动的评估循环，其中收集并重用模拟和硬件执行指标，以迭代地改进后续决策，如图所示, a framework that integrates LLM-guided reasoning into the SECDA ecosystem for FPGA accelerator DSE, and evaluated a single accelerator design using high-level simulation. SECDA-DSE combines a structured DSE Explorer, responsible for generating and evaluating candidate hardware accelerator configurations, with an LLM Stack that performs reasoning-guided refinement using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning. The framework incorporates a feedback-driven evaluation loop in which simulation and hardware execution metrics are collected and reused to iteratively improve subsequent decisions, as shown in Figure1.

在本文中，我们提出了 SECDA-DSE，并通过生成多个加速器内核并在 FPGA 上执行端到端真实硬件执行来扩展其评估。 具体来说，我们评估生成的加速器用于逐元素向量乘法、2D 卷积和矩阵转置。生成的设计成功在 FPGA 硬件上执行，并展示了特定于工作负载的架构特征和资源利用模式，突出了该框架无需进行大量微调即可跨不同计算工作负载调整探索决策的能力。In this paper, we present SECDA-DSE and extend its evaluation by generating multiple accelerator kernels and performing end-to-end real hardware execution on an FPGA. Specifically, we evaluate generated accelerators for element-wise vector multiplication, 2D-convolution, and matrix transpose. The generated designs successfully execute on FPGA hardware and exhibit workload-specific architectural characteristics and resource utilization patterns, highlighting the ability of the framework to adapt exploration decisions across different computational workloads without extensive fine-tuning.

II 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A FPGA 加速器协同设计FPGA Accelerator Co-Design

基于 FPGA 的加速器由于其灵活性、可重配置性以及跨不同应用提供节能计算的能力，已成为部署现代 AI 工作负载的日益重要的平台FPGA-based accelerators have become an increasingly important platform for deploying modern AI workloads due to their flexibility, reconfigurability, and ability to provide energy-efficient computation across diverse applications[11]。然而，开发定制 FPGA 加速器仍然是一个复杂的过程，涉及软硬件协同设计、硬件验证、高级综合 (HLS) 和迭代性能优化. However, developing custom FPGA accelerators remains a complex process involving hardware-software co-design, hardware verification, high-level synthesis (HLS), and iterative performance optimization[5].

为了缩短加速器设计过程，SECDA 方法To shorten this accelerator design process, the SECDA methodology[6]旨在通过使用基于 SystemC 的仿真和 FPGA 执行流程实现快速硬件-软件协同设计来简化加速器开发。 SECDA-TFLitewas introduced to simplify accelerator development by enabling rapid hardware-software co-design using SystemC-based simulation and FPGA execution flows. The SECDA-TFLite[7]该工具包通过使用可重用的架构模板和自动化硬件生成流程实现基于 TensorFlow Lite 的 AI 加速器开发和部署，进一步扩展了该生态系统。最近，SECDA-LLMtoolkit further extended this ecosystem by enabling TensorFlow Lite-based AI accelerator development and deployment using reusable architectural templates and automated hardware generation flows. More recently, SECDA-LLM[8]探索了 SECDA 生态系统中大型语言模型 (LLM) 的集成，以协助加速器开发工作流程。explored the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) within the SECDA ecosystem to assist accelerator development workflows.

虽然这些框架显着降低了开发开销并提高了 FPGA 加速器设计的可访问性，但加速器配置和硬件优化策略的探索仍然在很大程度上依赖于手动 DSE，需要专业知识来推理架构和硬件限制。While these frameworks significantly reduce development overhead and improve accessibility of FPGA accelerator design, the exploration of accelerator configurations and hardware optimization strategies still largely depends on manual DSE, requiring expert knowledge to reason over architectural and hardware constraints.

Ⅱ-BII-B 用于 FPGA 加速器的 DSEDSE for FPGA Accelerators

DSE 是一种广泛使用的方法，用于在工作负载和特定于设备的限制下识别高效的硬件配置。现有的 FPGA DSE 方法通常依赖于详尽的参数扫描、启发式搜索方法、贝叶斯优化或遗传算法等技术来探索架构参数空间DSE is a widely used approach for identifying efficient hardware configurations under workload and device-specific constraints. Existing DSE methodologies for FPGAs commonly rely on exhaustive parameter sweeps, heuristic search methods, bayesian optimization, or techniques such as genetic algorithms to explore architectural parameter spaces[16, 1].

这些方法已经证明了它们在优化延迟、吞吐量、功耗和资源利用率等指标方面的有效性。然而，传统的 DSE 方法主要在结构化参数空间上运行，并且通常缺乏对工作负载行为、架构模式或实现权衡进行语义推理的能力。此外，随着加速器复杂性的增加，由于设计排列和硬件评估周期数量不断增加，详尽的探索在计算上变得昂贵These approaches have proven their effectiveness in optimizing metrics such as latency, throughput, power consumption, and resource utilization. However, traditional DSE methods primarily operate over structured parameter spaces and often lack the ability to reason semantically about workload behavior, architectural patterns, or implementation trade-offs. Also, as the complexity of the accelerator increases, exhaustive exploration becomes computationally expensive due to the growing number of design permutations and hardware evaluation cycles[20].

SECDA-DSE [17]提供了一种通过 LLM 引导的推理层增强结构化参数探索的方法，该推理层可以使用先前的硬件知识、检索到的设计上下文和评估反馈来完善加速器配置。provides an approach in which structured parameter exploration is augmented with an LLM-guided reasoning layer that can use prior hardware knowledge, retrieved design context, and evaluation feedback to refine accelerator configurations.

Ⅱ-CII-C 硬件和系统设计法学硕士LLMs for Hardware and Systems Design

法学硕士的最新进展促使他们将其用作结合了代码生成、工具交互和结构化决策的工程任务的助手。 这些功能激发了人们对将法学硕士应用于硬件和系统设计工作流程的兴趣，包括 RTL 生成、HLS 辅助、编译器优化和架构探索Recent advances in LLMs have motivated their use as assistants for engineering tasks that combine code generation, tool interaction, and structured decision making. These capabilities have motivated growing interest in applying LLMs to hardware and system design workflows, including RTL generation, HLS assistance, compiler optimization, and architecture exploration[13, 12, 22].

最近的几项工作探索了使用法学硕士来生成硬件描述或协助加速器开发Several recent works have explored the use of LLMs for generating hardware descriptions or assisting accelerator development[4, 3, 18]。 RAG 和 CoT 提示等方法还可以提高复杂工程任务的推理质量和上下文理解。同时，参数高效的微调技术，例如低秩适应（LoRA）. Approaches such as RAG and CoT prompting also improve reasoning quality and contextual understanding for complex engineering tasks. In parallel, parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques such as Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)[10]无需完整的模型重新训练即可实现法学硕士的适应。enable adaptation of LLMs without requiring full model retraining.

尽管取得了这些进步，现有的工作主要集中在独立的代码生成任务或独立的硬件综合辅助上。有限的工作探索了将 LLM 引导推理集成到结构化 FPGA DSE 工作流程中，其中包含基于检索的上下文、迭代评估反馈和实际硬件执行。Despite these advances, existing work largely focuses on isolated code generation tasks or standalone hardware synthesis assistance. Limited work has explored the integration of LLM-guided reasoning within structured FPGA DSE workflows that incorporate a retrieval-based context, iterative evaluation feedback, and real hardware execution.

相比之下，SECDA-DSE 在统一的加速器协同设计框架内集成了结构化设计探索、扎根检索推理、评估反馈和硬件感知细化。通过将基于 SECDA 的硬件评估与法学硕士指导推理相结合，SECDA-DSE 迈向自适应和自动化加速器设计工作流程。In contrast, SECDA-DSE integrates structured design exploration, grounded retrieval reasoning, evaluation feedback, and hardware-aware refinement within a unified accelerator co-design framework. By combining SECDA-based hardware evaluation with LLM-guided reasoning, SECDA-DSE move towards adaptive and automated accelerator design workflows.

三、IIISECDA-DSE

SECDA-DSE 通过引入 LLM 指导的加速器 DSE 框架扩展了 SECDA 生态系统。该框架将结构化 DSE 与推理引导的细化相结合，以迭代方式生成、评估和改进目标工作负载和 FPGA 平台的加速器配置。SECDA-DSE extends the SECDA ecosystem by introducing an LLM-guided framework for accelerator DSE. The framework combines a structured DSE with reasoning-guided refinement to iteratively generate, evaluate, and improve accelerator configurations for target workloads and FPGA platforms.

SECDA-DSE工作流程如图所示The SECDA-DSE workflow is shown in Figure1。其输入为： (i) 目标工作负载（例如 CNN、DNN）； (ii) 目标 FPGA 器件； (iii) 约束或指导架构探索的架构指令，例如平铺参数、计算并行性和内存层次结构组织。根据这些输入，SECDA-DSE 生成 SECDA 本机加速器设计，通过仿真和硬件执行流程对其进行评估，并存储生成的硬件数据点以供未来的细化迭代。. It takes as input: (i) a target workload (e.g., CNNs, DNNs); (ii) a target FPGA device; and (iii) architectural directives that constrain or guide architecture exploration, such as tiling parameters, compute parallelism, and memory hierarchy organization. Based on these inputs, SECDA-DSE generates SECDA native accelerator designs, evaluates them through simulation and hardware execution flows, and stores the resulting hardware datapoints for future refinement iterations.

与不受约束的硬件生成方法不同，SECDA-DSE 使用模板约束的加速器生成在 SECDA 生态系统中运行。 虽然 Constrained 限制了对符合 SECDA 的架构模式的探索，但它能够可靠地生成可综合和可执行的加速器，同时仍然允许 LLM Stack 探索包含计算并行性、内存组织和数据流配置的大型设计空间。 此外，该框架通过推理早期迭代中发现的错误来迭代地完善生成的设计。Unlike unconstrained hardware generation approaches, SECDA-DSE operates within the SECDA ecosystem using template-constrained accelerator generation. While constrained limits exploration to SECDA-compliant architectural patterns, it enables the reliable generation of synthesizable and executable accelerators while still allowing the LLM Stack to explore a large design space encompassing compute parallelism, memory organization, and dataflow configurations. Furthermore, the framework iteratively refines generated designs by reasoning about errors identified in earlier iterations.

Ⅲ-AIII-A DSE 探索者DSE Explorer

DSE Explorer 负责加速器设计空间的结构化探索。其主要作用是在工作负载和 FPGA 特定约束下生成候选加速器配置。The DSE Explorer is responsible for structured exploration of the accelerator design space. Its primary role is to generate candidate accelerator configurations under workload and FPGA-specific constraints.

DSE Explorer 通过生成架构参数的排列来运行，包括计算单元尺寸、平铺策略、缓冲区分配和数据流配置。这些参数集被实例化到符合 SECDA 标准的加速器模板中，从而能够与现有 SECDA 仿真和 FPGA 执行流程集成。 每个生成的加速器配置都会生成一个单独的设计运行，其中包含加速器描述、生成的源文件、HLS 输出和 FPGA 执行工件。使用基于 SECDA 的仿真和下游综合流程来评估最终的硬件设计，以收集性能指标，包括执行延迟、资源利用率和数据移动成本。The DSE Explorer operates by generating permutations of architectural parameters, including compute unit dimensions, tiling strategies, buffer allocations, and dataflow configurations. These parameter sets are instantiated into SECDA-compliant accelerator templates, enabling integration with existing SECDA simulation and FPGA execution flows. Each generated accelerator configuration produces an individual design run consisting of the accelerator description, generated source files, HLS outputs, and FPGA execution artifacts. The resulting hardware design is evaluated using SECDA-based simulation and downstream synthesis flows to collect performance metrics including execution latency, resource utilization, and data movement costs.

这些评估输出存储为硬件数据点，稍后重新用于微调 LLM 堆栈并指导后续的细化迭代。通过将结构化参数探索与评估驱动的反馈相结合，DSE Explorer 使 SECDA-DSE 能够比详尽的探索方法更有效地导航大型加速器设计空间。These evaluation outputs are stored as hardware datapoints that are later re-used to fine-tune the LLM Stack and guide subsequent refinement iterations. By combining structured parameter exploration with evaluation-driven feedback, the DSE Explorer enables SECDA-DSE to navigate large accelerator design spaces more efficiently than exhaustive exploration approaches.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 法学硕士堆栈LLM Stack

LLM Stack 充当 SECDA-DSE 内的推理和编排层。它的作用是分析生成的加速器配置，推理硬件权衡，并指导跨迭代的探索细化。 它结合了 RAG、CoT 提示和参数高效的微调，以提供工作负载感知的加速器设计生成。图The LLM Stack acts as the reasoning and orchestration layer within SECDA-DSE. Its role is to analyze the generated accelerator configurations, reason about hardware trade-offs, and guide exploration refinement across iterations. It incorporates RAG, CoT prompting, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning to provide workload-aware accelerator design generation. Ⅲ-BIII-B1 检索增强生成Retrieval-Augmented Generation

RAG 模块使 LLM Stack 能够从矢量化 SECDA 知识库中检索上下文信息，该知识库由 SECDA-TFLite 源代码、架构模板、API 级上下文和先前评估的硬件数据点组成。 RAG 模块不会在每次迭代期间公开完整的 SECDA 代码库或完整的硬件日志，而是仅检索与当前探索步骤相关的最相关的代码片段和硬件摘要。我们采用基于图的检索方法来有效地识别相关代码上下文，使用代码注释上的模糊匹配来指导跨图节点的导航。与标准最近邻检索方法相比，该方法减少了检索开销，同时提高了生成过程中的上下文相关性。The RAG module enables the LLM Stack to retrieve contextual information from a vectorized SECDA knowledge-base consisting of SECDA-TFLite source code, architectural templates, API-level context, and previously evaluated hardware datapoints. Rather than exposing the complete SECDA codebase or full hardware logs during each iteration, the RAG module retrieves only the most relevant code fragments and hardware summaries associated with the current exploration step. We employ a graph-based retrieval approach to efficiently identify relevant code context, using fuzzy matching on code comments to guide navigation across graph nodes. This method reduced retrieval overhead compared to standard nearest-neighbor retrieval approaches while improving contextual relevance during generation.

这种基于检索的方法使 SECDA-DSE 能够维持可管理的上下文大小，同时仍然提供足够的架构上下文以供细化。在推荐新的设计配置之前，检索到的信息包括工作负载特征、先前的加速器配置、硬件利用率摘要和评估结果。This retrieval-based approach enables SECDA-DSE to maintain manageable context sizes while still providing sufficient architectural context for refinement. The retrieved information includes workload characteristics, prior accelerator configurations, hardware utilization summaries, and evaluation outcomes, before recommending new design configurations.

Ⅲ-BIII-B2 思路链提示与微调Chain-of-Thought Prompting and Fine-Tuning

SECDA-DSE 采用 CoT 提示来鼓励加速器探索期间的结构化多步骤推理。 CoT 提示使 LLM Stack 能够在生成候选架构细化之前推理工作负载行为、硬件约束、内存组织和计算并行性。SECDA-DSE employs CoT prompting to encourage structured multi-step reasoning during accelerator exploration. CoT prompts enable the LLM Stack to reason about workload behavior, hardware constraints, memory organization, and compute parallelism before generating candidate architectural refinements.

对于模型自适应，SECDA-DSE 使用 LoRA 进行参数高效微调For model adaptation, SECDA-DSE uses parameter-efficient fine-tuning with LoRA[10]。微调数据集是使用之前探索的加速器配置及其通过 DSE-Explorer 生成的相关硬件评估结果构建的，并通过人机交互来确保正确性。. The fine-tuning dataset is built using previously explored accelerator configurations and their associated hardware evaluation outcomes generated via DSE-Explorer, with a human-in-the-loop for ensuring correctness.

每个硬件数据点由工作负载和 FPGA 上下文、生成的架构配置、仿真和执行成功状态、执行延迟和资源利用率指标组成。这些数据点使 LLM 堆栈能够迭代地适应生成可行、性能感知和目标设备感知的配置。Each hardware datapoint consists of workload and FPGA context, generated architectural configuration, simulation and execution success status, execution latency, and resource utilization metrics. These datapoints enable the LLM Stack to iteratively adapt toward generating configurations that are feasible, performance-aware, and target-device-aware.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 评估和反馈循环Evaluation and Feedback Loop

评估模块负责验证生成的加速器设计并收集用于迭代细化的反馈。该工作流程从基于 SECDA 的 SystemC 仿真开始，以验证功能正确性并收集早期性能估计。然后，生成的加速器设计将通过 HLS，一旦成功，将通过 FPGA 执行流程。 收集的评估指标包括执行延迟、FPGA 资源利用率、DMA 传输特性、数据移动成本和硬件执行验证。这些结果存储在 SECDA-DSE 硬件数据点数据库中，并在后续探索迭代中重复使用。The Evaluation Module is responsible for validating generated accelerator designs and collecting feedback used for iterative refinement. The workflow begins with a SECDA-based SystemC simulation to verify functional correctness and collect early-stage performance estimates. Generated accelerator designs are then passed through HLS and once succeed go through FPGA execution flows. The collected evaluation metrics include execution latency, FPGA resource utilization, DMA transfer characteristics, data movement costs, and hardware execution validation. These results are stored within the SECDA-DSE hardware datapoint database and reused during subsequent exploration iterations.

为了减少无效的加速器建议，SECDA-DSE 使用 SECDA 本机架构模板和设备感知参数范围来限制生成，而不是依赖于不受约束的自由形式硬件生成。仿真失败、违反资源限制或下游综合流程失败的候选设计将被拒绝并记录为负数据点，这些数据点再次作为负强化反馈给法学硕士进行推理和纠正。To reduce invalid accelerator proposals, SECDA-DSE constrains generation using SECDA native architectural templates and device-aware parameter ranges rather than relying on unconstrained free-form hardware generation. Candidate designs that fail simulation, violate resource constraints, or fail downstream synthesis flows are rejected and logged as negative datapoints, which are again fed back as a negative reinforcement to the LLM to reason and correct.

DSE Explorer、LLM Stack 和评估模块之间的迭代交互使 SECDA-DSE 能够反复完善加速器探索决策，同时利用跨工作负载和 FPGA 平台的先前硬件评估知识。The iterative interaction between the DSE Explorer, LLM Stack, and Evaluation Module enables SECDA-DSE to repeatedly refine accelerator exploration decisions while leveraging prior hardware evaluation knowledge across workloads and FPGA platforms.

IV 评价Evaluation

为了扩展 SECDA-DSE 的评估，我们生成并评估了三个加速器工作负载：逐元素向量乘法、2D 卷积和矩阵转置。这些工作负载被有意选择来代表不同的加速器执行特征，包括算术密集型计算、面向卷积的空间计算和内存移动主导的执行模式。附录中提供了用于生成这些加速器设计的提示。To extend the evaluation of SECDA-DSE, we generated and evaluated three accelerator workloads: element-wise vector multiplication, 2D convolution, and matrix transpose. These workloads were intentionally selected to represent different accelerator execution characteristics, including arithmetic intensive computation, convolution-oriented spatial compute, and memory movement dominated execution patterns. The prompts used for generating these accelerator designs are provided in the Appendix.

SECDA-DSE 工作流程从用户提供的自然语言加速器规范开始。该提示通过 LLM 堆栈进行处理，其中 RAG 模块从矢量化知识库中检索相关的 SECDA 实现上下文、架构模板以及之前收集的硬件数据点。然后，检索到的上下文与 CoT 提示相结合，生成结构化加速器生成提示，并通过 SECDA 特定的架构指导进行丰富。The SECDA-DSE workflow begins with a natural language accelerator specification provided by the user. This prompt is processed through the LLM Stack, where the RAG module retrieves the relevant SECDA implementation context, architectural templates, and previously collected hardware datapoints from the vectorized knowledge-base. The retrieved context is then combined with CoT prompting to generate a structured accelerator generation prompt enriched with SECDA-specific architectural guidance.

使用这种丰富的上下文，SECDA-DSE 生成本机 SystemC 加速器设计以及所需的 SECDA 集成文件。在当前的实施中，生成的设计使用 SECDA 工具包通过人机交互评估工作流程。生成的加速器首先使用 HLS 进行验证，然后通过下游逻辑综合和 FPGA 执行流程对成功通过 HLS 的设计进行评估。Using this enriched context, SECDA-DSE generates native SystemC accelerator designs together with the required SECDA integration files. In the current implementation, the generated designs are passed through a human-in-the-loop evaluation workflow using the SECDA toolkit. The generated accelerators are first validated using HLS, and the designs that successfully pass HLS are then evaluated through downstream logic synthesis and FPGA execution flows.

The vector multiplication required four iterative refinement cycles before producing a valid FPGA executable design. The generated accelerator designs were not manually modified at any stage.

这些结果提供了早期证据，表明结合结构化 DSE、基于检索的推理和迭代硬件评估可以使自适应加速器生成工作流程能够处理不同的工作负载特征，同时减少 FPGA 加速器开发过程中传统上所需的手动工作。These results provide early evidence that combining structured DSE, retrieval-grounded reasoning, and iterative hardware evaluation can enable adaptive accelerator generation workflows capable of handling diverse workload characteristics while reducing the manual effort traditionally required during FPGA accelerator development.

V 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

虽然 SECDA-DSE 在多个加速器工作负载上展示了有希望的早期结果，但该框架仍处于积极的开发阶段，目前存在一些局限性。 主要挑战之一是，最初需要一组足够多样化的硬件数据点来有效地微调和细化 LLM 堆栈。 此外，当前的工作流程仍然包含人机循环评估阶段，用于在 FPGA 执行之前验证生成的设计。尽管这提高了早期开发期间的可靠性，但将框架扩展到更大的工作负载覆盖范围和更广泛的探索空间将需要更高水平的自动化。 另一个限制是当前的评估仅涵盖单个 FPGA 平台上的一小部分工作负载。尽管生成的加速器展示了工作负载感知行为和成功的 FPGA 执行，但需要对更多工作负载、架构和 FPGA 系列进行更广泛的评估，以评估 SECDA-DSE 的泛化能力。While SECDA-DSE demonstrates promising early-stage results across multiple accelerator workloads, the framework is still in an active development phase and currently presents some limitations. One of the primary challenges is that a sufficiently diverse set of hardware datapoints are initially required for effective fine-tuning and refinement of the LLM Stack. Furthermore, the current workflow still incorporates a human-in-the-loop evaluation stage for validating generated designs before FPGA execution. Although this improves reliability during early-stage development, scaling the framework toward larger workload coverage and broader exploration spaces will require significantly higher levels of automation. Another limitation is that the current evaluation covers only a small set of workloads on a single FPGA platform. Although the generated accelerators demonstrate workload-aware behavior and successful FPGA execution, broader evaluations across more workloads, architectures, and FPGA families are needed to assess the generalization capability of SECDA-DSE.

在未来的工作中，我们的目标是通过模型上下文协议（MCP）服务器将 SECDA 工具包直接集成到 SECDA-DSE 中，从而完全自动化加速器评估工作流程。这种集成将允许 LLM Stack 直接调用 SECDA 测试、模拟、HLS 和硬件执行功能，而无需手动干预，从而实现更加自主的硬件探索工作流程。 此外，当前的评估侧重于相对较小的工作负载集和单个 FPGA 平台，作为未来的工作，我们计划将 SECDA-DSE 的评估扩展到多个参数范围、LLM 和 FPGA，以评估模型大小、架构和 FPGA 板的选择如何影响加速器生成质量和硬件探索能力。In future work, our goal is to fully automate the accelerator evaluation workflow by integrating the SECDA toolkit directly into SECDA-DSE through a model context protocol (MCP) server. This integration will allow the LLM Stack to directly invoke SECDA testing, simulation, HLS, and hardware execution functions without requiring manual intervention, enabling a more autonomous hardware exploration workflow. Additionally, the current evaluation focuses on a relatively small set of workloads and a single FPGA platform, as future work we plan to extend evaluation of SECDA-DSE across multiple parameter scales, LLMs and FPGAs to evaluate how model size, architecture and choice of the FPGA board influence accelerator generation quality and hardware exploration capability.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 SECDA-DSE，这是一个基于 SECDA 生态系统的法学硕士指导框架，用于 FPGA 加速器设计空间探索，并通过端到端 FPGA 执行扩展了其评估。 SECDA-DSE 结合了结构化 DSE、检索增强推理、思维链提示和迭代硬件评估，根据自然语言规范生成加速器设计。 具体来说，我们生成并执行了针对 Xilinx Zynq-7000 FPGA 平台的逐元素向量乘法、2D 卷积和矩阵转置加速器。生成的设计成功地完成了 FPGA 实现流程，并展示了跨不同计算和内存访问模式的工作负载感知硬件行为。 我们的结果提供了进一步的证据，表明将 LLM 引导推理与结构化 FPGA 设计工作流程相结合可以支持跨不同工作负载的自适应加速器生成，同时减少手动设计工作并推进更加自动化的硬件设计方法。所有生成的加速器在 FPGA 上的成功执行进一步证明了 SECDA-DSE 适应不同工作负载特征的能力。We presented SECDA-DSE, an LLM-guided framework for FPGA accelerator design space exploration built upon the SECDA ecosystem, and extended its evaluation through end-to-end FPGA execution. SECDA-DSE combines structured DSE, retrieval-augmented reasoning, Chain-of-Thought prompting, and iterative hardware evaluation to generate accelerator designs from natural language specifications. Specifically, we generated and executed element-wise vector multiplication, 2D convolution, and matrix transpose accelerators targeting a Xilinx Zynq-7000 FPGA platform. The generated designs successfully progressed through the FPGA implementation flow and demonstrated workload-aware hardware behavior across different compute and memory access patterns. Our results provide further evidence that combining LLM-guided reasoning with structured FPGA design workflows can support adaptive accelerator generation across diverse workloads while reducing manual design effort and advancing more automated hardware design methodologies. The successful FPGA execution of all generated accelerators further demonstrates the ability of SECDA-DSE to adapt across different workload characteristics.
