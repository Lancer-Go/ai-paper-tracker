摘要Abstract

LLM 代理越来越多地在跨越数百个连续事件的开放式环境中运行，但它们在很大程度上仍然是无状态的：每个任务都是从头开始解决的，而没有将过去的经验转化为更好的未来行为。 中心障碍不是LLM agents increasingly operate in open-ended environments spanning hundreds of sequential episodes, yet they remain largely stateless: each task is solved from scratch without converting past experience into better future behavior. The central obstacle is not什么what记住但是to remember but如何使用how to use记住了什么，包括应用哪种检索策略、如何解释先前的结果以及当前策略本身何时必须改变。 我们介绍what has been remembered, including which retrieval policy to apply, how to interpret prior outcomes, and when the current strategy itself must change. 3.3 三层进化记忆Three-Tier Evolving Memory

原始剧集日志噪音太大且数量庞大，无法直接检索。代理需要逐步将特定事件中的经验提炼成一般模式，然后转化为可操作的规则。Raw episode logs are too noisy and voluminous to retrieve directly. The agent needs to progressively distill experience from specific episodes into general patterns and then into actionable rules.爱尔AEL通过自动升级的三个内存层来实现这一点。achieves this through three memory tiers with automatic promotion.

情景记忆Episodic memory记录每一集的原始结果：使用了哪些工具，它们产生了哪些信号，每个信号是否正确以及整体预测质量。 这为蒸馏提供了真实的训练数据。records each episode’s raw outcome: which tools were used, what signals they produced, whether each signal was correct, and the overall prediction quality. This provides the ground-truth training data for distillation.

语义记忆Semantic memory将情节记录聚合成跨情节模式，并定期（每 10 集）进行提取。 这些模式捕捉了任何单一事件中看不见的规律，例如“动量指标对于趋势股票来说是可靠的，但在反转期间会产生误导”。aggregates episodic records into cross-episode patterns, distilled periodically (every 10 episodes). These patterns capture regularities invisible in any single episode, such as “momentum indicators are reliable for trending stocks but misleading during reversals.”

程序记忆Procedural memory将高置信度语义模式提升为直接注入规划器提示的可执行规则。 这些规则代表了代理最值得信赖的知识，无需在查询时检索即可影响行为。promotes high-confidence semantic patterns into executable rules injected directly into planner prompts.
