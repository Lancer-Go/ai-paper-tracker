通过 ROI 重新思考和合成数据增强脑部 MRI 异常检测和推理Enhancing Brain MRI Anomaly Detection and Reasoning with ROI Rethink and Synthetic Data

Shangkun Li*    Jie Xu*    Yi Guo    Zeju Li†    Yuanyuan Wang†
摘要Abstract

医学视觉语言模型通常通过单遍推理生成诊断，而不指示哪些图像区域支持其结论。空间基础的缺乏限制了临床实用性：输出无法被审计，并且模型可能会产生正常扫描结果的幻觉。我们呈现Medical vision-language models typically generate diagnoses through single-pass inference without indicating which image regions support their conclusions. This lack of spatial grounding limits clinical utility: outputs cannot be audited, and models may hallucinate findings on normal scans. We present布雷马克BrReMark (Br艾因ainRe通过投资回报率思考think via ROI马克Marking），一个将显式区域标记引入脑 MRI 诊断的框架。该模型首先生成有关潜在异常的假设，并通过明确的边界框标记来验证这些假设，然后通过重新检查标记的证据来验证结论。训练将结构化推理轨迹的监督微调与使用定位准确性和诊断推理的复合奖励的强化学习结合起来。此外，我们集成了基于域随机化的病理学合成增强策略，以提高模型对分布外（OOD）数据的通用性。在内部基准测试中，BrReMark 改进了 mAPing), a framework that introduces explicit region marking into brain MRI diagnosis. The model first generates hypotheses about potential abnormalities and grounds them through explicit bounding box marking, then verifies conclusions by re-examining the marked evidence. Training combines supervised fine-tuning on structured reasoning trajectories with reinforcement learning using a composite reward over localization accuracy and diagnostic reasoning. Furthermore, we integrate a domain randomization-based pathology synthesis augmentation strategy to improve the model’s generalizability to out-of-distribution (OOD) data. On internal benchmark, BrReMark improves mAP50与基础模型相比，从 0.74% 提高到 37.54%，同时实现 21.57 的临床 F1 和 45.26% 的诊断准确率。在 NOVA OOD 基准测试中，它还获得了具有竞争力的整体性能，得分为 45.7from 0.74% to 37.54% compared to the base model, while achieving 21.57 Clinical F1 and 45.26% diagnostic accuracy. On NOVA OOD benchmark, it also achieves competitive overall performance with a 45.7%\%与最先进的技术相比，误报减少，表明对罕见病理的幻觉减少。这些发现表明，明确的假设验证基础是在分布内和 OOD 环境中实现值得信赖的开放式脑 MRI 诊断的实用途径。reduction in false positives compared to the state-of-the-art, indicating reduced hallucination on rare pathologies. These findings suggest that explicit hypothesis-verification grounding is a practical path toward trustworthy open-ended brain MRI diagnosis across both in-distribution and OOD settings.

1 简介Introduction

值得信赖的人工智能辅助诊断不仅需要准确的预测，还需要临床医生可以验证的可审计推理Trustworthy AI-assisted diagnosis requires not only accurate predictions but also auditable reasoning that clinicians can verify[18]。医学视觉语言模型 (VLM)（例如 LLaVa-Med. Medical vision-language models (VLMs) (e.g., LLaVa-Med[15], 华佗GPT-Vision, HuatuoGPT-Vision[6]、灵枢, and Lingshu[24]）在放射学对话和视觉问答方面取得了出色的表现，但它们的临床实用性仍然受到基本可信度差距的限制：模型在没有表明哪些图像区域支持诊断的情况下生成结论，使得输出无法审核，并且在正常扫描时容易产生幻觉) have achieved strong performance on radiology dialogue and visual question answering, yet their clinical utility remains limited by a fundamental trustworthiness gap: models generate conclusions without indicating which image regions support the diagnosis, making outputs impossible to audit and prone to hallucination on normal scans[25]。这个问题对于脑 MRI 来说尤其严重，其中病变位置直接影响鉴别诊断，而罕见神经系统疾病的流行带来了严重的分布外挑战. This issue is particularly acute for brain MRI, where lesion location directly informs differential diagnosis and the prevalence of rare neurological diseases introduces severe out-of-distribution challenges[4].

为了弥合这种可信度差距，新兴的“用图像思考”范式To bridge this trustworthiness gap, the emerging "Thinking with Images" paradigm[8, 22, 27]通过从被动观察转向主动感知，提供了一个有前途的解决方案。与依赖单遍推理的传统模型不同，这种范例有助于与视觉数据的迭代交互。这种多轮推理过程允许模型制定中间假设、放大诊断显着区域并执行自我校正。因此，最终的诊断决策得到可验证的视觉证据链的支持，使模型的行为与临床标准保持一致。与 fTSPL 不同，fTSPL 从 fMRI 激活生成文本以协助表示学习和预测offers a promising solution by shifting from passive observation to active perception. Unlike traditional models that rely on single-pass inference, this paradigm facilitates iterative interaction with visual data. This multi-turn reasoning process allows the model to formulate intermediate hypotheses, zoom in on diagnostically salient regions, and perform self-correction. Consequently, the final diagnostic decision is supported by a verifiable chain of visual evidence, aligning the model’s behavior with clinical standards. Unlike fTSPL, which generates text from fMRI activations to assist representation learning and prediction[23], BrReMark 明确确定结构 MRI 中的病变位置，并生成可审核的 ROI 标记以支持诊断。, BrReMark explicitly grounds lesion locations in structural MRI and produces auditable ROI markings to support diagnosis.

尽管最近取得了进展，但一些关键的限制阻碍了其在脑 MRI 诊断中的临床应用。主要是流行的基准测试，例如 VQA-RADDespite recent advancements, several critical limitations hinder its clinical application in brain MRI diagnosis. Primarily, prevailing benchmarks like VQA-RAD[13]和斯莱克and SLAKE[17]强调封闭式准确性，这本质上与现实世界临床场景的开放性本质不一致。因此，开放式异常检测在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索。此外，受这些封闭集范式和有限数据的限制，现有模型往往会过度拟合已知分布，严重损害其泛化到分布外 (OOD) 数据的能力emphasize closed-ended accuracy, which inherently misaligns with the open-set nature of real-world clinical scenarios. Consequently, open-ended anomaly detection remains largely underexplored. Furthermore, constrained by these closed-set paradigms and limited data, existing models tend to overfit to known distributions, severely compromising their ability to generalize to out-of-distribution (OOD) data[4]。开发能够处理开放式临床复杂性的新方法对于现实世界的 VLM 部署至关重要。. Developing novel methods capable of handling open-set clinical complexities is essential for real-world VLM deployment.

为此，我们提出To this end, we present布雷马克BrReMark (B雨rainRe通过投资回报率思考think via ROI马克Marking），一种新颖的“用图像思考”框架，通过明确反映人类诊断工作流程来解决开放式 OOD 临床挑战。具体来说，BrReMark 采用渐进式两阶段训练范例：监督微调 (SFT)，然后是组相对策略优化 (GRPO)ing), a novel "Think with Images" framework that tackles open-set OOD clinical challenges by explicitly mirroring the human diagnostic workflow. Specifically, BrReMark employs a progressive two-stage training paradigm: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) followed by Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO[20]）基于强化学习（RL）。我们的主要贡献有四个：)-based Reinforcement Learning (RL).   1. 1.

  2. 2.

    临床对齐 RL 优化Clinical-Aligned RL Optimization：为了促进自由形式、开放式诊断，我们设计了一个包含定位准确性、语义正确性和临床安全性的多组件奖励函数。在 GRPO 的推动下，这种综合奖励有效地将 VLM 的生成能力与严格的临床要求结合起来，将其性能从严格的封闭式指标提升为可靠的诊断叙述。: To facilitate free-form, open-ended diagnosis, we design a multi-component reward function encompassing localization accuracy, semantic correctness, and clinical safety. Driven by GRPO, this composite reward effectively aligns the VLM’s generative capabilities with stringent clinical requirements, elevating its performance from rigid closed-ended metrics to reliable diagnostic narratives.

  3. 3.

    合成驱动的 OOD 稳健性Synthesis-Driven OOD Robustness：我们通过病理数据增强扩展 SynthSeg 来解决罕见临床病变的稀缺问题，迫使模型在 RL 阶段定位结构异常。该策略增强了模型对未见过的 OOD 病例的鲁棒性，反映了现实世界医疗数据的复杂性。: We address the scarcity of rare clinical lesions by extending SynthSeg with pathological data augmentation, compelling the model to localize structural abnormalities during the RL stage. This strategy enhances the model’s robustness on unseen OOD cases, mirroring the complexities of real-world medical data.

  4. 4.

    综合评价Comprehensive Evaluation：我们引入了一个新的两轮交互式脑部 MRI 数据集，旨在实现 BrReMark 框架，代表了七个开源数据集的全面集成。我们根据内部和外部基准彻底验证了 BrReMark；与相同容量级别的其他型号相比，它取得了优异的结果。: We introduce a new two-turn interactive brain MRI dataset designed to realize BrReMark framework, representing a comprehensive integration of seven open-source datasets. We thoroughly validated BrReMark against internal and external benchmarks; it achieves superior results compared to other models within the same capacity class.

4 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 BrReMark，这是第一个用于开放式脑 MRI 诊断的扎根推理框架。通过假设-标记-验证认知链，BrReMark 将异常检测、描述和诊断统一在一个交互式范例中。我们的多组件奖励函数支持对开放式输出进行 RL 训练，从而在 BrReMark-Bench (mAPWe present BrReMark, the first grounded reasoning framework for open-ended brain MRI diagnosis. Through a hypothesis-mark-verification cognitive chain, BrReMark unifies anomaly detection, description, and diagnosis within a single interactive paradigm. Our multi-component reward function enables RL training on open-ended outputs, achieving substantial improvements on BrReMark-Bench (mAP50: 0.74%→\rightarrow37.54%）以及 NOVA 上的强泛化性。 未来的工作将考虑直接 3D 体积推理和多模态集成（例如 CT、其他 MRI 序列），并进行前瞻性临床验证。37.54%) and strong generalization on NOVA. Future work would consider to direct 3D volumetric reasoning and multi-modal integration (e.g., CT, other MRI sequences), with prospective clinical validation.
