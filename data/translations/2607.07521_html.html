摩擦产生的创造力：Creativity from Friction:
用于探索性结构设计的人机交互Human–AI Interaction for Exploratory Structural Design

摘要Abstract

根据用户输入生成最终答案的人工智能代理通常不能满足创意领域的需求。结构设计和建筑等领域需要交互式系统来帮助用户具体化和发展想法、探索替代方案并完善部分解决方案。此类设计的最终产品需要遵守许多约束，例如空间配置、机械行为、材料数量和成本。这些限制会在设计过程中产生摩擦，从而激发新颖且富有创意的解决方案。在本文中，我们讨论了当前生成式人工智能消除摩擦并提供最终解决方案的目标与通过迭代工作开发想法的结构设计师（例如结构设计师）的需求之间的不一致。我们提出了系统的设计尺寸，允许AI agents that generate final answers based on user input often do not meet the needs of creative fields. Fields such as structural design and architecture need interactive systems that help users externalise and develop ideas, explore alternatives, and refine partial solutions. The final product of such designs needs to comply with many constraints concerning, e.g., spatial configuration, mechanical behaviour, material quantities, and costs. These constraints create friction in the design process, which can stimulate novel and creative solutions. In this paper, we discuss the misalignment between current generative AI goals to remove friction and provide final solutions and the needs of creators, such as structural designers, who develop ideas through iterative work. We present the design dimensions of systems allowing for有限的人类与人工智能共同创造constrained human–AI co-creation依赖于视觉语言模型，使结构探索成为对话式、多模式的，并以遵循和增强学科创造性过程的方式响应不断变化的人类意图。通过基于这些原则的试点设计界面以及与该领域专家的研究，本文展示了结构设计师如何感知交互式人工智能系统，以及此类系统如何通过减少重复建模摩擦同时保留反思性设计摩擦来支持设计空间探索。that rely on vision-language models making structural exploration conversational, multimodal, and responsive to evolving human intent in ways that follow and augment the discipline’s creative process. Through a pilot design interface based on these principles and a study with experts in the field, this paper shows how structural designers perceive interactive AI systems and how such systems can support design space exploration by reducing repetitive modelling friction while preserving reflective design friction.

1 简介Introduction

生成式人工智能通常被评估为根据用户输入生成合理的最终输出（例如图像、文本、计划或模型）的能力。然而，创意设计工作很少会一次性进展到最终产品。设计师将部分想法具体化，比较替代方案，并使用中间模型进行思考、协作和创造。这一观点与舍恩的创意学科专业人士模型产生共鸣，他们从事“行动中的反思”，使他们的创作过程适应不断变化的约束和想法Generative AI is often evaluated for its ability to produce plausible final outputs such as an image, a text, a plan, or a model from user input. However, creative design work rarely progresses as a one-shot process to the final product. Designers externalise partial ideas, compare alternatives, and use intermediate models to think, collaborate, and create. This view resonates with Schön’s model for professionals of creative disciplines that engage in “reflection-in-action” adapting their creation process to ever evolving constraints and ideas(Schön, 1983)。因此，创意领域的生成式人工智能必须从工作流程自动化转向. Thus, generative AI in creative domains must shift from workflow automation to探索增强exploration augmentation Rezwana and Maher (2023); Wang et al. (2025).

结构设计使这种需求尤为明显。这是一门创造性的学科，但不是不受约束的学科。结构工程师必须平衡空间约束，同时考虑荷载、支撑、跨度、可施工性、经济性、安全性等。在此过程中，工程师寻求从管理所有这些限制的摩擦中产生的创造性解决方案。Structural design makes this need particularly visible. It is a creative discipline, but not an unconstrained one. Structural engineers must balance spatial constraints while reasoning about loads, supports, spans, constructability, economy, safety, among others. In doing so, engineers seek creative solutions that emerge from the friction of managing all these constraints.Teng et al. (2004)将结构设计中的创造力定义为新颖且满足功能限制的设计的产生，并强调发散思维的重要性，以从先前问题的解决方案中进行类比并探索不同的替代方案。define creativity in structural design as the production of a design that is novel and satisfies functional constraints, and highlight the importance of divergent thinking to draw analogies from solutions to previous problems and explore different alternatives.

结构探索一直是最近研究的焦点，其中探索不同结构形式的算法是首选，而不是对预定类型的单目标优化Structural exploration has been the focus of recent research in which algorithms that explore different structural forms are preferred rather than single-objective optimisations on predetermined typologies(Mueller, 2014; Danhaive and Mueller, 2021; Lee et al., 2016; Saldana Ochoa et al., 2021)。生成式人工智能通过允许多模式交互和高级推理，为扩大这种探索带来了新的机会. Generative AI brings new opportunities to expand this exploration by allowing multimodal interactions and advanced reasoningZhang et al. (2025); Suh et al. (2024)。这就提出了一个关键问题：. This raises a key question:我们如何正式定义此类交互，以便这些系统有效支持和增强设计过程？how can we formally define such interactions so that these systems effectively support and enhance the design process?

我们通过引入以下概念来回答这个问题We answer this question by introducing the concept of受约束的共同创造constrained co-creation结构设计：交互式人类-人工智能工作流程，其中创造性探索是由领域约束的持续协商形成的。这些系统支持的设计过程成为一种交互活动，人类和人工智能代理共同探索和完善不断发展的设计方案。与最终答案自动化不同，受约束的共同创造可以帮助设计人员外部化、检查、修改和评估替代方案，同时保持人力代理for structural design: interactive human–AI workflows in which creative exploration is shaped by the ongoing negotiation of domain constraints. The design process supported by these systems becomes an interactive activity in which human and AI agents jointly explore and refine evolving design alternatives. Unlike final-answer automation, constrained co-creation helps designers externalise, inspect, revise, and evaluate alternatives while maintaining human agencyHolter and El-Assady (2024)并允许基于领域的交互式工作流程and allowing domain-grounded interactive workflows(Song et al., 2024)。此类系统可以消除重复动作中的摩擦，同时保持约束中产生的摩擦并引导设计过程。. Such systems can remove friction from repetitive actions while maintaining friction that arises from the constraints and steers the design process.

本文通过以下方式为结构设计领域的人机交互做出贡献：(1) 定义生产性摩擦及其对于交互式结构设计的重要性；(2) 确定未来人机交互系统的四个设计维度；(3) 提出人工智能支持的设计工作流程的原型；(4) 报告一项初步研究，以调查专家如何在拟议的设计场景中与该原型交互。This paper contributes to human–AI interaction in the field of structural design by (1) defining productive friction and its importance for interactive structural design, (2) identifying four design dimensions for future human–AI systems, (3) presenting a prototype of an AI–supported design workflow, and (4) reporting a preliminary study to investigate how experts interact with this prototype in a proposed design scenario.

2 结构设计探索与人机共创Structural Design Exploration and Human–AI Co-Creation

2.1 结构设计作为创意约束谈判Structural Design as Creative Constraint Negotiation

结构设计是连接建筑学和物理学的学科，涉及确保建筑系统的机械性能，安全有效地抵抗施加的载荷，同时满足建筑、空间、材料、环境和施工要求。与许多创意领域主要通过审美或表现力来评判人工制品不同，结构设计与其工作原理密不可分。 Billington 将结构“艺术”定义为效率、经济和优雅这三个 E 的创造性综合Structural design is the discipline that connects architecture and physics and is concerned with ensuring the mechanical performance of building systems that safely and efficiently resist applied loads while satisfying architectural, spatial, material, environmental, and construction requirements. Unlike many creative domains in which an artefact may be judged primarily by aesthetic or expressive qualities, a structural design is inseparable from how it works. Billington defines structural “art” as a creative synthesis of the three E’s of efficiency, economy, and elegance(Billington, 1983)。图. Figure1展示了比林顿结构艺术的构造示例，其中结构不是通过不受约束的创造性行为构思出来的，而是在多重和多学科约束下形式和力量协商的结果illustrates constructed examples of Billington’s structural art where the structures are not conceived by an unconstrained creative act but are rather the result of the negotiation of form and force under multiple and multidisciplinary constraints(Allen and Zalewski, 2010)。其中，Chiasso Shed 桁架的几何形状遵循其内力，允许最少的材料使用；杜勒斯机场屋顶遵循悬链线的几何形状，形成高效的纯拉伸几何形状；布罗德盖特交换大楼嵌入了一个承重拱门，将力量从中心转移开，从而清理了建筑物下方的火车轨道；埃菲尔铁塔的形状可以抵抗横向风荷载的影响。这些通过创造性和有限的设计空间探索而实现的新颖项目，至今仍然是具有里程碑意义的作品。. Among them, the Chiasso Shed truss geometry follows its internal forces allowing for minimal material usage; the Dulles Airport roof follows the geometry of a catenary resulting in an efficient purely-tensile geometry; the Broadgate Exchange House embeds a load-bearing arch to transfer forces away from the centre, clearing the train tracks running below the building; and the Eiffel Tower was shaped to resist the effects of lateral wind loads. These novel projects, achieved through creative and constrained design space exploration, remain landmark works today.

最近关于建筑结构的生成人工智能的研究通过调查数据表示、生成算法、评估方法和优化循环如何用于结构设计自动化，扩展了这一轨迹Recent work on generative AI for building structures extends this trajectory by surveying how data representations, generative algorithms, evaluation methods, and optimisation loops are being used for structural design automation(Liao et al., 2024)。然而，大部分工作仍然主要将人工智能视为生成器或优化器。相比之下，语言和视觉语言代理提供了支持对话式和多模式形式的设计空间探索的新机会。此类代理不是向设计人员提供预先计算或参数化的替代方案，而是允许用户通过文本和多模式输入来引导结构模型的生成、转换和评估。事实上，建筑和结构工程的设计意图很少仅通过文本来传达。设计师使用草图、注释和部分模型来表达模糊的空间想法。最近基于草图生成的 3D 生成工作凸显了在工程中解释草图的前景和困难. However, much of this work still frames AI primarily as a generator or optimiser. Language and vision-language agents, in contrast, offer new opportunities to support a conversational and multimodal form of design space exploration. Instead of presenting designers with precomputed or parametrised alternatives, such agents can allow users to steer the generation, transformation, and evaluation of structural models through text and multimodal input. Indeed, the design intent in architecture and structural engineering is rarely communicated through text alone. Designers use sketches, annotations, and partial models to express ambiguous spatial ideas. Recent work on sketch-based 3D generation highlights both the promise and the difficulty of interpreting sketches in engineering(Tono et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024; He et al., 2025)。草图很有用，因为它们快速且富有表现力，有时可以编码隐性知识，但它们也不完整、含糊不清，并且当前模型难以可靠地解释。因此，挑战在于构建能够处理不完整的多模式人类表达的人工智能系统，同时保持不断发展的人工制品以结构逻辑为基础。. Sketches are useful because they are fast and expressive and sometimes encode tacit knowledge, but they are also incomplete, ambiguous, and difficult for current models to interpret reliably. The challenge is therefore to build AI systems that can work with incomplete multimodal human expressions while keeping the evolving artefact grounded in structural logic.

(2025)用于可视化分析中的代理界面。这些维度专门针对以前的共同创意和混合倡议框架for agentic interfaces in visual analytics. These dimensions specialise previous co-creative and mixed-initiative frameworks(Rezwana and Maher, 2023; Wang et al., 2025; Holter and El-Assady, 2024)到结构设计领域。我们建议这些接口应该支持：（1）结构设计知识的模型基础，（2）人类和人工智能可读的数据结构，（3）状态意识和交互历史，以及（4）设计意图的多模式表达。to the field of structural design. We propose that these interfaces should support: (1) model grounding in structural design knowledge, (2) human- and AI-readable data structures, (3) state awareness and interaction history, and (4) multimodal expression of design intent.

结构设计知识的模型基础。Model grounding in structural design knowledge.

可靠的系统必须以特定学科的逻辑为基础A reliable system must be grounded in discipline-specific logicSong et al. (2024)。对于结构设计，这包括结构载荷路径的基本概念、典型跨度、载荷和横截面的知识，以及对构件层次结构和边界条件定义的理解。如果没有这样的基础，人工智能系统可能会生成视觉上令人信服但结构上不正确的人工制品，这反过来可能会导致领域专家对系统失去信心。. For structural design, this includes basic notions of structural load paths, knowledge of typical spans, loads, and cross-sections, and understanding of member hierarchy and boundary condition definition. Without such a grounding, an AI system may generate artefacts that are visually convincing but structurally incorrect, which might, in turn, cause domain experts to lose confidence in the system.

人类和人工智能可读的数据结构。Human- and AI-readable data structures.

设计制品应以人类和人工智能系统均可访问的形式表示The design artefact should be represented in a form that is accessible to both humans and AI systemsCao et al. (2025); Feng et al. (2026)。对于人类来说，模型应该是可视的、可检查的和可编辑的。对于人工智能来说，文物背后的数据需要可访问，揭示其内部数据结构和互连。这种共享的表示方式可以让人类和人工智能进行编辑。. For the human, the model should be visual, inspectable, and editable. For AI, the data that underlie the artefacts need to be accessible, revealing their internal data structures and interconnections. This shared representation enables editing for both humans and AI.

状态意识和交互历史。State awareness and interaction history.

共同创意的人工智能系统必须能够访问、检查和修改不断发展的设计模型的当前状态A co-creative AI system must enable access, inspection, and modification of the current state of an evolving design modelFeng et al. (2026)。随着人工制品的发展，必须重新评估约束。编辑可能会删除支撑、断开元素连接或更改结构载荷路径。因此，系统应保持对当前状态、先前编辑、持久约束和用户目标的了解，以有效协助安全关键工程组件的设计并允许追踪责任 过度执行的修改。. As the artefacts develop, constraints must be re-evaluated. An edit may remove a support, disconnect an element, or change the structural load path. The system should therefore maintain awareness of the current state, previous edits, persistent constraints, and user goals to effectively assist in the design of safety-critical engineering components and to allow tracing the responsibility over executed modifications.

设计意图的多模式表达。Multimodal expression of design intent.

结构和建筑意图通常用多种“语言”来表达Structural and architectural intent is often expressed in many “languages”Shi et al. (2026); Baudoux et al. (2025)。设计师通过草图、绘图、注释以及口头或文字描述进行交流。沟通也常常是不完整且含糊的。因此，人类-人工智能工具应该适应这些不同的“语言”及其内在的模糊性。. Designers communicate through sketches, drawings, annotations, and verbal or text descriptions. Communication is also often incomplete and ambiguous. Human–AI tools should therefore accommodate these distinct “languages” and their intrinsic ambiguity.

5 讨论：人工智能用于创意结构设计Discussion: AI for Creative Structural Design

5.1 创造力是旅程，而不是目的地Creativity as the journey, not the destination

人工智能在创意领域的核心价值可能不是产生最终答案，而是扩展和导航可能的替代方案。在本研究中，我们观察到所有参与者都在手动草图阶段或 3D 建模阶段修改了他们最初的设计理念。 P03 报道称，人工智能支持的流程有助于提出从底层到屋顶的圆形内部庭院的想法，这是以前从未考虑过的。 P01两次改变设计思路，成功满足了约束条件。 P02在草图阶段也改变了设计思路，抹掉了建筑的顶部开间，创造出更有趣的错落效果。这种互动表明创意领域的专业人士在开发原创设计时找到了新的解决方案。因此，充当共同创意助手的人工智能系统可以丰富设计之旅，并激发结构设计师新的创意见解。The central value of AI in creative domains may not be the production of a final answer, but the expansion and navigation of possible alternatives. In the present study, we observed that all participants have modified their original design idea either in the manual sketch phase or during the 3D modelling session. P03 reported that the AI-supported process helped develop the idea of a circular internal court from the ground floor to the roof, which had not been considered before. P01 changed their design idea twice, achieving a design that successfully met the constraints. P02 also changed their design idea in the sketching phase, erasing the top bays of the building to create a more interesting staggering effect. This interaction shows that professionals in creative domains find new solutions as they develop their original designs. An AI system that acts as a co-creative assistant can, therefore, enrich the design journey and spark new creative insights from structural designers.

5.2 生产性摩擦和非生产性摩擦Productive and Unproductive Friction

创造性工作中并非应消除所有摩擦。一些摩擦是富有成效的，因为它迫使设计师面对限制、比较替代方案并做出判断。然而，其他摩擦是没有成效的，包括重复的建模操作、界面开销以及意图和几何之间的低级转换。Not all friction should be removed from creative work. Some friction is productive since it forces designers to confront constraints, compare alternatives, and make judgements. Other friction, however, is unproductive, including repetitive modelling actions, interface overhead, and low-level translation between intent and geometry.

在我们的研究中，所有参与者都认为减少重复建模是人工智能可以发挥作用的一个领域。 P01强调AI快速创造了重复元素。 P02 指出，人工智能可以从网格中快速生成结构，并通过简单的提示删除不需要的隔间，从而避免了手动删除，否则需要更长时间。 P03同样强调了主要结构网格和楼板的快速生成。这表明当前的原型在执行显式、有界和重复的建模操作方面是有效的。In our study, all participants identified the reduction of repetitive modelling as an area where AI was helpful. P01 emphasised that AI quickly created repetitive elements. P02 noted that the AI made it possible to quickly generate a structure from a grid and remove unwanted bays through simple prompts, avoiding manual removal that would otherwise have taken longer. P03 similarly highlighted the rapid generation of the main structural grid and floor slabs. This indicates that the current prototype was effective in executing explicit, bounded, and repetitive modelling operations.

同样，界面也保持了生产性摩擦。通过与界面交互，P01 注意到两个设计缺陷，首先是倾斜柱的长度，然后是确定支撑件彼此距离太近。因此，P01 改变了设计，完全删除了这些元素，从而产生了一个更简单、优雅且仍然可行的解决方案。真正的共创系统应该致力于识别和消除无效摩擦，同时保留或突出支持设计判断的反射摩擦。Similarly, the interface has maintained the productive friction. By interacting with the interface, P01 noticed two design flaws, first regarding the length of the inclined columns and then identifying supports too close to each other. As a result, P01 has changed the design to remove these elements altogether, resulting in a simpler, elegant, and still feasible solution. True co-creation systems should move towards identifying and removing unproductive friction while preserving or highlighting reflective friction that will support design judgement.

5.3 多模态作为共同创意的锚点Multimodality as a Co-Creative Anchor

多模式输入（例如草图和绘图）可以指导探索，而无需过度指定。草图的模糊性让参与者比单独的自然语言更自然地表达空间意图和设计方向。在我们的研究中，所有参与者都表示他们使用草图作为开始新设计的主要模式之一。 P02 补充说，开始考虑 2D 项目，然后使用 CAD 软件转向 3D，这对 3D 和草图有助于充分理解问题。Multimodal input, such as sketches and drawings, can guide exploration without over-specifying it. The ambiguity of sketches lets participants express spatial intent and design directions more naturally than natural language alone. In our study, all participants indicated that they use sketches as one of the main modals to start a new design. P02 added that starts thinking about projects in 2D and then goes to 3D using CAD software and that the pair of 3D and sketches helps to fully understand the problem.

然而，虽然 P01 也回应说使用草图感觉很自然，但 P01 在会议期间并没有使用该功能。 P01 补充说，许多重复操作，例如在给定距离复制多个元素，可以使用自然语言更轻松地进行交流。另一方面，P02指出，有时很难选择与人工智能交流的词汇。 P02 指出，“很难在提示中将我的想法用语言表达出来，就像很难向没有绘图的人解释设计理念一样。”However, while P01 also responded that using sketches felt natural, P01 did not use the feature during the session. P01 added that many repetitive operations, such as replicating multiple elements at a given distance, can be more easily communicated using natural language. On the other hand, P02 noted that it was sometimes hard to choose a vocabulary to communicate with AI. P02 noted that “it was hard to put my ideas into words for the prompt, similarly to how it is hard to explain a design idea to someone without drawing.”

这些经验证实，多模态是共同创造系统的关键。有些任务在提示中更容易描述，而对于其他任务来说，一张草图可能抵得上一千个字。These experiences confirm that multimodality is key for a co-creative system. Some tasks are more easily described in prompts, while for others a sketch might as well be worth a thousand words.

5.4 自动化、协作还是共同创造？Automation, Collaboration, or Co-creation?

用户被问及是否会将其交互归类为自动化、协作或共同创造，以及原因。参与者的反映揭示了对该机构在人类-人工智能结构设计工作流程中的不同期望。 P01 将当前的交互归类为自动化，将共同创造描述为能够对可行性或有问题的设计决策发出早期警告的人工智能系统。 P02 将交互描述为协作，因为人类和 AI 都可以修改模型。对于 P02 来说，共同创造意味着人工智能系统可以“自动提出建议”，或者在设计偏离原始限制时突出显示。 P03 还将当前的经历描述为一种协作，并赞赏随着项目的进展，能够控制设计过程并独立地“找出新想法”。这些回应支持了这样的论点：结构设计中的共同创造并不一定意味着更大的人工智能自主权。相反，它需要仔细分配代理，该代理应适应用户的偏好，如中讨论的Users were asked whether they would categorise their interaction as automation, collaboration, or co-creation, and why. The reflection of the participants revealed different expectations about the agency in human–AI structural design workflows. P01 categorised the current interaction as automation, describing co-creation as an AI system capable of issuing early warnings about feasibility or problematic design decisions. P02 described the interaction as collaboration because both the human and AI can modify the model. For P02, co-creation means an AI system that would “suggest things automatically” or highlight when the design was drifting from the original constraints. P03 also described the current experience as a collaboration and appreciated being in control of the design process and “figuring out new ideas” independently as the project progressed. These responses support the argument that co-creation in structural design does not necessarily mean greater AI autonomy. Instead, it requires a careful distribution of agency which should adapt to the user’s preference as discussed in(Holter and El-Assady, 2024).

5.5 局限性Limitations

本文提出了一种立场和一项初步研究，而不是对结构有效性的详尽评估。参与者样本很小，即使它允许人们识别交互模式和设计维度。该接口正在不断开发中，因此目前并不支持所有与 CAD 软件相当的绘图功能。特别是，用户报告缺少点击拖动和捕捉功能，这些功能可以促进人类和人工智能代理的操作。此外，在本研究中，设计方向的变化主要是由设计师自己发起的，而不是人工智能。目前的研究也没有证明人工智能的单独使用会带来更多的创造性成果。相反，它确定了设计师和结构制品之间的交互模式，可以指导人工智能驱动的界面如何更有效地支持共同创造。未来的工作可以研究这个受人工智能自主权增强的过程，它可以主动建议行动或发出潜在的设计重定向信号。This paper presents a position and a preliminary study rather than an exhaustive evaluation of structural validity. The participant sample was small, even if it allowed one to identify interaction patterns and design dimensions. The interface is in continuous development, so not all drawing functions comparable to CAD software are currently supported. In particular, users have reported missing functions of click-and-drag and snap that could facilitate the action of human and AI agents. Furthermore, in the present study, changes in the design direction were initiated mainly by designers themselves rather than AI. The current study also does not establish that AI use alone caused more creative outcomes. Instead, it identifies interaction patterns between designers and structural artefacts that can guide how AI-powered interfaces might more effectively support co-creation. Future work could study this process subjected to increased AI autonomy, where it could proactively suggest actions or signal potential design redirections.

6 结论Conclusion

我们认为，用于结构设计以及其他创意学科的创意人工智能系统不应被设计为最终答案生成器，而应被设计为探索设计空间的互动伙伴。本文通过观察参与者与原型人工智能界面的交互，展示了设计意图和用户目标如何与人工制品一起发展。然后，最终的制品由设计问题的约束和结构设计的内在可行性约束形成。未来的人工智能辅助结构设计系统应该遵循这个过程，通过消除建模重复带来的非生产性摩擦来提供帮助，同时保留支持设计师实现最终结果的生产性摩擦。We argue that creative AI systems for structural design, as well as for other creative disciplines, should not be designed as final-answer generators, but rather as interactive partners for exploring the design space. Observing participants interact with a prototype AI interface, this paper shows how design intent and user objective evolve together with the artefact. The final artefact is then shaped by the constraints of the design problem and by the intrinsic feasibility constraints of structural design. Future AI-assisted structural design systems should then follow this process, assisting by removing unproductive friction coming from modelling repetition while preserving productive friction that support designers in achieving their final outcome.
