图结构评分标准：将评分标准编译为法学硕士法官的类型化评估图Graph-Structured Rubrics: Compiling Rubrics into Typed Evaluation Graphs for LLM Judges

摘要Abstract

基于评分标准的评估者通常将评分标准视为提示上下文或平面 标准：它们指定要判断的内容，但隐含标准组成， 即使自然语言规则如此规定。我们介绍Rubric-based evaluators commonly treat rubrics as prompt context or flat criteria: they specify what to judge but leave criterion composition implicit, even when natural-language rules state it. We introduce图结构量规Graph-Structured Rubrics（GSR），它将一个标题编译成一个 在观察响应之前，与响应无关的类型评估图。 标准节点引发判断；变换、还原和门控 操作员通过命名端口组合它们；和特定于任务的输出映射， 称为“读数”，将独特的接收器转换为分数或偏好。 编译会拒绝格式错误或类型不兼容的图表。逐点评估 在图聚合之前单独判断标题维度；成对的 评估重用图表，对每个候选人进行一次判断 标准。在 GPT-OSS-120B 下，GSR 通过以下方式提高了精确分数一致性 四点得分比普罗米修斯式得分高出 0.62–6.75 个百分点 数据集并在数值上实现了最高的端到端成对精度 本地联系和弃权政策下的两个偏好基准。(GSR), which compiles a rubric into a response-independent typed evaluation graph before observing responses. Criterion nodes elicit judgments; transformation, reduction, and gating operators compose them through named ports; and a task-specific output mapping, termed Readout, converts the unique sink into a score or preference. Compilation rejects malformed or type-incompatible graphs. Pointwise evaluation judges rubric dimensions separately before graph aggregation; pairwise evaluation reuses the graph with one judgment for each candidate under every criterion. Under GPT-OSS-120B, GSR improves exact score agreement by 0.62–6.75 percentage points over Prometheus-style scoring on four pointwise datasets and achieves the numerically highest end-to-end pairwise accuracy on two preference benchmarks under native tie and abstention policies.

简介Introduction

评分标准已成为跨直接法学硕士评估的通用界面 评分和成对比较，但许多系统仍然将它们视为提示 标准的上下文或平面集合。此类表示指定了要做什么 判断，而不是标准级别的判断应如何相互作用以产生分数 或偏好。基于法学硕士的法官使这种评估具有可扩展性并且可以实现 与人类对开放式任务的判断基本一致Rubrics have become a common interface for LLM evaluation across direct scoring and pairwise comparison, yet many systems still treat them as prompt context or flat collections of criteria. Such representations specify what to judge but not how criterion-level judgments should interact to produce a score or preference. LLM-based judges make such evaluation scalable and can achieve substantial agreement with human judgments on open-ended tasks(31; 14).

然而，随着评估的任务变得更加异构，高聚合 与人类评级的一致并不能保证程序的稳定性。事先 研究记录对候选顺序、输出长度、评估者的敏感性 命名的质量标准之间的熟悉、锚定甚至混淆As evaluated tasks become more heterogeneous, however, high aggregate agreement with human ratings does not guarantee procedural stability. Prior studies document sensitivity to candidate order, output length, evaluator familiarity, anchoring, and even confusion between named quality criteria(27; 5; 25; 10)。 量规通过以下方式解决了该问题的标准规范部分： 明确相关要求。因此，最近的基准和 评估者采用了人类制定的标准，生成了标准，或者 特定于实例的清单. Rubrics address the criterion-specification component of this problem by making relevant requirements explicit. Accordingly, recent benchmarks and evaluators have adopted human-authored criteria, generated rubrics, or instance-specific checklists(32; 19; 16; 3)。 由于基于标准的判断越来越多地为基准报告提供信息， 奖励模型数据构建和模型选择工作流程， 量规的组成不仅影响准确性，而且影响再现性和 可审计性。. Because rubric-based judgments increasingly inform benchmark reporting, reward-model data construction, and model-selection workflows, the way a rubric is composed affects not only accuracy but also reproducibility and auditability.

在做出标准级判断后，仍然存在一个重要的差距： 它们的构成仍然是隐含的和无法审计的。将规则写入 提示并不能消除这个问题，因为LLM必须执行它们 内部。将这些规则留在模型推理中会创造机会 为了省略适用性检查，减少或应用门 顺序或分数上限的执行不一致。此外，标题规则 通常是分层的：标准判断提供中间还原，其 输出馈送门、约束和最终读数。该层次结构定义了一个 执行政策而不是附加标准。为了明确这一政策， GSR将其表示为有向无环图并执行确定性 按拓扑顺序排列的运算符，公开了如图所示的决策路径 图An important gap remains after criterion-level judgments have been produced: their composition can still be implicit and unauditable. Writing rules in the prompt does not remove this problem because the LLM must execute them internally. Leaving these rules inside model inference creates opportunities for applicability checks to be omitted, reductions or gates to be applied out of order, or score caps to be enforced inconsistently. Moreover, rubric rules are often hierarchical: criterion judgments feed intermediate reductions, whose outputs feed gates, constraints, and final Readout. This hierarchy defines an execution policy rather than additional criteria.
