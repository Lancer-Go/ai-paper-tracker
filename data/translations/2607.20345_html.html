缩小实验室与商店之间的差距：适用于零售类人机器人的数据高效的培训后和体验驱动的学习 VLA 框架Closing the Lab-to-Store Gap: A Data-Efficient Post-Training and Experience-Driven Learning VLA Framework for Retail Humanoids

Roger Sala Sisó1,2,∗, Tiago Silvério 1,∗, Jakob Sand1, Tran Nguyen Le2
∗ Equal Contribution   1 HIVE Robots, Denmark   2 Department of Engineering Technology, Technical University of Denmark, Denmark
摘要Abstract

缩小基准性能和可靠的现实操作之间的差距仍然是视觉-语言-动作 (VLA) 人形机器人的核心挑战，它必须处理执行错误、分布变化和环境变化。本文提出了 DEED（数据高效后训练和经验驱动学习），这是一种使用 Unitree G1-Edu 人形机器人和 GR00T N1.6 基础模型对超市芯片补货任务进行评估的系统级方法。 DEED 包含三个关键组成部分：(1) 数据高效的训练后流程，具有控制频率对齐、数据管理、任务相关视觉突出显示和减少 VLA 依赖性； (2) 对经验驱动的细化的现实世界研究，通过基于文本的优势前缀和视觉语言价值函数改编自 RECAP； （3）用于研究分布内和分布外行为的潜在空间分析工具。我们的结果表明，弥合实验室与商店之间的差距主要是一个系统集成挑战，而不是架构挑战：仔细的数据设计和有针对性的后期训练可以将在天真的微调下失败的策略转变为仅使用单个 GPU 的有效的现实世界系统。Closing the gap between benchmark performance and reliable real-world operation remains a central challenge for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) humanoid robots, which must handle execution errors, distribution shifts, and environmental variability. This paper presents DEED (Data-Efficient Post-Training and Experience-Driven Learning), a systems-level approach evaluated on a supermarket chip-restocking task using a Unitree G1-Edu humanoid robot and the GR00T N1.6 foundation model. DEED comprises three key components: (1) a data-efficient post-training pipeline with control-frequency alignment, data curation, task-relevant visual highlighting, and reduced VLA dependence; (2) a real-world study of experience-driven refinement, adapted from RECAP via a text-based advantage prefix and a vision-language value function; and (3) a latent-space analysis tool for studying in- and out-of-distribution behavior. Our results suggest that bridging the lab-to-store gap is primarily a systems integration challenge rather than an architectural one: careful data design and targeted post-training can transform a policy that fails under naive fine-tuning into a competent real-world system using only a single GPU.

II 契约框架DEED Framework

Ⅱ-AII-A VLA 后训练的数据高效配方Data-Efficient recipe for VLA Post-Training

针对实际部署微调 VLA 不仅仅涉及收集演示和训练模型。本节介绍 DEED 的数据高效 (DE-) 训练后配方：我们发现在开箱即用地微调 VLA（此处为 GR00T N1.6）时必要的实际决策和技巧，我们相信这可以帮助处于此位置的任何人避免常见的陷阱。Finetuning a VLA for real-world deployment involves more than collecting demonstrations and training a model. This section presents the Data-Efficient (DE-) post-training recipe of DEED: the practical decisions and tricks we found necessary when finetuning a VLA (here GR00T N1.6) out of the box, which we believe help anyone in this position avoid common pitfalls.

数据管理规则Data Curation Rules

模仿学习是一种直接的观察到行动的映射，没有隐含的意图概念或观察的哪些方面与任务相关，因此每个演示都是以同等的权重学习的。因此，数据管理对于防止噪音和系统性不良模式影响学习至关重要。下面的每条规则都对应于我们在训练管道开发过程中遇到并缓解的故障模式。Imitation learning is a direct observation-to-action mapping with no implicit notion of intent or of which aspects of the observation are task-relevant, so every demonstration is learned with equal weight. Data curation is therefore crucial to prevent noise and systemic bad patterns from compromising learning. Each rule below corresponds to a failure mode we encountered and mitigated during the development of the training pipeline.

  1. 1.

    确保均衡的州覆盖率。Ensure balanced state coverage.不平衡的数据集会使政策偏向于主导国家的行动和动态，从而降低代表性不足国家的表现，即使包含在培训中也是如此。Unbalanced datasets bias the policy toward the actions and dynamics of dominant states, degrading performance in underrepresented ones even when included in training.

  2. 2.

    只记录高效、成功的行为。Record only efficient, successful behavior.避免犹豫、纠正、回溯或低效路径，并在失败变得明显时修剪事件，而不是将失败尝试的导致记录为有效行为Avoid hesitations, corrections, backtracking, or inefficient paths, and trim episodes at the point where failure becomes evident rather than recording the lead-up to a failed attempt as valid behavior[6].

  3. 3.

    保留附带回收。Retain incidental recoveries.纯粹以干净的成功为目标训练的策略没有学习到的行为，一旦预测错误复合并使其进入表现不佳的状态，就可以返回任务A policy trained purely on clean successes has no learned behavior for returning to the task once prediction errors compound and drift it into poorly represented states[1];我们只修剪情节中失败的部分并保留恢复，让策略学习在偏离正常执行时如何响应。; we trim only the failed segment of an episode and keep the recovery, letting the policy learn how to respond when it departs from normal execution.

  4. 4.

    最大限度地提高行动的一致性，而不仅仅是状态覆盖范围。Maximize action consistency, not just state coverage.如果专家的行动在相似的州之间不一致，则该政策缺乏稳定的目标，要么收敛到平均、无效的行动，要么学习非预期的观察行动映射If the expert’s actions vary inconsistently across similar states, the policy lacks a stable target and either converges to an averaged, ineffective action or learns an unintended observation-action mapping[1];优先考虑在重复状态下采取一致的行动，而不是每次覆盖多个状态一次。; prioritize consistent actions at repeated states over covering many states once each.

  5. 5.

    最大限度地减少不受控制的环境变化。Minimize uncontrolled environmental variation.当观察变化是由外部因素（物体移动、照明波动、其他因素）驱动时，记录的转变不再反映一致的行动结果关系When observation changes are driven by external factors (objects shifting, lighting fluctuations, other agents), recorded transitions no longer reflect a consistent action-outcome relationship[1];在收集过程中稳定身体设置，而不是期望训练能够过滤掉这种情况。; stabilize the physical setup during collection rather than expecting training to filter this out.

  6. 6.

    避免无操作状态。Avoid no-operation states.情节边界处的空闲段实际上将未更改的状态与不一致的操作配对（空闲期间无操作，然后一旦运动开始就进行真正的操作）并鼓励较少的动态行为Idle segments at episode boundaries pair effectively unchanged states with inconsistent actions (no-op during idling, then a real action once motion begins) and encourage less dynamic behavior[6];一旦有意义的动作开始，就开始剧集，并在目标实现后立即结束。; start episodes once meaningful motion begins and end them as soon as the goal is achieved.

突出显示与任务相关的视觉提示。Highlighting Task-Relevant Visual Cues.

视觉是政策感知世界的主要方式，因此提取与任务相关的视觉特征至关重要。这对于 VLA 来说尤其具有挑战性，因为 VLA 的视觉主干是在互联网规模上进行预训练的，而不是具体的、特定于任务的数据，这使得它们容易产生误导性的相关性，例如背景纹理、视点或与任务无关的对象Vision is the primary modality through which the policy perceives the world, making extraction of task-relevant visual features critical. This is especially challenging for VLAs, whose visual backbones are pretrained on internet-scale rather than embodied, task-specific data, leaving them prone to misleading correlations such as background texture, viewpoint, or task-irrelevant objects[10]。 我们集成了经过调整的 IA-VLA 框架. 减少 VLA 依赖性的实用技巧。Practical Tricks to Reduce VLA Dependence.

由于 VLA 仍处于现实世界准备就绪的早期阶段，我们建议使用两个实用技巧来增强其在人形机器人上的适用性：As VLAs remain at an early stage of real-world readiness, we suggest two practical tricks that enhance their applicability on humanoids:

  1. 1.

    二进制手持控制器。Binary Hand Controller.操纵需要对物理交互进行高频、细粒度的适应。当前的基础模型受低频推理和纯粹基于图像的感知的限制，在端到端控制方面尚未可靠地满足这一要求Manipulation demands high-frequency, fine-grained adaptation to physical interactions. Current foundation models, constrained by low-frequency inference and purely image-based perception, do not yet meet this requirement reliably in end-to-end control[12, 13]。对于主要减少到打开和关闭手的任务，我们建议通过二进制信号将手控制为抓手，而不是学习连续的、细粒度的关节控制。二元决策与视觉特征密切且可靠地相关，尤其是对于腕式摄像头，避免了固有离散动作的连续驱动。. For tasks reducing primarily to opening and closing the hand, we recommend controlling the hand as a gripper via a binary signal rather than learning continuous, fine-grained joint control. A binary decision correlates strongly and reliably with visual features, especially with wrist-mounted cameras, avoiding continuous actuation for an inherently discrete action.

  2. 2.

    通过巴特沃斯滤波进行动作平滑。Action Smoothing via Butterworth Filtering.连续动作块之间的过渡是抖动运动的常见来源。当模型过度拟合时，块转换会人为地变得平滑，但代价是动作头和视觉表示级别的泛化程度降低。由于训练数据由平滑的轨迹组成，急动会将执行的动作推离训练分布，从而产生不稳定或不可靠的执行。因此，我们在推理时对预测的动作序列应用巴特沃斯滤波器，这是一种简单而有效的解决方案，可以保留策略的泛化性，同时保持执行的动作平滑和分布。Transitions between consecutive action chunks are a common source of jerky movement. When the model overfits, chunk transitions become artificially smooth, but at the cost of reduced generalization at both the action head and visual representation level. Since the training data consists of smooth trajectories, jerkiness pushes executed actions out of the training distribution, producing erratic or unreliable execution. We therefore apply a Butterworth filter to the predicted action sequence at inference time, a simple and effective solution that preserves the policy’s generalization while keeping executed actions smooth and in-distribution.

多子任务演示的连续片段Continuous Episodes for Multi-Subtask Demonstrations

对于多子任务任务，子任务转换是一个常见的失败点，因为分段演示隐式地训练模型以期望情节边界作为静态、重复动作的点（GR00T 通过重复最后一个情节内动作来填充目标块）For multi-subtask tasks, subtask transitions are a common failure point, since segmented demonstrations implicitly train the model to expect episode boundaries as points of static, repeated actions (GR00T pads target chunks by repeating the last in-episode action)[11]。我们建议记录跨越完整子任务转换的连续事件，而不是使用重叠边界分割长范围演示的常见策略，这也简化了数据收集。我们进一步建议避免在子任务语言提示中使用共享措辞，因为弱语言信号可以让策略继续前一个子任务，或者尽管提示没有变化，但以降低的质量执行下一个子任务。. Rather than the common strategy of segmenting long-horizon demonstrations with overlapping boundaries, we recommend recording continuous episodes that span full subtask transitions, which also simplifies data collection. We further advise avoiding shared wording across subtask language prompts, as a weak language signal can let the policy continue a previous subtask or execute the next one with degraded quality despite an unchanged prompt.

Ⅱ-BII-B 经验驱动的学习策略Experience-Driven Learning Strategy

DEED 的第二个组成部分是培训流程的体验驱动 (-ED) 阶段。它解决了 VLA 的一个基本限制：通过远程操作演示的监督微调获得的策略只能与所显示的数据一样好，并且没有机制可以根据其自身的部署经验进行改进。为了弥补这一差距，我们采用了 RECAP（通过优势条件策略进行经验和修正的 RL）配方，最初是在The second component of DEED is the Experience-Driven (-ED) stage of the training pipeline. It addresses a fundamental limitation of VLAs: a policy obtained through supervised fine-tuning on teleoperated demonstrations can only be as good as the data it was shown and has no mechanism to improve from its own deployment experience. To close this gap, we adapt the RECAP (RL with Experience and Corrections via Advantage-conditioned Policies) recipe, originally introduced withπ0.6∗\pi^{*}_{0.6}，到整个 DEED 中使用的解耦 GR00T 架构, to the decoupled GR00T architecture used throughout DEED[8, 2].

使 RECAP 适应 GR00T 提出了架构挑战：与提出 RECAP 的紧密耦合 VLA 不同，GR00T 将感知和推理（视觉语言骨干）与控制（流程匹配动作头）分开。我们通过基于文本的指令前缀（“Advantage=True/False”）调节策略并将价值函数限制为相机观察和文本指令来解决这个问题。因此，细化过程不需要对预训练模型进行架构修改，并且应该适用于其他 VLA 架构。Adapting RECAP to GR00T poses an architectural challenge: unlike the tightly coupled VLA for which RECAP was proposed, GR00T separates perception and reasoning (a Vision-Language backbone) from control (a Flow-Matching action head). We address this by conditioning the policy through a text-based instruction prefix (“Advantage=True/False”) and restricting the value function to camera observations and text instructions. The refinement procedure thus requires no architectural modification to the pretrained model and should be applicable to other VLA architectures.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 讨论Discussion

DEED 的两个阶段发挥着互补作用：数据高效阶段通过单个 GPU 上的少量演示将不可用的检查点转变为有效策略，而体验驱动阶段则根据部署经验对其进行改进，一次 RECAP 迭代会产生可测量的收益（即使不是统计上决定性的收益）。The two stages of DEED play complementary roles: the Data-Efficient stage turns an unusable checkpoint into a functioning policy from few demonstrations on a single GPU, while the Experience-Driven stage refines it from deployment experience, with one RECAP iteration yielding measurable if not statistically conclusive gains.

与固定姿态和场景几何的基准不同，我们的评估涉及机器人初始化、对象放置和物理交互中不可避免的变化，因此成功率反映了端到端部署的鲁棒性，而不是受控假设下的性能。在这种情况下，经验驱动的细化带来的收益不会在迭代中复合：第二次迭代的退化最好解释为分布漂移，因为自生成的推出开始主导训练集，提供最广泛覆盖的成功行为的远程操作演示失去了相对影响力，而优势重新标记变得越来越与策略自身的采样分布联系在一起，从而创建了一个反馈循环，逐渐缩小了有效的状态-行动分布。我们的分布内工具支持这一点，表明即使在组合数据集覆盖的情况下，细化的策略也偏离了演示的流形。Unlike benchmarks with fixed poses and scene geometry, our evaluation involves unavoidable variability in robot initialization, object placement, and physical interaction, so success rates reflect end-to-end deployment robustness rather than performance under controlled assumptions. Within this context, the gains from experience-driven refinement do not compound across iterations: the second-iteration degradation is best explained as distributional drift, as self-generated rollouts come to dominate the training set, teleoperation demonstrations providing the broadest coverage of successful behavior lose relative influence, and advantage relabeling becomes increasingly tied to the policy’s own sampling distribution, creating a feedback loop that progressively narrows the effective state-action distribution. Our in-distribution tool supports this, showing that the refined policies drift away from the demonstrated manifold even while remaining covered by the combined dataset.

这种退化是如何发生的表明经验驱动的改进在哪里会得到回报。该流程基于优势加权离线学习背后的假设：在一致的几何条件下，相似的状态在各个事件中重复出现，因此优势估计基于重复观察而不是一次性配置。这最适用于观点变化有限的静态任务，其中策略反复遇到近乎相同的状态，并且细化可以将分布集中在成功的行为周围，从而产生稳定的复利收益。然而，在我们的重新放养任务中，平衡、腰部旋转和改变脚的位置使得名义上相同的配置产生显着不同的观察结果，因此状态重复的一致性较差，优势估计变得更加嘈杂。更广泛地说，随着任务需要更多的泛化，细化的好处应该会减少：变化越大，自我生成的部署就越难覆盖它，并且细化更容易缩小到行为的子集。实际意义是，随着任务可变性的增加，维持遥操作和推出数据的固定混合，或定期重新引入演示，可能是维持收益所必需的。How this degradation arises suggests where Experience-Driven refinement pays off. The pipeline rests on the assumption behind advantage-weighted offline learning: that similar states recur across episodes under consistent geometry, so advantage estimates are grounded in repeated observations rather than one-off configurations. This holds best in more static tasks with limited viewpoint variation, where the policy repeatedly encounters near-identical states and refinement can concentrate the distribution around successful behavior, yielding stable, compounding gains. In our restocking task, however, balancing, waist rotation, and changing foot placement make nominally identical configurations produce substantially different observations, so states recur less consistently and advantage estimation becomes noisier. More broadly, the benefit of refinement should diminish as a task demands more generalization: the greater the variation, the harder it is for self-generated rollouts to cover it, and the more readily refinement narrows onto a subset of behaviors. A practical implication is that maintaining a fixed mixture of teleoperation and rollout data, or periodically reintroducing demonstrations, is likely necessary to sustain gains as task variability grows.

第二个结构性发现涉及重置行为：精细化的策略往往会在单次放置后停止，而不是返回到其开始配置，因为最终完成状态比重置的中间姿势具有更高的价值，因此重置转换获得较低或负面的优势，并在训练期间受到抑制。在实践中，我们命令机器人回到起始姿势，而不是依赖策略；在学习框架内解决这个问题需要改变情节或奖励定义，例如通过将子任务纳入价值函数或明确分配积极优势来重置轨迹。A second, structural finding concerns reset behavior: refined policies tend to stop after a single placement rather than return to their start configuration, since the terminal completion state carries higher value than the intermediate poses of a reset, so reset transitions receive low or negative advantage and are suppressed during training. In practice we command the robot back to its starting pose rather than rely on the policy; addressing this within the learning framework would require changing the episode or reward definition, for instance by incorporating subtasks into the value function or explicitly assigning positive advantage to reset trajectories.

最后，分发内工具说明了将监控视为框架的一流部分的价值。因为它衡量的是策略自身潜在表示的变化，并且与特定的 VLA 无关，所以它在两个阶段和模型版本之间都保持不变。在这里，它主要用作诊断，将观察到的性能下降转化为具体的、可测试的解释，但原则上可以在线使用相同的信号来检测部署的策略何时离开其训练分布，并触发人为干预或数据收集，从而关闭 DEED 构建的循环。Finally, the in-distribution tool illustrates the value of treating monitoring as a first-class part of the framework. Because it measures shift in the policy’s own latent representation and is agnostic to the specific VLA, it applies unchanged across both stages and across model versions. Here it served primarily as a diagnostic that turned an observed performance drop into a concrete, testable explanation, but the same signal could in principle be used online to detect when a deployed policy is leaving its training distribution and to trigger human intervention or data collection, closing the loop that DEED is built around.

IV 结论Conclusions

我们提出了 DEED，这是一种用于在真实零售环境中部署基于 VLA 的人形机器人的系统级框架，并在使用 Unitree G1-Edu 和 GR00T N1.6 的超市芯片补货任务中进行了演示。我们的主要发现是，弥合预训练 VLA 和可靠部署之间的差距主要是一项系统集成挑战，而不是架构挑战：在不修改底层模型的情况下，仔细的数据管理和有针对性的后训练将非功能性检查点转变为单个 GPU 上的有效策略，数据质量比数据量更重要。We presented DEED, a systems-level framework for deploying VLA-based humanoids in real retail settings, demonstrated on a supermarket chip-restocking task with a Unitree G1-Edu and GR00T N1.6. Our main finding is that bridging the gap between pretrained VLAs and reliable deployment is primarily a systems integration challenge rather than an architectural one: without modifying the underlying model, careful data curation and targeted post-training turned a non-functional checkpoint into a capable policy on a single GPU, with data quality mattering more than data volume.

这两个阶段起着互补的作用。数据高效阶段完成了大部分工作（0% 到 32% 的成功率），而单个体验驱动迭代将成功率提高到 42%。收益并没有复合：第二次迭代降低了鲁棒性，可能是由于自生成的首次展示开始主导数据时的分布漂移，这强调了在持续学习期间保持演示和首次展示的平衡混合的需要，特别是对于高可变性人形任务。我们的分布内分析提供了一个与模型无关的信号来诊断这种退化，并且可以扩展到触发干预或在线数据收集。The two stages play complementary roles. The Data-Efficient stage does the bulk of the work (0% to 32% success), while a single Experience-Driven iteration raises success to 42%. Gains did not compound: a second iteration reduced robustness, likely from distributional drift as self-generated rollouts came to dominate the data, underscoring the need to maintain a balanced mixture of demonstrations and rollouts during continual learning, especially for high-variability humanoid tasks. Our in-distribution analysis provides a model-agnostic signal for diagnosing such degradation and could be extended to trigger intervention or data collection online.

这些结论受到单一任务、一个平台、二元成功指标、少量评估事件和两次细化迭代的限制。未来的工作应该在更多任务和平台上评估 DEED，制定持续学习策略以保持演示-推出平衡，并将部署监控与自动适应结合起来。These conclusions are limited by a single task, one platform, a binary success metric, few evaluation episodes, and two refinement iterations. Future work should evaluate DEED across more tasks and platforms, develop continual-learning strategies that preserve the demonstration-rollout balance, and couple deployment monitoring with automatic adaptation.
