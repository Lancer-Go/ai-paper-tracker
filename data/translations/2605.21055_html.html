Ondrej Galeta and Lukas Sekanina
摘要Abstract

最近的趋势是利用机器学习模型来改进进化设计和优化过程。我们提出了一种新颖的基于变压器的变异算子，用于笛卡尔遗传规划（CGP），用于近似算术电路的自动设计。我们引入了一种 CGP 的混合方案，其中所提出的变异算子与标准变异算子进行切换，以防止电路逼近过程的停滞。我们还为底层变压器开发了一种新的训练方案，该方案利用由数千个代表各种近似乘数的 CGP 染色体组成的训练向量。对于多个目标误差约束，利用基于变压器的突变通过 CGP 演化的近似乘法器比最先进的 EvoApproxLib 近似电路库中提供的高度优化设计实现了更好的权衡。尽管训练和进化过程的计算要求很高，但它们似乎是改进现有近似电路和产生新的、可能获得专利的电路设计的必要步骤。A recent trend is to leverage machine learning models to improve the evolutionary design and optimization process. We propose a novel transformer-based mutation operator for Cartesian genetic programming (CGP) for the automated design of approximate arithmetic circuits. We introduce a hybrid scheme for CGP in which the proposed mutation operator is switched with the standard mutation operator to prevent stagnation of the circuit approximation process. We also develop a new training scheme for the underlying transformer that utilizes training vectors composed of thousands of CGP chromosomes representing various approximate multipliers. For several target error constraints, the approximate multipliers evolved with CGP utilizing the transformer-based mutation achieve better trade-offs than the highly optimized designs available in the state-of-the-art EvoApproxLib library of approximate circuits. Although both training and evolutionary processes are computationally demanding, they appear to be necessary steps for improving existing approximate circuits and producing new, potentially patentable circuit designs.

Ⅲ-DIII-D 具有基于 Transformer 的突变的 CGPCGP with a Transformer-Based Mutation

为了使 Transformer 算子可以直接与经典的 CGP 变异进行比较，目标是仅在 CPU 上运行整个提出的 CGP 算法。为了减少模型推理带来的计算开销，仅当最佳适应度值发生变化时才重新计算突变分布。否则，它使用与上一代相同的分布。原因是突变仅应用于整个染色体上的一个节点元素。这意味着如果适应度相同，则输入染色体是相同的，或者它提供对适应度没有影响的中性突变。To make the transformer operator directly comparable with classical CGP mutation, the aim is to run the whole proposed CGP algorithm exclusively on the CPU. To reduce computational overhead caused by model inference, the mutation distribution is recomputed only when the best fitness value changes. Otherwise, it uses the same distribution as in the previous generation. The reason is that a mutation is applied only to one node element across the entire chromosome. This means that if the fitness is the same, the input chromosome is the same, or it provides a neutral mutation that has no effect on fitness.

另一个优化源于这样的想法：在实践中，我们的变压器模型 会产生无用的结果 由于数据集有限。有时，它的输出 可能会导致死胡同，误导性的突变，使得在改善健康方面很难取得进展。因此，我们提出了一种不依赖于 Transformer 模型本身并仍然使用标准 CGP 变异算子作为备份的混合方案，该方案在执行长序列生成而没有任何进展时部署。然后使用经典的 CGP 变异算子，直到适应度再次提高并且模型分布发生变化。这个概念源于这样一个事实：所提出的变压器模型旨在间接模拟适应度函数。然而，它无法完美地建模，这可以将搜索方法引导到搜索空间中不太有希望的区域。 切换到经典的 CGP 变异算子可以被理解为一种更容易逃离这些区域的方法。Another optimization stems from the idea that, in practice, our transformer model can produce useless results due to a limited dataset. Sometimes, its output can lead to a dead end, misleading mutations in a way that makes it very difficult to achieve progress in improving fitness. Hence, we propose a hybrid scheme that does not rely on the transformer model itself and still uses the standard CGP mutation operator as a backup, which is deployed when a long sequence of generations is carried out without any progress. The classical CGP mutation operator is then used until fitness improves again and the model distribution changes. This concept stems from the fact that the presented transformer model aims to indirectly model the fitness function. Switching to the classical CGP mutation operator can then be understood as a way to escape from these regions more easily.

VI 结论Conclusions

为了生成比 EvoApproxLib 中提供的高度优化乘数更好的属性的近似乘数，我们向 CGP 引入了基于转换器的突变。 Transformer 模型引导 CGP 到搜索空间中更有希望的区域，特别是当标准突变未能逃离局部最优时。当不考虑 Transformer 模型的训练时间时，CGP TR 比标准 CGP 更快地提供相同质量的近似乘数。除了以下情况外With the aim of producing approximate multipliers showing better properties than the highly optimized multipliers available in the EvoApproxLib, we introduced a transformer-based mutation to CGP. The transformer model guided CGP to more promising areas of the search space, especially when the standard mutation failed to escape from local optima. When the training time of the transformer model is not considered, approximate multipliers of the same quality are delivered faster by CGP TR than the standard CGP. Aside from the case whereϵ=1%\epsilon=1\%，我们证实所提出的方法可以生成具有改进特性的近似乘法器。, we confirmed that the proposed method can generate approximate multipliers with improved characteristics.

作为使用基于 Transformer 的变异算子增强 CGP 的初步研究，这项工作为未来的研究提出了几个方向。在本研究中，针对每个目标 WCE 级别训练了一个单独的 Transformer 模型，并且实验仅限于 8 位无符号近似乘法器。一个重要的悬而未决的问题是如何设计和训练基于变压器的变异算子，该算子可以泛化到广泛的 WCE 目标、不同的位宽度以及可能不同类别的算术电路。拥有这样一个用于电路近似目的的通用变压器模型将显着提高设计效率。As an initial study on enhancing CGP with a transformer-based mutation operator, this work raises several directions for future research. In the present study, a separate transformer model was trained for each target WCE level, and experiments were limited to 8-bit unsigned approximate multipliers. An important open question is how to design and train a transformer-based mutation operator that generalizes across a broad range of WCE targets, varying bit widths, and potentially different classes of arithmetic circuits. Having such a universal transformer model for circuit approximation purposes would significantly improve design productivity.

这一挑战并非易事，因为 CGP 表现出高度特异性的基因型-表型-适应度关系，很难用统计学习模型捕获。另一个悬而未决的问题涉及为变压器构建合适的训练数据集。对于近似电路，CGP 可用于生成具有所需属性的广泛数据集；然而，目前尚不清楚这些数据集必须具备哪些特征才能有效支持学习。我们未来的工作将致力于解决这些问题。This challenge is non-trivial because CGP exhibits highly specific genotype–phenotype–fitness relationships that are difficult to capture with statistical learning models. Another open issue concerns the construction of suitable training datasets for the transformer. For approximate circuits, CGP can be used to generate extensive datasets with desirable properties; however, it remains unclear which characteristics such datasets must possess to effectively support learning. Our future work will aim to address these questions.
