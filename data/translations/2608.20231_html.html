摘要Abstract

对完全自动化的标准反对意见吸引了需求方：如果人类一无所获，谁来购买产出？本文认为，这种反对意见将会计角色与生物物种混淆了。我们对后通用人工智能经济进行了建模，其中企业拥有人工智能和机器人代理群体，它们同时充当能源、计算、维护和升级的生产者和消费者，并且企业之间相互交易这些流量。以下是三个结果。 (一)Standard objections to full automation appeal to the demand side: if humans earn nothing, who buys the output? This paper argues that the objection confuses an accounting role with a biological species. We model a post-AGI economy in which corporations own populations of AI and robotic agents that serve simultaneously as producers and as consumers of energy, compute, maintenance, and upgrades, and in which firms trade these flows among themselves. Three results follow. 1 简介Introduction

有记录的历史中的每一个经济体都有相同的物理基础：人体生产，人体消费。这种认同是如此完整，以至于经济学很少将其表述为一种假设。消费者是人；最终需求是家庭想要的； GDP 归根到底是为了我们。即使是关于变革性人工智能的文献，现在也非常重视取代人类Every economy in recorded history has had the same physical substrate: human bodies producing, human bodies consuming. The identification is so complete that economics rarely states it as an assumption. Consumers are people; final demand is what households want; GDP is, in the last instance, for us. 7 结论Conclusion

这篇论文的论点可以用三句话来阐述。消费者是一个会计角色，而企业拥有的机器可以填补这个角色，因此，企业相互交易的经济在没有我们的情况下就会结束——以技术所允许的最大增长率。增长缓慢的原因是经济的核心资本品（通用代理）必须被培育而不是制造； AGI 结束了这一点，将约束约束从人口统计转移到制造和能源，并将可行的增长提高了几个数量级。在由此产生的轨迹中，人类的利害关系只剩下一个数字，即所有权份额The paper’s thesis can be stated in three sentences. The consumer is an accounting role, and machines owned by corporations can fill it, so an economy of firms trading with one another closes without us—at the maximal growth rate the technology admits. The reason growth was ever slow is that the economy’s central capital good, the general-purpose agent, had to be reared rather than manufactured; AGI ends that, moving the binding constraint from demography to fabrication and energy and raising feasible growth by orders of magnitude. What remains of humanity’s stake in the resulting trajectory is a single number, the ownership shareεt\varepsilon_{t}，其在普通企业行为下的默认动力趋于零——因此最后一个经济政策问题，也是唯一仍然重要的问题，是谁拥有这些机器。, whose default dynamics under ordinary corporate behavior run toward zero—so that the last economic-policy question, and the only one that will still matter, is who owns the machines.

凯恩斯通过想象人类摆脱经济照顾来结束他关于我们的子孙的可能性的文章Keynes closed his essay on our grandchildren’s possibilities by imagining mankind freed from economic care(Keynes 1930)。自主经济在提供富足的同时也消解了他的主题：除非人类将自己写入资本表，否则经济不再关心人类。这并不是对厄运的预言；而是对厄运的预言。制度(i)与制度(ii)一样可行。这是一种关于选择现在存在于何处的主张——不是在劳动力市场，也不是在商品市场，而是在无聊的、决定性的谁拥有什么的登记册中。. The autonomous economy delivers his abundance while dissolving his subject: the economy no longer cares about mankind unless mankind writes itself into the cap table. That is not a prediction of doom; regime (i) is as feasible as regime (ii). It is a claim about where the choice now lives—not in the labor market, not in the market for goods, but in the boring, decisive registry of who owns what.
