一种模型，两个市场：出价感知生成推荐One Model, Two Markets: Bid-Aware Generative Recommendation

Yanchen Jiang Harvard University Work done during an internship at Google Research Zhe Feng \thepa Christopher P. Mah \thepa Aranyak Mehta \thepa Di Wang \thepa
摘要Abstract

使用语义 ID 的生成推荐系统，例如 TIGERGenerative Recommender Systems using semantic ids, such as TIGER[Rajput et al., 2023]，已成为顺序推荐中广泛采用的竞争范式。然而，现有的架构只是为语义检索而设计的，并没有解决诸如通过广告收入货币化和商业检索竞价合并等问题。我们建议, have emerged as a widely adopted competitive paradigm in sequential recommendation. However, existing architectures are designed solely for semantic retrieval and do not address concerns such as monetization via ad revenue and incorporation of bids for commercial retrieval. We proposeGEM-Rec，一个统一的框架，将商业相关性和货币化目标直接整合到生成序列中。我们引入控制令牌来将是否显示广告与显示哪个项目的决定分离。这使得模型能够直接从交互日志中学习有效的投放模式，这本质上反映了过去成功的广告投放。作为补充，我们设计了一种处理实时定价的出价感知解码机制，将出价直接注入推理过程，以引导一代人购买高价值的商品。我们证明这种方法保证了分配的单调性，确保较高的出价会微弱地增加广告展示的可能性，而无需模型重新训练。实验证明, a unified framework that integrates commercial relevance and monetization objectives directly into the generative sequence. We introduce control tokens to decouple the decision of whether to show an ad from which item to show. This allows the model to learn valid placement patterns directly from interaction logs, which inherently reflect past successful ad placements. Complementing this, we devise a Bid-Aware Decoding mechanism that handles real-time pricing, injecting bids directly into the inference process to steer the generation toward high-value items. We prove that this approach guarantees allocation monotonicity, ensuring that higher bids weakly increase an ad’s likelihood of being shown without requiring model retraining. Experiments demonstrate thatGEM-Rec允许平台动态优化语义相关性和平台收入。allows platforms to dynamically optimize for semantic relevance and platform revenue.

1 简介Introduction

推荐系统正在经历从区分性排名到生成信息检索的范式转变。通过将项目表示为分层语义 ID，TIGER 等模型Recommender Systems are undergoing a paradigm shift from discriminative ranking to Generative Information Retrieval. By representing items as hierarchical Semantic IDs, models like TIGER[Rajput et al., 2023]将推荐视为序列生成任务。这种方法通过学习预测用户轨迹的深层语义结构，取得了最先进的结果treat recommendation as a sequence generation task. This approach has achieved state-of-the-art results by learning to predict the deep semantic structure of user trajectories[Singh et al., 2024, Ju et al., 2025, Yang et al., 2024].

然而，将生成模型应用于工业平台提出了一个挑战：货币化。现实世界的系统必须动态地共同优化有机语义相关性和赞助商业相关性，从而推动用户和平台的实用性。标准生成模型无法解决这个问题，因为它们纯粹针对语义可能性进行优化。他们将每个项目视为有机预测目标，忽略了管理赞助内容的经济限制和拍卖动态。However, applying generative models to industrial platforms presents a challenge: Monetization. Real-world systems must dynamically co-optimize for organic semantic relevance and sponsored commercial relevance, driving utility for both the user and the platform. Standard generative models fail to address this because they optimize purely for semantic likelihood. They treat every item as an organic prediction target, ignoring the economic constraints and auction dynamics that govern sponsored content.

一个主要困难是有机项目和赞助项目在不同的目标下运作。有机项目是根据估计的用户对没有价格信号的语料库的偏好来选择的。赞助项目的选择基于混合目标：它们必须总体相关，但也必须与用户商业意图特别相关，包括出价和潜在的广告拍卖收入。简单地在混合数据流上训练模型会混淆这些信号。此外，拍卖出价实时波动。这些动态不仅是经济限制，而且是信息信号：广告商经常动态调整出价，以反映他们对商品库存、质量和与用户相关性的信心。仅根据历史日志训练的模型会忽略这种实时反馈，锁定过去的估值并阻止系统在不重新训练的情况下适应新的价格机会。A major difficulty is that organic items and sponsored items operate under different objectives. Organic items are selected based on estimated user preference over a corpus without price signals. Sponsored items are selected based on a mixed objective: they must be generally relevant, but also particularly relevant to user commercial intent, incorporating bids and potential ads auction revenue. Simply training a model on a mixed stream of data confuses these signals. Furthermore, auction bids fluctuate in real-time. These dynamics are not merely economic constraints but informative signals: advertisers often adjust bids dynamically to reflect their confidence in an item’s inventory, quality and relevance to the user. A model trained solely on historical logs ignores this live feedback, locking in past valuations and preventing the system from adapting to new price opportunities without retraining.

我们建议We proposeGEM-Rec，一个将货币化集成到生成推荐中的框架。我们使用显式控制标记来扩充语义词汇。这区分了是否显示广告和显示哪个项目的决定。通过对成功的交互日志进行训练，该模型学习了一种“可行性”策略——识别广告在历史上既具有语义相关性又具有经济可行性的上下文。, a framework that integrates monetization into generative recommendation. We augment the semantic vocabulary with explicit control tokens. This distinguishes the decision of whether to show an ad from which item to show. By training on successful interaction logs, the model learns a “feasibility” policy—identifying contexts where ads were historically both semantically relevant and economically viable.

然而，从历史中学习与最大化当前效用不同。历史日志反映了用户接受度的基线，但它们没有编码当前机会的明确经济价值。为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了一种出价感知解码机制，该机制将拍卖出价直接注入推理过程。这使得平台能够实时动态地引导一代人购买高价值物品，并微调收入和相关性之间的权衡，而无需重新训练模型。However, learning from history is distinct from maximizing current utility. Historical logs reflect a baseline of user acceptance, but they do not encode the explicit economic value of current opportunities. To bridge this gap, we introduce a Bid-Aware Decoding mechanism that injects auction bids directly into the inference process. This allows the platform to dynamically steer the generation toward high-value items in real time and fine-tune the trade-off between revenue and relevance without retraining the model.

我们的贡献如下：Our contributions are as follows:

  1. 1.

    统一生成架构：Unified Generation Architecture:我们提出了一种新颖的序列公式，将显式控制标记集成到语义 ID 词汇表中。这将内容检索（生成项目 ID）中的槽分配决策（预测显示模式）分解为因素，从而使单个统一模型能够同时满足自然请求和赞助请求。We propose a novel sequence formulation that integrates explicit control tokens into the Semantic ID vocabulary. This factorizes the decision of slot allocation (predicting the display mode) from content retrieval (generating the item ID), enabling a single unified model to serve both organic and sponsored requests.

  2. 2.

    市场政策的端到端学习：End-to-End Learning of Marketplace Policy:我们表明，生成模型隐式地学习了控制有效广告曝光的潜在上下文依赖关系。通过对成功的交互日志进行训练，该模型学会将显示广告的决定视为用户轨迹的一个组成部分。We show that the generative model implicitly learns the latent context dependencies that govern valid ad exposures. By training on successful interaction logs, the model learns to treat the decision to display an ad as an integral part of the user’s trajectory.

  3. 3.

    出价感知约束解码：Bid-Aware Constrained Decoding:我们推导出一种推理时间机制，将出价直接注入解码搜索空间。我们提供理论保证，这种方法满足分配单调性（出价越高，曝光率就越弱）和有机完整性（有机推荐的排名顺序保持不变），为从业者提供了对系统运行点的精确控制。We derive an inference-time mechanism to inject bids directly into the decoding search space. We provide theoretical guarantees that this approach satisfies Allocative Monotonicity (higher bids weakly increase exposure) and Organic Integrity (ranking order for an organic recommendation remains invariant), providing practitioners with precise control over the system’s operating point.

4.2 定价机制Pricing Mechanism

对于我们的实验评估，我们采用第一价格支付规则，获胜的广告商支付其出价。这与数字广告生态系统最近的结构性转变相一致，主要平台已过渡到最高价拍卖，以降低复杂性并提高透明度For our experimental evaluation, we adopt the First-Price payment rule, where the winning advertiser pays their bid. This aligns with the recent structural shift in the digital advertising ecosystem, where major platforms have transitioned to first-price auctions to reduce complexity and improve transparency[Google Ad Manager, 2019, Google AdSense, 2021, OpenX, 2017, PubMatic, 2018, Microsoft Xandr, 2025, Amazon Publisher Services, 2023, Magnite, 2021, Sivan et al., 2020]。因此，我们在结果中将“收入”报告为所有生成的广告位的中标出价的累积总和。. Accordingly, we report “Revenue" in our results as the cumulative sum of the winning bids for all generated ad slots.

虽然一价拍卖在实践中越来越普遍，并且允许系统直接根据可观察的出价进行操作，但理想情况下，我们还希望保证主导策略激励兼容性（DSIC），以确保这些出价真实地反映潜在的广告商价值。 在我们的设置中，从第一价格转向真实（例如，关键出价/VCG 式）支付在概念上是自然的，但在技术上却很重要，因为它需要解码随机性、诱导分配规则的单调性和反事实评估之间的仔细耦合交互。附录While First-Price auctions are increasingly common in practice and allow the system to operate directly on observable bids, ideally, we would also want to guarantee dominant-strategy incentive compatibility (DSIC) to ensure these bids truthfully reflect underlying advertiser values. In our setting, moving from first-price to truthful (e.g., critical-bid/VCG-style) payments is conceptually natural but technically nontrivial because it requires a carefully coupled interaction between decoding randomness, monotonicity of the induced allocation rule, and counterfactual evaluation. AppendixE讨论这些权衡并概述不需要架构更改的可能方向；我们将完整的 DSIC 实施留给未来的工作。discusses these tradeoffs and outlines possible directions that do not require architectural changes; we leave a full DSIC implementation to future work.
