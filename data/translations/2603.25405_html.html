长视机器人桌面游戏中保持内部状态一致性的系统设计System Design for Maintaining Internal State Consistency in Long-Horizon Robotic Tabletop Games

摘要

长视野桌面游戏对机器人技术提出了独特的系统挑战：小的感知或执行错误可能会使累积的任务状态无效，在决策模块中传播，并最终破坏交互。本文研究如何通过深思熟虑的系统设计而不是孤立的组件改进来维持回合制多人机器人桌面游戏的内部状态一致性。使用麻将作为代表性的长视野设置，我们提出了一个集成架构，该架构显式地维护感知、执行和交互状态，将高级语义推理与时间关键的感知和控制分开，并将经过验证的动作原语与触觉触发的恢复相结合，以防止过早的状态损坏。我们进一步引入交互级监控机制来检测威胁执行假设的轮次违规和隐藏信息泄露。除了演示完整的游戏操作之外，我们还提供了故障模式、恢复有效性、跨模块错误传播以及部署期间观察到的硬件算法权衡的经验特征。我们的结果表明，显式分区、受监控的状态转换和恢复机制对于在扩展游戏中维持可执行一致性至关重要，而整体或未经验证的管道会导致端到端可靠性明显下降。所提出的系统可作为研究长视野、回合制交互中的系统级设计原理的实证平台。Long-horizon tabletop games pose a distinct systems challenge for robotics: small perceptual or execution errors can invalidate accumulated task state, propagate across decision-making modules, and ultimately derail interaction. This paper studies how to maintain internal state consistency in turn-based, multi-human robotic tabletop games through deliberate system design rather than isolated component improvement. Using Mahjong as a representative long-horizon setting, we present an integrated architecture that explicitly maintains perceptual, execution, and interaction state, partitions high-level semantic reasoning from time-critical perception and control, and incorporates verified action primitives with tactile-triggered recovery to prevent premature state corruption. We further introduce interaction-level monitoring mechanisms to detect turn violations and hidden-information breaches that threaten execution assumptions. Beyond demonstrating complete-game operation, we provide an empirical characterization of failure modes, recovery effectiveness, cross-module error propagation, and hardware–algorithm trade-offs observed during deployment. Our results show that explicit partitioning, monitored state transitions, and recovery mechanisms are critical for sustaining executable consistency over extended play, whereas monolithic or unverified pipelines lead to measurable degradation in end-to-end reliability. The proposed system serves as an empirical platform for studying system-level design principles in long-horizon, turn-based interaction.

I 简介Introduction

桌面游戏为机器人系统提供了结构化但要求较高的设置Tabletop games provide a structured yet demanding setting for robotic systems[openchess, jenga, chinesepokerrobot]。它们需要对小物体的重复操纵、紧密的感知-行动循环、严格的轮流结构以及长期的持续操作，通常是在多个人类参与者在场的情况下. They require repeated manipulation of small objects, tight perception–action loops, strict turn-taking structure, and sustained operation over long horizons, often in the presence of multiple human participants[gamesmanros]。麻将是此类中特别具有挑战性的一个例子：游戏时间较长，物体排列密集，执行过程在感知、决策和操纵之间反复交替. Mahjong is a particularly challenging instance of this class: gameplay is prolonged, objects are densely arranged, and execution repeatedly alternates between perception, decision-making, and manipulation[mahjongrobot]。在这种设置中，小的感知或执行错误不是孤立的事件，而是可以跨组件传播，导致状态不一致，最终破坏游戏玩法. In such settings, small perception or execution errors are not isolated events but can propagate across components, leading to state inconsistency that ultimately derails gameplay[levine2012monitoring, bohne2023execution]。这使得错误恢复和状态维护成为系统级问题，而不是组件级问题。. This makes error recovery and state maintenance a system-level concern rather than a component-level one.

本文解决了一个系统级的研究问题：This paper addresses a system-level research question:哪些系统设计选择使机器人系统能够在与多人进行长视野、回合制桌面交互期间保持可执行状态的一致性和可恢复性？what system design choices enable a robotic system to maintain executable state consistency and recoverability during long-horizon, turn-based tabletop interactions with multiple humans?在物理桌面游戏中，成功的部署还需要不平凡的游戏能力：可靠地执行动作但与随机游戏没有区别的系统在实践中不太可能被接受。我们不是以最佳博弈论性能或详尽的规则执行为目标，而是专注于在现实物理和交互条件下持续、稳健的操作，将可执行状态一致性视为有意义（尽管不一定是最佳）游戏性能的先决条件。因此，回答这个问题需要将感知、决策、操纵、恢复和交互集成到一个闭环系统中，该系统能够在没有持续人类监督的情况下容忍错误。图In physical tabletop games, successful deployment further requires non-trivial game competence: a system that executes actions reliably but behaves indistinguishably from random play is unlikely to be acceptable in practice. Rather than aiming for optimal game-theoretic performance or exhaustive rule enforcement, we focus on sustained, robust operation under realistic physical and interactional conditions, treating executable state consistency as a prerequisite for meaningful, though not necessarily optimal, game performance. Answering this question therefore requires integrating perception, decision-making, manipulation, recovery, and interaction into a single closed-loop system capable of tolerating errors without constant human supervision. 早期的系统原型揭示了现实世界桌面交互中常见的部署驱动的约束。人类玩家在进行游戏过程中经常需要澄清机器人的行动和决策，并且在游戏中期经常出现与规则相关的问题，从而扰乱回合结构和执行流程。为了减少歧义并避免长视野操作过程中的临时干预，系统提供了两个辅助接口：记录结构化决策轨迹以供游戏结束后进行事后检查，并在出现问题时支持游戏内规则查询。我们没有将这些部署驱动的观察视为以人为中心的建模或行为研究问题，而是将它们合并为部署的系统级要求。Early system prototyping revealed deployment-driven constraints that are common in real-world tabletop interactions. Human players often needed clarification of the robot’s actions and decisions during ongoing gameplay, and rule-related questions frequently arose mid-game, disrupting turn structure and execution flow. To reduce ambiguity and avoid ad-hoc interventions during long-horizon operation, the system provides two auxiliary interfaces: it records structured decision traces for post-hoc inspection after gameplay, and supports in-game rule queries when questions arise. Rather than treating these deployment-driven observations as human-centered modeling or behavioral research questions, we incorporate them as system-level requirements for deployment.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 机器人桌面和棋盘游戏系统Robotic tabletop and board-game systems

机器人桌面游戏构成了一个结构化的应用领域，其中感知、操作和交互必须在严格的轮流和重复的决策执行循环下集成Robotic tabletop games constitute a structured application domain in which perception, manipulation, and interaction must be integrated under strict turn-taking and repeated decision–execution cycles[chinesepokerrobot, openchess, jenga, mahjongrobot, gamesmanros]。现有系统展示了棋盘上的自主或半自主游戏. Prior systems demonstrate autonomous or semi-autonomous play in board[openchess, chessdevelopment]和卡and card[chinesepokerrobot]通过将状态感知与机器人控制相结合来模拟游戏，强调规则控制的桌面交互对物理执行和感知提出了不平凡的要求。其他系统强调与人类玩家的交互，平衡游戏能力与回合结构、节奏和社会可接受性，并且通常通过端到端自主或面向用户的措施而不是孤立的组件指标来评估性能games by combining state perception with robotic control, highlighting that rule-governed tabletop interactions place nontrivial demands on physical execution and sensing. Other systems emphasize interaction with human players, balancing game competence with turn structure, pacing, and social acceptability, and often evaluate performance through end-to-end autonomy or user-facing measures rather than isolated component metrics[zarkowski2019multi, robots-in-games]。以框架为导向的工作进一步探索了如何在多个桌面游戏中重用共同的感知、决策和驱动组件。. Framework-oriented efforts further explore how common perception, decision-making, and actuation components can be reused across multiple tabletop games.

虽然这些系统建立了物理游戏机器人的可行性，但它们通常强调短视距操作或在游戏过程中假设可靠的状态可观察性。相比之下，我们的工作使用麻将作为一种长时间的、基于回合的桌面设置，其中任何感知或执行错误都可能立即导致内部状态不一致。显式状态维护和可恢复性是维持跨多个回合和参与者的完整游戏操作的核心系统要求。While these systems establish the feasibility of physical game-playing robots, they typically emphasize short-horizon operation or assume reliable state observability during gameplay. In contrast, our work uses Mahjong as a prolonged, turn-based tabletop setting in which any perceptual or execution error can immediately induce internal state inconsistency. Explicit state maintenance and recoverability are central system requirements for sustaining complete-game operation across multiple turns and participants.

Ⅱ-BII-B 集成机器人系统中的感知、推理和控制的划分Partitioning perception, reasoning, and control in integrated robotic systems

集成机器人系统越来越依赖感知、推理和控制之间的显式划分，以平衡语义灵活性与反应式执行，特别是在支持基础模型的部署中Integrated robotic systems increasingly rely on explicit partitioning between perception, reasoning, and control to balance semantic flexibility with reactive execution, especially in foundation-model-enabled deployments[fmreview, llmrobotics]。 许多最新的系统将高级语言或视觉语言推理融入模块化感知和控制组件中，将语义决策转化为可执行的动作，同时保留对感官反馈的响应能力. Many recent systems ground high-level language or vision–language reasoning in modular perception and control components, translating semantic decisions into executable actions while preserving responsiveness to sensory feedback[plan-seq-learn, mon2025embodied, fmreview]。 这种架构分离还为违反系统假设时的执行监控、诊断和故障定位提供了显式接口. This architectural separation also provides explicit interfaces for execution monitoring, diagnosis, and localization of failures when system assumptions are violated[willibald2025hierarchical].

最近的方法研究了如何在技能或任务段的粒度上检测和纠正执行偏差，从而在不破坏下游决策逻辑的情况下实现结构化恢复Recent approaches study how execution deviations can be detected and corrected at the granularity of skills or task segments, enabling structured recovery without corrupting downstream decision logic[willibald2025hierarchical]。 然而，许多评估仍然强调本地恢复有效性，而在实际部署中，跨感知、决策和交互循环的未恢复或部分恢复错误的系统级后果仍未得到充分探索. However, many evaluations still emphasize local recovery effectiveness, while the system-level consequences of unrecovered or partially recovered errors across perception, decision-making, and interaction loops remain underexplored in real deployments[fmreview, mon2025embodied]。 在基于回合的交互设置中，即使单个操作在本地看来成功，这种跨模块传播也可能使下游决策无效，从而激发明确的系统级状态一致性考虑. In turn-based interaction settings, such cross-module propagation can invalidate downstream decisions even when individual actions appear locally successful, motivating explicit system-level state-consistency considerations[willibald2025hierarchical, mon2025embodied].

最近的系统越来越多地利用大型语言模型和视觉语言模型来扩展具体任务中的语义推理和泛化Recent systems increasingly leverage large language models and vision–language models to expand semantic reasoning and generalization in embodied tasks[geminirobotics, helix, scaling_helix]。 一些方法通过使用基础模型进行高层规划来保持模块化，同时将时间关键的感知和控制委托给专门的组件. Some approaches preserve modularity by using foundation models for high-level planning while delegating time-critical perception and control to specialized components[dexgraspvla, memer]，而其他人则追求从多模式观测直接到行动的更统一的映射, whereas others pursue more unified mappings from multimodal observations directly to actions[openvla, pi0, pi05]。 在实践中，这种分离对于时间关键的循环和精确接地变得特别重要：面向接地的中间表示（例如掩模）可以提高低级操作保真度，而端到端 VLA 模型则为了简单性而牺牲了模块化可诊断性. In practice, this separation becomes particularly important for time-critical loops and precise grounding: grounding-oriented intermediate representations (e.g., masks) can improve low-level manipulation fidelity, while end-to-end VLA models trade modular diagnosability for simplicity[gondola, roboground, openvla]。 我们的工作通过研究故意分区如何支持长期、回合制桌面交互中的可执行状态一致性和恢复来补充这些努力。具体来说，我们研究架构分离、受保护的状态转换和受监控的执行如何共同限制错误传播并在扩展的范围内保持内部状态的有效性。. Our work complements these efforts by examining how deliberate partitioning supports executable state consistency and recovery in long-horizon, turn-based tabletop interaction. Specifically, we study how architectural separation, guarded state transitions, and monitored execution jointly constrain error propagation and preserve internal state validity over extended horizons.

六一AVI-A 故障案例分析Failure Case Analysis

在所有评估的游戏中，观察到三个致命故障，导致游戏不可逆转地终止。其中一个案例是由语音识别错误引起的，错误地检测到玩家已宣布获胜，从而提前终止了游戏。其余两起案例均源于YOLO的视觉误判：其中一例误判了棋子，导致系统执行了非法的碰击；在另一起案件中，错误分类导致了非法隐藏的“孔”。这些失败表明，长期游戏玩法很容易受到跨模块错误传播的影响。在某些情况下，一个组件中的故障可能会级联到其他组件，从而影响下游操作的执行并降低内部状态的一致性。Across all evaluated games, three fatal failures were observed that led to irreversible game termination. The remaining two cases originated from visual misidentification by YOLO: in one instance, a tile was misdetected, leading the system to execute an illegal pung; in another, a misclassification resulted in an illegal concealed kong. These failures illustrate that long-horizon gameplay is vulnerable to cross-module error propagation. In certain cases, a fault in one component can cascade into others, influencing downstream action execution and degrading internal state consistency.

因此，抓取和融合执行过程中的触觉反馈在防止此类致命结果方面发挥着关键作用。当抓取验证或放置确认失败时，系统延迟内部状态更新并触发恢复。如果没有这个验证步骤，错误的抓握或意外的棋子移动将直接破坏手牌构图，导致后续回合中出现不可逆转的不一致。As a result, tactile feedback during grasping and meld execution plays a critical role in preventing such fatal outcomes. When grasp verification or placement confirmation fails, the system delays internal state updates and triggers recovery. Without this verification step, erroneous grasps or unintended tile movements would directly corrupt hand composition, leading to irreversible inconsistencies across subsequent turns.
