EEG-MFTNet：具有多尺度时间卷积和变压器融合的增强型 EEGNet 架构，用于跨会话运动意象解码EEG-MFTNet: An Enhanced EEGNet Architecture with Multi-Scale Temporal Convolutions and Transformer Fusion for Cross-Session Motor Imagery Decoding

Panagiotis Andrikopoulos, Siamak Mehrkanoon1 *Corresponding author.Code available at: https://github.com/pandrikopoul/EEG-MFTNet
摘要Abstract

脑机接口（BCIs）可以实现大脑和外部设备之间的直接通信，为运动障碍患者提供关键支持。然而，由于噪声和跨会话变异性，从脑电图 (EEG) 中准确解码运动想象 (MI) 仍然具有挑战性。本研究介绍了 EEG-MFTNet，这是一种基于 EEGNet 架构的新型深度学习模型，并通过多尺度时间卷积和 Transformer 编码器流进行了增强。这些组件旨在捕获脑电图信号中的短期和长期时间依赖性。该模型使用依赖于主题的跨会话设置在 SHU 数据集上进行评估，其性能优于基线模型，包括 EEGNet 及其最新的衍生模型。 EEG-MFTNet 的平均分类精度为 58.9%，同时保持较低的计算复杂度和推理延迟。结果凸显了该模型在实时 BCI 应用中的潜力，并强调了架构创新在改进 MI 解码方面的重要性。这项工作有助于开发更强大、更具适应性的脑机接口系统，对辅助技术和神经康复具有影响。Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) enable direct communication between the brain and external devices, providing critical support for individuals with motor impairments. However, accurate motor imagery (MI) decoding from electroencephalography (EEG) remains challenging due to noise and cross-session variability. This study introduces EEG-MFTNet, a novel deep learning model based on the EEGNet architecture, enhanced with multi-scale temporal convolutions and a Transformer encoder stream. These components are designed to capture both short and long-range temporal dependencies in EEG signals. The model is evaluated on the SHU dataset using a subject-dependent cross-session setup, outperforming baseline models, including EEGNet and its recent derivatives. EEG-MFTNet achieves an average classification accuracy of 58.9% while maintaining low computational complexity and inference latency. The results highlight the model’s potential for real-time BCI applications and underscore the importance of architectural innovations in improving MI decoding. This work contributes to the development of more robust and adaptive BCI systems, with implications for assistive technologies and neurorehabilitation.

II 相关工作Related Work

使用脑电图信号的 MI 分类通过深度学习取得了重大进展，解决了受试者间变异性和会话间不一致等挑战。深度学习模型可以直接从原始脑电图数据进行端到端学习。早期模型，如深卷积网络和浅卷积网络MI classification using EEG signals has seen significant progress through deep learning, addressing challenges like inter-subject variability and session-to-session inconsistency. Deep learning models have enabled end-to-end learning directly from raw EEG data. Early models like Deep and Shallow ConvNets[25]证明了 CNN 用于脑电图解码的可行性。脑电图网络demonstrated the feasibility of CNNs for EEG decoding. EEGNet[13]通过引入使用深度和可分离卷积的紧凑架构进一步推进了这一点。它的成功激发了一些扩展：EEG-TCNetfurther advanced this by introducing a compact architecture using depthwise and separable convolutions. Its success inspired several extensions: EEG-TCNet[10]添加了用于远程建模的时间卷积网络（TCN），而 MI-EEGNetadded Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCNs) for long-range modeling, while MI-EEGNet[22]和脑电图-ITNetand EEG-ITNet[24]引入了多尺度和初始风格的模块。值得注意的是，EEG-ITNet 还将 TCN 与残差连接集成在一起，以增强时间特征提取。为了解决 TCN 中的时间不对称问题，EEG-CDILNetintroduced multi-scale and inception-style modules. Notably, EEG-ITNet also integrates TCNs with residual connections to enhance temporal feature extraction. To address temporal asymmetry in TCNs, EEG-CDILNet[15]使用循环扩张卷积进行平衡时间建模。脑电图基因网uses circular dilated convolutions for balanced temporal modeling. EEG-GENet[27]结合图形嵌入来捕获电极之间的空间关系。轻量级模型，例如 EEG-SimpleConvincorporates graph embeddings to capture spatial relationships between electrodes. Lightweight models like EEG-SimpleConv[6]表明更简单的架构仍然可以具有竞争力的性能，使它们适合实时应用程序。其他创新包括 MFRC-Netshow that simpler architectures can still perform competitively, making them suitable for real-time applications. Other innovations include MFRC-Net[14]，通过多尺度残差块和 MAFNet 平衡性能和效率, which balances performance and efficiency via multi-scale residual blocks, and MAFNet[9]，它使用注意力机制来提取多域特征。, which uses attention mechanisms to extract multi-domain features.

虽然大多数 MI 分类的先前研究都集中在 EEG 上，但基于 MEG 的模型也提供了与 EEG 分类相关的架构见解。 AA脑电图网络While the majority of prior research on MI classification has centered on EEG, MEG-based models also offer architectural insights relevant to EEG classification. AA-EEGNet[1]是 EEGNet 的一种注意力增强变体，通过强调显着的时空特征来提高跨学科泛化能力。类似地，DS-GTF, an attention-enhanced variant of EEGNet, improves cross-subject generalization by emphasizing salient spatiotemporal features. Similarly, DS-GTF[8]将图注意力网络与 Transformer 编码器融合以捕获空间和时间依赖性。此外，最近提出的架构虽然是为其他任务（例如睡眠分期）开发的fuses Graph Attention Networks with a Transformer encoder to capture spatial and temporal dependencies. Additionally, recently proposed architectures, although developed for other tasks such as sleep staging[11]和声音定位and sound localization[12]，为 MI 分类提供有价值的见解，因为它们具有相似的架构和信号处理挑战。尽管上述技术已经证明了令人鼓舞的分类结果，但仍然存在巨大的进步潜力。许多最近的模型仍然依赖于特定的预处理管道或工程输入表示，这可能会限制它们的适应性。例如，3D-CNN-GAN, offer valuable insights for MI classification, as they share similar architectural and signal processing challenges. Although the aforementioned techniques have demonstrated encouraging classification results, considerable potential for advancement remains. Many recent models still rely on specific preprocessing pipelines or engineered input representations that may limit their adaptability. For instance, 3D-CNN-GAN[7]需要将原始脑电图信号转换为时间频率相位（TFPF）图，这一步骤会引入额外的复杂性，并且可能无法在数据集中很好地推广。其他，例如 MI-EEGNetrequires the transformation of raw EEG signals into temporal-frequency-phase (TFPF) maps, a step that introduces additional complexity and may not generalize well across datasets. Others, like MI-EEGNet[22]，引入复杂的多分支架构，虽然有效，但会增加计算需求，并可能阻碍在资源受限设备上的部署。独特的是，我们的模型无需任何预处理步骤即可运行，完全依靠其架构设计来增强分类性能。此外，所提出的方法优先考虑低延迟和计算效率，从而提高了其在实际实时 BCI 系统中部署的可行性。, introduce complex multi-branch architectures that, while effective, increase computational demands and may hinder deployment on resource-constrained devices. Distinctively, our model operates without any preprocessing steps, relying entirely on its architectural design to enhance classification performance. Moreover, the proposed approach prioritizes low latency and computational efficiency, thereby increasing its feasibility for deployment in practical real-time BCI systems.

IV 数据说明Data Description

本研究使用SHU数据集This study uses the SHU dataset[16]，一个大规模、公开的 EEG 数据集，包含来自 25 名健康受试者的数据，每名受试者在多天内完成了 5 个会话。在每次训练中，参与者进行 100 次左手或右手运动想象试验，每次持续 4 秒。 EEG 信号以 250 Hz 的频率从 32 个电极记录，每次试验产生 1000 个时间样本，这些样本直接用作模型的输入。该数据集由原始作者进行了预处理，他们消除了基线漂移并应用 0.5–40 Hz FIR 带通滤波器来保留 MI 相关频率分量。选择 SHU 数据集是因为它强调会话间的变异性，这是基于脑电图的 BCI 中持续存在的挑战，因为非平稳信号受到电极移位、疲劳和精神状态等因素的影响。, a large-scale, publicly available EEG dataset containing data from 25 healthy subjects, each completing five sessions across multiple days. In each session, participants performed 100 trials of left- or right-hand motor imagery, each lasting 4 seconds. EEG signals were recorded from 32 electrodes at 250 Hz, yielding 1000 time samples per trial, which are used directly as input to the model. The dataset was preprocessed by the original authors, who removed baseline drifts and applied a 0.5–40 Hz FIR band-pass filter to retain MI-relevant frequency components. The SHU dataset was selected for its emphasis on session-to-session variability, an ongoing challenge in EEG-based BCIs due to non-stationary signals influenced by factors such as electrode shifts, fatigue, and mental state.

Each subject’s first session was used for training (with 20% held out for validation), while sessions 2–5 were used for testing. A separate model was trained per subject. VI 模型解读Model Interpretation

为了深入了解模型的决策过程，应用梯度×输入技术来评估每个脑电图通道的贡献。事实证明，这种方法在不同的深度学习架构中都有效，特别是在基于脑电图的 BCI 系统中To gain insight into the model’s decision-making process, the Gradient × Input technique was applied to assess the contribution of each EEG channel. This approach has proven effective across diverse deep learning architectures, particularly in EEG-based BCI systems[4]。选择主题 6 第 4 节进行此分析，因为它产生了最高的分类准确度。由于检查点选择具有最高验证准确度的权重，并且受试者 6 的训练快速收敛，因此生成的预测往往保持接近 50% 的置信度。因此，使用最终权重对受试者 6 的数据重新训练模型 50 个周期，从而产生更明显的置信度分数并实现更清晰的可信度分析。. Subject 6, Session 4 was selected for this analysis, as it yielded the highest classification accuracy. Since checkpointing selects the weights with the highest validation accuracy and Subject 6’s training converges rapidly, the resulting predictions tend to remain close to 50% confidence. Therefore, the model was retrained for 50 epochs on Subject 6’s data using the final weights, yielding more distinct confidence scores and enabling a clearer trustworthiness analysis.

可解释性方法涉及计算输入的逐元素乘积以及模型输出相对于输入的梯度。这为每个正确分类的试验生成了归因图，然后对时间进行平均以获得每个渠道的单个归因分数。渠道按平均绝对归因值进行排名，突出显示对模型决策最有影响力的渠道。The interpretability method involved computing the element-wise product of the input and the gradient of the model’s output with respect to the input. This yielded attribution maps for each correctly classified trial, which were then averaged across time to obtain a single attribution score per channel. Channels were ranked by the average absolute attribution values, highlighting those most influential to the model’s decisions.

使用 MNE-Python 生成头皮地形，以可视化通道重要性的空间模式，聚合每个类别的试验中的归因图。图Scalp topographies were generated using MNE-Python to visualize spatial patterns of channel importance, aggregating attribution maps across trials per class. Figure3表明左手和右手图像产生不同的激活模式。值得注意的是，右手运动想象与中央额顶叶区域（Fz、F4、FC2、Cz 和 FC1）的活动减少以及后部区域（PO3、O1、Oz 和 O2）的活动增加有关，而左手想象则表现出相反的模式。这些观察结果表明，该模型根据特定区域活动的存在和抑制来区分类别。shows that left- and right-hand imagery produce distinct activation patterns. Notably, right-hand motor imagery is associated with decreased activation in central frontoparietal regions (Fz, F4, FC2, Cz, and FC1) and increased activity in posterior areas (PO3, O1, Oz, and O2), whereas left-hand imagery exhibits the opposite pattern. These observations suggest the model distinguishes classes based on both the presence and suppression of activity in specific regions.

为了评估解释方法的可靠性，进行了电极删除测试。电极根据其归因重要性进行排名，并且最重要或最不重要的通道的增加比例设置为零。在每一步中，都会记录模型的预测置信度。To assess the reliability of the interpretation method, electrode deletion tests were conducted. Electrodes were ranked according to their attribution importance, and increasing proportions of either the most or the least important channels were set to zero. At each step, the model’s prediction confidence was recorded.
