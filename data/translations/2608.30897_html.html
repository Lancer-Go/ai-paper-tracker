1  简介1  Introduction

世界模型使实体主体能够超越当前的观察，预测环境在不同行为下如何演变。通过将学到的动态转化为模拟体验，它们支持在物理世界中执行行动之前的规划、评估和政策学习。视频扩散和流匹配骨干网的进展World models equip embodied agents with the ability to look beyond the current observation and anticipate how the environment may evolve under different behaviors. By turning learned dynamics into simulated experience, they support planning, evaluation, and policy learning before actions are executed in the physical world. Advances in video diffusion and flow-matching backbones[1, 2, 3]使视觉逼真的世界模拟变得越来越可行，支持机器人技术、交互式环境和政策制定，超出了物理世界可以安全或经济地收集的范围have made visually realistic world simulation increasingly feasible, supporting robotics, interactive environments, and policy development beyond what can be safely or economically collected in the physical world[4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9]。在这种范式中，动作条件视频生成通过建模视觉场景如何响应代理干预而演变，提供了可控预测的直接途径。然而，对于具体代理而言，仅有合理的外观是不够的。有用的世界模型必须捕获交互的物理后果：接触发生的位置、对象何时开始移动、力如何在场景中传播以及环境如何随时间响应。. Within this paradigm, action-conditioned video generation provides a direct route to controllable prediction by modeling how visual scenes evolve in response to agent interventions. For an embodied agent, however, plausible appearance alone is insufficient. A useful world model must capture the physical consequences of interaction: where contact occurs, when an object begins to move, how forces propagate through the scene, and how the environment responds over time.

尽管取得了这些进展，当前的世界模型仍然主要是通过在空间和时间上均匀平均均方误差来优化的。大多数视频标记描述静态背景、缓慢变化的上下文或与当前交互无关的区域，而决定性的预测错误集中在短暂的接触事件、操纵的对象和演员的动作上。因此，均匀平均允许丰富的、易于重构的令牌来主导梯度并稀释对稀疏物理交互的监督。这种不匹配在具体设置中尤其具有破坏性：在错误的时间进行接触、向错误的方向移动对象或未能响应命令的操作时，卷展栏平均看起来可能很真实。此类故障不仅仅是视觉缺陷；它们表明该模型尚未了解行为如何因果地改变世界。Despite this progress, current world models are still predominantly optimized with mean squared error averaged uniformly over space and time. Most video tokens describe static backgrounds, slowly varying context, or regions unrelated to the current interaction, whereas the decisive prediction errors are concentrated around brief contact events, manipulated objects, and actor motion. Uniform averaging therefore allows abundant, easily reconstructed tokens to dominate the gradient and dilute supervision for sparse physical interactions. This mismatch is especially damaging in embodied settings: a rollout may look realistic on average while placing contact at the wrong time, moving an object in the wrong direction, or failing to respond to the commanded action. Such failures are not merely visual defects; they indicate that the model has not learned how actions causally alter the world.

一种可能的补救措施是明确注释参与者、对象、运动和接触区域。细分模型，例如 SAMOne possible remedy is to annotate actors, objects, motion, and contact regions explicitly. Segmentation models such as SAM[10]和光流估计器，例如 RAFTand optical-flow estimators such as RAFT[11]可以跟踪这些区域并提供手工制作的损失重量。然而，这种解决方案很难扩展到世界模型数据：每帧推理引入了大量的计算、存储和预处理开销，而最终的监督继承了外部模型的偏差和故障模式。它还通过固定的注释管道来定义相关性，而不是通过操作和预测的未来之间的因果关系。can track these regions and provide handcrafted loss weights. This solution, however, is difficult to scale to world-model data: per-frame inference introduces substantial computation, storage, and preprocessing overhead, while the resulting supervision inherits the biases and failure modes of the external models. It also defines relevance through a fixed annotation pipeline rather than through the causal relationship between the action and the predicted future.

在这项工作中，我们询问世界模型本身是否可以提供缺失的监督信号。我们的主要观察是，额外的梯度不应分配给每个视觉显着区域，而应分配给预测未来对动作敏感的标记。如果删除操作会改变令牌的预测，则该令牌会捕获模型必须学习的依赖于动作的结果，而不受操作影响的令牌无论移动多少，都不会带来动作条件风险。这将损失分配变成了在线因果行动响应问题。该模型不需要参与者、操纵对象或联系界面的外部定义，因为它通过自己的条件响应来识别相关交互。这一原则与动作无关：只要可以学习零动作条件，它就适用于末端执行器姿势、关节命令、空间动作图和相机轨迹等，这与将世界模型建立在干预后果中而不仅仅是感知可能性的更广泛目标相一致In this work, we ask whether the world model itself can provide the missing supervision signal. Our key observation is that additional gradient should be assigned not to every visually salient region, but to tokens whose predicted future is sensitive to the action. If removing the action changes the prediction at a token, that token captures an action-dependent consequence that the model must learn, whereas a token that is unaffected by the action carries no action-conditional risk however much it moves. This turns loss allocation into an online causal action-response problem. The model needs no external definition of the actor, manipulated object, or contact interface, because it identifies relevant interactions through its own conditional response. This principle is action-agnostic: it applies to end-effector poses, joint commands, spatial action maps, and camera trajectories alike, whenever a null-action condition can be learned, which aligns it with the broader goal of grounding world models in the consequences of intervention rather than only in perceptual likelihood[12, 13].

我们建议We proposeCAER，一个通用的世界模型训练目标，通过以下方式识别行动因果区域： 对比有行动和没有行动的预测，然后强调未解决的相互作用，同时 保留总体系数质量。在四种异构的动作条件设置中，, a general world-model training objective that identifies action-causal regions by contrasting predictions with and without actions, then emphasizes unresolved interactions while preserving the overall coefficient mass. Across four heterogeneous action-conditioned settings,CAER始终优于统一的 MSE，并开发出越来越准确的交互焦点 训练取得进展。在此框架的基础上，未来的工作将研究更广泛的机制和 世界模型中行动因果关系的影响，包括干预措施如何塑造学习动力， 泛化性和可控性，这种重新加权如何扩展到更长的视野和更大的范围 骨干，以及这种对比如何为评估和数据选择提供信号。consistently outperforms uniform MSE and develops increasingly accurate interaction focus as training progresses. Building on this framework, future work will investigate broader mechanisms and effects of action causality in world models, including how interventions shape learned dynamics, generalization, and controllability, how such reweighting scales to longer horizons and larger backbones, and how this contrast can supply signals for evaluation and data selection.
