大语言模型代理中的技能可用性和呈现粒度：受控技能基准研究Skill Availability and Presentation Granularity in Large-Language-Model Agents: A Controlled SkillsBench Study

Xiaonan Xu Corresponding author: xiaonanxu5@gmail.com Computer Information Technology, Northern Arizona University, Flagstaff, AZ, USA Wenjing Wu Computer Science, University of Colorado Boulder, Boulder, CO, USA
摘要Abstract

技能文档在推理时向大语言模型代理提供程序知识。本文研究受控技能知识的呈现粒度是否会改变下游任务的成功。该实验使用固定的 SkillsBench 版本、由官方预言机运行验证的 30 个任务域平衡子集、两个支持推理的模型配置、六个技能条件以及每个任务条件模型单元的五个试验。技能可用性是最清晰的经验信号。相对于无技能，技能条件将 GPT-5.5 的任务平均通过率提高 26.7 至 36.0 个百分点，将 DeepSeek V4-Flash 提高 18.0 至 26.0 个百分点。最终数据包含 1,800 行，每个模型 900 行。任务是推理单元。在估计超过 30 个任务的配对对比之前，每个任务条件模型单元内会聚合 5 次试验。主要表现对比较小且不确定。 GPT-5.5 的低抽象指导与 DeepSeek V4-Flash 的高抽象指导相差 +0.7 个百分点，而 DeepSeek V4-Flash 的低抽象指导与高抽象指导相差 -6.7 个百分点，两者 95% 的引导置信区间均为零。在中等抽象指南中添加一个有效示例与无示例变体的差异分别为 +0.7 和 +1.3 个百分点。平均奖励稳健性检查保留了相同的实质性结论。在这个受控子集中，技能可用性比没有技能与更高的成功率相关，而测试的呈现粒度变化会产生较小的、不确定的且依赖于模型的效果。Skill documents provide procedural knowledge to large-language-model agents at inference time. This article studies whether the presentation granularity of controlled skill knowledge changes downstream task success. The experiment uses a pinned SkillsBench version, a 30-task domain-balanced subset validated by official oracle runs, two reasoning-enabled model configurations, six skill conditions, and five trials per task-condition-model cell. Skill availability is the clearest empirical signal. Relative to no skill, skill conditions increase task-mean pass rate by 26.7 to 36.0 percentage points for GPT-5.5 and by 18.0 to 26.0 percentage points for DeepSeek V4-Flash. The final data contain 1,800 rows, with 900 rows for each model. The task is the inference unit. Five trials are aggregated within each task-condition-model cell before paired contrasts are estimated over 30 tasks. The primary presentation contrasts are smaller and uncertain. Low-abstraction guidance differs from high-abstraction guidance by +0.7 percentage points for GPT-5.5 and -6.7 percentage points for DeepSeek V4-Flash, with both 95% bootstrap confidence intervals crossing zero. Adding one worked example to medium-abstraction guidance differs from the no-example variant by +0.7 and +1.3 percentage points. Mean-reward robustness checks preserve the same substantive conclusion. In this controlled subset, skill availability is associated with higher success than no skill, while the tested presentation-granularity changes yield small, uncertain, and model-dependent effects.

1.  简介1.  Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 代理将基于文本的推理与外部环境中的操作相结合。操作空间可以包括 shell 命令、代码执行、文件编辑、浏览器自动化和确定性任务验证器。 ReAct 风格的提示使推理和行动之间的耦合变得明确，而工具使用和代理系统将这种模式扩展到外部应用程序编程接口、模型库、内存和可重用行为Large language model (LLM) agents combine text-based reasoning with actions in external environments. The action space may include shell commands, code execution, file editing, browser automation, and deterministic task verifiers. ReAct-style prompting made the coupling between reasoning and action explicit, while tool-use and agent systems extended this pattern to external application programming interfaces, model libraries, memory, and reusable behaviors[1, 2, 3, 4]。技能文档是推理时间支持的一种相关形式。它们为代理提供程序知识、局部约束、示例和任务约定，而无需更改模型权重。. Skill documents are a related form of inference-time support. They give an agent procedural knowledge, local constraints, examples, and task conventions without changing model weights.

SkillsBench 为此设置提供了直接基准。它将专家编写的技能文件夹与跨 11 个域的容器化任务和确定性验证程序打包在一起SkillsBench provides a direct benchmark for this setting. It packages expert-written skill folders with containerized tasks and deterministic verifiers across 11 domains[5]。其主要比较衡量使用策划技能、自生成技能和不使用技能的绩效。本文研究同一基准机制内的一个更狭窄的因素：更改已控制的技能文档的呈现粒度是否会改变下游任务的成功。. Its main comparison measures performance with curated skills, self-generated skills, and no skills. This article studies a narrower factor inside the same benchmark machinery: whether changing the presentation granularity of an already controlled skill document changes downstream task success.

设计问题尚未解决，因为技能文档可以在多个抽象级别上编写。高度抽象的文档可以陈述原则和不变量。中等抽象文档可以提供程序指导和决策点。低抽象文档可以提供类似清单的操作步骤和恢复检查。一个有效的示例可以使过程更加具体，同时也可以引导代理走向特定的路径。这些是合理的设计假设。它们需要在固定任务、固定时间表、固定模型设置、保留的基准文件以及任务级别而不是原始运行级别的推理下进行测量。The design question is unresolved because skill documents can be written at several levels of abstraction. A high-abstraction document can state principles and invariants. A medium-abstraction document can give procedural guidance and decision points. A low-abstraction document can provide checklist-like operational steps and recovery checks. A worked example can make a procedure more concrete, while also directing the agent toward a particular path. These are plausible design hypotheses. They require measurement under fixed tasks, fixed schedules, fixed model settings, preserved benchmark files, and inference at the task level rather than at the raw-run level.

本文做出了三点贡献。它在冻结的、经 Oracle 验证的 SkillsBench 子集上提供了技能呈现粒度的受控双模型测量。它报告了两个主要问题的任务级别配对对比：低抽象与高抽象，以及带有一个工作示例的中等抽象与没有示例的相同抽象级别。它记录了完整性和执行控制，包括受保护的散列、冻结的时间表、最终行分离以及针对传输、依赖性、并发性和超时事件的协议修订。This paper makes three contributions. It provides a controlled two-model measurement of skill presentation granularity on a frozen, oracle-validated SkillsBench subset. It reports task-level paired contrasts for two primary questions: low abstraction versus high abstraction, and medium abstraction with one worked example versus the same abstraction level without an example. It documents integrity and execution controls, including protected hashes, frozen schedules, final-row separation, and protocol amendments for transport, dependency, concurrency, and timeout incidents.

主要结果将技能可用性与技能呈现粒度分开。 GPT-5.5 的技能条件比没有技能高 26.7 到 36.0 个百分点，DeepSeek V4-Flash 比没有技能高 18.0 到 26.0 个百分点。受控的呈现对比度要小得多。对于 GPT-5.5，低抽象与高抽象相差 +0.7 个百分点；对于 DeepSeek V4-Flash，低抽象与高抽象相差 -6.7 个百分点。向中等抽象变体添加一个有效示例与无示例变体的差异分别为 +0.7 和 +1.3 个百分点。这些估计支持了一个有限的结论。获得与任务相关的技能知识与该子集中更高的成功相关，而测试的呈现粒度变化具有较小的、不确定的且依赖于模型的影响。The main result separates skill availability from skill presentation granularity. Skill conditions are higher than no skill by 26.7 to 36.0 percentage points for GPT-5.5 and by 18.0 to 26.0 percentage points for DeepSeek V4-Flash. The controlled presentation contrasts are much smaller. Low abstraction differs from high abstraction by +0.7 percentage points for GPT-5.5 and -6.7 percentage points for DeepSeek V4-Flash. Adding one worked example to the medium-abstraction variant differs from the no-example variant by +0.7 and +1.3 percentage points. These estimates support a restrained conclusion. Access to task-relevant skill knowledge is associated with higher success in this subset, while the tested presentation-granularity changes have small, uncertain, and model-dependent effects.

2.  背景及相关工作2.  Background and Related Work

提示语言模型对任务上下文的表示方式很敏感。情境学习表明，以文本形式提供的演示可以在不更新权重的情况下改变任务行为Prompted language models are sensitive to how task context is represented. In-context learning showed that demonstrations supplied as text can change task behavior without weight updates[6]。思维链提示使用有效的推理范例来引发多步骤推理. Chain-of-thought prompting uses worked reasoning exemplars to elicit multi-step reasoning[7]。少样本校准、示例排序和提示格式研究表明，语义上接近的提示变体可能会产生较大的输出差异，特别是当评估使用单一固定提示格式时. Few-shot calibration, example ordering, and prompt-format studies show that semantically close prompt variants can produce large output differences, especially when evaluation uses a single fixed prompt format[8, 9, 10]。技能呈现属于更广泛的上下文设计类别，但它作用于代理使用的长程序文档，而不是仅作用于简短的任务提示。. Skill presentation belongs to this broader class of context design, but it acts on long procedural documents used by agents rather than on short task prompts alone.

LLM 代理工作将提示扩展到交互式环境中。 ReAct 交错推理轨迹和动作LLM agent work extends prompting into interactive environments. ReAct interleaves reasoning traces and actions[1]。 Toolformer 训练模型来决定何时以及如何调用外部工具. Toolformer trains models to decide when and how to call external tools[2]。 HuggingGPT 将法学硕士视为专业模型的规划者. HuggingGPT frames an LLM as a planner over specialist models[3]。 Voyager 为开放式具体任务构建可重用技能库. Voyager builds a reusable skill library for open-ended embodied tasks[4]。这些系统激励评估设置，其中成功取决于模型使用外部资源和程序上下文的能力。. These systems motivate evaluation settings where success depends on the model’s ability to use external resources and procedural context.

代理基准现在涵盖多种操作设置。 WebShop、WebArena 和 Mind2Web 研究自然语言目标下的 Web 交互Agent benchmarks now cover several operational settings. WebShop, WebArena, and Mind2Web study web interaction under natural-language goals[11, 12, 13]。 AgentBench 跨多个交互式环境评估 LLM 代理. AgentBench evaluates LLM agents across multiple interactive environments[14]。 SWE-bench 通过存储库级代码编辑来关注实际软件问题. SWE-bench focuses on real software issues with repository-level code editing[15]。 GAIA 测试工具使用、网页浏览、推理和多模式协助. GAIA tests tool use, web browsing, reasoning, and multimodal assistance[16]。 OSWorld 在真实计算机环境中评估多模式代理. OSWorld evaluates multimodal agents in real computer environments[17]。这些基准衡量代理在任务和环境变化下的能力。相反，本研究固定了 SkillsBench 任务子集，并操纵所提供的技能知识的形式。. These benchmarks measure agent competence under task and environment variation. The present study instead holds a SkillsBench task subset fixed and manipulates the form of supplied skill knowledge.

代理技能为可重用的程序知识提供了标准的包装形式。 Anthropic 将代理技能描述为代理可以动态发现和加载的指令、脚本和资源的组织文件夹Agent skills give reusable procedural knowledge a standard packaging form. Anthropic describes Agent Skills as organized folders of instructions, scripts, and resources that agents can discover and load dynamically[18]。 Anthropic 文档将代理技能描述为模块化功能，可打包指令、元数据和可选资源，并通过文件系统支持的技能文件夹按需加载内容. Anthropic documentation describes Agent Skills as modular capabilities that package instructions, metadata, and optional resources, with content loaded on demand through filesystem-backed skill folders[19]。 SkillsBench 通过专家编写的技能、确定性任务验证器以及技能/无技能比较来实现这一想法. SkillsBench operationalises this idea with expert-written skills, deterministic task verifiers, and skill/no-skill comparisons[5]。它报告了其基准测试中策划技能平均值+16.2个百分点，并且还发现具有两到三个模块的重点技能优于全面的文档. It reports a +16.2 percentage-point curated-skill average on its benchmark and also observes that focused skills with two to three modules outperform comprehensive documentation[5]。这些发现促使人们对技能包的哪些属性影响座席行为进行更有针对性的测量。. Those findings motivate more targeted measurements of which properties of a skill package affect agent behavior.

本研究的重点是表达而不是作者身份。官方策划的技能是逐字节保存的。四个重写的条件源自紧凑的内容分类账，它们代表源过程、约束、占位符和前沿策略。重写的变体改变了抽象级别和一个工作示例的存在，同时保持任务标识、验证者代码、模型配置、时间表和评估工具的固定。该设计将技能文档视为实验性上下文干预。The present study focuses on presentation rather than authorship. The official curated skills are preserved byte-for-byte. Four rewritten conditions are derived from compact content ledgers that represent source procedures, constraints, placeholders, and frontmatter policy. The rewritten variants change abstraction level and the presence of one worked example while keeping task identity, verifier code, model configuration, schedule, and evaluation harness fixed. This design treats a skill document as an experimental context intervention.

3.3.  技能条件及内容控制3.3.  Skill Conditions and Content Control

这六个条件将技能可用性与技能呈现分开。无技能条件会删除技能指令。原始策划的条件逐字节复制官方 SkillsBench 技能文件。高抽象条件将源技能重写为原理、不变量和决策规则，但没有可行的示例。中等无示例条件提供了带有决策点和陷阱的程序指导，但没有可行的示例。中等示例条件使用相同的中等抽象目标，同时根据账本允许的内容添加一个简短的工作示例。低抽象条件提供了类似清单的操作指南、具体占位符、逐步恢复检查，并且没有工作示例。The six conditions separate skill availability from skill presentation. The no-skill condition removes skill instructions. The original-curated condition copies the official SkillsBench skill files byte-for-byte. The high-abstraction condition rewrites the source skill as principles, invariants, and decision rules, with no worked example. The medium-no-example condition gives procedural guidance with decision points and pitfalls, with no worked example. The medium-with-example condition uses the same medium-abstraction target while adding exactly one short worked example based on ledger-allowed content. The low-abstraction condition presents checklist-like operational guidance, concrete placeholders, step-by-step recovery checks, and no worked example.

源任务包含88个官方技能文档。官方策划的条件通过哈希保留这些文件。 4个重写条件产生352个重写技能文件。每个重写的变体都是从紧凑的内容分类帐中生成的，并在三个级别进行审核。确定性结构审计检查所需的文件结构和条件约束。样式审核检查所请求的表示形式是否存在，并拒绝明显的格式违规。账本覆盖率审计检查是否保留了重要的源内容，并且是否引入了不受支持的添加内容。这些控制减少了内容漂移，同时将语义等价、标记长度、显着性、排序、术语和格式作为剩余变化的可能来源。The source tasks contain 88 official skill documents. The official curated condition preserves those files by hash. The four rewritten conditions produce 352 rewritten skill files. Each rewritten variant is generated from a compact content ledger and audited at three levels. The deterministic structural audit checks required file structure and condition constraints. The style audit checks that the requested presentation form is present and rejects obvious format violations. The ledger-coverage audit checks that essential source content is retained and that unsupported additions are not introduced. These controls reduce content drift, while leaving semantic equivalence, token length, salience, ordering, terminology, and formatting as possible sources of residual variation.

3.4.  模型配置和计划锁定3.4.  Model Configurations and Schedule Locks

评估了两种模型配置。 GPT-5.5 使用中等推理努力。 DeepSeek V4-Flash 通过官方 OpenAI 兼容的 Chat Completions 界面，使用高推理能力的思维模式。 DeepSeek 文档描述了思维模式努力控制Two model configurations are evaluated. GPT-5.5 uses medium reasoning effort. DeepSeek V4-Flash uses thinking mode with high reasoning effort through the official OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions interface. DeepSeek documentation describes thinking-mode effort controls with高high和and最大max值，并指出不支持的采样参数，例如温度和values, and states that unsupported sampling parameters such as temperature and顶部_ptop_p对思维模式没有影响have no effect in thinking mode[20]。运行使用协议锁定的高设置，而不是在计划冻结后升级到最大值。因此，跨模型比较描述了评估的配置，而不是匹配的推理预算或模型最佳设置。. The run used the protocol-locked high setting rather than escalating to max after the schedule was frozen. Cross-model comparisons therefore describe the evaluated configurations, not matched reasoning budgets or model-optimal settings.

冻结设计包含 30 个任务、六个条件、每个任务-条件-模型单元五次试验以及两种模型配置。每个模型都有 900 次预定运行，分为 5 个正式批次，每批 180 次。这些记录指定 GPT-5.5 的计划种子为 42，DeepSeek V4-Flash 的计划种子为 314。它们没有指定显式 API 种子或非默认 GPT-5.5 采样温度。因此，重复试验应理解为锁定线束设置下的重复调用，而不是种子随机重复。The frozen design contains 30 tasks, six conditions, five trials per task-condition-model cell, and two model configurations. Each model has 900 scheduled runs, split into five official batches of 180 runs. The records specify schedule seeds of 42 for GPT-5.5 and 314 for DeepSeek V4-Flash. They do not specify an explicit API seed or a non-default GPT-5.5 sampling temperature. Repeated trials should therefore be read as repeated calls under the locked harness settings, not as seeded stochastic replicates.

3.5.  完整性控制和执行修正3.5.  Integrity Controls and Execution Amendments

5.  讨论5.  Discussion

有技能条件和无技能的参考对比是最大的经验信号。当技能文档可用时，两种模型都显示出更高的任务平均通过率。这种模式与 SkillsBench 的发现一致，即任务相关的程序知识可以提高代理的绩效。幅度应理解为子集特定的。目前的子集包含 30 个经过 Oracle 验证的选定任务，而 SkillsBench 标题平均值是在更广泛的基准上计算的。模型版本、线束设置和任务选择标准也有所不同。参考比较验证所选子集保留了技能可用性信号，同时留下了较大幅度的源未解析。The reference comparison between skill conditions and no skill is the largest empirical signal. Both models show higher task-mean pass rates when a skill document is available. This pattern is consistent with the SkillsBench finding that task-relevant procedural knowledge can improve agent performance. The magnitude should be read as subset-specific. The present subset contains 30 oracle-validated selected tasks, while the SkillsBench headline average is computed over a broader benchmark. Model versions, harness settings, and task selection criteria also differ. The reference comparison verifies that the selected subset retains a skill-availability signal, while leaving the source of the larger magnitude unresolved.

主要表现对比比技能可用性对比更小且不稳定。低抽象检查表指导并不能可靠地优于高抽象原则指导。 GPT-5.5 的估计值接近于零，而 DeepSeek V4-Flash 的估计值为负。一种可能的机制是，详细的清单文本改变了外部指导和模型自身规划之间的平衡。 DeepSeek V4-Flash 是通过高度努力的思维模式进行评估的，因此额外的低级指令可能会重复或限制规划，而不是改进规划。该实验没有操纵推理模式或检查内部计划痕迹，因此这种解释仍然是一个假设。The primary presentation contrasts are smaller and less stable than the skill-availability contrasts. Low-abstraction checklist guidance does not reliably outperform high-abstraction principle guidance. The estimate is near zero for GPT-5.5 and negative for DeepSeek V4-Flash. A possible mechanism is that detailed checklist text changes the balance between external guidance and the model’s own planning. DeepSeek V4-Flash was evaluated with thinking mode at high effort, so additional low-level instructions may duplicate or constrain planning rather than improve it. The experiment did not manipulate reasoning mode or inspect internal planning traces, so this explanation remains a hypothesis.

工作示例对比度方向稳定但很小。一个示例可能有助于将其他程序性文档扎根于具体路径中。同一示例还可能消耗上下文或过度指定不适合当前任务实例的路径。观察到的 +0.7 和 +1.3 个百分点的估计值对于设计规则来说太小。最好将它们视为较大实验的候选效果，该实验会改变示例数量、示例长度以及与目标任务的示例相似度。The worked-example contrast is directionally stable but small. A single example may help by grounding an otherwise procedural document in a concrete path. The same example may also consume context or over-specify a path that does not fit the current task instance. The observed +0.7 and +1.3 percentage-point estimates are too small for a design rule. They are better treated as a candidate effect for a larger experiment that varies example number, example length, and example similarity to the target task.

最初策划的技能仍然是信息丰富的参考。受控重写减少了不受控制的表达差异，但它们可能会消除官方文档的实用功能。这些功能可以包括排序、强调、命名约定、文件本地提示以及内容分类帐无法完全捕获的简洁提醒。相对于原始策划的技能，GPT-5.5 所有重写变体的通过率较低，这与这种可能性是一致的。 DeepSeek V4-Flash 显示了不同的模式，其高度抽象略高于原始策划的技能，而其他重写的变体则低于它们。有证据表明，不要将重写的技能视为相同知识的纯粹等效呈现。The original curated skills remain an informative reference. Controlled rewrites reduce uncontrolled presentation differences, but they may remove pragmatic features of the official documents. Those features can include ordering, emphasis, naming conventions, file-local cues, and concise reminders that content ledgers do not fully capture. GPT-5.5’s lower pass rates for all rewritten variants relative to original curated skills are consistent with that possibility. DeepSeek V4-Flash shows a different pattern, with high abstraction slightly above original curated skills and the other rewritten variants below them. The evidence supports caution against treating a rewritten skill as a purely equivalent presentation of the same knowledge.

在解释抽象对比时，长度和显着性仍然是残留的混淆因素。审计检查了条件结构和账本覆盖范围，但抽象级别仍然可能与文档长度、本地重点、顺序、术语和清单密度共同变化。这种限制对于 RQ1 最为重要，因为低抽象指导会改变步骤的具体性和代理可用的操作脚手架的数量。接近零和负的 RQ1 估计支持对低抽象优势的谨慎态度，而不仅仅是对抽象的纯粹估计。Length and salience remain residual confounds in interpreting the abstraction contrast. The audits checked condition structure and ledger coverage, but abstraction level can still co-vary with document length, local emphasis, ordering, terminology, and checklist density. This limitation matters most for RQ1 because low-abstraction guidance changes both step concreteness and the amount of operational scaffolding available to the agent. The near-zero and negative RQ1 estimates support caution about a low-abstraction advantage, not a pure estimate of abstraction alone.

执行修订使评估更具可审计性，同时还定义了应复制的操作选择。验证器执行之前的传输重试、有界并发、Docker 构建分离、官方验证器超时最终确定和操作员授权的模型端超时最终确定都会影响长时间运行的代理活动如何成为干净的结果表。报告这些规则可以避免无声的消耗。这也意味着后续研究应该复制相同的最终确定政策或报告与之不同的情况。The execution amendments make the evaluation more auditable, while also defining operational choices that should be reproduced. Transport retries before verifier execution, bounded concurrency, Docker build separation, official verifier-timeout finalization, and operator-authorized model-side timeout finalization all affect how a long-running agent campaign becomes a clean result table. Reporting those rules avoids silent attrition. It also means follow-up studies should either reproduce the same finalization policy or report departures from it.

6.  有效性威胁6.  Threats to Validity

该研究使用一个基准和一个包含 30 个任务的子集。该子集是域平衡的且经过 Oracle 验证，但它并不代表完整的 SkillsBench 任务分布。每个模型的任务级样本大小为 30 个。灵敏度计算表明，效果分辨率有限，大约低于 8 到 16 个百分点，具体取决于实现的对比度方差。The study uses one benchmark and a 30-task subset. The subset is domain-balanced and oracle-validated, but it does not represent the full SkillsBench task distribution. The task-level sample size is 30 per model. The sensitivity calculation indicates limited resolution for effects below roughly 8 to 16 percentage points, depending on the realised contrast variance.

内容控制程序是近似的。内容分类帐和审计减少了不受支持的添加和遗漏，但它们不能保证语义等效性或保留原始策划技能中的所有实用线索。重写的变体可能在标记长度、显着性、排序、术语或格式方面有所不同，从而影响超出预期抽象操作的模型使用。The content-control procedure is approximate. Content ledgers and audits reduce unsupported additions and omissions, but they cannot guarantee semantic equivalence or preserve all pragmatic cues in the original curated skills. Rewritten variants may differ in token length, salience, ordering, terminology, or formatting in ways that affect model use beyond the intended abstraction manipulation.

这两种模型配置在计算上不匹配。 GPT-5.5 和 DeepSeek V4-Flash 使用特定于模型的推理设置和不同的传输路径。 DeepSeek 是使用协议锁定的高推理努力设置而不是最大设置运行的。此锁定避免了冻结后调整，但它阻止了有关 DeepSeek 最大努力行为的声明。因此，跨模型比较是描述性的，不应被解读为对法学硕士代理人群体的估计。The two model configurations are not compute-matched. GPT-5.5 and DeepSeek V4-Flash use model-specific reasoning settings and different transport paths. DeepSeek was run with the protocol-locked high reasoning-effort setting rather than the max setting. This lock avoids post-freeze tuning, but it prevents a claim about DeepSeek max-effort behavior. Cross-model comparisons are therefore descriptive and should not be read as estimates over a population of LLM agents.

基准结果是官方验证者奖励，而不是完整的现实世界任务正确性。分数奖励被视为主要二元分析的未通过，尽管它们保留在次要平均奖励摘要中。这种选择是严格且可重复的，但它放弃了任务性能的一些等级。The benchmark outcome is official verifier reward, not complete real-world task correctness. Fractional rewards are treated as non-passes for the primary binary analysis, although they are retained in secondary mean-reward summaries. This choice is strict and reproducible, but it discards some gradation in task performance.

为了完成评估活动，需要对执行进行修改。这些修正案保留了基准内容和最终行的完整性，但它们引入了对复制至关重要的操作选择。有界并发、传输重试、依赖缓存和操作员授权的模型端终结应该在后续研究中明确重现或报告。Execution amendments were necessary to complete the evaluation campaign. The amendments preserved benchmark content and final-row integrity, yet they introduce operational choices that can matter for replication. Bounded concurrency, transport retries, dependency caches, and operator-authorized model-side finalizations should be reproduced or reported explicitly in follow-up studies.

8.  结论8.  Conclusion

这种受控的 SkillsBench 测量将技能可用性与呈现粒度分开。对于两种评估的模型配置，技能条件显示出相对于无技能的正参考信号。主要表现对比很小且不确定。低抽象指导并不总是优于高抽象指导，并且在中抽象指导中添加一个工作示例只能产生很小的积极估计。证据支持有限的设计含义。与任务相关的技能知识在这一子集中很重要，而经过测试的演示粒度重写并没有提供相对于其他技能或相对于原始策划技能的可靠优势。This controlled SkillsBench measurement separates skill availability from presentation granularity. Skill conditions show a positive reference signal relative to no skill for both evaluated model configurations. The primary presentation contrasts are small and uncertain. Low-abstraction guidance does not consistently outperform high-abstraction guidance, and adding one worked example to medium-abstraction guidance yields only small positive estimates. The evidence supports a restrained design implication. Task-relevant skill knowledge matters in this subset, while the tested rewrites of presentation granularity do not provide a reliable advantage over one another or over the original curated skills.
