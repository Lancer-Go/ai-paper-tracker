1 简介Introduction

在许多应用中，视频字幕不仅充当描述，而且充当用户意图的界面。 视觉语言模型（VLM）生成语义上准确的字幕，但它们通常接受众包注释的训练，这些注释汇总了不同的人类解释。因此，他们经常描述广泛可见的内容，而忽略观看者的特定注意力和主观感知。 考虑一个以自我为中心的设置：观察同一厨房场景的两个用户可能会关注不同的物体或动作，但传统的 VLM 可能会生成一个全局正确的标题，该标题在实用上与用户想要检索、重新访问或采取行动的内容不一致。 在字幕索引检索中，用户通常通过引用参与的对象或动作而不是整个场景来搜索视频。当索引标题强调无人看管的背景内容时，相关视频可能会变得更难检索。In many applications, video captions serve not merely as descriptions but as interfaces to user intent. Vision-language models (VLMs) generate semantically accurate captions, yet they are typically trained on crowd-sourced annotations that aggregate across diverse human interpretations. As a result, they often describe what is broadly visible while ignoring viewer-specific attention and subjective perception. Consider an egocentric setting: two users observing the same kitchen scene may attend to different objects or actions, but a conventional VLM may produce a single globally correct caption that is pragmatically misaligned with what either user intends to retrieve, revisit, or act upon. In caption-indexed retrieval, users often search for videos by referring to attended objects or actions rather than the full scene. When an index caption emphasizes unattended background content, the relevant video can become harder to retrieve.

8 局限性Limitations

VEGAS 继承了底层 VLM 的局限性，包括幻觉、感知错误和不完美的令牌似然校准。如果 VLM 在有或没有凝视调节的情况下产生类似的幻觉解释，VEGAS 可能会给出过于有利的分数。 第二个限制是凝视揭示了观看者关注的地方，但并不总是他们如何解释他们所看到的内容。这对于字幕可能需要概念抽象、信息聚合或跨视觉元素推理的领域很重要。因此，当注意力与具体物体、动作或实体紧密联系时，VEGAS 可能是最有效的。 最后，VEGAS 需要注视注释。尽管基于网络摄像头的眼球追踪降低了收集成本，但它仍然需要受控的数据采集和参与者的参与。在已部署的系统中，智能眼镜中可能提供显式注视，而隐式注意力代理对于网络和视频平台可能更现实。VEGAS inherits the limitations of the underlying VLMs, including hallucinations, perceptual errors, and imperfect token likelihood calibration. If a VLM produces similar hallucinated interpretations with and without gaze conditioning, VEGAS may assign overly favorable scores. A second limitation is that gaze reveals where a viewer attends, but not always how they interpret what they see. This matters for domains where captioning may require conceptual abstraction, information aggregation, or reasoning across visual elements. Thus, VEGAS may be most effective when attention is closely tied to concrete objects, actions, or entities. Finally, VEGAS requires gaze annotations. Although webcam-based eye tracking reduces collection cost, it still requires controlled data acquisition and participant involvement. In deployed systems, explicit gaze may be available in smart glasses, while implicit attention proxies may be more realistic for web and video platforms.

9 结论Conclusion

尽管人类视频描述本质上是多样化的并且与人们观看的位置密切相关，但现有的 VLM 生成的视频字幕在很大程度上忽略了个人观看者的注意力。受这一观察的启发，我们引入了 VEGAS，这是一种跨模式的信息论指标，用于测量视频字幕与观看者视线的对齐程度，以及基于 VEGAS 的拒绝采样方法，该方法使用视线作为观看者意图的代理，从候选 VLM 输出中选择注意感知字幕。这种方法完全在推理时运行，不需要重新训练底层视觉语言模型。通过对现有 VLM 生成的候选字幕进行重新排序和选择，VEGAS 将观众注意力转化为更好的语义索引，用于下游检索和注意力感知交互。Existing VLMs generate video captions that largely ignore individual viewer attention, despite the fact that human video descriptions are inherently diverse and closely tied to where people look. Motivated by this observation, we introduce VEGAS, a cross-modal, information-theoretic metric that measures how well a video caption aligns with a viewer’s gaze, along with a VEGAS-based rejection sampling method that uses gaze as a proxy for viewer intent to select attention-aware captions from candidate VLM outputs. This approach operates entirely at inference time and requires no retraining of the underlying vision-language model. By re-ranking and selecting among candidate captions generated by existing VLMs, VEGAS turns viewer attention into a better semantic index for downstream retrieval and attention-aware interaction.

为了评估这个框架，我们策划了一个多模式数据集，将目光轨迹、视频和人类编写的字幕配对，跨越自我中心和教学幻灯片领域。 在 AEA 上，VEGAS 选择的字幕显着改善了与人类注释的语义对齐，并在下游字幕到视频检索中产生适度但一致的增益。 在 SlideVQA 上，增益较小且不具有统计显着性，这表明当字幕需要概念抽象而不是基于具体的关注对象或动作时，凝视是较弱的选择器。 总之，这些结果表明，测试时凝视可以为观看者感知的字幕选择提供实用的机制，同时还强调其好处取决于视觉注意力如何直接映射到语义解释。To evaluate this framework, we curate a multimodal dataset pairing gaze trajectories, videos, and human-written captions across egocentric and instructional slide domains. On AEA, VEGAS-selected captions significantly improve semantic alignment with human annotations and yield modest but consistent gains in downstream caption-to-video retrieval. On SlideVQA, the gains are smaller and not statistically significant, suggesting that gaze is a weaker selector when captions require conceptual abstraction rather than grounding concrete attended objects or actions. Together, these results show that test-time gaze can provide a practical mechanism for viewer-aware caption selection, while also highlighting that its benefits depend on how directly visual attention maps onto semantic interpretation.

未来的工作。Future work.

展望未来，VEGAS 开辟了几个方向。未来的工作可以通过用显着性模型、交互痕迹或其他隐式注意力代理取代显式注视，以及将 VEGAS 扩展到更长的视频和实时个性化来提高可扩展性。可以通过外部验证、接地约束或校准评分模型来提高鲁棒性，从而降低对 VLM 幻觉和可能性误校准的敏感性。 VEGAS 还可以被提炼成字幕模型或重新表述为可微分的训练目标，从而实现个人记忆检索、辅助问答和学习支持系统等应用。Looking ahead, VEGAS opens several directions. Future work could improve scalability by replacing explicit gaze with saliency models, interaction traces, or other implicit attention proxies, and by extending VEGAS to longer videos and real-time personalization. Robustness could be improved through external verification, grounding constraints, or calibrated scoring models that reduce sensitivity to VLM hallucinations and likelihood miscalibration. VEGAS could also be distilled into captioning models or reformulated as a differentiable training objective, enabling applications such as personal memory retrieval, assistant question answering, and learning-support systems.
