SciFigQual-Bench：具有完整手稿背景的科学图形质量评估基准SciFigQual-Bench: A Benchmark for Scientific Figure Quality Assessment with Full-Manuscript Context

Zihan Deng1†, Chuanzhi Xu2†11footnotemark: 1, Huiqi Liang2, Haoyang Li2, Xiaozhen Zhong3, Lequan Yu1 Equal contribution.Email: zhdeng@hku.hk, chuanzhi.xu@sydney.edu.au.
摘要Abstract

科学图像是在科学论文中呈现实验结论、阐述系统架构和支持比较论点的核心要素。 然而，现有的图像质量评估（IQA）主要针对自然照片或人工智能生成的内容而设计，不能直接应用于科学论文。现有的少数学术图表研究仍然局限于视觉表面比较，未能验证标题对齐、引文相关性或视觉误导性。 为了解决这个问题，我们建议Scientific images are the core elements of presenting experimental conclusions, elaborating system architecture, and supporting comparative arguments in scientific papers. However, existing image quality assessment (IQA) are predominantly designed for natural photographs or AI-generated content, which cannot be directly applied to scientific papers. The few existing studies on scholarly charts remain confined to visual-surface comparisons, failing to verify caption alignment, citation relevance, or visual misleadingness. To address this, we proposeSciFigQual-Bench，一个全文上下文基准，从五个维度（清晰度、布局、标题适合度、上下文相关性和误导风险）评估科学图像。数据涵盖2020年至2025年计算机科学顶级会议，6,308张图像由多位领域专家从五个维度独立评分，并聚合成金标准注释。 与以前的科学图形基准不同，我们的数据集将每个图像与其标题、引用句子和手稿上下文绑定在一起。 为了实现该基准的自动评估，我们设计了分阶段的跨模式评估, a full-text contextual benchmark that evaluates scientific image across five dimensions (clarity, layout, caption fit, context relevance, and misleading risk). The data covers the top computer-science conferences from 2020 to 2025, 6,308 images were independently scored by multiple domain experts in five dimensions and aggregated into the gold-standard annotations. Unlike previous scientific figure benchmarks, our dataset binds each image to its caption, citing sentence, and manuscript context. 1 简介Introduction

自然图像来源于现实世界中的光、影和物体，其评价标准大多停留在人类视觉感知的层面。例如，在图像质量评估（IQA）中，通常会从自然色彩、清晰度、美感等方面来评估风景照片，在内容层面上没有“对错”标准Natural images arise from light, shadows, and objects in the real world, and their evaluation criteria mostly remain at the level of human visual perception. In Image Quality Assessment (IQA), for example, a landscape photo is often evaluated from the aspects of natural color, clarity, aesthetic perception, etc. There is no "right or wrong" standard at the content level(Li et al. 2025)。这类图像往往与语言和文字无关，可以仅根据图像本身进行评价. These types of images are often unrelated to language and text, and can be evaluated solely based on the image itself(Chen et al. 2024).

然而，科学图像天然地与完整的学术文本语境绑定在一起，必须与描述、具体数据或结论相结合，才能具有评价价值。However, scientific images are naturally bound to the complete academic text context, and they must be combined with descriptions, specific data, or conclusions in order to have evaluative value(Tufte 2001; Cleveland and McGill 1984)。 如果科学论文中的图形存在与数据或结论密切相关的错误，例如轴截断、基线数据缺失、或者图形中的数据与文本中的描述不一致等，仅靠像素级别的目视检查无法识别此类缺陷。. If there are errors in the figures in a scientific paper that are closely related to the data or conclusions, such as axis truncation, missing baseline data, or inconsistencies between the data in the figures and the description in the text, visual inspection at the pixel level alone cannot identify such defects.

因此，在科学论文同行评审中，科学论文的质量评估本质上是三模式的：它取决于图像中可见的内容、标题中声称的内容以及手稿在引用段落中主张的内容。 然而，现有的自然 IQA 和 AIGC 对齐范例在单一视觉模式上运行，本质上将图像与其标题和引用手稿上下文脱钩。 如图所示In scientific paper peer review, quality assessment of scientific paper is therefore inherently tri-modal: it depends on what is visible in image, what is claimed in caption, and what the manuscript asserts in citing paragraphs. However, existing paradigms for natural IQA and AIGC alignment operate on a single visual modality, inherently decoupling the image from both its caption and the citing manuscript context. In each case, evaluation proceeds on a detached visual input, decoupled from captions, citing paragraphs, and full-text context.

在本文中，我们提出了 SciFigQual-Bench，这是一种基于证据的分阶段评审框架，用于在真实的完整手稿背景下评估人类发表的科学图像。我们将每个图形图像与其标题和源 PDF 中的引用段落集绑定在一起，形成完整的实验上下文 (CTX)。我们不采用单一的端到端视觉语言模型（VLM）提示，而是采用基于证据的分阶段评审流程：首先从每个组件收集多模态证据，然后进行跨模态融合，最后输出涵盖视觉清晰度（VC）、结构布局（SL）、字幕一致性（CC）、误导风险（MR）和上下文一致性（CTX）的可追溯的多维度分数。与人工智能生成的科学图像的忠实度基准不同In this paper, we propose SciFigQual-Bench, an evidence-grounded staged judging framework for evaluating human-published scientific images under authentic full-manuscript context. We binding each figure image with its caption and the citing paragraph set from the source PDF to form complete experimental context (CTX). Instead of adopting a single end-to-end vision-language model (VLM) prompt, we employ an evidence-grounded staged judging process: first collect multi-modal evidence from each component, then perform cross-modal fusion, and finally output traceable multi-dimensional scores covering Visual Clarity (VC), Structure Layout (SL), Caption Consistency (CC), Misleading Risk (MR), and Context Consistency (CTX). Unlike faithfulness benchmarks for AI-generated scientific images(Ye et al. 2026; Li et al. 2024a; Hsu et al. 2021)，我们的任务在真实的手稿背景下评估人类发布的数据，形成补充的评估目标。, our task evaluates human-published figures in a real manuscript context, forming a complementary evaluation objective.
