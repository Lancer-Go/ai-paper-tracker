奖励自适应迭代发现Reward-Adaptive Iterative Discovery：NHL26 自动化游戏测试案例研究: A Case Study on Automated Game Testing for NHL26

Florian Fuchs*, Jessy Gosselin-Grant, Boris Skuin, Michele Petteni,
Alessandro Sestini, Joakim Bergdahl, Amir Baghi, Linus Gisslén* Electronic Arts (EA)
{ffuchs,lgisslen}@ea.com
*Corresponding authors
摘要Abstract

测试是游戏行业的一项重大工作，需要很大一部分开发预算和人力。 我们提出了冰球游戏开发版本的案例研究Testing is a major effort for the gaming industry, requiring a significant part of development budget and people power. We present a case study on a development version of the ice hockey gameEA 体育 NHL 26EA SPORTS NHL 26，人类游戏测试人员测试守门员人工智能的行为漏洞。 为了减少开发阶段每次比赛或行为修改后重新测试守门员人工智能的工作量，我们建议, for which human playtesters test the goalie AI for behavioral exploits. To reduce the effort of re-testing the goalie AI after every game or behavior modification in the development phase, we propose奖励自适应迭代发现Reward-Adaptive Iterative Discovery (磁盘阵列RAID），一种使用迭代强化学习（RL）方法自动发现漏洞的新颖方法，该方法训练一群进球得分代理。 虽然以前的方法已经可以成功地找到漏洞，但强化学习算法往往会过度适应单一解决方案。 我们在现有 RL 算法之上引入了一个简单的扩展，以便它们找到多种不同的高质量解决方案。 对于我们第一次部署这种方法，在一次实验中，我们能够找到六种曲棍球得分利用策略，这些策略在质量上与游戏测试人员在长达数小时的手动测试会话中发现的策略相似。), a novel approach to automatically find exploits using an iterative Reinforcement Learning (RL) approach that trains a population of goal scoring agents. While previous approaches can already successfully find exploits, RL algorithms tend to overfit to a single solution. We introduce a simple extension on top of existing RL algorithms, such that they find multiple diverse high-quality solutions. For our first deployment of this approach, within a single experiment we were able to find six hockey scoring exploit strategies that were qualitatively similar to those that playtesters had found in hours-long manual testing sessions.

I 简介Introduction

随着游戏世界变得越来越广阔，游戏系统变得越来越复杂，测试游戏的工作量也随之增加。 为了减少游戏测试的重复性，之前的工作已经研究了通过使用自主代理玩游戏和报告错误来实现部分自动化With game worlds becoming vaster and game systems becoming more complex, the effort to test games grows likewise. To reduce repetitive aspects of game testing, prior work has investigated automating parts of it through the use of autonomous agents playing a game and reporting bugs[1, 6, 4]。 虽然强化学习 (RL) 代理可以在游戏中找到单独的漏洞. While Reinforcement Learning (RL) agents can find individual exploits within a game[2]，他们倾向于收敛到解决任务的一个“最佳”解决方案。 在本文中，我们使用, they tend to converge to one single “best” solution of solving a task. In this paper, we useEA 体育 NHL 26EA SPORTS NHL 26（简称 NHL）作为我们的测试用例。特别是，我们的目标是测试游戏的守门员人工智能，训练前锋寻找高机会得分策略，这可能代表潜在的漏洞。 我们的结果表明，标准强化学习算法往往会崩溃为一小部分高奖励行为，而不是探索更大、更多样化的行为集。 因此，漏洞发现变得有顺序：开发人员必须先解决一个问题，然后再重新训练代理以发现其他问题。 由于这会减慢漏洞发现过程，因此我们的目标是开发一种算法，无需人工干预即可发现多个潜在漏洞。(short NHL) as our test-case. In particular, we aim to test the goalie AI of the game, training a forward agent to find high chance scoring strategies, which could represent potential exploits. Our results indicate that standard RL algorithms tend to collapse to a small set of high-reward behaviors, rather than exploring a larger, more diverse set. Thus, exploit discovery becomes sequential: developers must fix one issue before retraining the agent to find others. Since this slows down the exploit finding process, we aim to develop an algorithm that can find multiple potential exploits without human intervention.

之前用于游戏测试的 RL 工作提出了增加游戏环境覆盖范围的方法Prior work on RL for game testing has proposed methods to increase coverage of a game environment[10, 19]。 然而，对探索的额外偏见是以代理人最初目标的最优性损失为代价的。 对于我们的用例，我们明确寻找高性能的评分策略，因此我们需要一种在评分机会方面实现高性能的方法，同时还可以在无需人工干预的情况下找到多种不同的解决方案。. However, the added bias towards exploration comes at the cost of a loss in optimality with respect to the original goal of the agent. For our use case, we are explicitly looking for high-performing scoring strategies and we therefore require a method that achieves high performance with respect to the scoring chance, while also finding multiple diverse solutions without human intervention.

质量多样性领域研究如何找到高性能的多种多样化解决方案The field of quality diversity investigates how to find multiple diverse solution that are high-performing[23]。虽然他们报告了令人印象深刻的成果，但现有的解决方案在复杂领域往往很脆弱，原因之一是与政策并行学习的不断变化的多样性目标. While they have reported impressive results, existing solutions are often brittle in complex domains, one reason being a moving diversity target learned in parallel to the policies[15]。 我们的目标是让游戏测试者能够独立使用我们的工具，因此希望该工具尽可能简单，不需要 RL 专业知识。. We aim for playtesters to independently use our tool and therefore want the tool to be as simple as possible to require little RL expertise.

根据这些要求，我们提出奖励自适应迭代发现（Following these requirements, we propose Reward-Adaptive Iterative Discovery (磁盘阵列RAID），一种通过迭代训练代理群体来找到一组高质量、多样化解决方案的方法。 图), an approach to find a set of high-quality, diverse solutions by iteratively training a population of agents. Figure1显示该方法结果的简要概述。 我们通过不奖励与任何先前训练的智能体发现的策略相似的策略来强化多样性。 通过测量顺序训练的智能体的多样性，并在单个智能体的训练期间保持多样性测量固定，与之前的工作相比，我们提高了鲁棒性。 我们以直观的、特定领域的方式定义多样性，以便非强化学习从业者可以解释和调整多样性度量，从而促进游戏测试人员使用来扩展他们的工具集。shows a brief overview of the results of the approach. We enforce diversity through not rewarding agents for strategies that are similar to those found by any previously trained agent. By measuring diversity over sequentially trained agents, leaving the diversity measure fixed during an individual agent’s training time, we increase robustness compared to previous work. We define diversity in an intuitive, domain-specific way, such that the diversity measure can be interpreted and adjusted by non-RL practitioners, facilitating the use by playtesters to extend their toolset.

II 相关工作Related Work

我们展示了与我们的方法相关的两个研究方向的相关工作：（1）自动游戏测试，特别是基于强化学习的方法，以及（2）行为多样性领域，这激发了我们的方法。We present related work in two research directions connected to our approach: (1) automated playtesting, specifically approaches based on reinforcement learning, and (2) the field of behavior diversity, which motivates our approach.

Ⅱ-AII-A 自动游戏测试Automated Playtesting

与最近应用强化学习来训练代理掌握游戏并超越人类玩家的研究相反Contrary to recent work that apply reinforcement learning to train agents to master a game and surpass human players[21, 22]，自动游戏测试的研究通常不太关心最大化与人类玩家相同的成功指标，而是更关注让代理以各种方式玩游戏，从而揭示对游戏中潜在问题的见解。 一个方向是最大化代理在游戏中访问的状态的覆盖范围。 一个值得注意的例子是, research on automated playtesting is generally less concerned with maximizing the same success metrics as human players, and more focused on having agents play a game in various ways that reveal insights on potential issues in the game. One direction for this is to maximize the coverage of states an agent visits in a game. A notable example is the work byGordillo et al. [10]，它使用基于计数的探索奖励方案来促进对游戏地图中较少出现的状态的访问。 另一个方向旨在训练智能体玩与人类玩家类似的游戏行为，例如可视化游戏设计师可以期望从用户那里得到的交互，或者平衡游戏中不同策略的有效性, that uses a count-based exploration reward scheme to promote visitation of less seen states in a game map. Another direction aims to train agents that play games with behavior similar to human players, for example to visualize the interactions a game designer can expect from users or to balance the effectiveness of different strategies within a game[13, 6, 8]. Sestini et al. [19]通过探索类似于人类玩家的策略来结合两个方向，并为探索新状态提供额外的内在奖励。combine both directions by exploring strategies similar to those of human players with an additional intrinsic reward for exploring new states.

虽然之前的工作在游戏状态覆盖和类似玩家的行为方面取得了良好的结果，但我们的目标是调查与游戏的原始人类成功指标的高性能密切相关的漏洞利用，在我们的例子中，在曲棍球比赛中进球率很高。 因此，我们的目标是开发一种方法来增加训练有素的智能体行为的多样性，同时尽可能地保持游戏成功指标的性能。While prior work achieves good results in terms of game state coverage and player-like behavior, we aim to investigate exploits that are closely tied to high performance with respect to the original human success metric of a game, in our case scoring goals with a high percentage in a hockey game. We therefore aim to develop an approach to increase the diversity in behaviors of the trained agents while preserving the performance with respect to the game’s success metric as much as possible.

V 结论、局限性和未来的工作Conclusion, Limitations, and Future Work

我们介绍We introduce奖励自适应迭代发现Reward-Adaptive Iterative Discovery，一种在迭代之间无需人工干预即可找到多种不同高机会评分策略的方法。 该算法通过顺序训练多个智能体来扩展标准强化学习设置，旨在让每个智能体通过不奖励与之前迭代类似的策略来提出新策略。 正如领域专家对所学策略的回顾所示，, a method to find multiple diverse high chance scoring strategies without human intervention in between iterations. The algorithm extends a standard reinforcement learning setup by training multiple agents sequentially, aiming for each agent to come up with a new strategy by not rewarding agents for strategies similar to those of previous iterations. As a domain expert’s review of the learned strategies shows,磁盘阵列RAID发现与人类游戏测试人员发现的类似的漏洞。 我们进一步认为，与之前需要 RL 专业知识来解释超参数以调整多样性的工作相比，定义 NHL 的多样性可以让游戏测试人员更好地遵循和调整该方法。finds exploits similar to those identified by human playtesters. We furthermore argue that defining diversity with respect to NHL allows game testers to better follow and adjust the method, compared to prior work that requires RL expertise to interpret the hyperparameters for tuning diversity.

局限性。Limitations.虽然我们的方法可以自主发现潜在的漏洞，但它仍然需要训练后的人工监督，因为该方法未涵盖区分游戏设计者意图的高机会得分策略和实际漏洞。 此外，由于强化学习的探索过程存在很大差异，我们的方法不足以确定工程师是否成功修复了发现的漏洞，需要依靠游戏测试人员来完成此验证任务。While our method finds potential exploits autonomously, it still requires human supervision post training, since telling high chance scoring strategies intended by game designers and actual exploits apart is not covered by the method. Furthermore, due to the high variance in RL’s exploration process, our method is not sufficient to determine whether a found exploit has been fixed successfully by an engineer, relying on playtesters for this verification task.

我们方法的运行时间（至少）随着我们想要找到的策略数量线性增长。 虽然之前的工作并行训练多种不同的行为Our method’s runtime grows (at least) linearly with the number of strategies we aim to find. While previous work trains multiple diverse behaviors in parallel[23]，我们故意放弃这个选项，以保持我们的方法简单，从而方便不熟悉 RL 的游戏测试人员使用它。, we deliberately forego this option to keep our method simple and therefore facilitate its use by playtesters not familiar with RL.

未来的工作。Future Work.我们设计的部分We design parts of the磁盘阵列RAID专门用于 NHL 的方法，尽管算法中概述了基本思想method specifically for the use on NHL, though the base idea outlined in Algorithm1也可以适用于其他领域。 虽然在 NHL 进球领域定义多样性有些直观，但未来的工作可以研究如何在更复杂的领域定义多样性，包括多人场景，甚至是输出文本作为动作的代理。 虽然以前以与领域无关的方式定义多样性的工作通常需要 RL 知识来解释和调整超参数，但研究与领域无关但仍可由非 RL 从业者解释的多样性定义将会很有趣，例如通过定义could be applicable to other domains as well. While diversity is somewhat intuitive to define for the domain of scoring goals in NHL, future work could research how diversity can be defined in more complex domains, including multiplayer scenarios or even agents outputting text as actions. While previous work that defines diversity in a domain-agnostic way often requires RL knowledge to interpret and adjust hyperparameters, it would be interesting to research definitions of diversity that are domain-agnostic but still interpretable by non-RL practitioners, for example by defining the minimum distance of trajectories used in磁盘阵列RAID以更一般的方式。in a more general way.
