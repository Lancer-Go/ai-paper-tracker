摘要。Abstract.对于城市管理者和设计师来说，面对复杂性和不确定性，改善城市社区的功能属性、增强地域韧性至关重要。目前，社区规划往往遵循自上而下的方式，缺乏有效的指标来量化居民的非正式行为，导致与原规划经常发生冲突。本研究介绍了 CommuniWave，这是一种机器学习模型，旨在有效检测和量化城市社区的非正式行为程度 (DIB)。该模型集成了基于mmaction2的行为捕获网络（BCN）、自主开发的YOLOv10模型（YLX）和使用随机森林的行为评估模型（BEM）。最终，该模型通过街道视频生成DIB波动图，促进动态监测，支持城市管理者做出精细化决策，增强社区的整体弹性。For urban managers and designers, improving the functional attributes of urban communities to enhance territorial resilience in the face of complexity and uncertainty is crucial. Currently, community planning often follows a top-down approach and lacks effective metrics to quantify informal behaviors of residents, leading to frequent conflicts with original plans. This study introduces CommuniWave, a machine learning model designed to efficiently detect and quantify the Degree of Informal Behavior (DIB) in urban communities. The model integrates a Behavior Capture Net (BCN) based on mmaction2, a self-developed YOLOv10 model (YLX), and a Behavior Eval Model (BEM) using random forest. Ultimately, by generating DIB fluctuation charts from street videos, the model facilitates dynamic monitoring, supporting urban managers in making refined decisions to enhance the overall resilience of communities.

一、简介1. Introduction

领土复原力是可持续发展的跨学科概念（Brunetta et al., 2019），强调决策者需要采取灵活、主动的方法来应对特定空间内的风险和挑战。在城市社区中，居民自发的自下而上的活动代表着不确定因素。然而，对这些活动的管理方法往往缺乏可持续的理解。以往的研究表明，自下而上和自上而下相结合的社区管理模式有助于减少冲突，从而促进可持续的领土复原力（Semeraro等，2020）。Territorial resilience is an interdisciplinary concept of sustainable development(Brunetta et al., 2019), emphasizing the need for decisionmakers to adopt flexible and proactive approaches in addressing risks and challenges within a given space. In urban communities, spontaneous bottomup activities by residents represent uncertain factors. However, management approaches toward these activities often lack sustainable understanding. Previous studies have shown that a community management model combining bottom-up and top-down approaches helps reduce conflicts, thereby promoting sustainable territorial resilience(Semeraro et al., 2020).

城市社区的非正式行为是一种非正式现象，其研究起源可以追溯到经济学家基思·哈特(Keith Hart)对非正式部门的讨论(Hart，1985)。在社区中，政府主导的城市规划与居民日常需求的脱节是非正式行为出现的主要原因（Anon.，2018）。这些行为通常表现为自发的、针对具体情况的反应，例如随机出现的街头摊位或自发形成的聚集场所（Chase、Crawford 和 Kaliski，2008）。社区居民的这些非正式行为往往伴随着快速的城市扩张和不平衡的土地政策，反映出基础设施供需失衡的问题(Frederic Deng and Huang，2004)。Informal behavior in urban communities is an informal phenomenon whose research origins can be traced back to economist Keith Hart's discussion of the Informal Sector(Hart, 1985). In communities, the disconnect between government-led urban planning and the daily needs of residents is the main reason for the emergence of informal behaviors(Anon., 2018). These behaviors often manifest as spontaneous, context-specific responses, such as randomly appearing street stalls or spontaneously formed gathering places(Chase, Crawford and Kaliski, 2008). These informal behaviors of community residents often accompany rapid urban expansion and unbalanced land policies, reflecting issues of imbalance between infrastructure supply and demand(Frederic Deng and Huang, 2004).

学者普遍认为这些自发活动对于社区生活具有积极意义。简·雅各布斯（Jane Jacobs）在《美国大城市的死与生》中强调，城市规划应该为社区生活中的“休闲公共互动”创造条件（Jacobs，1992）； Jan Geh指出，街道空间的活力取决于日常生活中的社会互动（Gehl，1971）；玛格丽特·克劳福德认为，城市管理者需要理解并接受街头小贩和无家可归者的“反公众”行为所带来的视觉“混乱”（Crawford，1995）。这些行为通过居民的生活体验不断重塑和重新定义公共空间和领域，促进邻里活动的多样性和活力（Mehta和Bosson，2021）。Scholars generally believe that these spontaneous activities have positive significance for community life. Jane Jacobs, in The Death and Life of Great American Cities, emphasized that urban planning should create conditions for “casual public interactions” in community life(Jacobs, 1992); Jan Geh pointed out that the vitality of street spaces depends on social interactions in daily life(Gehl, 1971); Margaret Crawford argued that urban managers need to understand and accept the visual "disorder" brought about by the "counter-publics" actions of street vendors and the homeless(Crawford, 1995). Through residents' life experiences, these behaviors continuously reshape and redefine public spaces and domains, promoting the diversity and vitality of neighborhood activities(Mehta and Bosson, 2021).

在当代社会，城市管理者已经开始尝试在合规的框架内引导这些自下而上的自发行为（Yao等，2024）。在中国北京，引入了专门的社区责任规划师（CDP）来协调邻里问题（Zhou et al., 2023）。这种自上而下的沟通公众参与模式也被应用于中国南京的城市社区治理中（曹，2022）。然而，这些模型的评估往往依赖于决策者和规划者的经验，缺乏有效的量化手段。In contemporary society, urban managers have begun to try to guide these bottom-up spontaneous behaviors within a compliant framework (Yao et al., 2024). In Beijing, China, dedicated community duty planners (CDPs) have been introduced to coordinate neighborhood issues(Zhou et al., 2023). This top-down communication public participation model has also been applied in the governance of urban communities in Nanjing, China(Cao, 2022). However, the evaluation of these models often relies on the experience of decision-makers and planners, lacking effective quantitative measures.

一些新兴的计算调查工具正在帮助设计师评估和量化城市中的人类活动。例如，语义和时空聚类方法通过智能手机 Twitter 签到数据分析人群移动模式（Steiger et al., 2015）；移动数据提供的居住位置信息可以比较不同城市人群的活动范围、锚点数量、移动频率等方面的差异(Xu et al., 2018)；通过将 POI 分类与自动注释算法相结合，还可以预测活动类型（Furletti et al., 2013）。然而，这些定量分析方法对于收集城市非正式行为数据并不实用，因为研究对象的行为多种多样，数据来源分散，难以全面反映街头非正式行为的整体情况。Some emerging computational survey tools are helping designers assess and quantify human activity in cities. For example, semantic and spatiotemporal clustering methods analyze crowd movement patterns through smartphone Twitter check-in data(Steiger et al., 2015); residence location information provided by mobile data can compare the differences in activity range, anchor point quantity, and movement frequency among populations in different cities(Xu et al., 2018); by combining POI classification with automatic annotation algorithms, activity types can also be predicted(Furletti et al., 2013). However, these quantitative analysis methods are not practical for collecting data on informal behaviors in cities, as the behavior of the research subjects is diverse and the data sources are fragmented, making it difficult to fully reflect the overall informal behavior on the streets.

机器学习的最新发展使研究人员能够通过低成本视频和照片记录的高精度分析来提高数据收集效率。 Hou等人使用深度卷积神经网络来分析视频中的人类行为并量化小型公共空间的使用情况（Hou et al., 2020）； Li 等人通过使用深度学习模型分析监控摄像头捕获的行为来评估不同街道的活力（Li、Yabuki 和 Fukuda，2022）。尽管这些先前的研究有效地识别了人类的活动和偏好，但由于城市社区非正式行为的复杂性，学者们仍然需要开发更合适的分类模型和工作流程。Recent developments in machine learning have enabled researchers to improve data collection efficiency through high-precision analysis of lowcost video and photo records. Hou and others used deep convolutional neural networks to analyze human behavior in videos and quantify the use of small public spaces(Hou et al., 2020); Li and others assessed the vitality of different streets by analyzing behaviors captured by surveillance cameras using deep learning models(Li, Yabuki and Fukuda, 2022). Although these previous studies effectively identified human activities and preferences, due to the complexity of informal behaviors in urban communities, scholars still need to develop more suitable classification models and workflows.

本文介绍了一种基于机器学习的模型，能够快速识别空间中的非正式行为程度（DIB）并对其进行评分。与传统工作流程相比，该方法提高了城市设计师的研究效率和精准管理能力，推进了基于城市社区的地域韧性建设进程。This paper introduces a machine learning-based model capable of rapidly identifying the Degree of Informal Behavior (DIB) in spaces and scoring them. Compared to traditional workflows, this approach enhances the research efficiency and precision management capabilities of urban designers, advancing the process of territorial resilience construction based on urban communities.

2.1 数据收集2.1 DATA COLLECTION

CommuniWave 模型的识别和训练过程依赖于手动注释的数据集。首先，在训练边界元法时，由于不同城市空间对人们的吸引力不同，居民对不同街道层面的非正式行为的判断标准也不同，因此需要对各类街道空间进行详细分析。我们收集了三种类型道路的 180 个视频片段作为训练数据集，包括： 1. 允许机动车和非机动车行驶的街道（一级道路，60 段）； 2、禁止机动车通行的街道（二级路段，60路段）； 3.只允许行人通行的街道（三级道路，60段）。The identification and training process of the CommuniWave model relies on manually annotated datasets. First, when training the BEM, due to the varying attractiveness of different urban spaces to people, residents have different standards for determining informal behaviors on different street levels Therefore, we need to conduct a detailed analysis of various types of street spaces. We collected 180 video segments from three types of roads as the training dataset, including: 1. Streets that allow motor vehicles and nonmotorized vehicles (First-class road, 60 segments); 2. Streets that do not allow motor vehicles (Second-class road, 60 segments); 3. Streets that only allow pedestrian traffic (Third-class road, 60 segments).

由于当前硬件和算法的限制，无论提供多大的训练数据集，模型都无法完全克服地理和文化的限制。此外，大规模模型在训练和推理阶段都面临着巨大的计算需求。在比较了不同解决方案的性价比后，我们选择了较小的数据集和模型。较小的模型提供较低的推理成本并且更容易训练。我们还旨在提供一个更加灵活和可定制的框架，使研究人员能够根据其特定任务中的独特行为进行量身定制的培训。对于YLX，我们选择了高效的YOLOv10框架，并开放了训练接口，使得研究人员可以通过提供自己的数据集轻松训练自己的YLX版本。研究人员还可以重新训练 BEM 以适应特定群体的非正式行为的评分。因此，CommuniWave能够更好地满足不同社区管理者的需求。为了解决与公共场所视频数据收集相关的隐私问题，Due to limitations in current hardware and algorithms, no matter how large the training dataset provided, the model cannot entirely overcome geographical and cultural constraints. Moreover, large-scale models face significant computational demands during both the training and inference stages. After comparing the performance-to-cost ratios of different solutions, we opted for a smaller dataset and model. A smaller model offers lower inference costs and is easier to train. We also aim to provide a more flexible and customizable framework, allowing researchers to conduct tailored training based on the unique behaviors in their specific tasks. For YLX, we selected the highly efficient YOLOv10 framework and opened the training interface, enabling researchers to easily train their own YLX version by providing their own dataset. Researchers can also retrain the BEM to adapt to the scoring of informal behaviors specific to certain groups. Consequently, CommuniWave can better meet the needs of different community managers. 2.2 参与者2.2 PARTICIPANTS

本研究严格遵循伦理标准和法律规定，招募10名志愿者（男6例，女4例，平均年龄31.0±13.89岁）。所有志愿者都签署了书面知情同意书，允许匿名使用他们的评级数据。志愿者均为城市社区居民，熟悉社区生活，确保能够准确评估视频内容中的非正式行为程度。调查包括180个视频片段，志愿者被要求对每个片段进行1到5的评分，分数代表：“1 - 无临时活动；2 - 少量临时活动；3 - 中等水平的临时活动；4 - 重要的临时活动；5 - 非常激烈的临时活动。”为了保证评分的客观性，视频播放顺序是随机的，并且对志愿者评分没有时间限制。为了整合所有志愿者的评分，同时考虑少数意见的敏感性并降低模型训练的复杂性，我们使用平均值（四舍五入到小数点后两位）作为每个视频的非正式行为得分（标签 y）。为了避免将志愿者的偏见和主观性引入我们的预测模型中，我们采用中值绝对偏差（MAD）方法（Xu et al., 2020）来过滤和消除志愿者评分中具有显着偏差的极端异常值。此外，我们还编制了一份具有不同背景的志愿者名单，确保每组分数都是由来自不同教育水平、年龄组和性别的志愿者提供的。这种方法有助于防止任何特定人口群体的偏见和主观性的影响。This study strictly adhered to ethical standards and legal regulations, recruiting 10 volunteers (6 males and 4 females, with an average age of 31.0 ± 13.89 years). All volunteers signed written informed consent forms, allowing the anonymous use of their rating data. The volunteers were all residents of urban communities and were familiar with community life, ensuring that they could accurately assess the level of informal behavior in the video content. The survey included 180 video clips, and volunteers were asked to rate each clip on a scale of 1 to 5, with the scores representing: "1 - No temporary activities; 2 - Slightly some temporary activities; 3 - Moderate level of temporary activities; 4 - Significant temporary activities; 5 - Very intense temporary activities." To ensure objectivity in the ratings, the order of video playback was randomized, and no time limit was imposed on the volunteers for rating. To integrate all volunteer ratings while considering the sensitivity of minority opinions and reducing the complexity of model training, we used the mean value, rounded to two decimal places, as the informal behavior score (label y) for each video. To avoid introducing bias and subjectivity from volunteers into our predictive model, we adopted the Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) method (Xu et al., 2020) to filter out and eliminate extreme outliers with significant bias in volunteer ratings. Additionally, we compiled a diverse list of volunteers with varied backgrounds, ensuring that each set of scores is provided by volunteers from different educational levels, age groups, and genders. This approach helps to prevent the influence of bias and subjectivity from any particular demographic group.

2.5 行为评估模型（BEM)2.5 BEHAVIOR EVAL MODEL（BEM)

我们采用综合方法来训练和优化随机森林回归模型，即 BEM。首先，BCN 中的 mmaction2 和 YLX 用于推断和识别我们的 cw_set 数据集。 BCN中识别的特征参数组合被用作BEM的输入参数“bem_set”，以及该组特征参数对应的街道级别标签，以训练模型对不同级别街道的目标预测能力。最终输出是街道上非正式行为程度的得分。We employed a comprehensive approach to train and optimize a random forest regression model, namely BEM. First, the mmaction2 and YLX in BCN were used to infer and recognize our cw_set dataset. The combination of feature parameters identified in BCN was used as the input parameter "bem_set" for BEM, along with the corresponding street level labels for that set of feature parameters, to train the model's targeted predictive ability for different levels of streets. The final output is the score for the degree of informal behavior on the street.

2.5.1 预处理2.5.1 Preprocessing

我们使用 PCA（主成分分析）（Pearson，1901）来降低 cw_set 数据集中低频或不显着特征的维度，通过解释的方差比图确定最佳维度。这个过程保留了足够的信息，同时减少了特征的数量。数据集按 8:2 的比例分为训练集和测试集，推断视频中不存在的行为特征统一设置为 0。We used PCA (Principal Component Analysis)(Pearson, 1901) to reduce the dimensionality of low-frequency or insignificant features in the cw_set dataset, determining the optimal dimensions through the explained variance ratio plot. This process retains enough information while reducing the number of features. The dataset was split into a training set and a test set at an 8:2 ratio, with behavior features not present in the inferred videos uniformly set to 0.

2.5.2 训练与优化2.5.2 Training and Optimization

随后，我们使用随机森林算法构建边界元法，通过多棵决策树的集成提高预测精度。每棵树独立地提供预测，最终预测是通过聚合所有树预测来获得的，通常通过多数投票或平均来确定。然后，我们通过随机搜索和RandomizedSearchCV优化模型参数，例如树的数量、最大特征数和深度，以找到最佳配置，降低过拟合的风险，提高模型精度。Subsequently, we used the random forest algorithm to build the BEM, improving prediction accuracy through the integration of multiple decision trees. Each tree independently provided a prediction, and the final prediction was obtained by aggregating all tree predictions, typically determined by majority voting or averaging. Then, we optimized the model parameters, such as the number of trees, maximum number of features, and depth, through random search and RandomizedSearchCV to find the best configuration, reduce the risk of overfitting, and improve model accuracy.

2.5.3 模型管理2.5.3 Model Management

我们利用 Databricks 在 https://github.com/mlflow/mlflow 发布的 mlflow 框架来管理实验过程，包括参数选择、模型训练和评估。通过mlflow，我们可以跟踪实验进度，比较不同参数组合的性能，并确定最佳配置。通过将随机搜索与mlflow相结合，大大提高了实验效率和重现性。在训练和评估过程中，我们使用 matplotlib 可视化 PCA 解释的方差比，使用 sklearn 评估模型性能，使用 joblib 保存最终模型以供以后部署。We utilized the mlflow framework, released by Databricks at https://github.com/mlflow/mlflow, to manage the experimental process, including parameter selection, model training, and evaluation. Through mlflow, we could track experiment progress, compare the performance of different parameter combinations, and determine the optimal configuration. By combining random search with mlflow, experimental efficiency and reproducibility were greatly enhanced. During training and evaluation, we used matplotlib to visualize the explained variance ratio from PCA, sklearn to assess model performance, and joblib to save the final model for later deployment.

四、讨论4.Discussion

4.1 结果总结4.1 SUMMARY OF RESULTS

特征重要性分析表明，不同类型的非正式行为对城市环境有不同的影响。正如预期的那样，居民的非正式街头售货是导致不同城市社区 DIB 值较高的主要因素之一。这一发现与之前的研究一致，之前的研究强调街头小贩是非正规城市经济的重要组成部分（Onodugo 等，2016）。然而，虽然自动售货活动在不同类型街道的 DIB 中发挥着重要作用，但每个街道类别都表现出独特的特征。例如，三轮车在高速公路上行驶、停放、行人过马路等扰乱交通秩序的行为，都是一级道路上的显着特征，符合城市交通安全法规。同时，广场舞等人群聚集活动在二级道路的特征重要性中凸显，体现了居民对自发聚集空间的偏好（肖、惠、文，2020）。值得注意的是，居民对三级道路上广场舞等活动的容忍度有所提高，进一步揭示了人们评价非正式行为的标准与不同的城市环境之间的密切关系。The feature importance analysis indicates that different types of informal behaviors have varying impacts on urban environments. As expected, informal street vending by residents is one of the primary factors contributing to higher DIB values in different urban neighborhoods. This finding aligns with previous studies, which highlight street vendors as a significant component of the informal urban economy(Onodugo et al., 2016). However, while vending activities play a prominent role in the DIB across different types of streets, each street class exhibits unique characteristics. For example, the driving and parking of tricycles on motorways, as well as pedestrians crossing roads, disrupt traffic order and are notable features on first-class roads, which aligns with urban traffic safety regulations. Meanwhile, activities involving crowd gatherings, such as square dancing, are prominent in the feature importance of second-class roads, reflecting residents' preference for spontaneous gathering spaces(Xiao, Hui and Wen, 2020). Notably, residents show increased tolerance for activities like square dancing on third-class roads, further revealing the close relationship between the standards by which people evaluate informal behaviors and the varying urban environments.

4.2 限制4.2 LIMITATIONS

由于城市社区非正式行为的随机性和不可预测性，BCN无法完全识别所有类别的非正式行为。这可能会导致视频评分任务期间出现异常。这意味着对于不同地区或街道环境、社会文化背景不同，BCN的标签库无法实现完全覆盖，需要收集定制数据集进行二次训练。此外，虽然BCN在街道非正式行为识别任务中表现良好，但它通常只能识别图像（视频）中的表面特征，缺乏理解图像更深层次语义内容的能力（例如，模型无法区分停在人行道上的汽车和停在指定停车位上的汽车）。这些问题可能会导致 BEM 在极少数情况下错过某些关键行为特征，从而导致对非正式行为程度的错误预测。而且，由于mmaction和YOLOv10模型无法完美处理任何输入格式的视频，这可能会导致程序崩溃。然而，研究人员可以审查 BCN 输出的数据，如果发现任何差异，可以进行手动更正，以减少 BCN 识别错误的影响。（Das et al., 2019）Due to the randomness and unpredictability of informal behaviors in urban communities, BCN cannot fully identify all categories of informal behaviors. This may result in anomalies during video scoring tasks. It means that for different regions or street environments with varying socio-cultural contexts, BCN's label library cannot achieve complete coverage, necessitating the collection of customized datasets for secondary training. Additionally, although BCN performs well in street informal behavior recognition tasks, it typically only identifies surface features in images (videos) and lacks the ability to understand the deeper semantic content of the images (for example, the model cannot distinguish between a car parked on a sidewalk and one parked in a designated parking space). These issues may cause BEM to miss certain critical behavioral features in rare cases, leading to incorrect predictions of the degree of informal behavior. Moreover, since the mmaction and YOLOv10 models cannot perfectly handle videos in any input format, this may cause the program to crash. However, researchers can review the data output by BCN, and if any discrepancies are found, manual corrections can be applied to reduce the impact of recognition errors from BCN.(Das et al., 2019)

4.3 结论4.3 Conclusions

管理城市社区非正式行为的主要挑战包括定义自发行为并灵活评估它们（Kapsalis 和 Kapsalis，2020）。 CommuniWave 通过为城市设计师提供基于街道视频数据预测 DIB 的机器学习框架，有效解决了这两个问题。利用mmaction2和YOLOv10强大的时空动作检测能力，模型内的BCN快速识别社区中的各种自发行为。与传统的实地研究方法相比，这显着提高了统计效率。The main challenges in managing informal behaviors in urban communities include defining spontaneous behaviors and assessing them flexibly (Kapsalis and Kapsalis, 2020). CommuniWave effectively addresses both issues by providing urban designers with a machine learning framework that predicts the DIB based on street video data. Leveraging the powerful spatiotemporal action detection capabilities of mmaction2 and YOLOv10, the BCN within the model quickly identifies various spontaneous behaviors in the community. Compared to traditional field research methods, this significantly improves statistical efficiency.

这使得城市管理部门能够灵活管理城市社区复杂的自发行为，避免因高压管理方式引发的冲突和纠纷，增强区域的地域韧性。此外，该模型的动态 DIB 分数直观地反映了捕获时间段内该位置内的行为波动。城市管理者可以根据 DIB 实施不同的措施，解决过去城市管理中的公平问题，其中非正式行为往往受到过度监管或监管不足（Lane 和 McDonald，2005）。值得注意的是，允许在指定区域内进行非正式行为满足了普通居民的日常需求。这种方式不仅满足了自上而下的管理要求，而且提高了居民的参与度和幸福感（Dias等，2018），有利于可持续的城市社区环境。This allows urban management departments to flexibly manage complex spontaneous behaviors in urban communities, avoiding conflicts and disputes caused by heavy-handed management approaches and enhancing the region’s territorial resilience. Additionally, the model’s dynamic DIB score intuitively reflects behavioral fluctuations within the location during the captured time period. Urban managers can implement different measures according to the DIB, addressing past fairness issues in urban management, where informal behaviors were often over- or under-regulated (Lane and McDonald, 2005). Notably, permitting informal behaviors within designated areas meets the daily needs of ordinary residents. This approach not only satisfies the top-down management requirements but also increases residents’ participation and happiness (Dias et al., 2018), facilitating a sustainable urban community environment.

此外，该模型具有适应性。城市管理部门可以根据数据收集方法（见2.1数据收集）对模型进行预训练，以适应不同城市和社区环境下的自发行为。因此，该模型在不损害自发社区行动活力的情况下增强了规范非正式行为的能力，为创建更具适应性和弹性的城市社区提供了强有力的数据支持和决策指导。这种数据驱动的方法不仅提高了管理灵活性，还减轻了管理过度或管理不足造成的不平等，有助于增强社区面对未来不确定性的整体弹性。通过将自上而下的规划要求与自下而上的居民需求相结合，该模型为可持续城市发展奠定了基础。Furthermore, the model is adaptable. Urban management departments can pre-train the model based on the data collection methods (see 2.1 Data Collection) to suit the spontaneous behaviors in different urban and community environments. As a result, the model enhances the ability to regulate informal behaviors without undermining the vitality of spontaneous community actions, providing robust data support and decision-making guidance for creating more adaptive and resilient urban communities. This data-driven approach not only improves management flexibility but also mitigates the inequities caused by over- or under-management, helping to strengthen the overall resilience of communities in the face of future uncertainties. By integrating top-down planning requirements with bottomup resident needs, the model lays the groundwork for sustainable urban development.
