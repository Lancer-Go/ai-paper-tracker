1 简介Introduction

Lindsey et al. (2025)报道了一个惊人的发现：当 Claude 3.5 Haiku 写押韵诗歌时，候选押韵词的跨层转码器 (CLT) 功能会在行间的换行标记处预先激活，这是一个“规划站点”，模型在生成导致押韵的单词之前决定押韵结尾。 Suppress + inject 对这些功能的干预会重定向线路结尾，但仅当应用于规划站点时；对其他标记位置的相同干预没有效果（“论大语言模型的生物学”的图 13）。 CLT 是应用于变压器激活的稀疏字典学习的后代reported a striking finding: when Claude 3.5 Haiku writes rhyming poetry, cross-layer transcoder (CLT) features for candidate rhyme words pre-activate at the newline token between lines, a “planning site” where the model decides the rhyme ending before generating the words that lead to it. Suppress + inject interventions on these features redirect the line ending, but only when applied at the planning site; the same intervention at other token positions has no effect (Figure 13 of “On the Biology of a Large Language Model”). CLTs are descendants of sparse dictionary learning applied to transformer activations(Bricken et al., 2023; Templeton et al., 2024; Cunningham et al., 2023)：它们分解残差流（作为变压器主要通信通道的层输出的运行总和；: they decompose the residual stream (the running sum of layer outputs that serves as the transformer’s main communication channel;Elhage et al., 2021）转化为可解释的、单语义的特征，可以单独抑制或注入。) into interpretable, monosemantic features that can be individually suppressed or injected.

这一发现在具有专有权重的专有模型上使用专有的可解释性工具得到了证明。外部研究人员无法检查模型或在同一系统上重现实验。在使用独立构建的基础设施进行独立训练的模型上进行复制之前，它仍然是单个实验室的观察结果。与此同时，神经网络规划的机制研究主要集中在训练搜索上This finding was demonstrated on a proprietary model, with proprietary weights, using proprietary interpretability tools. 我们称这个主题为We term this motif预产期prolepsis（希腊语：预期）：变压器在早期层做出决策的结构主题，而注意力路由头则维持对输出的承诺而不进行校正。 Prolepsis 具有三个属性：早期承诺、通过路由头持续传播（在任务相关的网络深度）以及不可撤销性：没有层可以逆转已提交的答案，即使它是错误的。(Greek: anticipation): the structural motif in which a transformer commits to a decision at early layers, and attention routing heads sustain that commitment to the output without correction. Prolepsis has three properties: early commitment, sustained propagation through routing heads (at task-dependent network depths), and irrevocability: no layer reverses the committed answer, even when it is wrong.
