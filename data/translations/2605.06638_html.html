1 简介Introduction

用于大语言模型 (LLM) 推理的强化学习 (RL) 的最新进展表明，数学和编码等可验证领域的训练可以引发增强的推理行为并提高基准性能Recent advances in reinforcement learning (RL) for large language model (LLM) reasoning have shown that training on verifiable domains such as mathematics and coding can elicit enhanced reasoning behavior and improve benchmark performance(Guo et al., 2025a; Jaech et al., 2024)。 然而，这些收益尚未转化为强大的长视野推理，因为一旦任务需要许多顺序推理步骤，同一模型的性能就会急剧下降，即使相应的子问题是单独可管理的. However, these gains do not yet translate into robust long-horizon reasoning, as the same model’s performance can degrade sharply once tasks require many sequential reasoning steps, even when the corresponding subproblems are individually manageable(Rameshkumar et al., 2025; Motwani et al., 2025; Lu et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025).

一个关键的限制是，可扩展的长视野推理训练同时需要三个属性：精确的可验证性、对推理难度的细粒度控制以及支持系统分析的大规模可用数据。当前推动强化学习进步的领域，例如数学和编码，通常满足第一个属性，但不满足后两个属性：高质量问题的策划成本高昂，并且只能对范围和难度提供有限的控制A key limitation is that scalable long-horizon reasoning training requires three properties simultaneously: exact verifiability, fine-grained control over reasoning difficulty, and data available at scale that supports systematic analysis. The domains currently driving RL progress, such as mathematics and coding, often satisfy the first property but not the latter two: high-quality problems are expensive to curate and offer only limited control over horizon and difficulty(Hendrycks et al., 2021; Jain et al., 2024)。现有的替代方案，包括综合任务. Existing alternatives, including synthetic tasks(Xie et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025a)和自我进化的管道and self-evolving pipelines(Zhao et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025)，通过自动生成和验证来降低数据收集的成本，但通常不能提供足够干净且可解释的控制轴集来分析缩放行为。 因此，我们仍然缺乏一个受控的训练环境来研究强化学习如何应对日益困难的长期推理问题。 表, reduce the cost of data collection through automatic generation and verification, but often do not provide a sufficiently clean and interpretable set of control axes for analyzing scaling behavior.
