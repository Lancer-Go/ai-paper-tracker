TimeLens：设备上的工件识别TimeLens: On-Device Artifact Recognition with
检索增强问答Retrieval-Augmented Question Answering
为大埃及博物馆for the Grand Egyptian Museum

1st Rawan Hesham    2nd Ali Ashraf    3rd Amr Ahmed    4th Malak Alaa    5th Omar Ahmed    6th Omar Wagih
摘要Abstract

TimeLens 是大埃及博物馆 (GEM) 的人工智能双语移动指南。 参观者将手机对准展品，即可看到实时识别的文物 并可以提出后续问题，并用英语或阿拉伯语回答。工作地址 画廊内部署特有的三个问题： 之间的细粒度视觉相似性 51 件编目文物（许多几乎相同的拉美西斯雕像），之间的差距 精心策划的训练数据和手持相机条件，以及人工智能指南的风险 陈述未经证实的历史事实。报告了两项工程贡献。 首先，通过数据质量驱动的方法开发了设备上的伪影检测器 迭代研究——从基础模型自动注释（YOLO-World），到 空间标签清理规则，到完全手工注释的数据集——隔离标签 质量是决定性因素：最终的 YOLOv8n 模型解决了之前的所有问题 课程失败，但仍保留实时运行的 5.97 MB TensorFlow Lite 资产 在中端手机上（mAP@0.5 = 0.995，mAP@0.5:0.95 = 0.924）。二、双语 检索增强生成 (RAG) 指南，以 108 条记录 ChromaDB 为基础 知识库，使用 Gemma 4 对七种候选语言模型进行了基准测试 选择E2B（Q4_K_M）；十项有针对性的优化可减少端到端延迟 超过 30 秒至约 10 秒。两个子系统都集成在一个生产中 具有双语界面、博物馆位置门控的 Flutter 应用程序，以及 文本转语音支持。TimeLens is an AI-powered bilingual mobile guide for the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM). Pointing a phone at an exhibit, a visitor sees the artifact recognized in real time and can ask follow-up questions answered in English or Arabic. The work addresses three problems specific to in-gallery deployment: fine-grained visual similarity among 51 catalogued artifacts (many near-identical Ramesside statues), the gap between curated training data and handheld camera conditions, and the risk of an AI guide stating unsupported historical facts. Two engineering contributions are reported. First, an on-device artifact detector was developed through a data-quality-driven iteration study — from foundation-model auto-annotation (YOLO-World), through spatial label-cleaning rules, to a fully hand-annotated dataset — isolating label quality as the decisive factor: the final YOLOv8n model resolves every previously failing class while remaining a 5.97 MB TensorFlow Lite asset that runs in real time on a mid-range phone (mAP@0.5 = 0.995, mAP@0.5:0.95 = 0.924). Second, a bilingual Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) guide, grounded in a 108-record ChromaDB knowledge base, was benchmarked across seven candidate language models, with Gemma 4 E2B (Q4_K_M) selected; ten targeted optimizations reduce end-to-end latency from over 30 s to approximately 10 s. Both subsystems are integrated in a production Flutter application with bilingual interface, museum location gating, and text-to-speech support.

I 简介Introduction

博物馆环境中的细粒度文物识别很困难，因为 背景混乱、低光捕捉、类别之间的视觉重叠，以及 开集的不确定性。大埃及博物馆 (GEM) 面临着严峻的挑战： 目标藏品包含 51 件编目文物，其中许多是 几乎一模一样的拉美西斯雕像，仅在微妙的姿势、头饰或 随附标准。一名游客站在两座外观相似的雕像前 不关心模型内部结构；他们关心应用程序是否提供值得信赖的信息 用他们自己的语言快速回答。Fine-grained artifact recognition in museum environments is difficult due to background clutter, low-light capture, visual overlap between classes, and open-set uncertainty. At the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) the challenge is acute: the target collection contains 51 catalogued artifacts, many of which are near-identical Ramesside statues differing only in subtle pose, headdress, or accompanying standards. A tourist standing in front of two visually similar statues does not care about model internals; they care whether the app gives a trustworthy answer quickly and in their own language.

当前的博物馆技术产品至少未能满足以下三个要求之一： GEM部署：实时设备识别（云API引入延迟和 连接依赖），阿拉伯语支持（大多数系统针对西方 观众）和无幻觉的答案（无根据的语言模型通常 发明看似合理的历史事实）Current museum-technology offerings fail on at least one of three requirements for GEM deployment: real-time on-device recognition (cloud APIs introduce latency and connectivity dependence), Arabic-language support (most systems target Western audiences), and hallucination-free answers (ungrounded language models routinely invent plausible-sounding historical facts)[1]。全球 增强现实市场规模到 2023 年将突破 500 亿美元，预计将超过 到 2027 年将达到 1100 亿美元. The global Augmented Reality market exceeded USD 50 billion in 2023 and is projected to surpass USD 110 billion by 2027[14];埃及迎来了约 1,490 万人 2023 年国际游客数量; Egypt welcomed approximately 14.9 million international visitors in 2023[15]，超过70%的游客依赖 访问期间的数字工具, and over 70% of tourists rely on digital tools during visits[16]，证实了对 同时满足所有三个要求的解决方案。, confirming strong demand for a solution that satisfies all three requirements simultaneously.

时间镜头TimeLens通过两项工程贡献弥补了这一差距。首先，一个 紧凑型 YOLOv8n 检测器addresses this gap through two engineering contributions. First, a compact YOLOv8n detector[5]经过精心策划的培训， GEM 工件的视频衍生数据集并导出到 TensorFlow Lite 端到端非极大值抑制，实现设备端实时识别，无干扰 每帧网络往返。二、双语检索增强一代 (RAG) 指南is trained on a carefully curated, video-derived dataset of GEM artifacts and exported to TensorFlow Lite with end-to-end non-maximum suppression, enabling real-time on-device recognition with no per-frame network round-trip. Second, a bilingual Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) guide[7]将每个答案都纳入精心策划的 108 条记录中 ChromaDB知识库，防止造假，支持英文和中文 阿拉伯语。这两个子系统都集成在生产 Flutter 应用程序中。grounds every answer in a curated 108-record ChromaDB knowledge base, preventing fabrication and supporting both English and Arabic. Both subsystems are integrated in a production Flutter application.

本文的主要发现是：（i）标签质量主导模型架构 以及用于细粒度博物馆文物检测的输入分辨率； (ii) 完全本地化 双语 RAG 管道在交互延迟时实现无幻觉答案； (iii) 组合系统在商用手机和单个小型 GPU 上运行，使得 适合真实博物馆部署。The key findings of this paper are: (i) label quality dominates model architecture and input resolution for fine-grained museum artifact detection; (ii) a fully local bilingual RAG pipeline achieves hallucination-free answers at interactive latency; and (iii) the combined system runs on commodity phones and a single small GPU, making it suitable for real museum deployment.

Comparative studies of museum-exhibit recognition from video-derived datasets report that YOLOv8 reaches very high mean Average Precision while remaining light enough for real-time mobile use, outperforming heavier backbones such as VGG-16 and ResNet on the speed/accuracy trade-off[2]。自适应 CNN 方法同样被用于通过自动 人工制品识别. 检索增强生成 (RAG) 将语言模型的输出基于段落 从知识库而不是参数存储器中检索，减少 知识密集型任务中的制造Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) grounds a language model’s output in passages retrieved from a knowledge base rather than its parametric memory, reducing fabrication in knowledge-intensive tasks[7]。最近的工作带来 上下文感知的 LLM 助理融入 AR 设置. Robustness challenges specific to heritage capture — low contrast, reduced lighting, and crowd occlusion — are active research topics[10, 11].

Ⅱ-DII-D 研究差距Research Gaps

之前的工作留下了 TimeLens 解决的三个综合差距：(i) 标签质量 细粒度、特定领域的检测； (ii) 对视频帧的诚实评价 随机分割泄漏接近重复帧的数据集； (iii) 脚踏实地、双语 面向公众的博物馆导游的一代。Prior work leaves three combined gaps that TimeLens addresses: (i) label quality for fine-grained, domain-specific detection; (ii) honest evaluation of video-frame datasets where random splits leak near-duplicate frames; and (iii) grounded, bilingual generation for public-facing museum guides.

IV-A 数据集和约束Dataset and Constraints

源材料由大约 50 个 GEM 文物的画廊内视频组成 在公开开放之前捕获，在 51 个领域产生大约 30,000 个候选帧 类的速度约为每秒一帧。部署限制排除了云 检测器：推理必须在设备上实时运行，没有每帧往返 （The source material consists of approximately 50 in-gallery videos of GEM artifacts captured before public opening, yielding roughly 30,000 candidate frames across 51 classes at about one frame per second. Deployment constraints rule out cloud detectors: inference must run on-device with no per-frame round-trip, in real time (≥2.5\geq\!2.5中端手机上的 FPS），具有紧凑型型号（FPS on mid-tier phones), with a compact model (<8<\!8MB TFLite 资产）， 并且对于手持拍摄具有鲁棒性。这些限制导致了 YOLOv8-nano 与 FP16 TensorFlow Lite 部署MB TFLite asset), and be robust to handheld capture. V-A 接地原则Grounding Principles

公共博物馆导游不得发明历史。三个基本原则支配着 设计：（i）答案仅限于检索到的博物馆背景； (ii) 检索是 语言匹配，因此阿拉伯语问题接收阿拉伯语上下文； (iii) 相关时 事实不存在，模型被指示说出来而不是捏造。跑步 本地推理消除了每个请求的成本、速率限制和访问者查询泄漏。A public museum guide must not invent history. Three grounding principles govern the design: (i) answers are constrained to retrieved museum context; (ii) retrieval is language-matched so an Arabic question receives Arabic context; (iii) when relevant facts are absent, the model is instructed to say so rather than fabricate. Running inference locally removes per-request cost, rate limits, and visitor-query leakage.

七VII讨论Discussion

标签质量是一流的。Label quality is first-order.受控迭代研究分离标签 质量作为细粒度博物馆文物检测的决定性因素：标签 单独的变化（+0.116 mAP@0.5）超过了分辨率缩放（0.000 mAP@0.5 增益）。 每个以前失败的类都通过手动注释来挽救，证实了这一点 一旦标签天花板被移除，建筑选择就变成了次要的。The controlled iteration study isolates label quality as the decisive factor for fine-grained museum artifact detection: a labeling change alone (+0.116 mAP@0.5) outweighs resolution scaling (0.000 mAP@0.5 gain). Every previously failing class was rescued by hand annotation, confirming that architectural choices are second-order once the label ceiling is removed.

接地，而不是模型尺寸，可以防止幻觉。Grounding, not model size, prevents hallucination.决定性的 RAG 收益 来自分层分块、语言分隔的集合和查询 分类——不是来自更大的模型。 Gemma 4 E2B (Q4_K_M) 1389–3585 MB VRAM 可提供无幻觉的双语答案，其中 2565 MB Llama 3.2 3B 产生乱码的阿拉伯语。The decisive RAG gains came from hierarchical chunking, language-separated collections, and query classification — not from a larger model. Gemma 4 E2B (Q4_K_M) at 1389–3585 MB VRAM delivers hallucination-free bilingual answers where a 2565 MB Llama 3.2 3B produces garbled Arabic.

局限性。Limitations.知识库已关闭：108条记录之外的问题 得到有限的答案。查询分类器是基于关键字的，可能会错误路由 回避预期词汇的问题。该模型没有跨请求的记忆。 在检测方面，验证指标反映了图像级别的划分；一个 视频级（时间顺序）分割将给出更保守的概括 估计。The knowledge base is closed: questions outside 108 records receive limited answers. The query classifier is keyword-based and can misroute questions that avoid expected vocabulary. The model has no memory across requests. On the detection side, validation metrics reflect an image-level split; a video-level (chronological) split would give a more conservative generalization estimate.

八VIII结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 TimeLens，这是一款由人工智能驱动的 Grand 的双语移动指南。 埃及博物馆由两个集成子系统组成。设备上的检测器 表明，对于细粒度的博物馆文物，标签质量是首要的 识别性能的决定因素：经过训练的紧凑型 YOLOv8n 模型 手工验证的标签达到 mAP@0.5 = 0.995 和 mAP@0.5:0.95 = 0.924（零） 破碎的类，适合 5.97 MB TFLite 资产。双语 RAG 指南 — ChromaDB 检索基于 FastAPI 背后的量化 Gemma 4 E2B 模型 — 提供事实 英语/阿拉伯语答案，端到端延迟约为 10 秒，无幻觉 涵盖 30 个问题的评估。两个子系统都在商用硬件上运行（a 中端手机和单个 GPU）并集成在生产 Flutter 中 具有双语界面和文本转语音支持的应用程序。This paper presented TimeLens, an AI-powered bilingual mobile guide for the Grand Egyptian Museum comprising two integrated subsystems. The on-device detector demonstrates that, for fine-grained museum artifacts, label quality is the primary determinant of recognition performance: a compact YOLOv8n model trained on hand-verified labels achieves mAP@0.5 = 0.995 and mAP@0.5:0.95 = 0.924 with zero broken classes, fitting in a 5.97 MB TFLite asset. The bilingual RAG guide — ChromaDB retrieval grounding a quantized Gemma 4 E2B model behind FastAPI — delivers factual English/Arabic answers at approximately 10 s end-to-end latency with no hallucinations across a 30-question evaluation. Both subsystems run on commodity hardware (a mid-range phone and a single GPU) and are integrated in a production Flutter application with bilingual interface and text-to-speech support.

未来的工作包括将知识库和工件数据集扩展到完整的 GEM 覆盖率，用学习的意图模型替换关键字查询分类器，添加 会话记忆，并现场进行正式的系统可用性量表研究 博物馆条件。Future work includes expanding the knowledge base and artifact dataset toward full-GEM coverage, replacing the keyword query classifier with a learned intent model, adding conversational memory, and conducting formal System Usability Scale studies in live museum conditions.
