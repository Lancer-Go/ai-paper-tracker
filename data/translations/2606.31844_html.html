1 简介Introduction

真实交通模拟是自动驾驶的关键生成数据引擎，可在复杂的多智能体环境中进行可扩展的训练和评估Realistic traffic simulation is a critical generative data engine for autonomous driving, enabling scalable training and evaluation in complex multi-agent environments[10, 34]。生成建模的最新进展导致自回归架构的广泛采用. Recent advances in generative modeling have led to the widespread adoption of autoregressive architectures[24, 22]，它将交通模拟表述为顺序轨迹生成。大多数现有方法, which formulate traffic simulation as sequential trajectory generation. Most existing approaches[27, 34, 36, 33]在模仿学习范式下，在大规模以自我为中心的驾驶日志上训练模拟器，通过最大化记录行为的可能性来学习上下文-轨迹映射。该范例极大地提高了微观交通模拟的真实性和保真度，实现了真实的多智能体交互，并在广泛使用的基准测试中实现了强大的性能train simulators on large-scale ego-centric driving logs under an imitation learning paradigm, learning context–trajectory mappings by maximizing the likelihood of logged behaviors. This paradigm has substantially advanced the realism and fidelity of microscopic traffic simulation, enabling realistic multi-agent interactions and achieving strong performance on widely used benchmarks[19].

尽管取得了这些进展，但一个基本问题仍然很大程度上未被探索：Despite these advances, a fundamental question remains largely unexplored:在全局情境闭环模拟中执行时，从本地观察到的驾驶日志中学到的策略能否保持行为理性？can policies learned from locally observed driving logs remain behaviorally rational when executed in globally contextualized closed-loop simulation?图Figure1说明了为什么这个假设可能失败。在现实世界的数据收集中，领先车辆可能会因下游障碍物而制动。然而，如果障碍物位于记录车辆的感知范围之外或被其他代理遮挡，记录的日志将捕获制动行为，同时忽略证明其合理性的上下文。从观察到的数据的角度来看，这种有效的操作似乎是一种无法解释的停止。当这种部分观察被视为完全监督时，学习模型可能会将制动与不完整的上下文相关联，即使真正的原因不存在，也会重现该动作illustrates why this assumption may fail. In real-world data collection, a leading vehicle may brake in response to a downstream obstacle. However, if the obstacle lies outside the perception range of the logging vehicle or is occluded by other agents, the recorded log captures the braking behavior while omitting the context that justifies it. From the perspective of the observed data, this valid maneuver appears as an unexplained stop. When such partial observations are treated as complete supervision, the learned model may associate braking with incomplete context, reproducing the action even when the true cause is absent[12]。这会导致闭环部署期间上下文-操作映射不明确以及不切实际的行为. This leads to ambiguous context–action mappings and unrealistic behaviors during closed-loop rollout[26, 7].

这种现象反映了交通模拟中更广泛的局部与全局不匹配This phenomenon reflects a broader local-to-global mismatch in traffic simulation[18]。大多数现有模拟器都是根据从以自我为中心的车辆平台（例如 Waymo）收集的大规模驾驶日志进行训练的. Most existing simulators are trained on large-scale driving logs collected from ego-centric vehicle platforms, such as Waymo[8], 努计划, nuPlan[3]，和阿尔戈宇宙, and Argoverse[32]，其中感知边界、遮挡和传感器视点是固有的。因此，这些日志提供了丰富的本地驾驶先验信息，但关于模型在长期部署过程中在全场景环境下应如何表现的证据有限。因此，关键的挑战是如何减轻由不完整的上下文动作学习引起的异常行为，特别是当这些行为不是在原始日志中直接观察到而是在闭环生成过程中出现时, where perception boundaries, occlusions, and sensor viewpoints are inherent. As a result, these logs provide rich local driving priors but limited evidence about how a model should behave under full scene context during long-horizon rollouts. The key challenge is therefore how to mitigate abnormal behaviors induced by incomplete context-action learning, especially when these behaviors are not directly observed in the original logs but emerge during closed-loop generation[4].

为了应对这一挑战，我们建议从被动模仿转向主动调整。我们不是仅仅从有偏自回归生成器的角度来看待模拟，而是引入了一种互补的方法To address this challenge, we propose to shift from passive imitation to active alignment. Instead of viewing simulation solely from the perspective of the biased autoregressive generator, we introduce a complementary上下文偏好评估器Contextual Preference Evaluator（CPE）评估完整场景上下文下生成行为的合理性。我们使用基础模拟器本身作为全局可观察的沙箱：从记录的初始状态开始，它生成多个假设推出，揭示从不完整的观察中学到的偏差如何表现为闭环故障。我们进一步将这些自我暴露的失败与人性化的驾驶先验结合起来，并将其转化为偏好监督。然后，CPE 通过对比偏好学习进行训练，以区分全局一致的行为和局部合理但上下文不一致的行为。在推理过程中，CPE 充当插件测试时间对齐模块，重新加权来自冻结模拟器的候选操作，引导自回归解码走向与完整交通场景更一致的行为。这将流量模拟从被动的下一个令牌采样转变为偏好引导的决策，提高了闭环行为的合理性。(CPE) that assesses the rationality of generated behaviors under complete scene context. We use the base simulator itself as a globally observable sandbox: starting from logged initial states, it generates multiple what-if rollouts that reveal how biases learned from incomplete observations manifest as closed-loop failures. We further ground these self-exposed failures with human-aligned driving priors and transform them into preference supervision. The CPE is then trained with contrastive preference learning to distinguish globally coherent behaviors from locally plausible but contextually inconsistent ones. During inference, CPE acts as a plug-in test-time alignment module that reweights candidate actions from the frozen simulator, guiding autoregressive decoding toward behaviors that are more consistent with the full traffic scene. This converts traffic simulation from passive next-token sampling into preference-guided decision making, improving closed-loop behavioral rationality.

A.2 数据预处理Data preprocessing

关注SMARTFollowing SMART[33]和CAT-Kand CAT-K[35]，我们首先使用 k 盘聚类为地图元素和运动轨迹构建离散标记词汇表。具体来说，地图词汇包含 1,024 个标记，其中每个标记代表由 10 个连续车道段组成的 5 米折线。运动词汇包含 2,048 个标记，其中每个标记对应一个 0.5 秒的轨迹片段。, we first construct discrete token vocabularies for both map elements and motion trajectories using k-disk clustering. Specifically, the map vocabulary contains 1,024 tokens, where each token represents a 5-meter polyline composed of 10 consecutive lane segments. The motion vocabulary contains 2,048 tokens, where each token corresponds to a 0.5-second trajectory fragment.

在数据加载过程中，我们将每个场景中的矢量化地图元素离散为相同长度的折线，并根据最近距离标准将它们与预定义的地图词汇进行匹配。通过将每个轨迹片段与词汇表中最接近的运动标记相匹配，以类似的方式离散化运动轨迹。此标记化过程将连续的地图和运动信息转换为可直接由自回归模拟器使用的离散表示。During data loading, we discretize the vectorized map elements in each scene into polylines of the same length and match them to the predefined map vocabulary according to the nearest-distance criterion. Motion trajectories are discretized in a similar manner by matching each trajectory fragment to its nearest motion token in the vocabulary. This tokenization procedure converts continuous map and motion information into discrete representations that can be directly used by the autoregressive simulator.

为了确保数据质量并保持代理行为的合理分布，我们使用 1 秒时间戳处出现的所有代理作为有效场景代理。对于每个场景，我们选择自我车辆周围 100 米半径内最多 32 个智能体作为训练智能体。如果该范围内可用代理少于32个，则保留所有有效代理；否则，从候选集中随机抽取 32 个代理。这种过滤策略保留了自我车辆周围最相关的本地交互，同时保持输入大小易于管理以进行训练。To ensure data quality and maintain a reasonable distribution of agent behaviors, we use all agents present at the 1-second timestamp as valid scene agents. For each scene, we select up to 32 agents within a 100-meter radius around the ego vehicle as training agents. If fewer than 32 agents are available within this range, all valid agents are retained; otherwise, 32 agents are randomly sampled from the candidate set. This filtering strategy preserves the most relevant local interactions around the ego vehicle while keeping the input size manageable for training.

A.3 模型架构Model Architecture

我们的模型由两个主要组件组成：场景编码器和轻量级解码器。 场景编码器遵循基于 Transformer 编码器块的类似 SMART 的架构，该架构将标记化的地图元素、代理历史和场景级交互上下文编码为上下文化的特征表示。 这种设计允许模型在完整的场景上下文下捕获多代理依赖性和地图感知空间关系。Our model consists of two main components: a scene encoder and a lightweight decoder. The scene encoder follows a SMART-like architecture based on Transformer encoder blocks, which encode the tokenized map elements, agent histories, and scene-level interaction context into contextualized feature representations. This design allows the model to capture multi-agent dependencies and map-aware spatial relationships under the complete scene context.

在编码的场景特征之上，我们采用了一个作为残差卷积网络实现的轻量级解码器。 具体来说，解码器由具有残差连接的多个时间一维卷积层构建，从而能够对候选运动序列中的局部时间模式进行有效建模。 与重型自回归 Transformer 解码器相比，这种卷积 ResNet 式解码器为上下文偏好评估提供了紧凑且高效的评分模块。On top of the encoded scene features, we employ a lightweight decoder implemented as a residual convolutional network. Specifically, the decoder is built from multiple temporal 1D convolutional layers with residual connections, enabling efficient modeling of local temporal patterns in candidate motion sequences. Compared with a heavy autoregressive Transformer decoder, this convolutional ResNet-style decoder provides a compact and efficient scoring module for contextual preference evaluation.

D.3 线下CPE排名分析Offline CPE Ranking Analysis

除了主论文中使用的在线考试时间指导之外，我们还进行了离线排名分析，以更好地理解基础模拟器和 CPE 的作用。 这个实验不作为我们的主要推理策略；相反，它作为诊断研究来检查基础模拟器是否已在其采样轨迹集中包含潜在的合理行为，以及 CPE 是否可以在完整的场景上下文中识别它们。In addition to the online test-time guidance used in the main paper, we conduct an offline ranking analysis to better understand the roles of the base simulator and CPE. This experiment is not used as our main inference strategy; instead, it serves as a diagnostic study to examine whether the base simulator already contains potentially reasonable behaviors in its sampled trajectory set, and whether CPE can identify them under complete scene context.

具体来说，对于每个场景，我们首先让基础模拟器从相同的记录初始状态生成 64 个闭环推出候选者，而不在解码期间应用 CPE 或自动标记器指导。 推出完成后，CPE 或自动标记器会在完整的模拟场景环境下评估每个候选轨迹，并分配总体偏好分数。 然后我们选择得分最高的 32 名作为最终输出。 这与我们的在线 CRAFT 推理不同，其中 CPE 应用于每个自回归解码步骤，以在轨迹完全生成之前重新加权候选动作。 因此，线下排名仅在已生成的期货中进行选择，而在线指导则主动改变生成过程。Specifically, for each scenario, we first let the base simulator generate 64 closed-loop rollout candidates from the same logged initial state without applying CPE or auto-labeler guidance during decoding. After the rollouts are completed, CPE or auto-labeler evaluates each candidate trajectory under the full simulated scene context and assigns an overall preference score. We then select the top 32 scoring rollout as the final output. This differs from our online CRAFT inference, where CPE is applied at each autoregressive decoding step to reweight candidate actions before the trajectory is fully generated. Therefore, offline ranking only selects among already generated futures, while online guidance actively changes the generation process.

离线排名结果提供了两个有用的观察结果。 首先，CPE 始终能够将更安全、行为更合理的部署排名较高，这表明学习到的偏好分数捕获了有意义的全局交互质量，而不仅仅是拟合注释标签。 其次，离线排名已经可以改善结果的事实表明，基础模拟器尚未完全发挥其行为潜力：其候选集中可能存在合理的未来，但原始采样策略无法可靠地选择。 这一观察结果进一步激发了我们的测试时指导设计，该设计将 CPE 纳入解码过程的早期，以在异常行为累积之前引导模拟器实现这种全局一致的行为。The offline ranking results provide two useful observations. First, CPE can consistently rank safer and more behaviorally rational rollouts higher, indicating that the learned preference scores capture meaningful global interaction quality rather than merely fitting the annotation labels. Second, the fact that offline ranking can already improve the results suggests that the base simulator has not fully exhausted its behavioral potential: reasonable futures may exist in its candidate set, but are not reliably selected by the original sampling strategy. This observation further motivates our test-time guidance design, which incorporates CPE earlier in the decoding process to steer the simulator toward such globally coherent behaviors before abnormal actions accumulate.

The Contextual Preference Evaluator is forced to score and select from a set of suboptimal proposals, resulting in the safest available but unnatural deceleration. This case factually demonstrates that the performance of CRAFT is fundamentally bottlenecked by the quality of the provided candidate space.

E.2 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

尽管 CRAFT 展示了闭环交通模拟的测试时行为对齐的有效性，但我们承认其当前的推理策略仍然留有很大的改进空间。 在这项工作中，搜索和重新加权过程被设计为偏好引导解码的简单且实验性的实现。 虽然可以有效减少异常行为，但它仍然可能受到基础模拟器提出的候选动作的质量和多样性的限制。 如果候选集中不包含必要的动作标记，CPE 只能对可用的选择进行重新排序，而不能恢复缺失的行为。Although CRAFT demonstrates the effectiveness of test-time behavioral alignment for closed-loop traffic simulation, we acknowledge that its current inference strategy still leaves substantial room for improvement. In this work, the search and reweighting procedure is designed as a simple and experimental realization of preference-guided decoding. While effective in reducing abnormal behaviors, it may still be constrained by the quality and diversity of the candidate actions proposed by the base simulator. If essential motion tokens are not included in the candidate set, CPE can only re-rank the available choices rather than recover the missing behaviors.

尽管如此，我们相信这种限制也凸显了未来交通仿真研究的一个重要方向。 大多数现有方法侧重于改进模仿学习或对模拟器进行后训练，而我们的工作表明，在测试时纠正行为偏差可以为更可靠的闭环模拟提供轻量级和补充性的路径。 这种观点与实际模拟器和世界模型尤其相关，其中重新训练大型生成模型可能成本高昂、不稳定或难以部署。Nevertheless, we believe this limitation also highlights an important direction for future traffic simulation research. Most existing methods focus on improving imitation learning or post-training the simulator, whereas our work suggests that correcting behavioral biases at test time can provide a lightweight and complementary path toward more reliable closed-loop simulation. This perspective is particularly relevant for practical simulators and world models, where retraining large generative models can be costly, unstable, or difficult to deploy.

未来的工作可能会探索更先进的解码和搜索策略，例如候选集的动态扩展、自适应引导强度或将偏好引导解码与基础模拟器先验分布的改进相结合的混合方法。 特别是，直接改进基础模拟器的提案分布可以帮助防止在 CPE 评估之前排除必要的标记，从而使本地驾驶先验和全局一致行为之间更加一致。Future work may explore more advanced decoding and search strategies, such as dynamic expansion of the candidate set, adaptive guidance strength or hybrid methods that combine preference-guided decoding with improvements to the base simulator’s prior distribution. In particular, directly improving the base simulator’s proposal distribution could help prevent essential tokens from being excluded before CPE evaluation, enabling stronger alignment between local driving priors and globally coherent behaviors.
