摘要Abstract

电子健康记录 (EHR) 特征工程是临床研究和人工智能的主要瓶颈，占数据科学家工作量的 39-45%。这在心力衰竭中尤其明显，心力衰竭影响了大约 670 万美国成年人，需要将零碎的 EHR 数据与特定疾病、基于指南的临床推理相结合。现有的基于规则和基于大语言模型 (LLM) 的方法仅提供部分自动化，可维护性和证据可追溯性有限。我们开发了 Nimblemind 多智能体系统 (nMAS)，这是一个用于自动心力衰竭特征工程的循证、基于标题的管道，并根据来自 9 个 EHR 源表的 500 条虚拟患者记录对其进行了评估。 nMAS 生成了 132 个结构化和 70 个评分聚合特征，验证了结构完整性、评分标准合规性和出处，并由受限制的法学硕士进行了审核。添加聚合特征将 HFrEF 的 AUROC 从 0.895 提高到 0.963，将 HFpEF 表型的 AUROC 从 0.870 提高到 0.910，并且基于 LLM 的证据支持和方法健全性的独立评估对这些特征进行了最高分的 81.5%。这些结果证明了针对复杂心血管 EHR 数据进行自动化、可审核的特征工程的可行性，尽管评估仅限于单个机构队列并且需要外部验证。Electronic health record (EHR) feature engineering is a major bottleneck in clinical research and AI, accounting for 39–45% of data scientists’ workload. This is especially pronounced in heart failure, which affects an estimated 6.7 million U.S. adults and requires integrating fragmented EHR data with disease-specific, guideline-based clinical reasoning. Existing rule-based and large language model (LLM)-based approaches offer only partial automation with limited maintainability and evidence traceability. We developed the Nimblemind Multi-Agent System (nMAS), an evidence-linked, rubric-grounded pipeline for automated heart-failure feature engineering, and evaluated it on 500 dummy patient records from nine EHR source tables. nMAS generated 132 structured and 70 rubric-scored aggregated features, verified for structural integrity, rubric compliance, and provenance, and audited by a restricted LLM. Adding the aggregated features improved held-out AUROC from 0.895 to 0.963 for HFrEF and 0.870 to 0.910 for HFpEF phenotyping, and an independent LLM-based rubric assessment of evidence support and methodological soundness scored the features at 81.5% of maximum points. These results demonstrate the feasibility of automated, auditable feature engineering for complex cardiovascular EHR data, though evaluation was limited to a single-institution cohort and external validation is needed.

1 简介Introduction

在训练临床机器学习模型或进行人群水平队列研究之前，必须将常规收集的临床数据转化为可靠、可分析的患者水平变量。该特征工程过程涉及从异构临床记录中提取、清理、协调和派生变量，代表了医疗保健数据科学工作流程的重要组成部分。数据科学家报告称，他们大约 39-45% 的工作时间用于数据加载和清理，而不是分析Before a clinical machine-learning model can be trained or a population-level cohort study can be conducted, routinely collected clinical data must be transformed into reliable, analysis-ready patient-level variables. This feature-engineering process involves extracting, cleaning, harmonizing, and deriving variables from heterogeneous clinical records and represents a substantial component of healthcare data-science workflows. Data scientists report spending approximately 39–45% of their working time on data loading and cleaning rather than analysis(anaconda2020datascience)。美国数据科学家的平均工资为每年 112,590 美元，即每小时约 54 美元. At the median U.S. data-scientist wage of $112,590 per year, or approximately $54 per hour(bls2026datascientists)，这相当于每周 40 小时工作大约 845-975 美元用于数据准备的劳动力。当必须针对新数据集、机构或研究问题重复重建特征工程工作流程时，这些成本会进一步加剧。由此导致的可重用数据基础设施的缺乏仍然是人工智能 (AI) 在医疗保健领域更广泛部署的障碍, this corresponds to roughly $845–$975 of labor per 40-hour workweek devoted to data preparation. These costs are further compounded when feature-engineering workflows must be repeatedly reconstructed for new datasets, institutions, or research questions. The resulting lack of reusable data infrastructure remains a barrier to the broader deployment of artificial intelligence (AI) in healthcare(bian2026learningutility).

心脏病学对临床特征工程提出了特别苛刻的要求，因为具有临床意义的变量分布在多个 EHR 领域，并且通常需要纵向解释。诊断、药物、实验室测量、程序和成像结果可以存储在单独的关系表中，而不是存储在单个可分析的数据集中Cardiology presents a particularly demanding setting for clinical feature engineering because clinically meaningful variables are distributed across multiple EHR domains and often require longitudinal interpretation. Diagnoses, medications, laboratory measurements, procedures, and imaging findings may be stored in separate relational tables rather than in a single analysis-ready dataset(kao2022ehrhf)。因此，构建患者级特征需要的不仅仅是提取单个变量：必须根据临床有意义的定义对异构记录进行协调、时间聚合和转换. Consequently, constructing patient-level features requires more than extracting individual variables: heterogeneous records must be reconciled, temporally aggregated, and transformed according to clinically meaningful definitions(roger2021hfepidemiology)。心力衰竭（HF）是一个具有代表性的案例，因为其特征取决于多个互补的临床证据来源，并影响大量且不断增长的患者群体。据估计，美国有 670 万成年人患有心力衰竭，预计到 2030 年患病率将增至 870 万. Heart failure (HF) provides a representative case because its characterization depends on multiple complementary sources of clinical evidence and affects a large and growing patient population. An estimated 6.7 million U.S. adults have HF, with prevalence projected to increase to 8.7 million by 2030(bozkurt2025hfstats)。 2023 年，452,573 份美国死亡证明中提到了心力衰竭，占所有死亡人数的 14.6%. In 2023, HF was mentioned on 452,573 U.S. death certificates, corresponding to 14.6% of all deaths(nchs2025mcdwonder).

心力衰竭表型分析还需要对异质测量应用明确的临床定义。现代分类使用指南指定的阈值，例如射血分数类别，而其他特征取决于实验室测量、用药史、合并症和特定疾病的评分标准HF phenotyping also requires the application of explicit clinical definitions to heterogeneous measurements. Contemporary classifications use guideline-specified thresholds, such as ejection-fraction categories, while other features depend on laboratory measurements, medication histories, comorbidities, and disease-specific scoring criteria(ostrominski2024hfguidelines; maddox2024hfref)。这些要求在简单地从 EHR 中提取信息和构建有临床意义的特征之间形成了区别：后者需要明确的规则来描述应使用哪些源变量、应如何组合它们以及应如何解释结果值。对于研究应用，这些转换还必须是可重复的并可追溯到其底层源数据。. These requirements create a distinction between simply extracting information from an EHR and constructing a clinically meaningful feature: the latter requires explicit rules describing which source variables should be used, how they should be combined, and how the resulting value should be interpreted. For research applications, these transformations must additionally be reproducible and traceable to their underlying source data.

这些要求催生了一种自动化特征工程方法，该方法将异构 EHR 集成与明确的临床规范和证据可追溯性相结合。我们通过 Nimblemind 多代理系统 (nMAS) 满足这一需求These requirements motivate an automated feature-engineering approach that combines heterogeneous EHR integration with explicit clinical specifications and evidence traceability. We address this need with the Nimblemind Multi-Agent System (nMAS)(shimgekar2025agentic)，一个用于心力衰竭特征工程的有证据关联、以标题为基础的管道。 nMAS 不是生成不受限制的临床摘要，而是应用源自临床规范的确定性评分规则，并使用受限语言模型审核步骤来评估生成的特征构建。每个派生特征仍然与其源 EHR 变量和预定义的评分标准相关联，从而实现自动化处理，同时保留可解释性和可审计性。, an evidence-linked, rubric-grounded pipeline for heart-failure feature engineering. Rather than generating unrestricted clinical summaries, nMAS applies deterministic scoring rules derived from clinical specifications and uses a restricted language-model auditing step to evaluate the resulting feature construction. Each derived feature remains linked to its source EHR variables and predefined scoring criteria, enabling automated processing while preserving interpretability and auditability.

在这项试点研究中，我们将 nMAS 应用于由 9 个 EHR 源表组装而成的 500 份虚拟心力衰竭患者记录。我们评估了生成的数据集的结构完整性、标题合规性和临床一致性。尽管下游预测性能被报告为二次评估，但主要目标是评估自动化特征工程是否产生结构有效、符合标准且临床一致的患者级特征。因此，本研究为证据关联、基于标题的特征生成作为准备复杂心血管 EHR 数据集的可重复使用方法的可行性提供了初步证据。In this pilot study, we applied nMAS to 500 dummy heart-failure patient records assembled from nine EHR source tables. We evaluated the resulting dataset for structural integrity, rubric compliance, and clinical coherence. Although downstream predictive performance is reported as a secondary evaluation, the primary objective is to assess whether automated feature engineering produces structurally valid, rubric-compliant, and clinically coherent patient-level features. This study therefore provides preliminary evidence for the feasibility of evidence-linked, rubric-grounded feature generation as a reusable approach for preparing complex cardiovascular EHR datasets.

2 相关工作Related Work

人工智能已广泛应用于诊断、风险分层和疾病管理，但大多数系统都在单一数据模式上运行，而不是集成纵向 EHR 数据Artificial intelligence has been widely applied to diagnosis, risk stratification, and disease management, but most systems operate on a single data modality rather than integrating longitudinal EHR data(boccuto2023aihf)。许多从单一丰富来源导出结构化临床变量，例如使用大型语言模型处理的非结构化临床文档. Many derive structured clinical variables from a single rich source, such as unstructured clinical documents processed with large language models(truhn2024gpt4pathology; balasubramanian2025llmpathology)，而不是跨多个 EHR 域协调异构数据。心脏病学也存在同样的模式，人工智能模型已开发用于基于心电图的心力衰竭风险预测, instead of reconciling heterogeneous data across multiple EHR domains. The same pattern exists in cardiology, where AI models have been developed for electrocardiogram-based heart-failure risk prediction(dhingra2025aiecg)。然而，这些单一模式方法并没有解决心力衰竭表型分析所需的结构化 EHR 数据的预处理和集成，这取决于跨多个 EHR 领域的指南定义的射血分数类别、用药史、实验室测量、合并症和其他临床变量的结合. However, these single-modality approaches do not address the preprocessing and integration of structured EHR data required for heart-failure phenotyping, which depends on combining guideline-defined ejection-fraction categories, medication history, laboratory measurements, comorbidities, and other clinical variables across multiple EHR domains(roger2021hfepidemiology).

通用 EHR 特征工程框架解决了这一预处理挑战的部分问题，但仍然与疾病无关。 FIDDLE 和 MIMIC-Extract 自动进行预处理和时间聚合，以生成机器学习就绪的表示General EHR feature-engineering frameworks address part of this preprocessing challenge but remain disease agnostic. FIDDLE and MIMIC-Extract automate preprocessing and temporal aggregation to generate machine learning-ready representations(tang2020fiddle; wang2020mimicextract); OHDSI 患者水平预测框架从常见临床领域导出标准化协变量; the OHDSI patient-level prediction framework derives standardized covariates from common clinical domains(reps2018plp);基于规则的方法（例如 Charlson 和 Elixhauser 指数）可根据诊断代码设计合并症特征; and rule-based methods such as the Charlson and Elixhauser indices engineer comorbidity features from diagnosis codes(quan2005comorbidity)。最近的基于 LLM 的方法放松了基于规则的约束，但不需要基于指南的推理：FeatEHR-LLM 从数据集模式而不是临床文献中生成特征提取代码. More recent LLM-based approaches relax the rule-based constraint but not the need for guideline-grounded reasoning: FeatEHR-LLM generates feature-extraction code from dataset schemas rather than clinical literature(karami2026featehrllm)，而 AgentScore 学习根据患者数据进行统计验证的候选人评分规则，而不是基于临床证据, while AgentScore learns candidate scoring rules validated statistically against patient data rather than grounded in clinical evidence(ruhrberg2026agentscore)。因此，这些方法无法得出许多有临床意义的心力衰竭预测因子，包括表型、疾病严重程度、指南指导的药物治疗和护理差距，这些预测因子需要使用基于指南的临床推理来综合诊断、药物、实验室结果、影像、手术和行为史。. Consequently, these approaches cannot derive many clinically meaningful heart-failure predictors, including phenotype, disease severity, guideline-directed medical therapy, and care gaps, which require synthesizing diagnoses, medications, laboratory results, imaging, procedures, and behavioral history using guideline-based clinical reasoning.

因此，很少有可重用框架能够满足心血管研究的全部特征工程要求。心力衰竭表型分析需要整合纵向 EHR 数据中的诊断、药物、实验室结果、影像、手术、行为史和指南定义的射血分数阈值。由于这些派生变量控制着临床风险分层、队列资格和治疗评估，因此特征工程需要基于指南的临床推理以及每个派生特征及其支持证据之间的可审计联系。Few reusable frameworks therefore address the full feature-engineering requirements of cardiovascular research. Heart-failure phenotyping requires integrating diagnoses, medications, laboratory results, imaging, procedures, behavioral history, and guideline-defined ejection-fraction thresholds across longitudinal EHR data. Because these derived variables govern clinical risk stratification, cohort eligibility, and treatment assessment, feature engineering requires both guideline-based clinical reasoning and an auditable link between every derived feature and its supporting evidence.

目前的工作通过一个与证据相关的、基于标准的自动化心力衰竭特征工程管道来解决这一差距。与强调通用预处理或数据驱动规则发现的现有框架不同，我们的方法在明确的标题中编码基于指南的临床推理，将异构 EHR 证据集成到临床有意义的特征中，并保留从每个特征到其基础证据和源记录的可追溯性。The present work addresses this gap with an evidence-linked, rubric-grounded pipeline for automated heart-failure feature engineering. Unlike existing frameworks that emphasize generic preprocessing or data-driven rule discovery, our approach encodes guideline-based clinical reasoning within an explicit rubric, integrates heterogeneous EHR evidence into clinically meaningful features, and preserves traceability from each feature to its underlying evidence and source records.

4 方法Method

工作流程概述。Workflow Overview.如图As shown in FigureLABEL:fig:architecture，工作流程从指定输入 EHR 导出的请求开始，并构建版本化标题中定义的目标功能。 Achievability Agent首先检查可用的源数据是否可以支持每个目标特征；缺少所需输入的功能将被报告为不受支持，而不是分配的推断值。对于支持的请求，查询解析器会将请求路由到多个 nMAS 管道之一, the workflow begins with a request specifying the input EHR exports and constructs the target features defined in the versioned rubric. The Achievability Agent first checks that the available source data can support each target feature; features whose required inputs are absent are reported as unsupported rather than assigned inferred values. For supported requests, the Query Parser then routes the request to one of several nMAS pipelines(shimgekar2025agentic);为了简洁起见，我们在这里提供两个代表性的管道：特征提取和特征工程。; for brevity, we provide two representative pipelines here: feature extraction and feature engineering.

在特征提取管道中，每个字段都通过基于规则/LLM 的提取层进行路由，具体取决于字段需要多少上下文解释，并直接从源文档返回字段的值以及确认该值的逐字源语句In the feature-extraction pipeline, each field is routed through rule/LLM-based extraction tiers, depending on how much contextual interpretation the field requires, and returns the field’s value directly from the source document along with a verbatim source sentence confirming that value(wang2026hpylori)。相反，特征工程将提取的值组合成评分细则、与证据相关的复合变量，这些变量仍然可以追溯到其源数据和证明其合理性的指南文献。本文专门关注特征工程管道，其中支持的特征分两个阶段生成，路由由配置的标题驱动 和字段定义而不是任何下游标签。 第一阶段根据 EHR 构建单个患者级别的表 源表：(1) 标准化每个表（空格、空标记、大写标识符和 ISO 日期时间）； (2) 对每个表中具有临床意义的事件键进行重复数据删除； (3) 暂时将每个源独立聚合到患者级别； (4) 将每个表的摘要合并到唯一的患者行中。. Feature engineering instead composes extracted values into rubric-scored, evidence-linked composite variables that remain traceable to their source data and to the guideline literature that justifies them. This paper focuses exclusively on the feature-engineering pipeline, in which supported features are produced in two stages, with routing driven by the configured rubric and field definitions rather than by any downstream label. Stage 1 builds a single patient-level table from the EHR source tables: (1) standardizes each table (whitespace, null tokens, uppercased identifiers, and ISO datetimes); (2) deduplicates each table on a clinically meaningful event key; (3) temporally aggregates each source to the patient level independently; (4) Merges per-table summaries into unique patient rows.

6 讨论Discussion

我们开发了一个管道，将分散的 EHR 输出转化为患者级别的心力衰竭队列，并具有源自标题、与证据相关的特征类别。添加这些功能后，HFrEF 准确度、AUROC 和 F1 分数分别提高了 0.120、0.068 和 0.118，HFpEF 性能分别提高了 0.057、0.040 和 0.054。基于独立文献的评分在 11 个特征类别中的 7 个得分高于 85/100。特征重要性分析显示，由标题衍生的特征占前 10 个 HFrEF 预测因子中的 6 个，在前 10 个 HFpEF 预测因子中占 2 个，这表明它们捕获了原始结构化变量之外的信息。We developed a pipeline that transforms fragmented EHR exports into a patient-level heart-failure cohort with rubric-derived, evidence-linked feature categories. Adding these features improved HFrEF accuracy, AUROC, and F1 score by 0.120, 0.068, and 0.118, respectively, and HFpEF performance by 0.057, 0.040, and 0.054, respectively. Independent literature-grounded grading scored seven of eleven feature categories above 85/100. Feature-importance analysis showed that rubric-derived features accounted for 6 of the top 10 HFrEF predictors and 2 of the top 10 HFpEF predictors, suggesting they captured information beyond the original structured variables.

尽管不是为临床决策支持而设计的，但生成的特征可以支持识别与已建立的护理途径相关的患者。例如，护理差距检查可识别缺乏指南指导的药物治疗的 EF 降低的心力衰竭患者，这是与 STRONG-HF 等优化策略相关的人群Although not designed for clinical decision support, the generated features may support identification of patients relevant to established care pathways. For example, care-gap checks identify reduced-EF heart-failure patients lacking guideline-directed medical therapy, a population relevant to optimization strategies such as STRONG-HF(mebazaa2022stronghf)，而肾脏合并症特征可能有助于识别可以从早期肾脏病转诊中受益的患者，这与观察性研究和荟萃分析中结果的改善有关, while kidney comorbidity features may help identify patients who could benefit from earlier nephrology referral, which has been associated with improved outcomes in observational studies and meta-analyses(cheng2025ckdreferral)。这些代表了潜在的应用而不是测量的临床效果。. These represent potential applications rather than measured clinical effects.

除了预测性能之外，该管道还为临床数据准备提供了可重复且可审核的方法。生成的特征保留与其源变量、评分标准和支持证据的链接，从而能够透明地审查每个特征的构建方式。这种设计将临床知识表示与特征执行分开，允许评分框架独立于底层数据处理管道进行更新。同样的分离使得该方法可以在心力衰竭之外重复使用。任何面临相同特征工程瓶颈的数据密集型慢性疾病都可以将其自己的基于指南的评分标准替换为该评分引擎，而无需重建其下方的提取和审计基础设施。病情发现目录是这种可重用性的一个具体实例，它将框架扩展到十三种心血管疾病，并允许未来的研究重用特征生成方法，而不是重复手动准备。Beyond predictive performance, the pipeline provides a reproducible and auditable approach for clinical data preparation. The generated features retain links to their source variables, scoring criteria, and supporting evidence, enabling transparent review of how each feature was constructed. This design separates clinical knowledge representation from feature execution, allowing the scoring framework to be updated independently from the underlying data-processing pipeline. The same separation makes the approach reusable well beyond heart failure. Any data-intensive chronic disease facing the same feature-engineering bottleneck could substitute its own guideline-grounded rubric into this scoring engine without rebuilding the extraction and auditing infrastructure underneath it. The condition-discovery catalog is one concrete instance of this reusability, extending the framework across thirteen cardiovascular conditions and allowing future studies to reuse feature-generation methods rather than repeat manual preparation.

从分散的 EHR 导出中构建可供分析的队列仍然是临床研究的主要瓶颈。如节中所述Constructing analysis-ready cohorts from fragmented EHR exports remains a major bottleneck in clinical research. As noted in Section1，数据准备占据了数据科学家的很大一部分时间, data preparation accounts for a substantial portion of data scientists’ time(anaconda2020datascience)，使可重用、版本控制的管道对于减少重复性手动工作很有价值。这种负担延伸到了临床工作流程：在心力衰竭临床试验预筛选工作中，每位患者的手动资格审查大约需要 15-20 分钟，其中 198 名患者的筛选需要协调员两周的努力, making reusable, version-controlled pipelines valuable for reducing repetitive manual work. This burden extends to clinical workflows: in a heart-failure clinical trial prescreening effort, manual eligibility review took approximately 15–20 minutes per patient, with screening of 198 patients requiring two weeks of coordinator effort(adupa2016hfpefprescreen).

该管道将确定性、基于指南的评分与有界模型辅助的评分标准生成和审核相结合。基于规则的方法提供透明度，但随着模式的发展可能会变得脆弱The pipeline combines deterministic, guideline-grounded scoring with bounded model-assisted rubric generation and auditing. Rule-based approaches provide transparency but can become brittle as schemas evolve(wang2018clinicalie)，而大型语言模型提供灵活的 临床抽象，但可能会产生不受支持的输出, whereas large language models offer flexible clinical abstraction but may produce unsupported outputs(ji2023hallucination)。通过将结构化执行与证据相关的评估标准开发相结合，该框架扩展了先前的证据相关临床人工智能方法. By combining structured execution with evidence-linked rubric development, this framework extends prior evidence-linked clinical AI approaches(shimgekar2025agentic; shimgekar2025nimblelabs; vassef2025onevlm; wang2026hpylori)迈向可重复使用的 EHR 特征工程。toward reusable EHR feature engineering.

应考虑几个限制。该管道在一个由 500 名患者组成的虚拟单机构队列中进行了评估，限制了统计功效和外部概括性。 HFrEF 和 HFpEF 是针对表型未知的患者而不是非 HF 对照进行分类，因此性能反映了 HF 队列中的表型特征，而不是一般疾病检测。评估侧重于内部一致性、特征可追溯性和下游分类，而不是外部临床有效性，一些聚合特征包含与表型相关的证据，在性能和消融分析中产生潜在的特征标签重叠。未评估预期临床影响和患者结果。合并症和行为特征部分依赖于正则表达式匹配，可能会错过非典型或机构特定的语言。最后，临床医生审查涵盖了提取标准和代表性输出，而不是整个队列。Several limitations should be considered. The pipeline was evaluated on a dummy, single-institution cohort of 500 patients, limiting statistical power and external generalizability. HFrEF and HFpEF were classified against phenotype-unknown patients rather than non-HF controls, so performance reflects phenotype characterization within an HF cohort rather than general disease detection. Evaluation focused on internal consistency, feature traceability, and downstream classification rather than external clinical validity, and some aggregated features incorporate phenotype-related evidence, creating potential feature-label overlap in performance and ablation analyses. Prospective clinical impact and patient outcomes were not assessed. Comorbidity and behavioral features rely partly on regular-expression matching and may miss atypical or institution-specific language. Finally, clinician review covered the extraction rubric and representative outputs rather than the full cohort.

未来的工作应该评估更大的、多机构队列的管道，并使用时间上分离的验证集来评估普遍性。更广泛的评估应包括心力衰竭和非心力衰竭人群、独立临床医生判定的表型、额外的表型和结果预测任务以及预期工作流程影响Future work should evaluate the pipeline on larger, multi-institutional cohorts with temporally separated validation sets to assess generalizability. Broader evaluation should include both heart-failure and non-heart-failure populations, independent clinician-adjudicated phenotypes, and additional phenotyping and outcome-prediction tasks, as well as prospective workflow impact(collins2024tripodai)。在进行此类验证之前，管道应仍然是用于研究和临床审查的与证据相关的特征工程层，而不是自主的临床决策。. Until such validation, the pipeline should remain an evidence-linked feature-engineering layer for research and clinical review rather than autonomous clinical decision-making.
