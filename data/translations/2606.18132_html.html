一、简介I. INTRODUCTION

在有限的交互下快速适应看不见的任务是机器人深度强化学习（RL）的一个关键挑战。元强化学习（Meta-RL）[1]-[4]通过将外环元学习与内环策略适应相结合来解决这一挑战，使代理能够学习如何从有限的经验中进行适应。然而，现有的元强化学习方法在元知识的表示、获取和重用方式方面仍然面临局限性。在表示方面，PEARL [5]等代表性方法采用端到端框架，其中任务推理和策略学习在固定高斯潜在先验下联合优化。尽管这种设计在计算上很方便，但它对潜在任务结构施加了很强的参数假设。在机器人任务中，任务语义通常独立于特定代理的动态，并且可能表现出多模态和非参数结构[6]。这种结构很难用单一高斯分布来描述，导致异构任务模式被压缩到平滑的潜在空间中，Rapid adaptation to unseen tasks with limited interaction is a key challenge in robotic deep reinforcement learning (RL). Meta-reinforcement learning (Meta-RL) [1]–[4] addresses this challenge by combining outer-loop meta-learning with innerloop policy adaptation, enabling agents to learn how to adapt from limited experience. However, existing Meta-RL approaches still face limitations in how meta-knowledge is represented, acquired, and reused. In terms of representation, representative methods such as PEARL [5] adopt an end-toend framework in which task inference and policy learning are jointly optimized under a fixed Gaussian latent prior. Although this design is computationally convenient, it imposes a strong parametric assumption on the latent task structure. In robotic tasks, task semantics are often independent of a specific agent’s dynamics and may exhibit multi-modal and non-parametric structures [6]. 二.相关工作II. RELATED WORK

A. 元强化学习A. Meta-Reinforcement Learning

Meta-RL 旨在通过利用从相关任务分布中收集的经验，使智能体能够快速适应看不见的任务。现有的方法可以大致分为基于梯度的方法、基于递归的方法和基于上下文的方法。基于梯度的方法学习初始化或更新规则，可以通过一些优化步骤有效地适应新任务[3]、[9]、[10]。虽然对于快速的实施例内适应是有效的，但它们主要在相同的代理动态下更新策略参数，使得学习到的先验知识难以在不同的形态中重用。基于循环的方法通过使用循环或顺序模型聚合过去的观察、行动和奖励，将适应编码为历史相关的策略[1]、[2]、[11]、[12]。然而，所得到的任务表示通常是隐式的并且与控制策略联合优化，这限制了可解释性和跨实施例重用。基于上下文的方法从最近的经验中显式地推断出潜在的任务表示，并根据该表示来调整策略[4]、[5]、[7]、[13]-[15]。最近的进展通过概率建模、度量学习、自监督目标、基于混合的表示或贝叶斯非参数先验改进了任务推理，表明更丰富的潜在结构有利于非平稳和非参数任务变化。尽管如此，大多数现有方法仍然在特定于实施例的动态下以端到端的方式学习任务推理和策略控制。因此，所学的Meta-RL aims to enable agents to rapidly adapt to unseen tasks by leveraging experience collected from a distribution of related tasks. Existing methods can be broadly categorized into gradient-based, recurrence-based, and context-based approaches. Gradient-based methods learn an initialization or update rule that can be efficiently adapted to a new task through a few optimization steps [3], [9], [10]. Although effective for fast within-embodiment adaptation, they mainly update policy parameters under the same agent dynamics, making the learned prior difficult to reuse across different morphologies. Recurrence-based methods encode adaptation into a historydependent policy by aggregating past observations, actions, and rewards with recurrent or sequential models [1], [2], [11], [12]. However, the resulting task representation is typically implicit and jointly optimized with the control policy, which limits interpretability and cross-embodiment reuse. Contextbased methods explicitly infer a latent task representation from recent experience and condition the policy on this representation [4], [5], [7], [13]–[15]. Recent advances improve task inference through probabilistic modeling, metric learning, self-supervised objectives, mixture-based representations, or Bayesian non-parametric priors, showing that richer latent structures are beneficial for non-stationary and non-parametric task variations. Nevertheless, most existing methods still learn task inference and policy control in an end-to-end manner under embodiment-specific dynamics. Consequently, the learned

B. 深度聚类和非参数任务表示B. Deep Clustering and Non-Parametric Task Representation

Meta-RL 中的任务推理与深度聚类密切相关，深度聚类将表示学习与潜在结构发现相结合[16]。现有的生成深度聚类方法，例如 VaDE 和 GMVAE [17]、[18]，通常采用高斯混合先验 [19]，但需要预定义的聚类数量，这限制了它们在任务结构未知或不断变化时的适用性。贝叶斯非参数模型通过使模型复杂性适应数据来解决这一限制[8]、[20]–[23]。特别是，DPMM 自动且显式地推断潜在组件的数量，使它们非常适合异构和非结构化任务分布 [8]、[24]。在 Meta-RL 中，贝叶斯参数和非参数潜在先验已被探索用于机器人任务推理 [4]、[7]。然而，它们通常嵌入在端到端管道中，其中任务表示和策略控制是在相同的特定于实施例的动态下学习的，这可能会将任务语义与特定于代理的运动模式纠缠在一起。因此，它们作为跨实施例的可重用元知识的潜力仍然有限。我们的工作通过将贝叶斯非参数先验合并到将任务级推理与低级控制分开的分离框架中来解决这一差距。Task inference in Meta-RL is closely related to deep clustering, which combines representation learning with latent structure discovery [16]. Existing generative deep clustering methods, such as VaDE and GMVAE [17], [18], commonly employ Gaussian mixture priors [19] but require a predefined number of clusters, limiting their applicability when task structure is unknown or evolving. Bayesian non-parametric models address this limitation by adapting model complexity to the data [8], [20]–[23]. In particular, DPMM infers the number of latent components automatically and explicitly, making them well suited for heterogeneous and unstructured task distributions [8], [24]. In Meta-RL, Bayesian parametric and non-parametric latent priors have been explored for robotic task inference [4], [7]. However, they are typically embedded in end-to-end pipelines where task representation and policy control are learned under the same embodiment-specific dynamics, which may entangle task semantics with agent-specific motion patterns. Consequently, their potential as reusable meta-knowledge across embodiments remains limited. Our work addresses this gap by incorporating a Bayesian nonparametric prior into a disentangled framework that separates task-level inference from low-level control.

C. 跨实体学习C. Cross-Embodiment Learning

ReMAP addresses this issue by learning DPMM-regularized task-level meta-knowledge on a dynamics-simplified agent and using it to guide embodimentspecific low-level controllers, thereby separating task reasoning from physical execution.

A. 初步假设和适用性A. Preliminary Assumptions and Applicability

所提出的框架学习动态简化代理上的任务级元知识，并在不同的实施例中重用它。这需要任务语义与特定于实施例的执行分离。因此，我们定义了以下假设来表征我们方法的适用性。The proposed framework learns task-level meta-knowledge on a dynamics-simplified agent and reuses it across different embodiments. This requires task semantics to be separable from embodiment-specific execution. We therefore define the following assumptions that characterize the applicability of our method.

a) 任务级目标不变性：a) Task-level objective invariance:我们专注于以运动为导向的任务，其目标可以独立于机器人形态来定义。任务目标应取决于环境变量，例如位置或速度，而不是机器人特定的变量，例如关节角度、执行器命令或接触配置。因此，达到空间目标或跟踪目标速度可以在不同代理之间共享，而由形态特定的操作界面定义的任务超出了预期范围。We focus on locomotion-oriented tasks whose objectives can be defined independently of robot morphology. The task goal should depend on environmental variables, such as position or velocity, rather than robot-specific variables such as joint angles, actuator commands, or contact configurations. Thus, reaching a spatial goal or tracking a target velocity can be shared across different agents, whereas tasks defined by morphology-specific manipulation interfaces are outside the intended scope.

b) 奖励一致性：b) Reward consistency:由于推理模块使用奖励作为任务上下文的一部分，因此奖励信号应该在代理之间保持一致的任务语义。奖励函数不必在数值上相同，但它们应该测量相同的任务相关进度，例如到达目标的距离或速度跟踪误差。特定于实施例的术语，包括联合能量惩罚或依赖于形态的动作成本，应该被标准化，以避免将特定于代理的动态泄漏到表示中。Since the inference module uses rewards as part of the task context, reward signals should preserve consistent task semantics across agents. The reward functions need not be numerically identical, but they should measure the same task-related progress, such as distance to a goal or velocity-tracking error. Embodiment-specific terms, including joint-energy penalties or morphology-dependent action costs, should be normalized to avoid leaking agent-specific dynamics into the representation.

c) 动力学抽象可行性：c) Dynamics abstraction feasibility:每个目标代理的任务相关行为应该通过简化的抽象来表示。尽管目标代理可能具有复杂的接触、非线性驱动或高维形态，但这些因素应该主要影响任务执行而不是任务识别。The task-relevant behavior of each target agent should be representable through a simplified abstraction. Although target agents may have complex contacts, nonlinear actuation, or high-dimensional morphology, these factors should mainly affect task execution rather than task identification.

在这些假设下，简化的代理保留了与任务相关的变量，同时消除了不必要的控制复杂性。因此，学习到的潜在表示可以捕获任务语义而不是特定于实施例的运动模式，从而实现跨异构代理的重用。Under these assumptions, the simplified agent preserves task-relevant variables while removing unnecessary control complexity. Consequently, the learned latent representation can capture task semantics rather than embodiment-specific motion patterns, enabling reuse across heterogeneous agents.

首先，在动力学简化代理上获取任务级元知识，可以更有效地学习任务语义，因为模型不需要同时解决高维具体实施例控制。一旦训练完成，任务推理模块和高级策略就被冻结并在目标代理之间重用，因此该成本在多个实施例中分摊。相比之下，每当实施例发生变化时，端到端 Meta-RL 基线都需要重新学习或大幅调整任务推理和控制。First, task-level meta-knowledge is acquired on the dynamicssimplified agent, where task semantics can be learned more efficiently because the model does not need to solve highdimensional embodiment-specific control at the same time. Once trained, the task inference module and the high-level policy are frozen and reused across target agents, so this cost is amortized over multiple embodiments. In contrast, end-to-end Meta-RL baselines need to relearn or substantially adapt both task inference and control whenever the embodiment changes.

其次，ReMAP 中低级学习的目标与端到端 Meta-RL 的目标根本不同。在以前的方法中，策略必须同时解释推断的任务上下文并发现复杂的动态相关的动作模式，这使得探索和训练样本效率非常低。在 ReMAP 中，低级控制器通过 SMAI 条件课程学习进行独立训练。它只需要跟踪难度逐渐增加的语义量级命令，同时获得特定于实施例的运动和探索能力。由于任务语义是由可重用的元知识模块处理的，因此低级策略可以专注于可执行的运动行为，而不是共同学习任务推理和控制。这种解耦设计大大减少了所需的交互数据，同时保留了强大的下游执行性能。总的来说，这些结果为问题 3 提供了肯定的答案：学习到的任务级元知识可以跨异构实施例有效地重用。Second, the objective of low-level learning in ReMAP is fundamentally different from that of end-to-end Meta-RL. In previous methods, the policy must simultaneously interpret the inferred task context and discover complex dynamicsdependent action patterns, which makes exploration and training highly sample-inefficient. In ReMAP, the low-level controller is trained independently through SMAI-conditioned curriculum learning. It only needs to track semantic-magnitude commands that gradually increase in difficulty, while acquiring embodiment-specific locomotion and exploration capabilities. Because task semantics are handled by the reusable metaknowledge module, the low-level policy can focus on executable motor behavior rather than jointly learning task reasoning and control. This decoupled design substantially reduces the required interaction data while preserving strong downstream execution performance. Overall, these results provide a positive answer to Q3: the learned task-level meta-knowledge can be effectively reused across heterogeneous embodiments.

六．结论VI. CONCLUSION

本文提出了 ReMAP，一种跨实施例 Meta-RL 的元知识重用框架。 ReMAP 在动力学简化代理上学习 DPMM 正则化任务级元知识，并通过 SMAI 引导的低级控制器将其传输到异构实施例。通过将任务推理与特定于实施例的执行解耦，可以重用冻结的任务推理模块和高级策略，而无需对每个目标代理进行重新训练。对多个运动实施例的实验表明，ReMAP 可以学习语义上可分离的任务表示，实现准确的目标和速度跟踪，并且与端到端 Meta-RL 基线相比，大大降低了传输成本。这些结果证明了可重用的任务级元知识对于高效的跨实施例策略转移的有效性。未来的工作将扩展语义接口并研究模拟到真实的部署。This paper presented ReMAP, a meta-knowledge reutilization framework for cross-embodiment Meta-RL. ReMAP learns DPMM-regularized task-level meta-knowledge on a dynamics-simplified agent and transfers it to heterogeneous embodiments through SMAI-guided low-level controllers. By decoupling task inference from embodiment-specific execution, the frozen task inference module and high-level policy can be reused without retraining for each target agent. Experiments on multiple locomotion embodiments show that ReMAP learns semantically separable task representations, achieves accurate goal and velocity tracking, and substantially reduces transfer cost compared with end-to-end Meta-RL baselines. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of reusable tasklevel meta-knowledge for efficient cross-embodiment policy transfer. Future work will extend the semantic interface and investigate sim-to-real deployment.
