摘要Abstract

物理推理在小语言模型中结构性地失败： 任何一步的错误都会向前传播，破坏每一步 推论如下。领域知识有限，产生幻觉 多步推导和分布敏感性 加剧这种失败。我们提出了阶梯式奖励框架 识别第一个推理错误，生成目标 结构化反馈，并训练模型修改其解决方案 通过 KL 正则化的策略梯度，而不暴露它 以地面实况解决方案作为生成目标。不像 依赖于注释的步骤级方法，没有偏好数据 需要构建并且外部验证器运行 仅在训练时进行。跨越五个物理学 与基准相比，我们的框架的准确度提高了 17-20% CoT 提示和最强基线 10-16% 会降低 计算误差从 56.9% 降低到 23.5%，并减少 最佳观察结果中的误解错误从 22.3% 降至 12.0% 案例。概念错误从 89.7% 减少到 68.7%，但仍然存在 作为所有条件下最难的故障模式。Physics reasoning fails structurally in small language models: an error at any step propagates forward, corrupting every inference that follows. Limited domain knowledge, hallucination under multi-step derivation, and distributional sensitivity compound this failure. We propose a step-level reward framework that identifies the first reasoning error, generates targeted structured feedback, and trains the model to revise its solution via policy gradient with KL regularization, without exposing it to ground truth solutions as generation targets. Unlike annotation-dependent step-level methods, no preference data construction is required and the external verifier operates exclusively at training time. Across five physics benchmarks, our framework delivers accuracy gains of 17–20% over CoT prompting and 10–16% over the strongest baseline, reduces calculation errors from 56.9% to 23.5%, and reduces miscomprehension errors from 22.3% to 12.0% in the best observed cases. Conceptual errors reduce from 89.7% to 68.7%, yet persist as the hardest failure mode across all conditions.
