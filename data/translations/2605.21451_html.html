They assert that, under mild conditions on the activation function, feedforward neural networks are dense in broad function classes, such as continuous functions on compact subsets ofℝd\mathbb{R}^{d}, LpL^{p}空间，或索博列夫空间。在过去的四十年中，这些定性的普遍性结果已经发展成为丰富的定量理论，涉及近似率、参数效率以及深度和宽度等架构特征的作用。这项调查让我们对这一理论有了一些了解。我们回顾了单隐藏层网络的经典密度结果，以及将近似误差与网络大小和目标函数的平滑度假设相关联的定量界限。特别强调深度与宽度的权衡，以及证明更深层次的架构可以为结构化函数类实现卓越的参数效率的结果。除了标准的前馈神经网络之外，我们还回顾了柯尔莫哥洛夫-阿诺德网络（KAN）的最新发展，它提供了一种替代的架构范式，其近似理论特性已经开始引起理论界的广泛关注。spaces, or Sobolev spaces. Over the past four decades, these qualitative universality results have evolved into a rich quantitative theory addressing approximation rates, parameter efficiency, and the role of architectural features such as depth and width. This survey presents several glimpses into this theory. We review classical density results for single-hidden-layer networks, as well as quantitative bounds that relate approximation error to network size and smoothness assumptions on target functions. Particular emphasis is placed on depth–width trade-offs and on results demonstrating that deeper architectures can achieve superior parameter efficiency for structured function classes. In addition to standard feedforward neural networks, we also review recent developments on Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks (KANs), which offer an alternative architectural paradigm and whose approximation-theoretic properties have begun to attract significant theoretical attention.

3 柯尔莫哥洛夫-阿诺德网络Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks

柯尔莫哥洛夫-阿诺德网络Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks（KAN）是最近提出的(KANs) are a recently proposed(Liu et al.,, 2025)神经网络架构明显受到了非凡的启发neural network architecture explicitly inspired by the remarkable柯尔莫哥洛夫-阿诺德表示定理Kolmogorov–Arnold representation theorem，它指出任何多元连续函数都可以写成连续单变量函数和加法运算的有限叠加, which states that any multivariate continuous function can be written as a finite superposition of continuous univariate functions and addition operations(Kolmogorov,, 1957)。与在神经元上采用固定激活函数的前馈神经网络不同，KAN 用可学习的单变量激活函数替换每个线性权重参数，通常使用 B 样条等基本函数进行建模。通过这种方式，KAN 可以在可训练的架构中直接参数化柯尔莫哥洛夫-阿诺德定理所建议的功能组件. Unlike feedforward neural networks that employ fixed activation functions at neurons, KANs replace each linear weight parameter with a learnable univariate activation function, often modelled using basis functions such as B-splines. In this way, KANs directly parameterise the functional components suggested by the Kolmogorov–Arnold theorem within a trainable architecture(Liu et al.,, 2025).

这种结构创新对近似理论产生了影响。与 FNN 的经典 UAT 原则上保证近似网络的存在相比，Kolmogorov-Arnold 方法将表示定理直接嵌入到架构中，并产生反映函数平滑性和架构设计的建设性近似保证。这使得 KAN 成为经典逼近理论和现代深度学习实践之间的一座有趣的桥梁。现在我们将回顾 Kolmogorov-Arnold 表示定理和 KAN 的基本架构。我们还将研究 KAN 的基本近似定理，因为This structural innovation has consequences for approximation theory. Compared with classical UATs for FNNs, which guarantee existence of approximating networks in principle, the Kolmogorov–Arnold approach embeds the representation theorem directly into the architecture and yields constructive approximation guarantees that reflect both function smoothness and architectural design. This makes KANs an intriguing bridge between classical approximation theory and modern deep learning practice. We shall now review the Kolmogorov–Arnold representation theorem and the basic architecture of a KAN. We shall also look at a basic approximation theorem for KANs due toLiu et al., (2025).

4 结束语Concluding remarks

尽管在理解神经网络表达能力方面取得了重大进展，但许多重要问题仍然悬而未决。我们强调了几个主要方向。Despite significant progress in understanding the expressivity of neural networks, many important questions remain open. We highlight a few broad directions.

虽然特定的分离结果表明，对于精心构造的函数类，深度具有指数级的好处，但仍然缺乏通用函数类的综合理论，特别是在高维度上。平滑、组合或结构化函数类的深度-宽度权衡的精确量化仍然是一个开放的挑战，目前还不清楚现有结果在多大程度上反映了基本现象与所使用的特定结构的人工制品。While specific separation results demonstrate exponential benefits of depth for carefully constructed function classes, a comprehensive theory for general function classes, particularly in high dimensions, is still lacking. Precise quantification of depth–width trade-offs for smooth, compositional, or structured function classes remains an open challenge, and it is unclear to what extent existing results reflect fundamental phenomena versus artefacts of the particular constructions used.

这里回顾的大多数理论结果都集中在带有 ReLU 或 sigmoidal 激活的前馈架构上。卷积网络 (CNN) 和更现代的架构（例如具有注意力机制的转换器、归一化层和图神经网络）的近似理论特性还远没有被很好地理解，为这些架构开发相对成熟的理论是一个重要的开放问题。Most theoretical results reviewed here focus on the feedforward architecture with ReLU or sigmoidal activations. The approximation-theoretic properties of convolutional networks (CNNs) and more modern architectures, such as transformers with attention mechanisms, normalisation layers, and graph neural networks, are far less well understood, and developing a comparably mature theory for these architectures is an important open problem.

KAN 的近似理论也处于早期阶段。虽然 KAN 提供了一种基于 Kolmogorov-Arnold 表示定理的有吸引力的替代架构范式，但仍需要在逼近率和参数效率方面与经典 FNN 进行严格、全面的比较。The approximation theory of KANs is also in its early stages. While KANs offer an appealing alternative architectural paradigm grounded in the Kolmogorov–Arnold representation theorem, a rigorous and comprehensive comparison with classical FNNs in terms of approximation rates and parameter efficiency remains to be carried out.

解决这些问题不仅可以加深我们对神经网络的理论理解，还可以为实践中具有表达能力和计算效率的架构设计提供信息。Addressing these questions will not only deepen our theoretical understanding of neural networks but also inform the design of architectures that are both expressive and computationally efficient in practice.
