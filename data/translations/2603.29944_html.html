摘要Abstract

量子传感技术为超灵敏生物医学传感提供了变革潜力，但其临床转化仍然受到经典噪声限制和对宏观整体的依赖的限制。我们提出了一个统一的世代框架，根据量子资源的利用来组织量子生物传感器的不断发展的格局。第一代设备利用离散能级进行信号传导，但遵循经典的缩放定律。第二代传感器利用量子相干性来达到标准量子极限，而第三代架构则利用纠缠和自旋挤压来接近海森堡极限的精度。我们进一步定义了新兴的第四代，其特征是量子传感与量子学习和变分电路的端到端集成，从而能够直接在量子域内进行自适应推理。通过分析带宽匹配和传感器组织接近度等关键参数，我们确定了关键技术瓶颈，并提出了从测量物理可观测值过渡到利用量子增强智能提取结构化生物信息的路线图。Quantum sensing technologies offer transformative potential for ultra-sensitive biomedical sensing, yet their clinical translation remains constrained by classical noise limits and a reliance on macroscopic ensembles. We propose a unifying generational framework to organize the evolving landscape of quantum biosensors based on their utilization of quantum resources. First-generation devices utilize discrete energy levels for signal transduction but follow classical scaling laws. Second-generation sensors exploit quantum coherence to reach the standard quantum limit, while third-generation architectures leverage entanglement and spin squeezing to approach Heisenberg-limited precision. We further define an emerging fourth generation characterized by the end-to-end integration of quantum sensing with quantum learning and variational circuits, enabling adaptive inference directly within the quantum domain. By analyzing critical parameters such as bandwidth matching and sensor–tissue proximity, we identify key technological bottlenecks and propose a roadmap for transitioning from measuring physical observables to extracting structured biological information with quantum-enhanced intelligence.

I 简介Introduction

量子传感已成为测量科学的变革范式，利用量子力学的基本原理以前所未有的灵敏度和精度检测物理量Quantum sensing has emerged as a transformative paradigm in measurement science, exploiting the fundamental principles of quantum mechanics to detect physical quantities with unprecedented sensitivity and precision[36, 47, 6, 8]。传统的生物医学传感依赖于宏观转导和受经典噪声限制的整体平均信号，与此相反，量子传感器将物理参数编码为量子态，可以以更高的精度测量其演化. In contrast to conventional biomedical sensing, which relies on macroscopic transduction and ensemble-averaged signals limited by classical noise, quantum sensors encode physical parameters into quantum states whose evolution can be measured with enhanced precision[49]。通过利用量子叠加、相干性和纠缠，量子传感能够检测极弱的磁场和电场、温度变化、压力波动以及化学或分子环境. By harnessing quantum superposition, coherence, and entanglement, quantum sensing enables detection of extremely weak magnetic and electric fields, temperature variations, pressure fluctuations, and chemical or molecular environments[142]。这些功能允许灵敏度超越标准量子极限 (SQL)，并且原则上接近海森堡极限. These capabilities allow sensitivities that surpass the standard quantum limit (SQL) and in principle approach the Heisenberg limit[150]。经典生物医学传感器最终依赖于微观层面的量子物理学，但它们并没有刻意操纵相干性或纠缠等量子资源。相比之下，量子传感器明确地将量子态设计为计量资源，以提高测量精度。. Classical biomedical sensors ultimately depend on quantum physics at the microscopic level, but they do not deliberately manipulate quantum resources such as coherence or entanglement. Quantum sensors, in contrast, explicitly engineer quantum states as metrological resources to enhance measurement precision.

代际框架的重要性。The Importance of a Generational Framework.生物医学量子传感的快速发展催生了多样化的传感平台，包括超导电路、氮空位 (NV) 中心、原子磁力计和纠缠增强计量学。尽管取得了这些进展，该领域仍然缺乏一个系统框架来根据传感架构所使用的量子资源来比较它们。现有的讨论往往仍然分散且针对特定平台，因此很难确定技术成熟度和未来的发展轨迹。在这里，我们提出了一种生物医学量子传感器的世代框架，该框架根据量子资源利用的深度来组织传感技术。该框架提供了概念的清晰度，为技术成熟度建立了一致的基准，并确定了从物理信号检测到智能量子增强推理的自然进展。Rapid advances in biomedical quantum sensing have produced a diverse landscape of sensing platforms, including superconducting circuits, nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers, atomic magnetometers, and entanglement-enhanced metrology. Despite this progress, the field lacks a systematic framework for comparing sensing architectures according to the quantum resources they employ. Existing discussions often remain fragmented and platform-specific, making it difficult to identify technological maturity and future development trajectories. Here we propose a generational framework for biomedical quantum sensors that organizes sensing technologies according to the depth of quantum resource utilization. This framework provides conceptual clarity, establishes consistent benchmarks for technological maturity, and identifies the natural progression from physical signal detection toward intelligent quantum-enhanced inference.

V 结论Conclusion

从这个角度来看，我们引入了一个世代框架，根据所使用的量子资源的深度来组织生物医学量子传感器的演变，涵盖第一代系统中的能级读出、第二代平台中基于相干性的计量、第三代架构中的工程纠缠和挤压，以及未来第四代中的集成量子学习和信息处理策略。这种分类法在快速扩展的领域提供了概念清晰性，在该领域中，技术成熟度、物理约束和量子资源利用通常可以互换对待。In this perspective, we introduced a generational framework that organizes the evolution of biomedical quantum sensors according to the depth of quantum resources employed, spanning energy-level readout in first-generation systems, coherence-based metrology in second-generation platforms, engineered entanglement and squeezing in third-generation architectures, and integrated quantum learning and information-processing strategies in a prospective fourth generation. This taxonomy provides conceptual clarity in a rapidly expanding field where technological maturity, physical constraints, and quantum resource utilization are often treated interchangeably.

第一代平台表明，仅存在量子能级并不能保证量子优势。第二代传感器利用相干相位演化作为计量资源，并在 MRI、基于 SQUID 的磁力测量和光泵磁力计等技术中实现了具有临床意义的部署，尽管许多实现仍然受到部分相干利用和整体平均而不是基本量子缩放的限制。第三代架构通过刻意设计多体相关性来重新分配测量噪声并增强灵敏度，从而引入了质的转变；固态、原子、光子和超导系统的实验演示证实了在受控实验室条件下可测量的增益，即使向复杂生物环境的转换仍然受到退相干、可扩展性和集成挑战的限制。拟议的第四代通过将传感嵌入更广泛的量子信息处理架构中来扩展这一轨迹。通过集成自适应控制、变分优化、分布式网络和转换接口，这些系统旨在直接在物理层实现数据驱动推理，特别是在安全性和侵入性限制测量重复的生物医学环境中。然而，量子处理和学习提出了大量的计算要求：变分量子电路需要迭代经典量子优化循环，其成本随着电路深度、量子位计数和变分参数的数量而变化，而分布式量子传感器网络增加了通信和同步开销。是否可以在临床决策的实时延迟限制内满足这些计算要求，例如术中指导或连续生理监测，仍然是一个开放且重要的部署挑战。First-generation platforms demonstrate that the presence of quantized energy levels alone does not guarantee quantum advantage. Second-generation sensors harness coherent phase evolution as a metrological resource and have achieved clinically meaningful deployment in technologies such as MRI, SQUID-based magnetometry, and optically pumped magnetometers, although many implementations remain limited by partial coherence utilization and ensemble averaging rather than fundamental quantum scaling. Third-generation architectures introduce a qualitative transition by deliberately engineering many-body correlations to redistribute measurement noise and enhance sensitivity; experimental demonstrations across solid-state, atomic, photonic, and superconducting systems confirm measurable gains under controlled laboratory conditions, even as translation to complex biological environments remains constrained by decoherence, scalability, and integration challenges. The proposed fourth generation extends this trajectory by embedding sensing within broader quantum information-processing architectures. By integrating adaptive control, variational optimization, distributed networks, and transduction interfaces, these systems aim to enable data-driven inference directly at the physical layer, particularly in biomedical contexts where safety and invasiveness limit measurement repetition. However, quantum processing and learning impose substantial computational demands: variational quantum circuits require iterative classical–quantum optimization loops whose cost scales with circuit depth, qubit count, and the number of variational parameters, while distributed quantum sensor networks add communication and synchronization overhead. Whether these computational requirements can be met within the real-time latency constraints of clinical decision-making, such as intraoperative guidance or continuous physiological monitoring, remains an open and important deployment challenge.

除了临床转化之外，量子传感器还在基础医学领域开辟了同样值得重视的新途径。在亚细胞尺度上，氮空位磁力测量能够直接观察单个神经元、趋磁细菌和免疫标记肿瘤切片的磁特征，为以前低于任何现有技术检测阈值的生物现象提供实验途径。在药理学和药物发现中，量子计算和纳米级量子传感器能够对蛋白质-药物相互作用进行分子模拟，并在单分子水平上对药物-靶标结合动力学进行无标记实时监测，从而通过机制洞察补充传统的高通量筛选Beyond clinical translation, quantum sensors are opening new avenues in fundamental medical science that merit equal emphasis. At the subcellular scale, nitrogen-vacancy magnetometry has enabled direct observation of magnetic signatures from individual neurons, magnetotactic bacteria, and immunolabeled tumor sections, providing experimental access to biological phenomena that were previously below the detection threshold of any existing technique. In pharmacology and drug discovery, quantum computing and nanoscale quantum sensors are enabling molecular simulation of protein–drug interactions and label-free, real-time monitoring of drug–target binding kinetics at the single-molecule level, complementing conventional high-throughput screening with mechanistic insight[115, 16]。由单个 NV 中心实现的纳米级核磁共振已经实现了 zeptoliter 样品体积的化学光谱，为天然条件下膜蛋白、代谢物和生物分子复合物的结构生物学建立了新的实验范式。这些对基础生物医学研究的贡献，涵盖生物物理学、神经科学、肿瘤学和药理学，表明量子传感的影响远远超出了临床诊断范围，应该为未来研究方向和临床整合工作的优先顺序提供信息。. Nanoscale nuclear magnetic resonance enabled by single NV centers has achieved chemical spectroscopy of zeptoliter sample volumes, establishing a new experimental paradigm for structural biology of membrane proteins, metabolites, and biomolecular complexes under native conditions. These contributions to basic biomedical research, spanning biophysics, neuroscience, oncology, and pharmacology, demonstrate that the impact of quantum sensing extends well beyond clinical diagnostics and should inform the prioritization of future research directions alongside clinical integration efforts.

仅世代深度并不能决定生物医学适用性。带宽、传感器样本几何形状、热兼容性和临床安全性施加了影响实际部署的独立约束，而生理过程跨越空间和时间范围，没有一个平台能够完全解决。近期里程碑，例如在环境条件下展示生物样品中纠缠增强的灵敏度或对第三代协议进行初步临床验证，对于测试实验室规模的量子增益是否转化为可测量的诊断改进非常重要。持续的进步将需要量子资源工程、器件物理和生物信息系统设计方面的协调进步，以及仔细的实验​​验证。因此，从能级读出到纠缠和学习集成架构的转变不仅反映了量子复杂性的增加，而且反映了不断扩大的雄心：从测量孤立的可观察量转向在现实临床约束下提取结构化生物信息。Generational depth alone does not determine biomedical suitability. Bandwidth, sensor–sample geometry, thermal compatibility, and clinical safety impose independent constraints that shape practical deployment, and physiological processes span spatial and temporal regimes that no single platform fully addresses. Near-term milestones, such as demonstrating entanglement-enhanced sensitivity in a biological sample under ambient conditions or conducting initial clinical validation of third-generation protocols, will be important for testing whether laboratory-scale quantum gains translate into measurable diagnostic improvements. Continued progress will require coordinated advances in quantum resource engineering, device physics, and biologically informed system design, alongside careful experimental validation. The transition from energy-level readout to entanglement-enabled and learning-integrated architectures therefore reflects not only increasing quantum complexity but an expanding ambition: to move from measuring isolated observables toward extracting structured biological information under realistic clinical constraints.

B 第一代量子医疗传感器First-Generation Quantum Medical Sensor

B.1 磁阻生物传感器 (GMR/TMR)Magnetoresistive biosensors (GMR/TMR)

磁阻生物传感器提供了生物医学应用中第一代量子传感的典型示例，其中利用离散的自旋相关电子态在环境条件下实现稳健的信号转导。 Baselt及其同事首先将巨磁阻传感器演示为生物传感平台，他们表明可以通过自旋相关散射引起的电阻变化来检测磁性标记的DNA杂交和蛋白质结合事件Magnetoresistive biosensors provide a paradigmatic example of first-generation quantum sensing in biomedical applications, where discrete spin-dependent electronic states are exploited for robust signal transduction under ambient conditions. Giant magnetoresistance sensors were first demonstrated as biosensing platforms by Baselt and colleagues, who showed that magnetically labeled DNA hybridization and protein binding events could be detected through resistance changes induced by spin-dependent scattering[12]。基于这一原理，Osterfeld 及其同事开发了高密度 GMR 纳米传感器阵列，能够同时对多种蛋白质相互作用进行实时动力学分析，从而实现临床相关规模的多重诊断和芯片实验室集成. Building on this principle, Osterfeld and colleagues developed high-density GMR nanosensor arrays capable of real-time kinetic analysis across multiple protein interactions simultaneously, enabling multiplexed diagnostics and lab-on-chip integration at clinically relevant scales[104]。隧道磁阻传感器通过利用自旋相关的穿过薄绝缘势垒的隧道效应扩展了这一范例，从而大大提高了灵敏度。最近的实施已将 TMR 传感器集成到针形磁极中，用于微创神经生理学测量，实现亚纳特斯拉灵敏度，足以解析单个神经元动作电位产生的磁场. Tunnel magnetoresistance sensors extend this paradigm by exploiting spin-dependent tunneling through thin insulating barriers, yielding substantially enhanced sensitivity. Recent implementations have integrated TMR sensors into needle-shaped magnetrodes for minimally invasive neurophysiological measurements, achieving sub-nanotesla sensitivity sufficient to resolve magnetic fields generated by individual neuronal action potentials[83]。补充研究表明，基于 TMR 的嵌入组织模型中的磁性纳米粒子的检测与磁热疗监测直接相关，其中治疗剂的精确空间定位至关重要. Complementary studies have demonstrated TMR-based detection of magnetic nanoparticles embedded within tissue phantoms, with direct relevance to magnetic hyperthermia monitoring where accurate spatial localization of therapeutic agents is essential[45]。总之，这些结果凸显了磁阻平台用于即时诊断和连续生理监测的可扩展性和临床相关性。. Together, these results highlight the scalability and clinical relevance of magnetoresistive platforms for point-of-care diagnostics and continuous physiological monitoring.

B.2 量子点和SETQuantum Dots and SET

量子点和单电子器件构成了在纳米尺度上运行的第一代量子传感器的补充类，其中电子态的量化能够实现超灵敏的分子检测。由于其尺寸可调的发射光谱和出色的光稳定性，量子点已成为基于荧光的生物医学成像不可或缺的工具。 Chan 和 Nie 的开创性工作表明，生物共轭量子点可以作为癌症生物标志物检测的强大探针，克服传统有机荧光团的光漂白局限性Quantum dots and single-electron devices constitute a complementary class of first-generation quantum sensors operating at the nanoscale, where quantization of electronic states enables ultrasensitive molecular detection. Quantum dots have become indispensable tools for fluorescence-based biomedical imaging due to their size-tunable emission spectra and exceptional photostability. The seminal work by Chan and Nie demonstrated that bioconjugated quantum dots could serve as robust probes for cancer biomarker detection, overcoming the photobleaching limitations of conventional organic fluorophores[32]。高及其同事随后的体内研究表明，肽功能化的近红外量子点可以通过受体介导的途径选择性地在肿瘤组织中积累，从而在生命系统中实现高对比度分子成像. Subsequent in vivo studies by Gao and colleagues showed that peptide-functionalized near-infrared quantum dots can selectively accumulate in tumor tissue through receptor-mediated pathways, enabling high-contrast molecular imaging in living systems[42]。最近的评论强调将量子点集成到混合电学和光学生物传感架构中，特别是对于灵敏度和小型化至关重要的核酸检测. More recent reviews have emphasized the integration of quantum dots into hybrid electrical and optical biosensing architectures, particularly for nucleic acid detection where sensitivity and miniaturization are critical[128].

单电子晶体管通过利用电荷量子化和库仑封锁效应，为量子生物传感提供了另一种途径。虽然早期的设备需要低温操作，但硅纳米加工的进步使得基于在绝缘体上硅基板上制造的多岛架构的室温生物传感器成为可能Single-electron transistors offer an alternative route to quantum-enabled biosensing by exploiting charge quantization and Coulomb blockade effects. While early devices required cryogenic operation, advances in silicon nanofabrication have enabled room-temperature biosensors based on multi-island architectures fabricated on silicon-on-insulator substrates[94]。这些设备表现出卓越的电荷灵敏度，能够检测生物分子相互作用，例如皮摩尔浓度下的链霉亲和素结合，以及临床相关阈值下前列腺特异性抗原的免疫检测. These devices exhibit exceptional charge sensitivity, enabling detection of biomolecular interactions such as streptavidin binding at picomolar concentrations and immunodetection of prostate-specific antigen at clinically relevant thresholds[93]。使用多孔硅肖特基结的宏观实施进一步证明单电子效应可以在厘米级区域内保留，为适合临床部署的低成本、大面积生物传感器阵列开辟了道路. A macroscopic implementation using porous silicon Schottky junctions further demonstrated that single-electron effects can be preserved across centimeter-scale areas, opening pathways toward low-cost, large-area biosensor arrays suitable for clinical deployment[5]。当与微流体和器官芯片平台集成时，这些纳米级量子传感器为实时监测细胞反应和个性化治疗筛选提供了有希望的途径。. When integrated with microfluidic and organ-on-chip platforms, these nanoscale quantum sensors offer promising routes toward real-time monitoring of cellular responses and personalized therapeutic screening.
