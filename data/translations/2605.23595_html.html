通过元学习进行经济高效的模型评估Cost-Effective Model Evaluation with Meta-Learning

Trinh Pham, Viet Huynh, Hongzhi Yin, Quoc Viet Hung Nguyen, Thanh Tam Nguyen
摘要。Abstract.

机器学习的快速发展催生了一个不断扩大的模型生态系统，使得在看不见的、未标记的数据上验证新发布的模型的可靠性变得越来越具有挑战性。传统的评估流程依赖于昂贵的注释、重复的微调或无法跨模型系列转移的狭隘假设。我们呈现The rapid growth of machine learning has produced an ever-expanding ecosystem of models, making it increasingly challenging to verify the reliability of newly released models on unseen, unlabeled data. Conventional evaluation pipelines depend on expensive annotation, repeated fine-tuning, or narrow assumptions that fail to transfer across model families. We present元评估器MetaEvaluator，一个经济高效、与模型无关的框架，用于对跨越不同架构和模式的未见过的模型进行快速、无标签的评估。, a cost-effective, model-agnostic framework for rapid, label-free assessment of unseen models spanning diverse architectures and modalities.元评估器MetaEvaluator利用对参考模型池的元学习来获得可转移的初始化，从而能够准确评估新模型，同时在整个池中分摊成本并消除对每个模型重新训练的需要。据我们所知，这是第一个与模型无关的框架，能够在完全未标记的数据集上评估新模型。大量实验表明leverages meta-learning over a pool of reference models to obtain a transferable initialization, enabling accurate evaluation of new models while amortizing cost across the pool and removing the need for per-model retraining. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first model-agnostic framework capable of evaluating new models on entirely unlabeled datasets. Extensive experiments show that元评估器MetaEvaluator与传统方法相比，以大幅降低的成本生成稳定且准确的性能估计，从而使基于未标记数据的新兴模型的可扩展基准测试变得实用。produces stable and accurate performance estimates at substantially reduced cost compared to conventional approaches, making scalable benchmarking of emerging models on unlabeled data practical.

1. 简介Introduction

机器学习的最新进展是由大型预训练模型系列推动的——涵盖推荐领域Recent progress in machine learning is driven by large pretrained model families—spanning domains from recommendation(Nguyen et al., 2024b; Sakong et al., 2024; Nguyen et al., 2024a; Trung et al., 2022; Toan et al., 2018)图学习to graph learning(Duong et al., 2022a; Thang et al., 2022; Huynh et al., 2021; Nguyen et al., 2023b; Duong et al., 2022b)——以及快速增长的数据收集，其中大部分仍未标记—and rapidly growing data collections, most of which remain unlabeled(Liu and Yang, 2025; He et al., 2025)。这种趋势给组织带来了一个核心部署问题：在许多新发布的和未见过的模型中为未标记的工作负载进行选择。考虑一个部署新的文本到 SQL 模型来查询内部企业数据库的组织。没有标记的问题 - 存在 SQL 对，手动注释需要领域专家和数周的努力. This trend introduces a core deployment problem for organizations: choosing among many newly released and unseen models for an unlabeled workload. Consider an organization that deploys a new Text-to-SQL model to query an internal enterprise database. No labeled question–SQL pairs exist, and manual annotation would require domain experts and weeks of effort(Nguyen et al., 2014, 2015b; Hung et al., 2017; Nguyen et al., 2017)，使得快速模型选择变得不切实际。此设置面临双重挑战：模型不可见，并且目标数据集完全未标记。在实践中，此类模型选择决策越来越与可信度问题交织在一起，包括对中毒的鲁棒性、联合和隐私保护部署以及可解释性, making rapid model selection impractical. This setting exposes a double challenge: the model is unseen, and the target dataset is entirely unlabeled. In practice, such model-selection decisions are increasingly intertwined with trustworthiness concerns—including robustness to poisoning, federated and privacy-preserving deployment, and explainability(Nguyen et al., 2024c; Nguyen Thanh et al., 2023; Nguyen et al., 2026a; Yang et al., 2024; Huynh et al., 2024, 2025; Nguyen et al., 2025a, b, 2023c, 2022)——这使得部署前可靠、无标签的评估变得更加有价值。然而，大多数现有的评估流程一次只在一个模型上运行。有些需要人工或伪标签—which makes reliable, label-free assessment before deployment all the more valuable. Most existing evaluation pipelines nevertheless operate on one model at a time. Some require human or pseudo-labeling(Boyeau et al., 2025; Angelopoulos et al., 2023; Fisch et al., 2024; van Breugel et al., 2023)。其他的则靠反复微调. Judge-based systems using large language models (LLMs) have also been proposed(Gu et al., 2024; Zheng et al., 2023a; Liu et al., 2025)。然而，这些技术是针对固定架构和特定于模型的行为而设计的，并且会产生大量的计算成本. 3. 问题表述Problem Formulation

受先前工作的限制的启发，我们形式化了一种设置，其中评估的模型和目标数据集在部署时都是不可见且未标记的。为了应对这一双重挑战，我们从经典的监督学习开始，并将其扩展到无标签评估和元学习。在单模型情况下，监督学习用于训练评估器，该评估器是数据集级信号的回归器，总结：训练-测试不匹配和模型的真实准确性。然后，我们通过在许多参考模型和转变中聚合此类监督评估问题，将该公式提升为元学习，以便评估器能够适应未标记工作负载的新架构。Motivated by the limitations of prior work, we formalize a setting in which both the evaluated model and the target dataset are unseen and unlabeled at deployment time. To address this double challenge, we start from classical supervised learning and extend it to label-free evaluation and meta-learning. In the single-model case, supervised learning is used to train an evaluator which is a regressor over dataset-level signals that summarize: train–test mismatch and the model’s true accuracy. We then lift this formulation to meta-learning by aggregating such supervised evaluation problems across many reference models and shifts so that the evaluator can adapt to new architectures on unlabeled workloads.
