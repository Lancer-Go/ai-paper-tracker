自我监督比临床监督更能推动医学基础模型的代表性趋同Self-supervision drives representational convergence in medical foundation models more than clinical supervision

Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh∗    Sebastian Ziegelmayer    Mahshad Lotfinia    Lisa Adams    Sven Nebelung    Jakob Nikolas Kather    Daniel Truhn
摘要Abstract

假设规模和临床监督将其表示集中到共享结构上，来自不同群体的医学图像编码器越来越多地被视为可互换的。这种趋同是否真实、产生它的原因以及它是否在临床上可用都未经测试，而且此类主张背后的相似性衡量标准也很脆弱。我们提出了跨 18 个图像和 7 个文本编码器的受控解剖，所有编码器都是开放重量并在本地运行，涵盖 7M 到 27B 参数和五种成像模式，包括来自六个数据集的 650,982 张胸部 X 光照片。为了隔离原因，我们训练编码器，使其在固定数据、架构和规模下仅改变目标，并在合成模型中重现效果。收敛程度不大，但高于随机下限，由自我监督目标驱动，而不是临床监督：匹配的自我监督编码器对齐最多（胸片上的 40.4％），标签监督的（21.1％）和图像文本（3.3％）要低得多，并且不随大小而增长（Spearman 0.302，Medical image encoders from different groups are increasingly treated as interchangeable, on the assumption that scale and clinical supervision concentrate their representations onto a shared structure. Whether this convergence is real, what produces it, and whether it is clinically usable are untested, and the similarity measures behind such claims are fragile. We present a controlled dissection across 18 image and 7 text encoders, all open-weight and run locally, spanning 7M to 27B parameters and five imaging modalities, including 650,982 chest radiographs from six datasets. To isolate cause, we train encoders that vary only the objective under fixed data, architecture, and scale, and reproduce the effect in a synthetic model. Convergence is modest but above a random floor, driven by the self-supervised objective, not clinical supervision: matched self-supervised encoders aligned most (40.4% on chest radiography), with label-supervised (21.1%) and image-text (3.3%) far lower, and did not grow with size (Spearman 0.302,p=0.223p=0.223）或能力。它是模态内的，没有达到临床语言，并且没有再现放射科医生如何判断病例相似性。然而，线性分类器跨编码器传输并传输到五家保留的医院，保留了大约 85% 的编码器内性能。因此，医学成像的收敛性是由预训练目标设定的，而不是从规模或临床监督中继承的。因此，互操作性是通过该目标进行设计的，并根据临床判断在患者亚组中验证共享几何结构最薄弱的地方。) or capability. It is within-modality, does not reach clinical language, and does not reproduce how radiologists judge case similarity. Yet a linear classifier transfers across encoders and to five held-out hospitals, retaining about 85% of within-encoder performance. Convergence in medical imaging is therefore set by the pretraining objective, not inherited from scale or clinical supervision. Interoperability is accordingly something to design for through that objective, and to validate where the shared geometry is weakest, across patient subgroups and against clinical judgment.

简介Introduction

基础模型已广泛应用于医学成像领域。现在存在用于胸部X光照片的图像编码器Foundation models have spread across medical imaging. Image encoders now exist for chest radiographs[57, 68, 77], 组织病理学, histopathology[8, 72, 76, 46, 18], 视网膜摄影, retinal photography[79]和肿瘤成像, and oncologic imaging[56]，以及生物医学视觉语言助手, alongside biomedical vision-language assistants[42, 78]以及更广泛地推动通用医疗人工智能and a broader push toward generalist medical artificial intelligence[49]。这种势头延伸到用于放射学问答、循证监督、可靠性评估和临床研究自动化的临床大语言模型. This momentum extends to clinical large language models for radiology question answering, evidence-grounded supervision, reliability assessment, and the automation of clinical research[65, 75, 2, 66, 64]。这些编码器是由不同的团队根据不同的数据、不同的规模独立生成的，但它们利用了一组共享的预训练方法：监督视觉变换器（ViT）. These encoders are produced independently, by different groups, on different data, and at different scales, yet they draw on a shared and small set of pretraining recipes: supervised vision transformers (ViTs)[15]，蒙版图像建模, masked-image modeling[24], 自蒸馏, self-distillation[6, 55], 对比实例判别, contrastive instance discrimination[9, 25, 22]和语言监督对比学习, and language-supervised contrastive learning[59]。一个引人注目的猜想，即柏拉图表示假说，认为随着此类模型的发展和训练更多的数据和任务，它们的内部表示会趋向于一个共享的世界统计模型. A striking conjecture, the Platonic Representation Hypothesis, holds that as such models grow and train on more data and tasks, their internal representations converge toward a shared statistical model of the world[28]。如果医学上确实如此，那么由不同机构构建的编码器将共享通用的几何结构，从而为互操作性、模型重用和不依赖于其背后的特定编码器的临床工具打开了大门。. Were this true in medicine, encoders built by separate institutions would share a common geometry, opening the door to interoperability, model reuse, and clinical tools that do not depend on the specific encoder behind them.

这种融合是否真实，以及驱动它的因素是什么，目前尚无定论。收敛性是根据代表性相似性度量和典型相关变体推断出来的Whether such convergence is real, and what would drive it, is unsettled. Convergence is inferred from representational-similarity measures, from canonical correlation variants[60, 50]居中内核对齐to centered kernel alignment[38]和邻居重叠分数and neighbor-overlap scores[28]，互补线通过模型拼接和共享潜在坐标直接测试可互换性, and a complementary line tests interchangeability directly through model stitching and shared latent coordinates[40, 3, 51]。最近的分析表明，这些测量结果很脆弱：跨模式对齐很小，很大程度上取决于评估机制，随着样本池的增长而崩溃，并且更强的模型对齐更多的趋势并不总是成立. Recent analyses show these measurements are fragile: cross-modal alignment is small, depends heavily on the evaluation regime, collapses as the sample pool grows, and the trend that stronger models align more does not consistently hold[37]。主导账户归功于规模、任务多样性，以及在医学领域，监督和基于报告的目标所注入的强烈临床信号。然而，相反的帖子报告说，仅图像自我监督与医疗编码器的语言监督相匹配或胜过. The dominant account credits scale, task diversity, and, in medicine, the strong clinical signal that supervised and report-based objectives inject. Yet a contrary thread reports that image-only self-supervision matches or beats language supervision for medical encoders[57, 68]，这增加了临床信号根本不是调整模型的因素的可能性。同样未知的是，医学编码器中的任何融合是否足够强大以在临床上有用，它是否随着模型大小和能力而增长，以及它在患者群体之间是否一致，因为代表性不足的人群的代表性差距可能会加剧医学成像中记录的不平等, raising the possibility that the clinical signal is not what aligns models at all. It is likewise unknown whether any convergence in medical encoders is strong enough to be clinically useful, whether it grows with model size and capability, and whether it is uniform across patient groups, since representational gaps for under-represented populations could entrench documented inequities in medical imaging[62, 20, 39, 54, 21].

我们对跨医学基础模型的代表性收敛进行了大规模的、受控的剖析，旨在在每个轴上进行广泛的研究（图 1）。We present a large, controlled dissection of representational convergence across medical foundation models, designed to be broad on every axis (Fig.1）。这些数据涵盖五种成像模式，包括胸部 X 光照片、十亿像素全幻灯片组织病理学切片、视网膜眼底照片、皮肤镜图像和乳房 X 光检查，以及根据国际疾病分类第十次修订版 (ICD-10) 层次结构和疾病共现建立的临床本体参考。仅胸部X光片基质就利用了来自美国、西班牙和越南三个国家的六个数据集，并包括一个专门的儿科队列。它包含来自记录患者标识符的站点中超过 219,000 名独特患者的 650,000 多张 X 线照片，六个站点中的五个作为跨站点泛化的外部目标。组织病理学部门在组织分类和肿瘤检测补丁旁边添加了超过 640,000 个图像标题对，其编码器是在不相交的机构集合上训练的整体幻灯片基础模型，这可以清晰地测试收敛是否反映共享训练数据。). The data span five imaging modalities, examined as chest radiographs, gigapixel whole-slide histopathology tiles, retinal fundus photographs, dermoscopic images, and mammograms, together with a clinical ontology reference built from the International Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision (ICD-10) hierarchy and disease co-occurrence. The chest radiograph substrate alone draws on six datasets from three countries, the United States, Spain, and Vietnam, and includes a dedicated pediatric cohort. It contains more than 650,000 radiographs from over 219,000 unique patients across the sites that record patient identifiers, with five of the six sites held out as external targets for cross-site generalization. The histopathology arm adds more than 640,000 image-caption pairs alongside tissue-classification and tumor-detection patches, and its encoders are whole-slide foundation models trained on disjoint institutional collections, which gives a clean test of whether convergence reflects shared training data.

编码器面板同样广泛：18 个图像编码器和 7 个生物医学文本编码器，涵盖 700 万到 270 亿个参数，发布年份从 2020 年到 2025 年，开发人员遍布学术界和工业界，涵盖监督、掩模图像、自蒸馏、对比和图像文本预训练。每个模型都是开放重量的，并作为冻结特征提取器在本地运行。为了将训练目标与临床信号分开，我们在固定数据、架构和规模的受控矩阵下训练编码器，同时改变训练目标（自监督、标签监督和图像文本），并在直接设置标签信息的完全指定的合成生成模型中重现效果。然后我们询问共享结构在实际部署转变下是否可用The encoder panel is equally broad: 18 image encoders and 7 biomedical text encoders, spanning 7 million to 27 billion parameters, release years from 2020 to 2025, and developers across academia and industry, and covering supervised, masked-image, self-distilled, contrastive, and image-text pretraining. Every model is open-weight and run locally as a frozen feature extractor. To separate the training objective from the clinical signal, we train encoders under a controlled matrix that fixes data, architecture, and scale while varying the training objective (self-supervised, label-supervised, and image-text), and we reproduce the effect in a fully specified synthetic generative model where label informativeness is set directly. We then ask whether the shared structure is usable under realistic deployment shift[19]：我们在一个基于锚的通用坐标系中一起传输线性诊断读数和缝合编码器: we transfer linear diagnostic readouts and stitch encoders together in a common anchor-based coordinate system[51]，并且我们审核跨人口统计亚组的共享几何形状的统一程度。据我们所知，这是第一个关于在医学基础模型中产生表征收敛的受控解释，也是第一个直接测试这种收敛是否可以跨编码器和站点部署的解释。, and we audit how uniform the shared geometry is across demographic subgroups. To our knowledge this is the first controlled account of what produces representational convergence in medical foundation models, and the first to test directly whether that convergence is deployable across encoders and sites.

我们的框架颠覆了通常的假设。我们没有将收敛视为规模和临床监督的副产品，而是问它是否是自我监督目标本身的属性，以及适度的收敛是否仍然可以在模型之间携带临床工具。我们将对齐视为依赖于制度的测量，而不是单个数字，并且我们在正结果旁边报告负结果，包括收敛失败的情况。其目的是清楚而诚实地说明医学成像中的表征融合何时是真实的、产生它的原因以及当临床工具背后的编码器发生变化时它是否可信。Our framing inverts the usual assumption. Instead of treating convergence as a byproduct of scale and clinical supervision, we ask whether it is a property of the self-supervised objective itself, and whether a convergence that is only modest can still carry clinical tools between models. We treat alignment as a regime-dependent measurement, not a single number, and we report negative results, including where convergence fails, next to the positive ones. The aim is a clear and honest account of when representational convergence in medical imaging is real, what generates it, and whether it can be trusted when the encoder behind a clinical tool changes.

讨论Discussion

独立训练的医学图像编码器越来越多地被视为可互换的，假设临床标签和报告监督将它们集中到共享的诊断表示上，更广泛地说，规模和任务性能将基础模型拉向世界的柏拉图式表示Independently trained medical image encoders are increasingly treated as interchangeable, on the assumption that clinical label and report supervision concentrate them onto a shared diagnostic representation, and more broadly that scale and task performance pull foundation models toward one Platonic representation of the world[28]。我们在一个大型且故意异构的小组中测试了这一假设，结果与预期相反。医疗编码器之间的融合是真实存在的，但程度并不高。它受自我监督培训目标的约束，而不是受临床监督的约束。这是一种未达到临床语言的模态内现象，它不会随着模型大小或性能而增强，并且在不同患者群体中表现不均匀。然而，它足够具体，可以通过共享坐标系跨编码器和跨站点携带诊断分类器。因此，共享代表比主流说法的预测更弱，但更有用。. We tested that assumption across a large and deliberately heterogeneous panel, and the answer inverts the expectation. Convergence among medical encoders is real but modest. It is governed by the self-supervised training objective and not by clinical supervision. It is a within-modality phenomenon that does not reach clinical language, it does not strengthen with model size or performance, and it is uneven across patient groups. Yet it is concrete enough that a diagnostic classifier can be carried across encoders and across sites through a shared coordinate system. The shared representation is therefore weaker, and more useful, than the prevailing account predicts.

核心结果是训练目标，特别是自我监督，是调整这些编码器的因素。这将柏拉图式的叙述中纠缠在一起的两个想法分开了。最初的假设将收敛归因于规模和下游绩效，并将其视为与目标无关的The central result is that the training objective, and specifically self-supervision, is what aligns these encoders. This separates two ideas that the Platonic account leaves entangled. The original hypothesis attributes convergence to scale and downstream performance and treats it as largely indifferent to the objective[28]。我们的受控和综合实验将目标置于中心，它们表明，将临床标签或报告目标添加到其他相同的主干上会使其代表远离而不是朝向其同行。该方向在医学外部得到了独立证实，其中自监督视觉编码器在图像分类和图像文本模型之上的数据集上具有最一致的成对相似性结构. Our controlled and synthetic experiments place the objective at the center, and they show that adding a clinical-label or report objective to an otherwise identical backbone moves its representation away from, and not toward, its peers. The direction is independently corroborated outside medicine, where self-supervised vision encoders carry the most consistent pairwise similarity structure across datasets, above image-classification and image-text models[10]。它还解释了医学成像中的一个长期难题，即仅图像自监督在下游任务上反复匹配或击败语言监督预训练. It also explains a standing puzzle in medical imaging, where image-only self-supervision repeatedly matches or beats language-supervised pretraining on downstream tasks[57, 68];目前的工作将这一优势定位于表示本身的几何形状。与最接近的医学基准的对比是方法论点。 TumorImagingBench 比较了 10 个肿瘤学编码器，并相关报告表明，性能较高的模型更加相似; the present work locates that advantage in the geometry of the representation itself. The contrast with the closest medical benchmark is the methodological point. TumorImagingBench compares ten oncology encoders and reports, correlationally, that higher-performing models are more similar[1]。通过在固定预算下改变目标并重现对具有已知生成结构的合成数据的影响，我们对产生相似性的原因给出了因果说明，并且杠杆是目标而不是分数。. By varying the objective under a fixed budget and reproducing the effect on synthetic data with known generative structure, we give a causal account of what produces the similarity, and the lever is the objective and not the score.

融合也有一个严格的界限。柏拉图式主张的最强版本是跨模式的，即图像和语言编码器在一个结构中相遇Convergence also has a hard boundary. The strongest version of the Platonic claim is cross-modal, that image and language encoders meet in one structure[28]，而我们的医学图像到报告测试不支持它：跨模式一致性处于机会层，随着评估池的增长而下降到零，这与胸片视觉语言模型并不总是需要图像的报告一致, and our medical image-to-report test does not support it: cross-modal agreement sits at the chance floor and falls toward zero as the evaluation pool grows, consistent with reports that chest radiograph vision-language models do not always need the image[45]。这与对表征对齐进行更广泛的重新检查相匹配，其中跨模式相似性很小，取决于测量机制，并且随着样本池的扩大而缩小. This matches a wider re-examination of representational alignment, in which cross-modal similarity is small, depends on the measurement regime, and shrinks as the sample pool enlarges[37]。这个教训超越了负面结果。对齐不是一个单一的数字，而是一个依赖于机制的测量，对池大小以及是否在全局或本地邻居中读取协议敏感，这就是为什么我们以基于邻居的分数为主导，并将每个值报告为池大小的函数。在单个小池中读取时，相同的医学编码器似乎会跨模式收敛；大规模阅读，但他们没有。没有说明其制度的趋同主张是不可解释的。. The lesson generalizes beyond the negative result. Alignment is not a single number but a regime-dependent measurement, sensitive to pool size and to whether agreement is read globally or in local neighborhoods, which is why we lead with neighbor-based scores and report every value as a function of pool size. Read at a single small pool, the same medical encoders would have appeared to converge across modalities; read at scale, they do not. A convergence claim that does not state its regime is not interpretable.

不存在扩展趋势可以得出一个实际的推论。对表征趋同的普遍解读以及柏拉图假设的明确主张是，更大更好的模型更接近共享表征A practical corollary follows from the absence of a scaling trend. The common reading of representational convergence, and an explicit claim of the Platonic hypothesis, is that larger and better models sit closer to the shared representation[28]，并且肿瘤学基准报告了能力和相似性之间的相同相关性, and the oncology benchmark reports the same correlation between capability and similarity[1]。在我们的面板中，编码器到共享几何体的距离既不跟踪其参数计数，也不跟踪其下游精度，也不跟踪其发布年份。根据这一证据，互操作性不能通过缩放来购买，同时有证据表明临床大语言模型的不同功能遵循不同的缩放法则. Across our panel, an encoder’s distance to the shared geometry tracked neither its parameter count, nor its downstream accuracy, nor its release year. Interoperability, on this evidence, cannot be bought by scaling, paralleling evidence that distinct capabilities of clinical large language models follow distinct scaling laws[74]。杠杆就是目标，这与以下发现一致：目标（而不是性能）决定了表示在不同设置中的一致性. The lever is the objective, consistent with the finding that the objective, and not performance, governs how consistent a representation is across settings[10]。对于现场组装越来越大的医疗编码器，这将设计问题从多大转移到了如何训练。. For a field assembling ever-larger medical encoders, this shifts the design question from how big to how trained.

实践中最重要的发现是弱几何对齐仍然足以实现功能互换性。诊断分类器适合传输到其他编码器和外部站点的一个编码器的坐标，学习的仿射图让一个编码器的特征驱动另一个编码器的冻结头。这通过模型拼接将适度的融合与处理可互换性的工作线联系起来The most consequential finding for practice is that weak geometric alignment is still enough for functional interchangeability. A diagnostic classifier fit in one encoder’s coordinates transferred to other encoders and to external sites, and a learned affine map let one encoder’s features drive another encoder’s frozen head. This connects the modest convergence to the line of work that treats interchangeability operationally, through model stitching[40, 3]并共享潜在坐标and shared latent coordinates[51]，以及最近的观察结果，即嵌入空间共享支持它们之间平移的通用几何形状, and to the recent observation that embedding spaces share a universal geometry that supports translation between them[31]。我们在临床环境中以及所部署的工具实际面临的编码器边界上演示了这一点，因此读数可以在编码器发生变化和医院发生变化时仍然存在。重要的是，共享几何结构是临床结构的：一致的发现间距离恢复了发现在患者中如何同时发生，而不是它们如何分组以进行计费，因此传输的结构携带诊断信息而不是管理信息。专家读者会在这个结构停止的地方加深理解。两名经过委员会认证的放射科医生确认了该表示的诊断内容，因为自动读取与他们的参考标签一致，但相同的几何形状并不能预测他们的病例级临床相似性判断。因此，共享结构可在诊断标签级别使用，而无需成为放射科医生如何判断病例之间相似性的模型。可部署的值存在于锚点坐标系中，而不是共识配置中，因为共识偏差漂移检测器失败，而基于锚点的分类器和拼接成功。因此，适度但真实且临床组织的融合足以使与编码器无关的临床工具变得可行. We demonstrate this in a clinical setting and across the encoder boundary that a deployed tool actually faces, so the readout survives a change of encoder and a change of hospital. It matters that the shared geometry is clinically structured: the consensus inter-finding distances recovered how findings co-occur in patients and not how they are grouped for billing, so the structure that transfers carries diagnostic and not administrative information. Expert readers sharpen where this structure stops. Two board-certified radiologists confirmed the diagnostic content of the representation, since an automated read agreed with their reference labels, yet the same geometry did not predict their case-level clinical-similarity judgments above chance. The shared structure is therefore usable at the level of diagnostic labels without being a model of how a radiologist judges similarity between cases. The deployable value lives in the anchor coordinate system and not in the consensus configuration, since the consensus-deviation drift detector failed while the anchor-based classifier and stitching succeeded. A modest but real and clinically organized convergence is therefore sufficient to make encoder-agnostic clinical tools feasible[49]，前提是它们是在一个公共坐标系中构建的，而不是假设两个编码器已经共享一种表示。, provided they are built in a common coordinate system and not on the assumption that two encoders already share one representation.

患者之间的共享表征并不统一，这为记录的问题增加了表征维度。众所周知，医学成像模型会对受保护的属性进行编码，对服务不足的群体诊断不足，并继承其训练数据的人口统计不平衡The shared representation is not uniform across patients, which adds a representational dimension to a documented problem. Medical imaging models are known to encode protected attributes, to underdiagnose under-served groups, and to inherit the demographic imbalance of their training data[20, 62, 39, 21, 54]。大多数证据涉及任务准确性和模型编码的内容；相反，我们观察到，在数据最薄的情况下，表示本身的跨编码器一致性最不稳定。我们刻意谨慎对待这一信号的强度。在我们的数据中，明显的亚组差异是由单个站点的最小类别驱动的，并且与邻居计数工件一致，因此它们并不是已证明的股权断裂。合理的解读范围较窄，但仍然有用：基于共识的医疗代表摘要对于代表性不足的群体来说最不可靠，不应假设共享代表在整个患者群体中表现一致，并且对它的任何依赖都需要在实际部署转变下进行针对亚组的检查. Most of that evidence concerns task accuracy and what a model encodes; we observe instead that the cross-encoder agreement of the representation itself is least stable where the data are thinnest. We are deliberately cautious about the strength of this signal. In our data the apparent subgroup differences are driven by the smallest categories at a single site and are consistent with a neighbor-count artifact, so they are not a demonstrated equity fracture. The defensible reading is narrower and still useful: consensus-based summaries of a medical representation are least reliable exactly for under-represented groups, a shared representation should not be assumed to behave uniformly across the patient population, and any reliance on it warrants subgroup-specific checks under realistic deployment shift[19].

这些结论受到一些限制。首先，共识配置没有达到其收敛容差，因此依赖于它的结果，即临床结构恢复、缩放残差和共识偏差检测器，是灵敏度有限的，并且可能在任一方向上有偏差，并且检测器是退化的。可能的原因是相对表示在 Procrustes 步骤之前需要居中或缩放，这可以进行纠正并重新运行基于共识的分析。其次，人口统计分析实际上是单站点的，因为受保护的属性仅记录一个来源，并且其最强的信号来自非常小的类别。其综合测试也将编码器对视为独立的，尽管共享编码器的对是相关的，因此这些 p 值是反保守的。需要具有较大子组计数的多站点链接来将真实差距与样本量效应分开。第三，稀有发现尾部是退化的，因为扩展词汇表中的超稀有发现会崩溃为重叠的案例集并返回相同的值。找到具有足够阳性病例的特定池将使患病率关系更加精确。第四，收敛声明适用于经过测试的面板，而不适用于所有编码器。没有公开嵌入的封闭模型被必然排除，并且该面板仅限于具有可分离图像编码器的模型，因此将图像补丁直接传递到语言主干的统一早期融合架构超出了其范围Several limitations bound these conclusions. First, the consensus configuration did not reach its convergence tolerance, so the results that depend on it, namely the clinical-structure recovery, the scaling residuals, and the consensus-deviation detector, are sensitivity-limited and could be biased in either direction, and the detector is degenerate. The likely cause is that the relative representations need centering or scaling before the Procrustes step, which can be corrected and the consensus-derived analyses rerun. Second, the demographic analysis is effectively single-site, because the protected attributes are recorded for only one source, and its strongest signals come from very small categories. Its omnibus tests also treat encoder pairs as independent, though pairs that share an encoder are correlated, so those p-values are anticonservative. A multi-site linkage with larger subgroup counts is needed to separate a real gap from a sample-size effect. Third, the rare-finding tail is degenerate, because ultra-rare findings from the extended vocabularies collapse to overlapping case sets and return identical values. Finding-specific pools with enough positive cases would make the prevalence relationship precise. Fourth, the convergence claims hold for the tested panel and not for all encoders. Closed models that expose no embeddings are excluded by necessity, and the panel is restricted to models with a separable image encoder, so unified early-fusion architectures that pass image patches straight into a language backbone are outside its scope[14, 47]，这一设计现已达到量产型号，如 Gemma 4 12B, a design that has now reached production models such as Gemma 4 12B[67]。收敛性是一种成对的属性，因此在该类别中测量它需要几个以这种方式构建的独立训练的医学模型，但目前还不存在。我们的帐户预测目标也应该控制它们的一致性，并且一旦此类模型出现，该预测就变得可测试。随着新的开放重量编码器的发布，该面板应该会增长。第五，受控编码器每个单元使用单个种子，因此它们的对齐不会在训练随机性上进行平均；额外的种子将收紧这些估计。第六，为了易于处理，在上限池上计算对齐，尽管它在上限以下是稳定的。精确或可扩展的大型池内核将确认整个池大小的值。第七，每个编码器都使用自己的本机预处理，这是其输入管道的一部分，并且与编码器身份混淆，因此一对的测量对齐同时反映了表示和预处理；共享预处理会隔离表示，但与编码器的部署方式不匹配。第八，以读者为基础的分析有其自身的局限性，由读者自己标记。胸部X光片是多标签的，并且大多数同时带有多个发现，因此对于许多三胞胎来说，对哪个病例在临床上更相似的单一判断是不明确的；因此，近乎偶然的三元组结果反映了不恰当的比较以及编码器的限制。该参考集还涵盖许多便携式和未穿透的放射线照片，这限制了阅读器间的一致性和自动读取，并且一些统一的发现标签在定义上不明确。以发现为条件的相似性任务和质量分层的参考集将更清晰地测试几何和读取。. Convergence is a pairwise property, so measuring it in that class needs several independently trained medical models built that way, and none exist yet. Our account predicts that the objective should govern their alignment as well, and that prediction becomes testable once such models appear. The panel should grow as new open-weight encoders are released. Fifth, the controlled encoders use a single seed per cell, so their alignment is not averaged over training randomness; additional seeds would tighten these estimates. Sixth, alignment is computed on capped pools for tractability, although it is stable below the cap. Exact or scalable large-pool kernels would confirm the values at full pool size. Seventh, each encoder uses its own native preprocessing, which is part of its input pipeline and is confounded with encoder identity, so a pair’s measured alignment reflects the representation and the preprocessing together; a shared preprocessing would isolate the representation but would not match how the encoders are deployed. Eighth, the reader-grounded analyses carry their own constraints, flagged by the readers themselves. Chest radiographs are multilabel, and most carry several findings at once, so a single judgment of which case is clinically more similar is ambiguous for many triplets; the near-chance triplet result therefore reflects an ill-posed comparison as much as the limits of the encoders. The reference set also spans many portable and underpenetrated radiographs, which bounds both inter-reader agreement and the automated read, and several harmonized finding labels are ambiguous in definition. A finding-conditioned similarity task and a quality-stratified reference set would test the geometry and the read more cleanly.

医学成像领域的表征趋同是真实存在的，但它比它的声誉更小、更陌生。它是自我监督目标的属性，而不是被假定驱动它的临床监督的属性。它保持在一种模式内，不会跨入临床语言，它不会随着模型变得更大或更好而增长，并且在患者之间是不平衡的。同样的证据还传达了意想不到的实用信息。即使是适度的、临床组织的融合也足以通过共享坐标系将诊断工具从一个编码器移动到另一个编码器，从一家医院移动到另一家医院，这指向不依赖于其背后的特定编码器的临床工具。在此基础上建立的方法不是等待规模化来制造单一的通用表示，而是为了实际对齐模型的目标进行训练，将对齐视为取决于其制度的测量，并在共享的表示被信任之前验证其在患者之间的行为是否一致。从这个角度来看，收敛与其说是扩展所要达到的目标，不如说是一种适度且可利用的规律性，而将它的本质与它所希望的分离出来，才是让它变得有用的原因。Representational convergence in medical imaging is real, but it is smaller and stranger than its reputation. It is a property of the self-supervised objective and not of the clinical supervision that was assumed to drive it. It holds within a modality and does not cross into clinical language, it does not grow as models get larger or better, and it is uneven across patients. The same evidence carries an unexpectedly practical message. Even a modest, clinically organized convergence is enough to move a diagnostic tool from one encoder to another and from one hospital to another through a shared coordinate system, which points toward clinical tools that do not depend on the specific encoder behind them. The way to build on this is not to wait for scale to manufacture a single universal representation, but to train for the objective that actually aligns models, to treat alignment as a measurement that depends on its regime, and to verify that a shared representation behaves uniformly across patients before it is trusted. Read this way, convergence is less a destination that scaling will reach than a modest and exploitable regularity, and separating what it is from what it is hoped to be is what will make it useful.
