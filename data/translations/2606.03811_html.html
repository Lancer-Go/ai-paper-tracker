人工智能代理启用自适应计算机蠕虫AI Agents Enable Adaptive Computer Worms

Jonas Guan*†\dagger1,2&Tom Blanchard*1,2 &Hanna Foerster*3 &Hengrui Jia*1,2    Gabriel Huang4 &Nicolas Papernot†\dagger1,2    1University of Toronto  2Vector Institute  3University of Cambridge  4ServiceNow    *Equal contribution  †\daggerCorresponding author
摘要Abstract

计算机蠕虫是一种通过将自身从一台计算机复制到另一台计算机而在网络上传播的恶意软件。传统蠕虫病毒（例如 WannaCry）利用预先确定的漏洞，可以通过修补这些漏洞来阻止其传播。在这里，我们展示了人工智能 (AI) 代理带来了一种全新的威胁：一种蠕虫病毒，可以针对遇到的每个目标生成量身定制的攻击策略。该蠕虫寄生性地使用受感染的机器来运行开放权重大型语言模型 (LLM)，以维持其推理，或扩大其范围以进行进一步的攻击。该蠕虫部署在涵盖 Linux、Windows 和 IoT（物联网）设备的机器网络上，通过利用常见的、现实世界的企业网络漏洞进行传播。由于该蠕虫是由被盗计算驱动的，因此攻击者每次新感染的边际成本为零。这在攻击者和防御者之间造成了不稳定的经济不对称。此外，由于该蠕虫不需要商业人工智能平台，因此集中式安全控制（例如服务拒绝或速率限制）在结构上是无关的。我们的结果表明，自我维持的人工智能驱动的网络威胁不再是理论上的。我们必须为自主生成的对手做好准备：恶意软件系统无需人类操作员即可传播，并且不是由固定的漏洞利用代码定义，而是由推理目标、适应观察和实时合成攻击逻辑的能力来定义。A computer worm is malware that spreads on a network by replicating itself from one machine to another. Traditional worms, like WannaCry, exploited predetermined vulnerabilities, and their spread can be halted by patching those vulnerabilities. Here we show that artificial intelligence (AI) agents enable a fundamentally new threat: a worm that generates tailored attack strategies to each target it encounters. The worm parasitically uses compromised machines to run open-weight large language models (LLMs) to sustain its reasoning, or extend its reach for further attacks. Deployed on a network of machines spanning Linux, Windows, and IoT (Internet of Things) devices, the worm propagated by exploiting common, real-world corporate network vulnerabilities. Since the worm is powered by stolen compute, the attacker’s marginal cost per new infection is zero. This creates a destabilizing economic asymmetry between attackers and defenders. Moreover, because the worm requires no commercial AI platform, centralized safety controls, such as service refusals or rate limiting, are structurally irrelevant. Our results demonstrate that self-sustaining AI-driven cyber-threats are no longer theoretical. We must prepare for autonomous generative adversaries: malware systems that propagate without human operators and are defined not by fixed exploit code, but by the capacity to reason about targets, adapt to observations, and synthesize attack logic in real time.

Computer worms exploit this: upon compromising a first victim, the worm replicates itself and spreads across the network at near-zero marginal cost to the attacker. To date, patching that finite set of software flaws has therefore sufficed to interrupt their spread.

But these results do not address our central question: can generated reasoning be coupled to self-replication to produce a computer worm that adapts across heterogeneous targets? Resolving this question is necessary for informed evaluation of AI cybersecurity capabilities, design of countermeasures, and governance.

The proof-of-concept implements features sufficient to address our main research question, while avoiding ones that would make it useful to malicious parties. 3.4 局限性Limitations

我们评估的概念验证继承了底层模型的功能限制。 个人利用尝试的成功率为 44%，其中大多数失败归因于格式错误的有效负载，而不是错误的策略。 该蠕虫尤其难以应对 Web 应用程序结构、Windows 命令环境和需要精确字符串操作的负载语法。 这些反映了当前一代单 GPU 模型的代码生成上限，而不是该方法的基本约束，并且随着语言模型在代码生成和结构化输出方面的改进，预计会缩小。 尽管每次尝试都存在脆弱性，但群架构通过并行、独立的推理轨迹进行补偿，以实现我们报告的结果。The proof-of-concept we evaluated inherits capability limitations of the underlying model. Individual exploitation attempts succeeded in 44% of cases, with the majority of failures attributable to malformed payloads rather than incorrect strategy. The worm struggled particularly with web application structures, Windows command environments, and payload syntax requiring precise string manipulation. These reflect the code-generation ceiling of a current-generation single-GPU model, not a fundamental constraint on the approach, and are expected to narrow as language models improve at code generation and structured output. Despite this per-attempt fragility, the swarm architecture compensated through parallel, independent reasoning trajectories to achieve our reported results.

FakeCorp 环境旨在将蠕虫的自主推理和适应隔离为所研究的变量。因此，每个目标主机都至少具有一个可利用的漏洞，并且没有部署端点检测、防病毒或主动防火墙软件。 因此，我们的结果表征了代理推理和利用现实个体漏洞的能力，而不是其在主要强化的网络中定位稀疏目标或在主动防御监控下持续存在的能力。 这些限制限制了人工智能驱动的蠕虫作为概念验证的操作成熟度。 它们不限制它实例化的威胁类别：核心发现，即生成推理、自我复制和寄生计算获取结合成一个自我维持的过程，独立于模型当前的能力上限和我们故意保留的隐形功能。The FakeCorp environment is designed to isolate the worm’s autonomous reasoning and adaptation as the variable under study. Every target host therefore bears at least one exploitable vulnerability, and no endpoint detection, antivirus, or active firewall software is deployed. Our results, therefore, characterize the agent’s ability to reason about and exploit realistic individual vulnerabilities, not its ability to locate sparse targets in a predominantly hardened network or to persist under active defensive monitoring. These limitations bound the operational maturity of the AI-driven worm as a proof-of-concept. They do not constrain the threat class it instantiates: the core finding, that generated reasoning, self-replication, and parasitic compute acquisition combine into a self-sustaining process, holds independently of the model’s current capability ceiling and of the stealth features we deliberately withheld.

技术对策。Technical counter-measures.

我们的蠕虫原型旨在证明人工智能驱动的蠕虫是可行的，而不是产生可操作部署的恶意软件来逃避检测并试图最大程度地造成伤害。Our worm prototype was designed to demonstrate that an AI-driven worm is feasible, not to produce an operationally deployable malware that evades detection and attempts to maximise harm.

检测。Detection.我们故意省略了会使检测或删除复杂化的功能：该蠕虫不会对其通信进行加密、采用多态代码、抑制取证假象或尝试隐藏其对本地计算资源的使用。 因此，它在我们的实验中产生了一致的行为签名，包括非标准端口上的信标回调、SSH 公钥的自动注入以及跨主机的系统凭证重用。 这些模式是网络监控和入侵检测的具体目标。 然而，至关重要的是，这些签名是我们概念验证范围内的产物，而不是人工智能驱动的蠕虫的固有属性。 未来的对手可以利用人工智能驱动的蠕虫用来生成漏洞的推理能力来生成规避策略，例如，使用蠕虫已经获得的提升权限来清理日志、模仿合法的流量模式或建立隐蔽的通信通道。We deliberately omitted features that would complicate detection or removal: the worm does not encrypt its communications, employ polymorphic code, suppress forensic artefacts, or attempt to conceal its use of local compute resources. As a result, it produces consistent behavioural signatures in our experiments, including beacon callbacks on non-standard ports, automated injection of SSH public keys, and systematic credential reuse across hosts. These patterns are concrete targets for network monitoring and intrusion detection. Crucially, however, these signatures are artefacts of our proof-of-concept scope, not inherent properties of AI-driven worms. A future adversary could direct the same reasoning capabilities that the AI-driven worm uses to generate exploits toward generating evasion strategies, for example, using the elevated privileges the worm already obtains to clean logs, mimic legitimate traffic patterns, or establish covert communication channels.

减少攻击面。Reducing the attack surface.防御这种更广泛的威胁类别需要在蠕虫到达之前缩小攻击面。人工智能辅助的渗透测试和模糊测试可以帮助组织在对手之前发现自己基础设施中可利用的弱点，并应用相同级别的 LLM 推理能力进行防御。然而，如果没有根据发现迅速采取行动的能力，仅靠发现是不够的。自动 CVE 验证、自动补丁验证以及预测补丁时间表的能力对于防御者了解他们面临的风险窗口至关重要。Defending against this broader threat class requires reducing the attack surface before a worm can reach it. AI-assisted penetration testing and fuzzing can help organisations discover exploitable weaknesses in their own infrastructure before an adversary does, applying the same class of LLM reasoning capabilities defensively. Discovery alone is insufficient, however, without the ability to act on findings quickly. Automated CVE verification, automated patch verification, and the ability to forecast patch timelines are critical for defenders to understand the window of risk they face.

操作员需要权衡蠕虫的传播时间与验证和部署补丁所需的时间，并相应地确定优先级。 人工智能驱动的蠕虫的推理架构比传统蠕虫的运行时间更长。 在我们的试验中，达到一半网络大约需要五天，这是由数百个 LLM 推理调用设定的速度，每个目标都需要侦察、制定策略和生成有效负载。 与以网络速度重放固定漏洞的蠕虫相比，这为防御者提供了更长的检测和响应窗口，尽管随着推理硬件和模型效率的提高，该窗口将会压缩。 随着模型的改进和推理成本的下降，传播时间将会压缩，使得自动化和快速的补丁部署成为日益紧迫的能力差距。Operators need to weigh the propagation timeline of a worm against the time required to verify and deploy a patch, and to prioritise accordingly. The reasoning architecture of the AI-driven worm operates on a longer timescale than traditional worms. Reaching half the network required approximately five days in our trials, a pace set by the hundreds of LLM inference calls each target demands for reconnaissance, strategy formulation, and payload generation. This affords defenders a longer window for detection and response than worms that replay fixed exploits at network speed, though this window will compress as inference hardware and model efficiency improve. As models improve and inference costs fall, the propagation timeline will compress, making automated and rapid patch deployment an increasingly urgent capability gap.

限制传播。Limiting propagation.核心计算机和网络安全原则提供了针对自主蠕虫传播的直接缓解措施。零信任架构需要对每个访问请求进行持续的身份验证和授权，限制了蠕虫在建立立足点后所依赖的横向移动。网络微分段限制了从任何单个受感染机器可访问的主机集，从而减少了有效传播表面。 我们的 FakeCorp 环境代表了这方面最坏的情况，这是一个扁平网络，其中每台主机都可以相互访问。 最大限度地减少每台主机上部署的软件依赖项的数量进一步缩小了自适应代理可用的攻击面。Core computer and network security principles offer direct mitigation against autonomous worm propagation. Zero-trust architectures, which require continuous authentication and authorisation for every access request, limit the lateral movement that the worm relies on after establishing a foothold. Network micro-segmentation constrains the set of hosts reachable from any single compromised machine, reducing the effective propagation surface. Our FakeCorp environment represents a worst-case scenario in this regard, a flat network in which every host is reachable from every other. Minimising the number of software dependencies deployed on each host further shrinks the attack surface available to an adaptive agent.

涉及人工智能的网络安全研究的道德标准。Ethical standards for cybersecurity research involving AI.

我们开发人工智能驱动的蠕虫病毒的经验凸显了对管理攻击性人工智能研究的社区范围标准的需求。我们发现必须为这种性质的研究设计新的制度流程。Our experience developing the AI-driven worm highlights the need for community-wide standards governing offensive AI research. We found that new institutional processes had to be designed for research of this nature.

除了遏制之外，社区还应该制定共享的编辑和访问标准。我们从本文中删除了技术实现细节，这将降低复制系统的障碍，同时保留足够的细节来验证我们的主张并为防御性研究提供信息。更广泛地说，修订指南应建立通用标准来确定保留哪些技术细节，平衡可重复性和误用风险。正如我们通过大学所追求的那样，在商定的条件下与经过审查的研究人员共享研究成果的机制，使社区能够审查和建立进攻性人工智能研究，而无需免费提供实施细节。这些标准将受益于跨学科的投入，借鉴生物安全领域建立的双重用途审查框架和脆弱性研究界的协调披露规范。Beyond containment, the community should develop shared standards for redaction and access. We redacted technical implementation details from this paper that would lower the barrier to reproducing the system, while retaining sufficient detail to validate our claims and inform defensive research. More broadly, redaction guidelines should establish common criteria for determining which technical details to withhold, balancing reproducibility against misuse risk. Mechanisms for sharing research artefacts with vetted researchers under agreed-upon conditions, as we are pursuing through our University, allow the community to scrutinise and build on offensive AI research without making implementation details freely available. These standards would benefit from cross-disciplinary input, drawing on the dual-use review frameworks established in biosecurity and the coordinated disclosure norms of the vulnerability research community.

6 讨论Discussion

我们的结果对发现法学硕士不足以跨越关键网络攻击阈值的评估提出了挑战Our results challenge evaluations that found LLMs as insufficiently capable of crossing key cyber-offensive thresholds(Wan et al., 2024; Rodriguez et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025; Wallace et al., 2026)。 我们证明，现成的开放权重法学硕士（经过量化以适合单个 GPU）足以驱动蠕虫代理，该蠕虫代理获得对机器的特权访问并自我复制。 促成因素是代理工具，它在模型的上下文窗口中管理信息，并为模型提供结构化接口以作用于目标系统。之前的进攻能力评估依赖于应用于孤立的 CTF 式任务的轻量级行动观察支架. We demonstrate that an off-the-shelf open-weight LLM, quantized to fit on a single GPU, suffices to drive a worm agent that gains privileged access to machines and replicates itself. The enabling factor is the agentic harness that curates information in the model’s context window and provides structured interfaces for the model to act on target systems. Prior offensive-capability evaluations relied on lightweight action-observation scaffolds applied to isolated CTF-style tasks(Wan et al., 2024; Rodriguez et al., 2025)，或者隐式地将最坏情况的开放权重能力视为主要通过特定领域的微调引起的, or implicitly treat worst-case open-weight capability as primarily elicited via domain-specific fine-tuning(Wallace et al., 2026);我们的结果证实了越来越多的论点，即约束约束是线束设计，而不是原始模型功能; our results corroborate the growing argument that harness design, not raw model capability, is the binding constraint(Singer et al., 2026; Sanz-Gómez et al., 2025; Mayoral-Vilches et al., 2025; Pan et al., 2025)。因此，语言模型的能力必须通过引发这些能力的工具来评估，而不是孤立地评估。. Language model capabilities must therefore be evaluated through the harness that elicits them, not in isolation from it.

该代理完全在本地托管的开放式法学硕士上运行，构成独立于集中式供应商控制的威胁。 为了明确证明这一点，我们故意不利用蠕虫可以从受感染的机器中获取的前沿模型 API 密钥或云计算。The agent operates entirely on a locally hosted, open-weight LLM, posing a threat independent of centralized vendor controls. To explicitly demonstrate this, we deliberately did not leverage frontier-model API keys or cloud compute that the worm could harvest from compromised machines.777有关最近的示例，请参阅 CVE-2026-25253，其中发现 OpenClaw 存在可窃取此类凭据的漏洞：For a recent example, see CVE-2026-25253 where OpenClaw was found to present a vulnerability that enables the stealing of such credentials:https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-25253.该蠕虫以完全分散的方式运行，没有任何一个控制点可以离线来中断其传播。这让我们得出结论，供应商端的控制即使是完美的，在结构上也与阻止蠕虫病毒的传播无关。然而，如果供应商端控制不完善，未来人工智能驱动的蠕虫可以集成 API 密钥和云计算来扩展其基础设施，并减少对受害者网络中本地硬件加速器可用性的依赖。The worm operates in a fully decentralized manner, and no single point of control can be taken offline to interrupt its spread. This allows us to conclude that vendor-side controls, even if they are perfect, would be structurally irrelevant to halting the worm’s propagation. However, in the event that vendor-side controls are imperfect, a future AI-driven worm could integrate API keys and cloud compute to expand its infrastructure and decrease its reliance on the availability of local hardware accelerators within the victim networks.

最近有关人工智能和网络安全的讨论大多集中在模型是否能够发现新的零日漏洞Much of the recent discourse around AI and cybersecurity has focused on whether models can discover novel zero-day vulnerabilities(Anthropic, 2026)。 然而，在实践中，大多数现实世界的网络攻击更多地依赖于利用已披露的漏洞、未修补的系统、错误配置和重复出现的弱点类，而不是发现新的零日攻击. In practice, however, the majority of real-world cyberattacks rely less on discovering new zero-days than on exploiting disclosed vulnerabilities, unpatched systems, misconfigurations, and recurring weakness classes(OECD, 2021; Ablon and Bogart, 2017)。 我们的蠕虫病毒正是针对这个攻击面，它不需要发现零日的最先进的能力，而只需要一个足以针对不同目标配置操作已知漏洞的模型。 此外，通过其可更新的知识库，该蠕虫可以在广泛部署补丁之前，在披露后数小时内合并新发布的漏洞，从而有效地利用披露和修复之间的窗口。因此，. Our worm targets precisely this attack surface, which does not require the state-of-the-art capability of discovering zero-days but only a model capable enough to operationalise known vulnerabilities against diverse target configurations. Moreover, through its updatable knowledge base, the worm can incorporate newly published vulnerabilities within hours of disclosure, before patches are widely deployed, effectively capitalizing on the window between disclosure and remediation. Consequently,网络安全专家cybersecurity experts面临攻击经济学的转变。该蠕虫以零边际成本在被盗的计算资源上传播，除了初始部署之外，不需要攻击者进行持续投资。对于以精确为导向的国家行为者来说，原则上可以将相同的能力与目标区分相结合，以实现大规模的持续、选择性访问。face a shift in attack economics. The worm spreads on stolen computational resources at zero marginal cost, requiring no ongoing investment from the attacker beyond initial deployment. For precision-oriented state actors, the same capabilities could in principle be combined with target discrimination to enable persistent, selective access at scale.

这些现实强调These realities stress that the人工智能科学界AI scientific community必须采用新方法来评估开放式和封闭式生态系统的网络安全能力。迄今为止，大多数攻击能力评估和报告都针对闭源 APImust adopt new methods for evaluating cybersecurity capabilities across both the open and closed-weight ecosystems. To date, the majority of offensive-capability evaluations and reports have targeted closed-source APIs(Singer et al., 2026; Anthropic, 2025; Lin et al., 2026)，使得开放重量的威胁在很大程度上未经审查。这一差距尤为紧迫，因为当攻击者完全控制本地执行环境、采用编程越狱或自动重构提示来覆盖模型拒绝时，可以绕过开放权重模型上的内置安全对齐和护栏。因此，保障措施不仅要考虑模型功能，还要考虑部署这些功能的工具、检索系统和执行环境。, leaving the open-weight threat largely unexamined. This gap is particularly urgent because built-in safety alignments and guardrails on open-weight models can be bypassed when an attacker completely controls the local execution environment, employing programmatic jailbreaks or automatically reframing prompts to override model refusals. Safeguards must therefore account not only for model capabilities but for the tools, retrieval systems, and execution environments through which those capabilities are deployed.

对于For行业和政策制定者industry and policymakers，这种威胁的分散性质使传统的监管方法变得复杂：没有单一供应商控制模型、硬件或线束。 因此，应对这一威胁需要研究、安全、行业和政策界采取协调一致的行动：测试线束级能力的评估框架、针对自主代理行为特征调整的检测系统，以及考虑开放权重推理的去中心化性质的监管措施。, the decentralized nature of this threat complicates conventional regulatory approaches: no single vendor controls the model, the hardware, or the harness. Addressing this threat will therefore require coordinated action across the research, security, industry, and policy communities: evaluation frameworks that test harness-level capabilities, detection systems tuned to the behavioural signatures of autonomous agents, and regulatory measures that account for the decentralized nature of open-weight inference.

A separate probe connects to the agent’s beacon (a binary TCP service deployed during exploitation) from the hypervisor and issues a privilege-level check, independently confirming both the presence of a foothold and whether root access was achieved. This two-layer approach (agent-reported event logs cross-checked against host-level inspection) grounds the reported success rates in observable system state.

F.5 实现坚持Achieving persistence

在两个不同的轨迹中，我们观察到代理通过预期复制管道之外的机制在受损目标上建立了持久性。在一种情况下，启动副本进程后，代理将其注册到主机的服务管理系统中，以便该进程在终止时会自动重新启动。在另一种情况下，它创建了一个定期重新启动副本的计划任务，导致代理即使在原始进程被终止后也重新出现。这些行为不是安全带要求的，不是实验成功标准的一部分，也没有受到任何明确提示的激励。该代理似乎从维护可操作副本的总体目标推断出，目标上可用的持久性机制可用于使副本更加健壮。 当这些持久性机制出现时，我们将其删除。我们的实验旨在评估自主利用、权限升级和跨封闭网络的复制，而不是在已经受到损害的主机上的长期持久性。In two separate trajectories, we observed that the agent established persistence on a compromised target through mechanisms that were outside the intended replication pipeline. In one case, after launching a replica process, the agent registered it with the host’s service-management system so that the process would be restarted automatically if terminated. In another case, it created a scheduled task that periodically relaunched the replica, causing the agent to reappear even after the original process had been killed. These behaviours were not requested by the harness, were not part of the experimental success criteria, and were not incentivised by any explicit prompt. The agent appears to have inferred, from the general objective of maintaining an operational replica, that persistence mechanisms available on the target could be used to make the replica more robust. We removed these persistence mechanisms when they appeared. Our experiments were designed to evaluate autonomous exploitation, privilege escalation, and replication across a contained network, not long-term persistence on already-compromised hosts.
