MECO：老年人情感和认知理解的多模态数据集MECO: A Multimodal Dataset for Emotion and Cognitive Understanding in Older Adults

摘要。Abstract.

尽管情感计算已取得相当大的进步，但老龄化人群中的多模式情感预测仍未得到充分探索，这主要是由于专用数据集的稀缺。现有的多模式基准主要针对年轻、认知健康的受试者，忽略了认知下降对情绪表达和生理反应的影响。为了弥补这一差距，我们推出了 MECO，While affective computing has advanced considerably, multimodal emotion prediction in aging populations remains underexplored, largely due to the scarcity of dedicated datasets. Existing multimodal benchmarks predominantly target young, cognitively healthy subjects, neglecting the influence of cognitive decline on emotional expression and physiological responses. To bridge this gap, we present MECO, aM多模态数据集ultimodal dataset forE运动和motion andC认知理解ognitive understanding inO老年人。 MECO 包括 42 名参与者，提供大约 38 小时的多模态信号，产生 30,592 个同步样本。为了最大限度地提高生态有效性，数据收集遵循基于社区的环境中的标准化协议。这些模式包括视频、音频、脑电图 (EEG) 和心电图 (ECG)。此外，该数据集还提供情绪和认知状态的全面注释，包括自我评估效价、唤醒度、六种基本情绪和简易精神状态检查认知分数。我们进一步建立了情绪和认知预测的基线基准。 MECO 作为老龄化人群情感和认知多模态建模的基础资源，促进下游应用，例如个性化情绪识别和现实环境中轻度认知障碍 (MCI) 的早期检测。完整的数据集和补充材料可在lder adults. MECO includes 42 participants and provides approximately 38 hours of multimodal signals, yielding 30,592 synchronized samples. To maximize ecological validity, data collection followed standardized protocols within community-based settings. The modalities cover video, audio, electroencephalography (EEG), and electrocardiography (ECG). In addition, the dataset offers comprehensive annotations of emotional and cognitive states, including self-assessed valence, arousal, six basic emotions, and Mini-Mental State Examination cognitive scores. We further establish baseline benchmarks for both emotion and cognitive prediction. MECO serves as a foundational resource for multimodal modeling of affect and cognition in aging populations, facilitating downstream applications such as personalized emotion recognition and early detection of mild cognitive impairment (MCI) in real-world settings. The complete dataset and supplementary materials are available athttps://maitrechen.github.io/meco-page/.

1. 简介Introduction

随着全球老龄化人口的快速增长，了解老年人的情感状态对于心理健康监测和认知评估变得越来越重要With the rapid growth of the global aging population, understanding affective states in older adults is increasingly critical for mental health monitoring and cognitive assessment(Beard et al., 2016)。认知能力下降，例如轻度认知障碍 (MCI)，可以显着改变情绪表达和生理反应. Cognitive decline, such as mild cognitive impairment (MCI), can markedly alter emotional expression and physiological responses(Ismail et al., 2015)，对老年人进行可靠的情感分析提出了重大挑战。这些耦合因素的自动化定量建模对于改善老年护理的早期检测和干预具有巨大潜力, posing significant challenges for reliable affective analysis in older adults. Automated and quantitative modeling of these coupled factors holds substantial potential for improving early detection and intervention in geriatric care(John et al., 2018).

尽管多模式情感计算最近取得了进展，但针对老年人的强大系统的开发仍然受到现有数据集交叉差距的限制。大多数公共基准由认知完整的年轻人主导，并假设模态一致性，即外在面部表情同步反映内在唤醒Despite recent advances in multimodal affective computing, the development of robust systems for older adults remains constrained by intersecting gaps in existing datasets. Most public benchmarks are dominated by young, cognitively intact individuals and assume modality congruence, where outward facial expressions synchronously reflect internal arousal(Mauss et al., 2005; Katsigiannis and Ramzan, 2018; Zheng and Lu, 2015; Koelstra et al., 2012)。这一假设未能捕捉到老年人群中非典型的情感表现，尤其是患有 MCI 的老年人群。 MCI 患者经常表现出面部冷漠. This assumption fails to capture atypical affective manifestations in older populations, particularly those with MCI. Patients with MCI frequently exhibit facial apathy(Robert et al., 2009)，导致迟钝的外在表达和活跃的内部生理唤醒之间的脱节。这使得传统的视觉驱动数据集不够充分，并导致现有模型误解情绪状态。此外，认知和情感之间的内在相互作用在很大程度上被忽视，因为当前的数据集通常单独注释这些状态, leading to a disconnect between blunted outward expressions and active internal physiological arousal. This renders traditional visually-driven datasets inadequate and causes existing models to misinterpret emotional states. Moreover, the intrinsic interplay between cognition and emotion is largely overlooked, as current datasets typically annotate these states in isolation(Luz et al., 2020; Soleymani et al., 2012; Park et al., 2020; Lee et al., 2024)，忽略了认知衰退主动调节情绪反应的临床现实，使得研究渐进性认知衰退如何重塑多模式情绪表征变得困难。为了捕捉这些复杂的相互作用，模型需要同步的行为（视频）和生理模式——脑电图（EEG）和心电图（ECG）——以及认知评估, ignoring the clinical reality that cognitive decline actively modulates emotional reactivity, making it difficult to investigate how progressive cognitive degradation reshapes multimodal emotional representations. To capture these complex interactions, models require synchronized behavioral (video) and physiological modalities-electroencephalography (EEG) and electrocardiography (ECG)-alongside cognitive assessments(Bagher Zadeh et al., 2018; Jiang et al., 2020; Yang et al., 2025).

为了解决这些限制，我们引入了 MECO，To address these limitations, we introduce MECO, aM多模态数据集ultimodal dataset forE运动和motion andC认知理解ognitive understanding inO老年人。以社区为基础收集 在标准化情绪引发协议下的设置下，MECO 捕获生态有效条件下的行为和生理反应。该数据集同步了 42 名老年参与者约 38 小时的多模式信号记录，其中包括 27 名健康对照 (HC) 和 15 名 MCI 患者。数据涵盖视频、音频、脑电图和心电图模式，总共产生 30,592 个样本。在情绪反应和认知表现之间相互作用的推动下，MECO 不仅提供情绪状态的全面注释，包括自我评估效价、唤醒度和六种基本情绪，还提供基于简易精神状态检查 (MMSE) 的认知分数。因此，MECO 支持一系列下游任务，包括老龄化人群的情绪认知模型、强大的情绪识别和情绪辅助认知障碍筛查。我们的贡献总结如下：lder adults. Collected in community-based settings under standardized emotion elicitation protocols, MECO captures behavioral and physiological responses in ecologically valid conditions. The dataset synchronizes records of around 38 hours of multimodal signals from 42 older participants, comprising 27 healthy controls (HC) and 15 individuals with MCI. The data covers video, audio, EEG, and ECG modalities, yielding a total of 30,592 samples. Motivated by interactions between emotional responses and cognitive performance, MECO provides not only comprehensive annotations of emotional states, including self-assessed valence, arousal, and six basic emotions, but also cognitive scores based on the Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE). Therefore, MECO supports a range of downstream tasks, including emotion–cognition modeling in aging populations, robust emotion recognition, and emotion-assisted cognitive impairment screening. Our contributions are summarized as follows:

  • •

    据我们所知，我们提出了第一个针对老年人的多模态数据集，该数据集联合模拟了情绪和认知状态。它整合了不同的行为和生理模式，解决了老龄化人群中缺乏捕捉情感和认知的资源的问题。To the best of our knowledge, we present the first multimodal dataset for older adults that jointly models emotion and cognitive states. It integrates diverse behavioral and physiological modalities, addressing the lack of resources capturing emotion and cognition within aging populations.

  • •

    我们建立了情感和认知预测的基线基准，证明了多模态建模的可行性并提供标准化的评估协议。We establish baseline benchmarks for emotional and cognitive prediction, demonstrating the feasibility of multimodal modeling and providing standardized evaluation protocols.

  • •

    MECO 为推进老年人群的情感计算提供了宝贵的资源，使情感-认知相互作用的研究成为可能，并在认知能力下降的情况下支持强大的情感识别模型。MECO provides a valuable resource for advancing affective computing in elderly populations, enabling the study of emotion–cognition interactions and supporting robust emotion recognition models under cognitive decline.

2. 相关工作Related Work

情绪识别Emotion Recognition情绪识别 (ER) 从行为和生理信号推断人类情绪Emotion Recognition (ER) infers human emotions from behavioral and physiological signals(Koelstra et al., 2012; Soleymani et al., 2012; Zheng and Lu, 2015)。通过捕获单个模式中缺少的信息，整合互补线索的多模式方法优于单模式方法. Multimodal approaches that integrate complementary cues outperform unimodal methods by capturing information absent in individual modalities(Zhang et al., 2024a; Soleymani et al., 2012)。然而，大多数研究都集中在年轻人或中年人群，而老年人和 MCI 患者的代表性不足。与年龄相关的生理反应和行为变化带来了 ER 挑战，例如脑电图特征改变和面部表情减弱. However, most studies focus on young or middle-aged populations, leaving older adults and individuals with MCI underrepresented. Age-related changes in physiological responses and behavior introduce ER challenges, such as altered EEG signatures and diminished facial expressivity(Poria et al., 2017)。这些人口统计数据中的多模态融合进一步受到信号噪声增加和受试者间高变异性的阻碍。因此，迫切需要针对老年人的多模式 ER 方法来实现准确的情绪预测并支持下游应用，包括心理健康监测和认知护理。. Multimodal fusion in these demographics is further hindered by increased signal noise and high inter-subject variability. Consequently, multimodal ER approaches for older adults are urgently needed to enable accurate emotion prediction and support downstream applications, including mental health monitoring and cognitive care.

4.4. 评估协议和指标Evaluation Protocol and Metrics

为了全面对 MECO 数据集进行基准测试，我们定义了两种评估协议来评估模型的个性化和广义能力：主题相关（SD）和主题无关（SI）。根据 SD 方案，每次试验均按时间顺序进行分割，以保留引发反应的时间动态。具体来说，每个试验中的前 60% 部分用于训练，接下来的 20% 用于验证，最后 20% 用于测试。 在 SI 协议下，采用主题五重交叉验证来评估未见过的参与者的模型泛化能力。所有受试者被分为五个不相交的子集，其中四个（约 80%）用于训练，其余一个（20%）用于每个折叠中的测试。To comprehensively benchmark MECO dataset, we define two evaluation protocols to assess both personalized and generalized capabilities of the models: Subject-Dependent (SD) and Subject-Independent (SI). Under the SD protocol, a chronological split is applied within each trial to preserve the temporal dynamics of the elicited responses. Specifically, the first 60% of segments in each trial are allocated for training, the next 20% for validation, and the final 20% for testing. Under the SI protocol, subject-wise five-fold cross-validation is adopted to evaluate model generalization across unseen participants. All subjects are partitioned into five disjoint subsets, with four (approximately 80%) used for training and the remaining one (20%) for testing in each fold.

对于情感分类任务，未加权平均召回率（UAR）和加权平均召回率（WAR）For emotion classification tasks, unweighted average recall (UAR) and weighted average recall (WAR)(Chumachenko et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025)。对于连续情绪回归和 MMSE 评分任务，一致性相关系数 (CCC) 和平均绝对误差 (MAE). For continuous emotion regression and MMSE score tasks, concordance correlation coefficient (CCC) and mean absolute error (MAE)(Nicolaou et al., 2011; Ringeval et al., 2015; Chu et al., 2024)被使用。具体来说，对于MCI筛查任务，采用准确性（ACC）和宏观F1分数（F1）来评估少数MCI类别的整体正确性和敏感性are used. Specifically, for MCI screening task, accuracy (ACC) and the macro F1-score (F1) are adopted to evaluate both overall correctness and sensitivity to the minority MCI class(Weiner et al., 2011).

6. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了 MECO，这是第一个致力于老年人情感和认知理解的多模态数据集，它将行为和生理信号与情感状态和认知评估的统一注释相结合。我们为单模态和多模态设置下的情绪和认知预测建立了基线基准，为可重复的评估提供了标准化参考。 然而，目前的研究存在一定的局限性。具体来说，分析仅限于刺激引发的数据，而未分析音频模态，并且数据集仅限于 42 个受试者。我们计划整合音频数据并招募更多受试者进行进一步分析。 此外，MECO 提供了丰富的多模态基础，可以为老年人群预训练强大的情绪识别模型，最终推进认知感知智能系统。In this work, we introduced MECO, the first multimodal dataset dedicated to emotion and cognitive understanding in older adults, which integrates behavioral and physiological signals with unified annotations for affective states and cognitive assessment. We established baseline benchmarks for both emotion and cognitive prediction under unimodal and multimodal settings, providing a standardized reference for reproducible evaluation. However, the current study has certain limitations. Specifically, the analysis is restricted to stimulus-elicited data, leaving the audio modality unanalyzed, and the dataset is limited to 42 subjects. We plan to integrate audio data and recruit more subjects for further analysis. Furthermore, MECO offers a rich multimodal foundation to pre-train robust emotion recognition models for geriatric populations, ultimately advancing cognitively aware intelligent systems.
