在快速移动的车辆上使用雷达进行多目标跟踪：Towards Multi-Object-Tracking with Radar on a Fast Moving Vehicle:
频域处理雷达的潜力On the Potential of Processing Radar in the Frequency Domain

Tim Hansen1, Arturo Gomez-Chavez1, Ilya Shimchik2, and Andreas Birk1 1Authors are with the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Constructor University Bremen, Germany.2Author is with the Constructor Knowledge Labs (CKL), Bremen, Germany.
摘要Abstract

我们在本文中提倡在频域中处理雷达数据，以实现针对噪声和结构误差的更高鲁棒性，特别是与基于特征的方法相比。这也适用于场景中的高动态性，即带有传感器的车辆的自我运动加上未知数量的其他移动物体的存在。除了高鲁棒性之外，频域处理还具有迄今为止被忽视的优点，即用于配准等的基于相关性的底层方法提供了有关场景中所有移动结构的信息。 一个典型的汽车应用案例是超车操作，这里将其在自动驾驶赛车的背景下用作激励示例。提出了二维傅里叶软件 (FS2D) 的初步实验和结果，使用 Boreas 数据集来演示仅雷达里程计，即没有传感器融合的雷达里程计，以支持我们的论点。We promote in this paper the processing of radar data in the frequency domain to achieve higher robustness against noise and structural errors, especially in comparison to feature-based methods. This holds also for high dynamics in the scene, i.e., ego-motion of the vehicle with the sensor plus the presence of an unknown number of other moving objects. In addition to the high robustness, the processing in the frequency domain has the so far neglected advantage that the underlying correlation based methods used for, e.g., registration, provide information about all moving structures in the scene. A typical automotive application case is overtaking maneuvers, which in the context of autonomous racing are used here as a motivating example. Initial experiments and results with Fourier SOFT in 2D (FS2D) are presented that use the Boreas dataset to demonstrate radar-only-odometry, i.e., radar-odometry without sensor-fusion, to support our arguments.

I 简介Introduction

汽车雷达的一个重要优势是其在恶劣天气条件下的稳健性An important advantage of automotive radar is its robustness in challenging weather conditions[1]，即它不像视觉或激光雷达那样受到雨雪的强烈影响。 此外，与激光雷达相比，它的成本相对较低, i.e., it is not as strongly affected by rain and snow as vision or lidar. Also, it is relatively low-cost compared to lidar[2]。 但雷达提供的分辨率相对较低且数据嘈杂. But radar delivers relatively low resolution and noisy data[2, 3]。此外，它本质上会受到干扰，这些干扰可以减轻，但在实践中仍然是一个因素 - 直到产生鬼物体。. Furthermore, it inherently suffers from interferences, which can be mitigated, but which still is a factor in practice - up to the generation of ghost objects.

因此，雷达处理特征的使用有其局限性——无论它们是由数据驱动的人工智能设计还是生成。原因是每个特征仅使用来自有限大小的内核的局部信息，该信息很容易被噪声破坏。当在频域中操作时，使用不同尺度的信息，范围从小尺度、局部数据到更大的结构。这通常会导致处理（例如注册）的鲁棒性更高。在注册方面，还有另一个迄今为止被忽视的优势。The use of feature for radar processing has hence its limitations - no matter whether they are engineered or generated by data-driven AI. The reason is that each feature only uses local information from a limited size kernel, which is easily corrupted by noise. When operating in the frequency domain, information on different scales is used ranging from small scale, local data up to larger structures. This leads in general to a higher robustness in the processing, e.g., for registration. In the context of registration, there is an other, so far neglected advantage.

无论是否，例如，傅里叶梅林No matter whether, e.g., Fourier-Mellin[4, 5], 傅里叶梅林软件, Fourier-Mellin-SOFT[6]，或 2D 傅里叶软件 (FS2D), or Fourier-SOFT in 2D (FS2D)[7]用于频域处理时，底层相关方法本质上提供了有关场景中所有移动结构的信息。are used for the processing in the frequency domain, the underlying correlation methods inherently provide information about all moving structures in the scene.

更准确地说，它们在理论上为刚性运动的每个自由度 (dof) 生成狄拉克脉冲。由于要配准的扫描中的缺陷，即噪声、遮挡、部分重叠等，这些实际上是更宽的峰值（图 1）。More precisely, they generate in theory a Dirac pulse for each degree-of-freedom (dof) for a rigid motion. These are in practice broader peaks due to the imperfections in the to be registered scans, i.e., noise, occlusions, partial overlap, etc. (Fig.1）。峰值表示传感器姿势的相对运动参数，即它们生成自我运动估计。此外，对于传感器数据中的每个移动结构，即对于每个要跟踪的对象，在每个自由度内生成峰值。因此，物体的运动由峰值和自我运动一起表示。因此，频域处理的主要优点是不需要先验地知道动态对象的数量并且不需要涉及的方法来检测对象。). The peaks indicate the relative motion-parameters of the sensor-poses, i.e., they generate ego-motion estimates. In addition, a peak is generated within each dof for each moving structure in the sensor data, i.e., for each of the to be tracked objects. So, the motion of the objects is indicated by peaks together with the ego-motion. A major advantage of the processing in the frequency domain is hence that the number of dynamic objects does not need to be known a priori and that no involved methods are required to detect the objects.

一般来说，多目标跟踪（MOT）是一个非常成熟的研究领域，特别是在使用传统成像传感器（即相机）的计算机视觉中Multi-Object-Tracking (MOT) in general is a very well established research field, especially in Computer Vision when using conventional imaging sensors, i.e., cameras[14]。但这对于雷达的使用来说并不成立——尽管最近基于雷达的运动估计方面的工作明显增加. But this does not hold with respect to the use of radar - though there is a clear increase in work on radar-based motion estimation in recent time[15, 16]。具有视觉功能的 MOT 往往依赖于特征，即非常局部的信息，由于与相机相比，雷达数据的质量较差，这些特征往往无法进行自我运动估计. MOT with vision tends to rely on features, i.e., very local information, which already tend to fail for ego-motion estimation due to the - in comparison to cameras - poor quality of radar data[17]。因此，雷达运动估计倾向于使用更稳健的替代方案，例如正态分布变换 (NDT). Motion-estimation with radar hence tends to use more robust alternatives like the Normal Distribution Transform (NDT)[18]或高斯混合模型 (GMM)or Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM)[19]以及在频域中运行的经典方法，例如傅里叶梅林变换as well as classical methods operating in the frequency domain like the Fourier-Mellin-Transform[20].

使用频域进行图像处理的基础已经在半个多世纪前奠定了The foundations of using the frequency domain for image processing have been laid more than half a century ago[21]。但概念上仍然有进步；例如，Fourier-Mellin 的扩展. But there are still conceptual advancements; for example, the extension of Fourier-Mellin[5, 4]到 3Dto 3D[6]。为了说明频域运算的潜力，我们在这里使用自己的 2D 傅里叶软件形式 (FS2D) 的工作. To illustrate the potential of operations in the frequency domain, we use here own work in form of Fourier-SOFT in 2D (FS2D)[7].

IV 结论Conclusions

我们在本文中证明，频域处理在处理汽车雷达数据方面具有很高的潜力。为此，提出了使用二维傅里叶软件（FS2D）作为一种新的配准方法的初步实验和结果，证明了仅雷达里程计，即没有传感器融合的雷达里程计。此外，我们指出了迄今为止被忽视的方面，即在频域中，底层相关方法本质上提供了有关场景中所有移动结构的信息。这不仅提高了动态场景中的鲁棒性，而且还可以大大简化对噪声数据的多对象跟踪。We made a case in this paper that processing in the frequency domain has a high potential for handling automotive radar data. To this end, initial experiments and results with Fourier SOFT in 2D (FS2D) as a new registration method are presented that demonstrate radar-only-odometry, i.e., radar-odometry without sensor-fusion. Furthermore, we pointed to the so far neglected aspect that in the frequency domain, the underlying correlation methods inherently provide information about all moving structures in the scene. This not only increases robustness in dynamic scenes, but it can also substantially ease multi-object-tracking on noisy data.
