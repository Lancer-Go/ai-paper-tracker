摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地用于筛选和排名求职者，从而激励候选人战略性地操纵算法招聘系统。我们学习Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used to screen and rank job applicants, creating incentives for candidates to strategically manipulate algorithmic hiring systems. Using controlled experiments, we show that prompt injection reliably improves applicant rankings when résumé quality is homogeneous and few candidates inject. However, its effectiveness rapidly diminishes as more candidates inject, collapsing when manipulation becomes widespread. When candidate quality is heterogeneous, prompt injection is less effective on average, but can occasionally allow lowerquality candidates to outrank higher-quality ones, raising fairness concerns. Overall, LLMbased screening is most vulnerable when manipulation is rare and candidate quality differences are small. 1 简介1 Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）现在位于招聘台的两边。雇主越来越多地在自动简历筛选和候选人分类渠道中部署法学硕士（Schroer，2025）。与此同时，求职者正在转向人工智能工具来起草经过优化的简历，以便在基于法学硕士的申请人跟踪系统（ATS）中表现良好。Large language models (LLMs) now sit on both sides of the hiring table. Employers increasingly deploy LLMs in automated résumé screening and candidate triage pipelines (Schroer, 2025). At the same time, job seekers are turning to AI tools to draft résumés optimized to perform well in LLMbased applicant tracking systems (ATS).

随着候选人意识到自动简历筛选系统依赖于法学硕士进行初步评估和排名，一些候选人已经开始战略性地瞄准模型本身。特别是，候选人尝试所谓的As candidates become aware that automated résumé screening systems rely on LLMs for initial evaluation and ranking, some have begun to strategically target the models themselves. In particular, candidates experiment with so-called及时注射prompt injection一些策略，例如在简历中嵌入不可见或白色文本，以影响法学硕士评估并提高其申请的排名（Gorelick，2025；Rumage，2025）。strategies, such as embedding invisible or white-colored text in résumés, to influence LLM evaluations and push their applications higher in the ranking (Gorelick, 2025; Rumage, 2025).

候选人行为的这种转变提出了一个自然而重要的问题：当面临即时注入时，基于法学硕士的简历筛选系统是否会继续根据潜在质量来选择候选人？This shift in candidate behavior raises a natural and consequential question: when faced with prompt injection, do LLM-based résumé screening systems continue to select candidates based on underlying quality?

出于这种担忧，我们研究了候选人的战略行为（特别是即时注入的使用）是否以及如何扭曲法学硕士驱动的简历筛选中基于质量的选择。我们的中心研究问题是：在战略提示注入的情况下，基于法学硕士的简历筛选系统在多大程度上保留了基于质量的候选人选择？Motivated by this concern, we study whether and how strategic candidate behavior, specifically, the use of prompt injection, distorts quality-based selection in LLM-driven résumé screening. Our central research question is: To what extent do LLM-based résumé screening systems preserve quality-based candidate selection in the presence of strategic prompt injection?

具体来说，我们检查以下内容：Specifically, we examine the following:

  1. 当候选人的基本素质相似时，及时注入是否会系统地改变他们在自动简历筛选中的相对排名？When candidates are similar in underlying quality, does prompt injection systematically alter their relative rankings in automated résumé screening?

  2. 在即时注入的情况下，候选人质量的异质性如何影响基于法学硕士的系统区分高质量候选人和低质量候选人的能力？How does heterogeneity in candidate quality affect the ability of LLM-based systems to distinguish high-quality candidates from lower-quality ones in the presence of prompt injection?

  3. 当多个候选人同时进行即时注入时，系统按质量对候选人进行排名的能力是否会恶化、稳定或表现出饱和效应？As multiple candidates engage in prompt injection simultaneously, does the system’s capacity to rank candidates by quality deteriorate, stabilize, or exhibit saturation effects?

为了回答这些问题，我们进行了一系列受控的简历排名实验，旨在隔离注入的自我推销文本的因果效应。我们考虑一种设置，其中候选人使用提示注入策略，即在他们的简历中附加额外的文本，使他们能够战略性地影响基于法学硕士的筛选器。我们操纵控制战略影响的两个关键因素：To answer these questions, we conduct a series of controlled résumé-ranking experiments designed to isolate the causal effect of injected self-promotional text. We consider a setting in which candidates use prompt injection strategies, i.e., append additional text to their résumés, allowing them to strategically influence an LLM-based screener. We manipulate two key factors that govern strategic impact:竞争强度competition intensity，通过实施即时注入的候选人数量来衡量，以及, measured by the number of candidates implementing prompt injection, and比较质量comparative quality，通过注入的简历相对于其他申请人的质量是高还是低来衡量。我们的设计包括单注射, measured by whether the injected résumé is high- or low-quality relative to other applicants. Our design includes both single-injection

（仅注入一份简历）和多次注入（注入多份简历）设置，以及质量同质或异质的候选池。我们进一步研究了直接程度不同的两种注入类型：（i）a(only one résumé is injected) and multi-injection (multiple résumés are injected) settings, as well as candidate pools that are either homogeneous or heterogeneous in quality. We further examine two types of injection that differ in their level of directness: (i) a描述性注入descriptive injection，它提供了关于候选人的评价性陈述，以及 (ii), which provides an evaluative statement about the candidate, and (ii) an指导性注射instructive injection，它明确指导模型的决策。这种区别使我们能够将隐性评价线索与显性决策指令的效果进行比较。最后，我们评估两个 LLM 的所有设置：GPT-4o-mini（闭源）和 DeepSeek-V3.2（开源），以评估我们的研究结果在模型架构中的稳健性。, which explicitly directs the model’s decision. This distinction allows us to compare the effects of implicit evaluative cues with explicit decision directives. Finally, we evaluate all settings across two LLMs: GPT-4o-mini (closedsource) and DeepSeek-V3.2 (open-source), to assess the robustness of our findings across model architectures.

我们的结果表明，当简历质量均匀且注入有限时，及时注入可以可靠地提高候选人排名。当只有一小部分候选人注入时，注入的简历相对于未注入的同行获得了实质性且一致的排名提升，特别是在 DeepSeek-V3.2 和指导注入下。随着简历质量变得参差不齐，注入的有效性就会减弱：基于法学硕士的系统平均优先考虑质量较高的简历。然而，操纵行为并未消除——在接近决策阈值时，注入的低质量简历有时会超过高质量简历，从而扭曲基于质量的选择。最后，随着注入变得普遍，它的好处崩溃了：随着更多候选人注入，排名增益和成功率趋向于零，这表明强烈的饱和效应。在各种模型和注入类型中，这种模式是稳健的：当候选人质量同质且操纵有限时，基于法学硕士的简历筛选最容易受到影响，并且随着质量多样性和竞争性操纵的增加而变得更有弹性，尽管偶尔存在排名扭曲。Our results show that prompt injection reliably improves candidate rankings when résumé quality is homogeneous and injection is limited. When only a small fraction of candidates inject, injected résumés achieve substantial and consistent rank gains relative to non-injected peers, particularly under DeepSeek-V3.2 and under instructive injection. As résumé quality becomes heterogeneous, the effectiveness of injection is attenuated: LLMbased systems prioritize higher-quality résumés on average. However, manipulation is not eliminated— near decision thresholds, injected lower-quality résumés can occasionally outrank higher-quality ones, distorting quality-based selection. Finally, as injection becomes widespread, its benefits collapse: rank gains and success rates converge toward zero as more candidates inject, indicating strong saturation effects. Across models and injection types, this pattern is robust: LLM-based résumé screening is most vulnerable when candidate quality is homogeneous and manipulation is limited, and becomes more resilient as quality diversity and competitive manipulation increase, though occasional ranking distortions persist.

5 结论5 Conclusion

通过对照实验，我们研究了基于法学硕士的简历筛选中的提示注入，并表明即使是最小的自我推销文字也可以影响候选人排名。当候选人资格相似且操纵很少时，及时注入是最有效的，但随着越来越多的候选人注入，其好处迅速减弱，在高竞争强度下崩溃。当候选人质量不同时，操纵平均较弱，但仍然会扭曲决策边界附近的结果，有时会导致质量较低的候选人排名超过质量较高的候选人。这些效果因模型而异：DeepSeek-V3.2 始终对描述性和指导性注入更加敏感，而 GPT-4o-mini 对于描述性自我推销相对稳健，但变得更加敏感。Using controlled experiments, we study prompt injection in LLM-based résumé screening and show that even minimal self-promotional text can influence candidate rankings. Prompt injection is most effective when candidates are similarly qualified and manipulation is rare, but its benefits diminish rapidly as more candidates inject, collapsing under high competitive intensity. When candidate quality differs, manipulation is weaker on average, yet can still distort outcomes near decision boundaries, occasionally allowing lower-quality candidates to outrank higher-quality ones. These effects vary across models: DeepSeek-V3.2 is consistently more responsive to both descriptive and instructive injections, whereas GPT-4o-mini is relatively robust to descriptive self-promotion but becomes

我们的研究结果强调，基于法学硕士的筛选系统的稳健性不仅取决于模型行为，还取决于它们运行的战略和竞争环境。专注于孤立攻击的评估可能会忽略候选者之间的拥塞和交互影响，从而严重错误地描述系统漏洞。因此，应在竞争性、多攻击者条件下评估稳健的招聘渠道，特别注意操纵有限但有影响力的情况。从设计的角度来看，减少对自由形式的自我推销输入的依赖并在决策阈值附近进行额外的审查可以帮助减轻排名扭曲并保持基于质量的选择。Our findings highlight that the robustness of LLM-based screening systems depends not only on model behavior but also on the strategic and competitive environment in which they operate. Evaluations that focus on isolated attacks can substantially mischaracterize system vulnerability by ignoring congestion and interaction effects among candidates. Robust hiring pipelines should therefore be evaluated under competitive, multi-attacker conditions, with particular attention to scenarios where manipulation is limited but impactful. From a design perspective, reducing reliance on free-form self-promotional inputs and applying additional scrutiny near decision thresholds can help mitigate ranking distortions and preserve quality-based selection.

道德考虑Ethical Considerations

这项工作研究了法学硕士介导的简历筛选中的漏洞，因此This work studies vulnerabilities in LLM-mediated résumé screening and thus has两用dual-use影响。尽管我们的实验有意保守——使用合成简历并将提示注入限制为两次简短注入，一次是描述性的，一次是指导性的，没有优化越狱、自动攻击搜索或部署指导——战略性插入文本可以改变排名的发现仍然可能被滥用，并且还可能鼓励更广泛地采用类似注入的策略，从而导致申请人和筛选系统之间的军备竞赛。implications. Although our experiments are intentionally conservative—using synthetic résumés and restricting prompt injection to two short injections, one descriptive and one instructive, without optimized jailbreaks, automated attack search, or deployment guidance—the finding that strategically inserted text can shift rankings could still be misused and may also encourage wider adoption of injectionlike tactics, contributing to an arms race between applicants and screening systems.

我们的结果引起了多个利益相关者的公平和安全担忧。对于申请人来说，排名扭曲（尤其是接近入围名单或面试门槛）可能会改变下游结果，并可能使那些已经面临结构性障碍并且可能较少获得战略简历写作或人工智能辅助优化指导的个人处于不利地位。对于招聘人员和组织来说，即使系统按预期使用，对有说服力但基础薄弱的自我评估的敏感性也可能导致错误的筛选，从而破坏信任、一致性和问责制。我们的异构池结果进一步表明，注入经验不足的候选人有时可能会超过经验丰富的候选人，这凸显了战略文本可能会扭曲基于质量的选择，而不仅仅是放大现有资格。尽管我们的实验使用受控筛选场景来隔离机制，但现实世界的管道在模型、提示、监督和验证步骤方面有所不同，因此即使定性风险仍然存在，定量效应大小也可能会有所不同。Our results raise fairness and security concerns for multiple stakeholders. For applicants, rank distortions—especially near shortlist or interview thresholds—can change downstream outcomes and may disproportionately disadvantage individuals who already face structural barriers and may have less access to coaching on strategic résumé writing or AI-assisted optimization.
