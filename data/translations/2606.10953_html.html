摘要。Abstract.

带家具的平面图是房地产可视化、室内设计和建筑工作流程的基础。然而，由于缺乏真实的、专业设计的带有对象级家具注释的平面图数据集，自动家具布置的进展受到限制。为了解决这一差距，我们引入了 AntPlan-270，这是一个包含 270 个建筑平面图的精选数据集，其中包含十个住宅房间类别的每个房间的家具边界框注释。在此数据集的基础上，我们提出了 Architect-Ant，这是一个由微调的视觉语言模型提供支持的可编辑自动家具框架。家具布局使用紧凑的、基于坐标的领域特定语言 (DSL) 来表示，该语言对对象类别和相对于房间几何形状的放置进行编码。为了改进空间推理，我们生成程序推理轨迹，捕获建筑约束，例如墙壁对齐、门窗间隙、流通、固定装置兼容性和特定于房间的家具库存，并使用它们来监督模型的微调。然后，我们对候选对象放置应用偏好优化，以进一步优化布局质量。生成的 DSL 可以光栅化为语义掩模，并用于调节基于 Flux 的 LoRA 渲染器，生成逼真的蓝图风格的布置平面图图像，同时保留可编辑的符号布局。布局陈设实验表明，Architect-Ant 可以生成几何上有效且功能上合理的布局，并提出了用于提供更大的纯结构平面图数据集的可扩展路径。Furnished floor plans are fundamental to real estate visualization, interior design, and architectural workflows. However, progress in automatic furniture arrangement has been limited by the lack of real, professionally designed floor-plan datasets with object-level furniture annotations. To address this gap, we introduce AntPlan-270, a curated dataset of 270 architectural floor plans with per-room furniture bounding box annotations across ten residential room categories. Building on this dataset, we present Architect-Ant, an editable automatic furnishing framework powered by a fine-tuned vision-language model. Furniture layouts are represented using a compact, coordinate-based domain-specific language (DSL) that encodes object categories and placements relative to the room geometry. To improve spatial reasoning, we generate procedural reasoning traces that capture architectural constraints such as wall alignment, door and window clearance, circulation, fixture compatibility, and room-specific furniture inventories, and use them to supervise fine-tuning of the model. We then apply preference optimization over candidate object placements to further refine layout quality. The generated DSL can be rasterized into semantic masks and used to condition a Flux-based LoRA renderer, producing realistic blueprint-style furnished floor-plan images while preserving the editable symbolic layout. Experiments on layout furnishing show that Architect-Ant produces geometrically valid and functionally plausible layouts, and suggest a scalable path for furnishing larger structure-only floor-plan datasets.

1. 简介Introduction

带家具的平面图是房地产可视化、室内设计和建筑交流的核心。家具使计划变得可解释：它传达了房间规模、可能的功能、流通以及空间是否可以支持预期用途。手动生成此类布局非常耗时，而自动布置仅当结果在几何上有效、实用且可用作对象级表示（而不仅仅是像素）时才有用。Furnished floor plans are central to real estate visualization, interior design, and architectural communication. Furniture makes a plan interpretable: it conveys room scale, likely function, circulation, and whether a space can support the intended use. Producing such layouts manually is time-consuming, while automatic furnishing is useful only when the result is geometrically valid, functional, and available as an object-level representation rather than only as pixels.

家具摆放是一个受约束的布局问题。布局必须将适当类型、大小和位置的对象放置在房间边界内，同时保持它们可访问、可见和可用。这些要求部分是几何的，部分是语义的。床不仅仅是一个应该避免碰撞的矩形；它也是一个应该避免碰撞的矩形。它是一个与墙壁、门、流通路径和其他家具有着典型关系的物体。仅当放置附近的物体后椅子仍可触及且可用时，椅子才有效。合理的布局必须满足一些约束，这些约束很容易用设计语言表达，但很难从干净的示例中学习，尤其是在缺乏完整的平面图示例的情况下。Furniture placement is a constrained layout problem. A layout must place objects of appropriate type, size, and position inside a room boundary while keeping them accessible, visible, and usable. These requirements are partly geometric and partly semantic. A bed is not just a rectangle that should avoid collisions; it is an object with typical relations to walls, doors, circulation paths, and other furniture. A chair is valid only if it remains reachable and usable after nearby objects are placed. A plausible layout must satisfy constraints that are easy to express in design language but difficult to learn from clean examples, especially when complete examples of furnished floor plans are scarce.

这个问题与建筑平面图生成不同，后者通常涉及房间、墙壁、邻接、边界和开口的组织。我们专注于家具阶段：给定一个房间或平面图几何形状，生成占据房间的物体并确定它们应该去哪里。此阶段有不同的故障模式。带家具的房间可能会因为家具重叠、挡住门、没有留下可通行的路径或违反基本使用限制而失败。This problem is distinct from architectural floor-plan generation, which typically concerns the organization of rooms, walls, adjacencies, boundaries, and openings. We focus on the furnishing stage: given a room or floor-plan geometry, generate the objects that occupy the room and determine where they should go. This stage has different failure modes. A furnished room may fail because furniture overlaps, blocks a door, leaves no traversable path, or violates basic use constraints.

家具布局是一个对象级问题。设计师编辑墙壁、开口、家具实例、尺寸、位置和关系，而不是像素。因此，我们用结构化文本表示任务：输入描述房间边界和相关建筑元素，例如门、窗和开口，输出描述家具对象的类别、位置和轴对齐范围。相同的表示公开了定义布局有效性所需的变量。碰撞、遏制、间隙、门障碍、可达性、墙壁亲和力和成对对象关系可以直接通过结构化几何和标签进行评估。在像素空间中，这些检查取决于首先恢复底层对象和几何形状。Furniture layout is an object-level problem. Designers edit walls, openings, furniture instances, dimensions, positions, and relations, not pixels. We therefore represent the task in structured text: the input describes the room boundary and relevant architectural elements such as doors, windows, and openings, and the output describes furniture objects with category, position, and axis-aligned extent. The same representation exposes the variables needed to define layout validity. Collision, containment, clearance, door obstruction, reachability, wall affinity, and pairwise object relations can be evaluated directly over structured geometry and labels. In pixel space, these checks depend on first recovering the underlying objects and geometry.

该公式所需的数据有限。公共数据集很少提供许多完整的、真实的、带家具的平面图作为离散的可编辑对象。建筑数据集可能提供图像或矢量几何，但它们通常描述墙壁、房间、门、窗户和其他建筑元素，而不是家具实例。存在提供的场景数据集，包括具有对象级布局的合成 3D 数据集；它们可以转换成这种形式，但当目标是了解房间通常是如何布置的时，它们并不能替代真实布置的平面图。在实践中，有用的提供信息更常见于图像、扫描或绘图中，其中结构必须由检测器或解析器提取。The data needed for this formulation is limited. Public datasets rarely provide many complete, real, furnished floor plans as discrete editable objects. Architectural datasets may provide images or vector geometry, but they usually describe walls, rooms, doors, windows, and other building elements rather than furniture instances. Furnished scene datasets exist, including synthetic 3D datasets with object-level layouts; they can be converted into this form, but they are a poor substitute for real furnished floor plans when the goal is to learn how rooms are typically furnished. In practice, useful furnishing information is more often found in images, scans, or drawings, where structure must be extracted by detectors or parsers.

这些提取的布局很有用，但噪音很大。它们可能包含不正确的类别、缺少家具、不准确的尺寸或不精确的位置。我们将它们用作轻量级适应的伪标签：足以将模型移向目标表示和近似房间统计数据，但不能作为每个提取的对象都是正确的证据。我们将由此产生的每个房间语料库（来自 10 个住宅房间类别的 270 个专业设计的平面图）称为 AntPlan-270；本文的实验重点关注家具最多的四个类别（卧室、浴室、厨房和客厅）。Those extracted layouts are useful but noisy. They may contain incorrect categories, missing furniture, inaccurate dimensions, or imprecise locations. We use them as pseudo-labels for lightweight adaptation: enough to move the model toward the target representation and approximate room statistics, but not as evidence that every extracted object is correct. We refer to the resulting per-room corpus, drawn from 270 professionally designed floor plans across ten residential room categories, as AntPlan-270; the experiments in this paper focus on the four most-furnished categories (bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, and living room).

我们分阶段训练结构化布局生成器。提示提供了对家具类别和粗略空间关系的初步了解。然后，伪标记布局上的轻量级微调阶段使模型适应目标格式和近似房间统计数据。基于规则的评估器使用几何和语义标准对采样布局进行评分，例如房间内的包容性、对象重叠、门访问、可穿过的路径、墙壁亲和力和对象-对象关系。然后，我们使用从这个基于规则的评估器导出的偏好来应用偏好优化，训练模型为得分更高的布局分配更高的概率。这些标准按严重程度进行加权，对障碍物或房间外放置等违规行为的处罚较大，对墙亲和力或成对关系等较弱的设计偏好的处罚较小。We train a structured layout generator in stages. Prompting provides an initial prior over furniture categories and coarse spatial relations. A lightweight fine-tuning stage on pseudo-labeled layouts then adapts the model to the target format and approximate room statistics. A rule-based evaluator scores sampled layouts using geometric and semantic criteria such as containment within the room, object overlap, door access, traversable paths, wall affinity, and object–object relations. We then apply preference optimization with preferences derived from this rule-based evaluator, training the model to assign higher probability to the better-scoring layouts. The criteria are weighted by severity, with larger penalties for violations such as obstruction or out-of-room placement and smaller penalties for weaker design preferences such as wall affinity or pairwise relationships.

训练使用三种信号。预训练模型提供了对象共现和共同关系的语义先验。伪标签以目标输出格式为模型提供近似房间规模的统计数据和示例。基于规则的评估器提供明确的设计偏好，而不需要额外的清晰演示。因此，这些规则充当了学习生成器的监督。The training uses three kinds of signal. The pretrained model supplies semantic priors over object co-occurrence and common relations. The pseudo-labels give the model approximate room-scale statistics and examples in the target output format. The rule-based evaluator supplies explicit design preferences without requiring additional clean demonstrations. The rules therefore act as supervision for the learned generator.

The trace first identifies anchor objects (typically large items that should be placed against walls), then iterates through the remaining inventory and places each item in turn, explicitly checking room containment, wall contact for wall-touch classes, door-swing clearance, and pairwise relationships against already-placed objects. Recovery is a training-time augmentation, not an inference-time repair loop; the model emits a single trace at inference. 5. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 Architect-Ant，一个用对象级结构化布局提供住宅平面图的框架。给定房间几何形状和所需的家具库存，Architect-Ant 在紧凑的 DSL 中生成家具类和轴对齐边界框，可以对其进行解析、评分、修改和渲染。该系统结合了来自 AntPlan-270 的伪标记布置布局、具有故障修复恢复功能的程序推理跟踪以及源自确定性规则评分器的偏好对。We presented Architect-Ant, a framework for furnishing residential floor plans with object-level structured layouts. Given room geometry and a requested furniture inventory, Architect-Ant generates furniture classes and axis-aligned bounding boxes in a compact DSL that can be parsed, scored, modified, and rendered. The system combines pseudo-labeled furnished layouts from AntPlan-270, procedural reasoning traces with fail-and-fix recovery, and preference pairs derived from a deterministic rule scorer.

在分布外的 CubiCasa 房间中，Architect-Ant 在四种房间类型中的三种中通过规则得分匹配或改进了监督基线。独立视觉语言评委给出了更加细致入微的结果：浴室和厨房的进步，客厅的接近平局，以及卧室的倒退。定性比较表明，收益通常涉及更柔和的视觉功能偏好，例如连贯的桌椅布置、更紧凑的厨房分组以及更好的墙壁附件，这些都没有被硬性规则分数完全体现。On out-of-distribution CubiCasa rooms, Architect-Ant matches or improves on the supervised baseline by rule score in three of four room types. The independent vision-language judge gives a more nuanced result: gains in bathroom and kitchen, a near tie in living room, and a regression in bedroom. Qualitative comparisons suggest that the gains often involve softer visual-functional preferences, such as coherent chair-table arrangements, tighter kitchen groupings, and better wall attachment, which are not fully captured by hard rule scores.

我们的消融表明，偏好对的构建方式会影响学习到的布局分布。合成对 DPO 将每个偏好定位到单个扰动的边界框，而更广泛的模型对 DPO 可以获得更高的规则分数，但会产生更差的定性布局。这表明验证者导出的偏好对于空间布局生成很有用，但应仔细构建它们，以便偏好信号反映预期的放置属性。Our ablations show that the way preference pairs are constructed affects the learned layout distribution. Synthetic-pair DPO localizes each preference to a single perturbed bounding box, while broader model-pair DPO can achieve higher rule scores but produce worse qualitative layouts. This suggests that verifier-derived preferences are useful for spatial layout generation, but they should be constructed carefully so that the preference signal reflects the intended placement property.

总体而言，当干净的对象级演示有限但可以使用弱注释和明确的空间规则时，Architect-Ant 为提供住宅平面图提供了一条实用的路线。通过将布局保持为结构化对象级表示，该方法支持必须检查、调整和渲染家具放置的工作流程，包括房地产可视化、室内设计和建筑平面图工作流程。类似的想法也可能适用于结合对象放置、显式约束和视觉输出的附近布局设计问题。Overall, Architect-Ant provides a practical route for furnishing residential floor plans when clean object-level demonstrations are limited but weak annotations and explicit spatial rules are available. By keeping the layout as a structured object-level representation, the method supports workflows where furniture placement must be inspected, adjusted, and rendered, including real estate visualization, interior design, and architectural floor-plan workflows. Similar ideas may also apply to nearby layout-design problems that combine object placement, explicit constraints, and visual output.
