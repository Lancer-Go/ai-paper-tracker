摘要Abstract

利用多元时间序列飞行数据提高飞行安全不仅需要准确检测风险事件，更重要的是在飞行员控制行为层面清晰解释其根本原因。现有的可解释的人工智能技术，例如特征重要性图，通常需要大量的领域知识才能将其转化为具有操作意义的解释。擅长语言推理的大型语言模型（LLM）为这个问题带来了一个有希望的解决方案。然而，在该领域应用法学硕士面临着几个关键挑战，包括模式不一致、分类能力有限、用于微调的特定任务数据的稀缺以及缺乏领域知识。 为了克服这些挑战，我们提出了 FlightLLM，这是一种基于先验引导的语义 LLM 方法，用于可解释的飞行安全分析。具体来说，我们首先执行特征工程来解决模态不一致问题，将统计描述符与具有物理意义的飞行指标相结合。这种表示由语义离散化模块进一步处理，该模块将抽象的数字模式转换为与语言推理更兼容的定性描述。 此外，由于LLM本身并不是强分类器，因此CatBoost作为统计专家被纳入其中，并将其预测结果作为事先指导注入到提示中。 进一步采用对比少样本学习策略来补偿有限的数据。最后，我们设计结构化提示，将航空特定知识嵌入到推理过程中。我们使用硬着陆这一具有复杂因果机制的代表性风险事件作为锚点，在 704 个真实 A320 飞行样本的数据集上评估 FlightLLM。实验结果表明，所提出的方法实现了有竞争力的分类性能，同时为事件原因生成直接合理的解释。这项工作为预测和解释飞行安全事件提供了一种新的解决方案。Improving flight safety with multivariate time series flight data requires not only accurate detection of risk events, but more importantly, clear interpretation of their underlying causes at the level of pilot control behavior. Existing explainable AI techniques, such as feature importance maps, often require considerable domain knowledge to translate them into operationally meaningful explanations. Large Language Models (LLMs), which excel at language reasoning, bring a promising solution to this issue. However, applying LLMs in this domain presents several key challenges, including modal inconsistency, limited classification ability, scarcity of task-specific data for fine-tuning, and lack of domain knowledge. To overcome these challenges, we propose FlightLLM, a prior-guided semantic LLM-based approach for interpretable flight safety analysis. Specifically, we first perform feature engineering to address modal inconsistency, combining statistical descriptors with physically meaningful flight indicators. This representation is further processed by a Semantic Discretization module, which converts abstract numerical patterns into qualitative descriptions that are more compatible with language reasoning. In addition, since LLMs are not inherently strong classifiers, CatBoost is incorporated as a statistical expert, and its prediction results are injected into the prompt as prior guidance. A contrastive few-shot learning strategy is further adopted to compensate for limited data. Finally, we design structured prompts to embed aviation-specific knowledge into the inference process. Using hard landing, a representative risk event with complex causal mechanisms, as an anchor point, we evaluate FlightLLM on a dataset of 704 real-world A320 flight samples. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves competitive classification performance while generating direct and reasonable explanations for event causes. This work provides a new solution to predict and explain flight safety events.

I 简介Introduction

民航被广泛认为是最安全的交通方式之一。然而，飞行安全事件的防控仍然是行业的核心关注点。根据国际航空运输协会（IATA）的安全报告，采取措施减少运行风险的发生仍然是重中之重。因此，对飞行安全事件的过程进行详细分析对于降低事故发生率、提高航空安全水平具有重要意义。现代民航领域，保障飞行安全已从简单的事件监测发展到复杂因素的分析。目标不再局限于确定发生了什么，而是揭示潜在的因果机制，特别是与飞行员控制行为和与环境相互作用相关的机制。对于专家和飞行员来说，了解事件背后的原因对于设计有效的培训计划和风险缓解策略至关重要。Civil aviation is widely recognized as one of the safest ways of transportation. However, the prevention and control of flight safety incidents remains a core focus of the industry. According to safety reports from the International Air Transport Association (IATA), taking measures to reduce operational risk occurrences remains a top priority. Consequently, conducting detailed analysis on the process of flight safety events is of significance for reducing incident rates and enhancing the level of aviation safety. In modern civil aviation field, ensuring flight safety has evolved from simple event monitoring to analysis of complex factors. The objective is no longer limited to identifying what happened, but to uncovering the underlying causal mechanisms, particularly those related to pilot control behavior and interactions with the environment. For experts and pilots, understanding the reasons behind the incidents is essential for designing effective training programs and risk mitigation strategies.

为了从可行的角度探讨这些原因并降低未来风险，航空业使用快速访问记录器 (QAR)，这是一种机载设备，可收集多达 2000 个飞行参数，为研究提供基础数据。 QAR 数据由具有多个变量的时间序列组成，记录从发动机启动到关闭的整个飞行周期。它捕获各种信息，包括设备状态（例如起落架、襟翼）、飞行员控制输入（例如俯仰、滚转）和运动学参数（例如高度、垂直速度）。 研究人员利用 QAR 数据来调查各种飞行风险事件，例如机尾撞击To explore these causes from an actionable angle and mitigate future risks, the aviation industry uses Quick Access Recorder (QAR), an onboard device which collects up to 2000 flight parameters, to provide the foundational data for research. QAR data consists of time series with multiple variables that record the entire flight cycle, from engine start to shutdown. It captures diverse information, including equipment status (e.g., landing gear, flaps), pilot control inputs (e.g., pitch, roll), and kinematic parameters (e.g., altitude, vertical speed). Researchers leverage QAR data to investigate various flight risk events, such as tail strike[35], 机场风险, airport risk[34], 硬着陆, hard landing[23], 长着陆, long landing[38]，以及跑道偏离, and runway veer-off[26]。 早期研究通常依赖于参数超出信号. Early studies often relied on parameter exceedance signals[37]评估风险，忽略了参数之间的潜在关系。传统的机器学习方法，例如聚类算法to assess risks, which overlooks the potential relationships between parameters. Traditional machine learning methods such as clustering algorithms[23]和支持向量机and SVM[11]已被应用于硬着陆的分类和分析。尽管在分类和预测方面有效，但这些方法将检测指标置于因果解释之上。他们很少回答事件发生的原因。 随着深度学习的不断发展，像 LSTM 这样的现代方法have been applied to classify and analyze hard landing. Although effective in classification and prediction, these methods prioritized detection metrics over causal explanations. They rarely answered why an incident occurred. With the continuous development of deep learning, modern approaches like LSTM[33]和变压器and Transformer[29, 4]也被用于该领域。这些方法擅长处理高维时间序列数据并实现高精度。 值得注意的是，之前基于 Transformer 架构的研究试图通过可视化通过时间间隔注意力机制学习到的注意力权重来增强可解释性。虽然这代表着向解释飞行安全事件迈出了有意义的一步，但这些模型本质上是“黑匣子”。内部注意力分布虽然信息丰富，但仍需要领域专家进行翻译。因此，模型输出和人类可以理解的因果叙述之间仍然存在巨大差距。这种限制限制了这些方法在现实世界风险预防和安全管理中的实际适用性。have also been used in this field. These approaches excel at processing high-dimensional time-series data and achieve high accuracy. Notably, prior studies based on Transformer architectures have attempted to enhance interpretability by visualizing attention weights learned through time-interval attention mechanisms. While this represents a meaningful step toward explaining flight safety events, these models are essentially “black boxes”. The internal attention distributions, although informative, still need to be translated by the domain experts. Consequently, a substantial gap persists between model outputs and causal narratives understandable to human. This limitation constrains the practical applicability of these methods in real-world risk prevention and safety management.

大型语言模型（LLM）的出现为这一挑战提供了一个有前景的解决方案。与以前的模型不同，法学硕士拥有自然语言推理能力，这减轻了传统黑盒机器学习方法固有的不透明性。他们可以生成与其预测结果一致的文本解释The emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) offers a promising solution to this challenge. Unlike the previous models, LLMs possess natural language reasoning capabilities, which mitigate the opacity inherent in traditional black-box machine learning methods. They can generate textual explanations aligned with their prediction outcomes[10]，使它们适合对飞行安全事件进行端到端解释。然而，将法学硕士应用于航空领域面临着一些挑战。首先，QAR 数据由高维多元时间序列组成，而 LLM 主要针对语言处理进行优化，并在数值推理中表现出固有的局限性, making them suitable for end-to-end interpretation of flight safety events. Nevertheless, applying LLMs to the aviation domain presents several challenges. First, QAR data consists of high-dimensional multivariate time series, whereas LLMs are primarily optimized for linguistic processing and exhibit inherent limitations in numerical reasoning[30]。因此，直接将原始时间序列数据输入法学硕士会导致分析效率低下和输出不稳定。跨模式的不一致是一个需要解决的巨大差距。最近的研究试图解决这个问题. Directly feeding raw time series data into LLMs therefore leads to analytical inefficiency and unstable outputs. The cross-modal inconsistency is a large gap to be addressed. Recent studies have tried to address this issue[12, 40, 18, 10]，对我们有参考价值。然而，QAR 数据的复杂性大于这些研究中使用的数据集的复杂性。 其次，法学硕士并不是为时间序列分类而设计的，因此在处理领域内的分类时会遇到不准确的情况, which have a reference value to us. However, the complexity of QAR data is greater than that of the datasets used in these studies. Second, LLMs are not designed for classifying time series, so they encounter inaccuracies when dealing with classification in the domain[15]。 第三，虽然微调是提高法学硕士能力的有效方法，但由于飞行安全事件发生的概率极低，缺乏大规模数据集仍然是一个障碍。 此外，由于航空运营和飞行过程的复杂性，法学硕士可能会产生与既定领域知识不一致的解释，从而导致潜在的幻觉风险。. Third, although fine-tuning is an effective way to improve LLMs’ capability, the lack of large-scale datasets remains an obstacle, given the extremely low occurrence probability of flight safety events. Furthermore, due to the complexity of aviation operations and flight processes, LLMs may generate explanations that are inconsistent with established domain knowledge, leading to potential hallucination risks.

为了解决上述挑战，本研究提出了 FlightLLM，这是一种基于法学硕士的方法，用于可解释的飞行安全分析。鉴于硬着陆是着陆阶段具有代表性的安全事件，我们选择它作为研究的锚点。整体框架围绕四个关键挑战进行组织：跨模式不一致、分类能力有限、数据稀缺和领域知识不足。为了应对第一个挑战，即连接高维 QAR 数据和文本数据，我们首先进行特征工程，将原始时间序列压缩为更紧凑和更有意义的表示。通过将从 TSFresh 库中自动提取的统计特征与源自航空领域知识的手动设计的物理特征相结合，我们构建了一个综合的特征空间。然而，对于法学硕士来说，仅凭数字特征仍然很难推理。因此，在此特征工程的基础上，我们进一步引入了语义离散化模块，通过分位数策略将数值模式转化为语义描述，这有助于我们充分利用法学硕士的语言处理优势。第三，为了弥补LLM分类的局限性，我们引入了统计专家机制，利用传统模型对数值的敏感性来指导LLM的推理。这种方法结合了传统模型的精确性和法学硕士的可解释性。此外，由于缺乏大规模数据进行微调，采用对比小样本学习策略来改善这种情况。我们检索最相似的正常着陆和硬着陆样本来构建对比上下文。通过这种方式，我们迫使LLM进行差异分析，从而在不改变LLM内部权重的情况下提高对模糊样本的分类性能。最后，由于通用法学硕士缺乏足够的航空专业知识，我们设计了一个结构化的提示，注入领域知识来减轻幻觉。实验结果表明，所提出的 FlightLLM 不仅在分类方面有效，而且还表现出卓越的可解释性。我们的工作可以帮助法学硕士提供直观、高质量的文字解释，为民航事故的分析和预防提供有价值的指导。To address the aforementioned challenges, this study proposes FlightLLM, an LLM-based approach for interpretable flight safety analysis. Given that hard landing is a representative safety event during the landing stage, we choose it as an anchor in our study. The overall framework is organized around four key challenges: cross-modal inconsistency, limited classification capability, data scarcity, and insufficient domain knowledge. To deal with the first challenge, bridging the high-dimensional QAR data and the textual data, we first conduct feature engineering, compressing the raw time series into a more compact and meaningful representation. By combining automatically extracted statistical features from the TSFresh library with manually designed physical features derived from aviation domain knowledge, we build a comprehensive feature space. However, numerical features alone are still difficult for LLMs to reason. Therefore, on this basis of feature engineering, we further introduce a Semantic Discretization module, transforming numerical patterns into semantic descriptions by quantile strategy, which assists us to fully utilize the LLMs’ linguistic processing strengths. Third, to compensate for the limitation of LLMs in classifying, we introduce a Statistical Expert mechanism, which utilizes the sensitivity of traditional models to numerical values to guide the LLMs’ reasoning. This approach combines the precision of traditional models with the interpretability of LLMs. Furthermore, given the lack of large-scale data for fine-tuning, a Contrastive Few-Shot Learning strategy is adopted to improve this situation. We retrieve the most similar normal and hard landing samples to construct a contrastive context. By this, we compel LLMs to perform analysis between differences, which improves classification performance on vague samples without changing the internal weights of LLMs. Finally, because general-purpose LLMs lack sufficient aviation expertise, we design a structured prompt, injecting domain knowledge to mitigate the hallucination. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed FlightLLM is not only effective in classification but also exhibits superior interpretability. Our work can help LLMs provide intuitive and high-quality textual explanations, offering valuable guidance for the analysis and prevention of incidents in civil aviation.

They respectively employed LIME and SHAP to identify critical impact factors. Midtfjord等人。et al. [25]利用 XGBoost 来预测跑道安全状况，包括温度和污染水平，并应用 SHAP 来确定有影响的变量。埃本斯佩格utilized XGBoost to predict runway safety conditions, including temperature and pollution levels, and applied SHAP to determine influential variables. Ebensperger等人。et al. [8]利用SHAP和特征排列来增强跑道配置辅助模型的可解释性。 虽然这些研究为环境风险评估提供了宝贵的见解，但它们的解释重点仍然是外部的，忽略了试点控制行动和操作程序的内部动态。然而，此类人为因素对于有针对性的飞行员培训和主动改善航空安全至关重要。解决这一差距需要解释将预测结果与飞行员操纵行为联系起来的框架，而不仅仅是环境条件。 为了从飞行员行为的角度推进着陆安全分析，一些研究探索了数据驱动的可解释性方法。杨leveraged SHAP and feature permutation to enhance the interpretability of runway configuration assitance model. While these studies contribute valuable insights into environmental risk assessment, their explanatory focus remains external, neglecting the internal dynamics of pilot control actions and operational procedures. However, such human factors are critical to targeted pilot training and proactive aviation safety improvement. VI 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出 FlightLLM 用于可解释的飞行安全事件分析。该方法弥合了法学硕士所需的QAR时间序列数据和文本数据之间的差距，弥补了有限数据的不足，并将领域知识注入到法学硕士中，使其能够更好地进行分类，更重要的是，为以硬着陆为代表的飞行安全事件生成因果解释。 特征工程模块将原始 QAR 数据转换为混合特征向量，将统计描述符与物理有意义的指标相结合。 语义离散化模块将连续数值映射到定性语义标签。它在一定程度上解决了法学硕士在数字标记处理方面的已知局限性。 统计专家提示模块引入了传统模型作为助手，为法学硕士进行推断提供了锚点。 此外，动态上下文检索模块选择对比样本，提供稳定分类决策的上下文信息。 实验结果表明，FlightLLM 实现了有竞争力的分类性能，同时提供了与航空动力学一致的解释。该方法显示出前所未有的可解释性并提供了可行的建议，这对于提高飞行安全具有重要意义。In this paper, we propose FlightLLM for interpretable flight safety event analysis. The approach bridges the gap between QAR time series data and text data needed by LLMs, compensates for the shortage of limited data and injects domain knowledge into LLMs, enabling them to perform better in classification, and more importantly, generate causal explanations for flight safety events represented by hard landing. The Feature Engineering module converts raw QAR data into hybrid feature vectors that combine statistical descriptors with physically meaningful indicators. The Semantic Discretization module maps continuous numerical values to qualitative semantic labels. It addresses the known limitations of LLMs in numerical token processing to some extent. The Statistical Expert Hinting module introduces a traditional model as an assistant, providing an anchor for LLMs to infer. In addition, the Dynamic Context Retrieval module selects contrastive samples, providing contextual information that stabilizes classification decisions. Experimental results indicate that FlightLLM achieves competitive classification performance while offering explanations consistent with aviation dynamics. The approach shows unprecedented interpretability and provides actionable advice, which is significant for improving flight safety.

尽管取得了令人鼓舞的结果，但我们的工作也存在一些局限性。 首先，我们没有对硬着陆特有的大型语言模型进行微调。所有实验都是使用公开的和预先训练的模型进行的。虽然该方法具有泛化能力，但针对航空领域的微调可以进一步提高推理的一致性和性能。 其次，虽然FlightLLM可以扩展来分析多个飞行安全事件，但需要修改提示以确保与每个事件的底层物理机制的一致性。这种特定于事件的提示修改引入了额外的手动工作。 因此，未来的研究可以集中于开发更多可转移的即时工程策略。统一且物理基础的提示框架将增强不同航空安全场景的可扩展性和稳健性。Despite the encouraging results, our work also has several limitations. First, we did not fine-tune the large language models specific to hard landing. All experiments were conducted using publicly available and pre-trained models. Although this approach has the generalization capability, fine-tuning aimed at aviation field may further enhance reasoning consistency and performance. Second, although FlightLLM can be extended to analyze multiple flight safety events, modifying the prompts is required to ensure consistency with the underlying physical mechanisms of each event. This event-specific prompt modification introduces additional manual effort. Future research can therefore focus on developing more transferable prompt engineering strategies. A unified and physically grounded prompting framework would enhance scalability and robustness for diverse aviation safety scenarios.
