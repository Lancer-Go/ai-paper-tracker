Early methods typically mapped front-view images to low-level control commands, while recent approaches use multi-view encoders, Transformer decoders, BEV representations, trajectory queries, and vision pretraining to unify scene understanding and planning[15, 18, 7, 12, 27, 23, 4]。 这些方法显着提高了规划性能，特别是在 NAVSIM 等现代评估协议下. These methods have significantly improved planning performance, especially under modern evaluation protocols such as NAVSIM[9, 2]。 然而，大多数端到端规划器仍然主要通过模仿记录的专家轨迹进行训练。 这种监督是稳定且数据高效的，但它与评估安全性、可行性、进度和舒适度的基于规则的规划指标并不完全一致。 CLOVER 的不同之处在于明确使用评估者提供的规划反馈来训练轨迹级价值估计器并细化候选提案分布，而不是仅依赖于单轨迹模仿。. Such supervision is stable and data-efficient, but it is not fully aligned with rule-based planning metrics that evaluate safety, feasibility, progress, and comfort. CLOVER differs by explicitly using evaluator-provided planning feedback to train a trajectory-level value estimator and to refine the candidate proposal distribution, rather than relying only on single-trajectory imitation.

Learned cost functions, ranking modules, and trajectory scorers have been used to improve selection quality, especially when paired with rich proposal sets[38, 20, 25]。 然而，自动驾驶中的轨迹评分具有挑战性，因为真正的规划评分取决于地图约束、未来障碍物状态、交互规则和不可微的度量计算。 学习的评分者必须根据当前观察和候选轨迹来估计这种质量，并且直接最大化这种不完美的分数可以利用评分者错误或减少多样性。 CLOVER 将评分器视为在评估器提供的子分数上进行训练的闭环价值估计器，并保守地使用它：不是无限制的分数最大化，而是通过教师选择的顶级分数来细化生成器. 2.4 与强化学习的关系Relation to Reinforcement Learning

CLOVER 与演员评论家强化学习有着表面上的相似之处：生成器提出轨迹，评分器对其进行评估。 然而，CLOVER并不是一种强化学习方法。 经典的强化学习依赖于环境交互、试错探索以及对访问状态的奖励优化。 相比之下，CLOVER 在记录的驾驶场景上离线运行；所有反馈都是使用基于规则的规划评估器根据生成的候选轨迹计算的，并且不执行在线环境交互或探索。 近期作品如EvadriveCLOVER shares a superficial similarity with actor–critic reinforcement learning: a generator proposes trajectories and a scorer evaluates them. However, CLOVER is not a reinforcement learning method. Classic reinforcement learning relies on environment interaction, trial-and-error exploration, and reward optimization over visited states. In contrast, CLOVER operates offline on logged driving scenes; all feedback is computed from generated candidate trajectories using a rule-based planning evaluator, and no online environment interaction or exploration is performed. Recent works such as Evadrive[19]探索 RL 式的自动驾驶规划，而 CLOVER 则专注于闭环价值估计和保守的自蒸馏提案选择规划。 因此，CLOVER 更适合被视为离线评估者引导的排名和提案细化框架，而不是基于 RL 的规划器。explore RL-style planning for autonomous driving, whereas CLOVER focuses on closed-loop value estimation and conservative self-distillation for proposal-selection planning. Thus, CLOVER is better viewed as an offline evaluator-guided ranking and proposal-refinement framework rather than an RL-based planner.

6 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

CLOVER 目前主要在每个场景的轨迹级别进行评分和排名。 虽然对于 PDMS 式评估有效，但时间聚合的 EPDMS 术语（例如扩展舒适度）取决于跨框架一致性，这只能通过我们可选的锚辅助重新排名来缓解。 未来的工作可以集成历史感知或序列级评分器，以在排序和细化过程中直接建模时间一致性。CLOVER currently performs scoring and ranking mainly at the per-scene trajectory level. While effective for PDMS-style evaluation, temporally aggregated EPDMS terms such as extended comfort depend on cross-frame consistency, which is only mitigated by our optional anchor-assisted reranking. Future work could integrate history-aware or sequence-level scorers to model temporal consistency directly during ranking and refinement.

7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presented四叶草CLOVER，一个闭环价值估计和排名框架，用于基于提案选择的端到端自动驾驶规划。 CLOVER 首先将单轨迹模仿扩展到评估者过滤的集合级提案覆盖范围，然后通过保守的闭环自蒸馏来改进生成器，从而解决了训练评估不匹配的问题。 轨迹级评分器适合生成建议的真实评估器子分数，而教师选择的最高分, a closed-loop value estimation and ranking framework for proposal-selection based end-to-end autonomous driving planning. CLOVER addresses the training–evaluation mismatch by first expanding single-trajectory imitation into evaluator-filtered set-level proposal coverage, and then refining the generator through conservative closed-loop self-distillation. A trajectory-level scorer is fitted to true evaluator sub-scores on generated proposals, while teacher-selected top-kk向量帕累托目标通过稳定性正则化指导生成器。 我们进一步分析了不完美的评分者何时可以可靠地改进提案生成，表明当评分者选择的目标在真实评估者的统计下丰富时，保守的细化可以增加高质量的提案支持。 NAVSIM v1、NAVSIM v2 EPDMS、NavHard 和补充 nuScenes 评估的实验证明了强大的规划性能，而提案质量分析表明 CLOVER 不仅改进了最终的 top-1 选择，还改进了潜在的候选分布。and vector-Pareto targets guide the generator with stability regularization. We further analyzed when an imperfect scorer can reliably improve proposal generation, showing that conservative refinement can increase high-quality proposal support when scorer-selected targets are statistically enriched under the true evaluator. Experiments on NAVSIM v1, NAVSIM v2 EPDMS, NavHard, and supplementary nuScenes evaluation demonstrate strong planning performance, while proposal-quality analyses show that CLOVER improves not only final top-1 selection but also the underlying candidate distribution.
