人脸去识别：从捕获到处理的以领域为中心的调查Face De-Identification: A Domain-Centric Survey from Capture to Processing

Hui Wei, Hao Yu, and Guoying Zhao, Fellow, IEEE Hui Wei, Hao Yu, and Guoying Zhao are with the ELLIS Institute Finland, Espoo, 02150, Finland, and also with the Center for Machine Vision and Signal Analysis, University of Oulu, 90570 Oulu, Finland. Guoying Zhao is the corresponding author. E-mail: guoying.zhao@oulu.fi
摘要Abstract

人脸去识别（De-ID）旨在删除或隐藏图像或视频中的个人可识别面部特征，以防止身份识别，同时保留下游任务的实用性。 随着对数据隐私和负责任的人工智能的日益重视，人脸去ID已成为跨越计算机视觉和隐私保护社区的活跃研究领域。 早期的方法以及许多当代的方法都在Face de-identification (De-ID) aims to remove or conceal personally identifiable facial features in images or videos to prevent identity recognition while preserving utility for downstream tasks. With the rising emphasis on data privacy and responsible AI, face De-ID has emerged as an active research area spanning computer vision and privacy-preserving communities. Early approaches, and many contemporary ones, operate in the数字化digital通过捕获后处理修改像素级或外观级特征来调整域。 最近的进展通过将隐私机制直接集成到人脸去ID中，将人脸去ID扩展到后处理之外domain by modifying pixel-level or appearance-level features through post-capture processing. Recent advances extend face De-ID beyond post-processing by integrating privacy mechanisms directly into传感器sensors在图像采集期间，桥接传感系统和下游视觉算法。 同时，during image acquisition, bridging sensing systems and downstream vision algorithms. In parallel,物理的physical-领域方法探索在捕获之前在现实环境中隐藏身份信息的可穿戴配件和材料。 在本次调查中，我们提出了第一个统一的-domain methods explore wearable accessories and materials that conceal identity information in real-world environments prior to capture. In this survey, we present the first unified概述overview它跨越整个数据采集管道，涵盖物理、传感器和数字领域。 通过这个以领域为中心的视角，我们系统地分析了当前的方法、技术进步以及每个阶段固有的独特挑战。 然后，我们审查和组织现有的评估协议，检查当前的实践并强调对标准化、综合基准的迫切需求。 最后，我们确定了关键的开放问题并概述了新兴的研究方向，以指导这个快速发展的领域的未来工作。 为了支持正在进行的研究，我们维护一个项目页面，用收集的数据集和开源代码组织相关文献：that spans the full data acquisition pipeline, encompassing the physical, sensor, and digital domains. Through this domain-centric lens, we systematically analyze current methodologies, technical progress, and the distinct challenges inherent to each stage. We then review and organize existing evaluation protocols, examining current practices and highlighting the critical need for standardized, comprehensive benchmarks. Finally, we identify key open problems and outline emerging research directions to guide future work in this rapidly evolving field. To support ongoing research, we maintain a project page that organizes relevant literature with collected datasets and open source code:https://github.com/CV-AC/Awesome-FaceDe-ID.
