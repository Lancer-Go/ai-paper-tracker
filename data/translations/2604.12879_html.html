FastGrasp：基于学习的全身控制方法，用于移动机械手快速灵巧抓取FastGrasp: Learning-based Whole-body Control method for Fast Dexterous Grasping with Mobile Manipulators

Heng Tao1∗, Yiming Zhong1∗, Zemin Yang1∗, Yuexin Ma1† 1ShanghaiTech University {taoheng2023, mayuexin}@shanghaitech.edu.cn∗Equal contribution †Corresponding author
摘要Abstract

快速抓取对于物流、制造和服务应用中的移动机器人至关重要。现有方法在高速运动下的冲击稳定性、实时全身协调以及跨不同物体和场景的泛化方面面临着根本性挑战，受到固定底座、简单抓手或缓慢触觉响应能力的限制。我们建议Fast grasping is critical for mobile robots in logistics, manufacturing, and service applications. Existing methods face fundamental challenges in impact stabilization under high-speed motion, real-time whole-body coordination, and generalization across diverse objects and scenarios, limited by fixed bases, simple grippers, or slow tactile response capabilities. We propose快速掌握FastGrasp，一个基于学习的框架，集成了抓取引导、全身控制和触觉反馈，用于移动快速抓取。我们的两阶段强化学习策略首先通过以对象点云为条件的条件变分自动编码器生成不同的抓取候选者，然后在最佳抓取选择的引导下执行移动底座、手臂和手的协调运动。触觉感应可实现实时抓握调整，以处理冲击效果和物体变化。大量的实验证明了在模拟和现实场景中的卓越抓取性能，通过有效的模拟到真实的传输实现了跨不同物体几何形状的稳健操作。我们的项目页面位于, a learning-based framework that integrates grasp guidance, whole-body control, and tactile feedback for mobile fast grasping. Our two-stage reinforcement learning strategy first generates diverse grasp candidates via conditional variational autoencoder conditioned on object point clouds, then executes coordinated movements of mobile base, arm, and hand guided by optimal grasp selection. Tactile sensing enables real-time grasp adjustments to handle impact effects and object variations. Extensive experiments demonstrate superior grasping performance in both simulation and real-world scenarios, achieving robust manipulation across diverse object geometries through effective sim-to-real transfer.Our project page is available athttps://taoheng-star.github.io/fastgrasp-page/.

I 简介INTRODUCTION

快速抓取能力使灵巧的移动机器人能够在高速运动时准确捕捉目标物体。它对于精确、高效的操作至关重要，在物流、家庭服务和工业制造领域有着重要的应用——提高分拣效率、用户体验和生产吞吐量。掌握这项技能标志着机器人技术从静态操作向动态交互发展的关键突破。Fast grasping capability enables dexterous mobile robots to accurately capture target objects during high-speed motion. It is essential for precise and efficient manipulation, with significant applications in logistics, domestic service, and industrial manufacturing—boosting sorting efficiency, user experience, and production throughput. Mastering this skill marks a key breakthrough in advancing robotics from static operations to dynamic interaction.

然而，现有的快速抓取研究在多个维度上表现出显着的局限性。静态灵巧操控方法However, existing research on fast grasping exhibits significant limitations in multiple dimensions. Static dexterous manipulation approaches[27, 31, 30]通过复杂的抓取合成方法实现了非凡的精度，但从根本上受到固定基础限制的限制，使它们无法处理超出其预定义工作空间的物体。相反，移动操纵系统achieve remarkable precision through sophisticated grasp synthesis methods but are fundamentally constrained by fixed base limitations, rendering them incapable of handling objects beyond their predefined workspace. Conversely, mobile manipulation systems[3, 8, 2]提供更大的工作空间覆盖范围，但通常采用简单的夹具，严重限制了它们灵巧的操作能力。此外，虽然触觉增强系统provide enhanced workspace coverage but typically employ simple grippers, severely limiting their dexterous manipulation capabilities. Additionally, while tactile-enhanced systems[4, 11]尽管它们在补偿视觉限制方面表现出了希望，但它们要么需要大量的计算资源，要么缺乏高速动态抓取场景所需的快速响应能力。show promise in compensating for visual limitations, they either require substantial computational resources or lack the rapid response capabilities necessary for high-speed dynamic grasping scenarios.

这些限制给移动底座和灵巧手的快速抓取任务带来了几个基本挑战：These limitations give rise to several fundamental challenges in this task of fast grasp with mobile base and dexterous hand:(i) 高速运动下稳定抓取物体(i) stable grasping of objects under high-speed motion：高速抓取会产生高冲击力，导致物体反弹、旋转或滑动。短暂的接触窗口需要精确的实时控制，任何时间或空间的错误都会导致失败；: High-speed grasping induces high impact forces, causing object rebound, rotation, or slippage. The brief contact window demands precise real-time control, as any temporal or spatial error leads to failure;(ii) 实时全身协调运动规划(ii) real-time whole-body coordinated motion planning：与在固定工作空间中实现高精度的静态灵巧系统或使用简单夹具的移动系统不同，快速灵巧抓取需要高速运动和多手指操作之间的无缝全身协调，这是一个挑战，需要在严格的时间约束下协同集成实时感知、动态规划和自适应控制；: unlike static dexterous systems that achieve high precision in fixed workspaces or mobile systems that use simple grippers, fast dexterous grasping requires seamless whole-body coordination between high-speed motion and multi-fingered manipulation—a challenge that demands the synergistic integration of real-time perception, dynamic planning, and adaptive control under strict temporal constraints;(iii) 抓取不同形状和大小的物体(iii) grasping objects with diverse shapes and sizes：不同环境中不同几何形状和不同姿势的物体需要具有高泛化能力和鲁棒性的控制策略。: objects of varying geometries and varying poses in diverse environments demand a control strategy with high generalization capability and robustness.

为了应对这些挑战，我们建议To address these challenges, we propose快速掌握FastGrasp，一个基于学习的框架，集成了全身控制、抓取引导和触觉反馈，用于移动快速抓取。我们的方法通过采用两阶段策略来解决高速运动下的稳定性问题，首先生成不同的候选对象，然后指导执行以最大程度地减少影响。针对实时全身协调，我们开发了统一的强化学习框架，可同时控制移动底座、手臂和手，确保导航和操纵的无缝集成。为了处理不同形状和尺寸的物体，我们采用了触觉反馈，可以快速进行抓握调整并增强对不同物体几何形状和环境条件的概括。我们的策略经过模拟训练，并通过模拟到真实的传输部署在真实的机器人上。我们的主要贡献总结如下：, a learning-based framework that integrates whole-body control, grasp guidance, and tactile feedback for mobile fast grasping. Our approach tackles the stability issues under high-speed motion by employing a two-stage policy that first generates diverse grasp candidates and then guides the execution to minimize impact effects. For real-time whole-body coordination, we develop a unified reinforcement learning framework that simultaneously controls the mobile base, arm, and hand, ensuring seamless integration of navigation and manipulation. To handle objects with diverse shapes and sizes, we incorporate tactile feedback that enables rapid grasp adjustments and enhances generalization across varying object geometries and environmental conditions. Our policy is trained in simulation and deployed on real robots through sim-to-real transfer. Our key contributions are summarized as follows:

  2. 2.

    我们采用预训练的抓取姿势生成器来提供高质量的抓取候选，指导动态抓取策略学习并增强快速接近和接触过程中的稳定性。We employ a pretrained grasp pose generator to provide high-quality grasp candidates, guiding dynamic grasping policy learning and enhancing stability during rapid approach and contact.

  3. 3.

    我们集成触觉传感以实现实时抓取调整，从而增强不同物体几何形状的鲁棒性。We integrate tactile sensing to enable real-time grasp adjustments, enhancing robustness across diverse object geometries.

  4. 4.

    我们展示了有效的模拟到真实的转换，在模拟和现实实验中实现了卓越的性能，验证了该框架的实用性和有效性。We demonstrate effective sim-to-real transfer, achieving superior performance in both simulation and real-world experiments, validating the framework’s practicality and effectiveness.

IV-A 概述Overview

我们的目标是使全身机器人能够快速接近并抓取超出其初始操作范围的物体，同时保持基本速度，然后快速从工作台上缩回，同时确保物体稳定性。灵感来自Our goal is to enable whole-body robots to rapidly approach and grasp objects beyond their initial operational range while maintaining base velocity, then quickly retract from the table while ensuring object stability. Inspired by[28]，我们的 FastGrasp 框架由两个阶段组成。, our FastGrasp framework consists of two stages.

首先，抓取指导生成：为了解决泛化抓取问题并降低全身协调复杂性，我们预先训练基于条件变分自动编码器（CVAE）的生成器，以从对象点云生成不同的抓取建议（第 1 节）。First, grasp guidance generation: To address generalizable grasping and reduce whole-body coordination complexity, we pre-train a conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE)-based generator to produce diverse grasp proposals from object point clouds (Sec.IV-B）。然后通过最大化手到物体的覆盖范围来选择最佳的抓取候选者，以指导 RL 策略实现稳定且可执行的抓取（第 1 节）。). The optimal grasp candidate is then selected by maximizing hand-to-object coverage to guide the RL policy toward stable and executable grasps (Sec.IV-C）。其次，具有触觉反馈的策略学习：我们使用指导信息和实时机器人状态作为输入来训练策略，以生成用于快速抓取执行的控制命令。为了解决高速运动过程中惯性力引起的抓取失败问题，我们集成了二进制触觉反馈来监控物体和手之间的物理交互（第 1 节）。). Second, policy learning with tactile feedback: We train the policy using guidance information and real-time robot states as inputs to generate control commands for fast grasping execution. To address grasp failures caused by inertial forces during high-speed motion, we integrate binary tactile feedback to monitor physical interactions between the object and hand (Sec.IV-D）。完整的框架如图所示。). IV-C 把握指导选择Grasp Guidance Selection

我们使用章节中的方法生成不同的掌握候选者We generate diverse grasp candidates using the method in SectionIV-B。然而，并非所有候选者都可以快速掌握执行力。物体背面的抓握可能会受到运动学限制或遮挡，从而妨碍实时完成。因此，我们设计了一个选择过程来识别有效且稳定的抓取建议。. However, not all candidates are feasible for fast grasping execution. Grasps on the object’s rear side may be kinematically constrained or occluded, preventing real-time completion. Therefore, we design a selection process to identify valid and stable grasp proposals.

我们的方法从两个角度采用分层过滤：Our method employs hierarchical filtering from two perspectives:可执行性过滤Executability filtering：我们过滤需要手臂绕过物体或遵循延伸轨迹的抓握。具体来说，基于机器人和目标物体之间的相对位姿，我们定义了用于抓取规划的前向抓取空间：即以从目标指向机器人的法线向量为基准的抓取锥体。在这个空间内的抓取姿势，称为向前抓取，其特点是最短的运动路径和最小的关节运动，从而实现最高的抓取效率。相比之下，非前向抓取（例如后向抓取）需要末端执行器进行超过90度的旋转并进行回避操作，这显着增加了运动复杂性和时间成本。因此，需要末端执行器旋转超过 90 度的抓取会在规划阶段被系统地过滤，因为这会显着增加执行时间并违反快速抓取要求。: We filter grasps requiring the arm to circumvent the object or follow extended trajectories. Specifically, based on the relative pose between the robot and the target object, we define a forward grasping space for grasp planning: namely, a grasping cone benchmarked against the normal vector pointing from the target towards the robot. Grasping poses within this space, referred to as forward grasps, are characterized by the shortest motion paths and minimal joint movement, thereby achieving the highest grasping efficiency. In contrast, non-forward grasps (such as rear grasps) require the end-effector to undergo rotations exceeding 90 degrees and perform avoidance maneuvers, which significantly increases motion complexity and time cost. Consequently, the grasps that require end-effector rotations exceeding 90 degrees are systematically filtered during the planning phase, as these significantly increase execution time and violate fast grasping requirements.碰撞检测过滤Collision detection filtering：我们消除了末端执行器会下降到物体最低点以下以防止与支撑表面发生碰撞的候选者。: We eliminate candidates where the end-effector would descend below the object’s lowest point to prevent collisions with supporting surfaces.

VI 结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种基于学习的移动快速抓取框架，通过全身控制、抓取引导和触觉反馈确保高速操作的稳定性。两阶段策略生成不同的候选对象，并在最优选择的指导下执行协调运动。统一的强化学习框架同时控制整个身体，而触觉反馈可以实现实时抓握调整并提高跨对象几何形状的泛化能力。广泛的模拟和真实实验验证了该方法的卓越性能和有效的模拟到真实的转换。This paper presents a learning-based framework for mobile fast grasping that ensures stability in high-speed operations through whole-body control, grasp guidance, and tactile feedback. A two-stage policy generates diverse grasp candidates and executes coordinated motions guided by optimal selection. A unified reinforcement learning framework simultaneously controls the whole body, while tactile feedback enables real-time grasp adjustment and improves generalization across object geometries. Extensive simulations and real-world experiments validate the approach’s superior performance and effective sim-to-real transfer.

当前的框架在复杂环境下高速运动时抓取平面物体和确保安全方面面临局限性。未来的工作将侧重于优化不规则物体的抓取策略，增强动态场景下的安全机制，并将快速导航与鲁棒操纵相结合，以提高系统的实用性。The current framework faces limitations in grasping flat objects and ensuring safety during high-speed motion in complex environments. Future work will focus on optimizing grasp strategies for irregular objects, enhancing safety mechanisms in dynamic scenarios, and integrating fast navigation with robust manipulation to improve system practicality.
