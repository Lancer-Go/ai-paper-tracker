GenAI 使用的复杂性：Sophistication in GenAI Use:

来自大公司的现场证据Field Evidence from a Large Firm

摘要：Abstract:我们研究了大公司后台员工在生成式人工智能 (genAI) 使用方面的复杂程度有何不同。使用专有数据，我们在 2025 年的八个月内观察了来自 15 个职能领域的近 4,000 名后台员工的 713,564 条员工提示及其相应的大型语言模型响应。我们记录了三个主要发现。首先，高级员工表现出更复杂的 genAI 使用，与补充 genAI 功能的领域专业知识一致。其次，不同职能部门的复杂程度差异很大，其中战略、数字创新和项目管理的复杂程度最高，这三个部门共同关注全公司的战略举措和组织变革。第三，我们没有观察到复杂性随着时间的推移而提高，也没有观察到正式人工智能培训后的持久改进，这表明复杂的使用可能很难改变。总之，我们的研究提供了对复杂的 genAI 使用的测量和见解，管理者可以使用它来改善结果，研究人员可以在未来的研究中使用它。We study how sophistication in generative AI (genAI) use varies among the back-office workforce of a large firm. Using proprietary data, we observe 713,564 employee prompts and their corresponding large language model responses from nearly 4,000 back-office employees across 15 functional areas over eight months in 2025. We document three main findings. First, senior employees exhibit more sophisticated genAI use, consistent with domain expertise complementing genAI capabilities. Second, sophistication varies considerably across functions and is highest in Strategy, Digital Innovation, and Project Management, three groups that share a focus on firmwide strategic initiatives and organizational change. Third, we observe neither improvements in sophistication over time nor lasting improvements following formal AI training, suggesting that sophisticated use can be difficult to change. Together, our study provides measures of and insights into sophisticated genAI use that managers can use to improve outcomes and that researchers can use in future research.

关键词：Keywords:生成式人工智能；大型语言模型；工作场所技术的采用；技术使用的复杂性generative AI; large language models; workplace technology adoption; technology use sophistication

1 简介Introduction

企业已迅速采用生成式人工智能 (genAI)，预计到 2025 年将花费 370 亿美元Companies have rapidly adopted generative artificial intelligence (genAI), spending an estimated $37 billion in 2025(Tully et al., 2025)。 然而，仅采用并不能保证投资回报。 由于公司面临成本和基于代币的使用限制，“鼓励正确的行为以充分利用每次人工智能交互”对于实现组织目标将是必要的. Yet adoption alone does not guarantee a return on investment. As companies face costs and token-based usage limits, “encouraging the right behaviors to get the most out of every AI interaction” is going to be necessary to achieve organizational goals(Cantrell et al., 2026)。 为了深入了解人们如何与 genAI 互动，我们研究了 genAI 复杂程度如何随着时间、资历级别和职能领域的变化而变化，并在一家已经广泛采用 genAI 的大公司中进行人工智能培训。. To provide insights into how people interact with genAI, we examine how genAI sophistication varies over time, across seniority levels and functional areas, and following AI training within a large firm that has already achieved widespread genAI adoption.

我们将复杂的 genAI 使用定义为通过了解该工具的工作原理及其应用场合而获得的熟练使用。 这种使用体现在清晰而具体的说明、刻意的提示技巧以及在广泛的任务组合中应用该工具。 尽管越来越多的文献研究了 genAI 是否被使用、使用频率以及用途We define sophisticated genAI use as skilled use informed by an understanding of how the tool works and where it can be applied. Such use is reflected in clear and specific instructions, deliberate prompting techniques, and application of the tool across a broad mix of tasks. While a growing literature has examined whether, how often, and for what purposes genAI is used(Bick et al., 2026; Chatterji et al., 2025; Handa et al., 2025; Counts et al., 2026; Bonney et al., 2026)，这些研究几乎没有提供关于员工如何与 genAI 互动的证据，更重要的是，他们的互动效果如何。 我们扩展了这些文献，同时还提供了管理者可以用来更好地衡量组织内人工智能使用情况的见解。, these studies provide little evidence on how employees interact with genAI and, more importantly, how well they do so. We extend this literature while also providing insights that managers can use to better measure AI use within their organizations.

我们研究了毕马威会计师事务所 (KPMG) 后台团队内 genAI 复杂程度的变化，该团队在 2025 年 1 月至 8 月的八个月期间为员工提供了多种 genAI 工具的访问权限：包括 Microsoft Copilot 和 aIQ Chat，这是一种企业特定的聊天工具，可提供对商业大语言模型 (LLM) 的访问，例如来自 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的模型。 对于 aIQ 聊天，我们观察完整的对话记录。 为了衡量 genAI 的复杂程度，我们提示法学硕士按用例对每个对话进行分类，并评估其指令的清晰度和特异性以及所采用的提示技术，从而产生复杂使用维度的对话级别度量。 对于 Microsoft Copilot，我们观察员工使用该工具的频率，但不观察他们的对话内容。 我们将对话级别的测量和 Copilot 使用情况汇总到员工月级别，并将它们与每个员工的资历、职能领域和已完成的 genAI 培训联系起来。 我们的样本包括 IT、营销、会计、财务和其他非面向客户的管理职能部门的员工。 由于大多数大型组织中都存在类似的职能，因此我们的设置应该为毕马威以外的公司的经理提供相关的证据。We study variation in genAI sophistication within the back-office group of KPMG LLP (KPMG), which provided its employees with access to multiple genAI tools during the eight-month period from January through August 2025: including Microsoft Copilot and aIQ Chat, an enterprise-specific chat tool that provided access to commercial large language models (LLMs) such as those from Anthropic and OpenAI. For aIQ Chat, we observe the full conversation transcripts. To measure genAI sophistication, we prompt an LLM to classify each conversation by use case and assess the clarity and specificity of its instructions and the prompting techniques employed, producing conversation-level measures of the dimensions of sophisticated use. For Microsoft Copilot, we observe how frequently employees use the tool but not the content of their conversations. We aggregate our conversation-level measures and Copilot usage to the employee-month level and link them to each employee’s seniority, functional area, and completed genAI training. Our sample includes employees in IT, marketing, accounting, finance, and other non-client-facing administrative functions. Because comparable functions exist in most large organizations, our setting should provide evidence relevant to managers in companies beyond KPMG.

我们首先记录员工的采用情况和使用频率。平均而言，83.9% 的员工月至少使用两种 genAI 工具之一，其中 Copilot 的使用频率高于 aIQ Chat。 这一比率超过了之前调查证据中报告的 32.1%We begin by documenting employee adoption and usage frequency. On average, 83.9% of employee-months include use of at least one of the two genAI tools, with Copilot used more frequently than aIQ Chat. This rate exceeds the 32.1% reported in prior survey evidence(Bick et al., 2026)，可能是因为我们研究的公司强烈鼓励其员工使用 genAI。 然而，广泛的采用掩盖了使用强度的巨大差异。 在样本期间使用 aIQ Chat 的员工中，前十分之一的人占所有对话的一半以上。 高级员工更频繁地使用这些工具，主要是因为 Copilot 的使用率更高。 不同职能领域的每月采用率也有所不同，从合规部门的 73% 到内部审计部门的 94%。 最后，在八个月的时间内，使用情况发生了适度变化，这与该公司已达到相对成熟的采用阶段相一致。, possibly because the firm we study strongly encourages its employees to use genAI. Broad adoption, however, masks substantial variation in usage intensity. Among employees who use aIQ Chat during the sample period, the top decile accounts for more than half of all conversations. Senior employees use the tools more frequently, primarily because of greater Copilot use. Monthly adoption rates also vary across functional areas, from 73% in Compliance to 94% in Internal Audit. Finally, usage changes modestly over the eight-month window, consistent with the firm having reached a relatively mature stage of adoption.

接下来我们记录员工使用 aIQ Chat 执行的任务。 这些用例类别构成了我们后续衡量每个员工应用该工具的广泛程度的基础。 与写作相关的任务，包括编辑用户提供的文本、生成新内容或两者兼而有之的请求，出现在大约四分之三的对话中。 员工还使用该工具进行知识检索、文档理解和软件指导，其中较小但有意义的比例致力于编码和数据分析、构思和其他任务。 用例也因资历级别和职能领域而异。 高级员工提出更多知识检索请求，而普通员工提出更多写作和个人请求。 通信以及销售和营销员工在写作和构思方面寻求更多帮助，而信息技术员工则提出更多涉及编码和软件指导的要求。 与采用率分析一起，这些发现为我们分析公司内部复杂程度如何变化提供了背景。We next document the tasks for which employees use aIQ Chat. These use-case categories form the basis for our subsequent measure of how broadly each employee applies the tool. Writing-related tasks, including requests to edit user-provided text, generate new content, or both, appear in roughly three-quarters of conversations. Employees also use the tool for knowledge retrieval, document understanding, and software guidance, with smaller but meaningful proportions devoted to coding and data analysis, ideation, and other tasks. Use cases also vary across seniority levels and functional areas. More senior employees make more knowledge-retrieval requests, whereas staff employees make more writing and personal requests. Communications and Sales & Marketing employees seek more assistance with writing and ideation, while Information Technology employees make more requests involving coding and software guidance. Together with the adoption analyses, these findings provide context for our analysis of how sophistication varies within the firm.

在此背景下，我们转向本文的主要分析并研究复杂使用的变化。 为了实施这个概念，我们使用了三种综合措施：用例多样性、深思熟虑的策略使用和及时的清晰度。 每一项措施都对应于复杂使用的一个组成部分：在广泛的任务组合中应用 genAI，采用有意的提示技术，并提供清晰而具体的指示。 我们对员工月份变化的分析得出了三个主要发现。 首先，我们没有观察到员工对 genAI 的使用在八个月的样本期内变得更加复杂。 其次，每项指标都随着资历的增加而上升，员工级别低于经理，经理级别低于员工，员工级别高于经理级别。 第三，不同职能领域的复杂程度差异很大。 它在战略、数字创新和项目管理方面排名最高，这三个组共同关注公司范围内的战略计划和组织变革。 会计与金融在所有三项复杂的使用指标上均落后。Against this backdrop, we turn to the paper’s primary analyses and examine variation in sophisticated use. To operationalize this concept, we use three composite measures: use-case diversity, deliberate strategy use, and prompt clarity. Each measure corresponds to one component of sophisticated use: applying genAI across a broad mix of tasks, employing deliberate prompting techniques, and providing clear and specific instructions. Our analyses of variation across employee-months yield three main findings. First, we do not observe employees’ genAI use becoming more sophisticated over the eight-month sample period. Second, each measure rises with seniority, with staff ranking below managers and managers below employees above the manager level. Third, sophistication varies considerably across functional areas. It is highest in Strategy, Digital Innovation, and Project Management, three groups that share a focus on firmwide strategic initiatives and organizational change. Accounting & Finance lags on all three sophisticated use measures.

我们的复杂性衡量标准需要谈话记录，而公司很少保留这些记录，而且处理成本高昂。 因此，我们研究这些指标是否与管理者仅从元数据中观察到的四个使用特征有关：员工要求该工具执行多少操作（野心）、员工对请求进行迭代的次数（持久性）、员工使用 aIQ Chat 的频率（频率）以及员工使用公司的另一个 genAI 工具 Copilot 的频率（灵活性）。 可靠的关联将为管理者提供一种低成本的方式来衡量员工使用 genAI 的程度，而不仅仅是频率。 在简单的相关性、带有额外控制的回归以及带有员工固定效应的模型中，我们一致发现，更大的野心和毅力（反映在更长的初始提示和对话中更多的迭代）伴随着更复杂的使用。 最后，我们研究了正式的人工智能培训是否与员工 genAI 复杂程度的提高有关。 在具有员工固定效应的模型中，我们发现员工完成正式 genAI 培训的当月使用更为复杂，但在接下来的几个月中则没有，这表明与这些培训相关的任何改进都是暂时的。 虽然我们的证据并未证明培训无效，但几乎没有证据表明完成正式培训会对观察到的行为产生持久的变化。Our sophistication measures require conversation transcripts, which firms rarely retain and are costly to process. We therefore examine whether these measures relate to four usage characteristics that managers can observe from metadata alone: how much an employee asks the tool to do (ambition), how much the employee iterates on a request (persistence), how often the employee uses aIQ Chat (frequency), and how often the employee also uses Copilot, another of the firm’s genAI tools (flexibility). A reliable association would give managers a low-cost way to gauge how well employees use genAI, not only how often. In simple correlations, regressions with additional controls, and models with employee fixed effects, we consistently find that greater ambition and persistence, reflected in longer initial prompts and more iteration within conversations, accompany more sophisticated use. Finally, we examine whether formal AI training is associated with increases in employee genAI sophistication. In models with employee fixed effects, we find more sophisticated use in the month an employee completes formal genAI training but not in the months that follow, suggesting that any improvement associated with these trainings is temporary. While our evidence does not establish that the training was ineffective, it provides little evidence that completion of formal training produces lasting changes in observed behavior.

我们的研究为管理者提供了一些实际的好处。 首先，我们提供了一个可定制的流程来衡量组织内复杂的 genAI 使用情况。 其次，我们提供来自一家被广泛采用的公司的描述性证据。 我们针对易于观察的指标跨员工群体以及在正式人工智能培训之前和之后的各个时期进行此操作。 这一证据创造了 genAI 基准测试机会，可以帮助管理者评估其组织的进展并为资源分配决策提供信息。 例如，随着时间的推移缺乏改进或培训后持续改进说明组织在促进员工 genAI 使用的持久变化时可能面临困难。Our study provides managers with several practical benefits. First, we provide a customizable process for measuring sophisticated genAI use within their organizations. Second, we provide descriptive evidence from a firm with widespread adoption. We do this across employee groups, in relation to easily observable measures, and across periods before and after formal AI training. This evidence creates genAI benchmarking opportunities that can help managers evaluate their organization’s progress and inform resource allocation decisions. For example, the absence of improvement over time or sustained improvement following training illustrates the difficulty organizations may face in fostering lasting changes in employee genAI use.

我们的论文还对学术文献做出了一些贡献。 现有研究记录了 genAI 的快速采用、常见工作场所用例以及使用特定 AI 工具对生产力的影响Our paper also makes several contributions to the academic literature. Existing research documents rapid genAI adoption, common workplace use cases, and productivity effects of access to particular AI tools(e.g., Bick et al., 2026; Arntz et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024; Bonney et al., 2026; Counts et al., 2026; Choi & Xie, 2026)。 然而这项研究并没有评估员工使用 genAI 的情况。 我们通过引入新的复杂使用衡量标准，并提供现场证据来说明在广泛采用后，一个组织内的员工之间复杂的 genAI 使用有何不同，我们对文献进行了补充。 我们发现，成熟度随着资历的增加而提高，并且在不同的职能领域之间存在很大差异。 随着时间的推移，我们也观察到很少有系统性的改进。 正式培训月份的复杂程度较高，但随后的几个月则不然。 总之，这些发现有助于我们更好地了解不断变化的 genAI 采用时间表，其中注意力从员工是否采用 genAI 转移到复杂的使用如何分布以及采用后的变化。. Yet this research does not evaluate how well employees use genAI. We add to the literature by introducing new measures of sophisticated use and by providing field evidence on how sophisticated genAI use varies among employees inside one organization after adoption is already widespread. We find that sophistication rises with seniority and differs substantially across functional areas. We also observe little systematic improvement over time. Sophistication is higher in the month of formal training but not in subsequent months. Together, these findings help us better understand the evolving genAI adoption timeline in which attention shifts from whether employees adopt genAI to how sophisticated use is distributed and changes after adoption.

7.4 与先前证据的比较Comparison with Prior Evidence

据我们所知，只有少数研究使用对话数据来检查与 genAI 使用质量相关的维度。 当前的两篇工作论文描述了交互类型或工具功效，而不是用户与 genAI 交互的熟练程度的变化。To our knowledge, only a small number of studies use conversation data to examine dimensions related to the quality of genAI use. This longer panel allows us to examine changes over time and, contrary to expectations, we find little evidence of sustained improvement. Second, Anthropic’s analysis does not link conversations to user or organizational characteristics. We link conversations to employee seniority, functional area, and training records, allowing us to examine differences across employees and changes within employees following formal training.

8 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

在我们的研究中，我们研究了一家已经广泛采用 genAI 的大公司后台员工的 genAI 使用情况和复杂程度。 员工可以使用类似的工具和组织支持，使我们能够在相对常见的环境中研究变化。 我们记录了 genAI 的使用和复杂程度如何随着时间、资历级别和职能领域的变化而变化，它们与容易观察到的行为有何关系，以及它们在公司提供的人工智能培训后如何变化。 我们提供了四个主要见解，并在此进行讨论。In our study, we examine genAI use and sophistication among the back-office employees of a large firm that has already achieved widespread genAI adoption. Employees have access to similar tools and organizational support, allowing us to study variation within a relatively common environment. We document how genAI use and sophistication vary over time and across seniority levels and functional areas, how they relate to easily observable behaviors, and how they change following firm-provided AI training. We provide four primary insights, which we discuss here.

首先，高级员工表现出更复杂的 genAI 使用。 这一结果与补充 genAI 功能的领域专业知识一致，但有几种可能的解释。 高级员工可能具有更高的授权能力，因此在指定目标、审查中间工作、提供反馈和推动他们想要的产出方面更加熟练。 另外，高级员工也可能有更强的动机来澄清请求并验证响应，因为他们对最终的工作产品面临更大的责任。 这些提出的解释都表明，复杂的 genAI 使用可能反映了更广泛的专业经验，而不仅仅是 genAI 专业知识或熟悉程度。First, senior employees exhibit more sophisticated genAI use. This result is consistent with domain expertise complementing genAI capabilities, but several explanations are possible. Senior employees may have greater delegation proficiency and therefore be more practiced at specifying objectives, reviewing intermediate work, providing feedback, and pushing for the output they want. Alternatively, senior employees may also have stronger incentives to clarify requests and verify responses because they face greater accountability for the resulting work product. These proposed explanations each suggest that sophisticated genAI use may reflect broader professional experience and not just genAI expertise or familiarity.

其次，不同职能领域的复杂程度差异很大。 战略、数字创新和项目管理在我们的衡量标准中表现出最高的复杂性。 这三个小组共同关注公司范围内的战略计划和组织变革，这可以为员工提供广泛的潜在应用程序和工作，这些应用程序和工作可以从明确定义和迭代任务中受益。 相比之下，会计与金融在所有三项衡量标准中都表现出低于平均水平的复杂程度，也是唯一一个在所有报告的使用衡量标准中显着低于样本平均值的功能领域。 这些群体差异可能反映了任务组合、工作流程适合度、员工特征、部门领导力或对 genAI 的态度。 例如，Second, sophistication varies substantially across functional areas. Strategy, Digital Innovation, and Project Management exhibit the highest sophistication across our measures. These three groups share a focus on firmwide strategic initiatives and organizational change, which may provide employees with both a broad set of potential applications and work that benefits from clearly defining and iterating on tasks. By contrast, Accounting & Finance exhibits below-average sophistication across all three measures and is also the only functional area significantly below the sample mean across all reported usage measures. These group differences may reflect task mix, workflow fit, employee characteristics, department leadership, or attitudes toward genAI. For example,Choi & Xie (2026)使用会计工作流程中内置的 genAI 记录会计师的工作效率和报告质量提升。 这表明，独立的聊天界面可能不太适合会计和财务中常见的电子表格密集型和完善的流程，而更适合与组织变革相关的不断发展的开放式任务。 总之，这些结果表明，复杂的使用可能不仅取决于员工的能力，还取决于工作是否需要我们的措施所捕获的明确指示、深思熟虑的策略和广泛的应用。document productivity and reporting-quality gains for accountants using genAI built into an accounting workflow. This suggests that a standalone chat interface may be less naturally suited to the spreadsheet-intensive and well-established processes common in Accounting & Finance than to the evolving and open-ended tasks associated with organizational change. Together, these results suggest that sophisticated use may depend not only on employee capabilities, but also on whether the work calls for the clear instructions, deliberate strategies, and broad application captured by our measures.

接下来，在八个月的样本期内，我们没有观察到复杂使用的明显改进。 虽然我们发现员工完成正式人工智能培训的当月其复杂程度有所提高，但在接下来的几个月中我们并没有发现其复杂程度有所提高。 虽然我们的证据并未证明培训无效，但几乎没有证据表明完成正式培训会对观察到的行为产生持久的变化。 时间和培训结果共同体现了组织在复杂的员工 genAI 使用方面进行持续变革时可能面临的困难。Next, we do not observe clear improvements in sophisticated use over the eight-month sample period. While we find increased sophistication in the month when an employee completes formal AI training, we do not find increased sophistication in the months that follow. While our evidence does not establish that the training was ineffective, it provides little evidence that completion of formal training produces lasting changes in observed behavior. Together, the time and training results convey the difficulty organizations may face in producing sustained changes in sophisticated employee genAI use.

最后，我们发现更长的初始提示和更多的对话迭代伴随着更复杂的使用。 在我们的分析中，这两种行为与复杂使用的关系比与使用频率的关系更一致。 虽然当无法获得详细的基于记录的衡量标准时，这些相对容易跟踪的行为可能会提供有用的信号，但管理者不应将这三种行为中的任何一种解释为绩效的直接衡量标准。Finally, we find that longer initial prompts and more iteration within conversations accompany more sophisticated use. In our analyses, these two behaviors are more consistently associated with sophisticated use than usage frequency. While these relatively easy-to-track behaviors may provide useful signals when detailed transcript-based measures are unavailable, managers should not interpret any of these three behaviors as direct measures of performance.

我们的分析是描述性的，并且具有重要的局限性。 虽然我们的综合衡量指标代表了复杂的使用，但我们无法确定法学硕士的输出是否满足用户的目标。 此外，我们无法直接衡量输出质量或 genAI 对生产力的影响。 接下来，自我们的窗口期结束以来，该领域继续快速发展。 模型开发已经改变了提示的最佳实践，人工智能代理的进步可能改变了 genAI 在编码等用例中的潜在效用。 因此，本文中报告的特定行为的使用率和流行率可能已经与当前的做法有所不同。Our analysis is descriptive and has important limitations. While our composite measures proxy for sophisticated use, we are not able to determine whether the LLM output satisfied the user’s goals. Further, we are not able to directly measure output quality or genAI’s influence on productivity. Next, in the time since our window concluded, the field has continued its rapid advance. Model developments have shifted best practices for prompting, and advancements in AI agents have likely changed the potential utility of genAI for use cases such as coding. Consequently, the rates of use and prevalence of particular behaviors reported in this paper may already differ from current practice.

尽管存在这些限制，但无论现有的人工智能技术如何，公司都可能从表现出良好人工智能行为和习惯的员工中受益。 此外，记录某个时间点的证据对于在不断变化的领域创建参考点和历史基准是必要的。 此外，虽然论文的描述性设计没有提供因果推断，但它可以激发研究设计提供因果推断。 对于这些观点，未来的研究将有必要了解 genAI 进入工作场所及其对工作场所的影响。Despite these limitations, companies are likely to benefit from employees who exhibit good AI behaviors and habits regardless of the existing AI technology. In addition, documenting evidence at a point in time is necessary to create reference points and historical benchmarks in a field that will continue to change. Further, while the paper’s descriptive design does not offer causal inference, it can motivate research designs that will. For these points, future research will be necessary to build knowledge of genAI’s entry into, and its effects on, the workplace.
