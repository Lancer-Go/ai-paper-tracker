了解数据时态对大型语言模型预训练的影响Understanding Data Temporality Impact on Large Language Models Pre-training

Romain Fabre    Hippolyte Pilchen    Franck Signe Talla    Patrick Perez    Edouard Grave
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）通常在打乱的语料库上进行训练，产生的模型的知识在训练时被冻结，并且其时间基础仍然知之甚少。在这项工作中，我们研究了预训练动态对获取时间敏感的事实知识的影响，特别关注数据排序。我们的主要贡献是双重的。首先，我们引入了超过 7,000 个基于时间的问题的综合基准和评估协议，可以分析模型是否正确地将事实与其相应的时间段关联起来。其次，我们在时间排序的 Common Crawl 快照上预训练 6B 参数模型，并将它们与标准的打乱预训练进行比较。我们的结果表明，顺序训练的模型与一般语言理解和常识的打乱基线相匹配，同时始终表现出更新和时间精确的知识。临时排序的预训练产量提高了事实的新鲜度，同时对旧数据的预训练峰值进行了改组，这可能是由于事实重复的增加。这些发现以及我们代码的发布，Large language models (LLMs) are typically trained on shuffled corpora, yielding models whose knowledge is frozen at train time and whose temporal grounding remains poorly understood. In this work, we study the impact of pre-training dynamics on the acquisition of time-sensitive factual knowledge, focusing specifically on data ordering. Our main contributions are twofold. First, we introduce a comprehensive benchmark of over 7,000 temporally grounded questions and an evaluation protocol that enables analysis of whether models correctly associate facts with their corresponding time periods. Second, we pretrain 6B-parameter models on temporally ordered Common Crawl snapshots and compare them against standard shuffled pre-training. Our results show that sequentially trained models match shuffled baselines on general language understanding and common knowledge while consistently exhibiting more up-to-date and temporally precise knowledge. Temporally ordered pre-training yields improved factual freshness, while shuffled pre-training peaks on older data, possibly due to increased factual repetition. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的训练通常被组织为多阶段管道，包括预训练、训练中期和训练后。在预训练期间，模型接受大量数据的训练The training of large language models (LLMs) is commonly organized as a multi-stage pipeline comprising pre-training, mid-training, and post-training. During pre-training, the model is trained on vast amounts of data(Brown et al., 2020)，通常由网页、科学 PDF 文档和代码存储库组成。此阶段为最终存储在模型参数中的知识奠定了基础。, usually made of webpages, scientific PDF documents and code repositories. This stage lays the foundation for the knowledge ultimately stored in the model’s parameters.

在实践中，预训练语料库是根据多个 Common Crawl (CC) 快照的过滤版本构建的In practice, pre-training corpora are constructed from filtered versions of multiple Common Crawl (CC) snapshots(Penedo et al., 2024)。尽管用于构建这些数据集的过滤方法在各个作品中相对一致，但结合质量过滤. Although the filtering recipes used to build these datasets are relatively consistent across works, combining quality filtering(Touvron et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024)具有重复数据删除功能with deduplication(Lee et al., 2022)，预训练期间数据的排序仍有待探索。 然而，数据排序可能在塑造模型参数中编码的事实知识方面发挥重要作用。当前训练流程的一个众所周知的限制是，一旦训练完成，模型的知识就会被有效冻结：法学硕士无法可靠地回答有关训练数据时间截止后发生的事件的问题。此外，模型对于针对截止日期前几年日期的问题通常比针对截止日期附近的问题更准确, the ordering of data during pre-training remains under-explored. Yet, data ordering may play an important role in shaping the factual knowledge encoded in model parameters. A well-known limitation of current training pipelines is that a model’s knowledge becomes effectively frozen once training is complete: LLMs cannot reliably answer questions about events occurring after the temporal cutoff of their training data. Moreover, models are often more accurate on questions targeting dates several years before the cutoff than on those near the cutoff itself(Zhao et al., 2024)，揭示了训练数据集范围和模型有效知识范围之间的差距。虽然最近的工作探索了持续学习以融入新的事实, revealing a gap between the training dataset horizon and the model’s effective knowledge horizon. While recent work has explored continual learning for incorporating new facts(Li et al., 2025)以及改善时间对准的方法and methods for improving temporal alignment(Park et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2024)，预训练动态（特别是数据采样顺序）对知识时间分布的影响仍然知之甚少。, the impact of pre-training dynamics—particularly data sampling order—on the temporal distribution of knowledge remains poorly understood.

2.3 顺序模型Sequential Model

在顺序实验中，我们保留基线架构和超参数，但按严格的时间顺序处理快照。顺序课程从 2018 年初延伸到 2025 年。虽然基线隐含地遇到了历史内容，但顺序模型明确利用 2018-2019 年期间在其原始时间背景下建立初始语言能力和世界知识。这使得模型能够在适应后续快照中出现的分布变化之前稳定其对“2020 年前”世界的表示。In the sequential experiment, we retain the baseline architecture and hyperparameters but process snapshots in strict chronological order. The sequential curriculum extends from early 2018 through 2025. While the baseline encounters historical content implicitly, the sequential model explicitly utilizes the 2018–2019 period to establish initial linguistic capabilities and world knowledge in their original temporal context. This allows the model to stabilize its representation of the “pre-2020” world before adapting to the distribution shifts present in later snapshots.

7 观点与结论Perspectives & Conclusions

我们的研究表明，整理预训练数据的标准做法是现代法学硕士中观察到的“知识视野”差距的主要驱动因素。通过引入 KairosQA（一种通过严格过滤维基数据来隔离时间意识的基准），我们表明顺序预训练允许模型实现“新近度峰值”，在当代事实上超越更大的模型。虽然打乱的基线由于过分优先考虑网络爬行中重复的历史数据而受到时间对齐惯性的影响，但我们的顺序方法成功地引入了事实新鲜度所需的新近度偏差。Our research demonstrates that the standard practice of shuffling pre-training data is a primary driver of the “knowledge horizon” gap observed in modern LLMs. By introducing KairosQA, a benchmark that isolates temporal awareness through rigorous filtering of Wikidata, we show that sequential pre-training allows models to achieve a “recency peak” that surpasses larger models on contemporary facts. While shuffled baselines suffer from temporal alignment inertia by over-prioritizing historical data repeated across web crawls, our sequential approach successfully induces the recency bias necessary for factual freshness.

然而，使用顺序预训练时的主要权衡是，当模型适应新的时间分布时，旧知识的相对遗忘。尽管总代币数量在一定程度上缓解了这一问题，但它强调了未来对时间感知预训练架构的需求，该架构可以显式地对数据的时间起源进行建模，以跟踪不断变化的事实而不擦除历史记录。在附录中However, the primary trade-off when using sequential pre-training is the relative forgetting of older knowledge as models adapt to newer temporal distributions. Although total token volume partially mitigates this, it highlights the need for future time-aware pre-training architectures that explicitly model the temporal origin of data to track evolving facts without erasing history. In AppendixA.3，我们提供了初步实验，强调了这一挑战的固有难度。通过开源我们的检查点和数据集，我们为持续学习和无缝增量更新的研究提供了基础，证明时间数据排序是开发真正最新的基础语言模型的一个基本的、尚未充分探索的因素。, we provide preliminary experiments that highlight the inherent difficulty of this challenge. By open-sourcing our checkpoints and dataset, we provide a foundation for research into continual learning and seamless incremental updates, demonstrating that temporal data ordering is a fundamental, under-explored factor in developing truly up-to-date base language models.

C.3 KairosQA 生成模板KairosQA generation templates

实验结果表明，主体或关系的模糊表述经常会导致生成的问题不精确。虽然这些生成错误在关系的特定子集中更为普遍，但根据主题上下文，它们也可能在其他关系中偶尔发生。为了缓解这个问题，我们确定最可靠的方法是为每个关系手动预定义特定的问题模板。这确保了问题结构合理并以正确答案为目标。随后，我们利用 GPT-4o mini 重新表述特定关系的问题，使模型能够访问特定主题以保持上下文准确性。为了确保数据集支持问题类型的多样化分布，并专门针对时间评估进行优化，我们指示法学硕士包含目标年份的指定占位符。这种结构设计确保每个重新表述的问题本质上都是“时间感知的”，从而能够精确获取与特定时间点相关的知识。Experimental results indicate that ambiguous formulations of subjects or relations frequently result in imprecise generated questions. While these generation errors are more prevalent within specific subsets of relations, they can also occur sporadically across other relations depending on the subject context. To mitigate this, we determined that the most robust approach is to manually predefine a specific question template for each relation. This ensures the question remains structurally sound and targets the correct answer. Subsequently, we leverage GPT-4o mini to reformulate the question for the specific relation, providing the model with access to the specific subject to maintain contextual accuracy. To ensure the dataset supports a diverse distribution of question types and is specifically optimized for temporal evaluation, we instruct the LLM to include a designated placeholder for the target year. This structural design ensures that each reformulated question is intrinsically “time-aware”, allowing for the precise elicitation of knowledge associated with a specific point in time.

为了生成高质量的干扰项，我们为模型提供了在所有记录年份（2020 年后）内给定主题的全套有效答案的访问权限。然后，我们应用严格的过滤过程，以确保生成的干扰因素彼此不同，并且与真实答案不同。这种重复数据删除是通过结合 F1 分数阈值和模糊匹配指标的混合方法来实现的，以消除冗余候选者。To generate high-quality distractors, we provide the model with access to the full set of valid answers for a given subject across all recorded years (post-2020). We then apply a rigorous filtering process to ensure the generated distractors are distinct from one another and from the ground-truth answers. This deduplication is achieved through a hybrid approach combining F1-score thresholds and fuzzy matching metrics to eliminate redundant candidates.
