Trace2Tower：LLM 代理的多级技能的转换感知 EigenTrace 归纳Trace2Tower: Transition-Aware EigenTrace Induction of Multi-Level Skills for LLM Agents

Jiazheng Sun    Boyu Yang    Binhao Yuan    Mingxuan Li    Xin Peng
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型代理越来越依赖执行跟踪来掌握复杂的交互任务。然而，当前的范例受到浅层轨迹检索和平面技能总结的瓶颈，从根本上忽略了代理行为的时间依赖性和结果条件拓扑。我们引入了 Trace2Tower，这是一个转换感知的 EigenTrace 框架，可将原始轨迹提炼为强大的技能层次结构。 Trace2Tower 将步骤级交互抽象为规范事件，构建由语义兼容性、转换动态和结果证据控制的统一图。通过一种新颖的对比谱分解，它隔离了稳定的、与成功相关的行为模式，同时严格抑制容易失败的捷径。这些模式有机地填充了由动作模板、程序例程和总体任务策略组成的动态技能塔，并通过验证者引导的反馈不断完善。在 ALFWorld 上，Trace2Tower 仅需要 10.35 个步骤和 0.26 个无效动作，就取得了 87.31% 的成功率；在WebShop上，准确率达到50.67%。在这两个基准测试中，Trace2Tower 在任务掌握和上下文高效的体验重用方面都显着优于现有基准。Large language model agents increasingly rely on execution traces to master complex interactive tasks. However, current paradigms are bottlenecked by shallow trajectory retrieval and flat skill summarization, fundamentally ignoring the temporal dependencies and outcome-conditioned topology of agent behavior. We introduce Trace2Tower, a transition-aware EigenTrace framework that distills raw trajectories into a robust skill hierarchy. Trace2Tower abstracts step-level interactions into canonical events, constructing a unified graph governed by semantic compatibility, transition dynamics, and outcome evidence. Through a novel contrastive spectral decomposition, it isolates stable, success-aligned behavioral modes while rigorously suppressing failure-prone shortcuts. These modes organically populate a dynamic skill tower of action templates, procedural routines, and overarching task strategies, continuously refined via verifier-guided feedback. On ALFWorld, Trace2Tower achieves 87.31% success requiring only 10.35 steps and 0.26 invalid actions; on WebShop, it reaches 50.67% exact success. Across both benchmarks, Trace2Tower significantly outperforms existing baselines in task mastery and context-efficient experience reuse.

简介Introduction

人们越来越期望法学硕士代理人能够通过持续的互动来改进，而不是孤立地解决每项任务LLM agents are increasingly expected to improve through continued interaction rather than address each task in isolation(Song et al. 2024b; Wang et al. 2026b)。在交互式环境中，代理行为通过一系列观察、动作、状态变化和反馈信号来发展. In interactive environments, agent behavior develops through a sequence of observations, actions, state changes, and feedback signals(Hu et al. 2025)。因此，一项行动的价值不仅取决于其直接效果，还取决于它如何影响后来的决策和最终结果。历史轨迹保留了这个决策过程，并提供了预训练模型之外的丰富的行为知识来源. The value of an action therefore depends not only on its immediate effect, but also on how it shapes later decisions and the final outcome. Historical trajectories preserve this decision process and provide a rich source of behavioral knowledge beyond the pretrained model(Song et al. 2024a)。它们揭示了有效的行动模式、有用的中间状态、恢复程序以及支持成功执行的长期策略. They reveal effective action patterns, useful intermediate states, recovery procedures, and long-term strategies that support successful execution(Fu et al. 2024; Agashe et al. 2024)。然而，原始轨迹很难直接重用。它们通常冗长、冗余，并且与特定任务紧密相关. However, raw trajectories are difficult to reuse directly. They are often lengthy, redundant, and closely tied to specific tasks(Hu et al. 2025; Yang et al. 2025)。它们还可能包含不必要的探索和不成功的行为分支，不应随意重用. They may also contain unnecessary exploration and unsuccessful behavioral branches that should not be reused indiscriminately(Song et al. 2024b)。因此，核心挑战是将不同的执行轨迹转换为紧凑且可靠的行为结构，保留决策模式，同时消除特定于任务的噪音和与故障相关的捷径。. The central challenge is therefore to transform diverse execution traces into compact and reliable behavioral structures that retain decision patterns while removing task-specific noise and failure-related shortcuts.

现有研究主要通过经验重用和技能构建来应对这一挑战。基于经验的方法将交互保留为演示、记忆、指南或检索的经验，并使用它们来指导未来的执行Existing studies mainly address this challenge through experience reuse and skill construction. Experience-based methods preserve interactions as demonstrations, memories, guidelines, or retrieved experiences and use them to guide future execution(Fu et al. 2024; Agashe et al. 2024; Yang et al. 2025)。基于技能的方法进一步将积累的经验转化为技能库中组织的持久且可重用的能力. Skill-based methods further convert accumulated experience into persistent and reusable capabilities organized in skill libraries(Wang et al. 2026b)。这些方向表明历史交互可以提高任务成功率、适应性和执行效率. These directions have shown that historical interactions can improve task success, adaptation, and execution efficiency(Fu et al. 2024; Wang et al. 2026b)。然而，大多数现有的表述仍然集中在个人经验、指导方针、计划或技能的内容或组织上. Nevertheless, most existing representations remain centered on the content or organization of individual experiences, guidelines, plans, or skills(Fu et al. 2024; Yang et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2026b)。它们主要关注应保留和重用哪些信息，而这些单元之间的行为关系仍然是隐含的。具有相似描述的事件在执行过程中可能具有不同的功能，而具有不同表面形式的事件可能支持相同的过程. They focus primarily on what information should be retained and reused, while behavioral relations among these units remain implicit. Events with similar descriptions may serve different functions during execution, whereas events with different surface forms may support the same procedure(Pysklo et al. 2026)。这种表示没有明确区分可靠的执行转换和与失败相关的捷径，也没有解释局部行为如何组成与成功一致的程序和任务级策略. Such representations do not explicitly distinguish reliable execution transitions from failure-related shortcuts or explain how local behaviors compose into success-aligned procedures and task-level strategies(Yu et al. 2026).

这些限制逐渐将研究从完整轨迹的重用转向构建更通用和持久的技能。 ExpeL 从积累的经验中提取可转移的见解，并将其与相关演示相结合以支持未来的任务These limitations have gradually shifted research from the reuse of complete trajectories toward the construction of more general and persistent skills. ExpeL extracts transferable insights from accumulated experience and combines them with relevant demonstrations to support future tasks(Zhao et al. 2024)。 SkillX 在多个抽象级别组织可重用的功能，以实现长期代理学习. SkillX organizes reusable capabilities at multiple levels of abstraction for long-term agent learning(Wang et al. 2026a)。 Trace2Skill 进一步使用成功和失败的执行来提取轨迹级课程并将其整合到可重用的技能目录中. Trace2Skill further uses both successful and failed executions to extract trajectory-level lessons and consolidate them into reusable skill directories(Ni et al. 2026)。从原始经验到结构化技能工件的进展如图所示. This progression from raw experience to structured skill artifacts is reflected in Figure1。尽管这些方法改善了经验的组织和重用，但支持技能形成的行为结构在很大程度上仍然是隐性的。它们没有明确识别与成功而非失败相关的稳定功能组、可靠的执行转换或重复模式。他们还为本地技能如何形成可重用程序和更高级别策略的建模提供有限的支持。这些限制激发了一种结构感知方法，该方法共同捕获语义关系、执行转换、任务结果和层次结构。. Although these methods improve the organization and reuse of experience, the behavioral structure that supports skill formation remains largely implicit. They do not explicitly identify stable functional groups, reliable execution transitions, or recurring patterns that are associated with success rather than failure. They also provide limited support for modeling how local skills form reusable procedures and higher-level strategies. These limitations motivate a structure-aware method that jointly captures semantic relations, execution transitions, task outcomes, and hierarchical composition.

为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 Trace2Tower，这是一个转换感知的 EigenTrace 框架，可将原始的 LLM 代理轨迹转换为结构化的多层次技能。 Trace2Tower 将执行抽象为规范事件，构建结果感知的转换图，并应用对比谱分解来发现与成功一致的行为模式。这些模式被组织成行动、程序和策略级别的技能塔，并由验证者引导细化。在 ALFWorld 和 WebShop 上的实验表明，Trace2Tower 实现了 87.31% 的任务成功率和 50.67% 的准确成功率，同时将 ALFWorld 执行步骤减少到 10.35，将无效操作减少到 0.26。 本文的主要贡献总结如下：To address this problem, we propose Trace2Tower, a transition-aware EigenTrace framework that transforms raw LLM agent trajectories into structured multi-level skills. Trace2Tower abstracts executions into canonical events, constructs an outcome-aware transition graph, and applies contrastive spectral decomposition to discover success-aligned behavioral modes. These modes are organized into an action-, procedure-, and strategy-level skill tower with verifier-guided refinement. Experiments on ALFWorld and WebShop show that Trace2Tower achieves 87.31% task success and 50.67% exact success, while reducing ALFWorld execution steps to 10.35 and invalid actions to 0.26. The main contributions of this paper are summarized as follows:

  • •

    我们提出了 Trace2Tower，这是一个框架，通过明确捕获超越传统技能提取和经验积累方法的行为关系、执行依赖性和结果信息，将 LLM 代理轨迹转化为结构化技能。We propose Trace2Tower, a framework that transforms LLM agent trajectories into structured skills by explicitly capturing behavioral relations, execution dependencies, and outcome information beyond conventional skill extraction and experience accumulation approaches.

  • •

  • •

相关工作Related Work

代理经验学习Agent Experience Learning

LLM 代理通过接口重用之前的交互。 ReAct 在动作循环中公开推理；反思将反馈转化为持续的指导； Voyager 存储可执行程序； ExpeL 将跨任务洞察与检索到的事件相结合； SEER 从成功的轨迹中检索阶梯级证据LLM agents reuse prior interactions through interfaces. ReAct exposes reasoning in the action loop; Reflexion converts feedback into persistent guidance; Voyager stores executable programs; ExpeL combines cross-task insights with retrieved episodes; and SEER retrieves step-level evidence from successful trajectories(Yao et al. 2022b; Shinn et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2023; Zhao et al. 2024; Cui et al. 2025)。记忆研究进一步表明，基于相似性的经验跟踪可能会传播错误或重播不一致的经验. Memory studies further show that similarity-based experience following can propagate errors or replay misaligned experiences(Xiong et al. 2025)。这些方法主要重用文本记忆、演示、轨迹或根据上下文相关性选择的工件。此类表示不会明确保留哪个转换使成功、哪个分支失败或后续操作之前的先决条件。相反，Trace2Tower 从轨迹中引入这些依赖性。. These methods mainly reuse textual memories, demonstrations, trajectories, or artifacts selected by contextual relevance. Such representations do not explicitly preserve which transition enabled success, which branch failed, or which prerequisites preceded later actions. Trace2Tower instead induces these dependencies from trajectories.

技能库建设Skill Library Construction

最近的工作为终身学习构建了持久存储库和结构化技能图。 SkillFlow 自动发现技能；基准评估任务流的获取和转移Recent work builds persistent repositories and structured skill graphs for lifelong learning. SkillFlow automates skill discovery; benchmarks assess acquisition and transfer over task streams(Zhang et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026a; Zheng et al. 2025); SkillX 细化分层技能; SkillX refines hierarchical skills(Wang et al. 2026a); SAGE 通过顺序交互积累技能; and SAGE accumulates skills through sequential interaction(Wang et al. 2026b)。技能图、SkillGraph 和 SkillDAG 进一步对技能间依赖关系进行建模，以进行检索和演化. Graph of Skills, SkillGraph, and SkillDAG further model inter-skill dependencies for retrieval and evolution(Liu et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026b; Bai et al. 2026)。这些系统改进了管理和选择，但结构很大程度上是在现有技能工件中定义的。它们没有解释重复执行事件如何形成程序，也没有解释为什么组合与成功相一致。相反，Trace2Tower 从语义、时间和结果关系中引入层次。. These systems improve curation and selection, but structure is largely defined among existing skill artifacts. They do not explain how recurring execution events form procedures or why compositions align with success. Trace2Tower instead induces levels from semantic, temporal, and outcome relations.

结构化技能入门Structured Skill Induction

Trace2Skill 将成功和失败的轨迹提取到可重用的技能目录中Trace2Skill distills successful and failed trajectories into reusable skill directories(Ni et al. 2026)。最近的方法还使技能关系变得明确：GraSP 将技能编译成依赖图以进行结构化执行，而 SkillGraph 从轨迹和反馈中演化出有向关系. Recent methods also make skill relations explicit: GraSP compiles skills into dependency graphs for structured execution, while SkillGraph evolves directed relations from trajectories and feedback(Xia et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026b)。这些方法组织技能级别结构，而 Trace2Tower 在可重用过程存在之前解决结构问题。它在规范事件上构建结果条件图，并在谱分解之前对比成功和失败的转换。因此，程序和策略继承了观察到的执行顺序和成功一致性，而不是强加于现有技能集合的关系。. These approaches organize skill-level structure, whereas Trace2Tower addresses structure before reusable procedures exist. It builds an outcome-conditioned graph over canonical events and contrasts successful and failed transitions before spectral decomposition. Procedures and strategies therefore inherit observed execution order and success alignment rather than relations imposed over an existing skill collection.

方法Method

Trace2Tower 通过事件抽象、转换感知 EigenTrace 构造和无参数对比谱分解，将累积的 LLM 代理轨迹提炼成强大的技能层次结构。提取的行为模式建立了行动、程序和策略级别的技能，这些技能通过分层检索和验证者反馈进行动态细化。Trace2Tower distills accumulated LLM agent trajectories into a robust skill hierarchy via event abstraction, transition-aware EigenTrace construction, and parameter-free contrastive spectral decomposition. The extracted behavioral modes establish skills at the action, procedure, and strategy levels, which are dynamically refined through hierarchical retrieval and verifier feedback.
