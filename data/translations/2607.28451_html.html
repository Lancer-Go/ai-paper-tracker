知道自己正在老化的机器：硬件感知自主智能框架Machines that know they are aging: a framework for hardware-aware autonomous intelligence

Cheng Siong Chin1,*, Jianhua Zhang2, Mohan Venkateshkumar3
1Faculty of Science, Agriculture, and Engineering, Newcastle University Singapore, Singapore 828608
2School of Information and Control Engineering, Qingdao University of Technology, Qingdao 266520, China
3Department of EEE, Amrita School of Engineering, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Chennai, India
*Corresponding author: cheng.chin@newcastle.ac.uk
摘要Abstract

自主系统不可避免地会老化，但它们的人工智能通常假设硬件保持原始状态。电池性能下降、传感器漂移、处理器累积计时误差以及内存可靠性下降，导致假设能力与实际能力之间的不匹配日益严重。这可能会导致不可知的崩溃，其中任务失败是由于累积的硬件退化而不是单个组件故障引起的。我们提出了老化感知自主智能（AAAI），这是一个将硬件健康状况直接集成到推理、规划和任务执行中的框架。 AAAI 建立在三个支柱之上：硬件自我意识，使用故障物理模型持续估计电源、传感、内存和计算子系统的运行状况；自适应推理，根据剩余硬件能力调整推理复杂度、规划范围和任务优先级；以生存为中心的智能，通过性能优化、资源节约和优雅降级，在整个任务目标中分配剩余使用寿命。 AAAI 没有引入新硬件，而是将预测、生命周期管理和硬件感知计算统一到闭环认知架构中。我们认为，这种集成对于在难以进入或安全关键环境中运行的自主系统至关重要，包括太空任务、海洋机器人和植入式医疗设备。通过使机器能够识别并响应自身的老化，AAAI 提高了弹性，延长了使用寿命，并支持更安全、更优雅地完成任务。Autonomous systems inevitably age, yet their artificial intelligence typically assumes hardware remains in its original condition. Batteries degrade, sensors drift, processors accumulate timing errors, and memory reliability declines, creating a growing mismatch between assumed and actual capability. This can lead to agnostic collapse, where mission failure arises from accumulated hardware degradation rather than a single component fault. We propose Aging-Aware Autonomous Intelligence (AAAI), a framework that integrates hardware health directly into reasoning, planning, and mission execution. AAAI is built on three pillars: hardware self-awareness, which continuously estimates the health of power, sensing, memory, and computation subsystems using physics-of-failure models; self-adaptive reasoning, which adjusts inference complexity, planning horizon, and task priorities according to remaining hardware capability; and survival-centric intelligence, which allocates remaining operational life across mission objectives through performance optimization, resource conservation, and graceful degradation. Rather than introducing new hardware, AAAI unifies prognostics, lifecycle management, and hardware-aware computing into a closed-loop cognitive architecture. We argue that such integration is essential for autonomous systems operating in inaccessible or safety-critical environments, including space missions, marine robotics, and implantable medical devices. By enabling machines to recognize and respond to their own aging, AAAI improves resilience, extends operational lifetime, and supports safer, more graceful mission completion.

简介Introduction

考虑一个自动水下航行器，它花了两年时间检查海底管道。它的电池只保留了原始容量的一小部分，它的深度传感器已经漂移，它的处理器由于连续计算而运行得很热。然而，软件仍然没有意识到，仍然像新系统一样规划任务，仍然尝试退化硬件无法再可靠执行的任务。这反映了自主系统设计方面的差距。硬件退化在电池电化学、传感器漂移和嵌入式处理器的热老化方面都有很好的特征，但许多已部署的系统并未将实时硬件状态集成到规划和决策中。其结果是假设能力与实际能力之间的不匹配日益加剧，直至以任务失败的形式出现。Consider an autonomous underwater vehicle that has spent two years inspecting subsea pipelines. Its battery retains only a fraction of its original capacity, its depth sensors have drifted, and its processor runs hot from continuous computation. Yet the software remains unaware, still planning missions as though the system were new, still attempting tasks that degraded hardware can no longer reliably perform. This reflects a gap in autonomous systems design. Hardware degradation is well-characterized across battery electrochemistry, sensor drift, and thermal aging in embedded processors, yet many deployed systems do not integrate real-time hardware state into planning and decision-making. The result is a growing mismatch between assumed and actual capability that compounds until it surfaces as mission failure.

合同没人看The contract nobody reads

现代人工智能系统隐含地假设了稳定的硬件：传感器忠实地测量世界，电池提供额定能量，处理器执行没有错误，内存保留其存储的内容。这一假设在短期内相当有效，但随着部署时间的延长，它变得越来越不可靠，而且底层物理原理几十年来已经得到充分记录。锂离子电池在每个充电周期都会经历不可逆的电化学变化，在电极表面沉积微观薄膜，通过众所周知的阿伦尼乌斯活化动力学逐渐降低容量Modern artificial intelligence systems implicitly assume stable hardware: that sensors faithfully measure the world, that batteries deliver their rated energy, that processors execute without error, and that memory retains what it stores. This assumption holds reasonably well in the short term but becomes increasingly unreliable over longer deployment timescales, and the underlying physics has been well-documented for decades. Lithium-ion batteries undergo irreversible electrochemical change with each charge cycle, depositing microscopic films on electrode surfaces that gradually reduce capacity through well-understood Arrhenius activation kinetics1。硅处理器在持续电压应力下性能下降，慢慢侵蚀晶体管时序裕度. Silicon processors degrade under sustained voltage stress, slowly eroding transistor timing margins2。存储单元累积辐射引起的位错误的速度随着温度和时间的推移而增加. Memory cells accumulate radiation-induced bit errors at rates that rise with temperature and time3。航天器上的太阳能电池板在运行的头十年内因辐射损伤损失了大约百分之二十到四十的输出. Solar panels on spacecraft lose approximately twenty to forty percent of their output to radiation damage within the first decade of operation4。这些不是极端情况。它们是已部署硬件的预期物理轨迹，而没有考虑到它们的人工智能系统在设计上与它们所依赖的底层越来越不一致。. These are not edge cases. They are the expected physical trajectory of deployed hardware, and AI systems that do not account for them are, by design, increasingly misaligned with the substrate on which they depend.

材料科学家和可靠性工程师非常详细地记录了所有这一切。很大程度上尚未解决的是这些知识与自治系统智能层的系统连接。很少有人工智能框架系统地提出这样的问题：鉴于硬件正在以这些特定的、可测量的方式恶化，智能层应该如何进行不同的推理？我们制造的机器会老化。核心的工程问题是智能层是否可以设计成包含这些知识。Materials scientists and reliability engineers have documented all of this in extraordinary detail. What remains largely unaddressed is the systematic connection of this knowledge to the intelligence layer of autonomous systems. Few AI frameworks have systematically asked: given that hardware is deteriorating in these specific, measurable ways, how should the intelligence layer reason differently? The machines we build will age. The central engineering question is whether the intelligence layer can be designed to incorporate that knowledge.

无人计划的崩溃The collapse nobody planned

我们将这种故障模式称为不可知崩溃。它并不表现为离散的故障事件，而是表现为硬件功能的逐渐漂移，跨越性能阈值而不触发传统诊断。硬件能力会悄无声息地下降，直到低于人工智能始终假定的阈值，并且任务性能会在没有警告的情况下下降。在海洋自主车辆的模拟中，该模式是一致的：与硬件无关的系统在长期任务的早期保持强大的任务性能，然后随着电池容量和传感器精度因正常磨损而下降而稳步恶化。硬件没有出现故障。它已经过了与硬件无关的控制系统可以继续有效运行的阶段。We term this failure mode agnostic collapse. It does not manifest as a discrete fault event, but rather as a gradual drift in hardware capability that crosses a performance threshold without triggering conventional diagnostics. Hardware capabilities decline silently until they fall below the thresholds the AI has always assumed, and mission performance degrades without warning. In simulations of a marine autonomous vehicle, the pattern is consistent: a hardware-agnostic system maintains strong task performance early in a long mission, then deteriorates steadily as battery capacity and sensor accuracy each decline through ordinary wear. The hardware did not fail. It aged past the point where a hardware-agnostic control system could continue to operate effectively.

两种故障模式会产生这种结果。首先，系统继续尝试计算要求较高的操作，包括深度学习推理、远程规划和高分辨率传感，其缩小的硬件无法再可靠地执行。在第二种情况下，它矫枉过正，在必要之前就默认为保守的安全模式，并浪费了可用的剩余容量。两者都源于相同的根本缺失：智能层没有推理其自身身体状况的机制。Two failure modes produce this outcome. In the first, the system continues attempting computationally demanding operations, including deep-learning inference, long-range planning, and high-resolution sensing, that its diminished hardware can no longer execute reliably. In the second, it overcorrects, defaulting to conservative safe mode well before necessary and squandering usable residual capacity. Both stem from the same underlying absence: the intelligence layer has no mechanism for reasoning about its own physical condition.

现有方法仅提供部分补救措施。预测工具可以预测组件的寿命终止Existing approaches offer only partial remedies. Prognostics tools can forecast component end-of-life5但知道电池容量几乎耗尽并不能告知系统在此期间应如何推理或计划。硬件冗余可以掩盖单个组件的故障but knowing that battery capacity is nearly exhausted does not inform how the system should reason or plan in the interim. Hardware redundancy can mask individual component failures6，但在受限平台上实施是有限且成本高昂的。所缺少的是对身体衰老的认知反应：随着硬件变化而修改其推理和计划的智能层。, but it is finite and costly to implement on constrained platforms. What has been missing is a cognitive response to physical ageing: an intelligence layer that modifies its reasoning and planning as its hardware changes.

现有方法提供了什么以及它们的停止点What existing approaches offer and where they stop

基于人工智能的设备健康监测的最新工作解决了这个问题的各个方面，但仍然停留在硬件管理层面，感知退化，调整资源分配，并保护组件免受进一步磨损。认知适应仍然不存在，其中系统主动推理其身体状况并相应地修改计划和优先级。下面将现有研究映射到它最密切关注的支柱，明确以前的工作结束和 AAAI 开始的地方。Recent work in AI-based device health monitoring addresses aspects of this problem but remains at the hardware management level, sensing degradation, adjusting resource allocation, and protecting components from further wear. Cognitive adaptation, in which the system actively reasons about its physical condition and modifies planning and prioritisation accordingly, remains absent. The following maps existing research to the pillar it most closely addresses, making explicit where prior work ends and where AAAI begins.

第一支柱：硬件自我意识Pillar 1: Hardware Self-Awareness

失效物理模型提供了理论基础Physics-of-failure modelling provides the theoretical foundation7。片上传感器现在可以检测早期的时间依赖性电介质击穿，商业人工智能平台包含能够在设备上学习以响应测量的硬件状态的自适应代理. On-chip sensors now detect early-stage Time-Dependent Dielectric Breakdown, and commercial AI platforms incorporate adaptive agents capable of on-device learning in response to measured hardware state8。负偏压温度不稳定性跟踪提供生产硬件中晶体管老化的实时指标. Negative Bias Temperature Instability tracking provides a real-time index of transistor ageing in production hardware9,10。这些贡献没有解决从监控到思考的步骤：健康信号到达工程师或调整本地设置，但它们不会改变系统的推理方式、选择做什么或分配剩余容量的方式。缩小这一差距是 AAAI 的首要职能。. What these contributions do not address is the step from monitoring to thinking: health signals reach engineers or trim local settings, but they do not change how the system reasons, what it chooses to do, or how it distributes its remaining capacity. Bridging that gap is the first function of AAAI.

支柱 2：自主生命周期管理Pillar 2: Autonomous Lifecycle Management

现有算法自动平衡活动和空闲硬件组件，以管理性能和寿命Existing algorithms autonomously balance active and idle hardware components to manage performance against longevity11,12。这些方法在已部署的系统中在技术上是可行的，但在资源分配层运行，无需将硬件状态连接到更高级别的推理或任务规划。. These approaches are technically feasible in deployed systems but operate at the resource-allocation layer without connecting hardware state to higher-level reasoning or mission planning.

支柱 3：主动延长寿命Pillar 3: Proactive Life Extension

Ageing-aware voltage scaling applies this logic at the circuit level, slowing transistor wear without triggering functional failure9。每个层都针对一个不同的层，而不需要跨层进行集成。. Each addresses a distinct layer without integrating across them.

支柱 4：跨层协同设计Pillar 4: Cross-Layer Co-Design

HAWQ-V214执行分层敏感性分析，为量化脆弱层分配更高的数值精度，同时压缩更有弹性的层。硬件协同设计工作流程在软件和硬件规格之间迭代，直到达到共同可行的最佳方案。灵敏度引导的任务路由将要求苛刻的操作引导至具有最大剩余可靠性余量的芯片区域performs layer-wise sensitivity analysis, allocating higher numerical precision to quantisation-vulnerable layers while compressing more resilient ones. Hardware co-design workflows iterate between software and hardware specifications until a jointly feasible optimum is reached. Sensitivity-guided task routing directs demanding operations toward chip regions with the greatest remaining reliability margin15。这些方法共同缺乏的是硬件健康状况和系统推理之间的运行时耦合。协同设计在构建时修复了软件和硬件之间的关系，但并不使已部署的系统能够随着其物理能力的减弱而进行不同的思考或计划。. What these approaches collectively lack is runtime coupling between hardware health and system reasoning. Co-design fixes the relationship between software and hardware at build time, but does not equip a deployed system to think or plan differently as its physical capabilities diminish.

认知差距The cognitive gap

现有的研究涉及硬件健康状况监控和模型复杂性匹配，但缺乏认知集成：一种统一的架构，其中连续的多子系统健康状况估计主动地实时塑造推理、规划和任务策略。据我们所知，尚未报道集成所有四个层并明确将硬件健康决策与更高级别任务目标联系起来的系统。 AAAI 的贡献在于将这些现有组件连接成一个连贯的闭环认知系统的架构设计。Existing research addresses hardware health monitoring and model-complexity matching but stops short of cognitive integration: a unified architecture in which continuous multi-subsystem health estimation actively shapes reasoning, planning, and mission strategy in real time. To the best of our knowledge, a system that integrates all four layers and explicitly links hardware health decisions to higher-level mission objectives has not been reported. The contribution of AAAI lies in the architectural design that connects these existing components into a coherent, closed-loop cognitive system.

三种优雅变老的方法Three ways to grow old gracefully

老化感知自主智能（AAAI）的第一个支柱是真正的硬件自我知识（图 1）。The first pillar of Aging-Aware Autonomous Intelligence (AAAI) is genuine hardware self-knowledge (Fig.1）。我们将每个关键子系统（即：功率、传感、内存和计算）的状况表示为 1（完全健康）和 0（失败）之间的数字，并使用材料科学家用于预测退化的相同故障物理模型不断更新). We represent the condition of each critical subsystem, namely: power, sensing, memory, and computation, as a number between one (fully healthy) and zero (failed), continuously updated using the same physics-of-failure models that materials scientists apply to predict degradation7。 AAAI 将上面概述的先前工作的四个部分集成并扩展为一个统一的认知架构，该架构将物理硬件状态与自适应推理、任务优先级和任务规划连接起来。. AAAI integrates and extends the four strands of prior work surveyed above into a unified cognitive architecture that connects physical hardware state to adaptive reasoning, task prioritisation, and mission planning.

跨子系统的健康指标不会独立发展。环境压力经常同时影响多个组件：温度升高会加速电池退化，同时降低处理器可靠性，而盐暴露会损坏传感器和周围电路等。将这些耦合的退化动态作为一个互连的过程进行监控，而不是单独跟踪组件，可以在任何单个组件达到关键阈值之前及早识别新出现的平台范围故障。Health indicators across subsystems do not evolve independently. Environmental stresses frequently affect multiple components simultaneously: elevated temperatures accelerate battery degradation while reducing processor reliability, and salt exposure damages sensors and surrounding circuitry alike. Monitoring these coupled degradation dynamics as an interconnected process, rather than tracking components in isolation, enables earlier identification of emerging platform-wide failures before any individual component reaches a critical threshold.

在你的限度内思考Think within your limits

硬件自我意识只能带来有限的自主操作效益，除非它主动通知系统行为。 AAAI 的第二个支柱提供了一个推理层，可根据可用硬件容量动态调整任务范围和复杂性。在名义计算和内存条件下，AAAI 部署具有扩展规划范围的高容量深度学习模型。随着测量的硬件容量下降，系统会按比例做出响应：替换轻量级推理架构、收缩其规划范围、重新排序其任务队列，以优先考虑具有最高效用成本比的操作。这种适应不是作为离散模式转换实现的，而是作为实时跟踪退化硬件包络的连续比例控制响应来实现的Hardware self-awareness confers limited autonomous operational benefit unless it actively informs system behaviour. The second pillar of AAAI provides a reasoning layer that dynamically modulates task scope and complexity as a function of available hardware capacity. Under nominal computational and memory conditions, AAAI deploys high-capacity deep learning models with extended planning horizons. As measured hardware capacity declines, the system responds proportionally: substituting lightweight inference architectures, contracting its planning horizon, and reordering its task queue to prioritise operations with the highest utility-to-cost ratio. This adaptation is not implemented as a discrete mode transition but as a continuous, proportional control response that tracks the degrading hardware envelope in real time16。在与硬件无关的系统将经历功能崩溃的退化阈值时，AAAI 通过动态调整计算需求与剩余物理容量来保持操作连续性。. At degradation thresholds where a hardware-agnostic system would undergo functional collapse, AAAI preserves operational continuity by dynamically aligning computational demand with remaining physical capacity.

有目标地生存Survive with purpose

第三个支柱将任务目标延伸到包括对剩余业务能力的积极管理。 AAAI 并未将运行寿命视为硬件耐用性的被动结果，而是将其视为在竞争任务优先级之间明确分配的资源，其目标是在剩余部署期间最大化任务效用。该框架转化为三种自适应操作状态。在健康状况下，系统可以正常运行并满负荷运行。随着退化的出现，它会转变为保护阶段，减少资源消耗，同时保留关键任务功能。在最后阶段，系统根据预先计算的优先级策略逐步关闭非必要子系统，将行为限制在最小的操作核心，并降低通信频率以最大化剩余操作持续时间。这是最字面意义上的优雅退化：不是崩溃，而是有计划的撤退，将有用的功能保留到最后一刻The third pillar extends the mission objective to include active management of remaining operational capacity. Rather than treating operational lifespan as a passive consequence of hardware endurance, AAAI treats it as a resource to be explicitly allocated across competing mission priorities, with the objective of maximising mission utility over the remaining deployment period. This framework translates into three adaptive operational states. Under healthy conditions, the system operates normally with full capability. As degradation emerges, it transitions into a conservation phase that reduces resource consumption while preserving mission-critical functions. In the final stage, the system progressively shuts down non-essential subsystems according to a precomputed prioritisation strategy, restricts behaviour to a minimal operational core, and reduces communication frequency to maximise remaining operational duration. This is graceful degradation in the most literal sense: not a collapse, but a planned withdrawal that preserves useful function to the very last moment17.

这在实践中意味着什么What this means in practice

在近地轨道上，微型卫星不断暴露于热循环和电离辐射下，这两种情况都会逐渐降低处理器、内存和电源子系统的性能。老化感知架构可以相应地调整其操作配置文件，例如通过在热应力轨道阶段减少处理器活动，这些调整可能会在较长的部署期内累积延长功能寿命。海上机器人和自主海洋系统也面临着类似的挑战，其中腐蚀、静水压力和机械振动逐渐损害结构和电子完整性。传统系统通常仅在性能下降变得严重之后才检测到故障。持续模拟组件健康状况的智能层可以主动重新分配资源，减少运营压力，并在恶化的条件下保留基本功能。In low Earth orbit, microsatellites are continuously exposed to thermal cycling and ionising radiation, both of which progressively degrade processors, memory, and power subsystems. An ageing-aware architecture can adapt its operational profile in response, for example by reducing processor activity during thermally stressful orbital phases, adjustments that may cumulatively extend functional lifetime over longer deployment periods. Comparable challenges arise in offshore robotics and autonomous marine systems, where corrosion, hydrostatic pressure, and mechanical vibration gradually compromise structural and electronic integrity. Conventional systems typically detect failure only after performance degradation has become critical. An intelligence layer that continuously models component health can instead proactively reallocate resources, reduce operational strain, and preserve essential functionality under deteriorating conditions.

这种能力对于心脏起搏器等植入式医疗设备尤其重要，其中电池或组件过早失效可能需要手术干预，而不是日常维护。因此，老化意识不仅代表了延长运行耐久性的工程策略，而且还代表了提高生命关键应用的安全性和可靠性的机制。This capability is particularly consequential in implantable medical devices such as cardiac pacemakers, where premature battery or component failure may require surgical intervention rather than routine maintenance. Ageing-awareness therefore represents not only an engineering strategy for extending operational endurance, but also a mechanism for improving safety and reliability in life-critical applications.

将这些领域联系在一起的是一个共同的疏忽的共同弱点：假设能力保持不变，直到它失败的那一刻。解决这个问题不需要新材料、更快的处理器或额外的电源。它主要要求智能层按照材料科学家长期以来建立的理论行事：硬件老化可预测，并且可预测性应该驱动行为。What unites these domains is a shared vulnerability to a common oversight: the assumption that capability remains constant until the moment it fails. Addressing this requires no new materials, faster processors, or additional power sources. It requires primarily that the intelligence layer act on what materials scientists have long established: hardware ages predictably, and that predictability should drive behaviour.

老龄化意识还带来了一类风险，必须在其潜在好处的同时予以承认。有权根据推断的硬件状态调整自己的决策的自治系统，在没有足够的约束的情况下，可能会做出设计者既没有预料到也没有授权的相应操作选择。这种担忧并非理论上的。涉及商业自动化系统的事件，包括航空中的模式混乱事件和早期自动驾驶汽车部署中暴露的局限性，表明假设和实际操作条件之间的微小差异如何传播为安全关键的结果。在 AAAI 的背景下，操作模式之间的转换，特别是向优雅退出的转变，必须受到明确的、可审计的标准的约束，并尽可能接受人工监督。该框架的目的并不是要取代高风险决策中的人类判断，而是通过显示操作员无法获得的硬件状态信息来告知人类判断。严格的安全架构，包括模式转换条件的正式规范、故障安全默认值和人在环确认阈值，是在医疗设备或载人航天器等安全关键环境中部署的重要先决条件。Ageing-awareness also introduces a category of risk that must be acknowledged alongside its potential benefits. Autonomous systems empowered to adjust their own decision-making in response to inferred hardware state may, without sufficient constraints, make consequential operational choices that were neither anticipated nor authorised by their designers. This concern is not theoretical. Incidents involving commercial automation systems, including mode-confusion events in aviation and the limitations exposed in early autonomous vehicle deployments, demonstrate how small discrepancies between assumed and actual operating conditions can propagate into safety-critical outcomes. In the context of AAAI, transitions between operational modes, particularly the shift to graceful withdrawal, must be governed by explicit, auditable criteria and remain subject to human oversight wherever possible. The framework is not intended to replace human judgement in high-stakes decisions, but to inform it by surfacing hardware state information that would otherwise be unavailable to operators. A rigorous safety architecture, encompassing formal specification of mode-transition conditions, fail-safe defaults, and human-in-the-loop confirmation thresholds, is an essential precondition for deployment in safety-critical contexts such as medical devices or crewed spacecraft.

结论Conclusion

自主系统越来越多地部署在维护或更换困难或不可能的环境中，但大多数当代人工智能架构将计算视为独立于其运行的物理硬件。随着系统进入从轨道基础设施、海底机器人到植入式医疗设备等安全关键且持续时间较长的环境，这种假设变得越来越不可靠。本评论的中心论点是，智能不能再与支撑它的平台的物质条件分开。硬件退化不是一个特殊事件，而是一个持续的物理过程，受材料老化、电化学、热应力和辐射损伤等既定原理的控制。描述和预测这些过程的科学能​​力是存在的。很大程度上缺乏能够将这些知识实时整合到运营决策中的人工智能框架。具有老化感知能力的人工智能通过持续监控组件健康状况、对跨子系统的相关退化进行建模以及根据身体能力下降来调整行为来解决这一差距。在此基础上设计的系统可以延长使用寿命、提高弹性并降低意外任务失败的可能性。更广泛地说，它们代表了自主性概念的转变：不是独立于其物理基础的计算，而是受身体衰老这一不可逆转现实的限制和响应的体现智能。仍然存在一些关键的限制。故障物理模型的准确性随部署条件的不同而变化，耦合退化效应很难提前详尽地描述，并且连续健康监控的计算开销本身必须考虑到硬件容量的下降。Autonomous systems are increasingly deployed in environments where maintenance or replacement is difficult or impossible, yet most contemporary AI architectures treat computation as independent of the physical hardware on which it runs. This assumption becomes increasingly unreliable as systems enter safety-critical and long-duration settings ranging from orbital infrastructure and subsea robotics to implantable medical devices. The central argument of this Comment is that intelligence can no longer be separated from the material condition of the platform that sustains it. Hardware degradation is not an exceptional event, but a continuous physical process governed by well-established principles of materials ageing, electrochemistry, thermal stress, and radiation damage. The scientific capacity to characterise and predict these processes exists. What is largely absent is an AI framework capable of integrating this knowledge into operational decision-making in real time. Ageing-aware artificial intelligence addresses this gap by continuously monitoring component health, modelling correlated degradation across subsystems, and adapting behaviour in response to declining physical capacity. Systems designed on this basis may extend operational lifetime, improve resilience, and reduce the probability of unplanned mission failure. More broadly, they represent a shift in how autonomy is conceived: not as computation independent of its physical substrate, but as embodied intelligence that is constrained by, and responsive to, the irreversible realities of physical ageing. Several critical limitations remain. The accuracy of physics-of-failure models varies with deployment conditions, coupled degradation effects are difficult to characterise exhaustively in advance, and the computational overhead of continuous health monitoring must itself be accounted for against declining hardware capacity.

开发这些能力需要材料科学、可靠性工程、控制理论、嵌入式系统和人工智能的紧密结合。基于人工智能的现场设备健康监测的最新进展表明，这种集成的要素已经出现； AAAI 的贡献在于将这些线索统一在一个连贯的认知框架内，将物理状态与适应性推理联系起来。建立跨学科基础可能被证明是下一代自主技术的决定性挑战之一。Developing these capabilities will require close integration across materials science, reliability engineering, control theory, embedded systems, and artificial intelligence. Recent advances in in situ AI-based device health monitoring indicate that elements of this integration are already emerging; the contribution of AAAI lies in unifying these strands within a coherent cognitive framework that links physical state to adaptive reasoning. Establishing that interdisciplinary foundation may prove to be one of the defining challenges for the next generation of autonomous technologies.
