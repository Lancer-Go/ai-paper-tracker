摘要。Abstract.

1. 简介Introduction

生成图像模型的进步迅速改变了视觉内容的创建、编辑和探索方式Advancements in generative image models have rapidly transformed the way visual content is created, edited, and explored(Rombach et al., 2022; Ho et al., 2020; Dhariwal and Nichol, 2021)。 这些模型的大部分进展都集中在视觉保真度和对输入条件的遵守上。 然而，随着这些模型变得更加强大，用户的期望也随之扩大：用户通常希望探索多个合理的输出，而不是寻求单一的忠实渲染，特别是当他们想要的结果仍然不清楚时。 用户期望的这种变化提出了从单个输入提示生成多样化输出库的挑战。. Much of the progress in these models has focused on visual fidelity and adherence to input conditioning. However, as these models become more capable, user expectations have expanded: rather than seeking a single faithful rendering, users often wish to explore multiple plausible outputs, particularly when their desired outcome is still unclear. This change in user expectations raises the challenge of generating a diverse gallery of outputs from a single input prompt.

实现这种多样性具有挑战性，因为最近最先进的文本到图像模型通常在同一提示生成的样本之间表现出有限的语义变化（图Achieving such diversity is challenging, as recent state-of-the-art text-to-image models often exhibit limited semantic variation across samples generated from the same prompt (Figure2）。 特别是，即使提示未明确说明，不同代人也倾向于趋同于相同的高级语义解释，区别仅在于 视觉上无关紧要的细节，或表现出严重的偏见). In particular, even when prompts are underspecified, different generations tend to converge on the same high-level semantic interpretation, differing only in visually insignificant details, or exhibiting severe biases(Cohen et al., 2025)。 造成语义多样性缺乏的一个可能因素是现代文本到图像模型的训练范式，它强调严格遵守高度详细的标题. A likely contributing factor to this lack of semantic diversity is the training paradigm of modern text-to-image models, which emphasizes strict adherence to highly detailed captions(Black Forest Labs, 2024; Gutflaish et al., 2025; Betker et al., 2023)。 虽然这种设计选择大大提高了可控性和提示忠实度，但它也使模型偏向于致力于提示的单一实现，为语义多样化的输出留下了很小的空间。. While this design choice substantially improves controllability and prompt faithfulness, it also biases the model toward committing to a single realization of the prompt, leaving little room for semantically diverse outputs.

先前的工作通过扰乱调节信号解决了这一限制Prior work has addressed this limitation by perturbing the conditioning signal(Sadat et al., 2023; Um and Ye, 2025)，在采样轨迹之间引入排斥力, introducing repulsive forces between sampling trajectories(Corso et al., 2023; Dahary et al., 2026)，或生成大型候选池，从中选择不同的子集, or generating large candidate pools from which diverse subsets are selected(Parmar et al., 2025)。 虽然在增加多样性方面取得了成功，但这些方法并没有为用户提供对所产生的变化的性质的明确控制。 因此，样本之间的差异是由随机效应而不是明确的语义因素驱动的。. While successful at increasing diversity, these approaches do not offer explicit user control over the nature of the resulting variations. Consequently, differences across samples are driven by stochastic effects rather than explicit semantic factors.

We refer to this process as语义浏览Semantic Browsing，并将其视为概念上不同的多样性方法，其中差异是明确指定的而不是自然出现的。 通过语义变化，我们指的是图像可解释属性的变化，例如对象属性或配置（例如姿势或空间排列）、全局外观因素（例如风格、调色板或照明）或上下文元素（例如天气或背景），同时保留提示的所有其他方面，见图, and view it as a conceptually different approach to diversity, where variations are explicitly specified rather than emergent. By semantic variations, we refer to changes in interpretable attributes of the image, such as object attributes or configurations (e.g., pose or spatial arrangement), global appearance factors (e.g., style, color palette, or lighting), or contextual elements (e.g., weather or background), while preserving all other aspects of the prompt, see Figure1.

These dimensions capture alternative semantic interpretations or design choices that are compatible with the original prompt but not explicitly specified by it. We then organize them into a structured set of prompt alternatives, each corresponding to a distinct semantic choice.

这种基于提示的公式对底层图像生成器提出了两个关键要求。 首先，生成器必须支持细粒度的提示级别控制，以便代理工作流指定的语义变化导致相应精确的视觉变化。其次，它必须保留未显式修改的图像的所有方面，确保生成的图库之间的差异仅源于预期的语义变化。 值得注意的是，最近最先进的文本到图像模型自然满足这些要求，因为它们经过训练严格遵守详细的文本规范This prompt-based formulation places two key requirements on the underlying image generator. First, the generator must support fine-grained prompt-level control, so that semantic changes specified by the agentic workflow result in correspondingly precise visual changes. Second, it must preserve all aspects of the image that are not explicitly modified, ensuring that differences across the generated gallery arise solely from the intended semantic variations. Notably, recent state-of-the-art text-to-image models naturally satisfy these requirements, as they are trained for strict adherence to detailed textual specifications(Black Forest Labs, 2024; Gutflaish et al., 2025)。这使得它们非常适合准确反映显式提示更改，同时保持未提及的属性的一致性。 这种训练范式以 FIBO 为例. This makes them well suited to accurately reflect explicit prompt changes while maintaining consistency in attributes that are not mentioned. This training paradigm is exemplified by FIBO(Gutflaish et al., 2025)，它在长的、结构化的字幕上训练文本到图像生成器，以提高及时的依从性和可控性。, which trains a text-to-image generator on long, structured captions to improve prompt adherence and controllability.

5. 结论、局限性和未来的工作Conclusions, Limitations and Future Work

我们提出了一种在文本到图像模型中生成语义多样性的结构化方法。在较高的层面上，这项工作采用了这样的观点：多样性源于明确的语义决策，而不仅仅是随机变化。通过在生成过程中做出具体的语义选择，输出之间的差异变得可解释和持久，而不是偶然的。因此，生成的结果不仅形成图像集合，而且形成替代场景解释的结构化且可导航的语义空间。We have presented a structured approach for generating semantic diversity in text-to-image models. At a high level, this work adopts a perspective in which diversity arises from explicit semantic decision-making rather than from stochastic variation alone. By committing to concrete semantic choices during generation, differences between outputs become interpretable and persistent rather than incidental. Consequently, the generated results form not just a collection of images, but a structured and navigable semantic space of alternative scene interpretations.

这种观点是由最近的文本到图像模型实现的，这些模型表现出很强的即时依从性，我们不将其视为对多样性的限制，而是将其视为精确语义控制的推动者。该公式不是针对单一的精炼输出进行优化，而是强调探索，使用多智能体推理过程来呈现并维持对未指定提示的多种合理解释。这些解释是通过继承的语义承诺序列构建的，导致结构化的语义变化，其中先前固定的决策保持一致，同时以受控和可解释的方式引入新的变化。This perspective was enabled by recent text-to-image models that exhibit strong prompt adherence, which we treated not as a limitation on diversity but as an enabler for precise semantic control. Rather than optimizing toward a single refined output, the formulation emphasized exploration, using a multi-agent reasoning process to surface and maintain multiple plausible interpretations of an under-specified prompt. These interpretations were constructed through sequences of inherited semantic commitments, leading to structured semantic variation in which previously fixed decisions remained consistent while new variations were introduced in a controlled and interpretable manner.

这里提出的方法有一些局限性，这些局限性主要源于其当前的实现。所探索的语义空间的质量和有用性取决于底层生成模型忠实地实现细粒度提示修改的能力，以及提出和评估变化的代理的语义推理能力。特别是，虽然现代 VLM 在保持一致性和合理性方面很有效，但相对于人们最终希望探索的解释的广度而言，它们提出丰富多样的语义替代方案的能力仍然有限，这可能会限制由此产生的语义空间的范围。The method presented here has several limitations that stem primarily from its current realization. The quality and usefulness of the explored semantic space depend on the underlying generative model’s ability to faithfully realize fine-grained prompt modifications, as well as on the semantic reasoning capabilities of the agents that propose and evaluate variations. In particular, while modern VLMs are effective at maintaining consistency and plausibility, their ability to propose rich and diverse semantic alternatives remains limited relative to the breadth of interpretations one might ultimately wish to explore, which can constrain the scope of the resulting semantic space.

更广泛地说，虽然这项工作专注于图像生成，但通过显式语义决策构建多样性的概念提出了一个更通用的范式。展望未来，我们相信结构化语义探索可以超越图像扩展到其他生成领域，例如视频、3D 内容或多模态生成，从而提供一种从孤立的输出转向连贯、可导航的替代空间的原则性方法。More broadly, although this work focused on image generation, the notion of structuring diversity through explicit semantic decisions suggests a more general paradigm. Looking forward, we believe that structured semantic exploration could extend beyond images to other generative domains, such as video, 3D content, or multimodal generation, offering a principled way to move from isolated outputs toward coherent, navigable spaces of alternatives.

为了严格评估我们方法的有效性，我们将其与以下方法进行比较。为了公平比较，所有基线都是使用相同的底层生成模型（FIBO）实现的，并且它们的超参数专门针对此设置进行了优化。To rigorously evaluate the effectiveness of our approach, we compare it against the following methods. For fair comparison, all baselines were implemented using the same underlying generation model (FIBO), and their hyperparameters were optimized specifically for this setting.

随机 VLM 播种。Stochastic VLM Seeding.

这是一个简单的基线，我们通过简单地改变初始 VLM 调用的随机种子（提示到 JSON）来生成目标图库大小（27 张图像），依赖于模型固有的多样性随机性。A naïve baseline where we generate the target gallery size (27 images) by simply varying the random seed of the initial VLM call (prompt-to-JSON), relying on the model’s inherent stochasticity for diversity.

事后多样性优化。Post-Hoc Diversity Optimization.

“生成和选择”基线，我们过度生成 79 个候选池，并选择 27 个图像的最佳子集，通过二次整数规划 (QIP) 最大化成对 DINO 距离A ’generate-and-select’ baseline where we over-generate a pool of 79 candidates and select the optimal subset of 27 images that maximizes pairwise DINO distance via Quadratic Integer Programming (QIP)(Parmar et al., 2025)。由于此优化的计算成本较高，我们对每个实例施加严格的 300 秒时间限制。至关重要的是，池大小 79 与我们提出的树生成方法中使用的 LLM 调用总数相匹配。这确保了在固定计算预算下进行公平比较，测试我们的结构化、分层扩展是否比简单地重复运行基本提示到 JSON 流程产生更好的多样性。. Due to the high computational cost of this optimization, we impose a strict 300-second time limit per instance. Crucially, the pool size of 79 matches the total number of LLM calls used in our proposed tree-generation method. This ensures a fair comparison under a fixed computational budget, testing whether our structured, hierarchical expansion yields better diversity than simply running the base prompt-to-JSON flow repeatedly.

Unlike the standard baseline which operates within a conventional probability distribution, this method forces the selection of lower-probability tokens. We include this to strictly evaluate whether the diversity gap can be closed simply by increasing the entropy of the unstructured generation process, or if our structured intervention is necessary.

This sublinear scaling confirms that our method remains computationally efficient even as the number of siblings at each node increases.
