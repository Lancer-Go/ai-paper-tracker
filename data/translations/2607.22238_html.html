摘要Abstract

贝叶斯优化（BO）是一种通过平衡探索和利用来顺序提出下一个候选可解释变量以优化目标变量的优化方法。 BO通常在评估预算有限的情况下使用，例如深度学习的超参数调整。 尽管有效，传统的 BO 在实际实验科学中可能收敛性较差，每次评估往往成本高昂且耗时。 最近，人们提出了 BO 方法，通过使用模拟实验数据的伪实验数据来加速优化。 然而，当只有有限数量的实验数据可用时，生成的伪实验数据可能质量不够。 在本研究中，我们开发了 PolyBO，即使在试验次数有限的情况下，也可以通过生成高质量的伪实验数据来缩短优化时间。 PolyBO 通过使用自适应更新的多功能参数模型生成伪实验数据来有效地执行 BO。 这种低容量多项式回归模型旨在即使在实验数据有限的情况下也能实现高效的 BO。 PolyBO使用由实验数据和伪实验数据组成的组合数据集更新BO代理模型，然后执行优化。 使用具有不同景观的综合基准函数，我们发现 PolyBO 将优化时间平均缩短了 42%。 对于现实世界的材料成分优化问题，与传统方法相比，PolyBO 将优化时间平均缩短了 96%。 总体而言，PolyBO 在每次实验需要较长时间的设置中实现了高效优化。Bayesian optimization (BO) is an optimization method that sequentially proposes the next candidate explainable variables for optimizing target variables by balancing exploration and exploitation. BO is often used under a limited evaluation budget, such as hyperparameter tuning of deep learning. Despite its effectiveness, conventional BO may have poor convergence in practical experimental science where each evaluation is often costly and time-consuming. Recently, BO methods have been proposed that accelerate optimization by using pseudo-experimental data that simulate experimental data. However, when only a limited number of experimental data are available, the generated pseudo-experimental data may be of insufficient quality. In this study, we developed PolyBO to improve optimization time by generating high-quality pseudo-experimental data even when the number of trials is limited. PolyBO performs BO efficiently by generating pseudo-experimental data with an adaptively updated versatile parametric model. This low-capacity polynomial regression model is intended to enable efficient BO even with limited experimental data. PolyBO updates the BO surrogate model with a combined dataset consisting of experimental data and pseudo-experimental data and then performs optimization. Using synthetic benchmark functions with diverse landscapes, we found that PolyBO reduced the optimization time by a median of 42%. For a real-world material composition optimization problem, PolyBO reduced the optimization time by a median of 96% compared with conventional methods. Overall, PolyBO achieves efficient optimization in settings where each experiment requires a long time.
