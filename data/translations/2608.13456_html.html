因果世界模型的统一视角：A Unifying Perspective on Causal World Models:
从观察到表征再到结构From Observations to Representations to Structure

Avinash Kori Affiliation: Department of Computing, Imperial College London, London, UK
{a.kori21,fabrizio}@imperial.ac.uk    Fabrizio Russo Affiliation: Department of Computing, Imperial College London, London, UK

摘要Abstract

世界模型 (WM) 越来越被视为智能代理的基础，智能代理可以在其训练分布之外进行预测、计划和行动。 在本文中，我们从因果角度跨多个抽象层次研究 WM，从感知观察到构建控制环境动态的结构的概念表示。 我们认为有用的 WM 必须超越 仅生成能力：它们还应该捕获确定和解释系统动态的实体属性、实体到实体交互以及实体到环境交互。 我们根据因果 WM (CWM) 旨在支持的任务提供了正式定义，将世界建模与因果表示学习、以对象为中心的学习、因果发现、结构因果模型和基于模型的决策等领域的现有工作联系起来。 最后，我们将 CWM 与可识别性文献联系起来，阐明何时可以从数据中恢复 WM 的组成部分以及最多达到哪些等价性。 由此，我们将 WM 置于支持因果推理和明智决策的表示和结构中。World Models (WM) are increasingly seen as a foundation for intelligent agents that can predict, plan, and act beyond their training distribution. In this paper, we study WMs from a causal perspective across multiple levels of abstraction, ranging from perceptual observations to building a conceptual representation of the structure governing the environment dynamics. We argue that useful WMs must go beyond generative capabilities alone: they should also capture entity properties, entity-to-entity interactions, and entity-to-environment interactions that determine and explain the dynamics of a system. We provide a formal definition of Causal WMs (CWMs) grounded in the tasks they are intended to support, connecting world modelling with existing work in causal representation learning, object-centric learning, causal discovery, structural causal models, and model-based decision-making. Finally, we relate CWMs to the literature on identifiability, clarifying when the components of a WM can be recovered from data and up to which equivalence. With this, we ground WMs in representations and structures that support causal reasoning and informed decision-making.

1 简介Introduction

世界模型已成为现代人工智能的核心愿望之一。 它们被用来描述在训练分布之外想象、计划和学习安全行动的智能体World models have become one of the central aspirations of modern Artificial Intelligence. They are invoked to describe agents that imagine, plan, and learn to act safely outside their training distribution(8; 16; 19; 26)。 挑战在于. The challenge is to建造build这些模型来自观察、轨迹、干预和领域知识，同时避免两个无益的极端：将WM视为没有明确变量或机制的非结构化黑匣子，或者预先假设已经指定了正确的变量和机制，以便它们可以直接用于因果干预。such models from observations, trajectories, interventions, and domain knowledge, while avoiding two unhelpful extremes: treating WMs as an unstructured black box with no explicit variables or mechanisms, or assuming in advance that the right variables and mechanisms have already been specified, so that they can be directly used for causal intervention.

最近的工作在这个范围内占据了不同的位置。 像 Dreamer 这样的预测世界模型从感觉流中学习紧凑的潜在状态，并使用想象的推出来控制不同的任务，但学习到的状态不需要有明确的因果或语义解释Recent work occupies different points along this spectrum. Predictive world models such as Dreamer learn compact latent states from sensory streams and use imagined rollouts for control across diverse tasks, but the learned states need not have an explicit causal or semantic interpretation(9)。 ResDreamer 等分层变体通过强调与真实感重建相关的决策相关信号，更接近任务级推理，同时仍将内部表示很大程度上视为学习的潜在基质. Hierarchical variants such as ResDreamer move closer to task-level reasoning by emphasising decision-relevant signals over photorealistic reconstruction, while still treating the internal representation largely as a learned latent substrate(31)。 LeJEPA 等联合嵌入方法通过将预测表示学习与正式的可识别性保证联系起来，解决了问题的不同部分，但它们本身并没有指定在世界上采取行动所需的因果变量、机制或干预目标. Joint-embedding approaches such as LeJEPA address a different part of the problem by connecting predictive representation learning with formal identifiability guarantees, but they do not by themselves specify the causal variables, mechanisms, or intervention targets needed for acting in the world(13)。 因果动力学方法说明了互补的结构路线：一旦给出状态变量，稀疏因果依赖性可以提高泛化能力并为下游任务产生可重用的状态抽象. Causal dynamics methods illustrate the complementary structural route: once state variables are given, sparse causal dependencies can improve generalisation and yield reusable state abstractions for downstream tasks(30; 29)。 最近关于鲁棒和通用智能体的研究使得动机特别直接：智能体需要支持关于行动、中介和分配变化的因果推理的 WM，而不仅仅是下一状态的预测. Recent work on robust and general agents makes the motivation especially direct: agents need WMs that support causal reasoning about actions, mediation, and distribution shifts, not merely next-state prediction(23)。然而，即使在这些文献中，WM 通常仍然主要作为过渡模型来操作. However, even in this literature, WMs are often still operationalised primarily as transition models(22; 3).

然而，“世界模式”这个词往往更多的是指一种野心，而不是一个明确的目标。Yet the phrase World Model often names an ambition more than a well-specified object.循环潜在动力学模型、下次观察预测器、模拟器、联合嵌入预测器、学习状态抽象以及存储在大型生成模型中的隐式知识都被称为 WMRecurrent latent dynamics models, next-observation predictors, simulators, joint-embedding predictors, learned state abstractions, as well as the implicit knowledge stored in a large generative model have all been referred to as WMs(7; 4)。 困难在于这个共同的标签混淆了不同的建模承诺。 预测器指定接下来可能发生的事情；表示通过映射来压缩观测值. The difficulty is that this common label conflates distinct modelling commitments. A predictor specifies what is likely to happen next; a representation compresses observations by mapping非结构化的unstructured对代表世界相关方面的可用结构化变量的观察；因果模型指定了支持干预分析的机制。 尚未明确的是如何将这些部分连接起来，以构建具有已知属性和保证的强大 WM：应该表示哪些变量，是否可以从数据中识别它们，它们之间的哪些关系是因果关系，何时可以将动作条件预测归因于干预效应解释，以及何时需要额外的信息或领域知识。observations into usable structured variables representing relevant aspects of the world; and a causal model specifies mechanisms enabling interventional analysis. What remains underspecified is how to connect these pieces towards building robust WMs with known properties and guarantees: which variables should be represented, whether they are identifiable from data, which relations among them are causal, when an action-conditioned prediction can be ascribed an interventional-effect interpretation, and when additional information or domain knowledge is required.

4 结论Conclusion

本文认为，CWM 应该被理解为结构化决策模型，而不是单一的预测器。 我们将 CWM 形式化为连接观察、表示和结构的模型，并使用这种形式化来连接表示学习、因果发现和基于模型的决策。 核心贡献是组件方面的视图，其中模型的每个部分只能在允许下游推理的可接受的等价性范围内进行识别。 这种分解阐明了可以从数据中学到什么，必须通过干预措施或领域知识提供什么，以及预测模型何时可以支持因果推理。 未来的工作应该将这种观点转化为严格的形式化，然后是实用的算法和实证测试，同时将其扩展到部分可观察性和潜在的混杂性。This paper argues that CWMs should be understood as structured decision models rather than as monolithic predictors. We formalised a CWM as a model linking observations, representations and structure, and used this formalisation to connect representation learning, causal discovery, and model-based decision-making. The central contribution is a component-wise view, where each part of the model may be identifiable only up to admissible equivalence that enables the downstream reasoning. This decomposition clarifies what can be learned from data, what must be supplied by interventions or domain knowledge, and when predictive models can support causal reasoning. Future work should turn this perspective into a rigorous formalisation, followed by practical algorithms and empirical testing, while extending it to partial observability and latent confounding.
