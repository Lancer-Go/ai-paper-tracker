ContactGuard：使用动作条件潜在世界模型进行接触前执行监控ContactGuard: Pre-Contact Execution Monitoring with Action-Conditioned Latent World Models

摘要Abstract

富含接触的操作故障通常只有在机器人进行接触后才会被检测到。这在腕部相机设置中尤其受到限制：近距离的抓手-物体视图有助于观察接触，但不良的方法可能在传统探测器做出反应之前就已经推动、错过、滑动或干扰了物体。我们介绍Contact-rich manipulation failures are often detected only after the robot has committed to contact. This is especially limiting in wrist-camera setups: close gripper–object views help observe contact, but a poor approach may already push, miss, slip, or disturb the object before conventional detectors react. We introduce接触卫士ContactGuard，用于分块视觉运动策略的接触前执行监视器。考虑到策略的计划行动块，ContactGuard 会预测其在潜在视觉空间中的短期后果，如果预测的未来潜在表明可能失败，则中止。其潜在世界模型是根据未标记的机器人轨迹进行训练的，可以在计划的动作下预测紧凑的多视图视觉嵌入，从而避免像素级视频预测。然后从一小组标记的预接触夹中训练一个轻量级故障探针。在部署时，ContactGuard 在即将发生的接触事件之前锚定预测，在策略自身的操作下向前滚动模型，并验证预测的接触后潜伏。在现实世界中接触丰富的操作任务中，ContactGuard 比直接和损坏的动作消融更准确地预测失败，并在不修改底层策略的情况下将其作为预接触中止信号传输到实时机器人。, a pre-contact execution monitor for chunked visuomotor policies. Given the policy’s planned action chunk, ContactGuard predicts its short-horizon consequence in latent visual space and aborts if the predicted future latent indicates likely failure. Its latent world model is trained from unlabelled robot trajectories to predict compact multi-view visual embeddings under planned actions, avoiding pixel-level video prediction. A lightweight failure probe is then trained from a small labelled set of pre-contact clips. At deployment, ContactGuard anchors prediction before an imminent contact event, rolls the model forward under the policy’s own actions, and verifies the predicted post-contact latent. Across real-world contact-rich manipulation tasks, ContactGuard predicts failure more accurately than direct and corrupted-action ablations, and transfers to live robot as a pre-contact abort signal without modifying the underlying policy.

1 简介Introduction

可靠的操纵需要的不仅仅是视觉上合理的运动。在接触丰富的任务中，接触附近的小错误可能决定成功或失败：夹具可能会偏离中心、将物体推开、仅捏住边缘、错过可变形区域或在物体就位之前关闭。这些故障对于视觉运动策略来说很难在遮挡、杂乱、可变形物体和分布变化的情况下避免，而且一旦接触扰乱了场景，这些故障就更难纠正。这激励Reliable manipulation requires more than visually plausible motion. In contact-rich tasks, small errors near contact can determine success or failure: the gripper may approach off-centre, nudge the object away, pinch only an edge, miss a deformable region, or close before the object is seated. These failures are difficult for visuomotor policies to avoid under occlusion, clutter, deformable objects, and distribution shift, and they are harder to correct once contact has disturbed the scene. This motivates接触前执行监控pre-contact execution monitoring：监视器不是检测机器人是否已经失败，而是询问它即将执行的操作是否可能失败。分块的视觉运动策略使这个问题变得自然，因为动作块通常包含有意义的交互事件，例如夹具闭合或物体接触。如果机器人可以在接触之前评估该块的后果，它就可以在进行之前停止不良交互。: instead of detecting whether the robot has already failed, the monitor asks whether the action it is about to execute is likely to fail. Chunked visuomotor policies make this question natural, because an action chunk often contains a meaningful interaction event, such as gripper closure or object contact. If the robot can evaluate the consequence of that chunk before contact, it can stop a poor interaction before committing to it.

推理未来后果的一种自然方法是学习世界模型。然而，对于操纵监控，该模型不需要生成逼真的未来帧。它只需要一个保留结果相关信息的表示：计划的交互是否可能成功或失败。因此，我们使用动作条件潜在预测，它将多视图观察编码为紧凑的视觉嵌入，并学习这些嵌入在机器人动作下如何演变。这遵循联合嵌入预测架构 (JEPA) 原则A natural way to reason about future consequences is to learn a world model. For manipulation monitoring, however, the model need not generate photorealistic future frames. It only needs a representation that preserves outcome-relevant information: whether the planned interaction is likely to succeed or fail. We therefore use action-conditioned latent prediction, which encodes multi-view observations into compact visual embeddings and learns how those embeddings evolve under robot actions. This follows the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) principle[1]，其中预测器被训练来预测目标信号的嵌入，而不是在像素空间中重建它；直接在表示空间中进行预测可以避免像素级视频生成的成本和模糊性。我们介绍, where a predictor is trained to forecast the embedding of a target signal rather than reconstruct it in pixel space; predicting directly in representation space avoids the cost and ambiguity of pixel-level video generation. We introduce接触卫士ContactGuard，基于此原理构建的预接触监视器。潜在世界模型是使用下一个潜在监督从未标记的机器人轨迹中训练出来的；训练后，其编码器和预测器被冻结。然后，仅使用预测的未来潜伏，在标记的接触前剪辑上训练轻量级逻辑回归探针，将与任务无关的视觉动态学习与小数据结果监督分开。, a pre-contact monitor built on this principle. A latent world model is trained from unlabelled robot trajectories using next-latent supervision; after training, its encoder and predictor are frozen. A lightweight logistic-regression probe is then trained on labelled pre-contact clips using only the predicted future latent, separating task-agnostic visual dynamics learning from small-data outcome supervision.

The deployed policy is treated as a black-box proposer: ContactGuard consumes only the current observation and the concrete action chunk already emitted by the policy, without accessing policy internals or requiring joint training. It scans the upcoming chunk for an imminent contact event, anchors prediction shortly before that event, rolls the independently trained latent world model forward under the proposed actions, and applies the frozen probe to the predicted post-contact latent. The system aborts before contact if that specific chunk is likely to lead to failure; otherwise the policy continues unchanged.

我们的实验测试了这种以行动为条件的未来潜伏是否包含仅从当前观察或计划行动中无法获得的故障信息。在现实世界中接触丰富的操作任务中，预测未来潜在的操作可以改善对当前潜在操作和损坏操作消融的故障预测。这些结果表明，潜在世界模型不仅可以用作规划或表示学习模块，还可以用作实际执行监视器，以防止物理接触之前可能发生的故障。Our experiments test whether this action-conditioned future latent contains failure information unavailable from the current observation or planned action alone. Across real-world contact-rich manipulation tasks, predicted future latents improve failure prediction over current-latent and corrupted-action ablations. These results suggest that latent world models can serve not only as planning or representation-learning modules, but also as practical execution monitors for preventing likely failures before physical contact.

5 结论、局限性和未来的工作Conclusions, Limitations, and Future Work

我们提出了We presented接触卫士ContactGuard，用于接触前操纵的实时、策略解耦的预测验证器。独立训练的动作条件潜在世界模型评估由其他不变的视觉运动策略提出的具体动作块，并使用轻量级故障探针对其预测的接触后结果进行评分。在四种现实世界的抓取设置中，ContactGuard 的性能优于匹配的当前状态监控和经过测试的外部运行时故障检测器。保持观察固定，同时仅替换建议的操作也会大大改变预测的故障概率，从而确认监视器对待处理的操作做出响应，而不仅仅是对静态视觉风险做出响应。这些结果支持以动作为条件的潜在想象力作为在场景改变接触之前否决可能失败的实际基础。, a real-time, policy-decoupled predictive verifier for pre-contact manipulation. An independently trained action-conditioned latent world model evaluates the concrete action chunk proposed by an otherwise unchanged visuomotor policy and scores its predicted post-contact consequence with a lightweight failure probe. Across four real-world grasp settings, ContactGuard outperforms matched current-state monitoring and the tested external runtime failure detectors. Holding the observation fixed while replacing only the proposed action also changes the predicted failure probability substantially, confirming that the monitor responds to the pending action rather than only to static visual risk. These results support action-conditioned latent imagination as a practical substrate for vetoing likely failures before scene-changing contact.
