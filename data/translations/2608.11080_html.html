Motivated by this, we followed the previous work[42]并提出了基于LLM的知识增强客流量预测的任务。具体来说，我们将每个时间序列实例（即车站过去几天的客流量观察）表达为自然语言查询，例如，“从2025年9月5日到9月16日，位于‘Bushwick North’功能区的轨道交通车站‘Myrtle-Wyckoff Avs’每天记录了以下客流量：18030 12496 … 18461。什么是2025 年 9 月 17 日的客流量是多少？”。同时，我们将每个 KG 关系三元组表达为自然语言语句，例如“轨道交通站‘Myrtle-Wyckoff Avs’属于轨道交通线‘Canarsie’”。然后，我们使用预训练的语言模型将所有查询和语言化关系三元组编码为嵌入，并通过嵌入的余弦相似度计算查询和关系三元组之间的相似度得分。对于每个查询，我们检索了顶部的and proposed the task of LLM-based knowledge-enhanced ridership prediction. Specifically, we verbalized each time series instance (i.e., the ridership observations over the past days of a station) into a natural language query, e.g., “From September 05 to September 16, 2025, the rail transit station ‘Myrtle-Wyckoff Avs’ located at the ‘Bushwick North’ functional area recorded the following ridership numbers: 18030 12496 … 18461 on each day. What is the ridership going to be on September 17, 2025?”. Meanwhile, we verbalized each KG relation triple into a natural language statement, e.g., “The rail transit station ‘Myrtle-Wyckoff Avs’ belongs to the rail transit line ‘Canarsie”’. We then encoded all the queries and verbalized relation triples as embeddings with a pretrained language model, and computed similarity scores between the queries and relation triples by cosine similarities of their embeddings. Next, we used an LLM to summarize the retrieved relation triples into an abstract, which serves as external knowledge for the query. Finally, we concatenated the query with the abstract to form a prompt and fed it into another LLM to perform knowledge-enhanced ridership prediction. The prompts are provided in our GitHub repository.

7 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了RTSKG，一种新的轨道交通车站知识图谱数据集，旨在显式建模轨道交通车站以及不同类型城市实体之间复杂的空间和语义交互，以执行城市级轨道交通车站相关任务。实验结果表明，利用 RTSKG 与利用其他城市 KG 相比，车站区域商店推荐和知识增强客流预测具有最佳性能。 通过为城市级轨道交通车站相关任务提供可重复使用的数据集，我们的研究不仅在轨道交通系统中具有潜在的应用，而且在其他城市基础设施系统中也具有潜在的应用，这有助于促进城市发展。In this paper, we propose RTSKG, a new rail transit station knowledge graph dataset, which is designed to explicitly model rail transit stations and the complex spatial and semantic interactions among different kinds of urban entities for city-level rail transit station related tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that leveraging RTSKG for station-area store recommendation and knowledge-enhanced ridership prediction has the best performance compared with leveraging other urban KGs. By providing a reusable dataset for city-level rail transit station related tasks, our study has potential applications not only in rail transit systems but also in other urban infrastructure systems, which contributes to enhancing urban development.
