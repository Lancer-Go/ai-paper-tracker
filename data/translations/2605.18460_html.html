当萤火虫聚集；增强自动化 通过质心引导的 Firefly 进行聚类 优化When Fireflies Cluster; Enhancing Automatic Clustering via Centroid-Guided Firefly Optimization

MKA Ariyaratne Corresponding author: mkanuradha@sjp.ac.lk Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Applied Sciences, University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka Azwirman Gusrialdi Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences,Tampere University, Tampere, Pirkanmaa, Finland Yury Nikulin Department of Mathematics and Statistics, University of Turku, Finland Jaakko Peltonen Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences,Tampere University, Tampere, Pirkanmaa, Finland
摘要Abstract

这项工作提出了一种用于数据聚类的 Firefly 算法 (FA) 的新颖变体，解决了 K 均值等传统方法的局限性，这些方法与不均匀的聚类形状、密度以及预定义聚类数量的需求作斗争。该算法引入了质心运动策略和多目标适应度函数，以平衡紧凑性、分离性和新颖的基于 TSP 的导航惩罚。它自动估计最佳簇数并动态调整簇边界。在机器人传感器网络中的应用凸显了其实用价值，实验表明与 K-Means 相比，聚类质量得到了提高，簇内路径距离也缩短了。这些结果证实了该算法在复杂空间聚类任务中的鲁棒性，并具有未来扩展到更高维度和自适应场景的潜力。This work presents a novel variant of the Firefly Algorithm (FA) for data clustering, addressing limitations of traditional methods like K-Means that struggle with non-uniform cluster shapes, densities, and the need for pre-defining the number of clusters. The proposed algorithm introduces a centroid movement strategy and a multi-objective fitness function that balances compactness, separation, and a novel TSP-based navigation penalty. It automatically estimates the optimal number of clusters and dynamically adjusts cluster boundaries. Application to robotic sensor networks highlights its practical value, with experiments showing improved clustering quality and reduced intra-cluster path distances compared to K-Means. These results confirm the algorithm’s robustness in complex spatial clustering tasks, with potential for future extensions to higher-dimensional and adaptive scenarios.

Given its broad applicability, cluster analysis has emerged as a prominent research area in data mining, finding utility across various scientific disciplines. Despite the proliferation of clustering algorithms in recent years and their widespread adoption in fields like computational biology, mobile communication, medicine, economics, and more, standardization remains a challenge. Despite efforts to develop standardized algorithms capable of performing effectively across all scenarios, significant progress in this regard remains elusive. 3.2.7 为什么健身功能效果好？Why the Fitness Function Works Well?

适应度函数由三个主要部分组成：紧致性、分离性和旅行商问题 (TSP) 惩罚。每个组件都解决聚类质量的特定方面。The fitness function consists of three main components: compactness, separation, and the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) penalty. Each component addresses a specific aspect of clustering quality.

紧凑性衡量簇内数据点围绕其质心分组的紧密程度。它的计算方式是簇中所有点距质心的平均距离。在聚类中，最小化簇内点之间的距离至关重要，因为它可以确保簇成员彼此高度相似。较低的紧凑度值表明簇更紧密、更有凝聚力。因此，该算法鼓励形成具有最小簇内方差的明确定义的簇。将紧凑性纳入适应度函数有助于生成有意义、可解释且内部一致的聚类。Compactness measures how closely the data points within a cluster are grouped around their centroid. It is calculated as the average distance of all the points in a cluster from the centroid. In clustering, minimizing the distance between points within a cluster is critical because it ensures that the cluster members are highly similar to one another. Lower compactness values indicate tighter and more cohesive clusters. Therefore, the algorithm encourages the formation of well-defined clusters with minimal intra-cluster variance. The inclusion of compactness in the fitness function helps generate clusters that are meaningful, interpretable, and internally consistent.

分离度是指不同簇质心之间的平均距离。高度分离意味着簇之间可以很好地区分，从而减少簇边界之间的重叠和模糊性。此属性很重要，因为它确保属于不同簇的数据点保持足够不同。通过将分离纳入适应度函数中，该算法促进了不仅内部内聚而且外部分离良好的簇的创建。这提高了聚类过程的整体判别质量，特别是在处理具有重叠或紧密定位的组的数据集时。Separation refers to the average distance between the centroids of different clusters. High separation implies that clusters are well-distinguished from one another, reducing overlap and ambiguity between cluster boundaries. This property is important because it ensures that data points belonging to different clusters remain sufficiently distinct. By incorporating separation into the fitness function, the algorithm promotes the creation of clusters that are not only internally cohesive but also externally well-separated. This improves the overall discriminative quality of the clustering process, especially when handling datasets with overlapping or closely positioned groups.

TSP 惩罚通过使用旅行商问题 (TSP) 启发式来估计簇内点之间的总遍历距离，从而评估每个簇内部结构的组织程度。该组件阻止形成不规则、分散或结构不良的空间排列的簇。相反，它鼓励内部点分布在空间上连贯且有组织的集群。 TSP 惩罚在空间聚类问题中特别有价值，其中聚类内点的相对排列很重要。因此，该措施通过引入简单距离最小化之外的附加结构约束来提高聚类质量。The TSP penalty evaluates how well-organized the internal structure of each cluster is by using a Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) heuristic to estimate the total traversal distance among points within a cluster. This component discourages the formation of clusters with irregular, scattered, or poorly structured spatial arrangements. Instead, it encourages clusters whose internal point distributions are spatially coherent and organized. The TSP penalty is particularly valuable in spatial clustering problems where the relative arrangement of points within clusters is important. As a result, this measure improves clustering quality by introducing an additional structural constraint beyond simple distance minimization.

3.2.8 为什么运动策略效果很好？Why the Movement Strategy Works Well?

萤火虫算法中的移动策略旨在有效地探索搜索空间，同时逐步提高聚类质量。在基于吸引力的移动机制中，适应度值较高的萤火虫代表较差的聚类解决方案，向适应度值较低的萤火虫移动，代表更好的解决方案。吸引力的程度取决于萤火虫之间的距离，附近的萤火虫比远处的萤火虫产生更强的影响力。这种机制充当局部搜索策略，引导群体走向搜索空间的有希望的区域。通过根据与性能更好的萤火虫的交互不断调整质心位置，该算法在连续迭代中逐步完善聚类质量。该策略有效地平衡了高质量解决方案的利用与更广泛的搜索空间探索，帮助算法避免不良的局部最小值，同时仍然收敛于改进的聚类配置。The movement strategy in the Firefly Algorithm is designed to efficiently explore the search space while gradually improving clustering quality. In the attraction-based movement mechanism, fireflies with higher fitness values, representing poorer clustering solutions, move toward fireflies with lower fitness values, representing better solutions. The degree of attraction depends on the distance between fireflies, where nearby fireflies exert stronger influence than distant ones. This mechanism acts as a local search strategy that guides the population toward promising regions of the search space. By continuously adjusting centroid positions based on interactions with better-performing fireflies, the algorithm incrementally refines clustering quality over successive iterations. This strategy effectively balances exploitation of high-quality solutions with broader exploration of the search space, helping the algorithm avoid poor local minima while still converging toward improved clustering configurations.

除了吸引更好的解决方案之外，运动方程还包含由参数控制的随机性分量In addition to attraction toward better solutions, the movement equation incorporates a randomness component controlled by the parameterα\alpha。即使一只萤火虫向另一只适应度较高的萤火虫移动，运动过程中也会引入随机扰动。包含随机性很重要，因为它可以防止总体过快地收敛到局部最优。如果没有足够的随机性，搜索过程可能会陷入搜索空间的次优区域。通过引入受控随机探索，该算法能够研究新的、可能更好的区域，否则这些区域可能尚未被探索。这种机制有助于保持萤火虫之间的多样性，并提高算法逃避局部最优的能力，最终增加识别全局最优聚类解决方案的可能性。. Even when a firefly moves toward another firefly with superior fitness, random perturbations are introduced into the movement process. The inclusion of randomness is important because it prevents the population from converging too rapidly to a local optimum. Without sufficient randomness, the search process may become trapped in suboptimal regions of the search space. By introducing controlled random exploration, the algorithm is able to investigate new and potentially better regions that may otherwise remain unexplored. This mechanism helps maintain diversity among the fireflies and improves the algorithm’s ability to escape local optima, ultimately increasing the likelihood of identifying globally optimal clustering solutions.

该方法的一个关键特征是它能够在优化过程中动态调整集群的数量 K。与 KKK-means 等必须事先指定簇数量的传统聚类算法不同，Firefly 算法允许具有不同 KKK 值的萤火虫彼此交互。通过这些相互作用，可以根据相邻萤火虫的相对接近度和质量来概率地添加或删除质心。这种自适应机制使算法能够自动发现最能代表数据集底层结构的适当数量的簇。动态修改 KKK 的灵活性对于预先未知簇的真实数量的复杂数据集特别有益。因此，该算法变得更加稳健和数据驱动，产生更好地捕获自然数据分布的聚类解决方案。A key feature of the proposed approach is its ability to dynamically adapt the number of clusters K during the optimization process. Unlike traditional clustering algorithms such as KKK-means, where the number of clusters must be specified beforehand, the Firefly Algorithm allows fireflies with different KKK values to interact with one another. Through these interactions, centroids may be probabilistically added or removed based on the relative proximity and quality of neighboring fireflies. This adaptive mechanism enables the algorithm to automatically discover an appropriate number of clusters that best represents the underlying structure of the dataset. The flexibility to modify KKK dynamically is particularly beneficial for complex datasets where the true number of clusters is unknown in advance. As a result, the algorithm becomes more robust and data-driven, producing clustering solutions that better capture natural data distributions.

5 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

本研究旨在开发一种独立于预定义聚类数量的聚类方法（This study aimed to develop a clustering approach that is independent of the predefined number of clusters (KK），同时确保给定监控系统的高效集群内导航。为了解决这个问题，提出了一种改进的萤火虫算法，结合了新颖的适应度函数和基于质心的运动策略。结果表明，所提出的元启发式方法能够有效地识别合适的集群结构，而无需事先指定) while ensuring efficient within-cluster navigation for the given monitoring system. To address this, a modified firefly algorithm incorporating a novel fitness function and a centroid-based movement strategy was proposed. The results demonstrate that the proposed meta-heuristic approach is capable of effectively identifying suitable cluster structures without prior specification ofKK，同时保持良好的聚类质量。, while maintaining good clustering quality.

研究结果强调，聚类算法的性能高度依赖于数据集的特征，聚类方法的选择不仅应以数据邻近性为指导，还应以应用程序的基本目标为指导。此外，考虑到聚类的 NP 困难性质，萤火虫算法等元启发式技术为传统方法提供了灵活而强大的替代方案。事实证明，所提出的萤火虫算法修改在必须避免初始化敏感性、需要自动确定聚类数量以及聚类目标超出简单的基于距离的标准的情况下特别有用。The findings highlight that the performance of clustering algorithms is highly dependent on the characteristics of the dataset, and that the choice of clustering method should be guided not only by data proximity but also by the underlying objectives of the application. Furthermore, given the NP-hard nature of clustering, meta-heuristic techniques such as the firefly algorithm provide a flexible and powerful alternative to traditional methods. The proposed modification of the firefly algorithm proves particularly useful in scenarios where initialization sensitivity must be avoided, the number of clusters needs to be determined automatically, and clustering objectives extend beyond simple distance-based criteria.

对于未来的工作，可以探索几个方向来增强所提出的方法。首先，萤火虫算法涉及到几个算法特定的参数，目前需要手动调优；开发自适应或自调整机制将提高其可用性和鲁棒性。其次，将调查范围扩大到其他元启发式算法可以提供比较见解并可能产生进一步的改进。最后，当前的研究仅限于二维数据集，在高维数据上评估所提出的方法将有助于评估其可扩展性和普遍适用性。For future work, several directions can be explored to enhance the proposed approach. First, the firefly algorithm involves several algorithm-specific parameters that currently require manual tuning; developing a self-adaptive or self-tuning mechanism would improve its usability and robustness. Second, extending the investigation to include other meta-heuristic algorithms could provide comparative insights and potentially yield further improvements. Finally, the current study is limited to two-dimensional datasets, and evaluating the proposed method on higher-dimensional data would help assess its scalability and general applicability.
