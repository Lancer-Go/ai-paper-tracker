TASU2：用于语音法学硕士的对齐和低资源适应的可控 CTC 模拟TASU2: Controllable CTC Simulation for Alignment and Low-Resource Adaptation of Speech LLMs

摘要Abstract

语音法学硕士后期培训越来越依赖于高效的跨模式对齐和强大的低资源适应，但收集大规模音频文本对的成本仍然很高。 TASU 等纯文本对齐方法通过模拟成绩单中的 CTC 后验来减轻这种负担，但它们对不确定性和错误率的控制有限，使得课程设计在很大程度上是启发式的。我们建议Speech LLM post-training increasingly relies on efficient cross-modal alignment and robust low-resource adaptation, yet collecting large-scale audio-text pairs remains costly. Text-only alignment methods such as TASU reduce this burden by simulating CTC posteriors from transcripts, but they provide limited control over uncertainty and error rate, making curriculum design largely heuristic. We proposeTASU2，一个可控的CTC模拟框架，可以模拟指定WER范围下的CTC后验分布，产生与声学解码接口更好匹配的文本衍生监督。这使得有原则的培训后课程能够在没有 TTS 的情况下平滑地改变监督难度。在多个源到目标的适应设置中，TASU2 比 TASU 改进了域内和域外识别，并且始终优于强大的基线，包括纯文本微调和基于 TTS 的增强，同时减轻源域性能下降。, a controllable CTC simulation framework that simulates CTC posterior distributions under a specified WER range, producing text-derived supervision that better matches the acoustic decoding interface. This enables principled post-training curricula that smoothly vary supervision difficulty without TTS. Across multiple source-to-target adaptation settings, TASU2 improves in-domain and out-of-domain recognition over TASU, and consistently outperforms strong baselines including text-only fine-tuning and TTS-based augmentation, while mitigating source-domain performance degradation.

1 简介Introduction

大语言模型的快速进展加速了语音LLM研究The rapid progress of large language models has accelerated Speech LLM research[Peng_2025, arora2025landscapespokenlanguagemodels]。 然而，强大的语音 LLM 性能通常伴随着对大规模音频文本对和计算密集型管道的严重依赖. However, strong Speech LLM performance often comes with heavy reliance on large-scale audio–text pairs and compute-intensive pipelines[radford2022robustspeechrecognitionlargescale]，使得后期训练、适应和复制成本高昂。 因此，最近的研究重新审视了语音和文本表示之间的轻量级对齐，包括 TASU, making post-training, adaptation, and reproduction costly. Recent studies therefore revisit lightweight alignment between speech and text representations, including TASU[peng2026tasutextonlyalignmentspeech], 乐高SLM, LegoSLM[ma2025legoslm]，和对齐前, and AlignFormer[fan2025alignformer].

其中，TASU（纯文本对齐语音理解）特别有吸引力，因为它能够Among them, TASU (Text-only Alignment for Speech Understanding) is particularly appealing because it enables纯文本text-only训练后：它从转录本中随机模拟 CTC 后验，允许在没有配对音频的情况下进行训练，同时保留真实音频推理。 在实践中，TASU 可以作为一种有效的课程，提高源域和域转移下的识别度。 然而，它的模拟对后验不确定性和由此产生的误差范围提供了有限的控制，使得难度调度在很大程度上是启发式的。post-training: it stochastically simulates CTC posteriors from transcripts, allowing training without paired audio while retaining real-audio inference. In practice, TASU can serve as an effective curriculum and improves recognition both on the source domain and under domain shift. Yet its simulation provides limited contro l over posterior uncertainty and the resulting error regime, making difficulty scheduling largely heuristic.

同时，In parallel,资源匮乏low-resource语音理解仍然是一个持续的挑战speech understanding remains a persistent challenge[9801640]。 许多目标域缺乏足够的配对音频，并且直接基于音频的微调可能会产生不稳定的增益和明显的增益. Many target domains lack sufficient paired audio, and straightforward audio-based fine-tuning can yield unstable gains and noticeable源域降级source-domain degradation [takashima2022updatingencoderspreventscatastrophic, burdisso2026textonlyadaptationllmbasedasr]。 已探索以文本为中心的适应，以减少对配对音频的需求，包括参数高效的调整. Text-centric adaptation has been explored to reduce the need for paired audio, including parameter-efficient tuning[liao2023zero]以及语言组件的纯文本更新and text-only updates of the language component[fang2025low]。 然而，使用纯文本作为训练信号仍然存在与声学解码接口不匹配的问题，并且与 TTS 等更强的基于音频的增强基线相比，其改进可能有限. However, using plain text as the training signal still suffers from a mismatch to the acoustic decoding interface, and its improvements can be limited compared to stronger audio-based augmentation baselines such as TTS[casanova2023asrdataaugmentationlowresource].

为了解决这两个限制，我们建议To address both limitations, we proposeTASU2, 一个, a可控的controllable用于语音 LLM 后期培训的文本到 CTC 模拟框架。 TASU2 不依赖无约束随机模拟，而是生成伪 CTC 后验分布text-to-CTC simulation framework for Speech LLM post-training. This enables a principled curriculum that smoothly varies supervision difficulty and error profiles without TTS or paired audio.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presentedTASU2，一个 WER 可控的文本到 CTC 模拟器，经过分发级监督训练。通过生成更好地匹配声学 CTC 行为的伪后验，TASU2 为语音基础模型和语音 LLM 提供更强的对齐信号。通过评估，它提高了鲁棒性和跨域泛化性，并实现了有效的源到目标的适应。在资源匮乏的目标中，TASU2 的性能优于基于 TTS 的增强，在配对音频稀缺时提供实用的替代方案。, a WER-controllable text-to-CTC simulator trained with distribution-level supervision. By generating pseudo posteriors that better match acoustic CTC behavior, TASU2 provides stronger alignment signals for speech foundation models and Speech LLMs. Across evaluations, it improves robustness and cross-domain generalization, and enables effective source-to-target adaptation. In low-resource targets, TASU2 can outperform TTS-based augmentation, offering a practical alternative when paired audio is scarce.
