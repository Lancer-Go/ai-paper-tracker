摘要Abstract

肌腱驱动的手是拟人化的，并且将执行器从关节上移开使得具有这种功能的手的建造成本低廉。 有两种效果可以产生这种节省。 通过电缆传递力消除了将电机安装在其驱动的关节内的要求，因此更小、更便宜的电机就足够了，并且一个电机可以通过一根电缆驱动多个关节，因此需要更少的电机。 它们也比直接驱动手更难学习。 产生节省的欠驱动传动装置本身很难在模拟器中表示，并且一根电缆驱动的接头不能独立控制。 我们呈现Tendon-driven hands are anthropomorphic, and moving the actuators off the joints is what makes a hand of this capability affordable to build. Two effects produce that saving. Routing force through a cable removes the requirement that a motor fit inside the joint it drives, so smaller and cheaper motors suffice, and one motor can drive several joints through a single cable, so fewer motors are needed. They are also harder to learn on than a direct-drive hand. The underactuated transmission that produces the saving is itself difficult to represent in a simulator, and the joints one cable drives are not independently commandable. We present航空手开式Aero Hand Open，一只肌腱驱动的拟人手，可进行模拟释放。 附带三样东西。 仿真模型再现了电缆传输本身。 识别出的驱动图将该模型连接到两个方向的电机命令，包括拇指的三向耦合。 强化学习包训练手部策略。 他们共同让策略完全在模拟中进行训练，并在没有微调和状态估计的情况下直接运行。 我们发布机械设计、仿真模型、确定的映射、培训环境和部署堆栈。, a tendon-driven anthropomorphic hand that is released simulation-ready. Three things ship with it. A simulation model reproduces the cable transmission itself. An identified actuation map connects that model to the motor commands in both directions, including the three-way coupling of the thumb. A reinforcement learning package trains policies for the hand. Together they let a policy be trained entirely in simulation and run on the hand with no fine-tuning and no state estimation. We release the mechanical design, the simulation model, the identified mapping, the training environment and the deployment stack.

2.1  设计概述2.1  Design overview

Aero Hand Open 是一款模块化、人性化、肌腱驱动的拟人手。它是围绕一个前提而设计的，即电缆驱动的手既可以在模拟中学习，又可以在现场修复。 它有五个手指和十六个旋转关节，测量Aero Hand Open is a modular, human-scale, tendon-driven anthropomorphic hand. It is designed around a single premise, that a cable-driven hand can be made both learnable in simulation and repairable in the field. It has five fingers and sixteen revolute joints, measures198×95×53.5198\times 95\times 53.5毫米，重量mm, and weighs374374g。 七个伺服电机驱动它们，其中十五个关节通过电缆驱动，拇指外展关节直接通过短连杆驱动。 手被故意驱动不足，本体感觉最小，唯一的感应是在七个电机编码器处。 它通过带有四个 M3 螺纹嵌件的平坦手腕侧表面安装到机器人手臂上。 下面的每个设计决策都服务于三个目标之一，即在整个人类抓取范围内的拟人化能力、足够简单以在模拟中忠实再现的传输，以及用户无需供应商即可重新打印和修复的完全 3D 打印结构（图 1）。g. Seven servomotors drive them, fifteen of the joints through cables and the thumb abduction joint directly through a short linkage. The hand is deliberately underactuated and proprioceptively minimal, and the only sensing is at the seven motor encoders. It mounts to a robot arm through a flat wrist-side surface with four M3 threaded inserts. Every design decision below serves one of three goals, namely anthropomorphic capability across the full range of human grasping, a transmission simple enough to reproduce faithfully in simulation, and a fully 3D-printed structure that a user can reprint and repair without a supplier (Fig.2a).
