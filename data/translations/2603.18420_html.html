2.1 主题建模Topic Modelling

潜在狄利克雷分配 (LDA)Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)(Blei et al., 2003)发现主题作为单词的分布，分配主题的文档混合物。神经主题模型和 BERTopicdiscovers topics as distributions over words, assigning documents mixtures of topics. Neural topic models and BERTopic(Grootendorst, 2022)通过预先训练的嵌入来扩展这一点，以增加复杂性为代价产生更连贯的主题。所有主题模型都有一个基本属性：它们按以下方式对文本进行分组：extend this with pre-trained embeddings, producing more coherent topics at the cost of increased complexity. All topic models share a fundamental property: they group text by内容content——哪些单词或嵌入区域在同一文档或段落中同时出现。应用于我们的语料库的主题模型会发现“军事词汇”、“浪漫语言”或“航海术语”等主题。它无法发现的是，一段军事词汇和一段浪漫语言可能具有相同的叙事功能——出发时刻——如果它们在不同的小说中可靠地占据相同的结构位置。—which words or embedding regions co-occur within the same document or passage. A topic model applied to our corpus would discover topics like “military vocabulary,” “romantic language,” or “nautical terminology.” What it cannot discover is that a passage of military vocabulary and a passage of romantic language may serve the same narrative function—the moment of departure—if they reliably occupy the same structural position across different novels.

2.2 计算叙事分析Computational Narrative Analysis

叙事结构的计算方法主要集中在标记框架上。故事语法和情节单元提取Computational approaches to narrative structure have largely focused on labelled frameworks. Story grammars and plot unit extraction(Chambers and Jurafsky, 2008)需要带注释的模式。情绪弧线分析require annotated schemas. Sentiment arc analysis(Reagan et al., 2016; Jockers, 2015)捕捉情感轨迹，但将叙事简化为单一价维度。人物网络分析captures emotional trajectory but reduces narrative to a single valence dimension. Character network analysis(Elson et al., 2010)捕捉关系结构，但不捕捉段落层次的叙事功能。叙事事件链的最新研究captures relational structure but not passage-level narrative function. Recent work on narrative event chains(Chambers and Jurafsky, 2008, 2009)学习典型的事件序列，但在事件级别而不是通道级别进行操作，并且需要事件提取管道。learns typical event sequences but operates at the event level, not the passage level, and requires event extraction pipelines.

Our contribution is a mechanism for learning such functions from data at scale.

预测联想记忆框架The Predictive Associative Memory framework(Dury, 2026b)形式化了基于相似性的检索和基于关联的检索之间的区别。经过时间共现训练的 JEPA 式预测器学习在嵌入空间中导航关联结构，检索与查询同时出现的项目，无论其表示相似性如何。在综合基准上，该预测器在时间关联的排名 1 上实现了 97% 的精度，而跨边界对的余弦相似度得分为零。时间混洗控制确认信号是真正的共现结构。formalises the distinction between similarity-based and association-based retrieval. 3.5 从情景回忆到概念形成From Episodic Recall to Concept Formation

PAM 和概念发现之间的理论关系需要解释。聚丙烯酰胺The theoretical relationship between PAM and concept discovery requires explanation. PAM(Dury, 2026b)专为单次连续体验而设计。代理经历状态流，而预测器则查看存储的经验的完整时间线，了解哪些过去的状态可以从当前状态关联地到达。目标是忠实的情景回忆——从经历的角度记住经历过的事情。在这种情况下，记忆是正确的行为，归纳迁移预计会失败（关联特定于经历过的共现）。was designed for single-pass, sequential experience. An agent experiences a stream of states, and a predictor that looks across the full timeline of stored experience learns which past states are associatively reachable from the current state. The goal is faithful episodic recall—remembering what was experienced, from the perspective at which it was experienced. In that regime, memorisation is correct behaviour and inductive transfer is expected to fail (associations are specific to experienced co-occurrences).

语料库方法以不同的方式运行。文本是固定的且可重播的。多时期训练反复重放相同的时间共现，类似于海马睡眠重放，将事件巩固为稳定的新皮质表征The corpus method operates in a different regime. The text is fixed and replayable. Multi-epoch training replays the same temporal co-occurrences repeatedly, analogous to hippocampal sleep replay consolidating episodes into stable neocortical representations(McClelland et al., 1995; Wilson and McNaughton, 1994)。容量瓶颈——2940 万个参数试图编码 3.73 亿个同现关系——使得完全记忆单个同现关系变得不可行。相反，该模型必须找到规律：在许多书籍中重复出现的过渡结构模式。. The capacity bottleneck—29.4M parameters attempting to encode 373 million co-occurrence relationships—makes full memorisation of individual co-occurrence relations infeasible. Instead, the model must find regularities: patterns of transition structure that recur across many books.

想象一下当“离开的时刻”出现在简·奥斯汀小说、西部片、哥特式恐怖片和哲学论文中时会发生什么。每个实例都会创建将出发通道与其特定邻居连接起来的共现对。在数百个此类实例中，模型无法存储每个单独的映射。它能做的是了解到某些段落——无论其语义内容如何——在过渡空间中占据相似的位置。他们有相似的前辈（制造紧张、说明赌注）和相似的后继者（旅程、新环境、不确定性）。这种共享的关系签名就是我们所说的Consider what happens when a “moment of departure” appears in a Jane Austen novel, a Western, a Gothic horror, and a philosophical essay. Each instance creates co-occurrence pairs linking the departure passage to its specific neighbours. Across hundreds of such instances, the model cannot store each individual mapping. What it can do is learn that certain passages—regardless of their semantic content—occupy a similar position in transition space. They have similar predecessors (building tension, stating stakes) and similar successors (journey, new setting, uncertainty). This shared relational signature is what we call a概念concept——过渡结构的循环模式。 “叙事功能”是许多此类概念（对抗、背离、启示）最直观的标签，但该方法还通过相同的机制发现话语语域、文学传统和场景模板。—a recurrent pattern of transition structure. “Narrative function” is the most intuitive label for many such concepts (confrontation, departure, revelation), but the method also discovers discourse registers, literary traditions, and scene templates through the same mechanism.

这就是 PAM 的情景回忆机制成为概念形成的方式：相同的训练信号（时间共现），不同的压缩机制。在严重的容量限制下，特定的关联被压缩成结构模式。 PAM提供了原理；瓶颈下的对比重播提供了巩固。This is how PAM’s episodic recall mechanism becomes concept formation: same training signal (temporal co-occurrence), different compression regime. Under severe capacity constraint, specific associations get compressed into structural patterns. PAM provides the principle; contrastive replay under bottleneck provides the consolidation.

这也解释了感应传输的分歧。在 AAR 中This also explains the divergence in inductive transfer. In AAR(Dury, 2026a)，段落之间的关联是偶然的——关于昆汀·塔伦蒂诺的段落和关于田纳西州诺克斯维尔的段落之所以相关只是因为它们碰巧回答了同一个问题。不存在任何结构规律可以让模型从看不见的对中预测这种关联。在语料库环境中，转换结构模式是反复出现的——“离开的时刻”在数千本书中出现了数千次。学习了这种结构模式的模型可以在一本看不见的小说中识别它，因为这种模式是文学过渡结构中的循环规律，而不是偶然的共现。, passage-to-passage associations are contingent—a passage about Quentin Tarantino and a passage about Knoxville, Tennessee are associated only because they happen to answer the same question. There is no structural regularity that would let a model predict this association from unseen pairs. In the corpus setting, transition-structure patterns are recurrent—the “moment of departure” appears thousands of times across thousands of books. A model that has learned this structural pattern can recognise it in an unseen novel, because the pattern is a recurrent regularity in literary transition structure, not a contingent co-occurrence.

Any domain meeting these conditions is a candidate—legal case law organised by citation co-occurrence, gene expression profiles organised by regulatory co-occurrence, or user interaction sequences organised by behavioural co-occurrence. 7 局限性Limitations

The concepts are presented as intrinsically interesting structure rather than as features useful for a specific application.

8 结论Conclusion

文本中的时间共现发现了反复出现的过渡结构概念——由哪些段落组织的模式Temporal co-occurrence within texts discovers recurrent transition-structure concepts—patterns organised by what passagesdo在上下文中而不是在他们的内容中in context rather than what they说say。训练一个对比模型，在该模型上，段落出现在其他段落附近，在压缩下强制泛化超越单个共现对，生成跨越 9,766 个文本的跨作者转换结构模式的多分辨率概念图。这些概念涵盖叙事功能、话语语域、文学传统、场景模板和主题惯例。它们与基于相似性的分组有本质上的不同：书籍覆盖范围更加多样化，未见过的小说作业更具选择性，并且按结构角色而不是语义内容进行组织。. Training a contrastive model on which passages appear near which other passages, under compression that forces generalisation beyond individual co-occurrence pairs, produces a multi-resolution concept map spanning cross-author transition-structure patterns over 9,766 texts. These concepts span narrative functions, discourse registers, literary traditions, scene templates, and subject-matter conventions. They are qualitatively different from similarity-based groupings: more diverse in book coverage, more selective in unseen-novel assignment, and organised by structural role rather than semantic content.

该方法不需要标签、流派元数据和主题注释。训练信号是时间共现的；这些概念是从压缩中产生的。当压缩机制与底层模式的规律性相互作用时，PAM 用于情景回忆和 AAR 用于多跳检索的相同架构和训练信号会产生性质不同的行为。The method requires no labels, no genre metadata, and no topic annotations. The training signal is temporal co-occurrence; the concepts emerge from compression. The same architecture and training signal that PAM uses for episodic recall and AAR uses for multi-hop retrieval here yields qualitatively different behaviour when the compression regime interacts with the regularity of the underlying patterns.
