摘要Abstract

分层高斯过滤 (HGF) 网络允许有效更新有关代理环境隐藏状态的后验分布（信念）。 HGF 父节点可以针对其子节点的均值或方差。在输入节点输入的新信息会根据每个节点均值和精度（逆方差）的一步更新方程导致整个网络中的一系列信念更新。然而，方差目标父项（波动性耦合）的更新方程的原始形式可能在参数空间的某些区域中导致负后验精度，这是逻辑上不可能的，导致更新算法因错误而终止。在本报告中，我们引入了对波动耦合节点的变分能量的修改二次近似，以避免负后验精度。关键思想是在变分能量的两个二次展开之间进行插值：一个在先前预测，一个在第二模式，其位置通过兰伯特以封闭形式获得Hierarchical Gaussian Filtering (HGF) networks allow for efficient updating of posterior distributions (beliefs) about hidden states of an agent’s environment. HGF parent nodes can target the mean or variance of their children. New information entering at input nodes leads to a cascade of belief updates across the network according to one-step update equations for each node’s mean and precision (inverse variance). However, the original form of the update equations for variance-targeting parents (volatility coupling) can in some regions of parameter space lead to negative posterior precision, a logical impossibility which causes the updating algorithm to terminate with an error. In this report, we introduce a modified quadratic approximation to the variational energy of volatility-coupled nodes that avoids negative posterior precision. The key idea is to interpolate between two quadratic expansions of the variational energy: one at the prior prediction and one at a second mode whose location is obtained in closed form via the LambertWW功能。由此产生的更新方程在整个参数空间中都是稳健的，即使对于较大的预测误差也能忠实地跟踪变分后验。function. The resulting update equations are robust across the entire parameter space and faithfully track the variational posterior even for large prediction errors.

1 简介Introduction

分层高斯滤波器Hierarchical Gaussian filters(HGFs; Mathys et al.,, 2011, 2014)是节点网络，允许对代理对其环境状态的推断进行建模，代理只能通过输入节点进行的观察或测量来间接访问该环境状态。它们依赖于分层生成模型中贝叶斯推理的变分近似，其中较高级别控制较低级别的波动性（变化率），以精度加权预测误差的形式产生一步更新方程。 HGF 在计算精神病学和认知神经科学中得到了广泛的应用，它们作为个人层面的学习、感知和信念更新模型，其参数可以根据行为数据进行估计are networks of nodes that allow for modelling an agent’s inference on states of its environment to which it has only indirect access through observations or measurements made by input nodes. They rest on a variational approximation to Bayesian inference in a hierarchical generative model where higher levels control the volatility (rate of change) of lower levels, yielding one-step update equations in the form of precision-weighted prediction errors. HGFs have found wide application in computational psychiatry and cognitive neuroscience, where they serve as individual-level models of learning, perception, and belief updating whose parameters can be estimated from behavioural data(e.g., Iglesias et al.,, 2013; Diaconescu et al.,, 2014; Powers et al.,, 2017; Lawson et al.,, 2017; Mikus et al.,, 2024)。它们也被应用于金融时间序列. They have also been applied to financial time series(Mathys et al.,, 2011)并进行主动推理and to active inference(Mathys and Weber,, 2020; Waade et al.,, 2021)。 Julia 包中提供了免费的软件实现. A freely available software implementation is provided in the Julia package分层高斯滤波.jlHierarchicalGaussianFiltering.jl111https://github.com/ComputationalPsychiatry/HierarchicalGaussianFiltering.jl, Python 包, the Python packagepyhgf222https://github.com/ComputationalPsychiatry/pyhgf和 MATLAB HGF 工具箱, and the MATLAB HGF Toolbox333https://github.com/ComputationalPsychiatry/hgf-toolbox，全部作为 TAPAS 的一部分分发, all distributed as part of TAPAS(Frässle et al.,, 2021)。 HGF 最近被推广为包括父节点和子节点之间的非线性值耦合以及现有的波动性耦合. HGFs were recently generalized to include nonlinear value coupling between parent and child nodes alongside the existing volatility coupling(Weber et al.,, 2025)，能够在 HGF 框架内构建预测编码层次结构和深度人工神经网络。, enabling the construction of predictive coding hierarchies and deep artificial neural networks within the HGF framework.

因此，有两种更新方程决定信息如何流经 HGF 网络：价值更新和波动性更新。两者都由一对更新方程组成，一个代表平均值，另一个代表描述节点的滤波器后验分布的高斯精度，并且在这两种情况下，平均值更新都是精度加权的预测误差。虽然这四个更新中的三个没有问题（值耦合的均值和精度更新，以及波动性耦合的均值更新），但波动性耦合的精度更新有时会导致负后验精度，这是逻辑上不可能的，因为高斯的方差必须为正。Accordingly, there are two kinds of update equations that determine how information flows through an HGF network: value updates and volatility updates. Both consist of a pair of update equations, one for the mean, the other for the precision of the Gaussian that describes the filter’s posterior distribution of a node, and in both cases the mean updates are precision-weighted prediction errors. While three of these four updates are unproblematic (mean and precision updates for value coupling, and the mean update for volatility coupling), the precision update for volatility coupling can sometimes give rise to negative posterior precision, which is a logical impossibility because the variance of a Gaussian has to be positive.

负后验精度可能会出现，因为对于节点状态和参数值的某些组合，节点变分能量的二次近似是不合适的。在本报告中，我们首先简要回顾一下 HGF 更新方程的推导方式。然后，我们详细解释原始推导如何以及为何会出现负后验精度。有了这种理解，我们就可以找到一种改进的近似，它不仅解决了负后验精度的问题，而且保留了原始方法的主要特征：对变分能量的二次近似、波动性预测误差以及基于精度加权预测误差的简单更新。新的更新方程使 HGF 网络在整个参数空间范围内保持稳健，允许使用更广泛的模型，并且无需在参数估计期间进行昂贵的边界检查。Negative posterior precision can arise because for some combinations of node states and parameter values, the quadratic approximation to the node’s variational energy is inappropriate. In this report, we first briefly revisit the way the HGF update equations are derived. We then give a detailed explanation of how and why negative posterior precision can arise with the original derivation. Armed with this understanding, we are then in a position to find a modified approximation which not only solves the problem of negative posterior precision but also preserves the main features of the original approach: quadratic approximation to the variational energy, volatility prediction errors, and simple updates on the basis of precision-weighted prediction errors. The new update equations make HGF networks robust across the entire expanse of parameter space, allowing for a wider range of models, and they remove the need for costly boundary checks during parameter estimation.

4 讨论Discussion

本报告中引入的更新方程解决了HGF波动率更新中负后验精度的问题。该解决方案基于三个想法。首先，在计算第一次二次展开式的精度时，我们仅使用变分能量的凹部分，不包括项The update equations introduced in this report solve the problem of negative posterior precision in HGF volatility updates. The solution rests on three ideas. First, when computing the precision of the first quadratic expansion, we use only the concave part of the variational energy, excluding the termJ2J_{2}它负责凸区域。这保证了各处的精确精度。其次，我们通过基于兰伯特的封闭形式近似来定位变分能量的第二模式（当它存在时）which is responsible for the convex region. This guarantees positive precision everywhere. Second, we locate the second mode of the variational energy—when it exists—via a closed-form approximation based on the LambertWW功能function(Corless et al.,, 1996)，避免迭代求根。第三，我们通过softmax加权混合矩匹配将两个二次展开组合成单个高斯后验，它尊重两种模式的相对高度并通过构造产生正精度。, avoiding iterative root-finding. Third, we combine the two quadratic expansions into a single Gaussian posterior through softmax-weighted mixture moment matching, which respects the relative heights of the two modes and produces positive precision by construction.

4.2 对参数估计的影响Implications for parameter estimation

新方程的稳健性对参数估计具有直接的实际影响。当通过最大后验或基于采样的方法将 HGF 模型拟合到行为数据时，优化器会探索参数空间，通过过滤整个输入序列来评估每个候选参数集的可能性。对于原始方程，在过滤过程中即使产生一个负精度实例的参数集也是无效的，从而导致未定义的可能性。这在目标函数中创建了硬边界，基于梯度的优化器很难克服这些边界，并且采样方法必须明确避免这些边界。修改后的方程完全消除了这些边界：每个参数集都会产生有效的过滤轨迹，因此产生明确定义的可能性。这应该使优化更快（在崩溃的参数集上不会浪费评估）和更可靠（没有优化器收敛到边界伪影的风险），并且它使得基于梯度的方法可以访问完整的参数空间。The robustness of the new equations has direct practical consequences for parameter estimation. When fitting HGF models to behavioural data via maximum-a-posteriori or sampling-based methods, the optimizer explores parameter space, evaluating the likelihood at each candidate parameter set by filtering the entire input sequence. With the original equations, parameter sets that produce even a single instance of negative precision during filtering are invalid, leading to undefined likelihoods. This creates hard boundaries in the objective function that gradient-based optimizers struggle with and that sampling methods must explicitly avoid. The modified equations eliminate these boundaries entirely: every parameter set yields a valid filtering trajectory and therefore a well-defined likelihood. This should make optimization both faster (no wasted evaluations at crashed parameter sets) and more reliable (no risk of optimizers converging to boundary artefacts), and it makes the full parameter space accessible to gradient-based methods.
