DA-WAM：驱动世界模型的决策一致的未来潜力DA-WAM: Decision-Aligned Future Latents for Driving World Models

Ruiguo Zhong1, Benshan Ma1, Xiaolong Chen1, Lang Zhang2, Mingyue Feng2, Yaonong Wang2, Pei Liu1, Jun Ma1,3 1The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) 2Leapmotor 3The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology rguo287@connect.hkust-gz.edu.cn Thanks: Project leader. 摘要Abstract

预测场景在自我行为下如何演变是安全自动驾驶的基础，但决策世界模型的全部潜力仍未实现。关键的挑战在于确保未来建模不仅具有预测性，而且具有决策信息性：预测的未来必须直接决定选择哪种轨迹。现有的方法将未来的表示学习与规划优化分离，或者在候选轨迹之间共享预测状态，从而淡化了应该指导选择的特定于动作的结果。 为了弥补这一差距，我们提出了 DA-WAM，这是一个将预测表示学习、动作条件未来建模和单一决策目标下的轨迹评分相结合的框架。 DA-WAM 通过在线编码器和稳定的动量目标在整个规划器优化过程中保持预测监督，从而允许未来的表示与驾驶任务共同进化。动作条件预测器为每个候选轨迹生成一个不同的未来潜在状态，然后由未来潜在条件分解评分器对其进行评估。对于专家匹配的轨迹，预测的未来潜伏由观察到的未来表示来监督，而安全关键的硬否定在规划边界附近提供额外的监督。 NAVSIM-v1 和 NAVSIM-v2 上的大量实验证明了最先进的性能，而消融和诊断分析则验证了关键组件。代码：Anticipating how scenes evolve under ego actions is fundamental to safe autonomous driving, yet the full potential of world models for decision-making remains unrealized. The critical challenge lies in ensuring that future modeling is not merely predictive, but decision-informative: the predicted future must directly shape which trajectory is selected. Existing approaches decouple future representation learning from planning optimization, or share predicted states across trajectory candidates, thereby diluting the action-specific consequences that ought to guide selection. To bridge this gap, we propose DA-WAM, a framework that unifies predictive representation learning, action-conditioned future modeling, and trajectory scoring under a single decision-making objective. DA-WAM maintains predictive supervision throughout planner optimization via an online encoder and a stable momentum target, allowing future representations to co-evolve with the driving task. An action-conditioned predictor generates a distinct future latent state per trajectory candidate, which is then evaluated by a future-latent-conditioned factorized scorer. For the expert-matched trajectory, the predicted future latent is supervised by the observed future representation, while safety-critical hard negatives provide additional supervision near planning boundaries. Extensive experiments on NAVSIM-v1 and NAVSIM-v2 demonstrate state-of-the-art performance, while ablations and diagnostic analyses validate the key components. Code:https://github.com/LeapWM/da-wam.

1 简介Introduction

安全有效的自动驾驶需要推理场景在每个候选自我行为下将如何演变。世界模型通过根据当前观察和建议的行动预测未来的视觉或潜在状态来应对这一挑战Safe and effective autonomous driving requires reasoning about how the scene would evolve under each candidate ego action. World models address this challenge by predicting future visual or latent states from current observations and proposed actions29; 13; 36。然而，根本问题不仅仅是模型是否可以预测未来，而是其预测是否具有决策信息：每个候选动作的预测结果是否直接决定如何评估该轨迹？因此，为了充分发挥世界模型的潜力，未来预测必须与决策紧密结合，以便每个候选者都根据专门针对该行动预测的未来进行评分。. Yet the fundamental question is not simply whether a model can predict the future, but whether its predictions are decision-informative: do the predicted consequences of each candidate action directly determine how that trajectory is evaluated? To realize the full potential of world models, future prediction must therefore be tightly coupled with decision making, such that each candidate is scored against the future predicted specifically for that action.

现有的方法沿着两个大方向追求未来预测，但这两个方向都不能单独保证这种耦合。 DriveWorld 等方法Existing approaches pursue future prediction along two broad directions, but neither direction alone guarantees this coupling. Methods such as DriveWorld19, 法律, LAW13, 驱动器-JEPA, Drive-JEPA28，和潜在的WAM, and Latent-WAM27使用时间预测主要是为了加强场景表示或轨迹学习。尽管这些方法学习了丰富的预测特征，但预测预训练和规划器优化通常是分阶段的；因此，固定或冻结的编码器无法使其未来的表示适应轨迹评分的特定要求。第二条工作线将预测的未来更直接地纳入规划中。驱动器WMuse temporal prediction primarily to strengthen scene representations or trajectory learning. Although these approaches learn rich predictive features, predictive pretraining and planner optimization are often stage-separated; fixed or frozen encoders consequently cannot adapt their future representations to the specific demands of trajectory scoring. A second line of work brings predicted futures more directly into planning. The scorer may therefore rely primarily on geometric cues rather than the scene-conditioned future content that distinguishes safe from unsafe outcomes.

我们认为，世界模型的规划价值取决于其预测对候选级别评分的直接影响程度。根据每个候选者自己预测的未来来评估每个候选者，将使规划者在区分几何相似但安全关键的轨迹时，能够利用特定于行动的后果，例如碰撞、车道偏离或违反交通规则。实现这种能力需要克服两个相互关联的障碍。在表示层面，预测特征必须在规划器优化期间继续适应，以便学习到的未来结构与规划目标保持一致。在规划层面，评分者必须评估每个候选人的独特未来潜在状态；共享或共同的未来掩盖了应该指导选择的特定行动的后果。We argue that the planning value of a world model is bounded by how directly its predictions influence candidate-level scoring. Evaluating each candidate against its own predicted future would allow the planner to exploit action-specific consequences, such as collisions, lane departures, or traffic-rule violations, when distinguishing geometrically similar yet safety-critical trajectories. Realizing this capability requires overcoming two interconnected barriers. At the representation level, predictive features must continue to adapt during planner optimization so that the learned future structure remains aligned with the planning objective. At the planning level, the scorer must evaluate a distinct future latent state for each candidate; shared or pooled futures obscure the action-specific consequences that should guide selection.

为此，我们提出 DA-WAM，一种决策一致的世界行动模型。 DA-WAM 通过 LoRA 适应的视频联合嵌入预测架构 (V-JEPA) 2.1 在线编码器与指数移动平均 (EMA) 目标编码器配对，在整个规划器优化过程中保持预测监督，确保未来的表示与驾驶任务共同演化，而不是在预训练后被冻结。动作条件预测器通过显式场景轨迹交互为每个候选轨迹生成不同的未来潜在状态，而因子化未来潜在条件评分器则联合评估每个候选者及其相应的潜在状态。由于离线日志仅为执行的专家轨迹提供观察到的未来，因此密集的 JEPA 监督适用于专家匹配的候选者，而安全关键的硬负例则在规划边界附近提供额外的本地比较。这些硬否定在几何上与专家匹配的候选者相似，但在安全结果上有所不同，阻止评分者仅依赖几何。To this end, we propose DA-WAM, a decision-aligned world action model. DA-WAM maintains predictive supervision throughout planner optimization via a LoRA-adapted Video Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (V-JEPA) 2.1 online encoder paired with an exponential moving average (EMA) target encoder, ensuring that future representations co-evolve with the driving task rather than being frozen after pretraining. An action-conditioned predictor generates a distinct future latent state for every trajectory candidate through explicit scene–trajectory interaction, and a factorized future-latent-conditioned scorer evaluates each candidate jointly with its corresponding latent state. Because offline logs provide observed futures only for the executed expert trajectory, dense JEPA supervision is applied to the expert-matched candidate, while safety-critical hard negatives supply additional local comparisons near planning boundaries. These hard negatives are geometrically similar to the expert-matched candidate but differ in safety outcomes, discouraging the scorer from relying on geometry alone.

这些方法背后的一个基本矛盾是预测表示的通用性和轨迹评分的特定任务需求之间的权衡。现有方法通常通过冻结预训练、多级管道或推理时间去除来解决这种权衡，这可能会削弱未来动态与策略优化之间的耦合。相比之下，我们在整个规划器优化过程中保持预测性 JEPA 监督。这使得未来的潜在表示能够与评分目标共同进化，并在推理过程中充当直接条件输入，而不是辅助训练时特征。A fundamental tension underlying these approaches is the trade-off between the generality of predictive representations and the task-specific needs of trajectory scoring. Existing methods typically address this trade-off through frozen pretraining, multistage pipelines, or inference-time removal, which can weaken the coupling between future dynamics and policy optimization. In contrast, we maintain predictive JEPA supervision throughout planner optimization. This enables future latent representations to co-evolve with the scoring objective and serve as direct conditioning inputs during inference rather than auxiliary training-time features.

2.2 动作条件驾驶世界模型Action-Conditioned Driving World Models

认识到视觉环境在不同的自我操纵下会发生不同的演变，最近的世界模型明确地根据假设的行动或轨迹建议来预测未来。驱动器WMRecognizing that visual environments evolve differently under different ego maneuvers, recent world models explicitly condition future predictions on hypothetical actions or trajectory proposals. World4Drive36预测多个意图引导的潜在未来，并使用内部评估器验证路径。同时，驱动未来forecasts multiple intention-guided latent futures and validates paths using an internal evaluator. Concurrently, DriveFuture8, 偶像, IDOL35、和液晶驱动器, and LCDrive25分别使用未来潜在的扩散指导、逆动力学细化和潜在的思想链推理。use future latents for diffusion guidance, inverse-dynamics refinement, and latent chain-of-thought reasoning, respectively.

虽然这些工作验证了动作调节的前提，但它们通常会汇总、汇集或弱融合跨提案的预测未来。因此，候选评估者收到同质化的场景表示，稀释了每个轨迹特有的细粒度、安全关键的后果。我们通过建立明确的一对一对应关系来弥补这一差距：DA-WAM 为每个候选轨迹生成一个独特的未来潜在状态，并将其直接输入评分器，从而允许轨迹选择使用特定于候选者的反事实证据。While these works validate the premise of action conditioning, they often aggregate, pool, or weakly fuse the predicted futures across proposals. Consequently, the candidate evaluator receives a homogenized scene representation, diluting the fine-grained, safety-critical consequences specific to each trajectory. We bridge this gap by establishing an explicit one-to-one correspondence: DA-WAM generates a distinct future latent state for every candidate trajectory and feeds it directly into the scorer, allowing trajectory selection to use candidate-specific counterfactual evidence.

2.3 候选生成和轨迹评分Candidate Generation and Trajectory Scoring

基于候选的规划器生成多个看似合理的轨迹，并根据场景上下文和规划目标选择最佳轨迹。 DiffusionDrive 通过锚引导截断扩散有效地模拟多模式动作分布Candidate-based planners generate multiple plausible trajectories and select the optimal one according to scene context and planning objectives. DiffusionDrive efficiently models multimodal action distributions through anchor-guided truncated diffusion18。 DrivoR 将多摄像头功能压缩为摄像头感知的寄存器标记，并使用单独的转换器解码器来生成候选对象并对其进行评分. DrivoR compresses multi-camera features into camera-aware register tokens and uses separate transformer decoders to generate and score candidates11。 GTRS 和 ZTRS 将候选评估制定为显式轨迹评分. GTRS and ZTRS formulate candidate evaluation as explicit trajectory scoring17; 16。除了几何和基于场景的评分之外，DriveSuprim 还采用从粗到细的轨迹选择来区分硬负轨迹. Beyond geometric and scene-based scoring, DriveSuprim employs coarse-to-fine trajectory selection to distinguish hard-negative trajectories34，BeyondDrive 构建安全关键、专家近似的硬底例来学习轨迹空间中的安全边界, and BeyondDrive constructs safety-critical, expert-proximate hard negatives to learn safety boundaries in trajectory space26.

这些工作极大地推进了候选轨迹的生成和评估。尽管如此，评分信号仍然主要锚定在当前场景几何形状和即时运动模式中。预测的未来很少以明确的、针对每个候选人的方式纳入评分器中，尽管它们有可能区分几何相似但安全关键的候选人。我们在已建立的基于候选的规划范式内进行操作，并用动作条件的未来潜在状态来增强评分器，从而能够根据预测的结果而不是仅基于当前的几何形状来选择轨迹。These works have significantly advanced both the generation and evaluation of trajectory candidates. Nonetheless, the scoring signal remains predominantly anchored in current scene geometry and immediate motion patterns. Predicted futures are rarely incorporated into the scorer in an explicit, per-candidate manner, despite their potential to distinguish geometrically similar but safety-critical candidates. We operate within the established candidate-based planning paradigm and augment the scorer with action-conditioned future latent states, thereby enabling trajectory selection based on predicted consequences rather than current geometry alone.

5 结论Conclusion

当世界模型的预测直接影响轨迹选择时，它对于自动驾驶来说是最有价值的。 DA-WAM 通过学习未来表征和驾驶任务、预测每个候选轨迹的不同未来以及根据相应结果评估每个候选轨迹，缩小了预测和规划之间的差距。专家匹配的监督使未来的学习与观察到的数据保持一致，而安全关键的硬否定则改善了规划边界附近的歧视。 NAVSIM-v1 和 NAVSIM-v2 的实验取得了有竞争力的结果，消融研究为主要设计选择的贡献提供了证据。 总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，仅预测合理的未来可能不足以进行有效的规划。预测还应该与正在评估的行动相对应，并有助于最终决策。通过加强这种联系，DA-WAM 提供了一种实用的方法，使未来的建模与轨迹规划更加相关。World models are most valuable for autonomous driving when their predictions directly inform trajectory selection. DA-WAM closes the gap between prediction and planning by learning future representations together with the driving task, predicting a distinct future for each candidate trajectory, and evaluating every candidate against its corresponding outcome. Expert-matched supervision keeps future learning consistent with the observed data, while safety-critical hard negatives improve discrimination near planning boundaries. Experiments on NAVSIM-v1 and NAVSIM-v2 achieve competitive results, and ablation studies provide evidence for the contribution of the main design choices. Overall, our findings suggest that predicting a plausible future alone may not be sufficient for effective planning. The prediction should also correspond to the action being evaluated and contribute to the final decision. By strengthening this connection, DA-WAM offers a practical approach to making future modeling more relevant to trajectory planning.
