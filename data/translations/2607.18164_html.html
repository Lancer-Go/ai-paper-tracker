摘要Abstract

数字孪生依靠代理模型来实时反映物理系统，但这些模型可能会随着操作条件的变化而退化，这种现象称为概念漂移。在漂移下保持代理保真度，特别是当模型还必须捕获任意不确定性时，仍然是一个开放的挑战。现有的自适应框架缺乏原则性的机制来检测何时需要更新、根据有限的流数据有效地调整模型以及证明更新确实可以提高预测性能。在这里，我们提出了一个自适应数字孪生框架，它集成了基于 Fisher 评分的多元漂移检测器、用于参数高效持续学习的低秩适应 (LoRA) 和 Mann–WhitneyDigital Twins rely on surrogate models to mirror physical systems in real time, yet these models can degrade as operating conditions evolve, a phenomenon known as concept drift. Maintaining surrogate fidelity under drift, particularly when models must also capture aleatoric uncertainty, remains an open challenge. Existing adaptive frameworks lack principled mechanisms for detecting when updates are needed, for efficiently adapting models from limited streaming data, and for certifying that updates genuinely improve predictive performance. Here we present an adaptive Digital Twin framework that integrates a Fisher score–based multivariate drift detector, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) for parameter-efficient continual learning, and a Mann–WhitneyUU测试在线统计验证。该框架通过 Fisher 得分向量监控代理模型置信度，在检测到漂移时触发对不到 1% 的模型参数进行有针对性的微调，并在部署更新的代理之前对预测性改进进行统计证明。该框架应用于随机线性系统和定向能量沉积增材制造工艺作为案例研究，成功地检测到短延迟的分布变化，并在突然和增量漂移下恢复预测准确性和不确定性量化。这些结果建立了一条统计上严格且计算上易于处理的途径，用于维持基于神经网络的数字孪生在整个运行生命周期的可信度。test for online statistical validation. The framework monitors surrogate-model confidence via Fisher score vectors, triggers targeted fine-tuning of fewer than 1% of model parameters upon drift detection, and statistically certifies predictive improvement before deploying the updated surrogate. Applied to a stochastic linear system and a directed energy deposition additive manufacturing process as case studies, the framework successfully detects distributional shifts with short delays and restores both predictive accuracy and uncertainty quantification under abrupt and incremental drift. These results establish a statistically rigorous and computationally tractable pathway for sustaining the trustworthiness of neural-network–based Digital Twins throughout their operational life cycle.

1.3.1 灾难性遗忘和持续学习Catastrophic Forgetting and Continual Learning

一旦检测到漂移，使虚拟孪生适应新的分布必须适应不断变化的动态，同时避免过度拟合有限的流数据和有用的先前结构的不必要的损失Once drift is detected, adapting the virtual twin to a new distribution must accommodate evolving dynamics while avoiding overfitting to limited streaming data and undesirable loss of useful prior structure[desale2023concept, kirkpatrick2017overcoming, aleixo2023catastrophic]。灾难性遗忘是在持续学习下研究的，其中正则化、重放、优化和基于表示的方法可以减轻遗忘。这些大多保持跨多个任务的性能，而在概念漂移的情况下，目标是覆盖过时的知识以实现可塑性。在这里，为了适应新概念而“忘记”意味着删除仍然相关的结构表示（即物理或预训练的网络作为主干）并降低鲁棒性。. Catastrophic forgetting is studied under continual learning, where regularization-, replay-, optimization-, and representation-based methods mitigate forgetting. These mostly preserve performance across multiple tasks, whereas under concept drift, the aim is rather to overwrite outdated knowledge for plasticity. Here, “forgetting” for the purpose of adapting to new concepts means erasing still-relevant structural representations (i.e., the physics or the pre-trained network as the backbones) and degrading robustness.

PEFT methods adapt large NNs by updating only a subset or a low-dimensional subspace of the parameters, reducing computational cost while improving training stability. Among these, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)[hu2022lora]是一种代表性的基于重新参数化的技术，它引入与预训练权重并行的可训练低秩矩阵，提供参数效率、低推理延迟和稳定的优化。尽管它们在大规模学习任务中取得了成功，但据我们所知，此类方法在基于神经网络的动力系统模型自适应和数字孪生实时决策中的应用尚未得到研究。is a representative reparameterization-based technique that introduces trainable low-rank matrices in parallel to the pretrained weights, offering parameter efficiency, low inference latency, and stable optimization. Despite their success in large-scale learning tasks, to the best of our knowledge, the application of such methods to NN-based model adaptation for dynamical systems and the real-time decision-making of Digital Twins has not yet been investigated.

1.3.2 MPC 和数字孪生的适应Adaptation in MPC and Digital Twins

从控制设计的角度来看，自适应 MPC 可以通过三种主要形式实现From a control-design perspective, adaptive MPC can be realized in three principal forms[sun2024adaptive]。第一个通过在线系统识别直接更新预测模型. The first updates the predictive model directly via online system identification[na2020output]。第二个调整控制器参数，例如调整非线性 MPC 中的权重矩阵. The second adapts controller parameters, such as tuning the weighting matrices in nonlinear MPC[kostadinov2020online]或分析调整控制范围or analytically adjusting the control horizon[turki2017analytical]，无需修改底层模型。第三种将 RL 与 MPC 集成，其中 RL 在线调整控制参数，同时保留 MPC 结构, without modifying the underlying model. The third integrates RL with MPC, where RL tunes the control parameters online while preserving the MPC structure[sun2024adaptive, vrabie2009neural, chaubey2026adaptive]。相关方法进一步将离线训练的深度强化学习与非线性MPC相结合来校准模型参数，确保数字系统和物理系统之间的同步. In this work, the focus is placed exclusively on model-level adaptation, as it directly determines the fidelity between the virtual twin and the physical system.

模型级自适应通常通过优化来估计不确定性下的参数Model-level adaptation typically estimates parameters under uncertainty via optimization[zhang2025self]，递归最小二乘法, recursive least squares[heirung2017dual], 卡尔曼滤波, Kalman filtering[bouffard2012learning, dutta2021new, chen2021unknown]，或基于散度的修正, or divergence-based correction[huang2023novel]，通过增材制造应用校准与温度相关的特性, with AM applications calibrating temperature-dependent properties[van2025real]或库普曼模型or Koopman models[chen2025adaptive]。然而，朴素的递归估计存在不稳定和对流数据过度拟合的风险，只能产生局部准确的模型，而不是全局模型。. Naive recursive estimation, however, risks instability and overfitting to streaming data, yielding only locally accurate models rather than global ones.

为了减轻过度拟合和遗忘，有几种策略限制对特定组件的更新：物理的残差学习-数据差异To mitigate overfitting and forgetting, several strategies constrain updates to specific components: residual learning of physics–data discrepancies[halaly2024continuous, jiahao2023online, chee2022knode, hewing2019cautious],逐层在线更新, layer-wise online updating[gasparino2023unmatched]和自适应偏差校正, and adaptive bias correction[jiahao2023online]。这些方法都通过保留预训练结构并最小化可调参数的数量来微调模型，突出了数据和参数高效微调的重要性。受这些工作的推动，目前的工作主张将基于 PEFT 的方法集成到数字孪生适应中，特别是当神经网络用作代理时，从有限的流数据中实现受控、高效和鲁棒的模型更新，同时平衡可塑性和稳定性。. These methods all fine-tune the model by preserving the pretrained structure and minimizing the number of tunable parameters, highlighting the importance of data- and parameter-efficient fine-tuning. Motivated by these works, the present work advocates integrating PEFT-based methods into Digital Twin adaptation, particularly when NNs are used as surrogates, enabling controlled, efficient, and robust model updates from limited streaming data while balancing plasticity and stability.

1.4 更新成功了吗？Is the Update Successful?

在安全关键和高后果的系统中，模型更新必须伴随系统验证，以确保适应真正提高而不是降低预测和控制性能。在值得信赖的科学机器学习背景下，验证和验证 (V&V) 不被视为一次性过程，而是跨越问题制定、模型开发、部署和领域转移的可信度评估的迭代过程In safety-critical and high-consequence systems, model updating must be accompanied by systematic validation to ensure that adaptation truly improves, rather than degrades, predictive and control performance. In the context of trustworthy scientific machine learning, verification and validation (V&V) are regarded not as one-time procedures but as iterative processes of credibility assessment spanning problem formulation, model development, deployment, and domain shift[jakeman2025verification]。特别是，训练和操作领域之间的分布转变需要持续验证以维持模型的可靠性。. In particular, distributional shift between the training and operational domains necessitates continuous validation to maintain model reliability.

然而，大多数作品都是离线或回顾性地评估更新，例如通过保留的迁移学习性能Yet most works evaluate updates offline or retrospectively, e.g. via held-out transfer-learning performance[bao2020online]或事后分类测试or post-hoc classification tests[trovato2026continual]，它对准确性进行基准测试，但不能保证闭环系统中的实时更新或保证部署期间的稳定性和约束满足。一个值得注意的例外是基于 Lyapunov 的 MPC, which benchmark accuracy but do not safeguard real-time updating in closed-loop systems or guarantee stability and constraint satisfaction during deployment. Overall, real-time validation mechanisms that assess model performance after updating, at test time, remain scarce. Developing principled criteria to certify the success of model updates in Digital Twin systems is therefore an important research direction.
