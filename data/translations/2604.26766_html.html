用于可靠临床分诊的领域适应小语言模型Domain-Adapted Small Language Models for Reliable Clinical Triage

Manar Aljohani    PhD1    Brandon Ho    MD2    Kenneth McKinley    MD2    Dennis Ren    MD2    Xuan Wang    PhD1

摘要Abstract

准确且一致的紧急严重程度指数 (ESI) 分配仍然是急诊科面临的一个持续挑战，其中高度可变的自由文本分类文档会导致误诊和工作流程效率低下。本研究评估开源小语言模型 (SLM) 是否可以作为临床分诊的可靠、保护隐私的决策支持工具。我们系统地比较了不同提示管道中的多个 SLM，发现临床片段、分诊叙述的简明摘要可以产生最准确的预测。 SLM Qwen2.5-7B 展示了准确性、稳定性和计算效率的最强平衡。通过使用专家策划和银标准儿科分诊数据进行大规模领域适应，经过微调的 Qwen2.5-7B 模型大大减少了不一致和临床重大错误，优于所有基线 SLM 和高级专有大语言模型（LLM，例如 GPT-4o）。这些发现强调了机构特定的 SLM 提供可靠、保护隐私的 ESI 决策支持的可行性，并强调了有针对性的微调相对于更复杂的推理策略的重要性。Accurate and consistent Emergency Severity Index (ESI) assignment remains a persistent challenge in emergency departments, where highly variable free-text triage documentation contributes to mistriage and workflow inefficiencies. This study evaluates whether open-source small language models (SLMs) can serve as reliable, privacy-preserving decision-support tools for clinical triage. We systematically compared multiple SLMs across diverse prompting pipelines and found that clinical vignettes, concise summaries of triage narratives, yielded the most accurate predictions. The SLM, Qwen2.5-7B, demonstrated the strongest balance of accuracy, stability, and computational efficiency. Through large-scale domain adaptation using expert-curated and silver-standard pediatric triage data, fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B models substantially reduced discordance and clinically significant errors, outperforming all baseline SLMs and advanced proprietary large language models (LLMs, e.g., GPT-4o). These findings highlight the feasibility of institution-specific SLMs for reliable, privacy-preserving ESI decision support and underscore the importance of targeted fine-tuning over more complex inference strategies.

简介Introduction

分类准确性和变异性。Triage Accuracy and Variability.及时、准确的分诊是急诊科 (ED) 运营的基石，影响患者的治疗结果、资源分配和整体效率。紧急严重程度指数 (ESI) 是一种广泛使用的五级分诊系统，根据病情严重程度和预期资源需求对患者进行分类，范围从 ESI 1（立即挽救生命干预）到 ESI 5（无预期资源）。 ESI 2 包括没有立即复苏需求的高风险患者，而 ESI 3-5 主要通过预期资源使用来区分：ESI 3 表示患者可能需要两种或多种资源，ESI 4 表示预计需要单一资源的患者Timely and accurate triage is a cornerstone of emergency department (ED) operations, influencing patient outcomes, resource allocation, and overall efficiency. The Emergency Severity Index (ESI) is a widely used five-level triage system that classifies patients by acuity and expected resource needs, ranging from ESI 1 (immediate life-saving intervention) to ESI 5 (no resources anticipated). ESI 2 includes high-risk patients without immediate resuscitation needs, while ESI 3–5 are primarily distinguished by expected resource use: ESI 3 indicates patients likely to require two or more resources, and ESI 4 those expected to require a single resource5。在实践中，ESI 分配通常是主观且可变的，特别是在工作量大或信息有限的情况下，研究仅报告. In practice, ESI assignment is often subjective and variable, especially under high workload or limited information, with studies reporting only∼\sim30–50% 正确分类率30–50% correct triage rates18。例如，一项多中心研究发现，使用 ESI v4 进行的儿科急诊就诊中只有 34.1% 的分配正确. For example, one multicenter study found correct assignment in just 34.1% of pediatric ED visits using ESI v413，而另一项成人 ED 研究报告了误诊, while another adult ED study reported mistriage in∼\sim仅 32% 的高危患者的遭遇和敏感性32% of encounters and sensitivity for high-acuity patients of only∼\sim66% 14。一项国际多中心评估报告的准确率接近. These misclassifications can delay life-saving care or overburden emergency resources, highlighting the need for reliable data-driven triage support systems.

最近关于自动化 ESI 预测的工作已经从使用结构化特征的传统机器学习模型发展到结合自由文本分类注释的深度学习方法。早期的方法使用 LSTM 等循环神经网络来模拟临床叙述Recent work on automating ESI prediction has progressed from traditional machine learning models using structured features to deep learning methods incorporating free-text triage notes. Early approaches employed recurrent neural networks such as LSTMs to model clinical narratives9，其次是基于 Transformer 的编码器，例如 BERT 和 ClinicalBERT，它们通过大规模预训练和特定领域的适应来提高性能, followed by transformer-based encoders such as BERT and ClinicalBERT, which improved performance through large-scale pretraining and domain-specific adaptation2。大型语言模型 (LLM) 的出现标志着下一阶段：GPT-3 等模型可扩展到数十亿个参数，并实现强大的零样本和少样本推理. The emergence of large language models (LLMs) marks the next stage: models like GPT-3 scale to billions of parameters and enable strong zero-shot and few-shot reasoning3，而 Med-PaLM 等医学法学硕士在没有广泛的特定任务监督的情况下表现出强大的临床理解, while medical LLMs such as Med-PaLM demonstrate robust clinical understanding without extensive task-specific supervision15。这些进步使法学硕士成为上下文感知 ESI 预测的有前途的工具，特别是当带注释的分类数据有限且需要临床推理时。. These advances position LLMs as promising tools for context-aware ESI prediction, particularly when annotated triage data is limited and clinical reasoning is required.

法学硕士分类的机遇和挑战。Opportunities and Challenges of LLMs for Triage.大语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展展示了分析复杂的非结构化临床叙述的新机会，例如分诊笔记或患者小插图Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated new opportunities to analyze complex unstructured clinical narratives, such as triage notes or patient vignettes15; 12; 10。法学硕士在提取临床特征、推理患者叙述和生成结构化输出方面表现出强大的能力，同时也引起了对幻觉、鲁棒性和领域可靠性的重要担忧. LLMs show strong abilities in extracting clinical features, reasoning over patient narratives, and generating structured outputs while also raising important concerns about hallucination, robustness, and domain reliability19; 1。这些优势表明，通过标准化 ESI 分配、减少认知负荷和及早识别高危患者来协助分诊具有潜在价值。. These strengths suggest potential value in assisting triage by standardizing ESI assignments, reducing cognitive load, and identifying high-risk patients earlier.

然而，在临床环境中部署专有的法学硕士仍然具有挑战性。这些模型通常是封闭式系统，需要外部 API 调用，从而引发 HIPAA 问题，并且需要大量计算资源，从而阻碍本地部署However, deploying proprietary LLMs in clinical environments remains challenging. These models are typically closed-box systems, require external API calls that raise HIPAA concerns, and demand substantial compute resources that hinder on-premise deployment17; 12。重要的是，它们的输出在重复运行中往往不一致，这对于安全关键任务（例如需要稳定性、可重复性和透明度的分类）来说是有问题的. Critically, their outputs are often inconsistent across repeated runs, which is problematic for safety-critical tasks such as triage that require stability, reproducibility, and transparency6。因此，尽管临床推理能力很强，但这些限制仍会减慢现实世界的操作速度。. As a result, these limitations slow real-world operationalization despite strong clinical reasoning capabilities.

小语言模型作为解决方案。Small Language Models as a Solution.开源Open-source小语言模型Small Language Models（SLM），通常(SLMs), typically≤\leq7B 参数，为专业、资源受限和隐私敏感的医疗保健环境提供了一个有前途的替代方案7B parameters, offer a promising alternative for specialized, resource-constrained, and privacy-sensitive healthcare environments16; 4。量化的最新进展. Recent advances in quantization4，参数高效微调（PEFT）, parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT)7，以及LoRA和QLoRA等低秩自适应方法, and low-rank adaptation methods such as LoRA and QLoRA4实现高效的领域适应，同时保持有竞争力的准确性。 SLM 具有多种优势：它们具有成本效益、可解释且可适应机构特定的分诊数据，而无需暴露患者信息enable efficient domain adaptation while maintaining competitive accuracy. SLMs provide several advantages: they are cost-efficient, interpretable, and adaptable to institution-specific triage data without exposing patient information4; 7; 16。他们的开放架构支持可重复性、透明评估和社区驱动的改进。由于 SLM 足够轻量，适合机构防火墙后面的本地部署，因此它们支持严格的隐私要求、减少推理延迟并实现版本控制的可重复性。值得注意的是，与大型法学硕士相比，SLM 通常会为面向规则的临床决策支持任务产生更稳定的输出，特别是在对结构化或基于指南的工作流程进行微调时。在医疗应用中，这些特性使 SLM 能够实现强大的域内性能，并且比 LLM 显着降低内存、计算和操作开销，从而非常适合可靠且保护隐私的紧急分诊系统。. Their open architectures support reproducibility, transparent evaluation, and community-driven improvement. Because SLMs are lightweight enough for on-premise deployment behind institutional firewalls, they support strict privacy requirements, reduce inference latency, and enable version-controlled reproducibility. Notably, SLMs often produce more stable outputs for rule-oriented clinical decision-support tasks than larger LLMs, especially when fine-tuned on structured or guideline-based workflows. In medical applications, these properties allow SLMs to achieve strong in-domain performance with significantly lower memory, compute, and operational overhead than LLMs, making them well-suited for reliable and privacy-preserving emergency triage systems.

之前的工作已经探索了类似的策略，使通用变压器适应医学问答Prior work has explored similar strategies for adapting general-purpose transformers to medical question answering15、笔记总结, note summarization8，以及放射学报告生成, and radiology report generation19。然而，很少有研究针对基于规则的结构化决策支持任务（例如安全性、隐私性和可解释性至关重要的临床 ESI 分类分配）检验 SLM 的微调。为了弥补这一差距，我们系统地评估了多个开源 SLM 框架，并根据去识别化的回顾性急诊科分诊数据微调了 7B 参数 SLM (Qwen2.5-7B)。我们评估了经过微调的 Qwen2.5-7B 模型在多大程度上可以近似人类 ESI 决策并分析其错误模式（分类不足、过度分类），以确定临床部署中信任感知改进的领域。我们的目标是评估小型、透明的语言模型作为紧急分诊临床决策支持的值得信赖的替代方案的可行性和准确性（图. However, few studies have examined the fine-tuning of SLMs for structured rule-based decision-support tasks such as clinical ESI triage assignment where safety, privacy, and interpretability are critical. To address this gap, we systematically evaluated multiple open-source SLM frameworks and fine-tuned a 7B-parameter SLM (Qwen2.5-7B) on de-identified retrospective emergency department triage data. We assessed the extent to which a fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B model can approximate human ESI decision-making and analyze its error patterns (undertriage, overtriage) to identify areas for trust-aware improvement in clinical deployment. Our objective was to assess the feasibility and accuracy of small, transparent language models as a trustworthy alternative for clinical decision support in emergency triage (Figure1).

材料与方法Materials & Methods

数据源。Data Sources.

数据集来自于The dataset was obtained from国立儿童医院 (CNH)Children’s National Hospital (CNH)急诊科 (ED) 记录。对约 117,600 名儿科患者的初始队列进行了筛选，以创建包含每次急诊就诊的关键分诊变量的结构化数据集。每条记录都包含去识别化的患者索引、临床工作人员指定的紧急严重程度指数 (ESI)、患者年龄（以月为单位）以及描述所提出投诉的非结构化自由文本分类注释。该数据集还包括哈希患者参考号 (FIN) 和记录的emergency department (ED) records. An initial cohort of approximately 117,600 pediatric encounters was screened to create a structured dataset containing key triage variables for each ED visit. Each record includes a de-identified patient index, the Emergency Severity Index (ESI) assigned by clinical staff, patient age in months, and the unstructured free-text triage note describing the presenting complaint. The dataset also includes a hashed patient reference number (FIN) and the documented主诉Chief Complaint，总结护理人员或患者报告并由分诊护士记录的急诊就诊的主要原因。其他字段可捕获生命体征（例如心率、体温、呼吸频率）和体检结果。当生命体征不可用时，枢轴评估字段提供补充观察。最后，每条记录都包含分诊过程中记录的患者既往病史 (PMH)。, summarizing the primary reason for the ED visit as reported by the caregiver or patient and recorded by the triage nurse. Additional fields capture vital signs (e.g., heart rate, temperature, respiratory rate) and physical exam findings. When vital signs were unavailable, a pivot assessment field provided supplemental observations. Finally, each record contains the patient’s past medical history (PMH) as documented during triage.

我们的数据集的包含/排除标准。Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria for Our Dataset.我们纳入了到儿科急诊科就诊的 0-21 岁个体的遭遇，这些个体接受了指定 ESI 水平的标准护理分流。在某些情况下，相遇被排除在外。首先，删除了患者绕过标准分诊的案例，例如由于高危、运输团队交接或复苏舱激活而直接送至房间的情况，因为大多数 ESI 1 患者会立即接受医生评估，而无需自行决定 ESI。其次，排除了到达前激活的情况（例如 EMS 警报、创伤或重症监护激活或来自转诊提供者的转移通知），因为这些事件预先确定了患者的安置并在标准分诊工作流程之外添加了信息。最后，主要的心理或行为健康表现被排除在外，因为它们遵循独特的分类途径，强调行为安全性，而不是生理敏锐度或基于资源的 ESI 决策。We included encounters for individuals aged 0–21 years presenting to the pediatric emergency department who underwent standard nursing triage with an assigned ESI level. Encounters were excluded under several conditions. First, cases in which patients bypassed standard triage—such as direct-to-room placement due to high acuity, transport team handoff, or resuscitation bay activation—were removed, as most ESI 1 patients receive immediate physician evaluation without a discretionary ESI determination. Second, encounters with pre-arrival activation (e.g., EMS alerts, trauma or critical care activations, or transfer notifications from referring providers) were excluded because these events predetermine patient placement and add information outside the standard triage workflow. Finally, primary mental or behavioral health presentations were excluded because they follow a distinct triage pathway emphasizing behavioral safety rather than physiologic acuity or resource-based ESI decisions.

数据过滤和管理。Data Filtering and Curation.

为了确保数据完整性和临床相关性，我们创建了一个经过过滤的遭遇子集（To ensure data completeness and clinical relevance, we created a filtered subset of encounters (n=353n=353）通过保留具有有意义的临床信息的记录。仅当主诉字段包含实质性临床内容而不是占位符文本（例如“参见枢轴”、“参见分类”或“无”）时，才会包含遭遇。生命体征字段需要有效的生理测量，体检字段必须包含记录的临床观察结果，而不是空条目。仅在提供临床相关补充信息时才保留“枢轴评估”字段，而当生命体征可用时，该字段通常不存在。记录还需要有效的既往病史 (PMH) 条目，不包括那些标记为“无”的条目。这种过滤确保每次遭遇都包含足够的结构化和非结构化信息，以支持可靠的语言模型提示和 ESI 预测。需要完整的数据使我们能够在受控条件下评估模型性能，并避免异构缺失造成的混淆。未来的工作将通过对演示中可用的不完整和不断变化的数据进行建模，将这种方法扩展到现实世界的分类设置。) by retaining records with meaningful clinical information. Encounters were included only if the Chief Complaint field contained substantive clinical content rather than placeholder text (e.g., “see pivot,” “see triage,” or “none”). Requiring complete data allowed us to evaluate model performance under controlled conditions and avoid confounding from heterogeneous missingness. Future work will extend this approach to real-world triage settings by modeling incomplete and evolving data available at presentation.

局限性和结论Limitations and Conclusion

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，它依赖于回顾性单中心儿科急诊数据，这可能会限制对其他机构、患者群体或分诊工作流程的推广。其次，评估需要相对完整的记录（主诉、生命体征和体检），而现实世界的分诊记录往往很少；因此，报告的业绩可能代表在更清晰的文件条件下的估计上限。第三，由于替代分类途径和非标准文件，主要的心理和行为健康表现和 ESI 1 遭遇被排除在外，从而降低了对这些患者群体的适用性。This study has several limitations. First, it relied on retrospective single-center pediatric ED data, which may limit generalizability to other institutions, patient populations, or triage workflows. Second, evaluation required encounters with relatively complete documentation (chief complaint, vital signs, and physical exam), whereas real-world triage notes are often sparse; therefore, reported performance likely represents an upper-bound estimate under cleaner documentation conditions. Third, primary mental and behavioral health presentations and ESI 1 encounters were excluded due to alternative triage pathways and nonstandard documentation, reducing applicability to these patient groups.

第四个限制是使用护士指定的 ESI 水平作为参考标准。现实世界的分诊显示出很大的可变性，并且可能偏离基于指南的护理；例如，研究地点的 ESI 3 过度分类率通常超过 25%。因此，在这些标签上训练的模型可能会重现现有的实践模式，从而难以区分模型错误和人类分类决策的可变性。我们小组未来的工作旨在通过开发以结果为锚定的分诊基准数据集来解决这一限制，该数据集使用客观的临床结果和资源利用率而不是仅使用护士分配的标签来评估分诊准确性。最后，本研究没有评估前瞻性部署或下游临床影响。A fourth limitation is the use of nurse-assigned ESI levels as the reference standard. Real-world triage demonstrates substantial variability and may deviate from guideline-based care; for example, ESI 3 overtriage rates at the study site routinely exceed 25%. Models trained on these labels may therefore reproduce existing practice patterns, making it difficult to distinguish model error from variability in human triage decisions. Future work from our group aims to address this limitation through the development of an outcome-anchored triage benchmark dataset that evaluates triage accuracy using objective clinical outcomes and resource utilization rather than nurse-assigned labels alone. Finally, this study did not evaluate prospective deployment or downstream clinical impact.

尽管存在这些局限性，我们的结果表明，开源小语言模型在根据机构特定的儿科分诊数据进行仔细微调时，可以显着提高自动 ESI 分配的准确性、稳定性和安全性。 Qwen2.5-7B 提供了最有利的性能和效率组合，优于更大的模型、简单检索和多智能体变体，同时保持适合实际临床工作流程的亚秒级推理时间。这些发现表明，轻量级、隐私保护和可重复的 SLM 代表了实现可扩展的分类决策支持的实用途径。未来的研究应该将评估范围扩展到嘈杂的实时分诊记录，包括当前被排除的患者群体，纳入以结果为锚定的基本事实，并评估临床医生的循环表现，以更好地描述基于 SLM 的分诊系统的操作影响。Despite these limitations, our results demonstrate that open-source small language models, when carefully fine-tuned on institution-specific pediatric triage data, can substantially improve the accuracy, stability, and safety of automated ESI assignment. Qwen2.5-7B offered the most favorable combination of performance and efficiency, outperforming larger models, naïve retrieval, and multi-agent variants while maintaining sub-second inference times suitable for real-world clinical workflows. These findings suggest that lightweight, privacy-preserving, and reproducible SLMs represent a practical pathway toward scalable triage decision support. Future research should extend evaluation to noisier real-time triage notes, include currently excluded patient populations, incorporate outcome-anchored ground truth, and assess clinician-in-the-loop performance to characterize better the operational impact of the SLM-based triage systems.
