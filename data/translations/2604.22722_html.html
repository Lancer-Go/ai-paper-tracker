摘要。Abstract.

1. 简介Introduction

密集向量相似性搜索仍然是现实世界检索增强生成（RAG）系统的基石。通过将查询和候选映射到共享表示空间，这些系统利用高效的近似最近邻 (ANN) 搜索以最小的延迟处理大规模数据集Dense vector similarity search remains the bedrock of real-world Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. By mapping queries and candidates into a shared representation space, these systems leverage efficient Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) search to handle large-scale datasets with minimal latency(Es et al., 2024; Cuconasu et al., 2024; Huly et al., 2024)。然而，这种范式因其依赖语义相似性作为生成效用的代理而受到越来越多的批评。越来越多的证据表明，具有高语义相似度（主题重叠）的段落通常无法提供答案关键信息，甚至可能引入误导生成器的语义干扰因素，特别是在长上下文环境中，不正确但相似的段落会增加解码的不确定性. However, this paradigm is increasingly criticized for its reliance on semantic similarity as a proxy for generative utility. Growing evidence suggests that passages with high semantic similarity (topical overlap) often fail to provide answer-critical information and can even introduce semantic distractors that mislead the generator, especially in long-context settings where incorrect but similar passages increase decoding uncertainty(Cuconasu et al., 2024; Yoran et al., 2024; Jin et al., 2025; Tian et al., 2025).

为了弥补这一差距，当前最先进的方法转向基于实用程序的检索To bridge this gap, current state-of-the-art approaches shift toward utility-based retrieval(Jiang et al., 2025; Dai et al., 2025; Tian et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2025)，其中相关性是由文档帮助大型语言模型 (LLM) 生成正确响应的有效性来定义的, where relevance is defined by how effectively a document helps a Large Language Model (LLM) produce a correct response(Huly et al., 2024)。在实践中，这通常是通过降低复杂度来衡量的：如果文档作为上下文的存在使模型更容易预测真实答案，则该文档被认为是有用的. In practice, this is often measured via perplexity reduction: a document is considered useful if its presence as context makes the ground-truth answer more predictable to the model(Jiang et al., 2025)。虽然概念上合理，但这些基于实用程序的方法面临着巨大的实用性障碍。依赖 LLM 进行查询生成或事后重新排名在计算上对于大规模部署来说是令人望而却步的. While conceptually sound, these utility-based approaches face a significant practicality wall. Relying on LLMs for query generation or post-hoc re-ranking is computationally prohibitive for large-scale deployment(Salemi and Zamani, 2024; Ke et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2025)。此外，从困惑中得出的效用信号是众所周知的噪声和随机性，对令牌级别的变化和解码动态敏感，这使得它们难以用作稳定的训练目标. Furthermore, utility signals derived from perplexity are notoriously noisy and stochastic, sensitive to token-level variations and decoding dynamics that make them difficult to use as stable training targets(Tian et al., 2026; Holtzman et al., 2020)。这需要复杂的多级架构来提高性能，但代价是极端的推理延迟和高计算开销. This necessitates complex, multi-stage architectures that improve performance at the cost of extreme inference latency and high computational overhead(Shi et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025).

在这项工作中，我们提出了效用对齐嵌入（UAE），这是一个旨在将密集检索的效率与基于效用的方法的卓越性能相结合的框架。我们的核心见解是，生成效用应该直接提取到双编码器（双编码器密集检索器）的嵌入空间中，绕过昂贵的测试时间 LLM 推理，同时捕获生成器的实际偏好。我们将这种对齐表述为分布匹配问题；而不是将嘈杂的效用分数视为直接回归目标（这可能导致不稳定的学习和过度拟合In this work, we propose Utility-Aligned Embeddings (UAE), a framework designed to merge the efficiency of dense retrieval with the superior performance of utility-based methods. Our core insight is that generative utility should be distilled directly into the bi-encoder’s (dual-encoder dense retriever) embedding space, bypassing expensive test-time LLM inference while capturing the generator’s actual preferences. 4. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了效用对齐嵌入（UAE），这是一个通过分布匹配弥合密集检索效率和基于效用模型高性能之间差距的框架。我们的结果表明，UAE 在运行时显着减少了语义干扰并提高了发电质量 180We present Utility-Aligned Embeddings (UAE), a framework that bridges the gap between the efficiency of dense retrieval and the high performance of utility-based models through distribution matching. Our results demonstrate that UAE significantly reduces semantic distractors and improves generation quality while operating 180×\times比基于 LLM 的重新排名方法更快。通过保持标准的 ANN 兼容性并作为多级管道的高质量基础，UAE 为公用事业驱动的 RAG 系统提供了实用且可扩展的解决方案。faster than LLM-based re-ranking methods. By maintaining standard ANN compatibility and serving as a high-quality foundation for multi-stage pipelines, UAE provides a practical and scalable solution for utility-driven RAG systems.
