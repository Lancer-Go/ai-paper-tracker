DLMs are commonly formulated in one of two ways: continuous or discrete. 5 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced嵌入式语言流程Embedded Language Flows（ELF），一种连续扩散语言模型，它使用连续时间流匹配在连续嵌入空间中制定语言生成。与之前的 DLM 相比，ELF 保持去噪轨迹连续，并且仅在最后一步应用离散化，从而能够直接适应连续扩散模型的技术。根据经验，与领先的离散 DLM 和现有的连续 DLM 相比，ELF 在语言生成任务中实现了强大的质量效率权衡，通过更少的采样步骤和更少的训练标记实现了更低的生成困惑。这些结果表明连续 DLM 仍然是基于扩散的语言建模的一个有前途的方向。(ELF), a continuous diffusion language model that formulates language generation in continuous embedding space using continuous-time Flow Matching. In contrast to prior DLMs, ELF keeps the denoising trajectory continuous and applies discretization only at the final step, enabling straightforward adaptation of techniques from continuous diffusion models. Empirically, compared with leading discrete DLMs and existing continuous DLMs, ELF achieves a strong quality–efficiency trade-off across language generation tasks, attaining lower generative perplexity with fewer sampling steps and fewer training tokens. These results suggest that continuous DLMs remain a promising direction for diffusion-based language modeling.
