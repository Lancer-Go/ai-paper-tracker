摘要Abstract—对遗留的 Fortran 进行现代化是一个数量问题：转换是单独例行的，但代码库可能非常庞大，并且在许多计算科学中，工作根本就没有完成。我们提出了一种代理工作流程，可以在生产规模上开展这项工作，并且我们开始衡量这种委托可以达到多远。在这项工作中，三个提示专用代理角色在代理自己编写和修订的版本控制规范下运行，而人类则拥有少量的门。该安排通过从域继承的精确验证预言机来保证安全，并且安全委托的边界恰好位于该预言机停止看到的地方。—Modernizing legacy Fortran is a problem of volume: the transformations are individually routine, but the codebases can be enormous, and across much of computational science the work simply goes undone. We propose an agentic workflow that takes this work on at production scale, and we set out to measure how far such delegation can reach. In this work, three prompt-specialized agent roles operate under a versioncontrolled specification that the agents themselves authored and revised, while humans hold a small number of gates. The arrangement is kept safe by an exact verification oracle inherited from the domain, and the boundary of safe delegation lies exactly where that oracle stops seeing.

我们在案例研究中应用了所提出的工作流程，将具有 48 年开发历史的成熟量子化学软件包 GAMESS（通用原子和分子电子结构系统）的二电子积分例程从固定形式的 Fortran 77 转换为自由形式的 Fortran 2008。这项工作的范围是 12 个源文件、56,448 行和 225 个用于计算电子斥力积分的子例程。这些代理在独立的工作树中作为三个 Claude 代码角色运行，并且工作跨越了四代 Claude 模型。由于 GAMESS 小组发布了一个标准测试套件，其打印能量被用户社区视为规范，因此我们可以采用这些能量的逐位再现作为合并标准，其中小数点后第 12 位的偏差将被视为失败而不是漂移。所有 12 个源文件都通过了 51 项测试验证，其中包括 49 项标准 GAMESS 测试和两项附加计算，并且在 612 次测试运行中，与化学相关的差异数量为零，并且每个文件还通过了用于持续集成的 Jenkins 测试。We apply the proposed workflow in a case study, converting the two-electron-integral routines of GAMESS (General Atomic and Molecular Electronic Structure System), a mature quantum-chemistry package with a 48-year development history, from fixed-form Fortran 77 to free-form Fortran 2008. The scope of this work was twelve source files, 56,448 lines, and 225 subroutines for computing electron repulsion integrals. The agents ran as three Claude Code roles in isolated worktrees, and the work spanned four Claude model generations. Because the GAMESS group ships a standard test suite whose printed energies its user community treats as canonical, we could adopt bit-for-bit reproduction of those energies as the merge criterion, where a deviation in the twelfth decimal place counts as a failure rather than drift. All twelve source files pass a 51-test validation battery comprising the 49 standard GAMESS tests and two additional calculations, and across 612 test runs the number of chemistry-relevant differences is zero, and every file also passes the Jenkins tests that are used for continuous integration.

The language has grown across a long line of standards, from FORTRAN 66 and 77 through Fortran 90, 95, 2003, 2008, and 2018, each adding what the previous generation lacked: free-form source, modules, array operations, object orientation, and parallelism. Yet the standards remain largely backwardcompatible, and much of the deployed code never moved forward with them. A great deal of it is still fixed-form FORTRAN 77, a style many scientific packages kept long after Fortran 90 made it optional. These legacy codes carry numerical methods that current science depends on while obstructing the tooling, threading, and accelerator work that future science requires. Rewriting them in a modern standard is conceptually unremarkable and practically unbounded: the transformations are individually routine, the codebases are enormous, and the work has therefore gone undone across much of computational science.

因此，对传统 Fortran 进行现代化是一个数量问题，而不是难度问题，而这正是代理人工智能（通过自动循环驱动的强大前沿模型）现在可以吸收的数量问题。读取文件、重写文件、编译文件、运行测试并迭代直至测试通过的代理可以降低每个文件的人力成本，从而降低未完成的工作。这使得主体现代化成为一个有吸引力的答案，并提出了本文的核心问题：此类工作实际上可以委派的程度以及限制它的因素。但是，转换数万行密集积分代码并不能消除瓶颈，而是重新定位瓶颈：没有人会读取机器编写的结果并手动检查它。问题是如何有人知道重写是错误的。对于量子化学内核来说，错误并不意味着崩溃。它可能意味着自洽场能量的小数点后第十二位中的一个默默不同的数字，并在几年后传播到公布的结果中。因此，对委托的限制不是由代理可以写入多少内容来决定，而是由可以检查多少内容来决定。在目前的情况下，这一限制是良性的，因为遗留下来的Modernizing legacy Fortran, then, is a problem of volume rather than difficulty, and it is exactly the kind of volume that agentic AI, capable frontier models driven through automated loops, can now absorb. An agent that reads a file, rewrites it, compiles it, runs a test, and iterates until the test passes drives down the per-file human cost that left the work undone. That makes agentic modernization an attractive answer, and it raises the central question of this paper: the extent to which such work can actually be delegated, and the factors that bound it. But converting tens of thousands of lines of dense integral code does not so much remove the bottleneck as relocate it: no one will read the machine-written result to check it by hand. The question is how anyone would know the rewrite was wrong. For a quantum-chemistry kernel, being wrong need not mean crashing. It can mean a silently different digit in the twelfth decimal place of a self-consistent-field energy, propagating into a published result years later. 我们的设置不同于上述所有工作，包括 Fortran 特定的工作，在我们认为尚未充分探索的三个轴上。首先，转换是语言和语义保留的：它是从 F77 到 F2008，而不是到 C++ 或 Kokkos 的移植，因此必须准确保留原始数值行为而不是重新表达。其次，接受标准是精确的数字再现，而不是这些努力所采用的功能或单元测试正确性，这很重要，因为看似合理的错误答案可以通过测试套件，但无法再现十二位数字的能量。第三，工作单元是一百万行调用图中的一个文件，其其他调用者不能与它一起修改（也参见 Kokkos [28]）。Our setting differs from all of the aforementioned work, including the Fortran-specific efforts, on three axes we think are underexplored. First, the transformation is in-language and semantics-preserving: it is F77 to F2008, not a port to C++ or Kokkos, so the original numerical behavior must be retained exactly rather than re-expressed. Second, the acceptance criterion is exact numerical reproduction rather than the functional or unit-test correctness these efforts adopt, which matters because a plausible-looking wrong answer can pass a test suite but cannot reproduce a twelve-digit energy. 六．结论VI. Conclusion

这项研究评估了受监督的代理工作流程是否可以在生产规模上维持遗留科学代码的现代化。 GAMESS 的双电子积分核心（12 个文件、56,448 行、225 个子例程）提供了严格的测试，工作流程几乎完全通过代理将其从 Fortran 77 转换为 Fortran 2008，每个文件都复制发布到最后打印数字的测试输出，并通过持续集成测试套件。This study evaluated whether a supervised agentic workflow could sustain legacy scientific-code modernization at production scale. The two-electron-integral core of GAMESS (twelve files, 56,448 lines, 225 subroutines) provided a demanding test, and the workflow converted it from Fortran 77 to Fortran 2008 almost entirely through agents, with every file reproducing the test outputs published to the last printed digit and passing the continuous integration test suite.

这项研究的贡献不在于现代化的代码，而在于对如何委派此类工作以及委派多远的说明。工作流本身是代理编写和修订的版本控制规范下的三个提示专业角色，将项目积累的能力保留在该规范中，而不是任何模型中。在转换中，机械转换与需要理解的部分完全分开，并且代理在后者中赢得了自己的位置，因为基于规则的变压器正是在这些点上失败的。值得注意的是，验证主要是由代理人进行的，而不是对他们进行的，并且可以从记录中读取由此产生的边界：代理人在没有帮助的情况下完成的工作，他们漏掉的一个缺陷类别，他们的差异检查无法捕获的逃逸，以及人类判断仍然至关重要的少数点。The contribution of this study lies less in the modernized code than in an account of how such work can be delegated, and how far. The workflow itself, three prompt-specialized roles under a version-controlled specification that the agents wrote and revised, keeps the project-accumulated competence in that specification rather than in any model. Within a conversion, the mechanical transformations separate cleanly from the parts that require comprehension, and the agents earn their place in the latter, since a rulebased transformer fails at exactly those points. Verification, notably, was carried out largely by the agents rather than performed on them, and the resulting boundary can be read from the record: the work the agents completed unaided, the one defect class that slipped past them, the escape their differential check could not have caught, and the handful of points at which human judgment stayed essential.

在所有这一切之下运行的是一个单一的启用属性：严格的检查，继承自将其发布的能量视为规范的域。这种检查比代理人的能力更重要，它决定了授权可以安全到达的距离，而传统的科学软件比通常假设的更容易提供此类检查。进一步推进该方法主要是规模和覆盖范围的问题：将现代化的文件集成在一起而不是一次一个，运行 GAMESS 提供的扩展测试套件，将工作流程扩展到大约百万行包的其余部分，并最终将 COMMON 块转换为单个文件更改无法到达的模块，从而实现完全现代化的 GAMESS。Running beneath all of this was a single enabling property: a strict check, inherited from a domain that treats its published energies as canonical. That check, more than the agents’ capability, set how far delegation could safely reach, and legacy scientific software supplies such checks more readily than is often assumed. Carrying the approach further is largely a matter of scale and coverage: integrating the modernized files together rather than one at a time, running the extended test suites GAMESS provides, extending the workflow across the rest of the roughly million-line package, and eventually converting the COMMON blocks into modules that a single-file change cannot reach, toward a fully modernized GAMESS.
