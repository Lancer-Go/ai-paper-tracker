摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在不同的任务中表现出卓越的性能，但它们经常生成看似合理但实际上不正确的响应。由于缺乏明确的不确定性估计，这个问题变得更加复杂，这使得用户很难判断模型输出的可靠性。现有的不确定性量化方法通常依赖于间接信号，例如采样代之间的熵。这些信号可能难以解释，并且无法充分利用模型评估其自身不确定性的能力。 我们提出了一种简单而有效的自我评估方法，用于法学硕士不确定性量化。我们的方法将采样的世代分组为语义上不同的簇，将它们转换为结构化多项选择问题中的答案选项，并使用法学硕士分配给每个选项的概率作为置信度估计。跨多个模型和数据集的实验表明，我们的方法始终优于基线方法。值得注意的是，它仅用两个额外样本即可实现具有竞争力的性能，证明了其有效性和效率。Large language models (LLMs) demonstrate remarkable performance across diverse tasks, but they often generate responses that appear plausible while being factually incorrect. This problem is compounded by the lack of explicit uncertainty estimates, which makes it difficult for users to judge the reliability of model outputs. Existing uncertainty quantification methods typically rely on indirect signals, such as entropy across sampled generations. These signals can be difficult to interpret and do not fully leverage the model’s ability to assess its own uncertainty. We propose a simple yet effective self-assessment method for uncertainty quantification in LLMs. Our approach groups sampled generations into semantically distinct clusters, converts them into answer options in a structured multiple-choice question, and uses the probability assigned by the LLM to each option as a confidence estimate. Experiments across multiple models and datasets show that our method consistently outperforms baseline approaches. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在不同的应用程序中取得了显着的成功。然而，确保其输出的可靠性和事实准确性仍然具有挑战性，特别是在医疗保健、法律和科学研究等高风险领域，这些领域的错误可能会造成严重后果。这个问题因他们能够生成流畅、有说服力的文本而变得更加复杂，这些文本可能会掩盖不准确之处并误导用户Large language models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable success across diverse applications. However, ensuring the reliability and factual accuracy of their outputs remains challenging, especially in high-stakes domains such as healthcare, law, and scientific research, where errors can have serious consequences. This issue is compounded by their ability to produce fluent, persuasive text that may obscure inaccuracies and mislead usersJi et al. (2023); Huang et al. (2025).

为了减轻这些风险，开发有效的方法来量化和传达法学硕士产出的不确定性至关重要。一种自然的方法是让法学硕士用自然语言表达不确定性；然而，之前的研究表明，他们经常表现出过度自信，即使在不正确的情况下也会给出过度自信的评估To mitigate these risks, it is crucial to develop effective methods for quantifying and communicating uncertainty in LLM-generated outputs. A natural approach is to let LLMs express uncertainty in natural language; however, prior work shows that they often exhibit overconfidence, giving overly confident assessments even when incorrectXiong et al. (2024); Kadavath et al. (2022)。因此，研究人员探索了替代方案，例如估计多代语义分歧的不确定性. Researchers have therefore explored alternatives, such as estimating uncertainty from semantic divergence across multiple generationsKuhn et al. (2023); Lin et al. (2024)。虽然部分有效，但这些方法主要衡量产出差异，没有利用法学硕士对其知识的内部理解. While partially effective, these methods primarily measure output discrepancies without leveraging the LLMs’ internal understanding of their knowledgeKadavath et al. (2022)，导致性能不佳。此外，许多方法依赖于间接不确定性度量，例如基于熵的分数, leading to suboptimal performance. Moreover, many methods rely on indirect uncertainty metrics, such as entropy-based scoresKuhn et al. (2023)，这对用户来说很难解释，从而限制了实际用途, which are difficult for users to interpret and thus limit practical utilityDevic et al. (2025).

因此，迫切需要利用法学硕士的内部能力，同时清晰传达模型置信度的不确定性量化方法。为此，我们提出了一种简单而有效的方法来满足这些要求。我们的主要贡献如下：Therefore, there is a pressing need for uncertainty quantification methods that leverage the internal capabilities of LLMs while clearly communicating model confidence. To this end, we propose a simple yet effective method that fulfills these requirements. Our main contributions are as follows:

引发自我评估。Eliciting self-assessment.我们的方法通过使用抽样代来构建结构化多项选择问题，从而利用法学硕士的内部不确定性表示，从而促使模型评估自己的答案。Our approach leverages the internal uncertainty representations of LLMs by using sampled generations to construct structured multiple-choice questions, thereby prompting the model to evaluate its own answers.

性能优越。Superior performance.我们的方法在不同的数据集和模型中始终优于现有基线。值得注意的是，它只需要两个额外的样本即可实现竞争性能，而某些基线需要多达十六个样本，这凸显了其有效性。Our method consistently outperforms existing baselines across diverse datasets and models. Notably, it achieves competitive performance with only two additional samples, whereas some baselines require up to sixteen, highlighting its effectiveness.

人类可解释性。Human-interpretability.我们使用每个选择的标记概率作为置信度得分，使用户能够直观地评估生成答案的可靠性。实验结果表明，这些置信度分数与实际正确性非常吻合，证明了我们方法的实用性。We use the token probability of each choice as a confidence score, enabling users to intuitively assess the reliability of generated answers. Experimental results show that these confidence scores align well with actual correctness, demonstrating the practicality of our method.

3.2 自我评估Self-Assessment

我们使用聚集的答案来构建一个结构化的多项选择问题（MCQ），该问题具有语义上不同的选择，其中每个选择对应一个答案集群。除了从答案簇中得出的选择之外，我们还包括一个附加选项“以上都不是”，以考虑所有选择可能不正确的情况，或者当法学硕士对任何提供的选择的正确性表现出高度不确定性时。然后将构建的 MCQ 呈现给原始 LLM，分配给特定选择的令牌概率作为人类可解释的置信度得分。We use the clustered answers to construct a structured multiple-choice question (MCQ) with semantically distinct choices, where each choice corresponds to one answer cluster. In addition to the choices derived from the answer clusters, we include an additional option, “None of the above”, to account for cases where all choices may be incorrect or when the LLM exhibits high uncertainty regarding the correctness of any provided choices. The constructed MCQ is then presented to the original LLM, and the token probability assigned to a specific choice serves as a human-interpretable confidence score.

5 结论Conclusion

我们呈现We present集群式自我评估Clustered Self-Assessment，一种简单而有效的不确定性量化方法，通过对采样答案进行聚类以构建具有语义不同选择的 MCQ 来增强 LLM 自我评估。跨不同模型和数据集的实验表明，我们的方法始终优于现有基线。进一步的分析表明，所得到的置信度分数经过良好校准，可用于训练预测 LLM 隐藏状态不确定性的探针，突出了我们方法的有效性和实用性。, a simple yet effective method for uncertainty quantification that enhances LLM self-assessment by clustering sampled answers to construct MCQs with semantically distinct choices. Experiments across diverse models and datasets show that our approach consistently outperforms existing baselines. Further analysis demonstrates that the resulting confidence scores are well calibrated and can be used to train a probe that predicts uncertainty from LLM hidden states, highlighting the effectiveness and practicality of our method.

6 局限性Limitations

我们的方法有一些局限性，这些局限性表明了未来改进的方向。Our method has several limitations that suggest directions for future improvement.

首先，它需要访问输出 logits 来计算不确定性得分。这种访问并不总是可用，特别是对于闭源模型来说，这限制了我们的方法在某些部署场景中的适用性。First, it requires access to output logits to compute the uncertainty score. Such access is not always available, particularly for closed-source models, which limits the applicability of our approach in certain deployment scenarios.

其次，我们的方法依赖于外部 NLI 模型来对生成的答案进行聚类。这种依赖引入了额外的计算开销并降低了独立性。此外，外部 NLI 模型可能难以捕获细微的语义或有效处理分布外的情况。未来工作的一个有希望的方向是利用 LLM 本身的内部表示进行答案聚类，因为 LLM 通常比较小的 NLI 模型编码更丰富的语义信息。集成此功能可以提高计算效率和不确定性量化的鲁棒性。Second, our method depends on an external NLI model to cluster generated answers. This reliance introduces additional computational overhead and reduces self-containment. Moreover, the external NLI model may struggle to capture nuanced semantics or handle out-of-distribution cases effectively. A promising direction for future work is to leverage the internal representations of the LLM itself for answer clustering, as LLMs typically encode richer semantic information than smaller NLI models. Integrating this capability could improve both computational efficiency and the robustness of uncertainty quantification.

最后，我们的方法直接使用 LLM 的 softmax 概率作为置信度分数，而不应用事后校准。虽然该设计已经实现了强大的性能，但结合校准步骤可以进一步增强置信估计的可靠性和可解释性，从而获得更加一致和值得信赖的结果。Finally, our method directly uses the LLM’s softmax probabilities as confidence scores without applying post-hoc calibration. While this design already achieves strong performance, incorporating a calibration step could further enhance the reliability and interpretability of the confidence estimates, leading to more consistent and trustworthy results.
