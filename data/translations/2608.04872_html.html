A-SR：通过层次协调进行符号回归的自我进化代理法学硕士A-SR: Self-Evolving Agentic LLMs for Symbolic Regression via Hierarchical Coordination

Wenxiao Zhao1,2, Dong Liu2, Kaiyi Xu1, Feng Liu1, Zhen Zhao1, Fei Ben1,
Shu Wang2, Wenhao Li3, Yingnian Wu2, Fenghua Ling1, Haobo Li1, Lei Bai1
摘要Abstract

符号回归旨在从数据中发现封闭式方程，但现有的法学硕士指导方法通常依赖于统一的提案循环，将异构搜索失败压缩为标量分数和单个提示。我们提出了 A-SR，一种自我进化的代理框架，它将控制单元从表达编辑转变为角色条件证据视图。 A-SR 通过协调协议、在线评估者奖励角色策略和状态路由过程内存之间的路由来协调公式发现。在搜索过程中，评估者反馈用于表征可靠性和生产力，更新角色级实用程序，并将精英主题、故障跟踪和有效性诊断路由到不同的代理。该框架在两个时间尺度上自我进化：在一次运行中，它调整搜索过程而不更新LLM参数；在运行过程中，记录的轨迹可以作为角色条件提案先验提炼成开源 LLM。对 LLM-SRBench 中的四个 LSR-Synth 科学领域进行平均，A-SR 将 Llama3.1-8B 的 Acc@0.01 相对于基线的结果从 25.79% 提高到 48.30%，而 A-SR-LoRA 将相应的 Qwen3-4B 结果从 24.58% 提高到 38.29%。在四项现实世界的科学发现任务中，A-SR 在 8 个报告指标中的 7 个上获得了最佳 ID/OOD NMSE。Symbolic regression aims to discover closed-form equations from data, yet existing LLM-guided methods often rely on a unified proposal loop that compresses heterogeneous search failures into a scalar score and a single prompt. We propose A-SR, a self-evolving agentic framework that shifts the control unit from expression edits to role-conditioned evidence views. A-SR coordinates formula discovery through routing among coordination protocols, online evaluator-reward role policy, and state-routed process memory. During search, evaluator feedback is used to characterize reliability and productivity, update role-level utilities, and route elite motifs, failure traces, and validity diagnostics to different agents. The framework self-evolves at two timescales: within a run, it adapts the search process without updating LLM parameters; across runs, recorded trajectories can be distilled into open-source LLMs as role-conditioned proposal priors. Averaged over the four LSR-Synth scientific domains in LLM-SRBench, A-SR improves Acc@0.01 over baselines from 25.79% to 48.30% with Llama3.1-8B, while A-SR-LoRA improves the corresponding Qwen3-4B result from 24.58% to 38.29%. On four real-world scientific discovery tasks, A-SR obtains the best ID/OOD NMSE on 7 of 8 reported metrics.

1 简介Introduction

科学发现通常寻求紧凑的数学定律，这些定律对于观测数据来说是准确的，可以作为符号表达式执行，并且在外推下是稳健的Scientific discovery often seeks compact mathematical laws that are accurate on observed data, executable as symbolic expressions, and robust under extrapolation(Bongard and Lipson 2007; Schmidt and Lipson 2009; Udrescu and Tegmark 2020; Cranmer et al. 2020)。符号回归 (SR) 通过搜索封闭式表达式来实现这一目标。之前的 SR 系统通过遗传编程、受物理启发的方程发现、神经引导表达式生成和混合进化神经搜索来实现这种搜索，包括 Eureqa、AI Feynman、PySR、DSR/uDSR 和基于 Transformer 的 SR 模型. Symbolic regression (SR) addresses this goal by searching for closed-form expressions. Prior SR systems have approached this search through genetic programming, physics-inspired equation discovery, neural-guided expression generation, and hybrid evolutionary-neural search, including Eureqa, AI Feynman, PySR, DSR/uDSR, and transformer-based SR models(Schmidt and Lipson 2009; Udrescu and Tegmark 2020; Cranmer 2023; Petersen et al. 2021; Landajuela et al. 2022; Biggio et al. 2021; Kamienny et al. 2022)。然而，实际的 SR 系统必须处理无效的程序、不稳定的参数化、冗余项和不良的域外行为。这些失败不仅仅是一个目标的不同程度：它们需要不同的证据和纠正措施，但许多系统将它们压缩为一种标量奖励和一种建议提示。. However, practical SR systems must handle invalid programs, unstable parameterizations, redundant terms, and poor out-of-domain behavior. These failures are not merely different degrees of one objective: they require different evidence and corrective actions, yet many systems compress them into one scalar reward and one proposal prompt.

最近的法学硕士引导的符号回归方法表明，语言模型为方程发现提供了有用的科学和程序先验Recent LLM-guided symbolic regression methods show that language models provide useful scientific and programmatic priors for equation discovery(Shojaee et al. 2025a; Grayeli et al. 2024; Shojaee et al. 2025b)。通过将方程表示为可执行程序，法学硕士可以提出方程骨架，同时评估器可以拟合连续参数并对候选人进行评分. By representing equations as executable programs, an LLM can propose equation skeletons while an evaluator fits continuous parameters and scores candidates(Chen et al. 2021; Austin et al. 2021; Kwon et al. 2023)。尽管如此，许多系统仍然依赖于统一的提案评估循环：法学硕士提出候选人，评估者返回分数，随后的提示对公式进行细化或重新采样。这样的循环暴露了有限的流程结构，并且经常难以决定何时探索、修复无效性、简化或验证。我们的搜索日志直接显示了这种不匹配：失败经常持续存在，因为下一个提案收到与当前失败模式不匹配的证据，例如当主要问题是无效、参数不稳定或冗余项时寻求新颖的结构。. Nevertheless, many systems still rely on a unified proposal-evaluation loop: an LLM proposes a candidate, an evaluator returns a score, and subsequent prompts refine or resample formulas. Such a loop exposes limited process structure and often struggles to decide when to explore, repair invalidity, simplify, or validate. Our search logs show this mismatch directly: failures often persist because the next proposal receives evidence mismatched to the current failure mode, such as seeking novel structure when the dominant issue is invalidity, parameter instability, or redundant terms.

最近的代理方法通过规划、工具、反射、记忆和结构化交互协议将提案与导航分开Recent agentic approaches separate proposal from navigation through planning, tools, reflection, memory, and structured interaction protocols(Yao et al. 2023b; Schick et al. 2023; Wu et al. 2023; Li et al. 2023)。在 LLM 引导的 SR 中，刻意进化 (DE) 采用符号编辑运算符、诊断和反射记忆来指导父表达式如何细化、变异、交叉或重新生成. In LLM-guided SR, Deliberate Evolution (DE) adapts symbolic edit operators, diagnostics, and reflective memory to steer how parent expressions are refined, mutated, crossed over, or regenerated(Pang et al. 2026)。然而，操作员级别的适应决定了要应用什么编辑；它不能完全确定提议者看到的证据。当以精英主题、失败痕迹或有效性诊断为条件时，相同的名义操作可以引发不同的搜索行为。. However, operator-level adaptation decides what edit to apply; it does not fully determine what evidence the proposer sees. The same nominal operation can induce different search behavior when conditioned on elite motifs, failure traces, or validity diagnostics.

On the four LSR-Synth scientific domains in LLM-SRBench, A-SR improves average Acc@0.01 over DE from 25.79% to 48.30% with Llama3.1-8B-Instruct, while A-SR-LoRA improves the corresponding Qwen3-4B-Instruct result from 24.58% to 38.29%. The gains do not come from role prompting alone: process diagnostics show that the full system maintains high validity without collapsing to a single role, and ablations show that fixed role rotation is insufficient without protocol selection, online role-policy adaptation, and routed memory.

5 相关工作Related Work

符号回归和法学硕士指导回归。Symbolic and LLM-guided regression.

通过科学规律发现、进化搜索、强化学习和神经序列模型对符号回归进行了研究Symbolic regression has been studied through scientific law discovery, evolutionary search, reinforcement learning, and neural sequence models(Schmidt and Lipson 2009; Sun et al. 2023; Cranmer 2023; Petersen et al. 2021; Landajuela et al. 2022; Biggio et al. 2021; Kamienny et al. 2022)。经典的 SR 研究还强调基准设计、语法或运算符约束，以及紧凑性、准确性和外推法之间的紧张关系. Classical SR research has also emphasized benchmark design, grammar or operator constraints, and the tension between compactness, accuracy, and extrapolation(Keijzer 2003; Vladislavleva et al. 2009; Orzechowski et al. 2018; Fortin et al. 2012; Virgolin et al. 2021)。 SRBench 和 SRSD 等基准研究强调了在方程族和数据体系之间公平比较 SR 系统的难度. Benchmark studies such as SRBench and SRSD emphasize the difficulty of comparing SR systems fairly across equation families and data regimes(La Cava et al. 2021; Matsubara et al. 2024)。 LLM-SR 将方程发现重新构建为使用 LLM 生成的方程骨架和数值参数拟合进行编程. LLM-SR reframes equation discovery as programming with LLM-generated equation skeletons and numerical parameter fitting(Shojaee et al. 2025a)，而LLM-SRBench提供了一个专为LLM集成SR而设计的基准，超越死记硬背, while LLM-SRBench provides a benchmark designed for LLM-integrated SR beyond rote recitation(Shojaee et al. 2025b)。 A-SR 采用可执行程序公式，但使评估者反馈成为角色、协议和内存的协调信号，而不仅仅是迭代提案细化的标量分数。. A-SR adopts the executable-program formulation but makes evaluator feedback a coordination signal for roles, protocols, and memory, rather than only a scalar score for iterative proposal refinement.

代理和评估者在环搜索。Agentic and evaluator-in-the-loop search.

最近的代理系统使用推理痕迹、自洽、结构化审议、反思、记忆、工具和可执行评估器来改进问题解决和程序发现Recent agentic systems use reasoning traces, self-consistency, structured deliberation, reflection, memory, tools, and executable evaluators to improve problem solving and program discovery(Wei et al. 2022; Wang et al. 2023; Yao et al. 2023a; Madaan et al. 2023; Shinn et al. 2023; Romera-Paredes et al. 2024)。多代理和工具增强的 LLM 系统进一步表明，跨角色、工具或交互协议分解任务可以提高复杂工作流程的稳健性和可检查性. Multi-agent and tool-augmented LLM systems further show that decomposing a task across roles, tools, or interaction protocols can improve robustness and inspectability in complex workflows(Li et al. 2023; Park et al. 2023; Qin et al. 2024; Liu et al. 2024; Wu et al. 2023)。 DE 是 LLM 引导的 SR 中最接近的先前工作：它通过自适应符号编辑运算符、分析工具和反射记忆将提案与导航分开. DE is the closest prior work in LLM-guided SR: it separates proposal from navigation through adaptive symbolic edit operators, analytical tools, and reflective memory(Pang et al. 2026)。 A-SR 在不同的控制级别进行组织：A-SR 不是围绕父公式选择表达式编辑运算符，而是协调哪个科学角色起作用以及该角色接收哪个内存视图。. A-SR is organized at a different control level: instead of selecting expression-edit operators around a parent formula, A-SR coordinates which scientific role acts and which memory view that role receives.

6 局限性Limitations

A-SR 使用显式协调器规则和轻量级在线实用程序，而不是学习的神经控制器。这种设计提高了开源和闭源 LLM 主干的透明度和兼容性，但协议选择器可能需要针对截然不同的科学领域进行重新校准。我们的结果还表明，不同的变体在不同的指标下表现出色：A-SR-Static 在某些 NMSE 聚合上可能更强，在线 A-SR 提高了解决任务的准确性，而 A-SR-LoRA 有利于 Qwen 上的科学领域发现。未来的工作可能会从更大的元 SR 任务库中学习协议选择，或者将轨迹蒸馏与 RL 风格的控制器训练相结合。A-SR uses explicit coordinator rules and lightweight online utilities rather than a learned neural controller. This design improves transparency and compatibility with both open-source and closed-source LLM backbones, but the protocol selector may require recalibration for substantially different scientific domains. Our results also show that different variants excel under different metrics: A-SR-Static can be stronger on some NMSE aggregates, online A-SR improves solved-task accuracy, and A-SR-LoRA benefits scientific-domain discovery on Qwen. Future work may learn protocol selection from larger meta-SR task banks or combine trajectory distillation with RL-style controller training.

7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 A-SR，一种用于符号回归的自我进化代理框架。 A-SR 通过协调协议选择、在线角色策略适应和角色感知进程内存路由来协调角色专用的 LLM 代理。通过静态、在线和 LoRA 蒸馏变体，A-SR 提高了科学领域的成功率，并提供了评估者反馈如何影响发现的可解释视图。通过将 LLM 引导的 SR 从统一提案循环和以编辑为中心的适应转向分层代理协调，A-SR 为可靠的科学方程发现提供了灵活的框架。We presented A-SR, a self-evolving agentic framework for symbolic regression. A-SR coordinates role-specialized LLM agents through coordination protocol selection, online role-policy adaptation, and role-aware process memory routing. Across static, online, and LoRA-distilled variants, A-SR improves scientific-domain success rates and provides an interpretable view of how evaluator feedback shapes discovery. By shifting LLM-guided SR from unified proposal loops and edit-centric adaptation toward hierarchical agent coordination, A-SR provides a flexible framework for reliable scientific equation discovery.

我们的基准评估遵循最近法学硕士指导的符号回归研究所使用的任务组织，其中方程发现不仅在随机符号表达式上进行评估，而且还在具有结构化变量和外推分裂的科学族上进行评估。我们使用 LLM-SRBench 作为主要的大规模开放基准，因为它包含转换后的符号回归问题和面向领域的科学任务，使我们能够评估广泛的表达恢复和科学概括。在主论文中，我们将任务分为 LSR-Transform 和 LSR-Synth 子集，后者涵盖物理、材料科学、化学和生物学。Our benchmark evaluation follows the task organization used by recent LLM-guided symbolic regression studies, where equation discovery is evaluated not only on random symbolic expressions but also on scientific families with structured variables and extrapolation splits. We use LLM-SRBench as the main large-scale open benchmark because it contains transformed symbolic-regression problems and domain-oriented scientific tasks, allowing us to evaluate both broad expression recovery and scientific generalization. In the main paper, we group the tasks into LSR-Transform and LSR-Synth subsets, with the latter covering Physics, Material Science, Chemistry, and Biology.

现实世界的科学任务。Real-world scientific tasks.

除了LLM-SRBench之外，我们还保留了LLM-SR中引入的四个现实世界的科学发现任务：两个振荡器系统、一个大肠杆菌生长任务和一个应力应变关系。这些任务很有用，因为它们测试了 LLM 引导的 SR 的不同故障模式。振荡器任务强调外推和相敏动力学；生长动力学测试饱和度和非线性生物机制；应力应变数据测试噪声和参数敏感状态下的材料响应模型。In addition to LLM-SRBench, we retain the four real-world scientific discovery tasks introduced in LLM-SR: two oscillator systems, an E. coli growth task, and a stress-strain relation. These tasks are useful because they test different failure modes of LLM-guided SR. Oscillator tasks stress extrapolation and phase-sensitive dynamics; growth dynamics tests saturation and nonlinear biological mechanisms; and stress-strain data tests material-response modeling under noisy and parameter-sensitive regimes.

输入输出格式。Input-output format.

每个任务都作为任务描述、可用变量列表、可执行方程程序模板以及训练/验证/OOD 数据拆分（如果可用）提供给 A-SR。法学硕士不直接观察目标方程。相反，每个角色都会针对允许的变量提出可执行的候选函数。然后评估器拟合自由常数并计算相应的度量。这种格式遵循程序化方程发现设置，同时使所有特定于角色的提案在同一评估员下具有可比性。Each task is provided to A-SR as a task description, a list of available variables, an executable equation-program template, and train/validation/OOD data splits when available. The LLM does not directly observe target equations. Instead, each role proposes executable candidate functions over the allowed variables. The evaluator then fits free constants and computes the corresponding metrics. This format follows the programmatic equation-discovery setting while making all role-specific proposals comparable under the same evaluator.

训练、验证和 OOD 分割。Train, validation, and OOD splits.

对于 LLM-SRBench 实验，我们在主表中报告训练/验证聚合性能，并保留 OOD 指标用于单独报告，因为 OOD 分割并未在所有基准组中统一定义。对于现实世界的科学任务，当分割可用时，我们会报告 ID 和 OOD NMSE。这种分离避免了将插值精度和外推稳健性混合到单个数字中，这对于科学方程发现尤其重要。For LLM-SRBench experiments, we report train/validation aggregate performance in the main table and reserve OOD metrics for separate reporting, since OOD splits are not uniformly defined across all benchmark groups. For the real-world scientific tasks, we report both ID and OOD NMSE when the split is available. This separation avoids mixing interpolation accuracy and extrapolation robustness into a single number, which is especially important for scientific equation discovery.

附录CAppendix C基准和记忆限制Benchmark and Memorization Limitations

为什么需要转变和科学的基准。Why transformed and scientific benchmarks are needed.

费曼式的符号回归基准对于测试代数恢复仍然有用，但它们本身并不足以证明现代法学硕士的科学发现。许多规范方程、变量名称和教科书推导都可能出现在预训练语料库中，因此对熟悉的表达式的精确恢复可以将真实的搜索行为与记忆的科学形式混合在一起。这种关注对于 LLM 引导的 SR 尤为重要，因为即使模型不直接观察目标方程，也可以利用变量名称、单位或问题描述中的语义线索。Feynman-style symbolic-regression benchmarks remain useful for testing algebraic recovery, but they are not by themselves sufficient evidence of scientific discovery with modern LLMs. Many canonical equations, variable names, and textbook derivations are likely to appear in pretraining corpora, so exact recovery on a familiar expression can mix genuine search behavior with memorized scientific form. This concern is especially important for LLM-guided SR because the model can exploit semantic cues in variable names, units, or problem descriptions even when it does not directly observe the target equation.

本文的基准选择。Benchmark choice in this paper.

因此，我们使用 LLM-SRBench 作为主要的大规模基准。其转换方程任务减少了模型简单地输出记忆的教科书公式的机会，而其科学合成领域则评估跨物理、材料科学、化学和生物学的结构化外推法。现实世界的 LLM-SR 任务提供了额外的检查设置，其中目标行为与噪声或实验驱动的科学现象相关。这些基准仍然不能消除记忆风险：变换后的方程可以保留可识别的代数主题，科学任务描述仍然可以提供有用的先验信息。因此，我们报告数值准确性、OOD 行为、公式结构和搜索动态，而不是仅依赖于精确的符号匹配。We therefore use LLM-SRBench as the main large-scale benchmark. Its transformed-equation tasks reduce the chance that a model simply emits a memorized textbook formula, while its scientific synthetic domains evaluate structured extrapolation across physics, material science, chemistry, and biology. The real-world LLM-SR tasks provide an additional check in settings where the target behavior is tied to noisy or experimentally motivated scientific phenomena. These benchmarks still do not eliminate memorization risk: transformed equations can preserve recognizable algebraic motifs, and scientific task descriptions can still provide useful prior information. For this reason, we report numerical accuracy, OOD behavior, formula structure, and search dynamics rather than relying only on exact symbolic match.

公式恢复的解释。Interpretation of formula recovery.

我们的公式级示例应被视为结构性证据，而不是声称 A-SR 永远不会从法学硕士的科学先验中受益。 A-SR 有意使用 LLM 作为提案先验，但主要问题是评估者引导的协调是否可以将先验转化为可靠的可执行搜索。因此，我们强调通过有效的中间候选、特定于角色的修复和内存路由达到最终表达式的情况，并且我们报告 Acc@0.01 和 OOD 指标，以将广泛的已解决任务可靠性与孤立的精确恢复示例分开。Our formula-level examples should be read as structural evidence rather than as a claim that A-SR never benefits from the LLM’s scientific prior. A-SR intentionally uses the LLM as a proposal prior, but the main question is whether evaluator-guided coordination can turn that prior into reliable executable search. We therefore emphasize cases where the final expression is reached through valid intermediate candidates, role-specific repairs, and memory routing, and we report Acc@0.01 and OOD metrics to separate broad solved-task reliability from isolated exact-recovery examples.
