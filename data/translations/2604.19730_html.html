摘要Abstract

当今一些性能最好的强化学习算法可能非常昂贵，因为它们使用测试时间缩放方法，例如对多个候选动作进行采样并选择最佳的一个。在这项工作中，我们建议Some of the most performant reinforcement learning algorithms today can be prohibitively expensive as they use test-time scaling methods such as sampling multiple action candidates and selecting the best one. In this work, we propose更快FASTER，一种方法，通过将动作样本的性能增益追溯到去噪过程的早期，获得基于扩散的策略的基于采样的测试时间缩放的好处，而无需计算成本。我们的主要见解是，我们可以对多个候选动作的去噪进行建模，并选择最好的一个作为马尔可夫决策过程（MDP），其目标是在去噪完成之前逐步过滤候选动作。通过这个 MDP，我们可以学习去噪空间中的策略和价值函数，该函数可以预测去噪过程中候选动作的下游价值，并在最大化回报的同时对其进行过滤。结果是一种轻量级的方法，可以插入现有的生成强化学习算法中。在在线和批量在线 RL 中应对具有挑战性的长视野操作任务，, a method for getting the benefits of sampling-based test-time scaling of diffusion-based policies without the computational cost by tracing the performance gain of action samples back to earlier in the denoising process. Our key insight is that we can model the denoising of multiple action candidates and selecting the best one as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) where the goal is to progressively filter action candidates before denoising is complete. With this MDP, we can learn a policy and value function in the denoising space that predicts the downstream value of action candidates in the denoising process and filters them while maximizing returns. The result is a method that is lightweight and can be plugged into existing generative RL algorithms. Across challenging long-horizon manipulation tasks in online and batch-online RL,更快FASTER不断改进底层策略并在比较方法中实现最佳整体性能。应用于预训练的 VLA，consistently improves the underlying policies and achieves the best overall performance among the compared methods. Applied to a pretrained VLA,更快FASTER实现相同的性能，同时大幅降低训练和推理计算要求。achieves the same performance while substantially reducing training and inference compute requirements.

1 简介Introduction

最近的强化学习（RL）方法使用表达性策略主干（例如广泛应用于图像/视频生成和机器人等领域的扩散模型）展示了强大的性能。然而，这些算法中的一些性能最好的算法在训练和测试时都非常昂贵。这是因为这些算法中的许多算法依赖于对多个候选动作进行采样并选择最佳的一个，例如根据哪一个具有最高值来确定。虽然有效，但这种方法会产生大量的计算成本。对于像现代视觉语言动作（VLA）模型这样的大型模型来说，这尤其成问题，这些模型的运行时间已经接近可接受的延迟极限。八个适度的样本数量将相应地增加推理成本，并且可能使这些方法在延迟敏感或资源受限的设置中不切实际。在这项工作中，我们问：我们能否在不损失计算成本的情况下恢复基于采样的测试时间缩放的好处？Recent reinforcement learning (RL) methods have demonstrated strong performance using expressive policy backbones such as diffusion models which are widely used in domains such as image/video generation and robotics. However, some of the most performant of these algorithms are prohibitively expensive both during training and at test time. This is because many of these algorithms rely on sampling multiple candidate actions and selecting the best one, for example as determined by which one has the highest value. While effective, such approaches incur substantial computational costs. This is especially problematic for large models like modern Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, which already operate near the limits of acceptable latency. A modest sample count of eight will multiply inference costs accordingly and can render these methods impractical in latency-sensitive or resource-constrained settings. In this work, we ask: can we recover the benefits of sampling-based test-time scaling without suffering its computational cost?

这种高计算成本来自于具有生成模型的策略，例如需要对所有候选者进行去噪的扩散或流匹配。先前的工作表明，生成模型的测试时间缩放方法是高性能的。诸如最佳方法之类的方法This high computational cost comes from policies with generative models such as diffusion or flow matching that need to denoise all candidates. Prior works have shown that test-time scaling methods for generative models are performant. Methods such as best-of-NN抽样和自我一致性对多个候选者进行抽样，并选择最大化给定指标的一个。虽然这些方法可以有效地最大化性能，但它们的扩展性很差，并且需要对所有候选动作进行完全去噪。基于蒸馏的方法通过训练策略直接重现高价值行为来摊销此成本，但需要训练单独的策略，这可能会很昂贵。所有这些方法的核心困难在于，如果不观察完全去噪的样本，就很难知道哪些样本值得执行，因为没有直接监督将给定的噪声样本与其下游结果联系起来，并且从噪声到动作的映射仅通过去噪过程隐式定义。sampling and self-consistency sample multiple candidates and select the one that maximizes a given metric. While these methods are effective at maximizing performance, they scale poorly and require full denoising over all action candidates. Distillation-based approaches amortize this cost by training the policy to directly reproduce high-value behaviors, but require training a separate policy which can be expensive. The core difficulty underlying all of these approaches is that, without observing the fully denoised samples, it is hard to know which ones are worth executing since there is no direct supervision linking a given noise sample to its downstream outcome, and the mapping from noise to action is only defined implicitly through the denoising process.

在这项工作中，我们的主要见解是，我们可以对多个候选样本的去噪进行建模，并选择最佳样本作为马尔可夫决策过程（MDP），其目标是在去噪完成之前逐步过滤候选样本。通过这个 MDP，我们可以通过传统的时间差异学习来学习去噪 Q 函数和策略，决定保留和删除哪些操作，同时最大化回报。为了在不需要详尽的推出的情况下监督价值函数，我们使用最终动作的价值函数作为动作过滤 MDP 的奖励，使模型能够将结果信息传播回去噪空间，而无需为每个候选者提供直接的结果标签。实际上，我们发现在噪声级别过滤候选（这是计算上最便宜的实例化）所达到的性能相当于对所有动作样本进行完全去噪并选择最高值的动作。因此，无论候选动作的数量有多少，去噪的计算成本（推理时的主要瓶颈）都减少到单次推出的成本。其结果是一种轻量级、模块化的方法，将广泛的行动样本选择的好处与完整策略执行的成本脱钩。In this work, our key insight is that we can model the denoising of multiple candidate samples and the selection of the best one as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) where the goal is to progressively filter candidates before denoising is complete. With this MDP, we can learn a denoise Q-function and policy with traditional temporal difference learning that decide which actions to keep and remove while maximizing the returns. To supervise the value function without requiring exhaustive rollouts, we use the value function over final actions as the reward of the action filtering MDP, enabling the model to propagate outcome information back to the denoising space without direct outcome labels for every candidate. Practically, we find that filtering candidates at the noise level—which is the cheapest instantiation computationally—achieves performance equivalent to fully denoising all action samples and selecting the highest value action. The computational cost of denoising—which is the dominant bottleneck at inference time—is thus reduced to that of a single rollout, regardless of the number of action candidates. The result is a lightweight, modular approach that decouples the benefits of broad action sample selection from the cost of full policy execution.

我们的主要贡献是一个框架，用于获得采样的性能增益并选择 RL 方法的多个动作中的最佳动作，而无需对所有动作进行去噪的计算开销。与对动作进行操作的方法不同，我们的方法直接对去噪过程进行操作以过滤掉候选者，从而使选择既便宜又模块化。我们在具有挑战性的操作基准（包括 Robomimic 和 LIBERO）上评估我们的方法，跨算法和模型能力，证明了相对于单样本基线的一致增益，并且与最佳方法接近相同。Our main contribution is a framework for obtaining the performance gains of sampling and selecting the best of multiple actions of RL methods without the computational overhead of denoising all actions. Unlike approaches that operate on actions, our method operates directly on the denoising process to filter down candidates, making selection both cheap and modular. We evaluate our method on challenging manipulation benchmarks, including Robomimic and LIBERO, across algorithms and model capacities, demonstrating consistent gains over single-sample baselines and near-parity with best-of-NN方法只需要一小部分计算开销。methods at a fraction of the computational overhead.

6 讨论Discussion

在这项工作中，我们建议In this work, we propose更快FASTER，一种获得基于采样的测试时间缩放的好处而不会产生计算成本的方法。通过将多个候选动作的去噪构建为 MDP，, a method for obtaining the benefits of sampling-based test-time scaling without incurring its computational cost. By framing the denoising of multiple action candidates as an MDP,更快FASTER学习根据下游值选择操作的过滤策略。尽管取得了这些有希望的结果，learns a filtering policy that selects actions according to their downstream value. Despite these promising results,更快FASTER有局限性。首先，虽然has limitations. First, while更快FASTER在计算效率上大大提高了其基本算法，但它并不是为了提高样本效率而设计的。在我们的实验中，substantially improves upon its base algorithms in computational efficiency, it is not designed to improve sample efficiency. In our experiments,更快的博览会FASTER-EXPO相对于强基线，取得了更好的样本效率，这主要归功于 EXPO 固有的样本效率，但在这方面并没有超越 EXPO 本身。提高基于过滤的方法的样本效率是未来工作的一个有趣的方向。此外，achieves better sample efficiency relative to strong baselines, owing largely to the inherent sample efficiency of EXPO, but does not surpass EXPO itself in this regard. Improving the sample efficiency of filtering-based approaches is an interesting direction for future work. Furthermore,更快FASTER专为使用初始噪声种子的策略类而设计，其中包含当代强化学习中一些性能最佳的策略表示。将其扩展到缺乏这种种子结构的政策类别将留待未来的工作。is designed for policy classes that use initial noise seeds, which encompass some of the most performant policy representations in contemporary reinforcement learning.
