1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地作为自主部署Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as autonomous代理人agents在更广阔的视野中进行计划、推理和行动。除了生成答案之外，LLM 代理还维护内部推理轨迹，以指导工具调用、行动承诺和跨决策步骤的内存更新。随着推理范式的广泛采用，例如思想链（CoT）that plan, reason, and act over extended horizons. Beyond generating answers, LLM agents maintain internal reasoning trajectories for guiding tool invocation, action commitment, and memory updates across decision steps. With the widespread adoption of reasoning paradigms, such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT)(Wei et al., 2022)，轨迹通常被视为智能体内部状态的可解释表示。然而，连贯的推理痕迹对于决策来说是脆弱的, trajectories are generally regarded as interpretable representations of the agent’s internal state. However, coherent reasoning traces are fragile for decision-making(Lam et al., 2025)。智能体可能会产生看似流畅且结构化的推理，但却违反逻辑或证据约束，反映出缺乏忠实的推理. Agents may generate seemingly fluent and structured reasoning, yet violate logical or evidential constraints, reflecting a lack of faithful reasoning(Zhao et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025b)。仅从最终任务的成功很难诊断此类违规行为，因为正确的结果可能来自偶然、冗余或下游纠正，从而掩盖了潜在的推理失败. Such violations are difficult to diagnose from final-task success alone, since correct outcomes can arise from chance, redundancy, or downstream correction, masking the underlying reasoning failure(Chang et al., 2025; Kim et al., 2024)，如图, as shown in Figure1。与单轮问答 (QA) 不同，单轮问答 (QA) 的推理可以是事后且一次性的，代理的推理输出会被重复使用、放大并写入内存. Unlike single-turn Question Answering (QA), where reasoning can be post hoc and disposable, the agent’s reasoning outputs are repeatedly used, amplified, and written into memory(An et al., 2025; Jiang et al., 2025; Tang et al., 2025)。因此，不忠实的信念状态（例如，不受支持的推论或隐藏的假设）可能会传播，使决策产生偏差，并在闭环代理系统中触发代价高昂的行动. Consequently, unfaithful belief states (e.g., unsupported inferences or hidden assumptions) can propagate, bias decisions, and trigger costly actions in closed-loop agent systems(Chakraborty et al., 2025)。风险不仅在于错误的答案，还在于由不忠实的内部信念驱动的系统性行为偏差。. The risk is not merely incorrect answers, but systematic behavioral drift driven by unfaithful internal beliefs.

在代理系统中，在将内部推理状态付诸行动之前，已采用现有方法来管理不确定性，例如自我一致性In agentic systems, existing methods have been adopted to manage uncertainty before committing internal reasoning states to actions, such as self-consistency(Wan et al., 2025; Xie et al., 2024), 多主体辩论, multi-agent debate(Liang et al., 2026, 2024b)，它维持多个候选推理轨迹，并依赖基于共识的聚合来确定行动信念, which maintain multiple candidate reasoning trajectories and rely on consensus-based aggregation for acted belief determination(Zhang and Xiong, 2025)。然而，它们所依据的前提是有问题的：共识就是忠诚。在实践中，多个采样轨迹经常共享相同的隐含假设或推理模板，从而导致结构相关但不忠实的信念状态被重复选择，通过多数投票进一步强化，并致力于记忆. Nevertheless, they rest on the problematic premise that consensus is faithfulness. In practice, multiple sampled trajectories frequently share the same implicit assumptions or inference templates, resulting in structurally correlated yet unfaithful belief states that are repeatedly selected, further reinforced by majority voting, and committed to memory(Ke et al., 2025)。此外，大多数现有方法在表面文本重写层面与推理进行交互. 2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 法学硕士的忠实推理Faithful Reasoning in LLMs

法学硕士在推理任务上表现出了出色的表现。现有的工作已经探索了激励策略，最引人注目的是 CoT 激励LLMs have shown strong performance on reasoning tasks. Existing work has explored the prompting strategies, most notably CoT prompting(Wei et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022)。扩展，例如基于程序的推理. Extensions such as program-based reasoning(Chen et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024)以及最少到最多的提示and least-to-most prompting(Zhou et al., 2023; Arora et al., 2023)进一步构建这些步骤并提高任务准确性further structure these steps and improve task accuracy(Ma et al., 2025)。然而，推理性能的提高并不一定意味着推理的忠实性：实证研究表明，生成的思维链可能依赖于虚假信号，而对其进行干预对模型预测的影响有限，这表明这种解释是事后合理化. However, improved reasoning performance does not necessarily imply faithful reasoning: empirical studies show that generated chains of thought may rely on spurious signals, and intervening on them has limited impact on model predictions, suggesting that such explanations are post-hoc rationalizations(Lyu et al., 2023; Turpin et al., 2023).

出于这些担忧的推动，越来越多的工作集中于评估法学硕士的推理可信度Motivated by these concerns, a growing body of work has focused on evaluating reasoning faithfulness in LLMs(Luo et al., 2024; Paul et al., 2024; Luo et al., 2025)。现有方法包括对推理痕迹的反事实干预. Existing approaches include counterfactual interventions on reasoning traces(Paul et al., 2024; Ding et al., 2024; Joshi et al., 2024)、因果探查方法, causal probing methods(Chi et al., 2024; Roy et al., 2025)，以及针对思想链忠诚度的有针对性的诊断, and targeted diagnostics for chain-of-thought faithfulness(Li et al., 2025)。除了评估之外，还有几种方法可以通过验证来提高忠实推理. Beyond evaluation, several methods improve faithful reasoning through verification(Dougrez-Lewis et al., 2025)或自洽or self-consistency(Wan et al., 2025).

尽管取得了这些进展，大多数关于忠实推理的研究都集中在法学硕士上。然而，在代理环境中，推理轨迹作为持久的信念状态来指导下游决策，从而允许错误的信念在长期范围内积累、传播并触发代价高昂的行动。以法学硕士为中心的忠实推理方法不足以进行代理决策，这强调了对代理特定机制的需求。Despite these advances, most studies on faithful reasoning focus on LLMs. In agentic settings, however, reasoning trajectories function as persistent belief states to guide downstream decisions, allowing erroneous beliefs to accumulate, propagate, and trigger costly actions over long horizons. LLM-centric methods to faithful reasoning are insufficient for agentic decision-making, underscoring the need for agent-specific mechanisms.

2.2 智能体的忠诚意识推理Faithfulness-Aware Reasoning in Agents

一种新兴的研究视角将法学硕士视为通过规划、工具使用和与外部环境交互执行多步骤推理的代理An emerging research perspective frames LLMs as agents that perform multi-step reasoning through planning, tool use, and interaction with external environments(Hong et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025a)。这些框架公开了明确的推理轨迹，以支持交互式环境中的复杂决策. These frameworks expose explicit reasoning trajectories to support complex decision-making in interactive settings(Yao et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2024)。然而，明确的推理并不能保证真实性：实证研究表明，智能体生成的计划或工具使用的基本原理可能不会因果地决定结果，而是作为合理的事后理由. However, explicit reasoning does not guarantee faithfulness: empirical studies demonstrate that agent-generated plans or tool-use rationales may not causally determine outcomes, but instead serve as plausible post-hoc justifications(Barez et al., 2025).

尽管有这些观察结果，现有的减轻不忠实代理人推理的方法仍然是事后或结果驱动的。许多方法通过对多个推理轨迹进行采样或应用外部批评来提高鲁棒性，而没有明确验证中间推理步骤是否得到智能体在决策时可用证据的支持Despite these observations, existing approaches for mitigating unfaithful agent reasoning remain post-hoc or outcome-driven. Many methods improve robustness by sampling multiple reasoning trajectories or applying external critics, without explicitly verifying whether intermediate reasoning steps are supported by the agent’s available evidence at the time of decision-making(Liang et al., 2024a; Kostka and Chudziak, 2025)。此外，许多方法在表面级轨迹重写或共识聚合上运行，这不足以识别可以重复通过启发式检查的结构相关但不受支持的信念状态. Moreover, numerous approaches operate at surface-level trajectory rewriting or consensus aggregation, which is insufficient to identify structurally correlated yet unsupported belief states that can repeatedly pass heuristic checks(Fu et al., 2025; Grötschla et al., 2025)。因此，当前的代理框架缺乏在行动之前审核和验证内部信念状态的机制，从而允许不忠实的推理传播并存储在内存中。. Consequently, current agentic frameworks lack a mechanism for auditing and verifying internal belief states before action, allowing unfaithful reasoning to be propagated and stored in memory.

局限性Limitations

这项工作有几个值得注意的局限性。首先，通过维护多个候选置信状态并执行迭代 A-R 循环引入了额外的计算开销。虽然This work exhibits several limitations worth noting. Firstly, extra computational overhead is introduced by maintaining multiple candidate belief states and performing iterative A-R cycles. Although节省SAVeR尽管将审计限制在一个小的、结构多样的子集上，但 A-R 循环仍然比单遍提示或轻量级细化策略更昂贵，特别是在推理链较短的任务中。其次，在简单的场景中，严格的忠实执行可能是不必要的，并且可能会引入冗余的推理操作。同时limits audit to a small, structurally diverse subset, the A-R loop remains more expensive than single-pass prompting or lightweight refinement strategies, particularly in tasks with short reasoning chains. Secondly, strict faithfulness enforcement may be unnecessary in simple scenarios and could introduce redundant reasoning operations. While节省SAVeR旨在本地化并最小化纠正不支持的推理步骤，目前缺乏明确的机制来使验证深度适应任务难度。未来的工作可以探索自适应审计策略，根据不确定性或任务复杂性进行验证，使代理能够动态地权衡推理的忠实性和效率。is designed to localize and minimally correct unsupported reasoning steps, it currently lacks an explicit mechanism to adapt verification depth to task difficulty. Future work could explore adaptive auditing policies that condition verification on uncertainty or task complexity, enabling agents to dynamically trade off reasoning faithfulness and efficiency.
