Compared with end-to-end GNN pipelines, GraphFAS decouples feature aggregation from model training, enabling direct integration with tabular models and direct compatibility with TreeSHAP-based explanations. Deployed in Alipay, GraphFAS delivers order-of-magnitude improvements in engineering efficiency while showing strong performance against expert-driven and graph-learning baselines on large-scale graphs.

图神经网络（GNN）通过迭代消息传递捕获结构依赖关系，在各种图学习任务上实现强大的性能。然而，GNN 在金融风险控制场景中面临着根本性的限制。首先，它们的多层转换可能会使决策过程更难解释与模型透明度的监管要求的冲突。其次，端到端训练的计算开销会带来显着的延迟，这与大规模工业网络上的低延迟特征生产要求相冲突。第三，GNN 嵌入通常缺乏在风险分析工作流程中易于验证的显式语义，这使得它们难以在生产环境中验证和部署。Graph neural networks (GNNs) capture structural dependencies through iterative message passing, achieving strong performance on various graph learning tasks. However, GNNs face fundamental limitations in financial risk control scenarios. First, their multi-layer transformations can make decision processes harder to interpret that conflict with regulatory requirements for model transparency. Second, the computational overhead of end-to-end training introduces significant latency that conflicts with low-latency feature production requirements on large-scale industrial networks. Third, GNN embeddings often lack explicit semantics that are easy to validate in risk analysis workflows, making them difficult to validate and deploy in production environments.

2.4. 研究差距和动机Research Gap and Motivation

现有方法表现出三个技术限制，这激励了我们的工作。首先，基于包装器的特征选择方法缺乏分布式适应性——Boruta 在独立环境中运行，没有分区感知聚合机制，这使得它难以处理大规模数据集。其次，当前的图学习方法迫使性能和可解释性之间进行权衡：GNN 产生不适合监管审计的不透明嵌入，而事后解释器提供近似值而不是精确的归因。第三，自动图到表格的管道依赖于静态预定义编码器，如果不进行复杂的再训练，这些编码器就无法适应不同的异构图结构。我们将这样的基准测试留给未来的工作。Existing approaches exhibit three technical limitations that motivate our work. First, wrapper-based feature selection methods lack distributed adaptations—Boruta operates on standalone environments without partition-aware aggregation mechanisms, making it intractable for large-scale datasets. Second, current graph learning methods force a trade-off between performance and interpretability: GNNs produce opaque embeddings unsuitable for regulatory audit, while post-hoc explainers provide approximations rather than exact attributions. Third, automated graph-to-tabular pipelines rely on static pre-defined encoders that cannot adapt to diverse heterogeneous graph structures without complex retraining. We leave such benchmarking to future work.

GraphFAS 通过以下方式解决这些差距：(1) 分布式 Boruta 适应，具有基于中值的聚合，用于大规模稳健的特征选择； (2) 非参数图特征生成，产生与原生 TreeSHAP 可解释性兼容的固有可解释结构统计数据； (3) 解耦的特征生成和自动选择，无需端到端再训练即可实现可扩展部署。GraphFAS addresses these gaps through: (1) a distributed Boruta adaptation with median-based aggregation for robust feature selection at scale; (2) non-parametric graph feature generation producing inherently interpretable structural statistics compatible with native TreeSHAP explainability; (3) decoupled feature generation and automated selection enabling scalable deployment without end-to-end retraining.

7. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 GraphFAS，这是一种分布式图特征选择系统，它在可解释性和可扩展性方面为工业限制下的端到端 GNN 管道提供了实用的替代方案。通过将非参数图特征生成与使用跨分区中值聚合的分布式 Boruta 式选择相结合，GraphFAS 生成与原生 TreeSHAP 可解释性兼容的明确、可解释的结构特征。它在支付宝部署了两年，每天处理数百万个种子节点，其性能优于基于 GNN 的方法，同时比手动特征工程实现了数量级的效率提升。将特征生成与模型训练解耦会牺牲一些表示能力，以换取实际收益，包括具有可扩展分布式处理的仅 CPU 执行、符合审计的结构统计以及无需重新生成特征的灵活下游模型更新。We present GraphFAS, a distributed graph feature selection system that provides a practical alternative to end-to-end GNN pipelines under industrial constraints for interpretability and scalability. By combining non-parametric graph feature generation with a distributed Boruta-style selection using median aggregation across partitions, GraphFAS produces explicit, interpretable structural features compatible with native TreeSHAP explainability. Deployed at Alipay for two years, processing millions of seed nodes daily, it outperforms GNN-based approaches while achieving order-of-magnitude efficiency gains over manual feature engineering. Decoupling feature generation from model training sacrifices some representational capacity in exchange for practical benefits, including CPU-only execution with scalable distributed processing, audit-compliant structural statistics, and flexible downstream model updates without regenerating features.
