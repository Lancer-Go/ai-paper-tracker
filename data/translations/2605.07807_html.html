1 简介Introduction

VLMs have the potential of reshaping traditional CAD workflows by extending the capabilities of conventional graphical design interfaces offered by widely used platforms such as FreeCAD[9]、SolidWorks, SolidWorks[10]或融合360or Fusion 360[5]。它们支持以文本提示为条件的生成设计. They enable generative design conditioned on text prompts[43, 13]以及多种输入方式，包括图像as well as diverse input modalities, including images[30, 22], 绘制的草图, drawn sketches[35, 19, 18], 点云, point-clouds[39, 12, 34, 22]或 3D 网格or 3D meshes[20]。在各种输入模式中，最近的工作越来越关注文本条件 CAD 生成. Among the various input modalities, recent work increasingly focuses on text-conditioned CAD generation[17, 22, 21, 43, 30, 6, 31]。语言为人机交互提供了自然、直观的媒介，这导致了文本到 CAD 研究线索的出现。. Language provides a natural and intuitive medium for human–computer interaction, which has led to the emergence of the Text-to-CAD research thread.

文本到 CAD 研究的主要目标是提高生成性能，而严格的评估却很少受到关注。当前的方法主要依赖于几何相似性度量Text-to-CAD research has primarily targeted improving generation performance, while rigorous evaluation has received far less attention. Current methods mostly rely on geometric similarity metrics[21, 1, 26, 43, 29, 42, 38, 13, 22]，例如根据单个参考 CAD 模型计算的倒角距离 (CD)。此方法仅限于文本到 CAD，因为提示可能指定某些设计约束或属性，但本质上是不明确的，并且无法确定唯一定义 CAD 模型所需的所有几何和结构细节。因此，几何相似性度量无法解释不受提示约束的自由度内有效设计变化的空间。相反，在这项工作中，文本到 CAD 被视为一项代码合成任务，其中自然语言描述被转换为生成有效 3D 几何图形的可执行参数化程序。这种观点激发了一种超越几何比较的评估范式，而是利用 CAD 模型的编程性质作为可执行、可测试的代码。我们从程序综合领域汲取灵感，其中流行的基准测试（例如 HumanEval）, such as Chamfer Distance (CD), computed against a single reference CAD model. This approach is limited for Text-to-CAD as the prompt may specify certain design constraints or attributes but is inherently ambiguous and cannot determine all geometric and structural details required to uniquely define a CAD model. Geometric similarity metrics thus fail to account for the space of valid design variations within the degrees of freedom unconstrained by the prompt. Instead, in this work, Text-to-CAD is viewed as a code synthesis task where natural language descriptions is translated into an executable parametric program that produces valid 3D geometry. This perspective motivates an evaluation paradigm that moves beyond geometric comparison, instead leveraging the programmatic nature of CAD models as executable, testable code.
