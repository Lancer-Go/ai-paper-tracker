1 简介Introduction

翻译的目的是通过最接近的自然对等物以目标语言传达源信息，同时保留语义、文体和语用效果。然而，语言与文化密不可分，文化优先性塑造了语言表达Translation aims to convey the source message in the target language through the closest natural equivalent while preserving semantic, stylistic, and pragmatic effects. However, language is inseparable from culture, and cultural priorities shape linguistic expression(Nida, 1964)。每种文化都围绕自己的重点领域组织词汇，导致某些意义领域变得越来越详细和复杂. Each culture organizes its vocabulary around its own areas of emphasis, leading certain domains of meaning to become increasingly detailed and complex(Larson, 1997)。 这种文化塑造的发展产生了一类词汇，称为. This culturally shaped development gives rise to a category of lexical items termed文化负载词culturally loaded words by Xu (1980)。这些词语深深植根于特定的社会文化背景，反映了社区的传统、信仰和价值体系. Such words are deeply embedded in specific socio-cultural contexts and reflect the traditions, beliefs, and value systems of a community(Baker, 1992; Newmark, 1998).

2 理论基础Theoretical Foundations

2.1 文化负载翻译Culturally Loaded Translation

Xu (1980)最先创造这个词的first coined the term文化负载词culturally loaded words描述编码社会传统、信仰和价值体系的词汇。翻译研究中的类似概念，例如to describe lexical items that encode society’s traditions, beliefs, and value systems. Similar concepts in translation studies, such as文化词汇cultural words (Newmark, 1988)和and文化特定项目culture-specific items (Aixelá, 1996)，一般指深深植根于特定社会文化背景的表达方式，如果没有文化背景知识就无法完全理解其含义, generally refer to expressions that are deeply rooted in particular socio-cultural contexts and whose meanings cannot be fully understood without cultural background knowledge(Baker, 1992; Newmark, 1998).

Taking Chinese culture as examples, “布衣” (布依bùyī) in ancient China referred to ordinary people and implied a modest lifestyle associated with coarse cloth garments. If translated literally as “coarse clothes,” English readers may find it confusing, since the phrase only refers to a type of fabric and does not convey the social meaning in Chinese. Another example is “鸿雁” (红岩hóngyàn), a bird image in Chinese ecological culture. Although “swan goose” is its literal zoological equivalent, it fails to capture the cultural meaning. In Chinese tradition, “鸿雁” often symbolizes a messenger carrying letters. Translating it as “message-bearing swans”222中文译者徐元冲译。Translated by Chinese translator Yuanchong Xu.更好地传达了这种文化意象，尽管它牺牲了严格的词汇对等。 这些例子表明，翻译具有文化负载的表达方式需要传达意义和文化意义。better conveys this cultural imagery, even though it sacrifices strict lexical equivalence. These examples show that translating culturally loaded expressions requires conveying both meaning and cultural significance.

作为现代翻译研究的奠基人，尤金·A·奈达（Eugene A. Nida）率先将文化分为五类：生态学、宗教学、物质学、语言学和社会学。As the foundational scholar of modern translation studies, Eugene A. Nida pioneered the categorization of culture into five types: ecology, religion, material, linguistics, and society(Nida, 1945, 1964):

  • •

    生态学Ecology：具有文化意义的动植物、气候、地理景观、生态现象等自然元素。: Natural elements such as flora, fauna, climate, geographical landscapes, and ecological phenomena that carry cultural meaning.

  • •

    宗教Religion：精神信仰，包括祖先崇拜、民间迷信和塑造世界观的神话概念。: Spiritual beliefs including ancestor worship, folk superstitions, and mythological concepts that shape worldviews.

  • •

    材质Material：日常生活中的有形手工艺品，如衣服、食物、工具和其他定义文明物质文化的实物。: Tangible artifacts of daily life like clothing, food, tools, and other physical objects that define a civilization’s material culture.

  • •

    语言学Linguistics：具有文化内涵的表达方式，如成语、谚语、俚语、谜语和固定短语，表现出独特的修辞模式。: Culturally embedded expressions like idioms, proverbs, slang, riddles, and fixed phrases that exhibit unique rhetorical patterns.

  • •

    社会Society：社会结构、风俗习惯、制度、亲属制度、礼仪规范以及管理人际关系的惯例。: Social structures, customs, institutions, kinship systems, etiquette norms, and conventions that govern interpersonal relations.

这种分类显示出很强的理论适应性，并继续为当代翻译研究提供信息This classification has shown strong theoretical adaptability and continues to inform contemporary translation research(Oo, 2020; Yu-wei and Wei, 2022; Prompan, 2024; Al Rujaibi et al., 2025)。在本研究中，我们采用这种五重分类法作为数据集构建的框架。. In this study, we adopt this five-fold taxonomy as the framework for dataset construction.

2.3 跨文化翻译理论Cross-culture Translation Theory

跨文化翻译长期以来一直是跨学科的中心话题。最有影响力的框架之一是 Venuti 的归化和异化分类法Cross-cultural translation has long been a central topic across disciplines. Among the most influential frameworks is Venuti’s domestication and foreignization taxonomy(Venuti, 1995)。归化使文本适应目标文化规范以增强可读性，而异化则保留源文化元素以突出文化差异。该框架强调了译者的代理作用，并为评估跨文化迁移提供了批判性的视角。它已被广泛用于分析译者如何通过平衡可访问性和真实性来处理特定文化的项目。. Domestication adapts a text to target-culture norms to enhance readability, whereas foreignization preserves source-culture elements to foreground cultural difference. This framework highlights the translator’s agency and offers a critical perspective for evaluating cross-cultural transfer. It has been widely used to analyze how translators handle culture-specific items by balancing accessibility and authenticity.

其他理论观点进一步加深了对翻译作为一种文化嵌入实践的理解。动态等效理论Other theoretical perspectives further deepen the understanding of translation as a culturally embedded practice. The dynamic equivalence theory(Nida, 1964)优先考虑等效的读者反应而不是字面对应。文化转向理论prioritizes equivalent reader response rather than literal correspondence. The cultural turn thoery(Bassnett and Lefèvere, 1990)将翻译概念化为一种由意识形态力量塑造的文化重写形式；规范理论conceptualizes translation as a form of cultural rewriting shaped by ideological forces; The norms theory(Toury, 1995)强调指导译者决策的社会文化约束。 这些框架共同表明，翻译不是一种中立的语言迁移，而是一个文化协商的过程——这是研究语言模型如何处理文化负载翻译的重要基础，我们也将在后续实验中对此进行探索。emphasizes the socio-cultural constraints that guide translators’ decisions. Together, these frameworks suggest that translation is not a neutral linguistic transfer but a process of cultural negotiation — an important foundation for examining how language models handle culturally loaded translation, which we will also explore in our subsequent experiments.

3.2 目标语言Target Language

以中国文化为源头，选择日语作为目标语言，作为其文化解释距离Building on Chinese culture as the source, we select Japanese as the target language, as its cultural interpretive distance(Bassnett and Lefèvere, 1990; Toury, 1995) from Chinese strikes a balance. Unlike translations between Chinese and Western languages, which often require extensive cultural explanation, Chinese and Japanese belong to the East Asian cultural sphere and share elements such as the kanji writing system and related aesthetic traditions. This shared foundation allows many culturally loaded concepts to be transferred more directly, while still requiring careful strategic choices when meanings diverge. For example, the Chinese term “阴司” (印西yīnsī) is rendered in the Japanese translation as 「閻魔の庁」. This substitution draws on shared religious conceptions between Chinese and Japanese cultures, where both traditions envision judgment by King Yama after death. In contrast, Western readers’ imagination of the “nether world” tends to evoke Greek Hades or the Christian purgatory, lacking the distinct East Asian imagery of Yama’s judgment. This intermediate cultural distance makes the Chinese–Japanese pair particularly revealing for studying culturally loaded translation.

3.3 翻译版Translation Version
