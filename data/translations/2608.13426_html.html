简化矩阵乘法：LLM 推理的输入自适应矩阵乘积简化Reduced Matrix Multiplication: Input-Adaptive Matrix-Product Reduction for LLM Inference

Zixuan Lan Affiliation: University of Chicago Email: zixuanlan@uchicago.edu    Yanhong Li Affiliation: Independent Researcher Email: yanhong.lbh@gmail.com    Jiawei Zhou Affiliation: Stony Brook University Email: jiawei.zhou.1@stonybrook.edu
摘要Abstract

基于 Transformer 的语言模型具有很强的性能，但由于重复的高维矩阵乘法而产生大量的推理成本。我们提出了简化矩阵乘法（RMM），这是一种免训练、输入自适应的推理方法，它通过沿收缩维度选择信息切片来减少 Transformer 矩阵乘积，而无需修改模型权重。在简单的保留率控制下，RMM 提供了平滑且可预测的准确性与效率的权衡。在从 1B 到 70B 参数的语言模型中，我们发现缩减容差取决于模型族、任务、组件和保留率，尽管它通常会随着模型规模的增加而提高。在适度减少的情况下，RMM 在评估的判别性、自回归生成和长上下文设置中保持稳健。我们进一步表明，同样的原理也适用于多模态视觉语言推理。机械消融揭示了 Transformer 中的结构不对称性：注意力侧计算比 MLP 组件更容易简化。最后，在 NVIDIA A100 上使用自定义内核进行的挂钟基准测试表明，这些计算节省可以转化为实际的运行时间增益，尤其是在序列长度较长的情况下。总之，这些结果将 RMM 定位为输入自适应推理时间优化的可扩展方向。代码和项目资源可在Transformer-based language models achieve strong performance but incur substantial inference cost due to repeated high-dimensional matrix multiplications. We propose Reduced Matrix Multiplication (RMM), a training-free, input-adaptive inference method that reduces Transformer matrix products by selecting informative slices along their contraction dimensions, without modifying model weights. Under a simple retention-ratio control, RMM provides a smooth and predictable accuracy–efficiency trade-off.Across language models ranging from 1B to 70B parameters, we find that reduction tolerance depends on the model family, task, component, and retention ratio, although it often improves with model scale. Under moderate reduction, RMM remains robust across the evaluated discriminative, autoregressive-generation, and long-context settings. We further show that the same principle extends to multimodal vision–language inference. Mechanistic ablations reveal a structural asymmetry within Transformers: attention-side computations are substantially more reducible than MLP components. Finally, wall-clock benchmarks with custom kernels on an NVIDIA A100 show that these computational savings can translate into practical runtime gains, especially at longer sequence lengths. Together, these results position RMM as a scalable direction for input-adaptive inference-time optimization. Code and project resources will be available athttps://github.com/Zesearch/rmm-llm.

1 简介Introduction

变压器型号Transformer models(Vaswani et al. 2017)规模持续提升continue to improve with scale(Kaplan et al. 2020)，但它们的推理成本也快速增长。随着模型尺寸的增加，有效的推理在实践中变得越来越重要。这种成本的主要来源是注意力层和前馈层中重复的高维矩阵乘积。这就提出了一个基本问题：在推理过程中，是否需要针对每个输入评估这些矩阵乘积的共享乘法轴上的所有索引，或者是否可以在保留模型行为的同时自适应地减少部分计算？, but their inference cost also grows rapidly. As model size increases, efficient inference becomes increasingly important in practice. A major source of this cost is the repeated high-dimensional matrix products in attention and feed-forward layers. This raises a basic question: during inference, do all indices along the shared multiplication axes of these matrix products need to be evaluated for every input, or can part of this computation be reduced adaptively while preserving model behavior?

先前的工作从多个角度探索了 Transformer 推理中的冗余计算Prior work has explored redundant computation in Transformer inference from several angles(Liu et al. 2021; Peng et al. 2023; Sajjad et al. 2023)。 一项工作通过结构化剪枝、低秩近似或降维来简化模型计算. One line of work simplifies model computation through structured pruning, low-rank approximation, or dimension reduction(Sun et al. 2024; Ma et al. 2023; Frantar and Alistarh 2023; Ashkboos et al. 2024; Gao et al. 2024)。 另一项工作通过令牌压缩、KV 缓存管理或解码时间调度来减少上下文级别的冗余. Another line of work reduces context-level redundancy through token compression, KV-cache management, or decoding-time scheduling(Li et al. 2023b; Pan et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2023; Xiao et al. 2024; Fu et al. 2025; Shah et al. 2024; Yuan et al. 2025)。 这些方法通过修改固定模型结构、缩短输入或缓存、改变执行流程来降低推理成本，但它们并没有直接询问是否可以针对当前输入自适应地减少每个 Transformer 矩阵乘积内部的缩减计算。 密切相关的方向通过在推理过程中跳过小幅度的激活条目来利用激活稀疏性. These methods reduce inference cost by modifying fixed model structures, shortening inputs or caches, or changing execution flow, but they do not directly ask whether the contracted computation inside each Transformer matrix product can be reduced adaptively for the current input. A closely related direction exploits activation sparsity by skipping small-magnitude activation entries during inference(Liu et al. 2025; Lee et al. 2024)。 尽管此类方法也使用依赖于输入的激活信息，但它们的主要目标是激活张量或隐藏状态的稀疏化。 我们的重点是不同的：我们不是稀疏隐藏状态本身，而是减少每个矩阵乘积的共享乘法轴。 根据操作的不同，该轴可能对应于线性或 MLP 投影中的隐藏通道、注意力分数计算中的注意力头特征维度或注意力值产品中的标记位置。 该框架将 RMM 与固定组件剪枝、激活状态稀疏化和令牌剪枝区分开来：目标是减少每个矩阵乘积在推理时执行的契约计算。. Although such methods also use input-dependent activation information, their primary object is the sparsification of activation tensors or hidden states. Our focus is different: rather than sparsifying hidden states themselves, we reduce the shared multiplication axis of each matrix product. Depending on the operation, this axis may correspond to hidden channels in linear or MLP projections, attention-head feature dimensions in attention-score computation, or token positions in the attention-value product. This framing distinguishes RMM from fixed component pruning, activation-state sparsification, and token pruning: the goal is to reduce the contracted computation performed by each matrix product at inference time.

在这项工作中，我们建议In this work, we propose简化矩阵乘法Reduced Matrix Multiplication(RMM)，一种无需训练、输入自适应的 Transformer 推理方法。 RMM 不是在注意力层和 MLP 层中执行完整的矩阵乘积，而是沿着每个乘积的共享乘法轴动态选择和计算信息索引，而不修改模型权重。除了加速之外，RMM 还提供了一种通过简单的保留率来减少计算的可控方法，从而能够对 Transformer 推理中的冗余进行系统研究。在从 1B 到 70B 参数的模型和不同的下游任务中，我们观察到一个总体趋势，即较大的模型可以容忍更积极的减少，尽管减少的稳健性仍然依赖于模型和任务。我们进一步观察到 Transformer 中明显的结构不对称性：注意力侧计算的可简化性大大提高，而 MLP 组件对简化更加敏感。我们还展示了 RMM 扩展到多模态视觉语言推理，并实现 Triton 内核来验证简化的矩阵乘积可以转化为实际的挂钟加速。(RMM), a training-free, input-adaptive method for Transformer inference. Rather than executing the full matrix products in attention and MLP layers, RMM dynamically selects and computes informative indices along the shared multiplication axis of each product, without modifying model weights. Beyond acceleration, RMM provides a controllable way to reduce computation through a simple retention ratio, enabling a systematic study of redundancy in Transformer inference. Across models ranging from 1B to 70B parameters and diverse downstream tasks, we observe a general trend that larger models tolerate more aggressive reduction, although reduction robustness remains model- and task-dependent. We further observe a clear structural asymmetry within Transformers: attention-side computations are substantially more reducible, whereas MLP components are much more sensitive to reduction. We also show that RMM extends to multimodal vision–language inference, and implement Triton kernels to verify that the reduced matrix products can translate into practical wall-clock speedups.

2 相关工作Related Work

先前的工作表明，Transformer 推理在神经元、层、注意力头和更大的结构单元之间包含大量冗余：模型通常可以容忍修剪或减少，同时保持性能Prior work has shown that Transformer inference contains substantial redundancy across neurons, layers, attention heads, and larger structural units: models can often tolerate pruning or reduction while preserving performance(Peng et al. 2023; Sajjad et al. 2023)。这些发现表明，并非所有推理时间计算都同样不可或缺。. These findings suggest that not all inference-time computation is equally indispensable.

一个主要方向是通过修改或压缩模型结构来减少计算量，包括结构化剪枝、非结构化剪枝、低秩近似、降维等One major direction reduces computation by modifying or compressing model structure, including structured pruning, unstructured pruning, low-rank approximation, and dimension reduction(Sun et al. 2024; Ma et al. 2023; Frantar and Alistarh 2023; Ashkboos et al. 2024; Gao et al. 2024)。此类方法通常在推理之前确定归约模式，并在固定的模型结构上进行操作，回答模型的哪些部分可以提前删除或压缩，而不是计算是否应随当前输入而变化。. Such methods typically determine the reduction pattern before inference and operate on a fixed model structure, answering which parts of the model can be removed or compressed in advance rather than whether computation should vary with the current input.

另一个方向通过令牌压缩、KV 缓存管理、解码时间跳过、稀疏注意力、高效注意力内核或调度优化来减少输入上下文、缓存、注意力模式或执行过程中的冗余Another direction reduces redundancy in the input context, cache, attention pattern, or execution process through token compression, KV-cache management, decoding-time skipping, sparse attention, efficient attention kernels, or scheduling optimization(Li et al. 2023b; Pan et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2023; Xiao et al. 2024; Shah et al. 2024; Yuan et al. 2025)。这些方法可以减少延迟或内存开销，特别是在长上下文设置中，但它们主要作用于输入、缓存令牌、注意力模式或执行流，而不是直接减少注意力和前馈层内的高维矩阵乘积。. These methods can reduce latency or memory overhead, especially in long-context settings, but they mainly act on inputs, cached tokens, attention patterns, or execution flow rather than directly reducing the high-dimensional matrix products inside attention and feed-forward layers.

与我们的工作密切相关的是免训练激活稀疏方法，包括 TEALClosely related to our work are training-free activation-sparsity methods, including TEAL(Liu et al. 2025)和猫and CATS(Lee et al. 2024)，它在推理过程中跳过或屏蔽低幅度的激活条目。这些方法也依赖于输入，并且通常无需培训。对于线性和 MLP 投影，RMM 与激活稀疏性共享相关机制，因为两者都使用当前激活来保留输入维度的子集。主要区别在于公式和范围：激活稀疏方法定义提供给投影层的激活条目的稀疏性，而 RMM 定义通用矩阵乘积的共享收缩轴上的缩减。这种矩阵乘积公式不仅适用于线性和 MLP 投影，还适用于注意力内部乘积，例如, which skip or mask low-magnitude activation entries during inference. These methods are also input-dependent and often training-free. For linear and MLP projections, RMM shares a related mechanism with activation sparsity, since both use the current activations to retain a subset of input dimensions. The main distinction lies in formulation and scope: activation-sparsity methods define sparsity over activation entries supplied to projection layers, whereas RMM defines reduction over the shared contraction axis of a general matrix product. This matrix-product formulation applies not only to linear and MLP projections, but also to attention-internal products such asQ​K⊤QK^{\top}和andP​VPV，其中收缩轴分别对应于注意力头特征维度和标记位置。因此，RMM 与投影设置中的激活稀疏性重叠，同时将相同的简化原理扩展到更广泛的 Transformer 矩阵乘积集。, where the contracted axes correspond to attention-head feature dimensions and token positions, respectively. Thus, RMM overlaps with activation sparsity in the projection setting while extending the same reduction principle to a broader set of Transformer matrix products.

我们的工作也与经典随机近似矩阵乘法相关Our work is also related to classical randomized approximate matrix multiplication(Drineas et al. 2006)，它通过根据重要性分布对列行对进行采样来近似矩阵乘积。原则上，此类估计器可以在单个 Transformer 前向传递中使用。然而，在固定的缩减预算下，随机抽样会引入每个实例的近似方差；减少这种方差通常需要更多的采样对，这会削弱可实现的计算节省。 RMM 采用相同的矩阵乘积视角，同时沿共享乘法轴使用确定性、激活感知的索引选择，产生可预测的约简乘积，我们跨 Transformer 组件评估其效果。总体而言，RMM 采用直接计算的视角：它减少了矩阵乘积本身。, which approximates a matrix product by sampling column-row pairs according to importance distributions. Such estimators can in principle be used within a single Transformer forward pass. However, under a fixed reduction budget, stochastic sampling introduces per-instance approximation variance; reducing this variance generally requires more sampled pairs, which weakens the achievable computational savings. RMM adopts the same matrix-product perspective while using deterministic, activation-aware index selection along the shared multiplication axis, yielding predictable reduced products whose effects we evaluate across Transformer components. Overall, RMM takes a direct computational perspective: it reduces matrix products themselves.

概述。Overview.

我们的实验旨在验证动态、激活感知修剪的有效性以及它在受控保留率下实现的经验见解。我们首先通过在 LLaMA 3.1 8B 上匹配稀疏预算下将 RMM 与代表性权重级别静态剪枝方法进行比较，确定动态选择的必要性。然后，我们扫描从 1B 到 70B 的模型规模的保留率，以检查随着计算减少而性能如何下降以及冗余如何随规模变化。接下来，我们在更现实的推理设置下测试鲁棒性，包括自回归生成和长上下文推理。我们进一步对注意力和 MLP 块进行组件式剪枝分析，以确定哪些计算更冗余，哪些计算更关键。最后，我们评估视觉语言模型的泛化，并报告 NVIDIA A100 GPU 上的挂钟延迟，以验证计算节省是否转化为实际的运行时改进。附录中提供了其他实验，包括与 TEAL 的比较、计算标准化组件分析、对其他 VLM 主干的评估、INT8 兼容性和 LLaMA-70B 延迟Our experiments are structured to validate both the effectiveness of dynamic, activation-aware pruning and the empirical insights it enables under controlled retention ratios. Next, we test robustness under more realistic inference settings, including autoregressive generation and long-context reasoning. We further perform component-wise pruning analyses on attention and MLP blocks to identify which computations are more redundant and which are more critical. Finally, we evaluate generalization on a vision–language model and report wall-clock latency on an NVIDIA A100 GPU to verify that the computational savings translate into actual runtime improvements. 局限性Limitations

Our emphasis is on establishing the core algorithmic idea and evaluating it in a controlled setting. We have not explored extensions beyond the training-free setting considered here. Another promising direction concerns multimodal models. While our results suggest that the framework is applicable beyond pure language modeling, we have not conducted a detailed study of how dynamic reduction behaves across the different components of vision–language models. It would be valuable to test whether their components exhibit different redundancy patterns under dynamic pruning.

降低推理成本的一个自然起点是A natural starting point for reducing inference cost is静态的static修剪： 通过分析参考数据集上的模型行为来识别权重或特征维度的固定子集， 然后永久删除剩余的组件。 这种范式启发了许多现有的方法，我们最初探索了类似的方向。 从系统角度来看，静态剪枝特别有吸引力： 如果在预填充期间可以识别固定的低维子空间， 解码期间可以重复使用相同的子空间， 允许减少 KV 缓存并显着降低内存带宽和 I/O 开销。pruning: one identifies a fixed subset of weights or feature dimensions by analyzing model behavior on a reference dataset, and then permanently removes the remaining components. This paradigm has inspired many existing methods, and we initially explored similar directions. From a systems perspective, static pruning is particularly attractive: if a fixed low-dimensional subspace can be identified during prefilling, the same subspace could be reused during decoding, allowing a reduced KV cache and significantly lower memory bandwidth and I/O overhead.

然而，我们的实证研究表明这种方法并不具有普遍性。 当静态子空间源自特定数据集（例如，WikiText）时， 得到的修剪模型通常在该数据集上表现良好， 但在其他任务或分布上进行评估时，性能会急剧下降。 当我们试图识别注意力头中的“不重要”维度时，我们观察到了类似的行为： 虽然某些维度对于给定任务始终处于不活动状态， 这种模式在任务、层或输入之间并不稳定。However, our empirical investigations revealed that this approach does not generalize. When a static subspace is derived from a specific dataset (e.g., WikiText), the resulting pruned model often performs well on that dataset, but degrades sharply when evaluated on other tasks or distributions. We observed similar behavior when attempting to identify “unimportant” dimensions within attention heads: while certain dimensions appear consistently inactive for a given task, such patterns are not stable across tasks, layers, or inputs.

这些发现表明，变形金刚中的高维表示不承认单一的、 全局有效的低维子空间。 相反，隐藏状态中编码的语义信息似乎可以跨维度迁移 取决于输入、层和解码步骤。 因此，任何固定的修剪规则都隐含地假设信息的静态分配， 并且必须要么牺牲泛化性，要么在积极的修剪下导致严重的准确性损失。These findings suggest that high-dimensional representations in Transformers do not admit a single, globally valid low-dimensional subspace. Instead, the semantic information encoded in hidden states appears to migrate across dimensions depending on the input, the layer, and the decoding step. Consequently, any fixed pruning rule implicitly assumes a static allocation of information, and must either sacrifice generalization or incur severe accuracy loss under aggressive pruning.

即使采用动态选择，我们也观察到足够积极的修剪（例如，50% 保留） 仍然可能导致某些任务的性能下降。 这凸显了免训练加速的更广泛的局限性： 不可能同时实现大幅加速， 无需重新训练，所有设置中的准确性损失都可以忽略不计。 尽管如此，我们的结果提供了强有力的证据，表明大型语言模型中存在大量冗余， 还有那个Even with dynamic selection, we observe that sufficiently aggressive pruning (e.g., 50% retention) can still lead to performance degradation on certain tasks. 更广泛地说，这些观察结果指出了一个有前途的研究方向： 将可压缩性和自适应计算直接纳入预训练目标中， 而不是仅仅依靠事后修剪或缩放。 RMM 通过演示动态子空间计算代表了实现这一目标的第一步 可以作为推理时间效率的可行抽象。More broadly, these observations point to a promising research direction: incorporating compressibility and adaptive computation directly into the pretraining objective, rather than relying solely on post-hoc pruning or scaling.
