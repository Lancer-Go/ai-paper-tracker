自动驾驶的时间解耦扩散规划Temporally Decoupled Diffusion Planning for Autonomous Driving

Xiang Li1, Bikun Wang1, John Zhang1, Jianjun Wang1 Corresponding author.
摘要Abstract

动态城市环境中的运动规划需要平衡当前安全与长期目标。虽然扩散模型有效地捕获了多模态决策，但现有方法将轨迹视为整体实体，忽略了异构时间依赖性，其中近期计划受到瞬时动态的约束，而长期计划受到导航目标的约束。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了时间解耦扩散模型（TDDM），它通过噪声作为掩模范例重新制定轨迹生成。通过将轨迹划分为具有独立噪声水平的片段，我们隐式地将高噪声视为信息空白，将弱噪声视为上下文线索。这迫使模型通过利用与保存更好的时间上下文的内部相关性来重建损坏的近期状态。在架构上，我们引入了时间解耦自适应层标准化（TD-AdaLN）来注入特定于段的时间步长。在推理过程中，我们的非对称时间无分类器指导利用弱噪声远期先验来指导立即路径生成。对 nuPlan 基准的评估显示 TDDM 接近或超过了最先进的基线，特别是在具有挑战性的 Test14-hard 子集中表现出色。Motion planning in dynamic urban environments requires balancing immediate safety with long-term goals. While diffusion models effectively capture multi-modal decision-making, existing approaches treat trajectories as monolithic entities, overlooking heterogeneous temporal dependencies where near-term plans are constrained by instantaneous dynamics and far-term plans by navigational goals. To address this, we propose Temporally Decoupled Diffusion Model (TDDM), which reformulates trajectory generation via a noise-as-mask paradigm. By partitioning trajectories into segments with independent noise levels, we implicitly treat high noise as information voids and weak noise as contextual cues. This compels the model to reconstruct corrupted near-term states by leveraging internal correlations with better-preserved temporal contexts. Architecturally, we introduce a Temporally Decoupled Adaptive Layer Normalization (TD-AdaLN) to inject segment-specific timesteps. During inference, our Asymmetric Temporal Classifier-Free Guidance utilizes weakly noised far-term priors to guide immediate path generation. Evaluations on the nuPlan benchmark show TDDM approaches or exceeds state-of-the-art baselines, particularly excelling in the challenging Test14-hard subset.

简介Introduction

运动规划是自动驾驶的核心技术挑战之一Motion planning is one of the core technical challenges in autonomous driving(Chen et al. 2024a; Aradi 2022)，目标是在动态变化和不确定的环境中生成安全、舒适和类人的轨迹, with the objective of generating safe, comfortable, and human-like trajectories in dynamically changing and uncertain environments(Chen et al. 2024b)。传统的规划方法，例如基于搜索的. Traditional planning methods, such as search-based(Stilman and Kuffner 2008)或基于优化的方法or optimization-based approaches(Fan et al. 2018)，依赖精确的环境模型和复杂的启发式规则, rely on precise environmental models and complex heuristic rules(Treiber et al. 2000; Dauner et al. 2023)。尽管它们在这些规则所涵盖的场景中可以非常高效且表现良好，但它们常常难以应对复杂城市场景的高度动态和交互性. Although they can be highly efficient and perform well in scenarios covered by these rules, they often struggle to cope with the highly dynamic and interactive nature of complex urban scenarios(Leonard et al. 2009).

近年来，基于学习的规划已成为主流范式。通过从大规模数据中学习驾驶策略，这些方法可以更好地推广到复杂多变的现实场景，减少对手工制定规则的依赖。在基于学习的方法中，模仿学习（IL）In recent years, learning-based planning has emerged as the mainstream paradigm. By learning driving policies from large-scale data, these methods can better generalize to complex and variable real-world scenarios, reducing the reliance on hand-crafted rules. Among learning-based approaches, imitation learning (IL)(Pomerleau 1988; Bansal et al. 2018)，尤其是端到端（E2E）方法, especially end-to-end (E2E) methods(Hu et al. 2023; Cheng et al. 2024a)，取得了重大成功。通过直接学习从感知到控制的映射，大大简化了传统规划框架的复杂性。然而，经典的模仿学习方法面临两个核心挑战。第一个是, has achieved significant success. By directly learning the mapping from perception to control, it greatly simplifies the complexity of the traditional planning framework. However, classic imitation learning methods face two core challenges. The first is分布转移distribution shift (Cheng et al. 2024b)，其中测试期间遇到的状态分布与训练数据不同，导致复合错误。第二是难以捕捉内在的, where the state distribution encountered during testing differs from the training data, leading to compounding errors. The second is the difficulty in capturing the inherent多模态决策multi-modal decision-making (Cui et al. 2019; Chen et al. 2024c)真实世界驾驶场景的特征。面对相同的情况，人类驾驶员可能会做出几个同样有效且安全的决策，而单个回归目标通常会产生过于保守或平均的策略。characteristic of real-world driving scenarios. Faced with the same situation, a human driver might make several equally valid and safe decisions, whereas a single regression target often yields overly conservative or averaged-out policies.

为了解决这个问题，最近的文献从确定性回归转向生成序列建模。为了与更广泛的自动化规划社区保持一致，区分这些范式至关重要：经典规划基于显式逻辑约束搜索最佳行动序列，而轨迹生成将任务重新构建为条件概率建模。生成模型不是明确的逻辑搜索，而是学习从学习的数据分布中采样多样化的、上下文合理的未来轨迹。To resolve this, recent literature shifts from deterministic regression to generative sequence modeling. To align with the broader automated planning community, it is crucial to distinguish these paradigms: classical planning searches for an optimal action sequence based on explicit logical constraints, whereas trajectory generation reframes the task as conditional probability modeling. Instead of explicit logical search, generative models learn to sample diverse, contextually plausible future trajectories from a learned data distribution.

结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了时间解耦扩散模型（TDDM），这是一种用于自动驾驶运动规划的新颖框架，解决了整体轨迹生成固有的局限性。通过时间解耦范式重新表述规划问题，我们的模型学会了捕获规划范围内的异构依赖关系。核心贡献——具有独立噪声的时间解耦训练方案、支持的 TD-AdaLN 架构和用于推理的非对称时间无分类器指导策略——协同工作，产生既能对即时条件做出反应又能与长期目标保持一致的轨迹。In this work, we introduced the Temporally Decoupled Diffusion Model (TDDM), a novel framework for autonomous driving motion planning that addresses the limitations inherent in monolithic trajectory generation. By reformulating the planning problem through a temporal decoupling paradigm, our model learns to capture the heterogeneous dependencies across a planning horizon. The core contributions—a temporally-decoupled training scheme with independent noise, a supporting TD-AdaLN architecture, and an Asymmetric Temporal Classifier-Free Guidance strategy for inference—work in synergy to produce trajectories that are both reactive to immediate conditions and consistent with long-term goals.

nuPlan 基准测试表明 TDDMExperiments on the nuPlan benchmark demonstrate that TDDM接近或超过approaches or exceeds最先进的基于学习的方法，在具有挑战性的 Test14-hard 子集上展现出卓越的稳健性和连贯性。这些结果证实，明确建模不同的时间结构对于复杂城市环境中的高性能规划至关重要。state-of-the-art learning-based methods, showcasing exceptional robustness and coherence on the challenging Test14-hard subset. These results confirm that explicitly modeling distinct temporal structures is vital for high-performance planning in complex urban environments.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitiation and future work.

尽管性能强劲，TDDM 在完全反应式闭环仿真中的功效仍有待改进。未来的工作将追求几个有前途的方向：增强代理交互，可能通过博弈论原理扩展我们的 CFG；通过探索历史轨迹的自回归生成，消除对预定义锚集的依赖；并进一步利用时间标记结构来实现更明确和细粒度的轨迹指导。Despite its strong performance, TDDM’s efficacy in fully reactive, closed-loop simulations is an area for improvement. Future work will pursue several promising directions: enhancing agent interaction, potentially by extending our CFG with game-theoretic principles; removing the reliance on a predefined anchor set by exploring autoregressive generation from historical trajectories; and further exploiting the temporal token structure for more explicit and fine-grained trajectory guidance.
