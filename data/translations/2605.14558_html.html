摘要Abstract

代理强化学习使用多轮轨迹训练大型语言模型，这些轨迹将长推理轨迹与短的面向环境的动作交织在一起。常见的策略梯度方法，例如 PPO 和 GRPO，平等地对待轨迹中的每个代币，从而实现统一的信用分配。在本文中，我们批判性地证明了这种统一的信用分配在很大程度上错误地分配了令牌级训练信号。从基于能量的建模角度来看，我们表明，通过与从给定提示中采样的不同推出的奖励方差的相关性来量化的令牌级训练信号，集中于动作令牌而不是推理令牌，即使动作令牌仅占轨迹的一小部分。我们将这种现象称为“行动瓶颈”。受这一观察的启发，我们提出了一种极其简单的令牌重新加权方法，Agentic reinforcement learning trains large language models using multi-turn trajectories that interleave long reasoning traces with short environment-facing actions. Common policy-gradient methods, such as PPO and GRPO, treat each token in a trajectory equally, leading to uniform credit assignment. In this paper, we critically demonstrate that such uniform credit assignment largely misallocates token-level training signals. From an energy-based modeling perspective, we show that token-level training signals, quantified by their correlations with reward variance of different rollouts sampled from a given prompt, concentrate sharply on action tokens rather than reasoning tokens, even though action tokens account for only a small fraction of the trajectory. We refer to this phenomenon as the Action Bottleneck. Motivated by this observation, we propose an embarrassingly simple token reweighting approach,行动焦点ActFocus，它降低了推理令牌的梯度，以及额外的基于能量的重新分配机制，进一步增加了具有更高不确定性的动作令牌的权重。跨越四种环境和不同的模型尺寸，, that downweights gradients on reasoning tokens, along with an additional energy-based redistribution mechanism that further increases the weights on action tokens with higher uncertainty. Across four environments and different model sizes,行动焦点ActFocus始终优于 PPO 和 GRPO，最终步骤增益分别高达 65.2 和 63.7 个百分点，而无需任何额外的运行时或内存成本。consistently outperforms PPO and GRPO, yielding final-step gains of up to 65.2 and 63.7 percentage points, respectively, without any additional runtime or memory cost.

1 简介Introduction

随着大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地部署为结合推理的自主代理As large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents that combine reasoning[8, 9]、规划, planning[37]，以及工具的使用, and tool use[31]为了解决多回合、长视野任务，强化学习 (RL) 已成为顺序决策设置的自然后训练框架to solve multi-turn, long-horizon tasks, reinforcement learning (RL) has emerged as the natural post-training framework for sequential decision-making settings[29, 27, 32, 1]。尽管不同的政策优化方法取得了快速进展. Despite rapid progress with different policy optimization methods[20, 22]，用 RL 训练 LLM 代理仍然很困难。核心挑战之一是学分分配, training LLM agents with RL remains difficult. One of the core challenges is credit assignment[17, 10]，其中，在多轮轨迹中，稀疏的情景奖励必须通过数千个生成的令牌传播回来，这使得很难确定轨迹的哪些部分应该驱动学习。优化不稳定使问题变得更加复杂, where, in multi-turn trajectories, a sparse episodic reward must be propagated back through thousands of generated tokens, making it difficult to determine which parts of a trajectory should drive learning. The problem is compounded by optimization instability[5]，因为延迟反馈、长时间生成的痕迹和嘈杂的策略更新通常会导致后期性能下降或崩溃。, as delayed feedback, long generated traces, and noisy policy updates often lead to degraded late-stage performance or collapse.

最近的证据表明，多回合、未指定的对话大大增加了法学硕士的结果可变性，从而加剧了代理强化学习中可靠学分分配的需求Recent evidence shows that multi-turn, underspecified conversations substantially increase outcome variability for LLMs, sharpening the need for reliable credit assignment in agentic RL[11]。在典型的代理强化学习部署中，这种结果的可变性通常取决于一些驱动状态转换的面向关键环境的动作标记，但这些标记隐藏在轨迹中的大量推理标记中. In a typical agentic RL rollout, such outcome variability often hinges on a few critical environment-facing action tokens that drive state transitions, yet these tokens are buried within massive amounts of reasoning tokens in the trajectory[35, 19, 30]。尽管这些操作令牌是输出中直接与环境交互的唯一部分，但标准策略优化方法会统一处理所有生成的令牌. Even though these action tokens are the only parts of the output that directly interact with the environment, standard policy optimization methods treat all generated tokens uniformly[20, 14]。因此，梯度更新均匀地分布在整个轨迹上，稀释了触发决策的关键动作标记的训练信号. Consequently, gradient updates are spread equally across the whole trajectory, diluting training signals for the critical action tokens that trigger the decision-making[2, 26]。在多轮设置中，这种不匹配会随着每轮额外的轮次而加剧：推理令牌不断累积，而环境仅由少数信息性动作驱动。. In multi-turn settings, this mismatch compounds with each additional turn: reasoning tokens keep accumulating while the environment is driven by only a few informative actions.

推箱子。Sokoban.

Unlike standard navigation problems, Sokoban is irreversible: boxes can be pushed but not pulled back, so an early mistake can permanently destroy a valid solution path. This makes the environment a useful testbed for long-horizon planning and action precision in multi-turn agent training.

结冰的湖。FrozenLake.

The agent must navigate a grid world to reach the goal while avoiding holes, but actions are executed on slippery tiles and may lead to unintended outcomes. As a result, good performance depends not only on moving toward the goal but also on choosing actions that remain robust under transition uncertainty.

网上商店WebShop[34]是一个现实的多轮网络交互环境，代理必须通过搜索、导航、选项选择和购买来满足自然语言购物请求。与符号环境相比，WebShop 引入了更丰富的语言基础、半结构化界面和定义不太明确的操作空间。因此，它可以作为研究当推理和行动在更现实的决策过程中展开时代币级信用分配机制是否继续提供帮助的补充环境。is a realistic multi-turn web interaction environment in which the agent must satisfy a natural-language shopping request through search, navigation, option selection, and purchase.
