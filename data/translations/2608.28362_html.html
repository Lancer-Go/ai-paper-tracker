最佳对抗性测试：从不诚实的考生中提取诚实的测试结果Optimal Adversarial Testing: Extracting Honest Test Results from Dishonest Test Takers

摘要Abstract

在应用程序中，通常需要测试对象或人，以确定他们在某些指标方面的质量。然而，除了自然噪声之外，测试结果还可能因被测试对象或人（测试者）的对抗行为而被破坏。例如，不诚实的考生可能会在考试中作弊，从而歪曲考试结果。随着人工智能技术的发展，这种利用人工智能技术作弊造成的扭曲现象变得越来越普遍和严重。在本文中，我们提出了最佳的测试策略，即使存在作弊者污染结果，仍然可以恢复所需的测试结果。所提出的测试策略将使用不同的测试安全措施对选定的一组测试者进行最佳重新测试。我们使用动态规划方法确定最佳测试策略。In applications, it is often required to test objects or people to determine their qualities in terms of certain metrics. However, besides being naturally noisy, the test results can be corrupted by adversarial behaviors of objects or people being tested (test takers). For example, dishonest test takers can cheat in the exams to distort the test results. With the development of AI technologies, such distortions driven by cheating using AI technologies are becoming more commonplace and severe. In this paper, we propose optimal testing strategies which can still recover needed test results even if there are cheaters polluting the results. The proposed testing strategies will optimally re-test selected group of test takers using different testing security measures. We determine the optimal testing strategies using a dynamic programming method.

5 结论、讨论和未来方向Conclusions, discussions and future directions

在应用程序中，通常需要测试对象或人，以确定他们在某些指标方面的质量。然而，除了自然噪声之外，测试结果还可能因被测试对象或人（测试者）的对抗行为而被破坏。例如，不诚实的考生可能会在考试中作弊，从而歪曲考试结果。随着人工智能技术的发展，这种扭曲变得越来越普遍。在本文中，我们提出了最佳的测试策略，即使存在作弊者污染结果，仍然可以恢复所需的测试结果。所提出的测试策略将使用不同的测试安全措施对选定的一组测试者进行最佳重新测试。我们使用动态规划方法确定最佳测试策略。在未来的工作中，我们将通过连续版本的测试问题来扩展我们的工作，其中测试的学生数量很大，并且需要每人监考成本的学生部分In applications, it is often required to test objects or people to determine their qualities in terms of certain metrics. However, besides being naturally noisy, the test results can be corrupted by adversarial behaviors of objects or people being tested (test takers). For example, dishonest test takers can cheat in the exams to distort the test results. With the development of AI technologies, such distortions are becoming more commonplace. In this paper, we propose optimal testing strategies which can still recover needed test results even if there are cheaters polluting the results. The proposed testing strategies will optimally re-test selected group of test takers using different testing security measures. We determine the optimal testing strategies using a dynamic programming method. In future works, we will extend our work with a continuous version of the testing problem where the number of tested students is large and the portion of students that needs a per-person proctoring cost ofxx可以用（可能连续值）概率密度函数来描述can be described by a (possibly-continuously-valued) probability density function ofp⁡(x)p(x)这是在连续值上定义的which is defined over continuously-valuedxx。在本文中，我们考虑忠实地找出顶部.
