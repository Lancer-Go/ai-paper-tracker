摘要Abstract

人类与人工智能协作写作的兴起对细粒度检测方法的需求不断增长，这些方法支持在混合作者文档中本地化可能由法学硕士生成的内容。现有的检测LLM生成文本的方法主要集中于文档级分类，无法识别文本的哪些部分是由LLM生成的。本文介绍了一种新方法来解决这一迫切需求。我们的方法在令牌级别（现代语言模型的自然单位）上运行，并建立在现有令牌级别检测分数的基础上。关键思想是平滑相邻标记分数以减少其变异性，同时使用自适应 Lepski 型规则根据本地作者结构选择带宽。我们的方法实现起来很简单，并且不需要令牌级标记数据进行训练。从理论上讲，我们描述了这种权衡，并表明所提出的方法在估计基础信号时实现了良好的均方误差性能。根据经验，我们在合成数据集和实际数据集中的各种基线上展示了我们的方法的强大性能。我们部署一个可公开访问的网站来实现这些方法The rise of human-AI collaborative writing has created a growing need for fine-grained detection methods that support localizing likely LLM-generated content in mixed-authorship documents. Existing methods for detecting LLM-generated text mainly focus on document-level classification and cannot identify which parts of the text are generated by LLMs. This paper introduces a new method to address this urgent need. Our method operates at the token level, the natural unit of modern language models, and builds on existing token-level detection scores. The key idea is to smooth adjacent token scores to reduce their variability, while using an adaptive Lepski-type rule to select the bandwidth according to the local authorship structure. Our method is simple to implement and does not require token-level labeled data for training. Theoretically, we characterize this trade-off and show that the proposed method achieves favorable mean square error performance in estimating the underlying signal. Empirically, we demonstrate strong performance of our method against a wide range of baselines in both synthetic datasets and a realistic dataset. We deploy a publicly accessible website that implements the methodshttps://huggingface.co/spaces/Jasmineame/detect-LLM-tokens.

1 简介Introduction

大语言模型 (LLM) 生成的文本越来越多地用于学术写作、教育、新闻制作和创意写作Large language model (LLM)-generated text is increasingly used in academic writing, education, news production, and creative writing(Milano et al., 2023; Demszky et al., 2023; Doshi and Hauser, 2024; Xie et al., 2023)。在实践中，最终文档通常既不是完全由人工编写，也不是完全由法学硕士生成，而是通常包含交错的人工编写和法学硕士生成的片段。例如，作者可以在使用法学硕士重写选定的段落、生成图形描述或继续稍后由人类编辑的故事时起草主要论点。随着法学硕士的不断发展，这种人类与法学硕士的共同创作变得越来越普遍. In practice, a final document is often neither entirely human-written nor entirely LLM-generated, but typically contains interleaved human-written and LLM-generated segments. For example, an author may draft the main argument while using an LLM to rewrite selected passages, generate descriptions of figures, or continue a story that is later edited by a human. Such human–LLM coauthoring has become increasingly common as LLMs continue to evolve(Lee et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2024a).

在这种设置中，文档级检测过于粗略：文档级检测器可能表明文本包含 LLM 生成的内容，但它无法识别 LLM 贡献的位置或其贡献如何在整个文档中分布。这种限制在科学出版和编辑评论中很重要In such settings, document-level detection is too coarse: a document-level detector may indicate that a text contains LLM-generated content, but it cannot identify where the LLM contributed or how its contribution is distributed throughout the document. This limitation matters in scientific publishing and editorial review(Thorp, 2023; Nature Editorial, 2023)以及人工智能使用值得披露的法律、政策和合规文件, as well as in legal, policy, and compliance documents where AI usage is worthy to be disclosed(European Parliament and Council of the European Union, 2024)。本地化人工智能生成的文本也有助于缩小幻觉检查的范围. Localizing AI-generated text can also help narrow the scope of hallucination checking(Farquhar et al., 2024)。受这些启发，我们研究了一个更细粒度的问题：识别人类与法学硕士共同撰写的文档中各个标记的作者身份。令牌是此任务的自然单位，因为它们是现代语言模型的基本输入输出单位，并且可以映射回本地化的文本片段。与句子或段落级别的检测相比. Motivated by these, we study a finer-grained problem: identifying the authorship of individual tokens in a human–LLM coauthored document. Tokens are a natural unit for this task because they are the basic input-output units of modern language models and can be mapped back to localized segments of text. Compared with sentence- or paragraph-level detection(Zhang et al., 2024b; Su et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026)，标记级检测更适合人工编辑和 LLM 生成的片段在句子内、跨句子边界或段落内交错的情况。该公式提供了一种可解释的方法，可以在一般人类与法学硕士协作环境中本地化法学硕士创作的内容, token-level detection is better suited to cases where human edits and LLM-generated fragments are interleaved within a sentence, across sentence boundaries, or inside a paragraph. This formulation provides an interpretable way to localize LLM-authored content in general human–LLM collaboration settings(Zeng et al., 2024a).

1.1 相关作品Related works

LLM 生成的文档检测LLM-generated document detection。现有的 LLM 生成文本检测工作主要研究了区分人类创作的文档和 LLM 创作的文档的二元分类问题。概括地说，这些方法分为四类。第一类由基于统计的检测器组成，它们根据标记对数概率、排名、似然曲率或其他相关量构建检测分数，然后在整个文档中聚合这些量. Existing work on LLM-generated text detection has primarily studied the binary classification problem of distinguishing human-authored document from LLM-authored document. Broadly speaking, these methods fall into four categories. The first category consists of statistics-based detectors, which construct detection scores from token log-probabilities, ranks, likelihood curvature or other related quantities, and then aggregate these quantities over the whole document(Gehrmann et al., 2019; Su et al., 2023; Bao et al., 2024; Hans et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2025)。第二类包括基于机器学习 (ML) 的检测器。一些方法从文本中提取某些特征统计数据并训练经典分类器，例如逻辑回归. The second category consists of machine learning (ML)-based detectors. Some methods extract certain feature statistics from text and train classical classifiers such as logistic regression(Tulchinskii et al., 2023; Guo et al., 2024; Mao et al., 2024; Verma et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2024);一些方法在聚合之前学习自适应标记级评分函数; some methods learn adaptive token-level scoring functions before aggregation(Chen et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025);其他人微调预训练的语言模型，例如基于 BERT 或 RoBERTa 的模型，用于文本分类; others fine-tunes pretrained language models, such as BERT- or RoBERTa-based models, for text classification(Solaiman et al., 2019; Ippolito et al., 2020; Hu et al., 2023; Tian et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2019)。第三类由基于重构或扰动的检测器组成，它们将原始文本与通过语言模型重构、扰动或以其他方式转换的文本进行比较. The third category consists of reconstruction- or perturbation-based detectors, which compare the original text with text reconstructed, perturbed, or otherwise transformed by a language model(Mitchell et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2026)。第四类是基于水印的方法. The fourth category is the watermarking-based methods(Kirchenbauer et al., 2023; Zhao et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025; Xie et al., 2025)。这些方法将可检测的统计信号嵌入到输出文本中，并根据检测这些信号的存在来检测法学硕士或人类创作的文本。. These methods embed detectable statistical signals into the output text, and detect LLM- or human-authored text based on detecting the existence of these signals.

尽管文档级检测器可以通过将每个标记视为文档来适应标记级检测，但这种粗略的过程会丢失整个文本的信息，因为每个标记都是独立分析的。另一种策略是在本地应用文档级检测器：对于每个目标标记，可以在该标记周围的短邻域上评估检测器，并使用所得分数来确定标记的作者身份。这种局部适应很有吸引力，因为它重用现有的检测器，而不需要额外的监督训练，但它本身并不能解决相邻令牌应如何加权或应如何选择本地带宽。这些问题激发了我们在本文中提出的建议。Although document-level detectors can adapt token-level detection by treating each token as a document, such a crude procedure loses information from the whole text because each token is analyzed independently. An alternative strategy is to apply a document-level detector locally: for each target token, one can evaluate the detector on a short neighborhood around that token and use the resulting score for determining the token’s authorship. This local adaptation is attractive because it reuses existing detectors without requiring additional supervised training, but it does not by itself resolve how neighboring tokens should be weighted or how the local bandwidth should be chosen. These issues motivate our proposal developed in this paper.

人类与人工智能共同创作下的法学硕士本地化LLM localization under human–AI coauthoring。在实践中，更有意义的目标是本地化 LLM 生成的文本，而不仅仅是对整个文档进行分类。现有的定位方法可大致分为基于机器学习的方法和基于分割的方法。基于 ML 的方法收集具有句子或标记级标签的混合作者数据集，并训练基于 RoBERTa 或 Transformer 的模型来预测作者标签. In practice, a more meaningful objective is to localize LLM-generated text rather than only to classify the entire document. Existing localization methods can be broadly divided into ML- and segmentation-based approaches. ML-based methods collect mixed-authorship datasets with sentence- or token-level labels and train RoBERTa- or transformer-based models to predict authorship labels(Bai et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023; Kushnareva et al., 2024; Zeng et al., 2024b, a; Zhang et al., 2024b; Li et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2025; Su et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2026)。然而，基于机器学习的方法需要句子或标记级别的标记数据，收集成本很高。相比之下，基于分段的方法通过将文本划分为预定义的单元（最常见的是句子或段落），避免了额外的 ML 模型训练。然后，他们通过选择这些单元之间的过渡点来识别法学硕士生成的区域。后一个任务是通过借鉴运筹学和统计学的技术解决集合优化或变化点检测问题来实现的. However, ML-based methods require labeled data at the sentence- or token-level, which is costly to collect. Segmentation-based methods, by contrast, avoid additional ML model training, by partitioning text into predefined units, most commonly sentences or paragraphs. They then identify LLM-generated regions by selecting transition points between these units. The latter task is achieved by solving a set optimization or change-point detection problem, borrowing techniques from operations research and statistics(see, e.g. Lei et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026)。还引入了几个人类与法学硕士共同创作的数据集来支持这些定位方法的评估. Several human–LLM coauthored datasets have also been introduced to support the evaluation of these localization methods(Lee et al., 2022; Dugan et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024)。对于基于分割的方法，其定位精度本质上受限于预定义单元的水平。这些方法隐含地假设每个预定义单元内的作者身份是同质的。因此，如果作者身份边界落在句子内，则此类方法无法对其进行本地化。. For segmentation-based methods, their localization precision is inherently limited to the level of the predefined units. These methods implicitly assume that authorship is homogeneous within each predefined unit. Consequently, if an authorship boundary falls within a sentence, such methods cannot localize it.

为了解决上述限制，我们提出了一种新颖的令牌级检测方法，该方法在每个令牌位置本地应用文档级检测统计数据。然后，通过具有自适应带宽的内核加权平滑，在相邻令牌之间聚合这些每个令牌的本地统计数据。这种设计不仅消除了对标记训练数据的需求，而且实现了细粒度的标记级本地化，而不对底层作者边界施加限制性假设。To address the above-mentioned limitations, we propose a novel token-level detection method, which applies a document-level detection statistic locally at each token position. These per-token local statistics are then aggregated across neighboring tokens via kernel-weighted smoothing with an adaptive bandwidth. This design not only eliminates the requirement for labeled training data, but also achieves fine-grained token-level localization, without imposing restrictive assumptions on the underlying authorship boundaries.

7 结论Conclusion

我们研究了人类与法学硕士共同撰写的文本中法学硕士生成的内容的标记级本地化。所提出的框架平滑了相邻标记上的标记级检测分数，并使用 Lepski 类型规则来使带宽适应本地作者结构。我们的理论解释了局部聚合产生的偏差-方差权衡，我们的实验表明，自适应聚合可以提高综合和现实混合作者设置的性能。未来的工作可能会将框架扩展到多语言文本、更强的对抗性编辑以及更丰富的人机交互形式。We studied token-level localization of LLM-generated content in human–LLM coauthored text. The proposed framework smooths token-level detection scores over neighboring tokens and uses a Lepski-type rule to adapt the bandwidth to local authorship structure. Our theory explains the bias–variance trade-off created by local aggregation, and our experiments show that adaptive aggregation improves performance across synthetic and realistic mixed-authorship settings. Future work may extend the framework to multilingual text, stronger adversarial editing, and richer forms of human–AI interaction.
