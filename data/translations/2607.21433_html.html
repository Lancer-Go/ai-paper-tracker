1 简介Introduction

思想链（CoT）提示的出现The emergence of chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting(Wei et al., 2022)以及专用推理模型，例如 OpenAI 的 o1and dedicated reasoning models such as OpenAI’s o1(OpenAI, 2024)和 DeepSeek-R1and DeepSeek-R1(DeepSeek-AI, 2025)将大型语言模型 (LLM) 推理中的计算瓶颈从模型大小转移到生成长度。 这些系统在得出最终答案之前会产生扩展的内部独白（通常是数千个标记）。 该领域普遍的假设是，较长的推理会产生更好的结果，这一假设支撑了人们对测试时计算扩展日益增长的兴趣has shifted the computational bottleneck in large language model (LLM) inference from model size to generation length. These systems produce extended internal monologues—often thousands of tokens—before arriving at a final answer. The prevailing assumption in the field is that longer reasoning yields better outcomes, an assumption that underpins the growing interest in test-time compute scaling(Snell et al., 2024).

然而，这一假设在各代人的层面上受到了有限的实证检验。 虽然总体基准显示推理模型优于非推理模型，但仍不清楚所产生的思维有多少实际上是富有成效的。 在超时之前产生 10,000 个循环推理令牌的一代所消耗的计算量与完全收敛于 4,000 个令牌的一代所消耗的计算量相同，但这两种结果在准确性上存在巨大差异。This assumption, however, has received limited empirical scrutiny at the level of individual generations. While aggregate benchmarks show that reasoning models outperform their non-reasoning counterparts, it remains unclear how much of the generated thinking is actually productive. A generation that produces 10,000 tokens of circular reasoning before timing out consumes the same compute as one that converges cleanly at 4,000 tokens—yet the two outcomes differ drastically in accuracy.

我们通过对 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B 进行的两项研究来调查这个问题，这是一个从 DeepSeek-R1 中提取并基于 Qwen2 架构构建的 70 亿参数模型We investigate this question through two studies conducted on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, a 7-billion parameter model distilled from DeepSeek-R1 and built on the Qwen2 architecture(Yang and others, 2024)。 我们通过涵盖广泛难度范围的三个基准对其进行评估： GSM8K. We evaluate it across three benchmarks spanning a wide difficulty range: GSM8K(Cobbe et al., 2021)（小学），MATH-500(grade school), MATH-500(Hendrycks et al., 2021)（竞赛级别），以及 AIME 1983–2024 的 200 个问题子集(competition-level), and a 200-problem subset of AIME 1983–2024(Neubig, 2024)（奥林匹克级别）。(olympiad-level).

在第二项研究中（第In the second study (Section4），我们询问在失败变得行为明显之前，这种收敛失败是否可以根据模型的内部状态进行预测。 我们使用前向钩子来检测模型，以捕获推理链中早期检查点的隐藏状态激活，然后训练线性探针来预测最终的收敛。 通过将这些探针与仅使用表面信号（logit 熵、重复统计）的行为基线进行比较，我们分离出内部表征对早期检测的贡献。), we ask whether this convergence failure is predictable from the model’s internal state before the failure becomes behaviorally apparent. 对历史数据的潜在记忆。Potential memorization on historical data.我们对 AIME 2025 的污染检查表明，6.6% 的非收敛历史生成是正确的，这可能是由于部分记忆了答案值。这种影响在截断后的数据中不存在，这表明我们的历史基线稍微高估了模型解决新问题的真实数学能力。Our contamination check on AIME 2025 shows that 6.6% of non-converged historical generations are correct, likely due to partial memorization of answer values. This effect is absent on post-cutoff data, suggesting our historical baseline slightly overestimates the model’s true mathematical capability on novel problems.

难度代理。Difficulty proxy.AIME 问题编号可以作为不完美的难度代理。每项考试中的问题并不是按难度统一排序的，而且不同考试年份的难度也有所不同。AIME problem number serves as an imperfect difficulty proxy. Problems are not uniformly ordered by difficulty within each exam, and difficulty varies across exam years.

7 结论Conclusion

我们提供的证据表明，思维链推理模型表现出两种不同的低效率模式：预算饱和，其中准确性在模型最大思维长度的一小部分处停滞不前，以及双峰收敛失败，其中很大一部分代人进入非终止循环，消耗全部代币预算而不影响准确性。We have presented evidence that chain-of-thought reasoning models exhibit two distinct inefficiency patterns: budget saturation, where accuracy plateaus at a small fraction of the model’s maximum thinking length, and bimodal convergence failure, where a substantial fraction of generations enter non-terminating loops that consume the full token budget without contributing to accuracy.

更引人注目的是，我们已经证明，收敛结果早在行为表现之前就已部分编码在模型的内部表示中。 隐藏状态激活的线性探针在 9 个测试检查点中的 8 个处显示出比行为基线一致的方向优势，信号可在任何表面故障指标出现之前早在 50 个标记处检测到。 最强的信号出现在第 20 层（共 28 层），这表明收敛信息是在中上表示层中编码的，而不是在输出相邻层中编码的。More strikingly, we have shown that the convergence outcome is partially encoded in the model’s internal representations well before it manifests behaviorally. A linear probe on hidden-state activations shows a consistent directional advantage over behavioral baselines at 8 of 9 tested checkpoints, with signal detectable as early as 50 tokens—before any surface-level indicators of failure appear. The strongest signal is found at layer 20 (of 28), suggesting convergence information is encoded in upper-middle representational layers rather than output-adjacent ones.

这些发现指出了一类推理时间优化策略，这些策略基于模型自身的内部表示而不是行为启发法。 虽然当前的探针精度不足以满足生产部署，但一致（如果适度）的早期信号的存在确立了机械提前退出方法的可行性，并激励通过更具表现力的探针、更大的数据集和因果验证进行进一步的研究。These findings point toward a class of inference-time optimization strategies that are grounded in the model’s own internal representations rather than in behavioral heuristics. While the current probe accuracy is insufficient for production deployment, the existence of a consistent, if modest, early signal establishes the feasibility of mechanistic early-exit approaches and motivates further investigation with more expressive probes, larger datasets, and causal validation.
