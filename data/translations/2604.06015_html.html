摘要Abstract

通常认为指令调优赋予语言模型遵循指令的领域通用能力，但其底层机制仍然知之甚少。遵循指令是否依赖于通用机制或组合技能部署？我们通过对三个指令调整模型中的九个不同任务进行诊断探索来研究这一点。Instruction tuning is commonly assumed to endow language models with a domain-general ability to follow instructions, yet the underlying mechanism remains poorly understood. Does instruction-following rely on a universal mechanism or compositional skill deployment? We investigate this through diagnostic probing across nine diverse tasks in three instruction-tuned models.

我们的分析提供了反对普遍机制的一致证据。 首先，在所有任务中训练的通用探针始终表现不佳，这表明代表性共享有限。 其次，跨任务迁移较弱，且因技能相似性而聚集。 第三，因果消融揭示了稀疏的不对称依赖关系，而不是共享的表示。任务还根据跨层的复杂性进行分层，结构约束较早出现，语义任务较晚出现。 最后，时间分析表明约束满足在发电过程中作为动态监控而不是发电前规划来运行。Our analysis provides converging evidence against a universal mechanism. First, general probes trained across all tasks consistently underperform task-specific specialists, indicating limited representational sharing. Second, cross-task transfer is weak and clustered by skill similarity. Third, causal ablation reveals sparse asymmetric dependencies rather than shared representations. Tasks also stratify by complexity across layers, with structural constraints emerging early and semantic tasks emerging late. Finally, temporal analysis shows constraint satisfaction operates as dynamic monitoring during generation rather than pre-generation planning.

这些发现表明，指令遵循更好地被描述为不同语言能力的熟练协调，而不是部署单一的抽象约束检查过程。These findings indicate that instruction-following is better characterized as skillful coordination of diverse linguistic capabilities rather than deployment of a single abstract constraint-checking process.

5.5 结果总结Summary of results

我们的综合分析提供了针对指令调整法学硕士中通用的、与任务无关的约束满足机制的汇聚证据。在四个互补的实验中，我们一致发现约束满足是通过组合技能共享而不是专用机制来实现的。普通探头的表现不如专家；跨任务转移有限且任务集群化；消融揭示了稀疏、不对称的依赖性；任务按复杂性分层，包括早期出现的结构任务和晚期出现的语义任务。特异性分析显示了模型相关的变化：Llama 表现出特定于约束的信号，而 Qwen 严重依赖通用语言建模特征，这种特异性与跨任务泛化能力相关。基于 PWCCA 的树状图显示了很大程度上不同的任务表示以及特定于模型的组织模式，而不是通用技能共享。Our comprehensive analysis provides converging evidence against a universal, task-agnostic constraint satisfaction mechanism in instruction-tuned LLMs. Across four complementary experiments, we consistently find that constraint satisfaction operates through compositional skill sharing rather than a dedicated mechanism. General probes underperform specialists; cross-task transfer is limited and task-clustered; ablation reveals sparse, asymmetric dependencies; and tasks stratify by complexity with early-emerging structural tasks and late-emerging semantic tasks. The specificity analysis shows model-dependent variation: Llama exhibits constraint-specific signals while Qwen relies heavily on general language modeling features, with this specificity correlating with cross-task generalization ability. PWCCA-based dendrograms show largely distinct task representations with model-specific organizational patterns rather than universal skill sharing.

时间动态揭示了约束满足在生成过程中动态出现，而不是通过生成前规划。探针精度在即时处理期间保持在基线，在生成开始时急剧上升，在主体生成期间稳定（连续监测），并在 EOS 时达到峰值（验证）。这种时间分布，与任务依赖性的组成结构和特定于模型的编码策略相结合，表明法学硕士中的指令遵循更好地被描述为不同语言能力的熟练协调，而不是部署单一的抽象约束检查过程。Temporal dynamics reveal that constraint satisfaction emerges dynamically during generation rather than through pre-generation planning. Probe accuracy remains at baseline during prompt processing, rises sharply at generation onset, stabilizes during body generation (continuous monitoring), and peaks at EOS (verification). This temporal profile, combined with the compositional structure of task dependencies and model-specific encoding strategies, suggests that instruction-following in LLMs is better characterized as skillful coordination of diverse linguistic capabilities rather than deployment of a single abstract constraint-checking process.

6 结论Conclusions

这项工作挑战了指令调整的法学硕士通过统一的、与任务无关的机制实现约束满足的假设。相反，我们的证据指向一种基本的组合架构，其中根据任务​​要求和特定于模型的组织策略来协调不同的语言技能。This work challenges the assumption that instruction-tuned LLMs implement constraint satisfaction through a unified, task-agnostic mechanism. Instead, our evidence points toward a fundamentally compositional architecture where diverse linguistic skills are coordinated depending on task requirements and model-specific organizational strategies.

约束的分层出现（早期层是结构性的，后面层是语义性的）与稀疏任务依赖性和动态时间配置文件相结合，表明指令跟随来自学习的协调模式，而不是专用的约束检查模块。不同模型在如何编码和组成约束方面存在巨大差异，这表明不同的架构针对相同的指令跟踪挑战开发了不同的解决方案。The hierarchical emergence of constraints (structural in early layers, semantic in later layers) combined with sparse task dependencies and dynamic temporal profiles, indicates that instruction-following arises from learned coordination patterns rather than dedicated constraint-checking modules. Substantial variation across models in how constraints are encoded and composed shows that different architectures develop distinct solutions to the same instruction-following challenges.

这些发现为未来的研究开辟了重要方向。下一步关键是研究约束满足背后的基本语言原语的几何组织——了解这些技能如何在表征空间中排列，什么决定了它们的可组合性，以及它们的几何关系如何实现或限制灵活的协调。These findings open important directions for future research. A critical next step is to investigate the geometric organization of the fundamental linguistic primitives underlying constraint satisfaction—understanding how these skills are arranged in representational space, what determines their composability, and how their geometric relationships enable or constrain flexible coordination.

虽然我们的研究为遵循指令的组成性质提供了一致的证据，但我们承认我们的方法和实验范围存在一些局限性。While our study provides converging evidence for the compositional nature of instruction-following, we acknowledge several limitations regarding our methodology and experimental scope.
