We present Multi-Method Causal Evidence Synthesis (MCES), a framework whose goal is to rank哪些候选司机which candidate drivers在观察系统中最有可能与一组结果变量相关，以及证据的强度。 MCES 在观察面板数据上运行涵盖八种不同数学传统的 11 种分析方法，并将其输出汇集到对驾驶员与结果关系进行排名的收敛证据评分 (CES) 中。 CES 量化了in an observational system are most likely relevant to a set of outcome variables, and with what strength of evidence. MCES runs eleven analytical methods across eight distinct mathematical traditions on observational panel data and pools their outputs into a Convergent Evidence Score (CES) that ranks driver–outcome relationships. 1.1 问题的普遍性The Ubiquity of the Problem

跨行业的组织通常面临一个基本问题：Organizations across industries routinely face a fundamental question:“为什么这个指标会发生变化，是什么原因导致的？”“Why did this metric change, and what caused it?”无论制造商在更换供应商后发现缺陷率上升，学区在课程更改后发现测试分数下降，还是 SaaS 公司在产品更新后发现保留率下降，诊断挑战都是相同的。观测数据丰富；因果关系的理解很少。Whether a manufacturer detects rising defect rates after changing suppliers, a school district sees test scores fall after a curriculum change, or a SaaS company sees falling retention after a product update, the diagnostic challenge is the same. Observational data is abundant; causal understanding is scarce.

10 结论Conclusion

观察因果推断是科学和工业领域最普遍的挑战之一。从业者绝大多数依赖单一的分析方法，每种方法都有详细记录的盲点。自动因果推理的最新进展集中在Observational causal inference is among the most ubiquitous challenges across science and industry. Practitioners overwhelmingly rely on single analytical methods, each with well-documented blind spots. Recent advances in automated causal inference have focused on选择selecting数据集的最佳方法或the optimal method for a dataset or聚合aggregating相同算法的实例，但都没有解决特定于方法的故障模式的基本限制。instances of the same algorithm, but neither addresses the fundamental limitation of method-specific failure modes.

MCES 将定性建议付诸实践，“使用多种方法，看看他们是否同意”MCES operationalizes the qualitative recommendation to “use multiple methods and see if they agree”(Munafò and Davey Smith 2018)。通过在八种不同的数学传统中运行十一种方法，并将它们不可通约的输出汇集到一个收敛证据分数中，它通过跨方法一致性产生了候选驾驶员-结果关系的可重复排名。结构行为分解可以防止特定类别的误报、代数恒等边缘，而这恰恰是在恒等组件是候选驱动因素的情况下进行的。. By running eleven methods across eight distinct mathematical traditions and pooling their non-commensurable outputs into a Convergent Evidence Score, it produces a reproducible ranking of candidate driver–outcome relationships by cross-method agreement. Structural–Behavioral Decomposition prevents a specific class of false positives, algebraic-identity edges, exactly in the setting where identity components are candidate drivers.

根据经验，在合成地面实况、真正的 Sachs 基准、六个外部贝叶斯网络结构基准以及两个额外的合成域上，MCES 将真实边缘置于其排名的首位 (Precision@5Empirically, on synthetic ground truth, the real Sachs benchmark, six external Bayesian-network structure benchmarks, and two additional synthetic domains, MCES places true edges at the top of its ranking (Precision@5== 1.01.0在主要场景上），同时显示空对在评估场景上达到中等或更高收敛的经验率较低。评估是故意不偏不倚的：池子并没有统一支配各个方法（在某些场景下单个方法得分更高），其价值在于它是一个on the primary scenario) while showing a low empirical rate of null pairs reaching Moderate-or-higher convergence on the evaluated scenarios. The evaluation is deliberately even-handed: the pool does not uniformly dominate individual methods (on some scenarios a single method scores higher), and its value is that it is a与方法无关的默认值method-agnostic default，由于没有哪种方法在评估的场景中是一致最佳的，因此池化可以避免在正确选择未知时提前采用单一分析传统。, since no individual method is uniformly best across the evaluated scenarios, so pooling avoids committing in advance to a single analytical tradition when the right choice is unknown.

核心要点是设计低调。 MCES 总结了异构分析方法是否收敛于相同的候选关系。它的目的是在方法选择不确定时优先考虑假设，而不是取代实验识别或明确的因果设计，并且其证据评分不是可转移的因果关系概率。作者可根据要求提供参考实现。The central takeaway is modest by design. MCES summarizes whether heterogeneous analytical approaches converge on the same candidate relationship. It is intended to prioritize hypotheses when method choice is uncertain, not to replace experimental identification or a well-specified causal design, and its evidence score is not a transferable probability of causation.
