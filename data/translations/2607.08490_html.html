摘要Abstract

随着新发现的出现，生物医学语言迅速发展，导致传统文本模型随着时间的推移失去语义保真度。 静态嵌入和共现图无法捕获这种演变，导致检索和知识发现任务的性能下降。本文介绍了一个Biomedical language evolves rapidly as new discoveries emerge, causing traditional text models to lose semantic fidelity over time. Static embeddings and co-occurrence graphs cannot capture such evolution, leading to performance degradation in retrieval and knowledge discovery tasks. I 简介INTRODUCTION

生物医学出版物的指数级增长已将科学文本转变为不断发展的知识生态系统。 PubMed 和相关存储库中每周都会出现数以千计的新摘要，引入新颖的概念、药物-化合物相互作用以及改变该领域语义结构的术语。捕获这种概念演变对于文献挖掘、知识图谱管理和检索增强生成中的应用至关重要。然而，大多数基于图或基于嵌入的文本模型仍然假设单词和实体之间的静态关系，随着时间的推移，随着含义的变化，这种关系很快就会过时。The exponential growth of biomedical publications has transformed scientific text into a continuously evolving knowledge ecosystem. Thousands of new abstracts appear weekly in PubMed and related repositories, introducing novel concepts, drug-compound interactions, and terminologies that shift the semantic structure of the domain. Capturing such concept evolution is critical for applications in literature mining, knowledge graph curation, and retrieval-augmented generation. However, most graph-based or embedding-based text models still assume static relationships between words and entities, which quickly become outdated as meaning drifts over time.

传统的共现图Traditional co-occurrence graphs[1]以及词嵌入，例如 word2vecand word embeddings such as word2vec[2]或手套or GloVe[3]将语义视为静止的。事实上，“免疫检查点”、“mRNA 疫苗”或“病毒载体”等生物医学术语随着研究的进展而演变，导致可测量的语义漂移treat semantics as stationary. In reality, biomedical terms like “immune checkpoint,” “mRNA vaccine,” or “viral vector” evolve as research advances, resulting in measurable semantic drift[4, 5, 7]。因此，静态模型在用于下游分析时会歪曲新兴关联并降低检索准确性。. Static models therefore misrepresent emerging associations and reduce retrieval accuracy when used for downstream analysis.

为了缓解这种情况，几个时间嵌入框架To mitigate this, several temporal embedding frameworks[6, 7]和动态图神经网络（DGNN）及其调查and dynamic graph neural networks (DGNNs), and surveys thereof[8, 10]，已提出。虽然有效，但许多方法需要对每个时间片进行重新训练或顺序微调；这些方法会产生大量的计算成本并限制可解释性。此外，大多数关注节点级漂移，即单个术语向量如何变化，而不是边缘级漂移，后者捕获术语之间不断变化的共现结构。 DATGR 不是重新训练嵌入，而是使用漂移加权逻辑更新规则重新连接单词共现图，从而无需完全重新嵌入即可实现连续适应。评价于, have been proposed. While effective, many methods require retraining or sequential fine-tuning for each time slice; these approaches incur significant computational cost and limiting interpretability. Furthermore, most focus on node-level drift, how individual term vectors change, rather than on edge-level drift, which captures the evolving co-occurrence structure among terms. Instead of retraining embeddings, DATGR rewires the word-co-occurrence graph using a drift-weighted logistic update rule, enabling continuous adaptation without full re-embedding. Evaluated on the生物医学研究中心BIOMRC语料库中，所提出的模型实现了约 0.066 的绝对 AUROC 改进（corpus, the proposed model achieved an absolute AUROC improvement of approximately 0.066 (0.6990.699与vs.0.6330.633）在静态基线上，同时保持可比的 AUPRC 性能（) over a static baseline, while maintaining comparable AUPRC performance (0.7380.738与vs.0.7440.744）。这些结果表明，轻量级、反馈驱动的边缘更新可以有效捕获语义演化，同时保持精度。). These results demonstrate that lightweight, feedback-driven edge updates can effectively capture semantic evolution while preserving precision.

三、III方法论METHODOLOGY

所提出的 DATGR 框架通过增量、边缘级图适应而不是完全重新嵌入来模拟生物医学语义的演变。它包含四个模块：（1）语料库的时间分割，（2）使用句子嵌入的漂移估计，（3）漂移加权图重新布线，以及（4）使用节点嵌入的链接预测。图。The proposed DATGR framework models the evolution of biomedical semantics through incremental, edge-level graph adaptation rather than full re-embedding. It comprises four modules: (1) temporal segmentation of the corpus, (2) drift estimation using sentence embeddings, (3) drift-weighted graph rewiring, and (4) link prediction with node embeddings. V 讨论和未来的工作Discussion and Future Work

V-A 新颖性和理论贡献Novelty and Theoretical Contribution

大多数现有的时态语言建模框架都集中在 (1) 对每个时间片重新训练嵌入Most existing frameworks for temporal language modeling focus on either (1) retraining embeddings for each time slice[4, 6], 或（2）采用循环或基于注意力的动态图神经网络来随时间传播节点状态, or (2) employing recurrent or attention-based dynamic graph neural networks to propagate node states through time[7, 8]。 虽然这些方法成功地捕获了时间变化，但它们通常会产生更高的计算成本、降低的可解释性以及隔离漂移对不断发展的网络结构的特定贡献的能力有限。. While these approaches successfully capture temporal variation, they typically incur higher computational cost, reduced interpretability, and limited ability to isolate the specific contribution of drift to the evolving network structure.

相比之下，DATGR 引入了一种以边缘为中心的时间适应范式，在概念上与先前的边缘重新布线方法（例如 DiffWire）相关In contrast, DATGR introduces an edge-centric paradigm for temporal adaptation, conceptually related to prior edge-rewiring approaches such as DiffWire[9]，但扩展为包含时间漂移信号。 DATGR 不是重新计算整个节点嵌入，而是将共现图中的边权重显式更新为估计漂移的封闭式函数。 这种从节点级到边缘级适应的转变形成了该框架的核心新颖性，使模型能够反映生物医学术语之间新兴或减少的关联，而无需重新学习整个语义空间。 因此，语义演化直接编码在图结构中，而不是分布在潜在嵌入维度上，从而使单个更改可追踪和可审计。, but extended to incorporate temporal drift signals. Rather than recalculating entire node embeddings, DATGR explicitly updates the edge weights in the co-occurrence graph as a closed-form function of estimated drift. This shift from node-level to edge-level adaptation forms the central novelty of the framework, enabling the model to reflect emerging or diminishing associations between biomedical terms without relearning the entire semantic space. As a result, semantic evolution is encoded directly in graph structure rather than distributed across latent embedding dimensions, making individual changes traceable and auditable.

另一个值得注意的方面在于逻辑更新规则，它平衡了历史持久性与漂移驱动的创新。 尽管大多数时间嵌入模型依赖于附加或线性更新，将所有变化视为同等重要，但 DATGR 中的逻辑函数引入了饱和非线性，可以抑制噪声，同时强调共现模式中强烈、一致的变化。 该公式提供了直观的正则化效果：大而稳定的漂移支配着小而瞬态的波动。Another notable aspect lies in the logistic update rule, which balances historical persistence with drift-driven innovation. Whereas most temporal embedding models rely on additive or linear updates that treat all changes as equally significant, the logistic function in DATGR introduces a saturating non-linearity that dampens noise while emphasizing strong, consistent shifts in co-occurrence patterns. This formulation provides an intuitive regularization effect: large, stable drifts dominate small, transient fluctuations.

V-B 经验和实际意义Empirical and Practical Significance

实验结果表明，DATGR 在保持精度的同时有效捕获语义漂移。 在所有时间窗口中，与静态基线相比，DATGR 将 AUROC 提高了约 0.066，表明在识别新兴关系方面具有更强的判别能力。 AUPRC 值仍然与基线相当，并在以后的窗口中稍微超过它，这表明召回率增加而不会降低精度。The experimental results indicate that DATGR effectively captures semantic drift while maintaining precision. Across all temporal windows, DATGR improves AUROC by approximately 0.066 compared with the static baseline, demonstrating stronger discriminative capability in identifying emerging relations. AUPRC values remain comparable to the baseline and slightly surpass it in later windows, indicating that recall increases without degrading precision.

这种平衡对于生物医学系统特别有价值，因为错误的关联可能会传播错误信息。 在保持 AUPRC 平价的同时实现 AUROC 收益的能力表明，DATGR 的重新布线引入了有意义的结构修正，而不是不加区别的连接扩展。 边缘编辑模式进一步揭示了稳定的、受控的适应，其中每个时间转换仅修改边缘的适度子集，足以捕获语义新颖性，同时保持网络一致性。This balance is particularly valuable for biomedical systems where false associations can propagate misinformation. The ability to achieve AUROC gains while preserving AUPRC parity suggests that DATGR’s rewiring introduces meaningful structural corrections rather than indiscriminate connectivity expansion. The edge-edit patterns further reveal steady, controlled adaptation, where each temporal transition modifies only a modest subset of edges, sufficient to capture semantic novelty while preserving network coherence.

维-DV-D 可解释性和理论视角Interpretability and Theoretical Perspective

除了定量指标之外，DATGR 的重新布线动态还为生物医学语言的演变提供了透明的视角。 每个边缘权重明确地反映了两个术语之间的共现强度，并根据可测量的语义漂移而演变。 可视化这些重新布线轨迹可以揭示出“mRNA疫苗”等关联性在2020年后如何加强，因为该术语取代了更早、更广泛的“RNA疫苗”用法，或者随着基于mRNA的递送方法的兴起，“病毒载体”的共现如何减弱。 这种显式的可追溯性将边缘变化直接与漂移幅度联系起来，提供了潜在时间嵌入或神经循环模型很难实现的可解释性。Beyond quantitative metrics, DATGR’s rewiring dynamics yield a transparent lens into biomedical language evolution. Each edge weight explicitly reflects the co-occurrence strength between two terms and evolves according to measurable semantic drift. Visualizing these rewiring trajectories reveals how associations such as “mRNA vaccine” intensified after 2020 as the term displaced the earlier, broader “RNA vaccine” usage, or how “viral vector” co-occurrences waned as mRNA-based delivery methods rose to prominence. This explicit traceability, linking edge changes directly to drift magnitudes, offers interpretability rarely achievable with latent temporal embeddings or neural recurrent models.

电压-频率V-F 更广泛的影响Broader Implications

DATGR 证明，无需深度再训练或不透明的神经循环即可实现有意义的时间适应。 通过将漂移编码为显式图结构而不是不透明的神经状态，它使得时间适应既可审计又可域转移。 在生物医学知识快速发展和大规模人工智能发现的时代，这种透明的机制提供了一条通往可持续、不断更新的科学知识图的道路，以平衡准确性、可解释性和效率。DATGR demonstrates that meaningful temporal adaptation can be achieved without deep retraining or opaque neural recurrence. By encoding drift as explicit graph structure rather than opaque neural state, it makes temporal adaptation both auditable and domain-transferable. In an era of rapidly evolving biomedical knowledge and large-scale AI discovery, such transparent mechanisms offer a path toward sustainable, continually updating scientific knowledge graphs that balance accuracy, explainability, and efficiency.

VI 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 DATGR，这是一种漂移感知时间图重新布线框架，它通过轻量级、可解释的边缘更新而不是完全嵌入重新训练来捕获生物医学语义演化。通过将基于嵌入的漂移估计与逻辑重新布线规则相结合，DATGR 在静态基线上实现了大约 0.066 的一致 AUROC 增益，同时保持了可比的精确召回性能，这表明边缘级自适应以比动态 GNN 或顺序微调方法低得多的计算成本有效地编码时间语义变化。This paper introduced DATGR, a Drift-Aware Temporal Graph Rewiring framework that captures biomedical semantic evolution through lightweight, interpretable edge updates rather than full embedding retraining. By integrating embedding-based drift estimation with a logistic rewiring rule, DATGR achieves consistent AUROC gains of approximately 0.066 over a static baseline while maintaining comparable precision-recall performance, demonstrating that edge-level adaptation effectively encodes temporal semantic change at substantially lower computational cost than dynamic GNNs or sequential fine-tuning approaches.

更广泛地说，DATGR 表明有意义的时间适应不需要依赖于不透明的神经循环或昂贵的再训练。其透明的、基于图的更新机制使其非常适合部署在知识图管理、生物医学 RAG 管道和语义检索系统中，在这些系统中，可解释性和及时性都至关重要。在此过程中，它为不断发展的科学文本提供了符号范式和神经范式之间的原则性桥梁。More broadly, DATGR illustrates that meaningful temporal adaptation need not rely on opaque neural recurrence or expensive retraining. Its transparent, graph-based update mechanism makes it well-suited for deployment in knowledge graph curation, biomedical RAG pipelines, and semantic retrieval systems where both interpretability and timeliness are critical. In doing so, it offers a principled bridge between symbolic and neural paradigms for evolving scientific text.
