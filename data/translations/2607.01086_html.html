LongVQUBench：对长期视频质量理解进行基准测试LongVQUBench: Benchmarking Long-Term Video Quality Understanding of
视觉语言模型Vision-Language Models

Arpita Nema    Hanwei Zhu    Xi Zhang    Weisi Lin
摘要Abstract

对于大型视觉语言模型（LVLM）来说，长期视频质量理解的评估仍然是一个开放的挑战。现有的视频质量基准主要关注短片和孤立的失真，忽视了长时间内容固有的时间连续性、累积退化和推理复杂性。为了解决这些限制，我们提出The evaluation of long-term video quality understanding remains an open challenge for large vision-language models (LVLMs). Existing video quality benchmarks predominantly focus on short clips and isolated distortions, overlooking the temporal continuity, cumulative degradation, and reasoning complexity inherent in long-duration content. To address these limitations, we present长VQU长凳LongVQUBench，长期视频质量理解的综合基准。 LongVQUBench 包含超过, a comprehensive benchmark for long-term video quality understanding. LongVQUBench contains over1,2001{,}200涵盖电影、纪录片、监控录像、自我中心录音和动画内容的各种视频，并附有diverse videos spanning movies, documentaries, surveillance footage, egocentric recordings, and animated content, accompanied by1,5001{,}500用于验证和测试的多项选择和开放式问题。为了评估不同时间范围内的感知推理，我们引入了三个逐渐复杂的评估级别：（i）用于分析局部扭曲的局部事件质量理解（LQU）； (ii) 用于整合多个降级事件的跨事件质量推理（CQR）； (iii) 用于长期整体感知评估的全局质量理解 (GQU)。此外，针形扭曲问答（NDQA）范式嵌入在所有三个级别中，其中稀疏地插入空间或时间伪影以探测细粒度的检测和推理能力。广泛的实验multiple-choice and open-ended questions for validation and testing. To assess perceptual reasoning across different temporal scopes, we introduce three progressively complex evaluation levels: (i) local event quality understanding (LQU) for analyzing localized distortions; (ii) cross-event quality reasoning (CQR) for integrating multiple degraded events; and (iii) global quality understanding (GQU) for holistic perceptual evaluation over extended durations. Furthermore, a needle distortion question-answering (NDQA) paradigm is embedded across all three levels, where spatial or temporal artifacts are sparsely inserted to probe fine-grained detection and reasoning capabilities. Extensive experiments on1414最先进的 LVLM 揭示了随着视频长度和推理深度的增加，性能显着下降，突显了它们的远程时间整合和感知归因能力有限。我们设想 LongVQUBench 是对 LVLM 的长期视频质量理解进行系统化、分层且可解释的评估的基础步骤。state-of-the-art LVLMs reveal significant performance degradation with increasing video length and reasoning depth, highlighting their limited capacity for long-range temporal integration and perceptual attribution. We envision LongVQUBench as a foundational step toward the systematic, hierarchical, and explainable evaluation of LVLMs’ long-term video quality understanding.

1 简介Introduction

大型视觉语言模型（LVLM）的快速进步通过将视觉感知与语言推理相结合，彻底改变了多模态理解。最新型号，例如 GPT-5The rapid progress of large vision-language models (LVLMs) has revolutionized multimodal understanding by integrating visual perception with linguistic reasoning. Recent models such as GPT-5[46], LLaVA-OneVision-1.5 [2]、Qwen2.5-VL, and Qwen2.5-VL[5]在细粒度识别、上下文感知图像描述方面表现出强大的能力exhibit strong capabilities in fine-grained recognition, context-aware image description[78]，以及短期和长期视频问答, and short- and long-term video question answering[75, 14, 57]，标志着从感知驱动分析到语义理解的转变。然而，长期视频质量理解（LVQU）仍未得到充分探索。与以事件和语义为中心的传统视频理解任务不同，LVQU 需要对感知保真度、时间一致性和长时间跨度的累积退化进行推理。这是评估现实世界长视频的稳定性和人类感知质量的重要能力，但仍然超出了当前 LVLM 的能力范围。, signaling a transition from perception-driven analysis to semantic comprehension. Nevertheless, long-term video quality understanding (LVQU) remains underexplored. Unlike conventional video understanding tasks centered on events and semantics, LVQU requires reasoning over perceptual fidelity, temporal coherence, and cumulative degradation across extended time spans. This is an essential ability for assessing the stability and human-perceived quality of real-world long-form videos, yet still beyond the reach of current LVLMs.

视频质量评估 (VQA) 通过量化人类对压缩伪影等失真的判断，为感知建模提供了补充基础Video quality assessment (VQA) provides a complementary foundation for perceptual modeling by quantifying human judgments of distortions such as compression artifacts[54, 44, 60, 34, 70, 68], 传输错误, transmission errors[42, 23, 45, 64]和瞬时闪烁, and temporal flicker[76, 9, 10]。经典数据集，包括 LIVE-VQA. Classical datasets, including LIVE-VQA[45], KoNViD-1k [20]、YouTube-UGC, and YouTube-UGC[52]，主要由具有受控失真的短片组成，这对于低级建模很有价值，但不足以捕获长视频的时间连续性和上下文动态。相比之下，面向 LVLM 的基准则强调语义视频理解，例如动作识别和多模态推理, consist mainly of short clips with controlled distortions, which are valuable for low-level modeling but insufficient for capturing the temporal continuity and contextual dynamics of long videos. In contrast, LVLM-oriented benchmarks have emphasized semantic video understanding, such as action recognition and multimodal reasoning[14, 16]。虽然LongVideoBench. Although LongVideoBench[57]将评估扩展到长形式语义，它仍然关注事件理解而不是感知质量，而 Q-Bench-Videoextends evaluation to long-form semantics, it remains focused on event comprehension rather than perceptual quality, while Q-Bench-Video[75]探索视频质量推理，但仅限于没有分层时间评估的短片。因此，仍然缺乏一个整合感知保真度、时间一致性和多模态推理的长期视频质量理解的统一基准。explores video-quality reasoning but is restricted to short clips without hierarchical temporal assessment. Therefore, a unified benchmark for long-term video quality understanding, integrating perceptual fidelity, temporal coherence, and multimodal reasoning, is still lacking.

为了弥补这些差距，我们引入To bridge these gaps, we introduce长VQU长凳LongVQUBench，用于评估 LVLM 的 LVQU 的综合基准。该基准包含超过, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating the LVQU of LVLMs. The benchmark contains over1,2001,200视频来源多样，包括电影、纪录片、监控录像、以自我为中心的录音和计算机生成的内容，持续时间从几分钟到近两个小时不等。这种多样性涵盖了广泛的感知失真，例如长时间观看时出现的灯光漂移、场景转换、编解码器伪影和生成失真。为了在不断增加的时间范围内系统地评估 LVQU，LongVQUBench 定义了三个层次级别：videos drawn from diverse sources, including films, documentaries, surveillance footage, egocentric recordings, and computer-generated content, spanning durations from a few minutes to nearly two hours. This diversity encompasses a wide range of perceptual distortions, such as lighting drift, scene transitions, codec artifacts, and generative distortions, that emerge over extended viewing periods. These levels progressively test a model’s capacity to identify localized distortions, integrate perceptual cues across events, and evaluate holistic perceptual integrity and temporal stability.

We evaluate1414零样本设置下最先进的 LVLM。结果表明，随着推理深度的增加，性能持续下降，揭示了时间定位、失真归因和全局质量推理的局限性。 LongVQUBench 为长期视频质量理解建立了第一个系统基准，提供了一个原则框架，以推进感知建模、时间推理和多模态集成，实现人类水平的长视频理解。state-of-the-art LVLMs under zero-shot settings. The results show a consistent decline in performance as reasoning depth increases, revealing limitations in temporal localization, distortion attribution, and global quality reasoning. LongVQUBench establishes the first systematic benchmark for long-term video quality understanding, providing a principled framework to advance perceptual modeling, temporal reasoning, and multimodal integration toward human-level long-form video comprehension.

2.3 质量保证方法VQA Methods

视频质量评估 (VQA) 旨在评估人类对视觉保真度和时间稳定性的感知判断。经典的全参考 (FR) 方法，例如 PSNR 和 SSIMVideo quality assessment (VQA) aims to estimate human perceptual judgment of visual fidelity and temporal stability. 3 长VQU长凳LongVQUBench

本节详细介绍了设计理念、数据构成和评估结构This section details the design philosophy, data composition, and evaluation structure of长VQU长凳LongVQUBench，用于评估 LVLM 长期视频质量能力的大型基准。该基准的开发是为了联合评估长时间内的感知保真度和时间推理，建立一个用于综合分析的统一测试平台。, a large-scale benchmark for assessing the long-term video quality ability of LVLMs. The benchmark is developed to jointly evaluate perceptual fidelity and temporal reasoning across extended durations, establishing a unified testbed for comprehensive analysis.

3.1 概述Overview

长VQU长凳LongVQUBench旨在检查 LVLM 如何在长时间范围内感知、推理和解释视频质量。与现有的短剪辑数据集不同is designed to examine how LVLMs perceive, reason, and explain video quality over long temporal horizons. Unlike existing short-clip datasets[75]，它关注质量的逐渐演变、感知退化的积累以及时间遥远的事件之间的推理依赖性。 该基准的构建具有三个设计目标：1）涵盖反映不同现实世界和合成场景的广泛长视频； 2) 确保视频时长和感知质量的代表性覆盖；和 3) 实现对时间推理和细粒度感知敏感性的结构化、分层评估。 数据集组成的概述如图所示, it focuses on the gradual evolution of quality, the accumulation of perceptual degradation, and the reasoning dependencies among temporally distant events. The benchmark is constructed with three design goals: 1) To cover a broad spectrum of long-form videos that reflect diverse real-world and synthetic scenarios; 2) To ensure representative coverage across both video duration and perceptual quality; and 3) To enable structured, hierarchical evaluation of temporal reasoning and fine-grained perceptual sensitivity. Each video spans a duration between several minutes and two hours, enabling analysis of temporal consistency and cross-event degradation in realistic viewing conditions.

多样化的视频源。Diverse Video Sources.这些视频涵盖多个领域，包括故事片、纪录片、监控录像、以自我为中心的录音、教学视频和计算机生成的场景。这种多样性捕获了广泛的运动模式、编辑风格和语义结构，确保了基准测试的通用性。这种多样性也反映了 LVLM 在分析长篇内容时面临的异构感知挑战。The videos cover multiple domains, including feature films, documentaries, surveillance footage, egocentric recordings, instructional videos, and computer-generated scenes. This diversity captures a wide range of motion patterns, editing styles, and semantic structures, ensuring the benchmark’s generality. Such variety also reflects the heterogeneous perceptual challenges faced by LVLMs when analyzing long-form content.

3.3.2 2）跨事件质量推理（CQR）：2) Cross-Event Quality Reasoning (CQR):

CQR 级别检查模型比较、关联和集成分布在长视频中的多个失真事件的能力。与专注于短期感知敏感性的 LQU 不同，CQR 的目标是跨扩展时间跨度的推理，其中多个降级可能连续或间歇地发生。此级别评估模型是否可以评估扭曲的相对严重性，捕获它们的时间关系，并推断它们的相互作用如何影响整体感知舒适度。The CQR level examines a model’s ability to compare, associate, and integrate multiple distortion events distributed across a long video. Unlike LQU, which focuses on short-term perceptual sensitivity, CQR targets reasoning across extended temporal spans where multiple degradations may occur sequentially or intermittently. This level evaluates whether a model can assess the relative severity of distortions, capture their temporal relationships, and infer how their interactions influence overall perceptual comfort.

3.3.3 3) 全球质量理解（GQU）：3) Global Quality Understanding (GQU):

GQU 级别评估模型在整个长视频（通常从一分钟到两个小时）内综合整体感知判断的能力。它需要对时间趋势、累积退化和长时间观看的感知稳定性进行推理。与关注局部扭曲或多事件关系的 LQU 和 CQR 不同，GQU 强调整体时间整合，跟踪感知质量如何随时间演变、稳定或恶化，以及这种演变如何影响最终的感知判断。The GQU level evaluates a model’s ability to synthesize an overall perceptual judgment across an entire long video, typically spanning from one minute to two hours. It requires reasoning about temporal trends, cumulative degradations, and perceptual stability over prolonged viewing. In contrast to LQU and CQR, which focus on local distortions or multi-event relations, GQU emphasizes holistic temporal integration, tracking how perceptual quality evolves, stabilizes, or deteriorates over time and how this evolution affects the final perceptual judgment.

所有 QA 对All QA pairs in长VQU长凳LongVQUBench通过受控的两阶段过程进行注释，以确保时间准确性、语义清晰度和感知一致性。在第一阶段，根据每个级别的推理维度（LQU、CQR 和 GQU）构建 QA 项目，通过识别失真事件、标记其时间边界并制定探索检测、比较和推理的问题。第二阶段，每个QA项目均由多位专家独立评审，分歧通过协商一致解决，以确保注释的可靠性。采用多项选择和开放式两种形式：前者以客观识别和定位为目标，后者评估解释性推理，并辅以基于 GPT 的相关性和完整性评分are annotated through a controlled two-stage process to ensure temporal accuracy, semantic clarity, and perceptual consistency.
