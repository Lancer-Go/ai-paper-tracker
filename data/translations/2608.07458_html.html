摘要Abstract

最近关于检索增强生成（RAG）的优化研究利用了块级 KV 缓存重用，以避免处理长检索上下文以提高效率，同时粗粒度块中仍然存在大量信息冗余和噪声。 本文通过提出 CoinRAG 在低预填充延迟约束下优化 Pareto 前沿，同时最大化准确性（Recent optimization studies on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) have exploited chunk-level KV cache reuse to avoid processing long retrieved contexts for higher efficiency, while significant information redundancy and noise still remain in the coarse-grained chunks. This paper optimizes the Pareto frontier under low prefill latency constraints while maximizing accuracy by proposing CoinRAG (Co语境化ntextualizedI信息nformationNugget 长上下文的 KV 缓存重用ugget KV Cache Reuse for Long-ContextRAG）。 这个名字隐喻地反映了我们的核心机制：就像组装小代币（或“硬币”）来积累更大的价值一样，CoinRAG 组合地重用离线计算的、细粒度的金块缓存，以语义更相关但更紧凑的方式有效地形成学习的上下文表示。 具体来说，CoinRAG 不是全块编码，而是通过两阶段检索来识别检索到的块中与查询相关的语义单元，并无缝地组装它们 具有块级上下文的切片 KV 表示。 对 LongBench 多跳问答任务的广泛评估表明，CoinRAG 显着降低了运营成本，并且在标准快速预填充延迟预算下，凭借新的 Pareto 前沿以及答案质量 (F1) 平均相对提高 5.3%，性能优于其他基线。). The name metaphorically reflects our core mechanism: much like assembling small tokens (or “coins”) to accumulate a larger value, CoinRAG compositionally reuses offline-computed, fine-grained nugget caches to form a learned contextual representation efficiently in a more semantically relevant but compact manner. Specifically, instead of full-chunk encoding, CoinRAG identifies query-relevant semantic units within retrieved chunks through two-stage retrieval and seamlessly assembles their sliced KV representations with a chunk-level context. Extensive evaluations on LongBench multi-hop question answering tasks demonstrate that CoinRAG significantly reduces operational costs and outperforms the other baselines with a new Pareto frontier and an average 5.3% relative improvement in answer quality (F1) under a standard fast prefill latency budget.

1 简介Introduction

检索增强生成 (RAG)Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)(Lewis et al., 2020; Gao et al., 2023b)是一种广泛采用的范例，用于利用外部知识增强大型语言模型 (LLM)is a widely adopted paradigm for enhancing large language models (LLMs) with external knowledge(Chen et al., 2024b)。 通过将检索到的文档与查询一起提供给 LLM，RAG 提高了事实一致性. By feeding retrieved documents to the LLM with the query, RAG improves factual consistency(Gao et al., 2023a)并减少幻觉and reduces hallucination(Shuster et al., 2021)无需模型重新训练。 然而，当 RAG 系统以完整文档或大文本块的粗粒度运行时，推理成本很高，导致较高的预填充延迟和计算冗余。 为了提高推理效率，一种有希望的补救措施是预先计算并重用键值 (KV) 缓存without model retraining. However, when RAG systems operate at the coarse granularity of full documents or large text chunks, inference is expensive, leading to high prefill latency and computational redundancy. To improve inference efficiency, a promising remedy is to precompute and reuse key-value (KV) caches(Pope et al., 2023)，一种已被广泛设计用于提示管理和多查询前缀共享的技术, a technique that has been extensively engineered for prompt management and multi-query prefix sharing(Kwon et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2024; Gim et al., 2024; Haoyang et al., 2025)。 至关重要的是，这种核心机械能力支撑着新兴的技术. Crucially, this core mechanical capability underpins the emerging缓存增强生成Cache-augmented Generation（CAG）范式(CAG) paradigm(Chan et al., 2025)，它建议将整个外部知识库预先加载到模型的上下文中，以消除实时检索。 基于RoPE的位置轮换, which proposes pre-loading entire external knowledge bases into the model’s context to eliminate real-time retrieval. RoPE-based position rotation(Su et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025)已进一步开发以捕获动态检索块的相对位置上下文，从而在推理过程中实现块级 KV 缓存重用。has been developed further to capture relative positional contexts of dynamically retrieved chunks, enabling chunk-level KV cache reuse during inference.

随着 RAG 在面向用户的 Web 应用程序中变得流行，推理必须以交互延迟回答问题，并维持大量实时查询流量的高吞吐量需求。 商业 Web 服务根据服务级别协议 (SLA) 运行，通常使用尾部延迟（百分位数）而不是所需响应时间的平均延迟。 现代 SLA 指定了诸如 99% 的请求在几毫秒内的目标。 在文献中，交互式 Web 服务的响应时间定义为从服务器传输到客户端的响应的第一个字节的时间，100 毫秒或更短的响应时间被认为是瞬时的As RAG has become popular for user-facing web applications, inference must answer questions at interactive latencies and sustain the high-throughput demands of large live query traffic. A commercial web service operates under a Service Level Agreement (SLA), which typically uses tail latency (percentiles) rather than average latency for the required response time. Modern SLAs specify targets like 99% of requests under some milliseconds. In the literature, response time for interactive web services is defined as the time to deliver the first byte of a response transferred from a server to a client, and a response time of 100 ms or less is perceived as instantaneous(PubNub, 2023; Miller, 1968)是理想的交互式响应时间预算。 先前关于低延迟机器学习推理的工作追求 100 毫秒内的 P99 尾部延迟预算and is a desirable interactive response time budget. Previous work on low-latency machine learning inference has pursued a P99 tail latency budget within 100 ms(Yun et al., 2015; Crankshaw et al., 2017; Gujarati et al., 2020)。 考虑到这一点，我们的研究采用首次令牌时间 (TTFT) 预算限制，即 P99 延迟低于 100 毫秒。 请注意，降低延迟也会带来更高的吞吐量。. With this in mind, our study adopts a Time-to-First-Token (TTFT) budget limit as the P99 latency under 100 ms. Note that lowering latency also leads to a higher throughput.

标准 RAG，即使具有 KV 缓存重用，在 TTFT 方面也是昂贵的。 为了在低延迟预算下满足 SLA，需要限制检索范围，从而降低答案准确性。 本文重点关注在低延迟预算下最大化 RAG 准确性，并采用一种新方法，通过在从具有精简表示的文本块中提前提取的更细粒度的信息上操作 RAG，这也降低了相关证据在长而嘈杂的上下文中丢失的风险Standard RAG, even with KV cache reuse, is expensive in terms of TTFT. To meet SLA under a low-latency budget, it needs to limit the retrieval scope, degrading answer accuracy. This paper focuses on maximizing RAG accuracy under a low-latency budget and takes a new approach by operating RAG at a finer-grained level of information extracted in advance from text chunks with a slim representation, which also reduces the risk that relevant evidence gets lost amid long, noisy contexts(Liu et al., 2024)。 具体来说，我们离线从文本块中提取信息块，而不是完全在推理时检索和编码它们。 我们的方法受到先前信息检索研究的启发，该研究使用人类识别的. Specifically, we extract information nuggets from text chunks offline, instead of retrieving and encoding them entirely at inference time. 本文做出以下贡献。 1) 它提出了一个 RAG 框架，用于高效推理，具有用于上下文 KV 缓存重用的基于细长块的表示，旨在在标准快速响应时间预算下实现更高的准确性。 2) 该方案采用以下技术： (a) 基于文本跨度的块的离线提取、块感知微调，以及 两阶段在线检索，从许多候选者中选择相关的信息； (b) 上下文化的KV缓存组成 用于来自相同块的与查询相关的块。This paper makes the following contributions. 1) It proposes a RAG framework for efficient inference with a slim nugget-based representation for contextual KV-cache reuse, targeted for higher accuracy under a standard fast response-time budget. 2) This scheme comes with the following techniques: (a) Offline extraction of text-span-based nuggets, nugget-aware fine-tuning, and two-stage online retrieval that selects relevant nuggets from many candidates; (b) Contextualized KV cache composition for query-relevant nuggets from the same chunks.

5 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 CoinRAG，这是一个轻量级的长上下文 RAG 框架，具有精简的表示形式，可通过上下文 KV 块缓存重用来提供有效的交互式响应。 关键的基础技术贡献包括：基于文本范围的基本信息块的离线提取， 块感知微调，一个两阶段在线检索管道，用于选择特定于查询的块，以及 上下文化的 KV 缓存组合。We present CoinRAG, a lightweight long-context RAG framework with a slim representation to deliver effective and interactive responses via contextual KV nugget cache reuse. Key underlying technical contributions include: an offline extraction of essential text-span-based information nuggets, nugget-aware finetuning, a two-stage online retrieval pipeline that selects query-specific nuggets, and contextualized KV cache composition.

根据经验，在标准 100 毫秒 P99 延迟预算下，CoinRAG 在 3 个数据集上的 F1 分数比最佳竞争对手 TurboRAG 的 F1 分数高出 4.7%、0.5% 和 14.6%，平均增益为 5.3%。 在没有延迟限制的情况下，CoinRAG 的 3 个数据集平均仍优于基线 5.2% F-1 改进或更多，这表明 CoinRAG 消除噪声和不必要的上下文所带来的收益超过了由于缺少某些交互而造成的损失。 当 P99 延迟预算紧张（例如，低于 75 毫秒）时，在线缓存组合期间的位置对齐最为有益，并且与超出该预算的无位置对齐保持相当。 总的来说，CoinRAG 通过新的 Pareto 前沿添加了重要的新颖贡献，可在交互式 TTFT SLA 预算下实现有效的长上下文 RAG 推理。Empirically, under a standard 100 ms P99 latency budget, CoinRAG outperforms the F1 score of the best competitor, TurboRAG, by 4.7%, 0.5%, and 14.6% for 3 datasets, with an average gain of 5.3%. Without a latency limit, CoinRAG still outperforms the baselines on average for 3 datasets by 5.2% F-1 improvement or more, indicating that the gain by removing noise and unnecessary context by CoinRAG outweighs the loss due to missing some interaction. Position alignment during online cache composition is most beneficial when the P99 latency budget is tight (e.g., under 75 ms), and remains comparable to no position alignment beyond that budget. In general, CoinRAG adds a significant novel contribution with a new Pareto frontier for effective long-context RAG inference under an interactive TTFT SLA budget.

局限性Limitations

虽然 CoinRAG 已经在标准低延迟 SLA 预算下展示了有效长上下文 RAG 的新帕累托前沿，但我们明确承认存在一些系统性限制。While CoinRAG has demonstrated a new Pareto frontier for effective long-context RAG under a standard low-latency SLA budget, we explicitly acknowledge several systemic constraints.
