摘要Abstract

最近提出了开环端到端神经运动规划器来改进机器人操纵器的运动规划。这些方法可以直接根据传感器观测进行规划，而无需在规划期间依赖特权碰撞检查器。然而，许多现有方法仅在不同的运行中为给定工作空间生成单个路径，并且不利用其开环结构进行推理时间优化。为了解决这个限制，我们引入了 Flow Motion Policy，这是一种用于机器人操纵器的开环、端到端神经运动规划器，它利用流匹配方法的随机生成公式来捕获规划数据集固有的多模态。通过对可行路径上的分布进行建模，流运动策略可以实现高效的推理时间最佳效果Open-loop end-to-end neural motion planners have recently been proposed to improve motion planning for robotic manipulators. These methods enable planning directly from sensor observations without relying on a privileged collision checker during planning. However, many existing methods generate only a single path for a given workspace across different runs, and do not leverage their open-loop structure for inference-time optimization. To address this limitation, we introduce Flow Motion Policy, an open-loop, end-to-end neural motion planner for robotic manipulators that leverages the stochastic generative formulation of flow matching methods to capture the inherent multi-modality of planning datasets. By modeling a distribution over feasible paths, Flow Motion Policy enables efficient inference-time best-of-NN抽样。该方法生成多个端到端候选路径，在规划后评估它们的碰撞状态，并执行第一个无碰撞解决方案。我们将流运动策略与基于采样的代表性方法和神经运动规划方法进行基准测试。评估结果表明，Flow Motion Policy 提高了规划的成功率和效率，突出了随机生成策略对于端到端运动规划和推理时间优化的有效性。实验评估视频可通过此获得sampling. The method generates multiple end-to-end candidate paths, evaluates their collision status after planning, and executes the first collision-free solution. We benchmark the Flow Motion Policy against representative sampling-based and neural motion planning methods. Evaluation results demonstrate that Flow Motion Policy improves planning success and efficiency, highlighting the effectiveness of stochastic generative policies for end-to-end motion planning and inference-time optimization. I 简介Introduction

运动规划在机器人操纵系统中发挥着重要作用，与高级控制策略一起运行以确保安全可行的操作。目标是计算一条有效的、无碰撞的路径，将初始配置连接到所需的目标配置Motion planning plays an important role in robotic manipulation systems, operating alongside high-level control policies to ensure safe and feasible actions. The objective is to compute a valid, collision-free path that connects an initial configuration to a desired goal configuration[25, 34]。在非结构化环境中实现机械手快速可靠的运动规划仍然是一个具有挑战性的问题。数十年的研究已经建立了许多算法来实现这一目标，其中概率完全基于采样. Achieving fast and reliable motion planning for manipulators in unstructured environments remains a challenging problem. Decades of research have established many algorithms to achieve this, among which probabilistically complete sampling-based[21]和轨迹优化and trajectory optimization[45]算法已广泛应用于机器人操纵器。algorithms have been widely used for robotic manipulators.

基于采样的规划器 (SBP) 在配置空间中构建一棵树，通过在配置空间内迭代生成随机样本、转向新采样的配置以及对生成的连接进行碰撞检查来连接起始配置和目标配置Sampling-based planners (SBPs) construct a tree in the configuration space to connect the start and goal configurations by iteratively generating random samples within the configuration space, steering towards the newly sampled configurations, and collision checking the resulting connections[20, 17]。由于其随机抽样过程，SBP 可能会在多次运行中针对同一规划问题生成不同的计划。然而，这些规划器在规划过程中依赖于特权碰撞检查器，而碰撞检查是运动规划的主要瓶颈，占计算时间的高达 90%. Due to their stochastic sampling process, SBPs may generate different plans for the same planning problem across multiple runs. However, these planners rely on a privileged collision checker during planning, and collision checking is the main bottleneck in motion planning, accounting for up to 90% of the computation time[8]，这使得并行性和推理时间优化具有挑战性, which makes parallelism and inference-time optimization challenging[14, 39]。尽管最近的高级变体. Although recent advanced variants[43, 12]引入基于启发式的知情采样器，使采样过程偏向于减少冲突查询数量的目标，这种启发式方法对于高维配置空间的设计具有挑战性。introduce heuristic-based informed samplers to bias the sampling process towards the goal to reduce the number of collision queries, such heuristics are challenging to design for high-dimensional configuration spaces.

轨迹优化方法Trajectory optimization methods[45, 16]使用数值优化技术迭代地细化初始轨迹，通常是起始配置和目标配置之间的直线插值。这些方法在优化过程中强制实施约束，包括避障和轨迹平滑度。尽管轨迹优化方法适合并行化并且可以从最佳方法中受益iteratively refine an initial trajectory, often a straight-line interpolation between the start and goal configurations, using numerical optimization techniques. These methods enforce constraints, including obstacle avoidance and trajectory smoothness, during optimization. Although trajectory optimization approaches are amenable to parallelization and can benefit from best-of-NN初始化策略initialization strategies[38]，他们仍然需要一个特权碰撞检查器来在整个优化过程中提供碰撞状态和梯度信息。, they still require a privileged collision checker to provide both collision status and gradient information throughout the optimization process.

最近，深度学习方法已被用来解决基于采样和轨迹优化规划器的局限性。在基于采样的规划中，神经网络被提议作为神经通知采样器Recently, deep learning methods have been leveraged to address the limitations of sampling-based and trajectory optimization planners. In sampling-based planning, neural networks have been proposed as neural informed samplers[29, 32, 15, 33]使树扩展朝着目标倾斜，减少碰撞检查的数量。神经网络也被用作隐式碰撞检查器to bias tree expansion towards the goal, reducing the number of collision checks. Neural networks have also been used as implicit collision checkers[18, 35]，实现更快的碰撞查询。然而，这些方法在规划过程中仍然需要碰撞查询，这限制了它们在最佳方法中的效率。, enabling faster collision queries. However, these approaches still require collision querying during planning, which limits their efficiency in best-of-NN抽样。对于轨迹优化，利用基于学习的方法生成高质量的初始轨迹sampling. For trajectory optimization, learning-based methods have been leveraged to generate high-quality initial trajectories[3, 41]，这有助于避免局部最小值并加速收敛。然而，这些方法还依赖于碰撞检查器在优化期间提供碰撞状态和梯度信息。, which can help to avoid local minima and accelerate convergence. Nevertheless, these methods also depend on collision checkers to provide collision status and gradient information during optimization.

此外，另一项工作利用深度学习进行开环、端到端运动规划Moreover, another line of work leverages deep learning for open-loop, end-to-end motion planning[31, 7, 42]，在规划过程中无需使用特权碰撞检查器。这些方法学习从规划问题信息（例如，起始配置、目标配置和环境观察）到可行路径的直接映射。然而，大多数现有方法都是确定性策略，为给定的规划问题生成单一路径。结果，他们无法利用最好的, eliminating the need for a privileged collision checker during planning. These methods learn a direct mapping from the planning problem information (e.g., start configuration, goal configuration, and environment observations) to a feasible path. This property can, in principle, be exploited for parallel planning and best-of-NN抽样。然而，规划者在规划过程中需要碰撞检查器，这是规划中的主要计算瓶颈sampling. 轨迹优化规划器从可能发生碰撞的初始轨迹开始，强制规划约束并利用数值优化将初始路径推向无碰撞空间，同时满足平滑度约束Trajectory optimization planners start from an initial trajectory, which may be in collision, and enforce planning constraints and utilize numerical optimization to push the initial path to collision-free spaces while satisfying smoothness constraints[45, 16, 23, 6]。然而，这些规划器强烈依赖于初始轨迹的质量，并且通常可以收敛到局部最小值。为了缓解这个问题，最近利用深度学习方法来学习规划数据集的底层分布，以热启动轨迹优化. However, these planners depend strongly on the quality of the initial trajectory and can often converge to local minima. To mitigate this issue, deep learning methods have recently been leveraged to learn the underlying distribution of planning datasets to warm-start trajectory optimization[3, 41, 24]。尽管轨迹优化规划器可以对一批初始解决方案进行操作并选择一个满足规划约束的解决方案，但它们仍然需要在规划过程中进行碰撞检查，这会增加计算时间。相比之下，Flow Motion Policy 在推理过程中并行执行碰撞检查，以实现高效的最佳结果.
