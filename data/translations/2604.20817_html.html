2 相关工作Related Work

傅里叶特征。Fourier Features.

傅里叶特征在计算机视觉中作为边缘和方向检测器已使用多年Fourier features have been used for many years in computer vision as edge and orientation detectors(Olshausen and Field, 1997; Olah et al., 2020; Fiquet and Simoncelli, 2023)。原始 Transformer 应用正弦位置编码. The original Transformer applies sinusoidal position encodings(Vaswani et al., 2017)，并且一些工作发现，明确地将高频分量注入输入有助于完成空间和数值任务, and several works have found that explicitly injecting high-frequency components into inputs helps with spatial and numerical tasks(Tancik et al., 2020; He et al., 2023; Hua et al., 2024)。 最近，也发现这种结构在未经设计的情况下出现。接受模加法训练的变形金刚将数字嵌入到圆上并旋转以计算答案. Recently, this structure is also found to emerge without being designed in. Transformers trained on modular addition embed numbers on a circle and rotating to compute the answer(Nanda et al., 2023; Zhong et al., 2023; Gromov, 2023)。在预训练的 LLM 中也是如此，数字标记嵌入分解为傅立叶分量，并且注意力层和 MLP 层中有可识别的加法电路. The same holds in pretrained LLMs that number token embeddings break into Fourier components, and there are recognizable addition circuits in the attention and MLP layers(Zhou et al., 2024; Kantamneni and Tegmark, 2025; Levy and Geva, 2025). Zhou et al. (2025)表明对这些傅里叶特征进行硬编码可以改善算术学习。 这些研究记录了光谱结构，但没有测试它是否意味着几何可分离性——我们的工作表明，这一区别至关重要。 在本文中，我们进一步研究了这些论文未解决的问题，即结构最初来自哪里。show that hard-coding these Fourier features improves arithmetic learning. In this paper, we further study the question these papers leave open, where the structure comes from in the first place.

我们已经证明，语言模型中的周期数表示表现出两层收敛：傅里叶尖峰是通用的，但线性可分模We have shown that periodic number representations in language models exhibit a two-tiered convergence: Fourier spikes are universal, but linearly separable mod-TT仅当数据、架构和优化器对齐时才会出现类。 核心教训是，表示中的可见结构并不能保证功能组织：LSTM 甚至原始令牌分布都会比 Transformer 产生更显着的傅里叶尖峰，但仍能实现机会级探测。classes emerge only when data, architecture, and optimizer align. The central lesson is that visible structure in representations does not guarantee functional organization: the LSTM and even the raw token distribution develop more prominent Fourier spikes than the Transformer yet achieve chance-level probing.

更一般地说，任何表示级诊断都可能将训练分布的统计工件误认为是学习结构。 我们的受控扰动方法为实例级归因方法（例如影响函数）提供了补充镜头More generally, any representation-level diagnostic could mistake statistical artifacts of the training distribution for learned structure. Our controlled perturbation approach offers a complementary lens to instance-level attribution methods such as influence functions(Koh and Liang, 2017)：我们不是将预测归因于单个训练示例，而是将学习的表示归因于数据分布的结构属性。: rather than attributing predictions to individual training examples, we attribute learned representations to structural properties of the data distribution.
