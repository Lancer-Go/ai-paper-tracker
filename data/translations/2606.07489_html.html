摘要Abstract

前沿人工智能系统通过从对话助理转变为端到端执行任务的自主代理，正在缩小智能和实用之间的差距。使用 Perplexity 搜索和计算机产品的生产数据，我们通过研究人工智能代理如何加速和重塑知识工作来研究这一转变。我们采用个人级别的基于任务的框架，其中代理具有较高的固定委托成本，但每步的边际执行成本较低。该框架预测，代理访问将可承受的任务边界扩展到微弱的高价值任务，并微弱地增加了实现的价值；当代理前预算受到约束时，盈余和价值成本比也会微弱增加。转向数据，出现了三个关键的实证结果。首先，使用具有几乎相同的初始查询对的会话作为两种产品尝试的相同基础任务的自然实验，计算机在每个用户会话中执行 26 分钟的自主工作，而搜索则为 33 秒。计算机自动执行任务分解和执行，否则搜索用户可能手动编排和实施。因此，计算机将后续查询分配转向更高阶的工作，例如验证和扩展。自主性还提高了执行质量，计算机上的每个查询不满意率比搜索上低 55%。其次，由于其自主优势，计算机将匹配任务的完成时间从 269 分钟减少到 36 分钟，与仅配备搜索的人类相比，预计时间和成本分别降低了 87% 和 94%。第三，计算机改变了用户尝试的工作范围：计算机查询更频繁地跨越职业界限，需要高阶认知，利用更广泛的专业知识，采用复合任务的形式，将相互依赖的子任务捆绑到单个查询中，并解锁相同用户之间的搜索使用中本质上不存在的工作活动。总之，证据表明人工智能代理可以加速工作流程、提高输出质量、降低成本并扩大自动化工作的广度和深度。Frontier AI systems are bridging the gap between intelligence and utility by shifting from conversational assistants to autonomous agents that execute tasks end to end. Using production data from Perplexity’s Search and Computer products, we study this transition by examining how AI agents accelerate and reshape knowledge work. We adopt an individual-level task-based framework where agents have a higher fixed delegation cost but a lower marginal execution cost per step. This framework predicts that agent access expands the affordable task frontier toward weakly higher-value tasks and weakly increases realized value; when the pre-agent budget binds, surplus and the value-to-cost ratio also weakly increase. Turning to the data, three key empirical findings emerge. First, using sessions with near-identical initial query pairs as natural experiments for the same underlying task attempted with both products, Computer performs 26 minutes of autonomous work per user session, versus 33 seconds for Search. Computer automates task decomposition and execution that Search users might otherwise manually orchestrate and implement. As a result, Computer shifts follow-up query distribution toward higher-order work such as verification and extension. Autonomy also increases execution quality, with per-query dissatisfaction rates 55% lower on Computer than on Search. Second, due to its autonomy advantage, Computer reduces completion time from 269 to 36 minutes on matched tasks, lowering estimated time and cost by 87% and 94%, respectively, compared to humans equipped with Search alone. Third, Computer changes the scope of work that users attempt: Computer queries more often cross occupational boundaries, require higher-order cognition, draw on broader expertise, take the form of composite tasks that bundle interdependent subtasks into a single query, and unlock work activities that are essentially absent from Search usage among the same users. Together, the evidence indicates that AI agents accelerate workflows, enhance output quality, reduce costs, and expand the breadth and depth of automated work.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能经济学的一个核心问题是它如何重塑知识工作A central question in the economics of AI is how it reshapes knowledge work(agrawal2026economics)。随着模型功能的进步，人工智能产品正在缩小智能和实用之间的差距，改变它们集成到现实世界工作流程的方式，并创造新的价值来源和新的工作结构。人工智能和用户行为也在共同进化，导致人工智能的功能、人工智能的使用方式及其下游经济影响发生变化。. As model capabilities advance, AI products are closing the gap between intelligence and utility, changing how they are integrated into real-world workflows and creating new sources of value and new structures of work. AI and user behavior are also co-evolving, producing a shifting landscape of what AI can do, how AI is used, and what its downstream economic impact is.

在过去的几年里，前沿产品已经从对话助理发展到副驾驶，再到座席。会话助理（例如聊天机器人）主要支持上下文或行动能力有限的孤立信息交换。副驾驶将这些功能嵌入到现有的工具和工作流程中，与用户协作在这些工具的界面中完成任务。代理更进一步：他们在后端连接更广泛的工具，并在几乎不需要人工参与的情况下返回完成的工件。这种转变是从人工智能作为对话助手到人工智能作为端到端工作执行引擎，其特点是更大的自主权和更深入地融入用户的整个数字环境。Over the last few years, frontier products have progressed from conversational assistants to copilots to agents. Conversational assistants (e.g., chatbots) primarily support isolated information exchange with limited context or ability to act. Copilots embed these capabilities into existing tools and workflows, co-working with users to complete tasks within those tools’ interfaces. Agents go further: they connect across a wider range of tools in the backend and return completed artifacts with little human involvement. The shift is from AI as a conversational assistant to AI as an end-to-end work execution engine, characterized by greater autonomy and deeper integration into the user’s entire digital environment.

7 效率Efficiency

计算机的自主执行通过机器计算增强了人类劳动，将执行工作从手动工作转移到监督。为了量化这种转变，我们估计了在两种情况下完成每个匹配任务所需的人力和计算时间和成本：(1) 搜索 + 人类，其中搜索处理信息检索和处理，但人类必须手动执行所有非研究操作；(2) 计算机 + 人类，其中模型执行完整的工作流程，人类仅提供监督。我们还测试了部分中的假设和引理Computer’s autonomous execution augments human labor with machine computation, shifting execution effort from manual work to oversight. To quantify this shift, we estimate the human and compute time and cost required to complete each matched task under two regimes: (1) Search + Human, where Search handles information retrieval and processing but the human must manually perform all non-research actions, and (2) Computer + Human, where the model executes the full workflow and the human provides only oversight. We also test the assumptions and lemmas in Section3.

9 讨论Discussion

本文记录了让用户访问人工智能代理支持的自主任务执行的下游任务级经济影响：计算机以更高的质量自主完成任务（第This paper documents the downstream task-level economic implications of giving users access to autonomous task execution enabled by AI agents: Computer completes tasks autonomously and with higher quality (Section6），这大大减少了所需的人力时间和成本（第), which dramatically reduces the human time and cost required (Section7）并将活动转向更广泛、认知要求更高的工作（第) and shifts activity toward broader and more cognitively demanding work (Section8）。我们提出一些结论性意见，并讨论未来研究的局限性和方向。). We make a few concluding comments and discuss the limitations and directions for future research.

自主权的作用。The role of autonomy.

自治结果阐明了下游效应发生的原因。代理消除了对话会话中的手动任务分解和执行循环，并产生更高质量的输出。因此，对用户尝试的预代理绑定约束不是信息访问而是执行能力。当执行任务委托给座席时，用户的角色从操作员转变为主管，从而将时间重新分配给更高阶的工作，例如指导、验证和任务扩展。The autonomy results clarify why the downstream effects occur. Agents eliminate the manual task-decomposition and execution loop in conversational sessions and produce higher quality outputs. The pre-agent binding constraint on what users attempt is therefore not information access but execution capacity. When execution is delegated to the agent, the user’s role shifts from operator to supervisor, reallocating time toward higher-order work such as direction, verification, and task extension.

从速度到范围。From speed to scope.

现有证据主要集中在生产力；我们的结果表明，生产力框架可能低估了自主代理的影响。尽管预计节省的时间和成本很大，但更重要的发现可能是范围扩展：用户在其主要领域之外承担更高复杂性的任务。The existing evidence has largely focused on productivity; our results suggest that a productivity framing might understate the impact of autonomous agents. Although the estimated time and cost savings are large, the more consequential finding may be scope expansion: users undertake tasks outside their primary domains and at higher levels of complexity.

局限性。Limitations.

我们注意到一些重要的注意事项。首先，90 天的观察窗口（2026 年 2 月 27 日至 5 月 27 日）捕捉到了早期采用期，其中用户不成比例地是高级用户和付费订阅者。随着产品的成熟，这些模式是否会推广到更广泛的人群仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。其次，匹配查询方法在控制任务内容的同时，仅适用于具有相近搜索等效项的计算机查询子集。正如我们所展示的，许多计算机查询缺乏此类类似物，并且我们对自主性和效率的估计可能无法推广到这些任务。然而，鉴于计算机查询往往更加复杂，这些任务的相应收益可能会更大。第三，虽然会话提供了组织不同任务的自然单元，但它们是嘈杂的代理：用户可以在多个会话中分配单​​个任务，或者相反，在单个会话中承担多个不相关的任务。第四，效率估计依赖于假设的每个工具的人类等效时间、人类监督时间和 LLM 估计。尽管盈亏平衡和敏感性分析显示了对误测的稳健性，但绝对量值应解释为近似值。第五，范围分析严重依赖于基于LLM的分类，这也引入了测量误差。然而，差距的大小表明这些模式不太可能仅由分类噪声驱动。最后，我们测量 Perplexity 生态系统内的用户行为，而不观察其外部的活动；因此，我们可能无法捕获用户工作流程和工具使用的全部范围。We note a few important caveats. First, the 90-day observation window (Feb 27–May 27, 2026) captures an early-adoption period in which users are disproportionately power users and paying subscribers. Whether the patterns generalize to a broader population as the product matures remains an open question. Second, the matched-query methodology, while controlling for task content, applies only to the subset of Computer queries with close Search equivalents. As we show, many Computer queries lack such analogues, and our estimates of autonomy and efficiency may not generalize to these tasks. Nevertheless, given that Computer queries tend to be more complex, the corresponding gains are likely to be larger for these tasks. Third, although sessions provide a natural unit for organizing distinct tasks, they are noisy proxies: users may distribute a single task across multiple sessions or, conversely, undertake multiple unrelated tasks within a single session. Fourth, the efficiency estimates rely on assumed per-tool human-equivalent times, human supervision time, and LLM estimates. Although the breakeven and sensitivity analysis shows robustness to mismeasurement, the absolute magnitudes should be interpreted as approximate. Fifth, the scope analysis relies heavily on LLM-based classification, which also introduces measurement error. However, the magnitude of the gaps suggests that the patterns are unlikely to be driven by classification noise alone. Lastly, we measure user behavior within the Perplexity ecosystem and do not observe activity outside it; as a result, we may not capture the full scope of users’ workflow and tool use.

未来的方向。Future directions.

我们的概念框架和实证分析仅限于个体工人和任务层面，因此对企业、消费者或更广泛的劳动力市场可能如何反应持开放态度。自然的下一步是研究这些微观层面的变化如何聚合到组织和劳动力市场结果。如果代理人降低了跨职业边界和专业水平的进入壁垒，并降低了协调成本，那么个人可能会产生以前需要团队才能完成的产出。然后，相关利润不仅限于现有任务的生产力，还包括工作包和团队结构的重组。未来的工作可以将代理的使用与下游工作场所的结果联系起来，以评估代理是否主要用于加速现有工人的速度、使工人能够承担跨职业的责任，或创造新的经济上可行的工作类别。我们的早期证据表明这三者都适用，但需要公司层面的生产和就业数据来评估代理人最终如何重塑工作捆绑、角色定义和团队结构。Our conceptual framework and empirical analysis are restricted to the individual-worker and task levels, and therefore leave open how firms, consumers, or the broader labor market may respond. A natural next step is to study how these micro-level changes aggregate to organizational and labor-market outcomes. If agents lower barriers to entry across occupational boundaries and expertise levels, and reduce coordination costs, individuals may produce outputs that previously required teams. The relevant margin then extends beyond productivity on existing tasks to the recomposition of job bundles and team structures. Future work could link agent usage to downstream workplace outcomes to assess whether agents are directed primarily toward accelerating existing workers, enabling workers to assume cross-occupational responsibilities, or creating new categories of economically viable work. Our early evidence points to all three, but firm-level production and employment data are needed to assess how agents ultimately reshape the bundling of work, the definition of roles, and the structure of teams.

F.3 周期性任务自动化Recurrent task automation

一些用户描述了从一次性提示转变为预定的、重复的自动化。报告的工作流程包括根据云文档汇总并发布到审核渠道的定期领导力报告、总结一周电子邮件、日历和项目跟踪器活动并提出下周优先事项的周末作业，以及自动 CRM 同步和潜在客户创建。一个经常性的好处是使以前被忽视的工作流（电子邮件积压、应收账款催收）保持最新状态，而不是批量处理它们。Several users described moving from one-off prompting to scheduled, recurring automations. Reported workflows included periodic leadership reports assembled from cloud documents and posted to a review channel, weekend jobs that summarize a week’s email, calendar, and project-tracker activity and propose the next week’s priorities, and automated CRM synchronization and lead creation. A recurring benefit was keeping previously neglected workstreams—email backlogs, receivables collection—continuously up to date rather than handling them in batches.

F.4 并行工作Parallel work

由于计算机是自主执行的，因此用户描述了委派任务并并行继续其他工作：启动批量异步工作并稍后检查，在无人值守的情况下设置多步骤项目，以及在离开之前提交任务。一些人将由此产生的工作流程描述为他们职业生涯中最富有成效的阶段之一。Because Computer executes autonomously, users described delegating tasks and continuing other work in parallel: firing off batches of asynchronous work and checking back later, setting up multi-step projects unattended, and submitting tasks before stepping away. Several characterized the resulting workflow as among the most productive stretches of their careers.

F.5 范围扩展Scope expansion

用户反复描述了自己从事专业领域之外的工作：处理软件和数据工程任务，在没有技术背景的情况下构建软件模块，以及在没有正式法律培训的情况下准备法律工作。一个反复出现的主题是跨越通常需要单独专家的职能，例如将会计、法律和合规工作结合起来。这些说法证实了第 1 节中的跨职业发现Users repeatedly described taking on work outside their domain of expertise: handling software and data-engineering tasks, building software modules without a technical background, and preparing legal work without formal legal training. A recurring theme was spanning functions that normally require separate specialists, such as combining accounting, legal, and compliance work. These accounts corroborate the cross-occupation findings in Section8.
