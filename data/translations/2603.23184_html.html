1 简介Introduction

根据人类反馈进行强化学习 (RLHF)，这是使大型语言模型 (LLM) 与人类价值观保持一致的基石Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), a cornerstone for aligning large language models (LLMs) with human values(Ouyang et al., 2022)，广泛应用于现代人工智能系统, is widely applied in modern AI systems(Li et al., 2026a, b; Wang et al., 2026c)，例如ChatGPT, such as ChatGPT(Achiam et al., 2023), 双子座, Gemini(Comanici et al., 2025)、和 DeepSeek, and DeepSeek(Guo et al., 2025)。 RLHF 中长期存在且艰巨的挑战在于奖励建模，即开发准确反映人类偏好的奖励模型，因为任何错误指定都可能误导强化学习 (RL) 过程，并最终导致次优对齐. A long-standing and formidable challenge in RLHF lies in reward modeling—developing reward models that accurately reflect human preference—since any misspecification can misguide the reinforcement learning (RL) process and ultimately lead to suboptimal alignment(Malik et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2026a; Miao et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024).

尽管奖励模型取得了快速进展Despite rapid progress in reward modeling(Wang et al., 2024a; Miao et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024)，当前的方法在很大程度上取决于训练的明确偏好数据，其中包括明确的正反馈和负反馈, current methods are heavily contingent upon explicit preference data for training, which comprises definitive positive and negative feedback(Liu et al., 2025b)。尽管这些数据产生高保真偏好信号，但其获取是劳动密集型且成本高昂的，从而限制了数据集的可扩展性并阻碍了广泛部署。相反，隐式偏好信号（例如点击、复制操作）提供了一种引人注目的替代方案。从用户交互中被动收集，没有明确的标签. Although this data yields high-fidelity preference signals, its acquisition is labor-intensive and costly, thereby constraining dataset scalability and impeding widespread deployment. In contrast, implicit preference signals (e.g., clicks, copy actions) offer a compelling alternative. Collected passively from user interactions without explicit labeling(Liu et al., 2025b)，隐性偏好自然丰富且划算, implicit preference is naturally abundant and cost-effective(Wang et al., 2025b)。因此，隐式奖励建模旨在根据隐式偏好数据训练奖励模型，为可扩展且具有成本效益的 RLHF 建立了一个有前途的范例。. Consequently, implicit reward modeling, which aims to train reward models from implicit preference data, establishes a promising paradigm for scalable and cost-efficient RLHF.

然而，根据隐式偏好数据进行奖励建模提出了两个独特的挑战。 ❶However, reward modeling from implicit preference data presents two unique challenges. ❶隐性偏好数据缺乏明确的负样本Implicit preference data lacks definitive negative samples。与显性偏好不同，隐性偏好信号仅包括正反馈和无反馈. Unlike explicit preference, implicit preference signals comprises only positive feedback and no-feedback(Wang et al., 2025b)。没有反馈的样本不能简单地被视为负面的，因为用户可能会感到满意，但不会采取行动（例如，阅读有用的响应而不复制它）。因此，标准的阳性-阴性分类方法不适用，因为将无反馈视为阴性不可避免地会导致假阴性。 ❷. Samples with no-feedback cannot simply be treated as negatives, as users may be satisfied yet refrain from taking action (e.g., reading a useful response without copying it). Consequently, standard positive-negative classification methods are inapplicable, as treating no-feedback as negative inevitably induces false negatives. ❷隐式偏好数据受到用户偏好偏差的影响Implicit preference data suffers from user preference bias。不同的响应有不同的引发用户反馈操作的倾向. Different responses have different propensities to elicit user feedback actions(Zheng et al., 2025b, 2026; Li et al., 2024a, b)。例如，虽然用户很容易在知识问答任务中复制满意的答案，但无论满意度如何，他们很少复制公开对话中的答案。这在无反馈样本中产生了潜在积极偏好的异质概率，使得大多数已建立的积极未标记（PU）学习方法不适用. For instance, while users readily copy satisfactory answers in knowledge question-answering tasks, they rarely copy responses in open dialogue regardless of satisfaction. This creates a heterogeneous probability of underlying positive preference among no-feedback samples, rendering most established positive-unlabeled (PU) learning methods inapplicable(Wang et al., 2025b; Zhou et al., 2025b)，因为他们通常假设未标记样本呈阳性的概率一致, as they typically assume a uniform probability of unlabeled samples being positive(Long et al., 2024). 总的来说，这些挑战阻碍了隐性奖励建模Collectively, these challenges impede implicit reward modeling，产生不准确的奖励信号，可能会误导后续的强化学习并阻碍有效的协调。, yielding inaccurate reward signals that can misguide subsequent reinforcement learning and hinder effective alignment.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 ImplicitRM，其目的是从隐性偏好中学习无偏见的奖励模型。核心思想是将训练样本分为四组：正主动、负主动、正被动、负被动。它是通过分层模型来实现的，该模型估计每个样本属于这些组的概率。在此基础上，我们通过似然最大化得出一个易于处理的学习目标。从理论上讲，我们证明所提出的目标解决了挑战❶-❷，因为它可以作为根据确定的正样本和负样本计算的理想目标的无偏估计器。根据经验，我们表明 ImplicitRM 从隐式偏好中学习准确的奖励模型，并提高了不同法学硕士和基准数据集的性能。To address these challenges, we propose ImplicitRM, which aims to learn unbiased reward models from implicit preference. The core idea is to stratify training samples into four groups: positive-active, negative-active, positive-passive, negative-passive. It is implemented by a stratification model that estimates the probability of each sample belonging to these groups. On this basis, we derive a tractable learning objective via likelihood maximization. Theoretically, we prove that the proposed objective resolves challenges ❶-❷ as it serves as an unbiased estimator of the ideal objective computed on definitive positive and negative samples. Empirically, we show that ImplicitRM learns accurate reward models from implicit preferences and improves performance across diverse LLMs and benchmark datasets.

3 方法论Methodology

3.1 动机Motivation

隐式偏好数据在用户与人工智能交互中是不明确的。例如，在深入研究中Implicit preference data is ambiguous in user-AI interactions. For instance, in deep research(Li et al., 2025)，如果用户认为答案具体且有用，可以将其复制到文档中；在旅游规划师中, users may copy answers into a document if they find them concrete and useful; in tour planner(Wang et al., 2026b)，如果用户发现有吸引力的行程，可以与朋友分享生成的行程。与显式偏好数据（例如，成对比较）不同，隐式偏好数据是从用户交互中被动获取的，从而消除了标签开销, users may share generated itineraries with friends if they find them attractive. Unlike explicit preference data (e.g., pairwise comparisons), implicit preference data is acquired passively from user interactions, eliminating labeling overhead(Liu et al., 2025b)。因此，隐式偏好数据为奖励建模提供了丰富且低成本的资源。. Consequently, implicit preference data offers an abundant and low-cost resource for reward modeling.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们调查了In this work, we investigate the隐性奖励模型implicit reward modeling问题，旨在从隐式反馈数据训练奖励模型。我们发现了这个问题固有的两个关键挑战：缺乏明确的负样本和存在用户偏好偏差。 为了应对这些挑战，我们引入了用于隐式奖励建模的 ImplicitRM。它首先将样本分层为潜在组，以捕获底层数据生成过程。这种分层使得能够构建相对于理想目标无偏的似然最大化目标，并且可以使用隐式偏好数据进行计算，从而解决了两个已确定的挑战。大量实验表明，ImplicitRM 可以有效地从隐式数据中学习准确的奖励模型，在不同的数据集和 LLM 中很好地泛化，并对下游 RLHF 性能产生显着改进。problem, which aims to train reward models from implicit feedback data. We identified two critical challenges inherent in this problem: the absence of definitive negative samples and the presence of user preference bias. To address these challenges, we introduced ImplicitRM for implicit reward modeling. It first stratifies samples into latent groups to capture the underlying data generative process. This stratification enables the construction of a likelihood maximization objective that is unbiased with respect to the ideal objective and can be computed using the implicit preference data, therefore addressing the two identified challenges. Extensive experiments demonstrate that ImplicitRM effectively learns accurate reward models from implicit data, generalizes well across diverse datasets and LLMs, and yields significant improvements on downstream RLHF performance.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations & Future Works.

这项工作有两个局限性需要未来的研究。首先，本次工作聚焦于培训目标；未来的研究可以探索专门的架构，例如专家混合，以联合建模奖励和倾向。 其次，这项工作假设观察到的反馈（例如，副本）表明积极的偏好。后续工作可以通过结合不确定性估计或稳健的损失函数来研究正反馈可能有噪音的情况（例如，误点击）。There are two limitations with this work that warrant future investigation. First, this work focuses on the training objective; future research could explore specialized architectures, such as Mixture of Experts, to jointly model reward and propensity. Second, this work assumes observed feedback (e.g., copy) indicates positive preference. Subsequent works can investigate situations where positive feedback can be noisy (e.g., misclicks), by incorporating uncertainty estimation or robust loss functions.
