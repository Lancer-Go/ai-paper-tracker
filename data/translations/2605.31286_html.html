Deformable-object folding is a representative challenge, requiring robots to handle clothing items from random initial states across varying categories, geometries, materials, and scenes. However, existing VLA systems commonly train separate policies for different object categories, while naively mixed multi-task training often suffers from task interference and degraded performance. DeMaVLA adopts a VLM backbone with an action expert and formulates continuous action generation using flow matching. To improve efficiency, the action expert is constructed by pruning every other transformer layer while preserving layer-wise alignment with the VLM backbone, reducing training and inference cost. DeMaVLA is first pre-trained on approximately 5,000 hours of selected real-world dual-arm demonstrations to acquire general manipulation priors. It is then post-trained on mixed folding data that aggregates self-collected demonstrations and corrective trajectories from real-robot failures across multiple folding tasks through a human-in-the-loop Data Aggregation (DAgger) pipeline. Experiments show that DeMaVLA achieves competitive performance on RoboTwin and strong real-world results on our household folding benchmark. These results highlight the value of scalable real-world data, efficient action generation, and corrective learning for general-purpose VLA policies in deformable-object manipulation.

1 简介Introduction

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）基础模型已成为通向通用机器人代理的一条有前途的途径Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models have become a promising route toward general-purpose robot agents[4, 17, 28, 10, 3, 37, 9, 35]。通过将视觉观察和语言指令转化为可执行动作，最新的系统从大型视觉语言模型中转移语义知识. By grounding visual observations and language instructions into executable actions, recent systems transfer semantic knowledge from large vision-language models[2, 1]进入物理控制。然而，现实世界的家庭部署需要的不仅仅是语义基础：机器人必须在不断变化的对象类别、物理状态、任务条件和场景布局下重用操作技能。可变形物体折叠是这一挑战的典型例子。服装物品可能会以随机、皱巴巴或自遮挡的状态出现，折叠衬衫、裤子、毛巾或裙子需要不同的动作序列，同时仍然依赖于共同的物理先验，例如抓住角落、对齐织物、抚平皱纹和完成长视野双手折叠。into physical control. Yet real-world household deployment requires more than semantic grounding: a robot must reuse manipulation skills under changing object categories, physical states, task conditions, and scene layouts. Deformable-object folding is a representative example of this challenge. Garment items may appear in random, crumpled, or self-occluded states, and folding shirts, pants, towels, or skirts requires distinct action sequences while still relying on shared physical priors such as grasping corners, aligning fabric, smoothing wrinkles, and completing long-horizon bimanual folds.

现有的基于 VLA 的折叠系统通常通过针对特定任务的适应来解决这种多样性问题，为不同的服装类别分配单独的策略。这种策略很难在家庭环境中扩展，因为机器人必须处理各种可折叠物品和随机初始状态，而无需手动选择和维护许多专用模型。一种直接的替代方案是在混合折叠数据上训练单一策略，但当不同的对象类别需要不兼容的中间状态或运动模式时，简单的多任务训练可能会引入任务干扰并降低性能。这些限制激发了统一的折叠策略，该策略可以跨服装类别共享可重复使用的折叠先验，同时保留特定于任务的执行。Existing VLA-based folding systems typically address this diversity through task-specific adaptation, assigning separate policies to different clothing categories. This strategy is difficult to scale in household environments, where the robot must handle diverse foldable items and random initial states without manually selecting and maintaining many specialized models. A straightforward alternative is to train a single policy on mixed folding data, but naive multi-task training can introduce task interference and degrade performance when different object categories require incompatible intermediate states or motion patterns. These limitations motivate a unified folding policy that can share reusable folding priors across clothing categories while preserving task-specific execution.

为了超越特定类别的折叠政策，我们引入了To move beyond category-specific folding policies, we introduce德玛VLADeMaVLA，可推广的 VLA 基础模型, a VLA foundation model for generalizableDe可成型的formableMa乳交。 DeMaVLA 被设计为双臂平台上多类别双手折叠的单检查点策略。该模型采用带有动作专家的 VLM 主干，并使用流匹配制定连续动作生成nipulation. DeMaVLA is designed as a single-checkpoint policy for multi-category bimanual folding on a dual-arm platform. The model adopts a VLM backbone with an action expert and formulates continuous action generation using flow matching[25, 4]，实现长视距双臂操控的平滑动作预测。具体来说，我们的动作专家是根据 Qwen3-VL 的 LLM 组件构建的，使其能够与语言主干共享相同的转换器架构。然而，直接使用完整的 LLM 作为动作专家会引入大量计算，特别是因为基于流的动作生成需要重复的专家前向传递。为了在提高效率的同时保留一致的架构，我们修剪了动作专家中的所有其他变压器层，从而在不更改整体 VLA 接口的情况下降低了训练和推理成本。, enabling smooth action prediction for long-horizon dual-arm manipulation. Specifically, our action expert is built from the LLM component of Qwen3-VL, enabling it to share the same transformer architecture as the language backbone. However, directly using the full LLM as an action expert introduces substantial computation, especially because flow-based action generation requires repeated expert forward passes. To retain the aligned architecture while improving efficiency, we prune every other transformer layer in the action expert, reducing training and inference cost without changing the overall VLA interface.

DeMaVLA 接受了可扩展的预训练和故障驱动的后训练。首先对来自公共和自行收集的数据集的选定现实世界双臂演示进行大约 5,000 小时的预训练，以获得一般操作先验。然后对混合折叠数据进行后训练，将自我收集的演示与通过人机循环数据聚合 (DAgger) 管道收集的纠正轨迹相结合。该流程通过在真实机器人上推出混合折叠策略并纠正不同服装类别的故障，直接针对统一模型的故障模式，大幅提高其多类别折叠能力。我们在 RoboTwin 上评估 DeMaVLADeMaVLA is trained with both scalable pre-training and failure-driven post-training. It is first pre-trained on approximately 5,000 hours of selected real-world dual-arm demonstrations from public and self-collected datasets to acquire general manipulation priors. It is then post-trained on mixed folding data that combines self-collected demonstrations with corrective trajectories collected through a human-in-the-loop Data Aggregation (DAgger) pipeline. By rolling out the mixed folding policy on real robots and correcting its failures across different clothing categories, this pipeline directly targets the failure modes of the unified model and substantially improves its multi-category folding capability. We evaluate DeMaVLA on RoboTwin[11]以及涵盖多种服装类别和随机初始状态的真实家庭折叠基准。and a real-world household folding benchmark covering multiple clothing categories and random initial states.

我们的主要贡献有三方面。首先，我们强调实施级设计在构建有效的 VLA 策略中的重要性，包括基于 LLM 的行动专家、跳层剪枝、流程匹配行动生成和训练时间 RTC。其次，我们证明了可扩展的现实世界预训练对于可变形物体操作至关重要，因为 DeMaVLA 受益于大约 5,000 小时的双臂演示和混合折叠数据。第三，DeMaVLA在模拟和现实家庭折叠方面都取得了强劲的表现，验证了大规模数据、高效模型设计和故障驱动的纠正学习相结合的有效性。Our main contributions are threefold. First, we highlight the importance of implementation-level design in building effective VLA policies, including an LLM-based action expert, skip-layer pruning, flow-matching action generation, and training-time RTC. Second, we demonstrate that scalable real-world pre-training is critical for deformable-object manipulation, as DeMaVLA benefits from approximately 5,000 hours of dual-arm demonstrations and mixed folding data. Third, DeMaVLA achieves strong performance in both simulation and real-world household folding, validating the effectiveness of combining large-scale data, efficient model design, and failure-driven corrective learning.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 VLA 基础模型VLA Foundation Models

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型通过将视觉感知、语言指令和动作生成连接到一个统一的模型中，改变了机器人策略学习。与传统的任务特定策略相比，VLA 为指令跟踪和多任务操作提供了可扩展的接口，并且在将语义知识转移到机器人控制方面表现出了强大的潜力Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have changed robot policy learning by connecting visual perception, language instructions, and action generation in a unified model. Compared with conventional task-specific policies, VLAs provide a scalable interface for instruction following and multi-task manipulation, and have shown strong potential in transferring semantic knowledge into robot control[38, 28, 16, 10, 3, 37, 9, 35]。最近的 VLA 建立在预训练的视觉语言模型 (VLM) 的基础上，其图像文本预训练为感知、基础和指令理解提供了有用的先验知识. Most recent VLAs build on pre-trained vision-language models (VLMs), whose image-text pre-training provides useful priors for perception, grounding, and instruction understanding[1, 2]。然后通过在 VLM 上下文之上添加政策负责人或行动专家来学习机器人行动. Robot actions are then learned by adding policy heads or action experts on top of the VLM context[20, 4].

扩展具体数据同样重要。最近的基金会政策收集跨不同实施例、任务和环境的机器人轨迹，以提高泛化能力Scaling embodied data is equally important. Recent foundation policies collect robot trajectories across diverse embodiments, tasks, and environments to improve generalization[32, 26, 33, 18]。相关系统进一步探索跨实体学习、开放世界机器人数据集、实时执行和通才操纵策略. Related systems further explore cross-embodiment learning, open-world robot datasets, real-time execution, and generalist manipulation policies[37, 9, 30, 24, 36, 7, 12]。对于连续控制，流匹配已成为一种实用的公式，因为它可以对多模态动作分布进行建模，同时生成平滑的动作块. For continuous control, flow matching has become a practical formulation because it models multimodal action distributions while generating smooth action chunks[25, 4].

DeMaVLA 遵循这种基于 VLM 和流的 VLA 范式，但侧重于不同的部署目标：学习跨多个服装类别和随机初始状态的统一折叠策略。 DeMaVLA 没有将每个类别视为独立的下游适应，而是从大规模双臂数据中学习共享的折叠先验，并通过单个检查点应用它们以实现不同的折叠技能。DeMaVLA follows this VLM- and flow-based VLA paradigm, but focuses on a different deployment goal: learning a unified folding policy across multiple clothing categories and random initial states. Instead of treating each category as an independent downstream adaptation, DeMaVLA learns shared folding priors from large-scale dual-arm data and applies them through a single checkpoint for diverse folding skills.

2.2 互动模仿学习Interactive Imitation Learning

人类演示为复杂的机器人操作提供了直接监督，使模仿学习成为现代 VLA 策略的核心训练策略。在标准被动设置中，首先收集专家轨迹，并通过行为克隆离线训练策略。这种方法简单且稳定，但它会受到协变量偏移的影响：一旦学习的策略偏离示范分布，错误就会复合，机器人可能无法恢复。交互式模仿学习通过从学习者自己的状态分布中收集监督来解决这一限制Human demonstrations provide direct supervision for complex robot manipulation, making imitation learning a central training strategy for modern VLA policies. In the standard passive setting, expert trajectories are collected first and a policy is trained offline through behavior cloning. This approach is simple and stable, but it suffers from covariate shift: once the learned policy deviates from the demonstration distribution, errors can compound and the robot may fail to recover. Interactive imitation learning addresses this limitation by collecting supervision from the learner’s own state distribution[8].

数据聚合（DAgger）通过迭代执行当前策略、查询访问状态上的专家操作以及将新标记的数据聚合到训练集中来形式化这一想法Data Aggregation (DAgger) formalizes this idea by iteratively executing the current policy, querying expert actions on visited states, and aggregating the newly labeled data into the training set[29]。与被动行为克隆相比，DAgger 式方法提高了样本效率和鲁棒性，因为它们针对的是学习策略可能出错的状态。人机交互变体允许人类专家决定何时进行干预，从而使这一想法在真正的机器人中变得实用。之前的工作研究了预算感知查询、远程操作校正、恢复数据、数据增强和人类人工智能副驾驶系统. Compared with passive behavior cloning, DAgger-style methods improve sample efficiency and robustness because they target states where the learned policy is likely to make mistakes. Human-in-the-loop variants make this idea practical for real robots by allowing a human expert to decide when to intervene. Prior work studies budget-aware querying, teleoperated corrections, recovery data, data augmentation, and human-AI copilot systems[14, 27, 22, 15, 21, 23]。 Human-Gated DAgger 进一步让操作员仅在需要时接管并在纠正后释放控制权. Human-Gated DAgger further lets the operator take over only when needed and release control after correction[19, 31, 34].

DeMaVLA 采用这种人为门控的多类别双手折叠方案。从预先训练的检查点初始化策略后，我们通过在真实机器人上推出策略并记录混合折叠任务期间故障状态下的纠正性人类干预来收集 DAgger 数据。这对于长视距可变形对象操作非常重要，其中抓取、对齐或中间结构状态中的小错误可能会复合成静态演示数据集中罕见的故障模式。通过将这些修正汇总到训练后，DeMaVLA 直接针对统一折叠策略的失败模式，并提高其在不同服装类别中的稳健性。DeMaVLA adopts this human-gated formulation for multi-category bimanual folding. After initializing the policy from the pre-trained checkpoint, we collect DAgger data by rolling out the policy on real robots and recording corrective human interventions at failure states during mixed folding tasks. This is important for long-horizon deformable-object manipulation, where small errors in grasping, alignment, or intermediate fabric states can compound into failure modes that are rare in static demonstration datasets. By aggregating these corrections into post-training, DeMaVLA directly targets the failure modes of the unified folding policy and improves its robustness across different clothing categories.

训练后。Post-training.对于实际部署，DeMaVLA 通过训练时实时分块 (RTC) 进行后训练For real-world deployment, DeMaVLA is post-trained with training-time real-time chunking (RTC)[5]。标准动作分块策略可能会受到推理延迟的影响：当模型生成下一个动作块时，机器人必须继续执行先前预测的块中的动作。而不是应用推理时间修复或指导. Standard action-chunking policies may suffer from inference latency: while the model is generating the next action chunk, the robot must continue executing actions from the previously predicted chunk. Instead of applying inference-time inpainting or guidance[6]，训练时 RTC 在训练后模拟这种延迟，并教导模型根据已提交的操作生成未来的操作。, training-time RTC simulates this latency during post-training and teaches the model to generate future actions conditioned on already committed actions.

具体来说，每个动作块被分为提交的前缀和预测的后缀。已提交的前缀对应于新预测可用之前已执行的操作，而后缀包含模型必须生成的未来操作。在训练后期间，前缀作为调节上下文保持干净，并且流噪声仅应用于后缀。训练损失仅根据后缀动作计算，鼓励 DeMaVLA 在尊重提交的前缀的同时产生时间一致的未来轨迹。在推理时，DeMaVLA 以先前提交的操作为条件并预测剩余的未来操作，从而实现异步操作块执行，而无需额外的修复或基于反向传播的指导。Specifically, each action chunk is divided into a committed prefix and a predicted postfix. The committed prefix corresponds to actions that will already be executed before the new prediction becomes available, while the postfix contains the future actions that the model must generate. During post-training, the prefix is kept clean as conditioning context, and flow noise is applied only to the postfix. The training loss is computed only on the postfix actions, encouraging DeMaVLA to produce temporally consistent future trajectories while respecting the committed prefix. At inference time, DeMaVLA conditions on the previously committed actions and predicts the remaining future actions, enabling asynchronous action-chunk execution without additional inpainting or backpropagation-based guidance.

3.4 人在环 DAggerHuman-in-the-Loop DAgger

尽管大规模预训练为 DeMaVLA 提供了广泛的操纵先验，但现实世界的多类别折叠仍然使策略暴露于故障状态，而仅靠被动演示很难覆盖这些故障状态。抓取、布料对齐或中间折叠姿势中的小错误可能会在较长的视野内累积，并导致机器人进入演示分布之外的状态。为了提高此类故障下的鲁棒性，我们采用了受 HG-DAgger 启发的人控交互式模仿学习管道Although large-scale pre-training provides DeMaVLA with broad manipulation priors, real-world multi-category folding still exposes the policy to failure states that are difficult to cover with passive demonstrations alone. Small errors in grasping, cloth alignment, or intermediate folding poses can accumulate over long horizons and lead the robot into states outside the demonstration distribution. To improve robustness under such failures, we adopt a human-gated interactive imitation learning pipeline inspired by HG-DAgger[19].

我们通过预训练和任务演示数据的行为克隆来初始化策略。在交互式教学过程中，混合折叠策略被部署在真实的机器人上，并在不同的服装类别上执行折叠展示。当策略进入不安全、不稳定或明显次优状态时，人类操作员会监控部署并进行干预。一旦触发干预，控制权就会完全转移给人类专家，专家会纠正行为并引导机器人回到有效的任务状态。修正完成后，控制权又交还给自主策略。这种人为门控机制避免了要求专家在混合控制下持续标记状态，而是围绕统一策略的实际故障模式收集高价值的纠正数据。We initialize the policy with behavior cloning on the pre-training and task demonstration data. During interactive teaching, the mixed folding policy is deployed on the real robot and executes folding rollouts across different clothing categories. A human operator monitors the rollout and intervenes when the policy enters an unsafe, unstable, or clearly suboptimal state. Once intervention is triggered, control is fully transferred to the human expert, who corrects the behavior and guides the robot back to a valid task state. After the correction is completed, control is released back to the autonomous policy. This human-gated mechanism avoids requiring the expert to continuously label states under mixed control, and instead collects high-value corrective data around the unified policy’s actual failure modes.

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 DeMaVLA，这是一种用于广义变形操纵的 VLA 基础模型。 DeMaVLA 超越了特定类别的折叠策略，通过使用单个检查点来处理具有不同服装、初始状态和长视野双手例程的多项家庭折叠任务。其设计结合了 Qwen3-VL 主干、基于 LLM 的层对齐动作专家、跳层修剪、流匹配动作生成和训练时 RTC，表明实施级架构和部署选择对于有效的现实世界 VLA 策略至关重要。为了支持可扩展的学习，DeMaVLA 在选定的现实世界双臂演示中进行了大约 5,000 小时的预训练，然后使用混合折叠数据和针对策略失败状态的人控 DAgger 校正进行后训练。 RoboTwin 和我们现实世界的家用折叠基准的实验验证了大规模数据、高效模型设计和故障驱动的纠正学习的结合，在模拟和现实世界的多类别折叠中展示了强大的性能。We introduced DeMaVLA, a VLA foundation model for generalizable deformable manipulation. DeMaVLA moves beyond category-specific folding policies by using a single checkpoint to handle multiple household folding tasks with different garments, initial states, and long-horizon bimanual routines. Its design combines a Qwen3-VL backbone, an LLM-based layer-aligned action expert, skip-layer pruning, flow-matching action generation, and training-time RTC, showing that implementation-level architecture and deployment choices are central to effective real-world VLA policies. To support scalable learning, DeMaVLA is pre-trained on approximately 5,000 hours of selected real-world dual-arm demonstrations and then post-trained with mixed folding data and human-gated DAgger corrections that target policy failure states. Experiments on RoboTwin and our real-world household folding benchmark validate this combination of large-scale data, efficient model design, and failure-driven corrective learning, demonstrating strong performance in both simulation and real-world multi-category folding.
