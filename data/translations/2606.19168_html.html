超越安全数据：预训练阶段调整与定期安全反思Beyond Safe Data: Pretraining-Stage Alignment with Regular Safety Reflection

Jinhan Li1 Kexian Tang111footnotemark: 1 Yihan Xu1 Zhuorui Ye Kaifeng Lyu1

摘要Abstract

为了实现大型语言模型 (LLM) 更深入的安全一致性，最近的工作研究了如何将安全干预措施提前推进到预训练阶段，主要是通过过滤不安全数据或将其重写为更安全的形式。 我们认为，预训练阶段的调整不应只限于确保数据安全：法学硕士可能会将看似良性的知识和能力转化为不安全的行为。为此，我们建议To achieve deeper safety alignment for large language models (LLMs), recent efforts have studied how to push safety interventions earlier into the pretraining stage, primarily by filtering unsafe data or rewriting it into safer forms. We argue that pretraining-stage alignment should go beyond making the data safe: LLMs may compose seemingly benign knowledge and capabilities into unsafe behaviors. To this end, we propose安全反思预训练Safety Reflection Pretraining, 一种预训练阶段对齐方法，定期将简短的安全反射插入到预训练语料库中，将自我监控直接集成到语言建模中，建立一种基础能力，随后通过兼容的训练后进行强化。 我们对 FineWeb-Edu 上预训练的 1.7B 模型进行的实验表明，安全反射预训练提高了安全分类的准确性，并大大降低了推理阶段和微调攻击的成功率。 作为对我们现实世界实验的补充，我们还引入了完全受控的合成环境，, a pretraining-stage alignment method which regularly inserts short safety reflections into pretraining corpora to integrate self-monitoring directly into language modeling, establishing a foundational capability that is subsequently reinforced by compatible post-training. Our experiments with 1.7B models pretrained on FineWeb-Edu show that Safety Reflection Pretraining improves safety classification accuracy and substantially reduces the success rates of inference-stage and finetuning attacks. Complementary to our real-world experiments, we also introduce a fully controlled synthetic environment,医疗安全世界MedSafetyWorld，具有明确的安全定义和推理结构，模型可以轻松地从安全数据中概括出不安全行为。 与数据过滤和重写相比，MedSafetyWorld 中的消融进一步证明了安全反射预训练在防止模型对从安全数据中归纳出的不安全行为采取行动方面具有明显的优势。 总而言之，我们的研究结果表明，预训练对齐不仅应该使训练数据安全，而且还应该塑造模型可能从安全数据中获取的行为。, with a clear definition of safety and a reasoning structure under which models can easily generalize unsafe behaviors from safe data. Ablations in MedSafetyWorld further demonstrate a clear advantage of Safety Reflection Pretraining in preventing models from acting on unsafe behaviors generalized from safe data, compared with data filtering and rewriting. Taken together, our findings suggest that pretraining alignment should not only make the training data safe, but also shape the behaviors that models are likely to acquire from safe data.

1 简介Introduction

在当前实践中，安全调整主要在训练后进行，包括对策划的安全数据集进行监督微调In current practice, safety alignment is primarily performed during post-training, including supervised fine-tuning on curated safety datasets(Bianchi et al., 2024), 根据人类或人工智能反馈进行强化学习, reinforcement learning from human or AI feedback(Ouyang et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022b; Dai et al., 2024), 以及具有特定安全奖励的强化学习, and reinforcement learning with safety-specific rewards(Mu et al., 2024; Yuan et al., 2025)。 然而，这些方法仍然存在各种漏洞：操纵输入提示. However, these methods still suffer from various vulnerabilities: manipulating input prompts(Zou et al., 2023)或者即使在良性任务上也可以微调模型or finetuning the model even on benign tasks(Qi et al., 2024)可能会损害甚至破坏对准过程中获得的安全护栏。 更根本的问题是执行安全调整may compromise or even break the safety guardrails acquired during alignment. A more fundamental concern is that safety alignment performed仅only训练后的过程可能仍然浅薄且脆弱，改变模型的行为而不会实质性改变其底层功能during post-training may remain shallow and fragile, changing the model’s behavior without substantially altering its underlying capabilities(Qi et al., 2025; Ji et al., 2025)。 这促使越来越多的工作在培训流程中尽早进行安全干预，特别是在预培训期间。. This has motivated a growing line of work on moving safety interventions earlier in the training pipeline, especially during pretraining.

在现有的预训练阶段对齐方法中，Among existing pretraining-stage alignment methods,数据过滤data filtering是最常见的方法。 减少模型生成中的不良属性，例如毒性is the most common approach. To reduce undesirable attributes in model generations, such as toxicity(Longpre et al., 2024; Soldaini et al., 2024)，数据过滤会在训练前删除预计包含有毒、有害或其他不良内容的文档或令牌, data filtering removes documents or tokens predicted to contain toxic, harmful, or otherwise undesirable content before training(Llama Team, 2024; Qwen Team, 2025)。 最近的研究进一步探索了抑制更高级别不良能力的方法，例如那些可能导致生物风险的能力. Recent studies have further explored such methods for suppressing higher-level undesirable capabilities, such as those that may contribute to biological risks(O’Brien et al., 2026; Rathi and Radford, 2026; Chen et al., 2025)。 另一种方法是. An alternative approach is数据重写data rewriting，它并没有完全删除不安全的数据，而是使用对齐的模型将其重写为更安全的形式, which does not completely remove unsafe data, but rather uses an aligned model to rewrite it into a safer form(Maini et al., 2025; Sam et al., 2026; Jiang et al., 2025)。 这保留了有助于模型更好地区分安全行为和不安全行为的信息. This keeps information that helps models better separate safe behaviors from unsafe ones(Li et al., 2025).

虽然有效，但数据过滤和数据重写都主要框架 预训练对齐作为控制模型内容的问题 从中学习。 推到理想的极限，这一系列工作的目标是构建 完全不包含不安全数据的预训练语料库。While effective, both data filtering and data rewriting primarily frame pretraining alignment as a problem of controlling what content the model learns from. Pushed to its ideal limit, this line of work would aim to construct a pretraining corpus that contains no unsafe data at all.但这足以进行预训练对齐吗？But would that be sufficient for pretraining alignment?

数据过滤和重写的局限性。Limitations of Data Filtering and Rewriting.

在本文中，我们认为深度安全调整需要的不仅仅是确保数据安全。 为了使这一点具体化，我们引入了一个完全受控的合成环境，In this paper, we argue that deep safety alignment requires more than making the data safe. To make this point concrete, we introduce a fully controlled synthetic environment,医疗安全世界MedSafetyWorld，具有明确的安全定义和涉及安全和不安全结果的推理结构。 在这种情况下，一些用户向法学硕士寻求正常的医疗建议，而另一些用户则询问如何滥用特定化合物对他人造成伤害。 我们表明，即使仅接受有关正常医学知识和查询的安全数据的训练，法学硕士仍然可以获得通过泛化响应有害查询的能力，并且这种能力可以通过推理阶段和微调攻击轻松引发。 参见, with a clear definition of safety and a reasoning structure that involves both safe and unsafe outcomes. In this setting, some users ask LLMs for normal medical advice, while others ask how to misuse specific compounds to cause harm to another person. We show that, even when trained only on safe data about normal medical knowledge and queries, LLMs may still acquire the capability to respond to harmful queries through generalization, and such capabilities can be easily elicited by inference-stage and finetuning attacks. 这种故障模式表明，即使预训练数据完全安全，LLM 也可以根据其预训练数据进行推理、组合和概括，从而产生不安全的行为。 一般来说，这种泛化可能仍然隐藏在模型的参数中，并且难以检测或预测，就像之前观察到的窄微调可能会意外地引发有害行为一样This failure mode suggests that LLMs can reason, compose, and generalize from their pretraining data to produce unsafe behaviors, even when the pretraining data is completely safe. In general, such generalization can remain latent in the model’s parameters and be difficult to detect or anticipate, just like prior observations that narrow finetuning can unexpectedly induce harmful behaviors(Qi et al., 2024; He et al., 2024; Betley et al., 2026; Draganov et al., 2026).

安全反思预训练。Safety Reflection Pretraining.

尽管模型泛化的确切形式可能很难预测，但这种泛化最终必须植根于模型在预训练期间内化的内容。 因此我们建议Although the exact form of model generalization can be difficult to anticipate, such generalization must ultimately be rooted in what the model internalizes during pretraining. We therefore propose安全反思预训练（SRP）Safety Reflection Pretraining (SRP)：我们不只是过滤或重写预训练语料库，而是定期在预训练文本中插入安全反射。 这里，安全反映是对前面的文本片段的安全性的简短判断。 具体来说，我们将预训练语料库划分为数百个标记的片段。 在每个片段之后，我们使用安全分类器生成并插入反射，该反射由判定标记“安全”或“不安全”以及指定不安全行为类型的类别标记组成。 参见: instead of only filtering or rewriting the pretraining corpus, we regularly insert safety reflections into the pretraining text. Here, a safety reflection is a short judgment on the safety of the preceding text segment. Concretely, we partition the pretraining corpus into segments of a few hundred tokens. After each segment, we use a safety classifier to generate and insert a reflection, which consists of a verdict token “Safe” or “Unsafe” and a category token that specifies the type of unsafe behavior.     更重要的是，使安全反射成为语言模型的一部分，可以鼓励模型内化一种自我监控的形式，以便在其生成变得不安全时能够识别并停止自身。 结合将数据与类似的安全反射格式对齐的兼容的训练后设计，SRP 大大降低了推理时间和微调攻击下的攻击成功率，同时保留了一般能力。 我们在 FineWeb-Edu 上的预训练实验中证明了这一点，并在More importantly, making safety reflection part of language modeling encourages the model to internalize a form of self-monitoring, so that it can recognize and stop itself once its generation becomes unsafe. Our ablation studies show that SRP works best when post-training is compatible with it, rather than done in an arbitrary way: keeping the same safety reflection format in the post-training data lets the model still perform reflections at inference time. 总的来说，我们的结果表明，除了确保训练数据安全之外，预训练阶段的对齐还可以旨在诱导本机模型行为，从而加强安全护栏。 我们的工作朝这个方向迈出了第一步，我们鼓励未来的工作进一步探索数据过滤和重写之外的预训练阶段对齐技术。Overall, our results suggest that, beyond making the training data safe, pretraining-stage alignment can also aim to induce native model behaviors that strengthen safety guardrails. Our work provides an initial step in this direction, and we encourage future work to further explore pretraining-stage alignment techniques beyond data filtering and rewriting.

与 SafeLM 的比较。Comparison with SafeLM.

安全LMSafeLM(Maini et al., 2025)介绍了几种以数据为中心的预训练阶段对齐方法。 其中，Harmility-Tag标注的预训练与我们的关系最为密切，因为它也在预训练语料库中插入了安全标签。 然而，SafeLM 不涉及定期的安全反思。 它将安全相关的标签插入到不安全的预训练数据中，但这些标签是随机放置的。 这意味着标签可能会出现在其要标记的不安全内容之前，而不是作为对前面片段的判断。 因此，SafeLM 依赖于修改后的推理过程：其标记机制旨在与调整后的推理算法 SafeBeam 一起工作，如果没有它，效率会大大降低。 相比之下，我们的方法定期插入反射，每个反射都充当对前一个文本段的安全判断。这训练模型执行安全反射，并允许该方法在标准解码算法下保持有效。introduces several data-centric methods for pretraining-stage alignment. However, SafeLM does not involve regular safety reflection. It inserts safety-related tags into unsafe pretraining data, but these tags are placed randomly. This means that a tag may appear before the unsafe content it is intended to mark, rather than serving as a judgment of the preceding segment. Consequently, SafeLM relies on a modified inference procedure: its tagging mechanism is designed to work together with the adjusted inference algorithm SafeBeam, and is substantially less effective without it. By contrast, our method inserts reflections at regular intervals, with each reflection serving as a safety judgment of the immediately preceding text segment. 7 结论Conclusions

总的来说，我们的结果表明，预训练阶段的对齐不仅应该使训练数据安全，而且还应该塑造模型可能从安全数据中获取的行为。Overall, our results suggest that pretraining-stage alignment should not only make the training data safe, but also shape the behaviors that models are likely to acquire from safe data.医疗安全世界MedSafetyWorld提供了一个具有干净数据的合成世界来支持这一论点，表明深度对齐必须深深植根于训练过程中。 SRP 通过诱导定期安全反思行为，在这个方向上迈出了具体的一步，但我们也注意到 SRP必须与训练后设计相结合，如果在训练后没有保留反射格式，模型可能会在推理时停止执行安全反射。 这是我们设置中的一个小问题，因为模型训练器通常控制预训练和训练后，因此模型训练器可以在训练后轻松保留相同的反射格式。 尽管如此，我们认为，更深层次的训练前阶段调整应该使安全相关行为更加稳健，不会被训练后覆盖。 我们鼓励未来的工作探索实现这一目标的进一步技术。offers a synthetic world with clean data to support this argument, suggesting deep alignment must be deeply rooted in the training process. SRP offers a concrete step in this direction by inducing regular safety reflection behaviors, but we also note that SRP has to be coupled with post-training designs, and if the reflection format is not preserved during post-training, the models may stop performing safety reflection at inference time. This is a minor issue in our setting since the model trainer typically controls both pretraining and post-training, and thus the model trainer can easily preserve the same reflection format during post-training. Nevertheless, we argue that a deeper form of pretraining-stage alignment should make safety-related behaviors more robust to being overwritten by post-training. We encourage future work to explore further techniques for achieving this goal.
