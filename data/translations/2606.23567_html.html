我们介绍了We introduced自我感知调度 (SAS)Self-Aware Scheduling (SAS)，一个用于优化的即插即用框架 预训练掩蔽扩散语言模型的揭露顺序。我们的理论分析保证优化我们的目标可以减少目标失配的上限，并在可实现性下实现精确性。 与用于策略优化的强化学习相结合，SAS 改进了结构化和 跨领域和规模的开放式推理——从具有 1B 模型的数独到数学和代码 使用 LLaDA-8B 进行推理，并且在代长度和半自回归方面保持稳健 块解码。总体而言，SAS 提供了对齐任意阶扩散推理的通用方法 具有概率目标，并提出了一条通往自我改进扩散法学硕士的实用路径 通过迭代调度和模型细化。根据备注，未来的自然方向, a plug-and-play framework for optimizing the unmasking order of pretrained masked diffusion language models. Our theoretical analysis guarantees that optimizing our objective reduces an upper bound on the target mismatch, and achieves exactness under realizability. Combined with reinforcement learning for policy optimization, SAS improves both structured and open-ended reasoning across domains and scales—from Sudoku with a 1B model to math and code reasoning with LLaDA-8B—and remains robust across generation lengths and semi-autoregressive block decoding. Overall, SAS provides a general recipe for aligning any-order diffusion inference with probabilistic objectives, and suggests a practical path toward self-improving diffusion LLMs via iterative scheduling and model refinement. 局限性Limitations

仍然存在一些限制。首先，SAS 从教师强制的自我意识奖励中学习，即冻结降噪器在真实轨迹上的路径可能性。这在训练和自由运行推理之间造成了潜在的差距。其次，我们当前的策略主要是在每个任务的基础上进行训练的，因此跨任务和跨域转移很有希望，但尚未完全表征。第三，虽然 SAS 在推理时对于冻结主干网来说是即插即用的，但学习顺序策略仍然需要额外的训练计算，并且该策略引入了少量但非零的推理开销。通过更好的迁移、减少教师强制不匹配以及混合自我意识/任务级奖励来解决这些问题是未来工作的重要方向。Several limitations remain. First, SAS learns from a teacher-forced self-aware reward, i.e., the frozen denoiser’s pathwise likelihood on ground-truth trajectories. This creates a potential gap between training and free-running inference. Second, our current policies are trained largely on a per-task basis, so cross-task and cross-domain transfer are promising but not yet fully characterized. Third, while SAS is plug-and-play with respect to the frozen backbone at inference time, learning the order policy still requires additional training compute, and the policy introduces a small but non-zero inference overhead. Addressing these issues through better transfer, reduced teacher-forcing mismatch, and hybrid self-aware/task-level rewards is an important direction for future work.

这些结果表明，直接将高维骨干表示暴露给顺序 政策可能会适得其反。尽管隐藏状态包含丰富的语义信息，但它们也 涉及许多因素，例如标记身份、句法结构和语义内容，这些因素可能会 与解码顺序决策不直接相关。这可能会使隐藏状态策略更难实施 优化且稳定性较差。相反，明确构建的置信度和不确定性特征，例如 作为顶级令牌概率、余量和熵，提供更直接、可解释和稳定的信号 用于决定接下来要显示的位置，同时保持轻量级和与模型无关。我们因此 将隐藏状态变体视为探索性设计研究，而不是所有结果的负面结果 基于代表性的政策。选择基于特征的策略并不是因为设计空间是 筋疲力尽，但因为它是我们发现的最稳定、最轻量且经验可靠的选择。These results suggest that directly exposing high-dimensional backbone representations to the order policy can be counterproductive. Although hidden states contain rich semantic information, they also entangle many factors—such as token identity, syntactic structure, and semantic content—that may not be directly relevant to the decode-order decision. This can make the hidden-state policy harder to optimize and less stable. In contrast, explicitly constructed confidence and uncertainty features, such as top-token probability, margin, and entropy, provide a more direct, interpretable, and stable signal for deciding which position to reveal next, while remaining lightweight and model-agnostic. We therefore view the hidden-state variant as an exploratory design study rather than a negative result about all representation-based policies. The feature-based policy is chosen not because the design space is exhausted, but because it is the most stable, lightweight, and empirically reliable option we found.
