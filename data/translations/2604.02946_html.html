通过基于来源的输入梯度指导从合成数据中学习Learning from Synthetic Data via Provenance-Based Input Gradient Guidance

Koshiro Nagano1  Ryo Fujii1  Ryo Hachiuma2  Fumiaki Sato3  Taiki Sekii3,†  Hideo Saito1
1Keio University  2Independent Researcher  3CyberAgent

摘要Abstract

使用合成数据的学习方法作为一种增加训练数据多样性、同时降低收集成本、从而提高模型判别鲁棒性的有效方法而引起了人们的关注。 然而，许多现有方法仅通过训练样本的多样化间接提高鲁棒性，并且没有明确教导模型输入空间中的哪些区域真正有助于区分；因此，该模型可能会学习由合成偏差和伪影引起的虚假相关性。 受此限制的启发，本文提出了一种学习框架，该框架使用训练数据合成过程中获得的来源信息（指示输入空间中的每个区域是否源自目标对象）作为辅助监督信号，以促进获取针对目标区域的表示。 具体来说，在合成过程中根据目标区域和非目标区域的信息对输入梯度进行分解，并引入输入梯度引导来抑制非目标区域上的梯度。 这抑制了模型对非目标区域的依赖，并直接促进了目标区域判别性表示的学习。实验证明了所提出的方法在多个任务和模式中的有效性和通用性，包括弱监督对象定位、时空动作定位和图像分类。Learning methods using synthetic data have attracted attention as an effective approach for increasing the diversity of training data while reducing collection costs, thereby improving the robustness of model discrimination. However, many existing methods improve robustness only indirectly through the diversification of training samples and do not explicitly teach the model which regions in the input space truly contribute to discrimination; consequently, the model may learn spurious correlations caused by synthesis biases and artifacts. Motivated by this limitation, this paper proposes a learning framework that uses provenance information obtained during the training data synthesis process, indicating whether each region in the input space originates from the target object, as an auxiliary supervisory signal to promote the acquisition of representations focused on target regions. Specifically, input gradients are decomposed based on information about target and non-target regions during synthesis, and input gradient guidance is introduced to suppress gradients over non-target regions. This suppresses the model’s reliance on non-target regions and directly promotes the learning of discriminative representations for target regions. Experiments demonstrate the effectiveness and generality of the proposed method across multiple tasks and modalities, including weakly supervised object localization, spatio-temporal action localization, and image classification.

1 简介Introduction

深度学习不仅成为图像分类和目标检测等计算机视觉任务的关键技术Deep learning has become a key technology not only for computer vision tasks such as image classification and object detection[24, 25]，而且还跨越许多其他领域，包括自然语言处理, but also across many other domains, including natural language processing[9, 5]和语音识别and speech recognition[3, 1]。除了深度神经网络 (DNN) 架构的进步之外，这一进步还受到训练计算环境改进的推动，这使得能够对大规模数据集进行预训练。如此大规模的训练赋予了 DNN 模型以前无法达到的泛化水平，从而能够获得较少依赖于任何特定数据集的鲁棒表示。 然而，在实践中，由于数据收集和注释的人力成本以及硬件采购的财务成本，构建充分涵盖现实应用中遇到的复杂多样条件的数据集仍然具有挑战性。当训练数据多样性有限时，训练期间输入数据的分布与部署期间遇到的输入数据分布之间会出现差距。例如，这种分布差距是由背景、照明和视点的变化以及对象之间的共现关系引起的，并且它降低了模型所学习的表示的泛化性。这个问题在需要高水平的通用性和鲁棒性的应用中尤其严重，例如机器人和自主代理。. Beyond advances in deep neural network (DNN) architectures, this progress has been driven by improvements in computational environments for training, which have enabled pretraining on large-scale datasets. Such large-scale training endows DNN models with a level of generalization that was previously unattainable, making it possible to acquire robust representations that are less dependent on any specific dataset. In practice, however, constructing datasets that sufficiently cover the complex and diverse conditions encountered in real-world applications remains challenging owing to the human cost of data collection and annotation and the financial cost of hardware procurement. When training data diversity is limited, a gap arises between the distribution of input data during training and that encountered during deployment. This distribution gap is caused, for example, by changes in background, illumination, and viewpoint, as well as co-occurrence relationships among objects, and it degrades the generalizability of the representations learned by the model. This problem is particularly serious in applications demanding high levels of generalizability and robustness, such as robotics and autonomous agents.

虚假相关性是指模型错误地学习了与目标对象无关的明显相关性或偏差，例如对象之间的背景或共现关系，而不是学习目标对象的真实特征。例如，当经常将特定背景与目标对象一起观察时，模型可能会错误地将背景与目标对象特征关联起来Spurious correlations refer to cases where a model mistakenly learns apparent correlations or biases unrelated to the target object, such as background or co-occurrence relationships among objects, instead of learning the true characteristics of the target object. For example, when a particular background is frequently observed together with the target object, the model may incorrectly associate the background with target-object features[26]。 这种识别鲁棒性的下降在域转移下变得尤其明显. Such degradation in recognition robustness becomes particularly pronounced under domain shift[14]。 还值得注意的是，在视频识别的弱监督学习中. It has also been noted that, in weakly supervised learning for video recognition[34]，学习往往依赖于对象部分或背景而不是目标动作。 已经提出了各种方法来抑制这个问题。其中包括调整最后一层以减少非鲁棒特征的贡献的方法, learning tends to rely on object parts or background rather than the target action. Various methods have been proposed to suppress this problem. However, these methods are limited in applicability because they require manual annotations, such as auxiliary labels related to attributes, capture environments, and gaze regions, and impose architectural constraints on DNNs.

2.2 使用合成数据学习Learning with Synthetic Data

近年来，人们积极研究通过训练数据增强来抑制虚假相关性的综合学习方法。先前的工作可以大致分为三种方法：使用模拟器、数据增强和生成模型，下面对每种方法进行描述。Synthetic learning methods that suppress spurious correlations through training data augmentation have been actively studied in recent years. Prior work can be broadly categorized into three approaches: using simulators, data augmentation, and generative models, each of which is described below.

2.2.1 模拟器的使用Use of Simulators

基于模拟器的综合学习方法已被广泛研究，用于在各种软件控制条件下构建大规模数据集。代表性例子包括使用 GTA-V 综合城市驾驶环境数据的研究Synthetic learning methods based on simulators have been widely studied for constructing large-scale datasets under a variety of software-controlled conditions. Representative examples include studies that synthesized urban driving-environment data using GTA-V[27]以及再现不同人类行为和姿势的研究and studies that reproduced diverse human actions and poses[41]。虽然这些研究可以自动获得详细的注释，但一个关键的挑战是开发模拟器的人力成本。 此外，由于合成数据相对于自然图像表现出域差距，因此在实践中通常需要域适应. While these studies can automatically obtain detailed annotations, a key challenge is the human cost of developing the simulator. Furthermore, because synthetic data exhibit a domain gap relative to natural images, domain adaptation is often required in practice[39].

2.2.2 数据增强Data Augmentation

为了避免模拟器开发成本和域间隙问题，许多研究已经探索从真实图像中合成看不见的数据。除了基于几何和光度变换的经典方法之外To avoid both the simulator development cost and the domain gap problem, many studies have explored synthesizing unseen data from real images. In addition to classical methods based on geometric and photometric transformations[21, 16]，已经提出了通过随机屏蔽分割图像块来提高鲁棒性的方法, methods have been proposed that improve robustness by randomly masking divided image patches[36]并通过混合多个图像和标签and by mixing multiple images and labels[46, 45]。然而，这些方法的一个基本限制是它们无法合成超出原始训练样本分布的多样性数据，例如捕获环境或目标物体外观的变化。. A fundamental limitation of these approaches, however, is that they cannot synthesize data with diversity beyond the distribution of the original training samples, such as variations in capture environments or target-object appearance.

2.2.3 图像生成模型的使用Use of Image Generation Models

近年来，图像生成模型极大地推进了综合学习方法Image generation models have greatly advanced synthetic learning methods in recent years[10, 32, 11]。特别是，能够生成高保真图像的扩散模型可以精确控制背景和前景物体的类型和外观以及相机位置等属性。例如，稳定扩散. In particular, diffusion models capable of generating high-fidelity images can precisely control attributes such as the types and appearances of background and foreground objects and camera position. For example, Stable Diffusion[28]可以通过文字提示编辑真实图像中的特定区域，使真实图像的某些部分转化为实际不存在的逼真元素。基于这种能力，人们提出了学习方法，生成具有不同外观的任务相关图像并将其纳入训练中can edit specific regions in a real image via text prompts, enabling parts of a real image to be transformed into photorealistic elements that do not actually exist. Based on this capability, learning methods have been proposed that generate task-relevant images with diverse appearances and incorporate them into training[10]。与模拟器或数据增强相比，这些方法可以更自然地表达合成区域，再现更接近真实环境的数据分布，从而提高模型的鲁棒性。然而，由于图像生成模型仍然可以产生合成伪影，因此模型准确性不一定会随着数据量而变化. Compared with simulators or data augmentation, these methods can express synthetic regions more naturally, reproducing data distributions closer to real environments and thereby improving model robustness. However, because image generation models can still produce synthetic artifacts, model accuracy does not necessarily scale with data volume[10].

上述现有工作侧重于训练样本的多样化，而模型识别精度的提高只是作为训练数据增加的副作用而间接提高的。相比之下，所提出的方法使用合成过程中的目标区域信息作为监督信号来直接抑制虚假相关性，包括与真实图像中的目标对象无关的背景和对象共现，以及合成过程中引入的偏差和伪影。The prior work described above focuses on diversifying training samples, and model recognition accuracy improves only indirectly as a side effect of increased training data. In contrast, the proposed method uses target region information from the synthetic process as a supervisory signal to directly suppress spurious correlations, including backgrounds and object co-occurrences unrelated to the target object in real images, as well as biases and artifacts introduced during synthesis.

5 结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种合成数据的学习框架，该框架使用合成过程中获得的输入空间中的来源信息作为辅助监督信号，以促进针对目标区域的表示的获取。通过引入输入梯度引导，根据目标和非目标区域分离输入梯度并抑制非目标区域上的梯度，防止模型输出依赖于非目标区域，例如背景和合成伪影，使模型能够直接学习目标区域的判别表示。跨多个任务和模式的评估，包括弱监督对象定位、弱监督时空动作定位和图像分类，证实了该方法的有效性和通用性。This paper proposed a learning framework for synthetic data that uses provenance information in the input space obtained during the synthetic process as an auxiliary supervisory signal to promote the acquisition of representations focused on target regions. By introducing input gradient guidance that separates input gradients based on target and non-target regions and suppresses gradients over non-target regions, the model output is prevented from depending on non-target regions, such as background and synthetic artifacts, enabling the model to directly learn discriminative representations of target regions. Evaluations across multiple tasks and modalities, including weakly supervised object localization, weakly supervised spatio-temporal action localization, and image classification, confirmed the effectiveness and generality of the proposed method.
