摘要Abstract

这篇从研究到实践的完整论文介绍了评估学生团队桌面练习（TTX）的方法。 TTX 使学习者团队能够为工作场所任务做好准备并练习危机响应，例如解决网络安全事件。虽然评估对于确定团队实现学习目标的程度至关重要，但 TTX 的复杂性和开放性往往会导致反馈延迟或不完整。 TTX学习平台可以记录团队的行动和沟通；然而，利用这些数据来评估绩效的探索尚未充分。为了解决这一差距，我们使用来自两个国家 81 名参与者的原始数据集比较了两种 TTX 后团队评估方法——聚类和大型语言模型 (LLM)。我们根据标准化评分标准根据教师分配的分数评估了这些方法。对处理 TTX 任务的团队进行聚类，使讲师能够向集群内的团队提供更快、更有针对性的反馈。该方法有效可靠，计算量要求低。法学硕士使用标准化的评估标准来评估团队的沟通。虽然 GPT-4o 经常与教师的分数不一致，但 GPT-5.2 的错误率要低得多。研究方法已集成到开源 TTX 学习平台 INJECT 中，以支持可扩展性和教学实践。为了鼓励社区采用，我们公开共享所有数据集、软件工具和成熟的 TTX 场景。This full paper in the research-to-practice track presents methods for assessing student teams in tabletop exercises (TTXs). TTXs enable learner teams to prepare for workplace tasks and practice crisis responses, such as resolving cybersecurity incidents. While assessment is essential for determining how well teams achieve learning objectives, the complex, open-ended nature of TTXs often leads to delayed or incomplete feedback. TTX learning platforms can record teams’ actions and communication; yet, leveraging these data to assess performance is underexplored. To address this gap, we compared two post-TTX team assessment methods—clustering and large language models (LLMs)—using an original dataset from 81 participants across two countries. We evaluated these methods against instructor-assigned scores based on standardized rubrics. Clustering grouped teams that approached TTX tasks similarly, enabling instructors to deliver faster, targeted feedback to teams within a cluster. This method was valid and reliable, with low computational requirements. LLMs used the standardized rubrics to assess teams’ communication. While GPT-4o frequently disagreed with instructor scores, GPT-5.2 demonstrated considerably lower error. The researched methods have been integrated into INJECT, an open-source TTX learning platform, to support scalability and teaching practice. To encourage community adoption, we publicly share all datasets, software tools, and a full-fledged TTX scenario.

V-B1 聚类Clustering

聚类通过将数据压缩为二维，同时保留特征之间的关系，对类似的团队进行分组。鉴于团队数量相对较少（我们的数据中为 13 和 10 个），预计会获得很少的集群。我们观察了三到四个集群，每个集群都突出了不同的团队行为和活动模式。Clustering grouped similar teams by compressing their data into two dimensions while preserving the relationships among features. Given the relatively small number of teams (13 and 10 in our data), it is expected to obtain few clusters. We observed from three to four clusters, each highlighting different patterns of team behavior and activity.

In TTXs centered around a single incident (like in our study), these features provided relevant information for clustering. However, they may be less informative if the task completion order is more arbitrary.

最后，我们注意到聚类的局限性。从电子邮件文本中派生的特征（例如常用词和 n 元语法）对于区分团队没有用处。许多短语（主要是与网络安全流程相关的短语）在大多数团队中都是相同的，从而形成一个集群。Finally, we note a limitation of clustering. Features derived from email texts, such as common words and n-grams, were not useful for distinguishing between the teams. Many phrases – mainly those related to cybersecurity processes – were equivalent across most teams, resulting in a single cluster.

维-DV-D 对教育研究和实践的影响Implications for Educational Research and Practice

在 TTX 中，学生群体规模受到限制（数十个团队），但手动评估仍然缓慢且不足。此外，正如我们来自真实教室的数据集所证明的那样，团队的规模可能不同并且来自不同的背景，这一事实使情况变得复杂。根据我们的研究和教学经验，我们提供建议，为教育工作者提供信息并帮助改进 TTX 评估实践。In TTXs, student cohort sizes are constrained (tens of teams), but manual assessment remains slow and insufficient. Furthermore, it is complicated by the fact that teams can vary in size and come from diverse backgrounds, as evidenced by our dataset from real classrooms. Based on our study and teaching experience, we provide recommendations to inform educators and help improve TTX assessment practices.

维-DV-D1 设计 TTX 时考虑评估Design TTXs With Assessment in Mind

为了使自动评估发挥作用，TTX 学习目标和活动的彻底设计至关重要。否则，日志数据在捕获学习过程时可能不太准确。 TTX 创建者应在设计阶段考虑评估目标，以便在 TTX 后阶段实现更高质量的分析。For automated assessment to work, a thorough design of TTX learning objectives and activities is crucial. Otherwise, the log data may be less accurate in capturing the learning process. TTX creators should consider assessment goals during the design phase to enable higher-quality analytics in the post-TTX phase.

维-DV-D3 谨慎对待 TTX 中基于 LLM 的评估Be Cautious About LLM-based Assessment in TTXs

尽管专业沟通是 TTX 中的一项重要技能，但通用法学硕士有时无法识别该特定领域上下文中的细微差别，即使在提示中的标准化标题的指导下也是如此。Although professional communication is an important skill in TTXs, a general-purpose LLM sometimes failed to recognize nuances in this domain-specific context, even when guided by a standardized rubric in the prompt.

维-DV-D4 考虑基于法学硕士的评估的道德影响Consider Ethical Implications of LLM-based Assessment

与所有自动化决策一样，输出必须由人工指导员审核，以确保责任和公平。我们的聚类仅考虑团队的活动日志，而不考虑任何外部因素，这与法学硕士等（可能有偏见的）黑匣子不同。此外，由于集群在本地运行，学习者的数据不会与外部服务共享，从而保护了隐私。As with all automated decisions, the output must be reviewed by a human instructor to ensure accountability and fairness. Our clustering considers only teams’ activity logs, not any external factors, unlike a (potentially biased) black box like an LLM. Moreover, since clustering runs locally, learners’ data are not shared with an external service, preserving privacy.

维-DV-D5 将原则推广到其他团队学习场景Generalize the Principles to Other Team Learning Scenarios

我们的评估方法不仅适用于 TTX，还可以适应相关的计算机教育环境。其中包括夺旗 (CTF)Our assessment methods are applicable not only in TTXs but can also be adapted to related computing education contexts. These include Capture the Flag (CTF)[33, 5]、网络防御演习（CDX）以及网络安全领域的其他复杂的协作解决问题演习，例如在网络靶场中, Cyber Defense Exercise (CDX), and other complex collaborative problem-solving exercises in cybersecurity, e.g., in cyber ranges[31, 58, 13]及以上and beyond[60]。尽管这两种情况都不与 TTX 完全相似，但它们之间的相关性在于它们向学生提供的信息有限，并且可能涉及开放式解决方案，从而使评估变得复杂。. Although neither of these contexts is entirely similar to TTX, they are related in that they provide limited information to the student and may involve open-ended solutions, complicating assessment.

VI 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and Future Work

这项研究评估了对实践网络安全事件解决的学生团队的自动评估。虽然聚类增强了评估，但法学硕士在这项任务中效率较低。尽管我们进行了修改并包含了任务上下文，但即使是相对较新的模型也很难使用结构化提示来评估团队的文本。此外，法学硕士不透明的内部机制限制了我们诊断根本原因的能力。这些发现强调，尽管人们对法学硕士的热情日益高涨，但它们的适用性可能在某些特定领域的场景中受到限制。如果法学硕士用于评估，则需要仔细审查其输出。This study evaluated the automated assessment of student teams who practiced cybersecurity incident resolution. While clustering enhanced assessment, LLMs were less effective in this task. Even a relatively recent model struggled to assess the teams’ texts using a structured prompt, despite our revisions and inclusion of task context. Moreover, the LLM’s opaque internal mechanisms limited our ability to diagnose the root cause. These findings highlight that, despite growing enthusiasm around LLMs, their applicability may be limited in certain domain-specific scenarios. Should LLMs be used for assessment, their outputs need to be carefully reviewed.

接下来，这项研究贡献了聚类框架，以推进 TTX 评估的最新技术水平。我们在一个独特的数据集上评估了我们的方法，该数据集包含来自两个国家的两个练习和团队，从而支持研究结果的普遍性。尽管 TTX 的规模看起来相对较小（数十个团队），但使用这种教学方法的教师工作量很高，限制了手动评估。我们的研究确定并证明了 TTX 文献中的这一差距，并提供了基于课堂的证据，证明聚类在支持团队评估方面的适用性。Next, this study contributed the clustering framework to advance the state of the art of assessment in TTXs. We evaluated our methods on a unique dataset comprising two exercises and teams from two countries, thereby supporting the generalizability of the findings. Even though the scale of TTXs may seem relatively small (tens of teams), the instructor workload using this teaching method is high, limiting manual assessment. Our study identified and demonstrated this gap in the TTX literature and provided classroom-based evidence of clustering’s applicability in supporting team assessment.
