I 简介Introduction

现代基础设施中自治系统的普遍存在（The ubiquity of autonomous systems in modern infrastructure (例如e.g.、机器人操纵器、自动驾驶车辆、无人机）极大地增强了对鲁棒闭环控制策略的需求。传统上，控制理论依赖于基于模型的显式设计来提供稳定性和安全性的理论保证。然而，随着系统动力学变得越来越难以分析表征，对基于学习的方法的依赖自然增加, robotic manipulators, autonomous vehicles, UAVs) has highly intensified the demand for robust, closed-loop control strategies. Traditionally, control theory has relied on explicit model-based designs to provide theoretical guarantees of stability and safety. However, with system dynamics becoming progressively more difficult to characterize analytically, reliance on learning-based methodologies has naturally increased[1].

尽管当今的计算能力很强，但弥合基于学习的控制综合的灵活性和形式验证的严格性之间的差距仍然是一个核心的开放问题。虽然标准的无模型学习方法在模拟环境中展示了令人印象深刻的能力，但对其最坏情况行为缺乏严格的正式保证，给物理部署带来了不容忽视的风险Despite the computational power available today, bridging the gap between the flexibility of learning-based control synthesis and the rigor of formal verification remains a central open problem. While standard model-free learning methods demonstrate impressive capabilities in simulation environments, the lack of strict formal guarantees regarding their worst-case behavior introduces risks for physical deployment that cannot be ignored[3]。本文的重点是弥合这一差距，实现自动合成经过形式验证以满足系统规范的控制策略。. This paper focuses on bridging this gap, enabling the automatic synthesis of control strategies that are formally verified to satisfy the system’s specifications.

七VII局限性Limitations

当前 SMC-ES 实现的主要限制在于与分布式参数同步相关的通信开销。正如原始 OpenAI-ES 公式中所述A primary limitation of the current SMC-ES implementation lies in the communication overhead associated with distributed parameter synchronization. As noted in the original OpenAI-ES formulation[37]，在分布式工作人员之间广播大型神经网络权重会造成巨大的网络负担。在提出的 SMC-ES 框架中，由于需要同时评估多个场景的政策，这一瓶颈被进一步放大。, broadcasting large neural network weights across distributed workers imposes a significant network burden. In the proposed SMC-ES framework, this bottleneck is further amplified by the necessity of evaluating policies across multiple scenarios simultaneously.

为了根据经验量化这种计算开销，我们使用 SafetyHalfCheetahVelocity 环境进行了扩展分析。由于此任务计算量轻，因此它可以作为我们的分布式架构的压力测试，在扩展到 128、256、512 和 1024 个 CPU 核心时积极暴露通信瓶颈。总执行时间分别为 4.05、2.41、1.36 和 1.02 小时。虽然绝对执行时间随着资源的增加而单调减少，但扩展表现出严重的收益递减。具体来说，并行效率的急剧下降（在从 512 核过渡到 1024 核时最为明显）表明存在严格的通信到计算瓶颈。To empirically quantify this computational overhead, we conducted a scaling analysis using the SafetyHalfCheetahVelocity environment. Because this task is computationally lightweight, it serves as a stress test for our distributed architecture, aggressively exposing the communication bottleneck when scaling across 128, 256, 512, and 1024 CPU cores. The total execution times yielded were 4.05, 2.41, 1.36, and 1.02 hours, respectively. While the absolute execution time monotonically decreases with added resources, the scaling exhibits severe diminishing returns. Specifically, the sharp drop in parallel efficiency, most notable when transitioning from 512 to 1024 cores, demonstrates a strict communication-to-computation bottleneck.

然而，值得注意的是，这种通信瓶颈是特定于实现的硬件挑战，而不是基本的算法缺陷。通过采用先进的分布式计算策略（例如共享随机种子方法）可以大大减少这种开销However, it is important to note that this communication bottleneck is an implementation-specific hardware challenge rather than a fundamental algorithmic flaw. This overhead can be drastically reduced by employing advanced distributed computing strategies, such as the shared random seed method[37]，它允许工作人员在本地重建参数扰动，而无需通过网络传输完整的权重矩阵。实现此类优化留待将来的工作，因为本文的主要目标是建立 SMC-ES 方法的理论和经验可行性。 除了模拟之外，我们还打算研究通过 SMC-ES 训练的策略从模拟到真实的可转移性，以及算法直接在物理硬件上进行连续微调的可行性。, which allows workers to reconstruct parameter perturbations locally without transmitting full weight matrices over the network. Implementing such optimizations is left for future work, as the primary objective of this paper is to establish the theoretical and empirical feasibility of the SMC-ES approach. Moving beyond simulation, we intend to investigate the sim-to-real transferability of policies trained via SMC-ES, as well as the algorithm’s viability for continuous fine-tuning directly on physical hardware.
