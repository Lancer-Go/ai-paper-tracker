RiskFlow：快速、忠实地生成安全关键型交通场景RiskFlow: Fast and Faithful Safety-Critical Traffic Scenario Generation

Qi Lan1, Yining Tang1, Yu Shen1, Yi Zhou1, Yuhao Wei1, Jie Li1\corresponding, Guofa Li1\corresponding
摘要Abstract

安全关键的交通场景生成对于评估罕见但高风险交互下的自动驾驶系统至关重要。现有的基于扩散的方法在闭环生成中提供了很强的可控性，但其迭代去噪过程的计算成本很高，并且可能会在长时间的推出过程中积累采样和引导误差，从而导致不切实际的运动伪影，例如抖动、异常加速和越野行为。为了解决这些问题，我们建议Safety-critical traffic scenario generation is essential for evaluating autonomous driving systems under rare but high-risk interactions. Existing diffusion-based methods offer strong controllability in closed-loop generation, but their iterative denoising process is computationally expensive and may accumulate sampling and guidance errors over long rollouts, causing unrealistic motion artifacts such as jitter, abnormal acceleration, and off-road behavior. To address these issues, we propose风险流RiskFlow，一个闭环安全关键型多智能体流量生成框架，它将未来的轨迹生成制定为行动空间中的运输。 RiskFlow 不依赖迭代降噪，而是在有限间隔内学习平均速度场，通过一次前向传递将高斯动作序列转换为未来的加速度和偏航率命令，使用基于 JVP 的目标进行高效稳定的训练。在测试时，RiskFlow 将输出空间指导应用于生成的动作，引导选定的关键代理进行危险交互，同时规范越野行为，并通过车辆动力学重建物理上可行的轨迹。使用 tbsim 闭环评估在 nuScenes 上进行的实验表明，RiskFlow 在多智能体和长视野设置中实现了强大的对抗性与现实主义权衡。与代表性基线相比，RiskFlow 不断提高真实性，同时保持具有竞争力的安全关键生成能力，并大幅缩短评估的推理时间。, a closed-loop safety-critical multi-agent traffic generation framework that formulates future trajectory generation as transport in the action space. Instead of relying on iterative denoising, RiskFlow learns an average velocity field over a finite interval to transform Gaussian action sequences into future acceleration and yaw-rate commands with a single forward pass, using a JVP-based objective for efficient and stable training. At test time, RiskFlow applies output-space guidance to the generated actions, steering selected critical agents toward risky interactions while regularizing off-road behavior, and reconstructs physically feasible trajectories through vehicle dynamics. Experiments on nuScenes with tbsim closed-loop evaluation show that RiskFlow achieves a strong adversariality-realism trade-off across multi-agent and long-horizon settings. Compared with representative baselines, RiskFlow consistently improves realism while maintaining competitive safety-critical generation capability, and substantially reduces inference time for evaluation.

简介Introduction

自动驾驶系统不仅应该在普通交通流中进行评估，还应该在碰撞、强行切入、紧急制动和多车并道等罕见的安全关键情况下进行评估。从真实道路收集此类事件成本高昂且效率低下，因为自然驾驶日志中的碰撞和严重安全后果很少，并且需要大量里程才能大规模观察Autonomous driving systems should be evaluated not only in common traffic flows, but also in rare safety-critical situations such as collisions, forced cut-ins, emergency braking, and multi-vehicle merging. Collecting such events from real roads is costly and inefficient, since crashes and severe safety outcomes are sparse in natural driving logs and require enormous mileage to observe at scale(Kalra and Paddock 2016)。因此，闭环安全关键场景生成已成为在可控且可重复的高风险交互下对自动驾驶堆栈进行压力测试的重要工具. Closed-loop safety-critical scenario generation has therefore become an important tool for stress-testing autonomous driving stacks under controllable and repeatable high-risk interactions(Ding et al. 2023)。关键挑战是平衡. The key challenge is to balance对抗性adversariality和and现实主义realism：生成的场景应引发具有挑战性的交互，同时尊重道路拓扑、交通语义、车辆动力学和多智能体行为。否则，高碰撞率可能来自不切实际的跳跃、过度加速或越野伪像，对实际评估几乎没有价值。: generated scenarios should induce challenging interactions while respecting road topology, traffic semantics, vehicle dynamics, and multi-agent behavior. Otherwise, high collision rates may come from unrealistic jumps, excessive acceleration, or off-road artifacts, offering little value for real evaluation.

自然交通模拟通过从 nuScenes 等大规模数据集中学习交互行为模型来提高闭环真实感Naturalistic traffic simulation improves closed-loop realism by learning interactive behavior models from large-scale datasets such as nuScenes(Caesar et al. 2020)。 SimNet、TrafficSim 和 BITS 等方法推出了数据驱动的多智能体行为，这些行为对不断变化的场景做出反应，而不是重放固定的轨迹. Methods such as SimNet, TrafficSim, and BITS roll out data-driven multi-agent behaviors that react to the evolving scene rather than replaying fixed trajectories(Bergamini et al. 2021; Suo et al. 2021; Xu et al. 2023)。然而，这些模拟器主要再现普通的交通动态和长期稳定性，而不是故意诱发对抗事件。因此，扩散模型在可控场景生成中变得很流行，例如场景扩散等方法. However, these simulators mainly reproduce ordinary traffic dynamics and long-horizon stability, rather than deliberately inducing adversarial events. Diffusion models have thus become popular for controllable scenario generation, with methods such as Scenario Diffusion(Pronovost et al. 2023),CTG, CTG(Zhong et al. 2023b), CTG++ (Zhong et al. 2023a), 安全SIM卡, SAFE-SIM(Chang et al. 2024), 和 CCDiff, and CCDiff(Lin et al. 2025)利用其多模式生成能力和灵活的测试时间指导，在安全关键目标下生成不同的交通场景。leveraging their multi-modal generative capacity and flexible test-time guidance to generate diverse traffic scenes under safety-critical objectives.

尽管基于扩散的方法很有效，但它有两个主要局限性。首先，DDPM式模型需要多步噪声和去噪Despite their effectiveness, diffusion-based methods have two key limitations. First, DDPM-style models require multi-step noising and denoising(Ho et al. 2020)，必须在闭环部署的每个计划周期重复执行。因此，采样和引导误差会通过车辆动力学累积，特别是在强碰撞引导下，导致轨迹抖动、异常加速度、变化的加加速度分布或越野行为。其次，扩散采样很慢：即使是加速采样器，例如 DDIM, which must be repeated at every planning cycle in closed-loop rollout. Sampling and guidance errors can therefore accumulate through vehicle dynamics, especially under strong collision guidance, leading to trajectory jitter, abnormal acceleration, shifted jerk distributions, or off-road behavior. Second, diffusion sampling is slow: even accelerated samplers such as DDIM(Song et al. 2020)在许多场景的闭环部署过程中仍然需要重复的模型评估。因此，安全关键型生成需要一种能够减少累积采样误差和推理成本的机制。still require repeated model evaluations during closed-loop rollout across many scenes. Safety-critical generation therefore needs a mechanism that reduces both accumulated sampling error and inference cost.

受这一观察的启发，我们建议Motivated by this observation, we propose风险流RiskFlow，一个基于流的框架，用于闭环安全关键流量生成。给定初始交通状态、地图上下文和多智能体历史记录，RiskFlow 从随机动作开始，并通过动作空间中的单个 MeanFlow 前向传递生成未来的动作序列，然后使用车辆运动学将它们解码为轨迹。为了引发安全关键事件，RiskFlow 将测试时指导直接应用于输出操作，引导关键代理进行危险交互，同时通过地图感知正则化阻止越野行为。通过指导最终动作而不是反复纠正中间去噪状态，RiskFlow 可以更直接地调整关键行为，并减少累积采样和引导误差造成的轨迹失真。, a flow-based framework for closed-loop safety-critical traffic generation. Given the initial traffic state, map context, and multi-agent history, RiskFlow starts from random actions and generates future action sequences with a single MeanFlow forward pass in action space, then decodes them into trajectories using vehicle kinematics. To induce safety-critical events, RiskFlow applies test-time guidance directly to the output actions, steering key agents toward risky interactions while discouraging off-road behavior via map-aware regularization. By guiding final actions rather than repeatedly correcting intermediate denoising states, RiskFlow can adjust key behaviors more directly and reduce trajectory distortion from accumulated sampling and guidance errors.

相关工作Related Work

交通行为建模和闭环仿真。Traffic behavior modeling and closed-loop simulation.

现实的交通行为建模支撑闭环自动驾驶评估，其中生成的代理必须对不断变化的场景做出反应，而不是重放记录的轨迹。基于学习的模拟器从互补的角度解决了这个问题：SimNet 模拟对自我行为的反应，TrafficSim 学习社会一致的联合多代理策略，BITS 通过分层意图和行为建模提高推出稳定性和多样性Realistic traffic behavior modeling underpins closed-loop autonomous-driving evaluation, where generated agents must react to evolving scenes rather than replay logged trajectories. Learning-based simulators address this from complementary angles: SimNet models reactive responses to ego actions, TrafficSim learns socially consistent joint multi-agent policies, and BITS improves rollout stability and diversity via hierarchical intent and behavior modeling(Bergamini et al. 2021; Suo et al. 2021; Xu et al. 2023)。同时，轨迹预测模型如 Scene Transformer. In parallel, trajectory prediction models such as Scene Transformer(Ngiam et al. 2021), QC网, QCNet(Zhou et al. 2023)，和路形者, and Wayformer(Nayakanti et al. 2023)为代理历史、地图上下文和交互提供强大的编码器。因果预测提高了分布变化下的稳健性provide strong encoders for agent histories, map context, and interactions. Causal forecasting improves robustness under distribution shift(Liu et al. 2022)，而反事实和基于干预的预测减少了虚假的社会或环境相关性, while counterfactual and intervention-based prediction reduce spurious social or environmental correlations(Chen et al. 2021; Ge et al. 2023)。然而，这些工作主要用作现实先验、交互编码器或开环预测器，并没有直接为可控闭环安全关键生成提供有效的机制，从而在保持道路可行性和物理真实性的同时诱导风险交互。. However, these works mainly serve as realism priors, interaction encoders, or open-loop predictors, and do not directly offer an efficient mechanism for controllable closed-loop safety-critical generation that induces risky interactions while preserving road feasibility and physical realism.

Despite these advances, adversariality still depends on iterative denoising and repeated guidance across sampling steps or planning cycles, increasing inference cost and potentially amplifying guidance errors over long rollouts.

结论Conclusion

我们介绍We introduce风险流RiskFlow，用于生成安全关键交通场景的闭环框架。 RiskFlow 不依赖迭代降噪，而是学习通过单个 MeanFlow 前向传递将高斯动作序列传输到未来的加速度和偏航率序列，并将测试时指导直接应用于生成的动作残差。与基于 TTC 的关键代理选择、车辆动力学推出和地图感知正则化相结合，该设计能够实现局部对抗性干预，同时保持道路可行性和物理合理性。 nuScenes 闭环仿真实验表明，RiskFlow 在多智能体和长视野设置中实现了有利的对抗性与现实性权衡：它保持有竞争力的安全关键发电能力，同时在更强的控制下持续提高现实性。 RiskFlow 还大幅提高了推理效率，通过用单遍动作生成代替重复去噪，降低了大规模闭环评估的成本。消融研究进一步表明，动作空间流的生成提高了轨迹的真实性，而地图引导则稳定了道路限制下的对抗性干预。总体而言，RiskFlow 提供了一种高效且基于物理基础的替代方案，用于在闭环自动驾驶评估中生成现实的安全关键交通场景。, a closed-loop framework for safety-critical traffic scenario generation. Instead of relying on iterative denoising, RiskFlow learns to transport Gaussian action sequences to future acceleration and yaw-rate sequences with a single MeanFlow forward pass, and applies test-time guidance directly to the generated action residuals. Together with TTC-based critical-agent selection, vehicle-dynamics rollout, and map-aware regularization, this design enables localized adversarial interventions while preserving road feasibility and physical plausibility. Experiments on nuScenes closed-loop simulation demonstrate that RiskFlow achieves a favorable adversariality-realism trade-off across both multi-agent and long-horizon settings: it maintains competitive safety-critical generation capability while consistently improving realism under stronger control. RiskFlow also substantially improves inference efficiency, reducing the cost of large-scale closed-loop evaluation by replacing repeated denoising with single-pass action generation. Ablation studies further show that action-space flow generation improves trajectory realism, while map guidance stabilizes adversarial interventions under road constraints. Overall, RiskFlow provides an efficient and physically grounded alternative for generating realistic safety-critical traffic scenarios in closed-loop autonomous-driving evaluation.
