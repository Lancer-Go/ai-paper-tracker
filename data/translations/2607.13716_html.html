CAVA：规范操作验证和证明CAVA: Canonical Action Verification and Attestation
用于代理人工智能系统的运行时治理for Runtime Governance of Agentic AI Systems

Zexun Wang
Ond Holdings Inc Correspondence: jw@nd.im
（2026 年 6 月）(June 2026)
摘要Abstract

代理人工智能系统越来越多地通过异构运行时进行操作：本地编码挂钩、SDK 工具、浏览器自动化、托管代理跟踪、API 网关和工作流引擎。因此，单个操作行为（例如发布代码、更改身份状态、转移资金或导出数据）可能由许多不兼容的运行时记录表示。这使得一个基本的治理问题很难回答：什么操作实际上被批准了，什么证据将批准与执行绑定在一起，以及独立验证者以后可以重现相同的操作身份吗？Agentic AI systems increasingly act through heterogeneous runtimes: local coding hooks, SDK tools, browser automation, managed-agent traces, API gateways, and workflow engines. A single operational act such as publishing code, changing identity state, moving money, or exporting data may therefore be represented by many incompatible runtime records. This makes a basic governance question difficult to answer: what action was actually approved, what evidence binds the approval to execution, and can an independent verifier reproduce the same action identity later?

本文介绍了This paper presents规范动作验证和证明Canonical Action Verification and Attestation(CAVA)，一个运行时语义层，用于将异构代理活动转换为规范的运行时操作对象。 CAVA 位于证明代理操作 (PCAA) 之下：PCAA 定义部署者拥有的路由-审查-证明治理流程，而 CAVA 定义流程管理的稳定操作对象。该论文形式化了规范操作身份、语义模式检测、批准绑定、收据完整性、运行时便携式投影和可选的证明基质。我们通过 96 个种子、384 个变体的基准测试来研究参考实现，涵盖语义等效、语义分离、包装器绕过、误报控制、批准绑定、收据再现性、证明篡改检测、运行时可移植性、语义模式检测、策略降级和 Azure 部署演练。我们还包括一个系统卡附录，其中包含消融、红队案例、比较边界、部署证据和剩余风险披露。在这个代表性语料库中，CAVA 在重写的运行时表单中保留了规范的操作身份，而原始文本和第一个令牌基线在包装器、策略可寻址模式检测和批准漂移下失败。其贡献是行动级规范化和策略可寻址语义模式的系统制定，作为部署方人工智能治理的必要基础。(CAVA), a runtime-semantics layer for converting heterogeneous agent activity into canonical runtime action objects. CAVA is positioned below Proof-Carrying Agent Actions (PCAA): PCAA defines the deployer-owned route-review-prove governance process, while CAVA defines the stable action object that process governs. The paper formalizes canonical action identity, semantic pattern detection, approval binding, receipt integrity, runtime-portable projection, and optional attestation substrates. The contribution is a systems formulation of action-level canonicalization and policy-addressable semantic patterns as a necessary substrate for deployer-side AI governance.

1 简介Introduction

当模型发出散文时，代理人工智能系统的操作风险很少被意识到。当运行时发生作用时，风险变得具体：执行命令、调用工具、浏览器提交表单、工作流改变状态、身份边界更改、触发支付、部署离开工作区或数据跨越组织边界。因此，企业治理需要的不仅仅是模型端策略、聊天日志或事后跟踪。它需要一个稳定的对象来标识正在决定的操作。The operational risk of an agentic AI system is rarely realized at the point where a model emits prose. Risk becomes concrete when the runtime acts: a command is executed, a tool is invoked, a browser submits a form, a workflow mutates state, an identity boundary changes, a payment is triggered, a deployment leaves the workspace, or data crosses an organizational boundary. Enterprise governance therefore needs more than model-side policy, chat logs, or post-hoc traces. It needs a stable object that identifies the action being decided.

In short, PCAA answers who has authority and what proof must close the action; CAVA answers what exactly the authority decision refers to.

3.2 所需特性Desired properties

10 讨论Discussion

10.1 为什么 CAVA 不应该只是一个产品功能Why CAVA should not be only a product feature

CAVA 作为一个可移植组件比作为一个隐藏的 OSuite 功能更可信。公共模式和验证器允许生态系统检查核心声明、重现哈希值并编写适配器。 OSuite 仍然可以通过硬件部分货币化：维护解析器覆盖范围、企业审批、证据图操作、保证导出、支持、签名者编排和托管集成。对于采用率和收入来说，这是正确的开放核心边界。CAVA is more credible as a portable component than as a hidden OSuite feature. A public schema and verifier allow the ecosystem to inspect the core claim, reproduce hashes, and write adapters. OSuite can still monetize the hard parts: maintained parser coverage, enterprise approvals, evidence graph operations, assurance exports, support, signer orchestration, and managed integrations. This is the right open-core boundary for both adoption and revenue.

10.2 Web3 属于哪里Where Web3 belongs

Web3应该谨慎使用。如果CAVA被定义为区块链，那么它的范围就会变得更窄，对企业的友好性也会降低。如果 CAVA 将账本、可验证凭证、智能账户和去中心化身份视为可选的证明通道，它就可以获得更强大的完整性选项，而无需强迫每个部署者进入结算底层。原则很简单：规范动作语义第一；证明基材第二。Web3 should be used carefully. If CAVA is defined as blockchain, it becomes narrower and less enterprise-friendly. If CAVA treats ledgers, verifiable credentials, smart accounts, and decentralized identity as optional attestation lanes, it gains stronger integrity options without forcing every deployer into a settlement substrate. The principle is simple: canonical action semantics first; attestation substrate second.

11 有效性的威胁Threats to Validity

代表语料库。Representative corpus.当前的基准是具有代表性的公共语料库，而不是企业行为的完整分布。它对于回归和公共解释很有用，但未来的证据应该包括更广泛的痕迹。The current benchmark is a representative public corpus, not a complete distribution of enterprise actions. It is useful for regression and public explanation, but future evidence should include broader traces.

解析器覆盖范围。Parser coverage.规范化的强度取决于适配器覆盖范围。私营企业系统可能需要自定义解析器包，解析器故障可能会导致错过升级或不必要的审查。Canonicalization is only as strong as adapter coverage. Private enterprise systems may need custom parser packs, and parser failures can produce either missed escalations or unnecessary review.

披露边界。Disclosure boundary.生产解析器包和客户连接器规则被保留。这可以保护商业和安全敏感的实施细节，但它限制第三方完全复制 OSuite 的托管行为。Production parser packs and customer connector rules are withheld. This protects commercial and security-sensitive implementation details, but it limits full third-party reproduction of OSuite’s managed behavior.

认证范围。Attestation scope.收据哈希和签名证明完整性和约束力，而不是商业智慧。正确证明的行动仍然可能是一个错误的决定。Receipt hashes and signatures prove integrity and binding, not business wisdom. A correctly attested action can still be a bad decision.

运行时深度。Runtime depth.某些运行时允许内联阻塞。其他仅提供观察者模式跟踪。 CAVA 必须披露这种差异，而不是要求统一执行。Some runtimes allow inline blocking. Others only provide observer-mode traces. CAVA must disclose that difference rather than claim uniform enforcement.

12 结论Conclusion

本文认为代理治理需要稳定的动作语义层。如果没有它，批准会绑定到字符串，跟踪会绑定到特定于运行时的记录，并且证据将很难在不断变化的执行表面上重放。 CAVA 提供规范的运行时操作对象、确定性指纹、接收路径和可选的证明架构，这些架构可以位于 PCAA 或类似的部署者拥有的治理循环之下。This paper argues that agent governance needs a stable action-semantics layer. Without it, approvals bind to strings, traces bind to runtime-specific records, and evidence becomes difficult to replay across changing execution surfaces. CAVA provides a canonical runtime action object, deterministic fingerprint, receipt path, and optional attestation architecture that can sit below PCAA or a similar deployer-owned governance loop.

当前的参考实现和基准还很早期但具体。它们展示了 CAVA 如何折叠等效的运行时表单、分离实质上不同的操作、抵制包装器、将批准绑定到指纹、复制收据、检测篡改以及跨运行时系列投射可比较的语义。下一步的研究步骤是扩展公共语料库，邀请对抗性解析器挑战，并根据真实的第三方代理痕迹评估 CAVA。产品步骤同样清晰：保持模式开放，保持托管证据层值得付费，并使运行时操作治理可衡量，而不是空谈。The current reference implementation and benchmark are early but concrete. They show how CAVA can collapse equivalent runtime forms, separate materially different actions, resist wrappers, bind approvals to fingerprints, reproduce receipts, detect tampering, and project comparable semantics across runtime families. The next research step is to expand the public corpus, invite adversarial parser challenges, and evaluate CAVA against real third-party agent traces. The product step is equally clear: keep the schema open, keep the managed evidence layer worth paying for, and make runtime action governance measurable rather than rhetorical.

附录NAppendix N已知差距和残余风险Known Gaps and Residual Risk

CAVA 尚未完成，本文不应假装不是这样。CAVA is not finished, and the paper should not pretend otherwise.

最重要的残余风险并不是 CAVA 有时升级太多或太少。这些是可以通过更好的测试发现的工程缺陷。更深层次的风险是夸大其词：出售观察者模式日志作为执行，出售收据哈希作为判断，或者出售公共框架，就好像它复制了托管产品一样。因此，本文的边界是技术贡献的一部分。诚实的限制使系统更加强大。The most important residual risk is not that CAVA sometimes escalates too much or too little. Those are engineering defects that can be found by better tests. The deeper risk is overclaiming: selling observer-mode logs as enforcement, selling receipt hashes as judgment, or selling a public skeleton as if it reproduces the managed product. The paper’s boundary is therefore part of the technical contribution. Honest limits make the system stronger.
