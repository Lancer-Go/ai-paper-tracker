P2O：联合政策，及时优化O: Joint Policy and Prompt Optimization

Xinyu Lu    Kaiqi Zhang    Jinglin Yang    Boxi Cao    Yaojie Lu    Hongyu Lin    Min He    Xianpei Han    Le Sun
摘要Abstract

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为增强大型语言模型（LLM）推理能力的强大范例。 然而，普通 RLVR 的探索效率低下，尤其是在面对成功率接近于零的“硬样本”时。 在这种情况下，对稀疏结果奖励的依赖通常会导致零优势估计，从而有效地使监督信号模型匮乏，尽管这些实例的信息价值很高。 为了解决这个问题，我们建议 PReinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has emerged as a powerful paradigm for enhancing the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs). However, vanilla RLVR suffers from inefficient exploration, particularly when confronting “hard samples” that yield near-zero success rates. In such scenarios, the reliance on sparse outcome rewards typically results in zero-advantage estimates, effectively starving the model of supervision signals despite the high informational value of these instances. To address this, we propose P2O，一种将即时优化与策略优化相结合的新颖框架。 磷O, a novel framework that synergizes Prompt Optimization with Policy Optimization. P2O 在训练迭代期间识别硬样本，并利用遗传帕累托 (GEPA) 提示优化算法来演化提示模板，指导模型发现成功的轨迹。 至关重要的是，与依赖输入增强的传统即时工程方法不同，PO identifies hard samples during training iterations and leverages the Genetic-Pareto (GEPA) prompt optimization algorithm to evolve prompt templates that guide the model toward discovering successful trajectories. Crucially, unlike traditional prompt engineering methods that rely on input augmentation, P2O 将这些优化提示引起的推理增益直接提取到模型参数中。 该机制为硬样本提供了更密集的正向监督信号并加速收敛。 大量实验证明 PO distills the reasoning gains induced by these optimized prompts directly into the model parameters. This mechanism provides denser positive supervision signals for hard samples and accelerates convergence. Extensive experiments demonstrate that P2O 不仅在分布内数据集上实现了卓越的性能，而且还表现出很强的泛化能力，在分布外基准上产生了显着的改进（平均+4.7%）。O not only achieves superior performance on in-distribution datasets but also exhibits strong generalization, yielding substantial improvements on out-of-distribution benchmarks (+4.7% avg.).

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在一系列复杂的推理任务中取得了令人瞩目的熟练程度Large Language Models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable proficiency across a spectrum of complex reasoning tasks(Jaech et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025)。 这些任务的一个决定性特征是存在能够提供确定性反馈的客观验证机制。 利用这一点，具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）. A defining characteristic of these tasks is the existence of objective verification mechanisms capable of providing deterministic feedback. Capitalizing on this, Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)(Lambert et al., 2024)已成为推理对齐的主导范式。 通过利用基于结果的监督，RLVR 使模型能够超越模仿学习的局限性，促进解决方案空间的自主探索，以优化与严格验证信号相一致的推理轨迹has emerged as a dominant paradigm for reasoning alignment. By leveraging outcome-based supervision, RLVR enables models to surpass the limitations of imitation learning, facilitating autonomous exploration of the solution space to optimize reasoning trajectories aligned with rigorous verification signals(Liu et al., 2025a).

尽管有这样的承诺，但当训练数据将“简单样本”与“硬样本”混合在一起时，RLVR 的性能会受到严重限制——具体来说，模型在整个训练过程中成功率接近于零的情况。Despite its promise, the performance of RLVR is severely constrained when the training data intermixes “simple samples” with “hard samples”—specifically, instances where the model achieves near-zero success rates acrossKK推出。 对于硬样本，由于探索困难和奖励稀疏，模型无法捕获可靠的梯度反馈rollouts. For hard samples, the model fails to capture reliable gradient feedback due to exploration difficulties and sparse rewards(Song et al., 2025)。 因此，优化过程在很大程度上依赖于来自简单样本的可访问信号。 这会导致模型过度拟合简单实例，使其陷入次优策略，该策略擅长执行琐碎任务，但无法解决复杂的推理问题。. Consequently, the optimization process heavily relies on the accessible signals from simple samples. This causes the model to overfit to simple instances, trapping it in a suboptimal policy where it excels at trivial tasks but fails to resolve complex reasoning problems.

缓解探索挑战的常见方法包括课程学习策略，随着训练的进展逐步引入更难的样本Common approaches to mitigate exploration challenges include curriculum learning strategies, which progressively introduce harder samples as training advances(Blakeman et al., 2025)，以及提供中间反馈的各种奖励塑造技术。 然而，课程学习需要基于启发式且计算成本昂贵的时间表生成, and various reward shaping techniques that provide intermediate feedback. However, curriculum learning requires heuristic-based and computationally expensive schedule generation(Bercovich et al., 2025)，而奖励塑造则需要特定领域的专家启发法。, while reward shaping demands domain-specific expert heuristics.

最近成功的提示优化方法The recent success of prompt optimization methods(Fernando et al., 2023; Opsahl-Ong et al., 2024; Yuksekgonul et al., 2025)提供了一种打破这种僵局的令人信服的方法。 这些方法表明，即使模型无法解决标准策略下的难题，精心演化的提示通常也能引出正确的推理路径。 这意味着解决方案位于模型的潜在搜索空间内，但无法通过标准梯度上升来访问offers a compelling way to break this stalemate. These methods demonstrate that even when a model fails to solve a hard problem under a standard policy, a carefully evolved prompt can often elicit the correct reasoning path. 为了有效地解决这个优化问题，我们考虑 GEPATo solve this optimization problem efficiently, we consider GEPA(Agrawal et al., 2025)，一个最先进的进化框架。 GEPA 将即时优化概念化为由基于语言的反射驱动的遗传过程。 它不是随机突变，而是采用“Reflection LLM”来分析当前提示性能的错误跟踪，并生成解决特定故障模式的有针对性的语义突变。 此外，GEPA 使用基于 Pareto 的选择机制维护多种提示，同时针对多个目标（例如准确性和简洁性）进行优化。 这使得框架能够有效地导航离散提示空间，避免陷入传统提示优化方法的局部最优。, a state-of-the-art evolutionary framework. This allows the framework to navigate the discrete prompt space effectively, escaping local optima that trap conventional prompt optimization methods.

Crucially, however, these methods typically rely on heuristically obtained hints or expert trajectory fragments derived from the dataset. There is a notable absence of “optimization” regarding the guidance itself; the prompts or hints are treated as static constants rather than dynamic variables. This reliance on predefined or oracle-dependent guidance limits the model’s ability to generalize beyond the scope of the provided hints. In contrast, P2O将提示视为可优化参数，与策略共同演化任务拓扑，实现双层自我改进。O treats the prompt as an optimizable parameter, jointly evolving the task topology with the policy to achieve bi-level self-improvement.

By leveraging optimized prompts to unlock successful trajectories for hard samples and subsequently internalizing these capabilities through context distillation, P2O有效地恢复了先前棘手实例的有效训练信号，从而防止策略陷入局部最优。 数学基准的大量实验表明 PO effectively recovers valid training signals for previously intractable instances, thereby preventing the policy from collapsing into local optima.
