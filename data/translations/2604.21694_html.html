高效的逻辑门网络Efficient Logic Gate Networks
用于视频复制检测for Video Copy Detection

Katarzyna Fojcik
摘要Abstract

视频副本检测需要在各种视觉扭曲下进行鲁棒的相似性估计，同时以非常大的规模进行操作。尽管深度神经网络实现了强大的性能，但其计算成本和描述符大小限制了在高吞吐量系统中的实际部署。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种基于可微分逻辑门网络（LGN）的视频复制检测框架，该框架用紧凑的基于逻辑的表示取代了传统的浮点特征提取器。 我们的方法结合了积极的框架小型化、二进制预处理和可训练的 LGN 嵌入模型，该模型可以学习逻辑运算和互连。训练后，模型可以离散化为纯布尔电路，从而实现极快且节省内存的推理。我们系统地评估跨多个数据集折叠和难度级别的不同相似性策略、二值化方案和 LGN 架构。 实验结果表明，与之前的模型相比，基于 LGN 的模型实现了具有竞争力或卓越的准确性和排名性能，同时生成的描述符小了几个数量级，并且推理速度超过每秒 11k 个样本。这些发现表明，基于逻辑的模型为可扩展且资源高效的视频副本检测提供了一种有前途的替代方案。Video copy detection requires robust similarity estimation under diverse visual distortions while operating at very large scale. Although deep neural networks achieve strong performance, their computational cost and descriptor size limit practical deployment in high-throughput systems. In this work, we propose a video copy detection framework based on differentiable Logic Gate Networks (LGNs), which replace conventional floating-point feature extractors with compact, logic-based representations. Our approach combines aggressive frame miniaturization, binary preprocessing, and a trainable LGN embedding model that learns both logical operations and interconnections. After training, the model can be discretized into a purely Boolean circuit, enabling extremely fast and memory-efficient inference. We systematically evaluate different similarity strategies, binarization schemes, and LGN architectures across multiple dataset folds and difficulty levels. Experimental results demonstrate that LGN-based models achieve competitive or superior accuracy and ranking performance compared to prior models, while producing descriptors several orders of magnitude smaller and delivering inference speeds exceeding 11k samples per second. These findings indicate that logic-based models offer a promising alternative for scalable and resource-efficient video copy detection.

1 简介Introduction

在线视频平台的快速增长加剧了对能够比较视频并识别视觉或语义关系的可扩展系统的需求。视频相似度计算支持许多任务，包括检索、事件分析和版权保护。虽然检索侧重于相对排名，但检测任务需要经过良好校准的相似性分数来支持基于阈值的决策，相关性的概念范围从共享事件到接近重复的内容。The rapid growth of online video platforms has intensified the need for scalable systems capable of comparing videos and identifying visual or semantic relationships. Video similarity computation underpins many tasks, including retrieval, event analysis, and copyright protection. While retrieval focuses on relative ranking, detection tasks require well-calibrated similarity scores to support threshold-based decisions, with the notion of relevance ranging from shared events to near-duplicate content.

视频复制检测（VCD）尤其具有挑战性，因为它必须识别精确的和近似重复的视频，这些视频可能会经历重新编码、裁剪或其他增强等转换（图 1）。Video Copy Detection (VCD) is particularly challenging, as it must identify both exact and near-duplicate videos that may undergo transformations such as re-encoding, cropping, or other augmentations (Fig.1）。大型平台不断摄取大量内容，要求 VCD 系统在严格的延迟限制以及存在有意和无意的失真的情况下对大量档案进行可靠的匹配。). Large-scale platforms continuously ingest massive volumes of content, requiring VCD systems to perform reliable matching against extensive archives under strict latency constraints and in the presence of both intentional and unintentional distortions.

尽管深度神经网络具有显着先进的视频理解和表示学习，但它们在大规模 VCD 管道中的部署仍然具有挑战性。高性能卷积架构和视觉转换器通常需要大量计算资源、高内存带宽和专用硬件加速器。即使经过优化的深度模型也会产生推理开销，在高吞吐量环境中扩展性很差，这促使人们寻找替代表示和推理机制，以保持鲁棒性，同时大幅降低计算成本。Although deep neural networks have substantially advanced video understanding and representation learning, their deployment in large-scale VCD pipelines remains challenging. High-performing convolutional architectures and vision transformers typically require significant computational resources, high memory bandwidth, and specialized hardware accelerators. Even optimized deep models incur inference overheads that scale poorly in high-throughput environments, motivating the search for alternative representations and inference mechanisms that preserve robustness while dramatically reducing computational cost.

在之前的工作中，我们探索了两个方向：使用选定的和极小型化的帧进行紧凑的视频表示In prior work, we explored two directions: compact video representations using selected and aggressively miniaturized frames[6]，以及 LILogic Net，一种可微分逻辑门模型，其学习结构可以离散化为固定布尔电路以进行快速推理, and LILogic Net, a differentiable logic-gate model whose learned structure can be discretized into a fixed Boolean circuit for fast inference[8]。在这项工作中，我们将这些想法统一到一个框架中，用紧凑的基于逻辑的嵌入模型代替传统的卷积特征提取。我们介绍由此产生的管道（图 2）。. In this work, we unify these ideas into a single framework that replaces conventional convolutional feature extraction with a compact logic-based embedding model. We introduce the resulting pipeline (Fig.2），解释其概念基础，并提供广泛的实验，证明基于可微分逻辑的网络为现实世界的视频复制和近似重复检测的传统深度模型提供了有效且高效的替代方案。据我们所知，这项工作代表了将可微逻辑门网络应用于视频分析问题的首次尝试，特别是视频复制和近似重复检测。), explain its conceptual foundations, and provide extensive experiments demonstrating that differentiable logic-based networks offer an effective and highly efficient alternative to conventional deep models for real-world video copy and near-duplicate detection. To the best of our knowledge, this work represents the first attempt to apply differentiable logic gate networks to a video analysis problem, and in particular to video copy and near-duplicate detection.

2.2 逻辑门网络Logic Gate Networks

与 VCD 的这些进展并行，一系列互补的研究探索了二进制或基于逻辑的神经计算，作为传统浮点深度模型的替代方案。二值化神经网络 (BNN)Parallel to these advances in VCD, a complementary line of research has explored binary or logic-based neural computations as an alternative to conventional floating-point deep models. Binarized neural networks (BNNs)[11, 19]降低计算复杂度，但仍然依赖加权运算。 Petersen 等人提出的逻辑门网络 (LGN) 是一个更根本的背离。reduce computational complexity but still rely on weighted operations. A more radical departure comes from Logic Gate Networks (LGNs), introduced by Petersen et al.[21]，他证明了完全由布尔门组成的网络可以通过可微松弛进行训练。随后的工作用卷积逻辑层扩展了这个想法, who showed that networks composed entirely of Boolean gates can be trained with differentiable relaxations. Subsequent works extended this idea with convolutional logic layers[22]和可学习的连接组and learnable connectome[8]。 面向硬件的研究凸显了 LGN 的极低延迟潜力，其中包括 FINN 等 FPGA 框架. Hardware-oriented research has highlighted the extremely low-latency potential of LGNs, with FPGA frameworks such as FINN[26]和自定义 RISC-V 指令加速逻辑推理and custom RISC-V instructions accelerating logic inference[27]。该方向强调可解释性、形式可验证性以及与数字硬件的紧密结合。. This direction emphasizes interpretability, formal verifiability, and strong alignment with digital hardware.

尽管取得了实质性进展，但之前的工作还没有尝试将基于逻辑门的架构与完整的 VCD 管道集成。现有的 VCD 系统几乎完全依赖于浮点 CNN 或 Transformer 特征，而逻辑网络主要在 MNIST 或 CIFAR-10 等分类环境中进行探索。目前的工作通过将我们之前的 VCD 紧凑框架表示与最近引入的 LILogic Net 架构合并来弥补这一差距Despite substantial progress, no previous work has attempted to integrate logic-gate-based architectures with a full VCD pipeline. Existing VCD systems rely almost exclusively on floating-point CNN or transformer features, while logic networks have primarily been explored in classification contexts such as MNIST or CIFAR-10. The present work bridges this gap by merging our prior compact-frame representation for VCD with the recently introduced LILogic Net architecture[8]，产生专为大规模部署而设计的端到端布尔视频嵌入模型。, yielding an end-to-end Boolean video embedding model designed for large-scale deployment.

3 方法论Methodology

3.1 模型架构Model architecture

逻辑门网络 (LGN) 是由 AND、OR 和 XOR 等二进制逻辑门组成的稀疏、失重的神经模型。在传统形式中，LGN 对离散输入进行操作并实现固定的布尔函数，这使得它们在推理时非常高效，但无法通过基于梯度的优化进行训练。 Petersen 等人提出的 Differentiable LGN 框架。Logic Gate Networks (LGNs) are sparse, weightless neural models composed of binary logic gates, such as AND, OR, and XOR. In their traditional form, LGNs operate on discrete inputs and implement fixed Boolean functions, which makes them extremely efficient at inference time but prevents training via gradient-based optimization. The Differentiable LGN framework introduced by Petersen et al.[21]通过放宽 LGN 的离散性质来解决这一限制，从而能够使用标准优化器进行端到端训练。addresses this limitation by relaxing the discrete nature of LGNs, enabling end-to-end training with standard optimizers.

在可微分 LGN 中，二进制输入被放宽为范围内的连续值In differentiable LGNs, binary inputs are relaxed to continuous values in the range[0,1][0,1]，逻辑门以概率方式解释。每个门计算布尔函数的软近似（例如，合取被建模为乘法），并且每个节点代表所有 16 个可能的 2 输入布尔函数的可学习混合。该公式允许梯度在训练期间通过门行为和网络结构传播。优化后，学习模型可以离散化为纯组合 LGN，从而产生无状态且高效的推理模型。, and logic gates are interpreted probabilistically. Each gate computes a soft approximation of Boolean functions (e.g., conjunction is modeled as multiplication), and every node represents a learnable mixture over all 16 possible 2-input Boolean functions. This formulation allows gradients to propagate through both gate behavior and network structure during training. After optimization, the learned model can be discretized into a purely combinatorial LGN, yielding a stateless and highly efficient inference model.

遵循 LILogic Net 框架的设计原则，我们采用 LGN 架构来共同学习门函数和层间连接（连接组）。这使得能够系统地探索表达性、稀疏性和计算效率之间的权衡。Following the design principles of the LILogic Net framework, we employ LGN architectures that jointly learn both the gate functions and the inter-layer connectivity (connectome). This enables a systematic exploration of the trade-offs between expressiveness, sparsity, and computational efficiency.

在本研究中，我们专门关注单层 LGN 模型，因为之前的 LILogic Net 结果表明，宽而浅的架构在准确性和效率之间提供了有利的平衡。每个模型由具有 1,000、2,000、4,000 或 8,000 个节点的单个逻辑门层组成，其中每个节点恰好接收两个输入并实现 16 个布尔函数的软组合。层内的所有节点共享相同的连接组策略。In this study, we focus exclusively on single-layer LGN models, as prior LILogic Net results demonstrate that wide, shallow architectures provide a favorable balance between accuracy and efficiency. Each model consists of a single logic gate layer with 1,000, 2,000, 4,000 or 8,000 nodes, where each node receives exactly two inputs and implements a soft combination of the 16 Boolean functions. All nodes within a layer share the same connectome strategy.

我们考虑两种互连优化策略（图 1）。We consider two interconnect optimization strategies (Fig.3):

- 完全可学习的密集连接体 (L)Fully Learnable Dense Connectome (L): 对于每个节点，其两个输入都连接到输入向量的所有输出。每个输入的连接权重使用可微分的 softmax 独立参数化，允许模型学习任意全局连接模式。虽然表达能力很强，但这种变体会产生更高的内存和计算成本。: For each node, both of its two inputs are connected to all outputs of the input vector. The connection weights for each input are parameterized independently using a differentiable softmax, allowing the model to learn arbitrary global connectivity patterns. While highly expressive, this variant incurs higher memory and computational cost.

- Top-K 稀疏连接组 (Top-K)Top-K Sparse Connectome (Top-K): 每个节点独立选择: Each node independently selectsKK初始化时两个输入的候选输入连接。在训练期间，softmax 仅应用于这些candidate input connections for each of its two inputs at initialization. During training, a softmax is applied only over theseKK候选者，产生加权组合。这增强了结构化稀疏性，同时保留了可微性。根据 LILogic Net 的先前发现，我们修复candidates, producing a weighted combination. This enforces structured sparsity while preserving differentiability. Based on prior findings in LILogic Net, we fixK=32K=32在所有 Top-K 实验中，它在表达性和效率之间提供了强有力的权衡。in all Top-K experiments, which offers a strong trade-off between expressiveness and efficiency.

3.2 数据集和预处理Dataset and Preprocessing

3.3.2 可微松弛Differentiable Relaxation

逻辑门网络在离散逻辑运算符上运行，这使得使用基于梯度的方法进行直接优化变得不可行。为了克服这个限制，我们用连续松弛代替硬门选择，其中每个逻辑门代表所有可能的双输入布尔函数的软组合。每个门都由一组可学习的实值分数参数化，这些分数定义了 16 个布尔运算符的分类分布。标准的 softmax 变换将这些分数转换为总和为 1 的非负系数。在训练期间，这种分布允许模型在不同的逻辑行为之间平滑地插值，从而使梯度能够流经门选择过程。Logic Gate Networks operate on discrete logic operators, which makes direct optimization with gradient-based methods infeasible. To overcome this limitation, we replace hard gate selection with a continuous relaxation in which each logic gate represents a soft combination of all possible two-input Boolean functions. Each gate is parameterized by a set of learnable real-valued scores that define a categorical distribution over the 16 Boolean operators. A standard softmax transformation converts these scores into non-negative coefficients that sum to one. During training, this distribution allows the model to smoothly interpolate between different logical behaviors, enabling gradients to flow through the gate selection process.

3.4 离散化Discretization

训练后，通过对门选择和互连进行二值化，将网络转变为完全离散、推理友好的形式。这是通过从每个 softmax 分布中选取最高概率的选择（模式）来实现的。因此，每个逻辑门在一对固定的二进制输入上运行并实现单个布尔函数。在推理时，每个节点一致使用两个二进制输入，允许整个模型纯粹使用布尔运算运行，无需任何浮点计算。这种离散化极大地提高了速度和内存效率，使该模型非常适合部署在嵌入式系统或其他硬件受限的环境中。After training, the network is transformed into a fully discrete, inference-friendly form by binarizing both the gate selections and the interconnections. This is achieved by taking the highest-probability choice (mode) from each softmax distribution. As a result, each logic gate operates on a fixed pair of binary inputs and implements a single Boolean function. At inference time, every node consistently uses two binary inputs, allowing the entire model to run purely with Boolean operations, without any floating-point computation. This discretization greatly improves both speed and memory efficiency, making the model well-suited for deployment in embedded systems or other hardware-constrained environments.

3.5 指标Metrics

由于数据集是平衡的，我们报告测试集上的准确度、精确度、召回率和 F1 分数。通过最大化 F1 分数在验证集上选择复制/非复制分类的相似性阈值。Since the dataset is balanced, we report accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score on the test set. The similarity threshold for copy/non-copy classification is selected on the validation set by maximizing the F1-score.

此外，我们还报告了微平均精度 (μAP)，这是视频复制检测的标准指标。 µAP 评估所有查询视频对的预测相似性得分的总体排名质量。具体来说，所有预测的相似性分数都会被汇集起来，并使用相关（复制）和不相关（非复制）对来计算全局精确召回曲线。 µAP 对应于该曲线下的面积，测量系统将真实副本排在非副本之上的程度，与特定阈值无关。In addition, we report micro Average Precision (µAP), a standard metric in video copy detection. µAP evaluates the overall ranking quality of predicted similarity scores across all query-video pairs. Specifically, all predicted similarity scores are pooled, and relevant (copy) and non-relevant (non-copy) pairs are used to compute a global precision-recall curve. µAP corresponds to the area under this curve, measuring how well the system ranks true copies above non-copies, independent of a specific threshold.

In contrast,μ\muAP 表现出非常窄的分布且方差极小，这表明基于排名的表现在很大程度上不受弃牌难度的影响。总之，这些结果表明 LGN 模型在不同条件下都能很好地泛化，并且性能下降仅限于极端变换的一小部分。AP exhibits very narrow distributions with minimal variance, suggesting that ranking-based performance remains largely unaffected by fold difficulty. Together, these results demonstrate that LGN models generalize well across varying conditions and that performance degradation is limited to a small subset of extreme transformations.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们探索了使用逻辑门网络进行视频副本检测，证明基于逻辑的表示可以实现强大的性能，同时保持极其紧凑和计算效率。据我们所知，这是第一项将 LGN 从静态域（例如图像或表格数据）扩展到时间结构化视频序列的研究。In this work, we explored the use of logic gate networks for video copy detection, demonstrating that logic-based representations can achieve strong performance while remaining extremely compact and computationally efficient. To our knowledge, this is the first study extending LGNs from static domains, such as images or tabular data, to temporally structured video sequences.

我们的实验分析表明，所提出的方法受益于粗略的空间表示和结构化逻辑建模，从而在常见的视觉扭曲下实现稳健的相似性估计。在多个评估折叠中，与仅预处理的基线相比，逻辑门网络持续提高了性能，并相对于现有的深度学习方法取得了有竞争力的结果，同时在描述符大小和推理速度方面提供了数量级的优势。Our experimental analysis showed that the proposed approach benefits from coarse spatial representations and structured logical modeling, leading to robust similarity estimation under common visual distortions. Across multiple evaluation folds, logic gate networks consistently improved performance compared to preprocessing-only baselines and achieved competitive results relative to existing deep learning methods, while offering orders-of-magnitude advantages in descriptor size and inference speed.

作为基于逻辑的视频分析的第一步，本研究仅限于相对简单的数据集和一组有限的转换。虽然此设置允许进行受控评估，但它也强调了未来在更具挑战性的基准上开展工作的需要，包括具有更强时间变化、复杂运动模式和更多样化失真类型的数据集。将逻辑门网络扩展到此类场景，以及探索更深层次的架构和时间建模，代表了进一步研究的重要方向。As a first step toward logic-based video analysis, this study is limited to a relatively simple dataset and a restricted set of transformations. While this setting allows for controlled evaluation, it also highlights the need for future work on more challenging benchmarks, including datasets with stronger temporal variation, complex motion patterns, and more diverse distortion types. Extending logic gate networks to such scenarios, as well as exploring deeper architectures and temporal modeling, represents an important direction for further research.
