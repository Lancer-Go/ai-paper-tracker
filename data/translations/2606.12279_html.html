摘要。机器学习领域的最新工作在推理时应用遗传算法来迭代改进优化问题的解决方案。所涉及的基本突变和重组算子与经典研究的算子有本质上的不同。突变不再是随机的；机器学习算法会改变解决方案，以改善目标。同样，重组也不是基于父解决方案的随机拼贴。相反，它是一个基于机器学习优化的运算符，其目标是根据其输入综合改进的解决方案。因此，这些变异和重组算子更有可能改善目标，但它们的计算成本要高得多。Abstract. Recent work in ML applies genetic algorithms at inference time to iteratively improve solutions to optimization problems. The basic mutation and recombination operators involved are qualitatively different from those studied classically. Mutations are no longer random; an ML algorithm mutates a solution with the goal of improving an objective. Similarly, recombination is not based on random collages of parent solutions. Instead, it is an ML optimization-based operator whose goal is to synthesize improved solutions from its inputs. Thus, these mutation and recombination operators are more likely to improve the objective, but their computational cost is much higher.

Answering this question requires a framework that treats these operators on equal footing and makes their trade-offs explicit.

七、讨论与结论7. Discussion and Conclusion

我们引入了 GMR 框架作为机器学习引导遗传算法的模型。其动机是经典的遗传算法模型没有捕获迭代 LLM 提示的设置：突变和重组不是盲目的随机扰动和交叉，而是学习算子，其目标是改进输入解决方案。这导致了一个不同的查询分配问题：策略应该如何在生成、变异和重组上花费预算？与经典 GA 不同，这里的每个操作可能都很昂贵，因此分配好查询预算就成为核心。We introduced the GMR framework as a model for ML-guided genetic algorithms. The motivation is that classical genetic-algorithm models do not capture the setting of iterative LLM prompting: mutation and recombination are not blind random perturbations and crossovers, but learned operators whose goal is to improve the input solutions. This leads to a different query-allocation problem: how should a policy spend its budget on generation, mutation, and recombination? Unlike in classical GAs, each operation here may be expensive, so allocating the query budget well becomes central.

我们的主要信息是，优化引导算子的行为可能与经典平均或随机拼接操作非常不同，并且生成、变异和重组在理论上可以发挥真正不同的作用。定理 1 表明，更强的引导算子可以比平均型算子带来更快的改进。定理 2 显示了一个简单的结构，其中每个运算符都是必需的。最后在模型的奇偶学习实例化中，我们表明Our main message is that optimization-guided operators can behave very differently from classical averaging or random-splicing operations, and that generation, mutation, and recombination can play genuinely different roles in theory. Theorem 1 shows that stronger guided operators can lead to much faster improvement than averaging-type operators. Theorem 2 shows a simple construction where each operator is necessary. Finally in the parity learning instantiation of the model, we show that多样性diversity是必要的：成功生成隐藏目标需要保持线性数量的候选者存活并重新组合它们。事实上，活池不够大的政策可能无法在次指数时间内找到目标。is necessary: successfully generating the hidden target requires maintaining a linear amount of candidates alive and recombining them. Indeed, policies with an insufficiently large live-pool provably fail to find the target in subexponential time.
