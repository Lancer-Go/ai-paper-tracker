等价的错觉：法学硕士量化效应的统计特征The Illusion of Equivalency: Statistical Characterization of Quantization Effects in LLMs

摘要Abstract

训练后量化广泛用于在资源受限的环境中部署大型语言模型，但其评估几乎完全依赖于准确性和复杂性。我们表明这些指标无法捕捉量化引起的行为变化。我们引入了正确性一致性，这是一种决策级度量，用于测量基本模型及其量化变体之间正确预测的重叠，与绝对精度无关。在从 8 位到 2 位的多个模型和量化方案中，我们发现即使任务性能似乎得到保留，在适度量化下也会出现行为分歧。为了解释这种效应，我们将量化作为注意力权重的结构算子进行分析，并使用统计和分布测量来量化分层失真。我们的结果揭示了低位宽下的非线性断点，并表明查询和键投影始终比值和输出投影更敏感。这些发现揭示了基础模型和量化模型之间等效的错觉，并激发了超越传统绩效指标的行为评估。Post-training quantization is widely used to deploy large language models in resource-constrained settings, yet its evaluation relies almost exclusively on accuracy and perplexity. We show that these metrics fail to capture behavioral changes induced by quantization. We introduce correctness agreement, a decision-level metric that measures overlap in correct predictions between a base model and its quantized variants, independent of absolute accuracy. Across multiple models and quantization schemes from 8-bit to 2-bit, we find that behavioral divergence emerges under moderate quantization even when task performance appears preserved. To explain this effect, we analyze quantization as a structural operator on attention weights and quantify layer-wise distortions using statistical and distributional measures. Our results reveal non-linear breakpoints at low bit-widths and show that query and key projections are consistently more sensitive than value and output projections. These findings expose an illusion of equivalence between base and quantized models and motivate behavioral evaluation beyond conventional performance metrics.

量化的基本目标是保留原始模型的功能行为，同时实现计算效率。在实践中，行为保留不仅限于任务级别的准确性，还包括事实知识、推理的稳健性以及一致的风格和安全相关行为。这些属性特别容易受到量化的影响，因为模型参数中的微小数值扰动可能会通过深层架构非线性传播并导致意外的行为变化The fundamental objective of quantization is to preserve the functional behavior of the original model while achieving computational efficiency. In practice, behavioral preservation extends beyond task-level accuracy to include factual knowledge, reasoning robustness, and consistent stylistic and safety-related behaviors. These properties are especially vulnerable to quantization, as small numerical perturbations in model parameters may propagate non-linearly through deep architectures and lead to unexpected behavioral changesDutta et al. (2024).

Our analysis exposes non-linear breakpoints where modest reductions in bit-width lead to disproportionate structural distortions, even when accuracy and perplexity appear stable. Examining multiple quantization bit-levels further allows us to isolate layer-wise and architecture-dependent sensitivities, identifying which components of LLMs are inherently robust and which are vulnerable to aggressive low-bit settings.

2 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

量化将神经网络权重的数值精度从浮点降低到较低位表示，以减少模型大小、内存带宽使用和资源受限硬件上的推理成本Quantization reduces the numerical precision of neural network weights from floating point to lower bit representations in order to decrease model size, memory bandwidth usage, and inference cost on resource-constrained hardwareXiao et al. (2023)。现有方法分为两类：量化感知训练（QAT）和训练后量化（PTQ）. Existing approaches fall into two categories: Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) and Post-Training Quantization (PTQ)Jacob et al. (2018)。 QAT 在训练期间结合了量化，并且需要访问训练管道，这使得它昂贵且不切实际。相比之下，PTQ 直接将量化应用于预训练模型，无需重新训练，避免了大规模优化的成本。因此，PTQ 是实践中的主导选择，并被广泛采用来压缩 LLM，同时保持有竞争力的准确性. QAT incorporates quantization during training and requires access to the training pipeline, making it expensive and impractical. In contrast, PTQ applies quantization directly to pretrained models without retraining, avoiding the cost of large-scale optimization. For this reason, PTQ is the dominant choice in practice and has been widely adopted for compressing LLMs while maintaining competitive accuracyDettmers et al. (2024); Kim et al. (2024); Xiao et al. (2023); Frantar et al. (2022).
