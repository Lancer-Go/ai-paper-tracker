FlowForm：将流体物理与拓扑一致性相结合进行卫星洪水合成FlowForm: Synergizing Fluid Physics with Topological Consistency for Satellite Flood Synthesis

摘要Abstract

开发强大的洪水评估模型需要高质量的配对卫星图像，但对于洪水特定图像的生成来说，此类数据仍然稀缺。尽管生成模型提供了一种有前途的数据增强方法，但现有方法通常会产生令人难以置信的洪水区域空间布局并扭曲场景结构。我们提出了 FlowForm，这是一种卫星泛洪合成框架，它将受 SWE 启发的潜在正则化与结构感知调节相结合。洪水描述符模块 (FDM) 对扩散瓶颈处辅助潜场中的稳态浅水方程的残差施加可微分惩罚。地形锚适配器 (TAA) 在 U-Net 的四个编码器尺度上注入深度、语义和边缘特征。我们进一步策划了 FloodScape，这是一个大规模、高分辨率的数据集，包含灾害前后获取的配对卫星图像。除了标准图像生成指标之外，我们还评估洪水区域的一致性、对地理上持续存在的洪水事件的零样本概括以及对各个组件的敏感性。在所有报告的比较中，FlowForm 实现了更高的视觉保真度、配对图像之间更大的相似性以及淹没区域更强的一致性。Developing robust flood assessment models requires high-quality paired satellite imagery, yet such data remain scarce for flood-specific image generation. Although generative models provide a promising means of data augmentation, existing methods often yield implausible spatial layouts of flooded regions and distort scene structures. We propose FlowForm, a framework for satellite flood synthesis that integrates SWE-inspired latent regularization with structure-aware conditioning. The Flood Descriptor Module (FDM) imposes differentiable penalties on residuals of the steady-state Shallow Water Equation in auxiliary latent fields at the diffusion bottleneck. The Terrain Anchor Adapter (TAA) injects depth, semantic, and edge features at four encoder scales of the U-Net. We further curate FloodScape, a large-scale, high-resolution dataset comprising paired satellite images acquired before and after disasters. In addition to standard image-generation metrics, we evaluate the consistency of flooded regions, zero-shot generalization to a geographically held-out flood event, and sensitivity to individual components. Across all reported comparisons, FlowForm achieves higher visual fidelity, greater similarity between paired images, and stronger consistency of flooded regions.

1 简介Introduction

随着变暖导致暴雨引发的洪水风险增加As rainstorm-induced flood risks increase under warming(Zhang et al. 2022)，卫星图像的及时灾后评估可以支持人道主义援助和灾后恢复, timely post-disaster assessment from satellite imagery can support humanitarian assistance and disaster recovery(Gupta et al. 2019)。数据驱动的灾后和变化检测模型具有先进的自动化评估. Data-driven post-disaster and change-detection models have advanced automated assessment(Asad et al. 2023; Zang et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2023b; Safavi and Rahnemoonfar 2022);然而，这些模型的可靠性取决于代表性的训练数据。与普通图像不同，遥感中对灾害的高质量观测仍然稀缺; however, the reliability of these models depends on representative training data. Unlike ordinary imagery, high-quality observations of disasters remain scarce in remote sensing(Rahnemoonfar et al. 2021; Gupta et al. 2019; Wang et al. 2025)。这种限制对于变化分析来说尤其严重，变化分析必须使用空间对齐的灾前和灾后观测来区分与事件相关的变化和持续场景。因此，一个有用的训练对需要的不仅仅是从同一位置获得两次采集：图像必须充分共同配准，洪水必须可见，并且不相关的变化必须最小化。云层覆盖和洪水事件的观测窗口较短，使得大规模收集此类数据对变得困难。. This limitation is particularly acute for change analysis, which must distinguish changes related to the event from persistent scenes using spatially aligned pre-disaster and post-disaster observations. Therefore, a useful training pair requires more than simply obtaining two acquisitions from the same location: the images must be sufficiently co-registered, the flood must be visible, and unrelated changes must be minimal. Cloud cover and the short observation windows of flood events make it difficult to collect such pairs at scale.

生成模型提供了一种可扩展的数据增强方法Generative models offer a scalable approach to data augmentation(Goodfellow et al. 2020; Ho et al. 2020; Song et al. 2020; Rombach et al. 2022; Tang et al. 2025; Toker et al. 2024)。扩散模型在这种情况下特别有效，因为它们可以合成详细的图像，同时容纳文本、元数据或图像调节。先前的遥感研究已经根据灾前输入合成了灾后场景，证明了这种以数据为中心的范式的可行性. Diffusion models are particularly effective in this context, as they synthesize detailed imagery while accommodating text, metadata, or image conditioning. Prior studies in remote sensing have synthesized post-disaster scenes from pre-disaster inputs, demonstrating the feasibility of this data-centric paradigm(Khanna et al. 2023; Lütjens et al. 2024; Rui et al. 2021)。然而，视觉上合理的卫星图像不一定代表所提供的事件前场景的逻辑上有效的变换。生成模型可以在将洪水放置在不正确的区域时产生令人信服的水纹理，或者可以在任意改变应保持不变的建筑物、道路和植被的同时描绘总体可信的灾后场景。因此，洪水图像的合成必须明确控制特定事件的变化和预期保持不变的内容。. However, a visually plausible satellite image does not necessarily represent a logically valid transformation of the provided pre-event scene. A generative model may produce convincing water textures while placing inundations in incorrect regions, or it may depict a generally credible post-disaster scene while arbitrarily altering buildings, roads, and vegetation that should remain unchanged. The synthesis of flood imagery must therefore explicitly control both event-specific changes and the content expected to remain invariant.

Public paired flood datasets are limited in scale and fidelity(Khanna et al. 2023; Gupta et al. 2019)，并且名义上的前/后标签本身并不能保证有用的合成对。时间错位、有限的洪水能见度、观测缺失和云污染削弱了可用数据的有效多样性。此外，这些因素损害了评估的可靠性，因为生成的图像可能与视觉变化不明确的目标进行比较。因此，针对洪水的基准应明确验证对有效性，同时确保地理和事件多样性。, and nominal pre/post labels do not inherently guarantee a useful synthesis pair. Temporal misalignment, limited flood visibility, missing observations, and cloud contamination diminish the effective diversity of available data. Furthermore, these factors compromise evaluation reliability because a generated image may be compared against a target whose visual changes are ambiguous. Consequently, a flood-specific benchmark should explicitly verify pair validity while ensuring geographic and event diversity.

为了满足这些要求，FlowForm 将受 SWE 启发的潜在正则化与结构感知调节相结合。具体来说，洪水描述符模块 (FDM) 预测来自扩散瓶颈的潜在深度代理以及辅助流场。有限差分算子评估这些场上的简化稳态 SWE 残差，并将所得残差平方添加到训练目标中。同时，地形锚适配器（TAA）根据扩散时间步长和空间位置自适应地选择相对深度、语义和边缘特征，并将所得的结构表示注入到 U-Net 编码器的四个尺度中。 FDM 在洪水状态特征上运行，而 TAA 通过视觉主干传达持久的场景线索。 FloodScape 提供训练和评估拟议配方所需的一致事件前和事件后图像以及结构条件。To address these requirements, FlowForm combines SWE-inspired latent regularization with structure-aware conditioning. Specifically, the Flood Descriptor Module (FDM) predicts a latent depth proxy alongside auxiliary flow fields from the diffusion bottleneck. Finite-difference operators evaluate simplified steady-state SWE residuals over these fields, and add the resulting squared residuals to the training objective. In parallel, the Terrain Anchor Adapter (TAA) adaptively selects among relative-depth, semantic, and edge features based on the diffusion timestep and spatial location, and injects the resulting structural representation into four scales of the U-Net encoder. While the FDM operates on flood-state features, the TAA conveys persistent scene cues through the visual backbone. FloodScape provides the aligned pre-event and post-event imagery and structural conditions required to train and evaluate the proposed formulation.

6 局限性Limitations

FlowForm 目前将洪水合成制定为从事件前到事件后图像的静态 2D 映射，而真实的洪水随着时间和空间的推移而不断演变。该公式为通过卫星观测了解洪水模式提供了有效的起点。在此基础上，未来的工作可以根据图像序列对洪水的时间演变进行建模，或将框架扩展到 3D 场景，以更丰富地表示地形和水。FlowForm currently formulates flood synthesis as a static 2D mapping from pre- to post-event imagery, while real floods evolve continuously over time and space. This formulation provides an effective starting point for learning inundation patterns from satellite observations. Building on it, future work could model temporal flood evolution from image sequences or extend the framework to 3D scenes for a richer representation of terrain and water.
