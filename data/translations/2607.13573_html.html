IMMNet：基于模型和数据驱动方法的混合融合，用于机动目标跟踪IMMNet: Hybrid Fusion of Model-based and Data-driven Approaches for Maneuvering Target Tracking

Yixuan Zhao zhaoyixuan123321@126.com Chaoqun Yang ycq@seu.edu.cn Lin Gao lingao_1014@126.com Yongxiao Tian tianyongxiao1991@163.com Ting Yuan tyuan@sjtu.edu.cn School of Automation, Southeast University, Nanjing, 210096, China School of ICE, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, Chengdu, 611731, China Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai University of Electric Power, Shanghai, 201300, China School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, 200240, China
摘要Abstract

由于复杂的运动动力学和模型失配，三维空间中的机动目标跟踪仍然是一个具有挑战性的问题。为了解决这个问题，本文提出 名为 IMMNet 的混合模型/数据驱动算法，它将交互多模型 (IMM) 算法的可解释结构与可学习的神经组件集成在一起。与端到端黑盒方法不同，所提出的IMMNet算法不仅可以保留实时雷达应用所必需的贝叶斯推理机制，而且可以自适应地从数据中学习运动模式和噪声特征。 大量实验表明，所提出的 IMMNet 算法在各种场景下始终优于现有算法，验证了其作为机动目标跟踪的稳健、可解释且实用的解决方案。Maneuvering target tracking in three-dimensional space remains a challenging problem due to complex motion dynamics and model mismatch. To address this, this paper proposes a hybrid model/data-driven algorithm named IMMNet, which integrates the interpretable structure of the interacting multiple model (IMM) algorithm with learnable neural components. Unlike end-to-end black-box methods, the proposed IMMNet algorithm not only can preserve the Bayesian inference mechanism that is essential for real-time radar applications, but also can adaptively learn motion patterns and noise characteristics from data. Extensive experiments demonstrate that the proposed IMMNet algorithm consistently outperforms the existing algorithms across various scenarios, validating it as a robust, interpretable, and practical solution for maneuvering target tracking.

1 简介Introduction

近年来，机动目标跟踪（MTT）在雷达监视、自动驾驶车辆和导弹防御领域的广泛应用变得越来越重要Recently, maneuvering target tracking (MTT) has become increasingly essential with its widespread applications in radar surveillance, autonomous vehicles and missile defense[24, 6, 19]。然而，由于目标运动模式高度可变和不可预测的轨迹特征，复杂动态环境中的 MTT 提出了重大挑战. However, MTT in complex dynamic environments poses significant challenges due to highly variable motion modes and unpredictable trajectory characteristics of targets[18, 1, 14]。与那些具有稳态或缓慢变化动态的目标不同，机动目标经常在不同的运动模式之间切换，导致速度和加速度的快速变化. Unlike those targets with steady-state or slowly varying dynamics, maneuvering targets frequently switch between different motion modes, leading to rapid changes in velocity and acceleration[8, 12]。这种行为使得传统的估计算法很难保持稳定和准确的跟踪. Such behaviors make it difficult for traditional estimation algorithms to maintain stable and accurate tracking[10]，从而凸显了对新状态估计算法的迫切需求。, thereby highlighting the critical need for new state estimation algorithms.

为了解决MTT的挑战，已经开发了各种状态估计算法，例如Singer机动模型To address the challenges of MTT, various algorithms of state estimation have been developed such as Singer maneuvering model[15]、输入估计法, input estimation method[3]和交互多模型（IMM）算法and interacting multiple model (IMM) algorithm[2]其中，IMM算法脱颖而出，成为最经典、最有效的解决方案之一。为了应对随时间变化的目标动力学，它将目标运动建模为切换马尔可夫过程，并融合多个并行运动模型的状态估计。然而，上述算法是模型驱动的，这意味着它们严重依赖于精确的状态空间公式以及过程和测量噪声协方差矩阵的准确估计。在涉及模型失配、参数漂移或环境干扰的实际场景中，它们的性能可能会显着下降。, etc. Among them, the IMM algorithm stands out as one of the most classical and effective solutions. To cope with time-varying target dynamics, it models target motion as a switching Markov process and fuses state estimates from multiple parallel motion models. However, the above algorithms are model-driven, which means that they depend critically on precise state-space formulations and accurate estimation of process and measurement noise covariance matrices. In practical scenarios involving model mismatch, parameter drift, or environmental disturbances, their performance may degrade significantly.

为了克服上述模型驱动算法的固有局限性，研究人员越来越多地转向数据驱动算法。例如，刘等人。使用神经网络生成 MTT 经典滤波器估计的校正项To overcome the inherent limitations of the above model-driven algorithms, researchers have increasingly turned to data-driven algorithms. For example, Liu et al. employed neural networks to generate correction terms for estimates of classical filters for MTT[11]。高等人。利用 RNN 直接从原始雷达序列学习目标运动学，无需任何预定义的运动模型. Gao et al. utilized RNNs to learn target kinematics directly from raw radar sequences without any predefined motion models[7]。张等人。提出了一种名为 TrMTT 的编码器-解码器 Transformer 架构. Zhang et al. proposed an encoder-decoder Transformer architecture named TrMTT[22]。数据驱动的算法表现出有希望的优势，特别是对未知噪声和模型失配的强大适应性。然而，其实际应用中仍然存在一些关键挑战。首先，大多数现有的数据驱动算法都存在可解释性有限、参数量过多以及对固定长度观察窗口的依赖等问题，这限制了它们在实时应用中的适用性。此外，数据驱动的算法通常通过黑盒操作确定模型概率，从而降低了其与严格贝叶斯推理原理的一致性。. Data-driven algorithms exhibit promising advantages, particularly strong adaptability to unknown noise and model mismatch. However, several critical challenges still persist in their practical applications. First, most existing data-driven algorithms suffer from limited interpretability, excessive parameter quantities, and dependence on fixed-length observation windows, which restricts their applicability in real-time applications. Furthermore, data-driven algorithms usually determine model probabilities through black-box operations, thereby reducing their consistency with rigorous Bayesian inference principles.

8 结论Conclusion

本文提出了IMMNet算法，这是一种混合模型/数据驱动算法，将IMM算法的结构可解释性与神经网络的自适应学习能力协同结合起来。对大规模 3D 机动目标数据集的大量实验表明，IMMNet 算法在不同运动模式的平均 RMSE 方面始终优于 IMM 算法。这些结果验证了在有原则的多模型架构中嵌入可学习模块的有效性，以减轻模型不匹配并提高跟踪性能。未来，我们将重点增强IMMNet算法的噪声鲁棒性，并将IMMNet算法扩展到多目标场景，以实现更实际、更复杂的跟踪应用。This paper proposed the IMMNet algorithm, a hybrid model/data-driven algorithm that synergistically combines the structural interpretability of the IMM algorithm with the adaptive learning capability of neural networks. Extensive experiments on a large-scale 3D maneuvering target dataset demonstrate that the IMMNet algorithm consistently outperforms the IMM algorithm in terms of average RMSE across diverse motion patterns. These results validate the effectiveness of embedding learnable modules within a principled multi-model architecture to mitigate model mismatch and improve tracking performance. In the future, we will focus on enhancing the noise robustness of the IMMNet algorithm and extending the IMMNet algorithm to multi-target scenarios for more practical and complex tracking applications.
