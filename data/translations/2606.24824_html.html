1 简介Introduction

混沌系统是重要而复杂的非线性物理系统，出现在电气系统中Chaotic systems are important yet complex nonlinear physical systems, arising in electrical systems[1, 2], 气候系统, climate systems[3, 4], 天文学, astronomy[5, 6, 7]，和生物学, and biology[8, 9]。它们内在的复杂性给建模和分析带来了根本性的挑战，主要是因为初始状态的小扰动会随着时间的推移呈指数级放大，如图 1 所示。. Their intrinsic complexity poses fundamental challenges for modelling and analysis, primarily because small perturbations in the initial state can be amplified exponentially over time, as illustrated in Fig.1c，其中轨迹通过顺序扰动初始化, where trajectories initialized with perturbations of order10−610^{-6}迅速发散。 在这项工作中，我们关注混沌系统的逆问题，即从目标最终观测值推断初始条件。这一长期存在的挑战对科学具有广泛的影响rapidly diverge. In this work, we focus on the inverse problem of chaotic systems, namely, inferring initial conditions from target final observations. This long-standing challenge has broad implications in science[10, 11, 12]和工程and engineering[13, 14]，因为恢复初始状态对于理解混沌物理系统的机制和历史至关重要, as recovering initial states is essential for understanding the mechanisms and histories of chaotic physical systems[15]。 尽管其意义重大，但这项任务具有挑战性，主要是由于三个根本原因。首先，此类逆问题是不适定的，这意味着它们的解可能不唯一或不稳定. Despite its significance, this task is challenging, mainly due to three fundamental reasons. First, such inverse problems are ill-posed, meaning that their solutions may be non-unique or unstable[16]。其次，逆问题的目标之一是推断其演化最终状态接近目标最终状态的初始状态。然而，初始状态的误差在前向演化过程中会放大，导致与目标最终状态的偏差更大。第三，当系统的某些主体或组件在进化过程中消失时，逆向推理可能会遭受信息丢失。例如，一颗行星可能会分散到行星系统之外. Second, one of the goals of inverse problems is to infer initial states whose evolved final states are close to target final states. However, errors in the initial states amplify during the forward evolution, leading to larger deviations from the target final states. Third, inverse inference may suffer from information loss when certain bodies or components of the system vanish during evolution. For example, a planet may be scattered out of the planetary system[17, 18, 19]，这会导致其信息丢失，并使重建仅依赖于其余组件。, which causes the loss of its information and makes the reconstruction rely solely on the remaining components.

对于确定性非混沌物理系统，一旦运动控制方程已知，传统数值方法就可以以较小的数值误差推断出初始条件。这些方法的收敛特性确保从目标最终状态开始，对系统进行时间上的向后积分，可以获得初始状态的高保真估计，其前向演化与目标紧密匹配For a deterministic and non-chaotic physical system, once the governing equations of motion are known, traditional numerical methods can infer the initial conditions with small numerical errors. The convergence properties of these methods ensure that by starting from the target final state and integrating the system backward in time, one can obtain a high-fidelity estimate of the initial state whose forward evolution closely matches the target[20, 21, 22]。然而，在混沌系统中，由于确定性非线性动力学将初始条件中的无穷小扰动放大为不可预测的行为，因此出现了随机性。因此，即使方程已知并向后积分，传统的数值方法也无法准确地恢复初始状态。特别是，当向前演化过程中发生信息丢失时，例如，当某些物体或部件从系统中消失时，传统的数值方法可能变得完全不适用。深度学习的最新进展为应对这一挑战开辟了新的可能性。神经网络已经显示出以加速、准确和高效的方式模拟混沌动力学的能力，导致该领域的方法越来越多。然而，现有的研究主要集中在混沌系统的其他任务上，例如预测任务. However, in chaotic systems, randomness arises because deterministic nonlinear dynamics amplify infinitesimal perturbations in the initial conditions into unpredictable behaviors. Consequently, traditional numerical approaches fail to accurately recover the initial state even when the equations are known and integrated backward. In particular, when information loss occurs during forward evolution, for example, when certain bodies or components vanish from the system, traditional numerical methods may become entirely inapplicable. Recent progress in deep learning has opened new possibilities for addressing this challenge. Neural networks have shown the capability to model chaotic dynamics with acceleration, accuracy, and efficiency, leading to a growing number of approaches in this area. However, existing studies mainly focus on other tasks of chaotic systems, such as the prediction task[23, 24, 25, 26]。一些研究也专注于使用深度学习来解决逆问题，但他们并没有关注更具挑战性的混沌系统设置. Several studies have also focused on using deep learning to solve inverse problems, but they have not focused on the more challenging setting of chaotic systems[27, 28, 29, 30, 31].

We analyze and address the inverse problem of chaotic systems systematically. The method builds upon the probabilistic generative model Conditional Flow Matching (CFM)[32, 33]，其优点源于两个关键见解。首先，在计算资源有限的情况下，混沌系统可以视为随机系统, the advantages of which stem from two key insights. First, under limited computational resources, a chaotic system can be regarded as a stochastic system[34, 35, 36]，因此我们了解其状态的分布而不是确定性轨迹。这种概率建模框架也更适合解决不适定问题，因为解决方案可能不是唯一的。 其次，我们直接学习初始状态和最终状态之间的端到端映射，而不是像传统数值求解器那样依赖于时间相关的迭代更新。这种设计避免了沿时间维度重复累积数值误差，使得即使视界长度增加，推理误差也更加可控。 由于没有与视界长度成比例的迭代，该设计减轻了混沌动力学中的误差累积。此外，我们引入了一个双向建模框架，可以共同学习正向和反向动力学，这使得模型能够捕获混沌系统的全局结构，而不是仅仅专注于再现初始状态。通过强制前向演化和时间反向动力学之间的相互一致性，我们的方法减轻了混沌系统中通常出现的前向演化过程中误差的放大。此外，对于受守恒定律约束的物理系统，我们将此框架扩展到 CBi-CFM，它强制学习的概率流路径在守恒定律定义的守恒流形内演化。此扩展使模型能够在混沌条件下生成物理一致的解决方案。, and we therefore learn the distribution of its states rather than the deterministic trajectory. This probabilistic modeling framework is also more suitable for addressing the ill-posed problem, since the solutions may not be unique. Second, we directly learn the end-to-end mapping between initial and final states, rather than relying on time-dependent iterative updates as in traditional numerical solvers. This design avoids repeatedly accumulating numerical errors along the temporal dimension, making the inference error more controllable even as the horizon length increases. Without iterations proportional to the horizon length, this design mitigates the error accumulation in chaotic dynamics. In addition, we introduce a bidirectional modeling framework that jointly learns both forward and reverse dynamics, which allows the model to capture the global structure of chaotic systems rather than focusing solely on reproducing initial states. By enforcing mutual consistency between the forward evolution and the time-reverse dynamics, our approach mitigates the amplification of errors during forward evolution that typically arise in chaotic systems. Furthermore, for physical systems governed by conservation laws, we extend this framework to CBi-CFM, which enforces the learned probability flow path to evolve within the conservation manifold defined by the conservation principles. This extension enables the model to generate physically consistent solutions under chaotic conditions.

4.3 保护法约束Conservation Law Constraint

守恒定律通过约束系统的演化并确保其动力学的一致性，在物理学中发挥着重要作用。根据诺特定理Conservation laws play an important role in physics by constraining the evolution of a system and ensuring the consistency of its dynamics. According to Noether’s theorem[76]，物理系统的每个连续对称性都对应于一个守恒量：平移对称性导致动量守恒，旋转对称性导致角动量守恒，时间不变性导致能量守恒。这些守恒定律不仅简化了复杂的动力学，而且还编码了对称性和物理不变量之间的因果关系。在数据驱动模型中实施此类约束可以引导学习到的动态保持物理一致性。因此，我们提出了一种约束 Bi-CFM 遵循守恒定律的方法，该方法可以应用于任何可以根据观测状态计算守恒量的系统。, every continuous symmetry of a physical system corresponds to a conserved quantity: translational symmetry leads to conservation of momentum, rotational symmetry to conservation of angular momentum, and time invariance to conservation of energy. Enforcing such constraints in data-driven models guides the learned dynamics to remain physically consistent.
