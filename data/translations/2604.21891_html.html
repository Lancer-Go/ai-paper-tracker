用于单位承诺的多阶段热启动深度学习框架A Multi-Stage Warm-Start Deep Learning Framework for Unit Commitment

Muhy Eddin Za’ter, Anna Van Boven, Bri-Mathias Hodge and Kyri Baker
摘要Abstract

保持电力供需之间的瞬时平衡对于可靠性和电网稳定性至关重要。系统运营商通过解决单元承诺（UC）任务来实现这一目标，即高维大规模混合整数线性规划（MILP）问题，该问题严格且严重地受到网格物理约束的控制。随着电网整合可变的可再生能源以及电网中的长期存储等新技术，必须针对多日范围和可能以更高的频率优化解决UC。因此，传统的 MILP 求解器越来越难以在这些严格的操作时间限制内计算解决方案。为了绕过这些计算瓶颈，本文提出了一种新颖的框架，利用基于变压器的架构来预测 72 小时范围内的发电机承诺时间表。此外，由于高维空间中的原始预测通常会产生物理上不可行的结果，因此该管道将自注意力网络与确定性后处理启发式集成，系统地强制执行最短的启动/停机时间并最大限度地减少过剩容量。最后，这些精细的预测被用作下游 MILP 求解器的热启动，同时采用基于置信度的变量固定策略来大幅减少组合搜索空间。完整的多级流水线在单总线测试系统上进行了验证，实现了 100% 的可行性并显着加快了计算时间。值得注意的是，在大约 20% 的测试实例中，所提出的模型达到了可行的操作时间表，并且总体系统成本比仅依赖求解器更低。Maintaining instantaneous balance between electricity supply and demand is critical for reliability and grid instability. System operators achieve this through solving the task of Unit Commitment (UC),ca high dimensional large-scale Mixed-integer Linear Programming (MILP) problem that is strictly and heavily governed by the grid physical constraints. As grid integrate variable renewable sources, and new technologies such as long duration storage in the grid, UC must be optimally solved for multi-day horizons and potentially with greater frequency. Therefore, traditional MILP solvers increasingly struggle to compute solutions within these tightening operational time limits. To bypass these computational bottlenecks, this paper proposes a novel framework utilizing a transformer-based architecture to predict generator commitment schedules over a 72-hour horizon. Also, because raw predictions in highly dimensional spaces often yield physically infeasible results, the pipeline integrates the self-attention network with deterministic post-processing heuristics that systematically enforce minimum up/down times and minimize excess capacity. Finally, these refined predictions are utilized as a warm start for a downstream MILP solver, while employing a confidence-based variable fixation strategy to drastically reduce the combinatorial search space. Validated on a single-bus test system, the complete multi-stage pipeline achieves 100% feasibility and significantly accelerates computation times. Notably, in approximately 20% of test instances, the proposed model reached a feasible operational schedule with a lower overall system cost than relying solely on the solver.

I 简介Introduction

保持电力供需之间的瞬时平衡对于防止电网不稳定和级联停电至关重要Maintaining instantaneous balance between electricity supply and demand is critical to prevent grid instability and cascading blackouts[6]。独立系统运营商 (ISO) 通过单位承诺 (UC) 不断清理批发电力市场来实现这一目标. Independent System Operators (ISOs) achieve this by continuously clearing wholesale electricity markets through Unit Commitment (UC)[5]，一项优化挑战，确定在预定时间范围内发电机的最佳二进制启动和关闭顺序。由于操作决策是暂时耦合的并受到严格的物理约束的控制，因此 UC 被表述为大规模混合整数线性规划 (MILP) 问题, an optimization challenge that determines the optimal binary startup and shutdown sequences of generators over a scheduled time horizon. Because operational decisions are temporally coupled and governed by strict physical constraints, UC is formulated as a large-scale Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) problem[1]。 UC 的计算复杂度随着生成器的数量呈指数级增长，使其成为一个 NP 难题. The computational complexity of UC scales exponentially with the number of generators, making it an NP-hard problem[3].

电网脱碳目标进一步加剧了这一瓶颈。向太阳能和风能等可变和不确定电力来源的转变引入了复杂的随机变量，并且需要更多限制性模型来补偿电网转动惯量的减小This bottleneck is further exacerbated by grid decarbonization goals. This transition toward variable and uncertain electricity sources such as solar and wind introduces complex stochastic variables and requires more restrictive models to compensate for the decreasing rotational inertia of the grid[2]。因此，传统的 MILP 求解器越来越难以在 ISO 要求的严格操作时间限制内计算解决方案. Consequently, traditional MILP solvers increasingly struggle to compute solutions within the strict operational time limits required by ISOs[14]。此外，随着电网规划不断发展以评估长期储能和多日天气事件的影响，运营商必须不断将 UC 范围从标准的 24 小时日前市场扩展到更长的时期. Furthermore, as grid planning evolves to evaluate the impact of long-duration energy storage and multi-day weather events, operators must increasingly expand the UC horizon from the standard 24-hour day-ahead markets to longer periods[18]。所有上述方面都需要努力研究替代解决方案，以实现更快的统一通信。由于 UC 是通过相同的结构拓扑和不同的外源数据输入反复求解的，因此机器学习 (ML) 已成为绕过这些计算限制的潜在新变革范式。. All of the above-mentioned aspects necessitate research efforts toward alternative solutions toward faster UC. Because UC is solved repeatedly with identical structural topologies but varying exogenous data inputs, Machine Learning (ML) has emerged as a potential new transformative paradigm to bypass these computational limits.

虽然机器学习通过预测发电机开/关状态来提供显着的加速，从而将计算成本昂贵的 MILP 转换为更快的线性程序 (LP)，但这个高维空间中的微小统计误差很容易产生物理上不可行的结果While ML offers significant speedups by predicting generator on/off statuses, thereby transforming the computationally expensive MILP into a faster Linear Program (LP), minor statistical errors in this highly dimensional space easily yield physically infeasible results[22, 12]。为了弥补这一差距，文献提出了各种方法. To bridge this gap, the literature proposes various methods[9]，包括通过特定架构完全绕过 MIP 求解器, including entirely bypassing the MIP solver via specific architectures[7, 17, 21]，或通过仅预测具有高预测置信度的生成器的状态来降低 MILP 维数, or reducing MILP dimensionality by predicting statuses only for generators with high prediction confidence[15]，利用预测作为求解器热启动, utilizing predictions as solver warm-starts[11]，或引入确定性后处理启发法来恢复可行性, or introducing deterministic post-processing heuristics to restore feasibility[14]。然而，一个关键的限制仍然存在：传统的神经架构难以处理极高维的状态空间，同时有效地捕获复杂的时间耦合，例如多小时的斜坡约束和最小的上升/下降时间. However, a critical limitation remains: traditional neural architectures struggle to process extremely high-dimensional state spaces while effectively capturing complex temporal couplings, such as multi-hour ramping constraints and minimum up/down times[10]。当扩展到更长的视野时，这种缺陷非常明显，可能导致次优、不可行和计算增益降低。. This shortfall is highly evident when expanding to longer horizons, potentially leading to sub-optimality, infeasibility, and reduced computational gains.

为了解决这些限制，我们提出了一种新颖的框架，该框架采用基于变压器的架构来本地学习未来多天的深度时间依赖性，并利用最近文献中最有效的基于机器学习的策略To address these limitations, we propose a novel framework that employs a transformer-based architecture to natively learn deep temporal dependencies over a multi-day ahead horizon, and utilizes the most effective ML-based strategies from the recent literature[22, 12, 10]。通过协同我们的自注意力基础序列建模网络. By synergizing our self-attention base sequence-modeling network[19]通过后处理启发式、预测性热启动和目标变量固定，我们的流程在长期内弥合了原始统计预测和 UC 问题的严格物理约束之间的差距。with post-processing heuristics, predictive warm-start, and targeted variable fixation, our pipeline bridges the gap between raw statistical predictions and the strict physical constraints of the UC problem over a long horizon.

IV 结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种基于机器学习的管道，用于提前 3 天的每小时单位承诺。所提出的方法包括三个阶段；第一阶段是自注意力模型，然后是后处理启发式步骤，其输出作为 UC MILP 求解器的热启动。所提出的方法在代表爱尔兰系统的单个总线系统上进行了验证，并与其他机器学习方法进行了比较。实验结果清楚地证明了使用机器学习方法作为 UC 求解器的热启动所获得的显着计算速度。This paper presents a machine learning based pipeline for 3 day-ahead hourly unit commitment. The proposed methods consists of three stages; the first stage is a self-attention model, followed by a post-processing heuristics step, which outputs are fed as a warm start for a UC MILP solver. The proposed method was validated on a single bus system that represents the Irish system, and compared against other machine learning methods. The results of the experiments clearly demonstrated the significant computation speed gained from using machine learning methods as a warm-start for UC solvers.

应该承认本文有一些明显的局限性。如前所述，假设需求、风能和太阳能是完美的预测。包括预测的不确定性将需要进行调整以保证可行性。此外，这项工作基于铜板模型，不包括传输约束，因此无法得出该提议的管道在更复杂的网络上有多成功的结论。 未来的工作应该将该框架应用于各种更大的电力系统网络，并纳入更准确的数据和约束。There are a few notable limitations to this paper that should be acknowledged. As mentioned, the demand, wind, and solar are assumed to be perfect forecasts. Including forecast uncertainty would require adjustments to guarantee feasibility. Furthermore, this work is based on a copper-plate model and does not include transmission constraints, so conclusions cannot be drawn as to how successful this proposed pipeline would be on a more complex network. Future work should apply this framework to various larger power systems networks and incorporate more accurate data and constraints.
