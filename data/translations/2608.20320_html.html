主动数据收集、出行行为建模和天气敏感需求预测的代理方法An Agentic Approach for Active Data Collection, Travel Behavior Modeling, and Weather-Sensitive Demand Prediction

纳尔吉斯·艾哈迈迪Narges Ahmadi一个，*a,*, 焦玉波, Yubo Jiaoa, 乔纳塔斯·奥古斯托·曼佐利, Jônatas Augusto Manzollia, 于江波, Jiangbo Yua, 路易斯·米兰达-莫雷诺, Luis Miranda-Morenoa

a麦吉尔大学土木工程系， 加拿大魁北克省蒙特利尔Department of Civil Engineering, McGill University, Montreal, Quebec, Canada

 

摘要Abstract

出行行为研究越来越多地将数字数据收集与预测建模结合起来，但这些阶段通常是单独开发和评估的。本研究提出并应用了一种集成会话数据收集、结构化数据处理和行为预测的三代理工作流程。一项由聊天机器人管理、图像增强的陈述偏好调查收集了 92 名学生通勤者在五个预定义天气场景中的模式选择，产生了 454 个受访者场景观察结果。使用多项 Logit 模型分析与天气相关的关联，而逻辑回归和随机森林提供了机器学习基准。九个本地部署的大型语言模型 (LLM)，参数范围从 2 到 350 亿个参数，在四个零样本提示和上下文条件下进行了评估，并通过角色、少数样本和基于视觉的配置进一步扩展。骑自行车对恶劣天气特别敏感，而在下雪的情况下，公共交通的使用量大幅增加。随机森林实现了 69.6% 的五级准确率，而最好的纯文本零样本 LLM 在没有特定任务拟合的情况下达到了 69.9%。习惯性旅行信息在法学硕士预测方面产生了最一致的改进，专家框架通常优于角色扮演，并且当习惯性旅行信息不可用时，角色信息是最有用的。少样本提示进一步改进了多个模型的五类预测，在少量示例后，收益通常趋于稳定。还使用向受访者提供的相同天气图像来评估具有视觉功能的模型，并且基于视觉的最佳配置达到了最高的观察到的五级准确度 71.5%，这表明视觉上下文可以为所选模型提供额外的预测信息。总而言之，研究结果表明，习惯性旅行信息、提示策略、演示和视觉背景可以有意义地塑造基于法学硕士的旅行选择预测。更广泛地说，该研究展示了如何在单个可审计的多代理工作流程中协调对话式调查管理、结构化数据处理、传统行为建模、机器学习和多模式法学硕士预测，而更广泛的应用需要使用更大、更具代表性的旅行者样本进行验证。Travel-behavior research increasingly combines digital data collection with predictive modeling, yet these stages are often developed and evaluated separately. This study proposes and applies a three-agent workflow integrating conversational data collection, structured data processing, and behavioral prediction. A chatbot-administered, image-augmented stated-preference survey collected mode choices from 92 student commuters across five predefined weather scenarios, yielding 454 respondent–scenario observations. Weather-related associations were analyzed using a multinomial logit model, while logistic regression and random forest provided machine-learning benchmarks. Nine locally deployed large language models (LLMs), ranging from 2 to 35 billion parameters, were evaluated across four zero-shot prompt-and-context conditions and further extended through persona, few-shot, and vision-based configurations. Cycling was particularly sensitive to adverse weather, while public transit use increased substantially under the Snowy scenario. Random forest achieved 69.6% five-class accuracy, while the best text-only zero-shot LLM reached 69.9% without task-specific fitting. Habitual travel information produced the most consistent improvement in LLM prediction, Expert framing generally outperformed Role-Play, and persona information was most beneficial when habitual travel information was unavailable. Few-shot prompting further improved five-class prediction for several models, with gains generally stabilizing after a small number of examples. Vision-capable models were also evaluated using the same weather images presented to respondents, and the best vision-based configuration reached the highest observed five-class accuracy of 71.5%, indicating that visual context can provide additional predictive information for selected models. Taken together, the findings show that habitual travel information, prompting strategy, demonstrations, and visual context can meaningfully shape LLM-based travel-choice prediction. More broadly, the study demonstrates how conversational survey administration, structured data processing, conventional behavioral modeling, machine learning, and multimodal LLM prediction can be coordinated within a single auditable multi-agent workflow, while broader application requires validation with larger and more representative traveler samples.

1 简介Introduction

了解和预测出行行为是交通规划的基本组成部分，包括有关个人何时、何地以及如何出行的相关决策。在这些决策中，模式选择尤为重要，因为它决定了需求在交通系统中的分配方式，并影响基础设施需求、环境影响以及旨在鼓励可持续出行的政策的有效性Understanding and predicting travel behavior is a fundamental component of transportation planning, encompassing interrelated decisions regarding when, where, and how individuals travel. Among these decisions, mode choice is particularly consequential because it determines how demand is distributed across transportation systems and influences infrastructure needs, environmental impacts, and the effectiveness of policies aimed at encouraging sustainable mobility(31; 4)。模式选择不仅取决于个人和特定选择的特征，还取决于旅行发生的背景。天气是一个重要的例子，因为降水、极端温度和不利的路况会改变步行和骑自行车的吸引力，并使旅行者转向公共交通或私家车，其影响因个人和季节而异. Mode choice is shaped not only by individual and alternative-specific characteristics but also by the context in which travel occurs. Weather is an important example, as precipitation, extreme temperatures, and adverse road conditions can alter the attractiveness of walking and cycling and shift travelers toward transit or private vehicles, with effects that vary across individuals and seasons(21; 7; 37)。因此，理解这种依赖于上下文的响应需要能够在观察到的旅行数据可能无法充分表示的条件下捕获异构旅行行为的方法。. Understanding such context-dependent responses therefore requires approaches capable of capturing heterogeneous travel behavior under conditions that may not be adequately represented in observed travel data.

收集捕捉这些情境变化的行为数据仍然具有挑战性。传统的旅行调查，无论是通过访谈、纸质问卷还是静态网络表格进行，实施和扩展成本高昂且耗时Collecting behavioral data that capture these contextual variations remains challenging. Traditional travel surveys, whether administered through interviews, paper questionnaires, or static web forms, can be costly and time-consuming to implement and scale(24; 3)。陈述偏好（SP）调查提供了一种重要的替代方案，允许研究人员在受控或假设条件下检查选择，包括在显示偏好数据中可能难以系统观察的天气场景。然而，传统的 SP 工具通常使用固定的文本描述来描述此类情况，要求受访者自己构建预期的上下文，并限制了更丰富的上下文信息的结合. Stated-preference (SP) surveys provide an important alternative by allowing researchers to examine choices under controlled or hypothetical conditions, including weather scenarios that may be difficult to observe systematically in revealed-preference data. However, conventional SP instruments typically describe such situations using fixed textual descriptions, requiring respondents to construct the intended context themselves and limiting the incorporation of richer contextual information(11)。这对于依赖天气的旅行选择尤其重要，因为降水、能见度、积雪或道路条件等视觉条件可能构成决策环境的重要组成部分。这些限制激发了数据收集方法的发展，这些方法可以将可扩展的调查部署与更丰富、更一致的旅行环境表示相结合。. This is particularly relevant for weather-dependent travel choices, for which visual conditions such as precipitation, visibility, snow accumulation, or road conditions may form an important part of the decision environment. These limitations motivate data-collection approaches that can combine scalable survey deployment with richer and more consistent representations of travel contexts.

人工智能 (AI) 的最新进展为解决这些限制提供了新的可能性。对话式聊天机器人可以通过引导式交互来管理调查，纳入受访者之前的答案，并将多媒体内容直接嵌入到调查体验中，从而有可能实现更灵活、更有吸引力的行为数据收集形式Recent advances in artificial intelligence (AI) provide new possibilities for addressing these limitations. Conversational chatbots can administer surveys through guided interactions, incorporate respondents’ previous answers, and embed multimedia content directly within the survey experience, potentially enabling more flexible and engaging forms of behavioral data collection(44; 35; 23)。同时，生成式人工智能工具可以支持预定义视觉场景的构建，而大型语言模型（LLM）则可以实现自然语言交互和异构信息处理。这些功能共同创造了超越静态、基于文本的调查问卷，转向对话式和上下文丰富的行为调查的机会。然而，尽管对话式人工智能在调查研究中的应用越来越多，但在旅行行为和模式选择研究中，基于聊天机器人的数据收集在很大程度上仍未得到探索，特别是对于集成图像增强上下文信息的陈述偏好实验。. At the same time, generative AI tools can support the construction of predefined visual scenarios, while large language models (LLMs) enable natural-language interaction and the processing of heterogeneous information. Together, these capabilities create opportunities to move beyond static, text-based questionnaires toward conversational and context-rich behavioral surveys. Despite growing applications of conversational AI in survey research, however, chatbot-based data collection remains largely unexplored in travel-behavior and mode-choice research, particularly for stated-preference experiments that integrate image-augmented contextual information.

旅行选择建模也取得了并行进展。基于随机效用理论的离散选择模型 (DCM) 仍然是模式选择分析的核心，因为它们具有可解释性和估计行为有意义的关系的能力Parallel advances have occurred in travel-choice modeling. Discrete choice models (DCMs), grounded in random-utility theory, remain central to mode-choice analysis because of their interpretability and ability to estimate behaviorally meaningful relationships(31; 4; 39)。随后，机器学习 (ML) 方法通过捕获可能难以以参数方式指定的非线性关系和复杂交互，扩展了预测工具包. Machine-learning (ML) methods have subsequently expanded the predictive toolkit by capturing nonlinear relationships and complex interactions that may be difficult to specify parametrically(18; 1)。最近，法学硕士引入了一种不同的范式，在这种范式中，他们不是估计解释变量和选择之间的固定数学映射，而是可以解释通过自然语言提示表达的旅行者特征和上下文信息，并在有限或没有特定任务训练的情况下生成个人级别的预测. More recently, LLMs have introduced a different paradigm in which, rather than estimating a fixed mathematical mapping between explanatory variables and choices, they can interpret traveler characteristics and contextual information expressed through natural-language prompts and generate individual-level predictions with limited or no task-specific training(45; 10)。这种灵活性还使得可以改变提供给模型的信息；例如，通过替代提示配置，人口统计特征、旅行历史、先前决策的示例或上下文描述。然而，出行行为预测中有两个重要问题仍未得到充分研究。第一个问题涉及系统设计的法学硕士配置和提示策略中预测性能如何变化。第二个问题是，具有视觉能力的法学硕士是否可以直接纳入旅行条件的视觉表示，例如向人类受访者呈现的相同天气图像，而不是完全依赖于文本描述。. This flexibility also makes it possible to vary the information provided to the model; for example, demographic characteristics, travel history, examples of previous decisions, or contextual descriptions, through alternative prompting configurations. Yet two important questions remain insufficiently examined in travel-behavior prediction. The first concerns how prediction performance changes across systematically designed LLM configurations and prompting strategies. The second is whether vision-capable LLMs can directly incorporate visual representations of travel conditions, such as the same weather images presented to human respondents, rather than relying exclusively on textual descriptions.

超出预测的是，法学硕士越来越多地被研究为Beyond prediction, LLMs have increasingly been studied as综合受访者synthetic respondents，作为个体的计算表示，根据特定的角色和选择上下文生成决策。这一系列研究最初是在经济学、政治学和调查研究中发展起来的，其中法学硕士生成的反应与人类决策进行了比较, serving as computational representations of individuals that generate decisions conditioned on specified personas and choice contexts. This line of research initially developed in economics, political science, and survey research, where LLM-generated responses have been compared with human decisions(19; 2)。最近的交通研究同样表明，根据旅行者特征、行为历史或潜在态度来调整法学硕士可以改善他们与观察到的旅行选择的对应关系. Recent transportation studies similarly suggest that conditioning LLMs on traveler characteristics, behavioral histories, or latent attitudes can improve their correspondence with observed travel choices(29; 36)。一个相关的发展是多代理系统的出现，其中专门的或基于角色的 LLM 代理执行互补功能或大规模代表异构决策者. A related development is the emergence of multi-agent systems in which specialized or persona-based LLM agents perform complementary functions or represent heterogeneous decision-makers at scale(29; 28; 13)。然而，LLM 生成的行为并不一定能够可靠地再现人类决策，特别是在有限上下文或零样本设置下. Nevertheless, LLM-generated behavior does not necessarily reproduce human decision-making reliably, particularly under limited-context or zero-shot settings(26; 6; 20)。尽管在其他行为领域取得了这些进展，但用于数据收集、数据处理和基于 LLM 的行为预测的专用代理集成在一个共同的系统中。. 2 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

2.1 出行行为与方式选择Travel Behavior and Mode Choice

模式选择是交通研究中最古老、模型化程度最高的问题之一，几十年来多项 Logit (MNL) 模型一直是其主导框架Mode choice is one of the oldest and most heavily modeled problems in transportation research, and the multinomial logit (MNL) model has been its dominant framework for decades(31; 4)。基于随机效用理论，MNL 及其扩展（例如嵌套和混合 Logit）仍然是估计旅行时间、成本和个人特征如何转化为离散旅行选择的标准工具，很大程度上是因为它们的系数承认直接的行为解释. Grounded in random utility theory, MNL and its extensions such as nested and mixed logit remain the standard tool for estimating how travel time, cost, and individual characteristics translate into a discrete travel choice, in large part because their coefficients admit a direct behavioral interpretation(39)。机器学习 (ML) 分类器，包括随机森林、梯度增强和支持向量机，已成为离散选择模型日益常见的补充。比较研究一再发现 ML 分类器在预测准确性方面可以匹配或超过 MNL，特别是当特征集很大或选择集不平衡时，尽管生成的模型更难用行为术语来解释. Machine learning (ML) classifiers, including random forests, gradient boosting, and support vector machines, have become an increasingly common complement to discrete choice models. Comparative studies have repeatedly found that ML classifiers can match or exceed MNL on predictive accuracy, particularly when the feature set is large or the choice set is imbalanced, though the resulting models are harder to interpret in behavioral terms(18; 1).

这两个模型家庭都面临着共同的挑战：旅行者的偏好不是固定的，而是随着天气、季节、建筑环境和个体异质性而变化。天气与主动模式选择之间的关联尤其明显，寒冷、潮湿和能见度低的条件会抑制步行和骑自行车，并使旅客转向乘坐公共交通工具或驾车Both model families face a common challenge: traveler preferences are not fixed but vary with weather, season, the built environment, and individual heterogeneity. Weather in particular has a well-documented association with active-mode choice, with cold, wet, and low-visibility conditions suppressing walking and cycling and shifting travelers toward transit or driving(7; 37; 21)。捕捉这种敏感性通常需要直接改变条件的陈述偏好（SP）方法，因为显示偏好数据可能无法涵盖受访者可能遇到的所有天气范围. Capturing this sensitivity often requires stated-preference (SP) methods that vary conditions directly, because revealed-preference data may not span the full range of weather a respondent might encounter(3; 24)。然而，MNL 和 ML 模型都需要标记数据，当应用条件与模型开发期间所呈现的条件不同时，它们的预测性能可能会恶化。这种限制激发了人们对基于 LLM 的选择预测日益增长的兴趣（第 2.3 节中进行了回顾）。. Both MNL and ML models, however, require labeled data, and their predictive performance may deteriorate when application conditions differ from those represented during model development. This limitation motivates the growing interest, reviewed in Section 2.3, in LLM-based choice prediction.

2.2 出行行为研究中的数据收集方法Data Collection Methods in Travel Behavior Research

传统上，旅行行为数据是通过纸质日记、电话采访和静态网络调查来收集的。每种工具都会权衡成本、覆盖范围和深度；例如，纸质调查的处理是劳动密集型的，电话访谈的规模化成本很高，而静态网络表格虽然分发成本低廉，但无论每个受访者的答案如何，都会向他们呈现相同的固定问题序列Travel behavior data has traditionally been collected through paper diaries, telephone interviews, and static web-based surveys. 6 讨论Discussion

结果展示了如何在旅行行为研究的通用多智能体工作流程中连接会话数据收集、传统行为模型和基于法学硕士的预测。除了任何单个模型的性能之外，该研究还为三个更广泛的问题提供了证据：人工智能辅助调查是否可以捕获有意义的上下文相关旅行行为，哪些形式的信息对于基于法学硕士的模式选择预测最有用，以及这些模型如何与现有的 DCM 和 ML 方法进行比较。The results demonstrate how conversational data collection, conventional behavioral models, and LLM-based prediction can be connected within a common multi-agent workflow for travel-behavior research. Beyond the performance of any individual model, the study provides evidence on three broader questions: whether an AI-assisted survey can capture meaningful context-dependent travel behavior, which forms of information are most useful for LLM-based mode-choice prediction, and how these models compare with established DCMs and ML approaches.

多代理工作流程和行为证据。Multi-agent workflow and behavioral evidence.该工作流程通过结构化输出连接数据收集、数据处理和数据建模代理，创建从调查管理到行为预测的透明和模块化流程。该组织本身并不能提高准确性；相反，它可以实现一致的数据传输和对性能提升的地方进行系统评估。聊天机器人调查在不同的天气情况下产生了一致的差异：随着情况恶化，自行车和自行车+交通的数量有所减少，而公共交通的份额却增加了很多。 MNL 估计与这些描述性模式一致，表明在不利情况下，自行车相关模式的相对效用较低，而交通和驾驶的相对效用较高。该协议支持调查响应的内部一致性，尽管它本身并没有建立外部有效性或观测天气下的实际旅行行为。The workflow links the Data Collection, Data Processing, and Data Modeling Agents through structured outputs, creating a transparent and modular process from survey administration to behavioral prediction. This organization does not improve accuracy by itself; rather, it enables consistent data transfer and systematic evaluation of where performance gains arise. The chatbot survey produced coherent differences across the weather scenarios: cycling and bike+transit declined as conditions worsened, whereas public transit gained substantial share. The MNL estimates were consistent with these descriptive patterns, showing lower relative utility for cycling-related modes and higher relative utility for transit and driving under adverse scenarios. This agreement supports the internal coherence of the survey responses, although it does not by itself establish external validity or actual travel behavior under observed weather.

LLM配置以及与传统模型的比较。LLM configuration and comparison with conventional models.LLM 的表现很大程度上受到提示中包含的信息的影响。 RC 将习惯性出行信息添加到 BC 中，产生了最一致的改进，表明习惯性出行行为包含人口统计和流动性资源未完全捕获的预测信息。 EXP 框架通常优于 RP，特别是对于较小的模型，而在较大的模型中，增加模型大小带来的收益会减少。LLM performance was strongly influenced by the information included in the prompt. RC, which adds habitual travel information to BC, produced the most consistent improvement, showing that habitual travel behavior contains predictive information not fully captured by demographics and mobility resources. EXP framing generally outperformed RP, particularly for smaller models, while gains from increasing model size diminished among larger models.

当角色增强和几次提示提供缺失的信息时，它们是最有用的。角色描述主要在 BC 下改进了预测，因为习惯性旅行信息不可用，但在 RC 下几乎没有增加。少数样本示例提高了多个模型（尤其是较小模型）的五级精度，尽管在少量演示后增益通常会稳定下来。Persona augmentation and few-shot prompting were most useful when they supplied missing information. Persona descriptions improved prediction mainly under BC, where habitual travel information was unavailable, but added little under RC. Few-shot examples improved five-class accuracy for several models, particularly smaller ones, although the gains generally stabilized after a small number of demonstrations.

因此，传统模式和基于法学硕士的模式发挥着互补作用。 MNL 提供行为解释，而逻辑回归和随机森林则提供经过数据训练的预测基准。随机森林实现了最高的训练模型精度，而最好的零样本法学硕士则产生了可比较的五类点估计，而无需针对调查记录进行特定任务的拟合。这一结果表明，当标记的旅行行为数据有限时，基于 LLM 的预测具有潜在价值，尽管小样本和多个评估配置需要谨慎解释。五类任务可以更清晰地评估模型是否区分各个模式。The conventional and LLM-based models therefore play complementary roles. The MNL provides behavioral interpretation, while logistic regression and random forest offer data-trained predictive benchmarks. Random forest achieved the highest trained-model accuracy, while the best zero-shot LLM produced a comparable five-class point estimate without task-specific fitting to the survey records. This result suggests potential value for LLM-based prediction when labeled travel-behavior data are limited, although the small sample and multiple evaluated configurations require cautious interpretation. The five-class task provides a clearer assessment of whether models distinguish among individual modes.

基于视觉的预测。Vision-based prediction.视觉实验通过向模型提供向受访者显示的相同生成的天气图像，直接连接多智能体工作流程的调查和预测阶段。基于视觉的最佳配置实现了 71.5% 的五级准确度点估计，而最高纯文本零样本 LLM 结果为 69.9%，随机森林为 69.6%。这些微小差异应被解释为描述性点估计差异，而不是统计优势的证据。视觉与少量镜头提示相结合也为选定的模型带来了收益。由于每种天气状况都由一张固定图像表示，因此结果表明这些特定图像为所选模型提供了额外的预测信号；它们没有对看不见的天气图像或视觉环境进行概括。The vision experiments directly connect the survey and prediction stages of the multi-agent workflow by providing the models with the same generated weather images shown to respondents. The best vision-based configuration achieved a five-class accuracy point estimate of 71.5%, compared with 69.9% for the highest text-only zero-shot LLM result and 69.6% for random forest. These small differences should be interpreted as descriptive point-estimate differences rather than evidence of statistical superiority. Vision combined with few-shot prompting also produced gains for selected models. Because each weather condition was represented by one fixed image, the results indicate that these specific images provided additional predictive signal for selected models; they do not establish generalization to unseen weather images or visual environments.

局限性和未来的研究。Limitations and future research.研究结果应根据一些局限性来解释。该样本由来自一所大学的 92 名学生组成，并不代表更广泛的人群。该分析基于既定的选择，而不是在实际天气条件下观察到的旅行，生成的图像中不受控制的视觉特征可能会影响受访者和模型。每种天气状况都由一张固定图像来表示，因此天气状况和图像身份不能单独识别。相对较小且不平衡的数据集也限制了对少数群体模式和旅行者亚组的详细分析，而结果可能因其他法学硕士系列和推理设置而异。该框架本身没有与传统的研究工作流程进行实验比较；因此，它的贡献涉及集成、可追溯性和模块化，而不是测量成本或处理工作量的减少。此外，配置比较是探索性的，报告的最高准确度可能部分反映了对同一样本的多个模型和提示设置的评估。未来的研究可以使用更大、更多样化的样本、额外的旅行目的和地点以及显示偏好验证来评估工作流程。受控图像实验可以隔离天气、路况和周围环境的影响。进一步的工作还可以检查预测校准、亚组表现和跨人群的可转移性。多代理工作流程可以支持自适应调查和仔细验证的综合受访者，但在用于交通规划或政策分析之前，应根据人类行为验证此类输出。The findings should be interpreted in light of several limitations. The sample consists of 92 students from one university and is not representative of the wider population. The analysis is based on stated choices rather than observed travel under actual weather conditions, and uncontrolled visual features within the generated images may have influenced both respondents and models. Each weather condition was represented by one fixed image, so weather condition and image identity were not separately identified. The relatively small and imbalanced dataset also limits detailed analysis of minority modes and traveler subgroups, while the results may vary across other LLM families and inference settings. The framework itself was not experimentally compared with a conventional research workflow; its contribution therefore concerns integration, traceability, and modularity rather than a measured reduction in survey cost or processing effort. In addition, the configuration comparisons were exploratory, and the highest reported accuracy may partly reflect the evaluation of multiple models and prompt settings on the same sample. Future research could evaluate the workflow with larger and more diverse samples, additional trip purposes and locations, and revealed-preference validation. Controlled image experiments could isolate the effects of weather, road condition, and surrounding environment. Further work could also examine prediction calibration, subgroup performance, and transferability across populations. The multi-agent workflow could support adaptive surveys and carefully validated synthetic respondents, but such outputs should be validated against human behavior before being used in transportation planning or policy analysis.

7 结论Conclusions

这项研究开发并评估了一个多代理工作流程，该工作流程将对话式调查设计、人工智能生成的视觉场景、结构化数据处理、传统选择和机器学习模型以及基于法学硕士的预测集成在统一的旅行行为研究框架内。该工作流程使用 92 名麦吉尔学生通勤者在五个图像增强天气场景中的规定模式选择进行了演示。This study developed and evaluated a multi-agent workflow that integrates conversational survey design, AI-generated visual scenarios, structured data processing, conventional choice and machine-learning models, and LLM-based prediction within a unified travel-behavior research framework. The workflow was demonstrated using stated mode choices from 92 McGill student commuters across five image-augmented weather scenarios.

实证结果支持三个主要结论。首先，对话式调查在不同天气条件下产生了行为一致的反应，恶劣天气减少了与自行车相关的选择，并增加了对公共交通的依赖。其次，MNL、随机森林和基于 LLM 的预测因子的比较凸显了它们的互补作用。 MNL 提供了可解释的行为关系，随机森林提供了强大的训练有素的预测基准，LLM 可以灵活地整合习惯性旅行信息、角色描述、示例和视觉上下文，而无需为每种信息类型提供单独的模型规范。第三，法学硕士的表现在很大程度上取决于提示设计和输入配置。习惯性旅行信息和以专家为导向的框架通常可以改善预测，而选定的具有视觉功能的模型则受益于向受访者显示的相同天气图像。最好的零样本法学硕士取得了与经过训练的机器学习基准相当的点估计，而无需针对调查观察结果进行特定任务的拟合。The empirical results support three main conclusions. First, the conversational survey produced behaviorally coherent responses across weather conditions, with adverse weather reducing cycling-related choices and increasing reliance on public transit. Second, the comparison of MNL, random forest, and LLM-based predictors highlighted their complementary roles. MNL provided interpretable behavioral relationships, random forest offered a strong trained predictive benchmark, and LLMs enabled flexible incorporation of habitual travel information, persona descriptions, examples, and visual context without requiring a separate model specification for each information type. Third, LLM performance depended strongly on prompt design and input configuration. Habitual travel information and expert-oriented framing generally improved prediction, while selected vision-capable models benefited from the inclusion of the same weather images shown to respondents. The best zero-shot LLM achieved a point estimate comparable to the trained machine-learning benchmark without task-specific fitting to the survey observations.

因此，这项研究的主要贡献不是取代已建立的旅行选择模型，而是展示了一个集成的、可扩展的工作流程，其中数据收集、处理、行为分析、预测建模和多模态推理可以通过专门的代理进行协调。该框架为将异构行为信息纳入旅行选择分析提供了实用基础，同时保留了用于解释、基准测试和验证的传统模型。更广泛地说，研究结果表明，法学硕士可以扩展交通研究中旅行者特征和背景条件的表达方式，前提是根据观察到的人类行为对他们的输出进行系统评估。The main contribution of this study is therefore not the replacement of established travel-choice models, but the demonstration of an integrated and extensible workflow in which data collection, processing, behavioral analysis, predictive modeling, and multimodal reasoning can be coordinated through specialized agents. This framework provides a practical basis for incorporating heterogeneous behavioral information into travel-choice analysis while retaining conventional models for interpretation, benchmarking, and validation. More broadly, the findings indicate that LLMs may expand how traveler characteristics and contextual conditions are represented in transportation research, provided that their outputs are systematically evaluated against observed human behavior.
