1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展在一般任务中表现出了出色的性能，然而，将其应用于教育、法律或 STEM 学科等高度专业化的领域仍然很困难，这主要是由于人类专家注释的稀缺。Recent advances in Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable performances in general tasks, however, adapting them to highly specialized domains such as education, law, or STEM subjects, remains difficult, which mainly due to the scarcity of human expert annotations.

为了缓解专家注释的稀缺性，最近的工作探索了无注释的自我训练，其中模型仅使用来自模型本身的未标记数据和信号来改进自身。 基于 SFT 的方法（例如 LMSI）根据其生成的响应或基本原理对模型进行微调To mitigate the scarcity of expert annotations, recent work has explored annotation-free self-training, where the model improves itself using only unlabeled data and signals derived from the model itself. SFT-based methods such as LMSI fine-tune the model on its own generated responses or rationales(Huang et al., 2023)。 相反，基于 GRPO 的方法根据模型自身的输出构建标量奖励。 TTRL 从多次推出的多数投票中提取伪标签，并使用 GRPO 算法对其进行优化. GRPO-based methods instead construct scalar rewards from the model’s own outputs. TTRL extracts pseudo-labels from majority voting over multiple rollouts and optimizes them with the GRPO algorithm(Zuo et al., 2025)，而 Intuitor 使用基于熵的置信信号作为奖励, while Intuitor uses entropy-based confidence signals as rewards(Zhao et al., 2025b)。 尽管取得了实证上的成功，这些无注释范式仍然面临着根本的局限性。基于 SFT 的自我训练可能会遭受灾难性遗忘，尤其是对于域外任务。基于 GRPO 的优化面临着组内奖励方差低下梯度消失和长时间训练过程中熵崩溃等挑战。更根本的是，GRPO 将每次推出都分解为标量奖励，仅产生轨迹级别的监督，并且不提供关于应加强或修改推理的哪一部分的指导。. Despite their empirical successes, these annotation-free paradigms still face fundamental limitations. SFT-based self-training can suffer from catastrophic forgetting, especially for out-of domain tasks. GRPO-based optimization faces challenges such as vanishing gradients under low within-group reward variance and entropy collapse during prolonged training. More fundamentally, GRPO collapses each rollout into a scalar reward, yielding only trajectory-level supervision and offering no guidance on which part of the reasoning should be reinforced or revised.

在策略蒸馏（OPD）通过针对教师模型的令牌级预测分布而不是严格的最终答案奖励来训练学生模型，从而提供更精细的监督。这种逻辑级别的目标沿着生成轨迹加强监督，并避免因组内奖励差异低而导致的学习崩溃On-policy distillation (OPD) offers finer supervision by training the student model against a teacher model’s token-level predictive distribution rather than a hard final-answer reward. Such logit-level targets densify supervision along the generation trajectory and circumvent the learning collapse caused by low within-group reward variances(Song and Zheng, 2026)。然而，OPD 的有效性依赖于教师分布的质量，这通常源自更强大的模型、验证者或策划的演示。 当此类外部信号不可用时，教师必须通过提示中的附加信息（即少量示例）从模型本身中获取信息。 这重新定义了问题，从单独设计自我生成的奖励到决定训练哪些未标记的样本以及哪些环境会产生有用的师生差距。 至关重要的是，这种自我改进的过程需要严格的数据选择。由于模型充当了自己的老师，不加区别地从自身生成的信号中提取信息可能会加剧其现有的错误校准、虚假推理和幻觉。. However, OPD’s effectiveness relies on the quality of the teacher distribution, which is typically derived from stronger models, verifiers, or curated demonstrations. When such external signals are unavailable, the teacher must be elicited from the model itself through additional information in prompts, i.e few-shot examples. This reframes the problem from designing self-generated rewards alone to deciding which unlabeled samples to train on and which contexts induce a useful teacher–student gap. Crucially, this self-improvement process requires rigorous data selection. Because the model acts as its own teacher, indiscriminately distilling from self-generated signals risks reinforcing its existing miscalibration, spurious reasoning, and hallucinations.

虽然传统的选择方法依赖于多数投票或熵等表面指标来过滤掉此类不可靠的数据，但这些信号往往被证明是不够的。 最近的工作转向法学硕士的内部动态，证明内部状态，特别是神经元激活，与模型真正不确定或产生幻觉时表现出很强的相关性While traditional selection methods rely on surface-level metrics like majority-voting or entropy to filter out such unreliable data, these signals often prove insufficient. Recent work has pivoted towards LLMs’ internal dynamics, demonstrating that internal states, particularly neuron activations, exhibit a strong correlation with when a model is genuinely uncertain or hallucinating(Gao et al., 2024; Nostalgebraist, 2020; Chen et al., 2025b, 2026).

C.2 计算成本。Computational Cost.

除了蒸馏训练成本之外，所提出的框架中的额外计算开销还源于基于神经元的小样本和样本选择过程。具体来说，这个过程需要前向传递初始LLM以获得神经元特征，然后计算这些特征的距离矩阵和相应的数据集统计数据。然而，整个训练过程仅依赖于预处理的数据，并且不进行实时计算。因此，数据准备成为一次性成本，因为距离矩阵和样本特征可以预先计算和缓存，使得计算开销可以接受并且与其他基于模型的数据选择方法相比具有竞争力。Besides the distillation training cost, the additional computational overhead in the proposed framework stems from the neuron-based few-shot and sample selection process. Specifically, this process requires forward passes through the initial LLM to obtain neuron features, followed by computing the distance matrix and the corresponding dataset statistics over these features. However, the entire training process relies solely on pre-processed data and incurs no real-time computation. As a result, data preparation becomes a one-time cost, since the distance matrix and sample features can be pre-computed and cached, making the computational overhead acceptable and competitive compared to other model-based data selection methods.
