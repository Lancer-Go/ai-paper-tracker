1 简介Introduction

合成图像检索 (CIR)Composed Image Retrieval (CIR)[3, 2, 16]旨在基于由参考图像和修改文本组成的多模态查询，从大规模图像数据库中检索与修改语义一致的目标图像。图。aims to retrieve target images from large-scale image databases that are consistent with the modification semantics, based on a multimodal query composed of a reference image and modification text. Fig.1(a) 是该任务的一个示例。与传统检索不同，CIR 中的文本通常是对参考图像的修改指令，而不是完整的描述。这一特性使得CIR更符合人类交互逻辑，从而在视觉理解方面具有重要的应用价值(a) is an example of this task. Unlike traditional retrieval, the text in CIR is typically a modification instruction for the reference image rather than a full description. This characteristic makes CIR more aligned with human interaction logic, thus holding significant application value in visual understanding[9, 18, 27, 15], 视频合成, video composition[7, 17, 4]，以及交互式搜索, and interactive search[8, 10, 23, 22].

Specifically, existing methods[5, 11]大多数依靠全局或局部对齐来表征图像和文本之间的关系，但缺乏对上下文结构的系统建模。参考图像内的区域之间的依赖关系和修改文本对局部语义的指令约束通常被简化为浅层注意机制。如图所示。mostly rely on global or local alignment to characterize the relationship between images and text, yet lack systematic modeling of contextual structure. The dependencies between regions within the reference image and the directive constraints of the modification text on local semantics are often simplified to shallow attention mechanisms. As shown in Fig.1(b)，这导致模型难以理解复杂的修改，削弱了其区分匹配和不匹配样本的能力，这可能导致对不匹配图像的误判，直接限制了CIR模型在复杂场景下的较高性能。 然而，由于以下两个挑战，解决上述限制并非易事。(b), this causes the model to struggle in understanding complex modifications, weakening its ability to distinguish matching and non-matching samples, which may lead to misjudgments of non-matching images, directly limiting the upper performance of CIR models in complex scenarios. However, addressing the aforementioned limitations is non-trivial due to the following two challenges.1）隐式依赖1) Implicit dependencies。捕获图像的模态内上下文面临着背景噪声和区域异质性的挑战，使得难以辨别不同局部区域之间的隐式依赖性。因此，如何有效地提取上下文语义并量化其相关性是第一个挑战。. Capturing the intra-modal context of the image faces challenges from background noise and regional heterogeneity, making it difficult to discern implicit dependencies across different local regions. Thus, how to effectively extract contextual semantics and quantify their relevance constitutes the first challenge.2）缺乏差异放大机制2) Absence of the discrepancy-amplification mechanism。在复杂的修改场景中，模型通常在匹配实例和非匹配实例之间表现出模糊的决策边界。这是因为现有的 CIR 方法通常使用余弦相似度来评估多模态查询和目标图像之间的对齐情况，但缺乏放大正负对之间差异的机制。因此，第二个挑战在于如何利用上下文相关性来共同优化训练过程，确保匹配对的相似度持续增强，同时抑制不匹配对的相似度。. In complex modification scenarios, models often exhibits blurred decision boundaries between matching and non-matching instances. This is because existing CIR methods typically use cosine similarity to assess the alignment between multimodal queries and target images, yet lack mechanisms to amplify the discrepancy between positive and negative pairs. Consequently, the second challenge lies in how to leverage contextual relevance to jointly optimize the training process, ensuring that the similarity of matched pairs is consistently enhanced while simultaneously suppressing that of mismatched pairs.

本文提出了一种双路径组合上下文网络（HINT），它能够从多模态查询中提取两种类型的上下文信息，包括用于捕获图像内区域之间关联的视觉模态内上下文，以及用于对图像和文本之间的多模态联合语义进行建模的跨模态上下文。 然后，模型基于上下文语义量化目标图像与上下文之间的相关性，并与余弦相似度得分进行协同优化，放大匹配和非匹配样本之间的相似度差异，从而提高 CIR 模型在复杂场景下的上层性能。 如图所示。This paper proposes a dual-patH composItional coNtextualized neTwork (HINT), which is capable of extracting two types of contextual information from multimodal queries, including the visual intra-modal context for capturing the associations between regions within the image, and the cross-modal context for modeling the multimodal joint semantics between the image and text. The model then quantifies the relevance between the target image and the context based on contextual semantics and co-optimizes with the cosine similarity score, amplifying the similarity differences between matching and non-matching samples, thereby improving the upper performance of the CIR model in complex scenarios. 4 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们关注现有 CIR 模型的一个关键局限性，即忽略了上下文信息在区分匹配样本中的作用。为了解决这个限制，我们进一步将其分解为两个关键挑战：缺乏隐式依赖关系和缺乏差异放大机制。基于此，我们提出了一种新颖的双路径组合上下文网络（HINT）来解决 CIR 任务。 HINT能够进行上下文感知编码，放大匹配和非匹配样本之间的相似性差异，从而提高CIR模型在复杂场景下的上层性能。它在两个 CIR 基准数据集的所有指标上都取得了最佳结果。In this work, we focus on a key limitation of existing CIR models, which is the neglect of the role of contextual information in discriminating matching samples. To address this limitation, we further break it down into two key challenges: the lack of implicit dependencies and the absence of a differential amplification mechanism. Based on this, we propose a novel dual-patH composItional coNtextualized neTwork (HINT) to tackle the CIR task. HINT is capable of performing context-aware encoding and amplifying the similarity differences between matching and non-matching samples, thereby improving the upper performance of CIR models in complex scenarios. It achieves optimal results across all metrics on two CIR benchmark datasets.
