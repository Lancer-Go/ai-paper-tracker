摘要Abstract

基于LLM的代理系统在复杂领域表现出了卓越的能力，但同时也面临着级联错误和调试困难的问题。 关键错误检测旨在定位失败轨迹中导致最终失败的最早错误步骤。 然而，进展面临两个主要挑战。首先，长轨迹使得识别个体错误变得困难，因为判断步骤的证据可能分散在遥远的指令、观察和先前的背景中。其次，失败的轨迹通常包含多个局部错误，其下游影响各不相同：有些被修复，有些保持无害，只有一些导致最终失败。在这项工作中，我们建议LLM-based agentic systems have shown remarkable capabilities in complex domains, while suffering from cascading errors and difficulty in debugging. Critical error detection aims to locate the earliest error step in a failed trajectory that is responsible for the final failure. However, progress faces two main challenges. First, long trajectories make it difficult to identify individual errors, since the evidence for judging a step may be scattered across distant instructions, observations, and prior context. Second, failed trajectories often contain multiple local errors whose downstream effects differ: some are repaired, some remain harmless, and only some contribute to the final failure. In this work, we propose轨迹调试TrajDebug，一个错误生命周期跟踪框架，通过多粒度历史压缩和基于证据的错误识别来解决长轨迹错误发现问题，并通过跟踪每个错误的解决状态和最终影响来支持关键归因。 我们进一步构建, an error-lifecycle tracing framework that addresses long-trajectory error discovery with multi-granularity history compression and evidence-based error identification, and supports critical attribution by tracing each error’s resolution status and terminal impact. Experiments across diverse agent benchmarks show that轨迹调试TrajDebug在现有基线上实现了最佳整体性能，应用研究进一步表明，其诊断为提高下游代理的成功提供了可操作的反馈。 我们将发布代码和数据以方便进一步研究achieves the best overall performance over existing baselines, and application studies further demonstrate that its diagnoses provide actionable feedback for improving downstream agent success. 1 简介Introduction

基于大语言模型 (LLM) 的代理系统在复杂的现实世界领域（例如软件工程）中表现出了卓越的能力Large language model (LLM)-based agentic systems have shown remarkable capabilities in complex, real-world domains, such as software engineering(jimenez2024swebench; deng2025swepro), 科学发现, scientific discovery(mialon2024gaia)，以及多轮客户服务工作流程, and multi-turn customer service workflows(barres2025tau2)。 这些轨迹通常跨越数百个推理、行动和观察步骤，最终的失败可能会追溯到早期的错误，并通过后续决策传播，从而使轨迹难以调试和改进。. These trajectories often span hundreds of reasoning, action, and observation steps, and a terminal failure may trace back to an early mistake that propagates through subsequent decisions, making the trajectory difficult to debug and improve.严重错误检测Critical error detection (zhang2025whoandwhen)解决这个问题 通过定位失败轨迹中最早的错误步骤来解决困难，该轨迹是因果关系的 与任务失败相关联，从而支持轨迹修复和系统 可靠性分析。addresses this difficulty by locating the earliest error step in a failed trajectory that is causally linked to the task failure, thereby supporting trajectory repair and system reliability analysis.

在长而复杂的智能体轨迹中，关键错误检测面临着两个关键挑战。 首先，Critical error detection faces two key challenges in long, complex agent trajectories. First,长轨迹使得识别个体错误变得困难。long trajectories make it difficult to identify individual errors.复杂的任务通常需要扩展的执行流程，在许多步骤中交织规划、推理、工具使用、环境反馈和修复尝试Complex tasks often require extended execution processes that interleave planning, reasoning, tool use, environment feedback, and repair attempts over many steps(yao2023tree; yao2022react)。 因此，判断一个步骤是否错误可能需要将其与分散在远程任务指令、观察结果和先前轨迹上下文中的证据相关联. As a result, judging whether a step is erroneous may require relating it to evidence scattered across distant task instructions, observations, and prior trajectory context(peng2023does)。 例如，如图. Second,失败的轨迹通常包含多个局部错误，其下游影响各不相同，从而掩盖了哪个错误对最终失败至关重要。failed trajectories often contain multiple local errors whose downstream effects differ, obscuring which error is critical to the final failure.失败的轨迹可能包含许多局部错误A failed trajectory may contain many local errors(cemri2026multi)，有些已经解决，有些无关紧要，还有一些只是早期错误的下游症状, some are resolved, some are inconsequential, and others are merely downstream symptoms of earlier mistakes(fan2026agentprocessbench)。 因此，关键误差不一定是轨迹中观察到的第一个局部误差，也不一定是时间上最接近最终故障的误差。. Thus, the critical error is not necessarily the first local error observed in the trajectory, nor the temporally closest error to the terminal failure.

现有方法通常只能解决该问题的一部分。 当前基于分类和基于约束的方法侧重于识别长轨迹中的候选错误步骤Existing methods often address only part of this problem. Current taxonomy-based and constraint-based methods focus on identifying candidate erroneous steps in long trajectories(zhu2025agentdebug; barke2026agentrx)，而因果诊断方法使用因果结构或反事实归因来过滤传播的错误, while causal-diagnostic methods use causal structures or counterfactual attribution to filter propagated errors(wang2026flat; li2026codetracer)。 然而，候选错误方法常常将关键性留给整体的最终判断，当许多局部错误共存时，这种判断是脆弱的。 因果归因方法对错误之间的依赖性进行建模，但当后来的反馈和修复尝试改变了早期错误的影响时，它们的判断可能仍然含糊不清。. However, candidate-error methods often leave criticality to a holistic final judgment, which is brittle when many local errors coexist. Causal attribution methods model dependencies among errors, but their judgments can remain ambiguous when later feedback and repair attempts alter the effects of earlier errors.

为了应对这些挑战，我们建议To address these challenges, we propose轨迹调试TrajDebug，一个用于关键错误检测的基于证据的错误生命周期跟踪框架。, an evidence-grounded error-lifecycle tracing framework for critical error detection.轨迹调试TrajDebug将任务分解为三个阶段：错误触发检测、错误状态分类和关键归因。 首先，为了识别长轨迹中的个体错误，decomposes the task into three stages: error trigger detection, error state classification, and critical attribution. Second, to address the ambiguity caused by multiple local errors with different downstream effects,轨迹调试TrajDebug通过共享的违反参考（例如任务约束或先前的观察）将相关触发器分组到错误实例中。 然后，它通过跟踪错误承诺是否得到解决，以及是否留下可观察的终端足迹（例如不可逆的状态更改、持久的任务违规或大量的恢复成本）来对每个实例的状态进行分类。 这些状态分类将已修复或保持无害的错误与与最终故障相关的错误区分开来。 最后，groups related triggers into error instances by their shared violated reference, such as a task constraint or prior observation.
