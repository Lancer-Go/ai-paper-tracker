We introduce a robust method for handling theory errors using simulation-based inference (SBI), a machine learning approach that empirically models their impact on the observations. This framework retains the rigour of Bayesian inference while avoiding restrictive assumptions about the functional form of the uncertainties. We begin by demonstrating that the common Gaussian parametrisation of theory errors breaks down under minor (1−3%1-3\%) 一维地球模型不确定性。为了解决这个问题，我们开发了两种利用 SBI 来提高矩张量解决方案质量的形式：一种使用基于物理的理论错误洞察，另一种使用端到端深度学习算法。然后，我们将矩张量反演的结果与标准高斯方法和 SBI 进行比较，并证明高斯假设会产生偏差并显着低估矩张量的不确定性。我们还表明，当反演短期数据和浅层各向同性事件时，这些影响尤其成问题。另一方面，SBI 产生更可靠、更好校准的震源机制后验。最后，我们成功地将我们的方法应用于两次经过深入研究的中震级地震：一次来自 1997 年的长谷火山口火山地震序列，另一次来自 2020 年的萨格勒布地震。) 1-D Earth model uncertainty. To address this issue, we develop two formalisms for utilising SBI to improve the quality of the moment tensor solutions: one using physics-based insights into the theory errors, and another utilising an end-to-end deep learning algorithm. 1 简介Introduction

全波形矩张量反演的震源机制解已被证明依赖于高质量的正演建模，这主要取决于精确的地球结构模型（Focal mechanism solutions of full-waveform moment tensor inversion have been shown to rely on high-quality forward modelling, which primarily depends on accurate Earth structure models (Hardebeck & Shearer, 2002; Hjörleifsdóttir & Ekström, 2010; Takemura et al., 2020; Rösler et al., 2021; Song et al., 2022; Simutė et al., 2023，参见, seePhạm et al., 2025最近的评论）。这些影响在地壳结构强烈异质或约束不良的地区尤其明显for a recent review). These effects are particularly pronounced in regions with strongly heterogeneous or poorly constrained crustal structure(Abercrombie & Ekström, 2001; Křížová et al., 2013; Petersen et al., 2021)。可靠的矩张量解决方案对于地震层析成像等下游应用也至关重要. Reliable moment tensor solutions are also critical for downstream applications such as seismic tomography(Blom et al., 2023).

除了表征个别事件之外，异质性还可能导致一维地球模型派生的源目录中的系统偏差Beyond characterising individual events, heterogeneities can lead to systematic biases in 1-D Earth model derived source catalogs(Hingee et al., 2011; Hejrani et al., 2017; Rösler et al., 2024a)。这些不准确性可能会导致矩张量分量之间的偏差和似是而非的权衡. These inaccuracies can induce biases in, and specious trade-offs between, moment tensor components(Dziewonski & Woodhouse, 1983; Ferreira & Woodhouse, 2006; Weston et al., 2011; Ferreira et al., 2011)。例如，此类效应已被证明会在检索到的构造事件震源解中产生系统性杂散非双耦合分量. Such effects have been shown to cause, for instance, systematic spurious non-double-couple components in retrieved seismic source solutions for tectonic events(Sawade et al., 2022; Rösler et al., 2024b)。此外，尚未解决的地球结构不确定性继续对解释震源解决方案构成重大挑战. Moreover, unresolved Earth structure uncertainty continues to pose significant challenges for interpreting seismic source solutions(e.g., Akuhara et al., 2025)。一个有吸引力的解决方案是贝叶斯推理，它提供了一个框架，可以直接在不确定性估计过程中处理理论或错误建模错误. An appealing solution is Bayesian inference, which provides a framework for treating theory or mismodelling errors directly in the uncertainty estimation procedure(Tarantola, 2005)。我们假设不确定的一维地球模型是本文中理论错误的主要来源。. We assume the uncertain 1-D Earth model to be the primary source of theory errors in the context of this work.

在源反演中纳入理论误差的影响可以显着提高反演质量Including the effect of theory errors in source inversions can significantly improve inversion quality(e.g., Yagi & Fukahata, 2011; Minson et al., 2013, 2014; Duputel et al., 2015; Phạm & Tkalčić, 2021)。这通常是通过将理论误差视为高斯似然函数中的附加项来实现的，如下所示. Several studies approximate the theory errors by sampling over the modelled Earth structure uncertainty empirically and estimating the resulting variability in the observations(Duputel et al., 2012; Vasyura-Bathke et al., 2021; Phạm & Tkalčić, 2021; Poppeliers & Preston, 2021; Phạm et al., 2024)，而其他人则依赖理论近似来了解理论误差对观测协方差结构的影响, while others rely on theoretical approximations for the effect of theory errors on the observation covariance structure(Duputel et al., 2014; Hallo & Gallovič, 2016)。所有这些先前的工作都表明，结合理论误差贡献可以比朴素的方法产生改进的解决方案。然而，他们进行了显着的近似，使逆问题可以通过传统方法处理。因此，最终的解决方案是否真正准确且值得信赖的问题仍然存在。. However, they perform significant approximations to make the inverse problem tractable by traditional means. The question of whether the resulting solutions are genuinely accurate and trustworthy thus still remains.

While these approaches remain valuable, particularly as a means for dealing with unknown (or challenging to model) theory uncertainties, it is desirable to continue to develop a complementary approach that directly integrates our ability to model theory errors.

基于模拟的推理 (SBI) 是一种很有前途的替代方案，可以充分处理理论错误的影响，而不会影响（或放弃）严格的贝叶斯矩张量反演方法。 SBI 使用机器学习 (ML) 模型通过直接模拟凭经验学习观测不确定性，原则上允许对任意复杂的不确定性源进行建模，而无需对其形式进行参数假设Simulation-based inference (SBI) is a promising alternative to fully treat the effects of theory errors without compromising (or abandoning) a rigorous Bayesian approach to moment tensor inversion. SBI uses machine learning (ML) models to learn the observational uncertainties empirically through direct simulation, in principle allowing arbitrarily complex sources of uncertainty to be modelled without making parametric assumptions about their form(Alsing et al., 2019; Cranmer et al., 2020; Zammit-Mangion et al., 2025). SBI 因实现快速、准确的贝叶斯推理而受到整个物理科学领域的广泛关注SBI has received significant attention across the physical sciences for enabling fast and accurate Bayesian inference(Gonçalves et al., 2020; Stockman et al., 2024; von Wietersheim-Kramsta et al., 2025; Atlas Collaboration, 2025; Dax et al., 2025)。将 SBI 应用于给定领域的一个关键实际挑战是准备观测数据，使其适合基于 ML 的概率建模. This is typically straightforward in low dimensions, but high-dimensional observations, such as full-waveform multi-station seismic data, can pose problems. The choice of data representation therefore plays a central role in determining both the accuracy and efficiency of SBI-based inversions(Gerardi et al., 2024; Park et al., 2025).

8 讨论Discussion

这项工作的重点是一维地球结构引起的不确定性，通过特定于站的时移以简化的方式处理方位角变化。下一步重要的是严格评估不同的反演方法在更真实的数据上的表现。分数压缩 SBI 方法与高斯似然紧密相关，可以更好地理解其行为和对真实数据的鲁棒性。尽管如此，3D 地球结构效应，特别是在较低时期，可能会加剧本工作中发现的高斯似然模型错误问题。This work has focused on uncertainties arising from 1-D Earth structure, with azimuthal variations treated in a simplified manner through station-specific time shifts. An important next step is to rigorously assess how the different inversion approaches perform on more realistic data. The score-compression SBI approach is closely tied to a Gaussian likelihood, whose behaviour and robustness on real data is better understood. Nonetheless, 3-D Earth structure effects, particularly at lower periods, will likely exacerbate the Gaussian likelihood mismodelling issues uncovered in this work.

9 结论Conclusions

本手稿证明，模型不确定性下准确的贝叶斯矩张量反演依赖于良好表征的可能性。标准高斯似然近似会在解决方案中引入偏差和过度自信。基于模拟的推理 (SBI) 使用机器学习生成更好的似然性模型，从而在具有挑战性的不确定性来源下实现准确且经过校准的矩张量反演。This manuscript has demonstrated that accurate Bayesian moment tensor inversions under model uncertainty rely on well-characterised likelihoods. Standard Gaussian likelihood approximations introduce bias and overconfidence in the solutions. Simulation-based inference (SBI) produces better models of the likelihood using machine learning, enabling accurate and calibrated moment tensor inversions under challenging sources of uncertainty.

我们表明，高斯似然近似在非常小的速度模型不确定性下会崩溃。可以预见的是，随着速度结构不确定性程度的增加We showed that Gaussian likelihood approximations break down under very minor velocity model uncertainty. 我们引入了两个使用 SBI 进行经验似然建模的框架。一种利用通用且轻量级的分数压缩算法，该算法使用正演模型的泰勒展开来降低全波形地震观测的维数。基于分数压缩的 SBI 产生的校准矩张量后验解比高斯似然方法好得多，尽管偶尔会有偏差。第二个框架使用完全数据驱动的方法，使用深度学习模型来压缩观察结果并对后验进行建模。后一个框架更加灵活，可以直接纳入更复杂的不确定性来源。重要的是，基于深度学习的 SBI 的灵活性和效率有可能在目录级别实现快速且稳健的贝叶斯矩张量反演。We introduced two frameworks for empirical likelihood modelling with SBI. One leveraged the generic and lightweight score compression algorithm, which uses a Taylor expansion of the forward model to reduce the dimensionality of full-waveform seismic observations. Score compression-based SBI produced much better calibrated moment tensor posterior solutions than a Gaussian likelihood approach, albeit with very occasional biases. The second framework used a fully data-driven approach, using a deep learning model to compress the observations and model the posterior. The latter framework is significantly more flexible and can straightforwardly incorporate more complex sources of uncertainty. Importantly, the flexibility and efficiency of deep learning-based SBI has the potential to enable rapid and robust Bayesian moment tensor inversion at the catalogue level.
