In Abstract Tree Indexing, a hierarchical abstract tree is built by ranking pairwise chunk similarities and applying an iterative “merging and collapse” process. In Multi-granular Agentic Retrieval, a retrieval and answering (R&A) agent dynamically interacts with a multi-granular knowledge base organized by both the tree index and a sparse keyword index. By adjusting the retrieval iterations and the utilization of the sparse index, Ψ-RAG adapts to diverse user requests from token-level question answering to document-level summarization.

六、结论6. Conclusion

我们提出了 Ψ-RAG，一种分层 Tree-RAG 框架，支持语料库级索引和跨文档多跳用户问题。通过基于层次聚类的抽象树索引和多粒度代理检索器，Ψ-RAG 解决了以下关键限制：We propose Ψ-RAG, a hierarchical Tree-RAG framework supporting corpus-level indexing and cross-document multihop user questions. By a hierarchical clustering-based abstract tree index and a multi-granular agentic retriever, Ψ- RAG addresses key limitations ofk-分布适应性差、结构孤立、抽象粗糙等均值型树索引。大量的实验结果表明，Ψ-RAG 在不同粒度的任务中取得了显着的成果，促进了 tree-RAG 在法学硕士快速发展时代的发展。-means-type tree indexes such as poor distribution adaptability, structural isolation, and coarse abstraction. 代理RAG。Agentic RAG.法学硕士智力的快速进步，更加关注他们的推理能力和对 RAG 框架的要求。最近的工作（Yao 等人，2022；Trivedi 等人，2023；Asai 等人，2024；Li 等人，2025c）结合了专门设计的 LLM 代理，将检索集成到 LLM 推理过程中。具体来说，代理递归地将问题分解为多个子问题，并在每一步调用检索器（Li et al., 2025d）。这使得法学硕士能够自主灵活地处理多跳问题。然而，它们中的大多数都是建立在用于令牌级别信息的扁平线性或倒排索引的基础上的。The rapid advancement in the intelligence of LLMs has heightened focus on their reasoning capabilities and demands on RAG frameworks. Recent efforts (Yao et al., 2022; Trivedi et al., 2023; Asai et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025c) incorporate specifically designed LLM agents to integrate retrieval into LLM reasoning process. Specifically, the agent recursively decomposes a question into multiple sub-questions and invokes the retriever at each step (Li et al., 2025d). This enables the LLM to autonomously and flexibly handle multi-hop questions. However, most of them are built upon flattened linear or inverted indexes for token-level information.

图-RAG。Graph-RAG.作为结构化 RAG 最流行的形式，知识图索引（Edge et al., 2024；Peng et al., 2025；Xiao et al., 2025）可以捕获文档块或实体之间的语义关系。他们经常使用命名实体识别和提取方法（例如开放信息提取（OpenIE））（Banko 等，2007）为整个语料库构建统一索引。然后，通过从一个节点走到另一个节点（Jimenez´ Gutierrez´ et al., 2024; 2025）、提取最短路径（Huang et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026）或提取子图（Guo et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025a）来捕获跨文档多跳关联。然而，图索引在捕获层次结构方面效果较差。一些Graph-RAG方法必须采用效率较低的补偿策略来处理总结性任务，包括但不限于分层图（Malkov & Yashunin，2018；Huang et al.，2025）、社区摘要（Edge et al.，2024；Wang et al.，2025）、基于LLM的分层过滤（Guo et al.，2025）或基于图神经网络的消息聚合（Zhang 等人，2025a）。再加上 OpenIE 的巨大开销，高效的索引构建仍然是 Graph-RAG 的一个关键挑战。As the most popular form of structured RAG, knowledge graph indexes (Edge et al., 2024; Peng et al., 2025; Xiao et al., 2025) can capture semantic relationships between document chunks or entities. They often build a unified index for an entire corpus using named entity recognition and extraction methods like the Open Information Extraction (OpenIE) (Banko et al., 2007). Cross-document multi-hop associations are then captured by walking from node to node (Jimenez´ Gutierrez´ et al., 2024; 2025), extracting shortest paths (Huang et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026), or extracting subgraphs (Guo et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025a). However, graph indexes are less effective at capturing hierarchy. Some Graph-RAG methods have to resort to less efficient compensatory strategies to handle summative tasks, including but not limited to hierarchical graphs (Malkov & Yashunin, 2018; Huang et al., 2025), community summaries (Edge et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025), LLM-based hierarchical filtering (Guo et al., 2025), or graph neural network-based message aggregation (Zhang et al., 2025a). Coupled with the significant overhead of OpenIE, efficient index construction remains a key challenge for Graph-RAG.

树-RAG。Tree-RAG.引入树索引来显式地建模文档的层次关系，同时实现对数复杂度的快速检索（Liu，2022）。用于最近邻搜索的传统树索引包括自上而下的分割（Bentley，1975；Omohundro，1989）和自下而上的层次聚类（Florek 等，1951；Sokal & Michener，1958；Jardine & van Rijsbergen，1971）。然而，传统算法的泛化能力有限。大多数现有的基于密集向量的 Tree-RAG 方法（Liu 等人，2021；Liu，2022；Jin 等人，2025b；Zhao 等人，2025；Fatehkia 等人，2024；Tao 等人，2025）依赖于显式文档结构（会话标题、目录等）或相邻文本块之间的上下文关系。因此，它们不能直接应用于缺乏明确结构的文档或跨文档检索。 RAPTOR（Sarthi et al., 2024）是第一个专为非结构化长文档设计的段落级 Tree-RAG 框架。它通过高斯混合模型 (GMM) 递归地对文档块进行聚类，并为每个聚类生成摘要。 MemTree (Rezazadeh et al., 2025)进一步使Tree-RAG适应了长期LLM记忆场景，但两者都仅限于段落级索引和单文档检索设置。据我们所知，唯一的语料库级 Tree-RAG 是 LATTICE（Gupta 等人，2025），它使用 LLM 评估的路径相关性得分来执行波束搜索检索。然而，没有讨论多跳 QA 的限制，并且其树构建过程在撰写本文时尚未开源。Tree indexes are introduced to explicitly model the hierarchical relationships of documents while achieving fast retrieval with logarithmic complexity (Liu, 2022). Traditional tree indexes for nearest neighbor search include top-down splitting (Bentley, 1975; Omohundro, 1989) and bottom-up hierarchical clustering (Florek et al., 1951; Sokal & Michener, 1958; Jardine & van Rijsbergen, 1971). However, traditional algorithms offer limited generalization. Most existing dense vector-based Tree-RAG methods (Liu et al., 2021; Liu, 2022; Jin et al., 2025b; Zhao et al., 2025; Fatehkia et al., 2024; Tao et al., 2025) rely on explicit document structure (session titles, tables of contents, etc.) or contextual relationships between adjacent text chunks. As a result, they are not directly applicable to documents lacking explicit structure or to cross-document retrieval. RAPTOR (Sarthi et al., 2024) is the first passage-level Tree-RAG framework designed for unstructured long documents. It recursively clusters document chunks via Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) and generates a summary for each cluster. MemTree (Rezazadeh et al., 2025) further adapts Tree-RAG to the long-term LLM memory scenario, but both of them are limited to the passage-level indexes and the single-document retrieval setting. To our knowledge, the only corpus-level Tree-RAG is LATTICE (Gupta et al., 2025), which performs beam search retrieval using an LLM-evaluated path relevance score. However, there are no discussions towards the limitations on multi-hop QA, and its tree building process is not open-sourced at the time of writing.

G. 讨论和未来的工作G. Discussion and Future Work

更新索引。Updating the index.实时文档插入和索引更新是 RAG 应用程序的常见要求，但它们对传统的 Tree-RAG 方法提出了挑战，因为添加新文档需要重新聚类整个语料库。我们的抽象树索引是基于局部成对相似性构建的，使得可以用新文档进行更新。Real-time document insertion and index updates are common requirements for RAG applications, yet they pose challenges for traditional Tree-RAG methods, as adding new documents necessitates re-clustering the entire corpus. Our abstract tree index is constructed based on local pairwise similarities, making it feasible to be updated with new documents.
