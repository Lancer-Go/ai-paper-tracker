卸载还是过载：移动机器人操纵工作负载的平台测量研究Offload or Overload: A Platform Measurement Study of Mobile Robotic Manipulation Workloads

Sara Pohland1 Xenofon Foukas2, Ganesh Ananthanarayanan2, Andrey Kolobov2, Sanjeev Mehrotra2, Bozidar Radunovic2, Ankit Verma2
1UC Berkeley, 2Microsoft Corp
摘要。Abstract.

移动机器人操纵——机器人导航空间和与物体交互的能力——是物理人工智能的核心能力。 基础模型在性能上取得了突破，但计算成本很高。 我们首次针对板载、边缘和云 GPU 平台上的移动机器人操作工作负载进行测量研究。 我们发现，完整的工作负载堆栈在较小的板载 GPU 上运行是不可行的，而较大的板载 GPU 会使机器人电池耗尽的速度加快几个小时。卸载减轻了这些限制，但也带来了自身的挑战，因为额外的网络延迟会降低任务的准确性，而且带宽要求使得简单的云卸载变得不切实际。最后，我们量化了跨机器人车队共享计算的机会和陷阱。 我们相信我们的测量研究对于设计移动机器人的推理系统至关重要。Mobile robotic manipulation–the ability of robots to navigate spaces and interact with objects–is a core capability of physical AI. Foundation models have led to breakthroughs in their performance, but at a significant computational cost. We present the first measurement study of mobile robotic manipulation workloads across onboard, edge, and cloud GPU platforms. We find that the full workload stack is infeasible to run on smaller onboard GPUs, while larger onboard GPUs drain robot batteries several hours faster. Offloading alleviates these constraints but introduces its own challenges, as additional network latency degrades task accuracy, and the bandwidth requirement makes naive cloud offloading impractical. Finally, we quantify opportunities and pitfalls of sharing compute across robot fleets. We believe our measurement study will be crucial to designing inference systems for mobile robots.

8. 对分布式机器人平台的需求Need for a Distributed Robotics Platform

我们的测量研究强调了对强大 GPU 的日益增长的需求，以支持新兴的移动机器人操作工作负载，以及具有成本效益的共享基础设施的潜力。此外，我们还证明，由于工作负载性能、网络延迟、带宽、功耗和货币成本之间固有的权衡，没有单一的部署策略（板载、边缘或云）适合所有部署场景和用例。Our measurement study highlights the growing need for powerful GPUs to support emerging mobile robotic manipulation workloads, as well as the potential for cost-effective shared infrastructure. Furthermore, we demonstrate that no single deployment strategy–onboard, edge, or cloud–is optimal for all deployment scenarios and use cases due to inherent tradeoffs across workload performance, network latency, bandwidth, power consumption, and monetary cost.

这些特征激发了对These characteristics motivate the need for a分布式机器人推理平台distributed robotics inference platform涵盖机器人、边缘和云资源。这样的平台应该支持基于带宽、延迟和计算要求的分布式工作负载编排，并支持 GPU、CPU 和网络资源的动态、QoS 感知调度。 尽管在移动等领域也探索了类似的想法that spans robots, edge, and cloud resources. Such a platform should support distributed orchestration of workloads based on bandwidth, latency, and compute requirements, and enable dynamic, QoS-aware scheduling of GPU, CPU, and network resources. Although similar ideas have been explored in domains such as mobile(Cuervo et al., 2010)和电信and telecommunications(Ananthanarayanan et al., 2025)，机器人系统引入了新的约束。特别是，封闭的感知-行动循环提出了严格的实时要求，为系统设计和实现带来了新的挑战，以确保物理世界任务的安全性和可靠性能。, robotic systems introduce new constraints. In particular, closed perception–action loops impose strict real-time requirements, creating new challenges for system design and implementation to ensure safety and reliable performance in physical-world tasks.

Offloading has been extensively studied in mobile and edge computing(Cuervo et al., 2010)，特别是对于资源受限设备上的深度神经网络推理, particularly for deep neural network inference on resource-constrained devices(Shuvo et al., 2023)和大规模视频分析and large-scale video analytics(Bai et al., 2026)。 然而，此类系统通常在软实时或开环设置下运行，其中偶尔的延迟波动是可以容忍的。相比之下，机器人系统在封闭的感知-行动循环中运行，其中延迟或计算量下降可能会直接降低控制性能或危及安全性。因此，机器人系统中的卸载考虑因素与移动计算中的卸载考虑因素不同。. However, such systems generally operate in soft real-time or open-loop settings, where occasional latency fluctuations are tolerable. In contrast, robotic systems operate within closed perception–action loops, where delays or dropped computations can directly degrade control performance or compromise safety. Thus, offloading considerations in robotic systems differ from those in mobile computing.

在机器人技术中，卸载受到的实证关注相对有限。早期调查强调了云基础设施加速 SLAM 和运动规划等工作负载的潜力，同时注意到网络延迟和连接变化带来的风险Within robotics, offloading has received comparatively limited empirical attention. 10. 结论Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们首次对板载、边缘和云 GPU 平台上的移动机器人操作工作负载进行了测量研究。 我们的研究结果表明，卸载此类工作负载对于机器人的性能和节省电池来说是有益的，但由于网络延迟和带宽对任务准确性的影响，也带来了自身的挑战。 最后，该研究强调了跨机器人车队共享计算的机遇和挑战。 我们希望这项研究有助于指导移动机器人推理系统领域的未来研究。In this work, we performed the first measurement study of mobile robotic manipulation workloads across onboard, edge, and cloud GPU platforms. Our findings reveal that offloading such workloads can be beneficial in terms of performance and battery savings for the robots, but introduces its own challenges, due to the effects of network latency and bandwidth on task accuracy. Finally, the study highlights the opportunities and challenges of sharing compute across robot fleets. We hope that this study will help guide future research in the space of inference systems for mobile robots.
