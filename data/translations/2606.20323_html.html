摘要Abstract

深度迁移学习（DTL）可以有效构建智能故障诊断系统（IFDS）。另一方面，DTL方法仍然严重依赖大量的标记数据。在处理机器或结构故障时，获取如此大量的数据可能具有挑战性。本文提出了一种在数据严重匮乏的情况下使用 DTL 设计基于振动的 IFDS 的新方法。利用现实世界系统固有的非线性的周期性多激励电平程序用于生成图像，这些图像可以通过预先训练的卷积神经网络 (CNN) 方便地进行分析以诊断故障。本文提出了一种新的数据可视化方法及其增强技术，以解决 IFDS 设计过程中遇到的典型数据缺乏问题。铁路受电弓结构的实验验证为该方法提供了有效的支持。Deep Transfer Learning (DTL) allows for the efficient building of Intelligent Fault Diagnosis Systems (IFDS). On the other hand, DTL methods still heavily rely on large amounts of labelled data. Obtaining such an amount of data can be challenging when dealing with machines or structures faults. This document proposes a novel approach to the design of vibration-based IFDS using DTL in condition of strong data scarcity. A periodic multi-excitation level procedure leveraging intrinsic non-linearities of real-world systems is used to produce images that can be conveniently analysed by pre-trained Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to diagnose faults. A new data visualization method and its augmentation technique are proposed in this paper to tackle the typical lack of data encountered during the design of IFDS. Experimental validation on a railway pantograph structure provides effective support for the proposed method.

1 简介Introduction

深度迁移学习 (DTL) 正在推动下一代机器和结构健康监测系统的兴起Deep Transfer Learning (DTL) is enabling the rise of the next-generation machine and structural health monitoring systemsavci2021review。深度学习 (DL) 在特征表示方面的优势与重用从大型数据集中提取的知识的能力（典型的迁移学习）相结合，可以有效构建智能故障诊断系统 (IFDS)。. The advantage of Deep Learning (DL) in feature representation combined with the ability to reuse the knowledge extracted from large datasets, typical of Transfer Learning, allows for the efficient building of Intelligent Fault Diagnosis Systems (IFDS).

另一方面，DTL 方法严重依赖大量标记数据。 在处理现实世界的机器或结构时，获取如此大量的数据可能具有挑战性。另一方面，大多数故障在实验室设施中都很罕见且无法重现。因此，研究人员将重点放在具有工业利益的系统上，这些系统可以轻松且低成本地进行篡改，例如小型框架或齿轮箱和滚动轴承等旋转机械。与科学文献中提出的 IFDS 的多样性相比，很少有作品提出解决在现实场景中设计 IFDS 时遇到的典型数据稀缺问题的技术。因此，解决数据缺乏仍然是一个悬而未决的问题On the other hand, DTL methods heavily rely on large amounts of labelled data. Obtaining such an amount of data can be challenging when dealing with real-world machines or structures. On the other side, most faults are rare and irreproducible in laboratory facilities. Therefore researchers focus on systems of industrial interest which can be tampered with easiness and low cost as small frames or also rotating machinery like gearboxes and rolling bearings. Compared to the multiplicity of IFDS presented in the scientific literature, few works propose techniques to tackle the typical data scarcity encountered when designing IFDS in real-world scenarios. Hence, tackling the data lack is still an open issueli2022perspective.

为了克服上述缺陷，我们利用典型结构的非线性设计了一种新颖的数据可视化方法及其增强技术。所提出的方法属于所谓的非参数技术，这意味着它仅依赖于直接从原始数据中提取的特征，而不需要物理模型。特别是，该方法适用于可以从外部控制激励水平的结构动态测试，例如在模态实验测试中，可以通过外部执行器调整所施加激励的幅度。To overcome the aforementioned deficiency, we exploited the typical structures’ nonlinearity to design a novel data visualization method and its augmentation technique. The proposed approach belongs to the so-called non-parametric techniques, meaning that it only relies on features that are extracted directly from raw data with no need of physical models. In particular, this method is suitable for structural dynamic tests where the excitation level can be controlled externally, as for example in modal experimental testing where the amplitude of the applied excitation can be tuned through external actuators.

基本思想是，在存在非线性的情况下，结构的频率响应特性取决于激励水平。 更重要的是，频域中的损伤特征也受到外力幅度的调制。 因此，对于给定的故障类型，可以在低或高激励水平下增强检测，如文献中所示The underlying idea is that in the presence of nonlinearity, the frequency response characteristics of the structure are dependent on the excitation level. What is more, also the damage signature in the frequency domain is modulated by the amplitude of the external force. Consequently, for a given fault type, detection may be enhanced at low or high excitation levels, as demonstrated in the literaturesantamato2024investigating。 因此，利用从激励水平获得的更多信息来生成 2D 图像。. Therefore, the increased information derived from the excitation level is exploited to produce 2D images.

在这项工作中，系统响应通过频率响应函数 (FRF) 传达，表示为接收函数（位移过激励）的 dB 幅度。 在不同激励水平下获取的频响函数被收集在单一彩色图中，从而产生类似频谱图的图像，其中时间轴被激励水平轴取代。 这种表示形式实现了一种新颖的数据增强自定义程序，其中同一故障的不同采集被混合以创建大量新的真实彩色图，从而利用激励水平的多重性。In this work, the system response is conveyed through the Frequency Response Function (FRF), represented as the dB-magnitude of the receptance function (displacement-over-excitation). The FRFs acquired at different excitation levels are collected in a single colour map, thus producing a spectrogram-like image where the axis of time is substituted by the axis of the excitation level. Such a representation enables a novel custom procedure of data augmentation, where different acquisitions of the same fault are mixed to create a multitude of new realistic colour maps, thus leveraging the excitation levels multiplicity.

Therefore we show how our IFDS is able to correctly classify the undamaged condition and the fault scenarios with a relatively low-size data set.

5 结论Conclusions

该文件提出了一种新颖的方法来解决 IFDS 设计过程中经常遇到的数据稀缺问题。利用现实世界系统固有的非线性的多激励级程序用于获取和增强可用数据，生成可以由预先训练的卷积神经网络 (CNN) 方便地分析以诊断故障的图像。所提出的方法已应用于设计 IFDS，用于真实比例铁路受电弓的结构健康监测。研究了三种情况：结构未损坏、螺栓连接有缺陷和阻尼器有缺陷。This document proposed a novel approach to tackle the problem of data scarcity often met during the design of IFDS. A multi-excitation level procedure leveraging intrinsic non-linearities of real-world systems is used to acquire and augment the available data, producing images that can be conveniently analysed by pre-trained Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to diagnose faults. The proposed approach has been applied to designing an IFDS for the structural health monitoring of a real-scale railway pantograph. Three scenarios were studied: undamage structure, a faulty bolted connection, and a faulty damper.

设计的 IFDS 能够正确分类 97.6% 的测试条件，查看在不同激励水平下获得的频响函数，显示出将螺栓损坏错误分类为未损坏条件的轻微趋势。尽管如此，所设计的IFDS已经能够克服受电弓在不同时间获取的数据所表现出的集群效应，为该方法的实际应用提供了经验支持。The designed IFDS was able to correctly classify 97.6% of the testing conditions, looking at the FRFs obtained at different excitation levels, showing a slight trend in misclassifying bolt damages as the undamaged condition. Nevertheless, the designed IFDS has been able to overcome the cluster effect shown by the pantograph’s data acquired at different times, providing empirical support for the real-world application of the proposed method.
