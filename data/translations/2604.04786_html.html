摘要Abstract

本文提出了一种解决组合约束满足问题的量子搜索方法，并通过幻方的生成进行了演示。我们将魔方构造重新表述为一个量子搜索问题，其中可逆的、约束敏感的预言机通过 Grover 算法标记振幅放大的有效配置。使用孪生结构和部分约束检查的经典预处理在量子编码之前生成紧凑的候选域。这项工作没有将经典和量子求解器集成在迭代循环中，而是使用经典组件进行结构化初始化，使用量子组件进行搜索，并将量子方法与经典的强力枚举和回溯进行基准测试。我们的 Qiskit 实现演示了多寄存器模块化算术电路、预言逻辑和扩散运算符的设计。实验是在小型网格实例上进行的，因为由于内存呈指数增长，较大的网格在经典状态向量模拟器上很难处理。结果验证了所提出的量子搜索管道的正确性，并证实了理论二次查询相对于经典搜索的优势。This paper presents a quantum search approach to combinatorial constraint satisfaction problems, demonstrated through the generation of magic squares. We reformulate magic square construction as a quantum search problem in which a reversible, constraintsensitive oracle marks valid configurations for amplitude amplification via Grover's algorithm. Classical pre-processing using the Siamese construction and partial constraint checks generates a compact candidate domain before quantum encoding. Rather than integrating classical and quantum solvers in an iterative loop, this work uses the classical component for structured initialisation and the quantum component for search, and benchmarks the quantum approach against classical brute-force enumeration and backtracking. Our Qiskit implementation demonstrates the design of multi-register modular arithmetic circuits, oracle logic, and diffusion operators. Experiments are conducted on small grid instances, as larger grids are intractable on classical statevector simulators due to exponential memory growth. The results validate the correctness of the proposed quantum search pipeline and confirm the theoretical quadratic query advantage over classical search.

2. 背景及相关工作2. Background and Related Work

Grover 算法于 1996 年推出，是量子计算的基础成果之一 [1]。给定包含 M 个标记解决方案的 N 个元素的非结构化数据库，该算法使用 O(sqrt(N/M)) 预言机查询以高概率定位标记元素。这代表了对 O(N/M) 经典下限的二次改进。该算法的工作原理是重复应用两个操作：特定于问题的相位标记预言机和将幅度反转为平均值的扩散算子。这些一起形成 Grover 迭代，最佳迭代次数大约为 (pi/4) 乘以 sqrt(N/M)。为结构化问题构建有效的量子预言机是一个活跃的研究领域。最近的贡献包括针对图问题的预言机设计，例如顶点覆盖 [2]、可组合预言机抽象框架 [3] 以及 Grover 搜索的幅度抑制变体 [4]。包括 CSP 在内的经典组合问题作为量子优势的候选应用引起了越来越多的兴趣，尽管实际实现仍然受到量子位计数和电路深度的限制。幻方本身在经典组合学中有着丰富的历史。他们已经通过详尽的枚举、约束传播回溯和群论构造方法来解决。它们的阶乘搜索复杂性使它们成为量子搜索的自然基准，但量子方法在很大程度上仍未得到探索[5]。本文以经典方法为基准，通过完整的量子搜索管道缩小了这一差距。Grover's algorithm, introduced in 1996, is one of the foundational results in quantum computing [1]. Given an unstructured database of N elements that contains M marked solutions, the algorithm locates a marked element with high probability using O(sqrt(N/M)) oracle queries. This represents a quadratic improvement over the classical lower bound of O(N/M). The algorithm works by repeatedly applying two operations: a problem-specific phase-marking oracle and a diffusion operator that inverts amplitudes about their mean. Together these form a Grover iterate, and the optimal number of iterations is approximately (pi/4) times sqrt(N/M). Constructing efficient quantum oracles for structured problems is an active area of research. Recent contributions include oracle designs for graph problems such as vertex cover [2], frameworks for composable oracle abstraction [3], and amplitude-suppression variants of Grover search [4]. Classical combinatorial problems, including CSPs, have attracted growing interest as candidate applications for quantum advantage, though practical implementations remain limited by qubit count and circuit depth. Magic squares themselves have a rich history in classical combinatorics. They have been tackled with exhaustive enumeration, constraint-propagation backtracking, and group-theoretic construction methods. Their factorial search complexity makes them a natural benchmark for quantum search, yet quantum approaches remain largely unexplored [5]. This paper closes that gap with a complete quantum search pipeline, benchmarked against classical methods.

4. 量子搜索管道：设计和组件4. Quantum Search Pipeline: Design and Components

拟议的管道贯穿五个连续阶段，每个阶段如下所述。The proposed pipeline runs through five sequential stages, each described below.

4.1 经典预处理4.1 Classical Pre-processing

在进行任何量子编码之前，经典滤波器会缩小搜索空间。使用的过滤策略是：（i）使用 Siamese（De la Loubère）结构固定奇数阶正方形的中心单元，这是奇数阶幻方的确定性放置规则； (ii) 应用部分行和列总和检查以尽早丢弃明显不可行的分配； (iii) 生成一个紧凑的候选域，并将其移交给量子处理器。这个修剪阶段不会改变量子搜索的正确性；它只是减少了叠加状态的数量，从而提高了幅度放大过程中的信噪比。Before any quantum encoding takes place, a classical filter shrinks the search space. The filtering strategies used are: (i) fixing the centre cell of odd-order squares using the Siamese (De la Loubère) construction, which is a deterministic placement rule for odd-order magic squares; (ii) applying partial row and column sum checks to discard clearly infeasible assignments early; and (iii) producing a compact candidate domain that is handed off to the quantum processor. This pruning stage does not change the correctness of the quantum search; it simply reduces the number of states in superposition, which improves the signal-to-noise ratio during amplitude amplification.

4.2 量子编码4.2 Quantum Encoding

从 1 到 n² 的每个整数均使用上限(log2(n²)) 量子位以二进制编码。 n×n 幻方有 n² 个单元，因此主寄存器总共需要 n² 乘以上限(log2(n²)) 个量子位。附加的辅助量子位处理算术和约束验证。对所有初级量子位应用哈达玛变换，以在所有候选状态上均匀叠加的方式初始化系统，为每个状态提供相等的起始幅度。Each integer from 1 to n² is encoded in binary using ceiling(log2(n²)) qubits. An n×n magic square has n² cells, so the primary register requires n² times ceiling(log2(n²)) qubits in total. Additional ancilla qubits handle arithmetic and constraint verification. A Hadamard transform is applied across all primary qubits to initialise the system in a uniform superposition over all candidate states, giving each one an equal starting amplitude.

4.3 预言机构建4.3 Oracle Construction

预言机是一种可逆量子电路，它将 -1 的相位翻转应用于满足全套幻方约束的每个状态。施工分三步进行。首先，量子模块化加法器计算行、列和对角线和并将它们存储在辅助寄存器中。其次，量子比较器检查每个和是否等于 M；当且仅当每个比较同时通过（包括元素唯一性检查）时，多控制门才会翻转目标辅助量子位。第三，所有中间计算都是未计算的（可逆的），以将辅助量子位恢复到其初始状态，这是预言机适当可逆和无干扰所必需的。The oracle is a reversible quantum circuit that applies a phase flip of -1 to every state satisfying the full set of magic square constraints. Construction proceeds in three steps. First, quantum modular adders compute the row, column, and diagonal sums and store them in ancilla registers. Second, quantum comparators check whether each sum equals M; a multi-controlled gate flips a target ancilla qubit if and only if every comparison passes simultaneously, including the element-uniqueness check. Third, all intermediate computations are uncomputed (reversed) to restore the ancilla qubits to their initial state, which is required for the oracle to be properly reversible and interference-free.

Performing significantly more or fewer than k iterations degrades this probability in a sinusoidal pattern. In practice, k is estimated classically before the circuit runs, or determined adaptively using techniques such as quantum counting.

4.5 测量与经典验证4.5 Measurement and Classical Verification

经过 k Grover 迭代后，测量主寄存器。量子态塌陷为候选构型。然后，经典的后处理步骤检查该配置是否满足所有幻方约束。如果没有（当 M > 1 或 k 稍微偏离时，发生这种情况的概率很小），则重新执行量子电路。有效配置作为输出返回并显示给用户。After k Grover iterations, the primary register is measured. The quantum state collapses to a candidate configuration. A classical post-processing step then checks whether this configuration satisfies all magic square constraints. If it does not (which happens with small probability when M > 1 or when k is slightly off), the quantum circuit is re-executed. Valid configurations are returned as output and displayed to the user.

5.2 Grover 算法与暴力搜索5.2 Grover's Algorithm versus Brute-Force Search

暴力搜索的工作原理是枚举所有 (n²)！ 1 到 n² 的排列，并根据幻方约束检查每一个排列。对于 n = 3，这意味着最多检查 9！ = 362,880 个候选安排。在我们的模拟中（图 2），强力方法在找到有效解决方案之前检查了 69,075 种排列，耗时 0.0744 秒。 Grover 的算法采用了完全不同的方法。它在量子叠加中同时编码所有 362,880 个候选排列，并使用预言和扩散算子来放大有效幻方的幅度。所需的 Oracle 查询的理论数量为 sqrt(362,880)，大约为 602，而暴力破解最多为 362,880。 Qiskit 模拟使用 9 量子位寄存器（q0 到 q8），并在 0.0053 秒内完成。表 1 总结了比较。Brute-force search works by enumerating all (n²)! permutations of 1 through n² and checking each one against the magic square constraints. For n = 3, this means checking up to 9! = 362,880 candidate arrangements. It encodes all 362,880 candidate permutations simultaneously in a quantum superposition and uses the oracle and diffusion operator to amplify the amplitudes of valid magic squares. The theoretical number of oracle queries required is sqrt(362,880) which is approximately 602, compared with up to 362,880 for brute force. 5.3 Grover算法与经典回溯5.3 Grover's Algorithm versus Classical Backtracking

经典回溯使用深度优先搜索来探索分配空间，修剪任何违反行、列或对角线约束的分支。修剪通常会显着降低平均情况的搜索成本。然而，最坏情况的复杂度仍然是 O((n²)!)，因为对于一般幻方 CSP 还没有已知的多项式时间剪枝策略。该方法本质上是顺序的：一次只有一个分配路径处于活动状态。 Grover 的算法提供了结构上不同的保证。不管约束是如何构造的，只要预言机可以实现为可逆电路，它只需要 O(sqrt(N/M)) 预言机查询。与最坏情况查询计数为 Omega(N/M) 的任何经典算法相比，这提供了二次方的加速。表 2 列出了两种方法之间的比较。Classical backtracking explores the assignment space using depth-first search, pruning any branch that violates a row, column, or diagonal constraint. Pruning typically yields a substantial reduction in average-case search cost. However, the worst-case complexity remains O((n²)!), because no polynomial-time pruning strategy is known for the general magic square CSP. The approach is inherently sequential: only one assignment path is active at a time. Grover's algorithm offers a structurally different guarantee. Regardless of how the constraints are structured, it requires only O(sqrt(N/M)) oracle queries, provided the oracle can be implemented as a reversible circuit. This delivers a quadratic speedup over any classical algorithm whose worst-case query count is Omega(N/M). Table 2 lays out the comparison between the two approaches.

The theoretical oracle call count is 473, and the quantum simulation finishes in 0.0032 seconds versus 0.0033 seconds for backtracking. These runtimes are essentially identical, which is entirely expected. At this small scale, simulation overhead swamps any potential advantage, and the theoretical crossover point where quantum hardware would genuinely outperform classical backtracking lies well beyond what current simulators can reach.

5.4 实施限制5.4 Implementation Limitations

当前的实施受到几个实际限制，每个限制都指向未来工作的具体方向。 模拟开销是最直接的瓶颈。 q 量子位电路的经典状态向量模拟需要 O(2^q) 的内存和时间。对于编码 3x3 幻方的全约束预言机，q 接近 30 个量子位，这远远超过了标准工作站的内存。这迫使在实验中使用简化的预言近似。 量子比特数量随着网格大小的增加而快速增长。 n x n 幻方的完整约束预言机需要 n² 乘以上限(log2(n²)) 个主量子位以及 O(n) 个辅助量子位来进行算术和比较运算。即使 n = 3，这也已经接近经典模拟器可以处理的边界。 电路深度和硬件噪声是紧密耦合的挑战。预言机和扩散算子都依赖于深度嵌套的多控制门。在近期量子器件上，此类门很容易受到退相干和双量子位门错误的影响，并且在没有量子纠错的情况下实现所需的保真度是很困难的。The current implementation is subject to several practical constraints, each of which points toward a concrete direction for future work. Simulation overhead is the most immediate bottleneck. Classical state vector simulation of a q-qubit circuit requires memory and time that scale as O(2^q). For a full-constraint oracle encoding a 3x3 magic square, q approaches 30 qubits, which far exceeds the memory of a standard workstation. This forces the use of simplified oracle approximations in the experiments. Qubit count grows quickly with grid size. A full constraint oracle for an n x n magic square needs n² times ceiling(log2(n²)) primary qubits plus O(n) ancilla qubits for arithmetic and comparison operations. Even for n = 3, this is already near the boundary of what a classical simulator can handle. Circuit depth and hardware noise are tightly coupled challenges. The oracle and the diffusion operator both rely on deeply nested multi-controlled gates. On near-term quantum devices, such gates are vulnerable to decoherence and two-qubit gate errors, and achieving the required fidelity without quantum error correction is difficult.

可逆算术增加了门开销。将模块化加法器和相等检查器编码为可逆电路会增加门数。每个额外的约束都会使预言机变得更大，并且对于复杂的约束集，预言机可以主导总电路成本。最后，当 n 较小时，真正的量子优势是不可见的。对于 n = 3，经典算法运行得非常快，以至于模拟开销完全掩盖了任何量子加速。理论Reversible arithmetic adds gate overhead. Encoding modular adders and equality checkers as reversible circuits multiplies the gate count. Each additional constraint makes the oracle larger, and for complex constraint sets the oracle can dominate the total circuit cost. Finally, genuine quantum advantage is invisible at small n. For n = 3, classical algorithms run so quickly that simulation overhead completely buries any quantum speedup. The theoretical

5.5.讨论5.5. Discussion

实验证实所提出的量子搜索管道对于 3x3 实例是正确的。预言机准确地标记了有效的配置，并且重复的 Grover 迭代以预期的方式放大了它们的幅度，产生了与已知幻方解高概率匹配的测量结果。The experiments confirm that the proposed quantum search pipeline is correct for 3x3 instances. The oracle marks valid configurations accurately, and repeated Grover iterations amplify their amplitudes in the expected way, producing measurement outcomes that match known magic square solutions with high probability.

值得精确地描述本文中出现的两个不同的问题表述。在第 5.1 节中，Grover 的算法在预生成的 5x5 幻方内的 N = 25 个单元格位置上执行索引搜索。这是一个简单的非结构化搜索，需要 5 个量子位和 3 次 Grover 迭代。在第 5.2 节和第 5.3 节中，任务发生了根本性的变化：预言机必须在 N = 9 的排列空间内识别有效的 3x3 幻方配置！ = 362,880 个排列，需要 9 个量子位和大约 602 个理论预言机调用。这是两个不同的问题，具有非常不同的 N 值和相应不同的预期加速。将它们混为一谈会导致关于量子优势的错误主张。It is worth being precise about the two distinct problem formulations that appear in this paper. In Section 5.1, Grover's algorithm performs an index search over N = 25 cell positions within a pre-generated 5x5 magic square. This is a simple unstructured search that requires 5 qubits and 3 Grover iterations. In Sections 5.2 and 5.3, the task changes fundamentally: the oracle must identify valid 3x3 magic square configurations within a permutation space of N = 9! = 362,880 arrangements, which requires 9 qubits and approximately 602 theoretical oracle calls. These are two different problems with very different N values and correspondingly different expected speedups. Conflating them would lead to incorrect claims about quantum advantage.

Grover 算法提供的二次加速是渐近陈述。对于索引搜索（N = 25），相对于暴力破解的加速是真实的，但幅度不大。对于约束搜索 (N = 362,880)，理论上查询数量从大约 362,880 个减少到大约 602 个，这是相当可观的。然而，这种好处目前无法在经典模拟器上得到证明，在经典模拟器中，指数内存成本使整个电路变得棘手。在实践中实现加速需要量子硬件能够在所需的保真度下支持至少 9 个纠错量子位。 尽管存在这些实际限制，这项工作还是做出了重要的概念贡献。它表明魔方约束满足可以忠实地编码为量子搜索问题，并且模块化预言设计可以自然地扩展到更大的网格和其他允许可逆约束编码的 CSP。The quadratic speedup delivered by Grover's algorithm is an asymptotic statement. For the index search (N = 25), the speedup over brute force is real but modest. For the constraint search (N = 362,880), the theoretical reduction from roughly 362,880 queries to roughly 602 is substantial. However, this benefit cannot currently be demonstrated on a classical simulator, where exponential memory costs make the full circuit intractable. Realising the speedup in practice will require quantum hardware that can support at least 9 error-corrected qubits at the required fidelity. Despite these practical limitations, the work makes an important conceptual contribution. It shows that magic square constraint satisfaction can be faithfully encoded as a quantum search problem, and that the modular oracle design scales naturally to larger grids and to other CSPs that admit reversible constraint encodings.
