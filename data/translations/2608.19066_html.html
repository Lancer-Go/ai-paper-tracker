1 简介Introduction

视觉-语言-动作 (VLA) 模型Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models[3, 44, 32, 16, 15, 2, 11, 34, 7]已成为一般操纵的标准政策类别。他们在 LIBERO 等基准测试中表现强劲have become a standard policy class for general manipulation. They perform strongly on benchmarks such as LIBERO[17]和卡尔文and CALVIN[21]并且无需额外修改即可转移到现实世界的机器人上。 然而，这些成功通常依赖于一个隐含的假设：部署时的视觉条件与训练期间使用的视觉条件非常匹配。在实践中，即使相机视点的微小偏差也可能导致性能大幅下降，这凸显了视点稳健性是可靠的现实部署的一个重要的开放挑战。 然而，在实际部署环境中，通过额外的数据收集和策略微调来提高稳健性通常是不切实际的，因为新的演示成本高昂或不可用。此外，还存在政策崩溃的风险；尝试针对新的相机视图微调模型可能会破坏其原始的操作技能——这个问题通常被称为灾难性遗忘。and can transfer to real-world robots without additional modification. However, these successes often rely on an implicit assumption: the visual conditions at deployment closely match those used during training. In practice, even small deviations in camera viewpoint can lead to substantial performance degradation, highlighting viewpoint robustness as an important open challenge for reliable real-world deployment. However, improving robustness through additional data collection and policy fine-tuning is often impractical in real deployment settings, where new demonstrations are costly or unavailable. Also, there is a risk of policy collapse; trying to fine-tune the model for new camera views might ruin its original manipulation skills—a problem often called catastrophic forgetting.

更根本的是，观点转变下的表现下降并不一定反映了高级推理或控制能力的失败；相反，它们可能源于部署时的观察结果与训练期间遇到的观察结果之间的不匹配。这提出了一个更广泛的问题：More fundamentally, performance drops under viewpoint shifts do not necessarily reflect failures in high-level reasoning or control capabilities; rather, they may stem from a mismatch between deployment-time observations and those encountered during training. This raises a broader question:是否应该完全在政策参数内学习对观点变化的鲁棒性，还是可以通过调整观察空间来实现？should robustness to viewpoint variation be learned entirely within policy parameters, or can it be achieved by adapting the observation space instead?

后者的观测空间适应不仅是可能的，而且具有成本效益。 在许多现实世界的设置中，相机的变化通常很小并且保持在有限的范围内。实际上，这些扰动相对于工作空间通常很小，通常仅为几到几十厘米。我们将此实用属性称为The latter observation space adaptation is not only possible but also cost-effective. In many real-world setups, camera changes are usually small and stay within a limited range. In practice, these perturbations are often small relative to the workspace, typically amounting to only a few to a few tens of centimeters. We refer to this practical property as地点Locality。 在此假设下，视点变化仅影响图像的有限部分，而大多数像素在几何上仍然可恢复。具体来说，大多数视觉内容可以使用深度信息和相机参数映射到规范视图。这意味着学习主要需要针对狭窄的缺失区域，这可以被视为修复问题。重要的是，这个问题更多地取决于视觉领域，而不是特定的场景或策略。从这个意义上说，观点规范化并不是一个完整的小说观点综合问题，而是一个更简单的局部适应问题，很大程度上独立于场景和政策。我们将局部性视为可通过经验检验的假设。. Under this assumption, viewpoint changes affect only a limited portion of the image, while most pixels remain geometrically recoverable. Specifically, most of the visual content can be mapped to the canonical view using depth information and camera parameters. This means that learning is mainly needed for a narrow missing region, which can be treated as an inpainting problem. Importantly, this problem depends more on the visual domain than on the particular scene or policy. In this sense, viewpoint canonicalization is not a full novel-view-synthesis problem, but a simpler local adaptation problem that is largely independent of the scene and policy. We treat Locality as an empirically testable assumption.

这使得视点适应更加实用。 在局部性下，不需要为任意场景和相机基线设计大型视图合成模型。 相反，可以在冻结的 VLA 策略之前放置一个轻量级标准化模块来恢复对齐的观察结果。 这种即插即用的设计可以恢复不同策略架构、任务套件和扰动规模中损失的大部分性能，而无需任何策略重新训练。This makes viewpoint adaptation much more practical. Under Locality, there is no need for a large view-synthesis model designed for arbitrary scenes and camera baselines. Instead, a lightweight canonicalization module can be placed before a frozen VLA policy to restore aligned observations. This plug-and-play design recovers a substantial portion of the lost performance across different policy architectures, task suites, and perturbation scales without any policy retraining.

6 局限性Limitation

我们以阅读结果的界限作为结束。We close with the boundaries within which our results should be read.

单视图输入。Single-view input.本文中的标准化器消耗单个源帧及其校准深度，因此源相机根本没有观察到的任何场景内容都无法由内部画家恢复。我们期望多视图扩展——例如，融合手腕上的相机或前一步框架——能够在不改变底层局部性减少的情况下提升这个上限，我们把这个方向留给未来的工作。The canonicalizer in this paper consumes a single source frame and its calibrated depth, so any scene content that the source camera does not observe at all cannot be recovered by the in-painter. We expect a multi-view extension — for example, fusing a wrist-mounted camera or a previous-step frame — to lift this ceiling without changing the underlying Locality reduction, and we leave that direction to future work.
