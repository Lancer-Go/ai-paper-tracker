使用可解释性作为有效心电图分类的训练时间可靠性信号Using Explainability as a Training-Time Reliability Signal for Efficient ECG Classification

Veerendhra Kumar Dangeti psxvd1@nottingham.ac.uk    Xiao Gu xiao.gu@eng.ox.ac.uk    Ying Weng Ying.Weng@nottingham.edu.cn    Shreyank N Gowda shreyank.narayanagowda@nottingham.ac.uk [ [ [
摘要Abstract

训练用于临床时间序列分析的深度神经网络对计算的要求很高，但许多医疗机构缺乏重复模型开发和部署所需的资源。这一挑战在心电图分类中尤为明显，其中大数据集和长训练计划使得效率实际上非常重要。渐进式数据丢失通过在学习后从梯度更新中排除样本来降低训练成本，但它依赖于模型置信度，并且可能会保留由于噪声或模糊性而不是有用信号而难以保留的样本。在这项工作中，我们引入了 ERTS，这是一种基于可解释性的可靠性训练信号，用于高效心电图分类。 ERTS 在训练期间使用解释质量来区分信息丰富的不确定性和不可靠的不确定性。基于渐进式数据选择，我们计算候选样本的 Grad-CAM 注意力图，并得出焦点分数来衡量模型预测是否受到连贯和局部模式的支持。低焦点的样本会被过滤掉，而那些具有有意义注意力的样本会被优先进行梯度更新。我们评估了三个心电图数据集和多个骨干架构的 ERTS，显示了宏 F1 的持续改进以及有效培训成本的降低。这些结果表明，解释质量可以作为提高临床时间序列学习效率和可靠性的实用信号。代码将被发布。Training deep neural networks for clinical time-series analysis is computationally demanding, yet many healthcare settings lack the resources required for repeated model development and deployment. This challenge is particularly evident in electrocardiogram classification, where large datasets and long training schedules make efficiency practically important. Progressive Data Dropout reduces training cost by excluding samples from gradient updates once they are learned, but it relies on model confidence and may retain samples that are difficult due to noise or ambiguity rather than useful signal. In this work, we introduce ERTS, an explainability-based reliability training signal for efficient ECG classification. ERTS uses explanation quality during training to distinguish between informative and unreliable uncertainty. Building on progressive data selection, we compute Grad-CAM attention maps for candidate samples and derive a focus score that measures whether model predictions are supported by coherent and localised patterns. Samples with low focus are filtered out, while those with meaningful attention are prioritised for gradient updates. We evaluate ERTS across three ECG datasets and multiple backbone architectures, showing consistent improvements in macro-F1 alongside reduced effective training cost. These results suggest that explanation quality can serve as a practical signal for improving both efficiency and reliability in clinical time-series learning. Code will be released.

1 简介Introduction

深度学习已成为生物医学信号分析的核心工具，在心电图 (ECG) 分类、心律失常检测以及根据生理时间序列数据进行更广泛的临床预测等任务上取得了出色的性能Deep learning has become a central tool in biomedical signal analysis, achieving strong performance on tasks such as electrocardiogram (ECG) classification, arrhythmia detection, and broader clinical prediction from physiological time-series data[43, 37, 27]。现代神经架构可以直接从多导联心电图记录中学习丰富的表示，从而在现实世界的医疗保健环境中实现自动诊断支持. Modern neural architectures can learn rich representations directly from multi-lead ECG recordings, enabling automated diagnostic support in real-world healthcare settings[37]。然而，这些收益伴随着计算成本的增加。训练深度模型通常需要很长的优化计划、重复的实验和大量的硬件资源，而这些资源在临床环境中通常是有限的。训练深度模型的环境和财务成本已成为人们日益关注的问题，最近的工作强调了与现代机器学习管道相关的碳足迹和资源需求. However, these gains come with increasing computational cost. Training deep models typically requires long optimisation schedules, repeated experimentation, and substantial hardware resources, which are often limited in clinical environments. Tthe environmental and financial cost of training deep models has become an increasing concern, with recent work highlighting the carbon footprint and resource demands associated with modern machine learning pipelines[38, 31, 23, 9]。这在医疗保健领域尤其重要，其中模型可能需要跨数据集、机构和不断变化的诊断要求进行重新训练。最近的几项研究表明，深度神经网络可以在心律失常检测和心电图解读任务上实现心脏病专家级别的性能，凸显了其大规模临床部署的潜力. This is particularly relevant in healthcare, where models may need to be retrained across datasets, institutions, and evolving diagnostic requirements. Several recent studies have demonstrated that deep neural networks can achieve cardiologist-level performance on arrhythmia detection and ECG interpretation tasks, highlighting their potential for large-scale clinical deployment[12, 25, 2, 10].

应对这一挑战的大多数努力都集中在模型级效率上，包括轻量级架构、剪枝、量化和知识蒸馏Most efforts to address this challenge have focused on model-level efficiency, including lightweight architectures, pruning, quantisation, and knowledge distillation[41, 29, 11, 14, 13]。虽然这些方法可以有效减少模型大小和推理成本，但并不能直接解决训练过程中的低效率问题。标准监督学习将所有样本暴露在所有时期的梯度更新中，隐含地假设每个示例在整个优化过程中保持同等的信息量。在实践中，许多样本在训练早期变得容易，并且在以后几乎贡献不了新的学习信号，而较小的子集继续影响决策边界或揭示故障模式。因此，统一处理所有样本可能会导致冗余计算。. While effective for reducing model size and inference cost, these approaches do not directly address inefficiencies during training. Standard supervised learning exposes all samples to gradient updates across all epochs, implicitly assuming that each example remains equally informative throughout optimisation. In practice, many samples become easy early in training and contribute little new learning signal later, while a smaller subset continues to influence decision boundaries or reveal failure modes. Treating all samples uniformly can therefore lead to redundant computation.

这一观察激发了以数据为中心的方法，旨在优先考虑信息样本或减少不必要的梯度更新。课程学习、困难示例挖掘、重要性采样和数据集修剪等方法探索了使训练适应不同样本效用的不同方法This observation has motivated data-centric approaches that aim to prioritise informative samples or reduce unnecessary gradient updates. Methods such as curriculum learning, hard example mining, importance sampling, and dataset pruning explore different ways of adapting training to the varying utility of samples[3, 17, 24, 48, 7]。最近的实证研究进一步表明，一旦模型已经学习了训练数据，很大一部分的价值可能会有限，从而激发了在优化过程中动态识别和优先考虑信息样本的方法. Recent empirical studies further suggest that a substantial fraction of training data may contribute limited value once a model has already learned them, motivating approaches that dynamically identify and prioritise informative samples during optimisation[42, 40, 33, 8]。最近，渐进式数据丢失 (PDD) 表明，简单的训练时间策略可以显着减少计算量，方法是在认为已学习的情况下逐步从梯度更新中排除样本，同时在最终修订阶段重新引入完整数据集. More recently, Progressive Data Dropout (PDD) has shown that a simple training-time strategy can significantly reduce computation by progressively excluding samples from gradient updates once they are considered learned, while reintroducing the full dataset during a final revision stage[33]。尽管 PDD 简单且有效，但它依赖于模型置信度作为有用性的代理。这个问题与噪声标签学习中更广泛的观察密切相关，其中困难的例子可能反映了信息丰富的边缘情况，或者源于注释错误和数据模糊性. Despite its simplicity and effectiveness, PDD relies on model confidence as a proxy for usefulness. This issue is closely related to broader observations in noisy-label learning, where difficult examples may either reflect informative edge cases or arise from annotation errors and data ambiguity[26, 1, 21].

然而，在临床数据中，不确定性并不总是能提供信息。样本可能仍然很困难，因为它包含对学习很重要的微妙或代表性不足的模式，但它也可能仍然很困难，因为它有噪音、标记较弱或与临床有意义的信号不一致。基于置信度的选择无法区分这些情况。因此，它可能会继续将计算分配给不可靠的样本，同时丢弃具有诊断信息但早期学习的示例。这种区别如图所示。In clinical data, however, uncertainty is not always informative. A sample may remain difficult because it contains subtle or underrepresented patterns that are important for learning, but it may also remain difficult because it is noisy, weakly labelled, or poorly aligned with clinically meaningful signal. Confidence-based selection cannot distinguish between these cases. As a result, it may continue to allocate computation to unreliable samples while discarding examples that are diagnostically informative but learned early. This distinction is illustrated in Fig.1，其中具有相似不确定性的两个样本表现出根本不同的解释特征，从而激发了解释引导选择的需要。这种限制在心电图分类中尤其重要，其中数据集经常表现出标签模糊性、患者间变异性和采集伪影, where two samples with similar uncertainty exhibit fundamentally different explanatory characteristics, motivating the need for explanation-guided selection. This limitation is particularly important in ECG classification, where datasets often exhibit label ambiguity, inter-patient variability, and acquisition artefacts[43, 37, 45]。在这种情况下，训练效率与可靠性密切相关。. In this setting, training efficiency is closely tied to reliability.

可解释的人工智能（xAI）提供了解决这一差距的潜在方法。基于显着性的方法（例如 Grad-CAM）被广泛用于识别输入的哪些区域影响模型的预测Explainable artificial intelligence (xAI) offers a potential way to address this gap. Saliency-based methods such as Grad-CAM are widely used to identify which regions of an input influence a model’s prediction[32, 5]。除了 Grad-CAM 之外，还提出了多种归因方法，包括基于梯度、基于扰动和博弈论的方法，每种方法都提供了关于模型如何利用输入特征的不同视角. Beyond Grad-CAM, a wide range of attribution methods have been proposed, including gradient-based, perturbation-based, and game-theoretic approaches, each offering different perspectives on how models utilise input features[35, 39, 19, 20]。在心电图分析中，这些方法主要用于事后评估模型注意力是否与有临床意义的波形形态相一致. In ECG analysis, these methods have primarily been used post hoc to assess whether model attention aligns with clinically meaningful waveform morphology[37, 15, 22, 18]。然而，这种观点表明了更广泛的机会。如果解释质量反映了模型预测是否基于有意义的信号，那么就有可能在训练期间使用可解释性来指导哪些样本值得进一步优化。最近，人们越来越有兴趣使用解释，不仅用于解释，还用于指导模型行为，例如通过解释正则化或与人类可理解的证据保持一致. However, this perspective suggests a broader opportunity. If explanation quality reflects whether model predictions are grounded in meaningful signal, then it may be possible to use explainability during training to guide which samples deserve further optimisation. More recently, there has been growing interest in using explanations not only for interpretation but also to guide model behaviour, for example through explanation regularisation or alignment with human-understandable evidence[28, 6].

在这项工作中，我们通过以下方式探索这个想法In this work, we explore this idea throughERTS，一种基于可解释性的可靠性训练信号，用于有效的心电图分类。 ERTS 通过解释引导过滤器增强了渐进式数据选择，该过滤器区分信息丰富的不确定性和不可靠的不确定性。首先使用标准渐进数据丢失标准来识别候选样本，然后使用 Grad-CAM 来评估模型的预测是否得到连贯和局部证据的支持。然后使用从解释图得出的焦点分数来过滤掉注意力分散或低质量的样本，同时保留那些既不确定又有意义的样本。, an Explainability-based Reliability Training Signal for efficient ECG classification. ERTS augments progressive data selection with an explanation-guided filter that distinguishes between informative and unreliable uncertainty. Candidate samples are first identified using standard progressive data dropout criteria, after which Grad-CAM is used to assess whether the model’s prediction is supported by coherent and localised evidence. A focus score derived from the explanation map is then used to filter out samples with diffuse or low-quality attention, while retaining those that are both uncertain and meaningfully grounded.

这项工作以三个研究问题为指导。解释质量可以作为区分训练期间信息丰富和不可靠不确定性的可靠信号吗？合并此信号是否可以改善跨数据集和模型架构的效率和预测性能之间的权衡。解释引导的选择能否提供可解释的见解，了解哪些样本对学习贡献最大？为了回答这些问题，我们跨多个 ECG 数据集和骨干架构评估 ERTS。This work is guided by three research questions. Can explanation quality serve as a reliable signal for distinguishing informative from unreliable uncertainty during training. Does incorporating this signal improve the trade-off between efficiency and predictive performance across datasets and model architectures. Can explanation-guided selection provide interpretable insight into which samples contribute most to learning. To answer these questions, we evaluate ERTS across multiple ECG datasets and backbone architectures.

我们的结果表明，与单独基于置信度的选择相比，ERTS 持续改进了宏观 F1，同时降低了训练成本。对保留样本的分析进一步表明，该方法优先去除低焦点正常记录，同时保留诊断相关的困难病例。这些发现表明，可解释性可以发挥超越事后解释的实际作用，充当训练时间信号，提高临床时间序列学习的效率和可靠性。Our results show that ERTS consistently improves macro-F1 while reducing training cost compared to confidence-based selection alone. Analysis of retained samples further indicates that the method preferentially removes low-focus normal recordings while preserving diagnostically relevant hard cases. These findings suggest that explainability can play a practical role beyond post hoc interpretation, acting as a training-time signal that improves both efficiency and reliability in clinical time-series learning.

2 相关工作Related Work

心电图分类的深度学习。Deep learning for ECG classification.

深度学习已成为自动心电图分析的主要方法，卷积和混合神经架构在心律失常检测、诊断分类和更广泛的临床预测任务方面取得了良好的成果Deep learning has become a dominant approach for automated ECG analysis, with convolutional and hybrid neural architectures achieving strong results on arrhythmia detection, diagnostic classification, and broader clinical prediction tasks[43, 37, 27]。 PTB-XL 等公共基准在标准化评估和暴露多标签分类、患者间变异性和临床意义概括的挑战方面发挥了重要作用. Public benchmarks such as PTB-XL have played an important role in standardising evaluation and exposing the challenges of multi-label classification, inter-patient variability, and clinically meaningful generalisation[43, 37]。这些发展使心电图分类成为研究高效且值得信赖的临床学习的一种既具有实际意义又具有方法学要求的环境。. These developments have established ECG classification as both a practically important and methodologically demanding setting for studying efficient and trustworthy clinical learning.

医疗人工智能和深度学习的效率。Efficiency in medical AI and deep learning.

大量工作试图通过架构设计、模型压缩和部署时间优化来减少深度学习的计算负担。代表性方向包括EfficientNet、MobileNet等轻量级架构，以及剪枝、量化、知识蒸馏等A large body of work has sought to reduce the computational burden of deep learning through architectural design, model compression, and deployment-time optimisation. Representative directions include lightweight architectures such as EfficientNet and MobileNet, as well as pruning, quantisation, and knowledge distillation[41, 29, 11, 14, 13]。在医疗人工智能中，这些想法尤其重要，因为许多医疗机构缺乏大规模计算基础设施，使得高效的模型开发和再培训成为重要的实际问题。然而，这项工作的大部分重点是模型效率而不是训练数据效率，从而留下了如何减少优化过程中不必要的计算的问题。. In medical AI, these ideas are especially relevant because many healthcare settings lack access to large-scale computational infrastructure, making efficient model development and retraining an important practical concern. However, most of this work focuses on model efficiency rather than training-data efficiency, leaving open the question of how to reduce unnecessary computation during optimisation itself.

数据选择和训练时间效率。Data selection and training-time efficiency.

除了以模型为中心的效率之外，越来越多的工作探索所有训练样本是否需要在整个学习过程中做出同等的贡献。课程学习、困难示例挖掘、重要性采样和数据集修剪都具有这样的直觉：可以通过优先考虑信息丰富的示例或减少冗余来改进训练Beyond model-centric efficiency, a growing line of work explores whether all training samples need to contribute equally throughout learning. Curriculum learning, hard example mining, importance sampling, and dataset pruning all share the intuition that training can be improved by prioritising informative examples or reducing redundancy[3, 34, 17, 24, 48]。数据节食和其他数据修剪策略等相关方法表明，一旦模型已经学习了训练数据，大部分训练数据的价值可能有限. Related methods such as Data Diet and other data pruning strategies show that substantial portions of training data may contribute limited value once the model has already learned them[24]。渐进式数据丢失 (PDD) 通过简单的训练时间策略扩展了这一想法，该策略逐步从梯度更新中排除样本子集，并在最终修订阶段恢复完整数据集. Progressive Data Dropout (PDD) extends this idea through a simple training-time policy that progressively excludes subsets of samples from gradient updates and restores the full dataset during a final revision stage[33]。虽然此类方法可以提高训练效率，但大多数依赖于置信度、难度或基于优化的启发式方法，这些方法没有明确说明硬样本是否具有临床意义或仅仅是噪声。. While such approaches can improve training efficiency, most rely on confidence, difficulty, or optimisation-based heuristics that do not explicitly account for whether hard samples are clinically meaningful or merely noisy.

噪声或模糊标签下的数据选择。Data selection under noisy or ambiguous labels.

在标签噪音或含糊不清的情况下，区分信息性和误导性的困难示例尤其重要。先前的工作表明，硬样本有利于学习，但也可能反映注释错误、域转移或低质量数据The distinction between informative and misleading hard examples is particularly important in settings with label noise or ambiguity. Prior work has shown that hard samples can be beneficial for learning, but may also reflect annotation error, domain shift, or low-quality data[44, 45, 47]。这种紧张关系在医疗保健领域尤其重要，其中标签模糊、评估者之间的分歧和采购伪影很常见. This tension is especially relevant in healthcare, where ambiguity in labels, inter-rater disagreement, and acquisition artefacts are common[46, 16, 4]。因此，在整个训练过程中仍然困难的样本不一定有价值。这促使选择标准超越了单纯的置信度，并询问模型的不确定性是否得到有意义的信号的支持。. As a result, a sample that remains difficult throughout training is not necessarily valuable. This motivates selection criteria that go beyond confidence alone and ask whether model uncertainty is supported by meaningful signal.

医学机器学习的可解释性。Explainability in medical machine learning.

可解释的人工智能（xAI）已成为检查安全关键领域模型行为的重要工具。在医学成像和信号分析中，基于显着性的方法（例如 Grad-CAM 和 Grad-CAM++）通常用于识别对预测最负责的输入区域Explainable artificial intelligence (xAI) has become an important tool for inspecting model behaviour in safety-critical domains. In medical imaging and signal analysis, saliency-based approaches such as Grad-CAM and Grad-CAM++ are commonly used to identify the input regions most responsible for a prediction[32, 5]。在心电图分析中，可解释性主要以事后方式使用，以评估习得的注意力是否与临床上合理的形态或已知的诊断标记相一致. Across three datasets and three backbone architectures, ERTS improves the best-performing PDD configuration while reducing effective epochs. 这项工作的主要限制是 ERTS 目前依赖 Grad-CAM 和简单的焦点评分标准。选择 Grad-CAM 是因为它是按类计算的，不需要输入扰动，从而使其与每历元过滤循环兼容。基于扰动的方法（例如 LIME）在训练过程中会非常昂贵，而更细粒度的归因方法（例如集成梯度）会增加额外的成本，即使所提出的焦点分数只需要相对浓度测量而不是像素精确的归因。因此，Grad-CAM 的空间聚合足以用于 ERTS 中使用的二进制保留/丢弃决策。然而，解释质量可能并不总是等同于空间或时间集中，因为一些心电图异常可能涉及跨时间或导联的分布模式。因此，未来的工作应该探索 Grad-CAM++、积分梯度、自适应阈值和临床知情的解释质量测量。对保留和过滤的样本进行专家审查的进一步验证也将有助于澄清低焦点示例是否对应于人工制品、模糊标签或真正困难的案例。The main limitation of this work is that ERTS currently relies on Grad-CAM and a simple focus-score criterion. Grad-CAM was chosen because it is computed per class without requiring input perturbation, making it compatible with a per-epoch filtering loop. Perturbation-based methods such as LIME would be prohibitively expensive during training, while more fine-grained attribution methods such as Integrated Gradients would add additional cost even though the proposed focus score only requires a relative concentration measure rather than pixel-precise attribution. Grad-CAM’s spatial aggregation is therefore sufficient for the binary keep/drop decision used in ERTS. Nevertheless, explanation quality may not always be equivalent to spatial or temporal concentration, since some ECG abnormalities may involve distributed patterns across time or leads. Future work should therefore explore Grad-CAM++, Integrated Gradients, adaptive thresholding, and clinically informed explanation-quality measures. Further validation with expert review of retained and filtered samples would also help clarify whether low-focus examples correspond to artefacts, ambiguous labels, or genuinely difficult cases.

总体而言，ERTS 将可解释性的作用从事后解释扩展到训练时间优化。该方法不仅询问样本是否困难，还询问样本是否因有意义的原因而困难。这为更高效、更可靠的临床时间序列学习提供了一种简单但有效的途径。Overall, ERTS extends the role of explainability from post hoc interpretation to training-time optimisation. Rather than only asking whether a sample is difficult, the method asks whether it is difficult for a meaningful reason. This provides a simple but effective route toward more efficient and reliable clinical time-series learning.

7 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了 ERTS，这是一种可解释性引导的数据选择框架，用于高效的临床时间序列学习。中心思想是使用解释质量作为训练时的可靠性信号，使模型能够区分信息丰富的不确定性和不可靠的不确定性。通过将此信号与渐进式数据丢失相结合，ERTS 细化了用于梯度更新的样本集，优先考虑那些表现出低置信度和有意义的注意力模式的样本。In this work, we introduced ERTS, an explainability-guided data selection framework for efficient clinical time-series learning. The central idea is to use explanation quality as a training-time reliability signal, enabling the model to distinguish between informative and unreliable uncertainty. By integrating this signal with Progressive Data Dropout, ERTS refines the set of samples used for gradient updates, prioritising those that exhibit both low confidence and meaningful attention patterns.

在多个心电图数据集和骨干架构中，ERTS 不断改善预测性能和训练效率之间的权衡。该方法实现了更高的宏 F1，同时减少了优化所需的有效 epoch 数量。值得注意的是，不仅基于置信度的选择策略得到了改进，而且与随机采样相结合时也得到了改进，这表明解释质量可以作为过滤不可靠训练数据的通用信号。Across multiple ECG datasets and backbone architectures, ERTS consistently improves the trade-off between predictive performance and training efficiency. The method achieves higher macro-F1 while reducing the number of effective epochs required for optimisation. Notably, improvements are observed not only for confidence-based selection strategies but also when combined with random sampling, suggesting that explanation quality acts as a general-purpose signal for filtering unreliable training data.

Rather than serving solely as a post hoc interpretability tool, explanation methods can play an active role during optimisation by guiding how models learn from data.
