摘要Abstract

众所周知，大型语言模型 (LLM) 会放弃其最初的立场以迎合用户的反对。虽然之前的研究很大程度上将这种行为归因于在强化学习过程中从人类反馈中学到的阿谀奉承，但我们假设一致性也是由模型在推理时的认知不确定性驱动的。在本文中，我们介绍了 MUSE，这是一个两阶段评估框架，用于理清推动 LLM 一致性的机制。具体来说，MUSE 将模型在响应查询时的认知不确定性与其在后续回合中屈服于用户抵制的可能性进行映射。我们证明，推动从众的机制不仅仅限于阿谀奉承。具体来说，我们描述了共同推动一致性的两个不同因素：Large language models (LLMs) are known to abandon their initial stance to conform to user pushback. While prior research largely attributes this behavior to sycophancy learned during reinforcement learning from human feedback, we hypothesize that conformity is also driven by a model’s epistemic uncertainty at inference time. In this paper, we introduce MUSE, a two-stage evaluation framework to disentangle the mechanisms driving LLM conformity. Specifically, MUSE maps a model’s epistemic uncertainty in responding to a query against its likelihood to yield to user pushback in a subsequent turn. We demonstrate that the mechanisms driving conformity extend beyond sycophancy alone. Specifically, we characterize two distinct factors that jointly drive conformity:阿谀奉承sycophantic conformity，即使模型的初始响应具有绝对的确定性，模型也会与用户的反馈保持一致，并且, where a model aligns with user pushback even with absolute certainty in its initial response, and不确定性驱动的一致性uncertainty-driven conformity，模型的一致性可能性随着其不确定性的增加而增加。此外，我们进行消融研究，以证明阿谀奉承和不确定性驱动的从众都会随着 1) LLM 对用户的专业知识的认知和 2) 用户建议的合理性而增长。更广泛地说，MUSE 通过区分联盟引起的阿谀奉承和训练语料库驱动的不确定性，提供更有针对性的干预策略。, where a model’s likelihood for conformity increases alongside its uncertainty. Furthermore, we conduct ablation studies to demonstrate that both sycophantic conformity and uncertainty-driven conformity grow with 1) the LLM’s perceived expertise of the user and 2) the plausibility of the user’s suggestions. More broadly, MUSE informs more targeted intervention strategies by distinguishing alignment-induced sycophancy and training-corpora-driven uncertainty.

2 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

2.1 法学硕士中的阿谀奉承Sycophancy in LLMs

先前的文献将阿谀奉承定义为模型将其响应与用户陈述或推断的信念相一致的现象，即使这些信念不合逻辑或事实上不准确Prior literature defines sycophancy as the phenomenon where a model aligns its response with a user’s stated or inferred beliefs, even when those beliefs are illogical or factually inaccurateSharma et al. (2023)。最近的证据将这种行为归因于 RLHF，它将聊天机器人优化为有用的助手. Recent evidence attributes this behavior to RLHF, which optimizes chatbots to be helpful assistantsKalai et al. (2025)。然而，在针对有用性进行优化时，模型会无意中被训练为将有用性优先于事实准确性。评估阿谀奉承的早期方法主要依赖于用不合逻辑的请求或事实上不准确的信息来提示模型. However, in optimizing towards helpfulness, models are inadvertently trained to prioritize helpfulness over factual accuracy. Early approaches for evaluating sycophancy have relied primarily on prompting a model with an illogical request or factually inaccurate informationChen et al. (2025); Ibrahim et al. (2026)并衡量其与有缺陷的前提保持一致的倾向，而不是抵制或纠正它。and measuring its tendency to align with the flawed premises rather than resist or correct it.

随着法学硕士的应用超越单次查询，为聊天机器人和副驾驶提供动力，最近的研究评估了多轮对话中阿谀奉承的影响。这些评估通过在最初选择答案（通常在问答设置中）后使模型受到用户的抵制来模拟对话压力，表明模型经常与实际上不准确的用户建议保持一致，并在对话过程中复合这些错误As LLMs are adopted beyond single-shot queries to power chatbots and copilots, more recent studies have evaluated the impact of sycophancy in multi-turn dialogues. These evaluations simulate conversational pressure by subjecting a model to user pushback after initially selecting an answer (typically in a question-answer setting), showing that models frequently align with factually inaccurate user suggestions and compound these errors across turns of conversationLaban et al. (2026); Guo et al. (2026); Kim et al. (2026)。具体来说，Kim 等人。将模型的初始响应形式化为对提示的评估，并将该立场的任何后续变化视为阿谀奉承的结果. Specifically, Kim et al. formalize a model’s initial response as its assessment of the prompt and any subsequent changing in that stance to be the result of sycophancyKim et al. (2026).

9 讨论和启示Discussion and Implications

9.1 规范性与信息性的一致性Normative vs. Informational Conformity

纯粹的阿谀奉承和不确定性驱动的从众之间的区别密切反映了既定的社会心理学理论。在这些文献中，人类的顺从性通常被归类为源于以下任一方面：The distinction between pure sycophancy and uncertainty-driven conformity closely mirrors established theories of social psychology. In these literature, human conformity is often categorized as stemming from either规范影响normative influence，为了维持社会和谐或避免冲突而发生让步，或, where yielding occurs to maintain social harmony or avoid conflict, or信息影响力informational influence，其中屈服发生是因为个人不确定并假设另一方拥有更准确的信息, where yield occurs because an individual is uncertain and assumes the other party possesses more accurate informationDeutsch and Gerard (1955)。我们的研究结果表明，法学硕士通过联盟引起的阿谀奉承表现出规范影响，同时通过不确定性驱动的一致性表现出信息影响。当模型缺乏强大的先验时，它可能会遵循其他模型作为正确答案的启发式方法，而用户感知到的专业知识会加剧这种行为。. Our findings suggest that LLMs manifest normative influence through alignment-induced sycophancy, while exhibiting informational influence through uncertainty-driven conformity. When a model lacks a strong prior, it is likely to defer to others as a heuristic for the correct answer, a behavior which is exacerbated by a user’s perceived expertise.

9.2 实践中的意义Implications in Practice

我们相信，纯粹的阿谀奉承和不确定性驱动的从众之间的区别可以为未来减少无根据的从众策略提供信息。由于遵从行为会随着模型和任务的不同而波动，因此调整策略必须针对具体情况而不是通用的。在高风险环境中，研究人员必须刻意平衡模型信念与灵活性。例如，协助医生的副驾驶应该保持协作，将自己的不确定性权重更高，以便专家纠正。相反，由于患者普遍缺乏领域专业知识，因此面向患者的诊断工具应该具有更高的信念和抵御阻力的能力。实现这种平衡需要解开潜在的整合机制，解决协调过程中阿谀奉承引发的缺陷，同时通过训练语料库和模型设计来减轻不确定性驱动的整合。We believe the distinction between pure sycophancy and uncertainty-driven conformity can inform future strategies for mitigating unwarranted conformity. Because conformity behaviors fluctuate across models and tasks, alignment strategies must be context-specific rather than universal. In high-risk settings, researchers must deliberately balance model conviction with flexibility. For example, a copilot assisting a physician should remain collaborative, weighting its own uncertainty higher to allow for expert correction. Conversely, because patients generally lack domain expertise, a patient-facing diagnostic tool should behave with higher conviction and resilience to pushback. Achieving this balance requires disentangling the underlying mechanisms of conformity, addressing sycophancy-induced flaws during alignment, while mitigating uncertainty-driven conformity through training corpora and model design.

10 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 MUSE，它在前沿模型和开放权重模型中证明了 LLM 一致性可以分解为两种不同的现象：（1）纯粹的阿谀奉承，模型在绝对确定性的情况下放弃了其初始立场；（2）不确定性驱动的一致性，模型产生的可能性与其认知不确定性一起扩展。根据经验，我们的研究结果表明，模型对推回的脆弱性是其基线阿谀奉承倾向和推理时的不确定性的共同作用。这项工作强调了法学硕士的一致性是一种复杂的、多方面的行为，对这种一致性的评估应该在对话压力之前考虑到这些系统的不确定性。此外，它使我们能够评估模型由于不确定性而不是阿谀奉承而导致的从众倾向。In this paper, we introduced MUSE, which demonstrates in both frontier and open-weight models, that LLM conformity can be decoupled into two distinct phenomena: (1) pure sycophancy, where a model abandons its initial stance despite absolute certainty, and (2) uncertainty-driven conformity, where a model’s likelihood of yielding scales alongside its epistemic uncertainty. Empirically, our findings show that a model’s vulnerability to pushback is a joint function of both its baseline sycophantic tendencies and its uncertainty at inference time. This work underscores that LLM conformity is a complex, multi-faceted behavior, and that evaluations of such conformity should account for the uncertainty of these systems prior to conversational pressure. Moreover, it enables us to assess a model’s tendency to conformity due to uncertainty as opposed to sycophancy.

局限性Limitations

我们的研究提出了一些值得未来探索的局限性。首先，在绝对确定性下将阿谀奉承操作为顺从（Our study presents several limitations that warrant future exploration. First, while operationalizing sycophancy as conformity under absolute certainty (H=0H=0）隔离了用户抵制的影响，但它没有隔离指令调整过程本身的影响。未来的工作应该比较基础模型和指令调整模型，以量化对齐引起的阿谀奉承。其次，将评估限制为包含有限答案选项集的设置可以实现精确的熵计算，但可能无法捕获开放式生成任务中存在的微妙的、风格上的一致性表现。最后，我们的框架依赖于两轮交互。未来的工作应该探索现实世界的互动，其中可能涉及多轮对话和复杂的修辞策略，这些策略可能会在扩展的轨迹上复合阿谀奉承的行为。一项有前途的研究方向是如何采用多智能体和 RAG 系统来减轻我们使用 MUSE 观察到的无根据的一致性。) isolates the impact of user pushback, it does not separate the effects of the instruction-tuning process itself. Future work should compare base and instruct-tuned models to quantify alignment-induced sycophancy. Second, constraining evaluations to settings containing a finite answer option set enables precise entropy calculation but may fail to capture the subtle, stylistic manifestations of conformity present in open-ended generative tasks. Finally, our framework relies on a two-turn interaction. Future work should explore real-world interactions which may involve, multi-turn dialogues and complex rhetorical strategies that may compound sycophantic behavior over extended trajectories. One promising line of study is how multi-agent and RAG systems can be adopted to mitigate the unwarranted conformity we observed using MUSE.

道德声明Ethics Statement

本研究仅使用公开数据集，未收集私人企业或患者数据。我们的研究结果应该作为一个警示性的例子，防止在高风险环境中部署可能不一致或评估不当的法学硕士。我们承认我们的研究具有双重用途的潜力：理论上，对手可以利用我们关于权威框架和认知不确定性的见解作为蓝图来有意操纵模型的一致性。然而，我们认为，透明地揭露这些漏洞是让社区能够制定更强大、更有弹性的缓解策略的必要的第一步。This study uses only publicly available datasets, and no private business or patient data was collected. Our findings should serve as a cautionary example against deploying potentially misaligned or inappropriately evaluated LLMs in high-risk settings. We acknowledge the dual-use potential of our research: adversaries could theoretically exploit our insights regarding authoritative framing and epistemic uncertainty as a blueprint to intentionally manipulate model conformity. However, we believe that transparently exposing these vulnerabilities is a necessary first step toward equipping the community to develop more robust, resilient mitigation strategies.
