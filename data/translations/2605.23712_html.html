从稀疏测量中重建流场的算子学习：语言模型方法Operator Learning for Reconstructing Flow Fields from Sparse Measurements: a Language Model Approach

Qian Zhang Division of Applied Mathematics, Brown University George Em Karniadakis george_karniadakis@brown.edu Division of Applied Mathematics, Brown University
摘要Abstract

从稀疏测量中重建流场是流体力学中的一个基本问题，对建模、控制和设计具有广泛的影响。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种新颖的算子学习框架，该框架利用语言模型的架构以无网格的方式执行流重建。我们将流场重建重新表述为序列到序列的学习任务，其中稀疏测量被视为上下文，未观察到的位置被视为查询。我们的模型学习从稀疏输入重建完整的流场，有效地捕获空间相关性和远程依赖性。我们在四个基准数据集上评估所提出的方法：（1）二维涡街模拟，（2）美国本土的每日平均温度数据，（3）基于耗散粒子动力学的三维血流模拟，以及（4）通过粒子跟踪测速获得的三维湍流射流测量。在所有情况下，即使数据高度不完整（观察到的数据少于 10%），我们的方法也表现出了有竞争力的重建精度，并实现了高效的性能。研究结果凸显了语言模型作为科学数据重建的强大且可扩展工具的潜力，并为科学和工程应用的基础模型的开发提出了一个有希望的方向。Reconstructing flow fields from sparse measurements is a fundamental problem in fluid mechanics with broad implications for modeling, control, and design. In this work, we propose a novel operator learning framework that leverages the architecture of language models to perform flow reconstruction in a mesh-free manner. We reformulate flow field reconstruction as a sequence-to-sequence learning task, where sparse measurements are treated as context and unobserved locations as queries. Our model learns to reconstruct the full flow field from sparse inputs, effectively capturing spatial correlations and long-range dependencies. We evaluate the proposed approach on four benchmark datasets: (1) two-dimensional vortex street simulations, (2) daily average temperature data across the contiguous United States, (3) three-dimensional blood flow simulations based on dissipative particle dynamics, and (4) three-dimensional turbulent jet flow measurements obtained via particle tracking velocimetry. Across all cases, our method demonstrates competitive reconstruction accuracy, even with highly incomplete data (less than 10% observed), and achieves efficient performance. The results highlight the potential of language models as robust and scalable tools for scientific data reconstruction, and suggest a promising direction toward the development of foundation models for scientific and engineering applications.

1 简介Introduction

机器学习的最新进展在广泛的科学和工程学科领域取得了重大突破Recent advances in machine learning have led to significant breakthroughs across a wide range of scientific and engineering disciplines[30, 10, 2, 5, 15, 41, 44, 33, 36, 35, 16, 25, 3, 6, 43, 45, 18]。受益于计算资源的快速增长，以前传统方法认为难以解决的问题现在可以重新表述为可以通过神经网络模型解决的优化任务。. Problems previously considered intractable by conventional approaches can now be reformulated as optimization tasks solvable by neural network models, benefiting from the rapid growth of computational resources.

在这种范式中，算子学习已成为复杂系统建模的强大框架Within this paradigm, operator learning has emerged as a powerful framework for modeling complex systems[23, 3, 13, 17, 40]。算子学习旨在使用神经网络来近似解算子，即从输入函数或初始/边界条件到输出解的映射。这些神经算子可以作为传统数值求解器的有效替代者，提供更快的推理速度，尤其是在高维或实时场景中。. Operator learning aims to use neural network to approximate solution operators, which are mappings from input functions or initial/boundary conditions to output solutions. These neural operators serve as efficient surrogates to traditional numerical solvers, offering significantly faster inference, especially in high-dimensional or real-time scenarios.

最近的一些发展已经证明了神经算子在不同方面的能力。 DeepONetSeveral recent developments have demonstrated the capabilities of neural operators in different aspects. The Laplace Neural Operator (LNO)[3]利用拉普拉斯变换来处理瞬态和非周期性动态。 ViTOleverages Laplace transforms to handle transient and non-periodic dynamics. ViTO[29]基于 Vision Transformers，通过将基于 Transformer 的架构合并到算子学习管道中，展示了在常规网格上高效的算子学习。, based on Vision Transformers, demonstrates efficient operator learning on regular meshes by incorporating transformer-based architectures into the operator learning pipeline.

尽管取得了这些进展，将算子学习应用于重建任务仍然具有挑战性，特别是在观测稀疏、嘈杂且分布不规则的环境中。一个代表性的例子是流体力学中的射流重建，其中只有一小部分流场可以通过测量获得，并且通常没有在结构化网格上对齐。Despite this progress, applying operator learning to reconstruction tasks remains challenging, especially in settings where the observations are sparse, noisy, and irregularly distributed. A representative example is jet flow reconstruction in fluid mechanics, where only a small portion of the flow field is accessible through measurements, often not aligned on a structured grid.

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新发展Recent developments in large language models (LLMs)[28, 7]已经表现出了对数据中的复杂模式和远程依赖性进行建模的卓越能力。与此同时，上下文学习的概念——模型根据输入中提供的示例学习执行任务，无需更新权重——已被探索用于科学机器学习have demonstrated remarkable ability to model complex patterns and long-range dependencies in the data. Inspired by these advancements, we propose RFormer (Reconstruction Former), an in-context operator learning framework that reformulates the sparse reconstruction problem using the architecture of decoder-only transformers. This formulation naturally aligns with transformer architectures and enables the model to generalize to arbitrary query locations without requiring structured meshes.

2.1 相关工作Related work

Traditional methods.经典的稀疏重建方法为这个问题提供了强大且可解释的基线。 Interpolation methodsClassical sparse-reconstruction methods provide strong and interpretable baselines for this problem. Interpolation methods[20]直接从附近的观测值估计未知值，并且很容易应用于不规则点集，但它们主要依赖于局部平滑度，并且可能难以处理远离传感器的相干结构。 Krigingestimate unknown values directly from nearby observations and are simple to apply on irregular point sets, but they rely mainly on local smoothness and can struggle with coherent structures far from sensors. Kriging[27]用概率空间协方差模型扩展插值，当协方差假设合适时，使其成为一种有原则的统计方法。 Gappy PODextends interpolation with a probabilistic spatial covariance model, making it a principled statistical approach when the covariance assumptions are appropriate. Voronoi CNN.Voronoi 曲面细分 CNN 为不规则传感器的稀疏流重建提供了一种神经替代方案Voronoi-tessellation CNNs provide a neural alternative for sparse flow reconstruction from irregular sensors[11]。他们通过 Voronoi 细分将域区域分配给附近的传感器，将分散的传感器测量结果转换为网格状表示，然后可以应用标准卷积网络。这种方法对于二维领域是有效的，其中不规则观察可以被光栅化到类似图像的域上，但对于完全非结构化的三维点云来说，预处理和卷积架构不太自然。. This approach is effective for two-dimensional fields where the irregular observations can be rasterized onto an image-like domain, but the preprocessing and convolutional architecture are less natural for fully unstructured three-dimensional point clouds.

4 结论Conclusion

在这项研究中，我们引入了一种新颖的算子学习框架，该框架利用语言模型的架构以无网格的方式从稀疏测量中重建流场。通过将重建任务转化为序列到序列的问题，我们的方法有效地捕获了流体动力学数据中固有的空间相关性和远程依赖性。In this study, we introduced a novel operator learning framework that leverages the architecture of language models to reconstruct flow fields from sparse measurements in a mesh-free manner. By casting the reconstruction task as a sequence-to-sequence problem, our approach effectively captures spatial correlations and long-range dependencies inherent in fluid dynamics data.

我们在四个不同的数据集上验证了我们的方法：（1）二维涡街模拟，（2）美国本土的日平均温度，（3）基于耗散粒子动力学的三维血流模拟，以及（4）通过粒子跟踪测速获得的三维湍流射流测量。在所有情况下，我们的模型都使用高度稀疏的输入（观测到的空间点少于 10%）实现了有竞争力的重建精度，并在推理时展示了计算效率。We validated our method on four different datasets: (1) two-dimensional vortex street simulations, (2) daily average temperature across the contiguous United States, (3) three-dimensional blood flow simulations based on dissipative particle dynamics, and (4) three-dimensional turbulent jet flow measurements acquired via particle tracking velocimetry. In all cases, our model achieved competitive reconstruction accuracy using highly sparse inputs (fewer than 10% of spatial points observed) and demonstrated computational efficiency at inference time.

结果凸显了将语言模型架构集成到科学和工程问题的算子学习框架中的潜力。通过将观察到的数据视为上下文，将未观察到的位置视为查询，该模型可以有效地推断出缺失的信息，例如自然语言处理中的问答。The results highlight the potential of integrating language model architectures into operator learning frameworks for scientific and engineering problems. By treating observed data as context and unobserved positions as queries, the model effectively infers missing information like question-answering in natural language processing.

未来的工作将集中于扩展该框架以纳入时间动力学，从而以更高的精度实时重建不断变化的流场。此外，我们的目标是开发用于一般流预测和重建任务的基础模型，这些模型可以针对特定应用进行微调或通过上下文学习直接使用。这些进步可以显着加速流体力学及相关领域的模拟和设计工作流程。Future work will focus on extending this framework to incorporate temporal dynamics, enabling real-time reconstruction of evolving flow fields with improved accuracy. In addition, we aim to develop foundation models for general flow prediction and reconstruction tasks, which can be fine-tuned for specific applications or used directly via in-context learning. Such advancements could significantly accelerate simulation and design workflows in fluid mechanics and related fields.
