软件工程和软件安全交叉点的大型语言模型：以证据为中心的结构化调查和研究议程Large Language Models at the Intersection of Software Engineering and Software Security: An Evidence-Centered Structured Survey and Research Agenda

Wei Lin    Tao Zhou    Zhaofei Xie       Changgui Hong Affiliation: Nanjing Liancheng Intelligent Technology Group, Nanjing, China Affiliation: {linwei, zhoutao, xiezhaofei, hongchanggui}@chinaliancheng.com
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 正在从代码完成转向存储库规模的代理，用于检索上下文、编辑文件、执行工具以及参与安全敏感的工作流程。然而，这些系统的证据仍然分为以功能任务完成为中心的软件工程评估和以漏洞检测、安全生成或面向漏洞利用的验证为中心的软件安全评估。这项以证据为中心的结构化调查综合了截至 2026 年 5 月 31 日的代表性工作，涉及软件工程任务、软件安全任务、适应机制、工件粒度和评估设计。除了任务分类之外，我们还引入了一个保证框架，该框架将功能正确性、安全性、操作可靠性、证据来源和代理权限分开。审查表明，执行反馈和存储库访问可以显着提高工程任务的完成度，但它们本身并不能建立安全性；相反，静态分析标签或漏洞分类分数很少能确定可部署的正确性。我们识别反复出现的有效性威胁——薄弱的测试预言、重复和暂时泄露的数据、不断变化的代理工具、仅代理的安全检查以及少报的预算和人为干预——并得出用于交叉研究比较的最低报告协议。由此产生的研究议程优先考虑联合安全和功能基准、存储库规模的威胁模型、校准的人类监督、纵向可维护性证据和可重复的代理评估。中心结论是，模型能力应该被判断为由适合任务的证据支持的保证案例，而不是单一的基准分数。Large Language Models (LLMs) are moving from code completion toward repository-scale agents that retrieve context, edit files, execute tools, and participate in security-sensitive workflows. The evidence for these systems, however, remains divided between software engineering evaluations centered on functional task completion and software security evaluations centered on vulnerability detection, secure generation, or exploit-oriented validation. This evidence-centered structured survey synthesizes representative work available through May 31, 2026 across software engineering tasks, software security tasks, adaptation mechanisms, artifact granularity, and evaluation design. In addition to a task taxonomy, we introduce an assurance framework that separates functional correctness, security, operational reliability, evidence provenance, and agent authority. The review shows that execution feedback and repository access can substantially improve engineering task completion, but do not by themselves establish security; conversely, static-analysis labels or vulnerability-classification scores rarely establish deployable correctness. We identify recurring validity threats—weak test oracles, duplicated and temporally leaked data, changing agent harnesses, proxy-only security checks, and under-reported budgets and human intervention—and derive a minimum reporting protocol for cross-study comparison. The resulting research agenda prioritizes jointly secure-and-functional benchmarks, repository-scale threat models, calibrated human oversight, longitudinal maintainability evidence, and reproducible agent evaluation. The central conclusion is that model capability should be judged as an assurance case supported by task-appropriate evidence, rather than by a single benchmark score.

Practitioner evidence also suggests productivity benefits for selected development activities, although reported gains depend strongly on task, developer experience, codebase context, and evaluation design[21].

2 审查方法Review Methodology

2.1 范围和研究问题Scope and Research Questions

本文是一篇以证据为中心的结构化叙述和三级调查，而不是一篇完整的协议驱动的系统文献综述。搜索截止日期为 2026 年 5 月 31 日。我们优先考虑基础模型和基准论文、最近的系统综述、实证评估以及将开发与软件安全直接联系起来的工作。综合单元不仅仅是论文，而是由特定工件、数据集、执行环境、指标和威胁模型支持的声明。由于基础研究使用不同的模型版本、提示、工具、重试预算、存储库快照和基准变体，因此报告的分数被视为特定于研究的观察结果，而不是作为汇总荟萃分析的输入。这一范围故意是透明的：该综述提供了可追溯的概念综合和可扩展的证据包，但并不要求对文献进行详尽的回忆。This article is an evidence-centered structured narrative and tertiary survey rather than a completed protocol-driven systematic literature review. The search cutoff is May 31, 2026. We prioritize foundational model and benchmark papers, recent systematic reviews, empirical evaluations, and work that directly connects development with software security. The unit of synthesis is not the paper alone, but the claim supported by a particular artifact, dataset, execution environment, metric, and threat model. Because the underlying studies use different model versions, prompts, tools, retry budgets, repository snapshots, and benchmark variants, reported scores are treated as study-specific observations rather than as inputs to a pooled meta-analysis. This scope is deliberately transparent: the review provides a traceable conceptual synthesis and an extensible evidence package, but does not claim exhaustive recall of the literature.

审查以四个研究问题为指导：The review is guided by four research questions:

  1. 问题一：RQ1:

    基于 LLM 的系统可以解决哪些软件工程和软件安全任务，它们需要哪些工件和上下文？Which software engineering and software security tasks are addressed by LLM-based systems, and what artifacts and context do they require?

  2. 问题2：RQ2:

    提示、微调、检索和代理工具的使用如何改变这些任务中观察到的功能和故障模式？How do prompting, fine-tuning, retrieval, and agentic tool use change the capabilities and failure modes observed across these tasks?

  3. 问题3：RQ3:

    哪些基准和指标提供了可靠的证据，哪些有效性威胁阻止了直接的交叉研究比较？Which benchmarks and metrics provide credible evidence, and which validity threats prevent direct cross-study comparison?

  4. 问题四：RQ4:

    工程和安全需求在哪里交叉，集成代理需要什么样的评估和治理机制？Where do engineering and security requirements intersect, and what evaluation and governance mechanisms are needed for integrated agents?

2.4 声称校准Claim Calibration

我们使用四个证据级别来校准结论。We calibrate conclusions using four evidence levels.E1（说明）E1 (illustrative)由定性示例、演示或小型策划任务组成，仅支持可行性主张。consists of qualitative examples, demonstrations, or small curated tasks and supports feasibility claims only.E2（受控基准）E2 (controlled benchmark)添加可重复的数据集、基线和与任务相关的指标，支持固定配置内的比较声明。adds repeatable datasets, baselines, and task-aligned metrics, supporting comparative claims within a fixed configuration.E3（存储库和执行）E3 (repository and execution)需要真实的项目背景、可执行的测试或漏洞利用、环境捕获和回归检查，支持对抽样项目的系统级声明。requires realistic project context, executable tests or exploits, environment capture, and regression checks, supporting system-level claims on the sampled projects.E4（运行）E4 (operational)此外还需要纵向使用、人力或成本衡量、事件或维护结果以及治理控制。更高级别的证据并不自动意味着更好的模型质量，但它支持更广泛的部署主张。因此，在整个调查中，我们将“在基准上执行”与“在实践中可靠或安全”区分开来。additionally requires longitudinal use, human-effort or cost measurement, incident or maintenance outcomes, and governance controls. Evidence at a higher level does not automatically imply better model quality, but it supports a broader deployment claim. Throughout the survey, we therefore distinguish “performs on a benchmark” from “is reliable or secure in practice.”

8.4 对抗鲁棒性和代理治理Adversarial Robustness and Agent Governance

Semantics-preserving renaming, control-flow restructuring, dead-code insertion, misleading comments, poisoned retrieval documents, and tool-output manipulation can alter model judgments. Repository-level agents additionally process untrusted files and issue text that may contain prompt injections. Evaluations should distinguish robustness to natural code evolution from robustness to deliberate manipulation and should test both the model and the surrounding tool chain.

这项调查通过共享分类法、明确的评估配置和以保证为中心的证据视图将软件工程和软件安全联系起来。综合回答了以下四个研究问题。对于 RQ1，任务范围从功能级生成和分类到存储库问题解决、安全修补、模糊测试和工具介导的分析；决定性的区别通常是可用的工件和上下文，而不仅仅是任务标签。对于 RQ2，提示和微调对于有界任务仍然很重要，而随着存储库上下文和迭代的增加，检索、执行反馈和代理变得更加重要。这些机制改变了能力和故障模式，并且它们的影响不能归因于没有受控消融的基本模型。This survey connected software engineering and software security through a shared taxonomy, an explicit evaluation configuration, and an assurance-centered view of evidence. The synthesis answers the four research questions as follows. These mechanisms change both capability and failure modes, and their effects cannot be attributed to the base model without controlled ablations.

文献支持谨慎乐观，而不是无条件自动化。存储库访问和迭代工具的使用可以提高任务完成率，但它们也带来了线束依赖、更大的攻击面、更高的成本和可重复性挑战。静态分析器、测试和法学硕士法官是保证案例的有用组成部分，但单独而言并不是足够的基本事实。长期可维护性、多语言传输、对抗稳健性和监督的组织成本仍然没有得到充分证明。The literature supports cautious optimism rather than unconditional automation. Repository access and iterative tool use can improve task completion, yet they also introduce harness dependence, larger attack surfaces, higher cost, and reproducibility challenges. Static analyzers, tests, and LLM judges are useful components of an assurance case but are not individually sufficient ground truth. Long-term maintainability, multilingual transfer, adversarial robustness, and the organizational cost of oversight remain under-evidenced.

因此，中心研究重点不是另一个孤立的排行榜，而是累积的证据基础设施：版本化基准、可执行环境、污染感知分割、联合功能和安全验证、透明的代理权限、完整的轨迹和预算日志以及以人为本的纵向结果。应当根据证据来宣称取得了进展。在此标准下，基于 LLM 的软件系统值得信赖，不是因为它产生了合理的补丁或获得了高标量分数，而是因为它的行为得到了可重现、与威胁模型一致且具有治理意识的保证案例的支持。The central research priority is consequently not another isolated leaderboard, but a cumulative evidence infrastructure: versioned benchmarks, executable environments, contamination-aware splits, joint functional and security validation, transparent agent permissions, complete trajectory and budget logs, and longitudinal human-centered outcomes. Progress should be claimed in proportion to the evidence. Under this standard, an LLM-based software system is trustworthy not because it produces a plausible patch or achieves a high scalar score, but because its behavior is supported by a reproducible, threat-model-aligned, and governance-aware assurance case.
