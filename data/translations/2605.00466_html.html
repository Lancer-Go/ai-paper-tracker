摘要Abstract

现实世界的时间序列预测面临着非平稳统计特性的根本挑战，包括均值和方差随时间的变化。 虽然可逆实例标准化 (RevIN) 通过稳定输入和非标准化输出而显示出希望，但它依赖于历史和未来分布保持相同的强烈假设。 我们观察到，在许多实际应用中，分布变化遵循与周期性位置相关的周期性模式（例如季节性和假日波动）。 为此，我们建议Real-world time series forecasting faces the fundamental challenge of non-stationary statistical properties, including shifts in mean and variance over time. While reversible instance normalization (RevIN) has shown promise by stationarizing inputs and denormalizing outputs, it relies on the strong assumption that historical and future distributions remain identical. We observe that in many practical applications, distribution shifts follow cyclical patterns that correlate with periodic positions (e.g., seasonal and holiday volatility). Extensive experiments on twelve real-world benchmarks demonstrate that PAMod achieves state-of-the-art performance with fewer computational resources. 潜在的限制Potential limitations。 虽然 PAMod 在具有周期性分布变化的预测任务中表现出强大的性能，但有几个局限性值得讨论：. While PAMod demonstrates strong performance in forecasting tasks with cyclical distribution shifts, several limitations merit discussion:

  • •

    固定周期假设Fixed cycle assumption。 PAMod 依赖于预定义的周期长度. PAMod relies on a predefined cycle lengthτ\tau，它可能不能很好地适应具有变化或未知周期性的序列（例如，不规则事件驱动模式或多频率周期，没有主导周期）。, which may not adapt well to series with varying or unknown periodicities (e.g., irregular event-driven patterns or multi-frequency cycles without a dominant period).

  • •

    通道共循环Channel-common cycle。当前的设计对所有通道应用相同的周期长度，当不同变量表现出不同的周期性行为（例如，每日周期的温度和每周周期的电力负载）时，这可能不是最佳的。通道自适应循环机制可以提供更加量身定制的解决方案。. The current design applies the same cycle length to all channels, which could be suboptimal when different variables exhibit distinct periodic behaviors (e.g., temperature with daily cycles and electricity load with weekly cycles). A channel-adaptive cycle mechanism could offer a more tailored solution.

  • •

    对周期失调的敏感性Sensitivity to cycle misalignment。如果假设的周期长度显着偏离真实的数据周期性，则相位和幅度嵌入可能无法捕获实际的分布结构，导致偏移建模的鲁棒性降低。. If the assumed cycle length significantly deviates from the true data periodicity, the phase and amplitude embeddings may fail to capture the actual distribution structure, leading to reduced robustness in shift modeling.

  • •

    仅限于周期性变化Limited to cyclical shifts。 PAMod 旨在对周期性结构的分布变化进行建模，但可能无法有效地处理突然的非周期性状态变化或超出学习周期模式的异常。. PAMod is designed to model cyclically structured distribution shifts, but may not effectively handle abrupt, non-cyclical regime changes or anomalies that fall outside learned periodic patterns.

未来的方向Future Directions。 扩展 PAMod 的功能和适用性还有几个方向：. Several directions remain open for extending PAMod’s capability and applicability:

  • •

    适应性循环学习Adaptive cycle learning。而不是使用固定的. Instead of using a fixedτ\tau，未来的版本可以从数据中学习或动态调整周期长度，例如通过周期性估计模块或多尺度周期库。, future versions could learn or dynamically adjust the cycle length from data, e.g., via periodicity estimation modules or multi-scale cycle banks.

  • •

    通道循环建模Channel-wise cycle modeling。结合特定于变量的循环嵌入将使 PAMod 能够更好地处理多元序列，其中不同通道遵循不同的周期机制。. Incorporating variable-specific cycle embeddings would allow PAMod to better handle multivariate series where different channels follow distinct periodic regimes.

  • •

    与结构依赖性的集成Integration with structural dependencies。尽管 PAMod 专注于时间循环性，但将其与变量间关系建模（例如基于图或基于注意力的通道交互）相结合可以进一步提高时空或高度相关的多变量设置中的性能。. Although PAMod focuses on temporal cyclicity, combining it with inter-variable relationship modeling (e.g., graph-based or attention-based channel interaction) could further improve performance in spatio-temporal or highly correlated multivariate settings.

  • •

    推广到其他非平稳任务Generalization to other non-stationary tasks。探索 PAMod 对具有结构化分布变化的其他领域的适应能力，例如金融波动、天气冲击的能源负荷预测或季节性影响的医疗保健监测，将测试其更广泛的实用性。. Exploring PAMod’s adaptation to other domains with structured distribution shifts—such as financial volatility, energy load forecasting with weather shocks, or healthcare monitoring with seasonal effects—would test its broader utility.

总之，虽然 PAMod 为循环分布建模提供了有效的框架，但自适应循环推理、通道感知设计以及与关系建模集成的进一步进步可以增强其灵活性和范围。In summary, while PAMod provides an effective framework for cyclical distribution modeling, further advances in adaptive cycle inference, channel-aware design, and integration with relational modeling could enhance its flexibility and scope.
