当言语安全但行动致命时：在隐藏状态风险空间中探索超越文本安全的物理危险When Words Are Safe But Actions Kill: Probing Physical Danger Beyond Text Safety in Hidden-State Risk Space

Weimeng Wang1 Ziqiang Wang1 Zihang Zhan1 Chuanpu Fu2 Qi Li1 Ke Xu111footnotemark: 1
1Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China
2Nanyang Technological University, Singapore Corresponding authors
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）越来越多地充当实体代理的高级规划器，其中语言上良性的指令一旦扎根于物理世界就可能变得不安全。我们研究这种物理上的危险是否与普通文本级内容危险相同的安全问题。通过隐藏状态方向分析和随机分割空测试，我们表明内容危险（CD）和物理危险（PD）在 Qwen2.5-3B/7B/14B/32B、Phi-3.5 和 SmolLM2 的 LLM 表示中形成可分离的信号。 基于 CD/PD 可分离性，我们提出了 PRISM，一种针对完全隐藏状态的单层 L2 正则化逻辑探针。 PRISM 在 SafeAgentBench 上的准确率达到 86.2-87.7%，FPR 为 11.7-13.7%，而同规模的 LLM 判断超块安全任务的 FPR 为 24.7-39.0%。 我们进一步引入了PhysicalSafetyBench-1K（PSB-1K），这是一个没有直接伤害关键字的1000个物理风险对的对比基准，以测试方法是否检测到物理接地的危险而不是明确的不安全措辞。 在 PSB-1K 上，PRISM 的准确率达到 99.6%，FPR 为 0.7%，而 Qwen2.5-3B 的判断则拒绝了 67.8% 的安全任务。 PRISM 还在 SafeText 和 EARBench 上进行复制，支持隐藏状态探测作为超越文本审核的物理安全的表示级方法。Large language models (LLMs) increasingly serve as high-level planners for embodied agents, where linguistically benign instructions can become unsafe once grounded in the physical world. We study whether this physically grounded danger is the same safety problem as ordinary text-level content danger. Through hidden-state direction analysis and random-split null tests, we show that content danger (CD) and physical danger (PD) form separable signals in LLM representations across Qwen2.5-3B/7B/14B/32B, Phi-3.5 and SmolLM2. Building on the CD/PD separability, we propose PRISM, a single-layer L2-regularized logistic probe over full hidden states. PRISM achieves 86.2–87.7% accuracy on SafeAgentBench with 11.7–13.7% FPR, while same-scale LLM judges over-block safe tasks at 24.7–39.0% FPR. We further introduce PhysicalSafetyBench-1K (PSB-1K), a contrastive benchmark of 1,000 physical-risk pairs without direct harm keywords, to test whether methods detect physically grounded danger rather than explicit unsafe wording. On PSB-1K, PRISM reaches 99.6% accuracy and 0.7% FPR, whereas a Qwen2.5-3B judge rejects 67.8% of safe tasks. PRISM also replicates on SafeText and EARBench, supporting hidden-state probing as a representation-level method for physical safety beyond text moderation.

2 相关工作Related Work

体现代理安全基准。Embodied-agent safety benchmarks.

现有的基准可以分为三类。 SafeAgentBench 在 AI2-THOR 中提供家务任务Existing benchmarks can be grouped into three categories. SafeAgentBench provides household tasks in AI2-THOR(Kolve et al., 2017);我们使用其 600 个可执行标记任务，包括 113 个内容危险和 187 个物理危险的不安全案例，同时注意到其基于关键字的拆分中的泄漏; we use its 600 executable labeled tasks, including 113 content-dangerous and 187 physically dangerous unsafe cases, while noting leakage in its keyword-based split(Yin et al., 2024)。 EARBench 包含跨 7 个领域和 28 个场景的 2,634 个样本，强调多代理设置中的上下文风险. EARBench contains 2,634 samples across seven domains and 28 scenarios, emphasizing contextual risks in multi-agent settings(Zhu et al., 2024)。 SafeText 通过 1,465 个安全对来瞄准基于文本的物理常识. SafeText targets text-based physical commonsense through 1,465 safety pairs(Levy et al., 2022)。更广泛的工作范围从不同角度评估具体或任务规划安全：AgentSafe 和 IS-Bench 探测危险指令和交互式家庭安全. A broader line of work evaluates embodied or task-planning safety from different angles: AgentSafe and IS-Bench probe hazardous instructions and interactive household safety(Liu et al., 2025; Lu et al., 2025); EAsafetyBench 和 SafeMind 添加了输入审核和多阶段风险分类法; EAsafetyBench and SafeMind add input moderation and multi-stage risk taxonomies(Wang et al., 2025; Chen and others, 2025);进一步的工作目标是操作级安全、形式逻辑规划、语义安全构成、安全强化学习和智能体交互风险; and further work targets manipulation-level safety, formal-logic planning, semantic-safety constitutions, safe reinforcement learning, and agent-interaction risk(Ni et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2025; Obi et al., 2025; Sermanet et al., 2025; Ji et al., 2023; Yuan et al., 2024; Ruan et al., 2024)。在之前的工作基础上，我们利用这些基准来验证物理隐藏状态空间。我们进一步介绍 PSB-1K，这是一个基准，旨在将物理上的危险与明确的内容危险语言分开。. Building on prior work, we leverage these benchmarks to validate the physical hidden-state space. We further introduce PSB-1K, a benchmark designed to disentangle physically grounded danger from explicit content-danger language.

探测和表示工程。Probing and representation engineering.

线性探针广泛用于检查中间表示是否编码概念信息Linear probes are widely used to examine whether intermediate representations encode conceptual information(Alain and Bengio, 2016; Belinkov, 2022)。大多数探索性研究使用监督分类器来测试预定义概念的可解码性；互补的无监督方法，例如 CCS，可以从 LLM 隐藏状态中恢复抽象概念，包括真实性. Most probing studies use supervised classifiers to test the decodability of a predefined concept; complementary unsupervised methods, such as CCS, recover abstract concepts including truthfulness from LLM hidden states(Burns et al., 2023)。第二条工作线不仅使用潜在方向进行测量，而且还进行干预。表示工程和推理时间干预识别与安全、诚实、拒绝或真实相关的方向，并用它们来引导行为. A second line of work uses latent directions not only for measurement but also for intervention. Representation Engineering and Inference-Time Intervention identify directions associated with safety, honesty, refusal, or truthfulness and use them to steer behavior(Zou et al., 2023a; Li et al., 2023; Turner et al., 2023);拒绝尤其是由单一方向调解的; refusal in particular is mediated by a single direction(Arditi et al., 2024)。这些发现与线性表示假设和LLM特征的新兴线性几何一致. These findings are consistent with the linear representation hypothesis and the emergent linear geometry of LLM features(Park et al., 2024; Marks and Tegmark, 2023; Todd et al., 2024; Cunningham et al., 2024).

我们使用的探针是诊断性的而不是导向性的。我们询问隐藏状态是否提供可测量的证据，表明在执行生成的计划之前，所体现的物理危险与文本级内容危险不同。因此，关键的区别在于所衡量的安全对象：先前的指示通常针对一个抽象概念，例如拒绝或真实性，而具体的物理危险不仅仅是文本政策危险的更强或更弱形式。因此，我们将 CDD 和 PDD 建模为两个与部署相关的信号，而不是将它们分解为单个概念轴，并且我们保留直接暴露相关故障模式的经验基线的详细比较。Our use of probes is diagnostic rather than steering-oriented. We ask whether hidden states provide measurable evidence that embodied physical danger differs from text-level content danger before the generated plan is executed. The key distinction is therefore the safety object being measured: prior directions usually target one abstract concept such as refusal or truthfulness, whereas embodied physical danger is not simply a stronger or weaker form of text-policy danger. We therefore model CDD and PDD as two deployment-relevant signals rather than collapsing them into a single concept axis, and we reserve the detailed comparison for empirical baselines that expose the relevant failure modes directly.

超越文本级别审核的安全监控。Safety monitoring beyond text-level moderation.

现有的 LLM 安全机制提供了多种过滤不安全指令的方法，但大多数都是针对文本级策略违规而不是物理执行风险而开发的。 Llama Guard 等专用防护模型可提供高效的安全/不安全分类Existing LLM safety mechanisms offer several ways to filter unsafe instructions, but most were developed for text-level policy violations rather than physical execution risks. Dedicated guard models such as Llama Guard provide efficient safe/unsafe classification(Inan et al., 2023; Llama Team, AI @ Meta, 2024)，以及相关护栏旁边, alongside related guardrails(Zeng et al., 2024; Han et al., 2024; Ghosh et al., 2024); LLM-as-judge 方法使用通用 LLM 来进行更多上下文安全评估; LLM-as-judge methods use general-purpose LLMs to produce more contextual safety evaluations(Zheng et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023; Kim et al., 2024);和潜在原型方法（例如 LPM）构建多层隐藏状态原型以在表示空间中进行调节; and latent-prototype approaches such as LPM construct multi-layer hidden-state prototypes for moderation in representation space(Chrabąszcz et al., 2025)。这些方法提供了自然的比较点，但它们在我们研究的 PD 设置中表现不佳：文本防护错过了缺乏违反政策措辞的物理危险计划，法官过度阻止了正常的家务任务，原型方法无法从内容危险转变为物理危险。. These methods provide natural points of comparison, but they perform poorly on the PD setting we study: text guards miss physically dangerous plans that lack policy-violation wording, judges over-block normal household tasks, and prototype methods fail to transfer from content danger to physical danger.

3 关键观察：隐藏状态空间中的危险是可分解的Key Observation: Danger Is Decomposable in Hidden-State Space

我们的主要见解是，体现代理的物理安全不仅仅是普通法学硕士文本安全的一个实例。我们使用隐藏状态分析作为这一主张的证据：如果 CD 和 PD 是相同的安全概念，则针对一种危险类型训练的探针应该干净地转移到另一种危险类型，并且它们的隐藏状态方向在统计上应该与随机不安全分割无法区分。相反，我们发现两者之间存在稳定的差距。内容危险和物理危险在冻结LLM的中后期隐藏状态中形成分离的方向，这种分离解释了为什么面向内容的检测器会错过许多物理危险指令。Our key insight is that embodied-agent physical safety is not merely an instance of ordinary LLM text safety. We use hidden-state analysis as evidence for this claim: if CD and PD were the same safety concept, probes trained on one danger type should transfer cleanly to the other and their hidden-state directions should be statistically indistinguishable from random unsafe splits. Instead, we find a stable gap between the two. Content danger and physical danger form separable directions in the middle-to-late hidden states of frozen LLMs, and this separation explains why content-oriented detectors miss many physically dangerous instructions.

本节建立了 PRISM 的表示级别基础。我们首先将 CDD 和 PDD 定义为两个方向估计，用于测试文本安全和物理安全风险是否占据相同的隐藏状态空间。然后，我们通过将其与随机分割零分布进行比较来验证观察到的分离不是高维拟合的意外。目标不是要求严格的正交性，而是表明具体的物理安全是一个独特的建模目标，需要显式的表示级处理。This section establishes the representation-level basis for PRISM. We then verify that their observed separation is not an accident of high-dimensional fitting by comparing it against a random-split null distribution. 5 评价Evaluation

实验围绕五个问题进行组织。首先，我们在 SafeAgentBench 上测试 PRISM 是否可以联合检测 CD 和 PD，同时保持较低的误报率。其次，缩放和跨数据集实验测试操作点是否在模型大小和基准上保持稳定。第三，PSB-1K 在没有明确伤害关键字的情况下隔离了物理接地危险，直接测试方法是否超出 CD 提示。第四，跨架构实验检查 CD/PD 分离是否在 Qwen 之外成立。最后，重要性、延迟和消融确定了 PRISM 的优势是否来自隐藏状态信号、所选层和两种危险的训练边界，而不是来自更复杂的分类器。The experiments are organized around five questions. SVM-RBF reaches 82.8%, 83.5%, 85.3%, and 86.0% accuracy across 3B–32B, and MLP reaches 83.0%, 82.5%, 82.7%, and 84.3%, yet PRISM’s logistic-regression probe remains the strongest or tied-strongest selected-layer classifier while being simpler, faster, and easier to calibrate. The direction ablations give the sharper evidence: CDD-only and LPM fail on physical-OOD detection, while PDD-only remains incomplete on content-OOD detection, and PRISM improves because it learns from the full binary label set (Figure2和附录图and Appendix Figure9在 CDD/PDD 平面中可视化这些错误和 PRISM 的阻塞决策）。visualize these errors and PRISM’s blocking decisions in the CDD/PDD plane).

数据集级别的消融来自 PSB-1K。通过删除明确的伤害词并使每个不安全的项目成为物理危险案例，PSB-1K 抑制了最简单的内容危险线索，并迫使方法在三种行为之间进行选择：错过物理风险、过度阻止安全任务或学习物理接地信号。 Llama Guard 属于第一个政权，Qwen Judge 属于第二个政权，PRISM 属于第三个政权。这支持了本文的中心设计选择：体现主体的安全性应该直接针对身体危险进行评估和培训，而不是被视为内容审核的副作用。The dataset-level ablation comes from PSB-1K. By removing explicit harm words and making every unsafe item a physical-danger case, PSB-1K suppresses the easiest content-danger cues and forces methods to choose between three behaviours: miss the physical risk, over-block safe tasks, or learn a physically grounded signal. Llama Guard falls into the first regime, the Qwen judge into the second, and PRISM into the third. This supports the central design choice of the paper: embodied-agent safety should be evaluated and trained on physical danger directly, not treated as a side effect of content moderation.

总而言之，这些消融支持了一个重点结论：PRISM 之所以成功，是因为体现的安全信息存在于中间隐藏状态，并且可以通过简单的线性探针提取，但前提是评估明确包括内容危险和物理危险故障。单轴基线错过了问题的一侧，零样本判断过度阻止了安全任务，PSB-1K 表明，即使措辞是良性的，物理上的危险仍然是一个明显的评估目标。Taken together, the ablations support a focused conclusion: PRISM succeeds because embodied-safety information is present in intermediate hidden states and is extractable by a simple linear probe, but only when the evaluation explicitly includes both content-danger and physical-danger failures. Single-axis baselines miss one side of the problem, zero-shot judges over-block safe tasks, and PSB-1K shows that physically grounded danger remains a distinct evaluation target even when the wording is benign.

6 结论Conclusion

体现代理人的安全性与之前的法学硕士文本安全性不同。我们通过实验隐藏状态分析验证了这一点：内容危险和物理危险方向在 Qwen2.5-3B 上是可分离的，超出了随机分割零值，并且在较大的 Qwen2.5 模型和两个非 Qwen 架构中显示出一致的角度模式。我们还引入了 PSB-1K，这是一种对比性的物理安全基准，它消除了直接的内容危险线索，并暴露了 LLM 法官在物理接地家务任务中的高 FPR 行为。 PRISM 将这一观察结果转化为表征级方法：单层线性探针可检测两种危险类型，在 Qwen2.5-3B 至 32B 上以 11.7-13.7% FPR 实现 86.2-87.7% 的准确度，在 SafeText 和 EARBench 上复制，并在 PSB-1K 上以 0.7% FPR 达到 99.6% 的准确度。该主张是有意限制的——PRISM 不是一个完整的体现代理安全系统，方向是可分离的而不是严格正交的——但它的贡献是明确的：物理危险应该被建模为一个独特的隐藏状态安全结构，而不是作为文本安全判断的补充。Embodied-agent safety is not identical to prior LLM text safety. We verify this through experimental hidden-state analysis: content-danger and physical-danger directions are separable beyond a random-split null on Qwen2.5-3B and show consistent angular patterns across larger Qwen2.5 models and two non-Qwen architectures. We also introduce PSB-1K, a contrastive physical-safety benchmark that removes direct content-danger cues and exposes the high-FPR behaviour of LLM judges on physically grounded household tasks. PRISM turns this observation into a representation-level method: a single-layer linear probe that detects both danger types, achieves 86.2–87.7% accuracy at 11.7–13.7% FPR across Qwen2.5-3B to 32B, replicates on SafeText and EARBench, and reaches 99.6% accuracy with 0.7% FPR on PSB-1K. The claim is deliberately bounded—PRISM is not a complete embodied-agent safety system, and the directions are separable rather than strictly orthogonal—but its contribution is clear: physical danger should be modeled as a distinct hidden-state safety structure rather than as a supplement to text-safety judgment.
