TFGformer：通过时频图学习和协变量融合进行多元时间序列预测TFGformer: Multivariate Time Series Forecasting via Time-Frequency Graph Learning and Covariate Fusion
††谢谢：thanks:该工作得到了国家自然科学基金委的资助，编号为 62272052This work is supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China under Grants 62272052

Yu Sun    Yuan Chang    Xiaohou SHI    Yan Sun
摘要Abstract

多变量时间序列预测需要对复杂的时间动态和变量间依赖关系进行建模。然而，在实践中，噪声相关性经常会引入干扰，而诸如日历特征和外部事件之类的关键上下文协变量却经常未被充分利用。这凸显了对模型的需求，该模型能够选择性地识别有意义的关系，同时有效地合并辅助信息。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一个将时频图结构学习与协变量感知表示融合相结合的统一框架。时频图 (TFG) 模块利用短时傅立叶变换 (STFT) 来捕获互补的时谱模式。然后，它通过加权马哈拉诺比斯距离学习自适应变量间相关性，并采用 Gumbel-Softmax 采样来导出稀疏的动态图，从而抑制不相关的依赖性。与此同时，基于 MLP 的模块将历史和未来的协变量融合到主序列表示中，使模型能够利用趋势先验和上下文信号。关于电力、交通和气象基准的大量实验表明，我们的方法始终优于最先进的基于 Transformer 的模型，证实了其在建模选择性变量相互作用和利用协变量提高预测准确性方面的有效性。Multivariate time series forecasting requires modeling complex temporal dynamics and inter-variable dependencies. However, in practice, noisy correlations often introduce interference, while crucial contextual covariates such as calendar features and external events are frequently underutilized. This highlights the need for models that selectively identify meaningful relationships while effectively incorporating auxiliary information. To address these challenges, we propose a unified framework integrating time–frequency graph structure learning with covariate-aware representation fusion. The Time-Frequency Graph (TFG) module leverages the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) to capture complementary temporal–spectral patterns. It then learns adaptive inter-variable correlations via a weighted Mahalanobis distance and employs Gumbel–Softmax sampling to derive sparse, dynamic graphs that suppress irrelevant dependencies. In parallel, MLP-based modules fuse historical and future covariates into the main sequence representation, enabling the model to exploit trend priors and contextual signals.Extensive experiments on electricity, traffic, and meteorology benchmarks demonstrate that our approach consistently outperforms state-of-the-art Transformer-based models, confirming its effectiveness in modeling selective variable interactions and leveraging covariates for improved forecasting accuracy.

I 简介Introduction

基于 Transformer 的模型在 NLP [1] 和视觉 [2] 方面取得了显着的成功，推动了多元时间序列预测 (MTSF) 基础模型的发展。 MTSF 从历史数据中揭示动态模式，在各种现实应用中发挥着至关重要的作用。随着数据集变得复杂，变量之间的依赖关系变得更加复杂。由于长期预测自然会受到误差累积的影响 [3]，因此在利用外部协变量的同时有效捕获这些时变关系仍然是一个关键挑战。Transformer-based models have achieved remarkable success in NLP [1] and vision [2], spurring the development of foundation models for multivariate time series forecasting (MTSF). MTSF uncovers dynamic patterns from historical data, playing a crucial role in various real-world applications. As datasets grow complex, inter-variable dependencies become more intricate. Since long-term forecasting naturally suffers from error accumulation [3], effectively capturing these time-varying relationships while leveraging external covariates remains a key challenge.

现有方法尝试以各种方式解决变量间的依赖性。例如，补丁TSTExisting approaches attempt to address inter-variable dependencies in various ways. For example, PatchTST[4]将序列分割成补丁，并采用与通道无关的处理来减轻弱相关变量的干扰；然而，它缺乏明确的跨变量建模和可解释性。智能变压器segments sequences into patches and employs channel-independent processing to mitigate interference from weakly correlated variables; however, it lacks explicit cross-variable modeling and interpretability. iTransformer[5]引入通道混合自注意力来捕获变量级别的依赖关系，但它忽略了各个变量内的细粒度时间动态。定时器XLintroduces channel-mixing self-attention to capture variable-level dependencies, yet it overlooks fine-grained temporal dynamics within individual variables. TimerXL[6]通过联合建模变量内和变量间模式对此进行了改进，但它仍然假设所有变量对之间的静态关联，未能考虑这些依赖性的强度和时间演变。在关系稀疏或随时间变化的情况下，这种限制变得尤其成问题。improves upon this by jointly modeling intra- and inter-variable patterns, but it still assumes static associations across all variable pairs, failing to account for the strength and temporal evolution of these dependencies. This limitation becomes particularly problematic in scenarios where relationships are sparse or change over time.

同时，日历属性、设备元数据和事件驱动信号等协变量携带有价值的先验知识，可以显着提高预测准确性。然而，大多数现有模型通过简单的串联或将它们视为额外的通道来合并它们，这通常会引入噪声并忽略它们的固有结构。虽然像TiDE这样的模型Meanwhile, covariates such as calendar attributes, device metadata, and event-driven signals carry valuable prior knowledge that can significantly enhance forecasting accuracy. Yet, most existing models incorporate them through simple concatenation or by treating them as extra channels, which often introduces noise and overlooks their inherent structure. Although models like TiDE[7]和 ChronosXand ChronosX[8]强调专用协变量融合的好处，TiDE 主要是基于 MLP 的架构，而 ChronosX 是为单变量、基于令牌的预测量身定制的。因此，两者都不适合在多变量、基于补丁的设置中显式地建模动态变量间依赖关系。highlight the benefits of dedicated covariate fusion, TiDE is primarily an MLP-based architecture, while ChronosX is tailored for univariate, token-based forecasting. Consequently, neither is ideally suited for explicitly modeling dynamic inter-variable dependencies in a multivariate, patch-based setting.

为了解决这些局限性，本文提出了一种新颖的多元时间序列预测框架，它将时频图学习与有效的协变量融合相结合。我们的主要贡献有三个：To address these limitations, this paper proposes a novel multivariate time series forecasting framework that integrates time–frequency graph learning with effective covariate fusion. Our main contributions are threefold:

  • •

    我们设计了一种新颖的时频图结构学习模块，利用短时傅立叶变换（STFT）和马哈拉诺比斯距离来捕获变量之间的动态和非线性依赖性。通过估计联合时频域中的成对关系概率，该模块自适应地构建指导注意力机制的注意力掩模，从而能够更准确地建模复杂的变量间交互。We design a novel time-frequency graph structure learning module that leverages Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) and Mahalanobis distance to capture dynamic and nonlinear dependencies among variables. By estimating pairwise relational probabilities in the joint time–frequency domain, the module adaptively constructs an attention mask that guides the attention mechanism, enabling more accurate modeling of complex inter-variable interactions.

  • •

    我们引入了基于 MLP 的协变量融合模块，该模块通过残差连接将历史和未来的协变量与主序列表示集成，而不会丢失原始数据特征。这种设计允许模型充分利用先前的趋势知识和外部动态背景。We introduce an MLP-based covariate fusion module that integrates both historical and future covariates with the primary sequence representation through residual connections, without losing the original data characteristics. This design allows the model to fully exploit prior trend knowledge and external dynamic context.

  • •

    我们对涵盖电力、交通和天气领域的七个广泛使用的基准数据集进行了全面的实验。结果表明，我们的方法在多个预测范围和评估指标上始终如一地实现了最先进的性能，验证了其在动态变量间关系建模和利用协变量信息方面的有效性。We conduct comprehensive experiments on seven widely used benchmark datasets spanning the electricity, traffic, and weather domains. The results demonstrate that our approach consistently achieves state-of-the-art performance across multiple forecasting horizons and evaluation metrics, validating its effectiveness in modeling dynamic inter-variable relationships and leveraging covariate information.

II 相关作品Related Works

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于变压器的时间序列预测Transformer-Based Time Series Forecasting

变压器在 MTSF 中显示出强大的潜力。 Informer 和 Autoformer [8] 等早期作品提高了对长期依赖关系的关注。然而，有效地建模复杂的变量间关系仍然是一个重大挑战。Transformers show strong potential in MTSF. Early works like Informer and Autoformer [8] improved attention for long-term dependencies. However, effectively modeling complex inter-variable relationships remains a significant challenge.

现有的基于 Transformer 的多元时间序列模型通常分为两类。 渠道无关Existing Transformer-based models for multivariate time series generally fall into two categories. Channel-independent[10]PatchTST 等方法分别对每个变量进行建模，重点关注变量内的时间模式。尽管有效，但它们并没有明确捕获变量之间的相互作用。 通道混合approaches, such as PatchTST, model each variable separately, focusing on intra-variable temporal patterns. Although effective, they do not explicitly capture interactions between variables. Channel-mixing[11]像 iTransformer 这样的方法将每个变量视为一个标记，并在变量维度上应用注意力，从而对变量间的关系进行建模，但可能会忽略每个变量内的细粒度时间动态。approaches like iTransformer treat each variable as a token and apply attention across the variable dimension, thereby modeling inter-variable relationships but potentially overlooking fine-grained temporal dynamics within each variable.

为了利用两个方向的优势，最近的混合设计（例如 TimerXL）将修补策略与通道混合机制相结合，从而能够同时对变量内和变量间模式进行建模。然而，这些模型隐含地假设所有变量对都具有相同的相关性。他们无法区分变量之间关系的不同强度或时间演变，当依赖关系稀疏或高度动态时，这可能会引入噪音。To leverage the strengths of both directions, recent hybrid designs such as TimerXL combine patching strategies with channel-mixing mechanisms, enabling simultaneous modeling of intra- and inter-variable patterns. Nevertheless, these models implicitly assume that all variable pairs share equal levels of correlation. They are unable to distinguish the varying strengths or temporal evolution of relationships among variables, which may introduce noise when dependencies are sparse or highly dynamic.

Ⅱ-BII-B 时间序列建模中的频域分析Frequency-Domain Analysis in Time Series Modeling

最近的研究表明，频域特征在描述多元同步性和周期性一致性方面具有天然的优势。受此推动，多种方法已从纯时域建模转向频域表示Recent research has revealed that frequency-domain features offer natural advantages in depicting multivariate synchrony and periodic consistency. Motivated by this, several methods have shifted from pure time-domain modeling to frequency-domain representations[12]，利用幅度和相位信息, leveraging amplitude and phase information[13]来表征变量之间的关系。然而，大多数现有方法依赖于全局傅里叶变换，它将整个序列映射为整体频率分量，从而忽略了时间局部性。因此，他们很难捕捉现实世界多元时间序列中常见的非平稳和时变相关结构。to characterize inter-variable relationships. Nevertheless, most existing approaches rely on global Fourier transforms, which map the entire sequence into holistic frequency components, thereby overlooking temporal locality. As a result, they struggle to capture the non-stationary and time-varying correlation structures that are common in real-world multivariate time series.

Ⅱ-CII-C 时间序列预测中的协变量利用Covariate Utilization in Time Series Forecasting

在实际的预测任务中，天气状况、日历特征和假期指标等辅助协变量提供了有价值的先验信息。尽管如此，主流预测模型通常采用相对基本的融合策略。一种常见的方法以简单的方式对协变量进行编码，然后将它们与主序列连接起来。另一种方法直接将协变量视为附加变量，并将其与主要输入一起处理。然而，由于异质协变量表现出不同的时间模式和统计特性，这种简单的串联或联合建模通常会引入噪声，并阻止模型有效地提取有用的协变量信息。作为改进这一点的尝试，像 ChronosX 这样的模型引入了融合模块。然而值得注意的是，它的设计是针对单变量场景的，并且在基于代币的框架上运行，而不是基于补丁。In practical forecasting tasks, auxiliary covariates such as weather conditions, calendar features, and holiday indicators provide valuable prior information. Despite this, mainstream forecasting models typically adopt relatively basic fusion strategies. One common approach encodes covariates in a simple manner and then concatenates them with the main series. Another approach directly treats covariates as additional variables and processes them together with the primary inputs. However, because heterogeneous covariates exhibit distinct temporal patterns and statistical properties, such naive concatenation or joint modeling often introduces noise and prevents the model from effectively extracting useful covariate information.As an attempt to improve this, models like ChronosX have introduced a fusion module. However, it is noteworthy that its design is targeted at univariate scenarios and operates on a token-based framework, rather than patch-based.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 结构概述Structure Overview

我们的模型架构如图 1 所示。Our model architecture is illustrated in Fig.1所提出的模型由三个主要组件组成：时频图（TFG）模块、协变量特征融合（CFM）模块和基于 Transformer 的序列建模模块。The proposed model consists of three main components: the Time–Frequency Graph (TFG) module, the Covariate Feature Fusion (CFM) module, and a Transformer-based sequence modeling module.

给定多变量时间序列以及历史和未来协变量，TFG 模块首先分析时间和频谱模式以学习动态变量间关系，生成指导跨变量交互的注意掩模。 CFM模块分为两个子模块：输入协变量融合（I-CFM）在进入Transformer之前将历史协变量与原始时间序列集成，而输出协变量融合（O-CFM）在Transformer处理后合并未来协变量以进一步细化隐藏表示。Given a multivariate time series along with historical and future covariates, the TFG module first analyzes temporal and spectral patterns to learn dynamic inter-variable relationships, producing an attention mask that guides cross-variable interactions. The CFM module is divided into two sub-modules: the Input Covariate Fusion (I-CFM) integrates historical covariates with the original time series before entering the Transformer, while the Output Covariate Fusion (O-CFM) incorporates future covariates after Transformer processing to further refine the hidden representations.

丰富的序列包含来自 TFG 的关系信息和来自 I-CFM 的协变量感知特征，被馈送到 Transformer 主干中。 TFG 模块生成的注意力掩码在注意力计算过程中应用，以显式地建模相关的变量间依赖关系。经过 Transformer 处理后，未来的协变量通过 O-CFM 融合，最终的预测头生成目标范围的多变量预测。The enriched sequence, containing relational information from TFG and covariate-aware features from I-CFM, is fed into the Transformer backbone. The attention mask generated by the TFG module is applied during attention computation to explicitly model relevant inter-variable dependencies. After Transformer processing, future covariates are fused via O-CFM, and the final forecasting head produces the multivariate predictions for the target horizon.

V 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 TFGformer，一种用于多元时间序列预测的新颖框架。它具有时频图模块，该模块使用 STFT 和可学习的马哈拉诺比斯距离来构建稀疏的动态依赖图，以引导注意力和过滤噪声，以及基于 MLP 的协变量融合模块来集成历史和未来上下文。七个基准测试的实验表明 TFGformer 在六个数据集上实现了卓越的性能。未来的工作将把这种架构扩展到异常检测、插补和大规模基础模型。We propose TFGformer, a novel framework for multivariate time series forecasting. It features a Time-Frequency Graph module that uses STFT and a learnable Mahalanobis distance to build sparse, dynamic dependency graphs for guiding attention and filtering noise, alongside an MLP-based Covariate Fusion Module to integrate historical and future contexts. Experiments on seven benchmarks demonstrate TFGformer achieves superior performance on six datasets. Future work will extend this architecture to anomaly detection, imputation, and large-scale foundation models.
