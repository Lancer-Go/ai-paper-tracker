Cross-architecture, cross-scale, and cross-dataset checks show that RIM does not always emerge. Previous work attributed RIM to neuron-level entanglement, but did not identify the geometry of the representation space underlying this entanglement or propose a training-time fix. We provide both: a representation-space analysis of RIM and the Safety-Direction Penalty (SDP), which penalizes movement along a learned safety direction during reasoning fine-tuning. The analysis extracts two activation-space directions, one encoding reasoning ability and the other safety behavior. These directions are coupled: fine-tuning that improves reasoning shifts safety representations, and prompts with larger shifts show larger safety degradation. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）现已广泛部署，但它们带来了安全风险。 对齐训练教会模型拒绝有害的请求，但这种行为对于进一步的微调来说并不稳健。Large language models (LLMs) are now widely deployed, but they pose safety risks. Alignment training teaches models to refuse harmful requests, yet this behavior is not robust to further fine-tuning.Betley et al. (2026)发现了紧急错位（EM）：对狭窄的错位数据进行微调，例如模型在未披露的情况下生成的不安全代码，可能会导致不相关领域的广泛错位。 推理引起的错位 (RIM)，由discovered Emergent Misalignment (EM): fine-tuning on narrow misaligned data, such as insecure code that the model produces without disclosure, can induce broad misalignment across unrelated domains. Reasoning-Induced Misalignment (RIM), identified byYan et al. (2026)，提出了一个更令人惊讶的案例：对纯粹良性推理数据（包括数学、代码和思维链痕迹）进行微调会降低安全对齐。 EM 需要包含较窄的错位内容的训练数据，而 RIM 则源自对纯推理数据的监督微调，根本没有错位内容。 我们将 RIM 视为一种条件故障模式：它不一定会出现在每个模型-数据集组合中。, presents an even more surprising case: fine-tuning on purely benign reasoning data, including mathematics, code, and chain-of-thought traces, degrades safety alignment. While EM requires training data that contain narrow misaligned content, RIM arises from supervised fine-tuning on pure reasoning data, with no misaligned content at all. We treat RIM as a conditional failure mode: it does not necessarily emerge for every model–dataset combination.Yan et al. (2026)将 RIM 归因于推理和安全电路之间的神经元级纠缠，但将缓解措施作为一个悬而未决的问题。 使纠缠的神经元失活会增加错位，同时降低推理能力，这证实了推理和安全共享相同的神经基质。attributed RIM to neuron-level entanglement between reasoning and safety circuits but left mitigation as an open problem. Deactivating the entangled neurons increases misalignment while degrading reasoning, confirming that reasoning and safety share the same neural substrate.

为了缓解 RIM，我们需要以允许选择性干预的形式来理解耦合；单独的神经元水平分析无法提供这一点，因为相同的神经元具有两种功能。 然而，先前的工作表明，单个模型行为（例如拒绝）对应于激活空间中的线性方向To mitigate RIM, we need to understand the coupling in a form that allows selective intervention; neuron-level analysis alone cannot provide this because the same neurons serve both functions. Prior work, however, has shown that individual model behaviors, such as refusal, correspond to linear directions in activation space(Arditi et al., 2024)，这表明即使不同的行为共享神经元，也可以将其作为方向分开。, suggesting that distinct behaviors can be separated as directions even when they share neurons.我们可以通过表示空间的几何形状来解释 RIM，并且该解释可以指导训练时缓解吗？Can we explain RIM via the geometry of the representation space, and can that explanation guide a training-time mitigation?

我们假设这种纠缠可以通过表示空间中推理和安全的几何形状来表征：改进推理的微调也应该扰乱编码安全行为的表示。 在此框架的基础上，我们从基础模型（微调之前的模型）的隐藏状态中提取两个方向。 推理方向区分正确推理和错误推理；安全方向将拒绝与遵守分开。 在 RIM 出现的 3B 和 7B 中，两个方向在中深层显示出一致的负余弦相似性：改进推理使表示远离安全行为。 安全表示受到更多扰动的提示显示出更大的安全降级。We hypothesize that this entanglement can be characterized through the geometry of reasoning and safety in the representation space: fine-tuning that improves reasoning should also perturb the representations that encode safety behavior. Building on this framework, we extract two directions from the hidden states of the base model, the model before fine-tuning. The reasoning direction separates correct from incorrect reasoning; the safety direction separates refusal from compliance. In 3B and 7B, where RIM appears, the two directions show consistently negative cosine similarity across the mid-to-deep layers: improving reasoning pushes representations away from safe behavior. Prompts where safety representations are perturbed more show larger safety degradation.

耦合跨越许多层，但实际干预需要知道要针对哪些层。 我们使用居中内核对齐（CKA；The coupling spans many layers, but a practical intervention requires knowing which layers to target. We use Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA;Kornblith et al. 2019）距离比率，以比较微调后每层表示在有害输入和无害输入上的变化程度。 特定于安全的变更集中在一组紧凑的安全决策层中。 线性探针提供独立的确认：在这些层中，模型在微调后仍然可以识别有害的请求。 然而，它的拒绝率从 90% 以上下降到随机机会水平。 该模型仍然知道请求是有害的，但不再根据该知识采取行动。) distance ratios to compare how much each layer’s representations change on harmful versus harmless inputs after fine-tuning. Safety-specific change concentrates in a compact set of safety-decision layers. Linear probes provide independent confirmation: within these layers, the model still recognizes harmful requests after fine-tuning. Its refusal rate, however, drops from over 90% to the random-chance level. The model still knows a request is harmful but no longer acts on that knowledge.

基于这两组发现，我们提出了安全方向惩罚（SDP）。 耦合决定了惩罚形式：对训练期间沿安全方向的平方位移的惩罚。 层定位决定了初始惩罚范围。 当初始应用触发超出惩罚集的层中的补偿性位移时，相同的诊断指导迭代范围扩展。 在 Qwen2.5-3B 和 7B 上，SDP 恢复了安全性，同时保留了基准推理性能。 图Based on these two sets of findings, we propose the Safety-Direction Penalty (SDP). 4 缓解方法：安全方向惩罚Mitigation Method: Safety-Direction Penalty

6 结论Conclusion

我们研究推理引起的错位（RIM）何时出现、它在表示空间中如何表现以及如何减轻它。在评估的架构、模型规模和推理数据集中，RIM 并未出现在每个模型-数据集-训练环境中，支持条件而不是通用表征。在满足我们的操作 RIM 标准的设置中，推理-安全耦合和沿安全方向的位移始终与行为安全退化相关。 CKA 比率和线性探针定位了一组紧凑的安全决策层，模型在其中继续识别有害请求，但不再可靠地拒绝它们。这些诊断激发安全方向惩罚（SDP）并指导其层范围：恢复安全时保留初始范围，而残余位移则激发诊断边界内的扩展。在评估的 RIM 设置下，SDP 将安全性恢复到基本模型水平，同时保留大多数评估的推理性能，无需安全训练数据、参考策略或推理时间干预。We study when Reasoning-Induced Misalignment (RIM) emerges, how it manifests in representation space, and how it can be mitigated. Across the evaluated architectures, model scales, and reasoning datasets, RIM does not emerge in every model–dataset–training setting, supporting a conditional rather than universal characterization. In settings that satisfy our operational RIM criterion, reasoning–safety coupling and displacement along the safety direction are consistently associated with behavioral safety degradation. CKA ratios and linear probes locate a compact set of safety-decision layers where the model continues to recognize harmful requests but no longer reliably refuses them. These diagnostics motivate the Safety-Direction Penalty (SDP) and guide its layer scope: the initial scope is retained when it restores safety, whereas residual displacement motivates expansion within the diagnostic boundary. Under the evaluated RIM settings, SDP restores safety to base-model levels while retaining most evaluated reasoning performance, without safety training data, a reference policy, or inference-time intervention.

局限性Limitations

证据支持 RIM 作为评估条件下推理微调的条件失败模式。我们的积极 RIM 和 SDP 结果集中在 Qwen2.5-Instruct，而 MetaMathQA、Gemma、Ministral 和 Qwen2.5-14B 检查提供了跨数据集、模型系列和尺度的初步证据。更广泛的对照研究可以进一步描述语料库、架构和规模如何影响 RIM 的出现。 SDP 目前在最后一个令牌表示中估计每层一个固定模板拒绝合规方向。未来的工作可以检查更多样化的拒绝响应、多维安全子空间和中间推理表示。我们将几何分析解释为 SDP 干预的局部说明，而不是模型安全性的一般因果特征。与外部微调防御的匹配比较以及跨模型的目标效率的进一步研究也将有助于了解 SDP 的收益和成本。The evidence supports RIM as a conditional failure mode of reasoning fine-tuning under the evaluated conditions. Our positive RIM and SDP results focus on Qwen2.5-Instruct, while the MetaMathQA, Gemma, Ministral, and Qwen2.5-14B checks provide initial evidence across datasets, model families, and scales. Broader controlled studies could further characterize how corpus, architecture, and scale affect the emergence of RIM. SDP currently estimates one fixed-template refusal–compliance direction per layer at the last-token representation. Future work could examine more diverse refusal responses, multidimensional safety subspaces, and intermediate reasoning representations. We interpret the geometric analysis as a local account of SDP’s intervention rather than a general causal characterization of model safety. Matched comparisons with external fine-tuning defenses and further study of targeting efficiency across models would also help contextualize SDP’s benefits and costs.
