摘要。Abstract.

为神经架构搜索 (NAS) 开发有效的代理（性能预测器）通常需要昂贵的微调或复杂表示的工程。我们提出了一种低成本嵌入策略，利用语言模型（LM）的归纳偏差来消除这些开销。通过将架构表示为 PyTorch 类定义文本，我们证明了现成的 LM 可以充当有竞争力的特征提取器，而无需 NAS 专门的微调。最终的预测器是通过将提取的面向代码的 LM 嵌入 (COLE) 通过轻量级回归头来构建的。我们还研究了提高嵌入质量和利用率的策略。我们在 NAS-Bench-201 和 einspace 搜索空间上的实验表明，使用冻结 LM 时，原始代码输入比其他基于文本的编码（例如 ONNX 到文本编码）具有更高的预测性能。我们还观察到 COLE 在 NAS-Bench-201 中使用 BANANAS 算法驱动卓越的代理辅助搜索。在针对 CIFAR-100 性能进行优化时，使用 COLE 替换用于架构表示的结构路径编码可以将搜索空间中达到最适合架构的 1% 以内所需的评估预算减少 34%（通过测试准确性）。由于任何神经架构都可以表示为代码，因此这些发现将 COLE 确立为推进 NAS 的多功能且高效的基础。Developing effective surrogates (performance predictors) for Neural Architecture Search (NAS) typically requires expensive fine-tuning or the engineering of complex representations. We propose a low-cost embedding strategy that leverages the inductive bias of Language Models (LMs) to eliminate these overheads. By representing architectures as PyTorch class definition text, we demonstrate that off-the-shelf LMs act as competitive feature extractors without NAS-specialized fine-tuning. The final predictor is constructed by passing the extracted Code-Oriented LM Embeddings (COLE) through a lightweight regression head. We also investigate strategies to improve embedding quality and utilization. Our experiments on the NAS-Bench-201 and einspace search spaces reveal that raw code inputs yield higher predictive performance than other text-based encodings (e.g., ONNX-to-text encodings) when using frozen LMs. We also observe COLE drives superior surrogate-assisted search using the BANANAS algorithm in NAS-Bench-201. When optimizing for CIFAR-100 performance, replacing structural path encodings with COLE for architecture representation allows for a 34% decrease in the evaluation budget required to reach within 1% of the fittest architecture in the search space (by test accuracy). 1. 简介Introduction

神经架构搜索 (NAS) 可自动发现搜索空间中给定任务的高性能神经架构。然而，由于复杂的评估函数，在大型搜索空间中进行 NAS 的计算成本会很高。虽然在搜索空间中识别最佳架构的目标很容易制定（例如，最小化损失度量），但评估适应度函数的成本使得强力搜索在计算上变得困难。这种计算瓶颈激发了性能预测器（代理）领域的发展，它寻求使用低成本估计器（从简单的学习曲线外推）来近似适应度函数Neural Architecture Search (NAS) automates the discovery of high-performing neural architectures for a given task in a search space. However, conducting NAS in large search spaces becomes computationally expensive due to complex evaluation functions. While the objective for identifying an optimal architecture in a search space is simple to formulate (e.g., minimizing a loss metric), the cost of evaluating the fitness function renders brute force searches computationally intractable. This computational bottleneck motivates the area of performance predictors (surrogates), which seek to approximate the fitness function using low-cost estimators ranging from simple Learning Curve Extrapolation(Yan et al., 2021b)复杂的图神经网络 (GNN)to complex Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)(Akhauri and Abdelfattah, 2024)，用快速推断有效地取代了昂贵的真实评估。对于这种方法，必须将架构转换为与代理模型兼容的输入表示。, effectively substituting the expensive ground-truth evaluations with quick inferences. For this method, architectures must be converted into input representations compatible with surrogate models.

诸如邻接矩阵或路径编码之类的结构表示足以捕获网络拓扑。然而，捕获诸如卷积核大小之类的超参数需要自定义策略，例如将所有可接受的值注册为单独的配置，这通常实现起来很复杂或计算效率低下Structural representations such as adjacency matrices or path encodings capture network topology sufficiently. However, capturing hyperparameters like convolution kernel sizes requires custom strategies, such as registering all acceptable values as separate configurations, which is often complex to implement or computationally inefficient(Qin et al., 2025a)。此外，这些编码方案本质上是严格的，通常依赖于针对特定的基于单元的搜索空间定制的固定维矩阵。这意味着将预测器应用于新颖的架构空间需要重新设计编码方案以适应不同的属性，例如操作的数量和安排。最近进行广义编码的努力已转向语言模型（LM），并提出将它们作为通用回归器。著名的框架，例如Qin等人。. This means applying the predictor to novel architecture spaces requires re-engineering of the encoding scheme to accommodate varying properties like quantity and arrangements of operations. 2. 相关工作Related Work

2.1. 搜索空间和 NAS 基准Search Spaces and NAS Benchmarks

搜索空间通常由基于单元的方法定义，其中单元是按组堆叠形成大型架构的可重复单元（操作块）Search spaces are commonly defined by a cell-based approach, where the cell is a repeatable unit (a block of operations) stacked in groups to form a large architecture(Zoph et al., 2018; Ying et al., 2019; Dong and Yang, 2020; Zela et al., 2022; Liu et al., 2019)。在基于小区的方法中，搜索算法优化小区而不是整个网络拓扑。每个单元都是一个有向无环图（DAG），节点之间具有预定义的操作集。例如，NAS-Bench-201 搜索空间的单元. In a cell-based approach, the search algorithm optimizes the cell rather than the entire network topology. Each cell is a directed acyclic graph (DAG) with a predefined operation set between nodes. For example, cells of the NAS-Bench-201 search space(Dong and Yang, 2020)包含归零、跳过连接、卷积和平均池化操作。最近，有人致力于基于上下文无关语法定义搜索空间contain zeroize, skip-connect, convolution, and average pooling operations. More recently, there has been work on defining search spaces based on context-free grammars(Ericsson et al., 2024; Schrodi et al., 2023)。例如，Einspace 利用函数语法来使用基本操作来构建架构，以推导树表示。这使得能够发现灵活派生和组合 CNN、Transformers 和 MLP 元素的新颖原语。 为了促进替代模型或 NAS 算法的可重复比较，存在广泛采用的 NAS 基准. Einspace, for example, leverages a functional grammar to use fundamental operations to construct architectures, represented as derivation trees. This allows discovery of novel primitives that flexibly derive and combine elements of CNNs, Transformers and MLPs. To facilitate reproducible comparison of surrogate models or NAS algorithms, there are widely adopted NAS benchmarks(Ying et al., 2019; Dong and Yang, 2020; Zela et al., 2022)。这些基准测试在其自己定义的搜索空间内提供预先计算的架构精度对，从而可以在 NAS 算法执行期间轻松进行评估。我们使用 NASLib 进行评估. These benchmarks provide pre-computed architecture-accuracy pairs within their own defined search spaces, allowing easy evaluation during a NAS algorithm execution. We conduct our evaluations using NASLib(White et al., 2021b)，一个模块化的开源框架，集成了这些基准测试并为搜索策略提供统一的界面。具体来说，我们与 BANANAS 搜索算法集成, a modular open-source framework that integrates these benchmarks and provides a unified interface for search strategies. Specifically, we integrate with the BANANAS search algorithm(White et al., 2021a)，一种替代辅助方法，由 NASLib 提供。 BANANAS 将神经预测器与贝叶斯优化相结合，通过突变生成候选架构，并根据替代预测选择最有前途的架构进行评估。在这项工作中，我们将 COLE 直接集成到 BANANAS 管道中，取代了结构路径编码（BANANAS 的默认架构表示策略）。, a surrogate-assisted approach, provided by NASLib. BANANAS combines a neural predictor with Bayesian Optimization, generating candidate architectures via mutation and selecting the most promising ones for evaluation based on surrogate predictions. In this work, we integrate COLE directly into the BANANAS pipeline, replacing the structural path encoding, BANANAS’ default architecture representation strategy.

2.2. 图预测器和编码Graph Predictors and Encodings

使用性能预测器时，必须将架构编码为兼容的表示形式。早期的工作依赖于结构编码，例如邻接矩阵或路径编码Architectures must be encoded into compatible representations when working with a performance predictor. Early works relied on structural encodings such as adjacency matrices or path encodings(White et al., 2021a)，它将图拓扑映射到固定维空间。后来，引入了图神经网络（GNN）来直接从可变图结构中学习嵌入。然而，标准 GNN 通常仅将操作视为标签；为了捕获更深层的语义，GATES, which map graph topology to fixed-dimensional spaces. Later, Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) were introduced to learn embeddings directly from variable graph structures. Standard GNNs, however, often treat operations merely as labels; to capture deeper semantics, GATES(Ning et al., 2020)提出明确地模拟操作信息流，模仿网络的实际数据处理。同样，SemiNASwas proposed to explicitly model operation-wise information flow, mimicking the actual data processing of the network. Similarly, SemiNAS(Luo et al., 2020)通过在微调之前对大型未标记数据集进行预训练来提高样本效率。无监督方法，例如 Arch2Vecimproved sample efficiency by utilizing pre-training on large unlabeled datasets before fine-tuning. Unsupervised methods such as Arch2Vec(Yan et al., 2020)和凯特and CATE(Yan et al., 2021a)通过使用变分自动编码器将离散架构映射到连续的潜在空间，进一步推进了这一方向。最近，弗兰further advanced this direction by using variational autoencoders to map discrete architectures into a continuous latent space. More recently, FLAN(Akhauri and Abdelfattah, 2024)提出了基于流的图注意力，以明确捕获架构内的信号传播，融合 GNN 和 Transformer 的优点。超级NASproposed flow-based graph attention to explicitly capture signal propagation within the architecture, merging the benefits of GNNs and Transformers. HyperNAS(Lv et al., 2025)通过结合全局编码方案来增强表示学习，该方案采用图卷积网络（GCN）来捕获单元级和宏观结构依赖性，同时利用共享超网络来增强学习架构嵌入的泛化和质量。enhanced representation learning by combining a global encoding scheme that employs a Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) to capture cell-level and macro-structural dependencies, while simultaneously leveraging a shared hypernetwork to enhance the generalization and quality of the learned architecture embeddings.

2.3. 零成本代理Zero-Cost Proxies

2.3.1. 零成本代理 (ZCP) 定义。Zero-Cost Proxies (ZCPs) Definition.

ZCP 是 NAS 中的一类技术，无需实际训练即可估计神经网络的性能ZCPs are a class of techniques in NAS that estimate performance of a neural network without actually training it(White et al., 2021b)。通常，他们会进行一次向前和向后传递数据来测量通过网络的梯度流。 ZCP 通过检查网络内激活的梯度或相关性来测量可训练性等值. Typically, they conduct one forward and backward pass of data to measure gradient flow through the network. ZCPs measure values such as trainability by inspecting gradients or correlations of activations within the network(Abdelfattah et al., 2021)。 ZCP 最近的一些工作包括突触流、雅可比协方差和 GradNorm 方法. Some recent work in ZCPs include the Synaptic Flow, Jacobian Covariance, and GradNorm methods(Tanaka et al., 2020; Mellor et al., 2021; Abdelfattah et al., 2021).

2.3.2. 局限性和最新进展 (RBFleX-NAS)Limitations and Recent Advances (RBFleX-NAS)

虽然 ZCP 提供极高的效率，但它们历来在不同搜索空间和任务之间的一致性方面遇到了困难While ZCPs offer extreme efficiency, they have historically struggled with consistency across different search spaces and tasks(Abdelfattah et al., 2021)。最近解决这些限制的一个值得注意的进展是 RBFleX-NAS. A notable recent advancement addressing these limitations is RBFleX-NAS(Yamasaki et al., 2025)，在 CIFAR-10 数据集的 NAS-Bench-201 上达到了 0.569 的 Kendall’s Tau 值，与其他 ZCP 相比平均提高了 46% 左右。, which reached a Kendall’s Tau value of 0.569 on NAS-Bench-201 for the CIFAR-10 dataset, around a 46% improvement on average against other ZCPs.

5. 讨论Discussion

这项研究表明，冻结的 LM 可以作为神经架构搜索的有效、零样本架构嵌入器。我们的结果将 COLE 定位为结构编码技术（如路径编码）和现有基于文本的编码的独特且改进的替代方案。通过使用原始代码作为输入模态，我们无需进行昂贵的特定领域微调，同时在“冷启动”场景中实现强大的预测性能。This study demonstrates that frozen LMs act as effective, zero-shot architecture embedders for Neural Architecture Search. Our results position COLE as a distinct and improved alternative to structural encoding techniques (like path encodings) and existing text-based encodings. By using raw code as the input modality, we bypass the need for expensive domain-specific fine-tuning while achieving strong predictive performance in “cold-start” scenarios.

5.4. 局限性和未来方向Limitations and Future Directions

尽管使用 COLE 取得了有希望的结果，但我们承认当前研究存在一些局限性。我们的下游搜索评估仅限于 NAS-Bench-201；验证 DARTS 等更大、更多样化的搜索空间的效率提升Despite the promising results of using COLE, we acknowledge several limitations in our current study. Our downstream search evaluation was restricted to NAS-Bench-201; validating the efficiency gains on larger, more diverse search spaces like DARTS(Liu et al., 2019)仍然有必要。我们与其他基于文本的编码的比较分析仅限于 NAS-Bench-201 和 einspace。将这种验证扩展到其他搜索空间将增强我们关于本机代码优越性的研究结果的普遍性。 未来的工作可以集中在优化嵌入提取管道，包括高级池化策略或回归头的架构。此外，虽然我们专注于冻结模型以最大限度地降低成本，但根据神经架构的特定语法微调 LM 仍然是提高性能的一个有希望的途径。此外，代码表示的统一性质自然有利于联合架构和超参数搜索，从而能够同时优化拓扑结构和训练方案（例如优化器和学习率调度器）。最后，将 COLE 扩展到多目标 NAS，例如硬件感知，提供了巨大的潜力。remains necessary. Our comparative analysis against other text-based encodings was limited to NAS-Bench-201 and einspace. Expanding this validation to other search spaces would strengthen the generalizability of our findings regarding the superiority of native code. Future work can focus on optimizing the embedding extraction pipeline, including advanced pooling strategies or architecture of the regression head. Furthermore, while we focused on frozen models to minimize cost, fine-tuning LMs on the specific syntax of neural architectures still represents a promising avenue to boost performance. Additionally, the unified nature of code representations naturally facilitates joint architecture and hyperparameter search, enabling the simultaneous optimization of topological structure and training scheme (e.g., optimizers and learning rate schedulers). Finally, extending COLE to multi-objective NAS, such as hardware awareness, offers significant potential.
