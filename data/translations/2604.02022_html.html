摘要Abstract

评估基于 LLM 的代理的安全性是一个越来越重要的挑战，因为实际部署中的风险通常是通过多步骤交互逐渐出现的，而不是来自孤立的提示或最终响应。然而，现有的轨迹级基准在三个关键方面仍然受到限制：（1）由于工具生态系统受限和场景覆盖范围狭窄，交互多样性不足； (2) 安全故障的可观察性有限，因为粗略的标签无法捕捉风险如何产生和演变； (3)缺乏长远的现实主义，短期或简化的轨迹低估了延迟的和与环境相关的风险。为了解决这些限制，我们引入了 ATBench，这是一种轨迹级基准，可以对代理安全进行结构化、多样化和现实的评估。 ATBench沿着三个正交维度（风险源、故障模式和现实世界危害）制定代理安全风险，为捕获不同的风险模式提供了可控框架。在此基础上，我们使用具有异构工具池的分类引导生成引擎构建轨迹，并结合长上下文延迟触发协议，通过多阶段交互来模拟现实风险的出现。该基准测试包含 1,000 条轨迹（503 条安全，497 条不安全），平均 9.01 次转弯和 3.95k 个代币，以及从涵盖 2,084 个可用工具的池中提取的 1,954 个调用工具。通过基于规则和基于法学硕士的过滤以及人工全面审核来确保数据质量。跨前沿法学硕士、开源模型和专业防护系统的实验表明，即使对于最强大的评估者来说，ATBench 仍然具有挑战性 - GPT-5.4 在二元安全分类上仅达到 76.7% F1，在细粒度风险源诊断上仅达到 33.6% - 同时实现分类分层分析、跨基准比较和长期故障模式诊断。数据和代码将在接受后发布。Evaluating the safety of LLM-based agents is an increasingly important challenge, as risks in realistic deployments often emerge gradually over multi-step interactions rather than from isolated prompts or final responses. Existing trajectory-level benchmarks, however, remain limited in three key dimensions: (1) insufficient interaction diversity, due to restricted tool ecosystems and narrow scenario coverage; (2) limited observability of safety failures, as coarse labels fail to capture how risks arise and evolve; and (3) lack of long-horizon realism, where short or simplified trajectories underestimate delayed and context-dependent risks. To address these limitations, we introduce ATBench, a trajectory-level benchmark that enables structured, diverse, and realistic evaluation of agent safety. ATBench formulates agentic safety risks along three orthogonal dimensions—risk source, failure mode, and real-world harm—providing a controllable framework for capturing diverse risk patterns. Building on this formulation, we construct trajectories using a taxonomy-guided generation engine with heterogeneous tool pools and incorporate a long-context delayed-trigger protocol that models realistic risk emergence through multi-stage interactions. The benchmark contains 1,000 trajectories (503 safe, 497 unsafe), averaging 9.01 turns and 3.95k tokens, with 1,954 invoked tools drawn from pools spanning 2,084 available tools. Data quality is ensured through rule-based & LLM-based filtering and a human full audit. Experiments across frontier LLMs, open-source models, and specialized guard systems show that ATBench remains challenging even for the strongest evaluators— GPT-5.4 achieves only 76.7% F1 on binary safety classification and 33.6% on fine-grained risk-source diagnosis—while enabling taxonomy-stratified analysis, cross-benchmark comparison, and diagnosis of long-horizon failure patterns. Data and code will be released upon acceptance.

1 简介1 Introduction

基于 LLM 的代理正在迅速从单轮文本交互发展到涉及工具使用、外部观察和持久状态的长视野执行（Yuan 等人，2024a；Luo 等人，2025；Chen 等人，2025b）。这种转变从根本上改变了安全评估的单位。在现实环境中，安全风险通常不会通过单一响应来揭示，而是通过扩展的交互痕迹逐渐显现出来——通过复合规划错误、不安全的工具使用、过度依赖环境反馈或延迟利用先前获得的权限。因此，评估代理安全性需要超越即时级别的评估，转向对整个交互轨迹进行推理。LLM-based agents are rapidly evolving from single-turn text interaction to long-horizon execution involving tool use, external observations, and persistent state (Yuan et al., 2024a; Luo et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025b). This shift fundamentally changes the unit of safety evaluation. In realistic settings, safety risks are often not revealed by a single response but instead emerge gradually over extended interaction traces — through compounding planning errors, unsafe tool use, over-reliance on environmental feedback, or delayed exploitation of previously acquired permissions. Consequently, evaluating agent safety requires moving beyond prompt-level assessment toward reasoning over full trajectories of interaction.

轨迹级代理安全评估。Trajectory-level agent safety evaluation.最近的工作已将代理安全评估从静态纯文本设置转向具有工具使用和多步骤执行的交互式环境（Debenedetti 等人，2024；Kuntz 等人，2025；Xia 等人，2025）。这种转变激发了轨迹级基准测试，评估完整执行轨迹的安全性，而不是孤立的提示或最终响应。 R-Judge 表明，行为层面的评估比仅做出反应的判断要困难得多（Yuan 等人，2024b）。 AgentSafetyBench 通过不同的环境和故障模式扩大了不安全代理行为的覆盖范围（Zhang 等人，2024）。 AgentAuditor 引入了人类级别的 LLM-asa-judge 框架以及 ASSEBench，用于真实的代理交互记录（Luo 等人，2025）。这些努力确立了轨迹级评估的重要性。最近的并行工作还研究了受限环境下预执行工具的安全性和欺骗性代理行为（Xie et al., 2025；Guo et al., 2025）。这些努力非常有价值，但它们仍然为更广泛的工具暴露、更可控的故障多样性覆盖以及更现实的长期交互留下了空间。表 1 总结了代表性比较。 ATBench 旨在通过现实主义下的可控多样性来解决这些差距。Recent work has moved agent safety evaluation beyond static text-only settings toward interactive environments with tool use and multi-step execution (Debenedetti et al., 2024; Kuntz et al., 2025; Xia et al., 2025). ToolAlpaca demonstrates generalized tool-use learning from simulated interactions (Tang et al., 2023a). ToolLLM introduces ToolBench, which collects and instantiates a large real-world API pool (Qin et al., 2023b). ToolACE improves data quality through self-evolving synthesis and dual-layer verification over a larger API pool (Liu et al., 2024). Recent work further shows that synthetic trajectories can support guardrail training at scale (Huang et al., 2025). Our construction pipeline is closely related to this line of work: the tool pool of ATBench is adapted from these resources, then normalized, deduplicated, and expanded with simulated tools for safety-oriented coverage completion. Unlike generic tool-use data construction, however, ATBench uses taxonomy-guided generation to control which risks are instantiated, where they are triggered, how they evolve across a trajectory, and what downstream harm they produce.

It provides a diverse and realistic testbed for measuring whether general-purpose models and specialized guards can generalize across heterogeneous tools, long contexts, delayed triggers, and fine-grained categories of failure and harm.

设计目标和基准范围。Design objective and benchmark scope.我们的施工方法结合了两种成分。首先，我们定义了一个三维安全分类法，指定目标风险空间，并构建异构工具池，提供通过不同场景实现该风险空间所需的可执行接口空间。其次，我们使用生成和管理管道实例化这些成分，其中包括基于规划器的轨迹合成、配对安全/不安全构造、延迟触发长上下文协议、基于规则和基于 LLM 的过滤以及完整的人工审​​计。Our construction method combines two ingredients. First, we define a three-dimensional safety taxonomy that specifies the target risk space and build a heterogeneous tool pool that provides the executable interface space needed to realize that risk space through diverse scenarios. Second, we instantiate these ingredients with a generation-and-curation pipeline that includes planner-based trajectory synthesis, paired safe/unsafe construction, a delayed-trigger long-context protocol, rule-based and LLM-based filtering, and full human audit.

3.2 三维安全分类和工具库3.2 Three-Dimensional Safety Taxonomy and Tool Pool

分类为建筑脚手架。Taxonomy as a construction scaffold.为了使多样性可控而不是偶然，我们沿着本文提出的三个正交维度组织不安全轨迹：To make diversity controllable rather than incidental, we organize unsafe trajectories along three orthogonal dimensions proposed in this paper:风险源risk source , 失效模式failure mode, 和, and现实世界的伤害real-world harm。这些维度回答了风险进入轨迹的位置、不安全行为如何实现以及产生什么后果。在构建管道中，这种分类法充当覆盖控制的脚手架，而不是其本身。它指定应实例化哪些代理风险片段，而生成引擎则确定如何在具体的长期轨迹中实现它们。. These dimensions answer where risk enters a trajectory, how unsafe behavior is realized, and what consequence it produces. In the construction pipeline, this taxonomy serves as a scaffold for coverage control rather than as an end in itself. It specifies which slices of agentic risk should be instantiated, while the generation engine determines how to realize them in concrete long-horizon trajectories.

分类结构。Taxonomy structure.在叶级别，分类包含 8 个风险源类别、14 个故障模式类别和 10 个危害类别。风险源轴围绕四个高级系列进行组织：用户输入、环境观察、外部实体以及内部逻辑和故障。失败模式轴涵盖行为失败和输出内容失败。危害轴捕获了下游后果，例如隐私、财务、安全、身体、声誉、社会、公平和功能危害。这些高级轴提供了结构化材料，用于以受控方式对不同的代理风险模式进行采样，而不是依赖于非结构化的场景清单。完整的分类定义保留在附录 A 中。At the leaf level, the taxonomy contains 8 risk-source categories, 14 failure-mode categories, and 10 harm categories. The risk-source axis is organized around four high-level families: user input, environmental observation, external entities, and internal logic and failures. The failure-mode axis covers behavioral failures and output-content failures. The harm axis captures downstream consequences such as privacy, financial, security, physical, reputational, societal, fairness, and functional harms. These high-level axes provide structured material for sampling diverse agentic risk patterns in a controlled manner, rather than relying on an unstructured inventory of scenarios. Full taxonomy definitions remain in Appendix A.

异构工具池。Heterogeneous tool pool.该分类法与异构工具池配对，提供实现不同场景所需的可执行材料。我们从现实的公共API（例如rapid api）、从ToolBench和ToolAlpaca（Qin等人，2023a；Tang等人，2023b）等先前工具使用资源改编的工具以及填补代表性不足的能力和风险相关区域的模拟工具中组装了这个工具池。然后，我们跨源标准化工具名称、参数和描述，并删除几乎重复的工具。这种设计允许通过不同的工具组合、界面条件和交互结构而不是通过单个模板来实例化相同的分类切片。从这个意义上说，分类法定义了多样性的轴，工具库提供了实现多样性的接口空间。The taxonomy is paired with a heterogeneous tool pool, which provides the executable material needed to realize diverse scenarios. We assemble this tool pool from realistic public APIs(e.g. rapid api), tools adapted from prior tool-use resources such as ToolBench and ToolAlpaca (Qin et al., 2023a; Tang et al., 2023b), and simulated tools that fill underrepresented capability and risk-relevant regions. We then normalize tool names, arguments, and descriptions across sources and remove near-duplicate tools. This design allows the same taxonomy slice to be instantiated through different tool combinations, interface conditions, and interaction structures rather than through a single template. In this sense, the taxonomy defines the axes of diversity, and the tool pool provides the interface space for realizing that diversity.

主要标签注释协议。Primary-label annotation protocol.每个轨迹接收一个二进制Each trajectory receives a binary安全safe / 不安全unsafe判决。对于不安全的轨迹，注释者以固定的因果顺序为每个分类维度分配一个主要标签。风险源识别最早引入不安全状况的因素。故障模式识别导致该风险的主要不安全行为。现实世界的危害确定了故障链的主要后果。当多个标签合理时，注释者会选择对主因果链最核心的标签。这种单一主标签政策使分类分层评估保持稳定。附录 B.1 中提供了完整的注释指南。verdict. For unsafe trajectories, annotators assign one primary label per taxonomy dimension in a fixed causal order. Risk source identifies the earliest factor that introduces the unsafe condition. Failure mode identifies the dominant unsafe behavior that realizes that risk. Real-world harm identifies the principal consequence of the failure chain. When multiple labels are plausible, annotators select the label that is most central to the main causal chain. This single-primary-label policy keeps taxonomy-stratified evaluation stable. Full annotation guidelines are provided in Appendix B.1.

3.3 现实主义下的多样性生成引擎3.3 Generation Engine for Diversity under Realism

生成引擎将分类定义的多样性和工具池支持的多样性转化为可执行的轨迹，同时保留现实性。对于每个样本，规划器对场景控制进行操作，这些场景控制共同指定沿三个风险维度采样的分类切片以及从工具池中提取的候选工具，然后实例化具体的用户任务、触发位置和预期的安全结果。由于相同的风险切片可以通过不同的任务制定、工具组合和交互结构来实现，因此基准不会崩溃为少量重复的模板。The generation engine translates the diversity defined by the taxonomy and supported by the tool pool into executable trajectories while preserving realism. For each sample, the planner operates on scenario controls that jointly specify a taxonomy slice sampled along the three risk dimensions and candidate tools drawn from the tool pool, and then instantiates a concrete user task, trigger location, and intended safety outcome. 4.2 ATBench 主要结果4.2 Main Results on ATBench

轨迹级安全结果。Trajectory-level safety results.表 2 显示，即使对于强大的评估者来说，ATBench 仍然具有挑战性。 GPT-5.4 实现了最高的粗粒度 F1，为 76.7%，其次是 Gemini-3.1-Pro（75.0%）和轨迹训练的 AgentDoG-Qwen3-4B（71.1%）。强大的开源通用模型得分大幅较低（Qwen3.5-397B 为 67.8%，Qwen3-235B 为 60.8%），这表明即使对于前沿系统，ATBench 也远未饱和。Table 2 shows that ATBench remains challenging even for strong evaluators. GPT-5.4 achieves the highest coarse-grained F1 of 76.7%, followed by Gemini-3.1-Pro at 75.0% and the trajectory-trained AgentDoG-Qwen3-4B at 71.1%. Strong open-source general models score substantially lower (Qwen3.5-397B at 67.8%, Qwen3-235B at 60.8%), indicating that ATBench is far from saturated even for frontier systems.

对于细粒度诊断来说，差距大大扩大。即使是强大的闭源模型，在风险源上也只能达到 33.6%，在故障模式上也只能达到 13.5%，而 GPT-5.4 的现实世界危害预测仍然有限，为 30.2%。事实证明，认识到一条轨迹是不安全的，远比恢复风险的起源、风险的发展方式以及风险产生的危害要容易得多。轨迹训练模型在此处保留了优势：AgentDoG-Qwen3-4B 实现了最强的细粒度结果（46.8% 风险源、16.5% 故障模式、40.6% 伤害），而现成的防护模型在结构化因果归因方面仍然相当弱。The gap widens considerably for fine-grained diagnosis. Even strong closed-source models reach only 33.6% on risk source and 13.5% on failure mode, while real-world harm prediction remains limited at 30.2% for GPT-5.4. Recognizing that a trajectory is unsafe proves far easier than recovering where the risk originates, how it unfolds, and what harm it produces. Trajectory-trained models retain an edge here: AgentDoG-Qwen3-4B achieves the strongest fine-grained results (46.8% risk source, 16.5% failure mode, 40.6% harm), whereas off-theshelf guard models remain considerably weaker on structured causal attribution.

Crucially, the added difficulty reflects controllable diversity under realism rather than synthetic noise: models must track executable tools, persistent state, and delayed dependencies within realistic trajectories.

5 讨论5 Discussion

ATBench 注重多样性和现实性之间的权衡。更广泛的工具覆盖范围、更丰富的场景构成和更细粒度的标签可以降低过度拟合狭窄故障模式的风险，但没有结构检查的多样性可能会产生模板化或不可信的数据。我们的构建流程通过延迟触发的长上下文协议、基于规则和基于 LLM 的过滤以及全面的人工审核来解决这种紧张关系，我们预计这种权衡仍将是未来基准设计的核心。ATBench centers on the trade-off between diversity and realism. Broader tool coverage, richer scenario composition, and finer-grained labels reduce the risk of overfitting to narrow failure patterns, but diversity without structural checks can produce templated or implausible data. Our construction pipeline addresses this tension through delayed-trigger long-context protocols, rule-based & LLM-based filtering, and full human audit, and we expect this trade-off to remain central for future benchmark design.

局限性。Limitations.ATBench 仍然有局限性。首先，每个不安全轨迹每个分类维度仅带有一个主要标签，这可能会在多原因情况下错过有效的次要解释。其次，所有轨迹都是用英语构建的；将基准扩展到多语言环境将扩大其对全球代理部署的适用性。第三，基准测试重点关注文本和工具轨迹；多式联运和具体化设置仍然是未来重要的扩展。ATBench still has limitations. First, each unsafe trajectory carries only one primary label per taxonomy dimension, which may miss valid secondary interpretations in multi-causal cases. Second, all trajectories are constructed in English; extending the benchmark to multilingual settings would broaden its applicability to global agent deployments. Third, the benchmark focuses on text-and-tool trajectories; multimodal and embodied settings remain important future extensions.

6 结论6 Conclusion

我们推出了 ATBench，这是一个长期代理安全评估的基准，它围绕一个简单的原则构建：最大限度地提高多样性，同时保持现实性。为了实现这一目标，该基准使用关于风险源、故障模式和现实世界危害的统一且分层的三维分类法作为结构化支架，然后通过分类法引导的生成引擎、异构工具池、共享状态设置-利用长上下文协议和人在环质量控制来实例化这种多样性。我们的实验表明，ATBench 对于通用模型和专业防护都具有挑战性，特别是在轨迹级安全评估和细粒度诊断方面。我们希望 ATBench 能够帮助标准化对代理安全系统更现实的评估，并鼓励未来在长上下文、工具介导的风险理解方面开展工作。We introduced ATBench, a benchmark for long-horizon agent safety evaluation built around a simple principle: maximize diversity while preserving realism. To operationalize this objective, the benchmark uses a unified and hierarchical three-dimensional taxonomy over risk source, failure mode, and real-world harm as a structured scaffold, and then instantiates that diversity through a taxonomy-guided generation engine, a heterogeneous tool pool, a shared-state setup–exploit long-context protocol, and human-in-the-loop quality control. Our experiments show that ATBench is challenging for both general-purpose models and specialized guards, especially on trajectory-level safety evaluation and fine-grained diagnosis. We hope ATBench can help standardize more realistic evaluation of agent safety systems and encourage future work on long-context, tool-mediated risk understanding.

B.2 长上下文延迟触发细节B.2 Long-Context Delayed-Trigger Details

本小节描述了长上下文设置中延迟触发轨迹生成的实现。This subsection describes the implementation of delayed-trigger trajectory generation in the long-context setting.

两集结构。Two-episode structure.对于长上下文场景，规划器将轨迹组织为两个连续的情节：SETUP 情节和稍后的 EXPLOIT 情节。设置事件建立可重用状态，而漏洞利用事件则构建为依赖于早期状态而不是新引入的本地触发器。For long-context scenarios, the planner organizes the trajectory into two contiguous episodes: a SETUP episode and a later EXPLOIT episode. The setup episode establishes reusable state, while the exploit episode is constructed to depend on that earlier state rather than on a newly introduced local trigger.

上下文继承。Context carry-over.在执行时，设置情节中生成的用户、助理和环境消息保留在共享对话历史记录中，并被转发到漏洞利用情节中。因此，后来的世代阶段取决于可见的先前转录本，而不是仅取决于当前的回合。At execution time, user, assistant, and environment messages produced in the setup episode remain in the shared conversation history and are carried forward into the exploit episode. Consequently, later generation stages condition on the visible prior transcript rather than on the current turn alone.

延迟触发器接地。Grounding of delayed triggers.当漏洞利用端步骤被标记为 source=CONTEXT 时，规划人员将被指示将其置于早期的工具输出或先前建立的权限中，而不是引入新的触发器。When an exploit-side step is marked as source=CONTEXT, the planner is instructed to ground it in earlier tool outputs or previously established permissions rather than introducing a fresh trigger.

安全和不安全的实现。Safe and unsafe realization.该轨迹包含指定的延迟触发风险点，通常在漏洞利用事件中。在不安全的轨迹中，智能体根据遗留的依赖性采取行动，风险就会被意识到。在安全轨迹中，相同的先前上下文仍然可用，但代理拒绝、验证或以其他方式消除依赖性。因此，与安全相关的分歧发生在延迟触发步骤，而两个变体共享相同的遗留上下文。The trajectory contains a designated delayed-trigger risk point, typically in the exploit episode. In unsafe trajectories, the agent acts on the carried-over dependency and the risk is realized. In safe trajectories, the same prior context remains available, but the agent refuses, verifies, or otherwise neutralizes the dependency. Thus, the safety-relevant divergence occurs at the delayed-trigger step while both variants share the same carried-over context.
