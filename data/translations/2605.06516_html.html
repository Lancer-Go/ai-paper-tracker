摘要Abstract

Benders 分解 (BD) 是一种广泛使用的解决方法，用于解决现实世界不确定性决策中出现的两阶段随机程序。然而，随着主问题随着削减次数的增加而增长，它经常会遇到收敛缓慢的问题。 在本文中，我们提出了 BD 强化学习 (RLBD)，这是一种使用基于神经网络的随机策略自适应选择剪切的框架。该策略是通过 REINFORCE 算法使用策略梯度方法进行训练的。我们评估了所提出的两阶段随机电动汽车充电站位置问题的方法，并将其与 vanilla BD 和 LearnBD 进行比较Benders decomposition (BD) is a widely used solution approach for solving two-stage stochastic programs arising in real-world decision-making under uncertainty. However, it often suffers from slow convergence as the master problem grows with an increasing number of cuts. In this paper, we propose Reinforcement Learning for BD (RLBD), a framework that adaptively selects cuts using a neural network-based stochastic policy. The policy is trained using a policy gradient method via the REINFORCE algorithm. We evaluate the proposed approach on a two-stage stochastic electric vehicle charging station location problem and compare it with vanilla BD and LearnBD(Jia and Shen, 2021)，一种使用支持向量机对剪切进行分类的监督学习方法。数值结果表明，RLBD 在计算效率方面取得了显着的提高，并对结构相似但数据输入和决策变量维度不同的问题表现出很强的泛化能力。, a supervised learning approach that classifies cuts using a support vector machine. Numerical results demonstrate that RLBD achieves substantial improvements in computational efficiency and exhibits strong generalization to problems with similar structures but varying data inputs and decision variable dimensions.

1 简介Introduction

两阶段随机程序为不确定性下的决策建模提供了一个通用框架，其中决策分为Two-stage stochastic programs provide a general framework for modeling decision-making under uncertainty, where decisions are separated into此时此地here-and-now（第一阶段）在不确定性出现之前做出的决定，以及(first-stage) decisions, made before uncertainty is realized, and观望wait-and-see（第二阶段）决策，在不确定性出现后通过追索行动适应观察到的结果。这种建模范例已广泛应用于各种应用中，包括设施位置问题(second-stage) decisions, which adapt to observed outcomes through recourse actions after the uncertainty unfolds. This modeling paradigm has been widely used in a broad range of applications, including facility location problems(Magnanti and Wong, 1981; Fischetti et al., 2016, 2017a)、供应链管理, supply chain management(Keyvanshokooh et al., 2016; Basciftci et al., 2023)、调度和路由问题, scheduling and routing problems(Adulyasak et al., 2015; Bodur and Luedtke, 2017; Yu et al., 2021, 2023)、能源系统运行, energy system operations(Alguacil and Conejo, 2000; Wang et al., 2011; Daneshvar et al., 2020)等。这些问题的一个关键挑战是第二阶段决策依赖于随机实现，从而导致大规模优化模型。随着场景数量的增加，直接解决由此产生的扩展形式问题在计算上变得令人望而却步。, among others. A key challenge in these problems is that the second-stage decisions depend on random realizations, leading to large-scale optimization models. As the number of scenarios increases, directly solving the resulting extensive-form problem becomes computationally prohibitive.

Benders 分解 (BD) 已成为解决两阶段随机规划的流行方法，它通过将原始问题分解为一个主问题和一组子问题Benders decomposition (BD) has emerged as a popular method for solving two-stage stochastic programs by decomposing the original problem into a master problem and a set of subproblems(Benders, 1962)。具体来说，在每次迭代时，算法都会生成近似追索函数（第二阶段价值函数）的割断，并迭代地收紧主问题。因此，BD 的计算性能主要取决于迭代过程中添加的剪切质量。在多切割公式中，算法为每个场景或子问题生成单独的切割，从而产生追索函数的更紧密和更准确的表示；然而，添加所有这些可能会显着增加主问题的规模并减慢后续解决的速度。另一方面，在单切割公式中，所有子问题（场景方面）信息在每次迭代时都会聚合到一个切割中，从而导致主问题较小，但追索函数的近似值可能较弱。这引入了加强主问题和保持计算效率之间的权衡。我们参考. Specifically, at each iteration, the algorithm generates cuts that approximate the recourse function (the second-stage value function) and iteratively tighten the master problem. As a result, the computational performance of BD depends critically on the quality of the cuts that are added during this iterative process. In the multi-cut formulation, the algorithm generates separate cuts for each scenario or subproblem, producing a tighter and more accurate representation of the recourse function; however, adding all of them can significantly increase the size of the master problem and slow down subsequent solves. On the other hand, in the single-cut formulation, all subproblem (scenario-wise) information is aggregated into one cut per iteration, leading to a smaller master problem but potentially weaker approximations of the recourse function. This introduces a trade-off between strengthening the master problem and maintaining computational efficiency. 2 相关工作Related Work

多种经典技术被开发来加速BD的收敛。其中，剪切管理是一项关键技术，它仔细控制在主问题中添加和保留哪些剪切。许多方法不是添加解决子问题的所有候选切割，而是在每次迭代时仅添加一组选定的信息切割，例如最违反的切割或帕累托最优切割，以在不过度扩大主问题的情况下提高收敛性A variety of classical techniques have been developed to accelerate the convergence of BD. Among them, cut management is a key technique that carefully controls which cuts to add and retain in the master problem. Instead of adding all candidate cuts from solving the subproblems, many approaches add only a selected set of informative cuts at each iteration, such as the most violated cuts or the Pareto-optimal cuts, to improve convergence without excessively enlarging the master problem(Magnanti and Wong, 1981; Fischetti et al., 2017b)。另一种广泛使用的启发式方法是删除不再有助于收紧公式的冗余或不活跃的削减，从而控制主问题的规模。另一方面，文献中已经研究了场景分组技术，通过聚合来自相似场景的多次切割来提高计算效率. Another widely used heuristic is to remove redundant or inactive cuts that no longer contribute to tightening the formulation, thereby controlling the size of the master problem. On the other hand, scenario grouping techniques have been studied in the literature to improve computational efficiency by aggregating multiple cuts from similar scenarios(Adulyasak et al., 2015; Maheo et al., 2019; Ramírez-Pico et al., 2023)。另一种研究流侧重于通过不精确地解决主问题或子问题来减少每次迭代的解决时间，从而导致所谓的不精确 BD. 6 结论Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们提出了一种 RLBD 框架，用于自适应选择 Benders 割，以提高求解两阶段随机程序的效率。将剪切选择过程建模为 MDP，并使用 REINFORCE 算法训练随机剪切选择策略。学习到的策略可以部署在具有不同不确定性分布和问题规模的测试实例上。针对随机电动汽车充电站位置问题的数值实验表明，RLBD 可以有效地推广到结构相似但数据输入（包括分布变化）和决策变量维度不同的问题。与现有的基准方法相比，RLBD 在中小型测试实例中实现了高达五倍的计算时间减少，并在较大的测试实例中获得了更小的最优性差距。In this work, we proposed an RLBD framework for adaptively selecting Benders cuts to improve the efficiency of solving two-stage stochastic programs. The cut-selection process was modeled as an MDP, and the stochastic cut-selection policy was trained using the REINFORCE algorithm. The learned policy can be deployed on testing instances with varying uncertainty distributions and problem sizes. Numerical experiments on a stochastic EV charging station location problem demonstrated that RLBD generalizes effectively to problems with similar structures but different data inputs (including distribution shifts) and decision variable dimensions. Compared with existing benchmark methods, RLBD achieves up to a fivefold reduction in computational time in small to medium testing instances and obtains smaller optimality gaps in larger testing instances.
