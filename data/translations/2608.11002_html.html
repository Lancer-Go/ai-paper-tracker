1. 简介Introduction

语言是人类和人工智能之间的主要界面，在塑造多模式生成内容方面发挥着决定性作用。 文本到图像（T2I）模型体现了这一趋势，通过大规模扩散框架在英语领域取得了显着的成功Language is a primary interface between humans and artificial intelligence, playing a decisive role in shaping multimodal generative content. Text-to-image (T2I) models exemplify this trend, achieving remarkable success in English through large-scale diffusion frameworks(58; 70)。 然而，这些进步很大程度上依赖于以英语为主的数据集，例如 COCO Captions. However, these advances largely rely on English-dominant datasets like COCO Captions(9)和LAION-5Band LAION-5B(51)，使得他们在多语言环境中的能力很大程度上未被充分开发。 与此同时，多语言 NLP 的研究强调语言多样性和包容性对于开发公平且具有文化意识的人工智能至关重要, leaving their capabilities in multilingual settings largely underexplored. In parallel, research in multilingual NLP has emphasized that linguistic diversity and inclusivity are crucial for developing equitable and culturally aware AI(25; 32)，强调将 T2I 评估扩展到英语之外的重要性。, underscoring the importance of extending T2I evaluation beyond English.

多语言 T2I 一代面临两个基本挑战：生成视觉内容必须考虑不同语言中嵌入的独特文化属性； 文本渲染任务（即生成具有特定文本内容的图像）需要处理不同的书写系统。 之前的工作已经努力将 T2I 模型扩展到多语言环境，方法是将现有编码器与有限的多语言基础结合起来Multilingual T2I generation faces two fundamental challenges: generating visual content must account for the unique cultural attributes embedded in different languages; the Text Rendering task—that is, generating images with specific textual content—requires handling diverse writing systems. Previous work has made efforts to extend T2I models to multilingual settings, either by incorporating existing encoders with limited multilingual foundations(29; 64; 47)或利用大型生成法学硕士进行更强有力的即时解释or by leveraging large generative LLMs for stronger prompt interpretation(70; 61)。 然而，现有的多语言 T2I 研究仍然有限. However, existing studies on multilingual T2I remain limited(50; 18; 67; 23)，主要关注图像质量，而忽略语言方面和文本渲染评估。, focusing mainly on image quality while overlooking linguistic aspects and text rendering evaluation.

5. 局限性和洞察力Limitation and Insight

限制。Limitation.拟议的 LingT2I 基准不可避免地涉及翻译，这可能会引入偏差；但是，我们采用严格的验证和人工检查来尽量减少此类影响。同样，虽然现有指标并非完全与语言无关，但我们采用多语言编码器并调整协议来提高公平性。重要的是，观察到的性能差距很大且一致，因此不太可能用这些因素来解释。The proposed LingT2I benchmark inevitably involves translation, which can introduce bias; however, we apply strict verification and human checks to minimize such effects. Similarly, while existing metrics are not fully language-agnostic, we adopt multilingual encoders and adapt protocols to improve fairness. Importantly, the observed performance gaps are large and consistent, and are therefore unlikely to be explained by these factors.

洞察力。Insight.我们的研究结果提出了未来研究的几个方向。Our findings suggest several directions for future research.首先，First,多语言能力应该通过本地架构设计而不是事后适应来实现。multilingual capability should be achieved through native architectural design rather than post-hoc adaptation.第二，Second,培训数据应按语系组织，并根据文化基础进行整理。第三，模型应在各个评估维度上保持平衡的性能，避免对单一质量方面的过度优化。training data should be organized by language family and curated with cultural grounding. Third, models should maintain balanced performance across evaluation dimensions, avoiding over-optimization toward a single aspect of quality.此外，In addition,具有偏见意识的数据管理和基于翻译的增强可能有助于减轻文化和人口偏见并提高跨语言公平性。bias-aware data curation and translation-based augmentation may help mitigate cultural and demographic biases and improve cross-lingual fairness.最后，Finally,考虑到书写系统面临的挑战，模型可以受益于特定于脚本的渲染模块或针对其结构特征定制的训练策略。given the challenges across writing systems, models could benefit from script-specific rendering modules or training strategies tailored to their structural characteristics.
