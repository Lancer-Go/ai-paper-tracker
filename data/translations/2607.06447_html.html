Danus：利用事实图内存编排数学推理代理Danus: Orchestrating Mathematical Reasoning Agents with Fact-Graph Memory

Jihao Liu    Guoxiong Gao    Zeming Sun    Bin Wu    Shurui Liu    Jiedong Jiang    Haocheng Ju    Leheng Chen    Ronnie Cheng    Xiping Zhang    Bin Dong [ dongbin@math.pku.edu.cn

摘要Abstract

最近基于法学硕士的数学推理代理已经开始解决研究级问题，并且在某些情况下，为解决开放问题做出了贡献。然而，有效地扩展和编排此类代理仍然具有挑战性，因为在保持中间声明有序且可靠的同时协调并行证明搜索很困难。在本文中，我们提出了 Danus，一个用于研究级数学推理的编排系统，以共享事实图为中心，作为全局内存管理机制。 Danus 由一个执行规划和协调的主代理、多个并行执行证明搜索的工作代理以及一个无状态验证器组成，该验证器在提议的数学声明被纳入事实图之前对其进行检查。每个经过验证的事实与其证明和逻辑依赖项存储在一起，允许系统增量构建长参数，同时保持共享证明状态的组织。主要代理定期总结不断变化的证明状态，将工作人员重定向到有希望的方向，并通过进度报告支持与人类数学家的互动。我们通过代数几何、奇点理论和组合学方面的六个研究级案例研究来评估 Danus，说明事实图记忆机制如何使 Danus 能够构建长而详细的数学证明。我们的结果表明，基于事实图的编排为扩展数学推理代理以解决长期研究问题提供了有效的途径。 Danus 是开源的。Recent LLM-based mathematical reasoning agents have begun to tackle research-level problems and, in several cases, have contributed to the resolution of open problems. However, scaling and orchestrating such agents effectively remains challenging, due to the difficulty of coordinating parallel proof search while keeping intermediate claims organized and reliable. In this paper, we propose Danus, an orchestration system for research-level mathematical reasoning centered on a shared fact graph as a global memory-management mechanism. Danus consists of a main agent that performs planning and coordination, multiple worker agents that carry out proof search in parallel, and a stateless verifier that checks proposed mathematical claims before they are admitted into the fact graph. Each verified fact is stored together with its proof and logical dependencies, allowing the system to build long arguments incrementally while keeping the shared proof state organized. The main agent periodically summarizes the evolving proof state, redirects workers across promising directions, and supports interaction with human mathematicians through progress reports. We evaluate Danus through six research-level case studies in algebraic geometry, singularity theory, and combinatorics, illustrating how the fact-graph memory mechanism enables Danus to construct long, detailed mathematical proofs. Our results suggest that fact-graph-based orchestration provides an effective route toward scaling mathematical reasoning agents for long-horizon research problems. Danus is open source.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）越来越多地被部署为代理系统的推理核心，这些系统检索知识、调用工具、执行代码、与外部环境交互，并通过反馈修改其输出。在这些系统中，性能不仅取决于基本模型，还取决于控制信息、工具、状态和反馈如何向模型公开的工具。通过适当的利用工程，基于 LLM 的代理在需要外部信息、迭代校正或长期执行的任务上可以超越非交互式提示基线Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as the reasoning cores of agentic systems that retrieve knowledge, call tools, execute code, interact with external environments, and revise their outputs through feedback. In these systems, performance depends not only on the base model, but also on the harness that governs how information, tools, state, and feedback are exposed to the model. With appropriate harness engineering, LLM-based agents can outperform non-interactive prompting baselines on tasks requiring external information, iterative correction, or long-horizon execution[27, 48].

By contrast, there has been little systematic study of how to scale mathematical reasoning systems built around generate–verify–revise loops by increasing the number of agents directly involved in proof generation. Scaling such agents is not simply a matter of spawning many agents to work on the same problem without further modification. It requires careful memory management and coordination. If the shared memory of multiple agents is not handled appropriately, it can confuse the agents, propagate irrelevant or erroneous intermediate artifacts, and ultimately hurt performance.

为了有效地扩展和编排数学推理代理，我们提出了 Danus，这是一个以共享事实图为中心的编排系统，充当全局内存管理机制。在 Danus 中，一个主代理执行规划和协调，而多个工作代理执行证明生成。共享事实图将中间非正式验证的事实组织起来，使系统能够构建长而详细的证明。To scale and orchestrate mathematical reasoning agents effectively, we propose Danus, an orchestration system centered on a shared fact graph that serves as a global memory-management mechanism. In Danus, a main agent performs planning and coordination, while multiple worker agents carry out proof generation. The shared fact graph keeps intermediate informally verified facts organized, enabling the system to construct long, elaborated proofs.

Danus 和人类数学家之间的互动也是经过精心设计的。主代理定期将当前的证明状态总结成报告，允许人类数学家检查证明的进度，并在必要时通过主代理提供高层指导。主代理还可以使用同一份报告来咨询 GPT-5.5-pro 等先进系统，以获得额外的数学指导。此外，Danus 还包括一个书写系统，一旦目标陈述得到证明，该系统就会将完整的证明转化为纸质的阐述，从而使最终的论证更容易被人类读者理解。我们展示了 Danus 在代数几何、奇点理论和组合学中几个具有挑战性的研究级问题上的有效性，并讨论了事实图记忆机制在产生长、详细和正确的证明中的作用。我们还明确报告了为每个问题提供的人类输入以及数学家在这些示例中如何与 Danus 合作。The interaction between Danus and human mathematicians is also carefully designed. The main agent regularly summarizes the current proof state into a report, allowing human mathematicians to inspect the progress of the proof and provide high-level guidance through the main agent when necessary. The same report can also be used by the main agent to consult advanced systems such as GPT-5.5-pro for additional mathematical guidance. In addition, Danus includes a writing system that turns the completed proof into a paper-style exposition once the target statement has been proved, making the resulting argument more accessible to human readers. We demonstrate the effectiveness of Danus on several challenging research-level problems in algebraic geometry, singularity theory, and combinatorics, and discuss the role of the fact-graph memory mechanism in producing long, detailed, and correct proofs. We also explicitly report the human input provided for each problem and how the mathematicians collaborated with Danus in these examples.

2 方法论Methodology

Danus 是一个用于研究级数学的自动化系统，建立在之前系统 Rethlas 的工人验证者核心之上Danus is an automated system for research-level mathematics, built on the worker–verifier core of the previous system, Rethlas[21]。它将一群证明搜索工作人员和一个无状态验证者与共享内存结合起来，所有这些都由一个主代理进行协调。该设计遵循严格的权力分离：主代理进行全局规划和协调，工作人员进行详细的证明搜索，验证者是正确性的唯一权威，单个事实图保存了每个验证结果，是系统唯一的事实来源（图. It couples a swarm of proof-search workers and a stateless verifier with a shared memory, all coordinated by a main agent. 2.1 工作流程概述Workflow overview

这些部分共同解决一个问题，从最初的陈述到完成的论文。数学家用自然语言向主代理提出一个问题。主代理制定初始计划，在需要时咨询 GPT-5.5-pro 高级数学策略，并向工作人员分配方向。这些工人是雷斯拉斯一代的代理人，他们并行运行，从多个方向探索问题，包括建设性和反驳性的路线。每个工人反复提出可验证的陈述以及支持性证据，并将其提交给验证者；其证明通过验证的声明被存储为These parts work together on a single problem, from the initial statement to a finished paper. A mathematician gives the main agent a problem in natural language. The main agent forms an initial plan, consulting GPT-5.5-pro for high-level mathematical strategy when needed, and assigns directions to the workers. The workers are Rethlas generation agents that run in parallel and explore the problem from several directions, including both constructive and refutational routes. Each worker repeatedly proposes a verifiable statement together with a supporting proof and submits it to the verifier; a statement whose proof passes verification is stored as a事实fact。这些事实形成一个有向无环图 (DAG)，称为. These facts form a directed acyclic graph (DAG), called the事实图fact graph，其边记录逻辑依赖关系。当工作人员继续进行时，主代理会定期读取他们的进度和事实图，总结当前状态，查阅 GPT-5.5-pro，并重新分配工作人员。迭代达到预设轮数后不停止；仅当主代理确认目标陈述或其反驳已作为事实图中已验证的事实出现时，它才会停止。然后，主代理可以将事实图转化为论文供人类专家检查。, whose edges record logical dependencies. As the workers proceed, the main agent periodically reads their progress and the fact graph, summarizes the current state, consults GPT-5.5-pro, and re-assigns the workers. The iteration is not stopped after a preset number of rounds; it stops only when the main agent confirms that the target statement, or its refutation, has appeared as a verified fact in the fact graph. The main agent can then turn the fact graph into a paper for human experts to check.

这种并行探索是与 Rethlas 的主要区别。 Rethlas 一次只进行一条推理，而 Danus 则同时运行多个工作线程，每个工作线程探索问题的不同方面，例如需要证明的不同引理、需要构造的反例或需要研究的玩具示例。这拓宽了探索范围，并使冗长、多步骤的论证变得容易处理。它还提出了事实图旨在解决的问题：让许多工作人员在不互相干扰的情况下为单一证明做出贡献。This parallel exploration is the main departure from Rethlas. Where Rethlas pursues one line of reasoning at a time, Danus runs several workers at once, each exploring a different facet of the problem, such as a different lemma to prove, a counterexample to construct, or a toy example to study. This broadens the exploration and makes long, multi-step arguments tractable. It also raises the problem that the fact graph is designed to solve: letting many workers contribute to a single proof without interfering with one another.

2.2 事实图The fact graph

结构。Structure.事实图是整个系统及其中心设计元素的唯一事实来源。它是一个有向无环图（DAG），其节点是事实，其边记录逻辑依赖关系。事实是一个数学陈述以及经过验证者检查的证明；从一个事实到另一个事实的边缘意味着第二个事实的证明使用第一个事实。工作人员可以利用图中已有的事实：原则上它可以访问整个图，并且实际上它通过搜索来检索相关事实。当工作人员提交新的陈述和证明时，它会记录该证明所依赖的事实的标识符，这些标识符将成为新事实的传入边缘。每个通过验证的语句都会添加到图中，因此该图会不断增长，直到包含目标定理（图The fact graph is the unique source of truth of the whole system and its central design element. It is a directed acyclic graph (DAG) whose nodes are facts and whose edges record logical dependencies. A fact is a mathematical statement together with a verifier-checked proof; an edge from one fact to another means that the proof of the second uses the first. A worker can draw on the facts already in the graph: in principle it has access to the entire graph, and in practice it retrieves the relevant facts by searching it. When a worker submits a new statement and proof, it records the identifiers of the facts that the proof depends on, and these become the new fact’s incoming edges. Every statement that passes verification is added to the graph, which therefore grows until it contains the target theorem (Figure2).

为什么是图表而不是单一的蓝图。Why a graph rather than a single blueprint.这种设计让许多员工能够协作。在 Rethlas 中，整个结果由一个 Markdown 蓝图承载，其中包含所有支持引理、定义和最终定理；工作人员反复修改了这张蓝图，并要求验证者检查并提出修改建议。与单一蓝图相比，事实图有两个优点。首先，它使每个员工的背景保持小而集中。这种上下文管理很重要，因为语言模型代理在仅包含相关材料的简短上下文中进行最可靠的推理；不相关的内容既会限制其能力，也会干扰其推理。单一蓝图迫使每个工作人员携带整个累积的证明，而事实图让每个工作人员仅利用其当前主张所需的事实，并一次提交一个事实，因此即使证明增长到很多页，工作环境仍然很小。其次，它支持并行工作：对于许多工作人员来说，单个文件很难同时编辑或用于单独的攻击线，而事实图可以让他们的贡献累积到一个共享结构中。This design is what lets many workers collaborate. In Rethlas, the whole result was carried by a single Markdown blueprint that held all supporting lemmas, definitions, and the final theorem; a worker repeatedly edited this blueprint and asked the verifier to check it and suggest revisions. Compared with a single blueprint, the fact graph has two advantages. First, it keeps each worker’s context small and focused. Such context management matters because a language-model agent reasons most reliably over a short context holding only relevant material; unrelated content both strains its capacity and interferes with its reasoning. A single blueprint forces every worker to carry the whole accumulated proof, whereas the fact graph lets each worker draw on only the facts it needs for its current claim and submit one fact at a time, so the working context stays small even as the proof grows to many pages. Second, it supports parallel work: a single file is hard for many workers to edit at once or to use for separate lines of attack, while the fact graph lets their contributions accumulate into one shared structure.

撤销。Revocation.事实图还支持撤销：后来发现错误的事实与直接或间接依赖于它的每个事实一起被删除。这种情况发生在以下两种情况之一：引用的参考文献中存在错误（通常由代理在最终审查期间发现，或由人类专家指出），或者更罕见的是概念混淆或有缺陷的证明。在我们的运行中，很少需要撤销，当我们讨论验证的可靠性时我们会回到这一点。The fact graph also supports revocation: a fact that is later found to be wrong is removed together with every fact that depends on it, directly or transitively. This happens in one of two situations: an error in a cited reference (usually found by the agent during a final review, or pointed out by a human expert) or, more rarely, a conceptual confusion or a flawed proof. In our runs, revocation was needed only very rarely, a point we return to when we discuss the reliability of verification.

2.3 超越事实图的记忆Memory beyond the fact graph

已验证的内容存在于事实图中；其他一切值得铭记于心的事情。记忆不是事实的一部分，而是事实的一部分。它是一种共享上下文，可以帮助工作人员避免重复彼此的失败尝试，并且它有两个层次。每个工作人员都保留一个私有的本地内存，即其自身活动的运行日志，主要用于稍后分析一系列推理是如何发展的。上面是一个全局内存，每个worker和主代理都可以读写。全局记忆记录了搜索的中间产品，例如计划、有希望的方向、死胡同以及构造的例子和反例。它还记录了每次GPT-5.5-pro咨询的提示和答复。这两层一起，可以防止每个工作人员重复自己的局部探索，并让主代理看到每个工作人员的搜索分支上发生了什么，从而保留对推理策略和计划的整体掌握。Content that has been verified lives in the fact graph; everything else worth keeping lives in memory. Memory is not part of the truth; it is shared context that helps the workers avoid repeating one another’s failed attempts, and it has two tiers. Each worker keeps a private local memory, a running log of its own activity, used mainly for later analysis of how a line of reasoning developed. Above it is a global memory that every worker and the main agent can read and write. The global memory records the intermediate products of the search, such as plans, promising directions, dead ends, and constructed examples and counterexamples. It also records the prompt and reply of every GPT-5.5-pro consultation. Together, the two tiers keep each worker from repeating its own local exploration and let the main agent see what has happened along each worker’s branch of the search, so that it retains an overall grasp of the reasoning strategy and the plan.

The main agent forms these judgments from its own understanding of the overall structure, its observation of each worker’s progress, and the contents of the global fact graph and memory. For fine points of mathematics it may consult GPT-5.5-pro at low frequency, at most once per hour, as an expert reference. Its strategic decisions therefore rest on a system-wide view of the run, augmented where needed by the mathematical strength of a frontier model.

4.2 写作流畅性与真实性之间的平衡Balance between writing fluency and truth faithfulness

对证明记录的忠实性和可读性是相反的方向，而且张力随着事实图的大小而增加。最忠实的手稿会简单地逐个写出目标定理所依赖的每一个事实；这将是正确的，因为每个事实都已通过验证，并且在我们的运行规模下运行到数百个事实时不可读。相反，一篇可读的论文需要动机、不同于发现顺序的呈现顺序以及常规细节的省略。但这种重写本身就是数学写作，它引入错误的速度远远高于图表本身。在我们的实验中反复出现的典型失败是，一份手稿的结果、结构和路线都是正确的，而书面推理却出了问题，恰恰是几个事实被压缩成一个事实（第 7 节）。Faithfulness to the proof record and readability pull in opposite directions, and the tension grows with the size of the fact graph. The most faithful manuscript would simply write out, one after another, every fact the target theorem depends on; it would be correct, since each fact has passed verification, and unreadable, running to hundreds of facts at the scale of our runs. A readable paper instead needs motivation, an order of presentation different from the order of discovery, and the omission of routine detail. But this rewriting is itself mathematical writing, and it introduces errors at a rate far above the graph’s own. The characteristic failure, seen repeatedly in our experiments, is a manuscript whose results, constructions, and route are all correct while the written reasoning goes wrong exactly where several facts were compressed into one (Sections2.7和and3.6).

补救措施是按顺序追求这两个目标：草稿是为了可读性而写的，完成的手稿必须按书面形式通过验证者。直到它完成为止，它被修改并重新提交，工作人员的提交-验证-修复周期提升到整篇论文，除了这里主要代理面对验证者并且不写入新的事实：每个修订从图中检索现有事实并更准确地呈现它们（章节The remedy is to pursue the two goals in sequence: the draft is written for readability, and the completed manuscript must then pass the verifier as written. Until it does, it is revised and resubmitted, the worker’s submit–verify–repair cycle lifted to the whole paper, except that here the main agent faces the verifier and writes no new facts: each revision retrieves existing facts from the graph and renders them more accurately (Sections2.5和and2.7）。这消除了大多数写作阶段的错误，同时论文仍然是论文，而不是事实记录。). This removes most writing-stage errors while the paper remains a paper, not a fact-by-fact transcript.

剩下的是平衡而不是解决方案：手稿仍然可以过于单薄地解释动机或过于压缩步骤，并且要求更严格将文本推回记录。发布的版本是我们选择的点，残留缺陷很小：Section中记录的人工修改What remains is a balance rather than a resolution: the manuscripts can still explain motivation too thinly or compress steps too far, and demanding more rigor pushes the text back toward the transcript. The released versions are the point we chose, and the residual defects are small: the human revisions recorded in Section3主要是符号和引文格式的调整。were chiefly adjustments of notation and citation format.

4.3 验证的重要性Importance of verification

Danus 从 Rethlas 获取验证器，并故意缩小其角色：一个独立的、无状态的服务，只关心其前面文本的正确性（第 1 节）Danus takes its verifier from Rethlas and deliberately narrows its role: an independent, stateless service concerned with nothing but the correctness of the text in front of it (Section2.5）。隔离是至关重要的：提示模型检查自己的工作只能轻微地抑制幻觉；检查员必须是一个单独的代理人，其背景包含提交内容、引用的事实，仅此而已，并且其技能规定了检查程序。通过这种方式设计，验证者几乎从不接受有缺陷的证明：在我们的实验中，记录中的少数错误在最终的人工审核中被发现（第 1 节）). The isolation is essential: prompting a model to check its own work suppresses hallucination only mildly; the checker must be a separate agent whose context holds the submission, the cited facts, and nothing else, and whose skills prescribe the checking procedure. 4.5 优点和局限性Strengths and limitations

前面的小节各讨论一个问题；为了结束讨论，我们总结了 Danus 的整体能力。 Danus 在我们的实验中表现出了许多优势。首先，验证者的质量令人满意：我们总共只观察到少数验证者错误，其中大部分源于引用的参考文献中的不精确或错误的陈述（第The preceding subsections each pursued a single question; to close the discussion, we summarize Danus’s capabilities as a whole. Danus showed many strengths in our experiments. First, the quality of the verifier was satisfactory: we observed only a handful of verifier errors in total, most of which stemmed from imprecise or erroneous statements in the cited references (Section3.5）。因此，事实图可以可靠地作为系统的事实来源。在此基础上，在主要代理人指导搜索和记忆向前推进其教训的情况下，达努斯扩展了其工作的深度和广度：它将一种复杂的技术带入一个持续综合的新环境中（第1节）). The fact graph could therefore serve reliably as the system’s source of truth. On this foundation, with the main agent directing the search and the memory carrying its lessons forward, Danus extended its work in depth and in width: it carried an intricate technique into a new setting in one sustained synthesis (Section3.1），保持长时间的并行搜索运行，直到目标定理得到验证（第), kept a long parallel search running until the target theorem stood verified (Section3.6），并将问题自发地分解为沿着建设性和反驳路线工作的部分（章节), and decomposed problems unprompted into parts worked along constructive and refutational routes (Sections3.2和and3.3）。在如此广度和长度的工作中，达努斯表现出了显着的数学能力：有时，它在很少或根本没有人工帮助的情况下完成了整个证明，在一种情况下，从问题陈述到完成手稿的整个流程（第 1 节）). In work of this breadth and length, Danus showed notable mathematical capability: at times it completed an entire proof with little or no human assistance, in one case the whole pipeline from problem statement to finished manuscript (Section3.4）；它通过人类专家没有预料到的途径找到了解决方案（章节); it reached solutions by routes the human experts had not anticipated (Sections3.2和and3.5），从远离问题本身的数学分支中提取工具（第), drew tools from branches of mathematics distant from the problem’s own (Section3.3），并且，当提供的参考不是最佳的时，自己寻找更好的参考（第), and, when a supplied reference was suboptimal, sought out a better one on its own (Section3.1）。最后，Danus 展示了一定程度的写作能力：当初稿错误地呈现了关键引理时，验证者拒绝了该草稿，Danus 自动修复了它（第 1 节）). Finally, Danus displayed a measure of writing ability: when a first draft rendered a key lemma incorrectly, the verifier rejected the draft and Danus repaired it automatically (Section3.3）；另一份手稿以同样的方式逐节更正（第); another manuscript was corrected section by section the same way (Section3.6).

Even when the route is found, Danus’s paper writing is not yet fully satisfactory: citation formatting occasionally strays from the conventions of the field, and its notation, though logically consistent, does not always guard against confusion; these imperfections required manual adjustment by the human experts afterwards. 5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 Danus，一个用于研究级数学推理的编排系统，以共享事实图为中心，作为全局内存管理机制。 Danus 将全局规划、并行证明搜索和验证分开：一个主代理协调总体策略，多个工作代理并行探索和证明本地声明，无状态验证者在将提议的声明纳入事实图之前对其进行检查。通过将每个经过验证的事实及其证明和逻辑依赖关系存储在一起，事实图允许 Danus 逐步积累长的数学论证，同时保持共享证明状态的组织和可靠。In this paper, we propose Danus, an orchestration system for research-level mathematical reasoning centered on a shared fact graph as a global memory-management mechanism. Danus separates global planning, parallel proof search, and verification: a main agent coordinates the overall strategy, multiple worker agents explore and prove local claims in parallel, and a stateless verifier checks proposed claims before they are admitted into the fact graph. By storing each verified fact together with its proof and logical dependencies, the fact graph allows Danus to accumulate long mathematical arguments incrementally while keeping the shared proof state organized and reliable.

我们通过代数几何、奇点理论和组合学方面的六个研究级案例研究对 Danus 进行了评估。这些例子表明，Danus 可以构造长而详细的证明，将难题分解为可行的子问题，修复有缺陷的证明依赖关系，并将经过验证的事实图转化为数学手稿。不同案例研究中的人工输入量各不相同，从没有数学指导到少量高级提示或更正，但在所有情况下，Danus 都进行了大量的证明构建。We evaluated Danus through six research-level case studies in algebraic geometry, singularity theory, and combinatorics. These examples show that Danus can construct long and detailed proofs, decompose difficult problems into workable subproblems, repair flawed proof dependencies, and turn verified fact graphs into mathematical manuscripts. The amount of human input varied across the case studies, ranging from no mathematical guidance to a small number of high-level hints or corrections, but in all cases Danus carried out substantial proof construction.

我们的结果表明，基于事实图的编排提供了一种有效的机制，可以将基于 LLM 的数学推理代理扩展到长期研究问题。通过将并行的本地证明搜索转变为有组织且累积的证明构建过程，Danus 能够将来自不同工作人员的许多贡献整合成连贯的数学论证。与此同时，我们的实验表明，人类数学家对于选择有意义的问题、识别缺失的想法以及进行最终的专家评审仍然至关重要。因此，我们将 Danus 视为迈向协作数学研究系统的一步，在该系统中，人工智能代理可以为证明构建做出重大贡献，同时保持与人类数学判断的集成。Our results suggest that fact-graph-based orchestration provides an effective mechanism for extending LLM-based mathematical reasoning agents to long-horizon research problems. By turning parallel local proof search into an organized and cumulative proof-building process, Danus enables many contributions from different workers to be assembled into coherent mathematical arguments. At the same time, our experiments show that human mathematicians remain essential for choosing meaningful problems, identifying missing ideas, and carrying out final expert review. We therefore view Danus as a step toward collaborative mathematical research systems in which AI agents can contribute substantially to proof construction while remaining integrated with human mathematical judgment.
