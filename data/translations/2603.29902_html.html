摘要Abstract

交错的文本和图像生成代表了多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM) 的重要前沿，提供了一种更直观的方式来传达复杂信息。当前的范式依赖于图像生成或检索增强，但它们通常将两者视为相互排斥的路径，未能将事实与创造力统一起来。我们认为该领域的下一个里程碑是Interleaved text-and-image generation represents a significant frontier for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), offering a more intuitive way to convey complex information. Current paradigms rely on either image generation or retrieval augmentation, yet they typically treat the two as mutually exclusive paths, failing to unify factuality with creativity. We argue that the next milestone in this field is代理工具规划Agentic Tool Planning，其中模型充当中央控制器，自主确定何时、何地以及调用哪些工具来为视觉关键查询生成交错响应。为了系统地评估这种范式，我们引入, where the model serves as a central controller that autonomously determines when, where, and which tools to invoke to produce interleaved responses for visual-critical queries. To systematically evaluate this paradigm, we introduceATP-长凳ATP-Bench，一个新颖的基准，包含 8 个类别的 7,702 个 QA 对（包括 1,592 个 VQA 对）和 25 个视觉关键意图，具有经过人类验证的查询和基本事实。此外，为了评估独立于端到端执行和更改工具后端的代理规划，我们提出了一种多代理 MLLM 作为法官（MAM）系统。 MAM 评估工具调用精度，识别错失的工具使用机会，并评估整体响应质量，而无需地面实况参考。我们对 10 个最先进的 MLLM 进行的广泛实验表明，模型在连贯的交错规划方面存在困难，并且在工具使用行为方面表现出显着的变化，这凸显了巨大的改进空间，并为推进交错生成提供了可行的指导。数据集和代码可在, a novel benchmark comprising 7,702 QA pairs (including 1,592 VQA pairs) across eight categories and 25 visual-critical intents, featuring human-verified queries and ground truths. MAM evaluates tool-call precision, identifies missed opportunities for tool use, and assesses overall response quality without requiring ground-truth references. 4.3 MAM：多代理 MLLM 作为法官MAM: Multi-Agent MLLM-as-a-judge

在我们的范式中评估模型性能引入了传统指标无法充分解决的挑战。首先，任务的开放性意味着基本事实可以作为高质量的参考，而不是每个工具调用的明确标准。其次，我们强调评估工具规划而不是端到端执行，从而将模型的内在规划能力与外部 API 约束隔离开来。第三，评估必须是多维度的，既评估工具调用的精度，包括其必要性、正确性和放置位置，又评估在需要此类支持时对错过的视觉元素的回忆。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一个多代理 MLLM 作为法官系统，该系统可以评估工具调用精度、错过的工具使用机会以及整体响应质量，而无需依赖地面事实或完全执行。该框架由三个专门代理组成：精确检查员、召回检查员和首席法官。Evaluating model performance in our paradigm introduces challenges that conventional metrics cannot adequately address. First, the open-ended nature of the tasks means that ground truths function as high-quality references rather than definitive standards for every tool invocation. Second, we emphasize evaluating tool planning rather than end-to-end execution, thereby isolating the model’s intrinsic planning ability from external API constraints. Third, evaluation must be multi-dimensional, assessing both the precision of tool calls, including their necessity, correctness, and placement, and the recall of missed visual elements when such support is required. To address these challenges, we propose a Multi-Agent MLLM-as-a-Judge system that evaluates tool-call precision, missed tool-use opportunities, and overall response quality without relying on ground truth or full execution. The framework comprises three specialized agents: the Precision Inspector, the Recall Inspector, and the Chief Judge.

It first verifies visual-critical necessity, requiring that the image provides clear added value over text, and checks tool boundary compliance to ensure the selected tool matches its technical capabilities. 7 讨论Discussion

这项工作有一些局限性，我们将其留到未来的工作中。 (1) 我们的交错生成设置侧重于文本图像输出，不涵盖音频或视频等更丰富的模式。 (2) 我们的工具包仅限于五个工具，因此无法捕获更广泛的代理功能。 (3) 我们专注于使用 MLLM 作为法官进行直接评估，而不研究替代流程，例如为 LLM 作为法官提供字幕图像。This work has several limitations, which we leave for future work. (1) Our interleaved generation setting focuses on text–image outputs and does not cover richer modalities such as audio or video. (2) Our toolkit is restricted to five tools and therefore does not capture broader agentic capabilities. (3) We focus on direct evaluation using MLLM-as-a-judge, and do not study alternative pipelines such as captioning images for LLM-as-a-judge.
