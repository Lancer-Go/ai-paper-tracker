使用稀疏自动编码器在中微子基础模型中查找和使用可解释的潜在变量Finding and using interpretable latents in a neutrino foundation model with sparse autoencoders

摘要Abstract

我们提出了基于稀疏自动编码器的机械可解释性在粒子物理学中的首次应用。研究在 IceCube 数据上预训练并针对方向重建进行微调的中微子基础模型，我们使用严格的验证协议（包括保留测试、匹配的干扰控制和跨独立字典训练的复制）确定模型表示中经过验证的物理概念图集。因果干预表明，方向头几乎没有在这幅地图上绘制。受这些未充分利用的信息的启发，我们在相同的事件级表示上训练不​​确定性头来预测模型的角度重建误差。与方向头不同，它因果关系取决于图集的质量和亮度特征。在We present a first application of sparse-autoencoder-based mechanistic interpretability to particle physics. Studying a neutrino foundation model pretrained on IceCube data and fine-tuned for direction reconstruction, we identify a validated atlas of physical concepts in the model representation, using a strict validation protocol consisting of held-out tests, matched nuisance controls, and replication across independent dictionary trainings. Causal interventions show that the direction head barely draws on this atlas. Motivated by this underused information, we train an uncertainty head on the same event-level representation to predict the model’s angular reconstruction error. Unlike the direction head, it depends causally on quality and brightness features from the atlas. At𝟐𝟎%20\%选择效率，这个可解释的估计器提高了中值角分辨率selection efficiency, this interpretable estimator improves the median angular resolution from20.2∘20.2^{\circ} to 3.2∘3.2^{\circ}。这些结果表明，机械可解释性可以揭示模型内部表示中编码的学习潜在物理现象，并帮助设计利用它的下游任务。. These results suggest that mechanistic interpretability can reveal learned latent physics encoded within a model’s internal representation and help design downstream tasks that exploit it.

1 简介Introduction

现代中微子望远镜根据大型、不规则探测器体积中记录的稀疏切伦科夫光重建粒子特性Modern neutrino telescopes reconstruct particle properties from sparse Cherenkov light recorded in large, irregular detector volumes[24, 2]。在 IceCube 中，每个事件都是嵌入南极冰中的数字光学模块 (DOM) 上的一组可变长度的光电倍增器脉冲. In IceCube, each event is a variable-length set of photomultiplier pulses on digital optical modules (DOMs) embedded in Antarctic ice[3, 1]。方向重建取决于可见光图案，还取决于探测器的几何形状、冰特性、噪声和触发条件。随着基于机器学习的重建变得更具表现力，携带这些效应的学习表示本身就成为物理学感兴趣的对象。. Direction reconstruction depends on the visible light pattern, but also on detector geometry, ice properties, noise and trigger conditions. As machine-learning-based reconstructions become more expressive, the learned representation that carries these effects becomes an object of physics interest in its own right.

基础模型非常适合这个领域。他们使用来自广泛探测器数据的共享表示，并将其耦合到特定于任务的头。这可以减少特定于任务的训练成本并提高统计效率，因为各种下游任务可以以不同的方式读取相同的表示。这一趋势反映了机器学习中扩展和预训练的更广泛成功Foundation models are a natural fit in this domain. They use a shared representation from broad detector data and couple it to task-specific heads. This can reduce task-specific training cost and improve statistical efficiency, as various downstream tasks can read the same representation differently. This trend mirrors the broader success of scaling and pretraining in machine learning[22, 9]，现在通过掩蔽粒子建模、预训练、联合微调和中微子特定基础模型在粒子物理学中崭露头角, and is now emerging in particle physics through masked-particle modeling, pretraining, joint fine-tuning, and neutrino-specific foundation models[25, 20, 37, 35, 38]。因此，基础模型提出了一个具体的可解释性问题：哪些结构被编码在共享表示中，哪些结构被给定的头使用？. Foundation models thus raise a concrete interpretability problem: which structures are encoded in the shared representation, and which of them are used by a given head?

标准事后解释本身并不能回答这个问题。显着图、集成梯度、类似 Grad-CAM 的方法和更广泛的可解释人工智能工具为输入或中间量分配重要性分数Standard post-hoc explanations alone do not answer this problem. Saliency maps, integrated gradients, Grad-CAM-like methods, and broader explainable-AI tools assign importance scores to inputs or intermediate quantities[33, 34, 31, 30]。虽然这些解释很有用，但它们通常是局部的、近似的且依赖于方法，并且不会暴露表示中潜在的物理概念。在粒子物理学中，最近的工作使用归因方法、探针、路径修补、沙普利值和符号回归将学习的表示与射流子结构可观测值连接起来. While these explanations are useful, they are often local, approximate, and method-dependent, and do not expose the physical concepts latent within a representation. In particle physics, recent work has connected learned representations to jet-substructure observables using attribution methods, probes, path patching, Shapley values, and symbolic regression[36, 27]。为了从特征归因方法转向理解模型的内部表示，我们转向机械可解释性. To move from feature attribution methods to understanding the model’s internal representation, we turn to mechanistic interpretability[14, 32, 29]。对决定模型输出的内部变量和电路的研究提出了有关中微子重建的两个问题。 机械可解释性能否恢复中微子基础模型内部表示中的概念？如果是，哪些功能可以被命名、测试和干预？. The investigation of the internal variables and circuits that determine the model’s output raises two questions with regard to neutrino reconstruction. Can mechanistic interpretability recover concepts within the internal representation of neutrino foundation models? If yes, which features can be named, tested, and intervened on?

我们使用稀疏自动编码器（SAE）研究这些问题。 SAE 学习激活空间中的超完备方向字典。单个神经元可以同时贡献多个可解释的概念，其表示是叠加的We study these questions with sparse autoencoders (SAEs). An SAE learns an overcomplete dictionary of directions in activation space. A single neuron can contribute to several interpretable concepts at the same time, its representations are superposed[18, 11]。 SAE 通过仅从几个学习方向重建每个激活来解决这个问题，其目的是分离在原始神经元基础中重叠的概念. An SAE addresses this problem by reconstructing each activation from only a few learned directions, which aim to separate concepts that overlap in the original neuron basis[16, 19, 13].

SAE 的主要批评是稀疏特征不能自动解释，而可解释的特征也不能自动因果关系。最近的研究表明，学习到的特征可能取决于训练运行，在标记概念上表现不佳，或者无法充当可靠的转向方向，即使它们看起来可以解释The main criticism of SAEs is that a sparse feature is not automatically interpretable, and an interpretable feature is not automatically causal. Recent studies have shown that learned features can depend on the training run, underperform linear probes on labelled concepts, or fail to act as reliable steering directions even when they appear interpretable[21, 28, 26]。为了解决这一差距，端到端稀疏字典学习保留了下游行为，而不是单独的激活结构. To address this gap, end-to-end sparse dictionary learning preserves downstream behavior rather than activation structure alone[10]。 因此，我们将 SAE 的可解释性视为必须经过严格验证的假设。候选特征必须能够经受住测试、匹配对照、字典变化和干预。. We therefore treat SAE interpretability as a hypothesis that must be rigorously validated. Candidate features must survive held-out tests, matched controls, dictionary variation, and interventions.

我们开发了这样的验证协议并将其应用于 PolarBERT，这是一种类似 BERT 的基础模型，在 IceCube 脉冲序列上进行训练，并针对方向重建进行了微调We develop such a validation protocol and apply it on PolarBERT, a BERT-like foundation model trained on IceCube pulse sequences and fine-tuned for direction reconstruction[35, 17]。公共蒙特卡罗设置提供真实方向、探测器级辅助标志和每个事件的角度误差. The public Monte Carlo setting provides true direction, detector-level auxiliary flag, and per-event angular errors[15]，实现严格的假设检验。由于稀疏字典是随机对象，因此我们在独立的 SAE 训练种子、字典目标和模型层上测试我们的结论。据我们所知，这是基于 SAE 的机械可解释性首次应用于粒子物理基础模型。, enabling rigorous hypothesis testing. Because sparse dictionaries are stochastic objects, we test our verdict across independent SAE training seeds, dictionary objectives, and model layers. To our knowledge, this is the first application of SAE-based mechanistic interpretability to a particle-physics foundation model.

我们展示了 PolarBERT 的冻结表示包含丰富的物理概念图集，包括探测器质量、辅助活动、事件亮度和探测器深度。We show that PolarBERT’s frozen representations contain a rich atlas of physical concepts including detector quality, auxiliary activity, event brightness, and detector depth.

然而，下游方向的头几乎没有使用这个图集。 删除或覆盖已识别的特征，无论是单独的还是在族中，都会使预测的方向基本保持不变。相反，一个没有简单物理名称的稳定轴主导着聚合雅可比灵敏度，尽管局部灵敏度在不同事件之间变化很大。这表明共享表示包含方向目标未充分利用的大量物理信息。Yet, the downstream direction head uses almost none of this atlas. Instead, one stable axis with no simple physics name dominates the aggregated Jacobian sensitivity, although local sensitivity varies substantially between events. This suggests that the shared representation contains substantial physical information that the direction objective leaves underused.

The interventions show that this head causally uses physically interpretable information in the representation.

5 展望Outlook

据我们所知，我们首次将基于稀疏自动编码器的机械可解释性应用于粒子物理学的基础模型。我们询问内部表示哪些物理信息以及下游头使用哪些部分。这两个问题有不同的答案。 PolarBERT 在各个层中包含经过验证的事件质量、辅助活动、亮度和探测器深度的读数。方向头显示出对我们训练的字典（包括功能训练的字典）中经过验证的物理读数的边际因果依赖性。相反，它的总体敏感性由稳定的因果轴主导，没有简单的物理解释。模型明显代表的内容和指导任务使用的内容几乎是脱节的。To our knowledge, we apply sparse-autoencoder-based mechanistic interpretability to a foundation model in particle physics for the first time. We ask what physical information is represented internally and which parts are used by downstream heads. The two questions have different answers. Across its layers, PolarBERT contains validated read-outs of event quality, auxiliary activity, brightness, and detector depth. The direction head shows marginal causal dependence on the validated physical read-outs across the dictionaries we trained, including functionally trained dictionaries. Its aggregate sensitivity is instead dominated by a stable causal axis with no simple physical interpretation. What the model demonstrably represents and what the direction task uses are almost disjoint.
