摘要。Abstract.

1. 简介Introduction

金融市场是动态系统，其统计特性随着时间的推移而变化，以响应不断变化的宏观经济条件、投资者行为、流动性、政策干预和意外外部事件。这些变化可能表现为回报分布、波动性、相关结构、交易活动或其他市场特征的变化Financial markets are dynamic systems whose statistical properties evolve over time in response to changing macroeconomic conditions, investor behavior, liquidity, policy interventions, and unexpected external events. These changes can manifest as shifts in return distributions, volatility, correlation structures, trading activity, or other market characteristics(4)。识别此类结构转变对于风险管理、投资组合配置、欺诈和市场操纵检测以及定量决策非常重要。变化点检测提供了一种原则性方法来定位数据生成过程发生变化的时刻，使分析师能够将金融时间序列分割成统计上不同的状态并识别潜在的重大市场事件. Identifying such structural transitions is important for risk management, portfolio allocation, fraud and market-manipulation detection, and quantitative decision-making. Change-point detection provides a principled approach for locating moments at which the data-generating process changes, enabling analysts to segment financial time series into statistically distinct regimes and identify potentially significant market events(3).

尽管在变点检测方面取得了实质性进展Despite substantial progress in change-point detection(17; 30; 21; 12)，其在金融数据中的应用仍然具有挑战性。 金融时间序列通常是嘈杂的、非平稳的、重尾的，并且具有随时间变化的波动性和依赖性结构的特征。结构性变化的性质和幅度也可能因资产、抽样频率和市场制度的不同而有很大差异。 因此，没有一种单一的变化点检测算法在所有条件下都能始终表现良好, its application to financial data remains challenging. Financial time series are typically noisy, non-stationary, heavy-tailed, and characterized by time-varying volatility and dependence structures. The nature and magnitude of structural changes might also vary considerably across assets, sampling frequencies, and market regimes. Consequently, no single change-point detection algorithm performs consistently well under all conditions(29)。对单变量序列中的突变均值漂移有效的方法可能不适合多变量数据中的逐渐协方差变化，而在综合基准上表现良好的算法在应用于现实世界的金融观察时可能会大幅退化。. A method effective for abrupt mean shifts in a univariate series may be unsuitable for gradual covariance changes in multivariate data, while an algorithm that performs well on synthetic benchmarks may degrade substantially when applied to real-world financial observations.

因此，选择合适的检测管道需要在多个层面做出决策，包括算法的选择、信号表示、预处理过程、超参数、检测阈值和后处理规则。这些决定往往是相互依赖的。例如，基于内核的检测器的有用性可能取决于输入的缩放和维数，而概率方法的性能可能对有关噪声分布或状态持续时间的假设敏感。在传统的工作流程中，这些选择是通过领域专业知识、重复实验和广泛的参数调整手动做出的，模型性能也对底层数据的质量敏感Selecting an appropriate detection pipeline therefore requires decisions at several levels, including the choice of algorithm, signal representation, preprocessing procedure, hyperparameters, detection thresholds, and post-processing rules. These decisions are often interdependent. For example, the usefulness of a kernel-based detector may depend on the scaling and dimensionality of the input, while the performance of a probabilistic method may be sensitive to assumptions about noise distributions or regime duration. In conventional workflows, these choices are made manually through domain expertise, repeated experimentation, and extensive parameter tuning, with model performance also being sensitive to the quality of the underlying data(22)。此类工作流程很难在大量资产和数据集上进行扩展，并且当观察到的市场的统计特征发生变化时，它们可能无法有效地适应。. Such workflows are difficult to scale across large collections of assets and datasets, and they may fail to adapt efficiently when the statistical characteristics of the observed market change.

2. 相关工作Related Work

2.1. 变化点检测Change Point Detection

变化点检测方法从根本上不同，因为它们假设在一个政权内是稳定的。假设的选择很大程度上决定了可以有效检测哪些类型的结构变化，从而决定一种方法最适合哪些应用场景Change-point detection methods differ fundamentally in what they assume to be stable within a regime. The choice of assumption largely determines which types of structural changes can be detected effectively and, consequently, which application scenarios a method is best suited for(29)。经典统计方法假设每个分段内的观测值共享恒定的分布参数，例如均值、方差或似然度，并通过最佳分段来识别变化点. Classical statistical methods assume that observations within each segment share constant distributional parameters, such as the mean, variance, or likelihood, and identify change points through optimal segmentation(19; 27; 18)或贝叶斯推理or Bayesian inference(13)。相反，更一般的非参数方法假设整个数据分布在一个范围内保持不变，使用最大平均差异等措施来检测变化. More general nonparametric approaches instead assume that the entire data distribution remains unchanged within a regime, detecting changes using measures such as maximum mean discrepancy(14)或基于集合的统计，为多元数据提供更大的灵活性or ensemble-based statistics, enabling greater flexibility for multivariate data(21)。对于高维和复杂的时间序列，最近的深度学习方法假设政权变化在学习的潜在表示中比在原始观察空间中更容易区分，KL-CPD 等方法利用表示学习来捕获微妙的结构变化. For high-dimensional and complex time series, recent deep learning methods assume that regime changes are more distinguishable in a learned latent representation than in the original observation space, with approaches such as KL-CPD leveraging representation learning to capture subtle structural changes(11)。其他方法通过频谱特性来表征状态，检测在时域中可能不明显的频域行为的变化. Other methods characterize regimes through spectral properties, detecting changes in frequency-domain behavior that may not be evident in the time domain(26).

4.2. 轨迹引导进化Trajectory-guided Evolution

在每个优化步骤开始时，代理都会检索现有轨迹以及几个最近的相关轨迹。这些轨迹和现有的可执行脚本构成了规划上下文。根据优化状态，代理选择三个轨迹级运算符之一（修订、替代策略或重组），并使用当前上下文和进化证据生成下一个可执行实验计划。详细的控制流程在算法中给出At the beginning of each optimization step, the agent retrieves the incumbent trajectory together with several recent relevant trajectories. These trajectories and the incumbent executable script form the planning context. Depending on the optimization state, the agent selects one of three trajectory-level operators—Revision, Alternative Strategy, or Recombination—and generates the next executable experiment plan using the current context and evolutionary evidence. The detailed control flow is given in Algorithm1.

修订。Revision.在普通优化期间，现有轨迹充当下一个实验的父轨迹。该代理会对现有脚本以及最近的成功轨迹进行反思，以准确地生成一个可执行修改。修改可能会改变时间序列表示、探测器配置、超参数或后处理程序，同时保留总体实验目标。During ordinary optimization, the incumbent trajectory serves as the parent of the next experiment. The agent reflects on the incumbent script together with recent successful trajectories to produce exactly one executable modification. Revisions may alter the time-series representation, detector configuration, hyperparameters, or post-processing procedure, while preserving the overall experimental objective.

替代策略。Alternative Strategy.每次修订后，代理都会将最终的验证分数与现任者的验证分数进行比较。当改进小于预定义阈值（或者实验未能产生有效分数）时，修订将被标记为停滞。在下一个非最终迭代中，代理执行替代策略而不是另一个修订。停滞的轨迹被视为负面证据，阻止重复相同的搜索方向，而现有脚本仍然作为实施的起点。这鼓励探索正交建模选择或以前未尝试过的 EDA 推荐模型。After each revision, the agent compares the resulting validation score with that of the incumbent. When the improvement is smaller than a predefined threshold (or the experiment fails to produce a valid score), the revision is marked as stagnant. At the next non-final iteration, the agent performs Alternative Strategy instead of another Revision. The stagnant trajectory is treated as negative evidence that discourages repeating the same search direction, while the incumbent script remains as the starting point for implementation. This encourages exploration of orthogonal modeling choices or previously untried EDA-recommended models.

重组。Recombination.在最终的优化迭代中，重组取代了修订和替代策略。该代理不是扩展单个轨迹，而是从多个高性能轨迹合成互补组件，同时保留现有脚本作为可执行起点。这使得最终的实验能够整合在整个搜索过程中发现的成功想法。During the final optimization iteration, Recombination replaces both Revision and Alternative Strategy. Rather than extending a single trajectory, the agent synthesizes complementary components from multiple high-performing trajectories while retaining the incumbent script as the executable starting point. This allows the final experiment to integrate successful ideas discovered throughout the search.

现任选拔。Incumbent Selection.每个生成的实验都会在验证集上执行和评估。仅当新实验产生严格优于当前现有项的有效验证分数时，现有项才会更新。否则，现任者将被保留。接受和停滞是有意独立的：接受一个小的积极改进，因为它改善了现有的，但如果改进没有超过有意义的改进阈值，它仍然被标记为停滞。因此，改进的实验成为新的现有实验，同时在接下来的非最终迭代中触发替代策略。Every generated experiment is executed and evaluated on the validation set. The incumbent is updated only when the new experiment produces a valid validation score that is strictly better than the current incumbent. Otherwise, the incumbent is preserved. Acceptance and stagnation are intentionally independent: a small positive improvement is accepted because it improves the incumbent, yet it is still marked as stagnant if the improvement does not exceed the meaningful-improvement threshold. Consequently, the improved experiment becomes the new incumbent while simultaneously triggering Alternative Strategy in the following non-final iteration.

6. 结论与限制Conclusion & Limitation

我们推出了 EvoTS-Agent，这是一种用于金融时间序列变化点检测的自我进化 LLM 代理。与反复完善单个解决方案或遵循固定工作流程的传统 LLM 代理不同，EvoTS-Agent 通过维护可执行的实验轨迹并根据经验反馈调整其搜索策略来执行验证引导的轨迹演化。通过精心策划的探索性数据分析、多样化的变化点检测模型库和验证引导的轨迹演化，代理自动选择、细化和综合检测管道。对四个基准数据集的实验表明，EvoTS-Agent 在多个骨干 LLM 中始终如一地实现强大的变化点检测性能，同时保持 100% 的执行成功率。进一步的分析表明，增益不仅来自于对不同检测算法的访问，还来自于针对每个数据集的统计特征自适应选择和发展最合适的检测管道的能力。We presented EvoTS-Agent, a self-evolving LLM agent for financial time-series change-point detection. Unlike conventional LLM agents that repeatedly refine a single solution or follow a fixed workflow, EvoTS-Agent performs validation-guided trajectory evolution by maintaining executable experiment trajectories and adapting its search strategy according to empirical feedback. Through curated exploratory data analysis, a diverse change-point detection model bank, and validation-guided trajectory evolution, the agent automatically selects, refines, and synthesizes detection pipelines. Experiments on four benchmark datasets demonstrate that EvoTS-Agent consistently achieves strong change-point detection performance across multiple backbone LLMs while maintaining a 100% execution success rate. Further analysis shows that the gain arises not only from access to diverse detection algorithms, but also from the ability to adaptively select and evolve the most appropriate detection pipeline for the statistical characteristics of each dataset.

EvoTS-Agent 的局限性之一是它对标记验证数据的依赖。虽然底层的变化点检测器是无监督的，但代理需要验证注释来确定在进化过程中应保留哪些实验轨迹。在实际部署中，获取此类标签可能需要专家注释或历史真实事件。因此，开发可靠的标签高效或无标签优化策略是未来研究的重要途径。One limitation of EvoTS-Agent is its dependence on labeled validation data. While the underlying change-point detectors are unsupervised, the agent requires validation annotations to determine which experimental trajectories should be retained during evolution. In practical deployments, obtaining such labels may require expert annotation or historical ground-truth events. Developing reliable label-efficient or label-free optimization strategies is therefore an important avenue for future research.
