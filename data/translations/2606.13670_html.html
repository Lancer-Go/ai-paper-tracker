使用大型语言模型对社会和行为科学进行自动再现性评估Automated reproducibility assessments in the social and behavioral sciences using large language models

摘要Abstract

社会和行为科学的可重复性通常由独立研究人员评估，他们重新分析原始数据，以评估已发表的研究结果是否可以恢复。然而，这种方法是资源密集型的并且难以扩展。在这里，我们展示了大型语言模型（LLM）可以自动化再现性评估。使用Reproducibility in the social and behavioral sciences is typically evaluated by independent researchers who reanalyze the original data to assess whether the published findings can be recovered. However, such approaches are resource-intensive and difficult to scale. Here, we show that large language models (LLMs) can automate reproducibility assessments. For 7 studies, the LLM could not produce a viable effect size estimate. For the remaining studies, our LLM pipeline recovered the original effect sizes in 41% of studies using a±\pmCohen 公差为 0.050.05 tolerance in Cohen’sdd。此外，我们的法学硕士管道在 96% 的情况下得出了与原始研究相同的定性结论，其中结论表明重新分析是否支持原始主张。相比之下，人类重新分析人员在 34% 的研究中恢复了原始效应大小，并在 74% 的案例中得出了相同的定性结论。总之，这些结果表明法学硕士可以作为自动再现性评估的可扩展工具，并为社会科学和行为科学的实证结果的系统审核提供基础。. Further, our LLM pipeline reached the same qualitative conclusion as the original study in 96% of cases, where conclusions indicate whether the reanalysis supports the original claim. For comparison, human reanalysts recovered the original effect sizes in 34% of studies and reached the same qualitative conclusion in 74% of cases. Together, these results show that LLMs can serve as a scalable tool for automated reproducibility assessment and provide a foundation for systematic auditing of empirical results in the social and behavioral sciences.

主要Main

科学进步取决于可重复的发现。然而，在社会科学和行为科学领域，对原始数据的重新分析表明，发表的结果并不总是能够恢复Scientific progress depends on reproducible findings. Yet, across the social and behavioral sciences, reanalyses of original data have shown that published results are not always recovered[1, 2, 3]。这些模式使得人们很难知道哪些已发表的研究结果是可信的，从而激发了大规模的努力来重新分析已发表的研究并检查其中心研究结果是否可以从原始数据中重现。理想情况下，期刊应该在出版前重新分析提交的内容，以便及早发现不可重复的结果，有些期刊已经开始这样做. These patterns make it difficult to know which published findings can be trusted, motivating large-scale efforts to reanalyze published studies and check whether their central findings can be reproduced from the original data. Ideally, journals would reanalyze submissions before publication to catch irreproducible results early, and some have started to do so[4].

然而，重新分析已发表的研究很难扩展，因为它需要对原始经验工作流程进行耗时的重建However, reanalyzing published studies is difficult to scale because it requires a time-intensive reconstruction of the original empirical workflow[2, 1, 5]。与复制工作不同，复制工作测试结果是否适用于新收集的数据. Unlike replication efforts, which test whether a finding holds in newly collected data[5, 6]，再现性涉及是否可以从原始数据和研究材料中恢复报告的结果, reproducibility concerns whether a reported result can be recovered from the original data and study materials[6].111我们在这个广泛的意义上使用“可再现性”，涵盖紧密的重新实现和声明级别的稳健性，并且我们的设计改变了模型接收的原始方法的多少。两端对分析师提出了不同的要求，并意味着不同的基准：紧密再现的原始报告结果，以及稳健性的可防御重新分析的分布。We use “reproducibility” throughout in this broad sense, spanning both close reimplementation and claim-level robustness, and our design varies how much of the original methodology the model receives. The two ends place different demands on the analyst and imply different benchmarks: the original reported result for close reproduction, and the distribution of defensible reanalyses for robustness.为此，分析师必须检查研究材料，识别相关文件和变量，准备数据，将论文的描述转换为可执行代码，运行分析，并将结果统计数据与原始发现进行比较。最近的 Multi100 合作，其中 507 名分析师重新分析了多年来发表的 100 项经济学、政治学和心理学研究For this, analysts must inspect the study materials, identify the relevant files and variables, prepare the data, translate the paper’s description into executable code, run the analysis, and compare the resulting statistic with the original finding. The recent Multi100 collaboration, wherein 507 analysts reanalyzed 100 published studies in economics, political science, and psychology over several years[2]，说明了这一努力的规模。总体而言，可重复性评估是资源密集型且针对特定研究的，这使得对实证文献的系统审核成本高昂且难以规模化。, illustrates the scale of this effort. Overall, reproducibility assessments are resource-intensive and study-specific, which makes systematic auditing of the empirical literature costly and difficult to scale.

使可重复性评估可扩展的一种方法是自动化重新分析本身。大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展产生了具有强大编码能力的系统One way to make reproducibility assessments scalable is to automate the reanalysis itself. Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have produced systems with strong coding capabilities[7]以及在科学规划、数据分析和研究援助中的日益广泛使用and growing use in scientific programming, data analysis, and research assistance[8, 9, 10]。在这里，我们建议使用法学硕士作为自动化分析师来进行再现性评估。原则上，此类系统可以处理研究材料并生成可执行的分析代码，这可以减少目前某些期刊的数据和再现性检查所需的大量人工工作. Here, we propose using LLMs as automated analysts for reproducibility assessment. In principle, such systems can process study materials and generate executable analysis code, which could reduce the extensive manual effort currently required for data and reproducibility checks at some journals[4]并使此类检查在更广泛的出版渠道中可行，作为常规质量控制的一部分。然而，基于法学硕士的重新分析具有挑战性，因为生成的代码可能包含错误，目前尚不清楚法学硕士能够如何准确地解释研究材料所暗示的分析决策，并且输出可能反映关于数据的不受支持的假设and make such checks feasible across a broader range of publication outlets as part of routine quality control. However, LLM-based reanalysis is challenging, in that the generated code may contain errors, it is unclear how accurately LLMs can interpret the analytical decisions implied by the study materials, and outputs may reflect unsupported assumptions about the data[11]。因此，法学硕士是否能够可靠地进行计算再现性评估尚不清楚。. Hence, whether LLMs can reliably carry out the computational reproducibility assessments is unknown.

We develop an agentic LLM workflow in which the model receives the original dataset, a focal statistical claim, and experimentally varied contextual information from the article, and is tasked to independently write and execute statistical code to reproduce the claims. Across a preregistered corpus of published empirical studies from psychology, political science, and economics, we address the following research question: To what extent can LLM-generated analyses reproduce the statistical results and substantive conclusions of published studies? To this end, we compare standardized effect sizes (Cohen’sdd）与原始发表的研究结果相结合，并分别评估法学硕士是否得出相同的实质性结论。对于一部分论文，我们还将法学硕士生成的效果大小与大规模人体再分析工作的效果大小进行了比较) with the original published findings and, separately, assess whether the LLM recovers the same substantive conclusion. For a subset of papers, we additionally compare the LLM-generated effect sizes against those from a large-scale human reanalysis effort[2]。对于我们的主要分析，我们使用 Claude Opus 4.7 作为最先进的 LLM 来生成代码。. 讨论Discussion

法学硕士可以重现社会和行为科学领域已发表的大部分研究结果。 LLM 生成的分析在 41% 的研究中与原始效应大小相符，在 96% 的研究中得出实质性结论，与 Multi100 中的人类重新分析相当（34% 和 74%；LLMs can reproduce a substantial share of published findings in the social and behavioral sciences. LLM-generated analyses matched the original effect size in 41% of studies and the substantive conclusion in 96%, comparable to the human reanalyses in Multi100 (34% and 74%;[2]）。这种差异有两个可能的来源，我们的设计并未完全分开。它可能反映了原始理论或方法中概念上的模糊性). Such divergence has two possible sources that our design does not fully separate. It may reflect conceptual ambiguity in the original theories or methods[12]，或者它可能反映模型分析中的错误。研究假设通常是简短的口头陈述，不能唯一地确定分析规范，并且这种声明的不规范, or it may reflect errors in the model’s analysis. Research hypotheses are typically short verbal statements that do not uniquely determine an analytical specification, and this underspecification of claims[13]是合法分析变异的合理来源。将这种变化与模型误差区分开来本身就是自动重新分析的一个核心挑战。is a plausible source of legitimate analytical variation. Distinguishing such variation from model error is itself a central challenge for automated reanalysis.

实际上，自动重新分析可以减轻期刊日益需要的数据质量和可重复性检查的负担，并将此类检查扩展到无法进行手动审核的渠道。基于法学硕士的再分析不是取代专家判断，而是可以充当可扩展的首过筛选，标记需要在出版之前或之后进行更仔细审查的主张，并可以支持对已发表文献进行系统稳健性审核Practically, automated reanalysis could reduce the burden of the data-quality and reproducibility checks that journals increasingly require, and extend such checks to outlets where manual review is infeasible. Rather than replacing expert judgment, LLM-based reanalysis could serve as a scalable first-pass screen that flags claims that warrant closer scrutiny before or after publication, and could support systematic robustness audits across the published literature[14]。 此用例有两个注意事项。首先，如果可重复性筛选成为提交要求，作者可能会优化稿件以通过检查，而不是改进基础分析。其次，筛选错误的成本是不对称的：错误地标记为不可重复的结果对作者造成了几乎没有追索权的伤害。如果自动筛选不是一种把关机制，而是一种有效的工具，可以在不需要进一步人工努力的情况下检查结果是否重现，从而减少期刊的审稿负担，而不会对作者造成惩罚，那么这两种风险都会减少。这将自动重新分析置于更广泛的科学透明度和开放性运动中. Two caveats apply to this use case. First, if reproducibility screening becomes a submission requirement, authors may optimize manuscripts to pass the check rather than to improve the underlying analysis. Second, the costs of screening errors are asymmetric: a result incorrectly flagged as non-reproducible harms authors with little recourse. Both risks are reduced if automated screening is framed not as a gatekeeping mechanism but as an efficient tool to check whether results reproduce where little further human effort is necessary, reducing the review burden for journals without penalizing authors. This positions automated reanalysis within the broader movement toward transparency and openness in science[15]，为迄今为止主要依赖手动、劳动密集型方法的工具包添加了可扩展的工具。, adding a scalable instrument to a toolkit that has so far relied largely on manual, labor-intensive approaches.

这些结果存在一些限制。首先，一个关键问题是训练数据污染。由于我们语料库中的研究是在模型训练截止之前发布的，法学硕士可能在预训练期间遇到了其中一些研究，这可能会夸大明显的可重复性。一个相关的问题适用于人体再分析基准。其次，LLM 生成的代码可能包含错误。我们的语料库涵盖 2009 年至 2018 年间发表的三个学科的研究，并且仅包含可访问原始数据的论文。由于数据有限或缺失，一些研究无法进行分析，这一限制广泛影响再现性审核Several limitations qualify these results. First, a key concern is training data contamination. Because the studies in our corpus were published before the model’s training cutoff, LLMs may have encountered some of them during pretraining, which could inflate apparent reproducibility. A related concern applies to the human-reanalysis benchmark. Second, LLM-generated code can contain errors. Our corpus covers studies from three disciplines published between 2009 and 2018, and includes only papers for which original data were accessible. Some studies could not be analyzed due to restricted or missing data, a constraint that affects reproducibility audits broadly[16]。这些选择限制意味着我们的结果可能无法推广到其他学科、更新的文献或数据可用性较低的研究。第三，比较异质分析的结果需要一个共同的指标。我们将报告的估计值转化为标准化效应量（科恩的. These selection constraints mean that our results may not generalize to other disciplines, more recent literature, or studies with lower data availability. Third, comparing results across heterogeneous analyses requires a common metric. We transformed reported estimates into a standardized effect size (Cohen’sdd）。此类转换所依据的假设并不适用于所有分析设置). Such transformations rest on assumptions that do not hold equally across all analytical settings[17, 18, 19, 20]。科恩的. Cohen’sdd因此，以牺牲一定的精度为代价提供了可比性。最后，自动重新分析评估是否可以从原始数据中恢复结果。然而，它并没有测试新收集的数据中的发现是否成立。再现性和复制性是相辅相成的。确认结果在计算上是可重现的并不能证明潜在的效果具有普遍性，这就是为什么使用新收集的数据进行复制仍然至关重要thus offers comparability at the cost of some precision. Finally, automated reanalysis assesses whether a result can be recovered from the original data. However, it does not test whether a finding holds in newly collected data. Reproducibility and replication are complementary. Confirming that a result is computationally reproducible does not establish that the underlying effect generalizes, which is why replication with newly collected data remains essential[21].

总之，我们的研究结果表明，法学硕士可以使再现性评估更具可扩展性和系统性，将其从偶尔的、资源密集型的任务转变为科学质量控制的常规组成部分。除了检查个别结果之外，同样的方法还可以使整个文献中的分析不确定性变得可见，显示出报告的发现在多大程度上取决于其背后的选择。认识到这一点需要人工监督来验证分析选择并解释边界案例，但在文献规模上审核实证主张现在变得可行。In sum, our findings indicate that LLMs can make reproducibility assessment more scalable and systematic, moving it from an occasional, resource-intensive undertaking towards a routine component of scientific quality control. Beyond checking individual results, the same approach can make analytical uncertainty visible across the literature, showing how far a reported finding depends on the choices behind it. Realizing this will require human oversight to verify analytical choices and interpret borderline cases, but auditing empirical claims at the scale of the literature is now becoming feasible.

LLM分析流程LLM analysis pipeline

我们将分析管道实现为 LLM 代理，对于每项研究，它都会接收目标声明、原始数据集和指定的论文变体，并被要求在隔离的沙箱中编写和执行统计代码以测试声明。代理无法访问论文的原始分析代码，而是独立编写和执行代码。基本提示将代理扮演为统计分析师，并要求其致力于对目标声明进行合理的分析。明确指示代理人不要复制论文中报告的任何测试统计数据；提交的统计数据必须来自代理对所提供的数据执行的代码。沙箱是为每次运行全新创建的，运行之间不会保留任何状态，因此一次运行的文件、已安装的软件包和中间结果不会影响后续运行。代理提交一份结构化报告，其中包含计算的测试统计量、样本大小、自由度和定性结论（支持/反对/不确定）。完整提示见附录We implemented the analysis pipeline as an LLM agent that, for each study, receives the target claim, the original dataset, and the assigned paper variant, and is asked to write and execute statistical code in an isolated sandbox to test the claim. The agent does not have access to the original analysis code of the paper, but instead writes and executes code independently. The base prompt casts the agent as a statistical analyst and asks it to commit to one reasonable analysis of the target claim. The agent is explicitly instructed not to copy any test statistic reported in the paper; the submitted statistic must come from code the agent executes on the provided data. Sandboxes are created fresh for each run, with no state carried over between runs, so that files, installed packages, and intermediate results from one run cannot influence subsequent runs. The agent submits a structured report containing the computed test statistic, sample size, degrees of freedom, and a qualitative conclusion (supports / opposite / inconclusive). The full prompt in AppendixA。我们的报告遵循 GUIDE-LLM 检查表. Our reporting follows the GUIDE-LLM checklist[22]。每项研究均通过 5 次独立运行进行分析。. Each study is analyzed across 5 independent runs.
