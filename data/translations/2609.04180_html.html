9 讨论与结论Discussion & Conclusion

辅助视图。Auxiliary Views.据我们所知，我们是第一个分离出辅助视图意义的人。前提很直观：数据增强可以提供帮助，并且多样性是预训练中的既定原则。然而，To our knowledge, we are the first to isolate the significance of auxiliary views. The premise is intuitive: data augmentation can help, and diversity is an established principle in pre-training. However,多样性仍然是一项未明确规定的原则diversity remains an underspecified principle在数据稀缺的情况下从头开始构建数据时很难实施。这促使人们更清楚地了解变化如何改善学习以及原因。that is difficult to operationalize when constructing data from scratch amid data scarcity. This motivates a clearer account of how variation improves learning and why.

我们的研究揭示了一种现象，即文档的概念重新表述，无论是作为教科书还是博客，所产生的效果与单纯的释义截然不同：Our study uncovers a phenomenon in which conceptual reformulations of a document, whether as a textbook or a blog, have an effect starkly distinct from mere paraphrasing:它们极大地改善了学习，并在知识编码方式上产生了明显的分层偏差和压缩they substantially improve learning and induce a distinct layer-wise bias and compression in how knowledge is encoded。更引人注目的是，尽管模型不太频繁地看到构建我们的探针的原始文档，但它们提高了事实回忆。这表明了一个潜在的影响：. More strikingly, they improve factual recall even though the model sees the original document, from which our probes are constructed, less frequently. This points to an underlying effect:模型更广泛的概念理解直接提高了其记忆特定事实的能力。a model’s broader conceptual understanding directly facilitates its ability to memorize specific facts.

此外，模型对辅助视图进行有效编码的能力仅出现在较大的模型中，并且随着模型大小的增加而增强。Furthermore, the model’s ability to encode auxiliary views effectively emerges only in our larger models and grows with model size.更大的模型通过更有效地整合不同的观点来学习得更好Larger models learn better by integrating diverse views more effectively，通过移动更少的权重并将学习重新分配到中间层和最后层，以更高的参数效率对它们进行编码。总之，这些结果有助于解释预训练语料库的有效性，超越简单的规模，并为通常引用的“良好”和“多样化”数据的概念提供更清晰的机制。, encoding them with greater parameter efficiency by moving fewer weights and redistributing learning toward the middle and final layers. Together, these results help explain the effectiveness of pre-training corpora beyond simple scale and provide a clearer mechanism for the commonly invoked notion of “good” and “diverse” data.

实用要点。Practical Takeaways.我们为从事领域适应的从业者提供三项建议：（1）继续对先决知识进行预训练，以缩小基础差距（2）在数据稀缺时应用释义，同时认识到其好处随着批量大小的增加而减弱（3）并考虑使用辅助视图进行合成增强。We offer three recommendations for practitioners engaged in domain adaptation: (1) continue pre-training on prerequisite knowledge to close foundational gaps (2) apply paraphrasing when data is scarce, while recognizing that its benefit diminishes as batch size grows (3) and consider synthetic augmentation with auxiliary views.

我们发现最后一点与科学领域特别相关。开放的科学语料库在不断发展，产生的研究缺乏围绕既定知识的辅助观点。我们的结果表明，综合这些观点有助于有效地获取知识。然而，这对于法学硕士可能缺乏生成高质量辅助观点的专业知识的专业长尾知识是否仍然有效仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。We find the last point especially relevant for scientific domains. Open scientific corpora are constantly evolving, producing research that lacks the auxiliary views which surrounds established knowledge. Our results show that synthesizing such views is helpful for acquiring knowledge effectively. However, whether this remains effective for specialized, long-tailed knowledge for which LLMs may lack the expertise to generate high-quality auxiliary views remains an open question.

虽然这项研究的范围可能会限制其普遍性，但我们希望这些见解对领域适应的从业者来说是有价值的，鼓励更多地考虑知识的表示方式，并引发对法学硕士知识获取机制的更深入讨论。While the scope of this study may limit its generalizability, we hope these insights prove valuable to practitioners in domain adaptation, encourage greater consideration of how knowledge is represented, and provoke deeper discussion of the mechanisms of knowledge acquisition in LLMs.

局限性Limitations

我们的分析仅限于三个领域，这可能会引入语料库级别的偏差。虽然我们关于辅助视图的主要结果在每个领域都是一致的，但我们关于上下文和先决知识的次要结果却并非如此。我们承认这限制了我们研究结果的概括性。Our analysis is restricted to three domains, which may introduce corpus-level biases. While our main results regarding auxiliary views were consistent across each domain, this is not the case for our secondary results regarding contextual and prerequisite knowledge. We acknowledge that this limits the generalization of our findings.

此外，上下文知识实验仅涵盖 arXiv 论文和法律意见，因为医学案例报告缺乏类似的引用结构。其结果为上下文和先决知识建立了不同的事实和推理偏差，但与标记匹配释义相比并没有一致的优势。Furthermore, the contextual-knowledge experiment covers only arXiv papers and legal opinions, because medical case reports lack an analogous citation structure. Its results establish different factual and inference biases for contextual and prerequisite knowledge, but not a consistent advantage over token-matched paraphrasing.

我们的研究结果对于预训练的普遍性也可能有限。虽然我们的预训练忠实控制使用原始优化器状态、学习率计划和批量大小为 1,024 的数据，但它是在稍后检查点进行的 100 步实验，而不是从头开始预训练。Our findings may also have limited generalizability to pre-training proper. While our pre-training-faithful control uses the original optimizer state, learning-rate schedule, and data at a batch size of 1,024, it is a 100-step experiment at a later checkpoint rather than pre-training from scratch.

此外，我们对生成器强度的研究可能无法推广到资源匮乏、高度专业化的领域，在这些领域，即使理解源材料也可能对生成器提出挑战。在这种情况下，尚不清楚教师模型的强度是否无关紧要。Furthermore, our investigation of generator strength may not generalize to low-resource, highly specialized domains, where even comprehending the source material may challenge the generator. In such settings, it remains unclear whether teacher-model strength is irrelevant.

最后，我们的结果受到我们研究模型规模（最多 32B）的限制。由于模型规模是 LLM 能力的主要决定因素，因此我们的研究结果可能无法扩展到更大的模型。Finally, our results are constrained by the scale of the models we study (up to 32B). Because model scale is a primary determinant of LLM capabilities, our findings may not extend to substantially larger models.

文档收集。Document collection.

arXiv 集是手动收集的。对于医疗文档，我们使用 NCBI 电子实用程序 API 来搜索 PubMed Central 以获取开放获取的病例报告并下载 JATS XML。对于法律文件，我们使用 CourtListener REST API 来搜索美国联邦上诉意见。跨域，我们过滤候选文档以获得 12 个合适长度的目标文档，例如，超过 4000 个标记的法院意见被拒绝，以避免尴尬的分块。The arXiv set was manually collected. For medical documents, we use the NCBI E-utilities API to search PubMed Central for open-access case reports and download JATS XML. For legal documents, we use the CourtListener REST API to search U.S. federal appellate opinions. Across domains, we filter candidate documents to obtain twelve target documents of suitable length, e.g., court opinions longer than 4000 tokens were rejected to avoid awkward chunking.

清洁。Cleaning.

对于 arXiv 论文，我们通过删除注释、图形、表格、仅演示命令、未解决的包含、分页符和参考书目/附录材料来清理原始 LaTeX；扩展简单的宏；提取标题、摘要和正文；解决修订命令；并修复错误的换行符，同时保留 LaTeX 环境。医疗病例报告从 JATS XML 转换为分段纯文本。法律意见需要额外的 PDF/OCR 清理：我们删除页眉、案卷/页面工件、装饰性分隔符、提取的脚注，并将悬空句子合并到段落中。For arXiv papers, we clean the raw LaTeX by removing comments, figures, tables, presentation-only commands, unresolved includes, page breaks, and bibliography/appendix material; expanding simple macros; extracting title, abstract, and main body; resolving revision commands; and repairing erroneous line breaks while preserving LaTeX environments. Medical case reports are converted from JATS XML into sectioned plain text. Legal opinions require additional PDF/OCR cleanup: we remove page headers, docket/page artifacts, decorative separators, extracted footnotes, and merge dangling sentences into paragraphs.

情境知识。Contextual Knowledge.

在我们的研究中，上下文视图是与源文档相关的引用作品。对于 arXiv 文档，我们将每篇论文解析为 arXiv 标识符，使用 Semantic Sc​​holar 和 OpenAlex 检索引用元数据，使用 arXiv 标识符识别引用，下载其源包，并使用相同的 arXiv 清理程序清理它们。对于法律文件，我们使用 CourtListener 引用链接来收集引用的司法意见并应用相同的法律意见清理程序。我们不构建医疗文档的上下文视图，因为病例报告在我们的设置中缺乏类似的引用结构。In our study, contextual views are cited works associated with the source document. For arXiv documents, we resolve each paper to an arXiv identifier, retrieve reference metadata using Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex, identify references with arXiv identifiers, download their source packages, and clean them with the same arXiv cleaning procedure. For legal documents, we use CourtListener citation links to collect cited judicial opinions and apply the same legal-opinion cleaning procedure. We do not construct contextual views for medical documents because case reports lack an analogous citation structure in our setup.

The computer science setting generates textbook chapters for college students with a basic machine-learning background, Stack Exchange–style questions from a confused student followed by grounded answers, and technical blog posts for a broader technical audience. The medical setting adapts these formats to clinical education: case-based textbook sections for medical students or residents, clinical teaching Q&A, and clinical blog posts. The legal setting uses casebook/treatise-style chapters for law students, Law Stack Exchange–style Q&A, and analytic legal commentary. For each view family, we first generate an outline, list of questions, or list of blog ideas and then generate the individual chapters, answers, or posts from that plan. We use GPT-5 to generate the outlines and GPT-5-mini to generate the contents of the chapters, questions, or posts.

先决条件视图。Prerequisite views.

必备视图与辅助视图分开生成。对于 arXiv 论文，我们使用课程设计提示来创建必备的教科书章节，教授理解论文所需的基础知识，同时排除论文自己的新颖想法。对于医疗病例报告，提示要求提供一般医学背景，例如相关解剖学、病理生理学、药理学、诊断解释、鉴别诊断和标准管理，同时明确禁止患者特定的时间顺序、检查、治疗过程、结果或来自源病例的新观察结果。对于法律意见，提示同样要求提供理论和程序背景，同时排除源案件的当事人、事实、结果、主张和新颖的推理。当对法律背景有用时，我们还包括具有里程碑意义或理论基础的引用意见，作为生成的先决条件章节的背景。我们使用 GPT-5 生成大纲，使用 GPT-5-mini 生成章节内容。Prerequisite views are generated separately from auxiliary views. For arXiv papers, we use a curriculum-design prompt to create prerequisite textbook chapters that teach the foundations needed to understand the paper while excluding the paper’s own novel ideas. For medical case reports, the prompt asks for general medical background–for example relevant anatomy, pathophysiology, pharmacology, diagnostic interpretation, differential diagnosis, and standard management–while explicitly forbidding patient-specific chronology, workup, treatment course, outcome, or novel observations from the source case. For legal opinions, the prompt similarly asks for doctrinal and procedural background while excluding the source case’s parties, facts, outcome, holding, and novel reasoning. When useful for legal background, we additionally include landmark or doctrinally foundational cited opinions as context for the generated prerequisite chapters. We use GPT-5 to generate the outlines and GPT-5-mini to generate the contents of the chapters.
