1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型Large language models(LLMs, Zhao et al., 2026)在各种自然语言处理任务中表现出了卓越的性能have exhibited exceptional performance across various natural language processing tasks(Li et al., 2025c; Chen et al., 2025b; Guan et al., 2025)。 最近，基于提示法学硕士的机器翻译（MT）在句子和短句翻译方面取得了显着进步. Recently, machine translation (MT) based on prompting LLMs has shown significant advancements in sentence and short-segment translation(Peng et al., 2023; He et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024; He et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025b)。 然而，在长文档翻译场景中，例如演讲稿、技术手册和文学文本，会出现关键问题，包括术语管理不善以及由于语境能力有限而导致文体元素不一致. 3.2 观察并采取行动的数据构建Observe-and-Act Data Construction

为了使代理能够准确识别并有效利用有益的上下文进行翻译，我们专注于探索不同的上下文推理策略并收集偏好数据。 然而，对所有内存类型执行联合搜索会导致难以处理的组合空间。 因此，我们将探索过程分解为三个连续的步骤，其中每个步骤专门研究一种特定上下文类型的效用。 这种顺序设计由两个关键优势驱动。 从功能上讲，它将搜索空间从组合乘积（To enable the agent to accurately identify and effectively utilize beneficial context for translation, we focus on exploring diverse context reasoning strategies and collecting preference data. However, performing a joint search over all memory types results in an intractable combinatorial space. Consequently, we decompose the exploration process into three sequential steps, where each step exclusively investigates the utility of one specific context type. This sequential design is driven by two critical advantages. Functionally, it transforms the search space from a combinatorial product (O​(∏2K)O(\prod{2^{K}})) 到加和 () to an additive sum (O​(∑2K)O(\sum{2^{K}})），使轨迹探索变得可行。 从策略上讲，它使我们能够获得有关每种上下文类型的具体贡献的细粒度反馈，从而有助于对代理策略进行精确调整。), rendering the trajectory exploration feasible. By leveraging a “3E” multi-granularity memory model, the agent captures and retrieves key contextual information in real time during sequential segment processing. To mitigate interference from redundancy,龙Loong采用观察并采取行动的推理过程来动态过滤候选上下文。 这些策略通过基于采样推理轨迹的强化学习进一步完善。 实证结果表明employs an observe-and-act reasoning process to dynamically filter context candidates. 局限性Limitations

首先，我们目前采用固定的分段策略（每个分段十个句子）。 虽然有效，但这种静态启发式缺乏与自然话语边界保持一致的灵活性，这可能会限制模型保持最佳连贯性的能力。 未来的工作可以结合动态分段机制来更好地捕获话语结构。 其次，与标准的一次性生成相比，所提出的由深度推理驱动的代理工作流程引入了额外的计算开销。 我们计划通过蒸馏或推测解码等优化技术在未来的迭代中解决这个效率瓶颈。 最后，虽然我们利用 COMET 作为强化学习的奖励模型，但它可能不能完美地代表人类在文档级翻译任务中的偏好。 我们期待采用更强大、更细致的评估指标，以便更好地使代理的优化与人类的判断保持一致。First, we currently adopt a fixed segmentation strategy (ten sentences per segment). While effective, this static heuristic lacks the flexibility to align with natural discourse boundaries, which may limit the model’s ability to maintain optimal coherence. Future work could incorporate dynamic segmentation mechanisms to better capture discourse structures. Second, the proposed agentic workflow, driven by deep reasoning, introduces additional computational overhead compared to standard one-pass generation. We plan to address this efficiency bottleneck in future iterations through optimization techniques such as distillation or speculative decoding. Finally, while we utilize COMET as the reward model for reinforcement learning, it may be an imperfect proxy for human preference in document-level translation tasks. We look forward to adopting more robust and nuanced evaluation metrics as they become available to better align the agent’s optimization with human judgments.
