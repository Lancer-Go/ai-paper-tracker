CIVA：批评家引发的对视觉世界模型代理的价值子空间攻击CIVA: Critic-Induced Value-Subspace Attacks on Visual World-Model Agents

Jiancheng WangMingli ZhuTong ZhangJiaqi RuanWei Wang    Siyuan LiangDacheng Tao[4pt] Corresponding author:pandaliang521@gmail.com
摘要Abstract

像 DreamerV3 这样的视觉世界模型代理通过循环潜在状态而不是单个观察来起作用，这削弱了逐帧观察攻击，并使它们的扰动在严格的每帧扰动约束下随时间急剧变化。我们研究对此类代理的白盒、因果、在线攻击，并提出批评者诱导的价值子空间攻击（Visual world-model agents such as DreamerV3 act through a recurrent latent state rather than a single observation, which weakens frame-wise observation attacks and makes their perturbations vary sharply over time under a strict per-frame perturbation constraint. We study white-box, causal, online attacks on such agents and propose Critic-Induced Value-Subspace Attacks (西瓦CIVA）。我们的主要观察结果是，在推出过程中，批评家引导的扰动集中在受害者自己的批评家引发的低维子空间中。基于这一观察，CIVA 首先使用评论家引导的 PGD 离线探测冻结的受害者，并通过 SVD 提取低秩值子空间。在测试时，它仅优化子空间系数，使用指数移动平均值 (EMA) 对其进行平滑，并将它们映射回像素。这种设计攻击对值敏感的循环动态，同时保持在线优化成本低且时间一致。对 DMC walker walk、Atari Pong 和 Crafter 的大量实验表明，CIVA 始终优于最近的五种方法；在 DMC walker walk 上，它实现了 26.07% 的最大奖励下降，同时保持较低的时间变化，TempAbs 为 0.646。). Our key observation is that, along a rollout, critic-guided perturbations concentrate in a low-dimensional subspace induced by the victim’s own critic. Based on this observation, CIVA first probes the frozen victim offline with critic-guided PGD and extracts a low-rank value-subspace by SVD. At test time, it optimizes only the subspace coefficients, smooths them with an exponential moving average (EMA), and maps them back to pixels. This design attacks value-sensitive recurrent dynamics while keeping the online optimization cheap and temporally coherent. Extensive experiments on DMC walker walk, Atari Pong, and Crafter show that CIVA consistently outperforms five recent methods; on DMC walker walk, it achieves the largest reward drop of 26.07% while keeping temporal variation low, with TempAbs of 0.646.

1 简介Introduction

视觉世界模型正在成为视觉决策的主流范例。梦想家风格的智能体不是单独对当前框架做出反应，而是将每个观察结果写入循环的潜在状态，并通过该内部空间中习得的动态来采取行动Visual world models are becoming a mainstream paradigm for visual decision-making. Rather than reacting to the current frame alone, Dreamer-style agents write each observation into a recurrent latent state and act through learned dynamics in that internal space[14, 15, 16, 17]。使用学习模型进行规划的相关内容. Related lines on planning with learned models[52]，变压器-, transformer-[42, 51, 71]和基于扩散的世界模型and diffusion-based world models[1]，大规模生成环境, large-scale generative environments[4]，可扩展的基于模型的控制, scalable model-based control[19]，以及机器人部署, and robot deployment[65]进一步强化循环潜在动态作为视觉强化学习的核心原语further reinforce recurrent latent dynamics as a core primitive in visual RL[6, 13, 66]。随着这些代理越来越接近安全关键用途，了解如何攻击它们变得越来越重要。. As these agents move closer to safety-critical use, understanding how to attack them becomes increasingly important.

现有的对强化学习的对抗性攻击已经表明，深层策略对视觉或状态观察中的小扰动高度敏感Existing adversarial attacks on RL already show that deep policies are highly sensitive to small perturbations in visual or state observations[54, 11, 47, 45, 5, 41, 21, 26, 35, 48, 10, 31, 63, 30, 32, 29, 36, 58, 59]。此后，从多个角度开发了更强的变体，包括强大的状态观察公式. Stronger variants have since been developed from several angles, including robust state-observation formulations[69, 68]、实时普遍观察攻击, real-time and universal observation attacks[56, 44, 61, 37]，策略感知攻击者, policy-aware attackers[53]，可检测性感知的隐形攻击, detectability-aware stealthy attacks[9]，以及基于策略分配的方法, and policy-distribution-based methods[7, 25, 2]。然而，这些方法中的大多数仍然直接在像素空间中优化扰动，并将每个帧视为本质上独立的目标。这种假设与视觉世界模型代理不匹配：重要的不仅是一帧是否受到扰动，而且是扰动是否在实际的每帧计算预算下继续引导循环潜在状态。. However, most of these methods still optimize perturbations directly in pixel space and treat each frame as an essentially independent target. That assumption is mismatched with visual world-model agents: what matters is not only whether one frame is perturbed, but whether the perturbation keeps steering the recurrent latent state under a realistic per-frame compute budget.

我们在白盒、因果在线攻击机制中研究了这一设置，针对冻结的 DreamerV3 受害者。攻击者只能修改当前的图像观察，并且必须在下一个环境步骤之前产生扰动。穿过 DMC 步行者步行（DeepMind Control SuiteWe study this setting in a white-box, causal, online attack regime against a frozen DreamerV3 victim. The attacker may modify only the current image observation and must produce the perturbation before the next environment step. Across DMC walker walk (DeepMind Control Suite[55]）、Atari Pong 和 Crafter，我们观察到两个一致的现象。首先，朴素的每帧全像素投影梯度下降（PGD）), Atari Pong, and Crafter, we observe two consistent phenomena. First, naive per-frame full-pixel projected gradient descent (PGD)[41]这里是一个弱原语：循环的潜在动态会稀释孤立的高频扰动，因此贪婪的逐帧攻击很难在长期范围内积累影响力。其次，有效的攻击方向并不均匀地分布在整个像素空间上。当我们跨时间堆叠评论家引导的扰动时，它们的能量集中在一个令人惊讶的低维子空间中。这与监督学习的子空间和低频发现相呼应is a weak primitive here: recurrent latent dynamics dilute isolated high-frequency perturbations, so greedy frame-wise attacks struggle to accumulate influence over long horizons. Second, effective attack directions do not spread uniformly across the full pixel space. When we stack critic-guided perturbations across time, their energy concentrates in a surprisingly low-dimensional subspace. This echoes subspace and low-frequency findings from supervised learning[67, 12, 44, 40, 23]，但这里的结构是由受害者自身的价值格局而不是输入统计数据引起的。, but the structure here is induced by the victim’s own value landscape rather than by input statistics.

这些观察结果表明了一种不同的攻击策略：一次识别价值敏感方向，然后随着时间的推移重复使用它们。我们将这个想法实例化为These observations suggest a different attack strategy: identify value-sensitive directions once, then reuse them causally over time. We instantiate this idea as西瓦CIVA。 CIVA 首先离线收集评论家引导的扰动，并应用奇异值分解 (SVD) 来提取低维、受害者感知的攻击子空间。在部署时，它不再在每一帧搜索完整的像素空间；相反，它仅优化子空间系数，并在将它们提升回像素之前使用指数移动平均值对其进行平滑。这直接解决了在严格条件下攻击世界模型智能体的核心难点。. CIVA first collects critic-guided perturbations offline and applies singular value decomposition (SVD) to extract a low-dimensional, victim-aware attack subspace. At deployment time, it no longer searches the full pixel space at every frame; instead, it optimizes only the subspace coefficients and smooths them with an exponential moving average before lifting them back to pixels. This directly addresses the core difficulty of attacking world-model agents under a tightℓ∞\ell_{\infty}预算：扰动必须保持足够强以影响循环潜在动态，同时保持计算成本低、时间连贯和视觉微妙budget: the perturbation must remain strong enough to influence recurrent latent dynamics while also staying computationally cheap, temporally coherent, and visually subtle[20, 70, 60, 24, 72]。在连续控制、离散控制和开放式任务中，CIVA 比代表性观察空间基线产生更强、更稳定的退化. Across continuous control, discrete control, and open-ended tasks, CIVA yields stronger and more stable degradation than representative observation-space baselines[69, 56, 53, 9, 7]，同时保持良好的时间平滑性和感知相似性。消融进一步表明，增益来自评论家引发的子空间、限制于该子空间的在线优化以及对其系数的时间平滑的组合。, while preserving favorable temporal smoothness and perceptual similarity. Ablations further show that the gains come from the combination of a critic-induced subspace, online optimization constrained to that subspace, and temporal smoothing on its coefficients.

我们的贡献有三方面：Our contributions are threefold:

  • •

    我们发现传统的逐帧观察攻击和视觉世界模型代理之间存在一个关键的不匹配：因为决策取决于反复出现的潜在状态，所以成功的攻击必须随着时间的推移塑造受害者的内部表示，而不仅仅是降低单个帧的质量We identify a key mismatch between conventional frame-wise observation attacks and visual world-model agents: because decisions depend on recurrent latent state, successful attacks must shape the victim’s internal representation over time rather than only degrade individual frames[38, 39].

  • •

    我们提出了 CIVA，这是一种评论家诱导的价值子空间攻击，它离线提取低等级的受害者感知扰动基础，并通过该子空间内的时间平滑执行有效的因果在线优化。We propose CIVA, a critic-induced value-subspace attack that extracts a low-rank victim-aware perturbation basis offline and performs efficient causal online optimization with temporal smoothing inside that subspace.

  • •

2 相关工作Related Work

世界模型强化学习。World-model reinforcement learning.

世界模型围绕循环潜在动态模型构建策略，该模型总结过去的观察结果并支持多步骤预测World models build policies around a recurrent latent dynamics model that summarizes past observations and supports multi-step prediction[14]。 Dreamer 系列表明，该配方可以在基本统一的配置下跨越连续控制、Atari 和开放式视觉领域. The Dreamer line demonstrates that this recipe scales across continuous control, Atari, and open-ended visual domains under a largely unified configuration[15, 16, 17]。并行的努力将相同的总体方向扩展到使用学习模型进行规划. Parallel efforts extend the same general direction to planning with learned models[52]，变压器-, transformer-[42, 51, 71]和基于扩散的世界模型and diffusion-based world models[1]，大规模生成环境, large-scale generative environments[4]，可扩展的基于模型的控制, scalable model-based control[19]，以及机器人部署, and robotic deployment[65];最近的调查总结了这一更广泛的趋势; recent surveys summarize this broader trend[6, 13, 66]。就我们的目的而言，重要的一点不仅是这些代理很强大，而且它们的决策过程是由反复出现的潜在状态介导的。这使得他们的对抗表面从根本上来说是时间性的，而不是帧局部性的。. For our purposes, the important point is not only that these agents are powerful, but that their decision process is mediated by a recurrent latent state. That makes their adversarial surface fundamentally temporal rather than frame-local.

对深层政策的对抗性攻击。Adversarial attacks on deep policies.

监督学习中的对抗性脆弱性Adversarial vulnerability in supervised learning[54, 11, 47, 45, 5, 41]直接延续到强化学习，观察中的小扰动可能会导致严重的行为退化carries over directly to RL, where small perturbations in observations can cause severe behavioral degradation[21, 26, 35, 48]。后续工作通过稳健的状态观察公式强化了这条路线. Follow-up work has strengthened this line through robust state-observation formulations[69, 68]，实时和普遍的扰动, real-time and universal perturbations[56, 61, 37]，策略感知攻击者, policy-aware attackers[53]，可检测性攻击, detectability-aware attacks[9, 20, 24]，以及基于政策分配的目标, and policy-distribution-based objectives[7, 70, 60]。其他工作在多智能体和深度强化学习环境中更广泛地研究对抗性智能体或鲁棒性概念. Other work studies adversarial agents or robustness notions more broadly in multi-agent and deep-RL settings[10, 25, 2, 38, 39]。这些方法提供了强大的攻击基线，但大多数仍然直接在像素空间中进行优化，并有效地分别针对每个帧。我们的设置有所不同，因为受害者是一个视觉世界模型代理，其行为取决于扰动如何在循环潜在动态中累积。. These methods provide strong attack baselines, but most still optimize directly in pixel space and effectively target each frame separately. Our setting differs because the victim is a visual world-model agent whose behavior depends on how perturbations accumulate inside recurrent latent dynamics.

强大的强化学习和防御。Robust RL and defenses.

从防御方面来说，通过与有经验的攻击者进行对抗性训练来研究稳健的强化学习From the defense side, robust RL has been studied via adversarial training with a learned attacker[49, 68]，针对最坏情况观察扰动的正则化, regularization against worst-case observation perturbations[46]，最坏情况感知训练，无需明确的对抗性部署, worst-case-aware training without explicit adversarial rollouts[33]，以及时间耦合扰动的博弈论公式, and game-theoretic formulations for temporally coupled disturbances[34]。该文献主要询问如何训练在扰动下保持可靠的智能体。我们的问题是不同的：在捍卫视觉世界模型代理之前，我们首先需要了解针对它们的强因果观察攻击是什么样的，以及它们的循环潜在结构是否会引起有效扰动的不同几何形状。. This literature mainly asks how to train agents that remain reliable under perturbation. Our question is different: before defending visual world-model agents, we need to understand what strong causal observation attacks look like against them in the first place, and whether their recurrent latent structure induces a different geometry of effective perturbations.

结构化、低维、时间连贯的攻击。Structured, low-dimensional, and temporally coherent attacks.

另一个相关主题询问对抗性扰动是否真的需要完整的像素空间。在监督学习中，普遍的扰动Another relevant thread asks whether adversarial perturbations really need the full pixel space. In supervised learning, universal perturbations[44]、低频攻击, low-frequency attacks[12]，和子空间攻击, and subspace attacks[67, 40, 23]所有这些都表明，有效方向通常位于比原始像素低得多或更平滑的空间中。在视频和流媒体设置中，时间协调攻击比独立的逐帧扰动更有效且更不易察觉all show that effective directions often lie in much lower-dimensional or smoother spaces than raw pixels. In video and streaming settings, temporally coordinated attacks can be more effective and less perceptible than independent frame-wise perturbations[64, 28]。实时通用扰动和策略感知攻击者等强化学习工作同样引入了跨框架结构以提高效率或攻击强度. RL work such as real-time universal perturbations and policy-aware attackers similarly introduces cross-frame structure to improve efficiency or attack strength[56, 53, 7]。 CIVA 在精神上是相关的，但在子空间的起源及其部署方式上有所不同。我们的攻击基础不是手工设计的、随机的或来自观察统计的；它是直接从目标代理推出时收集的受害者批评者的攻击梯度中提取的。然后，所得到的基础在帧之间按因果关系重复使用，并通过平滑子空间系数来增强时间一致性。这将低维攻击结构直接与循环世界模型代理的价值景观联系起来。. CIVA is related in spirit but differs in the origin of the subspace and in how it is deployed. Our attack basis is not hand-crafted, random, or derived from observation statistics; it is extracted directly from the victim critic’s attack gradients collected on rollouts of the target agent. The resulting basis is then reused causally across frames, with temporal coherence enforced by smoothing the subspace coefficients. This ties low-dimensional attack structure directly to the value landscape of a recurrent world-model agent.
