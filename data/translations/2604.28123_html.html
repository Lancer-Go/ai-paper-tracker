摘要Abstract

大型多模态模型 (LMM) 的标准训练后配方对策划的演示应用监督微调 (SFT)，然后进行具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR)。然而，SFT 引入了分布漂移，既不保留模型的原始功能，也不忠实地匹配监督分布。这个问题在多模态推理中被进一步放大，其中感知错误和推理失败遵循不同的漂移模式，并在随后的强化学习过程中复合。 我们介绍The standard post-training recipe for large multimodal models (LMMs) applies supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on curated demonstrations followed by reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). However, SFT introduces distributional drift that neither preserves the model’s original capabilities nor faithfully matches the supervision distribution. This problem is further amplified in multimodal reasoning, where perception errors and reasoning failures follow distinct drift patterns that compound during subsequent RL. We introduce棱镜PRISM，一个三级管道，通过在 SFT 和 RLVR 之间插入显式分布对齐阶段来减轻这种漂移。 基于策略蒸馏 (OPD) 原理，PRISM 将策略与具有专门感知和推理专家的专家混合 (MoE) 判别器之间的一致性视为黑盒、响应级对抗游戏，提供解开的纠正信号，引导策略走向监督分布，而无需访问教师逻辑。 虽然 126 万次公开演示足以进行广泛的 SFT 初始化，但分布对齐需要更高保真度的监督；因此，我们策划了 Gemini 3 Flash 的 113,000 个额外演示，具有密集的视觉基础和对最难解决的问题的逐步推理。 Qwen3-VL 上的实验表明，PRISM 在多种 RL 算法（GRPO、DAPO、GSPO）和多种多模态基准上持续改进下游 RLVR 性能，将平均精度比 SFT 提高 +4.4 点和 +6.0 点, a three-stage pipeline that mitigates this drift by inserting an explicit distribution-alignment stage between SFT and RLVR. Building on the principle of on-policy distillation (OPD), PRISM casts alignment as a black-box, response-level adversarial game between the policy and a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) discriminator with dedicated perception and reasoning experts, providing disentangled corrective signals that steer the policy toward the supervision distribution without requiring access to teacher logits. While 1.26M public demonstrations suffice for broad SFT initialization, distribution alignment demands higher-fidelity supervision; we therefore curate 113K additional demonstrations from Gemini 3 Flash, featuring dense visual grounding and step-by-step reasoning on the hardest unsolved problems. Experiments on Qwen3-VL show that PRISM consistently improves downstream RLVR performance across multiple RL algorithms (GRPO, DAPO, GSPO) and diverse multimodal benchmarks, improving average accuracy by +4.4 and +6.0 points over the SFT→\rightarrowRLVR 基线分别在 4B 和 8B 上。我们的代码、数据和模型检查点可在以下位置公开获取：RLVR baseline on 4B and 8B, respectively. 1 简介Introduction

受面向推理的大语言模型 (LLM) 成功的推动Driven by the success of reasoning-oriented large language models (LLMs)(Guo et al., 2025; Jaech et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2025a; Zeng et al., 2026; Lin et al., 2026a)，大型多模态模型（LMM）也表现出了强大的指令跟踪和推理能力, large multimodal models (LMMs) have also demonstrated strong instruction-following and reasoning capabilities(Bai et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024)。提高此类能力的流行范例是两阶段的训练后流程：首先通过离线监督微调（SFT）对策划的演示进行调整. A prevailing paradigm for improving such capabilities is a two-stage post-training pipeline: models are first adapted via offline supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on curated demonstrations(Liu et al., 2023; 2024)，然后通过具有可验证奖励的在线强化学习（RLVR）进一步优化, and then further optimized with online reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR)(Shao et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2024)，它使用自动验证器直接提高任务性能。在此管道中，SFT 通过将模型锚定到高质量监督来提供关键的能力引导，而 RLVR 则进一步细化针对特定任务目标的策略，并在很大程度上决定最终性能。因此，越来越多的工作集中在提高这两个阶段的有效性和稳定性上。对于 SFT，最近的方法通过重新加权或正则化下一个标记的可能性来优化它, which directly improves task performance using automatic verifiers. In this pipeline, SFT provides a crucial capability bootstrap by anchoring the model to high-quality supervision, while RLVR further refines the policy toward task-specific objectives and largely determines the final performance. As a result, a growing body of work has focused on improving the effectiveness and stability of both stages. For SFT, recent methods optimize it by reweighting or regularizing next-token likelihood(Qin and Springenberg, 2025; Zhu et al., 2025)。对于 RLVR，人们提出了多种方法来提高优化稳定性并减少方差，包括重新设计重要性加权和裁剪机制以稳定策略更新的 GRPO 式变体. For RLVR, a number of approaches have been proposed to improve optimization stability and reduce variance, including GRPO-style variants that redesign importance weighting and clipping mechanisms to stabilize policy updates(Yu et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2025; Yue et al., 2025c; Wang et al., 2026)。基本的直觉很简单：SFT 在模型的参数空间中建立隐式推理先验，而 RLVR 通过在线优化激活和完善这种能力. The underlying intuition is straightforward: SFT establishes an implicit reasoning prior in the model’s parameter space, whereas RLVR activates and refines this capability through online optimization(Chu et al., 2025; Yue et al., 2025b).

然而，最近的研究发现了一个引人注目且违反直觉的现象：离线监督不但不能可靠地改进模型，反而可能使模型处于妥协状态，既不能充分匹配示范政策分布，也不能保留模型原有的有利分布However, recent studies have uncovered a striking and counterintuitive phenomenon: instead of reliably improving the model, offline supervision may place the model in a compromised state, where it neither adequately matches the demonstration policy distribution nor retains the model’s original favorable distribution(Kang et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2026a)。从这个意义上说，SFT 可以成为分布漂移的来源，而不是纯粹的改进步骤。一个合理的解释是，SFT 优化模型以模仿在统一代币级别目标下从示范政策中采样的轨迹，而不区分过程和结果。 因此，模型可能会学习表面模式而不是忠实的推理能力，同时偏离其原始分布。虽然这种漂移对于从学习演示策略中获益匪浅的较弱模型来说通常是可以容忍的，但随着基础模型变得越来越强大，这种漂移的成本也越来越高：当模型已经拥有强大的推理分布时，对外部演示策略的令牌级模仿可能会取代模型的原生优势，而不是补充它们. In this sense, SFT can become a source of distributional drift rather than a pure improvement step. A plausible explanation is that SFT optimizes the model to imitate trajectories sampled from the demonstration policy under a uniform token-level objective, without distinguishing between process and outcome. As a result, the model may learn surface-level patterns rather than faithful reasoning capabilities, and simultaneously drift away from its original distribution. While this drift is often tolerable for weaker models that gain substantially from learning the demonstration policy, it becomes increasingly costly as the base model grows stronger: when the model already possesses a capable reasoning distribution, token-level imitation of an external demonstration policy can displace the model’s native strengths rather than supplement them(Zhang et al., 2026a; Kang et al., 2025)。这个问题在多模态模型中变得尤为明显，其中 SFT 引入的分布偏差与不完美的视觉基础相互作用：即使感知阶段的微小偏差也会扭曲推理的前提，从而放大整个 RL 中的错误. This issue becomes particularly pronounced in multimodal models, where the distributional bias introduced by SFT interacts with imperfect visual grounding: even slight deviations at the perception stage can distort the premises of reasoning and subsequently amplify errors throughout RL(Liu et al., 2025a; Chu et al., 2025)。 此外，与纯文本模型中相对统一的漂移不同，多模态漂移本质上是异构的：视觉基础和逻辑推理以不同的方式退化，单个纠正目标无法共同解决。这就提出了一个自然的问题：. Moreover, unlike the relatively uniform drift in text-only models, multimodal drift is inherently heterogeneous: visual grounding and logical reasoning degrade in qualitatively different ways that a single corrective objective cannot jointly address. This raises a natural question:在模型进入强化学习之前，我们如何修复 SFT 引入的分布漂移，特别是其对视觉感知和推理的异质影响？How can we repair the distributional drift introduced by SFT, particularly its heterogeneous impact on visual perception and reasoning, before the model enters RL?

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 PRISM，这是一个三阶段的训练后管道，它通过基于黑盒对抗策略蒸馏的显式对齐阶段来减轻 SFT 引入的分布漂移。 PRISM 的核心是一个由专门的感知和推理专家组成的 MoE 判别器，它提供了解开的纠正信号，以引导 SFT 后的政策在模型自身的推出动态下转向监督分布。 Qwen3-VL 上的大量实验表明，PRISM 在多种 RL 算法和多种多模态基准测试中持续改进下游 RLVR 性能。对训练动态和响应结构分布的分析表明，对齐阶段大大缩小了 SFT 留下的分布差距，并且这些修正在后续的 RL 优化中持续存在。We present PRISM, a three-stage post-training pipeline that mitigates the distributional drift introduced by SFT through an explicit alignment stage based on black-box adversarial on-policy distillation. The core of PRISM is an MoE discriminator with dedicated perception and reasoning experts, which provides disentangled corrective signals to steer the post-SFT policy toward the supervision distribution under the model’s own rollout dynamics. Extensive experiments on Qwen3-VL demonstrate that PRISM consistently improves downstream RLVR performance across multiple RL algorithms and diverse multimodal benchmarks. Analysis of training dynamics and response-structure distributions suggests that the alignment stage substantially narrows the distributional gap left by SFT, and that these corrections persist through subsequent RL optimization.

附录EAppendix E局限性和未来方向Limitations and Future Directions

由于对齐阶段和 MoE 鉴别器，PRISM 引入了额外的培训成本。尽管对齐阶段仅运行 500 步，但它需要在训练期间共同维护策略生成器和鉴别器，与标准 SFT 相比，增加了内存和计算开销PRISM introduces additional training cost due to the alignment stage and the MoE discriminator. Although the alignment stage runs for only 500 steps, it requires jointly maintaining the policy generator and the discriminator during training, increasing memory and compute overhead compared to a standard SFT→\rightarrowRLVR 管道。此外，当前的 MoE 设计依赖于结构化响应格式（显式视觉标题和推理轨迹）来分解感知和推理反馈，这可能会限制对自然不允许这种分解的任务的直接适用性。此外，我们的分布对齐分析依赖于结构代理（推理步骤和标题项），而不是直接的分布度量，例如嵌入空间散度，这会引入它们自己的模型选择偏差；开发与模型无关的对齐度量仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。未来的工作包括通过探索判别器反馈的自动或学习分解、扩展到更大的基础模型以及研究是否可以进一步缩短对齐阶段或在多个下游 RL 目标中分摊对齐阶段，将 PRISM 扩展到更广泛的任务格式。RLVR pipeline. In addition, the current MoE design relies on structured response formats (explicit visual caption and reasoning trace) to decompose perception and reasoning feedback, which may limit direct applicability to tasks that do not naturally admit such decomposition. Moreover, our distribution alignment analysis relies on structural proxies (reasoning steps and caption items) rather than direct distributional measures such as embedding-space divergences, which would introduce their own model-selection biases; developing model-agnostic alignment metrics remains an open problem. Future work includes extending PRISM to broader task formats by exploring automatic or learned decomposition of discriminator feedback, scaling to larger base models, and investigating whether the alignment stage can be further shortened or amortized across multiple downstream RL objectives.
