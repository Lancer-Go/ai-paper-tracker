海报：通过物理引导深度展开进行实用的 AI-RAN 跨频段信道预测Poster: Practical Cross-Band Channel Prediction for AI-RAN via Physics-Guided Deep Unfolding

Ruiqi Kong, He Chen∗, Xiaojun Lin Department of Information Engineering, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong SAR, ChinaEmail: {rqkong, he.chen, xjlin}@ie.cuhk.edu.hk

摘要。Abstract.

为了使跨频段信道预测适用于 AI 原生 RAN，算法必须能够跨不同环境进行泛化并支持实时推理。现有方法只能实现其中之一，但不能同时实现两者。 为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了 GUIDE，这是一种物理引导的深度展开框架，它将无线信道物理嵌入到可微层中。无需在看不见的环境中进行再训练，To make cross-band channel prediction practical for AI-native RAN, algorithms must generalize across diverse environments and support real-time inference. Existing approaches achieve one but not both. To bridge this gap, we introduce GUIDE, a physics-guided deep unfolding framework that embeds wireless channel physics into differentiable layers. Without retraining in unseen environments,指南GUIDE达到achieves2.75×2.75\times与基于深度学习的基线 FIRE 相比，波束成形增益仅略微增加了推理时间，并且beamforming gain than the deep learning-based baseline FIRE with only a slight increase in inference time, and1.39×1.39\times运行时波束成形增益优于基于模型的最强基线 R2F2beamforming gain than the strongest model-based baseline R2F2 while running over1610×1610\times更快。faster.

1. 简介Introduction

在 AI-RAN 设想的 AI 原生功能中，跨频带信道预测对于频分双工 (FDD) 蜂窝系统特别有价值Among the AI-native capabilities envisioned for AI-RAN, cross-band channel prediction is particularly valuable for frequency division duplexing (FDD) cellular systems(Banerjee et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2021)，其中下行链路 (DL) 信道状态信息 (CSI) 获取通常依赖于显式用户反馈, where downlink (DL) channel state information (CSI) acquisition typically relies on explicit user feedback(Bakshi et al., 2019)。在大规模 MIMO 中，这种反馈会产生大量带宽开销和延迟. In massive MIMO, this feedback incurs substantial bandwidth overhead and latency(Banerjee et al., 2024)。实现无反馈 FDD 的关键物理洞察是底层多径参数是. The key physical insight enabling feedback-free FDD is that the underlying multipath parameters are频率无关frequency-independent。 如果可以从上行链路(UL)信道准确地估计这些参数，则可以直接重建任意频率的DL信道。. If these parameters can be accurately estimated from the uplink (UL) channel, the DL channel at any frequency can be reconstructed directly.

4. 结论Conclusion

指南GUIDE通过物理引导的深度展开设计实现准确且环境可推广的跨频带信道预测。当直接从模拟部署到实际测量而无需重新培训时，achieves accurate and environment-generalizable cross-band channel prediction through a physics-guided deep unfolding design. When deployed directly from simulation to real-world measurements without retraining,指南GUIDE以实时推理速度获得接近真实的波束成形增益，并且大大优于所有基线。这些发现表明attains near-ground-truth beamforming gain at real-time inference speed and substantially outperforms all baselines. These findings suggest that指南GUIDE可以作为实时 AI-RAN 的实用且可部署的信道推理组件。can serve as a practical and deployable channel-inference component for real-time AI-RAN.
