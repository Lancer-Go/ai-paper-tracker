摘要Abstract

最近的研究表明，将海量英语网络语料库过滤成高质量的子集可以显着提高训练效率。然而，对于德语、法语或日语等资源丰富的非英语语言，激进的过滤会造成战略困境：从业者是否应该优先考虑Recent research has shown that filtering massive English web corpora into high-quality subsets significantly improves training efficiency. However, for high-resource non-English languages like German, French, or Japanese, aggressive filtering creates a strategic dilemma: should practitioners prioritize多样性diversity通过对大量经过轻微过滤的网络数据进行一次训练，或者优先考虑by training once on large amounts of lightly filtered web data, or prioritize品质quality通过严格过滤高质量核心并在多个时期重复它？by strictly filtering for a high-quality core and repeating it over multiple epochs?
我们通过构建应用于 5 亿网络文档的分层质量过滤器来研究德语的这种权衡，将过滤子集上的多轮训练与不同语料库上的单通道训练进行比较。我们在多个模型规模和代币预算上的实验表明，在较大、过滤较少的数据集上，重复高质量数据始终优于单遍训练。值得注意的是，即使在 7 个 epoch 之后，性能差距仍然存在。我们的研究结果表明，对于非英语法学硕士来说，通过质量过滤进行语义集中比简单地最大化独特数据量提供了一条更可行的高效语言建模途径。We investigate this trade-off for German by constructing hierarchical quality filters applied to 500M web documents, comparing multi-epoch training on the filtered subsets against single-pass training on a diverse corpus. Our experiments across multiple model scales and token budgets show that repeating high-quality data consistently outperforms single-pass training on larger, less filtered sets. Notably, the performance gap persists even after 7 epochs. Our findings suggest that for non-English LLMs, semantic concentration through quality filtering offers a more viable path to efficient language modeling than simply maximizing unique data volume.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的流行缩放法则强调一个简单的三元组：更多参数、更多计算和更多数据The prevailing scaling laws for large language models (LLMs) emphasize a simple triad: more parameters, more compute, and more dataKaplan et al. (2020); Hoffmann et al. (2022)。然而，随着该领域朝着样本效率更高的训练方向发展，“越多越好”范式越来越受到“质量第一”方法的挑战。之前的工作表明，在经过过滤的高质量内容的网络语料库上训练法学硕士，可以比在大量未经过滤的数据集上进行训练产生明显更强的性能. However, as the field moves towards more sample-efficient training, the "more is better" paradigm has been increasingly challenged by "quality-first" approaches. Previous works demonstrated that training LLMs on web corpora filtered for high-quality content can yield significantly stronger performance than training on massive, unfiltered datasetsDu et al. (2022); PaLM Team (2022); Gunasekar et al. (2023); Lozhkov et al. (2024).

虽然这种转变对于英语来说是有据可查的，但它对德语、法语、日语和汉语等非英语高资源语言提出了独特的挑战。这些语言拥有包含数千亿个标记的大量网络语料库，但它们缺乏英语那样丰富的数万亿个标记。在这些数据受限的场景中，严格的过滤会造成战略困境：从业者是否应该通过仅应用轻过滤器来维护大型代币池（例如 100B 代币）来优先考虑多样性，或者应该While this shift is well-documented for English, it presents a unique challenge for non-English high-resource languages such as German, French, Japanese, and Chinese. 连贯性Coherence.该过滤器的目标是基本的语言和结构完整性。它旨在删除“文字沙拉”文档、截断的 HTML 导出和碎片片段。高连贯性得分表明文档具有自然的句法流程，无论内容是否信息丰富。This filter targets basic linguistic and structural integrity. 5 研究质量与数量的权衡Investigating the Quality-Quantity Trade-off

在本节中，我们提出了一系列实验，旨在隔离数据密度对模型性能的影响。我们摆脱传统的“单遍”预训练范式，研究高质量的重复是否可以弥补独特令牌多样性的缺乏。我们的研究分为四个部分：固定 100B 代币预算下的代币分配策略研究、1B 参数制度下的缩放分析、重复上限探索以及下游对指令调优影响的评估。In this section, we present a series of experiments designed to isolate the impact of data density on model performance. We move away from the traditional "single-pass" pre-training paradigm to investigate whether high-quality repetition can compensate for a lack of unique token diversity. Our investigation is divided into four parts: a study of token allocation strategies under a fixed 100B token budget, a scaling analysis in the 1B parameter regime, exploration of the repetition ceiling, and an evaluation of the downstream impact on instruction tuning.

7 结论Conclusion

这项工作解决了非英语 LLM 开发的一个实际问题：当可用文本总量有限时，积极的质量过滤是否值得，或者它是否会放弃太多有用的预训练多样性？This work addresses a practical question for non-English LLM development: is aggressive quality filtering worthwhile when total available text is limited, or does it discard too much useful diversity for pre-training?

我们的实验表明，尽管可用网络数据池较小，但质量过滤仍然是有益的。在我们考虑的模型大小和代币预算中，在小型、高质量子集上进行多个时期训练的模型始终优于在较大、过滤程度较低的混合物上训练的模型。在我们的设置中，我们没有观察到重复高质量数据不再有帮助的早期饱和点。对过滤后的语料库进行额外的传递会继续带来改进，而添加更多未过滤的数据只会带来有限的收益。这表明，对于德语等高资源非英语语言来说，仔细过滤和多时期训练是一种可行且有效的策略，而不是一种冒险的权衡。Our experiments suggest that quality filtering remains beneficial despite the smaller pool of available web data. Across the model sizes and token budgets we considered, models trained for multiple epochs on small, high-quality subsets consistently outperform those trained on larger, less strongly filtered mixtures. Within our setups, we do not observe an early saturation point where repeating high-quality data ceases to help. Additional passes over the filtered corpus continue to yield improvements, whereas adding more unfiltered data brings only limited gains. This indicates that careful filtering and multi-epoch training are a viable and effective strategy rather than a risky trade-off for high-resource non-English languages like German.

我们的结果还显示了质量第一的预训练和指令调整之间的连续性。在精选的高质量子集上进行预训练的指令调整助手在正确性和有用性方面都优于在更多样化、过滤较少的数据上进行预训练的助手。将较低质量的文档引入预训练混合物中会降低指令调整模型的输出质量。Our results also show continuity between quality-first pre-training and instruction-tuning. Instruction-tuned assistants that were pre-trained on curated high-quality subsets outperform those pre-trained on more diverse, less filtered data in both correctness and helpfulness. Introducing lower-quality documents into pre-training mixtures decreased instruction-tuned model output quality.

在实践中，我们的研究结果表明，依赖于明确定义的规则和强大的注释器模型的广泛质量过滤为德语等非英语高资源语言的样本高效预训练提供了一条可行的途径。In practice, our findings suggest that extensive quality filtering relying on clearly-defined rules and strong annotator models provides a viable path towards sample-efficient pre-training in non-English high-resource languages like German.

局限性Limitations

语言范围。Language scope.我们的调查仅关注德语作为具有代表性的高资源非英语语言。虽然德语的网络语料库特征（数千亿个标记，但不是数万亿）可能会推广到法语、日语和中文等语言，但语料库较小或语言结构不同的语言可能会表现出不同的质量与数量权衡。未来的工作应该在不同的语系中验证这些发现。Our investigation focuses exclusively on German as a representative high-resource non-English language. While German’s web corpus characteristics (hundreds of billions of tokens but not trillions) likely generalize to languages like French, Japanese, and Chinese, languages with smaller corpora or different linguistic structures may exhibit different quality-quantity trade-offs. Future work should validate these findings across diverse language families.

模型和计算规模。Model and compute scale.我们的实验仅限于最多 1B 个参数的模型，并在最多 200B 个令牌上进行训练。虽然我们的研究结果表明，在这种规模上效率明显提高，但目前尚不清楚这些质量与数量的权衡是否适用于更大的行业级规模，在这种规模下，计算预算和可用的高质量数据都急剧增加。Our experiments are limited to models up to 1B parameters trained on at most 200B tokens. While our findings demonstrate clear efficiency gains at this scale, it remains unclear whether these quality-quantity trade-offs hold at substantially larger, industry-level, scales where both compute budgets and available high-quality data increase dramatically.

架构选择。Architecture selection.我们专注于密集变压器架构。专家混合模型可能在数据质量和重复方面表现出不同的行为。同样，我们不会探索架构创新，例如可能在预训练期间表现出不同行为的替代注意力机制。We focus exclusively on dense transformer architectures. Mixture-of-experts models might exhibit different behaviors with respect to data quality and repetition. Similarly, we do not explore architectural innovations like alternative attention mechanisms that might exhibit different behavior during pre-training.

毒性和偏差评估。Toxicity and bias assessment.我们不会评估我们的模型是否会产生有毒内容、人口统计偏见或有害的刻板印象。虽然我们的质量过滤器优先考虑教育和信息内容，与未经过滤的网络数据相比，这可能会降低某些风险，但我们不能保证积极的过滤能够消除有问题的内容或防止有偏见的模型行为。We do not evaluate our models for toxic content generation, demographic biases, or harmful stereotypes. While our quality filters prioritize educational and informative content, which may reduce certain risks compared to unfiltered web data, we cannot guarantee that aggressive filtering eliminates problematic content or prevents biased model behavior.

高质量的教育内容仍然可能会编码社会偏见，并且在多时代培训期间的重复暴露可能会放大而不是减轻这些问题。未来的工作应该对人口统计维度和毒性基准进行全面的偏差审计，以了解质量过滤策略如何与模型安全相互作用。此外，我们对指令调整模型的法学硕士法官评估不会评估安全性、适当性或有害输出的可能性。High-quality educational content can still encode societal biases, and repeated exposure during multi-epoch training might amplify rather than mitigate such issues. Future work should conduct comprehensive bias audits across demographic dimensions and toxicity benchmarks to understand how quality filtering strategies interact with model safety. Additionally, our LLM-as-judge evaluation for instruction-tuned models does not assess safety, appropriateness, or potential for harmful outputs.

尽管存在这些限制，我们的工作提供了宝贵的证据，表明质量过滤和多时期训练为高资源非英语语言的有效预训练提供了一条实用的途径，挑战了在数据受限的环境中最大化独特令牌曝光应该是主要目标的观念。Despite these limitations, our work provides valuable evidence that quality filtering and multi-epoch training offer a practical path toward efficient pre-training for high-resource non-English languages, challenging the notion that maximizing unique token exposure should be the primary objective in data-constrained settings.
