使用视觉可穿戴表示和多实例学习对老年肿瘤患者进行压力估计Stress Estimation in Elderly Oncology Patients Using Visual Wearable Representations and Multi-Instance Learning

Ioannis Kyprakis    Vasileios Skaramagkas    Georgia Karanasiou    Vasilis Bouratzis    Andri Papakonstantinou    Dimitar Stefanovski    Kalliopi Keramida    Aristofania Simatou    Ketti Mazzocco    Anastasia Constantinidou    Konstantinos Marias    Dimitrios I. Fotiadis    Manolis Tsiknakis
摘要Abstract

心理压力在心脏肿瘤学中具有临床相关性，但通常仅通过患者报告的结果测量（PROM）进行评估，并且很少纳入持续的心脏毒性监测。我们使用来自智能手表（体力活动和睡眠）和胸戴式心电图传感器的多模式可穿戴数据来估计老年人、多中心乳腺癌队列 (CARDIOCARE) 的感知压力。可穿戴流被转换为异构视觉表示，产生弱监督设置，其中单个感知压力量表（PSS）分数对应于许多未标记的窗口。轻量级预训练专家混合主干 (Tiny-BioMoE) 将每个表示嵌入到 192 维向量中，这些向量通过基于注意力的多实例学习 (MIL) 进行聚合，以预测第 3 个月 (M3) 和第 6 个月 (M6) 的 PSS。在留一受试者排除 (LOSO) 评估下，预测结果与问卷分数有一定程度的一致性（M3：Psychological stress is clinically relevant in cardio-oncology, yet it is typically assessed only through patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) and is rarely integrated into continuous cardiotoxicity surveillance. We estimate perceived stress in an elderly, multi-center breast cancer cohort (CARDIOCARE) using multimodal wearable data from a smartwatch (physical activity and sleep) and a chest-worn ECG sensor. Wearable streams are transformed into heterogeneous visual representations, yielding a weakly supervised setting in which a single Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) score corresponds to many unlabeled windows. A lightweight pretrained mixture-of-experts backbone (Tiny-BioMoE) embeds each representation into 192-dimensional vectors, which are aggregated via attention-based multi-instance learning (MIL) to predict PSS at month 3 (M3) and month 6 (M6). Under leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) evaluation, predictions showed moderate agreement with questionnaire scores (M3:R2=0.24R^{2}{=}0.24, 皮尔逊, Pearsonr=0.42r{=}0.42, 斯皮尔曼, Spearmanρ=0.48\rho{=}0.48; M6：; M6:R2=0.28R^{2}{=}0.28, 皮尔逊, Pearsonr=0.49r{=}0.49, 斯皮尔曼, Spearmanρ=0.52\rho{=}0.52），M3 的全球 RMSE/MAE 为 6.62/6.07，M6 的全球 RMSE/MAE 为 6.13/5.54。), with global RMSE/MAE of 6.62/6.07 at M3 and 6.13/5.54 at M6.

临床相关性—Clinical Relevance— 这项工作提供了一种可扩展的途径，可以根据老年心脏肿瘤学中被动收集的可穿戴数据来估计患者报告的压力，从而实现临床就诊之间的纵向监测。即使是中等的预测能力也可以通过在心脏毒性治疗周期中将压力与睡眠中断、活动下降和心脏动态结合起来来支持整体监测。This work provides a scalable pathway to estimate patient-reported stress from passively collected wearable data in elderly cardio-oncology, enabling longitudinal monitoring between clinic visits. Even moderate predictive skill can support holistic surveillance by contextualizing stress alongside sleep disruption, activity decline, and cardiac dynamics during cardiotoxic treatment cycles.

I 简介Introduction

心理压力是老年人，特别是那些面临癌症或心血管疾病的老年人的一个重要问题。慢性压力会扰乱神经、免疫和内分泌系统的调节，导致疾病风险增加、生活质量 (QoL) 下降和健康状况恶化Psychological stress is a significant concern for elderly individuals, particularly those facing cancer or cardiovascular disease. Chronic stress disrupts the regulation of nervous, immune, and endocrine systems, leading to increase in disease risk, decrease quality of life (QoL), and worsening of health outcomes[1, 2]。在癌症患者中，较高的压力水平与较低的治疗依从性、较难控制副作用以及较快的身体机能下降有关. In cancer patients, higher stress levels are associated with lower adherence to treatment, greater difficulty in managing side effects, and faster decline in physical function[3]。这些负担在老年人中更为明显，他们往往患有多种健康状况并且身体恢复能力下降。. These burdens are even more pronounced in older adults, who often have multiple health conditions and reduced physical resilience.

心脏肿瘤护理优先考虑癌症治疗引起的心脏并发症的风险识别和监测，符合当前建议在治疗前、治疗期间和治疗后进行评估的指南Cardio-oncology care prioritizes identification of risks and monitoring of heart complications caused by cancer treatments, in line with current guidelines recommending assessments before, during, and after therapy[4]。对老年乳腺癌患者心脏毒性的研究表明，这些人面临着独特的挑战和危险因素，通常需要密切监测，尤其是那些患有严重合并症、心血管危险因素、已确诊的心血管疾病、虚弱、其他疾病或独立性降低的患者. Studies of cardiotoxicity in older breast cancer patients show that these individuals face unique challenges and risk factors, often requiring close surveillance—especially for those experiencing experiencing significant comorbidities, cardiovascular risk factors, established cardiovascular disease, frailty, other illnesses, or reduced independence[5]。由于老年乳腺癌患者特别容易发生心血管事件和功能丧失，因此需要更广泛的监测策略来支持针对其需求的护理。. Because elderly breast cancer patients are particularly susceptible to cardiovascular events and loss of functionality, broader monitoring strategies are needed to support care tailored to their needs.

在心脏肿瘤学中，心理压力越来越被认为是心血管脆弱性、治疗耐受性和恢复的调节因素，与心脏毒性治疗期间的自主神经失调、睡眠障碍和身体失调相互作用In cardio-oncology, psychological stress is increasingly recognized as a modifier of cardiovascular vulnerability, treatment tolerance, and recovery, interacting with autonomic dysregulation, sleep disturbance, and physical deconditioning during cardiotoxic therapy[6, 7]。压力还会影响重要的健康行为，例如睡眠、体力活动和遵守处方疗法，这些行为在可能损害心脏的癌症治疗过程中至关重要. Stress also affects crucial health behaviors, such as sleep, physical activity, and adherence to prescribed therapies, which are vital during cancer treatments that may harm the heart[8]。然而，在常规心脏肿瘤护理中，压力仍然没有得到充分监测，主要是因为评估依赖于不频繁的自我报告和零星的就诊，而无法捕捉压力水平的日常变化. Yet, stress remains under-monitored in routine cardio-oncology care, mostly because assessments rely on infrequent self-reports and sporadic clinic visits which do not capture day-to-day changes in stress levels[9].

当前可穿戴技术的进步现在可以对与压力相关的生理信号进行持续的、真实的监测，包括心率变异性、活动和睡眠模式Current advancements in wearable technology now enable ongoing, real-world monitoring of stress-related physiological signals, including heart rate variability, activity, and sleep patterns[10, 11]。这些数据流使得超越基本总结成为可能，并开发更密切地反映身体如何管理压力的数字生物标记。然而，传统的分析方法往往难以捕捉压力反应的复杂性、动态性和特定背景的性质，特别是对于具有不同健康背景且有时数据不完整的老年患者. These data streams make it possible to move beyond basic summaries and develop digital biomarkers more closely reflecting how the body manages stress. However, traditional analysis methods often struggle to capture the complex, dynamic, and context-specific nature of stress responses, especially in older patients with diverse health backgrounds and sometimes incomplete data[12].

最近的工作将生理时间序列转换为图像（例如频谱图、循环图）以利用视觉主干Recent work converts physiological time series into images (e.g., spectrograms, recurrence plots) to leverage vision backbones[13]。基于视觉的学习方法通过捕获手动编码具有挑战性的时间动态和多尺度模式，在压力、疲劳和睡眠评估等情感计算任务中表现出了强大的性能. Vision-based learning approaches have shown strong performance in affective computing tasks such as stress, fatigue, and sleep assessment by capturing temporal dynamics and multi-scale patterns which are challenging to encode manually[14]。然而，大多数现有研究集中于健康人群，利用单一模态数据，忽略与临床部署相关的实际限制，例如模型大小、可解释性和稳健性. However, most existing studies concentrate on healthy cohorts, utilize single-modality data, and neglect practical constraints relevant to clinical deployment, such as model size, interpretability, and robustness[15].

使用可穿戴数据建模压力的另一个基本挑战是其弱监督性质。压力标签主要以稀疏的时间分辨率提供，通常来自问卷或定期评估，而生理信号是连续记录的A further fundamental challenge in modeling stress using wearable data is its weakly supervised nature. Stress labels are primarily available at sparse temporal resolutions, often derived from questionnaires or periodic assessments, whereas physiological signals are continuously recorded[9]。多实例学习 (MIL) 提供了一种非常适合解决这种差异的方法，它可以从未标记实例的集合中进行袋级预测，并且已经展示了在医学成像和生物信号分析方面的巨大潜力. Multi-instance learning (MIL) provides a well-suited approach for addressing this discrepancy by enabling bag-level predictions from collections of unlabeled instances and has been demostrating promising potential in medical imaging and biosignal analysis[16]。然而，MIL 在老年临床人群可穿戴数据视觉表示中的应用仍未得到充分探索。. Nevertheless, the application of MIL to visual representations of wearable data in elderly clinical populations remains insufficiently explored.

与此同时，数字健康领域对轻量级、高效模型的需求日益得到认可，其中计算资源、能源效率和可扩展性对于临床实施至关重要。专家混合 (MoE) 架构提供模块化和专业化，但通常计算密集型Concurrently, the demand for lightweight and efficient models is increasingly recognized in digital health, where computational resources, energy efficiency, and scalability are essential for clinical implementation. Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures offer modularity and specialization but are frequently computationally intensive[17]。紧凑且资源高效的专家模型的最新进展表明，精心设计的轻量级 MoE 变体可以保持强大的性能，同时仍然适合部署. Recent advances in compact and resource-efficient expert models indicate that well-designed lightweight MoE variants can maintain robust performance while remaining suitable for deployment[18]。然而，这些方法很少在生物医学压力模型中得到检验，特别是与 MIL 和视觉表示相结合。. However, these approaches have seldom been examined in biomedical stress modeling, especially in conjunction with MIL and visual representations.

受这一差距的推动，本研究探索了老年心脏肿瘤队列的压力预测，作为欧盟资助的临床研究 CARDIOCARE 的一部分Motivated by this gap, this study explores stress prediction in an elderly cardio-oncology cohort as part of the EU-funded clinical study CARDIOCARE[19]，使用来自可穿戴设备的时间序列数据和先进的深度学习技术。我们专注于通过 PROM 在稀疏的后续时间点评估感知压力，而可穿戴信号则在自由生活条件下连续记录。, using time-series data from wearable devices and advanced deep learning techniques. We focus on perceived stress assessed via PROMs at sparse follow-up timepoints, while wearable signals are continuously recorded in free-living conditions.

为了解决这种弱监督机制，我们提出了一种新颖的多模式框架，该框架将 BioMoE 架构与基于注意力的 MIL 相结合，以在标签有限时推断来自不同可穿戴流的压力。该方法利用互补的可穿戴设备衍生的表示，并学习将可变长度、部分缺失的证据聚合到患者级别的预测中，同时通过注意力提供实例级别的可解释性。通过针对具有临床意义且研究不足的心脏肿瘤人群，这项工作开发了可扩展的非侵入性工具，用于个性化监测、早期风险识别和接受癌症治疗的老年患者的生活质量支持。To address this weak-supervision regime, we propose a novel multimodal framework that combines BioMoE architectures with attention-based MIL to infer stress from diverse wearable streams when labels are limited. The approach leverages complementary wearable-derived representations and learns to aggregate variable-length, partially missing evidence into patient-level predictions, while providing instance-level interpretability through attention. By targeting a clinically meaningful and under-studied cardio-oncology population, this work advances scalable, non-invasive tools for personalized monitoring, early risk identification, and quality-of-life support in older patients undergoing cancer treatment.

II 相关工作Related Work

在心脏肿瘤学和心脏毒性相关队列中，压力估计模型的探索仍然明显不足。临床工作优先考虑心血管终点，而心理社会压力通常仅通过自我报告的调查问卷来捕获。最近的评论强调了老年乳腺癌患者的脆弱性以及心脏肿瘤监测途径中缺乏持续部署的定量压力估计工具In cardio-oncology and cardiotoxicity-related cohorts, stress estimation models remain notably underexplored. Clinical work prioritizes cardiovascular endpoints, while psychosocial stress is typically captured only through self-reported questionnaires. Recent reviews emphasize both the vulnerability of elderly breast cancer patients and the lack of continuously deployable, quantitative stress-estimation tools embedded in cardio-oncology monitoring pathways[20]。据我们所知，没有研究报告专门针对心脏肿瘤人群开发或验证的压力估计模型的预测性能。. To the best of our knowledge, no studies report predictive performance for stress estimation models developed or validated specifically in cardio-oncology populations.

在肿瘤学之外，压力估计通常使用可穿戴流中的手工特征（时间/频率/统计），然后使用传统分类器，心率变异性 (HRV) 和皮肤电活动 (EDA) 被反复认为是信息丰富的。在受控环境中，报告了接近天花板的表现：关于 WESAD（15 名健康受试者；心电图、EDA、呼吸、加速测量），Calvo 等人。Outside oncology, stress estimation commonly uses handcrafted features (time/frequency/statistical) from wearable streams, followed by conventional classifiers, with heart rate variability (HRV) and electrodermal activity (EDA) repeatedly identified as highly informative. In controlled settings, near-ceiling performance is reported: on WESAD (15 healthy subjects; ECG, EDA, respiration, accelerometry), Calvo et al.[21]使用 CNN 实现 99.8% 的准确率（个性化：99.6%），这主要归功于 HRV 和 EDA；海亚特等人。achieved 99.8% accuracy with CNNs (personalized: 99.6%), attributing major contribution to HRV and EDA; Heyat et al.[22]据一项智能 T 恤研究（20 名参与者）中使用心电图导出的 HRV 特征报告，受试者内准确率为 93.3%（F1 93.5%），受试者间准确率为 94.1%。多模态融合进一步提高了鲁棒性：Amin 等人。reported 93.3% accuracy (F1 93.5%) intra-subject and 94.1% inter-subject using ECG-derived HRV features from a smart t-shirt study (20 participants). Multimodal fusion further improves robustness: Amin et al.[23]显示 HRV+EDA 在所有设备（35 名参与者）中始终优于仅 HRV，消费者智能手表的 AUROC 高达 0.961，而包含 EDA 时，性能接近 0.98。showed HRV+EDA consistently outperformed HRV-only across devices (35 participants), with AUROC up to 0.961 for a consumer smartwatch and performance approaching 0.98 when EDA was included.

相比之下，生态有效性仍然有限。马丁内斯等人。In contrast, ecological validity remains limited. Martinez et al.[24]对 657 名信息工作者进行了八周的监测，发现 HRV 特征只能解释瞬时自我报告压力的 1% 差异（聚合后最大为 2.2%），凸显了实验室准确性与野外压力估计之间的差距。最近的视觉编码方法将生理时间序列映射到图像以利用视觉模型：Arya 等人。monitored 657 information workers for eight weeks and found that HRV features explained only  1% of variance in momentary self-reported stress (max  2.2% with aggregation), underscoring the gap between laboratory accuracy and in-the-wild stress estimation. Recent visual-encoding approaches map physiological time series to images to leverage vision models: Arya et al.[25]使用 HRV 的模糊递归图，并通过 SVM 对松弛状态进行分类，达到 96.6% 的准确率，而 Yang 等人。used fuzzy recurrence plots from HRV and achieved 96.6% accuracy with an SVM classifying relaxation states, while Yang et al.[12]在 WESAD 上使用格拉米安/马尔可夫场并报告了 90.96% 的准确度和 91.67% 的 F1。尽管取得了有希望的结果，但这种视觉管道在老年心脏肿瘤队列中仍然很大程度上尚未得到探索，其中标签的监督很弱（问卷水平），并且连续的可穿戴流需要超越启发式窗口的原则性聚合used Gramian/Markov fields on WESAD and reported 90.96% accuracy and 91.67% F1. Despite promising results, such visual pipelines remain largely unexplored in elderly cardio-oncology cohorts, where labels are weakly supervised (questionnaire-level), and continuous wearable streams require principled aggregation beyond heuristic windowing

三、III方法论Methodology

Ⅲ-AIII-A 数据集Dataset

所提出的框架根据 CARDIOCARE 临床研究中招募的 387 名患者的数据进行了评估。该队列由六十岁以上的老年女性组成，她们被诊断患有乳腺癌，并被认为存在癌症治疗相关心脏毒性的风险。数据收集于六个临床中心：塞浦路斯银行肿瘤中心（BOCOC，编号 ΕΕΒΚ/ΕΠ/2022/58）、卡罗林斯卡大学（KSBC，编号 2023-0062-01-413152）、约阿尼纳大学（UOI，编号 2023-0062-01-413152）。 25/23-11/2022）、雅典国立卡波季斯特里亚大学（NKUA，编号 456/14-10-2022，ΕΒΔ-683/22-11-2022）、卢布尔雅那 Onkoloski 研究所（IOL，编号 ERIDEK-0038/2023）和欧洲肿瘤研究所SRL（IEO，编号：R1754/22-IEO 1874）。在入组前获得了所有参与者的书面知情同意书，并获得了所有参与临床中心相关伦理委员会的批准。The proposed framework was evaluated on data from 387 patients, recruited within the CARDIOCARE clinacal study. The cohort consists of elderly women, over sixty years of age, diagnosed with breast cancer and considered at risk of cancer therapy–related cardiotoxicity. Data were collected across six clinical centers: Bank of Cyprus Oncology Center (BOCOC, No. ΕΕΒΚ/ΕΠ/2022/58), Karolinska Universitetssjukhuset (KSBC, No. 2023-0062-01-413152), University of Ioannina (UOI, No. 25/23-11/2022), National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA, No. 456/14-10-2022, ΕΒΔ-683/22-11-2022), Onkoloski Institut Ljubljana (IOL, No. ERIDEK-0038/2023), and Instituto Europeo Di Oncologia SRL (IEO, No. R1754/22-IEO 1874). Written informed consent was obtained from all participants prior to enrolment and approvals were received from the relevant Ethics Committees at all participating clinical centers.

使用可穿戴和临床级传感器获取多模态数据。通过 Garmin Venu SQ 智能手表收集身体活动和睡眠数据Multimodal data were acquired using wearable and clinical-grade sensors. Physical activity and sleep data were collected via the Garmin Venu SQ smartwatch[26]，每位参与者每周至少有两天的空闲时间。同时，使用 Polar H10 (Fs = 130Hz) 胸带收集心电图 (ECG) 数据, with a minimum availability of at least two days per week per participant. In parallel, electrocardiographic (ECG) data were collected using the Polar H10 (Fs = 130Hz) chest strap[27]，由注册后前六个月内每两周获取的至少 30 分钟的录音组成。, consisting of at least 30-minute recordings acquired biweekly during the first six months following enrolment.

使用患者报告的结果测量 (PROM) 进行压力评估，并使用感知压力量表 (PSS) 量化感知压力Stress assessment was performed using patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs), with perceived stress quantified using the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS)[28]。 PSS 在基线 (M0)、第 3 个月 (M3) 和第 6 个月 (M6) 完成。在本研究中，在 M3 和 M6 收集的 PSS 分数被用作压力估计的连续回归目标，代表参与者在每个评估时间点自我报告的感知压力。 该采集协议和后续时间表由该项目的肿瘤专家设计，以使测量结果与项目的临床监测要求保持一致。. The PSS was completed at baseline (M0), month 3 (M3), and month 6 (M6). In this study, PSS scores collected at M3 and M6 were used as continuous regression targets for stress estimation, representing participants’ self-reported perceived stress at each assessment timepoint. This acquisition protocol and follow-up schedule were designed by the project’s expert oncologists to align measurements with the project’s clinical monitoring requirements.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 预处理Preprocessing

来自智能手表和胸戴式心电图传感器的多模式可穿戴数据经过预处理并转换为适合基于视觉的学习的视觉表示。考虑到数据和老年临床队列的真实世界纵向性质，预处理侧重于时间结构、聚合级缺失的处理和标准化视觉编码，而不是积极的信号级去噪。Multimodal wearable data from smartwatches and chest-worn ECG sensors were preprocessed and transformed into visual representations suitable for vision-based learning. Given the real-world, longitudinal nature of the data and the elderly clinical cohort, preprocessing focused on temporal structuring, handling of aggregation-level missingness, and standardized visual encoding, rather than on aggressive signal-level denoising.

体力活动和睡眠数据独立处理，但遵循共同的每周汇总策略。对于每位患者，最早的可用日历日期被用作时间基线，并且所有后续智能手表记录都与该参考对齐。每日摘要被分为不重叠的每周部分，每日值缺失超过 60% 的周被排除在进一步分析之外。对于保留的周，在同一周内使用特征方式对缺失的每日条目进行估算。然后对每周 7 天的所有特征进行 z 分数标准化，强调相对的周内时间模式而不是绝对幅度。Physical activity and sleep data were processed independently but followed a common weekly aggregation strategy. For each patient, the earliest available calendar date was used as the temporal baseline, and all subsequent smartwatch recordings were aligned to this reference. Daily summaries were grouped into non-overlapping weekly segments, and weeks with more than 60% missing daily values were excluded from further analysis. For retained weeks, missing daily entries were imputed using feature-wise means within the same week. All features were then z-score normalized across the seven days of each week, emphasizing relative intra-week temporal patterns rather than absolute magnitudes.

IV-C 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

压力估计性能主要使用均方根误差 (RMSE) 进行评估，反映了从 PSS 问卷得出的感知压力分数的连续性。平均绝对误差 (MAE) 还被报告为补充的保持尺度的全局指标。对于 LOSO 评估，RMSE 总结为受试者级别折叠的平均值和标准差。此外，通过汇集所有 LOSO 测试折叠的预测并报告总体 RMSE、MAE、决定系数来计算全局性能（Stress estimation performance was primarily evaluated using root mean squared error (RMSE), reflecting the continuous nature of perceived stress scores derived from the PSS questionnaire. V 讨论Discussion

在这项研究中，我们评估了现实世界心脏肿瘤学环境中的压力估计，其中监督在时间上是粗糙的：仅在 M3 和 M6 处收集 PSS，而可穿戴设备提供密集的多模态信号，我们将其转换为视觉表示。这就产生了一个弱监督问题，其中许多短传感器窗口映射到单个问卷分数；因此，必须根据窗口水平生理/行为与长达数周的 PSS 回忆之间的不匹配来解释结果。In this study, we evaluated stress estimation in a real-world cardio-oncology setting where supervision is temporally coarse: PSS is collected only at M3 and M6, while wearables provide dense multimodal signals that we convert into visual representations. This creates a weakly supervised problem in which many short sensor windows map to a single questionnaire score; results must therefore be interpreted in light of the mismatch between window-level physiology/behavior and weeks-long PSS recall.

Evaluation should shift from point prediction toward clinically aligned objectives, including within-subject change detection and patient-level stratification, with validation against downstream outcomes (e.g., sleep disruption, symptom burden, and treatment tolerance).

VI 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们使用被动收集的智能手表和编码为视觉表示的心电图数据，研究了老年多中心心脏肿瘤队列的感知压力估计。在无泄漏 LOSO 评估下，所提出的多模态方法在 M3 和 M6 上均与 PSS 取得了中等程度的一致性，这表明基于视觉的可穿戴特征即使在异构的自由生活条件下也可以恢复临床相关的压力相关模式。未来的工作将优先考虑更多的时间本地监督，并提高对缺失和现场影响的鲁棒性，评估以患者内部变化检测和临床可行的监测为中心。In this work, we investigated perceived-stress estimation in an elderly, multi-center cardio-oncology cohort using passively collected smartwatch and ECG data encoded as visual representations. Under leakage-free LOSO evaluation, the proposed multimodal approach achieved moderate agreement with PSS at both M3 and M6, indicating that vision-based wearable signatures can recover clinically relevant stress-related patterns even in heterogeneous free-living conditions. Future work will prioritize more time-local supervision and improved robustness to missingness and site effects, with evaluation centered on within-patient change detection and clinically actionable monitoring.
