F3-Tokenizer：驯服音频自动编码器潜伏F3-Tokenizer: Taming Audio Autoencoder Latents for
理解与生成Understanding and Generation

Dinghao Zhou2,∗  Xingchen Song2,∗  Di Wu2,∗  Pengyu Cheng∗
Shengfan Shen1,∗  Sixiang Lv1,∗

1Nanjing University, China  2WeNet Open Source Community

摘要Abstract

连续音频自动编码器可以很好地重建波形，但经常会产生 潜在的理解结构较弱，而自监督音频编码器 捕获语义但不能直接解码。这种不匹配使 必须支持理解和生成的单个音频标记器。我们 使用两个组件使连续自动编码器潜伏适应此设置： 噪声正则化自动编码器瓶颈和潜在侧表示 编码器。瓶颈使用通道归一化和随机扰动 代替基于 KL 的变分训练，产生尺度控制的连续 重建和自回归生成的潜力。代表性 编码器使用 RQ-MTP 和 freeze-LLM 对冻结自动编码器潜伏进行训练 监督。生成的分词器提供高维表示 用于理解，同时保留标准化连续潜伏作为生成 目标。Continuous audio autoencoders reconstruct waveforms well but often produce latents with weak structure for understanding, while self-supervised audio encoders capture semantics but are not directly decodable. This mismatch complicates a single audio tokenizer that must support both understanding and generation. We adapt continuous autoencoder latents to this setting with two components: a noise-regularized autoencoder bottleneck and a latent-side representation encoder. The bottleneck uses channel normalization and stochastic perturbation instead of KL-based variational training, yielding scale-controlled continuous latents for reconstruction and autoregressive generation. The representation encoder is trained on frozen autoencoder latents with RQ-MTP and frozen-LLM supervision. The resulting tokenizer provides high-dimensional representations for understanding while preserving normalized continuous latents as generation targets.

1 简介Introduction

音频表示学习是语音领域最新进展的核心 语言模型、文本到语音合成和通用音频生成。一个 常见的策略是将波形压缩成一系列声学潜伏序列， 然后在该序列上训练语言条件生成器Audio representation learning sits at the center of recent progress in speech language models, text-to-speech synthesis, and general audio generation. A common strategy is to compress waveform into a sequence of acoustic latents and then train a language-conditioned generator over that sequenceBorsos et al. (2022); Wang et al. (2023); Sun et al. (2024); Peng et al. (2025)。因此，潜在空间的质量决定了 生成的生成器可以做什么：保留可理解的语音，保留说话者 和声学条件，支持长格式合成，并保持可控 文本或其他高级条件。. The quality of the latent space therefore determines what the resulting generator can do: preserve intelligible speech, retain speaker and acoustic conditions, support long-form synthesis, and remain controllable by text or other high-level conditions.

现有的音频表示往往只专门解决这个问题的一部分。 神经编解码器和音频自动编码器针对重建和 压缩，产生保留声学细节的潜伏，但可能很差 为音频理解而组织Existing audio representations tend to specialize in only part of this problem. Neural codecs and audio autoencoders are optimized for reconstruction and compression, producing latents that retain acoustic detail but may be poorly organized for audio understandingZeghidour et al. (2021); Défossez et al. (2022)。 相比之下，自监督音频编码器学习对以下有用的表示 识别和分类. Self-supervised audio encoders, in contrast, learn representations useful for recognition and classificationBaevski et al. (2020); Hsu et al. (2021); Han et al. (2024)，但他们不是 设计用于解码为波形。最近使用的连续音频发生器 潜在目标上的补丁式扩散或流动头, but they are not designed to be decoded into waveform. Recent continuous audio generators use patch-wise diffusion or flow heads over latent targetsLipman et al. (2022); Le et al. (2023); Sun et al. (2024); Jia et al. (2025); Peng et al. (2025); Wang et al. (2026)，然而潜在目标通常是从 重建模型并视为固定。, yet the target latent is often inherited from a reconstruction model and treated as fixed.

现有系统通常通过添加另一个来改进语义结构 表示路径。 X-Codec-2.0 将预训练的语义编码器与 量化前的声学编码器Existing systems often improve semantic structure by adding another representation path. X-Codec-2.0 fuses a pretrained semantic encoder with an acoustic encoder before quantizationYe (2025); VibeVoice 使用单独的 用于长格式连续语音的声学和语义标记器 一代; VibeVoice uses separate acoustic and semantic tokenizers for long-form continuous speech generationPeng et al. (2025); MiMo-Audio-Tokenizer 训练大型离散 具有语义和重构目标的分词器; MiMo-Audio-Tokenizer trains a large discrete tokenizer with both semantic and reconstruction objectivesXiaomi MiMo Team (2025); MingTok-Audio 探索了一种连续语音标记器 耳语蒸馏语义; and MingTok-Audio explores a continuous speech tokenizer with Whisper-distilled semanticsYan et al. (2025)。这些设计证实了 语义信息的价值，但它们可以引入单独的编码器， 外部教师、离散密码本或不匹配的表示格式 理解表示、解码器输入和连续生成之间 目标。我们将连续自动编码器隐藏为声学锚点： 低维潜在仍然是重建和生成目标，而 建立在其之上的表示编码器提供高维表示 为了理解。. These designs confirm the value of semantic information, but they can introduce separate encoders, external teachers, discrete codebooks, or mismatched representation formats between understanding representations, decoder inputs, and continuous generation targets. We keep the continuous autoencoder latent as the acoustic anchor: the low-dimensional latent remains the reconstructive and generative target, while a representation encoder built on top of it provides high-dimensional representations for understanding.

我们的重点是标记器本身。我们以 SpectroStream 风格为基础 STFT 域音频自动编码器Our focus is the tokenizer itself. We build on a SpectroStream-style STFT-domain audio autoencoderLi et al. (2025)，但取代其瓶颈 具有标准化、噪声规则化的连续潜在灵感 连续令牌自回归生成工作, but replace its bottleneck with a normalized, noise-regularized continuous latent inspired by continuous-token autoregressive generation workNextStep Team (2025)。这会产生 标准化连续潜伏，无需 KL 正则化，保持自动编码器 潜在的可解码且稳定的声学目标用于重建和 一代。在冻结的自动编码器潜在变量之上，我们训练一个潜在侧 具有 RQ-MTP 自监督和监督功能的表示编码器 冷冻法学硕士。在分词器训练期间，投影的音频嵌入进入 冻结的 LLM，以及由此产生的音频对齐状态决定下一个补丁流程 预测连续自动编码器潜在补丁的头。. This produces normalized continuous latents without KL regularization, keeping the autoencoder latent decodable and stable as an acoustic target for reconstruction and generation. On top of frozen autoencoder latents, we train a latent-side representation encoder with RQ-MTP self-supervision and supervision from a frozen LLM. During tokenizer training, projected audio embeddings enter the frozen LLM, and the resulting audio-aligned states condition a next-patch flow head that predicts continuous autoencoder latent patches.

范围是分词器设计，而不是完全统一的音频语言模型。我们学习 是否归一化自动编码器潜伏、表示学习和 发电侧流量监控提供三个属性：The scope is tokenizer design rather than a full unified audio-language model. We study whether normalized autoencoder latents, representation learning, and generation-side flow supervision provide three properties:声学的 保真度acoustic fidelity, 理解效用understanding utility, 和, and可预测性 音频生成predictability for audio generation。 我们将最终的系统称为 F3-Tokenizer，强调保真度， 高维表示和基于流的生成。. We refer to the resulting system as F3-Tokenizer, emphasizing fidelity, high-dimensional representations, and flow-based generation.

5 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

我们提出了 F3-Tokenizer，一个用于适应音频自动编码器潜在特征的框架 超越重建。归一化自动编码器产生低维， 可解码声潜伏We presented F3-Tokenizer, a framework for adapting audio autoencoder latents beyond reconstruction. The normalized autoencoder produces a low-dimensional, decodable acoustic latentzz用于重建和连续生成， 而表示编码器将相同的潜在流映射到 高维表示for reconstruction and continuous generation, while a representation encoder maps the same latent stream into high-dimensional representationsuu为了理解。这将两者分开 经常处于紧张状态的角色：保留波形级声学细节和 暴露对自回归模型有用的结构。for understanding. This separates the two roles that are often in tension: preserving waveform-level acoustic detail and exposing structure that is useful to an autoregressive model.

培训目标提供了补充约束。重建 保留局部声保真度，同时通道归一化和随机 扰动使瓶颈变得鲁棒，而不依赖于变分 KL 客观。随机量化预测提供纯音频自我监督， freeze-LLM 监督将表示与配对文本对齐，并且 下一个补丁流训练使连续声学目标可预测 自回归隐藏状态。跨越重建、探测和生成 实验中，这些约束产生的表示仍然可解码 同时提高理解并加速下游 TTS 培训。The training objectives provide complementary constraints. Reconstruction preserves local acoustic fidelity, while channel normalization and stochastic perturbation make the bottleneck robust without relying on a variational KL objective. Random-quantized prediction supplies audio-only self-supervision, frozen-LLM supervision aligns the representation with paired text, and next-patch flow training makes the continuous acoustic target predictable from autoregressive hidden states. Across reconstruction, probing, and generation experiments, these constraints produce representations that remain decodable while improving understanding and accelerating downstream TTS training.

仍然存在一些限制。食谱介绍了额外的目标和老师 依赖性，因此性能可以取决于随机量化的覆盖范围 目标、配对文本和冻结法学硕士监督。仅自动编码器的潜在变量可以 也可作为直接发电目标；未来的工作应该研究是否 表示衍生的音频令牌对于更难的生成变得更加重要 Any2Speech 等设置Several limitations remain.
