1 简介Introduction

熵正则化强化学习 (RL) 通过对与参考策略（或默认策略）的偏差进行惩罚来增强奖励目标，从而提高学习过程中的探索、鲁棒性和稳定性Entropy-regularised reinforcement learning (RL) improves exploration, robustness and stability during learning by augmenting the reward objective with a penalty on the divergence from a reference policy (or a default policy)(Haarnoja et al., 2017; Van Niekerk et al., 2019)。熵正则化强化学习源于线性 MDP 提出的. Entropy-regularised RL has its roots in Linear MDPs proposed by(Kappen, 2005; Todorov, 2006, 2009)，共享相同的目标函数。当参考策略设置为均匀随机策略时，它简化为最大熵RL的特殊情况, sharing the same objective function. When the reference policy is set to a uniform random policy, it reduces to the special case of max entropy RL(Ziebart, 2010)。此类方法的核心特征之一是，生成的策略不是学习具有最高累积奖励的单一确定性行为，而是尝试学习执行任务的所有方式，明确最大化相应策略的熵。当我们考虑最优控制和概率推理之间的联系时，这种随机策略是最优的. One of the core features of such methods is that instead of learning a single deterministic behaviour that has the highest cumulative reward, the resulting policies try to learn all of the ways of performing the task, explicitly maximising the entropy of the corresponding policy. Such a stochastic policy is optimal when we consider the connection between optimal control and probabilistic inference(Todorov, 2008).
