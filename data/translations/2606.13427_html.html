摘要。Abstract.

文化服装对视觉检索系统提出了独特的挑战，因为它们的身份通常取决于标准人工智能模型很难捕获的微妙结构和符号细节。我们介绍 VietFashion，这是一个以越南传统服装奥黛为中心的草图文本组成图像检索的新基准。 VietFashion 使设计师和研究人员能够使用传达服装结构的手绘草图和编码文化语义的文本描述相结合来检索具有文化意义的服装。该数据集使用 650 个草图进行初始化，并使用生成模型进行扩展，以生成超过 21,000 个带有对齐标题的逼真图像。描述详细服装属性的文字提示，这些属性摘自时尚杂志，以确保真实性和多样性。为了更好地反映设计意图固有的模糊性，VietFashion采用了多目标检索设置，其中单个查询可能对应于多个有效结果。我们建立标准化的评估协议和基准最先进的组合图像检索方法。实验结果揭示了细粒度文化语义和多模态构成建模方面的显着性能差距，将 VietFashion 定位为细粒度时尚检索的具有挑战性的基准。该数据集可在以下位置公开获取：Cultural garments pose a unique challenge for visual retrieval systems, as their identity often depends on subtle structural and symbolic details that are poorly captured by standard AI models. We introduce VietFashion, a new benchmark for sketch–text composed image retrieval centered on the Ao Dai, a traditional Vietnamese garment. VietFashion enables designers and researchers to retrieve culturally meaningful outfits using a combination of hand-drawn sketches, which convey garment structure, and textual descriptions, which encode cultural semantics. The dataset is initialized with 650 sketches and expanded using generative models to produce over 21,000 photorealistic images with aligned captions. Textual prompts that describe detailed outfit attributes, which are extracted from fashion magazines to ensure authenticity and diversity. To better reflect the inherent ambiguity of design intent, VietFashion adopts a multi-target retrieval setting, where a single query may correspond to multiple valid results. We establish standardized evaluation protocols and benchmark state-of-the-art composed image retrieval methods. Experimental results reveal significant performance gaps in modeling fine-grained cultural semantics and multi-modal composition, positioning VietFashion as a challenging benchmark for fine-grained fashion retrieval. The dataset is publicly available at:https://hng0303.github.io/VietFashion.

1. 简介Introduction

文化遗产的数字化是一项紧迫的任务，但现代图像检索系统在这方面往往存在不足。虽然视觉检索方面已经取得了重大进展The digitalization of cultural heritage is an urgent task, yet modern image retrieval systems often fall short in this area. While significant progress has been made in visual retrieval(Baldrati et al., 2023b; Saito et al., 2023; Baldrati et al., 2023a)，当前系统主要关注西方时尚或广泛的通用类别，如“连衣裙”或“衬衫”, current systems predominantly focus on Western fashion or broad, generic categories like ”dress” or ”shirt”(Wu et al., 2021; Gardères et al., 2025)。因此，他们未能捕捉到传统工艺的高度精细的细节，例如特定的领子剪裁、复杂的刺绣图案和独特的织物窗帘，这些细节定义了一种文化的视觉特征。这种技术差距阻碍了保护这些传统设计的努力，并限制了想要现代化或探索文化服装变体的创意设计师。. As a result, they fail to capture the highly fine-grained details of traditional craftsmanship, such as specific collar cuts, complex embroidery motifs, and unique fabric drapes, that define a culture’s visual identity. This technology gap hinders efforts to preserve these traditional designs and restricts creative designers who want to modernize or explore variations of cultural garments.

这个问题的一个特别有前途的解决方案是草图文本组合图像检索（ST-CIR）A particularly promising solution to this problem is Sketch–Text Compositional Image Retrieval (ST-CIR)(Sangkloy et al., 2022; Gatti et al., 2024)。在此设置中，用户提供手绘草图来建立服装的结构骨架（例如轮廓和布局），并使用文本提示添加文化内涵（例如纹理、颜色和特定图案）。这种组合为设计探索提供了强大的工具，使用户能够搜索难以仅用文字描述或精确绘制的特定文化属性。. In this setting, a user provides a hand-drawn sketch to establish the structural bones of an outfit (e.g., the silhouette and layout) and uses a text prompt to add the cultural flesh (e.g., textures, colors, and specific motifs). This combination offers a powerful tool for design exploration, allowing users to search for specific cultural attributes that are difficult to describe with words alone or draw with perfect accuracy.

然而，推进文化遗产 ST-CIR 面临重大障碍。当前研究中存在显着的架构不匹配；大多数 CIR 模型都针对图像文本输入进行了优化，并且很难将稀疏、抽象的草图与密集、彩色的照片连接起来However, advancing ST-CIR for cultural heritage faces major barriers. There is a significant architecture mismatch in current research; most CIR models are optimized for image-text inputs and struggle to connect sparse, abstract sketches with dense, colorful photos(Baldrati et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2024)。与自然照片不同，草图缺乏密集的像素信息，使得标准视觉语言模型难以提取有意义的特征. Unlike natural photos, sketches lack dense pixel information, making it difficult for standard vision-language models to extract meaningful features(Liu et al., 2017; Sain et al., 2023)。此外，缺乏将草图与基于文化的文本属性配对的高质量数据集。 FashionIQ 等现有数据集主要以西方为中心. Moreover, there is a lack of high-quality datasets that pair sketches with culturally grounded text attributes. Existing datasets like FashionIQ are predominantly Western-centric(Wu et al., 2021)。如果没有将结构草图与细粒度文本修饰符连接起来的基本事实，训练模型来导航复杂的组合查询就变得具有挑战性. Without a ground truth that connects a structural sketch to fine-grained textual modifiers, training models to navigate complex compositional queries becomes challenging(Liu et al., 2021; Baldrati et al., 2023a).

为了解决这一差距，我们推出了 VietFashion，这是一个旨在突破细粒度、基于文化的检索界限的基准。我们专注于越南传统服装奥黛 (Ao Dai)To address this gap, we present VietFashion, a benchmark designed to push the boundaries of fine-grained, culturally-grounded retrieval. We focus on the Ao Dai, the traditional Vietnamese garment(Voyage, ; Travel, )，由于其严格的结构优雅和庞大的基于属性的微妙变化库，它成为一个理想的主题。我们的目标是弥合手工创造力和综合多样性之间的差距。为了确保我们的数据集在文化上准确，我们直接从时尚档案和杂志中策划了丰富的描述符词汇（例如“插肩袖”、“莲花图案”）。这确保了文本组件充当传统设计术语的语言档案。收集数千对传统服装的素描照片是很困难的。我们不依赖有限的网络抓取数据，而是利用生成模型来合成高保真目标图像。我们将草图与 Qwen-2.5（用于文本）和 SANA-ControlNet（用于图像）结合起来，合成 21,000 张逼真的目标图像, which serves as an ideal subject due to its strict structural elegance and a vast library of subtle, attribute-based variations. We aim to bridge the gap between manual creativity and synthetic diversity. To ensure our dataset is culturally accurate, we curated a rich vocabulary of descriptors (e.g., ”raglan sleeves,” ”lotus motifs”) directly from fashion archives and magazines. This ensures that the text component serves as a linguistic archive of traditional design terms. Collecting thousands of sketch-photo pairs for traditional garments is difficult. Rather than relying on limited web-scraped data, we leverage generative models to synthesize high-fidelity target images. We combine sketches with Qwen-2.5 (for text) and SANA-ControlNet (for images) to synthesize 21,000 photorealistic target images(Yang et al., 2025; Xie et al., 2024)。我们创建了一个多目标环境，其中检索模型必须根据微妙的文本线索区分高度相似的图像（图 1）。. We create a multi-target environment where retrieval models must distinguish between highly similar images based on subtle textual cues (Fig.1）。与强制查询只有一个正确答案的旧数据集不同，我们采用类似于 CIRCO 基准的多目标查询设计). Unlike older datasets that force a single correct answer for a query, we adopt a multi-target query design similar to the CIRCO benchmark(Baldrati et al., 2023a)。对于每个查询，我们生成三个不同的有效目标图像。这减少了训练期间的假阴性错误，并更好地模拟了现实世界的设计过程，其中一个概念可以导致多种有效的解释。. For every query, we generate three distinct valid target images. This reduces false-negative errors during training and better models the real-world design process, where one concept can lead to multiple valid interpretations.

为了评估数据集对文化保护和设计的效用，我们建立了严格的基准套件，评估四种不同的检索范例：监督合成图像检索（CIR）To assess the dataset’s utility for cultural preservation and design, we established a rigorous benchmark suite evaluating four distinct retrieval paradigms: supervised composed image retrieval (CIR)(Baldrati et al., 2023b; Saito et al., 2023)、零射击 CIR (ZS-CIR), zero-shot CIR (ZS-CIR)(Gopu and Dunna, 2024; Lin et al., 2023)、草图文本 CIR (ST-CIR), sketch-text CIR (ST-CIR)(Sangkloy et al., 2022)，以及基于草图的图像检索（SBIR）, and sketch-based image retrieval (SBIR)(Eitz et al., 2012; Sangkloy et al., 2016; Dey et al., 2019)。我们的实验表明，即使是有监督的方法，召回率仍然很低。这强调了当前模型在区分细粒度的文化语义（例如特定的刺绣图案或衣领样式）与抽象草图时面临的重大挑战。通过提供一个数据集，其中每个草图都与精确的、属性驱动的修改配对，我们的 VietFashion 提供了一个新标准，用于评估人工智能如何理解钢笔笔划和单词特异性之间的相互作用。. Our experiments reveal that even supervised methods still remain low in recall. This underscores the significant challenge current models face in distinguishing fine-grained cultural semantics, such as specific embroidery patterns or collar styles, from abstract sketches. By providing a dataset where every sketch is paired with precise, attribute-driven modifications, our VietFashion offers a new standard for evaluating how well AI can understand the interplay between the stroke of a pen and the specificity of a word.

尽管多模态检索取得了进展，但现有数据集在应用于特定文化的时尚领域时表现出一些局限性。首先，大多数时尚 CIR 和 SBIR 数据集侧重于西方服装风格，缺乏传统服装的代表性。其次，许多基准假设单目标检索，这在训练过程中产生了歧义，因为视觉上相似的服装可能都是正确的检索结果。第三，现有数据集很少结合传统时尚单品的草图和自然语言查询，限制了现实世界用例中多模态检索模型的评估。Despite the progress in multimodal retrieval, existing datasets exhibit several limitations when applied to culturally specific fashion domains. First, most fashion CIR and SBIR datasets focus on Western clothing styles and lack representation of traditional garments. Second, many benchmarks assume single-target retrieval, which creates ambiguity during training because visually similar garments may all be correct retrieval results. Third, existing datasets rarely combine sketch and natural-language queries for traditional fashion items, limiting the evaluation of multimodal retrieval models in real-world use cases.

These datasets highlight the growing importance of cultural representation in vision-language research. However, they are primarily designed for tasks such as classification, visual understanding, or generative modeling, and do not explicitly address fine-grained retrieval under sketch–text compositional settings.

相比之下，VietFashion 是专门设计作为基于文化的领域中草图文本组成的图像检索的基准。它集成了（i）作为结构查询的草图，（ii）捕获细粒度文化属性的文本描述，以及（iii）模拟现实世界模糊性的多目标检索协议。这种组合将 VietFashion 与一般检索基准和现有文化遗产数据集区分开来，为研究细粒度文化服装检索提供了统一的框架。In contrast, VietFashion is specifically designed as a benchmark for sketch–text composed image retrieval in a culturally grounded domain. It integrates (i) sketches as structural queries, (ii) text descriptions capturing fine-grained cultural attributes, and (iii) a multi-target retrieval protocol that models real-world ambiguity. This combination distinguishes VietFashion from both general retrieval benchmarks and existing cultural heritage datasets, providing a unified framework to study fine-grained cultural garment retrieval.

2.2. 合成图像检索 (CIR)Composed Image Retrieval (CIR)

CIR 的目标是在给定由参考图像和文本修改组成的查询的情况下检索目标图像，从而允许用户表达细粒度的搜索意图，例如属性编辑或样式转换。早期的 CIR 基准测试，例如 FashionIQCIR aims to retrieve a target image given a query composed of a reference image and a textual modification, allowing users to express fine-grained search intent such as attribute editing or style transformation. Early CIR benchmarks such as FashionIQ(Wu et al., 2021)和CIRRand CIRR(Liu et al., 2021)建立了跨时尚和一般对象领域的组合检索的标准评估协议。 FashionIQ 包含大约 18K 个跨服装类别的三元组，并支持基于相对属性的检索，而 CIRR 通过自然语言修改查询将组合检索扩展到开放域对象。established standard evaluation protocols for composed retrieval across fashion and general object domains. FashionIQ contains approximately 18K triplets across clothing categories and supports relative attribute-based retrieval, while CIRR extends composed retrieval to open-domain objects via natural-language modification queries.

尽管早期 CIR 数据集很重要，但其规模和语义多样性有限，这限制了模型泛化和组合推理。为了解决这些限制，最近的研究重点是构建大规模数据集和开发自动注释管道。 FACap 数据集Despite their importance, early CIR datasets are limited in scale and semantic diversity, which restricts model generalization and compositional reasoning. To address these limitations, recent research focuses on constructing large-scale datasets and developing automatic annotation pipelines. The FACap dataset(Gardères et al., 2025)引入了超过 227K 自动构建的时尚 CIR 三元组，使用网络规模图像爬行与视觉语言模型过滤相结合，显着扩展了训练多样性并支持大型模型训练。同样，FIGROTD 基准introduces more than 227K automatically constructed fashion CIR triplets using web-scale image crawling combined with vision-language model filtering, significantly expanding training diversity and enabling large model training. Similarly, the FIGROTD benchmark(Le et al., 2026)引入了图像引导检索任务设置，支持仅草图、仅文本和组合查询，具有大约 16.5K 训练三元组。introduces an image-guided retrieval task setting supporting sketch-only, text-only, and composed queries, with approximately 16.5K training triplets.

另一个重要的发展是多目标 CIR 评估。 CIRCO 数据集Another important development is multi-target CIR evaluation. 5. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 VietFashion 数据集，这是一个针对草图文本组成的图像检索的新颖基准，重点关注越南传统服装奥黛。通过涉及 Qwen-2.5 和 SANA-ControlNet 的多级管道将 650 个人体草图与 21,000 个合成图像集成，我们建立了一个多目标环境，可以有效解决假阴性监督并模拟现实的检索不确定性。我们的基准测试结果显示了显着的性能差距，BLIP4CIR 实现了最先进的 Recall@10 为 0.3703，大约是最佳零样本方法的 9.2 倍。这种差异凸显了预训练模型中抽象草图和细粒度文化图像之间持续存在的领域差距，以及基于微妙文本属性消除视觉上相似轮廓的固有困难。展望未来，未来的研究应优先考虑自监督草图图像预训练和更复杂的融合机制，以增强专业文化领域的细粒度属性基础。In this work, we presented the VietFashion dataset, a novel benchmark for sketch-text composed image retrieval focused on the Vietnamese traditional garment, Ao Dai. By integrating 650 human sketches with 21,000 synthesized images via a multi-stage pipeline involving Qwen-2.5 and SANA-ControlNet, we established a multi-target environment that effectively addresses false-negative supervision and models realistic retrieval uncertainty. Our benchmarking results reveal a significant performance gap, with BLIP4CIR achieving a state-of-the-art Recall@10 of 0.3703, approximately 9.2 times that of the best zero-shot approach. This disparity highlights the persistent domain gap between abstract sketches and fine-grained cultural imagery in pretrained models, as well as the inherent difficulty of disambiguating visually similar silhouettes based on subtle textual attributes. Moving forward, future research should prioritize self-supervised sketch-image pretraining and more sophisticated fusion mechanisms to enhance fine-grained attribute grounding in specialized cultural domains.
