1 简介Introduction

专家混合 (MoE) 已成为扩展大型语言模型 (LLM) 的关键方法Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) has become a key approach for scaling large language models (LLMs)(Shazeer et al. 2017)。与在每一层执行完整前馈网络的密集 Transformer 不同，MoE 模型用多个专家替换每个 FFN 子层，并将每个令牌仅路由到一个小子集，从而在控制每个令牌计算的同时扩展模型容量。最近的型号进一步证明了这一优势：Qwen3.6-35B-A3B. Unlike dense Transformers that execute the full feed-forward network at every layer, MoE models replace each FFN sublayer with multiple experts and route each token to only a small subset, thereby expanding model capacity while controlling per-token computation. Recent models further demonstrate this advantage: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B(Qwen Team 2026)仅激活其 35B 参数中的约 3B，而 DeepSeek-V4-Pro 和 DeepSeek-V4-Flashactivates only about 3B of its 35B parameters, while DeepSeek-V4-Pro and DeepSeek-V4-Flash(DeepSeek-AI et al. 2026)分别采用1.6T/49B和284B/13B的总活跃参数配置。然而，稀疏激活并不能使 MoE 推理本质上高效。大多数基于教育部的法学硕士仍然使用固定顶-adopt total-to-active parameter configurations of 1.6T/49B and 284B/13B, respectively. However, sparse activation does not make MoE inference inherently efficient. Most MoE-based LLMs still use fixed top-kk路由，为每个代币激活相同数量的专家槽，尽管它们的贡献存在很大差异。因此，低贡献专家仍可能被完全执行，从而导致冗余计算和显着的推理开销，特别是在资源受限或内存带宽有限的部署中。routing, activating the same number of expert slots for every token despite substantial variation in their contributions. As a result, low-contribution experts may still be fully executed, causing redundant computation and notable inference overhead, especially in resource-constrained or memory-bandwidth-limited deployments.

为了进一步提高MoE推理效率，现有研究主要通过离线专家压缩或在线动态激活控制来减少专家冗余。离线剪枝合并方法To further improve MoE inference efficiency, existing studies mainly reduce expert redundancy through offline expert compression or online dynamic activation control. Offline pruning and merging methods(Liu et al. 2026b; Bai et al. 2025)在部署之前根据专家使用情况、激活响应、梯度/Fisher 信息或基于搜索的标准修改专家池，从而减少模型大小和常驻内存。 然而，这种静态压缩在所有输入上共享相同的专家结构，并且无法适应路由专家槽的特定于令牌的需求。即使在专家库被压缩之后，剩余的 MoE 层通常仍保留固定的顶部 -modify the expert pool before deployment based on expert usage, activation responses, gradient/Fisher information, or search-based criteria, thereby reducing model size and resident memory. However, such static compression shares the same expert structure across all inputs and cannot adapt to token-specific demands for routed expert slots. Even after the expert pool is compressed, the remaining MoE layers typically retain fixed top-kk路由并执行预设数量的专家，从而使路由集中的动态冗余未得到解决。 动态专家跳过与我们的目标更加一致，因为它在推理过程中自适应地减少了执行的专家槽。一些方法修改或重新训练路由器routing and execute a preset number of experts, leaving dynamic redundancy within the routed set unaddressed. Dynamic expert skipping is more closely aligned with our goal, as it adaptively reduces executed expert slots during inference. Some methods modify or retrain the router(Huang et al. 2024)，或引入额外的决策模块来学习 token-wise 专家预算, or introduce additional decision modules to learn token-wise expert budgets(Zhao et al. 2025)，而训练后方法则根据路由器分数不平衡、基于校准的层敏感性或激活统计确定跳过阈值, while post-training approaches determine skipping thresholds from router-score imbalance, calibration-based layer sensitivity, or activation statistics(Huang et al. 2026)。 尽管这些方法避免了永久删除专家，但他们的决策仍然在很大程度上依赖于路由器置信度或校准相关信号。前者反映相对路由偏好，而不是执行专家的有效转换贡献，而后者需要额外的校准时间前向传递，并且可能对校准大小、域分布和任务转移敏感。 如图. The former reflects relative routing preference rather than the effective transformation contribution of an executed expert, whereas the latter requires additional calibration-time forward passes and may be sensitive to calibration size, domain distribution, and task shift. 为了了解免校准专家槽跳跃的关键挑战，我们从三个角度研究该问题：路由偏好、专家结构和输入方向性。 首先，路由器门仅反映顶层之间的相对分配偏好To understand the key challenges of calibration-free expert-slot skipping, we examine the problem from three perspectives: routing preference, expert structure, and input directionality. First, router gates only reflect the relative assignment preference among the top-kk候选专家，而不是每个路由专家位对 MoE 输出的实际贡献。由于 FFN 结构、RMSNorm 缩放和输出映射的变化，具有相似门值的专家可能会产生截然不同的响应；相反，门较小的专家仍可能通过较强的转化能力做出不可忽视的贡献。因此，仅根据路由器分数跳过专家可能会错误地将路由偏好与专家贡献等同起来。其次，直接根据模型参数估计专家转换能力，无需校准激活或额外训练，这并非易事。 SwiGLU 专家的输出源自其门、向上和向下投影之间的非线性相互作用，无法通过单个矩阵范数、使用频率或全局路由统计来充分表征。最后，专家的贡献本质上取决于方向。全局响应较弱的专家可能仍会在路由器青睐的本地输入区域内表现出较强的行为。因此，可靠的无数据贡献估计器应该捕获每个专家的全局结构能力和路由器编码的方向专业化。candidate experts, rather than the actual contribution of each routed expert slot to the MoE output. Experts with similar gate values may produce substantially different responses due to variations in their FFN structures, RMSNorm scaling, and output mappings; conversely, an expert with a smaller gate may still contribute non-negligibly through stronger transformation capacity. Therefore, skipping experts solely based on router scores may incorrectly equate routing preference with expert contribution. Second, estimating expert transformation capacity directly from model parameters, without calibration activations or additional training, is non-trivial. The output of a SwiGLU expert arises from the nonlinear interaction among its gate, up, and down projections, which cannot be adequately characterized by a single matrix norm, usage frequency, or global routing statistic. Finally, expert contribution is inherently direction-dependent. An expert with weak global responses may still exhibit strong behavior within the local input region favored by the router. A reliable data-free contribution estimator should therefore capture both the global structural capacity of each expert and the directional specialization encoded by the router.

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 ACE，这是一种无需训练且保留检查点的方法，用于 MoE 语言模型中的令牌自适应专家跳过。 GSP 捕获全局 SwiGLU 转换能力，而 RCR 测量路由器条件的定向响应；只有当两种观点都认为贡献较低时，他们的保守融合才会跳过一个位置。在三个 MoE 模型中，ACE 对于激进的预算特别有效。在跳过 50% 时，它在所有三个模型上实现了最佳平均准确度，与最强的竞争对手相比，Qwen3.6 WikiText-2 困惑度降低了 7.96%，并加速了预填充和解码。当前结果使用工作负载匹配的阈值映射和单一确定性运行；未来的工作应该研究阈值转移、重复运行的不确定性和分布式专家调度。We introduced ACE, a training-free and checkpoint-preserving method for token-adaptive expert skipping in MoE language models. GSP captures global SwiGLU transformation capacity, while RCR measures router-conditioned directional response; their conservative fusion skips a slot only when both views find it low-contribution. Across three MoE models, ACE is particularly effective at aggressive budgets. At 50% skipping, it achieves the best average accuracy on all three models, reduces Qwen3.6 WikiText-2 perplexity by 7.96% versus the strongest competitor, and accelerates prefill and decoding. Current results use workload-matched threshold mapping and single deterministic runs; future work should examine threshold transfer, repeated-run uncertainty, and distributed expert dispatch.
