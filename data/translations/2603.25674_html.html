衡量什么是重要的，或者什么是方便的？：基于 LLM 的评分系统构建不相关因素的稳健性Measuring What Matters—or What’s Convenient?: Robustness of LLM-Based Scoring Systems to Construct-Irrelevant Factors

Cole Walsh    Rodica Ivan
摘要Abstract

自动化系统已在教育考试行业广泛采用，用于开放式回答评估和论文评分。这些系统通常达到与训练有素的人类评估者相当或更好的性能水平，但经常被证明容易受到与结构无关的因素（即与评估的结构无关的响应特征）和对抗条件的影响。鉴于大型语言模型在自动评分系统中的使用不断增加，人们重新关注“幻觉”以及这些基于法学硕士的自动评分方法对构建无关因素的稳健性。本研究调查了结构无关因素对基于双架构法学硕士的评分系统的影响，该评分系统旨在对情境判断测试中的短文式开放式回答项目进行评分。研究发现，评分系统对于用无意义的文本、拼写错误和书写复杂性填充响应通常具有鲁棒性。平均而言，重复大段文本会导致系统预测的分数较低，这与之前基于非法学硕士的评分系统的研究结果相矛盾，而偏离主题的回答会受到评分系统的严重惩罚。这些结果为未来基于 LLM 的评分系统在设计时考虑到结构相关性的稳健性提供了令人鼓舞的支持。Automated systems have been widely adopted across the educational testing industry for open-response assessment and essay scoring. These systems commonly achieve performance levels comparable to or superior than trained human raters, but have frequently been demonstrated to be vulnerable to the influence of construct-irrelevant factors (i.e., features of responses that are unrelated to the construct assessed) and adversarial conditions. Given the rising usage of large language models in automated scoring systems, there is a renewed focus on “hallucinations” and the robustness of these LLM-based automated scoring approaches to construct-irrelevant factors. This study investigates the effects of construct-irrelevant factors on a dual-architecture LLM-based scoring system designed to score short essay-like open-response items in a situational judgment test. It was found that the scoring system was generally robust to padding responses with meaningless text, spelling errors, and writing sophistication. Duplicating large passages of text resulted in lower scores predicted by the system, on average, contradicting results from previous studies of non-LLM-based scoring systems, while off-topic responses were heavily penalized by the scoring system. These results provide encouraging support for the robustness of future LLM-based scoring systems when designed with construct relevance in mind.

1 简介Introduction

开放式回答文本（包括简答题和论文）的自动评估是自然语言处理和人工智能（AI）在教育领域最早、探索最广泛的应用之一。随着时间的推移，自动评估书面作品的方法已经从使用手工特征（例如，类型标记比率、词性标记）和简单模型演变而来Automatic evaluation of open-response text, including short-answer responses and essays, is one of the earliest and most widely explored applications of natural language processing and artificial intelligence (AI) in education. Over time, methods for automatically evaluating written work have evolved from using handcrafted features (e.g., type-token ratios, part-of-speech tagging) and simple models[10]更复杂的方法，包括神经网络和基于变压器的模型to more complex approaches including neural networks and transformer-based models[10, 15]。随着每一个新的发展，自动评分系统始终表现出与人类评分者的强烈一致性. With each new development automated scoring systems have consistently demonstrated strong alignment with human raters[10].

尽管其整体性能强劲且被成功采用，但研究表明，许多自动评分系统对构造无关因素（即与评估的构造无关的响应特征）很敏感，无论底层架构如何。这些与结构无关的因素中最主要的是文本长度。涉及简单模型的早期研究表明，在文章中重复句子或整个段落可能会人为地抬高这些模型预测的分数Despite their strong overall performance and successful adoptions, studies have demonstrated the susceptibility of many automated scoring systems to construct-irrelevant factors (i.e., features of responses that are unrelated to the construct assessed) regardless of underlying architecture. Chief among these construct-irrelevant factors is text length; early studies involving simple models demonstrated that repeating sentences or entire paragraphs within an essay could artificially inflate scores predicted by these models[14]，而最近的研究表明，即使在基于变压器的评分系统中，这个问题仍然存在, while more recent studies have demonstrated that this issue persists even in transformer-based scoring systems[15]。其他研究已经证明注入特定单词或短语的巨大影响（例如，“从一个角度”、“另一方面”、“最终分析”）. Other studies have demonstrated outsized impacts of injecting particular words or phrases (e.g., “from one perspective”, “on the other hand”, “in the final analysis”)[14]并包括题外话and including off-topic text[14, 18]。在这种对抗性条件下，现有自动评分系统的脆弱性破坏了人们对这些系统准确测量预期结构而不是可利用代理的信任。. The fragility of existing automated scoring systems under such adversarial conditions undermines trust in these systems to accurately measure the intended construct rather than exploitable proxies.

近年来，自动评分研究开始关注直接采用大型语言模型 (LLM) 的系统In recent years, automated scoring research has begun to focus on systems employing large language models (LLMs) either directly[11]或作为更复杂的评分系统的一部分or as part of more complex scoring systems[1, 17]。虽然这些系统在许多情况下尚未达到基于变压器的系统所达到的最先进的性能指标. While these systems have not yet met state-of-the-art performance metrics achieved by transformer-based systems in many cases[1, 7]，他们在准确性方面的牺牲可以通过其他方式来弥补：创建人类可解释的分数和反馈，减少所需的培训数据量，并简化技术含量较低的评估开发人员的采用。随着基于法学硕士的评分解决方案的兴起，人们重新关注这些系统在对抗条件下的稳健性，特别是公众普遍意识到底层技术的局限性（例如“幻觉”）, what they sacrifice in accuracy they make up for in other ways: creating human-interpretable scores and feedback, reducing the amount of required training data, and easing adoption by less technical assessment developers. With this rise in LLM-based scoring solutions, there is a renewed focus on the robustness of these systems under adversarial conditions, especially with widespread public awareness of the limitations in the underlying technology (e.g., “hallucinations”[3]），而先前的自动评分模式并非如此。), which was not the case with prior modes of automated scoring.

这项研究通过调查以下因素如何影响此类系统产生的分数，有助于我们理解与结构无关的因素对基于 LLM 的评分系统的影响：This study contributes towards our understanding of the effects of construct-irrelevant factors on LLM-based scoring systems by investigating how the following factors influence scores produced by one such system:

  1. 1.

    无意义的文字，Meaningless Text,

  2. 2.

    写作的复杂性，以及Writing Sophistication, and

  3. 3.

    离题文本Off-Topic Texts

通过写作或语言能力的评估，写作复杂性通常被视为与结构相关的因素Writing sophistication is often considered a construct-relevant factor by assessments of writing or language proficiency[1, 13]。正如将在第 2 节中更详细讨论的那样。. As will be discussed in greater detail in Sec.2.1.1，我们构建了基于法学硕士的情境判断测试（SJT）评分系统，旨在衡量个人和专业技能（例如沟通、团队合作、解决问题和批判性思维）, we constructed our LLM-based scoring system for a situational judgment test (SJT) designed to measure personal and professional skills (e.g., communication, teamwork, problem-solving, and critical thinking)[12]其中写作复杂性的元素（例如拼写、语法、结构和组织）被认为与结构无关。那么，本研究的第二个目的是调查构建自动评分系统的可行性，该系统测量与用于测量写作或语言能力的评分系统截然不同的结构。where elements of writing sophistication (e.g., spelling, grammar, structure, and organization) were considered construct-irrelevant. This study, then, serves a secondary purpose of investigating the feasibility of constructing automated scoring systems that measure distinctly different constructs from scoring systems employed for measuring writing or language proficiency.

2.1.1 评估Assessment

我们使用 30 项开放式响应 SJT 收集数据，旨在评估学生的个人和专业技能并提供反馈。该工具主要用于学习计划中的低风险形成性（而不是总结性）使用，从四个维度评估学生的技能We collected data using a 30 item open-response SJT designed to assess and provide feedback on students’ personal and professional skills. This instrument, intended primarily for low-stakes formative (as opposed to summative) usage within a program of study, assessed students’ skills along four dimensions[8]:

  1. 1.

    人际交往能力：Intrapersonal skills:通过认识情绪、偏见和行为来了解和调节自己，保持动力，适应挑战，并致力于终身学习以实现个人成长。Understanding and regulating oneself by recognizing emotions, biases, and behaviors, staying motivated, adapting to challenges, and committing to lifelong learning for personal growth.

  2. 2.

    人际交往能力：Interpersonal skills:通过清晰沟通、表现出同理心、为共同目标而合作以及激励他人取得积极成果，与他人建立有意义的联系并有效合作。Building meaningful connections and working effectively with others by communicating clearly, showing empathy, collaborating toward shared goals, and inspiring others towards positive outcomes.

  4. 4.

    批判性思维和解决问题：Critical Thinking and Problem Solving:收集信息、评估选项并找到有效的问题解决方案，同时有效管理资源和风险。Gathering information, evaluating options, and finding effective solutions to problems while efficiently managing resources and risks.

2.3.1 实验1：无意义的文本Experiment 1: Meaningless Text

我们测试了在原始响应中附加四种类型的无意义文本的影响：We tested the impact of appending four types of meaningless text to the original response:

  1. A.

    原始回复的副本A copy of the original response

  2. B.

    一个句子，说明该项目评估了哪些能力（例如，“这个问题旨在评估协作。”）A sentence stating what competency was assessed by the item (e.g., “This question is designed to assess collaboration.”)

  3. C.

    一句话重述了场景中发生的事情。请参阅第 2 节中的示例。A sentence rephrasing what happened in the scenario. See example in Sec.2.1.1.

  4. D.

    一句公式化的话：“我会以尊重、非对抗、非评判的方式处理这种情况。”A formulaic sentence: “I would approach the situation in a respectful, non-confrontational, and non-judgmental manner.”

我们测试了对原始回复的两种类型的更改，这些更改会影响回复的整体书面质量和复杂程度：We tested two types of alterations to the original responses affecting the overall written quality and sophistication of the responses:

    引入拼写错误Introducing spelling errors

2.3.3 实验 3：离题反应Experiment 3: Off-Topic Responses

在这个实验中，我们研究了我们的自动评分系统如何解释偏离主题的回答（即没有解决向学生提出的场景和问题的回答）。为此，我们从原始数据集中创建了项目-响应组合的随机排列，以便每个响应仍然只表示一次并且与其原始项目不匹配。我们进行了这个实验的两个版本：In this experiment we investigated how off-topic responses (i.e., responses that did not address the scenario and question presented to the student) were interpreted by our automated scoring system. To do this we created random permutations of item-response combinations from our original dataset such that each response was still represented exactly once and was not matched with its original item. We therefore hypothesized that off-topic responses that still exhibited relevant features expected of high quality responses for the competency would receive higher scores than off-topic responses that exhibited features unrelated to the competency.

4 结论Conclusion

本研究调查了基于法学硕士的自动评分系统对构建无关因素的敏感性。我们的结果表明，所调查的评分系统对于用无意义的文本填充响应长度具有鲁棒性，无论是说明项目评估的能力还是重新表述所提供的场景提示。我们还发现，与之前对非 LLM 评分系统的研究结果相比，重复文本通常会对评分系统预测的分数产生有害影响This study investigated the susceptibility of an LLM-based automated scoring system to construct-irrelevant factors. Our results indicated that the scoring system investigated was robust to padding response length with meaningless text either in the form of stating what competency was assessed by an item or rephrasing the scenario prompt provided. We also found that duplicating text generally had deleterious effects on scores predicted by the scoring system, in contrast to results from previous studies of non-LLM-based scoring systems[14, 15]重复的文本会夸大预测分数。鉴于这些结果，基于法学硕士的评分解决方案可能会提供一种克服现有系统对文本长度过度索引的限制的途径。where duplicated text inflated predicted scores. Given these results, LLM-based scoring solutions may offer an avenue for overcoming limitations of existing systems that over-index on text length.

我们还发现，所调查的评分系统对于写作复杂性而言相对稳健。当字符错误率超过 30% 时，拼写错误才开始显着影响系统预测的分数，这一结果与之前对自动内容评分（而非写作或语言能力）稳健性的研究结果一致We also found that the investigated scoring system was relatively robust to writing sophistication. Spelling errors only began to noticeably affect scores predicted by the system at character error rates greater than 30%, a result aligned with previous studies of robustness of automatic content scoring (not writing or language proficiency)[6]。同样，词汇量和反应结构对预测分数的影响很小或没有影响；使用更短和更简单的单词和句子的回答通常获得与使用更复杂和多样化的单词和句子结构的回答相当的分数。相比之下，之前使用人类评分者的研究发现，有拼写错误和没有拼写错误的文本的评分存在很大差异. Vocabulary and response structure, similarly, had very little or no impact on predicted scores; responses employing shorter and simpler words and sentences generally received comparable scores to those using more complex and diverse word and sentence structure. In contrast, previous studies which made use of human raters found large differences in scoring for texts with and without spelling errors[2]。那么，基于人工智能的评分系统可能会在忽略拼写错误和写作复杂性方面提供优于人类评分的优势，这对于 SJT 和其他复杂结构的评估来说是一个特别重要的结论，其中写作复杂性通常与结构无关。. AI-based scoring systems may, then, offer advantages over human rating in ignoring spelling errors and writing sophistication, a particularly important conclusion for SJTs and other assessments of complex constructs where writing sophistication is typically construct-irrelevant.

最后，我们发现偏离主题的回答（即与所问问题的内容无关的回答）通常会受到评分系统的严厉处罚，并且经常被分配尽可能低的分数。虽然这不是本研究的主要目的，但我们也发现了这样的说法，即这里研究的评分系统考虑了不同项目的不同能力相关特征：在一个项目上获得高分的回答类型不一定会在评估不同特征的另一个项目上获得高分。Lastly, we found that off-topic responses (i.e., responses that did not relate to the content of the question asked) generally received large penalties from the scoring system and were frequently assigned the lowest possible score. Though not the main purpose of this study, we also found support for the claim that the scoring system investigated here considers different competency-related features for different items: the kinds of responses that would receive a high score for one item will not necessarily receive a high score for a different item where different features are evaluated.

4.1 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

本研究仅针对一项评估研究了一种基于法学硕士的评分系统。我们建议将这项工作扩展到其他基于法学硕士的评分系统，特别是那些以不同能力使用法学硕士的评分系统。特别是，这里研究的系统使用了双架构系统，该系统采用 LLM 进行特征提取，并保留传统的回归技术进行特征加权。例如，我们发现，该评分系统对于评估有拼写错误的答案非常稳健，这一结果与之前的研究结果一致This study only investigated one LLM-based scoring system for one assessment. We suggest extending this work to other LLM-based scoring systems, particularly those that use LLMs in different capacities. In particular, the system investigated here used a dual-architecture system employing LLMs for feature extraction and maintaining traditional regression techniques for feature weighting. We found, for instance, that this scoring system was robust to the evaluation of responses with spelling errors, a result that aligned with previous research[4, 6]。然而，这些研究提供的证据表明，我们的结论可能不会扩展到采用能力较差模型的基于 LLM 的系统（我们在这里使用 GPT-5.X 模型）. Those same studies provide evidence, however, that our conclusions may not extend to LLM-based systems employing less capable models (we used GPT-5.X models here)[4]。将这项研究扩展到其他系统可以进一步了解各种基于 LLM 的评分技术的优点和局限性。我们还建议所有自动评分系统的开发人员（不仅仅是那些基于法学硕士的开发人员）采用与此处演示的方法类似的方法来调查和分类与结构无关的因素对自动评分系统的影响，以建立公众对这些系统的信任。. Extending this research to other systems can further our understanding of the advantages and limitations of various LLM-based scoring techniques. We also suggest all developers of automated scoring systems (not just those that are LLM-based) adopt a similar approach to the one demonstrated here to investigate and categorize the influence of construct-irrelevant factors on automated scoring systems to build public trust in those systems.

在这项研究中，我们确实注意到了一个与结构无关的特定因素，它可能会影响我们的自动评分系统的行为：使用记忆的公式化短语。我们在这里只研究了一个这样的短语，发现对预测分数的影响非常小。我们之前注意到，这类短语本身可能包含一些与结构相关的信息，但如果频繁、肤浅地使用，可能表明考生有恶意行为。未来的工作应该更深入地研究公式短语在我们的评估和评分系统中的使用，以了解这些短语对评分系统预测的影响程度。In this study, we did note one particular construct-irrelevant factor that could influence the behavior of our automated scoring system: using memorized, formulaic phrases. We only investigated one such phrase here and found only a very small influence on predicted scores. We noted previously that these kinds of phrases on their own may contain some construct-relevant information, but when used frequently and superficially may be indicative of a test taker acting in bad-faith. Future work should more deeply investigate the use of formulaic phrases for our assessment and scoring system to understand the extent that these phrases can influence the predictions of the scoring system.

即使自动评分系统可以被证明对某些与结构无关的因素具有鲁棒性，开发系统来标记“异常”反应仍然有价值Even when automated scoring systems can be demonstrated to be robust to certain construct-irrelevant factors, there is still value in developing systems to flag “unusual” responses[5, 18]。例如，虽然我们的评分系统经常为偏离主题的回答分配最低的可用分数，但它并没有为所有此类回答分配最低的可用分数。因此，虽然提供偏离主题的回答通常对考生没有好处，但我们可能希望具体识别此类回答，以确保为他们分配特定的分数或为决策者提供额外的背景信息。. While our scoring system frequently assigned the lowest available score to off-topic responses, for instance, it did not assign all such responses the lowest available score. So while it would generally not be advantageous to a test taker to provide an off-topic response, we might wish to specifically identify such responses either to ensure they are assigned a specific score or to provide additional context for decision-makers.
