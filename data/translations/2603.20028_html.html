摘要Abstract

软件工程中人工智能的证据仍然严重依赖于个人任务的完成和存储库级问题的解决，而关于人工智能的证据Evidence on AI in software engineering still leans heavily toward individual task completion and repository-level issue solving, while evidence on团队级交付team-level delivery仍然比较稀缺。我们报告了对工业平台 Chiron 的回顾性纵向实地研究，该平台在四个可测量的交付阶段协调人类和人工智能代理：分析、规划、实施和验证。该研究涵盖了三个真实的软件现代化项目——COBOL 银行迁移（约 30k LOC）、大型会计现代化（约 400k LOC）和 .NET/Angular 抵押现代化（约 30k LOC）——在五种交付配置中观察到：传统基准和四个连续的平台版本（V1-V4）。remains comparatively scarce. We report a retrospective longitudinal field study of an industrial platform, Chiron, that coordinates humans and AI agents across four measured delivery stages: analysis, planning, implementation, and validation. The study covers three real software modernization programs – a COBOL banking migration (approximately 30k LOC), a large accounting modernization (approximately 400k LOC), and a .NET/Angular mortgage modernization (approximately 30k LOC) – observed across five delivery configurations: a traditional baseline and four successive platform versions (V1–V4).

纵向轨迹具有丰富的信息。 V1和V2在下游质量恢复之前压缩分析和规划。 V3 和 V4 添加了验收标准验证、存储库本机审查和混合人工代理执行，并且这些后续配置可同时提高交付速度、覆盖范围和下游问题负载。由于该研究是观察性、回顾性的，并且仅限于单个组织，因此结果应该以描述性而非因果性的方式解释。即使在这种保守的解读下，证据也与一个中心论点是一致的：当人工智能嵌入到精心策划的工作流程中而不是仅部署为孤立的编码助手时，代理软件交付的最大收益就会出现。The longitudinal trajectory is informative. V1 and V2 compress analysis and planning before downstream quality recovers. V3 and V4 add acceptance-criteria validation, repository-native review, and hybrid human-agent execution, and those later configurations simultaneously improve delivery speed, coverage, and downstream issue load. Because the study is observational, retrospective, and confined to a single organization, the results should be interpreted descriptively rather than causally. Even under that conservative reading, the evidence is consistent with a central thesis: the largest gains from agentic software delivery appear when AI is embedded in an orchestrated workflow rather than deployed only as an isolated coding assistant.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型明显改变了经济Large language models have clearly changed the economics of本地的local编程工作。受控和现场研究报告了编程任务的更快完成、吞吐量的可测量变化以及跨经验水平和工作环境的异质收益programming work. Controlled and field studies have reported faster completion of programming tasks, measurable changes in throughput, and heterogeneous gains across experience levels and work settings[2, 3, 4]。与此同时，测量文献一再警告说，软件生产力本质上是多维的，不应该被分解为单一的代理. At the same time, the measurement literature has repeatedly cautioned that software productivity is inherently multidimensional and should not be collapsed into a single proxy[1].

这种张力对于人工智能评估很重要。现代软件程序并不是仅通过孤立的代码生成来交付的。它经历了技术理解、规划、分解、实施、验证、审查和发布协调。然而，目前的大部分经验证据仍然集中在个人任务协助上That tension matters for AI evaluation. A modern software program is not delivered by isolated code generation alone. It moves through technical understanding, planning, decomposition, implementation, verification, review, and release coordination. Yet much of the present empirical evidence still focuses either on individual task assistance[2, 3, 4, 6]或存储库级问题解决基准，例如 SWE-bench 和 SWE-agentor on repository-level issue resolution benchmarks such as SWE-bench and SWE-agent[7, 8, 9]。这些评估很重要，但它们本身并不能回答这里感兴趣的管理和科学问题：. Those evaluations are important, but they do not by themselves answer the managerial and scientific question of interest here:当代理嵌入到人类与人工智能混合团队使用的端到端交付工作流程中时，会发生什么？what happens when agents are embedded in an end-to-end delivery workflow used by a hybrid human-AI team?

本文在工业环境中研究了这个问题。我们研究了 Chiron，这是一个团队级软件交付平台，它从以工具为中心的代理使用 (V1) 发展到跨文档、待办事项生成、验收标准验证、存储库本机审查和混合执行的日益集成的编排。该数据集涵盖了三个真实的现代化计划和五种交付配置。与对照试验不同，该研究不会孤立单个成分或模型；相反，它描述了实践中部署的不断发展的交付系统的行为。This paper examines that question in an industrial setting. We study Chiron, a team-level software delivery platform that evolved from tool-centric agent use (V1) toward increasingly integrated orchestration across documentation, backlog generation, acceptance-criteria validation, repository-native review, and hybrid execution. The dataset covers three real modernization programs and five delivery configurations. Unlike a controlled trial, the study does not isolate a single component or model; instead, it characterizes the behavior of an evolving delivery system deployed in practice.

文献给出了直接研究编排的充分理由。项目级开源证据表明，人工智能辅助可以提高产出，同时还可以增加集成时间，这凸显了团队级协调成本不必与个人生产力提升同步变化The literature gives good reason to study orchestration directly. Project-level open-source evidence suggests that AI assistance can improve output while also increasing integration time, highlighting that team-level coordination costs need not move in lockstep with individual productivity gains[5]。存储库级代理研究同样指出了脚手架、接口、上下文管理和验证循环的重要性，而不仅仅是原始模型功能. Repository-level agent research likewise points toward the importance of scaffolding, interfaces, context management, and verification loops rather than raw model capability alone[7, 8, 10, 11, 12]。软件工程之外的长期代理系统也表现出类似的模式：随着工作流程长度的增长，分解和局部纠错变得越来越重要. Long-horizon agentic systems outside software engineering exhibit a similar pattern: decomposition and local error correction become increasingly important as workflow length grows[13].

It does not measure post-release reliability or total lifecycle cost. What it does provide is a carefully reconstructed industrial record showing that progressively more orchestrated human-AI delivery configurations were associated with markedly shorter delivery times, lower downstream issue load, higher first-release coverage, and lower modeled staffing burden within one organization.

Chiron 是一个团队级软件交付平台，而不是一个独立的编码助手。该系统支持存储库摄取、技术堆栈和业务逻辑分析、文档合成、待办事项生成、代理和人工分配执行、基于验收标准的验证、拉取请求生成和存储库本机审查。因此，本文感兴趣的单元不是孤立的模型完成。这是一个从技术理解到经过验证的首次发布的交付计划。Chiron is a team-level software delivery platform rather than a standalone coding assistant. 5 讨论Discussion

5.1 证据支持什么What the evidence supports

该研究支持的最有力的主张是描述性的：在一个组织内，在三个现代化计划中，逐渐更加精心策划的人类人工智能交付配置与更短的交付时间、更低的下游验证阶段问题负载、更高的首次发布覆盖率和更低的建模人员负担相关。The strongest claim supported by the study is descriptive: within one organization, across three modernization programs, progressively more orchestrated human-AI delivery configurations were associated with much shorter delivery times, lower downstream validation-stage issue load, higher first-release coverage, and lower modeled staffing burden.

这种说法是有实质意义的。效果规模很大，它们在所有三个项目中都指向相同的方向，并且后来的版本提高了速度和下游结果，而不是仅仅用一个换另一个。它们还与软件交付的系统级视图保持一致：价值不仅是通过代码生成创造的，还通过构建信息流、分解工作、根据明确的验收标准进行验证以及在错误级联之前审查更改来创造That claim is substantively meaningful. The effect sizes are large, they point in the same direction across all three projects, and the later versions improve both speed and downstream outcomes rather than merely trading one for the other. They also align with a systems-level view of software delivery: value is created not only by code generation, but by structuring information flow, decomposing work, validating against explicit acceptance criteria, and reviewing changes before errors cascade[10, 11, 12, 13].

同时，证据确实At the same time, the evidence does不not证明更有力的因果主张是合理的，例如“只有精心策划才能带来收益”。在 V1 到 V4 中，多项内容可以同时更改：模型功能、提示、上下文构建过程、工作流程成熟度、审核习惯和操作员技能。因此，正确的解释是系统级而不是组件级。justify a stronger causal claim such as “orchestration alone causes the gains.” Across V1 through V4, multiple things can change at once: model capability, prompts, context-construction procedures, workflow maturity, review habits, and operator skill. The correct interpretation is therefore system-level rather than component-level.

5.3 对软件工程中人工智能评估的影响Implications for AI evaluation in software engineering

该研究还强调了更广泛文献的方法论观点。当前的许多评估仍然侧重于存储库级别的问题解决、编码基准或开发人员本地协助The study also highlights a methodological point for the broader literature. Much current evaluation still focuses on repository-level issue solving, coding benchmarks, or developer-local assistance[7, 8, 9]。这些评估仍然很重要，但它们并不能完全表征组织交付绩效。实证文献已经表明，项目层面的协调可能会根据工作环境减弱、放大甚至逆转当地生产率的提高. Those evaluations remain essential, but they do not fully characterize organizational delivery performance. The empirical literature already suggests that project-level coordination can attenuate, amplify, or even reverse local productivity gains depending on the work context[5, 6].

对于工业部署，这意味着至少三个测量规则。For industrial deployment, that implies at least three measurement rules.

首先，观察指标和建模指标应该分开。经过的时间、问题数量和覆盖范围是经验结果；人日和相当于高级日的日是对这些结果的基于情景的解释。First, observed and modeled metrics should be separated. Elapsed time, issue counts, and coverage are empirical outcomes; person-days and senior-equivalent days are scenario-based interpretations of those outcomes.

二是下游品质要阶段性。成熟的交付评估应该报告在审查、验证和发布后发现的缺陷，而不是将它们分解为单一计数。Second, downstream quality should be staged. A mature delivery evaluation should report defects found in review, in validation, and after release rather than collapsing them into a single count.

第三，要慎重选择分母。当干预改变任务粒度时，“每个任务”标准化的指标变得有用但不完美。因此，本文将验证阶段的问题负载视为务实的标准化，而不是明确的质量衡量标准。Third, denominators should be chosen carefully. When an intervention changes task granularity, metrics normalized “per task” become useful but imperfect. The paper therefore treats validation-stage issue load as a pragmatic normalization rather than a definitive quality measure.

6 对有效性和范围条件的威胁Threats to Validity and Scope Conditions

对有效性的主要威胁是结构性的而不是表面性的，应该认真对待。The principal threats to validity are structural rather than cosmetic, and they should be taken seriously.

回顾性重建和来源异质性。Retrospective reconstruction and provenance heterogeneity.

该基准是根据工程记录和从业者回忆回顾性编制的。由于可用的源材料不提供逐个细胞的来源表，因此某些测量可能比其他测量更持久。这种风险主要影响可解释性和不确定性，而不是报告表格的算术。The benchmark was compiled retrospectively from engineering records and practitioner recall. Because the available source material does not provide a cell-by-cell provenance table, some measurements may be more durable than others. This risk mainly affects interpretability and uncertainty, not the arithmetic of the reported tables.

单一组织设置和项目选择。Single-organization setting and project selection.

所有三个项目均来自同一个组织和同一个平台系列。因此，结果可能反映组织特定的工程实践、审查文化或项目组合。外部有效性是有限的。All three programs come from one organization and one platform family. The results may therefore reflect organization-specific engineering practices, review culture, or project mix. External validity is limited.

纵向平台演化。Longitudinal platform evolution.

这些版本是连续的交付配置，而不是孤立的消融。改进可能反映了累积的工作流学习、改进的集成或底层模型和提示的变化。该研究适用于评估平台演变，但不适用于组件级归因。The versions are successive delivery configurations, not isolated ablations. Improvements may reflect cumulative workflow learning, improved integration, or changes in underlying models and prompts. The study is appropriate for evaluating platform evolution, but not for component-level attribution.

任务粒度分母漂移。Task-granularity denominator drift.

代理配置比传统基线产生更细粒度的待办事项。问题负载指标通过标准化每 100 个任务来部分解决这个问题，但一种配置中的任务在语义上不一定等同于另一种配置中的任务。Agentic configurations produce more granular backlogs than the traditional baseline. The issue-load metric addresses this partially by normalizing per 100 tasks, but a task in one configuration is not necessarily semantically equivalent to a task in another.

观察到的下游问题并不完全是缺陷。Observed downstream issues are not total defects.

主要质量指标对达到验证的问题进行计数。 V4 的存储库本机审查可能会将一些问题从该渠道中转移出来，这在操作上很有价值，但意味着该指标捕获缺陷逃逸以进行验证，而不是总体缺陷生成。The main quality metric counts issues reaching validation. V4’s repository-native review likely diverts some issues out of that channel, which is valuable operationally but means the metric captures defect escape to validation rather than total defect generation.

覆盖率是一个发布准备情况指标。Coverage is a release-readiness metric.

首次发布的报道涵盖了最初交付时发布的内容。它不衡量发布后的收敛性、可维护性或总生命周期成本。First-release coverage captures what ships at initial handoff. It does not measure post-release convergence, maintainability, or total lifecycle cost.

建模工作取决于场景。Modeled effort is scenario-dependent.

原始人日和 SEE 日源自人员配置假设，而不是直接的工时日志。敏感性分析减少但并未消除该限制。Raw person-days and SEE-days are derived from staffing assumptions rather than direct labor-hour logs. The sensitivity analysis reduces, but does not eliminate, that limitation.

7 利益冲突和作者立场Conflict of Interest and Author Positionality

两位作者均在开发 Chiron 的 Taller Technologies 公司担任高管职务。这为有利的解释创造了明确的激励。为了减轻这种风险，本文报告了源材料中可用的所有项目版本表，将观察到的数据与建模指标分开，避免因果语言，并包括敏感性分析。本研究中未进行独立的外部审计或盲法裁决。读者应将本文解读为一份具有明确利益冲突披露的工业现场报告。Both authors hold executive roles at Taller Technologies, the company that developed Chiron. This creates clear incentives toward favorable interpretation. To mitigate that risk, the paper reports all project-version tables available in the source material, separates observed from modeled metrics, avoids causal language, and includes sensitivity analyses. No independent external audit or blinded adjudication was performed in the present study. Readers should interpret the paper as an industrial field report with explicit conflict-of-interest disclosure.

8 结论Conclusion

本文报告了对工业人类人工智能软件交付平台的回顾性纵向实地研究，涉及三个真实的现代化计划和五种交付配置。核心结果不仅仅是人工智能可以加速本地编码任务。相反，数据表明，当代理嵌入到结合了分解、验收标准验证、存储库本机审查和混合人工代理执行的结构化交付工作流程中时，就会出现最大的收益。This paper reported a retrospective longitudinal field study of an industrial human-AI software delivery platform across three real modernization programs and five delivery configurations. The core result is not merely that AI can accelerate local coding tasks. Rather, the data suggest that the largest gains appear when agents are embedded in a structured delivery workflow that combines decomposition, acceptance-criteria validation, repository-native review, and hybrid human-agent execution.

在观察到的产品组合中，与传统基线和早期以工具为中心的代理配置 V1 相比，成熟的 V4 配置与更短的测量交付时间、更低的下游验证阶段问题负载、更高的首次发布覆盖率以及更低的建模人员负担相关。即使在保守的描述性解释下，这些效应大小也足够大，实际上也很重要。Within the observed portfolio, the mature V4 configuration is associated with shorter measured delivery time, lower downstream validation-stage issue load, higher first-release coverage, and lower modeled staffing burden than both the traditional baseline and the early tool-centric agentic configuration V1. Those effect sizes are large enough to be practically important even under a conservative descriptive interpretation.

该论文的局限性是真实存在的：追溯出处、单一组织范围、不断变化的任务粒度以及缺乏发布后缺陷数据都限制了可以声明的内容。但这些限制并没有消除核心经验信号。如果用更强大的仪器和更广泛的项目覆盖范围来复制，这些发现将支持该领域评估软件工程人工智能的方式的转变：从孤立的代码生成事件转向精心策划的交付系统的设计和测量。The paper’s limitations are real: retrospective provenance, single-organization scope, changing task granularity, and the absence of post-release defect data all constrain what can be claimed. But those limitations do not erase the central empirical signal. If replicated with stronger instrumentation and broader project coverage, the findings would support a shift in how the field evaluates AI for software engineering: from isolated code-generation events toward the design and measurement of orchestrated delivery systems.
