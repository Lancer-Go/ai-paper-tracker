摘要：Abstract:

基于 LLM 的自主代理在一般推理任务上表现良好，但仍然难以在复杂的现实环境中可靠地使用任务结构、关键约束和先前经验。我们提出了一个基于案例的学习框架，将过去任务的经验转换为可重用的知识资产，允许代理将先前的案例经验转移到新任务并执行更结构化的分析。与主要基于预训练知识或静态提示的方法不同，我们的框架强调从真实案例中提取和重用与任务相关的知识、分析提示和操作技能。我们在六个复杂任务类别的统一基准上评估该方法，并将其与零样本、少样本、检查表提示和规则记忆基线进行比较。结果表明，我们的方法在所有任务中都实现了一致的强劲性能，并且在每种情况下都匹配或优于最佳基线，尤其是在更复杂的任务上取得了特别明显的收益。进一步的分析表明，基于案例的学习的优势随着任务复杂性的增加而增加，并且一个智能体获得的实用知识可以被其他智能体重复使用。这些发现表明，基于案例的学习为构建现实世界工作的专业代理提供了一条有前途的途径。LLM-based autonomous agents perform well on general reasoning tasks but still struggle to reliably use task structure, key constraints, and prior experience in complex real-world settings. We propose a case-based learning framework that converts experience from past tasks into reusable knowledge assets, allowing agents to transfer prior case experience to new tasks and perform more structured analysis. Unlike methods based mainly on pretrained knowledge or static prompts, our framework emphasizes extracting and reusing task-relevant knowledge, analytical prompts, and operational skills from real cases. We evaluate the method on a unified benchmark of six complex task categories and compare it with Zero-Shot, Few-Shot, Checklist Prompt, and Rule Memory baselines. Results show that our method achieves consistently strong performance across all tasks and matches or outperforms the best baseline in every case, with especially clear gains on more complex tasks. Further analysis shows that the advantage of case-based learning increases with task complexity, and that practical knowledge acquired by one agent can be reused by others. These findings suggest that case-based learning offers a promising path for building professional agents for real-world work.

一、简介1. Introduction

近年来，基于LLM的自主智能体在规划、推理和工具使用等开放式任务中表现出了强大的能力，提高了人们对它们最终能够支持科学研究、企业平台管理、生物医学分析和软件工程等复杂专业工作的期望[1,2]。然而，尽管当前的智能体具有一般的推理能力，但在实际的专业环境中常常会失败，特别是在需要精确的程序知识、积累的领域经验和强大的错误恢复的任务上。一个关键原因是专业能力不能仅从静态文本中获得。在实际的研究和工程实践中，专业知识是通过反复解决实际案例、从失败中学习、完善策略和积累可转移的经验而建立的。人类专家不会仅仅通过阅读文档就成为专家；他们通过实践成长。相比之下，当前大多数LLM代理框架仍然将每个任务视为一个孤立的事件，丢弃有用的工作流程、修复策略、故障模式和执行后的实际见解，而不是将它们转化为可重用的知识[3]。In recent years, LLM-based autonomous agents have shown strong capabilities in openended tasks such as planning, reasoning, and tool use, raising expectations that they may eventually support complex professional work in scientific research, enterprise platform management, biomedical analysis, and software engineering[1,2] . However, despite their general reasoning ability, current agents often fail in real professional settings, especially on tasks that require precise procedural knowledge, accumulated domain experience, and robust error recovery. A key reason is that professional competence cannot be acquired from static text alone. In real research and engineering practice, expertise is built through repeatedly solving real cases, learning from failures, refining strategies, and accumulating transferable experience. Human experts do not become experts simply by reading documentation; they grow through practice. In contrast, most current LLM agent frameworks still treat each task as an isolated event, discarding useful workflows, repair strategies, failure patterns, and practical insights after execution rather than turning them into reusable knowledge[3] .

这种限制在人工智能平台管理、科学分析和自动化系统治理等复杂领域尤其明显，这些领域的成功往往取决于难以直接从预训练语料库中获得的隐性操作知识。现有的方法，包括提示、检索增强生成、小样本学习和基于记忆的反思，只能部分解决这一差距[4-6]。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了基于案例的学习（CBL），这是一个框架，在这个框架中，智能体通过主动总结他们所学到的知识并将经验存储为结构化记忆来从现实世界的任务案例中学习。这些记忆还可以在代理之间转移，从而使经验可以共享和继承。具体来说，CBL将获得的经验组织成四个互补的模块：固定领域知识、系统级提示约束、技能模板和This limitation is especially evident in complex domains such as AI platform management, scientific analysis, and automated system governance, where success often depends on tacit operational knowledge that is difficult to obtain directly from pretraining corpora. 2.1 基于LLM的自治代理2.1 LLM-based Autonomous Agents

随着用于语言理解和生成的大型语言模型（LLM）的最新进展，研究人员越来越多地探索它们作为解决复杂任务的代理的潜力[3,7]。与传统对话系统不同，自主代理不仅必须生成文本，还必须规划任务、使用工具、与环境交互并根据反馈调整其行为[1,8]。With recent advances in large language models (LLMs) for language understanding and generation, researchers have increasingly explored their potential as agents for solving complex tasks[3,7] . Unlike traditional dialogue systems, autonomous agents must not only generate text, but also plan tasks, use tools, interact with environments, and adapt their behavior based on feedback[1,8] .

ReAct 等框架首先将推理和操作结合在迭代循环中，提高复杂任务的性能[9]。 Toolformer 进一步表明，模型可以学习何时调用外部工具，例如计算器、搜索引擎和 API [10]。随后的工作进一步将这一方向扩展到工具连接和系统级代理，能够在更开放的设置中编排 API、模型和外部服务[11-13]。后来的系统，包括 AutoGPT，将这一工作线扩展到多步骤自主规划，使代理能够分解目标、管理子任务和执行长期工作流程[14,15]。随后引入了 AgentBench 和 GAIA 等基准来评估智能体在推理、工具使用、多轮交互和任务完成等方面的能力[16,17]。Frameworks such as ReAct first combined reasoning and acting in an iterative loop, improving performance on complex tasks[9] . Toolformer further showed that models can learn when to call external tools such as calculators, search engines, and APIs[10] . Subsequent work further expanded this direction toward tool-connected and system-level agents capable of orchestrating APIs, models, and external services in more open-ended settings[11-13] . Later systems, including AutoGPT extended this line of work toward multi-step autonomous planning, enabling agents to decompose goals, manage subtasks, and execute long-horizon workflows[14,15] . Benchmarks such as AgentBench and GAIA were then introduced to evaluate agent capabilities in reasoning, tool use, multi-turn interaction, and task completion[16,17] .

尽管这些研究极大地提高了自主代理的一般任务解决能力，但它们仍然主要集中在单个任务中的规划、推理、工具使用和交互[18]。相比之下，人们对更多内容的关注要少得多。Although these studies have substantially improved the general task-solving ability of autonomous agents, they remain focused mainly on planning, reasoning, tool use, and interaction within individual tasks[18] . 为了克服智能体在每个新任务上有效地从头开始的限制，越来越多的研究将长期记忆机制引入到基于 LLM 的智能体中，使它们能够保留过去的经验并在未来的任务中重用它[3,19-21]。生成代理提出了记忆流的概念，其中根据重要性、相关性和新近度存储过去的事件，并检索相关经验以供以后决策。这项工作表明，持久记忆可以显着提高行为一致性和长期连贯性。 MemoryBank 受到认知心理学艾宾浩斯遗忘曲线的启发，通过基于时间的衰减来模拟记忆强度，允许代理保留高价值信息，同时逐渐丢弃不太有用的内容[20]。 Reflexion进一步提出，智能体在任务失败后进行自我反思，将语言摘要转化为后续决策的反馈，从而通过失败驱动学习进行改进[6]。反过来，ExpeL 尝试从过去的任务中提取更高层次的经验教训，以指导未来的小样本推理[22]。To overcome the limitation that agents effectively start from scratch on each new task, a growing body of research has introduced long-term memory mechanisms into LLM-based agents, enabling them to retain past experience and reuse it in future tasks[3,19-21] . Generative Agents proposed the concept of a memory stream, in which past events are stored according to importance, relevance, and recency, and relevant experiences are retrieved for later decisionmaking. This work showed that persistent memory can substantially improve behavioral consistency and long-term coherence. MemoryBank, inspired by the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve from cognitive psychology, models memory strength with time-based decay, allowing agents to preserve high-value information while gradually discarding less useful content[20] . Reflexion further proposed that agents perform self-reflection after task failure, converting linguistic summaries into feedback for subsequent decisions and thereby improving through failure-driven learning[6] . ExpeL, in turn, attempts to abstract higher-level lessons from past tasks to guide future few-shot reasoning[22] .

2.3 基于案例的推理和经验重用2.3 Case-Based Reasoning and Experience Reuse

基于案例的推理（CBR）是人工智能领域的一个经典研究方向[24,25]。其核心思想是，当面临新问题时，系统可以检索过去类似的案例，并利用它们的解决方案来指导当前的决策。传统的CBR通常涉及四个步骤：检索相似案例、重用现有解决方案、修改或调整它们以及保留新体验。这种想法在人类专家决策中也很普遍。例如，医生参考以前的临床案例，工程师借鉴历史故障处理策略，研究人员总结以前实验的教训。Case-Based Reasoning (CBR) is a classic research direction in artificial intelligence[24,25] . Its core idea is that, when facing a new problem, a system can retrieve similar past cases and use their solutions to guide current decision-making. Traditional CBR typically involves four steps: retrieving similar cases, reusing existing solutions, revising or adapting them, and retaining the new experience. This idea is also widespread in human expert decision-making. For example, physicians refer to prior clinical cases, engineers draw on historical faulthandling strategies, and researchers summarize lessons from previous experiments.

近年来，随着法学硕士的发展，研究人员对案例驱动学习对于智能体的价值重新产生了兴趣[1,3,22]。然而，大多数现有工作仍然停留在少镜头示例、演示提示或检索示例的水平[4,5,26]。换句话说，历史案例主要用作上下文示例，而不是作为代理总结通过完成这些案例所学到的知识的机会。In recent years, with the development of LLMs, researchers have renewed interest in the value of case-driven learning for agents[1,3,22] . However, most existing work still remains at the level of few-shot examples, demonstration prompting, or retrieval examples[4,5,26] . In other words, historical cases are mainly used as contextual examples, rather than as opportunities for agents to summarize what they have learned through completing those cases.

本文提出的基于案例的学习（CBL）与传统的小样本提示的本质区别在于，我们的目标不是呈现过去的答案，而是提炼从过去案例中学到的专业知识。换句话说，案例不是示范文本；而是案例。它们是学习的源泉，经验被转化为可重复使用的知识资产。这使得 CBL 更接近于一种职业成长机制，而不仅仅是一种示例检索形式。The essential difference between the Case-Based Learning (CBL) proposed in this paper and traditional few-shot prompting is that our goal is not to present past answers, but to distill the professional knowledge learned from past cases. Put differently, cases are not demonstration texts; they are sources of learning, and experience is transformed into reusable knowledge assets. This makes CBL closer to a mechanism for professional growth rather than simply a form of example retrieval.

2.4 上下文增强推理2.4 Context-Augmented Reasoning

为了弥补法学硕士参数知识的限制，许多研究采用了上下文增强策略，在推理过程中动态注入外部信息[27-29]。最具代表性的方法是检索增强生成（RAG），它通过从外部文档集合中检索相关知识来补充模型的上下文[30]。此外，few-shot提示有助于模型通过示例学习任务格式，而Chain-of-Thought及其后来的变体改进了中间推理的结构和执行[31-34]。To compensate for the limits of parametric knowledge in LLMs, many studies have adopted context augmentation strategies that dynamically inject external information during inference[27-29] . The most representative approach is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), which supplements the model’s context by retrieving relevant knowledge from external document collections[30] . In addition, few-shot prompting helps models learn task formats through examples, while Chain-of-Thought and its later variants improve the structure and execution of intermediate reasoningf[31-34] .

我们的工作在一个关键方面不同于 RAG 风格的方法：RAG 检索外部文档，而 CBL 重用代理本身获得的实践经验[5,26]。从这个意义上说，CBL 可以被视为一种情境增强形式，更明确地立足于从行动中学习。Our work differs from RAG-style approaches in one key respect: RAG retrieves external documents, whereas CBL reuses practical experience acquired by the agent itself[5,26] . In this sense, CBL can be viewed as a form of context augmentation that is more explicitly grounded in learning from action.

2.5 课程学习和渐进式能力发展2.5 Curriculum Learning and Progressive Capability Development

课程学习首先由 Bengio 等人提出[35]。其核心思想是通过允许模型从更简单的任务到更困难的任务逐步学习来模仿人类的教育过程。该策略已广泛应用于深度学习训练、强化学习任务调度、多阶段技能开发等领域。在认知科学中，它也对应于维果茨基的“最近发展区”概念，该概念表明学习者在面对适当的挑战性任务时进步最有效[36]。Curriculum Learning was first proposed by Bengio and colleagues[35] . Its core idea is to mimic the human educational process by allowing models to learn progressively from easier to more difficult tasks. This strategy has been widely applied in deep learning training, reinforcement learning task scheduling, and multi-stage skill development. In cognitive science, it also corresponds to Vygotsky’s concept of the Zone of Proximal Development, which suggests that learners improve most efficiently when faced with appropriately challenging tasks[36] .

本工作中的课程调度器（M_C）借鉴了这一思想，根据案例难度组织历史经验的注入，从而帮助智能体更稳定地吸收知识。尽管目前的实验规模仍然有限，但这一设计为未来逐步培养专家级智能体的工作指明了一个重要方向。The curriculum scheduler (M_C) in this work draws on this idea by organizing the injection of historical experience according to case difficulty, thereby helping the agent absorb knowledge more stably. Although the current experimental scale is still limited, this design points to an important direction for future work on progressively cultivating expert-level agents.

2.6 本作品的定位与特色2.6 Positioning and Distinction of This Work

总体而言，这项工作位于几个研究方向的交叉点：基于法学硕士的自主代理、长期记忆机制、基于案例的学习、情境增强推理和课程学习[5,25]。同时，它在一些重要方面与现有方法不同。与传统的代理研究相比，我们注重长期的专业成长而不是单一任务的执行。与记忆架构研究相比，我们更强调结构化的经验组织和知识迁移[19,20]。与RAG相比，我们重用的是实践经验而不是外部文档。与少样本提示相比，我们将经验提炼成可重用的知识，而不是简单地提供示例答案。与传统的CBR相比，我们研究的是专业代理人的演变，而不是静态的案例匹配。Overall, this work lies at the intersection of several research directions: LLM-based autonomous agents, long-term memory mechanisms, case-based learning, context-augmented reasoning, and curriculum learning[5,25] . At the same time, it differs from existing approaches in important ways. Compared with conventional agent research, we focus on long-term professional growth rather than single-task execution. Compared with memory architecture research, we emphasize structured experience organization and knowledge transfer[19,20] . Compared with RAG, we reuse practical experience rather than external documents. Compared with few-shot prompting, we distill experience into reusable knowledge rather than simply presenting example answers. Compared with traditional CBR, we study the evolution of professional agents rather than static case matching.

因此，本文的核心贡献是提出了一种基于案例的学习机制，用于形成现实世界的专业能力，使自主代理能够像人类专家一样通过实践学习，通过案例成长，并通过经验迁移不断发展。The central contribution of this paper, therefore, is the proposal of a case-based learning mechanism for the formation of real-world professional capability, enabling autonomous agents to learn through practice, grow through cases, and continuously evolve through experience transfer, much like human experts.

通常不会只关注最终结果；它们还反思了哪些操作路径是有效的，哪些错误应该避免，哪些程序值得重复使用，以及哪些经验教训可以用于解决未来的问题。这些总结出来的实践经验逐渐成为专业知识。typically do not focus only on the final outcome; they also reflect on which operational paths were effective, which errors should be avoided, which procedures are worth reusing, and which lessons can be transferred to future problems. These summarized practical experiences gradually become professional knowledge.

相比之下，大多数现有的LLM代理框架仍然将每个任务执行视为一个孤立的事件。任务完成后，在执行过程中发现的有效工作流程、用于错误恢复的方法以及从失败案例中汲取的经验教训通常会被丢弃，而不是转化为未来任务可重用的知识资产。因此，即使代理人反复遇到类似的问题，他们也常常需要从头开始重新寻找解决路径，缺乏真正积累经验的能力。In contrast, most existing LLM agent frameworks still treat each task execution as an isolated event. After a task is completed, the effective workflows discovered during execution, the methods used for error recovery, and the lessons learned from failure cases are usually discarded rather than transformed into reusable knowledge assets for future tasks. As a result, even when agents repeatedly encounter similar problems, they often need to rediscover solution paths from scratch, lacking any true capacity for cumulative experience.

为了解决这一限制，CBL 使智能体能够通过完成现实世界的案例来主动学习，并将所学到的知识转化为可重用和可转移的专业知识，以用于未来的任务。在整个工作流程中，当座席收到新任务时，系统首先从现有案例记忆库中检索相关经验，包括领域背景知识、历史失败教训、经过验证的成功技能和有序学习案例。然后，这些检索到的经验与当前任务描述一起组装成增强的提示上下文，提供给 LLM 代理以执行任务。任务完成后，系统进一步分析执行过程，识别哪些策略成功、哪些错误发生、哪些规则值得保留，然后相应更新内存模块以支持未来的任务。通过案例执行→经验总结→记忆巩固→复用的闭环，每一个完成的真实案例都会产生一个学习增量，让座席逐步发展出更稳定的专业能力。To address this limitation, CBL enables agents to actively learn by completing real-world cases and to convert what they learn into reusable and transferable professional knowledge for future tasks. In the overall workflow, when an agent receives a new task, the system first retrieves relevant experience from an existing case memory bank, including domain background knowledge, historical failure lessons, verified successful skills, and ordered learning cases. These retrieved experiences are then assembled together with the current task description into an enhanced prompt context, which is provided to the LLM agent for task execution. After the task is completed, the system further analyzes the execution process to identify which strategies succeeded, which errors occurred, and which rules are worth preserving, and then updates the memory modules accordingly to support future tasks. Through this closed loop of case execution → experience summarization → memory consolidation → reuse, each completed real-world case produces a learning increment, allowing the agent to gradually develop more stable professional capability.

3.2 四种结构化体验记忆模块3.2 Four Types of Structured Experience Memory Modules

为了确保案例经验能够可靠存储和有效重用，CBL将代理获得的知识组织成四个互补的结构化记忆模块，每个模块捕获专业能力的不同组成部分。固定领域知识（M_F）存储任务领域中稳定的背景知识，例如API规范、数据结构定义、平台约束和系统规则，为代理提供对专业环境如何运行的基本了解。系统提示约束（M_S）记录从历史失败案例中提取的行为规则，例如避免过早的接口调用、终止无效循环或在模式验证失败后重新检查格式。通过积累这些约束，代理可以避免重复常见错误并更稳健地执行任务。To ensure that case experience can be stored reliably and reused effectively, CBL organizes the knowledge acquired by the agent into four complementary structured memory modules, each capturing a different component of professional capability. Fixed Domain Knowledge (M_F) stores stable background knowledge in the task domain, such as API specifications, data structure definitions, platform constraints, and system rules, providing the agent with a basic understanding of how the professional environment operates. System Prompt Constraints (M_S) records behavioral rules distilled from historical failure cases, such as avoiding premature interface calls, terminating invalid loops, or rechecking formats after schema validation failure. By accumulating these constraints, the agent can avoid repeating common errors and execute tasks more robustly.

此外，技能库（M_K）将重复成功的操作抽象为可重用的方法模板，例如配置矢量数据库工作流程、执行模式验证或生成结构化评估报告。当未来任务中出现类似的子任务时，可以调用这些技能。最后，课程组织者（M_C）引入了根据难度组织过去的案例来进行课程学习的思想[9]。首先提供更简单、更可靠的案例，然后提供更复杂的案例，让代理人以更稳定和渐进的方式吸收历史经验。这四个模块共同使 CBL 能够比非结构化记忆方法更有效地保存、组织和重用实践经验。In addition, Skill Library (M_K) abstracts repeatedly successful operations into reusable method templates, such as configuring vector database workflows, performing schema validation, or generating structured evaluation reports. These skills can then be invoked when similar subtasks appear in future tasks. Finally, Curriculum Organizer (M_C) introduces the idea of curriculum learning by organizing past cases according to difficulty[9] . Easier and more reliable cases are provided first, followed by more complex ones, allowing the agent to absorb historical experience in a more stable and progressive manner. Together, these four modules enable CBL to preserve, organize, and reuse practical experience more effectively than unstructured memory approaches.

3.3 体验更新机制3.3 Experience Update Mechanism

与静态提示方法相比，CBL 的一个关键优势是，在完成每个案例后，代理真正学到了新的东西。为了实现这种能力，系统引入了经验更新功能，即记忆更新功能，它可以在每次任务完成后自动总结学习成果。A key advantage of CBL over static prompting methods is that, after completing each case, the agent genuinely learns something new. To realize this capability, the system introduces an experience update function, φ (the memory update function), which automatically summarizes learning outcomes after each task.

具体来说，φ首先分析执行日志以检测任务期间发生的故障模式，例如超时、运行时错误、空白输出、循环检测和模式验证失败。然后，系统将这些错误分类为标准化的故障类型，并根据其原因自动生成新的行为建议。例如，当检测到模式验证失败时，系统可以提取诸如“应重新检查字段格式”之类的规则。最后，这些新获得的经验被写入相应的记忆模块中，从而更新现有的知识库。Specifically, φ first analyzes execution logs to detect failure patterns that occur during the task, such as timeout, runtime error, blank output, loop detection, and schema validation failure. The system then classifies these errors into standardized failure types and automatically generates new behavioral suggestions based on their causes. For example, when schema validation failure is detected, the system may extract a rule such as “field formats should be rechecked.” Finally, these newly acquired experiences are written into the corresponding memory modules, thereby updating the existing knowledge base.

3.4 经验传递和跨智能体知识共享3.4 Experience Transfer and Cross-Agent Knowledge Sharing

CBL 超越现有内存架构的最重要的扩展之一是它强调经验的可转移性。由于所有学习成果在结构上都表示为自然语言知识模块，因此获得的经验不仅限于原始学习者，而是可以被其他智能体直接读取和使用。这意味着一个代理人在真实案例中遇到的陷阱可以被其他代理人提前避免；一名特工掌握的成功技能可以被其他特工直接继承；一个智能体总结的行为规则可以成为整个群体共享的专业知识。One of the most important extensions of CBL beyond existing memory architectures is its emphasis on the transferability of experience. Because all learning outcomes are structurally represented as natural-language knowledge modules, the acquired experience is not limited to the original learner, but can be directly read and used by other agents. This means that pitfalls encountered by one agent in real cases can be avoided in advance by others; successful skills mastered by one agent can be directly inherited by others; and behavioral rules summarized by one agent can become shared professional knowledge for the entire group.

换句话说，CBL产生的不仅仅是个体记忆，而是一种可共享、可传承的专业知识资产。这种机制对于在复杂领域构建专家级代理尤其重要，因为它不仅可以通过单个代理的孤立探索来提高自主代理能力，还可以通过经验转移来积累集体知识。In other words, what CBL produces is not merely individual memory, but a form of professional knowledge asset that is shareable and inheritable. This mechanism is particularly important for building expert-level agents in complex domains, because it allows improvements in autonomous agent capability to emerge not only from isolated exploration by individual agents, but also from collective knowledge accumulation through experience transfer.

4. 实验设置4. Experimental Setup

4.1 任务设计与案例构建4.1 Task Design and Case Construction

为了系统地评估智能体在复杂的现实世界任务中的分析能力和可迁移性，我们构建了一个由六个代表性任务类别组成的案例集。这些任务取自真实系统开发和部署中的高复杂度场景，要求代理不仅提供答案，还需要执行结构化诊断、因果分析和可执行解决方案生成。与传统的单轮 QA 基准[16,17] 不同，我们的任务强调分析过程是否完整、是否尊重关键约束以及代理是否可以生成在实际工作流程中有用的修复或治理策略。To systematically evaluate the analytical ability and transferability of agents in complex real-world tasks, we construct a case set consisting of six representative task categories. These tasks are drawn from high-complexity scenarios in real system development and deployment, requiring agents not only to provide answers, but also to perform structured diagnosis, causal analysis, and executable solution generation. Unlike traditional single-turn QA benchmarks[16,17] , our tasks emphasize whether the analysis process is complete, whether key constraints are respected, and whether the agent can produce repair or governance strategies that are useful in real workflows.

这六个任务类别涵盖了当前LLM应用中的几个主要范式，包括研究辅助工具编排、RLHF训练分析[37-39]、具有访问控制的企业多源RAG[5]、浏览器代码数据库长视野操作、具有分阶段审查漂移的在线偏好学习以及多智能体科学发现平台[40,41]。这些任务在结构、约束和评估标准上存在很大差异，形成了以复杂场景为中心的异构基准。有些任务是相对结构化的工程问题，具有明确的评估标准，而另一些任务是多阶段、多约束、更加开放的，使我们能够在不同复杂程度下测试基于案例的学习的有效性。The six task categories cover several major paradigms in current LLM applications, including research-assistant tool orchestration, RLHF training analysis[37-39] , enterprise multisource RAG with access control[5] , browser-code-database long-horizon operations, online preference learning with staged review drift, and multi-agent scientific discovery platforms[40,41] . These tasks differ substantially in structure, constraints, and evaluation criteria, forming a heterogeneous benchmark centered on complex scenarios. Some tasks are relatively structured engineering problems with clear evaluation standards, while others are multi-stage, multiconstraint, and more open-ended, allowing us to test the effectiveness of case-based learning across different levels of complexity.

每个任务类别都被实例化为围绕实际故障模式构建的独立案例。例如，工具编排任务涉及循环调用、预算超支或参数错误等问题； RLHF 任务包括随着人类偏好下降、KL 漂移或训练不稳定而提高奖励；多智能体科学发现任务涉及共享内存、新颖性约束、重复探索抑制和外部基准验证。通过这种方式，可以根据具有清晰工程背景的实际分析请求而不是抽象问题来评估模型。所有任务都遵循统一的评估协议：每个任务包括三轮测试，每轮包含五个提示变体，Each task category is instantiated as an independent case built around realistic failure modes. For example, tool-orchestration tasks involve issues such as looping calls, budget overruns, or parameter errors; RLHF tasks include reward improvement with declining human preference, KL drift, or training instability; and multi-agent scientific discovery tasks involve shared memory, novelty constraints, repeated-exploration suppression, and external benchmark validation. In this way, the model is evaluated on realistic analytical requests with clear engineering context rather than on abstract problems. All tasks follow a unified evaluation protocol: each task includes three test rounds, and each round contains five prompt variants,

导致每个任务有十五个评估样本。这种设计减少了提示措辞的影响，并提供了对任务级绩效的更稳定的估计。resulting in fifteen evaluation samples per task. This design reduces the influence of prompt phrasing and provides a more stable estimate of task-level performance.

4.2 方法和基线4.2 Methods and Baselines

为了评估基于案例的学习的有效性，我们将我们的方法与反映常见法学硕士分析范式的四个代表性基线进行比较。第一个是零射击，它完全依赖于模型的预训练能力[42]。第二个是Few-Shot，它提供了少量示例来指导结构化输出生成[4]。三是清单提示，将任务分解为固定的步骤，以约束推理过程。第四个是规则记忆，它显式地注入通用规则以提高输出的一致性和稳定性。To evaluate the effectiveness of case-based learning, we compare our method with four representative baselines that reflect common LLM analysis paradigms. The first is Zero-Shot, which relies entirely on the model’s pretrained capabilities[42] . The second is Few-Shot, which provides a small number of examples to guide structured output generation[4] . The third is Checklist Prompting, which decomposes the task into fixed steps to constrain the reasoning process. The fourth is Rule Memory, which explicitly injects general rules to improve output consistency and stability.

我们的方法与这些基线的不同之处在于引入了案例经验，允许模型使用从先前任务中积累的知识结构来分析新任务。这些经验并不是简单的示例串联，而是以结构化的形式组织起来，包括任务相关知识、分析提示模板以及可复用的操作技巧。Our method differs from these baselines by introducing case experience, allowing the model to analyze new tasks using knowledge structures accumulated from prior tasks. These experiences are not simple example concatenations, but are organized in structured form, including task-relevant knowledge, analytical prompt templates, and reusable operational skills.

4.3 评估指标和成功标准4.3 Evaluation Metrics and Success Criteria

由于六个任务类别的目标和约束不同，我们采用由特定任务的主要指标和共享的辅助指标组成的多层次评估框架。在任务级别，为每种案例类型分配一个与其目标直接一致的核心指标。例如，研究辅助工具编排通过任务完成情况以及是否满足预算和模式约束来评估； RLHF 训练分析强调保留偏好的准确性、训练稳定性以及不存在 NaN 或 KL 异常；企业多源RAG重点关注权限违规、引用一致性和接地质量；多主体科学发现是通过外部基准的实际收益来评估的。Because the six task categories differ in objectives and constraints, we adopt a multi-level evaluation framework consisting of task-specific primary metrics and shared auxiliary metrics. At the task level, each case type is assigned a core metric directly aligned with its objective. For instance, research-assistant tool orchestration is evaluated by task completion and whether budget and schema constraints are satisfied; RLHF training analysis emphasizes held-out preference accuracy, training stability, and the absence of NaN or KL anomalies; enterprise multi-source RAG focuses on permission violations, citation consistency, and grounding quality; and multi-agent scientific discovery is evaluated by real gains on external benchmarks.

在一般层面上，我们还报告跨任务的共享统计指标，包括平均得分、成功率、平均推理时间、平均令牌使用和错误计数。这些指标不仅捕获任务性能，还捕获效率和系统稳定性。仅当任务的主要指标和关键辅助约束都满足预定义阈值时，任务才算成功。换句话说，模型必须做的不仅仅是生成一个表面上合理的答案；它还必须满足任务的核心约束和治理要求。这使得评估更加符合现实世界的系统标准。At the general level, we additionally report shared statistical metrics across tasks, including mean score, success rate, mean reasoning time, mean token usage, and error count. These metrics capture not only task performance, but also efficiency and system stability. A task is counted as successful only when both its primary metric and key auxiliary constraints meet predefined thresholds. In other words, the model must do more than generate a superficially plausible answer; it must also satisfy the task’s core constraints and governance requirements. This makes the evaluation more closely aligned with real-world system standards.

4.4 实验流程及实施细节4.4 Experimental Procedure and Implementation Details

所有方法均在统一程序下进行评估。对于每个测试样本，模型接收任务描述和输入提示，然后生成完整的分析和解决方案。标准化后，输出被传递到评估模块，该模块计算特定于任务的指标和共享指标。All methods are evaluated under a unified procedure. For each test sample, the model receives the task description and input prompt, then produces a complete analysis and solution. After normalization, the output is passed to the evaluation module, which computes both taskspecific and shared metrics.

为了确保公平比较，实验严格控制几个因素。所有方法都使用相同的模型配置和推理参数。提示方案在不同方法之间保持一致，以保持样本水平的可比性。评估脚本在所有设置之间完全共享，确保统一的评分标准。此外，还记录推理时间和令牌消耗，以分析方法之间的计算成本差异。总体而言，该实验框架平衡了任务复杂性、方法可比性和评估一致性，为结果分析提供了可靠的基础。重要的是，这六个任务类别并​​不是简单地组合在一起，而是简单地组合在一起。它们形成了一个渐进的评估范围，从结构化工程任务到高度复杂的开放式任务。To ensure fair comparison, the experiments tightly control several factors. All methods use the same model configuration and inference parameters. The prompt protocol is kept consistent across methods to preserve sample-level comparability. The evaluation scripts are fully shared across all settings, ensuring a unified scoring standard. In addition, reasoning time and token consumption are recorded to analyze computational cost differences between methods. Overall, this experimental framework balances task complexity, methodological comparability, and evaluation consistency, providing a reliable basis for the results analysis. Importantly, the six task categories are not simply assembled together; they form a progressive evaluation spectrum ranging from structured engineering tasks to highly complex open-ended tasks.

5.1 六项任务的总体结果5.1 Overall Results Across the Six Tasks

Unlike approaches that enhance LLM agents merely by increasing context length or injecting static knowledge, the central idea of CBL is to treat each real task execution as a learnable case. In this way, the agent can gradually accumulate experience through the continual resolution of real problems and develop along a trajectory more similar to that of human experts. Specifically, when the system receives a new task, it first retrieves relevant historical experience from an existing case memory and combines this experience with the current task description to form an enhanced prompt context for the LLM agent to perform analysis and decision-making. After the task is completed, the system further summarizes effective strategies, lessons from failure, and reusable procedures from the current case, and stores them as knowledge that can be invoked again in future tasks. 为了支持案例经验的稳定存储和有效重用，CBL 将学到的知识组织成四个互补的结构化记忆模块。首先，固定领域知识模块（M_F）存储任务领域中稳定的背景知识，例如接口规范、数据结构定义和系统约束，从而为代理提供基础领域理解。其次，系统提示约束模块（M_S）记录从历史故障案例中提取的行为规则，帮助代理避免重复以前的错误。第三，技能库模块（M_K）存储经过多个案例反复验证的方法模板，允许直接重用成功的策略。最后，课程组织器模块（M_C）根据案例难度和成功率对历史经验进行排序，使系统首先吸收更简单、更稳定的经验，然后逐渐扩展到更复杂的任务。To support stable storage and effective reuse of case experience, CBL organizes learned knowledge into four complementary structured memory modules. First, the Fixed Domain Knowledge module (M_F) stores stable background knowledge in the task domain, such as interface specifications, data structure definitions, and system constraints, thereby providing the agent with foundational domain understanding. Second, the System Prompt Constraints module (M_S) records behavioral rules distilled from historical failure cases, helping the agent avoid repeating previous mistakes. Third, the Skill Library module (M_K) stores method templates that have been repeatedly validated across multiple cases, allowing successful strategies to be directly reused. Finally, the Curriculum Organizer module (M_C) orders historical experience according to case difficulty and success rate, enabling the system to first absorb easier and more stable experience before gradually extending to more complex tasks.

CBL的另一个关键特征是其明确的经验更新机制。每个任务完成后，系统会分析执行日志和轨迹，自动提取成功模式和失败模式，并进一步抽象为可重用的行为规则、技能模板或课程经验，然后写回相应的内存模块。因此，代理人完成一个案件时，不仅仅是完成一项任务，更是完成一项任务。它正在将执行过程本身转变为未来解决问题的知识资源。通过这一机制，CBL从一次性任务完成转向持续学习。Another key feature of CBL is its explicit experience update mechanism. After each task, the system analyzes execution logs and trajectories, automatically extracts success patterns and failure patterns, and further abstracts them into reusable behavioral rules, skill templates, or curriculum experience, which are then written back to the corresponding memory modules. Thus, when an agent completes a case, it is not merely finishing a task; it is transforming the execution process itself into knowledge resources for future problem solving. Through this mechanism, CBL moves from one-off task completion to continual learning.

Because the system represents learned results as structured natural-language knowledge modules, the rules, skills, and case experience summarized by the original agent from real tasks can be directly inherited and reused by other agents. This means that knowledge formed by one agent in practice can become a shared professional asset for the entire group, allowing capability improvement in multi-agent systems to emerge not from repeated exploration by individual agents alone, but from more efficient co-evolution through experience transfer.

6. 讨论6. Discussion

实验结果表明，基于案例的学习的核心价值并不是简单地为法学硕士提供更多的背景信息，而是为智能体配备更接近真实专家成长过程的学习机制。与依赖预训练知识、即时工程或少量示例的方法不同[4,5]，基于案例的学习强调让代理处理实际任务，分析实际问题，并通过实际故障和实际维修积累经验。由此，学习的对象不再局限于静态文本中的抽象规则，而是扩展到现实世界工作流程中衍生的实用知识。这是案例学习与传统知识增强方法的根本区别：前者侧重于经验的形成和迁移，而后者主要依赖于对现有文本知识的重组和调用。The experimental results show that the core value of case-based learning is not simply to provide LLMs with more background information, but to equip agents with a learning mechanism that more closely resembles the growth process of real experts. Unlike approaches that rely on pretrained knowledge, prompt engineering, or few-shot examples[4,5] , case-based learning emphasizes having agents work on real tasks, analyze real problems, and accumulate experience through real failures and real repairs. As a result, the object of learning is no longer limited to abstract rules in static text, but extends to practical knowledge derived from realworld workflows. This is the fundamental difference between case-based learning and conventional knowledge-augmentation methods: the former focuses on the formation and transfer of experience, whereas the latter relies mainly on recombining and invoking existing textual knowledge.

On structured or semi-structured tasks such as research-assistant tool orchestration, RLHF training analysis, and enterprisegrade multi-source RAG with access control, case-based learning already achieves consistently

最优或并列最优性能，表明它有效地提高了模型对关键约束和高价值评分信号的覆盖范围。在更复杂的任务上，例如长期操作、具有阶段性审查漂移的在线偏好学习以及多智能体科学发现平台，改进的余地变得更大。这表明任务越复杂、开放式和多阶段，通过基于案例的学习积累的任务内部经验就越能转化为有意义的绩效收益。这一趋势也与基于课程的能力发展背后更广泛的直觉相一致，即日益苛刻的任务使内部知识组织的差异更加明显[35]。optimal or tied-optimal performance, indicating that it effectively improves the model’s coverage of key constraints and high-value scoring signals. On more complex tasks, such as long-horizon operations, online preference learning with staged review drift, and multi-agent scientific discovery platforms, the margin of improvement becomes even larger. This suggests that the more complex, open-ended, and multi-stage a task is, the more task-internal experience accumulated through case-based learning can be converted into meaningful performance gains. This trend is also consistent with the broader intuition behind curriculum-based capability development, namely that increasingly demanding tasks make differences in internal knowledge organization more visible[35] .

这种模式具有明确的方法论含义。在结构相对良好的任务中，模型通常可以使用清单、规则记忆或显式程序模板来覆盖大部分关键点，因此基于案例的学习的优势主要体现在更稳定的高分和更完整的结构化输出上。然而，在复杂的任务中，真正的挑战通常不是模型是否“知道规则”，而是它是否能够在正确的时间调用正确的经验并围绕最关键的约束组织推理。例如，在长期操作中，模型必须在副作用操作后刷新状态，并在关键不变量失败时立即停止或回滚。在多智能体科学发现中，它必须同时管理共享内存、新颖性约束、预算分配和外部验证。对于此类问题，仅静态文本知识很少足以支持完整且稳定的推理，而通过基于案例的学习积累的任务经验可以提供更高层次的结构先验。更广泛地说，这种解释与先前的工作一致，表明仅长上下文或基于检索的增强并不能保证在推理过程中有效使用相关信息[3,28]。This pattern has clear methodological implications. In relatively well-structured tasks, models can often cover most key points using checklists, rule memory, or explicit procedural templates, so the advantage of case-based learning is mainly reflected in more stable high scores and more complete structured outputs. In complex tasks, however, the real challenge is usually not whether the model “knows a rule,” but whether it can invoke the right experience at the right time and organize its reasoning around the most critical constraints. For example, in long-horizon operations, the model must refresh state after side-effect actions and immediately stop or roll back when a key invariant fails. In multi-agent scientific discovery, it must simultaneously manage shared memory, novelty constraints, budget allocation, and external validation. For such problems, static textual knowledge alone is rarely sufficient to support complete and stable reasoning, whereas the task experience accumulated through casebased learning can provide a higher-level structural prior. More broadly, this interpretation is aligned with prior work suggesting that long-context or retrieval-based augmentation alone does not guarantee effective use of relevant information during reasoning[3,28] .

Its value lies not only in the stable gains observed across the current six tasks, but also in providing a developmental path for future professional agents that is more aligned with the way real experts actually grow.

7. 局限性和未来的工作7. Limitations and Future Work

尽管我们的结果表明案例驱动的经验迁移在提高复杂任务的性能和推理效率方面具有明显的潜力，但目前的工作仍然存在一些局限性。首先，案件资产的组织仍然相对静态。在本研究中，经验被表现为固定知识、系统提示、操作技能等结构化资产。虽然这提高了可解释性，但整体体验结构仍然在很大程度上取决于预定义的类别，并且尚未实现更动态和细粒度的自动更新。其次，任务之间关系的建模仍然不够。目前，经验转移主要发生在较浅层的知识和技能层面；更强大的任务图或分层经验系统尚未建立。因此，当任务仅部分相似但不同时，迁移可能会受到限制Although our results show that case-driven experience transfer has clear potential for improving both performance on complex tasks and reasoning efficiency, the current work still has several limitations. While this improves interpretability, the overall experience structure still depends largely on predefined categories and has not yet achieved more dynamic and finegrained automatic updating. Second, the modeling of relationships between tasks remains insufficient. At present, experience transfer occurs mainly at relatively shallow levels of knowledge and skills; a stronger task graph or hierarchical experience system has not yet been established. 八、结论8. Conclusion

本研究的结果表明，基于案例的学习的价值不仅在于为法学硕士提供更多提示或背景知识，还在于为代理人提供更接近真正专家成长过程的学习机制。与依赖预先训练的知识、即时工程或少量示例的方法不同，基于案例的学习强调让代理处理实际任务、分析实际问题并通过实际故障和修复积累经验。因此，学习的对象不再局限于静态文本中的抽象规则，而是扩展到从现实世界工作流程中提取的实用知识。The results of this study show that the value of case-based learning lies not merely in providing LLMs with more prompts or background knowledge, but in giving agents a learning mechanism that more closely resembles the growth process of real experts. Unlike methods that rely on pretrained knowledge, prompt engineering, or few-shot examples, case-based learning emphasizes having agents work on real tasks, analyze real problems, and accumulate experience through real failures and repairs. As a result, the object of learning is no longer limited to abstract rules in static text, but extends to practical knowledge drawn from real-world workflows.

在六个任务类别中，这种学习机制对于复杂任务尤其有效。在结构相对良好的任务上，基于案例的学习已经达到了最优或并列最优的性能；在更复杂的任务上，例如长期操作、分阶段审查治理和多智能体科学发现，其优势变得更大。这表明任务越复杂、开放式和多阶段，通过基于案例的学习形成的经验就越能转化为绩效收益。换句话说，复杂任务中的关键挑战不仅仅是“了解规则”，而是模型能否在正确的时间调用正确的经验并围绕关键约束组织推理。总体而言，基于案例的学习不仅仅是对基于静态文本的学习的补充，而且是智能体在现实世界任务中工作、学习和发展的一种方式。这种机制不仅提高了复杂任务的表现，还为未来的专业智能体提供了一条更符合真正专家实际成长的发展路径。Across the six task categories, this learning mechanism is especially effective on complex tasks. On relatively well-structured tasks, case-based learning already achieves optimal or tied-optimal performance; on more complex tasks, such as long-horizon operations, staged-review governance, and multi-agent scientific discovery, its advantages become even larger. This suggests that the more complex, open-ended, and multi-stage a task is, the more the experience formed through case-based learning can be translated into performance gains. In other words, the key challenge in complex tasks is not simply “knowing the rules,” but whether the model can invoke the right experience at the right time and organize reasoning around the critical constraints. Overall, case-based learning is not merely a supplement to static text-based learning, but a way for agents to work, learn, and evolve in real-world tasks. This mechanism not only improves performance on complex tasks, but also offers a development path for future professional agents that is more consistent with how real experts actually grow.

附录 A. 八项基准任务的其他详细信息Appendix A. Additional Details of the Eight Benchmark Tasks

本研究中的六个任务类别不是普通的问答样本，而是围绕实际系统开发、分析和治理中常见的高复杂性问题构建的案例集合。他们的共同特点是，没有统一的标准答案；相反，它们要求代理围绕症状、根本原因、约束和修复路径生成结构化且可执行的分析。为了避免正文过长，本附录提供了有关每项任务的背景、问题边界和评估重点的更多详细信息。The six task categories in this study are not ordinary question-answering samples, but a collection of cases built around high-complexity problems commonly encountered in real system development, analysis, and governance. Their shared characteristic is that they do not have a single standard answer; instead, they require the agent to produce structured and executable analyses around symptoms, root causes, constraints, and repair paths. To avoid excessive length in the main text, this appendix provides additional details on the background, problem boundaries, and evaluation focus of each task.

A.1 研究助理代理工具编排A.1 Research-Assistant Agent Tool Orchestration

此任务模拟一个能够调用搜索、网页抓取、PDF 阅读和摘要工具的研究辅助系统。该模型必须识别多工具工作流程中的循环工具调用、预算超支和参数格式错误等问题，并提出具有明确终止条件和状态管理能力的修复策略。关键问题是智能体是否认识到工具的使用不仅仅是自由形式的生成，而必须遵循可验证、可终止和可控的执行逻辑。This task simulates a research-assistant system capable of invoking search, web scraping, PDF reading, and summarization tools. The model must identify issues such as looping tool calls, budget overruns, and parameter format errors in multi-tool workflows, and propose repair strategies with explicit termination conditions and state-management ability. The key question is whether the agent recognizes that tool use is not simply free-form generation, but must follow execution logic that is verifiable, terminable, and controllable.

A.2 偏好优化和 RLHF 训练A.2 Preference Optimization and RLHF Training

该任务将智能体置于对齐训练分析师的角色，并要求其解释诸如奖励增加但实际质量下降、PPO 训练中的 KL 漂移以及数值不稳定等问题。目标不是重述 RLHF 训练流程，而是区分表面训练信号和真正的偏好改进，并提出旨在训练稳定性的修复策略。This task places the agent in the role of an alignment-training analyst and asks it to explain problems such as rising reward but declining real quality, KL drift in PPO training, and numerical instability. The goal is not to restate the RLHF training pipeline, but to distinguish between superficial training signals and genuine preference improvement, and to propose repair strategies aimed at training stability.

Unlike ordinary RAG systems, answer quality is not the only objective here; security and evidence consistency are also necessary conditions for success. The model must clearly identify where security boundaries should be established and how citation chains can remain stable.

附录 B. 基线定义Appendix B. Baseline Definitions

为了确保清晰的比较语义，本文使用的四种基线方法定义如下。To ensure clear comparison semantics, the four baseline methods used in this paper are defined as follows.

零射击。仅提供当前任务描述，没有任何其他示例、规则或流程提示。该基线用于在没有外部经验支持的情况下衡量基础模型的原始分析能力。Zero-Shot. Only the current task description is provided, without any additional examples, rules, or process prompts. This baseline is used to measure the raw analytical ability of the base model without external experience support.

少射。任务指令中添加了少量示例来模拟情境学习。该方法可以提供局部指导，但其知识迁移主要依赖于示例之间的表面相似性，通常缺乏明确的约束和稳定的任务结构控制。Few-Shot. A small number of examples are added to the task instruction to simulate incontext learning. This method can provide local guidance, but its knowledge transfer depends mainly on superficial similarity between examples and usually lacks explicit constraints and stable task-structure control.

检查清单提示。手动设计的分步检查表用于指导模型完成预定义的分析过程。该方法强调结构和过程控制，适用于工程顺序明确的问题，但其通用性通常受到检查表本身覆盖范围的限制。Checklist Prompt. A manually designed step-by-step checklist is used to guide the model through a predefined analysis process. This method emphasizes structure and process control and is suitable for problems with clear engineering order, but its generality is usually limited by the coverage of the checklist itself.

规则记忆。一般的经验约束以规则的形式注入到模型中，以提高输出的一致性和可控性。与Checklist Prompt相比，它更接近于静态的经验记忆机制，但通常缺乏任务级的上下文适应能力。Rule Memory. General experience constraints are injected into the model in rule form to improve output consistency and controllability. Compared with Checklist Prompt, it is closer to a static experience-memory mechanism, but it usually lacks task-level contextual adaptation ability.
