BeeVe：非语义声学状态的无监督发现，实现对蜂群健康的非侵入性评估BeeVe: Unsupervised Discovery of Non-Semantic Acoustic States, Towards a Non-Invasive Assessment of Honey Bee Colony Health

摘要。Abstract.

在没有监督的情况下发现生物信号中的结构是计算智能中的一个基本问题，但现有的生物声学方法假设发声产生模型或预定义的语义单元，使得非发声物种得不到很好的服务。蜜蜂是这一差距的一个引人注目的例子：它们的集体嗡嗡声来自机械肌肉振动，而不是任何交流发声器官，虽然有证据表明这些振动反映了蜂群的生理状态，但现有的发声框架并不适用。这项工作介绍了 BeeVe，这是一种用于发现蜜蜂集体嗡嗡声中声学状态的无监督框架。 BeeVe 使用自监督 Patchout Spectrogram Transformer (PaSST) 作为冻结特征提取器来生成一般声学嵌入，然后训练矢量量化变分自动编码器 (VQ-VAE)，这些嵌入完全没有标签，学习声学标记的有限离散码本 直接来自未标记的蜂巢音频。所有应用于蜜蜂音频的学习都是无监督的：在任何阶段都不使用标签、借口任务或对比目标。每个标记代表一个重复出现的声学模式，完整的密码本形成了完全无需注释即可发现的群体级声学状态的可重用词汇表。针对已知女王状态的事后评估表明，学习的标记将皇后权利和无女王条件分开，詹森-香农散度值在 0.609 和 0.688 之间，并且无女王条件进一步分解为三个内部一致的子状态，在不同密码本大小和随机种子的实验中保持稳定。令牌转换分析进一步证实了学习词汇中的非随机顺序结构（Discovering structure in biological signals without supervision is a fundamental problem in computational intelligence, yet existing bioacoustic methods assume vocal production models or predefined semantic units, leaving non-vocal species poorly served. Honey bees are a compelling instance of this gap: their collective buzzing arises from mechanical muscle vibrations rather than any communicative vocal apparatus, and while evidence suggests these vibrations reflect colony physiological state, no existing vocal framework applies. This work introduces BeeVe, an unsupervised framework for acoustic state discovery in collective honey bee buzzing. BeeVe uses the self-supervised Patchout Spectrogram Transformer (PaSST) as a frozen feature extractor to produce general acoustic embeddings, then trains a Vector-Quantized Variational Autoencoder (VQ-VAE) entirely without labels on those embeddings, learning a finite discrete codebook of acoustic tokens directly from unlabelled hive audio. All learning applied to bee audio is unsupervised: no labels, pretext tasks, or contrastive objectives are used at any stage. Each token represents a recurring acoustic pattern, and the full codebook forms a reusable vocabulary of colony-level acoustic states discovered entirely without annotation. Post-hoc evaluation against known queen status reveals that the learned tokens separate queenright and queenless conditions with Jensen-Shannon Divergence values between 0.609 and 0.688, and that the queenless condition further decomposes into three internally coherent sub-states stable across experiments with different codebook sizes and random seeds. Generalisation to unseen recordings preserves both token overlap (Jaccard = 0.947) and global manifold topology. These results demonstrate that unsupervised discrete codebook learning can recover repeatable acoustic structure from a non-vocal biological signal without annotation, opening a path toward non-invasive acoustic hive health monitoring.

5. 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

这项工作的核心问题是蜜蜂的集体嗡嗡声是否 包含结构化的、可重复的声学状态，无需 监督。三个独立实验的结果一致 支持它确实如此，具有令牌分离、稳定的子状态结构，以及 在看不见的记录上保留了流形拓扑，所有这些都没有任何记录 实行监督。The central question of this work is whether collective honey bee buzzing contains structured, repeatable acoustic states that can be recovered without supervision. The results across three independent experiments consistently support that it does, with token separation, stable sub-state structure, and preserved manifold topology on unseen recordings all emerging without any imposed supervision.

一个问题是为什么 HuBERT、wav2vec 或 AVES 等模型没有被用作 比较基线。这些模型对声音存在归纳偏差 生产系统：HuBERT 和 wav2vec 目标语音，AVES 目标动物 发声。蜜蜂的嗡嗡声并不是由这样的系统产生的；而是由蜜蜂发出的。是的 通过肌肉活动机械产生。应用声音偏向 机械生成信号的编码器在方法上是不合理的 而不是提供信息。 PaSST 被选为与领域无关的音频 不带有声音产生假设的编码器。连续嵌入 仅从 PaSST 捕获变异，但不产生可数的、可重复使用的 词汇；矢量量化步骤迫使模型致力于 重复出现的模式。离散化既不被认为是正确的，也不被认为是正确的 最优的；它通过一致性、重用性和可分离性进行事后验证。的 密码本在这里比重建更重要；与有声物种的研究不同， 当产生或重建类似的声音时，其益处有限 信号不是直接的通信手段。One question is why models such as HuBERT, wav2vec, or AVES were not used as comparison baselines. These models carry inductive biases toward vocal production systems: HuBERT and wav2vec target speech, AVES targets animal vocalizations. Honey bee buzzing does not arise from such a system; it is mechanically generated through muscle activity. Applying a vocally biased encoder to a mechanically generated signal would be methodologically unsound rather than informative. PaSST was selected instead as a domain-agnostic audio encoder that carries no vocal production assumptions. Continuous embeddings from PaSST alone capture variation but do not produce a countable, reusable vocabulary; the vector quantization step forces the model to commit to recurring patterns. The discretization is neither assumed to be correct nor optimal; it is validated post-hoc by consistency, reuse, and separability. The codebook matters here more than reconstruction; unlike work on vocal species, generating or reconstructing similar sounds carries limited benefit when the signal is not a direct communication means.

评价是否充分也是一个公平的挑战。 JSD、熵、 聚类纯度和标记重叠不是真实验证，这 工作并不声称它们是。他们评估有意义的必要条件 结构而不是基本事实。女王地位仅用作事后考虑 分析信号并且在训练期间从未接触到模型，即使是 半监督。它用于验证真实状态发现已经发生。 这些状态是否反映真实的生物结构或模型制品 如果没有生物学注释就无法完全解决。然而，子状态 A 占无女王框架的 57%，纯度超过 97%，足够稳健 这种一致的复发不能合理地归因于噪音。Whether the evaluation is sufficient is also a fair challenge. JSD, entropy, clustering purity, and token overlap are not ground truth validation, and this work does not claim they are. They evaluate necessary conditions for meaningful structure rather than ground truth. Queen status is used solely as a post-hoc analysis signal and is never exposed to the model during training, not even as semi-supervision. It serves to validate that real state discovery has occurred. Whether these states reflect genuine biological structure or model artefacts cannot be fully resolved without biological annotation. However, Sub-state A accounting for 57% of queenless frames at over 97% purity is robust enough that consistent recurrence cannot plausibly be attributed to noise.

最重要的限制是可扩展性。实验用了五个小时 来自 UrBAN 数据集受控子集的音频，选择进行研究 在可解释的条件下表示行为而不是最大化 性能。码本结果虽然稳定，但可能不代表完整的结果 所有蜂巢、季节和条件下声学状态的多样性 足足1000小时。学习本身并不能保证大规模的质量；什么 所证明的是受控条件下学习的稳定性。The most significant limitation is scalability. The experiments use five hours of audio from a controlled subset of the UrBAN dataset, chosen to study representation behaviour under interpretable conditions rather than to maximise performance. The codebook result, while stable, may not represent the full diversity of acoustic states across all hives, seasons, and conditions in the full 1000 hours. The learning itself does not guarantee quality at scale; what is demonstrated is the stability of learning under controlled conditions.

6. 结论Conclusion

这项工作证明，使用在 PaSST 嵌入上训练的矢量量化变分自编码器进行无监督离散表示学习，可以在没有任何标签监督的情况下恢复声学状态的离散词汇表。 学习到的令牌与皇后权利和无皇后状态分开，并且无皇后条件分解为连贯的子状态，令牌转换分析揭示了不同的非随机顺序模式，在所有三个实验中确认了连续令牌之间具有统计上显着的依赖性。This work demonstrates that unsupervised discrete representation learning, using a Vector-Quantized Variational Autoencoder trained on PaSST embeddings, can recover a discrete vocabulary of acoustic states without any label supervision. The learned tokens are separated from queenright and queenless states, and the queenless condition decomposes into coherent sub-states, token transition analysis reveals distinct non-random sequential patterns, with statistically significant dependencies between successive tokens confirmed across all three experiments.

更广泛的贡献是概念性的和技术性的。这项工作并不声称要识别新的生物行为；相反，它表明了 在大多数情况下，无需任何事先假设或注释即可从非声音物种中恢复结构化声学模式的能力 计算方法假设预定义的标签、已知的行为类别或声音产生结构。蜜蜂按照自己的条件对待， 他们的信号被建模为新兴殖民地国家而不是 从发声物种框架借用的交流输出。将这种方法扩展到完整的数据集并通过生物注释为发现的子状态奠定基础仍然是未来工作最直接的方向。The broader contribution is conceptual as much as technical. This work does not claim to identify new biological behaviours; rather, it demonstrates the capability to recover structured acoustic patterns from a non-vocal species without any prior assumptions or annotations, in a domain where most computational approaches assume predefined labels, known behaviour categories, or vocal production structure. Honey bees are treated on their own terms, with their signals modeled as emergent colony state rather than as communicative output borrowed from vocal species frameworks. Extending this approach to the full dataset and grounding the discovered sub-states through biological annotation remain the most immediate directions for future work.

这种非侵入式声学监测具有实际意义 超出了直接的技术成果。当女王流失和蜂拥而至时 检测已经是蜂巢监控中既定的目标， BeeVe 的无监督性质开启了识别状态的可能性 除了目前标记的内容之外，捕获检查的群体行为 时间表和预定义的分类器可能会完全错过。及早发现 任何未经物理检查的此类状态都会减少群体干扰，并且 降低及时干预的障碍，特别是对于小规模的干预 养蜂人。更广泛地说，是一种不需要注释数据即可部署的工具 有助于实现对物种敏感、低成本的监测， 在蜂群加速崩溃之际支持传粉媒介保护。Non-invasive acoustic monitoring of this kind carries practical implications beyond the immediate technical results. While queen loss and swarming detection are already established targets in hive monitoring, the unsupervised nature of BeeVe opens the possibility of identifying states beyond those currently labelled, capturing colony behaviour that inspection schedules and predefined classifiers may miss entirely. Early detection of any such state without physical inspection reduces colony disturbance and lowers the barrier to timely intervention, particularly for small-scale beekeepers. More broadly, a tool that requires no annotated data to deploy contributes to the movement toward species-sensitive, low-cost monitoring, supporting pollinator conservation at a time of accelerating colony collapse.
