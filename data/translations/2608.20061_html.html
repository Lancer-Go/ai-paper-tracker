摘要Abstract

专家混合 (MoE) 架构显着扩展了模型容量，而计算成本却没有成比例增加。 然而，通过扫描在模型大小和代币预算的极端规模上优化其超参数（尤其是学习率）在计算上仍然令人望而却步。 在本文中，我们提出了一种计算效率高的两步超参数传输框架，该框架通过跨缩放模型宽度传输模型来估计训练大型 MoE 模型的最佳学习率，然后外推到万亿代币的范围。 首先，我们制定最大更新参数化（Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures significantly expand model capacity without a proportional increase in computational cost. However, optimizing their hyperparameters—particularly the learning rate—at extreme scales of both model size and token budget via sweeping remains computationally prohibitive. In this paper, we propose a compute-efficient, two-step hyperparameter transfer framework that estimates optimal learning rates for training large MoE models by transferring them across scaling model widths, and subsequently extrapolating to trillion-token horizons. First, we formulate a Maximal Update Parameterization (μ\muP）利用多头潜在注意力（MLA）和 Muon 优化器对 MoE 架构进行适应，证明最佳学习率在宽度模型上一致转移。 其次，我们通过建立预测缩放定律来沿代币维度扩展这种可转移性。通过将线性回归应用于有限预算下的小型代理模型得出的最佳值，我们成功地将理想学习率外推到高保真度的大规模训练范围（例如 10 万亿个代币）（P) adaptation for MoE architectures utilizing Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA) and the Muon optimizer, demonstrating that optimal learning rates transfer consistently across width-scaled models. Second, we extend this transferability along the token dimension by establishing a predictive scaling law. By applying linear regression to the optimal values derived from small proxy models on limited budgets, we successfully extrapolate the ideal learning rate to massive training horizons (e.g., 10 trillion tokens) with high fidelity (R2=0.95R^{2}=0.95）。 因此，这表明小模型上的代理训练足以确定大规模 MoE 的广泛训练的最佳学习率。 我们应用所提出的方法从头开始预训练我们的基础模型（总共 155B 个，17B 个活动参数），稳定的训练和评估结果验证了可以以最小的消融成本准确预测全尺寸目标模型的最佳配置。). Consequently, this indicates that proxy training on small models is sufficient to determine the optimal learning rate for the extensive training of large-scale MoEs. We apply the proposed methodology to pretrain our foundation model (155B total, 17B active parameters) from scratch, and the stable training and evaluation results validate that optimal configurations for full-scale target models can be accurately predicted with minimal ablation costs.

1 简介Introduction

专家混合 (MoE) 架构Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures(39)已成为扩展 LLM 能力的强大范例，而无需按比例增加计算成本，从而实现更大的模型专业化和具有成本效益的推理。 因此，MoE 模型可以比在相同数据上训练的密集模型获得更高的有效容量，从而提供更强的性能，而无需任何额外的训练成本have emerged as a powerful paradigm for scaling LLM capacity without a proportional increase in computational cost, enabling both greater model specialization and cost-efficient inference. As a result, MoE models can achieve significantly higher effective capacity than dense counterparts trained on the same data, delivering stronger performance without any additional training cost(39; 3; 15; 33)。 著名的开源法学硕士，例如 DeepSeek-V3. Prominent open-source LLMs such as DeepSeek-V3(29),Qwen3-235B-A22B, Qwen3-235B-A22B(54),基米-K2.5, Kimi-K2.5(44)、GLM-5, and GLM-5(58)采用 MoE 设计来提供强大的性能，同时减少每个令牌的活动参数和活动推理 FLOP，通常在一系列任务上优于类似或更大尺寸的密集对应物。 然而，MoE 架构引入了额外的超参数来管理专家路由和负载平衡，这扩大了超参数空间，并使调整比密集模型更加复杂和昂贵。 在这些超参数中，学习率是训练成功的关键决定因素employ MoE designs to deliver powerful performance while reducing the per-token active parameters and active inference FLOPs, often outperforming dense counterparts of similar or larger size on a range of tasks. However, MoE architectures introduce additional hyperparameters to manage expert routing and load balancing, which expands the hyperparameter space and makes tuning considerably more complex and expensive than for dense models. Among these hyperparameters, the learning rate stands out as a critical determinant of successful training(6; 42; 23; 55; 26);但其最佳值对模型大小和代币预算高度敏感; yet its optimal value is highly sensitive to both model size and token budget(26; 8)。 这意味着规模的每一次变化——无论是模型大小还是训练范围——都需要新的、昂贵的超参数搜索。. This means that every change in scale—whether in model size or training horizon—demands a fresh and expensive hyperparameter search.

为了减轻大规模超参数扫描的高昂计算成本，诸如最大更新参数化（Maximal Update Parameterization）之类的框架（To mitigate the prohibitive computational cost of hyperparameter sweeps at massive scales, frameworks such as Maximal Update Parameterization (μ\muP) (56)和andμ\mu- 转移-Transfer(55)已被提议。 这些方法能够将最佳超参数（例如学习率）从小型代理模型零样本转移到更大的变体。 然而，这些方法最初是为密集模型设计的，其中缩放宽度是主轴。 MoE 架构引入了一个额外的扩展维度——稀疏性——由活跃专家与总专家的比率控制。 对于极端参数规模（例如超过 100B 总参数）的 MoE 模型，由于推理成本过高和硬件限制，简单地扩展宽度（例如隐藏大小）变得越来越棘手。 相反，增加专家总数同时保持活跃专家固定提供了一条更实用的路径，因为它扩展了模型容量，而无需按比例增加推理计算。 然而，稀疏性遵循不同的缩放行为have been proposed. These methods enable the zero-shot transfer of optimal hyperparameters (e.g., learning rates) from small proxy models to larger variants. However, these methods were originally designed for dense models, where scaling width is the primary axis. MoE architectures introduce an additional scaling dimension—sparsity—governed by the ratio of active to total experts. For MoE models at extreme parameter scales (e.g., over 100B total parameters), simply expanding the width (e.g., hidden size) becomes increasingly intractable due to prohibitive inference costs and hardware constraints. Instead, increasing the total number of experts while keeping active experts fixed offers a more practical path, as it expands model capacity without a proportional increase in inference compute. Nevertheless, sparsity follows distinct scaling behaviors(12; 47; 45)，目前还不清楚现有的用于宽度缩放的超参数传输框架是否可以推广到稀疏维度。 这一关键差距在很大程度上仍未得到解决，使得大规模 MoE 训练的最佳超参数配置预测成为一个悬而未决的问题。, and it remains unclear whether existing hyperparameter transfer frameworks derived for width scaling generalize to the sparsity dimension. This critical gap remains largely unaddressed, leaving optimal hyperparameter configuration prediction for large-scale MoE training an open problem.

Given these contradictions and practical constraints, we fix the batch size to maximize GPU efficiency and treat the learning rate as the primary transfer target. By decoupling learning rate optimization from batch size, we propose a learning rate scaling law that remains robust regardless of the batch size chosen to maximize throughput in a given hardware environment.
