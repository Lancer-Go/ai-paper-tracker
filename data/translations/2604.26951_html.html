摘要Abstract

扩散大语言模型 (dLLM) 提供并行解码和双向上下文，但最先进的 dLLM 需要数十亿个参数才能获得竞争性能。虽然 dLLM 的现有蒸馏方法减少了单一架构内的推理步骤，但没有一个解决跨架构知识转移，其中教师和学生在架构、注意力机制和分词器方面存在差异。 我们呈现Diffusion large language models (dLLMs) offer parallel decoding and bidirectional context, but state-of-the-art dLLMs require billions of parameters for competitive performance. While existing distillation methods for dLLMs reduce inference steps within a single architecture, none address cross-architecture knowledge transfer, in which the teacher and student differ in architecture, attention mechanism, and tokenizer. We present潮汐Tide，第一个跨架构 dLLM 蒸馏框架，包含三个模块化组件：(1), the first framework for cross-architecture dLLM distillation, comprising three modular components: (1)潮汐Tidal，它联合调节训练进度和扩散时间步长的蒸馏强度，以考虑教师依赖于噪声的可靠性； (2), which jointly modulates distillation strength across training progress and diffusion timestep to account for the teacher’s noise-dependent reliability; (2)比较演示CompDemo，通过互补掩模分割丰富了教师的背景，以改进重掩模下的预测； (3) 反向, which enriches the teacher’s context via complementary mask splitting to improve predictions under heavy masking; and (3) Reverse冷静Calm，一个交叉分词器目标，可反转块级似然匹配，产生有界梯度和双端噪声过滤。 通过两个异构管道将 8B 密集教师和 16B MoE 教师提炼为 0.6B 学生，在八个基准测试中平均优于基线 1.53 分，在代码生成方面产生显着收益，其中 HumanEval 分数达到 48.78，而 AR 基线为 32.3。, a cross-tokenizer objective that inverts chunk-level likelihood matching, yielding bounded gradients and dual-end noise filtering. Distilling 8B dense and 16B MoE teachers into a 0.6B student via two heterogeneous pipelines outperforms the baseline by an average of 1.53 points across eight benchmarks, yielding notable gains in code generation, where HumanEval scores reach 48.78 compared to 32.3 for the AR baseline.

1 简介Introduction

自回归语言模型主导自然语言生成Autoregressive language models dominate natural language generation(Vaswani et al., 2017; Yang et al., 2024; 2025)，然而扩散语言模型作为一种替代范式已经获得了关注。 早期作品，例如D3PM, yet diffusion language models have gained traction as an alternative paradigm. Earlier works, such as D3PM(Austin et al., 2021a)和MDLMand MDLM(Sahoo et al., 2024)，探索基本训练架构。 近期作品，如LLaDA, explore the basic training architectures. Recent works, such as LLaDA(Nie et al., 2025)和梦想and Dream(Ye et al., 2025)，将模型大小缩放到大型语言模型的大小。 例如最近的工作，LLaDA 2.0, scale model size to that of large language models. For example, recent work, LLaDA 2.0(Bie et al., 2025)，将模型大小缩放至 100B，实现了新的最先进性能。 与自回归模型相比，dLLM 迭代地对完全屏蔽的序列进行去噪，从而实现并行解码和双向上下文。 然而，有竞争力的 dLLM 需要 8B-16B 甚至更多的参数和计算成本, scales the model size to 100B, achieving a new state-of-the-art performance. Compared with autoregressive models, dLLMs iteratively denoise a fully masked sequence, enabling parallel decoding and bidirectional context. However, competitive dLLMs require 8B-16B or even more parameters and computation costs(Nie et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025; Bie et al., 2025; Ye et al., 2025)，对部署造成障碍（图, posing a barrier to deployment (Figure1).

开发更强大的小型模型的一种简单解决方案是知识蒸馏One simple solution for developing stronger small models is knowledge distillation(Hinton et al., 2015)，它将大模型压缩为较小的模型。 对于自回归 LM，使用 MiniLLM 等方法, which compresses large models into smaller ones. For autoregressive LMs, methods such as MiniLLM(Gu et al., 2024), 法学硕士, DistiLLM(Ko et al., ),GKD, GKD(Agarwal et al., 2024)，和TAID, and TAID(Shing et al., 2025)已经很成熟，具有各种设计，包括新的 Kullback-Leibler 散度损失、利用教师模型的反馈等。 然而，对于 dLLM，现有方法——CDLMare well established, with various designs, including new Kullback-Leibler divergence losses, leveraging the teacher model’s feedback, etc. For dLLMs, however, existing methods—CDLM(Kim et al., 2025), DDD (Hayakawa et al., 2024), 迷幻剂, LSD(Fu et al., 2025)和 SDTT, and SDTT(Deschenaux and Gulcehre, 2024)——只专注于—focus exclusively on步进压缩step compression在单一架构内，留下within a single architecture, leaving跨架构蒸馏cross-architecture distillation未经探索。 这个设置介绍了unexplored. This setting introduces三three根本性挑战。 首先，由于训练过程中时间步长的随机采样，教师的可靠性在扩散过程中剧烈波动，导致时间动态不一致。 其次，高噪声水平下的严重掩蔽极大地减少了可用的上下文，使得教师的原始输出信息量太小，无法传递丰富的空间表示。 第三，不同的标记器词汇使得标准标记级似然目标在数学上不适用。 在这项工作中，我们提出fundamental challenges.
