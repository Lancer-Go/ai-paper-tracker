LLM 奥林匹克：为什么模型评估需要密封考试LLM Olympiad: Why Model Evaluation Needs a Sealed Exam

Jan Christian Blaise Cruz and Alham Fikri Aji
MBZUAI
{jan.cruz,alham.fikri}@mbzuai.ac.ae
摘要Abstract

基准和排行榜是 NLP 最常传达进展的方式，但在 LLM 时代，它们越来越容易被误读。 分数可以反映基准追逐、隐藏的评估选择或意外接触测试内容，而不仅仅是广泛的能力。 封闭基准延迟了其中一些问题，但降低了透明度，并使社区更难从结果中学习。 我们主张一种补充做法：奥林匹克式的评估活动，其中问题在评估之前被密封， 提交的内容会提前冻结，所有参赛作品都通过一个标准化的工具运行。 评分后，将发布完整的任务集和评估代码，以便可以重现和审核结果。 这种设计旨在使强大的性能更难“制造”并且更容易信任。Benchmarks and leaderboards are how NLP most often communicates progress, but in the LLM era they are increasingly easy to misread. Scores can reflect benchmark-chasing, hidden evaluation choices, or accidental exposure to test content—not just broad capability. Closed benchmarks delay some of these issues, but reduce transparency and make it harder for the community to learn from results. We argue for a complementary practice: an Olympiad-style evaluation event where problems are sealed until evaluation, submissions are frozen in advance, and all entries run through one standardized harness. After scoring, the full task set and evaluation code are released so results can be reproduced and audited. This design aims to make strong performance harder to “manufacture” and easier to trust.

Yet benchmark outcomes can be脆弱的fragile：测量内容和结果汇总方式的微小选择可能会改变哪种方法看起来最好: small choices in what is measured and how results are aggregated can change which methods look best(Dehghani et al., 2021)。 图. 法学硕士时代的两种力量加剧了这种脆弱性。 首先，评估可能会变得“泄漏”：在网络规模的语料库上训练的模型可能已经看到了测试项目（或相近的变体），从而使泛化声明变得复杂Two LLM-era forces amplify this fragility. Second, evaluation protocols have many degrees of freedom. 直觉。Intuition.

我们的立场。Our position.

我们的立场是，NLP 应该通过密封的、奥林匹克式的评估协议（“LLM 奥林匹克”）来补充现有的基准，其中（i）评估问题在活动之前被密封，（ii）提交的内容被冻结并在标准化工具下集中评估，以及（iii）所有工件随后发布以供社区审核。 结果是这样的评估：通过特定于基准的优化或测试集暴露无法产生强大的性能，并且每个分数都带有社区可以验证的公共证据线索（任务、代码和日志）。 关键的转变是基准不仅是“数据集”，而且是We take the position that NLP should complement existing benchmarks with a sealed, Olympiad-style evaluation protocol—an “LLM Olympiad” where (i) evaluation problems are sealed until the event, (ii) submissions are frozen and evaluated centrally under a standardized harness, and (iii) all artifacts are released afterward for community audit. The result is an evaluation where strong performance cannot be manufactured through benchmark-specific optimization or test-set exposure, and where every score comes with a public trail of evidence—tasks, code, and logs—that the community can verify. The key shift is that the benchmark is not only “the dataset,” but the程序procedure：任务如何征求、密封、执行、评分，然后发布。: how tasks are solicited, sealed, executed, scored, and then released.

2 当今基准测试工具箱的局限性The Limits of Today’s Benchmarking Toolbox

开放式基准测试、封闭式基准测试和共享任务都具有重要的用途，并且该领域因为它们的存在而变得更好。 他们还优化了不同的权衡：开放基准优先考虑透明度和重用；封闭式基准测试优先考虑测试保密性；共享任务在通用脚本下优先考虑集中评分。 我们的立场并不是这些格式被误导了，而是在LLM时代它们留下了空白Open benchmarks, closed benchmarks, and shared tasks each serve important purposes, and the field is better because they exist. They also optimize different tradeoffs: open benchmarks prioritize transparency and reuse; closed benchmarks prioritize test secrecy; shared tasks prioritize centralized scoring under a common script. Our position is not that these formats are misguided, but that in the LLM era they leave gaps for高保证high-assurance声明——尤其是关于污染风险、可比性和激励措施的声明。claims—especially around contamination risk, comparability, and incentives.

2.1 开放基准测试Open benchmarks

开放基准是透明且易于重现的，这是像 GLUE 这样的套件的主要原因Open benchmarks are transparent and easy to reproduce, which is a major reason suites like GLUE(Wang et al., 2018),MMLU, MMLU(Hendrycks et al., 2021), GPQA Rein et al. (2024)，以及其他基准变得有影响力。 然而，一旦基准成为广泛关注的目标，它就会引发提示和评估设置的大量迭代，而小的测量选择可能会影响感知的赢家, and other benchmarks became influential. However, once a benchmark becomes a widely watched target, it invites heavy iteration on prompts and evaluation settings, and small measurement choices can affect perceived winners(Dehghani et al., 2021)。对于法学硕士来说，基于提示的评估通常会放大这一点：看似次要的选择（例如演示选择或排序）可能会改变结果，使“基准分数”不像看起来那么稳定. For LLMs, this is often amplified by prompt-based evaluation: seemingly minor choices (e.g., demonstration selection or ordering) can shift outcomes, making “the benchmark score” less stable than it appears(Lu et al., 2022; Perez et al., 2021).

开放基准还面临网络规模不断上升的污染风险Open benchmarks also face rising contamination risk at web scale(Xu et al., 2024)，激励审计工作, motivating auditing efforts(Li et al., 2024)和重复数据删除实践and deduplication practices(Lee et al., 2022)。 最后，基准测试可能会饱和，从而引发像 GLUE 这样的循环. Finally, benchmarks can saturate, prompting cycles like GLUE→\rightarrow超级胶水SuperGLUE(Wang et al., 2019).

2.2 封闭基准测试Closed benchmarks

封闭基准可以延迟直接测试集的过度拟合，但会牺牲社区的可审计性和可访问性，这也会影响社区的适应性。 如果无法访问任务和评估详细信息，就很难解释分数为何变化，或者它给该领域带来了什么。 这种信任瓶颈促使人们呼吁在评估中提供透明、标准化的报告Closed benchmarks can delay direct test-set overfitting, but trade away community auditability and accessibility, which also affect adaptability by the community. Without access to tasks and evaluation details, it is harder to interpret why a score changed, or what it teaches the field. This trust bottleneck motivates calls for transparent, standardized reporting in evaluation(Liang et al., 2022)。 封闭基准也不会消除私下迭代和选择性披露有利结果的激励. Closed benchmarks also do not remove incentives to iterate privately and selectively disclose favorable results(Singh et al., 2025).

一些基准测试通过利用两种不同的测试集来结合开放和封闭的数据访问：一种用于公开发布，一种用于私人使用Some benchmarks combine both open and closed data access by utilizing two different test sets: one for public release and one for private useChollet et al. (2025); Center for AI Safety et al. (2026)。这种方法平衡了可访问性和隐私；开发人员可以轻松地衡量公共集上的进度，然后请求在私有集上评估他们的模型。然而，这种方法仍然存在不足，因为实践者可能会针对特定任务进行过度优化。. This approach balances accessibility with privacy; developers can easily measure progress on the public set, then later request an evaluation of their model on the private set. However, this approach still falls short, as practitioners may over-optimize for the specific task.

2.3 共享任务Shared tasks

共享任务通过对隐藏的测试标签运行集中评估来提高可比性。 然而，他们通常会提前宣布任务和格式；强有力的结果可以反映针对已知目标的针对性工程，而不是一般准备。这是共享任务的一个功能——它们旨在衡量对特定目标的适应情况——但它与“考试式”评估的目标不同，后者在意外问题陈述下探索广泛的准备情况。Shared tasks improve comparability by running centralized evaluation on hidden test labels. However, they typically announce the task and format well in advance; strong results can reflect targeted engineering for a known target rather than general preparedness. This is a feature for shared tasks—they are designed to measure adaptation to a specific target—but it differs from the goal of an “exam-style” evaluation that probes broad preparedness under surprise problem statements.

对于 LLM 系统，剩余的自由度（提示约定、工具使用和计算预算）仍然会使比较不均匀，除非规则手册和工具很严格For LLM systems, remaining degrees of freedom (prompting conventions, tool use, and compute budgets) can still make comparisons uneven unless the rulebook and harness are strict(Reddi et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2022)。此外，许多共享任务提交都是高度特定于任务的。虽然这不一定是错误的，但它通常意味着此类任务并非旨在评估通用模型的功能。一旦任务被发布，它们最终可以进入训练语料库，重新引入长期泄漏风险. Moreover, many shared-task submissions are highly task-specific. While this is not necessarily wrong, it typically implies that such tasks are not intended for evaluating the capabilities of general-purpose models. Once tasks are released, they can eventually enter training corpora, reintroducing long-term leakage risk(Xu et al., 2024).

2.4 外卖Takeaway

这些工具仍然很有价值，但没有一个能够始终如一地提供以下组合：(i) 测量期间的密封评估，(ii) 运行时的标准化方法，以及 (iii) 发布后的事后可审计性。 这种差距比以前更重要。 基准排名越来越多地推动高风险决策：部署哪些模型、哪些安全声明值得信赖以及研究资金流向何处。 当这些排名建立在脆弱、可能受到污染或难以复制的评估程序上时，下游后果是真实的，而且还在不断扩大。 这激发了奥林匹克式的补充，有意借用每种形式的优势：保密These tools remain valuable, but none consistently provides the combination of (i) sealed evaluation during measurement, (ii) standardized methodology at run time, and (iii) post-hoc auditability after release. This gap matters more than it used to. Benchmark rankings increasingly drive high-stakes decisions: which models get deployed, which safety claims are trusted, and where research funding flows. When those rankings rest on evaluation procedures that are fragile, potentially contaminated, or difficult to reproduce, the downstream consequences are real—and growing. 3 LLM 奥林匹克竞赛：密封评估协议The LLM Olympiad: A Sealed Evaluation Protocol

我们提出了一个受奥林匹克启发的语言模型评估活动：过程是可预测的，但直到评分时问题才被发现。 目标不是取代现有的基准，而是偶尔添加一个支持更高保证声明的“检查”。We propose an Olympiad-inspired evaluation event for language models: the process is predictable, but the problems are not revealed until scoring. The goal is not to replace existing benchmarks, but to add an occasional “exam” that supports higher-assurance claims.

3.2 设计原则Design principles

该协议依赖于两种分离机制。 首先，The protocol rests on two separation mechanisms. First,任务密封task sealing：在评分开始之前，评估问题（甚至问题类型）都会保密，因此参与者无法针对特定任务。 第二，: evaluation problems—and even problem types—are kept confidential until scoring begins, so that participants cannot target specific tasks. Second,提交冻结submission freezing：在显示任何任务内容之前提交模型工件或端点版本，从而防止最后一刻的调整。 如果可行，提交的内容也会在评分过程中进行匿名处理，以减少任何人工判断部分的偏见。 至关重要的是，这两种形式的分离都不是永久性的：任务、评分代码和分析都会在事件发生后发布，以便社区可以审核、重现和构建结果: model artifacts or endpoint versions are committed before any task content is revealed, preventing last-minute tuning. When feasible, submissions are also anonymized during scoring to reduce bias in any human-judged components. Crucially, neither form of separation is permanent: tasks, scoring code, and analysis are released after the event so the community can audit, reproduce, and build on the results(Liang et al., 2022).

3.3 为什么需要它Why it is needed

法学硕士时代的三个压力推动了这一立场，并且每个压力都可以通过基于事件的协议来更好地解决，而不是通过对现有格式的增量修复。Three LLM-era pressures motivate this position, and each is better addressed by an event-based protocol than by incremental repairs to existing formats.

脆弱性。Fragility.

5 威胁模型Threat Model

我们假设强大的优化压力：参与者将努力推动绩效，如果存在模糊性，就会被利用，组织者可能会犯下导致结果偏差的错误。 这种威胁模型的目标不是假设敌对意图，而是设计一种程序，使博弈变得更加困难，并使故障更容易检测。We assume strong optimization pressure: participants will push hard for performance, ambiguity will be exploited if it exists, and organizers can make mistakes that bias outcomes. For the system track, tool use can bypass constraints unless access is controlled and logged; prompt injection is a concrete system risk(Liu et al., 2023)。 缓解措施包括工具代理（组织者控制的网关）、预算和工具调用日志记录. Mitigations include tool proxies (organizer-controlled gateways), budgets, and logging for tool calls(Liu et al., 2023).

第四阶段：报告和解释。Phase 4: Reporting and interpretation.

即使评估是干净的，如果报告将异构任务折叠成单个数字或者聚合不明确，结果仍然可能被误读。 排行榜排序可能取决于聚合选择Even if evaluation is clean, results can still be misread if reporting collapses heterogeneous tasks into a single number or if aggregation is unclear. Leaderboard ordering can depend on aggregation choices(Tatiana and Valentin, 2021)。 缓解措施是强大的报告：发布每个任务和每个轨道的结果，明确聚合，并报告预算和分数. Mitigation is robust reporting: publish per-task and per-track results, make aggregation explicit, and report budgets alongside scores(Liang et al., 2022)。 如果包括定性评估，则应将其视为诊断信号而不是主要排名信号，因为人类评估可能难以重现. 5.3 风险和诚实限制Risks and honesty limits

即使有严格的程序，奥林匹克式的评估也不能保证完美。 相反，它应该使剩余的不确定性Even with strong procedures, an Olympiad-style evaluation cannot promise perfect guarantees. Instead, it should make the remaining uncertainty明确的explicit因此结果得到适当的解释。so results are interpreted appropriately.

污染仍然是可能的。Contamination remains possible.

密封任务减少了事前目标，但不能保证网络规模的零污染Sealing tasks reduces pre-event targeting, but it cannot guarantee zero contamination at web scale(Xu et al., 2024)。 重叠可以通过重复、释义或间接包含在训练混合物中而发生。 因此，奥林匹克成绩应如下所示. Overlap can occur through duplication, paraphrase, or indirect inclusion in training mixtures. For this reason, Olympiad results should be framed as更有力的证据stronger evidence与典型的公共排行榜相比，它表明了总体准备情况，而不是作为“不暴露”的数学证明。 这也是为什么事后发布很重要：一旦任务公开，社区就可以进行重叠检查并审查令人惊讶的结果。of general preparedness than typical public leaderboards, not as a mathematical proof of “no exposure.” This is also why post-hoc release matters: once tasks are public, the community can run overlap checks and scrutinize surprising outcomes.

封闭端点的保证低于集中运行的工件。Closed endpoints are lower assurance than centrally run artifacts.

封闭端点可以实现更广泛的参与，但本质上更难审计。 端点可能会漂移、通过未公开的组件路由请求或改变负载下的行为——有时是无意的。 因此，我们建议在报告中将开放权重（组织者运行）和封闭权重（端点运行）标记为不同的保证层Closed endpoints enable broader participation, but they are intrinsically harder to audit. Endpoints can drift, route requests through undisclosed components, or change behavior under load—sometimes unintentionally. We therefore recommend labeling open-weights (organizer-run) and closed-weights (endpoint-run) as different assurance tiers in reporting(Reddi et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2022)。 这并不意味着排除封闭模型；其目的是防止苹果与橘子的比较被夸大。 我们承认版本承诺和时间限制是程序保障，而不是技术保证——它们无法检测端点后面隐藏的路由或未公开的工具使用。 因此，奥运会最强有力的保证声明适用于公开重量赛，组织者控制着整个执行环境。. This is not meant to exclude closed models; it is meant to prevent apples-to-oranges comparisons from being overstated. We acknowledge that version commitments and time-boxing are procedural safeguards, not technical guarantees—they cannot detect hidden routing or undisclosed tool use behind an endpoint. For this reason, the Olympiad’s strongest assurance claims apply to the open-weights track, where organizers control the full execution environment.

线束可能是错误的。The harness can be wrong.

集中评估减少了参与者的自由度，但将责任集中在了安全带上。 错误、边缘情况和看似很小的选择（超时、重试、聚合）都会影响结果。 最好的防御是程序性的：预先注册安全策略，要求错误修复触发重新运行所有提交，并在评分后发布任务和代码，以便社区可以复制和批评评估Centralized evaluation reduces participant-side degrees of freedom, but it concentrates responsibility in the harness. Bugs, edge cases, and seemingly small choices (timeouts, retries, aggregation) can affect outcomes. The best defense is procedural: preregister harness policies, require that bug fixes trigger re-running all submissions, and release tasks and code after scoring so the community can replicate and critique the evaluation(Liang et al., 2022).

小任务集可能不具有代表性。A small task set may not be representative.

由于密封任务屈指可数，奥赛面临着移动“基准抽签”的风险With only a handful of sealed tasks, the Olympiad risks moving the “benchmark lottery”(Dehghani et al., 2021)上游：排名可能反映了任务委员会碰巧包含的功能，而不是广泛的模型质量。 有几种设计选择可以缓解这种情况。 首先，公开征集（而不是委员会设计的任务）使人才库多样化，超出了组织者的口味。 其次，管理阶段可以强制实施明确的覆盖目标——需要来自不同能力系列的任务（例如提取、推理、生成、鲁棒性），因此没有单一技能主导总分。 第三，我们建议将按任务细分作为主要报告格式，让社区能够评估模型的排名是由真正的广度还是由一些有利的任务驱动。 即便如此，一个小事件不可避免地会稀疏地采样能力空间，并且汇总排名应该被解释为一个快照而不是一个全面的结论。 增加迭代中的任务多样性是试点计划的目标之一（附录upstream: rankings may reflect which capabilities the task committee happened to include rather than broad model quality. Several design choices can mitigate this. First, open solicitation (rather than committee-designed tasks) diversifies the pool beyond organizer taste. Second, the curation stage can enforce explicit coverage targets—requiring tasks from distinct capability families (e.g., extraction, reasoning, generation, robustness) so no single skill dominates the aggregate score. Third, per-task breakdowns, which we recommend as the primary reporting format, allow the community to assess whether a model’s ranking is driven by genuine breadth or by a few favorable tasks. Even so, a small event will inevitably sample capability space sparsely, and aggregate rankings should be interpreted as one snapshot rather than a comprehensive verdict. Increasing task diversity across iterations is one of the goals of the pilot plan (AppendixC).

6 结论Conclusion

法学硕士时代使评估变得更加重要，也更加难以信任。 开放基准最大限度地提高透明度，但很容易确定目标；封闭的基准测试可以保护测试集，但减少审查； 共享任务可以提高公平性，但通常会衡量已知目标的性能。 如果我们想要更有分量的分数——更难玩弄、更容易解释的分数——我们需要一个额外的工具。The LLM era has made evaluation both more important and harder to trust. Open benchmarks maximize transparency but are easy to target; closed benchmarks protect test sets but reduce scrutiny; shared tasks improve fairness but often measure performance on a known target. If we want scores that carry more weight—scores that are harder to game and easier to interpret—we need an additional tool.

我们建议举办奥运会式的密封评估活动作为该工具。 密封问题，冻结提交，让每个人都在一个安全带下运行，然后发布所有内容，以便社区可以审核 并从结果中学习。 这不会取代现有的基准；它偶尔会增加一次“检查”，当风险较高时，其结果会更加可信。 如果社区每年采用一次这样的活动，我们就会获得当前格式无法提供的东西：一个共享的、可重复的、高可信度的检查点，该领域可以指出并说，“这就是这些模型实际上可以做的事情，公平地衡量，解决没有人见过的问题。”We propose an Olympiad-style, sealed evaluation event as that tool. Seal the problems, freeze submissions, run everyone under one harness, and then release everything afterward so the community can audit and learn from the outcomes. This does not replace existing benchmarks; it adds an occasional “exam” whose results are more credible when the stakes are high. If the community adopts even one such event per year, we gain something no current format provides: a shared, reproducible, high-assurance checkpoint that the field can point to and say, “this is what these models could actually do, measured fairly, on problems no one had seen.”

局限性Limitations

这是一份立场文件：我们提出一项协议，但不报告已实施的奥林匹克竞赛的结果。 我们引用的经验证据（第 3.3 节）表明，激发我们提案的问题——污染、排名不稳定和选择性披露——是真实存在的，但它并不构成对协议本身的测试；这需要一名飞行员。 一些运营选择（治理、基础设施、预算和赛道定义）将取决于场地限制，并且可能需要迭代。 该提案还引入了一个不小的治理负担——密封评估需要可信的组织者、利益冲突政策和本身就可能失败的变更控制程序。 为未经证实的活动招募高质量的任务贡献是一个进一步的实际挑战，因为任务作者承担着努力和保密的限制，而没有保证回报。 密封任务减少了基准追逐，但无法消除污染风险，特别是对于封闭模型和网络规模训练。 因此，我们将其视为补充性的、更高保证的评估层，而不是现有基准的替代品。This is a position paper: we propose a protocol and do not report results from an implemented Olympiad. The empirical evidence we cite (Section 3.3) demonstrates that the problems motivating our proposal—contamination, rank instability, and selective disclosure—are real, but it does not constitute a test of the protocol itself; that requires a pilot. Several operational choices (governance, infrastructure, budgets, and track definitions) will depend on venue constraints and will likely require iteration. The proposal also introduces a nontrivial governance burden—sealed evaluation requires trusted organizers, conflict-of-interest policies, and change-control procedures that are themselves subject to failure. Recruiting high-quality task contributions to an unproven event is a further practical challenge, since task authors bear effort and confidentiality constraints without guaranteed return. Sealed tasks reduce benchmark-chasing but cannot eliminate contamination risk, especially for closed models and web-scale training. We therefore frame this as a complementary, higher-assurance evaluation layer rather than a replacement for existing benchmarks.

道德考虑Ethical Considerations

本文提出一个评价This paper proposes an evaluation程序procedure而不是新的模型或数据集。 即便如此，程序设计也具有伦理意义，因为它决定了激励、参与以及社区认为哪些主张是可信的。rather than a new model or dataset. Even so, procedure design has ethical implications because it shapes incentives, participation, and what kinds of claims the community treats as credible.

准入和公平。Access and fairness.

如果参与需要大量计算预算或专门的基础设施，密封任务活动可能会无意中让资源充足的团队获得特权。 为了缓解这种情况，活动应该发布明确的预算类别（时间/令牌/工具限制），为开放权重和封闭端点提供单独的轨道，并在分数旁边报告预算。 在可行的情况下，组织者可以通过资助少数学术团队的评估或提供低预算的基线轨道来进一步提高可及性。A sealed-task event could unintentionally privilege well-resourced teams if participation requires large compute budgets or specialized infrastructure. To mitigate this, the event should publish clear budget classes (time/token/tool limits), provide separate tracks for open-weights and closed endpoints, and report budgets next to scores. Where feasible, organizers can further improve accessibility by subsidizing evaluation for a small number of academic teams or by offering a low-budget baseline track.

数据来源和隐私。Data provenance and privacy.

任务提案应包括许可和出处信息，组织者应避免需要个人数据或敏感内容的任务。 当任务涉及现实世界的文本时，组织者应优先选择具有明确权限的来源，并删除个人识别信息。 当任务集在活动结束后发布时，随附的文档应描述数据源、预期用途和已知限制。Task proposals should include licensing and provenance information, and organizers should avoid tasks that require personal data or sensitive content. When tasks involve real-world text, organizers should prefer sources with clear permissions and remove personally identifying information. When the task set is released after the event, accompanying documentation should describe data sources, intended use, and known limitations.

双重用途和安全性。Dual use and security.

一些面向系统的评估（例如，即时注入压力测试）可以揭示对攻击者也有用的漏洞。 因此，组织者应考虑对安全敏感任务采取负责任的发布做法，例如延迟发布、对漏洞利用字符串进行部分编辑或在适当时协调披露。 更一般地说，分析报告应避免为滥用提供逐步指导，同时仍报告科学发现。Some system-oriented evaluations (e.g., prompt injection stress tests) can reveal vulnerabilities that are also useful to attackers. Organizers should therefore consider responsible release practices for security-sensitive tasks, such as delayed release, partial redaction of exploit strings, or coordinated disclosure when appropriate. More generally, the analysis report should avoid providing step-by-step guidance for misuse while still reporting scientific findings.

负责任的沟通。Responsible communication.

由于竞争性排行榜可能会鼓励过度解释，因此活动应强调每个任务的细分、能力概况和记录的限制，而不是单一的“赢者通吃”数字。 保证层的清晰标签（例如，开放权重与封闭端点）有助于防止结果呈现超出协议支持的确定性。Because competitive leaderboards can encourage overinterpretation, the event should emphasize per-task breakdowns, capability profiles, and documented constraints rather than a single “winner-takes-all” number. Clear labeling of assurance tiers (e.g., open-weights vs. closed endpoint) helps prevent results from being presented with more certainty than the protocol can support.

附录AAppendix A实际考虑Practical Considerations

本附录总结了影响成本和可审计性的基础设施、治理和报告的实际设计选择。 目标不是规定单一设计，而是明确权衡，以便研讨会可以选择现实的第一个版本。This appendix summarizes practical design choices for infrastructure, governance, and reporting that affect cost and auditability. The goal is not to prescribe a single design, but to make the tradeoffs explicit so a workshop can choose a realistic first version.

A.1 基础设施Infrastructure

奥林匹克运动会需要基础设施来支持两个可能存在紧张关系的目标：标准化（每个人都以相同的方式进行测试） 和可访问性（并非每个强大的模型都可以作为开放权重提交）。 实用的设置支持两种提交模式。The Olympiad needs infrastructure that supports two goals that can be in tension: standardization (everyone is tested the same way) and accessibility (not every strong model can be submitted as open weights). A practical setup supports two submission modes.

自由重量级（组织者运行）。Open-weights (organizer-run).

参与者提交一个打包的工件（例如，一个容器），该工件公开了一个简单的推理接口。 组织者在受控环境中的固定硬件类别上运行它。 这是最高保证的模式，因为它最大限度地减少了执行哪些代码以及在什么条件下执行的不确定性Participants submit a packaged artifact (e.g., a container) that exposes a simple inference interface. Organizers run it on a fixed hardware class in a controlled environment. This is the highest-assurance mode because it minimizes uncertainty about what code is executed and under what conditions(Reddi et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2022)。 为了减少摩擦，保持界面稳定（例如，“给定一个输入文件，写入一个输出文件”，或具有固定模式的本地服务器）， 并尽早发布硬件“类别”（即使试点仅支持一层）。. To reduce friction, keep the interface stable (e.g., “given an input file, write an output file,” or a local server with a fixed schema), and publish hardware “classes” early (even if a pilot supports only one tier).

封闭权重（端点运行）。Closed-weights (endpoint-run).

参与者提供端点以及版本承诺（和冻结窗口）。 组织者运行一个评估客户端来实施速率限制、请求预算和日志记录。 这些结果应被视为报告中的单独保证层。Participants provide an endpoint plus a version commitment (and a freeze window). Organizers run a single evaluation client that enforces rate limits, request budgets, and logging. These results should be treated as a separate assurance tier in reporting.

在这两种模式中，多种基础设施选择可以减少争议： (i) 发布决定论和重新运行政策； (ii) 执行线束中的预算（最大输入/输出长度、每个项目的最大挂钟时间以及系统的工具调用限制）； (iii) 记录足够的元数据以供事后审核（提交 ID、版本哈希、适用时的解码设置、运行时和工具调用）； (iv) 如果允许使用工具，则通过组织者控制的代理路由工具访问，以便可以记录和限制呼叫， 这也有助于管理已知的系统风险，例如提示注入Across both modes, several infrastructure choices reduce disputes: (i) publish a determinism and re-run policy; (ii) enforce budgets in the harness (max input/output length, max wall-clock time per item, and tool-call limits for systems); (iii) log enough metadata for post-hoc audit (submission ID, version hash, decoding settings when applicable, runtime, and tool calls); and (iv) if tools are allowed, route tool access through organizer-controlled proxies so calls can be logged and limited, which also helps manage known system risks like prompt injection(Liu et al., 2023)。 最后，密封任务存储应被视为核心基础设施：将任务存储在限制访问的存储库中，并在评估之前冻结档案。. Finally, sealed task storage should be treated as core infrastructure: store tasks in a restricted-access repository and freeze archives before evaluation.

A.2 治理Governance

密封评估增加了对明确治理的需求。 可行的结构将角色分开，因此没有一个组具有端到端控制： 指导委员会（规则和发布计划）、任务委员会（策划和密封）、 评估操作员（运行工具）和报告团队（分析和撰写）。 如果可能，任务作者不应与评估和报告角色重叠。Sealed evaluation increases the need for clear governance. A workable structure separates roles so no single group has end-to-end control: a steering committee (rules and release plan), a task committee (curation and sealing), evaluation operators (running the harness), and a report team (analysis and write-up). Where possible, task authors should not overlap with evaluation and reporting roles.

提前发布的关键政策包括：利益冲突政策（提交者不访问密封任务）， 保密窗口（谁可以访问任务以及任务何时会被披露）， 和更改控制（冻结后没有任何更改，除了公开的错误修复过程会触发重新运行所有提交）。 对错误和线束故障进行评分的轻量级上诉流程也很重要，因为大多数争议都是由边缘情况引起的。 在评估之前发布密封包的公共指纹（哈希）是一种简单的审计做法，可以减轻信任负担Key policies to publish in advance include: a conflict-of-interest policy (submitters do not access sealed tasks), a confidentiality window (who can access tasks and when tasks will be revealed), and change control (no changes after freeze except a disclosed bug-fix process that triggers re-running all submissions). A lightweight appeals process for scoring bugs and harness failures is also important, since most disputes arise from edge cases. Publishing a public fingerprint (hash) of the sealed bundle before evaluation is a simple audit practice that reduces the trust burden(Raji et al., 2020).

A.3 报告Reporting

报道使奥林匹克竞赛成为有用的科学，而不是一次性的竞赛。 我们推荐三层。Reporting is where the Olympiad becomes useful science rather than a one-off contest. We recommend three layers.

排行榜。Leaderboards.

按赛道（模型与系统）和访问模式（开放权重与封闭权重）分隔排行榜。 遵循透明、多指标评估的精神，报告总体分数以及每个任务的细分，并在分数旁边报告预算Separate leaderboards by track (model vs. system) and by access mode (open-weights vs. closed-weights). Report an overall score alongside per-task breakdowns, and report budgets next to scores, following the spirit of transparent, multi-metric evaluation(Liang et al., 2022)。 为了清楚起见，试点排行榜可以包括以下列：. For clarity, a pilot leaderboard can include the following columns:提交IDsubmission ID, 轨道track, 访问方式access mode, 总分overall score, 每项任务的分数per-task scores, 预算级budget class, 运行时间（中位数）runtime (median), 和, and错误率/超时error rate/timeouts.

分析报告。Analysis report.

包括常见故障模式的简短叙述、少量具体示例（如果需要，请仔细编辑）， 和基本的稳定性检查（例如，重复的项目或小的扰动）。 对于系统提交，报告工具使用统计信息（调用和失败）并记下该工具检测到的任何策略违规行为。Include a short narrative of common failure patterns, a small number of concrete examples (with careful redaction if needed), and basic stability checks (e.g., duplicated items or small perturbations). For system submissions, report tool-use statistics (calls and failures) and note any policy violations detected by the harness.

再现性捆绑。Reproducibility bundle.

结果发布后，发布密封任务、评分脚本和评估工具。 发布使评估可重播的运行清单：提交 ID、版本（或端点承诺）、预算类别、 解码策略、失败计数以及任何带有原因的排除。 如果无法发布详细日志（例如专有端点），则发布聚合元数据（运行时分布、错误计数、超时计数） 支持健全性检查。After results are released, publish the sealed tasks, scoring scripts, and evaluation harness. Release a run manifest that makes the evaluation replayable: submission IDs, versions (or endpoint commitments), budget class, decoding policy, failure counts, and any exclusions with reasons. If detailed logs cannot be released (e.g., proprietary endpoints), release aggregate metadata (runtime distribution, error counts, timeout counts) to support sanity checks.

本节概述了适合第一次研讨会迭代的最小试点。 目的是从小事做起，展示价值并迭代。This section outlines a minimal pilot suitable for a first workshop iteration. The aim is to start small, demonstrate value, and iterate.

C.1 范围和轨迹Scope and tracks

一个合理的飞行员可以从两个轨道开始： (一)A reasonable pilot can start with two tracks: (i)模型/开放重量Model / open-weights（组织者运行），以及 (二)(organizer-run), and (ii)模型/闭合重量Model / closed-weights（端点运行），报告为单独的保证层。 如果有能力，在第一年将系统赛道添加为非竞争性（已报告，但未根据模型赛道进行排名）， 因此，工具治理和利用规则可以在影响主要排名之前成熟。(endpoint-run), reported as a separate assurance tier. C.2 时间轴Timeline

一种可行的时间表是： (1) 征集任务（4-6周）； (2) 保管和密封（4周）； (3) 征集作品（4-6 周）； (4) 硬冻结期限； （5）评估窗口（1-2周）； (6) 与研讨会一致的结果发布；和 (7) 2-4周内事后发布任务和代码。One workable schedule is: (1) call for tasks (4–6 weeks); (2) curation and sealing (4 weeks); (3) call for submissions (4–6 weeks); (4) a hard freeze deadline; (5) evaluation window (1–2 weeks); (6) results release aligned with the workshop; and (7) post-hoc release of tasks and code within 2–4 weeks.

C.3 提交合同Submission contract

保持提交合同简单。 开放权重提交提供了一个公开单个推理入口点（CLI 或本地服务器）的容器， 声明的上下文/输出限制，以及所需的“试运行”格式检查。 封闭权重提交提供端点加身份验证方法（如果需要）， 版本承诺声明和冻结窗口协议，并声明限制，以便组织者进行预算。 在这两个轨道中，都需要一个简短的元数据表单（在公开之前保密）来描述解码设置以及可能影响输出的任何拒绝/安全策略。Keep submission contracts simple. Open-weights submissions provide a container exposing a single inference entry point (CLI or local server), declared context/output limits, and a required “dry run” format check. Closed-weights submissions provide an endpoint plus authentication method (if needed), a version commitment statement and freeze window agreement, and declared limits so organizers can budget. In both tracks, require a short metadata form (kept private until reveal) describing decoding settings and any refusal/safety policies that may affect outputs.

C.4 评价量表Evaluation scale

飞行员应该优先考虑干净地完成而不是庞大。 粗略目标是 5-10 个任务，每个任务有 100-500 个实例（总共 1k-5k 个实例）， 加上少量的一致性探针（重复或轻微扰动）以较低的额外成本测量稳定性。 限制每个实例的运行时间，以便完整的评估可以在几天而不是几周内完成。A pilot should prioritize finishing cleanly over being large. A rough target is 5–10 tasks with 100–500 instances each (1k–5k total instances), plus a small number of consistency probes (duplicates or minor perturbations) to measure stability at low additional cost. Cap per-instance runtime so the full evaluation can complete in days rather than weeks.

C.5 成功标准Success criteria

如果试点成功： (i) 操作可行性（安全带运行可靠，政策减少争议）； (ii) 可比性（结果较少依赖于隐藏的评估选择）； (iii) 学习价值（报告揭示了可操作的失败模式）；和 (iv) 可审核性（发布后，至少一个独立小组可以重现提交子集的结果或在新模型上重新运行该工具）。A pilot succeeds if it demonstrates: (i) operational feasibility (the harness runs reliably and policies reduce disputes); (ii) comparability (results depend less on hidden evaluation choices); (iii) learning value (the report surfaces actionable failure patterns); and (iv) auditability (after release, at least one independent group can reproduce results for a subset of submissions or re-run the harness on a new model).

C.6 第一年后的迭代Iteration after year one

如果试点成功，迭代应重点关注： 添加具有更强工具治理的成熟系统轨道， 扩大任务多样性，同时保留可验证的评分， 并维护一个小型未发布的项目库，以便每年保持新鲜感，而不会重复使用相同的问题。If the pilot is successful, iteration should focus on: adding a mature system track with stronger tool governance, expanding task diversity while preserving verifiable scoring, and maintaining a small unreleased item bank so each year stays fresh without reusing the same problems.
