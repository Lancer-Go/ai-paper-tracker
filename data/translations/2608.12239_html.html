I 简介Introduction

学习图像压缩（LIC）的最新进展已使其成为图像和视频编码领域的领先范例，与传统手工编解码器相比，在率失真性能方面取得了显着改进。 通过联合优化非线性变换和概率熵模型，LIC 系统能够学习紧凑的潜在表示，从而在保持重建质量的同时显着提高压缩效率。 尽管具有这些优势，现代 LIC 架构的计算和内存要求仍然无法在资源受限的设备上部署，从而限制了它们在现实场景中的实际适用性。Recent advances in learned image compression (LIC) have established it as a leading paradigm in image and video coding, achieving significant improvements in rate–distortion performance over traditional handcrafted codecs. By jointly optimizing nonlinear transforms and probabilistic entropy models, LIC systems are able to learn compact latent representations that substantially enhance compression efficiency while preserving reconstruction quality. Despite these advantages, the computational and memory requirements of modern LIC architectures remain prohibitive for deployment on resource-constrained devices, limiting their practical applicability in real-world scenarios.

从更广泛的角度来看，现有的 LIC 量化方法仍然面临着效率、准确性和通用性之间的根本权衡。均匀量化策略虽然简单，但无法反映网络组件之间高度不均匀的冗余，导致精度分配效率低下。相比之下，为一般视觉任务开发的混合精度方法通常是针对分类网络定制的，并依赖于启发式灵敏度度量或昂贵的搜索程序，这使得它们很难通过率失真优化和熵约束的潜在表示转移到 LIC 设置。此外，LIC模型具有独特的结构特征，例如基于超先验的熵建模和迭代分析-综合变换，这进一步使灵敏度估计和比特分配变得复杂。这些差异凸显了专门为 LIC 设计的专用混合精度量化策略的必要性。From a broader perspective, existing LIC quantization methods still face a fundamental trade-off between efficiency, accuracy, and generality. Uniform quantization strategies, while simple, fail to reflect the highly non-uniform redundancy across network components, leading to inefficient precision allocation. In contrast, mixed-precision approaches developed for general vision tasks are often tailored to classification networks and rely on heuristic sensitivity metrics or expensive search procedures, making them difficult to transfer to LIC settings with rate–distortion optimization and entropy-constrained latent representations. Moreover, LIC models possess unique structural characteristics, such as hyperprior-based entropy modeling and iterative analysis–synthesis transforms, which further complicate sensitivity estimation and bit allocation. These differences highlight the necessity of a dedicated mixed-precision quantization strategy specifically designed for LIC.

Ⅱ-CII-C 混合精度量化Mixed Precision Quantization

混合精度量化利用了神经网络中不同层对量化表现出不同敏感性的观察结果Mixed-precision quantization exploits the observation that different layers in a neural network exhibit varying sensitivity to quantization[9, 27]。它不是在所有层上应用统一的位宽，而是为更敏感的层分配更高的精度，为不太敏感的层分配更低的精度，从而在模型大小、计算效率和准确性之间实现更好的权衡。这种自适应分配对于深度架构特别有益，因为量化误差可能会在各层之间不均匀地累积。. Instead of applying a uniform bit-width across all layers, it assigns higher precision to more sensitive layers and lower precision to less sensitive ones, thereby achieving a better trade-off between model size, computational efficiency, and accuracy. This adaptive allocation is particularly beneficial for deep architectures, where quantization errors can accumulate unevenly across layers.

大多数现有方法Most existing approaches[29, 32]将混合精度量化制定为全局优化问题，旨在在模型大小、延迟或能耗等约束下确定每层的最佳位宽配置。这些方法通常将位宽选择集成到训练过程中，利用基于梯度的优化或强化学习来联合优化网络权重和精度分配。虽然有效，但由于搜索空间较大，此类方法通常会产生大量计算开销。formulate mixed-precision quantization as a global optimization problem, aiming to determine the optimal bit-width configuration for each layer under constraints such as model size, latency, or energy consumption. These methods typically integrate bit-width selection into the training process, leveraging gradient-based optimization or reinforcement learning to jointly optimize network weights and precision assignments. While effective, such approaches often incur substantial computational overhead due to the large search space.

These approaches first estimate the quantization sensitivity of each layer in a pre-trained model, typically based on metrics such as reconstruction error or loss perturbation. Bit-widths are then allocated by ranking layers according to their sensitivity, assigning higher precision to layers that are more critical to overall performance. This strategy significantly reduces the search complexity while maintaining competitive accuracy.

V 结论Conclusion
