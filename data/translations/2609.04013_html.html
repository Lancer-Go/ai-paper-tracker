摘要Abstract

慢性肾脏病 (CKD) 的早期筛查对于及时干预至关重要，但大多数机器学习 (ML) 和深度学习 (DL) 方法需要标记数据和模型训练，这限制了它们在现实世界筛查环境中的使用。本研究评估了大语言模型 (LLM) 在零样本和少样本上下文学习设置下用于 CKD 筛查的有效性，并将其与传统的 ML 和 DL 方法进行比较。我们提出了一个框架，该框架使用临床选择的表格特征和结构化提示模板来实现基于 LLM 的推理，而无需特定于任务的培训。 LLM 性能通过多种提示样式、功能配置和数据设置进行评估，并与标准 ML、DL 和表格基础模型 (TFM) 基线以及现有 CKD 筛选工具进行比较。结果表明，法学硕士仅使用少量示例即可实现具有竞争力的表现，通常在低数据环境下匹配或优于传统方法。然而，它们的性能仍然依赖于模型，并且随着输入复杂性的增加而不稳定。相比之下，ML、DL 和 TFM 模型在更大的训练数据下表现出更一致的改进。总体而言，研究结果强调了数据效率和稳定性之间的权衡，表明当标记数据有限时，法学硕士可以作为 CKD 筛查的灵活补充方法。Early screening of chronic kidney disease (CKD) is critical for timely intervention, yet most machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) approaches require labeled data and model training, limiting their use in real-world screening settings. This study evaluates the effectiveness of large language models (LLMs) for CKD screening under zero-shot and few-shot in-context learning settings and compares them with traditional ML and DL methods. We propose a framework that uses clinically selected tabular features and structured prompt templates to enable LLM-based inference without task-specific training. LLM performance is evaluated across multiple prompt styles, feature configurations, and data settings, and compared with standard ML, DL, and tabular foundation model (TFM) baselines, and existing CKD screening tools. The results show that LLMs can achieve competitive performance using only a small number of examples, often matching or outperforming traditional approaches in low-data settings. However, their performance remains model-dependent and less stable as input complexity increases. In contrast, ML, DL, and TFM models show more consistent improvement with larger training data. Overall, the findings highlight a trade-off between data efficiency and stability, suggesting that LLMs may serve as a flexible complementary approach for CKD screening when labeled data are limited.

I 简介Introduction

慢性肾病 (CKD) 是一种进行性疾病，其特征是肾功能持续丧失或肾损伤持续标志物。如果未被发现，CKD 可能会发展为不可逆的肾功能衰退和终末期肾病，需要昂贵的肾脏替代治疗Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is a progressive condition characterized by sustained loss of kidney function or persistent markers of kidney damage. If undetected, CKD can progress to irreversible renal decline and end-stage renal disease, requiring costly renal replacement therapy[18]。 CKD 影响着全球约 8.5 亿人，占全球人口的近 10%. CKD affects approximately 850 million people worldwide, nearly 10% of the global population[3]。低收入和中等收入国家 (LMIC) 的负担尤其严重，近 80% 的 CKD 患者居住在这些国家，其患病率和死亡率高于高收入国家. The burden is particularly severe in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), where nearly 80% of individuals with CKD reside and where prevalence and mortality are higher than in high-income settings[25, 4]。在南亚，基于社区的研究报告称 CKD 患病率较高，但认知度较低，其中孟加拉国的研究显示，农村和城郊人口的患病率约为 22%. In South Asia, community-based studies report high CKD prevalence and low awareness, including studies from Bangladesh showing prevalence around 22% in rural and peri-urban populations[24].

早期 CKD 在临床上通常是无症状的，但及时发现可以采取血压控制、糖尿病管理和肾保护治疗等干预措施，从而减缓疾病进展Early-stage CKD is often clinically silent, yet timely detection enables interventions such as blood pressure control, diabetes management, and nephroprotective therapy that can slow disease progression[27]。标准筛查取决于实验室测量，包括血清肌酐、估计肾小球滤过率 (eGFR) 和白蛋白肌酐比 (ACR)，这需要诊断基础设施和训练有素的人员. Standard screening depends on laboratory measurements, including serum creatinine, estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR), and albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR), which require diagnostic infrastructure and trained personnel[26]。这些要求限制了农村和资源有限地区的人群筛查，这些地区的实验室检测可能不可用、成本高昂或记录不一致。因此，需要可扩展、数据高效且上下文感知的 CKD 筛查方法，这些方法可以在异构功能可用性下运行。. These requirements limit population-level screening in rural and resource-constrained settings, where laboratory tests may be unavailable, costly, or inconsistently recorded. Consequently, there is a need for scalable, data-efficient, and context-aware CKD screening methods that can operate under heterogeneous feature availability.

传统的机器学习 (ML) 和深度学习 (DL) 模型已显示出 CKD 检测的前景，但大多数依赖于固定的特征空间、精心策划的数据集和稳定的部署条件Conventional machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) models have shown promise for CKD detection, but most rely on fixed feature spaces, well-curated datasets, and stable deployment conditions[23]。这些假设在现实世界的临床筛查中经常被违反：生物标志物可能缺失，特征定义可能因地点而异，并且人群分布可能发生变化。尽管插补和特征屏蔽方法可以部分解决缺失问题，但当早期 CKD 信号微妙时，它们可能会降低可靠性. These assumptions are often violated in real-world clinical screening: biomarkers may be missing, feature definitions may differ across sites, and population distributions may shift. Although imputation and feature-masking methods can partially address missingness, they may reduce reliability when early-stage CKD signals are subtle[14]。 之前的 CKD 预测研究依赖于具有固定特征集和标记训练数据的传统监督机器学习流程. Prior CKD prediction studies have relied on conventional supervised ML pipelines with fixed feature sets and labeled training data[8]。虽然在受控评估环境下有效，但当在缺少变量、异质输入或变化的群体特征的筛选环境中部署时，此类模型的适应性较差。. While effective under controlled evaluation settings, such models are less adaptable when deployed across screening contexts with missing variables, heterogeneous inputs, or shifting population characteristics.

大语言模型 (LLM) 为 CKD 筛查提供了补充方法。法学硕士可以执行零样本和少样本上下文学习Large language models (LLMs) offer a complementary approach to CKD screening. LLMs can perform zero-shot and few-shot in-context learning[2]，减少对大型标记数据集的依赖。 最近的一项研究, reducing their reliance on large labeled datasets. A recent study[9]结果表明，相对于全特征输入，特征引导的零样本提示可以匹配或提高 CKD 筛选性能。 这些功能与 CKD 筛查尤其相关，其中风险因素多种多样，数据可用性因人群和护理环境而异showed that feature-guided zero-shot prompting can match or improve CKD screening performance relative to full-feature inputs. These capabilities are particularly relevant to CKD screening, where risk factors are heterogeneous and data availability varies across populations and care settings[6]。然而，法学硕士在多大程度上可以作为定量 CKD 筛查模型，超出其在临床推理和交流中的既定作用，仍有待探索。. However, the extent to which LLMs can function as quantitative CKD screening models, beyond their established roles in clinical reasoning and communication, remains underexplored.

Ⅰ-AI-A 相关工作和研究差距Related Work and Research Gaps

现有的 CKD 筛查工具和风险评分提供了有用的社区级评估机制，但许多工具和风险评分是使用来自高收入国家的队列开发的，并且通常强调晚期疾病Existing CKD screening tools and risk scores provide useful community-level assessment mechanisms, but many were developed using cohorts from high-income countries and often emphasize later-stage disease[28]。这限制了它们在中低收入国家的适用性，因为这些国家最需要早期检测，而且实验室准入、人口特征和医疗保健途径存在很大差异。同样，基于 ML 和 DL 的 CKD 预测研究通常假设完整或一致测量的特征集，这使得它们对于现实世界筛查环境中常见的缺失变量和异构输入的鲁棒性较差. This limits their applicability in LMICs, where early detection is most needed and where laboratory access, population characteristics, and healthcare pathways differ substantially. Similarly, ML- and DL-based CKD prediction studies typically assume complete or consistently measured feature sets, making them less robust to missing variables and heterogeneous inputs that are common in real-world screening environments[23].

最近的工作探索了法学硕士在一系列肾脏病学和 CKD 相关应用中的应用，大多数研究侧重于临床支持、知识提取和面向患者的沟通。法学硕士已接受肾脏病理学支持、肾病学推理、活检建议和以指南为导向的决策支持方面的评估Recent work has explored LLMs across a range of nephrology and CKD-related applications, with most studies focusing on clinical support, knowledge extraction, and patient-facing communication. LLMs have been evaluated for kidney pathology support, nephrology reasoning, biopsy recommendation, and guideline-oriented decision support[29, 31, 32]。其他研究已将法学硕士应用于混合预测管道中的 CKD 知识图构建和特征解释. Other studies have applied LLMs to CKD knowledge-graph construction and feature interpretation within hybrid prediction pipelines[12, 16]以及患者教育和其他肾脏相关应用, as well as patient education and other kidney-related applications[17, 19]。评论同样强调了它们在总结、决策支持、教育和知识建模方面的潜力，同时指出了对可靠性、一致性和临床验证的担忧. Reviews similarly highlight their potential for summarization, decision support, education, and knowledge modeling, while noting concerns regarding reliability, consistency, and clinical validation[6, 23]。最近的一项研究评估了从结构化患者数据中进行的零样本 CKD 筛查，结果表明，相对于全特征输入，ML 选择的特征通常匹配或改善了 LLM 性能. A recent study evaluated zero-shot CKD screening from structured patient data and showed that ML-selected features generally matched or improved LLM performance relative to full-feature inputs[9].

尽管取得了这些进展，但仍存在一些差距。 首先，基于 LLM 的 CKD 筛选超出零样本特征引导推理的行为，特别是在少样本设置下，仍未得到充分探索。其次，据我们所知，之前没有研究系统地将零样本和少样本 LLM 与用于 CKD 筛查的传统 ML、DL 和表格基础模型基线进行比较。第三，LLM 衍生的特征重要性、基于 ML 的特征排名和已知的流行病学风险因素之间的一致性尚未得到系统检查。最后，关于基于法学硕士的跨数据集 CKD 筛查性能或与现有 CKD 风险评估工具的比较，证据有限。Despite this progress, several gaps remain. First, the behavior of LLM-based CKD screening beyond zero-shot feature-guided inference, particularly under few-shot settings, remains underexplored. Second, to our knowledge, no prior study has systematically compared zero- and few-shot LLMs with conventional ML, DL, and tabular foundation-model baselines for CKD screening. Third, the alignment between LLM-derived feature importance, ML-based feature ranking, and known epidemiological risk factors has not been systematically examined. Finally, limited evidence exists on LLM-based CKD screening performance across datasets or in comparison with established CKD risk assessment tools.

Ⅱ-BII-B 功能序列化和提示样式Feature Serialization and Prompt Style

为了使大型语言模型能够处理结构化表格数据，每个患者记录都通过特征序列化转换为文本表示。此步骤将协调的临床变量映射到文本中，同时保留特征名称、值编码和临床语义。相同的序列化过程应用于所有功能和选定功能设置，以确保跨提示配置和数据集进行可比较的评估。To enable large language models to process structured tabular data, each patient record is converted into a textual representation through feature serialization. Ⅲ-GIII-G 局限性Limitations

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，主要队列相对较小，这可能会限制性能估计的稳定性。尽管使用重复运行和独立数据集评估来减少这种担忧，但需要更大、更多样化的队列来获得更强的概括性主张。其次，LLM 的可靠性取决于即时设计、特征序列化、模型版本和令牌级对数概率的可用性；因此，结果可能会因未来的模型版本和部署设置而异。第三，所选的特征子集源自之前基于 ML 的分析，可能并不适合所有人群或临床环境。第四，独立数据集在抽样设置、特征可用性和病例对照组成方面与主要队列不同；因此，这些结果仅提供跨数据集性能的初步证据，而不是明确的临床验证。 最后，我们没有直接评估对患者级别缺失特征、异构输入、部署效率、临床安全性或成本的稳健性；这些因素以及前瞻性验证需要在临床使用前进行评估。This study has several limitations. First, the primary cohort is relatively small, which may limit the stability of performance estimates. Although repeated runs and an independent-dataset evaluation were used to reduce this concern, larger and more diverse cohorts are needed for stronger generalization claims. Second, LLM reliability depends on prompt design, feature serialization, model version, and availability of token-level log-probabilities; therefore, results may vary across future model releases and deployment settings. Third, the selected feature subset was derived from prior ML-based analysis and may not be optimal for all populations or clinical contexts. Fourth, the independent dataset differs from the primary cohort in sampling setting, feature availability, and case-control composition; therefore, these results provide only preliminary evidence of cross-dataset performance rather than definitive clinical validation. Finally, we did not directly evaluate robustness to patient-level missing features, heterogeneous inputs, deployment efficiency, clinical safety, or cost; these factors, together with prospective validation, require evaluation before clinical use.
