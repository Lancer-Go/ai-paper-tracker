摘要Abstract

物理人工智能应用的大规模深度学习模型取决于不同的训练数据收集工作。这些模型以及相应的训练数据必须满足模型在现实环境中部署所需的不同评估标准。 数据选择策略可以指导训练集的开发，但当前的框架没有考虑数据点如何影响不同指标的模糊性。 在这项工作中，我们提出了通过缩放感知迭代收集（MOSAIC）进行混合优化，这是一种通用数据选择框架，其运行方式如下：（i）将数据集划分为多个域； (ii) 将每个数据域的神经标度法则拟合到评估指标； (iii) 通过迭代地添加来自最大化度量变化的域的数据来优化数据混合。 我们将 MOSAIC 应用于自动驾驶 (AD)，其中端到端 (E2E) 规划器模型根据扩展预测驾驶员模型评分 (EPDMS)（驾驶规则合规性指标的聚合）进行评估。 在这里，MOSAIC 的性能优于 EPDMS 上的各种基线，数据减少高达 80%。Large-scale deep learning models for physical AI applications depend on diverse training data collection efforts. These models and correspondingly, the training data, must address different evaluation criteria necessary for the models to be deployable in real-world environments. Data selection policies can guide the development of the training set, but current frameworks do not account for the ambiguity in how data points affect different metrics. In this work, we propose Mixture Optimization via Scaling-Aware Iterative Collection (MOSAIC), a general data selection framework that operates by: (i) partitioning the dataset into domains; (ii) fitting neural scaling laws from each data domain to the evaluation metrics; and (iii) optimizing a data mixture by iteratively adding data from domains that maximize the change in metrics. We apply MOSAIC to autonomous driving (AD), where an End-to-End (E2E) planner model is evaluated on the Extended Predictive Driver Model Score (EPDMS), an aggregate of driving rule compliance metrics. Here, MOSAIC outperforms a diverse set of baselines on EPDMS with up to 80% less data.

1 简介Introduction

大规模深度学习模型由多样化的数据收集工作推动Large-scale deep learning models are fueled by diverse data collection efforts[41, 35]。这种做法在自动驾驶 (AD) 等物理人工智能 (AI) 应用中尤为突出，其中视频剪辑是在不同位置、天气和交通条件下收集的. This practice is particularly prominent in physical artificial intelligence (AI) applications such as autonomous driving (AD), where video clips are collected over different locations, weather, and traffic conditions[18, 12]。 在所有收集的数据上训练模型在计算上效率很低，而在物理人工智能中，这些数据可以扩展到数亿小时的剪辑。 这需要数据混合选择策略来构建和增长多样化且有影响力的样本的训练集，以最大化所需的性能指标。. It is computationally inefficient to train models on all collected data, which in physical AI can scale to hundreds of millions of hours of clips. This necessitates data mixture selection policies to construct and grow training sets of diverse and influential samples that maximize desired performance metrics.

数据集的选择和优化已经从各个角度进行了广泛的研究。 例如，影响（或重复）估计技术使用特征信息来选择有用的数据样本Dataset selection and optimization has been broadly studied from various perspectives. For instance, influence (or duplicate) estimation techniques use feature information to select useful data samples[1, 5, 49]，而主动学习策略在此特征空间上进行优化, while active learning strategies optimize over this feature space[46, 38]。 大型语言模型 (LLM) 及其多模态扩展已成功利用缩放定律来预测模型性能如何随数据集大小而提高. Large language models (LLMs) and their multi-modal extensions have successfully leveraged scaling laws to forecast how model performance improves with dataset size[26, 21, 58];这个前提已经扩展到其他应用程序，包括 AD; this premise has expanded to other applications including AD[3]。 此外，随着数据收集变得越来越复杂，缩放法则用于确定来自显式域（例如不同语言、数学、编码）的数据的最佳混合。. Further, as data collection becomes increasingly complex, scaling laws are used to determine optimal mixtures of data from explicit domains (e.g., different languages, math, coding)[23, 56, 36]。 尽管这些方法为物理人工智能系统提供了普遍的机会，但由于三个主要原因，它们并不能立即用于需要理解不同现实世界场景并与之交互的应用程序。 首先，通过一组潜在竞争指标来评估物理人工智能系统. Although these methods present a general opportunity for physical AI systems, they are not immediately usable for applications that require both understanding and interacting with diverse real-world scenarios for three main reasons. First, physical AI systems are evaluated over a set of potentially competing metrics[14, 57]。其次，不同的数据样本可以以不同的速率影响这些指标的不同组合。最后，数据池不一定可以立即分成对指标具有一致、可预测影响的子集。现有的数据混合方法假设定义明确且同质的域。然而，他们忽视了当数据源以不同的速率影响性能的不同方面时出现的异构和与指标相关的改进率. Second, different data samples can influence different combinations of these metrics at various rates. Finally, the data pool is not necessarily immediately separable into subsets that have consistent, predictable influence on the metrics. Existing data mixture methods assume well-defined and homogeneous domains. However, they overlook the heterogeneous and metric-dependent improvement rates that arise when data sources influence different aspects of performance at varying rates[20, 54]。例如，自动驾驶汽车等物理人工智能系统必须沿着路线行驶、遵守驾驶规则并避免碰撞. For example, a physical AI system such as an autonomous vehicle must progress along a route, follow driving rules, and avoid collisions[14]。 当用于训练时，高流量和行人较多的驾驶片段可能会对某些指标产生比其他指标更大的影响。 此外，找到对指标具有共同影响的潜在训练数据子集需要仔细选择和挖掘。. High-traffic and pedestrian-heavy driving clips, when used for training, may impact certain metrics more than others. Moreover, finding such a subset of potential training data that has shared effects on the metrics requires careful selection and mining.

在这项工作中，我们开发了一种数据选择和混合优化策略，以解决当前物理人工智能面临的多个竞争指标和不精确数据分区的挑战。为了解决不精确数据域的挑战，我们首先将数据池划分为一组可分离的集群，在这些集群中我们可以根据样本对指标的影响对样本进行排名。 我们估计每个集群对每个指标的影响，以及相应地聚合所有指标的总体效用函数。 这种影响是根据缩放定律来衡量的，如果使用来自特定集群的更多数据进行训练，则估计指标的改进。 最后，我们通过识别集群来迭代地将新数据添加到训练集中，这将最大化每个附加数据点的聚合效用的预期收益。通过这种方式，我们优化了生成的分区中的数据混合。图In this work, we develop a data selection and mixture optimization policy that addresses the present physical AI challenges of multiple competing metrics and imprecise data partitions. To address the challenge of imprecise data domains, we first partition a data pool into a set of separable clusters, within which we can rank samples on their influence to the metrics. We estimate the impact of each cluster on each metric, and correspondingly, an overall utility function that aggregates all the metrics. This impact is measured in terms of scaling laws that estimate the improvement to the metrics if more data from a specific cluster were used for training. Finally, we iteratively add new data to the training set by identifying the cluster which will maximize the expected gain to the aggregate utility with each additional data point. 9 有关缩放配合和计算预算的详细信息。Details on the Scaling Fits and Compute Budget.

MOSAIC 需要前期计算投资，以通过试运行来估计特定于集群的扩展曲线。为了保持成本可控，我们避免在试点实验期间从头开始进行全面训练。相反，我们采用持续训练方法：我们从基础模型的最终纪元检查点恢复训练，并对少量纪元的组合数据集进行微调。对于 OpenScene 实验，我们在从每个集群中挖掘 200 和 400 个剪辑后训练 5 个时期。对于 Navtrain 实验，我们在两次试点运行中挖掘 100 和 200 个片段后训练了 10 个时期。此过程提供准确的缩放估计，同时保持可管理的计算开销。MOSAIC requires an upfront compute investment to estimate cluster-specific scaling curves via pilot runs. To keep this cost tractable, we avoid full training from-scratch during the pilot experiments. Instead, we adopt a continual-training approach: we resume training from the base model’s final epoch checkpoint and fine-tune on the combined dataset for a small number of epochs. For the OpenScene experiments, we train for 5 epochs after mining 200 and 400 clips from each cluster. For the Navtrain experiments, we train for 10 epochs after mining 100 and 200 clips in the two pilot runs. This procedure provides accurate scaling estimates while maintaining a manageable computational overhead.
