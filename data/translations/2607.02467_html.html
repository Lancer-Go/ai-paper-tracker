人力资本而非模型基准预测预测中的混合智能Human Capital, Not Model Benchmarks, Predicts Hybrid Intelligence in Forecasting

薇薇安·明Vivienne Ming

人类信托；可能性科学；伦敦大学学院全球健康商学院The Human Trust; Possibility Science; UCL Global Business School for Health

预印本——试点研究。调查结果是初步的，旨在激发预先注册的复制。Preprint — pilot study. Findings are preliminary and intended to motivate a pre-registered replication.

意义。Significance.将人与人工智能配对是有益还是有害通常被报告为单一平均效应。该试点项目使用真实货币预测市场（Polymarket）作为客观的、外部解决的基准，表明人类与人工智能协作的价值取决于特定的、可衡量的人力资本形式。在个人预测者的层面上进行分析，混合表现是三峰的：大多数人要么遵循模型（与其匹配），要么用它来橡皮图章地印证先前的猜测（比单独的模型表现更差），而少数人则进行真正的补充推理并达到匹配的准确度，甚至超过（即低于）市场本身。协作特征——观点采择、智力谦逊和好奇心——而不是原始认知能力或模型基准，区分了谁达到了这种模式。结果是初步的，但在统计上是可靠的，并激发了目前正在准备的预注册复制。Whether pairing people with AI helps or hurts is usually reported as a single average effect. Using a real-money prediction market (Polymarket) as an objective, externally resolved benchmark, this pilot shows that the value of human–AI collaboration depends on a specific, measurable form of human capital. Analyzed at the level of the individual forecaster, hybrid performance is trimodal: most people either deferred to the model (matching it) or used it to rubber-stamp a prior guess (performing worse than the model alone), while a minority engaged in genuine complementary reasoning and reached accuracy matching or even exceeding (i.e., lower error than) the market itself. Collaborative traits—perspective-taking, intellectual humility, and curiosity—rather than raw cognitive ability or model benchmarks, distinguished who reached that mode. The results are preliminary but statistically robust, and motivate a pre-registered replication now in preparation.

简介Introduction

关于人类与人工智能协作的研究报告了相互矛盾的影响：一些人发现低技能工人的数量增加了 (1, 2)，另一些人发现这种好处主要集中在经验丰富的用户身上 (3)，还有一些人发现，相对于单独工作的模型，使用有能力的模型会降低人类的表现 (4, 5)，而另一些人则在复杂的精英任务中显示出可观的回报 (6)。一个反复出现的困惑是评估。在某些情况下，人工智能系统可能只是对问题及其解决方案进行了编码，因此测量的“能力”反映的是污染而不是推理（7, 8）。即使在限制较少的任务上，“质量”也常常由专家评分者评分，他们自己受到人工智能辅助散文的流畅性而不是作品实质的影响 (9, 10)。针对真实货币预测市场的预测回避了这两个问题：每个预测都是根据外部解析的基本事实在通用的、无风格的指标上进行评分。Studies of human–AI collaboration report conflicting effects: some find augmentation of lower-skilled workers (1, 2), others find the benefits accrue mainly to experienced users (3), still others find that access to capable models degrades human performance relative to the model working alone (4, 5), and others show substantial returns on complex, elite tasks (6). A recurring confound is evaluation. In some cases AI systems may simply have encoded the problem and its solution, so that measured “ability” reflects contamination rather than reasoning (7, 8). And even on less constrained tasks, “quality” is often scored by expert raters who are themselves swayed by the fluency of AI-assisted prose rather than the substance of the work (9, 10). Forecasting against a real-money prediction market sidesteps both problems: every prediction is scored against an externally resolved ground truth on a common, style-free metric.

我报告了一个试点项目，旨在估计（i）人力资本是否会调节人工智能辅助预测的价值，以及（ii）不同的交互方式是否会调节这种调节。由于每个参与者都输入了自己的概率预测，因此所有分析都是在个人预测者的层面上进行的。我将这项工作定义为假设生成：几个细胞很小，目的是获得效应大小估计（现已得到统计支持），为准备中的验证性试验提供动力。I report a pilot designed to estimate (i) whether human capital moderates the value of AI assistance in forecasting, and (ii) whether distinct interaction styles mediate that moderation. Because each participant entered their own probabilistic forecast, all analyses are conducted at the level of the individual forecaster. I frame the work as hypothesis-generating: several cells are small, and the aim is to obtain effect-size estimates—now statistically supported—to power a confirmatory trial in preparation.

方法Methods

参与者和设计。Participants and design.在加利福尼亚州伯克利，flyer 招募了 108 名成年人，并在三节课中每节课补偿 20 美元。主要研究由 78 名参与者（42 名加州大学伯克利分校学生；36 名社区成年人；平均年龄 28.7 岁，范围 18-60）组成，分为 26 个三人小组，并分配到纯人类条件（12 队）或混合条件（14 队），并可使用四种大型语言模型之一。由 30 名参与者（10 个团队）组成的单独池使用“苏格拉底式”模型，该模型保留答案，并单独报告（补充 S1）。参与者以团队形式合作，但每个人都输入了个人预测；分析位于One hundred eight adults were recruited by flyer in Berkeley, CA and compensated $20 per session for three sessions. The main study comprised 78 participants (42 UC Berkeley students; 36 community adults; mean age 28.7, range 18–60) organized into 26 three-person teams and assigned either to a Human-only condition (12 teams) or a Hybrid condition (14 teams) with access to one of four large language models. A separate pool of 30 participants (10 teams) worked with a “Socratic” model that withholds answers, reported separately (Supplementary S1). 预测任务和地面实况。Forecasting task and ground truth.这 30 个问题是 2025 年 11 月至 2026 年 1 月期间解决的实时 Polymarket 合同，涵盖经济、国际关系和商业。每个问题的外部认证解决方案 (o ∈ {0,1}) 都提供了基本事实，每个团队预测从池中随机抽取的 10 个问题。这四个模型还独立预测了所有 30 个问题（仅 AI 基线）：Llama 3.1 (8B)、Qwen3 (8B)、GPT-4o 和 Gemini 3 Pro。作为外部参考，我记录了市场的隐含概率（Brier 3.5）。The 30 questions were live Polymarket contracts resolving between November 2025 and January 2026, spanning economics, international relations, and business. Each question’s externally certified resolution (o ∈ {0,1}) provided ground truth, and each team forecast 10 questions drawn at random from the pool. The four models also forecast all 30 questions independently (AI-only baseline): Llama 3.1 (8B), Qwen3 (8B), GPT-4o, and Gemini 3 Pro. As an external reference I recorded the market’s implied probability (scaled Brier 3.5).

结果指标。Outcome metric.预测精度是 Brier 分数，即概率预测的均方误差，B = (1/N) Σ (pᵢ − oᵢ)²，报告缩放 ×100（范围 0–100；越低越好；无信息的 0.5 预测分数为 25）。Forecast accuracy is the Brier score, the mean squared error of the probabilistic forecast, B = (1/N) Σ (pᵢ − oᵢ)², reported scaled ×100 (range 0–100; lower is better; an uninformative 0.5 forecast scores 25).

人力资本措施。Human-capital measures.第 1 场讨论了 Raven 的高级渐进矩阵集 II（流体推理）、ICAR 公共领域 g 代理、IRI 观点采择量表、综合智力谦逊量表、认知好奇心量表、简要弹性量表和 TIPI 大五量表。Session 1 administered Raven’s Advanced Progressive Matrices Set II (fluid reasoning), the ICAR public-domain g proxy, the IRI Perspective-Taking subscale, the Comprehensive Intellectual Humility Scale, an epistemic-curiosity scale, the Brief Resilience Scale, and the TIPI Big-Five inventory.

交互风格。Interaction styles.混合参与者根据他们的过程被事后分为三种新兴风格：自动化者（采用模型的答案，几乎不添加推理）、验证者（使用模型来检查先前的猜测）和机器人（迭代补充推理）。因为风格是自然产生的而不是指定的，所以它与人力资本是共线的；不同风格之间的比较是描述性的。Hybrid participants were classified post hoc from their process into three emergent styles: Automators (adopt the model’s answer with little added reasoning), Validators (use the model to check a prior guess), and Cyborgs (iterative complementary reasoning). Because style is emergent rather than assigned, it is collinear with human capital; comparisons across styles are descriptive.

分析。Analysis.我报告了具有 95% 置信区间和个体点的参与者水平条件平均值（图 1）。考虑到异质的条件内方差，我使用非参数综合检验（Kruskal-Wallis）和韦尔奇的 t 检验进行事后配对比较，以及人力资本的皮尔逊相关性的准确性。效应大小为 Cohen d。I report participant-level condition means with 95% confidence intervals and individual points (Fig. 1). Given heterogeneous within-condition variances I use a non-parametric omnibus test (Kruskal–Wallis) with Welch's t-tests for post-hoc pairwise comparisons, and Pearson correlations of human capital with accuracy. Effect sizes are Cohen’s d.

三峰“K 形”。A trimodal “K-shape.”不同条件下的预测误差差异很大（四组的 Kruskal-Wallis χ2，p < 10⁻13；图 1A）。纯人类预测者的得分为 14.7（95% CI 14.4–15.0，n = 35），远高于概率，但远远落后于模型。至关重要的是，杂交并没有一律有帮助。自动化器大致再现了其工具的行为，但未再现其准确性（10.4，CI 9.7-11.0，n = 24）——比单独的人类更好（p < .001），但比仅人工智能的基线（5.5）差。验证者——使用 AI 来检查先前猜测的经典“人机循环”——比人类单独工作的表现要差得多（31.7，CI 20.6-42.7，n = 9；与仅人类相比 p = .017）。只有机器人在机器上取得了进步：3.8（CI 3.5-4.1，n = 9），比每个单独的人工智能模型的误差都低，并且在统计上与最强模型无法区分（实际上在数值上低于平均值），明显优于自动机（p < .001）。Forecasting error differed sharply across conditions (Kruskal–Wallis χ² across the four groups, p < 10⁻¹³; Fig. 1A). Human-only forecasters scored 14.7 (95% CI 14.4–15.0, n = 35), well above chance but far behind the models. Crucially, hybridization did not uniformly help. Automators reproduced roughly their tool’s behavior but not its accuracy (10.4, CI 9.7–11.0, n = 24)—better than humans alone (p < .001) yet worse than the AI-only baseline (5.5). Validators—the classic “human-in-the-loop” who uses AI to check a prior guess—were significantly worse than humans working alone (31.7, CI 20.6–42.7, n = 9; vs. human-only p = .017). Only Cyborgs improved on the machine: 3.8 (CI 3.5–4.1, n = 9), lower error than every individual AI model and statistically indistinguishable from—indeed numerically below the mean of—the strongest models, significantly better than Automators (p < .001).

与市场的关系。Relation to the market.Cyborg 预测者达到或超过（即，误差低于）3.5 的市场基准，而自动化者和验证者则没有接近它。机器人与仅使用人工智能的汇总基线的比较方向相同，但只是边缘（p = .06），反映了四模型基线而不是弱效应；机器人误差分别低于四个模型中的每一个。Cyborg forecasters matched or exceeded (i.e., achieved lower error than) the market benchmark of 3.5, whereas Automators and Validators did not approach it. The comparison of Cyborgs to the pooled AI-only baseline was in the same direction but only marginal (p = .06), reflecting the four-model baseline rather than a weak effect; Cyborg error was below each of the four models individually.

人力资本可以预测混合准确性，而基准却不能。Human capital predicts hybrid accuracy—benchmarks do not.在这四个模型中，仅人工智能精度跟踪了规模和基准站（GPT-4o 和 Gemini 3 Pro，4.6；Qwen3，5.5；Llama 3.1，7.1）。然而，在混合预测者中，认知能力（人类对基准分数的模拟）并不能预测准确性（g，r = -0.10；流畅推理，r = -0.08；均为 ns）。协作人力资本做到了：换位思考预测较低的错误（r = -0.32，p = .04），好奇心（r = -0.26）和智力谦逊（r = -0.25）趋势。与人类单独预测的对比是惊人的：在纯人类参与者中，每个特征都强烈预测准确性——智力谦逊（r = -0.66，p < .001），以及g，流畅的推理，好奇心和换位思考都接近r = -0.45（p < .01）。因此，人工智能的使用将原始能力与结果脱钩——模型提供了一个底线——而协作能力则控制着谁超越了该底线。Among the four models, AI-only accuracy tracked scale and benchmark standing (GPT-4o and Gemini 3 Pro, 4.6; Qwen3, 5.5; Llama 3.1, 7.1). Yet among hybrid forecasters, cognitive ability—the human analog of a benchmark score—did not predict accuracy (g, r = −0.10; fluid reasoning, r = −0.08; both ns). Collaborative human capital did: perspective-taking predicted lower error (r = −0.32, p = .04), with curiosity (r = −0.26) and intellectual humility (r = −0.25) trending. The contrast with solo human forecasting is striking: among Human-only participants, every trait strongly predicted accuracy—intellectual humility (r = −0.66, p < .001), and g, fluid reasoning, curiosity, and perspective-taking all near r = −0.45 (p < .01). Access to AI thus decouples raw ability from outcome—the model supplies a floor—while collaborative capacity instead governs who transcends that floor.

谁成为机器人。Who becomes a Cyborg.人力资本极大地限制了赛博格模式的出现。机器人在智力谦逊（6.5 vs. 4.4，d = 4.1）、好奇心（6.7 vs. 5.1，d = 4.0）和观点采择（6.5 vs. 4.6，d = 3.2）方面超过了其他混合预测者，在流畅推理（d = 1.5）和g（d = 1.7）方面差异较小但可靠（所有p < 0.01；图1）。 1B）。这种分离足够强烈，以至于在这个样本量下无法识别机器人出现对这些特征的逻辑模型——这是非常强关联的证据，而不是无效的。Human capital sharply gated emergence of the Cyborg mode. Cyborgs exceeded other hybrid forecasters on intellectual humility (6.5 vs. 4.4, d = 4.1), curiosity (6.7 vs. 5.1, d = 4.0), and perspective-taking (6.5 vs. 4.6, d = 3.2), with smaller but reliable differences in fluid reasoning (d = 1.5) and g (d = 1.7) (all p < .01; Fig. 1B). The separation was strong enough that a logistic model of Cyborg emergence on these traits was not identifiable at this sample size—evidence of a very strong association rather than a null.

质的互补。Qualitative complementarity.流程注释表明了机器人对的劳动分工：人类推动探索问题中不适定的、偶然的部分（可能会干预什么，市场可能会缺失什么），而模型则锚定了适定的、数据绑定的部分。自动化公司将两者都外包了；验证者使用该模型来具体化而不是挑战他们自己的推理。Process notes suggest a division of labour in Cyborg pairs: humans drove exploration of the ill-posed, contingent parts of a question (what could intervene, what the market might be missing) while the model anchored the well-posed, data-bound parts. Automators outsourced both; Validators used the model to crystallize, rather than challenge, their own reasoning.

讨论Discussion

这些结果重新审视了人工智能和生产力的问题，从人工智能是否有帮助到何时以及如何提供帮助。将所有混合交互方式（自动化器、验证器和机器人）汇集到一个平均“混合”单元中隐藏了强烈的三峰结果 - 包括表现不佳的条件（验证器）单独的人工智能，甚至是无人帮助的人类。组织这种模式的调节因素是协作人力资本，尤其是观点采择、好奇心和智力谦逊，这与更广泛的发现一致，即协作能力的异质性推动了混合智能的回报（11-14）。值得注意的是，重要的人力资本不是模型基准的人类模拟：原始认知能力可以预测人类的准确性并跟踪模型自身的准确性These results reframe the question of AI and productivity from whether AI helps to when and how it helps. Pooling all hybrid interaction styles (Automators, Validators, and Cyborgs) into a single average “hybrid” cell hides a strongly trimodal outcome—including a condition (Validator) that underperforms the AI alone and even the unaided human. The moderator that organizes the pattern is collaborative human capital, especially perspective-taking, curiosity, and intellectual humility, consistent with broader findings that heterogeneity in collaboration ability drives the returns to hybrid intelligence (11–14). Notably, the human capital that matters is not the human analog of a model benchmark: raw cognitive ability predicted solo human accuracy and tracked the models’ own

基准排序，但没有预测谁在人工智能方面取得了成功。使模型得分良好或使人单独推理良好的因素并不能使人类-人工智能团队有效。benchmark ordering, but did not predict who succeeded with AI. What makes a model score well, or a person reason well alone, is not what makes a human–AI team effective.

局限性。Limitations.这是一个试点项目，应该这样理解。机器人和验证器单元很小（每个 n = 9）；互动风格是自然出现的并且与人力资本共线，因此风格比较是描述性的，而不是因果性的；并且条件内结果方差很低，这会夸大一些标准化效应大小 - 这里的解释依赖于基于排名的综合测试和置信区间，而不是依赖于 d。市场比较受到四种模型基线的限制，并且每个问题的校准都没有经过独立的重新审核。预先注册的、充分动力的复制（准备中）将（i）预先注册风格分类和人力资本组合，（ii）在可行的情况下操纵而不是仅仅测量交互风格，以及（iii）修复相对于市场状态的预测启发时间戳。This is a pilot and should be read as such. The Cyborg and Validator cells are small (n = 9 each); interaction style is emergent and collinear with human capital, so style comparisons are descriptive, not causal; and within-condition outcome variance is low, which inflates some standardized effect sizes—interpretation here rests on the rank-based omnibus test and confidence intervals rather than on d. The market comparison is limited by a four-model baseline and per-question calibration was not independently re-audited. A pre-registered, adequately powered replication (in preparation) will (i) pre-register the style taxonomy and a human-capital composite, (ii) manipulate rather than merely measure interaction style where feasible, and (iii) fix the forecast-elicitation timestamp relative to market state.

补充说明（附带的，不属于预先指定设计的一部分）Supplementary notes (incidental, not part of the pre-specified design)

S1。一个永远不会给出答案的“苏格拉底式”模型。S1. A “Socratic” model that never answers.由 30 名参与者（10 个团队）组成的独立池使用最低层的开放模型进行重新调整，以保留直接答案，而不是提示推理。它没有达到每一个准确度基准，但却朝着预测的方向改变了行为：机器人率上升到 30%（相对于主混合池中的 21%），而潜在的自动化者由于无法复制答案，准确率下降到人类水平（14.9 相对于主池中的 10.4）。机器人仍然准确（4.2）。然而，用户满意度都很差（任务后 CSAT ≈ 1-2，满分为 10 分）。A separate pool of 30 participants (10 teams) worked with the lowest-tier open model re-tuned to withhold direct answers and instead prompt reasoning. It failed every accuracy benchmark, yet shifted behavior in the predicted direction: the Cyborg rate rose to 30% (vs. 21% in the main hybrid pool), and would-be Automators, unable to copy an answer, fell to human-level accuracy (14.9 vs. 10.4 in the main pool). Cyborgs remained accurate (4.2). User satisfaction, however, was uniformly poor (post-task CSAT ≈ 1–2 on a 10-point scale).

S2。脑电图认知参与。S2. EEG cognitive engagement.在数据收集过程中，移动脑电图（加州大学伯克利分校神经技术对撞机实验室）记录了相对伽马功率，作为志愿者认知努力的代表。自动化者的相对伽玛值比人类参与者低约 43%（0.57 比 1.0）。这是对 7 名参与者进行的机会主义、非盲观察，其中伽马参与是一种有争议的操作；据报道，这只是为了激励仪器化的后续行动。Midway through data collection, a mobile EEG (UC Berkeley Neurotech Collider Lab) recorded relative gamma power as a proxy for cognitive effort in volunteers. Automators showed ≈43% lower relative gamma than human-only participants (0.57 vs. 1.0). This is an opportunistic, unblinded observation in 7 participants, with gamma-as-engagement a contested operationalization; it is reported only to motivate instrumented follow-up.
