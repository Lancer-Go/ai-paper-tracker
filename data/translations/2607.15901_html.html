6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 DSWorld，一种用于数据科学工作流程的学习转换模型。通过对基于当前状态和操作的环境转换进行建模，DSWorld 使代理能够在训练和推理期间执行昂贵的计算之前预测数据科学操作的影响。为了支持有效的世界模型学习，我们构建了结合真实和合成过渡数据的大规模轨迹数据集，并进一步提出反射世界模型优化，以通过反射强化学习提高预测质量。大量实验表明，DSWorld 在不同的转换预测任务中实现了强大的预测性能，同时大大加速了自主代理的训练和推理。In this paper, we present DSWorld, a learned transition model for data science workflows. By modeling environment transitions conditioned on the current state and actions, DSWorld enables agents to anticipate the effects of data science operations before performing costly computation during both training and inference. To support effective world model learning, we construct a large-scale trajectory dataset combining real and synthetic transition data, and further propose Reflective World Model Optimization to improve prediction quality through reflective reinforcement learning. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DSWorld achieves strong predictive performance across diverse transition prediction tasks while substantially accelerating autonomous agent training and inference.

局限性Limitations

尽管取得了有希望的结果，这项工作仍然存在一些局限性。首先，DSWorld 目前专注于对数据科学转换进行建模，并且没有明确对自主代理工作流程中的外部工具调用转换进行建模。其次，转换预测的质量仍然受到底层 LLM 模拟器功能的限制，在复杂的工作流程场景中有时可能会产生不准确的预测。第三，与现实世界的自主工作流程相比，合成轨迹仍可能表现出分布差距，从而可能影响泛化性能。我们将这些限制留给未来的工作。Despite the promising results, this work still has several limitations. First, DSWorld currently focuses on modeling data science transitions and does not explicitly model external tool-call transitions in autonomous agent workflows. Second, the quality of transition prediction remains constrained by the capability of the underlying LLM simulator, which may occasionally produce inaccurate predictions in complex workflow scenarios. Third, synthesized trajectories may still exhibit distribution gaps compared with real-world autonomous workflows, potentially affecting generalization performance. We leave these limitations for future work.
