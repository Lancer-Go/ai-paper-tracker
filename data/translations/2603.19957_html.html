HiPath：用于结构化病理报告预测的分层视觉语言对齐HiPath: Hierarchical Vision-Language Alignment for Structured Pathology Report Prediction

Ruicheng Yuan    Zhenxuan Zhang    Anbang Wang    Liwei Hu    Xiangqian Hua    Yaya Peng    Jiawei Luo    Guang Yang
摘要Abstract

病理报告是一种结构化、多粒度的文档，编码诊断结论、组织学分级以及跨一个或多个解剖部位的辅助测试结果；然而现有的病理学视觉语言模型（VLM）将此输出减少为平面标签或自由格式文本。我们提出了 HiPath，这是一个基于冻结的 UNI2 和 Qwen3 主干的轻量级 VLM 框架，它将结构化报告预测作为其主要训练目标。三个可训练模块总计 15M 参数，解决了该问题的互补方面：用于多图像视觉编码的分层补丁聚合器 (HiPA)、通过最佳传输进行跨模态对齐的分层对比学习 (HiCL)，以及用于生成结构化诊断的基于时隙的掩蔽诊断预测 (Slot-MDP)。 HiPath 对来自三家医院的 749 万个真实的中国病理病例进行了训练，达到了 68.9% 的严格准确率和 74.7% 的临床可接受准确率，以及 97.3% 的安全率，优于相同冷冻骨干下的所有基线。跨医院评估证实了普遍性，只有 3.4Pathology reports are structured, multi-granular documents encoding diagnostic conclusions, histological grades, and ancillary test results across one or more anatomical sites; yet existing pathology vision-language models (VLMs) reduce this output to a flat label or free-form text. We present HiPath, a lightweight VLM framework built on frozen UNI2 and Qwen3 backbones that treats structured report prediction as its primary training objective. Three trainable modules totalling 15 M parameters address complementary aspects of the problem: a Hierarchical Patch Aggregator (HiPA) for multi-image visual encoding, Hierarchical Contrastive Learning (HiCL) for cross-modal alignment via optimal transport, and Slot-based Masked Diagnosis Prediction (Slot-MDP) for structured diagnosis generation. Trained on 749 K real-world Chinese pathology cases from three hospitals, HiPath achieves 68.9% strict and 74.7% clinically acceptable accuracy with a 97.3% safety rate, outperforming all baselines under the same frozen backbone. 1 简介Introduction

计算病理学的最新进展是由大规模自监督基础模型推动的Recent progress in computational pathology has been driven by large-scale self-supervised foundation models[3, 17, 10]和视觉语言模型 (VLM)and vision-language models (VLMs)[7, 8]将组织病理学图像与临床文本联系起来。这些模型已成为强大的通用特征提取器，并支持广泛的下游任务，包括组织分类、生物标志物预测和零样本识别that connect histopathology images with clinical text. These models have become strong generic feature extractors and support a wide range of downstream tasks, including tissue classification, biomarker prediction, and zero-shot recognition[12, 7].

然而，病理学的临床输出很少是单一标签。常规报告是一种结构化记录，共同指定 (i) 初步诊断、(ii) 组织学分级和 (iii) 免疫组织化学 (IHC) 结果，通常在同一患者的多个解剖部位重复However, the clinical output of pathology is rarely a single label. A routine report is a structured record that jointly specifies (i) a primary diagnosis, (ii) a histological grade, and (iii) immunohistochemistry (IHC) results, often repeated across multiple anatomical sites for the same patient[19]。这种结构是决策的核心：不同的领域有不同的临床后果，因此将所有错误归结为平坦准确度的评估可能会掩盖安全关键型故障。同时，开放式文本生成不能保证输出是槽一致的或可靠可解析的。. This structure is central to decision-making: different fields have different clinical consequences, so an evaluation that collapses all errors into flat accuracy can obscure safety-critical failures. At the same time, open-ended text generation offers no guarantee that the output is slot-consistent or reliably parseable.

这给病理学 VLM 留下了一个悬而未决的问题：我们能否训练和评估一个主要目标是结构化报告预测而不是平面分类或无约束生成的模型？据我们所知，现有的病理 VLM 并未针对这种情况下的结构化、槽级诊断预测进行训练或基准测试。一个相关的差距涉及语言和报告惯例。迄今为止，所有主要病理学 VLM 均仅在英语语料库上进行训练This leaves an open question for pathology VLMs: can we train and evaluate a model whose primary objective is structured report prediction, rather than flat classification or unconstrained generation? To our knowledge, existing pathology VLMs are not trained or benchmarked for structured, slot-level diagnosis prediction in this setting. A related gap concerns language and reporting conventions. All major pathology VLMs to date are trained exclusively on English corpora[7, 12, 8]，尽管在非英语环境下有大量的临床需求。中国约占全球癌症病例的24%, despite substantial clinical demand in non-English contexts. China accounts for approximately 24% of global cancer cases[1, 6]估计面临 90,000 名病理学家的短缺and faces an estimated shortage of 90,000 pathologists[20]。中国报告还遵循不同的惯例，包括以位点为前缀的片段标识符、标准化分级术语和结构化 IHC 格式。虽然中国团体引入了著名的 MIL 方法. Chinese reports also follow distinct conventions, including site-prefixed segment identifiers, standardised grading terminology, and structured IHC formatting. While Chinese groups have introduced notable MIL methods[15]、病理学基础模型, pathology foundation models[18]和 VLM, and VLMs[16]，没有大规模的结构化中文诊断预测目标。, none target structured Chinese diagnosis prediction at scale.

我们的贡献如下。 (1) 我们将结构化槽级报告预测正式化为病理 VLM 的主要目标，并表明它的性能大大优于平面分类。 (2) 我们在冻结主干上引入了一种轻量级架构，该架构结合了分层多图像聚合、基于 OT 的局部对齐和基于词汇的槽预测以进行结构化诊断。 (3) 我们提出了一个具有安全意识指标的四级临床评估方案，并在来自三家医院的 749000 个病例上进行了验证。该评估工具包包括304个术语词汇、同义词映射、粗略分类和安全边界定义，已公开发布，以促进结构化病理诊断的可重复研究。Our contributions are as follows. (1) We formalise structured slot-level report prediction as a primary objective for pathology VLMs and show that it substantially outperforms flat classification. (2) We introduce a lightweight architecture on frozen backbones that combines hierarchical multi-image aggregation, OT-based local alignment, and vocabulary-grounded slot prediction for structured diagnosis. (3) We propose a four-level clinical evaluation protocol with safety-aware metrics and validate it on 749 K cases from three hospitals. The evaluation toolkit, including the 304-term vocabulary, synonym mappings, coarse taxonomy, and safety boundary definitions, is publicly released to facilitate reproducible research in structured pathology diagnosis.

4 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 HiPath，一个轻量级视觉语言框架， 将结构化病理报告预测作为其主要学习内容 客观。在冻结的主干上只有 15 M 可训练参数， HiPath 严格准确率达到 68.9%，临床可接受率为 74.7% 749 K 案例中的准确性和安全性为 97.3%， 跨医院概括。未来的一些限制 工作：目前评估覆盖一地区三家医院 （需要更广泛的多中心验证）；该模型运行于 病理学家选择的 ROI 而不是整个切片图像；和案例 304 术语词汇表之外的术语被分配为最接近的术语，而不是 比拒绝。我们发布了评估协议，包括 词汇、同义词映射、粗略分类和安全边界 定义，以支持可重复性和进一步研究。We presented HiPath, a lightweight vision-language framework that treats structured pathology report prediction as its primary learning objective. With only 15 M trainable parameters on frozen backbones, HiPath achieves 68.9% strict accuracy, 74.7% clinically acceptable accuracy, and 97.3% safety on 749 K cases, with robust cross-hospital generalisation. Several limitations point to future work: the current evaluation covers three hospitals in one region (broader multi-centre validation is needed); the model operates on pathologist-selected ROIs rather than whole-slide images; and cases outside the 304-term vocabulary are assigned the nearest term rather than rejected. We release the evaluation protocol, including the vocabulary, synonym mappings, coarse taxonomy, and safety boundary definitions, to support reproducibility and further research.
