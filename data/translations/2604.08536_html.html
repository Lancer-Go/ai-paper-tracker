一、简介1. Introduction

在扩散和流匹配模型的最新进展的推动下，文本引导的图像生成和编辑已成为生成建模中最活跃的前沿[2,8,16,24,38]。仅基于自然语言指令生成或修改图像的能力使得视觉设计、内容创建等领域的多种应用成为可能。Text-guided image generation and editing have become the most active frontiers in generative modeling, driven by recent advances in diffusion and flow-matching models [2, 8, 16, 24, 38]. While significant progress has been made in fine-tuning–based approaches [5, 11, 25, 37], these methods require expensive optimization and exhibit limited generalization beyond the training distribution. Consequently, training-free and inversion-free methods have emerged as a practical alternative. By operating directly on pretrained models at inference time without modifying model weights, such methods offer broad applicability and efficient deployment [1, 12, 15, 19, 23, 49].

然而，一方面，反演通常会扭曲布局或同一性，并引入脆弱的正向-反向采样循环。另一方面，无反演方法规避了重建，但无法获得原始图像的忠实潜在表示。因此，它们经常遭受内容漂移、语义泄漏、弱对象定位和细粒度可控性不足的困扰。最近，奖励引导框架[9,27,45]试图解决可控性问题，但它们采用语义基础薄弱的粗略奖励，缺乏自适应策略，并且没有提供在采样轨迹上协调异构目标的机制。这些缺点阻碍了现有方法在真正的零样本设置中实现精确、一致和语义忠实的编辑。However, on one hand, inversion often distorts layout or identity and introduces brittle forward–reverse sampling loops. On the other hand, inversion-free methods circumvent reconstruction but lose access to a faithful latent representation of the original image. As a result, they frequently suffer from content drift, semantic leakage, weak object localization, and insufficient fine-grained controllability. More recently, reward-guided frameworks [9, 27, 45] attempt to address controllability, but they employ coarse rewards with weak semantic grounding, lack adaptive policies, and provide no mechanism for harmonizing heterogeneous objectives over the sampling trajectory. These shortcomings prevent existing methods from achieving precise, consistent, and semantically faithful edits in a truly zero-shot setting.

3.3.差异化奖励3.3. Differentiable Rewards

依赖单一的全局奖励可以提供语义对齐，但缺乏空间精度；它的梯度往往会扩散到整个图像，导致不相关区域被无意修改的语义泄漏。为了解决这个问题，我们构建了一个分层奖励工具包，提供细粒度的空间、感知和对象级别的控制。Relying on a single, global reward offers semantic alignment but lacks spatial precision; its gradients tend to diffuse across the entire image, leading to semantic leakage where unrelated regions are unintentionally modified. To counter this, we construct a hierarchical reward toolkit that provides fine-grained spatial, perceptual, and object-level control.

六、结论6. Conclusion

我们介绍We introduce奖励流程RewardFlow，一个免训练框架，使用多奖励 Langevin 动力学来引导预训练的文本引导图像编辑和生成模型。通过将全局、本地化和基于 VQA 的奖励与提示感知自适应策略和 KL 系链相结合，RewardFlow 实现了细粒度、空间精确的控制，同时保留了身份和布局。大量的实验表明，与强大的免训练基线相比，编辑保真度、构图对齐和生成质量得到了持续改进。我们相信，将可控生成视为奖励引导采样提供了一种通用的测试时间对齐策略，并有望扩展视频编辑。, a training-free framework that steers pretrained text-guided image editing and generation models using multi-reward Langevin dynamics. By combining global, localized, and VQA-based rewards with a prompt-aware adaptive policy and a KL tether, RewardFlow achieves fine-grained, spatially precise control while preserving identity and layout. Extensive experiments demonstrate consistent improvements in edit fidelity, compositional alignment, and generation quality over strong training-free baselines. We believe treating controllable generation as reward-guided sampling offers a general test-time alignment strategy, with promising extensions to video editing.
