摘要Abstract

用于根据人类反馈 (RLHF) 进行强化学习的奖励模型 (RM) 很容易受到奖励黑客攻击：当策略最大化学习到的代理奖励时，真正的质量就会停滞或下降。 我们假设奖励黑客通常是由翻转的优势标志引起的：翻转的标志不会减少不良响应的可能性，而是会导致更新增加不良响应的可能性。 通过考虑 RM 参数空间中的对抗性扰动，我们可以得出Reward models (RMs) used in reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) are vulnerable to reward hacking: as the policy maximizes a learned proxy reward, true quality plateaus or degrades. We make the assumption that reward hacking is often caused by flipped advantage signs: instead of reducing the likelihood of a bad response, a flipped sign causes the update to increase it. By considering an adversarial perturbation in the RM parameter space, we can derive a经认证的标志保护半径certified sign-preservation radius，这是在策略优化期间可以翻转优势标志的最小扰动。 基于这个公式，我们建议, which is the smallest perturbation that can flip the advantage sign during policy optimization. Based on this formulation, we propose签名认证策略优化 (SignCert-PO)Sign-Certified Policy Optimization (SignCert-PO)，在策略梯度更新中降低非鲁棒完成的权重。 与之前需要多个 RM 或访问 RM 训练数据的方法不同，SignCert-PO 是轻量级的，并且纯粹在策略优化阶段运行，仅使用 RM 参数和策略内完成。 在 TL;DR 总结和 AlpacaFarm 基准测试中，SignCert-PO 始终实现比基线更好的获胜率，并减少奖励黑客行为。, down-weighting non-robust completions in the policy gradient update. On TL;DR summarization and AlpacaFarm benchmarks, SignCert-PO consistently achieves a better win rate than baselines and reduces reward hacking.

1 简介Introduction

基于人类反馈的强化学习 (RLHF) 已成为使大型语言模型 (LLM) 与人类意图保持一致的标准范例Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) has become a standard paradigm for aligning large language models (LLMs) with human intent(Christiano et al., 2017; Stiennon et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022)。在典型的 RLHF 管道中，奖励模型 (RM) 在成对的人类偏好数据上进行训练，然后在策略优化期间用作真实的、不可观察的偏好信号的代理。 由于 RM 本质上是一个不完美的代理，因此针对它优化策略通常会导致策略利用奖励环境中的局部不准确性。这种现象被称为奖励黑客. In the typical RLHF pipeline, a reward model (RM) is trained on pairwise human preference data and then used as a proxy for the true, unobservable preference signal during policy optimization. Because the RM is inherently an imperfect proxy, optimizing the policy against it often causes the policy to exploit local inaccuracies in the reward landscape. This phenomenon is known as reward hacking(Gao et al., 2023)。随着优化的进展，代理奖励持续上升，而真正的质量却停滞或下降。当代理 RM 使用有限的偏好数据进行训练时，这一点尤其严重. As optimization progresses, proxy reward continues to rise while true quality stagnates or degrades. This is especially acute when the proxy RM is trained with limited preference data(Gao et al., 2023)或者当策略在强化学习（RL）优化期间偏离 RM 的训练分布时or when the policy drifts from the RM’s training distribution during policy optimization by reinforcement learning (RL)(Ackermann et al., 2025).

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 SignCert-PO，这是一种 RLHF 的轻量级方法，它通过降低完成的策略梯度贡献来减轻奖励黑客攻击，其优势迹象在 RM 参数扰动下很脆弱。我们的方法得出了经过认证的符号保留半径，该半径对于每次完成都量化了可以翻转优势符号的 RM 的最小扰动。这种按完成公式产生了标准 PG 更新的简单重新加权机制，仅需要当前 RM 头参数和策略内完成。对 Pythia 和 Qwen2.5 模型系列的 TL;DR 汇总和 AlpacaFarm 进行的实验表明，SignCert-PO 在大多数设置中实现了最高的 gold-RM 获胜率，同时增加的计算开销可以忽略不计。We presented SignCert-PO, a lightweight method for RLHF that mitigates reward hacking by down-weighting policy gradient contributions of completions whose advantage signs are fragile under RM parameter perturbations. Our approach derives a certified sign-preservation radius that quantifies, for each completion, the smallest perturbation to the RM that can flip the advantage sign. This per-completion formulation yields a simple re-weighting mechanism of the standard PG update, requiring only the current RM head parameters and on-policy completions. Experiments on TL;DR summarization and AlpacaFarm across Pythia and Qwen2.5 model families show that SignCert-PO achieves the highest gold-RM win rate in most settings while adding negligible computational overhead.

我们的方法做出了几个简化的假设（§Our method makes several simplifying assumptions (§3.2）。此外，我们的每个样本的对手公式比共享对手变体更加保守。分析更紧密耦合的公式是未来的方向。最后，将经过认证的符号保留半径扩展到一般 RM，而不产生全参数梯度范数计算的成本仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。). Further, our per-sample adversary formulation is more conservative than a shared-adversary variant. Analyzing the tighter coupled formulation is a future direction. Finally, extending the certified sign-preservation radius to general RMs without incurring the cost of full-parameter gradient norm computation remains an open problem.
