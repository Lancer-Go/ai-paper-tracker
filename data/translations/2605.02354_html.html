摘要Abstract

现实世界中的战略竞争——从标准战争到地缘政治对抗——通常涉及多个参与者组成联盟来与对手群体竞争。这些竞争不是简单的统一实体之间的战斗；相反，它们是“战争中的战争”，联盟互相争斗，同时努力解决资源和战略方面的内部冲突。然而，现有的博弈论模型通常分别处理这两个层面——联盟间竞争和联盟内竞争。本文通过介绍解决了这一根本性差距Strategic competitions in the real world—from standard wars to geopolitical rivalries—often involve groups of actors forming coalitions to compete against rival groups. These contests are not simple battles between unified entities; rather, they are “wars within wars,” where coalitions fight each other while simultaneously grappling with internal conflicts over resources and strategy. However, existing game-theoretic models typically treat these two layers—inter-coalition rivalry and intra-coalition competition—separately. This paper addresses this fundamental gap by introducing复合联盟-消耗博弈the Compound Coalition–Attrition Game ( CCAG），一个统一的理论框架，正式将联盟之间的消耗战与每个联盟内部同时发生的消耗战结合起来。在我们的模型中，联盟在外部竞争中的集体耐力是由其成员的战略选择内生决定的，这些成员在内部争夺最终战利品的份额。我们证明了纯策略均衡的不存在，并描述了博弈两层的独特混合策略纳什均衡。我们的分析揭示了强大的反馈循环：外部胜利的前景加剧了内部竞争，而内部不和则削弱了联盟的外部地位，从而产生成功的良性循环或分裂的恶性循环。我们通过详细的案例研究来验证该模型，比较传统市场（包括黄金、铜和白银）与加密货币市场（包括比特币、以太坊和 Solana），并在模拟框架中使用 2018 年至 2023 年的真实数据。结果证明了其在产业战略、企业决策和地缘政治竞争中的广泛适用性。因此，复合联盟-消耗博弈（CCAG）提供了一个强大的新工具，用于理解和预测复杂、多层战略环境中的结果。), a unified theoretical framework that formally integrates a war of attrition between coalitions with a simultaneous war of attrition within each coalition. In our model, the collective endurance of a coalition in external competition is endogenously determined by the strategic choices of its members, who internally compete for shares of the eventual spoils. We prove the nonexistence of pure-strategy equilibria and characterize the unique mixed-strategy Nash equilibrium for both layers of the game. Our analysis reveals powerful feedback loops: the prospect of external victory intensifies internal competition, while internal discord weakens the coalition’s external standing—producing virtuous cycles of success or vicious cycles of fragmentation. We validate the model through a detailed case study comparing the traditional market (including gold, copper, and silver) with the cryptocurrency market (including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana), using real data from 2018 to 2023 in a simulation framework. The results demonstrate its broad applicability in industrial strategy, corporate decision-making, and geopolitical competition. The Compound Coalition–Attrition Game (CCAG) thus offers a powerful new tool for understanding and predicting outcomes in complex, multilayered strategic environments.

混合策略均衡的存在性和唯一性Existence and Uniqueness of Mixed-Strategy Equilibrium

根据混合策略纳什均衡的一般存在定理According to the general existence theorem for mixed-strategy Nash equilibria（纳什，1950）[2](Nash, 1950)[2]，在任何具有有限数量的玩家和紧凑的凸策略空间（其中效用函数是连续且准凹的）的游戏中，至少保证存在一个混合策略均衡。, in any game with a finite number of players and compact, convex strategy spaces—where utility functions are continuous and quasi-concave—at least one mixed-strategy equilibrium is guaranteed to exist.

在所提出的模型的背景下，每个参与者的努力策略空间是连续且凸的，效用函数在策略参数中是连续且凹的。这些条件在连续博弈的理论结果的支持下，确保了混合策略均衡的存在。关于混合策略均衡的唯一性，由于非线性结构和效用函数的相互依赖，一般不能保证唯一性，取决于数值分析和具体模型假设。尽管如此，平衡稳定性的研究可以帮助区分更合理和稳健的平衡。In the context of the proposed model, each player's effort strategy space is continuous and convex, and the utility function is continuous and concave in strategic parameters. These conditions, supported by the theoretical results of continuous games, ensure the existence of a mixed-strategy equilibrium. Regarding the uniqueness of the mixed-strategy equilibrium, due to the nonlinear structure and the interdependence of utility functions, uniqueness is generally not guaranteed and depends on numerical analysis and specific model assumptions. Nevertheless, the study of equilibrium stability can help distinguish more plausible and robust equilibria.

尽管 CCAG 模型具有广泛的适用性，但其固有的局限性值得仔细考虑。主要限制在于常识和完全理性的假设。在目前的形式中，该模型假设所有参与者都是完全理性的效用最大化者，完全了解博弈结构和其他参与者的收益。事实上，信息往往是不完整和不对称的，参与者可能会表现出有限理性，或者受到简单回报最大化之外的因素的激励，例如意识形态、公平或恶意。未来的研究可以将该模型扩展到贝叶斯游戏，在贝叶斯游戏中，玩家拥有有关其他人的成本或耐力水平的不完整信息。这种扩展与地缘政治应用特别相关，在地缘政治应用中，信息是一个关键且不确定的因素。另一个限制涉及奖品 V 的静态性质。Despite its wide applicability, the CCAG model possesses inherent limitations that warrant careful consideration. The primary limitation lies in the assumptions of common knowledge and full rationality. Theoretical analyses demonstrated that strategic equilibria at both layers interact and influence each other, and this interplay can generate reinforcing cycles of success or failure.
