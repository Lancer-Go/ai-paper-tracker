We prove that this design cannot know what it destroyed: evicted values can be altered so that everything the serving system retains is unchanged while the true attention-output error grows arbitrarily, so no serving-time estimator of that error is consistent. Randomized eviction restores identifiability. 7 讨论Discussion

政权引导。Guidance by regime.

这项两项规模的研究支持三项具体建议。首先，对于 预填充式压缩，其中查询是已知的、确定性的或 在 LongBench 类型上，25-50% 预算的随机驱逐接近免费 工作量小，随机化不需要任何成本，也不需要证书； 监控属于更严格的预算。其次，对于答案级别的信任， 输出置信度门控；每个缓存端信号（包括我们的信号）都会丢失 那个比较。第三，对于流和代理内存设置，其中 在知道查询并且大约一半可回答之前，历史记录已被压缩 查询可能会悄无声息地中断，确定性方法无法看到损坏 （图The two-scale study supports three concrete recommendations. First, for prefill-style compression where the query is known, deterministic or randomized eviction at 25–50% budgets is close to free on LongBench-type workloads, randomization costs nothing, and no certificate is needed; monitoring belongs at harsher budgets. Second, for answer-level trust, gate on output confidence; every cache-side signal, ours included, loses that comparison. Third, for streaming and agent-memory settings, where history is compressed before queries are known and roughly half of answerable queries can silently break, deterministic methods cannot see the damage (Figure2），而且证书是网上最强的 我们测量的归因者是唯一一个其有效性与 设计，每个保留的尾部标记需要一个额外的标量，并且， 这个原型有双重解码开销。), and the certificate is the strongest online attributor we measured and the only one whose validity is tied to the design, at a cost of one extra scalar per retained tail token and, in this prototype, a twofold decode overhead.

随机化买了什么。What randomization buys.

该理论承诺可识别性；通过实验找到它的价值。 随机化尾部并没有使驱逐在实际任务中更加准确 （它也不是更糟），并且它没有产生更好的失败 预测器比自由输出置信度。它使一个数量变得可估量 事实证明，确定性设计无法估计注入的误差 由压缩通道本身，以及数量的两个地方 物有所值，流媒体中的损坏监控和重新计算调度 制度，正是实验证明的地方。The theory promised identifiability; the experiments locate its value. Randomizing the tail did not make eviction more accurate on real tasks (it is not worse either), and it did not yield a better failure predictor than free output confidence.
