1st Ali Iranmanesh    2nd Peng Liu
摘要Abstract

开放词汇体现的人工智能代理越来越依赖 视觉语言模型，例如用于物体感知的 CLIP 和任务接地。然而，共享嵌入空间 实现这种灵活性会带来结构性漏洞 印刷攻击，即物理场景中的印刷文本 语义上凌驾于视觉判断之上。虽然之前的工作有 在静态 2D 基准和 3D 中量化了这种威胁 导航任务，它对整个感知-计划-行动的影响 家用机器人操纵的管道尚未探索。Open-vocabulary embodied AI agents increasingly rely on vision-language models such as CLIP for object perception and task grounding. However, the shared embedding space that enables this flexibility introduces a structural vulnerability to typographic attacks, where printed text in a physical scene semantically overrides visual judgment. While prior work has quantified this threat in static 2D benchmarks and 3D navigation tasks, its impact on the full Sense-Plan-Act pipeline of household robot manipulation remains unexplored.

这项工作评估了基于栖息地的印刷攻击 使用 HomeRobot 基准进行模拟。我们介绍一个 解耦感知架构，暴露冻结的 CLIP 编码器到对抗性贴纸，同时保持几何 通过 DETIC 接地。在包含 59 个可归因事件的受控评估池中， 该攻击的总体攻击成功率 (ASR) 为 67.8%， 在不受控制的情况下，完全成功的事件中，成功率上升至 70.0% 视角和遮挡，没有感知优化。This work evaluates typographic attacks in a Habitat-based simulation using the HomeRobot benchmark. We introduce a decoupled perception architecture that exposes a frozen CLIP encoder to adversarial stickers while maintaining geometric grounding via DETIC. In a controlled evaluation pool of 59 attributable episodes, the attack achieves an overall Attack Success Rate (ASR) of 67.8%, rising to 70.0% among fully successful episodes, under uncontrolled viewing angles and occlusion with no perceptual optimization.

重要的是，我们发现感知错误通过持久的 3D 传播 产生动力学故障的语义图，此处定义为物理执行 由对手驾驶抓取和运输错误的物体 中毒的语义状态。在这些情况下，机器人会物理抓取 并将错误的物体传送到目标容器。这些结果确立了 印刷错误分类为真实的、可测量的和 对模块化安全性造成物理上的严重威胁 先前印刷攻击研究的操纵管道 未经审查。Critically, we find that perceptual errors propagate through the persistent 3D semantic map to produce kinetic failures, defined here as physically executed grasping and transport of the wrong object driven by an adversarially poisoned semantic state. In these cases, the robot physically grasps and delivers the wrong object to a target receptacle. These results establish typographic misclassification as a real, measurable, and physically consequential threat to the safety of modular manipulation pipelines that prior typographic attack research has left unexamined.
