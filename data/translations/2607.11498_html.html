1 简介Introduction

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型从大规模数据集中学习机器人操作策略，根据视觉观察和语言指令预测动作。 这些动作通常在机器人自己的 3D 坐标系中定义（Vision-language-action (VLA) models learn robotic manipulation policies from large-scale datasets, predicting actions from visual observations and language instructions. These actions are commonly defined in the robot’s own 3D coordinate frame (例如e.g.，机器人基架中的任务空间末端执行器命令），因此可靠的操纵需要 推理 关于目标对象相对于机器人在公制 3D 坐标中的位置。 然而，大多数 VLA 接收相机帧中的观察结果，无论是 RGB 图像还是深度图, task-space end-effector commands in the robot base frame), so reliable manipulation requires reasoning about where the target object lies in metric 3D coordinates relative to the robot. However, most VLAs receive observations in the camera frame, whether as RGB images or depth maps[1, 2, 3, 4, 5]。 这会造成帧不匹配：该策略观察相机帧中的场景，但预测机器人帧中的动作。. This creates a frame mismatch: the policy observes the scene in the camera frame but predicts actions in the robot frame.

当训练数据跨越不同的摄像机视点时，这种帧不匹配变得更难以处理，因为大规模数据集越来越多地聚合具有不同摄像机设置的机构之间的演示This frame mismatch becomes harder to handle when training data spans diverse camera viewpoints, as large-scale datasets increasingly aggregate demonstrations across institutions with different camera setups[6, 7, 8]。 在固定摄像机视点下， 所有观察都相对于机器人共享相同的视点，因此单个观察到动作的映射在整个数据集中保持一致。 在多种观点下，这 不再成立： 政策 此外，还必须了解从不同视点进行的观察如何映射到它们应产生的机器人框架动作。 两种情况都存在框架不匹配，但只有在观点变化的情况下，策略才必须概括跨观点的观察到行动的映射，而不是记住单个观点。. Under a fixed camera viewpoint, all observations share the same viewpoint relative to the robot, so a single observation-to-action mapping remains consistent across the dataset. Under a wide range of viewpoints, this no longer holds: the policy must additionally learn how observations from different viewpoints map to the robot-frame actions they should produce. The frame mismatch is present in both cases, but only under viewpoint variation must the policy generalize the observation-to-action mapping across viewpoints rather than memorize a single one.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 处理 VLA 中的摄像机视点变化Handling Camera Viewpoint Variation in VLAs

相机视点变化会降低 VLA 性能Camera viewpoint variation degrades VLA performance[10, 11]，并且它出现在两种不同的制度中。在训练时，大规模数据集汇总了不同相机设置的演示, and it arises in two distinct regimes. At training time, large-scale datasets aggregate demonstrations across diverse camera setups[6, 8, 7, 12]，因此政策必须同时适合多种观点。在评估时，部署摄像头可能与训练中看到的有所不同, so the policy must fit many viewpoints at once. At evaluation time, the deployment camera may differ from those seen in training[13, 14]，因此该策略必须推广到一个看不见的观点。我们的工作主要针对训练时间制度，此外我们还测试了对看不见的评估相机的泛化能力。, so the policy must generalize to an unseen viewpoint. Our work primarily targets the training-time regime, and we additionally test generalization to unseen evaluation cameras.

现有方法可以根据它们提供视点信息的方式进行分组。 第一行将策略的输入观察保留为摄像机视图图像，提供其周围的视点信息，作为显式摄像机或 3D 位置提示Existing methods can be grouped by how they supply viewpoint information. The first line leaves the policy’s input observation as a camera-view image, supplying viewpoint information around it, either as explicit camera or 3D positional cues[15, 16, 17, 18]或者作为动作标签的相机帧重新参数化or as a camera-frame reparameterization of the action labels[19]。在这两种情况下，策略的视觉输入仍然是摄像机视图图像，因此场景永远不会在动作所在的机器人框架中表达。 第二行综合视图，要么扩大训练时间覆盖范围. In both, the policy’s visual input stays a camera-view image, so the scene is never expressed in the robot frame that the actions live in. The second line synthesizes views, either expanding training-time coverage[20, 21]或将测试时间视图规范化为训练分布or canonicalizing test-time views toward the training distribution[22, 23]，但两者都依赖于生成模型，对于远离训练视图的观点，该模型的综合变得不可靠，从而限制了它们提供的稳健性。第三行，最接近我们的，通过构建共享的 3D 场景表示来作用于观察本身, but both rely on a generative model whose synthesis grows unreliable for viewpoints far from the training views, capping the robustness they offer. The third line, closest to ours, acts on the observation itself by building shared 3D scene representations[24, 25]，但这些表示不再保留预训练 VLA 所期望的图像结构。相反，我们将观察结果表示为以机器人为中心的点图，它保持该结构，同时将相同的机器人框架坐标分配给跨视点的物理点。这为不同视点的演示提供了一致的几何形状，并且相同的不变性转移到看不见的评估相机。, yet these representations no longer retain the image structure that pretrained VLAs expect. We instead express the observation as a robot-centric pointmap, which keeps that structure while assigning the same robot-frame coordinate to a physical point across viewpoints. This gives viewpoint-diverse demonstrations a consistent geometry, and the same invariance transfers to unseen evaluation cameras.

2.2 将 3D 几何图形注入 VLAInjecting 3D Geometry into VLAs

操纵本质上是一项 3D 任务，但 VLA 通常仅通过 2D RGB 图像感知场景。 因此，越来越多的工作通过显式 3D 几何来增强 VLA。 这些方法可以根据注入方式进行分组，每种方法要么添加专用模块或阶段，要么在部署时放弃 3D 度量。 第一行附有专用的3D编码器或几何专家Manipulation is inherently a 3D task, yet VLAs typically perceive the scene only through 2D RGB images. A growing body of work therefore augments VLAs with explicit 3D geometry. These methods can be grouped by how they inject it, and each either adds a dedicated module or stage or forgoes metric 3D at deployment. The first line attaches a dedicated 3D encoder or geometric expert[26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31]，引入了无法继承 VLA 预训练视觉权重的新模块。 第二行将几何图形重新渲染为 2D 视图, introducing new modules that cannot inherit the VLA’s pretrained visual weights. The second line re-renders the geometry as 2D views[32, 33]或使用生成世界模型预测未来的 3D 场景or predicts future 3D scenes with a generative world model[34]，为每个推理步骤添加渲染或生成阶段。第三行利用几何基础模型，注入其特征作为输入, adding a rendering or generation stage to every inference step. The third line leverages a geometric foundation model, injecting its features as inputs[35]或在训练期间将 VLA 特征与它们对齐or aligning VLA features to them during training[36]，因此在部署时策略不会收到度量 3D 输入。 相反，我们提供显式几何图形作为点图，即像素存储 3D 坐标的图像。因为点图保留相同的, so at deployment the policy receives no metric 3D input. We instead provide explicit geometry as a pointmap, an image whose pixels store 3D coordinates. Because pointmaps retain the sameH×WH\times W结构为 RGB，点图编码器重用了 VLA RGB 编码器的预训练权重，不添加特定于点云的编码器，也没有每次推理阶段，同时仍然在部署时提供度量 3D 输入。点图已经是现代 3D 视觉中的标准表示structure as RGB, the pointmap encoder reuses the pretrained weights of the VLA’s RGB encoder, adding no point cloud-specific encoder and no per-inference stage while still providing metric 3D input at deployment. Pointmaps are already a standard representation in modern 3D vision[37, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42, 43, 44]，最近的工作将它们应用于从头开始的扩散政策, and recent work applies them to from-scratch diffusion policies[45, 46]。然而，它们在预训练的 VLA 中的使用以及表达它们的框架仍未得到充分探索。. Yet their use within pretrained VLAs, and the frame in which to express them, remain underexplored.

4.1 机器人框架点图优于相机信息调节Robot-frame pointmaps outperform conditioning on camera information

4.2 图像形式的点图优于点云Image-form pointmaps outperform point clouds

给定相同的机器人框架 3D 点，我们接下来询问如何将它们提供给 VLA：作为保留 RGB 像素网格的图像形式点图（Given the same robot-frame 3D points, we next ask how to provide them to the VLA: as an image-form pointmap that preserves the RGB pixel grid (H×WH\times W），或者作为没有的点云。 点云携带相同的 3D 机器人框架点，但以两种代价丢弃图像形式的像素网格。首先，它不再与预训练 VLA 的基于图像的视觉路径保持一致，因此它需要专用的点云编码器和单独的融合机制，在 VLA 预训练期间使用的视觉标记之外添加 3D 特征流。其次，点云编码器通常将场景二次采样为固定数量的点，产生可能会忽略细粒度细节的稀疏输入。相反，点图为每个 RGB 像素保留一个 XYZ 值，因此它重用了 VLA 的图像编码器架构，并直接与相应的 RGB 标记融合。), or as a point cloud that does not. A point cloud carries the same 3D robot-frame points, but discards the image-form pixel grid at two costs. 6 局限性Limitations

我们的研究留下了几个悬而未决的问题。我们不会消除点图相对于动作专家的注入方式，也不会消除它与预训练配方的交互方式，因此将两者结合起来的最佳方式仍然悬而未决。与点云的比较使用单个采样预算，更大的预算可以缩小我们报告的差距。 此外，点图在训练和测试时都需要校准相机的内部和外部，这将它们限制在可以进行校准的设置上。 最后，我们的相机变化结果侧重于相机放置和外部因素的变化，尚未涵盖相机数量或其视野的变化，我们将其留给未来的工作。Our study leaves several questions open. We do not ablate how the pointmap is injected relative to the action expert, nor how it interacts with the pretraining recipe, so the best way to combine the two remains unsettled. The comparison against a point cloud uses a single sampling budget, and a larger budget could narrow the gap we report. In addition, pointmaps require calibrated camera intrinsics and extrinsics at both training and test time, which restricts them to setups where calibration is available. Finally, our camera-variation results focus on changes in camera placement and extrinsics, and do not yet cover changes in the number of cameras or their fields of view, which we leave to future work.

7 结论Conclusion

我们研究了在不同摄像机视角下收集演示时，VLA 应如何使用 3D 观测。在这种设置中，在训练期间可以从不同的摄像机视角观察相同的任务，需要策略将图像观察与机器人的动作框架相关联。 我们没有将这种相机与机器人的关系留给策略从 RGB、深度和校准输入进行推断，而是直接在机器人框架中将每个观察到的场景点表示为以机器人为中心的点图。 点图保留了密集We studied how VLAs should use 3D observations when demonstrations are collected under various camera viewpoints. In this setting, the same task can be observed from different camera viewpoints during training, requiring the policy to relate image observations to the robot’s action frame. Rather than leaving this camera-to-robot relationship for the policy to infer from RGB, depth, and calibration inputs, we express each observed scene point directly in the robot frame as a robot-centric pointmap. The pointmap preserves the denseH×WH\times W图像网格，允许通过预训练 VLA 的现有视觉路径将以机器人为中心的 3D 坐标与 RGB 标记融合。image grid, allowing robot-centric 3D coordinates to be fused with RGB tokens through the existing visual pathway of a pretrained VLA.

我们的实验指出了一个简单而有效的输入：以末端执行器为中心的机器人框架、图像形式的点图。以机器人为中心的点图改进了模拟和真实机器人实验中的预训练 VLA。 这些结果提出了 3D 感知 VLA 的一般原则：当校准可用时，在将观察结果传递给策略之前，在机器人动作的坐标系中表达观察结果。 以机器人为中心的点图实现了这一原则，同时保留了预训练 VLA 已使用的图像形式结构。Our experiments point to a simple and effective input: a robot-frame, image-form pointmap centered on the end effector. Robot-centric pointmaps improve pretrained VLAs in both simulation and real-robot experiments. These results suggest a general principle for 3D-aware VLAs: when calibration is available, express observations in the coordinate system where the robot acts before passing them to the policy. Robot-centric pointmaps implement this principle while retaining the image-form structure that pretrained VLAs already use.
