重新审视按政策蒸馏：Revisiting On-Policy Distillation:
经验故障模式和简单修复Empirical Failure Modes and Simple Fixes

摘要Abstract

在政策蒸馏 (OPD) 对于大型语言模型 (LLM) 训练后很有吸引力，因为它评估教师对学生生成的部署的反馈，而不是固定的教师跟踪。然而，在长期设置中，常见的采样令牌变体是脆弱的：它减少了与单令牌信号的分布匹配，并且随着推出远离教师经常访问的前缀而变得越来越不可靠。我们从估算器和实施方面重新审视 OPD。理论上，令牌级 OPD 相对于序列级反向 KL 有偏差，但它具有更严格的最坏情况方差界限；我们的玩具研究在经验上显示了相同的权衡，更强的未来奖励耦合会产生更高的梯度方差和更不稳定的学习。根据经验，我们确定了采样令牌 OPD 的三种故障模式：不平衡的单令牌信号、教师对学生生成的前缀的指导不可靠以及由令牌生成器或特殊令牌不匹配引起的失真。我们与老师一起解决这些问题On-policy distillation (OPD) is appealing for large language model (LLM) post-training because it evaluates teacher feedback on student-generated rollouts rather than fixed teacher traces. In long-horizon settings, however, the common sampled-token variant is fragile: it reduces distribution matching to a one-token signal and becomes increasingly unreliable as rollouts drift away from prefixes the teacher commonly visits. We revisit OPD from the estimator and implementation sides. Theoretically, token-level OPD is biased relative to sequence-level reverse-KL, but it has a much tighter worst-case variance bound; our toy study shows the same tradeoff empirically, with stronger future-reward coupling producing higher gradient variance and less stable learning. Empirically, we identify three failure modes of sampled-token OPD: an imbalanced one-token signal, unreliable teacher guidance on student-generated prefixes, and distortions caused by tokenizer or special-token mismatch. We address these issues with teacher top-KK本地支持匹配，实现为带有 top- 的截断反向 KLlocal support matching, implemented as truncated reverse-KL with top-pp推出采样和特殊令牌屏蔽。在单任务数学推理和多任务代理加数学训练中，该目标比采样令牌 OPD 产生更稳定的优化和更好的下游性能。rollout sampling and special-token masking. Across single-task math reasoning and multi-task agentic-plus-math training, this objective yields more stable optimization and better downstream performance than sampled-token OPD.

1 简介Introduction

在政策蒸馏 (OPD) 训练学生自行推出，同时与更强大的老师一起评估本地反馈。这使得 OPD 对于长视野推理和代理后训练具有吸引力，学生可以快速到达固定教师轨迹中罕见或不存在的前缀On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student on its own rollouts while evaluating local feedback with a stronger teacher. This makes OPD attractive for long-horizon reasoning and agentic post-training, where the student quickly reaches prefixes that are rare or absent in fixed teacher traces(Agarwal et al., 2024; Gu et al., 2024)。因此，实际问题不在于政策上的教师监督原则上是否有用，而在于一旦培训由学生生成的轨迹驱动，哪个目标仍然可靠。. The practical question is therefore not whether on-policy teacher supervision is useful in principle, but which objective remains reliable once training is driven by student-generated trajectories.

在当前的语言模型管道中，OPD 通常作为采样令牌比较来实现：在每个解码步骤中，学生仅通过其采样令牌的对数比进行更新。这种近似很便宜，但由于至少三个原因而很脆弱。它将分配水平的差异转化为高度不平衡的单令牌信号；它可能会过度信任老师对学生常见但对老师来说不典型的前缀；而且它很容易被标记器或特殊标记不匹配所扭曲。存在相应的估计器权衡。序列耦合程度更高的目标可以恢复令牌级 OPD 丢弃的信息，但更强的奖励耦合也会使优化变得更加嘈杂。In current language-model pipelines, OPD is usually implemented as a sampled-token comparison: at each decoding step, the student is updated only through the log-ratio on its sampled token. This approximation is cheap, but brittle for at least three reasons. It turns a distribution-level discrepancy into a highly imbalanced one-token signal; it can over-trust the teacher on prefixes that are common for the student but atypical for the teacher; and it is easily distorted by tokenizer or special-token mismatch. There is a corresponding estimator tradeoff. A more sequence-coupled objective can recover information that token-level OPD discards, but stronger reward coupling can also make optimization much noisier.

我们首先在估算器层面研究这种权衡。序列级反向 KL 将每个代币更新与未来奖励结合起来，而代币级 OPD 则放弃了这些条款。因此，令牌级 OPD 相对于序列级目标存在偏差，但它具有更严格的最坏情况方差界限。我们的玩具实验在经验上显示了相同的模式：随着未来奖励耦合的增加，梯度方差上升，优化变得不太稳定。这为长期训练后提出了一个简单的设计目标：保持监督足够局部以控制方差，同时使局部比较比单标记点估计更脆弱。We study this tradeoff first at the estimator level. Sequence-level reverse-KL couples each token update to future rewards, whereas token-level OPD drops those terms. Token-level OPD is therefore biased relative to the sequence-level objective, but it has a much tighter worst-case variance bound. Our toy experiment shows the same pattern empirically: as future-reward coupling increases, gradient variance rises and optimization becomes less stable. This suggests a simple design target for long-horizon post-training: keep supervision local enough to control variance, while making the local comparison less brittle than a one-token point estimate.

受这种观点的启发，我们用教师顶级监督取代了采样令牌监督Motivated by this view, we replace sampled-token supervision with teacher top-KK本地支持匹配。在每个前缀，我们比较教师和学生在教师本地合理支持上的分布，而不是仅奖励采样的令牌。我们将这个目标实现为带有 top- 的截断反向-KLlocal support matching. At each prefix, we compare teacher and student distributions on the teacher’s locally plausible support instead of rewarding only the sampled token. We implement this objective as truncated reverse-KL with top-pp推出采样和特殊令牌屏蔽。由此产生的更新仍然是本地的且廉价的，但与采样令牌 OPD 相比，对特殊采样延续和令牌化工件不太敏感。rollout sampling and special-token masking. The resulting update is still local and inexpensive, but less sensitive to idiosyncratic sampled continuations and tokenization artifacts than sampled-token OPD.

3.2 为什么采样令牌 OPD 在实践中很脆弱Why sampled-token OPD is brittle in practice

尽管从偏差-方差的角度来看，令牌级 OPD 很有吸引力，但采样令牌比较在实践中可能很脆弱。我们隔离了三个不同的问题：(1) 蒸馏信号高度不平衡，(2) 教师信号对于学生生成的前缀变得不太可靠，(3) 标记器和特殊标记不匹配可能会进一步扭曲单标记比较。Although token-level OPD is attractive from a bias–variance perspective, the sampled-token comparison can be brittle in practice. 我们假设有两个因素放大了这个问题。首先，教师分布通常很尖锐，因此即使是轻微的师生分歧也可能产生较大的对数比值。其次，教师的生成模式和学生的生成模式之间的差异使得学生前缀更有可能脱离教师的典型上下文。 同样的失败也出现在教师信号随位置变化的方式上。图We hypothesize that two factors amplify this issue. First, teacher distributions are often sharp, so even modest student-teacher disagreement can produce large log-ratio values. 我们的方法保留了令牌级别的 OPD，但用对每个前缀的教师选择的支持集进行分布级别比较来替换单令牌监督。 这会产生一个截断的反向 KL 目标，在保持计算效率的同时提高训练信号的平衡。 部分Our method retains token-level OPD but replaces one-token supervision with a distribution-level comparison over a teacher-selected support set at each prefix. 4.2 实用的稳定选择Practical stabilization choices

支持集重整化。Support-set renormalization.

重整化是必要的，因为目标是根据截断的支持而不是完整的词汇来评估的。如果没有它，优化可能会变得不稳定，因为支持内的教师和学生质量无法直接比较。Renormalization is necessary because the objective is evaluated on a truncated support rather than the full vocabulary. Without it, optimization can become unstable because the teacher and student mass inside the support is not directly comparable.

顶-Top-pp推出抽样。rollout sampling.

我们通过顶部生成推出We generate rollouts with top-pp抽样。无约束采样偶尔会产生极低概率的标记，这反过来又会产生前缀，其中教师分布的信息量较少，而学生分布已经恶化。顶-sampling. Unconstrained sampling occasionally produces extremely low-probability tokens, which in turn creates prefixes where the teacher distribution is less informative and the student distribution is already deteriorating. Top-pp采样使轨迹更接近典型的连续，并使教师信号更加可靠。sampling keeps trajectories closer to typical continuations and makes the teacher signal more reliable.

特殊令牌屏蔽。Special-token masking.

我们屏蔽有问题的特殊标记，以减少由于不兼容的标记化约定而导致的漏报。这是一个正交工程修复：在我们的实验中，它对采样令牌 OPD 基线有实质性帮助，而我们的本地支持目标对此不太敏感。原则上，我们还可以合并多标记标记变体或对等效标记化进行平均，但我们在这里不追求那些标记器特定的补救措施，因为屏蔽是最简单的与模型无关的校正。We mask problematic special tokens to reduce false negatives caused by incompatible tokenization conventions. This is an orthogonal engineering fix: in our experiments it materially helps the sampled-token OPD baseline, while our local support objective is much less sensitive to it. In principle, one could also merge multi-token marker variants or average over equivalent tokenizations, but we do not pursue those tokenizer-specific remedies here because masking is the simplest model-agnostic correction.

6 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

目前的目标仍然是一个被截断的替代品。The current objective is still a truncated surrogate.

我们的本地支持损失是在受限令牌子集和由推出策略（例如 top-）生成的前缀上进行评估的Our local-support loss is evaluated on a restricted token subset and on prefixes generated by a rollout policy such as top-pp采样。因此，它并不等同于全词汇量反向 KL，也不明确纠正生成训练前缀的采样过程。这种限制在我们的研究中仍未得到充分探索的两个地方最为重要：在增强教师顶部时如何合并采样令牌 -sampling. It is therefore not equivalent to full-vocabulary reverse-KL, nor does it explicitly correct for the sampling process that produced the training prefixes. This limitation matters most in two places that remain underexplored in our study: how to incorporate the sampled token when augmenting teacher top-KK支持，以及当推出和培训政策不同时是否需要重要性加权式修正。因此，我们将当前的公式视为实用的设计点，而不是支持集构造的最终答案。support, and whether importance-weighting-style corrections are needed when rollout and training policies differ. We therefore view the current formulation as a practical design point rather than a final answer to support-set construction.

奖励黑客解释仍然是一种机制假设。The reward-hacking explanation is still a mechanism hypothesis.

我们的定性案例使故障模式变得具体，但它们并没有分离出完整的因果机制。特别是，尖锐的教师分布和分布外前缀共同产生误导性局部奖励的假设应该被视为有证据支持的合理解释，而不是完全确定的因果关系。Our qualitative cases make the failure mode concrete, but they do not isolate a complete causal mechanism. In particular, the hypothesis that sharp teacher distributions and off-distribution prefixes jointly create misleading local rewards should be treated as a plausible explanation supported by evidence rather than as a fully identified causal account.

教师匹配仍然是任务成功的不完美代表。Teacher matching remains an imperfect proxy for task success.

即使 OPD 被明确定义为教师匹配目标，所产生的奖励仍然可能偏离成功行为的基本概念。我们的奖励黑客案例使这一差距变得具体：即使整体轨迹已经无益或有害，本地教师偏好的延续仍然可以获得奖励。我们的实验中也仍然存在与教师之间的明显差距，这表明更好的本地监督只是蒸馏问题的一部分，特别是当教师和学生存在很大差异时。缩小这一差距可能需要更强的推广控制、更好地处理分配转移、更好地利用教师的不确定性，以及与结果可验证的奖励相结合。Even when OPD is well defined as a teacher-matching objective, the resulting reward can still diverge from the underlying notion of successful behavior. Our reward-hacking cases make this gap concrete: locally teacher-preferred continuations can remain rewardable even when the overall trajectory is already unhelpful or harmful. A noticeable gap to the teacher also remains in our experiments, which suggests that better local supervision is only one part of the distillation problem, especially when teacher and student differ substantially. Closing that gap may require stronger rollout control, better handling of distribution shift, better use of teacher uncertainty, and combinations with outcome-verifiable rewards.

7 结论Conclusion

本文重新审视了长期后培训中的 OPD。核心权衡很简单：序列级耦合更接近底层目标，但方差可能更高，而采样令牌 OPD 很容易优化，但通常太脆弱，无法提供可靠的监督。老师顶-This paper revisits OPD in long-horizon post-training. The central tradeoff is straightforward: sequence-level coupling is closer to the underlying objective but can be much higher-variance, whereas sampled-token OPD is easy to optimize but often too brittle to provide reliable supervision. Teacher top-KK局部支持匹配通过保持目标局部性而占据中间立场，同时用截断的分布级别比较代替单令牌监督。在单任务数学推理和交替代理加数学训练中，它改进了采样令牌 OPD 的优化行为和下游性能。教师匹配和任务成功之间的剩余差距表明，更好的局部目标应该与对推出漂移和教师不确定性的更强控制相结合。local support matching occupies the middle ground by keeping the objective local while replacing one-token supervision with a truncated distribution-level comparison. Across single-task math reasoning and alternating agentic-plus-math training, it improves optimization behavior and downstream performance over sampled-token OPD. The remaining gap between teacher matching and task success suggests that better local objectives should be paired with stronger control of rollout drift and teacher uncertainty.

多任务迁移中的 OPD 与 RL。OPD versus RL in multi-task transfer.

我们的多任务结果促使我们对 OPD 和 RL 作为传输机制进行更直接的比较。在强化学习中，可以直接从跨任务的环境奖励中读取正迁移或负迁移。在 OPD 中，优化目标仍然是由教师导出的，因此迁移会通过教师认为的本地优选行为进行过滤。这种区别可能有助于解释为什么我们的多任务收益在数学方面最强，以及为什么附近的支持集定义在该设置中变得不那么统一。 OPD 和 RL 之间的匹配任务、匹配计算比较将有助于澄清何时教师引导的迁移跟踪环境级别的泛化以及何时教师与奖励的差距成为瓶颈。Our multi-task results motivate a more direct comparison between OPD and RL as transfer mechanisms. In RL, positive or negative transfer can be read directly from environment reward across tasks. In OPD, the optimization target remains teacher-derived, so transfer is filtered through what the teacher regards as locally preferable behavior. This distinction may help explain why our multi-task gains are strongest on the math side and why nearby support-set definitions become less uniform in that setting. A matched-task, matched-compute comparison between OPD and RL would help clarify when teacher-guided transfer tracks environment-level generalization and when the teacher–reward gap becomes the bottleneck.

持续学习作为测试平台。Continual learning as a testbed.

持续学习是 OPD 的另一个自然环境。教师引导的政策目标可以在学生适应新任务时充当保留机制，但该制度也将强调本文中提出的问题：分布转移、教师陈旧以及长期适应范围内近似误差的累积。因此，在那里测试 OPD 不仅会探究本地支持匹配是否可以减轻遗忘，而且还会探究一旦学生不断远离教师的原始领域，基于教师的目标是否仍然有用。Continual learning is another natural setting for OPD. A teacher-guided on-policy objective could act as a retention mechanism while the student adapts to new tasks, but that regime would also stress exactly the issues surfaced in this paper: distribution shift, teacher staleness, and the accumulation of approximation error over long adaptation horizons. Testing OPD there would therefore probe not only whether local support matching mitigates forgetting, but also whether teacher-based objectives remain useful once the student keeps moving away from the teacher’s original domain.

与其他稳定方向的关系。Relation to other stabilization directions.

这项工作是对奖励黑客攻击缓解、EMA 锚定稳定性等方向的补充This work is complementary to directions such as reward-hacking mitigation, EMA-anchor stabilization with top-KK KL (Zhang and Ba, 2026)，基于扰动的离策略校正, perturbation-based off-policy correction(Ye et al., 2026)，以及教师和学生推出之间的 logit 级融合, and logit-level fusion between teacher and student rollouts(Zhang et al., 2026)。这些方法解决了同一个更广泛问题的不同部分：一旦教师和学生的政策开始出现分歧，如何使教师衍生的学习信号保持有用。我们将本地支持匹配视为更大工具箱中的一个组成部分，而不是作为这些稳定策略的替代品。. These methods address different parts of the same broader problem: how to keep teacher-derived learning signals useful once teacher and student policies begin to diverge. We view local support matching as one component in that larger toolbox, rather than as a replacement for those stabilization strategies.
