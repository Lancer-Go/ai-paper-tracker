使用可本地部署的大型语言模型进行多模态生存分析Multimodal Survival Analysis with Locally Deployable Large Language Models

University of Oxford
moritz.gogl@keble.ox.ac.uk
&Christopher Yau
University of Oxford
christopher.yau@wrh.ox.ac.uk
摘要Abstract

我们使用本地可部署的大语言模型（LLM）研究整合临床文本、表格协变量和基因组图谱的多模式生存分析。由于许多机构面临严格的计算和隐私限制，这种设置鼓励使用轻量级的本地模型。我们的方法共同估计校准的生存概率，并通过师生蒸馏和有原则的多模式融合生成简洁的、基于证据的预后文本。在 TCGA 队列中，它的表现优于标准基线，避免了对云服务的依赖和相关的隐私问题，并降低了在基础法学硕士中可以观察到的幻觉或错误校准估计的风险。We study multimodal survival analysis integrating clinical text, tabular covariates, and genomic profiles using locally deployable large language models (LLMs). As many institutions face tight computational and privacy constraints, this setting motivates the use of lightweight, on‑premises models. Our approach jointly estimates calibrated survival probabilities and generates concise, evidence‑grounded prognosis text via teacher–student distillation and principled multimodal fusion. On a TCGA cohort, it outperforms standard baselines, avoids reliance on cloud services and associated privacy concerns, and reduces the risk of hallucinated or miscalibrated estimates that can be observed in base LLMs.

1 简介Introduction

生存分析估计事件随时间推移发生的概率，对于医疗决策（例如预测死亡率或疾病进展）至关重要。经典模型对结构化协变量（例如年龄、性别、基因组数据）进行操作，而临床实践也会生成丰富的非结构化数据，例如临床报告。最近的大型语言模型（LLM）可以对此类文本进行推理并生成人类可读的评估，但云托管模型引发了隐私问题，并且重量级本地部署对于许多机构来说是不切实际的Survival analysis estimates the probability of an event over time and is central to medical decision-making (e.g., forecasting mortality or disease progression). Classical models operate on structured covariates (e.g., age, sex, genomic data), while clinical practice also generates rich unstructured data, such as clinical reports. Recent large language models (LLMs) can reason over such text and produce human-readable assessments, but cloud-hosted models raise privacy concerns and heavyweight local deployments are impractical for many institutionskingsfundInfrastructureInnovation。 此外，基础法学硕士没有针对生存预测进行校准，因为他们没有接受原始生存数据的训练；他们通常会回忆已发布的汇总统计数据（并且可能会产生幻觉），而不是生成基于数据的估计. Moreover, base LLMs are not calibrated for survival prediction as they are not trained on raw survival data; they typically recall published summary statistics–and may hallucinate–rather than producing data‑grounded estimatesxiong2024.

我们提出了一个统一的、可局部部署的多模式生存框架，将紧凑的因果法学硕士与结构化协变量和基因表达配对。我们的模型共同生成校准的生存曲线和简洁的预后解释。师生管道首先向大型法学硕士教师询问固定范围内的数字生存概率和简短的评估；然后，学生从老师的口头推理和观察到的生存结果中学习。该架构支持离散时间风险模型或 Cox 比例风险 (CoxPH)We present a unified, locally deployable multimodal survival framework that pairs a compact causal LLM with structured covariates and gene expression. Our model jointly produces calibrated survival curves and concise prognosis explanations. A teacher–student pipeline first queries a large teacher LLM for numeric survival probabilities at fixed horizons and a brief assessment; the student then learns from both the teacher’s verbalized reasoning and the observed survival outcomes. The architecture supports either a discrete-time hazards model or a Cox proportional hazards (CoxPH)Cox1972模型，并通过串联或通过单独的门控头来融合模式。与之前的多模式生存模型相比model, and fuses modalities by concatenation or via separate gated heads. Compared to prior multimodal survival modelsZhao2021_BERTSurv; Song2025，我们的方法将生存估计与简洁的解释结合起来，同时保持轻量级和本地可部署性。, our approach couples survival estimation with concise explanations while remaining lightweight and locally deployable.

贡献。Contributions.

我们的工作做出了三个贡献：(1) 一个校准的本地可部署多模式生存框架，将紧凑的因果 LLM（1.5B 参数）与协变量和基因表达结合起来，支持离散头和 CoxPH 头的灵活融合； （2）师生微调方案，具有单一前向传递文本目标，可提取数字生存概率和基本原理； (3) 对 TCGA 队列的实证评估Our work makes three contributions: (1) a calibrated locally deployable multimodal survival framework that couples a compact causal LLM (1.5B parameters) with covariates and gene expression, supporting both discrete and CoxPH heads with flexible fusion; (2) a teacher–student fine-tuning scheme with a single forward-pass text objective that distills numeric survival probabilities and rationales; and (3) an empirical evaluation on a TCGA cohortTCGA2013显示出相对于基线的性能改善，以及简洁、口头的预后解释。showing improved performance over baselines, alongside concise, verbalized prognosis explanations.

默认情况下，所有样本都会导致文本丢失。然而，由于教师的预测是通过提示法学硕士获得的，而不是直接从观察到的生存结果中学习的，因此它们可能会被错误校准。遵循类似的想法By default, all samples contribute to the text loss. All samples remain in the dataset and fully contribute to survival objectives; only language-model targets are masked to reduce the influence of potentially miscalibrated supervision in text generation.

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一个统一的、可本地部署的多模式生存框架，该框架将非结构化文本（例如临床报告）、表格协变量和基因表达融合在紧凑的法学硕士骨干上，硬件要求相对较低。该方法通过师生计划共同学习校准的生存估计和简洁的口头评估。我们探索了关键的设计选择，包括不同的底层生存模型和融合策略。正在进行的工作将根据基因组学调整语言模型本身，以显示患者特定的、语言化的风险因素（例如显着基因或突变），从而在临床工作流程中实现更值得信赖的人类与人工智能协作。We presented a unified, locally deployable multimodal survival framework that fuses unstructured text (e.g., clinical reports), tabular covariates, and gene expression on a compact LLM backbone with comparatively low hardware requirements. The approach jointly learns calibrated survival estimates and concise, verbalized assessments via a teacher–student scheme. We explored key design choices, including different underlying survival models and fusion strategies. Ongoing work will condition the language model itself on genomics to surface patient-specific, verbalized risk factors (e.g., salient genes or mutations), enabling more trustworthy human–AI collaboration in clinical workflows.
