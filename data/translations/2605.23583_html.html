人工神经网络逆运动学求解需要多少训练样本How Many Training Samples Are Needed for the Inverse Kinematics Solutions by Artificial Neural Networks

Dong-Won Lim
The University of Suwon, Hwaseong 18323, Republic of Korea
dwlim@suwon.ac.kr ORCID: 0000-0002-3086-1237
摘要Abstract

逆运动学 (IK) 在机器人运动规划和控制中起着至关重要的作用。机器人机械臂的 IK 解可以通过几何、代数或雅克比方法等传统方法来完成，但这些方法都有缺点。人工神经网络 (ANN) 因其泛化能力和计算效率而成为近似 IK 解决方案的有前途的替代方案。这种方法基本上只训练末端执行器的几个样本，记录这些样本是为了解决 IK 问题。然而，一个基本问题仍然存在：多少训练样本足以实现可靠且准确的 IK 预测？本研究研究了将训练数据集的大小与基于 ANN 的 IK 求解器的准确性联系起来的数学框架。使用铰接式机器人操纵器，我们生成不同数量的关节位置对来训练前馈神经网络并评估其准确性、收敛性和泛化能力。结果表明，与处理采样大小上的近似精度的可比度量相比，超过 125 个的训练样本对模型效率的提高没有贡献，从而为数据效率提供了有价值的见解。这项工作为优化 ANN 解决方案的数据大小、平衡现实机器人应用的计算成本和模型准确性提供了实用指导。Inverse Kinematics (IK) plays a critical role in robotic motion planning and control. The IK solutions of a robot manipulator could be done by conventional ways such as geometric, algebraic, or Jacobian methods, which have drawbacks. The Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) have become a promising alternative for approximating IK solutions due to their generalization ability and computational efficiency. This approach basically trains only a few samples of the end effector that are recorded for the solution of the IK problem. However, a fundamental question remains: how many training samples are sufficient to achieve reliable and accurate IK predictions? This study investigates the mathematical framework of relating the size of training datasets and the accuracy of ANN-based IK solvers. Using an articulated robotic manipulator, we generate varying amounts of joint–position pairs to train feedforward neural networks and assess their accuracy, convergence, and generalization capability. The results reveal more training samples than 125 did not contribute to the improvement of the model efficiency that the comparable measure dealing with the approximation accuracy over the sampling size, offering valuable insight into data efficiency. This work provides practical guidance for optimizing the data sizing of ANN solutions, balancing computational cost and model accuracy for real-world robotic applications.

1 简介Introduction

在使用人工神经网络（ANN）的函数逼近领域，训练样本的选择和数量对于确定学习模型的准确性和泛化能力起着重要作用。作为通用函数逼近器，人工神经网络本质上是数据驱动的。这些网络捕获系统内潜在非线性关系的能力从根本上受到训练数据集的质量、分布和密度的限制。训练样本已被证明会在优化过程中影响人工神经网络的两个关键方面。首先，它们影响人工神经网络的收敛行为。其次，它们影响近似误差，即真实函数与模型预测之间的差异。训练数据不足或分布不良可能会导致输入空间中代表性不足的区域出现较高的局部错误。这可能会导致模型推断效果不佳或过度拟合噪声模式。相反，过大的数据集可能会导致不必要的计算支出，如果不精心设计，还可能会出现冗余或不平衡。如果要开发高效且准确的人工神经网络模型，就必须理解训练样本的复杂性与近似误差发生之间的相互关系。In the field of function approximation using artificial neural networks (ANNs), the selection and quantity of training samples play an important role in determin-ing the accuracy and generalization capability of the learned model. As universal function approximators, ANNs are inherently data-driven. The ability of these networks to capture the underlying nonlinear relationships within a system is fundamentally limited by the quality, distribution and density of the training dataset. The training samples have been shown to influence two key aspects of ANNs during the optimization process. First, they impact the convergence behavior of the ANNs. Second, they affect the approximation error, which is the discrepancy between the true function and the model’s prediction. Insufficient or poorly distributed training data can lead to high local errors in regions of the input space that are underrepresented. This can cause the model to extrapolate poorly or overfit to noisy patterns. In contrast, an excessively large dataset may result in unnecessary computational expenditure and may also exhibit redundancy or imbalance if it is not meticulously designed. It is imperative to comprehend the interrelation between the complexity of training samples and the occurrence of approximation error if efficient and accurate ANN models are to be developed.

在更广泛的人工智能研究中，特别是在大型语言模型（LLM）的开发中，也出现了对数据数量和效率的类似担忧。多年来，LLM 一直使用呈指数级增长的数据集进行训练，GPT-3 [1] 中达到 3000 亿个令牌，Chinchilla [2] 中达到 1.4 万亿个令牌，GPT-4 [3] 中达到 13 万亿个令牌，Llama 3 [4] 中超过 15 万亿个令牌。然而，研究人员预计，由于新鲜文本数据的可用性有限，这一趋势将趋于稳定。正如[5]中指出的，人工智能研究正在转向利用丰富的合成数据集（例如 Phi-3-Mini [6]）优化较小的模型，以保持性能，同时减少不必要的数据积累。这一趋势与基于 ANN 的逆向运动学 (IK) 建模面临的挑战直接相似，其中过多的训练样本可能会导致计算效率低下，而精度却没有实质性提高。Similar concerns about data quantity and efficiency arise in broader artificial intelligence research, particularly in the development of large language models (LLMs). Over the years, LLMs have been trained with exponentially increasing datasets, reaching 300 billion tokens in GPT-3 [1], 1.4 trillion tokens in Chinchilla [2], 13 trillion tokens in GPT-4 [3], and over 15 trillion tokens in Llama 3 [4]. However, researchers anticipate that this trend will plateau due to the finite availability of fresh textual data. As pointed out in [5], AI research is shifting toward optimizing smaller models with enriched synthetic datasets, such as Phi-3-Mini [6], to maintain performance while reducing unnecessary data accumulation. This trend directly parallels the challenges faced in ANN-based Inverse Kinematics (IK) modeling, where excessive training samples may lead to computational inefficiencies without substantial improvements in accuracy.

机器人机械臂IK的计算是运动控制中的一个基本问题。使用人工神经网络时，计算过程可以简化为三个主要步骤：训练、学习逆运动学函数以及获取末端执行器所需目标位置的关节角度。与需要显式数学建模的传统几何、代数或雅可比行列式方法不同，基于人工神经网络的方法避免了繁琐的参数校准和与奇点相关的问题[7-13]。传统的 IK 计算通常涉及 Denavit-Hartenberg (D-H) 参数，这些参数很难针对结构不一致（例如机械偏转或接头松动）进行调整。此外，传统方法可能导致工作路径效率低下，或者在某些奇点姿势下完全失败。The calculation of IK for robot manipulators is a fundamental problem in motion control. When using ANNs, the computation process can be streamlined into three primary steps: training, learning the inverse kinematics function, and obtaining joint angles for desired target locations of the end-effector. Unlike conventional geometric, algebraic, or Jacobian-based methods, which require explicit mathematical modeling, ANN-based approaches avoid cumbersome parameter calibration and issues related to singularities [7-13]. Traditional IK computations often involve Denavit-Hartenberg (D-H) parameters, which can be difficult to adjust for structural inconsistencies, such as mechanical deflections or joint looseness. Furthermore, conventional methods may lead to inefficient working paths or fail entirely in certain singularity postures.

在基于 ANN 的 IK 解决方案中，之前的研究表明机器人系统可以直接通过 ANN 学习其 IK 特征，而不需要明确的数学公式 [14]。一些方法将关节角度配置的反馈纳入人工神经网络学习过程[15]，使机器人能够自适应地提高其性能。训练样本的数量和质量直接影响近似误差，其中包括偏差（由于模型复杂性不足）、方差（由于对训练数据的敏感性）和不可约噪声。在监督学习的背景下，人工神经网络的泛化性能不仅取决于网络架构和优化算法，而且还取决于训练数据集的代表性和密度[9]。人们提出了不同的技术来提高训练数据获取的效率，例如主动学习、任务空间中的均匀网格采样和误差驱动的样本细化[11]，其目的是让人工神经网络接触到更多样化和更具代表性的示例集。In ANN-based IK solutions, previous studies have demonstrated that robotic systems can learn their IK characteristics directly via ANNs without requiring explicit mathematical formulations [14]. Some approaches incorporate feedback from joint angular configurations into the ANN learning process [15], allowing robots to adaptively improve their performance. The number and quality of training samples directly affect the approximation error, which consists of bias (due to insufficient model complexity), variance (due to sensitivity to training data), and irreducible noise. In the context of supervised learning, the generalization performance of an ANN hinges not only on the network architecture and optimization algorithm but also critically on the representativeness and density of the training dataset [9]. Different techniques have been proposed to improve the efficiency of training data acquisition, such as active learning, uniform grid sampling in task space, and error-driven sample refinement [11], which aim to expose the ANN to a more diverse and representative set of examples.

虽然文献中的许多作品都采用了相对大量的训练样本，但研究的样本量差异很大。 Duka [12] 使用 1,000 个随机生成的关节角度值集和 100 个隐藏神经元。然而，确定训练样本的最佳数量仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。一些研究人员提出整合不确定性估计技术（例如贝叶斯神经网络或集成模型）来识别低置信度预测并指导自适应采样[16]。此外，过多的数据可能导致计算效率低下或过度拟合，而采样不足可能导致泛化能力差和运动控制不可靠。在[17]中，作者试图回答样本数量上的边界问题，但不是一般问题设置。阿尔沃谢尔等。 al [18] 认为，使用 ANN 时不知道什么样本量要求是合适的。梅赫罗特拉等。 al[19]解决了隐藏层节点数与有效学习所需样本数之间的关系，但没有解决IK等近似问题。因此，平衡样本量和模型效率之间的权衡对于基于 ANN 的 IK 建模至关重要。While many works in the literature have adopted relatively large volumes of training samples, studies vary significantly in their sample sizes. Duka [12] used 1,000 randomly generated sets of joint angle values with 100 hidden neurons. However, determining the optimal number of training samples remains an open question. Some researchers have proposed integrating uncertainty estimation techniques—such as Bayesian neural networks or ensemble models—to identify low-confidence predictions and guide adaptive sampling [16]. Furthermore, excessive data may lead to computational inefficiencies or overfitting, while inadequate sampling may result in poor generalization and unreliable motion control. In [17], the author tried to answer for the bounding problem on the number of samples, but it was not on the general problem setting. Alwosheel et. al [18] argued that it is unknown what sample size requirements are appropriate when using ANN. Mehrotra et. al [19] addressed the relationship between the number of hidden layer nodes and the number of samples needed for effective learning, but not for the approximation such as IK. Thus, balancing the trade-off between sample size and model efficiency is critical in ANN-based IK modeling.

本研究旨在研究基于 ANN 的 IK 近似中训练样本大小与模型精度之间的关系。通过研究统计学习原理、近似理论和机器人特定的命令跟随设置，本研究旨在开发一种泛化方法，以便确保 ANN 模型的效率。This study aims to investigate the relationship between training sample size and model accuracy in ANN-based IK approximation. By examining statistical learning principles, approximation theory, and robotics-specific command-follow setting, this research seeks to develop a generalization methodology so that one can secure the ANN model efficiency.

6 结论Conclusions

本研究调查了 ANN 在机器人机械手操作 IK 分析中的应用。 ANN 的使用使得无需几何信息即可利用 IK 计算，但仅选择有限数量的训练样本点。 ANN 负责 IK 功能，该功能是根据记录的末端执行器位置和相应的关节角度得出的。然而，过多的样本点是不切实际的，因此，该研究探讨了训练样本与模型精度的数学关系。提供了一个框架，用于检查 ANN 的近似函数对于不同输入变量相对于样本数范围的变化程度。数学推导可用于 ANN 方法的实际实施。The present study investigates the application of ANNs in the context of IK analysis for robot manipulator operation. The employment of ANNs rendered the utilization of IK calculations with no geometric information needed, but with only a limited number of training sample points being selected. ANN is responsible for the IK function, which is derived from the recorded locations of the end effector and the corresponding joint angles. However, an excess of sample points is impractical, and consequently, the study explores the mathematical relation of the training samples to the model accuracy. A framework was provided for the purpose of examining the extent to which the approximation function of the ANN can vary for different input variables, in relation to the bounds of the sample number. The mathematical derivations can be for the practical implementation of the ANN methodology.

为了确定该方法的有效性，设置了一个三自由度绘图操纵器，并通过人工神经网络求解其 IK。然而，必须确定足够数量的训练样本以成功近似，同时确定所需的最小数量。研究重点是样本数量与跟踪性能之间的关系，随后将人工神经网络产生的跟踪误差与通过估计方法获得的结果进行比较。因此，每个 DOF 选择四个样本（20 毫米长的采样分辨率，即样本之间的距离）产生了令人满意的近似值，精度约为 2.73 毫米。当样本数量为 125 或更少时，理论误差界限提供可靠的误差估计。给定误差/间距比为 0.14，可容纳的最佳样本数量为 64，同时允许的距离误差为 2.73 mm。可以得出的结论是，为了满足执行中所需的准确性水平，应该确定更大数量的训练样本，同时牺牲更大数据量的负担。In order to ascertain the validity of the proposed methodology, a 3 DOF drawing manipulator was set, and its IKs were solved by ANN. However, it was imperative to ascertain a sufficient number of training samples for the successful approximation, whilst identifying a minimum required number. The investigation focused on the relationship between the number of samples and the tracking performance, with a subsequent comparison of the tracking errors generated by the ANN with the results obtained through estimation methods. Consequently, the selection of four samples per DOF (20 mm long sampling resolution, i.e. the distance between samples) yielded a satisfactory approximation, exhibiting an accuracy of approximately 2.73 mm. The theoretical error bound provides a reliable estimate of the error when the number of samples is 125 or less. Given the error/spacing ratio of 0.14, the optimal number of samples that can be accommodated is found to be 64, in conjunction with an allowable distance error of 2.73 mm. It can be concluded that, in order to satisfy the requisite level of accuracy in the execution, the larger number of training samples should be determined with some sacrifice in the burden of bigger data size.

本文通过模拟案例研究提供了获得 ANN 位移界限所需的数学框架。该估计可用于选择训练样本的数量。虽然出于清晰度和易处理性的目的，本研究集中于 3-DOF 操纵器和固定的 ANN 架构，但应该注意的是，所提出的数学框架绝不限于这种配置。错误边界引理与体系结构无关，可以扩展到更深的网络或替代激活函数。此外，本研究提出的采样效率指标适用于更高自由度的机械臂，其中输入空间的维数增加了训练数据密度的重要性。后续研究将调查该框架在各种机器人平台和学习架构中的适用性，目的是验证其更广泛的相关性。The present paper furnished the mathematical framework necessary to obtain the ANN displacement bound by means of a simulation case study. The estimate can be used to select the number of training samples. Whilst the present study concentrates on a 3-DOF manipulator and a fixed ANN architecture for the purposes of clarity and tractability, it should be noted that the proposed mathematical framework is by no means limited to this configuration. The error bounding lemmas are architecture-independent and can be extended to deeper networks or alternative activation functions. Furthermore, the sampling efficiency metric proposed in this study is applicable to higher-DOF manipulators, where the dimensionality of the input space increases the importance of training data density. Subsequent research will investigate the applicability of this framework across various robotic platforms and learning architectures, with the objective of validating its broader relevance.
