1. 简介Introduction

自动事实检查，即评估索赔真实性的过程，可以防止错误信息Automated fact checking, the process of assessing the veracity of claims, acts as a safeguard against misinformation(Guo et al., 2022b; Zeng et al., 2021)。 事实检查系统不仅要预测声明真实性标签，还要从文本文档和知识图等外部知识源检索和提供支持证据. Fact-checking systems are expected not only to predict claim veracity labels, but also to retrieve and present supporting evidence from external knowledge sources such as textual documents and knowledge graphs(Guo et al., 2022a; Zeng et al., 2021)。 这些证据有助于解释算法决策，这在公共卫生或法律等高风险领域尤其重要，并支持将事实核查系统集成到人类决策工作流程中. This evidence helps to explain algorithm decisions, which is particularly important in high-stakes domains, like public health or law, and supports the integration of fact-checking systems into human decision-making workflows(Vladika and Matthes, 2023).

传统的事实核查方法涉及两个阶段的方法：（1）从检索者排序的集合中识别证据The traditional approach to fact-checking involves a two-stage approach: (1) identification of evidence from a collection ranked by retrievers(Chen et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2021; Hanselowski et al., 2019; Hu et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2024; Deng et al., 2025a; Luken et al., 2018; Nie et al., 2019)接下来是（2）准确性预测followed by (2) veracity prediction(Vlachos and Riedel, 2014; Guo et al., 2022a; Zeng et al., 2021)。 最近，大型语言模型（LLM）通过应用检索增强生成（RAG）架构展示了强大的事实检查性能. More recently, Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated strong fact-checking performance through application of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures(Lewis et al., 2020)在准确性预测之前，检索到的文档被用作附加上下文in which retrieved documents are used as additional context prior to veracity prediction(Schlichtkrull et al., 2024; Akhtar et al., 2025; Pan et al., 2023; Lewis et al., 2020).

然而，在 RAG 设置中，法学硕士在评估索赔时可以利用检索到的证据和记忆的知识，从而很难确定其预测的来源。由于参数知识可能会过时However, in RAG settings, LLMs can make use of both retrieved evidence and memorised knowledge when assessing claims, making it difficult to determine the source of their predictions. Since parametric knowledge can become outdated(ChenghaoZhu et al., 2025)或反映培训期间引入的不准确之处or reflect inaccuracies introduced during training(Das et al., 2025)，对此类知识的依赖可能会影响事实核查决策的可靠性, reliance on such knowledge may affect the reliability of fact-checking decisions(Vladika et al., 2025; Kassem et al., 2025; Deng et al., 2025b; Vladika and Matthes, 2024; Deng, 2026)。 出色的表现可能部分反映了之前对预训练数据中包含的相关事实的了解，从而很难确定预测是否主要由检索到的证据或记忆的知识支持. Strong performance may partly reflect prior exposure to relevant facts included in pretraining data, making it difficult to determine whether predictions are primarily supported by retrieved evidence or memorised knowledge(Wei et al., 2025; Di Palma et al., 2025; Carlini et al., 2021)。 因此，仅凭优异的验证准确性并不能揭示验证者模型是否真正依赖于所提供的证据，还是仅仅恢复记忆的知识。. Consequently, superior verification accuracy alone cannot reveal whether a verifier model truly depends on the provided evidence or merely recovers memorised knowledge.

5. 真实REAL

使用 FAE，我们观察到当前基于 LLM 的验证者即使在支持证据被消除后也经常维持其原始预测，如图所示Using FAE, we observe that current LLM-based verifiers often maintain their original predictions even after the supporting evidence has been ablated, as illustrated in Figure3。这种行为表明验证决策并不完全基于检索到的证据，而是部分通过预训练期间获得的参数知识来维持。这种行为破坏了依赖证据的验证，因为尽管支持环境发生变化，最终的预测仍然可以保持稳定。. Unlike standard supervised fine-tuning (SFT), which optimises prediction correctness only under evidence-complete conditions, REAL additionally supervises the verifier’s behaviour under evidence-ablated conditions where the supporting evidence has been intentionally removed. The key intuition is that a verifier should not only learn what prediction to make when sufficient evidence is available, but also when not to maintain the same prediction once the supporting evidence becomes unavailable.

5.1. 证据消融Evidence Ablation

先前基于法学硕士的事实检查工作通常使用仅由主张及其支持证据组成的对来微调模型Previous work on LLM-based fact-checking typically fine-tunes models using pairs consisting only of a claim and its supporting evidence(Shcharbakova et al., 2025; Cheung and Lam, 2023; Kumar et al., 2025; Putta et al., 2025; Yoon et al., 2024)。在这种范式下，验证者只接受证据完整的训练条件，其中所需的证据始终与正确的标签一起可用。因此，当支持证据不可用时，模型从未被明确训练来执行基于证据的验证。. Under this paradigm, the verifier is exposed exclusively to evidence-complete training conditions, where the required evidence is always available together with the correct label. Consequently, the model is never explicitly trained to perform evidence-grounded verification when supporting evidence becomes unavailable.

因此，验证者没有接受过在支持证据被删除时改变其预测的训练。在训练期间，只要在原始证据完整输入下预测保持正确，证据消融后维持原始预测就不会受到惩罚。因此，验证者可以学会通过参数知识来保留预测，而不是根据证据可用性来调整其行为。As a result, the verifier is not trained to change its prediction when supporting evidence is removed. During training, maintaining the original prediction after evidence ablation is never penalised as long as the prediction remains correct under the original evidence-complete input. Consequently, the verifier may learn to preserve predictions through parametric knowledge instead of adapting its behaviour according to evidence availability.

这种不完整性给证据消融的监督带来了严峻的挑战。正样本可能会省略有效的支持证据，而通过证据消融构建的负样本可能仍包含能够支持原始主张的未注释证据。因此，逻辑上有效的验证者预测可能在训练期间受到错误的惩罚，从而削弱了反事实监督信号的可靠性。This incompleteness introduces a critical challenge for evidence-ablated supervision. Consequently, logically valid verifier predictions may be incorrectly penalised during training, weakening the reliability of the counterfactual supervision signal.

By aggregating evidence predictions across multiple LLMs, this process reduces the likelihood of leaving valid supporting evidence inside ablated contexts. 8. 结论Conclusion

本文研究了基于法学硕士的自动事实核查的一个关键限制，即强大的验证准确性并不一定意味着基于证据的推理。通过事实消融评估（FAE），我们发现当前的法学硕士验证者即使在支持证据被删除后也经常保留他们的预测，这揭示了验证过程中对参数知识的严重依赖。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了严格的证据消融学习（REAL），这是一种训练框架，通过反事实证据监督和增强的证据注释，在完整证据和消融证据条件下明确监督验证者的行为。四个事实检查基准的实验表明，REAL 极大地改善了基于证据的行为，同时在域内和域外评估下保持强大的事实检查性能。我们的结果进一步表明，即使在支持证据被删除后，许多现有验证者仍能保持正确的预测，这表明仅标准事实核查准确性可能无法完全反映预测是否真正得到检索到的证据的支持。通过明确评估和监督证据消融下验证者的行为，这项工作为分析和改进基于法学硕士的事实检查系统中的证据依赖性提供了一个实用的框架。This paper investigates a critical limitation in LLM-based automated fact-checking that strong verification accuracy does not necessarily imply evidence-grounded reasoning. Through Fact Ablated Evaluation (FAE), we show that current LLM verifiers often preserve their predictions even after the supporting evidence has been removed, revealing substantial reliance on parametric knowledge during verification. To address this issue, we propose Rigorous Evidence Ablation Learning (REAL), a training framework that explicitly supervises verifier behaviour under both complete evidence and ablated evidence conditions through counterfactual evidence supervision and enhanced evidence annotation. Experiments across four fact-checking benchmarks demonstrate that REAL substantially improves evidence-grounded behaviour while maintaining strong fact-checking performance under both in-domain and out-of-domain evaluation. Our results further show that many existing verifiers can maintain correct predictions even after the supporting evidence has been removed, indicating that standard fact-checking accuracy alone may not fully reflect whether predictions are genuinely supported by the retrieved evidence. By explicitly evaluating and supervising verifier behaviour under evidence ablation, this work provides a practical framework for analysing and improving evidence dependency in LLM-based fact-checking systems.

局限性Limitations

FAE 需要迭代证据消融和重复验证者推理，这使得评估在计算上比标准单遍事实检查评估更加昂贵。 因此，FAE 目前适合作为分析证据依赖性的诊断框架，而不是作为轻量级的大规模评估协议。 此外，现有事实检查基准中的证据冗余和不完整注释可能仍然允许模型在删除“黄金”证据后保留正确的预测，从而难以将基于证据的验证与参数召回完美分开。 尽管 REAL 通过证据增强部分缓解了这个问题，但不能始终保证所有支持证据的完全隔离。FAE requires iterative evidence ablation and repeated verifier inference, making evaluation computationally more expensive than standard single-pass fact-checking evaluation. Consequently, FAE is currently suitable as a diagnostic framework for analysing evidence dependency rather than as a lightweight large-scale evaluation protocol. In addition, evidence redundancy and incomplete annotations in existing fact-checking benchmarks may still allow models to preserve correct predictions after ‘gold’ evidence removal, making it difficult to perfectly separate evidence-grounded verification from parametric recall. Although REAL partially mitigates this issue through evidence enhancement, complete isolation of all supporting evidence cannot always be guaranteed.
