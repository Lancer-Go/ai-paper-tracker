你确定你确定吗？论教学调整对置信度和词汇多样性的影响Are You Sure You’re Sure? On the Impact of Instruction Tuning on Confidence and Lexical Diversity

摘要Abstract

经过指令调整的语言模型在一系列生成任务中取得了出色的性能，但最近也被证明表现出言语上的过度自信。在回答问题时，言语模型的过度自信可能与生成的支持理由的一致性有关。 在本文中，我们研究了生成的答案基本原理的词汇多样性的相应变化是否伴随着指令调整引起的模型置信度的变化。 我们跨问答基准评估了三个匹配的基础模型和指令调整模型，发现指令调整一致地改变了答案置信度，尽管预测准确性的变化有限并且基于可能性的校准有所减少。其次，我们观察到指令调整对基本原理多样性的非均匀影响：跨基本原理多样性持续下降，而表面层面的词汇多样性在不同模型和基准的方向和幅度上都存在变化。最后，我们发现在控制答案选择和基本原理长度后，这些差异仍然存在，证实置信度和基本原理多样性捕获了指令调整的不同影响。Instruction-tuned language models achieve strong performance across a range of generation tasks, but have also recently been shown to exhibit verbalized overconfidence. In question answering, verbalized model overconfidence may be associated with the consistency of the generated supporting rationales. In this paper, we study whether corresponding changes in the lexical diversity of generated answer rationales accompany changes in model confidence induced by instruction tuning. We evaluate three matched base and instruction-tuned models across question-answering benchmarks and find that instruction tuning consistently alters answer confidence, despite limited changes in predictive accuracy and decreases in likelihood-based calibration. Secondly, we observe a non-uniform effect of instruction tuning on rationale diversity: cross-rationale diversity consistently decreases, whereas surface-level lexical diversity varies in both direction and magnitude across models and benchmarks. Finally, we find that these differences persist after controlling for answer selection and rationale length, confirming that confidence and rationale diversity capture distinct effects of instruction tuning.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地应用于医学、金融和法律领域的问答和推理任务，其中模型置信度的可靠估计尤为重要Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly applied in question answering and reasoning tasks in medicine, finance, and law, where reliable estimates of model confidence are particularly important(26; 11; 32; 7)。 先前的工作表明，训练基础模型以遵循自然语言指令可以提高此类应用中的模型性能. Prior work has shown that training base models to follow natural-language instructions can improve model performance in such applications(31; 21)。 同时，训练后被证明可以改变模型置信分布. At the same time, post-training was shown to alter model confidence distributions(15; 29; 34).

然而，可以使用多个代理来估计模型置信度，包括分配给所选答案相对于替代答案的概率、重复代之间的一致性以及明确语言化的置信度Model confidence, however, can be estimated using several proxies, including the probability assigned to the selected answer relative to alternative answers, consistency across repeated generations, and explicitly verbalized confidence(15; 29; 34)。 在自由形式生成中，不确定性估计因生成的响应的表面形式的变化而变得更加复杂，因为语义上等效的答案可以使用实质上不同的词汇序列来表达. In free-form generation, uncertainty estimation is further complicated by variation in the surface form of generated responses, since semantically equivalent answers can be expressed using substantially different lexical sequences(16; 6)。 虽然最近的几项研究调查了指令调整和基于偏好的后训练如何影响模型置信度. While several recent studies investigate how instruction tuning and preference-based post-training affect model confidence(36; 13)据我们所知，先前的工作尚未检验置信度的变化是否伴随着生成的答案理由的词汇多样性的相应变化，这为所选答案提供了支持证据。, to the best of our knowledge, prior work has not examined whether changes in confidence are accompanied by corresponding shifts in the lexical diversity of generated answer rationales, which provide supporting evidence for the selected answer.

在这项工作中，我们研究指令调整如何影响基于可能性的答案不确定性和语言置信度，以及基础模型和指令调整模型之间的差异是否与模型基本原理的词汇多样性的相应变化相关。In this work, we investigate how instruction tuning affects likelihood-based answer uncertainty and verbalized confidence, and whether the differences between base and instruction-tuned models are associated with corresponding changes in the lexical diversity of model rationales.

我们的贡献如下：1）我们对三个模型系列和三个推理基准的基础模型和指令调整模型进行配对评估，比较置信度、校准和原理多样性； 2）我们执行仅限于基础模型和指令调整模型选择相同答案的示例的受控比较，同时匹配变体之间的基本原理长度，使我们能够独立于答案切换和生成长度来测量词汇多样性的变化； 3）我们表明，指令调整提高了模型置信度，但预测准确性没有相应提高，而词汇多样性的变化仍然是异质的，并且与不确定性或校准并不一致相关。 总之，我们的研究结果表明，指令调整后信心的增加与基本原理多样性或模型校准的统一转变无关。Our contributions are as follows: 1) we conduct a paired evaluation of base and instruction-tuned models across three model families and three reasoning benchmarks, comparing confidence, calibration, and rationale diversity; 2) we perform controlled comparisons restricted to examples for which base and instruction-tuned models select the same answer, while matching rationale length across variants, allowing us to measure changes in lexical diversity independently of answer switching and generation length; and 3) we show that instruction tuning increases model confidence without corresponding improvements in predictive accuracy, while changes in lexical diversity remain heterogeneous and are not consistently associated with uncertainty or calibration. Together, our findings show that increased confidence after instruction tuning is not associated with a uniform shift in either rationale diversity or model calibration.

2 相关工作Related Work

校准衡量模型的预测置信度与正确的经验概率的一致性程度Calibration measures how well a model’s predicted confidence aligns with the empirical probability of being correct(9; 14; 8)。 多项实证研究表明，模型置信度可能与预测准确性不一致，基于可能性的估计会出现置信度过高或过低的情况. Several empirical studies have shown that model confidence can be misaligned with predictive accuracy, with over- and under-confidence observed for likelihood-based estimates(15), 言语自信, verbalized confidence(29)和基于发电的不确定性测量, and generation-based uncertainty measures(34)。 最近的工作表明，训练后可以进一步改变模型置信度分布，即使下游任务性能有所提高，通过从人类反馈中学习等对齐方法也会影响言语置信度. Recent work has shown that post-training can further alter model confidence distributions, with alignment methods such as learning from human feedback affecting verbalized confidence even when downstream task performance improves(37; 29; 36).

另一项工作研究指令调整和其他后期培训对开放式生成中语言多样性的影响Another line of work studies the impact of instruction tuning and other post-training on linguistic diversity in open-ended generation(10; 35; 5)。 进一步的工作将词汇、句法和语义多样性作为模型输出中创造力的指标，将生成的输出与人类编写的文本或人类对创造力的判断进行比较. Further works examine lexical, syntactic, and semantic diversity as indicators of creativity in model outputs, comparing the generated outputs with human-written text or human judgments of creativity(30; 2; 22; 23).

据我们所知，尽管在输出多样性、模型置信度和校准方面进行了大量工作，但词汇或标记级多样性与语言置信度或校准之间的关系尚未得到研究。To the best of our knowledge, despite a substantial body of work on output diversity, model confidence, and calibration, the relationship between lexical or token-level diversity and verbalized confidence or calibration has not yet been investigated.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们研究了指令调整对模型置信度以及问答任务中生成的基本原理的词汇多样性的影响。 通过对基本原理多样性的分析，我们发现指令调整后交叉基本原理的变异性降低，而表面层面的词汇多样性表现出依赖于基准的模式。我们进一步表明，答案不确定性的减少并不总是伴随着词汇多样性的减少，并且多样性的变化并不总是反映言语置信度、答案选择或模型校准的变化。此外，在控制答案选择和基本原理长度后，相同的模式仍然存在。In this paper, we investigate the impact of instruction tuning on model confidence and the lexical diversity of generated rationales in question answering tasks. Through our analysis of rationale diversity, we find that cross-rationale variability decreases after instruction tuning, whereas surface-level lexical diversity exhibits benchmark-dependent patterns. We further show that decreases in answer uncertainty are not consistently accompanied by decreases in lexical diversity, and that diversity changes do not consistently reflect changes in verbalized confidence, answer selection, or model calibration. Moreover, the same patterns persist after controlling for answer selection and rationale length.

总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，指令调整对置信度和基本原理多样性的影响不同，激发了未来对不确定性估计的研究，共同考虑预测置信度和生成基本原理的变化。未来的工作可以进一步研究减少训练后的过度自信是否会以进一步减少理由多样性为代价，以及观察到的不确定性多样性模式是否超越词汇变异扩展到语义多样性。Overall, our findings show that instruction tuning affects confidence and rationale diversity differently, motivating future work on uncertainty estimation that jointly considers predictive confidence and variation in generated rationales. Future work could further investigate whether reducing post-training overconfidence comes at the cost of further reducing rationale diversity, and whether the observed uncertainty-diversity patterns extend beyond lexical variation to semantic diversity.

道德考虑Ethical Considerations

我们尝试公开可用的模型和基准数据集，并遵守相应资源的预期用途和许可条款。We experiment with publicly available models and benchmark datasets and adhere to the intended use and licensing terms of the respective resources.

然而，我们的结果强调了与解释指令调整语言模型的置信度相关的潜在风险。在评估的模型和基准中，我们发现指令调整增加了模型的置信度并减少了交叉原理的多样性，而预测准确性却没有相应的提高。 在下游应用中，特别是在高风险环境中，当模型置信度被解释为预测可靠的证据时，这种不匹配可能会鼓励对错误预测的无根据依赖。 基于可能性的置信度和言语置信度获得的不同校准结果进一步表明，应使用多种互补措施而不是单一代理来评估置信度，从而减少不适当依赖错误校准预测的风险。Our results nevertheless highlight potential risks associated with interpreting the confidence of instruction-tuned language models. Across the evaluated models and benchmarks, we find that instruction tuning increases model confidence and reduces cross-rationale diversity without a corresponding improvement in predictive accuracy. In downstream applications, particularly in high-stakes settings, this mismatch may encourage unwarranted reliance on incorrect predictions when model confidence is interpreted as evidence that the prediction is reliable. The differing calibration results obtained for likelihood-based and verbalized confidence further suggest that confidence should be assessed using multiple complementary measures rather than a single proxy, reducing the risk of inappropriate reliance on miscalibrated predictions.

同样，交叉推理多样性的减少可能会限制重复生成提供有关模型预测的独立证据的程度。因此，在生成环境中，应该进一步评估这种原理多样性转变的影响，包括与人类生成的原理和人类判断进行比较。Similarly, reduced cross-rationale diversity may limit the extent to which repeated generations provide independent evidence about a model’s prediction. In generative settings, the implications of such shifts in rationale diversity should therefore be evaluated further, including comparisons against human-generated rationales and human judgments.

最后，我们不会评估涉及不安全数据或人口统计敏感属性的问题上的指令调整模型性能。 因此，我们的研究结果不应被解释为指令调整会增加此类问题的有害或有偏见的输出的证据。 观察到的置信度和多样性模式是否扩展到安全敏感或人口统计敏感的提示需要单独评估。Finally, we do not evaluate instruction-tuned model performance on questions involving unsafe data or demographic-sensitive attributes. Consequently, our findings should not be interpreted as evidence that instruction tuning increases harmful or biased outputs for such questions. Whether the observed confidence and diversity patterns extend to safety-sensitive or demographic-sensitive prompts requires separate evaluation.
