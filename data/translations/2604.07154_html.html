摘要Abstract

We decompose Prostate-Specific Membrane Antigen (PSMA) PET uptake into an MRI-explainable physiological envelope and an orthogonal residual reflecting signal components not expressible within the MRI feature manifold. 1 简介Introduction

多模态成像分析越来越多地结合来自不同模态的数据，但共享数据和特定模态数据之间的结构关系仍未得到充分定义。现有方法通常优化跨模态预测或联合表示，而没有形式化可恢复和不可约信号分量之间的边界。这个边界具有实际意义：它决定了一种模态中可以从另一种模态中推断出来的部分，并为合​​理的获取策略提供信息。Multimodal imaging analysis increasingly combines data from distinct modalities, yet the structural relationship between what is shared and what is modality-specific remains insufficiently defined. Existing approaches often optimise cross-modal prediction or joint representations without formalising the boundary between recoverable and irreducible signal components. This boundary has practical implications: it determines the portion of one modality that can be inferred from another and informs rational acquisition strategies.

2 相关工作Related Work

通过跨模态合成、联合表示学习和诊断互补性的实证研究，多模态医学成像已得到广泛应用。在跨模态合成中，卷积模型、对抗模型以及最近基于扩散的模型已被用于将 MRI 转换为 PET，以减少辐射暴露、解决丢失的采集问题或为下游任务生成伪 PET 参考Multimodal medical imaging has been widely approached through cross-modal synthesis, joint representation learning, and empirical studies of diagnostic complementarity. In cross-modal synthesis, convolutional, adversarial, and more recently diffusion-based models have been used to translate MRI to PET in order to reduce radiation exposure, address missing acquisitions, or generate pseudo-PET references for downstream tasks[18, 2, 14]。这些方法表明，实质性 PET 结构可以通过 MRI 特征进行预测，但它们通常会优化图像相似性或任务性能，而不是定义 PET 信号的哪些部分基本上无法从 MRI 中恢复。与此同时，多模态表示学习侧重于分离潜在空间中的共享因素和模态特定因素，以提高鲁棒性和可解释性. These approaches demonstrate that substantial PET structure is predictable from MRI features, yet they typically optimise image similarity or task performance rather than defining what fraction of PET signal is fundamentally irrecoverable from MRI. In parallel, multimodal representation learning has focused on separating shared and modality-specific factors in latent space to improve robustness and interpretability[1, 5, 17]。域分离网络和相关的生成模型将表示分解为共享和私有组件，在保留特定于模态的内容的同时鼓励不变性。然而，这种分解通常是在学习的潜在空间中实现的，与观察到的信号场本身没有明确的几何关系. Domain separation networks and related generative models decompose representations into shared and private components, encouraging invariance while preserving modality-specific content. However, such decompositions are usually implemented in learned latent spaces without an explicit geometric relation to the observed signal fields themselves[4, 8].

临床上，mpMRI 和 PSMA PET 被认为在前列腺癌中是互补的，MRI 捕获宏观组织结构、扩散和灌注，而 PSMA PET 提供与受体表达和细胞内过程相关的分子信息Clinically, mpMRI and PSMA PET are recognised as complementary in prostate cancer, with MRI capturing macroscopic tissue architecture, diffusion, and perfusion, and PSMA PET providing molecular information linked to receptor expression and intracellular processes[15, 6]。组合 PET-MRI 已被证明可以比单独使用任一模式提高诊断性能，但 MRI 可解释的生理学与 PET 特异性分子信号之间的强度界限仍然难以量化. Combined PET-MRI has been shown to improve diagnostic performance over either modality alone, yet the intensity-wise boundary between MRI-explainable physiology and PET-specific molecular signal remains poorly quantified[3]。 INR 最近已在医学成像中用于连续信号建模和动力学参数估计（包括动态 PET），突显了它们在表示高维生理功能方面的灵活性. INRs have recently been adopted in medical imaging for continuous signal modelling and kinetic parameter estimation, including dynamic PET, highlighting their flexibility in representing high-dimensional physiological functions[8, 13, 9].

3 材料和方法Materials and Methods

我们引入了由 MRI 特征矩阵的列空间定义的几何分解。通过 SVD 将 PET 预测残差投影到该跨度上并惩罚并行分量，我们将多模态融合重新构建为正交子空间分离。这产生了模态互补性的结构化测量，并定义了跨模态可恢复性的正式上限。We introduce a geometric decomposition defined by the column space of the MRI feature matrix. By projecting the PET prediction residual onto this span via SVD and penalizing the parallel component, we reframe multimodal fusion as orthogonal subspace separation. This yields a structured measure of modality complementarity and defines a formal ceiling on cross-modal recoverability.

5 讨论Discussion

这项研究将多模态积分形式化为子空间分离问题。通过在体素空间中定义 MRI 衍生特征的列空间并相应地投影 PET 信号，我们将摄取分解为可在该子空间内表达的分量和正交余数。这种分离提供了组织级生理变化和 MRI 特征空间中无法表示的 PET 信号分量之间的结构区别。This study formalizes multimodal integration as a problem of subspace separation. By defining the column space of MRI-derived features in voxel space and projecting the PET signal accordingly, we decompose uptake into a component expressible within this subspace and an orthogonal remainder. This separation provides a structural distinction between tissue-level physiological variation and PET signal components not representable within the MRI feature space.

Pure MRI-to-PET synthesis cannot recover molecular processes that lie outside the MRI feature subspace. Our formulation makes this boundary explicit; while structural sequences provide the anatomical framework, they cannot translate into molecular expression. Rather than learning a monolithic latent space, our framework enforces representational clarity aligned with imaging physics. By using a projection-based regularization, we ensure that the model does not hallucinate PET uptake from MRI noise, but instead separates the signal into distinct physical and biological components. From a theoretical perspective, this formulation connects multimodal imaging with classical projection theory and modern representation learning. It offers a rigorous lens through which to interpret world models in medical AI, where shared and unique information coexist. This framework is highly extensible and can be generalized to other modality pairs, such as CT-to-PET or mpMRI, to systematically map the boundaries of cross-modal information.

隔离 MRI 对齐的包膜可以通过识别 PET 摄取在 MRI 衍生特征中无法代表的区域来支持更有针对性地使用分子成像，从而优化诊断和临床工作流程。Isolating the MRI-aligned envelope may support more targeted use of molecular imaging by identifying regions where PET uptake is not representable within MRI-derived features, to optimise diagnostic and clinical workflows.
