摘要Abstract

欠采样 k 空间数据的 MRI 重建方法自然会利用复值测量。稀疏相位检索的并行发展表明，仅幅度测量可以为信号恢复提供补充信息。然而，由于缺乏无需额外扫描时间即可获得信息丰富的震级测量的实际设置，它们在 MRI 重建中的使用在很大程度上仍未得到探索。 在这项工作中，我们研究了辅助 k 空间幅度信息用于加速稳态动态 MRI 重建的用途，并证明了 k 空间幅度在不同时间范围内的强一致性。基于这一观察，我们建议MRI reconstruction methods for undersampled k-space data naturally utilize complex-valued measurements. Parallel developments in sparse phase retrieval have shown that magnitude-only measurements may provide complementary information for signal recovery. However, their use in MRI reconstruction remains largely unexplored, due to lack of practical settings where informative magnitude measurements can be obtained without additional scan time. In this work, we investigate the use of auxiliary k-space magnitude information for accelerated steady-state dynamic MRI reconstruction, and demonstrate strong consistency of k-space magnitudes across time-frames. Building on this observation, we proposeℂ+Mag\mathbb{C}+\text{Mag}，一种基于量级的物理驱动的深度学习重建方法。所提出的方法采用基于 ADMM 的展开框架，具有新颖的幅度感知数据保真度公式，其中引入二次平滑优化和基于动量的更新来解决幅度约束的不可微性和非凸性。回顾性欠采样电影 MRI 和相衬流 MRI 数据集以及前瞻性欠采样实时电影 MRI 采集的实验表明，与传统 PD-DL 方法相比，伪影抑制有所改善，解剖恢复更清晰，相位信息保存更好，这通过盲法专家读者评估得到了进一步支持。, a magnitude-informed physics-driven deep learning reconstruction method. The proposed method employs an ADMM-based unrolling framework with a novel magnitude-aware data-fidelity formulation, where quadratically smoothed optimization and momentum-based updates are introduced to address the non-differentiability and non-convexity of the magnitude constraints. Experiments on retrospectively undersampled cine MRI and phase-contrast flow MRI datasets, as well as prospectively undersampled real-time cine MRI acquisitions, demonstrate improved artifact suppression, sharper anatomical recovery, and better preservation of phase information compared to conventional PD-DL methods, which is further supported through blinded expert reader evaluations.

I 简介Introduction

减少 MRI 采集时间通常需要从高度欠采样的 k 空间数据中恢复图像，从而导致严重不适定逆问题。经典的压缩感知（CS）方法通过稀疏性促进正则化来应对这一挑战Reducing MRI acquisition time often requires recovering images from highly undersampled k-space data, resulting in a severely ill-posed inverse problem. Classical compressed sensing (CS) approaches address this challenge through sparsity-promoting regularization[1]，而最近的物理驱动深度学习（PD-DL）方法将采集物理与学习先验相结合，以显着提高重建质量, while recent physics-driven deep learning (PD-DL) methods combine acquisition physics with learned priors to achieve substantially improved reconstruction quality[2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7]。然而，高加速率下的重建性能仍然从根本上受到所获取信息量有限的限制，经常导致残留伪影、解剖模糊或精细结构细节的丢失。. Nevertheless, reconstruction performance at high acceleration rates remains fundamentally constrained by the limited amount of acquired information, frequently leading to residual artifacts, anatomical blurring, or loss of fine structural detail.

与此同时，计算成像界广泛研究了相位检索问题，其目标是从仅幅度测量中恢复信号In parallel, the computational imaging community has extensively studied phase retrieval problems, where the goal is to recover signals from magnitude-only measurements[8, 9, 10]。特别是，理论研究表明，在适当的条件下，两个仅幅度测量可以携带相当于稀疏恢复问题中一个复杂线性测量的信息. In particular, theoretical studies have shown that, under suitable conditions, two magnitude-only measurements can carry information equivalent to one complex linear measurement in sparse recovery problems[11]，促进将复数值和仅幅度测量相结合用于 MRI 重建, motivating the use of combined complex-valued and magnitude-only measurements for MRI reconstruction[12]。然而，尽管有这一理论上的承诺，辅助震级信息在 MRI 重建中的使用在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索. However, despite this theoretical promise, the use of auxiliary magnitude information in MRI reconstruction has remained largely underexplored[13]由于两个原因：i）缺乏实用的采集设置，可以在不增加扫描时间的情况下获得仅包含信息的幅度测量，ii）在这些设置中使用手工设计的正则化器的改进有限。due to two reasons: i) lack of practical acquisition settings where informative magnitude-only measurements can be obtained without increasing scan time, ii) limited improvement from using hand-designed regularizers in these settings.

VI 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个 PD-DL 框架，用于将辅助幅度 k 空间信息合并到 MRI 重建中。我们将稳态动态 MRI 确定为该框架的实际应用，其中可以从相邻时间帧获得丰富的辅助震级信息，而不会产生额外的采集成本。回顾性欠采样电影、相衬流 MRI 数据的实验结果In this work, we proposed a PD-DL framework for incorporating auxiliary magnitude k-space information into MRI reconstruction. We identified steady-state dynamic MRI as a practical application of this framework, where informative auxiliary magnitude information can be obtained from neighboring time-frames without incurring additional acquisition cost. Experimental results on retrospectively undersampled cine, phase-contrast flow MRI data atR∈{6,8}\mathrm{R}\in\{6,8\}以及前瞻性欠采样实时电影 MRI 数据，在图像质量和相位保持方面比传统的 PD-DL 重建方法有了显着的改进。, and prospectively undersampled real-time cine MRI data, demonstrated substantial improvements over conventional PD-DL reconstruction methods in both image quality and phase preservation.

最后，我们注意到这项工作的重点是引入Finally, we note that the focus of this work is to introduce a新的反问题公式new inverse problem formulation能够将辅助震级信息合并到 PD-DL MRI 重建中，特别是动态 MRI，而无需额外的采集成本。网络架构、正则化器和优化策略的进步是对我们提出的框架的补充，并且可以自然地集成到其中。事实上，我们的消融研究表明，我们提出的公式可以与不同的优化方案和网络架构协同结合。因此，不同网络架构的进一步比较，包括那些支持时空正则化的网络架构，对于这项工作并不重要。that enables incorporation of auxiliary magnitude information into PD-DL MRI reconstruction, in particular for dynamic MRI with no additional acquisition costs. Advances in network architectures, regularizers, and optimization strategies are complementary to our proposed framework and can be naturally integrated within it. Indeed, our ablation studies demonstrate that our proposed formulation can be synergistically combined with different optimization schemes and network architectures. Thus, further comparisons of different network architectures, including those that enable spatiotemporal regularization, are not critical for this work.
