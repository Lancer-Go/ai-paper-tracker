摘要Abstract

Mamba 等循环线性注意力模型 (RLA) 提供了高效的线性时间序列建模作为 Transformer 的替代方案，但其固定容量的循环状态限制了长序列建模。从人类分层记忆中汲取灵感，我们提出了分层记忆曼巴（HMM）来解决这一限制。 HMM 基于预先训练的 Mamba 主干网络构建，集成了轻量级工作记忆，可从嵌入主干网络隐藏状态的快速感觉记忆中提取慢速段落级语义 (PLS)。 PLS 随后被压缩到持久的长期记忆中，用于任务相关的检索。语义信息的分层处理克服了 RLA 的表示瓶颈，并通过参数学习赋予 HMM 跨任务泛化能力，这在其他长上下文增强型 Mamba 变体中是没有观察到的。对密钥检索和 LongBench-E 任务的评估表明，与基于 Mamba 的强大模型相比，HMM 将检索成功率提高了 34.3-37.1%，推理准确性提高了 1.6-14.2%，同时仅添加 2% 的额外参数，并且训练开销极小。Recurrent linear attention models (RLAs) such as Mamba offer efficient linear-time sequence modeling as an alternative to Transformers, yet their fixed‑capacity recurrent states limit long‑sequence modeling. Drawing inspiration from hierarchical human memory, we propose Hierarchical Memory Mamba (HMM) to address this limitation. Building upon a pre‑trained Mamba backbone, HMM integrates a lightweight working memory that extracts slow paragraph‑level semantics (PLS) from the fast sensory memory embedded in the backbone’s hidden states. The PLS is subsequently compressed into persistent long‑term memory for task‑relevant retrieval. The hierarchical processing of semantic information overcomes the representation bottleneck of RLAs and endows HMM cross‑task generalization through parametric learning, which is not observed in other long‑context enhanced Mamba variants. Evaluations on Passkey Retrieval and LongBench-E tasks demonstrate that HMM improves retrieval success by 34.3-37.1% and reasoning accuracy by 1.6-14.2% over strong Mamba‑based models, while adding only 2% extra parameters and with minimal training overhead.

5 方法Method

受分层人类记忆的启发，HMM 采用预先训练的 Mamba 模型作为令牌级表示的感觉记忆骨干。工作记忆被提出来从隐藏状态中提取 PLS，并进一步将它们编码为 LTM 以进行任务相关的检索。工作记忆的运作由两个核心过程组成：语义编码和语义检索。Inspired by hierarchical human memory, HMM adopts a pre-trained Mamba model as sensory memory backbone for token-level representation. A working memory is proposed to extract PLS from hidden states and further encode them into LTM for task-relevant retrieval. 附录一Appendix I局限性Limitations

虽然 HMM 改进了基于 Mamba 的架构的长上下文建模，但它并未专门针对需要对大量事实信息进行精确检索的任务（例如法律或监管分析）进行优化。在这种情况下，准确访问大量历史事实至关重要，当前的分段和检索机制可能不够，需要更先进的检索或索引策略。While HMM improves long-context modeling for Mamba-based architectures, it is not specifically optimized for tasks that require precise retrieval over large collections of factual information, such as legal or regulatory analysis. In such scenarios, where accurate access to extensive historical facts is critical, the current segmentation and retrieval mechanisms may be insufficient, and more advanced retrieval or indexing strategies are needed.

在超长上下文中，Mamba 等线性模型的主要瓶颈在于循环状态压缩引起的可访问性问题，尽管理论上保留了有用的历史信息，但变得难以检索。 PLS 通过在长范围内保留稳定的语义锚来缓解这个问题，从而提高有效的信息可访问性，而不会显着降低实践中的有用细节。然而，这种设计的重点是提高可访问性，而不是实现对大型事实语料库的细粒度或精确检索，这仍然是一个开放的挑战。In ultra-long contexts, the primary bottleneck of linear models such as Mamba lies in the accessibility problem caused by recurrent state compression, where useful historical information becomes difficult to retrieve despite being theoretically retained. The PLS mitigates this issue by preserving stable semantic anchors over long ranges, thereby improving effective information accessibility without significantly degrading useful details in practice. However, this design focuses on improving accessibility rather than enabling fine-grained or exact retrieval over large factual corpora, which remains an open challenge.

未来的工作可能集中在改进分割策略、增强检索模块以及扩展框架以更好地支持需要精确可靠的信息访问的任务。Future work may focus on improving the segmentation strategy, enhancing the retrieval module, and extending the framework to better support tasks that demand precise and reliable information access.
