5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 ESPO（错误结构化提示优化），这是一个将提示优化从进化搜索重塑为结构化统计估计的框架。通过将优化分解为三个原则阶段 - 结构化错误诊断、多策略候选生成和引导稳定性选择 - ESPO 解决了搜索范式的根本弱点：不完整的错误观察、有限的多样性和不可靠的选择。我们证明了像 GEPA 这样的现有进化方法是 ESPO 的退化特例，并提供了一个泛化界限，将每个阶段都建立在概率论和统计学的既定结果中。在七个公共基准测试中，ESPO 将平均准确度提高了We introduced ESPO (Error-Structured Prompt Optimization), a framework that recasts prompt optimization from evolutionary search to structured statistical estimation. By decomposing optimization into three principled phases - structured error diagnosis, multi-strategy candidate generation, and bootstrap stability selection - ESPO addresses the fundamental weaknesses of the search paradigm: incomplete error observation, limited diversity, and unreliable selection. We showed that existing evolutionary methods like GEPA are a degenerate special case of ESPO, and provided a generalization bound grounding each phase in established results from probability theory and statistics. On seven public benchmarks, ESPO improves average accuracy by++比最先进的技术高出 3.76%（GEPA 为 74.67% 与 70.91%），并且在每个数据集上均匹配或超过 GEPA，同时生成的提示短 47%（1,004 个字符与 1,878 个字符）且推理速度更快。对另外四个学生模型（Gemma 3 12B、Mistral 14B、Qwen3 32B、Claude Haiku 4.5）进行的跨模型实验证实，增益转移广泛，其中 Qwen3 GSM8K 上的差距最大（3.76% over the state-of-the-art (74.67% vs. 70.91% for GEPA) and matches or exceeds GEPA on every dataset, while producing prompts 47% shorter (1,004 vs. 1,878 chars) and faster at inference. E.2 偏差传播。Bias propagation.

根据静态基准提高准确性的即时优化可能会继承或放大 (1) 用于诊断的训练/验证示例、(2) 反映 LLM 的错误聚类以及 (3) 用于选择的指标中存在的任何偏差。 ESPO 的结构化诊断明确地显示了错误类别，这可以帮助审计，但它不能保证与偏差相关的故障模式能够被识别或纠正。我们建议下游用户在部署之前评估与偏见相关的切片的优化提示，特别是对于社会敏感的应用程序。Prompt optimization that drives accuracy up against a static benchmark may inherit or amplify any biases present in (1) the training/validation examples used for diagnosis, (2) the reflection LLM’s clustering of errors, and (3) the metrics used for selection. ESPO’s structured diagnosis surfaces error categories explicitly, which can aid auditing, but it does not guarantee that bias-related failure modes will be identified or corrected. We recommend that downstream users evaluate optimized prompts on bias-relevant slices before deployment, especially for socially sensitive applications.

E.3 基准污染。Benchmark contamination.

我们使用的几个公共基准（Tweet、MMLU、GSM8K、HotpotQA、ScoNe、HoVer、PUPA）可能与 LLM 预训练数据重叠。我们的实验设计在所有优化器（Default、Bootstrap、COPRO、MIPROv2、GEPA、ESPO）中保持这种污染风险恒定，因此我们报告的相对改进并不是由 ESPO 看到更多污染数据驱动的。绝对精度数字应根据每个基准的标准污染警告进行解释。Several of the public benchmarks we use (Tweet, MMLU, GSM8K, HotpotQA, ScoNe, HoVer, PUPA) may overlap with LLM pre-training data. Our experimental design holds this contamination risk constant across all optimizers (Default, Bootstrap, COPRO, MIPROv2, GEPA, ESPO), so the relative improvements we report are not driven by ESPO seeing more contaminated data. Absolute accuracy numbers should be interpreted with the standard contamination caveats for each benchmark.

E.4 双重用途的考虑。Dual-use considerations.

ESPO 中的技术是通用的提示优化工具，可以应用于我们未研究的目标，包括具有负面下游影响的目标（例如，优化提示以绕过安全过滤器）。我们发布了本文研究的公共 NLP 基准的代码和提示；我们不会发布旨在规避安全机制的提示或配置。The techniques in ESPO are general prompt-optimization tools and could be applied to objectives we did not study, including ones with negative downstream impact (e.g., optimizing prompts to bypass safety filters). We release code and prompts for the public NLP benchmarks studied in this paper; we do not release prompts or configurations targeted at evading safety mechanisms.
