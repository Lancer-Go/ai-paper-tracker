Recent work shows that GRPO can reduce the base model’s reasoning capacity and underperform it in Pass@kk当whenkk较大，表明推理路径的覆盖范围减少。我们发现这种减少与 GRPO 集中于基础模型已经以高概率生成的响应有关。我们将这种集中追溯到 GRPO 更新中的两个机制。在响应级别，高概率响应通过重复出现主导群体梯度。在代币层面，GRPO 的重要性比例呈梯度变化，进一步强化了在当前政策下更有可能出现的代币。我们建议is large, indicating reduced coverage of reasoning paths. We find that this reduction is associated with GRPO concentrating on responses that the base model already generates with high probability. We trace this concentration to two mechanisms in the GRPO update. At the response level, high-probability responses dominate the group gradient through repeated occurrence. At the token level, GRPO’s importance ratio scales gradients, further reinforcing tokens that become more likely under the current policy. We propose雷科ReCo，一种解决这两种影响的重新加权方法。响应贡献通过其在推出组中的预期发生进行标准化，并且令牌级别重要性比率被基于方差的比率所取代，该比率为非饱和决策点提供更大的更新规模，其中替代令牌选择仍然合理。在 Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B/7B 和 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 的五个数学推理基准测试中，ReCo 改进了 Pass@, a reweighting method that addresses both effects. 1 简介Introduction

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为改进 LLM 推理的标准训练后方法，特别是在数学和编码等可以自动验证正确性的领域Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has become a standard post-training approach for improving LLM reasoning, especially in domains such as mathematics and coding where correctness can be automatically verified(Jaech et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025)。在 RLVR 方法中，组相对策略优化（GRPO）已被广泛用于训练推理模型。它对每个提示的一组推出进行采样，并计算相对于组平均值的优势，从而在没有批评者的情况下提供稳定的策略梯度. Among RLVR methods, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) has been widely adopted for training reasoning models. It samples a group of rollouts for each prompt and computes advantages relative to the group mean, providing stable policy gradients without a critic(Shao et al., 2024).

尽管 GRPO 取得了实证上的成功，但它也有一些关键的局限性。虽然 GRPO 提高了采样效率，但据观察，它降低了基础模型原本拥有的推理能力和解决问题轨迹的多样性Despite its empirical success, GRPO has key limitations. While GRPO improves sampling efficiency, it has been observed to reduce the reasoning capacity and the diversity of problem-solving trajectories that the base model originally possessed(Yue et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2026; Li et al., 2025a)。 这种限制通常可以通过 Pass@ 观察到. This limitation is often observed through Pass@kk，其中 GRPO 在较小的值下优于基本模型, where GRPO outperforms the base model for small values ofkk但对于较大的值无法超过甚至低于它but fails to surpass or even falls below it for large values ofkk (Yue et al., 2025; Zhu et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025a)。虽然这种模式已被广泛报道，但目前尚不清楚 GRPO 训练下的哪些分布变化导致了这种限制。. While this pattern has been widely reported, it remains unclear what distributional change under GRPO training leads to this limitation.

为了了解 GRPO 如何导致这种集中，我们检查其更新的两个主要组成部分，（i）为每个推出组采样哪些响应以及（ii）如何计算令牌级别梯度。在响应级别，我们分析哪些响应出现在推出组中。响应是从策略本身中采样的，使得高概率响应在组内更频繁地发生。由于 GRPO 对采样响应的贡献进行平均，因此频繁响应通过重复出现积累了更大的总权重，导致梯度质量集中在它们上。在令牌级别，GRPO 重要性比缩放每个采样令牌的梯度贡献。随着采样令牌在当前策略下变得更有可能，其更新规模增加，进一步强化了决策点上相同的本地选择。通过训练，这种反馈可以使一种选择变得越来越占主导地位，从而减少未来推出的替代路径。这两种机制使已经可能的推理路径变得越来越占主导地位，从而减少了多样性。To understand how GRPO leads to this concentration, we examine two main components of its update, (i) which responses are sampled for each rollout group and (ii) how token-level gradients are computed. At the response level, we analyze which responses appear in the rollout group. Responses are sampled from the policy itself, making high-probability responses occur more often within a group. Because GRPO averages contributions across sampled responses equally, frequent responses accumulate larger total weight through repeated occurrence, causing the gradient mass to concentrate on them. At the token level, the GRPO importance ratio scales the gradient contribution of each sampled token. As the sampled token becomes more likely under the current policy, its update scale increases, further reinforcing the same local choice at a decision point. Over training, this feedback can make one choice increasingly dominant, reducing the alternative paths available to future rollouts. These two mechanisms make already likely reasoning paths increasingly dominant, reducing diversity.

为了缓解这个问题，我们建议To mitigate this issue, we propose根据分配集中度重新调整 GRPO 的权重Reweighting GRPO Against Distributional Concentration (雷科ReCo），以及相应的响应级别和令牌级别的更正。在响应级别，我们通过组内的预期计数对每个响应的梯度贡献进行归一化，从而防止频繁响应主导组级别梯度。在令牌级别，我们将标准令牌级别重要性比率替换为基于方差的比率，该比率同时考虑了采样令牌概率和可供选择的概率质量。这降低了饱和选择的权重，并为非饱和决策点提供了更大的更新规模，其中多个令牌选择仍然合理。总之，这些修正减少了 GRPO 崩溃到频繁推理路径上的倾向，并有助于保持推理多样性。), with corresponding response-level and token-level corrections. At the response level, we normalize each response’s gradient contribution by its expected count within the group, preventing frequent responses from dominating the group-level gradient. At the token level, we replace the standard token-level importance ratio with a variance-based ratio that accounts for both the sampled token probability and the probability mass left for alternatives. This downweights saturated choices and gives larger update scale to non-saturated decision points where multiple token choices remain plausible. Together, these corrections reduce GRPO’s tendency to collapse onto frequent reasoning paths and help preserve reasoning diversity.

3 方法Method

3.1 概述Overview

我们建议We propose根据分配集中度重新调整 GRPO 的权重Reweighting GRPO Against Distributional Concentration (雷科ReCo），一种重新加权方法，对 GRPO 更新的两个主要组成部分进行修正，（i）为每个推出组采样哪些响应，以及（ii）如何计算令牌级别梯度。), a reweighting method that applies corrections to the two main components of the GRPO update, (i) which responses are sampled for each rollout group, and (ii) how token-level gradients are computed.

在响应级别，ReCo 通过推出组内每个响应的预期计数来标准化每个响应的梯度贡献。 由于高概率响应的采样频率更高，因此这种归一化可以防止它们通过重复出现来控制组级梯度。At the response level, ReCo normalizes each response’s gradient contribution by its expected count within the rollout group. Since high-probability responses are sampled more often, this normalization prevents them from dominating the group-level gradient through repeated occurrence.

在代币级别，ReCo 取代了标准代币级别重要性 比率与基于方差的比率。该比率不仅考虑了样本 令牌概率也分配给剩余概率 替代代币。因此，ReCo 减少了饱和决策时的更新规模 点，并在替代代币选择仍然可行的情况下给予代币相对较大的权重。At the token level, ReCo replaces the standard token-level importance ratio with a variance-based ratio. This ratio accounts not only for the sampled token probability but also for the remaining probability assigned to alternative tokens. As a result, ReCo reduces update scale at saturated decision points and gives relatively larger weight to tokens where alternative token choices remain plausible.

3.2 响应级别计数重新加权Response-Level Count Reweighting

在 GRPO 中，响应不是从可能的解决方案空间中统一采样的，而是从旧策略本身中采样的。因此，旧策略下高概率的响应更有可能在组内多次出现。由于 GRPO 更新对采样响应进行平均，因此这种重复发生会给此类轨迹带来更大的总贡献。In GRPO, responses are not sampled uniformly from the space of possible solutions but from the old policy itself. As a result, responses with high probability under the old policy are more likely to appear multiple times within the group. Because the GRPO update averages over sampled responses, this repeated occurrence gives such trajectories a larger aggregate contribution.

ReCo 使用预期发生来降低可能重复出现的响应的权重。对于固定提示ReCo uses expected occurrence to downweight responses that are likely to appear repeatedly. For a fixed promptqq和一条轨迹and a trajectoryoo，我们将其在组中的出现次数定义为, we define its occurrence count over the group as

一种可能的方法是直接计算重复或语义等效的响应，并通过观察到的计数标准化它们的贡献。然而，这在开放式法学硕士生成中很困难，因为精确的重复轨迹很少见，并且定义等价性需要外部标准。 ReCo 通过使用预期计数而不是观察到的等价计数来避免这种显式分组问题。One possible approach would be to directly count duplicated or semantically equivalent responses and normalize their contributions by the observed count. However, this is difficult in open-ended LLM generation where exact duplicate trajectories are rare and defining equivalence requires an external criterion. ReCo avoids this explicit grouping problem by using expected counts rather than observed equivalence counts.
