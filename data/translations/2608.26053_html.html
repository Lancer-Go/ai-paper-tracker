Whether this mechanism can improve robotic manipulation remains unclear, where long-horizon tasks require tracking partial progress, reasoning about object relations, recovering from mistakes, and steering noisy low-level policies. In this paper, we study whether VLMs can be trained to reason directly in natural language to guide low-level manipulation policies. 1 简介Introduction

自然语言推理提供了一种有效的机制，可以在更困难的测试问题上花费更多的计算量，并为广泛的泛化提供数据高效的方法。这个食谱显然在许多领域都很有用，包括视觉感知Reasoning in natural language provides an effective mechanism for spending more compute on harder test problems, and offers a data-efficient recipe for broad generalization. This recipe is clearly useful in many domains including visual perception[17, 9]。更广泛地说，即使最终输出不是语言. More broadly, even when the final output is not language[15]，语言推理可以帮助模型分解问题，识别相关约束，并做出可靠的预测。因此，机器人操纵是基于推理的基础模型的自然领域：操纵需要解释场景、理解物理约束、预测动作对轨迹未来部分的影响，并根据这种理解采取行动。人们可能期望在行动之前训练机器人策略进行推理，从而允许模型在问题实例上进行测试时计算，从而提高泛化能力。, language reasoning can help a model decompose the problem, identify relevant constraints, and make reliable predictions. Robotic manipulation is therefore a natural domain for reasoning based foundation models: manipulation requires interpreting a scene, understanding physical constraints, anticipating the effect of action on future parts of the trajectory, and acting conditioned on this understanding. One might expect that training robotic policies to reason before acting would improve generalization by allowing the model to spend test-time compute on the problem instance.

机器人操纵的推理已经受到相当多的关注。最近的通用机器人政策和视觉语言动作模型，包括 ECoTReasoning for robotic manipulation has already received a fair bit of attention. Recent generalist robot policies and vision-language-action models, including ECoT[64], 转向VLA, SteerVLA[21]和摩尔莫法, and MolmoAct[32]，包含从以对象为中心的注释和简短计划到深度感知感知标记和图像空间轨迹的中间表示。补充方法通过语义接口公开通才策略：, incorporate intermediate representations ranging from object-centric annotations and short plans to depth-aware perception tokens and image-space trajectories. However, these approaches do not train free-form natural-language reasoning of the kind that has proven effective in language. Prior work shows that structured reasoning supervision can improve grounding and perception[8], 但这些收益似乎主要来自训练时监督：经过推理训练后，在测试时生成推理几乎没有带来额外的好处, but these gains appear to arise mainly from training-time supervision: after training with reasoning, generating reasoning at test time provides little additional benefit[8, 18]。因此，现有的工作建立了结构化 CoT 作为辅助训练信号，但灵活的语言推理是否可以作为一种花费测试时间计算进行操作的机制，以及如何在现实中学习做到这一点，还没有确定。. Thus, existing work establishes structured CoT as an auxiliary training signal, but leaves open whether flexible language reasoning can serve as a mechanism for spending test-time compute for manipulation, and how to learn to do that in reality.

我们研究如何训练视觉语言模型（VLM）以使用自由形式的自然语言作为用于机器人操作的测试时间计算的机制。我们的主要想法是将专家数据转化为VLM推理的监督。给定场景、交互历史和专家操作，我们对 VLM 进行后训练以产生基于语言的推理，使其能够得出与专家的语义相似的指令。然后，以语言为条件的低级策略接受该 VLM 的指令并输出直接控制机器人的动作。 该训练过程赋予 VLM 推理能力，使其能够在测试时生成用于引导低级策略的语言指导。为了实例化这个想法并研究其背后的关键设计选择，我们重点关注We study how to train vision-language models (VLMs) to use free-form natural language as a mechanism to spend test-time compute for robotic manipulation. Our main idea is to turn expert data into supervision for VLM reasoning. Given a scene, interaction history, and an expert action, we post-train a VLM to produce language-based reasoning that allows it to arrive at a semantically similar instruction to the expert’s. A language-conditioned low-level policy then takes this VLM’s instruction and outputs the action that directly controls the robot. 局限性。Limitations.首先，我们的实验是在两个模拟领域（语言表和双手杂货包装）中进行的，具有固定的低级语言条件策略。虽然这有助于我们进行系统研究，但将方法扩展到真实的机器人仍然是未来的重要工作。其次，第一阶段仍然依赖于专家生成的推理，第二阶段依赖于VLM判断来提供语义奖励。虽然这减少了多转弯机器人部署的需求，但它也优化了替代目标。将我们的方法扩展到多轮在线强化学习将进一步改善长期行为。First, our experiments are conducted in two simulated domains (Language Table and bimanual grocery packing) with a fixed low-level language-conditioned policy. While this helps us perform a systematic study, extending the approach to real robots remains important future work. Second, Stage I still relies on expert-generated reasoning, and Stage II relies on a VLM judge to provide semantic rewards. While this reduces the need for multi-turn robot rollouts, it also optimizes a surrogate objective. Extending our approach to multi-turn online RL will further improve long-horizon behaviors.

Second, future work could reduce the separation between the high-level reasoner and the low-level robot policy. While our hierarchical design isolates the effect of reasoning, it may introduce a mismatch between high-level intent and low-level execution. It would also be interesting to study whether reasoning can support not only high-level steering, but also the generation of low-level actions themselves. In addition, joint training introduces technical systems challenges around synchronizing high-level reasoning with low-level action, which will be important to address. Third, the RL stage could be extended beyond single-step offline training. Our current formulation avoids expensive online interaction by rewarding semantic agreement with expert instructions, but it optimizes a surrogate objective rather than final task success. Multi-turn RL with feedback from task completion, intermediate progress, recovery behavior, or human preferences may further improve long-horizon reasoning. Finally,ℛ3\mathcal{R}^{3}可以扩展以支持在线改进。推理 VLM 可以根据环境反馈或人工修正进行更新，而不是仅仅依赖批量离线训练。自由形式推理还可以提供一种自然的探索机制，使机器人能够将其行为的支持扩展到离线数据所表示的范围之外。虽然当今大多数机器人强化学习方法都专注于强化现有的低级策略，但推理可以实现更广泛的定性探索，支持对新实施例、新颖对象和训练期间未遇到的故障模式的稳健适应。could be extended to support online improvement. Rather than relying solely on batched offline training, the reasoning VLM could be updated from environment feedback or human corrections. Free-form reasoning may also provide a natural mechanism for exploration, allowing the robot to expand the support of its behavior beyond what is represented in the offline data. While most robot RL methods today focus on sharpening an existing low-level policy, reasoning could enable qualitatively broader exploration, supporting robust adaptation to new embodiments, novel objects, and failure modes not encountered during training.

视觉问答任务设计。Visual question answering task design.我们首先介绍我们设计的视觉问答（VQA）任务，用于评估不同模型的感知和面向行动的推理能力。我们在语言表上构建了 5 类 VQA 问题：We first introduce the visual question answering (VQA) task we design to evaluate the perceptual and action-oriented reasoning abilities of different models. We construct 5 classes of VQA questions on Language Table:

  • •

    绝对位置Absolute Position。此类询问所查询的块或机器人手臂位于板上的位置。答案是从一组固定的棋盘区域中选择的，例如中心、顶部、底部、左侧、右侧和四个角区域。模型直接输出相应的区域短语。例如，输入问题是“红色立方体位于棋盘上的什么位置？”真实答案是“红色立方体位于棋盘的左上角”。. This class asks where a queried block or robot arm is located on the board. The answer is selected from a fixed set of board regions, such as center, top, bottom, left, right, and the four corner regions. The model directly outputs the corresponding region phrase. For example, an input question is “Where is the red cube located on the board?” and the ground-truth answer is “The red cube is in the top left of the board.”

  • •

    相对位置Relative Position。此类询问一个物体相对于另一物体或机器人手臂的方向。答案是从一组固定的相对方向中选择的，包括左、右、上、下和四个对角线方向。模型直接输出相对方向短语。例如，输入问题是“蓝色月亮相对于黄色星星在哪里？”真实答案是“蓝色月亮在黄色星星的右边。”. This class asks for the direction of one object relative to another object or the robot arm. The answer is selected from a fixed set of relative directions, including left, right, top, bottom, and the four diagonal directions. The model directly outputs the relative direction phrase. For example, an input question is “Where is the blue moon relative to the yellow star?” and the ground-truth answer is “The blue moon is to the right of the yellow star.”

  • •

    距离Distance。此类询问哪个块距离指定的锚点对象最近或最远，其中锚点可以是另一个块或机器人手臂。答案是从可见的块名称中选择的。问题不是选择题，而是选择题。模型输出选定的块名称。例如，输入问题是“哪个块距离手臂最近？”真实答案是“绿星”。. This class asks which block is nearest to or farthest from a specified anchor object, where the anchor may be another block or the robot arm. The answer is chosen from the visible block names. The question is not multiple choice; the model outputs the selected block name. For example, an input question is “Which block is nearest to the arm?” and the ground-truth answer is “The green star.”

  • •

    指令执行Instruction Execution。此类询问所显示的场景是否正确满足给定的指令。答案格式是二进制的：正确执行或错误执行。该课程要求模型理解指令，识别相关物体和目标条件，并验证最终的视觉状态。例如，输入提示为“……指令：将红色方块移动到棋盘中央。该指令是否正确且完全执行？A.正确执行。B.错误执行。”响应的格式为“思考：……答案：A”，并且仅对最终答案进行评估。. This class asks whether a shown scene correctly satisfies a given instruction. The answer format is binary: correct execution or incorrect execution. This class requires the model to understand the instruction, identify the relevant objects and goal condition, and verify the final visual state. For example, an input prompt is “… Instruction: move the red cube to the center of the board. Is the instruction correctly and fully executed? A. Correct execution. B. Incorrect execution.” The response is formatted as “Think: … Answer: A” and evaluation is performed only on the final answer.

  • •

    指令推理Instruction Inference。本课程询问哪种考生指令最能解释观察到的前后视觉过渡。答案格式是对候选指令的多项选择，模型选择一个选项字母。此类测试逆向指令理解：模型必须从视觉变化推断出预期的命令，同时区分相似的语言选择。例如，输入提示为“…假设执行正确，执行了哪条指令？A.向左移动红色立方体。B.将蓝色月亮移到绿色星星。C.将黄色五边形与蓝色立方体分开。D…E…”响应格式为“思考：…答案：B”，仅对最终答案进行评估。. This class asks which candidate instruction best explains an observed before-and-after visual transition. The answer format is multiple choice over candidate instructions, with the model selecting one option letter. This class tests inverse instruction understanding: the model must infer the intended command from the visual change while distinguishing among similar language choices. For example, an input prompt is “… Given that the execution is correct, which instruction was executed? A. move the red cube left. B. move the blue moon to the green star. C. separate the yellow pentagon from the blue cube. D… E…” The response is formatted as “Think: … Answer: B” and evaluation is performed only on the final answer.
