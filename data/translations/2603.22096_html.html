7 结论Conclusion

我们研究了如何为临床法学硕士代理人配备结构化经验记忆，以便可靠地重用过去的决策知识。为此，我们引入了 GSEM，它将临床经验形式化为适应症和禁忌症，将它们组织成双层记忆图编码决策结构和经验间关系依赖，并通过条件兼容的种子回忆和图遍历支持适用性感知检索。在每次任务完成后，GSEM 通过根据结果反馈校准记忆可靠性和关系结构来自我进化，而无需修改经验内容。实验表明，GSEM 始终优于三类基线，尤其是在多步骤治疗计划方面取得了显着的进步。未来的工作包括整合更丰富的反馈信号，在临床限制下加强安全性和归因，以及评估长期现实世界工作流程中的记忆进化。We studied how to equip clinical LLM agents with structured experience memory for reliable reuse of past decision knowledge. To this end, we introduced GSEM, which formalizes clinical experiences as indications and contraindications, organizes them into a dual-layer memory graph encoding decision structure and inter-experience relational dependencies, and supports applicability-aware retrieval via condition-compatible seed recall and graph traversal. After each task, GSEM self-evolves by calibrating memory reliability and relational structure based on outcome feedback, without modifying experience content. Experiments show that GSEM consistently outperforms three categories of baselines, with particularly strong gains on multi-step treatment planning. Future work includes incorporating richer feedback signals, strengthening safety and attribution under clinical constraints, and evaluating memory evolution in longer-horizon real world workflows.

局限性Limitations

这项研究有一些局限性，可以在未来的工作中解决。首先，我们使用自动协议在公共基准上评估 GSEM。虽然这些基准涵盖开放式推理和具有挑战性的质量保证，但它们并不能完全反映真实的临床工作流程、交互式决策过程、纵向随访或部署限制。因此，有必要在更现实的环境中进行额外的验证，最好是通过专家评估。其次，内存构建需要对多个轨迹进行采样并进行可靠性验证，这会增加计算量。随着内存图的增长，维护链接和执行图遍历可能会增加延迟。最后，我们的检索器依赖于具有离散操作的 LLM 引导的遍历策略。尽管这种设计能够实现可控的组合检索，但它可能对提示敏感并在路径选择中引入非确定性。未来的工作可以探索更稳定的检索策略和对安全关键型使用更强的约束，例如通过从反馈中学习检索策略。This study has several limitations that could be addressed in future work. First, we evaluate GSEM on public benchmarks with automatic protocols. While these benchmarks cover open-ended reasoning and challenging QA, they do not fully reflect real clinical workflows, interactive decision processes, longitudinal follow-up, or deployment constraints. Additional validation in more realistic settings, ideally with expert assessment, is therefore necessary. Secondly, memory construction requires sampling multiple trajectories and performing reliability validation, which increases computation. As the memory graph grows, maintaining links and performing graph traversal may add latency. Finally, our retriever relies on an LLM-guided traversal policy with discrete actions. Although this design enables controllable, compositional retrieval, it can be sensitive to prompting and introduce non-determinism in path selection. Future work could explore more stable retrieval policies and stronger constraints for safety-critical use, for example by learning a retrieval policy from feedback.

道德考虑Ethical Consideration

这项工作研究临床推理代理中的连续记忆，并不适合直接临床使用。在高风险环境中，如果过度信任其输出或在没有合格人员监督的情况下使用，错误的建议可能会造成伤害。为了减轻这些风险，任何现实世界的使用都应该包含人机交互验证。我们的在线记忆进化使用任务级准确性作为反馈信号。虽然简单且客观，但准确性是一个粗粒度的指标，可能无法捕获安全关键的细微差别，因此部署应实施额外的保护措施和监控，以防止不安全的漂移。我们根据公共基准进行评估，并且在实验中不会收集可识别的患者信息。This work studies continual memory in clinical reasoning agents and is not intended for direct clinical use. In high-stakes settings, erroneous recommendations may cause harm if its outputs are over-trusted or used without qualified human oversight. To mitigate these risks, any real-world use should incorporate human-in-the-loop verification. Our online memory evolution uses task-level accuracy as the feedback signal. While simple and objective, accuracy is a coarse-grained metric and may not capture safety-critical nuances, so deployment should impose additional safeguards and monitoring to prevent unsafe drift. We evaluate on public benchmarks and do not collect identifiable patient information in our experiments.
