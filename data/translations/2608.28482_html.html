正确的评分规则如何影响 LLM 预测How Proper Scoring Rules Shape LLM Forecasting

摘要Abstract

本文评估了奖励函数的选择如何影响 LLM 预测者的绩效和行为。我们比较了五种适当的评分规则作为已解决的现实世界事件的二元预测的训练目标。尽管这些规则对于真实性具有相同的理论动机 概率报告，所得模型在校准、概率方面有所不同 使用和估计偏差、信息和噪声的概况，具有较小的 总体准确性和辨别力方面的差异。 Brier 训练模型具有最低的观察到的 Brier 分数和最高的 AUC-ROC，而对数训练的模型具有最高的观察到的对数分数和最低的校准误差。具有相似总体性能的模型也可以通过偏差、信息和噪声的不同组合来达到该性能。因此，正确的评分规则不需要与训练互换 目标。奖励选择不仅可能影响法学硕士的预测效果，还可能影响法学硕士的预测。 其预测误差是如何构成的。每个条件使用一个种子， 所以一些差异可能反映了训练的随机性。This paper evaluates how reward function choice shapes the performance and behavior of LLM forecasters. We compare five proper scoring rules as training objectives for binary forecasts of resolved real-world events. Although the rules share the same theoretical incentive for truthful probability reporting, the resulting models differ in calibration, probability use, and estimated profiles of bias, information, and noise, with smaller differences in aggregate accuracy and discrimination. The Brier-trained model has the lowest observed Brier score and highest AUC-ROC, while the log-trained model has the highest observed log score and lowest calibration error. Models with similar aggregate performance also reach that performance through different combinations of bias, information, and noise. Proper scoring rules therefore need not behave interchangeably as training objectives. Reward choice may shape not only how well an LLM forecasts, but how its forecasting errors are structured. Each condition uses a single seed, so some differences may reflect training stochasticity.

1 简介Introduction

预测是语言模型推理的天然测试平台。它要求模型结合不完整和相互矛盾的证据，将不确定性表达为概率，并在有限的信息下做出预测。它还允许进行客观评估。一旦事件解决，就可以根据观察到的结果对预测进行评分。Forecasting is a natural testbed for language-model reasoning. It requires models to combine incomplete and conflicting evidence, express uncertainty as a probability, and make predictions under limited information. It also permits objective evaluation. Once an event resolves, the forecast can be scored against the observed outcome.

本文研究了奖励选择与法学硕士预测者行为的关系。我们根据已解决的新闻事件衍生的二元预测问题训练模型。这些问题涵盖政治、地缘政治、经济、商业、科学和体育，预测期限为 7 至 90 天。每个模型均使用 Dr.GRPO 进行优化This paper studies how reward choice relates to the behavior of LLM forecasters. Each model is optimized using Dr. GRPO(Liu et al., 2025)，组相对策略优化（GRPO）的一种变体，以适当的评分规则作为最终奖励。模型、数据、优化器、训练步骤和部署预算在不同条件下保持固定。, a variant of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), with a proper scoring rule as the terminal reward. 5 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

这项研究比较了五种适当的评分规则如何作为最终奖励 用于共享有限训练设置下的 LLM 预测。受过奖励训练的人 模型通常优于基本模型，但它们的性能概况 不同。具有相似 Brier 分数的变体在以下方面表现出更明显的差异 校准、区分、概率量表的使用和估计的平衡 偏见、信息和噪音。因此，总分仅包含部分 所学预测者之间的差异。This study compares how five proper scoring rules behave as terminal rewards for LLM forecasting under a shared finite-training setup. The reward-trained models generally outperform the base model, but their performance profiles differ. Variants with similar Brier scores show clearer differences in calibration, discrimination, probability-scale use, and the estimated balance of bias, information, and noise. Aggregate scores therefore capture only part of the variation among the learned forecasters.

这些结果说明了理论上正确的实际区别 目标。每个评分规则都鼓励真实的概率报告 期望，但规则会产生不同的奖励分配， 有限策略优化期间的群体相对优势。在我们的 实验中，这些差异与不同的学习相关 预测概况。这并不挑战评分的适当性 规则。相反，它表明他们作为法学硕士培训的经验行为 在有限的数据、抽样部署和固定的情况下，目标可能会有所不同 优化程序。These results illustrate a practical distinction among theoretically proper objectives. Each scoring rule incentivizes truthful probability reporting in expectation, but the rules generate different reward distributions and group-relative advantages during finite policy optimization. In our experiments, these differences are associated with distinct learned forecasting profiles. This does not challenge the propriety of the scoring rules. Rather, it shows that their empirical behavior as LLM training objectives can differ under limited data, sampled rollouts, and a fixed optimization procedure.
