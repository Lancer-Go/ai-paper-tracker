1 简介Introduction

“时间就是金钱”“Time is money,”正如本杰明·富兰克林的名言，强调了时间在人类生活中的基本价值。 在人机交互中，时间是一种关键资源，直接影响可用性、部署成本和安全关键决策。 对于以下情况尤其如此as famously stated by Benjamin Franklin, highlights the fundamental value of time in human life. In human–computer interaction, time is a critical resource that directly impacts usability, deployment cost, and safety-critical decision making. This is especially true for口语对话模型Spoken Dialogue Models（SDM）(SDMs)[4, 16, 29]，在现实世界的应用中越来越受到关注，例如个人助理、可穿戴设备和医疗保健系统, which are gaining increasing attention in real-world applications, such as personal assistants, wearable devices, and healthcare systems[17, 1]。 在这些场景中，响应不仅必须准确、自然，而且通常还必须严格限制持续时间。 例如，可能需要语音助手在驾驶时提供路况更新；由于电池或带宽限制，可穿戴设备可能需要简洁的语音反馈。同样，在医疗或紧急情况下，语音助手可能需要在严格的时间压力下提供简短但信息丰富的指令。 在所有这些情况下，控制响应持续时间的能力是实际部署的关键要求。 尽管时间可控性很重要，但它在 SDM 中仍然没有得到充分的探索。. In these scenarios, a response must not only be accurate and natural, but often also strictly bounded in duration. For example, a voice assistant may be required to provide a traffic update while driving; a wearable device may require concise spoken feedback due to battery or bandwidth constraints. Similarly, in medical or emergency scenarios, a voice assistant may need to deliver brief yet informative instructions under strict time pressure. In all of these cases, the ability to control response duration is a key requirement for practical deployment. Despite its importance, time controllability remains largely underexplored in SDMs.

在文本大型语言模型 (LLM) 领域，先前的研究表明模型通常很难遵循明确的长度约束指令In the domain of text Large Language Models (LLMs), prior studies have shown that models often struggle to follow explicit length-constraint instructions[73]。 此外，法学硕士的输出经常表现出冗长或长度偏差，这种现象与基于偏好的评估和对齐相关. Moreover, LLM outputs often exhibit verbosity or length bias, a phenomenon associated with preference-based evaluation and alignment[20, 28]。 这种趋势削弱了指令遵循能力并对用户体验产生负面影响。 虽然基准、提示和培训策略已经开始解决文本法学硕士的长度可控性问题. This tendency weakens instruction-following capability and negatively affects user experience. While benchmarks, prompting and training strategies have begun to address length controllability in text LLMs[73, 63, 30, 54]，该研究方向仍然活跃，并由于其巨大的实际重要性而继续引起人们的关注。, this research direction remains active and continues to attract attention due to its substantial practical importance.

然而，控制 SDM 中的响应持续时间比控制文本中的输出长度更具挑战性。 在语音生成中，字数仅代表实际持续时间。 单个单词可能包含不同数量的音节，并且语音持续时间会随着语音构成、语言上下文和韵律结构而变化However, controlling response duration in SDMs is considerably more challenging than controlling output length in text. In speech generation, word count is only a proxy for actual duration. A single word may contain different numbers of syllables, and speech duration is known to vary with phonetic composition, linguistic context, and prosodic structure[31]。 此外，语速可能因说话者、说话风格和交流条件而异，这取决于说话者和听者. Moreover, speaking rate may vary across speakers, speaking styles, and communicative conditions, which depends on both speaker and listener[36]。 因此，简单地限制生成单词的数量并不能保证对最终语音持续时间的准确控制。 这种不匹配使得持续时间控制成为口语对话系统中一个独特且要求更高的问题。. As a result, simply constraining the number of generated words does not guarantee accurate control over the final speech duration. This mismatch makes duration control a unique and more demanding problem in spoken dialogue systems.

2.2 长度控制大型语言模型Length-Control Large Language Models

对法学硕士生成长度的精确调节对于使这些系统适应受限的下游应用并满足特定的用户需求至关重要。 为了在不产生重新训练的计算开销的情况下强制执行精确的长度限制，研究人员提出了各种免训练和解码时间的干预措施。这些方法包含先进的采样算法Precise regulation over the generation length of LLMs is critical for adapting these systems to constrained downstream applications and fulfilling specific user requirements. To enforce exact length constraints without incurring the computational overhead of retraining, researchers have proposed various training-free and decoding-time interventions. These methods encompass advanced sampling algorithms[25]，特定任务的零样本提示策略, task-specific zero-shot prompting strategies[50]，以及动态增加序列结束（EOS）代币权重的解码干预措施, and decoding interventions that dynamically increase the weight of the End-of-Sequence (EOS) token[7].

为了进行更细粒度的调节，指令调整方法明确集成位置感知以持续监控剩余的生成长度。最近的方法通过将长度跟踪信号直接合并到生成过程中来实现这一点，例如修改输入编码以反映到目标长度的距离For more fine-grained regulation, instruction tuning approaches explicitly integrate positional awareness to continuously monitor the remaining generation length. Recent methodologies achieve this by incorporating length-tracking signals directly into the generation process, such as modifying the input encodings to reflect the distance to the target length[9]，将潜在跟踪标记与输出序列一起交错, interleaving latent tracking tokens alongside the output sequence[54]，或注入显式位置标记以强制执行严格的长度限制, or injecting explicit positional markers to enforce strict length constraints[59]。这些通用对齐策略使模型能够严格遵守预定义的长度约束，并可靠地执行复杂的文本操作操作，包括精确的复制和粘贴任务。. These generalized alignment strategies enable models to strictly adhere to predefined length constraints and reliably execute complex text manipulation operations including precise copy-and-paste tasks.

除了显式指令调整之外，并行研究还利用强化学习 (RL) 和偏好优化来使大型语言模型与人类期望保持一致。然而，标准对齐过程经常引入冗长偏差，其中模型倾向于将较长的响应与较高的质量相关联。 为了解决这个基本问题，基于强化学习的先进方法经过改进，将显式长度偏差与实际响应评估分离Beyond explicit instruction tuning, a parallel line of research leverages reinforcement learning (RL) and preference optimization to align large language models with human expectations. However, standard alignment processes frequently introduce verbosity biases, where models tend to associate longer responses with higher quality. To address this fundamental issue, advanced RL-based approaches have been refined to decouple explicit length biases from actual response evaluation[39, 32]。最近的强化学习范式超越了仅仅减轻表面冗长的范围，它已将重点转向主动管理模型的内部计算推理持续时间。根据目标，这些框架要么建立约束环境来强制执行简洁的推理步骤. Expanding beyond the mere mitigation of superficial verbosity, recent RL paradigms have shifted focus toward actively managing the internal computational reasoning duration of models. Depending on the objective, these frameworks either establish constrained environments to enforce concise reasoning steps[37]，或者在生成最终答案之前故意延长这些内部认知轨迹以扩展系统解决问题的能力, or deliberately prolong these internal cognitive trajectories to expand the problem-solving capabilities of the system prior to generating a final answer[38, 2, 30].

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了In this work, we introduced钛钴TiCo，一个简单而有效的训练后框架，使口语对话模型能够遵循明确的时间控制指令, a simple yet effective post-training framework that enables spoken dialogue models to follow explicit time-controlled instructions through口语时间标记Spoken Time Markers以及时间意识和时间可控性的两阶段培训过程。 我们还提出了and a two-stage training process for time awareness and time-controllability. We also presented钛钴工作台TiCo-Bench，第一个旨在评估口语对话模型中时间可控指令遵循的基准。 实验结果表明，与强大的开源、商业和级联基线相比，TiCo 显着提高了持续时间可控性，同时保持了与主干模型相当的响应质量。 此外，TiCo 的推广超出了训练期间观察到的持续时间范围，并有效地从语音查询转移到文本查询输入，这表明时间控制可以作为一种强大的中间规划能力来学习。 在未来的工作中，我们的目标是进一步提高时间标记预测的精度，将训练扩展到更长、更多样化的对话场景，并探索如何将时间控制与口语系统中其他可控对话行为相结合。, the first benchmark designed to evaluate time-controllable instruction following in spoken dialogue models. Experimental results demonstrate that TiCo substantially improves duration controllability over strong open-source, commercial, and cascaded baselines, while maintaining response quality comparable to the backbone model. Moreover, TiCo generalizes beyond the duration range observed during training and transfers effectively from speech-query to text-query inputs, suggesting that temporal control can be learned as a robust intermediate planning capability. In future work, we aim to further improve the precision of time-marker prediction, extend training to longer and more diverse conversational scenarios, and explore how time control can be integrated with other controllable dialogue behaviors in spoken language systems.
