DOPD：双同政策蒸馏DOPD: Dual On-policy Distillation

Xinlei Yu    Gen Li    Qingyi Si    Guibin Zhang    Yuqi Xu    Congcong Wang    Shuai Dong    Kaiwen Tuo    Xiangyu Zeng    Kaituo Feng    Qunzhong Wang    Yang Shi    Xiaobin Hu    Xiangyu Yue    Jiaqi Wang    Shuicheng Yan 1 NUS  2 MMLab, CUHK  3 PKU  4 Explore Academy, JD
摘要Abstract

在策略蒸馏 (OPD) 通过使用密集的代币级信号监督学生采样轨迹，提供卓越的容量传输。 为了提供高质量的监督来源，从而提升蒸馏的性能前沿，一个直观的方向是向教师或学生本身注入特权信息。 然而，这种额外的输入会导致潜在的故障模式，我们称之为On-policy distillation (OPD) offers superior capacity transfer by supervising student-sampled trajectories with dense token-level signals. To furnish high-quality supervision sources and thereby elevate the performance frontier of distillation, an intuitive direction is to infuse privileged information to either teacher or student itself. However, this additional input induces a potential failure mode we dub特权幻觉privilege illusion：这种模式将学生本应缩小的可转移能力差距与只能模仿但永远无法复制的信息不对称差距混为一谈。 代币级别监管固有的不一致性进一步放大了这个问题，其中只有一小部分代币携带关键的能力承载信号。 为此，我们建议: a pattern that conflates the transferable capability gap that students are meant to close, and the information asymmetry gap that can only be mimicked but never replicated. This issue is further amplified by the inherent non-uniformity of token-level supervision, where only a small subset of tokens carries pivotal capability-bearing signals. To this end, we propose多普勒DOPD，一种优势感知的双重蒸馏范式，根据优势差距和相对概率在特权教师和特权学生策略之间动态路由令牌级监督。 每个代币都接受教师或学生本身不同强度、目标和策略的监督，在传递可信能力的同时接收辅助信号，以减轻特权错觉。 对大语言模型 (LLM) 和视觉语言模型 (VLM) 设置的大量实验表明，DOPD 始终优于 Vanilla OPD 和其他同行。稳定性、鲁棒性、持续学习和分布外任务的进一步结果验证了其优越性。, an advantage-aware dual distillation paradigm that dynamically routes token-level supervision between privileged teacher and privileged student policies based on their advantage gap and relative probabilities. Each token receives supervision of different strength, objective, and strategy from either teacher or student itself, which transfers credible capability while simultaneously receiving auxiliary signals, to alleviate privilege illusion. Extensive experiments on both large language model (LLM) and vision-language model (VLM) settings demonstrate that DOPD consistently outperforms Vanilla OPD and other counterparts. Further results on stability, robustness, continual learning, and out-of-distribution tasks validate its superiority.

1 简介Introduction

蒸馏作为将高绩效教师政策的能力转化为次优学生政策的强大范例，通常依赖于偏离政策的轨迹，这可能会使学生面临与其自身不断发展的行为不一致的状态分布Distillation, as a powerful paradigm for transferring the capabilities of a high-performing teacher policy into a suboptimal student policy, typically relies on off-policy trajectories, which may expose the student to state distributions that are misaligned with its own evolving behavior[18, 32, 13, 56, 52]。相比之下，最近的 OPD 范式通过从当前的学生政策中推出并使用教师提供令牌级别的监督信号来解决这一限制. By contrast, recent OPD paradigms address this limitation by rolling out from the current student policy and using the teacher to provide token-level supervisory signals[23, 35, 42, 44]。这种公式不仅可以减轻分布变化，还可以通过学生采样的策略轨迹提供密集的每个代币教师监督，从而产生更高的蒸馏效率和卓越的性能。. This formulation not only mitigates distribution shift, but also delivers dense per-token teacher supervision via student-sampled on-policy trajectories, yielding higher distillation efficiency and superior performance.

Nevertheless, the theoretical gains afforded by privileged information training do not necessarily translate into transferable supervisory signals. Rather, they may stem from a hitherto uncharacterized failure mode,即i.e.，特权错觉：教师和学生之间表面上的表现差距实际上混淆了两个根本不同的组成部分。首先是师生之间内在的能力差距，这一差距有望通过蒸馏来弥补；第二个是由信息不对称造成的差距，这种差距是由于获得几乎无法学习的特权投入而产生的。 因此，不加区别地提炼这种教师分布可能会导致学生适应特权结果，而不是获得可转移的能力。 总而言之，, privilege illusion: the ostensible performance gap between teacher and student in fact conflates two fundamentally distinct components. The first is the intrinsic teacher-student capability gap, which is expected to close through distillation; the second is a gap driven by information asymmetry, which arises from the access to privileged inputs that remain almost unlearnable. Indiscriminately distilling such a teacher distribution may therefore cause the student to fit privileged outcomes rather than acquire transferable ability. 2 相关作品Related Works

2.1 师生可蒸馏性Teacher-student Distillability

长期以来，师生蒸馏一直被研究为将能力从较强的教师转移到较弱的学生模型的手段Teacher-student distillation has long been studied as a means of transferring capability from a stronger teacher to a weaker student model[20, 32, 55]。在大型模型时代，教师与学生的可提炼性已经成为一个比单纯的教师模仿更微妙的问题：最近的研究表明，教师不仅可以将标签，还可以将基本原理、轨迹、偏好和更广泛的行为模式传递给较小的学生. In the era of large models, teacher–student distillability has become a more nuanced question than mere teacher imitation: recent work shows that teachers can transfer not only labels, but also rationales, trajectories, preferences, and broader behavioral patterns to smaller students[13, 8, 1]。然而，这种转移在师资力量上并不是单调的。对能力差距的研究表明，过于强大的教师可能会提供有限的学生难以吸收的信号. However, such transfer is not monotonic in teacher strength. Studies on the capacity gap suggest that an overly powerful teacher may provide signals that are difficult for a limited student to absorb[4, 26, 45]。 最近，一些作品也在 OPD 设置上报告了这种现象，这表明可能需要更兼容的初始分布以实现更好的师生蒸馏. Recently, some works also report this phenomenon on OPD settings, indicating that a more compatible initial distribution may be needed for better teacher-student distillation[25, 7, 22, 1]。 总的来说，这些研究表明，有效的蒸馏不仅取决于更强大的教师模型，还取决于能力转移的内容和形式。. Collectively, these studies suggest that effective distillation depends not only on a more powerful teacher model but also on the content and form of capacity being transferred.

Critically, these methods fundamentally overlook the risk of privilege illusion, thus may fail to explicitly identify and distill genuine inherent capacity.

3.1 背景Background

3.1.1 特权错觉Privilege Illusion

现有的 OPD 从根本上依赖于这样的假设：更强大的教师可以提供更丰富、信息更丰富的监督Existing OPD fundamentally relies on the assumption that a stronger teacher provides richer and more informative supervision[23, 21]。 因此，在许多实际场景中，直观的探索是为教师或学生本身配备特权输入. Thus, in many practical scenarios, an intuitive exploration is to equip teachers or student itself with privileged inputs[35]。 例如，以推理为中心的任务中经过验证的提示. For instance, verified hints in reasoning-centric tasks[61, 30, 15]，或视觉感知场景中物体的边界框, or bounding boxes of objects in visual perception scenarios[28, 57]。 这里，如图所示. Here, as exampled in Figure11和and12，我们采用适度形式的特权信息，提供必要的线索，同时避免直接公开详细的执行过程和最终答案（各种形式的特权信息的影响将在第1节中讨论）, we employ a moderate form of privileged information that delivers essential cues while refraining from directly disclosing detailed execution procedures and final answers (influence of various forms of privileged information will be discussed in Section4.3.2）。 然而，当利用特权信息进行增强时，预测优势可能来自信息不对称而不是真正的固有能力。未经策划的蒸馏此类信号可能会鼓励学生模仿特权结果，而不是获得实用和可转移的能力，或者由于无法弥补的师生差距而引发蒸馏性，导致劣质和不稳定的蒸馏过程，以及不利的熵崩溃). However, when augmented with privileged information, the prediction advantage may arise from information asymmetry rather than genuine inherent capability. Uncurated distilling such signals can encourage the student to imitate privileged outcomes instead of acquiring practical and transferable abilities, or triggers distillability due to irreparable teacher-student gap, leading to inferior and unstable distillation process, and unfavorable entropy collapse[6, 54].

We evaluate three OPD variants, in which privileged information is granted to the teacher policy only, the student policy only, and both policies, respectively. We observe that introducing privileged information to either the teacher or the student separately delivers modest performance improvements over Vanilla OPD in the very early training phase, yet the information asymmetry between the two policies gives rise to late-stage performance degradation coupled with entropy collapse. When both policies are granted access to privileged information, the superficial advantage conferred by information asymmetry vanishes. 3.1.2 特权优势差距Privilege Advantage Gap

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们重新审视特权环境下的 OPD 并确定基本局限性：特权教师的明显优势并不总是与可转移能力相对应，而是可能源于信息不对称，并且这些监督信号在代币之间分布不均匀。 受这些观察的启发，我们提出了 DOPD，这是一种优势感知的双重同策略蒸馏框架，可在教师驱动的能力转移和学生的辅助自我优化之间自适应地路由令牌级监督。 通过利用特权优势差距和相对令牌概率，DOPD 有选择地将强全词汇教师蒸馏应用于能力承载令牌，同时对没有能力优势差距的令牌施加轻度或弱蒸馏。 跨 LLM 和 VLM 设置的大量实验表明，DOPD 始终优于 Vanilla OPD 和强大的竞争基线，产生卓越的蒸馏性能、鲁棒性、持续学习行为、分布外泛化和训练稳定性。In this work, we revisit OPD under privileged contexts and identify fundamental limitations: the apparent superiority of a privileged teacher does not always correspond to transferable capability, but may instead arise from information asymmetry, and these supervision signals are not evenly distributed across tokens. Motivated by these observations, we propose DOPD, an advantage-aware dual on-policy distillation framework that adaptively routes token-level supervision between teacher-driven capability transfer and auxiliary self-optimization from the student. By leveraging the privilege advantage gap and relative token probabilities, DOPD selectively applies strong full-vocabulary teacher distillation to capability-bearing tokens, while imposing light or weak distillation on tokens without a capacity advantage gap. Extensive experiments across LLM and VLM settings demonstrate that DOPD consistently outperforms Vanilla OPD and strong competitive baselines, yielding superior distillation performance, robustness, continual-learning behavior, out-of-distribution generalization, and training stability.

6 局限性和未来方向Limitations and Future Directions

尽管我们提出的 DOPD 很有效，但我们承认仍然存在一些小局限性。 首先，我们的方法取决于特权信息的可用性和质量，其构建会产生注释、生成和过滤过程的额外成本。 其次，与 Vanilla OPD 相比，它引入了额外的计算开销，需要对学生模型进行一次额外的前向传递。 第三，虽然当前的路由策略是直观的并且经验上稳定的，但它仍然依赖于启发式机制。Notwithstanding the efficacy of our proposed DOPD, we acknowledge that several minor limitations remain. First, our method hinges on the availability and quality of privileged information, the construction of which incurs additional costs for annotation, generation, and filtering processes. Second, it introduces extra computational overhead relative to Vanilla OPD, requiring one additional forward pass of the student model. Third, while the current routing strategy is intuitive, and empirically stable, it still relies on heuristic mechanisms.

未来的研究可能会沿着以下方向进一步推进 DOPD：开发更可靠和更具成本效益的机制来获取特权信息或发现替代策略来检测可用的优势差距，并具有更原则性或可学习的蒸馏路由。更广泛地说，教师和学生的动态蒸馏范式为法学硕士和 VLM 之外的选择性能力转移提供了有用的视角，吸引了未来关于更可解释、更高效和更值得信赖的蒸馏范式的工作。Future research may further advance DOPD along directions: developing more reliable and cost-effective mechanisms for obtaining privileged information or discovering alternative strategy to detect available advantage gap, with more principled or learnable distillation routing. More broadly, the paradigm of dynamic distillation from both teacher and student offers a useful lens for selective capacity transfer beyond LLMs and VLMs, inviting future work on more interpretable, efficient, and trustworthy distillation paradigms.
