摘要Abstract

在策略自蒸馏（OPSD）是有前途的，因为它消除了在策略蒸馏（OPD）所需的外部教师，但它仍然需要教师和学生之间的不对称信息，以确保自教师提供比学生更强的学习信号。 现有方法通过特权答案或视觉证据造成这种不对称。我们询问是否可以将两者删除，从而产生纯粹由输入条件驱动的更简单形式的 OPSD。 为此，我们建议On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) is promising as it removes the external teacher required by on-policy distillation (OPD), yet it still needs asymmetric information between teacher and student to ensure that the self-teacher provides a stronger learning signal than the student. Existing methods create this asymmetry either through privileged answers or visual evidence. We ask whether both can be removed, yielding a simpler form of OPSD driven purely by input conditioning. For this purpose, we propose视觉对比自蒸馏Visual Contrastive Self-Distillation，即, namelyVCSD，它将图像内容删除转换为符合策略的自蒸馏信号。 对于每个学生生成的响应前缀，EMA 教师在相同的提示和前缀下生成两个下一个令牌分布 - 一个以原始图像为条件，另一个以内容擦除控件为条件。 它们的标记对数概率差异突出显示了实例级视觉内容特别增加可能性的候选者。 我们利用这种对比来锐化教师在其合理支持下的原始图像分布，并将最终的完整分布目标提炼到学生身上。 使用 ViRL39K 数据集，VCSD 在 Qwen3-VL 和 Qwen3.5 模型中始终优于匹配的 OPSD。例如，在 Qwen3-VL 上，它改进了七个基准聚合, which converts image-content removal into an on-policy self-distillation signal. We use this contrast to sharpen the teacher’s original-image distribution within its plausible support, and distill the resulting full-distribution target into the student. 1 简介Introduction

This aligns training with the student’s inference-time trajectories, but typically requires a stronger teacher, increasing post-training cost and complexity(Liu et al., 2026; Yoon et al., 2026)。 按策略自我蒸馏 (OPSD) 通过从同一模型导出目标来消除外部教师，通常是通过指数移动平均 (EMA) 教师。 然而，当学生和自学者以相同的前缀接收到相同的信息时，目标可能会在学生当前的预测之外添加很少的内容。 因此，同策略采样决定. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) removes the external teacher by deriving targets from the same model, often through an exponential moving average (EMA) teacher. However, when the student and self-teacher receive the same information at the same prefix, the target may add little beyond the student’s current prediction. Effective OPSD still requires a teacher–student asymmetry that makes the self-teacher more informative than the student.

For language reasoning tasks, the teacher may be conditioned on privileged answers or reasoning traces(Zhao et al., 2026)、专家示范, expert demonstrations(Shenfeld et al., 2026)，或丰富的文字反馈, or rich textual feedback(Hübotter et al., 2026)。 对于视觉语言任务，教师可以接收视觉证据信号，例如作物、区域或其他更直接地暴露相关图像证据的视觉条件. For vision-language tasks, the teacher can receive visual evidence signals, such as crops, regions, or other visual conditions that expose the relevant image evidence more directly(Yuan et al., 2026; Sun et al., 2026; Tian et al., 2026)。 这些方法可以产生有效的监督，但取决于特定于任务的信息或额外的处理管道。 这些语言端信号并不总是可用，而视觉证据输入可能需要注释、外部定位模型或手动设计的图像转换。. These language-side signals are not always available, while visual evidence inputs may require annotations, external localization models, or manually designed image transformations.

然而，单独的对比度并不能定义可靠的蒸馏目标，因为令牌可能会表现出较大的相对变化，同时在原始图像下仍然不太可能。 因此，我们为两个教师的预测分配了互补的角色：原始图像分布识别出合理的候选者，而条件对比度根据他们对视觉内容的依赖来锐化他们的相对偏好。 它们共同产生了一个直观的全面分布目标，可以沿着其政策轨迹提炼到学生身上。The contrast alone, however, does not define a reliable distillation target, since a token may exhibit a large relative change while remaining unlikely under the original image. Together, they yield a visually informed full-distribution target that can be distilled into the student along its on-policy trajectory.

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 VCSD，它直接从匹配的视觉条件构造了策略自蒸馏所需的目标不对称性。 对于每个学生生成的前缀，EMA 教师都会在原始图像和内容擦除控件下进行评估。 它们的标记式对数概率对比在其合理的支持范围内锐化了原始图像分布，从而在没有外部教师、特权答案和视觉证据信号的情况下产生信息丰富的全分布目标。 在 Qwen3-VL 和 Qwen3.5 模型中，VCSD 相对于相应的基础模型和匹配的 OPSD 基线一致提高了视觉语言性能。 这些结果表明，模型自身条件预测的变化可以为自蒸馏提供有效的监督来源。We introduced VCSD, which constructs the target asymmetry required for on-policy self-distillation directly from matched visual conditioning. At each student-generated prefix, the EMA teacher is evaluated under the original image and a content-erased control. Their token-wise log-probability contrast sharpens the original-image distribution within its plausible support, producing an informative full-distribution target without an external teacher, privileged answers, and visual evidence signals. Across Qwen3-VL and Qwen3.5 models, VCSD consistently improves vision-language performance over both the corresponding base models and matched OPSD baselines. These results show that changes in a model’s own conditional predictions can provide an effective source of supervision for self-distillation.
