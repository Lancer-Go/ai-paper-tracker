GeoAgentBench：空间分析中工具增强代理的动态执行基准GeoAgentBench: A Dynamic Execution Benchmark for Tool-Augmented Agents in Spatial Analysis

Chi Wang1   Zhiming Zhang1   Haifeng Li1   Wentao Yang2

摘要Abstract

将大型语言模型 (LLM) 集成到地理信息系统 (GIS) 中标志着向自主空间分析的范式转变。然而，由于地理空间工作流程的复杂性、多步骤性质，评估这些基于法学硕士的代理仍然具有挑战性。现有的基准主要依赖于静态文本或代码匹配，忽略动态运行时反馈和空间输出的多模态性质。为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了 GeoAgentBench (GABench)，这是一种为工具增强型 GIS 代理量身定制的动态交互式评估基准。 GABench 提供了一个集成 117 个原子 GIS 工具的真实执行沙箱，涵盖 6 个核心 GIS 领域的 53 个典型空间分析任务。认识到精确的参数配置是动态 GIS 环境中执行成功的主要决定因素，我们设计了参数执行准确性 (PEA) 指标，该指标利用“最后尝试对齐”策略来量化隐式参数推断的保真度。作为补充，提出了基于视觉语言模型（VLM）的验证来评估数据空间准确性和制图风格的遵守情况。此外，为了解决因参数不一致和运行时异常导致的频繁任务失败问题，我们开发了一种新颖的代理架构——Plan-and-React——通过将全局编排与逐步反应执行解耦来模仿专家认知工作流程。对七个具有代表性的法学硕士进行的大量实验表明，计划与反应范式显着优于传统框架，实现了逻辑严谨性和执行鲁棒性之间的最佳平衡，特别是在多步推理和错误恢复方面。我们的研究结果强调了当前的能力边界，并为评估和推进下一代自主 GeoAI 建立了稳健的标准。源代码和数据集位于：github.com/geox-lab/GABench。The integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) into Geographic Information Systems (GIS) marks a paradigm shift toward autonomous spatial analysis. However, evaluating these LLM-based agents remains challenging due to the complex, multi-step nature of geospatial workflows. Existing benchmarks primarily rely on static text or code matching, neglecting dynamic runtime feedback and the multimodal nature of spatial outputs. To address this gap, we introduce GeoAgentBench (GABench), a dynamic and interactive evaluation benchmark tailored for tool-augmented GIS agents. GABench provides a realistic execution sandbox integrating 117 atomic GIS tools, encompassing 53 typical spatial analysis tasks across 6 core GIS domains. Recognizing that precise parameter configuration is the primary determinant of execution success in dynamic GIS environments, we designed the Parameter Execution Accuracy (PEA) metric, which utilizes a “Last-Attempt Alignment” strategy to quantify the fidelity of implicit parameter inference. Complementing this, a Vision-Language Model (VLM) based verification is proposed to assess data-spatial accuracy and cartographic style adherence. Furthermore, to address the frequent task failures caused by parameter misalignments and runtime anomalies, we developed a novel agent architecture—Plan-and-React—that mimics expert cognitive workflows by decoupling global orchestration from step-wise reactive execution. Extensive experiments with seven representative LLMs demonstrate that the Plan-and-React paradigm significantly outperforms traditional frameworks, achieving the optimal balance between logical rigor and execution robustness, particularly in multi-step reasoning and error recovery. Our findings highlight current capability boundaries and establish a robust standard for assessing and advancing the next generation of autonomous GeoAI. The source code and dataset are available at: github.com/geox-lab/GABench.

1 简介Introduction

空间分析是地理信息科学（GIScience）最基本的工具之一，广泛应用于城市规划、环境监测、灾害评估、交通管理等关键场景Spatial analysis serves as one of the most fundamental tools in Geographic Information Science (GIScience), widely applied in critical scenarios such as urban planning, environmental monitoring, disaster assessment, and traffic management(Liao et al., 2023; Larkin et al., 2023; Pham et al., 2021; Shahi et al., 2023; Shao et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025; Cui et al., 2024)。然而，地理空间分析任务本质上具有挑战性，经常需要集成多源异构数据并执行多步骤空间计算工作流程. However, geospatial analysis tasks are inherently challenging, frequently necessitating the integration of multi-source heterogeneous data and the execution of multi-step spatial computational workflows(Li et al., 2016; Liakos and Panagos, 2022; Wang et al., 2025; He et al., 2025)。在遥感、物联网等空间数据获取能力和技术快速进步的推动下，现代地理空间分析任务的复杂性进一步升级。因此，增强空间分析工作流程的自动化和智能化已成为地理空间人工智能（GeoAI）领域的一个突出研究目标. Driven by the rapid advancement in spatial data acquisition capabilities and technologies such as remote sensing and the Internet of Things (IoT), the complexity of modern geospatial analysis tasks has further escalated. Consequently, enhancing the automation and intelligence of spatial analysis workflows has emerged as a prominent research objective within the field of Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI)(Janowicz et al., 2020; Peng et al., 2025).

与许多孤立的预测任务不同，现实世界的地理空间分析通常表现为由不同空间操作组成的复杂工作流程。例如，综合分析管道可能包含多个步骤，例如数据清理、坐标重投影、空间叠加、空间统计建模和地图可视化，这些步骤通常受到严格的逻辑依赖关系的约束。传统的GeoAI研究主要集中于为单个任务构建专门的端到端模型，旨在通过参数优化直接拟合输入输出映射Unlike many isolated prediction tasks, real-world geospatial analysis typically manifests as complex workflows composed of diverse spatial operations. For instance, a comprehensive analytical pipeline may encompass multiple steps—such as data cleaning, coordinate reprojection, spatial overlay, spatial statistical modeling, and map visualization—which are often bound by strict logical dependencies. Traditional GeoAI research has predominantly focused on constructing specialized end-to-end models for single tasks, aiming to directly fit the input-output mapping through parameter optimization(Ronneberger et al., 2015; Kipf and Welling, 2017)。然而，这种范式忽视了地理空间分析任务中普遍存在的工作流程异构性。即使针对相同的地理空间目标，分析工作流程也很少是静态的。具体的处理路径高度依赖于数据源的特性；例如，当输入数据由多源异构矢量和栅格格式组成，或跨不同空间尺度和坐标参考系统 (CRS) 运行时. However, this paradigm overlooks the workflow heterogeneity that is pervasive in geospatial analysis tasks. Even when targeting the same geospatial objective, the analytical workflow is rarely static. The specific processing pathway is highly dependent on the characteristics of the data sources; for example, when input data consist of multi-source heterogeneous vector and raster formats, or operate across different spatial scales and coordinate reference systems (CRS)(Yao et al., 2024)，分析管道在数据预处理、空间算子选择、参数配置、空间统计建模等阶段必须进行动态适应和重新配置。受僵化架构的限制，传统的端到端模型缺乏这种多步骤、非线性分析逻辑所需的编排能力，导致它们无法处理复杂多变的现实地理场景。因此，在实际的GIS操作中，复杂的空间分析仍然严重依赖于领域专家使用专业软件的手动规划和执行。这种依赖严重阻碍了地理空间技术的民主化和自动化, the analytical pipeline must undergo dynamic adaptation and reconfiguration during stages such as data preprocessing, spatial operator selection, parameter configuration, and spatial statistical modeling. Constrained by their rigid architectures, traditional end-to-end models lack the orchestration capabilities required for such multi-step, non-linear analytical logic, rendering them ill-equipped to handle complex and variable real-world geographical scenarios. Consequently, in practical GIS operations, complex spatial analysis continues to rely heavily on manual planning and execution by domain experts using professional software. This reliance significantly hinders the democratization and automation of geospatial technologies(Li and Ning, 2023)，强调向高度智能和自主的地理空间系统过渡的迫切需要, underscoring the urgent need for transition toward highly intelligent and autonomous geospatial systems(Li et al., 2017).

近年来，在大语言模型（LLM）能力显着增强的推动下，构建以LLM为中央决策中心的工具增强代理已成为一个突出的研究热点In recent years, driven by the significant enhancement of Large Language Model (LLM) capabilities, constructing tool-augmented agents with LLMs as the central decision-making hub has emerged as a prominent research focus(Schick et al., 2023)。与传统的端到端模型相比，这些代理可以通过自然语言理解用户意图，将复杂问题分解为一系列可执行子任务，并动态调度外部工具来实现计算逻辑。目前，此类代理已在代码生成、数据分析和软件自动化等领域展现出巨大的潜力. In contrast to traditional end-to-end models, these agents can comprehend user intent through natural language, decompose complex problems into a series of executable subtasks, and dynamically schedule external tools to implement computational logic. Currently, such agents have demonstrated substantial potential in domains including code generation, data analysis, and software automation(Zhang et al., 2024, ; Xie et al., 2024)。基于这些成功，地理空间领域的相关研究进一步证实法学硕士拥有广泛的地理空间知识和深厚的空间推理能力. Building upon these successes, related research in the geospatial field has further corroborated that LLMs possess extensive geospatial knowledge and profound spatial reasoning capabilities(Roberts et al., 2023; Mai et al., 2022)，而视觉语言模型（VLM）在遥感图像的语义理解方面也表现出了卓越的性能, while Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have also exhibited exceptional performance in the semantic understanding of remote sensing imagery(Lobry et al., 2020; Kuckreja et al., 2024)。这些进步为空间智能体的发展奠定了坚实的认知基础。因此，引入配备空间推理和工具调用功能的代理被认为是弥合一般语义推理和专门空间计算之间差距的有前途的途径。在此架构下，代理利用LLM作为任务分解和工作流程规划的决策核心，同时精确编排外部GIS工具进行空间计算。这将传统上依赖于领域专家手动操作的复杂分析管道转变为自然语言驱动的自动化流程，显着降低了地理空间智能技术的进入门槛. These advancements establish a robust cognitive foundation for the development of spatial agents. Consequently, introducing agents equipped with spatial reasoning and tool-invocation capabilities is regarded as a promising pathway to bridge the gap between general semantic reasoning and specialized spatial computation. Under this architecture, the agent leverages the LLM as its decision-making core for task decomposition and workflow planning, while precisely orchestrating external GIS tools for spatial computation. This transforms complex analytical pipelines—which traditionally relied on manual operation by domain experts—into natural language-driven automated processes, significantly lowering the barrier to entry for geospatial intelligence technologies(Huang et al., 2024).

然而，实现这一愿景取决于一个关键的先决条件：能够系统地评估智能体是否真正具备执行复杂空间分析任务的能力。作为GIS中认知复杂度最高、逻辑链最长的计算过程，空间分析对智能体的规划能力、工具使用执行能力和运行时错误恢复能力提出了严格的要求。尽管学术界最近的努力已经产生了几个相关的评估基准——例如 ToolBenchHowever, realizing this vision hinges on a critical prerequisite: the ability to systematically evaluate whether agents truly possess the capability to execute complex spatial analysis tasks. As the computational process entailing the highest cognitive complexity and the longest logical chains within GIS, spatial analysis imposes stringent demands on an agent’s planning proficiency, tool-use execution, and runtime error recovery capabilities. 然而，现实世界的空间分析环境本质上充满了不确定性。即使逻辑上合理的计划也可能在运行时因坐标参考系统 (CRS) 不匹配、无效的空间拓扑或数据格式冲突等异常而失败However, real-world spatial analysis environments are inherently fraught with uncertainties. Even logically sound plans may fail at runtime due to anomalies such as Coordinate Reference System (CRS) mismatches, invalid spatial topologies, or data format conflicts(Longley et al., 2015)。在这种情况下，代理必须依靠实时反馈来进行错误诊断和动态调整。因此，非交互式评估不仅无法准确衡量智能体在复杂地理空间任务中的实际性能，而且在面对特定领域的运行时错误时也难以捕捉其自主调试和自我纠正的关键能力. In such instances, agents must rely on real-time feedback to perform error diagnosis and dynamic adjustments. Consequently, non-interactive evaluations not only fail to accurately gauge the agent’s actual performance in complex geospatial tasks, but also struggle to capture its crucial capabilities in autonomous debugging and self-correction when confronting domain-specific runtime errors(Shinn et al., 2023).

These tasks fail to adequately capture the highly intricate business requirements of real-world geospatial analysis. Instead, they are typically presented as combinations of isolated operators, lacking a profound simulation of complex spatial logical chains. Such oversimplification of analytical tasks renders it exceedingly difficult for existing benchmarks to systematically evaluate the comprehensive capabilities of agents when navigating authentic, long-chain geospatial analysis workflows.

最后，现有的评估方法主要将评估过程过度简化为文本级别的比较，从而忽略了空间分析可交付成果的多模态性质。全面的 GIS 分析工作流程通常不仅会产生文本输出，还会产生空间数据文件（例如 GeoJSON 或 TIFF）以及最终的制图表示。然而，此类空间数据集的几何正确性及其制图渲染的质量目前很少被整合到统一的评估范式中。Finally, existing evaluation methodologies predominantly oversimplify the assessment process into text-level comparisons, thereby neglecting the multimodal nature of spatial analysis deliverables. A comprehensive GIS analytical workflow typically yields not only textual outputs but also spatial data files (e.g., GeoJSON or TIFF) alongside ultimate cartographic representations. Nevertheless, the geometric correctness of such spatial datasets and the quality of their cartographic rendering are currently rarely integrated into a unified evaluation paradigm.

This taxonomy not only encompasses both vector and raster data models but also achieves a progressive transition in logical depth—from basic geometric operations to complex process simulations. This hierarchical architecture establishes a rigorous baseline for systematically evaluating an agent’s long-chain reasoning and multi-step tool orchestration capabilities.

任务集的构建始于GeoAnalystBench的严格筛选The construction of the task set began with a rigorous screening of GeoAnalystBench(Zhang et al., 2025)。我们评估了 50 个案例，排除了 10 个依赖闭源数据或专有 ArcGIS 格式的任务，确保与开放地理空间计算生态系统的兼容性。然而，更深入的分析表明，剩下的 40 个案例由于两个关键限制而无法直接用于自主工具编排：（1）粗略的逻辑粒度：原始的人类设计的工作流程被组织成高级逻辑抽象，例如“加载数据集”、“插值”或“合并”，而不是细粒度的可执行操作。这种原子粒度的缺乏使得代理很难动态地调度子任务； (2) 整体脚本：随附的 Python 代码主要由特定于任务的整体脚本组成，缺乏分解为可重用原子工具所需的模块化性。因此，我们没有选择使用原始源代码，而是选择完全重构分析逻辑——下一节将详细介绍结构重构的过程。为了更好地支持这种转变并实现端到端验证，我们通过修改指令来强制制图输出并标准化所有文件访问路径来重塑任务描述。. We evaluated its 50 cases and excluded 10 tasks that relied on closed-source data or proprietary ArcGIS formats, ensuring compatibility with open geospatial computing ecosystems. However, a deeper analysis revealed that the remaining 40 cases could not be directly utilized for autonomous tool orchestration due to two critical limitations: (1) Coarse Logical Granularity: The original human-designed workflows were organized into high-level logical abstractions—such as "load dataset," "interpolate," or "merge"—rather than fine-grained, executable operations. This lack of atomic granularity makes it difficult for agents to dynamically schedule sub-tasks; and (2) Monolithic Scripting: The accompanying Python code consisted primarily of task-specific monolithic scripts, which lacked the modularity required for decomposition into reusable atomic tools. Consequently, rather than utilizing the original source code, we opted for a complete reconstruction of the analytical logic—a process of structural refactoring detailed in the following section. To better support this transition and enable end-to-end verification, we reshaped the task descriptions by modifying instructions to mandate cartographic outputs and standardizing all file access paths.

为了进一步解决原始基准中的覆盖范围缺陷，特别是在复杂的水文分析场景中，我们系统地引入了来自经典 GIS 教科书的 13 项高难度任务To further address coverage deficiencies in the original benchmark—particularly within complex hydrological analysis scenarios—we introduced 13 high-difficulty tasks systematically sourced from classic GIS textbooks(Tang and Yang, 2012)。这一有针对性的扩展确保 GABench 全面覆盖所有六个核心 GIS 领域，最终形成一套包含 53 项具有明确地理意义的代表性任务。. 5 新颖的计划和反应架构A Novel Plan-and-React Architectures

自主地理空间代理的有效性取决于其底层推理范式如何应对长链逻辑一致性和实时数据不确定性的双重挑战。为了确定专业 GIS 工作流程的最佳推理逻辑，我们系统地评估了三种代表性代理框架——Base Agent、ReAct 和 Plan-and-Solve——以查明它们的基本局限性。基于这些发现，我们引入了计划与反应架构，这是一种新颖的设计，专门用于克服战略规划和执行灵活性之间的权衡。The effectiveness of autonomous geospatial agents hinges on how their underlying reasoning paradigms handle the dual challenge of long-chain logical consistency and real-time data uncertainty. To identify the optimal reasoning logic for professional GIS workflows, we systematically evaluate three representative agent frameworks—Base Agent, ReAct, and Plan-and-Solve—to pinpoint their fundamental limitations. Based on these findings, we introduce the Plan-and-React architecture, a novel design explicitly engineered to overcome the trade-off between strategic planning and execution flexibility.

基础代理充当工具使用评估的基本控制组。该范式为模型配备了标准化的工具定义（模式）和自然语言指令，使其能够感知来自动态执行沙箱的实时执行反馈。然而，它缺乏明确的多步推理或内部错误恢复机制，主要测试模型在零样本场景下的直接工具调度能力。如果没有内部推理循环，基础代理非常容易受到参数幻觉的影响，并且难以在复杂的工作流程中保持逻辑连续性。The Base Agent serves as the fundamental control group for tool-use evaluation. This paradigm equips the model with standardized tool definitions (schemas) and natural language instructions, enabling it to perceive real-time execution feedback from the dynamic execution sandbox. However, it lacks explicit multi-step reasoning or internal error-recovery mechanisms, primarily testing the model’s direct tool-scheduling capabilities in zero-shot scenarios. Without an internal reasoning loop, the Base Agent is highly susceptible to parameter hallucinations and struggles to maintain logical continuity in complex workflows.

反应范式The ReAct paradigm(Yao et al., 2023)通过遵循规范的“思想-行动-观察”循环来改进基础代理。在此架构中，代理在每一步执行本地推理，并根据环境观察动态确定下一步行动。虽然这种方法优先考虑实时响应能力，但它经常遭受长链地理空间任务中的推理漂移的影响。如果没有全局路线图来锚定其决策，代理可能会在深度工具调用序列期间忽视最终目标，从而导致冗余循环或发散的分析路径，最终超出执行限制。improves upon the Base Agent by following a canonical "Thought-Action-Observation" loop. In this architecture, the agent performs local reasoning at each step and dynamically determines the next action based on environmental observations. While this approach prioritizes real-time responsiveness, it frequently suffers from reasoning drift in long-chain geospatial tasks. Without a global roadmap to anchor its decisions, the agent may lose sight of the ultimate objective during deep tool-invocation sequences, leading to redundant loops or divergent analytical paths that eventually exceed execution limits.

计划与解决方法The Plan-and-Solve approach(Wang et al., 2023)通过强调执行前的全局任务分解来解决漂移问题。这种范例要求代理首先生成静态步骤序列作为整个任务的综合路线图。虽然它擅长对复杂的地理空间问题进行逻辑解构，但在遇到不可预见的数据异常时，它会表现出执行刚性。在 GIS 领域，坐标不匹配或拓扑错误很常见，计划和求解的严格“先计划，后执行”逻辑无法从运行时错误中恢复。一旦中间步骤失败，代理就缺乏调整其轨迹的机制，导致计划的其余部分无法使用。addresses the drift issue by emphasizing global task decomposition before execution. This paradigm requires the agent to first generate a static sequence of steps as a comprehensive roadmap for the entire task. While it excels at the logical deconstruction of intricate geospatial problems, it exhibits execution rigidity when encountering unforeseen data anomalies. In the GIS domain, where coordinate mismatches or topological errors are common, the rigid "plan-first, execute-later" logic of Plan-and-Solve fails to recover from runtime errors. Once an intermediate step fails, the agent lacks the mechanism to adjust its trajectory, rendering the remainder of the plan unusable.

我们提出的 Plan-and-React 架构明确旨在弥合这些差距。通过认识到专业 GIS 操作在战略上是严格的，在战术上是不可预测的，该框架在结构上将推理过程分解为两个协同组件：全局任务规划器和逐步反应执行器Our proposed Plan-and-React architecture is explicitly designed to bridge these gaps. By recognizing that professional GIS operations are both strategically rigorous and tactically unpredictable, this framework structurally decouples the reasoning process into two synergistic components: a Global Task Planner and a Step-wise Reactive Executor

Agent 不会过早地与环境交互，而是首先通过 Global Task Planner 充当首席分析师（如图所示）Instead of prematurely interacting with the environment, the agent first acts as a chief analyst through the Global Task Planner (as illustrated in Fig3，“全球规划”）。它理解复杂的自然语言指令，并在调用任何工具之前制定整体分析蓝图。该计划将总体地理问题分解为逻辑上自洽的子任务序列，建立了确定性分析锚点。该蓝图有效防止代理陷入困扰纯 ReAct 代理的无限循环或推理漂移。, ’Global Planning’). It comprehends the complex natural language instruction and formulates a holistic analytical blueprint prior to any tool invocation. This plan decomposes the overarching geographical problem into a logically self-consistent sequence of sub-tasks, establishing a deterministic analytical anchor. This blueprint effectively prevents the agent from falling into the infinite loops or reasoning drift that plague pure ReAct agents.

虽然总体计划提供了战略方向，但我们设计的逐步反应执行器（见图While the overarching plan provides strategic direction, our designed Step-wise Reactive Executor (see Fig3，“逐步执行”）处理多源空间数据的战术不确定性。它通过动态沙箱内的本地化“思想-行动-观察”循环来处理每个计划的子任务。这种设计的核心创新在于其有限的灵活性。如果工具调用失败（例如，由于“拓扑错误”），执行器不会放弃全局目标。相反，它利用执行反馈来自动诊断局部问题（例如，修复自相交几何图形）并在当前步骤的范围内重试执行。, ’Step-wise Execution’) handles the tactical uncertainties of multi-source spatial data. It processes each planned sub-task through a localized "Thought-Action-Observation" loop within the dynamic sandbox. The core innovation of this design lies in its constrained flexibility. If a tool invocation fails—for instance, due to a "Topology Error"—the executor does not abandon the global objective. Instead, it utilizes the execution feedback to autonomously diagnose the localized issue (e.g., repairing self-intersecting geometries) and retries the execution within the confines of the current step.

通过将全局结构指导与本地化、反馈驱动的错误恢复无缝集成，我们的计划与反应框架在逻辑完整性和操作灵活性之间实现了最佳平衡。它使代理能够保持明确的分析目标，同时动态适应多源空间数据的复杂性，从而为下一代自主 GeoAI 系统建立强大的新标准架构。By seamlessly integrating global structural guidance with localized, feedback-driven error recovery, our Plan-and-React framework achieves an optimal balance between logical integrity and operational flexibility. It empowers the agent to maintain a clear analytical goal while dynamically adapting to the complexities of multi-source spatial data, thereby establishing a robust new standard architecture for the next generation of autonomous GeoAI systems.

7 结论Conclusion

在这项研究中，我们提出了 GeoAgentBench (GABench)，这是一种开创性的动态和交互式评估基准，专为地理信息系统 (GIS) 领域的工具增强代理而设计。通过超越传统静态文本和代码匹配范例的限制，GABench 建立了严格的基于执行的框架，该框架将专业级沙箱与一百多个原子 GIS 工具以及各种复杂的多步骤空间分析任务集成在一起。我们引入了利用视觉语言模型（VLM）的多模式评估机制，进一步确保了空间代理的性能不仅根据逻辑编排进行评估，而且还根据最终空间输出的明确准确性和制图质量进行评估。一系列最先进的大型语言模型的实验结果表明，虽然当前的基础模型在高级任务分解方面表现出巨大的潜力，但在复杂的地理空间工作流程中的隐式参数推断和稳健的错误恢复方面仍然存在重大挑战。我们的研究结果强调，全局规划和局部反应式调试之间的协同作用（如规划和反应框架中所体现的那样）对于解决现实世界 GIS 操作的固有不确定性和严格的逻辑依赖性至关重要。随着该领域朝着实现真正的自治GIS迈进，GABench提供了必要的科学基础和标准化的度量系统来指导下一代GeoAI系统的开发。未来的工作将集中于扩展这一基准，以包括更复杂的时空建模和多智能体协作工作流程，进一步缩小通用人工智能和专业地理专业知识之间的差距，从而实现复杂空间问题解决的民主化和自动化。In this study, we have presented GeoAgentBench (GABench), a pioneering dynamic and interactive evaluation benchmark specifically engineered for tool-augmented agents in the domain of Geographic Information Systems (GIS). By transcending the limitations of traditional static text and code-matching paradigms, GABench establishes a rigorous execution-based framework that integrates a professional-grade sandbox with over a hundred atomic GIS tools and a diverse array of complex, multi-step spatial analysis tasks. Our introduction of a multimodal evaluation mechanism leveraging Vision-Language Models (VLMs) further ensures that the performance of spatial agents is assessed not only on logical orchestration but also on the definitive accuracy and cartographic quality of the final spatial outputs. The experimental results across a spectrum of state-of-the-art Large Language Models reveal that while current foundation models exhibit remarkable potential for high-level task decomposition, significant challenges remain in implicit parameter inference and robust error recovery within complex geospatial workflows. Our findings emphasize that the synergy between global planning and local reactive debugging, as embodied in the Plan-and-React framework, is essential for navigating the inherent uncertainties and strict logical dependencies of real-world GIS operations. As the field moves toward the realization of truly Autonomous GIS, GABench provides the necessary scientific foundation and standardized metric system to guide the development of next-generation GeoAI systems. Future work will focus on expanding this benchmark to include more sophisticated spatiotemporal modeling and multi-agent collaborative workflows, further bridging the gap between general artificial intelligence and specialized geographic expertise to democratize and automate complex spatial problem-solving.
