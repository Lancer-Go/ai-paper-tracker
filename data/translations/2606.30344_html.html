早期提示精度塑造了受控提示操作基准中的视觉捷径学习Early Cue Precision Shapes Visual Shortcut Learning in Controlled Cue-Manipulation Benchmarks

Chanho Park Woochan Lee Jangyeong Oh Geongho Gong Minsu Kim Yeachan Kwak Seongim Choi schoi1@gist.ac.kr Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology, Gwangju, Republic of Korea
摘要Abstract

视觉分类器可以实现高匹配分布精度，同时依赖在冲突或抑制下失败的低级线索。我们测试这种失败是否是由Visual classifiers can achieve high matched-distribution accuracy while relying on low-level cues that fail under conflict or suppression. We test whether this failure is shaped by早期提示精度early cue precision：在早期学习或下游探针拟合过程中，低级提示预测标签的可靠性。通过合成形状纹理任务、顺序数字训练、10 级冻结表示审计和基于 CIFAR-10 自然图像的纹理覆盖基准，我们操纵对象纹理匹配概率并评估匹配 ID 准确性、冲突准确性、纹理选择率和抑制行为。降级但可预测的输入不能替代提示去相关。在 10 级数字探针中，冲突准确度从类似机会提示精度下的 0.589 下降到目标完美纹理下的 0.005。在 CIFAR-10 冻结探针中，冲突精度从 0.569 下降到 0.114，而纹理选择从 0.049 上升到 0.855；这种顺序在纹理覆盖强度上仍然存在: the reliability with which a low-level cue predicts the label during early learning or downstream probe fitting. Across synthetic shape-texture tasks, sequential digit training, a 10-class frozen-representation audit, and a CIFAR-10 natural-image-based texture-overlay benchmark, we manipulate object-texture match probability and evaluate matched-ID accuracy, conflict accuracy, texture-choice rate, and suppression behavior. Degraded-but-predictive input does not substitute for cue decorrelation. In 10-class digit probes, conflict accuracy drops from 0.589 under chance-like cue precision to 0.005 under target-perfect texture. In CIFAR-10 frozen probes, conflict accuracy drops from 0.569 to 0.114, while texture choice rises from 0.049 to 0.855; this ordering persists across texture-overlay strengthsα∈{0.15,0.25,0.35,0.50}\alpha\in\{0.15,0.25,0.35,0.50\}。端到端 CIFAR-10 训练表明，低早期提示精度可以改善目标前冲突行为，但快捷方式丰富的微调可以迅速覆盖这种好处。因此，在下游适应过程中必须保持提示去相关，而不是视为一次性接种。. End-to-end CIFAR-10 training shows that low early cue precision improves pre-target conflict behavior, but shortcut-rich fine-tuning can rapidly overwrite this benefit. Cue decorrelation must therefore be maintained during downstream adaptation rather than treated as a one-time inoculation.

1 简介Introduction

这项研究始于生物直觉。感知的预测处理说明强调系统如何在不确定性下衡量感官证据和先前的期望This study began from a biological intuition. Predictive-processing accounts of perception emphasize how systems weight sensory evidence and prior expectations under uncertainty(Sterzer et al., 2018)，对先天性或早期失明的研究激发了关于视觉证据和皮质重组的发育依赖性改变的假设, and work on congenital or early blindness has motivated hypotheses about altered developmental reliance on visual evidence and cortical reorganization(Silverstein et al., 2013; Leivada and Boeckx, 2014; Morgan et al., 2018)。我们不会模拟失明、精神病、精神分裂症或人类神经发育。相反，这种动机引出了一个机器学习问题：鲁棒性是否取决于视觉输入的缺失或退化，或者取决于防止低级视觉线索在学习早期变得过于可靠？. We do not model blindness, psychosis, schizophrenia, or human neurodevelopment. Instead, this motivation leads to a machine-learning question: does robustness depend on the absence or degradation of visual input, or on preventing low-level visual cues from becoming excessively reliable early in learning?

现代视觉识别系统可以在匹配的测试数据上表现良好，同时依赖与任务因果结构不对应的证据。纹理、颜色、局部伪影和背景上下文在训练分布中可能是可靠的，但当对象身份和低级线索解耦时就会失败。这种现象是捷径学习的一个中心例子Modern visual recognition systems can perform well on matched test data while relying on evidence that does not correspond to the causal structure of the task. Texture, color, local artifacts, and background context can be reliable in the training distribution but fail when object identity and low-level cues are decoupled. This phenomenon is a central instance of shortcut learning(Geirhos et al., 2020)。纹理形状冲突研究表明，ImageNet 训练的卷积网络可以更喜欢纹理而不是全局形状，并且增加形状偏差可以提高鲁棒性. Texture-shape conflict studies have shown that ImageNet-trained convolutional networks can prefer texture over global shape, and that increasing shape bias can improve robustness(Geirhos et al., 2019)。关于数据集偏差、非鲁棒特征、虚假相关性、常见损坏、自然对抗性示例和偏差控制对象识别的相关工作进一步表明，高基准精度并不能保证在改变的证据下的稳定识别. Related work on dataset bias, non-robust features, spurious correlations, common corruptions, natural adversarial examples, and bias-controlled object recognition further shows that high benchmark accuracy does not guarantee stable recognition under altered evidence(Torralba and Efros, 2011; Ilyas et al., 2019; Sagawa et al., 2020; Hendrycks and Dietterich, 2019; Hendrycks et al., 2021; Barbu et al., 2019).

一种解释是架构性的：卷积网络是纹理偏向的，而变压器、自监督模型或视觉语言编码器可能更注重形状。架构和预训练很重要One interpretation is architectural: convolutional networks are texture-biased, whereas transformers, self-supervised models, or vision-language encoders may be more shape-oriented. Architecture and pretraining do matter(Dosovitskiy et al., 2021; Liu et al., 2022, 2021; Radford et al., 2021; Oquab et al., 2024)。然而，捷径获取也是一个学习历史问题。如果低级线索在训练早期或下游探针拟合期间可靠地预测了标签，则许多模型类可以学习一条决策规则，将该线索视为信息丰富的。相反，如果相同的提示是可见的但不可靠，或者如果在提示变得具有预测性之前结构信息被优先考虑，那么以后的决策可能会更少依赖捷径。. However, shortcut acquisition is also a learning-history problem. If a low-level cue reliably predicts the label early in training or during downstream probe fitting, many model classes may learn a decision rule that treats that cue as highly informative. Conversely, if the same cue is visible but unreliable, or if structural information is privileged before the cue becomes predictive, later decisions may rely less on the shortcut.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 捷径学习和纹理偏差Shortcut learning and texture bias

纹理形状提示冲突范式将图像分类器中的对象形状响应与纹理响应分开The texture-shape cue-conflict paradigm separated object shape responses from texture responses in image classifiers(Geirhos et al., 2019)。随后的工作将此类失败归结为捷径学习：模型使用的规则在训练分布下具有预测性，但在转变下不稳定. Subsequent work framed such failures as shortcut learning: a model uses a rule that is predictive under the training distribution but unstable under shift(Geirhos et al., 2020)。对纹理偏差起源的分析表明数据和增强与架构一样重要. Analyses of texture-bias origins suggest that data and augmentation can be as important as architecture(Hermann et al., 2020)。这支持我们对训练线索的可靠性和时机的关注。. This supports our focus on the reliability and timing of training cues.

捷径学习还与虚假相关性和群体稳健性有关。分布鲁棒优化、最后一层再训练和相关方法表明，学习表示和下游分类器可以发挥独立的作用Shortcut learning also connects to spurious correlations and group robustness. Distributionally robust optimization, last-layer retraining, and related methods show that learned representations and downstream classifiers can play separable roles(Sagawa et al., 2020; Kirichenko et al., 2023)。我们的冻结表示审核询问将快捷分类器放置在固定表示上的容易程度，而我们的端到端实验则测试早期线索可靠性是否会改变训练期间学到的决策规则。. Our frozen-representation audits ask how easily a shortcut classifier can be placed on a fixed representation, while our end-to-end experiments test whether early cue reliability changes learned decision rules during training.

2.2 架构、表示和评估Architecture, representation, and evaluation

视觉变形金刚Vision Transformers(Dosovitskiy et al., 2021)，现代卷积网络, modern ConvNets(Liu et al., 2022), 分层变压器, hierarchical transformers(Liu et al., 2021), 自监督特征, self-supervised features(Oquab et al., 2024)和语言监督的视觉编码器, and language-supervised vision encoders(Radford et al., 2021; Cherti et al., 2023)归纳偏差和预训练规模有所不同。因此，我们避免将 CNN 视为唯一的研究对象。我们的审核包括原始像素、随机基线、ResNet、ConvNeXt、ViT、Swin、DINOv2 和 OpenCLIP。问题不在于哪种模型普遍最好，而在于提示精度是否可以预测模型族内的捷径依赖。differ in inductive bias and pretraining scale. We therefore avoid treating CNNs as the only object of study. Our audits include raw pixels, random baselines, ResNet, ConvNeXt, ViT, Swin, DINOv2, and OpenCLIP. The question is not which model is universally best, but whether cue precision predicts shortcut reliance within model families.

当匹配的测试集保留快捷相关性时，仅匹配准确度是不够的。因此，我们优先考虑冲突准确性和纹理选择率，并使用预期校准误差作为辅助诊断Matched accuracy alone is insufficient when the matched test set preserves the shortcut correlation. We therefore prioritize conflict accuracy and texture-choice rate, and use expected calibration error as a secondary diagnostic(Guo et al., 2017)。由于提示冲突测试可以简化特征依赖，因此我们添加了抑制视图：纹理抑制输入在覆盖提示减少时测试对象识别；对象抑制输入测试纹理是否单独驱动纹理类预测。. Because cue-conflict tests can simplify feature reliance, we add suppression views: texture-suppressed inputs test object recognition when the overlay cue is reduced; object-suppressed inputs test whether texture alone drives texture-class predictions.

4 实验Experiments

4.1 研究 1：综合结构和去相关课程Study 1: Synthetic Structural and Decorrelation Curricula

4.1.1 设置Setup

我们首先使用了具有四种形状和四种纹理的合成形状纹理任务。目标标签是形状。训练不同的纹理形状匹配概率。我们比较了：正常训练、降级但预测输入、轮廓优先、掩模优先、边缘优先、混合结构优先、随机纹理优先和目标计划控制。模型是 SmallCNN 和 MiniViT。We first used a synthetic shape-texture task with four shapes and four textures. The target label was shape. Training varied texture-shape match probability. We compared: normal training, degraded-but-predictive input, silhouette-first, mask-first, edge-first, mixed-structural-first, random-texture-first, and a target-schedule control. Models were SmallCNN and MiniViT.

4.1.2 结果Results

两种架构中都出现了强大的捷径学习。在高捷径条件下，SmallCNN 正常训练达到了 0.894 的匹配 ID 准确率，但只有 0.056 的冲突准确率和 0.879 的纹理选择准确率。随机纹理优先达到 0.877 匹配 ID 精度、0.812 冲突精度和 0.102 纹理选择。 MiniViT 正常训练达到了 1.000 匹配 ID 精度，但只有 0.038 冲突精度和 0.937 纹理选择，而混合结构优先达到 0.643 冲突精度和 0.128 纹理选择。Strong shortcut learning emerged in both architectures. In the high-shortcut condition, SmallCNN normal training reached 0.894 matched-ID accuracy but only 0.056 conflict accuracy and 0.879 texture choice. Random-texture-first reached 0.877 matched-ID accuracy, 0.812 conflict accuracy, and 0.102 texture choice. MiniViT normal training reached 1.000 matched-ID accuracy but only 0.038 conflict accuracy and 0.937 texture choice, whereas mixed-structural-first reached 0.643 conflict accuracy and 0.128 texture choice.

这些实验证实了两点。快捷方式学习并不是 CNN 所特有的，视觉退化并不等同于降低快捷方式提示的预测精度。These experiments establish two points. Shortcut learning is not specific to CNNs, and visual degradation is not equivalent to reducing the predictive precision of a shortcut cue.

6 讨论Discussion

在受控合成任务、顺序数字学习、冻结表示审核和基于 CIFAR-10 自然图像的纹理覆盖中，出现了相同的定性模式。当纹理早期具有高度预测性时，模型可以实现较高的 ID 匹配精度，但在冲突和抑制下通常会失败。当相同的线索可见但预测性较差时，冲突行为就会改善。降级但可预测的输入还不够：如果低级提示仍然可以预测标签，模型通常会学习捷径。Across controlled synthetic tasks, sequential digit learning, frozen representation audits, and CIFAR-10 natural-image-based texture overlays, the same qualitative pattern appears. When texture is highly predictive early, models achieve high matched-ID accuracy but often fail under conflict and suppression. When the same cue is visible but less predictive, conflict behavior improves. Degraded-but-predictive input is not enough: if the low-level cue still predicts the label, models often learn the shortcut.

这些结果支持捷径学习的学习历史观点。中心问题不是线索是否存在，也不是架构是否是卷积的，而是线索在决策规则形成的阶段是否获得过度的可靠性。该观点与强调数据和增强作为纹理偏差来源的先前工作兼容These results support a learning-history view of shortcut learning. The central issue is not whether a cue is present, nor whether an architecture is convolutional, but whether a cue gains excessive reliability during the phase in which a decision rule forms. This view is compatible with prior work emphasizing data and augmentation as sources of texture bias(Hermann et al., 2020)。它还阐明了为什么简单的降级可能会失败：降级会改变视觉质量，但可能会使快捷方式的标签预测性完好无损。. It also clarifies why simple degradation can fail: degradation changes visual quality but may leave the shortcut’s label predictiveness intact.

端到端 CIFAR-10 结果尤其重要，因为它限制了索赔。低早期提示精度会减少或延迟目标偏向暴露之前对捷径的依赖，但捷径丰富的微调可以迅速覆盖这种好处。因此，线索去相关不应被视为一次性接种。强大的下游适应可能需要在整个微调过程中保持捷径预测性受控，或者将线索去相关与保留对象结构信息的目标结合起来。The end-to-end CIFAR-10 result is especially important because it limits the claim. Low early cue precision reduces or delays shortcut reliance before target-biased exposure, but shortcut-rich fine-tuning can rapidly overwrite the benefit. Thus, cue decorrelation should not be treated as a one-time inoculation. Robust downstream adaptation may require keeping shortcut predictiveness controlled throughout fine-tuning, or combining cue decorrelation with objectives that preserve object-structural information.

7 结论Conclusion

我们的受控基准支持视觉捷径学习的早期提示精度说明。早期具有高度预测性的低级纹理线索会导致较高的匹配 ID 准确性，但冲突和抑制行为较差。提示去相关比降级但可预测的输入更有效地减少或延迟快捷方式依赖，但其好处受到表示质量的限制，并且可以被以后的快捷方式丰富的微调所覆盖。这些结论有意仅限于受控提示操作：CIFAR-10 研究使用人工纹理覆盖，冻结探针审核测量固定表示上的下游分类器行为，而不是编码器的原始预训练历史。因此，鲁棒的视觉学习应该在早期学习和下游适应过程中控制快捷线索的可靠性，而不仅仅是改变架构或降低视觉输入质量。Our controlled benchmarks support an early cue-precision account of visual shortcut learning. Low-level texture cues that are highly predictive early lead to high matched-ID accuracy but poor conflict and suppression behavior. Cue decorrelation reduces or delays shortcut reliance more effectively than degraded-but-predictive input, but the benefit is bounded by representation quality and can be overwritten by later shortcut-rich fine-tuning. These conclusions are intentionally limited to controlled cue manipulations: the CIFAR-10 study uses artificial texture overlays, and frozen-probe audits measure downstream classifier behavior on fixed representations rather than the original pretraining histories of the encoders. Robust visual learning should therefore control the reliability of shortcut cues during both early learning and downstream adaptation, not only alter architecture or degrade visual input quality.
