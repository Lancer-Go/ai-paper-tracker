摘要Abstract

在长期操纵中桥接基于模型的控制和学习策略存在着一种无声的分歧：控制执行指定的目标，学习将该行为摊销为反应性策略，但现有协议丢弃任务语义，留下手工制作的奖励和偏离控制验证的行为。 我们引入语义统一 (SUN) 程序，这是类型化的可执行文件，其中几何和联系关系定义一次，然后编译成一致的模型预测控制 (MPC) 成本、满意度谓词、强化学习奖励、转换防护和诊断。 我们的夸父系统由大型视觉语言系统驱动，自动从语言和场景语义合成SUN程序，通过MPC筛选可行性，并在训练阶段条件策略的同时保留语义。在九项任务中，夸父取得了 82.03% 的宏观成功率，优于稀疏奖励 (35.67%) 和 Stage-BC (24.75%) 基线。在 8192 路规模下，它每小时生成的成功轨迹时间是人类远程操作的 10.57 倍。每个任务有 500 条轨迹，夸父数据训练 DP3 策略的模拟成功率达到 46.0%（替代方案为 22.4%），在物理 Franka 和 Kinova 机器人上达到 34.7%。这些结果表明，模拟筛选的任务语义可以有效地将控制分摊为稳健的策略，无需演示或手动密集奖励，统一符号规划和数据驱动的执行。Bridging model-based control and learned policies in long-horizon manipulation has harbored a silent disagreement: control executes specified objectives, learning amortizes that behavior into a reactive policy, yet existing protocols discard task semantics, leaving rewards hand-crafted and behavior drifting from what control verified. We introduce Semantically UNified (SUN) Programs, typed executables where geometric and contact relations are defined once and compiled into aligned Model Predictive Control (MPC) costs, satisfaction predicates, RL rewards, transition guards, and diagnostics. Our system, Kuafu, driven by large vision language systems, automatically synthesizes SUN Programs from language and scene semantics, screens feasibility via MPC, and retains semantics while training stage-conditioned policies. Across nine tasks, Kuafu achieves 82.03% macro-success, outperforming sparse-reward (35.67%) and Stage-BC (24.75%) baselines. At 8192-way scale, it generates 10.57× the successful trajectory time per hour of human teleoperation. With 500 trajectories per task, Kuafu data trains DP3 policies to 46.0% simulation success (vs. 22.4% for alternatives) and 34.7% on physical Franka and Kinova robots. These results establish that simulation-screened task semantics can effectively amortize control into robust policies, without demonstrations or manual dense rewards, unifying symbolic planning and data-driven execution.

I 简介Introduction

强化学习（RL）是一种有吸引力的多阶段操纵方法，因为它可以直接从交互中学习高维、接触丰富的行为，而不需要底层动力学的精确模型Reinforcement learning (RL) is an attractive method for multi-stage manipulation because it can learn high-dimensional, contact-rich behaviors directly from interaction without requiring an accurate model of the underlying dynamics[32]。 然而，强化学习需要密集的、结构化的奖励来揭示中间进度，而手动制作这些信号既费力又脆弱。对奖励条件或转换阈值的微小调整通常会产生截然不同的行为，需要大量的试错调整。 虽然最近的方法自动生成奖励. However, RL requires dense, structured rewards to expose intermediate progress, yet manually crafting these signals is laborious and brittle. Minor adjustments to reward terms or transition thresholds often yield drastically different behaviors, demanding extensive trial-and-error tuning. While recent methods automate reward generation[13]，它们遭受严重的延迟：候选人奖励的影响只能在全面的政策培训和推出后才能评估。 因此，每次迭代修订都会产生训练周期的全部成本，从而导致奖励结构的搜索效率低下。, they suffer from a critical latency: the impact of a candidate reward can only be evaluated after full policy training and rollout. Consequently, each iterative revision incurs the full cost of a training cycle, rendering the search over reward structures inefficient.

基于模型的规划和控制显式编码任务结构，定义几何目标和转换逻辑，以便在闭环动态下进行测试和修订Model-based planning and control explicitly encode task structures, defining geometric objectives and transition logic to enable testing and revision under closed-loop dynamics[24, 16]。 然而，这种方法对模型保真度高度敏感；成功的执行依赖于准确的状态重建、精确的交互模型以及严格遵守任务定义。 在实践中，即使很小的差异，例如模型不匹配、观测噪声或规格错误，也可能导致控制器故障。此外，构建这些强大的配方需要针对每项新任务进行大量的手动工程. However, this approach is highly sensitive to model fidelity; successful execution relies on accurate state reconstruction, precise interaction models, and strict adherence to task definitions. In practice, even minor discrepancies, such as model mismatch, observation noise, or specification errors, can cause controller failure. Furthermore, constructing these robust formulations demands substantial manual engineering for every new task[4].

这些互补的特性激发了人们努力连接基于模型的控制和学习。 最突出的是，基于模型的控制器用于生成政策训练的成功轨迹These complementary properties have motivated efforts to bridge model-based control and learning. Most prominently, model-based controllers are used to generate successful trajectories for policy training[11, 4]。 虽然这转移了物理基础的行为，但它放弃了定义的目标、约束、阶段转换或满意度边界. Although recent methods expose richer task structure to control and learning[18, 8]，这些表示通常被重新塑造为不同的学习信号，迫使阶段或完成逻辑被重新实现。 因此，指导策略的奖励可能会偏离通过控制验证的规范，从而导致只有在部署后才会出现不一致的情况。, the representations are often recast as distinct learning signals, forcing stage or completion logic to be re-implemented. As a result, the rewards guiding the policy may drift from the specification validated by control, allowing inconsistencies to surface only after deployment.

可执行任务表示。Executable Task Representations.长视野操作需要将离散任务进展与连续运动联系起来的表示。 符号规划和程序生成将动作序列与几何可行性或场景基础结合起来Long-horizon manipulation requires representations connecting discrete task progression with continuous motion. Symbolic planning and program generation couple action sequences to geometric feasibility or scene grounding[24, 22]。 基于语言的优化将任务描述转换为可组合的 3D 价值图或由 MPC 评估的奖励参数，将空间目标暴露给闭环控制. Language-grounded optimization converts task descriptions into composable 3D value maps or reward parameters evaluated by MPC, exposing spatial objectives to closed-loop control[9, 28]。 形式方法将时间进展编码为有限状态奖励或信号时间逻辑约束，用于学习和预测控制. Formal approaches encode temporal progress as finite-state rewards or signal-temporal-logic constraints for learning and predictive control[23, 17]。 虽然这些表示限制了搜索和可操作的任务进度，但它们通常仍然局限于单个规划、控制或学习阶段，破坏了整个管道的语义连续性。 因此，一个组件接受的状态可能仍然会产生不可行的改进、过早的切换或误导性的学习信号，这些信号在长期执行中会加剧。. While these representations constrain search and operationalize task progress, they generally remain confined to a single planning, control, or learning stage, breaking semantic continuity across the pipeline. Consequently, a state accepted by one component may still produce infeasible refinements, premature handoffs, or misleading learning signals that compound over long-horizon execution.

Since iterated objectives can overfit observed behavior or encode invalid task specifications, a high return therefor may not translate into strong performance on the true task metric[2]。 作为补充，优化的轨迹提供有监督的政策目标，而残差学习保留名义控制并学习纠正措施. III-B Program Validation and Acceptance

Independent task success. Because the same stage monitor is retained during learning and data generation, its completion logic must be checked against an independent criterion. For each task, we introduce an external evaluator gextg_{\text{ext}}, manually authored independently of SUN Program formation and frozen prior to synthesis. Crucially, gextg_{\text{ext}} is never exposed to the LLM, VLM, SUN Program, or the learned policy. Its binary outcome provides the sparse terminal signal used for residual learning, acceptance, calibration, and final evaluation. In parallel, the SUN Program independently declares completion through its internal predicate DKD_{K}. A false completion occurs when the SUN monitor declares the final stage complete while gextg_{\text{ext}} reports that the required physical outcome has not been achieved. This separation supplies the independent acceptance criterion used during rollouts.

V 结论Conclusions

我们提出了 Kuafu，一个从控制到学习的框架，它将 MPC 筛选的语义统一 (SUN) 程序保留为持久的、可执行的任务工件以及学习策略。 在三个独立的谱系中，夸父平均实现了 79.43% 的任务宏成功率，展示了多阶段操作任务的端到端鲁棒性。 在我们的评估中，完全切换成功率达到 82.03%，显着优于稀疏奖励基线 (35.67%) 和替代学习器；消融研究进一步分离了已验证的行为先验和有界残差校正的独特贡献。 利用 SUN 计划对数据生成的监督，夸父轨迹在固定量 DP3 训练中取得了 46.02% 的成功率，支持 8 个单任务视觉架构和 3 个多任务 VLA，并在 106 次物理试验中实现了零样本迁移。 结果确立了一个关键原则：即使在行为被分摊到学习策略之后，通过控制验证的任务语义仍然有效。We presented Kuafu, a control-to-learning framework that preserves an MPC-screened Semantically UNified (SUN) Program as a persistent, executable task artifact alongside a learned policy. Across three independent lineages, Kuafu achieves an average 79.43% task-macro success rate, demonstrating end-to-end robustness on multi-stage manipulation tasks. In our evaluation, the complete handoff reaches 82.03% success, significantly outperforming the sparse-reward baseline (35.67%) and alternative learners; ablation studies further isolate the distinct contributions of the verified behavioral prior and bounded residual correction. Leveraging the SUN Program’s supervision in data generation, Kuafu trajectories yield 46.02% success in fixed-volume DP3 training, support eight single-task visual architectures and three multi-task VLAs, and enable zero-shot transfer across 106 physical trials. Results establish a key principle: task semantics validated through control remain effective even after behavior is amortized into a learned policy.

范围和限制。Scope and limitations.SUN 程序依赖于已注册的场景接口，其中包含信息丰富的对象框架和轴，以及有限的类型运算符库。因此，如果不定义新的算子，夸父就无法处理可变形的物体或流体。 此外，我们的评估不涵盖保留的指令、运算符、场景语义或任务组合，限制了开放世界泛化的强烈主张。 从方法上讲，舞台监听器单调前进，并且在物理回归后无法回滚。 此外，Q95 指标与 MPC 相关，针对特定规划器衡量效率，而不是绝对轨迹质量。最后，验证（6 项任务，106 次试验）在测试的机器人夹具配置中建立了零样本可行性，但不能保证非结构化环境中广泛的模拟到真实的可靠性。SUN Programs rely on a registered scene interface with informative object frames and axes, as well as a finite, typed-operator library. Consequently, Kuafu cannot handle deformable objects or fluids without defining new operators. Furthermore, our evaluation does not cover held-out instructions, operators, scene semantics, or task compositions, limiting strong claims of open-world generalization. Methodologically, the stage monitor advances monotonically and cannot rollback after a physical regression. Additionally, the Q95 metric is MPC-relative, measuring efficiency against a specific planner rather than absolute trajectory quality. Finally, the validation (6 task, 106 trials) establishes zero-shot feasibility across the tested robot-gripper configurations but does not guarantee broad sim-to-real reliability in unstructured environments.
