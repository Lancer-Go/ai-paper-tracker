1 简介Introduction

视觉语言模型 (VLM)Vision-language models (VLMs)[25, 18, 22, 20, 8]在指令跟随和基础推理方面取得了快速进展，在字幕、视觉问答和多模式对话方面表现出色。随着 VLM 变得更加代理（通过扩展交换进行规划和行动），评估协议不仅越来越需要衡量正确性，还需要衡量交互策略：模型如何收集信息、维护状态以及在不确定性下进行适应。还有许多基准have advanced rapidly in instruction following and grounded reasoning, enabling strong performance on captioning, visual question answering, and multimodal dialogue. As VLMs become more agentic (planning and acting through extended exchanges), evaluation protocols increasingly need to measure not only correctness, but also interaction policies: how models gather information, maintain state, and adapt under uncertainty. Yet many benchmarks[5, 16, 28, 14, 30]保持静态和短视野，通常在给定一张图像（或一小部分固定集）的情况下对一次性答案进行评分，随着时间的推移，对策略、记忆和信念修正的可见性有限。remain static and short-horizon, typically scoring one-shot answers given one image (or a small fixed set), with limited visibility into strategy, memory, and belief revision over time.

我们研究了一个互补的设置：图像集合上的交互式隐藏目标识别。用户（或预言机）私下从视觉上相似的候选图像库中选择目标图像，模型必须通过询问有关可观察属性的受限问题来发现目标。这种表述提出了在单轮评估中通常不可见的代理挑战：选择有效减少歧义的问题，保留和应用没有漂移的约束，以及执行依赖于不断发展的候选集而不是孤立的任何单个图像的跨图像比较。We study a complementary setting: interactive hidden-target identification over image collections. A user (or oracle) privately selects a target image from a gallery of visually similar candidates, and the model must discover the target by asking constrained questions about observable attributes. This formulation surfaces agentic challenges that are often invisible in single-turn evaluation: choosing questions that efficiently reduce ambiguity, preserving and applying constraints without drift, and performing cross-image comparisons that depend on the evolving candidate set rather than any single image in isolation.

为了实施此设置，我们推出了 AMIGO，这是一个以简单但诊断循环为中心的交互式基准测试。该模型每轮只问一个二元问题，接收是/否/不确定反馈，并迭代缩小可行的候选集。如果模型违反协议规则，预言机将返回To operationalize this setting, we present AMIGO, an interactive benchmark centered on a simple but diagnostic loop. The model asks exactly one binary question per turn, receives Yes/No/Unsure feedback, and iteratively narrows the feasible candidate set. If the model violates protocol rules, the oracle returns跳过Skip，不透露任何信息。这使得不合规情况变得可衡量，并将浪费的互动与证据驱动的进展区分开来。 AMIGO还允许偶尔出现预言机不一致的情况，以测试模型是否检测到矛盾并寻求验证，而不是过早提交。, revealing no information. This makes non-compliance measurable and separates wasted interaction from evidence-driven progress. AMIGO additionally allows occasional oracle inconsistencies to test whether models detect contradictions and seek verification instead of committing prematurely.

In contrast, AMIGO evaluates whether an agentic VLM can acquire the missing information needed to solve the task: selecting informative questions over multiple turns, maintaining and revising a belief state over a candidate pool, and enforcing consistency under a constrained interaction protocol to identify a hidden target image.

3.2 数据集收集Dataset collection

来源和策展。Source and curation.

我们从塔吉特的在线时尚目录中收集服装图像，并对它们进行重复数据删除，以确保每件产品都是独一无二的。从历史产品数据库中提取的属性元数据通常会丢失，各个品牌的定义不一致，有时还包含错误或不相关的值。因此，我们优先考虑可视觉验证的属性，并构建半自动管道来大规模生产标准化标签。We collect dress images from Target’s online fashion catalog and deduplicate them to ensure each product is unique. Attribute metadata extracted from the historical product database is often missing, defined inconsistently across brands, and sometimes contains erroneous or irrelevant values. 8 强化学习轨迹集合Reinforcement Learning Trajectories Collection

AMIGO 还可以作为多模态强化学习的轨迹收集框架。每个步骤都提供了一个明确定义的状态（图库、对话上下文、累积的约束）、一个动作（一个受约束的问题）和一个观察（是/否/不确定/跳过反馈，可选的噪声），以及自然的长期目标。该结构支持无需额外注释的密集奖励，例如，对无效问题的惩罚（跳过）、基于可行集大小或估计信息增益减少的进度奖励，以及仅在唯一确定目标时正确识别的终端奖励。这些轨迹可以支持离线强化学习、对替代问题的偏好优化以及不完美反馈下的鲁棒性训练。AMIGO can also serve as a trajectory collection framework for multimodal reinforcement learning. Each step provides a well-defined state (gallery, dialogue context, accumulated constraints), an action (a constrained question), and an observation (Yes/No/Unsure/Skip feedback, optionally noisy), with a naturally long-horizon objective. This structure supports dense rewards without additional annotation, e.g., penalties for invalid questions (Skip), progress rewards based on reduction in feasible set size or estimated information gain, and terminal rewards for correct identification only when the target is uniquely determined. These trajectories can support offline RL, preference optimization over alternative questions, and robustness training under imperfect feedback.
