摘要Abstract

虽然多代理系统 (MAS) 使大型语言模型能够通过协作交互来处理复杂的推理任务，但由于计算图的离散、不可微性质和全局监控信号的稀疏性，优化其动态仍然是一项艰巨的挑战。 现有的黑盒优化器很难将轨迹级故障归因于特定的局部组件，从而导致低效、高方差的探索。 我们认为易于处理的 MAS 优化需要While Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) empower Large Language Models to tackle complex reasoning tasks through collaborative interaction, optimizing their dynamics remains a formidable challenge due to the discrete, non-differentiable nature of the computation graph and the sparsity of global supervisory signals. Existing black-box optimizers struggle to attribute trajectory-level failure to specific local components, resulting in inefficient, high-variance exploration. We argue that tractable MAS optimization needs结构性归纳偏差structural inductive biases解开错误信号。 我们建议to disentangle error signals. We propose时间和结构学分分配temporal and structural credit assignment，它沿两个轴分解目标：(i), which decomposes the objective along two axes: (i)时间信用temporal credit，使用状态空间瓶颈来识别关键回合，以及 (ii), using state-space bottlenecks to identify critical rounds, and (ii)结构性信贷structural credit，使用固定角色策略来隔离代理贡献。 利用这些分解的信号，我们引入了离散的、语言化的信号, using stationary role policies to isolate agent contributions. Leveraging these decomposed signals, we introduce a discrete, verbalized块坐标下降block coordinate descent迭代细化算法。 它不是不加区别地进行全局更新，而是在优化角色提示和聚合协议之间交替，使用 LLM 生成的“代理梯度”仅针对已识别的薄弱环节。 在不同的推理基准中，我们的方法大大降低了查询复杂性，同时提高了性能，为自我改进 MAS 提供了一条有原则且可解释的路径。algorithm for iterative refinement. Rather than indiscriminate global updates, it alternates between optimizing role prompts and aggregation protocols, using LLM-generated “proxy gradients” to target only the identified weak links. Across diverse reasoning benchmarks, our approach substantially reduces query complexity while improving performance, providing a principled and interpretable path toward self-improving MAS.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 已从静态文本生成器发展为动态推理引擎，在数学推理、代码生成和复杂规划方面取得了显着的成功Large Language Models (LLMs) have evolved from static text generators into dynamic reasoning engines, achieving remarkable success in mathematical reasoning, code generation, and complex planning[NEURIPS2020_1457c0d6, 10.5555/3600270.3602070]。 为了超越单模型推理的局限性，最近的研究已转向 MAS，其中专业代理通过迭代批评和修订周期进行协作. To transcend the limitations of single-model inference, recent research has shifted toward MAS, where specialized agents collaborate via iterative critique-and-revision cycles[10.5555/3692070.3694667, zhang2025reinforcement]。 通过将任务分配给不同的角色（例如提议者、辩论者、综合者），MAS 框架理论上可以解决单个整体模型难以解决的问题. By distributing tasks across diverse roles (e.g., proposer, debater, synthesizer), MAS frameworks can theoretically solve problems that are intractable for a single monolithic model[li-etal-2024-improving-multi].

然而，优化 MAS 的交互动态面临着巨大的挑战。 从优化的角度来看，基于 LLM 的 MAS 作为离散、不可微的计算图运行。 与反向传播将误差精确归因于特定权重的神经网络训练不同，MAS 优化受到以下问题的影响：However, optimizing the interaction dynamics of MAS presents a formidable challenge. From an optimization perspective, an LLM-based MAS operates as a discrete, non-differentiable computation graph. Unlike neural network training where backpropagation precisely attributes error to specific weights, MAS optimization suffers from the信用分配问题（CAP）Credit Assignment Problem (CAP)最严重的形式：监督信号通常是稀疏的（最终状态下的单个标量分数）和全局的（应用于整个轨迹）in its most severe form: the supervisory signal is typically sparse (a single scalar score at the terminal state) and global (applying to the entire trajectory)[SuttonBarto2018, Foerster2018COMA]。 现有方法通常将整个系统视为黑匣子. Existing approaches often treat the entire system as a black box[deng2022rlprompt, fernando2023promptbreeder, guo2023connecting]，应用无导数优化或启发式聚合, applying derivative-free optimization or heuristic aggregation[10.5555/3709347.3743784, ai2025majorityvotingllmaggregation].

虽然这些方法对简单的链有效，但在复杂的多轮协作中却很困难，因为它们忽略了推理过程的内部因果结构。 优化系统While effective for simple chains, these methods struggle in complex multi-round collaborations because they ignore the internal causal structure of the reasoning process. Optimizing a system withNN代理结束agents overRR回合本质上涉及在空间中进行搜索rounds essentially involves searching in a space of𝒪​(N×R)\mathcal{O}(N\times R)耦合提示。如果没有结构先验，这将面临灾难性的方差和低效的探索coupled prompts. Without structural priors, this faces catastrophic variance and inefficient exploration[zhou2024batch].

在这项工作中，我们认为使 MAS 优化易于处理需要强加In this work, we argue that making MAS optimization tractable requires imposing结构性限制structural constraints在计算图上解开误差信号。 我们提出了两种原则性的松弛，将混沌交互图转变为结构化的优化景观。on the computation graph to disentangle the error signal. We propose two principled relaxations that transform the chaotic interaction graph into a structured optimization landscape.

首先，通过聚合实现状态空间瓶颈。 在完全连接的 MAS 中，信息分散流动，因此无法精确定位First, state-space bottleneck via aggregation. This effectively models the interaction as a Markov Decision Process (MDP), where the aggregator creates a “state bottleneck”[puterman1994mdp, mnih2015dqn]。 这种架构选择不仅具有功能性，而且具有实用性。这是一个先决条件. 其次，通过参数共享实现固定策略Second, stationary policy via parameter sharing[GuptaEgorovKochenderfer2017, YuVeluVinitsky2022PPO]。 在每一轮中优化每个代理的独特提示会导致参数爆炸。 我们强加一个. 4.1 状态空间瓶颈State-Space Bottleneck

在 MAS 中，最终分数提供单一全局优化信号。 要更新提示，该信号必须表示为文本渐变。 这种反馈是否可行取决于状态空间瓶颈的可用性： 一种明确的共享状态，可以集中而不是分散信息流。In MAS, the terminal score provides a single global optimization signal. To update prompts, this signal must be expressed as a textual gradient. Whether such feedback can be made actionable depends on the availability of a state-space bottleneck: an explicit shared state that concentrates, rather than disperses, the information flow.

没有聚合。Without aggregation.

6 结束语Closing Remarks

在本文中，我们介绍了一种测试时时间和结构学分分配框架，该框架可沿回合和角色分解多代理 LLM 轨迹，从而在不改变推理动态或模型参数的情况下实现组件级归因。 在整个实验中，产生的信用信号揭示了明显的贡献不平衡，并确定了持续限制结果的一小部分阶段和代理。 利用这些信号，信用引导的即时优化将更新重点放在薄弱组件上，并在基准和模型系列中提供可靠的收益，减少浪费的全局更改，同时提高有效性。In this paper, we introduce a test-time temporal and structural credit assignment framework that decomposes multi-agent LLM trajectories along rounds and roles, enabling component-level attribution without altering inference dynamics or model parameters. Across the experiments, the resulting credit signals reveal pronounced contribution imbalance and identify the small set of stages and agents that consistently constrain outcomes. Leveraging these signals, credit-guided prompt optimization focuses updates on weak components and delivers reliable gains across benchmarks and model families, reducing wasteful global changes while improving effectiveness.

这些发现将时间和结构归因定位为多智能体推理系统的实用诊断和优化工具。 与此同时，该框架仍然局限于已完成的轨迹并依赖于评估信号；对于较长的时间范围，时间归因的计算量可能很大；当前实例化采用集中聚合的固定角色。未来的方向包括将信用信号整合到在线或推理时间适应中，通过自我一致性或共识批评将归因扩展到弱监督环境，以及将该框架应用于更动态的协调机制，例如分层、工具增强或开放式环境。These findings position temporal and structural attribution as a practical diagnostic and optimization tool for multi-agent reasoning systems. At the same time, the framework remains limited to completed trajectories and relies on evaluative signals; temporal attribution can be computationally heavy for long horizons; and the current instantiation assumes fixed roles with centralized aggregation. Future directions include integrating credit signals into online or inference-time adaptation, extending attribution to weakly supervised settings via self-consistency or consensus critics, and applying the framework to more dynamic coordination regimes such as hierarchical, tool-augmented, or open-ended environments.
