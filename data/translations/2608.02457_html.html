语法与语义的结合：理解科学公式Syntax Meets Semantics: Understanding Scientific Formulae

Yuni Susanti FIZ KarlsruheBerlinGermany yuni.susanti@fiz-karlsruhe.de and Moritz Schubotz FIZ KarlsruheBerlinGermany moritz.schubotz@fiz-karlsruhe.de
摘要。Abstract.

科学公式是学术交流的基本组成部分，但它们具有双重性——结构化Scientific formulae are a fundamental component of scholarly communication, yet their dual nature – as structured语法syntax和载体and carriers of语义semantics– 学术信息检索方面仍未得到充分探索。尽管先前的研究表明，联合建模句法和语义模态可以提高检索性能，但它们的底层表示之间的关系尚未得到系统的研究。在这项工作中，我们实证研究了公式语法和语义之间的跨模态对应关系。我们发现，尽管潜在相关性很强，但它们的原生表示空间表现出极弱的可观察对应性，这表明两种模式之间存在严重的表示不匹配。我们进一步评估是否可以使用标准表示学习和对齐技术来减少这种不匹配。我们使用基于图的编码器来表示句法结构，并使用基于文本的编码器来表示语义信息，然后应用对比学习来引入共享的表示空间。结果表明，学习到的对齐方式大大改善了跨模式检索，这表明显式表示学习可以恢复原始表示空间中缺少的对应关系。– remains underexplored in scholarly information retrieval. Although prior studies show that jointly modeling syntactic and semantic modalities improves retrieval performance, the relationship between their underlying representations has not been systematically investigated. In this work, we empirically study cross-modal correspondence between formula syntax and semantics. We find that their native representation spaces exhibit extremely weak observable correspondence despite strong latent correlation, indicating a substantial representation mismatch between the two modalities. We further evaluate whether this mismatch can be reduced using standard representation learning and alignment techniques. We represent syntactic structure using graph-based encoders and semantic information using text-based encoders, then apply contrastive learning to induce a shared representation space. Results show that the learned alignment substantially improves cross-modal retrieval, suggesting that explicit representation learning can recover correspondence absent from the original representation spaces.

Early approaches model formulae and their surrounding textual context as sequences of tokens, employing token-based representations for retrieval and matching(Krstovski and Blei, 2018; Yasunaga and Lafferty, 2019; Yuan et al., 2016)。由于公式固有的层次性质，许多后续研究采用树结构表示来明确捕获其结构. 3. 公式表示Formula Representation

科学公式的准确表示需要捕获其句法和语义维度，因为纯粹的句法表示可能会错过概念意义，而纯粹的语义表示可能会忽略精确的结构约束。 为了能够对这两种模式进行系统比较，我们为每个公式构建了单独的语法和语义表示。 这些表示作为第 5 节中实验的基础Accurate representation of scientific formulae requires capturing both their syntactic and semantic dimension, as purely syntactic representations may miss conceptual meaning, while purely semantic representations may ignore precise structural constraints. To enable a systematic comparison between these two modalities, we construct separate syntactic and semantic representations for every formula. These representations serve as the basis for the experiments in Section4.

4.3. 学习语法-语义对齐Learning Syntax–Semantic Alignment

上一节中的对应分析表明，尽管共享大量的潜在结构，但句法和语义表示表现出较弱的可观察对齐。因此，我们研究是否可以通过学习的跨模式表示对齐来减少这种不匹配。在跨模式对齐中，目标是学习共享空间，以便将不同的模式连贯地集成。然后，公式语法和语义的联合表示应该联合编码这两种信息，同时保持其一致性，即，任一模态的相似性在空间中有意义地反映。The correspondence analysis in the previous section indicates that syntactic and semantic representations exhibit weak observable alignment despite sharing substantial latent structure. We therefore investigate whether this mismatch can be reduced through learned cross-modal representation alignment. In cross-modal alignment, the goal is to learn a shared space such that different modalities are integrated coherently. The joint representation of formula syntax and semantic should then jointly encode both information while maintaining its coherence, i.e., the similarity in either modality is meaningfully reflected in the space.

5. 结论Conclusion

这项工作从跨模态的角度研究了科学公式的句法和语义表示之间的关系。通过对自然对应的多层次分析，我们表明，尽管共享大量的潜在结构，但这两种模态在其原生嵌入空间中表现出较弱的可观察对齐。这些发现揭示了符号和语义表示之间的几何不匹配：相关信息跨两种模式进行编码，但组织方式不同。This work investigates the relationship between syntactic and semantic representations of scientific formulae from a cross-modal perspective. Through a multi-level analysis of natural correspondence, we show that the two modalities exhibit weak observable alignment in their native embedding spaces, despite sharing substantial latent structure. These findings reveal a geometric mismatch between symbolic and semantic representations: related information is encoded across both modalities but organized differently.

我们进一步研究是否可以通过学习表示对齐来减少这种不匹配。使用基于图的结构编码器和基于文本的语义编码器，我们证明标准对比对齐技术可以显着提高跨模式检索性能。这些结果表明，公式语法和语义之间的对应关系不能从独立的嵌入空间直接访问，但可以通过显式对齐目标来恢复。We further examine whether this mismatch can be reduced through learned representation alignment. Using graph-based structural encoders and text-based semantic encoders, we demonstrate that standard contrastive alignment techniques can substantially improve cross-modal retrieval performance. These results suggest that the correspondence between formula syntax and semantics is not directly accessible from independent embedding spaces, but can be recovered through explicit alignment objectives.

总的来说，这项工作提供了公式模态之间关系的实证分析，并为研究它们在学术信息系统中的集成奠定了基础。未来的工作将调查更大、更多样化的科学语料库，如 并探索其在神经符号推理和科学知识发现等下游任务中的应用。Overall, this work provides an empirical analysis of the relationship between formula modalities and a foundation for studying their integration in scholarly information systems. Future work will investigate larger and more diverse scientific corpora, as well as exploring its application to downstream tasks such as neuro-symbolic reasoning and scientific knowledge discovery.
