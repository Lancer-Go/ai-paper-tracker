  • •

    个性化或特定领域的查询。Personalized or Domain-Specific Querying.当用户偏好差异很大或者特定领域的知识对于构建 SQL 查询至关重要时，语义提示会动态捕获这些本地约定。这种细粒度的适应经常被忽视，或者通过全局微调来实施的成本太高。When user preferences vary significantly or domain-specific knowledge is essential in crafting SQL queries, semantic hints capture these local conventions dynamically.   • •

相比之下，对于模式覆盖范围有限且无需调整的静态任务，传统的 SFT 可能仍然是一种有竞争力的解决方案。In contrast, for static tasks with limited schema coverage where adaptation is unnecessary, traditional SFT may remain a competitive solution.

7.2 系统限制System Limitations

我们承认当前设计中的几个界限：We acknowledge several boundaries in our current design:

  • •

    未评估部署阶段。Deployment Phase Not Evaluated.我们的实验仅涵盖开发阶段（第一阶段）。由于部署阶段（第二阶段）依赖于人机交互偏好信号，而 Spider 2.0–Snow 等公共基准测试并未提供这种信号，因此我们不会在本文中进行部署阶段实验，并将其留作未来的工作。Our experiments cover only the Development Phase (Phase I).     触发灵敏度。Trigger Sensitivity.语义提示的检索依赖于自然语言触发的精度。制定不当的触发器可能会导致提示选择的召回率或精确度较低，从而影响逻辑规划阶段。The retrieval of semantic hints relies on the precision of natural-language triggers. Poorly formulated triggers may lead to lower recall or precision in hint selection, impacting the Logic Planning stage.

  • •

    策略稀疏性。Strategy Sparsity.虽然策略层可以防止冲突损失，但在部署的早期阶段（冷启动），系统可能缺乏足够的统计数据来可靠地对罕见边缘情况的竞争策略进行排名。While the Strategy Layer prevents conflict loss, in the early stages of deployment (cold start), the system may lack sufficient statistical data to reliably rank competing strategies for rare edge cases.

大型语言模型使文本到 SQL 变得越来越实用，但由于方言特定的语法失败、模糊的业务逻辑和不断变化的用户偏好，在实际数据库环境中的稳健部署仍然具有挑战性。现有的适应范式都暴露了这种情况下的一个关键弱点：监督微调是严格的并且刷新成本高昂，测试时间缩放会产生大量的在线开销，并且原始检索通常会引入嘈杂的上下文而不是可操作的指导。Large language models have made Text-to-SQL increasingly practical, but robust deployment in real database environments remains challenging due to dialect-specific syntax failures, ambiguous business logic, and evolving user preferences. Existing adaptation paradigms each expose a key weakness in this setting: supervised fine-tuning is rigid and costly to refresh, test-time scaling incurs substantial online overhead, and raw retrieval often introduces noisy context rather than actionable guidance.
