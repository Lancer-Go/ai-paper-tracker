超越组织结构图：人工智能和无形工作的转变Beyond the Org Chart: AI and the Transformation of Invisible Work

斯蒂芬妮·罗森塔尔和沙姆西·伊克巴尔微软公司Stephanie Rosenthal and Shamsi Iqbal Microsoft Corporation { 沙姆西·索罗森塔尔srosenthal, shamsi } @microsoft.com

摘要Abstract

越来越多的新闻和研究文章报告称，人工智能的采用使专业人士能够模糊并扩展其企业角色的界限。为了了解人工智能公司的工作流程可能会发生怎样的变化，我们采访了一家大型科技公司的 24 名以产品为中心的人员，了解人工智能如何影响他们自己的工作、他们在产品团队中的工作以及他们的专业互动。我们的对话表明，人工智能不仅正在改变正式的角色职责和这些角色之间的协作，而且还改变了专业指导等非正式的文化实践，这些实践对于帮助专业人士安定在自己的岗位上、保持对工作的投入和职业发展至关重要。其中一些变化是积极的，例如同事之间的合作更加顺畅，但其他变化则更加微妙，使典型的职业发展机会（例如从专业网络接收反馈以及提升领导力和指导）面临风险。我们建议人工智能公司可以采取哪些措施，让无形的工作变得更加可见。此外，我们建议个人和领导者可以采取措施，通过人工智能转型来支持他们的同事，同时保持支持多元化思维、协作和非正式互动的健康的公司文化。An increasing number of news and research articles report that AI adoption is allowing professionals to blur and extend the boundaries of their corporate roles. With the goal of understanding how work processes might be changing in an AI-forward company, we interviewed 24 product-focused individuals at a large technology firm about how AI has impacted their own work, their work within their product team, and their professional interactions. Our conversations suggest that AI is not only changing formal role responsibilities and collaborations between those roles, but also changing informal cultural practices like professional mentoring that are key to helping professionals settle in their positions, stay engaged with their work, and grow their careers. Some of these changes are positive, such as smoother collaboration between peers, but other changes are more nuanced and put the typical career growth opportunities, like receiving feedback from professional networks and promoting leadership and mentorship, at risk. We propose steps that AI companies can take to make the invisible work more visible. Additionally, we propose efforts that individuals and leaders can take to support their colleagues through AI transformation while preserving healthy company cultures that support diverse thinking, collaboration, and informal interactions.

简介Introduction

智能 (AI) 快速融入知识工作行业，正在从根本上改变专业生产力、任务结构和决策流程。实证研究表明，生成式人工智能可以显着提高员工在各种任务中的表现。例如，最近的一项研究表明，在同一组 16 项任务中，智能体完成任务的速度比人类快 90%（Wang et al. 2025）。另一项对 5000 多名客户支持专家进行的大规模研究发现，与没有人工智能辅助的员工相比，人工智能辅助的员工完成了更多的任务，交付了更高质量的输出，并且工作速度更快（Brynjolfsson、Li 和 Raymond 2025）。尽管人们对生产力提高的具体程度存在分歧，但工人（Woodruff et al. 2024）和媒体（Vallance 2023）都预见到人工智能对“可见”工作的巨大影响，直接影响组织的底线。The rapid integration of intelligence (AI) into knowledge work industries is fundamentally transforming professional productivity, task structure, and decisionmaking processes. Empirical research shows that generative AI can significantly enhance worker performance across a variety of tasks. For example, one recent study showed that agents could complete tasks 90% faster than humans on the[˜] same set of 16 tasks (Wang et al. 2025). Another large-scale study of over 5000 customer support specialists found that AI-assisted workers completed more tasks, delivered higherquality outputs, and worked faster than those without AI assistance (Brynjolfsson, Li, and Raymond 2025). Despite the disagreements in exactly how much productivity improvement workers see, both workers (Woodruff et al. 2024) and the media (Vallance 2023) foresee the monumental impact of AI on “visible” work that directly affects an organization’s bottom line.

相关工作Related Work

最初对隐形工作的研究主要集中在传统上由妇女和母亲管理的家务劳动（Daniels 1987），但近年来已扩展到通常也落在妇女和少数族裔身上的各种其他任务。在工作场所，隐形工作是一个术语，用于描述运作良好的团体或组织所必需的任务，但常常被忽视、未被跟踪，尤其是没有奖励（Hatton 2017）。隐形工作的任务类型大致可分为人际、社会和文化性质，包括情感支持、冲突解决、指导、知识转移、办公室组织、沟通、协调、建立融洽关系、建立和维持专业关系的幕后网络以及指导他人（Wells and MacAulay；Time 2023；Szulanski 1996）。隐形工作对员工的压力、工作满意度、工作绩效、幸福感和自我价值产生实际影响，并导致工作生活界限模糊、人际关系紧张（Daniels 1987；Time 2023）以及组织信息流、学习和解决复杂任务（Szulanski 1996；Brown and Duguid 1991；Moreland 1996）。Original research on invisible work focused on domestic work traditionally managed by women and mothers (Daniels 1987), but has been extended in recent years to a variety of other tasks that typically also fall on women and minorities. In the workplace, invisible work is the term used to describe tasks that are necessary for well-functioning groups or organizations, but often go unnoticed, untracked, and especially unrewarded (Hatton 2017). The types of tasks that fall into invisible work can roughly be categorized as interpersonal, social, and cultural in nature, and include emotional support, conflict resolution, mentoring, transfer of knowledge, office organization, communication, coordination, building rapport, behind-the-scenes networking to build and maintain professional relationships, and coaching others (Wells and MacAulay; Time 2023; Szulanski 1996). Invisible work has real impacts on workers’ stress, job satisfaction, job performance, happiness and self-worth, and leads to blurred worklife boundaries and strained personal relationships (Daniels 1987; Time 2023) as well as organizational information flow, learning and solving complex tasks (Szulanski 1996; Brown and Duguid 1991; Moreland 1996).

Research on work and well-being has longestablished social support as a core resource rather than a peripheral benefit in organizations. Early stress research defines social support as resources provided by others that help individuals cope with demands, particularly by shaping how stressors are appraised and managed (Cohen and Wills 1985). In organizational contexts, support consistently predicts lower strain, higher satisfaction, and better performance, especially under high demands (House

Contemporary work-design theories formalize this insight: both the Job Demands–Resources (JD-R) model and related frameworks treat social support as a key job resource that buffers burnout and sustains engagement when workloads and uncertainty increase (Demerouti et al. 2001; Bakker and Demerouti 2017).

文献集中于一小部分构建工作场所关系的支持类型。经典类型区分任务支持（有形帮助和任务协助）、信息支持（建议、指导和知识）、情感支持（同理心、保证、关怀）以及评估或反馈支持（肯定、评估和指导）（House 1983；Cohen 和 Wills 1985）。荟萃分析表明，这些形式与多种结果相关：工具和信息支持与绩效密切相关，而情感和评价支持与幸福感、承诺和减少退缩联系更紧密（Viswesvaran、Sanchez 和 Fisher 1999；Ng 和 Sorensen 2008）。重要的是，支持的许多好处不仅来自解决问题，还来自归属感和合法性的关系信号，这些信号塑造了工作中的身份和动机（Kahn 1990）。The literature converges on a small set of support types that structure workplace relationships. Classic typologies distinguish task support (tangible help and task assistance), informational support (advice, guidance, and knowledge), emotional support (empathy, reassurance, care), and appraisal or feedback support (affirmation, evaluation, and coaching) (House 1983; Cohen and Wills 1985). A meta-analysis shows that these forms are related to a variety of outcomes: instrumental and informational support are strongly tied to performance, while emotional and appraisal support are more closely linked to well-being, commitment, and reduced withdrawal (Viswesvaran, Sanchez, and Fisher 1999; Ng and Sorensen 2008). Importantly, many benefits of support derive not only from problem-solving but from relational signals of belonging and legitimacy, which shape identity and motivation at work (Kahn 1990).

在此背景下，最近的从业者和学术讨论凸显了一种转变：工人越来越多地转向生成式人工智能（例如大型语言模型和对话代理），以获取传统上由同事或经理提供的支持形式（Hadley 和 Wright 2026）。最近的实证研究表明，工人使用生成式人工智能来提供信息和工具支持，例如起草、调试、意义构建和学习，通常可以大幅提高生产力，特别是对于经验不足的工人来说（Brynjolfsson、Li 和 Raymond 2025）。其他研究同样发现，开发人员和知识工作者重视生成式人工智能，将其视为始终可用的指导和任务脚手架来源，同时为指导和评估等以关系为中心的活动划定界限（Barke、James 和 Polikarpova 2023；Choudhuri 等人 2025）。在对人工智能在人力资源管理中的使用进行回顾时，进一步的分析发现，工人在从知识共享到合作再到影响工作满意度和工作安置的各个方面都转向人工智能（Pereira et al. 2023）。Against this backdrop, recent practitioner and academic discussions highlight a shift: workers are increasingly turning to generative AI (e.g., large language models and conversational agents) for forms of support traditionally provided by coworkers or managers (Hadley and Wright 2026). Recent empirical work shows that workers use generative AI for informational and instrumental support such as drafting, debugging, sense-making, and learning, often with substantial productivity gains, particularly for less experienced workers (Brynjolfsson, Li, and Raymond 2025). Other studies similarly find that developers and knowledge workers value generative AI as an always-available source of guidance and task scaffolding, while drawing boundaries around relationship-centered activities such as mentoring and evaluation (Barke, James, and Polikarpova 2023; Choudhuri et al. 2025). In a review of AI use in human-resource management, further analysis finds workers turning to AI for everything from knowledge sharing to cooperation to collaboration with effects on job satisfaction and job placement (Pereira et al. 2023).

员工还使用生成式人工智能来提供情感和类似评估的支持，包括安抚、建立信心和职业建议。对对话代理的研究表明，人们很容易将同理心和理解归因于能够产生流畅、反应灵敏的语言的系统，即使他们知道这些系统是人造的（Nass and Moon 2000；Pelau、Dabija 和 Ene 2021；Zhang et al. 2025b）。这有助于解释为什么人工智能介导的支持在当下会让人感到心理上有意义。然而，有证据表明感知支持与社会结果之间存在紧张关系：虽然人工智能可以模拟响应能力，但它并不能可靠地减少孤独感或取代人际关系的好处（Campbell 2021）。从经典支持的角度来看，这意味着人工智能可以在不完全复制人类压力缓冲和身份确认功能的情况下传递信息内容Workers also use generative AI for emotional and appraisal-like support, including reassurance, confidence building, and career advice. Research on conversational agents shows that people readily attribute empathy and understanding to systems that produce fluent, responsive language, even when they know those systems are artificial (Nass and Moon 2000; Pelau, Dabija, and Ene 2021; Zhang et al. 2025b). This helps explain why AI-mediated support can feel psychologically meaningful in the moment. However, evidence suggests a tension between perceived support and social outcomes: while AI can simulate responsiveness, it does not reliably reduce loneliness or replace the relational benefits of human connection (Campbell 2021). 最近的组织研究通过一系列悖论概述了人工智能在工作中的好处和成本，使替代叙述进一步复杂化。对工作中人工智能使用的研究表明，当同事得知他们依赖人工智能时，员工可能会受到社会评价惩罚，被认为能力较差或不太值得信赖，尤其是在没有明确规范的情况下披露人工智能使用情况时（Zhou et al. 2025；Reif、Larrick 和 Soll 2025）。这就产生了一个悖论：生成式人工智能可以提高个人效率，同时破坏关系地位，而关系地位本身就是一种支持途径。同样，工作中对人工智能的依赖可能会同时增强人类的能力，同时也会降低人类的专业知识（Ehsan 等人，2026）。Recent organizational research further complicates substitution narratives with a series of paradoxes outlining both the benefits and costs of AI at work. Studies of AI use at work show that workers may incur a social evaluation penalty when coworkers learn they rely on AI, being judged as less competent or less trustworthy, especially when AI use is disclosed without clear norms (Zhou et al. 2025; Reif, Larrick, and Soll 2025). This creates a paradox: generative AI can increase individual efficiency while undermining relational standing, which is itself a pathway to support. Similarly, dependence on AI at work may simultaneously augment human capabilities and also reduce human expertise (Ehsan et al. 2026).

综上所述，文献表明生成式人工智能是一把双刃剑。它可以提高个人任务的生产力，但也可能通过增加工作强度而产生压力（Ranganathan 和 Ye 2026）。在社会支持方面，员工越来越多地使用人工智能来扩展工具和信息支持，并提供低风险的协作支架，但尚不清楚它如何影响对职业成功和组织效率至关重要的人际关系。因此，组织面临的新挑战不是员工是否会使用人工智能来获得支持，而是如何衡量这种支持对通常没有记录的组织结构的影响，以及如何在其基础上设计规范和系统，在利用人工智能的可访问性和影响力的同时保持人与人之间的联系。在这项工作中，我们调查了人工智能对大型科技公司内部无形工作的影响，概述了七个关键影响领域的收益和风险，并提出了人工智能公司、个人和组织领导者可以采取的支持员工和维持健康工作文化的步骤。Taken together, the literature suggests that generative AI is a double-edged sword. It can increase productivity on individual tasks, but also may induce stress through the increased intensity of work (Ranganathan and Ye 2026). In regards to social support, workers are increasingly using AI to scale instrumental and informational support and provide low-stakes collaborative scaffolding, it is unclear how it affects interpersonal relationships that are critical to career success and organizational effectiveness. The emerging challenge for organizations is therefore not whether workers will use AI for support, but how to measure the impact of that support on the organizational structures that have typically gone undocumented and how to design norms and systems on top of them that preserve human connection while leveraging AI’s accessibility and reach. In this work, we investigate the impacts of AI on invisible work within a large technology company, outlining the benefits and risks across seven key impact areas, and propose steps that AI companies, individuals, and organization leaders can take to support their workers and maintain a healthy work culture.

研究方法Research Methods

为了了解人工智能的采用对无形工作的影响，我们对一家大型科技公司的 24 名专业人员进行了半结构化访谈。我们选择调查人工智能对一家公司工作的影响，与许多其他公司一样（Nahar et al. 2025；Choudhuri et al. 2025；Butler et al. 2025），该公司在很长一段时间内为其专业人员提供了许多不同的人工智能工具。我们的期望是，长期采用使我们的参与者能够找到对他们及其产品团队来说成功的一致使用模式，并且他们将能够反思他们的持续使用并识别更微妙的工作变化。In order to understand the impact of AI adoption on invisible work, we performed semi-structured interviews with 24 professionals at a large technology company. We chose to investigate AI impacts on work at a company that, like many others (Nahar et al. 2025; Choudhuri et al. 2025; Butler et al. 2025), has provided its professionals many different AI tools for an extended period of time. Our expectation is that longterm adoption has allowed our participants to find consistent patterns of use that are successful for them and their product teams, and they will be able to reflect on their sustained use and identify more subtle work changes.

采访参与者被要求描述他们定期完成的常见工作流程，以及这些工作流程如何因人工智能而发生（或未发生）改变。我们分析了与可见工作（例如，生产力和可交付成果）以及隐形工作（例如，协作、帮助和反馈）相关的常见主题的记录。我们的研究结果表明，人工智能的采用对隐形工作既有积极的影响，也有消极的影响，而这反过来又有可能影响未来的社会支持、个人职业发展和公司文化。The interview participants were asked to describe common workflows they accomplish regularly, and how those workflows have (or have not) changed due to AI. We analyzed the transcripts for common themes related to visible work (e.g., productivity and deliverables) as well as to invisible work (e.g., collaboration, help, and feedback). Our results show that AI adoption has both positively and negatively impacted invisible work, which in turn has the potential to impact social support, individual career growth and company culture in the future.

Themes outside of the question titles that were tagged included independence, creativity, impact, isolation, and collaboration.

3. 项目态势感知3. Project Situational Awareness

同时，通过人工智能写作工具更容易创建，但由于人数较少，操作起来也更困难is simultaneously easier to create through AI writing tools, but also harder to operationalize as fewer people are

阅读更多文档。reading more documents.

建立态势感知，而不是隐瞒信息，是在协作和开放环境中工作的重要组成部分（Webster 等，2008）。态势感知是高效团队绩效的关键，因为队友会创建共享的心理模型，使他们能够快速行动并减少误解（Salas 等人，1995）。花费大量时间准备文件和召开会议来共享信息（Baksa 和 Branyiczki 2023）。与领导者和管理者共享信息对于保持所展示作品的可见性和影响力非常重要（Zhang、De Pablos 和 Zhou 2013），这会影响未来项目工作的规划和作者的专业成长。此外，在广泛的分发列表上分享结果为“潜伏者”提供了听到他们原本不会知道的信息的机会（Cranefield、Yoong 和 Huff 2015），从而扩展了他们自己项目的工作知识、他们的专业网络以及潜在的技能组合。Building situational awareness, rather than withholding information, is an important part of working in collaborative and open environments (Webster et al. 2008). Situational awareness is key to high-functioning team performance, as teammates create shared mental models that allow them to move quickly and with fewer misunderstandings (Salas et al. 1995). Significant time is spent preparing documents and meeting to share information (Baksa and Branyiczki 2023). Sharing information with leaders and managers is important to maintaining visibility and impact of the work presented (Zhang, De Pablos, and Zhou 2013), which affects the planning of future project work and the professional growth of the writer. Additionally, sharing outcomes on wide distribution lists provides “lurkers” the opportunity to hear about information they would not have otherwise been privy to (Cranefield, Yoong, and Huff 2015), expanding their working knowledge for their own projects, their professional networks, and potentially their skill sets.

我们研究的参与者表示，他们的团队花费了大量时间在内部和更广泛的组织结构中共享有关其项目状态和项目成果的信息。共享上下文可以采取多种形式，包括会议、项目演示共享、书面文档和状态电子邮件。参与者 U 谈到了以与人工智能之前类似的方式继续建立态势感知的工作：Participants in our study said that their teams spend a lot of time sharing information about their project status and project outcomes both internally and across broader organizational structures. Sharing context can take many forms including meetings, project presentation share-outs, written documents, and status emails. Participant U spoke about the work to build situational awareness continuing in similar ways to before AI:

我认为与他人“完成工作”的人际交往仍然是人类独有的，而且很笨拙。我的意思是，它在某些方面会变得笨拙。就像你仍然需要穿越时区一样。你仍然把东西放在文件中，人们仍在对它们发表评论……我认为我们仍然是人类，我们仍然以类似的方式工作。I think that interpersonal “getting work done” with others is still uniquely human and it just is clunky. I mean, it’s just going to be clunky in some ways. Like you still have to navigate time zones. You still are putting things in documents and people are making comments on them... I think we are all still human and we still work similarly.

他们继续表示，建立知识共享表示的工作仍然需要时间，协商仍然是协作工作的瓶颈。虽然沟通的挑战没有改变，但参与者 J 评论了人工智能如何改变工作节奏，以及他们的队友正在做的工作意识也受到了影响：They continued to say that the work to build a shared representation of knowledge still takes time, and negotiation remains a bottleneck in collaborative work. While the challenges of communication have not changed, Participant J commented on how the pace of work has shifted due to AI and the awareness of work that their teammates are doing has also been impacted:

现在我想问一下大家在自己的人工智能探索中都在做什么。我开始有点担心我们进展得太快，以至于我们没有左右看。Now I have to ask what everyone’s doing in their own AI exploration. I’m starting to be a little worried that we’re moving so fast that we’re not looking side to side.

他们谈到他们需要更加谨慎地询问同事（“并排”）他们正在做什么以建立态势感知。They spoke about how they need to be more deliberate in asking their colleagues (“side to side”) what they are working on to build situational awareness.

关于支持态势感知的人工智能工具，所有参与者几乎普遍发现，使用人工智能可以更轻松地编写共享知识所需的文档。参与者 F 说“我想说这是一种赋权……就像在 [AI] 之前一样，我认为我无法写出大量的文章”，他们需要同事或主管的广泛支持。现在，他们在人工智能的帮助下撰写长篇研究文章。With regards to AI tools supporting situational awareness, one nearly-universal finding across all participants is that writing the documentation necessary for sharing knowledge is easier with AI. Participant F said “I would say it’s empowering.... Like before [AI], I don’t think I could write extensive articles” and they would have needed extensive support from colleagues or supervisors. Now, they write long research articles with the help of AI.

参与者 V 感觉他们的观众不再阅读他们生成的内容：Participant V feels like their audience is not reading the content they generate any more:

  • 在人工智能出现之前，注意力的持续时间就已经很有限了，而现在，注意力的持续时间已经缩小了，以前人们可以阅读一页纸的文档。现在他们想要 2 个要点、3 个要点。但这就像“我如何确保你有足够的信息来正确地合并这两个要点？”It’s just really hard to work with the attention spans that were already limited before AI, but now it’s like shrunk where before people would be okay reading a one-pager document. Now they want like 2 bullet points, 3 bullet points. But it’s like “how do I make sure you’re informed enough to actually incorporate those two bullet points correctly?”

他们接着谈到了一些例子，其中他们的合作者没有考虑到他们的研究，或者他们不得不不断重复自己的观点，因为同行没有注意。他们还描述了以新颖的方式提取信息的策略，包括短视频剪辑、演示文稿和降价文件。参与者 N 创建了一个代理，他们的同事可以询问之前的研究结果，而不必花时间重新分享。They went on to talk about instances where their collaborators did not take their research into account or where they had to constantly repeat their points because peers were not paying attention. They also described strategies for distilling information in novel ways including short video clips, presentations, and markdown files. Participant N made an agent that their colleagues could ask about prior research results rather than having to spend the time re-sharing.

一些参与者认为，他们正在使用人工智能来生成其他人将使用人工智能进行总结的文档，从而创建了一个人工信息管道，有可能错过工作的真正含义。最近，研究人员还认为，“AI slop”的出现是书面文档供应增加的产物，尽管令人沮丧，但内容仍具有积极的价值，并将影响“创意、信息和文化”的消费经济（Kommers et al. 2026）。然而，根据我们的研究，员工似乎正在将他们的写作风格调整为更简洁的方式，如果他们不能降低误解的风险，这可能会对生产力和项目可交付成果产生更广泛的影响。Some participants felt that they were using AI to produce documents that others would use AI to summarize, creating an artificial pipeline of information that risks missing the true meaning of the work. Recently, researchers have also argued that the emergence of “AI slop” is an artifact of the increase in the supply of written documentation, and despite the frustration, there is positive value in the content and will affect the “creative, information, and cultural” economies of consumption (Kommers et al. 2026). However, based on our study, it seems that workers are adapting their writing styles to more concise modalities, which may have a much broader impact on productivity and project deliverables if they cannot mitigate the risks of misunderstandings.

4. 声誉和责任4. Reputation and Accountability

是确保人工智能内容质量的关键驱动因素，个人必须通过大声疾呼和质疑他们所阅读的内容来让自己和同事承担责任。are key drivers of ensuring AI content quality, and individuals must hold themselves and their peers accountable by calling out and questioning what they read.

在工作中建立较高的声誉是另一项无形的工作任务，需要时间和责任来维持，并且也会影响工作任务的完成方式。研究表明，与组织中声誉较低的人相比，声誉较高的人会感受到更强的责任感，并且在遵守该标准时的紧张感和压力较小（Laird 等，2009）。虽然问责制通常是外部的（即其他人要求某人对其行为负责），但最近的研究表明，具有内部问责制的人更有可能承担额外的组织任务和隐形工作（Wang、Waldman 和 Ashforth 2019）。Building a high reputation at work is another invisible work task that takes time and responsibility to maintain, and also impacts the way that work tasks are completed. Research shows that people who have higher reputations feel a stronger sense of accountability and less tension and strain holding themselves to that standard than those with lower reputations in organizations (Laird et al. 2009). While accountability is often placed externally (i.e., others hold a person accountable for their actions), there is also recent work showing that people who have internally-placed accountability are more likely to take on additional organizational tasks and invisible work (Wang, Waldman, and Ashforth 2019).

我们的参与者经常谈论人工智能增强写作任务的声誉和责任。例如，参与者U认为AI写作看起来很不错，但不花大力气是无法使用的。Our participants often spoke about reputation and accountability with respect to AI-augmented writing tasks. For example, Participant U thinks that AI writing looks impressive, but it is not usable without considerable effort.

  • 太棒了，这是一个很棒的结构，但我必须做很多改变才能达到我需要的效果，以至于它实际上没有那么有帮助。”但看起来很棒！great, it was a great structure, but I have to change so much to get it to what I need that it’s actually not that helpful.” But it looked great!

他们花费大量时间修改和编辑自己的工作，以使其超出人工智能基线，然后再将其发送给同事和经理。参与者N还谈到花了很多时间“编辑人工智能生成的不良内容”。They spend a lot of time revising and editing their work to improve it beyond the AI baseline before they send it to their peers and managers. Participant N also talked about spending a lot of time “editing bad content” that AI had generated.

其他参与者还评论说要对研究人员发现的引文负责。参与者 R 谈到无法评估其领域之外的论文质量，这可能会影响该领域的同事评估其工作价值的方式：Other participants also commented on being accountable for the citations that research agents were finding. Participant R spoke about not being able to assess the quality of papers that were outside their fields, which may affect how colleagues in that field assess the value of their work:

  • 我自己更多地要求[人工智能研究工具]找到与我不是专家的领域相关的论文。但我无法真正判断它是否找到了相关论文。I myself more asking the [AI research tools] to find papers relevant to an area I’m not an expert in. But then I can’t really judge whether it found the relevant papers.

一些参与者表示，他们愿意花更多时间来了解某个主题，并相应地修改人工智能生成的内容，以便对自己正在制作的工作负责，包括引用其领域之外的研究。这些结果与研究结果一致，表明人们仍然感觉能够控制写作过程，并且不会感觉受到人工智能建议的影响（Bhat 等人，2026 年；Park 等人，2026 年）。Several participants said they were willing to take additional time to learn about a topic and revise AI-generated content accordingly in order to hold themselves accountable for the work they are producing, including citing research outside their field. Readers have the power to reduce the amount of low-quality AI content and increase productivity by holding writers accountable and AI content to high expectations (Niederhoffer et al. 2025).

5. 个人成长和学习5. Personal Growth and Learning

需要获得新的人工智能技能，人工智能还帮助工人掌握新的能力，因为从同事那里进行非正式学习可能更困难。are required to acquire new AI skills, and AI is also helping workers master new capabilities as informal learning from peers may be harder to come by.

使用人工智能作为个性化学习工具并不新鲜，人工智能驱动的辅导系统已经探索了数十年（Nwana 1990）。然而，聊天界面和深度模型的进步重新燃起了学校（Alam 2023）和工作中（Poquet 和 De Laat 2021）对此类工具的兴趣。最近的一项文献综述发现，发表的有关工作场所人工智能的论文中近一半关注学习和培训（Pereira 等人，2023）。Using AI as a personalized learning tool is not new, AI-driven tutoring systems have been explored for decades (Nwana 1990). However, the advancement in chat interfaces and deep models has renewed the interest in such tools in schools (Alam 2023) as well as at work (Poquet and De Laat 2021). One recent literature review found that nearly half of the papers published on AI in the workplace focus on learning and training (Pereira et al. 2023).

随着人工智能的不断改进和工具的不断变化，非正式学习被证明是至关重要的。自发的非正式学习可能对其他人来说是看不见的，但对于个人成长和职业发展至关重要（De Laat 和 Schreurs 2013）。过去，员工可能通过协作或会议与同事互动来获得这些学习机会。事实上，过去的研究表明，关系对于知识获取很重要（Allen 1977；Granovetter 1973），并且以下关键关系特征影响信息搜索和学习：（1）了解那个人知道什么； (2) 重视该人所知道的事情； （3）能够及时了解对方的想法； (4) 认为向该人寻求信息的成本不会太高（Borgatti 和 Cross 2003）。Informal learning is proving to be critical as AI is constantly improving and the tools are changing. Spontaneous informal learning may be invisible to others but is critical to personal growth and career development (De Laat and Schreurs 2013). In the past, workers may have gotten these learning opportunities through interactions with their coworkers through collaborations or in meetings. Indeed, past research has shown that relationships are important for knowledge acquisition (Allen 1977; Granovetter 1973) and the following key relational characteristics impact information seeking and learning: (1) knowing what that person knows; (2) valuing what that person knows; (3) being able to gain timely access to that person’s thinking; and (4) perceiving that seeking information from that person would not be too costly (Borgatti and Cross 2003).

然而，随着人工智能作为替代信息源的出现，传统的学习和从同伴那里寻求信息的机制似乎受到了影响。参与者S谈到了他们如何向同事寻求概念反馈，并希望在会议期间向其他人提供反馈：However, with the advent of AI as an alternative information source, traditional mechanisms of learning and information seeking from peers appear to be impacted. Participant S talked about how they seek conceptual feedback from their peers and also want to provide feedback to others during meetings:

我觉得特别是对于我的团队来说，我们仍然有很多会议......你不会因在这些会议中提出问题而受到赞扬。这更像是“这就是我们需要做的”。I feel like for my team in particular, we still have a lot of meetings... you’re not praised for asking questions in those meetings. It’s more like “this is what we need to do.”

虽然生产力似乎更快，因为创建者不必与现有基础设施作斗争或与合作者进行清晰的沟通，但我们相信，随着更多时间花在集成独立设计的不同部件上，而没有考虑到彼此的情况，最终会出现速度减慢的情况。之前的工作发现了类似的人工智能放大器在独立性和去技能方面的悖论（Ehsan et al. 2026）。While the productivity seems faster because the creator does not have to fight with existing infrastructure or communicate clearly with collaborators, we believe that there will be eventual slow-downs as more time is spent integrating disparate pieces that were designed independently without each other in mind. Prior work found a similar AI-as-Amplifier paradox with respect to independence and deskilling (Ehsan et al. 2026).

低价值人工智能内容的显着增加至少部分是由内容创作者对其工作负责的社会结构的侵蚀所推动的。Notable increases in low-value AI content are, at least in part, driven by the eroding social structures that hold content creators responsible for their work.我们的参与者都讨论了他们为保持对工作质量负责而做出的努力，尽管组织面临着更快地工作和完成更广泛的任务的压力。然而，他们也绝大多数认为人工智能增强内容的质量标准比在工作场所引入人工智能之前要低，因此并不觉得有必要修改和检查他们的工作。与此同时，我们的参与者指出，许多人正在使用人工智能来总结他们生成的内容，可能会遗漏作者试图在更长的上下文中传达的关键点。这有可能成为一个恶性循环，人工智能先生成内容，然后再次总结，而不是仅仅从头开始生成摘要。我们认为，为了减少低价值内容的出现，读者必须让同事对他们制作的作品负责，通过提出问题和提供反馈来保持质量水平。Our participants all discussed their efforts to maintain accountability for the quality of their work despite the organizational pressures to work more quickly and on a broader set of tasks. However, they also overwhelmingly felt that the quality bar was lower for AI-augmented content than it had been prior to the introduction of AI in the workplace, and therefore did not feel as compelled to revise and check their work. At the same time, our participants noted that many people were using AI to summarize content that they generated, potentially missing key points that the author was trying to convey in the longer context. This has the potential to become a vicious cycle in which AI is generating content and then summarizing it again, rather than merely generating the summary from the beginning. We believe that to reduce the occurrence of low-value content, readers must hold their colleagues accountable for the work they produce, maintaining a level of quality by asking questions and providing feedback.

Participants who were eager to expand their task scope in the ways that AI allowed felt more empowered than those participants who felt encumbered by AI technology that could not meet their needs or their high expectations. Junior participants who had joined the company more recently were more impacted by the lack of social support because they could not rely on their professional connections. 展望未来，人工智能公司必须集中精力寻找通过人工智能工具增加社会支持和协作的方法，而不是消除它。组织领导者、管理者和个人还必须识别人工智能驱动的自身和同事行为变化，并实施计划来支持隐形工作，帮助组织平稳运行。Moving forward, AI companies must focus efforts on identifying ways to increase social support and collaboration through their AI tools rather than eliminating it. Organization leaders, managers, and individuals must also identify AI-driven changes in their own behavior and those of their peers, and implement programs to support invisible work that helps make their organizations run smoothly.

道德考虑Ethical Considerations

这项研究得到了我们机构的研究伦理委员会的批准。参与者是通过公司电子邮件列表招募的，参与是自愿的。在开始访谈之前，参与者在被告知研究目的、任务要求、预期时间承诺以及随时跳过问题或退出而不受惩罚的权利后表示同意。该研究依赖于参与者的开放式回答。尽管我们向所有参与者提出了一组固定的问题，但偶尔也会根据回答提出其他问题。采访时间不超过一个小时，而且参与者没有得到报酬。采访回复是现场转录的，但没有录音，并且这些采访的相关部分以匿名格式保存以供进一步分析。没有保留任何个人身份信息。该研究的结果基于对四种工作职能的小样本的访谈回复，如果没有通过进一步访谈或调查进行更大规模的确认，则应谨慎对待更广泛的普遍性。This study was approved by our institution’s research ethics committee. Participants were recruited through company email lists and participation was voluntary. Before starting the interview, participants provided their consent after being informed of the purpose of the study, task requirements, expected time commitment, and their right to skip questions or withdraw at any time without penalty. The study relies on open ended responses from the participants. Although we had a fixed set of questions for all participants, occasionally additional questions were asked based on the responses. The interviews took no more than an hour and the participants were not compensated. Interview responses were transcribed live but not recorded, and relevant portions of those interviews were saved in an anonymized format for further analysis. No personally identifying information was retained. The findings from the study are based on interview responses from a small sample for four job functions and broader generalizability should be treated with caution without larger scale confirmation through further interviews or surveys.
