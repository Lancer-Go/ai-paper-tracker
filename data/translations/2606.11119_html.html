摘要Abstract

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）是增强大型语言模型中的推理和代理行为的一种有前途的方法。然而，部署密集型策略优化通常受到奖励对比不足的限制，当过于简单或复杂的提示产生低方差反馈时，以及当仅结果奖励为多轮部署中的每个决策分配相同的最终评估时，就会出现这种情况。过去的努力主要集中在将可用的推出资源分配给有希望的提示，但它们仅利用提示级别的样本信息性，而忽略了同一推出中各轮次的前缀级信息性的变化。这项工作的目标是多回合代理强化学习，通过将每个 ReAct 风格的思想-行动-观察回合建模为语义上不同的节点，允许预算分配从提示根扩展到具有进一步延续的回合级前缀，这自然形成树结构的推出。我们引入了对比探索树滚动分配（TRACE），这是一个统一的滚动分配框架，可以在固定采样预算内增强奖励对比。从技术上讲，TRACE 将推出预算分配给最有可能产生混合终端奖励的提示根和中间前缀。共享的可概括预测器根据前缀历史估计这些锚点的条件成功概率，以指导这种分配。由此产生的自适应树结构丰富了仅结果反馈并放大了策略更新信号。根据经验，TRACE 在典型的代理基准上实现了具有竞争力的性能和效率提升，例如，在相同的采样成本下，与竞争基准相比，Qwen3-14B 多跳 QA 平均准确度提高了 2.8 个百分点。Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) is a promising approach for enhancing reasoning and agentic behavior in large language models. However, rollout-intensive policy optimization is often limited by insufficient reward contrast, arising when overly simple or complex prompts generate low-variance feedback and when outcome-only rewards assign the same terminal assessment to every decision in a multi-turn rollout. Past efforts have focused on allocating available rollout resources to promising prompts, yet they only leverage sample informativeness at the prompt level and neglect variation in prefix-level informativeness across turns within the same rollout. This work targets multi-turn agentic RL by modeling each ReAct-style thought-action-observation turn as a semantically distinct node, allowing budget allocation to extend from prompt roots to turn-level prefixes with further continuations, which naturally forms tree-structured rollouts. We introduce Tree Rollout Allocation for Contrastive Exploration (TRACE), a unified rollout allocation framework that enhances reward contrast within a fixed sampling budget. Technically, TRACE allocates rollout budget to both prompt roots and intermediate prefixes that are most likely to yield mixed terminal rewards. A shared generalizable predictor estimates conditional success probability at these anchors from prefix histories to guide this allocation. The resulting adaptive tree structure enriches outcome-only feedback and amplifies the policy-update signal. Empirically, TRACE achieves competitive performance and efficiency gains on typical agentic benchmarks, e.g., improving Qwen3-14B Multi-Hop QA average accuracy by 2.8 points over competitive baselines at equal sampling cost.

1 简介Introduction

具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 已成为激励大型语言模型 (LLM) 中的推理和代理行为的关键机制，它使用可验证的反馈来塑造对解决方案路径和环境交互的探索Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has become a key mechanism for incentivizing reasoning and agentic behavior in large language models (LLMs), using verifiable feedback to shape exploration over solution paths and environment interactions(Jaech et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025a)。尽管取得了成功，但 RLVR 的计算成本很高，因为推出需要很长的思想链 (CoT). Despite its success, RLVR is computationally expensive because rollouts require long Chain-of-Thought (CoT)(Wei et al., 2022)用于推理的生成和用于代理任务的交错环境交互。因此，决定将有限的部署预算花在哪里是一个核心设计问题generation for reasoning and interleaved environment interactions for agentic tasks. Deciding where to spend limited rollout budget is therefore a central design problem(Zheng et al., 2025; Lin et al., 2025).

在实践中，大多数可扩展的 RLVR 管道依赖于结果奖励而不是过程奖励，从而避免了容易受到奖励黑客攻击的手工制定的中间奖励规则In practice, most scalable RLVR pipelines rely on outcome rewards rather than process rewards, avoiding hand-crafted intermediate reward rules that are vulnerable to reward hacking(Shao et al., 2024)。然而，此类推出提供的学习信号信息量极不均匀，并且学分分配稀疏。在提示级别，过于简单或困难的实例往往会产生低方差的结果奖励. The learning signal provided by such rollouts, however, is highly uneven in informativeness and sparse for credit assignment. At the prompt level, overly easy or hard instances tend to produce low-variance outcome rewards(Yu et al., 2025)。在轨迹层面，为长期部署分配单一终端奖励，在生成的代币和代理轮次之间的信用分配几乎没有局部对比. At the trajectory level, assigning a single terminal reward to a long rollout leaves little local contrast for credit assignment across generated tokens and agent turns(Lightman et al., 2024)。先前的工作通过及时选择缓解了即时级别的低效率. Prior work alleviates prompt-level inefficiency through prompt selection(Zheng et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2025; Bae et al., 2026)，它决定哪些提示进行采样，以及部署分配, which decides which prompts to sample, and rollout allocation(Li et al., 2025c)，它决定每个提示收到的转出次数。它们可以统一为对候选集进行评分，并将推出预算分配给可能产生对比结果变化的提示：跳过指定零推出的提示，而分配较大正预算的选定提示接收更多独立的根推出。然而，这种分配策略仅在即时根上起作用。一旦提示收到预算，每次部署仍会生成为原子轨迹。, which decides how many rollouts each prompt receives. Together, they can be unified as scoring a candidate set and assigning rollout budget toward prompts likely to produce contrastive outcome variation: a prompt assigned zero rollouts is skipped, whereas a selected prompt assigned a larger positive budget receives more independent root rollouts. Yet this allocation strategy acts only at the prompt root. Once a prompt receives budget, each rollout is still generated as an atomic trajectory.

多回合代理强化学习暴露了一个更精细的分配单元，即回合。虽然单轮推理可以在标记或句子边界进行分段，但 ReAct 风格Multi-turn agentic RL exposes a finer allocation unit, the turn. While single-turn reasoning can be segmented at token or sentence boundaries, ReAct-style(Yao et al., 2022)交互自然地将每个决策打包为一个思想-行动-观察节点。因此，完整的部署公开了语义上有意义的前缀，可以重新访问这些前缀以进行分支。这些访问过的前缀成为额外探索的候选分支点。一些前缀不太可能产生新的结果变化，而另一些前缀则可能产生具有不同最终奖励的反事实延续。从技术上讲，在这个级别上分配预算并不是抽样更独立的轨迹的问题；而是一个问题。这是一个选择，哪些前缀出现应该接收额外的延续分支。interaction naturally packages each decision as a thought-action-observation node. A complete rollout therefore exposes semantically meaningful prefixes that can be revisited for branching. These visited prefixes become candidate branching points for extra exploration. Some prefixes are unlikely to produce new outcome variation, whereas others are likely to yield counterfactual continuations with different final rewards. Technically, allocating budget at this level is not a matter of sampling more independent trajectories; it is a choice of which prefix occurrences should receive additional continuation branches.

上述观点很自然地将扁平的 rollout 集合转变为树形结构的 rollout。提示根是深度零锚点，内部前缀是非根转级锚点，因此根级分配成为在树节点上分配预算的特殊情况。共同的原则是The above viewpoint naturally turns flat rollout collection into tree-structured rollouts. Prompt roots are depth-zero anchors and internal prefixes are non-root turn-level anchors, so root-level allocation becomes a special case of allocating budget over tree nodes. The shared principle is混合奖励对比结构mixed-reward contrast construction：将推出预算分配给其后代集最有可能包含成功和失败结果的锚点。从根本上讲，该原则统一了提示过滤和部署计数分配，零预算拒绝提示，正组预算设置其根部署计数。在活动提示内，相同的原则将额外的分支分配给可能产生相反结果兄弟的前缀。: allocate rollout budget to anchors whose descendant sets are most likely to contain both successful and failed outcomes. At the root, this principle unifies prompt filtering and rollout-count allocation, with zero budget rejecting a prompt and positive group budget setting its root rollout count. Inside an active prompt, the same principle allocates extra branches to prefixes likely to produce opposite-outcome siblings.

3 通过对比构建的视角进行部署分配Rollout Allocation through the Lens of Contrast Construction

When rollouts sharing the same prompt root or prefix disagree in terminal outcome while only the continuation differs, they yield implicit pairwise preferences over sibling continuations rather than a single sparse terminal reward shared across the entire rollout. We therefore treat mixed-reward contrast construction as the allocation objective: a fixed rollout budget is useful to the extent that it induces such pairwise comparison at prompt roots and visited prefixes.

3.2 词根和前缀的共享混合结果原则A Shared Mixed-Outcome Principle for Roots and Prefixes

6 结论Conclusion

这项工作将混合奖励对比分配制定为多轮智能体的样本高效 RLVR 的统一视图。我们表明，提示过滤、转出计数分配和前缀分支都是针对转出树锚点的预算决策，并提出 TRACE 以在条件成功概率的共享通用预测器的指导下分配预算。 TRACE 将预算引导至其后代可能会暴露混合最终结果的锚点，从而加强了仅结果奖励下的群体相关更新信号和隐式逐步偏好对。数学推理、多跳 QA 和函数调用的实验证实了在匹配的预算下提高了准确性和推出信息量。此外，这种对比分配视图将代理 RLVR 采样重新定义为在 rollout 树中进行分支的问题，而不仅仅是绘制多少个独立的 rollout。This work formulates mixed-reward contrast allocation as a unified view of sample-efficient RLVR for multi-turn agents. We show that prompt filtering, rollout-count allocation, and prefix branching are all budget decisions over rollout-tree anchors, and present TRACE to allocate budget guided by a shared generalizable predictor of conditional success probability. TRACE directs budget toward anchors whose descendants are likely to expose mixed terminal outcomes, strengthening the group-relative update signal and implicit stepwise preference pairs under outcome-only rewards. Experiments on Mathematical Reasoning, Multi-Hop QA, and Function Calling confirm improved accuracy and rollout informativeness under a matched budget. Moreover, this contrast-allocation view reframes agentic RLVR sampling as a question of where to branch within the rollout tree, not only how many independent rollouts to draw.

局限性Limitations

TRACE 主要是为仅结果的 RLVR 开发的，因此没有明确终端验证的任务可能需要重新审视混合结果对比目标。对于分配，更高的预测精度可以带来更好的预算布局，同时预测器实例化仍然与 TRACE 框架正交。在这项工作中，我们在普通环境中实例化预测器，并且针对不同代理任务的更强大的替代方案仍然是未来的工作。根据经验，我们的评估主要针对 Qwen3-8B 和 Qwen3-14B 的数学推理、多跳 QA 和函数调用进行，为未来的探索留下更复杂和非平稳的代理场景。TRACE is mainly developed for outcome-only RLVR, and tasks without clear terminal verification may therefore require revisiting the mixed-outcome contrast objective. For allocation, higher prediction accuracy can lead to better budget placement, while predictor instantiations remain orthogonal to the TRACE framework. In this work, we instantiate the predictor in a vanilla setting, and stronger alternatives for different agentic tasks remain future work. Empirically, our evaluation is mainly conducted on Mathematical Reasoning, Multi-Hop QA, and Function Calling with Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-14B, leaving more complex and non-stationary agentic scenarios for future exploration.

RLVR 和代理 RLVR。RLVR and Agentic RLVR.

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为法学硕士在具有自动可检查结果的任务上的主要培训后范例Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has become a dominant post-training paradigm for LLMs on tasks with automatically checkable outcomes(Jaech et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025a; Team et al., 2025)。近端策略优化 (PPO). Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)(Schulman et al., 2017)仍然是一般策略梯度骨干，而组相对策略优化（GRPO）remains a general policy-gradient backbone, while Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)(Shao et al., 2024)对于 RLVR 来说特别有吸引力，因为它消除了昂贵的价值模型，并从采样的部署组中估计了优势。 RLVR 的大部分仍然处于扁平状态，其中对提示进行采样，生成完整的响应，并由最终奖励驱动更新。代理环境打破了这种抽象。部署是一系列思想-行动-观察的转变is especially attractive for RLVR because it removes the expensive value model and estimates advantages from groups of sampled rollouts. Much of RLVR still lives in a flat regime where a prompt is sampled, complete responses are generated, and a terminal reward drives the update. Agentic environments fracture this abstraction. A rollout is a chain of thought-action-observation turns(Yao et al., 2022)，以及搜索查询、工具调用和部分解决方案状态等中间前缀可以改变整个下游轨迹。 TRACE 使用此结构将推出预算从标量采样计数转变为跨根和前缀的结构分配决策。, and intermediate prefixes such as search queries, tool calls, and partial solution states can alter the entire downstream trajectory. TRACE uses this structure to turn rollout budget from a scalar sampling count into a structural allocation decision across roots and prefixes.

高效 RLVR 示例。Sample Efficient RLVR.

样本高效的 RLVR 通过决定哪些样本值得推出预算来改进策略更新。离线管理过滤器按难度、多样性、长度或估计的解决方案质量进行提示Sample-efficient RLVR improves policy updates by deciding which examples deserve rollout budget. Offline curation filters prompts by difficulty, diversity, length, or estimated solution quality(Ye et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025b; Wen et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2025a; Yang et al., 2024; Fatemi et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025)，降低训练成本，但增加预处理并随着策略的发展保持静态, reducing training cost but adding preprocessing and remaining static as the policy evolves(Qu et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2025; Mao et al., 2026b)。在线提示选择通过适应当前模型（通过基于推出的无信息提示的过滤）来解决此限制. Online prompt selection addresses this limitation by adapting to the current model, either through rollout-based filtering of uninformative prompts(Yu et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025; Cui et al., 2025; Meng et al., 2025; Bae et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026b)或通过估计即时难度的预测器or through predictors that estimate prompt difficulty(Zhang et al., 2025b; Zheng et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2025; Qu et al., 2025; Zeng et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025; Mao et al., 2026a; Qu et al., 2026; Zou et al., 2025a; Zhang et al., 2026a; Hu et al., 2025b; Shen et al., 2025)。互补的提示级别分配行询问每个选定的提示应接收多少个样本. A complementary prompt-level allocation line asks how many samples each selected prompt should receive(Li et al., 2025c; Yao et al., 2026)。该行对于动态推出会计是有效的，但它通常依赖于预先计算的每个提示的准确性，并且在没有明确推出的情况下概括到看不见的提示的能力有限。 TRACE 将过滤、推出计数和前缀分支视为一个分配操作的边界情况，使用共享的可泛化预测器对从提示根到内部前缀的候选锚点进行评分，并使用分配器根据预测的条件成功概率分配预算。. This line is effective for dynamic rollout accounting, but it typically relies on pre-calculated per-prompt accuracy and has limited ability to generalize to unseen prompts without explicit rollouts. TRACE treats filtering, rollout counts, and prefix branching as boundary cases of one allocation operation, using a shared generalizable predictor to score candidate anchors from prompt roots to internal prefixes and an allocator to assign budget according to predicted conditional success probability.

LLM 的树搜索。Tree Search for LLMs.

树搜索让法学硕士可以在交互前缀上进行分支并与替代延续进行比较，而不是对单个完整轨迹进行采样。测试时方法，例如思想树Tree search lets LLMs branch over interaction prefixes and compare with alternative continuations, rather than sampling a single complete trajectory. Test-time methods such as Tree-of-Thought(Yao et al., 2023; Long, 2023; Snell et al., 2024; Koh et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2023)和 MCTS 风格的解码and MCTS-style decoding(Xin et al., 2025)在测试时花费额外的计算来为每个提示搜索更强的答案。离线变体对推理树进行采样或评分，并将诱导比较提取为 SFT 或偏好学习数据spend additional computation at test time to search for a stronger answer for each prompt. Offline variants sample or score reasoning trees and distill the induced comparisons into SFT or preference-learning data(Feng et al., 2023; He et al., 2024b; Xie et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025a; Lai et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025a)。基于树的在线强化学习将分支引入训练，使用树的推出进行价值估计或更精细的信用分配. 稀疏结果奖励对于多回合智能体来说是粗略信号，因为一个最终的成功或失败可以分配回许多早期决策，而中间决策可以对最终结果承担截然不同的责任Sparse outcome rewards are coarse signals for multi-turn agents because one terminal success or failure can be assigned back to many earlier decisions while intermediate decisions can carry sharply different responsibility for the final result(Feng et al., 2025)。经典强化学习通过探索奖励、重放、目标重新标记、奖励塑造或基于价值的信用估计来解决这个问题. Classical RL attacks this with exploration bonuses, replay, goal relabeling, reward shaping, or value-based credit estimates(Pathak et al., 2017; Andrychowicz et al., 2017; Ng et al., 1999; Arjona-Medina et al., 2019)。在 LLM 代理中，流程奖励模型 (PRM) 可以生成步骤级或令牌级反馈，但密集的流程奖励注释成本高昂，并且在开放式工具使用设置中很脆弱。相反，树推出已经通过比较共享前缀的延续提供了一种隐式信用分配形式. In LLM agents, process reward models (PRMs) can generate step- or token-level feedback, but dense process rewards are costly to annotate and brittle across open-ended tool-use settings. Instead, tree rollouts already provide a form of implicit credit assignment by comparing continuations that share a prefix(Ji et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2026)。 TRACE 在没有显式过程监督的情况下加强了这种机制，通过将更多分支分配给其替代延续可能在结果上发散的前缀，将稀疏的最终奖励转变为更尖锐的本地偏好信号。. TRACE strengthens this mechanism without explicit process supervision by allocating more branches to prefixes whose alternative continuations are likely to diverge in outcome, turning sparse final rewards into sharper local preference signals.
