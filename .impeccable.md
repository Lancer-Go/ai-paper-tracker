## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience:** AI researchers and developers tracking daily academic papers.
- **Context:** Daily checking for the latest, most impactful papers ranked by comprehensive citation and community data.
- **Job to be done:** Efficiently discovering and tracking unrestricted, complete academic paper trends without missing vital data.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone:** Professional, authoritative, and frictionless.
- **3-Word Personality:** Convenient (方便), Comprehensive (全面), Unrestricted (自由/无限制).
- **Emotional Goals:** Instill **professional trust (专业信任)** in the data and the platform, making the user feel confident in their research process.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone:** Modern, professional, and data-dense. Must prioritize readability and data completeness.
- **Theme:** Currently dark mode utilizing Indigo/Cyan/Pink accents on deep blue/black backgrounds. **Must be structured to support a future Light Mode transition** (ensure semantic tokens are used).
- **Other Preferences:** Accessibility compliance is not required, allowing for higher data density and specialized layouts geared toward expert users.

### Design Principles
1. **Prioritize Data & Trust:** Paper metrics (citations, authors, ranks) must be the most prominent elements. Visuals should support the data, not distract from it, to reinforce professional trust.
2. **Frictionless Convenience:** Avoid unnecessary clicks, modals, or artificial pagination. Let researchers access comprehensive data immediately and without restrictions.
3. **Future-Proof for Light Mode:** Avoid hardcoded dark-mode hex values where possible. Use scalable design variables/tokens so that adding a light mode later will be seamless.
4. **Expert-Centric Density:** Since strict accessibility isn't a requirement, favor higher information density and power-user layouts over overly simplified interfaces.
