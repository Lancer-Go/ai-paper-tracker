摘要Abstract

跨领域知识对齐对于集成异构医疗系统至关重要，但现有方法通常将实体对齐视为静态匹配问题，忽略查询上下文和跨系统不对称性。 这种限制在综合医疗环境中尤其重要，因为概念之间的对应关系本质上是上下文相关的、非双射的和方向敏感的。Cross-domain knowledge alignment is essential for integrating heterogeneous medical systems, yet existing approaches typically treat entity alignment as a static matching problem, ignoring query context and cross-system asymmetry. This limitation is particularly critical in integrative medical settings, where correspondence between concepts is inherently context-dependent, non-bijective, and direction-sensitive.

在本文中，我们提出了查询条件实体对齐（QCEA），它将实体对齐重新表述为查询条件对应问题。 QCEA 不是学习实体表示之间的固定映射，而是将源实体的文本描述视为查询并对目标图中的候选实体进行排名，从而实现上下文相关的对齐。 该框架集成了语义编码、基于图的表示学习和方向感知转换模块，以捕获跨异构知识系统的不对称和多对多对应关系。In this paper, we propose Query-Conditioned Entity Alignment (QCEA), which reformulates entity alignment as a query-conditioned correspondence problem. Instead of learning a fixed mapping between entity representations, QCEA treats the textual description of a source entity as a query and ranks candidate entities in the target graph, enabling context-dependent alignment. The framework integrates semantic encoding, graph-based representation learning, and a direction-aware transformation module to capture asymmetric and many-to-many correspondence across heterogeneous knowledge systems.

我们在源自 SymMap 的中医-西医知识图上评估 QCEA，涵盖症状对齐和草药-分子对齐任务。 实验结果表明，与代表性基线相比，有一致的改进，特别是在排名敏感的指标（例如 Hit@K 和 MRR）上。 此外，下游检索增强生成（RAG）实验表明，改进的对齐可以带来更好的证据检索、更强的基础和更高的答案准确性。 这些发现强调，对齐不仅仅是数据集成步骤，而且是塑造跨系统医学推理中知识可访问性和可靠性的关键因素。We evaluate QCEA on TCM–WM knowledge graphs derived from SymMap, covering both symptom alignment and herb–molecule alignment tasks. Experimental results show consistent improvements over representative baselines, particularly on rank-sensitive metrics such as Hit@K and MRR. Furthermore, downstream retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) experiments demonstrate that improved alignment leads to better evidence retrieval, stronger grounding, and higher answer accuracy. These findings highlight that alignment is not merely a data integration step, but a key factor that shapes knowledge accessibility and reliability in cross-system medical reasoning.

2 文献综述Literature Review

跨系统医学知识集成涉及调整异构概念系统以支持可靠的检索和推理。 在中医和西医等环境中，术语和抽象的差异给一致的知识基础带来了挑战Cross-system medical knowledge integration involves aligning heterogeneous conceptual systems to support reliable retrieval and reasoning. In settings such as TCM and WM, discrepancies in terminology and abstraction create challenges for consistent knowledge grounding[nicholson2020constructing, xue2024biomedical]。 现有的研究涵盖实体对齐、语义匹配和医学知识集成，但这些方向仍然松散地联系在一起，特别是在处理上下文相关和不对称对应方面。. Existing research spans entity alignment, semantic matching, and medical knowledge integration, but these directions remain loosely connected, particularly in handling context-dependent and asymmetric correspondence.

2.1 知识图谱实体对齐Knowledge Graph Entity Alignment

知识图实体对齐侧重于通过学习保留跨图相似性的表示来识别异构图上的对应实体Knowledge graph entity alignment focuses on identifying corresponding entities across heterogeneous graphs by learning representations that preserve cross-graph similarity[zhu2024survey]。 现有方法的主要不同之处在于如何构建此类表示。 结构方法利用图拓扑来捕获关系一致性. Existing approaches differ mainly in how such representations are constructed. Structural methods leverage graph topology to capture relational consistency[wang2018cross, wu2019relation, yang2019aligning]，基于嵌入的模型学习统一的潜在空间以进行对齐, embedding-based models learn unified latent spaces for alignment[sun2017cross, chen2017multilingual, zhang2019multike, sun2018bootstrapping, xiang2021ontoea]，以及最近的努力探索通过对比学习、多视图建模和文本特征的使用来提高表示质量, and more recent efforts explore improving representation quality through contrastive learning, multi-view modeling, and the use of textual features[cheng2025easyea, yang2025daea].

尽管存在这些差异，大多数现有方法将对齐公式化为实体表示之间的固定成对相似性函数，即一旦学习了嵌入，实体之间的上下文无关的映射。 在跨系统医疗环境中，这种假设经常被违反，其中对应关系取决于实体语义的描述方式，并且可能因上下文而异，从而导致不对称和多对多关系。 因此，固定的成对匹配不足以捕获跨系统对应的可变性。 此外，这些方法并不自然地与检索管道保持一致，其中候选相关性以查询为条件并直接影响下游推理。Despite these differences, most existing methods formulate alignment as a fixed pairwise similarity function between entity representations, i.e., a context-independent mapping between entities once embeddings are learned. This assumption is often violated in cross-system medical settings, where correspondence depends on how entity semantics are described and may vary across contexts, leading to asymmetric and many-to-many relationships. As a result, fixed pairwise matching is insufficient to capture the variability of cross-system correspondence. In addition, these approaches are not naturally aligned with retrieval pipelines, where candidate relevance is conditioned on a query and directly affects downstream reasoning.

2.2 查询条件语义匹配和检索Query-Conditioned Semantic Matching and Retrieval

信息检索中的语义匹配通常被视为查询条件排序问题，代表性方法包括 DRMM、DPR 和 ColBERTSemantic matching in information retrieval is commonly framed as a query-conditioned ranking problem, with representative approaches including DRMM, DPR, and ColBERT[guo2016deep, karpukhin2020dense, khattab2020colbert]。 这些方法在 RAG 系统中被广泛采用，用于检索以下游生成的输入查询为条件的证据。. These methods are widely adopted in RAG systems, where they are used to retrieve evidence conditioned on the input query for downstream generation.

然而，这些模型在单个语义空间内运行，并且没有明确地对异构概念系统之间的对应关系进行建模。 虽然它们捕获依赖于查询的相关性，但对齐还需要解决跨系统的歧义，其中映射可能是不对称的或一对多的。 因此，即使以查询为条件，单独的检索模型也无法确保跨系统一致地访问一致的知识。However, these models operate within a single semantic space and do not explicitly model correspondence across heterogeneous conceptual systems. While they capture query-dependent relevance, alignment additionally requires resolving cross-system ambiguity, where mappings may be asymmetric or one-to-many. Consequently, even when conditioned on queries, retrieval models alone cannot ensure consistent access to aligned knowledge across systems.

3.8 讨论Discussion

QCEA 在三个方面不同于传统的实体对齐。 它将静态成对匹配替换为查询条件排名，将查询构造与图感知实体表示学习解耦，并引入了具有残差融合的方向感知塔克投影以进行目标端转换。QCEA departs from conventional entity alignment in three aspects. It replaces static pairwise matching with query-conditioned ranking, decouples query construction from graph-aware entity representation learning, and introduces a direction-aware Tucker projection with residual fusion for target-side transformation.

在这个公式下，双向训练引入了多对多的对齐结构，该结构通过多重正对比目标自然地进行了优化。 与侧重于相关性估计或成对相似性的检索模型和 LLM 增强对齐方法相比，QCEA 将对齐公式化为查询条件排名问题，将训练目标与基于检索的推理对齐。Under this formulation, bidirectional training induces a many-to-many alignment structure, which is naturally optimized via a multi-positive contrastive objective. In contrast to retrieval models and LLM-enhanced alignment methods that focus on relevance estimation or pairwise similarity, QCEA formulates alignment as a query-conditioned ranking problem, aligning the training objective with retrieval-based inference.

理论洞察力。Theoretical insight.

当跨系统对应关系依赖于上下文时，实体之间的固定确定性对应关系本质上是不够的，因为它无法表示单个函数内的一对多或依赖于描述的对齐。 在这种情况下，对齐目标取决于查询上下文，而不是仅由源实体确定，因为相同的源概念可能对应于不同描述下的不同目标。 QCEA 通过将对齐建模为查询条件排名函数来解决此限制，从而实现上下文感知和非双射对应。When cross-system correspondence is context-dependent, a fixed deterministic correspondence between entities is inherently insufficient, as it cannot represent one-to-many or description-dependent alignments within a single function. In such cases, the alignment target becomes conditional on the query context rather than solely determined by the source entity, since the same source concept may correspond to different targets under different descriptions. QCEA addresses this limitation by modeling alignment as a query-conditioned ranking function, enabling context-aware and non-bijective correspondence.

6 讨论和启示Discussion and Implications

实验结果一致表明，跨域对齐质量在多个维度的下游性能中起着核心作用。 QCEA 改进了排名靠前的指标，例如 Hit@1、Hit@10 和 MRR，特别是在症状任务中，这表明查询条件对齐有效地将相关候选者集中在排名顶部附近。The experimental results consistently indicate that cross-domain alignment quality plays a central role in downstream performance across multiple dimensions. QCEA improves top-ranked metrics such as Hit@1, Hit@10, and MRR, particularly in the Symptom task, suggesting that query-conditioned alignment effectively concentrates relevant candidates near the top of the ranking.

消融研究进一步强调，查询条件是驱动性能的主导因素，而方向感知转换和图形传播提供了互补的改进，特别是在具有非对称映射的更具挑战性的设置中。训练动态揭示了任务之间的结构差异，Herb 任务由于其多对多对应性质而表现出较慢的收敛速度。The ablation study further highlights that query conditioning is the dominant factor driving performance, while direction-aware transformation and graph propagation provide complementary improvements, especially in more challenging settings with asymmetric mappings. Training dynamics reveal structural differences between tasks, with the Herb task exhibiting slower convergence due to its many-to-many correspondence nature.

除了对齐指标之外，下游 RAG 评估还表明，对齐质量始终会影响答案的准确性、基础性和整体可靠性。 当对准度降低时，系统会表现出接地减少和不稳定性增加，而改进的对准度会导致更加一致和基于证据的输出。Beyond alignment metrics, downstream RAG evaluation demonstrates that alignment quality consistently affects answer accuracy, grounding, and overall reliability. When alignment is degraded, the system exhibits reduced grounding and increased instability, whereas improved alignment leads to more consistent and evidence-based outputs.

这些观察结果表明，对齐可以被视为影响如何获取和组织推理知识的结构层。 对齐不是充当独立的推理机制，而是塑造推理系统从中获取证据的候选空间。 从这个意义上说，对齐质量的改进往往会传播到下游推理性能。These observations suggest that alignment can be viewed as a structural layer that influences how knowledge is accessed and organized for reasoning. Rather than acting as a standalone reasoning mechanism, alignment shapes the candidate space from which reasoning systems draw evidence. In this sense, improvements in alignment quality tend to propagate to downstream reasoning performance.

对齐作为上游瓶颈。Alignment as an upstream bottleneck.

我们的 RAG 实验表明，对齐是一个上游瓶颈：对齐错误直接传播到检索中，并且很难通过生成模型来纠正。 在受控生成设置下，模型 (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct) 无法补偿缺失或错位的跨系统证据。 始终如一，提高对齐质量会带来更好的端到端性能，而对齐质量下降会导致相应的性能下降。Our RAG experiments reveal that alignment acts as an upstream bottleneck: errors in alignment propagate directly into retrieval, and are difficult to correct by the generation model. Under a controlled generation setting, the model (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct) is unable to compensate for missing or misaligned cross-system evidence. Consistently, improving alignment quality leads to better end-to-end performance, while degradation in alignment results in corresponding performance drops.

局限性。Limitations.我们的结论基于实证评估，本身并不建立对齐和下游推理之间的因果关系。 此外，实验是在传导环境中进行的，重点是中医与西医的整合，这可能会限制推广到其他领域。 最后，在 RAG 评估中使用轻量级语言模型强调了对齐质量的影响，但结果可能会因模型较大而有所不同。Our conclusions are based on empirical evaluation and do not by themselves establish causal relationships between alignment and downstream reasoning. 影响。Implications.这些发现表明，改善跨领域对齐是增强知识集成系统的实用且有效的方向。 未来的工作可能会探索将结构化对齐与生成模型相结合的混合方法，以及更广泛的多领域知识设置的扩展。These findings suggest that improving cross-domain alignment is a practical and effective direction for enhancing knowledge integration systems. Future work may explore hybrid approaches that combine structured alignment with generative models, as well as extensions to broader multi-domain knowledge settings.

7 结论Conclusions

我们提出了 QCEA，一种查询条件实体对齐框架，用于桥接异构 TCM 和 WM 知识图。 通过将对齐重新表述为查询条件排序问题，QCEA 捕获了跨系统医学知识中上下文相关的、不对称的和非双射的对应关系。 对 SymMap 派生数据集的实验表明，与代表性基线相比，取得了一致的改进，在语义模糊设置和排名敏感的检索场景中取得了显着的进步。 进一步的下游 RAG 评估表明，改进的一致性增强了跨系统证据的可用性和排名，从而提高了检索有效性、基础性和端到端可靠性，同时减少了跨系统医学 QA 中不受支持或不一致的输出。We presented QCEA, a query-conditioned entity alignment framework for bridging heterogeneous TCM and WM knowledge graphs. By reformulating alignment as a query-conditioned ranking problem, QCEA captures context-dependent, asymmetric, and non-bijective correspondence in cross-system medical knowledge. Experiments on SymMap-derived datasets demonstrate consistent improvements over representative baselines, with notable gains in semantically ambiguous settings and rank-sensitive retrieval scenarios. Further downstream RAG evaluation shows that improved alignment enhances the availability and ranking of cross-system evidence, leading to improved retrieval effectiveness, grounding, and end-to-end reliability, while reducing unsupported or inconsistent outputs in cross-system medical QA.

总的来说，这些结果表明查询条件对齐为整合异构医学知识和支持基于知识的推理提供了一种有效且实用的方法。 未来的工作将探索整合更丰富的临床背景，提高稀疏监督下的鲁棒性，并将对齐扩展到更复杂的多跳推理设置。Overall, these results suggest that query-conditioned alignment provides an effective and practical approach for integrating heterogeneous medical knowledge and supporting knowledge-grounded reasoning. Future work will explore incorporating richer clinical context, improving robustness under sparse supervision, and extending alignment to more complex multi-hop reasoning settings.
