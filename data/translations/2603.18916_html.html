摘要Abstract

本文提出了一份宣言，阐明了以下概念的基础：This paper presents a manifesto that articulates the conceptual foundations of代理业务流程管理Agentic Business Process Management(APM)，业务流程管理 (BPM) 的扩展，用于管理组织中执行流程的自主代理。 从管理角度来看，APM 代表了业务流程的传统流程视图的范式转变，由流程意识的实现和面向代理的抽象驱动，其中软件和人类代理充当主要功能实体，在显式流程框架内感知、推理和行动。这种观点标志着从传统的、面向自动化的 BPM 向自主性受到限制、协调并通过流程意识进行操作的系统的转变。(APM), an extension of Business Process Management (BPM) for governing autonomous agents executing processes in organizations. From a management perspective, APM represents a paradigm shift from the traditional process view of the business process, driven by the realization of process awareness and an agent-oriented abstraction, where software and human agents act as primary functional entities that perceive, reason, and act within explicit process frames. This perspective marks a shift from traditional, automation-oriented BPM toward systems in which autonomy is constrained, aligned, and made operational through process awareness.

我们介绍了实现 APM 系统所需的核心抽象和架构元素，并详细说明了此类 APM 代理必须支持的四个关键功能：We introduce the core abstractions and architectural elements required to realize APM systems and elaborate on four key capabilities that such APM agents must support:框架自治framed autonomy, 可解释性explainability, 对话的可操作性conversational actionability, 和, and自我修改self-modification。这些功能共同确保代理的目标与组织目标保持一致，并且代理在追求这些目标时以有框架但主动的方式行事。我们讨论了这些功能可以实现的程度，并确定了研究挑战，其解决方案需要 BPM、人工智能和多代理系统的进一步进步。因此，该宣言可作为连接这些社区并指导 APM 系统在实践中开发的路线图。. These capabilities jointly ensure that agents’ goals are aligned with organizational goals and that agents behave in a framed yet proactive manner in pursuing those goals. We discuss the extent to which the capabilities can be realized and identify research challenges whose resolution requires further advances in BPM, AI, and multi-agent systems. The manifesto thus serves as a roadmap for bridging these communities and for guiding the development of APM systems in practice.

APM 的一个核心挑战是理解如何设计代理，使其推理和行为始终符合其规定的框架，即指导其行为的规范、目标、约束和流程逻辑。问题不仅仅是如何向智能体提供这样的框架，而是如何确保智能体积极使用它、内化它，并将其视为具有工具价值的。这需要开发方法来定义、衡量和加强智能体的框架合规程度，将其作为一种持续的、可学习的属性。这就提出了构建激励结构来培养代理人对流程意识的“愿望”的困难。人类可以通过金钱补偿、津贴或认可来激励。人工智能代理需要一个功能对应物来重塑其内部效用模型。悬而未决的问题是如何将奖励设计为框架的一部分，使遵守框架对代理有利，同时仍然保留自主权。为了支持这一点，强化机制必须直接融入代理生命周期中。随着时间的推移，代理必须了解框架的哪些组件真正有用，哪些元素有效地限制它们，以及哪些部分可以在不违反安全、治理或组织政策的情况下降低优先级。A central challenge in APM is understanding how to design agents whose reasoning and behavior consistently align with their prescribed frame, meaning the norms, goals, constraints, and process logic meant to guide their actions. Over time, agents must learn which components of the frame are genuinely useful, which elements constrain them productively, and which parts might be deprioritized without violating safety, governance, or organizational policies.

APM 系统的可解释性方面的挑战集中在技术上合理的解释上，考虑到其预期和实际的解释对象，这些解释易于理解且可操作。Challenges with respect to the explainability in APM systems center around technically sound explanations that are easily understood and actionable, given their intended and actual explainees.

X1：如何指定或让智能体了解其他智能体对解释的偏好？X1: How to specify or let agents learn other agents’ preferences regarding explanations?

APM 系统中可以使用各种启发机制，以通过各种渠道有效捕获解释偏好，无论是在代理规范中预先明确声明、通过对话交互地引出，还是从用户行为隐式推断。A variety of elicitation mechanisms may be used in an APM system to effectively capture explanation preferences through various channels, whether explicitly declared upfront in the agentic specification, interactively elicited through dialogue, or implicitly inferred from user behavior.

系统必须适应保持一致的静态偏好和动态适应不断变化的环境的偏好，同时支持偏好随着解释者（人类或数字）理解的发展而自然演变。在这里，面临的挑战是找到保持解释最新和不让被解释者感到困惑（即限制其自主权）之间的最佳平衡点。最后，我们必须考虑何时以及如何提供完整与增量的解释更新。Systems must accommodate both static preferences that remain consistent and those that dynamically adapt to changing contexts, while supporting the natural evolution of preferences as the explainee’s, human or digital, understanding develops. Here, the challenge is to find the sweet spot between keeping explanations up to date and not confusing the explainee (i.e., limiting its autonomy). Finally, we have to think about when and how to provide full versus incremental explanation updates.

This is because making sense of agent behavior goes beyond explaining the AI models used under the hood; it must also account for the broader context in which agents perform their actions, the knowledge they may have acquired over time, possible cause-and-effect relationships among their actions, and the constraints (i.e., framing) imposed on their behavior. In this way, the explainee may be able to autonomously act upon the condition at hand. However, further work is needed to devise a systematic approach that enables the explainer to determine the most effective content for the explainee, to elicit such corrective action—taking into account both the explanandum and the behavioral intentions of the explainee.

并非每对动作执行都是因果相关的。因此，为了生成合理的解释，区分代理行为中或跨多个代理的虚假相关操作（例如，仅在时间上顺序的操作）与因果依赖操作（即，当一个操作直接触发另一个操作的执行时）非常重要。Not every pair of action executions is causally dependent. To generate sound explanations, it is therefore important to distinguish spuriously correlated actions (e.g., those that are merely temporally sequential) from causally dependent ones (i.e., when one action directly triggers the execution of another) within an agent’s behavior or across multiple agents.
