我们不是提出另一种可靠性估计器，而是问如何定义、诱导、量化对派生测量的过度依赖，并将其与一般的谨慎区分开来。特殊的心电图信号提供了一个具体的案例研究，但研究的主要对象是下游故障本身以及用于表征它的数量。我们的贡献是：Rather than proposing another reliability estimator, we ask how over-reliance on derived measurements should be defined, induced, quantified, and distinguished from generic caution. 讨论Discussion

DFOT作为研究目标。DFOT as a research target.

这项工作的主要贡献是问题定义和测量框架。模块化人工智能系统通常通过上游预测性能或下游任务准确性进行评估，而它们之间的接口在很大程度上未经检查。上游估计平均而言可能是准确的，但对于特定实例而言并不可靠，而下游模型可能会连贯地推理，但赋予该估计比其出处支持的更多权威。 DFOT 命名了这种故障，并将其归纳、修复、实例特异性和接口成本分成可测试的估计值。匹配洗牌干预进一步测试修订是否取决于与当前实例相对应的证据，而不仅仅是可靠性语言的存在。The principal contribution of this work is a problem definition and measurement framework. Modular AI systems are commonly evaluated through either upstream prediction performance or downstream task accuracy, leaving the interface between them largely unexamined. An upstream estimate may be accurate on average yet unreliable for a particular instance, while a downstream model may reason coherently but assign that estimate more authority than its provenance supports. DFOT names this failure and separates its induction, repair, instance specificity, and interface cost into testable estimands. The matched–shuffled intervention further tests whether revision depends on evidence corresponding to the current instance rather than on the mere presence of reliability language.

案例研究证实了什么。What the case study establishes.

PPG-ECG 案例研究表明该框架是非退化的且具有歧视性。这两个挑战都会重复地引发其预期的失败，度量链将 B2 与 K2 区分开来，并且比较会在来自患者不相交的锁定测试分区的记录上复制。这种对比在 P1 下测试的 LLM 系列和 Qwen3-8B 中的提示配方中也持续存在。The PPG–ECG case study shows that the framework is non-degenerate and discriminative. Both challenges reproducibly induce their intended failures, the metric chain distinguishes B2 from K2, and the comparison replicates on records from the patient-disjoint locked-test partition. The contrasts also persist across the tested LLM families under P1 and across prompt formulations within Qwen3-8B.

D1 和 D2 探测不适当依赖的互补形式：接受误导性的派生测量并允许超出范围的历史背景覆盖有效的当前测量。 K2 改进了 D1 匹配修复和 ESRM，尽管大量的残余误差为更强大的方法留下了明显的空间。 D2 匹配修复已经接近上限，这使得其较大的 ESRM 改进比其较小的 CRR 增益更具信息性。尽管剩余修复空间有限，K2 仍能更强有力地将匹配的当前病例证据与重组的捐赠者区分开来，这说明了为什么绝对 CRR 和 ESRM 应联合解释。D1 and D2 probe complementary forms of inappropriate reliance: accepting a misleading derived measurement and allowing out-of-scope historical context to override a valid current measurement. K2 improves D1 matched repair and ESRM, although substantial residual error leaves clear headroom for stronger methods. D2 matched repair is already near ceiling, making its larger ESRM improvement more informative than its smaller CRR gain. K2 more strongly separates matched current-case evidence from a shuffled donor despite limited remaining repair headroom, illustrating why absolute CRR and ESRM should be interpreted jointly.

因此，K2 应被视为新定义任务的参考基线，而不是最终的解决方案。 ECG 参考蒸馏提供了一种方便的部署时仅 PPG 可靠性信号，可用于运行该框架。这些消融表明正则化和类别级别结构解释了其部分好处，而锁定的全局洗牌比较支持正确的师生对应的额外贡献。未来的 DFOT 方法不需要使用相同的教师、架构、损失或证据表示。K2 should therefore be viewed as a reference baseline for the newly defined task rather than as a definitive solution. ECG-referenced distillation provides one convenient deployment-time PPG-only reliability signal with which to exercise the framework. The ablations suggest that regularization and category-level structure explain part of its benefit, while the locked global-shuffled comparison supports an additional contribution from correct teacher–student correspondence. Future DFOT methods need not use the same teacher, architecture, loss, or evidence representation.

为什么需要度量向量。Why a metric vector is necessary.

上游转移、下游修复、证据特异性和接口成本捕获不同的属性。改进的 AUROC 建立了更好的可靠性排名，而不是下游使用。增加 CRR 可以建立修复，但如果没有 ESRM，它就无法区分匹配案例证据的使用和一般谨慎。 UHR 单独量化了对基线时已可用的测量的不必要验证。Upstream transfer, downstream repair, evidence specificity, and interface cost capture different properties. Improved AUROC establishes better reliability ranking, not downstream use. Increased CRR establishes repair, but without ESRM it cannot distinguish matched-case evidence use from generic caution. UHR separately quantifies unnecessary verification on measurements that were already usable at baseline.

未解决的低 FPR 行为强化了这种分离：全局排名可以在不改善特定工作流程所需的严格操作区域的情况下提高。因此，DFOT 缓解是多目标的，涉及故障覆盖范围、修复、匹配案例特异性、严格区域行为和干预成本。探索性 2-pp UHR 限制是该向量内的接口工程约束，而不是通用的临床边界。The unresolved low-FPR behavior reinforces this separation: global ranking can improve without improving the stringent operating region required by a particular workflow. DFOT mitigation is therefore multi-objective, involving failure coverage, repair, matched-case specificity, stringent-region behavior, and intervention cost. The exploratory 2-pp UHR limit is an interface-engineering constraint within this vector, not a universal clinical margin.

超越生理感知的转移。Transfer beyond physiological sensing.

尽管实证案例研究使用同步 PPG 和 ECG，但只要上游模型生成由下游推理器使用的实例相关的派生测量，就会出现 DFOT。新的实例化需要派生的测量、独立定义的适当依赖标准、暴露不适当依赖的受控挑战，以及在保留接口的同时打破实例对应性的匹配洗牌或等效反事实控制。参考可能来自更高保真度的方式、重复测量、专家裁决或另一个独立指定的来源。跨领域传递的是问题定义、度量链和干预逻辑；特定的可靠性生成器和数值效应仍然是特定于应用的。Although the empirical case study uses synchronized PPG and ECG, DFOT can arise whenever an upstream model produces an instance-dependent derived measurement that is consumed by a downstream reasoner. A new instantiation requires a derived measurement, an independently defined criterion for appropriate reliance, a controlled challenge exposing inappropriate reliance, and a matched–shuffled or equivalent counterfactual control that breaks instance correspondence while preserving the interface. The reference may come from a higher-fidelity modality, repeated measurements, expert adjudication, or another independently specified source. What transfers across domains is the problem definition, metric chain, and intervention logic; the particular reliability generator and numerical effects remain application-specific.

研究议程。Research agenda.

未来的工作可以在同一冷冻下游协议中比较校准的不确定性头、集成、保形或选择性预测器、基于检索的验证以及更强的多模式学生。本研究的目的不是宣布 DFOT 可通过一种 ECG-PPG 方法解决，而是使先前扩散的界面故障变​​得可见、可测量和可比较。正式定义、受控挑战、关联估计值和参考基线建立了一个共同目标，可以根据该目标制定更强有力的缓解方法。Future work can compare calibrated uncertainty heads, ensembles, conformal or selective predictors, retrieval-based verification, and stronger multimodal students within the same frozen downstream protocol. The purpose of the present study is not to declare DFOT solved by one ECG–PPG method, but to make a previously diffuse interface failure visible, measurable, and comparable. The formal definition, controlled challenges, linked estimands, and reference baseline establish a common target against which stronger mitigation methods can be developed.

结论Conclusion

我们引入并形式化派生特征过度信任，提供五估计度量链，并使用匹配与洗牌干预措施来区分匹配案例修复与一般谨慎。一项协议锁定的 PPG-ECG 案例研究表明，该框架在测试患者、LLM 家庭和提示配方中具有可重复性和区分性。概念验证 K2 基线提高了可靠性排名、下游修复和证据包特异性，同时为更强大的缓解方法留下了明确的空间。尽管用生理信号进行了演示，但 DFOT 定义和评估链在下游模型对依赖于实例的派生测量进行推理的任何地方都适用更广泛。因此，DFOT 是一个具体的评估目标和一个开放的研究议程，以实现更可靠的测量到法学硕士的管道。We introduce and formalize derived-feature over-trust, provide a five-estimand metric chain, and use matched-versus-shuffled interventions to distinguish matched-case repair from generic caution. A protocol-locked PPG–ECG case study demonstrates that the framework is reproducible and discriminative across tested patients, LLM families, and prompt formulations. The proof-of-concept K2 baseline improves reliability ranking, downstream repair, and evidence-package specificity while leaving clear headroom for stronger mitigation methods. Although demonstrated with physiological signals, the DFOT definition and evaluation chain apply more broadly wherever downstream models reason over instance-dependent derived measurements. DFOT is therefore a concrete evaluation target and an open research agenda for more reliable measurement-to-LLM pipelines.

附录MAppendix M协议设计经验教训和纠正措施Protocol Design Lessons and Corrective Actions

无证据泄漏No-evidence leakage

早期的基线提示明确指示法学硕士在缺乏可靠性证据时进行验证。因此，该模型在表征之前避免了 DFOT，不会产生诱导子集。尽管运行在技术上已完成，但 CRR 和 ESRM 未定义，并且响应被排除。An early baseline prompt explicitly instructed the LLM to verify when reliability evidence was absent. The model consequently avoided DFOT before characterization, producing no induced subset. Although the run completed technically, CRR and ESRM were undefined and the responses were excluded.

临床状态/可靠性冲突Clinical-status/reliability collision

第二种协议使用一个可信/可疑输出来确保测量可靠性和节律状态。模型在临床上解释可疑，而评分者将其解释为不可靠。该门没有观察到有效的 D1/D2 特征，并在数以万计的无效调用之前停止了证据阶段。A second protocol used one TRUSTWORTHY/SUSPECT output for both measurement reliability and rhythm status. The model interpreted SUSPECT clinically, while the scorer interpreted it as unreliability. The gate observed no valid D1/D2 characterization and stopped the evidence stage before tens of thousands of invalid calls.

产生的规则Resulting rules

这些故障产生了六个要求：在缓解之前描述特征；使用机制特定的 D1/D2 提示；将测量用途与临床状态分开；仅随机化捐赠者来源，同时保持案例和证据模板固定（生成的标签和分数可能会共同变化）；在反应性缓解中重复使用准确的基线答案；并将提示、解析器、案例、阈值和统计数据冻结在一起。These failures produced six requirements: characterize before mitigating; use mechanism-specific D1/D2 prompts; separate measurement use from clinical status; randomize only the donor source while holding the case and evidence template fixed (the resulting label and score may change jointly); reuse the exact baseline answer in reactive mitigation; and freeze prompt, parser, cases, thresholds, and statistics together.
