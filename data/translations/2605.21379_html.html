1.2  已声明和未声明的内容1.2  What is and is not claimed

我们并不声称 PyVaCoAl/VaCoAl 是心灵，或者大脑实际上是一个 LFSR。该主张更为具体：引擎的架构承诺（XOR 和移位作为基本计算原语、原语多项式扩散作为每个块的结构指定操作、可逆组合绑定以及通过多数投票的收缩读出）逐个组件对应于 Marcus 在 2001 年确定的神经上合理的符号操作基质的缺失部分，并且对应关系足够精确，可以测试。在配套的观点中发展的生物阅读We do not claim that PyVaCoAl/VaCoAl is the mind, or that the brain is literally an LFSR. The claim is more specific: that the architectural commitments of the engine—XOR-and-shift as the elementary compute primitive, primitive-polynomial diffusion as the structurally specified operation per block, reversible compositional binding, and contractive readout through majority voting—correspond, component for component, to what Marcus identified in 2001 as the missing pieces of a neurally plausible symbol-manipulating substrate, and that the correspondence is precise enough to be testable. The biological reading developed in the companion Perspective[6]是对应关系的一个可证伪的实例；工程基材是另一个。代数思维程序不需要任何一个实例化都在字面上是正确的，以便从有一个具体的基础进行比较中获益。它所获得的成果是从“符号操纵在神经组织中必须是可能的”转变为“这是一个可以在其下进行符号操纵的代数，并且这就是基质必须看起来的样子。”is one falsifiable instantiation of the correspondence; the engineering substrate is another. The Algebraic Mind program does not need either instantiation to be literally correct in order to gain from having a concrete substrate to compare against. What it gains is the move from “symbol manipulation must be possible in neural tissue” to “here is an algebra under which it is possible, and here is what the substrate would have to look like.”

2.1  三大支柱2.1  The three pillars

Marcus [17]将符号操纵重构为三个可分离的假设，每个假设都可以独立成立或失败。我们以与下面进行的比较最相关的形式重述它们。reconstructs symbol manipulation as three separable hypotheses, each of which can stand or fall independently. We restate them in the form most relevant to the comparison developed below.

第 1 支柱（第 3 章）：变量运算。Pillar 1 (Chapter 3): Operations over variables.大脑代表变量之间的抽象关系，并且可以自由地将这些关系概括为变量的新实例。马库斯开发的典型示例是将动词词干与后缀组合在一起的变形规则The mind represents abstract relationships between variables and can freely generalize those relationships to novel instances of the variables. The canonical example Marcus develops is the inflection rule that combines a verb stem with the suffix-ed：该规则适用于任何动词，包括以前未遇到过的动词，因为它对变量进行操作: the rule applies to any verb, including verbs not previously encountered, because it operates on the variable茎stem而不是在存储的输入输出对的列表上。支柱的技术内容是必须存在一个操作rather than on a list of stored input–output pairs. The technical content of the pillar is that there must exist an operationf​(x)f(x)在变量的所有实例上统一定义that is defined uniformly over all instantiations of the variablexx，而不仅仅是存储的有限样本。马库斯称这些为, not merely over a stored finite sample. Marcus calls these普遍量化的一对一映射universally quantified one-to-one mappings（UQOTOM）。在标准的每变量多节点编码中，通过反向传播训练的多层感知器自然不支持对训练子空间之外的实例化的自由泛化；这是第三章的主要负面结果。(UQOTOMs). Multilayer perceptrons trained by backpropagation, in the standard many-nodes-per-variable encoding, do not naturally support free generalization to instantiations outside the trained subspace; this is the central negative result of Chapter 3.

第二支柱（第 4 章）：结构化表示。Pillar 2 (Chapter 4): Structured representations.思维有多种方式来表示由各组成部分组成的复杂结构，从而使各组成部分之间的结构关系得以保留和恢复。马库斯的常用例子是以下语法之间的对比：The mind has ways of representing complex structures composed of constituent parts, in such a way that the structural relationships among the constituents are preserved and recoverable. Marcus’s stock example is the syntactic contrast between桌子上的书the book that is on the table和and书上的表格the table that is on the book：成分相同，作用相同，结构关系不同，差异可以从表示中恢复。张量积表示: the constituents are identical, the roles are identical, the structural relationship differs, and the difference is recoverable from the representation. Tensor product representations[26]，循环卷积全息简化表示, circular convolution holographic reduced representations[22]，时间同步或异步方案, temporal synchrony or asynchrony schemes[25, 16]，以及联合编码都提供部分答案；马库斯在第 4 章中指出，每种方法都有特定的弱点，并提出小树作为替代方案。, and conjunctive coding all offer partial answers; Marcus argues in Chapter 4 that each has a specific weakness, and proposes the treelet as an alternative.

第三支柱（第五章）：与种类不同的个体。Pillar 3 (Chapter 5): Individuals distinct from kinds.大脑将特定个体的心理表征（猫菲利克斯，我坐的椅子）与这些个体实例化的心理表征（猫，椅子）区分开来。马库斯认为，这种区别是支持对象持久性、随时间追踪个体以及表示涉及特定实体而不是类别级别概括的命题所必需的。他指出，每个变量多个节点的编码破坏了这种区别，因为同一节点模式的两次激活无法区分，因为同一个人遇到了两次，而两个不同的个人却具有相似的属性。The mind distinguishes mental representations of specific individuals (Felix the cat, the chair I am sitting on) from mental representations of the kinds those individuals instantiate (cat, chair). Marcus argues that this distinction is required to support object permanence, to track individuals through time, and to represent propositions involving particular entities rather than category-level generalizations. He notes that many-nodes-per-variable encodings collapse the distinction, because two activations of the same node pattern cannot be distinguished as the same individual encountered twice versus two distinct individuals with similar properties.

2.2  三大支柱留下的空白2.2  What the three pillars leave open

马库斯明确表示他的计划没有解决什么问题。三个开放性问题，每个问题都在他的书中提出，是我们在以下各节中描述的工程基础的目标。Marcus is explicit about what his program does not settle. Three open questions, each foregrounded in his book, are the targets of the engineering substrate we describe in the following sections.

开放问题 A：寄存器运算的代数。Open question A: the algebra of operations on registers.Marcus 的小树提案（第 4.4 章）是关于如何在寄存器系统中实现分层结构的提案；它显然不是关于寄存器支持的基本操作的提案。小树提供了结构支架，其中的成分与角色绑定，但定义 Bind、Unbind 和递归组合的代数则留给实现。正如我们在第 4 节中展开的，这个悬而未决的问题在 GF(2) 中有一个明确的答案：XOR 和移位是 GF(2) 上的独特基本运算，它是可逆的，在移位组合下不可交换，并且可计算为单比特变换。在此读取中，小树是一个寄存器集，其基本运算是异或和移位，并且小树的代数是 GF(2) 的代数。Marcus’s treelet proposal (Chapter 4.4) is a proposal for how hierarchical structures can be implemented in a system of registers; it is explicitly not a proposal about the elementary operations the registers support. The treelet supplies the structural scaffold within which constituents are bound to roles, but the algebra under which Bind, Unbind, and recursive composition are defined is left to the implementation. As we develop in Section 4, this open question has a clean answer in GF(2): XOR-and-shift is the unique elementary operation over GF(2) that is reversible, non-commutative under shift composition, and computable as a single-bit-wise transformation. The treelet is, on this reading, a register set whose elementary operations are XOR-and-shift, and the algebra of the treelet is the algebra of GF(2).

What we do claim is that the program has, for the first time, a worked-out substrate to compare against—an algebra under which symbol manipulation is operational, a substrate under which the algebra is biologically plausible, and a set of testable predictions that distinguish the engine reading from the alternatives Marcus considered in 2001.
