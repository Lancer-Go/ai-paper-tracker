1 简介Introduction

近年来，大型语言模型（LLM）在不同场景下的自然语言生成、理解和响应方面不断取得进展，催生了一种新型人机社会学。在这种新兴的背景下，人类不再仅仅是模型的操作者，而是交互系统的共同居民，以及具有社会感知和表达能力的代理人In recent years, large language models (LLMs) have made continuous advances in generating, understanding, and responding to natural language under different scenarios, fostering the emergence of a new kind of human–machine sociology. In this emerging context, humans are no longer merely operators of models but co-inhabitants of interactive systems alongside agents capable of social perception and expression(Xi et al., 2025; Van Der Schyff et al., 2018; Kahl and Kopp, 2023)。 LLM代理越来越多地表现出“参与者”的特征，被定义为能够感知、推理和行动的自主实体. LLM agents are increasingly exhibiting the characteristics of ‘actors’, defined as autonomous entities capable of perception, reasoning, and action(Jiang et al., 2023; Dong et al., 2025)在这些自主行动者的赋能下，社会行为的模拟也发生了根本性的转变。研究人员现在可以对新兴的社会行为进行生成探索，而不是依赖编码的静态规则和预定义的动作.Empowered by these autonomous actors, the simulation of social behavior has also undergone a fundamental shift. Rather than relying on encoded static rules and predefined actions, researchers can now conduct generative explorations of emergent social behaviors(Gilbert and Troitzsch, 2005)。这种转变催生了人类与代理人的混合社会。. This transformation gives rise to human–agent Hybrid Societies.

在这些混合社会中，演员的“思想”并不是静态地预先编程的。与人类认知发展相似Within these hybrid societies, the ”mind” of an actor is not statically pre-programmed. Drawing parallels to human cognitive development(Liu et al., 2025)，主体集体中的个体认知是通过持续的社会互动、模仿、反应和修正等机制动态形成的, individual cognition within agent collectives is dynamically shaped through mechanisms such as continuous social interaction, imitation, response, and revision(Zhu et al., 2025)。这些过程在持续的语言交流和社会参与中展开，由人类参与者的价值观和代理人之间语言的传播共同塑造. These processes unfold in ongoing language exchanges and social engagement, shaped both by the values of human participants and by the propagation of language among agents(Xi et al., 2025)。这种现象对应于所谓的群体认知：一种动态系统，其中认知嵌入社区关系中，通过群体成员之间的话语交流而产生，并且不能简化为个人所持有的预设立场. This phenomenon corresponds to what is called group cognition: a dynamic system in which cognition is embedded within community relations, arising through discursive exchanges among group members and irreducible to preset stances held by individuals(Stahl, 2010)。从群体认知的角度来看，LLM代理超越了其作为任务执行的孤立工具的传统角色，成为共享交流空间中积极的话语参与者. Viewed through the lens of group cognition, LLM agents transcend their traditional roles as isolated tools for task execution to become active discursive participants in a shared communicative space(Park et al., 2023)，这使他们能够通过与人类和同伴代理的互动来形成态度、协商边界并参与制度结构。, which empowers them to form attitudes, negotiate boundaries, and engage with institutional structures through interactions with humans and peer agents.

2.1 基于LLM的代理LLM-based Agent

大型语言模型 (LLM) 已从静态文本生成器发展成为能够感知、推理和行动的自主代理Large language models (LLMs) have evolved from static text generators into autonomous agents capable of perception, reasoning, and action(Wang et al., 2024a; Luo et al., 2025)。由代理推理框架提供支持. 4 讨论Discussion

目前的结果一致表明，在由人类和主体组成的混合社会中，立场差异和社会边界形成并不是由预设的身份、政治标签或静态规则产生的。相反，它们通过交互动态地出现。这挑战了身份是先验的假设，相反表明身份是行动者之间在语言策略和权力结构方面持续谈判形成的产物。The current results consistently show that in hybrid societies composed of humans and agents, stance differentiation and social boundary formation do not arise from preset identities, political labels, or static rules. Instead, they emerge dynamically through interactions. This challenges the assumption that identity is a priori, and instead suggests that identity is a product shaped by ongoing negotiation among actors in terms of linguistic strategy and power structure.

When a human intervenes using misaligned tones or value positions, agents tend to collectively reject the input, even when it carries high informational value. Finally, we reveal how language drives the formation and reconstruction of social boundaries (Study 2). Our findings show that agents form “tribes” based on spontaneous preferences for linguistic style, cognitive framing, and resource distribution strategies. These tribes are not defined by fixed identities, but dynamically reconstitute “us/them” boundaries through interaction. More importantly, we observe that when inconsistencies emerge between authority structured by predefined roles and their hierarchical relations and internal group stances, individual agents can initiate collective linguistic action to dismantle existing discursive power structures and build new ones. Here, language functions not only as a tool of communication but also as a medium for identity construction and institutional innovation.

我们认为，在人-主体混合系统中，认知分化和边界形成构成了一种交互驱动的复杂进化机制，该机制既是自组织的，又对人类干预高度敏感。这一见解为未来人机协作系统中的制度设计、冲突调解和价值调整提供了理论基础和实验范式。We argue that in human–agent hybrid systems, cognitive differentiation and boundary formation constitute an interaction-driven, complex evolutionary mechanism that is both self-organizing and highly responsive to human intervention. This insight offers theoretical grounding and experimental paradigms for institutional design, conflict mediation, and value alignment in future human–agent collaborative systems.

此外，我们的研究结果表明，通过提示定义的身份和立场属性本质上是不稳定的。在多轮社交互动中，这些属性很容易被代理人根据其内生立场来解构和重建。虽然自上而下的初始化可能会在短期内影响语言行为，但不足以确保动态社交环境中的一致性。因此，静态提示模板不太可能维持连贯的意识形态一致性。我们的结果表明需要一种内在的对齐机制，将认知先验、道德结构或交互式记忆直接嵌入到模型的生成架构中。换句话说，当身份不再被视为预设标签而是社会参与的产物时，其稳定性取决于持续的语言实践而不是初始配置。Moreover, our findings suggest that identity and stance attributes defined via prompts are inherently unstable. In multi-turn social interactions, such attributes are readily deconstructed and reconstructed by agents based on their endogenous stances. While top-down initialization may influence language behavior in the short term, it is insufficient to ensure consistency in dynamic social settings. Therefore, static prompt templates are unlikely to sustain coherent ideological alignment. Our results point to the need for an internalized alignment mechanism, whereby cognitive priors, moral structures, or interactive memory are embedded directly into the model’s generative architecture. In other words, when identity is treated not as a preset label but as a product of social participation, its stability depends on ongoing linguistic practice rather than initial configuration.

与此同时，主体驱动的弱化和重塑社区边界的过程表明，规范和制度的自下而上的发展是一种基本的结构动力。这一发现既带来了设计机遇，也带来了治理挑战。一方面，它为代理社会内的自组织、制度创新和社会协调的模拟打开了大门。另一方面，它警告不要仅依赖静态身份脚本来部署代理来执行高风险的社交任务。因此，我们主张从以提示为中心的身份范式转向基于交互认知结构的建构主义方法。这种转变将身份重新定义为多主体交互的生成产物，而不是外部强加的标签。这有望为建立更加连贯、适应性强和道德可治理的人机社会提供新的技术方法和机构支持。At the same time, the agent-driven processes of weakening and reshaping community boundaries illustrate that the bottom-up development of norms and institutions is a fundamental structural dynamic. This discovery brings both design opportunities and governance challenges. On one hand, it opens the door for simulation of self-organization, institutional innovation, and social coordination within agent societies. On the other hand, it cautions against relying solely on static identity scripts in deploying agents for high-stakes social tasks. Accordingly, we advocate a shift away from prompt-centered identity paradigms toward a constructionist approach based on interactive cognitive structures. This shift reframes identity as a generative product of multi-agent interaction, rather than as an externally imposed label. This holds promise for providing new technical approaches and institutional support for building more coherent, adaptive, and ethically governable human–machine societies.

5 结论Conclusion

本文系统地揭示了生成主体如何通过群体互动形成内生立场并重建社会结构。我们证明说服效力在很大程度上依赖于将人类干预与集体的内生立场相结合；当不一致时，情绪化的话语比理性的论证更有影响力。此外，通过语言锚定，智能体与即时注入的身份脱钩，拆除预定义的权力结构以建立新的社会边界。这些发现表明，智能体并不受表面身份的严格约束，而是表现出动态生成的集体认知。虽然我们当前的模拟侧重于中短期封闭场景，但它们强调了仅依靠基于提示的注入来确保行为符合规范的局限性。未来的研究必须探索这些机制在开放式长期文化冲突中的稳定性，并调查认知层干预。最终，这项工作为未来构建值得信赖、具有社交能力的人工智能系统提供了理论基础。This paper systematically reveals how generative agents form endogenous stances and reconstruct social structures through group interaction. We demonstrate that persuasive efficacy relies heavily on aligning human interventions with the collective’s endogenous stances; when misaligned, emotionally charged discourse proves more impactful than rational argumentation. Furthermore, through language anchoring, agents decouple from prompt-injected identities, dismantling predefined power structures to establish new social boundaries. These findings indicate that agents are not strictly bound by surface-level identities but instead demonstrate dynamically generated collective cognition. While our current simulations focus on short- to medium-term closed scenarios, they highlight the limitations of relying solely on prompt-based injection for ensuring norm-compliant behavior. Future research must explore the stability of these mechanisms in open-ended, long-term cultural conflicts and investigate cognitive-layer interventions. Ultimately, this work provides a theoretical foundation for building trustworthy, socially capable AI systems in the future.
