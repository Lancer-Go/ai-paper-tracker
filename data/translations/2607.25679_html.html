摘要。Abstract.

多模态行为分析提供了一种可扩展的方法来评估抑郁、焦虑和压力，但通用融合模型常常忽略问卷标签的心理测量结构。在 DASS-21 中，风险标签是通过固定项目到子量表映射从有序症状项目派生的。我们建议Multimodal behavioral analysis offers a scalable approach to assessing depression, anxiety, and stress, yet generic fusion models often ignore the psychometric structure of questionnaire labels. In DASS-21, risk labels are derived from ordered symptom items through fixed item-to-subscale mappings. We propose动力桥DynaBridge，一个动态摘要引导的跨任务多模式框架，用于 DASS 结构的心理健康评估。 DynaBridge 对多个会话中的声音、视觉和文本提示进行编码，并使用冻结的法学硕士生成的 DASS 感知摘要作为参与者级语义证据来增强它们。它预测有序项目分布，从项目级软评分重建抑郁、焦虑和压力风险证据，并将这些证据与直接多模式风险预测融合。置信感知细化策略进一步保守地结合了高置信度语义线索。在官方 AdoDAS 验证中，DynaBridge 优于官方基线和代表性多模态方法，D/A/S 风险预测的平均 F1 为 0.5012，DASS-21 项目预测的平均 QWK 为 0.3216。这些结果显示了桥接多模态线索、语义摘要和 DASS-21 心理测量结构的价值。, a dynamic summary-guided cross-task multimodal framework for DASS-structured mental health assessment. DynaBridge encodes acoustic, visual, and textual cues across multiple sessions and augments them with frozen-LLM-generated DASS-aware summaries as participant-level semantic evidence. It predicts ordinal item distributions, reconstructs depression, anxiety, and stress risk evidence from item-level soft scores, and fuses this evidence with direct multimodal risk predictions. These results show the value of bridging multimodal cues, semantic summaries, and DASS-21 psychometric structure.

1. 简介Introduction

抑郁、焦虑和压力会影响日常功能、学习成绩和长期健康Depression, anxiety, and stress affect daily functioning, academic performance, and long-term well-being(World Health Organization, 2022)。标准化问卷和访谈仍然是心理评估的核心，但它们是偶发的、主观的且难以规模化。这激发了根据言语、面部行为、身体运动和语言使用等多模式行为线索进行自动心理健康评估. Standardized questionnaires and interviews remain central to psychological assessment, but they are episodic, subjective, and difficult to scale. This has motivated automatic mental health assessment from multimodal behavioral cues such as speech, facial behavior, body movement, and language use(Gratch et al., 2014; Ringeval et al., 2019).

最近的多媒体和情感计算方法通常通过特征融合、决策融合、注意力或多模态 Transformer 来集成声学、视觉和文本表示Recent multimedia and affective computing methods usually integrate acoustic, visual, and textual representations through feature fusion, decision fusion, attention, or multimodal Transformers(Zadeh et al., 2017; Tsai et al., 2019; Hazarika et al., 2020)。尽管有效，但大多数方法将评估制定为最终标签的普通分类或回归。这忽略了问卷衍生标签的一个重要属性：在抑郁焦虑压力量表（DASS）中，抑郁、焦虑和压力评分是根据有序症状项目的固定子集计算的. Although effective, most methods formulate assessment as ordinary classification or regression over final labels. This ignores an important property of questionnaire-derived labels: in the Depression Anxiety Stress Scales (DASS), depression, anxiety, and stress scores are computed from fixed subsets of ordinal symptom items(Lovibond and Lovibond, 1995; Antony et al., 1998)。因此，将项目响应和子规模风险视为独立输出可能会产生不一致的预测。. Treating item responses and subscale risks as independent outputs can therefore produce inconsistent predictions.

大型语言模型 (LLM) 提供有用的语义支持，因为它们可以总结零碎的自由反应并组织与症状相关的证据Large language models (LLMs) offer useful semantic support because they can summarize fragmented free responses and organize symptom-related evidence(Brown et al., 2020; OpenAI, 2023)。然而，直接使用法学硕士作为心理健康标签预测因素是有风险的：它们可能会从简短或模糊的文本中过度推断心理状态，并且可能与视听线索或心理测量规则不一致. However, directly using LLMs as mental health label predictors is risky: they may over-infer psychological states from short or ambiguous text and may not align with audio-visual cues or psychometric rules(Ji et al., 2023)。因此，我们仅使用冻结的法学硕士作为辅助语义证据生成器；他们的摘要从未访问过真实的 DASS 标签。. We therefore use frozen LLMs only as auxiliary semantic evidence generators; their summaries never access ground-truth DASS labels.

我们建议We propose动力桥DynaBridge，一个动态摘要引导的跨任务多模式框架，用于 DASS 结构的心理健康评估。 DynaBridge 连接多模式行为线索、LLM 生成的心理摘要、DASS-21 项目级序数预测以及抑郁/焦虑/压力风险评估。它从自由响应会话中生成渐进式 DASS 感知摘要，将其嵌入为参与者级语义模态，使用预定义的 DASS 映射从项目级软概率重建风险证据，并使用置信感知语义证据完善项目预测。我们的主要贡献是：, a dynamic summary-guided cross-task multimodal framework for DASS-structured mental health assessment. DynaBridge connects multimodal behavioral cues, LLM-generated psychological summaries, DASS-21 item-level ordinal predictions, and depression/anxiety/stress risk assessment. It generates progressive DASS-aware summaries from free-response sessions, embeds them as a participant-level semantic modality, reconstructs risk evidence from item-level soft probabilities using the predefined DASS mapping, and refines item predictions with confidence-aware semantic evidence.   • •

  • •

2. 相关作品Related Works

2.1. 多模式心理健康评估Multimodal Mental Health Assessment

根据行为信号进行自动心理健康评估已在多媒体、情感计算和临床访谈分析中进行了研究。 DAIC/DAIC-WOZ 和 AVEC 系列等资源提供用于心理困扰和抑郁严重程度估计的视听录音、文字记录和问卷注释Automatic mental health assessment from behavioral signals has been studied in multimedia, affective computing, and clinical interview analysis. Resources such as DAIC/DAIC-WOZ and the AVEC series provide audio-visual recordings, transcripts, and questionnaire annotations for psychological distress and depression severity estimation(Gratch et al., 2014; Ringeval et al., 2019)。在这些基准的基础上，之前的工作使用声音线索、面部行为、头部姿势、凝视、语言内容及其组合来进行抑郁症识别和严重程度估计. Building on these benchmarks, prior work has used acoustic cues, facial behavior, head pose, gaze, language content, and their combinations for depression recognition and severity estimation(Stepanov et al., 2018; Qureshi et al., 2019; Dham et al., 2017).

一个关键挑战是多模式融合。经典方法使用特征或决策级融合，而最近的方法则通过注意力和基于 Transformer 的架构来模拟跨模式交互。代表性方法包括张量融合网络A key challenge is multimodal fusion. Classical approaches use feature- or decision-level fusion, while recent methods model cross-modal interaction with attention and Transformer-based architectures. Representative methods include Tensor Fusion Network(Zadeh et al., 2017), 低秩多模态融合, Low-rank Multimodal Fusion(Liu et al., 2018), 多模态变压器, Multimodal Transformer(Tsai et al., 2019), 米莎, MISA(Hazarika et al., 2020)，多模式适应门, multimodal adaptation gates(Rahman et al., 2020), 立方体MLP, CubeMLP(Sun et al., 2022), 和 TensorFormer, and TensorFormer(Sun et al., 2023)。情绪和情绪感知模型进一步表明，情感背景可以改善抑郁症检测. Emotion- and sentiment-aware models further show that affective context can improve depression detection(Teng et al., 2024b, 2026a, a)。然而，大多数方法直接预测最终分数或类别。相比之下，DynaBridge 利用 DASS-21 项目到子量表结构，并根据顺序项目级证据重建风险预测。. However, most methods predict final scores or categories directly. In contrast, DynaBridge exploits the DASS-21 item-to-subscale structure and reconstructs risk predictions from ordinal item-level evidence.

2.2. 法学硕士辅助心理学理解LLM-Assisted Psychological Understanding

法学硕士最近被用于心理文本理解，包括访谈总结、症状证据提取和面向心理健康的推理。 LLM 生成的提示或摘要可以通过将稀疏的自由响应转化为更高级别的语义证据来补充原始转录本嵌入LLMs have recently been used for psychological text understanding, including interview summarization, symptom evidence extraction, and mental health-oriented reasoning. LLM-generated prompts or summaries can complement raw transcript embeddings by turning sparse free responses into higher-level semantic evidence(Teng et al., 2025b, 2026b, a; Hou et al., 2025; Teng et al., 2026c)。然而，法学硕士不应被视为独立的诊断预测因子，因为他们的判断可能缺乏可观察行为和经过验证的量表的基础。因此，DynaBridge 仅使用法学硕士作为冻结证据总结器。与之前的 LLM 辅助抑郁症检测不同，它将全局、领域级和项目级 DASS 摘要与多模态表示和 DASS 结构的项目风险重建相结合。. Nevertheless, LLMs should not be treated as standalone diagnostic predictors because their judgments may be weakly grounded in observable behavior and validated scales. DynaBridge therefore uses LLMs only as frozen evidence summarizers. Unlike prior LLM-assisted depression detection, it integrates global, domain-level, and item-level DASS summaries with multimodal representations and DASS-structured item-to-risk reconstruction.

特定于任务的模块提供了进一步的改进。 DASS 结构的序数模型通过保留症状严重程度的有序性质而使 A2 受益。项目到风险重建通过 DASS-21 评分规则将项目软概率转换为 D/A/S 证据，从而提高了 A1，加强了细粒度项目响应和粗粒度风险标签之间的一致性。仅当摘要置信度较高时，置信度感知项目细化才使用语义证据，从而进一步提高 A2。Task-specific modules provide further improvements. DASS-structured ordinal modeling benefits A2 by preserving the ordered nature of symptom severity. Item-to-risk reconstruction improves A1 by converting item soft probabilities into D/A/S evidence through the DASS-21 scoring rule, strengthening consistency between fine-grained item responses and coarse risk labels. Confidence-aware item refinement further improves A2 by using semantic evidence only when summary confidence is high.

辅助元数据预言机使用测试时不可用的字段，例如家庭结构、独生子女状况、学业成绩变化和情绪状态变化。它没有在最终系统中使用。其巨大的验证增益表明，潜在的上下文因素与 DASS 结果密切相关，这促使我们使用测试时可用的近似值，例如上下文先验、动态摘要和跨任务重建。The auxiliary metadata oracle uses fields unavailable at test time, such as family structure, only-child status, academic performance change, and emotional state change. It is not used in the final system. Its large validation gain suggests that latent contextual factors are strongly associated with DASS outcomes, motivating our use of test-time available approximations such as context priors, dynamic summaries, and cross-task reconstruction.

4.5. 分析Analysis

DynaBridge 改进结构化评估有以下三个主要原因。首先，动态 DASS 摘要提供了超出原始转录本嵌入的参与者级语义证据。通过汇总来自日常状态、积极记忆和消极记忆会话的信息，摘要捕获了跨会话的行为模式，而这些模式很难单独使用孤立的响应来表示。这为多模式行为特征提供了补充证据，特别是当症状相关线索分散在多个会话中时。DynaBridge improves structured assessment for three main reasons. First, dynamic DASS summaries provide participant-level semantic evidence beyond raw transcript embeddings. By aggregating information from the daily-state, positive-memory, and negative-memory sessions, the summaries capture cross-session behavioral patterns that are difficult to represent using isolated responses alone. This provides complementary evidence to multimodal behavioral features, especially when symptom-related cues are scattered across multiple sessions.

其次，项目风险重构明确结合了两项官方任务。由于 A1 风险标签源自 DASS-21 子量表评分，因此风险预测应得到连贯的项目级症状证据的支持。重建分支使用预定义的 DASS 结构将 A2 软项目概率转换为 D/A/S 风险证据，而直接分支保留项目预测可能无法完全捕获的多模态线索。它们的结合提高了心理测量的一致性和稳健性。Second, item-to-risk reconstruction explicitly aligns the two official tasks. Since A1 risk labels are derived from DASS-21 subscale scores, risk prediction should be supported by coherent item-level symptom evidence. The reconstructed branch converts A2 soft item probabilities into D/A/S risk evidence using the predefined DASS structure, while the direct branch preserves multimodal cues that may not be fully captured by item predictions. Their combination improves both psychometric consistency and robustness.

第三，置信感知项目细化通过保守地合并转录支持的语义证据来改进 A2。仅当项目级证据明确且足够可信时才会应用细化，以防止不受支持的 LLM 线索推翻行为预测​​。这对于描述可能仅在多模式反应中微弱表达的特定症状的项目特别有用。Third, confidence-aware item refinement improves A2 by incorporating transcript-supported semantic evidence conservatively. Refinement is applied only when item-level evidence is explicit and sufficiently confident, preventing unsupported LLM cues from overriding behavioral predictions. This is particularly useful for items describing specific symptoms that may be only weakly expressed in multimodal responses.

除了预测性能之外，DynaBridge 还提高了可检查性。最终的风险预测可以分解为直接多模态分支和项目派生分支，从而允许将高级 D/A/S 风险追溯到细粒度的 DASS 项目证据。虽然这并不能使系统进行临床诊断，但它提供了比单个端到端分类器更透明的评估过程。Beyond predictive performance, DynaBridge also improves inspectability. The final risk prediction can be decomposed into a direct multimodal branch and an item-derived branch, allowing high-level D/A/S risks to be traced back to fine-grained DASS item evidence. While this does not make the system clinically diagnostic, it provides a more transparent assessment process than a single end-to-end classifier.

尽管有这些优势，DynaBridge 仍然是一个面向筛选的计算系统，而不是临床诊断工具。其摘要引导分支取决于成绩单质量，并且报告的结果基于官方验证拆分，因为隐藏的测试标签不可用。未来的工作将研究跨队列稳健性、分布变化下的校准以及细粒度症状预测的临床基础解释。Despite these advantages, DynaBridge remains a screening-oriented computational system rather than a clinical diagnostic tool. Its summary-guided branch depends on transcript quality, and the reported results are based on the official validation split because hidden test labels are unavailable. Future work will study cross-cohort robustness, calibration under distribution shift, and clinically grounded interpretation of fine-grained symptom predictions.

5. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 DynaBridge，一种动态摘要引导的跨任务多模式框架，用于 DASS 结构的心理健康评估。 DynaBridge 没有将 D/A/S 风险预测和 DASS-21 项目预测视为独立任务，而是通过序数项目建模和 DASS 结构化项目风险重建明确地将它们联系起来。它进一步整合了声学、视觉、文本和冻结法学硕士在多个响应会话中生成的语义证据，同时使用置信度感知细化来保守地利用转录支持的项目级线索。官方验证分割的实验表明，在风险级别和项目级别预测方面，官方基线和代表性多模态方法都有一致的改进。这些结果表明，利用问卷衍生标签的心理测量结构有利于多模式心理健康评估。 DynaBridge 仍然是一个面向筛选的计算系统，而不是临床诊断工具。未来的工作将研究分布变化下更强的校准、跨队列稳健性以及细粒度症状预测的临床基础解释。In this work, we presented DynaBridge, a dynamic summary-guided cross-task multimodal framework for DASS-structured mental health assessment. Instead of treating D/A/S risk prediction and DASS-21 item prediction as independent tasks, DynaBridge explicitly bridges them through ordinal item modeling and DASS-structured item-to-risk reconstruction. It further incorporates acoustic, visual, textual, and frozen-LLM-generated semantic evidence across multiple response sessions, while using confidence-aware refinement to conservatively exploit transcript-supported item-level cues. Experiments on the official validation split show consistent improvements over the official baseline and representative multimodal methods on both risk-level and item-level prediction. These results suggest that exploiting the psychometric structure of questionnaire-derived labels is beneficial for multimodal mental health assessment. DynaBridge remains a screening-oriented computational system rather than a clinical diagnostic tool. Future work will study stronger calibration under distribution shift, cross-cohort robustness, and clinically grounded interpretation of fine-grained symptom predictions.
