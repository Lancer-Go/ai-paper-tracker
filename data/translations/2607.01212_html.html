I 简介Introduction

家具组装因其长期性和高精度性而成为机器人技术中长期存在的挑战。组装一件家具需要按照严格的顺序操作多个相互依赖的部件，早期的错误可能会传播并导致级联故障Furniture assembly is a long-standing challenge in robotics due to its long-horizon and high-precision nature. Assembling a piece of furniture requires manipulating multiple interdependent parts in strict order, where early mistakes can propagate and cause cascading failures[15, 20, 3, 38]。成功的装配还要求配合部件之间紧密的几何对齐. Successful assembly also demands tight geometric alignment between mating parts[45, 4, 29, 16]。这些挑战在真实规模的环境中进一步放大：大型、重型部件需要持续的双手协调，其中两只手臂必须共同操纵零件，同时尊重可达性限制并避免整个装配过程中的运动奇点. These challenges are further amplified in real-scale settings: large, heavy components require sustained bimanual coordination, where two arms must jointly manipulate parts while respecting reachability limits and avoiding kinematic singularities throughout the assembly process[10, 19].

In this paper, we formalize real-scale bimanual furniture assembly tasks and build a system with two complementary components. First, we develop a scalable bimanual simulation pipeline that generates expert demonstrations via motion planning across diverse furniture types and task horizons. This controlled environment enables verification of task feasibility under reachability and kinematic constraints, and serves as a testbed for large-scale evaluation. Second, to support real-world deployment, we develop a VR teleoperation system with design principles tailored for real-scale bimanual assembly, enabling coordinated dual-arm control by a single teleoperator on a Kinova Gen3 platform for high-quality demonstration collection.

Long-horizon trajectories induce distribution drift, where small deviations can drive the policy into out-of-distribution states and lead to compounding errors[33, 43]。为了解决这个问题，我们将每个程序集分解为基于语义的子任务，并在较短的、以语言为条件的片段上对 VLA 进行微调。这缩小了子任务内的状态分布并为任务阶段提供了线索。重要的是，我们在稳定、无接触的后撤臂状态下定义子任务边界，而不是在零件组装后立即在接触丰富的状态下定义。由于撤退后状态不受接触和力约束，因此小的执行错误不太可能放大为大的偏差，从而为每个后续子任务产生更一致的初始状态分布，并减少跨子任务的分布偏移。然而，这需要确定执行期间何时在子任务之间进行转换。我们通过引入进度增强型 VLA 来解决这个问题，该 VLA 联合预测操作，并使用显式子任务进度信号来触发推理过程中的转换。. 通过运动规划生成数据。Data Generation via Motion Planning.我们使用运动规划来生成模拟专家演示。对于单臂动作，我们直接规划末端执行器姿势轨迹。对于双手动作，我们禁用物理模拟，而是为被操纵对象规划轨迹，限制双臂作为刚性连接的末端执行器跟随。所有生成的轨迹都通过在模拟中重放并验证组装是否成功来进行验证。初始零件放置经过精心设计，以确保可行性：所有零件均保持在机器人可触及范围内，避免操作过程中对象间碰撞，并防止导致运动奇点的配置。We use motion planning to generate expert demonstrations in simulation. 控制和执行。Control and Execution.我们在末端执行器空间中操作双臂。在每个 10 Hz 控制步骤中，系统计算每个手臂的目标末端执行器位姿增量，将其转换为笛卡尔扭曲，并通过 Kinova 笛卡尔速度控制器执行，该控制器在机载执行低级关节速度跟踪。数据收集和 VLA 推理共享相同的末端执行器增量We operate both arms in end-effector space. At each 10 Hz control step, the system computes a target end-effector pose delta for each arm, converts it into a Cartesian twist, and executes it via the Kinova Cartesian velocity controller, which performs low-level joint-velocity tracking on-board. Data collection and VLA inference share this same end-effector-delta→\rightarrow速度控制器接口，仅在末端执行器位姿增量的来源上有所不同。velocity-controller interface, differing only in the source of the end-effector pose deltas.

我们使用带有 Quest2ROS 的 Meta Quest 3 耳机远程操作双 Kinova Gen3 手臂，该耳机以大约 72 Hz 的频率传输 6-DoF 控制器姿势。对于每个手臂，远程操作节点区分连续的 VR 姿势以获得末端执行器姿势增量，将它们从 VR 框架转换到手臂基础框架，并将所得的扭曲直接转发到速度控制器。We teleoperate the dual Kinova Gen3 arms using a Meta Quest 3 headset with Quest2ROS, which streams 6-DoF controller poses at approximately 72 Hz. For each arm, the teleoperation node differentiates consecutive VR poses to obtain end-effector pose deltas, transforms them from the VR frame to the arm base frame, and forwards the resulting twists directly to the velocity controller.

在部署时，VLA 策略输出绝对末端执行器姿势目标序列。对于每个手臂，我们计算当前姿态的增量，通过简单的比例 (P) 增益将其转换为笛卡尔扭曲，并通过相同的 Kinova 速度控制器执行它。At deployment, the VLA policy outputs sequences of absolute end-effector pose targets. For each arm, we compute the delta to the current pose, convert it into a Cartesian twist via a simple proportional (P) gain, and execute it through the same Kinova velocity controller.
