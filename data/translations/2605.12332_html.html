Non-towered airports rely on the Common Traffic Advisory Frequency (CTAF) for air traffic coordination and experience frequent near mid-air collisions due to the pilot self-announcement communication protocol. We propose a general vision-language model (VLM) approach to analyze the transcribed CTAF radio communications in natural language, METeorological Aerodrome Report (METAR) weather data, Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) flight trajectories, and Visual Flight Rules sectional charts of the airfield. We provide a preliminary study at Half Moon Bay Airport, with a qualitative real world case study and a quantitative evaluation using a new synthetic dataset of communications and weather modalities. We qualitatively evaluate our framework on real flight data using Gemini 2.5 Pro, demonstrating accurate identification of a right-of-way violation. The synthetic dataset is derived from real examples and includes a 12-category hazard taxonomy, and is used to benchmark three open-source (Qwen 2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, Gemma-2-9B) and three closed-source (GPT-4o, GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6) LLM models on the subset of inputs related to CTAF and METAR. 1 简介Introduction

传统的空中交通管制 (ATC) 采用复杂的决策来确保美国国家空域系统 (NAS) 的空中防撞Traditional air traffic control (ATC) employs complex decision-making to ensure mid-air collision-avoidance in the United States national airspace system (NAS)[mogford1995complexity]。无塔机场的空域运行遵循美国联邦航空管理局 (FAA) 咨询通告 (AC) 90-66C 文件中概述的程序. Airspace operations at non-towered airports follow procedures outlined in the Federal Aviation Administration’s (FAA) advisory circular (AC) 90-66C document[FAA_AC90_66C]。这种通信通过通用交通咨询频率（CTAF）在飞行员之间进行管理，以在没有人工控制的情况下提供一定程度的安全性。虽然这种避免碰撞的方法已经实施了近五十年，但它严重依赖飞行员的自愿决定来向其他人宣布飞机的位置和意图，导致这些无塔台机场发生了许多近空中碰撞事故. This communication is administered between pilots over the common traffic advisory frequency (CTAF) to offer a level of safety in the absence of a human controller. While this collision-avoidance method has been in place for nearly fifty years, its heavy reliance on pilot’s voluntary decisions to announce their aircraft’s position and intent to others has led to many near mid-air collisions at these non-towered airports[nasa_asrs_nonTower2025]。 CTAF 缺乏分层安全，凸显了纯粹飞行员间冲突消除的缺点。随着低空空域通用航空 (GA) 数量的增加，开发自动化 ATC 作为这些无塔机场的安全层可以缩小这一安全关键技术差距。. CTAF’s lack of layered safety highlights the shortcomings of purely pilot-to-pilot deconfliction. As the amount of general aviation (GA) in low-altitude airspace increases, the development of automated ATC as a safety layer at these non-towered airports could close this safety critical technology gap.

ATC 的冲突消除架构依赖于严格的基于规则或数据驱动的系统，只有当输入完整且格式精确（例如传感器数据或通信）时，这些系统才能正常运行ATC’s deconflition architecture relies on rigid rule-based or data-driven systems that function well only when inputs are complete and precisely formatted (e.g., sensor data or communication)[NAS_InTimeAviationSafety2022]。此外，这些架构主要是人机交互的。然而，无塔楼机场的运营本质上是非结构化的。 CTAF 交流是自发的、简短的且通常不完整的无线电呼叫，其含义在很大程度上取决于交通位置、天气和视觉条件等上下文线索. Furthermore, these architectures are primarily human-in-the-loop. However, non-towered airport operations are inherently unstructured. CTAF exchanges are spontaneous, abbreviated, and often incomplete radio calls whose meaning depends heavily on contextual cues such as traffic position, weather, and visual conditions[FAA_AC90_66C]。无塔楼机场通常还缺乏记录监控空域运行所必需的飞行数据所需的基础设施。. Non-towered airports also typically lack the infrastructure required to log flight data essential for monitoring airspace operations.

人工智能 (AI) 的最新进展在音频、视觉和语言领域取得了显着进展，使大型语言模型 (LLM) 和视觉语言模型 (VLM) 能够执行广泛的翻译、理解和推理任务Recent advances in artificial intelligence (AI) have produced remarkable progress across audio, vision, and language domains, enabling large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs) to perform a broad range of translation, comprehension, and reasoning tasks[vaswani2017attention, cui2025recent, yin2024survey, stahlberg2020neural, plaat2024reasoning]，开启了他们处理非塔式 ATC 数据的非结构化、异构性和语义丰富性质的可能性。法学硕士在自然语言、结构化数据和上下文信息方面表现出强大的推理能力, opening the possibility they could handle the unstructured, heterogenous, and semantically-rich nature of non-towered ATC data. LLMs have demonstrated strong reasoning capabilities over natural language, structured data, and contextual information[cheng2024callmenn]。这些模型经过大规模文本语料库的预训练，可以学习语言、数字数据和更高级别概念之间的关系，使它们能够推断出更深层的上下文信息，例如意图。 VLM 通过对视觉和文本输入进行联合推理，将这些功能扩展到多模式设置，使其非常适合涉及异构数据源（例如飞行轨迹、机场图表、天气信息和飞行员通信记录）的航空应用。. Pre-trained on large-scale text corpora, these models learn relationships among language, numerical data, and higher-level concepts, enabling them to infer deeper contextual information such as intent. VLMs extend these capabilities to multimodal settings by jointly reasoning over visual and textual inputs, making them well suited for aviation applications involving heterogeneous data sources such as flight trajectories, airport diagrams, weather information, and pilot communication transcripts.

我们提出基于法学硕士的模型，用于无塔机场周围空域运行的飞行后安全评估。我们考虑采用通用 VLM 方法来分析相关航空信号，包括以自然语言转录的 CTAF 无线电通信、气象机场报告 (METAR) 天气数据、广播自动相关监视 (ADS-B) 飞行轨迹和机场目视飞行规则 (VFR) 剖面图。 使用半月湾机场 (KHAF) 作为案例研究的定性实验表明，当提示这些输入时，当代 VLM 可以区分安全与不安全的情况。仅根据 CTAF 和 METAR 数据进行模型性能的初步定量评估，确认了各种模型和参数设置的性能。未来的工作包括对所有模式和大量现实世界例子的定量评估。 我们相信，基于 LLM 和 VLM 的模型可以作为支持无塔机场周围和/或在没有人工空中交通管制员的一般情况下安全空域运行的重要元素。We propose LLM-based models for post-flight safety assessments of airspace operations surrounding non-towered airports. We consider a general VLM approach to analyze the relevant aviation signals including transcribed CTAF radio communications in natural language, METeorological Aerodrome Report (METAR) weather data, Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) flight trajectories, and Visual Flight Rules (VFR) sectional charts of the airfield. A qualitative experiment using Half Moon Bay Airport (KHAF) as a case study suggests that a contemporary VLM can discriminate safe vs unsafe conditions when prompted with these inputs. Initial quantitative evaluations of model performance when prompted soley with CTAF and METAR data confirm performance across a variety of models and parameter setttings. Future work includes a quantitative evaluation across all modalities and a larger number of real world examples. We believe that LLM and VLM based models can serve as an important element to support safe airspace operations around non-towered airports and/or in general scenarios where human air traffic controllers are unavailable.

2 相关工作Related Work

尽管自动避碰（包括地形避让）有着悠久的历史While there is a long history of automated collision avoidance, including terrain avoidance[kuchar2007traffic]和头对头碰撞解决系统and head-to-head collision resolution systems[al2010probability]基于显式预测模型和控制理论已在现代航空中部署that have been deployed in modern aviation based on explicit predictive models and control theory[kochenderfer2012next]，用于飞机安全或意图预测的自然语言 CTAF 通信分析的研究相对较少。先前的工作考虑了飞行员通信的自然语言处理，包括对自动转录的自动语音识别（ASR）的广泛研究，以及航空安全数据库和事件评估的开发, analysis of natural language CTAF communications for aircraft safety or intent prediction has been relatively underexplored. Prior work has considered natural language processing for pilot communications, including extensive investigation of Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) for automatic transcription, and the development of aviation safety databases and incident evaluations[paradis2025kaona]。使用 LLM 的其他工作，例如 ChatGPT，已用于汇总历史地面延误计划 (GDP) 数据，以支持战略决策中的最佳空中交通流量管理. Other work using LLMs, such as ChatGPT, have been used for summarization of historical ground delay program (GDP) data to support optimal air traffic flow management in strategic decision making[abdulhak2024chatatc]。最后，之前的研究研究了无塔机场，并将 ATC 建模为马尔可夫决策过程 (MDP)，以实现 GA 运营的自主 ATC. Lastly, prior research has studied non-towered airports and modeled ATC as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) towards autonomous ATC of GA operations[mahboubi2015autonomous].

4.1 数据集Dataset

对于我们的定量研究，我们将输入限制为 METAR 天气数据和 CTAF 通信记录，这些记录是使用法学硕士进行处理的。 我们开发并评估For our quantitative study, we restrict the inputs to METAR weather data and CTAF communication transcripts, which are processed using a LLM. We develop and evaluate onCTAF-KHAF-合成CTAF-KHAF-Synthetic，根据真实数据示例得出的 KHAF 无塔机场 100 个飞行运行场景的综合基准。完整的生成器提示复制在附录中, a synthetic benchmark of 100 flight-operations scenarios at KHAF non-towered airport derived from real data examples. The full generator prompt is reproduced in Appendix8.1。 12 种危险类别取自 FAA AC 90-66C 中记录的事故模式.
