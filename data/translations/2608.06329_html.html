摘要Abstract

使用策划或自动生成的基准来评估面向任务的会话代理，但很少评估基准的质量。糟糕的基准可能包含不一致的任务、简单化的场景或有限的政策覆盖范围，从而导致评估不可靠。我们引入了一个无参考框架，使用法学硕士法官来评估基准的一致性、复杂性和政策覆盖范围，同时提供可操作的弱点诊断。我们通过证明与独立人工注释的一致性、评估由不同能力的法学硕士生成的基准以及受到受控质量下降扰动的基准来验证该框架。跨领域和判断模型，所提出的指标始终区分基准质量水平。我们进一步证明了该框架对手动策划的基准的适用性。我们的框架提供了一种实用的方法来评估合成的和手动策划的对话代理基准。Task-oriented conversational agents are evaluated using curated or automatically generated benchmarks, yet benchmark quality is rarely assessed. Poor benchmarks may contain inconsistent tasks, simplistic scenarios, or limited policy coverage, leading to unreliable evaluations. We introduce a reference-free framework that uses LLM judges to assess benchmark consistency, complexity, and policy coverage, while providing actionable diagnostics of weaknesses. We validate the framework by demonstrating agreement with independent human annotations and by evaluating benchmarks generated by LLMs of varying capabilities, as well as benchmarks subjected to controlled quality-degrading perturbations. Across domains and judge models, the proposed metrics consistently distinguish between benchmark quality levels. We further demonstrate the framework’s applicability to manually curated benchmarks. Our framework offers a practical approach for evaluating synthetic and manually curated conversational-agent benchmarks.

4 指标评估基准4 Metric Evaluation Benchmarks

为了评估所提出的指标如何区分基准之间的质量差异，我们在同一领域内构建了一系列不同质量的基准——这本身就是一个不小的挑战。To assess how well the proposed metrics distinguish quality differences among benchmarks, we construct a sequence of benchmarks of varying quality within the same domain – a nontrivial challenge in itself.

我们考虑两种生成此类测试基准的方法。首先，我们使用综合基准生成管道生成多个基准。在每种情况下，我们都会使用涵盖一系列能力的法学硕士（§4.1）来改变选定的法学硕士组件，同时保持其他组件固定。其次，我们对给定基准应用受控扰动来降低其质量（第 4.2 节）。We consider two approaches for generating such test benchmarks. First, we generate multiple benchmarks using a synthetic benchmark-generation pipeline. In each case, we vary selected LLM components while keeping the others fixed, using LLMs that span a range of capabilities (§4.1). Second, we apply controlled perturbations to a given benchmark to degrade its quality (§4.2).

这两种方法是互补的：使用不同能力的法学硕士生成基准会产生更现实的基准和更细粒度的质量差异，而基于扰动的方法可以更好地控制生成的数据集之间的质量差异。The two approaches are complementary: generating benchmarks with LLMs of varying capabilities yields more realistic benchmarks and finergrained quality differences, while the perturbationbased approach provides greater control over the differences in quality between the generated datasets.

8 结论8 Conclusion

我们引入了一个框架，用于评估会话代理基准的质量，定义捕获任务级一致性和复杂性的指标，以及基准级策略覆盖范围。除了总分之外，这些指标还提供了可行的诊断，揭示了低质量的任务和政策覆盖范围的差距。We introduced a framework for evaluating the quality of conversational-agent benchmarks, defining metrics that capture task-level consistency and complexity, and benchmark-level policy coverage. Beyond aggregate scores, these metrics provide actionable diagnostics, surfacing low-quality tasks and gaps in policy coverage.

我们使用来自不同能力的法学硕士的综合基准、受控扰动和独立的人类评估来验证指标，并说明它们在手动构建的基准上的使用。这些指标可靠地捕获质量差异，对受控退化保持敏感，并与人类判断保持一致。它们共同促进了对对话代理基准的更系统、更可靠的评估。We validated the metrics using synthetic benchmarks from LLMs of varying capabilities, controlled perturbations, and an independent human evaluation, and illustrated their use on a manually constructed benchmark. The metrics reliably capture quality differences, remain sensitive to controlled degradation, and align with human judgments. Together, they promote more systematic, reliable evaluation of conversational-agent benchmarks.

8

局限性Limitations

这项工作重点关注以下对话代理基准This work focuses on conversational-agent benchmarks followingτ-BENCH-和INTELLAGEN风格的评估框架。因此，我们的指标主要评估任务的一致性、复杂性以及与域策略的一致性。-BENCH- and INTELLAGENTstyle evaluation frameworks. Accordingly, our metrics primarily assess task consistency, complexity, and alignment with domain policies.

我们的综合基准实验依赖于策略驱动的 INTELLAGENT 生成管道。其他策略，例如工具驱动生成，可能会产生不同的任务分配。因此，未来的工作应该在更广泛的基准生成方法和领域中评估所提出的指标。Our synthetic-benchmark experiments rely on the policy-driven INTELLAGENT generation pipeline. Other strategies, such as tool-driven generation, may produce different task distributions. Future work should therefore evaluate the proposed metrics across a broader range of benchmarkgeneration methods and domains.

最后，我们不考虑非会话代理设置，例如代码生成或计算机交互，它们涉及不同的交互结构和评估挑战。Finally, we do not consider non-conversational agent settings, such as code generation or computer interaction, which involve different interaction structures and evaluation challenges.
