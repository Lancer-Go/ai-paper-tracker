摘要Abstract

在线日志异常检测对于维护大规模计算系统的可靠性至关重要。尽管最近基于语言模型的日志异常检测器实现了强大的检测性能，但它们的置信度估计仍然缺乏校准。我们表明，这些检测器经常对错误的预测赋予过高的置信度，特别是对于严重类别不平衡下的异常日志。此外，即使传统的校准指标表明校准良好，对错误预测的置信度仍然很高，这给运行监控系统带来了关键的可靠性差距。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了日志重建和距离（LoRD），这是一种用于可靠日志异常检测的轻量级事后校准框架。 LoRD 从正确分类的验证样本的潜在表示中学习预测路线特定的可靠性模型，并通过路线重建距离估计预测可靠性。根据估计的可靠性，LoRD 有选择地重新校准高风险预测，以抑制过度自信的错误，同时保留可靠的预测。对四个大规模日志基准数据集和基于多种语言模型的检测器的大量实验表明，LoRD 持续提高置信可靠性，并在不牺牲异常检测性能的情况下大幅减少过度自信的异常相关错误。Online log anomaly detection is critical for maintaining the reliability of large-scale computing systems. Although recent language model-based log anomaly detectors achieve strong detection performance, their confidence estimates remain poorly calibrated. We show that these detectors frequently assign excessive confidence to incorrect predictions, particularly for anomalous logs under severe class imbalance. Moreover, confidence on erroneous predictions remains persistently high even when conventional calibration metrics indicate good calibration, creating a critical reliability gap for operational monitoring systems. To address this issue, we propose Log Reconstruction and Distance (LoRD), a lightweight post-hoc calibration framework for reliable log anomaly detection. LoRD learns prediction-route-specific reliability models from latent representations of correctly classified validation samples and estimates prediction reliability through route-wise reconstruction distances. Based on the estimated reliability, LoRD selectively recalibrates high-risk predictions to suppress overconfident errors while preserving reliable predictions. Extensive experiments on four large-scale log benchmark datasets and multiple language model-based detectors demonstrate that LoRD consistently improves confidence reliability and substantially reduces overconfident anomaly-related errors without sacrificing anomaly detection performance.

I 简介Introduction

在人工智能和高性能计算时代，大规模数据系统变得越来越重要。维护系统可靠性在很大程度上依赖于持续的日志监控，因此日志异常检测对于识别异常事件和防止系统故障至关重要。现有的日志异常检测方法在语义日志分析和异常事件检测方面取得了实质性进展Large-scale data systems are increasingly critical in the era of artificial intelligence and high-performance computing. Maintaining system reliability relies heavily on continuous log monitoring, making log anomaly detection essential for identifying abnormal events and preventing system failures. Existing log anomaly detection approaches have achieved substantial progress in semantic log analysis and anomalous event detection[7, 14, 15, 21].

当前的日志异常检测方法通常可以分为两种范式Current methods for log anomaly detection can generally be categorized into two paradigms[12]。第一个模型对正常日志模式进行建模，并将与预期行为的偏差识别为异常。另一种将日志异常检测表述为监督分类问题，其中模型直接预测日志序列是正常还是异常。虽然前者通常需要较少的标记数据，但后者通常可以实现更强的基准. The first models normal log patterns and identifies deviations from expected behavior as anomalies. The other formulates log anomaly detection as a supervised classification problem, where models directly predict whether a log sequence is normal or anomalous. While the former typically requires less labeled data, the latter often achieves stronger benchmark[10, 31]性能，但很大程度上依赖于高质量的注释和可靠的预处理管道。在本文中，我们重点关注基于监督分类的日志异常检测器，并研究其置信估计的可靠性。performance but relies heavily on high-quality annotations and reliable preprocessing pipelines. In this paper, we focus on supervised classification-based log anomaly detectors and investigate the reliability of their confidence estimation.

然而，强大的分类性能并不能保证可靠的决策。在日志异常检测中，预测错误对操作的影响是不对称的However, strong classification performance does not guarantee reliable decision-making. In log anomaly detection, prediction errors are asymmetric in their operational impact[28]。正常日志序列被错误地分类为异常可能会触发误报，从而导致额外的检查成本并可能导致警报疲劳。虽然此类错误是不受欢迎的，但它们仍然向操作员或下游监控模块暴露可疑情况。相比之下，被错误分类为正常的异常日志序列构成漏检异常，从而允许潜在的异常行为绕过检测管道。当检测器为不正确的正常预测分配高置信度时，这种风险变得更加严重，因为系统可能会将决策视为可靠并抑制进一步的干预。因此，高置信度的漏报构成了监督日志异常检测器的主要可靠性风险。. A normal log sequence that is incorrectly classified as anomalous may trigger a false alarm, causing additional inspection costs and potentially contributing to alert fatigue. While such errors are undesirable, they still expose suspicious cases to operators or downstream monitoring modules. By contrast, an anomalous log sequence that is incorrectly classified as normal constitutes a missed anomaly, allowing the underlying abnormal behavior to bypass the detection pipeline. This risk becomes more severe when the detector assigns high confidence to the incorrect normal prediction, as the system may treat the decision as reliable and suppress further intervention. Therefore, high-confidence false negatives constitute a central reliability risk for supervised log anomaly detectors.

我们首先检查代表性监督日志异常检测器的置信行为。结果揭示了一个反复出现的可靠性问题：探测器可能确实是错误的。假阳性和假阴性通常都会获得与正确分类的样本相当的置信度分数，这表明检测器置信度与预测正确性不一致。这个问题不能简单地通过增加模型复杂性来解决。更复杂的检测器可能会获得更高的准确度和更有利的聚合校准分数，但它们的置信度分布通常高度集中在 1 附近，从而导致错误的预测过于自信。我们还发现，异常样本往往比正常样本表现出更大的校准误差。这些观察结果表明，强大的基准性能仍然可以隐藏重要的可靠性风险，特别是当异常日志序列被自信地预测为正常时。We first examine the confidence behavior of representative supervised log anomaly detectors. The results reveal a recurring reliability issue: detectors can be confidently wrong. Both false positives and false negatives often receive confidence scores comparable to correctly classified samples, suggesting that detector confidence is not consistently aligned with prediction correctness. This issue is not simply resolved by increasing model complexity. More complex detectors may obtain higher accuracy and more favorable aggregate calibration scores, yet their confidence distributions often remain highly concentrated near one, leaving incorrect predictions overconfident. We also find that anomalous samples tend to exhibit larger calibration errors than normal samples. These observations indicate that strong benchmark performance can still conceal important reliability risks, particularly when anomalous log sequences are confidently predicted as normal.

为了减轻这种风险，我们为监督日志异常检测器设计了一个基于选择器的校准框架。给定预先训练的对数异常检测器的输出，选择器预测检测器的当前预测是正确还是不正确。具体来说，对于每个输入，我们使用检测器的隐藏表示作为可靠性估计的基础。由于分配给正常类的预测和分配给异常类的预测表现出不同的错误模式，因此我们根据检测器的输出标签构建了两条预测路线。对于每条路线，自动编码器都在正确分类的验证样本的隐藏表示上进行训练，并将其重建误差用作可靠性分数。 尽管这两条路线遵循相同的基于自动编码器的评分原理，但它们的校准目标是不对称的。预测正常路线优先考虑潜在的误报，而预测异常路线则更加保守地处理以避免削弱可靠的异常预测。这种设计反映了日志异常检测的不对称风险，其中遗漏的异常通常比错误警报更严重。To mitigate this risk, we design a selector-based calibration framework for supervised log anomaly detectors. Given the output of a pre-trained log anomaly detector, the selector predicts whether the detector’s current prediction is correct or incorrect. Specifically, for each input, we use the detector’s hidden representation as the basis for reliability estimation. Since predictions assigned to the normal class and those assigned to the anomalous class exhibit different error patterns, we construct two prediction routes according to the detector’s output label. For each route, an autoencoder is trained on the hidden representations of correctly classified validation samples, and its reconstruction error is used as a reliability score. Although the two routes follow the same autoencoder-based scoring principle, their calibration objectives are asymmetric. The predicted-normal route prioritizes potential false negatives, while the predicted-anomalous route is treated more conservatively to avoid weakening reliable anomaly predictions. This design reflects the asymmetric risk of log anomaly detection, where missed anomalies are typically more critical than false alarms.

在这项工作中，我们首先表明，尽管基准性能很强，但受监督的日志异常检测器仍可能对错误分类的样本保持过度自信。然后，我们提出了一个基于选择器的校准框架，该框架使用其预测和隐藏表示来预测检测器的输出是正确还是不正确。最后，我们在多个数据集和代表性探测器上证明，我们的方法实现了面向任务的校准权衡，为异常样本（尤其是那些自信地预测为正常的样本）提供了更可靠的置信估计，同时将正常样本的校准退化保持在可接受的范围内。In this work, we first show that supervised log anomaly detectors can remain overconfident on misclassified samples despite strong benchmark performance. We then propose a selector-based calibration framework that predicts whether a detector’s output is correct or incorrect using its predictions and hidden representations. Finally, we demonstrate across multiple datasets and representative detectors that our method achieves a task-oriented calibration trade-off, yielding more reliable confidence estimates for anomalous samples, especially those confidently predicted as normal, while keeping the calibration degradation on normal samples within an acceptable range.

研究人员广泛采用序列建模技术进行系统日志分析，并取得了显着的成功。在这些应用中，日志异常检测已成为一项关键任务，通常被表述为自然语言处理中的文本分类问题。在深度神经网络进步的推动下，日益强大的文本分类模型大大提高了日志异常检测的准确性和鲁棒性。Researchers have extensively employed sequence modeling techniques for system log analysis with notable success. Among these applications, log anomaly detection has emerged as a critical task, often formulated as a text classification problem in natural language processing. Driven by advances in deep neural networks, increasingly powerful text classification models have substantially improved the accuracy and robustness of log anomaly detection.

DeepLog[2]是这一行中的一种代表性方法，它使用 LSTM 对正常日志序列进行建模，并根据与预测执行模式的偏差来检测异常。 日志异常is a representative method in this line, which models normal log sequences with an LSTM and detects anomalies based on deviations from predicted execution patterns. Ⅱ-BII-B 模型标定Model Calibration

模型校准的目的是使预测置信度更好地反映经验的正确性。现有方法可大致分为三类：基于正则化的训练、不确定性估计和事后校准Model calibration aims to make predictive confidence better reflect empirical correctness. Existing methods can be broadly grouped into three categories: regularization-based training, uncertainty estimation, and post-hoc calibration[4].

基于正则化的方法通过减少训练期间的过度自信来改进校准。典型的例子包括标签平滑Regularization-based methods improve calibration by reducing overconfidence during training. Typical examples include label smoothing[19], 置信度惩罚, confidence penalty[23], 混合, mixup[24], 焦点损失, focal loss[18]和类别平衡损失, and class-balanced loss[1]。这些方法可以改进校准，但需要检测器重新训练，并且可能会改变原始检测行为。. These methods can improve calibration, but require detector retraining and may alter the original detection behavior.

不确定性估计方法通过蒙特卡罗 dropout 等方法量化预测不确定性Uncertainty estimation methods quantify predictive uncertainty through approaches such as Monte Carlo dropout[3], 深度系综, deep ensembles[9]和贝叶斯神经网络, and Bayesian neural networks[27]。然而，它们通常需要额外的计算或多次前向传递，使得它们不太适合轻量级事后校准。. However, they often require extra computation or multiple forward passes, making them less suitable for lightweight post-hoc calibration.

事后校准方法调整训练模型的输出概率，而不修改其参数。代表性方法包括温度缩放和逻辑缩放Post-hoc calibration methods adjust the output probabilities of a trained model without modifying its parameters. Representative methods include Temperature Scaling and Logistic Scaling[6], 贝塔缩放, Beta Scaling[8]和选择性缩放, and Selective Scaling[25]。这些方法通过概率级映射或辅助校准模型改进置信度估计，并作为强大的事后校准基线。. These methods improve confidence estimation through probability-level mappings or auxiliary calibration models, and serve as strong post-hoc calibration baselines.

七VII结论Conclusion

我们提出了 LoRD，一种用于监督日志异常检测的轻量级事后校准框架。通过针对预测正常和预测异常路线训练单独的自动编码器，LoRD 从检测器潜在表示中捕获特定于路线的可靠性信号，并选择性地重新校准高风险预测，同时保留原始检测标签。对多个基准数据集和代表性检测器的实验表明，LoRD 有效降低了错误分类的异常样本的置信度，维持了值得信赖的异常警报，并且优于标准缩放和基于集合的校准基线。这些结果证明了面向任务、路径感知的校准对于提高现实世界日志异常检测系统的可靠性的有效性。 LoRD 的匿名演示实现位于：We propose LoRD, a lightweight post-hoc calibration framework for supervised log anomaly detection. By training separate autoencoders for predicted-normal and predicted-anomalous routes, LoRD captures route-specific reliability signals from detector latent representations and selectively recalibrates high-risk predictions while preserving the original detection labels. Experiments on multiple benchmark datasets and representative detectors show that LoRD effectively reduces confidence on misclassified anomalous samples, maintains trustworthy anomaly alarms, and outperforms standard scaling and ensemble-based calibration baselines. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of task-oriented, route-aware calibration for improving the reliability of real-world log anomaly detection systems. 八VIII局限性Limitations

LoRD 有几个限制。首先，其阈值和裕度选择取决于特定于路线的验证误差，因此当假阴性或假阳性很少时，校准边界可能不太稳定。其次，LoRD是一种面向任务的校准方法。它侧重于降低高风险错误的置信度，而不是改进所有聚合校准指标，这可能会导致 ECE、NLL 或 Brier 分数等指标的权衡。第三，LoRD 需要访问检测器隐藏表示，因此更适合白盒或灰盒检测器。未来的工作将把 LoRD 扩展到更强的分布变化和多类别诊断。LoRD has several limitations. First, its threshold and margin selection depend on route-specific validation errors, so the calibration boundary may be less stable when false negatives or false positives are rare. Second, LoRD is a task-oriented calibration method. It focuses on reducing the confidence of high-risk errors rather than improving all aggregate calibration metrics, which may lead to trade-offs in metrics such as ECE, NLL, or Brier score. Third, LoRD requires access to detector hidden representations and is therefore more suitable for white-box or gray-box detectors. Future work will extend LoRD to stronger distribution shifts and multi-class diagnosis.
