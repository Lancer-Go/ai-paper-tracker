2 背景Background

2.1 阿尔法零AlphaZero

阿尔法零AlphaZero[silver_general_2018]是一种零知识学习方法，无需人类知识即可在多种棋盘游戏中实现超人的表现。 训练在（i）自我对弈和（ii）优化之间交替进行。 在自我对弈中，每个动作都是通过运行蒙特卡罗树搜索（MCTS）的多次模拟来选择的is a zero-knowledge learning method that achieves superhuman performance in multiple board games without human knowledge. Training alternates between (i) self-play and (ii) optimization. In self-play, each move is selected by running many simulations of Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)[coulom_efficient_2007]由输出策略和值的神经网络引导。guided by a neural network that outputs a policy and a value.

每个 MCTS 模拟执行三个阶段：选择、扩展和反向传播。 在选择阶段，搜索使用 PUCT 公式从根向叶节点遍历树Each MCTS simulation performs three phases: selection, expansion, and backpropagation. In the selection phase, the search traverses the tree from the root toward a leaf node using a PUCT formula[schrittwieser_mastering_2020]。 在扩展阶段，将到达的叶子扩展为树，并扩展所有合法的子节点。 然后，神经网络评估状态，为这些孩子提供先验动作以及叶子的值估计。 在反向传播期间，该值沿着选定的路径从叶传播回根。 达到模拟预算后，从根的动作分布中采样一个动作，其中每个动作的概率与其在搜索过程中的访问次数成正比。 完成的自玩游戏存储在重播缓冲区中以进行优化。 在优化阶段，从重播缓冲区中随机采样自玩游戏来更新网络。. In the expansion phase, the reached leaf is expanded to the tree, and all legal child nodes are expanded. The neural network then evaluates the state to provide action priors for those children and a value estimate for the leaf. During backpropagation, the value is propagated from the leaf back to the root along the selected path. After the simulation budget is reached, an action is sampled from the root’s action distribution, where the probability of each action is proportional to its number of visits during search. The finished self-play games are stored in a replay buffer for optimization. In the optimization phase, a self-play game is sampled randomly from the replay buffer to update the network.

2.2 甘贝尔阿尔法零Gumbel AlphaZero

尽管AlphaZero取得了超人的性能，但它并不能保证模拟预算较小时的策略改进。 甘贝尔零框架Although AlphaZero has achieved superhuman performance, it does not guarantee policy improvement when the simulation budget is small. The Gumbel Zero framework[danihelka_policy_2022]是为了解决这个限制而引入的。 它修改根选择以提高样本效率并保证策略改进。 从根本上来说，它首先使用以下方法执行无替换候选选择：was introduced to address this limitation. It modifies the root selection to improve sample efficiency and guarantee policy improvement. At the root, it first performs without replacement candidate selection using theGumbel-顶-Gumbel-Top-kk伎俩trick [kool_stochastic_2019]在策略 logits 上，然后通过顺序减半分配模拟on the policy logits, then allocates simulations via sequential halving[karnin_almost_2013]逐步修剪较弱的候选者，直到只剩下一个行动；生存行动是在环境中确定性地采取的，而不是从访问计数中采样。 对于训练目标，由于只探索了有限数量的根子节点，因此不使用访问计数分布。 相反，基于 Q 的目标是通过使用已访问操作的备份 Q 估计和价值网络来估计未访问操作的值来构建的。 这些修改保留了整个 AlphaZero 训练循环，同时提高了小模拟预算下的效率，并在实践中产生了更强的早期训练性能。to progressively prune weaker candidates until a single action remains; that surviving action is taken in the environment deterministically rather than sampling from visit counts. For training targets, since only a limited number of root children are explored, visit-count distributions are not used. Instead, Q-based targets are constructed by using backed-up Q estimates for visited actions and the value network to estimate the values of unvisited ones. These modifications preserve the overall AlphaZero training loop while improving efficiency under small simulation budgets and yielding stronger early training performance in practice.

2.3 随机 AlphaZeroStochastic AlphaZero

要将 AlphaZero 扩展到随机环境，To extend AlphaZero to stochastic environments,随机 AlphaZeroStochastic AlphaZero [antonoglou_planning_2021]介绍事后introduces afterstates[sutton_reinforcement_2018]并将学习的模型纳入规划过程。 关键思想是将代理行为的影响与环境的随机性分开。 从给定的状态开始，应用一个动作确定性地产生一个独特的后状态。 由于环境的随机性，此后状态会根据概率分布产生几种可能的后继状态之一。and incorporates a learned model into the planning process. The key idea is to separate the effect of the agent’s action from the randomness of the environment. From a given state, applying an action deterministically yields a unique afterstate. With the environment’s randomness, this afterstate then produces one of several possible successor states according to a probability distribution.

Stochastic AlphaZero 中的搜索构建了一个在决策节点和机会节点之间交替的树，分别对应于状态和后状态。 在决策节点的选择过程中，算法应用 PUCT 公式的变体来选择操作。 在机会节点，它根据预测的机会概率对结果进行采样。The search in Stochastic AlphaZero constructs a tree that alternates between decision nodes and chance nodes, corresponding to states and afterstates, respectively. 5 讨论Discussion

在本文中，我们评估了各种俄罗斯方块拼图变体的游戏难度。 首先，我们根据三个关键设置制定游戏变体：持有块规则、预览持有块规则和俄罗斯方块块变体。 我们引入两个指标来评估不同游戏规则的难度：训练奖励和收敛迭代。 此外，我们还证明随机 Gumbel AlphaZero 在温和的设置下可以有效地实现接近最佳的性能，使其成为难度评估的理想选择。 最重要的是，我们的分析表明，增加持有块的数量或预览持有块的数量都会使游戏变得更容易，尽管前者的影响明显更强。 然而，添加新的块类型会显着增加游戏的难度，特别是对于 T-五联块。 这些见解加深了我们对俄罗斯方块拼图游戏难度的理解，为进一步研究随机拼图游戏提供了具体的参考。In this paper, we assess the game difficulty of various Tetris Block Puzzle variants. First, we formulate game variants based on three key settings: the holding block rules, the preview holding block rules, and the Tetris block variants. We introduce two metrics to evaluate the difficulty of different game rules: Training rewards and Convergence iterations. Additionally, we demonstrate that Stochastic Gumbel AlphaZero efficiently achieves near-optimal performance under mild settings, making it ideal for difficulty assessment. Most importantly, our analysis indicates that increasing either the number of holding blocks or the number of preview holding blocks makes the game easier, though the former has a markedly stronger impact. However, adding new block types significantly makes the game harder, especially for the T-pentomino block. These insights refine our understanding of the game difficulty of Tetris Block Puzzle and provide a concrete reference for further research on stochastic puzzle games.

展望未来，我们计划探索更多游戏变体，例如禁止选择特定块、更改拼图大小以及引入全新的块类型。 我们还打算进行研究，评估这些变化如何影响人类玩家未来感知的挑战和乐趣。Looking ahead, we plan to explore more game variants, such as banning specific blocks from choosing, changing puzzle sizes, and introducing entirely new block types. We also intend to conduct studies to evaluate how these variations affect human players’ perceived challenge and enjoyment in the future.
