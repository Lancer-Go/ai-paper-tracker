摘要Abstract

Guard 模型广泛用于检测用户提示和 LLM 响应中的有害内容。然而，最先进的防护模型仅依赖于终端层表示，而忽略了分布在内部层的丰富的安全相关特征。我们推出了 SIREN，这是一款利用这些内部功能的轻型防护模型。通过识别Guard models are widely used to detect harmful content in user prompts and LLM responses. However, state-of-the-art guard models rely solely on terminal-layer representations and overlook the rich safety-relevant features distributed across internal layers. We present SIREN, a lightweight guard model that harnesses these internal features. By identifying安全神经元safety neurons通过线性探测并通过自适应层加权策略将它们组合起来，SIREN 从 LLM 内部构建了一个有害检测器，而无需修改底层模型。我们的综合评估表明，SIREN 在多个基准测试中显着优于最先进的开源防护模型，同时使用via linear probing and combining them through an adaptive layer-weighted strategy, SIREN builds a harmfulness detector from LLM internals without modifying the underlying model. Our comprehensive evaluation shows that SIREN substantially outperforms state-of-the-art open-source guard models across multiple benchmarks while using250×250\times可训练参数更少。此外，SIREN 对未见过的基准表现出卓越的泛化能力，自然地实现了实时流检测，并且与生成保护模型相比显着提高了推理效率。总的来说，我们的结果强调了 LLM 内部状态作为实用、高性能有害性检测的有希望的基础。我们的代码位于fewer trainable parameters. Moreover, SIREN exhibits superior generalization to unseen benchmarks, naturally enables real-time streaming detection, and significantly improves inference efficiency compared to generative guard models. Overall, our results highlight LLM internal states as a promising foundation for practical, high-performance harmfulness detection. Our code is available athttps://github.com/CSSLab/SIREN.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 现已大规模部署Large language models (LLMs) are now deployed at scale(OpenAI, 2025; Anthropic, 2025; Google, 2025)并面临持续的内容安全挑战：用户可以提交有害的提示，模型可以生成有害的响应and face a persistent content safety challenge: users can submit harmful prompts, and models can generate harmful responses(Zou et al., 2023)。为了减轻由此产生的风险，法学硕士护栏已变得至关重要，安全专业. To mitigate the risks stemming from this, LLM guardrails have become essential, with safety-specialized守卫模型guard models正在成为主流解决方案emerging as a mainstream solution(Inan et al., 2023; Han et al., 2024; Zhao et al., 2025a)。这些模型通常根据用户提示和模型响应的开源 LLM 主干进行微调，通过从. These models, typically fine-tuned from open-source LLM backbones on both user prompts and model responses, perform harmfulness detection as a generative classification task by decoding from the终端terminal模型层layer of the model(Inan et al., 2023; Han et al., 2024; Zhao et al., 2025a).

然而，这种对终端层的依赖忽略了整个模型中编码的丰富的安全相关功能。最近的工作表明，LLM 内部表示编码了丰富的专业特征，利用这些表示可以显着提高分类任务的性能However, this reliance on the terminal layer overlooks rich safety-relevant features encoded throughout the model. Recent work has revealed that LLM internal representations encode rich specialized features, and leveraging these representations offers substantial performance improvements in classification tasks(Gurnee et al., 2023; Jiao et al., 2024; Lai et al., 2025)。此外，多项研究表明，LLM 的内部表示编码了内容安全的细粒度概念. Moreover, several studies demonstrate that the internal representations of LLM encode fine-grained concepts for content safety(Zhao et al., 2024, 2025b; Kadali and Papalexakis, 2025)。然而这些发现尚未系统地转化为实际的保障模型。 这一差距提供了一个机会：我们可以利用法学硕士的内部表示来构建更好的内容有害性检测器吗？. This gap presents an opportunity: can we harness the LLM internal representations to build better content harmfulness detectors?

2 相关工作Related work

2.1 法学硕士安全系统和护栏LLM Safety Systems and Guardrails

法学硕士的大规模部署需要高性能的安全机制来减少有害内容的生成。当前内容安全检测的主流方法可以大致分为两种范式：判别式分类器和生成式防护模型。The large-scale deployment of LLMs necessitates high-performing safety mechanisms to mitigate harmful content generation. Current mainstream approaches to content safety detection can be broadly categorized into two paradigms: discriminative classifiers and generative guard models.

Also, generative guards incur additional computational costs due to the autoregressive token generation during inference.

3 方法论Methodology

3.1 概述Overview

SIREN 分两个阶段运行。 我们首先采用线性探测来识别对内容安全分类表现出高度显着性的内部神经元，即SIREN operates in two stages. We start by employing linear probing to identify internal neurons exhibiting high salience for content safety classification, namely安全神经元safety neurons，在每一层内独立。 然后，我们通过性能加权聚合自适应地集成这些跨层安全神经元，作为内容安全分类器的特征。, within each layer independently. Then, we adaptively integrate these cross-layer safety neurons via performance-weighted aggregation, serving as the features for our content safety classifier.

6 结论Conclusion

内容安全识别对于在实际应用中部署大型语言模型至关重要。当前主流的防护模型主要依赖于终端层表示，并将安全检测制定为生成分类任务，忽略了 LLM 内部层编码的丰富的安全相关特征。Content safety identification has become essential for deploying large language models in real-world applications. Current mainstream guard models primarily rely on terminal-layer representations and formulate safety detection as a generative classification task, overlooking the rich safety-relevant features encoded across LLM internal layers.

在这项工作中，我们建议利用 LLM 内部神经元表示与我们的轻量级即插即用框架 SIREN 进行危害性检测。通过 L1 正则化探测识别安全神经元，并通过性能加权组合跨层聚合它们，SIREN 提取显着的安全信号以进行内容安全检测。通过全面评估，我们证明 SIREN 在检测性能方面始终优于最先进的开源防护模型，对未见的推理轨迹数据集和流式危害检测表现出强大的泛化能力，同时需要最少的可训练参数并提供更高的推理效率。我们的分析表明，安全相关信息在 LLM 内部表示中得到稳健编码，安全神经元上的自适应跨层聚合有效地利用这些功能来实现卓越的内容安全分类。In this work, we propose to leverage LLM internal neuron representations for harmfulness detection with our lightweight plug-and-play framework, SIREN. By identifying safety neurons through L1-regularized probing and aggregating them across layers with performance-weighted combination, SIREN extracts salient safety signals for content safety detection. Through comprehensive evaluation, we demonstrate that SIREN consistently outperforms state-of-the-art open-source guard models in detection performance, exhibits strong generalization to unseen datasets of reasoning traces and to streaming harmfulness detection, while requiring minimal trainable parameters and offering improved inference efficiency. Our analysis reveals that safety-relevant information is robustly encoded in LLM internal representations, and adaptive cross-layer aggregation on safety neurons effectively harnesses these features for superior content safety classification.

局限性Limitations

首先，我们的安全神经元选择依赖于线性表示假设，通过线性探测来识别层内的显着特征。虽然线性探测适用于标准的基于变压器的 LLM，但该方法可能需要适应与变压器设计显着不同的架构，或者目标概念在各个层内未编码或线性可分离的情况。其次，当前的工作重点是二元危害性分类（有害与安全），遵循安全基准测试的标准实践。将我们的工作扩展到具有多个不安全类别的细粒度安全分类法是未来工作的方向。我们的框架本质上支持多标签分类，并且随着分类法在基准上变得更加标准化，可以在广泛的细粒度安全数据集上进行训练。First, our safety neuron selection relies on the linear representation hypothesis to identify salient features within layers through linear probing. While linear probing applies to standard transformer-based LLMs, the approach may require adaptation for architectures that diverge significantly from transformer designs or where the target concept is not encoded or linearly separable within individual layers. Second, current work focuses on binary harmfulness classification (harmful vs. safe), following standard practice in safety benchmarking. Extending our work to fine-grained safety taxonomies with multiple unsafe categories is a direction for future work. Our framework inherently supports multi-label classification and can be trained on extensive fine-grained safety datasets as taxonomies become more standardized across benchmarks.

B.2 SIREN 可转移至代币级别归因SIREN is Transferable to Token-level Attribution

虽然 SIREN 接受了序列级有害性检测任务的训练，但其架构自然支持转移到令牌级归因，而无需任何额外的训练或微调。 在训练过程中，SIREN 首先学习识别一组稀疏的安全相关神经元，这些神经元的激活对每个标记位置的有害语义进行编码，并通过平均池聚合这些激活，以形成每个标记激活的简单线性聚合作为句子级表示。 因此，学习到的句子级分类器可以被视为对令牌级安全信号的平均值进行操作，而不是依赖于任何固有的全局或特定于序列的特征。 删除池操作允许相同的安全神经元和相同的 MLP 分类器独立应用于每个令牌的隐藏表示，直接生成每个令牌的危害性分数。 为了更好地证明 SIREN 在单个令牌分类中的有效性，我们将 SIREN 在数据集中的所有测试集序列中检测到的最安全和最有害的令牌可视化，如图所示While SIREN is trained on sequence-level harmfulness detection tasks, its architecture naturally supports transfer to token-level attribution without any additional training or fine-tuning. During training, SIREN first learns to identify a sparse set of safety-relevant neurons whose activations encode harmfulness semantics at each token position, and aggregates these activations via average pooling to form a simple linear aggregation of per-token activations as the sentence-level representation. As a result, the learned sentence-level classifier can be viewed as operating on an average of token-level safety signals, rather than relying on any inherently global or sequence-specific feature. Removing the pooling operation allows the same safety neurons and the same MLP classifier to be independently applied to each token’s hidden representation, directly producing per-token harmfulness scores.
