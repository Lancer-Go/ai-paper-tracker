3.1 偏好水平分析 (PLA)Preference-Level analysis (PLA)

多目标强化学习代理通常会根据一系列用户偏好学习一组帕累托最优解决方案。部署后，代理会做出决策来实现用户的预期目标结果（由用户偏好实用程序指定）。因此，代理人的决策和由此产生的行为可能会因一种偏好而异。A multi-objective reinforcement learning agent typically learns a set of Pareto optimal solutions over a range of user-preferences. When deployed, the agent makes decisions to fulfil the expected objective outcomes of the user - specified by the user-preference utility. Therefore the agent’s decisions and the resulting behaviours can vary from one preference to another.

在本节中，我们重点分析单个用户偏好的行为。直觉是，在情景跟踪中，可以从代理所采取的操作中观察到不同的行为片段。这些反映了代理如何学习做出决策以优化每个目标并适应特定的用户偏好，即目标之间的特定权衡比。这些行为对个人目标和总体目标权衡的重要性可能会有所不同。为了定量地测量这些，我们通过采用轨迹归因的概念来进行轨迹分析In this section, we focus on analysing the behaviours at individual single user preferences. The intuition is that, in an episodic trace, there can be different behavioural segments observed from the actions taken by the agent. These reflect how the agent learned to make decisions in order to optimize each objective and to accommodate a specific user preference, i.e. a specific trade-off ratio between the objective. The significance of these behaviours on the individual objectives and on the overall objective trade-off can vary. To measure these quantitatively, we conduct a trajectory analysis by adapting the notion of trajectory attribution from[5].

在 PLA 中，我们使用归因技术来确定某些轨迹对智能体学习行为的贡献。这种方法背后的直觉是，如果我们训练两种不同的策略 - 一种通过从训练数据中删除一组轨迹（即：补充策略）进行训练，另一种则使用完整的训练数据（即：原始策略） - 相对于原始策略的分布变化表明了缺失轨迹的重要性。这是 PLA 中使用的基本概念。In PLA, we use attribution techniques to determine the contribution of certain trajectories towards the agent’s learned behaviour. The intuition behind this approach is that if we train two different policies - one trained by removing a set of trajectories from the training data (i.e: complementary policy) and another with the complete training data (i.e: original policy) - the shift in the distribution relative to the original policy is an indication of the importance of the missing trajectories. This is the underlying notion used in PLA.

6 讨论Discussion

By correlating quantitative attribution scores with qualitative visual evidence, we can validate the findings of the preference level analysis and provide significant insights into how agents navigate complex trade-offs.
