人类HumAIN：人类感知的隐式社交机器人导航: Human-Aware Implicit Social Robot Navigation

Daeun Song1, Nhat Le2, Jeffrey Chen3, Mohammad Nazeri2, Amirreza Payandeh2,
Rohan Chandra3, Reuth Mirsky4, Ross Mead5, Ling Xiao6, Xuesu Xiao2 1Ewha Womans University, Korea. songd@ewha.ac.kr; 2George Mason University, USA. {nle47, mnazerir, apayande, xiao}@gmu.ac.kr; 3University of Virginia, USA. {fyy2wsm, rohanchandra} @virginia.edu; 4Tufts University, USA. reuth.mirsky@tufts.edu; 5Semio, USA. ross@semio.ai; 6Hokkaido University, Japan. ling@ist.hokudai.ac.jp
摘要Abstract

有效的社交机器人导航需要对人类行为敏感，这通常通过步态和方向等微妙的骨骼线索来揭示。我们提出人类感知的隐式社交机器人导航（Effective social robot navigation requires sensitivity to human behavior, often revealed through subtle skeletal cues like gait and orientation. We present Human-Aware Implicit Social Robot Navigation (人类HumAIN），一个新颖的框架，通过知识蒸馏将隐含的社会线索直接融合到规划循环中。我们首先采用基于 Transformer 的教师模型，该模型融合了丰富的多模态输入，包括历史图像、骨骼关键点、机器人状态和机器人的目标，以学习机器人未来轨迹规划的稳健、人类感知的表示。为了实现实时部署，我们将这些知识提炼成一个轻量级的学生模型。通过优化轨迹重建和与教师的潜在特征对齐，学生学会从最小的输入推断复杂的社会动态。 我们的方法通过高效的蒸馏架构弥合了预测与规划之间的差距，使机器人能够以适应性、鲁棒性和社会合规性的方式推理人类行为。 我们验证), a novel framework that fuses implicit social cues directly into the planning loop via knowledge distillation. We first employ a transformer-based teacher model that fuses rich multi-modal inputs, including historic images, skeletal keypoints, robot state, and a robot’s target goal, to learn robust, human-aware representations for the robot’s future trajectory planning. To enable real-time deployment, we then distill this knowledge into a lightweight student model. By optimizing for both trajectory reconstruction and latent feature alignment with the teacher, the student learns to infer complex social dynamics from minimal inputs. Bridging the prediction–planning gap with an efficient distilled architecture, our method enables robots to reason about human behavior in a manner that is adaptive, robust, and socially compliant. We validate人类HumAIN通过广泛的实验，与最先进的基线相比，所有指标的轨迹预测指标平均提高了 29.8%。这些结果凸显了使用隐式全身线索在资源有限的平台上实现类人导航意识的好处。through extensive experiments, where it improves trajectory prediction metrics by an average of 29.8% across all metrics compared to state-of-the-art baselines. These results highlight the benefit of using implicit, whole-body cues to achieve human-like navigation awareness on resource-constrained platforms.

I 简介Introduction

社交机器人导航是机器人技术的一个关键领域，其驱动力是医院对机器人在人类居住环境中自主操作的需求不断增长Social robot navigation is a critical area of robotics, driven by the growing demand for robots to operate autonomously in human-populated environments, from hospitals[diligent2020_spectrum]到公共场所to public spaces[starship2021]。与静态或工业领域的导航不同，社交导航需要对人类行为和舒适度敏感. Unlike navigation in static or industrial domains, social navigation requires sensitivity to human behaviors and comfort[mirsky2024conflict]。为了流畅地导航，机器人必须超越避免碰撞的范围，并推理其他人的预期路径，例如区分继续直行的行人和准备转弯的行人. To navigate fluently, a robot must go beyond collision avoidance and reason about others’ intended paths, such as differentiating a pedestrian continuing straight from one preparing to turn[mirsky2024recognition]。人类运动控制研究表明，行人通过身体姿势、头部方向和步态隐式揭示导航目标. Human motor control studies suggest that pedestrians implicitly reveal navigational goals through body pose, head orientation, and gait[imai2001interaction]。例如，身体姿势通常可以表明一个人是在行走还是站立，以及他们的预期行走方向。. For example, body pose can often indicate whether a person is walking or standing, and their intended walking direction.

然而，尽管这些信号很丰富，但许多运动规划方法将人类过度简化为低维 2D 点或移动圆However, despite the richness of these signals, many motion planning approaches oversimplify humans as low-dimensional 2D points or moving circles[ferrer2013robot]。它们通常仅使用距离保持对位置和速度做出反应. They typically react only to position and velocity using distance keeping[ferrer2013social]或邻近规则or proxemic rules[mead2017autonomous, charalampous2017recent]。虽然有效，但这种表示方式放弃了肢体语言，使其难以感知准备线索。因此，行人转弯似乎更难以预测。. While efficient, such representations discard body language, making it difficult to perceive preparatory cues. As a result, pedestrian turns can appear more difficult to anticipate.

虽然人类轨迹预测方法利用姿势来改进预测While human trajectory prediction methods have leveraged pose to improve forecasting[alahi2016social, salzmann2023hst]，将这些预测集成到机器人轨迹规划中仍然具有挑战性。许多系统以解耦的方式运行，预测人类路径，然后规划机器人轨迹来避开它, integrating these predictions into robot trajectory planning remains challenging. Many systems operate in a decoupled manner by forecasting a human path and then planning a robot trajectory to avoid it[poddar2023crowd]。这种模块化方法将预测视为固定约束，并且经常会产生过于保守的操作或低效的弯路。. This modular approach treats forecasts as fixed constraints and often yields overly conservative maneuvers or inefficient detours.

相比之下，人类隐含地使用身体姿势、臀部方向、目光和步态等社交线索来推断他人的短期运动并相应地调整自己的轨迹In contrast, humans implicitly use social cues such as body pose, hip orientation, gaze, and gait to infer others’ short-term motion and adjust their own trajectories accordingly[kitagawa2021human]。 然而，机器人通常缺乏传感器或计算预算来在部署时可靠地跟踪此类线索，这在社交推理所需的丰富信息与部署时可用的有限感觉运动输入之间造成了差距。一个自然的解决方案是与计算能力强大的教师一起学习表示，该教师在训练期间使用特权信息作为脚手架，然后将这些知识提炼给高效的学员，从原始观察中进行推理。. Yet robots often lack the sensors or compute budget to track such cues reliably at deployment time, which creates a gap between the rich information needed for social reasoning and the limited sensorimotor inputs available at deployment. A natural solution is to learn representations with a computationally powerful teacher that uses privileged information during training as scaffolding, then distill this knowledge into an efficient onboard student that reasons from raw observations.

受这种直觉的启发，我们提出了一个师生框架，将隐式社交线索纳入机器人规划中（图 1）。Inspired by this intuition, we propose a Teacher–Student framework that incorporates implicit social cues into robot planning (Fig.1）。我们使用包括骨骼关键点在内的特权多模态数据来训练 Oracle Teacher，以学习人类行为的丰富潜在表示。然后，我们将这种表示形式提炼成一个轻量级的感觉运动学生，该学生仅对原始图像和目标进行操作，从而无需明确的姿势跟踪即可实现符合社会规范的行为。). We train an Oracle Teacher with privileged multi-modal data, including skeletal keypoints, to learn a rich latent representation of human behavior. We then distill this representation into a lightweight sensorimotor student that operates only on raw images and a goal, enabling socially compliant behavior without explicit pose tracking.

II 相关工作Related Work

我们回顾了社交机器人导航、人类轨迹预测和导航表示学习方面的工作。We review work on social robot navigation, human trajectory prediction, and representation learning for navigation.

Learning-based approaches reduce manual design via imitation learning[nguyen2023toward, karnan2022voila]和强化学习and reinforcement learning[wang2023navistar, faust2018, chandra2025multi]，但在边缘情况下可能难以解释且脆弱, but can be less interpretable and brittle in edge cases[raj2024]。之前的工作将方向或步态线索与手工感知结合起来. Prior work incorporated orientation or gait cues with handcrafted perception[ratsamee2013social, narayanan2020proxemo]。最近，基于 VLM 的方法. More recently, VLM-based methods[vlmsocialnav, narasimhan2025olivia, payandeh2024social]提供语义基础，但它们的计算量很大并且对提示和域转移敏感provide semantic grounding, but they are computationally heavy and sensitive to prompts and domain shifts[wang2025maction]，而且他们常常需要昂贵的语言监督。这些限制激发了我们的方法，该方法通过表示学习和蒸馏来学习隐含的人类线索，而无需手工制定的规则或昂贵的标签。, and they often require costly language supervision. These limitations motivate our approach, which learns implicit human cues via representation learning and distillation without handcrafted rules or expensive labels.

Ⅱ-BII-B 人体轨迹预测Human Trajectory Prediction

人类轨迹预测与社交导航密切相关，因为预测运动支持安全和自然的机器人行为。早期的生成方法模拟了与循环网络或 GAN 的交互，例如社交 LSTMHuman trajectory prediction is closely related to social navigation, since anticipating motion supports safe and natural robot behaviors. Early generative approaches modeled interactions with recurrent networks or GANs, such as Social LSTM[alahi2016social]和社交 GANand Social GAN[gupta2018social]，捕获多代理依赖性。最近，基于 Transformer 和基于图的方法通过对时空关系的明确推理来推进该领域的发展。例如，人体场景变压器, which capture multi-agent dependencies. More recently, Transformer-based and graph-based methods have advanced the field by explicitly reasoning over spatial–temporal relations. For instance, Human Scene Transformer[salzmann2023hst]通过注意力、SocialPose 建模多人交互models multi-human interactions with attention, SocialPose[gao2025social]利用姿势特征进行人群预测、社交转移leverages pose features for forecasting in crowds, Social-Transmotion[saadatnejad2023social]利用人体姿势和边界框进行轨迹预测，以及 UPTorexploits human poses and bounding boxes for trajectory forecasting, and UPTor[nilavadi2025uptor]联合预测人机交互场景的 3D 身体姿势动态和轨迹。一些工作将预测器集成到 MPC 中以进行机器人部署jointly predicts 3D body pose dynamics and trajectories for human–robot interaction scenarios. Some works integrate predictors into MPC for robot deployment[poddar2023crowd]，但强大的预测准确性并不一定会产生符合社会规范的导航行为。, yet strong prediction accuracy does not necessarily yield socially compliant navigation behaviors.

Ⅱ-CII-C 导航表示学习Representation Learning for Navigation

表征学习Representation learning[bengio2013representation]通常通过自我监督学习（SSL）实现，使机器人能够获得强大的功能，而无需昂贵的标签。在导航领域，表示学习已以多种方式得到应用。之前的工作包括视觉-动作预训练, often achieved through self-supervised learning (SSL), enables robots to acquire robust features without costly labels. In navigation, representation learning has been applied in diverse ways. Prior work includes vision–action pretraining[nazeri2024vanp], 语言弱监督, language-weak supervision[narrate2nav]，以及以轨迹为中心的 SSL, and trajectory-centric SSL[nazeri2025verticoder]。 ViNT等大规模监管政策. Large-scale supervised policies such as ViNT[vint]和NoMaDand NoMaD[nomad]进一步改善转移，但大多数仍然关注低层控制，并且没有明确捕捉社会线索。further improve transfer, but most still focus on low-level control, and do not explicitly capture social cues.

总体而言，最近的工作表明，结合人体姿势可以改善轨迹预测，证明姿势和步态线索对于人体运动建模的价值Overall, recent work has shown that incorporating human body pose improves trajectory prediction, demonstrating the value of pose and gait cues for modeling human motion[gao2025social, nilavadi2025uptor]。然而，这些进步尚未转化为社交机器人导航，其中许多方法侧重于几何安全、语义基础或图像的一般表示。我们的方法将骨骼关键点的使用从轨迹预测扩展到导航，并采用 SSL 和蒸馏将隐式人类线索直接嵌入到机器人规划中。. However, these advances have not yet translated into social robot navigation, where many methods focus on geometric safety, semantic grounding, or general representations from images. Our approach extends the use of skeletal keypoints from trajectory prediction to navigation, and employs SSL and distillation to embed implicit human cues directly into robot planning.

V 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出In this paper, we present人类HumAIN，一种端到端的社交机器人导航方法，利用全身姿势作为轨迹规划的隐式提示。我们使用教师-学生策略将特权姿势知识提炼成适合真实机器人部署的轻量级纯视觉学生，从而实现符合社交要求的导航，而无需在运行时进行昂贵的骨骼跟踪。与最先进的基线的广泛比较表明, an end-to-end social robot navigation approach that leverages whole-body pose as an implicit cue for trajectory planning. We use a Teacher–Student strategy to distill privileged pose knowledge into a lightweight vision-only Student suitable for real-robot deployment, enabling socially compliant navigation without expensive skeletal tracking at runtime. Extensive comparisons against state-of-the-art baselines show that人类HumAIN实现了更好的社会依从性，平均增益为 29.8%，这表明隐性社会意识可以部署在资源有限的移动平台上。achieves better social compliance, with an average gain of 29.8%, demonstrating that implicit social awareness can be deployed on resource-constrained mobile platforms.

尽管取得了这些令人鼓舞的结果，可靠的现实世界社交导航仍然是一个开放的挑战。尽管我们的策略在代表性部署场景中表现出有希望的行为，但它还不够强大，无法在不受约束的现实环境中进行可靠部署。特别是，拥挤的场景、遮挡、多个互动的行人以及意想不到的人类行为仍然具有挑战性。此外，我们的方法受到可用数据集的质量和多样性的限制。由于 SCAND 是通过远程操作收集的，所展示的轨迹可能反映了操作员的偏好和交互偏差，使得学习广泛的社会行为变得困难。 未来的工作将侧重于通过更大规模、更多样化的数据集以及自然人类行为下拥挤的多人交互来提高鲁棒性。我们还计划探索更有效的表示学习和蒸馏策略，以捕获复杂的社会动态，同时保持适合实时部署。Despite these encouraging results, reliable real-world social navigation remains an open challenge. Although our policy exhibits promising behaviors in representative deployment scenarios, it is not yet robust enough for reliable deployment in unconstrained real-world environments. In particular, crowded scenes, occlusions, multiple interacting pedestrians, and unexpected human behaviors remain challenging. Furthermore, our approach is limited by the quality and diversity of available datasets. Since SCAND is collected through teleoperation, demonstrated trajectories may reflect operator preferences and interaction biases, making it difficult to learn broadly generalizable social behaviors. Future work will focus on improving robustness through larger-scale, more diverse datasets with crowded, multi-person interactions under natural human behavior. We also plan to explore more effective representation learning and distillation strategies that capture complex social dynamics while remaining suitable for real-time deployment.
