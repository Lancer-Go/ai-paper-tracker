1 简介Introduction

医学多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 越来越多地应用于临床决策支持任务，例如医学视觉问答 (VQA)Medical Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are increasingly applied to clinical decision support tasks such as medical visual question answering (VQA)[12]。这些模型通常通过对精选的临床指导数据集进行监督微调 (SFT) 构建，学习直接的输入输出映射，无需透明的推理过程即可生成预测。在临床实践中，诊断错误会带来严重后果，医生不太可能相信缺乏可解释推理链的模型预测。这凸显了对能够执行透明、逐步临床推理的医学 MLLM 的迫切需求。. Typically built through Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on curated clinical instruction datasets, these models learn a direct input-output mapping that produces predictions without transparent reasoning processes. In clinical practice, where diagnostic errors carry severe consequences, physicians are unlikely to trust model predictions that lack interpretable reasoning chains. This highlights an urgent need for medical MLLMs that can perform transparent, step-by-step clinical reasoning.

强化微调 (RFT)，其中 RL 算法，例如组相对策略优化 (GRPO)Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT), where RL algorithms such as Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)[16]针对可验证的奖励信号优化模型，最近启用了以推理为中心的模型，可以生成扩展的思想链（CoT）输出。这种范式已被应用于医学领域，产生了早期的医学推理模型optimize models against verifiable reward signals, has recently enabled reasoning-focused models that generate extended Chain-of-Thought (CoT) outputs. 我们呈现We present关怀CARE，一个解决基于 RFT 的医学推理模型中置信度错误校准的框架。通过将可扩展的 Medical-CoT 合成管道与信心感知奖励相结合（, a framework that addresses confidence miscalibration in RFT-based medical reasoning models. By combining a scalable Medical-CoT synthesis pipeline with a Confidence-Aware Reward (车CAR) GRPO 内的机制，) mechanism within GRPO,关怀CARE在强化学习优化过程中，明确地将模型表达的置信度与诊断准确性保持一致。三个医疗 VQA 基准的实验表明explicitly aligns the model’s expressed confidence with diagnostic accuracy during RL optimization. Experiments across three Medical VQA benchmarks show that关怀CARE实现了最佳的诊断准确性，同时获得最低的 ECE 和幻觉率，这表明准确性和校准可以共同提高，而不是权衡。这些结果表明，将置信信号纳入奖励设计是实现可靠的医疗人工智能系统的有效途径。我们真诚地希望这项工作能够鼓励在医疗 VQA 之外的安全关键领域进一步探索校准感知培训目标。achieves the best diagnostic accuracy while simultaneously obtaining the lowest ECE and Hallucination Rate, demonstrating that accuracy and calibration can be jointly improved rather than traded off. These results suggest that incorporating confidence signals into the reward design is an effective path toward reliable medical AI systems. We sincerely hope this work encourages further exploration of calibration-aware training objectives in safety-critical domains beyond medical VQA.
