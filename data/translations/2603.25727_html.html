摘要Abstract

自动语音识别（ASR）系统在策划的基准上已经达到了接近人类的准确性，但在当前评估未系统覆盖的条件下，在现实世界的语音代理中仍然失败。如果没有隔离特定故障因素的诊断工具，从业者就无法预测哪种条件、哪种语言会导致何种程度的性能下降。我们推出了 WildASR，这是一种完全源自真实人类语音的多语言（四种语言）诊断基准，它沿着三个轴考虑 ASR 稳健性：环境退化、人口结构变化和语言多样性。通过评估七个广泛使用的 ASR 系统，我们发现性能严重下降且不均匀，并且模型稳健性无法跨语言或条件转移。至关重要的是，模型经常在部分或降级输入下产生看似合理但未言明的内容，从而为下游代理行为带来具体的安全风险。我们的结果表明，有针对性的、因素隔离的评估对于理解和提高生产系统中的 ASR 可靠性至关重要。除了基准本身之外，我们还提供了三种分析工具，从业者可以使用它们来指导部署决策。Automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems have achieved near-human accuracy on curated benchmarks, yet still fail in real-world voice agents under conditions that current evaluations do not systematically cover. Without diagnostic tools that isolate specific failure factors, practitioners cannot anticipate which conditions, in which languages, will cause what degree of degradation. We introduce WildASR, a multilingual (four-language) diagnostic benchmark sourced entirely from real human speech that factorizes ASR robustness along three axes: environmental degradation, demographic shift, and linguistic diversity. Evaluating seven widely used ASR systems, we find severe and uneven performance degradation, and model robustness does not transfer across languages or conditions. Critically, models often hallucinate plausible but unspoken content under partial or degraded inputs, creating concrete safety risks for downstream agent behavior. Our results demonstrate that targeted, factor-isolated evaluation is essential for understanding and improving ASR reliability in production systems. Besides the benchmark itself, we also present three analytical tools that practitioners can use to guide deployment decisions.

自动语音识别ASR

现代 ASR 系统由于自我监督学习和大规模多语言培训而迅速发展。型号Modern ASR systems have advanced rapidly due to self-supervised learning and large-scale multilingual training. 然而，这些 ASR 基准在很大程度上反映了分布内的情况，导致性能饱和，并且对实际部署变化下的系统行为的了解有限However, these ASR benchmarks largely reflect in-distribution conditions, resulting in saturated performance and limited insight into system behavior under realistic deployment shifts(Koenecke et al., 2024; Barański2025investigation; Frieske and Shi, 2024)。 为了解决这一差距，一些工作研究了特定扰动（例如加性噪声、混响、重音语音或域不匹配）下 ASR 的鲁棒性. 3 野生ASRWildASR

现实世界的语音代理会遇到一系列声学和语言条件，而策划的基准测试很少涵盖这些条件，这些条件不仅会引发更高的错误率，还会引发彻底的幻觉。我们构建了一个诊断基准，而不是针对平均情况的准确性进行优化，该基准可以 (i) 反映真实的语音聊天使用情况，(ii) 隔离具体的 OOD 因素，以及 (iii) 实现每个因素的分析。我们将这些因素分为三个维度：Real-world voice agents encounter a long tail of acoustic and linguistic conditions that curated benchmarks rarely cover, and these conditions can trigger not just higher error rates but outright hallucinations. Rather than optimizing for average-case accuracy, we construct a diagnostic benchmark that (i) reflects real voice-chat usage, (ii) isolates concrete OOD factors, and (iii) enables per-factor analysis. We organize these factors into three dimensions:环境退化（地点）、人口变化（人物）和语言多样性（内容）environmental degradation (the where), demographic shift (the who), and linguistic diversity (the what).

多语言 ASR 的“基本事实”是什么？What is the “ground truth” for multilingual ASR?尽管 ASR 通常被视为定义明确的转录任务，但其基本事实概念本质上依赖于用例和文化。诸如是否保留填充词或部分话语之类的决定因语言和会话规范而异，并且可能会对下游解释产生重大影响。在某些情况下，这些现象传达了实用意义，而在另一些情况下，它们通常被正常化。虽然 WildASR 采用固定的转录目标来保持一致性，但我们的观察强调需要多语言基准来考虑文化特定的转录规范，并评估不同的标准化选择如何影响鲁棒性、幻觉行为和下游效用。Although ASR is often treated as a well-defined transcription task, its notion of ground truth is inherently use-case and culture dependent. Decisions such as whether to preserve filler words or partial utterances vary across languages and conversational norms, and can materially affect downstream interpretation. In some settings, these phenomena convey pragmatic meaning, while in others they are routinely normalized. While WildASR adopts a fixed transcription target for consistency, our observations highlight the need for multilingual benchmarks that account for culturally specific transcription norms and evaluate how different normalization choices impact robustness, hallucination behavior, and downstream utility.

ASR 在语音到语音系统时代已经过时了吗？Is ASR obsolete in the era of speech-to-speech systems?大型多模态和 S2S 模型的最新进展促使人们认为显式转录可能变得不必要，因为端到端系统可以直接对声学表示进行操作，同时保留副语言线索。事实上，即使回顾性记录包含识别错误或幻觉短语，现代语音代理通常也能维持流畅的对话。然而，我们的结果表明这并没有削弱 ASR 的重要性；相反，它重新定义了自己的角色。显式 ASR 提供​​透明、可审核和可检查的界面，这对于调试、合规性、检索、索引和结构化工具的使用至关重要。此外，虽然 S2S 系统可以容忍良性条件下的小错误，但我们对 OOD 输入下严重幻觉的发现表明，无法解释的端到端故障可能更难以检测和纠正。从这个角度来看，强大的 ASR 不应被视为遗留组件，而应被视为下一代语音代理的稳定输入层和安全护栏。未来的工作应该研究动态地将显式转录与音频本地推理相结合的混合架构，而不是将它们视为相互排斥的。Recent progress in large multimodal and S2S models has motivated the view that explicit transcription may become unnecessary, as end-to-end systems can directly operate on acoustic representations while preserving paralinguistic cues. Indeed, modern voice agents can often sustain fluent conversations even when retrospective transcripts contain recognition errors or hallucinated phrases. However, our results argue that this does not diminish the importance of ASR; rather, it reframes its role. Explicit ASR provides a transparent, auditable, and inspectable interface that is critical for debugging, compliance, retrieval, indexing, and structured tool use. Moreover, while S2S systems may tolerate minor errors in benign conditions, our findings on severe hallucinations under OOD inputs suggest that uninterpretable end-to-end failures may be harder to detect and correct. From this perspective, robust ASR should be viewed not as a legacy component, but as a stabilizing input layer and safety guardrail for next-generation voice agents. Future work should study hybrid architectures that dynamically combine explicit transcription with audio-native reasoning, rather than treating them as mutually exclusive.

6 结论和未来工作Conclusion and future work

这项工作介绍了 WildASR，这是一种多语言基准测试，旨在在跨越声学环境的各种 OOD 条件下对 ASR 系统进行压力测试（This work introduces WildASR, a multilingual benchmark designed to stress-test ASR systems under a diverse set of OOD conditions spanning acoustic environments (哪里where）、人口统计特征（), demographic characteristics (谁who）和语言现象（), and linguistic phenomena (什么what）。在所有评估的模型中，我们的结果揭示了支离破碎的鲁棒性景观：域内基准的强大性能并不能可靠地跨域、人口统计或交互设置转移，并且故障模式通常表现为严重的语义扭曲而不是逐渐退化。 由于语音界面和对话代理已成为人类与人工智能交互中日益重要的模式，因此这些发现的意义超出了 ASR 作为一项独立任务的范围。). Across all evaluated models, our results reveal a fragmented robustness landscape: strong performance on in-domain benchmarks does not reliably transfer across domains, demographics, or interaction settings, and failure modes often manifest as severe semantic distortions rather than gradual degradation. These findings carry broader implications beyond ASR as a standalone task since voice interfaces and conversational agents become an increasingly prominent mode of human–AI interaction.

由于从真实人类语音源构建多语言基准的范围以及大规模评估所需的资源，WildASR 自然存在一些局限性，这些局限性表明了未来有希望的方向：Due to the scope of constructing a multilingual benchmark from real human speech sources and the resources required for large-scale evaluation, WildASR naturally has limitations that point to promising future directions:

从诊断到缓解。From diagnosis to mitigation.我们当前的工作重点是识别和描述故障模式，包括幻觉行为，而不是解决它们。 WildASR 的因子隔离结构直接揭示了哪些 OOD 条件会导致每种模型和语言的最大退化，为有针对性的数据增强、微调或适应策略提供了一个自然的起点。特别是，我们的幻觉分析促进了幻觉感知解码和弃权机制的发展，这些机制在置信度较低时抑制转录。Our current work focuses on identifying and characterizing failure modes, including hallucination behavior, rather than resolving them. The factor-isolated structure of WildASR directly reveals which OOD conditions cause the largest degradation for each model and language, providing a natural starting point for targeted data augmentation, fine-tuning, or adaptation strategies. In particular, our hallucination analysis motivates the development of hallucination-aware decoding and abstention mechanisms that withhold transcription when confidence is low.

更广泛的语言、条件和样本覆盖范围。Broader language, condition, and sample coverage.当前的基准测试涵盖四种语言和一组定义的 OOD 因素，忽略了低资源语言和条件，例如多说话者重叠和实时流工件。此外，由于特定人群公开可用的真实语音数据稀缺，某些子集（特别是人口统计划分）的样本量有限。作为旨在揭示故障模式并识别广泛的退化模式的诊断基准，这些大小足以满足我们的分析目标，但扩大语言覆盖范围和每个条件的样本大小将进一步加强诊断覆盖范围。The current benchmark covers four languages and a defined set of OOD factors, leaving out low-resource languages and conditions such as multi-speaker overlap and real-time streaming artifacts. Additionally, certain subsets, particularly the demographic split, have limited sample sizes due to the scarcity of publicly available real speech data for specific populations. As a diagnostic benchmark designed to expose failure modes and identify broad degradation patterns, these sizes are sufficient for our analytical goals, but expanding both language coverage and per-condition sample sizes would further strengthen the diagnostic coverage.

更细粒度的报告和更广泛的模型覆盖范围。Finer-grained reporting and broader model coverage.由于篇幅限制，正文中的结果以聚合形式呈现，以支持跨条件分析和叙述清晰度；附录中提供了每个模型的完整细分。我们还欢迎未来评估 WildASR 的其他模型以扩大比较范围的工作。Due to space constraints, results in the main text are presented in aggregated form to support cross-condition analysis and narrative clarity; full per-model breakdowns are provided in the appendix. We also welcome future work that evaluates additional models on WildASR to broaden the comparative landscape.

更广泛地说，WildASR 强调了基于真实人类语音源和现实使用模式的基准的必要性，因为综合或狭隘策划的评估可能会掩盖部署中最重要的故障模式。我们希望这项工作能够成为开发 ASR 系统的基础，这些系统不仅准确，而且在现实世界的语音代理中可靠。More broadly, WildASR highlights the need for benchmarks grounded in real human speech sources and realistic usage patterns, as synthetic or narrowly curated evaluations risk obscuring failure modes that matter most in deployment. We hope this work serves as a foundation for developing ASR systems that are not only accurate, but dependable in real-world voice agents.

附录CAppendix C策展渠道详细信息Curation pipeline details

我们描述了§中介绍的管理流程的每个阶段We describe each stage of the curation pipeline introduced in §3.1:

DC（数据收集）。DC (Data Collection).我们从公开可用的语音语料库中获取原始音频，并在我们的目标语言中提供经过验证的转录本。表中列出了每个子类别的源数据集We source raw audio from publicly available speech corpora with verified transcripts across our target languages. Source datasets for each subcategory are listed in Table6.

SF（扬声器过滤）。SF (Speaker Filtering).对于人口统计子集，我们根据数据集注释验证说话者元数据（年龄、口音、母语），并丢弃标签不明确或缺失的样本。对于老年人，我们过滤选择与年龄相关的声学退化为主要特征的样本，最大限度地减少方言变化等混杂因素。For demographic subsets, we verify speaker metadata (age, accent, native language) against dataset annotations and discard samples with ambiguous or missing labels. For older adults, we filter to select samples where age-related acoustic degradation is the dominant feature, minimizing confounding factors such as dialect variation.

QF（质量过滤）。QF (Quality Filtering).我们丢弃难以理解或损坏的录音，并删除信噪比较差的样本。对于儿童语音，我们执行严格的过滤以排除低信噪比样本。对于语码转换，我们删除了没有实质性多语言混合的样本。We discard unintelligible or corrupted recordings and remove samples with poor signal-to-noise ratios. NR（音频标准化）。NR (Audio Normalization).所有音频均重新采样为 16kHz 单声道，并进行响度归一化，以确保跨源的输入条件一致。All audio is resampled to 16 kHz mono with loudness normalization to ensure consistent input conditions across sources.
