6 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and Future Work

我们提出了 Gumbel 机，这是一种反事实生成的模块化方法，它利用偏好对齐和重放随机状态We present the Gumbel Machine, a modular approach to counterfactual generation that leverages preference alignment and replayed random state withβ\beta-事后控制。 我们在两个学生撰写的摘要数据集上评估了我们的方法，并证明它有效地生成了反事实的学生作业，这些作业既符合标题，又与原始作业相似。这项工作表明，恢复的生成随机性可以作为可立即应用的可调控制信号。-Hindsight control. We evaluate our approach on two datasets of student-written summaries and demonstrate that it effectively generates counterfactual student work that is both rubric-valid and similar to the original. This work demonstrates that recovered generative randomness can serve as a tunable control signal with immediate application.

我们的工作概述了未来的方向，包括：在其他领域和生成任务中评估这种方法，评估生成的反事实相对于传统写作反馈的教学有效性，以及进一步调查恢复有效控制的噪音的必要和充分条件。Our work outlines future directions that include: evaluating this approach across other domains and generation tasks, assessing the pedagogical effectiveness of generated counterfactuals relative to conventional writing feedback, and further investigation into the necessary and sufficient conditions for recovering noise that is effective for control.

局限性Limitations

虽然我们的结果表明恢复的甘贝尔噪声可以作为反事实学生写作的实用相似性控制信号，但存在限制我们研究结果的解释和普遍性的局限性。While our results suggest that recovered Gumbel noise can serve as a practical similarity control signal for counterfactual student writing, there are limitations that constrain the interpretation and generalizability of our findings.

首先，我们的恢复和重播过程假设学生写作的数据生成过程可以通过代理 LLM 引入的自回归 Gumbel-Max 结构因果模型 (SCM) 来近似。正如之前的工作所指出的，Gumbel-Max SCM 是分类采样的几种参数化之一，是一种建模选择，而不是一种发现的结构First, our recovery-and-replay procedure assumes that the data-generating process for student writing can be approximated by the autoregressive Gumbel-Max structural causal model (SCM) induced by the proxy LLM. As prior work has noted, the Gumbel-Max SCM is one of several parameterizations of categorical sampling and is a modeling choice rather than a discovered structure(Chatzi et al., 2025; Benz et al., 2025)。真正的学生写作还涉及计划、修订和非本地编辑，因此严格的自回归假设充其量只是一个近似值。因此，我们的结果应该被解读为反事实. Real student writing also involves planning, revision, and non-local edits, so the strict autoregressive assumption is at best an approximation. Our results should therefore be read as counterfactuals在代理自回归 SCM 下under a proxy autoregressive SCM，而不是作为真实学生写作过程中的因果反事实。我们的消融研究支持了这种解释，我们观察到，针对写得好的摘要进行了优化的预训练法学硕士，将低可能性分配给学生撰写的摘要（可能包含与标题相关的错误），因此它产生的噪音是残差的，而不是有意义的。然而，在学生数据上训练法学硕士后，噪声变成了更有效的控制信号，从而成为更好但仍然有限的学生写作代理模型。, rather than as causal counterfactuals over the true student-writing process. Our ablation study supports this interpretation where we observe that a pre-trained LLM, optimized for well-written summaries, assigns low likelihood to student-authored ones (which can contain rubric-relevant errors), so the noise it produces is residual-like rather than meaningful. However, upon training the LLM on student data, the noise becomes a much more effective control signal, and thus a better, but still limited, proxy model of student writing.

其次，我们的方法假设可以访问精心设计的提示，指定评分标准，要求遵守参考，并明确定义目标分数变化。正如消融研究所示，参考条件作用是有效控制的关键促成条件，表明对不保留这种结构的即时变化的敏感性。此外，我们根据标题标准训练单独的对齐适配器，这提高了我们实验的可控性，但如果没有更先进的多任务或组合对齐策略，可能无法干净地扩展到更丰富的多标准反馈。Second, our approach assumes access to carefully engineered prompts that specify the rubric, request adherence to a reference, and clearly define the target score change. As shown in an ablation study, reference conditioning is a key enabling condition for effective control, suggesting sensitivity to prompt variation that does not preserve this structure. In addition, we train separate alignment adapters per rubric criterion, which improves controllability in our experiments but may not scale cleanly to richer, multi-criterion feedback without more advanced multitask or compositional alignment strategies.

第三，虽然我们的方法是在专有法学硕士和开放权重类型的法学硕士之间进行比较，但我们的比较基线主要是基于提示的。虽然这些基线代表了反事实生成方法的最新研究，但它们与 Gumbel 机的比较有些不匹配，因为它们不直接修改输出逻辑。虽然之前的大量研究已经探索了用于反事实生成的逻辑操纵技术，但这些技术并不能转化为有序任务设置，因为它们是为名义任务设计的，并且需要进行重大修改才能适应我们的任务，这对直接比较提出了挑战Third, while our approach is compared across LLMs of both proprietary and open-weight types, our comparative baselines are primarily prompting-based. 道德考虑Ethical Considerations

反事实的学生写作，即使旨在作为说明性反馈，也有可能被视为“理想的重写”而不是教学示例，这可能会无意中阻碍学生的能动性或掩盖学生的原始声音。如果在没有适当框架的情况下部署，此类系统可能会鼓励过度依赖人工智能生成的修订，将学习从技能开发转向模仿。这些风险强调了将反事实作为说明性演示而不是课堂环境中的权威纠正的重要性。Counterfactual student writing, even when intended as illustrative feedback, risks being perceived as an “ideal rewrite” rather than a pedagogical example, which could unintentionally discourage student agency or mask the student’s original voice. If deployed without appropriate framing, such systems may encourage over-reliance on AI-generated revisions, shifting learning from skill development toward imitation. These risks underscore the importance of positioning counterfactuals as illustrative demonstrations rather than authoritative corrections in classroom environments.

在公平性、偏见和问责制方面，我们系统的生成和评估组件都依赖于法学硕士，这些硕士可能会编码与语言背景、方言或写作风格相关的偏见，从而可能使某些学生群体比其他学生群体更有利。用于评估有效性的自动评分模型可能会进一步放大这些偏差，特别是当微妙的风格特征与质量混为一谈时。此外，使用学生写作作为输入会引起隐私问题，特别是在涉及未成年人的课堂部署中。因此，这种方法在现实世界中的任何使用都应包括偏见审计、人工监督、人工智能参与的透明度等保障措施，并遵守有关学生数据使用的当地法规。On fairness, bias, and accountability, both the generation and evaluation components of our system rely on LLMs that may encode biases related to language background, dialect, or writing style, potentially advantaging certain student populations over others. Automated scoring models used to assess validity may further amplify these biases, especially when subtle stylistic features are conflated with quality. Additionally, using student writing as input raises privacy concerns, particularly in classroom deployments involving minors. Any real-world use of this approach should therefore include safeguards such as bias audits, human oversight, transparency about AI involvement, and follow local regulations regarding the use of student data.
