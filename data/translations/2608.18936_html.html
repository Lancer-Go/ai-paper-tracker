可解释架构的图形设计Graphical Design of Interpretable Architectures

Pietro Barbiero Affiliation: IBM Research Affiliation: Zurich Email: pietro.barbiero@ibm.com
摘要Abstract

设计、实现和比较可解释的架构需要一种正式的语言来表示它们。最常见的表示在以下两个方面之一存在缺陷。符号方程无法让架构一目了然。概率图形模型和流程图不描述实际的张量操作，因此隐藏了关键见解并限制了可重复性。 为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了一种用于设计可解释的人工智能架构的图形符号，该符号改编自彭罗斯张量符号。这种图形符号提供了架构的全局视图，并一对一映射到 PyTorchDesigning, implementing, and comparing interpretable architectures requires a formal language to represent them. The most common representations fall short in one of two ways. Symbolic equations give no global view of an architecture at a glance. Probabilistic graphical models and flowcharts do not describe actual tensor manipulations, thus hiding key insights and limiting reproducibility. To close this gap, we introduce a graphical notation for designing interpretable AI architectures, adapted from Penrose tensor notation. This graphical notation gives a global view of an architecture and maps one to one onto PyTorch埃因苏姆einsum代码。我们首先使用这种表示法来描述可通过构造解释的架构，包括概念瓶颈、稀疏探针、原型网络、神经加性模型和线性模型的混合。然后，我们绘制了 Steerling-8B（一种前沿可解释语言模型）的关键架构组件。该图提供了对架构的全局洞察（例如，显示 Steerling 是残差模型）、每个单独操作的几何解释以及直接转换为 33 行 PyTorch 代码。code. We first use this notation to describe architectures that are interpretable by construction, including concept bottlenecks, sparse probes, prototype networks, neural additive models, and mixtures of linear models. We then diagram the key architectural components of Steerling-8B, a frontier interpretable language model. The diagram yields global insights into the architecture (e.g., showing that Steerling is a residual model), a geometric interpretation of each individual operation, and a direct translation into 33 lines of PyTorch code.

1 简介Introduction

Frontier AI 模型操纵称为张量的高维对象。因此，要理解、设计或实现这些模型，我们必须从高维角度思考。由于前沿模型由许多此类操作组成，因此其张量操作的直观和形式表示是理解、比较和设计最先进架构的关键。Frontier AI models manipulate high-dimensional objects called tensors. For this reason, to understand, design, or implement these models, we must think in high-dimensional terms. As frontier models are composed of many such operations, intuitive and formal representations of their tensor manipulations are key to understanding, comparing, and designing state-of-the-art architectures.

常见的形式表示，例如符号方程，可能会限制对架构的全局洞察。举个例子，表达式Common formal representations, such as symbolic equations, may limit global insights on the architecture at a glance. As an example, the expression

6 讨论Discussion

6.1 相关作品Related works

图形张量符号可以追溯到Graphical tensor notation dates to25，他介绍了物理学中张量收缩的图表。此符号已被分类量子力学界采用, who introduced diagrams for tensor contraction in physics. The notation has since been adopted by the categorical-quantum-mechanics community(11)，以及最近的理论计算机科学和机器学习。, and, more recently, by theoretical computer science and machine learning.

第一个工作是为深度学习架构提出通用的图解语言，而不关注可解释性。A first line of work has proposed general-purpose diagrammatic languages for deep learning architectures, without a focus on interpretability.10提出命名轴张量表示法来消除注意力等操作的歧义。propose named-axis tensor notation to disambiguate operations such as attention.2介绍了神经电路图，这是一种与实现有正式对应关系的图形语言，后来用于导出内存高效的注意力算法introduces neural circuit diagrams, a graphical language with a formal correspondence to implementation, later used to derive memory-efficient attention algorithms1. 14; 24; 19使用弦图（彭罗斯表示法的数学概括）对架构进行更广泛的范畴论解释，以将卷积神经网络、循环神经网络和变压器等架构统一到一个代数框架下。pursue a category-theoretic account of architectures more broadly, using string diagrams, a mathematical generalization of Penrose notation, to unify architectures such as convolutional neural nets, recurrent neural nets, and transformers under one algebraic framework.

However, this line of work analyses pre-trained opaque models and does not consider tensor manipulations required by inherently interpretable models.

本文采用了一种为训练模型的事后分析而开发的符号，据我们所知，第一次将该符号应用于正向问题：分析和设计可通过构造解释的架构，并具有从图表到 PyTorch 代码的直接、机械路径。This paper takes a notation developed for post-hoc analysis of trained models and, for the first time to our knowledge, applies this notation to the forward problem: analysing and designing architectures that are interpretable by construction, with a direct, mechanical path from diagram to PyTorch code.

6.2 限制和具体使用Limitations and concrete usage

张量图对于多线性运算来说是精确的，但非线性、掩蔽和离散运算（例如 top-k）需要临时扩展。接近实现也是一把双刃剑：图的大小随着张量操作的复杂性而增长，因此完整的前沿架构很快就会变得难以在会议论文中绘制。因此，我们认为张量图最适合与概率图模型等较粗略的形式主义搭配。概率图形模型可用于捕获随机变量之间的高级因果结构，而小张量图则指定如何实现每个条件概率函数。Tensor diagrams are exact for multilinear operations, but nonlinearities, masking, and discrete operations such as top-k require ad hoc extensions. Closeness to implementation is also a double-edged sword: diagram size grows with the complexity of the tensor manipulations, so full frontier architectures quickly become unwieldy to draw in conference papers. For this reason, we see tensor diagrams as best paired with a coarser formalism such as probabilistic graphical models. Probabilistic graphical models may be used to capture the high-level causal structure between random variables, while small tensor diagrams specify how each conditional probability function is implemented.

6.3 结论Conclusion

我们已经展示了张量图如何指导可解释的深度神经网络的设计。对于可解释性研究中最常见的张量操作，我们构建了一个“罗塞塔石碑”，并排显示匹配图、PyTorch代码、几何解释和符号方程，以便不同背景的读者可以比较和理解它们。我们还解决了一个更困难的情况：我们用大约 30 行代码绘制并实现了前沿可解释语言模型 Steerling-8B 的关键模块。We have shown how tensor diagrams can guide the design of interpretable deep neural networks. For the most common tensor manipulations in interpretability research, we have built a “Rosetta stone” showing the matching diagram, PyTorch code, geometric interpretation, and symbolic equation side by side, so readers from different backgrounds can compare and understand them. We also tackled a harder case: we diagrammed and implemented the key modules of a frontier interpretable-by-design language model, Steerling-8B in about 30 lines of code.

张量图富有表现力、形式化，并且直接映射到 PyTorch 代码上。由于这些原因，它们可以与概率图形模型等其他图形工具一起成为设计、比较和实现可解释架构的标准工具。Tensor diagrams are expressive, formal, and map directly onto PyTorch code. For these reasons, they could become a standard tool for designing, comparing, and implementing interpretable architectures, alongside other graphical tools such as probabilistic graphical models.
