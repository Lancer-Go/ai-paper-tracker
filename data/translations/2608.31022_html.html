MNIST-PRO：MNIST 重新成为 AI 代理的部分可观察世界MNIST-PRO: MNIST is Back as a Partially Observable World for AI Agents

Vernon Toh    Navonil Majumder    Zhengyuan Liu    Nancy F. Chen    Soujanya Poria Corresponding author: Soujanya Poria
摘要Abstract

部分可观察环境中的人工智能代理需要协调主动感知与工作记忆，以维持不断发展的感知状态。 然而，现有的基准很难隔离这种感知状态的构建和解释能力，因为它们引入了物理和控制的复杂性。 我们解决这个问题AI agents in partially observable environments need to coordinate active sensing with working memory to maintain an evolving perceptual state. However, existing benchmarks struggle to isolate this perceptual-state construction and interpretation capability because they introduce physical and control complexities. We address this withMNIST-PRO，一个基准，通过将 MNIST 数字识别转换为具有回溯约束的顺序的、基于一瞥的搜索任务来隔离代理感知。 我们评估了四种记忆表示形式的十个多模态模型，包括原始视觉历史、文本状态、结构化度量网格图和统一的视觉画布。 虽然模型在完全可观察性下表现出色，但部分可观察性暴露了明显的性能差距。 我们确定了三个不同的瓶颈。 首先，感知状态的构建和解释提出了挑战，因为代理人努力整合碎片化的一瞥。 其次，特工通常在看到完整序列之前就停止探索。 第三，即使面对随后的矛盾证据，模型也常常无法修正早期的错误信念。 这些结果表明，仅仅获取视觉证据是不够的。 代理还必须能够构建和更新可靠的感知状态。, a benchmark that isolates agentic perception by converting MNIST digit recognition into a sequential, glimpse-based search task with lookback constraints. We evaluate ten multimodal models across four memory representations, including raw visual history, textual states, structured metric grid maps, and a consolidated visual canvas. While models excel under full observability, partial observability exposes a clear performance gap. We identify three distinct bottlenecks. First, perceptual-state construction and interpretation present a challenge, as agents struggle to integrate fragmented glimpses. Second, agents often stop exploring before they see the full sequence. Third, models often fail to revise early, incorrect beliefs even when faced with subsequent contradictory evidence. These results show that simply acquiring visual evidence is not enough. Agents must also be able to build and update a reliable perceptual state.

1 简介Introduction

在这项工作中，我们研究了代理感知，这是一种随着人工智能代理导航图形用户界面（GUI）交互和机器人操作等复杂任务而变得越来越相关的能力In this work, we study agentic perception, an increasingly relevant capability as AI agents navigate complex tasks like graphical user interface (GUI) interaction and robotic manipulation(Brohan et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024; Xie et al., 2024)。 在代理感知中，人工智能代理主动从观察中收集信息，以构建不断发展的决策感知状态。 这与传统的主动视觉文献不同，后者传统上强调控制传感以获得信息丰富的观察结果. In agentic perception, AI agents actively gather information from their observations to construct an evolving perceptual state for decision-making. This differs from the classical active vision literature that has traditionally emphasized controlling sensing to acquire informative observations(Bajcsy et al., 2016)。 因此，主动视觉可以被视为代理感知的组成部分之一。 在部分可观察的环境中，智能体必须协调主动视觉感知与工作记忆，以构建持久的世界感知状态。 在任何给定时刻，代理的视觉感知都受到其视野或传感器位置的限制. As such, active vision can be viewed as one component of agentic perception. In partially observable environments, agents must coordinate active visual sensing with working memory to construct a persistent perceptual state of the world. At any given instant, the agent’s visual perception is limited by its field of view or sensor position(Savva et al., 2019)。 为了在这种不确定性下做出最佳决策，智能体必须不断更新其内部表示：它必须决定下一步看哪里，并将新的观察结果与先前的观察结果相结合. To make optimal decisions under this uncertainty, the agent must continuously update its internal representation: it must decide where to look next and consolidate new glimpses with prior observations(Wu and Xie, 2023; Xu et al., 2026; Yang et al., 2025).

由于现有基准中的混杂因素，评估多模式代理如何有效地构建这些感知状态具有挑战性。 一、标准多模态推理基准，如MMBenchEvaluating how effectively multimodal agents construct these perceptual states is challenging due to the confounding factors in existing benchmarks. First, standard multimodal reasoning benchmarks, such as MMBench(Liu et al., 2024)和MMMUand MMMU(Yue et al., 2024)，预先与每个问题一起提供相关的视觉输入，因此不需要模型在推理过程中主动收集或选择视觉证据。 二、主动控制基准测试，如ActiView, provide the relevant visual inputs together with each question upfront, and therefore do not require models to actively gather or select visual evidence during inference. Second, active control benchmarks, such as ActiView(Wang et al., 2025)和 ActiveVisionand ActiveVision(Zhang et al., 2026a)，要求模型迭代地获取和推理多个视觉观察结果。 然而，这些设置不一定区分保持连贯的、与任务相关的感知状态的智能体和严重依赖重新访问或保留低水平视觉观察的智能体。 这使得很难区分性能是否反映了真正的状态感知合成或可用视觉历史的有效使用。 最后，虽然体现了 ALFRED 等 3D 基准, require models to iteratively acquire and reason over multiple visual observations. However, these settings do not necessarily distinguish between agents that maintain a coherent, task-relevant perceptual state and agents that rely heavily on revisiting or retaining low-level visual observations. This makes it difficult to isolate whether performance reflects genuine stateful perceptual synthesis or effective use of the available visual history. Finally, while embodied 3D benchmarks such as ALFRED(Shridhar et al., 2020)需要顺序决策，它们对模拟导航和对象交互的依赖会引入低级执行故障，例如碰撞和不成功的交互操作，这可能会混淆感知、记忆、规划或控制故障的归因。require sequential decision-making, their reliance on simulated navigation and object interaction introduces low-level execution failures, such as collisions and unsuccessful interaction actions, that can confound the attribution of failures to perception, memory, planning, or control.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了In this work, we introducedMNIST-PRO，一个评估多模式人工智能代理如何探索和表示部分观察到的视觉环境的基准。 通过将 MNIST 数字识别转变为具有回溯约束的基于一瞥的顺序搜索，我们分析了主动探索和状态构建的不同作用。 我们的实验揭示了完全可观察性视觉识别和代理感知之间存在明显的性能差距。 我们证明，仅仅获取视觉证据并不能保证做出准确的决策。 相反，成功取决于代理人如何表达和解释他们的视觉历史。 虽然以编程方式将一瞥整合到视觉画布中可以提高某些模型的准确性，但其他模型却无法解释这些画布或探索信息最丰富的区域。 我们的结果表明，构建有能力的人工智能代理需要主动感知控制和结构化状态表示，而不是仅仅依赖于扩展原始上下文窗口。, a benchmark evaluating how multimodal AI agents explore and represent partially observed visual environments. By transforming MNIST digit recognition into a sequential, glimpse-based search with lookback constraints, we analyzed the distinct roles of active exploration and state construction. Our experiments revealed a clear performance gap between full-observability visual recognition and agentic perception. We demonstrated that simply acquiring visual evidence does not guarantee accurate decision-making. Instead, success depends on how agents represent and interpret their visual history. While programmatically consolidating glimpses into a visual canvas improves accuracy for some models, others fail to interpret these canvases or to explore the most informative regions. Our results show that building capable AI agents requires both active sensory control and structured state representation, rather than relying solely on expanding raw context windows.
