摘要Abstract

思维链 (CoT) 提示被广泛用作视觉语言模型 (VLM) 的测试时间扩展策略，但目前尚不清楚当 VLM 生成更长的推理轨迹时会扩展什么。我们询问 CoT 是否需要持续访问图像令牌，或者它是否主要对前向传递中先前已提供的视觉信息进行操作。我们介绍Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting is widely used as a test-time scaling strategy for Vision-Language Models (VLMs), but it remains unclear what is extended when VLMs generate longer reasoning traces. We ask whether CoT requires continued access to image tokens, or whether it mainly operates over visual information already made available earlier in the forward pass. We introduce视觉访问扫描Visual Access Sweep，一种因果干预，将注意力从生成的令牌查询屏蔽到沿层深度和生成时间的图像令牌键，并定义, a causal intervention that masks attention from generated-token queries to image-token keys along layer depth and generation time, and define the视觉访问边界Visual Access Boundary（VAB）作为保持任务准确性的最小访问区域。 在 Qwen2.5-VL 和 InternVL3 的六种模型配置中，无 CoT 直接应答和 CoT 提示均表现出有限的 VAB。在 14B 和 38B 尺度的 Qwen2.5-VL-32B 和 InternVL3 中，当针对 no-CoT 完全访问目标评估 CoT 时，其 VAB 层与 no-CoT 边界最多相差两层，尽管代数明显更长。这表明 CoT 主要不是通过在整个推理跟踪中延长直接图像令牌访问来提高性能，而是通过将语言端计算扩展到图像派生的隐藏状态信息来提高性能。 我们进一步表明，CoT 增益受到感知读数的限制。当模型可以可靠地读出所查询的视觉属性时，CoT 会有所帮助，但当读出不可靠时，CoT 就没有帮助。符号属性预言表明，一旦将真实属性作为文本提供，CoT 就可以改进计数，而单对象探测与解码检查表明，硬属性可以从隐藏状态线性恢复，但模型本身很难输出。总之，这些分析将瓶颈放在读出而不是计数上。(VAB) as the minimal access region that preserves task accuracy. Across six model configurations from Qwen2.5-VL and InternVL3, both no-CoT direct answering and CoT prompting exhibit finite VABs. In Qwen2.5-VL-32B and InternVL3 at 14B and 38B scales, when CoT is evaluated against the no-CoT full-access target, its VAB layer differs from the no-CoT boundary by at most two layers, despite substantially longer generations. This suggests that CoT does not primarily improve performance by prolonging direct image-token access throughout the reasoning trace, but by extending language-side computation over image-derived hidden-state information. We further show that CoT gains are constrained by perceptual readout. CoT helps when the queried visual attribute can be reliably read out by the model, but not when that readout is unreliable. A symbolic-attribute oracle shows that CoT can improve counting once ground-truth attributes are supplied as text, while a single-object probe-vs-decode check shows that hard attributes can be linearly recoverable from hidden states yet difficult for the model itself to output. Together, these analyses place the bottleneck at readout rather than counting.

1 简介Introduction

CoT 提示是法学硕士中的标准测试时间缩放工具CoT prompting is a standard test-time scaling tool in LLMs(Kojima et al., 2022; Wei et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023)并且也被 VLM 所采用and has also been adopted in VLMs(Zhang et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2025; Lin et al., 2025)。然而，它对视觉任务的好处并不统一：一些研究报告了显式中间推理的收益，而另一些研究则发现在感知重的视觉 QA 设置中收益有限或下降. However, its benefits on visual tasks are not uniform: some studies report gains from explicit intermediate reasoning, whereas others find limited or degraded gains on perception-heavy visual QA settings(Lu et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024b)。 CoT 对 VLM 性能贡献的机制是什么？ CoT 是否通过在生成过程中延长对图像标记的直接访问，或者通过对之前已经提供的视觉信息启用额外的语言端计算来提高性能？. What mechanism underlies CoT’s contribution to VLM performance? Does CoT improve performance by prolonging direct access to image tokens during generation, or by enabling additional language-side computation over visual information already made available earlier?

关于这个问题的现有证据主要是观察性的：研究表明，VLM 对图像标记的关注随着生成的进行而减弱Existing evidence on this question is largely observational: studies document that VLM attention to image tokens decays as generation proceeds(Tong et al., 2024; Rahmanzadehgervi et al., 2024; Kang et al., 2025; Yuan et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025a)，但注意力程度并不能证明必要性。关注度低可能意味着不再使用持续访问，或者访问失败；仅从注意力值来看，两者是无法区分的。因此，我们通过干预而不是通过解读注意力来测试必要性。, but attention magnitude does not establish necessity. Low attention can mean that continued access is no longer used, or that access is failing; the two are indistinguishable from attention values alone. We therefore test necessity by intervention rather than by reading off attention.

为了回答这个问题，我们引入To answer this question, we introduce the视觉访问边界Visual Access Boundary（VAB）。我们通过跨层深度和生成时间屏蔽从生成令牌查询到图像令牌键的直接注意力进行干预（图(VAB). We intervene by masking direct attention from generated-token queries to image-token keys across layer depth and generation time (Figure1(一))。我们称之为两轴干预(a)). We call this two-axis intervention视觉访问扫描Visual Access Sweep。这种干预将一个观察性问题变成了一个因果问题。如果在删除稍后生成的令牌对图像令牌的访问后保留了性能，则目标行为不需要这些直接的视觉访问。相反，当 CoT 提供帮助时，它似乎对隐藏状态中已经携带的图像衍生信息进行操作。. This intervention turns an otherwise observational question into a causal one. If performance is preserved after removing later generated-token access to image tokens, then those direct visual accesses are not necessary for the target behavior. Instead, when CoT helps, it appears to operate over image-derived information already carried in hidden states.

为了将视觉基础与下游推理隔离开来，我们设计了受控属性计数任务，其中对象属性和空间布局完全参数化，遵循受控视觉推理基准的精神。这避免了模型可以根据参数知识或数据集偏差而不是图像本身来回答的情况，同时保持推理操作固定为计数和仅改变查询的视觉属性。我们将无 CoT 直接回答（我们称为直接）与 CoT 提示进行比较。我们使用 GQA 派生的真实图像是/否任务进一步测试相同的结构是否出现在合成图像之外To isolate visual grounding from downstream reasoning, we design controlled attribute-counting tasks in which object attributes and spatial layouts are fully parameterized, following the spirit of controlled visual reasoning benchmarks. This avoids cases where a model can answer from parametric knowledge or dataset bias rather than from the image itself, while keeping the reasoning operation fixed as counting and varying only the queried visual attribute. We compare no-CoT direct answering, which we refer to as Direct, with CoT prompting. We further test whether the same structure appears beyond synthetic images using a GQA-derived real-image yes/no task(Hudson and Manning, 2019).

In every main model-task setting we test, accuracy is preserved while a broad upper-layer region is blocked, then drops sharply once the intervention reaches earlier layers. Taken together, these results adjudicate between the two mechanisms above. In Direct answering, there is a depth after which generated tokens no longer need direct attention to image tokens. Under CoT, generation becomes much longer, but the necessary visual-access region does not expand proportionally along the generation-time axis. Thus, in the regimes we test, CoT does not primarily improve performance by continuing to re-read the image throughout the reasoning trace. Instead, when CoT helps, it appears to operate over image-derived information already carried in hidden states. The same qualitative VAB structure also appears on the GQA-derived real-image task. Second, on Qwen2.5-VL across three scales, per-attribute CoT gains are predicted by multi-object perceptual readout accuracy at 3B and 7B and saturate at 32B. An oracle bypass that supplies the attribute symbolically restores CoT gains uniformly across attributes, and a probe-vs-decode gap on hard attributes provides separate single-object evidence that the bottleneck sits at perceptual readout rather than the counting operation itself (Section6).

我们的贡献如下。 (i) 我们引入了视觉访问扫描和视觉访问边界，这是一种因果干预和一种操作措施，它们共同映射了沿层深度和生成时间直接图像令牌访问的功能必要性。 (ii) 我们表明，在主要的 Qwen2.5-VL 和 InternVL3 设置中，有限的 VAB 出现在 Direct 和 CoT 提示下。在较大的模型中，当针对 no-CoT 完全访问目标进行评估时，CoT VAB 层仍保留在 no-CoT 边界的两层内，尽管代数更长。相同的定性 VAB 结构也出现在 GQA 派生的真实图像是/否任务中。 (iii) 在 Qwen2.5-VL 上，每属性 CoT 增益在三个尺度上跟踪多对象感知读出精度，并通过同一检查点上的预言机旁路统一恢复，无需图像输入。对硬属性的单独单对象探测与解码诊断提供了支持证据，证明瓶颈在于感知读出而不是计数操作本身。Our contributions are as follows. (i) We introduce Visual Access Sweep and the Visual Access Boundary, a causal intervention and an operational measure that together map the functional necessity of direct image-token access along both layer depth and generation time. (ii) We show that finite VABs appear under both Direct and CoT prompting across the main Qwen2.5-VL and InternVL3 settings. 2 相关工作Related Work

VLM 中的视觉注意力衰减。Visual attention decay in VLMs.

越来越多的工作表明，VLM 对图像标记的注意力通常会在生成过程中减弱、变得嘈杂或分配不当，并且有几种方法通过在推理过程中加强、重新分配或重新注入视觉信号来提高性能A growing body of work shows that VLM attention to image tokens often weakens, becomes noisy, or is misallocated during generation, and several methods improve performance by strengthening, redistributing, or re-injecting visual signals during inference(Tong et al., 2024; Rahmanzadehgervi et al., 2024; Kang et al., 2025; Yuan et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025a)。这些研究表明，改变视觉注意力可能是有益的。我们的问题是互补的。在未经修改的基于前缀的纯解码器 VLM 中，在生成过程中何时需要持续的直接图像令牌访问功能？ Visual Access Sweep 不是增强视觉注意力，而是沿着层深度和生成时间删除对图像令牌的生成令牌访问，从而测试注意力放大的必要性而不是效用。. These studies establish that modifying visual attention can be beneficial. Our question is complementary. In an unmodified prefix-based decoder-only VLM, when is continued direct image-token access functionally necessary during generation? Rather than enhancing visual attention, Visual Access Sweep removes generated-token access to image tokens along layer depth and generation time, thereby testing necessity rather than utility of attention amplification.

VLM 中的 CoT 和测试时间缩放。CoT and test-time scaling in VLMs.

CoT 和测试时间缩放在法学硕士中得到了很好的确立CoT and test-time scaling are well established in LLMs(Wei et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2025a)并已扩展到 VLM，一些研究报告了多模态推理的改进and have been extended to VLMs, with some studies reporting improved multimodal reasoning(Zhang et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2025; Lin et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025)以及其他在感知密集的环境中表现出有限或退化的收益and others showing limited or degraded gains in perception-heavy settings(Lu et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024b)。我们不是寻找最好的提示秘诀，而是问当 CoT 确实有帮助时，要扩展什么。因为生成的基本原理不需要忠实地揭示产生答案的计算. Rather than searching for the best prompting recipe, we ask what is being extended when CoT does help. Because generated rationales need not faithfully reveal the computation that produced the answer(Turpin et al., 2023; Lanham et al., 2023)，我们不会从 CoT 文本本身推断视觉用途；相反，我们通过干预来测试图像令牌访问的功能必要性。, we do not infer visual use from the CoT text itself; we instead test the functional necessity of image-token access by intervention.

主动感知和视觉重新访问。Active perception and visual re-access.

最近的几种方法通过允许模型在推理过程中获取额外的视觉证据来修改 VLM 推理，例如通过视觉记忆、区域重新聚焦或显式感知动作Several recent approaches modify VLM inference by allowing the model to acquire additional visual evidence during reasoning, for example through visual memory, region refocusing, or explicit perception actions(Zou et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025b; Yu et al., 2025)。这些方法研究如何通过添加感知推理反馈循环来改进模型。我们的工作提出了一个补充性的诊断问题。我们测试普通 CoT 是否已经从生成的令牌持续直接访问基于前缀的仅解码器 VLM 中功能上必需的初始图像令牌前缀。. These methods study how to improve models by adding a perception-reasoning feedback loop. Our work asks a complementary diagnostic question. We test whether ordinary CoT already makes continued direct access from generated tokens to the initial image-token prefix functionally necessary in prefix-based decoder-only VLMs.

VLM 中的机械解释和探测。Mechanistic interpretability and probing in VLMs.

关于注意力或激活的因果干预方法Causal-intervention methods on attention or activations(Vig et al., 2020; Geiger et al., 2021; Meng et al., 2022; Schwettmann et al., 2023; Gandelsman et al., 2024)探索性研究表明 VLM 隐藏状态编码模型无法用语言表达的视觉信息and probing studies showing VLM hidden states encode visual information the model fails to verbalize(Alain and Bengio, 2016; Belrose et al., 2023; Geva et al., 2023; Fu et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025b)两者都为我们的方法提供了信息。与调谐透镜式观察解码不同，VAB 通过因果屏蔽进行测量，其中直接图像令牌访问停止both inform our approach. Unlike tuned-lens-style observational decoding, the VAB measures via causal masking where direct image-token access stops being必要的necessary，我们通过预言机旁路将探测与解码差距与 CoT 增益联系起来，从而超越了一般的读出瓶颈观察。, and we go beyond generic readout-bottleneck observations by relating the probe-vs-decode gap to CoT gain through oracle bypass.

7 讨论Discussion

解释 VAB。Interpreting the VAB.

这项工作提供了当 VLM 生成更长的 CoT 跟踪时扩展内容的因果测试。在此处测试的制度中，视觉访问扫描揭示了直接和 CoT 提示下的有限视觉访问边界。扩展生成不需要在整个推理跟踪中持续直接访问图像令牌。在操作上，VAB 会标记何时从生成的令牌查询到图像令牌密钥的额外直接关注在功能上变得不必要。这并不意味着模型中不存在视觉证据。这些证据可能已经被转化为隐藏状态表示并通过残余流进行传递。这重新定义了视觉注意力衰退和视觉遗忘。关键问题不是对图像标记的关注是否减少，而是该任务是否仍然需要稍后的直接访问。This work provides a causal test of what is extended when VLMs generate longer CoT traces. Across the regimes tested here, Visual Access Sweep reveals a finite Visual Access Boundary under both Direct and CoT prompting. Extended generation does not require continued direct image-token access throughout the reasoning trace. Operationally, the VAB marks when additional direct attention from generated-token queries to image-token keys becomes functionally unnecessary. It does not imply that visual evidence is absent from the model. Such evidence may already have been transformed into hidden-state representations and carried forward through the residual stream. This reframes visual-attention decay and visual forgetting. The key question is not whether attention to image tokens decreases, but whether later direct access is still necessary for the task.

对 CoT 和主动感知的影响。Implications for CoT and active perception.

感知读出结果表明，当所需的视觉属性首先以模型推理轨迹可以使用的形式提供时，CoT 会有所帮助。当读出成功时，更长的推理可以改善下游的计数操作。当读出失败时，即使可能存在潜在的视觉信息，CoT 也无法可靠地恢复丢失的符号。这给出了测试时间缩放的实用规则：在将令牌花费在更长的推理上之前，测试是否可以可靠地读出相关的视觉属性。在 Qwen2.5-VL 中，廉价的多对象读出探针支持此类路由，以低得多的令牌成本保留大部分始终 CoT 精度（附录The perceptual-readout results suggest that CoT helps when the needed visual attribute has first been made available in a form the model’s reasoning trace can use. When readout succeeds, longer reasoning can improve the downstream counting operation. When readout fails, CoT does not reliably recover the missing symbol, even if latent visual information may be present. This gives a practical rule for test-time scaling: before spending tokens on longer reasoning, test whether the relevant visual attribute can be read out reliably. Within Qwen2.5-VL, a cheap multi-object readout probe supports such routing, preserving most of the always-CoT accuracy at substantially lower token cost (AppendixE）。同样的结果阐明了 MemVR 等主动感知方法的作用). The same result clarifies the role of active perception methods such as MemVR(Zou et al., 2025), 回顾, Look-Back(Yang et al., 2025b)，和视觉感知令牌, and Visual Perception Token(Yu et al., 2025)。这些方法是互补的：测试体系中的普通 CoT 本身不会关闭感知推理循环，而主动感知方法明确尝试获取以推理状态为条件的新视觉证据。. These methods are complementary: ordinary CoT in the tested regime does not by itself close the perception-reasoning loop, whereas active perception methods explicitly try to acquire new visual evidence conditioned on the reasoning state.

范围和限制。Scope and limitations.

本文中的证据涵盖了跨家庭和尺度的受控属性计数任务、GQA 派生的真实图像设置以及额外的交叉注意融合设置（附录The evidence in this paper covers controlled attribute-counting tasks across families and scales, a GQA-derived real-image setting, and an additional cross-attention fusion setting (AppendixC.5）。故意设计为需要迭代目视检查的任务仍在当前评估之外。扫描结果本身也不能识别产生边界的内部机制。可能会出现相同的 VAB 模式，因为视觉属性早期绑定到隐藏状态表示中，因为后期层通过残余流接收足够的图像派生信息，或者因为 CoT 提示将视觉提取前置到早期生成的标记中。视觉访问扫描隔离了一个通道的必要性（直接生成令牌到图像令牌注意力），因此区分这些帐户将需要对残余流和感知提取的时间分布进行补充干预。). Tasks deliberately designed to require iterative visual inspection remain outside the current evaluation. The sweep result also does not by itself identify the internal mechanism that produces the boundary. The same VAB pattern could arise because visual attributes are bound into hidden-state representations early, because late layers receive sufficient image-derived information through the residual stream, or because CoT prompting front-loads visual extraction into early generated tokens. Visual Access Sweep isolates the necessity of one channel (direct generated-token-to-image-token attention), so distinguishing these accounts will require complementary interventions on the residual stream and on the temporal distribution of perceptual extraction.
