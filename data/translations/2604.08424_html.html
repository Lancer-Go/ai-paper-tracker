摘要Abstract

航天器日益增强的自主性要求故障检测系统既可靠又可解释。 这项工作地址The increasing autonomy of spacecraft demands fault-detection systems that are both reliable and explainable. This work addresses可解释的人工智能eXplainable Artificial Intelligence船上用for onboard故障检测、隔离和恢复Fault Detection, Isolation and Recovery内within the姿态和轨道控制子系统Attitude and Orbit Control Subsystem通过引入一个增强神经异常检测器可解释性的框架。 我们提出了一种从中间神经激活中导出低维、语义注释编码的方法，称为窥视孔。 该框架应用于卷积自动编码器，产生可解释的指示器，能够识别和定位反应轮遥测中的异常情况。 窥视孔分析进一步揭示偏差检测并支持故障定位。 所提出的框架能够对检测到的异常进行语义表征，同时只需要少量增加计算资源，从而支持其机载部署的可行性。by introducing a framework that enhances interpretability in neural anomaly detectors. We propose a method to derive low-dimensional, semantically annotated encodings from intermediate neural activations, called peepholes. Applied to a convolutional autoencoder, the framework produces interpretable indicators that enable the identification and localization of anomalies in reaction-wheel telemetry. Peepholes analysis further reveals bias detection and supports fault localization. The proposed framework enables the semantic characterization of detected anomalies while requiring only a marginal increase in computational resources, thus supporting its feasibility for on-board deployment.

4 结论Conclusion

这项工作介绍了一个用于自主航天器中可解释的机载异常检测的框架，该框架基于从神经激活派生的低维、语义注释的编码向量，名为This work introduces a framework for explainable onboard anomaly detection in autonomous spacecraft, based on low-dimensional, semantically annotated encoding vectors derived from neural activations, named窥视孔peephole。该方法应用于经过真实反应轮遥测训练的卷积自动编码器，可以对检测到的异常进行可解释的洞察。. Applied to a convolutional autoencoder trained on real reaction wheel telemetry, the approach enables interpretable insights into the detected anomalies.

的The窥视孔peephole允许直接从潜在表示中识别异常的类型和起源，无需额外的神经分类器，从而保持透明度和较低的计算成本。初步结果还揭示了其进行偏差检查的潜力，强调了检测行为如何因反应轮和异常类型而异。allows the identification of the type and origin of anomalies directly from the latent representation, without additional neural classifiers, thus maintaining transparency and low computational cost. Preliminary results also revealed its potential for bias inspection, highlighting how detection behavior may vary across reaction wheels and anomaly types.
