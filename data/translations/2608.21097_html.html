当信任遇到真相：作为法官的法学硕士的信任与真相可分离性When Trust Meets Truth: Trust–Truth Separability in LLM-as-Judge

摘要Abstract

法学硕士作为法官系统可以产生多维度的评估，例如可信度、可靠性和事实性，并且这些输出通常被解释为独立证据。 我们用一对常见的判断来测试这个假设：LLM-as-Judge systems can produce multi-dimensional evaluations, such as trustworthiness, reliability, and factuality, and these outputs are often interpreted as independent evidence. We test this assumption for a common pair of judgments:信任评分trust scoring和and二元真值分类binary truth classification。 在正确性控制的质量保证方面，法学硕士法官比人类行为参考更紧密地将信任分数与真实判决联系起来，这表明信任和真实判断之间的分离较弱。 然后，我们通过仅更改人类和人工智能之间相同 QA 的源线索来应用压力测试。 来源归因不仅会改变信任分数，还会改变真实判决和 Logit 导出的正确侧概率。 结果表明，当前的法学硕士作为法官协议不应将信任分数视为真实判断的独立证据。. On correctness-controlled QA, LLM judges align trust scores with truth verdicts more tightly than human behavioral reference, suggesting weaker separations between trust and truth judgment. We then apply stress tests by changing only source cues of identical QA between Human and AI. Source attribution shifts not only trust scores but also truth verdicts and logit-derived correct-side probabilities. Results show that current LLM-as-Judge protocols should not treat trust scores as independent evidence for truth judgments.11 1用于再现性的材料：Materials for reproducibility:anonymous repository.

1 简介Introduction

LLM-as-Judge 已成为评估信息、产生多维判断（例如可信度、可靠性和流畅性）的实用协议。 这些判断被解释为关于质量不同方面的独立证据LLM-as-Judge has become a practical protocol for evaluating information, producing multi-dimensional judgments, such as trustworthiness, reliability, and fluency. These judgments are interpreted as independent evidence about different facets of qualityLi et al. 2024; Gu et al. 2025; Johnson et al. 2015。 这种解释假设法官能够将基本判断充分分开。 我们检查信息评估中常见对的假设：. This interpretation assumes that judges can keep the underlying judgments sufficiently separate. We examine the assumption for a common pair in information evaluation: a持续信任评分continuous trust score和一个and a二元真值判断binary truth judgment.

信任和真理是不同的评价，应该加以区分。 真实性判决询问内容是否事实上正确。 信任分数反映信息是否值得信赖，这可能涉及感知的可靠性、客观性或来源可信度。 预计它们之间会保持一致Trust and truth are different evaluations and should be distinguishable. A truth verdict asks whether the content is factually correct. A trust score reflects whether information is trustworthy, which may involve perceived reliability, objectivity, or source credibility. Alignment between them is expectedLi et al. 2025b。 然而，信息可能是正确的，但需要谨慎，或者看似可信，但可能是错误的. However, information can be correct yet warrant caution, or appear trustworthy yet be wrongLiao and Sundar 2022。 因此，问题在于与信任相关的因素（例如来源）是否可以改变事实真相的判断。. The problem is therefore whether trust-relevant factors (e.g., source) can shift factual truth judgments.

这种区别很重要，因为来源可能会以合理、有偏见或继承自人类偏好或数据的方式影响信任，而信任和正确性是纠缠在一起的This distinction is important because sources may affect trust in ways that are plausible, biased, or inherited from human preference or data, where trust and correctness are entangledMarecos et al. 2024; Bates et al. 2006。 然而，当信息以不同的来源线索显示但基于事实时，二元真相判决应该保持稳定。 受先前研究的启发，法学硕士表现出与人类相似的模式. However, a binary truth verdict should remain stable when the information is shown with different source cues but grounded in factuality. Inspired by prior work that LLMs demonstrate similar patterns as humansXie et al. 2024; Chen et al. 2024，我们首先对LLM信任分数和二元真值判断是否与人类类似地对齐进行初步分析。 人类和法学硕士判断正确性控制的 QA，以人类作为行为基线参考。 我们发现，人类对正确答案给予更高的信任，但他们的真理决策并没有朝着同一个方向一致；法学硕士表现出更紧密的信任与真相耦合。 因此，我们研究了 LLM-as-Judge 中的信任与真实可分离性：, we first conduct a preliminary analysis on whether LLM trust scores and binary truth judgments are aligned similarly to humans. Humans and LLMs judge correctness-controlled QA, with humans serving as a behavioral baseline reference. We find that humans give higher trust to correct answers, but their truth decisions do not align in the same direction; LLMs show tighter trust–truth coupling. We thus investigate the trust-truth separability in LLM-as-Judge:

要求：RQ:法学硕士作为法官能否在内容不变的源扰动下将信任评分与二元事实真相判断分开？Can LLM-as-a-Judge disentangle trust scoring from the binary factual truth judgment under the content-invariant source perturbations?

我们使用来源归因对法学硕士法官进行压力测试，这是一种可能影响法学硕士判断的出处线索We run stress tests on LLM judges using source attribution, a provenance cue that can affect LLM’s judgmentYe et al. 2024; Sun et al. 2026。 对于每个项目，我们保持 QA 内容固定，并且仅更改答案是否归因于人类或人工智能来源。 这测试了源线索是否仅改变信任分数或也泄漏到二元真相判决中。. For each item, we keep QA content fixed and change only whether the answer is attributed to a Human or AI source. This tests whether source cues shift only trust scores or also leak into binary truth verdicts.

我们发现 (1) 法学硕士法官比人类推荐人表现出更强的信任-真相关联。 (2) 人力资源提高了信任评分We find that (1) LLM judges show stronger trust–truth association than human references. Trust scores should therefore not be treated as independent evidence unless truth judgments remain stable under such perturbations.

4 讨论Discussion

我们的主张并不是信任和真理仅仅相关，而是它们并不可靠Our claim is not that trust and truth are merely correlated, but that they are not reliably可分离的separable在法学硕士法官中。 初步分析表明，法学硕士法官比人类更紧密地将信任与真实判决结合起来。 RQ 提供更清晰的测试：源线索会影响对相同 QA 的信任和真实性判断。 这些结果表明，法学硕士法官可能会模糊基于内容的真实性评估和类似信任的上下文评估之间的界限。in LLM judges. Preliminary analysis shows that LLM judges align trust with truth verdicts more tightly than humans. RQ provides sharper tests: source cues affect both trust and truth judgments on identical QA. These results suggest that LLM judges may blur boundary between content-based truth assessment and trust-like contextual evaluation.

这个边界很重要，因为信任和真相在信息评估中扮演着不同的角色。 先前关于人类判断的研究将可信度和质量等措施视为相关但可分离的组成部分This boundary matters because trust and truth play different roles in information evaluation. Prior work on human judgment treats measures such as trustworthiness and quality as related but separable componentsLiao and Sundar 2022; Yin et al. 2024; Jakesch et al. 2019; Reis et al. 2024。 对于法学硕士法官来说，我们的结果表明，改变信任的线索也会改变相同内容是否正确。 因此，问题不仅仅是信任评级中的来源敏感性，而是信任相关上下文泄漏到事实评估中。. For LLM-as-a-Judge, our results show that cues that change trust can also shift whether identical content is correct. The problem is therefore not source sensitivity in trust ratings alone, but leakage of trust-relevant context into factual evaluation.

这会产生直接后果。 法学硕士越来越多地被用作基准测试和数据注释的评估者This has direct consequences. LLMs are increasingly used as evaluators for benchmarking and data annotation(Li et al. 2024; Gu et al. 2025; Li et al. 2025a)。 这些管道通常要求法官输出多个字段，例如正确性、有用性，并将字段之间的一致性视为更有力的证据。 我们警告说，此类协议可能部分是循环的：法官可能会跨多个标签重新表达一项相关评估，而不是提供独立的衡量标准。. These pipelines often ask the judge to output multiple fields, such as correctness, helpfulness, and treat agreement among fields as stronger evidence. We caution that such agreements can be partly circular: the judge may be re-expressing one dependent assessment across multiple labels rather than providing independent measurements.

实践课是The practical lesson is the可分离性separability，不仅仅是准确性。 平均而言，法官可能是准确的，但仍然无法保持判断领域的清晰和有意义。 因此，信任评级不应被视为对真相判断的独立支持，除非在与信任相关但内容不变的线索发生变化时事实判断保持稳定。, not only accuracy. A judge can be accurate on average while still failing to keep judgment fields distinct and meaningful. Thus, trust ratings should not be treated as independent support for truth judgments unless factual verdicts remain stable when trust-relevant but content-invariant cues are varied.

5 相关工作Related Work

越来越多地使用法学硕士作为法官协议LLM-as-a-Judge protocols are increasingly usedLiu et al. 2023; Li et al. 2024; Gu et al. 2025; Zheng et al. 2023。 先前的研究表明，法学硕士作为法官不可靠、不一致，并且对非内容偏见（例如语气、来源和立场）敏感. Prior work has indicated that LLM-as-Judge is unreliable, inconsistent, and sensitive to non-content bias such as tone, source, and positionYe et al. 2024; Chen et al. 2024; Li et al. 2025c; Schroeder and Wood-Doughty 2025; Wataoka et al. 2025; Shi et al. 2025; Sun et al. 2026; Marioriyad et al. 2025。 因此，我们研究了多领域法官的一个相关但未经测试的风险：信任分数和真实判决可能会一起变化，而不是作为独立的证据。. We thus study a related but under-tested risk in multi-field judges: trust scores and truth verdicts may move together rather than serving as independent evidence.

这种风险对于与信任和真相相关的评估很重要。 先前的工作要求对 LLM 判决进行独立验证This risk is important to trust- and truth-related evaluation. Prior work calls for independent validation of LLM judgmentsWang et al. 2025; Schroeder and Wood-Doughty 2025。 我们通过测试法学硕士是否将源敏感的信任评分与基于内容的真实分类分开来判断，从而将这一问题付诸实践。 来源框架可能会改变感知的信任；但是，它不应改变事实正确性的判决。. We operationalize this concern by testing whether LLM judges separate source-sensitive trust scoring from content-grounded truth classification. Source framing may change perceived trust; however, it should not change the factual correctness verdict.

6 结论Conclusion

信任和真相旨在捕捉判断的不同方面，但我们的结果表明，目前的法学硕士法官在实践中经常推翻它们。 信任在源线索压力测试下密切跟踪真实判断。 因此，默认情况下，多维法学硕士作为法官的输出并不是独立证据。 在将它们用于可靠评估之前，必须根据经验验证它们的可分离性。Trust and truth are meant to capture different aspects of judgment, but our results show that current LLM judges often collapse them in practice. Trust tracks truth judgments closely under source-cue stress tests. Thus, multi-dimensional LLM-as-Judge outputs are not independent evidence by default. Their separability must be empirically verified before they are used for reliable evaluations.
