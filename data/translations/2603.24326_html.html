摘要Abstract

文档解析是一项细粒度的任务，其中图像分辨率会显着影响性能。虽然利用视觉语言模型的高级研究受益于高分辨率输入来提高模型性能，但这通常会导致视觉标记数量呈二次方增加，并显着提高计算成本。我们将这种低效率归因于文档图像中大量视觉区域冗余，例如背景。 为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 PaddleOCR-VL，这是一种新颖的从粗到细的架构，它专注于语义相关的区域，同时抑制冗余区域，从而提高效率和性能。具体来说，我们引入了一个轻量级的有效区域聚焦模块（VRFM），它利用本地化和上下文关系预测功能来识别有效的视觉标记。随后，我们设计并训练了一个紧凑而强大的 0.9B 视觉语言模型（PaddleOCR-VL-0.9B）来执行详细识别，由 VRFM 输出引导，以避免直接处理整个大图像。大量实验表明，PaddleOCR-VL 在页面级解析和元素级识别方面均实现了最先进的性能。它的性能显着优于现有解决方案，与顶级 VLM 相比具有强大的竞争力，并在使用更少的视觉标记和参数的同时提供快速推理，突出了有针对性的从粗到精解析的有效性，以实现准确高效的文档理解。 源代码和模型可公开获取Document parsing is a fine-grained task where image resolution significantly impacts performance. While advanced research leveraging vision-language models benefits from high-resolution input to boost model performance, this often leads to a quadratic increase in the number of vision tokens and significantly raises computational costs. We attribute this inefficiency to substantial visual regions redundancy in document images, like background. To tackle this, we propose PaddleOCR-VL, a novel coarse-to-fine architecture that focuses on semantically relevant regions while suppressing redundant ones, thereby improving both efficiency and performance. Specifically, we introduce a lightweight Valid Region Focus Module (VRFM) which leverages localization and contextual relationship prediction capabilities to identify valid vision tokens. Subsequently, we design and train a compact yet powerful 0.9B vision-language model (PaddleOCR-VL-0.9B) to perform detailed recognition, guided by VRFM outputs to avoid direct processing of the entire large image. Extensive experiments demonstrate that PaddleOCR-VL achieves state-of-the-art performance in both page-level parsing and element-level recognition. It significantly outperforms existing solutions, exhibits strong competitiveness against top-tier VLMs, and delivers fast inference while utilizing substantially fewer vision tokens and parameters, highlighting the effectiveness of targeted coarse-to-fine parsing for accurate and efficient document understanding. The source code and models are publicly available athttps://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR.

1 简介Introduction

文档作为核心信息载体，其复杂性和体量呈指数级增长，使得文档解析成为不可或缺的关键技术。文档解析的主要目标Documents serve as core information carriers, with their complexity and volume growing at an exponential rate, making document parsing an indispensable key technology. The primary goal of document parsing[23, 39, 13, 27]是为了能够深入理解文档的结构和语义。具体来说，它涉及识别文档元素（文本、公式、表格、图表、图像等）并确定正确的阅读顺序。文档解析在构建大型语言模型 (LLM) 训练语料库中发挥着至关重要的作用is to enable deep structural and semantic understanding of documents. Specifically, it involves recognizing the document elements (text, formulas, tables, charts, images, etc.) and determining the correct reading order. Document parsing plays a crucial role in constructing training corpora for large language models (LLMs)[4, 56, 1]以及 RAG（检索增强生成）等应用程序and applications like RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)[22]，使其准确性和效率成为主要研究重点。, making its accuracy and efficiency a major research focus.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 基于管道的方法Pipeline-based Methods

早期的作品主要遵循管道范式Early works predominantly followed a pipeline paradigm[9, 52]，其中文档解析被分解为几个连续的阶段。该过程通常从布局检测开始，识别布局元素（例如文本块、图形和表格）的空间边界。随后，一系列专门的专家模型被应用于这些检测到的区域，以执行文本识别、表格结构重建和数学公式识别等任务。尽管这种模块化设计能够实现特定于任务的优化，但它需要多个独立的模型，并导致整个系统复杂而繁琐。, in which document parsing was decomposed into several sequential stages. The process typically began with layout detection, identifying the spatial boundaries of layout elements such as text blocks, figures, and tables. Subsequently, a series of specialized expert models were applied to these detected regions to perform tasks like text recognition, table structure reconstruction, and mathematical formula recognition. Although this modular design enabled task-specific optimization, it required multiple independent models and resulted in a complex and cumbersome overall system.

2.2 通用 VLM 方法General VLM Methods

通用视觉语言模型的最新进展为文档理解开辟了新的可能性Recent progress in general-purpose vision-language models has opened up new possibilities for document understanding[3, 16, 26]。这些模型通常在大规模图像文本数据集上进行预训练，使它们能够在统一框架内捕获视觉和语言信号之间的跨模式对应关系。当应用于文档图像时，它们可以解释布局和文本语义，而无需依赖显式 OCR 或手工制作的布局模块。这允许跨不同任务（例如字幕、视觉问答，甚至形式理解）进行端到端处理和灵活适应。. These models are typically pretrained on large-scale image–text datasets, enabling them to capture cross-modal correspondences between visual and linguistic signals within a unified framework. When applied to document images, they can interpret both layout and textual semantics without relying on explicit OCR or handcrafted layout modules. This allows for end-to-end processing and flexible adaptation across diverse tasks such as captioning, visual question answering, and even form understanding.

专门的视觉语言模型被提出来解决通用 VLM 在文档解析中的局限性Specialized Vision–Language Models have been proposed to address the limitations of general-purpose VLMs in document parsing[39, 13, 46, 12, 55, 23]。其中，MinerU2.5. Among them, MinerU2.5[39]和海豚and Dolphin[13]采用统一的多模态架构，联合执行检测和识别，确保跨阶段的语义一致性，并提高类别泛化和对新元素类型的可扩展性。然而，由于它们的检测依赖于生成式或基于接地的多模态输出，因此预测的坐标可能会在排列密集或冗长的文档中漂移，从而导致错误传播和识别精度下降。其他型号如MinerU2-VLMemploy unified multimodal architectures that jointly perform detection and recognition, ensuring semantic consistency across stages and improving category generalization and extensibility to new element types. 3 方法Method

3.1 PaddleOCR-VL

如图As shown in Figure2，文档图像中的冗余视觉区域会导致端到端解析模型生成过多的视觉标记，从而导致计算成本大幅增加。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 PaddleOCR-VL，一个从粗到细的文档解析框架，它将布局分析与元素识别分离。, redundant visual regions in document images cause end-to-end parsing models to generate excessive vision tokens, leading to substantially increased computational cost. To address this issue, we propose PaddleOCR-VL, a coarse-to-fine document parsing framework that decouples layout analysis from element recognition.

如图所示As illustrated in Figure3，PaddleOCR-VL由两个阶段组成。在粗略阶段，轻量级有效区域聚焦模块 (VRFM) 识别信息丰富的文档区域、预测其类别并估计其阅读顺序。在精细阶段，将裁剪后的有效区域输入PaddleOCR-VL-0.9B中进行元素级识别。然后根据预测的阅读顺序合并识别的输出以重建最终的结构化文档。, PaddleOCR-VL consists of two stages. In the coarse stage, a lightweight Valid Region Focus Module (VRFM) identifies informative document regions, predicts their categories, and estimates their reading order. In the fine stage, the cropped valid regions are fed into PaddleOCR-VL-0.9B for element-level recognition. The recognized outputs are then merged according to the predicted reading order to reconstruct the final structured document.

这种设计有两个优点。首先，它避免处理大量不相关的背景，因此减少了传递给视觉语言模型的视觉标记的数量。其次，通过将区域定位与内容识别分开，每个模块可以专注于自己的子任务，从而提高效率和更强的识别性能。 VRFM 和 PaddleOCR-VL-0.9B 的详细信息分别在第 3.1.1 和 3.1.2 节中介绍。This design offers two advantages. First, it avoids processing large irrelevant backgrounds and therefore reduces the number of visual tokens passed to the vision-language model. Second, by separating region localization from content recognition, each module can specialize in its own subtask, leading to better efficiency and stronger recognition performance. The details of VRFM and PaddleOCR-VL-0.9B are introduced in Sections 3.1.1 and 3.1.2, respectively.
