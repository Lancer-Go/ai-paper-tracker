摘要Abstract

每天，用户都会生成数字痕迹（例如社交媒体帖子、聊天和在线互动），这些痕迹本身带有时间戳，可能反映他们心理状态的各个方面。这些痕迹可以组织成时间轨迹，捕捉用户的心理健康信号如何演变，包括改善、恶化或稳定的阶段。 在这项工作中，我们提出了一个可解释的框架，用于检测和分析用户数字痕迹中与抑郁相关的状态变化。该方法结合了多个基于 BERT 的模型来提取不同维度（例如情绪、情绪和抑郁严重程度）的互补信号。然后，这些信号随着时间的推移进行聚合，以构建用户级轨迹，并对其进行分析以识别有意义的变化点。 为了增强可解释性，该框架集成了一个大型语言模型，以生成简洁且人类可读的报告，描述心理健康信号的演变并突出关键转变。 我们在两个社交媒体数据集上评估该框架。结果表明，与基于法学硕士的直接报告相比，该方法产生的摘要更加连贯、信息丰富，从而实现了更高的用户历史覆盖率、更强的时间连贯性以及对变化点的更高敏感性。消融研究证实了每个组件的贡献，特别是时间建模和分割。总体而言，该方法提供了随时间变化的心理健康信号的可解释视图，支持研究和决策，而不以临床诊断为目标。Every day, users generate digital traces (e.g., social media posts, chats, and online interactions) that are inherently timestamped and may reflect aspects of their mental state. These traces can be organized into temporal trajectories that capture how a user’s mental health signals evolve, including phases of improvement, deterioration, or stability. In this work, we propose an explainable framework for detecting and analyzing depression-related status shifts in user digital traces. The approach combines multiple BERT-based models to extract complementary signals across different dimensions (e.g., sentiment, emotion, and depression severity). Such signals are then aggregated over time to construct user-level trajectories that are analyzed to identify meaningful change points. To enhance interpretability, the framework integrates a large language model to generate concise and human-readable reports that describe the evolution of mental-health signals and highlight key transitions. We evaluate the framework on two social media datasets. Results show that the approach produces more coherent and informative summaries than direct LLM-based reporting, achieving higher coverage of user history, stronger temporal coherence, and improved sensitivity to change points. An ablation study confirms the contribution of each component, particularly temporal modeling and segmentation. Overall, the method provides an interpretable view of mental health signals over time, supporting research and decision making without aiming at clinical diagnosis.

1 简介Introduction

近年来，日常数字痕迹，包括私人通信、网络搜索、移动痕迹和社交媒体互动，已日益成为个人表达情感、观点、行为和个人经历的媒介In recent years, everyday digital traces, including private communications, web searches, mobility traces, and social media interactions, have increasingly become a medium through which individuals express emotions, opinions, behaviors, and personal experiences[7, 43, 9]。当纵向分析时，这些痕迹可能隐含地反映了一个人心理状况的各个方面，并揭示了心理健康如何随着时间的推移而演变。用户生成的内容通常不提供静态表示，而是形成时间轨迹，其中可以通过语言和行为模式出现恶化、恢复或相对稳定的阶段。. When analyzed longitudinally, these traces may implicitly reflect aspects of a person’s psychological condition and reveal how mental well-being evolves over time. Rather than providing a static representation, user-generated content often forms a temporal trajectory in which phases of deterioration, recovery, or relative stability can emerge through linguistic and behavioral patterns.

几项研究调查了如何利用社交媒体数据通过自然语言处理、机器学习和深度学习技术自动检测精神困扰指标，包括抑郁症、重度抑郁症和自杀意念Several studies have investigated the use of social media data to automatically detect indicators of mental distress, including depression, major depressive disorder, and suicidal ideation, through natural language processing, machine learning, and deep learning techniques[35, 45, 41, 38]。早期的工作表明，从 Twitter 和 Reddit 等平台提取的语言和行为信号可以支持抑郁症状的识别，而最近的方法则利用基于 Transformer 的模型和更丰富的文本表示来提高分类性能. Early work showed that linguistic and behavioral signals extracted from platforms such as Twitter and Reddit can support the recognition of depressive symptoms, while more recent approaches have exploited transformer-based models and richer textual representations to improve classification performance[30, 20]。然而，大部分文献仍然依赖于后级或用户级分类设置，而对心理健康信号如何随时间演变进行建模的关注相对较少。最近关于抑郁症分类中时间边界的研究表明，纵向分割可以为区分抑郁和控制用户提供有用的信息. However, much of this literature still relies on post-level or user-level classification settings, whereas comparatively less attention has been devoted to modeling how mental-health signals evolve over time. Recent work on temporal boundaries in depression classification suggests that longitudinal segmentation can provide useful information for distinguishing depressive and control users[42].

基于 Transformer 的架构（例如 BERT 和相关语言模型）通过捕获语言中的上下文和语义关系，显着改进了文本分析。这些模型在情感分析、情绪识别和心理健康分类等任务中取得了最先进的性能Transformer-based architectures such as BERT and related language models have significantly advanced textual analysis by capturing contextual and semantic relationships within language. These models have achieved state-of-the-art performance in tasks including sentiment analysis, emotion recognition, and mental health classification[20, 16]。然而，它们仍然通常用于独立地对帖子进行分类，而没有明确地模拟心理健康信号如何随着时间的推移在交互序列中演变。因此，当前的方法通常对用户心理状况变化背后的时间动态提供有限的洞察，并且可能无法识别何时发生有意义的心理状态变化。. However, they are still commonly applied to classify posts independently, without explicitly modeling how mental health signals evolve across sequences of interactions over time. As a consequence, current approaches often provide limited insight into the temporal dynamics underlying changes in a user’s psychological condition and may fail to identify when meaningful shifts in mental state occur.

为了解决这一限制，这项工作引入了一个可解释的框架，用于在个人数字痕迹中追踪心理健康轨迹。我们的方法不仅分析单个帖子，还分析与用户相关的整个帖子序列，对语言、情感和心理模式如何随时间演变进行建模。该框架基于多分类策略，结合了多个基于 BERT 的微调模型，每个模型专门针对特定维度（例如情绪、情绪、心理健康类别）To address this limitation, this work introduces an explainable framework for tracing mental-health trajectories in individual digital traces. Our approach analyzes not only single posts but the entire sequence of posts associated with a user, modeling how linguistic, emotional, and psychological patterns evolve over time. The framework is based on a multi-classification strategy that combines several fine-tuned BERT-based models, each specialized in a specific dimension (e.g., sentiment, emotion, mental-health category)[4]。通过整合这些互补信号，系统构建了每个帖子的多维表示。然后，时间推理模块处理带注释的帖子序列以推断用户的轨迹，明确识别轨迹显着改变方向（朝向改善或恶化）的变化点以及稳定区间。. By integrating these complementary signals, the system builds a multidimensional representation of each post. A temporal reasoning module then processes the sequence of annotated posts to infer the user’s trajectory, explicitly identifying change points where the trajectory significantly changes direction (toward improvement or deterioration), as well as intervals of stability.

可解释性是拟议框架的核心部分，因为可解释性被广泛认为对于在临床和高风险环境中采用基于人工智能的心理健康检测系统至关重要Explainability is a central part of the proposed framework, as interpretability is widely recognized as essential for the adoption of AI-based mental-health detection systems in clinical and high-stakes settings[18, 12]。每个基于 BERT 的模型都会生成捕获与用户心理状态相关的语言、情感和语义模式的信号。这些信号随着时间的推移进行聚合，以提供此类模式如何在用户时间轴上演变的全局视图。为了使临床医生和研究人员能够访问这些信息，该框架采用大型语言模型 (LLM) 将丰富的信号转换为简洁且人类可读的摘要。这些摘要描述了总体趋势并突出了关键转变，将它们与数据中观察到的重复表达、主题和行为模式联系起来。更广泛地说，这种类型的系统可以集成到支持用户和专业人员（例如临床医生、心理学家、研究人员）的工具中，通过突出心理健康相关信号的有意义的变化并促进反思和早期认识。 与主要关注代币级别的特征归因的传统可解释人工智能方法不同，我们的框架提供了. Each BERT-based model produces signals that capture linguistic, emotional, and semantic patterns associated with the user’s mental state. These signals are aggregated over time to provide a global view of how such patterns evolve across the user’s timeline. To make this information accessible to clinicians and researchers, the framework employs a large language model (LLM) to transform the enriched signals into concise and human-readable summaries. These summaries describe the overall trend and highlight key transitions, linking them to recurring expressions, themes, and behavioral patterns observed in the data. More broadly, this type of system could be integrated into tools that support users and professionals (e.g., clinicians, psychologists, researchers) by highlighting meaningful changes in mental-health–related signals and facilitating reflection and early awareness. Unlike traditional explainable AI approaches, which primarily focus on feature attribution at the token level, our framework provides轨迹级可解释性trajectory-level explainability，其中解释被表达为时间模式、阶段和转变的结构化摘要，直接反映心理健康信号随时间的演变。, where explanations are expressed as structured summaries of temporal patterns, phases, and transitions that directly reflect the evolution of mental-health signals over time.

在这项工作中，我们在 Reddit 和 Twitter 的两个社交媒体数据集上应用和评估我们的框架，重点关注利用在线平台作为个人空间来分享体验的用户。这使得能够构建详细的时间表来捕捉他们的行为和情绪随时间的演变。实验结果表明，与忽略时间或使用单一模型的方法相比，我们的方法明确地模拟了时间动态，在检测心理健康信号的有意义的变化方面更有效。此外，该框架通过生成结构化且人类可读的摘要来保留可解释性。 该系统不适用于临床诊断；相反，它被设计为决策支持和研究工具，提供透明的信号和可解释的摘要。这些输出必须由临床医生或研究人员等领域专家在适当的临床和伦理背景下仔细解释和验证。In this work, we apply and evaluate our framework on two social media datasets from Reddit and Twitter, focusing on users who utilize online platforms as a personal space to share experiences. This enables the construction of detailed timelines that capture the evolution of their behavior and emotions over time. Experimental results show that our approach, which explicitly models temporal dynamics, is more effective at detecting meaningful shifts in mental-health signals compared to methods that ignore time or use a single model. In addition, the framework preserves interpretability by producing structured and human-readable summaries. The system is not intended for clinical diagnosis; rather, it is designed as a decision-support and research tool that provides transparent signals and interpretable summaries. These outputs must be carefully interpreted and validated by domain experts, such as clinicians or researchers, within an appropriate clinical and ethical context.

Several studies have shown that linguistic and behavioral patterns extracted from social media platforms can provide meaningful signals associated with psychological distress[35, 45, 21, 26]。随着 Twitter、Reddit 和 Facebook 等平台上用户生成的数据越来越多，机器学习和 NLP 技术已被广泛采用来自动识别与精神困扰相关的信号. With the increasing availability of user-generated data from platforms such as Twitter, Reddit, and Facebook, machine learning and NLP techniques have been widely adopted to automatically identify signals associated with mental distress[41, 6, 38, 15].

早期的方法主要依赖于传统的机器学习算法，包括朴素贝叶斯、随机森林、支持向量机以及手工设计的语言或情感特征Early approaches mainly relied on traditional machine learning algorithms, including Naive Bayes, Random Forests, support vector machines, and handcrafted linguistic or affective features[2, 29, 5, 17]。多项研究表明，从社交媒体活动中提取的词汇、情感和行为信号可以提供有意义的抑郁症状和心理脆弱性指标. Transformer 架构的引入，特别是 BERT 及其变体，通过实现更丰富的用户生成文本的上下文和语义表示，显着推进了该领域的发展The introduction of transformer architectures, particularly BERT and its variants, significantly advanced the field by enabling richer contextual and semantic representations of user-generated text[8, 23, 44]。最近的研究表明，基于 Transformer 的模型可以有效捕捉与抑郁和精神困扰相关的微妙语言模式. Recent studies have shown that transformer-based models can effectively capture subtle linguistic patterns associated with depression and mental distress[30, 20, 19, 16]。最近的研究还探索了大语言模型（LLM）和可解释人工智能（XAI）技术的整合，以提高心理健康预测系统的可解释性. In particular, approaches based on SHAP, LIME, and natural-language explanations have been proposed to bridge the gap between model predictions and human-understandable psychological interpretations[25, 32, 13, 33, 27].

除了后级分类之外，最近的几项研究还强调了对时间动态和纵向行为模式进行建模的重要性。维拉-佩雷斯等人。Beyond post-level classification, several recent studies have emphasized the importance of modeling temporal dynamics and longitudinal behavioral patterns. 3.1 数字迹线采集Digital trace acquisition

该框架旨在摄取用户在多个数字环境中生成的异构文本数据，例如社交媒体帖子、聊天、网络搜索、成绩单、临床记录以及通过 API 或数字服务获得的数据。在这项研究中，我们评估了社交媒体痕迹上的框架，而其他来源则代表了相同基于事件的表示的可能扩展。每条信息都被视为一个The framework is designed to ingest heterogeneous textual data generated by users across multiple digital environments, such as social media posts, chats, web searches, transcripts, clinical records, and data obtained through APIs or digital services. In this study, we evaluate the framework on social media traces, while other sources represent possible extensions of the same event-based representation. Each piece of information is treated as a数字踪迹digital trace，可能编码有关用户心理状态的隐式信号。 为了保持框架的通用性，所有跟踪都被映射到统一的事件表示中。每个, potentially encoding implicit signals about the mental state of the user. To keep the framework general, all traces are mapped into a unified event representation. 5 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

这项研究表明，将心理健康信号建模为时间轨迹，并通过多维分类和主题分析进行丰富，为生成结构化和可解释的报告提供了显着的好处。然而，应该承认一些局限性，这也为未来的工作指明了方向。This study demonstrates that modeling mental-health signals as temporal trajectories, enriched with multidimensional classification and topic analysis, provides significant benefits for generating structured and interpretable reports. However, some limitations should be acknowledged, which also point to directions for future work.

首先，目前的方法主要依赖于英文数据和模型。尽管多语言扩展是可行的（例如，使用 mBERT），但由于训练数据有限和心理状态表达的文化差异，代表性不足的语言或方言的性能可能会下降。第二个限制涉及用户之间数据可用性的可变性。稀疏或不规则分布的数字轨迹可能导致不完整或有噪声的轨迹，从而降低时间建模和变化点检测的可靠性。另一个限制涉及使用大型语言模型来生成报告。虽然这些模型增强了可解释性，但它们可能会引入幻觉或过度概括，特别是当上下文证据有限或不明确时。尽管 RAG 组件可以帮助缓解这个问题，但确保事实一致性和可追溯性仍然是一个关键挑战。 从伦理角度来看，该系统旨在作为决策支持工具而不是诊断工具。然而，必须谨慎管理与隐私、数据滥用和结果误解相关的风险，尤其是在处理敏感的个人数据时。First, the current approach relies primarily on English data and models. Although multilingual extensions are feasible (e.g., using mBERT), performance may degrade for underrepresented languages or dialects due to limited training data and cultural differences in the expression of mental states. A second limitation concerns variability in data availability across users. Sparse or irregularly distributed digital traces may lead to incomplete or noisy trajectories, thereby reducing the reliability of temporal modeling and change-point detection. Another limitation involves the use of large language models for report generation. While these models enhance interpretability, they may introduce hallucinations or overgeneralizations, particularly when contextual evidence is limited or ambiguous. Although a RAG component could help mitigate this issue, ensuring factual consistency and traceability remains a critical challenge. From an ethical perspective, the system is intended as a decision-support tool rather than a diagnostic instrument. Nevertheless, risks related to privacy, data misuse, and misinterpretation of results must be carefully managed, especially when dealing with sensitive personal data.

未来的工作将解决这些局限性并探索几个方向，包括多模态数据（例如图像、音频和可穿戴信号）的集成、临床数据集的评估以及外部上下文信息（例如临床注释）的合并，以提高鲁棒性和可解释性。Future work will address these limitations and explore several directions, including the integration of multimodal data (e.g., images, audio, and wearable signals), the evaluation on clinical datasets, and the incorporation of external contextual information, such as clinical annotations, to improve robustness and interpretability.

6 结论Conclusions

在本文中，我们介绍了一个可解释的框架，用于检测和分析用户数字痕迹中与抑郁相关的状态变化。与专注于孤立帖子的传统方法不同，所提出的方法将心理健康信号建模为时间轨迹，捕捉与抑郁相关的信号如何随时间演变。该框架集成了基于 BERT 的多维分类、每日聚合和平滑、通过分段线性分割进行变化点检测以及基于 LLM 的报告生成，以生成可解释的、面向阶段的摘要。In this paper, we introduced an explainable framework for detecting and analyzing depression-related status shifts in user digital traces. Unlike traditional approaches that focus on isolated posts, the proposed method models mental-health signals as temporal trajectories, capturing how depression-related signals evolve over time. The framework integrates multidimensional BERT-based classification, daily aggregation and smoothing, change-point detection through piecewise linear segmentation, and LLM-based report generation to produce interpretable, phase-oriented summaries.

在 Reddit 和 Twitter 数据集上进行的实验评估表明，合并时间结构可以显着提高生成报告的质量。特别是，与直接基于 LLM 的摘要相比，轨迹感知摘要可以更好地覆盖用户的历史记录、更强的时间一致性以及对有意义的变化点更高的敏感性。消融研究进一步证实了管道的每个组件（特别是分段和时间建模）都有助于系统的整体有效性。The experimental evaluation, conducted on Reddit and Twitter datasets, shows that incorporating temporal structure significantly improves the quality of the generated reports. In particular, trajectory-aware summaries provide better coverage of the user’s history, stronger temporal coherence, and higher sensitivity to meaningful change points compared to direct LLM-based summarization. The ablation study further confirms that each component of the pipeline (especially segmentation and temporal modeling) contributes to the overall effectiveness of the system.

总体而言，所提出的框架为数字环境中心理健康相关信号的更透明和纵向分析迈出了一步。虽然不用于诊断目的，但它提供了行为进化的结构化和可解释的表示，可以支持研究并帮助领域专家理解复杂的时间模式。Overall, the proposed framework offers a step toward more transparent and longitudinal analysis of mental-health–related signals in digital environments. While not intended for diagnostic purposes, it provides a structured and interpretable representation of behavioral evolution that can support research and assist domain experts in understanding complex temporal patterns.
