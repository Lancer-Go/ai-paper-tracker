1 简介Introduction

对代理人工智能 (AI) 的公众讨论一直以短视能力的提示、编排和展示为主。然而，企业部署面临着不同的问题。操作工作流被设计为通过批准规则、验证检查、职责分离和异常处理逻辑来近乎确定性地运行。一旦将大型语言模型 (LLM) 驱动或代理策略插入到该控制结构中，执行就不再仅通过一步似真性来描述；它由受约束过程上的轨迹分布来描述。因此，核心问题是政策是否能够维持可靠的Public discussion of agentic artificial intelligence (AI) has been dominated by prompting, orchestration, and demonstrations of short-horizon competence. Enterprise deployment, however, confronts a different problem. Operational workflows are engineered to behave near-deterministically through approval rules, validation checks, separation of duties, and exception-handling logic. Once a large language model (LLM)-driven or agentic policy is inserted into that control structure, execution is no longer described by one-step plausibility alone; it is described by a trajectory distribution over a constrained process. The central question is therefore whether a policy can sustain reliable路径paths通过工作流程，而不仅仅是它是否可以产生本地合理的下一步行动。through a workflow, not merely whether it can produce a locally plausible next action.

这种不匹配不再是假设的。加拿大航空的客户服务聊天机器人提供了不正确的丧亲票价指导，该公司随后被发现因疏忽失实陈述而承担责任This mismatch is no longer hypothetical. Air Canada’s customer-service chatbot supplied incorrect bereavement-fare guidance, and the firm was subsequently found liable for negligent misrepresentation[1]。麦当劳在运营结果参差不齐和持续出现订购错误后结束了国际商业机器 (IBM) 语音订购试点. McDonald’s ended its International Business Machines (IBM) voice-ordering pilot after mixed operational results and persistent ordering errors[2]。在投资组合层面，Gartner 预计 2025 年超过 40% 的代理 AI 项目将在 2027 年因成本不断上升、商业价值不明确或风险控制不足而被取消. At the portfolio level, Gartner projected in 2025 that more than 40% of agentic-AI projects would be canceled by 2027 because of escalating costs, unclear business value, or inadequate risk controls[3]; IBM 报告称，只有 25% 的人工智能计划实现了预期投资回报，并且只有 16% 的计划在企业范围内进行了扩展; IBM reported that only 25% of AI initiatives had delivered expected return on investment and only 16% had scaled enterprise-wide[4]。这些观察结果表明，可靠性和成本并不是次要的部署细节。它们是同等的设计约束。. These observations suggest that reliability and cost are not secondary deployment details. They are coequal design constraints.

我们将由此产生的分析问题称为We refer to the resulting analytical problem as the随机缺口stochastic gap。在确定性企业资源规划 (ERP) 或基于规则的系统中，下一个状态实际上是当前状态的函数。在代理系统中，下一个动作是从策略中采样或选择的，并且工作流在转换内核下演变。然后，局部不确定性、稀有分支和流程循环会沿着路径累积。实际问题不在于代理人能否行动，而在于历史进程实际上可以证明多少自主权是合理的。. In a deterministic enterprise resource planning (ERP) or rule-based system, the next state is effectively a function of the current state. In an agentic system, the next action is sampled or selected from a policy and the workflow evolves under a transition kernel. Local uncertainty, rare branches, and process loops can then accumulate along the path. The practical question is not whether an agent can act, but how much autonomy the historical process can actually justify.

The framework has four parts. First, we define state blind-spot mass and state-action blind mass as finite-sample analogues of deployment mass that lies outside or near the edge of historical support. Second, we add Shannon entropy and a reproducible risk weighting to obtain a deployment-side escalation rule for human intervention. Third, we show that the same gate that induces a reliability envelope also induces an expected oversight-cost identity, thereby coupling reliability and economics at the level of workflow visitation. Fourth, we validate the theory on a genuine multi-step enterprise workflow. We use the full Business Process Intelligence Challenge 2019 (BPI 2019) purchase-to-pay log for the descriptive audit and then build a chronological held-out模拟代理simulated agent从同一过程中将理论可靠性替代物与实现的步骤和案例结果进行比较。from the same process to compare theoretical reliability surrogates against realized step and case outcomes.

由此产生的图片比“代理需要人在环（HITL）监督”的口号更加具体。它显示了哪里历史支持足够，哪里分支模糊性迫使升级，一旦明确建模下一步决策，盲目的大规模如何重新出现，以及诱导升级政策如何决定部署成本。从这个意义上说，该框架解决了目前主导企业代理采用的两个问题：系统是否可信，以及所需的人工监督是否使部署在经济上可行。The resulting picture is more specific than the slogan that “agents need human-in-the-loop (HITL) oversight.” It shows where historical support is adequate, where branching ambiguity forces escalation, how blind mass reappears once next-step decisions are modeled explicitly, and how the induced escalation policy determines the cost of deployment. In this sense, the framework addresses the two concerns that now dominate enterprise agent adoption: whether the system can be trusted, and whether required human oversight leaves deployment economically viable.

7 讨论和限制Discussion and limitations

第一个限制是 BPI 日志是观察性的而不是代理生成的。这防止了对任意行为的直接反事实评估，并且意味着所保留的代理研究是模仿式的：相对于历史轨迹中实现的下一个动作来评估正确性。这适用于此处研究的部署前问题，但它不能替代已部署系统的在线评估。A first limitation is that the BPI log is observational rather than agent-generated. This prevents direct counterfactual evaluation of arbitrary actions and means that the held-out agent study is imitation-style: correctness is assessed relative to the next action realized in the historical trace. That is appropriate for the pre-deployment question studied here, but it is not a substitute for online evaluation of a deployed system.

第二个限制涉及国家代表权。该分析在状态抽象后使用一阶马尔可夫近似。在添加的变量在语义上与部署的控制状态一致的假设下，用更长的内存、潜在工具状态、检索的文档或对话历史来丰富表示通常会扩展有效状态空间并使支持变得更困难而不是更容易。从这个校准的意义上来说，所报告的盲目大众是更丰富的代理实现所面临的支持问题的下限。然而，沿不相关维度将状态分割的错误指定的细化可能会在数字上减少或扭曲盲质量，因此下限解释是有条件的而不是自动的。A second limitation concerns state representation. The analysis uses a first-order Markov approximation after state abstraction. Under the maintained assumption that added variables are semantically aligned with the deployed control state, enriching the representation with longer memory, latent tool state, retrieved documents, or dialogue history will usually expand the effective state space and make support harder rather than easier. In that calibrated sense, the reported blind masses are lower bounds on the support problem faced by richer agent implementations. A misspecified refinement that fragments the state along irrelevant dimensions could, however, reduce or distort blind mass numerically, so the lower-bound interpretation is conditional rather than automatic.

The weightw​(s)w(s)旨在可从 BPI 日志中的公开可用字段中复制，因此强调价值强度和异常敏感活动。在实际部署中，风险功能应该是特定于领域的，并且应该更直接地包含策略关键性、合规性负担和下游错误成本。is designed to be reproducible from publicly available fields in the BPI log and therefore emphasizes value intensity and exception-sensitive activities. In a real deployment, the risk functional should be domain-specific and should incorporate policy criticality, compliance burden, and downstream error cost more directly.

8 结论Conclusion

企业环境中的代理人工智能不应仅仅被视为一个提示性问题。这是一个随机控制问题，其中可靠性和监督成本由支持、分支模糊性和路径结构共同决定。相关对象不是孤立的下一步，而是通过受约束的工作流程的状态-动作轨迹。Agentic artificial intelligence in enterprise settings should not be analyzed as a prompting problem alone. It is a stochastic-control problem in which reliability and oversight cost are jointly determined by support, branching ambiguity, and path structure. The relevant object is not an isolated next step, but the state-action trajectory through a constrained workflow.

本文使用完整的 BPI 2019 年采购到付款日志表明，大型企业流程可以在州一级得到很好的覆盖，同时仍然保留下一步决策的重要盲目性。使用同一日志的按时间顺序保留的分割，它进一步表明，所得到的理论量可以预测已实现的代理行为：状态方面的最高概率是保留步骤准确性的良好替代品，并且门引起的可靠性成本边界仍然保守，但在案例级别上方向准确。Using the full BPI 2019 purchase-to-pay log, this paper shows that a large enterprise process can appear well covered at the state level while still retaining nontrivial blind mass over next-step decisions. Using a chronological held-out split of the same log, it further shows that the resulting theoretical quantities are predictive of realized agent behavior: state-wise top-probability is a good surrogate for held-out step accuracy, and the gate-induced reliability-cost frontier remains conservative but directionally accurate at the case level.

从操作上来说，结果支持一个简单的建议。企业代理部署应该从支持、熵、风险和监督成本审计开始，而不是仅仅从快速迭代开始。在这个框架内，随机差距变得可测量，合理的自主权数量也变得可测量。Operationally, the result supports a simple recommendation. Enterprise agent deployment should begin with a support, entropy, risk, and oversight-cost audit rather than with prompt iteration alone. Within that framework, the stochastic gap becomes measurable, and so does the amount of autonomy that can be justified.
