第 2.1 节 影响的替代途径Section 2.1 Alternative Pathways to Impact

上一节中的应用反映了对边缘和云计算、网络和网络安全以及强大、多代理、异构系统的人工智能基础的直接、协调和大量投资的顶峰。总的来说，本报告中描述的投资可以缩小能力差距。然而，我们也承认紧密协调行动的难度。幸运的是，随着广泛采用无人机技术的强大动力已经到位，替代结果仍然具有影响力。考虑以下场景。The applications in the previous section reflect the culmination of immediate, coordinated and substantial investments in edge and cloud computing, networking and cybersecurity, and AI foundations for robust, multi-agent, heterogeneous systems. Collectively, the investments described in this report can close the capability gap. However, we also acknowledge the difficulty of tightly coordinated action. Fortunately, with a strong drive to broadly adopt drone technology already in place, alternative outcomes can still be impactful. Consider the following scenarios.

场景A：边缘AI仅突破Scenario A: Edge AI Breakthrough Only

在这种情况下，研究和基础设施投资产生了新颖的系统，可以跨多个协调且无缝通信的无人机管理边缘、云和移动资源，从而实现强大的机群学习、高效的无人机推理以及自主行为的形式验证。然而，它们并不能提高可信度，也不能产生可用于生产级环境的可扩展人工智能。在这种情况下，到 2035 年，无人机应用将能够获得民主化的空中传感，以实现具有切实经济价值的受控本地部署。示例应用包括：In this scenario, research and infrastructure investments yield novel systems to manage edge, cloud, and mobile resources across multiple coordinated and seamlessly communicating drones, achieving robust fleet learning, efficient on-drone inference, and formal verification of autonomous behaviors. However, they do not improve trustworthiness, nor do they yield scalable AI ready for production-grade environments. Under this scenario, in 2035, drone applications would have access to democratized aerial sensing for controlled, local deployments with tangible economic value. Example applications include:

  • 家长从多个角度记录孩子的足球比赛，视频可以即时、私密、安全地传输和处理，以便从任何角度重播。A parent records their child's soccer game from multiple angles with video that is instantly, privately, and securely transmitted and processed for replay from any perspective.

  • 一家农业合作社监测数百英亩的作物压力，减少 30% 的用水量，同时提高产量。An agricultural cooperative monitors crop stress across hundreds of acres, reducing water usage by 30% while boosting yields.

局限性Limitations：如果没有成熟的安全框架，此类应用程序仍然局限于低风险环境中，在这种环境中，违规造成的后果最小，而在单运营商环境中，多组织协作受到限制。如果没有可扩展的政策框架，运营就仅限于私有财产或特别允许的区域。该技术存在，但无法扩展到共享空域或关键基础设施保护。例如，仅边缘人工智能的进步就可以使辅助无人机机队与当地拖拉机通信以协助农业工作。然而，如果没有可扩展的政策和安全性，该技术不太可能达到美国每年销售 20 万台拖拉机的水平，从而使我们扩大到 100 万架无人机的雄心勃勃的目标陷入危险（Odegard，2025）。仅靠边缘人工智能就能创造本地化卓越，无需进行系统性转型。: Without mature security frameworks, such applications remain confined to low-stakes environments where breaches carry minimal consequences and single-operator environments where multi-organization collaboration is limited. Without scalable policy frameworks, operations are restricted to private property or specially permitted zones. The technology exists but cannot scale to shared airspace or critical infrastructure protection. For example, advances in edge AI alone would enable fleets of helper drones that communicate with local tractors to assist agricultural workloads. However, without scalable policy and security, the technology is unlikely to reach the 200,000 tractors sold annually in the United States, putting our ambitious goal of scaling to 1 million drones in jeopardy (Odegard, 2025). Edge AI alone creates localized excellence without systemic transformation.

概率模型检查Probabilistic Model Checking一种验证技术，用于计算系统满足某些属性的概率，提供不安全行为可能性的界限，而不是绝对保证。对于具有固有不确定性的系统（例如基于学习的自主性）很有用。A verification technique that computes the probability of a system satisfying certain properties, providing bounds on the likelihood of unsafe behaviors rather than absolute guarantees. Useful for systems with inherent uncertainty like learning-based autonomy.

实时系统Real-Time System一个系统的正确性不仅取决于逻辑结果，还取决于是否满足时间期限。飞行控制是一种硬实时系统，错过 10 毫秒的截止时间可能会导致崩溃，而软实时系统（视频流）则可以容忍偶尔的延迟。A system where correctness depends not only on logical results but on meeting timing deadlines. Flight control is a hard real-time system where missing a 10ms deadline can cause crashes, unlike soft real-time systems (video streaming) where occasional delays are tolerable.

运行时验证Runtime Verification在运行期间监控已部署的系统，以检测违反安全属性的行为，从而在故障发生之前进行干预。补充在不可预测的环境中发展或运行的系统的部署前验证。Monitoring deployed systems during operation to detect violations of safety properties, enabling interventions before failures occur. Complements pre-deployment verification for systems that evolve or operate in unpredictable environments.

模拟与真实的差距Sim-to-Real Gap模拟环境和物理环境之间的差异导致在模拟中训练的人工智能模型在部署到真实硬件上时失败。由未建模的动态（风、传感器噪声）和微妙的环境因素（照明、纹理）产生。The discrepancy between simulated and physical environments that causes AI models trained in simulation to fail when deployed on real hardware. Arises from unmodeled dynamics (wind, sensor noise) and subtle environmental factors (lighting, textures).

蜂群Swarm紧密耦合的多智能体系统，其中许多简单智能体通过本地交互进行协调以实现集体行为（聚集、模式形成）。与舰队不同，舰队可能是异构且结构化的。A tightly coupled multi-agent system where many simple agents coordinate through local interactions to achieve collective behaviors (flocking, pattern formation). Distinct from fleets, which may be heterogeneous and structured.

试验台Testbed用于在操作部署之前试验和验证系统的受控环境。国家无人机测试平台将为全国研究人员提供共享基础设施（飞行范围、边缘计算、通信网络）和数据集。A controlled environment for experimenting with and validating systems before operational deployment. A national drone testbed would provide shared infrastructure (flight ranges, edge computing, communication networks) and datasets accessible to researchers nationwide.

信息价值 (Vol)Value-of-Information (VoI)根据收集的信息量来分配资源（传感、计算、通信、能源）的决策框架可以减少任务的不确定性或风险，而不是使用固定的策略。在约束条件下实现自适应资源管理。A decision-making framework that allocates resources (sensing, computation, communication, energy) based on how much information gathering reduces mission uncertainty or risk, rather than using fixed policies. Enables adaptive resource management under constraints.

WCET（最坏情况执行时间）WCET (Worst-Case Execution Time)在任何可能的条件下（最坏情况的传感器数据、最大内存使用量等），计算任务可以花费的最长时间。对于实时系统至关重要，因为实时系统的安全性取决于保证控制循环在有限时间内完成。The maximum time a computational task can take under any possible conditions (worst-case sensor data, maximum memory usage, etc.). Critical for real-time systems where safety depends on guaranteeing that control loops complete within bounded time.
