摘要Abstract

布局后的转型可能会减少局部违规，同时增加拥塞、布线绕道或功耗，最终恶化布线后 QoR。因此，阶段级决策只有在保持设计有效性并且其益处在整个流程中持续存在的情况下才有价值。这一特性对物理设计算法的演进提出了严格的要求：针对特定阶段优化算法不仅必须考虑其直接目标，还要考虑其对整个实现流程的最终多目标 QoR 的影响。transformation after placement may reduce local violations while increasing congestion, routing detours, or power, ultimately worsening post-route QoR. A stage-level decision is therefore valuable only if it preserves design validity and its benefit persists through the complete flow. This property imposes a stringent requirement on physical design algorithm evolution: optimizing an algorithm for a particular stage must account not only for its immediate objective, but also for its impact on the final multi-objective QoR of the entire implementation flow.

物理设计算法在紧密耦合的多级优化流程中运行，其中级局部增益可能消失或导致下游退化。现有的程序演化框架通常依赖于阶段局部目标或无差异的多指标反馈，这既不能保证更好的最终结果，也不能确定哪些未满足的需求应该指导下一次迭代。我们提出了 GoalEvolve，一个目标驱动的框架，使物理设计算法的演化对整个流程的最终结果质量 (QoR) 负责。给定多目标 QoR 目标区域，GoalEvolve 将未满足的需求转换为标准化的目标差距，识别主要瓶颈，并使用阶段解决的检查点证据来定位负责的阶段。然后，基于法学硕士的教师将搜索范围缩小到相关的算法决策和源区域，而并行的学生代理则通过全流程评估来实现和验证假设。局部效应、优化债务和下游保留被保留作为后续演化的机制证据。在八个 ASAP7 设计中，与默认的 OpenROAD 相比，GoalEvolve 将布线后 TNS 平均提高了 30.67%，并将泄漏和动态功耗降低了 21.18% 和 9.42%。相对于商业工具目标，它缩小了功耗主导设计的标准化功耗差距的 62.20%，超过了两种时序主导设计的 TNS 目标，并缩小了联合设计的等权时序功耗差距的 32.48%。在匹配预算下针对 Codex 目标模式进行评估的所有三种设计中，GoalEvolve 将 TNS 进一步提高了 26.46%，同时将泄漏和动态功耗分别降低了 12.38% 和 0.76%。Physical design algorithms operate within tightly coupled, multistage optimization flows, where stage-local gains may vanish or induce downstream degradation. Existing program-evolution frameworks often rely on stage-local objectives or undifferentiated multimetric feedback, which neither guarantee better final results nor identify which unmet requirement should guide the next iteration. We present GoalEvolve, a goal-driven framework that makes physical design algorithm evolution accountable for the final quality of results (QoR) of the complete flow. Given a multi-objective QoR target region, GoalEvolve converts unmet requirements into normalized target gaps, identifies the dominant bottleneck, and uses stage-resolved checkpoint evidence to locate the responsible stage. An LLM-based Teacher then narrows the search to a relevant algorithmic decision and source region, while parallel Student agents implement and validate hypotheses through full-flow evaluation. Local effects, optimization debt, and downstream retention are retained as mechanism evidence for subsequent evolution. Across eight ASAP7 designs, GoalEvolve improves post-route TNS by 30.67% on average and reduces leakage and dynamic power by 21.18% and 9.42% versus default OpenROAD. Relative to commercial-tool goals, it closes 62.20% of the normalized power gap on power-dominant designs, surpasses the TNS goals on both timing-dominant designs, and closes 32.48% of the equal-weight timing–power gap on joint designs. Across all three designs evaluated against Codex goal mode under matched budgets, GoalEvolve further improves TNS by 26.46% while reducing leakage and dynamic power by 12.38% and 0.76%, respectively.

传统的开发过程遵循算法优先的范式。研究人员通常从发现的弱点开始，制定优化策略，实施相应的机制，然后评估完整的流程。当所提出的机制和目标 QoR 之间的关系得到很好的理解时，这种方法是有效的。然而，在许多设计收敛问题中，期望的结果是明确的，但实现该结果的算法路径仍不清楚。阶段日志和中间报告提供了有用的诊断证据，但将该证据转化为下一个源级假设仍然需要大量的手动分析和领域专业知识。因此，算法开发通过诊断、实施和验证的重复循环进行，没有自动方式直接从剩余目标差距中得出下一个干预措施。The conventional development process follows an algorithm-first paradigm. Researchers typically begin with a perceived weakness, formulate an optimization strategy, implement the corresponding mechanism, and then evaluate the complete flow. This approach is effective when the relationship between the proposed mechanism and the target QoR is well understood. In many design-closure problems, however, the desired outcome is explicit while the algorithmic path toward it remains unclear. Stage logs and intermediate reports provide useful diagnostic evidence, but translating that evidence into the next source-level hypothesis still requires substantial manual analysis and domain expertise. Algorithm development therefore proceeds through repeated cycles of diagnosis, implementation, and validation, without an automated way to derive the next intervention directly from the remaining target gap.

LLM 代理使存储库规模的源代码探索和修改变得越来越实用。一般的程序演化系统将程序生成与自动评估相结合[12,15,16,20]，而最近的 EDA 系统将这种范例扩展到逻辑综合和物理设计的多个阶段[7,9,19,21]。这些研究表明，LLM 代理可以通过执行反馈迭代改进 EDA 算法。然而，它们的控制逻辑在紧密耦合的物理设计流程中留下了两个未解决的问题。首先，当候选者选择基于修改阶段的目标时，明显的局部增益可能会被后面的阶段丢失或推翻，因此不需要改善全流程结果。其次，多个报告的指标通常没有明确的优先级。此类反馈可以比较候选者，但并不表明哪个剩余目标赤字应控制下一个源修订。因此，代理可能会在相互竞争的目标之间交替，或者修改与当前瓶颈无关的机制，从而导致不集中的搜索和不必要的全流程评估。LLM agents make repository-scale source-code exploration and modification increasingly practical. General program-evolution systems combine program generation with automated evaluation [12, 15, 16, 20], while recent EDA systems extend this paradigm to logic synthesis and multiple stages of physical design [7, 9, 19, 21]. These studies show that LLM agents can iteratively improve EDA algorithms through execution feedback. Nevertheless, their control logic leaves two issues unresolved in tightly coupled physical design flows. First, when candidate selection is based on the objective of the modified stage, an apparent local gain may be lost or overturned by later stages and thus need not improve the full-flow outcome. Second, multiple reported metrics are often presented without an explicit priority. Such feedback can compare candidates, but does not indicate which remaining target deficit should govern the next source revision. As a result, the agent may alternate among competing objectives or modify mechanisms unrelated to the current bottleneck, leading to an unfocused search and unnecessary full-flow evaluations.

2 背景及相关工作2 Background and Related Work

本节追溯从专家驱动的物理设计算法开发到基于 LLM 的程序演化和存储库规模的 EDA 代理的进展。我们首先回顾代表性物理设计算法如何依赖领域专业知识进行开发和适应，然后研究进化和代理系统如何生成、评估和保留源变体。This section traces the progression from expert-driven physical design algorithm development to LLM-based program evolution and repository-scale EDA agents. We first review how representative physical design algorithms rely on domain expertise for development and adaptation, and then examine how evolutionary and agentic systems generate, evaluate, and retain source variants.

2.2 算法进化的代理人工智能2.2 Agentic AI for Algorithm Evolution

法学硕士的出现通过关闭搜索空间探索、代码生成和可执行评估之间的循环，为通用算法演化开辟了一条新路径。 FunSearch 在固定程序支架内搜索用户指定函数的实现，保留岛屿群体中的有效程序，并对得分较高的变体进行采样以用于后续提示 [16]。 AlphaEvolve直接编辑代码，并使用进化数据库根据多个评估者的反馈选择父程序[15]。 EoH 通过基于适应度的群体更新共同进化自然语言思想和可执行的启发式算法，而 ReEvo 将成对的性能差异转化为指导交叉和精英突变的直接和累积的反射 [12, 20]。 LLaMEA 在单亲循环中保留最佳算法，并反馈运行时适应性和错误以进行突变或重新设计 [17]。 ShinkaEvolve 通过探索感知父采样、新颖性过滤和自适应 LLM 选择来提高样本效率 [10]。这些系统共同证明了 LLM 代理可以通过执行反馈和结构化保留来维持经验算法的演化，为将该范例扩展到 EDA 提供了实践基础。The emergence of LLMs has opened a new path for general algorithm evolution by closing the loop among search-space exploration, code generation, and executable evaluation. FunSearch searches over implementations of a user-specified function within a fixed program scaffold, retains valid programs in island populations, and samples higher-scoring variants for subsequent prompts [16]. AlphaEvolve directly edits code and uses an evolutionary database to select parent programs according to feedback from multiple evaluators [15]. EoH co-evolves natural-language ideas and executable heuristics through fitness-based population updates, whereas ReEvo converts pairwise performance differences into immediate and accumulated reflections that guide crossover and elitist mutation [12, 20]. LLaMEA retains the best algorithm in a singleparent loop and feeds runtime fitness and errors back for mutation or redesign [17]. ShinkaEvolve improves sample efficiency through exploration-aware parent sampling, novelty filtering, and adaptive LLM selection [10]. Together, these systems demonstrate that LLM agents can sustain empirical algorithm evolution through execution feedback and structured retention, providing a practical foundation for extending this paradigm to EDA.

5 结论5 Conclusion

GoalEvolve 将物理设计算法开发重新定义为全流程反馈下的目标导向源演化。剩余的 QoR 差距确定了要修改的阶段和源机制，而检查点证据和机制记忆有利于下游相互作用中幸存下来的影响。实验表明，GoalEvolve 相对于默认 OpenROAD 改进了布线后 QoR，在异构时序功率配置文件中朝着商业工具目标取得了实质性进展，展示了演进机制的有用的跨设计转移，并在匹配的全流程评估预算下实现了比 Codex 目标模式更好的布线后 QoR。这些结果将目标引导的源演化确立为物理设计的实用方法。GoalEvolve reframes physical design algorithm development as goal-directed source evolution under full-flow feedback. Remaining QoR gaps identify the stage and source mechanism to revise, while checkpoint evidence and mechanism memory favor effects that survive downstream interactions. Experiments show that GoalEvolve improves post-route QoR over default OpenROAD, makes substantial progress toward commercial-tool targets across heterogeneous timing–power profiles, demonstrates useful cross-design transfer of evolved mechanisms, and achieves better post-route QoR than Codex goal mode under matched full-flow evaluation budgets. These results establish goal-guided source evolution as a practical methodology for physical design.

在这些匹配的评估预算下，GoalEvolve 在表 5 中的 9 个时序功耗比较中的 7 个中实现了更好的 QoR。在三种设计中平均，GoalEvolve 相对于 Codex 目标模式将 TNS 提高了 26.46%，同时将泄漏和动态功耗分别降低了 12.38% 和 0.76%。 GoalEvolve 改进了 Ariane 和 NVDLA-P 的所有三个 QoR 指标。在 NVDLAM 上，Codex 的 TNS 提高了 0.99 ns，动态功耗仅降低了 0.25%，而 GoalEvolve 将泄漏降低了 36.17%。这些结果表明，GoalEvolve 的结构化物理设计反馈可以在昂贵的全流程评估的相同预算下产生更高质量的源修订。Under these matched evaluation budgets, GoalEvolve achieves better QoR in seven of the nine timing–power comparisons in Table 5.
