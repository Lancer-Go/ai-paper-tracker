当规格发生冲突时：用于衡量 LLM 偏好的基于对称的框架When Specifications Conflict: A Symmetry-Based Framework for Measuring LLM Preferences

Tairan Wang*, Liang Zhou, Zikang Zhan*, Pingchuan Yan
摘要Abstract

越来越需要大型语言模型（LLM）来集成多个 信息来源可能不一致甚至冲突。然而， 仍缺乏可控、可归因的方法 系统地分析模型如何解决竞争之间的冲突 规格。我们提出了一个受控实验框架来研究 冲突规格下的模型偏好。通过构建 具有明确冲突的规范，该框架支持模型选择 直接观察和分析竞争规范之间的关系。一个 基于对称性的实验设计进一步减少了影响 混杂因素，允许系统地比较与不同表示类型相关的偏好。Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly required to integrate multiple sources of information that may be inconsistent or even conflicting. However, there is still a lack of controllable and attributable methods for systematically analyzing how models resolve conflicts between competing specifications. We propose a controlled experimental framework for studying model preferences under conflicting specifications. By constructing specifications with explicit conflicts, the framework enables model choices between competing specifications to be directly observed and analyzed. A symmetry-based experimental design further reduces the influence of confounding factors, allowing preferences associated with different representation types to be compared systematically.

简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地在多个环境中运行 上下文信息共同指定任务应该如何进行 执行的Large language models (LLMs) increasingly operate in settings where multiple pieces of contextual information jointly specify how a task should be performed(Lewis et al. 2020; Yang et al. 2018; Feldman and El-Yaniv 2019)。 这些规范可能采取多种形式，包括自然语言 说明、示例、形式约束和可执行测试。在实践中， 此类规范可能不完整、不一致或直接冲突. These specifications may take diverse forms, including natural language instructions, examples, formal constraints, and executable tests. In practice, such specifications may be incomplete, inconsistent, or directly conflicting(Hou et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2025; Liu and Roth 2025)。 当两个规范支持不兼容的行为时，模型必须 隐式地确定哪个规范控制其最终响应。 理解这种选择行为很重要，因为它揭示了 模型分配给不同形式的规范的隐式层次结构 对于设计可靠的基于法学硕士的系统至关重要。. When two specifications support incompatible behaviors, the model must implicitly determine which specification governs its final response. Understanding this selection behavior is important because it reveals the implicit hierarchy that models assign to different forms of specification and is essential for designing reliable LLM-based systems.

之前的工作研究了法学硕士如何检测、协调或应对冲突 设置中的信息，例如检索增强生成、事实检查、 和多上下文问答Prior work has studied how LLMs detect, reconcile, or respond to conflicting information in settings such as retrieval-augmented generation, fact-checking, and multi-context question answering(Ge et al. 2025; Li et al. 2025; Zeng et al. 2025; Hou et al. 2024; Kurfalı 2025; Lee et al. 2025)。 其他研究探讨了模型如何在竞争性证据或证据中进行选择 信息来源. Other studies have examined how models select among competing evidence or information sources(Schuster et al. 2026)。然而，现有研究 主要关注信息源之间的冲突，而如何 模型优先考虑不同形式的任务规范仍然较少 明白了。特别是，目前尚不清楚法学硕士是否表现出系统性 当相同的底层任务通过不同的方式表达时的偏好 表示，这些偏好如何随着模型的不同而变化，以及它们是否 概括超出受控设置。. However, existing studies have primarily focused on conflicts among information sources, while how models prioritize different forms of task specification remains less understood. In particular, it remains unclear whether LLMs exhibit systematic preferences when the same underlying task is expressed through different representations, how these preferences vary across models, and whether they generalize beyond controlled settings.

为了回答这些问题，我们引入了一个受控的、基于对称的框架 用于衡量冲突规格下的模型偏好。我们代表一个 通过竞争可执行映射来执行任务，这些映射在一个任务上产生不同的输出 歧视性询问。因为每个规范都支持不同的候选者 输出，模型的响应可以直接归因于一个映射，即 其他映射，或两者都不。我们进一步构造对称完备 平衡呈现顺序和分配的配置 表示类型到候选映射。这将解决冲突 陷入可归因的规格选择问题，同时减少 非目标因素的影响。To answer these questions, we introduce a controlled, symmetry-based framework for measuring model preference under conflicting specifications. We represent a task through competing executable mappings that produce different outputs on a discriminative query. Because each specification supports a distinct candidate output, the model’s response can be directly attributed to one mapping, the other mapping, or neither. We further construct symmetry-complete configurations that counterbalance presentation order and the assignment of representation types to candidate mappings. This turns conflict resolution into an attributable specification-selection problem while reducing the influence of non-target factors.

我们在受控数学基准中实例化该框架，其中包含 11 个函数系列中的 550 个冲突实例并比较四个 表示类型：纯自然语言、形式语言、自然化 形式语言和输入输出示例。我们的实验揭示了系统的 跨模型的表示偏好，包括正式形式和自然化形式 规范始终优于纯自然语言和 输入-输出示例。我们进一步证明归化的正式规范 其行为更像正式规范而不是中间规范 代表性，而示例的影响因模型和任务而异 家庭。We instantiate the framework in a controlled mathematical benchmark containing 550 conflict instances across 11 function families and compare four representation types: pure natural language, formal language, naturalized formal language, and input–output examples. Our experiments reveal systematic representation preferences across models, with Formal and Naturalized Formal specifications consistently preferred over Pure Natural Language and Input–Output Examples. We further show that naturalized formal specifications behave much more like formal specifications than like an intermediate representation, while the influence of examples varies across models and task families.

最后，我们将框架扩展到异构规范冲突 布尔代数、代码生成和临床规则设置。这些案例 证明该框架可以衡量归因规范 超出受控数学基准的偏好。总的来说，这部作品 将法学硕士中的冲突解决建立为受控的、可执行的和 对称平衡测量问题，能够系统分析 模型如何优先考虑不同形式的规范。Finally, we extend the framework to heterogeneous specification conflicts in Boolean algebra, code generation, and clinical rule settings. These cases demonstrate that the framework can measure attributable specification preferences beyond the controlled mathematical benchmark. Overall, this work establishes conflict resolution in LLMs as a controlled, executable, and symmetry-counterbalanced measurement problem, enabling systematic analysis of how models prioritize different forms of specification.

方法Method

我们提出了一个衡量冲突下模型偏好的框架 规格。该框架将冲突表示为竞争的可执行文件 映射并使用对称完全平衡来属性模型 产出，同时减少演示等非目标因素的影响 顺序和映射分配。We propose a framework for measuring model preference under conflicting specifications. The framework represents conflicts as competing executable mappings and uses symmetry-complete counterbalancing to attribute model outputs while reducing effects from non-target factors such as presentation order and mapping assignment.

基于对称性的可识别偏好归因框架A Symmetry-Based Framework for Identifiable Preference Attribution

为了表征冲突规范下的法学硕士偏好，我们制定了一个框架，该框架将多个竞争规范下的模型行为表示为候选映射的选择过程。To characterize LLM preferences under conflicting specifications, we formulate a framework that represents model behavior under multiple competing specifications as a selection process over candidate mappings.

我们首先定义一组相互冲突的规范，每个规范都会针对相同的输入产生不同的输出。这样，冲突被形式化为候选映射之间的不一致关系。We begin by defining a set of conflicting specifications, each of which induces a different output for the same input. In this way, conflict is formalized as an inconsistency relation among candidate mappings.

然后模型输出被解释为该选择过程的结果。由于每个规范确定查询输入的具体输出，因此模型预测可以直接归因于一个规范，将隐式决策转变为可观察变量。Model outputs are then interpreted as the outcomes of this selection process. Since each specification determines a concrete output for the queried input, the model prediction can be directly attributed to one specification, turning an implicit decision into an observable variable.

在此基础上，测量模型偏好需要处理两种类型的干扰因素。第一个因素由无法在单一配置中分解的因素组成，例如呈现顺序，其影响与其他因素相结合。为了解决这个问题，我们构建了对称配置，以便这些因素的影响在配置中以相反的方向出现，并在总体上抵消。Building on this formulation, measuring model preference requires handling two types of interfering factors. The first consists of factors that cannot be disentangled within a single configuration, such as presentation order, whose influence is coupled with other factors. To address this, we construct symmetric configurations so that the effect of such factors appears with opposite directions across configurations and cancels out in the aggregate.

第二个因素包括无法精确控制的因素，例如跨规范内容表达方式的细微差别。先前的工作表明，即使提示格式中保留意义的更改也会极大地影响模型行为The second consists of factors that cannot be precisely controlled, such as subtle differences in how content is expressed across specifications. Prior work has shown that even meaning-preserving changes in prompt formatting can substantially affect model behavior(Sclar et al. 2024)。这种变化可能会引入意想不到的不对称性，从而影响选择行为。为了减轻这种偏差来源，我们构建了结构对称且信息内容匹配的规范，同时避免可能引入系统词汇线索的固定表面模板。. Such variation may introduce unintended asymmetries and thereby affect selection behavior. To mitigate this source of bias, we construct specifications that are symmetric in structure and matched in informational content, while avoiding fixed surface templates that could introduce systematic lexical cues.

对称性在配置和规范级别上运行，减少了 不受控制的不对称性并通过聚合实现偏好估计 超过对称完全配置。Symmetry operates at both the configuration and specification levels, reducing uncontrolled asymmetries and enabling preference estimation through aggregation over symmetry-complete configurations.

讨论Discussion

我们的结果表明，模型表现出系统偏好 冲突规范下的表示形式。正式和归化 正式规范的选择频率高于结构化规范 替代方案，表明显式结构会加剧冲突 通过直接公开底层映射的关键约束来解决问题。 这也可以解释为什么归化的正式规范表现相似 到正式的语言，尽管它们的语言风格更自然。纯净 自然语言和基于示例的规范需要更多的推理 来自描述或有限的观察，但例子并不本质上 较弱。它们的影响取决于模型能力和任务结构： 更高功能的变体更频繁地选择示例支持的映射，并且 当基本规则更容易恢复时，示例更具竞争力 来自示威活动，特别是反对纯自然语言的示威活动 规格。这个解释和魏老师是一致的 等人。Our results show that models exhibit systematic preferences among representation forms under conflicting specifications. Formal and naturalized formal specifications are selected more frequently than less structured alternatives, suggesting that explicit structure facilitates conflict resolution by directly exposing key constraints of the underlying mapping. This may also explain why naturalized formal specifications behave similarly to formal ones despite their more natural-language style. Pure natural-language and example-based specifications require greater inference from descriptions or finite observations, but examples are not inherently weaker.
