摘要Abstract

不适定的问题，包括模棱两可、不明确或矛盾的查询，可能不允许有效答案或多个看似合理的答案，这对大型语言模型 (LLM) 提出了挑战。现有的方法主要通过模型输出来分析不适定性，并且通常关注特定的子类。我们研究是否可以在 LLM 内部状态的统一拓扑中表示不同的不适定源，以及该结构是否可以用于引导响应行为。我们将每个变换器层的提示标记的上下文隐藏状态建模为点云，并使用有限零维持久同源来表征其几何形状。每层由三个紧凑的描述符概括：平均有限寿命、归一化寿命熵和最大寿命浓度。跨层连接这些描述符会产生问题的拓扑表示。我们进一步引入拓扑条件激活控制，它检索拓扑相似的示例并构建特定于查询的激活干预措施，以鼓励源感知澄清或弃权。在三个开放权重法学硕士中，拓扑特征在不适定分类方面始终优于基于提示和池化隐藏状态基线，从而提高了平均准确度Ill-posed questions, including ambiguous, underspecified, or contradictory queries, may admit no valid answer or multiple plausible answers, posing a challenge for large language models (LLMs). Existing approaches largely analyze ill-posedness through model outputs and often focus on specific subclasses. We investigate whether diverse sources of ill-posedness can be represented within a unified topology of LLM internal states and whether this structure can be used to steer response behavior. We model the contextual hidden states of prompt tokens at each transformer layer as a point cloud and characterize its geometry using finite zero-dimensional persistent homology. Each layer is summarized by three compact descriptors: mean finite lifetime, normalized lifetime entropy, and largest-lifetime concentration. Concatenating these descriptors across layers yields a topology representation of the question. We further introduce topology-conditioned activation steering, which retrieves topologically similar examples and constructs query-specific activation interventions that encourage source-aware clarification or abstention. Across three open-weight LLMs, topology features consistently outperform prompt-based and pooled-hidden-state baselines for ill-posedness classification, improving average accuracy from67.4%67.4\% to 78.9%78.9\%在 AmbigQA 上，来自on AmbigQA, from79.9%79.9\% to 88.5%88.5\%在 SituatedQA 上，以及来自on SituatedQA, and from57.6%57.6\% to 69.6%69.6\%CLAMBER 9 路分类。拓扑条件转向提高了平均总可接受响应率on CLAMBER 9-way classification. Topology-conditioned steering increases the average total acceptable response rate from61.4%61.4\% to 70.6%70.6\%以及来自的可接受的回应and grounded acceptable responses from11.9%11.9\% to 16.4%16.4\%。这些结果表明，持久同源性既提供了不适定性的可解释表示，又提供了目标响应引导的有效机制。. These results show that persistent homology provides both an interpretable representation of ill-posedness and an effective mechanism for targeted response steering.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地部署为通用问答 (QA) 系统。然而，在许多情况下，用户问题可能不恰当，不承认有效答案或存在多个看似合理的答案。按照哈达玛的说法，当解决方案存在、唯一且持续依赖于数据时，问题就是适定的Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as general-purpose question answering (QA) systems. In many cases, however, a user question may be ill-posed, admitting no valid answer or multiple plausible answers. Following Hadamard, a problem is well-posed when a solution exists, is unique, and depends continuously on the data[11]。许多数据集和基准测试表明，质量保证中的不适定问题可能源自几个不同的来源：具有多种合理解释的歧义（词汇或语义）. Many datasets and benchmarks show that ill-posed questions in QA can originate from several distinct sources: ambiguities (lexical or semantic) with multiple plausible interpretations[20, 24, 30]、未指定的实体或事件引用, underspecified entity or event references[20]，缺少时间或地理背景, missing temporal or geographic context[29]，用户意图不明确, unclear user intent[30]，或过度指定的问题与冲突的陈述/条件, or over-specified problems with conflicting statements/conditions[19, 28]。即使在法学硕士表现可靠的明确问题上，一旦问题变得不适定，相同的模型也往往会动摇，其中困难既在于问题的表述，也在于模型的世界知识。. Even on well-specified questions where LLMs perform reliably, the same models tend to falter once a question becomes ill-posed, where the difficulty lies as much in the question’s formulation as in the model’s world knowledge.

最近的工作研究了不适定问题的几个子类。显着的进步包括消除歧义的输入重写Recent work has studied several subclasses of ill-posed problems. Notable advances include disambiguated input rewrites[20]，综合不同解释的答案, synthesizing answers across alternative interpretations[24]，关于歧义的选择性弃权或拒绝, selective abstention or rejection with respect to ambiguity[5]，以及主动的信息获取和启发, and active information acquisition and elicitation[30, 2, 17, 7]。这些工作表明，对不适定问题的强烈反应需要法学硕士根据不适定的根本根源调整其行为。至关重要的是，这种适应不应崩溃为固定的拒绝模板：有用的响应应保留特定于问题的实体、约束和上下文，同时针对缺失、模糊或冲突的信息。然而，大多数先前的方法将法学硕士推理视为黑匣子，并通过输入输出分析关注不适定的特定子类。这就提出了一个更广泛的问题：各种不适定源是否可以通过模型内部状态的拓扑表示在统一的分层拓扑空间中表示，并且这种结构是否可以用于引导推理转向与其潜在源一致的行为？. These works establish that a strong response to ill-posed problems requires LLMs to adapt their behaviors to the underlying sources of ill-posedness. Crucially, such adaptation should not collapse to a fixed refusal template: a useful response should preserve the question-specific entities, constraints, and context while targeting the missing, ambiguous, or conflicting information. However, most prior approaches treat LLM reasoning as a black box and focus on particular subclasses of ill-posedness through input-output analysis. This raises a broader question: Can various sources of ill-posedness be represented in a unified layer-wise topological space via a topological representation of the model’s internal states, and can this structure be used to steer reasoning toward behaviors aligned with their underlying sources?

5 结论Conclusion
