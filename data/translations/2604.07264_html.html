I 简介Introduction

近地轨道 (LEO) 巨型星座，例如 Starlink、OneWeb、 和 Kuiper 正在通过部署数千个网络来改变全球连接 通过卫星间链路（ISL）互连的卫星。运营 这些网络提出了独特的挑战：拓扑变化 随着卫星不断绕轨道运行，极地地区会经历周期性的变化 链路丢失，运营商必须实施复杂的路由约束 跨越延迟保证、区域避免、节点维护和 流量优先级。Low Earth Orbit (LEO) mega-constellations such as Starlink, OneWeb, and Kuiper are transforming global connectivity by deploying thousands of satellites interconnected via inter-satellite links (ISLs). Operating these networks presents unique challenges: the topology changes continuously as satellites orbit, polar regions experience periodic link dropout, and operators must enforce complex routing constraints spanning latency guarantees, region avoidance, node maintenance, and traffic prioritization.

如今，将运营商意图转化为网络配置需要 手动指定路由策略——这个过程很慢， 容易出错，并且无法适应 LEO 的动态特性 星座。基于意图的网络 (IBN)Today, translating operator intent into network configuration requires manual specification of routing policies—a process that is slow, error-prone, and does not scale to the dynamic nature of LEO constellations. Intent-based networking (IBN)[1]承诺 通过允许运营商表达高层次目标来弥合这一差距 自动编译成网络配置。然而， 现有的 IBN 方法针对地面网络的目标相对较 稳定的拓扑结构，不解决卫星的独特限制 巨型星座。promises to bridge this gap by allowing operators to express high-level goals that are automatically compiled into network configurations. However, existing IBN approaches target terrestrial networks with relatively stable topologies and do not address the unique constraints of satellite mega-constellations.

我们确定了意图驱动的 LEO 路由的三个关键挑战：We identify three key challenges in intent-driven LEO routing:

  1. 1.

    构图意图理解。Compositional intent understanding.运营商意图 组合多种约束类型（“禁用平面 7，避免极链接 高于 75°，上限利用率为 80%”） 正确分解并映射到形式约束表示。 基于规则的解析器可以处理简单的意图，但会急剧退化 成分的（40% vs. 86.2% 准确率）。Operator intents combine multiple constraint types (“disable plane 7, avoid polar links above 75°, and cap utilization at 80%”) that must be correctly decomposed and mapped to formal constraint representations. Rule-based parsers handle simple intents but degrade sharply on compositional ones (40% vs. 86.2% accuracy).

  2. 2.

    安全关键验证。Safety-critical verification.在生产网络中， 单个未检测到的约束违规可以级联到服务中 停电。编译器的输出必须在之前进行正式验证 到达路由层，但验证必须足够快 供交互式使用。In production networks, a single undetected constraint violation can cascade into service outages. The compiler’s output must be formally verified before reaching the routing layer, yet verification must be fast enough for interactive use.

  3. 3.

    高效的约束路由。Efficient constrained routing.应用约束 修改网络拓扑（禁用节点、删除边缘）， 并且路由器必须计算约束图上的有效路径 实时。传统的最短路径算法是正确的，但是 对于大规模的每个数据包决策来说太慢了。Applying constraints modifies the network topology (disabling nodes, removing edges), and the router must compute valid paths on the constrained graph in real time. Traditional shortest-path algorithms are correct but too slow for per-packet decisions at scale.
