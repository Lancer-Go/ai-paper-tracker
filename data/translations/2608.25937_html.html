摘要Abstract

多代理系统 (MAS) 有时已经有可能正确回答，但仍然报告错误的答案。解释这个结果很困难，因为生成、沟通和最终答案选择规则通常会同时变化。我们将多智能体推理概念化为候选生成、对等通信和终端选择的进化管道，其中没有质量控制的共识可能会表现出模因漂移的模式。我们研究两个问题：（1）法学硕士法官何时通过为多智能体系统中生成的候选人提供答案正确性信号来提供有效的选择压力，以及（2）何时使用该信号改进报告的答案。为了绘制判断可靠性，我们分析了来自 MMLU-Pro、GPQA、MedXpertQA 和 MuSR 的 15,336 个问题，并单独分析了 Humanity 的 Last Exam。为了测试这些规则，我们重播了从五个基准的 16,278 个问题中抽取的 81,390 个固定候选池。我们报告了三项发现。 (1) 生成的候选答案中通常已经存在正确答案，但系统仍然可以收敛并报告错误答案。 (2) 判断可靠性并不是模型的固定特征，而是随着任务、生成器以及正确答案的稀有程度而变化。 （3）将答案频率与法官的评估相结合，仅改变了最终的答案选择规则，并将准确率从63.82％提高到70.82-70.95％，主要是通过拯救数量超过流行错误的正确答案。在这里研究的系统中，生成更多候选者的价值取决于这些额外样本是否使正确答案出现、频繁或可识别。通过隔离生成、识别和选择，这些发现为设计多代理架构奠定了诊断基础，以保护生成的正确答案不被丢失。Multi-agent systems (MAS) sometimes already have the potential to answer correctly, but still report a wrong answer. Explaining this outcome is difficult because generation, communication and final answer-selection rules usually change simultaneously. We conceptualize multi-agent reasoning as an evolutionary pipeline of candidate generation, peer communication and terminal selection, wherein consensus without quality control can exhibit patterns of memetic drift. We study two questions: (1) when an LLM judge provides effective selection pressure by supplying a signal of answer correctness for candidates generated in a multi-agent system, and (2) when using that signal improves the reported answer. To map judge reliability, we analysed 15,336 questions from MMLU-Pro, GPQA, MedXpertQA and MuSR, with Humanity’s Last Exam analysed separately. To test these rules, we replayed 81,390 fixed candidate pools drawn from 16,278 questions across five benchmarks. We report three findings. (1) A correct answer is often already present among the generated candidates, but the system can still converge on and report a wrong answer. (2) Judge reliability is not a fixed trait of the model, but varies with the task, the generator and how rare the correct answer is. (3) Combining answer frequency with the judge’s evaluation changed only the final answer-selection rule and raised accuracy from 63.82% to 70.82–70.95%, primarily by rescuing correct answers that were outnumbered by popular errors. In the systems studied here, the value of generating more candidates depends on whether those extra samples make correct answers present, frequent or recognisable. By isolating generation, recognition and selection, these findings establish a diagnostic basis for designing multi-agent architectures that protect generated correct answers from being lost.

总之，这些多智能体交互实验展示了一种重复的失败模式：当流行的错误获得集体支持时，不受约束的通信可能会丢弃少数正确答案。在我们的框架中，模因漂移和多数偏向级联共同导致了这一代保留瓶颈。在生物进化中，选择通过保留具有更高适应性的变体来抵消随机漂移。多智能体推理中的类似需求是正确性敏感的选择压力：外部法学硕士法官根据内容而不是回答频率对候选人的理由进行排名。这为正确但寡不敌众的候选人影响所报告的答案创造了一条具体路径。下一个问题是法学硕士法官是否能够可靠地区分答案正确的候选人与答案错误的候选人，以及哪些因素决定其评估的保真度。因此，我们在将其信号应用于最终答案选择之前，将法官的可靠性映射到不同的候选供应机制中。Together, these multi-agent interaction experiments show a repeated failure mode: unconstrained communication can discard minority correct answers as popular errors gain collective support. In our framework, memetic drift and majority-biased cascades jointly contribute to this generation– retention bottleneck. In biological evolution, selection counteracts stochastic drift by preserving variants with higher fitness. The analogous need in multi-agent reasoning is correctness-sensitive selection pressure: an external LLM judge ranks candidate rationales based on their content rather than answer frequency. This creates a concrete path for a correct but outnumbered candidate to influence the reported answer. The next question is whether an LLM judge can reliably distinguish candidates with correct answers from candidates with incorrect answers, and what factors govern its evaluative fidelity. We therefore map judge reliability across different candidate supply regimes before applying its signal to final answer selection.

讨论Discussion

这项研究提出了一个统一的进化框架，用于解释多智能体语言模型系统何时保留生成的正确答案以及何时在下游丢失它们。我们的研究结果表明，这里研究的系统的准确性可以反映三个可分离的瓶颈，而不是将多智能体共识视为单一机制：候选生成是否产生正确的答案，LLM法官是否可以在可能的错误中识别出该答案（提供选择压力），以及答案选择规则是否作用于该信号（终端选择）。这种分离导致了多智能体系统中反复出现的失败：没有选择压力，未经过滤的同伴交互可能会导致模因漂移，其中智能体组可以生成正确的答案，但仍然无法报告它。因此，在法官引导的系统中，最终的准确性反映了正确答案的可用性、法官的可靠性和答案选择的综合影响。This study presents a unified evolutionary framework for explaining when multi-agent language model systems preserve generated correct answers and when they lose them downstream. Rather than treating multi-agent consensus as a single mechanism, our findings show that accuracy in the systems studied here can reflect three separable bottlenecks: whether candidate generation produces a correct answer, whether an LLM judge can recognise that answer among plausible errors (providing selection pressure), and whether the answer-selection rule acts on that signal (terminal selection). This separation accounts for a recurring failure in multi-agent systems: without selection pressure, unfiltered peer interaction can contribute to memetic drift, where groups of agents can generate a correct answer but still fail to report it. In judge-guided systems, final accuracy therefore reflects the combined effects of correct-answer availability, judge reliability and answer selection.

这些结果还有助于阐明缩放系统中测试时计算和代理间通信的作用。最近扩展推理计算的努力通常依赖于生成更多候选路径或延长多智能体辩论These results also help clarify the roles of test-time computation and inter-agent communication in scaled systems. Recent efforts to scale inference compute often rely on generating more candidate paths or prolonging multi-agent debate14,7,1,52,53,54。在最初的多智能体实验中，讨论迅速收敛，测试的协议更多地保留了一个流行的错误，而不是数量众多的正确答案，揭示了由模因漂移和多数偏向级联共同形成的生成保留瓶颈. Across the initial multi-agent experiments, discussions converged rapidly and the tested protocols more often preserved a popular error than an outnumbered correct answer, revealing a generation–retention bottleneck formed jointly by memetic drift and majority-biased cascades,28。额外的讨论大大增加了令牌的使用，但没有一致的准确性增益。对更多候选者进行采样可以扩大候选者库，但只有当答案选择规则可以识别正确的候选者或者生成使该候选者占绝大多数时，额外的生成才能改善报告的答案。在一项单独的固定池分析中，当正确答案存在但数量过多时，法官引导的选择提高了小型候选池的准确性。. Additional discussion rounds substantially increased token use without consistent accuracy gains. Sampling more candidates expands the candidate pool, but extra generation improves the reported answer only if the answer-selection rule can identify the correct candidate or if generation makes that candidate the overwhelming majority. In a separate fixed-pool analysis, judge-guided selection improved accuracy in small candidate pools when correct answers were present but outnumbered.

We ran each protocol three times, using seed values 42, 43 and 44. This produced 7,350 question evaluations per protocol. Within each run, the selected seed was sent with every model request. The five agents were executed as separate API requests, and each request included a distinct agent identifier in the system prompt. All requests used DeepSeek-V4-Flash (Preview) at temperature 0. The majority-voting protocol sampled five independent responses without peer communication and reported the most frequent answer. 候选生成和自适应候选库Candidate generation and adaptive candidate banks

候选跟踪包含一个生成的答案（封闭式选择任务的选项或 HLE QA 的自由形式响应）及其支持理由。通过跨多项选择推理基准（MMLU-Pro、GPQA、MedXpertQA、MuSR 和 HLE MC）和开放式答案 QA (HLE QA) 提示端点模型（DeepSeek-V4-Flash（预览版）和 Mimo v2.5）独立生成候选轨迹。推理条件包括“无思考”（禁用可选的内部推理）和“中等思考”（以中等努力启用提供者内部推理；扩展数据表 2）。对于使用官方答案键评分的基准测试，正确性标签指示所选答案是否与键匹配；它并不能证明支持理由没有逻辑错误。A candidate trace comprises one generated answer (an option for closed-choice tasks or a freeform response for HLE QA) together with its supporting rationale. Candidate traces were generated independently by prompting endpoint models (DeepSeek-V4-Flash (Preview) and Mimo v2.5) across multiple-choice reasoning benchmarks (MMLU-Pro, GPQA, MedXpertQA, MuSR and HLE MC) and open-answer QA (HLE QA). Inference conditions included "no-think" (disabling optional internal reasoning) and "medium-thinking" (enabling provider internal reasoning at medium effort; Extended Data Table 2). For benchmarks scored with official answer keys, a correctness label indicates whether the selected answer matches the key; it does not establish that the supporting rationale is free of logical errors.
