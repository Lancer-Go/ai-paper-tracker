1 简介Introduction

系统荟萃分析通过将多项独立研究的结果综合成定量结论，在循证研究中发挥核心作用Systematic meta-analyses play a central role in evidence-based research by synthesizing findings from multiple independent studies into quantitative conclusions[25, 43]。通过整合不同研究的证据，它们提高了统计功效，提高了效果估计的精确度，并使研究人员能够识别在个别研究中可能不明显的异质性来源。因此，荟萃分析被广泛应用于各个学科，包括临床医学（例如，用于制定循证治疗指南）. By integrating evidence across diverse studies, they increase statistical power, improve the precision of effect estimates, and enable researchers to identify sources of heterogeneity that may not be apparent in individual studies. As a result, meta-analyses are used widely across disciplines, including clinical medicine (e.g., for developing evidence-based treatment guidelines[28]）、心理学（例如评估行为和心理健康干预措施), psychology (e.g. for evaluating behavioral and mental health interventions[15, 21, 13]）、教育（例如，评估学习干预的有效性), education (e.g., for assessing the effectiveness of learning interventions[31, 4, 50]）、公共卫生（例如，为卫生政策和预防性干预措施提供信息), public health (e.g., for informing health policy and preventive interventions[7, 39]）和计算机科学（例如，综合人机交互技术对用户行为的影响), and computer science (e.g., for synthesizing the effect of human–computer interaction technologies on user behavior[29]）。因此，荟萃分析在建立证据基础以指导政策决策方面发挥着重要作用。). Meta-analyses therefore play an important role in building the evidence base to inform policy decisions.

然而，生成高质量的荟萃分析是一个手动过程，既昂贵又耗时。典型的荟萃分析需要付出大量努力来制定搜索策略、从多个数据库检索文献、筛选数百或数千项候选研究、评估资格、提取定量数据、计算标准化效应大小、执行统计综合以及根据 PRISMA 等指南准备报告However, producing a high-quality meta-analysis is a manual process that is expensive and time-consuming. A typical meta-analysis requires extensive effort to formulate search strategies, retrieve literature from multiple databases, screen hundreds or thousands of candidate studies, assess eligibility, extract quantitative data, compute standardized effect sizes, perform statistical synthesis, and prepare reporting according to guidelines such as PRISMA[43]。此前的研究估计，仅系统评价往往就需要一千多个人时，可能需要一年多的时间才能完成. Previous studies estimate that the systematic review alone often requires more than one thousand person-hours and may take more than a year to complete[9, 6]，在初步研究的发表和综合证据的可用性之间造成了很大的滞后。在这里，我们提出了一个自动元分析的代理框架，以可重复、可扩展、近实时的方式合成证据。, creating a substantial lag between the publication of primary studies and the availability of synthesized evidence. Here, we present an agentic framework for automatic meta-analysis to synthesize evidence in a reproducible, scalable, near real-time manner.
