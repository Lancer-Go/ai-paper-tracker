大海捞针：生态学中的转导主动标记Finding Needles in the Haystack: Transductive Active Labeling in Ecology

Rupa Kurinchi-Vendhan    Sara Beery
摘要Abstract

主动学习现在是标记生态数据的标准做法，使生态学家能够快速处理大量现场数据以了解和监测自然环境。当前的实践评估主动学习Active learning is now standard practice in labeling ecological data, enabling ecologists to quickly process large volumes of field data to understand and monitor natural environments. Current practices evaluate active learning归纳地inductively，估计保留测试集的预测性能。我们认为这种评估与大多数生态任务不一致，其目标是, estimating predictive performance on a held-out test set. We argue that this evaluation is misaligned with most ecological tasks, where the goal is to转导地transductively尽可能有效地标记整个数据池。我们证明，忽视人在循环中低估了继续标记的重要性，特别是对于长尾中可能具有不成比例的生态重要性的类别（稀有物种、不常见的行为等）。我们的分析表明，对于这条长尾，传导目标的重要性从预测转移到了label an entire pool of data as efficiently as possible. We demonstrate that ignoring the human-in-the-loop underestimates the importance of continuing to label, particularly for classes in the long tail which may be of disproportionate ecological importance (rare species, uncommon behaviors, etc.). Our analysis shows that, for this long tail, the transductive objective shifts importance from prediction to发现discovery：真正的挑战是找到“大海捞针”，即嵌入潜在几何中丰富类的密集区域中的稀有类的例子，我们用一种新颖的度量来量化它们: the true challenge becomes finding “needles in the haystack,” examples of rare classes that are embedded within dense regions of abundant classes in the latent geometry, which we quantify with a novel metric of抽样难度sampling difficulty。最后，为了将这些见解转化为实际的生态工作流程，我们提出了一种受生态稀疏曲线启发的保守混合停止标准，并表明将预测性能与发现标准相结合可以减少长尾池的过早停止，从而在发现（而不是分类）成为限制因素时提高稀有类别的恢复。所有可用的数据预处理和分析脚本. Finally, to translate these insights to practical ecological workflows, we propose a conservative hybrid stopping criterion inspired by ecological rarefaction curves, and show that combining predictive performance with discovery criteria reduces premature stopping on long-tailed pools, improving rare-class recovery when discovery—not classification—is the limiting factor. 旨在通过迭代选择信息最丰富的示例进行注释来降低标记成本。早期的工作引入了基于池的主动学习，其中模型从固定的未标记池中查询标签，并在每个获取步骤后重新训练aims to reduce labeling cost by iteratively selecting the most informative examples for annotation. Early works introduce pool-based active learning, where a model queries labels from a fixed unlabeled pool and is retrained after each acquisition step(lewis1995sequential)。该领域的研究主要集中于旨在最大化信息增益的抽样策略. Research in this area primarily focuses on sampling strategies designed to maximize information gain(weinstein2019selective; settles2009active; fu2013survey)。常见的方法包括不确定性抽样，即选择分类器最不自信的示例；基于多样性的采样，优先考虑扩大特征空间覆盖范围或减少标记样本之间冗余的示例；密度感知选择，有利于同样位于数据分布高密度区域的信息样本，以避免选择异常值. Common approaches include uncertainty sampling, which selects examples for which the classifier is least confident; diversity-based sampling, which prioritizes examples that expand coverage of the feature space or reduce redundancy among labeled samples; and density-aware selection, which favors informative samples that also lie in high-density regions of the data distribution to avoid selecting outliers(settles2009active).

In these settings, label efficiency depends primarily on which examples are selected, rather than on model retraining. We therefore focus on sampling behavior under realistic labeling workflows.

停止标准Stopping Criteria

确定何时在主动学习工作流程中不再值得额外标记。经典方法监控验证准确性、模型置信度或模型预测的稳定性determine when additional labeling is no longer worthwhile in an active learning workflow. Classic approaches monitor validation accuracy, model confidence, or stability of model predictions(settles2009active; vlachos2008stopping; bloodgood2009method; olsson2009intrinsic; zhu2010confidence; ishibashi2021stopping; wang2014stability; zhang2017stopping)。然而，此类预测信号本身可能无法反映部署池的真实进展。在许多生态工作流程中，池本身就是感兴趣的数据集，其相关结构可能很少、不均匀或很难被小型验证集捕获。因此，稳定的预测可能表明采样良好的区域过度自信或饱和，而不是足够的覆盖范围，而保留的测试性能可能取决于不代表整个池的分割。因此，仅基于保留的准确性或预测稳定性的停止标准可能为确定数据集何时已被充分探索提供有限的指导。. However, such predictive signals alone may not reflect true progress on the deployment pool. In many ecological workflows, the pool itself is the dataset of interest, and its relevant structure may be rare, uneven, or poorly captured by a small validation set. Stable predictions may therefore indicate overconfidence or saturation of well-sampled regions rather than sufficient coverage, while held-out test performance may depend on a split that is not representative of the full pool. As a result, stopping criteria based only on held-out accuracy or prediction stability may provide limited guidance for determining when a dataset has been sufficiently explored.

Intrinsic curvature is substantially weaker across all metrics, with low overlap and weak correlation with sampling time. This indicates that curvature alone does not capture the class-relative scarcity that determines whether a sample is difficult to discover.

这些消融支持两个结论。首先，实证研究结果对于聚类算法、距离度量和难度阈值的合理扰动是稳健的。其次，延迟发现的最强预测因素不仅仅是一般的几何非典型性，而是将局部密度与类隔离相结合的与标签相关的几何难度概念。这促使我们在主要分析中使用所提出的采样难度分数，并表明相关的标签感知几何诊断可能对未来的主动采样和数据集审计方法有用。These ablations support two conclusions. First, the empirical findings are robust to reasonable perturbations of clustering algorithm, distance metric, and difficulty threshold. Second, the strongest predictor of delayed discovery is not generic geometric atypicality alone, but a label-relative notion of geometric difficulty that combines local density with class isolation. This motivates our use of the proposed sampling-difficulty score in the main analysis and suggests that related label-aware geometric diagnostics may be useful for future active-sampling and dataset-auditing methods.

6.4 探索任务粒度的影响Exploring the Effect of Task Granularity

在生态监测中，相同的基础数据集可能会被标记为非常不同的特异性水平：一项研究可能会区分广泛的声源，例如生物学与人类学，或者可能需要细粒度的物种级识别。这些选择定义了标签本体，因此定义了嵌入空间的哪些区域在主动标记期间保持不明确。In ecological monitoring, the same underlying dataset may be labeled at very different levels of specificity: a study may separate broad acoustic sources such as biophony versus anthropophony, or it may require fine-grained species-level recognition. These choices define the label ontology, and therefore define which regions of embedding space remain ambiguous during active labeling.

如果发现难度是由表示几何驱动的，那么更改标签空间应该会改变难度分布。细粒度本体将数据分割成许多小的、重叠的子簇，这增加了罕见和几何困难示例的数量。更粗略的本体将语义上相似的类折叠成更大的组，减少局部重叠，并缩小仍然难以发现的示例集。根据这种观点，长尾难度不仅是班级频率的属性，也是班级频率的一个属性。它也是任务粒度的一个属性。If discovery difficulty is driven by representation geometry, then changing the label space should change the difficulty distribution. Fine-grained ontologies fragment the data into many small, overlapping subclusters, which increases the number of rare and geometrically difficult examples. Coarser ontologies collapse semantically similar classes into larger groups, reduce local overlap, and shrink the set of examples that remain difficult to discover. Under this view, long-tail difficulty is not only a property of class frequency; it is also a property of task granularity.
