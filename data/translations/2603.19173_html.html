摘要Abstract

随着代理人工智能系统生成和优化 GPU 内核的能力越来越强，进步受到基准的限制，这些基准奖励的是软件基准上的加速，而不是接近硬件高效执行。我们呈现As agentic AI systems become increasingly capable of generating and optimizing GPU kernels, progress is constrained by benchmarks that reward speedup over software baselines rather than proximity to hardware-efficient execution. The benchmark covers forward and backward workloads across BF16, FP8, and NVFP4, including kernels whose best performance is expected to rely on Blackwell-specific capabilities. Existing benchmarks often measure success by speedup over a software baseline, even though the real objective in kernel engineering is to approach hardware-efficient execution. This mismatch is becoming more consequential as each GPU generation introduces new performance-critical features at a rapid pace, while power efficiency becomes a primary constraint in data center deployments. In practice, manual optimization cannot keep up indefinitely with both the hardware feature cadence and the growth in model complexity, making AI-based kernel optimization increasingly necessary rather than optional.

3 基准建设Benchmark Construction

我们根据 AI 训练后应用中对高性能内核的需求（该领域呈现指数级增长）中得出的三个原则来设计 SOL-ExecBench：We design SOL-ExecBench based on three principles drawn from the need for high-performance kernels in AI post-training applications, a domain seeing exponential growth:

  • •

    基于应用程序的问题。Application-grounded problems.问题必须由最先进的和新兴的模型架构驱动，以便算子类型、张量形状和数据类型反映当前生产中实际运行的内容以及在不久的将来将运行的内容。Problems must be driven by state-of-the-art and emerging model architectures so that operator types, tensor shapes, and data types reflect what actually runs in production today and what will run in near future.

  • •

    锻炼最新的硬件功能。Exercising latest hardware features.问题和指标目标必须鼓励解决方案在较新的 GPU 架构中运用最新的硬件功能（例如，通过 Blackwell GPU 上的第五代 Tensor Core 实现 NVFP4）。Problems and metric targets must encourage solutions that exercise the latest hardware features in newer GPU architectures (e.g., NVFP4 via 5th-generation Tensor Cores on Blackwell GPUs).

  • •

    培训后生命周期。Post-training lifecycle.问题必须跨越更广泛的训练后生命周期，例如微调、RLHF 和推理服务，使用降低精度的数据类型（FP8、NVFP4）而不是仅推理的前向内核捕获前向和后向传递。Problems must span the broader post-training lifecycle such as fine-tuning, RLHF, and inference serving, capturing forward and backward passes with reduced-precision data types (FP8, NVFP4) rather than inference-only forward kernels.

6 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 SOL-ExecBench，这是围绕硬件光速 (SOL) 目标构建的 GPU 内核优化基准。 SOL-ExecBench 包含由 124 个前沿和新兴 AI 模型组装而成的 235 个问题，涵盖训练后和推理工作负载、现代精度格式以及受益于新硬件功能的内核。我们还介绍We presented SOL-ExecBench, a benchmark for GPU kernel optimization built around hardware Speed-of-Light (SOL) targets. SOL-ExecBench includes 235 problems assembled from 124 frontier and emerging AI models, covering post-training and inference workloads, modern precision formats, and kernels that benefit from new hardware features. We also introduce太阳能SOLAR，一个从 PyTorch 程序分析得出基于硬件的 SOL 边界的管道，为每个问题提供超出软件基线的稳定目标。我们引入了 SOL 分数，这是生成内核的一个新指标。与单独的加速不同，它通过测量评分基线与候选内核的 SOL 界限之间的差距来揭示剩余的空间。我们还提供了一个强大的评估工具，可以防御奖励黑客攻击，并根据我们从代理生成的解决方案中观察到的故障模式提供信息。我们的代理优化器可生成强大的基线解决方案，整体 SOL 得分中位数为, a pipeline that analytically derives hardware-grounded SOL bounds from PyTorch programs, giving each problem a stable target beyond a software baseline. We introduce the SOL score, a new metric for generated kernels. Unlike speedup alone, it reveals the remaining headroom by measuring how much of the gap between a scoring baseline and the SOL bound a candidate kernel closes. We also provide a robust evaluation harness with defenses against reward hacking, informed by the failure modes we observed from agent-generated solutions. Our agentic optimizer produces strong baseline solutions, with the overall median SOL score of0.7320.732，这表明仍然存在大量优化空间。展望未来，我们预计 SOL-ExecBench 将随着新的工作负载、更强大的基线和社区提交而不断发展，以使其与模型、内核和硬件的前沿保持一致。, which shows that substantial optimization headroom still remains. Looking ahead, we expect SOL-ExecBench to evolve with new workloads, stronger baselines, and community submissions so that it remains aligned with the frontier of models, kernels, and hardware.
