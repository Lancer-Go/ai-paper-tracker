摘要Abstract

完整长度的歌曲生成必须保持连贯性和音乐性，呈现详细的人声和伴奏声学效果，并遵循歌词和提示。 现有的基于语言模型的系统面临着结构性权衡：混合令牌建模保留了声乐乐器协调，但模糊了轨道特定的细节，而双轨预测改善了声学效果，但需要更长的序列并削弱了全局规划。 我们推出了 LeVo 2，一种用于可控全长歌曲生成的混合 LLM-Diffusion 框架。 LeVo 2 将这种权衡表述为分层建模：LeLM 首先预测混合标记以进行语义规划，然后并行预测声乐和伴奏标记以进行特定于曲目的细化，而基于扩散的音乐编解码器则重建全长波形。 这个扩展版本的一个核心贡献是一个以美学为导向的对齐训练计划。 在预训练期间，自动音乐美学评估框架将音乐性等级条件分配给大规模数据，在偏好对齐之前提供音乐性先验。 渐进式后期训练采用SFT、大规模离线DPO、闭环半在线DPO分别提高生成质量、可控性和音乐性。 然后，模块化扩展训练 Track-Specific LM 进行声学细化，同时保留对齐的语义规划器。 该时间表将音乐性学习、可控性对齐和声学细化分开，减轻了优化冲突和静态离线偏好对的限制。 专家听力测试和客观评估表明，LeVo 2 在六个主观维度上均优于开源基线，并在多个听力指标上接近领先的商业系统。 消融验证了训练策略、美学指导、缩放和分层架构的效果。Full-length song generation must preserve coherence and musicality, render detailed vocal and accompaniment acoustics, and follow lyrics and prompts. Existing language model-based systems face a structural trade-off: mixed-token modeling preserves vocal-instrument coordination but obscures track-specific details, whereas dual-track prediction improves acoustics but requires longer sequences and weakens global planning. We present LeVo 2, a hybrid LLM-Diffusion framework for controllable full-length song generation. LeVo 2 formulates this trade-off as hierarchical modeling: LeLM first predicts mixed tokens for semantic planning, then predicts vocal and accompaniment tokens in parallel for track-specific refinement, while a diffusion-based Music Codec reconstructs full-length waveforms. A central contribution of this extended version is an aesthetics-guided training schedule for alignment. During pre-training, an automated music aesthetic evaluation framework assigns musicality-tier conditions to large-scale data, providing musicality priors before preference alignment. Progressive post-training applies SFT, large-scale offline DPO, and closed-loop semi-online DPO to separately improve generation quality, controllability, and musicality. Modular extension then trains the Track-Specific LM for acoustic refinement while preserving the aligned semantic planner. This schedule separates musicality learning, controllability alignment, and acoustic refinement, mitigating optimization conflict and the limitations of static offline preference pairs. Expert listening tests and objective evaluations show that LeVo 2 outperforms open-source baselines across six subjective dimensions, and approaches leading commercial systems on several listening metrics. Ablations validate the effects of the training strategy, aesthetics guidance, scaling, and hierarchical architecture.

I 简介Introduction

音乐是人类文化的基本支柱，歌曲以其独特的方式融合了富有表现力的声乐和丰富的器乐伴奏来传达创作者的情感和思想。 在人工智能生成内容（AIGC）快速发展的推动下，自动音乐生成已成为学术界和工业界的活跃研究课题。 虽然在特定子领域（包括符号音乐生成）取得了显着进展Music serves as a fundamental pillar of human culture, with songs standing out by uniquely blending expressive vocals and rich instrumental accompaniment to convey the emotions and thoughts of creators. Driven by rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence Generated Content (AIGC), automated music generation has become an active research topic in both academia and industry. While remarkable progress has been made in specific sub-domains—including symbolic music generation[48, 10, 34, 50]、器乐的产生, instrumental music generation[2, 36, 8, 19, 29]、歌声合成, and singing voice synthesis[5, 15, 53, 27, 13]——自动生成一首完整的歌曲仍然是一个巨大的挑战。 它需要生成模型来同时合成和无缝集成富有表现力的人声和高保真乐器曲目，同时保留音乐性并遵循指令。 当生成完整长度的歌曲时，这项任务变得更加复杂，其中保持歌词对齐和长期结构连贯性变得更加困难。—automatically generating a complete song remains a substantial challenge. It requires generative models to simultaneously synthesize and seamlessly integrate expressive vocals and high-fidelity instrumental tracks while preserving musicality and following instructions. This task is further complicated when generating full-length songs, where maintaining lyric alignment and long-term structural coherence become more demanding.

歌曲生成的早期尝试，例如 JukeboxEarly attempts in song generation, such as Jukebox[9]，利用语言模型（LM）来预测从人声和伴奏的组合音频中提取的离散代码（称为“混合标记”）。 后续研究, utilized language models (LMs) to predict discrete codes—referred to as “mixed tokens”—extracted from the combined audio of vocals and accompaniment. Subsequent studies[23, 20, 46, 16, 47]进一步优化 LM 架构和建模范式，以增强整体音乐性。 然而，基于混合代币的方法表现出严重的局限性。 具体来说，受限的离散词汇和音轨的相互声学掩蔽掩盖了细粒度的细节并阻碍了高保真度的生成。 为了解决这个问题，Yufurther optimized LM architectures and modeling paradigms to enhance overall musicality. Nevertheless, mixed token-based approaches exhibit critical limitations. Specifically, the restricted discrete vocabulary and the mutual acoustic masking of audio tracks obscure fine-grained details and hinder high-fidelity generation. To address this, YuE[51]引入了双轨令牌预测策略来分别对人声和伴奏轨道进行建模。 松根introduces a dual-track token prediction strategy to separately model vocal and accompaniment tracks. SongGen[28]扩展了这个想法，并表明交错令牌预测策略有效地减少了不同令牌类型之间的干扰。 虽然这些方法产生了高质量的单独音轨，但它们的独立预测机制通常难以维持声乐与乐器的和谐。 此外，交错范式极大地增加了序列长度，严重限制了模型的可扩展性，并加剧了保持音乐性和遵循指令的难度。 同时，基于扩散的系统，例如 DiffRhythmextends this idea and shows that the interleaved token prediction strategy effectively reduces interference between different token types. While these methods produce high-quality individual tracks, their independent prediction mechanisms often struggle to maintain vocal-instrument harmony. Furthermore, the interleaved paradigm drastically increases the sequence length, severely limiting model scalability and exacerbating the difficulty of maintaining musicality and following instructions. Concurrently, diffusion-based systems, exemplified by the DiffRhythm[32, 4, 17]和 ACE-Stepand ACE-Step[12, 11]系列，避免离散标记，但在准确捕获时间音素声学对齐和保持全长歌曲的长期结构一致性方面遇到挑战。 最近的行业歌曲生成工具series, avoid discrete tokens but encounter challenges in accurately capturing temporal phoneme-acoustic alignments and maintaining long-term structural consistency across full-length songs. Recent industry song generation tools[37, 41, 31, 30]也出现了，但这些系统都没有披露其方法或培训策略。have also emerged, but none of these systems has disclosed its methodology or training strategy.

除了架构限制之外，歌曲生成社区长期以来一直受到高质量数据稀缺和嘈杂注释的限制。 一方面，虽然在此类数据集上训练的模型可以成功地适应一般音乐分布，但它们缺乏使生成的歌曲与人类偏好保持一致所需的音乐性先验。 另一方面，不可靠的数据注释会损害模型的指令跟随能力，导致抒情幻觉和不稳定的可控性。 为了缓解这些问题，最近的研究转向了偏好调整。 差异节奏+Beyond architectural limitations, the song generation community has long been constrained by the scarcity of high-quality data and noisy annotations. On the one hand, while models trained on such datasets can successfully fit the general music distribution, they lack the musicality priors required to align the generated songs with human preferences. On the other hand, unreliable data annotations impair the model’s instruction following capabilities, leading to lyrical hallucinations and unstable controllability. To mitigate these issues, recent studies have turned to preference alignment. DiffRhythm+[4]采用直接偏好优化 (DPO) 来增强音乐性，而 LeVoemploys Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to enhance musicality, while LeVo[22], 差异节奏 2, DiffRhythm 2[17]、和心穆拉, and HeartMuLa[47]进一步探索多偏好调整策略，以同时提高音乐性和遵循指令的能力。 然而，这些现有方法存在两个局限性。 首先，同时优化多个偏好目标通常会引起梯度冲突。 不同目标之间的相互干扰可能会导致平均效应，从而限制了各个个体能力的上限。 其次，这些方法完全依赖于静态、离线配对数据集。 这不仅限制了用于数据集构建的预训练模型的性能上限，而且还使对齐过程容易受到奖励黑客攻击。further explore multi-preference alignment strategies to simultaneously improve both musicality and instruction-following abilities. Nevertheless, these existing approaches exhibit two limitations. First, simultaneously optimizing multiple preference objectives often induces gradient conflicts. The mutual interference between different objectives may lead to an averaging effect, which restricts the upper bound of each individual capability. Second, these methods rely exclusively on static, offline paired datasets. This not only restricts the model’s performance upper bound within the capabilities of the pre-trained models used for dataset construction, but also renders the alignment process susceptible to reward hacking.

LeVo 2 将全局歌曲一致性和轨道级声学细节之间的权衡视为分层建模问题。 其核心组件 LeLM 通过两个协调的标记预测任务将全局语义规划与特定轨道的声学细化分开。 混合语义 LM 预测混合标记以捕获歌曲级音乐结构，包括旋律、节奏、节奏和声乐乐器协调。 以全局语义规划器的隐藏状态为条件，特定于轨道的 LM 并行预测声音和伴奏标记，恢复细粒度的声学细节，同时避免交错双轨预测引起的序列长度扩展。 然后，基于扩散的音乐编解码器将预测的标记重建为完整长度的歌曲波形。LeVo 2 treats the trade-off between global song coherence and track-level acoustic detail as a hierarchical modeling problem. Its core component, LeLM, separates global semantic planning from track-specific acoustic refinement through two coordinated token prediction tasks. The Mixed Semantic LM predicts mixed tokens to capture song-level musical structure, including melody, rhythm, tempo, and vocal-instrument coordination. Conditioned on the hidden states of this global semantic planner, the Track-Specific LM predicts vocal and accompaniment tokens in parallel, recovering fine-grained acoustic details while avoiding the sequence-length expansion caused by interleaved dual-track prediction. The diffusion-based Music Codec then reconstructs the predicted tokens into full-length song waveforms.

LeVo 2 的主要训练贡献是用于全长歌曲生成的解耦、美学引导的对齐策略。 训练可控歌曲生成器需要不同的学习信号来实现音乐性、歌词对齐、提示一致性和曲目级声学细化。 LeVo 2 将这些目标分配到不同的阶段，而不是在单一偏好目标下对其进行优化。 在预训练过程中，LeVo 2会缩放模型容量和训练数据，加入纯器乐来加强伴奏建模，并使用自动化的音乐审美评估框架来分配音乐性等级条件。 这些条件在偏好调整之前提供了音乐性先验。 在渐进式后训练过程中，SFT 首先将生成分布缩小到高质量歌曲，然后大规模离线 DPO 提高歌词对齐和提示一致性，闭环半在线 DPO 使用不断演化的策略样本进一步提高音乐性。 该计划减轻了优化冲突和静态离线偏好对的限制。 最后，模块化扩展训练 Track-Specific LM 来完善人声和伴奏声学效果，同时保留一致的全局语义规划器。The main training contribution of LeVo 2 is a decoupled, aesthetics-guided alignment strategy for full-length song generation. Training a controllable song generator requires different learning signals for musicality, lyric alignment, prompt consistency, and track-level acoustic refinement. LeVo 2 assigns these objectives to separate stages instead of optimizing them under a single preference objective. During pre-training, LeVo 2 scales model capacity and training data, includes pure instrumental music to strengthen accompaniment modeling, and uses an automated music aesthetic evaluation framework to assign musicality-tier conditions. These conditions provide musicality priors before preference alignment. During progressive post-training, SFT first narrows the generation distribution toward high-quality songs, large-scale offline DPO then improves lyric alignment and prompt consistency, and closed-loop semi-online DPO further improves musicality using samples from the evolving policy. This schedule mitigates optimization conflict and the limitations of static offline preference pairs. Finally, modular extension trains the Track-Specific LM to refine vocal and accompaniment acoustics while preserving the aligned global semantic planner.

Ⅱ-AII-A 音乐一代Music Generation

音乐生成的早期努力主要集中于符号音乐的生成Early efforts in music generation primarily focused on symbolic music generation[10, 43]。 然而，象征音乐本质上缺乏表现力的细微差别，例如音色、清晰度和演奏动态，而这些对于高保真和悠扬的音乐合成至关重要。 由大型语言模型 (LLM) 的扩展行为和推理能力驱动. However, symbolic music inherently lacks the expressive nuances, such as timbre, articulation, and performance dynamics, which are crucial for high-fidelity and melodious music synthesis. Driven by the scaling behaviors and reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs)[39, 1, 40], 多项研究, several studies[3, 2, 8]已经转向端到端的音乐生成。 这些方法通常通过矢量量化变分自动编码器 (VQ-VAE) 将音乐离散化为标记序列have shifted toward end-to-end music generation. These approaches typically discretize music into token sequences via Vector Quantised-Variational Autoencoders (VQ-VAE)[42]或残差矢量量化 (RVQ)or Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ)[52, 18]用于语言模型处理。 尽管取得了成功，但这些模型的性能常常受到量化损失的限制。for language model processing. Despite their success, the performance of these models is often constrained by quantization loss.

为了解决这个问题，采用扩散模型对连续表示进行建模，证明了高保真合成的有效性To address this, diffusion models have been adopted for modeling continuous representations, demonstrating effectiveness in high-fidelity synthesis[33, 35]。 然而，随着序列长度和模型大小的增加，这些方法在长上下文生成中面临着巨大的计算挑战，使得捕获时间音素声学对齐并保持全长音乐的长期结构一致性变得困难。 最近，混合架构如MeLoDy. Ⅲ-DIII-D 美学引导的三阶段训练范式Aesthetics-Guided Three-Stage Training Paradigm

为了提高分层 LeLM 架构的性能，我们提出了一种美学引导的三阶段训练范式：预训练、渐进式后训练和模块化扩展。 该范例明确划分了两个模块的职责：混合语义 LM 专注于对全局语义进行建模，以确保整体音乐性和声乐乐器和谐，而轨道特定 LM 则对各个轨道的声学细节进行建模。 因此，我们首先对混合语义LM进行预训练，以建立其全局语义规划能力。随后，通过渐进的后期训练，我们进一步调整其指令遵循和音乐性先验，产生悠扬且稳定的基础乐曲。 最后，Track-Specific LM 经过训练以补充细粒度的声学细节，以优化局部性能、提高音质并丰富细粒度的细微差别。To improve the performance of the hierarchical LeLM architecture, we propose an aesthetics-guided three-stage training paradigm: pre-training, progressive post-training, and modular extension. This paradigm explicitly divides the responsibilities of two modules: the Mixed Semantic LM focuses on modeling global semantics to ensure overall musicality and vocal-instrument harmony, while the Track-Specific LM models the acoustic details of individual tracks. Therefore, we first pre-train the Mixed Semantic LM to establish its global semantic planning capabilities. Subsequently, through progressive post-training, we further align its instruction-following and musicality priors, producing melodious and stable base compositions. Finally, the Track-Specific LM is trained to supplement fine-grained acoustic details to optimize local performance, improve sound quality, and enrich fine-grained nuances.

与仅依赖于扩展数据的方法不同，我们的范例强调不断注入音乐性先验知识。 为了实现这一目标，我们引入了一个自动化的音乐审美评估框架，它充当客观的“法官”，在整个模型的训练周期中指导数据利用和人类偏好优化。Unlike approaches that rely solely on scaling up data, our paradigm emphasizes the continuous injection of musicality prior knowledge. To achieve this, we introduce an automated music aesthetic evaluation framework, which acts as an objective “judge” to guide data utilization and human preference optimization throughout the model’s training cycle.

Ⅲ-DIII-D1 自动化音乐审美评估框架Automated Music Aesthetic Evaluation Framework

为了量化音乐的质量，我们受 SongEval 启发，开发了一个全面的审美评估框架To quantify the quality of music, we develop a comprehensive aesthetic evaluation framework inspired by SongEval[49]。 该框架利用从自监督学习模型 MuQ 中提取的表示. The framework leverages the representations extracted from the self-supervised learning model MuQ[54]预测歌曲的多个性能指标。 它通过一个有凝聚力的层次结构来评估歌曲的生成：首先检查基本的声学特性（人声和伴奏音质），然后评估直接影响主观聆听体验的维度（旋律、结构、编曲和混音），最后通过音乐性维度捕捉整体艺术共鸣。 该框架在由顶级模型生成的 11,717 个样本组成的多样化数据集上进行训练，表现出与人类专家评分的极高相关性。to predict multiple performance metrics of a song. It evaluates song generation through a cohesive hierarchy: first examining fundamental acoustic properties (Vocal and Accompaniment Sound Quality), then assessing dimensions that directly impact the subjective listening experience (Melody, Structure, Arrangement, and Mixing), and finally capturing the overall artistic resonance through a Musicality dimension. Trained on a diverse dataset of 11,717 samples generated by top-tier models, this framework demonstrates exceptionally high correlation with human expert ratings.

Ⅲ-DIII-D2 第一阶段：审美预训练Stage 1: Aesthetics-Conditioned Pre-training

在第一阶段，混合语义 LM 在大规模音乐数据上进行训练，以调整各种条件输入和混合标记之间的模式。 在此阶段，特定于轨道的 LM 被冻结，使模型能够仅专注于学习全局语义和声乐乐器和声。 为了确保稳健性，音频提示和文本描述都会经历 50% 的随机下降。 为了促进有效的学习并捕获长期的结构依赖性，我们采用了长度增量学习策略。 训练从较短的音频片段开始，逐渐增加序列长度，最终对全长歌曲数据进行训练。In the first stage, the Mixed Semantic LM is trained on large-scale music data to align the modalities between the various conditioning inputs and the mixed tokens. During this phase, the Track-Specific LM is frozen, allowing the model to focus solely on learning global semantics and vocal-instrument harmony. To ensure robustness, both audio prompts and text descriptions undergo a 50% random drop. To facilitate effective learning and capture long-term structural dependencies, we employ a length-incremental learning strategy. The training begins with shorter audio segments and progressively increases the sequence length, ultimately training on full-length song data.

为了注入音乐性先验，所有训练数据都通过我们的美学框架进行评估。 根据计算出的平均分和其类别内的相对排名，我们使用百分位数阈值将数据分为五个离散的音乐性等级：前 5%、5%-25%、25%-50%、50%-75% 和剩余的后 25%。 分配对应的“音乐性等级标签”作为附加条件。 这种机制使模型能够从海量、长尾噪声数据中学习核心声学和语义知识，而不会影响其生成能力。 此外，它还缓解了“一对多”映射问题，减少了条件分布熵以加速收敛，并在推理过程中释放了音乐感知无分类器指导（CFG）的能力。To inject musicality priors, all training data is evaluated by our aesthetic framework. Based on the calculated average score and relative ranking within its category, we divide the data into five discrete musicality tiers using percentile thresholds: Top 5%, 5%-25%, 25%-50%, 50%-75%, and the remaining bottom 25%. The corresponding “musicality tier tag” is assigned as an additional condition. This mechanism enables the model to learn core acoustic and semantic knowledge from massive, long-tail noisy data without compromising its generation capabilities. Furthermore, it mitigates the “one-to-many” mapping problem, reducing conditional distribution entropy to accelerate convergence, and unlocks the capability for musicality-aware Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) during inference.

Ⅲ-DIII-D3 第 2 阶段：解耦多阶段渐进式后训练Stage 2: Decoupled Multi-Stage Progressive Post-training

在扩展模型以捕获双轨声学细节之前，我们首先将混合语义 LM 与人类偏好保持一致。 在这个阶段，美学框架从调节工具转变为SFT的数据过滤器和DPO的奖励模型。 语义级偏好对齐减少了抒情幻觉并提高了模型的音乐性，同时降低了计算成本。 这一渐进调整过程的详细机制在第 1 节中详细阐述。Before expanding the model to capture dual-track acoustic details, we first align the Mixed Semantic LM with human preferences. In this stage, the aesthetic framework transitions from a conditioning tool to a data filter for SFT and a reward model for DPO. Semantic-level preference alignment reduces lyrical hallucinations and improves the model’s musicality while lowering computational cost. The detailed mechanisms of this progressive alignment process are elaborated in SectionIII-E.

Ⅲ-DIII-D4 第三阶段：以审美为条件的模块化拓展训练Stage 3: Aesthetics-Conditioned Modular Extension Training

在对齐的混合语义 LM 的基础上，我们冻结其权重并专门训练特定于轨道的 LM 来建模双轨标记，而不会破坏之前建立的语义和音乐性先验。 与第一阶段一致，美学层标签被注入到前缀上下文中，确保细粒度细微差别的并行建模保持与高质量音乐标准保持一致。Building upon the aligned Mixed Semantic LM, we freeze its weights and exclusively train the Track-Specific LM to model dual-track tokens without disrupting the previously established semantic and musicality priors. Consistent with Stage 1, the aesthetic tier tags are injected into the prefix context, ensuring that the parallel modeling of fine-grained nuances remains aligned with the high-quality musical standards.

在此阶段，我们最初采用教师强制方案，利用直接从真实歌曲中提取的混合标记作为输入来预测相应的双轨标记。 一旦训练稳定，我们就会引入声学增强策略。具体来说，我们使用 Music Codec 解码器仅通过 2 到 4 个扩散步骤将输入混合令牌重建为低质量音频，然后从该降级音频中提取混合令牌。 这种对声学细节的故意破坏迫使特定于轨道的 LM 学习如何恢复和补充细粒度的声学细微差别、优化本地轨道性能并提高整体音质。During this stage, we initially employ a teacher-forcing scheme, utilizing mixed tokens extracted directly from real songs as inputs to predict the corresponding dual-track tokens. Once the training stabilizes, we introduce an acoustic augmentation strategy. Specifically, we reconstruct the input mixed tokens into low-quality audio using the Music Codec decoder with only 2 to 4 diffusion steps, and subsequently extract the mixed tokens from this degraded audio. This intentional corruption of acoustic details forces the Track-Specific LM to learn how to recover and supplement fine-grained acoustic nuances, optimize local track performance, and improve overall sound quality.

Ⅲ-EIII-E 解耦多阶段渐进式后训练Decoupled Multi-Stage Progressive Post-training

尽管前两个阶段为 LeLM 提供了基础建模能力，但由于缺乏高质量数据和嘈杂的注释，该模型在音乐性和指令遵循方面仍然面临挑战。 因此，与人类偏好的多维对齐对于稳定而优美的歌曲生成仍然至关重要。 最近在多偏好对齐方面的尝试面临两个局限性：（1）同时优化不同的目标（例如客观可控性和主观音乐性）常常会引起梯度冲突，导致平均效应限制每种能力的上限； (2) 依赖静态离线数据集限制了模型的潜力，并增加了奖励黑客攻击的可能性。 我们使用解耦的多阶段渐进式后训练方案来进行这种调整。它将优化目标分为三个阶段：监督微调（SFT）缩小数据分布并建立高质量的生成基线，大规模离线DPO提高可控性并减少抒情幻觉，闭环半在线DPO提高艺术表现力。Although the first two stages equip LeLM with base modeling capabilities, the model still faces challenges in musicality and instruction following due to the scarcity of high-quality data and noisy annotations. Therefore, multi-dimensional alignment with human preferences remains essential for stable and melodious song generation. Recent attempts at multi-preference alignment face two limitations: (1) simultaneously optimizing diverse objectives (e.g., objective controllability and subjective musicality) often induces gradient conflicts, leading to an averaging effect that restricts the upper bound of each capability; and (2) relying on static offline datasets caps the model’s potential and increases susceptibility to reward hacking. We use a decoupled multi-stage progressive post-training scheme for this alignment. It separates the optimization objectives into three phases: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) narrows the data distribution and establishes a high-quality generation baseline, large-scale offline DPO improves controllability and reduces lyrical hallucinations, and closed-loop semi-online DPO improves artistic expressiveness.

Ⅲ-EIII-E1 监督微调 (SFT)Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)

在偏好对齐之前，我们建立一个基础生成模型以加速收敛并保持训练稳定性。 为了提高与最高音乐性层相关的生成质量，我们采用自动音乐美学评估框架作为严格的数据过滤器。 与预训练中使用的更广泛的前 5% 阈值不同，SFT 根据平均审美分数仅保留每个类别前 0.5% 的歌曲。 通过根据最高音乐性标签调节训练数据，我们有效地缩小了输出方差，并确保模型本质上有利于顶级音乐性。Before preference alignment, we establish a base generation model to accelerate convergence and maintain training stability. To improve the generation quality associated with the highest musicality tier, we employ our automated music aesthetic evaluation framework as a strict data filter. Different from the broader top 5% threshold used in pre-training, SFT retains only the top 0.5% of songs per category based on average aesthetic scores. By conditioning the training data on the highest musicality-tier tag, we effectively narrow the output variance and ensure the model inherently favors top-tier musicality.

Ⅲ-EIII-E2 大规模离线 DPO 实现可控性Large-Scale Offline DPO for Controllability

虽然 SFT 提高了整体质量，但该模型仍然可能会出现抒情幻觉和不稳定的指令遵循。 为了解决这个问题，我们利用大规模离线DPO，它通过大规模采样和严格的配对过滤来增强可控性和稳定性，效率高、成本低。 具体来说，我们通过大型语言模型生成 150,000 首不同的歌词，并从预定义的数据集中为每首歌词随机分配音频提示及其相应的文本描述。 然后，我们在三种不同的条件下为每个歌词生成八个候选样本：仅文本描述、仅音频提示、以及文本描述和音频提示。While SFT improves overall quality, the model may still suffer from lyrical hallucinations and unstable instruction following. To address this, we leverage large-scale offline DPO, which is highly efficient and cost-effective for enforcing controllability and stability through large-scale sampling and stringent paired filtering. Specifically, we generate 150,000 diverse lyrics via a large language model and randomly assign an audio prompt with its corresponding text description from a predefined dataset to each lyric. We then generate eight candidate samples per lyric under three distinct conditions: only the text description, only the audio prompt, and both the text description and the audio prompt.

评价重点关注三个核心维度：歌词对齐、提示一致性、审美评分。为了检测文本幻觉（例如，单词替换、插入或删除），我们使用自动语音识别（ASR）模型提取转录，将其转换为音素序列，并计算与目标序列的编辑距离，标记具有密集局部异常的样本。 使用 MuQ-MuLan 测量即时一致性The evaluation focuses on three core dimensions: lyric alignment, prompt consistency, and aesthetic score. To detect text hallucinations (e.g., word substitution, insertion, or deletion), we extract transcriptions using an Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) model, convert them into phoneme sequences, and compute the edit distance against the target sequence, flagging samples with dense local anomalies. Prompt consistency is measured using the MuQ-MuLan[54]模型根据文本描述的歌曲数据进行微调，而审美得分则来自我们的审美框架的平均维度得分。model fine-tuned on text-described song data, while the aesthetic score is derived from the average dimension scores of our aesthetic framework.

为了防止模型从低质量样本或噪声中学习，我们丢弃了按相似度和美观分数排名的最后 10% 的样本。 在偏好对构建过程中，我们优先考虑可控性。对于有幻觉的样本，我们选择在相同条件下生成的无幻觉、音乐性较高且相似度不下降的样本作为获胜样本。 对于相似度较低的样本，我们将它们与相似度提高至少 0.15、没有幻觉并保持音乐性的获胜样本配对。 此外，如果两个样本表现出至少 0.5 的音乐性差距（该阈值对应于我们的人类评估比较中 90% 的专家一致率），只要获胜样本没有幻觉并且显示出相同或改进的提示相似性，我们就将这一对纳入其中。 这种分层配对明确惩罚无法控制的行为，减少抒情幻觉并提高即时一致性。To prevent the model from learning from low-quality samples or noise, we discard the bottom 10% of samples ranked by similarity and aesthetic scores. During preference pair construction, we prioritize controllability. For hallucinated samples, we select a hallucination-free sample generated under the same condition with higher musicality and no drop in similarity as the winning sample. For samples with low similarity, we pair them with a winning sample that exhibits a similarity improvement of at least 0.15, lacks hallucinations, and maintains musicality. Furthermore, if two samples exhibit a musicality gap of at least 0.5—a threshold corresponding to a 90% expert agreement rate in our human evaluation comparison—we include this pair provided the winning sample is hallucination-free and shows equal or improved prompt similarity. This hierarchical pairing explicitly penalizes uncontrollable behaviors, reducing lyrical hallucinations and improving prompt consistency.

Ⅲ-EIII-E3 音乐性闭环半在线 DPOClosed-Loop Semi-Online DPO for Musicality

离线阶段之后，该模型表现出稳定的指令控制。 然而，离线算法受到用于构建预定义数据集的模型能力的限制，并且随着训练的进行，模型的策略逐渐偏离静态生成分布，导致优化效率下降。 受到强化学习最新进展的启发Following the offline stage, the model demonstrates stable instruction control. However, offline algorithms are bounded by the capabilities of the models used to construct the pre-defined datasets, and as training progresses, the model’s policy gradually deviates from the static generation distribution, leading to a degradation in optimization efficacy. Inspired by recent advances in reinforcement learning[21]，我们引入闭环半在线DPO方案。 这种方法减少了离线算法的局限性，提高了音乐性，同时避免了在线采样的计算成本。, we introduce a closed-loop semi-online DPO scheme. This approach reduces the limitations of offline algorithms and improves musicality while avoiding the computational costs of online sampling.

在此阶段，我们采用定期更新的生成器模型来动态采样新数据。 为了保持渐进式对齐，生成器每 100 步将其权重与训练模型同步一次。 我们利用大型语言模型生成 160,000 首新歌词，生成器为每个输入生成 10 个候选样本。 由于基础可控性在前一阶段得到了解决，偏好对构建现在侧重于音乐性。 对于每个输入，我们选择表现出最大音乐性得分差距的对，要求该差距大于 0.2——该阈值对应于我们的人类评估比较中 73% 的专家一致率。 至关重要的是，为了防止回归，我们强制获胜样本必须保持无幻觉，并且具有高于被拒绝样本的指令相似性得分。In this phase, we employ a periodically updated generator model to dynamically sample new data. To maintain a progressive alignment, the generator synchronizes its weights with the training model every 100 steps. We utilize a large language model to generate 160,000 new lyrics, and the generator produces 10 candidate samples for each input. Since base controllability is resolved in the previous stage, preference pair construction now focuses on musicality. For each input, we select the pair exhibiting the maximum musicality score gap, mandating that this gap be greater than 0.2—a threshold corresponding to a 73% expert agreement rate in our human evaluation comparison. Crucially, to prevent regression, we enforce that the winning sample must remain hallucination-free and possess an instruction similarity score higher than the rejected sample.

通过从逐步改进的策略中不断生成新样本，该模型减少了对静态数据分布的依赖。 此外，为了防止奖励黑客攻击，我们实施了混合验证协议。 该协议将人类评估与多种美学评估模型相结合，以监控训练进度并提高音乐性，同时保持稳定性。By continuously generating fresh samples from the progressively improving policy, the model reduces its dependence on the static data distribution. Furthermore, to prevent reward hacking, we implement a hybrid validation protocol. This protocol combines human evaluation with multiple aesthetic assessment models to monitor the training progress and improve musicality while preserving stability.

训练和推理细节Training and Inference Details

在 LeLM 的训练过程中，我们使用 64 个 NVIDIA H20 GPU，每个 GPU 的批量大小为 2。 训练过程遵循我们的三阶段范式：600,000 个步骤用于预训练；接下来是解耦后训练阶段，包括 5,000 步 SFT、5,000 步大规模离线 DPO 和 5,000 步闭环半在线 DPO；最后，200,000 步进行美观的模块化扩展。 在推理阶段，我们采用 top-k 采样，k 设置为 5000，温度为 0.8。 此外，为了引导模型实现高质量生成，我们应用了比例因子为 1.5 的音乐感知 CFG。During the training of LeLM, we utilize 64 NVIDIA H20 GPUs with a batch size of 2 per GPU. The training process follows our three-stage paradigm: 600,000 steps for pre-training; followed by a decoupled post-training phase consisting of 5,000 steps for SFT, 5,000 steps for large-scale offline DPO, and 5,000 steps for closed-loop semi-online DPO; and finally, 200,000 steps for aesthetics-conditioned modular extension. During the inference phase, we employ top-k sampling with k set to 5000 and a temperature of 0.8. Furthermore, to guide the model toward high-quality generation, we apply the musicality-aware CFG with a scale factor of 1.5.

主观评价Subjective Evaluations

考虑到音乐美感和音质很难用自动化模型准确评估，我们依赖专家对这些维度的主观评估。 我们设计了六维平均意见得分（MOS）听力测试。它由四个主观艺术指标组成：整体音乐性（OVL）、旋律（MEL）、编曲（ARR）和结构（STR）；以及两个声学指标：人声音质 (SQ-V) 和伴奏音质 (SQ-A)。我们请 20 名音乐专业人士对每个模型生成的 100 首中文歌曲和 100 首英文歌曲进行评估，对每个维度进行从 1 到 10 的细粒度评分。为了保证客观性，没有评估者参与模型的开发或训练。在测试过程中，参与者被要求聆听不同模型在相同输入条件下生成的完整长度的歌曲。这种比较设置可确保所得分数准确反映系统之间的相对性能差异。Considering that musical aesthetics and sound quality are difficult to evaluate accurately using automated models, we rely on expert subjective evaluations for these dimensions. We designed a six-dimensional Mean Opinion Score (MOS) listening test. To guarantee objectivity, none of the evaluators were involved in the model’s development or training. During the test, participants were asked to listen to complete full-length songs generated by different models conditioned on the same input. This comparative setup ensures the resulting scores accurately reflect the relative performance differences between the systems.

VI 讨论和道德声明Discussion and Ethics Statement

尽管有强有力的实证结果，歌曲生成仍然是一项具有挑战性的任务。首先，尽管 LeVo 2 在我们的评估中优于所有学术基线，但其音频质量仍然受到训练数据的可变性和规模的限制。因此，LeVo 2 与最强的专有系统之间仍然存在差距。其次，带注释的音乐数据的稀缺性和高成本导致我们严重依赖开源模型进行伪标记，这不可避免地影响了模型的指令跟踪能力。一方面，由于文本描述是使用Qwen2-Audio生成的，有效提示的多样性和丰富性受到限制。另一方面，歌词提取、结构识别和音乐字幕中累积的错误会给监督过程带来噪音，并降低指令对齐的准确性。这些因素解释了为什么尽管我们的渐进式训练后策略提高了可控性和音乐性，但与顶级专有系统相比，LeVo 2 仍然显示出可控性差距。Despite the strong empirical results, song generation remains a challenging task. First, although LeVo 2 outperforms all academic baselines in our evaluation, its audio quality is still constrained by the variability and scale of the training data. As a result, a gap remains between LeVo 2 and the strongest proprietary systems. Second, the scarcity and high cost of annotated music data lead us to rely heavily on open-source models for pseudo-labeling, which inevitably affects the model’s instruction-following ability. On the one hand, because text descriptions are generated using Qwen2-Audio, the diversity and richness of effective prompts are limited. On the other hand, accumulated errors in lyric extraction, structure recognition, and music captioning introduce noise into the supervision process and reduce the accuracy of instruction alignment. These factors explain why, although our progressive post-training strategy improves both controllability and musicality, LeVo 2 still shows a controllability gap compared with top proprietary systems.

我们充分认识到与音乐生成模型相关的潜在道德风险。本工作中使用的所有模型和训练数据仅用于学术研究目的。我们尊重原创艺术家和内容创作者的知识产权，并采取合理措施避免在未经适当授权的情况下使用受版权保护的材料。We fully recognize the potential ethical risks associated with music generation models. All models and training data used in this work are intended only for academic research purposes. 七VII结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 LeVo 2，这是一种新颖的混合 LLM-Diffusion 框架，专为稳定而悠扬的全长歌曲生成而设计。我们引入了分层表示建模架构，将生成过程解耦为用于全局语义编排的混合语义 LM 和用于并行双轨声学细化的轨道特定 LM。此外，为了克服多偏好对齐中高质量数据稀缺和梯度冲突带来的挑战，我们提出了一种美学引导的三阶段训练范式，具有解耦的渐进式训练后策略。通过依次集成监督微调（SFT）、大规模离线DPO和闭环半在线DPO，我们的框架成功地减轻了抒情幻觉，并超越了静态数据集的限制，最大限度地提高了艺术表现力。广泛的主观和客观评估表明，LeVo 2 始终优于最先进的开源模型，并缩小了与专有系统的差距。我们发布模型权重和代码，以促进自动化高保真音乐生成的未来进步。In this paper, we present LeVo 2, a novel hybrid LLM-Diffusion framework designed for stable and melodious full-length song generation. We introduce a hierarchical representation modeling architecture to decouple the generation process into a Mixed Semantic LM for global semantic orchestration and a Track-Specific LM for parallel dual-track acoustic refinement. Furthermore, to overcome the challenges posed by the scarcity of high-quality data and gradient conflicts in multi-preference alignment, we propose an aesthetics-guided three-stage training paradigm featuring a decoupled progressive post-training strategy. By sequentially integrating Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), large-scale offline DPO, and closed-loop semi-online DPO, our framework successfully mitigates lyrical hallucinations and maximizes artistic expressiveness beyond the limitations of static datasets. Extensive subjective and objective evaluations demonstrate that LeVo 2 consistently outperforms state-of-the-art open-source models and narrows the gap to proprietary systems. We release our model weights and code to facilitate future advancements in automated high-fidelity music generation.
