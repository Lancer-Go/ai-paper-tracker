摘要Abstract

统一的多模态理解/生成模型通过将细粒度的理解纳入其思想链 (CoT) 流程，显示出改进的图像编辑性能。 然而，一个关键问题仍未得到充分探索：什么形式的 CoT 和训练策略可以共同增强理解粒度和泛化能力？为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 Meta-CoT，这是一种对任何单图像编辑操作执行两级分解的范例，具有两个关键属性：Unified multi-modal understanding/generative models have shown improved image editing performance by incorporating fine-grained understanding into their Chain-of-Thought (CoT) process. However, a critical question remains underexplored: what forms of CoT and training strategy can jointly enhance both the understanding granularity and generalization? To address this, we propose Meta-CoT, a paradigm that performs a two-level decomposition of any single-image editing operation with two key properties:(1)可分解性。(1) Decomposability.我们观察到任何编辑意图都可以表示为三元组——（任务、目标、所需的理解能力）。受此启发，Meta-CoT 对编辑任务和目标进行分解，生成特定于任务的 CoT，并遍历所有目标上的编辑操作。这种分解增强了模型对编辑操作的理解粒度，并引导其在训练过程中学习三元组的每个元素，从而大幅提高了编辑能力。We observe that any editing intention can be represented as a triplet — (task, target, required understanding ability). Inspired by this, Meta-CoT decomposes both the editing task and the target, generating task-specific CoT and traversing editing operations on all targets. This decomposition enhances the model’s understanding granularity of editing operations and guides it to learn each element of the triplet during training, substantially improving the editing capability.(2)普遍性。(2) Generalizability.在第二个分解级别中，我们进一步将编辑任务分解为五个基本元任务。我们发现，对这五个元任务以及三元组的其他两个元素的训练足以在不同的、看不见的编辑任务中实现强泛化。 为了进一步使模型的编辑行为与其 CoT 推理保持一致，我们引入了In the second decomposition level, we further break down editing tasks into five fundamental meta-tasks. We find that training on these five meta-tasks, together with the other two elements of the triplet, is sufficient to achieve strong generalization across diverse, unseen editing tasks. To further align the model’s editing behavior with its CoT reasoning, we introduce theCoT-编辑一致性奖励CoT-Editing Consistency Reward，这鼓励在编辑过程中更准确、更有效地利用 CoT 信息。实验表明，我们的方法在 21 个编辑任务中实现了 15.8% 的总体改进，并且当仅在一小部分元任务上进行训练时，可以有效地推广到未见过的编辑任务。我们的代码、基准测试和模型发布于, which encourages more accurate and effective utilization of CoT information during editing. Experiments demonstrate that our method achieves an overall 15.8% improvement across 21 editing tasks, and generalizes effectively to unseen editing tasks when trained on only a small set of meta-tasks. 1 简介Introduction

最近的研究表明，思想链（CoT）可以有效增强统一生成/理解模型中的编辑性能Recent studies show that Chain-of-Thought (CoT) can effectively enhance editing performance in unified generation/understanding models[11, 8]。通过明确地推理编辑过程，模型激活其理解能力并产生更准确的编辑。我们认为，有效的图像编辑 CoT 范式应该具有两个属性：. By explicitly reasoning the editing process, the model activates its understanding ability and produces more accurate edits. We argue that an effective image editing CoT paradigm should possess two properties:(1)模型理解能力的激发。(1) Stimulation of the model’s understanding ability. (2) 跨不同编辑任务的泛化。(2) Generalization across diverse editing tasks.

现有的工作主要集中在第一个属性上，例如将空间定位线索或其他显式理解信息引入到CoT中Existing works mainly focus on the first property, such as introducing spatial localization cues or other explicit understanding information into the CoT[11]。然而，这种方法通常表现出有限的通用性，因为针对特定理解形式定制的 CoT 可能无法很好地适应更广泛的编辑任务。例如，基于本地化的 CoT 在风格迁移或视点转换等任务上表现不佳。因此，本文旨在研究一个关键问题：什么形式的CoT和训练策略可以同时增强图像编辑过程中的理解粒度和泛化能力？. However, such approaches often exhibit limited generality since CoTs tailored to specific understanding forms may not adapt well to broader editing tasks. For example, localization-based CoT performs poorly on tasks like style transfer or viewpoint transformation. Therefore, this paper aims to investigate a critical question: what forms of CoT and training strategy can simultaneously enhance understanding granularity and generalization capability during image editing?

For example, the editing instruction “Change the number of puppies to three” involves the task type “Quantity Modification”, editing targets “puppies”, and requires the model to possess understanding abilities in localization and counting. This design offers strong decomposability, enabling the model to optimize over task and target, thereby not only learning to comprehend diverse editing operations but also mastering how to apply distinct editing strategies to different entities. To capture the third element, required understanding ability, of the triplet, we incorporate data from a variety of visual understanding tasks during training, ensuring the model fully learns across all three elements.

2.1 理解/生成模型中的 CoTCoT in Understanding/Generation Models

思想链 (CoT) 推理最初是为了在大型语言模型中引发逐步逻辑推理而引入的，最近已扩展到视觉语言模型 (VLM) 以增强多模态推理Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, originally introduced to elicit step-by-step logical inference in large language models, has recently been extended to vision-language models (VLMs) to enhance multimodal reasoning[82, 59, 58, 20, 40]。早期的努力将视觉信息整合到文本 CoT 中. Early efforts integrated visual information into textual CoT[82, 40]，而后续工作通过将文本推理与视觉标记交织在一起开发了本机多模式 CoT, while subsequent work developed native multimodal CoT by interleaving textual reasoning with visual tokens[14, 63, 19, 50, 12, 29]。 CoT也被应用于图像生成. CoT has also been applied to image generation[21, 15, 10, 23]和编辑and editing[8, 11]。在图像编辑中，一些作品结合了 CoT 来改进指令跟踪和空间推理。例如百吉饼. In image editing, several works have incorporated CoT to improve instruction following and spatial reasoning. For instance, Bagel[8]和GoTand GoT[11]在编辑图像之前生成中间推理步骤，证明视觉理解和生成之间的一致性得到提高。 然而，当前的 CoT 编辑范式过于通用，无法激发模型的理解能力generate intermediate reasoning steps before editing images, demonstrating improved consistency between visual understanding and generation. However, current CoT paradigms for editing are either too generic to stimulate the model’s understanding capacity[8]或过于专业化而无法适应不同的任务or too specialized to adapt across diverse tasks[11]，激励我们开发一种范例，增强图像编辑的理解粒度和泛化能力。, motivating us to develop a paradigm that enhances both the understanding granularity and generalization in image editing.

This is motivated by two observations: (1) after SFT, the model already achieves highly accurate CoTs that should be preserved; (2) training both modules during RL causes unstable optimization and degrades the reasoning ability learned in SFT.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们研究了如何同时增强思想链（CoT）引导的图像编辑的理解粒度和泛化能力的问题。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 Meta-CoT，它首先采用 Triplet Decomposition 从任务和目标角度激发模型的推理能力。此外，我们提出了元任务分解，赋予 Meta-CoT 跨不同编辑场景的强大泛化能力。为了使 CoT 推理与编辑行为保持一致，我们引入了 CoT 编辑一致性奖励。对我们提出的 21 任务基准和 ImgEdit 进行的大量实验表明，我们的方法不仅显着提高了编辑性能，而且对看不见的编辑任务表现出很强的泛化能力。In this paper, we have investigated the problem of how to simultaneously enhance the understanding granularity and generalization capability of Chain-of-Thought (CoT)-guided image editing. To address this, we have presented Meta-CoT, which first employs the Triplet Decomposition to stimulate the model’s reasoning ability from both task and target perspectives. Furthermore, we have proposed the Meta-task Decomposition, which endows Meta-CoT with strong generalization capability across diverse editing scenarios. To align the CoT reasoning with the editing behavior, we have introduced the CoT-Editing Consistency Reward. Extensive experiments on our proposed 21-task benchmark and ImgEdit have demonstrated that our method not only significantly improves editing performance but also exhibits strong generalization to unseen editing tasks.
