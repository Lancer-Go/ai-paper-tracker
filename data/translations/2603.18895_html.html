从准确性到准备就绪：人类与人工智能决策的指标和基准From Accuracy to Readiness: Metrics and Benchmarks for Human–AI Decision-Making

Min Hun Lee mhlee@smu.edu.sg 0000-0003-3506-8972 Singapore Management UniversitySingapore
(2026)
摘要。Abstract.

人工智能 (AI) 系统被部署为人类决策中的协作者。然而，评估实践主要关注模型的准确性，而不是人类与人工智能团队是否准备好安全有效地协作。经验证据表明，许多失败都是由于错误的依赖造成的，包括当人工智能出错时过度使用，以及当人工智能有帮助时使用不足。Artificial intelligence (AI) systems are deployed as collaborators in human decision-making. Yet, evaluation practices focus primarily on model accuracy rather than whether human-AI teams are prepared to collaborate safely and effectively. Empirical evidence shows that many failures arise from miscalibrated reliance, including overuse when AI is wrong and underuse when it is helpful.

本文提出了一个评估以团队准备情况为中心的人类人工智能决策的测量框架。我们引入了由四部分组成的评估指标分类法，涵盖结果、依赖行为、安全信号和随着时间的推移学习，并将这些指标与人类人工智能入门和协作的理解-控制-改进 (U-C-I) 生命周期联系起来。This paper proposes a measurement framework for evaluating human-AI decision-making centered on team readiness. We introduce a four-part taxonomy of evaluation metrics spanning outcomes, reliance behavior, safety signals, and learning over time, and connect these metrics to the Understand–Control–Improve (U–C–I) lifecycle of human-AI onboarding and collaboration.

通过交互跟踪而不是模型属性或自我报告的信任来实施评估，我们的框架可以对校准、错误恢复和治理进行与部署相关的评估。我们的目标是支持关于人类与人工智能准备情况的更多可比基准和累积研究，推进更安全、更负责任的人类与人工智能协作。By operationalizing evaluation through interaction traces rather than model properties or self-reported trust, our framework enables deployment-relevant assessment of calibration, error recovery, and governance. We aim to support more comparable benchmarks and cumulative research on human–AI readiness, advancing safer and more accountable human–AI collaboration.

1. 简介Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 系统越来越多地作为协作者而不是自主决策者部署，支持医疗保健等高风险领域的人类判断Artificial intelligence (AI) systems are increasingly deployed as collaborators rather than autonomous decision-makers, supporting human judgment in high-stakes domains such as healthcare(Cai et al., 2019a; Lee et al., 2020, 2021; Wang et al., 2021)和公共服务and public services(Kuo et al., 2023; Završnik, 2020)。在这些环境中，人工智能系统越来越多地影响人们解释证据、校准信心、分配责任以及最终做出决策的方式. In these settings, AI systems increasingly shape how people interpret evidence, calibrate confidence, allocate responsibility, and ultimately make decisions(Holstein et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2021; Lai et al., 2021; Cai et al., 2021).

在过去的几年里，人机交互（HAI）实证研究表明，仅靠模型性能不足以实现安全有效的人机协作：当用户遵循不正确的建议、忽略正确的建议或在不确定性下应用不一致的干预策略时，即使高度精确的系统也会产生更糟糕的人机交互结果Over the past several years, empirical Human–AI Interaction (HAI) research has demonstrated that model performance alone is insufficient for safe and effective human–AI collaboration: even highly accurate systems can yield worse human–AI outcomes when users follow incorrect advice, ignore correct advice, or apply inconsistent intervention strategies under uncertainty(He et al., 2023; Bansal et al., 2021a; Buçinca et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2023; Lai et al., 2021)。作为对这些发现的补充，对负责任和值得信赖的人工智能的研究强调治理机制，例如监督、可竞争性、审计和跨部署的责任. Complementing these findings, research on accountable and trustworthy AI emphasizes governance mechanisms, such as oversight, contestability, auditing, and responsibility across deployment(Raji et al., 2020; Novelli et al., 2024; Mökander and Floridi, 2022; Kaur et al., 2022; Vashney, 2022; Toreini et al., 2020; Lee and Teo, 2026)。与此同时，可解释的人工智能（XAI）和交互式机器学习研究提出了许多机制——特征归因、示例、反事实、规则和不确定性估计——使模型行为变得可理解. Meanwhile, explainable AI (XAI) and interactive ML research has proposed many mechanisms—feature attributions, examples, counterfactuals, rules, and uncertainty estimates—to make model behavior intelligible(Ribeiro et al., 2016; Arya et al., 2019; Doshi-Velez and Kim, 2017; Wang et al., 2019; Rudin, 2019; Crisan et al., 2022; Guo et al., 2022; Kulesza et al., 2011)。然而，HAI 和 XAI 的经验证据表明，这些技术默认情况下并不能可靠地提高决策质量。相反，它们的效果取决于任务上下文、用户专业知识、时间安排以及与人类直觉和信心的交互. However, empirical evidence across HAI and XAI suggests these techniques do not reliably improve decision quality by default. Instead, their effects depend on task context, user expertise, timing, and interactions with human intuition and confidence(Chen et al., 2023; He et al., 2023; Lai et al., 2021; Buçinca et al., 2021; Lee and Chew, 2023).

尽管在机制上取得了进展，但评估实践仍然与人类人工智能系统在实际部署过程中的失败情况不一致。许多研究强调模型准确性、解释保真度或自我报告的信任度Despite progress on mechanisms, evaluation practices remain misaligned with how human–AI systems fail in practice during real-world deployment. Many studies emphasize model accuracy, explanation fidelity, or self-reported trust(Lai et al., 2021; Guo et al., 2024; Rudin, 2019; Ghassemi et al., 2021)，隐含地假设这些代理反映了用户是否准备好安全有效地与人工智能协作。然而，信任通常很难预测依赖行为，而解释可能会通过提供错误的确定性或合法性感觉来增加过度依赖, implicitly assuming these proxies reflect whether users are ready to collaborate with AI safely and effectively. Yet, trust often poorly predicts reliance behavior, and explanations can increase overreliance by providing a false sense of certainty or legitimacy(Lai et al., 2021; Lee and Chew, 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Bansal et al., 2021a; Ghassemi et al., 2021)。因此，现实世界的失败不仅是由于模型错误，而且是由于错误校准的人类依赖——当人工智能出错时过度依赖，当人工智能有帮助时使用不足，以及脆弱的“本地”适应不能在不同情况下推广. Consequently, real-world failures persist not only due to model error, but due to miscalibrated human reliance—overreliance when AI is wrong, underuse when AI is helpful, and brittle “local” adaptations that do not generalize across cases(He et al., 2023; Buçinca et al., 2021; Lee and Chew, 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Ghassemi et al., 2021)。至关重要的是，当评估仅报告准确性、感知信任或解释满意度时，这些故障模式通常是不可见的。. Critically, these failure modes are often invisible when evaluation reports only accuracy, perceived trust, or explanation satisfaction.

–改善 (U–C–I) 生命周期。 U-C-I 描述了人类与人工智能协作的关键能力何时发展：用户首先学习模型行为和局限性（理解），然后校准人工智能在实践中的使用方式和时间（控制），最后随着时间的推移完善协作策略和治理政策（改进）。四个指标系列描述了在整个生命周期中应该衡量的内容：结果指标评估决策质量，依赖和交互指标捕获人工智能建议如何被采纳或拒绝，安全和危害指标识别高风险协作失败，学习和准备指标衡量这些行为在重复使用中如何演变。总之，分类法使 U-C-I 生命周期变得可观察，从而能够评估人类与人工智能协作如何随着时间的推移而演变。–Improve (U–C–I) lifecycle. U–C–I describes when key capabilities in human–AI collaboration develop: users first learn model behavior and limitations (Understand), then calibrate how and when AI should be used in practice (Control), and finally refine collaboration strategies and governance policies over time (Improve). The four metric families describe what should be measured across this lifecycle: Outcome metrics evaluate decision quality, Reliance and Interaction metrics capture how AI advice is adopted or rejected, Safety and Harm metrics identify high-risk collaboration failures, and Learning and Readiness metrics measure how these behaviors evolve over repeated use. Together, the taxonomy makes the U–C–I lifecycle observable, enabling evaluation of how human–AI collaboration evolves over time.

先前的工作提出了多种评估人类与人工智能交互的措施，包括信任、依赖、模型预测的一致性和决策准确性Prior work has proposed a variety of measures for evaluating human–AI interaction, including trust, reliance, agreement with model predictions, and decision accuracy(Green and Chen, 2019; Lai et al., 2021; Schemmer et al., 2023; Bansal et al., 2021b)。然而，这些措施通常是孤立研究的，因此无法捕捉人类与人工智能协作的整个生命周期。我们将这些现有的结构综合成四个互补的指标系列：结果质量、依赖行为、安全和伤害信号以及随着时间的推移学习。这些类别反映了部署人工智能决策支持系统时出现的四个实际问题：发生了什么？人工智能是如何使用的？出了什么问题？随着时间的推移，协作如何演变？. However, these measures are often studied in isolation and therefore do not capture the full lifecycle of human–AI collaboration. We synthesize these existing constructs into four complementary metric families: outcome quality, reliance behavior, safety and harm signals, and learning over time. These categories reflect four practical questions that arise when deploying AI decision-support systems: What happened? How was the AI used? What went wrong? And how does collaboration evolve over time?

在此框架的基础上，我们进一步指定如何直接从可观察的交互痕迹而不是推断的态度或模型属性来计算这些指标。我们的分类法涵盖四个互补的类别。Building on this framing, we further specify how these metrics can be computed directly from observable interaction traces rather than inferred attitudes or model properties. Our taxonomy spans four complementary classes.

  1. (1)

    结果指标：捕获超出原始模型准确性的决策质量，例如团队收益和可避免的错误（例如相对于最佳可实现的人类人工智能决策的遗憾），反映人工智能的参与最终是否会改善或降低结果Outcome metrics: capture decision quality beyond raw model accuracy, such as team gain and avoidable error (e.g. regret relative to the best achievable human–AI decision), reflecting whether AI involvement ultimately improves or degrades outcomes(Bansal et al., 2021a; Guo et al., 2024).

  2. (2)

    依赖和交互指标：描述人工智能建议如何塑造人类判断，包括接受错误、更改为错误、覆盖频率和时间以及依赖斜率，从而实现行为校准和对人工智能正确性的敏感性Reliance & Interaction metrics: characterize how AI advice shapes human judgments, including accept-on-wrong, changed-to-wrong, override frequency and timing, and reliance slope, which operationalize behavioral calibration and sensitivity to AI correctness(Buçinca et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2023; Lai et al., 2021; Lee and Chew, 2023).

  3. (3)

    安全与危害指标：将风险归因于人工智能的影响和治理故障，而不仅仅是人为错误，包括人工智能引起的危害、未遂事件、规则与行为之间的矛盾以及回滚或升级事件Safety & Harm metrics: attribute risk to AI influence and governance breakdowns rather than human error alone, including AI-induced harm, near-misses, contradictions between rules and behavior, and rollback or escalation events(Raji et al., 2020; Ghassemi et al., 2021).

  4. (4)

    学习和准备指标：评估入职是否能产生持久的技能，例如故障识别、解释理解以及跨案例、任务或模型版本的保留或转移Learning & Readiness metrics: assess whether onboarding produces durable skill, such as failure identification, explanation comprehension, and retention or transfer across cases, tasks, or model versions(Cai et al., 2019b; Holstein et al., 2023).

2. 为什么仅靠准确性是不够的Why Accuracy Alone Is Insufficient

2.1. 为什么当前评估失败Why Current Evaluation Fails

尽管模型性能取得了快速进步，但人类人工智能系统在部署后以及实际工作流程的日常使用过程中仍会出现许多故障。越来越多的 HAI 研究表明，这种差距主要不是由于模型准确性不足，而是由于系统评估方式与实际使用方式之间的不匹配Despite rapid advances in model performance, many failures of human–AI systems arise after deployment, during everyday use in real workflows. A growing body of HAI research suggests that this gap is not primarily due to insufficient model accuracy, but to a mismatch between how systems are evaluated and how they are actually used(Lai et al., 2021; Guo et al., 2024)。在实践中，人工智能系统嵌入了时间压力、制度规范、问责结构和不断发展的用户策略中，而这些很少反映在标准评估协议中。以下三个评估假设说明了这种不匹配。. In practice, AI systems are embedded in time pressure, institutional norms, accountability structures, and evolving user strategies, which are rarely reflected in standard evaluation protocols. The following three evaluation assumptions illustrate this mismatch.

2.1.1. 准确度Accuracy≠\neq安全Safety

准确性衡量模型的预测是否与真实情况相符，但它并不能反映人类人工智能决策的质量。在医疗保健等高风险环境中，多项研究表明，用户在看到人工智能建议后可能会将最初的正确判断变成错误的判断，这种现象通常被称为人工智能引起的错误或自动化偏差Accuracy measures whether a model’s prediction matches ground truth, but it does not capture the quality of human–AI decisions. In high-stakes settings, such as healthcare, multiple studies show that users may change initially correct judgments to incorrect ones after seeing AI advice, a phenomenon often referred to as AI-induced error or automation bias(Bansal et al., 2021a; Buçinca et al., 2021; Lee and Chew, 2023)。这些错误在标准精度（例如 AUROC 或 F1 指标）中是不可见的，标准精度将 AI 输出视为独立于人类行为。此外，准确性无法区分用户识别并从中恢复的错误与默默传播到下游决策、文档或治疗计划的错误. These errors are invisible in standard accuracy (e.g. AUROC, or F1 metrics), which treat AI outputs as independent of human behavior. Moreover, accuracy does not distinguish between errors that users recognize and recover from versus errors that propagate silently into downstream decisions, documentation, or treatment plans(Ghassemi et al., 2021; Buçinca et al., 2021)。因此，在离线基准测试中表现出色的系统在集成到人工智能建议影响人类判断的真实工作流程中时，仍然可能会增加危害。. As a result, systems that appear high-performing in offline benchmarks may still increase harm when integrated into real workflows where AI advice shapes human judgment.

2.1.2. 信任Trust≠\neq信赖Reliance

信任通常通过任务后调查或李克特量表问卷来衡量，但行为证据始终表明报告的信任与实际依赖之间的一致性较差Trust is frequently measured through post-task surveys or Likert-scale questionnaires, yet behavioral evidence consistently shows weak alignment between reported trust and actual reliance(Lai et al., 2021; Buçinca et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2023; Lee and Chew, 2023)。用户可能会报告信任度较低，但在时间压力、认知负荷或组织期望下仍遵循人工智能建议。相反，用户可能会报告高度信任，而在关键或模棱两可的情况下选择性地忽略人工智能建议. Users may report low trust while still following AI recommendations under time pressure, cognitive load, or organizational expectations. Conversely, users may report high trust while selectively ignoring AI advice in critical or ambiguous cases(Bansal et al., 2021a; Lee and Chew, 2023)。出现这种脱节的原因是信任体现了态度，而依赖则反映了在约束条件下的行为——包括工作量、责任和感知风险。因此，主要依赖信任评分的评估忽略了用户在实践中何时、如何以及为何遵从或推翻人工智能建议，从而掩盖了重要的安全和治理问题。. This disconnect arises because trust captures attitudes, whereas reliance reflects situated behavior under constraints—including workload, accountability, and perceived risk. Evaluations that rely primarily on trust scores therefore miss when, how, and why users defer to or override AI advice in practice, obscuring important safety and governance concerns.

2.1.3. 性能Performance≠\neq准备状态Readiness

评估期间的高任务性能并不意味着用户已为实际部署做好准备。短期性能提升可能掩盖脆弱的策略，例如在不了解潜在的不确定性或故障模式的情况下复制人工智能输出High task performance during evaluation does not imply that users are prepared for real-world deployment. Short-term performance gains can mask brittle strategies, such as copying AI outputs without understanding underlying uncertainty or failure modes(He et al., 2023; Buçinca et al., 2021; Lee and Chew, 2023)。相比之下，准备程度取决于用户是否能够识别人工智能何时可能出错，适当地解释信心和不确定性，并在错误发生时从错误中恢复. In contrast, readiness depends on whether users can recognize when AI is likely wrong, interpret confidence and uncertainty appropriately, and recover from errors when they occur(Cai et al., 2019b; Holstein et al., 2023; Lee and Chew, 2023; Salimzadeh et al., 2024; Prabhudesai et al., 2023)。这些能力（例如故障检测、不确定性解释和错误恢复）很少被明确测量，但它们决定了人类-人工智能系统是否随着时间的推移、分布变化以及模型或工作流程的发展而保持安全. These capacities (e.g. failure detection, uncertainty interpretation, and error recovery) are rarely measured explicitly, yet they determine whether human–AI systems remain safe over time, under distribution shift, and as models or workflows evolve(Lai et al., 2021; Ghassemi et al., 2021).

总之，这些差距指向了根本性的不匹配：我们经常将人工智能系统评估为针对预测性能进行优化的工件，但却将它们部署为嵌入到人类工作流程中的团队成员。解决这种不匹配问题需要评估框架，不仅要捕捉人工智能的预测，还要捕捉人类如何学习如何使用它Together, these gaps point to a fundamental mismatch: we often evaluate AI systems as artifacts optimized for predictive performance, but deploy them as teammates embedded in human workflows. Addressing this mismatch requires evaluation frameworks that capture not only what the AI predicts, but how humans learn to work with it(Cai et al., 2019b)，依赖它, rely on it(Lai et al., 2021; Guo et al., 2024)，并随着时间的推移对其进行管理。, and govern it over time.
