1 简介Introduction

神经网络是处理和概括大量数据的强大工具，在计算机视觉等领域拥有成功的应用Neural networks are powerful tools for processing and generalizing large amounts of data, with successful applications in fields such as computer vision[46], 医疗保健, healthcare[41]和土木工程, and civil engineering[18]。然而，纯粹的数据驱动方法完全依赖于可用数据来包含描述系统所需的信息。因此，当数据稀缺、稀疏或不能充分代表底层行为时，它们的性能可能会恶化. However, purely data-driven approaches rely entirely on the available data to contain the information needed to describe the system. As a result, their performance can deteriorate when the data are scarce, sparse, or not sufficiently representative of the underlying behaviour[2].

物理信息神经网络 (PINN)Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)[40]通过用控制系统的物理定律约束神经网络解决方案来解决这一限制。在 PINN 框架中，这些定律作为附加项嵌入到损失函数中，以便训练网络以满足可用数据和规定的物理特性。自最初的 PINN 制定以来，该领域发展迅速address this limitation by constraining the neural-network solution with the physical laws governing the system. In the PINN framework, these laws are embedded in the loss function as additional terms, so that the network is trained to satisfy both the available data and the prescribed physics. Since the original PINN formulation, the field has grown rapidly[31, 36]，反映出科学界对这种方法越来越感兴趣。, reflecting the increasing interest of the scientific community in this approach.

当控制物理场表示为微分方程或微分方程组时，PINN 可以解释为基于神经网络的微分方程求解器。在此设置中，网络近似解，同时通过训练数据集施加初始条件和边界条件。 PINN 已作为微分方程求解器应用于多种问题，包括流体动力学When the governing physics is expressed as a differential equation, or as a system of differential equations, a PINN can be interpreted as a neural-network-based differential equation solver. In this setting, the network approximates the solution, while the initial and boundary conditions are imposed through the training dataset. PINNs have been applied as differential equation solvers in a wide range of problems, including fluid dynamics[5], 多相流中的传热, heat transfer in multiphase flows[28], 波物理, wave physics[38]和磁流体动力学方程and magnetohydrodynamic equations[47, 27].

除了正向问题之外，还可以制定 PINN 来解决反偏微分方程 (PDE) 问题。在这种情况下，目标是从解的稀疏测量中推断控制方程中的未知量，如中所述Beyond forward problems, PINNs can also be formulated to solve inverse Partial Differential Equations (PDE) problems. In this case, the objective is to infer unknown quantities in the governing equation from sparse measurements of the solution, as also discussed in[40]。在最简单的公式中，未知参数被视为附加的可训练变量并进行优化，以便预测的解决方案与测量值和物理模型保持一致。例如，该策略已用于推断地下水流中的未知参数. In the simplest formulation, the unknown parameter is treated as an additional trainable variable and optimized so that the predicted solution remains consistent with both the measurements and the physical model. This strategy has been used, for example, to infer unknown parameters in groundwater flow[15]和生物力学系统and biomechanical systems[32]。进一步的扩展包括将未知系数本身表示为神经网络的输出，使其依赖于选定的自变量。然后，逆问题就变成了函数关系而不是标量参数的识别，如. A further extension consists of representing the unknown coefficient itself as the output of a neural network, allowing it to depend on selected independent variables. The inverse problem then becomes the identification of a functional relation rather than a scalar parameter, as studied in[45].
