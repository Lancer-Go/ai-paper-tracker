摘要。Abstract.

空间和单细胞转录组学在破译细胞动力学方面具有革命性意义。作为重建细胞发育路径的基本范式，轨迹推断（TI）至关重要。然而，现有方法需要大量的手动干预和熟练掌握异构工具，这对高效 TI 分析构成了重大障碍。为了弥补这一差距，我们提出了 SpaCellAgent，这是一种自主大语言模型 (LLM) 多代理框架，可自动执行端到端时空分析和叙述生成。 SpaCellAgent 利用多代理架构进行战略工作流程规划，利用动态工具编排引擎进行自适应算法选择，以及通过反馈迭代改进性能的自我进化模块。我们在六个异构数据集上评估 SpaCellAgent，这些数据集涵盖复杂的时间发育轨迹、不同的测序平台和空间分辨的组织架构。 SpaCellAgent 始终证明分析效率提高了 40% 以上，同时保持了专家一致的性能。通过将自然语言规范转换为优化的分析工作流程并实现管道的完全自动化，SpaCellAgent 使先进的时空建模民主化，并为计算生物学建立了可扩展的、代理驱动的范例。代码和材料可在Spatial and Single-cell transcriptomics are transformative in deciphering cellular dynamics. As the fundamental paradigm for reconstructing cell developmental paths, trajectory inference (TI) is critical. However, existing methods require extensive manual intervention and proficiency in heterogeneous tools, posing a significant barrier to efficient TI analysis. To bridge this gap, we propose SpaCellAgent, an autonomous large language model (LLM) multi-agent framework that automates end-to-end spatiotemporal analysis and narrative generation. SpaCellAgent utilizes a multi-agent architecture for strategic workflow planning, a dynamic tool-orchestration engine for adaptive algorithm selection, and a self-evolution module that iteratively refines performance through feedback. We evaluate SpaCellAgent on six heterogeneous datasets encompassing complex temporal developmental trajectories, diverse sequencing platforms, and spatially-resolved tissue architectures. SpaCellAgent consistently demonstrates over 40% improvement in analytical efficiency while maintaining expert-aligned performance. By converting natural language specifications into optimized analytical workflows and fully automating the pipeline, SpaCellAgent democratizes advanced spatiotemporal modeling and establishes a scalable, agent-driven paradigm for computational biology. 1. 简介Introduction

单细胞 RNA 测序 (scRNA-seq) 通过实现单个细胞分辨率的转录组分析，彻底改变了我们对细胞多样性和发育过程的理解Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) has revolutionized our understanding of cellular diversity and developmental processes by enabling transcriptome profiling at individual cell resolution(Tanay and Regev, 2017)。该技术捕捉细胞群内的异质性. This technology captures the heterogeneity within cell populations(Tang et al., 2009)，揭示稀有细胞类型, revealing rare cell types(Shalek et al., 2013), 过渡状态, transitional states(Trapnell et al., 2014)，以及以前在批量测量中被掩盖的动态表达模式, and dynamic expression patterns that were previously obscured in bulk measurements(Macosko et al., 2015)。最近，空间转录组学技术的快速发展使得能够通过空间坐标保存基因表达，从而可以在其天然组织结构内绘制细胞状态图. Recently, the rapid development of spatial transcriptomics technologies has enabled the preservation of gene expression with spatial coordinates, allowing the mapping of cellular states within their native tissue architectures(Rodriques et al., 2019; Chen et al., 2022)。这些方法为细胞状态如何在时间和空间上演化提供了前所未有的见解. These approaches provide unprecedented insights into how cellular states evolve both temporally and spatially(Scialdone et al., 2016)，提供发展轨迹的全面视图, offering a comprehensive view of developmental trajectories(Papalexi and Satija, 2018), 疾病机制, disease mechanisms(Moffitt et al., 2018)和组织稳态, and tissue homeostasis(Rao et al., 2021; Schaum et al., 2018)。然而，从如此高维的细胞状态系统地揭示分子机制需要复杂的分析流程. However, systematically uncovering molecular mechanisms from such high-dimensional cellular states necessitates intricate analysis pipelines(Hu et al., 2021)。在这种方法论领域，轨迹推理 (TI) 脱颖而出，成为研究细胞发育和推断细胞命运的关键工具，有效地从静态快照中重建生物过程的连续进展. Within this methodological landscape, Trajectory Inference (TI) stands out as a pivotal instrument for investigating cellular development and inferring cell fate, effectively reconstructing the continuous progression of biological processes from static snapshots(Cannoodt et al., 2016a).

为了实现细胞发育轨迹分析，已经建立了多种计算方法，包括 MonocleTo operationalize cellular development trajectory analysis, a diverse array of computational approaches has been established, including Monocle(Qiu et al., 2017), 帕加, PAGA(Wolf et al., 2019), 弹弓, Slingshot(Street et al., 2018)和扩散伪时间 (DPT), and Diffusion Pseudotime (DPT)(Haghverdi et al., 2016)。这些方法基于不同的数学框架，从基于图形的算法到主曲线拟合和扩散图，以推断细胞排序和分支结构。然而，尽管理论上取得了进步，这些方法在不同的数据维度和轨迹拓扑中表现出不一致的性能. These methods are grounded in distinct mathematical frameworks, ranging from graph-based algorithms to principal curve fitting and diffusion maps, to infer cellular ordering and branching structures. However, despite their theoretical advancements, these methods exhibit inconsistent performance across varying data dimensionalities and trajectory topologies(Saelens et al., 2019)，因此需要领域专家进行手动选择和启发式调整。, thereby necessitating manual selection and heuristic tuning by domain experts.

大语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展促进了从以手动工具为中心的分析到自主 LLM 代理驱动探索的范式转变Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have catalyzed a paradigm shift from manual tool-centric analysis to autonomous LLMs agent-driven exploration(Wang et al., 2023; Boiko et al., 2023)。超越代码生成和复杂推理. Beyond code generation and complex reasoning(Guo et al., 2025; Achiam et al., 2023)，法学硕士现在可以通过将原始输出与有意义的见解联系起来，协调异构计算工具并实现科学解释的民主化, LLMs can now orchestrate heterogeneous computational tools and democratize scientific interpretation by bridging raw outputs with meaningful insights(M. Bran et al., 2024; Dai et al., 2024)。尽管具有这种变革潜力，但法学硕士代理工作流程与单细胞谱系重建的集成仍处于初级阶段，缺乏端到端的闭环解决方案。至关重要的是，现有方法缺乏自我进化和迭代完善的自动化机制；它们的静态性质通常会产生次优的推理结果，并带来令人望而却步的手动负担。因此，迫切需要一个统一的框架，利用预先训练的法学硕士的生物推理能力作为认知核心，自主编排 TI 工作流程。. Despite this transformative potential, the integration of LLMs agent workflows into single-cell lineage reconstruction remains nascent, lacking an end-to-end closed-loop solution. Crucially, existing approaches lack automated mechanisms for self-evolution and iterative refinement; their static nature often yields suboptimal inference results and imposes a prohibitive manual burden. Consequently, there is an imperative for a unified framework that leverages the biological reasoning abilities of pre-trained LLMs as a cognitive core, autonomously orchestrating TI workflows.

为了填补这些空白，我们引入了 SpaCellAgent，这是一个由 LLM 驱动的自主框架，可以执行端到端空间分析和生物叙述生成。与传统的静态和严格定义的管道相比To fill these gaps, we introduce SpaCellAgent, an autonomous LLM-driven framework that performs end-to-end spatial analysis and biological narrative generation. In contrast to traditional static and rigidly defined pipelines(Bacher and Kendziorski, 2016; Wagner and Klein, 2020)SpaCellAgent 引入了一种协作式多代理架构，可以为每个 LLM 动态分配专门的角色，例如计划者、执行者、评估者和报告者。具体来说，规划器分析轨迹分析目标和拓扑数据属性，将谱系重建过程分解为可执行的里程碑，而执行器通过动态工具编排引擎自主识别最佳算法策略，以生成健壮的实现代码。评估器评估重建谱系的质量，诊断特定的 TI 异常，例如难以置信的分支和伪时间反演，同时通过自我完善机制综合纠正反馈以进行迭代改进。此外，SpaCellAgent 还包含一个自我进化模块，可以跨任务持续积累可重用的错误修复和经过验证的分析模板，从而实现持续改进和知识积累。 广泛的基准测试表明，SpaCellAgent 在拓扑保真度方面优于最先进的 (SOTA) 基线，同时将分析时间缩短了 41.2%。至关重要的是，它兼顾了可扩展性和稳健性，提供稳定、专家一致的轨迹分析性能。, SpaCellAgent introduces a collaborative multi-agent architecture that dynamically assigns specialized roles, such as planner, executor, evaluator, and reporter, to each LLM. Specifically, the planner analyzes trajectory analysis objectives and topological data properties to decompose the lineage reconstruction process into executable milestones, while the executor autonomously identifies optimal algorithmic strategies through a dynamic tool orchestration engine to generate robust implementation code. The evaluator assesses the quality of the reconstructed lineage, diagnosing specific TI anomalies such as implausible branching and pseudotime inversion, while synthesizing corrective feedback for iterative improvement through a self-refinement mechanism. Additionally, SpaCellAgent incorporates a self-evolution module that persistently accumulates reusable error fixes and validated analysis templates across tasks, enabling continuous improvement and knowledge accumulation. Extensive benchmarking demonstrates that SpaCellAgent outperforms state-of-the-art (SOTA) baselines in topological fidelity while reducing the analysis time by 41.2%. Crucially, it reconciles scalability with robustness, delivering stable, expert-aligned trajectory analysis performance.

2.1. 单细胞和空间组学中的轨迹分析Trajectory Analysis in Single-Cell and Spatial Omics

细胞发育轨迹的计算重建通过不同的方法论范式不断发展The computational reconstruction of cellular developmental trajectories has evolved through distinct methodological paradigms(Cannoodt et al., 2016a; Saelens et al., 2019)。在 scRNA-seq 领域，基础工具建立了沿着潜在伪时间对细胞进行排序的前提. In the realm of scRNA-seq, foundational tools established the premise of ordering cells along a latent pseudotime(Haghverdi et al., 2016)。 Monocle 首创了这种方法，使用最小生成树 (MST) 和反向图嵌入来解决分支谱系. Monocle pioneered this approach using minimum spanning trees (MST) and reversed graph embedding to resolve branching lineages(Qiu et al., 2017)。 Slingshot 引入了稳定多谱系推理的主曲线. Slingshot introduced principal curves for stable multi-lineage inference(Street et al., 2018)，而 PAGA 利用图抽象来有效地协调聚类与轨迹拓扑, while PAGA utilized graph abstraction to effectively reconcile clustering with trajectory topology(Wolf et al., 2019)。 空间转录组学的出现促进了发育模型的范式转变，需要将物理拓扑整合到谱系重建中。最近的框架，例如 SpaceFlow. The advent of spatial transcriptomics has catalyzed a paradigm shift in developmental modeling, necessitating the integration of physical topology into lineage reconstruction. Recent frameworks, such as SpaceFlow(Ren et al., 2022)和故事and STORIES(Huizing et al., 2025)等人通过结合空间邻域约束来模拟组织规模的基因表达梯度，从而扩展了 TI。尽管在方法上取得了这些进步，现有的 TI 格局仍然分散，并且严重依赖人工专业知识。研究人员经常受到异构计算生态系统的限制，需要依赖经验的方法选择和对超参数空间的低效手动探索。缺乏标准化不仅阻碍了可重复性，而且还为领域科学家造成了阻碍。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 SpaCellAgent，这是一个法学硕士驱动的代理框架，可以自主协调自适应算法选择和迭代代码细化，确保跨不同生物环境的稳健且可重复的分析。, have extended TI by incorporating spatial neighborhood constraints to model tissue-scale gene expression gradients. Despite these methodological strides, the existing TI landscape remains fragmented and heavily reliant on manual expertise. Researchers are often constrained by heterogeneous computational ecosystems, necessitating experience-dependent method choice and inefficient manual exploration of the hyperparameter space. This lack of standardization not only hampers reproducibility but also creates a prohibitive barrier for domain scientists. To address these challenges, we propose SpaCellAgent, an LLM-driven agentic framework that autonomously orchestrates adaptive algorithm selection and iterative code refinement, ensuring robust and reproducible analysis across diverse biological contexts.

2.2. 法学硕士和科学发现代理系统LLM and Agent Systems for Scientific Discovery

LLM 的出现，例如 Gemini 2.5The advent of LLMs, such as Gemini 2.5(Comanici et al., 2025)，导致了代码生成、自然语言推理和复杂任务分解方面的范式转变。基于这些功能构建通用多代理架构，例如 ChatDev, has led to a paradigm shift in code generation, natural language reasoning, and complex task decomposition. Building on these capabilities, general-purpose multi-agent architectures, such as ChatDev(Qian et al., 2024)，展示了专业代理如何协作自主执行复杂的软件工程任务。最近，这种代理驱动的范例已显着扩展到物理领域，其中像 ChemCrow 这样的框架, have demonstrated how specialized agents can collaborate to autonomously execute intricate software engineering tasks. Recently, this agent-driven paradigm has notably extended to physics, where frameworks like ChemCrow(M. Bran et al., 2024)已成功证明了法学硕士在自动化材料特性预测和协调化学合成方面的功效。然而，在生物医学和生物信息学中，此类工作流程的采用主要局限于文本任务，例如文献检索和假设制定have successfully demonstrated the efficacy of LLMs in automating material property prediction and orchestrating chemical synthesis. However, in biomedicine and bioinformatics, the adoption of such workflows has been predominantly limited to textual tasks, such as literature retrieval and hypothesis formulation(Mathur et al., 2025; Xiao et al., 2024)。驱动闭环经验数据分析的潜力，特别是多步骤计算生物学工作流程，在很大程度上仍未得到充分开发。 现有的 LLM 代理框架主要针对通用软件工程或文本处理进行优化，缺乏生物数据分析的特定领域能力。具体来说，它们不支持异构 TI 分析算法的自主编排，也不具备处理高维、噪声组学数据所需的错误恢复机制。迄今为止，仍然缺乏能够执行端到端、闭环轨迹分析任务的统一框架。我们的 SpaCellAgent 通过建立一个 LLM 驱动的自主框架来填补这一空白，该框架动态优化算法选择，通过强大的自我优化工作流程提供端到端 TI 分析。. The potential for driving closed-loop, empirical data analysis, particularly for multi-step computational biology workflows, remains largely underexplored. Existing LLM agent frameworks, primarily optimized for general-purpose software engineering or text processing, lack the domain-specific abilities for biological data analysis. Specifically, they do not support the autonomous orchestration of heterogeneous TI analysis algorithms, nor do they possess the error-recovery mechanisms necessary for processing high-dimensional, noisy omics data. To date, a unified framework capable of executing end-to-end, closed-loop trajectory analysis tasks remains absent. Our SpaCellAgent fills this gap by establishing an LLM-driven autonomous framework that dynamically optimizes algorithmic selection, delivering end-to-end TI analysis through a robust, self-optimizing workflow.

3.2. LLM驱动的自主代理LLM-driven Autonomous Agents

法学硕士驱动的自主代理代表了一种范式转变，其中法学硕士充当规划、推理和行动执行的“大脑”。虽然个体代理在任务分解方面表现出了令人印象深刻的能力，但它在复杂场景中很容易不稳定。因此，最近的研究重点是多智能体协作，它通过为专门智能体分配不同的角色来模仿人类群体动态。通过促进代理间交互和迭代反馈，这种协作架构克服了单个代理的局限性，为处理计算生物学任务等复杂的工作流程提供了卓越的鲁棒性和准确性。LLM-driven autonomous agents represent a paradigm shift in which LLMs serve as the “brain” for planning, reasoning, and action execution. While the individual agent demonstrates impressive capabilities in task decomposition, it is prone to instability in complex scenarios. Consequently, recent research focuses on multi-agent collaboration, which mimics human group dynamics by assigning distinct roles to specialized agents. By facilitating inter-agent interaction and iterative feedback, this collaborative architecture overcomes the limitations of a single agent, offering superior robustness and accuracy for handling sophisticated workflows such as computational biology tasks.

This dual capability equips it with greater adaptability and scalability when confronting the high heterogeneity and complex dependencies inherent in spatial and single-cell data.

自我完善机制Self-refinement mechanism。 为了确保 TI 分析的稳健性，SpaCellAgent 采用了自主的自我优化机制。对于每个步骤，系统都会启动一个有界迭代过程，评估器在其中评估执行输出。成功的迭代致力于中间数据集状态并推进工作流程。相反，失败会触发细化周期：编码器接收包含完整交互历史记录、截断的错误日志和来自评估者的语义反馈的增强上下文。在强制策略发散以避免重复错误的提示的指导下，代理合成修改后的代码以重新执行。此循环将持续下去，直到成功或达到最大尝试限制。通过将基于规则的错误检测与 LLM 驱动的诊断相结合，该机制可以自主解决轨迹分析过程中遇到的语法错误和 API 差异，从而实现细胞动力学的无缝建模。. To ensure robustness in TI analysis, SpaCellAgent incorporates an autonomous self-refinement mechanism. Successful iterations commit to the intermediate dataset state and advance the workflow. In contrast, failures trigger a refinement cycle: the coder receives an augmented context containing the full interaction history, truncated error logs, and semantic feedback from the evaluator. Guided by prompts that enforce strategy divergence to avoid repetitive errors, the agent synthesizes revised code for re-execution. This loop persists until success is achieved or the maximum attempt limit is reached. By integrating rule-based error detection with LLM-driven diagnosis, this mechanism autonomously resolved syntax errors and API discrepancies encountered during trajectory analysis, enabling the seamless modeling of cellular dynamics.

7. 结论Conclusion

我们介绍 SpaCellAgent，一个基于 LLM 的自主进化多智能体框架，用于轨迹分析。通过将协作多代理架构与动态工具编排相集成，SpaCellAgent 可以规划工作流程、选择方法、执行分析并生成可解释的报告，从而缩小原始数据和生物洞察之间的差距。实验结果证实，SpaCellAgent 的性能与专家的性能相当，同时将分析时间缩短了 40% 以上，并降低了领域研究人员的可访问性障碍。消融研究验证了基于代理的分解、迭代自我完善和跨任务的知识重用在实现鲁棒性和稳定性方面的关键作用。未来的工作将把 SpaCellAgent 扩展到空间多组学集成，并与调控网络分析更紧密地耦合，将 SpaCellAgent 定位为计算生物学中可扩展的智能助手的基础步骤。We introduce SpaCellAgent, an autonomous self-evolving LLM-based multi-agent framework for trajectory analysis. By integrating a collaborative multi-agent architecture with dynamic tool orchestration, SpaCellAgent plans workflows, selects methods, executes analyses, and generates interpretable reports, closing the gap between raw data and biological insight. The results of the experiment confirm that the SpaCellAgent matches the performance of the experts while reducing the analysis time by more than 40% and lowering the accessibility barrier for the domain researchers. Ablation studies validate the critical roles of agent-based decomposition, iterative self-refinement, and knowledge reuse across tasks in achieving robustness and stability. Future work will extend the SpaCellAgent to spatial multi-omics integration and tighter coupling with regulatory network analysis, positioning SpaCellAgent as a foundational step toward scalable, intelligent assistants in computational biology.
