NeuroTrain：使用开放基准框架调查尖峰神经网络的本地学习规则NeuroTrain: Surveying Local Learning Rules for Spiking Neural Networks with an Open Benchmarking Framework

Alessio Caviglia alessio.caviglia@polito.it Filippo Marostica filippo.marostica@polito.it Roberta Bardini roberta.bardini@polito.it Alessandro Savino alessandro.savino@polito.it Stefano Di Carlo stefano.dicarlo@polito.it
摘要Abstract

尖峰神经网络 (SNN) 的快速扩展导致了训练算法的激增，这些算法在生物学灵感、计算结构和硬件适用性方面存在很大差异。尽管取得了这些进展，该领域仍然缺乏一个统一的、细粒度的分类法来系统地组织这些方法并阐明它们的概念关系。这项调查提供了 SNN 训练算法的全面分类，涵盖代理梯度反向传播、局部和三因素学习规则、生物启发的可塑性机制、ANN 到 SNN 转换管道和非标准优化策略。我们根据计算原理、学习信号和局部性属性来分析每个类。为了支持可重复的研究，我们发布了 NeuroTrain，这是一个基于 snnTorch 的开源框架，它在统一、模块化和可扩展的框架内实现了这些算法的代表性集合，从而实现了跨数据集、架构和训练机制的一致基准测试。通过整合零散的文献并提供可重用的基准测试框架，本次调查确定了常见模式，突出了开放的挑战，并概述了未来可扩展、高效的 SNN 训练工作的有希望的方向。The rapid expansion of spiking neural networks (SNNs) has led to a proliferation of training algorithms that differ widely in biological inspiration, computational structure, and hardware suitability. Despite this progress, the field lacks a unified, fine-grained taxonomy that systematically organizes these approaches and clarifies their conceptual relationships. This survey provides a comprehensive taxonomy of SNN training algorithms, spanning surrogate-gradient backpropagation, local and three-factor learning rules, biologically inspired plasticity mechanisms, ANN-to-SNN conversion pipelines, and non-standard optimization strategies. We analyze each class in terms of its computational principles, learning signals, and locality properties. To support reproducible research, we release NeuroTrain, an open-source snnTorch-based framework that implements a representative set of these algorithms within a unified, modular, and extendable framework, enabling consistent benchmarking across datasets, architectures, and training regimes. By consolidating fragmented literature and providing a reusable benchmarking framework, this survey identifies common patterns, highlights open challenges, and outlines promising directions for future work on scalable, efficient SNN training.

Beyond reviewing the main methodological families, we introduced NeuroTrain, an open framework designed to support the implementation, benchmarking, and comparison of神经网络SNN标准化、通用环境中的培训规则。通过解耦数据集、网络模型、训练规则和基准测试程序，NeuroTrain 旨在提高该研究领域的透明度、可重复性和可访问性，同时促进社区驱动的共享工具和实施的开发。training rules within a standardized, common environment. By decoupling datasets, network models, training rules, and benchmarking procedures, NeuroTrain aims to improve transparency, reproducibility, and accessibility in this research area, while fostering community-driven development of shared tools and implementations.

总而言之，几个重要的挑战仍然存在。首先，许多训练规则在数据集和架构之间的可移植性仍然有限，因此很难评估其真正的通用性。其次，公平的基准测试不仅需要通用的软件接口，还需要仔细处理超参数优化、计算成本、内存使用、稀疏性和可扩展性。第三，该领域将受益于算法基准测试和硬件感知评估之间更强的融合，特别是当神经形态系统走向现实世界部署时。因此，未来的工作将侧重于通过额外的训练规则、更广泛的数据集和模型覆盖范围、更丰富的基准测试指标以及与神经形态基准测试计划和面向硬件的评估流程更紧密的集成来扩展 NeuroTrain。从这个角度来看，NeuroTrain 的目的不是作为最终基准，而是作为 SNN 训练研究更加系统化和可重复性进展的不断发展的基础。As a takeaway, several important challenges remain open. First, many training rules still exhibit limited portability across datasets and architectures, making it difficult to assess their true generality. Second, fair benchmarking requires not only common software interfaces but also careful treatment of hyperparameter optimization, computational cost, memory usage, sparsity, and scalability. Third, the field would benefit from stronger convergence between algorithmic benchmarking and hardware-aware evaluation, especially as neuromorphic systems move toward real-world deployment. Future work will therefore focus on extending NeuroTrain with additional training rules, broader dataset and model coverage, richer benchmarking metrics, and tighter integration with neuromorphic benchmarking initiatives and hardware-oriented evaluation flows. In this perspective, NeuroTrain is intended not as a final benchmark, but as an evolving foundation for more systematic and reproducible progress in SNN training research.
