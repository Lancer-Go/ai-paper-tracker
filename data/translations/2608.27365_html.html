摘要Abstract

物理集成的 3D 高斯表示现在允许在显式材料模型下模拟和渲染重建的可变形对象。然而，现有的管道假设材料参数是已知的或手动指定的，当这些参数必须从观察到的物体动力学推断时，限制了它们的适用性。我们建议Physics-integrated 3D Gaussian representations now allow reconstructed deformable objects to be simulated and rendered under explicit material models. Existing pipelines, however, assume that material parameters are known or manually specified, limiting their applicability when these parameters must be inferred from observed object dynamics. We propose敲击GSKnockGS，一个交互响应 PhysicalGS 框架，可根据 3D 高斯对象在已知外力作用下的动力学来估计其弹性和密度尺度。我们不仅仅将物理模拟视为前向过程，而是将力引起的响应转化为校准信号：从观察到的动力学中提取时间响应特征，根据这些特征估计两个材料尺度，然后将估计值冻结并写回到同一个模拟器中，以便可以在从未拟合的交互上进行测试。, an interaction-response PhysicalGS framework that estimates the elasticity and density scales of a 3D Gaussian object from its dynamics under a known applied force. Rather than treating physical simulation only as a forward process, we turn the force-induced response into a calibration signal: temporal response features are extracted from the observed dynamics, the two material scales are estimated from those features, and the estimate is then frozen and written back into the same simulator so that it can be tested on an interaction it was never fitted to.

我们在参数恢复和响应级别保真度方面评估该框架。将估计的比例与隐藏的地面实况进行比较，并使用 3D 粒子轨迹、响应曲线统计数据和渲染帧质量根据目标测量重新模拟的对象。在五个保留的材料目标中，我们的方法比响应检索、全局回归或固定的默认材料更准确地恢复尺度，并且冻结的估计在方向和幅度不同的相互作用下仍然具有预测性。因此，交互响应携带足够的信息来校准物理接地 3D 高斯表示中的材料尺度。我们的研究是迈向交互式 PhysicalGS 系统的第一步，该系统可校准渲染外观和模拟响应一致的高斯资产。We evaluate the framework on both parameter recovery and response-level fidelity. The estimated scales are compared against hidden ground truth, and the re-simulated object is measured against the target using 3D particle trajectories, response-curve statistics, and rendered-frame quality. Across five held-out material targets, our method recovers the scales substantially more accurately than response retrieval, global regression, or a fixed default material, and the frozen estimate remains predictive under interactions that differ in direction and in magnitude. Interaction response therefore carries enough information to calibrate material scales in physically grounded 3D Gaussian representations. Our study is a first step toward interactive PhysicalGS systems that calibrate a Gaussian asset whose rendered appearance and simulated response are consistent.

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 KnockGS，这是一项针对物理集成高斯资产的响应条件校准的对照研究。特定于对象的库和简单的硬邻域局部脊估计器从已知的 Probe-A 响应中恢复两个 MPM 材料尺度；回写后，冻结估计比响应检索、KNN、全局脊和固定默认材料更准确地预测保留的方向和幅度探针。结果在 Pillow、Ficus 和 Vasedeck 上的特定对象循环中重复，并得到高斯相关粒子轨迹和渲染视觉保真度的支持。We presented KnockGS, a controlled study of response-conditioned calibration for physics-integrated Gaussian assets. An object-specific library and a simple hard-neighborhood local ridge estimator recover two MPM material scales from a known Probe-A response; after write-back, the frozen estimate predicts held-out direction and magnitude probes more accurately than response retrieval, KNN, global ridge, and fixed default materials. The result repeats in object-specific loops on Pillow, Ficus, and Vasedeck, and is supported by Gaussian-associated-particle trajectories and rendered visual fidelity.

同样重要的是，压力测试界定了结果。干净的合成表面和深度观察保留了有用的证据，但无 ID 的视觉轨迹和深度扰动会降低校准。除非在匹配条件下重建响应库，否则填充或网格不匹配以及跨对象共享将失败。缩小这一差距需要稳定的视觉对应、不确定性感知识别、更广泛的物理状态以及通过测量的力和物体级变形进行验证。Equally important, the stress tests delimit the result. Clean synthetic surface and depth observations retain useful evidence, but ID-free visual tracks and depth perturbations degrade calibration. Fill or grid mismatch and cross-object sharing fail unless the response library is rebuilt under matching conditions. Closing that gap requires stable visual correspondence, uncertainty-aware identification, broader physical states, and validation with measured force and object-level deformation.

6 讨论Discussion

支持的主张是根据已知的相互作用对两个 MPM 材料尺度进行受控、特定于对象、离散化条件的校准，然后在同一模拟器系列中进行未见过的相互作用预测。实验没有建立真实的 RGB/RGB-D 输入、真实的力反馈、测量的材料地面实况、模拟到真实的传输、跨对象共享估计器、填充未知反演、完整的多参数材料识别、损伤预测或有源探针最优性。观测梯使用综合观测，外部路线使用不同的本地参数化，被动可识别性结果适用于分析的弹簧质量/接触公式；任何一个都不应该被理解为更广泛的现实世界或类似的准确性声明。The supported claim is controlled, object-specific, discretization-conditioned calibration of two MPM material scales from a known interaction, followed by unseen-interaction prediction in the same simulator family. The experiments do not establish real RGB/RGB-D input, real force feedback, measured material ground truth, sim-to-real transfer, a cross-object shared estimator, fill-unknown inversion, full multi-parameter material identification, damage prediction, or active-probe optimality. The observation ladder uses synthetic observations, the external routes use different native parameterizations, and the passive-identifiability result applies to the analyzed spring–mass/contact formulation; none should be read as a broader real-world or like-for-like accuracy claim.

测量到的故障指出了未来的具体工作。无 ID 轨迹的退化激发了强大的视觉对应，而对合成深度扰动的敏感性则需要不确定性感知估计。固定探针 A 下的描述符模糊性激发了主动交互选择和更丰富的响应表示，并且当前的两尺度状态应该扩展到更丰富的物理参数化。最后，缩小模拟与现实之间的差距需要通过测量的相互作用和真实物体变形进行验证。这些是未来的方向，而不是当前系统的能力。The measured failures point to concrete future work. The degradation with ID-free tracks motivates robust visual correspondence, while sensitivity to synthetic-depth perturbations calls for uncertainty-aware estimation. Descriptor ambiguity under a fixed Probe A motivates active interaction selection and richer response representations, and the current two-scale state should be extended to richer physical parameterizations. Finally, closing the simulation-to-reality gap requires validation with measured interactions and real object deformation. These are future directions rather than capabilities of the current system.
