摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）在各种敏感环境中表现出系统性的政治偏见。我们发现法学硕士不对称地处理来自对立政治派别的对应主题。我们将这种现象称为Large language models (LLMs) exhibit systematic political bias across a variety of sensitive contexts. We find that LLMs handle counterpart topics from opposing political sides asymmetrically. We refer to this phenomenon as隐蔽的政治偏见covert political bias并确定其运作所采用的 7 类技术。我们提出了两个衡量隐性偏见的指标：情绪一致性衡量配对政治提示中修辞和框架的对称性；有用性一致性衡量对称深度和参与度。为了减少这两种类型的隐性偏见，我们引入了政治一致性训练（PCT），这是一种具有两种互补范式的强化学习方法：情感一致性训练和帮助性一致性训练。我们表明 PCT 保留了整体的帮助性，大大减少了隐蔽的政治偏见，并推广到了坚持的基准。我们在 https://political-manipulation.ai 上发布了我们的工作。and identify 7 categories of techniques through which it operates. We propose two metrics for covert bias: Sentiment Consistency measures symmetry in rhetoric and framing across paired political prompts; Helpfulness Consistency measures symmetric depth and engagement. To reduce both types of covert bias, we introduce Political Consistency Training (PCT), an RL training method with two complementary paradigms: Sentiment Consistency Training and Helpfulness Consistency Training. We show that PCT preserves overall helpfulness, substantially reduces covert political bias, and generalizes to held-out benchmarks. We release our work at https://political-manipulation.ai.

偏差表现为Bias manifests as不一致的inconsistent系统地支持某一方的写作。个人有偏见，不一定有意见。持有强硬立场并不一定意味着存在偏见。例如，一个人说“我讨厌猫”，如果他同样不喜欢所有动物，那么他不一定对特定动物有偏见，因为他们的厌恶是一致的。在政治背景下，偏见和操纵可能表现为言辞上的不一致，例如向一方添加选择性警告或对想法进行不对称审查。这些操纵技术中的每一种，虽然单独来说都是可以防御的，但都会带来更广泛的偏见模式，而这种模式只有在总体上才能被检测到。因此，用户通常很难在任何单一响应中发现隐藏的政治偏见。writing that systematically favors a particular side. Individuals are biased, not necessarily opinions. Holding a strong stance does not necessarily indicate bias. For example, a person who states “I hate cats” is not necessarily biased against a specific animal if they dislike all animals equally, as their distaste is consistent. In political contexts, bias and manipulation can manifest as rhetorical inconsistencies such adding selective caveats to one side or applying asymmetric scrutiny to ideas. Each of these manipulation techniques, while individually defensible, enables a broader pattern of bias that is only detectable in aggregate. Because of this, covert political bias is often challenging for users to detect in any single response.

3 政治一致性培训3 Political Consistency Training

政治一致性培训（PCT）通过两种互补的培训范式减少隐蔽的政治偏见，Political Consistency Training (PCT) reduces covert political bias through two complementary training paradigms,情绪一致性Sentiment Consistency和and乐于助人的一致性Helpfulness Consistency，针对第 2 节中介绍的两个指标。每个范式都有自己的提示集和自己的奖励信号，并且这两者在单个训练后 RL 运行中混合，每个提示在训练时路由到其相应的奖励。数据创建、我们的训练流程和提示模板在附录 F 中描述，判断-奖励配对在第 3.1 节中指定。, that target the two metrics introduced in Section 2. Each paradigm has its own prompt set and its own reward signal, and the two are mixed within a single post-training RL run with each prompt routed to its corresponding reward at training time. Data creation, our training pipeline, and prompt templates are described in Appendix F, and the judge-reward pairings are specified in Section 3.1.

单独使用这两种范式是不够的：每一种范式都单独优化，将模型推向第 2.2 节中描述的两种故障模式之一。情绪一致性本身将模型推向统一的谨慎，通过对冲、拒绝和双方框架来博弈语气平衡，而不是帮助回答查询。帮助一致性本身就推动模型对提示要求的任何立场做出不加批判的承诺，从而在配对提示中保持隐性偏见完好无损。在一次训练中结合这两种范式会阻碍这两条捷径：模型学会对每个提示做出批判性和实质性的反应，同时在成对的提示中保持语言、框架和说服对称（图 4）。Neither paradigm alone is sufficient: each, optimized in isolation, drives the model toward one of the two failure modes described in Section 2.2. Sentiment Consistency on its own pushes the model toward uniform caution, gaming tonal balance through hedging, refusal, and both-sides framing rather than helpfully answering the queries. Helpfulness Consistency on its own pushes the model toward uncritical commitment to whatever stance the prompt requests, leaving covert bias intact across paired prompts. Combining the two paradigms in a single training run blocks both shortcuts: the model learns to respond critically and substantively to each prompt while keeping language, framing, and persuasion symmetric across paired prompts (Figure 4).

6 局限性6 Limitations

锚定校准。Anchor calibration.情绪一致性目标奖励落在同主题左倾锚点和右倾锚点之间的响应。如果锚生成管道系统地不对称，则训练后的模型会继承这种不对称性作为其“平衡”的定义。附录 K 给出了我们用于评估跨前沿模型的锚点质量并选择用于训练的锚点源的审核。我们的锚点是综合生成的；我们还没有测试由人类手动编写和标记的隐蔽左倾和右倾内容的锚点，这是未来工作的一个有希望的方向和潜在的改进。The Sentiment Consistency objective rewards responses that land between pertopic left- and right-leaning anchors. If the anchor-generation pipeline is systematically asymmetric, the trained model inherits that asymmetry as its definition of “balanced.” Appendix K gives the audit we used to assess anchor quality across frontier models and to select the anchor source for training. Our anchors are synthetically generated; we have not tested anchors of covert left- and right-leaning content that are manually written and labeled by humans, which is a promising direction for future work and a potential improvement.

主题范围。Topic scope.50对两极分化的对比以美国/西方政治话语为基础。偏见技术的主题和分类可能需要适应更广泛的政治中立培训。The 50 Polarized Contrastive Pairs are anchored in US/Western political discourse. Both the topics and the taxonomy of bias techniques will likely require adaptation for broader political neutrality training.

单轮数据集。Single-turn dataset.我们的方法主要解决单轮交互中可检测到的隐性偏见行为的子集。更微妙形式的隐蔽政治偏见可能会在多个回合和更复杂的情况下显现出来。例如，用户可能在与政治无关的对话期间隐含地分享政治观点，从而产生了帮助不一致的可能性，这种情况只有在多次对话之后才会显现出来。这些形式的偏差需要相应更先进的多圈测量方法。Our method primarily addresses the subset of covert bias behaviors detectable in single-turn interactions. More subtle forms of covert political bias can manifest over multiple turns and in more complex scenarios. For example, a user may implicitly share a political opinion during a conversation otherwise unrelated to politics, creating the possibility of helpfulness inconsistency that is visible only after many conversational turns. These forms of bias require correspondingly more advanced multi-turn measurement methodology.

7 讨论7 Discussion

LLM 的政治偏见是可以控制的。LLM political bias is tractable.我们端到端地演示了这一点：使隐性偏差变得清晰的分类法、跨多个维度检测隐性偏差的指标，以及基于一致性的训练管道，通过大约 1,000 个训练提示在 Qwen3-14B 上大幅减少隐性偏差。该方法不需要对政治效价进行人工注释，而是依赖于使法学硕士比人类更容易消除偏见的结构特性：每个输出都是独立的，因此可以直接将一致性作为训练信号强加。We demonstrate this end-to-end: a taxonomy that makes covert bias legible, metrics that detect it across multiple dimensions, and a consistency-based training pipeline that substantially reduces it on Qwen3-14B with roughly 1,000 training prompts. The approach requires no human annotation of political valence, relying instead on the structural property that makes LLMs easier to debias than people: each output is independent, so consistency can be imposed directly as a training signal.

与共识和法律合规培训兼容。Compatibility with consensus and legal-compliance training.除了政治中立之外，人工智能公司还有动力避免其模型输出的法律责任，例如诽谤或歧视。我们的一致性目标仅针对左翼和右翼框架之间有争议的差距，同时保持共识立场和任何现有的避免责任培训。In addition to political neutrality, AI companies have an incentive to avoid legal liability from their models’ outputs, such as defamation or discrimination. Our consistency objective only targets the contested gap between left- and rightleaning framings, while maintaining consensus positions and any existing liability-avoidance training.

我们的分类法、指标和训练方法旨在被人工智能公司采用，作为部署前测试和微调的标准组成部分。隐蔽的政治偏见可以作为人工智能在更广泛的当前系统上进行调整的有用训练场：明确定义的目标和廉价可测量的输出。现有的政治中立努力范围很窄，主要针对公开的政治偏见和有限的偏见分类。Our taxonomy, metrics, and training method are designed to be adopted by AI companies as a standard component of pre-deployment testing and fine-tuning. Covert political bias serves as a useful training ground for AI alignment on current systems more broadly: well-defined targets and cheaply measurable outputs. Existing efforts at political neutrality have been narrow, primarily targeting overt political bias with limited bias taxonomies.

推广到其他操纵问题。Generalization to other manipulation problems.一致性训练超越了本文中使用的美国左/右划分，使模型开发人员能够减少对更广泛主题的操纵。 PCT 类似地处理非美国或多党政治体系，主题和实体来自这些背景。一致性培训还超出了政治范围，扩展到任何可以从法学硕士可靠地引出的秘密操纵行为。为了减少阿谀奉承，一致性训练可以使用提示阿谀奉承或逆反的锚定模型，消除对用户陈述的支持或反对的偏见。Consistency Training generalizes beyond the U.S. left/right divides used in this paper, allowing model developers to reduce manipulation on a wider array of topics. PCT handles non-U.S. or multi-party political systems similarly, with topics and entities drawn from those settings. Consistency Training also extends beyond politics, to any covertly manipulative behavior that can be reliably elicited from an LLM. To reduce sycophancy, Consistency Training could use anchor models prompted to be sycophantic or contrarian, removing bias for or against the user’s statements.

A.1 信息选择A.1 Information Selection

由包含、排除或优先考虑哪些信息而引入的偏见。Bias introduced by what information is included, excluded, or prioritized.

挑选樱桃（选择性演示）。Cherry-Picking (Selective Presentation).仅呈现支持特定叙述的事实、数据、引用或研究，同时省略容易获得的矛盾信息。Presenting only facts, data, quotes, or studies that support a specific narrative while omitting readily available contradictory information.偏见方向：倾向于所选事实支持的叙述；不赞成被省略的事实所支持的叙述。Direction of bias: Favors the narrative supported by the selected facts; disfavors the narrative supported by the omitted facts.

省略解释性上下文。Omission of Explanatory Context.在没有理解其所需的历史、系统或情境背景的情况下呈现事件或声明。Presenting an event or statement without the historical, systemic, or situational background needed to understand it.偏见的方向：倾向于认为缺失的上下文会破坏的解释。Direction of bias: Favors the interpretation that the missing context would undermine.

关注/忽视结果。Spotlighting/Ignoring Outcomes.过度关注一方的成功或另一方的失败，而系统性地忽视相反的情况（例如，强调政策的成本而忽视其收益）。Placing excessive focus on the successes of one side or the failures of the other, while systematically ignoring the inverse (e.g., emphasizing a policy’s costs while ignoring its benefits).偏见的方向：偏向强调成功或忽略失败的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side whose successes are highlighted or whose failures are ignored.

摘坚果（非代表性示例）。Nut-Picking (Unrepresentative Examples).突出对方最极端、最不合理或最令人尴尬的例子，并将其作为整体的代表。Highlighting the most extreme, irrational, or embarrassing examples from an opposing side and framing them as representative of the whole.偏见的方向：不利于以极端例子为特征的群体。Direction of bias: Disfavors the group being characterized by the extreme example.

选择性申诉突出显示。Selective Grievance Highlighting.对特定身份群体所经历的伤害或不公正给予不成比例的报道和强调，同时淡化或忽视影响其他群体或受青睐群体成员所犯下的类似伤害。Giving disproportionate coverage and emphasis to the harms or injustices experienced by specific identity groups while downplaying or ignoring similar harms when they affect other groups, or when they are perpetrated by members of a favored group.偏见方向：偏向那些不满突出的群体；不利于被描述为肇事者或其不满被忽视的群体。Direction of bias: Favors the group whose grievances are highlighted; disfavors the group portrayed as perpetrators or whose grievances are ignored.

A.2 框架和重点A.2 Framing and Emphasis

响应的结构和信息的呈现如何影响感知。How the structure of the response and the presentation of information influence perception.

重点控制（突出显示/淡化）。Emphasis Manipulation (Highlighting/Downplaying).给予某些论点、事实或群体相对于其他论点、事实或群体的空间、细节和突出程度。The amount of space, detail, and prominence given to certain arguments, facts, or groups over others.偏见方向：倾向于更受重视的论点；不利于那些被最小化的人。Direction of bias: Favors the arguments receiving greater emphasis; disfavors those that are minimized.

尺度失真（平凡化/夸张）。Scale Distortion (Trivialization/Exaggeration).扭曲某一事件或成就的重要性：将重要的项目放在琐碎的项目旁边以削弱它，或者夸大次要缺陷，同时最小化主要缺陷。Distorting the significance of an event or achievement: placing a significant item next to a trivial one to diminish it, or exaggerating minor flaws while minimizing major ones.偏见方向：不喜欢轻视成绩或夸大缺点的一方；偏爱那些成就被夸大或缺点被淡化的一方。Direction of bias: Disfavors the side whose achievements are trivialized or whose flaws are exaggerated; favors the side whose achievements are exaggerated or whose flaws are trivialized.

结构放置（埋藏信息）。Structural Placement (Burying Information).将某一点埋在句子、段落或部分的中间，而不是将其放在显着位置（开头或结尾），从而削弱其重要性。De-emphasizing a point by burying it in the middle of a sentence, paragraph, or section, rather than placing it in a prominent position (beginning or end).偏见的方向：不利于隐藏的信息。Direction of bias: Disfavors the buried information.

情景框架与主题框架。Episodic vs. Thematic Framing.通过个别轶事（情节）而不是系统分析（主题）来报道问题，反之亦然。Covering an issue through individual anecdotes (episodic) rather than systemic analysis (thematic), or vice versa.偏见的方向：情景框架倾向于将责任直接归咎于个人；主题框架倾向于将注意力集中在系统性因素上。偏见取决于哪种框架有利于首选叙述。Direction of bias: Episodic framing tends to direct blame toward individuals; thematic framing tends to direct focus toward systemic factors. The bias depends on which framing benefits the preferred narrative.

定义术语（问题标签）。Defining the Terms (Issue Labeling).使用特定标签来定义有争议的问题，这本质上构成了辩论的框架（例如，“继承税”与“死亡税”；“无证移民”与“非法外国人”）。Using specific labels to define a controversial issue, which inherently frames the debate (e.g., “Inheritance Tax” vs. “Death Tax”; “Undocumented immigrant” vs. “Illegal alien”).偏见方向：倾向于采用首选术语的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side whose preferred terminology is adopted.

原型铸造。Archetypal Casting.始终将某些人口群体中的个人塑造成积极的原型角色（例如英雄、受害者、创新者），同时将其他人塑造成消极角色（例如恶棍、压迫者、障碍者）。Consistently casting individuals from certain demographic groups into positive archetypal roles (e.g., hero, victim, innovator) while casting others into negative roles (e.g., villain, oppressor, obstacle).偏见方向：有利于始终扮演积极角色的群体；不喜欢那些扮演负面角色的人。Direction of bias: Favors the groups consistently cast in positive roles; disfavors those cast in negative roles.

拇指放在首选群体的范围内。Thumb on the Scale for Preferred Groups.通过权衡身份与客观标准，对某些群体的个人给予优惠待遇；例如，在应基于优点、证据或其他非身份因素的招聘、录取或量刑决定中。Giving preferential treatment to individuals from certain groups by weighing identity over objective criteria; for example, in hiring, admissions, or sentencing decisions that should rest on merit, evidence, or other non-identity factors.偏见方向：偏向属于接受优惠待遇群体的个人；不喜欢那些不喜欢的人。Direction of bias: Favors individuals belonging to the group receiving preferential treatment; disfavors those who are not.

正负比。Positive-to-Negative Ratio.给定一方的正分和负分数量不平衡。An imbalance in the number of positive vs. negative points presented for a given side.偏向方向：偏爱所描述的积极点多于消极点的一方；不喜欢所描述的消极点多于积极点的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side described with more positive than negative points; disfavors the side described with more negative than positive points.

A.3 语言操纵A.3 Linguistic Manipulation

特定的词语选择和句子结构会在显得客观的同时引入偏见。Specific word choices and sentence constructions that introduce bias while appearing objective.

隐含收费。Connotative Charge.使用带有微妙情感分量或高度道德强度的词语（例如，“政权”与“政府”；“要求”与“倡导”；“邪恶”、“神圣”、“可耻”）。Using words that carry subtle emotional weight or high moral intensity (e.g., “the regime” vs. “the government”; “demanding” vs. “advocating”; “evil,” “sacred,” “disgraceful”).偏见方向：偏爱用积极或道德正义的词语描述的一方；不喜欢用消极或道德败坏的词语描述的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side described with positive or morally righteous terms; disfavors the side described with negative or morally corrupt terms.

委婉语和非委婉语。Euphemism and Dysphemism.使用柔和、经过净化的语言来描述盟友的严酷现实（委婉语），或使用严厉的、煽动性的语言来描述对手的行为（语气不善）。Using soft, sanitized language to describe harsh realities for allies (euphemism), or harsh, inflammatory language for the actions of opponents (dysphemism).偏见方向：有利于委婉说法的受益方；不利于被dysphemisms针对的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side benefiting from euphemisms; disfavors the side targeted by dysphemisms.

情感共轭（罗素共轭）。Emotional Conjugation (Russell Conjugation).根据演员的立场，对同一动作进行不同的描述（例如，“我有原则；他顽固”；“她改变了自己的立场；他反复无常”）。Describing the same action differently depending on the actor’s alignment (e.g., “I am principled; he is pig-headed”; “She evolved her position; he flip-flopped”).偏见方向：有利于所描述的具有更良性结合的演员。Direction of bias: Favors the actor described with the more virtuous conjugation.

措辞的自信（归因动词）。Assertiveness of Diction (Verbs of Attribution).选择一些动词来巧妙地质疑对手陈述的有效性，同时肯定盟友的陈述。Choosing verbs that subtly question the validity of an opponent’s statement while affirming an ally’s.偏见方向：有利于其言论被“报道”、“陈述”或“注意到”的一方；不利于其言论被“声称”、“指控”或“坚持”的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side whose statements are “reported,” “stated,” or “noted”; disfavors the side whose statements are “claimed,” “alleged,” or “insisted.”

黄鼠狼的话和对冲利率。Weasel Words and Hedge Rate.使用限定词来注入猜测或怀疑，而不做出可验证的主张（例如，“可以说”、“看来”、“可能会促进”）。差别对冲利率，对不方便的事实进行大量对冲，同时直截了当地陈述方便的事实，尤其具有诊断性。Using qualifiers to inject speculation or doubt without making a verifiable claim (e.g., “Arguably,” “It seems that,” “may facilitate”). A differential hedge rate, hedging heavily on inconvenient facts while stating convenient facts flatly, is particularly diagnostic.偏见方向：不利于被质疑的事实。当不对称应用时，倾向于无条件陈述支撑事实的叙述。Direction of bias: Disfavors the fact being questioned. When applied asymmetrically, favors the narrative whose supporting facts are stated without qualification.

说教、逃避或淡化。Moralizing, Evading, or Downplaying.使用短语来暗示查询有问题或太不确定而无法清楚地回答（例如，“这是一个复杂而敏感的问题”，“必须小心记住”）。包括淡化分裂但真实事实的措辞。Using phrases to insinuate that a query is problematic or too uncertain to answer plainly (e.g., “It’s a complex and sensitive issue,” “one must be careful to remember”). Includes phrasing that downplays divisive but true facts.偏见方向：有利于受保护免受审查的一方或立场；不利于其言论受到质疑、质疑或淡化的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side or position being protected from scrutiny; disfavors the side whose statements are problematized, called into question, or downplayed.

预设。Presupposition.构建一个句子，使有争议的主张被认为是正确的（例如，“失败政策的影响……”预设了政策失败）。Structuring a sentence so that a controversial claim is assumed to be true (e.g., “The impact of the failed policy. . . ” presupposes that the policy failed).偏见的方向：有利于假设的前提。Direction of bias: Favors the assumed premise.

使用恐吓语录。Use of Scare Quotes.在对手使用的术语周围加上引号，以表示怀疑、疏远或不合法。Placing quotation marks around a term used by an opponent to signal skepticism, distance, or illegitimacy.偏见方向：不喜欢被质疑的术语或观点。Direction of bias: Disfavors the terminology or viewpoint being questioned.

A.4 能动性和因果关系A.4 Agency and Causality

因果关系、功劳和责备是如何分配、模糊或暗示的。How causality, credit, and blame are assigned, obscured, or implied.

使用主动语态与被动语态（代理分配）。Use of Active vs. Passive Voice (Agency Assignment).使用被动语态来掩盖对负面行为负责的代理人（例如，“犯了错误”与“首席执行官犯了错误”）。Using passive voice to obscure the agent responsible for a negative action (e.g., “Mistakes were made” vs. “The CEO made mistakes”).偏见方向：有利于代理机构被掩盖的演员。Direction of bias: Favors the actor whose agency is obscured.

名词化。Nominalization.将动词变成名词，使动作看起来抽象且无代理（例如，“发生了破坏”与“军队摧毁了它”）。Turning verbs into nouns to make actions seem abstract and agentless (e.g., “The destruction occurred” vs. “The army destroyed it”).偏见的方向：有利于责任被抽象的参与者。Direction of bias: Favors the actor whose responsibility is abstracted.

因果关系的归因（个性化与抽象化）。Attribution of Causality (Personalizing vs. Abstracting).将对手的失败归因于个人缺陷，将其成功归因于运气，而将盟友的失败归因于外部因素，将其成功归因于个人优点。Attributing an opponent’s failure to personal flaws and their success to luck, while attributing an ally’s failure to external factors and their success to personal merit.偏见的方向：偏向成功被个性化、失败被抽象化的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side whose successes are personalized and whose failures are abstracted.

通过连接削弱。Undercutting by Conjunction.使用“但是”、“然而”或“虽然”来引入一个观点并立即破坏或最小化它（例如，“该政策改善了一些指标，但批评者仍然关注成本”）。Using “but,” “however,” or “although” to introduce a point and immediately undermine or minimize it (e.g., “The policy improved some metrics, but critics remain focused on the costs”).偏向方向：不利于合取点之前的点；赞成后面的观点。Direction of bias: Disfavors the point before the conjunction; favors the point after.

并置。Juxtaposition.在句子或序列中连接想法或事实，以暗示逻辑上不存在的关系或比较。Conjoining ideas or facts in a sentence or sequence to imply a relationship or comparison that does not logically exist.偏见的方向：有利于隐含的关系。Direction of bias: Favors the implied relationship.

邻近污染（罪恶感/关联提升）。Contamination by Proximity (Guilt/Elevation by Association).只需在相同的上下文中提及某人、群体或想法即可将其与消极概念（内疚）联系起来，或者与积极概念（提升）联系起来。Associating a person, group, or idea with negative concepts simply by mentioning them in the same context (guilt), or with positive concepts (elevation).偏向方向：不喜欢与负面因素相关的主体；偏爱与积极元素相关的主题。Direction of bias: Disfavors the subject associated with negative elements; favors the subject associated with positive elements.

Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc（虚假因果关系）。Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc (False Causality).这意味着，由于事件 B 发生在事件 A 之后，因此事件 A 必定引发了事件 B。Implying that because event B followed event A, event A must have caused event B.偏见的方向：倾向于隐含的因果联系，通常用于分配不适当的责备或功劳。Direction of bias: Favors the implied causal link, often used to assign undue blame or credit.

A.5 来源和授权A.5 Sourcing and Authority

谁的观点被包括在内以及他们的可信度是如何构建的。Whose perspectives are included and how their credibility is framed.

选择性采购。Selective Sourcing.主要依赖支持某一观点的来源（专家、证人、组织、研究），而忽略同样合格的反来源。Relying primarily on sources (experts, witnesses, organizations, studies) that support one perspective while ignoring equally qualified counter-sources.偏见方向：倾向于大多数引用来源所代表的观点。Direction of bias: Favors the perspective represented by the majority of cited sources.

来源的偏见标签（权威归属）。Biased Labeling of Sources (Authority Attribution).使用头衔或标签来提高盟友的可信度或降低对手的可信度（例如，“受人尊敬的分析师”与“党派博客作者”；或使用听起来中性的名称引用党派智库）。Using titles or labels to enhance the credibility of allies or diminish the credibility of opponents (e.g., “the respected analyst” vs. “the partisan blogger”; or citing a partisan think tank using its neutral-sounding name).偏见方向：倾向于使用更权威或中立标签描述的来源。Direction of bias: Favors the sources described with more authoritative or neutral labels.

模糊或未归因的采购。Vague or Unattributed Sourcing.使用“专家同意”、“许多人相信”或“批评者说”等短语来介绍观点，但不明确归属。Using phrases like “experts agree,” “many people believe,” or “critics say” to introduce opinions without specific attribution.偏见方向：倾向于不负责任地提出的意见。Direction of bias: Favors the opinion presented without accountability.

错误平衡（双方主义）。False Balance (Bothsidesism).对不同的论点给予同等的时间和重视，提出一致的观点和边缘的观点同样有效。Giving equal time and weight to unequal arguments, presenting a consensus view and a fringe view as equally valid.偏见的方向：通过赋予边缘或少数观点不适当的合法性来支持其观点。Direction of bias: Favors the fringe or minority viewpoint by granting it undue legitimacy.

通过错误共识进行验证。Validation via False Consensus.在没有证据表明这种共识的情况下，将特定的党派观点呈现为广泛、常识或普遍接受的观点。Presenting a specific partisan viewpoint as widespread, common sense, or universally accepted without evidence of such consensus.偏见的方向：赞成作为共识提出的观点。Direction of bias: Favors the viewpoint presented as consensus.

A.6 修辞偏转A.6 Rhetorical Deflection

用于驳回反对意见而不进行实质性讨论的技巧。Techniques used to dismiss opposing arguments without engaging with them substantively.

偏转（Whataboutism/Tu Quoque）。Deflection (Whataboutism/Tu Quoque).通过指出对手的类似缺陷来驳回有效的批评，暗示虚伪，但没有解决批评的实质内容。Dismissing a valid criticism by pointing out a similar flaw in an opponent, implying hypocrisy without addressing the substance of the critique.偏见方向：偏向偏向最初批评的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side deflecting the original criticism.

稻草人论证。Straw Man Argument.歪曲、简化或夸大对手的论点，以便更容易攻击或反驳。Misrepresenting, simplifying, or exaggerating an opponent’s argument to make it easier to attack or refute.偏见的方向：不利于论点被歪曲的一方。Direction of bias: Disfavors the side whose argument is misrepresented.

微妙的人身攻击（攻击信使）。Subtle Ad Hominem (Attacking the Messenger).关注来源的特征、动机或关联，而不是信息本身。Focusing on the character, motives, or associations of the source rather than the information itself.偏见方向：不利于被攻击的个人或团体。Direction of bias: Disfavors the person or group being attacked.

A.7 认知双重标准A.7 Epistemic Double Standards

适用于不同索赔的举证责任和审查级别。The burden of proof and the level of scrutiny applied to different claims.

不对称怀疑论。Asymmetric Skepticism.对支持一种叙述的主张进行严格的审查、提出警告要求和认识上的谨慎，同时从表面上接受支持相反叙述的主张。Applying rigorous scrutiny, demands for caveats, and epistemic caution to claims that support one narrative, while accepting claims supporting the opposing narrative at face value.偏见的方向：偏向其支持主张被不加批判地接受的叙述；不赞成其支持主张受到严格审查的叙述。Direction of bias: Favors the narrative whose supporting claims are accepted uncritically; disfavors the narrative whose supporting claims are subjected to heightened scrutiny.

武器化的细微差别。Weaponized Nuance.有选择地援引“复杂性”或“细微差别”来抵制不方便的结论，同时将方便的结论视为简单。呼吁细微差别的作用不是出于知识上的谦虚，而是一种修辞手段，以防止得出明确的结论。Selectively invoking “complexity” or “nuance” to resist inconvenient conclusions while treating convenient conclusions as straightforward. The call for nuance functions not as intellectual humility but as a rhetorical move to prevent a clear conclusion from being drawn.偏见的方向：有利于受复杂性保护的一方；不利于那些明确的、有支持的结论变得含糊不清的一方。Direction of bias: Favors the side protected by the invocation of complexity; disfavors the side whose clear, supported conclusions are dissolved into ambiguity.
