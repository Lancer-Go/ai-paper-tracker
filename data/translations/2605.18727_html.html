1 简介Introduction

机器人技术和实体代理的最新进展扩大了可学习和评估的行为范围，包括指令遵循和长期任务组合Recent advances in robotics and embodied agents have expanded the range of behaviors that can be learned and evaluated, including instruction following and long-horizon task composition[62, 14, 23, 16, 22, 21, 32, 33, 11, 60, 37]。然而，在现实的物理环境中评估这些系统仍然很困难。现有的具体代理基准. Yet evaluating these systems in realistic physical environments remains difficult. Existing embodied-agent benchmarks[51, 24, 58, 40]对语言基础进行高级评估have advanced evaluation of language grounding[33, 39, 11]和规划and planning[60, 37, 39]，但许多仍然依赖于模拟、粗动作空间或以夹具为中心的操纵, but many still rely on simulation, coarse action spaces, or gripper-centric manipulation[11, 33, 39, 38]。因此，这些分数为在物理场景中执行精确的、现实世界的多指操作时提供基础指令提供了有限的证据。. Consequently, these scores provide limited evidence for grounding instructions in physical scenes while executing precise, real-world multi-finger manipulation.

灵巧操作的基准通过推进丰富的接触操作来解决这个问题的补充方面Benchmarks for dexterous manipulation address a complementary aspect of this problem by advancing contact-rich manipulation[13, 31, 17, 4, 52]、抓, grasping[9, 55, 53, 57, 59]，以及手动操作, and in-hand manipulation[8, 15]。然而，这些基准通常通过孤立的低级技能而不是还需要视觉基础、顺序状态意识和进度验证的指令条件任务来评估运动能力. However, these benchmarks typically evaluate motor competence through isolated low-level skills rather than instruction-conditioned tasks that also require visual grounding, sequential state awareness, and progress verification[57, 9, 55]。因此，现有的评估范式在补充方面仍然不完整：体现主体基准. Consequently, existing evaluation paradigms remain incomplete in complementary ways: embodied-agent benchmarks[56, 27, 54]通常低估了现实世界的灵巧执行，而灵巧操作基准通常缺乏评估指令驱动的具体行为所需的任务结构。often under-emphasize real-world dexterous execution, whereas dexterous manipulation benchmarks often lack the task structure needed to assess instruction-driven embodied behavior.

为了评估这种耦合设置，我们寻求一个现实世界的任务域，其中语义基础、顺序状态跟踪和细粒度灵巧控制是成功所必需的。德州扑克桌面交互提供了这样一个域，因为纸牌和筹码定义了语义结构的目标：策略可能需要识别特定的纸牌，将其放置在指定位置，或移动所请求面额的筹码。这些任务对体力要求也很高，因为薄卡（To evaluate this coupled setting, we seek a real-world task domain in which semantic grounding, sequential state tracking, and fine-grained dexterous control are necessary for success. Texas Hold’em tabletop interaction provides such a domain because cards and chips define semantically structured targets: a policy may need to identify a specific card, place it at a designated position, or move a chip of a requested denomination. These tasks are also physically demanding, since thin cards (∼0.3\sim 0.3毫米厚）和切屑需要在摩擦和扰动不确定性下进行丰富的接触操作。此外，每次动作后桌面状态都会发生变化，因此感知错误、动作选择不正确、执行不灵巧或无法从受干扰的场景中恢复可能会导致失败。这种组合使得该域不再可用作一般扑克智力的测试，而是可用作以指令为条件的灵巧桌面操作的受控评估设置。mm thick) and chips require contact-rich manipulation under friction and disturbance uncertainty. Moreover, the tabletop state changes after each action, so failures can arise from perception errors, incorrect action selection, poor dexterous execution, or failure to recover from a disturbed scene. This combination makes the domain useful not as a test of general poker intelligence, but as a controlled evaluation setting for instruction-conditioned dexterous tabletop manipulation.

2 相关工作Related Work

灵巧的机器人操作。Dexterous Robotic Manipulation.

灵巧操纵研究多指机器人手如何执行平行夹具难以执行的丰富接触行为，包括抓取、手内重新定向、铰接物体操作和双手协调。早期的大规模学习系统和真实机器人平台表明，可以从演示、强化学习或大型离线数据集中学习灵巧控制Dexterous manipulation studies how multi-fingered robot hands can perform contact-rich behaviors that are difficult for parallel grippers, including grasping, in-hand reorientation, articulated-object operation, and bimanual coordination. Early large-scale learning systems and real-robot platforms showed that dexterous control can be learned from demonstrations, reinforcement learning, or large offline datasets[46, 3, 2, 17]。更广泛地说，通用机器人策略学习表明，基于变压器的多任务视觉运动策略可以跨语言指令和视觉观察进行缩放操作. More broadly, general robot policy learning has shown that transformer-based and multi-task visuomotor policies can scale manipulation across language instructions and visual observations[6, 62, 41]，而扩散策略和数据生成系统为模仿学习提供了表达性的动作分布和可扩展的监督, while diffusion policies and data-generation systems provide expressive action distributions and scalable supervision for imitation learning[14, 25, 36, 26]。随后的灵巧工作扩大了多指行为的范围，包括双手控制. Subsequent dexterous work has expanded the range of multi-finger behaviors, including bimanual hand control[13], 铰接对象操纵, articulated object manipulation[4]、模拟到真实的点云策略, sim-to-real point-cloud policies[44], 物体重新定向, object reorientation[10]，手动操作协议, in-hand manipulation protocols[15]，以及通过轨迹优化和强化学习解决的具有挑战性的模拟操纵任务, and challenging simulated manipulation tasks solved with trajectory optimization and reinforcement learning[8]。最近的工作目标是可扩展的灵巧抓取合成、动态切换、可微分抓取生成和大规模灵巧演示数据. More recent work targets scalable dexterous grasp synthesis, dynamic handover, differentiable grasp generation, and large-scale dexterous demonstration data[9, 55, 53, 59, 57]。这些方法极大地推进了机器人策略学习和低水平灵巧技能学习，但它们通常单独评估运动能力，而不是在完整的语言条件感知-决策-行动循环内评估运动能力。. These methods substantially advance robot policy learning and low-level dexterous skill learning, but they often evaluate motor competence in isolation rather than within a complete language-conditioned perception-decision-action loop.

Simulation frameworks such as ManiSkill, ManiSkill2, and ManiSkill3 improve scalability and standardized evaluation for manipulation learning[38, 18, 52]。灵巧操作基准和数据集，包括Adroit、ROBEL、RoboHive、D4RL、Bi-DexHands、DexArt、BODex、DexH2R、DexGraspNet 2.0和Dex1B，进一步为多指控制、抓取、关节、切换和大规模灵巧学习提供重要的测试平台. Dexterous manipulation benchmarks and datasets, including Adroit, ROBEL, RoboHive, D4RL, Bi-DexHands, DexArt, BODex, DexH2R, DexGraspNet 2.0, and Dex1B, further provide important testbeds for multi-finger control, grasping, articulation, handover, and large-scale dexterous learning[46, 2, 31, 17, 13, 4, 9, 55, 59, 57]。然而，大多数灵巧基准测试强调孤立的运动技能，而许多语言条件的具体基准测试则依赖于模拟、简单的抓手或以手臂为中心的操作。 DexHoldem 通过 ShadowHand 设置连接这些线. 体现代理人。Embodied Agents.

实体代理使用多模态基础模型在模拟或真实环境中进行感知、推理和高级动作选择。 PaLM-E 研究体现了跨异构观察和实施例的多模态推理Embodied agents use multimodal foundation models for perception, reasoning, and high-level action selection in simulated or real environments. Together, these works make it increasingly important to test whether embodied systems can close the loop from instruction understanding and visual reasoning to real-world dexterous execution. DexHoldem complements this literature by making the final action step physically demanding: the agent must not only identify what should be done, but also execute fine-grained multi-finger manipulation of thin cards and chips without disturbing the tabletop state.

3 DexHoldem 系统设计DexHoldem System Design

DexHoldem 旨在评估人类机器人德州扑克桌面环境中的灵巧操纵策略和具体代理。 该系统的概述如图所示DexHoldem is designed to evaluate dexterous manipulation policies and embodied agents in a human-robot Texas Hold’em tabletop setting. An overview of the system is shown inFigure˜2。 该系统有两个耦合层：一个实体代理捕获观察结果，维护结构化的游戏状态记忆，并选择下一个活动阶段，而多任务策略根据视觉观察、本体感受状态和任务条件执行相应的原语。 该循环支持等待、感知、推理、动作执行、可恢复故障后的重新执行以及当桌面状态无法安全继续时的人为干预。 我们在下面详细介绍了如何对原子策略任务、代理感知和全系统评估进行基准测试。. The system has two coupled layers: an embodied agent captures observations, maintains a structured game-state memory, and chooses the next activity stage, while a multi-task policy executes the corresponding primitive from visual observations, proprioceptive states, and a task condition. The loop supports waiting, perception, reasoning, action execution, re-execution after recoverable failures, and human intervention when the tabletop state cannot be safely continued. We provide details below on how we benchmark atomic policy tasks, agentic perception, and full-system evaluation.

3.1 灵巧手政策凳Dexterous Hand Policy Bench

策略基准将原子灵巧执行与游戏级决策隔离开来。它由德州扑克桌面上由 14 种语言指导的原语组成的标准化套件组成，涵盖纸牌拾取、纸牌放置、纸牌展示以及跨多种筹码面额的筹码推或拉。对于每个原语，DexHoldem 提供 105 次遥控演示，总共产生 1,470 次演示。我们对每个原语使用 100 个训练轨迹和 5 个验证轨迹的固定分割，因此每个策略都在相同的多任务数据预算下进行训练，并根据相同的原语规范进行评估The policy benchmark isolates atomic dexterous execution from game-level decision making. It consists of a standardized suite of 14 language-instructed primitives on the Texas Hold’em tabletop, spanning card pickup, card placement, card revealing, and chip pushing or pulling across multiple chip denominations. For each primitive, DexHoldem provides 105 teleoperated demonstrations, yielding 1,470 demonstrations in total. We use a fixed split of 100 training trajectories and 5 validation trajectories per primitive, so every policy is trained under the same multi-task data budget and evaluated against the same primitive specification inTable˜6.

所有策略都使用 ShadowHand-UR 平台的共享观察-操作接口。在每个推出步骤中，策略都会接收来自自上而下、第三人称和腕戴式摄像机的同步视觉观察、当前手臂和手的本体感受状态以及指定所请求原语的任务条件。它在共享的 30 维动作空间中输出关节位置目标的短视序列，其中手臂有 6 个维度，灵巧手有 24 个维度。该接口使得基准模型与模型无关：可以在不更改物理任务、机器人状态表示或推出协议的情况下比较任务训练的模仿策略、预训练的机器人策略和语言条件的视觉动作模型。All policies use a shared observation-action interface for the ShadowHand–UR platform. At each rollout step, a policy receives synchronized visual observations from top-down, third-person, and wrist-mounted cameras, the current arm and hand proprioceptive state, and a task condition specifying the requested primitive. It outputs a short-horizon sequence of joint-position targets in the shared 30-dimensional action space, with 6 dimensions for the arm and 24 for the dexterous hand. This interface makes the benchmark model-agnostic: task-trained imitation policies, pretrained robot policies, and language-conditioned vision-action models can be compared without changing the physical task, robot state representation, or rollout protocol.

我们用四级结果标准对每次物理部署进行评分，该标准将任务完成与可重用桌面状态的保存分开。 1 级，We score each physical rollout with a four-level outcome rubric that separates task completion from preservation of a reusable tabletop state. Level 1,场景保存成功scene-preserving success，表示请求的原语已完成，并且该表仍可用于后续操作。 2级，, means the requested primitive is completed and the table remains usable for subsequent actions. Level 2,破坏性完成disruptive completion，意味着目标已实现，但执行对场景的干扰足以阻止正常的继续。 3级，, means the goal is achieved but the execution disturbs the scene enough to prevent normal continuation. Level 3,任务失败task failure，表示图元尚未完成，但场景保持足够稳定以进行重试。 4级，, means the primitive is not completed, but the scene remains stable enough for retry. Level 4,破坏性故障disruptive failure，意味着原语失败并且必须重置环境才能继续。在德州扑克设置中，破坏性故障包括牌掉落、筹码移出可玩区域或可能损坏灵巧手的不安全接触。该标题将仅达到本地目标的策略与以长视野桌面交互所需的精度执行原语的策略区分开来。, means the primitive fails and the environment must be reset before continuing. In the Texas Hold’em setting, disruptive failures include dropped cards, displaced chips outside the playable region, or unsafe contact that risks damaging the dexterous hand. This rubric distinguishes policies that merely reach a local objective from those that execute primitives with the precision required for long-horizon tabletop interaction.

3.2 代理感知台Agentic Perception Bench

DexHoldem 还包括一个代理感知基准，它将视觉状态解析与下游路由、扑克动作选择和物理执行隔离开来。每个问题对应于从真实游戏轨迹中采样的一个桌面状态，与前驱状态上下文一起呈现给感知者——每个前驱状态及其代理视图捕获和预标记的结构化游戏状态信息。采样状态本身的代理视图捕获是感知者必须从原始像素解析的唯一帧。遵循系统视觉指南，感知者将当前状态解析为结构化游戏状态，该状态分解为八个感知挑战，每个挑战作为单独的评估器列进行评分：DexHoldem also includes an agentic perception benchmark that isolates visual state parsing from downstream routing, poker-action selection, and physical execution. At 10% data, pretrained initialization reduces validation loss by only 1.2% relative to random initialization. At higher data fractions, the reduction reaches 9.0%, 10.7%, and 11.3% at 20%, 50%, and 100% data, respectively, but both initializations still follow similar convergence and data-scaling trends. We therefore interpret pretraining mainly as an optimization and initialization advantage once sufficient dexterous-hand data is available, rather than as evidence of qualitative few-shot transfer. In particular, it does not make the 10% or 20% regimes approach full-data validation loss or materially reduce the amount of DexHoldem-specific data needed to fit the target action distribution. This differs from the strong data-efficiency effects often associated with large-scale pretraining in language and vision, where pretraining can reduce task-specific supervision through few-shot, low-label, or zero-shot transfer[7, 12, 29, 20, 45].
