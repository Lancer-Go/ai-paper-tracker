当人工智能模糊贡献的界限时：作者校准的实证研究When AI Blurs the Boundaries of Contribution: An Empirical Study of Authorship Calibration

摘要Abstract

人工智能 (AI)，尤其是生成式 AI 的广泛采用，引发了关于用户如何与这些系统交互以生成新内容的紧迫问题。在本文中，我们引入了作者身份校准的概念，定义为用户在与人工智能交互时对其实际作者身份的认识。使用 CoAuthor 数据集，我们凭经验研究了作者校准在不同用户之间的差异以及它与他们使用人工智能的频率之间的关系。我们的结果揭示了高度的可变性：严重依赖人工智能的用户往往会误判其作者身份，而那些使用人工智能频率较低的用户则表现出更准确的作者身份校准。这些发现表明人工智能可以模糊用户对自己作者身份的看法。在学习环境中，错误校准可能会影响元认知监控和学习策略，最终影响学习成果。因此，促进作者身份校准对于促进负责任且具有教育意义的人工智能集成至关重要。The broad adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI), especially Generative AI, raises pressing questions about how users interact with these systems to produce new content. In this paper, we introduce the concept of authorship calibration, defined as users’ awareness of their actual authorship when interacting with AI. Using the CoAuthor dataset, we empirically examine how authorship calibration varies across users and how it relates to their frequency of AI use. Our results reveal high variability: users relying heavily on AI tend to misjudge their authorship, whereas those using AI less frequently exhibit more accurate authorship calibration. These findings suggest that AI can obscure users’ perception of their own authorship. In learning contexts, miscalibration can affect metacognitive monitoring and learning strategies, ultimately impacting learning outcomes. Fostering authorship calibration then appears essential for promoting responsible and educationally meaningful AI integration.

2.2 人工智能辅助写作中的作者身份Authorship in AI-assisted Writing

2.3 元认知、自我调节学习和校准Metacognition, Self-Regulated Learning and Calibration

Calibration is related to SRL, with calibration accuracy influencing the efficiency of self-regulatory behaviorsStone (2000)。校准良好的学习者可以在整个 SRL 周期中准确地调整他们的学习策略，从而提高学习成果，而校准错误的学习者可能会实施无效的策略，从而导致表现不佳. Well-calibrated learners can accurately adapt their learning strategies throughout the SRL cycle, thereby enhancing learning outcomes, whereas miscalibrated learners may implement ineffective strategies, resulting in suboptimal performanceAlexander (2013).

在当今快速发展的教育环境中，确保学习者始终了解自己的能力以及人工智能在学习中所扮演的角色是一项重大挑战。然而，据我们所知，在人工智能在教育领域的应用中，元认知校准的探索仍然很少。特别是，缺乏探索校准与人工智能辅助工作中的作者身份之间关系的研究。因此，我们建议研究学习者与人工智能的交互如何影响我们所说的作者校准，即用户在人工智能辅助任务期间对自己贡献的认识。In today’s rapidly evolving educational landscape, ensuring that learners remain aware of their own abilities and of the role AI plays in their learning is a major challenge. Yet, to the best of our knowledge, metacognitive calibration remains poorly explored within the context of AI usage in education. In particular, studies exploring how calibration relates to authorship in AI‑assisted work are lacking. We therefore propose to investigate how learner–AI interactions influence what we refer to as authorship calibration, meaning a user’s awareness of his own contribution during an AI‑assisted task.

6 讨论和未来的工作Discussion and Future Work

我们的实验研究表明，虽然许多用户拥有准确的作者身份校准分数，但很大一部分用户仍然表现出较差的校准，要么高估或低估了他们的作者身份。这证实了作者身份校准在人工智能辅助的环境中并不是一个微不足道的结构，并且用户之间仍然存在重要的差异，因为并非所有人都能够准确地评估它（RQ1）。此外，我们的结果证实，作者身份校准的准确性根据写作任务期间人工智能使用的频率而变化（RQ2）。具体来说，低 AI 使用组中的用户表现出更准确的作者校准，但有轻微低估的倾向。这可能反映了更强的元认知意识，因为这些用户仍然关注哪怕是最小的人工智能帮助，从而最大限度地减少自己的贡献。相比之下，高 AI 使用组中的用户显示出较差的作者身份校准，并且倾向于高估它。广泛的人工智能交互似乎模糊了人类生成的内容和人工智能生成的内容之间的区别。这可能是“努力混合”现象的结果：提示、选择和整合人工智能建议所涉及的认知工作在主观上被体验为相当于生成原始文本，从而产生更高的作者感。 这些观察结果引起了人们对人工智能辅助学习环境中作者身份的严重担忧。鉴于作者身份校准和高阶元认知过程之间的概念联系，改进作者身份校准可能代表着在人工智能辅助环境中提高学习成果的一条有希望的途径。Our experimental study reveals that while numerous users have accurate authorship calibration scores, an important proportion still show poor calibration, either overestimating or underestimating their authorship. This confirms that authorship calibration is not a trivial construct in AI‑assisted contexts, and that important differences persist across users, as not all of them are able to accurately assess it (RQ1). Besides, our results confirms that the accuracy of authorship calibration vary depending on the frequency of AI use during the writing task (RQ2). Specifically, users in the Low‑AI usage group show a more accurate authorship calibration, but a slight tendency to underestimate it. This may reflect stronger metacognitive awareness, as these users remain attentive to even minimal AI assistance and consequently minimize their own contribution. In contrast, users in the High‑AI usage group show poorer authorship calibration, with a tendency to overestimate it. Extensive AI interactions then appears to blur the distinction between human‑generated and AI‑generated content. This may be the results of an “effort blend” phenomenon: the cognitive work involved in prompting, selecting, and integrating AI suggestions is subjectively experienced as equivalent to generating original text, resulting in a higher sense of authorship. These observations raise important concerns about authorship in AI‑assisted learning environments. Given the conceptual links between authorship calibration and higher‑order metacognitive processes, improving authorship calibration may represent a promising pathway to enhancing learning outcomes in AI‑assisted contexts.

尽管引入了作者身份校准的概念并提供了有前景的见解，但这项研究仍存在一些局限性。首先，作者身份仅在写作任务中受到审查，而不是在真实的教育环境中受到审查。探索更广泛的教育任务将允许进行更丰富的实验，最终提供对学习者与人工智能协作的更全面的理解。其次，低人工智能使用群体和高人工智能使用群体之间的区别基于简单而有效的方法。将更细粒度的学习分析管道应用于教育数据集将允许对学习者与人工智能交互模式进行更丰富的分类，最终提供关于特定交互模式如何影响作者校准的更丰富的见解。Despite introducing the concept of authorship calibration and offering promising insights, this study has several limitations. First, authorship is examined only within writing tasks rather than in authentic educational settings. Exploring a wider range of educational tasks would allow for richer experiments, ultimately providing a more comprehensive understanding of learner–AI collaboration. Second, the distinction between Low‑ and High‑AI usage groups is based on a simple yet effective methodology. Applying more fine‑grained Learning Analytics pipelines to educational datasets would allow for richer classifications of learner–AI interaction patterns, ultimately providing richer insights into how specific interaction patterns influence authorship calibration.

最后，这项研究为未来的工作开辟了有希望的方向。一个关键途径在于设计和评估支持作者身份校准的方法，例如通过调整的用户界面或个性化的反馈。通过提高学习者对其实际贡献的认识并促进更准确的作者校准，此类干预措施可以促进更适当的元认知参与，最终改善学习过程和结果。此外，还需要进一步研究来检验作者身份校准如何与更广泛的元认知过程相关，特别是在人工智能辅助学习环境中。Finally, this study opens promising directions for future work. A key avenue lies in designing and evaluating methods that support authorship calibration, e.g. through adapted user interfaces or personalized feedback. By increasing learners’ awareness about their actual contribution and fostering more accurate authorship calibration, such interventions may promote more appropriate metacognitive engagement, ultimately improving learning processes and outcomes. In addition, further research is needed to examine how authorship calibration relates to broader metacognitive processes, particularly within AI‑assisted learning environments.

7 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了作者身份校准，即用户在人工智能辅助任务中准确感知自己作者身份的程度。我们的结果显示，用户之间存在明显的差异，作者身份校准受到人工智能使用水平的影响。虽然一些用户对作者身份保持着准确的认识，但其他用户却严重错误地判断了他们的贡献，这揭示了人工智能是多么容易模糊人类和人工智能制作的内容之间的界限。 重要的是，作者身份校准为理解人工智能辅助环境中的学习提供了强大的视角。准确的作者身份校准可能支持元认知监控和知情参与，而错误校准则可能导致无效行为和较弱的学习成果。 随着人工智能广泛融入教育环境，帮助学习者了解他们的实际作者身份显得至关重要。设计加强作者身份校准的工具和教学策略将是促进人工智能在教育环境中负责任、透明和具有教育意义的整合的关键。This paper introduces authorship calibration, the extent to which users accurately perceive their own authorship during AI-assisted tasks. Our results show clear variability across users, with authorship calibration being influenced by the level of AI usage. While some users maintain an accurate sense of authorship, others substantially misjudge their contribution, revealing how easily AI can blur boundaries between human- and AI-produced content. Importantly, authorship calibration offers a powerful lens for understanding learning in AI‑assisted environments. Accurate authorship calibration may supports metacognitive monitoring and informed engagement, whereas miscalibration risks unproductive behaviors and weaker learning outcomes. As AI becomes broadly embedded in education settings, helping learners stay aware of their actual authorship appears essential. Designing tools and pedagogical strategies that strengthen authorship calibration will be key to fostering responsible, transparent, and educationally meaningful integration of AI in educational settings.
