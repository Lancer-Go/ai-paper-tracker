代理联邦学习：分布式训练编排的未来Agentic Federated Learning: The Future of Distributed Training Orchestration

Rafael O. Jarczewski &Gabriel U. Talasso⋆ &Leandro Villas⋆ &Allan M. de Souza⋆ Institute of Computing, University of Campinas and Hub of Artificial Intelligence and Cognitive Architectures - H.IAAC, Campinas, Brazil
摘要Abstract

尽管联邦学习（FL）承诺隐私和分布式协作，但其在现实场景中的有效性常常受到客户端的随机异质性和不可预测的系统动态的阻碍。现有的静态优化方法无法适应这些波动，导致资源利用不足和系统偏差。在这项工作中，我们提出了向 Agentic-FL 的范式转变，这是一个框架，其中基于语言模型的代理 (LMagents) 承担自主编排角色。与严格的协议不同，我们演示了服务器端代理如何通过上下文推理来减轻选择偏差，而客户端代理则充当本地监护人，动态管理隐私预算并使模型复杂性适应硬件限制。这种集成不仅解决了技术效率低下的问题，还标志着 FL 向去中心化生态系统的演变，在该生态系统中，协作是自主协商的，为基于激励的模型和算法正义的未来市场铺平了道路。我们讨论了这种方法的可靠性（幻觉）和安全挑战，概述了联合环境中弹性多代理系统的路线图。Although Federated Learning (FL) promises privacy and distributed collaboration, its effectiveness in real-world scenarios is often hampered by the stochastic heterogeneity of clients and unpredictable system dynamics. Existing static optimization approaches fail to adapt to these fluctuations, resulting in resource underutilization and systemic bias. In this work, we propose a paradigm shift towards Agentic-FL, a framework where Language Model-based Agents (LMagents) assume autonomous orchestration roles. Unlike rigid protocols, we demonstrate how server-side agents can mitigate selection bias through contextual reasoning, while client-side agents act as local guardians, dynamically managing privacy budgets and adapting model complexity to hardware constraints. More than just resolving technical inefficiencies, this integration signals the evolution of FL towards decentralized ecosystems, where collaboration is negotiated autonomously, paving the way for future markets of incentive-based models and algorithmic justice. We discuss the reliability (hallucinations) and security challenges of this approach, outlining a roadmap for resilient multi-agent systems in federated environments.

2.1 联邦学习Federated Learning

FL是一种分布式协作模型训练范例，使多个客户能够贡献其计算资源和本地数据来构建共享的全局模型is a distributed and collaborative paradigm for model training that enables multiple clients to contribute their computational resources and local data toward building a shared, global modelMcMahan et al. (2017)。在此设置中，参与者收到一个初始模型，其结构取决于特定应用程序和环境，并使用其私有数据集在本地对其进行训练。在本地训练之后，每个客户端将其更新的模型参数发送到中央服务器，中央服务器通常使用与本地数据集大小成比例的加权平均值来聚合客户端更新，并将更新的全局模型广播回客户端。这个过程会在多轮通信中重复，直到全局模型收敛。. In this setting, participants receive an initial model whose structure depends on the specific application and environment, and train it locally using their private datasets. After local training, each client sends its updated model parameters to a central server, which aggregates the client updates, typically using a weighted average proportional to the local dataset sizes, and broadcasts the updated global model back to the clients. This process is repeated over multiple communication rounds until global model convergence.

这种模式具有多种优势，包括增强数据隐私和遵守数据保护法规，因为原始数据永远不会离开客户。根据应用程序的不同，这些客户端可以是个人智能手机、企业、医院或持有敏感信息的金融机构。而且，This paradigm offers several advantages, including enhanced data privacy and compliance with data protection regulations, as raw data never leaves the clients. Depending on the application, these clients can be personal smartphones, enterprises, hospitals, or financial institutions holding sensitive information. Moreover,FL利用分布式计算资源，并行化训练过程并减少对单个集中节点的依赖。其固有的分布式特性还提供了对更多样化和更具代表性的数据集的访问，从而实现更好的模型泛化和鲁棒性。leverages distributed computational resources, parallelizing the training process and reducing dependency on a single centralized node. 6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 Agentic-FL，这是一种不断发展的范式转变In this work, we introduce Agentic-FL, a paradigm shift that evolvesFL从静态优化过程到自主交易生态系统。我们认为，以非独立同分布数据异构性、资源波动性和安全风险为特征的分布式场景固有的复杂性，需要的不仅仅是固定的算法；它需要认知适应。from a static optimization process to an autonomous trading ecosystem. We argue that the inherent complexity of distributed scenarios, characterized by non-IID data heterogeneity, resource volatility, and security risks, demands more than fixed algorithms; it demands cognitive adaptation.

我们的分析表明，基于 LLM 的代理的集成在两个方面提供了强大的解决方案：在服务器上，代理充当协调者，能够通过上下文聚合和动态选择来对抗系统偏差；在客户端，代理充当“本地看门人”，管理隐私预算并实时调整培训，以确保包含资源有限的设备。Our analysis demonstrated that the integration of LLM-based Agents offers a robust solution on two fronts: on the Server, agents act as orchestrators capable of combating systemic bias through contextual aggregation and dynamic selection; on the Client, agents operate as “local gatekeepers” managing privacy budgets and adapting training in real time to ensure the inclusion of resource-limited devices.

通过降低异构客户端的进入壁垒并提高抵御攻击的能力，Agentic-FL不仅提高了训练效率，而且促进了更公平、更具代表性的系统。展望未来，我们将这些代理视为去中心化市场模型的先驱，其中协作不再仅仅是一种计算协议，而是成为一种智能的、自我管理的经济交换。By mitigating barriers to entry for heterogeneous clients and increasing resilience against attacks, Agentic-FL not only improves training efficiency but also promotes a fairer and more representative system. Looking to the future, we envision these agents as the forerunners of decentralized market models, where collaboration ceases to be merely a computational protocol and becomes an intelligent, self-managed economic exchange.
