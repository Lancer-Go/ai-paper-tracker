3D 膝关节 MRI 分割和跨领域通用性强度标准化方法的系统基准A Systematic Benchmark of Intensity Normalisation Methods for 3D Knee MRI Segmentation and Cross-Domain Generalisability

Oliver Mills    Philip Conaghan    Samuel Relton
摘要Abstract

强大的开箱即用性能对于医学影像中深度学习模型的临床部署至关重要。影响模型通用性的一个重要但尚未充分探索的因素是强度归一化，特别是对于磁共振成像 (MRI)，其中图像强度因扫描仪和协议而异。在这项研究中，我们系统地比较了七种归一化方法及其对膝盖 MRI 半月板分割的 3D U-Net 模型性能的影响。这些方法包括标准缩放方法、基于直方图的技术和基于高斯混合模型 (GMM) 的方法。模型在 IWOAI 2019 数据集上进行训练，并在内部和外部测试集 (SKM-TEA) 上进行评估，以评估普遍性。内部性能相似，但外部数据差异显着，Z 分数、Nyúl 直方图匹配和 CLAHE 比其他方法表现出更强的鲁棒性。然而，与数据集之间观察到的显着性能下降相比，这些差异很小。总体而言，虽然强度归一化对模型的普遍性有可测量的影响，但其影响相对于领域转移的影响是有限的，这凸显了需要补充策略来实现稳健的部署。Robust out-of-the-box performance is essential for the clinical deployment of deep learning models in medical imaging. An important but underexplored factor affecting model generalisability is intensity normalisation, particularly for magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), where image intensities vary across scanners and protocols. In this study, we systematically compared seven normalisation methods and their impact on the performance of a 3D U-Net model for meniscus segmentation from knee MRI. The methods included standard scaling approaches, histogram-based techniques, and a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM)-based method. Models were trained on the IWOAI 2019 dataset and evaluated on both internal and external test sets (SKM-TEA) to assess generalisability. Performance was similar internally but differences were significant on external data, with Z-score, Nyúl histogram matching, and CLAHE showing greater robustness than other methods. However, these differences were small compared to the significant performance drop observed between datasets. Overall, while intensity normalisation had a measurable effect on model generalisability, its impact was limited relative to the effects of domain shift, highlighting the need for complementary strategies for robust deployment.

1 简介Introduction

分割是医学图像分析中的一项关键任务，可以测量组织边界和体积。深度学习模型在该领域表现出了强大的性能，提高了效率和一致性Segmentation is a key task in medical image analysis, enabling measurement of tissue boundaries and volumes. Deep learning models have shown strong performance in this domain, offering improved efficiency and consistency[27]。然而，它们对数据特征变化很敏感，并且在与其训练数据分布不同的图像上表现不佳. However, they are sensitive to data characteristic changes and perform poorly on images that differ from their training data distribution[20]。由于扫描仪类型、成像协议和患者群体的不同，这个问题（称为域偏移）在医学成像中很常见。在 MRI 中，即使使用同一台扫描仪，信号也会发生变化。预处理和归一化技术通过保持图像强度分布一致来帮助减轻这些影响，从而稳定训练并提高模型鲁棒性. This issue, known as domain shift, is common in medical imaging due to variations in scanner type, imaging protocol, and patient population. In MRI, signal variations occur even when the same scanner is used. Preprocessing and normalisation techniques help mitigate these effects by keeping image intensity distributions consistent, and in doing so stabilising training and improving model robustness[10]。虽然对目标域数据进行微调模型可以帮助减少域转移的影响，但由于医学成像中缺乏准确的注释，这通常是不可行的。因此，模型通常根据来自选定中心的数据进行训练，这凸显了对在看不见的机构上表现良好的稳健模型的需求。最近的工作探索了基于人工智能的协调和领域适应方法，包括风格转移和对抗方法，以及 ComBat 等统计技术. While fine-tuning models on target-domain data can help reduce the impact of domain shift, it is often infeasible due to the scarcity of accurate annotations in medical imaging. As a result, models are usually trained on data from select centres, highlighting the need for robust models that perform well on unseen institutions. Recent work has explored AI-based approaches for harmonisation and domain adaptation, including style transfer and adversarial methods, as well as statistical techniques such as ComBat[19]。然而，这些方法通常需要访问目标域数据以及额外的训练或微调，这在许多部署设置中可能不切实际。相比之下，不需要进一步再训练的标准强度归一化方法在提高分布外鲁棒性方面的作用在脑 MRI 之外受到的关注有限。本研究通过系统地比较膝关节 MRI 中 3D 半月板分割的七种强度归一化方法来解决这一差距。. However, these approaches typically require access to target-domain data and additional training or fine-tuning, which may not be practical in many deployment settings. In contrast, the role of standard intensity normalisation methods not requiring further retraining in improving out-of-distribution robustness has received limited attention outside of brain MRI. This study addresses that gap by systematically comparing seven intensity normalisation methods for 3D meniscus segmentation in knee MRI.

半月板分割对于骨关节炎研究中的定量评估非常重要，以便研究组织在疾病途径中发挥的作用Meniscus segmentation is important for quantitative assessment in osteoarthritis research in order to investigate the role the tissue plays in the disease pathway[8, 22, 1, 24, 9]。然而，由于组织尺寸小、形状不规则且与周围组织的对比度低，这仍然是一项具有挑战性的任务. However, it remains a challenging task due to the tissue’s small size, irregular shape, and low contrast with surrounding tissues[36]。这些因素使得精确分割变得困难，即使对于人类专家来说也是如此，并且增加了模型对图像强度和质量变化的敏感性。因此，半月板分割提供了一个有用的测试用例，用于评估强度归一化方法如何影响不同 MRI 数据的模型鲁棒性。虽然强度标准化已在脑部和乳腺 MRI 中进行了研究，但其在肌肉骨骼成像中的作用仍未得到充分探索。. These factors make accurate segmentation difficult, even for human experts, and increase model sensitivity to variations in image intensity and quality. Therefore, meniscus segmentation provides a useful test case for evaluating how intensity normalisation methods affect model robustness across diverse MRI data. While intensity normalisation has been studied in brain and breast MRI, its role in musculoskeletal imaging remains underexplored.

1.1 标准化技术Normalisation techniques

归一化是计算机视觉中的标准预处理步骤，通过将图像强度纳入一致的范围来提高模型训练的稳定性和性能Normalisation is a standard preprocessing step in computer vision that improves model training stability and performance by bringing image intensities into a consistent range[23]。两种常见的归一化技术是 Z 分数和最小-最大缩放，它们都对图像强度值应用线性变换。在使用自然图像的一般计算机视觉任务中，Z 分数归一化通常使用数据集范围内的均值和方差来重新缩放图像。然而，由于图像间强度变化，这种方法不太适合 MRI。相反，通常使用图像特定的方法和标准差来转换图像。最小-最大缩放将图像重新缩放到指定范围，通常为 [0, 1]。此方法比 Z 分数对异常值更敏感，因此经常使用更稳健的最小-最大缩放方法，其中图像强度在转换之前被剪切到给定的百分位数，通常是 [1st, 99th] 百分位数. Two common normalisation techniques are Z-score and min-max scaling, which both apply linear transformations to image intensity values. In general computer vision tasks with natural images, Z-score normalisation typically uses dataset-wide mean and variance to rescale images. However, this approach is less suitable for MRI due to inter-image intensity variations. Instead, images are often transformed using image-specific means and standard deviations. Min-max scaling rescales images to a specified range, typically [0, 1]. This method is more sensitive to outliers than Z-score, so a more robust method of min-max scaling is often used, where image intensities are clipped to a given percentile before transformation, commonly [1st, 99th] percentiles[18].

然而，这些方法无法考虑数据集之间的采集变异性，也无法确保相同的解剖结构落在图像之间一致的强度范围内。与 CT 等成像方式不同，CT 等成像方式的像素强度具有内在含义，可以有针对性地选择组织类型（称为加窗），而 MRI 则在扫描过程中表现出较大的强度差异，这使得标准化和定量分析成为一项挑战However, these methods fail to account for acquisition variability across datasets or ensure that the same anatomical structures fall within consistent intensity ranges across images. Unlike imaging modalities such as CT, where pixel intensity has an intrinsic meaning which allows for targeted selection of tissue types (known as windowing), MRI exhibit large intensity variance across scans, making standardisation and quantitative analysis a challenge[28]。为了解决这个问题，Nyúl 和 Udupa 提出了一种直方图匹配方法，以更好地标准化 MR 图像强度。引入于. To address this, Nyúl and Udupa proposed a histogram matching method to better standardise MR image intensities. Introduced in[32]并精炼于and refined in[31]，根据训练图像计算直方图“模板”，并通过将直方图地标与该模板匹配来转换新图像。这种方法已成为 MRI 研究的标准，并已被证明在各种应用中有效，例如识别多发性硬化症, a histogram “template” is calculated from training image, and new images are transformed by matching histogram landmarks to this template. This method has become standard across MRI studies, and has been shown to be effective in various applications, such as identifying multiple sclerosis[40]。然而，有人担心这种方法并不独立于生物变异，因此可能会删除重要信息. However, there are concerns that this method is not independent of biological variation and may therefore remove important information[41]。这项研究重点关注大脑 MRI，提出了白条纹标准化，即使用特定的强度峰值（对应于正常出现的白质）进行标准化。然而，膝部 MRI 不存在等效方法，其他新提出的 MRI 标准化和协调方法也仅关注脑 MRI. That study, which focused on brain MRI, proposed white stripe normalisation, which uses a specific intensity peak (corresponding to normal-appearing white matter) for standardisation. However, no equivalent method exists for knee MRI, and other newly proposed MRI standardisation and harmonisation methods also focus solely on brain MRI[45, 39, 3].

MR 图像通常对比度低或不均匀，导致难以区分和分割特定结构。虽然 Z 分数和最小-最大缩放等归一化方法侧重于标准化强度分布，但它们对图像的对比度几乎没有影响。因此，经常使用对比度增强技术。常见的方法涉及修改图像的强度直方图MR images often suffer from low or non-uniform contrast, making it difficult to distinguish and segment specific structures. While normalisation methods such as Z-score and min-max scaling focus on standardising intensity distributions, they have little effect on the contrast of the image. Therefore, techniques for contrast enhancement are often used. Common approaches involve modification of the intensity histogram of an image[13]。直方图均衡 (HE) 通过缩放强度分布的累积分布函数来提高图像的全局对比度，从而产生更均匀的直方图. Histogram equalisation (HE) improves the global contrast of an image by scaling the intensity distribution’s cumulative distribution function, resulting in a more uniform histogram[14]。然而，HE 没有考虑空间强度变化，这可能导致局部对比度增强不足，以及强度不均匀区域的过度增强和增强不足。自适应直方图均衡 (AHE) 通过在本地应用 HE 来解决此限制. However, HE does not account for spatial intensity variations, which can lead to inadequate local contrast enhancement, as well as over- and under-enhancement in regions with intensity inhomogeneities. Adaptive histogram equalisation (AHE) addresses this limitation by applying HE locally[35, 11]。然而，这可能会过度放大均匀区域中的噪声。对比度限制自适应直方图均衡 (CLAHE) 通过使用限幅限制限制此类区域的增强来缓解此问题. However, this can over-amplify noise in homogeneous regions. Contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalisation (CLAHE) mitigates this issue by using a clipping limit to restrict enhancement in such regions[14]，并已被证明可以提高深度学习模型的性能, and has been been shown to improve performance of deep learning models[11, 12].

1.2 相关作品Related works

已经进行了研究，研究强度归一化方法对从 MRI 中提取放射组学特征的鲁棒性的影响，特别是在大脑和乳腺成像中Studies investigating the effect of intensity normalisation methods on the robustness of radiomics feature extraction from MRI have been performed, particularly in brain and breast imaging[2, 25, 38]。这些研究发现，强度归一化提高了特征的鲁棒性，尽管最佳方法因特定任务和数据集而异。然而，他们没有研究分割等深度学习任务，并且肌肉骨骼成像方面的工作仍然有限。. These studies found that intensity normalisation improved feature robustness, although the optimal method varied across specific tasks and datasets. However, they did not investigate deep learning tasks such as segmentation, and work on musculoskeletal imaging remains limited.

很少有研究系统地评估预处理如何影响 MRI 中的深度学习分割性能，尤其是在外部数据集上。一项研究仅在模型微调后测试外部数据Few studies have systematically evaluated how preprocessing affects deep learning segmentation performance in MRI, especially on external datasets. One study tested external data only after model fine-tuning[21]，而另一个人仅根据内部数据评估绩效, while another evaluated performance solely on internal data[4]。两项研究都没有得出结论性的结果，只有重新采样才能显着提高性能. Neither study found conclusive results, with only resampling providing noticeable improvements in performance[4]。相比之下，CT 研究发现加窗提高了分割性能. In contrast, a CT study found that windowing improved segmentation performance[18]，但由于缺乏内在的组织特异性强度值，该方法不适用于 MRI。据我们所知，只有一项研究调查了 MRI 标准化应用于其他中心的数据时对分割性能的影响, but this method is not applicable to MRI due to its lack of intrinsic tissue-specific intensity values. To our knowledge, only one study has investigated the effect of MRI standardisation on segmentation performance when applied to data from other centres[10]，但这侧重于大脑 FLAIR MRI，并采用针对该模式量身定制的最佳性能方法。这些研究凸显了对预处理如何影响 MRI 分割性能的理解有限，特别是在域转移和脑成像之外。, but this focused on brain FLAIR MRI, with the best-performing method tailored to that modality. These studies highlight the limited understanding of how preprocessing impacts MRI segmentation performance, particularly under domain shift and outside of brain imaging.

一项相关研究使用与当前工作相同的两个数据集来比较预训练模型在未见过的数据集上的性能A related study used the same two datasets as the present work to compare the performance of pretrained models on unseen datasets[37]。研究发现，序列变化导致的性能下降比扫描仪或中心效应更大。研究结果强调了解决医学成像领域转移的重要性，特别是当它涉及不同的成像协议时，并强调需要探索预处理策略，例如有助于缓解这一问题的强度归一化。. It found that sequence changes caused larger performance drops than scanner or centre effects. Findings emphasise the importance of addressing domain shift in medical imaging, especially when it involves different imaging protocols, and highlight the need to explore preprocessing strategies such as intensity normalisation that could help mitigate this issue.

2.3 标准化方案Normalisation schemes

比较了七种不同的数据预处理方法：Seven different data preprocessing methods were compared:

  • •

    Z 分数Z-score：每个图像均根据其各自的平均值和标准差进行标准化。: Each image was standardised by its individual mean and standard deviation.

  • •

    最小-最大缩放Min-max scaling：图像强度线性缩放至 [0, 1]。: Image intensities were linearly scaled to [0, 1].

  • •

    稳健的最小-最大缩放Robust min-max scaling：在执行最小-最大缩放之前，图像被裁剪到第 1 个和第 99 个百分位数。: Images were clipped to the 1st and 99th percentiles before performing min-max scaling.

  • •

    HE（直方图均衡）HE (histogram equalisation)：强度直方图在整个图像上全局均衡。: The intensity histogram was equalised globally across the entire image.

  • •

    CLAHE（对比度限制自适应直方图均衡）CLAHE (contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalisation)：使用大小为图像尺寸 1/10 的内核对图像直方图进行局部均衡。应用 0.01 的剪辑限制以防止过度增强。 HE 和 CLAHE 是使用 scikit-image 库实现的: Image histograms were equalised locally using kernels of size 1/10th of the image dimensions. A clip limit of 0.01 was applied to prevent over-enhancement. HE and CLAHE were implemented using the scikit-image library[43].

  • •

4 讨论Discussion

正如预期的那样，考虑到与训练图像的共享域，内部测试数据在不同标准化方法上的性能变化不大。大多数方法的性能相当，只有 HE 和 CLAHE 的性能明显较差。这可能是由于它们不一致的对比度增强进一步破坏了整个数据集中的组织特异性强度意义。Performance varied little on internal test data across normalisation methods, as expected given the shared domain with training images. Most methods performed comparably, with only HE and CLAHE performing significantly worse. This was likely due to their inconsistent contrast enhancement further disrupting tissue-specific intensity meaning across the dataset.

在存在域转移的外部测试集上，观察到方法之间存在微小但具有统计显着性的差异，LMM 分析确定了 Nyúl、Z-score 和 CLAHE 具有统计显着性优势。相对于图像之间的差异，方法间的差异很小（图 1）。On the external test set, where domain shift was present, small but statistically significant differences between methods were observed, with LMM analysis identifying statistically significant advantages for Nyúl, Z-score, and CLAHE. Inter-method variation was small relative to variation between images (Fig.1），突出了这个分割任务的难度。方法之间的差异不大（), highlighting the difficulty of this segmentation task. Differences between methods were modest (∼1%\sim 1\%DSC），而所有方法在内部和外部数据集之间都经历了更大的性能下降（DSC), whereas all methods experienced a substantially larger performance drop between internal and external datasets (∼10%\sim 10\%DSC），表明域移位对性能的影响比归一化方法的选择大得多。值得注意的是，边际DSC), indicating that domain shift has a much greater impact on performance than the choice of normalisation method. 内部和外部绩效之间的差异揭示了方法特定的行为。最小-最大缩放的性能下降最大，可能是由于对数据集特定强度异常值的敏感性，而 Nyúl 表现出最小的下降，表明对跨数据集强度变化具有更大的鲁棒性。 CLAHE 在内部测试集上表现较低，但在外部方法中名列前茅，这表明对比度改变可能在域转移下提供一些普适性优势。相比之下，基于 GMM 的方法在内部表现良好，但在外部表现较差，这可能是由于影响峰值估计的数据集之间的强度分布差异所致（图 1）。Differences between internal and external performance revealed method-specific behaviour. Min-max scaling suffered the largest performance drop, likely due to sensitivity to dataset-specific intensity outliers, whereas Nyúl showed the smallest drop, suggesting greater robustness to cross-dataset intensity variations. CLAHE, which showed lower performance on the internal test set, ranked among the top methods externally, suggesting that contrast alteration might offer some generalisability benefits under domain shift. In contrast, the GMM-based approach performed well internally but less well externally, likely due to differences in intensity profiles between datasets affecting peak estimation (Fig.4a).

先进的协调和领域适应方法，包括对抗性方法、风格转移和基于 ComBat 的技术，已被提出来解决医学成像中的领域转移问题。这些方法通常依赖于对目标域数据的访问或特定于任务的适应。相比之下，本研究评估了不需要目标域信息的简单强度归一化方法，为比较提供了实用的基线。Advanced harmonisation and domain adaptation methods, including adversarial approaches, style transfer, and ComBat-based techniques, have been proposed to address domain shift in medical imaging. These approaches often rely on access to target-domain data or task-specific adaptation. In contrast, this study evaluates simple intensity normalisation methods that require no target-domain information, providing a practical baseline for comparison.

5 结论Conclusion

本研究评估了不同强度归一化方法对膝关节 MRI 半月板内部和外部分割的影响。内部性能差异很小，所有方法在同一领域内训练和测试时表现相似。在外部数据上，使用线性混合效应模型进行的倍数分析揭示了微小但具有统计显着性的差异，Nyúl、Z-score 和 CLAHE 在基于数量的指标上优于其他方法。然而，与内部和外部数据之间观察到的性能下降相比，这些差异并不大。这表明，虽然归一化选择可以对模型的通用性产生可测量的影响，但相对于域转移的影响，其贡献是有限的。This study evaluated the impact of different intensity normalisation methods on knee MRI meniscus segmentation, both internally and externally. Internal performance differences were minimal, with all methods performing similarly when trained and tested within the same domain. On external data, fold-level analysis with linear mixed effect modelling revealed small but statistically significant differences, with Nyúl, Z-score, and CLAHE outperforming other methods on volume-based metrics. However, these differences were modest compared to the performance drop observed between internal and external data. This suggests that, while normalisation choice can have a measurable effect on model generalisability, its contribution is limited relative to the impact of domain shift.
