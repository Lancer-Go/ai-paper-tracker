摘要ABSTRACT

生成式人工智能正在改变文化艺术品的创造和传播方式，以及我们对创造力本身的理解。研究人员对于这些工具是否丰富或贫乏文化存在分歧，我们认为，这种分歧很大程度上来自于将创造力的两个不同组成部分混为一谈：新颖性（单一制品的属性）和多样性（群体的属性）。我们进一步认为，生成式人工智能背景下的创造力最好被理解为混合集体的属性，或者交互的人和算法的群体，而不是个人的属性。人工智能辅助构思可靠地提高了个体产出的新颖性，同时缩小了总体多样性，但这并不是将机器纳入循环的必然结果。由于人类和模型以互补的方式进行搜索，混合群体可以超越任何一种单独的群体，并且更加多样化，机器发现的解决方案可以进入人类文化并持续存在。决定结果的是组成：存在哪些主体、比例如何以及它们如何连接。问题不再是人工智能是否有助于或损害创造力，而是哪种混合可以让个人收益积累而不侵蚀集体多样性。Generative AI is changing how cultural artifacts are created and circulated, and with it our understanding of creativity itself. Researchers disagree about whether these tools enrich or impoverish culture, and we argue that much of that disagreement comes from conflating two distinct components of creativity: novelty, a property of single artifacts, and diversity, a property of populations. We argue further that creativity in the context of generative AI is best understood as a property of hybrid collectives, or populations of interacting people and algorithms, rather than of individuals. AIassisted ideation reliably raises the novelty of individual output while narrowing diversity in the aggregate, but this is not an inevitable consequence of putting machines in the loop. Because humans and models search in complementary ways, mixed groups can outperform and out-diversify groups of either kind alone, and machine-discovered solutions can enter human culture and persist there. What decides the outcome is composition: which agents are present, in what proportion, and how they are connected. The question is no longer whether AI helps or harms creativity, but which mixtures let individual gains accumulate without eroding collective diversity.

1 简介1 INTRODUCTION

生成式人工智能现已嵌入到写作、音乐、图像和代码等创造性工作中，改变了文化艺术品的创造和流通方式。这种转变加剧了关于创造力本身的持续争论：它是个人的能力，还是连接他们的系统的属性？以创造力理论为基础Generative AI is now embedded in creative work across writing, music, images and code, altering how cultural artifacts are created and circu­ lated. This shift sharpens an ongoing debate about creativity itself: is it a capacity of individuals, or a property of the systems that connect them? Building on theories of creativity1以及最近关于集体和计算创造力的工作and recent work on collective and computational creativity2,3，我们支持后者——生成式人工智能时代的创造力最好被理解为人类和算法交互的混合集体的新兴属性。这一框架遵循更广泛的社会描述，即以反馈循环、分布式认知和多层次动态为特征的人类算法系统, we argue for the latter—that creativity in the age of generative AI is best understood as an emergent property of hybrid collectives of interacting humans and algorithms. This fram­ ing follows broader accounts of societies as human–algorithm systems characterized by feedback loops, distributed cognition and multi­level dynamics4,5 .

该文献中的大部分分歧来自于将两种措施视为一种措施。新颖性是根据参考集衡量的单个工件的属性（即，它与之前的内容相差多远），而多样性是集合本身的属性（即，其成员彼此之间有多少差异）。有一种工具可以提高前者，同时降低后者，但该领域仍然缺乏在人口层面上评估两者的一致方法。借助认知科学、计算社会科学和文化进化，我们询问将人工智能嵌入创意集体中是否会扩大还是缩小社会的想象空间。Much of the disagreement in this literature comes from treating two measures as one. Novelty is a property of a single artifact measured against a reference set (i.e., how far it departs from what came before), whereas diversity is a property of a set itself (i.e., how much its mem­ bers differ from one another). A tool can raise the former while lowering the latter, and the field still lacks agreed ways of assessing either at the population level. Drawing on cognitive science, computational social science and cultural evolution, we ask whether embedding AI in creative collectives widens or narrows the space of what a society can imagine.

迈向混合创造力：人工智能作为合作伙伴Toward hybrid creativity: AI as a partner

除了个人行为之外，人工智能系统现在还塑造集体行为和社会系统AI systems now shape collective behavior and social systems in addi­ tion to individual behavior5,20,21。非人类演员可以以令人惊讶的方式改变群体动态：放置在网络中心并注入少量随机噪音的机器人帮助人类群体摆脱了协调游戏的死胡同，在协调游戏中，每个玩家都必须选择与所有邻居不同的颜色，并且只能看到他们的直接邻居，而噪音更大或更外围的机器人则不能. Non­human actors can alter group dynamics in surprising ways: bots placed centrally in a network and in­ jecting small amounts of random noise helped human groups out of the dead ends of a coordination game, in which every player had to choose a color differing from all of their neighbors’ and could see only their immediate neighbors, while noisier or more peripheral bots did not22。最近，仅人工智能网络开始时比仅人类或混合网络具有更高的创造力和更高的集体多样性，但在连续几代中，混合网络在多样性上超过了它们，因为人工智能代理保留了很少的故事，而人类链保留了连续性. More recently, AI­only networks began with higher rated creativity and higher collective diversity than human­only or mixed ones, yet over successive generations the mixed networks overtook them in diversity, because AI agents retained little of the stories they were given while human chains preserved continuity23 .

为什么混合系统的行为应该与人类系统不同？有两个答案。首先是对伴侣的信念改变了行为。那些认为自己在用人工智能即兴创作的音乐家，即使隐藏的人类正在做出反应，也会降低他们对音乐性的期望，并感觉从社会评判中解放出来，这加剧了创作风险Why should a mixed system behave differently from a human one? There are two answers. The first is that beliefs about the partner change behavior. Musicians who think they are improvising with an AI, even when a hidden human is responding, lower their expectations of musicality and feel released from social judgment, which emboldens creative risks24。人们对合作伙伴也没有固定的看法，他们使用与朋友、学生或经理相同的工具. People do not hold a fixed view of the partner, either, casting the same tool as friend, student or manager25;仅仅相信同事是人工智能也可以提高一致性; merely believing an associate is an AI can also increase conformity26。第二个答案是人类和模型生成信息的方式不同。人类保留他们所获得的东西，而模型则为了追求新材料而丢弃它. The second answer is that humans and models generate information differently. Humans preserve what they are given, while models discard it in pursuit of new material23，并且在集体搜索中，人类广泛探索，而模型则进行探索，在 99% 的轮次中，当无人处理时，会采用单一策略, and in collective search humans explore broadly while models exploit, settling into a single strategy in 99% of rounds when left alone27。这种差异就是为什么混合物可以单独击败任何一个种群，这与群体多样性的长期研究结果一致. 设计和信念一样重要：人类与人工智能协作的意义是通过轮流而不是单一的静态提示来积累的，因此只允许严格的一次性请求的工具可能会排除高效协作所需的共享代理Design matters as much as belief: meaning in human–AI collaboration accumulates through turn­taking rather than a single static prompt, so tools that permit only rigid one­shot requests probably foreclose the shared agency that productive collaboration requires29 .

3 混合创造力的机制3 MECHANISMS OF HYBRID CREATIVITY

我们围绕变异、选择和保留的标准文化进化周期组织机制We organize the mechanisms around the standard cultural­evolution­ ary cycle of variation, selection and retention30，其中创意生产经历三个阶段：生成变体、评估变体、传播幸存者。跨越这些阶段的是新颖性产生的机制，分为组合、在约束空间内的搜索以及空间本身的转变, under which creative production runs through three stages: generating variants, evaluating them, and transmitting the survivors. Cutting across those stages are the mechanisms by which novelty arises, divided into combination, search within a space of constraints, and transformation of the space itself31。阶段和机制是单独的轴而不是嵌套的层次结构，因为集体可以在生成时组合，并在选择时再次组合。. Stages and mechanisms are separate axes rather than a nested hierarchy, since a collective can combine while generating and com­ bine again while selecting.

通过评估选拔Selection through evaluation

选择决定了哪些产生的想法能够生存下来，而评估则在两个层面上进行，而文献往往将这两个层面模糊在一起：在单个创造者内部，他们通过多次筛选过滤自己的选择；以及跨创造者，他们的独立选择充当群体层面的选择压力。Selection determines which generated ideas survive, and evaluation operates at two levels that the literature tends to blur together: within a single creator, who filters their own options over many passes, and across creators, whose independent choices act as a population­level selection pressure.

5 后果5 CONSEQUENCES

上面回顾的个人水平收益是真实的。它们在人口层面上加起来是多少是一个单独的问题，答案取决于集体的组成方式。The individual­level gains reviewed above are real. What they add up to at the level of a population is a separate question, and the answer depends on how the collective is assembled.

趋同与发散Convergence and divergence

传播研究表明，构成、工具和文化背景在很大程度上决定了结果。人工智能通常被描述为搅拌机，它是扩大还是压缩空间取决于谁持有它。仅人工智能的故事链——每个智能体选择并重写邻居故事的迭代序列——从最多样化的开始，然后聚合，而混合的人类-人工智能网络在连续几代的多样性中超越了它们，大概是因为人类的连续性抵消了机器的新颖性追求Studies that build in transmission suggest that composition, tools and cultural context largely decide outcomes. AI is often described as a blender, and whether it expands or compresses the space depends on who is holding it. AI­only story chains—iterated sequences in which each agent selects and rewrites a neighbor’s story—start out the most diverse and then converge, while mixed human–AI networks overtake them in diversity over successive generations, presumably because human continuity offsets machine novelty­seeking23。当不同的人工智能模型（例如 GPT 和 Claude）组合在同一个网络中时，也会出现部分好处，即使每个模型本身都受到限制. Partial benefits also emerged when different AI models (e.g., GPT and Claude) were combined within the same network, even though each model was limited on its own23。在 Artbreeder 上，生成器和人类混音选择共同产生了向主题吸引子的漂移. On Artbreeder, the generator and human remix choices jointly produced the drift toward thematic attractors47。在围棋中，在人工智能到来之前，棋手网络的整合已经使空缺同质化. In Go, consolidation of the player network was already homogenizing openings before AI arrived73。这种融合并不是机器所独有的：累积的文化进化往往会减少多样性，因为人们集中精力改进已经表现良好的解决方案. Convergence of this kind is not unique to machines: cumulative cultural evolution tends to reduce diversity as populations concentrate on refining solutions that are already per­ forming well36 .

模型崩溃仍然是一个结构性风险，因为模型生成的文本和图像越来越多地成为下一代的训练数据，而重复的自我训练可以将输出范围缩小到几个主要特征Model collapse remains a structural risk, because model­generated text and images increasingly become training data for the next genera­ tion, and repeated self­training can narrow outputs toward a few dom­ inant traits20。不过，收敛至少部分是一种设计选择：将法学硕士与进化搜索和显式混合策略配对可以维持开放式探索. Convergence is at least partly a design choice, though: pairing LLMs with evolutionary search and explicit blending strategies sustains open­ended exploration75，并且小型策划的训练集允许创建者将模型的特质视为表达签名，而不是继承大型通用模型的单一文化, and small curated training sets allow creators to treat a model’s idiosyncrasies as an expressive signa­ ture rather than inherit the monoculture of large general models65 .

First, the field needs population­scale designs, since nearly all evidence concerns individuals or dyads with a single tool and, as argued in Section 3, pooling isolated participants does not make a population. Studying many humans and many models together over multiple rounds is necessary before anyone can say whether local gains and global losses simply add up, though such designs are hard to scale along structural, interactional and individual axes21,23,97。其次，群体组成应被视为自变量：如果混合网络优于同质网络，则有用的问题涉及比率、耦合和分工，直接与第 2 节中提到的群体多样性结果相关. Second, group composition should be treated as the independent variable: if mixed networks outperform homogeneous ones, the useful questions concern ratio, coupling and division of labor, connecting directly to the group­diversity results noted in Section 228。专业知识也属于这里，因为专业人士和外行从相同的系统中提取不同的东西. Expertise belongs here as well, since professionals and laypeople extract different things from identical systems56。人类和模型对相同干预的反应甚至不同：跨域提示可靠地提高了人们的原创性，并使模型输出保持不变. Humans and models do not even respond alike to the same intervention: cross­domain prompting reliably raises originality in people and leaves model output unchanged98。最后，测量是具有约束力的约束：该领域仍然缺乏评估人口层面创造力的标准化方法，并且其工具不支持相对于一代人的评估. Finally, measurement is the binding constraint: the field still lacks standard­ ized ways to assess creativity at the population level, and its tools under­support evaluation relative to generation45，因此严格的人口水平多样性指标是上述研究的先决条件。, so rigorous popula­ tion­level diversity metrics are a prerequisite for the studies above.

所有这三者都面临成本：第 5 节中的资源负担意味着环境足迹应该告知研究议程本身，包括何时需要大量的生成方法。All three run up against cost: the resource burden in Section 5 means environmental footprint should inform the research agenda itself, in­ cluding when heavy generative methods are warranted at all.

总而言之，最富有成效的重构是停止询问人工智能是否有助于或损害创造力，而开始询问人类和机器的哪种混合方式，以何种方式结合，可以让个人收益积累起来，而又不会侵蚀我们所依赖的集体多样性。In sum, the most productive reframing is to stop asking whether AI helps or harms creativity and to start asking which mixtures of humans and machines, coupled in which ways, allow individual gains to accu­ mulate without eroding the collective diversity on which we depend.
