摘要Abstract

晶格量子色动力学（LQCD）为计算强子可观测量提供了第一性原理框架，但其实际应用仍然受到将研究动机转化为可靠计算工作流程所需的大量专业知识的限制。在这里我们介绍Lattice quantum chromodynamics (LQCD) provides a first-principles framework for computing hadronic observables, but its practical use remains limited by the substantial expertise required to turn research motivation into reliable computing workflows. Here we presentLQCD大师LQCDMaster，一种工具增强、技能引导和领域专业化的科学计算代理，可将自然语言 LQCD 研究任务转换为可执行的 PyQUDA 计算工作流程，包括测量脚本、作业提交工件、执行日志和数值输出。该系统结合了代理规划、专家注释的 LQCD 技能和确定性 Wick 收缩工具来约束代码生成的代数脆弱组件。我们评估, a tool-augmented, skill-guided and domain-specialized scientific computing agent that converts natural-language LQCD research tasks into executable PyQUDA computing workflows, including measurement scripts, job-submission artifacts, execution logs and numerical outputs. The system combines agentic planning, expert-annotated LQCD skills and a deterministic Wick-contraction tool to constrain the algebraically fragile components of code generation. We evaluateLQCD大师LQCDMaster科学研究前沿的基准，包括 70 个 LQCD 计算任务，可观测量涵盖局部和非局部两点函数、威尔逊环、介子和重子三点函数。生成的工作流程以机器精度准确地再现了 70 个任务中的 63 个任务中专家编写的实现，另外三个差异归因于约定不匹配。在代表性的可观察量中，该代理将实施时间从几小时缩短到几分钟，同时保留端到端的数值验证。此外，我们还提供一个典型案例on a benchmark at the forefront of scientific research, comprising 70 LQCD computing tasks, with observables covering local and nonlocal two-point functions, Wilson loops, meson and baryon three-point functions. The generated workflows exactly reproduce expert-written implementations in 63 of 70 tasks at machine precision, with three additional discrepancies attributable to convention mismatches. Across representative observables, the agent reduces implementation time from hours to minutes while preserving end-to-end numerical validation. Further, we present a typical case ofLQCD大师LQCDMaster-驱动探索：用对角威尔逊线对光锥分布振幅进行点阵计算，这是一个可以通过标准方法获得但之前从未计算过的量，以及质子、氘核、氚核、超子的光谱计算（-driven exploration: a lattice computation of light-cone distribution amplitudes with diagonal Wilson-line, a quantity accessible with standard methods but never before computed, and computation of the spectrum of proton, deuteron, triton, hyperon (Λ\Lambda), 超氘核 (), hyperdeuteron (p​Λp\Lambda）和超氚顿（) and hypertriton (p​n​Λpn\Lambda）。这项工作通过自动化点阵QCD研究中的端到端科学计算工作流程，开创了代理科学计算的范式，降低了其障碍，促进了非标准科学思想的探索和验证。). This work pioneers the paradigm of agentic scientific computing by automating the end-to-end scientific computing workflows in lattice QCD research, lowering its barrier and facilitating the exploration and verification of non-standard scientific ideas.

1 简介Introduction

1.1 进入格子 QCD 的障碍The Barrier to Entry in Lattice QCD

晶格量子色动力学 (LQCD) 直接从 QCD 拉格朗日函数提供强核力的第一性原理定义Lattice quantum chromodynamics (LQCD) provides a first-principles definition of the strong nuclear force directly from the QCD Lagrangian[1]。在过去的四十年里，它已经成熟为计算强子质量的精密工具. Over the past four decades, it has matured into a precision tool for calculating hadron masses[2, 3]，研究核子结构, studying nucleon structure[4]，并探索新的物理, and probing new physics[5, 6]。典型的 LQCD 工作流程会经历几个阶段：定义物理可观测值、设计计算方案、在 Chroma 等库中编写代码. A typical LQCD workflow proceeds through several stages: defining the physics observable, designing the computational scheme, writing code in libraries such as Chroma[7]或 PyQUDAor PyQUDA[8]、对测试配置进行调试、在 GPU 集群上扩展到生产环境并分析生成的相关器。, debugging on test configurations, scaling to production on GPU clusters, and analyzing the resulting correlators.

在这些阶段中，将物理想法转换为可运行的脚本是最具挑战性的。它需要同时具备 LQCD 物理学方面的专业知识（灯芯收缩、Among these stages, converting a physics idea into a runnable script is the most challenging. It requires simultaneous expertise in LQCD physics (Wick contractions,γ\gamma-矩阵代数、奇偶投影仪）、API 约定（量规上下文管理、晶格场布局、MPI 缩减）和数值方法（多网格求解器、量规涂抹）。人类专家通常需要几个小时到几天的时间才能观察到每个可观察的结果，有时甚至更长，需要三到五个调试周期才能在生产中信任代码。-matrix algebra, parity projectors), API conventions (gauge context management, lattice field layouts, MPI reduction), and numerical methods (multi-grid solvers, gauge smearing). Human experts typically require from hours to days per observable, and sometimes longer, with three to five debugging cycles before code can be trusted in production.

一个更微妙但同样重要的障碍是认知：实施新想法的高昂工程成本阻碍了探索。许多物理上有趣但非标准的计算，例如涉及不同的计算A subtler but equally important barrier is cognitive: the high engineering cost of implementing a new idea discourages exploration. Many physically interesting but non-standard computations, such as those involving differentγ\gamma-矩阵组合、非常规的源-汇分离以及对算子定义的系统扫描在概念上是微不足道的，但实际上却令人望而却步。因此，通常不会尝试进行此类计算，从而产生一种形式-matrix combinations, unconventional source-sink separations, and systematic scans over operator definitions, are conceptually trivial but practically prohibitive. As a result, such computations are often not attempted, producing a form of探索偏差exploration bias在 LQCD 文献中：仅计算最传统的可观测值，不是因为其他想法缺乏物理价值，而是因为它们缺乏工程合理性。in the LQCD literature: only the most conventional observables are computed, not because other ideas lack physical merit, but because they lack engineering justification.

通用代码生成系统，例如 CodexGeneral-purpose code-generation systems such as Codex[16]和克劳德·代码and Claude Code[17]可以加速软件起草，但不能保证格计算所需的代数和场论的正确性。在 LQCD 中，程序可能在语法上有效，执行时没有运行时错误，但由于不正确的自旋颜色收缩、风味分配或费米子符号而产生无效的可观察量。因此，科学程序生成的核心挑战是生成基于领域理论、受专家定义的技能约束并与用于符号操作的确定性工具相结合的可执行工作流程。can accelerate software drafting, but are not designed to guarantee the algebraic and field-theoretic correctness required by lattice computations. The central challenge for scientific program generation is therefore generating executable workflows that are grounded in domain theory, constrained by expert-defined skills, and coupled to deterministic tools for symbolic manipulation.

LQCD大师LQCDMaster是格子 QCD 的自主科学计算代理。给定指定目标可观察、集成和运动学设置的自然语言研究请求，系统会生成可审查的 PyQUDA 工作流程，包括测量脚本、作业提交工件、执行日志和数值输出。我们在涵盖代表性晶格 QCD 生产任务的基准套件上评估了这种工作流程生成功能，目的是测试生成的工作流程是否以数值精度重现专家编写的参考实现，同时保留可比较的计算性能。is an autonomous scientific computing agent for lattice QCD. 技能是阶段性的：规划者配备了相关器物理技能（可观测值、插值算子、灯芯收缩结构、动量投影、顺序源要求）；执行器接收 PyQUDA 技能（源构建、求解器参数、MPI 数据布局、收缩实现、输出格式化）；仅仪表任务由专用的处理Skills are stage-routed: the planner is equipped with correlator physics skills (observables, interpolating operators, Wick-contraction structure, momentum projection, sequential-source requirements); the executor receives PyQUDA skills (source construction, solver parameters, MPI data layout, contraction implementation, output formatting); and gauge-only tasks are handled by a dedicated锥规pyquda-gauge技能。skill.

领域技能和工具的耦合使领域路由可操作，从而使代理的知识与对所选物理工作流程有效的工具保持一致。The coupling of domain skills and tools makes domain routing operational, aligning the agent’s knowledge with the tools valid for the selected physics workflow.
