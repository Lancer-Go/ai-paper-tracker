摘要Abstract

计算机辅助合成规划使用大型反应规则库将目标分子分解为可访问的前体，这些规则库为每个转化分配一个确定性的、可解释的标签。但化学是长尾的，使得手动编码变得棘手，并且现有工具依赖于无法适应新化学的固定规则集。在这里，我们提出了一个完全自动化的管道，其中大型语言模型 (LLM) 的多代理框架对反应进行分类，并在 665,901 个美国专利反应中自行编写规则，在验证循环下生成每个规则，并针对语料库对其进行测试。它将标准分类从 68 个类扩展至 14,073 个类，无需人工管理。借助轻量级指纹分类器，它可以对 97.7% 的未见反应进行分类，与领先的专有分类器相匹配，同时更精细地解析化学，并根据需要扩展到其培训分布之外的化学。其结果是一个活的反应性数据库和将生成模型转变为可靠的、自我扩展的符号系统的一般途径。Computer-assisted synthesis planning breaks target molecules into accessible precursors using large libraries of reaction rules that assign each transformation a deterministic, interpretable label. But chemistry is long-tailed, making manual encoding intractable, and existing tools rely on fixed rulesets that cannot adapt to new chemistries. Here we present a fully automated pipeline in which a multi-agent framework of large language models (LLMs) classifies reactions and writes the rules themselves across 665,901 US patent reactions, generating each rule under a verification loop that tests it against the corpus. It expands a standard taxonomy from 68 to 14,073 classes without human curation. With a lightweight fingerprint classifier, it classifies 97.7% of unseen reactions, matching a leading proprietary classifier while resolving chemistry more finely and extending on demand to chemistry outside its training distribution. The result is a living reactivity database and a general route to turning generative models into reliable, self-expanding symbolic systems.

1 简介Introduction

化学合成规划（其中复杂的目标分子被递归地分解为更简单的构建块）仍然是药物发现和材料设计中的核心挑战。在 1969 年的开创性工作中，Corey 和 Wipke 提出了计算机辅助合成规划 (CASP)，应用编码机械规则来建议合成途径Chemical synthesis planning, in which a complex target molecule is decomposed recursively into simpler building blocks, remains a core challenge in drug discovery and materials design. In their seminal 1969 work, Corey and Wipke proposed computer-assisted synthesis planning (CASP), applying encoded mechanistic rules to suggest synthetic pathways(corey1967general; corey1969computer; corey1972computer; corey1985computer)。迄今为止，这种基于规则的方法仍然很常见。最全面的实现包含数以万计的手动设计的反应规则，具有硬编码保护和不兼容逻辑. To date, this rule-based approach remains common; the most comprehensive implementations contain tens of thousands of manually designed reaction rules with hardcoded protection and incompatibility logic(grzybowski2018chematica)。然而化学反应的分布遵循幂律. Yet the distribution of chemical reactions follows a power law(zipf2013psycho; szymkuc2016computer)，以及罕见转换的长尾，每个转换都需要与常见的酰胺耦合相同的仔细编码，使得详尽的手动覆盖实际上变得棘手。随着大型语言模型 (LLM) 在化学领域的应用日趋成熟，自动提取和概括反应规则显得尤为及时, and the long tail of rare transformations, each demanding the same careful encoding as a commonplace amide coupling, makes exhaustive manual coverage practically intractable. Automating the extraction and generalisation of reaction rules is particularly timely, as the utilisation of Large Language Models (LLMs) in chemistry matures(bran2023chemcrow; jablonka2023gpt)。这些模型可能提供一种编码合成工具箱的新方法，用从化学数据自动推断符号反应变换来取代人类逻辑。. Such models may offer a new way to encode the synthetic toolbox, replacing human logic with the automatic inference of symbolic reaction transforms from chemical data.

反应规则生成的自动化需要解决两个不同的问题。首先，必须将反应分配给结构化分类法中的命名类。The automation of reaction rule generation requires solving two distinct problems. First, a reaction must be assigned to a named class within a structured taxonomy.carey2006analysis引入了十个超类的语义层次结构来分析常见的工业制药转化，并结合随后的药物化学分析introduced a semantic hierarchy of ten superclasses to analyse common industrial pharmaceutical transformations, and together with the subsequent medicinal chemistry analysis byroughley2011medicinal，这项工作为英国皇家化学学会命名反应的 RXNO 本体提供了信息, this work informed the Royal Society of Chemistry’s RXNO ontology of named reactionsrxno。其次，每个类内的广义变换必须被编码为可计算的反应规则。此类规则通常表示为 SMIRKS，这是一种基于文本的格式，对围绕反应中心及其局部原子环境的图形转换进行编码. Second, the generalised transformation within each class must be encoded as a computable reaction rule. Such rules are typically expressed as SMIRKS, a text based format which encodes a graph transformation around the reaction centre and its local atomic environmentdaylightsmirks。然而，构建这些模式在很大程度上取决于化学直觉。即使是单个转化也需要仔细指定原子映射、立体化学约束和官能团兼容性，并且这种工作很难扩展到实践中观察到的数千种反应类型。. However, constructing these patterns depends heavily on chemical intuition; even a single transformation requires careful specification of atom mappings, stereochemical constraints, and functional group compatibility, and the effort scales poorly to the thousands of reaction types observed in practice.

过去十年中出现了数据驱动的反应分类方法，这在很大程度上是由公开可用的 USPTO 反应数据集实现的Data-driven approaches to reaction classification have emerged over the past decade, enabled in large part by the publicly available USPTO reaction dataset introduced byLowe2012; Lowe2017。该语料库还支持结果预测的工作. This corpus, which has also supported work on outcome predictionJin2017predicting; coley2017prediction; Schwaller2018found; schwaller2019molecular和多步路线规划and multistep route planningcoley2017computer; Segler2017neural; segler2018planning; schwaller2020predicting，提供训练和评估分类器所需的标记数据规模, provides the scale of labelled data needed to train and evaluate classifiers(probst2021molecular; Schwaller2021mapping);然而，这些方法仍然依赖于专有工具来确定其真实标签。这种依赖性暴露了更深层次的限制，因为现有的分类工具是否; yet these methodologies still depend on a proprietary tool to determine their ground-truth labels. This dependence exposes a deeper limitation, since existing classification tools, whether the事实上的de facto标准名称RXNstandard NameRXN(schneider2016big; Jin2017predicting; joung2025electron)或开源替代品，例如 Rxn-INSIGHTor open-source alternatives such as Rxn-INSIGHT(rxn-insight)，在两方面都失败了：它们依赖于无法适应原始设计中缺少的转换的固定本体，并且依赖于具有固有有限模板覆盖范围的手动编码规则集。这种稀疏性直接损害了生成分子设计；由于可合成性受限的生成依赖于模板库来引导模型，因此与静态规则集相关的工具限制了对历史上建立的化学空间的探索。动态可扩展的、精细的反应命名和编码系统可以解决这个瓶颈。通过按需生成新型化学规则，研究人员可以明确地调节生成模型，以对新发现的转化解锁的以前无法访问的化学空间区域进行采样和绘制地图。, fail on both fronts: they rely on fixed ontologies that cannot accommodate transformations absent from their original design, and on manually encoded rule sets with inherently limited template coverage. This sparsity directly impairs generative molecular design; because synthesisability-constrained generation relies on template libraries to steer models, tools tethered to static rule sets restrict exploration to historically established chemical space. A dynamically expandable, granular reaction naming and encoding system could address this bottleneck. By generating rules for novel chemistry on demand, researchers could explicitly condition generative models to sample and map the previously inaccessible regions of chemical space unlocked by newly discovered transformations.

第二个问题，可扩展的规则编码，仍然没有解决。无模板方法采用基于图形或语言的模型直接从目标产物预测反应物，在反应结果预测方面表现出强大的性能The second problem, scalable rule encoding, remains unsolved. Template-free methods, which employ graph- or language-based models to predict reactants directly from a target product, have shown strong performance for reaction outcome predictionJin2017predicting; Schwaller2018found; schwaller2019molecular和逆综合规划and retrosynthetic planningcoley2017computer; Segler2017neural; segler2018planning; schwaller2020predicting，完全绕过模板创建, bypassing template creation entirely(schwaller2022machine)。然而，这些方法会产生物理上无效的分子和转化. However, these approaches suffer from generating physically invalid molecules and transformations(gil2023holistic)，这是一个对于反应分类特别重要的弱点。在这里，基于规则的匹配保留了一个独特的优势：SMIRKS 模式要么匹配反应，要么不匹配，因此成功的匹配提供了明确的、确定性的分配。相比之下，神经反应分类器返回类别的概率分布，即使是高度准确的模型也会偶尔错误分配反应，这种错误会悄悄地传播到下游任务中。这使得基于规则的匹配对于分类特别有吸引力，只要规则库足够全面；然而，还没有一种自动化方法能够产生所需规模的经过验证的文库。, a weakness that is particularly consequential for reaction classification. Here, rule-based matching retains a distinctive advantage: a SMIRKS pattern either matches a reaction or it does not, so a successful match provides an unambiguous, deterministic assignment. Neural reaction classifiers, by contrast, return probability distributions over classes, and even highly accurate models will occasionally mis-assign a reaction, with such errors propagating silently into downstream tasks. This makes rule-based matching especially attractive for classification, provided the rule library can be made sufficiently comprehensive; yet no automated method has yet produced a validated library at the scale required.

进一步的挑战在于反应模板的冗余：单个转换（例如酰胺键形成）可能会被语料库中数百个不同的模板捕获，每个模板都有微妙的结构变化，但只需要少数几个就可以最低程度地描述化学反应。法学硕士提供了一种自然的机制来自动化这个规则蒸馏过程A further challenge lies in the redundancy of reaction templates: a single transformation such as amide bond formation may be captured by hundreds of distinct templates in a corpus, each with subtle structural variations, yet only a handful are needed to minimally describe the chemistry. LLMs offer a natural mechanism to automate this rule distillation process(vaswani2017attention; radford2019language; openai_gpt4; google_gemini)。虽然这些模型展示了丰富的化学合成知识，包括结构、反应可行性和战略规划. While these models demonstrate strong knowledge of chemical synthesis, including structure, reaction feasibility, and strategic planning(bran2025chemical; xuan2025synthelite; armstrong2025synthstrategy; hassen2025atom)，他们在相关的 SMILES 符号方面表现出了显着的困难, they have shown notable difficulty with the related SMILES notation(bran2025chemical)，并且有效反应 SMARTS 的生成尚未得到证实。此外，尚未证明这些知识是否可以在数十万个反应中以足够的精度和一致性应用。即使可以达到这样的精度，当前法学硕士的每次反应推理成本也使得实时分类变得不切实际，从而激发了将法学硕士提炼的知识与快速神经符号匹配相结合的混合方法。, and the generation of valid reaction SMARTS has not been demonstrated. Furthermore, it has not been proven whether this knowledge can be applied with sufficient precision and consistency across hundreds of thousands of reactions. Even where such precision is achievable, the per-reaction inference cost of current LLMs makes real-time classification impractical, motivating hybrid approaches that combine LLM-distilled knowledge with fast neuro-symbolic matching.

Starting from the RXNO ontology as a seed, we employ an LLM to iteratively classify reactions while dynamically expanding the taxonomy to capture chemistry not represented in the original hierarchy, growing from6868种子班至seed classes to14,07314{,}073经过不断的完善。对于每个类别，法学硕士概括了中间层级的反应 SMIRKS；这些通过自主细化循环针对语料库进行验证，在不牺牲召回率的情况下消除误报。由此产生的 SMIRKS 与轻量级 MLP 和模板匹配相结合，可以快速、确定地将反应分配到其分类位置through successive refinement. For each class, the LLM generalises reaction SMIRKS at intermediate hierarchical tiers; these are validated against the corpus through an autonomous refinement loop that eliminates false positives without sacrificing recall. 2 结果与讨论Results and Discussion

2.1 化学数据大语言模型分析Large language model analysis of chemical data

将法学硕士应用于大规模反应注释的核心挑战是单个模型调用无法一次可靠地对数十万个反应进行分类。即使是最先进的长上下文模型，在检索和推理输入窗口深处的信息时也会表现出系统性退化，其中准确性遵循 U 形曲线，内容在上下文的开头和结尾达到峰值，而在中间急剧下降The core challenge in applying LLMs to large-scale reaction annotation is that a single model call cannot reliably classify hundreds of thousands of reactions in one pass. Even state-of-the-art long-context models exhibit systematic degradation when retrieving and reasoning over information positioned deep within their input window where accuracy follows a U-shaped curve, peaking for content at the beginning and end of the context while dropping sharply in the middle(liu2024lost)。随后的工作表明，这种性能损失不仅是位置性的，而且与输入长度本身成比例，即使相关证据紧邻查询之前放置，这种性能损失仍然存在. Subsequent work has shown that this performance loss is not solely positional but scales with input length itself, persisting even when the relevant evidence is placed immediately before the query(du2025context)。对于包含数千种机械上不同的转换类型的数百万个反应的语料库，因此预计将整个分类法和反应集天真地嵌入到单个提示中会产生不可靠的分配。. For a corpus of millions of reactions that encompass thousands of mechanistically distinct transformation types, it is therefore expected that naïvely embedding the entire taxonomy and reaction set into a single prompt produces unreliable assignments.

为了最大限度地减少此类错误，我们将分类任务分解为五个专门的 LLM 代理，整体架构如图所示To minimise such errors, we decompose the classification task into five specialised LLM agents, with the overall architecture shown in Figure1a.该设计利用了验证链范例a. This design leverages the Chain-of-Verification paradigm(dhuliawala2024chain)，借鉴将初始生成与独立验证隔离的原则，可以显着减少事实幻觉。为了确保机械上等效的转化产生相同的标签，首先根据共享的逆向合成模板将反应分组为模板级群组，由 RDChiral 在 AiZynthTrain 中提取, drawing on the principle that isolating initial generation from independent verification significantly reduces factual hallucination. To ensure mechanistically equivalent transformations yield identical labels, reactions are first grouped into template-level cohorts based on shared retrosynthetic templates, as extracted by RDChiral in AiZynthTrain(3aizynthtrain)。然后，粗粒度层次结构代理将每个群组映射到 68 个核心子类之一，详细代理将其细化为完整分类中最精细的适用细节级别。如果该群组无​​法分配给分类中的某个条目，则会对其进行标记。然后，一个单独的验证代理会根据反应 SMILES 和模板检查这些分配。验证者要么接受详细的分类，要么拒绝它并将其传递到下一阶段。. A coarse-grained hierarchy agent then maps each cohort to one of 68 core sub-classes, which a detailed agent refines to the finest applicable level of detail in the full taxonomy. If the cohort cannot be assigned to an entry in the taxonomy, it is flagged as such. A separate verification agent then checks these assignments against the reaction SMILES and template. The verifier either accepts the detailed classification, or rejects it and passes it on to the next stage.

验证后，处理反应语料库需要动态分类。固定分类方案，例如 RXNO 本体或 NameRXN，不可避免地会遇到新颖的化学反应，从而迫使提供无信息的“未分类”标签或彻底的错误分配。我们通过允许分类法根据新出现的数据模式自适应扩展而不是仅仅依赖模型的先验知识来克服这个问题Following verification, processing a reaction corpus requires a dynamic taxonomy. Fixed classification schemes, such as the RXNO ontology or NameRXN, inevitably encounter novel chemistry that forces uninformative "unclassified" tags or outright mis-assignments. We overcome this by allowing the taxonomy to expand adaptively based on emergent data patterns rather than relying solely on the model’s prior knowledge(kargupta2025taxoadapt)。为了实现这一目标，我们开发了一个生成器聚合器架构：当验证者的反应失败时，生成器代理会提出一个新的分类条目，其中包含代码、名称和层次位置。这不能修改现有的分类法，只能对其进行添加。然后，聚合器代理会删除这些提案的重复项，解决结构冲突，并自动更新活动层次结构。这种自动循环使我们的系统能够从最初定义的 68 个子类有机扩展. To achieve this, we developed a generator-aggregator architecture: when a reaction is failed by the verifier, a generator agent proposes a new taxonomy entry complete with a code, name, and hierarchical position. 2.2 自主反应模板生成Autonomous Reaction Template Generation

专家策划的合成和反应分类工具（例如 Synthia 和 Rxn-INSIGHT）分别依赖于手动编写反应 SMIRKS，这是一个劳动密集型瓶颈。在原始数据语料库中，由于用于提取的算法规则的人为因素，单个转换类别通常表现为数十个过于具体的模板。将它们提炼成一组最小的广义 SMIRKS 可以简化计算表示，并在中间分类水平上提供稳健、广泛的覆盖范围。这项任务的一个明显选择是法学硕士，然而，虽然法学硕士拥有广泛的化学知识，但他们创作语法有效和化学精确的 SMIRKS 的能力仍未得到证实。成功地实现这一管理过程的自动化将证明法学硕士可以可靠地将高级化学意图转化为严格的、确定性的规则，从而有效地自动化主要的化学信息学工作量。Expert-curated synthesis and reaction classification tools such as Synthia and Rxn-INSIGHT, respectively, rely on the manual authoring of reaction SMIRKS, a labor-intensive bottleneck. In raw data corpora, a single transformation category often manifests as dozens of overly specific, templates due to artefacts of the algorithmic rules used for extraction. Distilling these into a minimal set of generalised SMIRKS simplifies the computational representation and provides robust, broad coverage at intermediate taxonomic levels. An obvious choice for this task is am LLM, however while LLMs possess broad chemical knowledge, their ability to author syntactically valid and chemically precise SMIRKS remains unproven. Successfully automating this curation process would demonstrate that LLMs can reliably translate high-level chemical intent into rigid, deterministic rules, effectively automating a major cheminformatics workload.

为了实现这一目标，我们开发了一个迭代的、法学硕士驱动的泛化管道。为了减轻纯粹生成方法固有的过度概括，我们实施了一个五阶段验证的学习循环。每个反应类别被分为 80/20 的训练/测试部分。法学硕士使用训练集生成候选 SMIRKS，随后针对不同反应类别的更广泛的语料库进行筛选，以识别误报。这些误报被反馈到自主细化阶段，其中法学硕士根据定性准确性和误报指标以及失败案例的定性示例迭代修改 SMIRKS 模式。To achieve this, we developed an iterative, LLM-driven generalization pipeline. To mitigate the over-generalization inherent in purely generative approaches, we implemented a five-stage validated learning loop. Each reaction class was partitioned into an 80/20 train/test split. The LLM generated candidate SMIRKS using the training set, which were subsequently screened against the broader corpus of different reaction classes to identify false positives. These false positives were fed back into an autonomous refinement phase, where the LLM iteratively modifies the SMIRKS patterns in response to qualitative accuracy and false positive metrics and qualitative examples of failure cases.

3 结论Conclusion

What previously required years of expert curation can now be performed on demand at costs affordable to academic research laboratories.
