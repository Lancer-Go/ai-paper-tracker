摘要Abstract

在政治文本中检测施瓦茨价值观很困难，因为隐含的线索 通常取决于周围的论点和之间的细粒度区别 邻近值。我们研究什么时候背景和明确的道德 知识有助于句子级价值检测。使用 ValuesML/Touché ValueEval 格式，我们比较句子、窗口和完整文档输入； 具有精心策划的道德知识库的无 RAG 和检索增强设置； 受监督的 DeBERTa-v3-base/大型编码器；以及从 12B 到 123B 的零样本法学硕士 参数。结果表明，更多的上下文并不统一 更好：完整文档上下文通过以下方式改进了受监督的 DeBERTa 编码器 3.8–4.8 宏 F1 指向仅句子输入，但不一致 帮助零机会法学硕士。检索到的道德知识在以下方面更加有用： 匹配比较，改进每个测试的模型系列和上下文条件 早期融合下。然而，缩放 从 DeBERTa-v3-base 到大型以及从 12B 到大型 LLM 并不能保证 收益，并且简单的早期融合优于经过测试的后期融合和 编码器的交叉注意力 RAG 变体。每个值的分析表明，上下文和检索 对于社会定位或概念上令人困惑的价值观最有帮助。这些发现 建议价值敏感的 NLP 应该评估上下文、知识和模型 家庭共同而不是将较长的输入或较大的模型视为通用 改进。Detecting Schwartz values in political text is difficult because implicit cues often depend on surrounding arguments and fine-grained distinctions between neighboring values. We study when context and explicit moral knowledge help sentence-level value detection. Using the ValuesML/Touché ValueEval format, we compare sentence, window, and full-document inputs; no-RAG and retrieval-augmented settings with a curated moral knowledge base; supervised DeBERTa-v3-base/large encoders; and zero-shot LLMs from 12B to 123B parameters. The results show that more context is not uniformly better: full-document context improves supervised DeBERTa encoders by 3.8–4.8 macro-F1 points over sentence-only input, but does not consistently help zero-shot LLMs. Retrieved moral knowledge is more consistently useful in matched comparisons, improving each tested model family and context condition under early fusion. However, scaling from DeBERTa-v3-base to large and from 12B to larger LLMs does not guarantee gains, and simple early fusion outperforms the tested late-fusion and cross-attention RAG variants for encoders. Per-value analyses show that context and retrieval help most for socially situated or conceptually confusable values. These findings suggest that value-sensitive NLP should evaluate context, knowledge, and model family jointly rather than treating longer inputs or larger models as universal improvements.

1 简介Introduction

政治文本不仅支持政策，而且支持政策。他们还诉诸诸如此类的价值观 例如安全、自治、传统、平等和关怀。这些呼吁是核心 如何构建和证明政治立场的合理性Political texts do not only argue for policies; they also appeal to values such as security, autonomy, tradition, equality, and care. These appeals are central to how political positions are framed and justified(Feldman, 1988; Goren, 2005; Entman, 1993; Chong and Druckman, 2007), 但它们往往是间接的。 例如，一个句子可以通过以下方式表达对社会安全的关注： 关于移民的主张，或通过关于法律的声明援引普遍主义 保护，无需明确命名任一值。施瓦茨的基本理论 人类价值观为这种区别提供了一个完善的结构, but they are often indirect. For example, a sentence may express a concern for societal security through a claim about migration, or invoke universalism through a statement about legal protection, without naming either value explicitly. Schwartz’s theory of basic human values provides a well-established structure for such distinctions(Schwartz, 1992)，精炼的 19 值分类法使得 区分的细粒度足以进行计算分析, and the refined 19-value taxonomy makes the distinctions fine-grained enough for computational analysis(Schwartz et al., 2012)。然而，相同的粒度使得句子级 分类困难：值可能是隐含的、重叠的、稀有的和 取决于周围的政治争论. The same granularity, however, makes sentence-level classification difficult: values can be implicit, overlapping, rare, and dependent on the surrounding political argument(Falk and Lapesa, 2025).

最近的 NLP 工作已将此问题具体化为多标签人类价值 检测，尤其是在争论和政治文本设置中Recent NLP work has operationalized this problem as multi-label human value detection, especially in argument and political text settings(Kiesel et al., 2022, 2023; Mirzakhmedova et al., 2024; Kiesel et al., 2024)。 这些基准使得在共享标签上比较系统成为可能 空间，但它们也暴露了一个尚未解决的方法论问题： 在决定句子是否正确时，模型应该接收哪些信息 表达一个值？当价值提示时，仅目标句子可能是不够的 取决于文档主题或先前的声明。同时，添加一个 本地窗口或完整文档可能会引入干扰因素，削弱目标 句子，并创建不同模型系列处理的更长输入 不同。. These benchmarks have made it possible to compare systems on a shared label space, but they also expose a methodological question that remains unresolved: what information should a model receive when deciding whether a sentence expresses a value? A target sentence alone may be insufficient when the value cue depends on the document topic or on previous claims. At the same time, adding a local window or a full document can introduce distractors, dilute the target sentence, and create longer inputs that different model families handle differently.

检索到的知识提供了一种减少歧义的补充方法。而不是 只需从文档中提供更多文本，系统就可以检索简洁的文本 定义、注释指导或 Schwartz 值之间的对比并使用它们 作为外在的道德知识。检索增强方法显示了一般 将参数模型与外部证据相结合的效用Retrieved knowledge offers a complementary way to reduce ambiguity. Rather than only providing more text from the document, a system can retrieve concise definitions, annotation guidance, or contrasts among Schwartz values and use them as external moral knowledge. Retrieval-augmented methods have shown the general utility of combining parametric models with external evidence(Lewis et al., 2020; Karpukhin et al., 2020), 但同样的想法是否有助于细粒度的值检测并不明显。 检索到的价值知识可以澄清概念边界，例如, but it is not obvious that the same idea will help fine-grained value detection. Retrieved value knowledge may clarify conceptual boundaries such as仁爱：关怀Benevolence: caring与versus普遍主义：关注Universalism: concern or 安全性：个人Security: personal与versus安全：社会Security: societal，但也可能 添加不相关的材料或与长文档上下文交互不良。, but it may also add irrelevant material or interact poorly with long document contexts.

指令调整大型语言模型的兴起使问题进一步复杂化 比较。零样本设置中使用的大型语言模型可以遵循标签 在监督下，在较长的上下文中给出提示和推理中的定义 可以直接针对数据集调整编码器The rise of instruction-tuned large language models further complicates the comparison. Large language models used in a zero-shot setting can follow label definitions in prompts and reason over longer contexts, while supervised encoders can be tuned directly for the dataset(Brown et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022)。因此，实用的 评估需要分离几种效果 经常被混淆的问题：收益是否来自文档上下文、检索到的 道德知识、模型家庭、模型规模或用于融合的架构 通过输入检索知识。这种区别对于 一项社会敏感任务，其中总体宏观 F1 的改进可能会隐藏 特定值之间的收益和错误不均匀. Therefore, a practical evaluation needs to separate several effects that are often conflated: whether gains come from document context, retrieved moral knowledge, model family, model scale, or the architecture used to fuse retrieved knowledge with the input. This distinction is especially important for a socially sensitive task, where an improvement in aggregate macro-F1 may hide uneven gains and errors across specific values(Hovy and Spruit, 2016; Blodgett et al., 2020).

We show how to evaluate document context and retrieved moral knowledge under matched task conditions, compare supervised and zero-shot systems without treating scale as a sufficient explanation, and connect aggregate results to per-value behavior and qualitative prediction changes. 2 相关工作Related Work

价值评估系统。ValueEval systems.

我们以将价值观视为政治组织原则的工作为基础 判断和框架，以及施瓦茨作为计算方法的精致分类法 标签空间We build on work that treats values as organizing principles in political judgment and framing, and on Schwartz’s refined taxonomy as a computational label space(Feldman, 1988; Goren, 2005; Schwartz, 1992; Schwartz et al., 2012)。 ValueEval 和 Touché 行将这些标签用于参数和 政治文本. The ValueEval and Touché lines operationalize these labels for arguments and political texts(Kiesel et al., 2022, 2023; Mirzakhmedova et al., 2024; Kiesel et al., 2024)。 共享任务系统使用了变压器编码器、标签定义、 层次结构感知公式、类标记注意力和 DeBERTa 风格 微调. Shared-task systems have used transformer encoders, label definitions, hierarchy-aware formulations, class-token attention, and DeBERTa-style fine-tuning(Devlin et al., 2019; Fang et al., 2023; Tsunokake et al., 2023; Aziz et al., 2023; Kandru et al., 2023; Hematian Hemati et al., 2023; Papadopoulos et al., 2023; Honda and Wilharm, 2023; Ghahroodi et al., 2023; Yeste et al., 2024)。 最近的句子级施瓦茨研究进一步检验了道德存在， 层次结构、集合和高阶价值结构. Recent sentence-level Schwartz studies further examine moral presence, hierarchies, ensembles, and higher-order value structure(Yeste and Rosso, 2026a, b)。而不是提出另一个 共享任务系统，我们使用此设置作为受控测试平台来隔离 目标句子上下文、检索到的价值知识、模型族和 检索融合策略。. Rather than proposing another shared-task system, we use this setting as a controlled testbed to isolate the effects of target-sentence context, retrieved value knowledge, model family, and retrieval-fusion strategy.

法学硕士和价值检测。LLMs and value detection.

人类价值检测与更广泛的道德语言分析相关，包括 政治和社交媒体文本中的道德基础分类Human value detection is related to broader moral-language analysis, including moral-foundation classification in political and social-media text(Graham et al., 2009; Fulgoni et al., 2016; Johnson and Goldwasser, 2018; Abdulhai et al., 2024)。 最近的工作还表明，道德和价值注释包含系统的人类 和模型不确定性. Recent work also shows that moral and value annotations contain systematic human and model uncertainty(Falk and Lapesa, 2025), 激励每个价值 分析而不是仅通过宏 F1 进行评估。大型语言模型使得 零样本和基于指令的分类实用, motivating per-value analysis rather than evaluation by macro-F1 alone. Large language models make zero-shot and instruction-based classification practical(Brown et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022)，最近的研究评估了法学硕士 作为人类价值观的载体或检测者, and recent studies evaluate LLMs as carriers or detectors of human values(Yao et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025; Rodrigues et al., 2024)。 我们的任务与衡量模型自身的价值不同：我们询问法学硕士是否可以 识别外部政治句子中表达的价值观，并将它们作为 针对任务监督 DeBERTa 编码器的零样本系列. Our task differs from measuring a model’s own values: we ask whether LLMs can identify values expressed in external political sentences, and compare them as a zero-shot family against task-supervised DeBERTa encoders(He et al., 2023).

上下文和检索。Context and retrieval.

当含义跨句子分布时，文档感知模型非常有用Document-aware models are useful when meaning is distributed across sentences(Yang et al., 2016; Pappas and Popescu-Belis, 2017)，但是 句子级值检测需要标记一个标记的目标句子 而不是整个文档。更广泛的背景可以恢复隐含的价值线索， 但它也会带来干扰；因此，我们比较句子、窗口、 并明确记录输入。检索增强模型结合参数 有外部证据的陈述, but sentence-level value detection requires labeling one marked target sentence rather than the whole document. Wider context can recover implicit value cues, but it can also introduce distractors; therefore, we compare sentence, window, and document inputs explicitly. Retrieval-augmented models combine parametric representations with external evidence(Guu et al., 2020; Lewis et al., 2020; Karpukhin et al., 2020), 致密 句子嵌入提供了实用的检索机制, dense sentence embeddings provide a practical retrieval mechanism(Reimers and Gurevych, 2019)，并整合检索到的融合方法 模型不同阶段的证据, and fusion methods integrate retrieved evidence at different stages of a model(Izacard and Grave, 2021; Dong et al., 2025)。相比之下 问答或生成RAG，我们的检索注入紧凑的道德 定义和标签对比到多标签分类器；持有检索 固定让我们比较三种融合机制——早期融合、晚期融合和 交叉注意力——在相同的检索设置下。. In contrast to question-answering or generation RAG, our retrieval injects compact moral definitions and label contrasts into a multi-label classifier; holding retrieval fixed lets us compare three fusion mechanisms—early fusion, late fusion, and cross-attention—under the same retrieval setup.

9 讨论Discussion

核心含义是条件：相同的附加信息可以提供帮助 或伤害取决于模型和注释问题。对于监督编码器，文档 上下文和早期融合知识库检索是互补的：文档恢复 政治框架，而检索到的价值描述将相邻的 标签。对于零样本法学硕士，检索到的知识比简单地更可靠 添加更长的文档提示。研究结果还警告不要治疗水垢 或架构复杂性作为任务设计的替代：根据本协议， DeBERTa-v3-large 并没有持续改进 DeBERTa-v3-base（更大） 指令调整的 LLM 在零样本中的性能并不优于监督编码器 模式，并且测试的后期融合和交叉注意变体并没有比 简单的早期融合。The central implication is conditionality: the same added information can help or hurt depending on the model and annotation problem. For supervised encoders, document context and early-fusion KB retrieval are complementary: the document recovers the political frame, while retrieved value descriptions separate neighboring labels. For zero-shot LLMs, retrieved knowledge is more reliable than simply adding longer document prompts. The results also caution against treating scale or architectural complexity as a substitute for task design: under this protocol, DeBERTa-v3-large does not consistently improve over DeBERTa-v3-base, larger instruction-tuned LLMs do not outperform the supervised encoder in zero-shot mode, and the tested late-fusion and cross-attention variants do not improve over simple early fusion.

实际上，这些发现支持保守的默认值检测： 从监督编码器开始，选择文档上下文的数量 仔细地，并在标签边界存在时添加简单的早期融合道德知识 暧昧的。这种设置比 70B–123B 零样本 LLM 的训练和运行成本更低， 更容易跨种子重现，并且更容易检查，因为检索到的知识库 块是可见的。法学硕士作为补充系统仍然很有用，特别是对于 对标签定义进行压力测试并生成定性对比，但它们 对于大规模句子级注释来说，这是一个不太简单的默认值。 最后，每个值的分析显示了为什么聚合宏 F1 不够充分： 价值敏感的 NLP 系统也应该通过哪些价值得到帮助来进行评估， 哪些受到伤害，哪些仍然持续困难。Practically, these findings favor a conservative default for value detection: start with a supervised encoder, choose the amount of document context carefully, and add simple early-fusion moral knowledge when label boundaries are ambiguous. This setup is cheaper to train and run than 70B–123B zero-shot LLMs, easier to reproduce across seeds, and easier to inspect because the retrieved KB chunks are visible. LLMs remain useful as complementary systems, especially for stress-testing label definitions and generating qualitative contrasts, but they are a less straightforward default for large-scale sentence-level annotation. Finally, the per-value analysis shows why aggregate macro-F1 is not sufficient: value-sensitive NLP systems should also be evaluated by which values are helped, which are harmed, and which remain persistently difficult.

10 结论Conclusion

这项研究表明，只有在以下情况下，附加信息才有助于价值检测： 模型可以将其用于相关标签决策。完整文档上下文的好处 有监督的编码器，早期融合道德知识是一个有用的补充，并且 简单的 RAG 优于经过测试的后期融合和交叉注意变体。 更大的编码器和零样本 LLM 不会自动提高性能 在此协议下。This study shows that additional information helps value detection only when the model can use it for the relevant label decision. Full-document context benefits supervised encoders, early-fusion moral knowledge is a useful addition, and simple RAG outperforms the tested late-fusion and cross-attention variants. Larger encoders and zero-shot LLMs do not automatically improve performance under this protocol.

实际的结论是保守的：从 有监督编码器，有意选择上下文长度，添加可检查的 当标签不明确时早期融合道德知识，并根据值进行评估 因为聚合宏 F1 隐藏了哪些价值观得到帮助、损害或未解决。The practical takeaway is conservative: start with a supervised encoder, choose context length deliberately, add inspectable early-fusion moral knowledge when labels are ambiguous, and evaluate per value because aggregate macro-F1 hides which values are helped, harmed, or unresolved.

Although the dataset contains texts from multiple sources, the conclusions may not transfer directly to other domains, languages, or communicative settings such as social media, parliamentary debates, or longer argumentative essays. 道德考虑Ethical Considerations

人类价值检测很敏感，因为模型输出可以解释为 关于政治参与者、团体或社区的主张。我们的预期用途是 文本框架的聚合研究分析，而不是自动判断 个人信仰、道德品质或政治合法性。研究的模型 这里在不确定性下进行句子级的预测，并进行误差分析 表明上下文和检索到的知识都可以纠正和引入 错误。因此，输出应被视为分析信号，需要 人类的解释，而不是作为明确的标签。Human value detection is sensitive because model outputs can be interpreted as claims about political actors, groups, or communities. Our intended use is aggregate research analysis of textual framing, not automated judgment of individual beliefs, moral character, or political legitimacy. The models studied here make sentence-level predictions under uncertainty, and the error analysis shows that context and retrieved knowledge can both correct and introduce mistakes. Therefore, outputs should be treated as analytical signals requiring human interpretation, not as definitive labels.

如果使用值标签来描述说话者，错误分类可能会有害， 对政治观点进行排名，或支持温和和监视决策。 这种风险与少数派或有争议的政治立场尤其相关， 其中框架可以是微妙的并且依赖于上下文。因此，我们不鼓励使用 这些系统用于个人级别的分析、自动审核或 高风险的决策。适当的用途仅限于透明、 可审计的研究环境，同时检查总体趋势 示例和错误分析。Misclassification can be harmful if value labels are used to profile speakers, rank political viewpoints, or support moderation and surveillance decisions. This risk is especially relevant for minority or contested political positions, where framing can be subtle and context-dependent. Therefore, we discourage use of these systems for individual-level profiling, automated moderation, or high-stakes decision making. Appropriate uses are limited to transparent, auditable research settings where aggregate trends are inspected alongside examples and error analyses.

检索到的知识库是面向任务的，基于已发布的价值理论和 注释指导；尽管如此，它的措辞可以塑造模型行为，所以我们 记录知识库结构并使检索输出可检查。The retrieved KB is task-facing and based on published value theory and annotation guidance; nevertheless, its wording can shape model behavior, so we document KB construction and make retrieval outputs inspectable.
