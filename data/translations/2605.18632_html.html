神经网络检查点已悄然成为大规模数据 资源：现在存在数百万个经过训练的权重向量，每个编码 特定于任务、领域和架构的知识。 这份立场文件认为Neural network checkpoints have quietly become a large-scale data resource: millions of trained weight vectors now exist, each encoding task-, domain-, and architecture-specific knowledge. This position paper argues that模型检查点应被视为 一流的数据模态，以及权重空间中的生成建模 应标准化为核心机器学习原语model checkpoints should be treated as a first-class data modality, and that generative modeling in weight space should be standardized as a core machine learning primitive。 最近的进展表明神经权重 可以按需合成，通常匹配微调性能 同时将适应成本降低几个数量级。 我们认为这些结果反映了一个潜在的结构性事实： 高性能模型占据低维、高度结构化的区域 由对称性、平坦性、模块化和共享形成的权重空间 子空间。 基于这一观点，我们将现有方法组织成五个阶段的流程、调查 该方法已经实用的应用程序，并阐明当前的 限制：适配器规模和条件生成正在迅速发展， 同时不受限制的边境规模检查点合成仍然开放。我们的目标是改变社区的默认思维模式：优化 每个任务的模型到从学习的权重分布中采样模型，加速迈向一个时代. Recent advances demonstrate that neural weights can be synthesized on demand, often matching fine-tuning performance while reducing adaptation cost by orders of magnitude. We contend that these results reflect an underlying structural fact: high-performing models occupy low-dimensional, highly structured regions of weight space shaped by symmetry, flatness, modularity, and shared subspaces. Building on this view, we organize existing methods into a five-stage pipeline, survey applications where the approach is already practical, and clarify current limits: adapter-scale and conditional generation are advancing rapidly, while unrestricted frontier-scale checkpoint synthesis remains open. Our goal is to shift the community’s default mindset from optimizing models per task to sampling models from learned weight distributions, accelerating toward an era in which人工智能系统 定期改进或创建其他人工智能系统AI systems routinely improve or create other AI systems.
