摘要Abstract

尽管视频数据丰富，但自监督新颖视图合成（NVS）仍然难以扩展，这主要是由于真实视频训练的脆弱性以及多网络系统设计的扩展行为难以预测。 我们引入了 RayDer，这是一种统一的前馈变压器，它将相机估计、场景重建和渲染整合到一个主干中，将自监督 NVS 转变为适定的单模型缩放问题。 最小的动态状态被视为干扰因素，吸收随时间变化的内容，并能够在不受约束的现实世界视频上进行稳定的训练。 重要的是，RayDer 保持Self-supervised novel view synthesis (NVS) remains challenging to scale, despite the abundance of video data, largely due to the brittleness of training on realistic videos and the hard-to-predict scaling behavior of multi-network system designs. We introduce RayDer, a unified, feed-forward transformer that consolidates camera estimation, scene reconstruction, and rendering into a single backbone, turning self-supervised NVS into a well-posed single-model scaling problem. A minimal dynamic state, treated as a nuisance factor, absorbs time-varying content and enables stable training on unconstrained real-world video. Importantly, RayDer keeps静态的static-场景 NVS 作为其目标任务：动态内容纯粹用作可扩展的监督，而不是像动态场景 (4D) NVS 那样进行重建。 在多个模型大小和数据数量级上，RayDer 展现了数据和计算的干净幂律缩放，​​并且优于静态场景数据混合。 在大量基准测试中，RayDer 实现了强大的零样本开放集性能，可与最先进的监督方法相媲美。-scene NVS as its target task: dynamic content is leveraged purely as scalable supervision, not reconstructed as in dynamic-scene (4D) NVS. Across multiple model sizes and orders of magnitude in data, RayDer exhibits clean power-law scaling with data and compute, and outperforms static-scene data mixtures. On a large number of benchmarks, RayDer achieves strong zero-shot open-set performance competitive with state-of-the-art supervised approaches.

1 简介Introduction

原则上，新颖视图合成（NVS）应该是一个高度可扩展的学习问题： 给定一组（静态）场景的姿势视图，学习预测其他视图。 然而，多视图数据极其稀缺。 自监督 NVS 通过与视图合成联合学习相机几何形状来消除这种姿势要求，最近的方法甚至可以与现实条件下的姿势监督方法相媲美Novel view synthesis (NVS) should, in principle, be a highly scalable learning problem: given a set of posed views of a (static) scene, learn to predict other views. However, posed multi-view data is extremely scarce. Self-supervised NVS removes this pose requirement by learning camera geometry jointly with view synthesis, with recent methods even rivaling pose-supervised ones under real-world conditions[jiang2025rayzer]。 在这里，视图合成本身就是. Here, view synthesis is itself the目标target任务，而不是学习可转移特征的借口任务： 相机姿势标签在真实视频中大规模获取时噪声很大且成本高昂，因此删除它们正是使丰富的未标记视频可用于训练合成模型本身的原因。 尽管如此，互联网上仍存在大量视频task, not a pretext task for learning transferable features: camera-pose labels are noisy and expensive to obtain on real video at scale, so removing them is precisely what makes abundant, unlabeled video usable for training the synthesis model itself. Despite this, and large amounts of video being available on the internet[cf. youtube2025press]，这些方法依赖于限制性假设——最值得注意的是，来自策划数据集的静态场景——阻碍了大规模的可靠训练。 我们认为，可扩展的自监督 NVS 的主要障碍不是数据可用性，而是当前系统如何设计来使用该数据。, these methods rely on restrictive assumptions – most notably, static scenes from curated datasets – that prevent reliable training at scale. We argue that the main obstacle to scalable self-supervised NVS is not data availability, but rather how current systems are designed to use that data.

5 结论Conclusion

自监督新颖视图合成（NVS）长期以来一直承诺通过视频实现可扩展性，不需要地面实况相机姿势注释，但现有方法仍然受到难以扩展的多网络管道和限制性静态场景假设的限制。 在这项工作中，我们引入了 RayDer，这是一种统一的前馈变压器，它将相机估计、场景重建和渲染整合到一个可扩展的主干中，从而能够对不受约束的现实世界视频进行稳定的训练，同时保留静态场景 NVS 目标。 通过通过干扰变量、架构统一和自回归姿势学习进行显式动态处理，RayDer 使自监督 NVS 在数据、模型大小和计算方面的扩展变得干净。 根据经验，这会产生干净的幂律缩放行为、与受监督和基于视频扩散的系统竞争的强大的零镜头开放集性能，以及完全在没有姿势监督的情况下学习的可转移相机姿势表示。Self-supervised novel view synthesis (NVS) has long promised scalability through videos by not requiring ground-truth camera pose annotations, yet existing approaches remained constrained by hard-to-scale multi-network pipelines and restrictive static-scene assumptions. In this work, we introduced RayDer, a unified feed-forward transformer that consolidates camera estimation, scene reconstruction, and rendering into a single scalable backbone, enabling stable training on unconstrained real-world video while preserving the static-scene NVS objective. Through explicit dynamic state handling via a nuisance variable, architectural unification, and autoregressive pose learning, RayDer makes scaling of self-supervised NVS clean across data, model size, and compute. Empirically, this yields clean power-law scaling behavior, strong zero-shot open-set performance competitive with supervised and video diffusion-based systems, and transferable camera pose representations learned entirely without pose supervision.

除了具体的架构之外，我们的结果提出了更广阔的视角：许多先前的自监督 NVS 方法的一个主要限制不是缺乏监督，而是无法Beyond the specific architecture, our results suggest a broader perspective: one major limitation of many prior self-supervised NVS methods was not the absence of supervision, but the inability to使用可扩展的数据机制use scalable data regimes。 通过在通用视频上实现从头开始的稳定学习并展示干净的缩放，RayDer 将自我监督的 NVS 置于相同的缩放驱动范式中，该范式塑造了语言和一些视觉基础模型的进步。. By enabling stable learning from scratch on generic video and demonstrating clean scaling, RayDer positions self-supervised NVS within the same scaling-driven paradigm that has shaped progress in language and some vision foundation models.

展望未来，RayDer 为未来的工作开辟了多个方向，包括与部分监督和生成建模的集成、向 4D NVS 的扩展以及继续向 3D 世界基础模型扩展。Looking forward, RayDer opens up several directions for future work, including integration with partial supervision and generative modeling, extension toward 4D NVS, and continued scaling toward 3D world foundation models.
