摘要Abstract

与标准自回归模型相比，扩散大语言模型 (dLLM) 在并行生成方面具有理论上的优势。然而，单独的并行生成并不能保证实际的加速。实现这种效率需要专门的推理机制，例如扩散感知缓存和重用。因此，随着推理效率成为实际部署的先决条件，最近的研究积极探索跨算法、架构和系统的加速技术。然而，严格的比较仍然很困难，因为端到端延迟源于算法、架构和系统级因素之间复杂的权衡，而这些因素通常在现有基准测试中混为一谈。在本次调查中，我们为 dLLM 引入了统一的延迟分解框架，以理清这些因素并分析它们对实际部署中推理速度的影响。在此框架的指导下，我们沿着三个轴对加速技术进行分类，涵盖算法创新、架构和系统优化以及推理时间缩放。最后，我们提供了可重复基准测试的指南，并强调了实现并行生成的全部潜力所面临的挑战。Diffusion large language models (dLLMs) offer a theoretical advantage in parallel generation over standard autoregressive models. However, parallel generation alone does not guarantee practical speedups. Realizing this efficiency requires specialized inference mechanisms, such as diffusion-aware caching and reuse. Consequently, as inference efficiency becomes a prerequisite for practical deployment, recent research has actively explored acceleration techniques across algorithms, architectures, and systems. However, rigorous comparisons remain difficult, as end-to-end latency stems from intricate trade-offs between algorithmic, architectural, and system-level factors that are often conflated in existing benchmarks. In this survey, we introduce a unified latency decomposition framework for dLLMs to disentangle these factors and analyze their impact on inference speed in real deployments. Guided by this framework, we categorize acceleration techniques along three axes covering algorithmic innovations, architectural and system optimizations, and inference-time scaling. Finally, we provide guidelines for reproducible benchmarking and highlight open challenges for realizing the full potential of parallel generation.
