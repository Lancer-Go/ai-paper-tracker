越狱攻击下的鲁棒有害特征：来自大语言模型中注意力头专业化的机制证据Robust Harmful Features Under Jailbreak Attacks: Mechanistic Evidence from Attention Head Specialization in Large Language Models

Yanchen Yin    Dongqi Han    Linghui Li
摘要Abstract

越狱攻击绕过了 LLM 安全调整，但其机制仍知之甚少。我们提供的证据表明，攻击并没有全面消除安全功能，而是选择性地抑制特定的注意力头。我们确定了两种功能上不同的类型：Jailbreak attacks bypass LLM safety alignment, yet their mechanisms remain poorly understood. We provide evidence that attacks do not comprehensively eliminate safety features, but instead selectively suppress specific attention heads. We identify two functionally differentiated types:敌对妥协头 (ACH)Adversarially Compromised Heads (ACHs)集中在早期层，受到攻击时受到抑制，并且concentrated in early layers, which are suppressed under attacks, and安全对准头 (SAH)Safety-Aligned Heads (SAHs)在中间层，即使攻击成功，也能保持强大的激活。消融研究支持 ACH 的因果作用以及 SAH 对鲁棒激活的贡献：抑制少量 ACH 足以在通常拒绝的输入上引发类似越狱的行为，而删除 SAH 会大大削弱中间层安全激活。令牌级归因进一步表明 ACH 抑制是由攻击模板令牌专门驱动的，提供了一个机制解释为什么攻击可以通过 ACH 抑制绕过拒绝决策，同时留下由 SAH 维持的内部安全信号（我们称之为现象）in mid-layers, which maintain robust activations even when attacks succeed. Ablation studies support the causal role of ACHs and the contribution of SAHs to robust activations: suppressing a small number of ACHs is sufficient to induce jailbreak-like behavior on normally refused inputs, while removing SAHs substantially weakens mid-layer safety activations. Token-level attribution further shows that ACH suppression is driven specifically by attack-template tokens, providing a mechanistic account of why attacks can bypass refusal decisions through ACH suppression while leaving internal safety signals sustained by SAHs—a phenomenon we term强大的有害特征Robust Harmful Features。为了验证这种鲁棒性的实际意义，我们证明，只需读取这些持久激活（无需任何训练）即可产生具有强大对抗鲁棒性的有竞争力的聚合检测性能。. To validate the practical significance of this robustness, we show that simply reading these persistent activations—without any training—yields competitive aggregate detection performance with strong adversarial robustness.

The method requires white-box access to internal activations, but it uses only a single forward pass and no gradient computation or model intervention. The spatial segregation—ACHs concentrated in early layers and SAHs distributed across mid-layers—appears in the 70B model as well, providing preliminary evidence that this functional differentiation is not scale-specific. The core mechanism whereby attacks bypass refusal by suppressing ACHs while SAHs maintain robust activations is consistent across the studied scales, and token-level attribution similarly reveals selective suppression of ACHs by template tokens. Notably, the 70B model contains more SAHs (83) than ACHs (62), whereas the 8B model shows comparable counts (17 vs. 21); this may reflect richer robust safety representations in larger models, though the limited sample size warrants cautious interpretation.

局限性。Limitations.

该分析的主要限制是样本量。由于只有 40 个成功的攻击样本用于分类，50 个样本用于消融评估，统计估计具有较高的方差。我们将这些结果作为初步证据而不是明确的结论。未来的工作应该要么开发更强大的攻击，能够以更高的速率绕过 70B 规模的模型，要么采用不需要成功攻击样本的实验设计。The primary limitation of this analysis is sample size. With only 40 successful attack samples for classification and 50 samples for ablation evaluation, statistical estimates have higher variance. We present these results as preliminary evidence rather than definitive conclusions. Future work should either develop stronger attacks capable of bypassing 70B-scale models at higher rates, or adopt experimental designs that do not require successful attack samples.
