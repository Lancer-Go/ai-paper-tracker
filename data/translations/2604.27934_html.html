1 简介Introduction

立场检测是识别文本中表达的针对特定目标的态度或意见的任务，是理解从社交媒体到新闻文章等各种平台上的公共话语的关键任务Stance detection, the task of identifying the attitude or opinion expressed in text towards a specific target, is a crucial task for understanding public discourse on various platforms, from social media to news articlesMohammad et al. (2016); Augenstein et al. (2016)。早期研究主要集中于分析单峰文本内容。然而，随着在线多媒体内容的盛行，用户经常通过文字和图像的结合来表达观点和立场. Early research primarily focused on analyzing unimodal textual content. However, with the prevalence of multimedia content online, users frequently express opinions and stances through a combination of text and images(Liang et al., 2024a)。这就需要开发多模态姿态检测（MSD）方法，该方法可以有效地解释和融合来自不同模态的信息。. This necessitates the development of Multimodal Stance Detection (MSD) methods that can effectively interpret and fuse information from different modalities.

由于深度学习在不同应用领域取得的显著成就Owing to the remarkable achievements of deep learning in diverse application domains(Zeng et al., 2024; Lu and Yin, 2025; Cui et al., 2025)越来越多的研究将深度学习模型集成到 MSD 任务中。, an increasing number of studies have integrated deep learning models into MSD task.

最初的方法通常依赖于融合从独立文本和图像编码器中提取的特征，例如将 BERT 嵌入与 CNN 特征连接起来。例如，Initial approaches often relied on fusing features extracted from independent text and image encoders, like concatenating BERT embeddings with CNN features. For instance,Barel et al. (2024)表明通过结构性社会背景增强内容嵌入可以带来巨大的性能提升。最近，视觉语言模型（VLM）show that augmenting content embeddings with structural social context yields large performance gains. More recently, Vision-Language Models (VLMs)Zhang et al. (2024a); Li et al. (2025a)和多模态大语言模型 (MLLM)and Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)Wu et al. (2023); Caffagni et al. (2024); Wei et al. (2025a)在理解许多任务中的跨模式关系方面表现出了令人印象深刻的能力have demonstrated impressive capabilities in understanding cross-modal relationships at many tasksLu and Li (2026); Lu et al. (2025b)。目标多模式提示调整 (TMPT) 等框架. Frameworks like Targeted Multi-modal Prompt Tuning (TMPT)Liang et al. (2024a)已经证明了使用特定目标提示来调整预训练模型来捕获多模式姿态特征的有效性。研究人员还开始探索直接使用 MLLM，利用其新兴推理能力来完成多模式任务have shown the effectiveness of adapting pre-trained models using target-specific prompts to capture multimodal stance features. Researchers have also begun exploring the use of MLLMs directly, leveraging their emergent reasoning abilities for multimodal tasksVasilakes et al. (2025).

尽管取得了这些进步，但有效执行强大的多模态姿态检测，尤其是在涉及多模态信号冲突的复杂或微妙的场景中，仍然具有挑战性。具体来说，现有方法通常面临以下关键挑战：Despite these advancements, effectively performing robust multimodal stance detection, especially in complex or nuanced scenarios involving conflicting multimodal signals, remains challenging. Specifically, existing methods often face the following key challenges:

  • •
