迈向格莱斯静修：Toward a Gricean Retreat:
探索法学硕士的知识边界和所指特异性Probing LLMs for Knowledge Boundaries and Referent Specificity

Dananjay Srinivas    Saksham Khatwani    Maria Pacheco Affiliation: University of Colorado, Boulder Email: dasr8731@colorado.edu
摘要Abstract

当被问及超出其知识范围的实体时，法学硕士通常会捏造听起来合理的细节，而不是退缩到更安全、更笼统的主张。我们通过格里斯的视角来描述这一失败：一个对所指对象不确定的合作演讲者会退回到具体层次，以信息性换取真实性。我们询问法学硕士是否具备进行这次静修的要素。使用基于 T-REx 的基准来改变实体熟悉度和所指对象特异性，我们探索模型来回答两个问题：（i）它们的激活是否编码所指对象是否落入知识边界内，以及（ii）它们是否预测它们将要生成的所指对象的特异性？我们发现两者的答案都是肯定的，但是这两个信号在生成时并不一致。模型绝大多数更喜欢特定的参照物，即使实体对他们来说是未知的，即使提供了正确的通用替代品，模型也会这样做。格莱斯撤退的基础已经存在，但据此采取行动的政策却还没有。我们将我们的发现定位为实现 Gricean 对齐、培训或指导目标的第一步，这些目标将知识边界意识与生成过程中的指涉特异性结合起来。When asked about entities outside their knowledge boundary, LLMs routinely fabricate plausible-sounding details rather than backing off to safer, more general claims. We frame this failure through a Gricean lens: a cooperative speaker who is uncertain about a referent retreats up the specificity hierarchy, trading informativeness for truthfulness. We ask whether LLMs have the ingredients to perform this retreat. Using a T-REx-based benchmark that varies entity familiarity and referent specificity, we probe models to answer two questions: (i) do their activations encode whether a referent falls inside the knowledge boundary, and (ii) do they anticipate the specificity of the referent they are about to generate? We find that the answer to both is yes, but the two signals are not reconciled in generation. Models overwhelmingly prefer specific referents even when the entity is unknown to them, and do so even when offered correct generic alternatives. The substrate for a Gricean retreat is present, but the policy that would act on it is not. We position our findings as a first step toward Gricean alignment, training or steering objectives that couple knowledge-boundary awareness to referent-specificity during generation.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 已被证明能够从参数空间中获取有关现实世界实体的大量知识Large language models (LLMs) have been shown to be capable of eliciting a remarkable amount of knowledge about real-world entities from their parameter space22; 29。然而，这取决于实体在预训练数据中的良好代表性. However, this is contingent on the entity being well-represented in pretraining data6。 由于现实世界实体的长尾和不断变化的性质，法学硕士很难跟上所有实体的步伐. Due to the long-tailed and ever-changing nature of real-world entities, it is intractable for LLMs to remain abreast of them all12。这在研究中被描述为. This has been characterized in research as the知识边界问题knowledge boundary problem，这已被证明会导致, which has been shown to lead to事实幻觉factual hallucinations，即捏造有关法学硕士知识范围之外的实体的听起来合理的细节, i.e., the fabrication of plausible-sounding details about entities outside an LLM’s knowledge boundary14; 12.

人类不断面临同样的问题，并且可以通过提供尽可能多的信息同时忠实于他们实际知道的信息来解决它。Humans face this same problem constantly, and can resolve it by offering as much information as possible while staying faithful to what they actually know.10通过将其形式化formalizes this through the合作原则Cooperative Principle，对话者按要求参与对话的隐含规范。这一原则是通过几条对话格言来实现的；最直接控制这种权衡的两个因素是, the implicit norm that interlocutors contribute to a conversation as is required. This principle is realized through several conversational maxims; the two that govern this trade-off most directly are数量Quantity（根据需要提供尽可能多的信息），以及(be as informative as required), and品质Quality（只断言你所知道的）。考虑一个说话者遇到艾伦·佩珀这个名字，但不知道他的具体情况。无论名称出现在什么上下文中，他们都可以依靠常识性类别知识(only assert what you know). Consider a speaker who encounters the name Allan Peiper without knowing specifics about him. Drawing on whatever context the name appears in, they can fall back on commonsense category knowledge4; 5并称他为澳大利亚人、骑自行车的人或简称为一个人。我们将这一举动从特定的指称转向更普遍的指称，称为and refer to him as an Australian, a cyclist, or simply a person. We refer to this move, from a specific referent to a more general one, as a格莱斯撤退Gricean retreat.

这给法学硕士提出了一个自然的问题：当模型不确定所指对象时，它是否可以像合作演讲者那样进行格莱斯撤退，调节其生成内容的特殊性，以信息性换取真实性而不是捏造？现有的幻觉控制方法主要是在This opens up a natural question for LLMs: when a model is uncertain about a referent, can it perform a Gricean retreat as a cooperative speaker would, moderating the specificity of what it generates to trade informativeness for truthfulness rather than fabricating? These methods are expensive and all-or-nothing, correcting completed generations rather than calibrating them upfront.

在本文中，我们询问这种预先校准的成分是否已经存在于模型内部。我们从 T-REx 分区中整理了一个基准数据集In this paper, we ask whether the ingredients of such upfront calibration are already present inside the model. We curate a benchmark dataset from the T-REx partition7喇嘛of LAMA22，涵盖 4 个领域（人员、公司、产品和技能）的 8 个维基数据关系。对于每种关系，我们为主题生成三个级别的上下文基础、主题的综合替换以模拟模型知识边界之外的实体，以及不同特异性级别的对象的通用替换。然后，我们探究模型激活以提出两个问题：（i）模型内部是否表示所指对象是否在其知识边界内？ (ii) 该模型在内部是否代表了其即将到来的特异性选择，这是 Gricean 撤退所需要的能力？, covering 8 Wikidata relations across 4 domains (people, corporations, products, and skills). For each relation, we generate three levels of contextual grounding for the subject, synthetic substitutions of the subject to simulate entities outside the model’s knowledge boundary, and generic substitutions of the object at varying levels of specificity. We then probe model activations to ask two questions: (i) Does the model internally represent whether a referent is within its knowledge boundary? (ii) Does the model internally represent its upcoming specificity choice, a capability that a Gricean retreat would require?

我们发现这两个问题的答案都是肯定的，但是这两个信号在模型的实际生成行为中并不协调。探针可以可靠地区分已知实体和未知实体，并且可以预测模型将要生成的指示对象的特异性。然而，在生成时，模型强烈偏爱特定的指涉对象，无论实体是否落入其知识边界内（通过困惑度和外在惊喜引发测试来衡量）。换句话说，格莱斯撤退所需的信息已经存在，但据此采取行动的政策却不存在。We find that the answer to both questions is yes, but the two signals are not reconciled in the model’s actual generation behavior. Probes can reliably distinguish known from unknown entities, and can predict the specificity of the referent the model is about to generate. Yet when generating, models strongly prefer specific referents regardless of whether the entity falls inside their knowledge boundary, as measured by both perplexity and an extrinsic surprisal elicitation test. In other words, the information needed for a Gricean retreat is present, but the policy that would act on it is not.

语义层次和格莱斯准则Semantic Hierarchy and Gricean Maxims

法学硕士已被证明可以从其表示形式的语义层次结构中捕获概念LLMs have been shown to capture concepts from semantic hierarchy in its representations27; 24格莱斯格言强调对话者在相互交谈时做出的隐含假设——其中两个关键方面是“信息性”和“真实性”。Gricean Maxims underline the implicit assumptions that interlocutors make when conversing with each other – with two key aspects being “informativeness” and “truthfulness”.9我们将“信息性”的质量解释为法学硕士对低名生成的偏好，而上名生成几乎总是会导致“真实”的反应。 除了探索分层概念之外，我们还将我们的发现与模型控制一种质量而不是另一种质量的能力联系起来，与已知和未知的实体进行对比。We construe the quality of “informativeness” as an LLM’s preference for hyponymic generation, whereas hypernymic generations will almost always lead to a “truthful” response. In addition to probing for hierarchical concepts, we tie our findings to a model’s ability to control for one quality over another, when contrasted with known and unknown entities.

6 结论Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们概述了一种将模型行为与 Gricean 范式保持一致的新颖策略，其中模型必须平衡其信息性和真实性。 为了做到这一点，我们设计了一个数据管理过程，通过研究模型的训练数据来获取模型已知和未知的实体。 接下来，我们通过线性探测表明，LLM 的隐藏激活可以强有力地预测模型是否知道它是真实的（在训练数据中看到）还是合成的（模型看不到）。 然后，我们表明模型知道它是要转向特定一代还是通用一代。 鉴于模型知道实体何时落入其知识边界内，并且知道它具有衡量生成特异性的强烈信号，我们测试这是否有助于模型在我们的 Gricean 标准内表现良好。 我们观察到，在真实和合成场景中，该模型绝大多数更喜欢特定的世代。 我们表明，这不仅仅是由于解码过程开始时的小错误造成的，而且即使要求模型在不正确的特定选择或正确的通用选择之间进行选择，这个问题仍然存在。 这为法学硕士的格里斯协调思想的未来工作打开了大门，利用其隐藏激活中存在的现有装置来调整他们的行为，使其更加忠实于他们的知识。In this work, we have outlined a novel strategy for aligning model behaviors to a Gricean paradigm, where the model has to balance its informativeness with its truthfulness. In order to do this, we design a data curation process to obtain entities known and unknown to a model by studying its training data. Next, we show through linear probing that LLMs’ hidden activations can strongly predict whether the model knows if it is real (seen in the training data) or synthetic (unseen to the model). We then show that the model knows whether it is going to move towards a specific or a generic generation before the generation. Given that the model knows when entities fall within its knowledge boundary, and knowing that it has a strong signal to measure specificity of generation, we test whether this helps the model perform well within our Gricean standard. We observe that the model overwhelmingly prefers specific generations, in real and synthetic scenarios. We show that this is not just due to a small error at the start of the decoding process, but a problem that persists even when the model is asked to choose between an incorrect specific or a correct generic choice. This opens the door for future work in the idea of Gricean alignment of LLMs, to utilize the existing apparatus present in their hidden activations to align them to behave more faithfully to their knowledge.

局限性Limitations

我们的研究受到一些资源限制的限制。 首先，虽然我们检查以确保训练数据中很少存在合成实体，但污染的影响可能会扭曲我们的结果。 其次，使用基于 LLM 的数据生成方法可能会进一步污染我们的数据，因为我们只能验证一小部分样本的关系子集。 除了数据的限制外，我们在LLM作为法官的场景中可以测试的样本和模型的数量也受到限制，这意味着可能需要付出更多的努力来进行更深入的分析。 最后，我们只考虑关系的子集和模型的小子集，这可能会在我们的分析中留下空白。 我们将解决这些限制以及有关在未来工作中实施 Gricean 一致性的问题。Our study is limited by a few resource constraints. First, while we checked to ensure that synthetic entities were rarely present in the training data, the effect of the contamination may have skewed our results. Second, using an LLM based approach for data generation could further contaminate our data as we are only able to verify for subset of relationships for small set of samples. Besides limitations due to data, we were also constrained in the number of samples and models we could test in the LLM-as-a-judge scenario, which means more efforts may be needed to for a more in-depth analysis. Lastly, we only consider a subset of relationships and small subset of models, potentially leaving gaps in our analysis. We leave addressing these limitations along with the questions regarding implementing Gricean alignment to future work.
