摘要Abstract

人工智能（AI）越来越多地被引入系统工程活动中，特别是在需求工程（RE）中，其中质量评估和验证仍然严重依赖于专家判断。虽然最近的人工智能工具在分析和生成需求方面表现出了有前途的能力，但它们在正式系统工程流程中的作用以及它们与既定 INCOSE 标准的一致性仍然没有得到充分理解。本文研究了基于人工智能的工具在不取代专业知识的情况下可以在多大程度上支持系统工程师评估需求质量。 该研究采用结构化系统工程方法来比较人工智能辅助需求评估与人类专家评估。进行了一项对照研究，由经验丰富的系统工程师和基于人工智能的评估工具根据既定的 INCOSE“良好要求”标准评估系统要求。评估侧重于一致性、完整性、清晰度和可测试性，不仅检查准确性，还检查每个评估背后的决策逻辑。 结果表明，人工智能工具可以提供一致且快速的初步评估，特别是对于句法和结构质量属性。然而，专家判断对于上下文解释、歧义解决和权衡推理仍然至关重要。研究结果并没有将人工智能定位为系统工程师的替代者，而是支持其在可再生能源生命周期中作为决策支持机制的作用。从系统工程的角度来看，这项研究提供了关于如何将人工智能集成到 RE 工作流程中，同时保持可追溯性、问责制和工程一致性的经验证据。本文进一步讨论了 SE 实践的影响，包括减少工作量、质量保证流程和自动化的边界。研究结果为寻求负责任地将人工智能集成到系统工程过程中的从业者和研究人员提供了信息。Artificial Intelligence (AI) is increasingly introduced into systems engineering activities, particularly within requirements engineering (RE), where quality assessment and validation remain heavily dependent on expert judgment. While recent AI tools demonstrate promising capabilities in analyzing and generating requirements, their role within formal systems engineering processes—and their alignment with established INCOSE criteria—remains insufficiently understood. This paper investigates the extent to which AI-based tools can support systems engineers in evaluating requirement quality, without replacing professional expertise. The research adopts a structured systems engineering methodology to compare AI-assisted requirement evaluation with human expert assessment. A controlled study was conducted in which system requirements were evaluated against established INCOSE “good requirement” criteria by both experienced systems engineers and an AI-based assessment tool. The evaluation focused on consistency, completeness, clarity, and testability, examining not only accuracy but also the decision logic underlying each assessment. Results indicate that AI tools can provide consistent and rapid preliminary assessments, particularly for syntactic and structural quality attributes. However, expert judgment remains essential for contextual interpretation, ambiguity resolution, and trade-off reasoning. Rather than positioning AI as a replacement for systems engineers, the findings support its role as a decision-support mechanism within the RE lifecycle. From a systems engineering perspective, this study contributes empirical evidence on how AI can be integrated into RE workflows while preserving traceability, accountability, and engineering consistency. The paper further discusses implications for SE practice, including workload reduction, quality assurance processes, and the boundaries of automation. The results inform both practitioners and researchers seeking to responsibly integrate AI into systems engineering processes.

系统工程中的定义和作用Definition and Role in Systems Engineering

在系统工程中，需求通常是对系统必须满足的功能、约束或属性的声明，以实现利益相关者的目标（In systems engineering a requirement is typically a statement of needed functionality, constraint, or property that a system must satisfy to meet stakeholder objectives ([19]）。需求工程涉及引出这些需求并将其转化为精确的系统规范（). Requirements engineering involves eliciting these needs and translating them into precise system specifications ([19]）。精心编写的需求可确保开发团队和利益相关者对系统目标有共同的理解。). Well-written requirements ensure that development teams and stakeholders share a common understanding of the system’s goals.

相反，需求不足常常被认为是项目问题的主要原因——“低质量需求是最先出现的潜在错误之一……下游会以成本增加和进度超支的形式感受到”（Conversely, inadequate requirements are often cited as a primary cause of project issues – “low quality requirements are among the first potential mistakes… felt downstream in the form of increased costs and schedule overruns” ([11]）。清晰准确的需求使验证系统变得更加容易，并避免代价高昂的后期变更（). Clear and precise requirements make it easier to verify the system and avoid costly late-stage changes ([9]).

需求分类Classification of Requirements

需求可以按照几个维度进行分类。常见的分类是功能性需求与非功能性需求。功能需求指定系统必须执行的服务、行为或功能——本质上是系统应该做什么。相反，非功能性需求 (NFR) 定义系统应如何执行或它必须具备的质量（Requirements can be classified along several dimensions. A common classification is functional vs. non-functional requirements. Functional requirements specify the services, behaviors, or functions a system must execute – essentially, what the system should do. Non-functional requirements (NFRs), in contrast, define how the system should perform or the qualities it must have ([19]）。其中包括性能指标、安全级别、可用性、可靠性和其他质量属性或约束。例如，在线服务的功能需求可能是能够注册新用户，而非功能需求可能规定注册响应在 2 秒内发生（性能限制）。两种类型都很重要：功能需求确保系统的功能满足用户需求，NFR 确保系统在现实条件下可用且值得信赖（). These include performance metrics, security levels, usability, reliability, and other quality attributes or constraints. For example, an online service’s functional requirement might be the ability to register a new user, while a non-functional requirement could stipulate that the registration response occurs within 2 seconds (a performance constraint). Both types are vital: functional requirements ensure the system’s features meet user needs, and NFRs ensure the system is usable and trustworthy under real-world conditions ([19]).

人工智能的历史演变与突破Historical Evolution and Breakthroughs in AI人工智能经历了三个主要浪潮，每一次都重塑了可再生能源活动的自动化和支持方式。Artificial intelligence has progressed through three main waves, each reshaping how RE activities are automated and supported.

  1. 1.

    符号人工智能（20 世纪 50 年代中期 - 1990 年代初）。 第一波浪潮侧重于符号推理，其中专家系统通过 IF-THEN 规则和形式逻辑捕获领域知识。在 20 世纪 80 年代，此类基于规则的引擎已经在检查需求文档的内部一致性（Symbolic AI (mid-1950s – early 1990s). The first wave focused on symbolic reasoning, in which expert systems captured domain knowledge with IF–THEN rules and formal logic. During the 1980s, such rule-based engines were already inspecting requirements documents for internal consistency ([6]）。虽然这些系统对狭窄的领域有效，但它们与模糊的自然语言作斗争，并且随着项目范围的扩大很快变得脆弱。). Although effective for narrow domains, these systems struggled with ambiguous natural language and quickly became brittle as project scope grew.

  2. 2.

    数据驱动的机器学习（1990 年代 - 2000 年代末）。 计算能力的提高使得统计自然语言处理和经典机器学习 (ML) 算法（例如支持向量机和决策树）成为可能。研究人员开始对大量需求进行优先级排序、分类和聚类，减少人工工作量，同时突出潜在的缺陷（Data-Driven Machine Learning (1990s – late 2000s). Rising computational power enabled statistical natural-language processing and classical machine-learning (ML) algorithms for example, support-vector machines and decision trees. Researchers began to prioritize, classify and cluster large sets of requirements, reducing manual workload while highlighting potential defects ([6]）。与此同时，出现了模糊或主观术语的自动检测器，标记出诸如“快速”或“用户友好”之类的单词，这些单词破坏了软件规范的准确性（). At the same time, automated detectors of vague or subjective terms emerged, flagging words such as fast or user-friendly that undermine precision in software specifications ([11]).

  3. 3.

    深度学习和大型语言模型（2010 年代至今）。 第三次浪潮是由多层神经网络，尤其是 Transformer 架构引发的（Deep Learning and Large Language Models (2010s – present). The third wave was triggered by multilayer neural networks and, above all, the Transformer architecture ([23]）。在庞大的语料库上训练的深度模型在计算机视觉、语音和自然语言处理方面取得了最先进的结果。迁移学习使研究人员能够在适度的 RE 数据集上微调预训练的语言模型（例如 BERT），从而在功能/非功能分类等任务中产生巨大的收益（). Deep models trained on vast corpora achieved state-of-the-art results across computer vision, speech and NLP. Transfer learning allowed researchers to fine-tune pre-trained language models (e.g., BERT) on modest RE datasets, yielding substantial gains in tasks such as functional/non-functional classification ([10]）。马丁内斯·费尔南德斯（). Martínez-Fernández([14]）将这一时期描述为“深度学习的兴起浪潮”，其特点是越来越多的上下文感知技术，可以解析整个规范文档，映射可追溯性链接，甚至生成草案需求。) describes this period as the “rising wave of deep learning,” characterized by increasingly context-aware techniques that can parse entire specification documents, map traceability links and even generate draft requirements.

这三个浪潮共同描绘了从手动编码规则到以数据为中心、上下文敏感分析的转变，将 RE 从劳动密集型簿记转变为半自动化、洞察驱动的学科。Together, these three waves chart a shift from manually encoded rules to data-centric, context-sensitive analysis transforming RE from labour-intensive bookkeeping into a semi-automated, insight-driven discipline.

需求工程中的人工智能Artificial Intelligence in Requirements Engineering

人工智能已越来越多地应用于支持 RE，主要通过自然语言处理 (NLP) 和机器学习技术，旨在减少手动工作并提高一致性。早期的工作侧重于狭隘的、特定于任务的自动化，例如将需求分类为功能性或非功能性、检测模糊或主观语言，或者按主题对需求进行聚类。系统评价表明，如果有足够的训练数据，机器学习模型可以在明确定义的分类任务上实现高精度，特别是在软件需求数据集中（例如，AI has been increasingly applied to support RE, primarily through natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning techniques aimed at reducing manual effort and improving consistency. Early work focused on narrow, task-specific automation such as classifying requirements as functional or non-functional, detecting vague or subjective language, or clustering requirements by topic. Systematic reviews show that, given sufficient training data, machine-learning models can achieve high accuracy on well-defined classification tasks, particularly in software requirements datasets (e.g.,[7], [10]).

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展扩展了这些功能。基于 Transformer 的模型可以处理更长的上下文并捕获表面关键字之外的语义意图，从而实现更细致的需求分类和注释。 NLP4ReF 等系统证明，基于 LLM 的方法在需求分类方面可以优于传统的 NLP 流程，甚至可以低成本、高速生成候选需求（Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have expanded these capabilities. Transformer-based models can process longer contexts and capture semantic intent beyond surface-level keywords, enabling more nuanced classification and annotation of requirements. Systems such as NLP4ReF demonstrate that LLM-based approaches can outperform traditional NLP pipelines in requirement classification and even generate candidate requirements at low cost and high speed ([16]）。这些结果表明人工智能可以有效地协助日常可再生能源活动，特别是在大型或时间有限的项目中。). These results indicate that AI can meaningfully assist with routine RE activities, especially in large-scale or time-constrained projects.

然而，现有的研究仍然大多分散且针对特定领域。大多数研究侧重于孤立的任务（例如，FR/NFR 分类或需求生成），以以软件为中心的数据集为基础，或者依赖于准确性指标，而不将人工智能行为与专业系统工程判断进行比较。此外，虽然有几种方法隐含地引用了质量属性，但很少有明确地将人工智能评估与《INCOSE 写作要求指南》中定义的结构化标准保持一致。因此，目前尚不清楚人工智能评估在多大程度上与经验丰富的系统工程师如何推理需求质量、模糊性和可接受性相对应。However, existing research remains largely fragmented and domain-specific. Most studies focus on isolated tasks (e.g., FR/NFR classification or requirement generation), are grounded in software-centric datasets, or rely on accuracy metrics without comparing AI behavior to professional systems-engineering judgment. Moreover, while several approaches implicitly reference quality attributes, few explicitly align AI evaluations with the structured criteria defined in the INCOSE Guide to Writing Requirements. As a result, it remains unclear to what extent AI assessments correspond to how experienced systems engineers reason about requirement quality, ambiguity, and acceptability.

另一个限制涉及可解释性和问责制。尽管即时工程、专家角色和结构化输出已被证明可以提高人工智能性能和可用性，但人工智能系统仍然缺乏对技术真相、系统上下文和跨需求推理的访问。因此，文献一致强调人工智能应该充当决策支持工具而不是自主评估者，而人类专家保留验证和权衡决策的责任（Another limitation concerns explainability and accountability. Although prompt engineering, expert personas, and structured outputs have been shown to improve AI performance and usability, AI systems still lack access to technical truth, system context, and cross-requirement reasoning. Consequently, the literature consistently emphasizes that AI should function as a decision-support tool rather than an autonomous evaluator, with human experts retaining responsibility for validation and trade-off decisions ([3], [18]).

审查要求的过程旨在确保每项陈述符合公认的质量标准，并且可以通过拟议的人工智能框架进行有效分析。为了实现这一目标，该研究遵循了 INCOSE 写作要求指南 (GtWR) 中概述的原则，该指南定义了“良好要求”的特征。这些原则既是人工审查的基础，也是自动评估的基准。The process of reviewing the requirements aimed to ensure that each statement met recognized quality standards and could be effectively analyzed by the proposed AI framework. To achieve this, the study followed the principles outlined in the INCOSE Guide to Writing Requirements (GtWR), which defines the characteristics of a “good requirement.” These principles served as both the foundation for human review and the benchmark for automated evaluation.

INCOSE认为，一个好的需求必须是清晰、精确、可行的。在此基础上，本研究探讨了自然语言处理（NLP）和机器学习（ML）技术如何帮助自动分析和分类需求。所提出的 AI 管道使用支持向量机 (SVM)、卷积神经网络 (CNN) 和潜在狄利克雷分配 (LDA) 等模型集成了文本分类、聚类和主题建模。According to INCOSE, a good requirement must be clear, precise, and feasible. Building on this foundation, the present research examined how Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML) techniques can assist in automatically analyzing and classifying requirements. The proposed AI pipeline integrates text classification, clustering, and topic modeling using models such as Support Vector Machines (SVM), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), and Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA).

结论和贡献Conclusions and Contributions

这项研究考察了大型语言模型 (LLM) 作为需求工程中人工智能“副驾驶”的使用情况，重点关注它们在 INCOSE 框架内支持需求质量评估和分类的能力。研究结果表明，只要人工智能的部署嵌入到结构化的人机交互（HITL）流程中，人工智能就可以承担一部分日常分析工作。This research examined the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) as AI “copilots” in requirements engineering, with a focus on their ability to support requirement quality assessment and classification within an INCOSE-aligned framework. The findings demonstrate that AI can assume a portion of routine analytical work, provided its deployment is embedded within a structured human-in-the-loop (HITL) process.

关于RegardingRQ1研究表明，现代法学硕士可以根据 INCOSE“良好需求”标准对需求进行分类，其准确性与经验丰富的系统工程师相当。在识别有问题的需求时观察到高度一致，特别是结构和语言缺陷。然而，一致的, the study shows that modern LLMs can classify requirements according to INCOSE “good requirement” criteria with accuracy comparable to experienced systems engineers. High alignment was observed in identifying problematic requirements, particularly for structural and linguistic defects. However, a consistent理由差距rationale gap已确定：虽然人工智能和人类专家通常都同意修改的必要性，但他们在根本原因上可能存在分歧。人工智能强调正式的规则违反，而人类专家则优先考虑模糊性、缺失上下文和概念意图。was identified: while AI and human experts often agree on the need for revision, they may differ on the underlying cause. AI emphasizes formal rule violations, whereas human experts prioritize ambiguity, missing context, and conceptual intent.

对于ForRQ2分析证实，人工智能可以通过识别潜在意图而不是仅仅依赖语法来区分功能性和非功能性需求。不同的模型表现出不同的分类偏差，这表明模型选择必须与项目优先级保持一致，特别是在安全关键或性能驱动的系统中。, the analysis confirms that AI can distinguish between functional and non-functional requirements by identifying underlying intent rather than relying solely on syntax. Different models exhibit distinct classification biases, indicating that model selection must align with project priorities, especially in safety-critical or performance-driven systems.

在寻址中In addressingRQ3，结果凸显了人工智能在效率、一致性和可重复性方面的主要优势。人工智能采用统一的评估标准，不会出现认知疲劳或主观漂移。相反，人工智能在技术的现阶段的局限性仍然很明显：它在技术真理、物理可行性和跨文档工程逻辑方面无法提供完全准确的性能，并且仍然容易产生幻觉。, the results highlight AI’s primary advantages in efficiency, consistency, and repeatability. AI applies evaluation criteria uniformly and does not suffer from cognitive fatigue or subjective drift. Conversely, AI’s limitations remain noticeable at this stage of the technology: it does not provide totally accurate performance with regard to technical truth, physical feasibility, and cross-document engineering logic, and remains susceptible to hallucinations.

人工智能能力和人类专业知识之间的这些差距直接决定了人工智能应该如何运作。他们没有将人工智能的局限性视为障碍，而是为人工智能定义了明确的边界These gaps between AI capabilities and human expertise directly inform how AI should be operationalized. By introducing AI as a standardized pre-audit layer, organizations can reduce early lifecycle review effort and peer-review bottlenecks, while strictly preserving expert authority over system validation and decision-making.
