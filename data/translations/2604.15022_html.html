6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了一种黑盒路由攻击，它学习通用后缀来重新路由 LLM 路由器。 使用混合集成代理和编码器一致的目标，我们优化了特定于目标的后缀，该后缀使路由偏向更强和更昂贵的模型。 在 7 个路由器和 6 个数据集上进行的实验（包括真实世界的评估）显示出很强的有效性和泛化能力。 这些发现将路由定位为安全关键边界，并推动未来对成本感知路由器进行更强有力的监控。In this paper, we propose a black-box routing attack that learns a universal suffix to reroute LLM routers. Using a hybrid ensemble surrogate and an encoder-consistent objective, we optimize a target-specific suffix that biases routing towards stronger and more expensive models. Experiments on 7 routers and 6 datasets, including real-world evaluation, show strong effectiveness and generalization ability. These findings position routing as a security-critical boundary and motivate future stronger monitoring for cost-aware routers.

7 局限性Limitations

在这项工作中，我们研究了 LLM 路由的黑盒优化攻击，并为每个目标路由器训练一个单独的对抗性后缀，以将简单查询从廉价模型重新路由到昂贵模型。 这项研究有两个主要局限性。 首先，我们主要关注将查询转向更强大且通常更昂贵的模型，而在实践中，一些用户可能出于其他原因（例如延迟或安全性）而希望针对特定模型，而我们没有系统地调查这些原因。 其次，攻击假设可以访问路由器的候选模型列表以及为每个查询选择的模型的身份，这一假设在某些部署中可能不成立。In this work, we study a black-box optimization attack on LLM routing and train a separate adversarial suffix for each target router to reroute simple queries from cheap to expensive models. This study has two main limitations. First, we mainly focus on steering queries towards a stronger and typically more expensive model, whereas in practice, some users may wish to target a specific model for other reasons, such as latency or safety, which we do not systematically investigate. Second, the attack assumes access to the router’s candidate model list and to the identity of the model selected for each query, an assumption that may not hold in some deployments.
