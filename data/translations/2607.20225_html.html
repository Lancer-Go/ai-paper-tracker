摘要Abstract

虽然组合优化问题是许多科学和工程应用的核心，但由于搜索空间呈指数级增长，其解决方案仍然具有挑战性。变分量子算法为解决此类问题提供了一条有前途的途径，但其实际性能受到重复量子电路评估和经典参数更新的限制。在这项工作中，我们引入了 DQAOA-GPT，这是一种混合框架，集成了分布式量子近似优化算法（DQAOA），该算法将大型优化问题分解为较小的子问题，并使用基于 GPT 的量子电路生成来解决这些子问题。该方法不依赖迭代变分优化，而是使用经过训练的生成模型直接为分解的子问题生成高质量的量子电路。作为基准，我们在具有多达 100 个决策变量的密集 HUBO 优化问题上相对于传统 DQAOA 来评估 DQAOA-GPT。结果表明，DQAOA-GPT 显着降低了计算成本，同时保持了有竞争力的解决方案质量，对于较大的子问题规模，观察到了更大的加速。尽管这项工作侧重于基准规模验证，但该框架通过增加 GPU 资源和并行计算能力，为混合 HPC-QC 环境中的大规模组合优化提供了有前景的基础。While combinatorial optimization problems are central to many scientific and engineering applications, their solution remains challenging due to exponentially large search spaces. Variational quantum algorithms offer a promising route for tackling such problems, yet their practical performance is limited by repeated quantum circuit evaluations and classical parameter updates. In this work, we introduce DQAOA-GPT, a hybrid framework that integrates the distributed quantum approximate optimization algorithm (DQAOA), which decomposes a large optimization problem into smaller sub-problems, with GPT-based quantum circuit generation for solving those sub-problems. Rather than relying on iterative variational optimization, the proposed approach uses a trained generative model to directly generate high-quality quantum circuits for the decomposed sub-problems. As a benchmark, we evaluate DQAOA-GPT against conventional DQAOA on dense HUBO optimization problems with up to 100 decision variables. The results demonstrate that DQAOA-GPT significantly reduces computational cost while maintaining competitive solution quality, with larger acceleration observed for larger sub-problem sizes. Although this work focuses on benchmark-scale validation, the framework provides a promising foundation for larger-scale combinatorial optimization in hybrid HPC-QC environments through increased GPU resources and parallel computing capability.

I 简介Introduction

组合优化问题出现在广泛的科学和工程应用中，包括金融、物流、网络和材料设计Combinatorial optimization problems arise in a wide range of scientific and engineering applications, including finance, logistics, networks, and materials design[4, 24, 15, 13]。这些问题的特点是离散搜索空间大、变量相互作用复杂、目标景观高度非凸，使得传统优化方法难以有效解决. These problems are characterized by large discrete search spaces, complex variable interactions, and highly non-convex objective landscapes, making them difficult to solve efficiently with conventional optimization methods[14, 12, 9].

量子计算（QC）作为组合优化的潜在计算范式引起了广泛关注Quantum computing (QC) has attracted considerable attention as a potential computational paradigm for combinatorial optimization[5, 20, 2]。在最广泛研究的方法中，量子近似优化算法 (QAOA) 使用参数化量子电路通过交替成本和混合器演化来准备问题哈密顿量的近似基态. Among the most widely studied approaches, quantum approximate optimization algorithm (QAOA) uses parameterized quantum circuits to prepare approximate ground states of problem Hamiltonians through alternating cost and mixer evolutions[6, 21]。尽管 QAOA 已成为近期量子优化的主要候选者，但标准 QAOA 在应用于大规模组合优化问题时面临重大限制。随着问题规模的增加，直接编码需要更多的量子位、更深的电路以及在经典参数优化期间重复的量子电路评估，其中优化环境更加复杂，变分循环变得越来越昂贵。. Although QAOA has emerged as a leading candidate for near-term quantum optimization, standard QAOA faces major limitations when applied to large-scale combinatorial optimization problems. As the problem size increases, direct encoding requires more qubits, deeper circuits, and repeated quantum circuit evaluations during classical parameter optimization, where the optimization landscape is more complex and the variational loop becomes increasingly expensive.

为了解决这些限制，分布式 QAOA (DQAOA) 被开发为混合高性能计算 (HPC)-QC 框架，用于通过问题分解和协调执行来解决大型优化问题To address these limitations, distributed QAOA (DQAOA) has been developed as a hybrid high-performance computing (HPC)-QC framework for solving large optimization problems through problem decomposition and coordinated execution[11, 26]。这种 HPC-QC 集成提高了计算可扩展性，并解决了标准 QAOA 难以处理的大问题。尽管有这一优势，DQAOA 仍然依赖于每个分解子问题的迭代变分优化，需要重复的量子电路执行和经典参数更新。因此，变分循环仍然是主要的计算瓶颈，特别是当分解的子问题规模变大时。. This HPC-QC integration improves computational scalability and enables large problems that are difficult to handle with standard QAOA. Despite this advantage, DQAOA still relies on iterative variational optimization for each decomposed sub-problem, requiring repeated quantum circuit executions and classical parameter updates. As a result, the variational loop remains a major computational bottleneck, particularly when the decomposed sub-problem size becomes large.

生成式人工智能（尤其是变压器架构）的最新进展为量子算法设计开辟了新的方向Recent advances in generative AI, particularly transformer architectures, have opened new directions for quantum algorithm design[17, 1, 23]。 QAOA-GPT 旨在学习从问题实例到高质量量子电路的直接映射，绕过迭代变分优化. QAOA-GPT aims to learn a direct mapping from problem instances to high-quality quantum circuits, bypassing iterative variational optimization[23, 22]。通过在推理时生成优化的电路结构，这些方法可以显着减少量子经典反馈回路并提高整体计算效率。然而，现有的人工智能辅助方法主要在中小型问题规模上进行论证，并没有明确解决与大规模组合优化问题相关的可扩展性挑战。. By generating optimized circuit structures at inference time, these methods can significantly reduce the quantum-classical feedback loop and improve overall computational efficiency. Nevertheless, existing AI-assisted approaches are primarily demonstrated on small- to moderate-scale problem sizes and do not explicitly address the scalability challenges associated with large-scale combinatorial optimization problems.

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 DQAOA-GPT，这是一种混合计算框架，旨在通过将分布式量子优化与人工智能驱动的电路生成紧密集成来解决大规模组合优化问题In this work, we introduce DQAOA-GPT, a hybrid computational framework designed to address large-scale combinatorial optimization by tightly integrating distributed quantum optimization with AI-driven circuit generation[11, 23]。该框架由两个关键组件组成： (i) 基于 DQAOA 的分解和聚合策略，支持跨 HPC-QC 环境的可扩展执行，以及 (ii) 基于 GPT 的电路生成模型，用直接电路综合代替迭代参数优化。. The framework consists of two key components: (i) DQAOA-based decomposition and aggregation strategy that enables scalable execution across HPC–QC environments, and (ii) GPT-based circuit generation model that replaces iterative parameter optimization with direct circuit synthesis.

通过将分布式执行与学习电路生成相结合，DQAOA-GPT 消除了变分量子算法的主要瓶颈之一，同时保留了 DQAOA 的可扩展性优势。这种集成可以有效地探索大型且复杂的优化环境，同时减少计算开销。By coupling distributed execution with learned circuit generation, DQAOA-GPT eliminates one of the primary bottlenecks in variational quantum algorithms while preserving the scalability advantages of DQAOA. This integration enables efficient exploration of large and complex optimization landscapes with reduced computational overhead.

V 讨论Discussion

我们引入了 DQAOA-GPT，这是一种将分布式量子优化与生成电路综合相结合的混合框架，用于解决大规模组合优化问题。通过用基于 GPT 的电路生成取代迭代变分优化，该框架消除了昂贵的量子经典反馈循环，从而实现了显着的计算加速。随着子问题规模的增加，DQAOA 中的变分优化成本迅速增长，这种优势变得更加明显。We introduced DQAOA-GPT, a hybrid framework integrating distributed quantum optimization with generative circuit synthesis for large-scale combinatorial optimization problems. By replacing iterative variational optimization with GPT-based circuit generation, the framework eliminates the costly quantum-classical feedback loop, achieving substantial computational speedup. The advantage becomes more pronounced as sub-problem size increases, where variational optimization cost in DQAOA grows rapidly.

从计算的角度来看，所提出的框架本质上非常适合 HPC 环境。尽管本研究使用单个 GPU 来演示工作流程并确保公平比较，但 DQAOA-GPT 自然支持跨多个 GPU 和节点的并行执行，因为子问题可以独立解决。这将为通过支持 HPC 的并行化进一步减少运行时间和可扩展性提供清晰的途径。From a computational perspective, the proposed framework is inherently well-suited for HPC environments. Although the present study uses a single GPU to demonstrate the workflow and ensure a fair comparison, DQAOA-GPT naturally supports parallel execution across multiple GPUs and nodes, as sub-problem problems can be solved independently. This will provide a clear pathway for further runtime reduction and scalability through HPC-enabled parallelization.

总体而言，这项工作凸显了结合人工智能、高性能计算和质量控制来解决大规模组合优化问题的潜力。通过利用生成模型进行电路综合，DQAOA-GPT 提供了一种克服变分量子算法的关键局限性的实用方法。所提出的框架为混合量子经典优化提供了一条可扩展且有效的途径，并且代表了在现实应用中展示实用量子效用的一步。Overall, this work highlights the potential of combining AI, HPC, and QC to address large-scale combinatorial optimization problems. By leveraging generative models for circuit synthesis, DQAOA-GPT offers a practical approach to overcoming key limitations of variational quantum algorithms. The proposed framework provides a scalable and efficient pathway toward hybrid quantum-classical optimization and represents a step toward demonstrating practical quantum utility in real-world applications.
