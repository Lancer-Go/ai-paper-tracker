I 简介Introduction

围绕移动物体操作的人形机器人必须在几分之一秒内做出反应，并且躲避本身不能使它们失去平衡。 躲避球在短视界、全身环境中隔离了这个安全问题：机器人必须感知传入的球，确定哪些环节受到威胁，并足够快地协调躯干、手臂和腿，以避免接触，同时保持直立。 人类通过将眼睛盯着球并将整个身体移出球的路径来解决同样的问题。 最近的基于学习的控制器可以产生动态的人形运动Humanoid robots operating around moving objects must react within a fraction of a second, and the evasion itself must not cost them their balance. Dodgeball isolates this safety problem in a short-horizon, whole-body setting: the robot must perceive an incoming ball, determine which links are threatened, and coordinate the torso, arms, and legs quickly enough to avoid contact while remaining upright. Humans solve the same problem by keeping their eyes on the ball and moving their whole body out of its path. Recent learning-based controllers can produce dynamic humanoid locomotion[11, 37]、全身模仿, whole-body imitation[6, 22, 19]，以及视觉条件技能, and visually conditioned skills[35, 12]，但机载视觉的反应性全身安全仍然很困难, but reactive whole-body safety from onboard vision remains difficult[13, 15]，特别是对于人形生物。, especially for a humanoid.

控制屏障函数 (CBF) 编码无冲突集并纠正不安全命令，前提是相关状态可用Control barrier functions (CBFs) encode collision-free sets and correct unsafe commands, provided the relevant state is available[1]。 机载感知打破了这种方便的假设：传入的物体可能只是短暂可见，可能离开相机视野，或者可能显示为一些嘈杂的深度像素。 CBF-RL 可以将障碍信息转移到政策培训中. Onboard perception breaks this convenient assumption: the incoming object may be visible only briefly, may leave the camera field of view, or may appear as a few noisy depth pixels. CBF-RL can move barrier information into policy training[33]，但部署的策略只能内化其观察支持的安全结构。 因此，威胁表示和屏障设计必须被视为一个耦合的安全问题，而不是独立的模块。, but a deployed policy can internalize only as much safety structure as its observations support. The threat representation and the barrier design therefore have to be treated as one coupled safety problem rather than as independent modules.

我们通过感知感知的人形躲避球来研究这种耦合（图 1）。We study this coupling through perception-aware humanoid dodgeball (Fig.1）。 向人形机器人发射一个球，机器人必须避免与每个身体环节接触，并在部署时仅使用本体感觉和机载深度来保持平衡。 与围绕地图障碍物导航不同，危险是暂时的，并且机器人没有标称速度命令或参考运动来指定何时以及如何躲避。 短观察窗和全身碰撞几何结构使躲避球成为局部观察下安全性的直接考验。). A ball is launched toward a humanoid robot, which must avoid contact with every body link and preserve balance using only proprioception and onboard depth at deployment. Unlike navigation around a mapped obstacle, the hazard is transient, and the robot has no nominal velocity command or reference motion that specifies when and how to evade. The short observation window and whole-body collision geometry make dodgeball a direct test of safety under partial observation.

我们提出了 PAC-MAN，一种用于人形躲避球的感知感知 CBF-RL 框架。 核心问题是，当一项政策仅收到部署现实的观察结果时，它可以将多少障碍结构内在化。 PAC-MAN 研究两个级别：Link-CBF（训练期间使用的轻量级每链接奖励）和 Joint-CBF（更强大的全身关节空间投影），可以指导训练或在测试时作为特权运行时过滤器保持活动状态。 这两个级别将学习的障碍引导行为与在线障碍执行分开。We present PAC-MAN, a perception-aware CBF-RL framework for humanoid dodgeball. The central question is how much barrier structure a policy can internalize when it receives only deployment-realistic observations. PAC-MAN studies two levels: Link-CBF, a lightweight per-link reward used during training, and Joint-CBF, a stronger whole-body joint-space projection that can either guide training or stay active at test time as a privileged runtime filter. The two levels separate learned barrier-guided behavior from online barrier enforcement.

习得反射的效果如何取决于政策对威胁的看法。 部署的系统以 RGB 形式对球进行分割，并将深度图像屏蔽为紧凑的仅球观察。 模拟研究的主动万向节测试了是否能更长时间地保持威胁的视线，从而使更坚固的屏障结构可用。 对抗性运动先验（AMP）规范了人形机器人的移动方式，因此逃避表现为蹲伏、倾斜、侧步和跳跃；安全结构本身来自CBF术语。How well the learned reflex works depends on what perception tells the policy about the threat. The deployed system segments the ball in RGB and masks the depth image down to a compact ball-only observation. An active gimbal, studied in simulation, tests whether keeping the threat in view for longer makes the stronger barrier structure usable. An adversarial motion prior (AMP) regularizes how the humanoid moves, so the evasions come out as crouches, leans, sidesteps, and leaps; the safety structure itself comes from the CBF terms.

我们使用受控的、基于接触的基准来提出三个问题：障碍引导是否可以改善全身回避、策略可以在没有运行时过滤器的情况下内化多少障碍结构，以及可用的感知如何改变答案。 该基准测试针对冻结的雕像校准目标投掷，并涵盖单次投掷和部署循环，其中机器人走回其位置并在投掷之间恢复。 穿过特权状态预言机、固定机载摄像头和针对预言机的主动万向节，我们发现更坚固的屏障结构需要更多信息丰富的观察。 当准确的球状态可用时，具有运行时过滤功能的完整 Joint-CBF 最强，而较轻的 Link-CBF 是在有限的机载感知下的最佳可部署选项。 我们通过仅使用板载深度和本体感知在 Unitree G1 上部署固定相机 Link-CBF 策略零射击来验证此设计选择。We use a controlled, contact-based benchmark to ask three questions: whether barrier guidance improves whole-body avoidance, how much barrier structure a policy can internalize without a runtime filter, and how the available perception changes the answer. The benchmark calibrates on-target throws against a frozen statue and covers both single throws and a deployment loop in which the robot walks back to its station and recovers between throws. Across a privileged state oracle, a fixed onboard camera, and an oracle-aimed active gimbal, we find that stronger barrier structure needs more informative observations. The full Joint-CBF with runtime filtering is strongest when accurate ball states are available, whereas the lighter Link-CBF is the best deployable option under limited onboard perception. We validate this design choice by deploying the fixed-camera Link-CBF policy zero-shot on a Unitree G1 using only onboard depth and proprioception.

V 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

我们提出了 PAC-MAN，一种用于全身人形躲避球的感知感知 CBF-RL 框架，并仅使用板载深度和本体感知在 Unitree G1 上部署其固定摄像头 Link-CBF 策略。 PAC-MAN 将分割掩蔽深度与训练期间计算的控制屏障引导结合起来，并且人体运动先验规范了回避反射的形式。 我们的结果表明，感知感知 CBF-RL 是一个安全信息协同设计问题：适当的屏障结构取决于策略或过滤器可用的威胁表示。 凭借准确的球状态，联合 CBF 运行时过滤器是最强的配置，而仅用作奖励指导的相同联合空间屏障会降低固定相机策略。 Link-CBF 将以骨盆为中心的目标扩展到每个身体环节，同时容忍有限的机载感知，并且部署的策略在以下方面取得了成功We presented PAC-MAN, a perception-aware CBF-RL framework for whole-body humanoid dodgeball, and deployed its fixed-camera Link-CBF policy on the Unitree G1 using only onboard depth and proprioception. PAC-MAN couples segmentation-masked depth with control-barrier guidance computed during training, and a human motion prior regularizes the form of the evasive reflexes. Our results show that perception-aware CBF-RL is a safety-information co-design problem: the appropriate barrier structure depends on the threat representation available to the policy or filter. With accurate ball states, the Joint-CBF runtime filter is the strongest configuration, whereas the same joint-space barrier used only as reward guidance degrades the fixed-camera policy. Link-CBF extends the pelvis-centered objective to every body link while tolerating limited onboard perception, and the deployed policy succeeds on95%95\%没有特权球状态或运行时过滤器的物理投掷。 分段前端还可以让一种策略避开不同的球。 这些结果将学习策略中的内部化屏障结构与在线强制执行屏障区分开来：内部化受到策略可以推断的内容的限制，而执行则需要运行时准确的威胁状态。 未来的工作将通过物理跟踪器瞄准的万向节或准确的在线球状态估计器来关闭这种感知到 CBF 的循环，并将任务从恢复姿势时的躲避扩展到行走时的躲避。of physical throws without privileged ball states or a runtime filter. The segmentation front end also lets one policy evade different balls. These results distinguish internalizing barrier structure in a learned policy from enforcing a barrier online: internalization is limited by what the policy can infer, while enforcement requires accurate threat state at runtime. Future work will close this perception-to-CBF loop with a physical tracker-aimed gimbal or an accurate online ball-state estimator and extend the task from dodging at a recovered stance to dodging while walking.
