摘要Abstract

深度强化学习的最新进展越来越有利于简化、高度并行化的范式。值得注意的是，并行 Q 网络 (PQN) 算法无需依赖计算成本高昂的重播缓冲区或目标网络即可实现稳定的离策略学习。然而，在这些无缓冲区设置中运行的视觉编码器的表示能力和参数效率仍未得到充分探索。在这项工作中，我们系统地研究了 PQN 的卷积神经网络的架构设计空间。我们设计并严格评估了八种不同的 CNN 拓扑，在严格的参数约束下优化样本效率。此外，我们通过集成 Hadamax 编码范式和高级 Q 学习扩展（包括分布式、集成和决斗头）来研究表示和值估计增强的影响。 Atari-57 基准测试的大量实验表明，我们提出的复合架构 Aftab 的四分位均值 (IQM) 人类归一化得分为 6.479，相对于标准 PQN 基线的改进概率为 0.86。此外，对高度非平稳的 Procgen Hard 基准的结构弹性评估证实了分布外泛化，Aftab 产生的 IQM Procgen 标准化得分为 0.418，而基线为 0.382。最终，这项工作为无模型强化学习建立了一个高效、概率优越的结构参考，同时保留了无缓冲、并行优化的简单性和内存效率。Recent advancements in deep reinforcement learning have increasingly favored simplified, highly parallelized paradigms. Notably, the Parallelized Q-Network (PQN) algorithm achieves stable off-policy learning without relying on computationally expensive replay buffers or target networks. However, the representational capacity and parameter efficiency of visual encoders operating in these buffer-free settings remain underexplored. In this work, we systematically investigate the architectural design space of Convolutional Neural Networks for PQN. We design and rigorously evaluate eight distinct CNN topologies, optimizing for sample efficiency under strict parameter constraints. Furthermore, we study the impact of representation and value estimation enhancements by integrating the Hadamax encoding paradigm and advanced Q-learning extensions, including distributional, ensemble, and dueling heads. Extensive experiments on the Atari-57 benchmark demonstrate that our proposed composite architecture, Aftab, achieves an Interquartile Mean (IQM) Human-Normalized Score of 6.479, establishing a 0.86 Probability of Improvement over the standard PQN baseline. Additionally, structural resilience evaluations on the highly non-stationary Procgen Hard benchmark confirm out-of-distribution generalization, with Aftab yielding an IQM Procgen Normalized Score of 0.418 compared to the baseline’s 0.382. Ultimately, this work establishes an efficient, probabilistically superior structural reference for model-free reinforcement learning, all while preserving the simplicity and memory efficiency of unbuffered, parallelized optimization.

7.3 局限性Limitations

虽然我们的实证评估表明更深层次的结构拓扑和集成多头回归系统具有明显的统计优势，但必须明确解决几个核心限制。While our empirical evaluations demonstrate clear statistical advantages for deeper structural topologies and integrated multi-head regression systems, several core limitations must be explicitly addressed.

首先，尽管 Atari-57 和 Procgen Hard 基准测试呈现出高度多样化的视觉和程序配置，但我们的观察仍然仅限于离散动作、帧堆叠的强化学习范例。我们的架构发现，特别是乘法 Hadamard 特征流和高维分布式集成决斗头 (Aftab)，推广到高频连续控制任务或长范围时间推理套件（例如 DeepMind 控制套件）的程度First, although the Atari-57 and Procgen Hard benchmarks present highly diverse visual and procedural configurations, our observations remain restricted to discrete-action, frame-stacked reinforcement learning paradigms. The degree to which our architectural findings, specifically the multiplicative Hadamard feature streams and the high-dimensional Distributional Ensemble Dueling head (Aftab), generalize to high-frequency continuous control tasks or long-horizon temporal reasoning suites (such as the DeepMind Control Suite[Tassa et al., 2018]) 有待实证验证。) remains to be empirically verified.

8 结论Conclusion

以并行 Q 网络 (PQN) 框架为例，向高度并行、无缓冲区的时间差异学习的过渡代表了深度强化学习的方法论转变。然而，通过严格默认传统特征提取器，该领域可能会人为地阻碍这些简化算法的表示潜力。在这项研究中，我们引入了 Aftab，一个开源基准测试框架，旨在系统地评估在线、离线策略体系内的架构拓扑、非线性表示协同作用和高级回归范例。The transition toward highly parallelized, buffer-free temporal difference learning, exemplified by the Parallelized Q-Network (PQN) framework, represents a methodological shift in deep reinforcement learning. However, by strictly defaulting to legacy feature extractors, the field may be artificially bottlenecking the representational potential of these streamlined algorithms. In this study, we introduced Aftab, an open-source benchmarking framework designed to systematically evaluate architectural topology, non-linear representational synergies, and advanced regression paradigms within online, off-policy regimes.

通过对确定性 Atari-57 基准和高度非平稳的 Procgen Hard 套件进行全面、多阶段的实证评估，我们的大规模分析得出了有关样本效率和神经架构设计的重要见解：Through a comprehensive, multi-phase empirical evaluation across the deterministic Atari-57 benchmark and the highly non-stationary Procgen Hard suite, our large-scale analysis yields critical insights regarding sample efficiency and neural architecture design:

  • 1.

    拓扑深度作为主要驱动因素（第一阶段）：Topological Depth as a Primary Driver (Phase 1):我们的基线 CNN 消融提供了强有力的证据，证明结构归纳偏差决定了泛化能力。虽然 Alpha 变体在基础架构 (3.536 IQM HNS) 中实现了最高的总体性能，但 5 层 Gamma 变体成为最佳架构基础。 Gamma 实现了统计上相当的 3.481 IQM，同时具有卓越的计算效率，仅需要 184 万个总参数和 2450 万次 FLOP。相反，Eta 变体大幅扩展了通道宽度，将其参数数量提升至 2380 万，但 IQM 较低，为 3.114。这证实，在我们的评估协议下，原始参数扩展不能充分替代卷积深度提供的分层特征抽象。Our baseline CNN ablations provide strong evidence that structural inductive bias dictates generalization capability. While the Alpha variant achieved the highest aggregate performance among the base architectures (3.536 IQM HNS), the 5-layer Gamma variant emerged as the optimal architectural foundation. Gamma achieved a statistically comparable 3.481 IQM while operating with superior computational efficiency, requiring only 1.84 million total parameters and 24.5 million FLOPs. Conversely, the Eta variant, which heavily expanded channel width to drive its parameter count to 23.8 million, achieved a lower 3.114 IQM. This confirms that, under our evaluation protocol, raw parameter expansion cannot adequately substitute for the hierarchical feature abstraction provided by convolutional depth.

  • 2.

    空间瓶颈的惩罚（第一阶段）：The Penalty of Spatial Bottlenecks (Phase 1):我们的实验发现了严重限制策略表达性的特定拓扑陷阱。 Delta 变体（2.374 IQM）的显着性能下降说明了早期层中的积极空间减少，特别是通过Our experiments identified specific topological pitfalls that severely restrict policy expressivity. The significant performance degradation of the Delta variant (2.374 IQM) illustrates that aggressive spatial reduction in early layers, specifically via a9×99\times 9初始内核与反应控制任务所需的细粒度视觉粒度的严重损失相关。类似地，Theta 变体 (2.649 IQM) 证明，将终端卷积层中的输出维度减半会在回归头之前造成表征瓶颈。initial kernel, correlates with a critical loss of the fine-grained visual granularity required for reactive control tasks. Similarly, the Theta variant (2.649 IQM) demonstrated that halving the output dimensionality in the terminal convolutional layer imposes a representational bottleneck prior to the regression head.

  • 3.

    乘法特征交互的协同效应（第 2 阶段）：Synergies of Multiplicative Feature Interactions (Phase 2):将 Hadamard 非线性集成到最佳 Gamma 拓扑中大大提高了表示能力。 Gamma-Hadamax-Valid 架构用最大池下采样和逐元素乘法交互取代了标准空间跨步，并与零饱和 GELU 激活配对。这种组合产生的 IQM HNS 为 5.343，优于基线 Hadamax 控制（5.149 IQM），同时需要不到一半的参数数量（1.84M vs. 4.12M），并且显着减少了计算占用量（123.6M vs. 162.9M FLOPs）。这确立了乘法相互作用与优化的卷积深度有效地复合，从而在不依赖大量残差堆栈的情况下增强表示。Integrating Hadamard non-linearities into the optimal Gamma topology substantially improved representational capacity. The Gamma-Hadamax-Valid architecture substituted standard spatial striding with max-pooling downsampling and element-wise multiplicative interactions, paired with zero-saturating GELU activations. This combination yielded an IQM HNS of 5.343, outperforming the baseline Hadamax control (5.149 IQM) while requiring less than half the parameter count (1.84M vs. 4.12M) and a significantly reduced computational footprint (123.6M vs. 162.9M FLOPs). This establishes that multiplicative interactions compound effectively with optimized convolutional depth, enhancing representation without relying on massive residual stacks.

  • 4.

    无缓冲算法集成的稳定性（第 3 阶段）：Stability of Buffer-Free Algorithmic Integration (Phase 3):我们证明了复杂的回归头，传统上依赖大量的经验重放缓冲区和目标网络来稳定其损失情况We demonstrated that complex regression heads, which traditionally rely on massive experience replay buffers and target networks to stabilize their loss landscapes[Hessel et al., 2018]，可以在严格的在线并行机制中可靠地运行。通过解耦优势流（Dueling）、预测分类回报分布（Distributional）以及通过多头集成驱动定向认知探索，Aftab 获得了 6.479 IQM HNS 的最高总分。, can operate reliably in a strictly online, parallelized regime. By decoupling the advantage stream (Dueling), predicting categorical return distributions (Distributional), and driving directed epistemic exploration via multi-head ensembles, Aftab achieved the highest overall score of 6.479 IQM HNS.

  • 5.

    分布外泛化：Out-of-Distribution Generalization:为了明确评估针对大容量架构记忆的结构弹性，我们的框架在程序生成的 Procgen Hard 基准上进行了测试。最终的 Aftab 架构的 IQM Procgen 归一化总分 (PNS) 为 0.418，优于基线 PQN 模型 (0.382 IQM PNS)。这证实了本研究中建立的归纳偏差可以有效地转化为非确定性的、结构变化的视觉域，而不会过度拟合固定的像素配置。To explicitly evaluate structural resilience against high-capacity architectural memorization, our framework was tested on the procedurally generated Procgen Hard benchmark. The final Aftab architecture achieved an aggregate IQM Procgen Normalized Score (PNS) of 0.418, outperforming the baseline PQN model (0.382 IQM PNS). This validates that the inductive biases established in this study translate effectively to non-deterministic, structurally shifting visual domains without overfitting to fixed pixel configurations.

最终，这项研究表明，依赖历史上静态的“自然 CNN”骨干网限制了算法的泛化。通过系统地优化层次深度、结合非线性乘性特征交互以及部署方差减少的多头回归系统，Aftab 框架为并行深度强化学习建立了稳定且计算高效的架构参考。Ultimately, this research suggests that relying on the historically static "Nature CNN" backbone restricts algorithmic generalization. By systematically optimizing hierarchical depth, incorporating non-linear multiplicative feature interactions, and deploying variance-reduced, multi-head regression systems, the Aftab framework establishes a stable and computationally efficient architectural reference for parallelized deep reinforcement learning.
