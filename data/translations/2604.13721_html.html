FRAGATA：通过混合 RAG 进行 HPC 支持票证的语义检索超过 20 年的请求跟踪器历史FRAGATA: Semantic Retrieval of HPC Support Tickets via Hybrid RAG over 20 Years of Request Tracker History

Santiago Paramés-Estévez A Spanish version of this paper has been submitted at Jornadas SARTECO 2026.Galician Supercomputing Center (CESGA), Santiago de Compostela, e-mail: sparames@cesga.es.    Nicolás Filloy-Montesino Universidade de Vigo.    Jorge Fernández-Fabeiro2 and José Carlos-Mouriño Gallego2
摘要Abstract

超级计算中心的技术支持团队在数十年的时间里积累了大量已解决的事件，这些事件构成了关键的操作知识。在加利西亚超级计算中心 (CESGA)，这段历史已经通过请求跟踪器 (RT) 管理了二十多年，其内置搜索引擎具有很大的局限性，阻碍了支持人员的知识重用。本文介绍了The technical support team of a supercomputing centre accumulates, over the course of decades, a large volume of resolved incidents that constitute critical operational knowledge. At the Galician Supercomputing Center (CESGA) this history has been managed for over twenty years with Request Tracker (RT), whose built-in search engine has significant limitations that hinder knowledge reuse by the support staff. This paper presents弗拉加塔Fragata，一个语义票证搜索系统，将现代信息检索技术与完整的 RT 历史相结合。无论语言、拼写错误的存在或查询的具体措辞如何，系统都可以找到相关的过去事件。该架构部署在 CESGA 的基础设施上，支持增量更新而不中断服务，并将最昂贵的阶段卸载到 FinisTerrae III 超级计算机。初步结果显示，与 RT 的原生搜索相比，有了实质性的质的改进。, a semantic ticket search system that combines modern information retrieval techniques with the full RT history. The system can find relevant past incidents regardless of language, the presence of typos, or the specific wording of the query. The architecture is deployed on CESGA’s infrastructure, supports incremental updates without service interruption, and offloads the most expensive stages to the FinisTerrae III supercomputer. Preliminary results show a substantial qualitative improvement over RT’s native search.

1 简介Introduction

高性能计算 (HPC) 中心运行异构平台，托管数百个科学应用程序、编译器、并行库、资源调度程序和分布式存储系统。在此环境中，技术支持团队充当研究用户和基础设施之间的关键接口，解决从 MPI 编译错误到存储配额、Conda 环境配置或 GPU 节点上的网络故障等事件。加利西亚超级计算中心 (CESGA) 使用请求跟踪器 (RT) 管理此工作流程已有二十多年High-Performance Computing (HPC) centres operate heterogeneous platforms hosting hundreds of scientific applications, compilers, parallel libraries, resource schedulers, and distributed storage systems. In this environment, the technical support team acts as a critical interface between research users and the infrastructure, resolving incidents ranging from MPI compilation errors to storage quotas, Conda environment configuration, or network failures on GPU nodes. The Galician Supercomputing Center (CESGA) has managed this workflow for over two decades using Request Tracker (RT)[1]，一种广泛使用的开源票务工具。, a widely used open-source ticketing tool.

RT 提供两种主要交互渠道：网络界面（仅支持人员可用）和电子邮件。 CESGA 用户仅通过电子邮件与系统交互，发送和回复消息，RT 自动将其转换为票证和相关回复。这种动态对存储文本的质量有直接影响，详见章节RT offers two main interaction channels: a web interface (available only to support staff) and e-mail. CESGA users interact with the system exclusively via e-mail, sending and replying to messages that RT automatically converts into tickets and associated responses. This dynamic has direct implications for the quality of the stored text, as detailed in Section3.2.

CESGA 在 RT 中积累的历史跨越了支持人员和用户之间二十多年的对话，构成了宝贵的操作记忆：今天收到的事件中有很大一部分是多年前解决的问题的变体。然而，RT 版本 4.4.1 在其内置搜索中存在严重限制：它不索引完整的票证正文、区分大小写、不容忍拼写错误、不规范形态变体，并且缺乏任何语义相似性的概念。因此，老员工和新入职员工都很难找到相关的过去案例，导致重复工作、机构知识流失以及平均解决时间增加。The history accumulated by CESGA in RT spans over twenty years of conversations between support staff and users, constituting an invaluable operational memory: a significant proportion of the incidents received today are variants of problems solved years ago. However, RT version 4.4.1 has severe limitations in its built-in search: it does not index the full ticket body, is case-sensitive, does not tolerate typos, does not normalize morphological variants, and lacks any notion of semantic similarity. As a result, both veteran and newly onboarded staff struggle to locate relevant past cases, leading to duplicated effort, loss of institutional knowledge, and increased mean resolution time.

本文介绍了This paper presents弗拉加塔Fragata是在 CESGA 设计和部署的语义票证检索系统，旨在缓解这些限制。, a semantic ticket retrieval system designed and deployed at CESGA to mitigate these limitations.弗拉加塔Fragata适用于applies the检索增强生成Retrieval-Augmented Generation（RAG）范式(RAG) paradigm[8]，一种通过首先从知识库中检索最相关的文档并将其呈现为上下文来提高搜索质量的方法。具体来说，该系统结合了基于, an approach that improves search quality by first retrieving the most relevant documents from a knowledge base and presenting them as context. Specifically, the system combines dense retrieval based on嵌入embeddings，文本含义的数值向量表示，使用经典的 BM25 词汇检索, numerical vector representations of text meaning, with classical BM25 lexical retrieval[13]和and重新排名reranking通过via交叉编码器cross-encoders [10]。该系统还集成了补充资源（中心的技术文档、科学应用手册和存储库），并部署在由虚拟机和 FinisTerrae III 超级计算机组成的混合架构上，并具有搜索引擎的增量摄取和热重载功能。. The system also integrates complementary sources (the centre’s technical documentation, scientific application manuals, and repositories) and is deployed on a hybrid architecture comprising a virtual machine and the FinisTerrae III supercomputer, with incremental ingestion and hot-reload of the search engine.

主要贡献是：(i) 设计一个可重现的协议，用于提取、规范化和 RT 的 SQL 历史分块； (ii) 具有加权融合和查询感知重排序的混合检索架构； (iii) 热插拔机制，保证重新索引期间的持续可用性； (iv) 与 HPC 调度程序进行操作集成，以卸载昂贵的摄取阶段，而不会影响服务延迟。The main contributions are: (i) the design of a reproducible protocol for extraction, normalization, and chunking of RT’s SQL history; (ii) a hybrid retrieval architecture with weighted fusion and query-aware reranking; (iii) a hot-swap mechanism that guarantees continuous availability during re-indexing; and (iv) operational integration with an HPC scheduler to offload expensive ingestion stages without penalizing service latency.

3.2 标准化和分块Normalization and chunking

如上所述，RT 中的大多数交互都是通过电子邮件进行的。当用户回复消息时，电子邮件客户端会自动将先前消息的文本包含在对话线程中。这会导致每个票证中存储的原始消息包含对先前消息的引用，从而导致累积冗余：相同的信息在多个线程条目中部分或完全重复出现。此外，邮件还包含电子邮件的典型噪音：引用的标题、个人签名、机构横幅和不一致的格式。As noted above, most interactions in RT occur via e-mail. When a user replies to a message, the e-mail client automatically includes the text of previous messages in the conversation thread. This causes the raw messages stored in each ticket to contain references to prior messages, leading to cumulative redundancy: the same information appears partially or fully repeated across multiple thread entries. Additionally, messages contain noise typical of e-mail: quoted headers, personal signatures, institutional banners, and inconsistent formatting.

规范化阶段通过应用一系列操作来删除这些冗余信息和相关噪声：字符解码、删除已知标头和签名、抑制线程中较早引用的消息以及空白折叠。目标是将票证中包含的每条信息准确地呈现给检索系统一次，而不会出现可能扭曲结果的重复。The normalization phase removes this redundant information and associated noise by applying a sequence of operations: character decoding, removal of known headers and signatures, suppression of quoted messages from earlier in the thread, and whitespace collapsing. The goal is to present the retrieval system with each piece of information contained in a ticket exactly once, without duplications that could distort the results.

标准化后，分块过程将每个对话分割成有限长度的重叠片段。这种分割是必要的，因为嵌入模型对其可以处理的文本长度有限制，并且更短、更集中的片段会产生更精确的矢量表示。每个块都保留身份元数据（After normalization, a chunking process segments each conversation into overlapping fragments of bounded length. This segmentation is necessary because embedding models have a limit on the text length they can process, and shorter, more focused fragments produce more precise vector representations. Each chunk preserves identity metadata (票证 IDticket_id, 对话idconversation_id, 块IDchunk_id），过滤元数据（), filtering metadata (部门department, 最后更新last_updated, 源类型source_type），以及摄取可追溯性元数据。), and ingestion traceability metadata.

4.2 查询变体Query variants

在检索之前，引擎会生成原始查询的加权变体，以最大限度地提高找到相关文档的概率。变体包括： 查询的规范标准化形式；当在语料库词汇中检测到频繁候选者时进行单次编辑距离校正；基于意图的扩展，添加与查询类型相关的术语（定义、安装、容器、错误/故障排除）；以及通过精选的西班牙语/英语术语词典进行的翻译。Before retrieval, the engine generates weighted variants of the original query to maximize the probability of finding relevant documents. The variants include: the canonically normalized form of the query; a single-edit-distance correction when a frequent candidate is detected in the corpus vocabulary; an intent-based expansion that adds terms associated with the query type (definition, installation, containers, error/troubleshooting); and a translation via a curated Spanish/English term dictionary.

最后一个变体解决了 CESGA 环境的一个特征：三语制。支持票证包含西班牙语、英语和加利西亚语文本，因为用户在通信中可以自由使用这三种语言。将关键术语翻译成技术文档中的主要语言英语，可以标准化查询表示并改进与语料库片段的匹配，无论它们最初是用什么语言编写的。加利西亚语由于其语言接近西班牙语，因此隐含地受益于西班牙语变体和系统使用的多语言嵌入。这种多样化增加了对简短或模糊查询的回忆，并提供了针对语料库的语言异质性的鲁棒性。This last variant addresses a characteristic aspect of CESGA’s environment: trilingualism. Support tickets contain text in Spanish, English, and Galician, as users freely employ all three languages in their communications. Translating key terms into English, the predominant language in technical documentation, standardizes query representation and improves matching against corpus fragments, regardless of the language in which they were originally written. Galician, due to its linguistic proximity to Spanish, implicitly benefits from both Spanish-language variants and the multilingual embeddings used by the system. This diversification increases recall on short or ambiguous queries and provides robustness against the linguistic heterogeneity of the corpus.

5 增量摄取和热插拔Incremental ingestion and hot-swap

生产搜索服务无法承受重新索引所需的停机时间。A production search service cannot afford downtime for re-indexing.弗拉加塔Fragata通过由 Slurm 编排的摄取架构、FinisTerrae III 的作业调度程序和原子引擎重新加载机制来满足这一要求。addresses this requirement with an ingestion architecture orchestrated by Slurm, the job scheduler of FinisTerrae III, and an atomic engine reload mechanism.

5.1 工作模式Job model

每次摄取，无论是由 API 发起（Every ingestion, whether initiated by API (/摄取/网络/ingest/web, /摄取/pdf/ingest/pdf, /摄取/repo-docs/ingest/repo-docs, /摄取/rt-每周/ingest/rt-weekly）或由调度程序实现，具体化为具有清单的作业（) or by the scheduler, materializes as a job with a manifest (清单.jsonmanifest.json）和执行日志（) and an execution log (作业日志job.log）以原子方式持续存在。清单跟踪状态（) persisted atomically. 5.2 序列化关键部分Serialized critical section

改变全局状态、数据集合并、源目录更新、FAISS 索引附加和引擎重新加载的阶段在全局改变锁下执行。如果没有这种序列化，两个并发作业可能会损坏彼此的数据集、在不一致的快照上附加增量，或者使用不同代的工件重新加载引擎。可接受的成本是减少关键部分的并发性，考虑到中心的使用模式，这是可以接受的。The stages that mutate global state, dataset merge, source catalogue update, FAISS index append, and engine reload, execute under a global mutation lock. Without this serialization, two concurrent jobs could corrupt each other’s dataset, append deltas over inconsistent snapshots, or reload the engine with artifacts from different generations. The accepted cost is reduced concurrency in the critical section, which is acceptable given the centre’s usage pattern.

5.3 具有原子提升的增量追加Incremental append with atomic promotion

增量追加模块加载活动 FAISS 索引，添加增量文档，验证索引和文档存储增长，写入暂存位置，重新验证，然后才以原子方式提升新索引并将前一个索引旋转到带时间戳的备份。在在线流程中，任何不一致都会触发显式故障，而不是默默地启动完整重建，从而允许立即检测和诊断。The incremental append module loads the active FAISS index, adds the delta documents, validates index and docstore growth, writes to a staging location, re-validates, and only then atomically promotes the new index and rotates the previous one to a timestamped backup. In the online flow, any inconsistency triggers an explicit failure rather than silently launching a full rebuild, allowing immediate detection and diagnosis.

5.4 发动机热插拔Engine hot-swap

成功索引突变后，编排器请求重新加载引擎。管理器使用两个单独的锁：一个序列化构建，另一个保护指向活动引擎的指针。新引擎是在最终交换之外构建的，并且只有在构建成功完成并增加生成计数器的情况下，转换才会生效。如果构建失败，服务将继续使用先前的引擎进行响应，确保在不中断服务的情况下平稳降级。After a successful index mutation, the orchestrator requests an engine reload. The manager uses two separate locks: one serializes builds and the other protects the pointer to the active engine. The new engine is built outside the final swap, and the changeover only takes effect if the build completes successfully, incrementing a generation counter. If the build fails, the service continues responding with the previous engine, ensuring graceful degradation without service interruption.

5.5 摄取周期和事务水印Ingestion cycle and transactional watermark

每周批处理管道从由参考时间戳确定的 RT 中提取增量窗口（The weekly batch pipeline extracts an incremental window from RT determined by a reference timestamp (水印watermark）加上安全重叠。这种重叠是必要的，以确保不会错过最后一次修改发生在非常接近前一个窗口边界的票证：服务器之间的时钟差异、截止时间的正在进行的事务或数据传播延迟可能会导致在水印之前修改的票证未被记录。通过稍微重叠窗口，系统重新处理少量已知的票证，这些票证会通过重复数据删除而被丢弃，但避免了覆盖间隙。) plus a safety overlap. This overlap is necessary to ensure that tickets whose last modification occurred very close to the boundary of the previous window are not missed: clock differences between servers, in-flight transactions at the cutoff time, or data propagation delays could cause a ticket modified just before the watermark to go unrecorded. By slightly overlapping the windows, the system reprocesses a small number of already-known tickets, which are discarded by deduplication, but avoids coverage gaps.

该管道按部门准备数据、合并全局增量、更新索引并重新加载引擎。仅当所有先前阶段都成功时才确认水印，从而使时间指针推进相对于引擎状态的事务操作。The pipeline prepares data by department, consolidates a global delta, updates the index, and reloads the engine. The watermark is only confirmed if all previous stages have succeeded, making the temporal pointer advance a transactional operation with respect to the engine state.

8 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and future work

本文提出了This paper has presented弗拉加塔Fragata，一个基于混合 RAG 的 HPC 支持票证语义检索系统，部署在 CESGA 的基础设施上。通过嵌入进行密集检索、BM25 词汇检索、WRRF 加权融合和跨编码器重新排名，再加上具有引擎热插拔和选择性卸载到 FinisTerrae III 超级计算机的增量摄取架构，使得将二十多年的请求跟踪器历史记录转变为有效的可搜索资源成为可能，克服了 RT 4.4.1 本地搜索的限制。, a semantic retrieval system for HPC support tickets based on hybrid RAG and deployed on CESGA’s infrastructure. The combination of dense retrieval via embeddings, BM25 lexical retrieval, WRRF-weighted fusion, and cross-encoder reranking, together with an incremental ingestion architecture with engine hot-swap and selective offloading to the FinisTerrae III supercomputer, has made it possible to turn over twenty years of Request Tracker history into an effectively searchable resource, overcoming the limitations of RT 4.4.1’s native search.

未来的工作包括采用自动学习的意图分类器来代替静态启发式方法、使用精心策划的查询集进行定量评估以及支持团队本身生成的相关性判断、将系统扩展到具有增强生成功能的对话界面（引用源票证来合成可操作的答案），以及将部署推广到有兴趣利用自己的支持历史记录的其他 HPC 中心。Future work includes the incorporation of an automatically learned intent classifier in place of static heuristics, quantitative evaluation with a curated query set and relevance judgments generated by the support team itself, extension of the system to a conversational interface with augmented generation that synthesizes actionable answers citing source tickets, and generalization of the deployment to other HPC centres interested in exploiting their own support histories.
