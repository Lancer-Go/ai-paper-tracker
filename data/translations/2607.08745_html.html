AUTOPILOT VQA：对以事件为中心的行车记录仪理解的视觉语言模型进行基准测试AUTOPILOT VQA: Benchmarking Vision-Language Models for Incident-Centric Dashcam Understanding

Siddharth Damodharan
University of Colorado Colorado Springs    Radhika Gupta
University of Michigan    Ali Alshami
University of Colorado Colorado Springs    Ryan Rabinowitz
University of Notre Dame    Jugal Kalita
University of Colorado Colorado Springs
摘要Abstract

视觉语言模型、大语言模型和多模态大语言模型的最新进展改善了场景理解、决策、轨迹预测和视觉问答等自动驾驶任务。然而，评估这些模型是否能够可靠地推理安全关键事件仍然具有挑战性。为了解决这一差距，我们推出了 AUTOPILOT-VQA，这是一种以事件为中心的视觉问答基准，用于理解行车记录仪视频。该数据集通过围绕现实世界驾驶事件和接近事故设计的结构化问题来评估不同的系统。该基准涵盖多种安全相关类别，包括天气和照明条件、交通环境、道路布局、路面状态、标牌、涉及实体、事故发生、影响位置以及可避免性相关推理。通过要求模型回答有关上下文场景属性和事件级事件细节的基础问题，AUTOPILOT-VQA 超越了对象识别，转向了基于时间的、安全意识推理。该数据集作为 AUTOPILOT CVPR 2026 竞赛的一部分发布，为评估不同场景下自动驾驶系统的可靠性提供了标准化基准。我们的基准支持为现实世界的自动驾驶开发更具可解释性、稳健性和安全意识的视觉语言系统。Recent advances in Vision-Language Models, Large Language Models, and Multimodal Large Language Models have improved autonomous driving tasks such as scene understanding, decision making, trajectory prediction, and visual question answering. However, evaluating whether these models can reliably reason about safety-critical incidents remains challenging. To address this gap, we present AUTOPILOT-VQA, an incident-centric visual question answering benchmark for dashcam video understanding. The dataset evaluates different systems through structured questions designed around real-world driving incidents and near-incidents. The benchmark covers diverse safety-relevant categories, including weather and lighting conditions, traffic environment, road layout, road surface state, signage, involved entities, accident occurrence, impact location, and avoidability-related reasoning. By requiring models to answer grounded questions about both contextual scene properties and event-level incident details, AUTOPILOT-VQA moves beyond object recognition toward temporally grounded, safety-aware reasoning. The dataset is released as part of the AUTOPILOT CVPR 2026 competition and provides a standardized benchmark for assessing the reliability of autonomous driving systems in different scenarios. Our benchmark support developments for more interpretable, robust, and safety-conscious vision-language systems for real-world autonomous driving.

1 简介Introduction

自动驾驶车辆依靠对复杂交通场景的准确解释来确保在现实环境中安全可靠的运行。在日常驾驶中，自动驾驶系统必须处理大量多模式传感器数据，同时响应动态和不确定的条件，包括周围交通行为、道路几何形状、行人、障碍物以及天气或能见度变化。场景理解中的错误可能会传播到规划和控制中，从而导致不安全或错误的驾驶决策。因此，强大的感知和情境推理仍然是自动驾驶汽车开发的核心挑战Autonomous vehicles rely on accurate interpretation of complex traffic scenes to ensure safe and reliable operation in real-world environments. In everyday driving, autonomous systems must process large volumes of multimodal sensor data while responding to dynamic and uncertain conditions, including surrounding traffic behavior, road geometry, pedestrians, obstacles, and weather or visibility changes. Errors in scene understanding can propagate to planning and control, leading to unsafe or incorrect driving decisions. Consequently, robust perception and contextual reasoning remain central challenges in autonomous vehicle development[6, 7, 1].

过去十年来，自动驾驶领域取得了实质性进展，这主要是由大规模公共数据集和标准化基准驱动的。 KITTI 等开源资源Substantial progress has been made in autonomous driving over the past decade, largely driven by large-scale public datasets and standardized benchmarks. Open-source resources such as KITTI[6], 城市景观, Cityscapes[4],BDD100K, BDD100K[12], nuScenes [1], 阿尔戈宇宙, Argoverse[2]，以及 Waymo 开放数据集, and the Waymo Open Dataset[10]加速了物体检测、跟踪、车道理解、定位和轨迹预测方面的研究。这些基准使现代自动驾驶系统能够在常规驾驶条件和结构化城市环境下实现强大的性能。have accelerated research in object detection, tracking, lane understanding, localization, and trajectory forecasting. These benchmarks have enabled modern autonomous systems to achieve strong performance under routine driving conditions and structured urban environments.

尽管取得了这些进步，但罕见的安全关键事件仍然是自动驾驶系统最具挑战性的场景之一。事故、未遂事件和其他危险事件通常是由多个相互作用的因素引起的，而不是单一的感知故障。理解此类场景需要的不仅仅是检测可见物体或仅从视频中对事件进行分类。系统必须推理周围环境，识别所涉及的实体，解释道路使用者的行为，评估严重程度，并推断与紧急响应或预防行动相关的因素。此外，事故理解通常受益于基于语言的信息，例如自然语言场景描述或警察报告式摘要，它们可以提供有关故障、严重程度、涉及各方和事件进展的详细信息。由于这些事件很少发生，但后果却极其严重，因此结构化事故现场理解仍然是自动驾驶领域的一个重要的开放挑战Despite these advances, rare safety-critical incidents remain among the most challenging scenarios for autonomous driving systems. Accidents, near-misses, and other hazardous events often arise from multiple interacting factors rather than a single perception failure. Understanding such scenes requires more than detecting visible objects or classifying an event from video alone. A system must reason about the surrounding context, identify the entities involved, interpret road-user behavior, assess severity, and infer factors relevant to emergency response or preventive action. In addition, accident understanding often benefits from language-based information, such as natural-language scene descriptions or police-report-style summaries, which can provide details about fault, severity, involved parties, and incident progression. Because these events are infrequent but disproportionately consequential, structured accident-scene understanding remains an important open challenge in autonomous driving[9, 5].

为了应对这一挑战，我们引入To address this challenge, we introduce自动驾驶仪VQAAutopilot VQA，自动驾驶中结构化事故现场理解的基准。该数据集包含 600 多个行车记录仪视频剪辑，涵盖碰撞、险情和无事故基线序列。每个片段都通过六个语义组进行注释，产生超过 6,000 个问答对，涵盖环境条件、道路配置、相关实体行为、事件类别、故障归因和影响特征。该基准旨在评估模型是否能够超越低级感知进行推理，并针对安全关键驾驶场景生成结构化答案。, a benchmark for structured accident-scene understanding in autonomous driving. The dataset contains more than 600 dashcam video clips spanning collisions, near-misses, and no-incident baseline sequences. Each clip is annotated through six semantic groups, resulting in more than 6,000 question-answer pairs that cover environmental conditions, road configuration, involved-entity behavior, incident category, fault attribution, and impact characterization. The benchmark is designed to evaluate whether models can reason beyond low-level perception and produce structured answers about safety-critical driving scenarios.

我们通过 Kaggle 竞赛进一步建立了公共评估框架，使参与者能够在共享评分协议下开发和比较不同的建模流程。此设置支持可重复的评估，同时鼓励事故分析的多模式推理取得进展。We further establish a public evaluation framework through a Kaggle competition, enabling participants to develop and compare diverse modeling pipelines under a shared scoring protocol. This setting supports reproducible evaluation while encouraging progress on multimodal reasoning for accident analysis.

总的来说，我们的贡献是三重的。首先，我们引入了一个以事故为中心的视觉问答基准，并为安全关键的交通场景提供结构化注释。其次，我们提供一个开放且有竞争力的评估平台，用于跨方法的可重复比较。第三，我们提出的实证结果强调了当前模型在自动驾驶的情境和安全关键推理方面的局限性。Overall, our contributions are threefold. First, we introduce an accident-centric visual question answering benchmark with structured annotations for safety-critical traffic scenes. Second, we provide an open and competitive evaluation platform for reproducible comparison across methods. Third, we present empirical results that highlight the limitations of current models in contextual and safety-critical reasoning for autonomous driving.

2 相关工作Related Work

大规模数据集和标准化基准对于推进自动驾驶系统的计算机视觉至关重要，特别是在感知、跟踪、定位和交通场景理解方面Large-scale datasets and standardized benchmarks have been crucial to advancing computer vision for autonomous driving systems, particularly in perception, tracking, localization, and traffic scene understanding[7]。然而，其中许多任务仅关注日常驾驶条件，而复杂、安全关键环境中的高级推理仍有待探索。此外，理解主体交互、危险行为和事件背景需要超出常见感知框架所提供的能力。. However, many of these tasks focus only on routine driving conditions, while higher-level reasoning in complex, safety-critical environments remains unexplored. Furthermore, understanding agentic interactions, hazardous behaviors, and incident context requires capabilities beyond those provided by common perception frameworks.

最近的工作已经开始探索 VLM 的应用，以通过结构化评估框架驱动特定环境。例如，DrivingVQA 引入了一个视觉问答数据集，旨在评估模型在现实驾驶场景中的推理能力，强调当前模型在多模态推理方面遇到困难，并且严重依赖显着的视觉线索Recent work has begun to explore the application of VLMs to drive specific contexts through structured evaluation frameworks. For example, DrivingVQA introduces a visual question-answering data set designed to evaluate the reasoning of the model in real-world driving scenarios, highlighting that current models struggle with multimodal reasoning and rely heavily on salient visual cues[3]。此外，MetaVQA 提出了评估自动驾驶中的空间推理和场景理解的基准，证明先进模型表现出有限的空间意识和对动态环境的推理困难. Furthermore, MetaVQA proposes a benchmark for evaluating spatial reasoning and scene understanding in autonomous driving, demonstrating that advanced models exhibit limited spatial awareness and difficulty reasoning about dynamic environments[11].

最近的基准（例如 NuPlanQA）提供了大规模评估框架，通过将任务分解为感知、空间推理和决策组件来推动场景理解。这些作品强调，当前的视觉语言模型很难推广到复杂的驾驶环境，特别是对于需要结构化推理和上下文理解的任务More recent benchmarks, such as NuPlanQA, provide large-scale evaluation frameworks to drive scene understanding by decomposing tasks into perception, spatial reasoning, and decision-making components. These works highlight that current vision-language models struggle to generalize to complex driving environments, particularly for tasks requiring structured reasoning and contextual understanding[8].

虽然之前的工作主要集中在模型开发或静态基准评估，但我们的工作开发了一个专注于事故场景的结构化数据集。它通过有竞争力的、开放的基准测试框架来评估绩效。通过利用 Kaggle 竞赛，我们获得了多种建模方法，并更广泛地了解不同方法如何在安全关键的驾驶任务中执行。While prior work primarily focuses on model development or static benchmark evaluation, our work develops a structured dataset focused on accident scenarios. It evaluates performance through a competitive, open benchmarking framework. By leveraging a Kaggle competition, we obtain diverse modeling approaches and gain broader insights into how different methods perform on safety-critical driving tasks.

3 数据集和注释Dataset and Annotation

3.1 源材料Source Material

我们的评估基于 VQA-Autopilot 数据集，该数据集包含 600 多个行车记录仪视频剪辑，捕捉各种驾驶场景，从平安无事的高速公路驾驶到多车碰撞。至关重要的是，该数据集涵盖了完整的事故严重程度：大约 27% 的片段涉及直接碰撞，11% 的险情，17% 提前避免危险的情况，以及 27% 的无事故基线序列（见图 1）。这种平衡的覆盖范围阻止模型仅通过类先验利用来实现高精度。Our evaluation is built on the VQA-Autopilot dataset, which contains over 600 dashcam video clips capturing a wide range of driving scenarios, from uneventful highway driving to multi-vehicle collisions. Critically, the dataset covers the full incident severity spectrum: approximately 27% of clips involve direct collisions, 11% near-misses, 17% situations where a hazard was avoided in advance, and 27% no-incident baseline sequences (see Figure 1). This balanced coverage prevents models from achieving high accuracy through class prior exploitation alone.

3.2 注释模式Annotation Schema

每个视频均由人工注释者在 9 个结构化问题组 (A-I) 中进行标记，总共产生 28 个子问题。这些类别涵盖环境条件（天气、时间、照明）、道路环境（交通环境、道路配置、车道结构、路面、交通管制存在）和事件特征（事件类别、主要和次要实体身份和行为、故障归因、预防措施和影响位置）。总共，这会在数据集中生成 6,000 多个带注释的问答对。Each video was labeled by human annotators across nine structured question groups (A–I), yielding 28 sub-questions in total. The categories span environmental conditions (weather, time of day, lighting), road context (traffic environment, road configuration, lane structure, road surface, traffic control presence), and incident characterization (incident category, primary and secondary entity identity and behavior, fault attribution, prevention measures, and impact location). In total, this produces over 6,000 annotated question-answer pairs across the dataset.

3.3 数据集统计Dataset Statistics

如图 A-F 所示，数据集捕获了所有注释维度的真实多样性。大多数事件发生在白天（70%）晴朗或部分多云（68%）的条件下，反映了行车记录仪的自然部署。高速公路和交叉路口场景合计占数据集的 65%。主要事件实体包括其他车辆（31%）、动物（16%）、自我车辆故障场景（13%）以及弱势道路使用者，包括骑自行车者和行人（18%）。路面主要是干燥的 (83%)，17% 的片段中存在潮湿条件，主要是在雨天序列中。As shown in Figures A–F, the dataset captures realistic diversity across all annotation dimensions. The majority of incidents occur during daytime (70%) under clear or partly cloudy conditions (68%), reflecting naturalistic dashcam deployment. Highway and intersection scenarios together account for 65% of the dataset. Primary incident entities include other vehicles (31%), animals (16%), ego-vehicle fault scenarios (13%), and vulnerable road users, including cyclists and pedestrians (18%). Road surfaces are predominantly dry (83%), with wet conditions present in 17% of clips, predominantly in rainy weather sequences.

4 Kaggle 竞赛及结果Kaggle Competition and Results

4.1 任务定义Task Definition

主办的 Kaggle 竞赛采用行车记录仪视频数据上的结构化视觉问答 (VQA) 任务的形式。在给定的驾驶视频中，描述近乎不雅和事故场景，参与者需要预测一组预定义问题的答案，这些问题捕捉场景的特定方面。The Hosted Kaggle Competition is formatted as a structured Visual Question Answering (VQA) task on dashcam video data. Within a given driving video, depicting both near-indecent and incident scenarios, participants are required to predict answers to a predefined set of questions that capture specific aspects of the scene.

向参与者提出的问题涵盖驾驶情境中的不同维度，包括环境条件（天气和时间）、场景情境（交通环境和道路配置）和事件特定属性（例如，参与方、事件行为和事件原因）。除了所包含的任务外，还有更高级别的推理任务，例如识别潜在的事件预防措施和具体影响点。The questions participants are given span different dimensions within the driving context, including environmental conditions (weather and time), scene context (traffic environment and road configuration), and incident-specific attributes (e.g., involved parties, incident behaviors, and causes of incidents). In addition to the included tasks, there are higher-level reasoning tasks, such as identifying potential incident-prevention measures and specific impact points.

对于每个视频，参与者必须输出一组与预定义答案类别相对应的固定整数值预测。这种结构可以实现提交之间的一致性，并确保对模型性能和理解进行细粒度评估For each video, participants must output a set of fixed, integer-valued predictions corresponding to a predefined answer category. This structure enables consistency across submissions and ensures fine-grained evaluation of model performance and understanding

总体而言，该任务旨在评估以安全为中心的驾驶场景中的感知能力和更高层次的推理，重点是事故理解。Overall, the task is designed to evaluate both perceptual capabilities and higher-level reasoning in safety-centered driving scenarios, with an emphasis on accident understanding.

4.2 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

使用所有视觉问答 (VQA) 领域的平均每个问题准确度来评估提交内容。对于每个问题，准确性计算为测试集中正确预测的比例。Submissions are evaluated using mean per-question accuracy across all visual question answering (VQA) fields. For each question, accuracy is calculated as the proportion of correct predictions across the test set.

4.3 排行榜概览Leaderboard Overview

AUTOPILOT VQA 挑战赛吸引了社区的大力参与，共有 224 名注册参赛者、73 名活跃参与者、59 个团队以及总共 686 份提交作品。这种参与程度表明人们对以安全为中心的多模式推理任务有浓厚的兴趣。The AUTOPILOT VQA challenge attracted strong community participation, with 224 registered entrants, 73 active participants, 59 teams, and 686 total submissions. This level of engagement indicates substantial interest in safety-centered multimodal reasoning tasks.

在公共排行榜阶段结束时，表现最好的团队得分为 0.65835，接下来的两支团队得分分别为 0.65505 和 0.65371。排名靠前的提交内容之间的差距很窄，这表明接近排行榜上限的性能提升越来越难以获得，并且可能需要仔细的优化、集成或更强大的多模态表示。At the close of the public leaderboard phase, the top-performing team achieved a score of 0.65835, with the next two teams scoring 0.65505 and 0.65371 respectively. The narrow margin separating the top submissions suggests that performance gains near the leaderboard ceiling were increasingly difficult to obtain and likely required careful optimization, ensembling, or stronger multimodal representations.

对排行榜进行更广泛的审查揭示了分数的长尾分布。虽然有几个团队的准确率超过了 0.60，但许多提交的准确率都集中在 0.39-0.40 附近，这表明朴素的基线或部分优化的系统很难在整个问题集中泛化。A broader review of the leaderboard reveals a long-tailed distribution of scores. While several teams exceeded 0.60 accuracy, many submissions clustered near 0.39–0.40, indicating that naive baselines or partially optimized systems struggled to generalize across the full question set.

4.4 误差分析Error Analysis

尽管排行榜表现颇具竞争力，但中等的最高分表明许多问题类别仍然具有挑战性。错误可能集中在需要因果推理或细粒度关系推理的任务中，而不是简单的感知中。Despite competitive leaderboard performance, the moderate top score indicates that many question categories remain challenging. Errors are likely concentrated in tasks requiring causal inference or fine-grained relational reasoning rather than simple perception.

例如，仅通过强大的视觉识别就可以解决识别天气、一天中的时间或广泛的交通环境的问题。相反，预测哪个实体可以阻止事件发生、确定与故障相关的行为或估计可能的影响区域需要对多个代理之间的交互进行时间理解和推理。For example, identifying weather, time of day, or broad traffic environment may be solvable through strong visual recognition alone. In contrast, predicting which entity could have prevented the incident, determining fault-relevant behaviors, or estimating likely impact regions requires temporal understanding and reasoning over interactions between multiple agents.

该基准还包括未知和不适用的类，这会带来额外的复杂性。模型不仅必须学会对可见证据进行分类，还必须学会识别何时信息不足或何时问题不适用于场景。The benchmark also includes unknown and non-applicable classes, which introduce additional complexity. Models must learn not only to classify visible evidence, but also to recognize when insufficient information is present or when a question does not apply to the scenario.

4.5 观察到的趋势Observed Trends

排行榜结果显示了一些趋势。首先，竞争激烈的系统与该领域的其他系统之间似乎存在明显的区别，只有少数团队的平均准确度超过 0.60。这表明强大的性能可能取决于先进的模型适应，而不是直接的零样本提示。Several trends emerge from the leaderboard results. First, there appears to be a clear separation between highly competitive systems and the remainder of the field, with only a limited number of teams surpassing 0.60 mean accuracy. This suggests that strong performance likely depends on advanced model adaptation rather than direct zero-shot prompting.

其次，顶级团队之间的密集聚集意味着接近当前绩效极限的回报递减。排行榜的微小改进可能对应于重大的工程工作，这表明即使对于强大的方法，基准仍然具有挑战性。Second, the dense clustering among top teams implies diminishing returns near current performance limits. Small leaderboard improvements may correspond to significant engineering effort, indicating that the benchmark remains challenging even for strong methods.

第三，团队之间的巨大分数差异表明模型选择、预处理策略和结构化输出的处理发挥着重要作用。由于提交需要每个视频进行多个同步预测，因此强大的输出格式和任务分解可能会对最终结果产生重大影响。Third, the wide score variance across teams suggests that model selection, preprocessing strategy, and handling of structured outputs play an important role. Since submissions require multiple synchronized predictions per video, robust output formatting and task decomposition may materially affect final results.

4.6 结论Conclusion

AUTOPILOT VQA 竞赛展示了多模式模型对于自动驾驶理解的前景和当前的局限性。虽然顶级系统实现了有意义的性能，但没有任何方法可以接近人类的可靠性，特别是考虑到任务的安全关键性质。The AUTOPILOT VQA competition demonstrates both the promise and current limitations of multimodal models for autonomous driving understanding. While top systems achieved meaningful performance, no method approached near-human reliability, particularly given the safety-critical nature of the task.

这些结果表明，当前的 VLM 管道在感知方面仍然比在结构化推理方面更强。未来的进展可能需要明确模拟时间因果关系、代理交互、不确定性校准和分层决策的方法。These results suggest that current VLM pipelines remain stronger at perception than at structured reasoning. Future progress may require methods that explicitly model temporal causality, agent interactions, uncertainty calibration, and hierarchical decision-making.

此外，Kaggle 等竞争性基准设置提供了一种宝贵的机制，可以快速评估更广泛的研究社区的各种方法。通过将开放参与与安全相关任务相结合，这些挑战可以加速实现更强大的自动驾驶智能的进展。Additionally, competitive benchmark settings such as Kaggle provide a valuable mechanism for rapidly evaluating diverse approaches from the broader research community. By combining open participation with safety-relevant tasks, such challenges can accelerate progress toward more robust autonomous driving intelligence.
