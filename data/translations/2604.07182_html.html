摘要Abstract — 作为世界上仅次于水的第二大消费饮料，茶不仅是一种文化必需品，而且是一种具有深远规模和影响力的全球经济力量。它不仅仅是一杯饮料，它代表了自然、文化和人类对反思时刻的渴望之间的安静谈判。因此，茶叶病害的精准识别和检测至关重要。为了这个目标，我们评估了几个卷积神经网络（CNN）模型，其中三个模型在 teaLeafBD 数据集上表现出了显着的性能，包括 DenseNet201、MobileNetV2、InceptionV3。 teaLeafBD 数据集包含七个类别，六个疾病类别和一个健康类别，在反映现实世界挑战的各种现场条件下收集。在CNN模型中，DenseNet201取得了最高的测试准确率99%。为了增强模型的可靠性和可解释性，我们实施了梯度加权类激活映射（Grad-CAM）、遮挡敏感性分析和对抗性训练技术来提高模型的抗噪声能力。最后，我们开发了一个原型，以便在现实农业中利用该模型的功能。本文阐述了深度学习模型在现实生活中茶叶病害检测和管理中对疾病进行分类的能力。— As the world's second most-consumed beverage after water, tea is not just a cultural staple but a global economic force of profound scale and influence. More than a mere drink, it represents a quiet negotiation between nature, culture, and the human desire for a moment of reflection. So, the precise identification and detection of tea leaf disease is crucial. With this goal, we have evaluated several Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) models, among them three shows noticeable performance including DenseNet201, MobileNetV2, InceptionV3 on the teaLeafBD dataset. teaLeafBD dataset contains seven classes, six disease classes and one healthy class, collected under various field conditions reflecting real-world challenges. Among the CNN models, DenseNet201 has achieved the highest test accuracy of 99%. In order to enhance the model reliability and interpretability, we have implemented Gradientweighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM), occlusion sensitivity analysis and adversarial training techniques to increase the noise resistance of the model. Finally, we have developed a prototype in order to leverage the model’s capabilities on real life agriculture. This paper illustrates the deep learning model’s capabilities to classify the disease in reallife tea leaf disease detection and management.

一、简介I. INTRODUCTION

茶是世界范围内流行的饮料，也是许多国家的重要经济作物。 [1] 茶园的质量和生产力在很大程度上取决于叶子的健康状况，而叶子很容易受到各种真菌、细菌和虫媒疾病的影响。斑枯病、灰枯病、红锈病等常见病害对茶叶造成严重危害，造成重大经济损失。 [2] 为了有效地进行作物管理，必须尽早准确地识别这些疾病。然而，专家的手动检查是传统疾病检测的基础，这种方法费力、耗时，而且由于人为偏差而常常不可靠。 [3]Tea is a popular beverage worldwide and a significant commercial crop in many nations. [1] Tea plantations' quality and productivity are heavily reliant on the health of their leaves, which are highly open to being affected to a variety of fungal, bacterial, and pest-borne diseases. Common diseases like blister blight, grey blight, and red rust cause serious damage to tea leaves, resulting in significant economic losses. [2] To be effective in crop management, these diseases must be identified early and accurately. However, manual inspection by specialists is the foundation of traditional disease detection, which is labor-intensive, time-consuming, and frequently unreliable due to human bias. [3]

由于具有快速、可靠和自动化诊断的能力，用于植物病害检测的计算机视觉和深度学习技术已成为Due to their capacity to produce rapid, reliable, and automated diagnoses, computer vision and deep learning techniques for plant disease detection have become

近年来越来越受欢迎。在图像分类任务中，卷积神经网络 (CNN) 已被证明表现更好。 [3] CNN 在检测植物病害方面非常成功，因为它们直接从输入图像中提取深层特征，而传统的机器学习技术需要手工制作特征。 [2]increasingly popular in recent years. In image classification tasks, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) have proven to perform better. [3] CNNs are very successful at detecting plant diseases because they directly extract deep features from input images, in contrast to traditional machine-learning techniques that need handcrafted features. [2]

本研究论文旨在开发一种基于 CNN 的茶叶病害检测模型。目标是创建一个自动化系统，能够仅根据视觉特征准确地区分健康茶叶和患病茶叶。 [2] 该研究的目的是为实时茶园监测提供实用且适用的解决方案，以便及早发现病害并帮助农民减少损失。 [1] 该系统可以帮助农民和农业工人及早发现疾病、减少作物损害并促进精准农业实践。 [2]This research paper aims to develop a CNN-based model for detecting tea leaf diseases. The goal is to create an automated system capable of accurately differentiating between healthy and diseased tea leaves based solely on visual characteristics. [2] The study's objective is to provide a practical and applicable solution for real-time tea plantation monitoring, allowing for early disease detection and assisting farmers in reducing losses. [1] This system can assist farmers and agricultural workers in detecting diseases early on, reducing crop damage, and promoting precision agriculture practices. [2]

在这种背景下，我们的研究以两个主要问题为指导：（1）图像处理和基于人工智能的自动化系统在准确识别茶叶病害方面的有效性如何？ （2）早期发现茶叶病害在多大程度上有助于提高生产质量和减少损失？In this context, our research was guided by two primary questions: (1) How the effectiveness of image processing and AI-based automated systems in accurately identifying tea leaf diseases? (2) To what extent early detection of tea leaf diseases contributes to improving production quality and minimizing losses?

B. 预处理B. Preprocessing

所有图像大小均调整为 224×224 像素，以符合预训练模型的输入规格，并标准化为 [0,1] 范围以利于稳定优化。数据集被分为由 70% 训练、20% 验证和 10% 测试组成的子集，确保对以前未见过的样本进行评估。此预处理步骤确保了所有架构的均匀性，并最大限度地减少了图像分辨率和对比度变化的影响。All images were resized to 224×224 pixels to align with the input specifications of the pre-trained models, and were normalized to the range of [0,1] to facilitate stable optimization. The dataset was divided into subsets comprising 70% training, 20% validation, and 10% testing, ensuring that the evaluation was performed on previously unseen samples. This preprocessing step ensured evenness across all architectures and minimized the impact of variations in the image resolution and contrast.

为了评估不同特征提取策略的模型性能，我们对三种成熟的 CNN 架构进行了基准测试。To assess model performance across different feature extraction strategies, we benchmarked three well-established CNN architectures.

  • 盗梦空间V3InceptionV3基于 inception 系列，通过分解卷积并使用多分支模块来提高效率并捕获多尺度特征 [13]。builds on the inception family by factorizing convolutions and using multi-branch modules to improve efficiency and capture multi-scale features [13].

  • 密集网201DenseNet201通过引入密集连接层来扩展 CNN 系列，其中每个层直接接收来自所有先前层的输入，以增强特征重用和梯度流 [14]。extends the CNN family by introducing densely connected layers, where each layer directly receives inputs from all previous layers to enhance feature reuse and gradient flow [14].

选择这些架构是因为它们在叶病成像任务中取得了成功，并且具有互补的设计特征。These architectures were selected for their proven success in leaf disease imaging tasks and complementary design characteristics.

五、可解释的人工智能V. EXPLAINABLE AI

除了准确可靠之外，模型还必须易于理解，才能用于疾病检测应用。为了使深度学习模型不像“黑匣子”，我们使用了一种称为 Grad-CAM [16] 的方法。这有助于我们确定叶子的哪些部分对模型的预测影响最大。In addition to being accurate and reliable, models must be easy to understand for use in disease detection applications. To render the deep learning models less like a "black box" we used a method called Grad-CAM [16]. This helps us determine which parts of the leaf affect the model's predictions the most.

B. 遮挡灵敏度B. Occlusion Sensitivity

为了证明我们的模型易于理解，我们使用了图 10 所示的遮挡敏感性分析。遮挡敏感性分析表明该模型始终依赖于疾病特异性病变区域 [15]。封锁感染区域（例如坏死点、真菌斑块、昆虫进食痕迹和变色）会导致预测置信度显着下降，这证实了模型的决策与每种茶叶疾病的视觉病理学一致。To demonstrate that our model is easy to understand, we used occlusion sensitivity analysis shown in Fig. 10. Occlusion Sensitivity analysis revealed that the model consistently relied on disease-specific lesion regions [15]. Blocking infected areas—such as necrotic spots, fungal patches, insect feeding marks, and discoloration—caused a significant drop in prediction confidence, confirming that the model’s decision-making aligned with the visual pathology of each tea leaf disease.

图 10. 使用遮挡敏感性识别茶叶病害Fig. 10. Tea Leaf Disease identification using Occlusion Sensitivity

Grad-CAM 和遮挡敏感性证明 DenseNet201 的预测既准确又可供农业专家和农民解释。这些可解释性方法突出了茶叶上确切的患病区域，使农民和现场技术人员能够轻松理解模型为何做出特定预测[17]。这种透明度增加了人们对系统的信任，并支持其在现实世界茶叶病害诊断中的实际应用。Grad-CAM and occlusion sensitivity demonstrated that DenseNet201’s predictions were both accurate and interpretable for agricultural experts and farmers. These explainability methods highlighted the exact diseased regions on tea leaves, enabling farmers and field technicians to easily understand why the model made a particular prediction [17]. This transparency increases trust in the system and supports its practical use for real-world tea leaf disease diagnosis.

C. 局限性和未来工作的讨论C. Discussion of Limitations and Future Work
