摘要Abstract

领域专业化可以改善垂直领域的LLM行为，但往往会削弱从原始模型继承的一般能力。最近的多教师按策略蒸馏 (MOPD) 管道通过使用教师反馈监督学生生成的轨迹来恢复模型功能，但通常假设教师对齐的提示覆盖范围，要求提示与教师的培训分布相匹配。当一般教师是训练后数据未知的开源模型时，这个假设很难满足。我们没有尝试重建这种隐藏的分布，而是使用现成的代理一般提示来研究一般能力恢复。我们在这种不完全覆盖的情况下确定了普通 MOPD 的两种失效模式：混合冲突的恢复和保存梯度产生的恢复-保存抵消作用，以及来自具有不等校正需求的均匀平均样本的弱信号平坦化。我们建议Domain specialization can improve LLM behavior in vertical domains, but often weakens the general capabilities inherited from the original model. Recent Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD) pipelines recover model capabilities by supervising student-generated trajectories with teacher feedback, but typically assume teacher-aligned prompt coverage, requiring prompts to match the teachers’ training distributions. This assumption is difficult to satisfy when the general teacher is an open-source model whose post-training data are unknown. Instead of attempting to reconstruct this hidden distribution, we study general capability recovery with readily available proxy general prompts. We identify two failure modes of vanilla MOPD in this incomplete-coverage situation: recovery-preservation counteraction from mixing conflicting recovery and preservation gradients, and weak-signal flattening from uniformly averaging samples with unequal correction demand. We propose具有对抗意识的多教师策略蒸馏Counteraction-Aware Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (钙MOPDCaMOPD），它通过解耦交替训练和基于间隙的样本选择来解决这些问题。 CaMOPD 提供一般恢复专用更新，定期检查域提示以进行保存，并选择具有较大平均标记级师生对数概率差距的样本来集中校正信号。在角色扮演对话和医学推理 QA 场景中，CaMOPD 在基线的总体恢复方面表现最佳，同时保持特定领域的行为。梯度相干性分析进一步支持 CaMOPD 在产生更多相干校正信号方面的预期效果。), which addresses these issues with decoupled alternating training and gap-based sample selection. CaMOPD gives general recovery dedicated updates, periodically reviews domain prompts for preservation, and selects samples with larger averaged token-level teacher-student log-probability gaps to concentrate correction signals. Across role-play dialogue and medical reasoning QA scenarios, CaMOPD performs best in general recovery over baselines while maintaining domain-specific behavior. Gradient coherence analyses further support the intended effect of CaMOPD in producing more coherent correction signals.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）的巨大成功加速了它们在角色扮演等垂直领域的部署The remarkable success of large language models (LLMs) has accelerated their deployment across vertical domains, from role-play(Wang et al., 2025)临床决策支持to clinical decision support(Baichuan-M3 Team, 2026)。然而，领域专业化面临着一个根本性的悖论：专业化过程本身往往会削弱最初使这些模型有价值的通用能力，从而产生了能力保留的需求. However, domain specialization faces a fundamental paradox: the process of specialization itself often weakens the general capabilities that made these models valuable in the first place, creating a need for capability preservation(McCloskey and Cohen, 1989; Kumar et al., 2022)。经典的缓解策略混合通用领域数据或使用重放机制来减少遗忘. Classical mitigation strategies mix general-domain data or use replay mechanisms to reduce forgetting(Lopez-Paz and Ranzato, 2017; Rolnick et al., 2019; Dong et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024)。然而，这些方法严重依赖大量高质量的原始数据，并给优化训练计划带来了挑战。. However, these approaches heavily rely on massive amounts of high-quality original data and introduce challenges in optimizing training schedules.

最近，多教师同策略蒸馏（MOPD）作为一种通过使用令牌级教师反馈来监督学生生成的轨迹来将多种能力恢复到单个学生模型中的方法而引起了人们的关注Recently, multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) has attracted attention as a way to recover diverse capabilities into a single student model by supervising student-generated trajectories with token-level teacher feedback(Yang et al., 2026; GLM-5 Team, 2026)。然而，现有的 MOPD 管道通常假设教师对齐的提示覆盖范围：MOPD 期间使用的提示应充分匹配产生教师的数据分布. However, existing MOPD pipelines often assume teacher-aligned prompt coverage: the prompts used during MOPD should sufficiently match the data distribution that produced the teachers(LLM-Core Xiaomi, 2026; Baichuan-M3 Team, 2026)。这一前提给数据收集带来了沉重的负担，并且当教师原始的培训后数据不可用时，可能会昂贵、耗时，甚至不可能。. This premise places a heavy burden on data collection and can be expensive, time-consuming, or impossible when teacher’s original post-training data are unavailable.

为了避免过度依赖昂贵的教师原始数据收集，我们考虑使用代理提示来恢复一般能力。具体来说，我们从开源通用模型开始，将其后训练为垂直领域模型，然后应用MOPD来恢复其失去的通用能力：原始开源模型充当通用教师，专业模型既充当学生初始化又充当领域教师。在这种情况下，领域提示可以从专业化管道中获得，而普通教师的原始训练后数据是未知的。我们没有尝试重建或覆盖这个隐藏的分布，而是使用现成的公共通用域提示作为To avoid over-reliance on costly collection of the teacher’s original data, we consider using proxy prompts to recover general capability. Specifically, starting from an open-source general model, we post-train it into a vertical-domain model, after which MOPD is applied to recover its lost general capability: the original open-source model serves as the general teacher, and the specialized model serves as both the student initialization and the domain teacher. In this situation, domain prompts are available from the specialization pipeline, while the general teacher’s original post-training data are unknown. 2 相关工作Related work

2.1 合规蒸馏On-Policy Distillation

在策略蒸馏 (OPD) 向老师询问学生生成的输出，使其自然适合模型生成轨迹的 LLM 后期培训On-policy distillation (OPD) queries a teacher on student-generated outputs, making it a natural fit for LLM post-training on model-generated trajectories(Agarwal et al., 2024; Gu et al., 2024; Qwen Team, 2025; DeepSeek-AI, 2026)。然而，OPD 信号在不同轨迹或标记上的作用并不相同。其中一项工作研究信号不平衡、师生兼容性以及嘈杂或低一致性的教师反馈，表明 OPD 可能会引入不可靠的更新方向. However, OPD signals are not uniformly useful across trajectories or tokens. One line of work studies signal imbalance, teacher-student compatibility, and noisy or low-coherence teacher feedback, showing that OPD can introduce unreliable update directions(Li et al., 2026; Fu et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2025, 2026)。另一条路线通过在代币或样本级别校准、放松、选择或重新加权本地监督来改进 OPD. Another line improves OPD by calibrating, relaxing, selecting, or reweighting local supervision at the token or sample level(Zheng et al., 2026; Ko et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026; Kim and Baek, 2026; Huang et al., 2025a; Xie et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2026)。与这些细化局部信号的方法不同，CaMOPD 引入了基于间隙的样本选择，以显着提高跨样本梯度相干性。. Unlike these methods that refine local signals, CaMOPD introduces gap-based sample selection to explicitly improve cross-sample gradient coherence.

2.2 多教师按策略蒸馏Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation

多教师 OPD (MOPD) 通过使用多位教师作为令牌级别的指导来源来扩展 OPD，并已在最近的几个 LLM 后期培训管道中使用。 MiMo-V2-Flash 和 DeepSeek-V4 使用 MOPD 式蒸馏进行基础模型后训练和广泛的功能集成Multi-teacher OPD (MOPD) extends OPD by using multiple teachers as token-level guidance sources, and has been used in several recent LLM post-training pipelines. MiMo-V2-Flash and DeepSeek-V4 use MOPD-style distillation for base-model post-training and broad capability integration(LLM-Core Xiaomi, 2026; DeepSeek-AI, 2026)。 Baichuan-M3、Nemotron-Cascade 2和GLM-5将相关思想应用于垂直域或分阶段后训练，后两者使用跨阶段蒸馏来整合或恢复在训练阶段退化的能力. Baichuan-M3, Nemotron-Cascade 2, and GLM-5 apply related ideas to vertical-domain or staged post-training, with the latter two using cross-stage distillation to integrate or recover capabilities that degrade across training stages(Baichuan-M3 Team, 2026; Yang et al., 2026; GLM-5 Team, 2026)。与这些假设完全对齐的教师数据的管道不同，CaMOPD 针对具有代理提示的不完全覆盖设置，专注于减少跨域梯度冲突并提高信号一致性。. Unlike these pipelines that assume fully aligned teacher data, CaMOPD targets an incomplete-coverage setting with proxy prompts, focusing on reducing cross-domain gradient conflicts and elevating signal coherence.

2.3 减轻灾难性遗忘Mitigating Catastrophic Forgetting

专业化过程中的能力退化与持续适应过程中的灾难性遗忘密切相关Capability degradation during specialization is closely related to catastrophic forgetting in continual adaptation(Luo et al., 2023)。现有的缓解策略通常将域数据与通用域或预训练数据混合在一起. Existing mitigation strategies commonly mix domain data with general-domain or pretraining data(Dong et al., 2024; Béthune et al., 2025)，或使用重播机制重新访问先前任务或综合示例, or use replay mechanisms to revisit prior-task or synthesized examples(Lopez-Paz and Ranzato, 2017; Rolnick et al., 2019; Lesort et al., 2020; Huang et al., 2024)。受重放的启发，CaMOPD 安排恢复和保存更新以减轻梯度反作用。. Inspired by replay, CaMOPD schedules recovery and preservation updates to mitigate gradient counteraction.

5 结论Conclusion

我们研究了在不完全覆盖设置下领域专业化后的一般能力恢复，其中只有代理一般提示可供一般教师使用。我们确定了 Vanilla MOPD 的两种故障模式：恢复-保存抵消和弱信号平坦化。为了解决这些问题，我们提出了 CaMOPD，它将交替恢复/保存更新与基于间隙的样本选择相结合。角色扮演对话和医学推理 QA 实验表明，CaMOPD 可以在保持领域能力的同时改善基线的总体恢复。梯度和选择分析进一步表明 CaMOPD 减少了混合更新抵消并产生更相干的校正信号。We studied general capability recovery after domain specialization under an incomplete-coverage setting, where only proxy general prompts are available for the general teacher. We identified two failure modes of Vanilla MOPD: recovery-preservation counteraction and weak-signal flattening. To address them, we proposed CaMOPD, which combines alternating recovery/preservation updates with gap-based sample selection. Experiments on role-play dialogue and medical reasoning QA show that CaMOPD improves general recovery over baselines while maintaining domain capability. Gradient and selection analyses further suggest that CaMOPD reduces mixed-update counteraction and produces more coherent correction signals.

局限性Limitations

我们的实验分别在角色扮演和医学推理 QA 领域用 4B 和 8B 模型实例化 CaMOPD。由于计算限制，我们在这项工作中不评估更大的模型规模；将CaMOPD扩展到更大的专业模型是未来探索的重要方向。此外，我们使用固定的 32K 响应长度预算来进行策略内部署。选择此设置是为了避免在蒸馏过程中人为限制长推理模型的输出能力，但它没有研究不同的推出长度预算如何影响恢复-保存权衡。未来的工作可以在更短和适应性的推出预算下检查 CaMOPD。Our experiments instantiate CaMOPD with 4B and 8B models in the role-play and medical reasoning QA domains, respectively. Due to computational constraints, we do not evaluate larger model scales in this work; extending CaMOPD to larger specialized models is an important direction for future exploration. In addition, we use a fixed 32K response-length budget for on-policy rollouts. This setting is chosen to avoid artificially limiting the output capacity of long-reasoning models during distillation, but it does not study how different rollout length budgets affect the recovery-preservation trade-off. Future work can examine CaMOPD under shorter and adaptive rollout budgets.

Ethical Considerations

这项工作研究了恢复一般能力同时保留特定领域行为的训练后方法。在医学推理QA设置中，模型和评估仅用于医学问答和教育推理基准的研究。由此产生的系统不应被解释为提供专业的医疗建议、诊断或治疗建议。在临床或面向患者的环境中的任何部署都需要更强大的验证、专家监督、安全护栏以及遵守适用的医疗法规。我们的培训和评估使用公开的数据集和开源模型。我们不使用私人患者记录或个人身份医疗信息。This work studies post-training methods for recovering general capabilities while preserving domain-specific behavior. In the medical reasoning QA setting, the models and evaluations are used only for research on medical question answering and educational reasoning benchmarks. The resulting systems should not be interpreted as providing professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Any deployment in clinical or patient-facing settings would require substantially stronger validation, expert oversight, safety guardrails, and compliance with applicable medical regulations. Our training and evaluation use publicly available datasets and open-source models. We do not use private patient records or personally identifiable medical information.
