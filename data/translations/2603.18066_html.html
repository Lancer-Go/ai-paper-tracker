预测编码网络的可综合 RTL 实现A Synthesizable RTL Implementation of Predictive Coding Networks

Timothy Oh
University of California, Riverside
（2025 年 8 月）(August 2025)
摘要Abstract

反向传播使现代深度学习成为可能，但由于全局错误传播、相分离和对集中式内存的严重依赖，很难实现作为在线、完全分布式硬件学习系统。预测编码提供了一种替代方案，其中推理和学习源自相邻层之间的局部预测误差动态。本文提出了一种直接在硬件中实现离散时间预测编码更新的数字架构。每个神经核心都维护自己的活动、预测误差和突触权重，并且仅通过硬连线连接与相邻层进行通信。通过统一的每神经元钳位原语支持监督学习和推理，该钳位原语强制执行边界条件，同时保持内部更新时间表不变。该设计是一个确定性、可综合的 RTL 基板，围绕顺序 MAC 数据路径和固定的有限状态调度构建。该系统不是在学习基底内执行特定于任务的指令序列，而是在固定的本地更新规则下演化，并通过连接性、参数和边界条件强加任务结构。这项工作的贡献不是新的学习规则，而是直接在硬件中执行预测编码学习动态的完整可合成数字基底。Backpropagation has enabled modern deep learning but is difficult to realize as an online, fully distributed hardware learning system due to global error propagation, phase separation, and heavy reliance on centralized memory. Predictive coding offers an alternative in which inference and learning arise from local prediction-error dynamics between adjacent layers. This paper presents a digital architecture that implements a discrete-time predictive coding update directly in hardware. Each neural core maintains its own activity, prediction error, and synaptic weights, and communicates only with adjacent layers through hardwired connections. Supervised learning and inference are supported via a uniform per-neuron clamping primitive that enforces boundary conditions while leaving the internal update schedule unchanged. The design is a deterministic, synthesizable RTL substrate built around a sequential MAC datapath and a fixed finite-state schedule. Rather than executing a task-specific instruction sequence inside the learning substrate, the system evolves under fixed local update rules, with task structure imposed through connectivity, parameters, and boundary conditions. The contribution of this work is not a new learning rule, but a complete synthesizable digital substrate that executes predictive-coding learning dynamics directly in hardware.

1 简介Introduction

现代机器学习系统通常通过反向传播进行训练，反向传播通过将全局损失信息与紧密协调的前向/后向计算计划相结合来计算梯度。尽管非常有效，但这种范例很难作为硬件中的完全分布式学习基础来实现。向后传递需要结构化的全局错误传播、中间激活存储以及通过内存和互连的大量数据移动。Modern machine learning systems are typically trained by backpropagation, which computes gradients by combining global loss information with a tightly coordinated forward/backward computation schedule. Although highly effective, this paradigm is difficult to realize as a fully distributed learning substrate in hardware. The backward pass requires structured global error propagation, intermediate activation storage, and substantial movement of data through memory and interconnect.

预测编码提供了一种替代方案，其中推理和学习是通过最小化层次结构中的预测误差而产生的Predictive coding offers an alternative formulation in which inference and learning arise from minimizing prediction errors across a hierarchyRao and Ballard (1999); Friston (2005)。在标准预测编码网络（PCN）中，每一层都会预测下面的层；每个单元仅使用本地可用的量来更新其状态和突触权重：其自身的活动、其自身的预测误差、来自上方相邻层的突触前活动以及来自下方相邻层的预测误差。这种局部性使得预测编码作为物理嵌入式学习系统的候选算法基础具有吸引力。. In standard predictive coding networks (PCNs), each layer predicts the layer below; each unit updates its state and synaptic weights using only locally available quantities: its own activity, its own prediction error, presynaptic activity from the adjacent layer above, and prediction errors from the adjacent layer below. This locality makes predictive coding attractive as a candidate algorithmic substrate for physically embedded learning systems.

本文提出了一种数字微架构，可以在单个神经元级别直接实现预测编码方程。每个This paper presents a digital micro-architecture that directly implements predictive coding equations at the level of individual neurons. Each神经核心neural core对应于单个标量单元并在每个时钟周期执行固定的有限状态调度。相邻层之间的通信严格通过硬连线连接进行。不需要共享参数存储器和全局学习阶段控制器。这项工作的目的不是提出新的学习规则，而是展示从预测编码式本地学习到结构化、可综合的数字基础的具体映射。corresponds to a single scalar unit and executes a fixed finite-state schedule per tick. Communication is strictly between adjacent layers via hardwired connections. No shared parameter memory and no global learning-phase controller are required. The objective of this work is not to propose a new learning rule, but to demonstrate a concrete mapping from predictive-coding-style local learning to a structured, synthesizable digital substrate.

2 相关工作Related Work

神经形态和本地学习硬件。Neuromorphic and local-learning hardware.

大量工作都针对使用尖峰神经网络 (SNN) 的受大脑启发的硬件。 Intel的Loihi、IBM的TrueNorth、SpiNNaker和BrainScaleS等平台实现了片上本地化 通过脉冲时间依赖性可塑性（STDP）和相关赫布规则进行学习A substantial body of work has targeted brain-inspired hardware using spiking neural networks (SNNs). Platforms such as Intel’s Loihi, IBM’s TrueNorth, SpiNNaker, and BrainScaleS implement on-chip local learning through spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP) and related Hebbian rulesDavies et al. (2018); Furber et al. (2014); Schmitt et al. (2017)。 这些系统通过利用事件驱动的异步计算实现了令人印象深刻的能源效率 具有稀疏二进制尖峰通信。. These systems achieve impressive energy efficiency by exploiting event-driven, asynchronous computation with sparse binary spike communication.

目前的工作在这个设计空间中沿着三个轴占据了不同的点。 首先，神经表示是连续值的而不是基于尖峰的：每个核心都维护一个 在局部梯度动力学下连续演化的标量浮点状态，而不是 由离散事件驱动的积分和激发膜电位。 其次，学习规则是从二次能量导出的赫布预测误差更新 函数，而不是 STDP；权重更新取决于突触后预测误差和突触前预测误差 活动，而不是峰值时间巧合。 第三，底层是针对标准数字合成的同步确定性RTL设计 流，而不是异步网格或模拟混合信号结构。 这些选择优先考虑预测编码更新之间的直接、可验证的对应关系 方程和硬件数据路径——以事件驱动的能效增益为代价 尖峰系统提供。 预测编码动力学和尖峰事件驱动计算是否可以有效地结合起来 这是未来工作的一个悬而未决的问题。The present work occupies a different point in this design space along three axes. First, the neural representation is continuous-valued rather than spike-based: each core maintains a scalar floating-point state that evolves continuously under local gradient dynamics, not an integrate-and-fire membrane potential driven by discrete events. Second, the learning rule is a Hebbian prediction-error update derived from a quadratic energy function, not STDP; the weight update depends on the postsynaptic prediction error and presynaptic activity, not on spike-timing coincidence. Third, the substrate is a synchronous deterministic RTL design targeting standard digital synthesis flows, rather than an asynchronous mesh or analog mixed-signal fabric. These choices prioritize a direct, verifiable correspondence between the predictive-coding update equations and the hardware datapath—at the cost of the energy efficiency gains that event-driven spiking systems provide. Whether predictive-coding dynamics and spiking event-driven computation can be usefully combined is an open question for future work.

预测编码作为分层推理。Predictive coding as hierarchical inference.

预测编码已被开发为皮层计算的功能帐户，其中感知和学习是通过最小化分层生成模型中的预测误差而产生的Predictive coding has been developed as a functional account of cortical computation in which perception and learning arise from minimizing prediction error in hierarchical generative modelsRao and Ballard (1999); Friston (2005)。自由能原理提供了更广泛的解释框架，其中系统通过最小化变分自由能来维护自身，激励预测编码作为具体的算法实例化. The free energy principle provides a broader interpretive framework in which systems maintain themselves by minimizing variational free energy, motivating predictive coding as a concrete algorithmic instantiationFriston (2009).

与反向传播的关系。Relationship to backpropagation.

预测编码已被分析为在特定建模假设下实现类似反向传播学习规则的潜在途径。特别是，Whittington 和 Bogacz 表明，在具有局部赫布可塑性的预测编码网络中，钳位输出下的收敛推理会产生近似反向传播梯度的误差信号Predictive coding has been analyzed as a potential route to backpropagation-like learning rules under specific modeling assumptions. In particular, Whittington and Bogacz show that in a predictive coding network with local Hebbian plasticity, converged inference under clamped outputs yields error signals that approximate backpropagation gradientsWhittington and Bogacz (2017)。这种联系激发了预测编码实现作为本地学习基础的候选者，同时也澄清了预期与反向传播等效的条件。. This connection motivates predictive-coding implementations as candidates for local learning substrates, while also clarifying the conditions under which equivalence to backpropagation is expected.

当前的架构在基于滴答的增量机制中运行，而不是假设参数更新之间的推理完全收敛。萨尔瓦托里等人。为 PCN 提出稳定且全自动的学习算法并分析增量预测编码变体The present architecture operates in a tick-based incremental regime rather than assuming full convergence of inference between parameter updates. These works motivate incremental schedules, but do not directly guarantee stability for a specific discretization and finite-precision hardware datapath.

反向传播是生物和硬件不匹配的结果。Backpropagation as a biological and hardware mismatch.

Lillicrap 等人总结了对反向传播与神经系统之间的生物学合理性和机制不匹配的担忧。Concerns about biological plausibility and mechanistic mismatch between backpropagation and neural systems are summarized by Lillicrap et al.Lillicrap et al. These critiques motivate alternatives that reduce global coordination and enforce locality.

3 反向传播及其约束Backpropagation and Its Constraints

反向传播需要全局协调，这对于与分布式生物学习和某些类别的嵌入式硬件系统协调具有挑战性Backpropagation requires global coordination that is challenging to reconcile with distributed biological learning and with certain classes of embedded hardware systemsLillicrap et al. (2020)。首先，标准梯度计算需要在整个网络中向后传播误差信息，从而创建一个不纯粹本地的依赖结构。其次，训练通常被组织成不同的阶段（前向、后向、更新），需要中间激活的同步和存储。第三，反向传播假设计算图的可微性，而生物系统涉及不连续和随机信号. First, standard gradient computation requires propagating error information backward through the entire network, creating a dependency structure that is not purely local. Second, training is typically organized into distinct phases (forward, backward, update) that demand synchronization and storage of intermediate activations. Third, backpropagation assumes differentiability of the computational graph, whereas biological systems involve discontinuous and stochastic signalingRumelhart et al. (1986)。虽然这些问题并不妨碍反向传播在传统加速器上实现，但它们激发了对替代学习公式的研究，这些公式允许适合嵌入式在线适应的本地更新结构。. While these issues do not prevent backpropagation from being implemented on conventional accelerators, they motivate research into alternative learning formulations that admit local update structure suitable for embedded online adaptation.

6.2 顺序数据路径Sequential datapath

每个核心在预测和权重更新阶段迭代突触前指数。因此，每个刻度的周期计数与扇入呈线性关系。这种设计选择以吞吐量换取减小的面积和统一的每核实现。Each core iterates across presynaptic indices during prediction and weight update stages. 7 稳定性和收敛性考虑因素Stability and Convergence Considerations

预测编码通常表示为连续时间内预测误差能量的梯度下降Predictive coding is often presented as gradient descent on a prediction-error energy in continuous timeFriston (2005)。这里描述的硬件实现了具有有限精度的离散时间变体、固定的每滴答计算时间表、可选的钳位以及同时的活动和权重更新。这些偏差很重要：理想化连续时间动力学的稳定性属性不会自动转移到浮点算术中基于刻度的显式欧拉离散化。. The hardware described here implements a discrete-time variant with finite precision, a fixed per-tick computation schedule, optional clamping, and simultaneous activity and weight updates. These departures matter: stability properties of idealized continuous-time dynamics do not automatically transfer to a tick-based explicit Euler discretization in floating-point arithmetic.

因此，我们将这项工作中的稳定性视为一种经验属性，将其描述为步长、初始化、激活选择和滴答预算的函数。Accordingly, we treat stability in this work as an empirical property to be characterized as a function of step sizes, initialization, activation choice, and tick budget.

9 讨论：走向嵌入式自适应计算Discussion: Toward Embedded Adaptive Computation

所提出的系统是由一个实际的硬件问题激发的：当学习被实现为局部动态过程而不是作为在前向评估、后向梯度传播和存储在集中式存储器中的参数更新之间交替的过程时，会发生什么变化。预测编码在这种情况下很有吸引力，因为它的更新规则分解为严格的局部术语，允许每个单元仅使用相邻层信号来更新其状态和突触。由此产生的基底更像是分布式物理过程，而不是由集中控制器执行的传统程序。The proposed system is motivated by a practical hardware question: what changes when learning is implemented as a local dynamical process rather than as a procedure that alternates between forward evaluation, backward gradient propagation, and parameter updates stored in centralized memory. Predictive coding is attractive in this context because its update rules decompose into strictly local terms, allowing each unit to update its state and synapses using only adjacent-layer signals. The resulting substrate resembles a distributed physical process more than a conventional program executed by a centralized controller.

这种观点在系统中表达任务结构的地方发生了变化。在传统的冯诺依曼设置中，任务行为通常主要通过在固定基板上执行的外部编写的软件来指定。在当前的架构中，行为是由输入、局部状态动态、局部可塑性和外部施加的边界条件之间的相互作用更直接地塑造的。This perspective shifts where task structure is expressed in the system. In a conventional von Neumann setting, task behavior is typically specified primarily through externally authored software executed on a fixed substrate. In the present architecture, behavior is shaped more directly by the interaction between inputs, local state dynamics, local plasticity, and externally imposed boundary conditions.

9.1 与凡人计算的关系Relationship to Mortal Computation

Hinton 引入了凡人计算的概念来描述算法和硬件密不可分的系统：因为每个物理实例都有未知的模拟变化，所以训练后的参数仅对特定的硬件有用，并随之消亡Hinton introduces the concept of mortal computation to describe systems in which algorithm and hardware are inseparable: because each physical instance has unknown analog variations, the trained parameters are useful only for that particular piece of hardware and die with itHinton (2022)。目前的工作比传统的不朽计算更接近设计空间的这一端。预测编码更新规则不是在通用基底上执行的软件——它们直接实例化为硬件数据路径本身。 FSM调度、MAC累加、本地状态寄存器、权重更新逻辑就是流程；硬件和软件之间没有分离。 这个框架是描述性的，而不是基础性的。其价值在于强调固定的局部动态基底如何支持推理和适应，而无需单独的编程学习阶段——这是它与凡人计算视角共享的结构属性，尽管当前的设计使用同步数字逻辑而不是模拟电路。进一步将其扩展到真正的模拟凡人计算（接受特定于硬件的参数保真度以换取能源效率）是否将使预测编码基底受益，这是未来工作的一个悬而未决的问题。. The present work sits closer to this end of the design space than to conventional immortal computation. The predictive-coding update rules are not software executing on a general-purpose substrate — they are directly instantiated as the hardware datapath itself. The FSM schedule, the MAC accumulation, the local state registers, and the weight update logic are the process; there is no separation between hardware and software. This framing is descriptive rather than foundational. The value is to highlight how a fixed local dynamical substrate can support both inference and adaptation without separate programmed learning phases — a structural property it shares with the mortal-computation perspective, even though the present design uses synchronous digital logic rather than analog circuits. Whether extending this further toward true analog mortal computation — accepting hardware-specific parameter fidelity in exchange for energy efficiency — would benefit predictive-coding substrates is an open question for future work.

9.2 与莫拉维克悖论的关系Relationship to Moravec’s Paradox

莫拉维克悖论强调了符号操作和感觉运动推理之间的不对称性，符号操作干净地映射到顺序数字计算，感觉运动推理在生物系统中很强大，但对传统架构来说具有挑战性Moravec’s Paradox highlights an asymmetry between symbolic manipulation, which maps cleanly onto sequential digital computation, and sensorimotor inference, which is robust in biological systems but challenging for conventional architecturesMoravec (1988)。目前的工作并没有声称要解决这个悖论。相反，它为测试相关假设提供了具体的基础：感知和控制的某些方面可以更好地实现为分布式动态系统中的快速局部推理，而不是作为由全局记忆介导的显式符号计算。. The present work does not claim to resolve this paradox. Rather, it provides a concrete substrate for testing a related hypothesis: some aspects of perception and control may be better realized as fast local inference in distributed dynamical systems than as explicit symbolic computation mediated by global memory.

由于所提出的架构在固定更新规则下执行持续的局部错误最小化，因此它可能非常适合在约束下自然表达为推理的任务，特别是在噪声、流式传输或部分观察的设置中。证实这一假设需要在匹配的计算和能源预算下与传统数字基线进行实证比较。Because the proposed architecture performs continual local error minimization under fixed update rules, it may be well suited to tasks naturally expressed as inference under constraints, particularly in noisy, streaming, or partially observed settings. Substantiating this hypothesis requires empirical comparisons to conventional digital baselines under matched compute and energy budgets.

9.3 局限性和未来方向Limitations and future directions

当前的设计存在一些限制。首先，该架构重用了一个顺序浮点数据路径，该数据路径迭代突触前索引，减少了每个神经元的面积，但随着扇入的增长而增加了滴答延迟。其次，非线性激活及其导数的可综合实现需要仔细的数值设计。第三，连续时间分析并不暗示耦合离散时间、有限精度、钳位系统的收敛性和稳定性保证；应根据经验绘制稳定区域，并在可能的情况下进行理论分析。Several limitations follow from the current design. First, the architecture reuses a sequential floating-point datapath that iterates over presynaptic indices, reducing area per neuron but increasing tick latency as fan-in grows. Second, synthesizable implementations of nonlinear activations and their derivatives require careful numerical design. Third, convergence and stability guarantees for the coupled discrete-time, finite-precision, clamped system are not implied by continuous-time analyses; stability regions should be mapped empirically and, where possible, analyzed theoretically.

这些限制激发了未来在架构扩展（并行性与面积/功率）、适合综合的激活近似以及识别本地在线推理有益的机制的任务驱动基准方面的工作。These limitations motivate future work on architectural scaling (parallelism vs area/power), activation approximations suitable for synthesis, and task-driven benchmarks that identify regimes where local online inference is beneficial.
