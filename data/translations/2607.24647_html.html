摘要Abstract

人工智能驱动的自主研究 (AR) 系统在广泛的任务中变得越来越有效。然而，他们的表现仍然主要根据最终结果的质量来评估。在本文中，我们认为解决方案搜索过程的效率是一个同样重要但经常被忽视的绩效维度。强大的 AR 系统不仅应该产生高质量的结果，而且还应该以尽可能小的预算实现这些结果。 随着 AR 从数学和编码等验证成本低廉的领域扩展到解决方案评估可能需要昂贵的物理实验的现实科学环境，搜索效率将变得越来越重要。为了捕捉这个维度，我们建议使用帕累托前沿的曲线下面积 (AUC) 以及最终结果质量来评估 AR 系统。 我们比较了十二个系统优化任务中的几个搜索算法系列，包括爬山、束搜索、树搜索和进化搜索。我们发现没有一个搜索结构始终是最有效的。我们还表明，搜索效率和最终结果质量是不同的性能维度：最终实现最佳结果的方法可能会缓慢改进，并在达到该结果之前消耗更多的评估预算。因为最有效的搜索策略通常是事先未知的，所以我们引入了一种称为自适应过程AI-driven autonomous research (AR) systems are becoming increasingly effective across a broad range of tasks. Their performance, however, is still evaluated primarily by the quality of the final outcome. In this paper, we argue that the efficiency of the solution-search process is an equally important but often overlooked dimension of performance. A strong AR system should not only produce high-quality results, but also reach them with as small a budget as possible. Search efficiency will become increasingly important as AR expands from domains with inexpensive verification, such as mathematics and coding, to real-world scientific settings in which solution evaluation may require costly physical experiments. To capture this dimension, we propose evaluating AR systems using the area under the curve (AUC) of the Pareto frontier, alongside final outcome quality. We compare several families of search algorithms, including hill climbing, beam search, tree search, and evolutionary search, across twelve systems-optimization tasks. We find that no single search structure is consistently the most efficient. We also show that search efficiency and final outcome quality are distinct performance dimensions: a method that eventually achieves the best result may nevertheless improve slowly and consume substantially more evaluation budget before reaching that result. Because the most effective search policy is generally unknown in advance, we introduce an adaptive procedure called流体fluid搜索，它使用组合老虎机在搜索过程的森林中动态分配固定的评估预算。在评估的任务中，search, which uses a portfolio bandit to dynamically allocate a fixed evaluation budget across a forest of search processes. Across the evaluated tasks,流体fluidsearch 实现了最高的整体搜索效率，与预先为每个任务给出最佳搜索结构的每任务预言机的性能非常匹配。search achieves the highest overall search efficiency, closely matching the performance of a per-task oracle that is given the best search structure for each task in advance.

1  简介1  Introduction

人工智能驱动的自主研究 (AR) 系统正在成为解决数学、算法设计、软件工程、材料发现以及其他科学和工程领域难题的越来越有前途的方法AI-driven autonomous research (AR) systems are becoming an increasingly promising approach to solving difficult problems across mathematics, algorithm design, software engineering, materials discovery, and other scientific and engineering domains[16]。通过反复提出假设或候选解决方案，对其进行评估，并使用由此产生的反馈来指导后续决策，这些系统可以在有限的人为干预下进行越来越长且复杂的研究过程。现有的 AR 系统通常主要根据它们发现的最佳最终解决方案的质量进行评估。这种基于结果的评估是自然的但不完整的。一个强大的 AR 系统不仅应该找到强大的解决方案，而且应该在合理的预算内找到它们。一个只有经过多年的计算或数十亿美元的实验才能最终发现优秀解决方案的系统，无论其最终结果的质量如何，其实用价值都是有限的。. By repeatedly proposing hypotheses or candidate solutions, evaluating them, and using the resulting feedback to guide subsequent decisions, these systems can conduct increasingly long and complex research processes with limited human intervention. Existing AR systems are typically evaluated primarily by the quality of the best final solution they discover. This outcome-based evaluation is natural but incomplete. A capable AR system should not only find strong solutions, but find them within a reasonable budget. A system that eventually discovers an excellent solution only after years of computation or billions of dollars in experiments would be of limited practical value, regardless of the quality of its final result.

Evaluating only the best solution found conflates these two dimensions and ignores the search trajectory by which that solution was reached. Accordingly, we propose evaluating the full performance curve over the available budget, using the area under the curve (AUC) of the Pareto frontier as a measure of research efficiency. A smart AR system should not only find outstanding solutions, but also develop strong intuitions and find them early. This broader view will become increasingly important as AR moves beyond cheaply verifiable tasks such as mathematics, coding, and algorithm optimization toward discovery in the physical world. In many scientific and engineering settings, evaluating a candidate may require laboratory experiments, physical fabrication, clinical studies, or other costly interventions. The structure of the problem may also be only partially known or incomplete. Under these conditions, inefficient exploration is not merely an inconvenience, it can make such an AR system practically unusable.

AR 系统通常主要根据结果质量进行评估：可用预算用尽时获得的最终分数、通过率或竞赛排名。著名的基准测试报告了该终端性能AR systems are typically evaluated primarily by outcome quality: the final score, pass rate, or competition ranking achieved when the available budget is exhausted. Prominent benchmarks report this terminal performance[15, 6, 25, 38]，以及比较替代搜索结构的研究同样根据最终结果对系统进行排名, and studies comparing alternative search structures likewise rank systems by their final outcomes[33]。即使评估预算有所不同，每个预算设置通常也被视为产生单独终点的独立实验，而不是作为预算回报曲线上的一个点，其形状本身应该被优化。. Even when the evaluation budget is varied, each budget setting is generally treated as an independent experiment producing a separate endpoint, rather than as a point on a budget-reward curve whose shape should itself be optimized.

然而，仅终端性能并不能完全衡量系统质量。搜索效率与最终结果同样重要。假设两个系统最终达到相同的性能水平，如果一个系统仅使用另一个系统所需的十分之一的计算、工具调用、实验或验证预算就达到了该水平，那么它显然是在资源限制下能力更强的系统。最近的研究表明，即使使用相同的底层模型，同一任务的执行时间也可能相差大约 40%，具体取决于代理线束的设计Terminal performance alone, however, provides an incomplete measure of system quality. Search efficiency matters alongside final outcome. Given two systems that eventually achieve the same level of performance, if one reaches that level using only one-tenth of the computation, tool calls, experiments, or validation budget required by the other, it is clearly the more capable system under resource constraints. Recent work shows that, even with the same underlying model, execution time on the same task can differ by approximately 40% depending on the design of the agent harness[1]。同样的原则也适用于 AR 系统：编排、搜索策略、内存、验证和停止标准可以极大地影响达到给定结果所需的资源。一个优秀的AR系统不仅应该产生高质量的解决方案，还应该高效地找到解决方案。. The same principle applies to AR systems: orchestration, search policy, memory, verification, and stopping criteria can substantially affect the resources required to reach a given result. An outstanding AR system should produce not only high-quality solutions, but also find the solutions efficiently.

更重要的是，当候选人验证成本低廉时，主要通过最终结果来评估系统是很自然的。例如，在编码或数学中，可以通过运行单元测试或评估证明或公式来检查候选解决方案，这些方法相对便宜且快速。随着 AR 扩展到更广泛的科学领域，验证成本高昂，例如湿实验室实验、原型制造、临床评估或物理模拟，这种假设就被打破了。在这些情况下，每次试验都会产生相当大的时间、金钱或材料成本。因此，通过更少的实验达到相同水平的解决方案质量可能会决定 AR 程序在经济和操作上是否可行。What is more, evaluating systems primarily by terminal outcome is natural when candidate verification is cheap. In coding or mathematics, for example, a candidate solution may be checked by running unit tests or evaluating a proof or formula, which are relatively inexpensive and fast. This assumption breaks down as AR expands into broader scientific domains in which verification is costly, such as wet-lab experimentation, prototype fabrication, clinical evaluation, or physical simulation. In these settings, every trial incurs a meaningful cost in time, money or materials. Reaching the same level of solution quality with fewer experiments may therefore determine whether an AR program is economically and operationally feasible.

因此，效率应被视为首要目标，而不是次要报告指标。 AR 系统应该根据支出预算和获得的奖励之间的完整关系来评估，而不仅仅是根据最大可用预算获得的奖励。Efficiency should consequently be treated as a first-class objective rather than as a secondary reporting metric. An AR system should be evaluated by the full relationship between expended budget and achieved reward, not only by the reward obtained at the largest available budget.

Our contribution is to make the search-efficiency dimension of this regime explicit. Efficient search within a fixed frame, however, is necessary but not sufficient for more general research autonomy. On several tasks, even the strongest policies eventually plateau. One possible explanation is that these plateaus reflect a limitation of the proposer: the model may be unable to express or reliably realize the representational primitive required for further progress. This constitutes a form of the “vocabulary gap”—the difficulty of inventing and stabilizing a new representational primitive rather than merely recombining primitives already available. More efficient traversal of a fixed search space cannot recover a solution that lies outside the space induced by the proposer. A second limitation arises from the verifier. In our experiments, verification is fixed, inexpensive, formal, and immediate. This controlled setting allows us to isolate the effect of search structure, but it excludes discoveries whose value becomes apparent only through subsequent reuse or interaction with later developments. Such cases exhibit a “verifier gap”: a potentially valuable change cannot be recognized by the current evaluation procedure because the evidence required to assess it does not yet exist.

因此，效率和结果之间的区别可以被理解为更一般区别的框架内实例。搜索效率衡量系统在固定空间内取得进展的速度；它不能确定该空间本身是否足够。一项政策可能效率最高，但仍受到代表性上限的限制，就像另一项政策最终可能会达成强有力的解决方案，但收敛速度太慢而无法实用一样。因此，效率是固定框架内搜索的适当目标，但它本身并不能捕获扩展、修改或替换框架的转换。The distinction between efficiency and outcome can therefore be understood as the within-frame instance of a more general distinction. Search efficiency measures how rapidly a system makes progress within a fixed space; it does not determine whether that space is itself adequate. A policy may be maximally efficient yet remain constrained by a representational ceiling, just as another policy may eventually reach a strong solution while converging too slowly to be practical. Efficiency is therefore the appropriate objective for search within a fixed frame, but it does not by itself capture transformations that expand, revise, or replace the frame.

这些扩展并没有降低效率的重要性。发明新原语或修改自己的验证器的系统仍然消耗计算、实验和人力资源。他们的绩效曲线可能比这里研究的前沿奖励更复杂，但根本问题保持不变：在给定的预算下产生了多少有用的进展？这项工作中展示的适应性原则——维持多个搜索过程，避免过早致力于单一结构，并将资源重新分配给持续取得进展的过程——为在更高水平的研究自主权上解决这个问题提供了自然的基础。These extensions do not diminish the importance of efficiency. Systems that invent new primitives or revise their own verifiers still consume computational, experimental, and human resources. Their performance curves may be more complex than the frontier reward studied here, but the underlying question remains unchanged: how much useful progress is produced for a given budget? The adaptive principle demonstrated in this work—maintaining multiple search processes, avoiding premature commitment to a single structure, and reallocating resources to processes that continue to yield progress—provides a natural foundation for addressing this question at higher levels of research autonomy.
