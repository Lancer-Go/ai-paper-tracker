摘要Abstract
当旅行者要求人工智能搜索引擎推荐酒店时，哪些来源会被引用——查询框架重要吗？我们审核了 Google Gemini 对东京 156 个酒店查询的 1,357 条基本引用，并记录了一种我们称之为“意图来源鸿沟”的系统模式。体验式查询 55.9% 的引用来自非 OTA 来源，而交易性查询的这一比例为 30.8%，差距为 25.1 个百分点（When a traveler asks an AI search engine to recommend a hotel, which sources get cited—and does query framing matter? We audit 1,357 grounding citations from Google Gemini across 156 hotel queries in Tokyo and document a systematic pattern we call the Intent-Source Divide. Experiential queries draw 55.9% of their citations from non-OTA sources, compared to 30.8% for transactional queries—a 25.1 percentage-point gap (p<5×10−20p<5\times 10^{-20}）。这种效应在日语中被放大，其中体验式查询吸引了 62.1% 的非 OTA 引用，而英语中这一比例为 50.0%，这与更加多样化的日本非 OTA 内容生态系统一致。对于酒店长期向 OTA 支付需求获取费用的行业来说，这种模式很重要，因为它表明人工智能搜索可能会让酒店发现不再完全受佣金中介机构的控制。). The effect is amplified in Japanese, where experiential queries draw 62.1% non-OTA citations compared to 50.0% in English—consistent with a more diverse Japanese non-OTA content ecosystem. For an industry in which hotels have long paid OTAs for demand acquisition, this pattern matters because it suggests that AI search may make hotel discovery less exclusively controlled by commission-based intermediaries.

1. 简介Introduction

长期以来，酒店分销领域一直围绕着一个简单的交易进行组织：在线旅行社帮助旅行者发现酒店，而酒店则通过佣金为这种发现付费。佣金率通常为预订价值的 15% 至 25%The hospitality distribution landscape has long been organized around a simple bargain: Online Travel Agencies help travelers discover hotels, and hotels pay for that discovery through commission. Commission rates commonly range from 15% to 25% of booking value(dedge2024, )，对于独立酒店来说，OTA 预订可占客房收入的 61% 以上, and for independent properties OTA-originated bookings can represent over 61% of room revenue(cloudbeds2025, )。这种安排成本高昂，但酒店历来都接受它，因为 OTA 控制着旅行者在发现时使用的比较界面。经济学是有据可查的：OTA 提高了知名度和入住率，但利润却很高. This arrangement is costly, but hotels have historically accepted it because OTAs control the comparison interface that travelers use at the moment of discovery. The economics are well documented: OTAs boost visibility and occupancy but extract high margins(anderson2009billboard, ; ghose2012ranking, )，市场进入动态表明，通过 OTA 平台巩固品牌忠诚度可以阻止独立酒店进入, and market entry dynamics show that brand loyalty consolidated through OTA platforms can deter independent hotel entries(bianco2025deter, ).

生成式人工智能搜索可能会改变这种安排。当旅行者向 Gemini 或其他人工智能系统询问酒店推荐时，系统不再仅仅对链接进行排名；它综合了一个答案并引用了它所依赖的来源。因此，酒店的战略问题不再只是它们是否出现在 OTA 平台上，而是人工智能系统是否继续通过这些平台进行发现，或者开始直接引用酒店拥有的内容和其他非 OTA 来源。从这个意义上说，人工智能搜索不仅仅是一个新的营销渠道——它可能是对酒店发现控制权的重新分配。Generative AI search may change that arrangement. When a traveler asks Gemini or another AI system for a hotel recommendation, the system no longer merely ranks links; it synthesizes an answer and cites the sources it relies on. The strategic question for hotels is therefore no longer only whether they appear on OTA platforms, but whether AI systems continue to route discovery through those platforms or begin citing hotel-owned content and other non-OTA sources directly. In that sense, AI search is not simply a new marketing channel—it is a possible redistribution of who controls hotel discovery.

1.1. GEO 和引文审核：我们对人工智能搜索的了解GEO and Citation Audits: What We Know About AI Search

生成式人工智能搜索引擎综合多个来源的答案，并将这些来源引用为基础参考，创建一个发现架构，其中可见性可能取决于内容相关性，而不仅仅是页面权限。新兴的 GEO 文献已经开始描述这些模式：传统的 SEO 信号不能直接预测 AI 引用Generative AI search engines synthesize answers from multiple sources and cite those sources as grounding references, creating a discovery architecture where visibility may depend on content relevance rather than page authority alone. The emerging GEO literature has begun to characterize these patterns: traditional SEO signals do not straightforwardly predict AI citation(aggarwal2024geo, )，人工智能搜索显示出系统性地偏向于编辑内容而不是品牌拥有的内容, AI search shows systematic bias toward editorial content over brand-owned content(chenm2025geo, )，查询意图显着影响源选择, query intent significantly shapes source selection(chenx2025role, )，人工智能引用主要集中在少数媒体, and AI citations concentrate heavily among a small number of outlets(yang2025news, )。跨平台的引文审核发现了情绪、商业和地理偏见. Citation audits across platforms have identified sentiment, commercial, and geographic biases(li2024generative, )以及“发现差距”，其中发现式查询产生与命名实体查询截然不同的引用模式and a “discovery gap” in which discovery-style queries produce very different citation patterns than named-entity queries(sharma2026discovery, )。我们的研究将这种引文审核方法应用于酒店领域。. Our study adapts this citation-audit methodology for the hospitality domain.

1.2. 理论基础：查询意图和来源多样性Theoretical Grounding: Query Intent and Source Diversity

我们的研究借鉴了两条既定的研究路线。首先，信息检索文献早已认识到查询意图塑造搜索行为和结果特征。布罗德Our study draws on two established lines of research. First, the information retrieval literature has long recognized that query intent shapes search behavior and result characteristics. Broder(broder2002taxonomy, )提出了区分导航、信息和事务查询的基本分类法，证明不同的意图类型激活不同的检索策略并满足不同的信息需求。我们的交易/体验区别映射到这个分类：交易酒店查询类似于布罗德的交易类（目标：完成特定操作），而体验查询类似于信息类（目标：获取有关某个主题的知识）。该文献的主要见解是，查询意图不仅仅是用户特征，而且是查询的结构特征，它决定了哪些类型的网络内容构成令人满意的答案。proposed a foundational taxonomy distinguishing navigational, informational, and transactional queries, demonstrating that different intent types activate different retrieval strategies and satisfy different information needs. Our transactional/experiential distinction maps onto this taxonomy: transactional hotel queries resemble Broder’s transactional class (goal: complete a specific action), while experiential queries resemble the informational class (goal: acquire knowledge about a topic). The key insight from this literature is that query intent is not merely a user characteristic but a structural feature of the query that determines what kinds of web content constitute a satisfactory answer.

二、算法审计文献Second, the algorithm auditing literature(introna2000shaping, ; kulshrestha2017quantifying, )已经确定搜索引擎排名算法嵌入了系统偏差，使信息源获得可见性。我们的研究将这一传统扩展到生成式人工智能搜索，其中机制从排名转变为引用——人工智能系统不会对来源进行排名，而是选择引用哪些内容作为其综合响应的基础。我们采用的引文审核方法在概念上等同于传统搜索引擎研究的排名审核方法，适用于生成范式。has established that search engine ranking algorithms embed systematic biases in which information sources gain visibility. Our study extends this tradition to generative AI search, where the mechanism shifts from ranking to citation—the AI system does not rank sources but selects which to cite as grounding for its synthesized response. The citation-audit methodology we employ is conceptually equivalent to the ranking-audit methodology of traditional search engine studies, adapted for the generative paradigm.

这两条线索汇聚成一个简单的理论预测：如果人工智能搜索作为一个内容匹配系统（检索内容与查询的信息需求最匹配的来源）运行，那么具有不同意图类型的查询应该系统地检索不同的来源类型，因为最能回答事务性查询（结构化数据、价格比较、预订界面）的网页内容不同于最能回答体验式查询的内容（叙述性描述、编辑推荐、气氛特征）。如果观察到意图与来源的鸿沟，将与内容匹配在人工智能搜索引文中发挥重要作用是一致的，尽管它不会排除权威信号作为影响因素。These two strands converge in a simple theoretical prediction: if AI search operates as a content-matching system (retrieving sources whose content best matches the query’s information need), then queries with different intent types should systematically retrieve different source types, because the web content that best answers a transactional query (structured data, price comparisons, booking interfaces) differs from the content that best answers an experiential query (narrative descriptions, editorial recommendations, atmosphere characterizations). The intent-source divide, if observed, would be consistent with content-matching playing a significant role in AI search citation, though it would not rule out authority signals as a contributing factor.

1.3. 差距：尚无研究针对酒店业做到了这一点The Gap: No Study Has Done This for Hospitality

尽管人工智能搜索引用模式和酒店-OTA 分销经济学方面的研究不断增多，但据我们所知，还没有研究探讨人工智能搜索如何改变酒店业的中介。这种差距很重要，因为酒店是一种罕见的环境，其中发现中介可以清楚地识别并且在经济上很重要。如果 Gemini 引用 Booking.com、Expedia 或 Jalan，则该酒店是通过基于佣金的中介机构出现的。如果双子座引用酒店自己的网站，那么酒店正在更直接地竞争发现。引用来源并没有告诉我们客人将在哪里预订，但它确实告诉我们在做出推荐时谁捕捉到了发现时刻。在一个长期从 OTA 那里租用发现服务的市场中，这种区别具有直接的战略和经济意义。此外，酒店具有与查询意图相互作用的空间维度（车站访问查询不同于邻里氛围查询），三层市场结构（国际连锁酒店、国内连锁酒店、独立酒店）在网络存在、内容深度和 OTA 依赖性方面产生了自然变化。Despite growing work on AI search citation patterns and on hotel-OTA distribution economics, to our knowledge, no study has examined how AI search changes intermediation in hospitality. This gap matters because hotels are a rare setting where the discovery intermediary is clearly identifiable and economically important. If Gemini cites Booking.com, Expedia, or Jalan, the hotel is being surfaced through a commission-based intermediary. If Gemini cites the hotel’s own website, the hotel is competing for discovery more directly. Citation source does not tell us exactly where the guest will book, but it does tell us who captured the discovery moment at the moment the recommendation was made. In a market where discovery has long been rented from OTAs, that distinction has direct strategic and economic significance. Additionally, hotels have a spatial dimension that interacts with query intent (a station-access query differs from a neighborhood-atmosphere query), and the three-tier market structure (international chains, domestic chains, independents) creates natural variation in web presence, content depth, and OTA dependency.

1.4. 为什么选择东京Why Tokyo

我们在东京研究这个问题的三个原因使其成为理想的自然实验室。首先，东京的酒店市场呈现出明确的三层结构：国际连锁酒店（万豪、凯悦、希尔顿、洲际酒店）以及针对英语发现而优化的全球品牌网站；国内连锁酒店（APA酒店、东横INN、多米酒店、三井花园酒店）拥有内容丰富的日语网站，服务于庞大的国内市场；以及网络形象高度可变的独立酒店（日式旅馆、精品酒店、胶囊酒店、设计酒店）。这种三层结构会在网站内容深度、语言覆盖范围和 OTA 依赖性方面产生自然变化，使我们能够检查人工智能引用模式在不同细分市场之间是否存在系统性差异。We study this question in Tokyo for three reasons that make it an ideal natural laboratory. First, Tokyo’s hotel market exhibits a well-defined three-tier structure: international chains (Marriott, Hyatt, Hilton, InterContinental) with global brand websites optimized for English-language discovery; domestic chains (APA Hotels, Toyoko Inn, Dormy Inn, Mitsui Garden Hotels) with content-rich Japanese-language websites serving a large domestic market; and independent properties (ryokans, boutique hotels, capsule hotels, design hotels) with highly variable web presences. This three-tier structure generates natural variation in website content depth, language coverage, and OTA dependency that allows us to examine whether AI citation patterns systematically differ across market segments.

其次，东京在两个语言上独立的网络生态系统中运作。有关东京酒店的英语和日语网络内容在很大程度上是不同的语料库：它们具有不同的领域、不同的编辑声音、不同的 OTA 平台（Booking.com 和 Expedia 主导英语网络；Jalan、Rakuten Travel 和 Ikyu 主导日语网络）以及不同的非 OTA 内容类型。由于 Google 搜索基础（Gemini 检索源材料的机制）以查询语言搜索网络，因此英语和日语查询有效地呈现了不同的信息市场。这种双语结构使我们能够测试人工智能引用模式是否反映了底层网络生态系统的属性，而不是人工智能模型本身的属性。Second, Tokyo operates within two linguistically separate web ecosystems. English-language and Japanese-language web content about Tokyo hotels are largely distinct corpora: they feature different domains, different editorial voices, different OTA platforms (Booking.com and Expedia dominate the English web; Jalan, Rakuten Travel, and Ikyu dominate the Japanese web), and different non-OTA content types. Because Google Search grounding—the mechanism through which Gemini retrieves source material—searches the web in the query language, English and Japanese queries effectively surface different information markets. This bilingual structure allows us to test whether AI citation patterns reflect properties of the underlying web ecosystem rather than properties of the AI model itself.

第三，东京是全球最大的入境旅游市场之一：2024年日本接待外国游客3690万人次，创该国历史最高数字，东京为主要门户Third, Tokyo is one of the world’s largest inbound tourism markets: Japan recorded 36.9 million foreign visitors in 2024, the highest figure in the country’s history, with Tokyo as the primary gateway(jnto2025, )。东京酒店的可发现性具有重大的经济影响。高游客量、竞争激烈的酒店市场以及国际和国内预订平台的共存，使东京成为人工智能搜索中介问题在经验上易于处理且具有实际意义的环境。. Hotel discoverability in Tokyo has substantial economic consequences. The combination of high visitor volume, a competitive hotel market, and the coexistence of international and domestic booking platforms makes Tokyo a setting where the question of AI search intermediation is both empirically tractable and practically significant.

2. 数据和方法Data and Methods

2.1. 查询设计Query Design

我们设计了一个结构化查询语料库，其中包含 156 个查询，这些查询被组织为类别匹配对。每对都包含一个交易变体和一个体验变体，可满足同一地理区域内相同旅行者的需求类别，并以英语和日语执行。这种配对设计确保一对内查询之间唯一变化的变量是意图框架，而不是主题、地理范围或语言，使我们能够隔离意图对引文来源构成的影响。We designed a structured query corpus of 156 queries organized as category-matched pairs. Each pair consists of a transactional variant and an experiential variant addressing the same traveler need category in the same geographic area, executed in both English and Japanese. This paired design ensures that the only variable changing between queries within a pair is the intent framing—not the topic, geographic scope, or language—allowing us to isolate the effect of intent on citation source composition.

查询类别。Query categories.我们确定了涵盖酒店搜索主要维度的四个旅行者需求类别：(1)We identified four traveler need categories that span the primary dimensions of hotel search: (1)预算Budget—价格敏感的发现，其中OTA由于价格比较功能而具有强大的结构优势； (2)—price-sensitive discovery, where OTAs have a strong structural advantage due to price comparison functionality; (2)评级/质量Rating/Quality——以质量为导向的发现，其中综合评论分数和星级评定是主要信号； (3)—quality-oriented discovery, where aggregated review scores and star ratings are the primary signals; (3)方便Convenience——以位置和访问为导向的发现，其中靠近交通节点和步行性是关键标准；和（4）—location and access-oriented discovery, where proximity to transit nodes and walkability are the key criteria; and (4)商务Business——以工作为导向的发现，其中工作空间质量、安静的环境和商业设施定义了需求。选择这些类别是为了涵盖先前对酒店选择行为的研究已确定为主要决策因素的功能和体验维度的范围。—work-oriented discovery, where workspace quality, quiet environments, and business amenities define the need. These categories were selected to cover the range of functional and experiential dimensions that prior research on hotel choice behavior has identified as primary decision factors.

For each category, the transactional variant was designed to resemble a booking-oriented query—the kind of query that would naturally lead to an OTA listing (e.g., “Cheap hotel in Shinjuku,” “Best rated hotel in Shibuya”). The experiential variant was designed to describe the same need in terms of the guest experience rather than a bookable attribute (e.g., “Good value hotel with local charm in Shinjuku,” “Hotel with exceptional service and atmosphere in Shibuya”). 5. 局限性Limitations

这项研究有六个局限性，限制了我们研究结果的普遍性和因果解释。This study has six limitations that bound the generalizability and causal interpretation of our findings.

1.单一平台、单一时间点。1. Single platform, single point in time.所有 156 个查询均于 2026 年 3 月在具有 Google 搜索基础的 Gemini 2.5 Flash 上执行。Google 定期更新底层语言模型和确定检索和引用哪些网络资源的基础机制。模型版本的更改、基础源排名的修改或 Google 搜索索引的更新可能会改变我们记录的引用模式。此外，其他人工智能搜索平台（支持浏览的 ChatGPT、Perplexity、Claude）采用不同的检索架构，并且可能表现出不同的源类型偏好。 ChatGPT 使用 Bing 而不是 Google 搜索进行网络基础，它访问不同的索引并应用不同的排名信号。 Perplexity 维护着自己的搜索索引和引文系统。我们的研究结果特定于 Gemini-Google 搜索基础管道，不应假设在没有经验交叉验证的情况下可以跨平台推广。All 156 queries were executed on Gemini 2.5 Flash with Google Search grounding in March 2026. Google regularly updates both the underlying language model and the grounding mechanism that determines which web sources are retrieved and cited. A model version change, a modification to grounding source ranking, or an update to Google Search’s index could alter the citation patterns we document. Furthermore, other AI search platforms—ChatGPT with browsing enabled, Perplexity, Claude—employ different retrieval architectures and may exhibit different source-type preferences. ChatGPT uses Bing rather than Google Search for web grounding, which accesses a different index and applies different ranking signals. Perplexity maintains its own search index and citation system. Our findings are specific to the Gemini-Google Search grounding pipeline and should not be assumed to generalize across platforms without empirical cross-validation.

2. 相关性，而非因果性。2. Correlation, not causation.我们观察到，网站内容更丰富（社区指南、常见问题解答页面、观光信息、访问地图）的酒店收到更多来自 Gemini 的直接引用。然而，我们无法确定内容深度会导致直接引用。观察到的关联很容易混淆：拥有强大品牌的知名酒店可能既投资网站内容，又因品牌认知度、搜索引擎权威或与内容深度相关但又不同的反向链接配置文件而受到引用。经常在第三方网站上提及的酒店可能会积累域名权重，这有利于其独立于其自身内容的直接引用率。 K5 案例（第 3.4 节）强化了这一限制：内容功能强大但搜索可回答深度较浅的酒店没有被引用，这表明内容本身并不能决定引用 - Google 搜索排名也发挥着作用。因果测试——修改酒店的网站内容并测量双子座引用行为的后续变化——需要采用前后测量的受控干预设计，我们建议将其作为未来的工作，但在本研究中尚未进行。We observe that hotels with richer website content—neighborhood guides, FAQ pages, sightseeing information, access maps—receive more direct citations from Gemini. However, we cannot establish that content depth causes direct citation. The observed association is susceptible to confounding: well-known hotels with strong brands may both invest in website content and receive citations due to brand recognition, search engine authority, or backlink profiles that are correlated with but distinct from content depth. A hotel that is frequently mentioned on third-party websites may accumulate domain authority that benefits its direct citation rate independently of its own content. The K5 case (Section 3.4) reinforces this limitation: a hotel with strong content features but shallow search-answerable depth was not cited, suggesting that content alone does not determine citation—Google Search ranking also plays a role. The causal test—modifying a hotel’s website content and measuring subsequent changes in Gemini citation behavior—would require a controlled intervention design with pre-post measurement, which we propose as future work but have not conducted in this study.
