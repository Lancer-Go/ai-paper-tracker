摘要Abstract

代理强化学习需要将功劳分配给面向环境的操作，例如搜索、单击、编辑、导航命令和对象交互。标准 GRPO 使用最终验证者结果作为相对于所有操作令牌的统一优势。这个结果信号是有用的，但结构上不完整：它惩罚失败的推出中的有用探索，并强化成功推出中的冗余或倒退行动。我们提出了 TRIAGE，一种角色类型的信用分配框架，为结果信用添加了语义角色轴。结构化法官将每个细分分类为决定性进展、有用探索、无进展基础设施或回归，并且固定的角色条件规则将这些标签映射到有界细分级流程奖励。这使得验证者的结果成为优化方向的来源，同时纠正了仅结果信用的两个主要盲点。我们进一步表明，角色条件信用是仅从角色标签即可表达的最佳分段级别修正（将每个分段的优势残差投影到角色变量上），因此只要法官可靠，固定的角色常数就会减少优势估计误差，并且我们将其与较低方差的政策梯度联系起来。在 ALFWorld、Search-QA 和 WebShop 中，TRIAGE 提高了两种策略模型的 GRPO 成功率，并且优于标量法官衍生的过程奖励和结果监督的共享骨干价值基线。消融表明，收益来自角色类型，而不仅仅是添加密集奖励：成功轨迹内回归的可靠检测是主要贡献者，而探索信用提供了一致的次要收益；在完成 ALFWorld 和 WebShop 推出后，TRIAGE 还减少了额外的面向环境的转弯Agentic reinforcement learning requires assigning credit to environment-facing actions such as searches, clicks, edits, navigation commands, and object interactions. Standard GRPO uses the final verifier outcome as a uniform advantage over all action tokens. This outcome signal is useful but structurally incomplete: it punishes useful exploration in failed rollouts and reinforces redundant or regressive actions in successful rollouts. We propose TRIAGE, a role-typed credit assignment framework that adds a semantic role axis to outcome credit. A structured judge classifies each segment as decisive progress, useful exploration, no-progress infrastructure, or regression, and a fixed role-conditioned rule maps these labels to bounded segment-level process rewards. This keeps verifier outcomes as the source of optimization direction while correcting the two main blind spots of outcome-only credit. We further show that role-conditioned credit is the optimal segment-level correction expressible from role labels alone—a projection of the per-segment advantage residual onto the role variable—so that the fixed role constants reduce advantage estimation error whenever the judge is reliable, and we connect this to lower-variance policy gradients. Across ALFWorld, Search-QA, and WebShop, TRIAGE improves success rates over GRPO for two policy models and outperforms both a scalar judge-derived process reward and an outcome-supervised shared-backbone value baseline. Ablations show that the gain comes from role typing rather than merely adding dense rewards: reliable detection of regression inside successful trajectories is the dominant contributor, while exploration credit provides a consistent secondary gain; on completed ALFWorld and WebShop rollouts, TRIAGE also reduces environment-facing turns by an additional10.4%10.4\%和and14.8%14.8\%相对于GRPO。relative to GRPO.

1 简介Introduction

具有可验证奖励的强化学习已成为改善语言模型推理和代理行为的标准方法Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards has become a standard recipe for improving language-model reasoning and agentic behavior(Shao et al., 2024; DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025; Trung et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2026a)。在组相对策略优化（GRPO）中，策略对提示的多个轨迹进行采样，从验证者接收最终奖励，并将相对优势分配给采样的输出。这个方法很有吸引力，因为它不需要学习价值模型，并且直接针对部署策略进行优化。然而，当输出是代理轨迹而不是单一答案时，中心信用分配问题就会发生变化：. In Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), a policy samples multiple trajectories for a prompt, receives final rewards from a verifier, and assigns relative advantages to the sampled outputs. This recipe is attractive because it requires no learned value model and optimizes directly against the deployment policy. However, when the output is an agentic trajectory rather than a single answer, the central credit-assignment question changes:当监督仅作为最终验证者结果时，哪些面向环境的行动值得赞扬？which environment-facing actions deserve credit when supervision arrives only as a final verifier outcome?

此设置中的决策单位不是任意令牌跨度。它是一个面向环境的部分：搜索查询、单击、文件编辑、命令、对象交互或更改外部状态或代理信息状态的工具调用。在 WebShop、Search-QA 和 ALFWorld 中，此类细分范围从决定性操作（最终购买、答案提交、对象放置）到信息收集操作（搜索、检查、读取）和低价值基础设施（重复导航或冗余点击）。The unit of decision in this setting is not an arbitrary token span. It is an environment-facing segment: a search query, click, file edit, command, object interaction, or tool call that changes either the external state or the agent’s information state. Across WebShop, Search-QA, and ALFWorld, such segments range from decisive actions (final purchases, answer submissions, object placements) to information-gathering actions (searches, inspections, reads) and low-value infrastructure (repeated navigation or redundant clicks).

因此，结果信用是有用的，但结构上并不完整。标准 GRPO 平等对待轨迹内的所有分段：如果轨迹成功，则所有动作标记都会得到强化；如果失败，一切都会被压制。这造成了两个系统性盲点。首先，失败的推出可能包含有用的探索性行动，但这些行动不应继承全部负面结果。其次，成功的部署可能包含多余或有害的操作，这些操作不应仅仅因为代理后来恢复而继承正面信用。最终结果告诉我们轨迹是否解决了任务，但它不能说明每个部分发挥的局部作用。Outcome credit is therefore useful but structurally incomplete. Standard GRPO treats all segments equally within a trajectory: if the trajectory succeeds, all action tokens are reinforced; if it fails, all are suppressed. This creates two systematic blind spots. First, failed rollouts can contain useful exploratory actions that should not inherit the full negative outcome credit. Second, successful rollouts can contain redundant or harmful actions that should not inherit positive credit merely because the agent later recovered. Final outcome tells us whether the trajectory solved the task, but it cannot say what local role each segment played.

最近的信用分配和政策监管方法部分解决了这个问题。状态锚定估计器比较来自匹配状态的动作；过程奖励模型学习密集的进度信号；结果统计方法估计重复部分是否集中在成功或失败的推出中；和令牌重要性方法在采样输出中重新加权监督Recent credit-assignment and on-policy supervision methods address parts of this problem. State-anchored estimators compare actions from matched states; process reward models learn dense progress signals; outcome-statistical methods estimate whether recurring segments concentrate in successful or failed rollouts; and token-importance methods reweight supervision within sampled outputs(Wang et al., 2025; Lu et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2026b)。这些方法很有用，但它们通常对每个部分进行评分，而不指定其语义角色：任务进度、信念状态进度、无害的基础设施和回归不应接受相同的信用规则。我们通过比较两个密集信号控制（具有相同判断和上下文窗口的标量 LLM 过程奖励基线，以及结果监督的共享骨干价值基线）来直接测试这种区别，因此经验问题不是密集分段奖励是否有帮助，而是角色类型是否会添加超出它们的信息。. These approaches are useful, but they usually score each segment without specifying its semantic role: task progress, belief-state progress, harmless infrastructure, and regression should not receive the same credit rule. We test this distinction directly by comparing against two dense-signal controls—a scalar LLM process-reward baseline with the same judge and context window, and an outcome-supervised shared-backbone value baseline—so the empirical question is not whether dense segment rewards help, but whether role typing adds information beyond them.

因此，我们的核心主张是：Our central claim is therefore:除了结果轴之外，代理强化学习还需要角色轴agentic RL needs a role axis in addition to an outcome axis。最重要的区别是探索并不是没有进展。探索的即时任务进度通常为零，并且可能出现在成功和失败的轨迹中。纯粹的结果统计估计者可能会低估它，因为探索性行动并不总是针对成功的。当没有立即完成子目标时，通用过程评分器还可以将探索与无进展混为一谈。然而，抑制探索正是稀疏奖励代理训练变得脆弱的原因：该策略在获得足够的信息来采取果断行动之前，学会避免信息收集行动。. The most important distinction is that exploration is not no-progress. Exploration often has zero immediate task progress and may appear in both successful and failed trajectories. A purely outcome-statistical estimator can under-credit it because exploratory actions are not always success-specific. A generic process scorer can also conflate exploration with no-progress when no subgoal is completed immediately. Yet suppressing exploration is precisely how sparse-reward agent training becomes brittle: the policy learns to avoid information-gathering actions before it has enough information to act decisively.

我们提出了 TRIAGE，一个用于角色感知信用评估的简单框架。就像医疗分类一样，在分配治疗之前根据患者需要的关注类型对患者进行分类，TRIAGE 首先将每个面向环境的片段分类为语义角色，然后再决定它应该从轨迹结果继承多少信用。 TRIAGE 使用结构化的 LLM 法官作为We propose TRIAGE, a simple framework for role-aware credit estimation. Like medical triage, which sorts patients by the kind of attention they need before allocating treatment, TRIAGE first sorts each environment-facing segment into a semantic role before deciding how much credit it should inherit from the trajectory outcome. TRIAGE uses a structured LLM judge as a角色分类器role classifier，而不是作为无约束的奖励模型。鉴于每个细分市场都有一定的当地背景，法官会分配一个主要角色：决定性的进展、有用的探索、无进展的基础设施或倒退。然后，强化学习算法将角色映射到不同的信用规则。决定性的进展会获得与结果挂钩的强大信用，有用的勘探会获得有限的正信用，无进展的基础设施会被抑制为零，而回归也会受到抑制，即使它出现在其他成功的轨迹中。, not as an unconstrained reward model. Given a bounded local context around each segment, the judge assigns one primary role: decisive progress, useful exploration, no-progress infrastructure, or regression. The RL algorithm then maps roles to different credit rules. Decisive progress receives strong outcome-aligned credit, useful exploration receives bounded positive credit, no-progress infrastructure is dampened toward zero, and regression is suppressed even when it appears in an otherwise successful trajectory.

这种设计故意将语义诊断与优化方向分开。法学硕士非常适合回答本地问题，例如某个操作是否检查了相关文件、缩小了搜索范围、损坏了状态或重复了已知信息。它不太适合替代验证器。因此，TRIAGE 将 GRPO 结果优势保留为基本训练信号，并仅使用角色分类器在分段级别添加有界过程奖励或惩罚。This design deliberately separates semantic diagnosis from optimization direction. An LLM is well suited to answering local questions such as whether an action inspected a relevant file, narrowed a search, damaged state, or repeated known information. It is less suited to replacing the verifier. TRIAGE therefore keeps the GRPO outcome advantage as the base training signal and uses the role classifier only to add bounded process rewards or penalties at the segment level.

6 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

局限性。Limitations.

角色标签是语义估计，而不是基本事实。法官可能会高估合理的探索，错过微妙的回归，或者过于依赖最终结果。 TRIAGE 通过仅将判断用于结构化角色诊断并将验证者结果保留为基本优化信号来缓解这一问题，但它并不能消除判断错误。Role labels are semantic estimates, not ground truth. A judge can overvalue plausible exploration, miss subtle regressions, or rely too much on final outcomes. TRIAGE mitigates this by using the judge only for structured role diagnosis and keeping verifier outcomes as the base optimization signal, but it does not remove judge error.

角色的有用性也取决于上下文。相同的搜索、读取或测试命令可能一次提供信息，但之后又变得多余，因此分类器必须以本地状态和冗余为条件，而不是仅以操作字符串为条件。最后，角色感知信用不是因果识别：它改善了局部归因，但需要反事实环境干预来证明某个部分是必要的。Role usefulness is also context-dependent. The same search, read, or test command can be informative once and redundant later, so the classifier must condition on local state and redundancy rather than action strings alone. Finally, role-aware credit is not causal identification: it improves local attribution, but counterfactual environment interventions would be needed to prove that a segment was necessary.

未来的工作。Future work.

本文为每个段使用一个主要角色来保持信号的可审计性。一个自然的扩展是软角色分配，例如，This paper uses one primary role per segment to keep the signal auditable. A natural extension is a soft role distribution, e.g.,(pD,pE,pN,pR)(p_{D},p_{E},p_{N},p_{R})，信用计算为特定于角色的常量下的期望。这可以更好地代表混合部分，例如揭示有用证据同时引入干扰因素的搜索，但它需要可靠的校准和更强大的审计程序。, with credit computed as an expectation under role-specific constants. This could better represent mixed segments, such as a search that reveals useful evidence while also introducing distractors, but it would require reliable calibration and stronger audit procedures.

TRIAGE 还与分段存储和结果统计估计器兼容。分桶可以决定哪些细分共享统计证据，而角色标签则决定如何解释该证据。对于精确动作参数稀疏且重复片段很少的领域来说，将两者结合起来是一个有前途的方向。TRIAGE is also compatible with segment bucketing and outcome-statistical estimators. Bucketing can decide which segments share statistical evidence, while role labels decide how that evidence should be interpreted. Combining the two is a promising direction for domains where exact action arguments are sparse and repeated segments are rare.

最后，离散的四角色标签只是角色感知判断的第一层。在更困难的任务或更强的基础代理上，明显的循环、错误的购买和重复的检查变得很少，并且信用问题从检测粗略的故障转变为估计每个部分对任务或信念状态的推进程度。在该制度中，相同的框架可以使用更强大的判断来分配更细粒度的过程奖励Finally, the discrete four-role label is only the first layer of role-aware judging. On harder tasks or stronger base agents, obvious loops, wrong purchases, and repeated inspections become rare, and the credit problem shifts from detecting coarse failures to estimating how much each segment advances the task or belief state. In that regime the same framework can use a stronger judge to assign finer-grained process rewards之内within每个角色而不是单个离散标签。each role rather than a single discrete label.

代理信用分配。Agentic credit assignment.

代理强化学习需要在面向环境的决策中分配信用，而不仅仅是在代币之间分配信用。表Agentic RL requires assigning credit across environment-facing decisions rather than only across tokens. 流程奖励模型和法学硕士评委。Process reward models and LLM judges.

过程奖励模型通过对中间推理或代理步骤进行评分来提供密集监督Process reward models provide dense supervision by scoring intermediate reasoning or agent steps(Lightman et al., 2024)。 LLM-as-judge 方法可以评估生成的输出、批评轨迹或分配评分标准. Unstructured process scores can be brittle: they may punish correct actions in failed trajectories, over-credit plausible narration, or conflate exploration with lack of progress. TRIAGE uses the judge more narrowly as a structured classifier over segment roles. This reduces the burden on the judge and makes the resulting signal easier to audit.

语言代理的探索。Exploration in language agents.

语言代理通常依赖于信息收集操作，例如搜索、检查、读取和测试执行Language agents often rely on information-gathering actions such as search, inspect, read, and test execution(Yao et al., 2023b; Schick et al., 2023)。相关的提示和自我改进方法还利用多个采样推理路径、搜索树或自我生成的基本原理来公开有用的中间信息. Related prompting and self-improvement methods also exploit multiple sampled reasoning paths, search trees, or self-generated rationales to expose useful intermediate information(Wang et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023a; Zelikman et al., 2022)。这些动作改变了智能体的信念状态，而不是立即完成任务。在稀疏奖励强化学习中，此类行为很容易被错误分类为中性或浪费。 TRIAGE 使信念状态进展成为一个明确的信用类别，允许训练保留有用的探索，同时仍然抑制冗余或不相关的探索。. These actions change the agent’s belief state rather than immediately completing the task. In sparse-reward RL, such actions are easy to misclassify as neutral or wasteful. TRIAGE makes belief-state progress an explicit credit category, allowing training to preserve useful exploration while still suppressing redundant or irrelevant exploration.

在政策蒸馏和代币加权。On-policy distillation and token weighting.

在政策蒸馏和令牌重要性方法完善了对采样轨迹的监督On-policy distillation and token-importance methods refine supervision on sampled trajectories(Xu et al., 2026b, a; Agarwal et al., 2024)。这些方法主要以令牌或响应粒度运行。 TRIAGE 在代理细分层面上运行，可应用于 RL 优势或蒸馏损失：角色标签可以控制哪些动作会得到强烈的蒸馏或强化。. These methods mostly operate at token or response granularity. TRIAGE operates at the agentic segment level and can be applied to either RL advantages or distillation losses: role labels can gate which action turns receive strong distillation or reinforcement.

8 结论Conclusion

我们认为，代理信用分配需要区分每个面向环境的部分所扮演的角色。缺少的关键区别是探索并非没有进展：一项行动可以改善代理人的信念状态，而无需立即完成子目标。 TRIAGE 通过结构化的角色判断和角色条件信用规则来实现这一想法，将 GRPO 结果优势保持为优化方向，同时添加有界的、角色类型的修正。在 ALFWorld、Search-QA 和 WebShop 中，这两种策略模型的成功率比 GRPO 提高了多达We argued that agentic credit assignment requires distinguishing what role each environment-facing segment plays. The key missing distinction is that exploration is not no-progress: an action can improve the agent’s belief state without immediately completing a subgoal. TRIAGE operationalizes this idea with a structured role judge and role-conditioned credit rules, keeping the GRPO outcome advantage as the optimization direction while adding a bounded, role-typed correction.
