1 简介Introduction

最近的基础图像和视频生成模型Recent foundation image and video generation models[podell2024sdxl, labs2025flux1kontextflowmatching, openai_gptimage_2025, blattmann2023stable, cui2025emu3, xie2025show, wu2025janus, chen2025janus, team2024chameleon]通过利用互联网上的数据进行训练，在语义理解、可控生成和视觉推理等方面取得了显着进展。大规模生成模型现在能够从各种多模态输入合成高度逼真的图像和视频，展示出令人印象深刻的泛化能力，远远超出了训练数据的分布范围。这些功能使基础生成模型成为具身智能的有吸引力的起点have made remarkable progress in semantic understanding, controllable generation, and visual reasoning through training with data on the Internet. Large-scale generative models are now capable of synthesizing highly realistic images and videos from various multimodal inputs, demonstrating impressive generalization far beyond the distribution of their training data. Such capabilities make foundation generative models an attractive starting point for embodied intelligence[yuan2026fast, ye2026world, shang2026roboscape, long2026scaling]，机器人需要在行动之前推理复杂的环境并想象未来的交互。, where robots are required to reason about complex environments and imagine future interactions before acting.

然而，体现一代However, embodied generation[long2025survey, liu2025aligning, li2025comprehensive]引入了与传统图像和视频合成根本不同的挑战。与自然图像生成不同，具体场景需要严格的多视图一致性、摄像机之间精确的几何和物理一致性、明确的机器人具体约束以及时间一致的交互动态。生成的观察结果必须与机器人运动学、相机校准和下游操纵策略保持兼容，而不仅仅是看起来视觉上真实。因此，直接将现有的基础图像或视频生成模型应用于具体场景通常会导致几何形状不一致、机器人状态不可信以及与机器人控制的兼容性差。introduces challenges that differ fundamentally from conventional image and video synthesis. Unlike natural image generation, embodied scenarios require strict multi-view consistency, accurate geometric and physical coherence across cameras, explicit robot embodiment constraints, and temporally consistent interaction dynamics. The generated observations must remain compatible with robot kinematics, camera calibration, and downstream manipulation policies rather than merely appearing visually realistic. Consequently, directly applying existing foundation image or video generation models to embodied scenarios often leads to inconsistent geometry, implausible robot states, and poor compatibility with robot control.

最近体现的世界模型Recent embodied world models[agarwal2026cosmos, zhang2026qwen, shang2026roboscape]尝试通过使用机器人轨迹不断调整预先训练的基础模型来弥补这一差距attempt to bridge this gap by continually adapting pre-trained foundation models using robot trajectories[Neill2023open_x_embodiment, tian2026interndata, yin2026geniesim30, jiang2025galaxea, walke2023bridgedata, brohan2022rt]或以自我为中心的操纵视频or egocentric manipulation videos[akhtyamov2025egowalk, grauman2022ego4d]。虽然这些方法已经取得了令人鼓舞的进展，但它们的适应范式仍然与基础模型预训练的范式有根本的不同。后期训练通常仅在特定于机器人的数据集上执行，而不是保留最初赋予基础模型强大的语义理解和可控生成的多样化图像和视频生成任务。由于与互联网规模的视觉语料库相比，具体数据集要小得多，多样性较低且重复性高. While these approaches have demonstrated encouraging progress, their adaptation paradigm remains fundamentally different from that of foundation model pre-training. Instead of preserving the diverse image and video generation tasks that originally endow foundation models with strong semantic understanding and controllable generation, post-training is typically performed solely on robot-specific datasets. Since embodied datasets are considerably smaller, less diverse and highly repetitive compared with internet-scale visual corpora[gadre2023datacomp, schuhmann2022laion, kakaobrain2022coyo-700m]，这种适应不可避免地削弱了从基础模型继承的泛化能力，并限制了具体生成的多样性、可控性和可扩展性。, such adaptation inevitably weakens the generalization capability inherited from foundation models and limits the diversity, controllability, and scalability of the embodied generation.

数据平衡Data Balancing

我们的训练语料库包含异构多领域数据，这很容易导致领域偏差和对主导数据分布的过度拟合。我们采用以数据域和生成任务类型（多视图生成、场景编辑、单视图生成）为条件的样本级重新加权，核心目标是保持机器人操作场景的全覆盖。Our training corpus contains heterogeneous multi-domain data, which easily leads to domain bias and overfitting toward dominant data distributions. We adopt sample-level reweighting conditioned on data domains and generation task types (multi-view generation, scene editing, single-view generation), with the core target of maintaining full coverage of robotic manipulation scenarios.

具体来说，我们首先消除无效的具体情节，包括视觉效果损坏、时间戳错位、本体感受或动作信号缺失、执行记录失败以及缺乏机器人与物体交互的静态轨迹的序列。所有剩余的样本都通过从子任务和结构化场景标签派生的分层注释进行分类。分组标准涵盖任务目标、场景上下文、目标对象、空间关系、子任务阶段和原子操作技能。Specifically, we first eliminate invalid embodied episodes, including sequences with corrupted visuals, misaligned timestamps, missing proprioceptive or action signals, failed execution records, and static trajectories lacking robot-object interactions. All remaining samples are categorized via hierarchical annotations derived from subtask and structured scene labeling. Grouping criteria cover task goals, scene contexts, target objects, spatial relations, subtask phases, and atomic manipulation skills.

这种细粒度的分组比简单的数据集级加权支持更精确的平衡：共享相同实施例-任务-对象组合的冗余样本被下采样，而具有新颖视觉效果、对象布局、操作技能或时间阶段的剪辑被优先考虑。较高的采样权重被分配给长尾类别，包括稀有物体、很少使用的操作技能和核心交互阶段（抓取、释放、放置和物体状态转换）。由此产生的训练分布消除了多余的重复演示，同时保留了多样化的视觉场景和全面的操作技能覆盖范围。Such fine-grained grouping supports more precise balancing than simple dataset-level weighting: redundant samples sharing identical embodiment-task-object combinations are downsampled, whereas clips with novel visuals, object layouts, manipulation skills or temporal stages are prioritized. Higher sampling weights are assigned to long-tail categories, including rare objects, seldom-used manipulation skills, and core interaction phases (grasping, releasing, placing, and object state transitions). The resulting training distribution eliminates redundant repeated demonstrations while retaining diverse visual scenes and comprehensive manipulation skill coverage.

Rather than specializing a foundation model solely on robot trajectories, we jointly optimize general-domain generation and embodied synthesis within a unified autoregressive framework, preserving the original generation capability while acquiring robot-centric geometric reasoning and interaction modeling.

可控图像生成和图像编辑方面的最新进展通过指令引导合成和参考条件编辑显着改进了语义操作。代表性方法，包括 InstructPix2PixRecent advances in controllable image generation and image editing have substantially improved semantic manipulation through instruction-guided synthesis and reference-conditioned editing. Representative approaches, including InstructPix2Pix[brooks2023instructpix2pix], 任意门, AnyDoor[chen2024anydoor], 奥尼金, OmniGen[xiao2025omnigen], GPT-Image-2.0 [openai_gptimage_2025]和 FLUX 上下文, and FLUX Kontext[labs2025flux1kontextflowmatching]，实现具有强大指令遵循性和语义一致性的高质量编辑。然而，这些方法主要是针对自然图像而设计的，并且通常假设单视图观察，这使得它们很难直接应用于实体操纵，其中多个校准相机、机器人实施例和交互状态必须保持几何一致。, enable high-quality editing with strong instruction following and semantic consistency. However, these methods are primarily designed for natural images and generally assume single-view observations, making them difficult to directly apply to embodied manipulation, where multiple calibrated cameras, robot embodiments, and interaction states must remain geometrically consistent.

同时，多视图生成在图像合成和 3D 内容创建中引起了越来越多的关注。代表性方法，包括MVDreamMeanwhile, multi-view generation has attracted increasing attention in image synthesis and 3D content creation. Representative methods, including MVDream[shi2024mvdream], 同步梦想家, SyncDreamer[liu2024syncdreamer], 奇迹3D, Wonder3D[long2024wonder3d], 时代3D, Era3D[li2024era3d], 零123++, Zero123++[shi2023zero123++]，以及相关的多视图扩散模型, and related multi-view diffusion models[gao2024cat3d, tang2024mvdiffusion++]，明确强制执行跨视图一致性，以促进高质量的 3D 重建和资产生成。然而，这些方法主要关注以对象为中心的生成，并且没有明确考虑机器人实施例、校准的操纵相机或交互感知场景合成。, explicitly enforce cross-view consistency to facilitate high-quality 3D reconstruction and asset generation. Nevertheless, these methods mainly focus on object-centric generation and do not explicitly consider robot embodiments, calibrated manipulation cameras, or interaction-aware scene synthesis.

我们的工作通过在单个自回归框架内统一可控图像编辑、多视图体现场景生成和体现传输来连接这两个研究方向。此外，我们引入了一种结构化控制公式，将工作空间布局、背景外观、前景不相关对象、目标对象和照明分解为独立的控制维度，从而实现更丰富的具体场景变化，同时保留机器人几何形状、具体约束和多视图一致性。Our work bridges these two research directions by unifying controllable image editing, multi-view embodied scene generation, and embodied transfer within a single autoregressive framework. Furthermore, we introduce a structured control formulation that disentangles workspace layout, background appearance, foreground irrelevant objects, target objects, and lighting into independent control dimensions, enabling significantly richer embodied scene variations while preserving robot geometry, embodiment constraints, and multi-view consistency.

4.3 体现视频生成和合成数据引擎Embodied Video Generation and Synthetic Data Engines

视频生成和具体世界模拟最近对于可扩展的机器人学习变得越来越重要。基础视频模型，例如 MovieGenVideo generation and embodied world simulation have recently become increasingly important for scalable robot learning. Foundation video models such as MovieGen[polyak2024movie], 万, Wan[wan2025wan], 宇宙, Cosmos[agarwal2026cosmos], 精灵, Genie[bruce2024genie]、Qwen-机器人世界, and Qwen-RobotWorld[zhang2026qwen]在长视距视频合成和未来观测预测方面展示了令人印象深刻的能力。同时，大规模机器人数据集和仿真平台，包括Open X-Embodimentdemonstrate impressive capabilities in long-horizon video synthesis and future observation prediction. Meanwhile, large-scale robot datasets and simulation platforms, including Open X-Embodiment[o2024open], 机器人之家, RoboCasa[nasiriany2024robocasa], 摩尼技能, ManiSkill[mu2021maniskill], 桥数据 V2, BridgeData V2[walke2023bridgedata]和机器人, and DROID[khazatsky2024droid]，大大加速了数据驱动的机器人学习的发展。最近，体现视频数据集和合成数据管道，例如 RoboMIND, have substantially accelerated the development of data-driven robot learning. More recently, embodied video datasets and synthetic data pipelines, such as RoboMIND[wu2024robomind], 机器人币, RoboCoin[wu2025robocoin], 精灵模拟, GenieSim[yin2026geniesim30], 实习生A1, InternA1[tian2026interndata]，和 AgiBotWorld, and AgiBotWorld[bu2025agibot]，进一步探索大规模具体视频生成和机器人数据增强。, further explore large-scale embodied video generation and robot data augmentation.

尽管取得了这些进步，现有的方法通常独立地合成孤立的图像、模拟资产或简短的机器人视频，而没有对可控场景生成、具体传输和长视野轨迹推出进行联合建模。相比之下，我们的工作将具体场景生成、具体传输、交错子任务子目标预测和具体视频生成统一在单个自回归模型中。通过以多个时间分辨率联合学习体现视频，我们的模型捕获了长视野任务进展和细粒度交互动态，将基础世界模型转换为可扩展的体现数据引擎，用于下游策略学习和未来的代理体现系统。Despite these advances, existing approaches typically synthesize isolated images, simulation assets, or short robot videos independently, without jointly modeling controllable scene generation, embodied transfer, and long-horizon trajectory rollout. In contrast, our work unifies embodied scene generation, embodied transfer, interleaved subtask-subgoal prediction, and embodied video generation within a single autoregressive model. By jointly learning embodied videos at multiple temporal resolutions, our model captures both long-horizon task progression and fine-grained interaction dynamics, transforming foundation world models into scalable embodied data engines for downstream policy learning and future agentic embodied systems.

To the best of our knowledge,小米机器人-U0Xiaomi-Robotics-U0是第一个支持高质量多视图体现生成和顺序体现视频生成的统一体现合成模型，也是第一个引入用于可扩展体现数据生成的结构化控制公式的模型。大量实验证明了单步和顺序体现生成任务的最先进性能，在人类评估方面优于 GPT-Image-2.0，在 World Arena 基准上取得了最先进的结果，并通过生成的数据大幅提高了下游机器人策略的稳健性。我们希望这项工作为实现可扩展的实体智能代理数据引擎迈出了实际的一步。is the first unified embodied synthesis model supporting both high-quality multi-view embodied generation and sequential embodied video generation, as well as the first to introduce a structured control formulation for scalable embodied data generation. Extensive experiments demonstrate state-of-the-art performance across both single-step and sequential embodied generation tasks, outperforming GPT-Image-2.0 in human evaluation, achieving state-of-the-art results on the World Arena benchmark, and substantially improving the robustness of downstream robot policies through generated data. We hope this work provides a practical step toward scalable agentic data engines for embodied intelligence.

尽管取得了令人鼓舞的结果，Despite the promising results,小米机器人-U0Xiaomi-Robotics-U0有几个限制。首先，具体传输仍然依赖深度估计作为中间表示，这可能会引入偶尔的伪影并限制对对象纹理和局部外观的细粒度控制。未来的工作将直接研究原始观察结果的具体多视图图像编辑。其次，具体场景生成和视频生成是分开执行的，使得长期部署容易受到累积错误的影响；联合生成场景和后续视频是一个有前途的方向。最后，当前的 32K 上下文窗口限制了长时间的视频建模，将模型扩展到更长的上下文将能够生成分钟级的具体交互。has several limitations. First, embodied transfer still relies on depth estimation as an intermediate representation, which may introduce occasional artifacts and limit fine-grained control over object textures and local appearances. Future work will investigate embodied multi-view image editing directly on raw observations. Second, embodied scene generation and video generation are performed separately, making long-horizon rollout susceptible to accumulated errors; jointly generating scenes and subsequent videos is a promising direction. Finally, the current 32K context window limits long-duration video modeling, and extending the model to longer contexts will enable minute-level embodied interaction generation.
