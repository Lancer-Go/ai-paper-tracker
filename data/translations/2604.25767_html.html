扩大残疾学生公共教育的短期和长期影响The Short- and Long-Term Impacts of Expanding Public Education for Disabled Students

摘要Abstract

1949 年至 1980 年间，美国各州都强制公立学校为残疾学生提供教育服务。这是美国历史上最大的教育改革之一，但对其影响却知之甚少。鉴于这一时期的数据稀缺，我编制了调查和行政数据集，并利用任务时间的变化建立了双重差异设计。我表明这些指令增加了对残疾学生的服务和学前教育的入学率。成年后，在强制执行时学龄以下的残疾人没有受过教育的可能性降低了约 20%，受教育年限增加了 0.23 年，并且更有可能参加工作。尽管这项政策可能会夺走非残疾学生的资源，但事实上，非残疾个人的教育和就业也有所增加。这些效果与这些规定使每个学生的支出增加了 15% 的证据相一致。家庭也受到影响：这些规定增加了残疾儿童母亲的就业以及残疾人成为户主的可能性。从长远来看，这些任务通过产生超过其成本的政府收入来收回成本。这些结果提供了新的证据，证明扩大残疾学生的教育机会会产生巨大而广泛的影响。Between 1949 and 1980, every U.S. state mandated public schools to provide educational services for disabled students. This is one of the largest education reforms in U.S. history, but little is known about its impacts. Given scarce data in this period, I compile survey and administrative datasets and set up a difference-in-difference design using variation in the mandates’ timing. I show that the mandates increased both services for disabled students and preschool enrollments. In adulthood, disabled individuals below school age at a mandate’s implementation became about 20% less likely to have no education, attained up to 0.23 more years of education, and were more likely to have worked. Although this policy could have taken away resources from non-disabled students, in fact, education and employment also increased for non-disabled individuals. These effects align with evidence that the mandates increased spending per student by up to 15%. Families were also impacted: the mandates increased employment among mothers of disabled children and the probability that disabled individuals became household heads. Over the long term, the mandates paid for themselves by generating government revenues in excess of their cost. These results provide new evidence on the large, broad impacts of expanding access to education for disabled students.

1 简介Introduction

从历史上看，许多公立学校不为残疾学生提供教育服务或支持（也称为特殊教育）。相反，残疾学生在教育方面遇到困难，或者完全被公立学校排除在外。但是，在 1949 年至 1980 年间，随着积极分子推动改善残疾学生的教育，美国每个州都颁布了立法，要求公立学校识别患有各种残疾的儿童并为其提供教育服务。这可以说是美国近代历史上最大的公共教育扩张之一，有可能使与启蒙计划等其他主要项目一样多的学生获得教育机会。如今，美国约 15% 的学生接受这些服务Historically, many public schools did not provide educational services or support for students with disabilities (also known as special education). Instead, disabled students struggled in their education or were excluded from public schools altogether. But, between 1949 and 1980, as activists pushed to improve the education of disabled students, every state in the US enacted legislation mandating public schools to identify and provide educational services for children with all types of disabilities. This is arguably one of the largest expansions of public education in recent US history, potentially enabling access to education for as many students as other major programs like Head Start. Today, about 15% of students in the US receive these services[ncesStudentsDisabilities2024].

鉴于它们影响了如此多的学生，这些服务可能具有巨大而广泛的经济影响，理解这些是政策的关键问题。在本文中，我研究了这些国家规定对残疾学生及其非残疾同龄人从学前到成年的因果影响，以及对教育、劳动力市场结果和家庭生活等广泛结果的因果影响。Given that they affected so many students, these services have potentially large and wide-reaching economic impacts, and understanding these is a key question for policy. In this paper, I study the causal impacts of these state mandates on disabled students and their non-disabled peers from preschool to adulthood and on a wide range of outcomes, including education, labor market outcomes, and family life.

在任务要求各州为残疾学生提供服务之前，很少收集有关他们教育的数据，部分原因是它被认为不属于公立学校系统的范围或只是公立学校系统的边缘部分。尽管这些任务很重要，但数据的缺乏可能是导致这些任务前期工作稀缺的一个可能原因。我首先收集每个州相关立法的数据库。为了研究相关结果，我从调查、行政和人口普查来源中确定并编译了许多数据集。据我所知，这些数据源使我能够对这些任务进行首次定量因果研究，并就残疾学生的教育机会如何影响他们的经济和家庭生活提供详细的新证据。Before the mandates required states to provide services for disabled students, little data was collected on their education, in part because it was considered outside the scope of or only a marginal part of the public school system. This lack of data is one possible reason for the scarcity of prior work on these mandates despite their importance. I begin by assembling a database of the relevant legislation in each state. To study relevant outcomes, I identify and compile a number of datasets from survey, administrative, and Census sources. Together, these data sources allow me to conduct, to my knowledge, the first quantitative causal study of these mandates and to offer detailed new evidence on how access to education for disabled students impacts their economic and family lives.

利用各州之间强制执行时间的差异和交错的双重差异设计，我分别估计了残疾和非残疾学生的因果影响。 我使用一个新颖的州级数据集验证了这一实证设计，该数据集涉及 1952 年以来接受残疾服务的学生人数。Using variation in the timing of the mandates between states and a staggered difference-in-difference design, I estimate causal impacts separately for both disabled and non-disabled students. I validate this empirical design using a novel state-level dataset on the number of students receiving services for a disability from 1952 forward.

使用这种设计和关于个人结果的几个附加数据集，我发现强制为残疾学生提供服务可以改善残疾人的成年教育和劳动力市场结果，并对他们的父母和同龄人产生积极的溢出效应。我以之前的工作为基础，该工作显示了为残疾学生提供考试成绩等短期结果服务的好处，但很少能够观察到学生退出教育之外的结果（例如，Using this design and several additional datasets on individuals’ outcomes, I find that mandating the provision of services for disabled students improved adulthood education and labor market outcomes for disabled individuals and had positive spillovers for their parents and peers. I build on previous work that has shown the benefits of providing services for disabled students for short-term outcomes like test scores, but which has rarely been able to observe outcomes beyond a student’s exit from education (eg,[ballisLongRunImpactsSpecial2021, hurwitzSpecialEducationIndividualized2019]）。我添加了证据，证明这些服务对更广泛的结果产生积极影响，甚至持续到成年期。我还使用学校财务数据来了解学校支出如何充当这些影响的机制。在整篇论文中，我提供的证据排除了除了授权之外可以解释这些成果的因素。). I add evidence on the positive impacts of these services on a broader set of outcomes extending even later into adulthood. I also use data on school finances to understand how school spending may act as a mechanism for these impacts. Throughout the paper, I present evidence ruling out factors other than the mandates that could explain these gains.

我首先要表明，这些规定立即改善了残疾学生在学校获得的服务。为了分析个人获得的服务，我使用了一项鲜为人知的健康调查，即国家健康检查调查 (NHES)，该调查提供了有关儿童残疾及其在此期间在学校获得的服务的最丰富的信息之一。 我发现这些规定使残疾学生更有可能获得学校推荐以接受服务并实际接受这些服务，增加了约 20 个百分点。残疾学生也更有可能被转入“特殊教育”班级，并且不太可能经常缺课。I begin by showing that the mandates resulted in immediate improvements in the services disabled students received at school. To analyze the services received on the individual level, I use a little-known health survey, the National Health Examination Survey (NHES), which gives one of the richest pictures of children’s disabilities and the services they received at school over this period. I find that the mandates made disabled students much more likely to receive a school recommendation to receive services and to actually receive these services, a large increase of about 20 percentage points. Disabled students also became more likely to be transferred into “special education” classes and less likely to be frequently absent from school.

我表明，除了改善残疾学生获得的服务外，这些规定还提高了学校的整体入学率。我使用当前人口调查 (CPS) 10 月补充中的个人入学数据来量化这一增长。根据其规定，这些命令导致学前班入学人数大幅增加。他们还增加了义务教育年龄以上学生的入学率，这表明学生在校学习的时间更长。随着公共教育提供的服务的改善，我发现这些任务导致了从私立教育向公立教育的转变。Along with improving the services received by disabled students, I show that the mandates increased overall school enrollments. I quantify this increase using individual-level data on enrollment from the Current Population Survey (CPS) October Supplement. In line with their provisions, the mandates caused large increases in preschool enrollments. They also increased enrollments among students over the age of compulsory schooling, indicating that students stayed in school longer. As the services offered by public education improved, I find that the mandates caused shifts from private to public education.

在表明这些任务扩大了向残疾人提供的公立学校服务和入学人数之后，我接下来提出证据表明它们提高了成年后的教育程度。为了研究成年期的结果，我使用了 1970 年至 2007 年人口普查和美国社区调查 (ACS) 的数据。我使用了类似的双重差异设计，利用个人出生年份相对于所在州立法时间的变化。我发现，到 25 岁至 35 岁之间，这些强制令使残疾人的受教育程度大幅提高。任务执行时学龄以下的残疾人到 12 年级的受教育程度平均提高了约 0.23 年。他们的教育成果很差（例如根本不上学）的可能性也大大降低。Having shown that the mandates expanded the public school services available to disabled individuals and school enrollments, I next present evidence that they improved educational attainment in adulthood. To study adulthood outcomes, I use data from the Census and American Community Survey (ACS) from 1970-2007. I use a similar difference-in-differences design, exploiting variation in an individual’s birth year relative to the timing of the legislation in their state. I find that, by age 25-35, the mandates caused large improvements in educational attainment among disabled individuals. Disabled individuals under school age at the time of the mandates’ implementation had an average increase in educational attainment of approximately 0.23 years through grade 12. They also became much less likely to have very poor education outcomes, such as no schooling at all.

谈到对非残疾同龄人的影响，任何溢出效应的理论迹象可能是积极的，也可能是消极的：公共教育质量的提高可能有利于非残疾学生，但将资源从他们身上转移可能反而会伤害他们。我的证据表明溢出效应是积极的。使用类似于残疾人的双重差分设计，我发现，在本州颁布强制令时，年轻的非残疾人的教育程度也有所提高，学龄以下的人的受教育年限平均增加了 0.24 年。然而，这种增长发生在更高层次的教育中：虽然残疾学生的中小学教育程度大多有所提高，但非残疾学生的高等教育程度却有所提高。Turning to impacts on their non-disabled peers, the theoretical sign of any spillover effect could be positive or negative: improvements in the quality of public education could benefit non-disabled students, but redirecting resources away from them could instead hurt them. My evidence suggests that spillovers are positive. Using a difference-in-difference design analogous to that used for disabled individuals, I find that non-disabled individuals who were young when a mandate was enacted in their state also experienced an increase in educational attainment, with an average increase of 0.24 years of education for those under school age. However, this increase occurred at higher levels of education: while disabled students mostly experienced increases in their primary and secondary education, non-disabled students experienced increases in higher education.

为什么这些规定会产生如此积极的影响，特别是对于非残疾人？我使用州和学区财务数据库来解开这些改进的机制。正如预期的那样，鉴于授权不包括州资助条款，州政府没有根据这些授权增加支出。相反，学区通过地方财产税来资助增加的支出。从长远来看，在公共教育就业增加的推动下，学区每名学生的支出大幅增长约 15%。根据之前关于增加资金对教育程度影响的工作的估计，我强调，这种支出的增加可以合理地解释非残疾学生所经历的积极溢出效应的很大一部分。Why did the mandates have such positive impacts, especially for non-disabled individuals? I unpack the mechanism for these improvements using a database of state and school district finances. As expected given that the mandates did not include state funding provisions, state governments did not increase spending in response to these mandates. Instead, school districts funded increases in expenditures via local property taxes. In the long term, school districts experienced large increases of about 15% in spending per student, driven by increased employment in public education. Using estimates from prior work of the impact of increased funding on educational attainment, I highlight that this increase in spending can plausibly explain a sizeable share of the positive spillover experienced by non-disabled students.

我发现，教育的这些进步也伴随着残疾人和非残疾人劳动力市场结果的改善。在 25 岁至 35 岁之间，这些规定将残疾人拥有一定工作经验的可能性提高了 2.9 个百分点，并减少了无法工作的人获得相同金额的社会保障残疾福利。对于非残疾人来说，这些任务使就业增加了 2.8 个百分点，收入也相应大幅增加。I find that these improvements in education also came with improvements in labor market outcomes for both disabled and non-disabled individuals. At age 25-35, the mandates increased the probability of disabled individuals having some work experience by 2.9 percentage points and reduced the receipt of Social Security disability benefits for those unable to work by the same amount. For non-disabled individuals, the mandates increased employment by 2.8 percentage points, with correspondingly large increases in income.

这些任务还对另一个群体产生了积极的溢出效应：残疾儿童的父母。在这项规定出台之前，残疾儿童的母亲可能对失学或在学校学习困难的儿童承担更大的照顾责任。根据任务规定，我发现残疾儿童母亲的就业有所增加。The mandates also had positive spillovers for another group: the parents of disabled children. Before the mandates, mothers of disabled children may have had greater care responsibilities for children who were out of school or struggling in school. Following the mandates, I show that employment of mothers of disabled children increased.

除了教育和就业之外，这些指令的影响还延伸到残疾人的家庭和社会生活。正如预期的那样，如果教育的改善使他们更加独立，我发现有证据表明，在成年后，受这些义务影响的残疾人更有可能成为自己的家庭主宰并成为父母。Beyond education and employment, the mandates’ impacts also extended into the families and social lives of disabled people. As would be expected if improved education led them to be more independent, I find evidence that, in adulthood, disabled people affected by the mandates became more likely to head their own households and to become parents.

最后，我量化了这些任务的货币成本和收益。我发现这些任务对受影响的个人来说有很大的金钱利益，而且从公共基金的角度来看，这些任务以收入增加而增加税收的形式收回成本。除了这些金钱利益之外，这些规定对残疾人的最大好处（例如整体福祉的改善）很可能无法量化。Finally, I quantify the monetary costs and benefits of the mandates. I find that the mandates have large monetary benefits for affected individuals and, from a public funds perspective, pay for themselves in the form of increased tax revenue resulting from higher incomes. On top of these monetary benefits, it is likely that the largest benefits of these mandates for disabled individuals, such as overall improved wellbeing, are not quantifiable.

2 美国残疾学生教育服务的历史History of educational services for disabled students in the United States

1949 年至 1980 年间，美国每个州都执行了一项任务，要求公立学校为残疾学生提供教育服务。尽管美国的特殊教育体系有着悠久的历史，但大多数残疾学生在这些规定之前并未获得足够的教育支持。这些指令主要在 20 世纪 70 年代颁布，其动机是推动更加平等的教育运动和残疾儿童家长的积极行动。Between 1949 and 1980, every state in the United States implemented a mandate requiring public schools to provide educational services for disabled students. Despite a long history of special education systems in the US, the majority of disabled students did not receive adequate support for their education before these mandates. The mandates, enacted mostly in the 1970s, were motivated by a movement for more equal education and activism by parents of disabled children.

4 对残疾学生接受教育服务的影响Impacts on educational services received by disabled students

这些规定是否真正改善了为残疾学生提供的服务？在本节中，我首先描述这一时期学生接受的最常见服务类型。然后我表明，这些规定导致残疾学生获得服务的可能性大幅增加。他们还增加了公立学校的入学率，这主要受到三个重大转变的推动：(1) 幼儿教育的增加，(2) 义务教育年龄以上学生的增加，以及 (3) 从私立教育向公立教育的转变。Did the mandates actually improve the services provided to disabled students?
