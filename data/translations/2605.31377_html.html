1. 简介Introduction

新闻检索的目的是识别一组全面的新闻 现代新闻系统中下游排名、推荐和内容分析的相关文章News retrieval aims to identify a comprehensive set of relevant articles for downstream ranking, recommendation, and content analysis in modern news systems(Tang et al., 2025)。 然而，该领域的用户查询通常是抽象的或不明确的，特别是在处理新出现的事件、突发事件或不断发展的主题时，使得有效的语义查询扩展成为高召回率检索的关键先决条件. However, user queries in this domain are often abstract or underspecified, particularly when addressing emerging events, breaking stories, or evolving topics, making effective semantic query expansion a critical prerequisite for high-recall retrieval(Carpineto and Romano, 2012; Lavrenko and Croft, 2001; Li et al., 2026)。此外，新闻语料库表现出明显的时间动态：新文章不断发表，而以前的相关文章很快就会过时，导致相关性分布频繁且突然的变化. Moreover, news corpora exhibit pronounced temporal dynamics: new articles are continuously published while previously relevant articles quickly become outdated, leading to frequent and abrupt shifts in relevance distributions(Li and Croft, 2003; Elsas and Dumais, 2010)。这些特征要求检索机制既. These characteristics demand retrieval mechanisms that are both语义表达semantically expressive和and时间自适应temporally adaptive.

传统新闻检索方法通过查询扩展解决语义稀疏和时间漂移问题Traditional news retrieval methods address semantic sparsity and temporal drift through query expansion(Carpineto and Romano, 2012)和新鲜度感知排名and freshness-aware ranking(Piryani et al., 2026)。 伪相关反馈和基于语言模型的扩展等技术丰富了未指定的查询，以提高召回率. Techniques such as pseudo-relevance feedback and language-model-based expansion enrich under-specified queries to improve recall(Lavrenko and Croft, 2001)，而互补方法利用时间信号来优先考虑最近或时间上对齐的内容, while complementary approaches leverage temporal signals to prioritize recent or temporally aligned content(Li and Croft, 2003; Elsas and Dumais, 2010)。然而，这些方法通常以特定的、按查询的方式执行扩展，并且缺乏随时间演变的持久语义结构。这限制了他们平衡有效性和快速适应现实世界环境中不断变化的相关性的能力。. However, these methods typically perform expansion in an ad hoc, per-query manner and lack persistent semantic structures that evolve over time. This limits their ability to balance effectiveness and rapid adaptation to shifting relevance in real-world settings.

最近，检索增强生成（RAG）已成为标准范式 用于在外部知识中奠定语言模型的基础，提高语义覆盖率 在知识密集型和时间敏感的信息访问环境中Recently, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) has become a standard paradigm for grounding language models in external knowledge, improving semantic coverage in knowledge-intensive and time-sensitive information access settings(Lewis et al., 2020; Izacard and Grave, 2021; Borgeaud et al., 2022; Vu et al., 2024)。对经典 RAG 的代理扩展引入了规划、迭代查询重构和自我完善，从而促进更具表现力和适应性的语义扩展. Agentic extensions to classical RAG introduce planning, iterative query reformulation, and self-refinement, fostering more expressive and adaptive semantic expansion(Yao et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Asai et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2024)。虽然对复杂推理有效，但许多代理 RAG 方法通过短视野、每个查询的推理循环进行操作，通过冗余、动态扩展产生显着的延迟和令牌成本。这种推理和检索的紧密耦合阻碍了跨查询或时间的重用，限制了它们在时间敏感、高效的新闻环境中的可行性. While effective for complex reasoning, many agentic RAG approaches operate via short-horizon, per-query inference loops, incurring significant latency and token costs through redundant, on-the-fly expansions. This tight coupling of reasoning and retrieval hinders reuse across queries or time, limiting their viability in time-sensitive, efficient news settings(Wang et al., 2024; Singh et al., 2026).

现有代理 RAG 系统的一个基本限制是，语义扩展被视为每个查询推理的瞬态副产品，而不是持久资产。这种设计未能利用两个关键机会：重用和关系建模。由于扩展在每次请求后都会被丢弃，因此系统会遭受冗余计算，并且无法随着时间的推移积累语义知识。因此，它无法对子主题如何相互关联或查询的各个方面如何演变进行建模。这在新闻检索中尤其有害，因为高级意图（例如，“消费电子产品中的人工智能”）保持稳定，但特定的子主题和表面实现每天都在变化。当前的方法往往会过度拟合最近的显着术语（例如“人工智能芯片”），同时忽略语义上连贯但不太突出的子主题，例如“设备上推理”或“保护隐私的个性化”。随着时间的推移，短暂扩展和持久意图之间的结构不匹配会导致相关但尚未充分探索的内容的系统回忆丢失。A fundamental limitation of existing agentic RAG systems is that semantic expansion is treated as a transient byproduct of per-query reasoning rather than a persistent asset. This design fails to capitalize on two critical opportunities: reuse and relational modeling. Because expansions are discarded after each request, the system suffers from redundant computation and cannot accumulate semantic knowledge over time. Consequently, it fails to model how subtopics relate to one another or how a query’s facets evolve. This is particularly detrimental in news retrieval, where high-level intent (e.g., ”AI in consumer electronics”) remains stable, but specific subtopics and surface realizations shift daily. Current methods tend to overfit recently salient terms (e.g., ”AI chips”) while neglecting semantically coherent but less prominent subtopics like ”on-device inference” or ”privacy-preserving personalization.” Over time, this structural mismatch between transient expansions and persistent intent causes systematic recall loss for relevant but underexplored content.

受这一观察的启发，我们认为语义扩展必须具体化为持久的、结构化的表示，以捕获主题之间的关系，而不是充当瞬态的工件。虽然现有的基于图的方法提高了匹配效率Motivated by this observation, we argue that semantic expansion must be materialized as a persistent, structured representation that captures the relationships between topics, rather than serving as a transient artifact. While prior graph-based methods improve matching efficiency(Edge et al., 2025; Zhu et al., 2025a)，他们未能解决如何通过代理推理构建此类结构或适应时间动态。为了弥补这一差距，我们建议, they fail to address how such structures can be constructed via agentic reasoning or adapted to temporal dynamics. To bridge this gap, we propose动态树DynaTree，一个将扩展与实时检索分离以实现重用的框架。在第一阶段，我们分摊代理 RAG 来构建映射查询语义空间的可重用检索树。在第二阶段，轻量级的每日子树选择机制适应不断变化的新闻分布，而无需重复的代理执行或模型重新训练。这种设计实现了高召回率和低延迟，使 DynaTree 成为对不断更新的语料库进行实时检索的理想选择。 最后，我们的方法已经被部署了 在生产级新闻检索系统中, a framework that decouples expansion from real-time retrieval to enable reuse. In the first stage, we amortize agentic RAG to build a reusable retrieval tree that maps the query’s semantic space. In the second stage, a lightweight daily subtree selection mechanism adapts to evolving news distributions without repeated agent execution or model retraining. This design achieves high recall with low latency, making DynaTree ideal for real-time retrieval over continuously updated corpora. 语义扩展和时间敏感信息检索Semantic Expansion and Time-Sensitive Information Retrieval

语义扩展一直是一项核心技术 用于提高抽象或未指定查询的召回率。 经典相关性反馈和概率相关性模型 提供丰富查询表示的原则性方法 使用伪相关文档Semantic expansion has been a central technique for improving recall on abstract or underspecified queries. Classic relevance feedback and probabilistic relevance models provide principled ways to enrich query representations using pseudo-relevant documents(Lavrenko and Croft, 2001)。 随后的调查总结了伪相关反馈 和基于知识的扩展 作为信息检索的核心范式. 5. 结论Conclusion

我们建议We propose动态树DynaTree，一个两阶段代理检索框架 用于有效且高效的新闻检索 在不断变化的时间分布下。 通过将语义扩展具体化为可重用的检索树 并将其构建与在线优化解耦， DynaTree 摊销代理推理成本 同时在紧凑的评估代理的指导下，通过动态子树选择实现轻量级的日常适应。 广泛的离线实验和直接在线部署 在 Syft 生产系统中 展示出相对于强大的 RAG 和代理基线的持续改进， 表明持久的语义结构 随着时间的推移，可以保持强大的召回率和排名质量。 这些结果凸显了结构感知的实用价值， 大规模真实世界新闻系统的代理检索 及其为用户提供更可靠的检索体验的潜力。, a two-stage agentic retrieval framework for effective and efficient news retrieval under evolving temporal distributions. By materializing semantic expansion as a reusable retrieval tree and decoupling its construction from online optimization, DynaTree amortizes agentic reasoning cost while enabling lightweight daily adaptation via dynamic subtree selection, guided by a compact evaluation proxy. Extensive offline experiments and direct online deployment in the Syft production system demonstrate consistent improvements over strong RAG and agentic baselines, showing that persistent semantic structures can maintain robust recall and ranking quality over time. These results highlight the practical value of structure-aware, agentic retrieval for large-scale, real-world news systems and its potential to deliver more reliable retrieval experiences to users.
