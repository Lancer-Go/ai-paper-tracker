摘要Abstract

在过去的一年里，空间智能越来越受到人们的关注。之前的很多著作都是从这个角度来研究它的Over the past year, spatial intelligence has drawn increasing attention. Many prior works study it from the perspective of视觉的visual-空间智能，模型可以通过视觉输入获取视觉空间信息。然而，在缺乏视觉信息的情况下，仅靠语言智能是否足以赋予模型空间智能，以及模型如何利用空间智能来执行相关任务？-spatial intelligence, where models have access to visuospatial information from visual inputs. However, in the absence of visual information, whether linguistic intelligence alone is sufficient to endow models with spatial intelligence, and how models perform relevant tasks with文本text-只有输入仍未被探索。因此，在本文中，我们从语言的角度关注空间智能的一个基本且关键的能力：视点旋转理解（VRU）。具体来说，要求 LLM 和 VLM 推断其最终视点，并在给定视点旋转和多个步骤观察的文本描述的环境中预测相应的观察结果。我们发现 LLM 和 VLM 在我们提出的数据集上表现都很差，而人类可以轻松达到 100% 的准确率，这表明当前模型能力与空间智能的要求之间存在巨大差距。为了揭示潜在机制，我们进行了分层探究分析和头部因果干预。我们的研究结果表明，尽管模型在隐藏状态中编码视点信息，但它们似乎很难将视点位置与相应的观察结合起来，导致最终层产生幻觉。最后，我们有选择地微调因果干预确定的关键注意力头，以提高 VRU 性能。实验结果表明，这种选择性微调可以提高 VRU 性能，同时避免通用能力的灾难性遗忘-only inputs still remain unexplored. Therefore, in this paper, we focus on a fundamental and critical capability in spatial intelligence from a linguistic perspective: viewpoint rotation understanding (VRU). Specifically, LLMs and VLMs are asked to infer their final viewpoint and predict the corresponding observation in an environment given textual description of viewpoint rotation and observation over multiple steps. We find that both LLMs and VLMs perform poorly on our proposed dataset while human can easily achieve 100% accuracy, indicating a substantial gap between current model capabilities and the requirements of spatial intelligence. To uncover the underlying mechanisms, we conduct a layer-wise probing analysis and head-wise causal intervention. Our findings reveal that although models encode viewpoint information in the hidden states, they appear to struggle to bind the viewpoint position with corresponding observation, resulting in a hallucination in final layers. Finally, we selectively fine-tune the key attention heads identified by causal intervention to improve VRU performance. 1 简介Introduction

随着大语言模型（LLM）和视觉语言模型（VLM）的快速发展，空间智能越来越受到关注With the rapid development of large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs), spatial intelligence has attracted increasing attention(Feng et al., 2025)。 空间智能通常涉及感知和精神上操纵空间关系. Spatial intelligence generally involves perceiving and mentally manipulating spatial relationships(Yang et al., 2025b)，并且需要无数的能力，例如关系推理, and requires myriad capabilities such as relational reasoning(Wang et al., 2024)，距离估计, distance estimation(Zhang et al., 2025b)、自我中心与非中心观点之间的观点转换, viewpoint transformation between egocentric and allocentric perspectives(Li et al., 2025b; Xue et al., 2025)等, etc.

在这项工作中，我们重点关注空间智能的基本和关键能力：In this work, we focus on a fundamental and critical capability in spatial intelligence:视点旋转理解viewpoint rotation understanding (VRU) (Zhang et al., 2025a)。具体来说，我们研究模型在经历一系列视点旋转并接收观察结果后是否能够准确推断环境中的最终位置和相应的观察结果。. Specifically, we investigate whether models can accurately infer the final position and the corresponding observation in an environment, after undergoing a sequence of viewpoint rotations and receiving observations.

许多先前的研究Many prior studies(Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2025b; Zhang et al., 2025c; Ma et al., 2024)已努力使用视觉数据进行基准测试和提高空间智能。然而，值得注意的是，空间智能的存在与感官形态无关，即使是盲人也可以通过其他感官感知空间have taken efforts on benchmarking and improving the spatial intelligence using visual data. Regrettably, whether VLMs and LLMs can comprehend viewpoint rotation (i.e., VRU) without visual information still remains underexplored. 为了揭示 VRU 性能不佳的内在机制，从而为构建具有更强空间智能的高级模型提供见解，我们进行了全面的可解释性研究，包括分层和头部分析。具体来说，为了评估LLM和VLM编码视点旋转信息的能力，我们在VRUBench上进行了分层探测分析。我们发现模型在每一步都具有很强的编码视点旋转方向和角度的能力，而编码绝对方向的能力在早期到中间的层中逐渐出现，并在后面的层中逐渐减弱。To uncover the intrinsic mechanisms underlying the poor VRU performance, which could provide insights for building advanced models with stronger spatial intelligence, we conduct comprehensive interpretability studies including layer-wise and head-wise analysis. Concretely, to evaluate the capabilities of LLMs and VLMs for encoding the information of viewpoint rotation, we conduct a layer-wise probing analysis on VRUBench. We find that models have a strong capability to encode the direction and angle of the viewpoint rotation at each step, while the capability of encoding absolute orientation gradually emerges in early-to-middle layers and diminishes in later layers.

Experimental results demonstrates that selective fine-tuning can achieve enhanced VRU performance with only 50% of the GPU hours required for full fine-tuning, while preserving the general capabilities.

对于数据集合成，我们构建了一个包含 100 个可能出现在真实室内房间中的对象的模拟环境。视点旋转角度For dataset synthesis, we construct a simulated environment containing 100 objects that may appear in real indoor rooms. LLM 与 VLMLLMs vs. VLMs

除了人类评估结果之外，我们还观察到不同模型类型之间的系统差异，即 VLM 通常优于 LLM。这种优势不仅在不同的模型系列中可见（例如，Qwen2.5-VL-7B 与 LLaMA2-7B），而且在共享相同架构的 VLM 及其相应的 LLM 主干之间也一致存在（例如，Qwen2.5-VL-7B 与 Qwen2.5-7B）。它表明，尽管在推理时没有可用的视觉信息，但使用视觉数据训练的 VLM 仍然表现出比从未接触过视觉数据的 LLM 更强的 VRU 能力。Apart from the human evaluation results, we also observe systematic differences across model types, that is, VLMs generally outperform LLMs. This advantage is observed not only across different model families (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-7B vs. LLaMA2-7B), but also consistently between VLMs and their corresponding LLM backbones sharing the same architecture (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-7B vs. Qwen2.5-7B). It demonstrates that although no visual information is available at inference time, VLMs trained with visual data still exhibit stronger VRU ability than LLMs that have never been exposed to visual data.
