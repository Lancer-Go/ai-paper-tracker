摘要Abstract

通过对比学习利用多模态大语言模型（MLLM）已成为提高通用多模态检索（UMR）性能的主流范例。 然而之前的作品都忽略了Leveraging Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) via contrastive learning has become a mainstream paradigm for improving the performance of Universal Multimodal Retrieval (UMR). However, previous works have ignored the谷物盲症grain blindness将对比范式适应检索任务时。 粒度盲目性是指模型倾向于忽略查询中包含的粒度级信息，这对于有效处理复杂查询至关重要。 这源于对比学习将样本视为二元分类（正/负），而忽略了每个负样本所携带的不同信息。 为了解决这个问题，我们认为应该根据负样本与正样本的相似性来区别对待，使模型能够从每个负样本中学习不同的颗粒信息。 在本文中，我们介绍了一个简单但有效的框架，称为 ELVA，这是一种基于规则的新型 RL 框架，可通过排名驱动的 MLLM 来减轻颗粒盲目性。 1）我们不依赖奖励模型，而是通过可验证的奖励扩展强化学习 （RLVR）到检索任务，允许模型在没有明确的排名标签的情况下探索新的排名行为。 2）通过利用基于规则的奖励，我们的方法联合优化负样本的排名，同时扩大正负样本之间的相似度差距。 为了更精确地衡量粒盲性，我们进一步引入了专门针对多粒查询场景设计的新基准MRBench。 ELVA 在标准检索基准测试中取得了最先进的结果，其在 MRBench 上显着提升了 13.1%，进一步证明了其在缓解谷物盲目性方面的有效性。when adapting the contrastive paradigm into retrieval tasks. Grain blindness refers to the tendency of the model to overlook grain-level information contained in the query, which is crucial for effectively handling complex queries. This stems from contrastive learning treating samples as a binary classification (positive/negative), while ignoring the different information carried by each negative sample. To address this, we argue that negatives should be treated differently according to their similarity to the positive sample, enabling the model to learn distinct grain information from each negative. In this paper, we introduce a simple but effective framework, called ELVA, a novel rule-based RL framework that mitigates grain blindness through ranking-driven MLLMs. 1) Instead of relying on reward models, we extend Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) to retrieval tasks, allowing the model to explore new ranking behaviors without explicit ranking labels. 2) By utilizing rule-based rewards, our approach jointly optimizes the ranking of negative samples while enlarging the similarity gap between positive and negative. To more precisely measure grain blindness, we further introduce MRBench, a new benchmark specifically designed for multi-grain query scenarios. ELVA achieves state-of-the-art results across standard retrieval benchmarks, and its notable 13.1% improvement on MRBench further demonstrates its effectiveness in alleviating grain blindness.

1 简介Introduction

通用多模态检索（UMR）是指一种通用检索范式，它将不同的检索任务统一在一个框架内，并能够泛化到未见过的检索任务Universal Multimodal Retrieval (UMR) refers to a general retrieval paradigm that unifies diverse retrieval tasks within a single framework and enables generalization to unseen retrieval tasks[liu2025lamra, lyu2025puma, zhu2025retrv, kong2025modality]。 这标志着与之前的工作相比的重大转变，之前的工作主要关注特定于模态的检索任务，包括文本到文本. This marks a substantial shift from prior efforts, which primarily focused on modality-specific retrieval tasks, including text-to-text[zhao2024dense, ma2024fine], 文本转图像, text-to-image[fu2024linguistic, zhang2020context]，以及图像到图像, and image-to-image[baldrati2022effective, saito2023pic2word]检索。 最近，研究人员开始探索多模态大型语言模型（MLLM）retrieval. Recently, researchers have begun exploring Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)[li2024llava, wang2024qwen2, dong2025scalable, xiaomi2025mimo]对于 UMR，利用其广泛的预训练知识和强大的泛化能力。 由于 MLLM 最初是针对生成目标（例如，下一个标记预测）进行训练的，因此最近的工作通过对比学习将其适应检索任务，有效地将其生成能力转移到检索任务for UMR, leveraging their extensive pretrained knowledge and strong generalization ability. Since MLLMs are originally trained for generative objectives (e.g., next-token prediction), recent works have adapted them to retrieval tasks via contrastive learning, effectively transferring their generative abilities to retrieval tasks[gu2025breaking, meng2025vlm2vec, zhang2025qwen3, lin2025sail].

尽管现有方法取得了令人印象深刻的性能，但我们的研究表明它们仍然存在以下问题Although existing methods achieve impressive performance, our study reveals that they still suffer from谷物盲症grain blindness当将对比范式适应检索任务时。 粒度盲目性是指模型忽略查询中包含的粒度级别信息的倾向。 因此，这些方法很难应对多粒度查询，如图所示when adapting the contrastive paradigm to retrieval tasks. Grain blindness refers to the tendency of the model to overlook grain-level information contained in a query. Consequently, these methods struggle with multi-grain queries, as shown in Figure1。 在这种情况下，查询包含多个级别的颗粒信息，例如“喷火”动作和“Charmander”实体，这对模型全面捕获多颗粒信息的能力提出了很高的要求。 为了解决谷物失明问题，我们的研究强调了需要面对的两个关键挑战。. In such a case, the query contains multiple levels of grain information, such as the action “fire-breathing" and the entity “Charmander", which place high demands on the model’s ability to comprehensively capture multi-grain information. To tackle the grain blindness, our study highlights two key challenges that need to be confronted.

3.4.2 负采样策略Negative Sampling Strategy

我们引入了专为 RL 阶段量身定制的平衡负采样策略。之前的工作We introduce a balanced negative sampling strategy tailored for the RL stage. Prior work[liu2025lamra]直接对 SFT 的前 100 个候选进行采样，我们发现这对于 RL 来说是有问题的：当候选与查询过于相似时，奖励分布变得太窄，产生微弱或消失的梯度directly sampled the top-100 candidates for SFT, which we find problematic for RL: when candidates are overly similar to the query, the reward distribution becomes too narrow, yielding weak or vanishing gradients[yu2025dapo]并阻碍有效的政策学习。 为了解决这个问题，我们使用均衡的否定组合构建每个查询的候选集： (1) 50 个过滤后的硬负例，通过删除相似度阈值以上的候选者并选择剩余的前 50 个获得and hindering effective policy learning. To address this, we construct each query’s candidate set using a balanced mix of negatives: (1) 50 filtered hard negatives, obtained by removing candidates above a similarity threshold and selecting the top 50 remaining ones[li2025u, gu2025breaking];和 (2) 从整个库中随机抽取 50 个阴性样本。 这种组合增加了奖励方差并提供了更丰富的学习信号：过滤子集提供了受控难度以增强排名能力，而随机子集增加了分布多样性并防止过度拟合。 总的来说，这种混合采样策略为 RL 优化产生了更稳定、信息更丰富的信号。; and (2) 50 randomly sampled negatives from the full pool. This combination increases reward variance and provides richer learning signals: the filtered subset offers controlled difficulty to enhance ranking capability, while the random subset adds distributional diversity and prevents overfitting. Overall, this mixed sampling strategy yields a more stable and informative signal for RL optimization.

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 ELVA，这是一种新颖的框架，旨在通过对比范式与通用多模态检索 (UMR) 相适应 MLLM 来解决颗粒盲目性问题。 我们确定了解决颗粒盲目性的两个挑战：1）训练范式的属性对检索的影响； 2）如何在没有排名标签的情况下激励新能力。 我们还引入了一个专门设计用于评估谷物盲目性缓解的新基准。 大量的实验表明，ELVA取得了显着的收益，并有效缓解了谷粒盲症。 我们期望我们的框架将为推进多模式信息检索提供有意义的指导，并鼓励在该领域的持续探索。In this paper, we present ELVA, a novel framework designed to address the grain blindness when adapting the MLLMs via contrastive paradigm to Universal Multimodal Retrieval (UMR). We identify two challenges for addressing the grain blindness: 1) training paradigm’s properties impact on retrieval; 2) how to incentivize the new ability without the ranking labels. We also introduce a new benchmark specifically designed to evaluate the grain blindness mitigation. Extensive experiments demonstrate that ELVA achieves significant gains and effectively mitigates grain blindness. We expect that our framework will offer meaningful guidance for advancing multimodal information retrieval and encourage continued exploration in this domain.
