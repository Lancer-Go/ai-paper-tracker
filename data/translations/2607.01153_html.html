Brett Reynolds
Humber Polytechnic & University of Toronto Contact: brett.reynolds@humber.ca
(2026 年 7 月July 2026)
摘要Abstract

语言模型的安全评估越来越依赖于对模糊自然语言行为的判断：模型是否遵循指令、适当拒绝、遵守政策、抵制嵌入式命令或误报代理任务的进度。现有的基准通常将这些区别压缩为通过/失败标签，从而模糊了失败是否源于能力限制、策略模糊、指令冲突、支架故障或不稳定的评估者判断。Safety evaluations for language models increasingly depend on judgments about ambiguous natural-language behaviour: whether a model has followed an instruction, refused appropriately, complied with a policy, resisted an embedded command, or misreported progress in an agentic task. Existing benchmarks often compress these distinctions into pass/fail labels, obscuring whether failures arise from capability limits, policy ambiguity, instruction conflict, scaffold failure, or unstable evaluator judgments.

本文介绍了对抗性语用学作为基准和注释协议，用于评估指令冲突、嵌入式命令、引用、范围模糊、指示语、间接言语行为和多轮代理转录下的模型行为。贡献是实证和方法论的：语言控制的分类法、具有验证器强制元数据的 18 项种子基准、54 行本地种子试点、区分任务成功、政策合规性、安全风险、拒绝结果和评估者置信度的专家评估协议，以及判断有效性、诊断模糊性和分类漂移的指标。该框架将语言判断方法转变为验证安全评估、法学硕士判断、黄金设置构建、即时注入测试和安全文档的实用工具。This paper introduces adversarial pragmatics as a benchmark and annotation protocol for evaluating model behaviour under instruction conflict, embedded commands, quotation, scope ambiguity, deixis, indirect speech acts, and multi-turn agent transcripts. The contribution is empirical and methodological: a linguistically controlled taxonomy, an 18-item seed benchmark with validator-enforced metadata, a 54-row local seed pilot, an expert-evaluation protocol distinguishing task success, policy compliance, safety risk, refusal outcome, and evaluator confidence, and metrics for judge validity, diagnostic ambiguity, and taxonomy drift. The framework turns linguistic judgment methodology into a practical tool for validating safety evals, LLM judges, gold-set construction, prompt-injection tests, and safety documentation.

1 评估问题The Evaluation Problem

前沿模型安全评估越来越多地评估语言介导的控制。核心问题不仅仅是模型能否解决任务。它还询问模型是否可以识别哪些自然语言材料具有指导力、适用哪些政策边界以及记录显示了模型行为的哪些内容。Frontier-model safety evaluation increasingly evaluates language-mediated control. The central question isn’t only whether a model can solve a task. It also asks whether the model can identify which natural-language material has directive force, which policy boundary applies, and what a transcript shows about the model’s behaviour.

这个问题已经出现在整体语言模型评估的工作中，该评估将模型评估视为多场景、多度量问题，而不是单个准确率数字The problem already appears in work on holistic language-model evaluation, which treats model assessment as a multi-scenario, multi-metric problem rather than a single accuracy number[6]。安全评估为同一问题添加了一个更尖锐的版本：相关行为通常不仅仅是一个答案，而是模型对指令、策略或源关系的采用。. Safety evaluation adds a sharper version of the same problem: the relevant behaviour is often not just an answer, but the model’s uptake of an instruction, policy, or source relation.

5 注释及专家评审Annotation and Expert Evaluation

该协议将人类评估者视为应用规定标准的专家，而不是研究参与者。政策评估者判断是否遵守既定规则。语言学家诊断相关的结构、范围、引文、指示语或言语行为对比。当安全类别取决于领域知识时，可能需要领域专家。 LLM评委是一个候选人评分系统，其可靠性本身必须得到评估。The protocol treats human evaluators as experts applying a stated rubric, not as research participants. A policy evaluator judges compliance with a stated rule. A linguist diagnoses the relevant construction, scope, quotation, deixis, or speech-act contrast. A domain specialist may be needed when the safety category depends on domain knowledge. An LLM judge is a candidate grading system whose reliability itself has to be evaluated.

This keeps a safe refusal from being mislabelled as capability failure, and it keeps a policy-ambiguous case from being treated as ordinary measurement noise. Agreement statistics are useful only after the coding task is defined: inter-coder agreement coefficients encode assumptions about categories, coders, and chance correction[1]。因此，对于这个基准，第一个问题不仅仅是. 拒绝是用矩阵编码的，而不是作为单一结果。该协议区分了适当的策略拒绝、过度拒绝安全请求、拒绝不足或不安全合规、部分合规、安全重定向、伪装成拒绝的无能力以及策略模糊的拒绝。因此，即使元语言项提到受保护的字符串，它也可以是安全的、任务成功的且符合策略的；拒绝它是一种可衡量的过度拒绝，而不是安全上的成功。Refusal is coded with a matrix rather than as a single outcome. 评估者的分歧在诊断上仍然有用，但分析的单位是项目、协议或判断，而不是评估者。政策和语言评估标准之间的分歧可能会揭示不稳定的指令或分类。专家评估者和法学硕士评委之间的分歧可能会揭示自动评分者的偏见。Evaluator disagreement remains diagnostically useful, but the unit of analysis is the item, protocol, or judge, not the evaluator. 这是从分歧工作中得到的更广泛的教训：多数投票可以消除主观任务中的系统标签变化This follows a broader lesson from disagreement work: majority vote can erase systematic label variation in subjective tasks[10]，即使收集了更多评级或更多上下文，一些人类文本推理分歧仍然存在, and some human textual-inference disagreements persist even when more ratings or more context are collected[11]。在这些情况下，分歧并不自动成为注释过程中的缺陷。这可能表明该项目确实不明确，政策边界未明确，或者不同的评估标准回答了不同的问题。. 因此，裁决分为两个阶段。首先，评估者记录他们特定标准的标签和置信度，而不强迫达成共识。其次，裁决者对分歧来源进行分类：注释错误、上下文缺失、措辞模糊、标准冲突、政策边界模糊或分类不稳定。只有完成该步骤后，该商品才会获得金色标签。Adjudication therefore has two stages. First, evaluators record their criterion-specific labels and confidence without forcing consensus. Second, an adjudicator classifies the disagreement source: annotation error, missing context, wording ambiguity, criterion conflict, policy-boundary ambiguity, or taxonomy instability. Only after that step should the item receive a gold label.

决策规则是保守的。如果专家评估者在上下文澄清后达成一致，则分歧将被视为注释错误或缺失上下文。如果政策和语言标准仍然存在紧张关系，则发布的项目会保留裁决者的标准冲突注释，而不是将分裂转化为有关评估者的人口主张。如果释义更改标签，而预期的控制维度保持固定，则该项目将被标记为措辞模糊或分类漂移。无法稳定裁决的项目将作为诊断模糊案例保留，而不是默认丢弃。The decision rule is conservative. If expert evaluators converge after context is clarified, the disagreement is treated as annotation error or missing context. If policy and linguistic criteria remain in tension, the released item preserves the adjudicator’s criterion-conflict note rather than converting the split into a population claim about evaluators. If paraphrases change the label while the intended control dimension stays fixed, the item is marked for wording ambiguity or taxonomy drift. Items that cannot be stably adjudicated are retained as diagnostic-ambiguity cases rather than discarded by default.

8 限制和后续步骤Limitations and Next Steps

当地种子试点验证测量管道，而不是基准的最终心理测量属性。它使用 18 个手工创作的种子项目、三个本地模型、无害的玩具有效载荷和一名专家评审员。结果支持了有关管道可行性、对比测量和判断验证需求的主张。他们没有建立前沿模型安全差异或最终的黄金标签。The local seed pilot validates the measurement pipeline rather than the benchmark’s final psychometric properties. It uses 18 hand-authored seed items, three local models, harmless toy payloads, and one expert adjudicator. The results support claims about pipeline feasibility, contrastive measurement, and judge-validation needs. They don’t establish frontier-model safety differences or final gold labels.

下一个实证阶段应该针对几个公共或可访问的模型运行 50-100 个项目的开发集。比较三种评分途径：专家人工评估、法学硕士作为法官注释以及项目元数据的规则辅助评分。设计应跨项目、现象族、模型、应用面、评价标准、判断提示。The next empirical pass should run a 50–100 item development set against several public or accessible models. Compare three grading routes: expert human evaluation, LLM-as-judge annotation, and rule-aided scoring from item metadata. The design should cross item, phenomenon family, model, application surface, rubric criterion, and judge prompt.

开发通行证应包括每个项目的独立政策和语言评估，最好在每个标准系列中进行两次评估。它不应收集普通用户标签或将评估者响应视为有关评估者群体的数据。应重复或稍微解释百分之十到百分之二十的项目，以衡量项目和方案的稳定性。只有在对分歧来源进行分类后才能制作金标。The development pass should include independent policy and linguistic evaluation for each item, ideally with two evaluations in each criterion family. It shouldn’t collect ordinary-user labels or treat evaluator responses as data about evaluator populations. Ten to twenty percent of items should be repeated or lightly paraphrased to measure item and protocol stability. Gold labels should be produced only after the disagreement source has been classified.

每个计划的标签应该命名其预期的投影。对于源权威项目，投影可能是在无害的释义和源顺序扰动中的稳定性，但对应用程序表面的敏感性。失败的投影是有关该项目或类别的证据，然后才算作模型错误。Each planned label should name its expected projection. For a source-authority item, the projection may be stability across harmless paraphrase and source-order perturbation, but sensitivity to application surface. A failed projection is evidence about the item or category before it counts as a model error.

LLM-法官验证是一个实验，而不是捷径。下一遍应该使用至少两个判断提示，扰乱源顺序表示，包括流畅但违反指令的输出，并将判断标签与裁决的专家标签进行比较。报告法官提示的敏感性和欺骗法官的失败类型，特别是引用/使用错误、来源权威错误和过度拒绝安全分析请求。LLM-judge validation is an experiment, not a shortcut. The next pass should use at least two judge prompts, perturb source-order presentation, include fluent but instruction-violating outputs, and compare judge labels with adjudicated expert labels. Report judge-prompt sensitivity and the failure types that fool the judge, especially quotation/use errors, source-authority errors, and over-refusal of safe analysis requests.

最低发展表应报告现象族的表现，而不仅仅是总分。第二个表应报告按项目系列和标准冲突划分的裁决不稳定性。第三个应该确定黄金标签在裁决后发生变化的案例，因为这些案例可能是分类不稳定的案例，而不仅仅是模型失败的案例。The minimum development table should report performance by phenomenon family, not only aggregate score. A second table should report adjudication instability by item family and criterion conflict. A third should identify cases where the gold label shifted after adjudication, because those are likely taxonomy-instability cases rather than mere model failures.

第四个表应报告应用表面转移。它将仅提示的项目与网页、文档、电子邮件、工具结果和成绩单包装分开，风险类型分为完整性、机密性、工具滥用、策略绕过和评估者欺骗。这可以防止颜色令牌的成功被误认为代理安全稳健性。A fourth table should report application-surface transfer. It should separate prompt-only items from webpage, document, email, tool-result, and transcript wrappers, with risk types split into integrity, confidentiality, tool misuse, policy bypass, and evaluator deception. This prevents colour-token success from being mistaken for agent-security robustness.

9 对安全评估的影响Implications for Safety Evaluation

该基准是为实际安全工作而设计的。模型策略团队可以使用它在模糊边界变得分级失败之前找到这些边界。红队成员可以使用它来区分快速注入敏感性和普通任务失败。系统卡作者可以使用它来避免从隐藏脆弱类别的总通过率中声称安全稳定性。外部评估人员可以使用它来审核安全案例是否基于可靠的标签。The benchmark is designed for practical safety work. Model-policy teams can use it to find vague boundaries before those boundaries become grading failures. Red-teamers can use it to separate prompt-injection susceptibility from ordinary task failure. System-card authors can use it to avoid claiming safety stability from aggregate pass rates that hide fragile categories. External evaluators can use it to audit whether a safety case rests on reliable labels.

更大的方法论点是，语言是这些人工智能安全评估中的控制对象。无法区分引用与命令、权威与内容、政策分析与不安全的制定、拒绝与无能的基准无法衡量预期的结构。The larger methodological point is that language is the object of control in these AI safety evaluations. A benchmark that can’t distinguish quotation from command, authority from content, policy analysis from unsafe enactment, or refusal from inability isn’t measuring the intended construct.

10 结论Conclusion

对抗性语用学重新定义了一个常见的安全评估问题。许多看起来像模型推理失败或策略失败的失败也是语言介导控制的失败。种子基准、分类法、专家评估协议、验证器和漂移指标使这些失败变得可检查。Adversarial pragmatics reframes a common safety-evaluation problem. Many failures that look like model reasoning failures or policy failures are also failures of language-mediated control. The seed benchmark, taxonomy, expert-evaluation protocol, validator, and drift metrics make those failures inspectable.

在应用安全评估工作中，同样的机制将来源权威、政策范围、拒绝校准、法学硕士判断有效性和成绩单证据作为一流的衡量目标。该工件的实用价值在于将专家的语言判断转变为可审计的评估工作流程。In applied safety-evaluation work, the same machinery makes source authority, policy scope, refusal calibration, LLM-judge validity, and transcript evidence first-class measurement targets. The artifact’s practical value lies in turning expert linguistic judgment into an auditable evaluation workflow.

如果它使安全声明更容易审核，那么该项目就会成功：哪个字符串具有权威，应用了哪个策略边界，哪个裁决标准产生了标签，哪个转录证据支持失败归因，哪个类别在释义或评估者更改下变得不稳定，以及剩余稳定标签许可的更广泛声明。The project succeeds if it makes safety claims easier to audit: which string had authority, which policy boundary applied, which adjudication criterion produced the label, which transcript evidence supports the failure attribution, which category becomes unstable under paraphrase or evaluator change, and what broader claim the remaining stable label licenses.
