简介Introduction

提高第二语言的会话能力得益于频繁、有意义的互动和及时的反馈，但持续的一对一辅导成本高昂且难以规模化。大型语言模型 (LLM) 为英语作为第二语言 (ESL) 学习者提供了一种拓宽会话练习机会的有前途的方法。然而，有效的 ESL 辅导需要的不仅仅是流畅的对话：辅导老师必须根据学习者的贡献和周围的互动环境来决定是否、何时以及如何进行干预Developing conversational proficiency in a second language benefits from frequent, meaningful interaction and timely feedback, but sustained one-to-one tutoring is costly and difficult to scale. Large language models (LLMs) offer a promising means of broadening access to conversational practice for learners of English as a second language (ESL). Effective ESL tutoring, however, requires more than fluent dialogue: a tutor must decide whether, when, and how to intervene based on the learner’s contribution and the surrounding interactional context(Peña-Acuña and Corga Fernandes Durão 2024)。在语言学习互动中，学习者的每句话都促进了对话，并揭示了学习者语言能力发展的各个方面. In language-learning interaction, each learner utterance both advances the conversation and reveals aspects of the learner’s developing linguistic competence(Seedhouse 2004)。因此，导师必须保持参与和学习者的能动性，同时决定语言或语用问题是否值得关注以及哪种教学行动是适当的。. The tutor must therefore maintain engagement and learner agency while deciding whether a linguistic or pragmatic problem warrants attention and which instructional action is appropriate.

现有的法学硕士导师经常通过推理时间控制来引发教学行为。一般辅导系统使用提示或专门配置来鼓励引导式提问、暗示和苏格拉底式推理，而语言辅导员还可以根据学习者的熟练程度来调整反应或策略性地使用学习者的第一语言Existing LLM tutors often elicit pedagogical behavior through inference-time control. General tutoring systems use prompting or specialized configurations to encourage guided questioning, hinting, and Socratic reasoning, while language tutors may additionally condition responses on learner proficiency or strategically use the learner’s first language(OpenAI 2023b, a, 2025; Anthropic 2025; Almasi and Kristensen-McLachlan 2025; Liu et al. 2024)。 在 ESL 中，之前的工作探索了提示响应生成和单独的导师行为规划. Within ESL, prior work has explored prompted response generation and separate tutor-act planning(Vanzo et al. 2025; Kwon et al. 2024)。尽管这些研究证明了明确的教学控制的价值，但仍然存在两个局限性：首先，这种控制通常仍然是推理时间支架，而不是由开放响应模型内化的决策政策。这种依赖性使得在个人设备上本地部署的紧凑、独立的导师的开发变得复杂，其中推理成本、延迟和学习者对话的隐私是重要的考虑因素。其次，来自真实 ESL 辅导互动的教学注释数据仍然有限，并且仍然缺乏将学习者的对话移动和修复状态与后续辅导策略联系起来的统一操作框架。. Although these studies demonstrate the value of explicit pedagogical control, two limitations remain: First, such control often remains an inference-time scaffold rather than a decision policy internalized by an open response model. This dependence complicates the development of compact, self-contained tutors for local deployment on personal devices, where inference cost, latency, and the privacy of learner conversations are important considerations. Second, pedagogically annotated data from authentic ESL tutoring interactions remain limited, and a unified operational framework that links a learner’s dialogue move and repair status to the tutor strategy that follows is still lacking.

因此，ESL 交互中可靠的学习者相关教学决策对于紧凑的开放模型来说仍然是一个尚未解决的挑战。如果没有这种能力，模型在做出不适当的教学决策时可能仍然看似有帮助，例如，纠正一个基本上可以接受的话语，而不是承认它以鼓励学习者并维持对话流程，或者在学习者仍在反思不正确但可修复的尝试时提供答案，而不是提供支持持续反思和保留学习者能动性的提示。Reliable learner-contingent pedagogical decision-making in ESL interaction therefore remains an unresolved challenge for compact open models. Without this capability, a model may remain seemingly helpful while making an inappropriate pedagogical decision—for example, correcting a largely acceptable utterance rather than acknowledging it to encourage the learner and sustain conversational flow, or supplying the answer while the learner is still reflecting on an incorrect but repairable attempt rather than offering a hint that supports continued reflection and preserves learner agency.

策略标签描述了响应的目的，而不是其表面措辞。不同的话语可能实现相同的策略，并且当一轮执行多种功能时允许多标签注释，例如在提供提示之前确认学习者。这些策略在帮助级别上也有所不同：发出错误信号比提供更正更能保留更多的学习者主动性，而有针对性的提示比一般性邀请重试提供更多支持。Strategy labels describe what a response is intended to accomplish, not its surface wording. Different utterances may realize the same strategy, and multi-label annotation is allowed when one turn performs multiple functions, such as acknowledging the learner before providing a hint. The strategies also differ in assistance level: signaling an error preserves more learner agency than supplying a correction, whereas a targeted hint provides more support than a general invitation to try again.

从学生的行动到导师策略的映射是有条件的而不是确定性的。有问题的答案可能需要错误信号、提示、指导性修改或直接纠正，具体取决于学生的熟练程度、任务难度、对话历史和先前的支持。类似地，接受的答案可能会得到确认、详细说明或随后进行任务进展。因此，分类法构建了由以下方法解决的中央生成问题：The mapping from student moves to tutor strategies is conditional rather than deterministic. A problematic answer may call for an error signal, hint, guided revision, or direct correction depending on student proficiency, task difficulty, dialogue history, and prior support. Similarly, an accepted answer may be confirmed, elaborated, or followed by task progression. The taxonomy thus structures the central generation problem addressed by塔克特TACT：为当前学生状态选择适当的教学行为，并将该行为表达为连贯的自然语言导师响应。: selecting an appropriate pedagogical action for the current student state and expressing that action as a coherent natural-language tutor response.

讨论Discussion

塔克特TACT通过将目标从模仿下一个老师的话语转变为针对学习者当前状态选择适当的反馈动作并自然地实现，将辅导理论和模型后训练联系起来。这种区别很重要，因为真实的教师数据包含教学信号和对话噪音：直接模仿可能会重现简洁、致谢或主题管理，而无需了解潜在的教学决策。分类法揭示了这一决定，SFT 提供领域样式，GRPO 优化预期的辅导功能。bridges tutoring theory and model post-training by shifting the objective from imitating the next teacher utterance to selecting an appropriate feedback action for the learner’s current state and realizing it naturally. This distinction is important because authentic teacher data contain both pedagogical signal and conversational noise: direct imitation may reproduce brevity, acknowledgments, or topic management without learning the underlying instructional decision. The taxonomy exposes that decision, with SFT providing domain style and GRPO optimizing the intended tutoring function.

Multiple responses may be valid if they address the learner’s immediate need, preserve agency, and maintain a coherent lesson trajectory. Evaluation and reward should therefore recognize defensible tutoring functions rather than require mechanical agreement with the reference wording or label.
