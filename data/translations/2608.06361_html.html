低频陷阱：视频语言模型无法完成简单的事件记账The Low-Frequency Trap: Video–Language Models Fail at Simple Event Bookkeeping

Sarvesh Baskar1\equalcontrib, Zikui Cai1\equalcontrib, Shayan Shabihi1\equalcontrib, Anirudh Satheesh1, Muhammad R. Islam1,
Udari Madhushani Sehwag2, Tom Goldstein1, Furong Huang1
摘要Abstract

现实世界的视频基准测试提供了广泛的覆盖范围，但其固定剪辑涉及事件计数、速率、持续时间和视觉复杂性，使得故障模式难以隔离。虽然现有的程序化基准提供了更好的控制，但它们主要只对最终答案进行评分，而不是根据可执行的基本事实审核报告的事件。为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了基于跟踪的参数分析，用于三个受控视频任务、弹球墙接触、视觉眨眼和分类状态转换中的事件计数。在 2,190 个视频中，我们改变了事件数量Real-world video benchmarks provide broad coverage, but their fixed clips entangle event count, rate, duration, and visual complexity, making failure modes hard to isolate. While existing programmatic benchmarks offer better control, they primarily score only the final answer rather than auditing reported events against executable ground truth. To bridge this gap, we introduce trace-grounded parametric profiling for event counting in three controlled video tasks, bouncing-ball wall contacts, visual blinks, and categorical state transitions. Across 2,190 videos, we vary event countNN和频率and frequencyFF同时保持渲染固定。每个视频都包含用于能力表面估计和时间戳级别评估的可执行事件跟踪。我们的结果揭示了阶段性的暂时性失败。在 80% 的可靠性阈值下，Gemini 3.6 Flash 可以可靠地对 0.5 和 1.0 Hz 频率下的 12 个持续状态转换事件进行计数，但对于瞬态闪烁事件却没有可靠的正计数区域。因此，事件表示（持久的或瞬态的）决定了模型最初是否访问证据——随着计数和频率的增加，这一限制变得更加复杂。在高计数、高频率的情况下，最终计数中只有 0.2% 是正确的，模型仅恢复了 18.1% 的真实事件。为了测试视觉访问是否是主要瓶颈，我们提高了采样率。尽管这将弹跳球的准确率从 19.6% 提高到 29.3%，但报告的序列与实际情况相符的概率仅为 3.7%。因此，额外的帧数可能会夸大最终分数，而不会产生忠实的事件恢复。不同的提示策略产生的收益同样有限，并且对现实世界视频的评估显示，在低事件计数下，成功集中度相同。最终，基于跟踪的分析将视频评估从总体准确性指标转变为对时间推理失败的位置以及报告的证据是否真正支持最终答案的详细诊断。while holding rendering fixed. Each video includes an executable event trace for capability-surface estimation and timestamp-level evaluation. Our results reveal a staged temporal failure. At an 80% reliability threshold, Gemini 3.6 Flash reliably counts persistent state transitions up to 12 events at 0.5 and 1.0 Hz, yet demonstrates no reliable positive-count region for transient blinking events. Thus, event representation (persistent or transient) dictates whether a model initially accesses the evidence – a limitation that compounds as count and frequency increase. In the high-count, high-frequency regime, only 0.2% of final counts are correct and the model recovers just 18.1% of true events. To test if visual access is the primary bottleneck, we increase sampling rate. Although this boosts Bounce Ball accuracy from 19.6% to 29.3%, the reported sequence agrees with the ground truth only 3.7% of the time. Extra frames can therefore inflate final scores without producing faithful event recovery. Different prompting strategies yield similarly limited gains, and evaluations on real-world videos show the same concentration of success at low event counts. Ultimately, trace-grounded profiling shifts video evaluation from aggregate accuracy metrics to a detailed diagnostic of where temporal reasoning fails and whether the reported evidence actually supports the final answer.

1 简介Introduction

视频语言模型发展迅速，在标准视频基准测试中展现出强大的性能Video-language models have advanced rapidly, demonstrating strong performance across standard video benchmarks(Fu et al. 2025; Liu et al. 2024; Chandrasegaran et al. 2024; Mangalam, Akshulakov, and Malik 2023; Li et al. 2024b)。这些基准提供了有用的广度和排名排行榜，但它们很少确定模型失败的原因。自然剪辑共同改变了视觉混乱、摄像机运动、事件密度、持续时间和语义，因此错误不能归因于特定的时间需求。这里考虑的以编程方式综合的基准提供了更多的控制.
