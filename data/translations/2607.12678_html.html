CAD 平面图的文本辅助多模式全景符号识别Text-Aided Multi-Modal Panoptic Symbol Spotting for CAD Floor Plan Drawings

Yan Gong111Equal contribution. Bohao Li222Equal contribution. Bowen Du Junchen Ye
摘要Abstract

计算机辅助设计 (CAD) 平面图包含图形基元和文本注释，为智能设计理解提供补充的几何和语义线索。 在 CAD 分析任务中，随着工业数字化和基于深度学习的自动化需求的不断增长，全景符号识别变得越来越重要。 然而，大多数现有方法仍然主要以原始为中心且未充分利用文本注释，尽管它们具有关键的语义价值。 即使少数文本感知方法也常常只处理表面注释，而没有对 CAD 注释的复杂语法和分层语义进行正确建模，从而导致语义丢失和识别性能不佳。 为了解决这些限制，我们提出了 TextCAD，这是一种多模式框架，可以联合建模图形基元和文本注释以进行全景符号定位。 具体来说，我们设计了一个类型属性相关编码器（TACE），通过联合建模注释的类型和属性来显式编码注释中的组合语义。 我们进一步引入了具有多级语义过滤（MSF）和基元下采样的语义层次对齐框架，该框架可以自适应地将注释语义与不同语义级别的图形基元对齐，并实现准确的跨模式语义注入和融合。 对真实建筑设计数据集的实验表明，TextCAD 有效提高了符号识别性能并实现了最先进的结果。Computer-Aided Design (CAD) floor plan drawings contain both graphical primitives and textual annotations, which provide complementary geometric and semantic cues for intelligent design understanding. Among CAD analysis tasks, panoptic symbol spotting has become increasingly important with the growing demand for industrial digitalization and deep learning-based automation. However, most existing methods remain primarily primitive-centric and underexploit textual annotations, despite their critical semantic value. Even the few text-aware approaches often treat annotations only superficially, without properly modeling complex syntax and hierarchical semantics of CAD annotations, which leads to semantic loss and suboptimal spotting performance. To address these limitations, we propose TextCAD, a multimodal framework that jointly models graphical primitives and textual annotations for panoptic symbol spotting. Specifically, we design a Type–Attribute Correlation Encoder (TACE) to explicitly encode the compositional semantics within annotations by jointly modeling their types and attributes. We further introduce a Semantic Hierarchy Alignment framework with Multi-level Semantic Filtering (MSF) and primitive downsampling, which adaptively aligns annotation semantics with graphical primitives at different semantic levels and enables accurate cross-modal semantic injection and fusion. Experiments on real-world building-design datasets show that TextCAD effectively improves symbol spotting performance and achieves state-of-the-art results.
