摘要Abstract

大型推理模型 (LRM) 通过分配额外的推理时间计算来生成扩展的思想链推理，从而提高性能。然而，最近的研究表明，连续的测试时间缩放通常会产生递减甚至负的回报，因为较长的迹线表现出不确定性增加、错误复合以及与原始问题的偏差。我们建议Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) improve performance by allocating additional inference-time compute to generate extended chain-of-thought reasoning. However, recent studies reveal that sequential test-time scaling often yields diminishing or even negative returns, as longer traces exhibit increased uncertainty, error compounding, and drift from the original problem. 1  简介1  Introduction

大型推理模型 (LRM)Large Reasoning Models (LRMs)(Google DeepMind, 2024; Guo et al., 2025; OpenAIo1, 2024; Yang et al., 2025a; xAI, 2025)在多步骤任务中取得出色表现achieve strong performance on multi-step tasks(Cobbe et al., 2021; Hendrycks et al., 2021; American invitational mathematics examination, 2025; Zhuo et al., 2024; Rein et al., 2024)通过分配额外的推理时间计算来生成显式的思想链推理by allocating extra inference-time compute to generate explicit Chain-of-Thought reasoning(Wei et al., 2022; Muennighoff et al., 2025)。这种测试时间缩放 (TTS) 范例. This test-time scaling (TTS) paradigm(Snell et al., 2024; Muennighoff et al., 2025)随着模型“思考时间更长”，性能会得到提高，它已成为缩放参数或预训练数据的一个令人信服的替代方案。, where performance improves as models “think longer”, has emerged as a compelling alternative to scaling parameters or pre-training data.

然而，最近的研究表明，思考时间更长并不总是意味着思考得更好However, recent studies show that thinking longer does not always mean thinking better(Ghosal et al., 2025a; Gema et al., 2025; Yue et al., 2025; Shojaee et al., 2025)：随着迹线的增长，LRM 表现出不确定性、重复循环、漂移和误差复合的增加: as traces grow, LRMs exhibit increased uncertainty, repetitive cycling, drift, and error compounding(Ghosal et al., 2025a; Chen et al., 2024; Heyman and Zylberberg, 2025; Mirzadeh et al., 2024)，额外的计算通常会放大而不是纠正错误。这与人类解决难题的方式形成鲜明对比：我们不是坚持单一思路，而是回忆类似的已解决问题来指导和验证我们的推理, and additional compute often amplifies rather than corrects the mistake. This contrasts with how humans tackle hard problems: instead of persisting along a single line of thought, we recall analogous solved problems to guide and verify our reasoning(Gick and Holyoak, 1980; Gentner, 1983; Hofstadter and others, 2001).

Recent dynamic methods such as Search-o1(Li et al., 2025)和RATand RAT(Wang et al., 2024c)将检索与思考但目标交织在一起interleave retrieval with thinking but target事实知识factual knowledge，未解决的充当程序支架的示例。, not solved examples that serve as procedural scaffolds.

在这项工作中，我们建议In this work, we propose思考检索ThinkRetrieve，一个框架，用于检索相关的已解决示例并将其在每个思考步骤中直接注入到 LRM 的推理轨迹中。我们框架的核心思想是，在每个推理步骤之后，模型都会生成一个中间答案，然后用于从外部示例库中检索类似的已解决问题。然后，在模型继续推理之前，检索到的示例包含问题及其逐步解决方案，然后将其插入思维轨迹中。这个过程在每个步骤中都会重复，使模型能够不断地将其推理建立在具体的解决示例中，就像人类在解决困难任务时回忆和参考类似的问题一样, a framework that retrieves relevant solved examples and injects them directly into the reasoning trace of LRMs at each thinking step. The core idea of our framework is that, after each reasoning step, the model generates an intermediate answer, which is then used to retrieve a similar solved problem from an external example bank. This retrieved example, containing both the question and its step-by-step solution, is then inserted into the thinking trace before the model continues reasoning. This process repeats at each step, allowing the model to continuously ground its reasoning in concrete worked-out examples, much like how humans recall and reference similar problems when solving a difficult task(Gick and Holyoak, 1980; Hofstadter and others, 2001)。至关重要的是，与之前在推理过程中检索事实文档或知识的工作不同. Crucially, unlike prior work that retrieves factual documents or knowledge during reasoning(Li et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024c), 思考检索ThinkRetrieve通过推理链检索完整已解决的问题，向模型展示如何推理而不是使用哪些事实。retrieves complete solved problems with reasoning chains, showing the model how to reason rather than what facts to use.

附录LAppendix L算法Algorithm
