摘要Abstract

强化学习 (RL) 显着提高了大型语言模型 (LLM) 代理与环境交互和解决多轮任务的能力。 然而，有效的训练仍然具有挑战性，因为稀疏的、只注重结果的奖励使得很难将功劳分配给智能体行动轨迹中的各个步骤。 常见的补救措施是引入密集的中间监督，例如过程奖励模型或辅助自监督信号，但这会增加监督和调整的复杂性，并且通常很难跨任务和领域泛化。 本文提出了 AEM，一种无监督的信用分配方法，可在 RL 训练期间自适应调节熵动态，以实现更有效的探索-利用权衡。 理论上，我们将熵分析从令牌级别提升到响应级别，以减少令牌采样方差，并证明自然梯度下的熵漂移本质上是通过优势和相对响应惊喜的乘积来控制的。 具体来说，我们得出了一个实用的代理来重塑训练动态，从而实现从探索到利用的自然过渡。 跨各种基准和 1.5B–32B 模型的大量实验证明了 AEM 的有效性，其中特别值得注意的Reinforcement learning (RL) has significantly advanced the ability of large language model (LLM) agents to interact with environments and solve multi-turn tasks. Yet effective training remains challenging, as sparse, outcome-only rewards make it difficult to assign credit to individual steps in an agent’s action trajectory. A common remedy is to introduce dense intermediate supervision, such as process reward models or auxiliary self-supervised signals, but this increases supervision and tuning complexity and often generalizes poorly across tasks and domains. This paper presents AEM, a supervision-free credit assignment method that adaptively modulates entropy dynamics during RL training to achieve a more effective exploration–exploitation trade-off. 从法学硕士到代理强化学习。From LLMs to Agentic RL.

大型语言模型得到越来越多的探索 作为交互式代理，通过多轮推理、工具使用以及与外部环境的闭环交互来解决任务。 代表作品如ReActLarge language models are increasingly explored as interactive agents that solve tasks through multi-turn reasoning, tool use, and closed-loop interaction with external environments. Representative works such as ReAct(yao2023react)和模具成型机and Toolformer(schick2023toolformer)证明法学硕士可以将推理与行动和外部工具调用交织在一起，将法学硕士的角色从被动生成者转变为交互式决策者。 训练此类智能体越来越依赖于 RL，其中基于群体的方法（例如 RLOO）demonstrate that LLMs can interleave reasoning with actions and external tool invocation, shifting the role of LLMs from passive generators to interactive decision-makers. Training such agents increasingly relies on RL, where group-based methods such as RLOO(ahmadian2024back)和GRPOand GRPO(shao2024deepseekmath)已成为一种主导方法。 将这些方法从单轮代理设置扩展到多轮代理设置会加剧稀疏奖励：反馈仅在最后到达，为中间决策提供很少的指导。缺乏分级监管会产生高方差梯度和模糊的信用分配，阻碍政策识别推动成功的行动。have emerged as a dominant approach. Extending these methods from single-turn to multi-turn agentic settings exacerbates sparse rewards: feedback arrives only at the end, providing little guidance for intermediate decisions. The lack of step-level supervision yields high-variance gradients and ambiguous credit assignment, hindering the policy from identifying actions that drive success.

Agentic RL 中的信用分配。Credit Assignment in Agentic RL.

信用分配是代理强化学习中长期存在的挑战，奖励延迟且稀疏。现有的方法可以通过其信用信号的来源和结构来大致区分。有的依靠Credit assignment is a long-standing challenge in agentic RL with delayed and sparse rewards. Existing approaches can be broadly distinguished by the source and construction of their credit signals. Some rely on外部信号external signals，例如价值函数或阶梯级监督, such as value functions or step-level supervision(schulman2017ppo; lightman2023let)，但会引入额外的建模和扩展开销。其他人获得信用, but introduce additional modeling and scaling overhead. Others derive credit内部internally从采样轨迹from sampled trajectories(fenggroup; wang2025information)、规避辅助监管；一些方法从轨迹属性中隐式推断信用，而其他方法则通过结构化传播进一步细化信用, avoiding auxiliary supervision; some methods infer credit implicitly from trajectory attributes, while others further refine credit through structured propagation(caoatpo; ding2026treegrpo)或明确的奖励重新分配or explicit reward redistribution(wang2025spa)，这以多轮设置中的额外计算为代价提高了粒度。 尽管存在差异，这些方法都忽略了一个更基本的量：策略自身的熵。由于熵反映了每个决策的模型不确定性，因此它提供了一个自然且内在的信号，用于区分信心与决策 探索性的步骤——我们的信用调制方法正是建立在这个信号的基础上的。, which improves granularity at the cost of additional computation in multi-turn settings. Despite their differences, these methods all overlook a more fundamental quantity: the policy’s own entropy. As entropy reflects model uncertainty at each decision, it provides a natural and intrinsic signal for distinguishing confident from exploratory steps—and it is precisely this signal that our method builds on for credit modulation.

熵感知策略优化。Entropy-Aware Policy Optimization.

熵在强化学习中被广泛用作正则化信号来鼓励探索Entropy has been widely used in RL as a regularization signal to encourage exploration(cui2025entropy; petrenko2026entropy; chen2026flexible)并稳定优化and stabilize optimization(pmlr-v48-mniha16)。最近的工作探索了调节训练的熵感知目标，例如熵正则化策略优化. Recent works have explored entropy-aware objectives to modulate training, such as entropy-regularized policy optimization(xu2025epo)和熵引导的优势缩放and entropy-guided advantage scaling(wang2025harnessing)。这些方法表明，政策熵反映了模型的不确定性，并且可以作为基于结果的奖励之外的信息信号。 然而，现有方法主要依赖于令牌级熵控制或主要使用熵来稳定训练，而不是塑造响应级信用分配来自适应调节优化动态。相比之下，我们的工作利用响应级熵作为内在信号来调节每个响应的优势，从而自适应地调节策略熵，同时在代理强化学习中实现可扩展的信用分配。. These methods demonstrate that policy entropy reflects model uncertainty and can serve as an informative signal beyond outcome-based rewards. However, existing approaches primarily rely on token-level entropy control or use entropy mainly to stabilize training, rather than shaping response-level credit assignment to adaptively regulate the optimization dynamics. In contrast, our work leverages response-level entropy as an intrinsic signal to modulate per-response advantages, thereby adaptively regulating policy entropy while enabling scalable credit assignment in agentic RL.

7 结论Conclusions

本文提出了 AEM，一种用于多轮代理强化学习的无监督信用分配框架，它使用响应级熵作为内在信号。 我们的分析表明，熵动力学受优势和相对响应意外之间的相互作用控制，这激发了自适应熵调制规则来调节策略更新。通过在训练期间明确控制熵动力学，AEM 能够以完全自适应的方式从探索到利用的自然过渡。作为现有政策估计器的轻量级插件，它可以在无需辅助模型、密集监督或限制性结构假设的情况下改善信用分配。在 ALFWorld、WebShop 和 SWE-bench-Verified 中，AEM 持续改进强大的基线，减轻过早的熵崩溃，并产生更强大的最终性能。这些结果强调了响应水平熵不仅是理解多轮智能体训练的有用视角，而且也是自适应探索-利用控制的实用机制。This paper presents AEM, a supervision-free credit assignment framework for multi-turn agentic RL that uses response-level entropy as an intrinsic signal. Our analysis shows that entropy dynamics are governed by the interaction between advantage and relative response surprisal, which motivates an adaptive entropy modulation rule to regulate policy updates. By explicitly steering entropy dynamics during training, AEM enables a natural transition from exploration to exploitation in a fully self-adaptive manner. As a lightweight plug-in to existing policy estimators, it improves credit assignment without auxiliary models, dense supervision, or restrictive structural assumptions. Across ALFWorld, WebShop, and SWE-bench-Verified, AEM consistently improves strong baselines, mitigates premature entropy collapse, and yields stronger final performance. These results highlight response-level entropy not only as a useful lens for understanding multi-turn agent training, but also as a practical mechanism for adaptive exploration–exploitation control.

局限性Limitations

在实践中，In practice,Hresp​(s)H_{\mathrm{resp}}(s)对于开放式 LLM 政策来说，不能直接计算，因为它需要对整个响应空间进行求和。因此，我们使用基于组的长度归一化熵代理来近似相对响应令人惊讶。虽然我们的实验提供了统计证据，表明该代理与所需的熵动力学一致并改进了训练，但它仍然是一种启发式代理而不是精确的估计器。因此，AEM 不能保证最佳熵调制，其行为可能取决于采样的 rollout 组的质量和多样性。设计更准确的响应水平相对惊喜估计器是未来工作的一个有希望的方向。is not directly computable for open-ended LLM policies, as it would require summing over the entire response space. We therefore approximate the relative response surprisal with a group-based, length-normalized entropy proxy. While our experiments provide statistical evidence that this proxy is aligned with the desired entropy dynamics and improves training, it is still a heuristic surrogate rather than an exact estimator. Consequently, AEM does not guarantee optimal entropy modulation, and its behavior may depend on the quality and diversity of the sampled rollout group. Designing more accurate estimators of response-level relative surprisal is a promising direction for future work.
