1 简介Introduction

生成推荐（GR）Generative recommendation (GR)(geng2022recommendation; gao2023chat; zhu2023large; hou2024large; deng2025onerec)已成为工业推荐系统领域有前途的范例。通过利用用户行为的自回归性质，GR 旨在使用大语言模型 (LLM) 架构根据用户的历史上下文来预测用户的下一次交互，从而为用户提供更加个性化和响应更快的体验。这种方法在电子商务、在线广告和内容流媒体等各种场景中显示出巨大的潜力，在这些场景中，了解用户行为的顺序性质对于提供相关推荐至关重要。通过对用户交互之间的时间依赖性进行建模，GR 试图捕获驱动用户行为的潜在偏好和兴趣，最终产生更准确和有效的推荐。has emerged as a promising paradigm in the field of industrial recommender systems. By leveraging the autoregressive nature of user behavior, GR aims to predict the next interactions of users based on their historical contexts using large language model (LLM) architectures, thereby providing users with a more personalized and responsive experience. This approach has shown considerable potential in various scenarios, including e-commerce, online advertising, and content streaming, where understanding the sequential nature of user behavior is crucial for delivering relevant recommendations. By modeling the temporal dependencies between user interactions, GR seeks to capture the underlying preferences and interests that drive user behavior, ultimately leading to more accurate and effective recommendations.

GR 的核心是项目标记化，以桥接项目语义和 GR 模型。然而，现有的 GR 方法通常难以有效地将复杂的用户行为和项目语义上下文同时融入到推荐模型中。 一方面，虽然存在图集成方法来利用来自用户-项目关系图的关系信息，但它们存在可扩展性问题或仅利用本地图信息。例如，图序列化方法将图转换为序列，但长序列化可能会导致 LLM 的昂贵计算。另一方面，图神经网络（GNN）通常只能感知每个用户的一个小子图，但它们无法充分利用整体图信息。这些限制阻碍了基于图的方法在行业规模的生成顺序推荐中的有效性。 另一条平行的工作线是At the core of GR is item tokenization to bridge item semantics and the GR model. However, existing GR methods often struggle to effectively incorporate complex user-behavioral and item-semantic contexts simultaneously into the recommendation model. On the one hand, while there exist graph integration methods to leverage the relational information from user-item relational graphs, they suffer from scalability issues or only utilize local graph information. For instance, graph serialization methods transform the graph into a sequence, but the long serialization can incur expensive computation of LLMs. Graph neural networks (GNNs), on the other hand, typically perceive only a small subgraph for each user, but they cannot fully exploit the holistic graph information. 7 结论与未来工作Conclusion & Future Work

在本文中，我们介绍了 G2Rec，这是一种可扩展的项目模式，旨在通过有效集成用于语义标记化的基于整体图的共同参与模型来增强生成顺序推荐系统。我们的方法解决了现有方法的局限性，这些方法通常难以实现可扩展性以及复杂的用户行为和项目语义信息的准确合并。通过构建项目-项目共同参与图并采用软聚类算法，G2Rec 得出兴趣原型，将用户交互历史序列转换为信息更丰富的项目兴趣概况。这种转换允许推荐模型捕获全面的语义用户兴趣概况，而不依赖于预定义的真实兴趣。 我们在公共数据集上进行的广泛实验，以及在产品表面上的成功在线部署，证明了 G2Rec 相对于当前最先进方法的优越性。结果表明，G2Rec 能够提供更准确、更全面的用户行为建模，最终提高工业应用中的推荐性能。 G2Rec 代表了生成顺序推荐领域向前迈出的重要一步，为行业规模的应用程序提供了可扩展且有效的解决方案。未来的工作可能会探索对该框架的进一步增强，例如合并额外的数据源或改进聚类算法以捕获更细微的兴趣。In this paper, we introduced G2Rec, a scalable item schema designed to enhance generative sequential recommendation systems by effectively integrating holistic graph-based co-engagement modeling for semantic tokenization. Our approach addresses the limitations of existing methods, which often struggle with scalability and the accurate incorporation of complex user-behavioral and item-semantic information. By constructing an item-item co-engagement graph and employing a soft clustering algorithm, G2Rec derives interest prototypes that transform user interaction history sequences into the more informative item interest profiles. This transformation allows the recommendation model to capture comprehensive and semantic user interest profiles without relying on predefined ground-truth interests. Our extensive experiments on public datasets, along with successful online deployment on product surfaces, demonstrate the superiority of G2Rec over current state-of-the-art methods. The results highlight that G2Rec have the ability to provide more accurate and holistic modeling of user behavior, ultimately leading to improved recommendation performance in industrial applications. G2Rec represents a significant step forward in the field of generative sequential recommendation, offering a scalable and effective solution for industry-scale applications. Future work may explore further enhancements to the framework, such as incorporating additional data sources or refining the clustering algorithm to capture even more nuanced interests.
