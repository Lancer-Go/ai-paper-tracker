使用概念激活向量的 L2 口语评估系统的偏差分析Bias Analysis of L2 Speaking Assessment Systems Using Concept Activation Vectors

Arya Labroo    Mengjie Qian    Kate Knill
摘要Abstract

自动口语评估系统越来越多地部署在高风险环境中，以对第二语言 (L2) 学习者的口语测试进行评分，因此表明他们的分数取决于口语熟练程度而不是不相关的说话者属性（例如第一语言 (L1) 或年龄）至关重要。基于 Transformer 的基础模型提高了这些 L2 口语评分者的准确性，但其黑盒表示使公平性和可解释性分析变得更加困难。在之前使用概念激活向量 (CAV) 检测基于特征的评分器中不需要的属性（“概念”）的偏差的工作的基础上，我们将基于 CAV 的分析扩展到两个神经口语评估系统：基于文本的 BERT 评分器和基于 Whisper 的语音和文本多模态评分器。 CAV 将人类可解释的概念表示为模型激活空间中的方向，使我们能够区分概念是否编码在模型的内部表示中以及它是否影响预测分数，后者使用基于梯度的灵敏度度量进行量化。由于 CAV 依赖于线性可分离性，而这在复杂的神经嵌入空间中不太可能，因此我们还研究稀疏自动编码器 (SAE) 是否通过在稀疏潜在空间中学习 CAV 并将其映射回激活空间来提供更清晰的概念方向。我们的分析表明，概念的可恢复性在很大程度上取决于所探测的表示和架构，而不仅仅是概念。对概念的敏感性也取决于架构。 SAE 使概念更加线性可恢复，但削弱了原始激活空间敏感性，尤其是在低维层中。这些发现强调了在审计口语评估系统中的偏差时区分概念可恢复性和概念影响的必要性。Automatic speaking assessment systems are increasingly deployed in high-stakes settings to mark second language (L2) learners’ speaking tests, making it critical to show that their scores depend on speaking proficiency rather than irrelevant speaker attributes such as first language (L1) or age. Transformer-based foundation models have improved the accuracy of these L2 speaking graders, but their black-box representations make fairness and interpretability analysis more difficult. Building on prior work that used Concept Activation Vectors (CAVs) to detect bias towards unwanted attributes (‘concepts’) in feature-based graders, we extend CAV-based analysis to two neural speaking assessment systems: a text-based BERT grader and a speech-and-text multimodal grader based on Whisper. CAVs represent human-interpretable concepts as directions in a model’s activation space, allowing us to distinguish between whether a concept is encoded in a model’s internal representations and whether it influences the predicted score, the latter quantified using a gradient-based sensitivity metric. Since CAVs rely on linear separability, which is less likely in complex neural embedding spaces, we also investigate whether sparse autoencoders (SAEs) provide cleaner concept directions by learning CAVs in a sparse latent space and mapping them back to activation space. Our analysis shows that concept recoverability depends strongly on the representation and architecture being probed, rather than on the concept alone. Sensitivity to concepts is also architecture-dependent. SAEs make concepts more linearly recoverable, but attenuate the original activation-space sensitivity, especially in low-dimensional layers. These findings highlight the need to distinguish concept recoverability from concept influence when auditing bias in speaking assessment systems.

I 简介Introduction

第二语言 (L2) 自动口语评估系统根据考生对一个或多个问题的口头回答来评估其熟练程度。这些系统的目标是匹配真人考官给予考生的分数。 这些系统的基于 Transformer 基础模型的版本已经实现了最先进的性能，满足评分精度要求，例如Second language (L2) automatic speaking assessment systems estimate a candidate’s proficiency level from their spoken responses to one or more questions. The goal of these systems is to match the scores a human examiner would award to the candidate. Transformer foundation model based versions of these systems have achieved state-of-the-art performance which meet the scoring accuracy requirements e.g.[26, 4, 3, 21, 20, 19, 22]。这些系统将音频和/或文本（ASR 转录本）作为输入，将输入转换为嵌入表示，然后将其传递到预测候选人测试分数的回归头。 BERT 等模型. These systems take audio and/or text (ASR transcripts) as input, convert the input into an embedding representation which is then passed to a regression head that predicts the candidate’s test score. 随着这些基于基础模型的自动口语评估系统越来越多地部署在移民和就业认证等高风险环境中，它们的分数反映口语能力而不是说话者的不相关属性变得至关重要。公平的评分者应该依赖流利程度、语法控制和内容等能力证据，并对第一语言 (L1) 或年龄等属性保持不敏感。尽管如此，这些属性在数据中大量存在，并且可以从用于预训练其基础模型的大型、记录不足的语料库中进入评分者，或者从特定于任务的微调数据中的不平衡中进入评分者，其中在给定熟练程度下代表性过高的群体可以成为预测分数的捷径。As these foundation model based automatic speaking assessment systems are increasingly deployed in high-stakes settings such as immigration and employment certification, it becomes critical that their scores reflect speaking ability rather than irrelevant attributes of the speaker. A fair grader should rely on evidence of ability such as fluency, grammatical control, and content, and remain insensitive to attributes such as first language (L1) or age. These attributes are nonetheless richly present in the data and can enter a grader either from the large, under-documented corpora used to pre-train its foundation model, or from imbalance in the task-specific fine-tuning data, where an over-represented group at a given proficiency can become a shortcut for predicting the score.

这种偏见已在广泛的机器学习应用中得到记录，包括商业性别分类和面部分析系统中的人口统计准确性差异Such biases have been documented across a wide range of machine learning applications, including demographic accuracy disparities in commercial gender classification and face analysis systems[8]以及词嵌入中编码的性别刻板印象and gender stereotypes encoded in word embeddings[6]。 然而，检测现代神经模型中的偏差更具挑战性，因为总体性能指标无法揭示哪些内部表示驱动模型的预测，并且这些表示通常是高度分布和纠缠的。 然而，一系列可解释性工作表明，人类可解释的信息可以从网络的内部激活中恢复：线性分类器探针可以解码中间层的属性. However, detecting bias in modern neural models is more challenging, because aggregate performance metrics do not reveal which internal representations drive a model’s predictions, and these representations are often highly distributed and entangled. A line of interpretability work has shown, however, that human-interpretable information can be recovered from a network’s internal activations: linear classifier probes can decode properties from intermediate layers[1]，并且各个表征方向可以与语义概念保持一致, and individual representational directions can be aligned with semantic concepts[5]。 基于这些想法，TCAV 框架. Building on these ideas, the TCAV framework[16]引入了概念激活向量（CAV），它将人类可解释的概念表示为线性分类器学习的激活空间中的方向，以将展示该概念的示例的激活与不展示该概念的示例的激活分开。然后通过使用梯度测量模型预测对该方向的敏感性来量化概念的影响。 应用于图像分类时，TCAV 既可以用来解释模型预测，也可以用来揭示偏差，例如将“女性”概念与“围裙”类别相关联。至关重要的是，CAV 区分概念是否编码在模型的表示中以及它是否实际上影响模型的预测。introduces Concept Activation Vectors (CAVs), which represent a human-interpretable concept as a direction in the activation space learned by a linear classifier to separate activations of examples that exhibit the concept from those that do not. The influence of a concept is then quantified by measuring the sensitivity of the model’s prediction to that direction using gradients. Applied to image classification, TCAV has been used both to explain model predictions and to uncover biases, for example by associating the concept ‘female’ with the class ‘apron’. Crucially, CAVs distinguish between whether a concept is encoded in a model’s representations and whether it actually influences the model’s prediction.

They validated the method by recovering a deliberately injected L1 bias and showing that concepts flagged on training data corresponded to bias on expert-scored evaluation data. This work extend their analysis to Transformer-based graders, showing that the linear recoverability of a concept is not a reliable indicator of its influence on the predicted score. Building on this observation, we investigate how concept recoverability and influence vary across the layers of modern Transformer foundation models, and whether learning CAVs in a sparse latent representation improves concept-based bias analysis. This question is motivated by a key assumption of CAVs that a concept can be represented by a single linear direction, an assumption that becomes increasingly restrictive as Transformer representations become higher-dimensional and more entangled. Sparse autoencoders (SAEs), which learn a sparse, higher-dimensional latent space in which features may be more disentangled, have recently been shown to recover more interpretable features from Transformer activations than the raw neurons[7, 14]。 这就提出了一个自然的问题：在 SAE 潜在空间中学习 CAV 是否​​可以改善基于概念的偏差分析？. This raises a natural question: does learning CAVs in an SAE latent space improve concept-based bias analysis?

这项工作旨在通过以下贡献来应对这些挑战：i）我们将基于 CAV 的偏差分析扩展到基于 Transformer 的自动口语评估系统； ii）我们比较纯文本（BERT）的表征水平偏差This work aims to address these challenges with the following contributions: i) we extend CAV-based bias analysis to Transformer-based automatic speaking assessment systems; ii) we compare representation-level bias across text-only (BERT)[13]和语音文本（耳语）评分器and speech-text (Whisper) graders[11]，iii）我们研究稀疏自动编码器作为基于概念的偏差分析的补充表示。, iii) we investigate sparse autoencoders as a complimentary representation for concept-based bias analysis.

三、III稀疏自动编码器Sparse Autoencoders

CAV 假设一个概念可以通过单个线性方向捕获。虽然这对于较小的模型来说通常是合理的，但随着模型规模的扩大，它会减弱，因为大型语音和语言模型往往会在重叠、非正交的方向上表示许多因素，即叠加。这就产生了两个问题：一个概念可以被编码但不能线性分离；即使线性分类器确实分离了示例，所得的方向也可能将预期概念与其他相关因素混合在一起。 稀疏自动编码器 (SAE) 学习稀疏的高维表示，这些表示已被证明可以从 Transformer 激活中恢复更多可解释的特征A CAV assumes a concept can be captured by a single linear direction. While this is often reasonable for smaller models, it weakens as models scale, since large speech and language models tend to represent many factors in overlapping, non-orthogonal directions, known as superposition. This creates two problems: a concept may be encoded but not linearly separable; and even when a linear classifier does separate the examples, the resulting direction may mix the intended concept with other correlated factors. Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) learn sparse, higher-dimensional representations that have been shown to recover more interpretable features from Transformer activations[7, 14]。因此，我们研究在 SAE 潜在空间中学习 CAV 是否​​可以为偏差分析提供更清晰的概念方向。. We therefore investigate whether learning CAVs in the SAE latent space yields cleaner concept directions for bias analysis.

VI 结论Conclusions

我们将表征级 CAV 偏差分析扩展到两个 现代口语评分器、基于文本的 BERT 评分器和文本和语音 Whisper 评分器，并添加了该方法的稀疏自动编码器变体。核心发现是，概念的线性可恢复性由测量它的表示形式决定，而不是概念本身，并且可恢复性并不意味着影响。这在 Whisper 分级机中得到了体现，它编码的人口统计信息远多于 BERT，但仍然接近不敏感的基线，而 BERT 对深度和熟练程度变得更加敏感。 通过其 L1 方向反映了数据集的分数不平衡。对于公平性分析，编码与影响力的分离是关键点，因为被编码的属性本身并不是偏见的证据。We extended representation-level CAV bias analysis to two modern speaking graders, a text-based BERT grader and a text-and-speech Whisper grader, and added a sparse autoencoder variant of the method. The central finding is that linear recoverability of a concept is governed by the representation it is measured in, not the concept itself, and that recoverability does not imply influence. This was seen in the Whisper grader, which encoded far more demographic information than BERT yet stayed close to the baseline of no sensitivity, while BERT grew more sensitive to proficiency with depth and reflected the dataset’s score imbalance through its L1 directions. For fairness analysis, this separation of encoding from influence is the key point, since an attribute being encoded is not in itself evidence of bias.

在稀疏潜在空间中应用分析并没有改善它。 SAE 使概念更容易恢复，但削弱了测量到的基线灵敏度，在低维层中最严重。这两个指标要求 不同的事情。可恢复性只需要一个分类器来分离概念，而梯度度量要求解码器映射的方向仍然与激活空间中的得分梯度对齐，而重建目标没有理由保留这一点。这激发了分数感知 SAE，经过训练来重建评分器的输出或通过分数梯度对重建误差进行加权，以便稀疏空间保留 偏差度量需要的方向。Applying the analysis in the sparse latent space did not improve it. The SAE made concepts more recoverable but attenuated the measured sensitivity towards the baseline, most severely in low-dimensional layers. The two metrics ask different things. Recoverability only needs a classifier to separate the concept, whereas the gradient metric requires the decoder-mapped direction to still align with the score gradient in activation space, which the reconstruction objective has no reason to preserve. This motivates a score-aware SAE, trained to reconstruct the grader’s output or to weight reconstruction error by the score gradient, so that the sparse space retains the directions the bias metric needs.
