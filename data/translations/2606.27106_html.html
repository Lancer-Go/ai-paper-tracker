摘要Abstract

我们提出了一种将大语言模型（LLM）应用于外国维和任务背景下的威胁评估的新颖方法。以 PINPOINT 项目及其用例（格鲁吉亚欧盟监测团）为基础，我们将跨学科风险模型与基于 OSINT 的媒体收集和法学硕士支持的威胁提取相结合。拟议的工作流程将媒体内容映射到与任务相关的威胁，提取结构化信息并应用几个额外的基于 LLM 的处理步骤来提高相关性和基础。对从媒体文档中提取的威胁进行的评估表明，自动生成的结果与人类对威胁和任务相关性等核心方面的判断高度一致。这些结果表明，法学硕士为维和任务中的分析人员提供了一种有前景的支持方法。We present a novel approach for applying Large Language Models (LLMs) to threat assessment in the context of foreign peacekeeping missions. Building on the PINPOINT project and its use case, the EU Monitoring Mission in Georgia, we combine an interdisciplinary risk-model with OSINT-based media collection and LLM-supported threat extraction. The proposed workflow maps media contents to mission-relevant threats, extracts structured information and applies several additional LLM-based processing steps to improve relevance and grounding. An evaluation of threats extracted from media documents shows high agreement between automatically generated results and human judgment for core aspects such as threat and mission relevance. These results indicate that LLMs provide a promising approach to support analysts in the context of peacekeeping missions.

The clear aim of the proposed approach is thus to complement and enhance human judgment, but not to automate or replace it.

However, this is met with concerns and skepticism about accountability, transparency, privacy or the tendency to completely eliminate human judgment from decision processes. 9 结论与展望Conclusion and Outlook

我们提出了一种应用法学硕士的半自动方法来支持外国维和任务中的威胁评估。该方法基于基于指标的风险模型和佐治亚州 EUMM 的用例，结合了基于 OSINT 的媒体收集、特定任务检索以及法学硕士支持的威胁提取和接地。由此产生的工作流程将多语言媒体内容转换为结构化和多方面的威胁信息。We have presented a semi-automatic approach for the application of LLMs to support threat assessment in foreign peacekeeping missions. Based on an indicator-based risk model and the use-case of the EUMM in Georgia, the approach combines OSINT-based media collection, mission-specific retrieval as well as LLM-supported threat extraction and grounding. The resulting workflow transforms multilingual media contents into structured and faceted threat information.

与领域专家的评估对威胁相关性、任务相关性和位置相关性等核心方面产生了高度一致。这些结果表明法学硕士可以通过提高风险评估的可扩展性和及时性为分析师提供宝贵的支持。然而，威胁级别估计和行为者识别的较低分数强调了对人工验证和谨慎操作保障的持续需求——我们提倡采用人机交互的方法。由于潜在风险模型和基于法学硕士的处理的普遍性，该方法适合于更广泛（且具有成本效益）的应用到维和任务以外的其他情况。An evaluation with domain experts yields high agreement for central aspects such as threat relevance, mission relevance and location relevance. These results indicate that LLMs can provide valuable support for analysts by improving the scalability and timeliness of risk assessment. However, lower scores for threat-level estimation and actor identification underscore the continued need for human validation and careful operational safeguards - we advocate a human-in-the-loop approach. Due to the generality of the underlying risk model and LLM-based processing, the approach lends itself to broader (and cost-effective) application to further contexts and beyond peacekeeping missions.

10 局限性Limitations

总体而言，我们的目标是实施一种通用且稳健的方法，从而产生（基于媒体的）指标，这些指标可以构成更全面的威胁评估方法的一部分。然而，当前方法本身以及项目内开展的工作存在一些局限性，如下所述：Overall, we have aimed to implement a generic and robust approach, allowing to produce (media-based) indicators which can form part of a more comprehensive approach to threat assessment. However, there are several limitations to the current approach itself as well as the work carried out within the project as outlined below:

检查指标的数量和类型Number and types of examined indicators：尽管该方法基于通用模型并且本质上是通用的，但它仅在有限的范围内进行了研究。两个时期只审查了四个指标。尽管分析似乎表明两个检查的时间段之间具有良好的一致性，但随着任务的进展，应该进行进一步的检查。此外，还需要检查其他指标以支持该方法的目标通用性。: even though the approach is based on a general model and generic in nature, it has only been investigated within a limited scope. Only four indicators have been examined for two periods of time. Whereas analysis seems to indicate a good level of agreement between the two examined time-periods, further examination as the mission evolves over should be performed. Furthermore, other indicators need be examined to support the targeted generality of the approach.

总体威胁评估Overall threat assessment：使用提议的方法仅构成更通用的威胁评估方法的一个方面。与其他要素的组合以及所提出的方法与这些要素的相互作用需要进一步研究。只有结合起来才能提供有关威胁的整体情况。: using the proposed method only forms one aspect of a more general methodology for threat assessment. The combination with other elements and the interplay of the proposed method with such elements needs to be further examined. Only a combination will provide a holistic picture regarding threats.

缓解措施Mitigations：建议的缓解措施处于相当笼统的水平。它们应考虑具体情况并根据实际任务情况进行调整。: the suggested mitigations are on a rather general level. They should take into account specifics and be tailored to the actual mission situation.

抵御操纵或虚假信息的稳健性Robustness against manipulation or disinformation：尽管该方法在选择来源和内容以及根据（感知的）可靠性水平对来源进行限定方面遵循原则性方法，但不能排除操纵和虚假信息活动影响这些来源和内容。一方面，可以添加自动支持来检测此类操纵、异常或异常值。另一方面，我们强调在“人在环”方法中对人际互动（和监督）的持续需求。: even though the method follows a principled approach in the selection of sources and contents as well as a qualification of sources in terms of their (perceived) level of reliability, it cannot be ruled out that manipulation and disinformation campaigns influence these sources and contents. On the one hand, automatic support to detect such manipulation, anomalies or outliers could be added. On the other hand, we emphasize the continuing need for human interaction (and supervision) in a human-in-the-loop approach.

仅限单一任务Single mission only：这里开展的工作仅涉及单一维和特派团，在地理、行动者和范围方面都有其特殊性。进一步的任务可能会提供不同的要求、优先事项和见解，这可能会导致拟议方法的修改或扩展。: The work undertaken here concerns only a single peacekeeping mission, with its peculiarities in terms of geography, actors and scope. Further missions may provide different requirements, priorities and insights which may lead to modifications or extensions of the proposed approach.

融入实践Integration into practice：虽然该方法是与从业人员共同制定的，并在现场与特派团成员直接讨论，但需要审查将其纳入既定程序和流程的情况。需要考虑特派团的具体要求和程序。这还可能需要允许特派团工作人员在没有项目团队参与的情况下调整和修改参数的机制（出于安全或保密原因）。: whereas the approach was developed jointly with practitioners and directly discussed with members of the mission in situ, its integration into established procedures and processes needs to be examined. Mission-specific requirements and procedures would need to be taken into account. This may also entail mechanisms allowing mission staff to adjust and modify parameters without involvement of the project team (due to security or confidentiality reasons).

总体而言，正如评估期间概述的那样，所实施的方法似乎很有前途。与不同任务相关的后续活动为验证我们的初步发现、完善方法并提高其稳健性和适用范围提供了途径。Overall, the implemented method seems promising as outlined during the evaluation. Follow-up activities in connection with different missions provide an alley to verify our initial findings, refine the approach and increase its robustness and field of applicability.
