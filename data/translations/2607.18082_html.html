摘要Abstract

基于 Rubric 的强化学习 (RL) 最近在改进开放式任务的大型语言模型 (LLM) 方面表现出了良好的前景。基于标题的强化学习的一个广泛认可的局限性是有限的探索：没有任何推出能够满足的标准（Rubric-based Reinforcement Learning (RL) has recently shown promise in improving Large Language Models (LLMs) on open-ended tasks. A widely recognized limitation of rubric-based RL is limited exploration: criteria that no rollout manages to satisfy (未探索的标准Unexplored Criteria) 没有收到优化信号。最近的方法通过在推出生成过程中纳入标题信息作为外部指导来解决这个问题，但它们引入了) receive no optimization signal. Recent methods address this by incorporating rubric information as external guidance during rollout generation, yet they introduce a训练-推理不匹配train–inference mismatch：该策略在外部指导下生成的部署上进行了优化，而在推理时缺乏该指导，从而通过自回归解码导致错误累积。此外，这些以探索为中心的方法忽视了一种根本不同的故障模式，我们称之为: the policy is optimized on rollouts produced under external guidance while this guidance is absent at inference time, causing error accumulation through autoregressive decoding. Moreover, these exploration-focused approaches overlook a fundamentally different failure mode that we term抑制标准Suppressed Criteria—某些推出可以满足的标准，但其学习信号在优化过程中会丢失，因为标量奖励聚合为它们分配了非正的总体优势。我们的分析表明，抑制标准非常普遍：超过 57% 的样本在整个训练过程中表现出这种失败模式，每个样本平均有 1.8 个抑制标准。为了同时解决未探索和抑制的标准而不引入训练推理不匹配，我们建议—criteria that are satisfied by some rollouts yet whose learning signals are lost during optimization because scalar reward aggregation assigns them non-positive aggregate advantages. 1 简介Introduction

近年来，强化学习可验证奖励（RLVR），通过GRPO等方法实例化In recent years, Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR), instantiated by methods such as GRPO(Shao et al., 2024)，已成为提高大型语言模型推理能力的重要范例, has emerged as a prominent paradigm for improving the reasoning capabilities of large language models(DeepSeek-AI, 2025; Cui et al., 2026)。 RLVR 在数学推理和代码生成等可验证领域取得了显着的成功，其中准确的答案或可执行的测试用例提供了稳定可靠的监督. RLVR has achieved notable success in verifiable domains such as mathematical reasoning and code generation, where exact answers or executable test cases provide stable and reliable supervision(He et al., 2024; Wei et al., 2025)。然而，在医疗咨询和长篇写作等开放式场景中，模型输出通常不承认唯一的黄金答案，并且很难用确定性规则进行验证。为了将 RLVR 扩展到此类领域，最近的工作探索了基于标题的奖励，它将响应质量分解为可解释的评估标准，使用 LLM-as-a-Judge. However, in open-ended scenarios such as medical consultation and long-form writing, model outputs typically do not admit a unique gold answer and are difficult to verify with deterministic rules. To extend RLVR to such domains, recent work has explored rubric-based rewards, which decompose response quality into interpretable evaluation criteria, use LLM-as-a-Judge(Gu et al., 2024a)分配多维分数，并将它们聚合成 RL 训练的标量奖励to assign multi-dimensional scores, and aggregate them into a scalar reward for RL training(Gunjal et al., 2025; Viswanathan et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2026a).

虽然基于标题的 RL 在这些开放式领域中显示出了有希望的结果，但它仍然面临着有限探索的根本挑战：因为 GRPO 只优化了采样的 rollout 组中存在的行为While rubric-based RL has shown promising results in these open-ended domains, it still faces a fundamental challenge of limited exploration: since GRPO only optimizes over behaviors present in the sampled rollout group(Yue et al., 2025; Nguyen et al., 2025)，没有任何部署能够满足的标准，称为, criteria that no rollout manages to satisfy, termed未探索的标准Unexplored Criteria，接收不到有效的优化信号。 最近的方法通过在推出生成期间使用标题作为外部指导来解决这个问题。例如，RuscaRL, receive no effective optimization signal. Recent methods address this issue by using rubrics as external guidance during rollout generation. For example, RuscaRL(Zhou et al., 2025)使用标题标准作为明确的脚手架，以引发更多样化和更高质量的推广，而 HeRLuses rubric criteria as explicit scaffolding to elicit more diverse and higher-quality rollouts, while HeRL(Zhang et al., 2026)利用失败的轨迹及其未满足的标准作为生成修订后的部署的事后指导。 然而，这些探索增强方法仍然有两个重要的缺点。首先，他们介绍了一个leverages failed trajectories and their unmet criteria as hindsight guidance for generating revised rollouts. However, these exploration-enhancement methods still have two important drawbacks. First, they introduce a训练-推理不匹配training-inference mismatch：在训练期间，rollouts 是使用特权信息生成的，因此每个令牌都在外部引导的前缀下进行优化；然而，在推理时，模型必须仅根据自己的前缀生成。这种差异构成了曝光偏差: during training, rollouts are generated with privileged information, so each token is optimized under externally guided prefixes; at inference time, however, the model must generate solely from its own prefixes. This discrepancy constitutes exposure bias(Agarwal et al., 2024; Song and Zheng, 2026; Li et al., 2026b)，其中在引导训练期间可以避免的早期生成错误可能会通过后续的自回归解码传播。这种错误累积对于开放式任务尤其成问题，因为长响应会放大不匹配。, where early generation error that could be avoided during guided training may propagate through subsequent autoregressive decoding. Such error accumulation is especially problematic for open-ended tasks, where long responses amplify the mismatch.

5 相关工作Related Work

基于Rubric的强化学习。Rubric-based RL.

Rubrics 提供了一个结构化接口，用于将 RLVR 扩展到开放式任务，其中响应质量无法通过确切答案或可执行测试来验证。通过将质量分解为明确的标准，例如事实性、完整性、安全性、证据基础和任务效用，基于标准的奖励比整体标量判断提供了更多可解释和可控的监督Rubrics provide a structured interface for extending RLVR to open-ended tasks where response quality cannot be verified by exact answers or executable tests. By decomposing quality into explicit criteria, such as factuality, completeness, safety, evidence grounding, and task utility, rubric-based rewards offer more interpretable and controllable supervision than holistic scalar judgments(Arora et al., 2025; Du et al., 2025)。现有的基于标题的 RL 方法通常会将 LLM 法官的标准分数汇总为 PPO 或 GRPO 优化的标量奖励. Existing rubric-based RL methods typically aggregate criterion-wise scores from an LLM judge into a scalar reward for PPO or GRPO optimization(Gunjal et al., 2025; Viswanathan et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025)。后续工作通过完善评分标准构建、奖励聚合或标准权重来改进这一范式，使评分标准奖励更具辨别力和稳健性. Subsequent work improves this paradigm by refining rubric construction, reward aggregation, or criterion weighting, making rubric rewards more discriminative and robust(Chen et al., 2026; Lan, 2026)。并行工作线使用标题反馈来增强探索。鲁斯卡RL. A parallel line of work uses rubric feedback to enhance exploration. RuscaRL(Zhou et al., 2025)条件在标题指导上生成，而HeRLconditions rollout generation on rubric guidance, while HeRL(Zhang et al., 2026)使用失败轨迹的事后反馈来构建修订后的部署。尽管这些方法有助于发现与先前未满足的标准相关的行为，但它们在推出生成期间依赖于特权信息，引入了训练推理不匹配，并且还忽略了被抑制标准的另一个关键失败。相比之下，CriPO 通过基于策略的自我蒸馏，恢复了对基于标准标题的 RL 的两种故障模式的标准级监督，而不依赖于外部引导的推出。uses hindsight feedback from failed trajectories to construct revised rollouts. Although these methods help discover behaviors associated with previously unmet criteria, they rely on privileged information during rollout generation, introducing a training-inference mismatch, and also overlook another crucial failure of suppressed criteria. In contrast, CriPO recovers criterion-level supervision for both failure modes for standard rubric-based RL through on-policy self-distillation, without relying on externally guided rollouts.

按政策自蒸馏。On-policy Self-distillation.

在策略自蒸馏（OPSD）使用特权或反馈增强上下文下的当前策略作为自教师，将辅助信息转换为密集的代币级监督，同时避免离策略教师不匹配On-policy Self-distillation (OPSD) uses the current policy under privileged or feedback-augmented contexts as a self-teacher, converting auxiliary information into dense token-level supervision while avoiding off-policy teacher mismatch(Agarwal et al., 2024; Zhao et al., 2026; Hübotter et al., 2026)。尽管有其前景，但由于代币级梯度噪声、师生不一致和特权信息泄漏，密集 OPSD 往往不稳定. Despite its promise, dense OPSD is often unstable due to noisy token-level gradients, teacher–student inconsistency, and privileged-information leakage(Yang et al., 2026; Kim et al., 2026b; Zhu et al., 2026; Li et al., 2026a; Kim et al., 2026a)。因此，最近的工作越来越多地将 OPSD 与 RLVR 结合起来，使用参考解决方案、经过验证的轨迹或环境反馈来改进稀疏奖励优化. Recent work therefore increasingly combines OPSD with RLVR, using reference solutions, verified traces, or environment feedback to improve sparse-reward optimization(Yang et al., 2026; Lu et al., 2026; Pan et al., 2026)。这些研究大多数集中在可验证的领域，例如数学推理，其中特权监督与目标相对较好地一致。最近的并行工作已经开始探索 OPSD 在开放式任务中基于标题的后期培训. Most of these studies focus on verifiable domains such as mathematical reasoning, where privileged supervision is relatively well aligned with the objective. Recent concurrent work has begun exploring OPSD for rubric-based post-training in open-ended tasks(Rezaei et al., 2026; Gu et al., 2026)。与这些主要执行密集响应级自蒸馏的方法不同，CriPO 保留 GRPO 作为基于奖励的主干，并使用 OPSD 通过本地化代币级监督为未探索和抑制的标准提供有针对性的修正。. Unlike these methods, which primarily perform dense response-level self-distillation, CriPO retains GRPO as the reward-grounded backbone and uses OPSD to provide targeted corrections for unexplored and suppressed criteria through localized token-level supervision.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们研究了针对开放式任务的基于规则的强化学习，并确定了由标量奖励优化引起的两种普遍的标准级失败模式：未探索的标准，其中没有推出满足标准，因此没有可用的学习信号；以及抑制标准，其中满足标准的行为由于非积极的总体优势而受到惩罚或忽略。为了解决这两个问题，我们提出了标准蒸馏策略优化（CriPO），它将 GRPO 保留为稳定的基于奖励的骨干网，同时使用策略自蒸馏进行有针对性的代币级别修正。具体来说，CriPO 通过使用标准注入自教师的局部前向 KL 蒸馏来注入未探索的标准的缺失行为，并通过使用反事实自教师定位与标准相关的令牌并翻转其令牌级别优势来保留有用的抑制行为。医学和科学基准实验表明，CriPO 在跨模型规模和领域的范围内持续改进 GRPO 和评分标准引导的基线，同时达到融合 GRPO 的最佳性能In this work, we study rubric-based reinforcement learning for open-ended tasks and identify two prevalent criterion-level failure modes caused by scalar reward optimization: unexplored criteria, where no rollout satisfies a criterion and thus no learning signal is available, and suppressed criteria, where criterion-satisfying behaviors are penalized or ignored due to non-positive aggregate advantages. To address both issues, we propose Criterion-Distilled Policy Optimization (CriPO), which retains GRPO as a stable reward-grounded backbone while using on-policy self-distillation for targeted token-level correction. Specifically, CriPO injects missing behaviors for unexplored criteria through localized forward-KL distillation with a criterion-injection self-teacher and preserves useful suppressed behaviors by locating criterion-relevant tokens with a counterfactual self-teacher and flipping their token-level advantages. Experiments on medicine and science benchmarks show that CriPO consistently improves over GRPO and rubric-guided baselines across model scales and domains, while reaching the best performance of converged GRPO with roughly2×2\times更少的优化步骤。我们希望我们的工作能够激励社区进一步研究如何共同解决基于标准标题的强化学习中的有限探索和奖励模糊性。fewer optimization steps. We hope our work can inspire the community to further investigate how to jointly address both limited exploration and reward ambiguity in standard rubric-based RL.
