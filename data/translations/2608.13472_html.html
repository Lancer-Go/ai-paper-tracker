摘要Abstract

模拟电路设计是非线性和高维设计空间中的一个耗时的迭代过程，很大程度上依赖于专家的直觉。在最近的发展中，大型语言模型（LLM）通过将自然语言推理引入电路设计任务，引入了一种有前途的方法。大多数基于 LLM 的传统方法提供了分散的解决方案，仅关注规模调整或拓扑生成。这些方法需要手动添加特定的技术知识，效率低下，并且在电路选型时容易产生幻觉。此外，满足不同规格的固有权衡使得当前的方法迭代且乏味。另一个缺点是无法创建创新的拓扑，这可能会由于依赖传统拓扑而导致设计次优。在本文中，我们提出了 AaLLM，这是一种开源端到端多代理 LLM 工作流程，它将用户规范作为输入并输出适当的网表，包括拓扑生成和电路尺寸调整。 AaLLM 自动从研究论文和教科书创建相关知识库，以消除繁琐的手动数据收集。使用该知识库实施检索增强生成（RAG）模型来模拟电路设计专业知识。此外，AaLLM 使用一种新颖的三代理反馈系统，包括确定电路组件值的设计器、仔细检查这些值的批评者以及通过在其他两个代理之间进行仲裁来最小化电路大小迭代的评估器。此外，AaLLM 通过利用微调的序列到序列 (Seq2Seq) 模型生成新颖、有效的拓扑，该模型可以学习传统拓扑中每个连接的效果，并将它们重新组合以形成新电路。 AaLLM 生成的新颖拓扑的品质因数 (FoM) 与已知拓扑相当，对于某些电路而言，其品质因数 (FoM) 高出 3 倍。对多种电路拓扑进行测试，我们的结果显示，与最先进的多代理 LLM 管道相比，推理时的 SPICE 调用数量减少了 3 至 4.5 倍。结果还显示，与现有方法相比，挂钟时间减少了 40 倍。Analog circuit design is a time-consuming, iterative process in a nonlinear and high-dimensional design space that relies heavily on expert intuition. Among recent developments, Large Language Models (LLMs) have introduced a promising approach by bringing natural language reasoning to circuit design tasks. The majority of conventional LLM-based approaches provide fragmented solutions that focus either only on sizing or topology generation. These methods require adding specific technical knowledge manually, which is inefficient and prone to hallucinations during circuit sizing. Moreover, the inherent trade-off in meeting different specs makes current approaches iterative and tedious. Another shortcoming is the inability to create innovative topologies, which may lead to sub-optimal designs due to reliance on conventional topologies. In this paper, we present AaLLM, an open-source end-to-end multi-agent LLM workflow that takes user specs as input and outputs the appropriate netlist, encompassing both topology generation and circuit sizing. AaLLM automates the creation of a relevant knowledge base from research papers and textbooks to combat tedious manual data collection. A Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) model is implemented to emulate circuit design expertise using this knowledge base. Moreover, AaLLM uses a novel tri-agent feedback system comprising a Designer that determines circuit component values, a Critic that scrutinizes these values, and an Evaluator that minimizes circuit sizing iterations by arbitrating between the other two agents. Additionally, AaLLM generates novel, valid topologies by leveraging a fine-tuned Sequence-to-Sequence (Seq2Seq) model that learns the effect of each connection in conventional topologies and recombines them to form new circuits. AaLLM-generated novel topologies achieve a figure of merit (FoM) comparable to that of known topologies, and up to 3x higher for certain circuits. Testing on several circuit topologies, our results show a 3x - 4.5x decrease in the number of SPICE calls at inference when compared to state-of-the-art multi-agent LLM pipelines. The results also show a 40x decrease in wall-clock time compared to existing approaches.

I 简介Introduction

现代模拟电路日益复杂，加上严格的性能要求和工艺变化，使得手动电路设计变得不切实际且耗时The increasing complexity of modern analog circuits, combined with strict performance requirements and process variations, has made manual circuit design impractical and time-consuming[1]。因此，模拟电路设计对自动化的需求无论如何强调都不为过。设计过程分为拓扑选择和电路元件尺寸调整，以实现目标规格。. Therefore, the need for automation in analog circuit design cannot be overstated. The design process is subdivided into topology selection and sizing of circuit elements to achieve the target specs.

机器学习 (ML) 方法在模拟电路尺寸调整方面显示出了巨大的前景。基于神经网络 (NN) 的代理模型、图神经网络 (GNN) 和强化学习 (RL) 等技术已应用于此目的，在速度和准确性方面有了显着提高Machine Learning (ML) methods have shown significant promise in analog circuit sizing. However, most of them learn direct mappings from performance specs (例如e.g.、增益、功率、带宽）到电路设计参数（, gain, power, bandwidth) to circuit design parameters (例如e.g.、元件值、晶体管尺寸），但没有捕获底层设计推理。换句话说，这些模型就像黑匣子一样运行，无法深入了解为什么选择一组特定的设计参数。缺乏推理使得在不满足规格时难以诊断故障或调整设计策略。除了机器学习之外，基于优化的方法也被广泛用于电路尺寸调整, component values, transistor sizes) without capturing the underlying design reasoning. In other words, these models operate as black boxes, offering no insight into why a particular set of design parameters was chosen. This lack of reasoning makes it difficult to diagnose failures or adapt the design strategy when specs are not met. Beyond ML, optimization-based approaches have also been widely used for circuit sizing[6]。虽然这些方法可以实现高精度，但它们依赖于大量的 SPICE 仿真迭代，这使得它们的计算成本昂贵且耗时。. While these methods can achieve high accuracy, they rely on extensive SPICE simulation iterations, making them computationally expensive and time-consuming.

许多现有方法仅关注大小调整，而没有解决拓扑生成问题。拓扑生成具有挑战性，因为它需要了解不同的电路结构如何影响性能权衡。现有的拓扑生成方法要么从小型预定义库中进行选择，要么从基本构建块构建电路，而无需利用对电路物理的任何理解Many existing approaches focus solely on sizing while leaving the topology generation problem unaddressed. Topology generation is challenging as it requires understanding how different circuit structures affect performance trade-offs. Existing methods for topology generation either select from a small predefined library or construct circuits from basic building blocks without leveraging any understanding of circuit physics[7]。前者受到拓扑数量有限的限制，阻碍了对已编目之外的设计的探索。另一方面，后者产生大量候选拓扑，但缺乏有效的方法来评估哪些拓扑具有电功能，从而导致合成时间较长。这些限制凸显了对统一方法的需求，该方法可以在单个框架内处理拓扑生成和电路尺寸调整。. The former is constrained by the limited number of topologies, preventing exploration of designs beyond what has been cataloged. On the other hand, the latter produces a large number of candidate topologies, but it lacks an efficient way to evaluate which ones are electrically functional, resulting in long synthesis times. These limitations highlight the need for a unified approach that handles both topology generation and circuit sizing within a single framework.

However, most of these approaches treat sizing as a single-shot generation task without simulation feedback, determining circuit parameter values based on pervasive pattern recognition rather than circuit reasoning. This often leads to hallucinated sizing decisions that fail to meet specs or violate device physics. Furthermore, existing LLM-based approaches lack the ability to diagnose why a design fails; they can identify that a spec is unmet, but cannot trace the root cause to a specific circuit component. Without iterative refinement and diagnostic reasoning, these methods often improve one spec at the expense of another.

This module is used to evaluate and select the most suitable topology for the given specs. The retrieval module searches a curated database of analog circuit design literature to provide the agents with relevant design knowledge during both topology selection and sizing. This allows the agents to ground their decisions in established circuit theory rather than relying solely on patterns learned during training. As a result, AaLLM handles unfamiliar spec combinations by drawing on relevant prior work, rather than being limited to what it has seen during fine-tuning. Furthermore, the retrieval module autonomously expands its knowledge base by fetching and processing new research papers from academic repositories, ensuring the agents have access to up-to-date design information. This dynamic update capability eliminates the need for manual curation, keeping the system up to date with the latest research in the field.

为了支持新颖的拓扑生成，AaLLM 采用了微调To support novel topology generation, AaLLM employs a fine-tuned[11]在组件连接的完整组合空间上运行的生成模型。这使得该框架能够产生现有文献中可能未出现的传统和新颖的拓扑。然后，在将生成的候选拓扑传递到规模调整阶段之前，通过 RAG 模块评估其可行性，确保只有可行的拓扑才能进行优化。generative model that operates over the full combinatorial space of component connections. This allows the framework to produce both conventional and novel topologies that may not appear in existing literature. The generated candidates are then evaluated for feasibility through the RAG module before being passed to the sizing stage, ensuring that only viable topologies proceed to optimization.

对于电路尺寸调整，AaLLM 采用三代理架构，包括设计者、批评者和评估者，它们在迭代循环中协同工作以细化电路参数。该循环依赖于 SPICE 模拟，为代理进行推理提供准确的电路性能规格。在每次迭代中，设计者确定电路组件值，批评者仔细检查这些值，评估者通过在其他两个代理之间进行仲裁来最小化电路尺寸迭代。优化遵循基于课程的方法，其中设计目标按照深思熟虑的顺序来实现，从而使每个规范都能得到满足，而不会破坏之前满足的规范。For circuit sizing, AaLLM employs a tri-agent architecture comprising a Designer, a Critic, and an Evaluator, which work together in an iterative loop to refine the circuit parameters. The loop relies on SPICE simulations, providing accurate circuit performance specs for the agents to reason over. At each iteration, the Designer determines circuit component values, the Critic scrutinizes these values, and the Evaluator minimizes circuit sizing iterations by arbitrating between the other two agents. The optimization follows a curriculum-based approach, where design objectives are addressed in a deliberate sequence, allowing each spec to be met without disrupting previously satisfied ones.

AaLLM 将两种不同类型的语言模型配对：一种专门针对拓扑生成进行微调，另一种通过多代理系统协作推理来执行电路大小调整。这种组合使得每个模型都能应用在最有效的地方。微调模型从电路数据中捕获拓扑级模式，而多代理系统为选型过程带来灵活的电路推理。为了进一步增强代理的推理能力，AaLLM 结合了 RAG，其中从模拟电路教科书和技术论文中检索相关设计知识，并将其用作拓扑生成和尺寸调整期间的上下文。这使得代理能够根据既定的电路理论做出决策，即使对于不熟悉的拓扑或规格组合，他们也能做出明智的设计选择。对于拓扑生成，AaLLM 采用序列到序列 (Seq2Seq) 模型，将一组所需规范转换为有效的电路网表。通过将拓扑生成视为翻译任务，该模型可以学习各种电路设计的结构模式，从而使其能够生成传统和新颖的拓扑。 Seq2Seq 模型非常适合此任务，因为它们自然地捕获电路元件之间的依赖关系。此外，对特定电路数据的微调使模型能够学习控制有效模拟拓扑的结构约束和连接规则，从而产生比提示通用模型更可靠的输出。AaLLM pairs two distinct types of language models: one fine-tuned specifically for topology generation and another that collaboratively reasons through a multi-agent system to perform circuit sizing. This combination allows each model to be applied where it is most effective. The fine-tuned model captures topology-level patterns from circuit data, while the multi-agent system brings flexible circuit reasoning to the sizing process. To further enhance the agents’ reasoning capabilities, AaLLM incorporates RAG, in which relevant design knowledge from analog circuit textbooks and technical papers is retrieved and used as context during both topology generation and sizing. This enables the agents to ground their decisions in established circuit theory, allowing them to make informed design choices even for unfamiliar topologies or spec combinations. For topology generation, AaLLM employs a Sequence-to-Sequence (Seq2Seq) model that translates a set of desired specs into a valid circuit netlist. By treating topology generation as a translation task, the model learns structural patterns across a wide range of circuit designs, enabling it to produce both conventional and novel topologies. Seq2Seq models are well-suited for this task as they naturally capture the dependencies between circuit elements. Moreover, fine-tuning on circuit-specific data enables the model to learn the structural constraints and connectivity rules that govern valid analog topologies, yielding more reliable outputs than prompting a general-purpose model.

Ⅲ-FIII-F Tri-Agent 尺寸环Tri-Agent Sizing Loop

AaLLM 采用三个专门的 LLM 代理（评估者、批评者和设计者），在 SPICE 模拟器周围的闭环中运行。每个代理扮演人类设计师按顺序应用的角色：诊断当前结果（批评者），决定策略（评估者），并执行电路参数更改（设计者）。这些角色是故意分开的，而不是将它们分解为单个代理，因为每个角色都受益于不同的推理模式。AaLLM employs three specialized LLM agents (Evaluator, Critic and Designer) operating in a closed loop around a SPICE simulator. Each agent plays a role that a human designer would apply in sequence: diagnose the current result (Critic), decide on a strategy (Evaluator), and execute a circuit parameter change (Designer). These roles are deliberately separated rather than collapsing them into a single agent, as each benefits from a different reasoning mode.

Ⅲ-GIII-G 基于课程的优化Curriculum-Based Optimization

模拟电路规格是相关的。换句话说，改变一个规范会导致其他规范的改变。课程控制器强制执行模仿人类设计师如何处理问题的流程。优化通过三个连续阶段进行：直流偏置、交流分析和最终的瞬态分析。每个阶段仅在满足其目标时才会升级，后续阶段会运行背景检查，如果早期目标回归超出宽松的容忍范围，则可以降级循环。活动课程阶段在每次迭代时都会直接注入评论家和设计师的提示中，以便代理在整个轨迹中推理出比完整多目标规范更简单的局部问题。Analog circuit specs are correlated. In other words, changing one spec leads to a change in others. The curriculum controller enforces a process flow that mimics how a human designer would approach the problem. Optimization proceeds through three sequential phases: DC biasing, AC analysis and finally transient analysis. Each phase is promoted only when its objectives are met, and later phases run background checks that can demote the loop if earlier objectives regress beyond relaxed tolerances. The active curriculum phase is injected directly into the Critic’s and Designer’s prompts at every iteration, so that the agents reason about a locally simpler problem than the full multi-objective specs throughout the trajectory.

V 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 AaLLM，这是一种用于模拟电路设计的开源全自动框架，可在单个自动化管道中处理拓扑生成和电路尺寸调整。经过微调的 FLAN-T5 模型生成候选拓扑作为二分组件节点矩阵，RAG 模块使用已建立的电路理论选择最合适的拓扑，三代理调整循环通过 SPICE 在环仿真来细化电路参数。 AaLLM 能够生成超出其训练语料库的电功能新颖拓扑。我们的结果显示，与 SOTA 方法相比，使用 AaLLM 的 SPICE 模拟减少了 3 至 4.5 倍，挂钟时间减少了 40 倍。In this work, we introduce AaLLM, an open-source, fully-automated framework for analog circuit design that handles both topology generation and circuit sizing in a single automated pipeline. A fine-tuned FLAN-T5 model generates candidate topologies as bipartite component-node matrices, a RAG module selects the most suitable topology using established circuit theory, and a tri-agent sizing loop refines the circuit parameters through SPICE-in-the-loop simulation. AaLLM is capable of generating electrically functional novel topologies beyond its training corpus. Our results show a 3x - 4.5x decrease in SPICE simulations and a 40x decrease in wall-clock time using AaLLM compared to SOTA methods.
