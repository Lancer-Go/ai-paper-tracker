摘要Abstract

经典的持续学习（CL）主要侧重于使模型能够通过以参数为中心的机制来更新和保留知识，例如训练策略、架构设计和权重适应。 然而，新兴范式正在重塑 CL 的范围，超越这种传统的模型适应观点。 例如，在策略学习拓宽了更新机制的空间；测试时训练将 CL 从训练阶段延伸到推理阶段；内存、技能库和交互协议等外部线束组件将模型功能的进化边界扩展到静态参数空间之外。 总的来说，这些发展表明从以参数为中心的学习向系统级适应的转变。 为了描述这一转变，我们从三个维度审视持续学习的演变：Classical continual learning (CL) has primarily focused on enabling models to update and retain knowledge through parameter-centric mechanisms, e.g., training strategies, architectural designs, and weight adaptation. However, emerging paradigms are reshaping the scope of CL beyond this traditional model adaptation view. For instance, on-policy learning broadens the space of update mechanisms; test-time training extends CL from the training phase to inference; and external harness components such as memory, skill libraries, and interaction protocols extend the evolutionary boundaries of model capabilities far beyond the static parameter space. Collectively, these developments indicate a transition from parameter-centric learning toward system-level adaptation. To characterize this transition, we examine the evolution of continual learning through three dimensions:当When, 如何How, 和, and哪里Where学习发生。 的learning occurs. The如何How维度包括离策略、在策略和超梯度优化机制。 的dimension encompasses off-policy, on-policy, and beyond-gradient optimization mechanics. The当When维度捕获了训练前、训练后和推理时间阶段的演变。 的dimension captures evolution across pre-training, post-training, and inference-time stages. 4 未来展望与讨论Future Outlook and Discussion

这项调查回顾了大型模型时代的持续学习如何远远超出其经典设置：能力现在在模型生命周期的不同点进行更新，由参数以及外部记忆、技能和协议承载，并由更广泛的更新机制驱动。基于总体情况，而不是任何单一方法，我们退一步讨论几个开放趋势以及它们对未来工作提出的问题。我们的中心观察是，该领域正在从一个孤立的算法问题（即在单一模型中抑制灾难性遗忘）转向一个更广泛的问题，即如何在整个系统中组织能力：它应该存在于哪里，何时应该更新，以及如何组合、保留和释放。我们围绕五个主题组织讨论：结构性上限，限制了能力可以被推入更长期背景的程度（第 1 节）This survey has reviewed how continual learning in the era of large models has spread well beyond its classical setting: capability is now updated at different points in the model lifecycle, carried by parameters as well as by external memory, skills, and protocols, and driven by a widening range of update mechanisms. 4.1 从更长的上下文到持久的能力From Longer Context to Persistent Capability

LLM时代的一个决定性变化是，能力的载体不再局限于参数张量，而是向外迁移，从参数到外部存储器，再到上下文窗口。了解每层的容量、可压缩性和衰减是后续讨论的基础。A defining change of the LLM era is that the carrier of capability is no longer confined to the parameter tensor but migrates outward, from parameters to external memory and then to the context window. The trade-off between stability and plasticity, classically posed at the parameter layer, thus shifts to the memory layer and calls for its own metrics.

总而言之，本节集中讨论一个仍然缺乏系统解决方案的问题：从长远来看，哪些能力应该内化为参数，哪些能力应该保留在内存中，哪些能力应该在上下文中使用和丢弃。然而，在询问如何组织这种调度之前，我们首先面临一个先前的反对意见：已经用于手动管理这些载体的线束工程本身是否足够，我们接下来将讨论这一点（第 1 节）。Taken together, this section converges on a question that still lacks a systematic solution: which capabilities should be internalized into parameters over the long run, which should be retained in memory, and which should be used and discarded within context. Before asking how such scheduling might be organized, however, we first confront a prior objection: whether the harness engineering already used to manage these carriers by hand is in itself enough, which we take up next (Section4.2).

4.2 为什么线束级别的积累还不够Why Harness-Level Accumulation Is Not Enough

由于单一运营商无法满足需求，因此必须同时将能力分散到多个运营商，并且必须采取某种措施来管理它们的组合方式。如今，管理工作主要由手工完成，这就提出了一个值得提出的问题：现在的工程进展是否已经在一定程度上相当于持续学习。这个问题很重要，因为许多广泛部署的能力扩展手段，例如编码助手中的规则文件、代理框架中系统提示的演变以及工具库的稳定增长，并不是来自学术意义上的学习算法，而是来自迭代的工程实践。Since no single carrier suffices, capability has to be spread across several at once, and something has to manage how they are combined. Today that management is largely done by hand, which prompts a question worth asking: whether the engineering progress now carried on the harness already amounts, in part, to continual learning. The question matters because many widely deployed means of extending capability, such as rule files in coding assistants, the evolution of system prompts in agent frameworks, and the steady growth of tool libraries, do not come from learning algorithms in the academic sense but from iterative engineering practice.

我们的立场是，安全带的工程进展已经实现了功能层面持续学习目标的一部分，但并没有在机械层面取代它，原因有三个。Our position is that the engineering progress on the harness already attains part of the goal of continual learning at the functional level, but does not replace it at the mechanistic level, for three reasons.第一First，线束提供的是外部可编辑的能力，而不是自我积累的能力：规则文件或代理工具列表可以被人类反复修改，使系统行为不断改进，但修改的代理是人，并不构成系统自身经验驱动的闭环。通过持续学习，我们的意思是主体通过与环境的交互自主地获取和更新能力。, the harness offers externally editable capability rather than self-accumulated capability: a rule file or an agent tool list can be revised repeatedly by humans so that system behavior keeps improving, but the agent of that revision is a person, and it does not constitute a closed loop driven by the system’s own experience. By continual learning we mean that the subject acquires and updates capability autonomously through interaction with the environment.第二Second，少数确实关闭循环的方法，例如 Promptbreeder, the few methods that do close the loop, such as Promptbreeder[fernando2023promptbreeder], 航行者号, Voyager[wang2023voyager]和 AgentEvolver, and AgentEvolver[zhai2025agentevolver]，表明harness层确实可以承载真正意义上的持续学习，但其更新机制要么是进化搜索，要么是强化学习，并且在评估、稳定性和可重复性方面还远未成熟，如第1节所述, show that the harness layer can indeed carry continual learning in the genuine sense, yet their update mechanisms are either evolutionary search or reinforcement learning, and they remain far from mature in evaluation, stability, and reproducibility, as discussed in Section4.4. 第三Third，工具上的能力与上下文强耦合，并且比参数中的能力更不容易转移：为一个项目编写的规则文件或为一个框架积累的技能库绑定到特定的上下文，并且当上下文发生变化时很难无损地转移，而参数张量虽然修改成本很高，但一旦内化就具有相对可转移的能力。现在可以澄清一个常见的混淆：工程进展正在接近 AGI 的直觉取决于线束上可见的能力积累，但该能力的很大一部分存在于模型周围的脚手架中，而不是模型本身。, capability on the harness is strongly coupled to context and transfers less readily than capability in parameters: a rule file written for one project or a skill library accumulated for one framework is bound to a specific context and is hard to transfer without loss when that context changes, whereas a parameter tensor, though costly to modify, carries capability that is comparatively transferable once internalized. A common conflation can now be clarified: the intuition that engineering progress is approaching AGI rests on the visible accumulation of capability on the harness, but a substantial part of that capability resides in the scaffolding around the model rather than in the model itself.

这与值得公正地进行的公开辩论有关。一种观点认为，即使在基础模型时代，持续学习仍然至关重要，因为部署的模型是训练时世界的快照，必须应对任务转移和时移遗忘This connects to an open debate worth presenting evenhandedly. A second position argues that the lack of continual learning is the primary bottleneck on the path from current systems to AGI, since prompt engineering and long rolling contexts are brittle patches that do not generalize beyond text-centric domains[patel2025timelines]。第三种观点反驳说，持续学习是一个系统问题，而不是学习问题，认为更多的上下文和更多的计算将使由多个模型、记忆和检索组成的系统的行为与持续学习的系统没有区别，因此不需要让模型太类似于人类. A third position counters that continual learning is a systems problem rather than a learning problem, contending that more context and more computation will let a system composed of several models, memory, and retrieval behave indistinguishably from one that learns continually, so that one need not make the model resemble a human too closely[lambert2025contra]。这些立场映射了本次调查的中心张力，即工程补偿和机械内在化之间。我们自己的理解是，工具上的工程进步在功能上替代了持续学习，但尚未在机械上构成持续学习，未来持续学习研究应该扮演的角色是逐渐将现在工程实践承载的能力积累转化为系统自身机制承载的过程。这就留下了一个悬而未决的问题，激发了本节其余部分的讨论：如果手工调整的工程本身不是一种自我驱动的机制，那么这样的机制会是什么样子？我们转向下一节（节. Our own reading is that engineering progress on the harness functionally substitutes for, but does not yet mechanistically constitute, continual learning, and that the role future continual-learning research should take on is to gradually convert the capability accumulation now carried by engineering practice into a process carried by the system’s own mechanisms. This leaves an open question that motivates the rest of this section: if hand-tuned engineering is not in itself a self-driven mechanism, what might such a mechanism look like? 4.3 协调内存、技能、协议和参数Coordinating Memory, Skills, Protocols, and Parameters

我们认为手工调整的工程在功能上是有用的，但其本身并不是一种自我驱动的机制，现在我们要问一个真正的机制可能是什么样子。仅模型和仅利用工具的更新都无法完全捕获 LLM 系统中能力的组织方式，因为能力现在同时分布在参数、外部存储器、技能和协议中。因此，一个自然的方向是询问模型和线束是否以及如何以协调的方式更新。我们没有将两者的协调演进视为一个固定的解决方案，而是将其作为多航母能力组织所建议的方向，并通过尚未系统研究的三个耦合来构建它。Having argued that hand-tuned engineering is functionally useful but not in itself a self-driven mechanism, we now ask what a genuine mechanism might look like. Neither model-only nor harness-only updates fully capture how capability is organized in LLM systems, since capability is now distributed across parameters, external memory, skills, and protocols at once. A natural direction is therefore to ask whether, and how, the model and the harness should be updated in a coordinated manner. Rather than treating the coordinated evolution of the two as a settled solution, we present it as a direction suggested by this multi-carrier organization of capability, and we frame it through three couplings that have not yet been studied systematically.

参数和线束之间的双向传输。Bidirectional transfer between parameters and harness.SKILL0 采取的路线，先探索线束上的技能，然后将其内化为参数The route taken by SKILL0, which explores skills on the harness before internalizing them into parameters[lu2026skill0]，说明一个方向。相反的方向可能同样重要：将难以从参数调用的潜在能力显式为工具上的可见技能或记忆项，从而提高调用效率和可解释性。完整的双向机制必须回答何时将功能整合到参数中（对于频繁调用并在任务之间广泛共享的项目），以及何时将功能返回到工具（对于特定于上下文或尚未稳定的项目）。这与计算机系统中的高速缓存和内存层次结构管理有结构上的类比，但尚未存在相应的正式框架。, illustrates one direction. The reverse direction may matter just as much: making latent capability that is hard to invoke from parameters explicit as a visible skill or memory item on the harness, so as to improve invocation efficiency and interpretability. A complete bidirectional mechanism would have to answer when to consolidate capability into parameters, for items that are frequently invoked and broadly shared across tasks, and when to return capability to the harness, for items that are context-specific or not yet stable. This has a structural analogy with cache and memory hierarchy management in computer systems, but no corresponding formal framework yet exists.

跨运营商的调度能力。Scheduling capability across carriers.从这个角度来看，协调在很大程度上是一个调度问题：什么应该留在上下文中，什么应该写入内存，什么应该巩固为技能，以及什么最终应该内化为参数。任何这样的时间表还必须考虑载体的不同更新节奏，因为在个人交互的层面上，记忆被写入最频繁，在任务的层面上，技能以中等速率扩展，而在项目或长期约束的层面上，协议和参数的修订最慢。协调以不同速率移动的载体之间的写入和回收是将这一方向从直觉转变为机制的一个关键的开放问题，并且它也缺乏统一的解决方案。Seen this way, coordination is in large part a scheduling problem: what should stay in context, what should be written to memory, what should be consolidated into skills, and what should ultimately be internalized into parameters. Any such schedule would also have to account for the differing update cadences of the carriers, since memory is written most frequently, at the level of individual interactions, skills expand at an intermediate rate, at the level of tasks, and protocols and parameters are revised most slowly, at the level of projects or long-term constraints. Coordinating writing and reclamation across carriers that move at different rates is a key open problem for turning this direction from intuition into mechanism, and it too lacks a unified solution.

遗忘变得多方面且难以定位。Forgetting becomes multi-faceted and hard to localize.在经典的持续学习中，遗忘是一维的，因为参数被覆盖。复合载体下至少有四个方面：参数层的灾难性遗忘、记忆层的检索衰减和条目老化、上下文层的窗口溢出和注意力稀释、上下文漂移导致的技能和协议层的能力不匹配。它们在时间尺度、可观察性和可逆性方面存在差异，很难用单一指标来描述，并且很难定位遗忘实际发生的位置。鉴于此，将遗忘从参数层的灾难重铸为跨复合载体主动压缩和释放的过程可能更有用，这反过来又需要统一标准来决定作用于哪一层、多快以及保留或释放什么。从这种观点来看，制定这样的标准是将协调进化从直觉转向理论的主要障碍。In classical continual learning, forgetting is one-dimensional, in that parameters are overwritten. Under composite carriers it has at least four faces: catastrophic forgetting at the parameter layer, retrieval decay and entry aging at the memory layer, window overflow and attention dilution at the context layer, and capability mismatch at the skill and protocol layer caused by context drift. These differ in timescale, observability, and reversibility, are hard to describe with a single metric, and make it difficult to localize where forgetting actually occurs. Given this, it may be more useful to recast forgetting, from a catastrophe at the parameter layer into a process of active compression and release across composite carriers, which would in turn call for unified criteria for which layer to act on, how fast, and what to retain or release. Developing such criteria is, on this view, the central obstacle to moving coordinated evolution from intuition to theory.

4.4 长期代理作为真正的测试平台Long-Horizon Agents as the Real Testbed

如果沿着这些思路进行协调更新不仅仅是一种直觉，它就必须是可测试的。未来的持续学习不应通过短期任务或静态基准来判断，而应通过智能体是否能够在开放环境中的长期、多步骤交互中保持其目标、跟踪状态、纠正错误并积累经验来判断。这正是剖面的承载天花板的设置If coordinated updating along these lines is to be more than an intuition, it has to be testable. Future continual learning should be judged not by short tasks or static benchmarks, but by whether an agent can hold its goal, track state, correct errors, and accumulate experience across long, multi-step interactions in open environments. This is precisely the setting in which the carrier ceilings of Section4.1以及第 3 节中讨论的协调更新and the coordinated updating discussed in Section4.3相互作用以及问题最容易出现的地方。interact and where problems surface most readily.

为什么固定集上的训练是不够的。Why training on a fixed set is insufficient.静态基准衡量模型附带的能力，而长期任务衡量操作期间能力的净增益。在固定测试集​​上得分较高的模型不需要在数十或数百个交互步骤中保持目标不漂移或状态丢失。这将评估标准从单点精度推向轨迹级测量，并解释了为什么测试时训练等推理时更新机制变得必要：能力必须在推理和部署期间得到补充，而不是在训练时冻结。最近的长期内存基准测试和调查正好回应了这一差距，将代理内存视为从存储到反射再到体验的演变，并将长期一致性视为主要驱动因素A static benchmark measures the capability a model ships with, whereas a long-horizon task measures the net gain of capability during operation. A model that scores highly on a fixed test set need not keep its goal from drifting or its state from being lost over tens or hundreds of interaction steps. This pushes the evaluation criterion from single-point accuracy toward trajectory-level measures, and it explains why inference-time update mechanisms such as test-time training become necessary: capability must be replenished during inference and deployment rather than frozen at training time. Recent long-horizon memory benchmarks and surveys respond to exactly this gap, casting agent memory as an evolution from storage to reflection to experience and treating long-range consistency as a primary driver[luo2026storage].

内存压缩和架构是结合在一起的。Memory compression and architecture are drawn in together.在长期运行中，记忆不能无限制地增长，必须不断地压缩、巩固和遗忘，这与第 1 节中讨论的选择性减少相呼应。Over long runs, memory cannot grow without bound and must be continually compressed, consolidated, and forgotten, echoing the selective reduction discussed in Section4.1。当上下文和外部记忆都达到上限时，承载长期能力的负担就会回到模型架构本身。因此，长期行动不仅仅是一个长期任务的问题，更是一个长期任务的问题。它迫使内存机制、更新时序和架构设计一致改变，这也是为什么很少通过单点改进获得长期能力的原因。. When both context and external memory reach their ceilings, the burden of carrying long-horizon capability flows back to the model architecture itself. Long-horizon operation is therefore not merely a matter of longer tasks; it forces memory mechanisms, update timing, and architectural design to change in concert, which is also why long-horizon capability is rarely obtained through a single-point improvement.

错误积累是核心难点。Error accumulation is the core difficulty.当任务跨越数十到数百个步骤时，每个步骤的小错误会沿着调用链累积并放大：步骤检索不正确When a task spans tens to hundreds of steps, small per-step errors accumulate and amplify along the call chain: an incorrect retrieval at stepkk可能会导致代理在步骤中调用错误的技能can lead the agent to invoke the wrong skill at stepk+1k{+}1并整体改变后来的决定。这种复合误差是在经典连续学习的顺序学习环境中研究的，但它在 LLM 代理系统中呈现出新的特征。首先，错误不仅出现在参数层，而且出现在由内存、技能、协议和参数组成的复合状态中。其次，大多数线束操作是不可微分的，因此传统的基于梯度的灵敏度分析不再适用。第三，智能体在训练和评估之间缺乏明确的界限，因此错误的状态可能会通过即时反馈被写回到内存中，并形成正反馈循环。反射通过言语自我反省来纠正and shift later decisions as a whole. Such compounding error is studied in the sequential-learning setting of classical continual learning, but it takes on new features in LLM agent systems. First, errors arise not only at the parameter layer but across a composite state made up of memory, skills, protocols, and parameters. Second, most harness operations are non-differentiable, so conventional gradient-based sensitivity analysis no longer applies. Third, an agent lacks a clear boundary between training and evaluation, so an erroneous state may be written back into memory through immediate feedback and form a positive feedback loop. Reflexion corrects through verbal self-reflection[shinn2023reflexion]TTRL 通过部署时更新进行纠正and TTRL corrects through deployment-time updates[ttrl2025]，表明抗漂移路线是可行的，但两者仍然是单一机制并且缺乏统一的框架。因此，为长视野智能体建立可验证的错误累积模型和抗漂移机制是未来几年最具挑战性和最有价值的方向之一。, showing that drift-resistant routes are feasible, yet both remain single-mechanism and lack a unified framework. Establishing a verifiable model of error accumulation and drift-resistant mechanisms for long-horizon agents is thus one of the most challenging and valuable directions for the coming years.

4.5 持续学习是通向 AGI 的首要任务Continual Learning as a Priority on the Path to AGI

最开放性的问题是，内存和线束路线距离真正的通用人工智能还有多远。由于对 AGI 的任何预测都存在过度乐观或悲观的风险，因此本小节没有提供明确的答案。相反，它汇集了上述发展中可见的几个信号，然后对研究重点做出了明确的判断。The most open-ended question is how far the memory-and-harness route still is from genuine AGI. Because any forecast of AGI risks excess optimism or pessimism, this subsection offers no definite answer. Instead it draws together several signals visible in the developments reviewed above and then states a clear judgment about research priorities.

信号一：研究单元从模型延伸到系统。Signal one: the unit of study extends from the model to the system.经典的持续学习研究的是模型，而LLM时代真正的工作单元越来越多地是一个由模型、内存、技能、协议、工具和环境组成的系统。值得追求的范例是端到端的可学习代理框架，其中线束的组件不再是手工构建的脚手架，而是与模型共同训练和进化的可学习模块。 AgentEvolver 提供早期演示Classical continual learning studies a model, whereas the real unit of work in the LLM era is increasingly a system composed of the model, memory, skills, protocols, tools, and environment. A paradigm worth pursuing is an end-to-end learnable agent framework, in which the components of the harness are no longer hand-built scaffolding but learnable modules trained and evolved jointly with the model. AgentEvolver offers an early demonstration[zhai2025agentevolver]，但系统级持续学习的完整理论尚不存在。, but a complete theory of system-level continual learning does not yet exist.

信号二：可验证的奖励作为桥梁。Signal two: verifiable rewards as a bridge.具有可验证奖励的强化学习Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards[shao2024deepseekmath]，以 DeepSeek-R1 风格训练管道, training pipelines in the style of DeepSeek-R1[deepseekai2025deepseekr1]，以及 TTRL 在部署时使用可验证信号, and the use of verifiable signals at deployment time by TTRL[ttrl2025]共同指出一个转变：当任务结果可以通过代码执行、数学解决方案的正确性或通过单元测试自动验证时，代理可以在没有人工标签的情况下不断改进自己。因此，可验证的奖励是从工程进步到持续学习机制的关键桥梁。这种局限性同样明显，因为许多实际任务，例如写作、咨询和长期规划，没有明确的可验证信号，因此未来的工作需要半可验证奖励或复合反馈的框架。point together to one shift: when a task outcome can be verified automatically, through code execution, the correctness of a mathematical solution, or passing unit tests, an agent can keep improving itself without human labels. Verifiable rewards are thus a key bridge from engineering progress to a continual-learning mechanism. The limitation is equally clear, since many real tasks, such as writing, consulting, and long-horizon planning, carry no explicit verifiable signal, so future work needs frameworks for semi-verifiable rewards or composite feedback.

信号三：遗忘被重新解释为能力管理而不是缺陷。Signal three: forgetting reinterpreted as capability management rather than a defect.正如章节中的重铸As recast in Section4.3，选择性遗忘最好被视为管理能力的主动手段，而不是被动的下降；内存系统，例如 MemoryBank, selective forgetting is better seen as an active means of managing capability than as a passive decline; memory systems such as MemoryBank[zhong2024memorybank]和原子存储器and atomic memory[xu2025amem]、工具库的剪枝、上下文的有限性都指向这一点，基础模型视角同样将选择性遗忘作为一个重要方向，区分任务转移和时间转移遗忘, the pruning of tool libraries, and the finiteness of context all point this way, and the foundation-model perspective likewise treats selective forgetting as an important direction, distinguishing task-shift from time-shift forgetting[bell2025futurecl].

对 AGI 优先事项的判断。A judgment about AGI priorities.我们倾向于将持续学习在AGI道路上的作用定位如下：前沿模型的持续学习是第一要务，而特定领域的持续学习则不是。未来持续学习的价值不在于让狭隘模型反复吸收某一领域的知识，而更多在于让前沿模型在广泛的知识和技能上不断成长。要实现这一目标，需要在这些维度上进行协调改进，涵盖参数和线束，而不是仅仅依赖于特定领域的知识或仅涉及模型或线束的单点更改，尽管在现阶段，仅更改模型或仅更改线束仍然可以产生相当大的收益。这一判断与上述争论的两个极端是一致的：它接受了持续学习是通向AGI的主要瓶颈的诊断，也接受了系统化路线可以接近这一点的乐观态度，同时强调了前沿模型层面上能力的系统化组织。有人进一步认为，一旦真正解决了在线学习问题，模型就可以将其在所有副本中学到的知识汇集起来，以便单个系统有效地学习每项工作，这有助于解释为什么前沿模型的持续学习具有如此压倒性的优先级We are inclined to locate the role of continual learning on the path to AGI as follows: continual learning for the frontier model is the first priority, whereas domain-specific continual learning is not. The value of future continual learning lies less in having a narrow model repeatedly absorb the knowledge of one domain, and more in letting the frontier model keep growing across a broad range of knowledge and skills. Achieving this requires coordinated improvement across these dimensions, spanning both the parameters and the harness, rather than relying on domain-specific knowledge alone or on single-point changes that touch only the model or only the harness, even though, at the present stage, changing only the model or only the harness can still yield sizable gains. This judgment is consistent with both poles of the debate above: it accepts the diagnosis that continual learning is a primary bottleneck toward AGI, and it also accepts the optimism that a systematized route can come close, while placing the emphasis on the systematic organization of capability at the level of the frontier model. It has further been argued that, once online learning is truly solved, a model could pool what it learns across all of its copies, so that a single system effectively learns every job, which helps explain why continual learning for the frontier model carries such an overriding priority[patel2025timelines].

对距离的诚实估计。An honest estimate of the distance.综合考虑这些信号，我们倾向于认为，记忆与装备路线正在迅速接近工程级别通用助理的能力上限，但真正的通用人工智能（一个能够自主能力扩展、跨域转移和强大的长视野决策能力的系统）仍然有相当大的距离，而且这个距离不会仅仅通过更长的上下文、更大的记忆或更多的技能来缩小。其他条件仍然不确定，但不断学习是道路上不可避免的一部分；将其从孤立的算法问题重塑为能力如何组织的系统问题，是本次调查所倡导的观点所要继续指向的方向。Taking the signals together, we are inclined to believe that the memory-and-harness route is rapidly approaching the capability ceiling of a general assistant at the engineering level, but that genuine AGI, a system capable of autonomous capability expansion, cross-domain transfer, and robust long-horizon decision making, remains a considerable distance away, and that this distance will not be closed simply by longer context, larger memory, or more skills. Other conditions remain uncertain, yet continual learning is an unavoidable part of the path; recasting it from an isolated algorithmic problem into a systematic question of how capability is organized is the direction that the perspective advocated in this survey is meant to keep pointing toward.
