NEAT-NC：用于机器人路径规划的 NEAT 引导导航单元NEAT-NC: NEAT guided Navigation Cells for Robot Path Planning

Hibatallah MELIANI 0009-0006-5803-5013 ISI Laboratory, National School of Applied Sciences (ENSA), Abdelmalek Essaadi UniversityTetouanMorocco meliani.hibatallah@etu.uae.ac.ma , Khadija SLIMANI 0000-0001-8036-2260 EsieaLab LDR, Higher School of Computer Science, Electronics and Automation (ESIEA)ParisFrance ISI Laboratory, National School of Applied Sciences (ENSA), Abdelmalek Essaadi UniversityTetouanMorocco and Samira KHOULJI 0000-0003-2015-7913 ISI Laboratory, National School of Applied Sciences (ENSA), Abdelmalek Essaadi UniversityTetouanMorocco
摘要。Abstract.

为了在空间中导航，大脑使用不同的细胞（例如位置细胞、网格细胞、头部方向细胞、边界细胞和速度细胞）对环境进行内部表示。所有这些细胞与感觉输入一起使有机体能够探索其周围的空间。受这些生物学原理的启发，我们开发了 NEAT-NC，一种增强拓扑引导导航细胞的神经进化。本文的目标是使用空间认知细胞提高动态环境中 NEAT 算法的路径规划性能。这种方法使用导航细胞作为输入，并进化出代表大脑海马体部分的循环神经网络。在不同的静态和动态场景下评估所提出算法的性能。这项研究强调了 NEAT 对复杂和不同环境的适应性，展示了生物学理论的实用性。这表明我们的方法非常适合机器人和游戏的实时动态路径规划。To navigate a space, the brain makes an internal representation of the environment using different cells such as place cells, grid cells, head direction cells, border cells, and speed cells. All these cells, along with sensory inputs, enable an organism to explore the space around it. Inspired by these biological principles, we developed NEAT-NC, a NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topology guided Navigation Cells. The goal of the paper is to improve NEAT algorithm performance in path planning in dynamic environments using spatial cognitive cells. This approach uses navigation cells as inputs and evolves recurrent neural networks, representing the hippocampus part of the brain. The performance of the proposed algorithm is evaluated in different static and dynamic scenarios. This study highlights NEAT’s adaptability to complex and different environments, showcasing the utility of biological theories. This suggests that our approach is well-suited for real-time dynamic path planning for robotics and games.

1. 简介Introduction

在导航过程中，大脑形成一种内部空间表征，对环境中位置之间的关系进行编码。这种表示形式称为心理地图。思维地图是在位置细胞的帮助下制作的，当有机体处于特定位置时，位置细胞会放电并形成对该位置的记忆During navigation, the brain forms an internal spatial representation that encodes the relationships among locations in the environment. This representation is called a mental map. A mental map is made with the help of place cells that fire when the organism is in a specific location and form a memory of the place(O’Keefe, 2023) (Sheffield and Dombeck, 2019)。在海马体中发现了位置细胞. Place cells were discovered in the hippocampus(O’Keefe and Dostrovsky, 1971) (Hartley et al., 2014)。后来又发现了其他细胞，比如网格. Later, other cells were discovered, such as grid(Hafting et al., 2008) (Jacobs et al., 2013), 头部方向, head direction(Taube, 2007), 边界, border(Boccara et al., 2010; Lever et al., 2009; Solstad et al., 2008)和速度细胞and speed cells(Kropff et al., 2015)，在大脑的不同部分。当生物体位于地图的某个位置时，位置细胞会被激活，并且当它面对目标时会最大程度地开火, in different parts of the brain. Place cells are activated when the organism is in a certain location of the map and fire at maximum when it is facing the goal(Ormond and O’Keefe, 2022)。边界细胞是当生物体靠近障碍物或边缘时会放电的神经元。当头部面向某个方向时，头部方向细胞就会放电。速度细胞是神经元，其放电率取决于个体的跑步速度。所有这些细胞以及感觉输入使生物体能够在它们周围的空间中导航。基于这些原理，许多研究开发了自主机器人路径规划算法。. Border cells are neurons that fire when the organism is near obstacles or edges. Head direction cells fire when the head is faced towards a certain direction. Speed cells are neurons where the firing rate depends on the running speed of the individual. All these cells, along with sensory inputs, enable an organism to navigate space around them. Based on these principles, much research has developed algorithms for autonomous robot path planning.

自主机器人需要使用传感器从环境中收集信息并处理该输入，以成功地在环境中导航并避开障碍物。路径规划是自主移动机器人技术的核心功能，也是自主机器人导航中最关键的问题之一。它需要利用路径长度、时间和平滑度等指标，在最佳时间内找到一条可行的无障碍路径。路径规划问题主要有两类：静态路径规划问题和动态路径规划问题。第一种类型仅包含环境中的静态障碍物，第二种类型既包含静态障碍物又包含移动障碍物。Autonomous robots need to collect information from the environment using sensors and process this input to successfully navigate the environment and avoid obstacles. Path planning is a core function of autonomous mobile robot technology and is one of the most critical problems in autonomous robot navigation. It requires finding a feasible obstacle-free path in an optimal amount of time, using indicators such as path length, time, and smoothness. There are mainly two types of path planning problems: static and dynamic. The first type contains only static obstacles in the environment, and the second contains both static and moving obstacles.

由于现实世界问题的复杂性增加以及对更具适应性和灵活性的解决方案的需求，路径规划技术已经从经典的确定性方法转向先进的元启发式、生物启发式和基于人工智能的方法。一些已知的算法是遗传算法（GA）、模拟退火（SA）、粒子群优化（PSO）和蚁群优化（ACO）。随着人工智能（AI）和机器学习（ML）的快速发展，这些技术已被广泛用于解决这一问题。它们的适应性和从经验中学习的能力使它们特别适合动态和不确定的环境。还使用了受生物学启发的其他算法，例如增强拓扑的神经进化（NEAT），这是一种优化神经网络权重和结构的进化算法。它随着时间的推移适应网络复杂性的能力使其能够在动态和不可预测的环境中有效地进行强化学习。The path planning techniques have moved from classical deterministic methods to advanced metaheuristic, bio-inspired, and AI-based approaches due to the increased complexity of real-world problems and the need for more adaptive and flexible solutions. Some of the known algorithms are Genetic Algorithm (GA), Simulated Annealing (SA), Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), and Ant Colony Optimization (ACO). With the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), these techniques have been widely used to address this problem. Their adaptive nature and ability to learn from experience make them particularly well suited for dynamic and uncertain environments. Other algorithms inspired by biology were also used, such as NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies (NEAT), which is an evolutionary algorithm that optimizes both the weights and structure of neural networks. Its ability to adapt network complexity over time makes it effective for reinforcement learning in dynamic and unpredictable environments.

The paper focuses on developing a brain-inspired navigation model that draws on spatial cognition cells observed in biological neural systems.The goal of the paper is to improve the NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topology (NEAT) algorithm by feeding inputs required to navigate a dynamic environment. Instead of forming a cognitive mental map, we use the firing of different spatial cells to help the agent navigate the environment. To our knowledge no one has integrated those biological principles into the NEAT algorithm.

在本文中，我们提出了 NEAT 的改进版本，它可以通过添加模仿海马体和导航细胞的架构来解决动态导航问题。In this paper, we propose an improved version of NEAT that can solve the dynamic navigation problem by adding an architecture that mimics the hippocampus and navigation cells.

3. 方法论Methodology

所提出的增强拓扑引导导航单元的 NeuroEvolution（NEAT-NC）使用导航单元作为输入，并将其馈送到循环神经网络（RNN）以解决静态和动态路径规划。该算法使用这些细胞来检测目标、代理周围的障碍物并决定其方向和速度。此外，RNN 还充当空间记忆的角色，可以记住障碍物并避开它们。适应度函数旨在鼓励智能体遵循自信的路径，同时最大限度地减少遍历时间。该算法返回代理的角速度和线速度作为输出。The proposed NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topology guided Navigation Cells (NEAT-NC) uses navigation cells as input and feeds them to the recurrent neural network (RNN) to solve static and dynamic path planning. The algorithm uses those cells to detect goal, obstacles around the agent and decide its direction and speed. In addition, RNN acts as a spatial memory that remembers obstacles and avoids them. A fitness function is designed to encourage agents to follow confident paths while minimizing traversal time. The algorithm returns as outputs the agent’s angular and linear velocity.

3.2. 遗传算子Genetic Operators

NEAT 通过选择、交叉和变异来发展网络。该算法根据个体适应度值从群体中选择个体，并应用精英主义来保留最佳解决方案。然后，选定的个体进行交叉以产生后代。所提出的算法使用结构突变，特别是添加节点和连接以及权重突变，从而调整网络的连接权重，从而能够演化出新的、更有效的解决方案。NEAT evolves networks through selection, crossover, and mutation. Based on individual fitness values, the algorithm selects individuals from the population, and elitism is applied to preserve the best solutions. The selected individuals then undergo crossover to generate offspring. The proposed algorithm uses structural mutations, specifically adding nodes and connections and weight mutations, which adjust the network’s connection weights, enabling the evolution of new, more effective solutions.

It primarily rewards progress toward the goal while penalizing unsafe or inefficient behaviors. Successful goal attainment yields a substantial terminal bonus, with additional rewards for reaching the goal in fewer steps to promote time-efficient navigation. To emulate hippocampus-inspired spatial behavior, the fitness function also incorporates a straight-path bias by penalizing excessive steering.

6. 结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种受大脑启发的导航框架，使用 NEAT 引导的导航单元 (NEAT-NC) 架构来演化循环神经网络 (RNN)。该算法使用位置、边界细胞、头部方向细胞和速度细胞作为 RNN 的输入，有效模仿生物系统的空间映射能力。 NEAT-NC 成功导航不同类型的环境，提高静态和动态环境中路径规划问题的成功率、路径长度和速度。这些发现凸显了将生物学理论整合到算法设计中的潜力。这项工作通过引入自主导航的认知框架来补充我们之前关于七自由度逆运动学的研究。未来的研究将集中于更先进的导航模型以及将导航和操纵集成到在 3D 环境中运行的完全交互式代理中。This paper presented a brain-inspired navigation framework using the NEAT-guided Navigation Cells (NEAT-NC) architecture to evolve a Recurrent Neural Network (RNN). The algorithm uses place, border cells, head direction cells and speed cell as input for the RNN, effectively mimicking the spatial mapping capabilities of biological systems. NEAT-NC successfully navigates different types of environments, improving success rate, path length, and speed in path planning problems in static and dynamic environments. The findings highlight the potential of integrating biological theories into algorithm design. This work complements our previous study on 7-DOF inverse kinematics by introducing a cognitive framework for autonomous navigation. Future research will focus on more advanced navigation models and the integration of navigation and manipulation into a fully interactive agent operating in a 3D environment.
