2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 MLLM 的 Vision 代币修剪Vision Token Pruning for MLLMs

视觉标记修剪是一种广泛研究的方法，通过在语言模型之前或内部删除或合并冗余视觉标记来提高 MLLM 效率。现有方法的主要区别在于用于确定应保留哪些视觉信息的标准。 FastV 和 PruMerge 等基于注意力的方法利用来自语言模型或视觉编码器的注意力信号来识别显着标记Vision token pruning is a widely studied approach to improving MLLM efficiency by removing or consolidating redundant visual tokens before or within the language model. Existing methods primarily differ in the criterion used to identify which visual information should be retained. Attention-based methods such as FastV and PruMerge exploit attention signals from the language model or visual encoder to identify salient tokens[5, 26]，而拟合修剪, while FitPrune[33]通过匹配剪枝前后的注意力统计来导出剪枝配置。其他方法明确针对视觉表示之间的冗余：VisionZipderives pruning configurations by matching attention statistics before and after pruning. Other methods explicitly target redundancy among visual representations: VisionZip[32]保留主导标记，同时压缩上下文标记，DivPrunepreserves dominant tokens while compressing contextual ones, DivPrune[1]促进保留代币和 DART 之间的多样性promotes diversity among retained tokens, and DART[29]根据标记与代表性枢轴的重复来删除标记。最近的工作还研究了修剪的失败模式；例如，RVISremoves tokens according to their duplication with representative pivots. Recent work has also examined failure modes of pruning; for example, RVIS[13]在解码过程中适应修剪，以响应任务相关视觉信息的变化。adapts pruning during decoding in response to changes in task-relevant visual information.

尽管存在这些差异，现有方法从根本上依赖于所有输入的单一固定修剪标准。 VIP-Router 相反，将现有的剪枝策略视为互补的候选策略，并在其中执行样本级别的选择。Despite these differences, existing approaches fundamentally rely on a single fixed pruning criterion for all inputs. VIP-Router instead treats existing pruning strategies as complementary candidates and performs sample-level selection among them.

It achieves parity with five separate ratio-specific routers (38.09 vs. 38.07) while reducing the model overhead from five Stage-A models to just one, supporting the use of query-conditioned spatial interaction in the shared router.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 VIP-Router，这是一个轻量级框架，它重新构建了视觉令牌剪枝，从跨输入统一应用固定标准到为每个样本选择最合适的剪枝策略。 在剪枝敏感的 VTC-Bench Group A 设置上的五个缩减比率中，VIP-Router 持续提高了 Best Fix 的准确性和实用性，在不同的 MLLM 主干上保持有效，并将零样本转移到看不见的基准。 除了这些经验收益之外，我们的结果表明，修剪策略选择本身构成了自适应推理的重要轴。In this paper, we introduced VIP-Router, a lightweight framework that reframes vision token pruning from applying a fixed criterion uniformly across inputs to selecting the most suitable pruning strategy for each sample. Across five reduction ratios on the pruning-sensitive VTC-Bench Group A setting, VIP-Router consistently improves both accuracy and utility over Best Fixed, remains effective across different MLLM backbones, and transfers zero-shot to unseen benchmarks. Beyond these empirical gains, our results show that pruning-strategy choice itself constitutes an important axis of adaptive inference.
