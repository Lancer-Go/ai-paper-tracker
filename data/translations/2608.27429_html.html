Concretely, we formulate the reactant-to-product mapping as a Continuous-time Markov Chain (CTMC) over the graph-structured integer-valued electron occupation space defined on all bonding, non-bonding, and hydrogen sites. Beyond in-distribution accuracy, we evaluate robustness across two out-of-distribution settings - structural complexity and reaction type - and find that MAELLE maintains strong performance where existing methods degrade. Finally, because the learned flow operates over the full electron redistribution, MAELLE naturally recovers mechanistic trajectories that align with known chemistry and can predict side products of a reaction.

1 简介1 Introduction

预测化学反应的结果是化学和机器学习界面的核心问题之一，它对药物发现、材料设计以及如何实现目标分子的更广泛问题产生影响 [1, 2]。可能的反应空间比药物样分子已经难以处理的空间更大，并且单个逆合成计划可能将十几个或更多步骤链接在一起，每个步骤都必须成功才能使路线可行[3]。因此，具有校准不确定性的可靠预测是合成化学任何有意义部分自动化的先决条件。Predicting the outcome of a chemical reaction is one of the central problems at the interface of chemistry and machine learning, with consequences for drug discovery, materials design, and the broader question of how to realize a target molecule once it has been proposed [1, 2]. The space of possible reactions is larger than the already intractable space of drug-like molecules, and a single retrosynthetic plan may chain together a dozen or more steps, each of which must succeed for the route to be viable [3]. Reliable prediction, with calibrated uncertainty, is therefore a prerequisite for automating any meaningful portion of synthetic chemistry.

反应预测通过一系列的表示循环，每种表示都反映了当时占主导地位的 ML 范式：按相似性分类的固定分子指纹 [4,5,6,7]；使用序列和 Transformer 模型进行 SMILES 到 SMILES 的转换 [8, 9]；混合图编码器/SMILES解码器架构 [10, 11]；最近，生成图编辑方法通过直接编辑反应物图来生成产物 [12, 13]。Reaction prediction has cycled through a succession of representations, each reflecting the dominant ML paradigm of its moment: fixed molecular fingerprints classified by similarity [4, 5, 6, 7]; SMILESto-SMILES translation with sequence and Transformer models [8, 9]; hybrid graph-encoder/SMILESdecoder architectures [10, 11]; and, most recently, generative graph-edit approaches that produce the product by editing the reactant graph directly [12, 13].

2.1 基于翻译的反应预测2.1 Translation-based reaction prediction

Schwaller 等人的早期工作。 [9] 将反应预测视为从反应物的 SMILES 字符串到产物的 SMILES 字符串的转换。由于分子承认许多有效的 SMILES 字符串，因此这些模型通常使用 SMILES 增强进行训练 - 每个分子有多个字符串 [9, 26] - 这是数据效率低下的。 Graph2SMILES [11] 通过将反应物编码为 2D 图来部分解决这个问题，无需增加输入，同时仍然使用 Transformer 解码器将乘积解码为 SMILES 字符串。Early work by Schwaller et al. [9] cast reaction prediction as translation from the SMILES string of the reactants to that of the products. Because a molecule admits many valid SMILES strings, these models are typically trained with SMILES augmentation – multiple strings per molecule [9, 26] – which is data-inefficient. Graph2SMILES [11] partially addresses this by encoding the reactants as a 2D graph, removing the need to augment the input, while still decoding the product as a SMILES string with a Transformer decoder.

SMILES [28] 和 SELFIES [29] 等序列表示使得使用完善的自回归文本模型可以轻松生成产品，但它们是有代价的：模型生成产品Sequence representations such as SMILES [28] and SELFIES [29] make products easy to generate with well-established autoregressive text models, but they come at a cost: the model generates the product从头de novo，与反应物没有结构联系。这会产生三个后果。首先，生成不受约束，因此不能保证预测的反应是原子保守的——这是一个关键的化学约束。其次，由于反应物-产物对应关系并不明确，因此恢复实际变化的内容需要单独的原子映射工具[30]；即使预测看起来是原子守恒的，也没有什么可以确保将反应物与产物连接起来的有效机制途径，因此化学上合理的转变和幻觉的转变可能看起来很相似。第三，从头开始生成整个产品会使这些模型在复杂性超出训练范围的分子上退化，即使底层化学很简单 [16,17,18]。, with no structural link to the reactants. This has three consequences. First, generation is unconstrained, so there is no guarantee that the predicted reaction is atom-conservative – a critical chemical constraint. Second, because the reactant-product correspondence is not explicit, recovering what actually changed requires a separate atom-mapping tool [30]; and even when a prediction appears atom-conserving, nothing ensures a valid mechanistic pathway connects reactants to products, so a chemically sound transformation and a hallucinated one can look alike. Third, generating the entire product from scratch makes these models degrade on molecules whose complexity falls outside the training domain, even when the underlying chemistry is simple [16, 17, 18].

2.2 图形编辑的反应2.2 Reactions as graph edits

为了将化学变化直接构建到模型中，另一项工作将反应预测为反应物图的拓扑变换，并且可以通过选择编辑操作来强制执行原子守恒。 MEGAN [12] 使用 EditAtom、EditBond、AddAtom 和 AddBenzene，将反应显式建模为一系列图形编辑； EditAtom、AddAtom 和 AddBenzene 等操作会破坏原子守恒，尽管可以将操作集限制为仅键编辑以保留原子守恒，如 GTPN [13] 中所示。To build the chemical change directly into the model, another line of work predicts reactions as topological transformations of the reactant graph, and atom conservation can be enforced through the choice of edit operations. MEGAN [12] uses EditAtom, EditBond, AddAtom, and AddBenzene, explicitly modeling a reaction as a sequence of graph edits; operations such as EditAtom, AddAtom, and AddBenzene break atom conservation, though one can restrict the operation set to bond-only edits to preserve it, as in GTPN [13].

图编辑使转换变得明确，并免费提供原子映射，并且原子守恒是可以实现的 - 但编辑纯粹是拓扑性的，因此不能保证连接两个端点的机械路径。键的形成是最明显的情况：图形编辑模型只是在两个原子之间插入一个键，而从化学角度来说，形成该键的电子是从分子的其他地方吸取的。Graph edits make the transformation explicit and give atom mapping for free, and atom conservation is achievable – but the edits are purely topological, so no mechanistic pathway is guaranteed to connect the two endpoints. Bond formation is the clearest case: a graph-edit model simply inserts a bond between two atoms, whereas chemically the electrons forming that bond are drawn from elsewhere in the molecule.

Modeling reactions this way is informative about what actually happens, but capturing the elementary steps explicitly has traditionally required expert-labeled mechanisms or heavy physical calculation. Kayala et al. [19] assemble a dataset of 1,630 reactions with 2,989 mechanistic steps and train a model to rank orbital reactivity for a single elementary step, wrapping it in a recursive loop to reach the product. In the same spirit, Joung et al. [22] scale the mechanistic dataset to 290K reactions and over 2 million elementary steps, and train a flow-matching model over the bond-electron matrix. Both are informative but depend on expert-labeled elementary steps, and are therefore less scalable than the end-to-end approaches above.

ELECTRO [21] 消除了标签要求，直接从端到端反应物-产物对插入电子移动。然而，它仅限于具有线性电子流拓扑的反应——其机制具有明确定义的电子流起点和终点——因此不涵盖循环机制。 NERF [27] 也避免了机制标签，但采用了非自回归、一次性的观点：它预测对于每个键，反应物和产物之间添加或删除的电子对的净数量。因为它只模拟每个键的变化，而不是位点之间的转移，所以电子流的方向是隐含的——预测记录了键获得或失去电子，但不记录这些电子来自或去向的地方。它还仅对键电子进行建模，从表示中排除孤对电子和其他价电子。ELECTRO [21] removes the labeling requirement, interpolating electron moves directly from end-toend reactant-product pairs. However, it is restricted to reactions with linear electron flow topology – those whose mechanism has a well-defined start and end to the electron flow – and so does not cover cyclic mechanisms. NERF [27] also avoids mechanism labels but takes a non-autoregressive, one-shot view: it predicts, for each bond, the net number of electron pairs added or removed between reactants and products. Because it models only the change at each bond and not a transfer between sites, the direction of electron flow is left implicit – the prediction records that a bond gained or lost electrons, but not where those electrons came from or went. It also models bond electrons only, excluding lone pairs and other valence electrons from the representation.

MAELLE 将反应建模为定向电子重新分布，同时保证重原子守恒（表 1）。与 ELECTRO 一样，它不需要基本步骤标签，但它不限于线性流：我们的 FLOW 操作将电子对从源点移动到汇点，使流的方向明确 - 回答电子来自哪里和去哪里 - 并自由组合成循环和线性机制。我们用最佳传输（第 4.3 节）恢复净电子移动，代替机制标签，对反应物和产物之间的插值图进行采样，并在这些电子编辑上训练离散流匹配模型。MAELLE models reactions as directional electron redistribution while guaranteeing heavy-atom conservation (Table 1). Like ELECTRO, it needs no elementary-step labels, but it is not limited to linear flow: our FLOW operation moves an electron pair from a source site to a sink site, making the direction of flow explicit – answering where electrons come from and where they go – and composing freely into cyclic as well as linear mechanisms. In place of mechanism labels, we recover the net electron moves with Optimal Transport (Section 4.3), sample interpolant graphs between reactants and products, and train a discrete flow-matching model over these electron edits.

2.4 图的离散生成建模2.4 Discrete generative modeling on graphs

从生成建模的角度来看，图上的离散生成模型通常将生成视为在去噪扩散或流匹配过程下对节点和边类型的迭代替换，该过程从易于采样的源分布（例如均匀分布）映射到数据分布。 DiGress [31] 和 DeFoG [32] 分别通过采用离散去噪扩散过程 [33, 34] 和离散流匹配 [35] 来生成图。From the standpoint of generative modeling, discrete generative models on graphs typically cast generation as iterative substitution of node and edge types under a denoising diffusion or flowmatching process that maps from a source distribution that is easy to sample, such as a uniform distribution, to the data distribution. DiGress [31] and DeFoG [32] generate graphs by employing discrete denoising diffusion processes [33, 34] and discrete flow matching [35], respectively.

与我们的设置更相关的是，RetroBridge [36] 解决了逆合成的问题，即在与我们的工作相反的设置中预测给定产品的可能前体。该模型通过马尔可夫桥学习产物和反应物的两个耦合分布之间的桥梁[37]。More related to our setting, RetroBridge [36] tackles the problem of retrosynthesis, i.e., predicting possible precursors given the products, in a reversed setting compared to our work. The model learns a bridge between two coupled distributions of products and reactants via Markov bridge [37].

两条线都将生成模型作为分类节点/边类型的重采样——来自噪声先验（DiGress、DeFoG）或耦合端点分布（RetroBridge）。 MAELLE 相反，在整数值电子占据上运行，其中跃迁是电子转移（流动/添加/删除）而不是类型替换；这使得该过程通过构造实现原子守恒，并产生机械上可解释的轨迹。Both lines model generation as resampling of categorical node/edge types — from a noise prior (DiGress, DeFoG) or a coupled endpoint distribution (RetroBridge). MAELLE instead operates over integer-valued electron occupation, where transitions are electron transfers (flow/add/delete) rather than type substitutions; this makes the process atom-conserving by construction and yields mechanistically interpretable trajectories.

5.4 有信心的副产品预测5.4 Side product prediction with confidence

通过将反应建模为生成问题、模拟多个轨迹并采用最流行的输出，我们在某种程度上模仿了真实的化学过程如何发生，其中多个途径与其他途径竞争。为了表征这种行为，我们从 USPTO-480K 的测试集中抽取了 5000 个案例，其中 MAELLE 至少有 2 个高置信度预测，置信度不低于 7.8%，与 64 个样本中的 5 个相对应。然后将这些示例与 Molecular Transformers 和 Graph2SMILES 的预测进行匹配。作为基于序列的方法，这些模型依赖于波束搜索在令牌空间中对不同结果进行采样，而 MAELLE 则在化学友好的电子空间中进行采样。By modeling reaction as a generative problem, simulating multiple trajectories, and taking the most popular outputs, we are somewhat mimicking how a real chemistry process happens, where multiple pathways compete with others. To characterize this behavior, we sampled 5000 cases from the test set of USPTO-480K where MAELLE has at least 2 high-confidence predictions with confidence no less than 7.8%, corresponding with 5 out of 64 samples. These examples are then matched with the predictions of Molecular Transformers and Graph2SMILES. As sequence-based methods, these model relies on beam-search for sampling different outcomes in the token space, in contrast with MAELLE which samples in a more chemistry-friendly electron space.

6 结论6 Conclusion

我们引入了 MAELLE，一种反应预测模型，通过离散流匹配在电子占据空间中运行。通过将反应表述为在源自最佳传输的辅助移动状态空间上的 CTMC，MAELLE 弥合了机器学习和化学家用来推理反应性的推箭形式主义之间的差距。我们的实验证明了三个关键发现：(i) 尽管在比基于 SMILES 的方法更受约束的表示中运行，但 USPTO-480K 上的竞争性分布性能； (ii) 在分子量和反应类型泛化方面具有很强的分布外稳健性，其中 MAELLE 优于所有基线； (iii) 通过多个 CTMC 轨迹采样预测化学上合理的副产物的能力，这是确定性或基于束搜索的方法无法获得的能力。We introduced MAELLE, a reaction prediction model that operates in the electron occupation space via discrete flow matching. By formulating reactions as CTMCs over an auxiliary move-state space derived from optimal transport, MAELLE bridges the gap between machine learning and the arrowpushing formalism that chemists use to reason about reactivity. Our experiments demonstrate three key findings: (i) competitive in-distribution performance on USPTO-480K despite operating in a more constrained representation than SMILES-based methods; (ii) strong out-of-distribution robustness on both molecular-weight and reaction-type generalization, where MAELLE outperforms all baselines; and (iii) the ability to predict chemically plausible side products through multiple CTMC trajectory sampling, a capability inaccessible to deterministic or beam-search-based approaches.
