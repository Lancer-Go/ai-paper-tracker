谁来管理机器？WHO GOVERNS THE MACHINE?

人工智能系统的机器身份治理分类法 (MIGT)A Machine Identity Governance Taxonomy (MIGT) for AI Systems
跨越企业和地缘政治边界运营Operating Across Enterprise and Geopolitical Boundaries

安德鲁·库尔茨，CISSPAndrew Kurtz, CISSP
andrewkurtz@acm.org

克劳迪娅·克拉维卡博士Klaudia Krawiecka, PhD
kkrawiecka@acm.org

摘要Abstract

人工智能治理存在盲点：AI系统使用的机器身份 采取行动。人工智能代理、服务帐户、API 令牌和自动化工作流程现已超过人类数量 企业环境中的身份比例超过 80 比 1The governance of artificial intelligence has a blind spot: the machine identities that AI systems use to act. AI agents, service accounts, API tokens, and automated workflows now outnumber human identities in enterprise environments by ratios exceeding 80 to 1[92]，但还没有集成框架 存在是为了统治他们。后果是可衡量的：单个不受监管的自动化代理 在 2024 年 CrowdStrike 中断中造成了 5.4 至 100 亿美元的损失，民族国家参与者 包括 Silk Typhoon 和 Salt Typhoon 已将不受管制的机器凭证操作为 针对关键基础设施进行间谍活动的主要媒介。与此同时，三大 欧盟、美国和中国等司法管辖区正在从根本上发展 不兼容的人工智能治理框架，造成了现有的跨司法管辖区冲突 企业治理方案具备导航能力。, yet no integrated framework exists to govern them. The consequences are measurable: a single ungoverned automated agent produced $5.4 to $10 billion in losses in the 2024 CrowdStrike outage, and nation-state actors including Silk Typhoon and Salt Typhoon have operationalized ungoverned machine credentials as primary vectors for espionage against critical infrastructure. At the same time, three major jurisdictions-the European Union, the United States, and China-are developing fundamentally incompatible AI governance frameworks, creating cross-jurisdictional conflicts that no existing enterprise governance program is equipped to navigate.

其中对人工智能身份治理这一新兴领域有四项原创贡献。首先，它 提出人工智能身份风险分类法（AIRT），对所产生的风险进行全面列举 特别是在人工智能系统和企业身份治理的交叉点，包括 37 种风险 跨八个领域的子类别，每个子类别都基于记录的事件、监管 识别、从业者患病率数据和威胁情报。二、机器介绍 身份治理分类法 (MIGT)，一个由六个组织组成的综合治理框架 同时解决技术治理差距、监管合规性的领域 差距，以及现有框架仅解决的跨司法管辖区协调差距 隔离。第三，提出了企业身份治理的外国国家行为者威胁模型， 建立丝绸台风、盐台风、伏特台风和朝鲜人工智能增强型 身份欺诈行动已经将人工智能身份漏洞作为主动攻击 需要现有 IAM 框架提供的治理响应的向量。四、它 提出跨司法管辖区的监管协调结构来映射企业人工智能身份 同时履行欧盟、美国和中国框架下的治理义务，确定 不可调和的冲突，并提供企业管理这些冲突的治理机制 节目级别。四个阶段的实施路线图转化了 MIGT 的六项治理 域转化为可操作的企业计划。该文件是写给政策制定者、监管者、 以及在人工智能治理、网络安全和地缘政治风险交叉领域开展工作的从业者。There are four original contributions to the emerging field of AI identity governance within. First, it proposes the AI-Identity Risk Taxonomy (AIRT), a comprehensive enumeration of risks arising specifically at the intersection of AI systems and enterprise identity governance, comprising 37 risk sub-categories across eight domains, each grounded in documented incidents, regulatory recognition, practitioner prevalence data, and threat intelligence. Second, it introduces the Machine Identity Governance Taxonomy (MIGT), an integrated governance framework organized across six domains that simultaneously addresses the technical governance gap, the regulatory compliance gap, and the cross-jurisdictional coordination gap that existing frameworks address only in isolation. Third, it presents the foreign state actor threat model for enterprise identity governance, establishing that Silk Typhoon, Salt Typhoon, Volt Typhoon, and North Korean AI-enhanced identity fraud operations have already operationalized AI identity vulnerabilities as active attack vectors requiring governance responses that no existing IAM framework provides. Fourth, it proposes the cross-jurisdictional regulatory alignment structure mapping enterprise AI identity governance obligations under EU, U.S., and Chinese frameworks simultaneously, identifying irreconcilable conflicts and providing a governance mechanism for managing them at the enterprise program level. A four-phase implementation roadmap translates the MIGT’s six governance domains into actionable enterprise programs. The paper is addressed to policy makers, regulators, and practitioners operating at the intersection of AI governance, cybersecurity, and geopolitical risk.

代理人工智能。Agentic AI.

具有自主感知能力的人工智能系统， 环境中的决策和行动，通常无需执行多步骤任务 持续的人类方向。代理人工智能系统与早期的生成人工智能系统的不同之处在于 他们采取行动，而不仅仅是回应。就本文而言，这种区别具有特定的意义 治理权重：代理人工智能系统获取并行使访问权、消费和生产 企业数据，调用工具和外部服务，将权限委托给下属代理，以及 以前一代软件不需要的方式跨系统积累事实上的特权 需要解决的身份治理框架。An artificial intelligence system capable of autonomous perception, decision-making, and action within an environment, often executing multi-step tasks without continuous human direction. Agentic AI systems differ from earlier generative AI systems in that they act, not merely respond. For the purposes of this paper, that distinction carries specific governance weight: agentic AI systems acquire and exercise access rights, consume and produce enterprise data, invoke tools and external services, delegate authority to subordinate agents, and accumulate de facto privileges across systems in ways that no prior generation of software required identity governance frameworks to address.

代理人工智能系统的多维操作配置文件包括四个分析 不同的功能，每种功能都创建了不同的身份治理表面。首先，The multidimensional operational profile of an agentic AI system comprises four analytically distinct capabilities, each of which creates a distinct identity governance surface. First,工具调用tool invocation：代理人工智能系统调用外部API，执行代码，查询数据库， 触发工作流程，并通过编程接口与企业应用程序交互 具有真实的访问权限。每个工具调用都代表执行的经过身份验证的操作 根据代理的身份并在其提供的凭证范围内，使工具的使用成为一种 特权滥用和渗透的主要媒介。第二，: agentic AI systems call external APIs, execute code, query databases, trigger workflows, and interact with enterprise applications through programmatic interfaces that carry real access authority. Each tool invocation represents an authenticated action performed under the agent’s identity and within the scope of its provisioned credentials, making tool usage a primary vector for both privilege abuse and exfiltration. Second,多步自主 执行multi-step autonomous execution：与响应单个提示并终止的生成式人工智能系统不同， 代理人工智能系统跨时间规划和执行一系列动作，积累上下文， 状态，以及每个步骤的有效访问，而不必返回人类监督 他们之间。这带来的治理挑战不仅仅是端点访问控制之一 但行为轨迹：单独授权步骤的总体效果可能会产生 没有任何单一授权决定考虑或批准的结果。三、: unlike a generative AI system that responds to a single prompt and terminates, an agentic AI system plans and executes sequences of actions across time, accumulating context, state, and effective access across each step without necessarily returning to human oversight between them. The governance challenge this creates is not merely one of endpoint access control but of behavioral trajectory: the aggregate effect of individually authorized steps may produce outcomes that no single authorization decision contemplated or approved. Third,代理对代理的委托agent-to-agent delegation：代理人工智能系统越来越多地在多代理中运行 体系结构，产生子代理，将子任务委托给专门的代理，并传递 通过代理链的指令和凭证，其信任关系很少 经过密码验证，其责任结构很少被明确指定。每个 授权边界是潜在的问责差距和潜在的特权升级面。 四、: agentic AI systems increasingly operate within multi-agent architectures, spawning subagents, delegating subtasks to specialized agents, and passing instructions and credentials through agent chains whose trust relationships are rarely cryptographically verified and whose accountability structures are rarely explicitly assigned. Each delegation boundary is a potential accountability gap and a potential privilege escalation surface. Fourth,环境交互与记忆environmental interaction and memory：代理人工智能系统读取和写入 持久存储，从文档、电子邮件、数据库和网页中提取内容，在某些情况下 架构跨会话维护内存。这些相互作用中的每一个都为 间接提示注入、未授权数据聚合、跨会话权限累积 静态访问治理模型在结构上无法检测到。: agentic AI systems read from and write to persistent storage, ingest content from documents, emails, databases, and web pages, and in some architectures maintain memory across sessions. Each of these interactions creates a pathway for indirect prompt injection, unauthorized data aggregation, and cross-session privilege accumulation that static access governance models are structurally unable to detect.

总的来说，这四种能力意味着代理人工智能系统不是被动的 计算资源，而是一个活跃的、带有身份的企业参与者，其访问足迹， 行为轨迹和问责结构需要相当于以下的治理纪律： 在某些方面比适用于特权人类用户的要求更高。Taken together, these four capabilities mean that an agentic AI system is not a passive computational resource but an active, identity-bearing enterprise actor whose access footprint, behavioral trajectory, and accountability structure require governance disciplines equivalent to, and in several respects more demanding than, those applied to privileged human users.

AIRT（人工智能身份风险分类法）。AIRT (AI-Identity Risk Taxonomy).

中提出的人工智能身份风险分类法 第 5 节：跨 8 个领域 37 个风险子类别的二级分类，包括 全面列举人工智能系统和人工智能系统交叉点所产生的风险 企业身份治理。The AI-Identity Risk Taxonomy proposed in Section 5. A two-level classification of 37 risk sub-categories across eight domains, comprising comprehensive enumeration of risks arising specifically at the intersection of AI systems and enterprise identity governance.

API 密钥。API Key.

应用程序编程接口密钥。静态凭证用于 向外部服务验证软件系统或人工智能代理。 API 密钥是主要目标 国家资助的凭证盗窃操作，因为它们提供持久且通常广泛的访问 企业系统和人工智能基础设施。An Application Programming Interface key. A static credential used to authenticate a software system or AI agent to an external service. API keys are a primary target of state-sponsored credential theft operations because they provide persistent, often broad access to enterprise systems and AI infrastructure.

ARIA（基于代理关系的身份和授权）。ARIA (Agent Relationship-Based Identity and Authorization).

治理模式 将每个代理到代理的委托记录为一个独特的、可加密验证的关系 治理图，在复杂的多代理工作流程中实现细粒度的问责制。A governance model that records every agent-to-agent delegation as a distinct, cryptographically verifiable relationship in a governance graph, enabling fine-grained accountability across complex multi-agent workflows.

ATF（代理信任框架）。ATF (Agentic Trust Framework).

云发布的代理信任框架 安全联盟于 2026 年 2 月成立。人工智能代理的零信任治理框架 引入了四个成熟度级别模型，其中代理自主权是通过证明获得的 可信度，而不是作为二元条件授予。The Agentic Trust Framework published by the Cloud Security Alliance in February 2026. A zero-trust governance framework for AI agents that introduces a four-maturity-level model in which agent autonomy is earned through demonstrated trustworthiness rather than granted as a binary condition.

CAC（中国国家互联网信息办公室）。CAC (Cyberspace Administration of China).

中国政府的主要监管 负责互联网内容治理、网络安全监督和人工智能算法的机构 监管，包括中国算法备案制度的管理。The primary Chinese government regulatory body responsible for internet content governance, cybersecurity oversight, and AI algorithm regulation, including administration of China’s algorithm filing regime.

CSL（网络安全法）。CSL (Cybersecurity Law).

中国网络安全基础法规，最初颁布 于 2017 年进行了重大修订，并于 2026 年 1 月 1 日生效，纳入了专用人工智能 遵守规定并扩大域外范围。China’s foundational cybersecurity statute, originally enacted in 2017 and significantly amended effective January 1, 2026, to incorporate dedicated AI compliance provisions and expanded extraterritorial reach.

DID（去中心化标识符）。DID (Decentralized Identifier).

全球唯一的加密 W3C 标准 可验证的标识符，不需要集中的注册机构。提议DID 作为人工智能代理身份的基础，因为它们使代理能够证明自己的身份并 依赖系统的来源，而不依赖于单个身份提供者。A W3C standard for a globally unique, cryptographically verifiable identifier that does not require a centralized registration authority. DIDs are proposed as a foundation for AI agent identity because they enable agents to prove their identity and provenance to relying systems without dependence on a single identity provider.

欧盟人工智能法案。EU AI Act.

欧盟《人工智能法案》于 2017 年生效 2024 年 8 月 1 日，这是世界上第一个具有约束力的全面人工智能法规。它对人工智能进行了分类 按风险级别划分系统，并施加与风险成比例的义务，其中最重要的 高风险人工智能系统要求将于 2026 年 8 月 2 日生效。The European Union’s Artificial Intelligence Act, which entered into force on August 1, 2024 and is the world’s first comprehensive binding AI regulation. It classifies AI systems by risk level and imposes obligations proportional to risk, with the most significant high-risk AI system requirements taking effect on August 2, 2026.

GPAI（通用人工智能模型）。GPAI (General-Purpose AI Model).

根据欧盟人工智能法案，一个基础模型经过训练 可以执行多种任务的大规模广泛数据。 GPAI 模型受特定 该法第 53 条和第 55 条规定的义务，包括技术文件、版权 合规性和培训数据透明度要求。Under the EU AI Act, a foundation model trained on broad data at scale that can perform a wide range of tasks. GPAI models are subject to specific obligations under Articles 53 and 55 of the Act, including technical documentation, copyright compliance, and training data transparency requirements.

IAM（身份和访问管理）。IAM (Identity and Access Management).

学科及技术体系 负责管理数字身份并控制对企业资源的访问。 传统的 IAM 框架主要是针对人类用户身份而设计的， 结构上不适合人工智能代理身份的治理。The discipline and set of technical systems responsible for managing digital identities and controlling access to enterprise resources. Traditional IAM frameworks were designed primarily for human user identities and are structurally inadequate for the governance of AI agent identities.

JIT（即时）供应。JIT (Just-in-Time) Provisioning.

一种访问治理方法，其中访问权限 在特定任务需要时授予，并根据任务自动撤销 完成，而不是作为长期权利预先分配。 JIT 供应消除了 长期特权并直接解决 AIRT 中的动态访问要求不匹配风险 域 II。An access governance approach in which access rights are granted at the moment they are needed for a specific task and automatically revoked upon task completion, rather than being pre-assigned as standing entitlements. JIT provisioning eliminates standing privilege and directly addresses the Dynamic Access Requirement Mismatch risk in AIRT Domain II.

MCP（模型上下文协议）。MCP (Model Context Protocol).

人工智能代理到应用程序的新兴标准 连接性使人工智能代理能够检索数据、触发工作流程并在企业内采取行动 系统。 MCP连接携带真正的访问权限并代表新兴的身份 治理面在第 8.4 节中作为未来的研究方向进行了阐述。An emerging standard for AI agent-to-application connectivity that enables AI agents to retrieve data, trigger workflows, and act within enterprise systems. MCP connections carry real access authority and represent an emerging identity governance surface addressed in Section 8.4 as a future research direction.

A2A（代理到代理协议）。A2A (Agent-to-Agent Protocol).

由 Google 开发并发布于 2025 年 4 月，实现 AI 之间的通信、任务委派和能力发现 跨异构框架和组织边界运行的代理。哪里MCP 控制单个代理如何连接到工具和数据源，A2A 控制代理如何连接 相互寻找、验证和协调。每个代理发布一个An open protocol developed by Google and published in April 2025 that enables communication, task delegation, and capability discovery between AI agents operating across heterogeneous frameworks and organizational boundaries. Where MCP governs how an individual agent connects to tools and data sources, A2A governs how agents find, authenticate, and coordinate with one another. Each agent publishes an代理卡Agent Card, 声明其功能、支持的模式和身份验证的结构化 JSON 清单 要求，对等代理和编排系统使用哪些来确定是否以及如何 委派任务。就本文而言，A2A 是一种主要的新兴身份治理 表面：通过A2A建立的Agent-Agent委托关系，承载真实的访问 权威，跨组织和信任边界，目前缺乏密码学 MIGT 域 II 和域 IV 控制所需的可验证问责结构。, a structured JSON manifest declaring its capabilities, supported modalities, and authentication requirements, which peer agents and orchestration systems use to determine whether and how to delegate tasks. For the purposes of this paper, A2A is a primary emerging identity governance surface: agent-to-agent delegation relationships established through A2A carry real access authority, cross organizational and trust boundaries, and currently lack the cryptographically verifiable accountability structures that the MIGT’s Domain II and Domain IV controls require.

MIGT（机器身份治理分类）。MIGT (Machine Identity Governance Taxonomy).

机器身份治理 第 6 节中提出的分类法。六域综合治理框架解决 技术治理差距、监管合规差距和跨司法管辖区 同时协调间隙。 MIGT 提供治理能力、控制和 企业人工智能身份计划的问责机制。The Machine Identity Governance Taxonomy proposed in Section 6. A six-domain integrated governance framework addressing the technical governance gap, the regulatory compliance gap, and the cross-jurisdictional coordination gap simultaneously. 1. 简介：人工智能与身份交叉点的治理真空1. Introduction: The Governance Vacuum at the Intersection of AI and Identity

到 2026 年，大多数组织都无法回答有关其人工智能系统的三个基本问题： 他们访问？当他们造成伤害时谁负责？并在一个国家管理它们吗 给另一个人带来责任？In 2026, most organizations cannot answer three basic questions about their AI systems: What can they access? Who is responsible when they cause harm? And does governing them in one country create liability in another?

这些不是极端情况的问题。它们是企业风险的基本问题 管理，即与身份和访问管理 (IAM) 程序具有相同的问题 三十年来一直在回答人类身份问题。对于大多数组织来说，这三个问题的答案都是 2026年，是：我们不知道。这就是要解决的治理真空。These are not edge-case questions. They are the foundational questions of enterprise risk management, namely the same questions that identity and access management (IAM) programs have answered for human identities for three decades. The answer to all three, for most organizations in 2026, is: we do not know. That is the governance vacuum that is addressed.

在操作层面，治理真空体现在从业者面临的三个问题上： 直接与代理人工智能部署合作的报告越来越紧迫地被问到：什么 应该为代理提供工具来完成任务吗？这样做允许处理哪些数据？ 工具访问和数据访问的组合是否仍在授权范围内？ 分配的任务（即最初的意图）？这些不是抽象的政策问题。他们是 运行时访问治理决策，确定 AI 代理是否在其内部运行 批准的边界或积累的能力和数据暴露，没有单一的授权 考虑的决定。缺乏能够解决这些问题的治理框架 代理人工智能系统运行的速度和规模是治理的操作表达 本文讨论的是真空。现有的 IAM 框架并非旨在评估 动态地进行工具权限组合，以强制执行任务范围的数据访问边界 机器速度，或检测单独授权工具调用的聚合效果 超出了他们所承担的任务范围。结果是组织正在使这些 通过在部署时提供的访问权限来隐式做出决策，而无需 治理基础设施，以验证这些补助随着代理行为的发展仍然适当 跨任务、会话和系统交互。At the operational level, the governance vacuum manifests in three questions that practitioners working directly with agentic AI deployments report being asked with increasing urgency: What tools should an agent be given to complete a task? What data is it permitted to process in doing so? And does the combination of tool access and data access remain within the authorized scope of the task it was assigned (i.e., the original intent)? These are not abstract policy questions. They are the runtime access governance decisions that determine whether an AI agent operates within its sanctioned boundaries or accumulates capability and data exposure that no single authorization decision contemplated. The absence of governance frameworks capable of answering them at the speed and scale at which agentic AI systems operate is the operational expression of the governance vacuum this paper addresses. Existing IAM frameworks were not designed to evaluate tool-permission combinations dynamically, to enforce task-scoped data access boundaries at machine speed, or to detect when the aggregate effect of individually authorized tool invocations exceeds the scope of the task that justified them. The result is that organizations are making these decisions implicitly, through the access grants they provision at deployment time, without the governance infrastructure to verify that those grants remain appropriate as agent behavior evolves across tasks, sessions, and system interactions.

Agentic AI is among the fastest-developing capability domains in the history of enterprise technology: the transition from generative AI systems that respond to agentic AI systems that act, plan, delegate, and accumulate access has occurred within a timeframe measured in months rather than the years or decades over which prior technology transitions allowed governance frameworks to mature. 5.1 分类结构和方法5.1 Taxonomy Structure and Methodology

AIRT 的结构为两级分类法。八个风险领域首先通过其主要组织背景和责任结构来组织风险。第二级有 37 个风险子类别，列举了每个领域内具体的、可操作的风险项目。每个子类别都有四个属性：风险机制的描述、严重性评级（严重、高或中）、风险表现的主要攻击或故障向量以及对 IAM 从业者的治理影响。该分类法旨在在其范围内（人工智能系统和身份治理的交叉点）进行详尽的分类，而不是全面涵盖所有人工智能风险。The AIRT is structured as a two-level taxonomy. Eight Risk Domains are first and organize risks by their primary organizational context and accountability structure. The second level has 37 Risk Sub-Categories that enumerate specific, actionable risk items within each domain. Each sub-category is characterized by four attributes: a description of the risk mechanism, a severity rating (Critical, High, or Medium), the primary attack or failure vector through which the risk manifests, and governance implications for IAM practitioners. The taxonomy is designed to be exhaustive within its scope-the intersection of AI systems and identity governance-rather than comprehensive across all AI risks.

8.5 A 结束观察8.5 A Closing Observation

人工智能和身份交叉点的治理真空不是未来的问题。是礼物 危机，记录在前八节中，包括数据、事件、监管分析和 威胁情报总体上没有给现有的假设留下任何空间 框架是足够的，或者渐进的改进将及时缩小差距。The governance vacuum at the intersection of AI and identity is not a future problem. It is a present crisis, documented in the preceding eight sections with data, incidents, regulatory analysis, and threat intelligence that collectively leave no room for the comfortable assumption that existing frameworks are adequate or that incremental improvement will close the gap in time.

人工智能代理不再是实验性的。它们是生产基础设施。 81%的技术团队 已通过规划阶段进入主动测试或生产部署阶段AI agents are no longer experimental. They are production infrastructure. 81% of technical teams have moved past the planning phase into active testing or production deployment[41]。然而仅 14.4% 的受访者表示，人工智能代理在完全安全和 IT 批准的情况下上线. Yet only 14.4% report that AI agents go live with full security and IT approval[41]，这意味着 如今进入企业生产环境的绝大多数人工智能代理都是这样做的 没有充分的治理审查，没有加密身份，没有行为监控， 并且没有指定责任人。, meaning that the overwhelming majority of AI agents entering enterprise production environments today are doing so without adequate governance review, without cryptographic identity, without behavioral monitoring, and without designated human accountability owners.

MIGT 并没有解决这个问题。没有分类法或框架可以解决操作问题 治理问题。这需要投资、组织承诺和持续的 执行。 MIGT 提供的是结构化的知识基础，可以解决问题 可能出现的问题：全面的风险分类、专为风险管理而设计的治理框架 人工智能身份风险的具体特征，基于从业者的实施路线图 经验和跨司法管辖区的监管协调结构为全球组织提供了 驾驭日益分散的治理环境的工具。The MIGT does not solve this problem. No taxonomy or framework solves an operational governance problem. That requires investment, organizational commitment, and sustained execution. What the MIGT provides is the structured intellectual foundation that makes solving the problem possible: a comprehensive risk classification, a governance framework designed for the specific characteristics of AI identity risk, an implementation roadmap grounded in practitioner experience, and a cross-jurisdictional regulatory alignment structure that gives global organizations the tools to navigate an increasingly fragmented governance landscape.

所提出的问题——谁来管理机器——并没有一个普遍的答案。它有一个 组织答案、监管答案和地缘政治答案，它们并不相同 在任意两个司法管辖区回答。但在每一个司法管辖区、每一个企业、每一个国家 监管框架，答案以同样的方式开始：库存、所有者、凭证 政策和审计跟踪。了解您拥有什么、谁对其负责以及它是什么 做。The question posed-who governs the machine-does not have a universal answer. It has an organizational answer, a regulatory answer, and a geopolitical answer, and they are not the same answer in any two jurisdictions. But in every jurisdiction, in every enterprise, and under every regulatory framework, the answer begins the same way: with an inventory, an owner, a credential policy, and an audit trail. With knowing what you have, who is responsible for it, and what it is doing.

这就是治理的起点。 MIGT 为接下来的一切提供了框架。That is where governance begins. The MIGT provides the framework for everything that follows.
