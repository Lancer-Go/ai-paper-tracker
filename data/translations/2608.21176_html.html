摘要Abstract

自动语音质量评估旨在预测与人类主观感知一致的平均意见分数（MOS），对于评估语音生成、增强和通信系统至关重要。对于语音信号，尤其是合成语音，失真通常发生在局部，并且整体感知质量通常由少量感知显着的失真区域主导。然而，大多数现有方法主要是使用话语级 MOS 进行优化，它仅提供粗粒度的监督，并且没有明确指示在感知上重要的失真发生的位置。为了解决这个限制，我们引入显式失真定位作为语音质量评估的辅助知识。我们使用帧级失真注释构建了第一个部分失真的语音数据集，并训练定位模型以生成失真线索。基于这些线索，我们提出了 DAMOS，一种失真感知语音质量评估框架，它将定位信息集成到 MOS 预测管道中。多个公共基准的实验表明，DAMOS 始终优于现有方法，并表现出强大的跨数据集泛化能力，验证了显式失真定位在语音质量评估中的有效性。Automatic speech quality assessment aims to predict Mean Opinion Scores (MOS) consistent with human subjective perception and is essential for evaluating speech generation, enhancement, and communication systems. For speech signals, especially synthetic speech, distortions often occur locally, and overall perceptual quality is usually dominated by a small number of perceptually salient distortion regions. However, most existing methods are primarily optimized with utterance-level MOS, which provides only coarse-grained supervision and offer no explicit indication of where perceptually important distortions occur. To address this limitation, we introduce explicit distortion localization as auxiliary knowledge for speech quality assessment. We construct the first partially distorted speech dataset with frame-level distortion annotations and train a localization model to generate distortion cues. Building on these cues, we propose DAMOS, a distortion-aware speech quality assessment framework that integrates localization information into the MOS prediction pipeline. Experiments on multiple public benchmarks demonstrate that DAMOS consistently outperforms existing methods and exhibits strong cross-dataset generalization, validating the effectiveness of explicit distortion localization for speech quality assessment.

I 简介Introduction

随着语音生成、增强和通信技术的快速发展，语音质量评估（SQA）变得越来越重要。可靠的SQA不仅影响用户体验，而且对于评估和改进各种语音相关系统也起着至关重要的作用Speech quality assessment (SQA) has become increasingly important with the rapid development of speech generation, enhancement, and communication technologies. Reliable SQA not only affects user experience but also plays a critical role in evaluating and improving various speech-related systems[8, 29, 9]。例如，SQA 是评估语音合成系统性能和测量语音增强算法有效性的重要工具. For instance, SQA serves as an essential tool for assessing the performance of speech synthesis systems and measuring the effectiveness of speech enhancement algorithms[16, 19, 27]，促进高质量和感知优化的语音技术的开发。, enabling the development of high-quality and perceptually optimized speech technologies.

平均意见得分 (MOS) 是 SQA 最广泛使用的指标The mean opinion score (MOS) is the most widely used metric for SQA[13]，代表人类听众评估的语音的整体感知质量。在传统的 SQA 数据集中, representing the overall perceptual quality of a speech utterance evaluated by human listeners. In conventional SQA datasets[7, 36, 22, 21, 23]，每个语音样本都分配有一个 MOS 值，该值反映了听者对整个话语的整体印象。这种话语级评分机制已成为现有 SQA 模型的主要监督信号。, each speech sample is assigned a single MOS value that reflects the listener’s overall impression of the entire utterance. This utterance-level scoring mechanism has become the primary supervision signal for existing SQA models.

尽管MOS提供了全局语音质量的有效测量并且现有方法已经取得了显着的进步Although MOS provides an effective measurement of global speech quality and existing methods have achieved remarkable progress[31, 4, 11, 12], 这种话语级监督给当前的 SQA 模型带来了根本性的限制。分配给整个话语的单个 MOS 值仅提供粗粒度的指导，但不能指示哪些时间区域导致感知退化或哪里发生质量损害，这使得模型难以明确识别和强调感知上重要的失真区域。相反，模型必须从单个粗略标签推断局部失真和整体质量之间的关系。这种间接监督限制了模型感知局部扭曲的能力。而且，人类对语音质量的主观评价很少由所有时间段的平均贡献来决定；它通常由少数感知上显着的扭曲区域主导。话语级标签和局部感知显着性之间的这种不匹配阻碍了与人类听觉感知一致的质量表示的学习。, such utterance-level supervision introduces a fundamental limitation for current SQA models. A single MOS value assigned to an entire utterance provides only coarse-grained guidance, but does not indicate which temporal regions contribute to the perceived degradation or where quality impairments occur, making it difficult for models to explicitly identify and emphasize perceptually important distortion regions. Models must instead infer the relationship between local distortions and overall quality from a single coarse label. This indirect supervision limits the model’s ability to perceive local distortions. Moreover, human subjective evaluation of speech quality is rarely determined by the average contribution of all temporal segments; it is often dominated by a small number of perceptually salient distortion regions. Such a mismatch between utterance-level labels and local perceptual salience hampers the learning of quality representations aligned with human auditory perception.

这种现象在合成语音中尤其明显，其中文本到语音和语音转换过程的不稳定性经常会引入局部失真，例如发音错误、局部语音崩溃、声码器伪影和瞬态异常。尽管这种失真只持续很短的时间，但它们会严重降低整体收听质量，这意味着决定语音质量的关键因素不仅仅是存在多少失真，还有它们发生的位置以及它们如何影响感知质量。我们认为，当前的 SQA 缺乏的不是更复杂的网络架构，而是明确描述失真发生位置的辅助知识。为模型配备此类先验知识将使他们能够专注于真正影响感知质量的关键区域。然而，引入显式失真定位带来了两个挑战：首先，现有的公共 SQA 数据集仅提供话语级 MOS，而没有帧级失真注释；其次，如何有效地将定位信息集成到 MOS 预测管道中（从而增强质量评估而不是引入噪声）仍有待探索。This phenomenon is particularly pronounced in synthetic speech, where the instability of text-to-speech and voice conversion processes frequently introduces local distortions such as pronunciation errors, local speech collapse, vocoder artifacts, and transient anomalies. Although such distortions occupy only a short duration, they can disproportionately degrade overall listening quality, implying that the key factor determining speech quality is not merely how many distortions exist but where they occur and how they affect perceptual quality. We argue that what current SQA lacks is not a more complex network architecture but auxiliary knowledge that explicitly describes where distortions occur. Equipping models with such prior knowledge would allow them to focus on the key regions that truly affect perceptual quality. Introducing explicit distortion localization, however, raises two challenges: first, existing public SQA datasets provide only utterance-level MOS without frame-level distortion annotations; second, how to effectively integrate localization information into the MOS prediction pipeline—such that it enhances quality assessment rather than introducing noise—remains unexplored.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 DAMOS，一种基于显式畸变定位的畸变感知框架。我们首先使用自动生成的帧级注释构建部分失真的语音数据集，并训练定位模型以提供细粒度的失真线索。然后，我们设计了一个本地化引导的质量预测框架，将失真线索与 SSL 特征相结合，使模型能够专注于影响整体质量的局部区域并学习更有效的质量表示。To address these challenges, we propose DAMOS, a distortion-aware framework based on explicit distortion localization. We first construct a partially distorted speech dataset with automatically generated frame-level annotations and train a localization model to provide fine-grained distortion cues. We then design a localization-guided quality prediction framework that integrates distortion cues with SSL features, enabling the model to focus on local regions that affect overall quality and learn more effective quality representations.

II 显式失真定位Explicit Distortion Localization

显式失真定位的目标不是改进失真检测本身，而是为下游语音质量评估提供可转移的失真感知监督。如上所述，现有的 SQA 数据集缺乏帧级失真注释，导致在训练期间无法获得明确的定位信息。为了弥补这一差距，我们首先使用自动生成的帧级标签构建部分失真的语音数据集，然后训练专用的失真定位模型。预训练模型随后提供帧级失真线索作为所提出的失真感知 MOS 预测框架的辅助知识。The objective of explicit distortion localization is not to improve distortion detection itself, but to provide transferable distortion-aware supervision for downstream speech quality assessment. As discussed above, existing SQA datasets lack frame-level distortion annotations, making explicit localization information unavailable during training. To bridge this gap, we first construct a partially distorted speech dataset with automatically generated frame-level labels and then train a dedicated distortion localization model. The pretrained model subsequently provides frame-level distortion cues as auxiliary knowledge for the proposed distortion-aware MOS prediction framework.

Ⅱ-AII-A 部分失真数据集构建Partial-Distortion Dataset Construction

学习失真定位需要帧级监督。然而，由于干净语音和失真语音之间的过渡不明确，手动注释失真边界成本高昂且高度主观。我们不依赖手动注释，而是使用自动生成的帧级标签构建部分扭曲的语音数据集。Learning distortion localization requires frame-level supervision. Manually annotating distortion boundaries, however, is prohibitively expensive and highly subjective due to the ambiguous transition between clean and distorted speech. Instead of relying on manual annotation, we construct a partially distorted speech dataset with automatically generated frame-level labels.

所提出的数据集是根据两个原则设计的。首先，失真边界应该是明确可控的，无需人工干预即可实现准确的帧级注释。其次，合成的语音应保留真实的失真特征，以便学习的定位知识可以推广到下游语音质量评估。The proposed dataset is designed according to two principles. First, distortion boundaries should be explicitly controllable, enabling accurate frame-level annotation without human intervention. Second, the synthesized speech should preserve realistic distortion characteristics so that the learned localization knowledge can generalize to downstream speech quality assessment.

为了满足第一个原则，从 LibriSpeech 中选择干净的语音话语To satisfy the first principle, clean speech utterances are selected from LibriSpeech[24]。所有话语都会重新采样为 16 kHz，并随机裁剪为 5 到 15 秒之间的持续时间。比目标持续时间短的话语在裁剪之前会循环重复，以便保留而不是丢弃短话语。. All utterances are resampled to 16 kHz and randomly cropped to durations between 5 and 15 seconds. Utterances shorter than the target duration are cyclically repeated before cropping, so that the short utterances are preserved rather than discarded.

在失真注入之前，使用 16 ms 帧长度和 8 ms 帧移位应用基于能量的语音活动检测 (VAD)。能量低于峰值能量 40 dB 以上的帧将被删除。检测到的语音区域在两个边界处进一步收缩 20 毫秒，并且仅保留长度超过 0.3 秒的片段。这种预处理抑制了语音起始点和偏移量周围的模糊过渡区域，从而提高了生成的帧级注释的可靠性。Before distortion injection, an energy-based voice activity detection (VAD) is applied using a 16 ms frame length and an 8 ms frame shift. Frames whose energy is more than 40 dB below the peak energy are removed. The detected speech regions are further contracted by 20 ms at both boundaries, and only segments longer than 0.3 s are retained. This preprocessing suppresses ambiguous transition regions around speech onsets and offsets, thereby improving the reliability of the generated frame-level annotations.

为了满足第二个原则，我们根据失真与语音活动的相互作用引入四类失真，从而合成不同的退化模式。To satisfy the second principle, we synthesize diverse degradation patterns by introducing four categories of distortions according to their interaction with speech activity.

全球扭曲Global distortions无论语音活动如何，都可以修改语音信号，包括编解码器压缩、编解码器串联、低比特率压缩、重采样和混响。modify the speech signal regardless of speech activity, including codec compression, codec tandeming, low-bitrate compression, resampling, and reverberation.

加性扭曲Additive distortions引入独立于语音活动的环境噪声，包括加性高斯白噪声 (AWGN)、粉红噪声和真实世界的 DNS 噪声introduce environmental noise independently of speech activity, including additive white Gaussian noise (AWGN), pink noise, and real-world DNS noises[28]。在混合之前，DNS 噪声记录会使用低于峰值能量 35 dB 的阈值进行相同的能量有效性检查，以删除近乎静音的区域。. Prior to mixing, DNS noise recordings undergo the same energy validity check using a threshold of 35 dB below the peak energy to remove near-silent regions.

对于每个话语，随机插入一到三个不重叠的失真片段。每个片段的持续时间在 0.5 秒到 3 秒之间，相邻片段之间的最小间隔为 0.3 秒。畸变段的位置遵循特定于类别的约束，以确保准确的边界控制和现实的退化模式。For each utterance, one to three non-overlapping distortion segments are randomly inserted. Each segment has a duration between 0.5 s and 3 s, with a minimum interval of 0.3 s between adjacent segments. The locations of distortion segments follow category-specific constraints to ensure both accurate boundary control and realistic degradation patterns.

由于在合成过程中失真位置是明确已知的，因此可以自动生成帧级二进制标签。每个话语都分为不重叠的 160 毫秒帧。如果一帧超过一半的持续时间与任何注入的失真片段重叠，则该帧被标记为失真；否则，视为干净。该标准提高了针对合成失真和定位预测之间轻微时间边界不匹配的鲁棒性。Since the distortion locations are explicitly known during synthesis, frame-level binary labels can be automatically generated. Each utterance is divided into non-overlapping 160 ms frames. A frame is labeled as distorted if more than half of its duration overlaps with any injected distortion segment; otherwise, it is regarded as clean. This criterion improves robustness against slight temporal boundary mismatch between synthesized distortions and localization predictions.

三、III失真感知 MOS 预测Distortion-Aware MOS Prediction

Ⅲ-AIII-A 概述Overview

部分SectionII引入了显式失真定位模型，该模型提供可靠的帧级定位线索。剩下的挑战是如何有效利用此类定位知识进行话语级 MOS 预测。一个简单的解决方案是将预测的定位图与质量表示直接融合。然而，这种单阶段融合仅在网络的单个点引入失真信息，并且无法在整个质量预测过程中充分利用其指导。introduces an explicit distortion localization model that provides reliable frame-level localization cues. The remaining challenge is how to effectively exploit such localization knowledge for utterance-level MOS prediction. A straightforward solution is to directly fuse the predicted localization map with the quality representation. However, such one-stage fusion introduces distortion information at only a single point of the network and cannot fully exploit its guidance throughout the quality prediction process.

我们认为显式失真定位应该参与整个 MOS 预测流程，而不是单独作为融合阶段的辅助特征。具体来说，定位知识应该首先指导失真敏感表示的提取，然后动态调节特征学习以强调感知退化区域，最后保留到质量估计，以便局部失真线索可以直接有助于话语级 MOS 预测。遵循这一原则，我们提出了一种失真感知 MOS 预测框架，该框架由三个连续阶段组成，即失真敏感层适应（DSLA）、失真引导特征调制（DistortionFiLM）和局部质量回归（LQR）。如图所示We argue that explicit distortion localization should participate in the entire MOS prediction pipeline rather than serving as an auxiliary feature at the fusion stage alone. Specifically, localization knowledge should first guide the extraction of distortion-sensitive representations, then dynamically condition feature learning to emphasize perceptually degraded regions, and finally be preserved until quality estimation so that localized distortion cues can contribute directly to utterance-level MOS prediction. Following this principle, we propose a distortion-aware MOS prediction framework consisting of three successive stages, namely Distortion-Sensitive Layer Adaptation (DSLA), Distortion-Guided Feature Modulation (DistortionFiLM), and Localized Quality Regression (LQR). As illustrated in Fig.1，这三个组件在整个质量预测管道中逐步发现、注入和保留显式失真定位知识。, these three components progressively discover, inject, and preserve explicit distortion localization knowledge throughout the entire quality prediction pipeline.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 失真引导特征调制Distortion-Guided Feature Modulation

识别失真敏感表示后，剩下的挑战是如何有效地将显式失真定位知识注入质量预测过程。如果没有明确的定位线索，模型必须从单个话语级质量得分隐式推断失真位置。然而，语音失真通常仅限于话语的一小部分，而基于 Transformer 的 SSL 编码器则对整个语音序列执行远程上下文建模。因此，局部失真线索逐渐与丰富的感知干净区域耦合，使得退化的帧越来越难以区分——我们将这种现象称为局部全局耦合After identifying distortion-sensitive representations, the remaining challenge is how to effectively inject explicit distortion localization knowledge into the quality prediction process. Without explicit localization cues, the model must implicitly infer distortion locations from a single utterance-level quality score. However, speech distortions are typically confined to only a small portion of an utterance, whereas Transformer-based SSL encoders perform long-range contextual modeling over the entire speech sequence. Consequently, localized distortion cues become progressively coupled with abundant perceptually clean regions, making degraded frames increasingly difficult to distinguish—a phenomenon we refer to as local-global coupling[14, 15]。这种耦合限制了模型仅在话语级监督下利用局部失真信息的能力。. This coupling limits the model’s ability to exploit localized distortion information under utterance-level supervision alone.

值得注意的是，这种鲁棒性概况与表中报告的预训练定位模型的经验误差特征一致Notably, this robustness profile aligns with the empirical error characteristics of the pretrained localization model reported in TableVII，其中检测器始终表现出高精度，但跨失真类别的召回率相对较低，表明保守的定位行为很少产生误报，但偶尔会错过模糊的失真边界。在实践中，这种召回主导的错误类似于零掩模条件的部分局部版本，即真实失真帧的零星遗漏，而不是全一掩模模拟的系统过度触发。因此，观察到的对零掩模条件的鲁棒性不仅是扰动实验的有利产物，而且与现实世界的部署直接相关：所提出的框架在定位模型实际上容易出现的特定类型的缺陷下保持稳定，而考虑到探测器保守的精度导向行为，不加区别的过度检测的更具破坏性的故障模式相对不太可能发生。, where the detector consistently exhibits high precision but comparatively lower recall across distortion categories, indicating a conservative localization behavior that rarely produces false positives but occasionally misses ambiguous distortion boundaries. In practice, such recall-dominated errors resemble a partial, localized version of the zero-mask condition—i.e., sporadic omission of true distortion frames—rather than the systematic over-triggering simulated by the all-one mask. The observed robustness to the zero-mask condition is therefore not merely a favorable artifact of the perturbation experiment, but is directly relevant to real-world deployment: the proposed framework remains stable under the specific type of imperfection that the localization model is actually prone to, while the more damaging failure mode of indiscriminate over-detection is comparatively unlikely to occur given the detector’s conservative precision-oriented behavior.

总而言之，这些结果表明显式失真定位的贡献是双重的：它为推理过程中的特征调制提供直接的帧级指导，并且还通过训练时监督塑造潜在的质量表示，即使在测试时定位信息降级或不可用时，这种表示仍然存在。相对于 BVCC，在 TCD-VoIP 上观察到的更大的退化进一步表明，对于具有时间局部退化的语音信号来说，这种好处更为明显，例如通信语音中常见的数据包丢失和传输伪影，其中准确的失真定位对于质量预测更为重要。我们注意到，目前的分析依赖于合成掩模扰动，而不是定位模型的实际误差分布；在自然发生的本地化错误下评估框架仍然是未来工作的一个有趣的方向。Taken together, these results indicate that the contribution of explicit distortion localization is twofold: it provides direct, frame-level guidance for feature modulation during inference, and it also shapes the underlying quality representations through training-time supervision, which persists even when localization information is degraded or unavailable at test time. The larger degradation observed on TCD-VoIP relative to BVCC further suggests that this benefit is more pronounced for speech signals with temporally localized degradations, such as packet loss and transmission artifacts common in communication speech, where accurate distortion localization is more consequential for quality prediction. We note that the present analysis relies on synthetic mask perturbations rather than the localization model’s actual error distribution; evaluating the framework under naturally occurring localization errors remains an interesting direction for future work.

V 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们研究了显式失真定位作为语音质量评估辅助知识的潜力。与仅依赖于话语级 MOS 监督的现有方法不同，我们认为为模型提供有关In this paper, we investigate the potential of explicit distortion localization as auxiliary knowledge for speech quality assessment. Different from existing approaches that rely solely on utterance-level MOS supervision, we argue that providing models with information about发生扭曲的地方where distortions occur可以帮助他们学习更好地符合人类感知机制的高质量表示。为此，我们构建了一个部分失真的语音数据集，其中包含自动生成的帧级注释，并训练了一个专用的定位模型以提供可转移的失真感知线索。can help them learn quality representations that better align with human perceptual mechanisms. To this end, we constructed a partially distorted speech dataset with automatically generated frame-level annotations and trained a dedicated localization model to provide transferable distortion-aware cues.

基于获得的定位知识，我们提出了 DAMOS，这是一种失真感知语音质量评估框架，可在整个 MOS 预测管道中逐步利用显式失真信息。具体来说，DSLA 自适应地识别失真敏感的表示，DistortionFiLM 通过失真引导调制注入帧级定位线索，LQR 保留局部质量信息直到最终估计。 对多个基准的大量实验表明，DAMOS 始终优于现有的最先进方法，并实现了强大的跨数据集泛化，证实显式失真定位提供了超出传统 MOS 监督的补充信息。Based on the obtained localization knowledge, we proposed DAMOS, a distortion-aware speech quality assessment framework that progressively exploits explicit distortion information throughout the MOS prediction pipeline. Specifically, DSLA adaptively identifies distortion-sensitive representations, DistortionFiLM injects frame-level localization cues through distortion-guided modulation, and LQR preserves localized quality information until the final estimation. Extensive experiments on multiple benchmarks demonstrate that DAMOS consistently outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods and achieves strong cross-dataset generalization, confirming that explicit distortion localization provides complementary information beyond conventional MOS supervision.

尽管取得了这些有希望的结果，但仍有几个方向有待解决。首先，当前的框架依赖于综合生成的失真掩模来训练定位模型和进行推理时间鲁棒性分析；在本地化模型自然发生的真实、非合成失真误差分布下评估 DAMOS 可以更直接地衡量其实际鲁棒性。其次，定位和质量预测模型是分开训练的，在MOS训练期间定位主干保持冻结；联合优化失真定位和感知质量估计可以使这两个目标进一步相互加强。最后，将框架扩展到完全帧级、可解释的质量评估（其中预测的失真位置直接作为人类可解释的质量分数证据）代表着朝着更透明和诊断性语音质量评估迈出的自然的下一步。Despite these promising results, several directions remain open. First, the current framework relies on synthetically generated distortion masks for both training the localization model and conducting our inference-time robustness analysis; evaluating DAMOS under the localization model’s naturally occurring error distribution on real, non-synthetic distortions would provide a more direct measure of its practical robustness. Second, the localization and quality prediction models are trained separately, with the localization backbone kept frozen during MOS training; jointly optimizing distortion localization and perceptual quality estimation may allow the two objectives to reinforce each other further. Finally, extending the framework toward fully frame-level, interpretable quality assessment—where predicted distortion locations are directly exposed as human-interpretable evidence for a quality score—represents a natural next step toward more transparent and diagnostic speech quality assessment.
