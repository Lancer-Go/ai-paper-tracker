摘要。Abstract.

人们经常使用手写笔记和草图来具体化想法以进行构思。为了将大型语言模型（LLM）集成到这种实践中，我们提出了 Thinkink。提示可以是手写文本或绘制的草图，其中法学硕士生成的响应可视化为类似墨水的文本和草图，在空间上集成到共享画布中。语义树简化了墨迹解释，轻量级 UI 使用状态机提供显式控制。该工具采用三阶段流程设计。一项形成性研究（N=12）检查了传统和数字墨水方法的当前实践。结果为诊断研究（N = 6）的技术探索提供了信息，以确定可用性和人类与法学硕士互动的挑战。这激发了 Thinkink 的设计，最终研究 (N=10) 检验了人们如何将其融入到他们的构思实践中。我们为墨水本机法学硕士交互提供设计含义和工具，用户和法学硕士可以在共享的 2D 画布中书写和绘图。People often use handwritten notes and sketches to externalize ideas for ideation. To integrate large language models (LLMs) into this practice, we propose Thinkink. Prompts can be handwritten text or drawn sketches with LLM-generated responses visualized as ink-like text and sketches spatially integrated into a shared canvas. A semantic tree streamlines ink interpretation, and a lightweight UI provides explicit control using a state machine. The tool was designed using a three-stage process. A formative study (N=12) examined current practices with conventional and digital inking methods. The results informed a technical probe for a diagnostic study (N=6) identifying usability and human-LLM interaction challenges. This motivated the design of Thinkink, with a final study (N=10) examining how people incorporate it into their ideation practices. We contribute design implications and a tool for ink-native LLM interaction where the user and LLM write and draw in a shared 2D canvas.

2. 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

本节讨论有关构思、数字墨迹和法学硕士支持的墨迹的相关工作。 我们不会回顾更广泛的创造力支持文献，从早期的不确定阶段到后来的明确阶段，因为我们的工作特别关注构思。This section discusses related work on ideation, digital inking, and LLM-supported inking. 2.1. 构思和支持技术Ideation and Supporting Technologies

创意是产生满足目标的新证据或信息的过程，通常被描述为发散思维或头脑风暴Ideation is the process of generating new evidence or information that satisfies a goal, it is often described as divergent thinking or brainstorming(koestler1964act)。 当人们进行构思时，他们依赖于. When people engage in ideation, they rely on归纳推理abductive reasoning，这意味着从通常不完整的证据出发，寻找合理的解释, which means starting from evidence, usually incomplete, to find a plausible explanation(Thagard1997Abductive; walton2014abductive)。 这与其他类型的推理不同，例如演绎推理（应用规则得出必要的结论）和归纳推理（从示例中概括）。 在构思过程中，人们特别依赖溯因推理来具体化概念、注意模式并识别新信息，包括新的解释或下一个可能性. This is different than other types of reasoning, such as deductive reasoning which applies rules to derive necessary conclusions and inductive reasoning which generalizes from examples. In ideation, people specifically rely on abductive reasoning to externalize concepts, notice patterns, and identify new information, including new interpretations or next possibilities(ideationrelyonabductive)。 因此，构思不仅是想法的生成，而且是理解部分证据的演绎和解释过程. Thus, ideation is not only idea generation, but also an abductive and explanatory process of making sense of partial evidence(ExplanatoryReasoning1996).

外化增强溯因推理Externalization enhances abductive reasoning(pittphilsci10414; Hutchins1995; Kirsh1999)。 外化的一种方法是自我解释，或“橡皮鸭”，即人们大声解释自己的内部想法，以阐明假设并加深理解. One approach to externalization is self-explanation, or “Rubberducking”, where people explain their internal thoughts out loud to articulate assumptions and deepen understanding(Eliciting_self-explanations1994; RobotDuck2023)。 我们的重点是通过书写和勾画想法来使用视觉进行外化，这种方法非常适合视觉思考者. Our focus is externalization using visuals by writing and sketching thoughts, an approach that works well for visual thinkers(Thagard1997Abductive; Spaceforthinking_jiansent2010)。 这两种方法都有助于检查和重组自己的思维，但根据定义，它们主要使用一个人已有的知识；他们没有提出新的观点、类比或候选方向。. Both approaches help inspect and reorganize own thinking, but by definition they largely work with knowledge a person already has; they do not contribute new perspectives, analogies, or candidate directions.

最近的工作利用法学硕士来解决这个问题，并将其作为更广泛议程的一部分，以创建增强而不是取代认知的“思维工具”Recent work uses LLMs to address this issue, framed as part of a broader agenda to create “tools for thought” that augment rather than replace cognition(Tools_for_Thought2025)。 在构思中，法学硕士可以通过三种方式支持分歧：. In ideation, LLMs can support divergence in three ways: they can broaden语义广度semantic breadth通过从更广泛的概念空间提出概念，提出替代框架或方向，以及by surfacing concepts from a wider conceptual space, suggest alternative framings or directions, and维持分歧sustain divergence通过帮助用户在过早地集中于单一解决方案之前进行探索。by helping users exploring before converging too early on a single solution.

之前的工作已经将法学硕士探索为通过即时框架进行头脑风暴以引发创造性思维Prior work has been explored LLMs as brainstorming through prompt frameworks for eliciting creative thoughts(CHANG2025101755)、协作脑力写作 ，一种小组构思方法，参与者迭代地编写并构建彼此的想法，以及 想法评估, collaborative brainwriting , a group ideation method in which participants iteratively write and build on one another’s ideas, as well as idea evaluation(AI-Augmented_Brainwriting2024)，以及基于角色的人类-人工智能构思的描述 描述不同的劳动分工，例如人工智能作为主要创意生成器与人工智能作为人类主导创意的支持, and role-based accounts of human–AI ideation that describe different divisions of labor, such as AI as the primary idea generator versus AI as support for human-led ideation(Ideation_with_Generative_AI2025).  (gu2023llmspotentialbrainstormingpartners)。 案例研究还表明，法学硕士如何在复杂领域（例如数学、科学研究和其他技术问题解决任务）中充当集思广益的合作伙伴. Case studies have also shown how LLMs can act as brainstorming partners in complex domains such as mathematics, scientific research, and other technical problem-solving tasks(gu2023llmspotentialbrainstormingpartners)。 总的来说，这项工作表明法学硕士可以扩大构思过程中考虑的可能性空间。 然而，与法学硕士的互动仍然主要以对话和文本为中心，通过提示、聊天或文档交换想法。 这种交互模式可能不是最理想的，特别是对于通过素描和手写将想法具体化的视觉思考者来说，因为它需要在墨水画布和法学硕士界面之间不断来回切换。 我们的工作通过将人们的草图和手写与法学硕士在统一画布上生成的反应交织在一起来填补这一空白。. Collectively, this work suggests that LLMs can expand the space of possibilities considered during ideation. However, interaction with LLMs remains largely conversational and text-centric, with ideas exchanged through prompts, chat, or documents. This mode of interaction can be suboptimal, especially for visual thinkers who externalize ideas through sketching and handwriting, as it requires constant back-and-forth between the inking canvas and the LLM interface. Our work fills this gap by intertwining people’s sketches and handwriting with LLM-generated responses on a unified canvas.

2.2. 数字墨水工具Digital Inking Tools

数字工具演示了笔输入如何（Digital tools demonstrate how pen input (即i.e.“墨水”）可以通过直接、低摩擦的交互来增强记笔记、素描和思考。 他们表明，墨水不仅是一种记录介质，而且还是一种交互原语。the “ink”) can enhance note-taking, sketching, and thinking through direct, low-friction interaction. It shows how ink can act as a simple input command without forcing users to leave the canvas. However, InkSeine routes those interpretations into a separate search interface rather than using the canvas itself as a shared space for AI-mediated ideation and response. We are inspired from this work by using ink as input, but we also integrate the resulting information in an ink-like form directly within a 2D spatial canvas, not in a separate interface. This demands careful interaction design, as a single workspace must support both traditional inking actions, such as writing, erasing, and moving drawings, and AI-specific actions, such as writing prompts, erasing prompts, and generating responses, while remaining easy to understand and fluid to use during open-ended exploration(Pen_+_touch2010; WritLarge2017).

更广泛地说，之前的数字墨迹工具主要将墨迹视为用户编写的输入，用于记笔记、搜索或分析命令，而我们将画布视为共享的构思空间，人工智能也可以直接在墨迹中做出贡献。More broadly, whereas prior digital inking tools treat ink primarily as user-authored input for note-taking, search, or analytic commands, we treat the canvas as a shared ideation space in which AI also contributes directly in ink.

5.3. 改进领域Improvement Areas

从这些挑战中，我们得出以下改进领域，为技术探索的完善提供信息。From these challenges, we derive the following improvement areas to inform the refinement of the technical probe.

UI 和交互设计：使系统模式明确并限制每个模式内的交互UI and Interaction Design: Make System Modes Explicit and Constrain Interactions Within Each Mode

界面应该使写作、编辑、选择和基于 LLM 的模式清晰区分并易于切换，以便用户可以了解系统正在关注什么以及当前可以执行哪些操作。将每种模式限制为一组小的、定义明确的交互，并由可见控件、模式指示器和简短的入门等轻量级提示支持，将减少意外操作，阐明系统行为，并在不牺牲界面简单性的情况下提高可学习性。The interface should make writing, editing, selection, and LLM-based modes clearly distinct and easy to switch between, so users can understand what the system is attending to and what actions are currently possible. Limiting each mode to a small, well-defined set of interactions, supported by lightweight cues such as visible controls, mode indicators, and brief onboardingm, would reduce accidental actions, clarify system behavior, and improve learnability without sacrificing interface simplicity.

UI 和交互设计：优先考虑可恢复性和可控自动化UI and Interaction Design: Prioritize Recoverability and Controllable Automation

混合墨水-法学硕士系统应围绕可逆性、持久性和可检查性进行设计。强大的撤消/重做、可编辑的合并结果、持久的响应历史记录、明确的上下文范围和手动 LLM 触发将帮助用户防止错误、快速恢复，并在错误的时间或错误的上下文中执行时覆盖自动化。Hybrid ink–LLM systems should be designed around reversibility, persistence, and inspectability. Robust undo/redo, editable merge outcomes, persistent response history, explicit context scoping, and manual LLM triggering would help users prevent errors, recover from them quickly, and override automation when it acts at the wrong time or with the wrong context.

LLM 集成：启用显式提示和见解LLM Integration: Enable Both Explicit Prompting and Insights

系统应区分按需提示和见解作为两种明确但可互操作的模式。允许用户手动调用 LLM，同时单独控制 Insights，将保留用户代理并减少中断，而不会牺牲偶然的帮助。The system should distinguish between on-demand prompting and Insights as two explicit but interoperable modes. Allowing users to manually invoke the LLM, while separately controlling Insights, would preserve user agency and reduce interruptions without sacrificing serendipitous assistance.

LLM 输出：使内容和类型与用户需求保持一致LLM Outputs: Align Content and Types with Users Demands

LLM 的回答应该在内容和表达上进行调整。除了文本之外，输出还可以包括校对和面向评论的反馈、墨迹草图、渲染数学、Markdown 和上下文感知排版，确保它们在技术和面向演示的工作流程中保持清晰和有用。LLM responses should adapt in both content and presentation. Beyond text, outputs can include proofreading and critique-oriented feedback, ink-like sketches, rendered mathematics, Markdown, and context-aware typography, ensuring they remain clear and useful across technical and presentation-oriented workflows.

8. 讨论Discussion

与法学硕士的墨水原生交互可以跨数学、物理和图形设计等领域以及构思、协作和 AR/VR 等场景构建新工具。 这篇论文代表了实现与法学硕士的墨水原生交互的垫脚石，这是一个在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索的领域。 尽管我们的研究侧重于特定的场景和领域，并且涉及的参与者数量有限，但它们为未来的研究提供了有用的见解。 在我们的形成、诊断和使用研究中，我们演示了如何建立一个基础，将 2D 空间画布上的非结构化绘图转换为法学硕士更容易理解的结构化表示。 我们的研究结果进一步表明，设计由墨水原生 LLM 支持的创意工具并不是确定单个最佳界面的问题，而是平衡不同用户和任务之间相互竞争的设计目标，重点关注不同的设计方面。Ink-native interaction with LLMs could enable building new tools across domains such as mathematics, physics, and graphic design, as well as in scenarios including ideation, collaboration, and AR/VR. This paper represents a stepping stone toward realizing ink-native interactions with LLMs, an area that remains largely underexplored. Although our studies focused on a specific scenario and domains, and involved a limited number of participants, they provide useful insights for future research. Across our formative, diagnostic, and usage studies, we demonstrate how to build a foundation for transforming unstructured drawings on a 2D spatial canvas into structured representations that is more understandable for LLMs. Our findings further suggest that designing ink-native LLM-powered tools for ideation is not a matter of identifying a single optimal interface, but rather of balancing competing design goals across different users and tasks, focusing on different design aspects.

构思工作流程：线性与非线性进展Ideation Workflow: Linear vs. Non-linear Progression

形成性研究表明，外化是非线性的，通常始于不完整的标记、单词或结构。 然后，诊断和使用研究表明了为什么这很重要：参与者使用 2D 画布进行分支，保持替代方案可见，并在绘图中提供地面提示。 然而，并非所有用户都受益于相同程度的开放性。一些人重视自由形式的探索，而另一些人则想要更清晰的结构。 这表明了未来系统的一个关键设计选择：是优先考虑开放空间探索还是提供更多线性支架、摘要或模板。 对于不同的任务，例如设计创意、技术推理和日常计划，以及自然以有组织的方式与自由形式工作的用户来说，正确的平衡可能会有所不同。The formative study showed that externalization is non-linear and often begins with incomplete marks, words, or structures. The diagnostic and usage studies then showed why this matters: participants used the 2D canvas to branch, keep alternatives visible, and ground prompts in drawings. However, not all users benefited from the same degree of openness. Some valued free-form exploration, while others wanted clearer structure. This suggests a key design choice for future systems: whether to privilege open spatial exploration or provide more linear scaffolds, summaries, or templates. The right balance will likely differ for different tasks, such as design creativity, technical reasoning, and everyday planning, as well as for users who naturally work in organized versus free-form ways.

UI 和交互设计：受约束交互与无约束交互UI and Interaction Design: Constrained vs. Unconstrained Interactions

这些研究一致支持最小界面设计的价值。 参与者欣赏低视觉混乱和笔优先的互动，这与纸张的即时性相一致。 诊断研究还表明，当模式、系统注意力和可用行动不清楚时，极简主义可能会变得模糊。 Thinkink 的状态机设计提高了易读性和控制性，但使用研究仍然揭示了学习曲线。 这需要做出设计决策：保留低摩擦界面，而不是添加显式约束、提示和引导。 更受限制的交互可能特别有利于使用数字墨迹工具经验较少的用户，而专家用户可能更喜欢更少的护栏和更多的交互选择。 因此，自适应披露可能是有希望的：为新手提供简单的默认设置，随着用户获得信心，会显示更灵活的交互。The studies consistently supported the value of a minimal interface design. Participants appreciated low visual clutter and pen-first interaction, which aligned with the immediacy of paper. The diagnostic study also showed that minimalism can become ambiguity when modes, system attention, and available actions are unclear. Thinkink’s state-machine design improved legibility and control, but the usage study still revealed a learning curve. This requires a design decision: preserving a low-friction interface versus adding explicit constraints, cues, and onboarding. More constrained interactions may especially benefit users with less experience using digital inking tools, while expert users may prefer fewer guardrails and more interaction choices. Adaptive disclosure may therefore be promising: simple defaults for novices, with more flexible interactions revealed as users gain confidence.

LLM 整合：用户与 LLM 机构LLM Integration: User vs. LLM Agency

各项研究的参与者希望法学硕士能够充当嵌入画布中的创意合作伙伴，而不是充当单独的聊天机器人。 他们重视扩展思维的见解，但也希望有方法明确地询问模型、控制模型的行为以及检查或调整上下文。 总之，这些发现表明洞察和提示应被视为独立但可互操作的设计维度，而不是单一的连续体。 不同的任务可能需要不同程度的法学硕士主动性：用户不确定自己目标的任务（例如日常决策）可以从更多见解中受益，而用户目标更明确的任务（例如解决数学问题等专业工作）可能需要更明确的提示。Participants across studies wanted LLMs to act as an ideation partner embedded in the canvas, not as a separate chatbot. They valued Insights that expanded thinking, but also wanted ways to explicitly ask the model, control when it acted, and inspect or adjust context. Together, these findings suggest that Insights and Prompt should be treated as separate but interoperable design dimensions rather than a single continuum. Different tasks may require varying levels of LLMs initiative: tasks where users are uncertain about their goals, such as everyday decision-making, can benefit from more Insights, whereas tasks where users have clearer objectives, such as specialized work like math problem-solving, may require more explicit prompting.

法学硕士输出：通用与特定任务的内容和表示LLM Outputs: Generic vs. Task-specific Content and Representations

探索性问题对于广泛的构思很有用，但通用的纯文本输出不足以进行技术和创造性工作。 参与者想要图表、更好的格式、数学符号和可编辑的视觉效果。 因此，未来由墨水原生 LLM 支持的工具应该决定是针对通用支持进行优化，还是针对与数学、设计创造力或科学推理等目标任务相匹配的领域调整输出进行优化。Exploratory questions were useful for broad ideation, but generic text-only outputs were insufficient for technical and creative work. Participants wanted diagrams, better formatting, mathematical notation, and editable visuals. Thus, future ink-native LLM-powered tools should decide whether to optimize for general-purpose support or for domain-tuned outputs matched to target tasks such as mathematics, design creativity, or scientific reasoning.

9. 结论Conclusion

手写和基于草图的构思可以帮助人们具体化和探索想法，但当前的法学硕士界面仍然主要基于聊天，不太适合空间墨水工作流程。 我们提出了Handwritten and sketch-based ideation helps people externalize and explore ideas, yet current LLM interfaces remain largely chat-based and poorly suited to spatial ink workflows.
