迈向 AI 原生大规模敏捷软件开发的保证闭包Towards Assurance Closure in AI-Native Large-Scale Agile Software Development

Ricardo Britto
Ericsson, Sweden
Blekinge Institute of Technology, Sweden
ricardo.britto@ericsson.com
（2026 年 8 月）(August 2026)
摘要Abstract

AI-Native 宣言设想了大规模敏捷软件开发，其中人类越来越多地控制意图、风险和异常，而代理则执行更多的工程过程。认识到最终状态需要的不仅仅是更好的代码生成：它需要The AI-Native Manifesto envisions large-scale agile software development in which humans increasingly govern intent, risk, and exceptions while agents execute more of the engineering process. Realizing that end-state requires more than better code generation: it requires保证结束assurance closure，这意味着系统可以确定什么必须是真实的，确定并获取适当的证据，判断该证据的可信度，通过改变保持其有效性，并利用由此产生的不确定性来约束代理人的权威。现有的工作已经提供了跨形式方法、测试、模拟、保证案例、数字孪生和运行时保证的许多必要机制。我们确定了使周围的保证推理充分可机器操作的六个剩余差距，提出了一种在共享语义保证层上构建具有六种相应功能的高级架构，并制定了四个研究问题，以将该架构转变为可靠的、人在环的、人工智能原生的研发。, meaning that the system can establish what must be true, determine and obtain appropriate evidence, judge the credibility of that evidence, preserve its validity through change, and use the resulting uncertainty to bound agent authority. Existing work already provides many of the necessary mechanisms across formal methods, testing, simulation, assurance cases, digital twins, and runtime assurance. We identify six residual gaps in making the surrounding assurance reasoning sufficiently machine-operable, propose a high-level architecture with six corresponding capabilities built on a shared semantic assurance layer, and formulate four research questions to turn that architecture into dependable, human-on-the-loop, AI-native R&D.

1 简介Introduction

AI-Native 宣言提出了一种大规模敏捷软件开发的形式，其中人类逐渐从实施工作转向监督控制，同时仍然对意图、风险、政策和特殊决策负责，因为代理承担了越来越大的工程权力The AI-Native Manifesto proposes a form of large-scale agile software development in which humans progressively shift from implementation work to supervisory control, while remaining responsible for intent, risk, policy, and exceptional decisions, as agents assume increasing engineering authority[britto2026manifesto]。其验证优先的保证原则是这种转变的先决条件：只有当系统能够为信任代理所产生的内容提供充分的理由时，授权才有意义。. Its verification-first assurance principle is a prerequisite for that transition: delegation is meaningful only if the system can provide adequate grounds for trusting what agents produce.

我们将未解决的需求开发为We develop that unresolved requirement as保证结束assurance closure：确定必须真实的内容、确定和获取适当证据、判断证据的可信度、在系统变化时保持其有效性，并利用由此产生的不确定性来约束代理人权威的能力。这些任务所需的单独技术已经以多种形式存在。研究挑战是使选择、连接、解释和维护它们的元保证过程充分可机器操作，以进行人工智能原生研发。因此，本文是一份立场文件和研究议程，而不是新验证方法的提案。: the ability to establish what must be true, determine and obtain appropriate evidence, judge the credibility of that evidence, preserve its validity as the system changes, and use the resulting uncertainty to bound agent authority. The individual techniques needed for these tasks already exist in many forms. The research challenge is to make the meta-assurance process that selects, connects, interprets, and maintains them sufficiently machine-operable for AI-native R&D. This paper is therefore a position paper and research agenda rather than a proposal for a new verification method.

本文的其余部分组织如下：第 2 部分回顾了相邻的工作并确定了妨碍保证结束的六个剩余差距。第 3 节介绍了一个高级架构，其六种功能为这些差距提供了综合响应。第 4 节将该架构转变为四个研究问题，必须先解决这些问题，然后才能支持可靠的人机交互委托。第五节最后回到了保证在实现完整的人工智能原生愿景中的作用。The remainder of the paper is organized as follows: Section 2 reviews adjacent work and identifies six residual gaps that prevent assurance closure. Section 3 presents a high-level architecture whose six capabilities provide an integrated response to those gaps. Section 4 turns that architecture into four research questions that must be addressed before it can support dependable, human-on-the-loop delegation. Section 5 concludes by returning to the role of assurance in realizing the full AI-native vision.

2 相关工作Related Work

保证封闭建立在几个成熟领域的基础上，而不是取代它们。需求工程和形式化规范提供了使预期行为更加精确的方法；最近的代理工作表明，模型可以越来越多地生成和推理形式规范，但公认的形式化仍然可能省略假设或承认不正确的行为Assurance closure builds on several mature areas rather than replacing them. Requirements engineering and formal specification provide ways to make expected behavior more precise; recent agentic work shows that models can increasingly generate and reason about formal specifications, but also that accepted formalizations may still omit assumptions or admit incorrect behavior[hamblin2026specbench, agarwal2026verusspecgym]。形式化方法、静态分析、测试和基于模型的技术提供了补充形式的证据。行业经验（例如 Amazon 的 ShardStore）显示了将正确性分解为属性并在最有用的地方应用不同技术的价值. Formal methods, static analysis, testing, and model-based techniques provide complementary forms of evidence. Industrial experience, such as Amazon’s ShardStore, shows the value of decomposing correctness into properties and applying different techniques where they are most useful[bornholt2021shardstore].

其他领域涉及如何组织和维护保证证据。保证案例构建索赔、证据和怀疑理由Other fields address how assurance evidence is organized and maintained. Assurance cases structure claims, evidence, and reasons for doubt[goodenough2012confidence]，而 DARPA ARCOS 的目标是提高证据评估和保证案例构建的自动化程度, while DARPA ARCOS targets greater automation in evidence evaluation and assurance-case construction[darpaArcos]。数字孪生研究强调，只有当模型对其预期目的可信时，模拟证据才有用. Digital-twin research emphasizes that simulation evidence is useful only when the model is credible for its intended purpose[shao2023credibility]。连续和运行时保证反过来又展示了保证参数和安全约束如何在系统运行和变化时保持活跃. Continuous and runtime assurance, in turn, show how assurance arguments and safety constraints can remain active as systems operate and change[sljivo2024dynamic, slagel2024rta]。这些基础是坚实的。仍然缺少的是一种集成机制，使代理研发系统能够执行专家工程师目前为其提供的大部分保证推理。. These foundations are substantial. What is still missing is an integrated mechanism that enables an agentic R&D system to perform much of the assurance reasoning that expert engineers currently provide for them.

从这一情况中，我们发现了六个差距，按照逐步转变的顺序排列，以实现更大的授权。From this landscape, we identify six gaps, ordered as a stepwise transformation toward greater delegated authority.

GAP 1——自主规范充分性。GAP 1—Autonomous specification adequacy.在允许代理实施更改之前，系统需要知道预期行为的可用描述是否足以采取行动。这不仅仅是将散文翻译成正式语言。要求可能不完整、相互不一致、太弱而无法区分正确和不正确的行为，或者基于从未明确的假设。最近的基准测试表明，即使有正式的工具可用，规范级别的推理仍然很困难Before an agent is allowed to implement a change, the system needs to know whether the available description of the intended behavior is good enough to act on. This is more than translating prose into a formal language. Requirements may be incomplete, mutually inconsistent, too weak to distinguish correct from incorrect behavior, or based on assumptions that have never been made explicit. Recent benchmarks show that specification-level reasoning remains difficult even when formal tools are available[hamblin2026specbench, agarwal2026verusspecgym]。因此，差距在于检测当前规范的能力. The gap is therefore the ability to detect that the current specification is尚不足以进行授权not yet adequate for delegation并在实施之前确定缺失的内容。and to identify what is missing before implementation proceeds.

GAP 2——保障策略综合。GAP 2—Assurance strategy synthesis.一旦预期的行为足够明确，下一个问题就是什么证据应该证明特定的改变是合理的。不同的属性需要不同的技术：局部不变量可能适合形式证明，API 契约适合静态或基于属性的检查，分布式故障场景适合模拟或故障注入，操作属性适合运行时监控。基于风险的 V&V 已经在实践中做出了这样的选择，异构保证已被证明在行业中有价值Once the expected behavior is sufficiently clear, the next question is what evidence should justify a particular change. Different properties call for different techniques: a local invariant may be suited to formal proof, an API contract to static or property-based checks, a distributed failure scenario to simulation or fault injection, and an operational property to runtime monitoring. Risk-based V&V already makes such choices in practice, and heterogeneous assurance has proven valuable in industry[bornholt2021shardstore, shaw2026assurance]。仍然主要由人类完成的工作是为每个变化构建相应的保障计划：决定哪些主张重要、必须在多大程度上支持它们、哪些证据可以重复使用以及哪些地方仍然需要人类判断。. What remains largely human work is constructing a proportionate assurance plan for each change: deciding which claims matter, how strongly they must be supported, which evidence can be reused, and where human judgment is still required.

GAP 3——可扩展的保证证据生成。GAP 3—Scalable assurance evidence generation.仅当所需的证据实际上可以以人工智能原生速度生成时，保证计划才有用。现有方法已经提供了多种生成证据的方法，包括定理证明、模型检查、静态分析、测试、模糊测试、模拟、仿真、可执行参考模型、数字孪生和运行时监视器。未解决的问题是代理系统如何在客户端实例化这些方法An assurance plan is useful only if the required evidence can actually be produced at AI-native speed. Existing methods already provide many ways to generate evidence, including theorem proving, model checking, static analysis, testing, fuzzing, simulation, emulation, executable reference models, digital twins, and runtime monitors. The unresolved issue is how an agentic system can instantiate these methods at the正确的范围和保真度right scope and fidelity为了手头的改变。例如，如果只有很小的交互相关，则动态验证不应要求复制整个生产系统。相反，系统可能需要构建一个集中的可执行环境，其中仅包含挑战受影响行为所需的服务、状态、工作负载、故障和假设。无论使用什么环境，其本身对于所评估的声明来说都必须是可信的for the change at hand. For example, dynamic validation should not require reproducing the whole production system if only a small interaction is relevant. The system may instead need to construct a focused executable environment that contains only the services, state, workloads, faults, and assumptions needed to challenge the affected behavior. Whatever environment is used must itself be credible for the claim being evaluated[shao2023credibility]。因此，研究问题是可扩展的、以变革为中心的保证证据的生成，而不是数字孪生等任何单一机制。. The research problem is therefore scalable, change-focused generation of assurance evidence, not any single mechanism such as a digital twin.

GAP 4——证据的可信度和充分性。GAP 4—Evidence credibility and sufficiency.提供证据并不等同于有充分的理由相信某个主张。保证案例和基于失败者的推理已经提供了将主张与证据联系起来并询问仍然存在哪些怀疑理由的方法Producing evidence is not the same as having a good reason to believe a claim. Assurance cases and defeater-based reasoning already provide ways to relate claims to evidence and to ask what reasons for doubt remain[goodenough2012confidence]。代理开发进一步增加了复杂性：代码、规范、测试、模拟和评估可能共享相同的模型系列、检索的上下文或错误的解释。因此，几个明显独立的工件可能会重复相同的错误。差距在于评估一系列证据是否相关、足够有力、足够独立以及是否没有未解决的阻碍因素的能力。这不应该过早地减少为单一的综合置信度得分。. Agentic development adds a further complication: the code, specification, tests, simulations, and evaluations may share the same model family, retrieved context, or mistaken interpretation. Several apparently independent artifacts may therefore repeat the same error. The gap is the ability to assess whether a body of evidence is relevant, sufficiently strong, sufficiently independent, and free of unresolved defeaters for the decision being made. This should not be reduced prematurely to a single synthetic confidence score.

GAP 5——持续变化下的保证有效性。GAP 5—Assurance validity under continuous change.保证永远不是永久的。证明可能取决于稍后会发生变化的接口假设；测试可能不再代表生产行为；仿真模型可能会变得陈旧；或者新的操作证据可能会使先前接受的索赔无效。持续和动态的保证已经认识到这个生命周期问题Assurance is never permanent. A proof may depend on an interface assumption that later changes; a test may no longer represent production behavior; a simulation model may become stale; or new operational evidence may invalidate a previously accepted claim. Continuous and dynamic assurance already recognizes this lifecycle problem[sljivo2024dynamic]。人工智能原生研发提高了所需的速度和粒度：每个软件或环境变化都应自动揭示哪些声明、假设、模型、测试、证明和观察受到影响，哪些证据仍然可重用，以及哪些必须重新生成。如果没有这种能力，更快的生成只会在下游造成保证瓶颈。. AI-native R&D raises the required speed and granularity: each software or environment change should automatically reveal which claims, assumptions, models, tests, proofs, and observations are affected, which evidence remains reusable, and what must be regenerated. Without this capability, faster generation simply creates an assurance bottleneck downstream.

GAP 6——保证管理的授权和监督控制。GAP 6—Assurance-governed delegation and supervisory control.弥补上述差距仍然留下了一个治理问题：应该允许代理人利用现在可用的保证做什么？当安全条件受到威胁时，运行时保证架构已经限制了自主组件Closing the preceding gaps still leaves a governance question: what should agents be allowed to do with the assurance available now? Runtime-assurance architectures already constrain autonomous components when safety conditions are threatened[slagel2024rta]。人工智能原生软件开发需要对工程权限进行类似的控制循环。提出更改、编辑代码、合并、发布或部署的能力应取决于当前的保证状态和错误的后果。与此同时，人类必须保持足够的可见性，以了解未解决的不确定性，干预异常情况，并减少或撤销权威。因此，差距不仅仅在于更多的自动化，还在于基于证据的、可逆的授权和有意义的人类监督。. AI-native software development needs an analogous control loop over engineering authority. The ability to propose a change, edit code, merge, release, or deploy should depend on the current assurance state and the consequences of being wrong. At the same time, humans must retain enough visibility to understand unresolved uncertainty, intervene in exceptional cases, and reduce or revoke authority. The gap is therefore not simply about more automation, but about evidence-based, reversible delegation with meaningful human supervision.

3 保证闭合架构Assurance-Closure Architecture

这六个差距表明，无法通过在开发流程中添加一个额外的验证工具来实现保证关闭。图The six gaps suggest that assurance closure cannot be achieved by adding a single additional verification tool to the development pipeline. Figure1提出了一个高级架构，其中保证成为围绕代理研发的持续控制功能。presents a high-level architecture in which assurance becomes a continuous control function around agentic R&D.

C1——规格保证C1–Specification Assurance解决 GAP 1。它将人类意图、要求、约束和已知系统行为转化为机器可执行的义务，但其主要职责是挑战它们，而不仅仅是将它们形式化。它应该识别歧义、缺失的假设、冲突、弱约束和尚未指定的重要行为。因此，它的输出不是特定符号中的“正式规范”，而是对于下一级授权而言足够完整和明确的规范状态，以及仍然需要人工输入的任何未解决的问题。addresses GAP 1. It turns human intent, requirements, constraints, and known system behavior into machine-actionable obligations, but its key responsibility is to challenge them rather than merely formalize them. It should identify ambiguity, missing assumptions, conflicts, weak constraints, and important behavior that remains unspecified. Its output is therefore not “a formal specification” in a particular notation, but a specification state that is sufficiently complete and explicit for the next level of delegation, together with any unresolved questions that still require human input.

C2——保障策略综合C2–Assurance Strategy Synthesis解决 GAP 2。考虑到受影响的索赔、架构、风险和不确定性，它决定应提供哪些证据以及在什么深度。它可以为某些属性选择形式证明，为其他属性选择测试或模糊测试，为交互密集的行为选择可执行验证环境，为在部署之前无法完全建立的属性选择运行时监控，或者在无法委托判断时进行人工审查。该功能故意是方法中立的：其目的是构建并不断调整保障策略，而不是优先考虑特定技术。addresses GAP 2. Given the affected claims, architecture, risk, and uncertainty, it decides what evidence should be produced and at what depth. It may choose formal proof for some properties, testing or fuzzing for others, executable validation environments for interaction-heavy behavior, runtime monitoring for properties that cannot be fully established before deployment, or human review when judgment cannot be delegated. The capability is deliberately method-neutral: its purpose is to construct and continuously adapt an assurance strategy rather than to privilege a specific technology.

C3——保证执行结构C3–Assurance Execution Fabric通过将该策略转化为证据来解决 GAP 3。它编排异构工具和代理，将其范围限定到系统的受影响部分，并构建所选方法所需的验证上下文。传统的静态和形式化技术、BDD 和基于模型的测试、基于属性的测试和模糊测试、模拟和仿真、有针对性的数字孪生、运行时验证和专家评审都可以参与其中。研究的一个中心问题是效率：结构应该产生足够的证据来挑战相关的主张，而无需为每次更改复制或重新验证整个系统。addresses GAP 3 by turning that strategy into evidence. It orchestrates heterogeneous tools and agents, scopes them to the affected parts of the system, and constructs the validation context required by the selected methods. This is where conventional static and formal techniques, BDD and model-based testing, property-based testing and fuzzing, simulation and emulation, targeted digital twins, runtime verification, and expert review can all participate. A central research concern is efficiency: the fabric should generate enough evidence to challenge the relevant claims without reproducing or re-verifying the entire system for every change.

C4——证据裁决C4–Evidence Adjudication解决了 GAP 4。它解释了 C3 产生的证据，而不是将工具的成功视为正确性的证明。该能力必须推理相关性、覆盖范围、出处、方法的合理性、证据来源之间的共享依赖性、不确定性和未解决的失败者。它还应该认识到何时需要更多证据，或者何时明显不同的证据实际上基于相同的假设。结果是一个结构化的保证判断，解释了为什么现有证据对于所考虑的决策来说是充分的或不充分的。addresses GAP 4. It interprets the evidence produced by C3 rather than treating tool success as proof of correctness. The capability must reason about relevance, coverage, provenance, method soundness, shared dependencies between evidence sources, uncertainty, and unresolved defeaters. It should also recognize when more evidence is needed or when apparently diverse evidence is actually based on the same assumption. The result is a structured assurance judgment that explains why the available evidence is or is not adequate for the decision under consideration.

C5—持续保证状态C5—Continuous Assurance State解决 GAP 5。它维护意图、声明、假设、系统元素、保证活动、证据和运行时观察之间的依赖关系。当事情发生变化时，C5 确定哪些保证仍然有效，哪些已经过时，以及保证策略的哪些部分必须重新运行。目的是逐步保证：在假设仍然成立时保留强有力的证据，同时精确地使不再适用的证据无效。addresses GAP 5. It maintains the dependencies between intent, claims, assumptions, system elements, assurance activities, evidence, and runtime observations. When something changes, C5 determines which assurances remain valid, which have become stale, and which parts of the assurance strategy must be rerun. The aim is incremental reassurance: preserve strong evidence when its assumptions still hold, while precisely invalidating the evidence that no longer applies.

C6——授权和监督控制C6–Delegation and Supervisory Control解决了 GAP 6。它将当前的保证状态映射到具体的工程权限和升级规则。代理可能被允许探索或准备变更，但无法合并它；更有力的证据可能允许合并，但仍需要人工批准才能部署。如果证据无效，当局应自动签订合同。 C6 还暴露了剩余的不确定性以及限制人员访问的原因，使他们能够监督意图、风险和例外情况，而不是重新执行所有实施工作。addresses GAP 6. It maps the current assurance state to concrete engineering permissions and escalation rules. An agent might be allowed to explore or prepare a change while being prevented from merging it; stronger evidence may permit merging but still require human approval for deployment. If evidence becomes invalid, authority should contract automatically. C6 also exposes the remaining uncertainty and the reasons for restricting access to humans, enabling them to supervise intent, risk, and exceptions rather than re-performing all implementation work.

A 语义保证层semantic assurance layer支持所有六种功能。它提供共享的、机器可读的知识，将意图、声明、假设、风险、架构、实现、依赖性、保证活动、证据、出处、失败者、有效性条件、运行时观察和经过验证的工程经验联系起来。机器可读的保证模型和可追溯性并不是什么新鲜事supports all six capabilities. It provides shared, machine-readable knowledge linking intent, claims, assumptions, risks, architecture, implementation, dependencies, assurance activities, evidence, provenance, defeaters, validity conditions, runtime observations, and validated engineering experience. Machine-readable assurance models and traceability are not new[foster2021sacm, darpaArcos];该层的作用是为六种功能提供一个可以做出自动保证决策的共同状态。它还保留了有用的经验，例如失败的假设、反例、有效的保证策略和无效的证据，具有明确的范围和出处，以便未来的保证决策可以重用知识，而无需将过去的经验视为普遍有效。; the role of this layer is to provide the six capabilities with a common state on which automated assurance decisions can be made. It also preserves useful experience, such as failed assumptions, counterexamples, effective assurance strategies, and invalidated evidence, with explicit scope and provenance so that future assurance decisions can reuse knowledge without treating past experience as universally valid.

4 研究议程Research Agenda

架构如图The architecture in Figure1是关于保证关闭所需功能的假设，而不是已实施的解决方案。实现这一目标需要在这些功能的自动化、连接和评估方面取得进展。我们围绕四个研究问题组织这项工作。is a hypothesis about the capabilities required for assurance closure, not an implemented solution. Realizing it requires advances in how those capabilities are automated, connected, and evaluated. We organize that work around four research questions.

RQ1——授权的规范充分性。RQ1–Specification adequacy for delegation.代理系统如何确定规范足够完整、正确、一致和有区别，以证明特定级别的授权是合理的？需要进行研究来发现缺失的假设和薄弱的约束，生成反例，比较独立得出的解释，并决定何时需要人工澄清而不是进一步的自主工作来解决未解决的歧义。How can an agentic system determine that a specification is sufficiently complete, correct, consistent, and discriminating to justify a particular level of delegation? Research is needed to detect missing assumptions and weak constraints, generate counterexamples, compare independently derived interpretations, and decide when unresolved ambiguity requires human clarification rather than further autonomous work.

RQ2——自动保证策略和证据生成。RQ2–Automated assurance strategy and evidence generation.考虑到变化、其受影响的索赔、架构、不确定性和后果概况，系统如何构建具有成本效益的保证活动组合，然后在适当的范围内实例化它？这包括在证明、分析、测试、模糊测试、模拟、目标可执行环境、运行时监控和人工审查之间进行选择，并确定每种所选技术可以提供有用证据的最小可信上下文。Given a change, its affected claims, architecture, uncertainty, and consequence profile, how can the system construct a cost-effective portfolio of assurance activities and then instantiate it at the appropriate scope? This includes both selecting among proof, analysis, testing, fuzzing, simulation, targeted executable environments, runtime monitoring, and human review, and determining the minimum credible context in which each selected technique can provide useful evidence.

RQ3——证据的可信度和充分性。RQ3–Evidence trustworthiness and sufficiency.如何评估部分由代理人提供的证据的适用性、合理性、出处、对共同假设的依赖、覆盖范围和未解决的失败者？例如，代理编写的测试并不自动成为强有力的证据；最近的结果表明，增加其数量可能对存储库级别的问题解决几乎没有什么改善How can evidence produced partly by agents be assessed for applicability, soundness, provenance, dependence on shared assumptions, coverage, and unresolved defeaters? Agent-written tests, for example, are not automatically strong evidence; recent results show that increasing their volume may provide little improvement in repository-level issue resolution[chen2026agenttests]。因此，研究应重点关注何时异质证据真正证实了某个主张，以及何时仅通过不同的文物再现了相同的解释。. Research should therefore focus on when heterogeneous evidence genuinely corroborates a claim and when it merely reproduces the same interpretation through different artifacts.

RQ4——持续保证和动态授权。RQ4–Continuous assurance and dynamic delegation.如何逐步维护保证依赖性，以便更改自动使过时的证据失效、触发相应的再保证并调整代理权限？授权应该是可逆的：削弱的证据或违反的假设应该减少允许的行动，而恢复的证据可能会扩大它们。这还需要研究人类有效监督循环所需的信息，而不会成为手动验证瓶颈。How can assurance dependencies be maintained incrementally so that change automatically invalidates stale evidence, triggers proportionate re-assurance, and adjusts agent authority? Delegation should be reversible: weakened evidence or violated assumptions should reduce permissible actions, while restored evidence may expand them. This also requires studying the information humans need to supervise the loop effectively without becoming a manual verification bottleneck.

评价原则。Evaluation principle.有用的演示者应该将同一系统暴露在受影响的声明、不确定性和风险方面不同的变化中。一个有意义的结果将是一个保证系统，该系统导出实质上不同且可防御的策略，构建所需的验证上下文，识别剩余的怀疑原因，在合理时重用证据，并授予不同级别的权限。因此，评估不仅应考虑任务成功，还应考虑错误保证、缺陷和失败者发现、保证成本、不必要的人为干预、更改后的重新保证延迟以及保证状态和授权之间的校准。A useful demonstrator should expose the same system to changes that differ in affected claims, uncertainty, and risk. A meaningful result would be an assurance system that derives materially different and defensible strategies, constructs the required validation context, identifies remaining reasons for doubt, reuses evidence when justified, and grants different levels of authority. Evaluation should therefore consider not only task success but also false assurance, defect and defeater discovery, assurance cost, unnecessary human intervention, re-assurance latency after a change, and the calibration between the assurance state and delegated authority.

5 结论Conclusion

人工智能原生愿景最终取决于人类角色的变化：人们应该能够管理意图、可接受的风险和特殊决策，而不必重现代理执行的每一项工程操作。因此，验证优先保证不是一项外围质量活动；这是负责任授权的一个条件。现有的验证和确认技术提供了许多必要的构建块，但它们尚未提供作为集成的、机器可操作的功能的保证闭合。The AI-native vision ultimately depends on a change in the human role: people should be able to govern intent, acceptable risk, and exceptional decisions without having to reproduce every engineering action that agents perform. Verification-first assurance is therefore not a peripheral quality activity; it is a condition for responsible delegation. Existing verification and validation techniques provide many of the necessary building blocks, but they do not yet provide assurance closure as an integrated, machine-operable capability.

本文通过六个差距、相应的高级架构和四个研究问题来描述这种缺失的能力。研究的核心挑战是围绕现有方法进行保证推理，必须保证什么，需要什么证据，是否应该相信证据，何时不再适用，以及它证明什么权威，以代理发展的速度和规模运作，同时保留有意义的人类控制。我们认为，对于完整的人工智能原生大规模敏捷软件开发愿景来说，缩小这一差距是必要的，从日益强大的代码生成转向可信赖的、有限的研发权力授权。This paper has framed that missing capability through six gaps, a corresponding high-level architecture, and four research questions. The central research challenge is to make the assurance reasoning around existing methods, what must be assured, what evidence is needed, whether the evidence should be believed, when it ceases to apply, and what authority it justifies, operate at the speed and scale of agentic development while preserving meaningful human control. Closing that gap is, in our view, necessary for the full AI-native large-scale agile software development vision to move from increasingly capable code generation to trustworthy, bounded delegation of R&D authority.
