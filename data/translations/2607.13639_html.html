摘要Abstract

我们介绍OvisOCR2，一个0.8B的文档解析模型。 OvisOCR2 被设计为端到端解析器：给定文档页面图像，它以自然阅读顺序生成 Markdown 表示，涵盖文本、公式、表格和可视区域。我们构建了一个数据引擎，将过滤后的真实文档注释与合成页面相结合，合成页面的渲染图像和 Markdown 目标源自相同的 HTML 源。训练方法包括监督微调、在 4B 分支上采用多组件奖励设计的强化学习、在策略蒸馏到 0.8B 模型以及模型融合。在 OmniDocBench v1.6 上，OvisOCR2 取得了 96.58 的最高总分，将端到端模型置于之前由管道方法主导的排行榜顶部，凸显了端到端文档解析的潜力。在 PureDocBench 上，OvisOCR2 也取得了最高的 Avg3 分数 75.06。除了这两个公共基准之外，我们还根据内部基准评估 OvisOCR2，该基准旨在涵盖更广泛的长尾和具有挑战性的场景。 OvisOCR2 在比较方法中获得了最佳的整体性能，进一步证明了其泛化性和鲁棒性。 OvisOCR2 可在We introduce OvisOCR2, a 0.8B document parsing model. OvisOCR2 is designed as an end-to-end parser: given a document page image, it generates a Markdown representation in natural reading order, covering text, formulas, tables, and visual regions. We build a data engine that combines filtered real-document annotations with synthetic pages whose rendered images and Markdown targets are derived from the same HTML source. The training recipe includes supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning on a 4B branch with a multi-component reward design, on-policy distillation into the 0.8B model, and model fusion. On OmniDocBench v1.6, OvisOCR2 achieves a state-of-the-art overall score of 96.58, placing an end-to-end model at the top of this leaderboard previously dominated by pipeline methods and highlighting the potential of end-to-end document parsing. On PureDocBench, OvisOCR2 also achieves the highest Avg3 score of 75.06. Beyond these two public benchmarks, we evaluate OvisOCR2 on an in-house benchmark designed to cover a broader set of long-tail and challenging scenarios. OvisOCR2 obtains the best overall performance among the compared methods, providing further evidence of its generalization and robustness. OvisOCR2 is available athttps://huggingface.co/ATH-MaaS/OvisOCR2.

1 简介Introduction

文档解析将视觉丰富的文档图像转换为结构化的、机器可读的表示形式。除了简单的 OCR 之外，该任务不仅需要保留文本内容，还需要保留页面组织、阅读顺序、表格、公式、图形、页眉、页脚和其他依赖于布局的元素。解析后的页面通常以 Markdown 形式表示，因此下游应用程序可以对它们进行索引、检索和使用Document parsing converts visually rich document images into structured, machine-readable representations. Moving beyond plain OCR, the task requires preserving not only textual content but also page organization, reading order, tables, formulas, figures, headers, footers, and other layout-dependent elements. Parsed pages are commonly represented in Markdown, so they can be indexed, retrieved, and used by downstream applications[1, 2, 3, 4, 5]。我们研究页面级图像到 Markdown 设置，其中模型处理文档图像并生成页面的统一 Markdown 表示。. We study the page-level image-to-Markdown setting, where a model processes a document image and generates a unified Markdown representation of the page.

目前完成这项任务的方法分为两个系列。管道方法将页面分解为布局分析、区域级内容识别和页面级合并Current approaches to this task fall into two families. Pipeline methods decompose a page into layout analysis, region-level content recognition, and page-level merging[3, 6, 5]。他们在 OmniDocBench v1.6 等主流文档解析排行榜上保持强势. They remain strong on mainstream document parsing leaderboards such as OmniDocBench v1.6[1, 7]，其中在撰写本文时排名前三的方法都是基于管道的。然而，这种设计使部署变得复杂。布局解析和内容识别通常部署为单独的模型，通常具有不同的运行时负载。错误也会跨阶段累积：下游识别器无法完全纠正错过的表格边界、不精确的公式裁剪或错误的读取顺序分配。端到端方法采取相反的路线，使用单个模型一次性读取文档页面图像并生成 Markdown 表示, where the top three methods at the time of writing are pipeline-based. This design, however, complicates deployment. Layout parsing and content recognition are usually deployed as separate models, often with different runtime loads. Errors also accumulate across stages: missed table boundaries, imprecise formula crops, or wrong reading-order assignments cannot be fully rectified by a downstream recognizer. End-to-end methods take the opposite route, using a single model to read the document page image and generate the Markdown representation in one pass[8, 9, 10]。一次性设计简化了部署，并让模型在整个生成过程中都以页面级上下文为条件。然而，现有的端到端方法在主流基准的解析性能方面仍然落后于管道方法。. The one-pass design simplifies deployment and lets the model condition on page-level context throughout generation. Nevertheless, existing end-to-end methods still lag behind pipeline methods in parsing performance on mainstream benchmarks.

我们相信端到端的方法更加优雅，并通过后训练 Qwen3.5-0.8B（Qwen3.5 系列中最小的模型）将 OvisOCR2 开发为紧凑的文档解析器We believe the end-to-end approach is more elegant, and develop OvisOCR2 as a compact document parser by post-training Qwen3.5-0.8B, the smallest model in the Qwen3.5 family[11]。我们的目标是使用超越当前领先公共排行榜的管道方法的单一端到端模型，实现最先进的文档解析，同时保持较小的部署占用空间。通过紧凑的主干网实现这一目标需要精心设计的数据引擎和适合长输出的训练方法。数据引擎将真实文档与合成页面结合起来。真实文档注释由专门的解析器生成，规范化为统一的 Markdown 模式，并通过基于规则的过滤和子集级抽查进行清理。合成页面生成从具有挑战性的示例构建的 HTML 模板开始；相同的 HTML 源用于渲染文档图像并生成 Markdown 注释。训练过程通过监督微调、4B 分支上的 RL 训练、在策略蒸馏为 0.8B 模型以及模型融合来进行。. Our goal is to achieve state-of-the-art document parsing while keeping the deployment footprint small, using a single end-to-end model that surpasses the pipeline methods currently leading public leaderboards. Achieving this with a compact backbone requires both a carefully designed data engine and a training recipe suited to long outputs. The data engine combines real documents with synthetic pages. Real-document annotations are produced by specialized parsers, normalized into a unified Markdown schema, and cleaned through rule-based filtering and subset-level spot-checking. Synthetic page generation starts from HTML templates built from challenging samples; the same HTML source is used to render document images and produce Markdown annotations. Training proceeds through supervised fine-tuning, RL training on a 4B branch, on-policy distillation into the 0.8B model, and model fusion.

我们根据两个公共基准和一个内部基准评估 OvisOCR2。在 OmniDocBench v1.6 上We evaluate OvisOCR2 on two public benchmarks and an in-house benchmark. On OmniDocBench v1.6[1, 7]，OvisOCR2 的总分达到 96.58，创下了新的技术水平，并将端到端解析器领先于该排行榜上的领先管道方法。在 PureDocBench 上, OvisOCR2 reaches an overall score of 96.58, setting a new state of the art and moving an end-to-end parser ahead of the leading pipeline methods on this leaderboard. On PureDocBench[2]，OvisOCR2也以Avg3分数75.06排名第一。为了补充这些公共基准，我们构建了一个包含 1,000 多页的内部基准，涵盖更广泛的文档场景。 OvisOCR2 在该基准测试中获得了最高的总体得分，并且在所有三个难度级别中均处于领先地位。特别是，对于手写和复杂表格文档，这些文档解析起来都具有挑战性，但在现实工作流程中很常见，OvisOCR2 在相应子集上提供了最强的整体性能。, OvisOCR2 also ranks first with an Avg3 score of 75.06. To complement these public benchmarks, we construct an in-house benchmark of more than 1,000 pages spanning a broader range of document scenarios. OvisOCR2 achieves the highest overall score on this benchmark and leads across all three difficulty tiers. In particular, for handwriting and complex-table documents, which are both challenging to parse yet common in real-world workflows, OvisOCR2 delivers the strongest overall performance on the corresponding subsets.

2 数据引擎Data engine

训练端到端文档解析器需要在多个级别上进行准确的监督：细粒度的文本转录、结构上有效的序列化、表格和公式的忠实恢复以及全局一致的阅读顺序。这些要求使得数据准备本身成为系统设计的关键部分。没有单一的数据源是足够的：现实世界的文档提供自然的布局和变化的视觉条件，但它们的注释嘈杂且难以标准化；合成文档提供了可控的训练目标和可扩展的覆盖范围，但如果简单地合成，可能会受到有限的现实性的影响。为了解决这个问题，我们构建了一个由两个互补管道组成的数据引擎，一个真实世界数据管道和一个合成数据管道，如图 1 所示。Training an end-to-end document parser requires supervision that is accurate at multiple levels: fine-grained text transcription, structurally valid serialization, faithful recovery of tables and formulas, and globally coherent reading order. These requirements make data preparation itself a key part of the system design. No single data source is sufficient: real-world documents provide natural layouts and varied visual conditions, but their annotations are noisy and difficult to standardize; synthetic documents offer controllable training targets and scalable coverage, but may suffer from limited realism if synthesized naively. To address this, we build a data engine composed of two complementary pipelines, a real-world data pipeline and a synthetic data pipeline, as shown in Fig.2.

  • •

    真实世界的数据管道。Real-world data pipeline.该管道将大规模的真实文档图像转换为可靠的文档到 Markdown 训练数据。它将模型暴露给现实世界的视觉分布，包括不同的模板、扫描质量、字体样式、语言、布局结构、表格、公式、图形和其他异构文档元素。This pipeline transforms large-scale real-world document images into reliable document-to-Markdown training data. It exposes the model to real-world visual distributions, including diverse templates, scan qualities, font styles, languages, layout structures, tables, formulas, figures, and other heterogeneous document elements.

  • •

    综合数据管道。Synthetic data pipeline.该管道通过合成具有精确的真实注释的可控文档样本来补充现实世界的数据。它的作用是扩大训练分布的长尾覆盖范围，特别是对于自然收集的文档中代表性不足的罕见或困难的情况，例如表格拓扑、密集的公式文本交错、极端的多列布局和长 Markdown 输出。This pipeline complements real-world data by synthesizing controllable document samples with precise ground-truth annotations. Its role is to expand the long-tail coverage of the training distribution, especially for rare or difficult cases that are underrepresented in naturally collected documents, such as table topologies, dense formula-text interleaving, extreme multi-column layouts, and long Markdown outputs.

2.1 真实世界的数据管道Real-world data pipeline

现实世界的管道通过转换和过滤将文档图像转换为训练数据。它首先从 OCR 解析器获取结构化输出，将它们规范化为通用 Markdown 模式，然后通过基于规则的检查和子集级抽查来过滤生成的 Markdown 输出。在此过程中，解析器输出用作结构化候选而不是最终标签；在数据进入训练语料库之前应用确定性转换和保守过滤，以提高序列化一致性和结构有效性。The real-world pipeline turns document images into training data through conversion and filtering. It first obtains structured outputs from OCR parsers, normalizes them into a common Markdown schema, and then filters the resulting Markdown outputs through rule-based checks and subset-level spot-checking. In this process, parser outputs are used as structured candidates rather than final labels; deterministic conversion and conservative filtering are applied before the data enter the training corpus to improve serialization consistency and structural validity.

    表格对齐。Table alignment.我们检查表格行、列、标题和单元格内容是否与原始表格相对应，特别是对于密集表格和合并单元格。We inspect whether table rows, columns, headers, and cell contents correspond to the original table, especially for dense tables and merged cells.

  • •

    视觉区域对齐。Visual-region alignment.我们在原始文档图像上渲染 HTML 图像标签中编码的边界框，并检查它们是否覆盖了预期的图形或图表。We render the bounding boxes encoded in HTML image tags on the original document image and check whether they cover the intended figures or charts.

  • •

    阅读顺序一致性。Reading-order consistency.我们检查采样的输出是否遵循自然的人类阅读顺序，特别是在多列页面和文本、公式、表格和视觉区域密集混合的页面中。We examine whether the sampled outputs follow natural human reading order, especially in multi-column pages and pages with dense mixtures of text, formulas, tables, and visual regions.

这个阶段是有意保守的。目标不是通过自由格式生成来重写或修复转换后的注释，而是防止低质量的数据子集降低训练语料库的质量。当子集仅包含偶尔的小错误时，我们将其与基于规则的转换期间应用的样本级过滤器一起保留。相反，当子集频繁出现错误时，它将从训练语料库中删除。This stage is intentionally conservative. The goal is not to rewrite or repair the converted annotations through free-form generation, but to prevent low-quality data subsets from degrading the training corpus. When a subset contains only occasional minor errors, we retain it together with the sample-level filters applied during rule-based conversion. In contrast, when a subset exhibits frequent errors, it is removed from the training corpus.

2.2 综合数据管道Synthetic data pipeline

合成数据管道遵循真实来源原则，从同一 HTML 源生成文档图像及其 Markdown 目标，而不是通过解析渲染图像来派生目标。这种设计使训练目标具有确定性，并避免合成标签中解析器产生的噪声。该管道首先挖掘通过多方面评估确定的硬样本，使用多模态模型将挖掘的硬样本转换为初始 HTML 模板，采用基于代理的生成过程将其扩展为不同的 HTML 页面，从结构化源中导出 Markdown 基本事实，使用 Playwright 渲染文档图像The synthetic data pipeline follows a source-of-truth principle, generating both the document image and its Markdown target from the same HTML source instead of deriving the target by parsing the rendered image. This design makes the training targets deterministic and avoids parser-derived noise in synthetic labels. The pipeline starts by mining hard samples identified through multi-faceted assessments, uses a multimodal model to convert mined hard samples into initial HTML templates, employs an agent-based generation procedure to expand them into diverse HTML pages, derives Markdown ground truth from the structured source, renders document images with Playwright[13]，最后将得到的图文对经过质检后打包成训练数据。, and finally packages the resulting image-text pairs into training data after quality inspection.

2.2.1 用于 HTML 模板生成的硬样本挖掘Hard sample mining for HTML template generation

合成数据管道从硬样本挖掘开始。我们不是随机合成通用文档页面，而是首先从通过多方面评估确定的失败案例中收集困难样本。当这些样本揭示了代表性不足的文档模式时，例如表格布局、不规则文档结构、页眉/页脚干扰、手写区域、页码模糊和复杂的阅读顺序模式，就会选择这些样本。The synthetic data pipeline begins with hard sample mining. Instead of randomly synthesizing generic document pages, we first collect difficult samples from failure cases identified through multi-faceted assessments. These samples are selected when they reveal underrepresented document patterns, such as table-heavy layouts, irregular document structures, header/footer interference, handwritten regions, page-number ambiguity, and complex reading-order patterns.

每个开采的硬样本不仅被视为孤立的故障，而且被视为可重复使用的合成模式的证据。我们分析失败背后的视觉和结构因素，并将具有相似排版、表格结构、页面组织和本地化要求的样本分组到同一综合系列中。这种分组允许一个模板涵盖一类相关的故障模式，而不是过度拟合到单个页面。Each mined hard sample is treated not merely as an isolated failure, but as evidence of a reusable synthesis pattern. We analyze the visual and structural factors behind the failure and group samples with similar typography, table structure, page organization, and localization requirements into the same synthesis family. This grouping allows one template to cover a class of related failure modes rather than overfitting to a single page.

给定一个具有代表性的硬样本或一组相似的硬样本，我们使用多模态模型来推断文档的视觉和结构意图。该模型将观察到的硬样本转换为初始 HTML 模板，保留后续多样化所需的关键布局结构。对于需要精确定位的元素，模板使用显式 DOM 跨度或元素级包装器，从而能够在渲染期间恢复紧密的边界框。Given a representative hard sample or a cluster of similar hard samples, we use a multimodal model to infer the visual and structural intent of the document. The model converts the observed hard sample into an initial HTML template that preserves the key layout structure needed for subsequent diversification. For elements requiring accurate localization, the template uses explicit DOM spans or element-level wrappers, enabling tight bounding boxes to be recovered during rendering.

因此，生成的 HTML 模板不是最终的合成样本，而是已挖掘的硬样本分布的编程抽象。它保留了在实际故障中观察到的挑战性因素，同时暴露了可控变量以供后续多样化。这种以硬样本为中心的设计将观察到的错误转换为具有清晰训练目标的可扩展文档生成器。The generated HTML template is therefore not a final synthetic sample, but a programmatic abstraction of the mined hard sample distribution. It preserves the challenging factors observed in real failures while exposing controllable variables for subsequent diversification. This hard-sample-centric design converts observed errors into scalable document generators with clean training targets.

获得初始模板后，我们使用基于代理的生成过程将模板扩展为一组不同的 HTML 页面。代理编辑和扩展 HTML 模板，同时保留种子模板的视觉标识和结构意图。此过程支持迭代代码细化、有效性检查和受控多样化。After obtaining the initial template, we use an agent-based generation procedure to expand the template into a diverse set of HTML pages. The agent edits and extends the HTML template while preserving the visual identity and structural intent of the seed template. This procedure supports iterative code refinement, validity checking, and controlled diversification.

多样化过程在内容和结构层面引入了随机性。内容级别的变化涵盖语义字段、文本长度、数值、公式和特定领域的术语。结构级别的变化涵盖表结构、部分层次结构、页面组织和视觉区域放置。这些变体共同将每个种子模板扩展为具有不同内容和布局配置的综合系列。The diversification process introduces randomness at both content and structure levels. Content-level variation covers semantic fields, textual length, numerical values, formulas, and domain-specific terminology. Structure-level variation covers table structure, section hierarchy, page organization, and visual-region placement. Together, these variations expand each seed template into a synthesis family with diverse content and layout configurations.

为了在多样化过程中保持标签有效，代理受到有效性规则的约束。每个生成的页面必须保持可渲染、视觉上合理、符合类别并且可转换为有效的 Markdown。该管道进一步维护特定于域的随机内容池，允许合成内容在语义上变化，同时保持生成的标签干净和一致。这确保了合成数据规模大、结构多样且注释可靠。To keep labels valid during diversification, the agent is constrained by validity rules. Each generated page must remain renderable, visually plausible, category-compliant, and convertible into valid Markdown. The pipeline further maintains domain-specific randomized content pools, allowing synthetic content to vary semantically while keeping the generated labels clean and consistent. This ensures that the synthetic data are large in scale, structurally diverse, and reliably annotated.

3 培训Training

3.1 培训管道Training pipeline

端到端文档解析需要广泛的格式学习和可靠的结构化生成。该模型必须将文档页面图像转换为 Markdown 表示形式，同时保留文本保真度、公式可渲染性、表格结构、阅读顺序和页面级完整性。这些要求需要超越令牌级别模仿的监督信号，因为表格、公式和长页面中的许多错误在结构上更容易验证，而不是仅通过下一个令牌丢失来表达。End-to-end document parsing requires both broad format learning and reliable structured generation. The model must convert a document page image into a Markdown representation while preserving text fidelity, formula renderability, table structure, reading order, and page-level completeness. These requirements call for supervision signals beyond token-level imitation, because many errors in tables, formulas, and long pages are easier to verify structurally than to express through next-token loss alone.

因此，我们将培训流程组织成具有不同角色的阶段。有监督的微调构建了最初的端到端文档解析策略；强化学习利用文本、公式和表格奖励进一步改进了硬页上的策略；在政策蒸馏将 4B 教师的奖励一致行为转移到可部署的 0.8B 学生；然后模型融合形成最终模型。We therefore organize the training pipeline into stages with different roles. Supervised fine-tuning builds the initial end-to-end document parsing policy; reinforcement learning further improves the policy on hard pages using text, formula, and table rewards; on-policy distillation transfers the reward-aligned behavior of a 4B teacher into a deployable 0.8B student; model fusion then forms the final model.

3.2 监督微调Supervised fine-tuning

SFT 阶段建立了从文档图像生成 Markdown 表示的基本策略。 SFT 使用数据部分中描述的完整数据混合。该混合物构建在由真实世界数据管道处理的真实文档页面上，并由合成数据管道中的受控样本进行补充。真实数据会产生自然布局、扫描质量变化、文档类型和视觉噪声。合成数据增加了表格、公式密集型页面、长 Markdown 输出和其他长尾结构的覆盖范围。因此，该阶段为下游 RL 和 OPD 阶段提供了广泛的覆盖范围和格式一致性。The SFT stage establishes the base policy for generating Markdown representations from document images. SFT uses the full data mixture described in the data section. The mixture is built on real document pages processed by the real-world data pipeline and is complemented by controlled samples from the synthetic data pipeline. Real data contribute natural layouts, scan quality variations, document types, and visual noise. Synthetic data increase the coverage of tables, formula-intensive pages, long Markdown outputs, and other long-tail structures. This stage therefore provides broad coverage and format consistency for the downstream RL and OPD stages.

我们在同一文档解析目标上使用全参数 SFT 训练 Qwen3.5-0.8B 和 Qwen3.5-4B 模型。 0.8B 模型训练两个 epoch，而 4B 模型训练 20% 的 epoch，以减少较大分支的训练成本。 0.8B SFT 检查点初始化可部署的学生分支，而 4B SFT 检查点作为 4B RL 的基础策略；然后将生成的 4B RL 检查点用作 OPD 教师。我们使用 16K 最大序列长度和动态图像分辨率预算，保留细粒度的视觉信息，而不强制所有页面采用固定分辨率。We train both the Qwen3.5-0.8B and Qwen3.5-4B models with full-parameter SFT on the same document parsing objective. The 0.8B model is trained for two epochs, whereas the 4B model is trained for 20% of an epoch to reduce the training cost of the larger branch. The 0.8B SFT checkpoint initializes the deployable student branch, while the 4B SFT checkpoint serves as the base policy for 4B RL; the resulting 4B RL checkpoint is then used as the OPD teacher. We use a 16K maximum sequence length together with a dynamic image-resolution budget, preserving fine-grained visual information without forcing all pages into a fixed resolution.

3.3 强化学习Reinforcement learning

强化学习使用采样输出的奖励信号进一步改进了端到端文档解析策略。许多重要的解析错误是结构性的而不是纯粹的词汇性的：表格可能包含大部分正确的文本，但单元格拓扑不正确；公式可能看起来与字符串相似，但无法呈现或表示不同的表达式；长页面可能会出现遗漏、重复或截断。这些错误通过令牌级模仿损失来弱表达，但可以通过编程检查、基于渲染的比较和结构感知解析来测量。RL further improves the end-to-end document parsing policy using reward signals on sampled outputs. Many important parsing errors are structural rather than purely lexical: a table may contain mostly correct text while having an incorrect cell topology; a formula may look similar as a string but fail to render or represent a different expression; long pages may suffer from omissions, repetition, or truncation. These errors are weakly expressed by token-level imitation loss, but they can be measured through programmatic checks, rendering-based comparison, and structure-aware parsing.

我们使用组相对策略优化（GRPO）We use Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)[14]作为主要的 RL 算法。对于每个提示，我们从策略中采样多个响应，并从可验证的奖励中计算群体相对优势，而无需训练额外的价值模型。这种表述非常适合文档解析，其中响应很长、结构化并且可以通过文本、公式和表格分析器进行评估。 GRPO 训练模型在同一页面的多个输出中选择更好的候选者，从而强化更完整、稳定和结构有效的生成行为。as the main RL algorithm. For each prompt, we sample multiple responses from the policy and compute group-relative advantages from verifiable rewards, without training an additional value model. This formulation is well suited to document parsing, where responses are long, structured, and evaluable by text, formula, and table analyzers. GRPO trains the model to prefer better candidates among multiple outputs for the same page, thereby reinforcing generation behaviors that are more complete, stable, and structurally valid.

3.3.1 符合政策的硬案例建设On-policy hard case construction

RL 数据的构建方式与广泛的 SFT 混合数据不同。该阶段主要使用来自合成数据管道的样本，其中真实结构足够准确以支持文本、公式和表格奖励。保留较小的高质量真实文档集，以保留自然的页面分布和不同的视觉条件。RL data are constructed differently from the broad SFT mixture. The stage primarily uses samples from the synthetic data pipeline, where ground-truth structures are accurate enough to support text, formula, and table rewards. A smaller set of high-quality real documents is retained to preserve natural page distributions and varied visual conditions.

训练集通过策略过滤进一步形成：我们首先在候选页面上运行当前策略，并使用奖励函数对生成的响应进行评分。非常简单和极其困难的样本提供的有用学习信号很少，而响应变化很小的奖励平坦样本对于群体相关学习不太有用。因此，训练强调模型偶尔可以产生明显优于其平均行为的响应的页面。The training set is further shaped through on-policy filtering: we first run the current policy on candidate pages and score the generated responses with the reward function. Very easy and extremely hard samples provide little useful learning signal, and reward-flat samples with little variation across responses are less useful for group-relative learning. Training therefore emphasizes pages on which the model can occasionally produce responses that are clearly better than its average behavior.

3.3.2 多元奖励设计Multi-component reward design

强化学习奖励是为完整的页面输出而设计的，并避免将文档解析质量降低到单个文本相似度分数。标量奖励是根据真实情况中实际存在的组件计算的：文本、表格和显示公式。全局有效性检查（例如最大长度截断和不可解析的结构）被用作将受影响的可用组件归零的保护措施，而不是形成独立的奖励项。The RL reward is designed for complete page outputs and avoids reducing document parsing quality to a single text-similarity score. The scalar reward is computed from the components that are actually present in the ground truth: text, tables, and display formulas. Global validity checks, such as max-length truncation and unparseable structures, are applied as guards that zero out the affected available components rather than forming an independent reward term.

3.3.3 可扩展的多模式强化学习训练Scalable multimodal RL training

用于文档解析的多模态强化学习训练需要处理长文本响应、页面级图像输入和基于渲染的奖励计算。为了使训练具有可扩展性，我们应用了以下优化。Multimodal RL training for document parsing needs to handle long text responses, page-level image inputs, and rendering-based reward computation. To make the training infra scalable, we apply the following optimizations.

首先，奖励计算使用分层并行性和快捷路径。推出后，多个奖励工作人员并行对页面样本进行评分。在每个worker内部，公式奖励首先执行匹配和归一化：归一化的精确匹配直接分配满分，缺失或无效的预测分配零，只有剩余的情况进入CDM渲染比较。 CDM 计算是通过可重用的进程池执行的，每个任务都有超时设置，防止少量有问题的公式阻塞工作人员。表奖励遵循相同的原则：规范化的精确匹配绕过 TEDS，而无效或不可解析的表则收到零。对于长页面或异常输出，页面级匹配器还使用超时回退来防止匹配成为尾部延迟瓶颈。奖励分析保留在训练循环中，以便将速度减慢归因于匹配、标准化、基于渲染的公式奖励或表结构评分。First, reward computation uses hierarchical parallelism and shortcut paths. After rollout, page samples are scored by multiple reward workers in parallel. Within each worker, the formula reward performs matching and normalization first: normalized exact matches are assigned full credit directly, missing or invalid predictions are assigned zero, and only the remaining cases enter CDM rendering comparison. CDM computation is executed through a reusable process pool with per-task timeouts, preventing a small number of problematic formulas from blocking the worker. The table reward follows the same principle: normalized exact matches bypass TEDS, while invalid or unparseable tables receive zero. For long pages or abnormal outputs, the page-level matcher also uses timeout fallback to prevent matching from becoming a tail-latency bottleneck. Reward profiling is kept in the training loop so that slowdowns can be attributed to matching, normalization, rendering-based formula rewards, or table-structure scoring.

其次，多模式输入传输路径针对高分辨率文档页面进行了优化。视觉预处理可以产生大的页面级张量，并且在工作人员之间重复传输它们会增加主机端的内存压力和通信量。因此，训练使用对象存储支持的引用传递：大型视觉张量存储一次，并由轻量级句柄和紧凑元数据一起引用。策略更新和参考约束计算在需要时检索并组装实际的视觉输入。这减少了传输开销，并防止高分辨率页面或长批次将训练瓶颈转移到序列化和数据移动。Second, the multimodal input transfer path is optimized for high-resolution document pages. Visual preprocessing can produce large page-level tensors, and repeatedly transmitting them across workers would increase host-side memory pressure and communication volume. Training therefore uses object-store-backed reference passing: large visual tensors are stored once and referenced by lightweight handles together with compact metadata. The policy update and reference-constraint computation retrieve and assemble the actual visual inputs when needed. This reduces transfer overhead and prevents high-resolution pages or long batches from moving the training bottleneck to serialization and data movement.

第三，演员更新针对长响应进行了优化。我们使用公共前缀掩码，以便同一提示的多个推出之间的长共享前缀不会主导损失，在生成分支发散后将梯度集中在标记上。在严格的on-policy、单epoch更新设置下，系统还避免了额外的旧策略概率传递。这些优化减少了参与者端计算和长页面输出的内存压力。Third, the actor update is optimized for long responses. We use a common-prefix mask so that long shared prefixes among multiple rollouts of the same prompt do not dominate the loss, focusing gradients on tokens after the generation branches diverge. Under the strictly on-policy, single-epoch update setting, the system also avoids an additional old-policy probability pass. These optimizations reduce actor-side computation and memory pressure on long page outputs.

5 结论Conclusion

在本报告中，我们介绍了 OvisOCR2，这是一个用于页面级文档解析的紧凑型 0.8B 端到端模型。我们构建了一个数据引擎，将过滤后的真实文档注释与源对齐的合成页面相结合，并使用应用监督微调、强化学习、策略蒸馏和模型融合的训练配方。根据公共和内部基准进行评估，OvisOCR2 在 OmniDocBench v1.6 和 PureDocBench 上建立了新的最先进结果，同时在我们的内部比较中获得了最高的总体得分。未来的工作将集中于提高对退化的现实世界图像的鲁棒性，并进一步增强对手写密集和复杂表格文档的性能。In this report, we present OvisOCR2, a compact 0.8B end-to-end model for page-level document parsing. We build a data engine that combines filtered real-document annotations with source-aligned synthetic pages, and use a training recipe that applies supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, on-policy distillation, and model fusion. Evaluated on both public and in-house benchmarks, OvisOCR2 establishes new state-of-the-art results on OmniDocBench v1.6 and PureDocBench, while also achieving the highest overall score in our in-house comparison. Future work will focus on improving robustness to degraded real-world images and further strengthening performance on handwriting-heavy and complex-table documents.
