使用多智能体强化学习协作运输任意物体的形状形成Shape Formation for the Cooperative Transportation of Arbitrary Objects Using Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

摘要Abstract

协作对象运输在许多领域都至关重要，包括工业到家庭服务。 一种流行的运输策略是在多机器人系统顶部搬运物体。相应的任务通常通过将其分解为 三个相互关联的子问题：编队控制、协作导航和避免碰撞。 现实世界物体带来的一个特殊挑战是它们可能具有任意形状和不均匀的质量分布，因此需要安全支撑物体的机器人编队。 在这项工作中，我们通过提出一种新颖的多智能体强化学习方法来解决运输此类现实世界物体的模式形成控制的挑战。 我们的方法使多机器人系统能够自动将自身定位在物体下方以支撑其重量，同时在形成过程中避开障碍物。 我们对不同环境和不同数量机器人的评估表明，我们的方法可以产生可靠地产生平衡编队的策略，并推广到具有复杂几何形状和不均匀质量分布的杂乱场景和物体。Cooperative object transportation is essential in numerous domains, including industrial to domestic services. A popular transportation strategy is to carry objects on top of multi-robot systems. The corresponding task is typically solved by decomposing it into three interconnected subproblems: formation control, cooperative navigation, and collision avoidance. A particular challenge posed by real-world objects is their potentially arbitrary shape and non-uniform mass distribution, necessitating robot formations that securely support the object. In this work, we address the challenge of pattern formation control for transporting such real-world objects by proposing a novel multi-agent reinforcement learning approach. Our approach enables a multi-robot system to autonomously position itself underneath an object to support its weight while avoiding obstacles during the formation process. Our evaluations with diverse environments and varying numbers of robots show that our approach leads to policies that reliably produce balanced formations and generalize to cluttered scenes and objects with complex geometry and non-uniform mass distribution.

Moreover, the existing strategies primarily focus on navigation during transport, rather than on the prior challenge of forming a stable load-bearing formation beforehand. For the transportation of such objects, we cannot rely on specifically designed interfaces and require formation control approaches that generalize to arbitrary objects.

VI 结论CONCLUSION

在本文中，我们提出了一种基于多智能体强化学习的框架，用于负载感知的协作对象传输。我们特别解决了运输之前的形成阶段，并在集中训练分散执行范式中使用多代理近端策略优化来训练分散机器人策略。由此产生的策略使机器人能够在杂乱的环境中导航，进入物体的足迹，并自组织成稳定的支撑配置，以支持具有规则和高度不规则几何形状以及不均匀质量分布的物体。In this paper, we present a framework based on multi-agent reinforcement learning to load-aware cooperative object transport. We specifically addressed the formation phase preceding transport and trained decentralized robot policies using multi-agent proximal policy optimization within a centralized training decentralized execution paradigm. The resulting policies enable robots to navigate cluttered environments, enter an object’s footprint, and self-organize into stable support configurations for objects with both regular and highly irregular geometries and non-uniform mass distributions.

跨多个环境和机器人数量的后评估表明，所提出的方法实现了高成功率、小执行步骤以及随着机器人数量的增加而可扩展。此外，学习到的形式非常接近混合整数线性规划最优解决方案，表明这些策略隐式地捕获了底层的负载平衡结构。我们的结果表明，将负载共享目标明确地纳入学习公式中可以实现超越纯粹几何策略的协调多机器人运输。 据我们所知，我们的工作是第一个明确解决物体搬运任务模式形成过程中物体质量分布不均匀的问题，同时确保所有机器人参与运输。Post-evaluation across multiple environments and numbers of robots demonstrates that the proposed approach achieves high success rates, small execution steps, and scalability with increasing numbers of robots. Moreover, the learned formations closely approximate mixed integer linear programming-optimal solutions, indicating that the policies implicitly capture the underlying load-balancing structures. Our results show that explicitly incorporating load-sharing objectives into the learning formulation enables coordinated multi-robot transport beyond purely geometric strategies. To the best of our knowledge, our work is the first to explicitly address non-uniform object mass distribution during pattern formation in object-carrying tasks, while also ensuring the participation of all robots in transportation.

尽管我们取得了令人鼓舞的结果，但仍有几个方面值得未来的研究。我们的目标是将框架扩展到提升后的完整运输执行，集成物体几何形状和质量分布的机载估计，并研究更具可扩展性的策略架构（例如基于图形的表示），以进一步改善大规模多机器人系统中的协调。Despite our encouraging results, there as several aspects that warrant future research. We aim at extending the framework toward full transport execution after lifting, integrating onboard estimation of object geometry and mass distribution, and investigating more scalable policy architectures such as graph-based representations to further improve coordination in large-scale multi-robot systems.
