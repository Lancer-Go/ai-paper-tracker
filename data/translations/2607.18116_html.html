局限性Limitations

尽管 SGA 在空间接地方面取得了显着改进，但仍然存在一些局限性。首先，当前的实现专门用于 Manim 引擎中的 2D 几何图元；将其扩展到 3D 场景将需要更复杂的相机空间投影和深度排序仪器。其次，虽然 SGA 解决了物理重叠问题，但它目前并未解决时间同步问题（例如，确保绘制形状时标签准确显示）。最后，MMMC-Code 基准仅限于英语数学内容，未来需要开展工作来评估该框架在不同语言和教学传统中的稳健性。此外，MVQS 尚未针对人类专家注释进行验证；这种相关性分析仍然是未来工作的重要方向。Despite the significant improvements in spatial grounding provided by the SGA, several limitations remain. First, the current implementation is specialized for 2D geometric primitives within the Manim engine; extending this to 3D scenes would require a more complex instrumentation of camera-space projections and depth-sorting. Second, while the SGA resolves physical overlaps, it does not currently address temporal synchronization issues (e.g., ensuring a label appears exactly when a shape is drawn). Finally, the MMMC-Code benchmark is restricted to English-language mathematical content, and future work is required to evaluate the framework’s robustness across diverse linguistic and pedagogical traditions. Additionally, MVQS has not yet been validated against human expert annotations; such correlation analysis remains an important direction for future work.

道德声明Ethics Statement
