摘要Abstract

在金融市场中，对价格变动做出系统性反应的连续政策可能会被其他市场参与者所预测。本文研究了大型语言模型（LLM）智能体是否通过以下方式表现出这种定向结构：In financial markets, a sequential policy that reacts systematically to price movements may become predictable to other market participants. We compare returns during long and flat intervals along the same stock’s intraday path after removing the overall fraction of long decisions. This exposure-matched measure reveals persistent negative timing across modality, horizon, state, and model family. Shuffling saved action sequences substantially attenuates the effect, showing that alignment between actions and subsequent returns drives the negative score. 1 简介Introduction

金融市场理论表明，价格可以聚合分散的信息，订单流（提交交易的顺序）可以向其他市场参与者透露信息Financial-market theory shows that prices can aggregate dispersed information and that order flow, the sequence of submitted trades, can reveal information to other market participants(Grossman and Stiglitz, 1980; Kyle, 1985)。因此，即使每项行动单独看来都是合理的，也可以预期会出台一项对显着价格变动做出系统反应的政策。证据将个人交易与注意力、过去的回报、市场表现、反馈、过度自信和高昂的营业额联系起来. 4 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们开发了 RetailAgent，一个用于解释连续 LLM 交易行为的受控框架。在涵盖输入模式、决策范围、账户状态和模型系列的配置中，代理表现出一致的负暴露匹配库存时间。轨迹调整大大削弱了这种影响，而自我调节则增加了政策的持久性，并通过这两种行动改变了股票交易日之间的条件时机。这些发现揭示了法学硕士行动轨迹中超出平均暴露和测试的基于价格的信号的稳定方向结构。未来的工作可以检验这种结构在交易成本、市场影响和战略反馈下是否仍然可行。In this paper, we developed RetailAgent, a controlled framework for interpreting sequential LLM trading behavior. Across configurations spanning input modality, decision horizon, account state, and model family, agents exhibit consistently negative exposure-matched within-stock timing. Trajectory shuffling substantially attenuates this effect, while self-conditioning increases policy persistence and shifts conditional timing among stock-days with both actions.
