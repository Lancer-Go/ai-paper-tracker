1. 简介Introduction

Text-to-SQL 旨在使非技术用户能够通过自然语言 (NL) 问题访问关系数据库Text-to-SQL aims to enable non-technical users to access relational databases through natural language (NL) questions(Liu et al., 2025a; Hong et al., 2025)并已成为大语言模型（LLM）时代数据分析和商业智能的重要接口and has become an important interface for data analytics and business intelligence in the era of large language models (LLMs)(Zhu et al., 2025b, 2024; Luo et al., 2026a, 2018)。 尽管最近取得了进展，但现实世界中的文本到 SQL 仍然具有挑战性。 实用的文本转SQL系统需要在大型异构模式中定位相关表和列，将查询条件与数据库内容对齐，并构建复杂的查询逻辑. Despite recent progress, real-world Text-to-SQL remains challenging. A practical Text-to-SQL system needs to locate relevant tables and columns in large and heterogeneous schemas, align query conditions with database contents, and construct complex query logic(Li et al., 2024a; Luo et al., 2025)。 因此，正确的 SQL 生成需要将这些推理步骤协调为有效的解决过程。. Correct SQL generation therefore requires coordinating these reasoning steps into an effective solving procedure.

从固定管道到编排。From Fixed Pipelines to Orchestration.为了解决这些困难，一些文本到 SQL 系统引入了精心设计的管道，将文本到 SQL 分解为更易于管理的子任务，例如模式链接、值检索、SQL 生成、修订和选择To handle these difficulties, some Text-to-SQL systems introduce meticulously designed pipelines to decompose Text-to-SQL into more manageable subtasks, such as schema linking, value retrieval, SQL generation, revision, and selection(Dong et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2025a; Cao et al., 2024; Xie et al., 2025)。 一些方法通过结合经过大规模监督训练的阶段特定推理模型来进一步强化此类管道. Some methods further strengthen such pipelines by incorporating stage-specific reasoning models trained with large-scale supervision(Hu et al., 2026; Sheng and Shuai, 2025; Wang et al., 2025b)。 这些设计通过引入结构化推理程序和特定于任务的适应而取得了强大的性能。. These designs have achieved strong performance by introducing structured reasoning procedures and task-specific adaptation.

Some queries may be resolved through direct SQL generation, whereas others may require additional evidence-gathering, refinement, or iterative generation as intermediate feedback emerges. Therefore, effective orchestration requires both a composable decision space and a policy that adapts its orchestration decisions to each query’s evolving reasoning process.

(C2) 用于编排学习的稀缺工作流数据。(C2) Scarce Workflow Data for Orchestration Learning.学习这种自适应编排需要大规模、高质量和多样化的文本到 SQL 工作流数据，这些数据涵盖不同的查询和数据库、工作流组合或决策模式和推理轨迹。然而，此类数据很少可用，并且通过手动注释获得也是不切实际的。这提出了在不依赖人工设计的工作流程的情况下导出工作流程级别监督的挑战。Learning such adaptive orchestration requires large-scale, high-quality, and diverse Text-to-SQL workflow data that covers different queries and databases, workflow compositions, or decision patterns and reasoning trajectories. This raises the challenge of deriving workflow-level supervision without relying on human-crafted workflows.

(C3) 大型、非独特且嘈杂的工作流程空间。(C3) Large, Non-Unique, and Noisy Workflow Space.工作流程空间是组合的并且本质上是多样化的。一个问题可能承认多个有效的工作流程，并且没有一个规范的工作流程通常可以作为基本事实。 此外，达到正确答案的工作流程不一定可靠，因为它的成功可能是偶然的或不稳定的。 因此，有效的编排学习需要识别高质量的工作流程，同时保留有用的工作流程多样性。The workflow space is combinatorial and inherently diverse. A question may admit multiple valid workflows, and no single canonical workflow can generally serve as the ground truth. Moreover, a workflow that reaches the correct answer is not necessarily reliable, since its success may be accidental or unstable. Effective orchestration learning therefore requires identifying high-quality workflows while preserving useful workflow diversity.
