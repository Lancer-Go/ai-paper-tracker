摘要Abstract

四项词语类比（Four-term word analogies (AA:BB::CC:DD）通常被几何建模为“平行四边形”，但最近的研究表明，该模型很难捕捉人类如何产生类比，简单的局部相似性启发法通常可以提供更好的解释) are classically modeled geometrically as “parallelograms,” yet recent work suggests this model poorly captures how humans produce analogies, with simple local-similarity heuristics often providing a better account[15]。但是，平行四边形模型失败是因为它是一个糟糕的类比关系模型，还是因为人们不太擅长生成保留关系的类比？我们比较了人类和大语言模型（LLM）在同一组类比问题上的类比完成情况. But does the parallelogram model fail because it is a bad model of analogical relations, or because people are not very good at generating relation-preserving analogies? We compared human and large language model (LLM) analogy completions on the same set of analogy problems from[15]。我们发现，LLM 生成的类比被可靠地判断为比人类生成的类比更好，并且与分布嵌入空间 (GloVe) 中的平行四边形结构更加一致。至关重要的是，我们表明，对人类类比的改进是由更大的平行四边形对齐和减少对可访问单词的依赖驱动的，而不是增强对局部相似性的敏感性。此外，LLM 的优势不是由 LLM 的一致优越响应驱动的，而是由人类产生的弱完成的长尾驱动的：当仅比较两个系统的模态（最频繁）响应时，LLM 优势消失。然而，更大的平行四边形对齐和更低的词频继续预测哪些 LLM 完成的评分高于人类。总的来说，这些结果表明平行四边形模型对单词类比的解释还不错。相反，人类可能常常无法做到. We find that LLM-generated analogies are reliably judged as better than human-generated ones, and are also more closely aligned with the parallelogram structure in a distributional embedding space (GloVe). Crucially, we show that the improvement over human analogies was driven by greater parallelogram alignment and reduced reliance on accessible words rather than enhanced sensitivity to local similarity. Moreover, the LLM advantage is driven not by uniformly superior responses by LLMs, but by humans producing a long tail of weak completions: when only modal (most frequent) responses by both systems are compared, the LLM advantage disappears. However, greater parallelogram alignment and lower word frequency continue to predict which LLM completions are rated higher than those of humans. Overall, these results suggest that the parallelogram model is not a poor account of word analogy. Rather, humans may often fail to生产produce满足此关系约束的完成，而法学硕士则更加一致。completions that satisfy this relational constraint, whereas LLMs do so more consistently.

大语言模型（LLM）的出现提供了再次重新审视平行四边形模型的机会。尽管法学硕士是高度不透明的系统，具有复杂的注意力机制和难以访问的潜在表示，但法学硕士在模拟人类语义判断方面表现出了令人惊讶的能力The emergence of large language models (LLMs) offers an opportunity to revisit the parallelogram model again. Despite being highly opaque systems with complex attention mechanisms and inaccessible latent representations, LLMs have shown a surprising capacity to simulate human semantic judgments[16]并展示在情境学习等任务中表现出来的新兴关系推理能力and demonstrate emergent relational reasoning abilities manifested in tasks like in-context learning[1]。在这里，我们询问法学硕士生成的类比是否被认为比人类生成的类比质量更高，以及是否可以通过简单的认知模型（例如平行四边形模型或局部相似性启发法）捕获任何此类优势。为了解决这些问题，我们向六名最先进的法学硕士提出了一系列先前向人类提出的单词类比问题. Here, we ask whether LLM-generated analogies are perceived to be higher quality than human-generated analogies, and whether any such advantage is captured by simple cognitive models such as the parallelogram model or local similarity heuristics. To address these questions, we prompt six state-of-the-art LLMs with a large set of word analogy problems previously given to humans[15]并收集人类对法学硕士和人类完成程度如何保持预期关系的判断。and collect human judgments of how well LLM and human completions preserve the intended relation.

我们发现人们可靠地判断 LLM 类比比人类生成的类比更好，并且平行四边形和局部相似性度量可以更好地捕获 LLM 类比。我们还表明，法学硕士相对于人类的优势是通过外部分布嵌入空间中更大的平行四边形对齐以及使用不太容易访问的单词来预测的。然而，这种优势并不是由法学硕士产生一致的优质反应驱动的，而是由人类产生了低质量补全的长尾驱动的：当比较仅限于最频繁的（模态）反应时，法学硕士的优势消失了，即使法学硕士比人类更平行四边形排列且使用频率更低的单词的补全继续获得更高的评级。这些结果表明，虽然平行四边形模型可能无法很好地描述人类如何生成类比，但它们仍然捕捉到了人们在良好类比中所看重的东西，并且法学硕士更擅长产生满足这种模式的响应，即使这种模式不需要反映在模型自身的内部表示中。We find that people reliably judge LLM analogies to be better than human-generated analogies, and that LLM analogies are better captured by both parallelogram and local similarity measures. We also show that the advantage of LLMs over humans is predicted by greater parallelogram alignment in an external distributional embedding space and by the use of less accessible words. However, this advantage is driven not by LLMs producing uniformly superior responses, but by humans generating a long tail of low-quality completions: when the comparison is restricted to only the most frequent (modal) responses, the LLM advantage disappears, even though completions where LLMs are more parallelogram-aligned and use less frequent words than humans continue to receive higher ratings. These results suggest that while parallelogram models may poorly describe how humans generate analogies, they nonetheless capture what people value in good analogies—and that LLMs are better at producing responses that satisfy this pattern, even though this pattern need not be reflected in the models’ own internal representations.

讨论Discussion

通过直接比较法学硕士对经典四词词类比的完成与人类的完成，我们询问当今的法学硕士是否像人类一样解决类比问题，如果不是，那么差异揭示了捕获每个系统的表示和启发式方法。我们发现（1）平均而言，人们认为法学硕士生成的类比比人类生成的类比更好； (2) 与人类的反应相比，平行四边形模型可以更好地预测 LLM 类比，尽管与人类的反应很相似，整体上局部相似性启发法可以更好地捕获它们。 (3) 当 LLM 生成的类比更清晰地实例化平行四边形关系并涉及不易理解（低频）的单词时，它们被认为更好。 (4) 这一优势不是由法学硕士在全面方面产生优异的反应所驱动，而是由人类产生了较弱的完成结果的长尾所驱动。当仅比较模态（最常见）响应时，LLM 优势消失，尽管 LLM 和人类模态完成之间的评级差异继续通过更大的平行四边形对齐和更低的词频来预测。By directly comparing LLMs’ completions of classic four-term word analogies with human completions, we asked whether today’s LLMs solve analogies like people do and, if not, what the differences reveal about the representations and heuristics that capture each system. We found (1) People judge LLM-generated analogies to be better than human-generated ones, on average; (2) LLM analogies are better predicted by the parallelogram model than human responses, although, much like people’s, they are better captured by local similarity heuristics overall. (3) LLM-generated analogies are considered better when they more cleanly instantiate the parallelogram relation and involve less accessible (lower-frequency) words. (4) This advantage is driven not by LLMs producing superior responses across the board, but by humans generating a long tail of weaker completions. When only modal (most frequent) responses are compared, the LLM advantage disappears, though rating differences between LLM and human modal completions continue to be predicted by greater parallelogram alignment and lower word frequency.

同时，我们还观察到词汇可访问性对关系评级的强大影响。法学硕士的完成频率往往低于人类完成的频率，人们倾向于对频率较低的完成给予更高的评分。然而，至关重要的是，在控制可访问性后，平行四边形对齐的效果保持稳定，这表明法学硕士的反应被评为更好，不仅因为它们使用了不太常见或更“聪明”的单词，而且因为它们更好地满足了平行四边形模型捕获的关系约束。At the same time, we also observe a robust effect of lexical accessibility on relation ratings.
