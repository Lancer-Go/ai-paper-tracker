摘要Abstract

对话式人工智能有潜力帮助用户进行初步医疗咨询，特别是在医疗保健专业人员的接触机会有限的情况下。然而，许多现有的医疗对话系统以单轮问答模式运行或依赖基于模板的数据集，限制了对话的真实性和多语言适用性。在这项工作中，我们介绍Conversational artificial intelligence has the potential to assist users in preliminary medical consultations, particularly in settings where access to healthcare professionals is limited. However, many existing medical dialogue systems operate in a single-turn question–answering paradigm or rely on template-based datasets, limiting conversational realism and multilingual applicability. In this work, we introduce医疗援助对话MedAidDialog，一个多语言多轮医疗对话数据集，旨在模拟现实的医患咨询。该数据集扩展了, a multilingual multi-turn medical dialogue dataset designed to simulate realistic physician–patient consultations. The dataset extends the拨号盘MDDial通过使用大型语言模型生成综合咨询来构建语料库，并将其进一步扩展为涵盖七种语言的并行多语言语料库：英语、印地语、泰卢固语、泰米尔语、孟加拉语、马拉地语和阿拉伯语。 在此数据集的基础上，我们开发了corpus by generating synthetic consultations using large language models and further expands them into a parallel multilingual corpus covering seven languages: English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, and Arabic. Building on this dataset, we develop医疗援助LMMedAidLM，一种对话式医学模型，使用量化小语言模型的参数高效微调进行训练，无需高端计算基础设施即可进行部署。我们的框架还包含可选的患者背景信息（例如年龄、性别、过敏），以个性化咨询过程。实验结果表明，所提出的系统可以通过多轮对话有效地进行症状诱发并生成诊断建议。我们进一步进行医学专家评估，以评估所生成咨询的合理性和连贯性。, a conversational medical model trained using parameter-efficient fine-tuning on quantized small language models, enabling deployment without high-end computational infrastructure. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed system can effectively perform symptom elicitation through multi-turn dialogue and generate diagnostic recommendations. We further conduct medical expert evaluation to assess the plausibility and coherence of the generated consultations.

2 相关工作Related Work

之前有关医学对话的工作已经从结构化和面向任务的诊断系统发展到神经和基于法学硕士的对话助理。早期的数据集和系统强调症状收集、槽位填充或诊断预测，但往往缺乏自然的多轮医患互动Prior work on medical dialogue has progressed from structured and task-oriented diagnosis systems toward neural and LLM-based conversational assistants. Early datasets and systems emphasized symptom collection, slot filling, or diagnosis prediction, but often lacked natural multi-turn physician–patient interaction(Zeng et al., 2020; Liu et al., 2022)。最近的资源明确针对多轮医疗咨询。例如，MDDial 引入了英语鉴别诊断对话数据集，但它是通过模板构建的，并且仍然部分脚本化. More recent resources explicitly target multi-turn medical consultation. For example, MDDial introduces an English differential-diagnosis dialogue dataset, but it is constructed through templates and remains partially scripted(Macherla et al., 2023)。 MedDG 和仲景推进中文多轮医学对话，重点是实体感知咨询并利用现实世界对话改进主动询问. MedDG and Zhongjing advance multi-turn medical conversation in Chinese, with a focus on entity-aware consultation and improving proactive inquiry using real-world dialogue(Liu et al., 2022; Yang et al., 2024)。 MediTOD 进一步提供带有详细注释的英语病史采集数据集，尽管它主要是为结构化的面向任务的交互而设计的. MediTOD further provides an English medical history-taking dataset with detailed annotations, though it is primarily designed for structured task-oriented interaction(Saley et al., 2024).

与此同时，ChatDoctor、Med-Chat 等医学法学硕士及相关系统已表明，与通用法学硕士相比，特定领域的微调可显着提高医疗响应质量In parallel, medical LLMs such as ChatDoctor, Med-Chat, and related systems have shown that domain-specific fine-tuning substantially improves medical response quality over general-purpose LLMs(Li et al., 2023; Chu et al., 2024)。然而，许多此类系统仍然针对单轮问答或指令遵循进行优化，这假设患者可以在一次提示中提供完整且准确的信息。这与真实的临床实践不同，在真实的临床实践中，医生在给出建议或形成诊断之前会反复询问后续问题。 AMIE 将诊断框架定义为对话式历史记录和推理. However, many such systems are still optimized for single-turn question answering or instruction following, which assumes that patients can provide complete and precise information in one prompt. This differs from real clinical practice, where doctors iteratively ask follow-up questions before giving advice or forming a diagnosis. AMIE frames diagnosis as conversational history-taking and reasoning(Tu et al., 2024)，而 DoctorAgent-RL 进一步将多轮临床对话建模为 RL 的自适应决策过程, while DoctorAgent-RL further models multi-turn clinical dialogue as an adaptive decision process with RL(Feng et al., 2025)。其他方法，例如扁鹊、T-Agent 和连续实体推理，明确地建模提问行为、医学术语流或跨对话轮次的实体转换. Other approaches, such as BianQue, T-Agent, and continuous entity reasoning, explicitly model questioning behavior, medical term flow, or entity transitions across dialogue turns(Chen et al., 2023; Hu et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025).

由于由于隐私和治理限制，真实的临床对话很难发布，因此一些研究探索了合成对话的生成。 NoteChat 根据临床记录生成患者与医生的对话Because real clinical conversations are difficult to release due to privacy and governance constraints, several studies have explored synthetic dialogue generation. NoteChat generates patient–physician conversations conditioned on clinical notes(Wang et al., 2024)，而 MDDial 使用基于模板的结构化诊断数据合成, while MDDial uses template-based synthesis from structured diagnostic data(Macherla et al., 2023)。此类工作显示了合成数据对于训练会话医疗系统的价值，但大多数现有数据集仍然是单语言、模板受限，或者没有设计为多语言并行语料库。. Such work shows the value of synthetic data for training conversational medical systems, but most existing datasets remain either single-language, template-constrained, or not designed as multilingual parallel corpora.

Recent evaluation work in medical dialogue also shows that success should not be measured only by final-answer accuracy, but also by questioning quality, safety, and turn-level clinical relevance(Macherla et al., 2023; Tu et al., 2024; Gong et al., 2026).

3 任务定义Task Definition

我们研究多语言多轮医疗对话生成的问题，其中对话代理与患者互动以收集症状并提供初步诊断指导。与单轮医疗问答不同，这项任务需要对连续的医患交互进行建模，其中诊断推理通过多次对话交流出现。We study the problem of multilingual multi-turn medical dialogue generation, where a conversational agent interacts with a patient to collect symptoms and provide preliminary diagnostic guidance. Unlike single-turn medical question answering, this task requires modeling sequential physician–patient interactions where diagnostic reasoning emerges through multiple conversational exchanges.

局限性Limitations

尽管取得了令人鼓舞的成果，但我们的工作仍存在一些局限性。首先，虽然Despite promising results, our work has several limitations. First, although the医疗援助对话MedAidDialog数据集结合了真实和合成的医学对话，合成数据仍可能引入偏差或简化模式，无法完全捕捉真实临床相互作用的复杂性。其次，我们的评估仅限于源自原始数据集的一组固定疾病，这限制了系统推广到更广泛的医疗状况的能力。第三，虽然我们通过翻译层整合了多语言交互，但底层对话模型主要用英语进行训练，这可能会导致微妙的翻译错误或在资源匮乏的语言中失去临床细微差别。最后，当前系统侧重于基于文本的对话，尚未纳入其他临床相关模式，例如医学图像、报告或实验室数据。dataset combines real and synthetic medical dialogues, synthetic data may still introduce biases or simplified patterns that do not fully capture the complexity of real clinical interactions. Second, our evaluation is limited to a fixed set of diseases derived from the original dataset, which restricts the system’s ability to generalize to a broader range of medical conditions. Third, while we incorporate multilingual interaction through a translation layer, the underlying dialogue model is trained primarily in English, which may lead to subtle translation errors or loss of clinical nuance in low-resource languages. Although we evaluate the system using both automatic metrics and expert medical review, errors in diagnosis or advice may still occur. We also acknowledge potential risks related to bias in synthetic data generation and language translation errors in multilingual settings. To mitigate these risks, we employ quality filtering for synthetic dialogues and conduct human expert evaluation to assess safety and clinical plausibility.
