摘要Abstract

视频大语言模型 (Video-LLM) 的最新进展在视频问答 (VideoQA) 方面取得了可喜的性能。然而，现有的基准主要是通过答案的正确性来评估的，而相关视频证据中的预测的基础在很大程度上仍未得到检验。答案生成和证据理解之间的这种脱节激发了构建Recent advances in Video Large Language Models (Video-LLMs) have yielded promising performance on video question answering (VideoQA). Nevertheless, existing benchmarks are predominantly evaluated through answer correctness, while the grounding of predictions in relevant video evidence remains largely unexamined. This disconnect between answer generation and evidence understanding motivates the construction of the循证视频问答基准 (EG-VQA)Evidence-Grounded Video Question Answering Benchmark (EG-VQA)，一种开放式评估协议，其中每个 QA 对都明确注释有支持时间证据，因此需要联合推理和精确的证据定位。 EG-VQA 由 2,067 个视频和 11,838 个 QA 对组成，并带有细粒度的证据注释。为了评估预测的证据，, an open-ended evaluation protocol in which each QA pair is explicitly annotated with supporting temporal evidence, thereby requiring joint reasoning and precise evidence localization. EG-VQA is comprised of 2,067 videos and 11,838 QA pairs with fine-grained evidence annotations. To evaluate predicted evidence,循证 F1 (EG-F1)Evidence-Grounded F1 (EG-F1)被引入作为一个统一的度量，其中联合测量时间对齐和与地面真实证据的语义一致性。实验评估表明，即使是强大的专有模型也难以准确地预测其预测，从而暴露了答案正确性和忠实证据定位之间的根本差异。 为了弥补这一差距，is introduced as a unified metric in which temporal alignment and semantic consistency against ground-truth evidence are jointly measured. Experimental evaluation reveals that even strong proprietary models struggle to accurately ground their predictions, exposing a fundamental discrepancy between answer correctness and faithful evidence localization. To bridge this gap,EG推理机EG-Reasoner，提出了一种经过显式监督训练的循证推理模型。开源模型实现了最先进的性能，其结果可与专有系统相媲美，尤其是在反事实问题等推理密集型任务上观察到明显的增益。这些发现表明，仅靠扩展不足以实现强大的视频理解，而结构化证据监督对于开发更可靠和可解释的 VideoQA 系统至关重要。, an evidence-grounded reasoning model trained with explicit supervision, is proposed. State-of-the-art performance is achieved among open-source models, with results competitive against proprietary systems, particularly pronounced gains are observed on reasoning-intensive tasks such as counterfactual questions. These findings demonstrate that scaling alone is insufficient for robust video understanding and that structured evidence supervision is essential for the development of more reliable and interpretable VideoQA systems.

1 简介Introduction

视频问答 (VideoQA) 是评估模型理解动态视觉内容和对时间事件进行推理的能力的关键测试平台Video Question Answering (VideoQA) serves as a critical testbed for evaluating a model’s capacity to comprehend dynamic visual content and reason over temporal events(Zhong et al., 2022; Lin and Liu, 2026; Jiang et al., 2025)。尽管大型多模态模型最近取得了进展，但仍然存在一个基本限制：当前的基准通常无法区分真正的视频理解和表面相关性的利用. Despite recent advances in large multimodal models, a fundamental limitation persists: current benchmarks often fail to distinguish between genuine video understanding and the exploitation of superficial correlations(Ko et al., 2023).

许多广泛使用的 VideoQA 数据集都是以多项选择格式构建的，其中模型从预定义选项中选择答案Many widely used VideoQA datasets are constructed in a multiple-choice format, wherein models select answers from predefined options(Tapaswi et al., 2016; Xiao et al., 2021; Wu and Yu, 2024; Fu et al., 2025)。尽管这种范式简化了评估，但它无意中引入了选项偏差，使模型依赖于语言捷径，而不是根据视觉证据进行预测. Although this paradigm simplifies evaluation, it inadvertently introduces option bias, allowing models to rely on linguistic shortcuts rather than grounding predictions in visual evidence(Liu et al., 2023b; Chen et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2023a)，如图, as shown in Figure1（一）。此外，这些基准通常缺乏细粒度的证据注释，因此很难验证相关视频片段是否支持给定的预测。 因此，模型经常产生缺乏可解释推理的黑盒答案(a). Furthermore, fine-grained evidence annotations are typically absent from these benchmarks, rendering it difficult to verify whether relevant video segments support a given prediction. Consequently, models frequently produce black-box answers devoid of interpretable reasoning(Liu et al., 2022)。即使在开放式环境中，评估通常也仅限于答案的正确性，而忽略了对正确颞区的关注. 3.1 数据管理和问题设计Data Curation and Question Design

EG-VQA 的基础是基于三个现有视频源、ActivityNet Captions 构建的The foundation of EG-VQA is built from three existing video sources, ActivityNet Captions(Caba Heilbron et al., 2015), 嗨休息, HiREST(Zala et al., 2023)和 YouCook2, and YouCook2(Zhou et al., 2018)，它提供跨不同视频域的相对高质量的时间分段事件注释，包括开放域活动、教学过程和以事件为中心的长格式视频。这些原始注释首先被标准化为统一格式，其中每个视频都表示为带有时间戳的片段的有序序列，并与简洁的文本事件描述配对。由于原始片段边界和描述有时会偏离实际视频内容，因此应用过滤协议来删除损坏或无法访问的剪辑，纠正未对齐的时间边界，并排除事件多样性较低的视频，否则会产生琐碎的问题。完整的过滤标准在附录中提供, which provide relatively high-quality temporally segmented event annotations across diverse video domains, including open-domain activities, instructional procedures, and long-form event-centric videos. These raw annotations are first standardized into a uniform format where every video is represented as an ordered sequence of timestamped segments paired with concise textual event descriptions. Because the original segment boundaries and descriptions occasionally deviate from the actual video content, a filtering protocol is applied to remove corrupted or inaccessible clips, correct misaligned temporal boundaries, and exclude videos with low event diversity that would otherwise yield trivial questions. The complete filtering criteria are provided in AppendixA.1。经过整理后，仍保留了 2,067 个高质量视频，其校正后的时间描述作为地面真相证据主干。. After curation, 2,067 high-quality videos remain, with their corrected temporal descriptions serving as the ground-truth evidence backbone.

EG-VQA 中的问题针对四个推理级别。描述性问题侧重于直接可观察的内容，例如动作、对象和状态。时间（Temp.）问题需要理解事件顺序和程序顺序。因果问题要求推断事件发生的原因或产生的后果。反事实（Cntrf.）问题评估推理特定事件发生变化的假设场景的能力。这些类别的复杂性不断上升，从表面感知发展到牢固地扎根于特定视频片段的多步骤推理。The questions in EG-VQA target four levels of reasoning. Descriptive(Desc.) questions focus on directly observable content, such as actions, objects, and states. Temporal(Temp.) questions require understanding event order and procedural sequences. Causal(Caus.) questions demand inferring why an event occurs or what consequences it produces. Counterfactual(Cntrf.) questions assess the ability to reason about hypothetical scenarios in which a specific event is altered. These categories ascend in complexity, progressing from surface perception to multi-step reasoning firmly grounded in specific video segments.

6 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 EG-VQA，这是一个基准，要求模型将其答案建立在明确的时间证据上。 此外，还提出了基于证据的 F1 (EG-F1) 度量来联合测量证据的时间对齐和语义一致性。 实验表明，当前的视频法学硕士（包括强大的专有模型）很难始终如一地做出预测，凸显了答案正确性和证据本地化之间的根本差距。 为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 EG-Reasoner，这是一种经过显式监督训练的循证推理模型，与专有系统相比，它在开源模型中实现了最先进的性能，并获得了具有竞争力的结果。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，纳入结构化证据监督对于可靠且可解释的视频理解至关重要，我们希望 EG-VQA 能够成为证据感知多模态推理未来研究的有用测试平台。This paper introduces EG-VQA, a benchmark that requires models to ground their answers in explicit temporal evidence. Additionally, the Evidence-Grounded F1 (EG-F1) metric is proposed to jointly measure temporal alignment and semantic consistency of evidence. Experiments show that current Video-LLMs, including strong proprietary models, struggle to consistently ground their predictions, highlighting a fundamental gap between answer correctness and evidence localization. To address this issue, we propose EG-Reasoner, an evidence-grounded reasoning model trained with explicit supervision, which achieves state-of-the-art performance among open-source models and competitive results compared to proprietary systems. Overall, our findings suggest that incorporating structured evidence supervision is essential for reliable and interpretable video understanding, and we hope EG-VQA can serve as a useful testbed for future research on evidence-aware multimodal reasoning.

A.1 过滤协议Filtering protocol

数据验证。Data Validation.

时间对齐细化。Temporal Alignment Refinement.

尽管源数据集提供时间分段注释，但原始片段边界和描述可能并不总是准确反映底层视频内容。为了解决这个问题，人类注释者被要求检查每个视频及其相应的片段，并在必要时细化时间边界。与实际视觉事件明显不一致的片段将被纠正或删除。Although the source datasets provide temporally segmented annotations, the original segment boundaries and descriptions may not always accurately reflect the underlying video content. To address this, human annotators are instructed to review each video and its corresponding segments, and to refine temporal boundaries when necessary. Segments that exhibit significant misalignment with the actual visual events are corrected or removed.

描述一致性检查。Description Consistency Check.

我们进一步检查片段描述和观察到的视频内容之间的一致性。不明确、不完整或与视觉证据不一致的描述将被修改或丢弃。具有严重噪声或不可靠元数据的样本被完全排除。We further examine the consistency between segment descriptions and the observed video content. Descriptions that are ambiguous, incomplete, or inconsistent with the visual evidence are revised or discarded. Samples with severely noisy or unreliable metadata are excluded entirely.

信息密度过滤。Informational Density Filtering.

为了确保生成的数据集支持重要的推理，我们删除了信息密度低的视频。具体来说，仅包含单个事件、最小时间变化或实体之间交互不足的视频会被过滤掉，因为它们往往会产生琐碎或纯粹描述性的问题。To ensure that the resulting dataset supports non-trivial reasoning, we remove videos with low informational density. Specifically, videos that contain only a single event, minimal temporal variation, or insufficient interaction between entities are filtered out, as they tend to yield trivial or purely descriptive questions.

人工验证。Human Verification.

所有细化步骤均由训练有素的人类注释者按照标准化审查协议进行。指示注释者在验证片段注释时优先考虑时间准确性和语义一致性。All refinement steps are conducted by trained human annotators following a standardized review protocol. Annotators are instructed to prioritize both temporal accuracy and semantic consistency when validating segment annotations.

应用上述过滤标准后，我们总共保留了 2,067 个高质量视频，作为构建 EG-VQA 基准的基础。After applying the above filtering criteria, we retain a total of 2,067 high-quality videos, which serve as the foundation for constructing the EG-VQA benchmark.

A.2 及时设计和迭代细化Prompt Design and Iterative Refinement

我们使用人机循环管道构建问答（QA）对，该管道将大型语言模型生成与迭代提示细化相结合。目标是产生基于时间注释视频内容的高质量、面向推理的问题。We construct question–answer (QA) pairs using a human-in-the-loop pipeline that combines large language model generation with iterative prompt refinement. The goal is to produce high-quality, reasoning-oriented questions that are grounded in temporally annotated video content.

初始提示设计。Initial Prompt Design.

给定时间分段的视频元数据，我们为每个推理类别（描述性、时间性、因果性和反事实）设计特定于类型的提示。每个提示都明确鼓励生成开放式问题，这些问题需要对一个或多个时间段进行推理，而不是依赖于肤浅的模式或静态描述。Given the temporally segmented video metadata, we design type-specific prompts for each reasoning category (descriptive, temporal, causal, and counterfactual). Each prompt explicitly encourages the generation of open-ended questions that require reasoning over one or multiple temporal segments, rather than relying on superficial patterns or static descriptions.

故障模式识别。Failure Mode Identification.

直接应用大型语言模型通常会产生次优的 QA 对。为了系统地识别常见问题，我们将初始提示应用于保留的视频子集并手动检查输出。最常见的故障模式包括：Direct application of large language models often results in suboptimal QA pairs. To systematically identify common issues, we apply the initial prompts to a held-out subset of videos and manually inspect the outputs. The most frequent failure modes include:

  • •

    与视频无关的问题：Video-independent questions:无需参考视频内容即可回答的问题。Questions that can be answered without referring to the video content.

  • •

    弱接地：Weak grounding:与时态元数据仅松散相关的问题或答案。Questions or answers that are only loosely related to the temporal metadata.

  • •

    不完全推理：Incomplete reasoning:省略必要的时间或上下文信息的答案。Answers that omit necessary temporal or contextual information.

迭代细化。Iterative Refinement.

基于这些观察，我们通过对基础、时间特异性和推理深度引入更严格的约束来迭代地完善提示。此过程遵循生成 - 检查 - 优化循环，其中提示被反复调整和重新评估，直到生成的 QA 对始终满足我们的质量标准。在此过程中，我们明确过滤模板化或基础薄弱的问题，以减少在没有真正视频理解的情况下可能被利用的潜在捷径模式。Based on these observations, we iteratively refine the prompts by introducing stricter constraints on grounding, temporal specificity, and reasoning depth. This process follows a generate–inspect–refine loop, where prompts are repeatedly adjusted and re-evaluated until the generated QA pairs consistently satisfy our quality criteria. During this process, we explicitly filter templated or weakly grounded questions to reduce potential shortcut patterns that could be exploited without genuine video understanding.

最终部署。Final Deployment.

一旦稳定下来，细化的提示将应用于整套过滤后的视频，以大规模生成候选 QA 对。这种迭代设计确保生成的数据集强调重要的、推理密集型的和基于证据的问题。Once stabilized, the refined prompts are applied to the full set of filtered videos to generate candidate QA pairs at scale. This iterative design ensures that the resulting dataset emphasizes non-trivial, reasoning-intensive, and evidence-grounded questions.

A.3 质量控制协议Quality Control Protocol

为了确保生成的 QA 对的可靠性，我们采用了两阶段质量控制协议，将结构化自动评估与人工验证相结合。To ensure the reliability of generated QA pairs, we employ a two-stage quality control protocol that combines structured automatic evaluation with human verification.

第一阶段：结构化多模型评估。Stage 1: Structured Multi-model Evaluation.

每个候选 QA 对均由来自不同架构系列的两个大型语言模型（DeepSeek-V3.2 和 Gemini-2.5-Flash）独立评估。我们采用由三个标准组成的结构化评分标准：Each candidate QA pair is independently evaluated by two large language models from distinct architectural families (DeepSeek-V3.2 and Gemini-2.5-Flash). We adopt a structured scoring rubric consisting of three criteria:

  • •

    问题合理性：Question Reasonableness:问题是否成立并需要视频理解。whether the question is well-posed and requires video understanding.

  • •

    答案正确性：Answer Correctness:答案是否与视频内容一致。whether the answer is consistent with the video content.

  • •

    证据对齐：Evidence Alignment:答案是否正确地基于相关的时间段。whether the answer is properly grounded in the relevant temporal segments.

每个维度按0-10分进行评分，将三个维度相加得到总分。如果总分大于或等于 25，则认为样本是可接受的。Each dimension is scored on a scale of 0–10, and the total score is obtained by summing the three dimensions. A sample is considered acceptable if the total score is greater than or equal to 25.

基于协议的过滤。Agreement-based Filtering.

我们采用双模型协议策略来提高稳健性。当两个模型都同意决策（接受或拒绝）时，直接采用结果。否则，样本将被标记为需要手动审核。We adopt a dual-model agreement strategy to improve robustness. When both models agree on the decision (accept or reject), the result is directly adopted. Otherwise, the sample is flagged for manual review.

第二阶段：人类裁决。Stage 2: Human Adjudication.

所有标记的样本均由人工注释者审查，他们根据相同的评估标准确定其有效性。注释者被要求优先考虑语义正确性和基础保真度。All flagged samples are reviewed by human annotators, who determine their validity based on the same evaluation criteria. Annotators are instructed to prioritize both semantic correctness and grounding fidelity.

事后验证。Post-hoc Validation.

数据集构建后，我们对 1,000 个 QA 对的随机采样子集执行额外的手动检查。超过 95% 的采样对被验证为有效，证明了质量控制协议的有效性。After dataset construction, we perform additional manual inspection on a randomly sampled subset of 1,000 QA pairs. More than 95% of the sampled pairs are verified as valid, demonstrating the effectiveness of the quality control protocol.

注释者间的一致性。Inter-annotator Consistency.

为了确保一致性，注释者之间的分歧通过讨论解决，并相应完善注释指南。To ensure consistency, disagreements among annotators are resolved through discussion, and annotation guidelines are refined accordingly.
