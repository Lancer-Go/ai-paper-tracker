从预测到协作：From Prediction to Collaboration:
交互式象征音乐分析Interactive Symbolic Music Analysis

摘要Abstract

自动符号音乐分析已经取得了实质性进展，但现有系统通常是针对单一使用模式（例如全乐谱预测）而设计的，因此与分析工作流程中出现的更广泛的操作（包括部分完成、局部校正和迭代细化）不匹配。因此，强大的基准模型和支持交互式分析使用的系统之间仍然存在差距。我们提出了一个统一的符号罗马数字（RN）分析框架，通过将强大的预测性能与对约束完成和修订的直接支持相结合来缩小这一差距。该方法旨在通过计算昂贵的预训练表示一次并在迭代细化期间重用它们来提供准确性和交互响应性之间的实际权衡，从而使强大的预训练模型更适合交互设置。它支持完整的分数分析、现有标签的有针对性的修订以及通过共享建模框架从部分上下文中推断缺失的注释。 Dilemmadata（同类中最大且最异构的基准）上的实验表明，所提出的方法是强大的 RN 分析基线，同时还支持部分标签的屏蔽补全。与用于多级候选检查和编辑的原型界面一起，这些结果将自动 RN 分析不仅定位为预测问题，而且还作为未来音乐分析交互式工具的基础。Automatic symbolic music analysis has made substantial progress, yet existing systems are typically designed for a single mode of use, such as full-score prediction, and therefore do not match the broader range of operations that arise in analysis workflows, including partial completion, local correction, and iterative refinement. As a result, there remains a gap between strong benchmark models and systems that can support interactive analytical use. We present a unified framework for symbolic Roman-numeral (RN) analysis that narrows this gap by combining strong predictive performance with direct support for constrained completion and revision. The method is designed to provide a practical trade-off between accuracy and interactive responsiveness by computing expensive pretrained representations once and reusing them during iterative refinement, making powerful pretrained models more amenable to interactive settings. It supports complete score analysis, targeted revision of existing labels, and inference of missing annotations from partial context through a shared modeling framework. Experiments on Dilemmadata, the largest and most heterogeneous benchmark of its kind, show that the proposed approach is a strong RN-analysis baseline while also supporting masked completion from partial labels. Together with a prototype interface for multi-level candidate inspection and editing, these results position automatic RN analysis not only as a prediction problem, but also as a foundation for future interactive tools for music analysis.

1 简介Introduction

罗马数字 (RN) 分析描述了与本地调相关的和弦和和声函数。它广泛用于西方调性音乐，也可以应用于一些调式和扩展调性曲目。自动 RN 分析仍然具有挑战性，因为和声标签高度依赖于上下文，并且必须在多个音乐层之间保持一致。尽管最近的系统提高了基准准确性，但它们仍然大多被视为一次性满分预测器。这种评估设置仅部分匹配音乐学实践，分析师反复检查不确定的段落，纠正局部错误，并从部分上下文中完成缺失的标签。因此，强大的 RN 预测器和真正支持分析师在环工作流程的工具之间仍然存在实际差距。Roman numeral (RN) analysis describes chords and harmonic functions relative to a local key. It is widely used for Western tonal music and can also be applied to some modal and extended-tonal repertoire. Automatic RN analysis remains challenging because harmonic labels are highly context-dependent and must stay coherent across multiple musical layers. While recent systems have improved benchmark accuracy, they are still mostly framed as one-shot full-score predictors. This evaluation setting only partially matches musicological practice, where analysts iteratively inspect uncertain passages, correct local mistakes, and complete missing labels from partial context. As a result, there is still a practical gap between strong RN predictors and tools that genuinely support analyst-in-the-loop workflows.

正在进行的研究沿着两个互补的方向取得进展。预训练符号编码器，特别是 MusicBERT 及其以 RN 为中心的改编版本 RNbert，提供丰富的上下文表示和强大的 RN 性能Ongoing research has progressed along two complementary directions. Pretrained symbolic encoders, especially MusicBERT and its RN-focused adaptation RNBert, provide rich contextual representations and strong RN performance[20, 17]。同时，基于图的方法（例如 ChordGNN 和 AnalysisGNN）可以对音乐事件之间的显式关系结构和功能依赖关系进行建模. In parallel, graph-based approaches such as ChordGNN and AnalysisGNN model explicit relational structure and functional dependencies across musical events[9, 8]。这些优势自然是协同作用的：序列预训练捕获广泛的音乐背景，而图形推理支持跨音符、开始和节拍级别结构的连贯决策。受这种互补性的推动，我们构建了一个混合骨干网，将 MusicBERT 嵌入与 AnalysisGNN 风格的图形模块结合起来，并将其用作涵盖盲 RN 分析、部分标签完成和交互式修订的分析师在环工作流程的预测引擎。. These strengths are naturally synergistic: sequence pretraining captures broad musical context, while graph reasoning supports coherent decisions across note-, onset-, and beat-level structures. Motivated by this complementarity, we build a hybrid backbone that couples MusicBERT embeddings with an AnalysisGNN-style graph module, and use it as the prediction engine for an analyst-in-the-loop workflow that spans blind RN analysis, partial-label completion, and interactive revision.

我们的模型通过共享图形表示集成了多个粒度（音符、开始和节拍）的信息。细粒度预测是在音符级别执行的，包括音符级别功能角色的预测。对于更粗略的音乐决策，我们在开始和节拍级别引入事后投票器，聚合选定的任务，例如完整的罗马数字标签、节奏和短语相关结构。这种设计保留了局部细节，同时实现了更高级别的谐波决策，这对于满分推理和下游编辑工作流程非常有用。Our model integrates information at multiple granularities (note, onset, and beat) through a shared graph representation. Fine-grained prediction is performed at the note level, including prediction of note-level functional roles. For coarser musical decisions, we introduce post-hoc voters at onset and beat levels that aggregate selected tasks such as full Roman numeral labels, cadence, and phrase-related structure. This design preserves local detail while enabling higher-level harmonic decisions that are useful for full-score inference and for downstream editing workflows.

我们在 Dilemmadata 上进行评估We evaluate on Dilemmadata[5]，一个新的 RN 分析基准，结合了 AugmentedNet 数据集, a new RN analysis benchmark that combines the AugmentedNet dataset[16]和远程听力语料库and the Distant Listening corpus[7]进入更大、更异构的评估环境。除了盲法满分分析之外，我们还研究了部分标签完成和迭代校正的屏蔽预测。总之，这些功能不仅将 RN 分析定位为预测任务，而且还作为研究人工编辑和部分分析环境下模型行为的设置。into a larger and more heterogeneous evaluation setting. In addition to blind full-score analysis, we study masked prediction for partial-label completion and iterative correction. Together, these capabilities position RN analysis not only as a prediction task, but also as a setting for studying model behavior under human edits and partial analytical context.

2 相关工作Related Work

先前有关自动罗马数字分析的工作主要集中在改进盲目满分预测。早期系统使用循环和多任务公式Prior work on automatic Roman numeral analysis has largely focused on improving blind full-score prediction. Early systems used recurrent and multi-task formulations[1, 14]，而后来的基于注意力的模型改进了对较长范围谐波上下文的处理, while later attention-based models improved the handling of longer-range harmonic context[2]。 AugmentedNet 通过将多任务音调预测与跨多个语料库的合成数据增强相结合，进一步加强了这一工作，表明冗余分析任务可以改善预测. AugmentedNet further strengthened this line of work by combining multi-task tonal prediction with synthetic data augmentation across multiple corpora, showing that redundant analysis tasks improve prediction[16]。这些系统建立了强大的自动基线，但它们主要被评估为一次性预测器，而不是迭代分析工作流程的工具。. These systems establish strong automatic baselines, but they are primarily evaluated as one-shot predictors rather than as tools for iterative analytical workflows.

第二个工作重点是谐波一致性和结构化输出依赖性。米奇A second line of work focuses on harmonic coherence and structured output dependencies. Micchi等人。et al.介绍introduced青蛙frog，它使用自回归输出层来强制谐波子标签之间的一致性, which uses an autoregressive output layer to enforce consistency among harmonic sub-labels[15]。他们的公式基于神经自回归分布估计（NADE）. Their formulation is based on Neural Autoregressive Distribution Estimation (NADE)[12]，适应象征性和谐造型。对标签一致性的强调与我们的设置直接相关，因为交互式校正和部分完成不仅需要局部准确性，还需要相关谐波决策之间的兼容性。, adapted to symbolic harmony modeling. This emphasis on label consistency is directly relevant to our setting, because interactive correction and partial completion require not only local accuracy but also compatibility among related harmonic decisions.

最近的工作还扩展了可用于 RN 分析的表征和架构工具包。 ChordGNN 直接在音符级别对乐谱结构进行建模，并通过图神经网络预测起始谐波标签Recent work has also expanded the representational and architectural toolkit available for RN analysis. ChordGNN models score structure directly at the note level and predicts onset-wise harmonic labels via graph neural networks[9]，而 AnalysisGNN 通过基于图形的架构将这个方向扩展到统一的多任务音乐分析（每个音符 20 个不同的属性）, while AnalysisGNN extends this direction toward unified multi-task music analysis (20 distinct properties for each note) with graph-based architectures[8]。与此同时，自监督预训练的影响力越来越大：MusicBERT 提供了强大的符号表示. In parallel, self-supervised pretraining has become increasingly influential: MusicBERT provides strong symbolic representations[20]，RNbert 表明微调这些表示可以显着提高 RN 分析的准确性, and RNBert shows that fine-tuning these representations can substantially improve RN-analysis accuracy[17]。最近的一个补充方向是通过引入基于多线性专家混合的可解释的 RNBERT 变体，更直接地针对音乐学应用. A complementary recent direction targets musicological applications more directly by introducing an interpretable RNBERT variant based on multilinear mixture-of-experts[18]。我们的工作直接建立在这两条链的基础上，将预训练的符号编码器与基于图的关系推理结合在一个框架中。. Our work builds directly on these two strands by combining pretrained symbolic encoders with graph-based relational reasoning in a single framework.

最后，更广泛、更多样化的语料库实现了更强大、更现实的评估设置，包括 When in RomeFinally, broader and more diverse corpora have enabled stronger and more realistic evaluation settings, including When in Rome[4]和远程听力语料库and the Distant Listening Corpus[7]。我们在此进展的基础上，同时针对最近 RN 工作中较少强调的用例：支持盲预测、部分标签约束完成以及迭代分析循环修订的单一系统。. We build on this progress while targeting a use case that remains less emphasized in recent RN work: a single system that supports blind prediction, constrained completion from partial labels, and iterative analyst-in-the-loop revision.

3 方法论Methodology

我们的目标是在一个框架内支持三种互补的分析模式：（i）完整分数推理，（ii）现有标签的本地修订，以及（iii）完成部分注释的段落。我们的系统将基于预训练表示的受 RNbert 启发的序列组件与 AnalysisGNN 风格的多任务图模型相结合Our goal is to support three complementary analysis modes within one framework: (i) complete-score inference, (ii) local revision of existing labels, and (iii) completion of partially annotated passages. Our system combines an RNBert-inspired sequence component based on pretrained representations with an AnalysisGNN-style multi-task graph model[17, 8].

3.1 问题表述Problem Formulation

给定一个象征性的分数Given a symbolic scoreSS，我们预测罗马数字组件和相关结构任务的音符级标签。遵循常见的 RN 分解, we predict note-level labels for Roman-numeral components and related structural tasks. Following common RN decompositions[16, 6, 9, 17]，我们将和声分析构建为对本地调、音阶程度、转位、和弦质量、完整罗马数字以及节奏、乐句、部分、踏板点、根音、低音、每个音符的功能程度、非和弦音状态等辅助标签的多任务分类，总共20个分析任务。音符级别的预测是主要的模型输出；随后由聚合模块计算起始、节拍和潜在测量级别的减少。, we frame harmonic analysis as multi-task classification over local key, scale degree, inversion, chord quality, complete Roman numeral, and auxiliary labels such as cadence, phrase, section, pedal-points, root, bass, functional degree of every note, and non-chord-tone status, with a total of 20 analysis tasks. Note-level predictions are the main model output; onset-, beat-, and potentially measure-level reductions are computed afterwards by aggregation modules.

3.2 完整的推理模型Complete Inference Model

完整分析模型是一个基于符号音符图的多任务图神经网络，并通过我们的 REMI 标记化 MusicBERT 变体的音符表示进行了增强The complete-analysis model is a multi-task graph neural network over symbolic note graphs, augmented with note representations from our REMI-tokenized MusicBERT variant[11]。在序列分支中，每个乐谱都使用 REMI 风格的词汇进行标记，并通过预先计算的标记到音符映射与音符对齐。对于长乐谱，标记序列被分成与模型的最大位置长度匹配的块，并且将所得的上下文状态汇集到音符级别。. In the sequence branch, each score is tokenized with a REMI-style vocabulary and aligned to notes through precomputed token-to-note maps. For long scores, the token sequence is divided into chunks matching the model’s maximum positional length, and the resulting contextual states are pooled to note level.

图分支对音符级特征进行操作，包括音高拼写和调号嵌入，以及编码同时性、时间连续性、重叠和休息关系的边缘The graph branch operates on note-level features, including pitch spelling and key-signature embeddings, together with edges encoding simultaneity, temporal succession, overlap, and rest relations[10]。该模型实例化节拍和测量节点，支持多个音乐级别的分层聚合和预测。 MusicBERT 音符嵌入在图形编码之前与符号音符特征连接，并由特定于任务的分类头进行解码，这些分类头预测 RN 组件以及其他音符级任务，如节奏、短语、部分、根音、低音和非和弦音标签。. The model instantiates beat and measure nodes, which support hierarchical aggregation and prediction at multiple musical levels. MusicBERT note embeddings are concatenated with the symbolic note features before graph encoding, and decoded by task-specific classification heads that predict RN components along with other note-level tasks such as cadence, phrase, section, root, bass, and non-chord-tone labels.

3.3 蒙版编辑条件模型Masked Edit-Conditioned Model

交互式完成和修订由专门用于屏蔽预测的模型处理。该模型是从全推理模型中提炼出来的，并通过标签调节模块进行了扩展。对于选定的任务子集，在消息传递之前，将已知标签嵌入并注入到编码器中作为音符级条件偏差。未知位置接受屏蔽嵌入，而已知标签在推理时通过硬约束强制执行。Interactive completion and revision are handled by a model specialized for masked prediction. This model is distilled from the full-inference model and extended with label-conditioning modules. For a selected subset of tasks, known labels are embedded and injected into the encoder as a note-level conditioning bias before message passing. Unknown positions receive a masked embedding, while known labels are enforced through hard constraints at inference time.

训练使用显式屏蔽任务选择以及结合随机节点屏蔽和起始跨度屏蔽的混合屏蔽策略。对屏蔽任务的监督集中在目标节点上，而已知的上下文节点充当条件证据。这种设计使得屏蔽检查点适合部分完成和分析师在环修订，而不是作为盲目满分推理的直接替代。Training uses explicit masked task selection together with a hybrid masking policy that combines random node masking and onset-span masking. Supervision for masked tasks is concentrated on target nodes, while known context nodes act as conditioning evidence. This design makes the masked checkpoint suitable for partial completion and analyst-in-the-loop revision rather than as a direct replacement for blind full-score inference.

3.4 优化与培训Optimization and Training

基本训练目标是多任务交叉熵，在所有监督任务上进行标签平滑，并结合特征正则化。对于屏蔽模型，从全推理模型构建的冻结教师提供保留损失：（i）保留任务或非目标节点上的 KL 蒸馏，（ii）通过学生和教师笔记表示之间的均方误差进行特征锚定，以及（iii）L2-SP 正则化以惩罚初始化漂移。训练循环混合了屏蔽和未屏蔽批次，以便模型学习完成行为，而不会破坏完整分析性能。The base training objective is multi-task cross-entropy with label smoothing across all supervised tasks, combined with feature regularization. For the masked model, a frozen teacher constructed from the full-inference model supplies preservation losses: (i) KL distillation on preserved tasks or non-target nodes, (ii) feature anchoring via mean-squared error between student and teacher note representations, and (iii) L2-SP regularization to penalize drift from initialization. The training loop mixes masked and unmasked batches so that the model learns completion behavior without collapsing full-analysis performance.

4 实验Experiments

我们评估四种制度。首先，我们与之前的模型和不同的模型设置相比，测量了完整的推理性能。其次，我们使用编辑条件模型评估屏蔽完成，其中部分分析是已知的，并且模型通过显式已知标签保留来预测丢失的标签。第三，我们评估不同的优化和微调场景以提高性能。最后，我们研究事后解码配置，包括起始/节拍投票器、迭代细化和起始级波束搜索。We evaluate four regimes. First, we measure full inference performance compared to previous models and different model settings. Second, we evaluate masked completion with our edit-conditioned model, where part of the analysis is known and the model predicts the missing labels with explicit known-label preservation. Third, we evaluate different optimization and finetuning scenarios for boosting performance. Finally, we study post-hoc decoding configurations, including onset/beat voters, iterative refinement, and onset-level beam search.

这些制度不能直接互换。完全推理是对整个分数的盲目预测，而屏蔽完成使用已知的上下文标签，因此是一个不同的评估问题。因此，我们单独报告屏蔽模型指标，并且不会将它们当作来自同一设置的完整推理数进行比较。These regimes are not directly interchangeable. Full inference is blind prediction over the whole score, whereas masked completion uses known contextual labels and is therefore a different evaluation problem. We consequently report masked-model metrics separately and do not compare them as if they were full-inference numbers from the same setting.

在这种情况下，一个反复出现的问题是需要平滑相关任务之间的矛盾预测。我们的模型预测每个音符大约 20 个标签，其中许多标签是部分冗余的，或者是在更高的时间级别（例如开头或片段）更自然地定义的。因此，同一开头内的音符有时会收到不一致的标签。为了解决这个问题，我们研究了几种聚合策略，包括均值池和学习投票。时间平滑性是另一个挑战：因为预测是在细粒度上进行的，所以固定跨度可能会表现出虚假的局部标签变化。因此，我们还使用基于置信度的锁定、波束搜索和相关事后策略来评估迭代屏蔽预测。One recurring issue in this setting is the need to smooth contradictory predictions across related tasks. Our model predicts around 20 labels per note, many of which are partially redundant or are more naturally defined at higher temporal levels such as onsets or segments. As a result, notes within the same onset can occasionally receive inconsistent labels. To address this, we investigate several aggregation strategies, including mean pooling and learned voting. Temporal smoothness is another challenge: because predictions are made at fine granularity, stationary spans can exhibit spurious local label changes. We therefore also evaluate iterative masked prediction with confidence-based locking, beam search, and related post-hoc strategies.

6 讨论Discussion

让神经 RN 分析在实践中有用的一个主要障碍是结构性的，而不是基于建模的：符号分数以一组注释的形式呈现给基于序列、图形和切片的网络，并且分析任务被训练和评估为每个注释每个任务一个标签。然而，分析师不会孤立地对票据进行推理。它们使用放置在变化点处的和声标签，范围为节拍、小节或较长的片段。因此，RNHybrid 和相关系统产生的信息粒度比自然写入和读取的分析标签更细。解决这一差距，而不仅仅是提高音符级别的准确性，是这项工作的主要动机之一。A central obstacle to making neural RN analysis useful in practice is structural rather than modelling-based: symbolic scores are presented to sequence, graph, and slice-based networks alike as sets of notes, and analysis tasks are trained and evaluated as one label per task per note. Analysts, however, do not reason about notes in isolation; they work with harmonic labels placed at change-points and scoped to beats, measures, or longer segments. RNHybrid and related systems therefore produce information at a finer granularity than analytical labels are naturally written and read. Adressing this gap, rather than only improving note-level accuracy, is one of the main motivations of this work.

我们通过罗马数字组件上的多级聚合层来解决输出端的这一差距。每个音符的任务概率被组合成前 3 个罗马数字候选值，每个候选值都具有明确的可能性，分为四个级别：每个音符、每个独特的开始、每个节拍和每个小节。单击Verovio界面中的注释（图We address this gap on the output side with a multi-level aggregation layer over Roman-numeral components. Per-note task probabilities are combined into top-3 Roman-numeral candidates, each with an explicit likelihood, at four levels: per note, per unique onset, per beat, and per measure. 这个框架还强调了度量驱动的评估往往隐藏的符号分析的一个特征：一些重要的段落允许多种可辩护的分析。不同的分析人员可以合理地选择不同的调制点、模糊发声中的不同和弦根音以及同一音乐的不同分段，因此针对单个参考的二进制准确性充其量只是实际实用性的粗略代理。因此，本文的目标不是要求完成以用户为中心的分析师利益验证，而是建立支持此类工作流程所需的建模和系统组件：强盲预测、部分标签的可控完成以及用于检查和修改模型建议的界面。This framing also highlights a feature of symbolic analysis that metric-driven evaluation tends to hide: some non-trivial passages admit multiple defensible analyses. Different analysts may legitimately choose different modulation points, different chord roots in ambiguous voicings, and different segmentations of the same music, so that binary accuracy against a single reference is at best a coarse proxy for practical utility. The goal of the present paper is therefore not to claim a completed user-centered validation of analyst benefit, but to establish the modeling and system components needed to support such workflows: strong blind prediction, controllable completion from partial labels, and an interface for inspecting and revising model suggestions.

7 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

我们提出了一个框架，将符号 RN 分析重塑为预测到分析的连续体：一个混合 MusicBERT +  图主干，使盲满分推理与 Dilemmadata 上强大的已发布基线竞争，并与编辑条件屏蔽检查点和基于 Verovio 的界面配对，将每个音符的概率转换为最高值We presented a framework that recasts symbolic RN analysis as a prediction-to-analysis continuum: a hybrid MusicBERT + graph backbone that makes blind full-score inference competitive with strong published baselines on Dilemmadata, paired with an edit-conditioned masked checkpoint and a Verovio-based interface that turns per-note probabilities into top-kk音符、开始、节拍和小节级别的罗马数字候选。除了强盲预测之外，该框架还支持部分标签的约束补全，并且随着提供更多上下文标签，屏蔽补全单调改进。Roman-numeral candidates at note, onset, beat, and measure level. Beyond strong blind prediction, the framework supports constrained completion from partial labels, and masked completion improves monotonically as more contextual labels are provided.

总而言之，这些结果表明自动 RN 分析不仅可以开发为基准预测任务，还可以作为未来交互式分析工具的技术基础。在本文中，我们评估此类使用所需的建模功能，包括盲推理、部分标签完成以及接口支持的检查和修订，而不是完整的面向分析师的工作流程的最终用户利益。Taken together, these results suggest that automatic RN analysis can be developed not only as a benchmark prediction task, but also as a technical foundation for future interactive analysis tools. In this paper, we evaluate the modeling capabilities required for such use, including blind inference, partial-label completion, and interface-supported inspection and revision, rather than the end-user benefits of a complete analyst-facing workflow.

未来的工作将通过与音乐学家的研究来量化这种设置的实际价值，重点关注注释速度、纠正行为以及在实际分析工作中感知的有用性。我们还计划改进用于编辑条件解码的推理时间控制模块，添加不确定性估计，并评估更广泛的库中的传输。Future work will quantify the practical value of this setup through studies with musicologists, focusing on annotation speed, correction behavior, and perceived usefulness during real analytical work. We also plan to improve the inference-time control modules for edit-conditioned decoding, add uncertainty estimates, and evaluate transfer across broader repertoires.
