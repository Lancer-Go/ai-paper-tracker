摘要Abstract

梯度下降反向传播是机器学习中大多数神经网络架构采用的常见优化策略。然而，事实证明，找到指导训练的最佳超参数具有挑战性。尽管人们普遍认为选择适当的参数对于避免过度拟合和实现公正的结果至关重要，但这种选择仍然很大程度上基于实证实验和经验。本文提出了一种新的学习率概率框架，学习率是随机梯度下降的关键参数。该框架将经典贝叶斯统计发展为涉及两个对抗贝叶斯过程的双贝叶斯决策机制。理论上的最佳学习率可以从这两个过程中得出并用于随机梯度下降。各种分类、分割和检测任务的实验证实了理论上推导的学习率的实际意义。本文还讨论了所提出的双贝叶斯框架对网络训练和模型性能的影响。Backpropagation with gradient descent is a common optimization strategy employed by most neural network architectures in machine learning. However, finding optimal hyperparameters to guide training has proven challenging. While it is widely acknowledged that selecting appropriate parameters is crucial for avoiding overfitting and achieving unbiased outcomes, this choice remains largely based on empirical experiments and experience. This paper presents a new probabilistic framework for the learning rate, a key parameter in stochastic gradient descent. The framework develops classic Bayesian statistics into a double-Bayesian decision mechanism involving two antagonistic Bayesian processes. A theoretically optimal learning rate can be derived from these two processes and used for stochastic gradient descent. Experiments across various classification, segmentation, and detection tasks corroborate the practical significance of the theoretically derived learning rate. The paper also discusses the ramifications of the proposed double-Bayesian framework for network training and model performance.

本文提出了一种双贝叶斯学习框架，从中导出使用 SGD 进行神经网络训练的最佳学习率（大约This paper proposes a double-Bayesian learning framework from which the optimal learning rate for neural network training with SGD is derived (approx.0.0160.016）。广泛的网格搜索表明，派生的学习率和接近它的学习率在不同的分类、分割和对象检测任务中表现更好。无论训练集大小如何，SGD 在这些任务中的表现始终优于 Adam，只有一项实验除外。 Adam 在非常小的学习率下表现最好，这与文献一致。动量对 Adam 没有显着影响，而较高的动量值 (0.4-0.8+) 会导致 SGD 获得更好的结果。此外，SGD 在噪声条件下始终优于 Adam。噪声水平越高，性能差距就越大，这表明 SGD 对噪声的鲁棒性更强。 Adam 在某些情况下实现了比 SGD 更低的验证损失，但未能提供更好的测试性能，表明存在过度拟合的趋势。虽然 SGD 实现了最佳的整体性能，但 Adam 更快地达到了最小验证集损失，这可能解释了它的如此受欢迎。). An extensive grid search showed that the derived learning rate and rates close to it performed better across different classification, segmentation, and object detection tasks. SGD consistently outperformed Adam across these tasks, regardless of training set size, except for one experiment. Adam performed best with very small learning rates, which is consistent with the literature. Momentum did not significantly affect Adam, whereas higher momentum values (0.4-0.8+) led to better results with SGD. Furthermore, SGD consistently outperformed Adam under noisy conditions. The performance gap widened at higher noise levels, indicating a greater robustness to noise by SGD. Adam achieved lower validation loss than SGD in several cases but failed to deliver better test performance, indicating a tendency toward overfitting. While SGD achieved the best overall performance, Adam reached its minimum validation set loss more quickly, which may explain its high popularity.

所提出的双贝叶斯框架是一种新的决策方法。通过将决策过程描述为两个贝叶斯过程的组合，可以以新的方式回答分类器或预测器何时最佳的问题。知识分布在这两个过程中，不可能同时获取它们的结果。强加的不确定性和测量限制让人想起其他领域的类似现象，例如物理学中的海森堡不确定性原理。还值得一提的是，黄金比例在这种信息论方法中发挥着重要作用。它定义了一种平衡，其中所涉及的两个过程之一的不确定性最小化。The proposed double-Bayesian framework is a new approach to decision-making. The question of when a classifier or predictor is optimal can be answered in new ways by describing a decision process as a combination of two Bayesian processes. Knowledge is distributed across these two processes, and it is impossible to access their outcomes simultaneously. The imposed uncertainty and measurement limitations are reminiscent of similar phenomena in other areas, such as Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle in physics. It is also worth mentioning that the golden ratio plays a prominent role in this information-theoretical approach. It defines an equilibrium in which the uncertainty of one of the two processes involved is minimized.

总之，双贝叶斯方法为学习和神经网络训练提供了新的见解。它为 SGD 中的动量项赋予了新的含义，并解释了为什么它的使用是有益的，这超出了对其作为平滑项的传统理解。导出的学习率将产生偏差较小的模型，从而提高其可解释性和可解释性。In summary, the double-Bayesian approach offers new insights into learning and neural network training. It gives new meaning to the momentum term in SGD and explains why its use is beneficial, which extends beyond the traditional understanding of it as a smoothing term.
