1 简介Introduction

强化学习（RL）代理已成功应用于无法获得足够完整和准确的系统模型的具有挑战性的自主决策任务。他们在机器人控制等领域表现出了强劲的表现Reinforcement learning (RL) agents have been successfully applied to challenging autonomous decision-making tasks where sufficiently complete and accurate system models are not available. They have shown strong performance in domains such as robotic control[22]、自动驾驶, autonomous driving[30]，以及无人机控制, and drone control[49]。然而，由于 RL 代理部署在现实环境中，它们必须应对在模拟环境中通常不存在或简化的实际挑战，它们通常是在模拟环境中开发和测试的. However, as RL agents are deployed in real-world settings, they must contend with practical challenges that are often absent or simplified in simulation environments, where they are typically developed and tested[29]。两个特别重要的挑战是处理非平稳性. Two particularly important challenges are handling non-stationarity[54]并满足运行过程中的安全约束and satisfying safety constraints during operation[19]。许多真实环境都是非静态的，这意味着它们的动态会随着时间的推移而变化。在这种环境中运行的强化学习代理必须快速适应这些变化，同时还要保留以前遇到的情况的知识，以防它们再次发生。同时，真实的系统在学习和部署过程中必须满足安全约束。因此，即使智能体适应不断变化的动态，也必须保持安全。. Many real environments are non-stationary, meaning their dynamics change over time. RL agents operating in such environments must rapidly adapt to these changes, while also retaining knowledge of previously encountered conditions in case they recur. At the same time, real systems must meet safety constraints during both learning and deployment. Thus, an agent must remain safe even as it adapts to changing dynamics.

作为一个激励人心的例子，考虑执行长期太空任务的火星探测器。尽管强化学习代理可以在接近火星的条件下进行训练，但由于看不见的地形、天气变化和漫游车组件的逐渐退化，真实的环境会有所不同。因此，强化学习智能体必须适应不断变化的非平稳动态。此外，如果漫游车重新访问之前遇到的地形，智能体不需要重新学习如何在之前遇到的条件下有效地采取行动。同时，特工的行为必须符合安全限制，因为损坏漫游车可能会危及整个任务。许多长期的现实世界部署中都出现了类似的要求，这使得这成为现实世界控制的核心问题。As a motivating example, consider a Mars rover on a long-duration space mission. Although an RL agent can be trained under conditions that approximate Mars, the true environment will differ due to unseen terrain, weather variability, and the gradual degradation of rover components. An RL agent must, therefore, adapt to evolving, non-stationary dynamics. Moreover, if the rover revisits previously encountered terrain, the agent should not need to relearn how to act effectively in those previously encountered conditions. Simultaneously, the agent’s behavior must comply with safety constraints, as damaging the rover could jeopardize the entire mission. Similar requirements arise in many long-horizon real-world deployments, making this a central problem for real-world control.

3 定义安全持续强化学习Defining Safe Continual Reinforcement Learning

在正式定义安全的持续强化学习问题之前，我们先澄清我们对问题性质的关注点和假设。Before formally defining a safe continual reinforcement learning problem, we clarify our focus and assumptions about the nature of the problem.

  • 1.

    离散的、戏剧性的任务变化。Discrete, dramatic task changes.我们假设环境变化作为离散任务发生。虽然真正的非平稳性可能是渐进和微妙的，但我们感兴趣的是更具挑战性的情况，涉及动态或条件的突然、实质性变化。We assume environmental changes occur as discrete tasks. While real non-stationarity can be gradual and subtle, our interest is in more challenging cases involving abrupt, substantial changes in dynamics or conditions.

  • 2.

    已知的任务边界。Known task boundaries.我们假设任务更改对于代理来说是已知的（即给出了任务边界）。如果没有这些信息，持续的强化学习性能将受到单独任务识别机制的质量的限制。我们的目标是评估连续强化学习方法本身，而不是任务推理，并且考虑到我们考虑的戏剧性任务转变，这个假设是合理的。We assume task changes are known to the agent (i.e., task boundaries are given). Without this information, continual RL performance would be limited by the quality of a separate task identification mechanism. Our goal is to evaluate continual RL methods themselves rather than task inference, and given the dramatic task shifts we consider, this assumption is reasonable.

  • 3.

    软安全约束。Soft safety constraints.我们将安全建模为软约束，这意味着安全是在期望中强制执行的（或通过平均惩罚/约束满足），而不是作为永远不能违反的硬性要求。这使我们能够研究安全性和持续适应之间的权衡。相比之下，严格的安全约束要求智能体永远不会违反安全，这使得“永远不会忘记”安全关键行为至关重要，并对持续学习提出了严格要求。We model safety as a soft constraint, meaning safety is enforced in expectation (or via penalties/constraint satisfaction on average) rather than as a hard requirement that must never be violated. This allows us to study the trade-off between safety and continual adaptation. In contrast, hard safety constraints would require the agent to never violate safety, making “never forgetting” safety-critical behavior essential and placing strict demands on continual learning.

7 讨论和开放问题Discussion and Open Questions

我们讨论了实证研究的意义、悬而未决的问题和未来的工作以及本研究的局限性。We discuss the implications of our empirical studies, open questions and future work, and limitations of this study.

7.1 影响Implications

当前的方法无法解决安全连续强化学习问题。Current approaches do not solve the safe continual RL problem.我们的实验表明，现有的安全强化学习和持续强化学习方法并不能充分解决安全持续强化学习问题。动态的突然变化以及奖励最大化和安全约束之间的冲突使得设置特别具有挑战性，并且可能需要专门的方法。虽然我们的安全 EWC 方法有效地平衡了安全性与灾难性遗忘，但其奖励塑造机制降低了安全连续世界环境中任务的成功率，这是该环境中安全性与性能之间难以平衡的结果。同时，我们的CF-EWC方法增加了奖励并减少了灾难性遗忘，但没有有效维持安全约束。未来的方法应该进一步减少灾难性遗忘，促进非平稳环境中的积极前向和后向转移，同时满足关键的安全约束。Our experiments indicate that existing safe RL and continual RL methods do not adequately address safe continual reinforcement learning. Sudden changes in dynamics and conflicts between reward maximization and safety constraints make the setting particularly challenging and likely require specialized methods. While our Safe EWC method effectively balances safety with catastrophic forgetting, its reward shaping mechanism reduced task success in the Safe Continual World environment, a consequence of the difficult balance between safety and performance in this environment. At the same time, our CF-EWC approach increased reward and reduced catastrophic forgetting, but did not effectively maintain safety constraints. Future approaches should further reduce catastrophic forgetting and promote positive forward and backward transfer in non-stationary environments, while simultaneously satisfying critical safety constraints.

未来的方法应该明确保留安全约束。Future methods should explicitly retain safety constraints.尽管持续强化学习技术主要减轻奖励遗忘，但我们观察到，即使安全相关状态保持不变，任务变化也会增加成本（图Although continual RL techniques primarily mitigate forgetting of reward, we observe that task changes can increase cost even when safety-relevant states remain unchanged (Figure5）。与安全持续强化学习的制定相一致，未来的工作应该开发明确保留跨任务安全约束的机制。). Consistent with the safe continual RL formulation, future work should develop mechanisms that explicitly preserve safety constraints across tasks.

目标之间的紧张关系必须得到解决。The tension among objectives must be resolved.安全的持续强化学习需要平衡任务性能、持续学习和安全性，并且适当的权衡取决于应用程序。这种平衡可以通过直接优化安全的连续强化学习目标来改善，类似于约束策略优化中的拉格朗日公式。或者，方法可以结合安全强化学习和连续强化学习的机制，例如使用 EWC 来保留对非平稳设置中的安全重要的参数，并使用重放缓冲区来演练安全关键状态。将安全强化学习算法扩展到连续设置（例如任务非平稳性下的拉格朗日 PPO）是另一个有前途的方向。Safe continual RL requires balancing task performance, continual learning, and safety, and the appropriate trade-off is application dependent. This balance may be improved by directly optimizing a safe continual RL objective, analogous to Lagrangian formulations in constrained policy optimization. Alternatively, methods may combine mechanisms from safe RL and continual RL, such as using EWC to preserve parameters important for safety in non-stationary settings and replay buffers to rehearse safety-critical states. Extending safe RL algorithms to continual settings (e.g., Lagrangian PPO under task non-stationarity) is another promising direction.

7.2 悬而未决的问题和未来的工作Open Questions and Future Work

我们的研究确定了几个悬而未决的问题，这些问题激发了安全持续强化学习的未来研究：Our study identifies several open questions that motivate future research in safe continual reinforcement learning:

什么构成了适当的安全持续 RL 基准？What constitutes an appropriate safe continual RL benchmark?选择合适的基准并非易事。在许多标准 RL 环境（例如 CartPole）中，适应新的动态相对简单，因此很难有意义地评估持续学习。相反，将非平稳性与安全约束（例如，安全连续世界）相结合可能会使问题变得异常困难。未来的工作应该开发额外的环境来补充这里介绍的环境，并为算法开发提供实用的、有区别的基准。Selecting suitable benchmarks is nontrivial. In many standard RL environments (e.g., CartPole), adapting to new dynamics is relatively simple, making it hard to meaningfully evaluate continual learning. Conversely, combining non-stationarity with safety constraints (e.g., Safe Continual World) can make the problem prohibitively difficult. Future work should develop additional environments that complement those introduced here and provide practical, discriminative benchmarks for algorithm development.

什么时候需要持续适应？When is continual adaptation necessary?在 HalfCheetah 中，安全的 RL 方法（CPPO-PID）和其他基于 PPO 的方法表现出有限的灾难性遗忘。在动态逐渐变化的设置中，采用安全强化学习方法可能比安全连续强化学习方法所需的额外计算更好。未来的工作应该描述何时需要持续适应以及何时简单的适应就足够了。In HalfCheetah, a safe RL method (CPPO-PID) and other PPO-based approaches exhibited limited catastrophic forgetting. In settings with gradual dynamics shifts, adapting a safe RL method may be preferable to the additional computation required by safe continual RL methods. Future work should characterize when continual adaptation is necessary and when simpler adaptations suffice.

哪些机制对于安全持续强化学习有效？Which mechanisms are effective for safe continual RL?由于安全持续强化学习整合了安全强化学习和持续强化学习的目标，因此进展可能会利用这两个领域的机制。安全强化学习方法可以通过重放、权重合并或网络扩展来扩展，而连续强化学习方法可以进行增强以满足安全约束。系统地识别和适应每个文献中的有效机制是一个关键方向。Because safe continual RL integrates the goals of safe RL and continual RL, progress will likely draw on mechanisms from both areas. Safe RL methods may be extended with replay, weight consolidation, or network expansion, while continual RL methods may be augmented to satisfy safety constraints. Systematically identifying and adapting effective mechanisms from each literature is a key direction.

方法应该如何处理硬安全约束？How should methods handle hard safety constraints?这项工作考虑了软约束，即对违规行为进行处罚，但不严格禁止。许多应用程序需要绝对不能违反的硬约束（例如，火箭发动机控制）。在持续学习的情况下确保硬安全可能需要严格防止忘记安全关键行为的方法。为这种机制开发算法仍然是一个重要的悬而未决的问题。This work considers soft constraints, where violations are penalized but not strictly forbidden. Many applications require hard constraints that must never be violated (e.g., rocket engine control). Ensuring hard safety under continual learning may require approaches that strictly prevent forgetting of safety-critical behavior. Developing algorithms for this regime remains an important open problem.

不完善的任务检测将如何影响安全的持续强化学习？How will imperfect task detection impact safe continual reinforcement learning?在这项研究中，我们假设可以可靠地检测到任务变化，考虑到环境变化的巨大性质，这是一个合理的假设。然而，在一些实际领域，非平稳性的本质会更加微妙。在这些领域中，安全持续强化学习的核心问题可能会更容易，因为不会显着影响性能的环境变化将需要较少的适应。与此同时，任务检测将变得更加困难，可能会影响依赖任务检测的持续学习机制（如 EWC）的性能。未来的工作应该评估任务检测方法，并研究环境变化对持续适应和任务检测之间权衡的影响。In this study, we assumed that task changes could be reliably detected, a reasonable assumption given the dramatic nature of our environment changes. However, in some real domains, the nature of the non-stationarity will be more subtle. In these domains, the core problem of safe continual RL will likely be easier, as environment changes that do not significantly impact performance will require less adaptation. At the same time, task detection will be more difficult, potentially impacting the performance of continual learning mechanisms that rely on task detection, like EWC. Future work should evaluate task detection methods and study the impact of environment changes on the tradeoff between continual adaptation and task detection.

7.3 局限性Limitations

虽然我们努力系统地探索安全持续强化学习的问题，但这项工作有局限性。值得注意的是，我们评估了一组有限的广泛采用的安全和连续强化学习算法，所有这些算法都是同策略算法。这种方法使我们能够进行公平的比较并检查这些算法的安全和持续学习特性。此外，While we have made an effort to systematically explore the issue of safe continual RL, this work has limitations. Notably, we evaluated a limited set of widely adopted safe and continual RL algorithms, all of which are on-policy algorithms. This approach allowed us to make fair comparisons and examine the safe and continual learning properties of these algorithms. Furthermore,此类算法代表了实时控制复杂的非平稳系统所需的算法，无需先验模型或离线训练的好处this category of algorithms is representative of those needed to control complex, non-stationary systems in real time without prior models or the benefit of offline training。未来的研究应该考虑其他类别的强化学习算法，例如离策略和离线强化学习算法。随着更多算法在该领域进行测试和开发，我们对安全连续强化学习相关挑战的理解将会得到提高。. Future studies should consider other classes of RL algorithms, such as off-policy and offline RL algorithms. Our understanding of the challenges associated with safe continual RL will improve as more algorithms are tested and developed in this area.

8 结论Conclusion

强化学习方法已经成功地控制了没有精确物理模型的复杂系统；然而，大多数算法假设环境是固定的，并且没有考虑系统及其环境中意外的非固定行为变化。此外，在真实环境中部署的代理必须满足安全要求。尽管连续强化学习和安全强化学习分别解决了非平稳性和安全性问题，但它们的交叉点仍然相对未被充分研究。本文在安全持续强化学习的统一问题下研究了这些挑战，并强调了文献中的明显差距。Reinforcement learning methods have been successful in controlling complex systems without accurate physical models; however, most algorithms assume stationary environments and do not account for unexpected non-stationary behavior changes in the system and its environment. Moreover, agents deployed in real settings must satisfy safety requirements. Although continual RL and safe RL address non-stationarity and safety, respectively, their intersection remains relatively understudied. This paper investigates these challenges under the unified problem of safe continual reinforcement learning and highlights a clear gap in the literature.

我们通过结合非平稳和受限马尔可夫决策过程框架来形式化安全连续强化学习，并引入了三个基准：（i）损坏的半猎豹速度，其中猎豹在最大速度约束下突然损坏或修复； (ii) Damaged Ant Velocity，对蚂蚁机器人应用相同的设置； (iii) 安全连续世界，其中机械臂执行连续的家务任务，同时避免洒出咖啡杯。我们对标准强化学习、安全强化学习、连续强化学习和安全连续强化学习等八种算法进行了基准测试，其中包括两种提出的方​​法。我们的结果表明，现有方法不能解决安全连续强化学习问题：虽然安全强化学习方法在某些非平稳设置中可以表现良好，但安全连续强化学习方法通​​常是必要的，并且需要新的算法。We formalized safe continual RL by combining non-stationary and constrained Markov decision process frameworks, and introduced three benchmarks: (i) Damaged HalfCheetah Velocity, in which a cheetah is suddenly damaged or repaired under a maximum-velocity constraint; (ii) Damaged Ant Velocity, which applies the same setting to an ant robot; and (iii) Safe Continual World, in which a robotic arm performs sequential household tasks while avoiding spilling a coffee mug. We benchmark eight algorithms spanning standard RL, safe RL, continual RL, and safe continual RL, including two proposed methods. Our results show that existing methods do not solve safe continual RL: while safe RL methods can perform well in some non-stationary settings, safe continual RL methods are often necessary, and new algorithms are required.

在提出的方法中，Safe EWC 提供了一个简单且有竞争力的基线，可以平衡遗忘和安全性，但其基于成本的奖励塑造可能会降低任务绩效。 CF-EWC 减少了灾难性遗忘并可以提高奖励，但它不能可靠地维持安全约束。这些发现激发了更有效地平衡奖励、安全和遗忘的方法。我们进一步提供终身训练动态的定性分析，研究在任务变化中保留安全约束的问题（包括任务边界处安全违规的增加），并进行消融，表明 Safe EWC 的惩罚安排是合理的，并且各个组件做出了有意义的贡献。最后，我们概述了未解决的问题并讨论了限制，以指导安全持续强化学习的未来工作。Among the proposed approaches, Safe EWC provides a simple and competitive baseline that balances forgetting and safety, but its cost-based reward shaping can reduce task performance. CF-EWC reduces catastrophic forgetting and can improve reward, but it does not reliably maintain safety constraints. These findings motivate methods that more effectively balance reward, safety, and forgetting. We further provide qualitative analyses of lifelong training dynamics, study the problem of retaining safety constraints across task changes (including increased safety violations at task boundaries), and perform ablations showing that Safe EWC’s penalty placement is well justified and that individual components contribute meaningfully. Finally, we outline open problems and discuss limitations to guide future work in safe continual reinforcement learning.

A.1.1 超参数Hyperparameters

超参数是通过环境的相关变体中的代理任务选择的。对于 PPO 来说，这是任务序列中的第一个任务，性能被衡量为最终的平均奖励。对于安全强化学习算法，这是任务序列中考虑安全约束的第一个任务，性能的衡量标准是最终平均奖励减去最终平均成本。对于连续强化学习算法，代理任务由各自任务序列中的前两个任务组成，通过对第二个任务的最终奖励与重新访问第一个任务时的最终奖励进行平均来衡量性能。所有超参数均在其评估环境的 5 个阶段进行评估。在 MuJoCo Damaged Velocity 任务中，每个任务对算法进行 500,000 个时间步的训练，而在 Safe Continual World 环境中，每个任务对算法进行 1,000,000 个时间步的训练。使用树结构 Parzen 估计器算法的 50 次试验来优化超参数。Hyperparameters were chosen through a proxy task in a relevant variation of the environment. For PPO, this was the first task in the task sequence, and performance was measured as the final average reward. For the safe RL algorithms, this was the first task in the task sequence with the safety constraint being considered, and performance was measured as the final average reward penalized by the final average cost. For the continual RL algorithms, the proxy task consisted of the first two tasks in the respective task sequence, and performance was measured by averaging the final reward on the second task with the final reward when revisiting the first task. All hyperparameters were evaluated on 5 episodes of their evaluation environments. On the MuJoCo Damaged Velocity tasks, algorithms were trained for 500,000 timesteps per task, and on the Safe Continual World environment, algorithms were trained for 1,000,000 timesteps per task. Hyperparameters were optimized using 50 trials of the Tree-Structured Parzen estimator algorithm.

我们选择这些有限交互代理任务有两个原因。首先，它在计算上更加可行。在安全连续世界上的一次训练几乎需要一整天才能完成。通过对 8 种算法进行 50 次试验，在给定预算的情况下优化整个任务的超参数是不可行的。其次，我们相信这代表了真正的终身强化学习的要求将会实现。在现实世界中，我们不会知道代理将遇到的所有可能的环境条件或任务。我们需要开发能够适应这些意外情况的技术。对于两种环境，安全 RL 算法的软安全约束成本限制均设置为 25。该值用于（非连续）HalfCheetah Velocity 任务We chose these limited-interaction proxy tasks for two reasons. First, it was much more computationally feasible. A single training run on the Safe Continual World took almost a full day to finish. With 50 trials of 8 algorithms, it was infeasible to optimize hyperparameters on the full task given our budget. Second, we believe this represents the requirements of real lifelong RL will be done. In the real world, we will not know all possible environmental conditions or tasks the agent will encounter. We need to develop techniques that can adapt to these unexpected scenarios. The soft safety constraint cost limit for the safe RL algorithms was set to 25 for both environments. This value was used for the (non-continuous) HalfCheetah Velocity task in[23]。由于我们在 Safe Continual World 中容忍杯子的一些倾斜（25 度应该没问题），因此我们保持这个值一致。实际上，该容差需要由领域专家或操作员设置。. Since we tolerate some tilting of the mug in Safe Continual World (25 degrees should be fine), we kept this value consistent. In practice, this tolerance will need to be set by a domain expert or operator.
