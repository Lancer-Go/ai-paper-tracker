摘要Abstract

多跳 QA 基准经常奖励大型语言模型 (LLM) 的虚假正确性，掩盖不可靠或有缺陷的推理步骤。为了转向严格推理，我们建议Multi-hop QA benchmarks frequently reward Large Language Models (LLMs) for spurious correctness, masking ungrounded or flawed reasoning steps. To shift toward rigorous reasoning, we propose安全SAFE，一个动态基准测试框架，用严格可验证的接地实体序列取代了未接地的思想链（CoT）。 我们的框架分为两个阶段： (1) 训练时验证，我们建立原子错误分类法和基于知识图 (KG) 的验证管道，以消除标准基准中的噪音监督，将多达 14% 的实例识别为无法回答，以及 (2) 推理时验证，在此验证的数据集上训练的反馈模型实时动态检测不可靠的步骤。 实验结果表明, a dynamic benchmarking framework that replaces the ungrounded Chain-of-Thought (CoT) with a strictly verifiable sequence of grounded entities. Our framework operates across two phases: (1) train-time verification, where we establish an atomic error taxonomy and a Knowledge Graph (KG)-grounded verification pipeline to eliminate noisy supervision in standard benchmarks, identifying up to 14% of instances as unanswerable, and (2) inference-time verification, where a feedback model trained on this verified dataset dynamically detects ungrounded steps in real-time. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的前沿已转向复杂推理，例如多跳问答 (QA)，要求模型综合多个文档中的证据以得出最终答案。虽然思想链 (CoT) 提示显着提高了这些任务的表现The frontier of Large Language Models (LLMs) has shifted towards complex reasoning, such as multi-hop question answering (QA), requiring models to synthesize evidence across multiple documents to derive a final answer. While Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting has substantially improved performance on these tasks(Wei et al., 2022)，他们的推理痕迹很难被证实或验证, their reasoning traces are hard to ground or verifyTurpin et al. (2023)。例如，中间步骤可能包含未经验证的实体、不受支持的关系或隐含的捷径，但仍会导致正确的答案。. For example, intermediate steps may contain unverified entities, unsupported relations, or implicit shortcuts that nevertheless lead to the correct answer.

我们的目标是通过从自由形式的思维生成转向使用知识图（KG）的严格遍历，使推理痕迹可验证，从而消除虚假推理。具体来说，我们将推理过程形式化为可验证的接地实体序列，并满足以下严格要求：（1）每个步骤必须是原子的，对应于单个有效的KG三元组； (2) 所有实体必须明确锚定在所提供的上下文中； (3) 每个关系都必须是可独立解释和验证的。Our goal is to make reasoning traces verifiable and thus eliminate spurious reasoning, by shifting from free-form thought generation to rigorous traversal using Knowledge Graph (KG). Specifically, we formalize the reasoning process as a verifiable sequence of grounded entities, subject to the following strict requirements: (1) each step must be atomic, corresponding to a single valid KG triple; (2) all entities must be explicitly anchored in the provided context; and (3) every relation must be independently explainable and verifiable.

为了严格执行训练时间和推理时间的这些基础约束，我们构建了一个动态基准测试框架。对于训练时间验证，我们执行两个关键的离线步骤。首先，我们应用上述严格约束来验证标准多跳基准To rigorously enforce these grounding constraints on both train-time and inference-time, we build a dynamic benchmarking framework. For train-time verification, we execute two critical offline steps. First, we apply the above strict constraints to verify standard multi-hop benchmarks(Ho et al., 2020; Yang et al., 2018; Trivedi et al., 2022)，我们删除了多达 14% 的噪声实例。其次，我们形式化了原子错误分类法，以构建有针对性的监督信号来训练外部反馈模型。在推理时，该模型动态验证多跳推理过程的每个原子步骤，实时检测并纠正与有效 KG 路径的任何偏差。与LLM自纠相比，本质上容易出错, where we remove up to 14% of noisy instances. Second, we formalize an atomic error taxonomy to construct targeted supervision signals for training an external feedback model. At inference-time, this model dynamically verifies each atomic step of the multi-hop reasoning process, detecting and correcting any deviation from a valid KG path on-the-fly. Compared with LLM self-correction which is inherently error-prone(Huang et al., 2023; Tyen et al., 2024)，这种推理时间验证确保了严格的推理轨迹。 总的来说，这些训练时间和推理时间阶段构成了, this inference-time verification ensures strictly grounded reasoning trajectories. 7 局限性Limitations

虽然我们利用知识图 (KG) 来确保验证管道的质量，但我们承认由于 KG 中固有的噪声或不完整的覆盖范围，可能会出现错误标记。为了解决这个问题，我们没有积极排除可能包含有价值的推理轨迹的实例，而是提供带有反馈标签的原始数据集作为增强资源。这种非破坏性方法允许未来的研究人员审核、完善或利用数据的完整上下文，同时考虑自动诊断中潜在的标签噪声。While we utilized a Knowledge Graph (KG) to ensure the quality of our verification pipeline, we acknowledge the potential for mislabeling due to inherent noise or incomplete coverage in the KG. To address this, rather than aggressively excluding instances that might contain valuable reasoning trajectories, we provide the original dataset with our feedback labels as an augmented resource. This non-destructive approach allows future researchers to audit, refine, or leverage the full context of the data while accounting for potential label noise in automated diagnostics.

此外，SAFE 框架的功效本质上依赖于推理生成器的指令跟踪能力。我们观察到，尽管反馈模型成功地产生了准确的诊断和明确的纠正指导，但推理生成器未能有效地将这些指令合并到其后续的生成步骤中。这种指令跟踪失败可能会导致迭代循环中出现未纠正的错误或重复错误。这凸显了对基础语言模型遵守复杂、逐步约束的能力的依赖，表明未来的工作必须解决纠正反馈和生成器执行之间的一致性。Furthermore, the efficacy of the SAFE framework inherently relies on the reasoning generator’s instruction-following capabilities. We observed instances where, despite the feedback model successfully producing accurate diagnoses and explicit corrective guidance, the reasoning generator failed to effectively incorporate these instructions into its subsequent generation step. Such instruction-following failures can lead to uncorrected errors or repeated mistakes within the iterative loop. This highlights a dependency on the base language model’s capacity to adhere to complex, stepwise constraints, suggesting that future work must address the alignment between corrective feedback and the generator’s execution.

最后，我们当前的实验设置是在所有必要的黄金通道都存在于所提供的上下文中的假设下进行的。在实际的开放域多跳场景中，初始检索可能会忽略关键证据，从而需要动态或迭代检索步骤。然而，这项研究的主要范围有意集中在隔离和评估模型的推理能力上，具体来说，在所需信息已经可用的情况下，它能够如何准确地合成和导航多跳逻辑。当反馈模型诊断出缺失的前提时，扩展 SAFE 框架以主动触发外部信息检索仍然是未来工作的一个有希望的方向。Finally, our current experimental setting operates under the assumption that all necessary gold passages are present within the provided context. In practical open-domain multi-hop scenarios, initial retrievals may omit crucial evidence, necessitating dynamic or iterative retrieval steps. The primary scope of this study, however, is deliberately focused on isolating and evaluating the model’s reasoning capabilities—specifically, how accurately it can synthesize and navigate multi-hop logic given that the required information is already available. Extending the SAFE framework to actively trigger external information retrieval when the feedback model diagnoses a missing premise remains a promising direction for future work.
