摘要Abstract -我们提出了一个统一的强化学习（RL）框架，该框架发现用于数据稀缺设备建模的紧凑参数化量子电路（PQC）。通过近端策略优化 (PPO) 优化的图神经网络 (GNN) 策略使用保留过程或几何组上的留一组交叉验证 (LOGOCV) 错误作为奖励来搜索电路架构。与六个经典基线相比，该框架在所有 11 个目标上实现了最低的平均绝对误差 (MAE)，HEMT 的误差 (Ioff) 降低了 59%，倍数变异性 (VTH) 降低了 81%，NWFET 的误差 (VTH、SS、Ioff) 降低了 84%，倍数变异性 (Ioff) 降低了 82%。这些结果证明了 RL 选择的经典模拟 PQC 作为具有低 OOD 误差和改进的物理一致性的紧凑替代品的潜力，尽管在两个评估的设备数据集上没有施加明确的物理约束、惩罚项或设备特定方程。We present a unified reinforcement-learning (RL) framework that discovers compact parametrized quantum circuits (PQCs) for data-scarce device modeling. A graph neural network (GNN) policy optimized by proximal policy optimization (PPO) searches circuit architectures using leave-one-group-out crossvalidation (LOGOCV) error on held-out process or geometry groups as the reward. The framework achieves the lowest mean absolute error (MAE) on all 11 targets versus six classical baselines, with 59% lower error (Ioff) and 81% tighter fold variability (VTH) for HEMTs and 84% lower error (VTH, SS, Ioff) and 82% tighter fold variability (Ioff) for NWFETs. These results demonstrate the potential of RL-selected, classically simulated PQCs as compact surrogates with low OOD error and improved physical consistency, despite imposing no explicit physical constraints, penalty terms, or device-specific equations, on the two evaluated device datasets.

一、简介I. INTRODUCTION

先进的器件架构正在不断发展，以满足高压电源转换和 3 纳米以下逻辑的独特需求。 p- GaN 栅极 GaN HEMT 可实现具有可控阈值电压的常关操作，以实现高性能功率开关 [1]，而全栅极纳米线 FET (NWFET) 则可抑制短沟道效应和断态泄漏，从而持续向低功耗逻辑扩展 [2]。Advanced device architectures are evolving to meet the distinct demands of high-voltage power conversion and sub-3-nm logic. p- GaN gate GaN HEMTs enable normally-off operation with controllable threshold voltage for high-performance power switching [1], while gate-all-around nanowire FETs (NWFETs) suppress short-channel effects and off-state leakage for continued scaling toward low-power logic [2].

它们的优化受到昂贵的制造和 TCAD 周期以及电荷捕获、热梯度和参数提取的随机变化的限制。因此，高价值的设计探索需要对未制造的工艺分割和保留的几何组合进行物理上一致的外推，而不仅仅是测量设备之间的插值（Their optimization is limited by costly fabrication and TCAD cycles and by stochastic variation from charge trapping, thermal gradients, and parameter extraction. High-value design exploration thus requires physically consistent extrapolation to unfabricated process splits and held-out geometry combinations, not merely interpolation among measured devices (图1Fig. 1 ).

现有的代理通常在架构中嵌入特定于设备的方程[16]-[18]，阻碍跨设备系列的重用，或者仅报告分布内测试[16][17]。相反，我们不施加明确的物理约束、惩罚项或特定于设备的方程，使分布外 (OOD) 预测成为明确的目标；物理一致性来自 OOD 奖励。参数化量子电路（PQC）提供了一个紧凑的傅立叶结构函数类，其表达能力随着深度和数据重新上传而增长[7][8]，而不是参数计数[9]。关系图注意力策略将候选电路表示为异构图，通过 PPO 进行优化，并采用留一分组交叉验证奖励，为看不见的过程或几何组选择架构，而不是平均训练拟合。单一搜索框架适用于平面 HEMT 和 3D NWFET，无需重新推导器件方程，即可发现针对每种器件优化的电路（Existing surrogates often embed device-specific equations in the architecture [16]-[18], impeding reuse across device families, or report only within-distribution tests [16][17]. We instead impose no explicit physical constraints, penalty terms, or device-specific equations, making out-of-distribution (OOD) prediction the explicit objective; physical consistency emerges from the OOD reward. Parametrized quantum circuits (PQCs) provide a compact Fourier-structured function class whose expressivity grows with depth and data re-uploading [7][8] rather than parameter count [9]. A relational graph-attention policy represents candidate circuits as heterogeneous graphs, optimized by PPO with a leave-one-groupout cross-validation reward, selecting architectures for unseen process or geometry groups rather than average training fit. A single search framework applies to both planar HEMTs and 3D NWFETs without re-deriving device equations, discovering a circuit optimized for each (图4Fig. 4）：HEMT 使用基于物理的预训练，然后对测量的晶圆数据进行校准，而 NWFET 直接从 3D TCAD 学习。在 11 个目标中，所选电路将 HEMT 的 OOD 误差降低了 6-34%，将 NWFET 的 OOD 误差降低了 2539%，HEMT 分割间的变异性提高了约 5 倍。据我们所知，这是第一个经过实验验证的、RL 架构搜索的 PQC 框架 [10]): HEMTs use physics-based pretraining followed by calibration to measured wafer data, while NWFETs learn directly from 3D TCAD. Across 11 targets, the selected circuits reduce OOD error by 6-34% for HEMTs and 2539% for NWFETs, with about 5× tighter HEMT split-to-split variability. 四．结论IV. CONCLUSION

我们展示了第一个经过实验验证、强化学习搜索的 PQC 框架，直接针对 OOD 设备预测进行了优化。We demonstrate the first experimentally validated, RL-searched PQC framework optimized directly for OOD device prediction.表4Table 4强调，首次提出的框架独特地结合了多目标 OOD 预测、RL 搜索量子电路以及跨不同电源和逻辑器件系列的可扩展性，而无需嵌入特定于器件的方程highlights that, for the first time, the proposed framework uniquely combines multi-target OOD prediction, RL-searched quantum circuits, and scalability across distinct power and logic device families without embedding device-specific equations.单个物理感知工作流程概括了测量的 p-GaN HEMT 工艺分割和未见过的 3D-NWFET 几何组合，在六个经典基线中实现了所有 11 个目标的最低 OOD MAE。与经过相同训练的 ANN 相比，PQC 将 MAE 降低了 50.1%，同时使用的参数减少了 4.7 倍；与所有基线相比，HEMT 的降低幅度达到 59%，NWFET 的降低幅度达到 84%。至关重要的是，它保留了所有 16 个预训练的 HEMT 物理趋势，将阈值滞后一致性提高了 5.4 倍，并在没有明确物理约束的情况下保持非线性 NWFET 几何缩放。这些结果将 OOD 奖励的量子架构搜索建立为一个框架，以发现数据稀缺设备优化的参数高效替代方案。A single physics-aware workflow generalizes across measured p- GaN HEMT process splits and unseen 3D-NWFET geometry combinations, achieving the lowest OOD MAE for all 11 targets among six classical baselines. Compared with the identically trained ANN, the PQC reduces MAE by up to 50.1% while using 4.7× fewer parameters; versus all baselines, reductions reach 59% for HEMTs and 84% for NWFETs. Crucially, it preserves all 16 pretrained HEMT physical trends, improves threshold–hysteresis consistency by 5.4×, and maintains nonlinear NWFET geometry scaling without explicit physical constraints. These results establish OOD-rewarded quantum architecture search as a framework to discover parameter-efficient surrogate for datascarce device optimization.
