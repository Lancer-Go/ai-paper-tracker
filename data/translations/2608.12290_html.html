超越反复试验：图像到视频依从性的代理优化Beyond Trial-and-Error: Agentic Optimization for Image-to-Video Adherence

Aman Tyagi    Hemanth Boinpally    Jonathan Chen    Douglas Gebert    Steven Hickson
摘要Abstract

现代黑盒图像到视频 (I2V) 模型在自动内容创建方面提供了强大的功能，但它们缺乏细粒度的控制和可靠性，给专业工作流程带来了重大挑战。它们固有的随机性会导致文本提示或超参数发生微小变化，从而产生截然不同的输出，通常需要低效、强力的试错过程。 为了解决这些限制，我们引入了“代理自我改进”框架，它将视频合成重新构建为闭环、目标导向的优化。我们的框架使用新颖的两阶段方法系统地导航生成参数空间。在第一阶段，迭代提示优化循环使用多模式大语言模型 (mLLM) 来细化输入提示。此细化实现了两个自动评估：戴维森场景图 (DSG) 查询确保语义一致性，以及用于工件检测的常见错误问题 (CMQ)。在第二阶段，我们使用贝叶斯优化来有效地共同优化随机种子和 CFG 尺度。该搜索以一套质量指标为指导，包括从 DSG 和 CMQ 评估中得出的新颖的视频文本依从性 (VTA) 分数。 我们的框架显着优于无引导的搜索方法：在人类偏好研究中，通过我们的代理方法生成的视频比基线输出更受青睐，获胜率高达 69%。这项工作提供了一种实用且可扩展的方法，用于增强最先进的视频生成模型的可预测性和控制，使该领域超越投机好奇心，转向可靠的、可用于生产的工具。Modern black-box Image-to-Video (I2V) models offer powerful capabilities in automated content creation, yet their lack of fine-grained control and reliability presents significant challenges in professional workflows. Their inherent stochasticity causes minor variations in textual prompts or hyperparameters to yield drastically different outputs often necessitating inefficient, brute-force trial-and-error processes. To address these limitations, we introduce the “Agentic Self-Improvement” framework, which reframes video synthesis into a closed-loop, goal-directed optimization. Our framework systematically navigates the generation parameter space using a novel two-stage approach. In the first stage, an iterative prompt optimization loop uses a multimodal Large Language Model (mLLM) to refine the input prompt. This refinement implements two automated evaluations: Davidsonian Scene Graph (DSG) queries ensure semantic adherence, and Common Mistake Questions (CMQ) for artifact detection. At the second stage, we use Bayesian optimization to efficiently co-optimize stochastic seeds and CFG scales. This search is guided by a suite of quality metrics, including the novel Video-Text Adherence (VTA) score derived from the DSG and CMQ evaluations. Our framework significantly outperforms unguided search methods: in human preference studies, videos generated via our agentic approach were strongly preferred over baseline outputs, achieving win rates up to 69%. This work provides a practical and extensible methodology for enhancing the predictability and control of state-of-the-art video generation models, moving the field beyond speculative curiosities toward reliable, production-ready tools.

1 简介Introduction

大规模黑盒文本和图像到视频 (I2V) 模型的出现释放了自动化内容创建的强大功能，为数字媒体和讲故事开辟了新领域The advent of large-scale, black-box text and image-to-video (I2V) models has unlocked powerful capabilities in automated content creation, promising a new frontier for digital media and storytelling[9, 30]。然而，这些系统的生成潜力与生产质量视频合成的实际需求之间仍然存在巨大差距. However, a significant gap remains between the generative potential of these systems and the practical demands of production-quality video synthesis[34, 5]。对于专业应用，最重要的要求是精确遵守创意简报，其中生成的视频必须忠实反映文本提示和给定的输入图像. For professional applications, the foremost requirement is precise adherence to creative briefs, where the generated video must faithfully reflect both a textual prompt and a given input image[7, 26, 18, 22]。核心挑战在于当前模型固有的随机性和敏感性；超参数的微小变化，例如随机种子或无分类器指导（CFG）尺度，可能会导致显着不同且通常不可预测的输出. The core challenge lies in the inherent stochasticity and sensitivity of current models; minor variations in hyperparameters, such as random seeds or classifier-free guidance (CFG) scales, can lead to dramatically different and often unpredictable outputs[1, 27]。这迫使从业者进入一种蛮力、反复试验的工作流程，生成大量视频变体，希望能够偶然地与他们的意图保持一致——这一过程效率低下，计算成本高昂。弥合这一差距需要超越推测生成，转向一个系统框架，以智能地导航这些模型的复杂参数空间。. This forces practitioners into a brute-force, trial-and-error workflow, generating a multitude of video variations in the hope that one serendipitously aligns with their intent—a process that is inefficient and computationally expensive. Bridging this gap requires moving beyond speculative generation towards a systematic framework for intelligently navigating the complex parameter space of these models.

为了解决这些限制，本文引入了“代理自我改进”，这是一个框架，它将 I2V 模型的生成管道从开环、推测过程重新构建为闭环、目标导向的优化。我们的框架采用两阶段策略，系统地针对方差的主要来源：文本提示和模型的超参数。首先，提示优化阶段使用多模式大语言模型（mLLM）来迭代地细化用户的初始提示。为了指导这种细化，mLLM 生成一组结构化评估并对其进行评分：戴维森场景图 (DSG) 查询To address these limitations, this paper introduces “Agentic Self-Improvement”, a framework that re-architects the generation pipeline for I2V models from an open-loop, speculative process to a closed-loop, goal-directed optimization. Our framework employs a two-stage strategy that systematically targets the primary sources of variance: the textual prompt and the model’s hyperparameters. First, a prompt optimization stage uses a multimodal Large Language Model (mLLM) to iteratively refine the user’s initial prompt. To guide this refinement, the mLLM generates and scores against a structured set of evaluations: Davidsonian Scene Graph (DSG) queries[8]探查语义内容，以及旨在检测典型生成工件的常见错误问题 (CMQ)。其次，超参数优化阶段使用贝叶斯优化来系统地搜索最佳随机种子和 CFG 尺度。整个过程以一套质量指标和直接从 DSG 和 CMQ 评估中得出的新颖的视频文本一致性 (VTA) 分数为指导，提供输出质量和对齐的定量测量。其结果是视频内容不仅具有高质量，而且符合用户指定的意图。that probe semantic content, and Common Mistake Questions (CMQ) designed to detect typical generation artifacts. Second, a hyperparameter optimization stage uses Bayesian optimization to systematically search optimal random seeds and CFG scales. The entire process is guided by a suite of quality metrics and the novel Video-Text Adherence (VTA) score derived directly from the DSG and CMQ evaluations providing a quantitative measure of the output’s quality and alignment. The result is video content that is not only of high quality but is also aligned with the user’s specified intent.

2.2 视频合成的可控性Controllability in Video Synthesis

虽然文本到视频模型变得越来越强大，但仅依靠文本提示进行控制对于需要精确输出的专业应用程序来说通常是不够的While text-to-video models have become increasingly powerful, relying solely on a textual prompt for control is often insufficient for professional applications that demand precise outputs[4]。这刺激了对更细粒度控制机制的研究。其中一项工作重点是合并额外的调节输入，例如用于外观控制的参考图像，以指导生成过程. This has spurred research into more fine-grained control mechanisms. One line of work focuses on incorporating additional conditioning inputs, such as a reference image for appearance control, to guide the generation process[6]。其他方法利用深度图、边缘图或人体姿势信息等空间控制来指示场景的几何形状和角色运动. A significant challenge in this domain is the disentanglement of content from motion, and achieving precise, multi-faceted control over the generated video’s content, style, and dynamics remains a key area of research[40, 3].

2.3 自动化即时工程Automated Prompt Engineering

扩散模型的快速工程或快速优化已经超越了手动调整，转向了自动化和系统化的方法。大量工作探索了基于梯度的方法，该方法将提示嵌入视为连续向量，可以对其进行优化以最大化可微奖励函数，该技术已被证明可以有效地发现“硬”、可解释的提示Prompt engineering or prompt optimization for diffusion models, has moved beyond manual tuning towards automated and systematic approaches. A significant body of work has explored gradient-based methods, which treat prompt embeddings as continuous vectors that can be optimized to maximize a differentiable reward function, a technique that has been shown to effectively discover “hard”, interpretable prompts[36]。另一个突出的方向涉及强化学习（RL）的使用，其中策略网络被训练以根据奖励模型的反馈来完善提示，奖励模型可以被设计为反映人类偏好或其他所需的图像特征. Furthermore, researchers have leveraged large language models (LLMs) as optimizers themselves, employing them in black-box settings to iteratively rewrite and improve prompts based on the generated image outcomes, demonstrating strong performance without requiring direct access to the diffusion model’s gradients[14, 13, 8]。基于这些改善文本对齐的努力，我们的工作推进了结构化提示方法. 2.4 超参数优化和代理人工智能Hyperparameter Optimization and Agentic AI

生成模型的性能也严重依赖于一组超参数，例如无分类器指导（CFG）规模和初始随机种子The performance of generative models is also critically dependent on a set of hyperparameters, such as the classifier-free guidance (CFG) scale and the initial random seed[17]。调整这些参数的传统方法是强力网格搜索，这种方法的计算成本很高，而且通常不是最优的。为了解决这个问题，必须探索更系统的优化技术。通过构建目标函数的概率模型，贝叶斯优化可以智能地探索参数空间，以更少的评估找到最优配置. The conventional method for tuning these parameters is a brute-force grid search, which can be computationally expensive and often suboptimal. To address this, more systematic optimization techniques have to be explored. By building a probabilistic model of the objective function, Bayesian optimization can intelligently explore the parameter space to find optimal configurations with fewer evaluations[28]。我们的工作通过在统一框架内共同优化确定性超参数和随机种子来扩展这种方法。. Our work extends this approach by co-optimizing both deterministic hyperparameters and the stochastic random seed within a unified framework.

“代理人工智能”的概念代表了从被动的单轮生成模型到主动的、目标导向的系统的转变，这些系统可以自主地计划、推理和行动以实现特定的目标。在内容创建的背景下，正在开发代理框架来自动执行复杂的多步骤生成任务The concept of “Agentic AI” represents a shift from passive, single-turn generative models to active, goal-directed systems that can autonomously plan, reason, and act to achieve a specified objective. In the context of content creation, agentic frameworks are being developed to automate complex, multi-step generative tasks[38]。这些系统通常采用大型语言模型作为中央“代理”，可以将高级目标分解为一系列较小的步骤，使用各种工具（例如生成模型）执行这些步骤，然后评估结果以完善其计划. These systems often employ a large language model as a central ”agent” that can decompose a high-level goal into a sequence of smaller steps, execute those steps using various tools (such as generative models), and then evaluate the results to refine its plan[38, 19]。我们的“主体自我完善”框架就是受到这种范式的启发。 “代理”一词通过与自主 LLM 代理的正式定义（动态感知、推理、行动）相一致，将我们的方法与标准生成-评估管道区分开来。. Our “Agentic Self-Improvement” framework is inspired by this paradigm. The term ’Agentic’ distinguishes our approach from standard generate-evaluate pipelines by aligning with formal definitions of autonomous LLM agents (dynamic perception, reasoning, action[37, 33]）。我们的代理不是静态度量，而是自主构建特定于提示的感知规则（DSG/CMQ），对结构故障进行推理，并跨语言（提示重写）和数学（贝叶斯搜索）参数空间执行目标导向的操作，将视频生成过程概念化为闭环优化问题。). Instead of a static metric, our agent autonomously constructs a prompt-specific perception rubric (DSG/CMQ), reasons over structural failures, and executes goal-directed actions across linguistic (prompt rewriting) and mathematical (Bayesian search) parameter spaces, conceptualizing the video generation process as a closed-loop optimization problem.

3.1 通过结构化评估快速优化Prompt Optimization via Structured Evaluation

视频生成的主要挑战是用户提示与训练基础模型的数据分布之间的语义差距。为了弥补这一差距，我们的框架采用了迭代提示优化策略，将初始文本提示细化为 I2V 模型的明确且有效的指令。A primary challenge in video generation is the semantic gap between a user’s prompt and the data distributions on which foundation models are trained. To bridge this gap, our framework employs an iterative prompt optimization strategy that refines the initial text prompt into an unambiguous and effective directive for the I2V model.

3.1.1 语义基础的问题生成：Question Generation for Semantic Grounding:

该过程首先将输入提示和初始图像帧摘要转换为一组具体的、机器可验证的问题。我们使用多模态大型语言模型（mLLM）（例如 Gemini）来生成两类评估查询：The process begins by translating the input prompt and initial image frame(s) brief into a set of concrete, machine-verifiable questions. We use a multimodal Large Language Model (mLLM), such as Gemini, to generate two categories of evaluative queries:

戴维森场景图 (DSG) 问题：利用戴维森场景图方法Davidsonian Scene Graph (DSG) Questions: Leveraging the Davidsonian Scene Graph methodology[8]对于细粒度的视觉评估，这些问题探讨了所需场景的基本语义组成部分。他们将提示解构为可验证的查询，涉及代理的存在、其行为和状态、对象拥有以及图形格式的位置。例如，如图for fine-grained visual evaluation, these questions probe the fundamental semantic components of the desired scene. They deconstruct the prompt into verifiable queries about the presence of agents, their actions and states, object possession, and location in a graphical format. For instance, in Figure2（上）对于提示“一群妇女坐在建筑物的台阶上”被分解为可验证的问题，例如“这群妇女在场吗”、“妇女们坐下了吗？”和“妇女们坐在台阶上吗？”。这种结构化分解使我们的框架能够系统地检查每个核心语义元素的存在性和正确性。(Top) for the prompt “A group of women sitting on the steps of a building” is broken down into verifiable questions such as,“Is the group of women present”, “Are the women sitting down?”, and “Are the women sitting on steps?”. This structured decomposition allows our framework to systematically check for the presence and correctness of each core semantic element.

常见错误问题 (CMQ)：虽然 DSG 有效地涵盖了语义内容，但它可能无法捕获视频生成模型的常见故障模式。 CMQ 旨在针对这些特定的弱点，重点关注对象持久性、自然运动和时间一致性等标准，同时还检查视觉故障或伪影。继续与上图中相同的示例Common Mistake Questions (CMQ): While DSG effectively covers semantic content, it may not capture common failure modes of video generation models. CMQs are designed to target these specific weaknesses, focusing on criteria such as object permanence, natural movement, and temporal consistency while also checking for visual glitches or artifacts. Crucially, however, our Bayesian Optimizer still decisively outperforms this highly competitive baseline across all metrics (e.g., 42% vs. 8% for RAHF). This confirms that our framework does not merely rely on post-hoc sorting; rather, the Bayesian search actively and effectively guides the generation process into higher-quality parameter regions.

5 讨论Discussion

我们的实验结果验证了这项工作的假设：与标准基线相比，系统的闭环优化过程产生明显更好的结果。我们关于提示优化的实验证实，将提示视为可细化的目标，而不是固定的输入，是改善语义对齐的关键步骤。这种技术还有助于提示与模型无关，因为不同的模型对不同的提示技术敏感Our experimental results validate the hypothesis of this work: a systematic, closed-loop optimization process yields perceptibly superior results compared to a standard baseline. Our experiments on prompt optimization confirm that treating the prompt as a refinable target, rather than a fixed input, is a critical step for improving semantic alignment. Such a technique also helps the prompt to be model agnostic as different models are sensitive to different prompting techniques[10]。随后通过贝叶斯搜索进行的端到端评估表明，超参数空间的智能搜索在发现人类偏好的视频方面比无引导的随机搜索更有效。总的来说，这些发现表明，通过系统地解决两个主要的方差来源（提示和超参数），我们的框架成功地将视频生成从推测性的试错方法转变为更加可预测和可控的过程。. The subsequent end-to-end evaluation via Bayesian search demonstrates that an intelligent search of the hyperparameter space is substantially more effective at discovering human-preferred videos than an unguided, random search. Collectively, these findings show that by methodically addressing the two primary sources of variance—the prompt and the hyperparameters—our framework successfully transforms video generation from a speculative, trial-and-error method into a more predictable and controllable process.

即时优化的成功凸显了一个强大的范例：使用 mLLM 的先进多模态理解作为直接反馈信号来引导生成模型的创造性输出。核心原理是“生成器-评论家”循环，其中 I2V 模型充当生成器，mLLM 充当复杂的视觉评论家。通过生成结构化 DSG 和 CMQ 问题，我们为 mLLM 提供了详细的评价标准，迫使其验证生成视频的细粒度语义和时间属性。这超越了简单化的 CLIP 分数相似性，而是利用 mLLM 对场景、动作和对象的丰富的组合理解来指导生成过程。这种方法具有高度可扩展性，指向未来的系统，mLLM 可以提供自由形式的文本反馈或对更抽象的质量（如叙事连贯性或情绪基调）的视频进行批评，从而创建一个强大的自我纠正循环，以实现更可控和一致的视频合成。然而，这种 mLLM 引导的方法自然受到当前视频理解能力的限制。虽然最先进的 mLLM 擅长识别对象和描述一般场景，但它们理解细微运动和复杂时间动态的能力仍然是一个活跃的研究领域The success of the prompt optimization highlights a powerful paradigm: using the advanced multimodal understanding of mLLMs as a direct feedback signal to steer the creative output of generative models. The core principle is a “generator-critic” loop where the I2V model acts as the generator and the mLLM functions as a sophisticated visual critic. By generating structured DSG and CMQ questions, we provide the mLLM with a detailed rubric for its critique, forcing it to verify the fine-grained semantic and temporal properties of the generated video. This moves beyond simplistic CLIP-score similarity and instead leverages the mLLM’s rich, compositional understanding of scenes, actions, and objects to guide the generation process. This approach is highly extensible, pointing toward future systems where mLLMs could provide free-form textual feedback or critique videos on more abstract qualities like narrative coherence or emotional tone, creating a powerful, self-correcting cycle for more controllable and aligned video synthesis. However, this mLLM-guided approach is naturally constrained by the current capabilities of video understanding. While state-of-the-art mLLMs excel at identifying objects and describing general scenes, their ability to comprehend nuanced motion and complex temporal dynamics is still an active area of research[41]。我们预计，随着多模式人工智能和机器人领域的日益融合，这种能力将显着进步. We anticipate that this capability will advance significantly with the increasing convergence of multimodal AI and the field of robotics[31]。对能够从视频中解释和预测复杂物理交互的实体代理的需求将不可避免地推动对运动和因果关系有更深刻理解的模型的发展。随着这些更复杂的视频理解 mLLM 的出现，它们可以作为更强大的“批评者”无缝集成到我们的框架中，不仅可以优化语义内容，还可以优化物理上合理的动态和复杂的角色动作。. The demand for embodied agents that can interpret and predict complex physical interactions from video will inevitably push the development of models with a more profound grasp of motion and causality. As these more sophisticated video-understanding mLLMs become available, they can be seamlessly integrated into our framework as more powerful ‘critics,’ enabling the optimization of not just semantic content, but also physically plausible dynamics and complex character actions.

尽管它很有效，但我们承认有一些局限性，为未来的研究开辟了道路。我们的代理框架虽然比无指导的手动迭代更有效，但仍然会带来巨大的计算成本。未来的工作可以探索更具样本效率的优化算法，或者将大型 mLLM 批评家提炼为更小的、专门的奖励模型，以减少推理开销。此外，我们的框架目前作为一个两阶段过程运行，首先优化提示，然后优化超参数。更先进的方法是同时探索提示和参数的联合优化，这可以揭示提示措辞和超参数敏感性之间不明显的相互作用。Despite its effectiveness, we acknowledge several limitations that open avenues for future research. Our agentic framework, while more efficient than unguided manual iteration, still carries a significant computational cost. Future work could explore more sample-efficient optimization algorithms or the distillation of the large mLLM critic into a smaller, specialized reward model to reduce inference overhead. Furthermore, our framework currently operates as a two-stage process, first optimizing the prompt and then the hyperparameters. A more advanced approach would be to explore the joint optimization of both prompts and parameters simultaneously which could uncover non-obvious interactions between prompt phrasing and hyperparameter sensitivity.

6 结论Conclusion

这项工作解决了控制黑盒 I2V 模型以实现符合用户意图的可靠、高质量输出的关键挑战。我们引入了主体自我改进框架，这是一种新颖的两阶段方法，可以系统地减少生成方差。我们的方法首先使用 mLLM 驱动的评估循环来优化文本提示以实现语义清晰度，然后采用贝叶斯优化来发现卓越的超参数。通过人类偏好研究，我们证明这种结构化的闭环过程明显优于标准的无引导搜索方法。通过将视频合成重新定义为目标导向的优化问题，这项工作提供了一种实用且可扩展的方法，将可预测性注入最先进的生成模型中。这一转变使 I2V 系统超越了推测性工具，转向了要求严格的现实应用所需的强大、可控的引擎。This work addressed the critical challenge of controlling black-box I2V models to achieve reliable, quality outputs that adhere to user intent. We introduced the Agentic Self-Improvement framework, a novel two-stage methodology that systematically reduces generative variance. Our approach first uses an mLLM-driven evaluation loop to optimize the textual prompt for semantic clarity and then employs Bayesian optimization to discover superior hyperparameters. Through human preference studies, we demonstrate that this structured, closed-loop process significantly outperforms standard, unguided search methods. By reframing video synthesis as a goal-directed optimization problem, this work provides a practical and extensible methodology for instilling predictability into state-of-the-art generative models. This shift moves I2V systems beyond speculative tools and toward robust, controllable engines required for demanding, real-world applications.
