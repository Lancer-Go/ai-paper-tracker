1. 简介Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 与数据库系统的日益集成正在改变结构化数据的设计、查询、记录和维护方式The growing integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into database systems is transforming how structured data is designed, queried, documented, and maintained(Zhou et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024)。最新进展包括文本到 SQL 生成、模式检索、数据库副驾驶、自动化文档和多模式查询处理. Recent advances include text-to-SQL generation, schema retrieval, database copilots, automated documentation, and multimodal query processing(Leis et al., 2025; Deng et al., 2021; Jo and Trummer, 2024)。尽管取得了这些进展，但一项基本功能在很大程度上仍未得到探索：使人工智能系统能够理解以实体关系图（ERD）表示的概念数据库模式。. Despite this progress, one fundamental capability remains largely unexplored: enabling AI systems to understand conceptual database schemas represented as Entity–Relationship Diagrams(ERDs).

ERD 仍然是概念数据库设计的主要抽象，在物理实现之前捕获实体、关系、完整性约束和继承结构ERDs remain the dominant abstraction for conceptual database design, capturing entities, relationships, integrity constraints, and inheritance structures prior to physical implementation(Chen, 1976; Thalheim, 2013a)。然而，实际上，这些图表很少以机器可读的形式提供。相反，它们主要以嵌入在教科书、讲座幻灯片、软件文档、PDF、屏幕截图、协作平台和在线存储库中的渲染图像的形式分发. In practice, however, these diagrams are rarely available in machine-readable form. Instead, they are predominantly distributed as rendered images embedded in textbooks, lecture slides, software documentation, PDFs, screenshots, collaborative platforms, and online repositories(Ouyang et al., 2025; Niu et al., 2025)。虽然数据库系统可以确定性地处理符号模式表示，但它们无法直接解释这些视觉工件。因此，从 ERD 图像中恢复结构化模式是人工智能辅助数据库工程的一项基本功能，支持自动模式理解、文档生成、遗留系统现代化、模式迁移和智能数据库助手等应用。. While database systems can deterministically process symbolic schema representations, they cannot directly interpret these visual artifacts. Recovering structured schemas from ERD images is therefore a fundamental capability for AI-assisted database engineering, enabling applications such as automated schema understanding, documentation generation, legacy system modernization, schema migration, and intelligent database assistants.

最近的视觉语言模型 (VLM) 在文档理解和多模态推理方面表现出了卓越的能力，使其成为从 ER 图中提取结构化信息的自然候选者Recent Vision–Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in document understanding and multimodal reasoning, making them a natural candidate for extracting structured information from ER diagrams(Achiam et al., 2023; Team et al., 2023)。然而，ERD 提出的挑战与自然图像或传统文档所带来的挑战有很大不同。它们的语义不仅来自文本标签，还来自形式图形语法、符号符号、空间组织和视觉连接的拓扑。因此，正确的解释需要共同恢复图表元素、它们的语义角色以及定义底层概念模式的结构关系，而不是简单地识别对象或阅读文本。. However, ERDs present challenges that differ substantially from those posed by natural images or conventional documents. Their semantics arise not only from textual labels, but also from formal graphical syntax, symbolic notation, spatial organization, and the topology of visual connections. Correct interpretation therefore requires jointly recovering diagram elements, their semantic roles, and the structural relationships that define the underlying conceptual schema, rather than simply recognizing objects or reading text.

尽管多模态推理取得了快速进展，但现有基准主要集中在图表、流程图、科学图表、几何图和文档布局上Despite rapid progress in multimodal reasoning, existing benchmarks focus primarily on charts, flowcharts, scientific figures, geometric diagrams, and document layouts(Pan et al., 2024a; Lu et al., 2021; von Engelhardt, 2002)。据我们所知，没有基准系统地评估对以 ER 图表示的概念数据库模式的结构化理解。现有数据集强调视觉感知或高级文档理解，而数据库工程应用程序需要重建可执行的、机器可读的模式表示，以便下游工具可以直接使用。这种脱节限制了我们衡量和改进概念数据库设计的多模式推理的能力。. To the best of our knowledge, no benchmark systematically evaluates the structured understanding of conceptual database schemas represented as ER diagrams. Existing datasets emphasize visual perception or high-level document comprehension, whereas database engineering applications require reconstructing executable, machine-readable schema representations that can be directly consumed by downstream tools. This disconnect has limited our ability to measure and improve multimodal reasoning for conceptual database design.

ERUnderstand 是评估 VLM 从 ERD 图像恢复结构化数据库模式的能力的基准。与主要关注识别或问答的现有图表和文档理解基准不同，ERUnderstand 通过将模型输出与机器可读的真实表示进行比较来评估结构化模式重建。每个 ERD 都与编码实体、属性、关系、键和 EER 构造的标准化 JSON 模式配对，从而能够在统一的评分框架下对各个结构组件进行细粒度评估。ERUnderstand is a benchmark for evaluating the ability of VLMs to recover structured database schemas from ERD images. Unlike existing diagram and document understanding benchmarks that focus primarily on recognition or question answering, ERUnderstand evaluates structured schema reconstruction by comparing model outputs against machine-readable ground-truth representations. Each ERD is paired with a standardized JSON schema encoding entities, attributes, relationships, keys, and EER constructs, enabling fine-grained evaluation of individual structural components under a unified scoring framework.

数据收集和表示。Data Collection and Representation.我们从公开的教育资源中收集了 182 个 ER 图，包括数据库教科书、课程材料和在线存储库。在删除重复项并手动过滤低质量示例后，该集合包含 148 个独特的高质量概念 ERD。为了确保可重复性，同时尊重版权限制，我们发布源参考和元数据，而不是重新分发原始图像。We collected 182 ER diagrams from publicly available educational resources, including database textbooks, course materials, and online repositories. 我们最初根据图中每种元素类型的频率尝试加权分数。尽管这种方法反映了模型在实体和属性等频繁元素上的性能，但它引入了“饱和效应”：大多数模型在这些类别中获得了非常高的分数（> 0.90），从而模糊了模型之间有意义的性能比较。We initially experimented with weighting scores according to the frequency of each element type in the diagrams. Although this approach reflected model performance on frequent elements such as entities and attributes, it introduced a “saturation effect:” most models achieved very high scores (>0.90) in these categories, obscuring meaningful performance comparisons between models.

简单平均和谐波平均Simple and Harmonic Averaging

我们还测试了简单平均和谐波聚合。简单的平均显示出希望，但仍然对性能高度可变的类别给予了不成比例的权重。调和聚合强调最低分数，过于重视弱关系、N 元关系等稀有元素。由于这些构造在数据集中很少出现，因此较小的绝对误差会导致类别得分非常低，从而主导总体得分，从而产生过于悲观的总体结果，无法反映一般模型的功能。We also tested simple averaging and harmonic aggregation. Simple averaging showed promise but still gave disproportionate weight to categories with highly variable performance. Harmonic aggregation, which emphasizes the minimum score, gave too much importance to rare elements such as weak relationships and N-ary relationships. Because these constructs appear infrequently in the dataset, small absolute errors resulted in very low category scores that dominated the aggregate, producing overly pessimistic overall results that did not reflect general model capabilities.

Macro-F1（最终选择）Macro-F1 (Final Choice)

我们最终采用了 Macro-F1，它计算类别级别 F1 分数的未加权平均值。这种方法将所有 ERD 元素类型视为对于全面的图表理解同样重要，无论它们在数据集中的频率如何。虽然这意味着罕见但结构上重要的元素（弱实体、N 元关系）与常见元素（常规实体、属性）具有相同的权重，但我们相信这准确地反映了完整 ERD 理解的目标。在弱实体或 N 元关系上系统性失败的模型在理解 ERD 表示法方面存在根本差距，即使这些元素很少出现。We ultimately adopted Macro-F1, which computes the unweighted mean of category-level F1-scores. This approach treats all ERD element types as equally important for comprehensive diagram understanding, regardless of their frequency in the dataset. While this means that rare but structurally significant elements (weak entities, N-ary relationships) receive equal weight to common elements (regular entities, attributes), we believe this accurately reflects the goal of complete ERD comprehension. A model that fails systematically on weak entities or N-ary relationships has a fundamental gap in understanding ERD notation, even if these elements appear infrequently.

Macro-F1 提供对所有图表组件的平衡评估，同时避免基于频率的加权的陷阱。这种选择符合我们评估真正的结构理解而不是针对常见情况进行优化的目标。Macro-F1 provides balanced assessment across all diagram components while avoiding the pitfalls of frequency-based weighting. This choice aligns with our goal of evaluating genuine structural comprehension rather than optimizing for common cases.
