摘要Abstract

长上下文 LLM 推理越来越受到 KV 缓存的内存和带宽成本的限制，但积极的压缩可以消除检索和多步推理所需的特定于层的证据。我们引入了 FreqDepthKV，一种推理时间缓存压缩方法，它将相邻层 KV 状态分解为共享低频深度分量和稀疏高频残差。轻量级在线探针根据注意力头对重建敏感注意力逻辑的贡献将注意力头分配给共享深度、残差深度或精确缓存模式，从而允许压缩策略适应提示结构而无需重新训练。在长上下文问答、针检索、摘要和代码生成基准测试中，FreqDepthKV 在大幅减少缓存预算的情况下保持了任务准确性。通过 32k-token 预填充窗口，FreqDepthKV 达到 58.3 Exact Match、63.0 F1、32.5 ROUGE-L 和 48.1 pass@1，紧密匹配完整 KV，同时优于之前的压缩缓存方法。它还将解码吞吐量提高到 70.4 token/s，将 TTFT 缩短到 2.06 秒，并将峰值 KV 内存降低到 6.2 GB，实现 3.9 倍的有效压缩比。Long-context LLM inference is increasingly limited by the memory and bandwidth cost of KV caches, yet aggressive compression can remove the layer-specific evidence needed for retrieval and multi-step reasoning. We introduce FreqDepthKV, an inference-time cache compression method that factorizes adjacent-layer KV states into shared low-frequency depth components and sparse high-frequency residuals. A lightweight online probe assigns attention heads to shared-depth, residual-depth, or exact cache modes according to their contribution to reconstruction-sensitive attention logits, allowing the compression policy to adapt to prompt structure without retraining. Across long-context question answering, needle retrieval, summarization, and code generation benchmarks, FreqDepthKV preserves task accuracy under substantially smaller cache budgets. With a 32k-token prefill window, FreqDepthKV reaches 58.3 Exact Match, 63.0 F1, 32.5 ROUGE-L, and 48.1 pass@1, closely matching full KV while outperforming prior compressed-cache methods. It also improves decoding throughput to 70.4 tokens/s, reduces TTFT to 2.06 seconds, and lowers peak KV memory to 6.2 GB, achieving a 3.9x effective compression ratio.

1 简介Introduction

长上下文推理将大型语言模型的瓶颈从参数存储转移到键值（KV）缓存。在自回归解码期间，每个生成的令牌都会关注来自所有先前令牌、层和头的缓存键和值，从而使内存流量和缓存占用空间随着上下文长度线性增长。对于长上下文问答、多文档摘要和代码完成，这种成本尤其严重，其中提示可能包含许多不相关的标记，但仍然需要保留少量证据来确定最终答案。现有的 KV 缓存压缩方法通过令牌驱逐、重要保留、量化或结构化共享来减少占用空间Long-context inference has shifted the bottleneck of large language models from parameter storage to the key-value (KV) cache. During autoregressive decoding, every generated token attends over cached keys and values from all previous tokens, layers, and heads, making memory traffic and cache footprint grow linearly with context length. This cost is especially acute for long-context question answering, multi-document summarization, and code completion, where prompts may contain many irrelevant tokens but still require preserving a small amount of evidence that determines the final answer. Existing KV cache compression methods reduce this footprint through token eviction, heavy-hitter retention, quantization, or structured sharingLi et al. (2024); Jiang et al. (2025); Javidnia et al. (2025)。然而，这些方法通常将冗余视为令牌级或精度级现象，而没有充分探索相邻变压器层经常编码类似的深度缓存结构的事实。. However, these approaches often treat redundancy as either a token-level or precision-level phenomenon, while leaving underexplored the fact that adjacent transformer layers frequently encode similar depth-wise cache structure.

MiniCache 演示了可以通过利用相邻层之间的冗余来沿深度维度压缩 KV 缓存MiniCache demonstrates that KV caches can be compressed along the depth dimension by exploiting redundancy between neighboring layersLiu et al. (2024a)。这个观察结果很强大：许多层存储相关的表示，因此共享或合并它们的缓存可以在不修改模型权重的情况下大幅减少内存。然而均匀深度压缩引入了一种新的故障模式。在重检索和重推理的提示中，决定性证据通常局限于特定的令牌头层交互。平均看来冗余的层可能仍然包含消除针状句子歧义、保留代码依赖性或维护推理链所需的高频残留信息。一旦这些特定于层的残差被擦除，下游注意力逻辑就可以改变足以改变生成的答案，即使重建的缓存具有较低的聚合误差。最近的工作也类似地指出，缓存压缩可能依赖于工作负载，并且在对抗性或证据敏感的提示下可能会失败. This observation is powerful: many layers store correlated representations, so sharing or merging their caches can substantially reduce memory without modifying model weights. Yet uniform depth compression introduces a new failure mode. In retrieval-heavy and reasoning-heavy prompts, the decisive evidence is often localized to particular token-head-layer interactions. A layer that appears redundant on average may still contain high-frequency residual information needed to disambiguate a needle sentence, preserve a code dependency, or maintain a reasoning chain. Once these layer-specific residuals are erased, downstream attention logits can shift enough to change the generated answer, even when the reconstructed cache has low aggregate error. Recent work has similarly noted that cache compression can be workload-dependent and can fail under adversarial or evidence-sensitive promptsChen et al. (2025); Haverbeck et al. (2026); Slothouber (2026).

我们建议We propose频率深度KVFreqDepthKV，一种频率引导深度共享方法，用于长上下文 LLM 推理中的鲁棒 KV 缓存压缩。中心思想是将相邻层的 KV 缓存分解为广泛共享的低频深度分量和保留检索敏感证据的稀疏高频残差。 FreqDepthKV 不是对每个层或磁头应用相同的压缩规则，而是在预填充期间使用在线探针来估计哪些磁头可以安全地共享深度分量，哪些磁头需要残差校正，哪些磁头应该保持精确。这会产生一个适应提示结构的缓存布局，同时保留与标准自回归解码的兼容性。, a frequency-guided depth sharing method for robust KV cache compression in long-context LLM inference. The central idea is to decompose the KV cache across adjacent layers into low-frequency depth components that are broadly shared and sparse high-frequency residuals that preserve retrieval-sensitive evidence. Rather than applying the same compression rule to every layer or head, FreqDepthKV uses an online probe during prefill to estimate which heads can safely share depth components, which require residual correction, and which should remain exact. This produces a cache layout that adapts to prompt structure while retaining compatibility with standard autoregressive decoding.

FreqDepthKV 做出了四项贡献。首先，它引入了 KV 缓存的深度频率分解，压缩冗余的层间通道，同时保留检索和推理所需的高频令牌证据。其次，它使用轻量级在线探针将每个注意力头分配给三种缓存模式之一：共享深度、剩余深度或精确，从而无需重新训练即可实现每层压缩。第三，它添加了在预填充期间根据缓存的注意力逻辑计算的重建感知路由损失，允许压缩比适应上下文长度和提示结构。第四，它在长上下文 QA、摘要和代码完成中报告一致的速度和内存增益，同时在激进的 KV 预算下保持准确性。FreqDepthKV makes four contributions. First, it introduces a depth-frequency factorization of the KV cache that compresses redundant inter-layer channels while preserving high-frequency token evidence needed for retrieval and reasoning. Second, it uses a lightweight online probe to assign each attention head to one of three cache modes: shared-depth, residual-depth, or exact, enabling per-layer compression without retraining. Third, it adds a reconstruction-aware routing loss computed from cached attention logits during prefill, allowing compression ratios to adapt to context length and prompt structure. Fourth, it reports consistent speed and memory gains across long-context QA, summarization, and code completion while maintaining accuracy under aggressive KV budgets.

FreqDepthKV 是对先前缓存管理策略的补充。 H2O、StreamingLLM、Scissorhands、SnapKV 和 PyramidKV 等标记选择方法通过保留有影响力或最近的标记来减少序列维度，而 KVQuant 和 KIVI 等量化方法会降低数值精度。其他方法探索自适应合并、工作负载感知压缩和层间共享FreqDepthKV is complementary to prior cache management strategies. Token-selection methods such as H2O, StreamingLLM, Scissorhands, SnapKV, and PyramidKV reduce the sequence dimension by retaining influential or recent tokens, while quantization approaches such as KVQuant and KIVI reduce numerical precision. Other methods explore adaptive merging, workload-aware compression, and inter-layer sharingWang et al. (2024); Yang et al. (2024a); Ma et al. (2026); Yao et al. (2025); Cai et al. (2025)。相比之下，FreqDepthKV 直接针对深度维度，构建在 MiniCache 之上. In contrast, FreqDepthKV targets the depth dimension directly, building on MiniCacheLiu et al. (2024a)而是用频率感知残差保存和注意 logit 感知路由取代统一层共享。这种设计的目的是保持深度压缩的内存优势，同时避免丢失长上下文任务所依赖的稀疏证据。but replacing uniform layer sharing with frequency-aware residual preservation and attention-logit-aware routing. This design is intended to keep the memory benefits of depth compression while avoiding the loss of sparse evidence that long-context tasks depend on.

Token-selection methods reduce the sequence dimension by retaining recent tokens, attention sinks, or heavy hitters, as in H2O, StreamingLLM, Scissorhands, SnapKV, PyramidKV, and related adaptive eviction schemesBui et al. (2026); An et al. (2026); Xiang et al. (2025); Wang et al. (2024); Yao et al. These methods are effective when importance is concentrated in a small subset of positions, but their compression decision is usually made over tokens rather than over the inter-layer structure of the cache. As a result, they can discard evidence that is weak under local saliency measures but becomes decisive after later-layer composition, a failure mode highlighted in recent analyses of compression risk and workload sensitivityChen et al. KVQuant and KIVI quantize cached keys and values, while other approaches pursue lossless or near-lossless representations, eigen-space projection, heterogeneous attention, and dynamic sparse computationYang et al. (2024b, c); Saxena et al. (2024); Yang et al. (2025, 2026); Xiao et al. (2025)。系统级研究进一步表明，最佳的 KV 策略取决于模型大小、设备内存、工作负载组合和服务架构. System-level studies further show that the best KV policy depends on model size, device memory, workload mix, and serving architectureQin et al. (2024); Gokhale et al. These methods are largely orthogonal to FreqDepthKV: quantization changes the numerical representation of retained cache entries, and sparse or block attention changes which entries are attended to, whereas FreqDepthKV changes how adjacent layers share depth-frequency components before decoding.

深度共享和层间冗余最接近我们的设置。 MiniCache 表明相邻的 Transformer 层包含大量的 KV 冗余，并且可以沿深度维度进行压缩Depth-wise sharing and inter-layer redundancy are closest to our setting. MiniCache shows that adjacent transformer layers contain substantial KV redundancy and can be compressed along the depth dimensionLiu et al. (2024a); Haffari et al. (2024); Liu et al. (2024b)。后续工作探索分层缓存共享、层间注意力相似性、浅/深层平衡和模型引导合并. Subsequent work explores layer-wise cache sharing, inter-layer attention similarity, shallow/deep layer balancing, and model-guided mergingYang et al. However, these approaches typically apply sharing or merging at a relatively coarse granularity, assuming that layer similarity implies safe cache substitution. FreqDepthKV instead treats depth redundancy as frequency-structured: low-frequency components are shared across adjacent layers, while sparse high-frequency residuals are retained for token-head interactions that affect cached attention logits.

This enables aggressive compression while preserving the layer-specific evidence needed for long-context retrieval, summarization, and code generation.

6 结论Conclusion

FreqDepthKV 通过将层间冗余视为频率结构信号而不是统一共享机会来降低长上下文 KV 缓存成本。通过存储共享的低频深度分量并选择性地保留稀疏的高频残差，它保留了最影响检索和推理的令牌头层证据。其在线头路由器使用缓存注意力逻辑上的重建感知损失进一步调整共享深度、残差深度和精确模式的压缩决策，无需重新训练并保持与标准自回归解码的兼容性。FreqDepthKV reduces long-context KV cache cost by treating inter-layer redundancy as a frequency-structured signal rather than a uniform sharing opportunity. By storing shared low-frequency depth components and selectively preserving sparse high-frequency residuals, it keeps the token-head-layer evidence that most affects retrieval and reasoning. Its online head router further adapts compression decisions across shared-depth, residual-depth, and exact modes using a reconstruction-aware loss over cached attention logits, requiring no retraining and remaining compatible with standard autoregressive decoding.

在长上下文 QA、摘要和代码生成基准测试中，FreqDepthKV 实现了最强的压缩缓存准确性，同时提高了推理效率。它达到 58.3 EM、63.0 F1、32.5 ROUGE-L 和 48.1 pass@1，与 Full KV 紧密匹配，同时将峰值 KV 内存降低至 6.2 GB，并将吞吐量提高至 70.4 token/s。与MiniCache相比Across long-context QA, summarization, and code generation benchmarks, FreqDepthKV achieves the strongest compressed-cache accuracy while improving inference efficiency. It reaches 58.3 EM, 63.0 F1, 32.5 ROUGE-L, and 48.1 pass@1, closely matching Full KV while reducing peak KV memory to 6.2 GB and increasing throughput to 70.4 tokens/s. Compared with MiniCacheLiu et al. (2024a)，FreqDepthKV 在使用更少内存的同时改进了所有任务指标，这表明深度共享在与残差保存和路由配合使用时最为有效。这些消融证实了深度频率分解、稀疏残差、在线路由、重构感知损失和精确缓存回退都有助于在激进的 KV 预算下实现鲁棒性。, FreqDepthKV improves all task metrics while using less memory, showing that depth sharing is most effective when paired with residual preservation and routing. The ablations confirm that the depth-frequency factorization, sparse residuals, online routing, reconstruction-aware loss, and exact-cache fallback each contribute to robustness under aggressive KV budgets.

总体而言，FreqDepthKV 提供了一条实现内存高效长上下文推理的实用途径：它保留了深度 KV 压缩的优势，同时避免了在检索敏感提示中擦除特定于层的证据的主要失败模式。它与令牌选择和量化方法的兼容性为结合序列级、精度级和深度频率压缩的缓存系统提供了更广泛的方向。Overall, FreqDepthKV provides a practical route to memory-efficient long-context inference: it preserves the benefits of depth-wise KV compression while avoiding the main failure mode of erasing layer-specific evidence in retrieval-sensitive prompts. Its compatibility with token-selection and quantization methods suggests a broader direction for cache systems that combine sequence-level, precision-level, and depth-frequency compression.

7 未来的工作Future Work

FreqDepthKV 的局限性之一是路由当前在预填充期间决定，然后在解码期间保持固定。未来的工作可以使缓存策略具有生成感知能力，当生成的序列揭示新的依赖关系时，允许头在共享深度、剩余深度和精确模式之间移动。这样的策略可以将此处使用的深度频率信号与令牌级驱逐或来自 H2O、SnapKV、PyramidKV 和自适应缓存合并等方法的合并标准相结合One limitation of FreqDepthKV is that routing is currently decided during prefill and then held fixed during decoding. Future work could make the cache policy generation-aware, allowing heads to move between shared-depth, residual-depth, and exact modes as the generated sequence reveals new dependencies. Such a policy could combine the depth-frequency signal used here with token-level eviction or merging criteria from methods such as H2O, SnapKV, PyramidKV, and adaptive cache mergingBui et al. (2026); Xiang et al. (2025); Wang et al. (2024); Yao et al. (2025)。这对于多轮推理和代码完成可能特别有用，其中相关证据在生成中间标记后可能会发生变化。. This may be especially useful for multi-turn reasoning and code completion, where the relevant evidence can shift after intermediate tokens are produced.

第二个方向是通过精度和系统级KV优化来组合深度频率共享。 FreqDepthKV 与 KVQuant 和 KIVI 等量化方法正交，并提供优化缓存布局和内存流量的服务架构A second direction is to compose depth-frequency sharing with precision and systems-level KV optimization. FreqDepthKV is orthogonal to quantization methods such as KVQuant and KIVI, as well as serving architectures that optimize cache placement and memory trafficLi et al. (2024); Jiang et al. (2025); Qin et al. (2024); Gokhale et al. (2026)。一个自然的扩展是将不同的数值精度分配给共享低频系数、稀疏残差和精确缓存头，从而产生联合深度频率和位宽分配问题。这可以进一步减少带宽，同时保留保护检索敏感的注意力逻辑的高频残差。. A natural extension is to assign different numerical precisions to shared low-frequency coefficients, sparse residuals, and exact-cache heads, yielding a joint depth-frequency and bit-width allocation problem. This could reduce bandwidth further while preserving the high-frequency residuals that protect retrieval-sensitive attention logits.

最后，未来的工作应该研究深度频率缓存压缩的训练时间和工作负载感知变体。尽管 FreqDepthKV 不需要重新训练，但可以对模型进行训练或稍微调整，以使相邻层缓存结构更具可压缩性，而不会削弱长上下文推理Finally, future work should study training-time and workload-aware variants of depth-frequency cache compression. The same framework could also be specialized for domains with distinct evidence patterns, such as tables, retrieval-augmented QA, multimodal inputs, and multi-agent inferenceCorallo et al. These directions would help determine when depth-frequency sharing should remain a purely inference-time system and when model- or workload-specific adaptation provides additional robustness.
