TriViewBench：受控的复杂性缩放TriViewBench: Controlled Complexity Scaling
用于 MLLM 中的多视图结构推理for Multi-View Structural Reasoning in MLLMs

Yu-Yang Chen    Lan-Zhe Guo
摘要Abstract

1 简介Introduction

多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM) 在各种视觉问答任务中取得了出色的性能Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) achieve strong performance across various visual question answering tasks[yin2024survey, kuang2025natural, jin2025efficient]。 然而，在多视图设置中，可靠的推理需要的不仅仅是识别单个对象：模型必须跨视图对齐身份，通过使用互补视点解决遮挡问题， 并从碎片化的视觉线索中重建连贯的结构表征. In multi-view settings, however, reliable reasoning demands more than recognizing individual objects: models must align identities across views, resolve occlusion by using complementary viewpoints, and reconstruct coherent structural representations from fragmented visual cues[yeh2025seeing, yang2025mmsi, park2025nuplanqa]。 随着场景复杂性以受控和可测量的方式增加，当前的 MLLM 是否能够保持这种推理水平仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。. Whether current MLLMs can maintain this level of reasoning as scene complexity increases in a controlled and measurable way remains an open question.

现有的视觉推理基准提供了有价值的进展，但表现出一些结构限制，使得这个问题难以回答Existing visual reasoning benchmarks provide valuable progress but exhibit several structural limitations that make this question difficult to answer[wang2025spatial457, ma20253dsrbench, lee2025spatialmosaic]。 大多数基准都是根据不受约束的真实世界图像构建的. Most benchmarks are constructed from unconstrained real-world images[daxberger2025mm, park2025nuplanqa, yang2025mmsi, lee2025spatialmosaic]。 虽然此类数据提供多样性，但场景复杂性并未明确参数化：对象数量、空间排列和遮挡程度同时变化且不可预测，因此性能下降不能明确归因于特定因素。 大多数基准测试也在单视图设置中运行，这限制了跨视角集成的需求. While such data offers diversity, scene complexity is not explicitly parameterized: object count, spatial arrangement, and occlusion degree vary simultaneously and unpredictably, so performance degradation cannot be cleanly attributed to specific factors. Most benchmarks also operate in single-view settings, which limit the demand for cross-perspective integration[johnson2017clevr, ma20253dsrbench, gong2025space10]。 此外，真实图像基准通常缺乏精确的对象级几何和可见性注释，从而无法将错误细粒度分解为局部、计数或结构故障。. Furthermore, real-image benchmarks typically lack precise object-level geometry and visibility annotations, preventing fine-grained decomposition of errors into local, counting, or structural failures.

为了解决这些限制，我们引入To address these limitations, we introduce三视图工作台TriViewBench，一个受控多视图基准测试，由具有显式参数化对象计数和遮挡的合成 3D 场景构建。 每个场景都是从正面、侧面和自上而下的角度渲染的， 加强跨视图推理和结构一致性。 该基准测试包含 1,923 个场景和超过 14K 个问答 (QA) 对，分为四个复杂级别和三个推理类别：, a controlled multi-view benchmark built from synthetic 3D scenes with explicitly parameterized object count and occlusion. Each scene is rendered from front, side, and top-down viewpoints, enforcing cross-view reasoning and structural consistency. The benchmark contains 1,923 scenes and over 14K Question-Answer (QA) pairs organized into four complexity levels and three reasoning categories:当地决策Local Decision, 物体计数Object Counting, 和, and全球复苏Global Recovery。 通过独立改变复杂性轴并根据推理需求构建任务，TriViewBench 可以直接测量性能扩展和可解释的故障分析。. By varying complexity axes independently and structuring tasks by reasoning demand, TriViewBench enables direct measurement of performance scaling and interpretable failure analysis.

2 相关工作Related Work

多模态大语言模型。Multimodal Large Language Models.

最近的 MLLM 将视觉编码器与大型语言模型相结合来执行各种视觉任务， 包括图像字幕、视觉问答和场景理解Recent MLLMs combine vision encoders with large language models to perform diverse visual tasks, including image captioning, visual question answering, and scene understanding[comanici2025gemini, hurst2024gpt, liu2023visual, liu2024improved, li2024llava, bai2025qwen3, wang2025internvl3]。 模型规模和架构设计已被证明会影响推理能力，尽管它们的相对贡献仍然存在争议. Model scale and architectural design have been shown to influence reasoning capability, though their relative contributions remain contested[bai2025qwen3, wang2025internvl3, bai2025qwen25vltechnicalreport]。 TriViewBench 通过在相同条件下评估涵盖各种规模和架构的 18 个模型来测试这些因素，发现架构系列比单独的参数计数更可靠地预测性能。. TriViewBench tests these factors by evaluating 18 models spanning a wide range of scales and architectures under identical conditions, finding that architectural family predicts performance more reliably than parameter count alone.

视觉推理基准。Visual Reasoning Benchmarks.

大多数现有的视觉推理基准都是围绕单个图像构建的Most existing visual reasoning benchmarks are built around single images[ma20253dsrbench, antol2015vqa, marino2019ok, yue2024mmmu, li2023seed], 这限制了跨视图信息集成的需求： 模型一次只需要处理一个视角，并且永远不需要协调不同视角的观察或通过咨询互补角度来解决遮挡问题。 越来越多的工作将评估扩展到多图像或多视图设置，以解决这一差距。 缪尔长凳, which limits the demand for cross-view information integration: models need only process one perspective at a time and are never required to reconcile observations across viewpoints or resolve occlusion by consulting complementary angles. A growing body of work has extended evaluation to multi-image or multi-view settings to address this gap. MuirBench[wang2024muirbench]涵盖 12 个多图像任务类别，发现即使 GPT-4o 也只能达到 68.0% 的准确率。 MMSI-基准covers 12 multi-image task categories and finds that even GPT-4o achieves only 68.0% accuracy. MMSI-Bench[yang2025mmsi]以多图像空间智能为目标，发现最强大的开源模型仅达到 30.7% 的准确率，而人类基线为 97.2%，六位领域专家花费了 300 多个小时精心打造 手动 1,000 个问题。 全角度长凳targets multi-image spatial intelligence and finds that the strongest open-source model attains only 30.7% accuracy against a human baseline of 97.2%, with six domain experts spending over 300 hours crafting 1,000 questions manually. All-Angles Bench[yeh2025seeing]评估对现实世界场景的多视图理解，并将交叉视图遮挡识别为主要故障模式。 这些基准测试表明，多视图推理对当前的 MLLM 提出了真正的挑战，但它们对现实世界图像的依赖引入了两个相互关联的限制。 首先，收集和注释多视图现实世界数据是劳动密集型的：为空间关系、遮挡状态和结构属性建立真实答案需要对每个场景进行仔细的手动验证。 其次，也是更根本的一点，真实图像中的场景复杂性是不受控制的。对象数量、空间排列和遮挡程度同时变化，并且没有明确的参数化。 当准确性下降时，不可能将失败归因于特定的因果因素，也不可能衡量性能如何随单个复杂性维度变化。 TriViewBench 通过合成解决了这两个限制， 参数化场景生成：地面实况注释是从场景元数据自动派生的，无需任何手动标记，并且复杂度轴独立变化， 能够将观察到的故障直接归因于对象基数、遮挡严重性或其组合。evaluates multi-view understanding across real-world scenes and identifies cross-view occlusion as a central failure mode. These benchmarks demonstrate that multi-view reasoning poses genuine challenges to current MLLMs, but their reliance on real-world imagery introduces two interrelated limitations. First, collecting and annotating multi-view real-world data is labor-intensive: establishing ground-truth answers for spatial relations, occlusion states, and structural attributes requires careful manual verification for each scene. Second, and more fundamentally, scene complexity in real images is uncontrolled. Object count, spatial arrangement, and occlusion degree vary simultaneously and without explicit parameterization. When accuracy drops, it is impossible to attribute failure to specific causal factors or to measure how performance scales with individual complexity dimensions. TriViewBench addresses both limitations through synthetic, parameterized scene generation: ground-truth annotations are derived automatically from scene metadata without any manual labeling, and complexity axes are varied independently, enabling direct attribution of observed failures to object cardinality, occlusion severity, or their combination.

综合且可控的基准。Synthetic and Controllable Benchmarks.

克莱弗CLEVR[johnson2017clevr]表明，受控合成生成能够实现真实图像评估不可见的精确故障诊断，建立了一种范式，从此为许多诊断基准提供了信息。 在对象计数领域，VLMCountBenchshowed that controlled synthetic generation enables precise failure diagnosis invisible to real-image evaluations, establishing a paradigm that has since informed numerous diagnostic benchmarks. In the domain of Object Counting, VLMCountBench[guo2025your]显示 MLLM 在成分计数场景下失败，这与我们的发现一致。 TriViewBench 将这种受控范式扩展到三视图设置，添加遮挡作为显式复杂性轴，并引入先前综合基准中缺少的跨视图结构推理维度。shows that MLLMs fail under compositional counting scenarios, consistent with our findings. TriViewBench extends this controlled paradigm to a three-view setting, adding occlusion as an explicit complexity axis and introducing a cross-view structural reasoning dimension absent from prior synthetic benchmarks.

These benchmarks reveal important limitations in model spatial awareness, but focus primarily on metric estimation, orientation, and 3D grounding rather than the structural reconstruction and cross-view identity resolution that characterize TriViewBench’s task design. 5 局限性Limitations

TriViewBench 由合成场景构建而成，可以精确控制复杂性变量，但限制了对现实世界图像的概括。 合成渲染不复制光度变化， 自然场景的纹理多样性和环境杂乱， 以及此处确定的功能层次结构和故障模式是否转移到现实世界的多视图设置仍然开放。TriViewBench is constructed from synthetic scenes, affording precise control over complexity variables but limiting generalization to real-world imagery. Synthetic renderings do not replicate photometric variation, texture diversity, and environmental clutter of natural scenes, and whether the capability hierarchy and failure modes identified here transfer to real-world multi-view settings remains open.

该基准测试采用固定的三视图配置（正面、侧面、顶部），无法捕获实践中遇到的连续视点变化。 在任意相机放置或少于三个视图的情况下评估结构推理超出了当前范围。The benchmark adopts a fixed three-view configuration (front, side, top), which does not capture the continuous viewpoint variation encountered in practice. Evaluating structural reasoning under arbitrary camera placement or with fewer than three views is outside the current scope.

这三个推理类别针对多视图结构理解的特定部分，并未涵盖所有相关功能。 开放式场景描述、3D 轨迹推理等任务 或场景图构造超出了当前设计。The three reasoning categories target a specific slice of multi-view structural understanding and do not cover all relevant capabilities. Tasks such as open-ended scene description, 3D trajectory reasoning, or scene graph construction fall outside the current design.

最后，CoT 评估对每个模型使用单一提示格式。即时的设计选择（包括几次演示或针对模型系列量身定制的格式）是否会改变全球复苏中观察到的能力门控模式，这仍然是一个悬而未决的问题，这值得进一步研究。Finally, the CoT evaluation uses a single prompt format for each model. It remains open whether prompt design choices, including few-shot demonstrations or formatting tailored to model families, could alter the capability-gating pattern observed in Global Recovery, and this merits further investigation.

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 TriViewBench，这是一个受控基准，用于在明确定义的复杂性下诊断 MLLM 中的多视图结构推理。 通过在四个级别上独立改变对象数量和遮挡，该基准可以将性能变化直接归因于特定的结构因素。We introduced TriViewBench, a controlled benchmark for diagnosing multi-view structural reasoning in MLLMs under explicitly defined complexity. By independently varying object count and occlusion across four levels, the benchmark enables direct attribution of performance changes to specific structural factors.

对 18 个 MLLM 的评估揭示了四个一致的发现。 首先，所有 18 个模型都表现出相同的能力层次结构（局部决策Evaluation of 18 MLLMs reveals four consistent findings. First, all 18 models exhibit an identical capability hierarchy (Local Decision>>物体计数Object Counting>>全局恢复）无一例外地跨架构、规模和系列， 将其建立为当前 MLLM 的系统属性，而不是特定于模型的工件。 其次，性能随复杂性以明显不同的速率单调下降：本地决策任务适度下降（相对下降 12.11%），对象计数大幅下降（相对下降 59.14%），全局恢复严重恶化（相对下降 80.02%）。 第三，对象计数的错误方向分析揭示了两种机械上独立的故障模式：单视图遮挡失明（模型无法检测到从单一角度部分隐藏的对象）和跨视图身份混淆（模型跨视图多次计数同一物理对象）。 这些模式的大小相当，但方向相反， 指出了明显的感知瓶颈，而不是单一的潜在缺陷。 第四，CoT 激励的总体效益接近于零（Global Recovery) without exception across architectures, scales, and families, establishing this as a systematic property of current MLLMs rather than a model-specific artifact. Second, performance degrades monotonically with complexity at markedly different rates: Local Decision tasks decline modestly (12.11% relative drop), Object Counting degrades substantially (59.14% relative drop), and Global Recovery deteriorates severely (80.02% relative drop). Third, error direction analysis on Object Counting uncovers two mechanistically independent failure modes: single-view occlusion blindness, in which models fail to detect objects partially hidden from a single perspective, and cross-view identity confusion, in which models count the same physical object multiple times across views. These modes are comparable in magnitude but opposite in direction, pointing to distinct perceptual bottlenecks rather than a single underlying deficit. Fourth, CoT prompting yields near-zero overall benefit (Δ=−0.16%\Delta=-0.16\%），其对全球复苏的影响受到强烈的能力限制：大型开源模型（), and its effect on Global Recovery is strongly capability-gated: large open-source models (≥\geq14B) 平均提高14B) improve by an average of+17.03%+17.03\%而小型号（while small models (≤\leq3B) 均匀恶化。 即使有了 CoT，也没有模型能够达到人类水平的表现， 在最困难的水平上差距超过30个百分点。3B) uniformly deteriorate. Even with CoT, no model approaches human-level performance, with a gap exceeding 30 percentage points at the hardest level.

这些发现共同表明，根本限制不是缺乏推理步骤，而是无法形成完整且一致的跨视图空间表示。 TriViewBench 提供了一个受控诊断框架，用于指导结构多视图理解的未来进展。These findings collectively indicate that the root limitation is not a lack of reasoning steps but the inability to form complete and consistent cross-view spatial representations. TriViewBench provides a controlled diagnostic framework for guiding future progress on structural multi-view understanding.
