摘要Abstract

高斯 DDPM 中的一个中心误差度量是精确反向链和学习的高斯反向过程之间的路径空间 KL 散度。这个量与分类器引导等程序尤其相关，它会扰乱整个反向轨迹而不仅仅是最终样本。先前的分析表明，标准各向同性逆协方差不可避免地会受到影响。A central error measure in Gaussian DDPMs is the path-space KL divergence between the exact reverse chain and the learned Gaussian reverse process. This quantity is especially relevant for procedures such as classifier guidance, which perturb the entire reverse trajectory rather than only the terminal sample. Prior analyses show that standard isotropic reverse covariances suffer an unavoidableΩ​(1/T)\Omega(1/T)路径-KL 误差作为去噪步骤的数量path-KL error as the number of denoising stepsTT成长。我们证明，匹配完整的后验协方差打破了这个障碍，产生了有序的改进，将路径 KL 减少为grows. We show that matching the full posterior covariance breaks this barrier, yielding an order-wise improvement that reduces the path KL toO​(1/T2)O(1/T^{2})。为了使完整的协方差匹配实用，我们引入了 Lanczos 高斯采样器 (LGS)，这是一种无需训练、无需矩阵的方法，仅使用协方差向量乘积从最优反向协方差中进行采样，这些乘积可通过后验均值的雅可比向量乘积获得。 LGS 避免了密集的协方差存储和辅助协方差模型。我们证明 LGS 近似误差随着 Lanczos 步骤的数量呈指数衰减，其中每个 Lanczos 步骤都需要一个雅可比向量积。根据经验，在标准图像基准测试中，仅使用三个这样的步骤就可以比强对角协方差基线（包括 OCM-DDPM）提高样本质量。这表明完全协方差匹配对于快速 DDPM 采样来说既具有理论上的价值，又具有实用性。. To make full covariance matching practical, we introduce the Lanczos Gaussian sampler (LGS), a training-free, matrix-free method for sampling from the optimal reverse covariance using only covariance-vector products, which are available through Jacobian-vector products of the posterior mean. LGS avoids dense covariance storage and auxiliary covariance models. We prove that LGS approximation error decays exponentially in the number of Lanczos steps, where each Lanczos step requires a single Jacobian-vector product. Empirically, using only just three such steps improves sample quality over strong diagonal-covariance baselines, including OCM-DDPM, across standard image benchmarks. This identifies full covariance matching as both theoretically valuable and practically accessible for fast DDPM sampling.

1 简介Introduction

高斯去噪扩散概率模型（DDPM）研究中的一个中心近似误差是精确逆时去噪链与学习的高斯逆过程之间的路径空间 KL 散度。扩散模型的许多用途需要精确的反向轨迹，而不是简单的精确端点样本。分类器指导是一个典型的例子，因为它在每个去噪步骤修改反向链。无条件路径 KL 控制此类分类器引导采样的分布误差。A central approximation error in the study of Gaussian Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPMs) is the path-space KL divergence between the exact reverse-time denoising chain and the learned Gaussian reverse process. Many uses of diffusion models require an accurate reverse trajectory as opposed to simply accurate endpoint samples. Classifier guidance is a canonical example, since it modifies the reverse chain at each denoising step. The unconditional path KL controls the distributional error of such classifier-guided sampling.

先前的分析表明，对于标准高斯 DDPM 参数化，其中后验（去噪）核被选择为具有各向同性协方差，路径 KL 通常不会比Prior analyses show that for standard Gaussian DDPM parameterizations, where the posterior (denoising) kernel is chosen to have isotropic covariance, the path KL generally cannot decay faster thanΩ​(1/T)\Omega(1/T)作为去噪步骤的数量as the number of denoising stepsTT成长。我们在这里看到，即使允许反向过程在每个去噪步骤中使用真实后验协方差的最佳对角线近似，这种限制仍然存在。grows. 尽管有上述值，但已知匹配最佳后验协方差在实践中具有挑战性，原因至少有两个。首先，研究表明，相对于在去噪核中简单地使用缩放的恒等协方差，学习最优后验协方差会使训练不稳定并降低样本质量。其次，即使假设可以学习最佳协方差，实际（高）维度的存储要求也是令人望而却步的。在这方面已经取得了两个重要进展：首先，我们观察到在高斯 DDPM 中，Tweedie 型恒等式通过后验均值的雅可比行列式表达后验协方差。因此，协方差向量乘积可以通过雅可比向量乘积来计算，而无需显式存储协方差。其次，为了解决存储问题，可以通过使用 Hutchinson 迹估计器来提取最佳协方差的对角线，而无需显式存储协方差。The above value notwithstanding, matching the optimal posterior covariance is known to be challenging in practice for at least two reasons. First, it has been shown that learning the optimal posterior covariance can make training unstable and degrade sample quality relative to simply using a scaled identity covariance in the denoising kernel. Second, even assuming one could learn the optimal covariance, storage requirements are prohibitive in practical (high) dimensions. Two important pieces of progress have been made in this regard: First, it has been observed that in Gaussian DDPMs Tweedie-type identities express the posterior covariance via the Jacobian of the posterior mean. Second, to combat storage issues, the diagonal of the optimal covariance can be extracted without explicitly storing the covariance through the use of the Hutchinson trace estimator.

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们从理论和算法的角度研究了高斯 DDPM 中协方差匹配的价值。在理论方面，我们表明匹配完整的后验协方差打破了标准In this paper we study the value of covariance matching in Gaussian DDPMs from both theoretical and algorithmic perspectives. On the theoretical side, we show that matching the full posterior covariance breaks the standardO​(1/T)O(1/T)path-KL 障碍：最优协方差匹配实现path-KL barrier: optimal covariance matching achieves anO​(1/T2)O(1/T^{2})路径 KL 率，参见定理path KL rate, see Theorem3。我们进一步表明，这种改进对于分类器指导是重要的，其中使用标量协方差可以产生. We further show that this improvement is consequential for classifier guidance, where using a scalar covariance can yield anΩ​(1/T)\Omega(1/T)条件端点-KL 误差，同时获得最佳协方差可以恢复conditional endpoint-KL error, while access to the optimal covariance restores theO​(1/T2)O(1/T^{2})率。rate.

在算法方面，我们引入了 Lanczos Gaussian 采样器，这是一种无需训练的方法，仅使用从预训练评分网络的雅可比向量积获得的协方差向量积来从完整后验协方差中进行采样。该方法适用于任何没有辅助协方差训练或密集协方差存储的预训练高斯 DDPM，并且我们表明其逼近误差随着 Lanczos 步骤的数量呈指数衰减。在标准图像生成基准中，LGS 持续提高了各向同性和对角协方差基线的样本质量，这表明完全协方差匹配不仅在理论上有益，而且实际上可用于快速扩散采样。On the algorithmic side, we introduce the Lanczos Gaussian sampler, a training-free method for sampling from the full posterior covariance using only covariance-vector products obtained from Jacobian-vector products of a pretrained score network. The method applies to any pretrained Gaussian DDPM without auxiliary covariance training or dense covariance storage, and we show that its approximation error decays exponentially in the number of Lanczos steps. Across standard image-generation benchmarks, LGS consistently improves sample quality over isotropic and diagonal-covariance baselines, demonstrating that full covariance matching is not only theoretically beneficial but also practically accessible for fast diffusion sampling.
