摘要Abstract

法学硕士越来越多地被部署为能够访问工具、数据库和外部服务的自主代理，但从业者（跨不同部门）缺乏系统方法来评估已知威胁类别如何转化为特定代理部署中的具体风险。我们呈现LLMs are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents with access to tools, databases, and external services, yet practitioners (across different sectors) lack systematic methods to assess how known threat classes translate into concrete risks within a specific agentic deployment. We present马特拉MATRA是一个针对代理 AI 系统的实用威胁建模框架，它采用既定的风险评估方法来系统地评估已知的 LLM 威胁如何转化为特定于部署的风险。, a pragmatic threat modeling framework for agentic AI systems that adapts established risk assessment methodology to systematically assess how known LLM threats translate into deployment-specific risks.马特拉MATRA从基于资产的影响评估开始，并利用攻击树来确定系统架构内发生这些影响的可能性。我们展示begins with an asset-based impact assessment and utilizes attack trees to determine the likelihood of these impacts occurring within the system architecture. 4 结论Conclusions
