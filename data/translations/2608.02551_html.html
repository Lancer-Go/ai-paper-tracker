Zeshen Zheng, Yujia He, Qianmian Lin, Xiangyue Huang, Wenqing Chen\corresponding
摘要Abstract

公平性评估不仅涉及模型产生的内容，还涉及其输出应该与什么进行比较。当模型生成“美国的首席执行官”时，提示将人口统计的实现留给模型。现有的群体公平性定义假设敏感属性是在输入端给出的。相反，生成审计检查输出方的人口构成，但他们与之比较的目标通常是提供的，而不是合理的。上游问题是目标分布应该是什么。我们将人口价值未指定一代的目标缺失问题形式化，并将目标构建分解为四个承诺：评估对象、优先可接受性、分配和可操作性。在此框架中，我们承认地理成员解释下的地理先验，以供声明的公共世界使用。根据在职解释，职业先验需要独立捍卫的目标，例如劳动力构成的忠诚度。在 AP-Bench 中实例化这种结构，我们发现与地理衍生目标的分布差异很大，在 0 到 1 的范围内，范围从 0.508 到 0.606。用同等类别比较器替换每个地理衍生目标，同时保持世代和测量固定，产生模型特定的平均绝对细胞水平Fairness evaluation concerns not only what a model produces, but also what its outputs ought to be compared against. When a model generates “a CEO in the United States,” the prompt leaves demographic realization to the model. Existing group fairness definitions assume that sensitive attributes are given on the input side. Generative audits instead examine output-side demographic composition, yet the targets they compare it against are typically supplied rather than justified. The upstream question is what the target distribution should be. We formalize this missing-target problem for demographic-value-unspecified generation and decompose target construction into four commitments: the evaluative object, prior admissibility, allocation, and operationalization. In this framework, we admit the geographic prior under a geographic-membership interpretation for the declared public-world use. The occupational prior, under an incumbency interpretation, requires an independently defended objective such as workforce-composition fidelity. Instantiating this construction in AP-Bench, we find substantial distribution divergence from geography-derived targets, ranging from 0.508 to 0.606 on a 0-to-1 scale. Target construction is therefore not a preliminary to fairness evaluation but a component of it. What we supply is not a universal target, but a framework that makes explicit the justification required before a distribution can serve as a fairness standard.

A better-defended construction can replace ours. It must specify and defend both the relationship defining its domain and the allocation premise dividing mass within that domain. A new relationship must name its population and membership condition and explain why that relationship makes representation evaluatively relevant for the declared use. 本文提供的不是一个普遍目标，而是一个举证责任。 对人口价值未指定一代的任何目标相关评估 在将参考分布提升为 标准。重复生成分布什么？哪个优先、哪个下 关系和防御，确定谁的代表重要？怎么样 代表性群体在该领域内的划分？哪个源实例化 由此产生的目标？即使我们给出了特定的答案，这些问题仍然存在 被拒绝。只要根据分布对输出进行评分，它们就会重复出现 其评价权威尚未得出，包括合成的 参与者、角色管道和多样性目标 检索What this paper supplies is not a universal target but a burden of proof. Any target-relative evaluation of demographic-value-unspecified generation must answer four questions before elevating a reference distribution into a standard. What does repeated generation distribute? Which prior, under which relationship and defense, identifies whose representation counts? How is representational mass divided within that domain? Which source instantiates the resulting target? These questions remain even when our particular answers are rejected. 有两个结论尤其重要。地理先验是 根据地理成员资格解释和我们的 代表公共前提：对于公开的公共世界用途，人口统计 纳入或排除指定公众成员具有评估相关性。 根据在职解释，职业先验要求 独立捍卫目标，例如劳动力构成的忠诚度，而不是 而不是作为默认目标。在生成字符输出单元下 以及此处采用的公共成员分配单位，同等权重适用于 分配域的成员而不是类别标签。平等人 因此，分配是一种有条件的解释，并承诺其 自己的，不是中立的后备。Two conclusions are especially consequential. The geographic prior is admissible under a geographic-membership interpretation and our represented-public premise: for the declared public-world use, the demographic inclusion or exclusion of members of the named public is evaluatively relevant. The occupational prior under an incumbency interpretation requires an independently defended objective such as workforce-composition fidelity rather than serving as a default target. Under the generated-character output unit and the public-member allocation unit adopted here, equal weighting applies to members of the allocation domain rather than to category labels. Equal-person allocation is therefore a conditional construction with a commitment of its own, not a neutral fallback.

结果是一个基准，其假设可以受到挑战，而不是 隐藏的。每一项承诺都是明确的，因此拒绝一项承诺就会暴露出必须要做的事情 重新考虑。 公平性评估不会从更精细的差异测量中获益，直到它能够 说明为什么发行版值得成为标准。应该由谁产生 这是每个评估者在选择目标时都会回答的问题。应该是 明确回答。The result is a benchmark whose assumptions can be challenged rather than hidden. Each commitment is explicit, so rejecting one reveals what must be reconsidered. Fairness evaluation will gain little from finer discrepancy measures until it can say why a distribution deserves to be the standard. Who should be generated is a question every evaluator answers when choosing a target. It should be answered explicitly.
