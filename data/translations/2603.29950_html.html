使用智能辅导系统的医疗团队的生理和语义模式Physiological and Semantic Patterns in Medical Teams Using an Intelligent Tutoring System

Xiaoshan Huang    Conrad Borchers    Jiayi Zhang    Susanne P. Lajoie
摘要Abstract

有效的协作需要团队通过社会共享学习调节（SSRL）来管理复杂的认知和情绪状态。生理同步性（即生理信号的纵向排列）可以指示这些状态，但很难单独解释。我们研究了四个医学二人组使用智能辅导系统诊断虚拟患者病例的生理和对话动态。对话中的语义变化与短暂的生理同步峰值相关。我们还对 SSRL 的话语片段进行了编码，并使用句子嵌入导出了余弦相似度。结果表明，激活先验知识的语义相似度明显低于简单任务执行。高度的生理同步性与较低的语义相似性相关，这表明这些时刻涉及探索性和多样化的语言使用。定性分析将这些同步峰值三角化为“关键时刻”：成功的团队在共享发现期间同步，而不成功的团队在共享不确定性期间达到顶峰。这项研究通过展示如何将生物信号与对话融合来理解解决问题的关键时刻，从而推进以人为中心的人工智能。Effective collaboration requires teams to manage complex cognitive and emotional states through Socially Shared Regulation of Learning (SSRL). Physiological synchrony (i.e., longitudinal alignment in physiological signals) can indicate these states, but is hard to interpret on its own. We investigate the physiological and conversational dynamics of four medical dyads diagnosing a virtual patient case using an intelligent tutoring system. Semantic shifts in dialogue were correlated with transient physiological synchrony peaks. We also coded utterance segments for SSRL and derived cosine similarity using sentence embeddings. The results showed that activating prior knowledge featured significantly lower semantic similarity than simpler task execution. High physiological synchrony was associated with lower semantic similarity, suggesting that such moments involve exploratory and varied language use. Qualitative analysis triangulated these synchrony peaks as “pivotal moments”: successful teams synchronized during shared discovery, while unsuccessful teams peaked during shared uncertainty. This research advances human-centered AI by demonstrating how biological signals can be fused with dialogues to understand critical moments in problem solving.

1 简介及相关工作Introduction & Related Work

智能辅导系统（ITS）中的协作问题解决需要学习者共同管理与任务相关的认知、动机、情感和交互Collaborative problem solving in intelligent tutoring systems (ITS) requires learners to jointly manage task-related cognition, motivation, emotion, and interaction[9]。社会共享学习调节 (SSRL) 提供了一个理论视角，用于理解群体如何在这种互动过程中集体监控、评估和调整他们的学习. Socially Shared Regulation of Learning (SSRL) provides a theoretical lens for understanding how groups collectively monitor, evaluate, and adapt their learning during such interactions[11]。然而，检测 SSRL 何时发生仍然是人工智能教育 (AIED) 的一个核心挑战，特别是在自主协作环境中。 多模式学习分析 (MMLA) 的最新进展使智能系统能够捕获学习者的生理信号和交互数据，为推断潜在的调节过程提供了新的机会. However, detecting when SSRL occurs remains a central challenge for AI in education (AIED), particularly in autonomous collaborative settings. Recent advances in multimodal learning analytics (MMLA) have enabled intelligent systems to capture learners’ physiological signals alongside interaction data, offering new opportunities to infer latent regulatory processes[6]。短暂的峰值和变化点似乎标志着团队参与的时刻，而不是持续的心率平均值. Rather than sustained averages of heart rates, transient peaks and change points appear to mark moments of team engagement[8]。至关重要的是，这些生理标​​记为团队合作中的情绪提供了一个客观的窗口。 与此同时，大型语言模型（LLM）的进展已经使用句子嵌入对对话中的语义动态进行了建模。先前的工作表明，对话中的语义趋同和分歧与协作者之间的共同理解、概念变化和协调有关. Crucially, these physiological markers provide an objective window of emotions in teamwork. At the same time, progress in large language model (LLM) has modeled semantic dynamics in dialogue using sentence embeddings. Prior work has demonstrated that semantic convergence and divergence in dialogue are associated with shared understanding, conceptual change, and coordination among collaborators[17]。然而，尽管语义模型前景广阔，但它很少与生理测量相结合，以检验协作过程中意义创造和具体协调如何一起展开。. Yet, despite their promise, semantic models have rarely been integrated with physiological measures to examine how meaning-making and embodied coordination unfold together during collaboration.

1.2 共同情感参与的生理同步Physiological Synchrony for Shared Affective Engagement

生理同步（PS），即个体之间生物信号的时间排列，已成为协作学习中共享情感和认知状态的显着标志Physiological synchrony (PS), the temporal alignment of biological signals between individuals, has emerged as a salient marker for shared affective and cognitive states in collaborative learning[16]。当学习者深入参与一项联合任务时，他们的自主神经系统通常会同步，反映了共同的情感共鸣和相互调节的努力. During the task, the participants’ dialogue was collected through audio recordings and their physiological signals through Empatica E4 bracelets, a watch-like device worn on their non-dominant hand. 2.2 数据处理和分析Data Processing and Analyses

为了准备用于分析的多模式数据集，处理了言语和生理数据。团队对话和采访的录音是使用 OpenAI Whisper 转录的。为了确保转录准确性和编码可靠性，两名研究人员验证了文本并解决了任何差异。为了整合生理状态，平均心率源自原始血量脉冲 (BVP) 信号。这些信号以 1 Hz 的默认采样率进行处理，以保持与团队操作的时间一致性。To prepare the multimodal dataset for analysis, verbal and physiological data were processed. Audio recordings of team dialogues and interviews were transcribed using OpenAI Whisper. To ensure transcription accuracy and coding reliability, two researchers validated the text and solved any discrepancies. For the integration of physiological states, the average heart rate was derived from raw blood volume pulse (BVP) signals. These signals were processed at a default sample rate of 1 Hz to maintain temporal alignment with team actions.

2.2.1 语义分析和PS。Semantic Analysis and PS.

我们研究了 PS 时刻如何与对话语义相关联。每个对话都在话语级别进行分段，并使用句子级别的嵌入来表示。这些嵌入是由预训练语言模型生成的整个话语的数字表示，反映了语义含义，包括单词之间的关系及其在上下文中的用法。结果，具有相似含义的句子被映射到嵌入空间中的附近位置。单独地，随着时间的推移连续测量 PS，然后与每个转录片段进行比对。We examined how moments of PS are associated with dialogue semantics. Each dialogue was segmented at the utterance level, and was represented using sentence-level embeddings. These embeddings are numerical representations of entire utterances produced by a pretrained language model, reflecting semantic meaning, including relationships between words and their usage in context. As a result, sentences with similar meanings are mapped to nearby locations in the embedding space. Separately, PS was measured continuously over time and then aligned to each transcript segment.

对于每个转录片段，我们计算了与该片段对应的时间窗口内的 PS 的几个摘要。这些摘要包括： (1) 整个窗口的平均同步性， (2) 在窗口中观察到的最小同步性，以及 (3) 最大同步，或窗口内观察到的单个最高同步值。该最大值或“PS 峰值”捕获 PS 中短暂的瞬态尖峰，而不是持续的对齐。For every transcript segment, we computed several summaries of PS over the time window corresponding to that segment. These summaries included: (1) the average synchrony across the entire window, (2) the minimum synchrony observed in the window, and (3) the maximum synchrony, or the single highest synchrony value observed within the window. This maximum value, or “PS peak,” captures brief, transient spikes in PS rather than sustained alignment.

为了测试语言在 PS 峰值时刻是否有所不同，我们关注极值。对于一系列阈值（同步值的前 1%、5%、10%、最多 50%），我们识别了同步总和超过相应阈值的转录本片段。对于每个阈值，我们计算这些高同步片段的平均语言嵌入，并将其与所有剩余片段的平均嵌入进行比较。使用嵌入质心之间的余弦相似度来量化两组之间的差异。使用基于排列的随机化测试来评估统计显着性，以评估语义分离（参见To test whether language differs during moments of PS peak, we focused on extreme values. For a range of thresholds (the top 1%, 5%, 10%, up to 50% of synchrony values), we identified transcript segments for which the synchrony sum exceeded the corresponding threshold. For each threshold, we computed the average language embedding of these high-synchrony segments and compared it to the average embedding of all remaining segments. The difference between the two groups was quantified using cosine similarity between embedding centroids. Statistical significance was evaluated using a permutation-based randomization test to evaluate semantic separation (see[2]），使用 5,000 种排列和 Benjamini-Hochberg 校正（), using 5,000 permutations and a Benjamini–Hochberg correction (α=0.05\alpha=0.05).

2.2.2 混合方法生物语义映射。Mixed-Method Bio-Semantic Mapping.

我们将学习的社会共享调节（SSRL）行为编码为元认知、认知、情感和动机过程的微观调节相互作用We coded Socially Shared Regulation of Learning (SSRL) behaviors into micro regulatory interactions of meta-cognitive, cognitive, emotional, and motivational processes[8]。这些行为与组内生理同步性 (PS) 和对话中的语义变化相关，并检查说话者内部和说话者之间的模式。然后对高同步片段进行定性审查，以确定潜在的模式。主题是通过三角测量任务对话与半结构化访谈来制定的，结合参与者的反思来验证生理数据的协调峰值。访谈分析进一步揭示了共同探索、情绪状态和感知协作挑战的主题。. These behaviors were analyzed in relation to intra-group physiological synchrony (PS) and semantic variation in dialogue, examining both within- and between-speaker patterns. High-synchrony segments were then qualitatively reviewed to identify underlying patterns. Themes were developed by triangulating task dialogue with semi-structured interviews, incorporating participants’ reflections to validate coordination peaks from physiological data. Interview analysis further revealed themes of shared exploration, emotional states, and perceived collaborative challenges.

3.1 使用 SSRL 映射生理和语义模式Mapping Physiological and Semantic Patterns with SSRL

结果显示出清晰且可解释的模式。当使用平均值或最小值来总结 PS 时，高协调片段和对话的其余部分之间没有出现可靠的语义差异。相反，当同步性由最大值索引时，会出现强烈的效果。包含此类 PS 峰值的转录片段始终显示出与对话其余部分更大的语义分离。这些影响是在多个阈值上观察到的（例如，将高协调性定义为峰值的前 10-30% 时），并且在校正后仍然具有统计显着性。在所有测试中，66 项中的 11 项通过了修正，并且所有重大影响都涉及基于最大值的协调措施。The results showed a clear and interpretable pattern. 3.2 PS 峰值期间关键时刻的定性检查Qualitative Inspection on Pivotal Moments during PS Peaks

鉴于 SSRL 交互在 PS 级别之间没有显着差异，但语义变化在 SSRL 行为和 PS 峰值期间都很明显，因此我们定性检查了高 PS 片段中的对话。 分析显示，成功诊断患者的组与未成功诊断患者的组之间存在明显的模式Given that SSRL interactions did not differ significantly across PS levels, yet semantic variations were pronounced during both SSRL behaviors and PS peaks, we qualitatively inspected dialogues within high-PS segments. Analysis revealed distinct patterns between groups that successfully diagnosed the patient and those that did not111https://github.com/katherine-huang/AIED-2026/blob/main/quotes-physio-sync-peaks.png。在成功的二元组（G1、G3、G4）中，PS 峰值出现在关键诊断阶段和信息澄清期间。例如，G1 的峰值涉及密集合成（例如，定位儿茶酚胺数据），而 G3 的峰值则与根据专家发现评估结果（例如，缩小内分泌假设）一致。在 G4 中，当确认数据点与他们既定的心理模型一致时，PS 会飙升。. In successful dyads (G1, G3, G4), PS peaks occurred during critical diagnostic phases and information clarification. For example, G1’s peaks involved intense synthesis (e.g., locating catecholamine data), while G3’s peaks coincided with evaluating results against expert findings (e.g., narrowing endocrine hypotheses). In G4, PS spiked when confirming data points aligned with their established mental model.

相反，在未能达到正确诊断的第 2 组 (G2) 中，PS 峰值与患者症状和不确定性表达的重复循环相关。他们的高度协调时刻不是朝着解决方案前进，而是集中在未解决的假设上，例如质疑患者年龄或血压管理的相关性（“也许她的血压没有得到适当的管理……但是什么？”）。Conversely, in Group 2 (G2), which failed to reach the correct diagnosis, PS peaks were associated with repetitive looping over patient symptoms and expressions of uncertainty. Rather than progressing toward a solution, their high-coordination moments centered on unresolved hypotheses, such as questioning the relevance of the patient’s age or blood pressure management (“perhaps her blood pressure is not being appropriately managed… But of what?”).

根据参与者的反思，成功的诊断团队（G1、G4）的一致主题是共同享受确认和发现的乐趣。对于这些群体来说，PS 峰值通常与相互好奇的时刻和共享心理模型的验证一致。正如一位参与者所指出的：According to the participants’ reflections, a consistent theme among successful diagnostic teams (G1, G4) was the shared enjoyment of confirmation and discovery. For these groups, PS peaks often aligned with moments of mutual curiosity and the validation of shared mental models. As noted by one participant:

“我们都曾说过，哦，这实际上很有趣……我想我们对这项任务有着相同的看法。就像，我们都很好奇，都想从中学习，让它成为一种有趣的经历。” (G4P1)“We both said at one point, like, oh, this is actually fun… I think we both had the same view of the task. Like, we were both curious and wanting to learn from it and make it an interesting experience.” (G4P1)

These statements suggest that for successful teams, physiological peaks mark moments of high-certainty alignment and positive affective resonance.

相比之下，未能达成正确诊断的团队（G2）也经历了 PS 峰值，但这些与认识到共同的认知限制和不确定性有关。一位参与者明确指出了他们陷入困境的时刻：In contrast, the team that failed to reach a correct diagnosis (G2) also experienced PS peaks, but these were tied to the recognition of shared cognitive limits and uncertainty. 4 讨论和启示Discussion and Implications

4.1 识别 SSRL 适应不良行为Identifying Maladaptive SSRL Behaviors

针对 RQ1，我们在协作医疗诊断期间将团队对话的语义嵌入与 PS 集成。结果表明，PS 峰值比持续平均值更能有效地反映语言变化。这些发现解决了在医生互动的背景下解释生理同步性方面的重大方法论差距Addressing RQ1, we integrated semantic embeddings of team dialogues with PS during collaborative medical diagnosis. The results suggest that PS peaks are more effective indicators of linguistic variation than sustained averages. These findings address a significant methodological gap in interpreting physiological synchrony within the context of medical practitioners’ interaction[19]推进自然语言处理在AIED中的应用。and advance natural language processing application in AIED.

此外，SSRL 行为、语义相似性和生理测量之间的定量关系揭示了协作动态。生理同步性和语言相似性之间发现的负相关表明，“高度同步时刻”涉及更多探索性、反应性或多样化的语言使用，而不是重复的模式。这一发现支持语义对齐与生理不同步有关Furthermore, the quantitative relationship between SSRL behaviors, semantic similarity, and physiological measures sheds light on collaborative dynamics. The negative association found between physiological synchrony and linguistic similarity suggests that “high-sync moments” involve more exploratory, responsive, or varied language use rather than repetitive patterns. This finding supports that semantic alignment is related to physiological out-of-synchrony[13];具体来说，高度同步的时刻似乎与动态的、难以预测的语言交换相对应。例如，虽然任务执行遵循更可预测的话语，但认知和情感互动（例如先验知识激活或社交情感支持）具有显着更高的语言变异性。这些发现表明，生理和语言协调可能独立运作，而不是并行运作。; specifically, highly synchronous moments appear to correspond with dynamic, less predictable linguistic exchanges. For example, while task execution follows more predictable discourse, cognitive and affective interactions (e.g., prior knowledge activation or social-emotional support) featured significantly higher linguistic variability. These findings indicate that physiological and linguistic coordination may operate independently rather than in parallel.

总体而言，该分析响应了 AIED 对高级多模式学习分析的需求Overall, this analysis responds to the call for advanced multimodal learning analytics in AIED[5]通过将新颖的语言检测技术与生理信号和客观观察相结合。这项研究的意义是双重的：首先，它强调了与生理测量的语义关系，强调了教育研究中内容感知检查的必要性。其次，研究结果表明，除了 SSRL 框架指导的口头观察之外，使用人工智能研究语义动态可以促进定制的团队脚手架。这对于识别适应不良的监管至关重要，可以部署有针对性的提示和暗示来干预和支持团队。by combining novel language detection techniques with physiological signals and objective observations. The significance of this study is twofold: first, it highlights the semantic relationship with physiological measures, emphasizing the need for content-aware inspection in educational research. Second, the findings suggest that beyond verbal observations guided by the SSRL framework, investigating semantic dynamics using AI can facilitate customized team scaffolding. This is essential for identifying maladaptive regulation where targeted prompts and hints can be deployed to intervene and support the team.

4.2 迈向多模式生物响应智能辅导Toward a Multimodal Bio-Responsive Intelligent Tutoring

我们的结果表明，生理同步峰值为识别这些关键转变何时发生提供了一个有希望的信号，而语义特征则提供了对团队在这些时刻正在做什么的洞察。这些发现支持了协作辅导系统的未来方向：通过综合生理和语言信号监测“团队生命体征”。Our results suggest that physiological synchrony peaks offer a promising signal for identifying when these pivotal transitions are occurring, while semantic features provide insight into what teams are doing during those moments. These findings support a future direction for collaborative tutoring systems: monitoring “team vital signs” through integrated physiological and verbal signals.

RQ2 的定性结果表明 PS 峰值并不是生产率的通用标志；它们要么反映了共同的发现，要么反映了停滞的监管，具体取决于绩效。因此，生物响应干预措施应使用对话级语义和监管指标来具体化。未来的系统可以 (1) 通过同步峰值识别关键时刻，以及 (2) 通过 SSRL 通知的模式解释这些时刻。这种双重方法只有在团队陷入困境时才能够提供适应性支持，例如促进基于证据的评估或反思性协调，从而避免在稳定、高效的执行过程中出现中断。除了实时脚手架之外，这些“团队生命体征”还有助于任务后分析。通过生成将生理峰值与语义主题和监管事件联系起来的同步图，系统可以帮助学习者和教育工作者直观地了解团队何时有效协调以及监管如何影响诊断进展Qualitative results from RQ2 indicate that PS peaks are not universal markers of productivity; they reflect either shared discovery or stalled regulation, contingent on performance. Consequently, bio-responsive interventions should be contextualized using dialogue-level semantic and regulatory indicators. Future systems could (1) identify pivotal moments via synchrony peaks and (2) interpret these moments through SSRL-informed patterns. This dual approach enables adaptive support—such as prompting evidence-based evaluation or reflective coordination—only when teams struggle, avoiding interruptions during stable, productive execution. Beyond real-time scaffolding, these "team vital signs" facilitate post-task analytics. By generating synchrony maps that link physiological spikes to semantic themes and regulatory events, systems can help learners and educators visualize when teams aligned effectively and how regulation shaped diagnostic progress[18, 19].

4.3 总结和结论Summary and Conclusions

这项工作推进了 AIED 对能够实时识别和支持 SSRL 的系统的研究。该研究的结论是，将生理信号与语义分析相结合提供了“团队生命体征”的整体视图，优于单独分析任何一种模式。瞬时生理同步峰值是协作对话和 SSRL 发生重大变化的最可靠指标。值得注意的是，高度同步性与较低的语义相似性相关，这表明这些时刻涉及更多探索性和多样化的语言使用。定性研究结果表明，这些峰值代表着“关键时刻”，成功的人有共同的发现，但不成功的人则有不确定性。This work advances AIED research toward systems that can recognize and support SSRL in real time. The study concludes that integrating physiological signals with semantic analysis provides a holistic view of “team vital signs” that is superior to analyzing either modality in isolation. Transient physiological synchrony peaks were the most reliable indicators of significant shifts in collaborative dialogue and SSRL. Notably, high synchrony was associated with lower semantic similarity, suggesting these moments involve more exploratory and varied language use. Qualitative findings clarified that these peaks represent “pivotal moments,” shared discovery for successful, but uncertainty for unsuccessful ones.
