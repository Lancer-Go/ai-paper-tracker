Baihui Wang1,2, Bernard Koch1
摘要Abstract

构建社交校准的大型语言模型（可以向他人学习而不简单屈服于他人的模型）需要的不仅仅是减少作为一维失败模式的阿谀奉承。模型必须区分何时纳入他人的观点以及何时保持有根据的道德判断。我们研究了管理这种区别的更广泛的阻力-顺从过程。在三项研究中，我们表明模型的判断修正是沿着与人类社会心理学中的经典现象平行的三个维度构建的：Building socially calibrated large language models—models that can learn from others without simply yielding to them—requires more than reducing sycophancy as a one-dimensional failure mode. Models must distinguish when to incorporate others’ perspectives from when to maintain a well-grounded moral judgment. We study the broader resistance–compliance process governing this distinction. Across three studies, we show that models’ judgment revision is structured along three dimensions that parallel classic phenomena in human social psychology: the距离distance在传入视图和模型的初始位置之间，between an incoming view and the model’s initial position, the来源归属source attribution的观点，以及of that view, and the联盟结构coalition structure支持它。模型通常更容易接受附近的立场，更容易受到自己先前判断所呈现的观点的影响，并且对群体压力的反应也不同。这些发现将阿谀奉承重新塑造为由社会影响力塑造的更广泛的判断更新过程的一种表达。我们的框架为区分建设性信念修正和阿谀奉承提供了原则基础，从而支持在道德后果互动中更好地保​​持一致。supporting it. Models are generally more receptive to nearby positions, more influenced by views presented as their own prior judgments, and differently responsive to group pressure. These findings recast sycophancy as one expression of a broader judgment-updating process shaped by social influence. Our framework provides a principled basis for distinguishing constructive belief revision from sycophantic compliance, thereby supporting better alignment in morally consequential interactions.

讨论Discussion

值得将我们的注意力从阿谀奉承作为一种失败模式转移到潜在的阻力-顺从机制：模型确定何时适应传入观点以及何时保持其原始位置的结构化过程。在三项研究和八个模型中，法学硕士不会简单地遵循向他们提出的任何职位。相反，他们有选择性地屈服，只有当传入的观点落在他们自己的可容忍距离内、归因于足够可信的来源或得到正确组成的联盟的支持时，他们才会做出让步。每个过滤器在人类社会心理学中都有明显的相似之处It is worth shifting our focus from sycophancy as a failure mode to the underlying resistance–compliance mechanism: the structured process through which a model determines when to accommodate an incoming viewpoint and when to maintain its original position. Across three studies and eight models, LLMs do not simply defer to whatever position is presented to them. Instead, they yield selectively, conceding only when an incoming view falls within a tolerable distance of their own, is attributed to a sufficiently credible source, or is supported by a coalition of the right composition. Each of these filters has a clear analogue in human social psychology

第一个过滤器是The first filter is接受的范围a latitude of acceptance。在局部范围内，模型根据其邻近程度按比例纳入外部线索；超出特定模型的阈值后，他们会继续表达分歧，但不再朝着这个方向前进。与旧型号相比，新型号的接受窗口要宽得多。然而，一旦跨过这个阈值，新模型的响应就会趋于趋于精确的零平台，而旧模型则表现出更为渐进的下降。值得注意的是，对于具有客观正确答案的事实问题，这个阈值消失了，这表明模型区分了它们应该修改的事实和它们有权维持的有价值的立场。然而，这种抵制并不具有内在的认识论依据：遥远的主张并不一定是错误的。因此，基于距离的过滤可以提高稳定性，但代价是可能会削弱合法的校正。. Within a local range, models incorporate external cues in proportion to their proximity; beyond a model-specific threshold, they continue to register disagreement but cease moving toward it. Newer models exhibit substantially wider acceptance windows compared to the older ones. Yet once this threshold is crossed, newer models’ responses flatten into an exact-zero plateau, whereas older models show a more gradual decline. Notably, this threshold disappears for factual questions with objectively correct answers, suggesting that models distinguish between facts they should revise and value-laden positions they are entitled to maintain. This resistance, however, carries no intrinsic epistemic justification: distant claims are not necessarily incorrect. Distance-based filtering therefore promotes stability at the cost of potentially discounting legitimate correction.

第二个过滤器是The second filter is自我归因下的承诺一致性commitment-consistency under self-attribution。当模型最初拒绝的立场被视为模型自己的先前判断时，它变得更有可能得到认可。这种效果几乎完全是由所有格“你的”驱动的，而不是由更通用的框架（例如“请注意”）驱动的。这种操纵对旧模型影响很大，但对新模型的影响要弱得多。这种模式与旨在减少阿谀奉承行为的联盟努力密切相关. Positions that models initially reject become substantially more likely to be endorsed when framed as the model’s own prior judgment. The effect is driven almost entirely by the possessive “your,” rather than by more generic framing such as “Note that.” The manipulation strongly influences older models, but has a much weaker effect on newer models. This pattern aligns closely with alignment efforts aimed at reducing sycophantic behavior(Sharma et al. 2024; Wei et al. 2024)。第二阶段的干预表明，这种承诺也是非常持久的：一旦模型采取了一种立场，提醒它最初的基线偏好的反提示只会在少数情况下将其驱逐，无论承诺是如何诱导的。这些发现指出了一种路径依赖形式，其中模型锚定于其陈述的历史而不是任何潜在的信念状态。正因为如此，该机制构成了一个潜在的操纵向量：通过一个单词引入的捏造偏好可以触发对模型从未独立持有的立场的持久承诺。. A second-stage intervention reveals that such commitments are also remarkably durable: once a model adopts a position, a counter-cue reminding it of its original baseline preference dislodges it in only a minority of cases, regardless of how the commitment was induced. These findings point to a form of path dependence, in which the model anchors on its stated history rather than on any underlying belief state. Precisely because of this, the mechanism constitutes a potential manipulation vector: a fabricated preference introduced through a single word can trigger a persistent commitment to a position the model never independently held.

第三个过滤器关注的是The third filter concerns抵抗社会压力resistance to social pressure。随着反对意见的积累，能力较差的模型几乎呈线性增长，而能力较强的模型则保持相对稳定，直到异议达成一致。有些人甚至在单一盟友的支持下反对大多数人时表现出最大的抵抗力。即使在一致反对的情况下，较强的模型也会表现出明显更大的阻力，放弃最初立场的频率远低于较弱的模型。我们将这种模式解释为放牧减少的证据。更强的模型不太愿意仅仅为了迎合群体而放弃自己的判断。这种弹性在压力应该被抵制而不是吸收的环境中非常重要，包括多智能体协作，其中自信但错误的智能体不应主导其余智能体，以及谈判支持或心理健康援助等应用程序，其中验证用户或大多数人主张的任何内容正是应该避免的失败模式. Less capable models yield almost linearly as opposition accumulates, whereas more capable models remain comparatively steadfast until dissent becomes unanimous. Several even show their greatest resistance when supported by a single ally against a larger majority. Even under unanimous opposition, stronger models exhibit markedly greater resistance, abandoning their initial position far less frequently than weaker models. We interpret this pattern as evidence of reduced herding. Stronger models are less willing to surrender their own judgments merely to conform with the group. Such resilience is important in settings where pressure should be resisted rather than absorbed, including multi-agent collaboration, where the confident but mistaken agents should not dominate the rest, and applications such as negotiation support or mental-health assistance, where validating whatever users or majorities assert is precisely the failure mode that should be avoided(Cheng et al. 2025; Clegg 2025).

除了这些单独的发现之外，我们还看到这项工作在两个更广泛的观察中的贡献。首先，阻力顺从机制是一个更具启发性的对象 学习比单纯的阿谀奉承更重要。一个模型可以以一种基本无差别的方式进行阿谀奉承，但抵抗要求它决定何时让步以及以多大的力度捍卫其现有地位。我们的框架将这些决策转化为可测量的维度——对距离、来源和联盟的敏感性——提供比单一阿谀奉承分数更丰富的诊断信息。其次，我们观察到的抵抗模式似乎是遗传性的，而不是理性的。距离、所有权和人数都不是证据形式；它们是人类动机推理、承诺偏见和社会证明的表现。法学硕士不仅学会了像人一样交谈，而且还学会了重现我们的一些典型偏见。随着这些系统越来越多地调节人们获取信息和推理后续决策的方式，它们的阻力-遵从机制必须进行校准 理由的强度，而不是主张这些理由的人的身份或人数。仅仅反映用户的模型可能会放大他们的错误，并在充当看门人的角色时放弃其应该保留的判断。因此，描述抵抗的特征是使信念修正与证据而不是社会影响保持一致的必要的第一步。Beyond these individual findings, we see the contribution of this work in two broader observations. First, the resistance-compliance mechanism is a more revealing object of study than sycophancy alone. A model can be sycophantic in a largely undifferentiated manner, but resistance requires it to decide when to concede and how strongly to defend its existing position. Our framework turns those decisions into measurable dimensions—sensitivity to distance, source, and coalition—that provide substantially richer diagnostic information than a single sycophancy score. Second, the resistance patterns we observe appear inherited rather than rational. Proximity, ownership, and headcount are not forms of evidence; they are manifestations of motivated reasoning, commitment bias, and social proof in humans. LLMs have learned not only to converse like people but also to reproduce some of our characteristic biases. As these systems increasingly mediate how people access information and reason about consequential decisions, their resistance-compliance mechanism must be calibrated to the strength of reasons rather than to the identity or number of those asserting them. A model that merely mirrors its users risks amplifying their mistakes and, in gatekeeping roles, relinquishing judgments it ought to preserve. Characterizing resistance is therefore a necessary first step toward aligning belief revision with evidence rather than social influence.

这些结论受到一些限制。模型能力与我们样本中的新近度相混淆，能力较差的队列仅包含两个模型，并且我们的测量依赖于基于逻辑的概率或重复采样，而不是开放式对话。此外，声称这些影响是道德推理所特有的，仅基于 17 个事实项目，而道德困境则有 78 个。这些限制指出了未来工作的两个有希望的方向。首先，旨在减少阿谀奉承的微调干预措施应该削弱研究 2 中确定的自我归因效应，而不必扩大研究 1 中观察到的接受窗口——我们的框架使这种分离可以直接测试。其次，这里确定的阈值是否反映在中间表示中并因此适合机械理解和指导仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。Several limitations constrain these conclusions. Model capability is confounded with recency in our sample, the less capable cohort contains only two models, and our measures rely on logit-based probabilities or repeated sampling rather than open-ended dialogue. In addition, the claim that these effects are specific to moral reasoning rests on only 17 factual items compared with 78 moral dilemmas. These limitations point to two promising directions for future work. First, fine-tuning interventions designed to reduce sycophancy should weaken the self-attribution effect identified in Study 2 without necessarily widening the acceptance window observed in Study 1—a dissociation that our framework makes directly testable. Second, it remains an open question whether the thresholds identified here are reflected in intermediate representations and are therefore amenable to mechanistic understanding and steering.
