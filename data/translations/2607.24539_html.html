用于电网诊断的多模式法学硕士的任务条件忠实度审计Task-Conditional Faithfulness Auditing of Multimodal LLMs for Grid Diagnosis

Tianqiao Zhao, , Meng Yue, , and Jianhui Wang T. Zhao is with The University of Texas at Arlington, Arlington, TX, USA. M. Yue is with Brookhaven National Laboratory, Upton, NY, USA. J. Wang is with Southern Methodist University, Dallas, TX, USA.
摘要Abstract

多模态大语言模型 (LLM) 可以结合拓扑、测量和事件文本进行网格诊断，但答案准确性并不能证明使用了适合任务的证据。这封信提出了进行任务条件忠诚度审计的总体框架。它比较了自我报告的依赖、干预产生的行为依赖和预先注册的工程重要性。该框架首先记录特定任务的证据要求，并将其与受控模态消融下自我报告的依赖和行为变化进行比较。为了解决检测到的差异，我们设计了一种证据门控的纠正和重新审核机制，可以在证据约束下重新生成失败的响应，并独立地重新消融它们，以验证改进的基础而不损失性能。案例研究评估了 IEEE 39 和 118 总线场景中三种不同规模的法学硕士。这些结果验证了框架检测、诊断和纠正任务条件忠实性故障的能力。Multimodal large language models (LLMs) can combine topology, measurements, and incident text for grid diagnosis, yet answer accuracy does not establish that task-appropriate evidence was used. This letter proposes a general framework in order to conduct task-conditional faithfulness audit. It compares self-reported reliance, intervention-derived behavioral reliance, and preregistered engineering importance. The framework first registers task-specific evidence requirements and compares them with self-reported reliance and behavioral changes under controlled modality ablations. To resolve detected discrepancies, we design an evidence-gated correction and re-audit mechanism that regenerates failed responses under evidence constraints and independently re-ablates them to verify improved grounding without performance loss. Case studies evaluate three differently scaled LLMs on IEEE 39- and 118-bus scenarios. These results validate the framework ability to detect, diagnose, and correct task-conditional faithfulness failures.

I 简介Introduction

基于法学硕士的电网决策支持正在电力调度、 数字异常检测和多模式能源管理系统 安全LLM-based grid decision support is emerging across power dispatch, numeric anomaly detection, and multimodal energy-management-system security[2, 3, 1]。相关 电力系统故障诊断研究也证明了 结合异构测量而不是依赖单一测量 信息来源. Related power-system fault-diagnosis studies also demonstrate the value of combining heterogeneous measurements rather than relying on a single information source[4]。多式联运可用性， 然而，并不能保证证据的使用适合任务。一个正确的 事件标签可以从事件叙述中复制，即使 分支状态或动态证据被忽略。这很重要，因为 拓扑识别、安全筛查和影响评估 需要不同的证据途径。更广泛地说，合理的模型 解释可能无法反映推动预测的因素，并且 多模式模型可能过度依赖一种模式. Multimodal availability, however, does not guarantee task-appropriate evidence use. A correct event label may be copied from an incident narrative even when branch-status or dynamic evidence is ignored. This matters because topology identification, security screening, and impact estimation require different evidence pathways. More broadly, plausible model explanations may not reflect the factors that drive a prediction, and multimodal models can over-rely on one modality[5, 6].

这封信询问多模式网格法学硕士是否使用每项任务所需的证据以及应如何纠正差异。它有助于：1）预先注册的任务合同，将工程证据要求与模型解释分开； 2) 结合响应性和签名模态效应的有效性感知 SR–Abl–Ref 审核； 3）诊断和纠正性重新审核门，需要可衡量的行为改进而不造成重大绩效损失； 4）通过轻量级系统和模型适配器在网格系统和法学硕士之间传输的通用实施管道。三模型 IEEE 39/118 概念证明应用一份冻结的审计合同并明确报告输出有效性，以便格式错误的干预集不会夸大忠诚度。This letter asks whether a multimodal grid LLM uses the evidence required by each task and how discrepancies should be corrected. It contributes: 1) a preregistered task contract that separates engineering evidence requirements from model explanations; 2) a validity-aware SR–Abl–Ref audit combining responsiveness and signed modality effects; 3) a diagnosis and corrective re-audit gate that requires measurable behavioral improvement without material performance loss; and 4) a generic implementation pipeline that transfers across grid systems and LLMs through lightweight system and model adapters. A three-model IEEE 39/118 proof of concept applies one frozen audit contract and reports output validity explicitly so that malformed intervention sets cannot inflate faithfulness.

Ⅱ-EII-E 实施和适应Implementation and Adaptation

该框架使用一个通用的审计引擎和两个轻量级的审计引擎 接口。系统适配器映射网格数据 进入规范输入，生成注册的反事实，并检查 物理一致性。型号适配器手柄 提示、解析、工具语法和解码。任务定义， 参考规则、干预措施、指标、门限和报告仍然存在 已修复，因此新系统或 LLM 只需更换适配器。工作流程注册任务和阈值，验证适配器， 执行匹配的完整输入和消融调用，计算有效性， 响应性、签名效应和 SR–Abl–Ref 分数，然后是路线 未能证明约束、物理工具或人工审查的案例 在独立重新审核之前。格式错误的输出仍然无效，并且执行清单记录了适配器、提示、干预、模型设置、工具输出和 场景标识符。The framework uses a common audit engine with two lightweight interfaces. The system adapter maps grid data into canonical inputs, generates registered counterfactuals, and checks physical consistency. The model adapter handles prompting, parsing, tool syntax, and decoding. Task definitions, reference rules, interventions, metrics, gates, and reporting remain fixed, so a new system or LLM requires only adapter replacement. The workflow registers tasks and thresholds, validates the adapters, executes matched full-input and ablated calls, computes validity, responsiveness, signed effects, and SR–Abl–Ref scores, then routes failed cases to evidence constraints, physics tools, or human review before independent re-auditing. Malformed outputs remain invalid, and an execution manifest records the adapters, prompts, interventions, model settings, tool outputs, and scenario identifiers.

IV 结论Conclusion

在这封信中，我们提出了基于多模式 LLM 的网格诊断的任务条件忠实度审计。该框架联合评估性能、报告和行为依赖、解释保真度、签名模态效应和工程参考重要性。结果表明，报告的一致性可以超过压力下的行为基础，而有针对性的纠正可以提高表现和基础。对快捷方式和冲突文本的特定于模型的响应进一步激发了报告的有效性和响应性以及总体忠实度分数。未来的工作将评估公用事业规模网格工作流程中的审计。In this letter, we proposed a task-conditional faithfulness audit for multimodal LLM-based grid diagnosis. The framework jointly evaluates performance, reported and behavioral reliance, explanation fidelity, signed modality effects, and engineering reference importance. The results show that reported alignment can exceed behavioral grounding under stress, while targeted correction improves both performance and grounding. Model-specific responses to shortcut and conflicting text further motivate reporting validity and responsiveness alongside aggregate faithfulness scores. Future work will evaluate the audit in utility-scale grid workflows.
