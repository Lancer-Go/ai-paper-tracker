PokeGym：视觉语言模型的视觉驱动的长期基准PokeGym: A Visually-Driven Long-Horizon Benchmark for Vision-Language Models

Ruizhi Zhang SIAS, UESTCShenzhenChina , Ye Huang 0000-0001-5668-5529 SIAS, UESTCShenzhenChina , Yuangang Pan 0000-0002-7950-4900 CFAR/IHPC A*STARSingapore , Chuanfu Shen SIAS, UESTCShenzhenChina , Zhilin Liu SIAS, UESTCShenzhenChina , Ting Xie SIAS, UESTCShenzhenChina , Wen Li SIAS, UESTCShenzhenChina and Lixin Duan 0000-0002-0723-4016 SIAS, UESTCShenzhenChina

摘要。Abstract.

虽然视觉语言模型 (VLM) 在静态视觉理解方面取得了显着进展，但它们在复杂的 3D 体现环境中的部署仍然受到严重限制。现有的基准测试存在四个关键缺陷：（1）被动感知任务规避了交互动态； (2) 简化的 2D 环境无法评估深度感知； (3)特权状态泄漏绕过真正的视觉处理； (4) 人工评估成本高昂且无法扩展。 我们推出 PokeGym，这是一个视觉驱动的长期基准测试，在 Pokémon Legends: Z-A（一款视觉复杂的 3D 开放世界角色扮演游戏）中实例化。 PokeGym 强制执行严格的代码级隔离：代理仅根据原始 RGB 观察结果进行操作，而独立评估员则通过内存扫描验证是否成功，确保纯粹基于视觉的决策和自动化、可扩展的评估。该基准包括 30 个任务（30-220 个步骤），涵盖导航、交互和混合场景，具有三种指令粒度（视觉引导、步骤引导、仅目标），系统地解构视觉基础、语义推理和自主探索能力。 我们的评估揭示了当前 VLM 的一个关键限制：物理死锁恢复（而不是高层规划）构成了主要瓶颈，死锁与任务成功呈强烈负相关。 此外，我们发现了一个元认知差异：较弱的模型主要遭受无意识死锁（忘记了陷阱），而高级模型则表现出有意识死锁（认识到陷阱但无法恢复）。 这些发现强调需要将明确的空间直觉集成到 VLM 架构中。 代码和基准测试将在 GitHub 上提供。While Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have achieved remarkable progress in static visual understanding, their deployment in complex 3D embodied environments remains severely limited. Existing benchmarks suffer from four critical deficiencies: (1) passive perception tasks circumvent interactive dynamics; (2) simplified 2D environments fail to assess depth perception; (3) privileged state leakage bypasses genuine visual processing; and (4) human evaluation is prohibitively expensive and unscalable. We introduce PokeGym, a visually-driven long-horizon benchmark instantiated within Pokémon Legends: Z-A, a visually complex 3D open-world Role-Playing Game. PokeGym enforces strict code-level isolation: agents operate solely on raw RGB observations while an independent evaluator verifies success via memory scanning, ensuring pure vision-based decision-making and automated, scalable assessment. The benchmark comprises 30 tasks (30–220 steps) spanning navigation, interaction, and mixed scenarios, with three instruction granularities (Visual-Guided, Step-Guided, Goal-Only) to systematically deconstruct visual grounding, semantic reasoning, and autonomous exploration capabilities. Our evaluation reveals a key limitation of current VLMs: physical deadlock recovery, rather than high-level planning, constitutes the primary bottleneck, with deadlocks showing a strong negative correlation with task success. Furthermore, we uncover a metacognitive divergence: weaker models predominantly suffer from Unaware Deadlocks (oblivious to entrapment), whereas advanced models exhibit Aware Deadlocks (recognizing entrapment yet failing to recover). These findings highlight the need to integrate explicit spatial intuition into VLM architectures. The code and benchmark will be available on GitHub.

1. 简介Introduction

最近的视觉语言模型（VLM）在静态视觉理解和指令跟随方面取得了令人瞩目的进展Recent Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have achieved impressive progress in static visual understanding and instruction following(Dai et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2024; Ma et al., 2024; Ding et al., 2025)。 然而，目前尚不清楚这些功能在多大程度上转化为视觉丰富的 3D 环境中的自主行为. Yet it remains unclear to what extent these capabilities translate into autonomous behavior in visually rich 3D environments(Huang et al., 2023; Yu and Lu, 2024; Das et al., 2018)，智能体必须从像素进行感知，在部分可观察性下行动，并通过持续交互追求长期目标, where agents must perceive from pixels, act under partial observability, and pursue long-horizon goals through continuous interaction(Xi et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024a; Yang et al., 2025b; Lin et al., 2025)。 一个主要障碍是缺乏能够忠实、大规模地评估它的基准。. A central obstacle is the lack of benchmarks that can evaluate it faithfully and at scale.

体现 VLM 代理的有效基准应该共同实现至少四个属性：长视野交互、逼真的 3D 视觉推理、纯视觉观察的决策以及可扩展的自动化评估。 然而，现有协议通常会牺牲以下一个或多个属性：An effective benchmark for embodied VLM agents should jointly enable at least four properties: long-horizon interaction, realistic 3D visual reasoning, decision-making from pure visual observations, and scalable automated evaluation. However, existing protocols typically trade away one or more of these properties:

  1. (1)

    静态图像基准和单轮任务，例如视觉问答 (VQA) 或图像字幕Static image benchmarks and single-turn tasks, such as visual question answering (VQA) or image captioning(Ging et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2025a; Xu et al., 2024; Antol et al., 2015; Mensink et al., 2023)，将评估减少为瞬时识别，并绕过持续规划和控制的挑战, reduce evaluation to momentary recognition and bypass the challenges of persistent planning and control(Wasi et al., 2026; Qiu et al., 2026).

  2. (2)

    2D 游戏或网格世界中的交互式基准测试Interactive benchmarks in 2D games or grid worlds(Pleines et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2026)引入顺序决策，但其简化的视觉效果与现实世界场景的复杂性不匹配，无法捕捉深度感知和 3D 空间推理。introduce sequential decision-making, but their simplified visuals do not match the complexity of real-world scenes, failing to capture depth perception and 3D spatial reasoning.

  3. (3)

    更真实的 3D 环境通常会暴露特权内部状态，例如坐标或符号世界表示More realistic 3D environments often expose privileged internal states, such as coordinates or symbolic world representations(Fan et al., 2022; Dagan et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Zhu et al., 2023; Madge and Poesio, 2024)，允许智能体绕过现实世界视觉智能体必须解决的感知负担。, allowing agents to bypass the perceptual burden that real-world visual agents must solve.

  4. (4)

    相反，将代理限制为纯视觉输入的游戏基准通常依赖于人类评估Conversely, game benchmarks that restrict agents to pure visual inputs frequently rely on human evaluation(Tan et al., 2025c, b; Team et al., 2024; Bolton et al., 2025)，限制了可扩展性、再现性和客观性。, limiting scalability, reproducibility, and objectivity.

因此，现有基准的强劲表现可能并不反映强大的体现能力。As a result, strong performance on existing benchmarks may not reflect robust embodied competence.

This game serves as an ideal testbed because its mechanics mirror the core challenges of real-world embodiment: partial observability forces agents to build spatial memory, navigation and diverse object interactions test fine-grained visual-action grounding, while intricate quest structures and extended temporal dependencies demand robust long-horizon planning and error recovery.

PokeGym 解决了纯视觉真实感和自动评估之间的紧张关系：代理仅根据原始 RGB 观察进行操作，而任务成功则通过使用字节数组 (AOB) 内存扫描的状态提取来独立验证。PokeGym resolves the tension between pure visual realism and automated evaluation: the agent acts solely from raw RGB observations, while task success is verified independently through state extraction using Array of Bytes (AOB) memory scanning.

PokeGym 包含源自 10 个任务的 30 个任务，轨迹范围从 30 到 220 个环境步骤，涵盖导航、交互和混合长视野场景。 每个任务都在三种指令粒度下实例化：视觉引导、步骤引导和仅目标。 这些粒度为解开具体能力创建了一个受控环境：明确提示下的视觉基础、程序指导下的语义推理以及稀疏目标下的自主探索。PokeGym contains 30 tasks derived from 10 quests, with trajectories ranging from 30 to 220 environment steps and covering navigation, interaction, and mixed long-horizon scenarios. Each task is instantiated under three instruction granularities: Visual-Guided, Step-Guided, and Goal-Only. These granularities create a controlled setting for disentangling embodied capabilities: visual grounding under explicit cues, semantic reasoning under procedural guidance, and autonomous exploration under sparse goals.

除了成功率之外，PokeGym 还支持对具体失败的细粒度诊断，凸显了 PokeGym 不仅作为评估套件的价值，而且还作为具体 VLM 研究的诊断测试平台的价值。Beyond success rates, PokeGym also supports fine-grained diagnosis of embodied failures, highlighting the value of PokeGym not only as an evaluation suite, but also as a diagnostic testbed for embodied VLM research.

我们的主要贡献总结如下：Our primary contributions are summarized as follows:

  1. (1)

    我们推出 PokeGym，这是一个视觉驱动的长期基准测试，用于 3D 开放世界游戏中的具体 VLM。 它的机制抓住了现实世界体现的核心挑战。We introduce PokeGym, a visually-driven, long-horizon benchmark for embodied VLMs in a 3D open-world game. Its mechanics capture core challenges of real-world embodiment.

  2. (2)

  3. (3)

    我们建立了一个受控诊断框架，用于理清 VLM 中的关键体现功能。 具体来说，我们设计了 30 个跨三个教学粒度的长期任务，以独立评估视觉基础、语义理解和自主探索。We establish a controlled diagnostic framework for disentangling key embodied capabilities in VLMs. Specifically, we design 30 long-horizon tasks across three instructional granularities to independently assess visual grounding, semantic understanding, and autonomous exploration.

  4. (4)

We further uncover a metacognitive divide between weaker and stronger models when trapped.

  1. (1)

    可自由控制摄像机并改变视角。Freely controllable camera with changing viewpoints.游戏镜头可以旋转，从不同角度观察世界。 这使得观察空间高度依赖于视点：关键目标可能位于屏幕之外、部分被遮挡或只能从特定角度识别。 因此，智能体必须通过转动摄像头、检查附近区域并调整与物体的距离来主动寻找有用的信息，而不是被动地对固定视图做出反应。The game camera can be rotated to view the world from different angles. As a result, the agent must actively look for useful information by turning the camera, checking nearby areas, and adjusting its distance to objects rather than passively reacting to a fixed view.

4.5. 故障原因诊断Failure Causes Diagnosis

前述分析中的物理指标无法解释潜在的认知崩溃： 智能体挣扎是因为它没有注意到碰撞，还是因为它缺乏逃离公认陷阱的空间直觉？Physical metrics in preceding analysis cannot explain the underlying cognitive breakdown: does the agent struggle because it is oblivious to the collision, or because it lacks the spatial intuition to escape an acknowledged trap?

为了回答这个问题，我们使用粒度诊断来弥合代理的宏观语义推理与其微观物理执行之间的差距。 我们将episode失败的根本原因分为四种类型，这几乎可以涵盖我们任务中的失败。 这些类别是通过将主体的主观内部推理与其客观物理状态进行对比来定义的：To answer this, we bridge the gap between the agent’s macro-level semantic reasoning and its micro-level physical execution using a granular diagnosis. We categorize the root causes of episode failures into four types, which can almost cover the failures in our tasks. These categories are defined by contrasting the agent’s subjective internal reasoning against its objective physical state:

  • •

    无意识死锁：Unaware Deadlock:代理在物理上被卡住了，但它却遭受了幻觉的进展。 其内部推理声称路径是清晰的或策略是有效的，完全没有注意到碰撞。The agent is physically stuck, yet it suffers from hallucinated progress. Its internal reasoning claims that the path is clear or the strategy is effective, completely oblivious to the collision.

  • •

    意识到死锁：Aware Deadlock:代理的推理明确地认识到物理僵局。 然而，它所选择的恢复行动未能解决空间陷阱，使其在一个小区域内振荡。The agent’s reasoning explicitly recognizes the physical deadlock. Yet, its chosen recovery actions fail to resolve the spatial trap, keeping it oscillating in a small area.

  • •

    丢失：Lost:代理取得物理进展并不断更新其坐标，但未能在步数限制内达到目标。 推理日志证实目标不可见，表明漫无目的地徘徊。The agent makes physical progress and continuously updates its coordinates, but fails to reach the goal within the step limit. The reasoning log confirms that the target is not visible, indicating aimless wandering.

  • •

    执行失败：Execution Failure:智能体成功探索并声明它在推理中看到了目标。 然而，它在执行方面遇到了困难，在接近过程中卡在相邻的微观几何形状上，或者从范围外向交互按钮发送垃圾邮件。The agent successfully explores and states it sees the target in reasoning. However, it struggles with execution, stuck on adjacent micro-geometry during approach or spamming the interaction button from out of range.

By forcing the judge to compare the agent’s subjective text generation against the ground-truth physical trajectory, GPT-5.2 determines the failure categories, avoiding the high cost and subjective bias in manual analysis.

5. 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 PokeGym，这是一个严格的基准，可以解决具体游戏 VLM 研究中的环境现实性和可扩展评估之间的根本紧张关系。通过实施严格的代码级隔离，代理仅根据原始 RGB 观察结果进行操作，而独立评估者则通过 AOB 内存扫描来验证是否成功。 该设计首次实现了复杂 3D 开放世界游戏中长期视觉驱动决策的自动评估。 我们对 8 个 VLM 的分析表明，物理死锁恢复（而不是高层规划）构成了主要瓶颈，这凸显了将显式空间直觉集成到 VLM 架构中的必要性。 此外，模型层之间已发现的元认知差异，即较弱的模型遭受无意识死锁，而较强的模型则表现出意识死锁，这表明故障缓解策略必须是特定于功能的。We present PokeGym, a rigorous benchmark that resolves the fundamental tension between environmental realism and scalable evaluation in embodied game VLM research. By enforcing strict code-level isolation, agents operate solely on raw RGB observations while an independent evaluator verifies success via AOB memory scanning. This design enables the first automated assessment of long-horizon, visually-driven decision-making in complex 3D open-world games. Our analysis across 8 VLMs reveals that physical deadlock recovery, rather than high-level planning, constitutes the primary bottleneck, highlighting the need to integrate explicit spatial intuition into VLM architectures. Furthermore, the identified metacognitive divergence between model tiers, where weaker models suffer from Unaware Deadlocks while stronger models exhibit Aware Deadlocks, suggests that failure mitigation strategies must be capability-specific.

附录一Appendix I局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

PokeGym 目前专注于纯像素 RGB 观察，它为视觉基础和空间推理提供了一个干净的测试平台，但忽略了其他重要的感官模式。在现实世界环境和复杂的交互环境中，听觉感知是决策的基本渠道，通常传达仅靠视觉无法获得或不那么显着的信息。 例如，在游戏中，音频提示可以发出对话、环境事件、附近威胁和状态变化的信号，这些对于及时有效的行动至关重要。 因此，未来的一个自然方向是通过实时音频输入来增强基准，从而能够评估真正的多模式具体代理，并使环境更接近人类在世界上的感知和行为方式。PokeGym currently focuses on pure-pixel RGB observations, which provides a clean testbed for visual grounding and spatial reasoning but omits other important sensory modalities. In both real-world settings and complex interactive environments, auditory perception is a fundamental channel for decision-making, often conveying information that is unavailable or less salient in vision alone. In games, for example, audio cues can signal dialogue, environmental events, nearby threats, and changes in state that are crucial for timely and effective action. A natural future direction is therefore to augment the benchmark with real-time audio input, enabling the evaluation of genuinely multi-modal embodied agents and bringing the setting closer to how humans perceive and act in the world.
