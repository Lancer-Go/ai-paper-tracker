叙事地图意义建构的语义交互：基于洞察力的评估Semantic Interaction for Narrative Map Sensemaking: An Insight-based Evaluation

Brian Felipe Keith-Norambuena    Fausto German    Eric Krokos    Sarah Joseph    Chris North Universidad Católica del Norte, Av. Angamos 0610, Antofagasta, 1270709, Chile Virginia Tech, 620 Drillfield Drive, Blacksburg, VA, 24061, USA U.S. Government, Washington, D.C. 20500, USA
(2026)
摘要Abstract

语义交互 (SI) 使分析师能够通过直接操作可视化将其认知过程整合到人工智能模型中。虽然已经提出了用于叙事提取的 SI 框架，但对其有效性的实证评估仍然有限。本文提出了一项评估 SI 用于叙事地图意义建构的用户研究，涉及 33 名参与者，在三种条件下进行：时间线基线、基本叙事地图和具有 SI 功能的交互式叙事地图。结果表明，基于地图的原型比时间线基线产生了更多的见解，启用 SI 的条件达到了统计显着性，并且基本地图条件趋势相同。 SI 启用条件显示出最高的平均性能；地图条件之间的差异不具有统计显着性，但显示出较大的效应量（Semantic interaction (SI) enables analysts to incorporate their cognitive processes into AI models through direct manipulation of visualizations. While SI frameworks for narrative extraction have been proposed, empirical evaluations of their effectiveness remain limited. This paper presents a user study that evaluates SI for narrative map sensemaking, involving 33 participants under three conditions: a timeline baseline, a basic narrative map, and an interactive narrative map with SI capabilities. The results show that the map-based prototypes yielded more insights than the timeline baseline, with the SI-enabled condition reaching statistical significance and the basic map condition trending in the same direction. The SI-enabled condition showed the highest mean performance; differences between the map conditions were not statistically significant but showed large effect sizes (d>0.8d>0.8），表明该研究检测它们的能力不足。定性分析确定了两种不同的 SI 方法——纠正性方法和附加性方法——使分析师能够对提取的叙述进行质量判断和组织结构。我们还发现 SI 用户通过较少的参数操作实现了相当的探索广度，这表明 SI 可以作为模型细化的替代途径。这项工作提供了经验证据，证明基于地图的表征在叙事意义建构方面优于时间线，并提供了关于分析师如何使用 SI 进行叙事细化的定性见解。), suggesting that the study was underpowered to detect them. Qualitative analysis identified two distinct SI approaches—corrective and additive—that enable analysts to impose quality judgments and organizational structure on extracted narratives. We also find that SI users achieved comparable exploration breadth with less parameter manipulation, suggesting that SI serves as an alternative pathway for model refinement. This work provides empirical evidence that map-based representations outperform timelines for narrative sensemaking, along with qualitative insights into how analysts use SI for narrative refinement.

1 简介Introduction

可视化分析 (VA) 旨在通过交互式探索支持大型复杂数据集的分析Visual analytics (VA) seeks to support the analysis of large and complex data sets through interactive exploration[cook2005illuminating]。 VA 系统允许用户通过可视化与统计和人工智能 (AI) 模型进行交互，以探索数据中的联系和假设，从而帮助用户进行意义建构过程. VA systems assist users in their sensemaking process by allowing them to interact with statistical and artificial intelligence (AI) models through visualizations to explore connections and hypotheses in the data[endert2012semantic]。语义交互（SI）. Semantic interaction (SI)[endert2012semantic, bradel2014multi]使用户能够通过与可视化的直观交互将他们的认知过程整合到底层计算模型中。 SI 不需要直接参数操作，而是允许分析师执行自然交互，并将其解释并转换为模型更新。这种方法在各种 VA 领域中都显示出了前景，最近的工作已经开发了专门用于叙事地图提取的 SI 框架enables users to incorporate their cognitive processes into the underlying computational models through intuitive interactions with visualizations. Rather than requiring direct parameter manipulation, SI allows analysts to perform natural interactions that are interpreted and translated into model updates. This approach has shown promise in various VA domains, and recent work has developed SI frameworks specifically for narrative map extraction[keith2023iui].

叙事地图——基于图形的表示，捕捉故事中事件之间的联系——是叙事意义建构的有用工具Narrative maps—graph-based representations that capture connections between events in a story—are a useful tool for narrative sensemaking[keith2020maps]。然而，虽然已经提出了叙事地图的 SI 框架，但对其支持真正意义建构任务的有效性的实证评估仍然有限。了解分析师实际如何使用 SI 功能、他们制定哪些策略以及 SI 是否会带来更好的分析结果需要进行用户研究。. However, while SI frameworks for narrative maps have been proposed, empirical evaluations of their effectiveness in supporting real sensemaking tasks remain limited. Understanding how analysts actually use SI capabilities, what strategies they develop and whether SI leads to better analytical outcomes requires user studies.

在本文中，我们提出了一项评估叙事地图意义构建的语义交互的用户研究。我们通过 33 名参与者研究 2021 年古巴抗议活动的叙述，将支持 SI 的原型与基线条件进行比较。我们的研究采用基于洞察力的评估方法In this paper, we present a user study that evaluates semantic interaction for narrative map sensemaking. We compare an SI-enabled prototype with baseline conditions using 33 participants examining narratives from the 2021 Cuban protests. Our study employs an insight-based evaluation methodology[north2011comparison]评估 SI 如何有效地支持意义建构过程。我们的贡献包括：to assess how effectively SI supports the sensemaking process. Our contributions include:(1)经验证据表明，基于地图的可视化显着优于时间轴基线，SI 具有暗示性但不具有统计显着性的额外优势；empirical evidence that map-based visualizations significantly outperform timeline baselines, with suggestive but not statistically significant additional benefits from SI;(2)识别分析师用于完善叙述的不同 SI 策略（纠正性策略和附加策略）；identification of distinct SI strategies—corrective and additive—that analysts employ for narrative refinement;(3)分析 SI 对探索行为的影响，表明它可以作为基于参数的细化的替代方案；和analysis of SI’s effect on exploration behavior, suggesting it serves as an alternative to parameter-based refinement; and(4)从观察到的用户策略和感知中得出 SI 系统设计的实用见解。practical insights for SI system design derived from observed user strategies and perceptions.

3 系统说明System Description

我们评估了具有语义交互功能的叙事地图系统，该系统建立在 Keith 等人提出的混合多模型 SI 框架之上。We evaluate a narrative map system with semantic interaction capabilities, built upon the mixed multi-model SI framework proposed by Keith et al.[keith2023iui]。本节简要描述与理解用户研究相关的系统关键组件。. This section briefly describes the key components of the system that are relevant to understanding the user study.

叙事提取。Narrative Extraction.我们的工作属于基于事件的叙事提取范式Our work falls within the event-based narrative extraction paradigm[keith2023survey]，其中每个文档代表一个事件，而叙述是此类文档事件的按时间顺序、主题连贯的序列。该系统遵循 Keith 和 Mitra 的方法从新闻文章集中提取叙事地图, where each document represents a single event and a narrative is a temporally ordered, thematically coherent sequence of such document-events. The system extracts narrative maps from news article collections following the approach of Keith and Mitra[keith2020maps]。文章使用句子级 Transformer 嵌入（全 MiniLM-L6-v2）进行表示，聚合后生成文档级表示，并使用统一流形逼近和投影 (UMAP) 投影为 2D. Articles are represented using sentence-level Transformer embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) aggregated to produce document-level representations, projected to 2D using Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP)[mcinnes2018umap]用于可视化，并使用基于分层密度的噪声应用空间聚类 (HDBSCAN) 进行聚类for visualization, and clustered using Hierarchical Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise (HDBSCAN)[mcinnes2017hdbscan]确定主题组。然后，线性编程优化通过最大化连贯性（一种结合内容相似性（通过嵌入余弦相似性）和主题相关性（基于聚类分配）的衡量标准）将叙述图构建为有向无环图，但受到覆盖范围的限制，以确保叙述跨越不同的主题。用户可以调整三个参数：to identify topical groups. A linear programming optimization then constructs the narrative map as a directed acyclic graph by maximizing coherence—a measure combining content similarity (via embedding cosine similarity) and topical relatedness (based on cluster assignments)—subject to coverage constraints that ensure that the narrative spans diverse topics. Users can adjust three parameters:地图尺寸map size（主要故事的预期长度），(expected length of the main story),覆盖范围coverage（主题多样性），以及(topic diversity), and时间敏感性temporal sensitivity（优先选择时间上最接近的连接）。(preference for temporally proximate connections).

Analysts can perform边缘交互edge interactions添加或删除事件之间的联系，表明哪些关系是有意义的或虚假的。to add or remove connections between events, signaling which relationships are meaningful or spurious.节点交互Node interactions允许固定事件以确保将其包含在内或从叙述中删除不相关的事件。最后，allow pinning events to ensure their inclusion or removing irrelevant events from the narrative. Finally,集群交互cluster interactions使分析师能够通过对事件进行分组来定义自定义主题集群，从而直接影响一致性度量的主题相似性部分。这些交互通过逆变换转化为模型更新，修改聚类分配并施加结构约束enable analysts to define custom topical clusters by grouping events, directly influencing the topical similarity component of the coherence measure. These interactions are translated into model updates through inverse transformations that modify cluster assignments and impose structural constraints[keith2023iui]。更新可以在交互中累积，从而使模型能够逐步从分析师的推理中学习。. Updates can accumulate across interactions, allowing the model to incrementally learn from the analyst’s reasoning.

4 用户研究User Study

评估方法。Evaluation Methodology.我们使用基于洞察的评估方法通过用户研究评估了我们的 SI 框架We evaluated our SI framework through a user study using an insight-based evaluation methodology[saraiya2005insight, north2011comparison]。与传统的基准评估相比，基于洞察力的评估捕捉了分析师如何使用 VA 系统并获得洞察力，从而为探索性意义建构任务的系统有效性提供更现实的评估. In contrast to traditional benchmark evaluations, insight-based evaluations capture how analysts use VA systems and arrive at insights, providing a more realistic assessment of the effectiveness of the system for exploratory sensemaking tasks[guo2015case, alves2022personality].

参与者被要求尽可能多地了解 2021 年古巴抗议活动的原因和影响以及它们之间的关系。这项开放式任务使分析师可以自由地探索数据集，并近似现实的叙事意义建构场景。如果用户能够通过与基线相比获得更多见解，我们认为我们的 SI 系统是有效的。Participants were asked to identify as many insights as possible about the causes and effects of the 2021 Cuban protests and how they relate to each other. This open-ended task gives analysts freedom to explore the dataset and approximates a realistic narrative sensemaking scenario. We consider our SI system effective if users are able to gather more insights with it compared to baselines.

数据和参与者。Data and Participants.我们使用了报道 2021 年古巴抗议活动的新闻数据We used news data covering the 2021 Cuban protests[keith2023iui]。该数据集包含来自各个政治领域多个来源的 160 篇新闻文章，为评估叙事意义建构能力提供了适当的复杂程度。我们招募了 36 名研究生和本科生，主要是计算机科学领域的学生，还有通信、国家安全和工程领域的其他参与者。排除辍学者和注意力检查失败的参与者后，最终样本由 33 名参与者组成。大多数参与者表示对 2021 年古巴抗议活动知之甚少（平均熟悉度 = 1.5，满分为 5 分）。. The dataset contained 160 news articles from multiple sources across the political spectrum, providing an appropriate level of complexity for evaluating narrative sensemaking capabilities. We recruited 36 graduate and undergraduate students, primarily in Computer Science, with additional participants in Communications, National Security, and Engineering. After excluding dropouts and participants who failed attention checks, the final sample consisted of 33 participants. Most participants reported minimal prior knowledge of the 2021 Cuban protests (mean familiarity = 1.5 on a 5-point scale).

这项研究得到了弗吉尼亚理工大学机构审查委员会的批准 (IRB-21-929)。所有参与者在参与前都通过同意信息表提供了知情同意。会议结束后，参与者获得了礼品卡作为补偿。在有声思考过程中收集的屏幕和音频记录被安全存储，并且只有研究团队可以访问；本文中报告的所有数据均已匿名。This study was approved by the Institutional Review Board at Virginia Tech (IRB-21-929). All participants provided their informed consent prior to participation through a consent information sheet. Participants were compensated with gift cards upon completion of the session. Screen and audio recordings collected during think-aloud sessions were stored securely and accessible only to the research team; all data reported in this article have been anonymized.

实验条件。Experimental Conditions.参与者被随机分配到三种条件之一。的Participants were randomly assigned to one of three conditions. The时间轴（TL）Timeline (TL)条件作为基线，提供了一个基于时间线的原型，具有搜索功能，代表了探索临时文档集合的传统方法。的condition served as the baseline, providing a timeline-based prototype with search functionality that represents a traditional approach to exploring temporal document collections. The final distribution was 10 participants in TL, 13 in BM, and 10 in IM. They were given 1 hour to explore the Cuban protests data and write a report documenting their insights.

措施和洞察编码。Measures and Insight Coding.我们收集了定量和定性数据。定量测量包括确定的独特见解的数量、计算有效性任务前后自我报告的熟悉度（随着知识差距的缩小），以及关于可用性、SI 有效性和信任的李克特规模调查响应。我们采用了有声思考协议，在整个会议过程中捕获屏幕活动和录音。完整的交互日志记录了用户交互、参数更改和读取的事件。见解是从三个来源编码的：来自会议记录的口头见解、来自参与者报告的书面见解以及从交互中推断出的隐含见解。We collected both quantitative and qualitative data. Quantitative measures included the number of unique insights identified, self-reported familiarity before and after the task to compute effectiveness as the fraction of the knowledge gap closed, and Likert-scale survey responses on usability, SI effectiveness, and trust. We employed a think-aloud protocol, capturing screen activity and voice recordings throughout the session. Complete interaction logs recorded user interactions, parameter changes, and events read. Insights were coded from three sources: spoken insights from session transcripts, written insights from participant reports, and implicit insights inferred from interactions.

Insight编码由第一作者进行，他迭代开发了分层编码方案（图Insight coding was performed by the first author, who iteratively developed the hierarchical coding scheme (Figure1）在分析过程中。然后将编码的见解提交给合著者中的领域专家进行审查；他们提供了关于类别是否合理的反馈，并标记了有问题的编码以供重新检查。我们没有进行正式的评估者间可靠性评估，我们承认这是一个限制。然而，基于洞察的评估中编码一致性的标准化实践仍然不发达) during the analysis. 7 结论Conclusions

本文提出了一项用户研究，评估叙事地图表示和语义交互的意义建构。通过对 33 名参与者的研究，我们证明基于地图的可视化在洞察生成方面优于时间轴基线。虽然支持 SI 的原型显示出最强的性能，但 IM-BM 差异并未达到统计显着性。然而，观察到的较大效应量（This paper presented a user study that evaluated narrative map representations and semantic interaction for sensemaking. Through a study with 33 participants, we demonstrated that map-based visualizations outperform timeline baselines for insight generation.
