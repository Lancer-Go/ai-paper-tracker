摘要Abstract — 相机陷阱已成为生物多样性监测的基石，但将大量图像转化为可用生态数据的人工智能通常被锁定在商业平台后面，或者在与不列颠群岛不匹配的动物群上进行训练。为了消除障碍并提高采用率，我们发布了一个开源对象检测模型，适用于 31 个类别、28 种常见的英国哺乳动物和鸟类，以及人类、校准杆和车辆的实用类别，这些模型取自经过十年来通过 Conservation AI 及其后继者 Trap Tracker 运营部署从多个站点收集的 48,165 个标记实例的精选数据集。该模型是在 80/10/10 类分层分割上进行训练和测试的 YOLO26x 检测器，在保留的验证集上，在 0.5 的交并集 (IoU) 处实现了 0.984 的平均精度（IoU 0.5-0.95 处为 0.956），精度为 0.988，召回率为 0.965。在一个看不见的保留测试中，31 个类别的平均每物种置信度范围为 0.96 到 0.99，其中 0.17% 的假阴性率集中在困难的夜间、远处或遮挡的图像中。这些指标来自与训练相同的站点和摄像机池的数据，因此全新站点的性能将留给未来的工作。我们在非商业许可下以 ONNX 格式发布经过训练的权重，并提供本地桌面和实时摄像头支持，明确针对没有机器学习经验的生态学家。此版本是对过去十年中开发的多种付费模型的有意平衡。— Camera traps have become a cornerstone of biodiversity monitoring, but the artificial intelligence that turns vast quantities of images into usable ecological data is often locked behind commercial platforms or trained on fauna that does not match that of the British Isles. In an attempt to remove barriers and increase uptake, we release an open-source object detection model for 31 classes, 28 common UK mammal and bird species, plus utility classes for humans, calibration poles, and vehicles, drawn from a curated dataset of 48,165 labelled instances assembled from multiple sites over a decade of operational deployment through Conservation AI and its successor, Trap Tracker. The model, a YOLO26x detector trained and tested on an 80/10/10 class-stratified split, achieves a mean Average Precision of 0.984 at Intersection over Union (IoU) of 0.5 (0.956 at IoU 0.5-0.95) on the held-out validation set, with precision 0.988 and recall 0.965. On an unseen held-out test split, mean per-species confidence ranged from 0.96 to 0.99 across the 31 classes, with a 0.17% false-negative rate concentrated in difficult night-time, distant, or occluded images. These metrics are from data from the same pool of sites and cameras as training, so performance at entirely new sites is left to future work. We release the trained weights in ONNX format under a non-commercial licence, with local desktop and realtime camera support, aimed explicitly at ecologists with no machine-learning experience. This release is a deliberate counterweight to the multiple paid for models that have developed over the last decade.

一、简介I. INTRODUCTION

相机陷阱对于检测野生动物非常有效，通常优于线样线、活体陷阱和粪便调查等方法 [1], [2]。它们的局限性从来不是数据收集，而是数据处理：英国的一个中型部署通常每月生成数万张图像，而大陆聚合则达到数千万张图像 [3]、[4]。小型单物种研究仍然可以手工审查，但在调查和景观尺度上，手工审查崩溃了，甚至公民科学平台也崩溃了。Camera traps are highly effective for detecting wildlife, often outperforming methods such as line transects, live traps, and scat surveys [1], [2]. Their limitation has never been data collection but data processing: a single mid-sized UK deployment routinely produces tens of thousands of images per month, and continental aggregations run into the tens of millions [3], [4]. Small single-species studies can still be reviewed by hand, but at survey and landscape scale manual review collapses, and even citizen-science platforms, which

其后果不仅仅是带来不便。收集成本高昂且具有生态价值的图像未经处理；数据可以回答的监测问题没有得到解答；对于许多组织来说，自动化分析现在带来的深远收益根本没有实现。从历史上看，组织通过以下两种方式之一来应对这一负担。许多人在内部保留注释，由小型专家团队手工标记数十万或数百万张图像；这保留了对敏感数据的控制，并确保了一致的、专业的标签，但正是这种手动负担使得传统的相机陷阱分析如此缓慢且难以扩展。其他人转向公民科学平台，塞伦盖蒂快照就是典型的例子，证明志愿者分类器可以高精度处理数百万张图像[5]。但公民科学并不是人人都能接触到的。敏感物种位置、数据治理限制以及缺乏面向公众的平台使许多项目无法使用它，即使有可用的资源，志愿者的努力也是有限的资源，其准确性因物种、栖息地和相机配置而异[6]。The consequence is not merely inconvenience. Imagery that is expensive to collect and ecologically valuable goes unprocessed; monitoring questions that the data could answer go unanswered; and the profound gains that automated analysis now makes possible are, for many organisations, simply not realised. Historically, organisations met this burden in one of two ways. Many kept annotation in-house, with small expert teams labelling hundreds of thousands or millions of images by hand; this preserved control over sensitive data and ensured consistent, expert labelling, but it is exactly this manual burden that made conventional cameratrap analysis so slow and so difficult to scale. Others turned to citizen-science platforms, Snapshot Serengeti being the canonical example, demonstrating that volunteer classifiers could process millions of images at high accuracy [5]. But citizen science is not available to everyone; sensitive species locations, data-governance constraints, and the absence of a public-facing platform keep many projects from using it, and even where it is available, volunteer effort is a finite resource whose accuracy varies substantially with species, habitat, and camera configuration [6].

深度学习提供了第三条道路，现在它在该领域占据主导地位。该应用正式始于 Norouzzadeh 等人 2018 年的演示，即卷积神经网络可以识别、计数和描述 320 万张塞伦盖蒂快照图像数据集中的 48 个物种的行为，在分布内测试数据上的分类准确率超过 93%，与志愿者分类器相当，但以机器速度运行 [7]。这项工作以及北美相机陷阱图像 (NACTI) 数据集和 MLWIC2 模型 [8]、[9] 的并行出现，建立了自那时起主导该领域的标准方法：收集大型标记数据集，在其上训练深度分类器或检测器，报告 90 年代高的准确度数据，发布模型。威利等人。展示了将深度学习与公民科学标签相结合的类似结果[10]。随后的工作完善了该方法：Norouzzadeh 等人。添加了主动学习Deep learning offered a third path, and it now dominates the field. The application began in earnest with Norouzzadeh et al.'s 2018 demonstration that convolutional neural networks could identify, count, and describe behaviours for 48 species across the 3.2-million-image Snapshot Serengeti dataset, with classification accuracy exceeding 93% on within-distribution test data, comparable to volunteer classifiers but operating at machine speed [7]. This work, and the parallel emergence of the North American Camera Trap Images (NACTI) dataset and the MLWIC2 model [8], [9], established the standard approach that has dominated the field since: collect a large labelled dataset, train a deep classifier or detector on it, report accuracy figures in the high 90s, release the model. 贯穿这些文献的一个持续而重要的发现是，分布内的绩效数据并不是保护从业者在该领域所经历的。 Beery 等人的“Recognition in Terra Incognita”首次仔细证明了在一组固定相机位置上训练的相机陷阱模型很难推广到新位置 [14]：在一组相机站上训练的模型学会将物种与这些特定站的背景、植被和照明特征相关联，而不是与动物本身相关联。塔巴克等人。具体显示了相同的效果，在美国相机陷阱数据上训练的模型在加拿大同一物种图像上进行测试时，准确率下降到 81.8% [9]。 Whytock 等人随后在加蓬的工作专门为跨站点评估而设计，提供了来自 227 个独立摄像机站的 23,868 张图像，包括训练中未代表的物种，并报告了该样本外集的总体准确度为 90%，虽然很强，但仍低于相机陷阱模型通常发布的样本内准确度 [15]。最近的工作完全重新定义了这个问题，认为机器学习社区青睐的跨站点框架忽略了保护主义者真正关心的问题：随着生态系统和相机部署随着时间的推移而漂移，在固定站点上保持可靠的识别[26]。诚实的结论是，高精度数据应始终与评估协议一起阅读，并且几乎任何部署到新合作伙伴站点（具有新摄像机和新植被）的模型都会出现相对于其训练分布基准的性能下降。A persistent and consequential finding throughout this literature is that within-distribution performance numbers are not what conservation practitioners experience in the field. Beery et al.'s "Recognition in Terra Incognita" was the first careful demonstration that camera-trap models trained on a fixed set of camera locations generalise poorly to new locations [14]: a model trained on a set of camera stations learns to associate species with the backgrounds, vegetation, and lighting characteristic of those specific stations rather than with the animals themselves. Tabak et al. showed the same effect concretely, with a model trained on US camera-trap data dropping to 81.8% accuracy when tested on Canadian images of the same species [9]. Whytock et al.'s subsequent work in Gabon, explicitly designed for cross-site evaluation, held out 23,868 images from 227 independent camera stations, including species not represented in training, and reported 90% overall accuracy on this out-of-sample set, strong but still below the within-sample accuracies typically posted by camera-trap models [15]. More recent work reframes the problem entirely, arguing that the cross-site framing favoured by the ML community misses the question conservationists actually care about: maintaining reliable recognition at a fixed site as ecosystems and camera deployments drift over time [26]. The honest conclusion is that high accuracy numbers should always be read alongside their evaluation protocol, and that almost any model deployed to a new partner site, with new cameras and new vegetation, will see some performance degradation relative to its training-distribution benchmark.

当 Conservation AI 项目在 2010 年代中期开始研究自动相机陷阱分类时 [16]，可用的工具包括少数学术版本，没有消费者友好的软件，几乎没有商业活动。在鼎盛时期，Conservation AI 平台每周处理大约 400 万张相机陷阱图像，在合作伙伴组织使用时免费 [17]。当该平台在 2025 年作为公共服务结束并被重建为 Trap Tracker [18] 时，这种情况已经发生了显着变化，并且值得区分经常被混淆的两件事：检测和分类模型本身，以及将它们集成到端到端上传分析服务的平台。When the Conservation AI project began working on automated camera-trap classification in the mid-2010s [16], the available tooling consisted of a handful of academic releases, no consumer-friendly software, and almost no commercial activity. At its peak, the Conservation AI platform processed approximately four million camera-trap images per week, free at the point of use for partner organisations [17]. By the time the platform wound down as a public service in 2025 and was rebuilt as Trap Tracker [18], this picture had shifted markedly, and it is worth distinguishing two things that are often conflated: the detection and classification models themselves, and the platforms that integrate them into end-toend upload-to-analysis services.

MegaDetector, developed at Microsoft AI for Earth by Beery, Morris and Yang and now maintained as part of the PyTorch Wildlife project, has become the de facto standard for the detection step in camera-trap pipelines [19]. Its deliberately species-agnostic design, returning only "animal", "person", or "vehicle", is its core strength: by not committing to a species taxonomy it generalises remarkably well across geographies and is used by 60+ organisations worldwide [21], with Vélez et al. reporting F1 values between 0.87 and 0.96 across diverse deployment contexts [28]. Vélez et al.'s 2023 review remains the most useful published comparison across ease of use, programming requirements, and species-classification capability [30]; its conclusion is that no single platform dominates, and that choice depends substantially on the user's resources and target species.

在平台方面，商业层已经围绕相机陷阱人工智能发展起来。在这里，重要的是要准确说明什么是封闭的，什么是未封闭的：领先的通用模型是免费和开放的，有能力的从业者可以免费下载和运行它们。日益商业化的是使它们可用的层、托管处理、批量上传能力、支持以及通用探测器不提供开箱即用的物种级、区域调整分类。 Animal Detect [31] 等商业平台正是封装了这一层，建立在开放模型的基础上，但以信用或订阅为基础收费，咨询公司提供相机陷阱图像处理作为收费服务；甚至是具有免费等级的研究型平台，其中包括 Wildlife Insights [22] 和 Agouti [32]，计量表使用或超出阈值的处理费用。这样做是有正当理由的，维持模型开发、提供用户支持、资助持续的标签。我们并不认为商业管道是不合适的。我们发布的模型故意位于这条线的开放一侧：开放权重，可独立于任何平台运行，免费供非商业用途。On the platform side, a commercial layer has grown up around camera-trap AI. Here it is important to be precise about what is and is not enclosed: the leading general-purpose models are free and open, and a capable practitioner can download and run them at no cost. What is increasingly commercialised is the layer that makes them usable, hosted processing, bulk-upload capacity, support, and the specieslevel, regionally-tuned classification that a general-purpose detector does not provide out of the box. Commercial platforms such as Animal Detect [31] package exactly this layer, building on the open models but charging on a credit or subscription basis, and consultancies offer camera-trap image processing as a billed service; even research-oriented platforms with free tiers, Wildlife Insights [22] and Agouti [32] among them, meter usage or charge for processing beyond a threshold. There are legitimate reasons for this, sustaining model development, providing user support, funding ongoing labelling. and we make no claim that commercial pipelines are inappropriate. The model we release sits deliberately on the open side of that line: open weights, runnable independently of any platform, free for noncommercial use.

这些发展共同产生了一种奇特的逆转。技术上限急剧上升，而小型非政府组织、大学研究团体、野化倡议、公民科学网络和资金不足的法定机构的实际准入范围却在缩小。在全球范围内，这种差距更加明显：面临哺乳动物动物区系丧失风险最高的地区恰恰是技术专业知识、能力和基础设施最少的地区[33]。因此，可用的物种级人工智能的付费墙对资源贫乏、生物多样性高风险的地区影响最大Together these developments have produced a peculiar inversion. The technical ceiling has risen sharply, while practical access for small NGOs, university research groups, rewilding initiatives, citizen-science networks, and underfunded statutory agencies has narrowed. Globally the disparity is starker still: the regions facing the highest risk of mammalian defaunation are precisely those with the least technical expertise, capacity, and infrastructure [33]. A. 训练、验证和测试集A. Training, Validation and Test Sets

数据集使用 80/10/10 分区进行分割，即 80% 用于训练（38,522 个实例），10% 用于验证（4,805 个实例），10% 用于测试（4,838 个实例）。这三个数据集共同构成了完整的 48,165 个实例数据集，该数据集使用类分层采样：每个类都以相同的 80/10/10 比例（精确到 0.1%）独立划分，因此每个类，包括最小的杓鹬小鸡 (219/27/28) 和校准杆 (120/15/16)，在所有三个分区中都有意义地表示。验证分割在训练期间用于模型选择；测试分割被完全保留，仅用于第五节中报告的培训后评估。The dataset is split using an 80/10/10 partition, i.e., 80% used for training (38,522 instances), 10% for validation (4,805 instances), and 10% for testing (4,838 instances). The three together comprise the full 48,165-instance dataset, which uses class-stratified sampling: each class is independently partitioned in the same 80/10/10 ratio (to the nearest 0.1%), so every class, including the smallest ones such as Curlew chick (219/27/28) and Calibration Pole (120/15/16), is meaningfully represented in all three splits. The validation split is used during training for model selection; the test split is held back entirely and used only for the post-training evaluation reported in Section V.

应该注意的是，这构成了从与训练集相同的数据池导出的类分层保留，而不是站点不相交或时间不相交的保留。因此，下面的标题指标是“分布内”评估。It should be noted that this constitutes a class-stratified hold-out derived from the same data pool as the training set, as opposed to a site-disjoint or temporally disjoint hold-out. 三．模型与训练III. MODEL AND TRAINING

我们使用 YOLO26x，YOLO26 目标检测系列的超大变体，根据公开发布的预训练权重进行初始化 [51]。使用 YOLO26 而不是更定制的架构的决定是经过深思熟虑的：相机陷阱数据上的对象检测不再是架构级别的开放研究问题。现在决定一个模型在该领域是否有用的不是网络设计，而是它所训练的数据、报告其准确性的诚实性以及非专业人士部署它的难易程度——我们在这里优先考虑的方面。 YOLO26 是一个得到良好支持的系列，可对商用 GPU 进行高效推理，干净地导出为可移植的、独立于框架的格式 (ONNX) 以方便部署，并为想要以准确性换取速度的用户提供了通往更轻版本 (YOLO26n、s、m、l) 的途径。We use YOLO26x, the extra-large variant of the YOLO26 object detection family, initialised from publicly released pretrained weights [51]. The decision to use YOLO26 rather than a more bespoke architecture is deliberate: object detection on camera-trap data is no longer an open research problem at the architecture level. What now determines whether a model is useful in the field is not the network design but the data it is trained on, the honesty with which its accuracy is reported, and the ease with which a non-specialist can deploy it – aspects we prioritise here. YOLO26 is a wellsupported family with efficient inference on commodity GPUs, clean export to a portable, framework-independent format (ONNX) for easy deployment, and a path to lighter variants (YOLO26n, s, m, l) for users who want to trade accuracy for speed.

在验证拆分中，我们报告了标准对象检测指标（参见表 II）。精度是正确检测的比例；召回率是实际存在的动物中被检测到的比例。 F1 分数是精度和召回率的调和平均值，操作点设置在使其最大化的置信阈值。指标是在 F1 最佳阈值 0.62 下报告的；运行不能容忍误报的管道的用户可能更喜欢更高的阈值（0.75–0.85）。On the validation split, we report standard object-detection metrics (see Table II). Precision is the fraction of detections that are correct; recall is the fraction of animals actually present that are detected. The F1 score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall, and the operating point is set at the confidence threshold that maximises it. Metrics are reported at this F1-optimal threshold of 0.62; users running pipelines intolerant of false positives may prefer a higher threshold (0.75–0.85).

平均精度 (mAP) 是探测器性能的现场标准总结。对于每个类别，平均精度捕获模型在其置信阈值变化时保持高精度和高召回率的程度，奖励发现大多数动物同时发出很少误报的模型，而 mAP 是所有类别的平均值。仅当其预测框与真实框重叠足够多（以交并交（IoU）衡量）时，检测才算正确。我们报告了两种变体：mAP@0.5 需要至少 50% 的重叠，这是一种宽松的测试，本质上询问动物是否被发现并大致定位； mAP@0.5:0.95 平均性能超过逐渐严格的重叠阈值（0.5 至 0.95），因此额外奖励紧密、准确的定位。第二个是要求更高的措施，也是持续培训往往会显示其效果的地方。Mean Average Precision (mAP) is the field-standard summary of detector performance. For each class, Average Precision captures how well the model maintains both high precision and high recall as its confidence threshold is varied, rewarding models that find most animals while raising few false alarms, and mAP is the mean of this across all classes. A detection only counts as correct if its predicted box overlaps the true box by enough, measured as Intersection-over-Union (IoU). We report two variants: mAP@0.5 requires at least 50% overlap, a lenient test that essentially asks whether the animal was found and roughly located; mAP@0.5:0.95 averages performance over progressively stricter overlap thresholds (0.5 to 0.95) and so additionally rewards tight, accurate localisation. The second is the more demanding measure and is where continued training tends to show its effect.

五、限制和注意事项V. LIMITATIONS AND CAVEATS

根据标准绩效指标，报告的指标确实很强大。然而，这种发布只有在其贡献和局限性都透明的情况下才值得保护界的信任。在此背景下，有几个问题需要考虑。The metrics reported are genuinely strong by standard performance metrics. However, a release of this kind only merits the trust of the conservation community to the extent that the work is transparent about both its contributions and its limitations. Against this backdrop there are several issues to consider.

首先是评估的性质。本文中的所有指标（验证和测试等）都是根据从与训练相同的总体池中提取的数据计算的，并按类别分层保留进行分区。这是适当的证据，表明该模型已经稳健地学习了 31 个类别，并且没有过度拟合特定的图像子集，但它本身并没有证明性能转移到新的合作伙伴站点、新的相机模型或新的季节性背景。本文回顾的文献在这一点上近十年来一直保持一致：相机陷阱分类/目标检测模型倾向于像学习动物一样学习相机，并且在首次部署到新环境时，分布内评估的标题准确度数据通常会下降[8]、[14]、[15]、[52]。根据我们团队之前大规模处理数据的操作经验，以及我们在此处的测试拆分中观察到的置信行为的一致性，我们预计The first concerns the nature of the evaluation. All metrics in this paper, validation and test alike, are computed on data drawn from the same overall pool as training, partitioned by class-stratified hold-out. This is appropriate evidence that the model has learned the 31 classes robustly and is not overfitting to a particular subset of images, but it does not, on its own, demonstrate that performance transfers to a new partner site, a new camera model, or a new seasonal context. The literature reviewed in this paper has been consistent on this point for nearly a decade: camera-trap classification/object detection models tend to learn the cameras as much as the animals, and headline accuracy figures from in-distribution evaluation routinely drop on first deployment to a novel context [8], [14], [15], [52]. Based on our group's prior operational experience processing data at scale, and on the consistency of confidence behaviour we observe on the test split here, we would expect

在英国大多数运营条件下，mAP@0.5 可以轻松保持在 0.9 以上，但本文并未证明这一点，我们将标记的跨站点基准确定为未来最重要的工作。与此相关的是，我们的测试评估报告置信度分布和评论标记率，而不是针对手动标记的基本事实的精确度和召回率。我们在之前的工作中已经进行了此类完全标记的评估[53-55]，并且同样的方法也适用于此；本文的选择是优先考虑公开发布和行为验证，并遵循手工标记的基准测试。mAP@0.5 to remain comfortably above 0.9 in most operational UK conditions, but this paper does not prove that, and we identify a labelled cross-site benchmark as the most important piece of work moving forward. Related to this, our test evaluation reports confidence distributions and reviewflagging rates rather than precision and recall against manually-labelled ground truth. We have produced fully labelled evaluations of this kind in prior work [53–55], and the same methodology applies here; the choice in this paper was to prioritise the open release and behavioural verification, with hand-labelled benchmarking to follow.

支撑上述所有内容的是我们想要做出的一项承诺：当前版本不是一次性快照。随着更多合作伙伴数据的积累、长尾类别中的标签空白的填补以及我们吸收上述分布外基准的经验教训，我们打算继续发布该英国哺乳动物模型的逐步改进版本。每个后续版本都将在相同的非商业许可下发布，具有相同级别的文档和相同的可复制培训配置。基于现有权重的从业者应该期待稳定的升级路径，而不是移动的目标。除了英国之外，本文概述的区域发布计划已经在进行中，首先是撒哈拉以南非洲模式，然后扩展到更多地区。Underpinning all of the above is a commitment we want to make: the present release is not a one-off snapshot. We intend to continue releasing progressively improved versions of this UK mammals model as additional partner data accumulates, as labelling gaps in the long-tail classes are filled, and as we incorporate lessons from the out-ofdistribution benchmark above. Each successor version will be released under the same non-commercial licence, with the same level of documentation and the same reproducible training configuration. Practitioners building on the present weights should expect a stable upgrade path rather than a moving target. Beyond the UK, the regional-release programme outlined in the paper is already underway, starting with a sub-Saharan African model and extending to further regions thereafter.
