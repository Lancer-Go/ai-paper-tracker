Yi Chen, Rushuai Yang, Qiang Chen, Dongyan (Lucy) Huo
Department of Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

1 简介Introduction

近年来，深度强化学习（DRL）已成为顺序决策最成功的算法范式之一。通过将强化学习算法与表达性神经网络函数逼近相结合，DRL 在游戏、机器人控制和自动赛车等许多人工智能领域取得了显着的经验成功In recent years, deep reinforcement learning (DRL) has emerged as one of the most successful algorithmic paradigms for sequential decision-making. By combining reinforcement learning algorithms with expressive neural-network function approximation, DRL has achieved remarkable empirical success in many AI domains such as game playing, robotic control, and autonomous racing(Mnih et al., 2015; Rajeswaran et al., 2018; Wurman et al., 2022; Tang et al., 2025)。这些成功表明，DRL 可以成为从模拟或交互数据中学习复杂控制策略的强大工具，特别是当显式动态编程解决方案在计算上不可行时。. These successes suggest that DRL can be a powerful tool for learning complex control policies from simulation or interaction data, especially when explicit dynamic-programming solutions are computationally infeasible.

运筹学（OR）中的许多顺序决策问题可以自然地表述为马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）。示例包括库存控制、排队控制和资源分配。经典动态规划为此类问题提供了原则框架，但随着状态和动作空间的增长，精确的解决方法很快就会变得棘手。因此，近似动态规划（ADP）和基于模拟的策略优化长期以来一直发挥着重要作用Many sequential decision-making problems in operations research (OR) can naturally be formulated as Markov decision processes (MDPs). Examples include inventory control, queueing control, and resource allocation. Classical dynamic programming provides a principled framework for such problems, but exact solution methods quickly become intractable as the state and action spaces grow. Approximate dynamic programming (ADP) and simulation-based policy optimization have therefore long played an important role(Bertsekas, 2025; Powell, 2011)。从数学角度来看，ADP 和 DRL 紧密相连：两者都通过近似价值函数、行动价值函数或策略来寻求贝尔曼评估和改进步骤的易于处理的近似。这种联系推动了越来越多的工作应用 DRL 算法来解决大规模 OR 问题。. From a mathematical perspective, ADP and DRL are closely connected: both seek tractable approximations to Bellman evaluation and improvement steps, either by approximating value functions, action-value functions, or policies. This connection has motivated a growing body of work applying DRL algorithms to solve large-scale OR problems.(Delarue et al., 2020; Dai and Gluzman, 2022; Gijsbrechts et al., 2022; Harsha et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026).

尽管存在这种天然的联系，但将现成的 DRL 算法应用于可操作的 MDP 很少是即插即用的。一个关键的挑战是操作界面。标准 DRL 实现通常假设可以通过简单且固定的表示来枚举、采样和优化操作。在有限动作问题中，神经网络通常为固定目录中的每个动作输出一个逻辑值或值。在连续动作问题中，动作通常表示为固定维度的欧几里德向量，参与者输出该向量或此类向量上的分布参数。许多“或”问题不适合任何一个模板。它们的可行动作通常是状态相关的、高维度的，并且由容量、兼容性和完整性约束隐式定义。Despite this natural connection, applying off-the-shelf DRL algorithms to operational MDPs is rarely plug-and-play. A critical challenge is the action interface. Standard DRL implementations typically assume that actions can be enumerated, sampled, and optimized over through a simple and fixed representation. In finite-action problems, a neural network often outputs one logit or value for each action in a fixed catalog. In continuous-action problems, the action is usually represented as a fixed-dimensional Euclidean vector, and the actor outputs either this vector or the parameters of a distribution over such vectors. Many OR problems do not fit either template. Their feasible actions are often state dependent, high dimensional, and implicitly defined by capacity, compatibility, and integrality constraints.

操作界面的困难是操作 MDP 中成功的 DRL 应用通常需要大量针对具体情况的算法工程的一个关键原因。研究人员可能需要设计针对特定问题的行动分解、可行性修正、屏蔽规则或专门的网络架构。这些技术可能很有效，但它们也降低了标准 DRL 求解器作为跨操作 MDP 的可重用工具的程度。从这个意义上说，挑战不仅在于 OR 问题很大或随机；还在于 OR 问题很大或随机。同样重要的是，OR 模型的自然动作空间通常与标准 DRL 实现所期望的动作表示不一致。The action-interface difficulty is a critical reason why successful DRL applications in operational MDPs often require substantial case-specific algorithmic engineering. Researchers may need to design problem-specific action decompositions, feasibility corrections, masking rules, or specialized network architectures. These techniques can be effective, but they also reduce the extent to which standard DRL solvers can be used as reusable tools across operational MDPs. In this sense, the challenge is not only that OR problems are large or stochastic; it is also essential the natural action spaces of OR models often do not align with the action representations expected by standard DRL implementations.

现有文献在解决这个问题方面取得了重要进展。例如，动作表示方法将大型有限动作集嵌入到低维空间中，允许来自一个动作的反馈泛化到类似的动作The existing literature have made important progress toward addressing this issue. For example, action-representation methods embed large finite action sets into lower-dimensional spaces, allowing feedback from one action to generalize to similar actions(Chandak et al., 2019; Dulac-Arnold et al., 2015)。当固定的动作目录或简单的可行区域可用时，可行性保留方法（例如屏蔽、动作消除）可以防止执行不可行的动作. Feasibility-preserving methods such as masking, action elimination, can prevent infeasible actions from being executed when a fixed action catalog or a simple feasible region is available(Huang and Ontañón, 2022; Zahavy et al., 2018; Pham et al., 2018)。最近的一项工作将强化学习与基于优化的行动结合起来 选择，或者通过将学习到的价值信息嵌入到动作选择问题中，或者通过在策略表示中合并优化层. A more recent line of work combines reinforcement learning with optimization-based action selection, either by embedding learned value information into an action selection problem or by incorporating an optimization layer inside the policy representation(Delarue et al., 2020; Harsha et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025; Hoppe et al., 2025).

这些方法很有价值，但并没有完全解决出现的接口问题 人们希望将标准的现成 DRL 算法应用于具有状态相关的可行操作集的 MDP。动作表示方法主要是为固定的有限动作目录设计的，而许多可行集是由约束隐式定义的，并且可能太大而无法枚举。掩蔽和消除还依赖于明确的目录或动作的有限超集。优化层方法通常将优化分数视为通用的学习效用系数，并且需要对该层进行专门的训练。这些方法要么依赖于特定的动作表示， 针对特定问题的可行性机制，或定制的基于优化的学习架构。These approaches are valuable, but do not fully address the interface problem that arises when one wants to apply standard off-the-shelf DRL algorithms to MDPs with state-dependent feasible action sets. Action-representation methods are primarily designed for fixed finite action catalogs, whereas many feasible sets are defined implicitly by constraints and may be too large to enumerate. Masking and elimination also rely on an explicit catalog or a finite superset of actions. Optimization-layer methods typically treat the optimization score as a generic learned utility coefficient and require specialized training for the layer. These approaches either depend on a particular action representation, a problem-specific feasibility mechanism, or a customized optimization-based learning architecture.

在本文中，我们提出了一种具有状态相关的可行操作集的 MDP 的新颖操作接口，我们将其称为In this paper, we propose a novel action interface for MDPs with state-dependent feasible action sets, which we call the贝尔曼泰勒分数解码Bellman-Taylor score decoding。中心思想是标准化学习界面而不是操作动作空间。该策略不是直接在不规则的可行动作集上学习策略，而是在欧几里德空间中学习分数向量。然后，动作解码器通过解决原始可行集上的优化问题，将该分数映射为可实现的自然动作。解码器的动机是动作后贝尔曼连续值（或最佳动作值函数）的泰勒近似，其中学习的分数旨在表示动作后系统配置对未来值的边际效应。. The central idea is to standardize the learning interface rather than the operational action space. Instead of learning a policy directly over the irregular feasible action set, the policy learns a score vector in a Euclidean space. An action-decoder then maps this score into an implementable natural action by solving an optimization problem over the original feasible set. The decoder is motivated by a Taylor approximation of the post-action Bellman continuation value (or the optimal action-value function), where the learned score is intended to represent the marginal effect of the post-action system configuration on future value.

这种结构将学习与可行性分开。 DRL 算法在常规欧几里得分数空间上运行，而可行性、完整性和组合耦合则由动作解码器处理。一旦指定了解码器，原始 MDP 就会产生一个潜在分数 MDP，其中动作是分数向量。标准的连续动作 DRL 算法，例如近端策略优化，可以直接应用于这个引发的问题。重要的是，解码器仅在前向传递中求解，将采样分数转换为可行的自然动作。策略梯度更新不需要通过解码器进行区分。这与可微优化层方法不同，其中优化器是 可训练计算图的一部分，必须通过微分或近似 通过替代梯度。This construction separates learning from feasibility. The DRL algorithm operates on a regular Euclidean score space, while feasibility, integrality, and combinatorial coupling are handled by the action decoder. Once the decoder is specified, the original MDP induces a latent-score MDP in which the action is the score vector. Standard continuous-action DRL algorithms, such as proximal policy optimization, can then directly be applied to this induced problem. Importantly, the decoder is solved only in the forward pass to convert a sampled score into a feasible natural action. The policy-gradient update does not require differentiating through the decoder. This is distinct with differentiable optimization-layer methods, where the optimizer is part of the trainable computation graph and must be differentiated through or approximated by a surrogate gradient.

拟议的框架并没有消除可操作的 MDP 的固有困难。相反，它将模型结构限制在解码器中，从而使学习算法保持标准。该模型指定了系统的可行操作集、即时奖励和操作后表示。该策略学习与状态相关的分数，解码器将其转化为可行的操作动作。这在现成的连续动作 DRL 求解器和具有状态相关、受约束且通常是组合动作的 MDP 之间架起了一座桥梁。权衡是解码器引发的策略类受到限制：并非原始 MDP 中的每个可行策略都可以由分数解码策略表示。我们的理论分析量化了这种权衡。The proposed framework does not eliminate the inherent difficulty of operational MDPs. Instead, it confines model structure to the decoder, allowing the learning algorithm to remain standard. The model specifies the feasible action set, immediate reward, and a post-action representation of the system. The policy learns state-dependent scores, which the decoder translates into feasible operational actions. This creates a bridge between off-the-shelf continuous-action DRL solvers and MDPs with state-dependent, constrained, and often combinatorial actions. The trade-off is that the decoder-induced policy class is restricted: not every feasible policy in the original MDP can be represented by a score-decoding policy. Our theoretical analysis quantifies this trade-off.

作为该框架的应用，我们研究了排队网络控制问题。多类别、多池排队系统中的动态调度是一个经典且实际重要的问题，其应用包括呼叫中心和住院病人溢出管理等As an application of this framework, we investigate a queueing network control problem. Dynamic scheduling in multi-class, multi-pool queueing systems is a classical and practically important problem, with applications such as call centers and inpatient overflow management(Dai and Shi, 2019; Chen et al., 2020)。最近的研究也探索了使用 DRL 来解决此类问题，但成功的实施通常依赖于特定问题的操作分解、定制的策略架构或针对排队模型定制的方差减少技术。相比之下，我们的框架允许我们直接应用标准 PPO 求解器，而无需引入任何额外的特定于问题的算法工程，例如方差减少，这在同行工作中是常见且必不可少的. Recent studies have also explored the use of DRL for such problems, but successful implementations often rely on problem-specific action decompositions, customized policy architectures, or variance-reduction techniques tailored to the queueing model. In contrast, our framework allows us to apply a standard PPO solver directly without introducing any additional problem-specific algorithmic engineering such as variance reduction, which are common and essential in peer works(Dai and Gluzman, 2022; Dong et al., 2025)。在这种情况下，Bellman-Taylor 得分解码本质上成为一种基于学习索引的调度规则：强化学习策略学习状态相关索引，动作解码器选择总得分最大的可行调度动作。通过模拟实验，我们表明生成的解码 PPO 策略在一系列排队实例中优于各种基准。. In this setting, the Bellman-Taylor score decoding essentially becomes a learned index-based dispatching rule: the RL policy learns state-dependent indices, and the action decoder selects the feasible dispatching action with the largest total score. Through simulation experiments, we show that the resulting decoded PPO policy outperforms various benchmarks across a range of queueing instances.

2.1 深度强化学习和标准动作界面Deep Reinforcement Learning and Standard Action Interfaces

对于状态和动作空间有限的小规模 MDP，经典动态规划和精确 MDP 方法（包括值迭代、策略迭代和线性规划）提供了精确求解方法For small-scale MDPs with finite state and action spaces, classical dynamic programming and exact MDP methods, including value iteration, policy iteration, and linear programming, provide exact solution approaches(Sutton et al., 1998)。然而，当状态和动作空间变大时，由于维数灾难，这些精确的方法很快就会在计算上变得令人望而却步。. However, when the state and action spaces become large, these exact methods quickly become computationally prohibitive, due to the curse of dimensionality(Bellman, 1957; Powell, 2011)。这一挑战激发了关于近似算法的广泛文献，在近似动态规划方面取得了实质性进展. Deep reinforcement learning can be viewed as a neural-network-based extension of this approximation paradigm, in which value functions or policies are represented by deep neural networks and learned from trajectory data. Owing to the strong approximation power of neural networks, DRL has achieved remarkable empirical success in a variety of benchmark problems in AI domains, such as game playing and robotic control(Mnih et al., 2015; Rajeswaran et al., 2018; Wurman et al., 2022; Tang et al., 2025).

2.2 行动表示和可行性保护政策Action Representations and Feasibility-Preserving Policies

当可行动作几何形状变得复杂，或者可行动作集因状态而异时，DRL 中的标准可行性机制取决于动作的表示方式。相关的工作流修改了动作参数化，以便可以通过标准函数近似来处理大的动作空间。动作嵌入方法学习大型离散动作集的低维表示，并使用该表示来概括跨动作的反馈When the feasible-action geometry becomes complex, or the feasible action sets vary across states, the standard feasibility mechanisms in DRL depend on how the action is represented. A related stream of work modifies the action parameterization so that large action spaces can be handled by standard function approximation. 7 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and Future Work

本文提出 Bellman-Taylor 分数解码作为具有状态相关可行动作集的 MDP 的动作接口。主要思想是将学习与可行性分开。标准的连续动作 DRL 算法在欧几里得潜在空间中学习状态相关分数，而优化解码器将这些分数映射到原始 MDP 中的可行自然动作。这种结构允许特定于问题的操作结构，例如容量约束、完整性限制和组合耦合，由解码器而不是定制的策略架构来处理。性能分析表明，最优性差距可以分解为结构近似误差和算法学习误差。在排队网络案例研究中，解码器产生学习的最大权重类型调度规则，数值结果表明标准 PPO 实现的性能明显优于经典路由启发式和 ADP 基准。This paper proposes Bellman-Taylor score decoding as an action interface for MDPs with state-dependent feasible action sets. The main idea is to separate learning from feasibility. A standard continuous-action DRL algorithm learns state-dependent scores in a Euclidean latent space, while an optimization decoder maps these scores into feasible natural actions in the original MDP. This construction allows problem-specific operational structure, such as capacity constraints, integrality restrictions, and combinatorial coupling, to be handled by the decoder rather than by a customized policy architecture. Performance analysis suggests that the optimality gap can be decomposed into a structural approximation error and an algorithmic learning error. In the queueing network case study, the decoder yields a learned max-weight-type dispatching rule, and numerical results demonstrate that a standard PPO implementation outperform classical routing heuristics and ADP benchmarks considerably.

未来的研究仍有几个方向开放。首先，本文重点讨论无限期折扣奖励公式。许多运营模型可以根据长期平均成本标准进行更自然的评估。将 Bellman-Taylor 评分解码扩展到平均成本 MDP 需要用相对值或偏差函数替换折扣连续值。其次，我们的框架假设过渡机制和有意义的行动后表示是明确可用的。这在许多程式化 OR 模型中是很自然的，但在动态未知的数据驱动设置中可能会受到限制。一个重要的方向是从数据中学习或适应动作后表示，同时保留保留可行性的解码。第三，当前的实现通过 PPO 使用在策略学习。将框架扩展到离线强化学习将扩大其适用性，并可能提高样本效率，特别是在模拟成本昂贵或现实世界探索有限的系统中。Several directions remain open for future research. First, this paper focuses on the infinite-horizon discounted-reward formulation. Many operational models are more naturally evaluated under a long-run average-cost criterion. Extending Bellman–Taylor score decoding to average-cost MDPs would require replacing the discounted continuation value with a relative value or bias function. Second, our framework assumes that the transition mechanism and a meaningful post-action representation are explicitly available. This is natural in many stylized OR models, but may be restrictive in data-driven settings where the dynamics are unknown. An important direction is to learn or adapt the post-action representation from data while retaining feasibility-preserving decoding. Third, the present implementation uses on-policy learning through PPO. Extending the framework to offline reinforcement learning would broaden its applicability and may improve sample efficiency, especially in systems where simulation is expensive or real-world exploration is limited.
