I 简介Introduction

根据国际电信联盟（ITU）最新发布的6G愿景According to the latest 6G vision released by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU)[24]，未来的无线通信系统将需要更高的吞吐量、更低的延迟、更高的可靠性、大规模连接和更高效的频谱利用, future wireless communication systems will demand higher throughput, lower latency, greater reliability, massive connectivity, and more efficient spectrum utilization[14, 5]。大规模多输入多输出 (MIMO) 阵列对于实现这些目标至关重要，它提供空间复用增益，从而提高容量和频谱效率. Massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) arrays are essential to achieving these goals, offering spatial multiplexing gains that improve both capacity and spectral efficiency[35]。在频分双工（FDD）MIMO系统中，用户设备（UE）估计下行链路信道状态信息（CSI）并将其反馈给基站（BS）进行预编码。由于传输完整的 CSI 需要大量开销，因此高效的压缩和重建机制至关重要。. In frequency division duplexing (FDD) MIMO systems, user equipment (UE) estimates the downlink channel state information (CSI) and feeds it back to the base station (BS) for precoding. Since transmitting full CSI entails substantial overhead, efficient compression and reconstruction mechanisms are critical.

传统的CSI压缩技术，例如压缩感知Conventional CSI compression techniques, such as compressed sensing[16]和基于码本的方法and codebook-based methods[39]，提供理论保证，但在复杂的大规模部署中往往表现不佳, provide theoretical guarantees but often underperform in complex, large-scale deployments[19]。通过将 CSI 矩阵建模为高维图像并训练自动编码器进行端到端压缩和重建，基于深度学习 (DL) 的方法已成为有效的替代方案。这种方法首先在 CsiNet 中得到演示. Deep learning (DL)-based methods have emerged as effective alternatives by modeling CSI matrices as high-dimensional images and training autoencoders for end-to-end compression and reconstruction. This approach was first demonstrated in CsiNet[44]，在保持重建精度的同时显着减少了反馈开销。后续工作结合了卷积结构、注意力机制和轻量级网络设计，以进一步提高性能并减少推理延迟, which achieved significant reductions in feedback overhead while maintaining reconstruction accuracy. Subsequent works have incorporated convolutional structures, attention mechanisms, and lightweight network designs to further improve performance and reduce inference latency[7, 26, 11, 30, 40, 33, 38]。与这些研究进展并行的是，第三代合作伙伴项目 (3GPP) 在版本 18 (R18) 中启动了人工智能驱动的 CSI 反馈作为研究项目，并随后将其提升为版本 20 中的工作项目. In parallel with these research advances, the 3rd Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) initiated AI-driven CSI feedback as a study item in Release 18 (R18) and subsequently advanced it to a work item in Release 20[1, 32]。更广泛地说，人工智能已被认为是 6G 的关键推动者，并且正在被纳入多个 3GPP 工作组的空中接口设计中. More broadly, AI has been recognized as a key enabler of 6G and is being incorporated into air interface design across multiple 3GPP working groups[21, 46, 22].

尽管取得了这些进步，基于人工智能的 CSI 反馈的实际部署仍然具有挑战性。当前的系统通常将模型训练集中在人工智能数据中心，这些数据中心要么根据从BS上传的CSI生成个性化模型，要么构建基于聚合数据训练的统一模型Despite these advances, practical deployment of AI-based CSI feedback remains challenging. Current systems typically centralize model training in AI data centers, which either generate personalized models based on CSI uploaded from BSs or construct unified models trained on aggregated data[18, 6, 12]。统一模型有利于广泛的适用性，但在专门环境中可能表现出次优的性能，同时还会导致计算成本高、训练时间长以及对专家调优的更大依赖。相比之下，在本地化 CSI 上训练的特定场景小模型可以在目标场景中实现接近最优的性能，支持动态模型切换或部署期间的快速微调. Unified models facilitate broad applicability but may exhibit suboptimal performance in specialized environments, while also incurring high computational costs, long training times, and greater reliance on expert tuning. In contrast, scene-specific small models trained on localized CSI can achieve near-optimal performance in their target scenarios, supporting dynamic model switching or rapid fine-tuning during deployment[29, 23, 42, 47]。 例如，3GPP R18. For example, the 3GPP R18型号切换Model Switch策略引入多种预定义配置（例如38.901 UMi/UMa）供在线选择strategy introduces multiple predefined configurations (e.g., 38.901 UMi/UMa) for online selection[1, 34]。然而，为个性化部署而训练的模型通常在使用后被丢弃，导致计算资源和存储的浪费。 更重要的是，现有文献主要关注模型训练和推理，而对跨部署的系统模型存储、管理和重用关注有限，这构成了本工作旨在解决的一个关键差距。. However, models trained for personalized deployment are typically discarded after use, resulting in wasted computational resources and storage. More importantly, existing literature predominantly focuses on model training and inference, with limited attention to systematic model storage, management, and reuse across deployments, which constitutes a critical gap that this work aims to address.

为了解决这些限制，我们提出了一个基于模型存储库的部署框架，如图 1 所示。To address these limitations, we propose a model repository-based deployment framework, illustrated in Fig.1，符合 3GPP AI/ML 生命周期管理框架, which aligns with the 3GPP AI/ML lifecycle management framework[22]。我们的框架主要关注模型存储和存储库，设计其与其他模块的有效连接。集中式存储库维护特定于场景的人工智能模型的目录，每个模型都用标准化元数据进行注释。在管理上，当BS通过提供少量的本地CSI或简短的场景描述来提交Delivery Request（检索请求）时，存储库会返回最合适的预训练模型，并进行模型传输以供BS进行推理和进一步的模型更新，从而最大限度地减少本地微调的需要，避免冗余训练。. Our framework focuses primarily on model storage and repository, designing its effective connection with other modules. A centralized repository maintains a catalog of scene-specific AI models, each annotated with standardized metadata. In management, when a BS submits a Delivery Request (retrieval request) by providing a small amount of local CSI or a brief scene description, the repository returns the most appropriate pre-trained model with model transfer for the BS’s inference and further model updating, minimizing the need for local fine-tuning and avoiding redundant training.

高效的模型检索需要信息丰富Efficient model retrieval requires informative yet隐私意识privacy-aware元数据。为此，我们采用并定制 Learnware 范例来进行 CSI 反馈部署。学习软件被定义为模型加规范metadata. To this end, we adopt and tailor the Learnware paradigm to CSI feedback deployment. Learnware is defined as Model plus Specification[50, 48, 49, 43]，其中每个模型都附有一个规范，该规范描述了其功能，而无需公开训练数据。 在这项工作中，我们将 Learnware 规范框架扩展到 CSI 压缩任务。每个规范由两个部分组成。语义规范对架构属性进行编码，例如输入和输出维度、压缩比和任务类型。统计规范通过从码本指纹频率向量导出的紧凑嵌入总结了训练数据分布。这种设计可以实现高效的模型比较和检索，而无需共享原始 CSI。 它已被显示在, where each model is accompanied by a specification that characterizes its capability without exposing the training data. In this work, we extend the Learnware specification framework to the CSI compression task. Each specification consists of two components. The semantic specification encodes architectural attributes such as input and output dimensions, compression ratios, and task types. The statistical specification summarizes the training data distribution through compact embeddings derived from codebook fingerprint frequency vectors. This design enables efficient model comparison and retrieval without sharing raw CSI. IV 部署策略Deployment Strategies

本节描述了 Learnware 框架的部署阶段，包括在线检索机制、与替代方法的比较以及加速模型查找的优化搜索策略。This section describes the deployment stage of our Learnware framework, including the online retrieval mechanism, comparisons with alternative methods, and an optimized search strategy to accelerate model lookup.

IV-A 部署阶段Deploying Stage

在部署过程中，用户侧BS首先通过监控模型不匹配来激活模型管理。然后，它向 Learnware 市场提交模型交付请求，该市场会识别并交付最合适的模型。这个过程面临两个主要挑战：（1）有效检索满足用户任务要求的模型； (2) 最大化返回模型的效用，同时最小化适应开销。During deployment, the user-side BS first activates Model Management by monitoring for model mismatch. It then submits a model Delivery Request to the Learnware marketplace, which identifies and delivers the most suitable models. This process presents two main challenges: (1) effectively retrieving models that satisfy the user’s task requirements; and (2) maximizing the utility of the returned models while minimizing adaptation overhead.

基线方法表示原始模型存储库，上传本地 CSI 数据的子集作为验证集，评估该集上的每个候选模型，并返回性能最高的模型。尽管这种方法实现了很高的检索精度，但它存在几个缺点：它损害了数据隐私和知识产权，产生大量的通信开销，并且由于顺序模型评估而导致扩展性差。这些限制使其对于实时 CSI 部署来说不切实际。The baseline approach, denoted the Original Model Repository, uploads a subset of local CSI data as a validation set, evaluates every candidate model on this set, and returns the highest‐performing model. Although this method achieves high retrieval accuracy, it suffers from several drawbacks: it compromises data privacy and intellectual property, incurs substantial communication overhead, and scales poorly due to sequential model evaluations. These limitations render it impractical for real‐time CSI deployment.

在存储库开发的早期阶段，规范空间的覆盖范围可能很稀疏，导致匹配不理想。此外，BS 可能缺乏对理想局部性能的了解，从而难以评估检索到的模型与经过最佳训练的模型之间的差距。我们通过基于可用样本、允许的训练迭代和资源限制，结合由用户定义的阈值控制的微调步骤来解决这个问题。微调后未能改进的模型将被丢弃，转而使用原始模型，而那些显示出改进的模型则被采用进行部署。In the early stages of repository development, coverage of the specification space may be sparse, leading to suboptimal matches. We address this by incorporating a fine-tuning step governed by a user-defined threshold, based on available samples, allowable training iterations, and resource constraints. VI 结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种基于 Learnware 的框架，用于在无线通信系统中智能部署 CSI 反馈模型。该框架托管在集中式人工智能数据中心，支持跨不同部署场景的模型重用，减少培训开销并提高适应性。为了解决传统模型存储库的关键局限性，例如数据隐私问题、通信成本和低效检索，所提出的方法引入了语义和统计规范，以实现准确和轻量级的模型选择。 Learnware 模型存储库通过利用码本指纹分布进行检索，消除了传输原始 CSI 数据和详尽模型评估的需要。可调速度的检索策略确保了可扩展性，同时基于用户定义的阈值进行微调可实现高效的模型适应。 广泛的模拟验证了 Learnware 模型存储库的性能始终优于基线方法。 与经过微调的通用模型相比，Learnware 方法实现了更高的 SGCS 性能，This paper proposes a Learnware-based framework for intelligent deployment of CSI feedback models in wireless communication systems. Hosted in a centralized AI data center, the framework supports model reuse across diverse deployment scenarios, reducing training overhead and improving adaptability. To address key limitations of conventional model repositories, such as data privacy concerns, communication costs, and inefficient retrieval, the proposed approach introduces semantic and statistical specifications to enable accurate and lightweight model selection. The Learnware Model Repository eliminates the need for transmitting raw CSI data and exhaustive model evaluations by leveraging codebook fingerprint distributions for retrieval. An adjustable-speed retrieval strategy ensures scalability, while fine-tuning based on a user-defined threshold enables efficient model adaptation. Extensive simulations verify that the Learnware model repository consistently outperforms baseline methods.
