1 简介Introduction

人类一眼就能毫不费力地推断出物体的重量、刚性或柔性。即使没有运动提示或交互，金属杯和泡沫杯也会立即唤起人们对质量、硬度和密度的独特期望。这种执行单视图物理推理的能力在人类感知中发挥着至关重要的作用，支持抓取、工具使用和预测物体动态等行为Humans can effortlessly infer how heavy, rigid, or flexible an object is from a single glance. A metal mug and a foam cup immediately evoke distinct expectations about mass, stiffness, and density even without motion cues or interaction. This ability to perform single-view physical reasoning plays a crucial role in human perception, supporting behaviors such as grasping, tool use, and anticipating object dynamics[hamrick2011internal]。这自然会提出一个问题：. This naturally raises the question:人工智能系统能否学会仅从单个图像推断物体的物理属性？Can an AI system learn to infer an object’s physical properties from just a single image?

尽管这个问题很重要，但它在很大程度上仍未得到解决。与视觉属性不同，物理属性是潜在的，无法直接观察到。它们必须从外观、3D 几何和材料语义的微妙线索中推断出来。现有方法通常依赖于多视图线索Despite its importance, this problem remains largely unsolved. Unlike visual attributes, physical properties are latent and cannot be directly observed. They must be inferred from subtle cues in appearance, 3D geometry, and material semantics. Existing methods often rely on multi-view cues[zhai2024physical, xu2024gaussianproperty]或多视图图像捕获的准确 3D 表示（or accurate 3D representation from multi-view image captures (例如e.g., 神经射频, NeRF[mildenhall2020nerf]）。这些方法实现了强大的性能，但需要大量输入视图和大量优化。因此，它们在只有单个图像可用的日常场景中是不切实际的。另一方面，单图像物理推理是许多下游应用（包括声音合成）的基础). These approaches achieve strong performance but require many input views and heavy optimization. As a result, they are impractical in everyday scenarios where only a single image is available. On the other hand, single-image physical reasoning is fundamental to many downstream applications including sound synthesis[dou2025hearing], 虚拟编辑, virtual editing[hsu2025autovfx], 模拟, simulation[dagli2025vomp]，以及具身推理, and embodied reasoning[jiang2025phystwin].

先前的单图像工作面临两个主要限制：1）缺乏 3D 几何意识。大多数先前的工作都集中在像素级材料识别上Prior single-image efforts face two main limitations: 1) Lack of 3D geometric awareness. Most prior works focus on pixel-level material recognition[Bell2015MaterialRecognitionInTheWild, Sharan2013RecognizingMaterials]，将该问题纯粹视为与对象几何形状无关的 2D 分类任务。这使得他们无法估计 3D 一致的物理量，例如体积或质量。 2）缺乏外表以外的物理基础。 Image2Mass 等方法, treating the problem purely as a 2D classification task agnostic to the object’s geometry. This prevents them from estimating 3D-consistent physical quantities such as volume or mass. 2) Lack of physical grounding beyond appearance. Methods such as Image2Mass[pmlr-v78-standley17a]通过数据驱动的学习，直接从 RGB 外观回归物理量，但视觉外观本身不足以提供有关物理行为的线索。如果没有明确的物质知识或物理知识，此类模型无法推广到看不见的环境、材料和对象组合。directly regress physical quantities from RGB appearance through data-driven learning, but visual appearance alone provides insufficient clues regarding physical behavior. Without explicit material knowledge or physical knowledge, such models fail to generalize to unseen environments, materials, and object compositions.

4.3 实际应用Real-world Application

For hand-object interaction, SiPhy can annotate physical properties for datasets that lack such labels. For image-to-audio generation, SiPhy provides material priors for sound synthesis, where material is often more informative than appearance alone.

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 SiPhy，一个统一几何线索的单图像框架， 语义理解和语言驱动的物理知识来估计像素级和对象级物理属性。尽管没有多视图操作 在监督下，SiPhy 在合成和现实世界中均取得了出色的表现 数据集，通过基于零件的对比对齐和重量感知来实现 体积估计的细化。 SiPhy 为重建繁重的管道提供了可扩展的替代方案，但是 仍然受到单视图几何先验的质量和 从一张 RGB 图像推断物理属性存在固有的歧义。 透明、反光和高度纹理的物体仍然具有挑战性，因为深度估计和基于 CLIP 的材料识别可能变得不可靠。 HAT 目前使用离散的重/轻优先；将其扩展到连续或成分厚度估计留待将来的工作。We presented SiPhy, a single-image framework that unifies geometric cues, semantic understanding, and language-driven physical knowledge to estimate both pixel-level and object-level physical properties. Despite operating without multi-view supervision, SiPhy achieves strong performance across synthetic and real-world datasets, enabled by part-based contrastive alignment and heaviness-aware refinement for volume estimation. SiPhy offers a scalable alternative to reconstruction-heavy pipelines, but remains constrained by the quality of single-view geometric priors and the inherent ambiguity of inferring physical attributes from one RGB image. Transparent, reflective, and highly textured objects remain challenging because depth estimation and CLIP-based material recognition can become unreliable. HAT currently uses a discrete heavy/light prior; extending it to continuous or compositional thickness estimation is left for future work.
