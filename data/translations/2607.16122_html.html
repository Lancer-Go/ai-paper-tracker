摘要Abstract

评估不应只是衡量模型当前的性能。他们应该告诉我们下一次模型迭代需要修复什么，并提供一种生成有针对性的训练后数据的方法。大多数评估流程都会识别薄弱的示例、主题或类别，但它们会隐含潜在的能力失败：他们说Evaluations should do more than measure a model’s current performance. They should tell us what to fix for the next model iteration and provide a way to generate targeted post-training data. Most evaluation pipelines identify weak examples, topics, or categories, but they leave the underlying capability failure implicit: they say哪里where模型失败了，而不是失败了a model fails, not为什么why。我们引入了 CRAFT，这是一种将任何基于评分标准的评估数据集转换为针对特定模型的弱能力诊断的方法。 CRAFT 将每个评分标准视为能力探测：它从每个提示-标题对中提取能力描述，将这些描述聚集到分层能力树中，在每个节点对目标模型进行评分，并以每个故障最明显的粒度跨树级别动态选择低性能节点。然后，选定的弱能力将指导生成有针对性的监督微调数据。保持数据生成、微调和评估设置固定，我们将 CRAFT 与即时级 EvalTree 聚类和非目标随机生成在四个开源模型、两个专业领域（金融和法律）以及 13 个与诊断数据脱节的保留基准上进行比较。在重复温度解码下，CRAFT 在所有四个模型中实现了最强的金融领域平均值；在法律领域，四个模型中的三个模型最强，并且保持在第四个模型的最佳基线的解码方差带内。因此，在标题标准级别（而不是提示或类别）诊断弱点，可以更清晰地了解模型不能做什么，并且在对诊断进行微调后可以得到明显更好的模型。. We introduce CRAFT, a method that converts any rubric-based evaluation dataset into a model-specific diagnosis of weak capabilities. CRAFT treats each grading criterion as a capability probe: it extracts a capability description from every prompt–rubric pair, clusters these descriptions into a hierarchical capability tree, scores the target model at every node, and selects low-performing nodes dynamically across tree levels, at the granularity where each failure is clearest. The selected weak capabilities then direct the generation of targeted supervised fine-tuning data. Holding the data-generation, fine-tuning, and evaluation setup fixed, we compare CRAFT against prompt-level EvalTree clustering and untargeted random generation on four open-source models, two professional domains (finance and legal), and 13 held-out benchmarks disjoint from the diagnostic data. CRAFT achieves the strongest finance-domain average for all four models under repeated temperature decoding; on legal domain, it is strongest for three of four models and remains within the decoding-variance bands of the best baseline on the fourth. Diagnosing weaknesses at the level of rubric criteria, rather than prompts or categories, thus yields both a sharper picture of what a model cannot do and measurably better models after fine-tuning on that diagnosis.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型的评估有两个目的。第一个，衡量模型今天的表现，受到了极大的关注：广泛的套件和有针对性的基准对模型在更多的场景、任务和指标中进行排名Evaluations of large language models serve two purposes. The first, measuring how a model performs today, has received enormous attention: broad suites and targeted benchmarks rank models across ever more scenarios, tasks, and metrics[12, 26, 21, 30]。第二个，告诉我们要修复什么，以便模型明天表现更好，尽管它是训练后数据收集最重要的目的，但仍然不发达。基准结果通常仍然总结为任务或类别级别的分数。这样的总结表明. The second, telling us what to fix so the model performs better tomorrow, remains underdeveloped, even though it is the purpose that matters most for post-training data collection. Benchmark results are still typically summarized as task- or category-level scores. Such summaries identify哪里where模型很弱；他们很少能够足够精确地识别失败的答案要求来指导下一个微调数据收集步骤。a model is weak; they rarely identify the failed answer requirements precisely enough to guide the next fine-tuning data collection step.

这种差距很重要，因为一个弱分数会合并许多不同的失败。以能力和行为为导向的评估This gap matters because a single weak score conflates many distinct failures. Capability- and behavior-oriented evaluations[28, 36]通过将模型行为分解为特定的技能或测试来实现更精细的解决方案。但即使在以技能为导向的观点下，数学基准的低分也可能反映出选择了错误的方程、犯了算术错误、跳过中间步骤或省略了所需的单元，即使这些失败发生在同一提示下，也需要不同的干预措施。停留在“数学弱”的评价无法在其中进行选择。move toward finer resolution by decomposing model behavior into specific skills or tests. But even under a skill-oriented view, a low score on a math benchmark may reflect choosing the wrong equation, making an arithmetic error, skipping an intermediate step, or omitting the required units, failures that call for different interventions even when they occur on the same prompt. An evaluation that stops at “weak on math” cannot choose among them.

量规为这种更精细的诊断提供了天然的基础。评分标准将答案质量分解为明确的要求：例如，数学问题评分标准可能会单独检查解决方案是否识别相关量、建立正确的方程、准确求解并使用正确的单位陈述最终答案。评分标准越来越多地用于评估和强化学习Rubrics provide a natural substrate for this finer diagnosis. Rubric criteria decompose answer quality into explicit requirements: for example, a math-problem rubric might separately check whether the solution identifies the relevant quantities, sets up the correct equation, solves it accurately, and states the final answer with the right units. Rubrics are increasingly used in evaluation and reinforcement learning[27, 32, 11, 22, 17, 33]，但现有的工作将标准视为评分工具：做出判断或训练奖励模型。相反，我们将标题数据集视为包含数千个功能探针的池。每个标准都衡量模型必须能够做到的事情。当在数据集中聚合时，评分标准会揭示出反复出现的弱点，而当单独处理每个评分标准时，这些弱点是看不见的。, but existing work treats criteria as scoring devices: to make judgments or to train reward models. We instead treat a rubric dataset as a pool of thousands of capability probes. Each criterion measures something the model must be able to do. When aggregated across a dataset, rubrics reveal recurring weaknesses that are invisible when each rubric is treated individually.

我们提出了 CRAFT（用于可操作微调的聚类评分标准），这是一种将评分标准注释的评估数据转换为针对特定模型的弱能力诊断的方法。 CRAFT 从每个提示-标题对中提取能力描述，将这些描述聚集成分层能力树，并在每个节点评估目标模型。由于弱点并不总是以相同的粒度出现，因此 CRAFT 会跨树级别动态选择性能低下的节点，而不是承诺固定的深度。在 CRAFT 生成这个弱节点集后，一个单独的下游生成步骤使用选定的节点来生成针对模型弱能力的微调数据。We propose CRAFT (Clustering Rubrics for Actionable Fine-Tuning), a method that converts rubric-annotated evaluation data into a model-specific diagnosis of weak capabilities. CRAFT extracts a capability description from each prompt–rubric pair, clusters these descriptions into a hierarchical capability tree, and evaluates the target model at every node. Because weaknesses do not always appear at the same granularity, CRAFT selects low-performing nodes dynamically across tree levels rather than committing to a fixed depth. After CRAFT has produced this weak-node set, a separate downstream generation step uses the selected nodes to produce fine-tuning data focused on the model’s weak capabilities.

2.1 量规Rubrics

基于评分标准的评估将响应判断分解为明确的标准，而不是要求单一的整体分数。这使得评分信号更容易解释：模型可能在事实基础上是正确的，但在完整性上是错误的，或者在推理上是正确的，但在最终答案格式上是错误的。 HealthBench 等基准测试Rubric-based evaluation decomposes a response judgment into explicit criteria rather than asking for a single holistic score. This makes the grading signal more interpretable: a model can be right on factual grounding but wrong on completeness, or correct in reasoning but wrong in final-answer format. Benchmarks such as HealthBench[2]和 PRBenchand PRBench[1]在专业领域使用大量专家编写的评分标准，相关数据集将评分标准监督扩展到其他专业和培训后环境use large collections of expert-written rubrics in professional domains, and related datasets extend rubric supervision to other specialized and post-training settings[40, 20]。第二个工作是使用类似标准的标准来改进基于法学硕士的评估，其中标准使法官的决策过程更加明确和细粒度. A second line of work uses rubric-like criteria to improve LLM-based evaluation, where criteria make the judge’s decision process more explicit and fine-grained[22, 17]。另一项工作使用标准或细粒度反馈作为训练后信号，包括奖励建模和人工智能反馈管道. Another line of work uses criteria or fine-grained feedback as post-training signals, including reward modeling and AI-feedback pipelines[39, 27, 33, 23, 11, 7]。 CRAFT 将相同的标准级信号用于不同的目的：它不是仅将标准视为独立的评分项目或奖励，而是将它们聚集在数据集中，以恢复重复出现的功能和故障的结构化图。. CRAFT uses the same criterion-level signal for a different purpose: instead of treating criteria only as independent scoring items or rewards, it clusters them across the dataset to recover a structured map of recurring capabilities and failures.

2.2 提示级层次聚类和 EvalTreePrompt-Level Hierarchical Clustering and EvalTree

最接近的先验方法是 EvalTreeThe closest prior method is EvalTree[37]，它通过将评估提示组织到分层能力树中来分析模型。 EvalTree对整个提示进行聚类，用自然语言能力标签描述每个内部节点，在每个子树上对目标模型进行评分，并使用低性能提示聚类来指导目标数据生成。提示级节点可以表示模型正在努力解决某种问题；它无法隔离答案中的哪个功能被遗漏。 CRAFT 集群提示 - 标题对。这使得一个提示可以提供多个叶，一个对应于它测试的每个标准，并让一个节点代表一种响应能力，例如应用法定例外或在正确的行项目中建立财务计算。因此，与 EvalTree 的比较直接测试当所选数据通过相同的下游微调工作流程时，评分标准是否在提示聚类之外添加诊断信息。, which profiles a model by organizing evaluation prompts into a hierarchical capability tree. EvalTree clusters whole prompts, describes each internal node with a natural-language capability label, scores the target model on each subtree, and uses low-performing prompt clusters to guide targeted data generation. A prompt-level node can say that a model struggles with a kind of question; it cannot isolate which capability inside the answer was missed. CRAFT clusters prompt–rubric pairs instead. This lets one prompt contribute multiple leaves, one for each criterion it tests, and lets a node represent a response capability such as applying a statutory exception or grounding a financial calculation in the correct line item. The comparison to EvalTree therefore directly tests whether rubric criteria add diagnostic information beyond prompt clustering when the selected data are passed through the same downstream fine-tuning workflow.

3.1 量规Rubrics

标题是响应必须满足的一组明确要求。每个标准都是简短的自然语言声明，例如“响应引用了控制法规”或“响应在计算比率之前确定了现金流量项目”。每个标准都可以独立评分为通过/失败或使用小的离散分数。响应可能会通过一些标准，但会失败其他标准。这使得评分标准不仅可以用作评分工具，还可以用作能力探针：每个标准都指定模型必须能够执行的操作，以使答案可接受。从这个意义上说，标准类似于对一项所需技能的小型行为测试，将评分标准与细粒度的行为评估联系起来A rubric is a set of explicit requirements that a response must satisfy. Each criterion is a short natural-language statement, such as “the response cites the controlling statute” or “the response identifies the cash-flow line item before computing the ratio.” Each criterion can be scored independently as pass/fail or with a small discrete score. A response might pass some criteria and fail others. This makes rubrics useful not only as grading instruments, but also as capability probes: each criterion names something the model must be able to do for the answer to be acceptable. In this sense, a criterion resembles a small behavioral test of one required skill, connecting rubric scoring to fine-grained behavioral evaluation[28, 36]。 CRAFT 建立在这一观察的基础上，通过聚合整个数据集的标准，而不是仅将它们用作每个示例的评分规则。. CRAFT builds on this observation by aggregating criteria across a dataset rather than using them only as per-example scoring rules.

3.2 从量规中提取能力Extracting Capabilities from Rubrics

单个提示可以包含许多标准，并且这些标准可以测试不同的技能。因此，我们将数据集扁平化为提示-标题对，并独立处理每一对。对于每一对，一个单独的语言模型都会生成一个简短的、脱离上下文的描述，描述满足标准所需的能力。输出是包含原始提示、评分标准和提取的能力描述的元组集合。这种表示方式允许标准聚集在一起，即使它们来自不同的提示或使用不同的措辞，只要它们测试相同的底层功能即可。A single prompt can contain many criteria, and those criteria may test different skills. We therefore flatten the dataset into prompt–rubric pairs and process each pair independently. For every pair, a separate language model produces a short, decontextualized description of the capability needed to satisfy the criterion. The output is a collection of tuples containing the original prompt, the rubric criterion, and the extracted capability description. This representation allows criteria to cluster together even when they come from different prompts or use different wording, as long as they test the same underlying capability.

3.3 层次聚类Hierarchical Clustering

我们将提取的能力描述组织成一棵树，采用自下而上的逐级程序，改编自 Clio 的层次结构器We organize the extracted capability descriptions into a tree with a bottom-up, level-by-level procedure adapted from the hierarchizer of Clio[31]。与经典的凝聚聚类不同，经典的凝聚聚类在每一步都会贪婪地合并最接近的一对节点，我们的方法一次构建一个完整的级别：给定当前级别的集群，LLM 写入更通用的功能标签的较小库存，每个当前集群都被路由到恰好一个标签，并且重复该过程，直到该级别包含目标数量的顶级集群。叶子对应于单独的提示-标题对，每个内部节点都带有自然语言能力描述，该描述比其子节点更通用，同时保留其域内容（图. Unlike classical agglomerative clustering, which greedily merges the closest pair of nodes at each step, our method constructs one entire level at a time: given the clusters at the current level, an LLM writes a smaller inventory of more general capability labels, every current cluster is routed to exactly one label, and the process repeats until the level contains a target number of top clusters. Leaves correspond to individual prompt–rubric pairs, and every internal node carries a natural-language capability description that is more general than its children while preserving their domain content (Figure1).

固定顶层。Fixed top levels.

当标题数据集已经提供分类元数据（例如域标签或每个标准子类别注释）时，我们使用它来定义树的最上层而不是学习它们：每个元数据属性成为一个固定级别（例如，域位于级别 0，带注释的子类别位于级别 1）。然后，将能力独立地聚类到每个结果组中，并将学习到的子树移植到相应的固定节点下。这保证了学习的集群永远不会混合带注释的类别，并保留数据集作者想要的任何分类法。如果没有此类元数据可用，则整个数据集形成一个组并学习完整的树。When the rubric dataset already provides categorical metadata, such as a domain label or a per-criterion subcategory annotation, we use it to define the uppermost levels of the tree rather than learning them: each metadata attribute becomes one fixed level (e.g., domain at level 0, annotated subcategory at level 1). Capabilities are then clustered independently within each resulting group, and the learned subtrees are grafted under the corresponding fixed nodes. This guarantees that learned clusters never mix annotated categories and preserves any taxonomy the dataset authors intended. This yields a pass/fail label for each prompt–rubric pair, which corresponds to a leaf in the capability tree. This judge step is important because all node scores inherit the criterion-level judge decisions; CRAFT therefore depends on judges that can apply rubric criteria consistently rather than only produce a holistic preference. We then aggregate these leaf scores upward: each internal node receives the average pass rate over the prompt–rubric pairs in its subtree. As a result, every node has both a semantic description and an empirical estimate of how reliably the target model satisfies that capability.

这个评分树让 CRAFT 在最清晰表达的粒度上选择弱能力。在我们的实验中，不同的模型通常在不同的深度表现出弱点：更强的模型可能只在狭窄的后代节点上失败，而较弱的模型可能会在更接近根的更广泛的父节点上遇到困难。因此，CRAFT 通过自上而下的跨级搜索来选择性能较低的节点，而不是致力于固定的树深度。仅当节点具有足够的支持（我们的主要实验中超过 30 个相关的评分标准）时，该节点才符合资格，因此所选节点反映了稳定的能力估计，而不是稀疏的工件。搜索从严格的通过率阈值开始，并逐渐放宽阈值，直到收集到目标数量的弱节点。一旦选择了一个节点，它的祖先和后代就被排除在外，从而防止重叠选择并留下一组不相交的弱能力区域。这个过程故意很简单：它使用树为每个弱点选择有用的粒度，而不是强加一个全局深度。部分This scored tree lets CRAFT choose weak capabilities at the granularity where they are most clearly expressed. In our experiments, different models often exhibit weaknesses at different depths: a stronger model may fail only on narrow descendant nodes, whereas a weaker model may struggle with broader parent capabilities closer to the root. CRAFT therefore selects low-performing nodes with a top-down cross-level search rather than committing to a fixed tree depth. A node is eligible only if it has sufficient support–more than 30 associated rubric criteria in our main experiments–so selected nodes reflect stable capability estimates rather than sparse artifacts. The search starts with a strict pass-rate threshold and relaxes it incrementally until the target number of weak nodes is collected. Once a node is selected, its ancestors and descendants are excluded, preventing overlapping selections and leaving a set of disjoint weak-capability regions. This procedure is intentionally simple: it uses the tree to choose a useful granularity for each weakness rather than imposing one global depth. 6.5 消融：动态与固定级别节点识别Ablation: Dynamic vs. Fixed-Level Node Identification

CRAFT 的一个核心设计选择是如何从带注释的能力树中选择弱能力。 CRAFT 不是修复树的单个级别并选择该级别性能最低的节点，而是使用动态搜索，以自然出现的任何粒度显示性能不佳的节点。为了验证这个动态过程是否有助于 CRAFT 的优势，并且不是一个可互换的实现细节，我们运行了一个消融，以隔离节点选择策略，同时保持其他所有内容固定。A central design choice in CRAFT is how weak capabilities are selected from the annotated capability tree. Rather than fixing a single level of the tree and selecting the lowest-performing nodes at that level, CRAFT uses a dynamic search that surfaces underperforming nodes at whichever granularity they naturally emerge. To verify that this dynamic procedure contributes to CRAFT’s advantage, and is not an interchangeable implementation detail, we run an ablation that isolates the node-selection strategy while holding everything else fixed.

我们在树的两个深度实例化固定级别选择：L5（最精细的级别）和 L4（较粗的级别），以便比较不依赖于一种特定的粒度选择。每个固定级别变体选择与动态过程相同数量的弱节点，并且管道的每个其他组件都保持不变。这隔离了动态选择是否比简单地从树中选择弱节点来提高性能。我们在法律和金融领域的两个模型 Qwen3-4B 和 Llama-3.1-8B 上运行消融，并在主要实验中使用的相同基准套件上评估每个最终的微调模型。表We instantiate fixed-level selection at two depths of the tree: L5, the finest level, and L4, one level coarser, so that the comparison is not tied to one particular choice of granularity. Each fixed-level variant selects the same number of weak nodes as the dynamic procedure, and every other component of the pipeline is unchanged. This isolates whether dynamic selection improves performance beyond simply choosing weak nodes from the tree. We run the ablation on two models, Qwen3-4B and Llama-3.1-8B, in both the legal and finance domains, and evaluate every resulting fine-tuned model on the same benchmark suites used in the main experiments. 动态选择在每个消融行中实现最佳平均值。重要的模式是固定深度替代方案的不稳定性：L4 在某些设置中更好，而 L5 在其他设置中更好。这正是 CRAFT 旨在避免的故障模式。如果模型的弱点存在于能力树中的不同深度，则固定级别要么将广泛的弱点分解为稀疏节点，要么将狭窄的弱点合并为过于普遍的弱点。动态选择通过在证据最强的粒度上选择薄弱节点来回避这种选择，这解释了为什么它仍然是跨模型和领域最可靠的策略。Dynamic selection achieves the best average in every ablation row. The important pattern is the instability of the fixed-depth alternatives: L4 is better in some settings, while L5 is better in others. This is exactly the failure mode CRAFT is designed to avoid. If the model’s weaknesses live at different depths in the capability tree, a fixed level either fragments broad weaknesses into sparse nodes or merges narrow weaknesses into overly general ones. Dynamic selection sidesteps this choice by selecting weak nodes at the granularity where the evidence is strongest, which explains why it remains the most reliable strategy across models and domains.

7 结论Conclusion

CRAFT 围绕一个实际的培训后问题重新构建了基于评分标准的评估：接下来我们应该培训什么？标准基准摘要可以识别薄弱的任务或类别，但它们很少指定模型未能满足哪些答案要求。 CRAFT 使用每个评分标准作为能力探测，将提示评分对聚类成评分层次结构，并跨深度动态选择薄弱节点。输出并不是新的微调配方；它是一组特定于模型的训练目标，源自测量的失败规则。CRAFT reframes rubric-based evaluation around a practical post-training question: what should we train on next? Standard benchmark summaries can identify weak tasks or categories, but they rarely specify which answer requirements the model failed to satisfy. CRAFT uses each rubric criterion as a capability probe, clusters prompt–rubric pairs into a scored hierarchy, and selects weak nodes dynamically across depths. The output is not a new fine-tuning recipe; it is a model-specific set of training targets derived from measured rubric failures.

从经验上看，这些目标很重要，尽管并不是在每个基准上都取得一致的胜利。在固定教师生成、数据预算、微调超参数和最终评估的情况下，CRAFT 为每个模型提供了最强的重复解码金融领域平均值。在法律上，它为四个模型中的三个提供了最强的重复解码域平均值，并且具有与第四个模型上的最佳基线重叠的解码方差带。这些比较隔离了增益的来源：随机显示结构和弱点定位有助于超越额外的域内数据，EvalTree 表明在此设置中，标题标准是比整个提示更有用的诊断单元。 CRAFT 在八个模型域设置中的七个中显示出最强的平均性能，并支持中心主张：当评估不仅仅报告低分，而是将测量到的故障转化为有针对性的微调数据时，评估会更有用。Empirically, those targets matter, though not as uniform wins on every benchmark. Holding teacher generation, data budget, fine-tuning hyperparameters, and final evaluation fixed, CRAFT gives the strongest repeated-decoding finance-domain average for every model. In legal, it gives the strongest repeated-decoding domain average for three of four models and has overlapping decoding-variance bands with the best baseline on the fourth. The comparisons isolate where the gain comes from: Random shows that structure and weakness targeting help beyond additional in-domain data, EvalTree shows that rubric criteria are a more useful diagnostic unit than whole prompts in this setting. CRAFT shows strongest average performance in seven of eight model-domain settings and supports the central claim: evaluations are more useful when they do not stop at reporting low scores, and instead translate measured failures into targeted fine-tuning data.
