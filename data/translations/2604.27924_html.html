摘要Abstract

同行评审是一个多阶段的过程，涉及评审、反驳、元评审、最终决定和随后的手稿修改。大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展激发了协助或自动化该流程不同阶段的方法。在本次调查中，我们综合了（i）同行评审生成的技术，包括微调策略、基于代理的系统、基于强化学习的方法以及增强生成的新兴范例； (ii) 审查后任务，包括反驳、元审查和与审查一致的修订； (iii) 评估方法涵盖以人为本、以参考为基础、以法学硕士为基础和以方面为导向。我们对数据集进行分类，比较模型选择，并讨论局限性、道德问题和未来方向。该调查旨在为在整个同行评审工作流程中构建、评估和集成法学硕士系统提供实用指导。以下存储库提供了论文集：Peer review is a multi-stage process involving reviews, rebuttals, meta-reviews, final decisions, and subsequent manuscript revisions. Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have motivated methods that assist or automate different stages of this pipeline. In this survey, we synthesize techniques for (i) peer review generation, including fine-tuning strategies, agent-based systems, RL-based methods, and emerging paradigms to enhance generation; (ii) after-review tasks including rebuttals, meta-review and revision aligned to reviews; and (iii) evaluation methods spanning human-centered, reference-based, LLM-based and aspect-oriented. We catalog datasets, compare modeling choices, and discuss limitations, ethical concerns, and future directions. The survey aims to provide practical guidance for building, evaluating, and integrating LLM systems across the full peer review workflow. A collection of papers is available at the following repository:https://github.com/formula12/Awesome-Peer-Review.

2.1 基础方法Foundation Approaches

在法学硕士广泛采用之前，自动生成完整、高质量的同行评审是很困难的Prior to the widespread adoption of LLMs, the automated generation of complete, high-quality peer reviews was difficultDrozdz and Ladomery [2024]。生成评论的早期尝试通常解决不同的问题，例如现有评论的多文档摘要. Early attempts to generate reviews often addressed a different problem, such as the multi-document summarization of existing reviewsLi et al. [2022], Fabbri et al. [2019]，或依赖引用网络，而不是直接从源手稿生成新颖的批评, or relied on citation networks rather than generating novel critique directly from a source manuscriptLi and Zhuge [2021]。因此，2023 年之前的研究主要集中在一系列更易于管理的子任务上。这些基础性工作将同行评审过程解构为分析过程. Consequently, research in the pre-2023 era predominantly focused on a suite of more manageable sub-tasks. These foundational efforts deconstructed the peer review process into analyticalTeufel et al. [1999], 预测性, predictiveKang et al. [2018]，以及狭义的生成组件, and narrowly generative componentsDycke et al. [2023b]。尽管这些研究的能力有限，无法提供完整的评论，但它们为后续突破建立了关键数据集。. While limited in their ability to produce complete reviews, these researches established critical datasets for subsequent breakthroughs.

6 结论Conclusion

在大型语言模型和最新范例的进步的推动下，同行评审过程的格局已经发生了变革。这项调查全面综合了最先进的方法，涵盖评审生成以及评审后任务，包括反驳、元评审和修订。我们还提出了评估方法、指标和数据集的系统分类法。尽管生成方法有了显着改进，但仍然存在重大挑战，包括强大的内容级评估和广泛的功能。随着该领域的进步，未来的研究必须优先考虑透明和道德的自动化审查部署。The landscape of peer review process has undergone a transformative shift, driven by advances in large language models and latest paradigms. This survey has provided a comprehensive synthesis of state-of-the-art methods, spanning review generation as well as after-review tasks including rebuttal, meta-review, and revision. We also present a systematic taxonomy of evaluation methods, metrics and datasets. Despite remarkable improvements in generation methods, significant challenges remain, including robust content-level evaluation and extensive capabilities. As the field advances, future research must prioritize transparent and ethical deployment of automated reviews.

局限性Limitations

尽管本次调查致力于提供同行评审形势的全面且最新的概述，但仍然存在一些局限性。首先，大型语言模型、基于代理的系统和评估协议的快速进展意味着新的方法和基准可能在发布后很快就会出现，这可能会使我们的一些分析过时。其次，大多数可用的数据集和评估研究都集中在 NLP 和机器学习领域，限制了我们的研究结果对其他科学领域的推广。最后，我们的调查基于公开的文献和资源；专有系统或未发表的工业进步可能无法得到充分体现。尽管存在这些限制，我们相信这项调查仍然是迄今为止同行评审过程及其评估最全面、最细粒度的分类。While this survey strives to provide a comprehensive and up-to-date overview of the peer review landscape, several limitations remain. First, the rapid pace of progress in large language models, agent-based systems, and evaluation protocols means that new methodologies and benchmarks may emerge soon after publication, potentially outdating some of our analyses. Second, the majority of available datasets and evaluation studies are concentrated in NLP and machine learning domains, limiting the generalizability of our findings to other scientific fields. Finally, our survey is based on publicly accessible literature and resources; proprietary systems or unpublished industrial advances may not be adequately represented. Despite these limitations, we believe this survey remains the most comprehensive and fine-grained taxonomy of peer review process and its evaluation to date.

评估生成的同行评审的质量是一项多方面且不平凡的任务。该领域开发了各种各样的评估指标，这些指标已经从标准的文本相似性衡量标准发展到更复杂的多维框架，旨在捕捉评论的功能效用。这些指标可以大致分为五个方面：基于参考的文本相似性、集合重叠、方面和焦点级别分析、基于任务的代理以及无监督或基于奖励的方法。Evaluating the quality of generated peer reviews is a multifaceted and non-trivial task. The field has developed a diverse array of evaluation metrics, which have evolved from standard text-similarity measures to more sophisticated, multi-dimensional frameworks that aim to capture the functional utility of a review. These metrics can be broadly categorized into five facets: reference-based text similarity, set overlap, aspect- and focus-level analysis, task-based proxies, and unsupervised or reward-based methods.

A.1 基于参考的文本相似度Reference-based Text Similarity

评估生成评论的最初方法，特别是那些将任务框架为总结形式的方法，依赖于衡量生成评论与真实人类评论之间相似性的标准指标。这些指标提供了一种可扩展的自动化方法来评估表面质量。Initial approaches to evaluating generated reviews, particularly those framing the task as a form of summarization, relied on standard metrics that measure the similarity between a generated review and a ground-truth human review. This approach treats the generated and reference reviews as sets of comments and assesses their intersection.

Only pairs with high relatedness and sufficient specificity are counted as matches, ensuring that low-utility or generic comments do not inflate metric scores. A.3 方面和焦点级别指标Aspect and Focus Level Metrics

附录的这一部分讨论了与使用 2023 年后同行评审数据集相关的实际挑战，并提出了缓解策略。我们的目标是帮助研究人员做出明智的选择，并激发未来数据集构建和细化的改进。This part of the appendix discusses the practical challenges associated with using post-2023 peer review datasets and proposes mitigation strategies. Our goal is to help researchers make informed choices and inspire improvements in future dataset construction and refinement.

挑战。Challenges.

尽管在数据集规模、多样性和注释丰富性方面取得了显着进步，但最近的同行评审生成数据集仍然存在一些局限性。许多语料库仍然针对分数预测或分类等辅助任务进行优化，而不是全文评论生成，这导致单个评论句子和特定论文内容之间的一致性较弱。基于对话的数据集通常会遇到说话者角色不一致、对话线索不完整或作者回答不明确的问题，这使得它们很难在多智能体训练设置中使用。注释稀缺是另一个常见的障碍：提供细粒度批评标签的数据集通常很小，限制了它们在监督学习中的实用性。此外，评论级别的分数和方面注释很少与句子级别的元数据配对，这使得评估或优化本地评论质量变得困难。领域覆盖范围也仍然狭窄，大多数数据集仅专注于 NLP 和 ML 场所，这引发了人们对普遍性的担忧。Despite significant advancements in dataset scale, diversity, and annotation richness, several limitations persist across recent datasets for peer review generation. Many corpora remain optimized for auxiliary tasks such as score prediction or classification, rather than full-text review generation, which results in weak alignment between individual review sentences and specific paper content. Dialogue-based datasets often suffer from inconsistency in speaker roles, incomplete conversational threads, or ambiguous author responses, making them difficult to use in multi-agent training setups. Annotation scarcity is another common obstacle: datasets offering fine-grained critique labels are often small, limiting their utility for supervised learning. Additionally, review-level scores and aspect annotations are rarely paired with sentence-level metadata, making it hard to evaluate or optimize local review quality. Domain coverage also remains narrow, with most datasets focusing exclusively on NLP and ML venues—raising concerns about generalizability.

缓解措施。Mitigation.

为了解决这些问题，未来的数据集工作应强调结构的一致性和一致性。在评论句子和他们批评的特定部分之间建立映射将能够实现内容感知的生成和解释。对话数据集可以受益于更严格的角色标准化和过滤管道，消除不完整或不连贯的线索。在注释预算有限的情况下，半监督扩展技术（例如标签传播、弱监督或基于 LLM 的伪注释）可以帮助扩展细粒度标签。此外，将评论级别的分数与句子级别的效用判断配对将使批评生成的评估和训练更加精确。最后，未来的数据集应致力于扩展到 NLP/ML 领域之外，并纳入来自不同学科的评论，以更好地支持通用评论生成模型和跨领域评估。To address these issues, future dataset efforts should emphasize structural coherence and alignment. Establishing mappings between review sentences and the specific sections they critique would enable content-aware generation and interpretation. Dialogue datasets can benefit from more rigorous role standardization and filtering pipelines that remove incomplete or incoherent threads. Where annotation budgets are limited, semi-supervised expansion techniques—such as label propagation, weak supervision, or LLM-based pseudo-annotation—could help scale fine-grained labels. Additionally, pairing review-level scores with sentence-level utility judgments would enable more precise evaluation and training of critique generation. Finally, future datasets should aim to extend beyond the NLP/ML domain and incorporate reviews from diverse disciplines to better support general-purpose review generation models and cross-domain evaluation.

未来的系统可能不会专注于完全自动化，而是专注于创建更无缝、更强大的人机协作工具。这可能涉及实时交互式界面，人类审阅者可以在其中指导、纠正和查询人工智能助手，利用其快速检索文献、分析数据和起草文本的能力，同时保留最终的人类监督和判断。Rather than aiming for full automation, future systems will likely focus on creating more seamless and powerful human-AI collaboration tools. The central challenge in this domain extends beyond the technical task of processing pixels or parsing table cells; it lies in bridging a profound semantic gap. Therefore, the most formidable aspect of multimodal integration is not the technical encoding of visual data, but rather teaching a model to perform this complex interpretive leap from perception to conceptual understanding. C.4 方法论之间的比较Comparison Between Methodologies

基于代理的方法与强化学习。Agent-Based Methods vs. Reinforcement Learning.

基于代理的建模和强化学习代表了生成同行评审的两种不同的理念。基于代理的方法，例如 AgentReviewAgent-based modeling and Reinforcement Learning represent two distinct philosophies for generating peer reviews. Agent-based approaches, such as AgentReviewJin et al. [2024a]，擅长模拟整个同行评审生态系统复杂的、交互式的社会动态。然而，这些系统的主要目标通常是研究突发行为，而不是优化任何单个生成的评论的质量。本次重点关注过程模拟。相比之下，强化学习直接针对评论工件的优化。 REMOR 等框架, excel at simulating the complex, interactive social dynamics of the entire peer review ecosystem. However, the primary goal of these systems is often to study emergent behaviors rather than to optimize the quality of any single generated review. This focus on process simulation. In contrast, Reinforcement Learning directly targets the optimization of the review artifact. Frameworks like REMORTaechoyotin and Acuna [2025]将评论生成框架视为一种优化策略，以最大化多目标奖励函数，该函数经过精心设计，以概括人类对构成有用评论的偏好。这使得基于强化学习的方法能够有效地引导一代人远离普通法学硕士经常产生的肤浅、过度积极的反馈。frame review generation as a policy that is optimized to maximize a multi-objective reward function, which is carefully designed to encapsulate human preferences for what constitutes a helpful review. This allows RL-based methods to effectively steer generation away from the shallow, overly positive feedback that vanilla LLMs often produce.

迭代细化与单遍生成。Iterative Refinement vs. Single-Pass Generation.

迭代细化是一种强大的技术，它模仿人类起草、批评和修改的认知过程Iterative refinement is a powerful technique that mimics the human cognitive process of drafting, critiquing, and revisingXu et al. [2025], Kamoi et al. [2024]。这种方法对于同行评审等复杂、多方面的任务特别有效，因为它允许模型解决在单次传递中经常错过的目标，并可以释放 LLMS 的全部潜力。主要缺点是延迟和计算成本显着增加，因为每个反馈和优化周期都会构成额外的、昂贵的调用。. This approach is particularly effective for complex, multi-faceted tasks like peer review, as it allows the model to address objectives that are often missed in a single pass and can unlock the full potential of LLMS. The primary drawback is a significant increase in latency and computational cost, as each feedback-and-refine cycle constitutes an additional, expensive call.

单遍生成是标准自回归方法，是计算效率最高的方法。然而，它本质上受到其无法执行全局规划或纠正早期错误的限制。Single-pass generation, the standard autoregressive approach, is the most computationally efficient method. However, it is inherently limited by its inability to perform global planning or correct early mistakes.

检索增强与自包含生成。Retrieval-Augmented vs. Self-Contained Generation.

检索增强生成 (RAG)Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)Lewis et al. [2021]对于将同行评审建立在可验证的外部知识上至关重要，这是评估论文新颖性或检查事实不准确等任务的必要条件。然而，RAG 引入了显着的架构复杂性和新的关键故障点：检索器。最终生成的质量从根本上受到检索上下文质量的瓶颈；不相关或低质量的检索会导致糟糕的、无用的评论。is critical for grounding peer reviews in verifiable, external knowledge, a necessity for tasks like assessing a paper’s novelty or checking for factual inaccuracies. However, RAG introduces significant architectural complexity and a new critical point of failure: the retriever. The quality of the final generation is fundamentally bottlenecked by the quality of the retrieved context; irrelevant or low-quality retrieval leads to poor, unhelpful reviews.

自包含生成仅依赖于法学硕士的参数知识，其架构更简单，推理速度更快。它的主要局限性是它的知识是静态的，可能已经过时，并且容易出现事实发明（幻觉），这使得它从根本上不适合严格评估提交的内容对快速发展的领域的贡献。因此，虽然独立的生成可能足以评估清晰度或演示等方面，但对论文的技术实质和新颖性的任何深入评估都需要以外部知识为基础。Self-contained generation, which relies solely on the LLM’s parametric knowledge, is architecturally simpler and faster at inference. Its primary limitation is that its knowledge is static, potentially outdated, and prone to factual invention (hallucination), making it fundamentally unsuited for rigorously evaluating a submission’s contribution to a rapidly evolving field. Therefore, while self-contained generation may suffice for assessing aspects like clarity or presentation, any deep evaluation of a paper’s technical substance and novelty necessitates grounding in external knowledge.
