摘要Abstract

专家混合 (MoE) 已成为千亿参数语言模型事实上的架构，但其在设备上部署的数十亿规模的优势在很大程度上尚未被开发。为了缩小这一差距，我们提出Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) has become the de facto architecture for hundred-billion-parameter language models, yet its advantages at sub-billion scales for on-device deployment remain largely unexplored. To close this gap, we present移动教育部MobileMoE，一系列设备上的 MoE 语言模型，具有数十亿个活动参数（活动参数为 0.3-0.9B，总计为 1.3-5.3B），为设备上的 LLM 建立了新的 Pareto 前沿。我们首先制定了设备上的 MoE 缩放法则，在移动内存和计算限制下联合优化 MoE 架构，确定设备上的最佳点——细粒度和共享专家的适度稀疏性——同时实现内存和计算最优。在派生架构的基础上，我们使用四阶段配方来训练 MobileMoE，涵盖预训练、训练中期、指令微调和量化感知训练，所有这些都在开源数据集上进行。在 14 项基准测试中，MobileMoE 与领先的设备上密集法学硕士相媲美或超过 2-4, a family of on-device MoE language models with sub-billion active parameters (0.3-0.9B active and 1.3-5.3B total) that establish a new Pareto frontier for on-device LLMs. We first formulate an on-device MoE scaling law that jointly optimizes MoE architecture under mobile memory and compute constraints, identifying an on-device sweet spot – moderate sparsity with fine-grained and shared experts – that is simultaneously memory and compute-optimal. Building on the derived architectures, we train MobileMoE with a four-stage recipe covering pre-training, mid-training, instruction fine-tuning, and quantization-aware training, all on open-source datasets. Across 14 benchmarks, MobileMoE matches or exceeds leading on-device dense LLMs with 2-4×\times更少的推理 FLOP，并且与最先进的 MoE OLMoE-1B-7B 相当或超过，参数减少高达 60%。为了弥合移动部署的最后一英里，我们在商用智能手机上提供了第一个高效的 MoE 推理，并提供全面的设备分析。在可比的 INT4 重量存储器中，MobileMoE-S 提供fewer inference FLOPs, and matches or surpasses the state-of-the-art MoE OLMoE-1B-7B with up to 60% fewer parameters. To bridge the last mile to mobile deployment, we provide the first efficient MoE inference on commodity smartphones with comprehensive on-device profiling. While existing scaling laws target server-scale LLMs, we formulate an on-device MoE scaling law to derive architecture under mobile memory and compute constraints, with an end-to-end training recipe to scale sub-billion-active MoE on devices.

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presented移动教育部MobileMoE，一系列设备上的 MoE 语言模型。我们的工作开发了一种通用的设备上 MoE 扩展法则，可在移动内存和计算限制下联合优化架构；一种端到端配方，可扩展 MobileMoE 训练，为设备上 LLM 的基准性能建立新的帕累托前沿；以及第一个在商用智能手机 CPU 上高效的设备上 MoE 部署，并跨 CPU 和 GPU 后端进行系统分析，这些共同证明 MoE 是边缘的实用路径。几个有希望的方向可以进一步建立在这项工作的基础上。在教育部培训方面，精炼、面向推理的后期培训和多模式扩展可以释放更好的性能和能力。在 MoE 运行时方面，动态路由、模型压缩（例如专家剪枝、混合精度量化）和移动 NPU 部署可以进一步提高设备上的效率。虽然现有的设备上模型仍然主要是密集的，但我们表明 MoE 提供了更有效的替代方案。我们希望 MobileMoE 为下一代设备端 AI 开辟新方向，为智能手机、可穿戴设备和实体代理等边缘设备带来强大、高效的稀疏 LLM，实现本地智能，降低云计算需求，同时提供私密、低延迟的推理。, a family of on-device MoE language models. Our work develops a generalized on-device MoE scaling law that jointly optimizes architecture under mobile memory and compute constraints, an end-to-end recipe that scales MobileMoE training to establish a new Pareto frontier for on-device LLMs in benchmark performance, and the first efficient on-device MoE deployment on commodity smartphone CPUs, with systematic profiling across CPU and GPU backends, which together demonstrate MoE as a practical path at the edge. Several promising directions can further build on this work. On the MoE training side, distillation, reasoning-oriented post-training, and multimodal extensions could unlock better performance and capabilities. On the MoE runtime side, dynamic routing, model compression (e.g., expert pruning, mixed-precision quantization), and mobile NPU deployment could yield further on-device efficiency. While existing on-device models remain predominantly dense, we show that MoE offers a more efficient alternative. We hope MobileMoE opens new directions for next-generation on-device AI, bringing capable, efficient sparse LLMs to edge devices such as smartphones, wearables, and embodied agents, enabling local intelligence that lowers cloud compute demand while delivering private, low-latency inference.
