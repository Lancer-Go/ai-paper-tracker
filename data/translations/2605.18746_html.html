ESI-基准ESI-Bench：迈向闭合感知-行动循环的具体空间智能: Towards Embodied Spatial Intelligence that Closes the Perception-Action Loop

Yining Hong1   Jiageng Liu2   Han Yin1   Manling Li3   Leonidas Guibas1   Li Fei-Fei1   Jiajun Wu1   Yejin Choi1

1Stanford University   2UCLA   3Northwestern University
https://esi-bench.github.io/
摘要Abstract

空间智能通过感知-行动循环展开：智能体采取行动来获取观察结果，并推理观察结果如何随着行动而变化。 而不是被动地处理事情Spatial intelligence unfolds through a perception–action loop: agents act to acquire observations, and reason about how observations vary as a function of action. Rather than passively processing what is见过seen，他们积极地揭示什么是, they actively uncover what is看不见的unseen- 封闭的结构、动力学、遏制和功能无法仅通过被动传感来解决。 通过将观察者重新塑造为行动者，我们超越了先前假设神谕观察的空间智能表述。 我们介绍- occluded structure, dynamics, containment, and functionality that cannot be resolved from passive sensing alone. We move beyond prior formulations of spatial intelligence that assume oracle observations by recasting the observer as an actor. We introduceESI-基准ESI-Bench，一个涵盖 10 个任务类别和 29 个子类别的体现空间智能的综合基准，建立在 OmniGibson 之上，以 Spelke 的核心知识系统为基础。特工必须决定部署哪些能力——感知、运动和操纵——以及如何对它们进行排序以积极积累与任务相关的证据。 我们对最先进的 MLLM 进行了广泛的实验，发现主动探索大大优于被动探索，智能体在没有明确指令的情况下自发发现紧急空间策略，而随机多视图通常会增加噪声而不是信号，尽管消耗了更多的图像。大多数失败不是源于薄弱的感知，而是源于行动的盲目性：糟糕的行动选择导致糟糕的观察，进而导致级联错误。虽然显式 3D 基础可以稳定深度敏感任务的推理，但事实证明，不完美的 3D 表示会扭曲空间关系，从而比 2D 基线更有害。人类研究进一步表明，与在矛盾下寻求证伪观点和修正信念的人类不同，无论证据质量如何，模型都会过早地以高置信度做出承诺，暴露出元认知差距，而单靠更好的感知和更具体的互动都无法弥补这一差距。所有数据、数据集构建代码和评估脚本均可在以下位置公开获取：, a comprehensive benchmark for embodied spatial intelligence spanning 10 task categories and 29 subcategories built on OmniGibson, grounded in Spelke’s core knowledge systems. Agents must decide what abilities to deploy — perception, locomotion, and manipulation — and how to sequence them to actively accumulate task-relevant evidence. We conduct extensive experiments on state-of-the-art MLLMs and find that active exploration substantially outperforms passive counterparts, with agents spontaneously discovering emergent spatial strategies without explicit instructions, while random multi-view often adds noise rather than signal despite consuming far more images. Most failures stem not from weak perception but from action blindness: poor action choices lead to poor observations, which in turn drive cascading errors. While explicit 3D grounding stabilizes reasoning on depth-sensitive tasks, imperfect 3D representation proves more harmful than 2D baselines by distorting spatial relations. Human studies further reveal that unlike humans who seek falsifying viewpoints and revise beliefs under contradiction, models commit prematurely with high confidence regardless of evidence quality, exposing a metacognitive gap that neither better perception nor more embodied interaction alone can close. All data, dataset construction codes, and evaluation scripts are publicly available athttps://esi-bench.github.io/.

1 简介Introduction

认知科学中的感知通常被描述为Perception is often characterized in cognitive science as感知引导的行动perceptually guided action (Varela et al., 1991; Gibson, 1979)：感知-行动循环，了解观察如何随着行动而变化: a perception–action loop where knowing how observations change as a function of action(O’Regan and Noë, 2001)，并了解哪些行为引发信息感知，通常比感知本身更具挑战性。这对于空间智能尤其重要，它不仅涉及到什么, and knowing which actions elicit informative sensing, are often more challenging than sensing itself. This is especially critical in spatial intelligence, which concerns not only what is见过seen，而且还有什么是, but also what is看不见的unseen。被动感知无法获得的隐藏结构、动力学、包容性和功能等潜在物理特性必须通过交互主动揭示，使空间智能内在体现。. Latent physical properties such as occluded structure, dynamics, containment, and functionality that are inaccessible to passive sensing must be actively revealed through interaction, making spatial intelligence inherently embodied.

我们超越了先前假设被动预言观察的空间智能表述We move beyond prior formulations of spatial intelligence that assume passive oracle observations(Liu et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2025a, d)通过将观察者重新塑造为演员。 我们的工作与之前的工作在三个关键方面进行了对比：（1）by recasting the observer as an actor. Our work contrasts with prior works in three key ways: (1)从空间感知到空间能力from spatial sensing to spatial competence，对智能体的评估不仅取决于他们能感知到什么，还取决于他们是否知道部署哪些具体能力来解决空间任务； (2), where agents are evaluated not only on what they can perceive, but on whether they know which embodied abilities to deploy to solve spatial tasks; (2)选择性传感selective sensing，代理必须确定哪些观察值值得获取，优先考虑与任务相关的信息而不是冗余或无信息的输入；和（3）, where agents must determine which observations are worth acquiring, prioritizing task-relevant information over redundant or uninformative inputs; and (3)解决感知模糊性resolving perceptual ambiguities，智能体必须通过误导性的观察进行推理，以推断出隐藏的空间结构和超出直接观察到的潜在物理约束。, where agents must reason through misleading observations to infer hidden spatial structures and underlying physical constraints beyond what is directly observed.

我们介绍We introduceESI-基准ESI-Bench，一个涵盖 10 个任务类别、29 个子类别和 3,081 个任务实例的体现空间智能的综合基准，通过关注仅通过被动观察无法回答的问题来解决关键的感知-行动差距。我们的品类设计遵循 Spelke 的核心知识体系, a comprehensive benchmark for embodied spatial intelligence spanning 10 task categories, 29 subcategories and 3,081 task instances, addressing the critical perception–action gap by focusing on questions that cannot be answered from passive observation alone. Our category design follows Spelke’s core knowledge systems(Spelke and Kinzler, 2007)，它确定了空间智能的四个能力：对象表示、布局和几何、数字表示以及代理和目标驱动的操作。基于这些理论基础，我们进行了人类调查，以确定最具挑战性的空间任务，这些任务需要每个教师内部的具体互动和操作，这些任务被提炼成涵盖不同形式的空间推理的结构化分类法，如图所示, which identify four faculties of spatial intelligence: object representation, layout and geometry, number representation, and agents & goal-driven actions. Building on these theoretical foundations, we conduct human surveys to identify the most challenging spatial tasks that require embodied interaction and manipulation within each faculty, which are distilled into a structured taxonomy spanning diverse forms of spatial reasoning, as illustrated in Figure1。只有当智能体拥有身体、信念状态和结果中的实际利害关系时，这些任务才变得有意义：智能体必须确定部署哪些能力（感知、运动、操纵）、采取哪些行动（移动到哪里、探测什么、如何操纵）以及如何以正确的顺序执行它们以成功回答问题。. These tasks only become meaningful when an agent has a body, a belief state, and physical stakes in the outcome: the agent must determine what abilities to deploy (perception, locomotion, manipulation), which actions to take (where to move, what to probe, how to manipulate), and how to execute them in the right order to successfully answer the questions.

我们对最先进的 MLLM 进行了广泛的实验，涵盖三种范式：被动单视图、被动随机多视图和主动探索，以及区分感知错误和行动错误的真实预言。我们的实验揭示了多个关键见解：(1)We conduct extensive experiments on state-of-the-art MLLMs across three paradigms: passive single-view, passive random multi-view, and active exploration, alongside a ground-truth oracle that separates perception errors from action errors. Our experiments reveal multiple key insights: (1)积极探索解锁新兴空间策略active exploration unlocks emergent spatial strategies：在没有明确指示的情况下，主动代理会自发地发现不同的动作组合，从而比被动代理获得实质性收益，而被动随机多视图尽管消耗了更多的图像，但通常会增加噪音而不是信号； (2): without explicit instruction, active agents spontaneously discover diverse action compositions, driving substantial gains over passive counterparts while passive random multi-view, despite consuming far more images, often adds noise rather than signal; (2)行动盲目性主导知觉盲目性action blindness dominates perceptual blindness：对于大多数任务来说，瓶颈不是感知而是行动选择，因为给予预言机观点的模型在很大程度上取得了成功，但某些任务暴露了任何行动都无法克服的硬感知天花板；和（3）: for most tasks the bottleneck is not perception but action selection, as models given oracle viewpoints succeed largely, yet certain tasks expose a hard perceptual ceiling that no action can overcome; and (3)故障级联和复合failures cascade and compound：次优操作会产生无信息视图，从而引发更糟糕的后续操作，从而形成无法在步骤预算内恢复的复合错误链。: suboptimal actions produce uninformative views, which trigger worse subsequent actions, creating a compounding chain of errors that cannot be recovered within the step budget.

我们进一步研究显式 3D 表示是否有帮助。我们发现，虽然显式 3D 基础通过恢复 2D 投影从根本上丢失的信息来稳定深度敏感任务的推理，但不完美的重建证明比 2D 基线更有害，因为几何伪影会主动扭曲细粒度的空间关系并误导下游推理。 最后，人类研究揭示了认知校准中的一个关键差距（We further investigate whether explicit 3D representations can help. We find that while explicit 3D grounding stabilizes reasoning on depth-sensitive tasks by recovering information that 2D projections fundamentally lose, imperfect reconstructions prove more harmful than 2D baselines, as geometric artifacts actively distort fine-grained spatial relations and mislead downstream reasoning. Finally, human studies expose a critical gap in epistemic calibration (即i.e.，模型的置信度与其证据的质量和不确定性相匹配）。我们观察到，与积极寻求伪造观点、探索正交角度并在矛盾时修正信念的人类不同，无论证据质量如何，模型都会过早地以一致的高置信度做出承诺，锚定第一印象并忽略矛盾的观察结果，这是一种元认知失败，无论是更好的感知还是更具体的互动都无法单独解决。, a model’s confidence matches the quality and uncertainty of its evidence). We observe that unlike humans who actively seek falsifying viewpoints, explore orthogonal angles, and revise their beliefs when contradicted, models commit prematurely with uniformly high confidence regardless of evidence quality, anchoring to first impressions and ignoring contradictory observations, a metacognitive failure that neither better perception nor more embodied interaction alone can close.

OmniGibson, built on NVIDIA Isaac Sim and PhysX 5, supports embodied spatial evaluation through rigid-body contact physics, particle-based fluids, transparency rendering, realistic lighting and reflections, and extended object states such as fill levels and toggled states. For each task instance, we randomly sample a BEHAVIOR-1K scene and select rooms based on room type and task-category requirements from a combined room-object list. 3.2 任务构建Task Construction

任务建议。Task Proposal.

GPT-4o 根据场景图和任务类别要求进行提示，从完整 BEHAVIOR-1K 库存中抽取的 200 个候选类别的随机样本中选择与任务相关的对象，应用特定于任务的物理标准来选择最合适的类别并解析每个类别的特定模型实例。除了对象选择之外，GPT-4o 还确定场景中对象和代理的初始位置，并生成地面实况动作轨迹，提供解决任务所需的最佳动作序列。所选对象及其空间配置隐含地定义了任务，并给出了真实答案GPT-4o is prompted with the scene graph alongside task category requirements to select task-relevant objects from a random sample of 200 candidate categories drawn from the full BEHAVIOR-1K inventory, applying task-specific physical criteria to choose the most appropriate categories and resolve a specific model instance per category. Beyond object selection, GPT-4o also determines the initial positions of both the objects and the agent within the scene, and generates a ground-truth action trajectory providing the optimal sequence of actions needed to resolve the task. The selected objects and their spatial configuration implicitly define the task, with the ground-truth answery∗y^{*}直接从结果场景状态导出。derived directly from the resulting scene state.

场景实例化。Scene Instantiation.

给定 GPT-4o 建议的对象选择和初始位置，我们将对象加载到场景中。在放置之前，我们使用 bbox 相交测试检查与现有场景内容的冲突。然后通过基于物理的运动学采样将对象放置在支撑表面上，并进行固定数量的模拟步骤。解决后，我们通过重新查询的 bbox 的稳定性检查、使用分段掩码的每个视图对象存在检查以及适用时的接触标志验证来验证每个配置。未通过任何检查的配置将被拒绝。Given GPT-4o-proposed object selections and initial positions, we load objects into the scene. Before placement, we check conflicts with existing scene content using bbox intersection tests. Objects are then placed on supporting surfaces via physics-based kinematic sampling and settle for a fixed number of simulation steps. After settling, we validate each configuration through stability checks from re-queried bboxes, per-view object existence checks using segmentation masks, and contact-flag validation when applicable. Configurations failing any check are rejected.

代理轨迹集合。Agent Trajectory Collection.

该代理以 GPT-4o 提出的姿势进行初始化，使用受控随机化和旨在保留场景配置和属性的预定义放置规则进行采样。在初始化时，我们应用与场景实例化相同的验证电池：每个视图对象存在检查、bbox 重新查询以及适用时的接触标志验证。所提出的行动是逐步执行的，每个步骤都呈现为以自我为中心的观察，并使用相同的检查进行验证。在任何步骤验证失败的轨迹将被丢弃。The agent is initialized at the GPT-4o-proposed pose, sampled using controlled randomization and predefined placement rules designed to withhold the scene configuration and properties. At initialization, we apply the same verification battery used for scene instantiation: per-view object existence checks, bbox re-querying, and contact-flag validation when applicable. Proposed actions are executed step by step, with each step rendered as an egocentric observation and verified using the same checks. Trajectories failing verification at any step are discarded.

元数据保存。Metadata Saving.

完成轨迹收集后，我们将每个任务实例保存到结构化 JSON 文件，其中包含场景、房间、楼层、每个对象类别和模型实例、验证的初始位置和四元数、代理初始姿势和四元数Upon completing trajectory collection, we save each task instance to a structured JSON file containing the scene, room, floor, per-object category and model instance, verified initial position and quaternion, agent initial pose and quaternionp0p_{0}、每个视图对象存在标志、问题、真实答案和动作轨迹。此元数据提供了任务实例的独立、可重现的记录，可以直接重新加载到 BEHAVIOR 环境中。, per-view object existence flags, question, ground-truth answer, and action trajectory. This metadata provides a self-contained, reproducible record of the task instance that can be directly reloaded into the BEHAVIOR environment.

人工验证和生成器偏差审计。Human Verification and Generator Bias Audit.

所有生成的任务实例均由人工注释者使用渲染的每步观察和元数据进行审查。注释者验证三个标准：正确性，确保初始状态和轨迹在物理上有效；可回答性，确保任务可以通过交互解决并且在空间上明确；和非平凡性，确保任务不能仅通过视觉偏见或先验知识来解决，并且需要真正的空间不确定性。每个实例都由三名注释者独立审查，分歧由多数票解决。不符合任何标准的实例将被丢弃。由于 GPT-4o 用作对象、位置、问题和轨迹的提案引擎，因此我们进一步审核生成的任务是否存在可能的语言和对象类别偏差。附录All generated task instances are reviewed by human annotators using rendered per-step observations and metadata. Annotators verify three criteria: correctness, ensuring the initial state and trajectory are physically valid; answerability, ensuring the task is solvable through interaction and spatially unambiguous; and non-triviality, ensuring the task cannot be solved from visual bias or prior knowledge alone and requires genuine spatial uncertainty. Each instance is independently reviewed by three annotators, with disagreements resolved by majority vote. Instances failing any criterion are discarded. Because GPT-4o is used as a proposal engine for objects, placements, questions, and trajectories, we further audit the generated tasks for possible linguistic and object-category biases. AppendixH报告详细的人工验证协议、验证分数、快捷基线、多样性统计数据以及 GPT 和匹配的人工生成任务之间的比较。这些结果表明，GPT-4o 生成的任务是高质量的，表现出有限的捷径偏差，并且与人类生成的任务具有相似的难度。reports detailed human verification protocol, verification scores, shortcut baselines, diversity statistics, and comparison between GPT and matched human-generated tasks. These results show that GPT-4o-generated tasks are high-quality, exhibit limited shortcut bias, and have similar difficulty to human-generated tasks.
