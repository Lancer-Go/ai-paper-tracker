监管视角下的安全、隐私和代理人工智能：Security, privacy, and agentic AI in a regulatory view:
从定义和区别到规定和反思From definitions and distinctions to provisions and reflections

Shiliang Zhang∗  Sabita Maharjan Shiliang Zhang and Sabita Maharjan are with Department of Informatics, University of Oslo, Norway (e-mail: {shilianz, sabita}@uio.no).
摘要Abstract

人工智能 (AI) 技术的快速扩散带来了动态的监管环境，立法框架努力跟上技术进步的步伐。随着人工智能范式转向更大的自主性，特别是以代理人工智能的形式，精确阐明监管规定变得越来越具有挑战性。这一挑战在安全和隐私领域更加严峻，其中自主代理的能力往往模糊了传统的法律和技术界限。本文通过分析 2024 年至 2025 年间发布的 24 份相关文件，回顾了不断发展的欧盟 (EU) 人工智能监管规定。通过这次回顾，我们对关键定义进行了澄清。我们解构对安全、隐私和代理人工智能的监管解释，将它们与密切相关的概念区分开来，以解决歧义。我们综合审查的文件，以阐明针对不同类型人工智能的监管规定的现状，特别是与安全和隐私方面相关的监管规定。我们分析并反思监管层面的现有规定，以更好地将安全和隐私义务与人工智能和代理行为结合起来。这些见解有助于政策制定者、开发人员和研究人员了解随着算法机构不断增加的社会中的合规性和人工智能治理。The rapid proliferation of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies has led to a dynamic regulatory landscape, where legislative frameworks strive to keep pace with technical advancements. As AI paradigms shift towards greater autonomy, specifically in the form of agentic AI, it becomes increasingly challenging to precisely articulate regulatory stipulations. This challenge is even more acute in the domains of security and privacy, where the capabilities of autonomous agents often blur traditional legal and technical boundaries. This paper reviews the evolving European Union (EU) AI regulatory provisions via analyzing 24 relevant documents published between 2024 and 2025. From this review, we provide a clarification of critical definitions. We deconstruct the regulatory interpretations of security, privacy, and agentic AI, distinguishing them from closely related concepts to resolve ambiguity. We synthesize the reviewed documents to articulate the current state of regulatory provisions targeting different types of AI, particularly those related to security and privacy aspects. We analyze and reflect on the existing provisions in the regulatory dimension to better align security and privacy obligations with AI and agentic behaviors. These insights serve to inform policymakers, developers, and researchers on the compliance and AI governance in the society with increasing algorithmic agencies.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 在科学、工业和社会领域经历了快速发展，从预先训练的预测模型转变为可以在开放环境中规划、行动和适应的强大系统Artificial intelligence (AI) has undergone rapid evolution across scientific, industrial, and societal domains, shifting from pretrained predictive models to powerful systems that can plan, act, and adapt in open-ended environments[radanliev2025artificial]。人工智能机制、模型、系统和应用现已渗透到能源领域. AI mechanisms, models, systems, and applications now permeate energy[melguizo2026can], 医疗保健, healthcare[kim2025understanding], 金融, finance[vukovic2025ai], 制造, manufacturing[fosso2026building], 交通, transportation[yan2025generative]、自动化, automation[spencer2025ai]和车辆and vehicles[sharma2026generative]、政府服务, government services[chen2025uncovering]和知识工作, and knowledge work[tan2025artificial]。最近，自主代理人工智能的兴起. More recently, the rise of autonomous agentic AI[hasselwander2026toward]扩大了应用范围，通过 API、软件工具和数据源的自主编排实现复杂的工作流程。与被动响应人类提示的前辈不同，智能体人工智能具有感知、推理和主动执行复杂、多步骤目标的能力，并且只需最少的人为干预has expanded the scope of applications, enabling complex workflows through autonomous orchestration of APIs, software tools, and data sources. Unlike their predecessors, which passively respond to human prompts, agentic AI possesses the capability to perceive, reason, and proactively execute complex, multi-step goals with minimal human intervention[biswas2025building]。从人工智能作为一种工具到人工智能作为一种主动力量的转变正在迅速重塑人类生活的各个领域。. This transition from AI as a tool to AI as an active agent is rapidly reshaping various domains in human life.

然而，人工智能自主权的不断增强带来了挑战，特别是在安全和隐私方面However, this increasing AI autonomy introduces challenges, particularly regarding security and privacy[leo2026threat, hosseini2025role, zhang2025data]。随着人工智能代理越来越多地被允许直接访问外部工具、数据库和 API 来实现其目标，攻击面显着扩大. As AI agents are increasingly granted direct access to external tools, databases, and APIs to fulfill their objectives, the attack surface expands significantly[deng2025ai]。代理行为会引入安全漏洞，例如跨多代理生态系统传播的提示注入. Agentic behaviors introduce security vulnerabilities, such as prompt injection propagating across multi-agent ecosystems[ferrag2025prompt]，自主协作期间未经授权的数据泄露, unauthorized data exfiltration during autonomous collaboration[huang2025securing]，以及智能体绕过安全护栏以追求不一致的子目标的可能性, and the potential for agents to bypass safety guardrails in pursuit of misaligned sub-goals[huang2025commercial]。当代理依赖第三方库、API 和模型组件时，供应链风险就会加剧. Supply chain risks intensify when agents rely on third-party libraries, APIs, and model components[jannelli2025agentic]。中毒或依赖性混乱可能会损害代理的输出和操作. Poisoning or dependency confusion can compromise agent outputs and actions[esen2025risks]。隐私影响同样重要。隐私风险从以模型为中心的泄露扩展到以管道为中心的暴露. The privacy implications are equally critical. Privacy risks extend beyond model-centric leakage to pipeline-centric exposure[ray2026comprehensive]。持续代表用户行事的代理可能需要访问个人上下文和敏感数据. Agents that persistently act on behalf of users might require access to personal context and sensitive data[zhang2025towards]，提出了有关数据最小化、同意管理和自主决策的黑箱性质的问题。长期任务和内存组件增加了保留敏感信息超出合法使用范围的可能性, raising issues about data minimization, consent management, and the black-box nature of autonomous decision-making. Long-horizon tasks and memory components increase the likelihood of retaining sensitive information beyond legitimate use[dwivedi2025agentic]。工具调用可以跨越管辖范围和合同框架，从而引发数据保护制度下的合规性问题. Tool calls can cross jurisdictional boundaries and contractual frameworks, raising compliance questions under data protection regimes[hughes2025ai].

为了应对这些干扰，监管环境正在努力适应。欧盟 (EU) 作为数字治理的全球先行者，推出了《欧盟人工智能法案》等框架In response to these disruptions, the regulatory landscape is striving to adapt. The European Union (EU), a global forerunner in digital governance, has introduced frameworks like the EU AI Act[nolte2025robustness]对人工智能风险进行分类和减轻。欧盟的补充文书和框架强化了适用于人工智能系统的隐私和安全基线。此类示例包括数据保护的 GDPRto categorize and mitigate AI risks. Complementary EU instruments and frameworks reinforce privacy and security baselines that are applicable to AI systems. Such examples are the GDPR for data protection[beltran2025ai], NIS2 网络和信息系统安全指令, the NIS2 Directive for network and information system security[kianpour2025digital]，针对具有数字元素的安全设计产品的网络弹性法案, the Cyber Resilience Act for secure-by-design products with digital elements[mueck2025introduction]，以及数据访问和共享的数据法案, and the Data Act for data access and sharing[hohmann2025reflections]。 2024年至2025年间，欧盟发布了一系列监管文件，旨在实施《人工智能法案》并使其与现有隐私和安全法相协调. Between 2024 and 2025, EU has issued a range of regulatory documents aiming at operationalizing the AI Act and harmonizing it with existing privacy and security law[calvano2026building]。这些材料探讨了人工智能系统的定义，阐明了风险类别，提出了合格评定程序，并概述了网络安全和数据治理要求。. These materials explore definitions of AI systems, clarify risk categories, propose conformity assessment procedures, and outline cybersecurity and data governance requirements.

然而，安全、隐私、个人数据、生成人工智能、通用人工智能、大语言模型、代理人工智能等关键术语的概念模糊性仍然存在，尤其是当应用于可以以意想不到的方式行事的人工智能系统时。这种模糊性使得从业者很难解释义务However, conceptual ambiguity persists around key terms - security, privacy, personal data, generative AI, general-purpose AI, large language models, agentic AI - especially when applied to AI systems that can act in unanticipated ways. This ambiguity makes it difficult for practitioners to interpret obligations[nizza2026ais]。缺乏区别可能会导致合规实践不一致和监管差距。此外，虽然存在隐私和安全的高级原则，但解决代理人工智能独特风险的具体监管规定仍然支离破碎且不太明确。有必要澄清现有的隐私和安全法规如何适用于可以独立于实时人类监督而运行的系统。有必要阐明与代理人工智能背景下的隐私和安全相关的监管规定，这有望弥合抽象指令和可实施控制之间的差距。. A lack of distinctions can lead to inconsistent compliance practices and regulatory gaps. Furthermore, while high-level principles for privacy and security exist, specific regulatory provisions that address the unique risks of agentic AI remain fragmented and less articulated. There is a need to clarify how existing privacy and security mandates apply to systems that can act independently of real-time human oversight. Articulating the regulatory provisions that pertain to privacy and security in the context of agentic AI is necessary, which is anticipate to bridge the gap between abstract mandates and implementable controls.

为了解决这一差距，本文对欧盟不断发展的代理人工智能格局进行了回顾和监管分析。我们专注于安全、隐私和代理人工智能的交叉点。我们的贡献总结如下：To address this gap, this paper provides a review and regulatory analysis of the evolving EU landscape concerning agentic AI. We focus on the intersection of security, privacy, and agentic AI. Our contributions are summarized as follows:

3.2 隐私、安全和密切相关的概念Privacy, security, and closely relevant concepts

隐私和安全并不是技术和数字化发展中的新话题。我们在本文中检查了人工智能领域，特别是代理人工智能领域如何处理这些方面。在深入讨论细节之前，我们想先奠定与隐私和安全相关的基本概念的基础，并提供概念之间的区别，以避免监管不确定性。特别是，我们研究了经过审查的欧盟人工智能监管文件如何定义/引用这些概念，旨在获得相关背景，如表所示Privacy and security are not new topics in the evolution of technology and digitization. We in this paper check how such aspects is handled in the area of AI, particularly agentic AI. Before we dive into the details, we would like to lay the foundation of basic concepts related to privacy and security, and provide distinctions between the concepts to avoid regulatory uncertainties. Particularly, we examine how the reviewed EU AI regulatory documents define/refer to those concepts, aiming to gain a relevant context, as shown in Table4.

我们还注意到，欧盟法规在谈论安全时大量提及信息系统。我们要指出的是，信息系统和人工智能系统之间的关系是根本性的。人工智能代表了更广泛的信息系统中的一个专门的逻辑和推理层，如果没有信息系统提供服务，人工智能就不可能存在。人工智能系统可以看作是人工智能模型集成到具有计算能力、数据存储和网络资源的信息系统中的应用。此外，人工智能，尤其是代理人工智能的存在，增加了信息系统的安全攻击面。这是因为代理AI不再只是信息系统的“居民”或“租户”，而是可以自主与信息系统交互的“用户”或“操作者”，We also notice that EU regulations refer a lot to information system when talking about security. We would like to note that the relationship between information system and AI system is fundamental. AI represents a specialized layer of logic and inference that resides within the broader of an information system, and AI cannot exist without an information system to provide services. An AI system can be viewed as an application of an AI model integrated into an information system with computation power, data storage, and network resources. Further, the presence of AI, especially agentic AI, increases the security attack surface of the information systems. This is because agentic AI is no longer just a “resident” or “tenant” of the information system, but a “user” or “operator” that can interact with the information system autonomously,例如e.g.，在执行代码或修改数据库时。因此，对代理人工智能的攻击就成为对信息系统的直接安全漏洞，表现为网络与信息安全或网络安全或其他新型问题。, in executing code or modifying database. Therefore, an attack on the agentic AI becomes a direct security breach of the information system, in the formats network and information security or cybersecurity, or other new types of issues.

信息系统传统上是通过代码审查和网络日志进行审计的。然而，由于 LLM 和 GPAI 是“概率性”或黑匣子，它们将非确定性风险引入到其他确定性信息系统中。监管现在要求“特定于人工智能”的网络安全措施，例如输入/输出过滤和对抗性测试。Information systems are traditionally audited through code reviews and network logs. However, because LLMs and GPAI are “probabilistic”, or black boxes, they introduce non-deterministic risks into otherwise deterministic information systems. Regulation now requires “AI-specific” cybersecurity measures, such as input/output filtering and adversarial testing.

人工智能系统被视为在更广泛的信息系统背景下运行的高级功能层。信息系统提供的是运算能力，而AI系统提供的是推理能力。随着人工智能从被动生成（LLM）转向主动编排（代理人工智能），信息系统“工具”和“用户”之间的法律区别继续模糊，需要统一的数字治理方法。AI system is viewed as a high-level functional layer that operates within the broader context of an information system. While an information system provides the capabilities for operation, the AI System provides the capabilities for inference. As AI moves from passive generation (LLMs) to active orchestration (Agentic AI), the legal distinction between “the tool” and “the user” of the information system continues to blur, requiring a unified approach to digital governance.

5 结论Conclusions

这项工作回顾了 2024 年至 2025 年间发布的欧盟人工智能监管文件。我们检查了与隐私、安全和代理人工智能相关的监管定义，并将它们与密切相关的概念区分开来。我们研究了隐私、安全和代理人工智能的监管规定，并分析和反映了人工智能特别是新兴的代理人工智能概念的规定状况。我们的研究表明，尽管存在适用的规定，但人工智能规定的背景化，特别是针对不同类型人工智能的规定仍处于早期阶段。我们还展示了人工智能条款与一般信息系统和非歧视领域条款之间的联系，以便将人工智能条款与更广泛的监管合规背景联系起来。我们预计，未来需要通过以更精细的方式区分、解释和阐明人工智能的隐私和安全条款，来减轻监管的不确定性。This work reviews EU AI regulatory documents that are published between 2024 and 2025. We check regulatory definitions related to privacy, security, and agentic AI, and distinct them from closely relevant concepts. We examine the regulatory provisions for privacy, security, and agentic AI, and analyze and reflect the status of the provisions for AI especially for the emerging concept of agentic AI. Our study reveals that, though applicable provisions exist, the contextualization of the provision for AI particularly for different types of AI remains in its early stage. We also show the connections between the provisions for AI and those for general information systems and indiscriminative areas, so as to bridge AI provisions with a broader context in regulation compliance. We envision that future efforts are needed in mitigating regulatory uncertainties, by differentiating, interpreting, and articulating privacy and security provisions for AI in a more granular manner.
