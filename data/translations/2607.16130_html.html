人工智能生命周期治理中可审计可信度级别的方法A Methodology for Auditable Trustworthiness Levels in AI Lifecycle Governance

Andrea Ferrario aferrario@ethz.ch Institute of Biomedical Ethics and History of Medicine, University of Zürich, Zürich, Switzerland SUPSI, Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (IDSIA), Lugano, Switzerland ETH Zürich, Zürich, Switzerland
摘要Abstract

人工智能治理越来越需要判断人工智能系统是否随着时间的推移保持足够的可信度，观察到的变化是否可以容忍，以及如何以透明和有争议的方式记录这些判断。然而，现有的人工智能可信度工作要么水平太高，无法支持生命周期监控和重新评估，要么指标驱动范围太窄，无法满足治理需求。因此，我们提出了一种用于人工智能治理中可审计可信度级别的轻量级方法。该方法有两个组成部分：用于表示和学习可信度级别的正式框架，以及用于随着时间的推移记录、监控和重新评估可信度级别的轻量级人工智能生命周期治理程序。正式框架通过可测量维度的上下文相关协议对治理相关的可信度进行建模，并将可信度级别学习为可信度配置文件的可解释规则。该方法使用决策树作为可解释的概念验证模型类，产生明确的可信度高原、可读的级别转换以及两个简单的生命周期诊断：边界裕度和配置文件漂移。治理过程将这些正式对象嵌入到面向一致性的工作流程中，用于设计时标记、部署后监控、重新评估和报告。它还为协议设计、验证、监控和控制分配人员职责和控制门 重新评估。我们说明了合成人工智能生命周期跟踪的方法，涉及退化、冲击、更新、异构监控节奏和系统比较。我们的方法并不能取代法律或其他专家判断：它通过提供证据基础来记录和跟踪人工智能治理相关的变化，从而支持合规性记录和生命周期监控。AI governance increasingly requires judgments about whether an AI system remains adequately trustworthy over time, whether observed changes are tolerable, and how such judgments should be documented in a transparent and contestable way. Yet existing work on AI trustworthiness remains either too high-level to support lifecycle monitoring and reassessment or too narrowly metric-driven to connect with governance needs. We therefore propose a lightweight methodology for auditable trustworthiness levels in AI governance. The methodology has two components: a formal framework for representing and learning trustworthiness levels, and a lightweight AI lifecycle governance procedure for documenting, monitoring, and reassessing them over time. The formal framework models governance-relative trustworthiness through a context-sensitive protocol of measurable dimensions and learns trustworthiness levels as interpretable rules over trustworthiness profiles. Using decision trees as an interpretable proof-of-concept model class, the methodology yields explicit trustworthiness plateaus, readable level transitions, and two simple lifecycle diagnostics: boundary margins and profile drift. The governance procedure embeds these formal objects in a conformity-oriented workflow for design-time labeling, post-deployment monitoring, reassessment, and reporting. It also assigns human responsibilities and control gates for protocol design, validation, monitoring, and reassessment. We illustrate the methodology on synthetic AI lifecycle traces involving degradation, shocks, updates, heterogeneous monitoring cadences, and system comparison. Our methodology does not replace legal or other expert judgment: it supports conformity documentation and lifecycle monitoring by providing an evidential basis for documenting and tracking AI governance-relevant changes over time.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能治理取决于的判断既不是纯技术的，也不是纯法律的。提供商、部署者和监督机构必须评估人工智能系统在重新配置、更新或使用环境发生变化后是否仍然值得信赖。在这里，我们在面向治理的意义上使用人工智能的可信度：人工智能系统是可信的，因为对于给定的预期目的和使用环境，它满足其设计和治理中记录的社会技术要求。他们还必须确定人工智能系统的副本或变体之间的可信度差异何时足以保证记录、审查或监督。这种基于可信度的问题出现在广泛的系统中，从基于法学硕士的助理和临床决策支持系统到推荐代理和银行业流程自动化的人工智能系统。它们在合格评定、监控和重新评估成为关键人工智能生命周期治理程序的环境中尤其突出，例如欧盟人工智能法案（AIA）下的高风险人工智能系统的情况AI governance depends on judgments that are neither purely technical nor purely legal. Providers, deployers, and oversight bodies must assess whether an AI system remains trustworthy after reconfiguration, updating, or shifts in its context of use. Here, we use AI trustworthiness in a governance-oriented sense: an AI system is trustworthy to the extent that, for a given intended purpose and context of use, it satisfies the socio-technical requirements documented in its design and governance. They must also determine when differences in trustworthiness across copies or variants of an AI system are material enough to warrant documentation, review, or oversight. Such trustworthiness-based questions arise across a wide range of systems, from LLM-based assistants and clinical decision-support systems to recommender agents and AI systems automating processes in banking. They are especially salient in settings where conformity assessment, monitoring, and reassessment become key AI lifecycle governance procedures, as in the case of high-risk AI systems under the EU AI Act (AIA)(EU AI Act, 2024).

一个核心困难是，目前人工智能可信度方面的工作有两个方向。一方面，治理框架通过稳健性、透明度、公平性、安全性和问责制等广泛要求来阐明可信性A central difficulty is that current work on AI trustworthiness is pulled in two directions. On the one hand, governance frameworks articulate trustworthiness through broad requirements such as robustness, transparency, fairness, safety, and accountability(Kemmerzell et al., 2025; Mehrotra et al., 2026)。另一方面，系统工程和 MLOps 中的技术工作通过本地指标或基准来操作这些要求的较小子集，通常没有明确说明如何随着时间的推移将这些测量集成到人工智能治理中. On the other hand, technical work in system engineering and MLOps operationalizes narrower subsets of these requirements through local metrics or benchmarks, often without an explicit account of how such measurements should be integrated into AI governance over time(Rabanser et al., 2026; Kreuzberger et al., 2023; Bayram et al., 2024)。因此，可信度在人工智能治理中经常被提及，但很少以支持简单且可审计的人工智能生命周期监控的方式形式化。此外，许多与治理相关的可信度判断侧重于设计或审查活动：它们可能通过专家程序、清单或参考案例进行记录，但它们本身并不能产生在不断变化的人工智能生命周期条件下进行部署后和定量可信度分配的机制。. As a result, trustworthiness is frequently invoked in AI governance but rarely formalized in a way that supports simple and auditable AI lifecycle monitoring. Furthermore, many governance-relevant trustworthiness judgments focus on design or review activities: they may be documented through expert procedures, checklists, or reference cases, yet they do not by themselves yield a mechanism for post-deployment and quantitative trustworthiness assignment under changing AI lifecycle conditions.

2 背景Background

2.1 人工智能可信度：共同点AI Trustworthiness: Common Ground

值得信赖的人工智能Trustworthy AI是一种范式，旨在确定人工智能系统的设计、开发、部署和治理标准，以反映指导其在社会中发挥作用的核心原则。例如，AI HLEG 框架基于人类机构和监督、技术稳健性和安全性、隐私和数据治理、透明度、多样性和公平性、社会和环境福祉以及问责制等原则is the paradigm that seeks to identify the criteria under which AI systems should be designed, developed, deployed, and governed so as to reflect core principles that ought to guide their role in society. For example, the AI HLEG framework is grounded in principles such as human agency and oversight, technical robustness and safety, privacy and data governance, transparency, diversity and fairness, societal and environmental well-being, and accountability(EU AI HLEG, 2019)。随着时间的推移，监管机构、标准化机构、政府和国际组织提出了部分重叠的方法来界定人工智能可信度的范围. Over time, regulators, standardization bodies, governments, and international organizations have proposed partially overlapping approaches to delineating the scope of AI trustworthiness(EU AI HLEG, 2019; EU AI Act, 2024; The White House, 2022; United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, 2022; OECD, 2022; ISO/IEC, 2020).

关于可信赖人工智能的文献现在非常广泛：我们参考最近的评论来深入探讨定义和观点The literature on Trustworthy AI is now extensive: we refer to recent reviews for a deep dive into definitions and perspectives(Kaur et al., 2022; Kemmerzell et al., 2025; Mehrotra et al., 2026)。在这里，我们修正了与我们的框架相关的主要想法。这些方法的一个共同的基本假设是，使人工智能系统值得信任的不是单一属性，而是满足. Here, we fix the main ideas relevant to our framework. A common underlying assumption across these approaches is that what makes an AI system worthy of trust is not a single property, but the satisfaction of多重社会技术要求multiple socio-technical requirements (Mehrotra et al., 2026; Kemmerzell et al., 2025; Kaur et al., 2022)。这些要求的确切表述因框架而异，取决于如何将抽象原则转化为操作标准，并且在跨领域中保持上下文敏感。例如，基于法学硕士的自学助手的可信度概况将不同于临床决策支持系统、推荐代理或多代理人工智能系统协调银行合规程序。也就是说，常见讨论的维度包括公平、透明度、隐私和数据治理、技术稳健性和安全性、问责制、人类机构和监督以及社会或环境福祉. The exact formulation of these requirements varies across frameworks, depends on how abstract principles are translated into operational criteria, and remains context-sensitive across domains. For instance, the trustworthiness profile of an LLM-based self-study assistant will differ from that of a clinical decision-support system, a recommender agent, or a multi-agent AI system coordinating compliance procedures in banking. That said, commonly discussed dimensions include fairness, transparency, privacy and data governance, technical robustness and safety, accountability, human agency and oversight, and societal or environmental well-being(Kemmerzell et al., 2025; Kaur et al., 2022)。在下文中，我们将此类要求称为. In what follows, we refer to such requirements as可信度维度trustworthiness dimensions。人工智能的可信度取决于各个维度的选择，也取决于它们如何随着时间的推移相互作用、权衡和相互强化。. AI trustworthiness depends on the choice of individual dimensions and also on how they interact, trade off, and reinforce one another over time.

核心含义是，可信度必须嵌入人工智能生命周期治理框架中，该框架能够将高级原则转化为可审计的实践A central implication is that trustworthiness must be embedded in an AI lifecycle governance framework capable of translating high-level principles into auditable practices(Mökander et al., 2022; Laux et al., 2024; Floridi et al., 2022)。生命周期视角很重要，因为人工智能系统可能会发生变化：合格评定建立初始基线，部署后监控跟踪持续合规性，“实质性修改”可以作为与治理相关的不连续条件。这种倾向在《欧盟人工智能法案》中尤为明显——请参阅其中的附件 IV 和第 72 条. The lifecycle perspective is important because AI systems are subject to change: conformity assessment establishes an initial baseline, post-deployment monitoring tracks continued compliance, and ‘substantial modification’ can function as a governance-relevant discontinuity condition. This orientation is particularly visible in the EU AI Act—see Annex IV and Article 72 therein(EU AI Act, 2024)。因此，挑战在于提供足够明确的可信度表示，可以跨时间连接这些义务。下一节将评估用于评估人工智能可信度的现有方法和指标在多大程度上解决了这一挑战。. Thus, the challenge is to provide a sufficiently explicit representation of trustworthiness that can connect these obligations across time. The next section assesses the extent to which this challenge is addressed by existing methods and metrics for evaluating AI trustworthiness.

Hospital Alpha represents a stable controlled environment governed by a coarse local expert rule. Its measured trustworthiness profile undergoes only very slow degradation and all observations remain in trustworthiness level 4. Hospital Beta is governed by a more refined expert-defined rule and exposed to lifecycle events—adverse shocks and corrective updates—that generate substantially more lifecycle variation. Its reference trajectory occupies all four levels, making empirical learning governance-informative. However, the learned rule does not fully recover the refined expert scale: it performs reasonably on the training segment but deteriorates on the held-out post-deployment segment, and it compresses the four-level reference scale into a coarser operational rule. Thus, the governance framework does not simply produce a learned rule, but it also reveals when this rule is too coarse, too fragile, or insufficiently sensitive to rare low-trustworthiness regimes. The diagnostics support this interpretation. Hospital Alpha shows only small profile drift, consistent with slow degradation inside a locally accepted highest-regime envelope. Hospital Beta exhibits stronger variability in trustworthiness space, including profiles lying directly at learned regime boundaries. Thus, two hospitals deploying copies of the same AI system may reasonably differ in both local governance granularity and the value of empirical learning. In the Alpha environment, learning adds little beyond the expert rule. In the Beta environment, learning is useful because its limitations reveal compression, boundary fragility, and post-deployment instability that warrant further review.

7 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusions

我们在人工智能治理中引入了一种用于可审计可信度级别的轻量级方法，其中包括（i）用于学习可信度级别的正式框架，以及（ii）用于在整个人工智能生命周期中记录、监控和重新评估可信度的人工智能治理程序。We introduced a lightweight methodology for auditable trustworthiness levels in AI governance that comprises (i) a formal framework for learning trustworthiness levels, and (ii) an AI governance procedure for documenting, monitoring, and reassessing trustworthiness throughout the AI lifecycle.

该方法有两个主要贡献。首先，它使可信度判断更加明确和更具争议性：维度、度量、聚合选择、标签程序、学习规则和监控输出都可以随着时间的推移进行记录和修改。其次，它通过将相对静态的设计时治理标准转换为可重复应用于新的可信度配置文件的动态监控层，将部署前治理与部署后监控连接起来。This methodology offers two main contributions. First, it makes trustworthiness judgments more explicit and contestable: dimensions, measures, aggregation choices, labeling procedures, learned rules, and monitoring outputs can all be documented and revised over time. Second, it connects pre-deployment governance with post-deployment monitoring by converting a comparatively static design-time governance standard into a dynamic monitoring layer that can be repeatedly applied to new trustworthiness profiles.因此，由此产生的规则应被理解为人工智能生命周期治理的工具，而不是独立的合规机制The resulting rule should therefore be understood as a tool for AI lifecycle governance rather than as a stand-alone compliance mechanism。它提供了一个有争议的基于证据的层，可以通过随着时间的推移为不同类型的人工智能系统明确级别分配、转换、边际、漂移和协议更新，从而为合格评定、上市后监控和重新评估提供信息. It provides a contestable evidence-based layer that can inform conformity assessment, post-market monitoring, and reassessment by making level assignments, transitions, margins, drift, and protocol updates explicit over time for different types of AI systems(EU AI Act, 2024).

一些可能的批评值得澄清。首先，A few possible criticisms deserve clarification. First,正式框架在算法上是轻量级的。the formal framework is algorithmically lightweight.这是我们方法的一个特点。在治理环境中，简单性支持可审计的基于阈值的推理，方便记录，并使政权过渡更容易审查和质疑。因此，我们的目标不是引入一种新的最先进的机器学习算法。同时，我们的框架并不依赖于一个固定的模型类。当专家定义的可信区域不再基于阈值时，更具表现力的模型就变得合适；可信度协议变得高维，并且每个维度包括多个度量；更丰富的数据收集可以实现更复杂的鲁棒性或评分分析；或者治理行为者希望在更广泛的冲击和更新策略下施加对可信度敏感的惩罚或更强的稳定性约束。根据专家规则的结构、可用数据以及模型选择期间施加的简单性约束，学习的规则可以保留、压缩或细化原始尺度。This is a feature of our approach. In governance settings, simplicity supports auditable threshold-based reasoning, facilitates documentation, and makes regime transitions easier to review and contest. Thus, our goal was not to introduce a new state-of-the-art machine learning algorithm. At the same time, our framework is not tied to one fixed model class. More expressive models become appropriate when expert-defined trustworthiness regions are no longer threshold-based; the trustworthiness protocol becomes high-dimensional and includes multiple measures per dimension; richer data collection enables more complex robustness or scoring analyses; or governance actors wish to impose trustworthiness-sensitive penalties or stronger stability constraints under a broader range of shocks and update strategies. Depending on the structure of the expert rule, the available data, and the simplicity constraints imposed during model selection, the learned rule may preserve, compress, or refine the original scale.

第二，Second,除了专家定义的规则之外，学习可能几乎没有任何帮助。learning may add little or nothing beyond the expert-defined rule.当部署在可信度机制中产生的信息变化太少时，当现有治理干预措施过滤了困难案例时，当受监控的配置文件信息量很弱时，或者当标签稀疏、嘈杂或随着时间的推移不稳定时，就会发生这种情况。在这种情况下，最负责任的结果可能是保留专家规则作为操作治理标准。This can happen when deployment generates too little informative variation across trustworthiness regimes, when difficult cases are filtered by existing governance interventions, when monitored profiles are weakly informative, or when labels are sparse, noisy, or unstable over time. In such settings, the most responsible outcome may be to retain the expert rule as the operative governance standard.

三、Third,该框架并没有消除在分配可信度标签时进行专家判断的需要。the framework does not eliminate the need for expert judgment in assigning trustworthiness labels.这并不是我们的提议所特有的，而是在必须根据特定上下文的规范和操作约束来确定可接受、边界和不可接受的系统状态的领域中以治理为导向的评估的标准特征。我们的框架假设参考标签是根据特定于相关机构、领域和治理环境的书面裁决程序生成的：它并不试图在所有情况下标准化此类程序。因此，重要的是在任何给定机构中用于生成标签的程序都要记录下来并进行版本控制。This is not peculiar to our proposal, but a standard feature of governance-oriented assessment in domains where acceptable, borderline, and unacceptable system states must be determined under context-specific normative and operational constraints. Our framework assumes that reference labels are produced under a documented adjudication procedure that is specific to the relevant institution, domain, and governance setting: it does not attempt to standardize such procedures across all contexts. For this reason, what matters is that the procedure used to generate labels at any given institution be documented and versioned.

最后，Finally,该框架的信息量取决于机构实际构建和维护的可信度对象。the framework is only as informative as the trustworthiness object that an institution can actually construct and maintain.在现实世界中，主要的困难可能不在于学习可信度级别规则，而在于定义有意义的协议、聚合异构度量、随着时间的推移获得足够的信息配置文件以及生成稳定的参考标签。当维度测量较弱、标签稀疏或不一致、类高度不平衡或协议版本随时间漂移时，学习到的规则可能会变得粗糙、不稳定或实际上缺乏信息。因此，该方法最好被理解为一个制度上依赖的治理层：只有当底层的测量、聚合、标签和审查程序本身被记录下来并且足够成熟时，它才变得可行。未来的工作应该在真实的特定领域案例上测试该框架，将决策树与其他可解释的模型类进行比较，并根据机构特定的协议完善验证和重新标记程序。进一步的工作还应该研究标签噪声和协议漂移下的稳健性，并将该方法扩展到多代理设置和机构工作流程。In real-world settings, the main difficulty may lie less in learning the trustworthiness-level rule than in defining a meaningful protocol, aggregating heterogeneous measures, obtaining sufficiently informative profiles over time, and producing stable reference labels. Where dimensions are weakly measured, labels are sparse or inconsistent, classes are highly imbalanced, or protocol versions drift over time, the learned rule may become coarse, unstable, or effectively uninformative. The methodology is therefore best understood as an institutionally dependent governance layer: it becomes actionable only when the underlying measurement, aggregation, labeling, and review procedures are themselves documented and sufficiently mature. Future work should test the framework on real domain-specific cases, compare decision trees with other interpretable model classes, and refine validation and relabeling procedures under institution-specific protocols. Further work should also study robustness under label noise and protocol drift, and extend the approach to multi-agent settings and institutional workflows.
