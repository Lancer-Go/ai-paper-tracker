大型视觉语言模型 (VLM) 通过将视觉编码器的视觉表示集成到大型语言模型（通常通过投影层）来实现跨模式推理Large vision-language models (VLMs) enable cross-modal reasoning by integrating visual representations from vision encoders into large language models, typically through projection layers(liu2023visual, bai2025qwen3)或交叉注意力模块or cross-attention modules(alayrac2022flamingo)。 代表性的开源 VLM 系列沿着互补的方向发展： InternVL 从动态高分辨率和强视觉编码器扩展到本机多模态预训练、基于 RL 的推理和高效部署. Despite their strong performance across diverse vision-language tasks, the internal representations learned by these models remain highly entangled, making them difficult to interpret mechanistically. Recent studies have interpreted VLM representations at multiple levels of granularity, including neurons(fang2024towards, liu2025modality, xu2025deciphering), 模块, modules(bi2025unveiling, xia2025one, jiang2025investigating)、和层数, and layers(jiang2025devils, shi2025vision, song2026does)。 除了代表性分析之外，机械解释方法还被用来通过因果追踪来定位功能组件. However, these analyses are still typically grounded in architectural units or potentially polysemantic components, rather than fine-grained and interpretable conceptual features. This limitation motivates feature-level decompositions of multimodal representations into interpretable conceptual features(lou2025saev, zhang2025large).
