Voxtral TTS

摘要Abstract

我们推出了 Voxtral TTS，这是一种富有表现力的多语言文本转语音模型，可从短至 3 秒的参考音频生成自然语音。 Voxtral TTS 采用混合架构，将语义语音标记的自回归生成与声学标记的流匹配相结合。这些标记使用 Voxtral Codec 进行编码和解码，Voxtral Codec 是一种使用混合 VQ-FSQ 量化方案从头开始训练的语音标记生成器。在母语人士进行的人类评估中，Voxtral TTS因其自然性和表现力而成为多语言语音克隆的首选，比ElevenLabs Flash v2.5获得了68.4%的胜率。我们根据 CC BY-NC 许可发布模型权重。We introduce Voxtral TTS, an expressive multilingual text-to-speech model that generates natural speech from as little as 3 seconds of reference audio. Voxtral TTS adopts a hybrid architecture that combines auto-regressive generation of semantic speech tokens with flow-matching for acoustic tokens. These tokens are encoded and decoded with Voxtral Codec, a speech tokenizer trained from scratch with a hybrid VQ-FSQ quantization scheme. In human evaluations conducted by native speakers, Voxtral TTS is preferred for multilingual voice cloning due to its naturalness and expressivity, achieving a 68.4% win rate over ElevenLabs Flash v2.5. We release the model weights under a CC BY-NC license.

1 简介Introduction

自然且富有表现力的文本转语音 (TTS) 仍然是灵活人机交互的基石，其应用涵盖虚拟助手、有声读物和辅助工具。虽然最近的神经 TTS 模型实现了很强的清晰度，但捕捉人类语音的细微差别和表现力仍然是一个开放的挑战，特别是在零样本语音设置中。Natural and expressive text-to-speech (TTS) remains a cornerstone of flexible human-computer interactions, with applications spanning virtual assistants, audiobooks, and accessibility tools. While recent neural TTS models achieve strong intelligibility, capturing the nuances and expressivity of human speech remains an open challenge, particularly in the zero-shot voice setting.

最近的零样本 TTS 系统通常根据从短语音提示中提取的离散语音标记来生成生成条件，从而能够泛化到看不见的说话者并跨长序列进行自然合成Recent zero-shot TTS systems typically condition generation on discrete speech tokens extracted from a short voice prompt, enabling generalization to unseen speakers and natural synthesis across long sequences[Borsos et al., 2023, Wang et al., 2023]。同时，基于扩散和流的模型对于模拟语音生成中丰富的声学变化非常有效. In parallel, diffusion and flow-based models are effective for modeling rich acoustic variation in speech generation[Popov et al., 2021, Le et al., 2023]。最近的语音编解码器表明，语音可以分解为低速率语义流和更高速率的声学流. Recent speech codecs demonstrate that speech can be factorized into a low-rate semantic stream and a higher-rate acoustic stream[Défossez et al., 2024]。像 Moshi 这样的分层生成器已经利用了这种结构，使用时间步上的时间变换器和编解码器级别上的深度变换器。然而，这些系统中的声学生成仍然是深度方向的自回归。对于 TTS，这提出了一个问题：是否必须对密集声学分量进行自回归建模，或者是否可以使用条件连续模型更有效地生成它。. Hierarchical generators such as Moshi already exploit this structure using a temporal transformer over timesteps and a depth transformer over codec levels. However, acoustic generation in these systems remains depth-wise autoregressive. For TTS, this raises the question whether the dense acoustic component must be modeled auto-regressively at all, or whether it can instead be generated more effectively with a conditional continuous model.

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 Voxtral TTS，这是一种围绕表示感知混合架构构建的多语言零样本 TTS 系统。语音提示通过 Voxtral Codec 进行标记，Voxtral Codec 是一种低比特率语音标记器，具有 ASR 提取的语义标记和有限标量量化 (FSQ) 声学标记In this work, we introduce Voxtral TTS, a multilingual zero-shot TTS system built around a representation-aware hybrid architecture. A voice prompt is tokenized through Voxtral Codec, a low-bitrate speech tokenizer with an ASR-distilled semantic token and finite scalar quantized (FSQ) acoustic tokens[Mentzer et al., 2023]。给定这种因式分解表示，仅解码器的变压器自动回归预测语义标记序列，而轻量级流匹配模型则预测以解码器状态为条件的声学标记。该设计结合了自回归建模的优势（可实现远程一致性）和连续流量匹配（可实现丰富的声学细节）。我们采用直接偏好优化 (DPO). Given this factorized representation, a decoder-only transformer auto-regressively predicts the semantic token sequence, while a lightweight flow-matching model predicts the acoustic tokens conditioned on the decoder states. This design combines the strengths of auto-regressive modeling for long-range consistency with continuous flow-matching for rich acoustic detail. We adapt Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)[Rafailov et al., 2023]通过将语义标记生成上的标准偏好目标与用于声学预测的基于流的偏好目标相结合，实现这种混合离散连续设置to this hybrid discrete-continuous setting by combining a standard preference objective over semantic token generation with a flow-based preference objective for acoustic prediction[Ziv et al., 2025].

Voxtral TTS支持9种语言，支持短至3秒的语音提示，专为低延迟流式推理而设计。跨 SEED-TTS 的自动评估Voxtral TTS supports 9 languages, supports voice prompts as short as 3 seconds, and is designed for low-latency streaming inference. Across automatic evaluations on SEED-TTS[Anastassiou et al., 2024]和 MiniMax-TTSand MiniMax-TTS[Zhang et al., 2025]，它实现了很强的清晰度和自然度，在说话者相似度分数上击败了 ElevenLabs v3。在多语言零样本语音克隆的人类评估中，它优于 ElevenLabs Flash v2.5，胜率达 68.4%，同时在富有表现力的旗舰语音评估方面与强大的专有系统保持竞争力。, it achieves strong intelligibility and naturalness, beating ElevenLabs v3 on speaker similarity scores. In human evaluation for multilingual zero-shot voice cloning, it is preferred over ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 with a 68.4% win rate, while remaining competitive with strong proprietary systems on expressive flagship-voice evaluations.

2.2 解码器骨干网Decoder Backbone

Voxtral TTS 的解码器主干遵循 Ministral 3B 的架构The decoder backbone of Voxtral TTS follows the architecture of Ministral 3B[Liu et al., 2026]，一个仅自回归解码器的变压器。输入序列由语音参考音频标记和后跟文本标记组成，从中自动回归生成输出音频标记。每个音频帧由 37 个离散标记表示（1 个语义标记，36 个声学标记）。每个码本都有自己的嵌入查找表（语义有 8192 个条目，每个声学有 21 个条目），这些表被求和以产生每个音频帧的单个嵌入。, an auto-regressive decoder-only transformer. The input sequence consists of voice reference audio tokens followed by text tokens, from which the output audio tokens are auto-regressively generated. Each audio frame is represented by 37 discrete tokens (1 semantic, 36 acoustic). 6 vLLM-Omni 中的推理和服务Inference and Serving in vLLM-Omni

Voxtral TTS 通过 vLLM-Omni 提供Voxtral TTS is served through vLLM-Omni[Yin et al., 2026]，vLLM 的扩展, an extension of the vLLM[Kwon et al., 2023]用于多阶段多模式模型。 Voxtral TTS 被分解为两级管道：预测音频标记（语义和声学）的生成阶段，然后是将标记转换为波形的编解码器解码阶段。这两个阶段通过共享内存上的异步分块流协议进行通信，从而在生成完整波形之前实现第一音频延迟。for multi-stage multimodal models. Voxtral TTS is decomposed into a two-stage pipeline: a generation stage that predicts the audio tokens (semantic and acoustic), followed by a codec decoding stage that converts the tokens into a waveform. The two stages communicate through an asynchronous chunked streaming protocol over shared memory, enabling first-audio latency well before the full waveform has been generated.

6.2 异步分块流Asynchronous Chunked Streaming

两个管道阶段在单独的调度循环中运行。为了将自回归生成阶段解码与编解码器解码阶段波形合成重叠，引入了异步分块流协议。The two pipeline stages run in separate scheduling loops. To overlap the autoregressive generation stage decoding with codec decoding stage waveform synthesis, an asynchronous chunked streaming protocol is introduced.

在每个生成步骤之后，vLLM-Omni 传输管理器将音频令牌存储到每个请求缓冲区中。一旦缓冲区长度达到预定义的长度，一大块令牌就会被发送到编解码器解码阶段。为了确保块之间的一致性，除了新帧之外，每个发出的块还包括先前帧的切片。这种重叠使编解码器解码器的因果滑动窗口注意力能够保持跨块边界的时间一致性。After each generation step, the vLLM-Omni transfer manager stores the audio tokens to a per-request buffer. Once the buffer length reaches a pre-defined length, a chunk of tokens are emitted to the codec decoding stage. To ensure coherence between chunks, each emitted chunk includes a slice of previous frames in addition to the new frames. This overlap enables the codec decoder’s causal sliding-window attention to maintain temporal coherence across chunk boundaries.

7 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 Voxtral TTS，这是一种多语言 TTS 模型，它利用混合架构来自动回归生成语义标记和声学标记的流程匹配。这些标记与 Voxtral Codec 中的标记相对应，Voxtral Codec 是一种语音标记生成器，它将 ASR 提取的语义标记与 FSQ 声学标记相结合。We introduced Voxtral TTS, a multilingual TTS model that leverages a hybrid architecture for auto-regressive generation of semantic tokens and flow-matching for acoustic tokens. The tokens correspond to those from Voxtral Codec, a speech tokenizer that combines ASR-distilled semantic tokens with FSQ acoustic tokens.

Voxtral TTS 能够从短至 3 秒的参考音频生成富有表现力的语音克隆语音，并且在人类评估中优于 API 基线。我们根据 CC BY-NC 许可将 Voxtral TTS 作为开放权重发布，以支持富有表现力的 TTS 系统的进一步研究和开发。Voxtral TTS is able to generate expressive, voice-cloned speech from as little as 3 seconds of reference audio, and is preferred to API baselines in human evaluations. We release Voxtral TTS as open weights under the CC BY-NC license to support further research and development of expressive TTS systems.
