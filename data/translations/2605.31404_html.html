摘要Abstract

基于大语言模型 (LLM) 的导航系统通常会构建显式的空间表示（例如拓扑图、语义栅格图），并将其转换为文本描述作为 LLM 的输入。然而，这种基于文本的空间表示的语言结构以及它们所包含的上下文特征（例如拓扑、几何）的选择通常被视为中立的工程决策，而不是塑造法学硕士行为的关键因素。为了填补这一空白，我们提出了一个双重干预框架，将语言结构与不同的语境线索分开，以评估法学硕士在导航规划方面的语言归纳偏差。在该框架中，表征干预改变了语言格式和语言压缩程度，澄清了语言表征何时支持或抑制导航规划。语境干预结合语境特征组合和冲突探索，明确阐明了法学硕士在处理不同语境线索时的偏好和弱点。跨不同空间推理任务和多个模型尺度的实验揭示了一个一致的模式：拓扑信息是坚固的盾牌和稳健规划的支柱；语言格式是一把双刃剑，其效果取决于模型大小、任务需求和压缩级别；语义信息是致命的致命弱点——不正确的语义线索可能会系统性地破坏规划过程。总的来说，我们的研究表明，基于 LLM 的导航中有效的基于文本的空间表示应该保持拓扑完整性，校准模型容量的表示压缩，并确保语义正确性，而不是简单地采用单一表示。我们的代码公开于Large Language Model (LLM)-based navigation systems commonly construct explicit spatial representations (e.g., topological graphs, semantic raster maps) and translate them into textual descriptions as LLMs’ inputs. However, the linguistic structures of such text-based spatial representations and the choices of contextual features (e.g., topology, geometry) they contain are often treated as neutral engineering decisions rather than key factors that shape LLMs’ behavior. To fill the gap, we propose a dual-interventional framework that disentangles linguistic structures from different contextual cues to evaluate the linguistic inductive bias of LLMs for navigation planning. In the framework, representation intervention varies the linguistic format and the degree of linguistic compression, clarifying when linguistic representations support or inhibit navigation planning. Context intervention, combined with contextual feature combination and conflict probing, explicitly clarifies the preferences and weaknesses of LLMs when processing different contextual cues. Experiments across diverse spatial reasoning tasks and multiple model scales reveal a consistent pattern: topological information is a sturdy shield and the backbone of robust planning; linguistic format is a double-edged sword whose effect depends on model size, task demands, and the compression level; and semantic information is a fatal Achilles’ heel—incorrect semantic cues can systematically derail the planning process. Overall, our study shows that effective text-based spatial representations in LLM-based navigation should preserve topological integrity, calibrate representational compression to model capacity, and ensure semantic correctness, rather than simply adopting a single representation. Our code is publicly available athttps://github.com/jonesdong150/LLM-Navigation-Inductive-Bias.

1 简介Introduction

空间智能是具体人工智能的核心能力，特别是在导航任务中，智能体必须规划目标导向的路径，而不是依赖于反应性避障Spatial intelligence is a central capability of embodied artificial intelligence, particularly in navigation tasks where agents must plan goal-directed paths rather than rely on reactive obstacle avoidanceDeitke et al. (2020); Savva et al. (2019)。 最近，大型语言模型（LLM）已被采用作为高级导航规划器，负责对符号空间表示进行推理并生成导航子目标. Recently, Large Language Models (LLMs) have been adopted as high-level navigation planners, responsible for reasoning over symbolic spatial representations and generating navigation sub-goalsWei et al. (2022); Yao et al. (2022); Wang et al. (2023a)。 大多数现有系统都遵循分层架构，其中法学硕士从显式空间输入（例如拓扑图或语义图）推断抽象导航决策，而较低级别的控制器则执行操作. Most existing systems follow a hierarchical architecture, in which LLMs infer abstract navigation decisions from explicit spatial inputs—such as topological graphs or semantic maps—while lower-level controllers execute actionsShah et al. (2023); Huang et al. (2022a); Rana et al. (2023).

尽管进展迅速，但一个基本问题仍未得到充分探讨：Despite rapid progress, a fundamental question remains insufficiently explored:序列化空间输入的语言表示如何塑造法学硕士的导航推理行为？How does the linguistic representation of serialized spatial inputs shape LLMs’ navigation reasoning behavior?先前的工作主要将序列化视为中立的工程步骤，将结构化空间表示转换为文本以促进规划，对语言结构和上下文线索如何影响推理结果进行有限的系统分析Prior work largely treats serialization as a neutral engineering step, converting structured spatial representations into text to facilitate planning, with limited systematic analysis of how linguistic structure and contextual cues influence reasoning outcomesChen et al. 工程指导：Engineering Guidance:对于现实世界的部署，语义消歧是强制性的。仅仅依靠拓扑 ID 不足以抵消 LLM 固有的语义偏见。具有重复标签的场景必须使用独特的语义描述符（例如“北卧室”）进行增强，以防止导航系统因标签混淆而崩溃。For real-world deployment, semantic disambiguation is mandatory. Relying solely on topological IDs is insufficient to counteract the LLM’s inherent semantic bias. Scenes with duplicate labels must be augmented with unique semantic descriptors (e.g., “North Bedroom”) to prevent the navigation system from collapsing due to label confusion.

5 结论Conclusion

基于 LLM 的导航系统通常将显式空间表示序列化为文本，但LLM-based navigation systems commonly serialize explicit spatial representations into text, yet the语言结构linguistic structure这个连载和of this serialization and the上下文线索contextual cues接触模型通常被视为中立的工程选择。本文挑战了这一假设，认为序列化本身构成了一种建模决策，可以在基于 Transformer 的规划中主动塑造注意力、证据权重和故障模式。exposed to the model are often treated as neutral engineering choices. This paper challenges this assumption by arguing that serialization itself constitutes a modeling decision that actively shapes attention, evidence weighting, and failure modes in Transformer-based planning.

为了使这个界面可分析，我们引入了一个双重干预框架，该框架可以解开（i）表示干预To make this interface analyzable, we introduce a dual-interventional framework that disentangles (i) representation intervention over语言格式linguistic format和and压缩程度degree of compression在严格的信息对等下，以及（ii）控制线索可用性并注入线索冲突以揭示与规划相关的偏好和漏洞的背景干预。under strict information equivalence, and (ii) context intervention that controls cue availability and injects cue conflicts to reveal planning-relevant preferences and vulnerabilities.

在任务和模型尺度上，我们确定了三个隐喻所捕获的语言归纳偏差的一致模式。首先，剑：显式语言结构充当条件支架，有利于部署友好的模型，但在高压缩或空间复杂性下表现出依赖于尺度的反转和开销。其次，盾牌：拓扑连接为导航规划提供了最稳定的支柱，即使两者都足够，也能主导基于几何的线索。第三，阿喀琉斯之踵：语义模糊性可能会推翻原本正确的结构证据，穿透拓扑鲁棒性并系统性地破坏全球路线建设。Across tasks and model scales, we identify a consistent pattern of linguistic inductive bias captured by three metaphors. First, the Sword: explicit linguistic structure acts as a conditional scaffold, benefiting deployment-friendly models but exhibiting scale-dependent reversals and overhead under high compression or spatial complexity. Second, the Shield: topological connectivity provides the most stable backbone for navigation planning, dominating geometry-based cues even when both are sufficient. Third, the Achilles’ Heel: semantic ambiguity can override otherwise-correct structural evidence, penetrating topological robustness and systematically derailing global route construction.

这些发现转化为基于 LLM 的导航的具体工程指南：将拓扑完整性保留为一流约束，校准表征压缩以建模容量和空间负载，以任务和部署感知的方式选择语言格式，并将语义正确性和消歧视为强制而非辅助。These findings translate into concrete engineering guidance for LLM-based navigation: preserve topological integrity as a first-class constraint, calibrate representational compression to model capacity and spatial load, select linguistic format in a task- and deployment-aware manner, and treat semantic correctness and disambiguation as mandatory rather than auxiliary.

我们的研究因其关注旨在确保信息等效性的受控合成环境而受到限制。未来工作的一个重要方向是将所提出的干预框架扩展到具有感知噪声和不完整注释的现实世界基准，测试相同的归纳偏差特征是否在现实的具体设置下持续存在。Our study is limited by its focus on controlled synthetic environments designed to ensure information equivalence. An important direction for future work is to extend the proposed intervention framework to real-world benchmarks with perceptual noise and incomplete annotations, testing whether the same inductive-bias signatures persist under realistic embodied settings.
