MOC：基于 LLM 的多代理系统中的多阶通信MOC: Multi-Order Communication in LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems

Yao Guan    Lin Wang    Zhihu Lu    Ziyi Wang    Wenzhu Yan    Qiang Duan
摘要Abstract

尽管基于大语言模型（LLM）的多智能体系统取得了显着进展，但大多数研究都集中在优化协调拓扑上，而在很大程度上没有充分探索同样关键的问题：Despite the remarkable progress of Large Language Model (LLM) based Multi-Agent Systems, most research focuses on optimizing coordination topology while largely underexploring the equally critical problem:如何有效地在Agent之间传递和优化消息？how to transmit and optimize messages among agents effectively?当前的通信方案通常依赖于一阶邻居响应的直接串联，这会导致证据接受域受到限制，并导致多跳路径上的关键见解被稀释。为了解决这些限制，我们提出了多阶通信（MOC）方案，该方案重建代理间通信以捕获多跳依赖关系，并结合结构消息合并策略以确保效率。具体来说，我们形式化了通信机制来构建结构化的多阶证据流，然后设计了语义拓扑合并算法来优化令牌约束内的语义保真度。跨越六个不同数据集和不同参数规模的 LLM 主干的广泛实验表明，MOC 能够持续提高任务性能并降低通信成本。该代码可在Current communication schemes typically rely on the direct concatenation of first-order neighbor responses, which induces a restricted evidence receptive field and leads to the dilution of crucial insights over multi-hop paths. To address these limitations, we propose the Multi-Order Communication (MOC) scheme, which reconstructs the inter-agent communication to capture multi-hop dependencies and incorporates a structural message consolidation strategy to ensure efficiency. Specifically, we formalize the communication mechanism to construct a structured multi-order evidence stream, and subsequently design a Semantic-Topological Merging algorithm to optimize semantic fidelity within token constraints. Extensive experiments across six diverse datasets and LLM backbones of varying parameter scales demonstrate that MOC consistently improves task performance and reduces communication costs. 1 简介Introduction

大语言模型（LLM）Large Language Model (LLM)(Zhao et al., 2023; Chang et al., 2024; Naveed et al., 2025)基于多代理系统 (MAS) 已成为解决复杂问题任务的一种有前途的范例，这些任务需要长期推理、工具使用和可靠验证based Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) have emerged as a promising paradigm for complex problem-solving tasks that require long-horizon reasoning, tool use, and reliable verification(Guo et al., 2024; He et al., 2025)。通过编排自主代理集体进行多步骤规划和迭代审议，MAS 可以有效地将复杂的目标分解为可管理的子目标，为不同的代理分配专门的角色，并促进解决方案空间的并行探索. Through orchestrating collectives of autonomous agents to engage in multi-step planning and iterative deliberation, MAS can effectively decompose intricate objectives into manageable sub-goals, assign specialized roles to distinct agents, and facilitate parallel exploration of the solution space(Li et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024; Tran et al., 2025)。这种新兴的集体智能从根本上是由多智能体拓扑和通信方案的优化控制的，即智能体如何连接以及如何通信。. Such emergent collective intelligence is fundamentally governed by the optimization of multi-agent topology and communication scheme, i.e., how agents are connected and how they communicate.

最近的工作主要集中在多智能体协调的拓扑设计上Recent works have predominantly focused on topology design for multi-agent coordination(Hong et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024b; Wu et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025b; Qian et al., 2025)，探索如何构建代理连接以实现有效协作。人们已经探索了广泛的拓扑结构，从轻质结构（例如链）, exploring how to structure agent connectivity to enable effective collaboration. A broad spectrum of topologies has been explored, ranging from lightweight structures such as chain(Hong et al., 2024)和树and tree(Wu et al., 2024)，密集或随机连接，例如完全连接图和随机图, to dense or stochastic connectivity such as fully connected and random graphs(Qian et al., 2025)，以及最近的任务自适应动态拓扑，可根据任务生成或重新配置连接, and more recently to task-adaptive dynamic topologies that generate or reconfigure connectivity conditioned on the task(Zhuge et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024b; Zhang et al., 2025b)。虽然这些以拓扑为中心的研究证实，适当构建代理交互可以在有限监督下改善协调，但同样重要但被忽视的问题仍然存在：. While these topology-centric studies verify that appropriately structuring agent interactions can improve coordination under limited supervision, an equally critical yet overlooked question remains:除了连接代理之外，我们应该如何构建通信方案来有效地传输和优化消息？Beyond connecting agents, how should we structure the communication scheme to transmit and optimize messages effectively?

在实践中，底层的通信机制仍然处于初级阶段In practice, the underlying communication mechanism remains rudimentary(Chen et al., 2024a)，因为现有的基于 LLM 的 MAS 大多数采用简单的通信方案，直接连接直接连接的前驱代理的输出。这种僵化的交互范式受到来自以下原因的严重限制：, as existing LLM-based MAS mostly adopt a simple communication scheme that directly concatenates outputs from immediately connected predecessor agents. 6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们解决了基于 LLM 的 MAS 中有效消息传输和优化的关键但尚未充分探索的问题。我们提出了多阶通信（MOC），这是一种拓扑感知方案，通过跨多跳距离呈现原始上游响应来扩展证据接受域。为了确保实际效率，我们引入了语义拓扑合并算法，该算法可以在严格遵守通信层次结构的同时修剪语义冗余。In this paper, we addressed the critical yet underexplored problem of effective message transmission and optimization in LLM-based MAS. We proposed Multi-Order Communication (MOC), a topology-aware scheme that expands the evidence receptive field by surfacing raw upstream responses across multiple hop distances. To ensure practical efficiency, we introduced a Semantic-Topological Merging algorithm that prunes semantic redundancy while strictly honoring the communication hierarchy.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and Future Works.虽然 MOC 在增强基于 LLM 的 MAS 方面显示出有希望的成果，但其沟通顺序While MOC shows promising results in enhancing LLM-based MAS, its communication orderKK目前是固定的而不是自适应确定的。尽管高阶证据可以丰富目标代理的上下文，但它也可能引入不正确的推理，甚至来自受损代理的潜在恶意消息。未来的工作将探索自适应和信任感知方案，根据拓扑结构、证据一致性和消息可靠性动态选择跳范围和上游消息，从而在保持通信效率的同时提高鲁棒性。is currently fixed rather than adaptively determined. Although higher-order evidence can enrich the target agent’s context, it may also introduce incorrect reasoning or even potentially malicious messages from compromised agents. Future work will explore adaptive and trust-aware schemes that dynamically select both the hop range and upstream messages based on topology structure, evidence consistency, and message reliability, thereby improving robustness while preserving communication efficiency.
