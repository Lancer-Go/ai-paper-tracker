摘要Abstract

人工智能（AI）在日常生活中的日益融合给人类如何与自主代理互动带来了新的挑战和问题。人类和人工智能共同行动的多智能体实验可以为研究社会决策提供重要机会，但研究人员缺乏可用于运行此类实验的可用工具。我们引入了两种旨在减少这些障碍的工具。第一个是 COGRID，是一个基于多代理网格的模拟库，具有双 NumPy 和 JAX 后端。第二个是多用户体育馆 (MUG)，将此类模拟环境直接转化为基于网络的交互式实验。 MUG 支持与任意数量的人类和人工智能交互，利用服务器权威或点对点网络与回滚网络代码来解决延迟。这些工具共同使研究人员能够部署人机交互研究，促进对心理学、认知和决策的核心问题及其与人机交互的关系的探究。这两种工具都是开源的，可供更广泛的研究社区使用。文档和源代码可在以下位置获取The increasing integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in everyday life brings with it new challenges and questions for regarding how humans interact with autonomous agents. Multi-agent experiments, where humans and AI act together, can offer important opportunities to study social decision making, but there is a lack of accessible tooling available to researchers to run such experiments. We introduce two tools designed to reduce these barriers. The first, COGRID, is a multi-agent grid-based simulation library with dual NumPy and JAX backends. The second, Multi-User Gymnasium (MUG), translates such simulation environments directly into interactive web-based experiments. MUG supports interactions with arbitrary numbers of humans and AI, utilizing either server-authoritative or peer-to-peer networking with rollback netcode to account for latency. Together, these tools can enable researchers to deploy studies of human-AI interaction, facilitating inquiry into core questions of psychology, cognition, and decision making and their relationship to human-AI interaction. Both tools are open source and available to the broader research community. 1 简介1 Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 研究在生产高性能系统方面取得了不可否认的进步，但大多数基准测试和评估都强调孤立的性能而不是与人类的交互。在现实世界中，人工智能不仅必须具备能力，而且还必须与人类（其主要受益者）有效互动，并做出与人类决策过程相一致和补充的决策（Russell，2019；Wilder 等，2020）。Artificial intelligence (AI) research has made undeniable progress in producing highly capable systems, yet most benchmarks and evaluations emphasize isolated performance rather than interaction with humans. In real-world settings, AI must not only be competent but also interact effectively with humans— its main beneficiaries—and make decisions that align with and complement human decision-making processes (Russell, 2019; Wilder et al., 2020).

许多文献都关注在游戏中或在既定基准上优于人类的智能体（例如，(FAIR)† 等人 (2022)、Perolat 等人 (2022) 和 Silver 等人 (2021)），而很少关注这些智能体如何与人类协作者交互或影响。即使考虑到人类交互，也并不总是包含最先进的人工智能系统。例如，人类与人工智能团队的研究通常依赖于调查、基于规则的专家系统或绿野仙踪范例，而不是完全自主的、基于学习的代理（例如，Duan 等人，2024 年；Salikutluk 等人，2024 年；Schelble 等人，2022 年；Zhang 等人，2021 年）。尽管早期工作强调了理解人类感知、偏好和协调动态的重要性（Carroll 等人，2020；Schmutz 等人，2024；Strouse 等人，2021），但由于缺乏可用于进行受控人类人工智能实验的可访问平台，进展仍然受到限制。Much of the literature has focused on agents that outperform humans in games or on established benchmarks (e.g., (FAIR)† et al. (2022), Perolat et al. (2022), and Silver et al. (2021)), with far less attention to how such agents interact with or impact human collaborators. Even when human interaction is considered, state-of-the-art AI systems are not always incorporated. For example, research on human-AI teaming often relies on surveys, rule-based expert systems, or Wizard-of-Oz paradigms rather than fully autonomous, learning-based agents (e.g., Duan et al., 2024; Salikutluk et al., 2024; Schelble et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2021). Although early work highlights the importance of understanding human perceptions, preferences, and coordination dynamics (Carroll et al., 2020; Schmutz et al., 2024; Strouse et al., 2021), progress remains constrained by the lack of accessible platforms for conducting controlled human-AI experiments.

与纯模拟或仅针对人类的研究相比，研究人机交互面临着独特的挑战。在强化学习的背景下，在纯模拟中为单个代理设计自定义设置可能很麻烦（Bamford 等人，2022）。添加人机交互使此类查询进一步复杂化（Ouyang et al., 2022），需要实施必要的基础设施，从网络到用户界面和数据收集管道，这些基础设施通常对于复杂任务来说是非标准的，需要为每项研究从头开始重建。这些都是进入实证人类人工智能研究的重大障碍。Studying human-AI interaction poses unique challenges compared to pure simulation or human-only studies. In the context of reinforcement learning, designing custom settings for single agents in pure simulation can be cumbersome (Bamford et al., 2022). Adding human interaction further complicates such inquiries (Ouyang et al., 2022), requiring the implementation of necessary infrastructure, from networking to user interfaces and data collection pipelines that are often non-standard for complex tasks and rebuilt from scratch for each study. These are significant barriers to entry in empirical human-AI research.

第二个工具 MUG 将模拟环境的部署简化为基于网络的交互式实验。 MUG 采用基于 Python 的环境，遵循 Gymnasium 或 PettingZoo API，并使它们可以在浏览器中运行，无需游戏引擎或遵循视频游戏开发周期（Glazer & Madhav，2015）。环境可以在服务器上运行，也可以通过 Pyodide 直接在参与者的浏览器中运行（Python 编译为 WebAssembly；Team，2021）。对于多人游戏实验，它支持使用 GGPO 风格（Good Game Peace Out；Cannon，2019）回滚网络代码进行点对点网络以进行延迟校正。 MUG 提供通用的实验基础设施，包括登陆页面、等候室、配对、数据收集、人工智能推理和调查，因此研究人员无需为每项研究重建这些组件。The second tool, MUG, streamlines the deployment of simulation environments into interactive web-based experiments. MUG takes Python-based environments that follow the Gymnasium or PettingZoo APIs and makes them playable in the browser without the need for game engines or following the video game development cycle (Glazer & Madhav, 2015). Environments can run on the server or directly in participants’ browsers via Pyodide (Python compiled to WebAssembly; Team, 2021). For multiplayer experiments, it enables peer-to-peer networking with GGPO-style (Good Game Peace Out; Cannon, 2019) rollback netcode for latency correction. MUG provides common experiment infrastructure—including landing pages, waiting rooms, matchmaking, data collection, AI inference, and surveys—so that researchers do not need to rebuild these components for each study.

2.1 多智能体环境和实验2.1 Multi-Agent Environments and Experiments

强化学习中主体-环境循环的标准接口是 Gymnasium (Towers et al., 2023)。它定义了代理如何与环境交互的 API；然而，它并没有标准化，甚至没有允许多代理交互。绝大多数多代理环境通过较小的扩展或更改来增强 Gymnasium API，并维护大部分接口（即 Bamford 等人，2022 年；Liang 等人，2018 年；Terry 等人，2021 年）。The standard interface for the agent-environment cycle in reinforcement learning is Gymnasium (Towers et al., 2023). It defines the API for how an agent can interact with the environment; however, it does not standardize or even allow for multi-agent interactions. The vast majority of multi-agent environments augment the Gymnasium API through minor extensions or alterations, maintaining most of the interface (i.e., Bamford et al., 2022; Liang et al., 2018; Terry et al., 2021).

虽然单代理环境的开发在很大程度上是围绕 Gymnasium API 统一的，但强化学习研究社区中多代理 API 的采用却不太统一，尽管一些平台已经朝着标准方法迈出了一大步。作为 Gymnasium API 的扩展，PettingZoo 接口（Terry 等人，2021）已得到广泛采用。 PettingZoo 为多代理设置中的代理环境循环提供标准化 API，以及许多现有的基准环境，例如多代理 Atari 套件。Whereas the development of single-agent environments has largely unified around the Gymnasium API, the adoption of a multi-agent API in the reinforcement learning research community has been less uniform, although several platforms have made strides toward a standard approach. In an extension of the Gymnasium API, the PettingZoo interface (Terry et al., 2021) has seen significant adoption. PettingZoo provides a standardized API for the agent-environment cycle in multi-agent settings, alongside a number of existing benchmark environments, such as the multi-agent Atari suite.

用于与环境交互的 API 只是多智能体模拟框架的一个组件。另一个关键组成部分是环境本身的实现，包括状态表示和转换动态。在单代理环境中，广泛的成熟框架和平台支持环境的构建和扩展（例如，Chevalier-Boisvert et al., 2023；Samvelyan et al., 2021；Sukhbaatar et al., 2015）。相比之下，多代理域提供的此类资源要少得多。现有平台通常定义自己的环境创建格式，这些格式在复杂性、抽象级别和可定制性方面差异很大。An API for interacting with an environment is only one component of a multi-agent simulation framework. The other critical component is the implementation of the environment itself, including the state representation and transition dynamics. In the single agent setting, a wide range of mature frameworks and platforms support the construction and extension of environments (e.g., Chevalier-Boisvert et al., 2023; Samvelyan et al., 2021; Sukhbaatar et al., 2015). In contrast, the multi-agent domain offers substantially fewer such resources. Existing platforms typically define their own environment-creating formats, which vary considerably in complexity, abstraction level, and degree of customizability.

All interaction is implicit, mediated through actions and observations in the shared environment. There is no built-in text chat or structured messaging system, which limits applicability to studies that require communication. Future work will address this by incorporating communication capabilities into MUG in the form of text-based chat. Furthermore, MUG is currently aimed at interactions within simulation environments, but could be extended to support complex interactions that include communication, demonstration, preference elicitation, and other advanced features.

The goal of this work is to support research in multi-agent systems, particularly in human-AI interaction and complementarity. By providing tools that support the rapid development and testing of ideas, we hope to contribute to progress in building autonomous agents that better serve the humans with whom they interact in the real world.

A.1 COGRID 煮过头A.1 COGRID Overcooked

Overcooked 环境是通过子类化 CoGridEnv 并通过 COGRID 的注册系统定义特定于任务的组件来实现的。每个组件（对象、奖励和特征）都是一个在初始化时注册的 Python 类。环境布局是通过配置文件中的 ASCII 网格编码指定的。完整的源代码和实现细节可在 cogrid.readthedocs.io 的在线文档中找到。The Overcooked environment is implemented by subclassing CoGridEnv and defining the task-specific components through COGRID’s registration system. Each component—objects, rewards, and features—is a Python class that is registered at initialization. The environment layout is specified via an ASCII grid encoding in the configuration file. Full source code and implementation details are available in the online documentation at cogrid.readthedocs.io.
