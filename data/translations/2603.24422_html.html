摘要。Abstract.

生成检索（GR）已成为现代搜索系统的一个有前途的范例。与多级级联架构相比，它具有端到端联合优化和计算效率高等优点。 OneSearch作为具有代表性的工业规模部署的生成搜索框架，带来了显着的商业和运营效益。然而，其对复杂查询的理解不足、对潜在用户意图的低效利用以及对狭窄历史偏好的过度拟合限制了其进一步的性能提升。 为了应对这些挑战，我们建议Generative Retrieval (GR) has emerged as a promising paradigm for modern search systems. Compared to multi-stage cascaded architecture, it offers advantages such as end-to-end joint optimization and high computational efficiency. OneSearch, as a representative industrial-scale deployed generative search framework, has brought significant commercial and operational benefits. However, its inadequate understanding of complex queries, inefficient exploitation of latent user intents, and overfitting to narrow historical preferences have limited its further performance improvement. 1. 简介Introduction

利用广泛的世界知识和强大的语言建模能力，大型语言模型（LLM）正在深刻地重塑搜索和推荐系统。与直接将法学硕士应用或提炼为较小的模型以实现召回、相关性和排名等孤立模块相比Leveraging extensive world knowledge and powerful language modeling capabilities, large language models (LLMs) are profoundly reshaping search and recommendation systems. Compared to directly applying or distilling LLMs into smaller models for isolated modules such as recall, relevance, and ranking(Zhai et al., 2024; Zhu et al., 2025; Dong et al., 2026; Tang et al., 2025)，一个更有前景的研究前沿在于采用端到端生成检索来取代传统的多级级联架构，例如用于视频推荐的 OneRec, a more promising research frontier lies in employing end-to-end generative retrieval to replace the traditional multi-stage cascading architecture, as exemplified by OneRec for video recommendation(Deng et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025), OneSug 用于查询建议, OneSug for query suggestion(Guo et al., 2025)、OneLoc为本地生活服务, OneLoc for local life services(Wei et al., 2025)，以及用于广告的 MTGR, and MTGR for advertisement(Han et al., 2025)。 对于电子商务搜索，需要同时考虑查询-项目相关性和用户-项目协作偏好，代表性的生成模型是OneSearch。它可以直接优化最终目标，以显着降低的计算开销实现卓越的业务性能。. For e-commerce search, which requires jointly considering query-item relevance and user-item collaborative preference, the representative generative model is OneSearch. It enables direct optimization of the final objective, achieving superior business performance with substantially lower computational overhead.

然而，随着用户偏好变得越来越多样化并且搜索查询变得越来越复杂，我们确定了限制 OneSearch 性能的三个关键限制：However, as user preferences grow increasingly diverse and search queries become more complex, we identify three key limitations that constrain the performance of OneSearch:

1) 对复杂查询的理解不够。Insufficient understanding of complex queries.典型的搜索查询由 2-3 个短关键字组成，但许多搜索查询并未指定具体的项目目标。例如，“室内健身器材”可能合理地对应于跑步机或哑铃，但不对应于山地自行车。此外，长尾查询经常表现出与目标项目显着的词汇差异，例如否定型查询（例如“缓解疲劳，不补充”）和问题型查询（例如“什么游泳必需品？”）。这些复杂的查询需要更强的语义理解和推理能力。但是，OneSearch 将原始查询作为输入，并在严格的延迟限制下通过单次前向传递生成目标项。尽管它在训练期间（SFT 第一阶段）纳入了类别级监督，但该模型仍然缺乏深度理解这些模糊查询的能力。Typical search queries consist of 2–3 short keywords, yet many do not specify concrete item targets. For instance, “indoor fitness equipment” may reasonably correspond to treadmills or dumbbells, but not mountain bikes. Furthermore, long-tail queries frequently exhibit significant lexical disparity from target items, such as negation-type queries (e.g., “relieve fatigue, no supplements”) and question-type queries (e.g., “what swimming essentials?”). These complex queries demand stronger semantic understanding and reasoning capabilities. However, OneSearch takes the raw query as input and generates target items in a single forward pass under strict latency constraints. Although it incorporates category-level supervision during training (SFT Stage 1), the model still lacks the capacity for deep comprehension of these ambiguous queries.

2) 对用户上下文的个性化意图推理不足。Insufficient personalized intent reasoning over user context.除了查询级别的理解之外，有效的电子商务搜索还需要对特定于用户的上下文进行推理，但 OneSearch 的定期更新严重依赖于历史共现模式和日志拟合目标，不可避免地导致浅层匹配，无法揭示真实的用户意图。例如，假设用户对某些花卉过敏并搜索“季节性鲜花”，模型应首先推断当前季节，识别哪些品种正在盛开，并主动避免过敏物种——即使这些商品在同一查询下历史上表现出强烈的转化。虽然法学硕士可以通过明确的思想链 (CoT) 擅长进行此类个性化意图推理，但令牌生成的大幅增加使得测试时计算对于在线部署而言过于昂贵。Beyond query-level understanding, effective e-commerce search further requires reasoning over user-specific context—yet OneSearch’s periodic updates rely heavily on historical co-occurrence patterns and log-fitting objectives, inevitably resulting in shallow matching that fails to uncover the true user intent. For example, given a user allergic to certain flowers searching for “seasonal fresh flowers,” the model should first reason about the current season, identify which varieties are in bloom, and proactively avoid allergenic species—even if such items historically exhibit strong conversion under the same query. While LLMs can excel at such personalized intent reasoning through explicit chain-of-thought (CoT), the substantially increased token generation renders test-time computation prohibitively expensive for online deployment.

3) 具有分配偏差的脆弱奖励系统。Fragile reward system with distributional bias.多阶段、定期更新的偏好奖励系统阻止 OneSearch 及时适应新出现的查询和用户意图。此外，奖励模型主要根据历史用户行为日志进行训练，很容易受到低效采样和潜在奖励黑客的影响。这些问题共同导致 OneSearch 过度拟合狭隘的历史偏好，从而强化了搜索系统固有的长尾分布偏差。The multi-stage, periodically updated preference reward system prevents OneSearch from adapting in a timely manner to newly emerging queries and user intents. Furthermore, the reward model, primarily trained on historical user behavior logs, is susceptible to inefficient sampling and potential reward hacking. These issues collectively cause OneSearch to overfit narrow historical preferences, reinforcing the long-tail distributional bias inherent in the search system.

2.2. 潜在推理和自我蒸馏Latent Reasoning and Self-Distillation

To circumvent this cost, latent reasoning methods internalize reasoning into continuous hidden representations without explicit verbalization. Coconut(Hao et al., 2025)用潜在空间中的连续思维向量取代文本推理步骤。科迪replaces textual reasoning steps with continuous thought vectors in the latent space. 5. 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 OneSearch-V2，这是一种推理增强型生成搜索框架，解决了复杂查询理解、个性化推理和自适应偏好对齐方面的关键限制。通过思想增强的查询理解、推理内化的自蒸馏和直接的行为反馈优化，V2 在保持部署效率的同时实现了实质性的改进。严格的在线 A/B 测试还展示了显着的对话收益，特别是对于具有模糊语义的挑战性查询。该框架为具有挑战性的细分市场（包括长尾查询、低活跃用户和冷项）带来了特别显着的收益。This paper presents OneSearch-V2, an reasoning enhanced generative search framework addressing critical limitations in complex query understanding, personalized reasoning, and adaptive preference alignment. Through thought-augmented query understanding, reasoning-internalized self-distillation, and direct behavior feedback optimization, V2 achieves substantial improvements while maintaining deployment efficiency. Rigorous online A/B tests also demonstrate significant conversation gains, particularly for challenging queries with ambiguous semantics. The framework delivers particularly pronounced gains for challenging segments including long-tail queries, low-activity users, and cold items.
