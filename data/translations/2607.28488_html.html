范围SCOPE：通过耦合策略进行端到端协调的供应链运营: Supply-Chain Operations through Coupled Policies for End-to-End Coordination

Yunhao Liang1  Xianqi Cao1  Pujun Zhang∗,1,2
Yuan Qu∗,1,2  Yongzhi Qi3  Ningxuan Kang3  Max Z.J. Shen1,2
摘要Abstract

供应链人工智能能否超越孤立的决策模块，走向统一的运营规划？完整的补货计划指定每个地点携带哪些产品、哪个上游工厂供应这些产品、补货的频率以及如何安排交货。这些决策在操作上是耦合的：所选的类别会改变传递到后续阶段的需求和负载；货源分配和补货频率重塑交付请求；而路线的可行性和成本又决定了早期选择的系统价值。然而，在现代供应链中，这些决策通常由单独的部门处理，并通过单独的系统进行优化，这可能导致缺货、库存暴露和本可避免的运输。我们建议Can supply-chain AI move beyond isolated decision modules toward unified operational planning? A complete replenishment plan specifies which products each location carries, which upstream facility supplies it, how often it is replenished, and how deliveries are routed. These decisions are operationally coupled: the selected assortment changes the demand and load passed to later stages; source assignment and replenishment frequency reshape the delivery requests; and route feasibility and cost, in turn, determine the system value of the earlier choices. Yet in modern supply chains, these decisions are often handled by separate departments and optimized through separate systems, which can lead to stockouts, inventory exposure, and avoidable transportation. We propose范围SCOPE：通过端到端协调的耦合策略进行供应链操作，这是一种复合策略模型，将供应链实体表示为令牌，通过共享操作表示将它们置于上下文中，并将每个令牌类型映射到相应的决策接口。每个决策都建立在早期决策形成的部分计划的基础上，而完整的计划则使用共享的系统级实用程序进行评估。我们在城市生鲜补货中实例化了该框架，其中服务频率、品类、运力压力和路网路线相互作用强烈，并根据叮咚和京东这两个处于不同补货梯队的大型供应链的真实运营数据对其进行了评估。在这两种设置中，: Supply-Chain Operations through Coupled Policies for End-to-End Coordination, a composite policy model that represents supply-chain entities as tokens, contextualizes them through a shared operational representation, and maps each token type to the corresponding decision interface. Each decision builds on the partial plan formed by earlier decisions while the completed plan is evaluated using a shared system-level utility. We instantiate this framework in urban fresh-retail replenishment, where service frequency, assortment, capacity pressure, and road-network routing interact strongly, and evaluate it on real operational data from Dingdong and JD.com, two large-scale supply chains operating at different replenishment echelons. Across both settings,范围SCOPE始终优于单独优化每个决策阶段的方法以及供应链运营中常用的以实践为导向的基线。这些结果表明，学习和协调跨部门运营耦合可以带来更有效的端到端供应链决策。consistently outperforms methods that optimize each decision stage separately, as well as practice-oriented baselines commonly used in supply-chain operations. These results show that learning and coordinating cross-department operational couplings lead to more effective end-to-end supply-chain decisions.

1 简介Introduction

供应链是现代社会的操作系统：它们协调跨组织、设施和地区的产品、产能、库存和服务承诺Supply chains are the operating systems of modern society: they coordinate products, capacity, inventory, and service commitments across organizations, facilities, and regions(Mentzer et al. 2001)，他们的失败威胁着经济安全、公共健康和必需品的供应, and their failures threaten economic security, public health, and the availability of essential goods(Baumgartner et al. 2020; The White House 2021; Christopher and Peck 2004; Tang 2006)。然而，操作这些系统的计算工具仍然以旧的范式为主：每个部门都解决自己的优化程序，这里是需求预测器，那里是路线求解器，而其交互决策的系统级后果则未建模。因此，最近的文献呼吁将机器学习整合到供应链运营中. Yet the computational tools that operate these systems remain dominated by an older paradigm: each department solves its own optimization program, a demand forecaster here, a route solver there, while the system-level consequences of their interacting decisions are left unmodeled. Recent literature has therefore called for integrating machine learning into supply-chain operations(Baryannis et al. 2019; Brintrup et al. 2020)。我们认为，人工智能的核心挑战不是更准确地预测需求或优化下游路线，而是了解部门决策之间的耦合 确定彼此的结果并在系统级目标下协调它们。. We argue that the central AI challenge is not to forecast demand or optimize a downstream route more accurately, but to learn the couplings through which departmental decisions determine each other’s outcomes and coordinate them under system-level objective.

在真正的现代供应链中，平台被组织成功能决策层：销售团队决定运输什么产品，补货团队决定库存移动的时间和频率，物流团队决定车辆和路线如何满足最终的需求。这种分解在组织上是自然的，但在算法上是不完整的，呼应了关于去中心化供应链和激励失调的长篇文献In the real modern supply chain, platforms are organized into functional decision layers: merchandising teams decide what products to carry, replenishment teams decide when and how often inventory moves, and logistics teams decide how vehicles and routes serve the resulting demand. This decomposition is organizationally natural but algorithmically incomplete, echoing a long literature on decentralized supply chains and incentive misalignment(Cachon 2003; Cachon and Zipkin 1999; Lariviere and Porteus 2001)：对于某一层而言局部最优的决策通常会将成本、风险或容量负担转移到另一层。: decisions that are locally optimal for one layer routinely shift cost, risk, or capacity burden onto another.

What they lack is a system view of the decision pipeline: existing methods optimize departments in isolation, ignoring upstream effects on downstream problems and whole system value.
