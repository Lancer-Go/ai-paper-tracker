SVR：通过联合判决进行自我验证细化——自适应测试时间计算的置信强化学习SVR: Self-Verifying Refinement via Joint Verdict–Confidence Reinforcement Learning for Adaptive Test-Time Compute

Hongyu Chen1, Liang Lin1,2,3, Guangrun Wang1,2,3,*
Email: chenhy527@mail2.sysu.edu.cn, linliang@live.com, wanggrun@gmail.com 1Sun Yat-sen University; 2Guangdong Key Laboratory of Big Data Analysis and Processing;3X-Era AI Lab
摘要。Abstract.

扩展测试时间计算可以改进语言模型推理，但统一的预算会浪费简单输入上的计算，而验证者引导的细化则依赖于外部反馈。我们引入了自我验证细化（SVR），这是一种无预言机的多轮强化学习框架，它学习使用自我验证作为计算控制策略。在每一轮，模型都会产生一个解决方案以及离散的正确性判定和置信度分数；仅当判决正确且置信度超过阈值时，它才会保留当前答案，否则会使用自己的自我验证继续细化。地面实况正确性仅用于构建训练奖励，并且永远不会通过细化提示或推理时所需的方式暴露于策略。 SVR 使用 GRPO 在固定范围轨迹上进行训练，使用奖励来促进解决方案的正确性、校准感知自我验证和停止就绪正确状态；自适应停止仅在推理时激活。在 Qwen3.5-2B 的 7 个数学推理基准上，SVR 的宏观平均精度为 0.563，平均推理次数仅为 2.99。在评估的完整系统比较中，它超出了标准 GRPO、强大的多轮基线和固定预算预言机引导的分数反馈参考，同时比固定十轮推理所需的轮次要少得多。这些结果表明，学习的自我验证可以作为答案保留和自适应测试时间计算分配的有效内部控制信号。Scaling test-time computation can improve language-model reasoning, but uniform budgets waste computation on easy inputs, while verifier-guided refinement relies on external feedback. We introduce Self-Verifying Refinement (SVR), an oracle-free multi-turn reinforcement learning framework that learns to use self-verification as a compute-control policy. At each turn, the model produces a solution together with a discrete correctness verdict and a confidence score; it retains the current answer only when the verdict is Correct and confidence exceeds a threshold, and otherwise continues refinement using its own self-verification. Ground-truth correctness is used only to construct training rewards and is never exposed to the policy through refinement prompts or required at inference. SVR is trained with GRPO on fixed-horizon trajectories using rewards that promote solution correctness, calibration-aware self-verification, and stop-ready correct states; adaptive stopping is activated only at inference. On seven mathematical reasoning benchmarks with Qwen3.5-2B, SVR achieves a macro-average accuracy of 0.563 with only 2.99 inference turns on average. In the evaluated complete-system comparison, it exceeds standard GRPO, strong multi-turn baselines, and a fixed-budget oracle-guided score-feedback reference while requiring substantially fewer turns than fixed ten-turn inference. These results demonstrate that learned self-verification can serve as an effective internal control signal for answer retention and adaptive test-time compute allocation.

1. 简介Introduction

扩展测试时间计算已成为改进语言模型推理的核心策略。模型可以在采样、搜索或迭代细化上花费额外的推理计算，而不是致力于单个响应Scaling test-time computation has become a central strategy for improving language-model reasoning. Models can spend additional inference compute on sampling, search, or iterative refinement rather than committing to a single response(Wei et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023)。然而，额外计算的价值高度依赖于实例：一个简单的问题可能在一次尝试后就已经解决了，而一个困难的问题可能需要多次修改。额外的细化也并非总是有益的，因为稍后的回合可能会修复不正确的解决方案或覆盖已经正确的答案。因此，测试时推理不仅是一个缩放问题，也是一个分配计算和保留正确中间答案的问题。. Yet the value of additional computation is highly instance-dependent: an easy problem may already be solved after one attempt, whereas a difficult problem may require several revisions. Additional refinement is also not uniformly beneficial, because a later turn may repair an incorrect solution or overwrite an answer that was already correct. Test-time reasoning is therefore not only a scaling problem, but also a problem of allocating computation and retaining the right intermediate answer.

现有方法仅解决该问题的一部分。尽管额外计算的边际值存在变化，但固定预算方法为每个输入分配相同数量的样本、搜索步骤或细化次数Existing approaches address only part of this problem. Fixed-budget methods assign the same number of samples, search steps, or refinement turns to every input despite variation in the marginal value of additional computation(Alomrani et al., 2025)。自适应分配方法而是在输入之间分配不同的计算预算，但通常在推理之前做出输入级分配决策，而不是依次决定是否保留或细化当前答案. Adaptive allocation methods instead assign different compute budgets across inputs, but typically make an input-level allocation decision before reasoning rather than deciding turn by turn whether to retain or refine the current answer(Zhai et al., 2026)。自校正方法主要研究如何修改先前的响应，同时保留外部指定的细化量. Self-correction methods primarily study how to revise a previous response while leaving the amount of refinement externally specified(Shinn et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2025)。验证者引导的方法可以通过奖励模型、流程验证者、执行结果或正确性检查来通知选择或修订. Verifier-guided methods can inform selection or revision through reward models, process verifiers, execution results, or correctness checks(Cobbe et al., 2021; Snell et al., 2024; Kamoi et al., 2025)，但这些外部信号可能成本高昂或在部署时不可用。如果没有用于控制细化和答案保留的内部信号，扩展轨迹可能会浪费令牌并将正确的中间答案暴露给有害的修订, but these external signals may be costly or unavailable at deployment. Without an internal signal for controlling refinement and answer retention, extending a trajectory may waste tokens and expose correct intermediate answers to harmful revision(Sui et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2026)。这就提出了我们工作的核心问题：模型是否可以仅使用策略本身生成的信号来学习保留或完善其当前答案，而无需在推理时获取正确性反馈？. This raises the central question of our work: can a model learn to retain or refine its current answer using only signals generated by the policy itself, without access to correctness feedback at inference?

一个自然的候选者是模型对其当前答案的自我验证。然而，一旦该信号控制了计算，校准错误就会变成分配错误：信心不足可能会将已经正确的答案暴露给不必要的和潜在有害的修订，而过度自信可能会在额外的计算修复之前终止不正确的轨迹。众所周知，语言模型是错误校准的A natural candidate is the model’s self-verification of its current answer. Once this signal controls computation, however, calibration errors become allocation errors: underconfidence can expose an already correct answer to unnecessary and potentially harmful revision, whereas overconfidence can terminate an incorrect trajectory before additional computation repairs it. Language models are known to be miscalibrated(Guo et al., 2017; Jiang et al., 2021);尽管他们可以表现出有意义的自我评价信号; although they can exhibit meaningful self-evaluation signals(Kadavath et al., 2022)，将言语信心与答案正确性保持一致对于推理模型来说仍然具有挑战性, aligning verbalized confidence with answer correctness remains challenging for reasoning models(Yoon et al., 2025; Stangel et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2026)。这促使我们不仅将结构化自我验证视为对不确定性的事后描述，而且将其视为决定是否保留当前答案或分配另一个细化回合的政策级别信号。. This motivates treating structured self-verification not merely as a post-hoc description of uncertainty, but as a policy-level signal for deciding whether to retain the current answer or allocate another refinement turn.

Each turn produces a solution, a categorical verdict—正确Correct, 不正确Incorrect, 或, or不确定Unsure——以及当前答案正确概率的置信度估计。在到达最大圈数之前，SVR只有在判决成立时才停止并返回当前答案—and a confidence estimate of the probability that the current answer is correct. Before reaching the maximum number of turns, SVR stops and returns the current answer only when the verdict is正确Correct并且信心超过阈值；否则，它会根据原始问题、之前的解决方案以及策略自身的自我验证来构造下一个提示。真实正确性用于构建训练奖励，但从未在细化提示中暴露或推理时间控制器要求的。因此，“无预言机”描述的是细化策略的信息边界，而不是训练过程中缺乏正确性监督。and confidence exceeds a threshold; otherwise, it constructs the next prompt from the original problem, the previous solution, and the policy’s own self-verification. Ground-truth correctness is used to construct training rewards but is never exposed in refinement prompts or required by the inference-time controller. Thus, “oracle-free” describes the information boundary of the refinement policy rather than the absence of correctness supervision during training.

SVR 使用联合判定-置信强化学习（基于组相对策略优化 (GRPO) 的多轮目标）在固定范围细化轨迹上进行训练SVR is trained on fixed-horizon refinement trajectories using Joint Verdict–Confidence Reinforcement Learning, a multi-turn objective based on Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)(Shao et al., 2024)。每回合求解、自我验证和格式分数被平均为一个轨迹返回，仅监督最终完成的标记；中间轮次有助于返回并提供细化上下文，但不是独立的优化样本。自我验证术语通过 Brier 式目标将置信度与答案正确性结合起来. Per-turn solve, self-verification, and format scores are averaged into one trajectory return, which supervises only the final-completion tokens; intermediate turns contribute to the return and provide refinement context but are not independent optimization samples. The self-verification terms align confidence with answer correctness through a Brier-style objective(Brier, 1950)，惩罚过度自信的错误，并促进错误识别和纠正符合停止资格的状态。由于所有训练轨迹都有固定的范围，因此目标不会直接惩罚已实现的回合或令牌使用。计算节省是在推理时产生的，其中判决置信停止规则可以在最大圈数之前终止轨迹。, penalize overconfident errors, and promote error recognition and correct states eligible for stopping. Because all training trajectories have a fixed horizon, the objective does not directly penalize realized turn or token usage. Compute savings arise at inference, where the verdict–confidence stopping rule can terminate a trajectory before the maximum number of turns.

SVR reaches an All-7 macro-average accuracy of 0.563 with 2.99 inference turns on average (Figure1和表and Table1）。在完整系统比较中，它比最强的非预言机多轮基线高出 7.5 个百分点，并且比评估的固定预算预言机指导参考高出 4.4 个百分点。使用相同训练策略的固定预算扫描表明，没有一个共享停止回合与自适应 SVR 相匹配，这支持了这样的解释：其优势来自于实例相关的答案保留，而不是简单地生成更多细化回合。 SVR 还可以与 10 个样本 GRPO 多数投票的总体准确性相匹配，同时消耗大约一半的代币。总之，这些结果支持学习的自我验证作为答案保留和自适应测试时间计算分配的内部信号。). It outperforms the strongest non-oracle multi-turn baseline by 7.5 percentage points and scores 4.4 points above the evaluated fixed-budget oracle-guided reference in a complete-system comparison. Fixed-budget sweeps using the same trained policies show that no single shared stopping turn matches adaptive SVR, supporting the interpretation that its advantage comes from instance-dependent answer retention rather than simply generating more refinement turns. SVR also matches the aggregate accuracy of ten-sample GRPO majority voting while consuming approximately half as many tokens. Together, these results support learned self-verification as an internal signal for both answer retention and adaptive test-time compute allocation.

我们的贡献是三重的。首先，我们介绍 SVR，这是一个无需预言机的闭环细化框架，它使用策略生成的判决和置信度估计来决定是否保留或修改当前答案。其次，我们开发了联合判决-置信强化学习，这是一种固定范围、多回合的目标，可聚合回合级解决方案、自我验证并将信号格式化为轨迹级回报。该目标训练策略为推理时间停止生成判决置信信号，而无需直接优化已实现的推理成本。第三，在七个推理基准中，SVR 实现了比评估基线更强的总体准确性计算权衡，并以大约一半的代币成本匹配十个样本 GRPO 多数投票。Our contributions are threefold. First, we introduce SVR, an oracle-free closed-loop refinement framework that uses a policy-generated verdict and confidence estimate to decide whether to retain or revise the current answer. Second, we develop Joint Verdict–Confidence Reinforcement Learning, a fixed-horizon, multi-turn objective that aggregates turn-level solution, self-verification, and formatting signals into a trajectory-level return. This objective trains the policy to produce a verdict–confidence signal for inference-time stopping without directly optimizing the realized inference cost. Third, across seven reasoning benchmarks, SVR achieves a stronger aggregate accuracy–compute trade-off than the evaluated baselines and matches ten-sample GRPO majority voting at approximately half the token cost.

2. 相关工作Related Work

自适应测试时计算。Adaptive test-time compute.增加测试时间计算可以通过显式中间推理、重复采样和结构化搜索来改进语言模型推理。思维链提示引发中间推理步骤Increasing test-time computation can improve language-model reasoning through explicit intermediate reasoning, repeated sampling, and structured search. Chain-of-thought prompting elicits intermediate reasoning steps(Wei et al., 2022)，自一致性聚合独立采样的推理路径, self-consistency aggregates independently sampled reasoning paths(Wang et al., 2023)，树结构方法在选择答案之前探索和评估替代推理路径, and tree-structured methods explore and evaluate alternative reasoning paths before selecting an answer(Yao et al., 2023)。最近的工作越来越多地将推理计算视为资源分配问题：额外计算的边际效用因输入而异，从而激发了有限预算下的按提示分配. Recent work increasingly treats inference compute as a resource-allocation problem: the marginal utility of additional computation varies across inputs, motivating per-prompt allocation under finite budgets(Snell et al., 2024; Alomrani et al., 2025; Zhai et al., 2026)。例如，学习如何难以思考，预测输入预算对的奖励分配，并使用它们来分配最佳的-. Learning How Hard to Think, for example, predicts reward distributions for input–budget pairs and uses them to allocate best-of-kk在解码器之间采样或路由输入samples or route inputs between decoders(Damani et al., 2025)。这些方法主要根据输入级预测来分配计算。然而，随着解决方案的发展，持续推理的价值可能会发生变化：额外的计算可能会浪费代币. These methods allocate compute primarily from input-level predictions. Yet the value of continued reasoning can change as a solution evolves: additional computation may waste tokens(Sui et al., 2025)甚至导致模型放弃之前的正确答案and even cause models to abandon previously correct answers(Zhou et al., 2026)。相反，SVR 将自适应分配转移到不断发展的解决方案状态，使用策略生成的自我验证来决定当前答案是否需要另一轮细化。. SVR instead moves adaptive allocation to the evolving solution state, using policy-generated self-verification to decide whether the current answer warrants another refinement turn.

多轮细化和强化学习。Multi-turn refinement and reinforcement learning.多轮推理允许模型修改先前的响应，而不是将每个问题视为单次生成任务。反射使用言语反馈和情景记忆来指导后续尝试Multi-turn reasoning allows a model to revise previous responses instead of treating each problem as a single-shot generation task. Reflexion uses verbal feedback and episodic memory to guide subsequent attempts(Shinn et al., 2023)，而SCoRe则通过多轮在线强化学习训练语言模型进行自我纠正, while SCoRe trains language models to self-correct through multi-turn online reinforcement learning(Kumar et al., 2025)。 SETS结合了测试时的采样、自我验证和自我修正，无需额外的模型训练. SETS combines sampling, self-verification, and self-correction at test time without additional model training(Chen et al., 2025)。 MURPHY 将 GRPO 扩展到具有回顾性信用分配的反馈条件多转代码生成. MURPHY extends GRPO to feedback-conditioned multi-turn code generation with retrospective credit assignment(Ekbote et al., 2025)，而 iGRPO 选择高回报模型生成的草稿，并在第二优化阶段训练草稿条件的细化, whereas iGRPO selects a high-reward model-generated draft and trains a draft-conditioned refinement in a second optimization stage(Hatamizadeh et al., 2026)。与此同时，具有可验证奖励的强化学习已成为数学推理的主要方法。 DeepSeekMath 推出组相对策略优化 (GRPO). In parallel, reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards has become a major approach to mathematical reasoning. DeepSeekMath introduced Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)(Shao et al., 2024)，以及 DeepSeek-R1、Open-Reasoner-Zero 和 VAPO 等系统表明，基于结果的强化学习可以提高长链推理和基准性能, and systems such as DeepSeek-R1, Open-Reasoner-Zero, and VAPO show that outcome-based reinforcement learning can improve long-chain reasoning and benchmark performance(DeepSeek-AI, 2025; Hu et al., 2025; Yue et al., 2025)。数据高效的 RLVR 进一步表明，可以从非常有限的训练示例中获得巨大的推理收益. Data-efficient RLVR further shows that substantial reasoning gains can emerge from very limited training examples(Wang et al., 2025)。在这些方面，细化和转弯水平控制仍然是分开的：多转弯结构是预设的或外部组织的，而 RLVR 则优化单响应轨迹。 SVR 将自我验证集成到多轮 RL 中，因此相同的策略可以学习以改进答案并生成用于推理时间控制的信号。. Across these lines, refinement and turn-level control remain separate: multi-turn structures are preset or externally organized, while RLVR optimizes single-response trajectories. SVR integrates self-verification into multi-turn RL, so the same policy learns both to refine answers and to produce the signal used for inference-time control.

验证和基于置信度的控制。Verification and confidence-based control.验证为提高推理质量提供了重要机制。结果验证者从抽样候选人中选择最终答案Verification provides an important mechanism for improving reasoning quality. Outcome verifiers select final answers from sampled candidates(Cobbe et al., 2021)，而过程奖励模型评估中间推理步骤, while process reward models evaluate intermediate reasoning steps(Lightman et al., 2024)。形式化工具还可以为形式化可检查任务合成步骤级验证标签，从而减少人工注释的需要. Formal tools can also synthesize step-level verification labels for formally checkable tasks, reducing the need for human annotation(Kamoi et al., 2025)。虽然有效，但这些验证者引导的方法通常需要辅助模型、执行环境或推理时特定于任务的正确性信号。相关的工作研究置信度校准。现代神经网络经常被错误校准. Although effective, these verifier-guided methods generally require an auxiliary model, an execution environment, or a task-specific correctness signal at inference. A related line of work studies confidence calibration. 4. 实验Experiments

我们的实验解决了三个问题。首先，SVR 是否比单轮、固定预算多轮和预言机引导参考提高了精度计算边界？其次，它的学习停止策略是否比统一固定轮次细化或独立测试时间采样更有效地分配推理计算？第三，哪些奖励和自我检查组件导致了这种行为，并且在训练种子中所得的收益是否稳定？Our experiments address three questions. First, does SVR improve the accuracy–compute frontier over single-turn, fixed-budget multi-turn, and oracle-guided references? Second, does its learned stopping policy allocate inference computation more effectively than uniform fixed-turn refinement or independent test-time sampling? Third, which reward and self-check components are responsible for this behavior, and are the resulting gains stable across training seeds?
