智能体可以推广到开放世界吗？Can Agents Generalize to the Open World?

摘要Abstract

虽然大型语言模型 (LLM) 代理在静态基准方面表现出熟练程度，但它们在现实场景中的部署受到用户查询、工具集和交互动态的动态特性的阻碍。 为了解决这种泛化差距，我们将While Large Language Model (LLM) agents demonstrate proficiency in static benchmarks, their deployment in real-world scenarios is hindered by the dynamic nature of user queries, tool sets, and interaction dynamics. To address this generalization gap, we formalize开放代理OpenAgent（开放世界中的工具使用代理），一种问题设置，其特征是跨查询、操作、观察和域维度的分布变化。 为了系统地诊断其影响，我们构建了一个受控沙箱环境，在其中定义了四层层次结构中的细粒度环境变化，(Tool-Use Agent in Open-World), a problem setting characterized by distributional shifts across query, action, observation, and domain dimensions. To systematically diagnose its impact, we construct a controlled sandbox environment where we define fine-grained environmental shifts across a four-tier hierarchy,感知Perception, 互动Interaction, 推理Reasoning, 和, and内化Internalization，并进行一系列全面的实验。 我们的分析得出了一系列关键见解，表明通过监督微调（SFT）和强化学习训练的智能体在面对开放的环境变化时会遭受不同程度的性能下降。 基于这些见解，我们提出了扰动增强微调，这是一种基于扰动的 SFT 干预策略，为增强代理在现实环境中的鲁棒性和实用性奠定了基础。我们的代码将发布于：, and conduct a comprehensive series of experiments. Our analysis yields a series of key insights, demonstrating that agents trained via both Supervised Fine-Tuning(SFT) and Reinforcement Learning suffer from varying degrees of performance degradation when confronting open environmental shifts. Building on these insights, we propose Perturbation-Augmented Fine-Tuning, a disturbance-based intervention strategy for SFT that lays the foundation for enhancing agent robustness and utility in realistic environments. 4.1 实验装置Experimental Setup

受控沙箱环境。Controlled Sandbox Environment.

我们利用合成沙箱环境来实现对代理功能的受控探测。与现实世界中的 API 引入无法控制的瞬态噪声不同，沙箱允许我们精确隔离环境变量并建立纯粹的封闭环境基线来测量开放环境的变化。我们综合了一系列不同的工具使用任务，重点关注简单的地理 POI 查询和计算问题，以测试推理边界，其完整规范详见附录We utilize a synthetic sandbox environment to enable controlled probing of agent capabilities. Unlike real-world APIs which introduce uncontrollable transient noise, a sandbox allows us to precisely isolate environmental variables and establish a pure closed environment baseline to measure open environment shifts. We synthesized a diverse set of tool-use tasks focused on simple geographical POI queries and calculation problems to test reasoning boundaries, the full specifications of which are detailed in the AppendixA.

数据集和协议。Datasets and Protocols.

该数据集包含 6,050 个训练样本和 880 个评估样本。为了确保严格的评估，我们在数据集之间强制执行严格的分离，在训练阶段不会出现测试集中的变异模式。这保证了报告的性能反映了真正的稳健性，而不是信息泄漏或模式记忆。The dataset comprises 6,050 training samples and 880 evaluation samples. To ensure rigorous evaluation, we enforced a strict separation between the datasets where no variation patterns from the test set appear during the training phase. Qualitative case studies reveal that SFT tends to interpret observations as successful confirmations, even when the feedback explicitly redirects the agent. Consequently, it ignores the guidance and hallucinates successful observations to force the continuation of its pre-planned trajectory. In contrast, RL maintains high adaptability by parsing the instruction and migrating to the new tool immediately, and then continues reasoning.

我们假设这种行为差异源于训练范式的差异。 SFT 针对清晰的演示进行了优化，其中观察结果始终验证操作。这可能会使模型忽略反馈内容与后续策略之间的因果联系，从而使其行为类似于开环执行策略。相反，强化学习鼓励更加闭环的行为，因为在交互过程中忽略错误信号会受到惩罚。这种交互机制可能会迫使智能体将环境观察视为关键状态更新，确保优先考虑明确的环境指令以最大化奖励。We hypothesize that this behavioral divergence stems from differences in training paradigms. SFT is optimized on clean demonstrations where observations consistently validate actions. This likely conditions the model to disregard the causal link between feedback content and the subsequent policy, thereby making its behavior resemble an open-loop execution policy. Conversely, RL encourages a more closed-loop behavior because ignoring error signals is penalized during interaction. This interaction mechanism potentially forces the agent to treat environmental observations as critical state updates, ensuring that explicit environmental instructions are prioritized to maximize reward.
