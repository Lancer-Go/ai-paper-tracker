摘要Abstract

表格预测是众多应用程序中的一项关键任务。大型语言模型最近的成功引发了各种将其适应表格领域的方法。一种流行的策略涉及训练或微调专门的表格基础模型 (TFM)，例如 TabPFN。然而，TFM 需要大量的计算资源，并且频繁的模型重新训练通常是不切实际的。情境学习 (ICL)，特别是少样本提示，提供了一种资源高效的替代方案来提高绩效。然而，识别最相关的行作为镜头仍然是表格数据的一个挑战。本文介绍了 ARASH（自适应、特定于查询的检索和镜头选择），这是一种通过基于训练集中的局部邻域分析选择最佳镜头来提高 TFM 效率的方法。我们的结果表明，ARASH 将 TabPFN 的提示长度和内存使用量减少了 1261.5Tabular prediction is a critical task across numerous applications. The recent success of large language models has sparked various approaches for adapting them to the tabular domain. A prevalent strategy involves training or fine-tuning specialized Tabular Foundation Models (TFMs) such as TabPFN. However, TFMs require substantial computational resources, and frequent model retraining is often impractical. In-context learning (ICL), specifically, few-shot prompting, offers a resource-efficient alternative to enhance performance. Yet, identifying the most relevant rows to serve as shots remains a challenge for tabular data. This paper introduces ARASH (Adaptive, query-specific Retrieval And Shot selection), a method that improves TFM efficiency by selecting optimal shots based on local neighborhood analysis within the training set. Our results demonstrate that ARASH reduces the prompt length and memory usage of TabPFN by 1261.5×\times和2.56and 2.56×\times，同时提供相当的精度。, respectively, while providing comparable accuracy.

Large LMs (LLMs) can perform supervised prediction in a few-shot manner by conditioning on interleaved input-label demonstrations. Empirical studies in non-tabular domains indicate that ICL performance is highly sensitive to the selected demonstrations, the method of textualization, and the number of shots allocated[4]。因此出现了基于检索的提示，通过识别与嵌入空间中的查询类似的标记实例来自动进行演示选择. Retrieval-based prompting has consequently emerged to automate demonstration selection by identifying labeled instances similar to the query within an embedding space[5, 6].

IV 实施ARASHARASH Implementation

本节详细介绍 ARASH 的高效且可移植的实现技术。为了最大限度地减少开销，ARASH 使用两阶段方法，其中对所有查询执行一次预处理，并且仅在必要时调用基础模型以减少推理延迟。此外，其便携式架构无缝支持各种 LM。This section details the efficient and portable implementation techniques of ARASH. To minimize overhead, ARASH uses a two-stage approach where preprocessing is performed once for all queries, and the foundation model is called only when essential to reduce inference latency. Additionally, its portable architecture seamlessly supports various LMs.

IV-A 高效的处理和推理Efficient Processing and Inference

ARASH 被实现为一个两阶段管道，将数据集级预处理与查询时推理完全分开。在预处理阶段，所有仅依赖于训练数据的计算都会执行一次，包括特征归一化、数据分析、聚类、聚类统计和聚类级镜头预算的分配。此阶段的输出形成可在推理过程中利用的可重用工件。具体来说，ARASH 存储 (1) 特征模式和标准化参数，例如分类级别和缩放统计数据，以确保测试查询的编码与训练数据一致； (2) 簇分配函数以及簇级标签统计； (3) 映射每个集群的镜头预算表ARASH is implemented as a two-stage pipeline that cleanly separates dataset-level preprocessing from query-time inference. In the preprocessing stage, all computations that depend solely on the training data are performed once, including feature normalization, data profiling, clustering, cluster statistics, and allocation of cluster-level shot budgets. The outputs of this stage form reusable artifacts that are leveraged during inference. Specifically, ARASH stores (1) the feature schema and normalization parameters, such as categorical levels and scaling statistics, to ensure that test queries are encoded consistently with the training data; (2) the cluster assignment function along with cluster-level label statistics; and (3) a shot-budget table that maps each clustercc到整数预算to an integer budgetkck_{c}。在推理阶段，这些缓存的工件被重用，通过确定其集群、加载相关演示并生成直接预测或调用冻结的基础模型来有效地处理每个查询。这种分离在表格预测任务中至关重要，其中许多查询通常在同一数据集上进行评估，从而允许 ARASH 分摊分析和聚类的一次性成本，而不是对每个测试实例重复它。. During the inference stage, these cached artifacts are reused to efficiently process each query by determining its cluster, loading relevant demonstrations, and producing a direct prediction or invoking a frozen foundation model. This separation is crucial in tabular prediction tasks, where many queries are typically evaluated on the same dataset, allowing ARASH to amortize the one-time cost of profiling and clustering instead of repeating it for every test instance.

VI 相关工作Related Work

经典的基于树的方法和神经方法为给定的表格数据训练模型。 表格预测以树集成为主，尤其是梯度增强决策树，例如 XGBoostClassical tree-based and neural methods train models for a given tabular data. Tabular prediction is dominated by tree ensembles, especially gradient-boosted decision trees, such as XGBoost[2]。这些模型可以很好地处理异构特征、缺失值、小数据和非平滑边界。. These models handle heterogeneous features, missing values, small data, and non-smooth boundaries well.

These approaches train model parameters for a target dataset. In contrast, ARASH studies inference with a frozen model, focusing on selecting labeled rows, their regions, and query-specific shot budgets.

大语言模型引入了ICL，其中任务适应是通过提示中提供的演示而不是通过参数更新来执行的Large language models introduced ICL, in which task adaptation is performed through demonstrations provided in the prompt rather than through parameter updates[3]。先前的工作表明 ICL 性能对演示的选择很敏感. Prior work has shown that ICL performance is sensitive to the choice of demonstrations[4]。这一观察结果激发了基于检索的 ICL 方法，该方法选择与查询相关的示例，而不是依赖固定或随机提示，包括最近邻提示. This observation has motivated retrieval-based ICL methods, which select query-relevant examples instead of relying on fixed or random prompts, including nearest-neighbor prompting[5, 6]。 多样性感知方法进一步改进选择：行列式点过程提供了选择不同子集的原则方法. Diversity-aware methods further improve selection: determinantal point processes provide a principled way to select diverse subsets[30]并已应用于代表性的上下文示例and have been applied to representative in-context examples[32]，而基于聚类的方法选择与查询相似且多样化的演示, while clustering-based approaches select demonstrations that are both similar to the query and diverse[33]。这些技术主要是为具有自然几何局部性的非表格数据而设计的，而表格数据具有任意的行顺序，使得局部性和纯度更难定义。. These techniques are primarily designed for non-tabular data with natural geometric locality, whereas tabular data has arbitrary row order, making locality and purity harder to define.

Black-box prompting techniques such as full-context methods[7]包括所有演示，无论查询或训练集如何。虽然简单，但这种方法会产生不必要的计算和内存成本，并且可能会在提示中引入不相关的信息，从而可能降低性能。另一个类别根据查询构造提示。这些方法使用固定预算include all demonstrations regardless of the query or training set. Although they reduce prompt length by focusing on local demonstrations, their accuracy may degrade when local regions exhibit impure label distributions. ARASH constructs prompts based on both locality and purity, enabling more effective context selection tailored to tabular data.
