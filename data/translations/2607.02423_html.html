摘要Abstract

主动小样本学习 (AFSL) 通过识别最有价值的未标记样本进行注释并用作小样本演示，使法学硕士适应专业领域，有效降低人工注释成本，同时提高性能。然而，现有的方法通常依赖于输出级信号来进行样本识别，例如预测熵或基于外部嵌入的测试时数据的语义相似性，这通常忽略了模型的内部动态，而模型的内部动态可以查明特定的知识差距。 为了弥补这一差距，我们建议Active Few-Shot Learning (AFSL) adapts LLMs to specialized domains by identifying the most valuable unlabeled samples for annotation and use as few-shot demonstrations, effectively reducing human annotation costs while promoting high performance. However, existing methods typically rely on output-level signals for the sample identification, such as predictive entropy or semantic similarities with test-time data based on external embeddings, which often overlook models’ internal dynamics which could pinpoint specific knowledge gaps. To bridge this gap, we propose神经网络FSNeuFS, 一个, a新Neuron-Aware Active F呃-ew-S热门学习框架，将选择范式从输出级代理转变为模型的内部动态。hot Learning framework that shifts the selection paradigm from output-level proxies to models’ internal dynamics.神经网络FSNeuFS利用神经元激活模式直接表示样本，并包括双重标准选择策略，该策略：（1）通过神经元模式确保少样本样本多样性，以实现更广泛的示例覆盖范围，同时（2）优先识别信息丰富且具有挑战性的少样本样本LLM倾向于通过量化产生幻觉utilizes neuron activation patterns to represent sample directly, and includes a dual-criteria selection strategy that: (1) ensures few-shot sample diversity with neuron patterns for broader example coverage, while (2) prioritizing on identifying informative and challenging few-shot samples LLMs tend to hallucinate by quantifying神经元共识neuron consensus。 对三个数据集的实验表明. Experiments on three datasets demonstrate that神经网络FSNeuFS在推理和文本分类任务中表现出色，优于现有的 AFSL 基线。 消融研究进一步强调，内部神经元激活比外部嵌入提供了更有原则性和更有效的选择信号，验证了所提出的方法的优越性excels in both reasoning and text classification tasks, outperforming existing AFSL baselines. 1 简介Introduction

虽然法学硕士的最新进展极大地改善了文本分类等一般任务，但这些模型通常无法在教育、医学或法律等专业领域中表现良好。由于数据的稀缺性和微调所需的计算成本，通常会使现成的模型适应这些专家领域变得困难。 为了使法学硕士适应特定的下游任务，少样本上下文学习（ICL）已成为一种流行的免训练适应策略。通过在查询上下文中提供示例，ICL 在实际应用中展示了良好的性能While recent advances in LLMs have greatly improved general tasks like text classification, these models often fail to perform well out-of-the-box in specialized domains such as education, medicine, or law. Adapting off-the-shelf models to these expert fields is typically made difficult by the scarcity of data and the computational costs required for fine-tuning. To adapt LLMs for specific downstream tasks, few-shot in-context learning (ICL) has emerged as a popular training-free adaptation strategy. By providing examples within the query context, ICL has demonstrated promising performance in practical applications(Brown et al., 2020)。 Few-shot的选择已被确定为下游任务性能的关键因素. The selection of few-shot has been identified as a critical factor for downstream task performance(Min et al., 2022).

现有的少量示例选择工作通常假设访问大型带注释的数据集，其中在推理时检索与测试数据最相似的示例会产生最佳性能Existing work on few-shot example selection typically assumes access to a large annotated dataset, where retrieving examples most similar to the test data at inference time yields the best performanceYu et al. (2023a); Margatina et al. (2023)。然而，构建用于测试时检索的大型注释数据集通常是不切实际的，特别是对于需要昂贵的专家级注释的专业领域。因此，在未标记数据设置下对少样本选择策略的探索仍然有限。最近的工作已经开始通过将主动学习（AL）引入小样本 ICL 来解决这一差距，即主动小样本学习（AFSL）. However, constructing large annotated datasets for test-time retrieval is oftentimes impractical, especially for specialized domains that require expensive expert-level annotations. Consequently, exploration of few-shot selection strategies under unlabeled data settings remains limited. Recent work has begun to address this gap by introducing active learning (AL) into few-shot ICL, i.e Active Few-Shot Learning (AFSL)Ahmadnia et al. (2025).

AFSL 将传统的 AL 技术扩展到少样本范例，从大型未标记池中选择最有可能产生强大测试时性能的样本，将其注释为 ICL 演示，为在保持性能的同时降低注释成本提供了一种有前途的解决方案AFSL extends traditional AL techniques to the few-shot paradigm, selecting the samples from a large unlabeled pool that are most likely to yield strong test-time performance, to be annotated as ICL demonstrations, providing a promising solution for reducing annotation costs while maintaining performanceXia et al. (2025); Ahmadnia et al. (2025)。 AFSL 方法在很大程度上遵循主流 AL 方法的直觉，即优先考虑高度不确定的样本来选择信息丰富的样本. AFSL methods largely follow the intuitions of mainstream AL approaches, which either prioritize highly uncertain samples to select informative samplesMeng et al. (2019); Gal et al. (2017)，确保所选集合中的语义多样性, ensure semantic diversity in the selected setYuan et al. (2020), or consider both Guo et al. (2024); Yu et al. (2023b); Hongjin et al. (2022).

局限性Limitations

尽管有效Despite the effectiveness of神经网络FSNeuFS，我们承认有一些限制。所提出的框架依赖于访问内部 FFN 激活和非嵌入矩阵，限制了其对开放权重模型而非黑盒 API 的适用性。此外，虽然我们采用两级过滤来提高效率，但与检索静态预计算嵌入相比，分析大型未标记池中的神经元模式会产生更高的计算开销。, we acknowledge several limitations.
