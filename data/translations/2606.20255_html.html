语域差距：尼日利亚公共话语的意义情报框架The Register Gap: A Meaning Intelligence Framework for Nigerian Public Discourse

塞莱斯廷·阿奇Celestine Achi

AGENTPR™ / 人工智能驱动的公关 / Cihan Digital AcademyAGENTPR™ / AI-Powered PR / Cihan Digital Academy

celestine.achi@gmail.com

摘要Abstract

我们引入了意义智能框架（MIF™），这是一种针对尼日利亚公共话语的九维注释和评估模式，可将表面情绪与真正的交流意图分开。尼日利亚语言的现有基准（包括 NaijaSenti 和 AfriSenti）将情绪分类视为三向极性任务（积极、消极、中性）。我们认为，人工智能系统在尼日利亚话语中的主要失败模式不是翻译失败，而是语境失败：同一个话语根据说话者、听众和情境的不同，具有相反的语用力量。 MIF 在九个评分维度上运用这一洞察：语域、表面情绪、真实意图、讽刺、编码潜台词、风险等级、注释者信心、演讲者情绪和推荐的沟通行动。我们构建了一个涵盖标准英语、尼日利亚英语、尼日利亚洋泾浜语和混合代码语域的 30 项校准数据集，并在零样本和模式通知提示条件下评估前沿语言模型 (Gemini 2.5 Flash)。主要发现是寄存器差距：零样本寄存器分类准确度为 33.3%，当模型在上下文中接收 MIF 模式时，该准确率上升至 73.3%（+40 分）。在图式提示下，综合意义智能得分增加了 5.4 分（73.2 至 78.6），其中在语域识别、编码潜台词检测（+10 分）和战略行动推荐（+10.3 分）方面的实际收益最大。我们还发现了一个动员盲点：该模型将伪装成幽默的动员信号错误分类为这两种情况下的常规警告，展示了一种对媒体监控和危机沟通产生直接后果的失败模式。我们发布了框架规范、注释指南和 30 项公共校准集以支持可重复性，同时保留用于防污染评估的私人保留语料库。We introduce the Meaning Intelligence Framework (MIF™), a nine-dimension annotation and evaluation schema for Nigerian public discourse that separates surface sentiment from true communicative intent. Existing benchmarks for Nigerian languages, including NaijaSenti and AfriSenti, treat sentiment classification as a three-way polarity task (positive, negative, neutral). We argue that the dominant failure mode of AI systems on Nigerian discourse is not translation failure but context failure: the same utterance carries opposite pragmatic force depending on speaker, audience, and situation. The MIF operationalises this insight across nine scored dimensions: register, surface sentiment, true intent, irony, coded subtext, risk tier, annotator confidence, speaker emotion, and recommended communications action. We construct a 30-item calibration dataset spanning Standard English, Nigerian English, Nigerian Pidgin, and code-mixed registers, and evaluate a frontier language model (Gemini 2.5 Flash) under zero-shot and schema-informed prompting conditions. The headline finding is the Register Gap: zero-shot register classification accuracy is 33.3%, rising to 73.3% (+40 points) when the model receives the MIF schema in-context. The composite Meaning Intelligence Score increases by 5.4 points (73.2 to 78.6) under schema-informed prompting, with the largest practical gains in register identification, coded-subtext detection (+10 points), and strategic action recommendation (+10.3 points). We also identify a mobilisation blind spot: the model misclassifies a mobilisation signal disguised as humour as a routine warning in both conditions, demonstrating a failure mode with direct consequences for media monitoring and crisis communications. We release the framework specification, annotation guidelines, and the 30-item public calibration set to support reproducibility, while retaining a private holdout corpus for contamination-protected evaluation.

一、简介1. Introduction

尼日利亚公共话语至少跨越四个语域：标准英语（SE）、尼日利亚英语（NE，其特点是英语语法中包含尼日利亚词汇，如 go-slow、K-leg、flashed me）、尼日利亚洋泾浜语（NP，一种具有自己语法和广泛影响力的克里奥尔语），以及在单个话语中交织其中两种或多种语言的代码混合语音（CM）。语域转换具有务实的重要性：说话者在句子中间从标准英语转换为洋泾浜语经常表示情绪升级、讽刺或团结——这些信息对于将所有尼日利亚语文本视为单一语言变体的模型来说是看不见的。Nigerian public discourse operates across at least four registers: Standard English (SE), Nigerian English (NE, characterised by English grammar with Nigerian lexical items such as go-slow, K-leg, flashed me), Nigerian Pidgin (NP, a creole with its own grammar and enormous reach), and code-mixed speech (CM) that interleaves two or more of these within a single utterance. Register shifts carry pragmatic weight: a speaker who moves from Standard English into Pidgin mid-sentence is frequently signalling emotional escalation, sarcasm, or solidarity — information that is invisible to models that treat all Nigerian text as a single linguistic variety.

考虑一下“你没有尽力”这句话。在毕业典礼上，这是真诚而热情的赞扬：演讲者承认持续的努力和成就。对于一位第三次归还仍存在故障的汽车的机械师来说，同样的话构成了讽刺性的谴责。这两种情况的表面情绪都是积极的。交际意图是截然相反的。任何使用相同情感标签标记两个实例的系统都无法理解其中任何一个。Consider the utterance “You don try well well.” At a graduation ceremony, this is genuine, effusive praise: the speaker acknowledges sustained effort and achievement. Addressed to a mechanic who has returned a car still faulty for the third time, the identical words constitute sarcastic condemnation. The surface sentiment is positive in both cases. The communicative intent is diametrically opposed. Any system that labels both instances with the same sentiment tag has not understood either one.

这是本工作的核心论文：前沿人工智能系统在尼日利亚公共话语中的主要失败模式不是翻译问题，而是语境问题。现有的尼日利亚 NLP 基准，尤其是 NaijaSenti（Muhammad 等人，2022）和 AfriSenti 的尼日利亚 Pidgin 组件（Muhammad 等人，2023），通过为资源匮乏的尼日利亚语言建立带注释的情感语料库，做出了基础性贡献。然而，这些基准在三向极性范式（积极、消极、中立）内运作，从设计上讲，这些范式无法捕捉上述的务实分歧。This is the core thesis of the present work: the dominant failure mode of frontier AI systems on Nigerian public discourse is not a translation problem but a context problem. Existing Nigerian NLP benchmarks, most notably NaijaSenti (Muhammad et al., 2022) and the Nigerian Pidgin component of AfriSenti (Muhammad et al., 2023), have made foundational contributions by establishing annotated sentiment corpora for low-resource Nigerian languages. However, these benchmarks operate within a three-way polarity paradigm (positive, negative, neutral) that, by design, cannot capture the pragmatic divergence illustrated above.

我们引入了意义智能框架 (MIF™)，这是一个弥补这一差距的九维注释和评估模式。 MIF 将表面情绪（字面的、上下文盲读所得出的结论）与真实意图（说话者在给出完整上下文的情况下的实际含义）分开，并将两者注释为独立维度，并与语域、反讽标记、编码潜台词、风险等级、注释者信心、说话者情绪和建议的沟通操作一起注释。签名诊断是发散的项目：表面情感和真实意图指向相反方向的话语，我们将其形式化为计算标志并用作专用评估指标。We introduce the Meaning Intelligence Framework (MIF™), a nine-dimension annotation and evaluation schema that addresses this gap. The MIF separates surface sentiment (what a literal, contextblind reading of the words would conclude) from true intent (what the speaker actually means given the full context), and annotates both as independent dimensions alongside register, irony markers, coded subtext, risk tier, annotator confidence, speaker emotion, and recommended communications action. The signature diagnostic is the divergent item: an utterance where surface sentiment and true intent point in opposite directions, which we formalise as a computed flag and use as a dedicated evaluation metric.

2.相关工作2. Related Work

2.1 尼日利亚语言基准2.1 Nigerian Language Benchmarks

NaijaSenti（Muhammad 等人，2022）为尼日利亚四种最广泛使用的语言引入了第一个大规模人工注释的 Twitter 情绪数据集，其中包括大约 14,000 条标记为三向极性的尼日利亚洋泾浜推文。 AfriSenti（Muhammad 等人，2023）将此扩展到 14 种非洲语言，拥有超过 110,000 条推文，包括尼日利亚洋泾浜语，并被用作 SemEval-2023 任务 12 的基础。 （2021）提出了尼日利亚洋泾浜语的语义丰富化，观察到像“ginger”（动机，不是植物）和“tank”（感激，不是容器）这样的词带有标准英语情感模型看不见的含义。最近，NaijaNLP (2025) 调查了尼日利亚资源匮乏的 NLP 的整体情况，对包括 SentiLeye 在内的数据集进行了编目，SentiLeye 是一种基于词典的情感分析资源，源自 346,000 条尼日利亚银行相关推文。NaijaSenti (Muhammad et al., 2022) introduced the first large-scale human-annotated Twitter sentiment dataset for the four most widely spoken languages in Nigeria, including approximately 14,000 Nigerian Pidgin tweets labelled for three-way polarity. AfriSenti (Muhammad et al., 2023) extended this to 14 African languages with over 110,000 tweets, including Nigerian Pidgin, and was used as the basis for SemEval-2023 Task 12. Oyewusi et al. (2021) proposed semantic enrichment for Nigerian Pidgin, observing that words like ‘ginger’ (motivation, not a plant) and ‘tank’ (gratitude, not a container) carry meanings invisible to standard English sentiment models. More recently, NaijaNLP (2025) surveyed the full landscape of Nigerian low-resource NLP, cataloguing datasets including SentiLeye, a lexicon-based sentiment analysis resource derived from 346,000 Nigerian banking-related tweets.

除了情感之外，赛义德等人。 (2024) 引入了尼日利亚洋泾浜的隐式话语关系分类，将 Penn Discourse Treebank 注释投影到合成 NP 数据上，并训练了一个专用分类器，该分类器在 F1 中比零样本英语基线高出 34%。这项工作表明，尼日利亚洋泾浜语的话语级 NLP 是可行的，但它解决了一个根本不同的任务：对句子之间的逻辑关系（原因、对比、连词）进行分类，而不是对单个话语的交际意图进行分类。 INJONGO（Yu 等人，2025）是第一个针对 16 种非洲语言的大规模多文化意图检测和槽位填充数据集，进一步进行了意图分类，但其意图分类由专为任务导向的对话系统设计的对话式 AI 动作（transfer、book_flight、play_music、make_call）组成，而不是 MIF 所采用的务实交际意图（PRAISE、SARC、MOBILIZE、GRIEVANCE_CODED）。地址。区别很重要：INJONGO 询问用户希望系统做什么；INJONGO 询问用户希望系统做什么；INJONGO 询问用户希望系统做什么。 MIF 询问说话者的实际含义。Beyond sentiment, Saeed et al. (2024) introduced Implicit Discourse Relation Classification for Nigerian Pidgin, projecting Penn Discourse Treebank annotations onto synthetic NP data and training a dedicated classifier that outperformed zero-shot English baselines by 34% in F1. This work demonstrates that discourse-level NLP for Nigerian Pidgin is viable, but it addresses a fundamentally different task: classifying logical relations between sentences (cause, contrast, conjunction) rather than the communicative intent of a single utterance. INJONGO (Yu et al., 2025), the first large-scale multicultural intent detection and slot-filling dataset for 16 African languages, takes intent classification further — but its intent taxonomy consists of conversational AI actions (transfer, book_flight, play_music, make_call) designed for task-oriented dialogue systems, not the pragmatic communicative intents (PRAISE, SARC, MOBILIZE, GRIEVANCE_CODED) that the MIF addresses. The distinction matters: INJONGO asks what a user wants the system to do; the MIF asks what a speaker actually means.

这些贡献共同建立了尼日利亚 NLP 的数据基础设施。 MIF 建立在这个基础上，但从根本上背离了它：MIF 不是问“这个文本是积极的、消极的还是中性的？”（NaijaSenti、AfriSenti），或者“什么话语关系链接这些句子？”（Saeed 等人），或者“用户想要完成什么任务？”（INJONGO），MIF 询问“说话者的实际含义是什么，以及传播专业人员应该对此做什么？”这种重新构建引入了维度（意图、讽刺、潜台词、风险、行动）没有现有的基准能够解决。These contributions collectively establish the data infrastructure for Nigerian NLP. The MIF builds on this foundation but departs from it in a fundamental way: rather than asking ‘is this text positive, negative, or neutral?’ (NaijaSenti, AfriSenti), or ‘what discourse relation links these sentences?’ (Saeed et al.), or ‘what task does the user want to accomplish?’ (INJONGO), the MIF asks ‘what does the speaker actually mean, and what should a communications professional do about it?’ This reframing introduces dimensions (intent, irony, subtext, risk, action) that no existing benchmark addresses.

2.2 讽刺和反讽检测2.2 Sarcasm and Irony Detection

讽刺检测是 NLP 的一个重要子领域，已建立的基准包括 MUStARD、SemEval-2018 Task 3 和 Reddit SARC 语料库。最近的工作探索了法学硕士的提示策略：李等人的语用元认知提示（PMP）。 (2024) 及其上下文感知扩展 (Iskandardinata et al., 2025) 证明检索增强提示可以将讽刺检测提高高达 9.87% 的宏 F1。 SarcasmBench 发现，GPT-4 在讽刺方面的表现不如有监督的微调较小模型，而且思想链提示实际上会损害性能，因为讽刺检测是一个整体认知过程，而不是一步一步的逻辑过程。Sarcasm detection is a substantial subfield of NLP, with established benchmarks including MUStARD, SemEval-2018 Task 3, and the Reddit SARC corpus. Recent work has explored prompting strategies for LLMs: Pragmatic Metacognitive Prompting (PMP) by Lee et al. (2024) and its contextaware extension (Iskandardinata et al., 2025) demonstrate that retrieval-augmented prompting can improve sarcasm detection by up to 9.87% macro-F1. SarcasmBench found that GPT-4 underperforms supervised fine-tuned smaller models on sarcasm, and that chain-of-thought prompting can actually hurt performance because sarcasm detection is a holistic cognitive process rather than a step-by-step logical one.

MIF 将讽刺视为更广泛的分析框架内的一个维度 (D4)，而不是作为一项独立的分类任务。至关重要的是，MIF 认识到尼日利亚话语中的讽刺通常是语域编码的：从标准英语到洋泾浜语的转换本身就是一种讽刺标记，而仅由英语或全球北方语料库构建的讽刺基准并未捕捉到这种现象。The MIF treats irony as one dimension (D4) within a broader analytical framework, rather than as a standalone classification task. Critically, the MIF recognises that sarcasm in Nigerian discourse is often register-encoded: the switch from Standard English into Pidgin is itself a sarcasm marker, a phenomenon not captured by sarcasm benchmarks constructed from English-only or Global North corpora.

2.3 非洲 NLP 的文化背景2.3 Cultural Context in African NLP

最近的几部作品强调了 NLP 评估中的文化差距。 AfroBench（Adelani 等人，2023）提供了对非洲语言的 15 个 NLP 任务的统一评估，但不包括语用或意图级任务。 AfriStereo（2025）从非洲的角度解决了法学硕士中的刻板偏见。 TriLex (2025) 提出了一种用于南非语言情感词典扩展的检索增强框架。奥钦等人。 (2025) 研究了资源匮乏、文化微妙的肯尼亚 WhatsApp 消息中的法学硕士情绪，指出“根据说话者的地区和文化背景，同一个短语可能带有积极、中性或消极的含义。”这一观察结果与 MIF 的基本原则直接一致，但 MIF 进一步将其操作为评分的、可重复的注释模式。Several recent works have highlighted the cultural gap in NLP evaluation. AfroBench (Adelani et al., 2023) provides a unified evaluation across 15 NLP tasks in African languages but does not include pragmatic or intent-level tasks. AfriStereo (2025) addresses stereotypical bias in LLMs from African perspectives. TriLex (2025) proposes a retrieval-augmented framework for sentiment lexicon expansion in South African languages. Ochieng et al. (2025) study LLM sentiment in low-resource, culturally nuanced Kenyan WhatsApp messages, noting that ‘the same phrase may carry positive, neutral, or negative connotations depending on the speaker’s region and cultural background.’ This observation aligns directly with the MIF’s foundational principle, but the MIF goes further by operationalising it into a scored, reproducible annotation schema.

3. 意义智能框架3. The Meaning Intelligence Framework

3.1 设计原则3.1 Design Principles

MIF 建立在三个原则之上：The MIF is built on three principles:

上下文规则。The Context Rule.如果没有先将尼日利亚语的表达方式与演讲者、听众和情况联系起来，就不要从字面上解释它。这不是指导方针；这是框架的基本公理，在校准测试期间违反它的注释者不会获得认证。Never interpret a Nigerian expression literally without first anchoring it to the speaker, the audience, and the situation. This is not a guideline; it is the framework’s foundational axiom, and annotators who violate it during calibration testing are not certified.

表面和意图是分开的。Surface and intent are separate.D2（表面情绪）的评分就像注释者没有上下文一样。 D3（真实意图）是根据完整的上下文进行评分的。当这些分歧时，该项目被标记为分歧 - 框架的签名诊断。D2 (surface sentiment) is scored as if the annotator had no context. D3 (true intent) is scored with full context. When these diverge, the item is flagged as divergent — the framework’s signature diagnostic.

可操作性。Actionability.该框架的最终输出不是一个标签，而是对传播专业人员的建议行动。维度 D6（风险层）和 D9（建议行动）将语言分析转化为操作智能。The framework’s ultimate output is not a label but a recommended action for a communications professional. Dimensions D6 (risk tier) and D9 (recommended action) translate linguistic analysis into operational intelligence.

3.4 意义智力分数3.4 The Meaning Intelligence Score

对于模型评估，我们将意义智力得分 (MIS™) 定义为每维度准确度的加权组合：For model evaluation, we define the Meaning Intelligence Score (MIS™) as a weighted composite of per-dimension accuracies:

MIS = 10·文字 + 25·语境 + 20·讽刺编码 + 15·情感 + 20·风险 + 10·行动MIS = 10·literal + 25·context + 20·sarcasm_coded + 15·emotion + 20·risk + 10·action

其中文字 = 平均值（D1，D2 准确性），上下文 = D3 准确性，sarcasm_coded = 平均值（D4，D5 准确性），情感 = D8 准确性，风险 = D6 准确性，操作 = D9 准确性。权重反映了框架的优先级：上下文解释（25%）和风险映射（20%）获得最高权重，因为它们代表了当前人工智能能力和人类专家表现之间的最大差距，并且对运营部署产生了最高的影响。where literal = mean(D1, D2 accuracy), context = D3 accuracy, sarcasm_coded = mean(D4, D5 accuracy), emotion = D8 accuracy, risk = D6 accuracy, and action = D9 accuracy. The weights reflect the framework’s priorities: contextual interpretation (25%) and risk mapping (20%) receive the highest weights because they represent the greatest gap between current AI capability and human expert performance, and carry the highest consequences for operational deployment.

5. 评价5. Evaluation

5.1 方法5.1 Method

我们在两种条件下对所有 30 个校准项目评估 Gemini 2.5 Flash（型号字符串 google/gemini-2.5-flash）：We evaluate Gemini 2.5 Flash (model string google/gemini-2.5-flash) on all 30 calibration items under two conditions:

条件 A（零样本）：Condition A (zero-shot):该模型仅接收每个维度的任务描述和标签清单，而没有 MIF 的解释指导（上下文规则、寄存器定义、意图类定义、反讽标记、潜台词代理测试或风险升级规则）。The model receives only the task description and the label inventories for each dimension, without the MIF’s interpretive guidance (the Context Rule, register definitions, intent class definitions, irony markers, the subtext proxy test, or risk escalation rules).

条件 B（基于模式）：Condition B (schema-informed):该模型接收完整的 MIF 指导作为系统提示，包括上下文规则、带有示例的寄存器定义、带有消歧指导的意图类定义（例如，戏谑/侮辱边界、保全面子/真诚希望的区别）、反讽标记、潜台词代理测试和风险等级标准。The model receives the full MIF guidance as a system prompt, including the Context Rule, register definitions with examples, intent class definitions with disambiguation guidance (e.g., the banter/insult boundary, the face-saving/genuine hope distinction), irony markers, the subtext proxy test, and risk-tier criteria.

两种条件都使用温度 0、单次传递，并将话语、上下文和先前回合作为用户消息呈现。该模型返回一个结构化 JSON 对象，其中包含每个维度的预测。Both conditions use temperature 0, single pass, with the utterance, context, and prior turn presented as the user message. The model returns a structured JSON object with predictions for each dimension.

5.4 分析5.4 Analysis

寄存器间隙。The Register Gap.基于模式的提示下最大的单一改进是寄存器分类：+40 个百分点（33.3% 至 73.3%）。零样本模型无法从代码混合文本中可靠地区分尼日利亚英语和洋泾浜语。由于语域转换在尼日利亚语话语中携带着实用信息（从 SE 到 NP 的滑动通常标志着情绪升级或讽刺），语域错误分类会将错误传播到下游维度。The largest single improvement under schema-informed prompting is register classification: +40 percentage points (33.3% to 73.3%). Zero-shot, the model cannot reliably distinguish Nigerian English from Pidgin from code-mixed text. Since register shifts carry pragmatic information in Nigerian discourse (a slide from SE to NP often marks emotional escalation or sarcasm), register misclassification propagates errors into downstream dimensions.

动员盲点。The Mobilisation Blind Spot.项目 CAL-021 是一个伪装成幽默的动员信号，引用了已知的抗议路口，在这两种情况下都被错误分类为警告。该模型正确地将项目识别为高风险（在各种条件下，高风险召回率为 100%），但无法识别机制：它在没有检测组织的情况下检测到了危险。在实时媒体监控中，这种区别将观察列表条目与危机激活区分开来。Item CAL-021, a mobilisation signal disguised as humour referencing a known protest junction, was misclassified as WARN in both conditions. The model correctly identified the item as HIGH risk (HIGH-risk recall was 100% across conditions), but could not identify the mechanism: it detected danger without detecting organisation. In live media monitoring, this distinction separates a watch-list entry from a crisis activation.

行动差距。The Action Gap.战略行动建议 (D9) 是最弱的实际维度，零样本率为 55.2%，在该模式下提高到 65.5%。该模型经常识别文本的含义，但不知道通信团队应该采取什么措施。 MIF 的层级到行动映射编码了有关升级阈值和响应协议的专家判断，为运营部署提供了最大的实际提升。Strategic action recommendation (D9) was the weakest practical dimension at 55.2% zero-shot, improving to 65.5% under the schema. The model frequently identifies what a text means without knowing what a communications team should do about it. The MIF’s tier-to-action mapping, which encodes expert judgment about escalation thresholds and response protocols, provides the largest practical uplift for operational deployment.

意图准确性。Intent accuracy.零样本意图准确度达到 86.7%，在 schemainformed 提示下保持不变。需要满足两项资格。首先，校准项目带有故意的诊断上下文描述，这些描述比现实世界社交媒体监控中通常可用的薄弱、嘈杂的上下文更丰富。其次，残余错误集中在不同的项目（模式下的准确度为 80% 到 90%）和风险最高的意图类别（MOBILIZE、GRIEVANCE_CODED）。包含 500 项真实世界数据的试点语料库将测试这种准确性在自然条件下是否成立。Zero-shot intent accuracy of 86.7% is notably high, unchanged under schemainformed prompting. Two qualifications apply. First, calibration items carry deliberately diagnostic context descriptions that are richer than the thin, noisy context typically available in real-world social media monitoring. Second, the residual errors concentrate on divergent items (80% to 90% accuracy under schema) and the highest-stakes intent classes (MOBILIZE, GRIEVANCE_CODED). The 500item pilot corpus of real-world data will test whether this accuracy holds under naturalistic conditions.

6. 讨论6. Discussion

MIF 填补了非洲 NLP 评估领域中几个服务良好地区之间的空白。在其下方，NaijaSenti 和 AfriSenti 大规模提供极性级别的情感注释。除此之外，INJONGO 还为面向任务的对话提供对话式 AI 意图检测，Saeed 等人。提供尼日利亚洋泾浜语的话语关系分类。除此之外，完整的话语分析和语用注释仍然是理论语言学的领域。对文献的系统调查证实，没有现有框架将表面情感与交际意图分开，作为尼日利亚语或任何非洲语言的独特评分维度，并且之前的工作也没有使用“意义智能”一词作为 NLP 中的命名概念。 MIF 占据了这个应用的中间地带：足够丰富，可以捕获对媒体情报和危机传播至关重要的实用现象；有足够的约束，可以由训练有素的（但不是专业语言学家）注释者进行可靠注释；并以产生用于模型比较的单一复合指标（MIS）的方式进行评分。The MIF addresses a gap in the African NLP evaluation landscape that sits between several wellserved areas. Below it, NaijaSenti and AfriSenti provide polarity-level sentiment annotation at scale. Alongside it, INJONGO provides conversational AI intent detection for task-oriented dialogue, and Saeed et al. provide discourse relation classification for Nigerian Pidgin. Above it, full discourse analysis and pragmatic annotation remain the domain of theoretical linguistics. A systematic survey of the literature confirms that no existing framework separates surface sentiment from communicative intent as distinct scored dimensions for Nigerian or any African language, and no prior work uses the term ‘Meaning Intelligence’ as a named concept in NLP. The MIF occupies this applied middle ground: rich enough to capture the pragmatic phenomena that matter for media intelligence and crisis communications, constrained enough to be annotated reliably by trained (but not specialist-linguist) annotators, and scored in a way that produces a single composite metric (the MIS) for model comparison.

该框架的商业动机——它旨在为媒体智能平台提供动力——是一个功能，而不是一个限制。 D6（风险）和 D9（行动）的包含使注释基于现实世界的后果。正确识别讽刺但无法推荐适当的沟通响应的模型在任何具有操作意义的意义上都没有完全理解文本。通过将行动建议纳入评分维度，MIF 不仅衡量理解力，还衡量判断力。The framework’s commercial motivation — it was designed to power a media intelligence platform — is a feature, not a limitation. The inclusion of D6 (risk) and D9 (action) grounds the annotation in real-world consequences. A model that correctly identifies sarcasm but fails to recommend the appropriate communications response has not fully understood the text in any operationally meaningful sense. By including action recommendation as a scored dimension, the MIF measures not just comprehension but judgment.

发散/欺骗性积极标志形式化了一种在情感分析研究中尚未受到足够重视的失败模式。欺骗性正面言论（自动情绪工具将其评分为赞扬，但实际上构成谴责）是品牌监控中风险最高的类别，正是因为它是最有可能被错过的类别。 MIF 使这种故障模式变得可见且可测量。The divergent/deceptive-positive flags formalise a failure mode that has received insufficient attention in sentiment analysis research. A deceptive positive — an utterance that automated sentiment tools score as praise but which actually constitutes condemnation — is the highest-risk category for brand monitoring precisely because it is the category most likely to be missed. The MIF makes this failure mode visible and measurable.

7. 局限性7. Limitations

这项工作有几个重要的限制，这些限制限制了我们提出的主张并定义了未来工作的路线图。This work has several important limitations that scope the claims we make and define the roadmap for future work.

单一模型、单一通道。该评估涵盖两种条件下的一个前沿模型，温度为 0 时单次通过。正式协议要求在发布命名模型比较之前进行三轮多数投票并评估至少两个其他模型系列（GPT 类和 Claude 类）。我们以名称来报告 Gemini 2.5 Flash，因为这是方法论预印本，而不是模型排名。Single model, single pass. The evaluation covers one frontier model under two conditions with a single pass at temperature 0. The formal protocol calls for three-run majority voting and evaluation of at least two additional model families (GPT-class and Claude-class) before named model comparisons are published. We report Gemini 2.5 Flash by name because this is a methodological preprint, not a model ranking.

构建校准项目。包含 30 项的校准集使用编写的上下文-话语对以及丰富、明确的上下文描述。现实世界的社交媒体数据提供了更稀疏、更嘈杂的背景，我们预计在这些条件下零样本准确性会降低。校准集旨在用于注释者认证和框架验证，而不是作为尼日利亚话语的代表性样本。Constructed calibration items. The 30-item calibration set uses authored context-utterance pairs with rich, unambiguous context descriptions. Real-world social media data offers thinner, noisier context, and we expect zero-shot accuracy to degrade under those conditions. The calibration set is designed for annotator certification and framework validation, not as a representative sample of Nigerian discourse.

金色标签的单一注释器。金色标签由框架设计者分配。虽然这确保了框架设计意图的内部一致性，但它并没有建立注释者之间的协议。正式的注释协议要求三重注释和裁决；一旦注释团队获得认证并且 500 项试点语料库被标记，将报告注释者间协议统计数据。Single annotator for gold labels. Gold labels were assigned by the framework designer. While this ensures internal consistency with the framework’s design intent, it does not establish inter-annotator agreement. The formal annotation protocol calls for triple annotation with adjudication; inter-annotator agreement statistics will be reported once the annotation team is certified and the 500-item pilot corpus is labelled.

8. 道德考虑8. Ethical Considerations

校准数据集使用构建的示例而不是真实的社交媒体帖子，这避免了与复制可识别的用户生成内容相关的隐私问题。所有项目均旨在说明话语模式，而不针对真实的个人、品牌或社区。注释指南包括明确规定，禁止使用该框架来针对种族或宗教群体，并且带有种族、宗教或安全潜台词 (D5 = ETH/REL/SEC) 标记的项目会触发强制人工审查。The calibration dataset uses constructed examples rather than real social media posts, which avoids privacy concerns associated with reproducing identifiable user-generated content. All items are designed to be illustrative of discourse patterns without targeting real individuals, brands, or communities. The annotation guidelines include explicit provisions against using the framework to target ethnic or religious groups, and items flagged with ethnic, religious, or security subtext (D5 = ETH/REL/SEC) trigger mandatory human review.

该基准的私人坚持部分是在严格的访问控制和保密协议下维护的，以防止评估结果受到污染。我们发布了 30 项公共校准集和完整的框架规范，以支持可重复性，同时保护正式评估的完整性。The private holdout component of the benchmark is maintained under strict access controls and non-disclosure agreements to prevent contamination of evaluation results. We release the 30-item public calibration set and the complete framework specification to support reproducibility while protecting the integrity of formal evaluation.

9. 结论和未来工作9. Conclusion and Future Work

我们引入了意义情报框架，这是一种针对尼日利亚公共话语的九维注释和评估模式，它超越了情感极性，捕捉语域、意图、讽刺、编码潜台词、风险、情感和建议的行动。该框架的核心见解——人工智能在尼日利亚语话语中的失败是上下文失败，而不是翻译失败——得到了语域差距发现的验证：当前沿模型在上下文中接收 MIF 模式时，语域分类准确性提高了 40 个点，无需任何微调。We have introduced the Meaning Intelligence Framework, a nine-dimension annotation and evaluation schema for Nigerian public discourse that goes beyond sentiment polarity to capture register, intent, irony, coded subtext, risk, emotion, and recommended action. The framework’s core insight — that AI failures on Nigerian discourse are context failures, not translation failures — is validated by the Register Gap finding: a 40-point improvement in register classification accuracy when a frontier model receives the MIF schema in-context, without any fine-tuning.

未来的工作将沿着四个轨道进行：（1）一个包含 500 项真实世界尼日利亚语话语的试点语料库，具有三重注释和注释者间协议报告； （2）三轮多数投票协议下的正式多模型评估； （3）在MIF注释数据上微调小语言模型，以衡量提示和训练之间的差距； (4) 将框架扩展到相关的西非洋泾浜语和代码混合变种。Future work proceeds along four tracks: (1) a 500-item pilot corpus of real-world Nigerian discourse with triple annotation and inter-annotator agreement reporting; (2) formal multi-model evaluation under the three-run majority-voting protocol; (3) fine-tuning of a small language model on MIF-annotated data to measure the gap between prompting and training; and (4) extension of the framework to related West African Pidgins and code-mixed varieties.
