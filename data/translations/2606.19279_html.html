摘要Abstract

神经符号语义是支离破碎的：经典的、模糊的、概率的和神经系统都通过自己的归纳规则来定义真理。 NeSyCat 扩展了 ULLER，将它们纳入真理的单一归纳定义、强单子的参数化和真值的聚合结构中。 NeSyCat 迄今为止还缺乏对神经网络学习的谓词和函数的描述。我们提供Neurosymbolic semantics is fragmented: classical, fuzzy, probabilistic and neural systems each define truth by their own inductive rules. NeSyCat, extending ULLER, subsumes them under a single inductive definition of truth, parametric in a strong monad and an aggregation structure on truth-values. NeSyCat has so far lacked an account of predicates and functions learned by neural networks. We use the distribution monad for reference semantics and metric evaluation, and complement it by a monad for numerically stable, differentiable training: the lazy log-tensor monad over the log-semiring. For efficient training in batches, we furthermore employ a batch monad.
