编码器就足够了吗？ LLM对抗性评估的编码器和解码器安全法官的系统比较Do Encoders Suffice? A Systematic Comparison of Encoder and Decoder Safety Judges for LLM Adversarial Evaluation

Han Jeon    Shiv Medler    Joseph Voyles    Matt Wood
摘要Abstract

随着大型语言模型 (LLM) 在聊天机器人和日常应用程序中的广泛采用，公司正在寻求低延迟、低成本但有效的护栏。法学硕士输出的安全评估通常依赖于基于法学硕士的法官，这种法官虽然有效，但速度缓慢且规模昂贵。在本文中，我们评估了来自 ModernBERT 系列（ModernBERT、Ettin）的经过微调的现代编码器分类器是否能够可靠地识别用户模型对话中有害的 LLM 输出，而相对于基于 LLM 的判断器，不会造成显着的性能损失。我们将这些编码器分类器与基于规则的前缀匹配、微调的 LLM 分类器和 LLM 法官在开源对抗数据集中使用不同的判断提示策略进行基准测试。 LLM 评委包括来自 StrongReject、ShieldGemma、JailbreakBench、AILuminate、SorryBench、Claude-as-a-judge 的评估方法，以及包括 LlamaGuard 3 和 4 在内的微调安全分类器。编码器分类器在多数投票标签策略下对评委标记的数据进行微调，然后根据黄金坚持数据集进行评估，以评估他们与 LLM 评委相比的表现。绝对性能通过 F1、假阴性率和精确召回率报告。我们通过攻击技术分解结果，特别是单轮提示、分解、升级和上下文操作，以确定编码器分类器与 LLM 法官的一致或分歧。这些发现为编码器分类器何时可以作为基于 LLM 的评估的成本和延迟高效替代方案提供了指导。With the widespread adoption of large language models (LLMs) in chatbots and everyday applications, companies are seeking guardrails that are low on latency and cost but effective. Safety evaluation of LLM outputs has generally relied on LLM-based judges which are effective but slow and expensive at scale. In this paper, we evaluate whether fine-tuned modern encoder classifiers from the ModernBERT family (ModernBERT, Ettin) can reliably identify harmful LLM outputs in user-model conversations without significant performance loss relative to LLM-based judges. We benchmark these encoder classifiers against rules-based prefix matching, fine-tuned LLM classifiers, and LLM judges with varying judge prompt strategies across open-source adversarial datasets. The LLM judges include evaluation methodologies from StrongReject, ShieldGemma, JailbreakBench, AILuminate, SorryBench, a Claude-as-a-judge, and fine-tuned safety classifiers including LlamaGuard 3 and 4. The encoder classifiers are fine-tuned on judge-labeled data under a majority-voting label strategy, then evaluated against a golden holdout dataset to assess how they performed in comparison to the LLM judges. Absolute performance is reported via F1, false negative rate, and precision-recall. We break down results by attack techniques, specifically single-turn prompting, decomposition, escalation, and context manipulation to identify where encoder classifiers align with or diverge from LLM judges. The findings offer guidance on when encoder classifiers can serve as a cost and latency efficient alternative to LLM-based evaluation.

1 简介及相关作品Introduction and Related Works

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地部署在各种重要环境中，包括银行聊天机器人、航空公司客户服务和其他面向客户的应用程序；然而，这些法学硕士很容易受到对抗性提示和越狱的影响（Chao et al., 2024）。随着这些系统的复杂性和重要性不断增加，从业者必须平衡护栏有效性、API 成本和延迟（Wang 等人，2023a）。目前，主流范式在很大程度上依赖于基于解码器的安全判断：要么使用 LLM 作为判断提示的大型自回归模型（Zheng 等人，2023），要么使用较小的微调解码器，例如 LlamaGuard（Meta AI，2024）。虽然这两种方法都很有效，但这些方法都存在与推理成本和延迟相关的相同问题，并且在分布变化下性能可能会下降（Schwinn 等人，2026），导致无法泛化训练期间未见的新攻击模式。虽然多通道策略已被证明可以提高解码器在指令跟踪（Deng 等人，2023）和推理（Wang 等人，2023b）方面的性能，但它们进一步强调，解码器本身并不能够像双向编码器设计那样提供完整性和理解性来处理上下文。Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly being deployed in various significant settings including banking chatbots, airline customer service, and other customer-facing applications; however, these LLMs are susceptible to adversarial prompts and jailbreak (Chao et al., 2024). Practitioners must balance guardrail effectiveness, API cost, and latency as these systems grow in complexity and criticality (Wang et al., 2023a). Currently, the dominant paradigm relies heavily on decoder-based safety judges: either large autoregressive models used with LLM-as-a-judge prompts (Zheng et al., 2023) or smaller fine-tuned decoder such as LlamaGuard (Meta AI, 2024). While both are effective, these approaches have the same problems related to cost and latency of inferences, and performance can degrade under distribution shifts (Schwinn et al., 2026) leading to failures to generalize against novel attack patterns unseen during training. While multi-pass strategies have been shown to improve decoder performance on instruction-following (Deng et al., 2023) and reasoning (Wang et al., 2023b), they further highlight that decoders do not natively process context with the completeness and understanding that bidirectional encoders provide by design.

安全分类从根本上来说是一项语言理解任务，因为确定响应是否有害需要阅读和理解完整的输入，而不是生成新的标记。我们认为这使得判别式编码器架构成为一种自然的选择，因为编码器在单次传递中双向处理整个输入，捕获整个上下文中的有害意图，而无需自回归解码的顺序从左到右的约束。最近的工作证实，编码器在分类任务上优于较大的仅解码器模型（Weller 等人，2026），而 ModernBERT（Warner 等人，2024）对这种架构重新产生了兴趣，因为它具有扩展的上下文窗口和现代预训练。因此，我们研究了 ModernBERT 模型家族在对抗性安全评估中的表现，这些模型在问题和答案中都有不明确的模式和指导方针。本文通过对七个安全法官通过多数投票聚合的标签上的 ModernBERT 系列编码器 (Ettin) 进行微调来解决这一差距，然后根据四个前沿模型基线在保留的基准上评估生成的分类器。Safety classification is fundamentally a language understanding task since determining whether a response is harmful requires reading and comprehending the full input rather than generating new tokens. We argue this makes discriminative encoder architectures a natural fit because encoders process the entire input bidirectionally in a single pass, capturing harmful intent across the full context without the sequential left-to-right constraints of autoregressive decoding. Recent work confirms encoders outperform larger decoder-only models on classification tasks (Weller et al., 2026), and ModernBERT (Warner et al., 2024) has renewed interest in this architecture as it comes with extended context windows and modern pretraining. Thus we look at how the family of ModernBERT models perform in adversarial safety evaluation which have ambiguous patterns and guidelines from both questions and answers. This paper addresses that gap by fine-tuning a ModernBERT-family encoder (Ettin) on labels aggregated from seven safety judges via majority vote, then evaluating the resulting classifier on a held-out benchmark against four frontier model baselines.

1.1 法学硕士作为法官和基于解码器的安全评估LLM-as-a-Judge and Decoder-Based Safety Evaluation

最近的安全评估工作大致分为两类：综合基准框架和独立数据集。 StrongReject (Souly et al., 2024)、SorryBench (Xie et al., 2025) 和 HarmBench (Mazeika et al., 2024) 等基准将提示集与内置评估管道相结合，而 AdvBench (Zou et al., 2023)、ToxicChat (Lin et al., 2023)、BeaverTails 等数据集(Ji et al., 2023) 和 XSTest (Röttger et al., 2023) 提供对抗性提示和注释，但没有固定的评估协议。Recent safety evaluation work broadly falls into two categories: integrated benchmark frameworks and standalone datasets. Benchmarks such as StrongReject (Souly et al., 2024), SorryBench (Xie et al., 2025), and HarmBench (Mazeika et al., 2024) combine prompt sets with built-in evaluation pipelines, while datasets like AdvBench (Zou et al., 2023), ToxicChat (Lin et al., 2023), BeaverTails (Ji et al., 2023), and XSTest (Röttger et al., 2023) provide adversarial prompts and annotations without a fixed evaluation protocol.

在这两个类别中，LLM 作为法官范式（Zheng 等人，2023）已成为默认评分机制：灵活且上下文敏感，但规模昂贵。在这项工作中，我们使用 StrongReject、SorryBench 和 HarmBench 的判断方法作为训练信号，并根据 JailbreakBench 和 AILuminate 的真实保留标签进行评估。Across both categories, the LLM-as-a-judge paradigm (Zheng et al., 2023) has emerged as the default scoring mechanism: flexible and context-sensitive, but expensive at scale. In this work, we use judge methodologies from StrongReject, SorryBench, and HarmBench as training signals, and evaluate against ground-truth holdout labels from JailbreakBench and AILuminate.

解决安全分类问题的另一种方法是使用微调的解码器分类器。有几种变体，包括 LlamaGuard（Meta AI，2024）、Qwen3Guard（阿里巴巴，2024）和 ShieldGemma（Google，2024），它们是自回归模型，可根据定义的危害分类法生成安全标签。这些模型受益于微调期间的指令，并学会以数十亿个参数为代价处理多轮对话上下文。这会导致显着的推理延迟，并且需要随着攻击风格的演变而反复微调。在我们的评估中，我们利用 LlamaGuard 3 和 4 作为训练信号判断器和结果基线。Another approach to the safety classification problem is to use fine-tuned decoder classifiers. There are several variants including LlamaGuard (Meta AI, 2024), Qwen3Guard (Alibaba, 2024), and ShieldGemma (Google, 2024) that are autoregressive models that produce safety labels based on defined harm taxonomies. These models benefit from instructions during fine tuning and learned to handle multi-turn conversational context at the cost of being multi-billion parameters. This causes significant inference latency and requires repeated fine-tuning as attack styles evolve. In our evaluation, we leverage LlamaGuard 3 and 4 as training-signal judges and baselines in our results.

2.2 泄漏预防和数据集分区Leakage Prevention and Dataset Partitioning

我们的推理管道中的 16 个目标模型中的每一个都会对语料库中的每个问题产生响应，这意味着相同的对抗性问题在数据集中出现最多 16 次，每个模型出现一次，并且进一步跨越不同的攻击技术和配置。单纯的行级随机分割是不够的，因为模型 A 对训练集中问题的响应可能是欺骗模型 B 对测试集中同一问题的响应的先兆。由于分类器可以学习问题级特征，因此这会导致从训练到评估的数据泄漏。该模型正在执行记忆任务，而不是真正的响应级别有害性检测。Each of the 16 target models in our inference pipeline produces a response to every question in the corpus, meaning the same adversarial question appears up to 16 times the dataset, once per model, and further across different attack techniques and configurations. A naïve row-level random split would be insufficient, as model A’s response to a question in the training set would potentially be a precursor to also fooling model B’s response to the same question in the test set. Because the classifier can learn question-level features, this introduces data leakage from training to evaluation. The model is performing a memorization task rather than a true response-level harmfulness detection.

5 评估协议Evaluation Protocol

我们的编码器分类器是根据七名具有不同设计理念的法官汇总的多数票标签进行训练的，这意味着训练信号是不同有害概念的混合体。评估协议旨在首先表征混合信号的结构，然后测量分类器如何有效地从中学习。Our encoder classifiers are trained on majority-vote labels aggregated from seven judges with varying design philosophies, meaning the training signal is a blend of different notions of harmfulness. The evaluation protocol is designed to first characterize the structure of that blended signal then measure how effectively classifiers learn from it.

分类器性能。Classifier performance.

我们对 OOD 保留集（JailbreakBench + AILuminate；跨 4 个前沿模型的 42,899 行）进行评估。保留集带有来自原始基准作者的真实标签，因此评估根据人类裁决的注释来衡量分类器的准确性，而不是根据用于训练的多数投票信号。坚持测试同时概括了三个维度：数据集变化，因为这两个基准都没有出现在训练中；模型转变，因为坚持模型包括训练池组装后发布的前沿系统；和评估一致性，因为法官遵循官方基准协议，可以与已发布的攻击成功率进行直接比较。We evaluate on an OOD holdout set (JailbreakBench + AILuminate; 42,899 rows across 4 frontier models). The holdout tests generalize across three dimensions simultaneously: dataset shift, since neither benchmark appears in training; model shift, since the holdout models include frontier systems released after the training pool was assembled; and evaluation alignment, since judges follow official benchmark protocols, enabling direct comparison with published attack success rates.

6.2 多轮攻击下的失效模式Failure Modes Under Multi-Turn Attacks

基于编码器的分类器始终难以应对多轮攻击，尤其是分解攻击，其中有害意图分布在中间步骤中，并且只有在跨轮聚合时才变得明显。由于分类器仅观察最终的（问题、响应）对，因此它永远不会看到中间交互，其中有害意图被分散在其他良性子查询中。尽管编码器模型处理最终交换中可用的完整上下文窗口，但它们缺乏对产生响应的推理路径的直接可观察性。这种限制反映在结果中，其中分解攻击在评估的技术中产生最高的假阴性率。增加模型大小可以部分缓解该问题：400M 编码器将整体误报率从 42.2% 降低到 14.6%，更值得注意的是，分解攻击时从 50.6% 降低到 11.6%。这些结果表明，即使没有显式访问中间回合，较大的编码器模型对于分布式损害模式也更加稳健。Encoder-based classifiers consistently struggle with multi-turn attacks, particularly decomposition, where harmful intent is distributed across intermediate steps and only becomes apparent when aggregated across turns. Because the classifier observes only the final (question, response) pair, it never sees the intermediate interactions in which harmful intent is fragmented across otherwise benign sub-queries. Although the encoder models process the full context window available in the final exchange, they lack direct observability into the reasoning path that produced the response. This limitation is reflected in the results, where decomposition attacks produce the highest false negative rates among the evaluated techniques. Increasing model size partially mitigates the issue: the 400M encoder reduces the overall false negative rate from 42.2% to 14.6%, and more notably from 50.6% to 11.6% on decomposition attacks. These results suggest that larger encoder models are more robust to distributed harm patterns even without explicit access to intermediate turns.

虽然 150M 模型实现了更高的总体 F1，但 400M 模型在所有攻击类型中始终实现了更低的漏报率。最显着的改进发生在分解攻击上，模型从错过大约一半的有害响应转变为检测到近 90%。然而，这仍然是基于编码器的方法的基本限制。现代越狱技术越来越依赖于多回合策略，例如快速分解、升级和上下文操纵，其中有害意图只有通过交互中上下文的积累才会出现。由于编码器分​​类器对固定的分类输入而不是完整的对话进行操作，因此它们可能无法准确评估需要对对话轮次或用户意图进行推理的复杂攻击。因此，基于编码器的分类器最好被视为高效的一线过滤器，而成本较高的基于解码器的判断器或轨迹感知系统可能仍然需要对高级多轮越狱进行综合评估。附录 A 提供了按技术分类的完整评估结果。While the 150M model achieves higher overall F1, the 400M model consistently achieves lower false negative rates across all attack types. The most significant improvement occurs for decomposition attacks, where the model transitions from missing roughly half of harmful responses to detecting nearly 90%. Nevertheless, this remains a fundamental limitation of encoder-based approaches. Modern jailbreak techniques increasingly rely on multi-turn strategies such as prompt decomposition, escalation, and context manipulation, where harmful intent emerges only through the accumulation of context across interactions. Because encoder classifiers operate on a fixed classification input rather than the full conversation, they may fail to accurately assess sophisticated attacks that require reasoning over conversation turns or user intent. As a result, encoder-based classifiers are best viewed as efficient first-line filters, while higher-cost decoder-based judges or trajectory-aware systems may still be necessary for comprehensive evaluation of advanced multi-turn jailbreaks. Full evaluation results by technique are provided in Appendix A.

7 结论Conclusion

我们首次对微调编码器分类器作为安全判断进行了大规模评估，这些评估以 563,216 个 q-a 对中基于解码器的方法为基准，其中包括 16 个目标模型、14 个数据集和四种攻击技术变体。经过微调的 Ettin 分类器在 OOD 保留集上的表现优于几个明显更大的安全判断器（例如 LlamaGuard-3/4、Claude Judge、StrongReject），同时仍低于最强的基准对齐评估器（AILuminate、SorryBench）。这些结果表明，在将更困难的问题传递给基于解码器的模型之前，编码器可以用作安全分类的首轮过滤器。从成本和延迟的角度来看，编码器分类器比 LlamaGuard 3 和 4 以及一些 LLM-as-a-judge 策略更便宜、更有效。未来的工作将扩展到对人类策划的数据集进行测试，因为法官间的一致性相当低（Fleiss 的We present the first large-scale evaluation of fine-tuned encoder classifiers as safety judges, which are benchmarked against decoder-based approaches in 563,216 q-a pairs encompassing 16 target models, 14 datasets, and four variations of attack techniques. The finetuned Ettin classifier outperformed several safety judges that were significantly larger (e.g., LlamaGuard-3/4, Claude Judge, StrongReject) on the OOD holdout set, while remaining below the strongest benchmark-aligned evaluators (AILuminate, SorryBench). These results indicate that encoders can be used as a first-pass filter to safety classification before passing on more difficult problems to decoder-based models. From a cost and latency perspective, encoder classifiers are significantly cheaper and more effective than LlamaGuard 3 and 4 and some LLM-as-a-judge strategies.
