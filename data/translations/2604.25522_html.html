摘要Abstract

人类生命的开始可以通过两种彩票来表征：你的基因（先天）和你出生的家庭（后天）。这些因素从出生起就开启了健康和人力资本的轨迹。利用三个纵向社会科学数据集，我们系统地分析了个体的基因型、他们成长的家庭的社会经济地位（SES）以及他们在成年后意识到的特征之间的关系。我们通过多基因指数（PGI）来代理个人的遗传倾向，并通过父母教育和父亲（前）职业地位的潜在因素来代理家庭社会经济地位。然后，我们研究 PGI、父母 SES 及其相互作用如何影响 45 种社会经济、人体测量、健康、行为和人格特征的晚年生活结果。我们发现这些表型与遗传和社会经济有很强的关联，但没有证据表明存在相当大的基因-环境相互作用。The start of a human’s life can be characterized by two lotteries: that of your genes (nature) and the family you were born into (nurture). These set in motion a trajectory, from birth onward, in health and human capital. Leveraging three longitudinal socialscience data sets, we systematically analyze the relationship between an individual’s genotype, the socioeconomic status (SES) of the families they grew up in, and their realized traits in adulthood. We proxy an individual’s genetic predisposition by polygenic indexes (PGIs) and family SES by a latent factor of parental education and father’s (former) occupational status. We then investigate how PGIs, parental SES, and their interaction contribute to later-life outcomes across a range of forty-five socioeconomic, anthropometric, health, behavioral, and personality traits. We find strong genetic and socioeconomic associations with these phenotypes, but no evidence of sizable gene-environment interactions.

1 简介1 Introduction

家庭在塑造人生机会方面发挥着至关重要的作用，优势的强大代际传递就证明了这一点[1][–][11]。事实上，人类生命的开始可以通过两种彩票来表征：一种分配基因（先天），另一种分配养育环境（后天）[12][,][13]。两者都通过家庭关系运作，并且都对生活机会产生重大影响。双胞胎研究表明，对于多种性状，个体之间约 30% 至 70% 的变异可归因于遗传学差异[14]。然而，在这个范围内的遗传力估计意味着，虽然基因很重要，但环境也发挥着重要作用。一个特别重要的环境因素是父母的社会经济地位（SES），即父母根据教育、职业、收入和相关特征所占据的社会地位。父母的社会经济地位与家庭的经济资源及其投资于孩子的发展和福祉的能力密切相关。大量文献研究了家庭社会经济地位如何影响儿童的早期生活状况以及随后的成年结局[15][–][20]。Families play an essential role in shaping life chances, as evidenced by the strong intergenerational transmission of advantage[1][–][11] . Indeed, the start of a human’s life can be characterized by two lotteries: one assigning genes (nature), and the other assigning a rearing environment (nurture)[12][,][13] . Both operate through familial ties, and each has a substantial influence on life chances. Twin studies suggest that for a wide variety of traits, some 30 to 70 percent of the variation between individuals can be attributed to differences in genetics[14] . However, heritability estimates in this range imply that while genes matter, environments also play an important role. One particularly important environmental factor is parental socioeconomic status (SES), the social position that parents occupy based on education, occupation, income, and related characteristics. Parental SES is strongly tied to a family’s economic resources, and their ability to invest in the development and well-being of their children. A large literature studies how familial SES shapes early-life conditions and the subsequent adult outcomes of children[15][–][20] .

We systematically analyze the relationship between the offspring’s (henceforth, the child’s) genetic propensity towards a trait (genotype), the socioeconomic status (SES) of the families they grew up in when they were children, and their actual trait in adulthood (phenotype).

HRS 是一项纵向家庭调查，提供约 26,000 人的丰富数据，代表 50 岁以上的美国人口。从 1992 年到 2016 年，每位受访者最多可获得 13 波数据。我们使用公开的 HRS 核心调查和 2006 年、2008 年和 2010 年的关联遗传数据。HRS 核心调查每两年进行一次，采用面对面和电话访谈相结合的方式。The HRS is a longitudinal household survey providing rich data on about 26,000 individuals, representative of the U.S. population over the age of 50. Up to 13 waves of data per respondent are available, from 1992–2016. We use the publicly available HRS core survey and linked genetic data for the years 2006, 2008, and 2010. HRS core surveys are conducted biennially using a combination of face-to-face and telephone interviewing.

WLS 是一项对 1957 年从威斯康星州高中毕业的 10,317 名男性和女性样本进行的长期研究。调查数据是从 1957 年、1964 年、1975 年、1992 年、2004 年和 2011 年从原始受访者或其父母那里收集的。The WLS is a long-term study of a sample of 10,317 men and women who graduated from Wisconsin high schools in 1957. Survey data were collected from the original respondents or their parents in 1957, 1964, 1975, 1992, 2004, and 2011.

ELSA 是一项针对 50 岁及以上英国人口的健康、社会、福祉和经济状况动态的研究。自 2002 年启动以来，已有超过 18,000 人参与了这项研究，并且每两年对同一个人进行一次重新采访。ELSA is a study on the dynamics of health, social, well-being, and economic circumstances in the English population aged 50 and older. More than 18,000 people have taken part in the study since it started in 2002, with the same people re-interviewed every two years.

4.5 统计方法4.5 Statistical Methods

我们使用 HRS、ELSA 和 WLS 的汇总数据估计表型、多基因指数 (PGI)、社会经济地位 (SES) 之间的关系以及 SES 和 PGI 之间的相互作用。该模型通过 OLS 对合并样本进行估计，包括数据集固定效应以及年龄、性别和前 10 个遗传主成分的控制。在估计之前，表型被标准化为均值为零和方差为一。完整 SNP PGI 和 SES 因子也经过标准化，均值为 0，方差为 1。作为稳健性检查，我们还分别估计每个数据集的模型，并使用聚合全基因组重要 SNP 的顶级 SNP PGI。仅限于全基因组显着 SNP 的 PGI 通常缺乏功效，因此不会呈现。We estimate the relationship between phenotypes, polygenic indices (PGIs), socioeconomic status (SES), and the interaction between SES and PGI using pooled data from HRS, ELSA, and WLS. The model is estimated by OLS on the pooled sample and includes dataset fixed effects, as well as controls for age, sex, and the first 10 genetic principal components. Prior to estimation, phenotypes are standardized to have mean zero and variance one. The Full SNPs PGI and the SES factor are also standardized to have mean zero and variance one. As a robustness check, we also estimated the models separately for each dataset and using the top SNPs PGI aggregating the genome-wide significant SNPs. PGIs restricted to genome-wide significant SNPs generally lacked power and are therefore not presented.

B.1 GWAS目录和选择标准B.1 GWAS catalog and criteria for selection

为了为本研究选择最相关和最强大的 GWAS，我们使用了以下方法。首先，我们查阅了 GWAS 目录（To select the most relevant and powerful GWASs for this study we used the following methodology. First, we consulted the GWAS Catalog (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/downloads; 2019年5月30日版本）[73]，其中包含776个GWAS。其次，我们根据文献将所有 GWAS 分为 11 个主要性状家族[74]。第三，我们使用以下标准来过滤掉不适合我们研究的 GWAS：; version of 30 May 2019)[73] , which contained 776 GWASs. Second, we divided all GWASs into 11 major trait families based on the literature[74] . Third, we used the following criteria to filter out GWASs not suited for our study:

  • 全基因组显着 SNP 计数低（关联计数Low genome-wide significant SNP count (Association count< 6).

  • 更多相关特征可用。More relevant trait available.

  • 患病率低。Low prevalence.

  • 不在 HRS 中。Not in HRS.

  • 非欧洲样品。Non-European sample.

  • 存在更大的 GWAS（针对此性状）。Larger GWAS (for this trait) exists.

  • 使用的其他研究/措施。Other study/measure used.

我们尽可能使过滤步骤可量化。然而，在确定特定特征与我们研究的相关性时，我们必须依赖主观评估。例如，当我们使用主要的“冠状动脉疾病”和主要的“I型糖尿病”表型 GWAS 来代替“冠状动脉疾病、I 型糖尿病”时，我们将一个特征标记为“更多相关的 GWAS 可用”。We made the filter steps quantifiable when possible. However, in determining the relevance of a specific trait to our study we had to rely on subjective assessment. We labeled a trait as “More relevant GWAS available” when, for instance, instead of “coronary artery disease, type I diabetes mellitus” we used the main “coronary artery disease” and main “type I diabetes” phenotype GWAS.

鉴于我们有三个数据集来测试 PGI，该特征也必须存在于这些数据集中。因此，对于这个初始过滤，我们通过标记“不在 HRS 中”来过滤掉不在 HRS 中的特征。此过滤步骤基于 Google 搜索术语“健康与退休研究”并结合特征。Given that we had three datasets to test the PGI in, the trait also had to be present in those datasets. For example, we included the phenotype ‘obesity’ because we assessed that this phenotype provides information on top of what ‘BMI’ provides.
