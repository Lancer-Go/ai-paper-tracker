1 简介Introduction

联邦学习 (FL) 通过聚合本地优化的模型更新，实现跨机构的协作模型训练，而无需交换本地数据Federated Learning (FL) enables collaborative model training across institutions without exchanging local data, by aggregating locally optimized model updates[rieke2020future]。 在医学成像中，FL 利用多机构数据数量和多样性来提高鲁棒性和泛化性，同时尊重隐私和治理约束. In medical imaging, FL leverages multi-institutional data quantity and diversity to improve robustness and generalization while respecting privacy and governance constraints[Sheller2020FederatedLI]，随着放射学领域越来越多的转化证据, with growing translational evidence in radiology[pati2022federated, bujotzek2025real]及以上and beyond[oldenhof2023industry].

1.1 动机Motivation

医学图像分割是自动诊断和计算机辅助治疗的关键组成部分，因为它提供了解剖和病理结构的空间有意义的表示Medical image segmentation is a key component of automated diagnosis and computer-assisted therapy, as it provides spatially meaningful representations of anatomical and pathological structures[antonelli2022medical]。 分割模型的监督训练依赖于体素级注释掩模，在临床实践中，由于评估者间的变异性、目标识别的难度、人类注释错误以及自动生成标签的使用增加，体素级注释掩模通常是异构的和有噪声的. Supervised training of segmentation models relies on voxel-level annotation masks, which in clinical practice are often heterogeneous and noisy due to inter-rater variability, difficulty of target recognition, human annotation errors, and the increasing use of automatically generated labels[karimi2020deep]。 因此，分割标签噪声通常表现为轮廓不一致、缺失或附加实例，或者目标的类标签混淆，所有这些都会降低模型性能. As a result, segmentation label noise commonly manifests as contour inconsistencies, missing or additional instances, or confused class labels of targets, all of which degrade model performance[karimi2020deep]（图(Figure1）。 由于分割输出经常用于诊断、治疗计划和定量生物标志物提取等下游任务，因此这种退化可能会传播到临床工作流程中并影响决策的可靠性). Because segmentation outputs are frequently used in downstream tasks such as diagnosis, treatment planning, and quantitative biomarker extraction, such degradation can propagate into clinical workflows and affect decision-making reliability[aliotta2019quantifying, poel2022impact, ma2024segment]。 这些基于噪声的挑战在 FL 中进一步放大。 首先，随着图像采集、管理和标记在各个机构之间独立执行，数据和注释的异质性自然增加. These noise-based challenges are further amplified in FL. First, both data and annotation heterogeneity increase naturally as image acquisition, curation, and labeling are performed independently across institutions[fang2022robust]。 其次，FL 的隐私保护性质限制了对数据和标签的集中检查，使噪声检测和缓解变得复杂，使得噪声客户端可能只会通过性能恶化而变得明显，正如实际部署中所报告的那样. Second, the privacy-preserving nature of FL restricts centralized inspection of data and labels, complicating noise detection and mitigation, such that noisy clients may only become apparent through performance deterioration, as reported in real-world deployments[bujotzek2025real]。 第三，分布式标签噪声可以通过模型聚合传播，允许部分或完全有噪声的客户端降低其他干净客户端的学习效果。. Third, distributed label noise can propagate through model aggregation, allowing partially or fully noisy clients to degrade learning on otherwise clean clients.
