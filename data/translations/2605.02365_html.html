通过群体水平皮质动力学建模连续认知状态Modeling sequential cognitive states via population level cortical dynamics [*][∗]

M·弗吉尼亚·博莱利M. Virginia Bolelli[†], 卢卡·格列柯, Luca Greco[†]和达里奥·普兰迪, and Dario Prandi[‡]

摘要。Abstract.在这项工作中，我们提出了大脑活动的循环和顺序模式的数学模型，将异宿动力学与离散神经场模型相结合。我们首先表明，具有生物学现实平衡的空间离散神经场方程不能支持异宿循环。另一方面，异斜动力学经常出现在 Lotka-Volterra 型系统中，但这些方程并不直接对应于神经元过程。为了解决这个问题，我们使用通用逼近定理的一个版本，通过可解释为高维 Amari 型神经场系统的神经网络来逼近任何目标动态。当目标动力学包含异宿循环时，近似矢量场生成紧密遵循异宿连接的周期性轨迹。作为一个案例研究，我们考虑了集中注意力冥想背后的认知过程。我们展示了该模型如何再现认知状态之间的顺序转换，并得出结论，提供了近似动态的神经解释。In this work, we present a mathematical model for cyclic and sequential patterns of brain activity, combining heteroclinic dynamics with discrete neural-field models. We first show that spatial-discrete neural-field equations with biologically realistic equilibria cannot support heteroclinic cycles. On the other hand, heterocline dynamics often arise in Lotka–Volterra-type systems, but these equations do not directly correspond to neuronal processes. To address this, we use a version of the Universal Approximation Theorem to approximate any target dynamics by a neural network interpretable as a high-dimensional Amari-type neural-field system. When the target dynamics contains a heteroclinic cycle, the approximating vector field generates a periodic trajectory that closely follows the heteroclinic connection. As a case study, we consider the cognitive processes underlying focused-attention meditation. We show how the model reproduces sequential transitions among cognitive states and we conclude providing a neural interpretation of the approximating dynamics.

1. 简介。1. Introduction.实验证据表明，神经动力学通常表现出亚稳态：瞬态、弱稳定的配置，系统在过渡到另一个状态之前保持有限的时间[3,9,35,43]。这些状态不是孤立的事件，而是以有序序列的形式出现，可以在不同的任务、测量模式和分析级别上进行跟踪，这表明了一个共同的动态主题。已经报道了多个认知和知觉过程中的顺序亚稳态模式，包括运动记忆巩固[29]、双眼竞争[27]和集中注意力冥想[17]。这种跨领域模式的重复表明顺序切换不是特定于任务的，而是反映了更广泛的神经活动组织原则[33]。Experimental evidence indicates that neural dynamics often exhibit metastable states: transient, weakly stable configurations in which the system remains for a finite time before transitioning to another state [3, 9, 35, 43]. Rather than isolated events, these states appear as ordered sequences that can be tracked across different tasks, measurement modalities, and levels of analysis, suggesting a common dynamical motif. Sequential metastable patterns have been reported across several cognitive and perceptual processes, including motor memory consolidation [29], binocular rivalry [27], and focused attention meditation [17]. The recurrence of such patterns across domains indicates that sequential switching is not task-specific but reflects a broader organizing principle of neural activity [33].

这种行为的自然数学框架是由呈现稳定异宿序列的动力系统给出的。在这样的系统中，轨迹在一系列鞍平衡附近演化，这些鞍平衡由沿不稳定流形的轨道连接，并产生有序但灵活的转变[24,23,32,2,1,10]。事实上，稳定和不稳定流形的全局几何形状固定了访问状态的顺序，而鞍平衡的局部不稳定性使得动力学对扰动敏感，因此是可调节的[35,34,39,22]。表现出这种行为的经典模型包括 Lotka–Volterra 系统 [34, 24] 和基于图的动态结构 [5]。然而，虽然在现象学上有效，但这些模型通常不能直接用神经机制来解释。A natural mathematical framework for such behavior is given by dynamical systems presenting stable heteroclinic sequences. In such systems, trajectories evolve near a sequence of saddle equilibria connected by orbits along their unstable manifolds and give rise to ordered yet flexible transitions [24, 23, 32, 2, 1, 10]. Indeed, the global geometry of the stable and unstable manifolds fixes the order in which the states are visited, while the local instability of the saddle equilibria makes the dynamics sensitive to perturbations and thus modulable [35, 34, 39, 22]. Classical models exhibiting such behavior include Lotka–Volterra systems [34, 24] and graph-based dynamical constructions [5]. However, while phenomenologically effective, these models are generally not directly interpretable in terms of neural mechanisms.

We combine tools from dynamical systems theory, especially heteroclinic connections, with classical neural activity models such as the spatial-discrete neural-field equations. These two approaches may seem incompatible at first, but we show that they can be integrated using machine learning techniques. In particular, we derive a suitable version of the universal approximation theorem to build an interpretable neural network that can reproduce the desired cyclic dynamics.
