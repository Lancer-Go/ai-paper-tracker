切片潜力的摊销最佳传输Amortized Optimal Transport from Sliced Potentials

张明福Minh-Phuc Truong[†]凯阮Khai Nguyen[⋄][⋆]

摘要Abstract

我们提出了一种新颖的摊销优化方法，通过利用从切片 OT 导出的 Kantorovich 势来预测跨多对度量的最佳运输 (OT) 计划。我们引入两种摊销策略：基于回归的摊销（RA-OT）和基于目标的摊销（OA-OT）。在 RA-OT 中，我们制定了一个函数回归模型，将原始 OT 问题中的 Kantorovich 势视为响应，将切片 OT 中获得的势视为预测变量，并通过最小二乘法估计这些模型。在 OA-OT 中，我们通过优化 Kantorovich 双目标来估计功能模型的参数。在这两种方法中，预测的 OT 计划随后都是从估计的潜力中恢复的。作为摊销 OT 方法，RA-OT 和 OA-OT 都可以通过重用从先前实例中学到的信息来快速近似新的解决方案，从而有效地解决不同测量对中重复的 OT 问题。此外，通过利用切片 OT 提供的结构，所提出的模型更加简洁，独立于测量的特定结构，例如离散情况下的原子数量，同时实现了高精度。我们展示了我们的方法在 MNIST 数字传输、颜色传输、球形数据供需传输以及小批量 OT 条件流匹配等任务上的有效性。We propose a novel amortized optimization method for predicting optimal transport (OT) plans across multiple pairs of measures by leveraging Kantorovich potentials derived from sliced OT. We introduce two amortization strategies: regression-based amortization (RA-OT) and objective-based amortization (OA-OT). In RA-OT, we formulate a functional regression model that treats Kantorovich potentials from the original OT problem as responses and those obtained from sliced OT as predictors, and estimate these models via least-squares methods. In OA-OT, we estimate the parameters of the functional model by optimizing the Kantorovich dual objective. In both approaches, the predicted OT plan is subsequently recovered from the estimated potentials. As amortized OT methods, both RA-OT and OA-OT enable efficient solutions to repeated OT problems across different measure pairs by reusing information learned from prior instances to rapidly approximate new solutions. Moreover, by exploiting the structure provided by sliced OT, the proposed models are more parsimonious, independent of specific structures of the measures, such as the number of atoms in the discrete case, while achieving high accuracy. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approaches on tasks including MNIST digit transport, color transfer, supply–demand transportation on spherical data, and mini-batch OT conditional flow matching.

1 简介1 Introduction

最优传输 (OT) [73, 74] 已被广泛认为是统计学、机器学习和数据科学领域的基本且强大的工具。它在广泛的应用中发挥着关键作用。例如，OT 提高了生成模型的质量，包括自动编码器 [70, 52]、生成对抗网络 [6, 33]、基于扩散和流的模型 [39, 54, 71] 以及漂移模型 [35]。它也广泛用于域适应，帮助建立源域和目标域之间的对应关系[19,20,23,76]。除了这些领域之外，OT 在计算生物学 [15, 62]、化学 [75]、图像处理 [29]、公平性 [36]、信号处理 [38]、计算机图形学 [67, 66, 12]、统计推断 [8, 9] 和依赖性测量 [17, 16] 等领域也有大量应用。Optimal transport (OT) [73, 74] has been widely recognized as a fundamental and powerful tool in statistics, machine learning, and data science. It plays a key role across a broad range of applications. For instance, OT has improved the quality of generative models, including autoencoders [70, 52], generative adversarial networks [6, 33], and diffusion and flow-based models [39, 54, 71], as well as drifting models [35]. It is also widely used in domain adaptation, where it helps establish correspondences between source and target domains [19, 20, 23, 76]. Beyond these areas, OT has found numerous applications in computational biology [15, 62], chemistry [75], image processing [29], fairness [36], signal processing [38], computer graphics [67, 66, 12], statistical inference [8, 9], and dependency measurement [17, 16], among others.

计算最优传输 (OT) [53] 已成为 OT 中的一个核心但具有挑战性的领域。除了特殊情况，例如高斯测度之间的 OT（允许封闭形式解 [24]），计算 OT 通常很昂贵。在离散设置中，两种测度均受有限集支持，OT 可以表示为支持点数量具有超立方复杂度的线性规划问题 [51]。人们提出了各种方法来减轻这种计算负担，包括小批量随机近似[68,28,27,49]和低秩近似[61,60]。对于一般措施，OT 通常通过其对偶公式来解决，其中涉及 Kantorovich 势的函数优化问题 [74]。Computational optimal transport (OT) [53] has emerged as a central yet challenging area within OT. Except for special cases such as OT between Gaussian measures, which admits a closed form solution [24], computing OT is generally expensive. In the discrete setting, where both measures are supported on finite sets, OT can be formulated as a linear programming problem with super cubic complexity in the number of support points [51]. Various approaches have been proposed to mitigate this computational burden, including stochastic approximation with mini-batches [68, 28, 27, 49] and low rank approximations [61, 60]. For general measures, OT is typically solved through its dual formulation, which involves a functional optimization problem over Kantorovich potentials [74].

一种广泛使用的方法是熵正则化[21]，它通过添加熵惩罚来平滑传输问题。在离散设置中，这种正则化确保了独特的传输计划，可以通过矩阵缩放算法（例如 Sinkhorn-Knopp [65]）或等效地通过迭代 Bregman 投影 [7] 来有效计算。除了将计算复杂性降低到二次时间之外，熵正则化还提高了估计运输成本 [31] 和运输计划 [44, 57] 的统计率。在一般情况下，它消除了对偶公式中的约束，从而实现无约束优化[32]。由于这些原因，熵正则化 OT 在实践中作为经典 OT 的替代品被广泛使用。One widely used approach is entropic regularization [21], which smooths the transport problem by adding an entropy penalty. In the discrete setting, this regularization ensures a unique transport plan that can be efficiently computed via matrix scaling algorithms such as Sinkhorn–Knopp [65], or, equivalently, through iterative Bregman projections [7]. Beyond reducing computational complexity to quadratic time, entropic regularization also improves the statistical rates for estimating both the transport cost [31] and the transport plan [44, 57]. In the general cases, it removes constraints in the dual formulation, enabling unconstrained optimization [32]. For these reasons, entropic regularized OT is widely used in practice as a surrogate for classical OT.

在许多涉及大量测量对的场景中，例如小批量生成模型[33, 35]、数据集比较[3]、3D点云自动编码器[1]、点云最近邻分类和回归[58]、地震信号分析[26]和单细胞扰动建模[14]，必须重复求解最优传输（OT）。为了加速此类设置中的计算，采用摊销优化 [64, 4] 来直接根据输入测量来预测 OT 解决方案。摊销有两个主要目标：运输成本和运输计划。预测运输成本相对简单，因为它减少为估计非负标量。这个方向已经被广泛探索，例如通过学习嵌入或嵌入网络[45,18,37,34]，以及回归到切片最佳传输指标[50]。相比之下，摊销运输计划更具挑战性，因为它涉及预测输入分布的联合度量并隐式确定成本。据我们所知，唯一现有的方法是 Meta-OT [5]，它学习从输入测量中预测熵正则化 Kantorovich 势作为计划的代理。In many scenarios involving numerous pairs of measures, such as mini-batch generative models [33, 35], dataset comparison [3], 3D point-cloud autoencoders [1], point-cloud nearest neighbor classification and regression [58], seismic signal analysis [26], and single-cell perturbation modeling [14], optimal transport (OT) must be solved repeatedly. To accelerate computation in such settings, amortized optimization [64, 4] has been employed to predict OT solutions directly from input measures. Two primary targets arise for amortization: the transportation cost and the transportation plan. Predicting the transportation cost is relatively straightforward, as it reduces to estimating a nonnegative scalar. This direction has been widely explored, for example through learned embeddings or embedding networks [45, 18, 37, 34], as well as regression onto sliced optimal transport metrics [50]. In contrast, amortizing the transportation plan is significantly more challenging, as it involves predicting a joint measure over the input distributions and implicitly determines the cost. To the best of our knowledge, the only existing approach is Meta-OT [5], which learns to predict entropically regularized Kantorovich potentials from the input measures as a proxy for the plan.

在离散设置中，Meta-OT 构建了一个摊销模型（通常是一个神经网络），它将由原子支持和相应权重表示的输入测量映射到维度与原子数量匹配的向量，从而逼近 Kantorovich 势之一。在连续设置中，Meta-OT 采用超网络作为摊销模型来预测参数 Kantorovich 势的参数。在这两种情况下，Meta-OT 都采用基于目标的摊销策略，其中摊销模型是通过直接优化 Kantorovich 双目标来学习的。 Meta-OT 的主要限制是它直接采用输入测量的原始表示，因此，其摊销模型包含许多参数。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种摊销运输计划的新方法。关键的想法是使用In the discrete setting, Meta-OT constructs an amortized model, typically a neural network, that maps input measures, represented by their atomic supports and corresponding weights, to a vector whose dimension matches the number of atoms, thereby approximating one of the Kantorovich potentials. In the continuous setting, Meta-OT instead employs a hypernetwork as the amortized model to predict the parameters of a parametric Kantorovich potential. In both cases, Meta-OT adopts an objective-based amortization strategy, where the amortized models are learned by directly optimizing the Kantorovich dual objective. The main limitation of Meta-OT is that it directly takes the raw representation of input measures, hence, its amortized models contains many parameters. In this work, we propose a new approach to amortizing the transportation plan. The key idea is to use来自切片 OT 的 Kantorovich 势Kantorovich potentials derived from sliced OT[56, 46] 作为摊销模型的输入，而不是原始输入措施。这种表示大大减少了输入的维度，从而减少了摊销模型所需的参数数量。[56, 46] as inputs to the amortized model, rather than the raw input measures. This representation substantially reduces the dimensionality of the input, and consequently lowers the number of parameters required for the amortized model.

5 结论5 Conclusion

本文提出了两种新颖的摊销 OT 方法：RA-OT 和 OA-OT，它们利用切片 OT 来有效地预测跨多个度量对的 OT 计划。 RA-OT 使用回归将切片 OT 电位映射到原始 OT 电位，而 OA-OT 在切片 OT 电位范围内优化 Kantorovich 对偶。这两种方法都可以快速、准确地解决重复的 OT 问题，而且都很简约，并且不依赖于措施的结构或规模。 MNIST 数字传输、颜色传输、球形供需问题和条件流匹配的实验证明了该方法的实际有效性和通用性。The paper presents two novel amortized OT methods, RA-OT and OA-OT, which leverage sliced OT to efficiently predict OT plans across multiple measure pairs. RA-OT uses regression to map sliced OT potentials to original OT potentials, while OA-OT optimizes the Kantorovich dual within the span of sliced OT potentials. Both approaches enable rapid and accurate solutions for repeated OT problems, are parsimonious, and do not depend on the structure or size of the measures. Experiments on MNIST digit transport, color transfer, spherical supply–demand problems, and conditional flow matching demonstrate the practical effectiveness and generality of the methods.
