These hallucinations are difficult to detect in reasoning-intensive tasks, where the content appears coherent but contains errors like logical flaws and unreliable intermediate results. While step-level analysis is commonly used to detect internal hallucinations, it suffers from limited granularity and poor scalability due to its reliance on step segmentation. To address these limitations, we proposeTokenHD，用于训练令牌级幻觉检测器的整体管道。具体来说，, a holistic pipeline for training token-level hallucination detectors. 1  简介1  Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在推理密集型任务（例如解决复杂的数学问题）上取得了显着的性能Large language models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable performance on reasoning-intensive tasks, such as solving complex mathematical problems[39, 15, 12]和代码生成and code generation[21, 7]。尽管如此，法学硕士仍然饱受幻觉之苦. Nevertheless, LLMs still suffer from hallucinations[25, 20, 26]：生成的内容可能看起来连贯，但包含事实错误或逻辑不一致，使得此类错误难以检测并破坏响应的可靠性。: generated content may appear coherent yet contain factual errors or logical inconsistencies, making such errors difficult to detect and undermining response reliability.

为了应对这些挑战，人们提出了各种事后检测方法来审查法学硕士生成内容的真实性。早期的研究主要集中在事实幻觉上To address these challenges, various post-hoc detection methods have been proposed to scrutinize the truthfulness of LLM-generated content. Earlier research primarily focused on factual hallucinations[10, 34, 36]，例如检测生成的语句和可信知识源之间的矛盾。然而，随着检索增强生成（RAG）系统的出现, such as detecting contradictions between generated statements and trusted knowledge sources. However, with the emergence of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems[13]以及搜索工具的集成and the integration of search tools[29]，这些基于知识的幻觉可以在很大程度上得到缓解。相比之下，推理密集型任务（例如数学）中的幻觉通常表现为微妙的逻辑错误或不正确的中间结果，这使得它们更难被发现。为了定位这些错误，常见的做法是将解决方案分解为各个步骤，并通过步骤级监督来训练检测器。具体来说，过程奖励模型（PRM）, these knowledge-based hallucinations can be largely mitigated. In contrast, hallucinations in reasoning-intensive tasks (e.g., mathematics) often manifest as subtle logical errors or incorrect intermediate results, making them significantly harder to detect. To locate these errors, a common practice is to decompose a solution into individual steps and train a detector with step-level supervision. Specifically, Process Reward Models (PRMs)[30, 45, 56]通过为每个步骤分配正确性标签来采用这种范式，从而查明内部推理过程出错的地方。adopt this paradigm by assigning a correctness label to each step, thereby pinpointing where the internal reasoning process goes wrong.

然而，PRM 面临一些限制，因为它们需要显式的步骤分割，当模型输出是自由形式或缺乏明确的分离边界时，这很困难。此外，它们本质上仅限于步骤级分析，缺乏细粒度和灵活的幻觉定位。而基于搜索的方法，例如蒙特卡罗树搜索（MCTS）Nevertheless, PRMs face several limitations because they necessitate explicit step segmentation, which is difficult when model outputs are free-form or lack clear separation boundaries. Furthermore, they are inherently restricted to step-level analysis, lacking fine-grained and flexible hallucination localization. While search-based methods such as Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)[45, 49, 56]可以通过采样统计数据来估计中间正确性，但它们会因密集的策略模型查询而产生过高的计算开销，从而限制了可扩展性。为了应对这些挑战，我们将重点转向文本的原子单元，提出can estimate intermediate correctness via sampling statistics, they incur prohibitive computational overhead from intensive policy-model queries, limiting scalability. To address these challenges, we shift our focus to the atomic units of text by proposingTokenHD，令牌级幻觉检测的整体框架。与 PRM 不同，, a holistic framework for token-level hallucination detection. Unlike PRMs,TokenHD直接对自由格式文本进行操作并评估每个标记的幻觉。这种设计能够精确定位幻觉，同时大大减少推理延迟，因为我们的检测器直接分配分数，而不需要生成语言分析。图operates directly on free-form text and evaluates hallucination for each token. 拟议的The proposedTokenHD包括可扩展的数据合成引擎和专门的培训配方。为了克服现有数据集中令牌级幻觉标签的稀缺性，我们首先开发一个数据合成管道来获取具有令牌级注释的高质量样本。具体来说，对于每个候选样本，我们提示多个批评模型来识别幻觉的文本片段。这些片段通过文本恢复过程进行细化，转换为标记空间，并通过平均合并为单个标签序列。为了提高聚合质量，超越简单平均，我们进一步设计了一种自适应集成策略，优化每个批评模型的权重以执行加权聚合。利用这些令牌级别的监督，我们使用专门为解决幻觉令牌的稀疏性而设计的重要性加权策略来训练我们的检测器。我们还建立了严格的评估协议来评估检测性能，并在数学和 STEM 基准上进行广泛的实验。最后，我们表明我们的检测器可以在不同的任务域和策略模型中很好地泛化，同时还探索了几种实用策略来增强其泛化能力。comprises a scalable data synthesis engine and a specialized training recipe. To overcome the scarcity of token-level hallucination labels in existing datasets, we first develop a data synthesis pipeline to obtain high-quality samples with token-level annotations. Specifically, for each candidate sample, we prompt multiple critic models to identify hallucinated text fragments. These fragments are refined through a text restoration process, converted into token space, and merged into a single label sequence through averaging. To improve the aggregation quality beyond simple averaging, we further design an adaptive ensemble strategy that optimizes weights for each critic model to perform weighted aggregation. Leveraging these token-level supervisions, we train our detector using an importance-weighted strategy specifically designed to address the sparsity of hallucinated tokens. We also establish a rigorous evaluation protocol to assess detection performance and conduct extensive experiments on mathematical and STEM benchmarks. Finally, we show that our detector can generalize well across diverse task domains and policy models, while also exploring several practical strategies to enhance its generalization capability.

7  结论与局限性7  Conclusion and Limitations
