扫描电镜SEM：用于视觉语言模型事后去偏的稀疏嵌入调制: Sparse Embedding Modulation for Post-Hoc Debiasing of Vision-Language Models

摘要Abstract

连接视觉和语言的模型（例如 CLIP）是多模式人工智能的关键组成部分，但其大规模、未经整理的训练数据引入了严重的社会和虚假偏见。现有的事后去偏差方法通常直接在密集的 CLIP 嵌入空间中运行，其中偏差和任务相关信息高度纠缠。这种纠缠限制了他们在不降低语义保真度的情况下消除偏见的能力。 在这项工作中，我们建议Models that bridge vision and language, such as CLIP, are key components of multimodal AI, yet their large-scale, uncurated training data introduce severe social and spurious biases. Existing post-hoc debiasing methods often operate directly in the dense CLIP embedding space, where bias and task-relevant information are highly entangled. This entanglement limits their ability to remove bias without degrading semantic fidelity. In this work, we propose稀疏嵌入调制Sparse Embedding Modulation (扫描电镜SEM），一个事后零样本去偏框架，在稀疏自动编码器（SAE）潜在空间中运行。通过将 CLIP 文本嵌入分解为解开的特征，), a post-hoc, zero-shot debiasing framework that operates in a Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) latent space. By decomposing CLIP text embeddings into disentangled features,扫描电镜SEM识别和调节与偏差相关的神经元，同时保留与查询相关的神经元。这可以实现更精确的非线性干预。 在四个基准数据集和两个 CLIP 主干上，identifies and modulates bias-relevant neurons while preserving query-relevant ones. This enables more precise, non-linear interventions. Across four benchmark datasets and two CLIP backbones,扫描电镜SEM在检索和零样本分类方面取得了显着的公平性收益。我们的结果表明，稀疏潜在表示为视觉语言模型的事后去偏提供了有效的基础。achieves substantial fairness gains in retrieval and zero-shot classification. Our results demonstrate that sparse latent representations provide an effective foundation for post-hoc debiasing of vision-language models.

1 简介Introduction

尽管他们有能力，但其训练数据的大规模、未经整理的性质带来了深刻的偏见Despite their capabilities, the large-scale, uncurated nature of their training data introduces profound biases[birhane2021multimodal]。 因此，根据这些数据训练的模型继承并放大了社会刻板印象和其他虚假相关性. Consequently, models trained on this data inherit and amplify societal stereotypes and other spurious correlations[agarwal2021evaluating, hamidieh2024identifying, hosseini2025seeing]。 这导致了严重的失败：模型将“医生”与“男性”联系起来，将“护士”与“女性”联系起来. These failures degrade model reliability and fairness in downstream applications, raising concerns on their wide adoption.

3.1 动机：量化解开Motivation: Quantifying Disentanglement
