Claw AI 实验室：一个自主多智能体研究团队Claw AI Lab: An Autonomous Multi-Agent Research Team

Fan Wu1,*  Cheng Chen1,2,*  Zhenshan Tan4,*  Taiyu Zhang3  Xinzhen Xu1  Yanyu Qian1 Dingcheng Gao5  Lanyun Zhu1  Qi Zhu3  Yi Tan6  Deyi Ji3  Guosheng Lin1 Tianrun Chen3,†\dagger  Deheng Ye†\dagger  Fayao Liu2,†\dagger 1NTU 2A*STAR 3Moxin Technology Co., LTD 4NUIST 5THU 6USTC *Equal contribution †\daggerCorresponding authors https://github.com/Claw-AI-Lab/Claw-AI-Lab
摘要Abstract

我们推出了 Claw AI Lab，这是一个实验室原生的自主研究平台，可将自动化研究从隐藏的提示到论文管道推进到交互式人工智能实验室。我们不是将系统围绕单个代理或固定的串行工作流程，而是允许用户通过一个提示实例化一个完整的研究团队，通过统一的仪表板提供可定制的角色、协作工作流程、实时监控、工件检查和回滚/恢复控制。该平台还支持不同的探索、多智能体讨论和复制研究模式，使自主研究在实践中更加可操纵和像实验室一样。 Claw AI Lab 的一个关键实际贡献在于其 Claw-Code Harness，它将本地代码库、数据集和检查点连接到可运行的实验，并将执行工件反馈到研究循环中。因此，该工具不仅改进了执行集成，还改进了实验完成度和结果完整性：实验更容易检查、迭代并忠实地转移到最终论文中，从而减少了常见的失败模式，例如部分运行和格式错误的结果报告。在我们对五个人工智能研究案例研究的内部评估中，以 AutoResearchClaw 作为基线，Claw AI Lab 在想法新颖性、实验完整性和论文呈现质量方面始终受到人工智能专家评委的青睐。我们将 Claw AI Lab 视为迈向新范式的早期一步：We present Claw AI Lab, a lab-native autonomous research platform that advances automated research from a hidden prompt-to-paper pipeline into an interactive AI laboratory. Rather than centering the system around a single agent or a fixed serial workflow, we allow users to instantiate a full research team from one prompt, with customizable roles, collaborative workflows, real-time monitoring, artifact inspection, and rollback/resume control through a unified dashboard. The platform also supports distinct research modes for exploration, multi-agent discussion, and reproduction, making autonomous research substantially more steerable and laboratory-like in practice. A key practical contribution of Claw AI Lab lies in its Claw-Code Harness, which connects local codebases, datasets, and checkpoints to runnable experiments and feeds execution artifacts back into the research loop. As a result, the harness improves not only execution integration, but also experimental completion and result integrity: experiments are easier to inspect, iterate on, and faithfully transfer into final papers, reducing common failure modes such as partial runs and malformed result reporting. In our internal evaluation on five AI research case studies, using AutoResearchClaw as the baseline, Claw AI Lab is consistently preferred by AI expert judges on idea novelty, experiment completeness, and paper presentation quality. We view Claw AI Lab as an early step toward a new paradigm:自主研究作为可用、交互式和可靠性意识的科学基础设施autonomous research as usable, interactive, and reliability-aware scientific infrastructure.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型的最新进展使得自主研究变得越来越可行。现有系统，例如 AutoResearchClawRecent progress in large language models has made autonomous research increasingly plausible. Prior systems such as AutoResearchClaw(Liu et al., 2026)、自动研究, autoresearch(Karpathy, 2026)和其他端到端研究代理已经证明了很大程度上自动化研究工作流程的可行性，其中一个主题可以在有限的人为干预下从想法开发推进到实验、分析和论文写作, and other end-to-end research agents have demonstrated the feasibility of largely automated research workflows, in which a topic can be pushed from idea development toward experiments, analysis, and paper writing with limited human intervention(Lu et al., 2024; Yamada et al., 2025; Schmidgall et al., 2025)。与此同时，最近的工作已经将这一空间扩展到一次性论文生成之外，探索多智能体科学协作、假设生成以及更具互动性的科学自动化形式. At the same time, recent work has expanded this space beyond one-shot paper generation, exploring multi-agent scientific collaboration, hypothesis generation, and more interactive forms of science automation(Gottweis et al., 2025; Ghareeb et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025)。 Claw AI Lab 向前迈出了不同的一步：它不再将自主研究主要视为自动化纸张生产，而是将其重新定义为交互式 AI 实验室的运作。. Claw AI Lab takes a different step forward: instead of treating autonomous research primarily as automated paper production, it reframes it as the operation of an interactive AI laboratory.

这个框架是 Claw AI Lab 设计的核心。该系统被设计为实验室原生多智能体研究平台，使用户能够通过单一提示创建完整的人工智能研究实验室，并具有可定制的角色、协作工作流程和人工干预。其界面以统一的仪表板为中心，提供实时事件流、多项目监控、工件检查和一键回滚功能。 Claw AI Lab 还支持三种不同的研究模式——探索、讨论和再现——使系统超越隐藏的串行管道，走向更加可见、协作和可控的研究环境。从这个意义上说，Claw AI Lab 在精神上更接近于交互式和连续的科学系统，而不是纯粹的离线纸张生成管道This framing is central to the design of Claw AI Lab. The system is designed as a lab-native multi-agent research platform that enables users to create a full AI research lab from a single prompt, with customizable roles, collaborative workflows, and human intervention. Its interface centers the user experience around a unified dashboard with real-time event streams, multi-project monitoring, artifact inspection, and one-click rollback. Claw AI Lab also supports three distinct research modes—Explore, Discussion, and Reproduce—which move the system beyond a hidden serial pipeline and toward a more visible, collaborative, and controllable research environment. In this sense, Claw AI Lab is closer in spirit to interactive and continual science systems than to a purely offline paper-generation pipeline(Schmidgall et al., 2025; Gottweis et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025).

这种实验室视角很重要，因为真正的研究不是一次性生成任务。它是交互式的、迭代的、角色专门化的并且工件较多。因此，Claw AI Lab 旨在使自主研究在实践中更加有用：用户可以启动项目、监控代理、检查中间工件并在整个研究过程中进行干预，而不仅仅是在开始或结束时进行干预。从这个意义上说，我们的贡献不仅仅是更强的自动化，而是更强大的自主研究系统抽象——将研究视为一个持久且可检查的过程，而不是黑盒管道。This laboratory perspective is important because real research is not a one-shot generation task. It is interactive, iterative, role-specialized, and artifact-heavy. Accordingly, Claw AI Lab is designed to make autonomous research more usable in practice: users can launch projects, monitor agents, inspect intermediate artifacts, and intervene throughout the research process rather than only at the beginning or the end. In this sense, our contribution is not simply stronger automation, but a stronger systems abstraction for autonomous research—one that treats research as a persistent and inspectable process rather than a black-box pipeline.

Claw AI Lab 的一个关键实际优势在于它如何处理实验执行和结果整合。最近的系统表明，编码代理已经可以在真实的训练代码和评估指标上运行有用的研究循环A key practical advantage of Claw AI Lab lies in how it handles experimental execution and result consolidation. Recent systems show that coding agents can already run useful research loops over real training code and evaluation metrics(Karpathy, 2026; Zheng et al., 2025)。我们的平台推出了 Claw-Code Harness. Our platform introduces Claw-Code Harness(UltraWorkers, 2026)作为读取本地代码库、数据集和检查点、编写可运行代码并支持完整研究成果（包括论文、代码、图表和实验日志）的生成的核心组件。这种设计赋予了该工具比简单执行包装器更广泛的作用：它成为将本地研究资产链接到可运行实验并将实验输出连接回更广泛的研究工作流程的接口。因此，该工具增强了实施、执行和报告之间的连续性。as a core component that reads local codebases, datasets, and checkpoints, writes runnable code, and supports the production of complete research deliverables, including papers, code, figures, and experiment logs. This design gives the harness a broader role than that of a simple execution wrapper: it becomes the interface that links local research assets to runnable experiments and connects experiment outputs back to the broader research workflow. As a result, the harness strengthens the continuity between implementation, execution, and reporting.

这一点对于实验的完成尤为重要。在自主研究中，常见的失败模式是实验仅部分运行，中间输出仍然难以检查，或者最终报告包含的结果表不能忠实反映实际的执行输出。最近的基准表明，多步骤研究执行、复制和证据跟踪仍然比单独的表面生成要困难得多This point is especially important for experimental completion. In autonomous research, a common failure mode is that experiments run only partially, intermediate outputs remain difficult to inspect, or final reports contain result tables that do not faithfully reflect the actual execution outputs. Recent benchmarks suggest that multi-step research execution, replication, and evidence tracking remain significantly more difficult than surface-level generation alone might suggest(Starace et al., 2025; Dong et al., 2026)。 Claw AI Lab 就是专门针对这一差距而设计的。通过将工具嵌入到仪表板原生、以工件为中心的工作流程中，Claw AI Lab 使实验输出更加可见、更容易跟踪，并且更容易传播到最终报告中。换句话说，该工具不仅改善了实验是否可以运行，还改善了实验是否能够完成、可检查和正确反映研究工件。与纯粹以管道为中心的自主研究观点相比，这是 Claw 提高研究完成质量的一个核心实际原因。. Claw AI Lab is designed explicitly against this gap. By embedding the harness inside a dashboard-native, artifact-centered workflow, Claw AI Lab makes experimental outputs more visible, easier to trace, and easier to propagate into final reports. Put differently, the harness improves not only whether experiments can run, but whether they are carried through to complete, inspectable, and correctly reflected research artifacts. This is a central practical reason why Claw improves research completion quality in comparison to a purely pipeline-centric view of autonomous research.

总的来说，Claw AI Lab 为该领域指明了更广泛的方向。自主研究的未来可能不仅仅在于更长的隐藏管道，还在于交互式、可检查和具有可靠性意识的人工智能实验室系统。从这个角度来看，Claw 的贡献不仅是一个更强大的平台，而且还为自主研究应该成为什么样子提供了更强有力的框架：不仅是论文写作的自动化，而且是可用科学基础设施的建设。Taken together, Claw AI Lab points toward a broader direction for the field. The future of autonomous research may not lie in ever longer hidden pipelines alone, but in interactive, inspectable, and reliability-aware AI laboratory systems. From this perspective, the contribution of Claw is not only a stronger platform, but a stronger framing for what autonomous research should become: not merely the automation of paper writing, but the construction of usable scientific infrastructure.

2 方法论Methodology

我们推出了 Claw AI Lab，这是一个分层多代理框架，通过将端到端研究过程分解为五个结构化层来实现自动化：想法、规划、编码、实验和写作。 如主工作流程所示，我们的系统通过将角色专业化、迭代细化和跨阶段反馈结合到统一的闭环管道中来模仿现实世界的研究实践。We present Claw AI Lab, a hierarchical multi-agent framework that automates the end-to-end research process by decomposing it into five structured layers: Idea, Planning, Coding, Experiment, and Writing. As illustrated in the main workflow, our system mimics real-world research practices by combining role specialization, iterative refinement, and cross-stage feedback into a unified closed-loop pipeline.

概述。Overview.

与之前以线性方式运行的基于管道的研究代理不同Unlike prior pipeline-based research agents that operate in a linear fashion(Liu et al., 2026; Lu et al., 2024)，Claw AI Lab采用金字塔式架构，高级概念逐步转化为可执行工件。每一层都由具有不同职责的专用代理处理，而中间输出则通过验证循环不断完善。这种设计遵循了向角色专业研究代理和交互式科学自动化更广泛的发展, Claw AI Lab adopts a pyramid-style architecture, where high-level concepts are progressively transformed into executable artifacts. Each layer is handled by dedicated agents with distinct responsibilities, while intermediate outputs are continuously refined through validation loops. This design follows the broader move toward role-specialized research agents and interactive science automation(Schmidgall et al., 2025; Gottweis et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025)。它实现了全局协调和局部优化，确保早期决策与下游执行保持一致。. It enables both global coordination and local optimization, ensuring that early-stage decisions remain aligned with downstream execution.

想法层。Idea Layer.

该过程从多智能体讨论阶段开始，其中多个智能体协作探索问题空间。该系统不依赖单一观点，而是通过平行的想法提案鼓励不同的观点，然后进行结构化的辩论和完善。然后通过共识机制选择并巩固最有希望的方向。 这种讨论驱动的设计提高了稳健性和多样性，并更好地反映了如何通过协作而不是孤立的生成来形成真正的研究想法。The process begins with a multi-agent discussion phase, where multiple agents collaboratively explore the problem space. Instead of relying on a single perspective, the system encourages diverse perspectives through parallel idea proposals, followed by structured debate and refinement. A consensus mechanism then selects and consolidates the most promising direction. This discussion-driven design improves robustness and diversity, and better reflects how real research ideas are formed through collaboration rather than isolated generation.

规划层。Planning Layer.

给定选定的想法，系统将其分解为由任务、依赖项和里程碑组成的结构化计划。规划代理通过验证循环（“足够好？”）迭代地完善该计划，在执行之前修改不完整或不明确的组件。 至关重要的是，规划不是一次性的过程。相反，它支持自适应细化，允许根据下游阶段的反馈（例如编码失败或实验结果）进行更新。这确保了计划保持可行并符合实际限制。Given the selected idea, the system decomposes it into a structured plan consisting of tasks, dependencies, and milestones. A planning agent iteratively refines this plan through a validation loop (“Good Enough?”), where incomplete or ambiguous components are revised before execution. Crucially, planning is not a one-shot process. Instead, it supports adaptive refinement, allowing updates based on feedback from downstream stages (e.g., coding failures or experimental results). This ensures that the plan remains feasible and aligned with practical constraints.

编码层。Coding Layer.

编码层将批准的实验计划转化为可运行的研究代码。以爪码安全带为中心The Coding Layer turns an approved experiment plan into runnable research code. Centered on the Claw-Code Harness(UltraWorkers, 2026)，它使用代理编码循环，其中模型可以检查本地代码库、数据集和检查点，然后通过受控工具（包括 bash、读取文件、写入文件、编辑文件、glob 搜索和 grep 搜索）编写、运行、调试和优化实验文件。 该工具通过在沙盒工作区中执行每项任务并注入只读 Python 控制器来执行时间预算、指标报告、结果最终确定和 NaN/Inf 检测，进一步提高了实验可靠性。它还执行冒烟测试和反伪造检查，以检测虚假指标、占位符代码或模拟实现。, it uses an agentic coding loop where the model can inspect local codebases, datasets, and checkpoints, then write, run, debug, and refine experiment files through controlled tools, including bash, read file, write file, edit file, glob search, and grep search. The harness further improves experimental reliability by executing each task in a sandboxed workspace and injecting a read-only Python controller for time-budget enforcement, metric reporting, result finalization, and NaN/Inf detection. It also performs smoke tests and anti-fabrication checks to detect fake metrics, placeholder code, or mock implementations.

实验层。Experiment Layer.

一旦实施完成，系统就会在计算资源上部署实验并收集指标和日志。实验层作为迭代优化循环运行，分析结果以指导后续调整。 重要的是，反馈不仅在实验阶段传播，而且跨层传播。例如，意外的结果可能会触发计划阶段的更新，而重复的失败可能会导致重新审视最初的想法。这种跨层反馈可以实现持续改进并防止错误累积。Once the implementation is finalized, the system deploys experiments on computational resources and collects metrics and logs. The experiment layer operates as an iterative optimization loop, where results are analyzed to guide subsequent adjustments. Importantly, feedback is propagated not only within the experiment stage but also across layers. For example, unexpected results may trigger updates in the planning stage, while repeated failures may lead to revisiting the original idea. This cross-layer feedback enables continuous improvement and prevents error accumulation.

书写层。Writing Layer.

最后阶段将实验结果转化为结构化的研究成果。该系统生成大纲，生成可视化效果，起草手稿，并进行迭代审查和细化。 通过将写入集成到同一管道中，Claw AI Lab 可确保实验结果和报告结果之间的一致性，从而缩小执行和文档之间的差距。The final stage transforms experimental outcomes into structured research outputs. The system generates an outline, produces visualizations, drafts the manuscript, and performs iterative review and refinement. By integrating writing into the same pipeline, Claw AI Lab ensures consistency between experimental results and reported findings, reducing the gap between execution and documentation.

3 实验Experiments

3.1 实验装置Experimental Setup

Claw AI Lab 以完全自主的项目模式运行。它使用GPT-5.4作为主要模型和编码模型，Gemini-3-Pro-Image-Preview作为纸质插图的图形生成模型，Qwen3.5-Plus/Qwen-Plus作为后备模型。 AutoResearchClaw使用GPT-5.4作为主要模型，Gemini-2.5-Pro-Flash-Image作为图像模型，GPT-4o/GPT-4o-mini作为后备模型。 我们将我们的方法与 AutoResearchClaw 在四个不同主题上进行比较。主题 1-​​3 是研究主题，而主题 4 是再现主题。这四个主题是：（1）“量化生成视频模型中的幻觉”，（2）“基于 LIAR 数据集的假新闻分类解决方案”，（3）“使用公共教育数据提高学生成绩的 Q-Learning 方法”，以及（4）“再现和分析 PhyCustom”Claw AI Lab is run in fully autonomous project mode. It uses GPT-5.4 as both the main model and the coding model, Gemini-3-Pro-Image-Preview as the figure-generation model for paper illustrations, and Qwen3.5-Plus/Qwen-Plus as fallback models. AutoResearchClaw uses GPT-5.4 as the main model, Gemini-2.5-Pro-Flash-Image as the image model, and GPT-4o/GPT-4o-mini as fallback models. We compare our method with AutoResearchClaw on four diverse topics. Topics 1–3 are research topics, while Topic 4 is a reproduction topic. The four topics are: (1) “Quantifying Hallucination in Generated Video Models”, (2) “LIAR Dataset-Based Fake News Classification Solution”, (3) “A Q-Learning Approach for Student Performance Improvement Using Public Educational Data”, and (4) “Reproducing and Analyzing PhyCustom(Wu et al., 2025)关于通量on Flux(Labs, 2024)”。 每篇生成的论文均由两位 LLM 评估员 ChatGPT 5.4 Thinking 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro 进行评审，涵盖技术深度和可重复性、结构和章节流程、新颖性和贡献、清晰度和术语、逻辑论证以及引文和证据支持等六个维度。 每次审查都是在新的对话窗口中进行，以减少上下文遗留。研究论文使用相同的学术评论提示，而复制论文则使用单独的复制导向提示。.” Each generated paper is reviewed by two LLM evaluators, ChatGPT 5.4 Thinking and Gemini 3.1 Pro, across six dimensions, including technical depth&reproducibility, structure&section flow, novelty&contributions, clarity&terminology, logical argumentation and citations&evidence support. Each review is conducted in a fresh conversation window to reduce context carry-over. Research papers use the same academic review prompt, while the reproduction paper uses a separate reproduction-oriented prompt.
