1.1 从语言符号到音乐结构From Language Tokens to Musical Structure

GPT 风格的模型取得了显着的成功，很大程度上是通过代表 具有可重用离散标记的有限词汇量的语言GPT-style models have achieved remarkable success in large part by representing language with a finite vocabulary of reusable discrete tokens20; 1。这些代币提供了一个紧凑的 原始序列和上下文建模之间的接口：一组有限的单元 可以跨上下文重新组合以表示有效的无界范围 单词、句子和高阶结构。这种组合重用是 被广泛认为是基于令牌的序列建模规模化的关键原因 有效地。. This compositional reuse is widely viewed as a key reason why token-based sequence modeling scales effectively.

象征性音乐似乎也受到同样的对待。它由离散的 注意事件和展示的重复、层次结构、远程依赖性，以及 重复使用。因此，和弦、主题、乐句和乐段看起来类似于 语言符号。现有的符号音乐标记化转移了这一点 通过事件流、复合令牌的不同粒度的直觉， 常规时间步长表示和学习的片段词汇Symbolic music appears to invite the same treatment. It consists of discrete note events and exhibits repetition, hierarchy, long-range dependency, and reuse. Chords, motifs, phrases, and sections therefore appear analogous to linguistic tokens. Existing symbolic-music tokenizations transfer this intuition at different granularities through event streams, compound tokens, regular time-step representations, and learned segment vocabularies8; 10; 22; 16; 9; 17。这些方法可治疗反复发作的 音乐组合作为候选可重复使用 单位，假设更大或更结构化的单位可以提供更好的 序列建模的接口。. These approaches treat recurring musical combinations as candidate reusable units, assuming that larger or more structured units may provide a better interface for sequence modeling.

Tokenization does not become predictively compressive merely because multiple observations are grouped into a single symbol. Compression emerges when the representation places observable facts in coordinates where their recurring regularities form stable and efficiently predictable conditional distributions for a bounded model.

这种区别揭示了代币的构成存在更深层次的模糊性。 标记不仅由观察结果的分组方式定义，还由位置定义 代表性应该开始，上下文结构应该保留在哪里 未解决。因此，标记化的基本任务是构建 音乐事实在其中变得可预测压缩的坐标系。This distinction reveals a deeper ambiguity in what constitutes a token. A token is not defined only by how observations are grouped, but by where representation should begin and where contextual structure should remain unresolved. The fundamental task of tokenization is therefore to construct the coordinate system in which musical facts become predictively compressible.

这项工作将标记化重新定义为坐标构建问题，而不是 而不是搜索更大或更可重用的符号。有效性-无损 框架通过两个互补的边界形式化了这一观点： 事实-令牌边界决定了哪些可观察信息 确定性规律应该进入表示中。代币状态 边界确定哪些关系必须保持可用于上下文 计算。这些原则共同将代币定义为具有预测效果的代币 坐标载体：将可观察事实转化为 预测坐标，同时保留上下文自由度 需要确定关系。This work reframes tokenization as a coordinate construction problem rather than a search for larger or more reusable symbols. The Effectiveness–Losslessness Framework formalizes this view through two complementary boundaries: The Fact–Token Boundary determines which observable information and deterministic regularities should enter the representation. The Token–State Boundary determines which relations must remain available for contextual computation. Together, these principles define a token as a predictively effective coordinate carrier: a representation that transforms observable facts into predictive coordinates while preserving the contextual degrees of freedom required to determine relations.

象征性音乐验证了这个框架。音乐坐标解除嵌套和 可逆规范化提高了预测可压缩性，同时 无信息的重新参数化和固定的关系投影则不然。 仅序列压缩是不够的，并且保持上下文自由 允许更高阶的组织出现。Symbolic music validates this framework. Sequence compaction alone is insufficient, and preserving contextual freedom allows higher-order organization to emerge.

除了具体的应用之外，该框架还提出了更广泛的 上下文模型的解释。一旦可观察的事实和确定性 规律性通过有效坐标、上下文状态来表示 可以被视为剩余关系不确定性的学习压缩器 这很难明确指定。这种解释并不与 特定的架构。从这个角度来看，基于 Transformer 的模型代表了一种 近似这种上下文压缩的有效机制。Beyond the specific application, the framework suggests a broader interpretation of contextual models. Once observable facts and deterministic regularities are represented through effective coordinates, contextual states can be viewed as learned compressors of the remaining relational uncertainty that is difficult to specify explicitly.
