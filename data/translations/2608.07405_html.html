Yonglong Zhang    Zongwu Xie    Yang Liu E-mail address:liuyanghit@hit.edu.cn [
摘要Abstract

基础分割模型可以在无需手动训练掩模的情况下为航天器图像提供监督，但它们的预测随文本提示而变化，并且可能包含在蒸馏过程中放大的几何误差。本文提出了 GeoDistill-Refine，这是一个两阶段框架，可将离线 SAM 3 伪掩模传输到紧凑的分割网络。六个固定提示通过未加权的 50% 投票融合在一起，以稳定教师的输出。学生首先学习前景轮廓，然后使用从伪掩模导出的符号距离场、骨架和区域目标进行细化。根据提示一致性、有效提示比和伪掩模区域合理性计算得出的样本级门可以减少不可靠伪几何的影响。在 SpaceSense-Bench HJM 密码箱组上，与普通伪标签学生相比，GeoDistill-Refine 将图像 IoU 和边界 F1 分别提高了 0.0456 和 0.1380。对 SPEED+ Lightbox 和 Sunlamp 领域以及 TANGO 的外部评估显示出竞争性的区域重叠以及边界质量或前景精度的提高。部署的 TinyUNet 包含 0.263 M 个参数，在 RTX 4090 上每个图像大约需要 1.1 毫秒； SAM 3 伪掩模构造和辅助几何分支仅在训练期间使用。Foundation segmentation models can provide supervision for spacecraft imagery without manual training masks, but their predictions vary with textual prompts and may contain geometric errors that are amplified during distillation. This paper presents GeoDistill-Refine, a two-stage framework that transfers offline SAM 3 pseudo-masks to a compact segmentation network. Six fixed prompts are fused by an unweighted 50% vote to stabilize the teacher output. The student first learns the foreground silhouette and is then refined with signed-distance-field, skeleton, and area objectives derived from the pseudo-mask. A sample-level gate, computed from prompt agreement, the valid-prompt ratio, and pseudo-mask area plausibility, reduces the influence of unreliable pseudo-geometry. On the SpaceSense-Bench HJM lockbox set, GeoDistill-Refine improves Image IoU and Boundary F1 by 0.0456 and 0.1380, respectively, over a plain pseudo-label student. External evaluations on the SPEED+ Lightbox and Sunlamp domains and on TANGO show competitive regional overlap together with gains in boundary quality or foreground precision. The deployed TinyUNet contains 0.263 M parameters and requires approximately 1.1 ms per image on an RTX 4090; SAM 3 pseudo-mask construction and the auxiliary geometry branches are used only during training.

I 简介Introduction

在轨服务、主动碎片清除以及与非合作目标的交会需要航天器具有强大的视觉感知能力。前景分割为目标定位、相对姿态估计和三维重建提供像素级空间约束On-orbit servicing, active debris removal, and rendezvous with non-cooperative targets require robust visual perception of spacecraft. Foreground segmentation provides pixel-level spatial constraints for target localization, relative pose estimation, and three-dimensional reconstruction[1, 2, 3]。然而，分割以前未见过的航天器仍然很困难，因为照明、背景、表观比例和结构配置差异很大。 SPEED+、TANGO 和 SpaceSense-Bench 报告的结果表明，与照明域移动和目标几何形状变化相关的性能下降. Segmenting previously unseen spacecraft nevertheless remains difficult because illumination, background, apparent scale, and structural configuration vary substantially. Results reported on SPEED+, TANGO, and SpaceSense-Bench have shown the performance degradation associated with illumination-domain shifts and changes in target geometry[4, 5, 6].

大多数现有方法依赖于手动注释的像素。这种注释的成本很高，因为真正的在轨图像很少，而且航天器通常包含低对比度边界和细长的附属物。模拟提供了廉价的标签，但无法完全再现真实的材料、照明或背景分布。大型基础模型提供了不同的监督途径，尽管它们的内存和计算要求使得直接在船上部署不切实际。Most existing approaches depend on manually annotated pixels. Such annotations are costly because real on-orbit imagery is scarce and spacecraft often contain low-contrast boundaries and slender appendages. Simulation provides inexpensive labels but cannot fully reproduce real materials, illumination, or background distributions. Large foundation models offer a different route to supervision, although their memory and computational requirements make direct onboard deployment impractical.

开放词汇检测器和提示分割模型可以根据文本提示为未标记图像生成对象框或掩模Open-vocabulary detectors and promptable segmentation models can generate object boxes or masks for unlabeled images from textual prompts[7, 8, 9]。最近的航天器研究证明了自动掩模生成、伪标签蒸馏到紧凑学生以及结构化提示. Recent spacecraft studies have demonstrated automatic mask generation, pseudo-label distillation into compact students, and structured prompting[10, 11, 12]。然而，由此产生的教师预测仍然对措辞、目标规模和局部结构敏感。现有的伪标签处理通常依赖于增强一致性、框融合或置信度过滤，对边界、连接性和区域错误的处理有限。距离场监督可以改善航天器边界建模. The resulting teacher predictions, however, remain sensitive to wording, target scale, and local structure. Existing pseudo-label processing commonly relies on augmentation consistency, box fusion, or confidence filtering, with limited treatment of boundary, connectivity, and area errors. Distance-field supervision can improve spacecraft boundary modeling[13]，但是直接从噪声伪掩模导出的密集几何目标可能会传播并在空间上放大教师误差。, but dense geometric targets derived directly from noisy pseudo-masks may propagate and spatially amplify teacher errors.

本文介绍了 GeoDistill-Refine，这是一种用于紧凑型航天器分割的无注释训练框架。冻结的 SAM 3 教师首先通过多提示共识生成离线伪掩码。然后，学生学习主要轮廓，然后使用来自相应检查点的可靠性门控伪几何进行细化。部署时仅保留单帧 RGB 学生。这里的无标注特指学生优化时无需手动蒙版；验证注释仍然用于检查点选择和阈值校准。This paper introduces GeoDistill-Refine, an annotation-free training framework for compact spacecraft segmentation. A frozen SAM 3 teacher first generates offline pseudo-masks through multi-prompt consensus. The student then learns the primary silhouette before being refined with reliability-gated pseudo-geometry from the corresponding checkpoint. Only the single-frame RGB student is retained at deployment. Here, annotation-free refers specifically to student optimization without manual masks; validation annotations are still used for checkpoint selection and threshold calibration.

SpaceSense-Bench 用于评估对看不见的航天器的概括。相反，SPEED+ 和 TANGO 在具有挑战性的照明和不同图像分布下提供域内蒸馏的外部验证；它们并不被用来声称对看不见的航天器进行了概括。在保留的 HJM 密码箱集上，与普通伪标签学生相比，GeoDistill-Refine 将图像 IoU 和边界 F1 分别提高了 0.0456 和 0.1380。消融结果进一步支持在评估的学生和损失配置下的轮廓优先热启动和可靠性门控符号距离场（SDF）细化。SpaceSense-Bench is used to assess generalization to unseen spacecraft. SPEED+ and TANGO instead provide external validation of in-domain distillation under challenging illumination and different image distributions; they are not used to claim unseen-spacecraft generalization. On the held-out HJM lockbox set, GeoDistill-Refine improves Image IoU and Boundary F1 by 0.0456 and 0.1380, respectively, over a plain pseudo-label student. Ablation results further support the silhouette-first warm start and reliability-gated signed distance field (SDF) refinement under the evaluated student and loss configuration.

It reduces the contribution of anomalous pseudo-geometry while retaining the majority-voted mask supervision for every training sample.

II 相关工作Related Work

A 航天器分割和对未见过目标的概括Spacecraft Segmentation and Generalization to Unseen Targets

视觉航天器感知包括目标检测、组件分割和相对姿态估计。最初的 SPEED 挑战赛建立了一个通用的合成真实基准，并记录了区分星载视觉与地面感知的数据稀缺性和领域差距Visual spacecraft perception encompasses target detection, component segmentation, and relative pose estimation. The original SPEED challenge established a common synthetic-to-real benchmark and documented the data scarcity and domain gap that distinguish spaceborne vision from terrestrial perception[14]。 SPEED+ 和 TANGO 随后提供了合成图像或硬件在环图像，用于研究照明变化和成像域间隙. SPEED+ and TANGO subsequently provided synthetic or hardware-in-the-loop imagery for studying illumination changes and imaging-domain gaps[4, 5]，而航天器部件数据集支持检测和语义分割基准, while spacecraft-part datasets support detection and semantic segmentation benchmarks[2]。 SpaceSense-Bench 扩大了航天器和传感模式的范围，并使用航天器不相交协议来强调看不见的目标和小型结构的难度. SpaceSense-Bench broadens the range of spacecraft and sensing modalities and uses a spacecraft-disjoint protocol to emphasize the difficulty of unseen targets and small structures[6].

许多分割系统将编码器-解码器架构与轻量级主干相结合，以平衡轮廓恢复和计算成本Many segmentation systems combine encoder–decoder architectures with lightweight backbones to balance contour recovery and computational cost[15, 16, 3]。尽管如此，大多数都需要手动标记并关注已知目标或成像域转移。在没有训练面罩的情况下对以前未见过的航天器结构的适应仍然没有得到充分的研究。. Most nevertheless require manual labels and focus on known targets or imaging-domain transfer. Adaptation to previously unseen spacecraft structures in the absence of training masks remains less thoroughly studied.

B 基础模型伪标签和无注释蒸馏Foundation-Model Pseudo-Labels and Annotation-Free Distillation

伪标记、自我训练和知识提炼为利用未标记图像提供了既定机制Pseudo-labeling, self-training, and knowledge distillation provide established mechanisms for exploiting unlabeled imagery[17, 18, 19]。 Mean Teacher 和 FixMatch 通过时间平均或增强一致性来稳定监督. Mean Teacher and FixMatch stabilize supervision through temporal averaging or augmentation consistency[20, 21];交叉伪监督、ST++ 和 U2PL 等分割方法进一步使用相互预测、检查点稳定性或低置信度证据; segmentation methods such as Cross Pseudo Supervision, ST++, and U2PL further use mutual prediction, checkpoint stability, or low-confidence evidence[22, 23, 24]。这些方法确立了伪标签可靠性的重要性，但通常仍然是半监督的，并根据模型置信度或训练时间一致性来估计质量。. These methods establish the importance of pseudo-label reliability, but generally remain semi-supervised and estimate quality from model confidence or training-time consistency.

Grounding DINO、SAM、SAM 3 和 SEEM 可以直接根据文字和视觉提示生成盒子或面具Grounding DINO, SAM, SAM 3, and SEEM can produce boxes or masks directly from textual and visual prompts[7, 8, 9, 25]，减少对特定任务教师的需求。然而，他们的冻结预测随着措辞和提示条件的不同而变化。一项预测的置信度分数既不能衡量有效描述的一致性，也不能暴露多个提示所共有的错误。, reducing the need for a task-specific teacher. Their frozen predictions nevertheless vary with wording and prompt conditions. A confidence score for one prediction neither measures agreement across valid descriptions nor exposes errors shared by several prompts.

在航天器视觉中，基础模型支持自动注释、轻量级学生蒸馏和提示驱动的能力扩展In spacecraft vision, foundation models have supported automatic annotation, lightweight student distillation, and prompt-driven capability extension[10, 11, 12]。现有研究强调注释生成、候选融合或迭代蒸馏；提示间的分歧和从伪掩模导出的几何的可靠性仍然很少被探讨。 GeoDistill-Refine 使用固定的多提示共识，同时保留其用于伪几何监督的可靠性估计，将提示稳定与异常几何的处理分开。. Existing studies emphasize annotation generation, candidate fusion, or iterative distillation; inter-prompt disagreement and the reliability of geometry derived from pseudo-masks remain less explored. GeoDistill-Refine uses fixed multi-prompt consensus while reserving its reliability estimate for pseudo-geometric supervision, separating prompt stabilization from the treatment of anomalous geometry.

C 边界建模和距离场监控Boundary Modeling and Distance-Field Supervision

边界感知分割通过明确的轮廓或拓扑线索补充区域目标。 Gated-SCNN 维护专用的形状流、距离变换和边界损失对参考轮廓的位移进行编码，并且 clDice 的目标是薄结构的连接性Boundary-aware segmentation complements region objectives with explicit contour or topology cues. Gated-SCNN maintains a dedicated shape stream, distance-transform and boundary losses encode displacement from reference contours, and clDice targets the connectivity of thin structures[26, 27, 28, 29, 30]。这些方法将监督扩展到像素重叠​​之外，但它们的几何目标通常源自手动掩模。. These approaches extend supervision beyond pixelwise overlap, but their geometric targets are typically derived from manual masks.

航天器分割的相关想法也得到了探索。 GABI 将距离信息与边界特征相结合，用于困难成像条件下的轮廓恢复Related ideas have also been explored for spacecraft segmentation. GABI combines distance information with boundary features for contour recovery under difficult imaging conditions[13]。无注释蒸馏改变了基本假设，因为伪掩模中的错误会传播到其 SDF、骨架和区域目标中。因此，在不考虑目标可靠性的情况下应用此类目标可能会放大教师的错误。 GeoDistill-Refine 通过在轮廓学习后引入伪几何监督并使用样本级可靠性门对其进行调制来解决这一差距。. Annotation-free distillation changes the underlying assumption because errors in a pseudo-mask propagate into its SDF, skeleton, and area targets. Applying such objectives without accounting for target reliability can therefore amplify teacher errors. GeoDistill-Refine addresses this gap by introducing pseudo-geometric supervision after silhouette learning and modulating it with a sample-level reliability gate.

V 结论Conclusion

GeoDistill-Refine 将离线多提示 SAM 3 伪掩模提取到紧凑型航天器前景分割器中，无需使用手动掩模进行学生更新。其两阶段计划首先学习前景轮廓，然后引入可靠性门控 SDF、骨架和区域监督。在预先保留的 HJM 密码箱集上，该设计比 Plain Student 将图像 IoU 和边界 F1 提高了 0.0456 和 0.1380。 KCP 消融表明轮廓优先热启动和门控 SDF 解释了当前 TinyUNet 配置下观察到的大部分细化优势。 SPEED+ 和 TANGO 为困难的实验室图像提供了补充证据，其中质量自适应配置提高了边界质量或前景精度，但每个指标都没有超过外部再现基线。GeoDistill-Refine distills offline multi-prompt SAM 3 pseudo-masks into a compact spacecraft foreground segmenter without using manual masks for student updates. Its two-stage schedule first learns the foreground silhouette and then introduces reliability-gated SDF, skeleton, and area supervision. On the pre-reserved HJM lockbox set, this design improves Image IoU and Boundary F1 by 0.0456 and 0.1380 over Plain Student. The KCP ablations suggest that the silhouette-first warm start and gated SDF account for most of the observed refinement benefit under the current TinyUNet configuration. SPEED+ and TANGO provide complementary evidence on difficult laboratory imagery, where the quality-adaptive configuration improves boundary quality or foreground precision but does not exceed the external reproduction baseline on every metric.

部署的模型包含大约 0.263 M 个参数，在 RTX 4090 上每个图像需要大约 1.1 毫秒；教师和辅助几何头不参与推理。证据仅限于二进制分割，并带有用于检查点选择和阈值校准的验证掩码。只有 SpaceSense-Bench 支持关于看不见的航天器身份的结论，并且骨架和区域目标的独立贡献仍未解决。The deployed model contains approximately 0.263 M parameters and requires about 1.1 ms per image on an RTX 4090; the teacher and auxiliary geometry heads are absent from inference. The evidence is limited to binary segmentation with validation masks used for checkpoint selection and threshold calibration. Only SpaceSense-Bench supports conclusions about unseen spacecraft identities, and the independent contributions of the skeleton and area objectives remain unresolved.
