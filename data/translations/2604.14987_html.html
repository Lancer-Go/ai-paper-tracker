摘要Abstract —无线芯片中的隐蔽通道 (CC) 构成了严重的安全威胁，因为它们可以将敏感信息从芯片泄露给外部攻击者。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种部署在射频接收器上的基于人工智能的防御机制，其中模型直接监视原始 I/Q 样本，以实时检测嵌入在其他标称信号中的 CC 的存在。我们首先压缩最先进的卷积神经网络（CNN），实现参数减少 80%，这是高效边缘部署的基本要求。在基于开源硬件特洛伊木马 (HT) 的 CC 数据集上进行评估时，压缩的 CNN 的 CC 检测平均准确率为 90.28%，识别底层 HT 的平均准确率为 86.50%，SNR 值的平均结果高于 1 dB。对于SNR的实际通信场景—Covert channels (CCs) in wireless chips pose a serious security threat, as they enable the exfiltration of sensitive information from the chip to an external attacker. In this work, we propose an AI-based defense mechanism deployed at the RF receiver, where the model directly monitors raw I/Q samples to detect, in real time, the presence of a CC embedded within an otherwise nominal signal. We first compact a state-of-the-art convolutional neural network (CNN), achieving an 80% reduction in parameters, which is an essential requirement for efficient edge deployment. When evaluated on the open-source hardware Trojan (HT)-based CC dataset, the compacted CNN attains an average accuracy of 90.28% for CC detection and 86.50% for identifying the underlying HT, with results averaged across SNR values above 1 dB. For practical communication scenarios where SNR > 20 dB，该模型在这两项任务上的准确率均超过 97%。与基线模型相比，这些结果对应于小于 2% 的最小性能下降。压缩后的 CNN 进一步针对替代分类器进行了基准测试，展示了出色的精度与模型大小权衡。最后，我们设计了一个轻量级的CNN硬件加速器，并在FPGA上进行了演示，实现了非常低的资源利用率和107 GOPs/W的效率。作为首款专为 CC 检测而提出的 AI 硬件加速器，我们将其与用于调制识别等射频信号分类任务的最先进的 AI 加速器进行了比较，显示出卓越的性能。20 dB, the model achieves over 97% accuracy for both tasks. These results correspond to a minimal performance degradation of less than 2% compared to the baseline model. The compacted CNN is further benchmarked against alternative classifiers, demonstrating an excellent accuracy-model size tradeoff. Finally, we design a lightweight CNN hardware accelerator and demonstrate it on an FPGA, achieving very low resource utilization and an efficiency of 107 GOPs/W. Being the first AI hardware accelerator proposed specifically for CC detection, we compare it against state-of-the-art AI accelerators for RF signal classification tasks such as modulation recognition, showing superior performance.

一、简介I. INTRODUCTION

隐蔽通道 (CC) 对无线芯片来说是一个重大的安全威胁。 CC 是指通过 RF 发射器泄露敏感信息的隐秘通信通道，例如加密密钥、加密操作、配置状态等。最近的工作甚至证明整个 AI 模型都可以通过 CC 泄露[1]。一般来说，攻击的工作方式是将被盗信息的比特路由到 CC 创建机制，CC 创建机制负责将它们嵌入到 RF 传输中。图 1 说明了这种威胁模型。 RF 发射器（又名 Alice）受到 CC 创建机制的损害，该机制将敏感信息隐藏在合法信号中。与 Alice（又名 Bob）通信的标称 RF 接收器并不显眼，并且不知道信息泄漏。流氓 RF 接收器或窃听者（又名 Eve）拦截传输，并了解 CC 的运行方式，恢复泄漏的比特。例如，获得密钥随后将允许 Eve 解密通信。泄露的数据被称为Covert Channels (CCs) represent a significant security threat for wireless chips. A CC refers to a stealthy communication channel that leaks sensitive information via the RF transmitter, such as encryption keys, cryptographic operations, configuration states, etc. Recent work has even demonstrated that entire AI models can be exfiltrated through CCs [1]. In general, the attack works by routing the bits of the stolen information to the CC creation mechanism, which is responsible for embedding them into the RF transmission. Fig. 1 illustrates this threat model. The RF transmitter, a.k.a. Alice, is compromised with a CC creation mechanism that conceals sensitive information within an otherwise legitimate signal. The nominal RF receiver communicating with Alice, a.k.a. Bob, is inconspicuous and unaware of information leakage. A rogue RF receiver or eavesdropper, a.k.a. Eve, intercepts the transmission and, knowing how the CC operates, recovers the leaked bits. For instance, obtaining the cipher key would subsequently allow Eve to decrypt the communication. CC 创建机制本身可以被视为硬件木马 (HT)，这就是我们在这项工作中使用术语 HTbased CC (HT-CC) 的原因。 HT 代表了更广泛的威胁模型，包括对硬件的任何恶意修改 [2]–[8]。一般来说，HT 由两个主要组件组成：触发器和负载。触发可以是随机的、受控的或始终开启的，而有效负载除了信息泄漏之外，还可能执行性能下降甚至拒绝服务等操作。除了触发器和有效负载类型之外，HT 还可以根据其在集成电路 (IC) 供应链中的插入阶段（例如，在电子设计自动化 (EDA) 工具使用、设计、将第三方 IP 模块集成到片上系统 (SoC) 或制造过程中）、其插入级别（例如寄存器传输级 (RTL)、门级、晶体管级或布局）及其在芯片上的位置（例如存储器、处理器、电源管理单元、模拟和器件）进行分类。例如，在射频收发器中，数模攻击 [9]、[10] 是一种可能导致性能下降或拒绝服务的威胁。在这种情况下，SoC 数字 IP 块内的隐藏触发器通过共享测试基础设施向 RF 收发器传送恶意数字字，从而修改其配置。这种类型的攻击可以在运行时使用集成到射频收发器中的轻量级片上监视器来检测[11]。The CC creation mechanism can itself be viewed as a Hardware Trojan (HT), which is why we use the term HTbased CC (HT-CC) in this work. HTs represent a broader threat model, encompassing any malicious modification of hardware [2]–[8]. In general, a HT consists of two main components: a trigger and a payload. The trigger can be random, controlled, or always-on, while the payload, besides information leakage, may perform actions such as performance degradation or even denial of service. Beyond trigger and payload types, HTs can be classified by their insertion phase in the Integrated Circuit (IC) supply chain, e.g., during Electronic Design Automation (EDA) tools usage, design, integration of thirdparty IP blocks into a System-on-Chip (SoC), or fabrication, their insertion level, e.g., Register-Transfer-Level (RTL), gatelevel, transistor-level, or layout, and their location on die, e.g., memory, processor, power management unit, analog and mixed-signal blocks, etc. For instance, in RF transceivers, a digital-to-analog attack [9], [10] is a plausible threat for inducing performance degradation or denial-of-service. In this scenario, a hidden trigger within a digital IP block of the SoC delivers a malicious digital word through the shared test infrastructure to the RF transceiver, thereby modifying its configuration. This type of attack can be detected at run time using lightweight on-chip monitors integrated into the RF transceiver [11].

许多作品展示了用于 RF 收发器的 HT-CC 的不同实现方式 [12]–[25]。同时，这些工作提出了防御策略，以在零时间使用测试或在系统运行时在线识别 HT-CC。提供了对现有 HT-CC 设计及其相应防御的更详细检查Numerous works have showcased different implementations of HT-CCs for RF transceivers [12]–[25]. Simultaneously, these works propose defense strategies to identify the HT-CC either at time zero using testing or on-line at run-time while the system is operating. In addition, we discuss two further categories: HT-CC prevention methods that impede the feasibility of the HT-CC attack, and generic HT defenses that apply to any HT threat and either detect the underlying HT mechanism at pre-silicon or post-silicon or prevent HT insertion.

1) Non-AI HT-CC detection defenses:这些防御可以分为两个子类别：测试时的标准测量，用于验证是否符合通信协议，以及运行时的专门技术，旨在检测 HT 相关活动。These defenses can be divided into two sub-categories: standard measurements at test time that verify compliance with the communication protocol, and specialized techniques at run-time designed to detect HT-related activity.

标准测量包括评估信噪比 (SNR)、误差矢量幅度 (EVM)、误码率 (BER)、检查是否符合频谱模板规范以及分析 I/Q 星座图。Standard measurements include evaluating Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), Error Vector Magnitude (EVM), Bit Error Rate (BER), checking adherence to spectral mask specifications, and analyzing I/Q constellation diagrams.

运行时的专业技术包括自适应信道估计（ACE）[22]、信道噪声分析[17]和不变的侧信道指纹识别[28]。 ACE 防御利用室内通信信道的慢衰落特性来区分信道损伤和 HT 活动。 Channel noise profiling involves characterizing the distribution of noise present in the communication channel. The goal is to identify any unexpected systematic components that could potentially be attributed to HT activity.不变侧通道指纹识别包括在电源上创建不变指纹并在正常操作的同时不断检查其一致性，其中不合规性意味着潜在的 HT 活动。Specialized techniques at run-time include Adaptive Channel Estimation (ACE) [22], channel noise profiling [17], and invariant side-channel fingerprinting [28]. The ACE defense leverages the slow-fading characteristics of indoor communication channels to distinguish between channel impairments and HT activity. Channel noise profiling involves characterizing the distribution of noise present in the communication channel. The goal is to identify any unexpected systematic components that could potentially be attributed to HT activity. Invariant side-channel fingerprinting consists of creating an invariant fingerprint on the power supply and continually checking its conformity concurrently with the normal operation, where non-compliance implies potential HT activity.

2) AI-based HT-CC detection:上面讨论的非 AI HT-CC 检测防御通常过于复杂，无法在运行时系统地应用在标称 RF 接收器中，因为这样做会增加设计工作量、复杂性和成本。 Most of these approaches also operate offline. They require prior data collection and off-chip processing, leading to CC detection latency.而且，每种方法通常只针对特定的HT-CC攻击模型，这意味着必须结合多种防御才能覆盖所有已知的HT-CC攻击类型并提供最大的安全性，这进一步增加了总体防御成本。The non-AI HT-CC detection defenses discussed above are generally too complex to be applied systematically at run time in nominal RF receivers, as doing so would increase design effort, complexity, and cost. Most of these approaches also operate offline. They require prior data collection and off-chip processing, leading to CC detection latency. Moreover, each method typically targets only specific HT-CC attack models, meaning that multiple defenses must be combined to cover all known HT-CC attack types and provide maximum security, which further increases overall defense cost.

四．具有基于 AI 的 HT-CC 检测的射频收发器架构IV. RF TRANSCEIVER ARCHITECTURE WITH AI-BASED HT-CC DETECTION

基于人工智能的 HT-CC 检测可作为运行时、硅后防御。由于它直接检测原始接收传输帧中由 CC 活动引起的异常，因此它提供了一种通用解决方案，能够解决各种现有 HT-CC 攻击模型、其日益复杂的问题，甚至未知或未记录的变体。基于人工智能的 HT-CC 并没有像第二部分所述那样依赖多种对策组合，而是提供了统一的最终防线。AI-based HT-CC detection operates as a run-time, postsilicon defense. Because it detects abnormalities directly in raw received transmission frames caused by CC activity, it offers a generic solution capable of addressing the wide range of existing HT-CC attack models, their growing sophistication, and even unknown or undocumented variants. Rather than relying on multiple combinations of countermeasures, as outlined in Section II, AI-based HT-CC provides a unified final line of defense.

在[26]中，重点放在设计CNN模型上，但该工作没有解决模型应该驻留在哪里以及应该执行推理的问题。我们确定了执行 CNN 推理的三个可能位置：在受 HT 感染的 RF 发射器 (Alice) 上、在云中（需要 Alice 或标称 RF 接收器 (Bob) 向上游转发信号）或在 Bob 本身上（这需要在片上集成 CNN 硬件加速器）。In [26], the emphasis is placed on designing the CNN model, but the work does not address where the model should reside and inference should be performed. We identify three possible locations for executing CNN inference: on the HTinfected RF transmitter (Alice), in the cloud which would require Alice or the nominal RF receiver (Bob) to forward the signal upstream, or on Bob itself, which would necessitate integrating a CNN hardware accelerator on-chip.

将 CNN 模型放在 Alice 上并不是一个安全的选择，因为插入 HT 的同一个复杂攻击者也可能篡改本地 CNN 推断，以抑制任何指示 CC 活动的警报。Placing the CNN model on Alice is not a secure option, since the same sophisticated attacker who inserted the HT could also tamper with the local CNN inference to suppress any alerts indicating CC activity.

九．结论IX. CONCLUSION

我们提出了一种轻量级 810 mW 107 GOPs/W CNN 硬件加速器，用于片上实时 CC 检测。加速器直接在 RF 接收器 ADC 输出处处理原始 I/Q 样本，编码为图像，并与 RF 接收器一起高速运行。为了实现高效的边缘部署，我们首先将[26]中最先进的 CNN 模型压缩了 80%，精度仅略有下降，但在感兴趣的 SNR 范围内仍然实现了超过 96% 的 CC 检测精度和正确的 HT 类型分类。我们通过将 CNN 与替代分类器架构进行基准测试，进一步证明了选择 CNN 来完成此任务的合理性，证明了模型大小和准确性之间的卓越权衡。所有分类器均使用[26]中硬件生成的 HT-CC 数据集进行训练，该数据集包含所有主要 CC 类型，从而证明所提出的策略可实现统一的、独立于 CC 类型的基于 AI 的检测解决方案，这与需要针对各个 CC 类型量身定制的多种专用对策的方法形成鲜明对比。We proposed a lightweight 810 mW 107 GOPs/W CNN hardware accelerator for on-chip real-time CC detection. The accelerator processes raw I/Q samples directly at the RF receiver ADC output, encoded as images, and operates at speed with the RF receiver. To enable efficient edge deployment, we first compressed the state-of-the-art CNN model in [26] by 80% with only a minor accuracy degradation, still achieving over 96% CC detection accuracy and correct HT type classification within the SNR range of interest. We further justified the choice of a CNN for this task by benchmarking it against alternative classifier architectures, demonstrating a superior trade-off between model size and accuracy. All classifiers were trained using the hardware-generated HT-CC dataset from [26], which encompasses all major CC types, thereby demonstrating that the proposed strategy enables a unified, CC type-independent AI-based detection solution, in contrast to approaches requiring multiple dedicated countermeasures tailored to individual CC types.
