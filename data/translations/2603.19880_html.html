摘要Abstract

测试时强化学习 (TTRL) 使大型语言模型 (LLM) 能够通过从多数投票共识中获得伪奖励来增强未标记测试流的推理能力。 然而，现有的 TTRL 方法完全依赖于正伪标记策略。 在答案分布高度分散的挑战性场景下，这种依赖变得脆弱，导致共识薄弱，无意中将不正确的轨迹强化为监督信号。 在本文中，我们提出了 SCRL（选择性互补强化学习），这是一种强大的测试时强化学习框架，可以有效减轻标签噪声放大。 SCRL 开发了选择性阳性伪标签，它执行严格的共识标准来过滤不可靠的多数。 作为补充，SCRL 引入了熵门负伪标签，这是 TTRL 中的第一个负监督机制，可以根据生成的不确定性可靠地修剪不正确的轨迹。 对多个推理基准的广泛实验表明，SCRL 相对于基线实现了实质性改进，同时在有限的部署预算下保持了强大的泛化性和训练稳定性。 我们的代码位于Test-Time Reinforcement Learning (TTRL) enables Large Language Models (LLMs) to enhance reasoning capabilities on unlabeled test streams by deriving pseudo-rewards from majority voting consensus. However, existing TTRL methods rely exclusively on positive pseudo-labeling strategies. Such reliance becomes vulnerable under challenging scenarios where answer distributions are highly dispersed, resulting in weak consensus that inadvertently reinforces incorrect trajectories as supervision signals. In this paper, we propose SCRL (Selective-Complementary Reinforcement Learning), a robust test-time reinforcement learning framework that effectively mitigates label noise amplification. SCRL develops Selective Positive Pseudo-Labeling, which enforces strict consensus criteria to filter unreliable majorities. Complementarily, SCRL introduces Entropy-Gated Negative Pseudo-Labeling, the first negative supervision mechanism in TTRL, to reliably prune incorrect trajectories based on generation uncertainty. Extensive experiments on multiple reasoning benchmarks demonstrate that SCRL achieves substantial improvements over baselines, while maintaining robust generalization and training stability under constrained rollout budgets. To bridge this gap, Test-Time Reinforcement Learning (TTRL) has emerged as a critical paradigm for unsupervised reasoning(Zuo et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025b; Jayalath et al., 2025; Yuan et al., 2025)。 TTRL 允许模型通过生成多样化的部署并利用多数投票共识来获得策略更新的伪奖励，从而在未标记的测试流上进行自我改进。. 6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 SCRL，一种用于测试时强化学习的新颖框架，以减轻无监督环境中的标签噪声放大。 SCRL 集成了选择性正伪标签（它执行严格的共识标准，以防止在弱共识下强化不可靠的多数）和熵门负伪标签（测试时强化学习中的第一个负监督机制），它通过隔离低频和高不确定性的答案来可靠地修剪不可信的解决方案。这种双重机制确保模型增强高置信度轨迹，同时可靠地识别和惩罚错误答案，而不会丢弃罕见但可能正确的轨迹。实证结果证实 SCRL 显着优于基线，提供强大的泛化能力和训练稳定性。In this work, we propose SCRL, a novel framework for test-time reinforcement learning to mitigate label noise amplification in unsupervised settings. SCRL integrates Selective Positive Pseudo-Labeling, which enforces strict consensus criteria to prevent reinforcing unreliable majorities under weak consensus, with Entropy-Gated Negative Pseudo-Labeling, the first negative supervision mechanism in test-time reinforcement learning, which reliably prunes implausible solutions by isolating answers with both low frequency and high uncertainty. This dual mechanism ensures that the model reinforces high-confidence trajectories while reliably identifying and penalizing incorrect answers, without discarding rare but potentially correct trajectories. Empirical results confirm that SCRL significantly outperforms baselines, delivering robust generalization and training stability.
