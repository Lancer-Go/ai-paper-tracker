1 简介Introduction

统一多模式模型 (UMM) 的最新进展 专注于设计能够无缝集成视觉理解和生成的统一架构Recent advancements in Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) focus on designing unified architectures capable of seamlessly integrating visual understanding and generation(chameleon; emu3; showo; illume; niu2025does; illume+; bagel; xie2025reconstruction)，标志着从纯 AR 到混合 AR-Diffusion 架构的明显转变。 与专业型号不同， 这些统一模型具有处理交错多模态输入和输出的固有能力。 至关重要的是，这种结构上的统一使他们有可能在单个端到端模型中协调复杂的认知工作流程，包括推理、执行、自我反思和完善。 然而，有效强化 UMM 以利用其内在理解来改善多模式生成仍然是一个很大程度上尚未探索的挑战。, marking a distinct shift from pure AR to hybrid AR-Diffusion architectures. Distinct from specialized models, these unified models possess the innate capability to process interleaved multimodal inputs and outputs. Crucially, this structural unification endows them with the potential to orchestrate complex cognitive workflows within a single end-to-end model, encompassing reasoning, execution, self-reflection, and refinement. However, effectively reinforcing UMMs to leverage their intrinsic understanding to improve multimodal generation remains a largely unexplored challenge.

强化学习 (RL)，特别是组相对策略优化 (GRPO)Reinforcement Learning (RL), notably Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)(deepseekmath), 在加强法学硕士的推理能力方面取得了显着的成功, has demonstrated remarkable success in reinforcing reasoning capabilities in LLMs(deepseekmath; deepseekr1)并优化流量匹配扩散模型中的视觉生成and optimizing visual generation in flow-matching diffusion models(flowgrpo; dancegrpo)。 为了实现复杂的任务，例如推理文本到图像的生成或自我反思的细化，最近的工作. To enable complex tasks like reasoning text-to-image generation or self-reflective refinement, recent works(bagel; omnigen2; huang2025irg)主要依靠专有模型来合成高质量数据。 尽管有效，但这种范式不可避免地引入了额外的冷启动 SFT 阶段，这意味着性能提升可能源于更强大的教师模型的提炼。 相比之下，我们认为，由于统一模型已经通过大规模预训练获取了基本原语和隐式推理相关数据，因此可以使用强化学习来激活和增强这些休眠能力，而无需冷启动阶段。primarily rely on proprietary models to synthesize high-quality data. Although effective, this paradigm inevitably introduces an additional cold-start SFT stage, implying that the performance gains might stem from the distillation of the stronger teacher models. In contrast, we argue that since unified models already acquire fundamental primitives and implicit reasoning-related data through large-scale pretraining, it is possible to activate and enhance these dormant capabilities using RL without the cold-start stage.

在多模式生成中应用 GRPO 的成功依赖于产生稳定、稳健信号的奖励模型。 为了增强广泛的、现实世界的多模式生成能力，需要这样的奖励模型 准确评估不同的现实世界样本。 然而，当前的视觉生成强化学习经常忽视这一点，追求训练相关指标的高分The success of applying GRPO in multimodal generation relies on a reward model yielding stable, robust signals. To enhance broad, real-world multimodal generation capabilities, such a reward model is required to accurately assess diverse real-world samples. However, current visual generation RL often overlooks this, chasing high scores on training-aligned metrics(flowgrpo; dancegrpo)。 这可能会带来过度拟合的风险，并且无法保证不同下游基准的持续改进。 在追求通用评估器的过程中，多模态大型语言模型（MLLM）因其强大的理解能力和广泛的世界知识而成为首要候选者。 在人类偏好数据集上微调这些模型可以产生具有更高对准精度的专门奖励模型. This risks reward overfitting and fails to guarantee consistent improvements across diverse downstream benchmarks. In the pursuit of a universal evaluator, Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have emerged as the premier candidates, due to their robust understanding capabilities and extensive world knowledge. Fine-tuning these models on human preference datasets can yield specialized reward models with improved alignment accuracy(unifiedreward; hpsv3; llavacritic)。 然而，它将模型的分布转向有限域，并隐含地缩小了 MLLM 处理开放世界样本的能力。 因此，在不损害其固有理解的情况下探索来自通用 MLLM 的稳定、高质量的奖励信号变得至关重要。. However, it shifts the model’s distribution towards a limited domain and implicitly narrows the MLLM’s capacity to handle open-world samples. Therefore, it becomes crucial to explore the stable, high-quality reward signals from general MLLMs without compromising their inherent understanding.

在本文中，我们提出了 AlphaGRPO，这是一种将 GRPO 扩展到 AR-Diffusion UMM 中的多模态生成的新颖框架。 它通过释放模型的内在潜力来增强统一的多模式理解和生成能力，而无需额外的冷启动阶段。 具体来说，我们将多模态生成制定为首先生成文本，然后生成图像的统一轨迹。 我们专注于自我反思的细化，这需要从初始生成结果中自主诊断偏差并执行纠正策略。这个过程需要能力的全面协同，包括多模态感知、理解和生成。我们引入假阳性纠正来消除训练期间的错误改进信号。此外，我们将 AlphaGRPO 应用于推理文本到图像的生成，以验证 AlphaGRPO 在不同多模态任务中的通用性和鲁棒性。 为了确保可靠的奖励信号并提高现实场景中的鲁棒性，我们引入了可分解可验证奖励（DVReward）。该机制利用 LLM 将复杂的用户请求分解为原子的、可验证的问题，并使用 MLLM 置信度分数根据生成的视觉内容验证它们。In this paper, we propose AlphaGRPO, a novel framework that extends GRPO to multimodal generation in AR-Diffusion UMM. It enhances unified multimodal understanding and generation capabilities by unlocking the model’s intrinsic potential, without an additional cold-start stage. Specifically, we formulate multimodal generation as the unified trajectory that first generates text, then the image. We focus on the self-reflective refinement, which requires autonomously diagnosing misalignments from the initial generation results and executing correction strategies. This process demands a comprehensive synergy of capabilities, including multimodal perception, understanding, and generation. We introduce the False-Positive Rectification to eliminate the false improvement signals during training. Furthermore, we apply AlphaGRPO to reasoning text-to-image generation to validate the generalizability and robustness of AlphaGRPO across diverse multimodal tasks. To ensure reliable reward signals and promote robustness in real-world scenarios, we introduce the Decompositional Verifiable Reward (DVReward). This mechanism utilizes an LLM to decompose complex user requests into atomic, verifiable questions and verify them against the generated visual content using MLLM confidence scores.

2 试点研究Pilot study

在详细介绍我们的方法之前，我们进行了一项试点研究，以调查协调统一多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 所必需的两个基本前提： (1) 预训练的 UMM 是否具有自我反思细化所需的潜在推理模式以及如何激活它，以及 (2) 当前的 MLLM 是否可以提供可靠的、有区别的奖励信号来评估开放世界场景中的视觉生成。Before detailing our methodology, we conducted a pilot study to investigate two fundamental premises essential for aligning unified Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs): (1) whether pretrained UMMs possess the latent reasoning patterns required for self-reflective refinement and how to activate this, and (2) whether current MLLMs can provide reliable, discriminative reward signals to evaluate visual generation in open-world scenarios.

明确的错误寻求激活潜在的推理。Explicit error-seeking activates latent reasoning.为了探索如何激活潜在推理，我们探索了最先进的 UMM Bagel，它有两个任务：验证，模型判断生成的图像和用户提示之间是否存在错位，以及反射，模型被告知图像包含错误并要求对其进行诊断。 我们的实验揭示了验证中的一个严重失败：如图所示To explore how to activate latent reasoning, we probe the state-of-the-art UMM, Bagel, with two tasks: Verification, where the model judges whether misalignments exist between the generated image and the user prompt, and Reflection, where the model is told that the image contains mistakes and is asked to diagnose them. Our experiments reveal a critical failure in verification: as shown in Figure3，该模型很难正确识别明显的错误，而是经常断言图像有效地满足了用户的原始意图。 这表明普遍存在确认偏差, the model struggles to correctly identify obvious errors, instead frequently asserting that the image effectively fulfills the user’s original intent. This indicates a pervasive confirmation bias(huang2023large)，模型很容易假设生成的内容是正确的。 相反，当切换到反射模式时，它有效地打破了这种确认循环，并且模型成功地检查细节以识别阴影位置的问题。 这一实证发现表明，反射机制最大限度地激活 UMM 的内在视觉理解，提供关键的监督信号来协助生成任务。基于这一见解， 我们利用这一机制作为我们提出的 Alpha-GRPO 的核心基础，专门设计了框架来加强训练期间的自我反思细化能力。, where the model easily assumes the generated content is correct. Conversely, when switched to Reflect Mode, it effectively breaks this confirmation loop and the model successfully scrutinizes details to identify issues with the shadow’s position. This empirical finding demonstrates that the reflection mechanism maximizes the activation of UMMs’ intrinsic visual understanding, providing a critical supervision signal to assist generative tasks. Building on this insight, we leverage this mechanism as the core foundation of our proposed Alpha-GRPO, specifically designing the framework to reinforce Self-Reflective Refinement capabilities during training.

4.3 训练数据构建Training Data Construction

为了确保 AlphaGRPO 的稳健性和泛化性，我们策划了一个大规模的提示集。 我们采用“Primitive-to-Prompt”自下而上的策略来合成训练数据。 首先，我们收集一个视觉元素池，其中包含视觉基元的综合池（例如对象、属性、空间关系）。 遵循 TIIF-Bench 的分类法To ensure the robustness and generalization of AlphaGRPO, we curate a large-scale prompt set. We adopt a “Primitive-to-Prompt” bottom-up strategy to synthesize training data. First, we collect a visual elements pool containing a comprehensive pool of visual primitives (e.g., objects, attributes, spatial relations). Following the taxonomy of TIIF-Bench(tiif)，我们定义了 39 个不同的组合任务，例如空间推理、属性绑定和计数。 对于每项任务，我们采用 LLM Qwen3-235B-A22B 通过从池中随机采样元素来合成提示。 为了确保全面的复杂性覆盖，我们指示模型生成三个难度级别（简单、中等、困难）的提示。我们总共生成了 19,500 个训练提示（每个任务 500 个，难度比为 3:5:2）和 1,024 个测试提示。, we define 39 distinct compositional tasks, e.g., spatial reasoning, attribute binding, and counting. For each task, we employ the LLM, Qwen3-235B-A22B, to synthesize prompts by stochastically sampling elements from the pool. To ensure comprehensive complexity coverage, we instruct the model to generate prompts across three difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, Hard). In total, we generate 19,500 training prompts (500 per task with a 3:5:2 difficulty ratio) and 1,024 test prompts.
