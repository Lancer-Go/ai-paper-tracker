时间序列分类多对一预训练的自适应输入训练ADAPTive Input Training for Many-to-One Pre-Training on Time-Series Classification

摘要。Abstract.

最近关于时间序列模型的工作利用自我监督训练来学习有意义的特征和模式，以提高下游任务的性能并推广到看不见的模式。虽然这些预训练方法在一对多场景中显示出了巨大的前景，其中模型在一个数据集上进行预训练并在下游数据集上进行微调，但当在预训练期间添加更多数据集时，它们很难推广到新数据集。这是为时间序列数据构建基础模型的一个基本挑战，因为它限制了开发可以从各种可用数据集中学习的模型的能力。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了一种新的时间序列数据预训练范例，称为Recent work on time-series models has leveraged self-supervised training to learn meaningful features and patterns in order to improve performance on downstream tasks and generalize to unseen modalities. While these pretraining methods have shown great promise in one-to-many scenarios, where a model is pre-trained on one dataset and fine-tuned on a downstream dataset, they have struggled to generalize to new datasets when more datasets are added during pre-training. This is a fundamental challenge in building foundation models for time-series data, as it limits the ability to develop models that can learn from a large variety of diverse datasets available. To address this challenge, we present a new pre-training paradigm for time-series data called自适应ADAPT，它可以有效地对齐时间序列域中数据的物理属性，从而尽管预训练数据的输入大小和通道维度存在极大差异，也可以实现混合批量预训练。我们对 162 个时间序列分类数据集进行了训练，并为分类基准设置了新的最先进性能。我们成功地在各种数据集上同时训练了时间序列域内的模型，这是在时间序列域中构建通用基础模型的主要构建块。, which can efficiently align the physical properties of data in the time-series domain, enabling mixed-batch pre-training despite the extreme discrepancies in the input sizes and channel dimensions of pre-training data. We trained on 162 time-series classification datasets and set new state-of-the-art performance for classification benchmarks. We successfully train a model within the time-series domain on a wide range of datasets simultaneously, which is a major building block for building generalist foundation models in time-series domains.

1. 简介Introduction

时间序列数据分析在许多现实生活中的应用中至关重要，包括医疗领域的应用Analysis of time-series data is critical in many real-life applications, including those in the medical[Acosta2022]、金融, financial[10.1145/3502289], 工业, industrial[app132212374], 农业, agricultural[Amankulova2023]和环境领域and environmental domains[amoatey2024effects]等, among others[LIU2023501, 9745049]。即使特定任务的可用数据有限，预训练和迁移学习也允许将大型模型应用于不同的任务。然而，时间序列数据的独特特征和可变性往往使得开发在预训练后能够成功适应不同下游模式的通用模型具有挑战性。之前的研究主要集中在特定于定制模态的设计上，在单一数据集上进行预训练，以改善模型训练的归纳偏差。这种方法与自然语言处理（NLP）和计算机视觉（CV）等其他领域使用的主导策略形成鲜明对比，后者专注于在许多大型数据集上训练单个模型，并导致了. Pre-training and transfer learning have allowed for the application of large models to diverse tasks even when there is limited task-specific data available. However, the unique characteristics and variability of time-series data often make it challenging to develop generalist models that can be successfully adapted to different downstream modalities after pretraining. Previous research has mainly focused on custom modality-specific designs, pre-trained on a singular dataset, to improve the inductive bias of model training. This approach is in contrast to the dominant strategies used in other domains, such as natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision (CV), which focus on training a single model on many large datasets and has led to the creation of基础模型foundation models比如GPT4such as GPT4[openai2023gpt4], 混合, Mixtral[jiang2024mixtral], 拉姆达, LaMDA[thoppilan2022lamda]、wav2vec2.0, wav2vec2.0[10.5555/3495724.3496768], 达勒-2, DALLE-2[ramesh2022hierarchical], T5  [JMLR:v21:20-074]等。, among others.

这些模型表现出了卓越的性能以及泛化到未见过的数据和任务的能力。虽然预训练模型已通过微调（一对多）应用于不同的下游模式，但还没有任何工作能够成功地在各种时间序列类型和模式上预训练时间序列模型。所做的工作于These models have shown remarkable performance and ability to generalize to unseen data and tasks. While pre-trained models have been applied to differing downstream modalities via fine-tuning (one-to-many), no work has been able to successfully pre-train a time-series model over a wide range of time-series types and modalities. The work performed in[zhang2022selfsupervised]发现在预训练期间添加数据集会抑制学习并迅速降低模型性能（一对一和四对一场景之间的性能下降约 25%）。上述每个基础模型都说明了数据量、模型大小和性能之间相对简单的相关性；通过在大型数据集上训练更大的模型，我们可以训练具有卓越迁移学习能力的强大模型。found that adding datasets during pre-training inhibited learning and rapidly degrade model performance (a roughly 25% decrease in performance between the one-to-one and four-to-one scenario). Each of the above foundation models illustrates a relatively simple correlation between volume of data, model size, and performance; by training larger models on large datasets, we can train powerful models with exceptional transfer learning capabilities.

多对一训练是非常理想的，因为它可以让我们扩大训练数据量、模型大小，轻松转移到新的领域和模式，并允许我们训练一个大型模型以应用于看不见的时间序列。此外，如果未能在多对一或多对多环境中预训练模型，会对未来产生许多负面影响。有关更多详细信息，请参阅分析和限制部分。Many-to-one training is very desirable as it may allow us to scale up the amount of training data, model size, ease transferring to new domains and modalities and allow us to train one large model for application on unseen time-series. Furthermore, there are many negative future implications in failing to pre-train models in a many-to-one or many-to-many setting. Refer to the Analysis and Limitations section for more details.

为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了一个新的框架，该框架在时间序列分类基准上实现了最先进的性能。我们的框架在 162 个时间序列数据集上训练单个模型进行分类，每个数据集都有不同的长度、通道维度和模态。为了创建与输入无关的模型，我们建议在数据加载过程中使用平均自适应池，这有利于时间序列数据的混合批量训练。我们的框架还被设计为完全与模型无关，这使我们能够利用特定于时间序列的模型架构的未来改进。 我们提出的框架解决了构建时间序列分析基础模型的几个关键挑战： (i) 模型必须能够处理任何维度、模态或通道大小的输入。 (ii) 他们需要使用现代并行计算策略进行培训。首先，我们希望在混合批量训练中训练大批量的模型，要求所有数据以可以在具有相同输入长度和模态大小的批次内混合的方式对齐。 (iii) 大量研究致力于创建强大的时间序列特定模型架构。我们希望我们的训练策略完全与模型无关，以利用该研究领域未来可能的改进。To address this challenge, we propose a new framework that achieves state-of-the-art performance on time-series classification benchmarks. Our framework trains a single model on 162 time-series datasets for classification, each with varying length, channel dimension, and modality. To create an input-agnostic model, we propose the use of average adaptive pooling during the data loading process, which facilitates mixed batch training for time-series data. Our framework is also designed to be completely model agnostic, which allows us to leverage future improvements in time-series-specific model architectures. Our proposed framework addresses several key challenges in building foundation models for time-series analysis: (i) The models must be able to process inputs of any dimension, modality, or channel sizes. (ii) They need to be trained using modern parallel computing strategies. Primarily we would like to train the models with large batch sizes in mixed batch training, requiring all data be aligned in a way that it can be mixed within batches with the same input length and modality size. (iii) A great deal of research has gone into creating powerful time-series specific model architectures. We would like our training strategy to be completely model agnostic to leverage possible future improvements in this research area.

通过介绍By introducing the自适应ADAPT框架中，我们提出了预训练模型在不同时间序列传感器数据上的最先进的性能，这反过来将有助于各种现实生活中的下游任务。framework, we brought forward the state-of-the-art performance of pretrained models on diverse time-series sensor data, which will in turn contribute to various real-life downstream tasks.

2. 相关工作Related Work

时间序列的基础模型。Foundation Models in Time-Series.

基础模型已经在大量数据集上进行了训练，以便在各种任务中具有广泛的适用性，如Foundation models have been trained on vast datasets for broad applicability across various tasks, as defined in[Stanford_CRFM_2021]。然而，一些模型，无论是全部还是部分，都在时间序列领域中声明了这一名称，例如最近研究中提到的模型. However, some models, either in full or in part, claim this designation in the time-series domain, such as those mentioned in recent studies[zhou2023fits, wu2023timesnet]，不符合 CRFM 的标准，不仅因为它们缺乏广泛的训练数据集，还因为它们缺乏可以帮助抵消和解决问题的适当的预试验架构。这些模型的主要限制是训练数据相对稀缺。不同时间序列数据的可用性表明，采用多对一的预训练方法可以克服这一障碍，从而能够有效适应不同的时间序列应用。, do not meet the CRFM’s criteria, not just because they lack the extensive training datasets but also the appropriate pretrianing architectures that can help counteract and solve the problem. These models’ primary limitation is the relative scarcity of training data. The availability of diverse time-series data suggests that adopting a many-to-one pre-training approach may overcome this hurdle, enabling effective adaptation to different time-series applications.

预训练策略。Pre-Training Strategies.

基础模型在 NLP 和计算机视觉领域取得了重要地位，在解决各种问题方面取得了显着的成功Foundation models have gained a significant status in the fields of NLP and computer vision, exhibiting remarkable success in solving a wide range of problems[zhou2023comprehensive]。这些模型的成功很大程度上归功于自我监督的预训练策略，这些策略利用现代并行计算技术对大量数据进行训练。最近的研究，例如. These models largely owe their success to self-supervised pretraining strategies that utilize modern parallel computing techniques to train on massive amounts of data. Recent research, such as[touvron2023llama]发现，除了模型架构方面的进步之外，长时间对更多数据进行训练对于构建强大的基础模型至关重要。为这些模型添加更多数据集很简单，因为每个常用研究领域（即文本或图像）中的基础数据在数据集中都是一致的。, has found that, besides advancements in model architecture, training on more data for longer periods is critical in building powerful foundation models. Adding in more datasets for these models is simple since the underlying data in each of the commonly studied domains (i.e., text or image) is consistent across datasets.

最近的一些工作侧重于通过简单地采用其他领域的预训练模型来跳过时间序列模型的预训练阶段Some recent works have focused on skipping the pre-training phase of time-series models by simply adopting pre-trained models from other domains[zhou2023fits, li2023time, chang2024llm4ts]。目的是采用大型预先训练的语言或视觉模型，并针对下游任务对其进行微调。虽然我们认为这项工作很重要，但它仍然没有为我们提供在不同时间序列上训练和扩展模型的解决方案。研究时间序列特定的预训练策略不仅在构建时间序列特定模型方面发挥着基础作用，而且还作为未来多模态时间序列模型的组成部分，例如在 CLIP 的时间序列特定变体中. The purpose is to take large pre-trained language or vision models and finetune them for downstream tasks. While we think this work is important, it still does not provide us with a solution for training and scaling models on diverse time-series. Investigating time-series specific pre-training strategies play a fundamental role in both building time-series specific models but also as components in future multi-modality time-series models, for example in time-series specific variants of CLIP[radford2021learning].

时间序列在模态类型、输入长度、模态维度、采样率和其他因素方面是独特的。因此，最近的工作重点是学习时间序列域之间传输的通用特征Time-series are unique in terms of modality types, input lengths, modality dimensions, sampling rates, and other factors. As a result, recent works have focused on learning universal features for the time-series domain that transfer between time-series[Yue_Wang_Duan_Yang_Huang_Tong_Xu_2022],[zhang2022selfsupervised]。这些工作的一个共同特点是强调学习策略，其中模型在预训练期间只能访问一个数据集，并针对下游领域数据进行微调。由于在预训练期间没有有效的解决方案来对齐不同的数据，因此这是一个现实的场景，因为时间序列域包含具有不同特征的各种数据模式。 最近用于时间序列域内预训练的自监督框架的一些示例包括 ts2vec. One common feature of these works is the emphasis on learning strategies in which the model only has access to one dataset during pretraining and is fine-tuned on downstream domain data. Since there is no effective solution for aligning different data during pretraining, this has been a realistic scenario as the time-series domain comprises of a wide range of data modalities with varying characteristics. Some recent examples of self-supervised frameworks for pre-training within the time-series domain include ts2vec[Yue_Wang_Duan_Yang_Huang_Tong_Xu_2022], TS-TCC [ijcai2021p324], 成本, COST[woo2022cost], 克洛克斯, CLOCS[Kiyasseh2020CLOCSCL], 克劳达, CLUDA[ozyurt2023contrastive]和跨国公司and TNC[tonekaboni2021unsupervised]。这些模型中的每一个都对从时间序列数据集中获取通用表示做出了重要贡献。. Each of these models represents important contributions to acquiring universal representations from time-series datasets in the一对多one-to-many使用自我监督学习的场景。scenario using self-supervised learning.

多对一预训练。Many-to-one Pre-training.

之前在时间序列多对一环境中训练模型的工作是有限的，最近才在 TF-C 中进行探索Previous work in training models in the many-to-one setting for time-series is limited, only explored very recently in TF-C[zhang2022selfsupervised]。该方法侧重于输入信号的时间和频率分量之间的对比目标，以提高从预训练数据集到目标模态的泛化能力。 TF-C 为时间序列分类设定了最先进的性能；然而，当在多对一场景中进行训练时，模型性能会迅速下降。为了允许多对一的训练，他们将四个预训练数据集合并为一个（SleepEEG、FD-A、HAR 和 ECG）。为了解决维度不匹配的问题，他们将每个数据集限制为只有一个通道维度，并截断或填充每个数据集 1,500 个观测值。他们的实验表明，与单一数据集上的预训练相比，性能下降了 25%。. This method focuses on a contrastive objective between the time and frequency components of the input signal to improve generalization from the pretraining dataset to the target modality. TF-C sets a state-of-the-art performance for time-series classification; however when trained in the many-to-one scenario, model performance degrades rapidly. In order to allow for many-to-one training they merge four pre-training datasets into one (SleepEEG, FD-A, HAR, and ECG). To address mismatch in dimensionalities, they limited each dataset to only one channel dimension and truncated or padded each dataset 1,500 observations. Their experiment demonstrated a 25% decrease in performance compared to pre-training on a singular dataset.

GATO 中提出了跨多种模式实施混合批量训练的类似解决方案A comparable solution for implementing mixed-batch training across multiple modalities is presented in GATO[reed2022generalist]，这是一个在控件、文本和图像数据上训练的大型基础模型。一个关键的贡献是它们的嵌入方案，该方案允许本体感觉数据、图像、文本、连续动作和离散代理动作以混合批量形式表示。他们通过为每种模态应用单独的嵌入结构来实现这一点，并且每个嵌入策略都与模态之间的通道尺寸相匹配。虽然这种结构似乎适用于时间序列领域，但有太多的模式可以适应这种策略（在我们的设置中，它需要超过 150 种不同的嵌入策略）。其次，在时间序列领域，嵌入被纳入模型的结构中（通常作为线性或卷积层），这意味着为了将模型和嵌入分开，我们首先需要为数据库中的每个数据集训练一个小型嵌入模型。最后，虽然 GATO 中输入序列的相对长度相似，但时间序列域内的数据在输入长度上存在显着差异，这意味着对齐通道维度是不够的。我们提出的解决方案，, which is a large foundation model trained on controls, text, and image data. One key contribution is their embedding scheme which allows for proprioception data, image, text, continuous actions and discrete agent actions to be represented in a mixed batch form. They performed this by applying a separate embedding structure for each modality and each embedding strategy matches the channel dimensions between the modalities. While it may seem like this structure could work for the time-series domain, there are far too many modalities to adapt this strategy (in our setup, it would require over 150 different embedding strategies). Secondly, in the time-series domain, embedding is incorporated into the structure of the model (usually as linear or convolutional layers), meaning that in order to separate the model and embedding, we would first need to train a small embedding model for each dataset in the database. Finally, while the relative lengths of the input sequences in GATO were similar, data within the time-series domain significantly differs in input length, meaning that aligning the channel dimensions is insufficient. Our proposed solution,自适应ADAPT，克服了所有这些挑战，并为帮助构建时间序列领域的基础模型提供了基础。, overcomes all these challenges and provides a basis for helping construct foundation models in the time-series domains.

3.2. 自适应输入训练Adaptive Input Training

时间序列数据由截然不同的输入长度和通道维度组成。这意味着当最大和最小输入大小之间存在较大差异时，截断或填充可能会严重降低或稀释输入。对齐通道维度的困难也限制了处理不同通道维度的模型架构要求。物理限制意味着允许对基于时间序列的模型进行混合批量训练而不严重降低模型性能是一个悬而未决的问题。我们建议在数据加载期间使用平均自适应池，以便将每个输入转换为相同的表示空间，无论大小如何。Time-Series data consists of widely different input lengths and channel dimensions. This means that truncation or padding will may seriously degrade or dilute the inputs when there are large discrepancies between the maximum and minimum input size. Difficulties in aligning channel dimensions also put constraints on the model architecture requirements for processing varying channel dimensions. The physical constraints mean that allowing mixed batch training for time-series based models without seriously degrading model performance was an open problem. We propose the use of average adaptive pooling applied during data-loading in order to transform each input to the same representation space regardless of size.

我们认识到，自适应地汇集数据可能会丢失数据中的细粒度信息，并可能导致时间扭曲。然而，许多流行数据集和数据类型的维度大小通常小于嵌入的目标维度。这意味着我们经常对数据进行上采样。我们将证明，由时间扭曲引起的任何精度损失都可以通过使用模型性能的提高来减轻We recognize that adaptively pooling the data risks losing fine-grained information in the data and may cause temporal distortions. However, the dimensional size of many popular datasets and data types are often smaller than that of the target dimension for embedding. This means that we are often up-sampling the data. We will show that any accuracy losses caused by the temporal distortions are mitigated by the increase in model performance using自适应ADAPT.

6. 结论Conclusion

在本文中我们介绍In this paper we introduce自适应ADAPT研究用于时间序列分析的多对一预训练策略。我们的自适应池化嵌入策略允许使用 150 多个数据集训练时间序列模型，并在不同的分类基准上建立新的最先进技术。我们证明自适应池可以用作时间序列嵌入策略，可以处理各种物理属性、输入长度和模式以及不同类别的数据集复杂性的数据。to investigate many-to-one pre-training strategies for time-series analysis. Our adaptive pooling embedding strategy allows for training a time-series model using over 150 datasets and establishing a new state of the art on different classification benchmarks. We demonstrate that adaptive pooling can be applied as a time-series embedding strategies which can handle data over a wide range of physical properties, input lengths and modalities, as well as dataset complexity with diverse classes.自适应ADAPT仅改变数据集的物理属性，并且没有对模态进行任何特殊考虑，表明物理数据集属性（输入长度和通道维度）的多样性阻碍了时间序列模型在多对一训练场景中学习质量表示。我们希望我们的工作将有助于为时间序列和传感器数据创建强大的基础模型，并使这些领域的进步与其他领域保持一致。only changes the physical properties of the dataset and does not make any special considerations for the modality, showing that it was the diversity of the physical dataset properties (input length and channel dimension) that have prevent time-series models from learning quality representations in the many-to-one training scenario. We hope our work will help enable the creation of capable foundation models for the time-series and sensor data and bring the advancements in these areas in line with other domains.
