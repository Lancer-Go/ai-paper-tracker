摘要Abstract

人类的道德决定在很大程度上取决于环境。然而，对法学硕士道德的研究主要是研究固定场景。我们通过引入 Contextual MoralChoice 来解决这一差距，这是一个道德困境的数据集，具有道德心理学中已知的系统性情境变化，可以改变人类的判断：后果论、情感论和关系论。通过评估 22 个法学硕士，我们发现几乎所有模型都是上下文相关的，将判断转向违反规则的行为。与人类调查相比，我们发现模型和人类最容易被不同的上下文变化所触发，并且在基本情况下与人类判断一致的模型不一定在上下文敏感性上一致。这就提出了控制上下文敏感性的问题，我们通过激活引导方法来解决这个问题，该方法可以可靠地增加或减少模型的上下文敏感性。A human’s moral decision depends heavily on the context. Yet research on LLM morality has largely studied fixed scenarios. We address this gap by introducing Contextual MoralChoice, a dataset of moral dilemmas with systematic contextual variations known from moral psychology to shift human judgment: consequentialist, emotional, and relational. Evaluating 22 LLMs, we find that nearly all models are context-sensitive, shifting their judgments toward rule-violating behavior. Comparing with a human survey, we find that models and humans are most triggered by different contextual variations, and that a model aligned with human judgments in the base case is not necessarily aligned in its contextual sensitivity. This raises the question of controlling contextual sensitivity, which we address with an activation steering approach that can reliably increase or decrease a model’s contextual sensitivity.

1 简介1 Introduction

随着大型语言模型（LLM）从文本完成引擎过渡到自主道德代理（Dillion 等人，2025），理解他们的道德能力已成为一个紧迫的问题。过去的工作通过道德决策任务的表现（Scherrer et al., 2023; Alexander and Moore, 2025; Abdulhai et al., 2023; Ji et al., 2025）和道德推理的质量（Chiu et al., 2026; Kilov et al., 2025; Aharoni et al., 2024）来评估这种能力。然而，此类评估将道德行为视为固定的模型属性，而不是依赖于情境的判断。As large language models (LLMs) transition from text-completion engines to autonomous ethical agents (Dillion et al., 2025), understanding their moral competence has become a pressing concern. Past work has assessed this competence through performance on moral decision tasks (Scherrer et al., 2023; Alexander and Moore, 2025; Abdulhai et al., 2023; Ji et al., 2025) and the quality of moral reasoning (Chiu et al., 2026; Kilov et al., 2025; Aharoni et al., 2024). 6 引导道德情境敏感性6 Steering Moral Contextual Sensitivity

在确定法学硕士表现出情境敏感性后，我们研究了是否可以精确控制这些判断转变。这种控制对于使法学硕士与其部署目标保持一致至关重要。例如，法律人工智能系统可能需要降低敏感性以维持严格基于规则的立场，而个人助理机器人可能需要引导特定的关系或情感敏感性以反映特定的价值体系。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一种激活引导方法，从模型的内部激活空间中提取上下文敏感度方向（第 6.1 节）。在推理时应用这个方向，我们证明了上下文敏感性可以可靠地增加或减少，而对一般模型功能的影响有限（第 6.2 节）。After establishing that LLMs exhibit contextual sensitivity, we investigate whether these judgment shifts can be precisely controlled. Such control is critical for aligning LLMs to their deployment goal. For instance, a legal AI system may require muting sensitivity to maintain a strictly rule-based stance, while a personal assistant bot may need to be steered toward specific relational or emotional sensitivities to reflect a particular value system. To address this, we propose an activation steering method that extracts a contextual sensitivity direction from the model’s internal activation space (Sec. 6.1). Applying this direction at inference time, we demonstrate that contextual sensitivity can be reliably increased or decreased with only limited impact on general model capabilities (Sec. 6.2).

7 讨论7 Discussion

我们的研究结果表明，法学硕士的道德判断从根本上来说是情境敏感的，但情境敏感性在多大程度上是理想的模型属性仍然是一个开放的规范问题。我们确定了解决这种紧张局势的三个主要观点，这将在第 2 节中详细讨论。克：一个Our findings demonstrate that LLM moral judgment is fundamentally context-sensitive, yet to what extent context sensitivity is a desirable model property remains an open normative question. We identify three main viewpoints for navigating this tension, discussed in detail in Sec. Current models appear to exhibit a “shallow mimicry” of humanlike patterns. This also highlights the challenge of价值多元化value pluralism：如果模型应该对上下文的细微差别敏感，则不清楚模型应该反映哪些具体敏感性以及反映到什么程度。正如我们所展示的，激活引导可以动态地将模型的敏感性与规范目标保持一致，类似于 Rao 等人的上下文政策方法。 （2023）。我们在第二部分讨论未来工作的方向。 H.: if the model should be sensitive to contextual nuances, it is not clear which specific sensitivities the model should reflect and to what degree. 8 结论8 Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced情境道德选择Contextual MoralChoice，一个高度模糊的道德困境的数据集，在结果主义、情感和关系维度上有系统的变化。通过对 22 名法学硕士的综合评估，我们发现大多数模型在情境扰动下将道德判断显着转向违反规则的行为。重要的是，转变的幅度与模型在中立基础场景中的规则遵守情况无关。与人类调查相比，我们发现模型和人类最容易被不同的上下文变化所触发，并且在基本情况下与人类判断一致的模型不一定在上下文敏感性上一致。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，当前的一致性框架并未转化为上下文敏感性的一致性。最后，我们证明这种敏感性可以通过激活引导来控制，为校准法学硕士的道德敏感性提供了技术途径。, a dataset of high-ambiguity moral dilemmas with systematic variations across consequentialist, emotional, and relational dimensions. Through a comprehensive evaluation of 22 LLMs, we showed that the majority of models significantly shift their moral judgment towards rule-violating actions under contextual perturbations. Importantly, the magnitude of the shift is irrespective of the model’s rule adherence in a neutral base scenario. Comparing with a human survey, we find that models and humans are most triggered by different contextual variations, and that a model aligned with human judgments in the base case is not necessarily aligned in its contextual sensitivity. Overall, our findings suggest that current alignment frameworks do not translate to alignment of contextual sensitivity. Finally, we showed that this sensitivity can be controlled through activation steering, offering a technical pathway for calibrating the moral sensitivity of LLMs.

局限性Limitations

任务框架和生态有效性。Task framing and ecological validity. 情境道德选择Contextual MoralChoice评估受控、单轮、二元选择环境中的道德应用。这脱离了现实世界的道德机构，包括在非结构化环境、迭代对话和非二元解决方案（例如澄清请求或妥协）中检测道德显着性（Kilov 等人，2025；Pyatkin 等人，2023；van Nuenen 和 Sachdeva，2026）。此外，我们的研究仅限于英语提示和三个问题模板，这可能无法全面捕捉跨语言的道德细微差别。evaluates moral application in controlled, single-turn, binary-choice settings. This abstracts away from real-world moral agency, which involves detecting moral salience in unstructured contexts, iterative dialogue, and non-binary resolutions such as clarification requests or compromise (Kilov et al., 2025; Pyatkin et al., 2023; van Nuenen and Sachdeva, 2026). Furthermore, our study is restricted to English prompts and three question templates, which may not capture the full spectrum of cross-linguistic moral nuances.

上下文维度的范围。Scope of contextual dimensions.虽然我们关注结果主义、情感和关系因素，但这些因素仅代表影响人类规范判断的变量的一小部分。我们的实验设计隔离了单一变异，以确保清晰的归因；然而，这排除了对多个相互竞争的道德因素（例如，关系义务与结果主义压力）之间相互作用影响的研究，这在复杂的困境中很常见。While we focus on consequentialist, emotional, and relational factors, these represent only a fraction of the variables influencing human normative judgment. Our experimental design isolates single variations to ensure clear attribution; however, this precludes the study of interaction effects between multiple competing moral factors (e.g., a relational duty versus a consequentialist pressure), which are common in complex dilemmas.

因果和机制限制。Causal and mechanistic constraints.我们的行为分析确定了模型特征（可访问性、大小、预训练语料库大小）和敏感性之间的相关性，但无法隔离预训练中的特定因果驱动因素。同样，我们的评估缺乏对道德无关扰动下不变性的控制（Ribeiro et al., 2020），这将进一步阐明观察到的敏感性的稳健性。Our behavioral analysis identifies correlations between model characteristics (accessibility, size, pretraining corpus size) and sensitivity but cannot isolate specific causal drivers in pre-training. Similarly, our evaluation lacks controls for invariance under morally irrelevant perturbations (Ribeiro et al., 2020), which would further clarify the robustness of the observed sensitivity.

禁用法学硕士的推理。Disabling the reasoning in LLMs.我们在直接响应条件下评估模型，并没有系统地测试推理支架的效果，例如We evaluate models under direct-response conditions and do not systematically test the effects of reasoning scaffolds such as思路链chain-of-thought提示、自洽解码或多步骤审议。先前的工作表明，此类支架可以显着改变模型性能和输出分布（Wei 等人，2022b；Wang 等人，2022b），包括道德判断任务（Jin 等人，2022）。因此，启用这些推理模式可能会产生不同的结果。prompting, selfconsistency decoding, or multi-step deliberation. Prior work shows that such scaffolds can substantially alter model performance and output distributions (Wei et al., 2022b; Wang et al., 2022b), including in moral judgment tasks (Jin et al., 2022). Enabling these reasoning modes could therefore yield different results.

模型转向的通用性有限。Limited generalizability of model steering.最后，在我们的转向分析中，我们专门评估了 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct。尽管我们在此模型中实现了对上下文敏感性的稳健控制，但我们在本节中的发现可能无法推广到其他模型。Finally, in our steering analysis, we exclusively evaluate Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Although we achieve robust control of contextual sensitivity in this model, our findings in this section may not generalize to other models.

Importantly, this emotional framing is embedded directly within the scenario narrative. This differs from research that induces emotions externally (e.g., via a film clip) prior to judgment (Valdesolo and DeSteno, 2006). By adding emotionally salient details, we aim to elicit empathy and test whether stronger emotional engagement makes a rule-breaking action to reduce suffering seem more acceptable (Haidt, 2001). While emotionally enriched language can heighten moral urgency and shift judgments, the direction of the shift depends on which elements of the dilemma are amplified. For example, vivid descriptions of direct harm can increase deontological responses (Bartels, 2008), whereas intensifying victims’ suffering can promote utilitarian choices aimed at relieving it (Doerflinger and Gollwitzer, 2020). E.1 基本偏好分析E.1 Analysis of base preferences

如图 2 所示，大多数模型表现出边际行动可能性的双峰分布。这表明模型并不处于统一的不确定性状态；相反，它们对于每个场景都是决定性的。我们观察到大多数模型都存在遵守规则的系统性偏差，选择违反规则行为的概率质量集中在 0.25 以下。这表明在没有额外背景的情况下，义务论遵守具有强大的内部先验。As shown in Fig. 2, most models exhibit bimodal distributions of marginal action likelihoods. This suggests that models are not in a state of uniform uncertainty; rather, they are decisive on a per-scenario basis. We observe a systemic bias toward rule-adherence for most models, with the bulk of probability mass for choosing the rule-violating action concentrated below 0.25. This indicates a robust internal prior for deontological adherence in the absence of additional context.

规模和演变。Scale and evolution.我们的结果凸显了道德决断力的明显演变趋势。虽然早期的模型（例如 Llama-2-7B-Chat）表现出更平坦的分布，并且在 0 处具有显着的密度Our results highlight a clear evolutionary trend in moral decisiveness. 对所有令牌进行引导是最有效的。Steering at all tokens is most effective.我们首先观察到，将引导向量应用于所有标记位置（图 12）比仅将其应用于最后一个提示标记（图 13）要有效得多。这可能是因为法学硕士中微妙的上下文敏感性并不局限于提示末尾的单个“决策点”。相反，它被处理为全局语义转变，在编码时影响场景的整个潜在表示。We first observe that applying the steering vector to all token positions (Fig. 12) is substantially more effective than applying it only to the last prompt token (Fig. 13). 情境敏感性与响应不稳定。Contextual sensitivity vs. response instability.虽然之前的工作表明法学硕士可能对表面的即时变化过于敏感（Oh 和 Demberg，2025），但我们采用了 Scherrer 等人的框架。 （2023）边缘化语义等效的提示。这种方法确保引发强大的道德偏好，这是分析情境敏感性的先决条件。我们的结果的统计显着性以及具有决定性基线的模型中的分类偏好逆转表明，对于大多数模型而言，这些转变不是随机不确定性或即时不稳定的产物。相反，它们代表了对环境的真实反应，表明一些法学硕士的敏感性是一种结构化行为，需要对其方向进行详细评估并与人类判断保持一致。While prior work suggests LLMs may be overly sensitive to superficial prompt variations (Oh and Demberg, 2025), we employ the framework of Scherrer et al. The statistical significance of our results, alongside categorical preference reversals in models with decisive baselines, indicates that, for the majority of models, these shifts are not artifacts of stochastic uncertainty or prompt instability. Instead, they represent a genuine reaction to context, suggesting that the sensitivity of several LLMs is a structured behavior that warrants detailed evaluation of its direction and alignment with human judgment.

H 未来的工作H Future Work

基于我们工作的发现，未来研究的几个方向是对法学硕士的情境道德敏感性建立更稳健、更有机械基础的理解。Building on the findings of our work, several directions for future research emerge towards developing a more robust and mechanistically grounded understanding of contextual moral sensitivity in LLMs.

跨文化和跨语言评估。Cross-cultural and cross-lingual evaluation.下一步关键是将评估框架扩展到多语言和非 WEIRD 环境（Henrich 等，2010）。未来的研究应该评估在英语场景中观察到的语境敏感性模式是否可以在其他语言中复制，或者文化语言框架是否会显着改变模型的道德景观。除了行为评估之外，引导向量的跨语言可转移性值得研究。当模型以不同语言提示时，从英语语料库中提取的引导向量是否可以有效地调节激活仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。比较特定语言模型中的道德几何形状可以揭示某些道德敏感性是否是基于规模的预训练的普遍产物，或者是训练语料库的主导语言的特定文化。A critical next step is to extend the evaluation framework to multilingual and non-WEIRD contexts (Henrich et al., 2010). Future research should evaluate whether the contextual sensitivity patterns observed in English-language scenarios are replicated in other languages, or if cultural-linguistic framing substantially alters a model’s moral landscape. Beyond behavioral evaluation, the cross-lingual transferability of steering vectors warrants investigation. It remains an open question whether a steering vector extracted from an English corpus can effectively modulate activations when the model is prompted in a different language. Comparing this geometry of morality across language-specific models could reveal whether certain ethical sensitivities are universal artifacts of scale-based pre-training or culturally specific to the dominant language of the training corpus.

By applying similar dictionary learning techniques to the activation space across diverse, unstructured corpora, one might be able to identify latent moral tendencies that a model has developed during training which might be different from traditional human ethical categories (Schramowski et al., 2022).

上下文敏感性的电路级本地化。Circuit-level localization of contextual sensitivity.从表示工程转向机械可解释性，未来的研究应该致力于定位负责处理上下文变化的特定变压器电路。路径修补（Wang et al., 2022a）或激活清理（Chan et al., 2022）等技术可用于识别我们观察到的敏感性是否集中在特定的注意力头中，或者它是否是激活流全局几何的属性。Moving from representation engineering to mechanistic interpretability, future studies should aim to localize the specific transformer circuits responsible for processing contextual variations. Techniques such as path patching (Wang et al., 2022a) or activation scrubbing (Chan et al., 2022) could be used to identify whether the sensitivity we observe is concentrated in specific attention heads or if it is a property of the activation stream’s global geometry.

一、潜在风险I Potential Risks

虽然这项工作的目的是揭露和描述当前对齐实践中的差距，但有几个风险值得承认。最直接的是，第 6 节中提出的激活指导方法可以由具有白盒模型访问权限的对抗参与者重新利用，以在推理时故意抑制道德规则的遵守。除了操纵技术本身的滥用之外，公开发布旨在在情感、关系和结果主义框架下引发违反规则反应的场景可以使其用作针对已部署系统的对抗性提示模板。最后，我们与人类调查数据的比较不应被视为暗示人类判断是规范上理想的校准目标；考虑到关系条件中记录的狭隘效应和我们参与者样本的怪异偏差（C.5.1 节），将模型转向类似人类的敏感性会带来编码而不是纠正人类偏见的风险。While this work is intended to expose and characterize a gap in current alignment practices, several risks warrant acknowledgment. Most directly, the activation steering methodology presented in Section 6 could be repurposed by adversarial actors with white-box model access to deliberately suppress moral rule adherence at inference time. Beyond misuse of the steering technique itself, publicly releasing scenarios designed to elicit rule-violating responses under emotional, relational, and consequentialist framing could enable their use as adversarial prompt templates against deployed systems. Finally, our comparison with human survey data should not be taken to imply that human judgment is a normatively ideal calibration target; given the documented parochialism effects in the relational condition and the WEIRD skew of our participant sample (Section C.5.1), steering models toward human-like sensitivity risks encoding rather than correcting for human biases.
