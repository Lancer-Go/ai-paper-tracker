ThinkDeception：用于可解释的多模态欺骗检测的渐进式强化学习框架ThinkDeception: A Progressive Reinforcement Learning Framework for Interpretable Multimodal Deception Detection

摘要。Abstract.

多模式欺骗检测对于识别欺诈意图至关重要，但现有方法主要依赖于端到端黑盒范例。这些方法严重缺乏可解释性——无法提供透明的推理轨迹，并且难以明确捕捉欺骗行为中固有的微妙的、跨模式的不一致之处。为了超越这些限制，我们提出了 ThinkDeception，一种新颖且可解释的多模式欺骗检测框架。作为一项开创性的努力，它将多模态大型语言模型（MLLM）引入该领域，将欺骗检测从传统的二元分类任务转变为显式的认知推理过程。在第一个精心注释的分步多模态思想链 (CoT) 数据集的帮助下，我们开发了一个基础模型 ThinkDeception-Base，以实证方式验证模态不一致在解码欺骗中的关键作用。在此基础上，我们的核心创新在于提出配备渐进式训练策略的视音频一致性组相对策略优化（VAC-GRPO）。与标准 GRPO 不同，我们将训练数据分为四个渐进的难度等级，引导模型经历基于心理的“从易到难”的认知转变。通过创新地将这种动态课程安排程序与多维、过程感知的奖励机制和反思性学习范式结合起来，我们显着提高了模型的整体推理质量。对主流基准的大量实验表明，ThinkDeception 建立了一种新的最先进技术 (SOTA)，在检测精度和基本原理质量方面均显着优于现有方法。最终，这项工作成功地将欺骗检测领域推向可解释的多模态认知推理。Multimodal deception detection is critical for identifying fraudulent intentions, yet existing approaches predominantly rely on end-to-end black-box paradigms. These methods suffer from a severe lack of interpretability—failing to provide transparent reasoning trajectories and struggling to explicitly capture the subtle, cross-modal inconsistencies inherent in deceptive behaviors. To transcend these limitations, we propose ThinkDeception, a novel and interpretable multimodal deception detection framework. As a pioneering effort, it introduces Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) into this domain, transforming deception detection from a traditional binary classification task into an explicit cognitive reasoning process. Facilitated by the first meticulously annotated step-by-step multimodal Chain-of-Thought (CoT) dataset, we develop a foundational model, ThinkDeception-Base, empirically validating the critical role of modal inconsistency in decoding deception. Building upon this foundation, our core innovation lies in proposing Visual-Audio Consistency Group Relative Policy Optimization(VAC-GRPO) equipped with a progressive training strategy. Distinct from standard GRPO, we stratify the training data into four progressive difficulty tiers, guiding the model through a psychologically grounded “easy-to-hard” cognitive transition. By innovatively coupling this dynamic curriculum scheduler with a multi-dimensional, process-aware reward mechanism and a reflective learning paradigm, we significantly elevate the model’s overall reasoning quality. Extensive experiments on mainstream benchmarks demonstrate that ThinkDeception establishes a new state-of-the-art (SOTA), significantly outperforming existing methods in both detection accuracy and rationale quality. Ultimately, this work successfully drives the field of deception detection toward interpretable, multimodal cognitive reasoning.

1. 简介Introduction

尽管整合视觉和听觉信息的多模态欺骗检测方法取得了重大进展，但它们仍然受到两个主要瓶颈的限制Although multimodal deception detection methods integrating visual and acoustic information have achieved significant progress, they remain constrained by two major bottlenecks(Guo et al., 2024)(Zhu et al., 2025)。首先，由于数据收集和注释相关的成本很高，现有数据集的规模有限，渲染模型容易出现局部过度拟合，并阻碍了它们提取普遍适用的欺骗性线索的能力。其次，数据源场景（法庭、实验室设置）的多样性带来了显着的领域差异. First, due to the high costs associated with data collection and annotation, existing datasets are limited in scale, rendering models prone to local overfitting and hindering their ability to extract universally applicable deceptive cues. Second, the diversity of data source scenarios(courtrooms,laboratory settings) introduces significant domain discrepancies(Cai et al., 2025)加上跨模态固有的异质性，严重阻碍了多模态表示的学习和模型的跨领域泛化能力。随着多模态大语言模型（MLLM）在视频理解等领域的快速发展, coupled with the inherent heterogeneity across modalities, severely impedes the learning of multimodal representations and the cross-domain generalization capabilities of the models. With the rapid advancement of multimodal large language models(MLLMs) in areas such as video understanding(Li et al., 2024a)(Liu et al., 2024)(Chen et al., 2024)(Li et al., 2024b)(Rong et al., 2025)(Wang et al., 2024)(Ye et al., 2025)与此同时，我们也开始思考一个问题：我们能否充分利用多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的推理潜力，提出一种类似于人类认知的方法，逐步分析欺骗性线索，最终得出判断？, we also begin to contemplate a question: Can we fully leverage the reasoning potential of multimodal large language models(MLLMs) to propose a method that, akin to human cognition, progressively analyses the deceptive cues step by step and ultimately arrives at a judgement?

3.2. 渐进式培训策略Progressive Training Strategy

5. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了ThinkDeception，首次成功地将大型语言模型的推理能力引入到欺骗检测领域。这项工作推动了欺骗识别的范式转变，从传统的二元分类任务到可解释的推理过程。在训练阶段，我们设计了一种渐进式学习策略，引导模型以由易到难的方式内化欺骗性特征。同时，我们引入了VAC-GRPO，使模型能够进行严格的分步推理。这种架构使模型能够生成事实上一致的推理步骤并精确捕获潜在的欺骗性线索。跨多个基准数据集的综合实验表明，ThinkDeception 在检测准确性和推理质量方面都建立了新的最先进水平。In this paper, we propose ThinkDeception, successfully introducing the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models into the domain of deception detection for the first time. This work drives a paradigm shift in deception recognition from a traditional binary classification task to an interpretable reasoning process. During the training phase, we design a progressive learning strategy to guide the model in internalizing deceptive features in an easy-to-hard manner. Concurrently, we introduce VAC-GRPO, enabling the model to conduct rigorous step-by-step reasoning. This architecture empowers the model to generate factually consistent reasoning steps and precisely capture latent deceptive cues. Comprehensive experiments across multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that ThinkDeception establishes a new state-of-the-art in both detection accuracy and reasoning quality.
