罕见感知自动编码：重建空间不平衡数据Rare-Aware Autoencoding: Reconstructing Spatially Imbalanced Data

Alejandro Castañeda Garcia    Jan van Gemert    Daan Brinks    Nergis Tömen
摘要Abstract

自动编码器可能会受到图像内容的空间非均匀采样的挑战。这在医学成像、生物学和物理学中很常见，其中信息模式很少出现在特定图像坐标处，因为背景在大多数样本中的这些位置中占主导地位，使重建偏向于大多数外观。在实践中，自动编码器偏向于主导模式，导致细粒度细节的丢失，并导致稀有空间输入的重建模糊，尤其是在空间数据不平衡的情况下。我们通过两个互补的组件来解决空间不平衡问题：（i）基于自熵的损失，它增加了统计上不常见的空间位置的权重；（ii）样本传播，这是一种重播机制，在训练期间有选择地将模型重新暴露于难以跨批次重建的样本。我们在无监督重建环境中对现有的数据平衡策略进行基准测试，这些策略最初是为监督分类而开发的。利用这些方法的局限性，我们的方法通过鼓励模型关注统计上罕见的位置，专门针对空间不平衡，与现有基线相比提高重建一致性。我们在具有受控空间不平衡条件的模拟数据集中以及跨越物理、生物和天文学领域的三个不受控制的、多样化的现实世界数据集中进行验证。我们的方法在各种重建指标上都优于基线，特别是在空间不平衡分布下。这些结果强调了批量数据表示的重要性，并强调了无监督图像重建中的稀有样本。我们将提供所有代码和相关数据。Autoencoders can be challenged by spatially non-uniform sampling of image content. This is common in medical imaging, biology, and physics, where informative patterns occur rarely at specific image coordinates, as background dominates these locations in most samples, biasing reconstructions toward the majority appearance. In practice, autoencoders are biased toward dominant patterns resulting in the loss of fine-grained detail and causing blurred reconstructions for rare spatial inputs especially under spatial data imbalance. We address spatial imbalance by two complementary components: (i) self-entropy-based loss that upweights statistically uncommon spatial locations and (ii) Sample Propagation, a replay mechanism that selectively re-exposes the model to hard to reconstruct samples across batches during training. We benchmark existing data balancing strategies, originally developed for supervised classification, in the unsupervised reconstruction setting. Drawing on the limitations of these approaches, our method specifically targets spatial imbalance by encouraging models to focus on statistically rare locations, improving reconstruction consistency compared to existing baselines. We validate in a simulated dataset with controlled spatial imbalance conditions, and in three, uncontrolled, diverse real-world datasets spanning physical, biological, and astronomical domains. Our approach outperforms baselines on various reconstruction metrics, particularly under spatial imbalance distributions. These results highlight the importance of data representation in a batch and emphasize rare samples in unsupervised image reconstruction. We will make all code and related data available.

1 简介Introduction

我们研究自然科学中的小数据成像问题，其中获取和标记数据的成本很高，考虑到这种成本，自监督和无监督学习尤其相关。因此，自动编码器是一个很好的选择，因为它们不需要额外的标签，因为它们的目标是重建输入图像。此外，自动编码器由于其在广泛任务中的多功能性而成为重要的深度学习模型We investigate small-data imaging problems in the natural sciences, where obtaining and labeling data is expensive, and given this cost, self-supervised and unsupervised learning are particularly relevant. Consequently, autoencoders are a good alternative since they do not need additional labels, as their objective is to reconstruct the input image. Additionaly, autoencoders are essential deep learning models due to their versatility across a wide range of tasks[vincent2008extracting, bengio2013representation]包括去噪including denoising[vincent2008extracting]、异常检测, anomaly detection[an2015variational]、潜在动力学建模, latent dynamics modeling[watter2015embed]、生成建模, generative modeling[diederik_p__kingma_baac31fa]和聚类, and clustering[makhzani2015adversarial]这使得它们与未标记的小数据成像问题相关。which makes them relevant for unlabeled small-data imaging problems.

在这里，我们重点关注自动编码器模型系列，用于解决图像中对象的空间位置在数据集中变化很大的问题。这在自然科学中尤其重要，其中，Here, we focus on the autoencoder family of models for problems where the spatial locations of objects in an image varies strongly in the dataset. This is especially relevant in the natural sciences, where,例如e.g.，不同星系中的恒星没有典型的方向，并且一个培养皿中细胞的空间图像位置不同于另一培养皿中细胞的空间图像位置。空间位置变化的原因可以追溯到采集困难, stars in different galaxies have no canonical orientation and the spatial image location of cells in one petri dish, differs from the spatial image locations of cells in a different petri dish. Reasons for variance in the spatial location can be traced back to acquisition difficulties[chen_sun_f5900b3a, linus_ericsson_30c98a67, peng_zheng_d3e44641]，对于单一来源, either for a single source[finn2017model]，或连续流，例如视频, or a continuous stream such as video[srivastava2015unsupervised, xiaofeng_zhang_aa4031cb].

我们观察到，数据集中不同空间图像位置上记录的对象放置的高方差会导致无监督学习问题的不平衡。当训练样本数量较小且在训练数据集中的空间图像位置上对对象进行不均匀采样时，这种不平衡尤其存在；见图。We make the observation that high variance, in recorded object placements over different spatial image locations in the dataset, leads to an imbalance in the unsupervised learning problem. This imbalance is particularly present when the number of training samples is small, and objects are not equally sampled over spatial image locations in the training dataset; see Fig.1为例。不平衡是一个已知问题for an illustration. Imbalance is a known problem[yifan_zhang_caa37b4d, charika_de_alvis_6e7a21a0, lu_yang_c3b53d32, pintea2023step]当类别标签不平衡时用于监督学习。然而，在无监督的环境中，标签不可用，不平衡的影响很少被探讨。在这里，我们研究空间图像位置的不平衡如何影响自动编码器。for supervised learning when there is an imbalance in the class labels. However, in the unsupervised setting, where labels are not available, the effect of imbalance is less explored. Here, we investigate how imbalance in the spatial image locations affects autoencoders.

我们的损失旨在减少由于收敛到平均像素值而引起的模糊。直觉是对批次中统计上罕见的像素值赋予更大的重要性，理论上我们对完整分布感兴趣，但实际上我们关注批次级别，因为我们不想对模型在特定更新中不会看到的空间位置进行加权。鼓励该模型优先考虑空间不平衡的位置而不是统计冗余的位置。具体来说，我们首先测量与批次中的每个像素相关的自熵，这些像素形成了优先考虑的稀有位置，称为“熵掩模”。Our loss aims to decrease the blurriness caused by converging to the average pixel values. The intuition is to assign greater importance to pixel values that are statistically rare across a batch, in theory we are interested in the full distribution, but in practice we look at the batch level since we do not want to weight spatial locations that the model won’t see in a particular update. The model is encouraged to prioritize spatially imbalanced locations over statistically redundant locations. Specifically, we first measure the self-entropy, associated with each pixel across the batch, those pixel forms the rare location to prioritize denominated as "entropy mask".

5 讨论Discussion

自动编码器是无监督学习的核心工具，可实现跨不同任务的高效表示学习。然而，尽管空间不平衡对下游绩效产生了至关重要的影响，但其影响仍未得到充分研究。不平衡的空间分布可能会导致表示不理想，从而降低泛化性并掩盖重要的变化。这项研究朝着解决经常被忽视的数据不平衡挑战迈出了第一步。通过系统地解决这个问题，我们展示了如何在不改变底层架构的情况下改进无监督自动编码器。Autoencoders are a core tool in unsupervised learning, enabling efficient representation learning across diverse tasks. However, the effect of spatial imbalance remains underexplored, despite its critical impact on downstream performance. Imbalanced spatial distributions can lead to suboptimal representations, reducing generalization and obscuring important variations. This study takes a first step toward addressing an often-overlooked challenge of data imbalance. By tackling this issue systematically, we show how unsupervised autoencoders can be improved without altering their underlying architectures.

总之，我们的方法通过改进对代表性不足的空间位置的学习来解决空间不平衡的问题，促进更平衡的重建。基于自熵的目标通过最大化信息内容来减轻模糊和过度平滑，产生更清晰、更高保真度的重建，从而保留跨架构的精细细节。样本传播算法 (spp) 提高了平均准确度并增强了对罕见事件的保留，同时减少了对已学习特征的关注，从而在整个数据集上总体上获得了更好的性能。In summary, our methods address the issue of spatial imbalance by improving the learning of underrepresented spatial locations, promoting a more balanced reconstruction. The self-entropy-based objective mitigates blurring and over-smoothing by maximizing information content, producing sharper, higher-fidelity reconstructions that preserve fine details across architectures. The sample propagation algorithm (spp) improves average accuracy and enhances retention of rare events while reducing focus on already learned features, leading to better performance in general across the whole dataset.

未来的工作：Future Work:虽然我们的方法展示了显着的改进，但对数据分布行为的更深入了解对于确定何时以及如何将这些技术有效地应用于特定于任务的约束至关重要。While our approach demonstrates significant improvements, a deeper understanding of data distribution behaviors is essential to determine when and how these techniques can be effectively applied on task-specific constraints.

限制：Limitations:我们的研究并不关注特定的应用领域，需要在更复杂和特定领域的设置中进行进一步验证，以评估不同数据集和任务的性能。Our study does not focus on a specific application domain, further validation in more complex and domain-specific setups is necessary to assess performance across diverse datasets and tasks.
