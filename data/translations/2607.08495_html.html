Discussions of AI-related inequality typically default to a familiar template inherited from older debates about the digital divide: some people can afford or access a resource and others cannot, and the resulting gap in capability tracks existing axes of disadvantage. This template is intuitive because it maps directly onto established sociological vocabulary—who has access, who does not—and requires no revision to how inequality is normally conceived. The CAD does not fit this template, and readers accustomed to it may reasonably wonder how a difference in interaction architecture, rather than in access per se, constitutes a form of inequality at all. An analogy familiar to the scholarly community itself may help. Consider two researchers who each have exactly the same number of hours allocated to research in a given week—an identical endowment of the resource that "availability" and "quantity" in the Sharp et al. sense would measure. The first researcher works alone: before any thinking can begin, she must search the literature herself, locate the relevant passages in her own accumulated notes and files, retrieve and often physically photocopy materials, and assemble these into raw material for thought. The second researcher works within a well-resourced research university, supported by research assistants and administrative staff who continuously organize, index, and retrieve from the literature and from the researcher’s own archive; the researcher need only say "bring me this, and that," and the relevant materials appear. Both researchers have identical research time in the sense the conventional framework would measure, and both may have identical access to the same journals, databases, and computing resources. Yet the substantive research output the two can produce in that time differs enormously, because the cognitive labor of constituting the material basis for thought has been distributed differently between researcher and support staff. This is, of course, a familiar and consequential difference between research environments—it tracks, among other things, persistent disparities between well-resourced research universities and institutions where faculty must perform this labor unaided. The CAD names the AI-mediated analogue of this difference: a gap that access-based measures do not register because they ask whether one has an AI system, not how the cognitive labor of using it well is divided between the system and the person.

3 上下文访问划分的技术基础The Technical Basis of the Context Access Divide

3.1 上下文提供的三种架构Three Architectures of Context Supply

当代人工智能助手界面的特征可以根据谁对上下文管理承担主要责任以及检索跨数据源延伸的程度来定义：Contemporary AI assistant interfaces can be characterized along a spectrum defined by who bears primary responsibility for context curation, and how far that retrieval extends across data sources:

手动附件模型 (MAM)。Manual Attachment Model (MAM).用户负责识别哪些文件、文档或数据与给定的查询相关，并通过显式附件机制（例如文件上传按钮、复制粘贴）提供它们。人工智能系统仅处理明确提供的内容。这种模型在消费者人工智能界面中很常见，其中可以进行云存储集成，但集成仅作为用户选择和附加单个文件的机制，选择判断权仍由用户决定。The user is responsible for identifying which files, documents, or data are relevant to a given query and supplying them via explicit attachment mechanisms (e.g., file upload buttons, copy-paste). The AI system processes only what is explicitly provided. This model is common in consumer AI interfaces where cloud storage integrations are available, but the integration surfaces only as a mechanism for users to select and attach individual files—the selection judgment remains with the user.

Walled 动态上下文检索模型（Walled DCRM）。Walled Dynamic Context Retrieval Model (Walled DCRM).人工智能系统可以自动从定义的生态系统内的数据源检索上下文，例如单个提供商平台内的用户文件、电子邮件和日历，而无需每次都手动附加。系统确定要检索的内容并将其自动集成到响应中，但此功能受到提供商生态系统边界的限制。 Representative examples include the Gemini assistant embedded in Google Drive (which can search across Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Calendar dynamically) and Microsoft 365 Copilot (which retrieves across Office documents, Teams, and Outlook).至关重要的是，围墙式 DCRM 通过专有的封闭式集成来运行：虽然这些平台名义上可能支持 MCP 等开放协议，但围墙环境中的动态检索功能不会扩展到外部数据源或第三方服务。这种架构将平台锁定作为一种结构特征：其知识库分布在多个提供商之间的用户，或者依赖提供商生态系统之外的本地存储文件或专用数据库的用户，即使在围墙环境中体验 DCRM 时，仍部分处于 MAM 条件下。The AI system can autonomously retrieve context from data sources within a defined ecosystem—for example, a user’s files, email, and calendar within a single provider’s platform—without requiring manual attachment at each turn. The system determines what to retrieve and integrates it into the response autonomously, but this capability is bounded by the provider’s ecosystem boundaries. Representative examples include the Gemini assistant embedded in Google Drive (which can search across Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Calendar dynamically) and Microsoft 365 Copilot (which retrieves across Office documents, Teams, and Outlook). Critically, Walled DCRM operates through proprietary, closed integrations: while these platforms may nominally support open protocols such as MCP, the dynamic retrieval capability within the walled environment does not extend to external data sources or third-party services. This architecture creates platform lock-in as a structural feature: users whose knowledge corpora are distributed across multiple providers, or who rely on locally stored files or specialized databases outside the provider’s ecosystem, remain partially in MAM conditions even while experiencing DCRM within the walled environment.

开放动态上下文检索模型（开放 DCRM）。Open Dynamic Context Retrieval Model (Open DCRM).作为任务处理的一部分，人工智能系统可以跨生态系统边界自主查询数据源，包括个人知识库、本地文件系统、电子邮件、日历、专业数据库和第三方服务。系统每次都会确定相关的上下文、检索并合并它，而无需明确的用户指令。该架构由开放的、供应商中立的协议（例如 MCP）与代理 RAG 管道和工具使用功能相结合来实现，允许用户连接异构数据源，而不管提供商如何。The AI system can autonomously query data sources across ecosystem boundaries—including personal knowledge bases, local file systems, email, calendars, specialized databases, and third-party services—as part of task processing. The system determines what context is relevant, retrieves it, and incorporates it without explicit user instruction at each turn. This architecture is enabled by open, vendor-neutral protocols such as MCP combined with agentic RAG pipelines and tool-use capabilities, allowing users to connect heterogeneous data sources regardless of provider.

这三种模型不仅仅是方便性范围内的点；它们代表了人类和机器之间认知劳动的不同性质的分布，以及用户和平台提供商之间不同性质的关系。 Walled DCRM 和开放式 DCRM 之间的区别尤其重要：两者都在各自的范围内减轻了用户的手动附件，但只有开放式 DCRM 这样做，而不以强加生态系统依赖性作为这种缓解的代价。These three models are not simply points on a convenience spectrum; they represent qualitatively different distributions of cognitive labor between human and machine, and qualitatively different relationships between users and platform providers. The distinction between Walled DCRM and Open DCRM is particularly consequential: both relieve the user of manual attachment within their respective scopes, but only Open DCRM does so without imposing ecosystem dependency as the price of that relief.

3.2 情境管理的认知负担The Cognitive Burden of Context Curation

在 MAM 下，有效的人工智能使用要求用户在主要任务开始之前执行预先的检索任务。此先前检索涉及：Under the MAM, effective AI use requires the user to perform a prior retrieval task before the primary task can begin. This prior retrieval involves:

  1. 1.

    召回Recall：用户必须记住存在相关文档。: The user must remember that a relevant document exists.

  2. 2.

    鉴定Identification：用户必须在潜在的数千个文件中找到特定文件。: The user must locate the specific file among potentially thousands.

  3. 3.

    选择Selection：用户必须判断要提供多个潜在相关文件中的哪一个。: The user must judge which of multiple potentially relevant files to supply.

  4. 4.

    附件Attachment：用户必须执行上传或引用文件的机械操作。: The user must perform the mechanical act of uploading or referencing the file.

每个步骤都会带来认知和时间成本。更重要的是，步骤（1）和（2）引入了结构性限制：用户只能提供他们已经知道存在并且可以定位的上下文。随着个人知识库的增长（在知识密集型专业领域，包含数万个文件的库并不罕见），用户无法回忆或找到相关材料的可能性大幅增加。这种模式与个人信息管理文献的发现是一致的：信息重新查找的实证日记研究发现，检索失败的主要原因不是搜索工具的缺陷，而是回顾性记忆的失误——根本无法回忆起相关项目的存在。Each of these steps imposes cognitive and temporal costs. More importantly, steps (1) and (2) introduce a structural limitation: the user can only supply context they already know exists and can locate. As personal knowledge corpora grow—in knowledge-intensive professional domains, corpora of tens of thousands of files are not unusual—the probability that a user will fail to recall or locate relevant material increases substantially. This pattern is consistent with findings from the personal information management literature: empirical diary studies of information re-finding find that the dominant cause of failed retrieval is not deficiencies in search tools but lapses in retrospective memory—the failure to recall that a relevant item exists at all(Elsweiler et al., 2007)。因此，在 MAM 下，人工智能的效用受到用户自身检索能力的限制，而用户自身的检索能力本身又受到人类记忆限制的限制。. Under the MAM, therefore, AI utility is bounded by the user’s own retrieval capacity, which is itself bounded by human memory limitations.

围墙式 DCRM 消除了提供商生态系统内的这些成本，但结构上类似的限制适用于生态系统边界：用户只能利用围墙环境中的上下文。对于智力资本分布在本地文件、多个云提供商、专业数据库或特定领域工具的知识工作者来说，围墙边界在更高的聚合水平上再现了 MAM 的结构限制。将研究笔记存储在本地知识库中、将工作文档存储在一个云提供商中以及跨多个电子邮件系统存储通信的用户会发现，Walled DCRM 提供了部分缓解，同时使他们的语料库的大部分内容无法进行自主检索。Walled DCRM eliminates these costs within the provider’s ecosystem, but a structurally analogous limitation applies at the ecosystem boundary: the user can only draw on context that resides within the walled environment. For knowledge workers whose intellectual capital is distributed across local files, multiple cloud providers, specialized databases, or domain-specific tools, the walled boundary reproduces the MAM’s structural limitation at a higher level of aggregation. The user who stores research notes in a local knowledge base, working documents in one cloud provider, and correspondence across multiple email systems will find that Walled DCRM provides partial relief while leaving substantial portions of their corpus inaccessible to autonomous retrieval.

在开放式 DCRM 下，这些成本跨越生态系统边界转移到人工智能系统。人类指定一个目标；系统确定需要什么上下文，并从连接的任何源中检索它，而不管提供者是谁。这种转移不仅仅是一种节省时间的便利，它从质上改变了人工智能辅助有效的任务类别，特别是对于拥有大型、异构、跨生态系统语料库的知识工作者来说。Under the Open DCRM, these costs are transferred to the AI system across ecosystem boundaries. The human specifies a goal; the system determines what context is needed and retrieves it from whatever sources are connected, regardless of provider. This transfer is not merely a time-saving convenience—it qualitatively alters the class of tasks for which AI assistance is effective, particularly for knowledge workers with large, heterogeneous, cross-ecosystem corpora.

3.3 阈值效应及其嵌套结构A Threshold Effect and Its Nested Structure

我们认为，三种架构之间的转变表现出阈值动态，而不是平滑的改进梯度。原因是人工智能辅助的效用是上下文完整性的非线性函数。对于需要跨多个文档进行综合或参考用户最近未查阅过的材料的任务（特别是那些以高度联合上下文依赖性为特征的任务，如下文所述），即使对于技术熟练的用户，MAM 也可能产生接近于零的边际效用，这不是因为 AI 模型无能力，而是因为相关上下文从未进入交互。We argue that the transitions across the three architectures exhibit threshold dynamics rather than a smooth gradient of improvement. The reason is that the utility of AI assistance is a nonlinear function of context completeness. For tasks requiring synthesis across multiple documents or reference to material the user has not recently consulted—particularly those characterized by high conjunctive context dependency, as elaborated below—the MAM may produce near-zero marginal utility even for technically sophisticated users, not because the AI model is incapable, but because the relevant context never enters the interaction.

期望阈值动态而不是持续改进的理论基础取决于知识综合任务的属性：它们通常具有所谓的特征The theoretical basis for expecting threshold dynamics rather than continuous improvement rests on a property of knowledge synthesis tasks: they are often characterized by what might be called连接上下文依赖conjunctive context dependency，其中输出的质量不取决于所提供上下文的平均相关性，而是取决于特定关键元素的存在。一份法律摘要包含了十个相关先例中的八个，但忽略了两个最切中要害的先例，这可能比毫无用处更糟糕——它可能肯定会产生误导。一篇文献综述涵盖了某个领域的主要发现，但错过了最近一篇推翻关键假设的论文，将会引导研究人员走向错误的方向。在此类任务中，部分上下文不会产生相应的部分效用；它产生了质量不同且可能较差的输出。, where the quality of the output depends not on the average relevance of supplied context but on the presence of specific critical elements. A legal brief that incorporates eight of ten relevant precedents but omits the two most on-point ones may be worse than useless—it may be affirmatively misleading. A literature review that covers the main findings in a field but misses the recent paper that overturns a key assumption will lead the researcher in the wrong direction. In such tasks, partial context does not yield proportionally partial utility; it yields a qualitatively different—and potentially inferior—output.

Open DCRM, which would deliver contextuality without lock-in, does not generate equivalent commercial returns for any single provider, creating a systematic market under-provision of the architecture that would most benefit users with heterogeneous, cross-ecosystem corpora.

该市场结构分析表明，治理干预可能需要超越透明度要求，以解决有利于 Walled 而非开放 DCRM 的结构性激励问题。相关政策工具可能包括要求平台支持开放上下文检索协议的互操作性要求、确保用户可以在不丢失 DCRM 功能的情况下跨提供商迁移知识库的数据可移植性要求，以及有利于公共部门人工智能部署中的开放式 DCRM 架构的采购标准。这种动态的更广泛的政治经济维度——包括它与学术知识基础设施的历史封闭模式的关系，例如开放统计软件的商业化和商业出版商对开放学术知识库的收购——提出了分配正义和互惠正义的问题，这些问题需要超出本文的范围进行专门的分析。This market structure analysis suggests that governance interventions may need to go beyond transparency requirements to address the structural incentives that favor Walled over Open DCRM. Relevant policy instruments might include interoperability mandates requiring platforms to support open context retrieval protocols, data portability requirements ensuring users can migrate knowledge corpora across providers without loss of DCRM functionality, and procurement standards that favor Open DCRM architectures in public sector AI deployment. The broader political-economic dimensions of this dynamic—including its relationship to historical patterns of enclosure in academic knowledge infrastructure such as the commercialization of open statistical software and the acquisition of open scholarly repositories by commercial publishers—raise questions of distributive and reciprocal justice that warrant dedicated analysis beyond the scope of this paper.

6 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

6.1 局限性Limitations

应该注意几个限制。首先，本文主要是概念性的； CAD及其分配效应的实证测量仍然是一个重要的议程项目。在生态有效的知识工作环境中衡量 MAM 与 DCRM 的生产力影响需要对拥有大量个人语料库的员工进行对照研究——这是一个在方法上具有挑战性的要求。Several limitations should be noted. First, this paper is primarily conceptual; empirical measurement of the CAD and its distributional effects remains an important agenda item. Measuring the productivity effects of MAM vs. DCRM in ecologically valid knowledge-work settings would require controlled studies with workers possessing large personal corpora—a methodologically challenging requirement.

其次，本文确定的三种架构（MAM、Walled DCRM 和开放式 DCRM）之间的界限在实践中并不总是很清晰，特别是 Walled DCRM 类别涵盖了在生态系统范围、检索自主性和对第三方扩展的开放性方面不同的广泛实现。未来的分析工作可能需要开发更细粒度的上下文维度度量来捕获这种变化。Second, the boundaries among the three architectures identified in this paper—MAM, Walled DCRM, and Open DCRM—are not always sharp in practice, and the Walled DCRM category in particular encompasses a wide range of implementations differing in ecosystem scope, retrieval autonomy, and openness to third-party extension. Future analytical work may need to develop more fine-grained measures of the contextuality dimension that can capture this variation.

第三，CAD 分析基于检索模型之间相对稳定的区别。随着人工智能平台融合 DCRM 功能，这种区别可能会演变。然而，即使 DCRM 变得普遍，CAD 分析仍然有助于理解Third, the CAD analysis is predicated on a relatively stable distinction between the retrieval models. This distinction may evolve as AI platforms converge on DCRM capabilities. However, even if DCRM becomes universal, the CAD analysis would remain relevant for understanding过渡动力学transition dynamics——鸿沟存在的时期将产生分配后果，这种后果在技术差距缩小后可能会持续存在。—the period during which the divide exists will have distributional consequences that may persist after the technical gap closes.

6.2 未来的方向Future Directions

我们确定了未来工作的三个优先方向。首先，实证研究测量了上下文检索架构对不同语料库大小和工作类型的知识工作者群体的生产力影响。其次，分析 DCRM 采用的决定因素——哪些组织、专业领域和用户群体实现了 DCRM 访问，以及通过什么途径。第三，上下文检索架构的治理分析作为人工智能平台监管的一个维度，包括透明度要求和潜在的设计要求。We identify three priority directions for future work. First, empirical studies measuring the productivity effects of context retrieval architecture across knowledge-worker populations differing in corpus size and work type. Second, analysis of the determinants of DCRM adoption—which organizations, professional domains, and user populations achieve DCRM access, and through what pathways. Third, governance analysis of context retrieval architecture as a dimension of AI platform regulation, including transparency requirements and potential design mandates.

For knowledge-intensive workers whose professional capital is distributed across large accumulated corpora, it represents a qualitative threshold in AI usefulness. The threshold dynamics of the CAD mean that users on opposite sides are not simply experiencing better or worse AI—they are experiencing different categories of cognitive partnership with AI systems.

随着人工智能代理更加深入地融入知识工作，决定上下文检索的架构选择将越来越多地影响人工智能生产力收益的分配。 MCP 作为开放基础设施的迅速采用——从 2024 年 11 月的新颖协议到 2025 年 12 月所有主要人工智能提供商支持的 Linux 基金会标准——表明通用 DCRM 的技术能力已经建立。这种能力是否会转化为公平的访问，还是将技术复杂、资源充足的知识工作者的现有优势结合起来，将取决于平台设计、组织部署和治理方面的选择，而这些选择尚未得到充分理解。确保这些选择是透明地做出的，并确保由此产生的不平等得到承认和解决，需要分析框架来关注两个层面的分析以及连接它们的机制。As AI agents become more deeply integrated into knowledge work, the architectural choices that determine context retrieval will increasingly shape the distribution of AI productivity benefits. The rapid adoption of MCP as open infrastructure—from a novel protocol in November 2024 to a Linux Foundation standard backed by all major AI providers by December 2025—demonstrates that the technical capacity for universal DCRM is already being built. Whether this capacity translates into equitable access, or instead compounds existing advantages among technically sophisticated, well-resourced knowledge workers, will depend on choices in platform design, organizational deployment, and governance that are not yet well understood. Ensuring that these choices are made transparently, and that the resulting inequality is recognized and addressed, requires analytical frameworks that attend to both levels of analysis—and to the mechanisms that connect them.
