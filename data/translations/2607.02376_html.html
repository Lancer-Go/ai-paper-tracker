安全关键型实时自治系统的硬件强制语义协调Hardware-Enforced Semantic Coordination for Safety-Critical Real-Time Autonomous Systems

Uwe M. Borghoff    Paolo Bottoni    Remo Pareschi
摘要Abstract

代理人工智能的最新进展正在产生日益复杂的自主系统，这些系统集成了大型语言模型、世界模型、优化引擎、专门的神经架构、自主平台和人类操作员。虽然当前的许多研究都集中在提高推理能力上，但安全关键的实时部署还需要在不确定性下同时运行的异构组件之间进行有界且可验证的协调。 软件介导的协调在有限延迟、确定性协调和可执行的安全保证至关重要的领域存在基本限制。Recent advances in agentic AI are producing increasingly complex autonomous systems that integrate large language models, world models, optimization engines, specialized neural architectures, autonomous platforms, and human operators. While much current research focuses on improving reasoning capabilities, safety-critical real-time deployment also requires bounded and verifiable coordination among heterogeneous components operating concurrently under uncertainty. Software-mediated coordination presents fundamental limitations in domains where bounded latency, deterministic coordination, and enforceable safety guarantees are essential.

因此，我们提出了一种硬件强制语义协调架构，其中选定的协调语义通过现场可编程门阵列（FPGA）直接在硬件级别实现。 该方法建立在基于主题的通信空间 Petri 网（TB-CSPN）框架之上，该框架将语义推理与交互管理分开。Hence, we propose a hardware-enforced semantic coordination architecture in which selected coordination semantics are implemented directly at the hardware level via field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs). The approach builds on the Topic-Based Communication Space Petri Net (TB–CSPN) framework, which separates semantic reasoning from interaction management.

在这种方法中，选定的 TB-CSPN 协调机制被映射到 FPGA 原语，创建硬件本机语义协调层。 重点不是加速，而是直接在硬件中强制执行时间同步、语义门控、授权约束和有界协调行为。 语义推理仍然是自适应的和软件驱动的，而嵌入式协调语义则变得确定性。In this approach, selected TB–CSPN coordination mechanisms are mapped onto FPGA primitives, creating a hardware-native semantic coordination layer. Focus is not on acceleration, but on enforcing temporal synchronization, semantic gating, authorization constraints, and bounded coordination behavior directly in hardware. Semantic reasoning remains adaptive and software-driven, while embedded coordination semantics become deterministic.

I 动机Motivation

代理人工智能的主要研究方向是改进推理：更好的基础模型、更好的规划器以及符号和子符号组件的更好集成。这些进步是真实且必要的。 然而，它们不足以满足安全关键的实时部署。 原因是实时自治系统不会失败主要是因为它们的推理组件很弱。它们之所以失败，是因为推理组件之间的交互是无限制的、不确定的且不可执行的。多代理系统可能行为不当，不是因为任何单个代理推理不正确，而是因为消息到达太晚、同步条件解释不一致，或者在操作压力下绕过了授权步骤。在实时自治系统中，协调本身就是一个首要的安全问题。 当前的代理人工智能系统依赖于围绕异步消息传递、API 级交互和以云为中心的执行模型构建的基于软件的协调框架。这些机制非常适合许多非关键应用程序，在这些应用程序中可以容忍偶尔的延迟、重试或不一致的响应时间。然而，在实时和对抗环境中，它们面临着重要的限制：调度决策通常是不确定的，延迟可能会发生不可预测的变化，并发交互可能会引起竞争条件或不明确的同步行为。由于协调通常分布在软件服务、消息队列、中间件和远程执行环境中，因此审核结果行为或证明安全约束的执行可能很困难。The dominant research direction in agentic AI is improving reasoning: better foundation models, better planners, and better integration of symbolic and sub-symbolic components. These advances are real and necessary. They are, however, insufficient for safety-critical real-time deployment. The reason is that real-time autonomous systems do not fail primarily because their reasoning components are weak. They fail because the interactions between reasoning components are unbounded, non-deterministic, and unenforceable. A multi-agent system may misbehave not because any single agent reasons incorrectly, but because messages arrive too late, synchronization conditions are interpreted inconsistently, or authorization steps are bypassed under operational pressure. In real-time autonomous systems, coordination itself is a first-class safety concern. Current agentic AI systems depend on software-based coordination frameworks built around asynchronous messaging, API-level interaction, and cloud-centric execution models. These mechanisms are well-suited to many non-critical applications, in which occasional delays, retries, or inconsistent response times can be tolerated. In real-time and adversarial environments, however, they face important limitations: scheduling decisions are often non-deterministic, latency can vary unpredictably, and concurrent interactions may give rise to race conditions or ambiguous synchronization behavior. As coordination is typically distributed across software services, message queues, middleware, and remote execution environments, auditing the resulting behavior, or proving enforcement of safety constraints, can be difficult.

因此，核心挑战不仅是让智能体更有能力，而且还要让它们的交互更加可靠。软件协调本身可以提供适应性，但不一定提供确定性定时或不可绕过的安全执行。在实时自治系统中，协调本身就成为首要的安全问题。单个代理人有效地推理或产生适合当地的行动是不够的；它们的相互作用也必须发生在可预测的时间和逻辑边界内。多代理系统可能失败，不是因为任何单个推理组件有缺陷，而是因为消息到达太晚、同步条件解释不一致，或者在操作压力下绕过授权步骤。The central challenge is therefore not only to make agents more capable, but also to make their interactions more dependable. Software coordination alone provides adaptability, but it does not necessarily provide deterministic timing or non-bypassable safety enforcement. In real-time autonomous systems, coordination itself becomes a first-class safety concern. It is not sufficient for individual agents to reason effectively or generate locally appropriate actions; their interactions must also occur within predictable temporal and logical boundaries. A multi-agent system may fail not because any single reasoning component is defective, but because messages arrive too late, synchronization conditions are interpreted inconsistently, or authorization steps are bypassed under operational pressure.

解决这一挑战的一种可能方法是 TB-CSPN 框架，它通过主题介导的 Petri 网结构使协调语义变得明确。 TB-CSPN 没有将交互视为软件组件之间非正式的消息交换，而是将协调表示为结构化令牌流，具有系统模型中定义的同步点、有界决策窗口和授权条件。因此，交换信息的含义和操作角色成为系统模型内的明确协调元素。这在代理的推理内容和它们的交互如何受到限制之间提供了更清晰的区分。 之前有关“守卫群”的工作通过分层自治、分离语义推理、协调、任务形成和物理安全执行来扩展这一想法。这使得自适应人工智能组件能够保持灵活性，同时较低层施加更严格的操作限制。目前的工作通过询问协调层本身是否可以在硬件中实现、将选定的协调规则转变为确定性和可执行的执行属性来延续这一轨迹。One possible approach to addressing this challenge is the TB–CSPN framework, which makes coordination semantics explicit through topic-mediated Petri-net structures. Instead of treating interaction as an informal exchange of messages among software components, TB–CSPN represents coordination as a structured token flow, with synchronization points, bounded decision windows, and authorization conditions defined within the system model. The meaning and operational role of exchanged information therefore become explicit coordination elements within the system model. This provides a clearer separation between what agents reason about and how their interactions are constrained. Previous work on Guarded Swarms extended this idea through layered autonomy, separating semantic reasoning, coordination, mission formation, and physical safety enforcement. This allows adaptive AI components to remain flexible while lower layers impose stronger operational constraints. The present work continues this trajectory by asking whether the coordination layer itself can be implemented in hardware, turning selected coordination rules into deterministic and enforceable execution properties.

FPGA 非常适合这个角色，因为它们将硬件级执行与大量可配置性结合在一起。与固定功能电路不同，它们可以重新编程以实现不同的数字逻辑结构，这使得它们对于同时需要性能和适应性的系统具有吸引力。它们广泛应用于需要低延迟处理、快速数字信号处理和灵活电路修改的场合。因此，FPGA 不仅用于原型设计，还用于在严格的实时、资源和时序限制下运行的已部署系统。FPGAs are well suited to this role because they combine hardware-level execution with substantial configurability. Unlike fixed-function circuits, they can be reprogrammed to implement different digital logic structures, making them attractive for systems that require both performance and adaptability. They are widely used where low-latency processing, fast digital signal handling, and flexible circuit modification are essential. Consequently, FPGAs are used not only for prototyping but also in deployed systems operating under stringent real-time, resource, and timing constraints.

它们的相关性在自主和国防/安全相关应用中尤其明显。 例如，基于 FPGA 的设计已用于根据无人机视频流对军用车辆进行实时机载分类Their relevance is especially clear in autonomous and defense/security-related applications. 这些特性使 FPGA 成为实现本工作中提出的协调层的自然候选者。目标不是将 FPGA 用作通用 AI 加速器，而是利用其确定性执行模型来实现选定的协调功能。通过将同步、令牌处理、时序约束和授权逻辑映射到可编程硬件上，系统可以保留基于软件的语义推理的适应性，同时在硬件级别强制执行关键交互规则。These properties make FPGAs a natural candidate for implementing the coordination layer proposed in this work. The objective is not to use the FPGA as a general-purpose AI accelerator, but to exploit its deterministic execution model for selected coordination functions. By mapping synchronization, token handling, timing constraints, and authorization logic onto programmable hardware, the system can preserve the adaptability of software-based semantic reasoning while enforcing critical interaction rules at the hardware level.

Ⅱ-AII-A 语义推理层Semantic Reasoning Layer

该软件层包括系统的自适应推理组件：基于人工智能的顾问代理、优化模块、概率推理机制和传感器解释服务。这些组件处理任务目标、环境观测、操作员输入和特定于平台的约束，以生成建议、计划或候选行动。 它们不是确定性的执行保证，而是提供灵活性和上下文智能，依赖于大型语言模型、世界模型、统计估计器、基于学习的感知模块或搜索和优化程序。这些方法本质上是自适应的，可以根据上下文、不确定性、模型状态或输入历史产生不同的输出。在语义推理的层面上，这是可以接受的并且通常是可取的。然而，由于这些组件是自适应的、概率性的和上下文敏感的，因此在将操作发布到物理系统之前，需要一个协调层来施加定时、同步、授权和安全约束。This software layer comprises the system’s adaptive reasoning components: AI-based consultant agents, optimization modules, probabilistic inference mechanisms, and sensor interpretation services. These components process mission goals, environmental observations, operator input, and platform-specific constraints to generate recommendations, plans, or candidate actions. Rather than deterministic execution guarantees, they provide flexibility and contextual intelligence, relying on, e.g., large language models, world models, statistical estimators, learning-based perception modules, or search and optimization procedures. These methods are inherently adaptive and can produce different outputs depending on context, uncertainty, model state, or input history. This is acceptable and often desirable at the level of semantic reasoning. However, because these components are adaptive, probabilistic, and context-sensitive, a coordination layer is necessary to impose timing, synchronization, authorization, and safety constraints before actions are released to the physical system.

Ⅱ-BII-B 基于 FPGA 的语义协调层FPGA-Based Semantic Coordination Layer

这一层构成了本文的核心贡献。它将选定的 TB-CSPN 协调机制映射到 FPGA 可执行逻辑上，将关键交互规则从软件转移到可编程硬件。 FPGA 层处理紧凑的语义标记，根据其主题和代理标识符对其进行路由，并在发布协调操作之前强制执行同步条件。 FPGA 层实现了任务形成的有限时间窗口、集体决策的基于阈值的触发规则以及防止过时或过早令牌影响系统行为的时间门。 FPGA 层还可以支持授权屏障，以确保在敏感转换发生之前满足人工批准或监督约束。This layer constitutes the paper’s core contribution. It maps selected TB-CSPN coordination mechanisms onto FPGA-executable logic, moving critical interaction rules from software to programmable hardware. The FPGA layer processes compact semantic tokens, routes them based on their topic and agent identifiers, and enforces synchronization conditions before releasing coordinated actions. The FPGA layer implements bounded time windows for mission formation, threshold-based firing rules for collective decisions, and temporal gates that prevent stale or premature tokens from affecting system behavior. The FPGA layer can also support authorization barriers to ensure that human approval or supervisory constraints are met before sensitive transitions occur.

此外，FPGA 层可以对公平策略和有界任务发布条件进行编码，以减轻特定代理、主题或令牌类别主导协调过程的风险。这些功能不需要 FPGA 在完整的符号或概率意义上执行语义推理。相反，硬件层强制执行与已解释的语义标记相关的协调规则。因此，自适应推理仍然保留在软件中，而时间敏感的协调约束则通过确定性的硬件行为来强制执行。Additionally, the FPGA layer can encode fairness policies and bounded mission-release conditions to mitigate the risk that specific agents, topics, or token classes dominate the coordination process. These functions do not require the FPGA to perform semantic reasoning in the full symbolic or probabilistic sense. Rather, the hardware layer enforces the coordination rules associated with already-interpreted semantic tokens. Thus, adaptive reasoning remains in software, whereas timing-sensitive coordination constraints are enforced with deterministic hardware behavior.

该架构不是在硬件中存储大量语义负载，而是使用由索引元数据字段组成的紧凑语义令牌，其中可能包括主题标识符、代理标识符、置信度、时间戳、生存时间 (TTL) 值和优先级标志。丰富的语义内容保留在软​​件层或外部存储器中，而现场可编程门阵列 (FPGA) 仅处理与协调相关的内容的表示。这种设计使硬件逻辑保持轻量级、可预测性并且适合实时执行。因此，FPGA不被视为通用的符号推理引擎。相反，它充当语义协调处理器，评估令牌属性，强制执行定时和同步规则，并保证确定性硬件约束下的交互语义。Instead of storing large semantic payloads in hardware, the architecture uses compact semantic tokens composed of indexed metadata fields, which may include a topic identifier, an agent identifier, a confidence level, a timestamp, a time-to-live (TTL) value, and priority flags. The rich semantic content remains in the software layer or external memory while the field-programmable gate array (FPGA) processes only the representation of that content relevant to coordination. This design keeps the hardware logic lightweight, predictable, and suitable for real-time execution. Therefore, the FPGA is not treated as a general symbolic reasoning engine. Rather, it functions as a semantic coordination processor that evaluates token attributes, enforces timing and synchronization rules, and guarantees interaction semantics under deterministic hardware constraints.

Ⅱ-CII-C 物理安全层Physical Safety Layer

在最低级别，模拟或嵌入式安全机制通过靠近执行器操作并独立于更高级别的推理和协调执行限制来提供直接的物理保护。它们的功能包括执行器夹紧、推力限制、紧急超越以及在通信、计算或监控不可靠的情况下的故障安全行为。通过将这些防护措施放置在物理接口附近，系统可以降低软件错误、消息延迟、代理受损或导致不安全运动的错误任务决策的风险。At the lowest level, analog or embedded safety mechanisms provide direct physical protection by operating close to the actuators and enforcing limits independently of higher-level reasoning and coordination. Their functions include actuator clamping, thrust limitation, emergency override, and fail-safe behavior in the event of unreliable communication, computation, or supervision. By placing these safeguards near the physical interface, the system mitigates the risk of software errors, delayed messages, compromised agents, or incorrect mission decisions resulting in unsafe motion.

这保留了守卫群方法所提出的有限自治原则。 虽然代理可以自适应推理、通过基于令牌的机制进行协调并追求任务目标，但本地安全执法限制了他们的操作行为。 物理层充当数字决策和现实世界驱动之间的最终屏障。在不取代协调或推理的情况下，它确保即使在退化或对抗性条件下，自主权在操作上仍然受到限制。This preserves the principle of bounded autonomy posited by the Guarded Swarms approach. While agents can reason adaptively, coordinate via token-based mechanisms, and pursue mission objectives, local safety enforcement constrains their operational behavior. The physical layer acts as a final barrier between digital decision-making and real-world actuation. Without replacing coordination or reasoning, it ensures that autonomy remains operationally bounded, even under degraded or adversarial conditions.

IV 协调而不是推理Coordination Rather Than Reasoning

所提出的架构采取了特定的立场：实时自治系统的核心工程挑战不是改进推理，而是加强协调。更快的推理可以改善感知、规划或决策支持，但它本身并不能确保代理正确同步、尊重授权约束或在可预测的时间范围内发布操作。The proposed architecture adopts a specific stance: that the central engineering challenge of real-time autonomous systems is not improving reasoning but enforcing coordination. Faster inference can improve perception, planning, or decision support, but it does not on its own ensure that agents synchronize correctly, respect authorization constraints, or release actions within predictable temporal bounds.

V 设计挑战Design Challenges

所提出的架构提出了一些研究挑战，在实际系统中实现 FPGA 支持的 TB-CSPN 协调之前必须解决这些挑战。这些挑战不仅涉及硬件实现，还涉及语义的保存、时间正确性和操作限制下的适应性。The proposed architecture raises several research challenges that must be addressed before FPGA-backed TB–CSPN coordination can be realized in practical systems. These challenges concern not only hardware implementation but also the preservation of semantic meaning, temporal correctness, and adaptability under operational constraints.

V-A 语义标记压缩Semantic Token Compactification

硬件执行需要紧凑的令牌表示。 TB-CSPN 令牌无法在 FPGA 内部携带任意大的语义有效负载。相反，丰富的语义内容必须保留在软件组件、外部存储器或共享知识结构中，而硬件层则处理索引元数据，例如主题或代理标识符、时间戳、置信度值、TTL 字段和优先级标志。挑战在于确定哪些语义属性对于协调至关重要，以及如何在不丢失正确交互管理所需信息的情况下对它们进行编码。Hardware execution requires compact token representations. TB–CSPN tokens cannot carry arbitrarily large semantic payloads inside the FPGA. Instead, rich semantic content must remain in software components, external memory, or shared knowledge structures, while the hardware layer processes indexed metadata such as topic or agent identifiers, timestamps, confidence values, TTL fields, and priority flags. The challenge is to determine which semantic attributes are essential for coordination and how to encode them without losing the information required for correct interaction management.

V-B 秩序与公平Ordering and Fairness

TB-CSPN 令牌在语义上是有区别的，这与许多经典 Petri-net 设置中的令牌不同。因此，令牌排序会影响系统行为。从硬件角度来看有效的调度策略仍然可能产生不良的语义效果，例如延迟高优先级令牌、反复偏向某些代理或使特定主题匮乏。因此，公平策略必须与语义一致性规则一起设计，以便有界执行不会以协调正确性为代价。TB–CSPN tokens are semantically differentiated, unlike tokens in many classical Petri-net settings. As a result, token ordering can influence system behavior. A scheduling policy that is efficient from a hardware perspective may still produce undesirable semantic effects, such as delaying high-priority tokens, repeatedly favoring certain agents, or starving specific topics. Fairness policies must therefore be designed together with semantic coherence rules, so that bounded execution does not come at the cost of coordination correctness.

电压-CV-C 时间协调Temporal Coordination

所提出的模型还需要明确的时间语义。同步窗口、TTL 过期、阈值触发和任务释放条件取决于时间敏感的令牌评估。这些机制不同于经典的 Petri-net 触发规则，因为转换可能不仅取决于令牌可用性，还取决于有效性间隔、截止日期和时间顺序。因此，FPGA 实现必须支持精确定时，同时保持与更高级别协调语义的兼容。The proposed model also requires explicit temporal semantics. Synchronization windows, TTL expiration, threshold firing, and mission-release conditions depend on time-sensitive token evaluation. These mechanisms differ from classical Petri-net firing rules because transitions may depend not only on token availability, but also on validity intervals, deadlines, and temporal ordering. FPGA implementation must therefore support precise timing while remaining compatible with higher-level coordination semantics.

维-DV-D 动态重新配置Dynamic Reconfiguration

最后一个挑战涉及实际任务中的动态重新配置。 随着智能体的加入、离开、失败或接受新目标，协调结构可能必须不断发展。在保留硬件级保证的同时支持这种适应仍然是一个悬而未决的问题，也是未来工作的中心方向。A final challenge concerns dynamic reconfiguration in real missions. Coordination structures may have to evolve as agents join, leave, fail, or receive new objectives. Supporting such adaptation while preserving hardware-level guarantees remains an open problem and a central direction for future work.

VI 应用领域Application Domains

所提出的架构适用于多个自治或半自治实体必须在实时、安全关键、资源受限或对抗条件下进行协调的系统类别。在这种环境中，仅靠自适应推理是不够的：异构代理之间的交互还必须保持时间限制、可审计和可操作可执行。The proposed architecture is intended for classes of systems in which multiple autonomous or semi-autonomous entities must coordinate under real-time, safety-critical, resource-constrained, or adversarial conditions. In such environments, adaptive reasoning alone is insufficient: interactions among heterogeneous agents must also remain temporally bounded, auditable, and operationally enforceable.

自主无人机群提供了一个代表性的例子。这些系统中的任务执行取决于及时同步、有限通信、集体行动的可靠授权以及通信退化或敌对性干扰下的恢复能力。类似的要求也出现在基于边缘的防御系统中，其中协调决策通常必须在严格的延迟和安全约束下在操作平台附近做出。Autonomous drone swarms provide a representative example. Mission execution in these systems depends on timely synchronization, bounded communication, reliable authorization of collective actions, and resilience under degraded communication or adversarial interference. Similar requirements arise in edge-based defense systems, where coordination decisions must often be made near the operational platform under strict latency and safety constraints.

应急管理和搜救行动进一步说明了该方法的相关性。在这些场景中，异构代理结合了传感器解释、路线规划、人工监督和快速任务分配，同时在不可预测或退化的环境中运行。类似的协调挑战也出现在精准农业和智能农业等民用领域，其中分布式平台协调大范围的监测、处理和资源分配。包括电网监控和基础设施保护在内的关键能源系统还需要在时间和操作限制下传感、控制和响应组件之间进行可靠的交互。Emergency management and search-and-rescue operations further illustrate the relevance of the approach. In these scenarios, heterogeneous agents combine sensor interpretation, route planning, human supervision, and rapid task allocation while operating in unpredictable or degraded environments. Comparable coordination challenges arise in civilian domains such as precision and smart agriculture, where distributed platforms coordinate monitoring, treatment, and resource allocation across large areas. Critical energy systems, including grid monitoring and infrastructure protection, also require dependable interaction among sensing, control, and response components under temporal and operational constraints.

这些例子具有代表性而非详尽无遗。更一般地说，该体系结构的目标系统是自适应语义推理必须与有界且可执行的协调语义共存。因此，所提出的方法的更广泛的贡献不在于任何特定的应用领域，而在于为实时自主系统提供物理接地的协调基础。These examples are representative rather than exhaustive. More generally, the architecture targets systems in which adaptive semantic reasoning must coexist with bounded and enforceable coordination semantics. The broader contribution of the proposed approach therefore lies not in any specific application domain, but in providing a physically grounded coordination substrate for real-time autonomous systems.

七VII定位和范围Positioning and Scope

本文定位为概念和架构贡献，而不是实施或基准研究。它不提供完整的 FPGA 原型、硬件综合结果或经验性能测量。相反，其目的是定义实时代理系统中 FPGA 支持的语义协调的设计空间，并阐明如何将选定的 TB-CSPN 协调机制映射到可编程硬件上。This paper is positioned as a conceptual and architectural contribution rather than an implementation or benchmarking study. It does not present a complete FPGA prototype, hardware synthesis results, or empirical performance measurements. Instead, its purpose is to define the design space for FPGA-backed semantic coordination in real-time agentic systems and to clarify how selected TB–CSPN coordination mechanisms could be mapped onto programmable hardware.

该贡献包括四个主要内容。 首先，它提出了一种面向设计的架构，将自适应语义推理、硬件强制协调和本地物理安全执行分开。 其次，它概述了一个执行模型，其中紧凑的语义令牌、时间门、同步条件和授权屏障由基于 FPGA 的协调层管理。 第三，它确定了令牌压缩、有界内存、时序语义、公平性、动态重新配置以及软件推理组件和硬件逻辑之间的接口的实现约束和权衡。最后，它概述了硬件原生语义协调的研究路线图，涵盖代表性实时场景中的形式规范、FPGA 实现、验证和评估。The contribution consists of four main elements. First, it proposes a design-oriented architecture separating adaptive semantic reasoning, hardware-enforced coordination, and local physical safety enforcement. Second, it outlines an execution model in which compact semantic tokens, temporal gates, synchronization conditions, and authorization barriers are managed by an FPGA-based coordination layer. Third, it identifies implementation constraints and trade-offs for token compactification, bounded memory, timing semantics, fairness, dynamic reconfiguration, and the interface between software-reasoning components and hardware logic. Finally, it outlines a research roadmap toward hardware-native semantic coordination, covering formal specification, FPGA realization, verification, and evaluation in representative real-time scenarios.

因此，本文为更广泛的研究计划迈出了第一步。 它并没有声称所有协调逻辑都应该转移到硬件，或者单独使用 FPGA 执行来解决自治系统的挑战，而是认为具有强大时序和安全要求的选定协调功能可能会受益于硬件级执行。因此，核心贡献是阐明基于 TB-CSPN 协调语义的硬件强制语义协调为何以及如何支持更具确定性、可审计性和安全意识的自治系统。Accordingly, the paper offers an initial step toward a broader research program. Rather than claiming that all coordination logic should be moved to hardware, or that FPGA execution alone solves the challenges of autonomous systems, it argues that selected coordination functions with strong timing and safety requirements may benefit from hardware-level enforcement. The central contribution is therefore to articulate why and how hardware-enforced semantic coordination, grounded in TB–CSPN coordination semantics, could support more deterministic, auditable, and safety-aware autonomous systems.

This may represent an important step toward deployable, verifiable, and safety-aware autonomous systems operating in real-world environments.

未来工作的一个重要方向涉及硬件强制协调与退化或对抗条件下自适应任务级重新配置之间的相互作用。在当前实现的 Guarded Swarms 方法中An important direction for future work concerns the interaction between hardware-enforced coordination and adaptive mission-level reconfiguration under degraded or adversarial conditions. More advanced coordination strategies could allow stress or abort signals to propagate back to the coordination layer, triggering collective reassessment, swarm-wide abort conditions, or dynamic mission redistribution among the remaining agents.

支持自适应协调，同时保持有限的时序、通信约束和硬件级安全保证仍然是一个开放的挑战。解决这个问题可能需要新的协调语义和通信机制，这些机制在延迟、带宽和分布式平台几何结构的严格约束下运行。Supporting adaptive coordination while preserving bounded timing, communication constraints, and hardware-level safety guarantees remains an open challenge. Addressing this problem will likely require new coordination semantics and communication mechanisms that operate under strict constraints on latency, bandwidth, and distributed platform geometry.
