摘要Abstract

语言模型部署在需要划分的环境中：不应公开系统提示、对用户隐藏思维链推理、敏感数据通过共享上下文传递。我们测试模型是否可以在书写中保留提示信息。我们给每个模型一个秘密单词，并指示不要透露它，然后要求它写一个故事。第二个模型试图在二元辨别测试中识别故事中的秘密。这个秘密词从未真正出现在任何输出中，但我们测试的所有五个前沿模型都通过主题选择、图像和背景以主题方式泄露它，其泄露率与偶然性显着不同，高达 79%。 当被告知要主动隐藏秘密时，模型会写道Language models are deployed in settings that require compartmentalization: system prompts should not be disclosed, chain-of-thought reasoning is hidden from users, and sensitive data passes through shared contexts. We test whether models can keep prompted information out of their writing. We give each model a secret word with instructions not to reveal it, then ask it to write a story. A second model tries to identify the secret from the story in a binary discrimination test. The secret word never appears literally in any output, but all five frontier models we test leak it thematically—through topic choice, imagery, and setting—at rates significantly different from chance, up to 79%. When told to actively hide the secret, models write远离away from它，而且这种回避本身是可以察觉的。泄漏是跨模型可读的， 在两个模型系列中，它会随着模型大小的变化而急剧变化，并且对于像笑话这样的简短写作来说会完全消失。为模型提供一个诱饵概念来“改为关注”，部分地将泄漏从真正的秘密转移到诱饵上。关注秘密似乎打开了前沿法学硕士无法关闭的信息通道，即使在接到指示时也是如此。it, and this avoidance is itself detectable. The leakage is cross-model readable, scales sharply with model size within two model families, and disappears entirely for short-form writing like jokes. Giving the model a decoy concept to “focus on instead” partially redirects the leakage from the real secret to the decoy. Attending to a secret appears to open up an information channel that frontier LLMs cannot close, even when instructed to.

4 讨论Discussion

A secret in the prompt shapes the model’s writing, and another model can detect that shaping. The literal word is always suppressed, but the concept is not. This holds across seven models, three word sets, system prompt vs. user prompt, and two independent cross-model guessers.

附录EAppendix E位置偏差Position bias

当仅测试一个订单时，多个模型在 2AFC 任务中表现出强烈的位置偏好。例如，在单顺序试验中，DeepSeek 在 76% 的情况下选择“故事 1”，而 Sonnet 在 72% 的情况下选择“故事 1”，无论哪个故事包含秘密。这种偏差会增加或减少原始准确性，具体取决于秘密恰好占据的位置。Several models show strong position preferences in the 2AFC task when only one order is tested. For example, in single-order trials, DeepSeek chose “Story 1” 76% of the time and Sonnet chose “Story 1” 72% of the time, regardless of which story contained the secret. This bias inflates or deflates raw accuracy depending on which position the secret happens to occupy.

我们通过以两个顺序运行每对（目标在位置 1 和位置 2）并求平均值来控制这一点。这种双阶设计完美地消除了位置偏差：始终选择故事 1 的模型在 50% 的时间内都是正确的，无论其真实的辨别能力如何。论文中报告的所有数字均采用双阶设计。We control for this by running every pair in both orders (target in position 1 and position 2) and averaging. This both-orders design perfectly cancels position bias: a model that always picks Story 1 will be correct 50% of the time regardless of its true discrimination ability. All numbers reported in the paper use the both-orders design.
