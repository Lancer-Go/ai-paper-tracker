用于自主边缘资源分配的自校准代理人工智能框架A Self-Calibrating Agentic AI Framework for Autonomous Edge Resource Allocation

Fin Gentzen, Marla Grunewald, Iulisloi Zacarias, Mounir Bensalem and Admela Jukan
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地部署为自主代理，从静态对话界面过渡到能够进行复杂推理、工具执行和决策的动态系统。然而，由于开放环境中缺乏可靠的地面事实以及随着时间的推移操作漂移增加的风险，这些代理人工智能系统的操作可靠性从根本上受到挑战。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出并实验评估了一个代理人工智能框架，旨在加强法学硕士驱动系统中的自主完整性。我们设计了一种自校准机制，通过结合 ARIMA 预测器来减轻漂移并动态逼近地面实况，而不需要持续的人工监督。为了证明我们方法的有效性和可靠性，我们将其应用于分析边缘计算网络中零知识工作负载的资源使用情况的复杂领域。实验结果表明，所提出的自校准代理框架成功地分析了零知识工作负载，在资源使用预测方面比基线LLM代理提高了91.7%，与纯分析相比，预测速度提高了71.7%，为在去中心化基础设施中部署自主人工智能奠定了坚实的基础。此外，使用所提出的 ARIMA 跳跃算法生成地面实况比标准 ARIMA 预测算法快 52%，同时达到相同的精度。Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents, transitioning from static conversational interfaces to dynamic systems capable of complex reasoning, tool execution, and decision-making. However, the operational reliability of these agentic AI systems is fundamentally challenged by the absence of reliable ground truth in open-ended environments and the risk of increasing operational drift over time. To address this challenge, we propose and experimentally evaluate an agentic AI framework, designed to enforce autonomous integrity within LLM-driven systems. We design a self-calibration mechanism that mitigates drift and dynamically approximates ground truth by incorporating an ARIMA forecaster, without requiring continuous human oversight. To demonstrate the effectiveness and reliability of our methodology, we apply it to the complex domain of profiling the resource usage of zero-knowledge workloads in edge computing networks. Experimental results show that the proposed self-calibrating agentic framework successfully profiles the zero-knowledge workloads, achieving a higher accuracy than baseline LLM agents by 91.7% for resource usage prediction and improving the prediction speed by 71.7% compared to pure profiling, establishing a robust foundation for deploying autonomous AI in decentralized infrastructures. Furthermore, the ground truth generation using the proposed ARIMA leaping algorithm is 52% faster than a standard ARIMA forecasting algorithm, while achieving the same accuracy.
