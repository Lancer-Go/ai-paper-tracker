摘要Abstract

X 波段 SAR 卫星 (8–12 GHz) 在灾害响应、环境监测和军事情报方面发挥着关键作用。然而，它们缺乏强大的物理层身份验证（PLA），这是一个与加密解决方案正交的安全层。现有的 PLA 系统通常基于射频指纹识别，通常仅限于 6 GHz 以下的频率，并且依赖于经典的深度学习。然而，这种方法不符合区分卫星硬件的 IQ 相位非线性。 在本文中，我们提出X-band SAR satellites (8–12 GHz) play a critical role in disaster response, environmental monitoring, and military intelligence. Yet, they lack robust physical-layer authentication (PLA), a security layer orthogonal to cryptographic solutions. Existing PLA systems, typically based on radio-frequency fingerprinting, are often limited to sub-6 GHz frequencies and rely on classical deep learning. However, this approach underfits the IQ phase nonlinearities that distinguish satellite hardware. The commercial expansion of X-band (8–12 GHz) SAR constellations has accelerated markedly: ICEYE—a Finnish private company owning the world’s largest synthetic aperture imaging radar constellation—now operates more than 60 active satellites. Its competitors, Capella Space and Beijing Smart Satellite Space Technology, are similarly scaling deployments. These systems are used for time-critical decisions in disaster management, environmental monitoring, agricultural surveillance, and national security intelligence[41, 12, 18, 24]。然而，它们的战略意义使其成为那些寻求伪造合成孔径雷达图像或破坏观测服务的对手的有吸引力的目标。. Their strategic significance, however, makes them an attractive target for adversaries seeking to forge SAR imagery or disrupt observation services.

然而，卫星网络并未普遍执行身份验证机制Yet, satellite networks do not universally enforce authentication mechanisms[13]。即使存在加密保护，它们仍然容易受到密钥泄露、旧硬件无法进行软件更新以及在长达十年的任务生命周期中最终出现加密泄露的影响. Even where cryptographic protections exist, they remain vulnerable to key leakage, legacy hardware incapacity for software updates, and eventual cryptographic compromise over decade-long mission lifetimes[27]。物理层安全 (PLS) 通过独立机制解决此漏洞：硬件引起的信号损伤使每个无线电发射器在物理上都是独一无二的，并且极难复制. Physical-layer security (PLS) addresses this vulnerability via an independent mechanism: hardware-induced signal impairments make every radio transmitter physically unique and are extremely hard to reproduce[36]。虽然这些物理缺陷提供了强大的身份验证锚点，但由于恶劣的太空环境，它们可能会在较长时间内发生漂移，因此需要通过迁移学习定期更新模型以保持指纹准确性。. While these physical impairments provide a robust authentication anchor, they are subject to drift over extended periods due to the harsh space environment, necessitating periodic model updates via transfer learning to maintain fingerprint accuracy.

Ⅱ-AII-A 合成孔径雷达成像卫星SAR Imaging Satellites

合成孔径雷达（SAR）是一种主动微波遥感技术。 SAR 卫星用无线电脉冲照射地面，并根据反射回波重建高分辨率图像。由于该系统依靠自身照明并以穿透云层的微波频率运行，因此 SAR 可以在白天或晚上获取图像，不受天气影响。相比之下，无源光学卫星不具备这种能力，这使得合成孔径雷达成为灾难响应、海上监视和军事情报的首选工具Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) is an active microwave remote sensing technology. A SAR satellite illuminates the ground with radio pulses and reconstructs a high-resolution image from the reflected echoes. Because the system relies on its own illumination and operates at microwave frequencies that penetrate clouds, SAR acquires imagery day or night, independently of weather. Passive optical satellites, by contrast, offer no such capability, which makes SAR the instrument of choice for disaster response, maritime surveillance, and military intelligence[41, 20, 24].

IV-B 瞄准成像脉冲的基本原理Rationale for Targeting Imaging Pulses

我们的测试平台专门捕获 SAR 成像脉冲，而不是 卫星的 X 波段数据下行链路。三个特性使成像脉冲成为物理层身份验证的自然选择。Our testbed exclusively captures SAR imaging pulses rather than the satellite’s X-band data downlink. Three properties make imaging pulses the natural choice for physical-layer authentication.

被动可用性。Passive availability.成像脉冲是在每次轨道通过期间向地球广播的高功率照明，并且可以由任何与卫星具有直接视线可见性的地面接收器收集。相比之下，数据下行链路是指向特定许可地面站的定向高增益波束，如果没有事先协调，特别是靠近目标接收器，则被动拦截是不切实际的。Imaging pulses are high-power illuminations broadcast toward Earth during every orbital pass, and can be collected by any ground receiver with direct line-of-sight visibility to the satellite. The data downlink, by contrast, is a directed high-gain beam steered toward a specific licensed ground station, making passive interception impractical without prior coordination, and in particular, proximity to the intended receiver.

信号丰富度。Signal richness.成像脉冲是宽带 LFM 线性调频脉冲，ICEYE 标准模式高达 299 MHz，以高功率传输。它们提供了一个大的频谱画布，可以在其中显示硬件损伤。数据下行链路采用窄带相位调制通信波形，例如QPSK或8PSK，其减少 带宽和不同的调制方案产生根本不同的损伤特征。因此，在一个指纹上训练的指纹无法替代另一个指纹。Imaging pulses are wideband LFM chirps, up to 299 MHz for ICEYE standard modes, which are transmitted at high power. They provide a large spectral canvas over which hardware impairments can manifest. The data downlink employs narrowband phase-modulated communication waveforms, e.g., QPSK or 8PSK, whose reduced bandwidth and distinct modulation scheme produce a fundamentally different impairment signature. As a result, a fingerprint trained on one cannot substitute for the other.

收集频率。Collection frequency.成像脉冲在每次通过的照明阶段连续发射，而数据下行链路会话相对较少，并且与许可下行链路站的可用性相关。这种不对称性极大地促进了数据集收集，既可以用于研究目的（独立的地面接收器可以在许多通道中积累大型语料库），也可以用于配备自己地面基础设施的星座运营商的操作部署。Imaging pulses are emitted continuously throughout the illumination phase of every pass, whereas data downlink sessions are comparatively infrequent and tied to the availability of licensed downlink stations. This asymmetry substantially facilitates dataset collection, both for research purposes—where independent ground receivers can accumulate large corpora across many passes—and for operational deployment by a constellation operator equipped with its own ground infrastructure.

在本文中，据我们所知，我们介绍了第一个结合量子和经典机器学习的混合架构，为在具有挑战性的 X 波段运行的卫星提供 PLA。 我们开发了In this paper we have introduced, to the best of our knowledge, the first hybrid architecture that combines quantum and classical ML to provide PLA to satellites operating in the challenging X-band. We have developed准SARQuaSAR，一种量子经典混合卷积架构，在 X 波段 SAR 卫星的二进制物理层认证中实现了 97.3% 的验证精度和宏 F1 = 0.973，在跨越 37 颗运行 ICEYE 卫星的 3.76 TB 真实世界 IQ 数据集上，其性能比纯经典基线高 7.5 个百分点。更重要的是，这些结果仅使用经典机器学习所需数据的十分之一即可获得。我们的框架也经过分析剖析：(i) 消融分析证实，变分量子电路提供了同等大小的经典层无法提供的非冗余判别信号，并且通过 IQ 原生幅度相位编码而不是传统的角度嵌入加载其输入，在训练次数减少 46% 的情况下，值得进一步提高 2.2 个百分点； (ii) 梯度显着性将身份验证决策本地化到原始 IQ 域中的亚毫秒脉冲起始窗口； (iii) 可解释性分析确定了量子和经典分支的相对贡献。最后，我们在三种对抗性攻击场景下测试了我们的解决方案——重放、精心设计的智商注入和星载欺骗——, a quantum-classical hybrid convolutional architecture, achieving 97.3% validation accuracy and macro-F1 = 0.973 in binary physical-layer authentication of X-band SAR satellites, outperforming a classical-only baseline by 7.5 percentage points on a 3.76 TB real-world IQ dataset spanning 37 operational ICEYE satellites.
