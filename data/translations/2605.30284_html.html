ProjectionBench：评估渐进式信息披露下法学硕士的科学假设生成ProjectionBench: Evaluating Scientific Hypothesis Generation in LLMs Under Progressive Information Disclosure

Andrew J. Lew, Yuan Cao & Markus J. Buehler
Unreasonable Labs
Mountain View, CA 94043, USA
{ajlew,yuancao,mbuehler}@unreasonablelabs.ai
摘要Abstract

科学发现本质上是一个创造性和不确定性的过程，需要超出已知知识回忆的推理。尽管已经提出了许多基准来通过多跳检索来评估大语言模型（LLM）在深度研究任务中的性能，但它们对真正的科学发现至关重要的创新推理能力在很大程度上尚未经过测试。我们引入了一个基准框架，用于评估科学发现和推理中的模型性能，从原始问题构建到经典的零假设检验。在我们的框架中，模型最初仅接收最近论文中的主题和研究问题，并逐步揭示技术细节。在信息披露的每个阶段，该模型的任务是生成解决研究问题的假设，将其与原始论文的结论进行比较，并通过构成原子声明的自动语义相似性进行评估。这种对与真实结论的语义分歧的渐进评估能够评估模型的创新性（在最少信息下）和基础推理能力（在完整的实验细节下），这对于使用法学硕士进行科学发现目的至关重要。我们的框架为系统评估法学硕士的科学推理和发现能力提供了基础，这对于推进下一代人工智能科学家/联合科学家系统的发展至关重要。具体来说，我们在这里评估了涵盖生物活性材料、机械材料和纳米材料的 45 篇论文中的 GPT-5、GPT-5.4、Gemini 2.5 pro 和 Gemini 3.1 pro 预览版。我们发现 GPT-5.4 和 Gemini 3.1 pro 的表现优于上一代同类产品，尤其是 GPT-5.4 即使在最小的背景下也能保持 0.7 的 F1 分数与真实结论的一致性。Scientific discovery is an inherently creative and uncertain process, requiring reasoning beyond the recall of known knowledge. While many benchmarks have been proposed to evaluate large language model (LLM) performance on deep research tasks via multi-hop retrieval, their innovative reasoning abilities essential for true scientific discovery remain largely untested. We introduce a benchmark framework for evaluating model performance in scientific discovery and reasoning, building up from a raw problem to the classical null hypothesis test. In our framework, models initially receive only the topic and research question from a recent paper, with technical details progressively revealed. At each stage of information disclosure, the model is tasked with generating hypotheses that address the research question, which is compared with the conclusions from the original paper and evaluated via automated semantic similarity of constituent atomic claims. This progressive evaluation of semantic divergence from ground-truth conclusions enables assessment of a model’s innovativeness (under minimal information) to grounded reasoning capabilities (under full experimental details), both critical for using LLMs for scientific discovery purposes. Our framework provides a foundation for systematically evaluating scientific reasoning and discovery capabilities in LLMs, crucial for advancing the development of next-generation AI scientist/co-scientist systems. Specifically, here we evaluate GPT-5, GPT-5.4, Gemini 2.5 pro, and Gemini 3.1 pro preview across 45 papers spanning bioactive materials, mechanical materials, and nanomaterials. We find that GPT-5.4 and Gemini 3.1 pro outperform their previous generation counterparts as expected, and GPT-5.4 in particular maintains 0.7 F1 score alignment with ground truth conclusions even under minimal context.

During earlier stages of scientific exploration, researchers may not yet have a dataset if they are still in the experimental ideation phase. At this stage, a researcher might have only a general topic and research question, and may be considering how a proposed experiment could shape or refine their further understanding of natural relationships. Despite progress in evaluation schemes, existing benchmarks primarily emphasize literature retrieval, citation alignment, or specific intensive analysis tasks; a lightweight, general probe of scientific discovery ability remains largely missing.

为了解决这一差距，我们提出了一个基准框架，通过建立零假设检验的经典结构来评估法学硕士的科学发现能力。由于假设创新性和推理准确性都是科学发现的核心，因此基准旨在捕获模型行为如何在不同级别的上下文信息中演变。具体来说，它涵盖从最小配置（仅提供主题和研究问题）到最大配置（包括基本事实零假设和实验程序）的设置。鉴于该模型以前从未观察到过论文，一个强大的科学发现模型应该能够提出新颖的假设，从原始研究问题中近似得出真实的结论，并在引入额外的实验背景时得出准确的结论。To address this gap, we propose a benchmarking framework that evaluates an LLM’s capacity for scientific discovery by building up to the classical structure of null hypothesis testing. Because both hypothesis innovativeness and reasoning accuracy are central to scientific discovery, the benchmark is designed to capture how model behavior evolves across different levels of contextual information. Specifically, it spans settings from a minimal configuration - where only the topic and research question is provided - to a maximal configuration that includes the ground truth null hypothesis and experimental procedures. Given papers never observed by the model before, a strong model for scientific discovery should be able to propose novel hypotheses that approximate ground-truth conclusions from a raw research question, and derive accurate conclusions when additional experimental context is introduced.

2 发现任务Discovery Task

在这里，我们在零假设检验的经典框架内考虑科学发现的任务。也就是说，实验“存在只是为了让事实有机会反驳原假设”（Fisher，1935）。在建立原假设检验的模拟中，我们将被测模型暴露给二元素提示。在第一个元素中，我们向模型提供了不同数量的研究背景。在第二个要素中，我们要求模型提供对研究问题的解决方案或结果的最佳预测。通过改变研究背景的数量，从原始主题和研究问题到包括基本零假设，然后包括特定的实验程序，我们测试了从头科学发现到结构化推理的整个模型能力。具体使用的框架见附录提示1。Here, we consider the task of scientific discovery within the classic framework of the null hypothesis test. Namely, that experiments “exist only in order to give the facts a chance of disproving the null hypothesis” (Fisher, 1935). In this emulation of building up to null hypothesis testing, we expose models-under-test to a two-element prompt. In the first element, we present a variable amount of research context to the model. In the second element, we ask for the model to provide its best projection on the solution or findings of the research question. By varying the amount of research context from just the raw topic and research question, to including a base null hypothesis, and then including a specific experimental procedure, we test the full gamut of model ability from de novo scientific discovery to structured reasoning. The specific framework used is in the Appendix as Prompt 1.

科学发现的理想模式应该将强大的创造力与严谨的推理结合起来。一个高度创造性的模型应该能够在最小的背景下产生新颖的见解，而一个强大的推理模型应该在提供实验程序时准确地推断出结论。我们的基准旨在捕获该范围的两端，从而能够全面评估模型的科学研究能力。An ideal model for scientific discovery should combine strong creativity with rigorous reasoning. A highly creative model should be able to generate novel insights even with minimal context, while a strong reasoning model should accurately infer conclusions when experimental procedures are provided. Our benchmark is designed to capture both ends of this spectrum, enabling a comprehensive assessment of a model’s capability in scientific research.

3 自动评分方法Automated Grading Method

3.1 细粒度分级方法Granular Grading Approach

一旦模型预测了实验过程的结果，我们就可以将该预测与已知的地面事实进行比较，以便对其性能进行评级。科学假设的规范表述集中于自变量和因变量之间的“如果/那么”或“何时/那么”式关系Once a model has projected the results of an experimental procedure, we can compare that projection to known ground truth in order to grade its performance. A canonical formulation of scientific hypotheses concentrates on “if/then” or “when/then” style relationships between independent and dependent variablesElsevier (2024)。然而，当比较预测结果和真实结果的内容时，根据研究问题的复杂性，陈述可能由多个这样的关系组成（例如，如果. However, when comparing the content of projected and ground truth results, statements could be composed of multiple such relationships depending on the complexity of the research question (e.g. IfXX，那么, thenYY和andZZ).

因此，在这里，我们首先将科学研究的潜在复杂结果分解为其原子声明，每个声明都格式化为自变量和因变量之间的关系。具体来说，自变量是由研究者系统地操纵的实验条件，因变量是在实验中作为自变量的实验操纵的结果而测量的结果。我们利用 GPT-5 作为评估模型，并使用附录中的提示 2，将科学结果陈述分解为其组成主张。Thus, here we first break down the potentially complex results of a scientific study into its atomic claims, with each claim formatted as a relationship between independent and dependent variables. Specifically, independent variables are the conditions of an experiment that are systematically manipulated by the investigator and dependent variables are the outcomes that are measured in an experiment as a result of experimental manipulation of the independent variables. We break down a scientific result statement into its constituent claims by leveraging GPT-5 as an evaluatory model, using Prompt 2 in the Appendix.

一旦我们将真实结果分解为其组成关系主张，我们就可以类似地从预测结果中提取关系主张。我们使用类似的提示进行此提取，只是现在使用附录中的提示 3 提供先前的事实声明列表并提示提取类似的陈述。Once we have the ground truth result broken up into its constituent relationship claims, we can analogously extract the relationship claims from the projected result. We utilize a similar prompt for this extraction, only now providing the previous list of ground truth claims and prompting for the extraction of analogous statements, using Prompt 3 in the Appendix.

最后，由于无法保证预测结果的声明与真实结果的声明一一对应，因此我们使用附录中的提示 4 从预测结果中提取与真实结果没有直接相似性的剩余无关声明。Finally, as there is no guarantee that the claims of the projected result map one-to-one with the claim of the ground truth result, we use Prompt 4 in the Appendix to extract remaining extraneous claims from the projected result that do not have a direct analogue to the ground truth.

总而言之，我们对预测结果和地面真实结果的比较如图所示To summarize, our comparison of projected and ground truth results is visualized in Figure1每个结果陈述都被分解为其组成声明，需要考虑三种潜在情况：a）地面真相声明与预测声明配对，b）地面真相声明缺少相应的预测声明，以及c）无关的预测声明没有匹配的地面真相声明。Each result statement is broken down into their constituent claims, with three potential cases to consider: a) a ground truth claim is paired with a projected claim, b) a ground truth claim is missing a corresponding projected claim, and c) an extraneous projected claim does not have a matching ground truth claim.

3.2 计算成绩分数Calculating Grade Score

由于投影的组成部分可能无法一对一地映射到真实情况，因此我们可以通过测量其精度和召回率来评估投影的性能，然后使用 F1 分数将这两个测量值合并为一个等级。为此，我们首先评估每个预测的主张是否为真阳性、假阳性或不确定 - 再次利用 GPT-5 作为评估模型。具体来说，我们在法学硕士法官中使用 GPT-5As the components of the projection may not map one-to-one onto the ground truth, we can assess how well the projection performs by measuring its precision and recall, and then combine these two measures into a single grade using the F1 score. To do so, we first evaluate if each projected claim is a true positive, false positive, or inconclusive - again leveraging GPT-5 as an evaluatory model. Specifically, we use GPT-5 in an LLM-as-a-judgeGu et al. (2025)能够将真实情况和预测声明与附录中的提示 5 标题进行比较。请注意，来自真实情况和测试模型的所有投影都通过 GPT-5 进行声明提取，以便所有对齐比较都是在通过 GPT-5 过滤的输出上完成的。这是为了减少法官模型可能比其他模型更倾向于自己的措辞风格而产生的偏见。为了解决对位置偏见的潜在担忧capacity to compare the alignment between ground truth and projected claims with the Prompt 5 rubric in the Appendix. Note that all projections from ground truth and models-under-test are passed through GPT-5 for claim extraction, such that all alignment comparisons are done on outputs filtered through GPT-5. This is to lessen biases that may arise from the judge model potentially favoring its own phrasing styles over those of other models. In order to address potential concerns over positional biasShi et al. (2025)我们运行两次对齐评分，翻转真实值和预测声明的顺序，如果评估不同，则对对齐分数进行平均。we run the alignment scoring twice, flipping the order of the ground truth and projected claims, and average the alignment scores if the evaluations differ.

对于每个具有配对真实声明的预测声明，我们可以简单地计算配对声明之间的对齐分数。然而，对于没有相应地面事实的每个无关的预测主张，我们改为与每个地面事实主张进行比较，并取平均分来衡量其总体影响。真实阳性被定义为阳性分数的大小（与真实情况一致的预测主张），如方程式所示For each projected claim with a paired ground truth claim, we can simply calculate the alignment score between paired claims. However, for each extraneous projected claim without a corresponding ground truth, we instead compare to each ground truth claim and take the average score to measure its overall impact. 6 结论Conclusions

科学发现本质上具有创造性、不确定性和前瞻性。与通过考试式问题评估已知知识的基于回忆的基准不同，预测新颖研究的结果并评估模型产生真正新见解的能力提出了一个更具挑战性的问题，需要细致入微的推理和深入的领域理解。Scientific discovery is inherently creative, uncertain, and forward-looking. Unlike recall-based benchmarks that evaluate known knowledge through exam-style questions, anticipating the outcomes of novel research - and assessing a model’s ability to generate genuinely new insights - poses a fundamentally more challenging problem, requiring nuanced reasoning and deep domain understanding.

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个初步框架，用于对模型预测科学成果的能力进行基准测试。从零假设检验中汲取灵感，我们的方法评估大型语言模型如何有效地推理出合理的科学结果，而不是简单地检索它们。在高上下文环境中，该框架衡量模型对丰富的结构化输入进行综合和推理的能力；在低上下文环境中，它评估模型从最少指定的研究问题生成有效和创新预测的能力。这些结果还为所提供的上下文量如何影响模型性能提供了实用指导。In this work, we propose an initial framework for benchmarking a model’s capacity to project scientific outcomes. Drawing inspiration from null hypothesis testing, our approach evaluates how effectively large language models can reason toward plausible scientific results rather than simply retrieve them. In high-context settings, the framework measures a model’s ability to synthesize and reason over rich, structured inputs; in low-context settings, it assesses the model’s capacity to generate valid and innovative projections from minimally specified research questions. These results also provide practical guidance on how the amount of context provided may influence model performance.

未来的工作将侧重于将基准扩展到更广泛的科学领域，纳入更广泛的模型，并完善评估协议——例如，通过改变上下文粒度并纳入对推理过程的更深入分析。我们还承认当前评估设置的潜在局限性：使用 GPT-5 作为 GPT 系列模型的判断可能会引入偏差。为了解决这个问题，未来的迭代将使用独立模型纳入跨家庭评估。最终，我们将该基准定位为对机器辅助发现进行更严格评估的早期步骤，并作为开发能够更有意义地加速科学研究的系统的路标。Future work will focus on expanding the benchmark across broader scientific domains, incorporating a wider range of models, and refining evaluation protocols—for example, by varying context granularity and incorporating deeper analysis of reasoning processes. We also acknowledge a potential limitation in our current evaluation setup: using GPT-5 as the judge for GPT-family models may introduce bias. To address this, future iterations will incorporate cross-family evaluation using independent models. Ultimately, we position this benchmark as an early step toward more rigorous evaluation of machine-assisted discovery, and as a guidepost for the development of systems that can more meaningfully accelerate scientific research.
