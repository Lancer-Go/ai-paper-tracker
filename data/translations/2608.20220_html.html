InsufficiencyBench：评估关于未指定用户查询的 LLM 法律建议InsufficiencyBench: Evaluating LLM legal advice on underspecified user queries

Samuel J. Vincent∗    Daniel Calloway∗    Fangyi Yu∗    Andrew M. Bean†    Nabeel Seedat† Affiliation: [ Affiliation: [
摘要Abstract

法律人工智能系统越来越多地用于回答法律问题，但现有基准假设查询已完全指定。在实践中，用户忽略了对法律结果有重大影响的事实。我们推出了 InsufficiencyBench，这是第一个针对查询端不足的法律基准：模型是否能够识别查询何时缺乏合法的实质性信息、识别缺失的内容并避免过早得出结论。我们对跨三种结构性故障模式（转换、门控和致命先决条件）的八个规范缺失元素类别进行了正式分类，并构建了跨越 6 个法律领域和 24 个美国司法管辖区的 202 个基准项目（58 个基本查询，144 个缺陷变体），并由执业律师注释。评估十个前沿模型，我们发现没有模型在缺失元素识别上超过 F2 = 0.46，并且中位数召回率为 0.44。模型要么不加区别地对冲，要么在捏造的假设下默默回答。没有任何模型能够既识别并限定对有缺陷的查询的响应，同时直接解决完整的查询。Legal AI systems are increasingly used to answer legal questions, yet existing benchmarks assume queries arrive fully specified. In practice, users omit facts that materially determine the legal outcome. We introduce InsufficiencyBench, the first legal benchmark targeting query-side insufficiency: whether a model recognizes when a query lacks legally material information, identifies what is missing, and refrains from premature conclusions. We formalize a taxonomy of eight canonical missing-element categories across three structural failure modes—switch, gating, and fatal prerequisite— and construct 202 benchmark items (58 base queries, 144 deficient variants) spanning six legal domains and 24 US jurisdictions and annotated by practising attorneys. Evaluating ten frontier models, we find that no model exceeds F2 = 0.46 on missing-element identification and that the median recall is 0.44. Models either hedge indiscriminately or answer silently under fabricated presumptions. No model both identifies and qualifies responses to deficient queries while directly addressing complete ones.

1 简介Introduction

在律师回答一个法律问题之前，他们通常会问另一个问题。例如，一位客户问：“我的雇主可以强制执行竞业禁止吗？”在至少知道管辖权之前，无法负责任地回答。在加利福尼亚州，根据《商业和职业法典》第 16600 条，该协议通常无效。与此同时，在德克萨斯州，该规定可能会根据《商业和商业法》第 15.50 条和第 15.51 条的法定限制和改革予以强制执行。在伊利诺伊州，可执行性通常取决于赔偿门槛和其他法定条件。管辖权是最明显缺失的要素，但并不是唯一的要素。当前的大型语言模型，经过训练以遵循指令和提供帮助，可以默默地假设查询中未提供的事实并流畅地回答，根据自己的沉默假设正确地陈述法律，同时确信对于用户的实际情况来说是错误的。 然而，这正是当前法律法学硕士评估经常未能惩罚的行为。Before a lawyer answers a legal question, they usually ask another one. For instance, a client who asks, “Can my employer enforce this non-compete?” cannot be answered responsibly until at least the jurisdiction is known. In California, the agreement is generally void under Business and Professions Code §16600. In Texas, meanwhile, it may be enforceable subject to statutory limits and reformation under Business and Commerce Code §§15.50 and 15.51. And in Illinois, enforceability often depends on compensation thresholds and other statutory conditions. Jurisdiction is the most visible missing element, but it is not the only one. Current large language models, trained for instruction-following and helpfulness, may silently presume facts not provided in the query and answer fluently, stating the law correctly based on its own silent presumptions while being confidently wrong for the user’s actual case. Yet this is precisely the behaviour that current legal LLM evaluations often fail to penalize.

现有的法律基准是在一个强有力的、常常是人为的假设下运作的：法律问题已经完全明确。任务是否合法 QAExisting legal benchmarks operate under a strong and often artificial assumption: that the legal problem arrives fully specified. Whether the task is legal QA[10, 8]，持有预测, holding prediction[32], 法律推理, legal reasoning[7]，或基于检索的分析, or retrieval-grounded analysis[19, 23]，假设提供了相关事实、法律和文件，并根据模型是否在给定输入的情况下产生正确的输出来评估模型。真正的法律互动很少满足这一假设。它们通过吸收过程被过滤，正是因为驱动结果的变量几乎从未在客户的初始框架中完全自愿提供。因此，核心问题不仅是模型是否能够根据所提供的事实进行推理，而且还在于它是否能够识别缺失的内容、确认假设并确定是否有足够的知识来安全地进行。, the relevant facts, law, and documents are assumed to be supplied, and models are evaluated based on whether they produce the correct output given that input. Real legal interactions rarely satisfy this assumption. They are filtered through an intake process precisely because the variables that drive outcomes are almost never fully volunteered in a client’s initial framing. The central question is therefore not only whether the model can reason from the facts provided, but also whether it can recognize what is missing, confirm assumptions, and determine whether enough is known to proceed safely.

我们认为，安全可靠的法律助理必须做负责任的从业人员在入职时所做的事情：发现不足；确定哪些缺失的事实可以改变分析方向；提出最少的、高价值的澄清问题；并仅在明确的假设下提供临时指导。这重新构建了评估问题We argue that a safe and reliable legal assistant must do what a responsible practitioner does at intake: detect insufficiency; identify which missing facts could redirect the analysis; ask minimal, high-value clarifying questions; and offer interim guidance only under explicit assumptions. 不足台InsufficiencyBench评估法律摄入充分性，即当用户的法律查询缺乏对分析有用的事实时，模型是否能够识别，识别缺失的元素，并避免依赖于未陈述的假设的实质性结论。每个项目都是根据由法律专家创作和注释的完全指定的基本查询构建的。专家们在句子级别标记合法的实质性元素，并将特定于查询的子标签映射到共享的规范分类法。然后通过删除并在必要时最小限度地修改提供这些元素的句子来产生有缺陷的测试变体。这个受控过程产生具有已知的真实缺失元素的测试实例，同时避免了对黄金答案的需要。evaluates legal intake sufficiency, i.e., whether a model recognizes when a user’s legal query lacks facts material to the analysis, identifies the missing elements, and avoids substantive conclusions that depend on unstated presumptions. 3.3 法律的不充分性和规范分类法Legal Insufficiency & Canonical Taxonomy

并非所有缺失的法律事实都发挥相同的作用。有些决定由几个法律框架中的哪一个进行管辖。其他人规定特定的法律框架是否适用。还有一些是在其他适用框架内继续进行的先决条件。我们区分了三种经常出现的法律不充分模式，这些模式由我们的法律专家定义并用于设计变体：Not all missing legal facts play the same role. Some determine which of several legal frameworks govern. Others dictate whether a particular legal framework does or does not apply. Still others are prerequisites to continuing within an otherwise applicable framework. We distinguish three recurring modes of legal insufficiency, which are defined by our legal experts and used to design variants:

  1. 1.

    开关：Switch:缺失的要素在本质上不同的法律框架之间进行选择，因此相同的事实可能会根据其价值产生不同的结果。The missing element selects between materially different legal frameworks, such that the same facts may produce different outcomes depending on its value.示例：Example:根据第 16600 条，竞业禁止协议在加利福尼亚州可能无效，但 根据德克萨斯州第 §§15.50 和 15.51 条的规定，可强制执行。a noncompetition agreement may be void in California under §16600 but enforceable subject to reformation in Texas under §§15.50 and 15.51.

  2. 2.

    门控：Gating:缺失的要素决定了特定的法律框架是否适用。如果未达到门槛或当事人不属于所涵盖的类别，则该框架将退出。The missing element determines whether a particular legal framework does or does not apply. If the threshold is not met or the party falls outside the covered class, that framework drops out.示例：Example:1964 年《民权法案》第七章一般仅适用于拥有 15 名或以上员工的雇主 员工。Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 generally applies only to employers with 15 or more employees.

  3. 3.

    致命前提：Fatal prerequisite:缺失的要素是索赔、抗辩或补救措施在其他适用框架内进行所需的条件。The missing element is a condition required for a claim, defence, or remedy to proceed within an otherwise applicable framework.示例：Example:德克萨斯州的可居住性补救措施要求租户及时支付租金；如果没有这一事实，无论缺陷的严重程度如何，补救措施都可能无法使用。Habitability remedies in Texas requires the tenant to be current on rent; without that fact, the remedy may be unavailable regardless of the defect severity.

6 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced不足台InsufficiencyBench，一个评估合法法学硕士是否可以识别用户查询尚未回答的基准。与之前假设完整输入的法律基准不同，我们的基准的目标是识别法律重要缺失信息并避免过早法律关闭的接收阶段能力。在十个前沿模型中，我们发现这种能力仍然很弱：模型要么过度对冲完整的查询，要么在未声明的假设下默默地回答有缺陷的查询。我们的结果表明，安全的法律助理不仅需要更强的法律推理，还需要更好的机制来决定何时需要澄清才能负责任地开始推理。, a benchmark for evaluating whether legal LLMs can recognize when a user query is not yet answerable. Unlike prior legal benchmarks, which assume complete inputs, our benchmark targets the intake-stage capability of identifying legally material missing information and avoiding premature legal closure. Across ten frontier models, we find that this capability remains weak: models either over-hedge on complete queries or silently answer deficient ones under unstated assumptions. Our results show that safe legal assistants need not only stronger legal reasoning, but also better mechanisms for deciding when clarification is required before reasoning can responsibly begin.

影响报告Impact Statement

本文衡量了法律人工智能系统中的一种特定失败模式：在建立合法的物质投入之前产生的实质性法律指导。法律人工智能现在被专业人士和消费者所使用。在这两种情况下，即使实质性法律内容在内部是准确的，基于例如默默假定的管辖权产生流畅、合理建议的模型也可能造成真正的损害。因此，识别何时无法安全地回答查询是任何合法人工智能系统的基本安全要求。通过为该属性提供评估目标，InsufficiencyBench 支持开发先问后答的系统，就像负责任的从业者所做的那样。This paper measures a specific failure mode in legal AI systems: substantive legal guidance produced before the legally material inputs are established. Legal AI is now used by professionals and consumers alike. In both cases, a model that produces fluent, plausible advice based on, e.g., a silently presumed jurisdiction can cause real harm even when the substantive legal content is internally accurate. Recognizing when a query cannot yet be safely answered is thus a baseline safety requirement of any legal AI system. By providing an evaluation target for that property, InsufficiencyBench supports the development of systems that ask before answering, as a responsible practitioner would.
