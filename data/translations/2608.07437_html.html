1 简介Introduction

实证科学的许多领域都遵循共同的工作流程：研究人员从科学问题和数据集开始，将问题转化为可检验的假设，选择适当的统计检验，计算 p 值，然后得出结论Many areas of empirical science follow a common workflow: a researcher begins with a scientific question and a dataset, translates the question into a testable hypothesis, selects an appropriate statistical test, computes a p-value, and then draws a conclusion[Wasserstein02042016]。这种假设检验框架出现在从医学到经济学等跨学科的高风险环境中。例如，在临床试验中，研究人员测试新疗法是否比安慰剂更有效，而在经济学中，政策制定者评估最低工资上涨等干预措施是否会影响就业结果。方法必须与问题和数据相匹配；否则，精确的 p 值可能支持错误的科学结论。. This hypothesis-testing framework appears in high-stakes settings across disciplines, from medicine to economics. For example, in clinical trials, researchers test whether a new treatment is more effective than a placebo, while in economics, policymakers evaluate whether interventions such as minimum wage increases affect employment outcomes. The method must match the question and data; otherwise, a precise p-value can support the wrong scientific conclusion.

As our P-Bench experiments show, current LLM agents may make subtle inferential errors even on well-defined hypothesis-testing tasks, undermining the validity of any conclusion drawn downstream and casting doubt on whether fully autonomous end-to-end discovery is presently achievable. 3 P 型工作台P-Bench

P-Bench 是基于经济学、生物学和医学领域的真实科学数据构建的开放式假设检验任务的基准。每项任务都基于高质量来源的统计声明，例如同行评审的顶级科学期刊和规范课程笔记，因此答案反映了领域专家已经计算并采取行动的假设检验。P-Bench is a benchmark of open-ended hypothesis-testing tasks built from real scientific data spanning economics, biology, and medicine. Each task is anchored to a statistical claim from a high-quality source, such as peer-reviewed top scientific journals and canonical course notes, so the answer key reflects a hypothesis test that domain experts have already computed and acted on.

6 讨论Discussion

我们引入了 P-Bench，这是 425 个现实世界假设检验任务的基准，具有专家审核的答案；Fisher-R1 是一种开放权重代理，通过 SFT 和 RL 进行综合统计任务训练，并具有经过验证的结果奖励。我们的结果表明，即使是当前的前沿模型仍然缺乏用于开放式假设检验的可靠统计推理。 Fisher-R1-14B 的性能优于强大的专有和开源基线，包括 GPT-5.4 和 DeepSeek-V4-Pro。 P-Bench 目前评估每个任务的单个假设检验。将其扩展到具有多重比较校正的多重测试管道是自然的下一步。可靠的统计机构可以通过在方法驱动的错误进入文献之前发现这些错误来提高科学的可重复性。它们还可能为薄弱的主张提供虚假的合法性。因此，我们发布 P-Bench 和 Fisher-R1 作为评估和监督工具，而不是作为人工统计审查的替代品。未来的工作应该进一步研究如何使主体明确地证明假设的合理性，量化方法选择的不确定性，并识别何时没有单一的假设检验足以解决科学问题。We introduced P-Bench, a benchmark of 425 real-world hypothesis-testing tasks with expert-audited answer keys, and Fisher-R1, an open-weight agent trained on synthetic statistical tasks via SFT and RL with verified-outcome rewards. Our results show that even current frontier models still lack reliable statistical reasoning for open-ended hypothesis testing. Fisher-R1-14B outperforms strong proprietary and open-source baselines, including GPT-5.4 and DeepSeek-V4-Pro. P-Bench currently evaluates a single hypothesis test per task. Extending it to multi-test pipelines with multiple-comparison correction is a natural next step. Reliable statistical agents can improve scientific reproducibility by catching method-driven errors before they enter the literature. They could also lend false legitimacy to weak claims. We therefore release P-Bench and Fisher-R1 as evaluation and oversight tools, not as substitutes for human statistical review. Future work should further study how to make agents explicitly justify assumptions, quantify uncertainty over method choice, and recognize when no single hypothesis test is adequate for a scientific question.
