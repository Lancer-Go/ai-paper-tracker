AI-Native 6G 网络中语义通信的异构感知置信同步Heterogeneity-Aware Belief Synchronization for Semantic Communication in AI-Native 6G Networks

摘要Abstract

6G网络将不仅仅作为通信基础设施；相反，它们有望演变成智能系统，其中数千个自主人工智能（AI）代理相互连接。这些代理部署在各种平台上，包括低地球轨道 (LEO) 卫星、高空平台 (HAP)、无人机 (UAV)、边缘服务器和地面设备。这些代理不断观察其环境并交换信息。语义通信提供了一种有效的机制来交换有意义的信息而不是原始数据。然而，其有效性取决于通信代理是否具有足够一致的信念来正确解释和解码所传输的消息。这一假设在 6G 网络中变得难以满足，因为异构 AI 模型在不同的计算约束下运行，并不断从本地环境中获取不同的知识。本文提出了一种适用于 6G AI 原生网络的异构感知信念同步框架。它使用部署在多访问边缘计算（MEC）服务器上的潜在翻译模型。这些模型将信念更新从一个代理转换为特定于代理的知识，而不需要联合训练和同质模型架构。仅在必要时通过潜在翻译模型交换紧凑信念更新，该框架可以保护隐私，降低同步成本，并最大限度地减少本地知识漂移。我们通过多层地面/非地面网络的案例研究验证了该框架。结果表明，它保持了较低的同步成本（通过传输的参数数量来衡量），并且在案例研究中的异构代理之间保持了较低的置信对齐误差。6G networks will not be serving as communication infrastructures only; rather, they are expected to evolve into intelligent systems, where thousands of autonomous artificial intelligence (AI) agents are interconnected. The agents are deployed across a wide range of platforms including low Earth orbit (LEO) satellites, high-altitude platforms (HAPs), unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), edge servers, and terrestrial devices. These agents continuously observe their environment and exchange information. Semantic communication provides an efficient mechanism for exchanging meaningful information instead of raw data. However, its effectiveness depends on the communicating agents having sufficiently aligned beliefs to correctly interpret and decode the transmitted messages. This assumption becomes difficult to satisfy in the 6G network where heterogeneous AI models operate under diverse computational constraints and continuously acquire different knowledge from their local environments. This article presents a heterogeneity-aware belief synchronization framework for 6G AI-native networks. It uses latent translation models deployed on multi-access edge computing (MEC) servers. These models translate belief updates from one agent to agent-specific knowledge without requiring joint training and a homogeneous architecture of models. By exchanging compact belief updates through a latent translation model only when necessary, the framework preserves privacy, reduces synchronization cost, and minimizes local knowledge drift. We validate the framework through a case study on a multi-layered terrestrial/non-terrestrial network. Results demonstrate that it maintains low synchronization cost, measured by the number of parameters transmitted, and low belief alignment error across the heterogeneous agents in the case study.

I 简介Introduction

第六代 (6G) 网络是围绕一种根本不同的范式进行设计的。除了使通信速度更快之外，6G 网络还有望互连大量自主和人工智能驱动的设备。 这些设备包括无人机、工厂机器人、自动驾驶车辆、环境传感器和平流层 HAP。 6G的另一个特点是非地面网络（NTN）与传统网络系统的融合Sixth-generation (6G) networks are being designed around a fundamentally different paradigm. In addition to making communication faster, 6G networks are expected to interconnect a vast amount of autonomous and AI-powered devices. These devices include drones, factory robots, self-driving vehicles, environmental sensors, and stratospheric HAPs. Another feature of 6G is the integration of non-terrestrial networks (NTNs) with traditional network systems[1]。卫星、HAP、无人机和地面基站作为一个跨越太空、空中和地面的统一网络协同工作。这些平台中的每一个都不会简单地发送和接收数据，而是会托管自己的人工智能代理，能够感知、推理并根据其理解采取行动. Satellites, HAPs, UAVs, and terrestrial base stations work together as a unified network spanning space, air, and ground. Each of these platforms will not simply send and receive data, but it will host its own AI agent, capable of perceiving, reasoning, and acting according to its understanding[2]。这一转变凸显了人工智能原生网络的概念，其中人工智能不再被视为外部能力，而是集成在网络内。. This shift highlights the concept of AI-native networks, in which AI is no longer treated as an external capability but is integrated within the network.

集成的 6G 网络涵盖资源有限的物联网传感器和无人机、HAP、卫星和边缘服务器。这些平台在计算资源、能耗、内存和通信能力方面存在显着差异。人工智能代理是根据其托管平台的计算能力和应用目标来选择的。 低地球轨道 (LEO) 卫星在严格的有效载荷、功率和能量限制下运行。这些限制了它们只能使用轻量级人工智能模型来执行传感、事件检测和简单决策等任务。在The integrated 6G network spans resource-constrained IoT sensors and UAVs to HAPs, satellites, and edge servers. These platforms differ significantly in their computational resources, energy consumption, memory, and communication capabilities. AI agents are selected according to the computational capabilities and application objectives of their hosting platforms. Low Earth orbit (LEO) satellites operate under strict payload, power, and energy constraints. These constrain them to lightweight AI models for tasks such as sensing, event detection, and simple decision-making. At the平流层stratospheric layer，更高的计算能力允许使用能够进行复杂推理和规划的法学硕士。的, higher computational capacity allows the use of LLMs capable of complex reasoning and planning. The陆地层terrestrial layer是最异构的层，包括从运行轻量级 AI 模型的资源受限设备到能够运行 LLM 的服务器is the most heterogeneous layer comprising devices that range from resource-constrained devices running lightweight AI models to servers capable of running LLMs[3]。这些不同的计算约束和网络任务的性质导致了异构人工智能代理的部署。. These diverse computational constraints and the nature of tasks across the network lead to the deployment of heterogeneous AI agents.

由于这些代理的架构不同，因此它们也开发出不同的知识内部表示。他们的信念被编码在不同的潜在空间中。每个代理不断地从其本地环境中学习。卫星可能会了解它在地球上空数百公里处观察到的大片区域的特征； HAP 从平流层收集的数据中学习。地面传感器仅了解其周围环境中发生的情况。这种局部学习正是使每个智能体有效地完成自己的任务并形成不同的信念的原因。在由数千个学习代理组成的网络中，这些学习代理涵盖卫星、HAP 和 地面设备中，不能假设两个代理对他们存储的世界具有相同的信念。如果代理人独立运作，那么代理人之间的信念分歧就不会出现问题。 尽管存在这些差异，代理必须为不同的应用程序交换信息。卫星可以检测大范围的洪水，HAP可以监测受影响区域内的道路和桥梁的状况，而地面传感器可以测量当地的水位和降雨量。就个人而言，每个代理仅拥有环境的部分视图。通过分享他们的观察结果，代理人可以补充彼此的知识。这使他们能够做出更明智、更协调的决策。Because these agents differ in architecture, they also develop different internal representations of knowledge. Their beliefs are encoded in distinct latent spaces. Each agent continuously learns from its local environment. A satellite might learn the characteristics of the large regions it observes from hundreds of kilometers above the Earth; a HAP learns from data collected at the stratospheric level. A ground sensor learns only what happens in its immediate surroundings. This local learning is precisely what makes each agent effective at its own task, forming different beliefs. In a network of thousands of learning agents spanning satellites, HAPs, and ground devices, no two agents can be assumed to share the same belief of the world they store. This divergence of beliefs across the agents would not be problematic if agents operated independently. Despite these differences, the agents must exchange information for different applications. A satellite may detect flooding over a large region, a HAP may monitor the condition of roads and bridges within the affected area, while ground sensors measure local water levels and rainfall. Individually, each agent possesses only a partial view of the environment. By sharing their observations, agents can complement one another’s knowledge. This allows them to make more informed and coordinated decisions.

有效的语义沟通需要发送者和接收者保持足够相似的信念，以便能够准确地解释语义消息Effective semantic communication requires the sender and receiver to maintain sufficiently similar beliefs so that the semantic messages can be interpreted accurately[8]。这一要求在大规模 6G 网络中尤其具有挑战性。此类网络将地面、空中和卫星平台集成到统一的人工智能原生基础设施中。因此，人工智能代理在具有广泛不同的计算资源、模型架构和操作角色的设备上运行，这使得语义通信变得更具挑战性. This requirement is particularly challenging in large-scale 6G networks. Such networks integrate terrestrial, aerial, and satellite platforms into a unified AI-native infrastructure. Consequently, AI agents run on devices with widely varying computational resources, model architectures, and operational roles, which makes semantic communication significantly more challenging[9]。应对这一挑战需要一种不断调整异构代理信念的机制，以便语义通信随着时间的推移保持可靠。然而，设计信念同步机制需要一些相互关联的约束。. Addressing this challenge requires a mechanism that continuously aligns the beliefs of heterogeneous agents so that semantic communication remains reliable over time. However, designing a belief synchronization mechanism requires some interrelated constraints.

  • •

    同步成本：Synchronization cost:同步大型模型（例如参数共享）会消耗大量带宽，降低语义通信的资源效率。Synchronizing large models (e.g., parameter sharing) can consume significant bandwidth, reducing the resource efficiency of semantic communication.

  • •

    隐私：Privacy:代理可以从敏感的本地数据中学习，需要同步机制，仅共享与任务相关的信念，同时保护隐私。Agents may learn from sensitive local data, requiring synchronization mechanisms that share only task-relevant beliefs while preserving privacy.

  • •

    知识漂移：Knowledge drift:持续同步会削弱代理的本地知识，这使得在更新期间保留本地信念至关重要。Continuous synchronization can weaken an agent’s local knowledge, which makes it essential to preserve local beliefs during updates.

  • •

    异质性：Heterogeneity:代理在架构、功能和资源限制方面有所不同。这需要跨不同模型和设备运行的同步方法。Agents differ in architectures, capabilities, and resource constraints. This requires synchronization methods that operate across diverse models and devices.

现有研究已经单独解决了其中一些挑战，如表所示Existing studies have addressed some of these challenges individually, as shown in TableI。然而，仍然缺乏能够同时处理异构性、隐私、同步成本和知识漂移的统一解决方案。为了解决这一差距，本文提出了一种部署在 MEC 服务器上的基于潜在翻译的框架，使异构 AI 代理能够有效地同步其信念，同时支持可靠的语义通信。. However, a unified solution that can handle heterogeneity, privacy, synchronization cost, and knowledge drift together is still missing. To address this gap, this article presents a latent translation-based framework deployed on MEC servers, enabling heterogeneous AI agents to synchronize their beliefs efficiently while supporting reliable semantic communication.

II 现有技术和机会Existing Technologies And Opportunities

在6G网络中，信息交换有望通过语义通信来实现。 在传统通信中，目标是将比特准确地传输到接收器。然而，语义交流超越了这种传统方法In 6G networks, the exchange of information is expected to be achieved through semantic communication. In traditional communication, the goal is to accurately transmit the bits to the receiver. However, semantic communication moves beyond this conventional approach[10]。 韦弗认为，沟通分为三个层面：技术层面、语义层面和有效性层面。. According to Weaver, communication has three levels: technical, semantic, and effectiveness[11]。技术层面重点关注通信信道上比特的可靠传输；语义和有效性级别分别涉及保留预期含义和支持成功完成任务. The technical level focuses on the reliable transmission of bits over the communication channel; the semantic and effectiveness levels concern preserving the intended meaning and supporting successful task completion, respectively[12]。语义通信通过仅传输预期信息来针对语义和有效性级别。它没有遵循“先传输后理解”的范例，而是使用“先理解然后传输”的方法。这仅提取和传输语义相关的信息，同时消除冗余数据。 这代表着从“如何传输”到“传输什么”的转变，使通信更加高效，并且正是人工智能原生 6G 网络所需要的. Semantic communication targets the semantic and effectiveness levels by transmitting only the intended information. Instead of following a "transmit-first-and-understand-later" paradigm, it uses an “understand-first-and-then-transmit" approach. This extracts and transmits only semantically relevant information while eliminating redundant data. This represents a shift from "how to transmit" to “what to transmit," making communication much more efficient and precisely what an AI-native 6G network needs[13], [14]。然而，语义通信仅在一种假设下起作用，即接收代理可以正确解码和解释其接收到的压缩含义。为此，接收代理必须与发送代理共享大量背景信念，以正确解码和解释消息。在语义通信文献中，主体对世界的这种信念通常被称为知识库。发送者使用其知识库对语义消息进行编码，而接收者使用其自己的知识库来解码和重构预期含义。. However, semantic communication only works under one assumption that the receiving agent can correctly decode and interpret the compressed meaning it receives. For this to happen, the receiving agent must share a significant body of background beliefs with the sending agent to correctly decode and interpret the message. In the semantic communication literature, this belief of an agent about the world is typically referred to as a knowledge base. The sender uses its knowledge base to encode the semantic message, while the receiver uses its own knowledge base to decode and reconstruct the intended meaning.

早期的语义通信系统通过联合训练具有相同架构和数据集的发送者和接收者模型来解决置信同步要求Early semantic communication systems addressed the belief synchronization requirement by jointly training the sender and receiver models having identical architectures and dataset[4]。结果，智能体开发了一种共同的内部潜在表征。这使得语义消息能够被可靠地编码和解码。在这样的受控环境中，语义通信取得了令人印象深刻的性能。. As a result, agents developed a common internal latent representation. This allowed semantic messages to be encoded and decoded reliably. Within such a controlled environment, semantic communication achieves impressive performance.

Ⅱ-AII-A 信念同步方法Belief Synchronization Approaches

最近的研究开发了同步知识库的策略。其中一种方法是参数共享，其中通信代理定期通过网络交换其语义编码器和解码器模型的参数。收发器协作学习 (TCL-SC) 框架Recent studies have developed strategies for synchronizing the knowledge bases. One of the approaches is parameter sharing, where communicating agents periodically exchange the parameters of their semantic encoder and decoder models over the network. The Transceiver Cooperative Learning (TCL-SC) framework[5]通过允许发送者和接收者通过参数交换协作更新他们的模型来遵循这一策略。然而，这种方法假设通信代理共享相同的神经网络架构。 另一种方法论follows this strategy by allowing the sender and receiver to cooperatively update their models through parameter exchange. However, this approach assumes that communicating agents share the same neural network architecture. Another methodology[6]使用联合知识蒸馏解决信念同步问题。每个代理不交换模型参数，而是共享模型学到的知识以同步全局知识库。这种方法减少了通信开销，并且代理保持一致的语义理解。然而，这种方法假设一个全局知识库足以对网络中所有代理之间的语义消息进行编码和解码。addresses belief synchronization using federated knowledge distillation. Instead of exchanging model parameters, each agent shares the knowledge learned by the model to synchronize a global knowledge base. This approach reduces communication overhead, and agents maintain a consistent semantic understanding. However, this approach assumes one global knowledge base is sufficient to encode and decode semantic messages among all the agents in the network.

Ⅱ-BII-B 研究差距和机会Research Gap and Opportunities

这些现有的研究要么使用人工智能代理的联合训练，要么假设同构架构。在 6G 网络中，架构上不同的代理部署在不同的设备上，联合训练和知识共享变得不可行。此外，单个全局模型可能无法在数千个异构代理之间翻译语义消息。 这些限制凸显了对一种机制的需求，该机制允许异构代理之间无缝信念同步，而无需联合训练它们。这种机制允许新的人工智能代理顺利集成，而无需修改现有的人工智能代理。接下来我们提出这样一个机制。These existing studies either use joint training of AI agents or assume homogeneous architecture. In a 6G network, where architecturally different agents are deployed at different devices, joint training and knowledge sharing become infeasible. Moreover, a single global model might not be able to translate semantic messages among thousands of heterogeneous agents. These limitations highlight the requirement of a mechanism that allows seamless belief synchronization among the heterogeneous agents without jointly training them. Such a mechanism allows smooth integration of new AI agents without requiring modifications in existing AI agents. We propose such a mechanism next.

三、III异质性感知信念同步框架Heterogeneity-Aware Belief Synchronization Framework

所提出的框架能够实现异构代理之间的信念同步 通过引入潜在翻译模型。这些模型将发送代理的置信更新转换为每个接收代理的特定于代理的置信更新。 这些潜在翻译模型部署在分布在网络中的 MEC 服务器上。 通过使用 MEC 服务器，计算密集型语义翻译任务可以从网络设备上卸载，从而继续保持高效。The proposed framework enables belief synchronization among heterogeneous agents by introducing latent translation models. These models translate the belief updates of the sending agent into agent-specific belief updates for each receiving agent. These latent translation models are deployed on MEC servers distributed across the network. By using MEC servers, the computationally intensive semantic translation task is offloaded from the network devices so they continue to remain efficient.

Ⅲ-AIII-A 信念同步工作流程Belief Synchronization Workflow

同步过程包括四个步骤，如图 1 所示。The synchronization process consists of four steps as shown in Fig.2.

  1. 1.

    观察：Observation:每个人工智能代理不断观察其本地环境，并根据其对世界的新信念更新其内部信念。Each AI agent continuously observes its local environment and updates its internal beliefs based on its new belief about the world.

  2. 2.

    信念更新生成：Belief Update Generation:代理不是传输整个模型参数，而是传输信念更新，表示为Rather than transmitting entire model parameters, the agent transmits a belief update, denoted byΔ​B\Delta B.

  3. 3.

    语义翻译：Semantic Translation:信念更新被传输到其本地 MEC 服务器，其中潜在翻译模型将其转换为与接收代理的潜在表示兼容的特定于代理的信念更新。The belief update is transmitted to its local MEC server, where the latent translation model converts it into agent-specific belief updates compatible with the latent representations of the receiving agents.

  4. 4.

    信念传播：Belief Propagation:翻译后的信念更新仅分发给连接到 MEC 服务器的相关代理，发送者除外。相关接收代理在接收到信念更新后更新其信念状态。The translated belief updates are distributed only to the relevant agents connected to the MEC server, except the sender. The relevant receiving agents update their belief state upon receiving a belief update.

在我们提出的方法中，异质性是通过潜在的翻译模型来解决的，该模型专门将信念更新从一个代理转换为特定于代理的信念更新。这使得小型神经网络、法学硕士和其他人工智能代理能够同步他们的信念。每个 MEC 服务器仅将翻译后的紧凑信念更新传播到信息对其很重要的相关连接代理。例如，如果路边传感器生成指示发生交通事故的置信更新，MEC 会将此更新转发给附近的自动驾驶车辆和交通管理代理以进行路线优化，同时排除连接到该 MEC 服务器的不相关农业无人机或环境传感器。In our proposed methodology, heterogeneity is addressed through latent translation models that specialize in translating belief updates from one agent to agent-specific belief updates. This allows small neural networks, LLMs, and other AI agents to synchronize their beliefs. Each MEC server propagates translated compact belief updates only to the relevant connected agents for which the information is important. For example, if a roadside sensor generates a belief update indicating a traffic accident, the MEC forwards this update to nearby autonomous vehicles and traffic management agents for route optimization, while unrelated agricultural drones or environmental sensors connected to that MEC server are excluded.

此外，仅当一个代理观察到其信念发生变化时，信念更新才会传播。这可以防止不必要的更新，就像在基于轮的同步方法中一样，即使信念没有改变，也会传播更新。这种不必要的更新可能会导致接收器偏离其本地知识。交换未经检查的知识或完整模型参数可能会暴露私有的本地信息。与交换完整模型参数的一些现有方法不同，所提出的框架仅传输紧凑的信念更新，从而减少原始知识暴露。 此外，同步是通过 MEC 服务器完成的，而不是联合训练网络代理。这允许网络的自然扩展，因为人工智能代理可以在不与现有代理联合训练的情况下进行集成。在此集成过程中，潜在翻译模型中的小型适配器使用校准示例进行训练，以将其潜在空间与现有表示空间对齐。训练后，智能体可以通过 MEC 服务器与其他智能体交换翻译后的信念更新。这使得新的智能体无需重新训练即可逐渐建立自己的信念，从而使网络随着时间的推移变得更加强大。 接下来我们在多层 6G 场景中评估该框架。Additionally, the belief updates are propagated only when one agent observes a change in its belief. This prevents unnecessary updates, as in the round-based synchronization approaches where the updates are propagated even if there is no change in belief. Such unnecessary updates might cause the receiver to drift from its local knowledge. Exchanging unchecked knowledge or full model parameters might expose private local information. Unlike some existing approaches that exchange complete model parameters, the proposed framework transmits only compact belief updates, thereby reducing raw knowledge exposure. Furthermore, synchronization is done through MEC servers instead of jointly training agents of the network. This allows natural expansion of the network, as AI agents can be integrated without jointly training with existing agents. During this integration, a small adapter in the latent translation model is trained using calibration examples to align its latent space with the existing representation space. After training, the agent can exchange translated belief updates with other agents through the MEC server. This allows new agents to gradually build their beliefs without retraining, making the network more robust over time. We next evaluate this framework in a multi-layer 6G scenario.

V 开放的挑战和未来的方向Open Challenges And Future Directions

随着现代网络中人工智能代理的不断扩散以及代理架构的多样性不断增加，在网络设计中采用以人工智能为中心的视角变得至关重要。语义通信是实现这种方法的第一步。所提出的语义通信方法引入了一种新的范式，其中代理信念的维护被分离为专用的As the proliferation of AI agents in modern networks continues and the diversity of agent architectures continues to increase, it becomes crucial to adopt an AI-centric perspective on network design. Semantic communication is the first step towards this approach. The proposed semantic communication methodology introduces a new paradigm in which the maintenance of agent beliefs is separated into a dedicated知识层knowledge layer。这为广泛的机会和应用打开了大门，从基础潜在翻译模型的开发到在实时带宽限制下保持人工智能代理网络同步的调度协议的设计。在本节中，我们将重点介绍这些挑战并讨论未来研究的有希望的方向。. This opens the door to a broad range of opportunities and applications ranging from the development of foundational latent translation models to the design of scheduling protocols that keep the network of AI agents synchronized under real-time bandwidth constraints. In this section, we highlight such challenges and discuss promising directions for future research.

V-A 信念同步的开放挑战Open Challenges in Belief Synchronization

所提出的方法在现实环境中可能面临一些开放的挑战。我们在下面提出了一些这样的挑战。There are several open challenges that the proposed methodology might face in real-world settings. We present a few such challenges below.

V-A1 潜在空间之间的置信转换Belief Conversion Between Latent Spaces

信念同步的一个基本挑战在于异构潜在空间之间的信息转换。这样做需要系统跨不同架构的潜在空间对齐表示。不同的模型根据其架构和训练目标以不同的方式学习知识的内部表示。相似的概念可能在不同模型的嵌入空间中占据不同的区域。这种缺乏对齐使得潜在空间之间的转换变得非常重要，并且需要能够有效理解未对齐并弥合不同架构之间理解差距的机制。随着模型的复杂性和规模不断增长，这变得越来越困难。A fundamental challenge in belief synchronization lies in converting information between heterogeneous latent spaces. Doing so requires the system to align representations across latent spaces of different architectures. Different models learn internal representations of knowledge differently based on their architecture and training objectives. Similar concepts might occupy distinct regions in the embedding spaces of dissimilar models. This lack of alignment makes translation between latent spaces non-trivial and necessitates mechanisms that can effectively understand the misalignment and bridge the gap in understanding between different architectures. This becomes increasingly difficult to do as the complexity and size of models continue to grow.

V-A2 潜在翻译模型架构Latent Translation Model Architecture

该方法的关键是潜在翻译模型，负责调整模型之间的潜在表示，从而将信念的变化从一个潜在空间翻译到另一个潜在空间。这无法使用简单的多层感知器 (MLP) 来执行，因为 MLP 是在固定的输入/输出大小上进行训练的，并且具有固有的前馈结构。然而，在所提出的方法中，潜在翻译模型必须能够从任何维度的模型获取输入，并根据网络中使用的架构维度的要求产生不同形状的翻译输出。因此，现有的方法不具备执行此任务的灵活性。必须从工程角度来应对这一挑战，并且必须开发能够有效执行跨模型转换的独特架构。The crux of the proposed methodology is the latent translation model, responsible for aligning the latent representation between models, thus translating the change in beliefs from one latent space to another. This cannot be performed using simple multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) because MLPs are trained on fixed input/output sizes and have an inherent feed-forward structure. However, in the proposed methodology, the latent translation model must be able to take an input from a model of any dimension and produce translated outputs of different shapes as required by the architectural dimensions used in the network. Thus, existing methodologies do not possess the flexibility to execute this task. This challenge must be approached from an engineering point of view, and a unique architecture capable of performing cross-model translation effectively must be developed.

V-A3 信念同步调度Belief Synchronization Scheduling

世界状态在现实世界环境中不断变化。虽然模拟通常使用离散时间间隔来更新状态和同步知识，但现实场景需要连续时间内的最佳调度。在同步之前何时探测世界、传播知识和积累更新的决策直接影响系统性能。频繁的同步可以提高一致性，但会增加计算和带宽成本，从而产生必须根据应用程序要求进行权衡的权衡。The world state continuously changes in real-world settings. While simulations often use discrete time intervals to update states and synchronize knowledge, real-world scenarios require optimal scheduling in continuous time. Decisions on when to probe the world, propagate knowledge, and accumulate updates before synchronization directly affect system performance. Frequent synchronization improves consistency but increases computational and bandwidth costs, creating a tradeoff that must be balanced based on the application requirements.

V-A4 预防灾难性遗忘Prevention of Catastrophic Forgetting

所提出的方法要求每次启动信念同步时，系统中所有代理的信念状态都会进行更新，相应地，权重和偏差也会发生变化。这是模型能够刷新其信念并将世界状态纳入其潜在空间的基本机制。然而，更新模型参数的过程并不简单，因为不小心改变它们可能会导致权重发生巨大变化，最终达到模型失去其最初训练的任务的所有意义的程度，并且智能体开始产生错误的输出。因此，了解要更改哪些参数以及要保留哪些参数是确保所提出方法的有效性的一个重要挑战。The proposed methodology entails that each time belief synchronization is initiated, the belief states of all the agents in the system would undergo an update, and accordingly, the weights and biases would change as well. This is the fundamental mechanism by which a model is able to refresh its belief and incorporate the world state in its latent space. However, the process of updating a model’s parameters is non-trivial, as carelessly changing them may lead the weights to change very drastically, ultimately reaching a point where the model loses all sense of the task it was initially trained on, and the agent starts producing erroneous outputs. Thus, knowing which parameters to change and which to preserve is an important challenge of ensuring the effectiveness of the proposed approach.

V-A5 应对恶意人工智能代理Addressing Malicious AI Agents

该案例研究假设一个没有对手的理想网络。在现实场景中实施该框架时不能采取这样的假设。恶意方可能会利用系统对信念状态更新的依赖，并可能启动垃圾更新来降低网络性能，或者精心设计更新，以扭曲系统的集体信念以实现其有害目标。 受损的知识层将使攻击者能够完全控制网络上的整个人工智能操作。因此，识别此类恶意更新仍然是确保系统安全并防止系统信念扭曲和腐败的关键挑战The case study assumes an ideal network with no adversaries. Such an assumption cannot be taken while implementing the framework in real-world scenarios. A malicious party might take advantage of the system’s dependence on belief state update and might initiate garbage updates to degrade network performance or meticulously craft updates in order to skew the collective belief of the system to achieve its pernicious goals. A compromised knowledge layer will allow the attacker to gain complete control over the entire AI operations across the networks. Thus, identifying such malicious updates remains a critical challenge in order to ensure the security of the system and prevent belief distortion and corruption in the system

V-B 未来的研究方向Future Research Directions

信念同步引入了语义通信的新范式。因此，我们确定了以下有前景的研究方向。Belief synchronization introduces a new paradigm in semantic communication. As a result, we identify the following promising research directions.

V-B1 潜在空间兼容性和对齐Latent Space Compatibility and Alignment

将信念的变化从一种潜在空间架构转化为另一种潜在空间架构，不仅需要透彻理解模型的配置，还需要透彻理解学习过程以及模型到底正在学习什么。需要可用于探测模型的嵌入空间并比较不同模型架构之间的潜在表示的方法。例如，变压器将学习到的表示分布在所有可训练组件上，包括注意力层和前馈层，而卷积神经网络将它们分布在卷积滤波器和其他可训练层上。类似地，自动编码器、扩散模型和其他深度学习模型通过特定于架构的参数化和中间激活来表示学习的信息。Translating a change in belief from one latent space architecture to another requires a thorough understanding of not only the model’s configuration but also the learning process and what exactly is being learned by the model. There is a need for methods that can be used to probe the embedding space of a model and compare latent representations across different model architectures. For instance, transformers distribute learned representations across all trainable components, including attention and feed-forward layers, while convolutional neural networks distribute them across convolutional filters and other trainable layers. Similarly, autoencoders, diffusion models, and other deep learning models represent learned information through architecture-specific parameterizations and intermediate activations.

因此，有必要研究是否所有架构都普遍学习相同的模式，如果不是，那么它们学习的信念状态之间的根本区别是什么。尽管近年来进行了一些研究，但重点仍然主要集中在大型语言模型上。需要进行更全面的研究，尝试理解不同模型架构和类型中学习的嵌入空间的几何形状。Thus, there is a need to investigate whether all architectures learn the same patterns universally and, if not, what is the fundamental difference between their learned belief states. Although some studies have been conducted in recent years, the focus remains mostly on large language models. A more holistic study is required that attempts to understand the geometry of the learned embedding space across different model architectures and types.

V-B2 高效且数学上有效的翻译模型架构Efficient And Mathematically Valid Translation Model Architectures

本研究提出的潜在翻译模型仍然是一个开放且有前途的研究方向。关注任务性能和模型效率同样重要，因为知识更新必须实时处理，长时间的延迟可能会导致系统知识过时。当我们考虑代理随机加入和离开网络的动态拓扑时，现有架构的问题会进一步加剧。在这种情况下，潜在翻译模型也必须适应并且必须避免执行冗余计算。对每个可能的模型架构配对使用简单的神经网络变得难以管理。因此，需要设计这样的架构来满足这些场景，同时保持计算成本。The latent translation model proposed in this study remains an open and promising research direction. It is equally important to focus on task performance and model efficiency since knowledge updates must be processed in real time and extended delays may lead to the system having stale knowledge. The issues with existing architectures are further exacerbated when we consider a dynamic topology in which agents join and leave the network randomly. In such a scenario, the latent translation model must also adapt and must avoid performing redundant calculations. Using a simple neural network for each possible pairing of model architectures becomes impossible to manage. Thus, such an architecture needs to be engineered that caters to these scenarios while maintaining computational costs.

V-B3 通过纳入 ISAC 来扩展框架Extending Framework By Incorporating ISAC

我们当前的框架仅执行通信。它也可以通过集成传感进行扩展，从而使其成为语义集成传感和通信（ISAC）系统。除了明确观察世界状态之外，这种扩展将引入获取知识的新维度。反过来，额外的传感能力可以通过允许系统先发制人地检测攻击者并终止联系来提高系统安全性，从而防止其损害信念同步过程。Our current framework only performs communication. It can be extended by integrating sensing as well, thus making it a semantic integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) system. Such an extension would introduce a new dimension of obtaining knowledge apart from explicitly observing the world state. In turn, the additional sensing capability can improve system security by allowing the system to preemptively detect an attacker and terminate contact, thereby preventing it from compromising the belief synchronization process.

V-B4 同步与资源分配联合优化Joint Optimization of Synchronization and Resource Allocation

在6G网络中，信念同步成为网络功能的重要组成部分。与其他网络操作的情况一样，置信同步也必须在通常的网络约束（例如带宽、功率和其他信道条件约束）下操作。因此，我们不能以孤立的方式处理置信同步，而是必须与其他网络操作联合优化它。这为优化问题引入了一个新的维度，并将导致行动变量和调度问题的决策空间之间的新关系和权衡。In a 6G network, belief synchronization becomes a vital part of network functionality. As is the case with other network operations, belief synchronization must also operate under the usual network constraints such as bandwidth, power, and other channel condition constraints. Thus, instead of treating belief synchronization in an isolated manner, we must optimize it jointly with other network operations. This introduces a new dimension to the optimization problem and would lead to new relationships and tradeoffs between action variables and decision spaces of scheduling problems.

VI 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了一种在以 AI 为中心的 6G 网络中异构 AI 代理之间进行语义通信的新范式。我们解决了由不同复杂性和架构的模型组成的网络中的异构性问题。通过提出信念同步的想法，我们引入了一个与网络中其他层分开运行的知识层。基于信念同步的思想，我们提出了一个框架，将其有效地整合到网络中。由于潜在信念转换的计算复杂性，我们提出了一种 MEC 卸载方案，有助于减轻边缘设备的负担，并将计算转移到单独的专用机器上。我们通过案例研究验证了所提出的方法，在案例研究中我们评估了其性能并将其与同构同步等现有方法进行了比较。结果表明，所提出的方法在模拟设置中保持了较低的误差。该案例研究是使用简化的线性矩阵向量表示进行的，为评估提供了受控环境。我们通过确定几个未来的挑战和研究方向来结束这项研究。In this article, we introduced a new paradigm for semantic communication among heterogeneous AI agents in AI-centric 6G networks. We addressed the issue of heterogeneity in a network consisting of models of different complexities and architectures. By proposing the idea of belief synchronization, we introduced a knowledge layer that operates separately from the other layers in the network. Based on the idea of belief synchronization, we proposed a framework to effectively incorporate it into a network. Owing to the computational complexity of latent belief translation, we propose an MEC offloading scheme that helps reduce the burden on edge devices and instead moves the computation to a separate dedicated machine. We validated the proposed methodology by conducting a case study in which we evaluated its performance and compared it to existing methodologies such as homogeneous synchronization. The results demonstrate that the proposed methodology maintained lower error in the simulated setting. The case study was conducted using a simplified linear matrix–vector representation, providing a controlled environment for evaluation. We conclude the study by identifying several future challenges and research directions.
