摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）的快速增长引发了 关于人类创造力和个人表达的关键问题 AI辅助创作的时代。人类何时采纳人工智能建议， 对个人声音有何影响？The rapid proliferation of large language models (LLMs) raises critical questions about human creativity and individual expression in an era of AI-assisted creation. When do humans adopt AI suggestions, and what are the implications for individual voice?

本研究通过游戏化写作来检验这些问题 74 名参与者（214 份回复）回复的练习 提示，同时人工智能生成的单词建议在他们写作时可用。游戏 模拟一个反乌托邦的未来，其中人工智能试图从人类残存的个性中学习，并抑制类似人工智能的写作。在此过程中，它试图创建 揭示真实用户偏好而不是默认偏好的条件 行为，例如接受人工智能生成的现成建议。请注意，这是对“有用的助手”设计模式的故意反转；系统明确禁止你接受AI建议。This study examines these questions through a gamified writing exercise where 74 participants (214 responses) replied to prompts while AI-generated word suggestions were available as they wrote. The game simulates a dystopian future in which an AI is attempting to learn from what remains of human individuality, and disincentivizes AI-like writing. In doing so, it attempts to create conditions that reveal authentic user preferences rather than default behaviors, such as accepting a readily available AI-generated suggestion. Note that this is a deliberate inversion of the ”helpful assistant” design pattern; the system is explicitly forbidding you from accepting AI suggestions.

我们分析不同任务类型的用户行为模式、用户 行为和反应特征以了解因素 影响创造性任务中的人机交互。该研究的重点是 当用户选择保持创作自主权而不是违反游戏规则并接受人工智能 援助。它还探讨了这些选择如何与响应模式、任务特征和用户行为相关。这种游戏化的方法提供了一个学习框架 真实的人机交互和具有挑衅性的镜头 理解效率与真实性之间的紧张关系 人工智能增强创造力。We analyze user behavior patterns across different task types, user behaviors, and response characteristics to understand the factors influencing human-AI interaction in creative tasks. The study focuses on when users choose to maintain creative autonomy versus violating the rules of the game and accepting AI assistance. It also explores how these choices relate to response patterns, task characteristics, and user behavior. This gamified approach offers both a framework for studying authentic human-AI interaction and a provocative lens for understanding the tension between efficiency and authenticity in AI-augmented creativity.

I 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 出现在越来越多的日常写作工具中，从电子邮件界面到代码环境，在用户撰写文本时提供建议。这些系统提出了一些重要问题，即用户如何将大量人工智能生成的文本整合到自己的写作中，以及个人写作风格如何受到影响。尽管公众越来越担心人工智能生成的文本变得通用，但我们对实时提供人工智能建议时影响个人行为的因素知之甚少。Large language models (LLMs) appear in an increasing number of everyday writing tools, from email interfaces to code environments, offering suggestions as users compose text. These systems raise important questions about how users integrate this abundance of AI-generated text into their own writing, and how individual writing style is affected. Although there is growing public concern about AI-generated text becoming generic, we know relatively little about what factors impact individual behavior when AI suggestions are offered in real time.

大多数现有研究都在高度控制的研究任务或大规模平台日志中检验人工智能辅助写作。这些方法提供了有价值的信息，但没有揭示用户在决定接受或拒绝人工智能建议时做出的微小的、即时的决定。他们也很少测试激励用户抵制使用人工智能的情况。此外，许多现有的研究都涉及明确设计用于减少摩擦并使人工智能的采用尽可能方便的环境。Most existing studies examine AI-assisted writing in either highly controlled research tasks or large-scale platform logs. These approaches provide valuable information but do not reveal the small, moment-by-moment decisions users make when deciding whether to accept or reject AI suggestions. They also rarely test situations in which users are given incentives to resist using AI. Furthermore, many existing studies concern environments which are explicitly designed to reduce friction and make AI adoption as convenient as possible.

为了弥补这一差距，我们设计了To address this gap, we designed防滑Nonslop，一个简单的网络游戏，描绘了一个人工智能控制世界的世界，如图所示。, a simple web-based game that caricatures a world in which artificial intelligences control the world, as shown in Fig.1。在部署该游戏期间收集的数据可以提供一些关于玩家如何响应提示的见解，而本地法学硕士在明确禁止或惩罚接受这些建议的上下文中提供下一个单词建议。该框架提供了一个机会来详细观察用户在人工智能辅助可用但不鼓励时以及当他们特别意识到自己的选择时如何行动。. Data collected during the deployment of this game may provide some insight into how players respond to prompts while a local LLM provides next-word suggestions in a context which explicitly disallows or penalizes acceptance of those suggestions. This framework provides an opportunity to observe on a granular basis how users act when AI assistance is available but discouraged, and when they are made especially conscious of their choices.

我们分析了 74 名参与者提交的 214 份有效提交内容，重点关注用户何时尝试采用人工智能建议的词语，以及各个用户的整体模式有何不同。我们还检查提示级别的趋势并按行为对用户进行聚类。We analyze 214 valid submissions from 74 participants, focusing on when users attempted to adopt AI-suggested words, and how individual users differ in their overall patterns. We also examine prompt-level trends and cluster users by behavior.

这项工作做出了三个贡献。首先，它介绍了一个用于研究人类与人工智能写作交互的轻量级框架。其次，它提供了对非激励条件下人工智能建议使用的基于数据的分析。第三，它提供了有关任务类型和用户行为与人工智能采用之间关系的观察。This work makes three contributions. First, it introduces a lightweight framework for studying human–AI writing interaction. Second, it provides a data-based analysis of AI suggestion use under disincentivized conditions. Third, it offers observations about how task type and user behavior relate to AI adoption.

这些贡献有助于解决用户何时更有可能或不太可能接受人工智能生成的建议、任务类型和提示特征如何塑造该行为以及单个用户是否倾向于表现出一致的策略等问题。These contributions help address questions of when users are more or less likely to accept AI-generated suggestions, how task type and prompt characteristics might shape that behavior, and whether individual users tend to exhibit consistent strategies.

II 背景Background

Ⅱ-AII-A 人工智能辅助写作和人类与人工智能的共同创造AI-assisted writing and human-AI co-creation

近年来，大型语言模型已广泛融入我们的日常生活中。他们经常提供下一个单词或下一个句子的建议、重写建议或生成现有工作的延续。这些进步有可能提高生产力并减少认知负荷。因此，人类与人工智能在写作中的共同创造已经成为一种常见的互动，而不是专门的或有限的互动In recent years, large language models have been widely integrated into our everyday lives. They often offer next-word or next-sentence suggestions, rewrite suggestions, or generate continuations of existing work. These advances have the potential to increase productivity and reduce cognitive load. As a result, human-AI co-creation in writing has become a common interaction rather than a specialized or limited one[9].

此前对人工智能辅助写作的研究主要集中在评估这些系统对生产力、写作质量和用户满意度的影响。先前的研究表明，当以低粒度（例如单个单词或句子）和更高粒度（例如段落级延续）提供建议时，人工智能辅助可以改善写作结果。人们发现，在这两项任务中使用人工智能可以提高写作速度，帮助用户克服写作障碍，并提高用户满意度和所有权，特别是对于新手作家或摘要和解释等结构化任务Prior research on AI-assisted writing has largely focused on evaluating the effects of these systems on productivity, writing quality, and user satisfaction. Prior work has shown that AI assistance can improve writing outcomes when suggestions are provided at both low levels of granularity, such as individual words or sentences, and higher levels, such as paragraph-level continuations. The use of AI in both of these tasks has been found to increase writing speed, help users overcome writer’s block, and improve user satisfaction and ownership, particularly for novice writers or structured tasks such as summarization and explanation[4]。该领域的界面设计经常进行优化，以最大限度地提高采用率，例如通过提供相关的自动完成建议或一键插入生成的文本。. Interface designs in this space are frequently optimized to maximize adoption, for example by offering relevant autocomplete suggestions or one-click insertion of generated text.

然而，这种对效率和易用性的强调使得人类与人工智能交互的重要方面未被充分探索。特别是，相对较少的关注是作者在选择是否接受、修改或拒绝人工智能建议时做出的即时决定。在当今世界，我们做出这些决定的频率几乎与我们在键盘上打字的频率一样。这些小的、频繁的选择很少被研究，但它们在塑造人工智能系统对创意输出的影响力方面发挥着核心作用However, this emphasis on efficiency and ease of use has left important aspects of human–AI interaction underexplored. In particular, relatively little attention has been paid to the moment-to-moment decisions writers make when choosing whether to accept, modify, or reject AI suggestions. In today’s world, we make these decisions almost as often as we type on a keyboard. These small, frequent choices are rarely studied, yet they play a central role in shaping how much influence AI systems exert over creative output[2].

随着人类与人工智能共同创造的兴起，人们也越来越意识到有关代理和作者身份的问题。用户可以选择是否采用人工智能建议，不仅基于感知的正确性或效率，还基于创意意图或围绕原创性的社会规范With the rise of human-AI co-creation, there is also a growing awareness of questions raised regarding agency and authorship. Users may choose whether to adopt AI suggestions based not only on perceived correctness or efficiency but also on creative intent or social norms around originality[9]。尽管如此，许多研究都是在这样的假设下进行的：增加人工智能的使用，反映人工智能能力的增强，是用户的目标。. Despite this, many studies are conducted under the assumption that increased AI use, reflecting increased AI capability, is a goal for users.

与旨在优化人工智能采用的系统相比，很少有研究考察生成式人工智能的使用完全是可选的，甚至是不鼓励的。了解用户在这些条件下的行为方式可以揭示隐藏的偏好和策略，而这些偏好和策略经常被默认的设计选择所掩盖。检查用户何时拒绝可用的人工智能建议可以深入了解人们如何在功能日益强大的生成系统中协商创意自主权和作者权。In contrast to systems designed to optimize AI adoption, fewer studies have examined contexts in which generative AI use is entirely optional or even discouraged. Understanding how users behave under these conditions can reveal hidden preferences and strategies that are so often obscured by default design choices. Examining when users decline available AI suggestions offers insight into how people negotiate creative autonomy and authorship in the presence of increasingly capable generative systems.

Ⅱ-BII-B 人工智能辅助写作中的创造力和声音Creativity and voice in AI-assisted writing

除了效率和输出质量问题之外，人工智能辅助写作还引发了对创造力和作者身份的担忧。写作通常不仅被理解为一种交流手段，而且被理解为一种个人和创造性的表达形式，其中对措辞、结构和风格的选择具有意义。当生成系统提出流畅且适合上下文的延续时，它们可能会巧妙地塑造这些选择，影响作者表达想法的方式以及他们对结果文本的所有权感Beyond questions of efficiency and output quality, AI-assisted writing raises concerns about creativity and authorship. Writing is often understood not only as a means of communication, but as a form of personal and creative expression in which choices around wording, structure, and style carry meaning. When generative systems propose fluent and contextually appropriate continuations, they may subtly shape these choices, influencing how writers express ideas and how much ownership they feel over the resulting text[9].

随着大型语言模型在大量现有文本上进行训练，对语言同质化和个人声音侵蚀的担忧变得越来越突出。虽然此类模型擅长产生统计上合理的延续，但它们的输出可能会趋向于风格规范。这导致研究人员质疑生成写作工具的广泛使用是否会缩小表达多样性，特别是当人工智能建议作为默认或规范完成时Concerns about homogenization of language and erosion of individual voice have become increasingly prominent as large language models are trained on vast bodies of existing text. While such models excel at producing statistically plausible continuations, their outputs may converge toward stylistic norms. This has led researchers to question whether widespread use of generative writing tools might narrow expressive diversity, particularly when AI suggestions are presented as defaults or normative completions[6].

尽管这些问题具有相关性，但人工智能辅助写作的实证研究很少关注用户何时以及为何选择不采用人工智能生成的语言。大多数研究都考察了隐含地奖励接受的环境，这使得阻力难以观察或解释。相比之下，在人工智能辅助可用但不鼓励或至少成为有意识的选择的环境中检查写作行为，可以揭示隐藏在更传统设计中的创造性自我调节模式。Despite the relevance of these issues, empirical work on AI-assisted writing has rarely focused on when and why users choose not to adopt AI-generated language. Most studies examine environments that implicitly reward acceptance, making resistance difficult to observe or interpret. By contrast, examining writing behavior in settings where AI assistance is available but discouraged or at least made to be a conscious choice can surface patterns of creative self-regulation that remain hidden in more conventional designs.

Ⅱ-CII-C 游戏化Gamification

游戏化是指在非游戏环境中使用游戏设计元素Gamification refers to the use of game design elements in non-game contexts[3]。游戏化有很多种类型，从航空公司忠诚度计划的简单奖励计划到适应故事讲述、界面、机制或游戏其他方面的惯例，再到多种软件和流程。. There are many types of gamification, from the simple reward schemes of an airline loyalty program to adapting conventions of storytelling, interfaces, mechanics or other aspects of games to software and processes of many kinds.

流行的浏览器游戏，例如Popular browser games such as无限工艺Infinite Craft [1]是一款围绕单词组合和发现而构建的极简生成游戏，它还说明了如何将生成模型放入简单的规则集中可以产生令人惊讶的输出，将模型行为转变为游戏和解释的场所，而不仅仅是实用性。, a minimalist generative game built around word combination and discovery, also illustrate how putting a generative model inside a simple ruleset can yield surprising outputs, turning model behavior into a site of play and interpretation rather than mere utility.

防滑Nonslop可以被视为一种游戏化形式的写作辅助，或者也许是写作反辅助。该应用程序利用常见的游戏元素，例如评分、惩罚（针对撰写侵权词语）以及难度级别之间基于分数的进展。它还具有基于邪恶人工智能的科幻比喻的叙事叙事。can be seen as a gamified form of writing assistance, or perhaps writing anti-assistance. The application utilizes common game elements such as scoring, punishment (for writing infringing words), and score-based progression between difficulty levels. It also features a diegetic narrative based on a sci-fi trope of evil AI.

游戏化系统通常用于通过重塑激励措施并使反馈即时且清晰来影响行为。分数、进度和惩罚可以改变“成功”的感觉，并且可以使原本自动的交互变成明确的选择。对于研究来说，这很重要，因为它允许设计者创建受控环境，其中目标行为（例如，采用自动完成建议）既不是纯粹鼓励也不是纯粹阻止，而是成为可以在用户和任务之间观察和比较的战略决策Gamified systems are often used to influence behavior by reshaping incentives and making feedback immediate and legible. Points, progression, and penalties can change what “success” feels like, and can make otherwise automatic interactions into explicit choices. For research, this matters because it allows designers to create controlled contexts where a target behavior (e.g., adopting an autocomplete suggestion) is neither purely encouraged nor purely prevented, but instead becomes a strategic decision that can be observed and compared across users and tasks[10].

在人工智能辅助写作的背景下，这种激励塑造特别有用。大多数现实世界的写作助手旨在最大限度地减少摩擦并使建议接受正常化。基于游戏的框架可以挑战这一规范。它可以使建议的使用可见，并赋予其后果，从而在用户重视便利性而不是自主性时浮出水面。通过这种方式，游戏化环境可以作为研究围绕人工智能采用的微观决策的工具，而这些决策在以生产力为导向的工具中很难隔离。In the context of AI-assisted writing, this incentive shaping is particularly useful. Most real-world writing assistants are designed to minimize friction and normalize suggestion acceptance. A game-based framing can challenge that norm. It can make suggestion use visible, attach consequences to it, and thereby surface when users value convenience versus autonomy. In this way, a gamified environment can act as an instrument for studying micro-decisions around AI adoption that are difficult to isolate in productivity-oriented tools.

Ⅱ-DII-D 批判性游戏和人工智能作为挑衅Critical play and AI as Provocation

游戏研究和设计研究中的批判性游戏和相关传统将交互系统视为质疑价值的一种方式，而不是简单地优化结果。此类作品经常引入摩擦、倒置或讽刺，以使隐含的规范变得可见并引发反思。在这个框架中，“不良”体验——惩罚、不适、约束——可以是故意的设计选择，暴露了系统对其用户的假设以及它所赋予的特权的行为。Critical play and related traditions in game studies and design research treat interactive systems as a way to interrogate values rather than simply optimize outcomes. Such works often introduce friction, inversion, or satire to make implicit norms visible and to prompt reflection. In this framing, “undesirable” experiences—penalties, discomfort, constraint—can be deliberate design choices that expose what a system assumes about its users and what behaviors it privileges.

人工智能本身可以成为一种艺术材料，而基于人工智能方法构建的文物可以用作批判性审问的工具。例如，我们可能会构建人工智能系统来解决人类如何与技术交互的问题。 Khalifa 等人的 DeepTingle 就是一个例子。Artificial intelligence can itself be an artistic material, and artifacts built on AI methods can be used as tools for critical interrogation. For example, we may build AI systems that problematize how humans interact with technology. One example of this is DeepTingle by Khalifa et al.[5]。 DeepTingle 的出发点是观察到基于人工智能的文本处理工具假设你要写的内容是“中性”英语，事实上根本不是中性的。相反，它假设你实际上想更像查克·廷格尔（Chuck Tingle）那样写作，查克·廷格尔是科幻小说、政治讽刺同性恋色情小说的著名作家。 Chuck Tingle 使用英语的方式非常独特，这与大多数基于人工智能的写作支持工具发生冲突。通过翻转脚本，并探索如果 Chuck Tingle 的语言被假定为默认语言会发生什么，DeepTingle 阐明了写作助手固有的假设以及这些工具对我们的影响力。. DeepTingle starts from the observation that the “neutral” English that AI-based text processing tools assume that what you want to write is, in fact, not neutral at all. Instead, it assumes that you actually want to write more like Chuck Tingle, a renowned author of science fiction political satire gay erotica. Chuck Tingle has a very characteristic way of using the English language, which would run afoul of most AI-based writing support tools. By flipping the script, and exploring what would happen if Chuck Tingle’s language was presumed to be the default, DeepTingle illuminates the assumptions inherent in writing assistants and the power such tools have over us.

计算诗学的相关工作将语言模型和文本生成系统视为批判性和创造性探究的材料，而不是作为产生无摩擦或优化写作的工具。在这一领域的工作中，生成系统被用来故意引入约束、夸张或陌生化，通过使语言惯例变得陌生而使它们变得可见。这些方法强调对效率的探索和反思，并突出语言技术中嵌入的规范性假设及其所优先的表达形式Related work in computational poetics has framed language models and text-generation systems as materials for critical and creative inquiry rather than as tools for producing frictionless or optimized writing. In this line of work, generative systems are used to deliberately introduce constraint, exaggeration, or defamiliarization, rendering linguistic conventions visible by making them strange. Such approaches emphasize exploration and reflection over efficiency, and foreground the normative assumptions embedded in language technologies and the forms of expression they privilege[8].

防滑Nonslop采用类似的倒置，但将目标从风格规范转向采用规范。游戏没有将人工智能建议接受视为默认设置，而是让建议的使用变得明显且重要。这创造了一个空间，让玩家关于是否接受方便的延续的微观决定成为独特的行为。重要的是，我们的目标不是规定人工智能写作工具的“正确”使用，而是揭示当无摩擦采用的通常激励被逆转时出现的策略和偏好。adopts a similar inversion, but shifts the target from stylistic norms to adoption norms. Instead of treating AI suggestion acceptance as an assumed default, the game makes suggestion use obvious and consequential. This creates a space where players’ micro-decisions on whether to accept a convenient continuation become distinct behavior. Importantly, the goal is not to prescribe “correct” use of AI writing tools, but to reveal the strategies and preferences that emerge when the usual incentives for frictionless adoption are reversed.

该系统是使用 Phaser 3 JavaScript 框架实现的The system was implemented using the Phaser 3 JavaScript framework[7]。采用模拟风格是为了将低保真外观与功能极其强大的人工智能概念进行对比。该界面还需要从 Web-LLM 库加载一个小模型，这是 Qwen 2.5 0.5B Instruct 的量化版本。不幸的是，这一要求意味着大约 53% 的尝试用户由于操作系统、浏览器、网络或隐私限制而无法参与。. The interface also required loading a small model from the Web-LLM library, a quantized version of the Qwen 2.5 0.5B Instruct. Unfortunately, this requirement meant that approximately 53% of attempted users were not able to participate due to OS, browser, network or privacy restrictions.

该游戏分为 3 个级别，玩家的进度基于 AI 对反应的评估。根据感知到的难度，提示在质量上按级别划分。例如，一级提示要求用户描述天空，而三级提示要求他们想象并证明一个新的假期。它们还根据关键词和意图进行分类，并分为创意、观察、个人、哲学或解释性之一。The game was tiered into 3 levels, with a player’s progression based on the AI evaluation of a response. The prompts were divided by level on a qualitative basis, according to their perceived difficulty. For example, a level one prompt asked users to describe the sky, while a level 3 prompt asked them to imagine and justify a new holiday. They were also categorized according to keyword and intent, and classified as one of creative, observational, personal, philosophical, or explanatory.

游戏还分为两个难度，玩家在加载时选择。在“简单”模式下，玩家不会使用带有屏幕效果和视觉计数的人工智能建议单词。然而，他们仍然能够使用这个词。在“硬”模式下，用户完全无法使用人工智能建议的单词。当他们尝试这样做时，会生成与简单模式相同的视觉效果和计数，但该单词未被接受到提交中，并且用户被迫选择不同的措辞。由于结果没有显着差异且样本量较小，因此下面包含的所有结果和分析都结合了游戏两个版本的结果。The game was also separated into two difficulties, which a player chose from at loading time. In “easy” mode, a player was discouraged from using an AI-suggested word with screen effects and a visual tally. However, they were still able to use the word. In “hard” mode, a user was entirely prevented from using an AI-suggested word. When they attempted to do so, the same visual effects and tally were generated as in easy mode, but the word was not accepted into the submission and the user was forced to select a different phrasing. As results did not significantly differ and sample size was small, all results and analysis included below combine results from both versions of the game.

在这两种模式下，与人工智能生成的建议的交互在游戏中都被视为违反规则，而不是中立的功能。建议的词语在游戏的叙述中被视为禁止使用，并且尝试使用它们会触发明确的视觉反馈和惩罚。因此，尝试采用人工智能建议的单词构成了故意违反系统的限制。这种框架使得建议的使用不仅被解释为接受援助，而且被解释为在受规则约束的环境中有意义的行为选择。In both modes, interaction with AI-generated suggestions is framed within the game as a violation of its rules rather than a neutral feature. Suggested words are presented as forbidden in the game’s narrative, and attempts to use them trigger explicit visual feedback and penalties. As a result, attempted adoption of an AI-suggested word constitutes a deliberate transgression of the system’s constraints. This framing allows suggestion use to be interpreted not merely as assistance uptake, but as a meaningful behavioral choice within a rule-bound environment.

在此过程中使用了两名法学硕士。Two LLMs were used in this process.

首先，上面提到的 Qwen 2.5 0.5B Instruct 模型用于在用户输入对每个提示的响应时执行浏览器内推理。该模型在每个空白处生成建议的下一个单词。建议词仅限于前 k 个建议，k 为 5。建议词还根据 NLTK 停用词列表进行过滤，以便用户不会阻止使用“the”或“a”等中性词。值得注意的是，大约 8% 的情况下，此过滤器会导致缺乏任何可用的建议，游戏界面在本应显示建议的地方显示空白。不幸的是，这可能导致一些用户感到困惑。First, the Qwen 2.5 0.5B Instruct model mentioned above was used to perform in-browser inference as the user typed their response to each prompt. This model generated suggested next words at each whitespace. Suggested words were limited to the top-k suggestions, with k being 5. Suggested words were also filtered according to the NLTK stopword list, so that a user would not be discouraged from using neutral words such as “the” or “a”. It is worth noting that approximately 8% percent of the time, this filter resulted in a lack of any usable suggestions, with the game interface displaying a blank where it would have displayed a suggestion. Unfortunately, this may have resulted in confusion for some users.

生成前 k 个建议并过滤 NLTK 停用词后，最有可能的下一个单词将在文本输入字段中以及输入文本字段上方的窗口中显示为自动完成文本。After the top-k suggestions were generated and filtered for NLTK stopwords, the most likely next word was displayed as autocomplete text within the text-input field and also in a window above the input text field.

用户提交回复后，将使用第二个法学硕士。此时调用了 OpenAI 的 gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18 来为用户提交的内容提供分数。在测试了多个选项并定性检查输出与游戏基调的一致性以及其成功遵循提示中提供的评分指南后，选择了该模型。Once a user submitted a response, the second LLM was used. At this point a call to OpenAI’s gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18 was made to provide a score for the user’s submission. This model was chosen after testing multiple options and qualitatively examining the output for its consistency with the tone of the game and its success in following scoring guidelines as provided in the prompt.

该实验生成的分数基于 1) 相关性、2) 语法和 3) 连贯性的数字分数，并使用鼓励 AI 在游戏的幽默语气中回答的提示生成。根据法学硕士的评估，每份提交的三个标准的得分均为 5 分。然后，游戏根据这 3 个个人得分的总和给出最终得分和口头评估。如果总分至少为 10 分（满分为 15 分），则用户将进入下一级别。否则，他们会重复同一级别。选择这个数字是为了使进步可以实现，但在审查测试中生成的分数后具有一定的挑战性。The score generated for this experiment was based on a numerical score for each of 1) relevance, 2) grammar and 3) coherence, and was generated using a prompt that encouraged the AI to answer within the humorous tone of the game. Each submission was given a score out of 5 for each of the three criteria, as assessed by the LLM. The game then offered a final score and verbal assessment based on the total of those 3 individual scores. A user progressed to the next level if that total score was at least 10 out of a possible 15. Otherwise, they repeated the same level. This number was chosen to make progression achievable but mildly challenging after a review of scores generated in testing.

对于人类来说，评估写作质量是一项具有挑战性和主观性的任务。当自动化时，它变得更具挑战性，特别是考虑到量化固有的主观品质（例如连贯性）的困难。这里使用的法学硕士驱动的评估方法并不总是能提供完美的结果。在评估中，法学硕士对语法正确性的批评虽然大部分是正确的，但确实经常忽略错误，或者相反，过度正确。然而，该方法确实强化了游戏的基调，强调了有缺陷的法学硕士作为潜在的质量评判标准，并且还激励玩家在游戏中进行多次尝试。此评估的温度设置为 0，以保持多个实验的再现性。Evaluating writing quality is a challenging and subjective task for humans. It becomes even more challenging when automated, especially given the difficulty in quantifying inherently subjective qualities such as coherence. The LLM-driven method of evaluation used here did not always provide perfect results. In evaluation, the LLM’s critique of grammatical correctness, while mostly correct, did often ignore errors or, conversely, overcorrect. However, the method did provide a reinforcement of the game’s tone, emphasizing a flawed LLM as a potential judge of quality, and also provided an incentive for players to make multiple attempts at the game. The temperature for this evaluation was set to 0, to maintain reproducibility over multiple experiments.

IV 分析Analysis

这项分析的目的不是评估写作质量，而是了解用户在人工智能援助可用但不鼓励时如何协商。我们专注于三个层面的分析：(1) 所有提交内容的一致采用模式，(2) 用户群体之间的行为差​​异，以及 (3) 提示级别差异，这些差异影响人工智能建议何时或多或少被使用。在整个分析过程中，我们将人工智能采用的违规行为视为反映用户选择效率或轻松时刻的行为信号，而不是作为成功或失败的指标。The goal of this analysis is not to evaluate writing quality, but to understand how users negotiate AI assistance when it is available but discouraged. We focus on three levels of analysis: (1) consistent adoption patterns across all submissions, (2) differences in behavior across groups of users, and (3) prompt-level differences that shape when AI suggestions are more or less likely to be used. Throughout the analysis, we treat AI adoption transgressions as a behavioral signal reflecting moments where users chose efficiency or ease, rather than as an indicator of success or failure.

我们对收集到的 74 位参与者提交的 214 份有效写作内容进行了多部分分析。我们的目标是了解用户何时选择采用人工智能建议的词语、这些选择如何影响他们的写作，以及人工智能的使用是否与玩家的进度、反应模式或行为相关。下面的分析整合了描述性统计数据、根据行为对玩家进行分类的聚类方法以及按提示进行的比较。We conducted a multi-part analysis of the collected 214 valid writing submissions produced by 74 unique participants. Our goal was to understand when users chose to adopt AI-suggested words, how these choices shaped their writing, and whether AI usage correlated with players’ progression, response patterns, or behavior. The analyses below integrate descriptive statistics, clustering methods to segment players according to behavior, and per-prompt comparisons.

不同类别之间的差异表明，人工智能采用的违规行为很大程度上取决于即时需求的认知工作类型。强调正确性、合理性或常识的解释性提示表现出最高的人工智能采用违规率。相比之下，创造性和观察性提示（反应基于想象力或即时感知）显示人工智能的使用最少。The differences across categories suggest that AI adoption transgression is strongly shaped by the kind of cognitive work a prompt demands. In contrast, creative and observational prompts—where responses are grounded in imagination or immediate perception—showed minimal AI use.

对这种模式的一种解释是，用户认为更具吸引力或个人共鸣的提示可能会减少与人工智能生成的延续的冲突。当用户有明确的内部反应或个人参考点时，人工智能建议的违规行为可能会提供较少的感知价值。相反，需要解释或常识的提示可能会通过与语言模型的优势更紧密地结合来邀请人工智能的帮助。玩家可能不相信他们会产生比语言模型更好的答案，甚至是截然不同的答案。One interpretation of this pattern is that prompts which users find more engaging or personally resonant may reduce collisions with AI-generated continuations. When users have a clear internal response or personal reference point, AI suggestion transgressions may offer less perceived value. Conversely, prompts that require explanation or general knowledge may invite AI assistance by aligning more closely with the strengths of language models. A player might not believe that they would produce a better answer than the language model, or even a distinctly different one.

总而言之，这些发现表明提示的性质确实在塑造用户与人工智能建议的交互方式方面发挥着作用。需要论证、解释或领域一般推理的提示似乎会鼓励与人工智能完成的冲突，而基于个人经验或感官描述的提示往往会引起较少的使用，可能是因为它们不要求用户说实话或描述任何客观现实。值得注意的是，这种效果直接来自观察到的用户行为，而不是跨类别的任何指导差异。Taken together, these findings demonstrate that the nature of the prompt does play a role in shaping how users interact with AI suggestions. Prompts requiring justification, explanation, or domain-general reasoning appear to encourage collisions with AI completions, whereas prompts grounded in personal experience or sensory description tend to elicit less use, possibly because they do not require the user to tell the truth or describe any objective reality. Notably, this effect emerges directly from observed user behavior, rather than any instructional differences across categories.

IV-F 游戏测试后调查Post-Playtest Survey

为了将行为日志置于情境中，我们进行了一项简短的游戏后调查。七名参与者在游戏结束后立即回答了一系列问题。该调查询问了参与者之前使用写作辅助工具的情况，建议的单词是否感觉像是他们自然使用的单词，他们是否试图预测系统的限制，以及游戏是否改变了他们对自己的写作或人工智能写作辅助的看法。To contextualize the behavioral logs, we conducted a short post-playtest survey. Seven participants answered a series of questions immediately after gameplay. The survey asked about participants’ prior use of writing assistance tools, whether the suggested words felt like words they might naturally have used, whether they tried to anticipate the system’s restrictions, and whether the game changed how they thought about their own writing or AI writing assistance.

由于样本量较小，这些反应被视为轶事而不是定量分析的基础。尽管如此，它们还是提供了有用的背景。除一名参与者外，所有参与者都报告至少有时会使用拼写检查、自动完成或基本语法工具，并且大多数人都使用过与 AI 相关的写作工具，例如 Claude 或 ChatGPT。参与者普遍将这些工具描述为写作任务的助手，而不是主要执行者。没有一个接受调查的参与者表示使用过人工智能探测器。当被问及当系统建议他们可能会使用的单词时他们会做什么时，参与者描述了一些策略，例如替换同义词、改变句子结构、忽略建议或选择更具有情感针对性的单词。对于避免类似人工智能写作的工具的想法，人们的反应不一：一些参与者看到了专业写作、电子邮件或社交媒体中可能的用例，而其他人则质疑基于人工智能的工具是否可以有意义地帮助作家听起来不那么人工智能生成。这些回答表明，参与者普遍熟悉写作辅助工具，但不太熟悉专门围绕检测或避免类似人工智能写作的工具。Because of the small sample size, these responses are treated as anecdotal rather than as a basis for quantitative analysis. Still, they provide useful context. All but one participant reported using spellcheck, autocomplete, or basic grammar tools at least sometimes, and most had used AI-related writing tools such as Claude or ChatGPT. Participants generally described these tools as assistants rather than as primary executors of a writing task. None of the surveyed participants reported having used an AI detector. When asked what they did when the system suggested a word they might otherwise have used, participants described strategies such as substituting synonyms, changing sentence structure, ignoring the suggestion, or choosing more emotionally specific words. Responses to the idea of a tool for avoiding AI-like writing were mixed: some participants saw possible use cases in professional writing, email, or social media, while others questioned whether an AI-based tool could meaningfully help writers sound less AI-generated. These responses suggest that participants were broadly familiar with writing assistance tools, but less familiar with tools specifically framed around detecting or avoiding AI-like writing.

V 讨论Discussion

这项研究旨在研究当人工智能援助可用但明确不鼓励时，用户如何协商人工智能援助。在整个数据集中，尝试违反人工智能建议限制的情况很少见，近四分之三的提交内容根本没有尝试。我们并没有将其解释为简单地回避人工智能，而是将其解释为证据，表明建议使用是由任务框架和激励措施决定的情境选择。当人工智能辅助没有被定位为默认辅助或效率增益时，用户似乎愿意放弃它，即使它很容易获得。这表明商业写作工具的广泛采用可能不是由用户偏好驱动，而是由标准化和奖励接受的界面设计选择驱动。This study set out to examine how users negotiate AI assistance when it is available but explicitly discouraged. Across the dataset, attempted transgression of AI-suggestion restrictions was rare, with nearly three quarters of submissions containing no attempts at all. Rather than interpreting this as simple avoidance of AI, we interpret it as evidence that suggestion use is a situated choice shaped by task framing and incentives. When AI assistance is not positioned as the default or as an efficiency gain, users appear willing to forgo it, even when it is readily available. This suggests that much of the widespread adoption observed in commercial writing tools may be driven less by user preference than by interface design choices that normalize and reward acceptance.

不同提示类别中人工智能采用越界的差异进一步表明，任务框架在塑造人类与人工智能的协作方面发挥着核心作用。强调正确性、合理性或常识的解释性提示表现出最高的建议使用率。相比之下，基于想象力或即时感知的创造性和观察性提示显示人工智能的采用率最低。一种解释是，当用户认为某项任务具有外部正确或权威的答案时，人工智能建议与他们对帮助的期望一致。当任务是体验性或表现性的时，人工智能生成的延续可能会提供较少的感知价值，甚至感觉具有侵入性。Differences in AI adoption transgressions across prompt categories further suggest that task framing plays a central role in shaping human–AI collaboration. Explanatory prompts, which emphasize correctness, justification, or general knowledge, exhibited the highest rates of attempted suggestion use. In contrast, creative and observational prompts, which are grounded in imagination or immediate perception, showed minimal AI adoption. One interpretation is that when users perceive a task as having an externally correct or authoritative answer, AI suggestions align with their expectations of assistance. When tasks are experiential or expressive, AI-generated continuations may offer less perceived value or even feel intrusive.

基于游戏的框架The game-based framing of防滑Nonslop并不是为了激发传统意义上的绩效或最大限度地提高参与度，而是为了创造一个可以观察甚至强调围绕人工智能使用的微观决策的空间。通过将可见的后果附加到建议的使用上，游戏将通常不可见的交互转变为明确的选择。这支持使用轻量级游戏机制作为研究人类与人工智能交互的研究镜头，特别是在默认界面设计模糊了谈判和代理时刻的领域。was not intended to motivate performance or maximize engagement in the conventional sense, but to create a space where micro-decisions around AI use could be observed and even emphasized. By attaching visible consequences to suggestion use, the game transformed what is typically an invisible interaction into an explicit choice. This supports the use of lightweight game mechanics as a research lens for studying human–AI interaction, particularly in domains where default interface designs obscure moments of negotiation and agency.

本研究的重点是简短的、独立的写作任务。将该框架扩展到更长或累积的写作活动，例如多段落叙述或连续提示，可以揭示人工智能采用策略如何随着时间的推移而演变。通过反复接触，随着风格一致性和长期语音变得更加相关，用户可能会重新协商便利性和控制之间的平衡。更大或更持久的参与者池还可以分析用户内部的变化，从而使未来的研究能够区分新奇效果和学习策略。The present study focused on short, self-contained writing tasks. Extending this framework to longer or cumulative writing activities, such as multi-paragraph narratives or sequential prompts, could reveal how AI adoption strategies evolve over time. With repeated exposure, users may renegotiate the balance between convenience and control as stylistic consistency and long-term voice become more relevant. A larger or more persistent participant pool would also enable analysis of within-user change, allowing future studies to distinguish novelty effects from learned strategies.

这些发现对人工智能辅助书写系统的设计具有影响。默认无摩擦建议接受的界面可能会掩盖用户的矛盾心理或对自主的偏好，特别是在创造性或表达性任务中。引入可选的摩擦、透明度或上下文控制可以让用户更谨慎地协商人工智能辅助何时以及如何进入他们的写作过程。同时These findings have implications for the design of AI-assisted writing systems. Interfaces that default to frictionless suggestion acceptance may mask user ambivalence or preference for autonomy, particularly in creative or expressive tasks. Introducing optional friction, transparency, or contextual controls could allow users to more deliberately negotiate when and how AI assistance enters their writing process. While防滑Nonslop通过游戏框架夸大这些动态，类似的原则可能适用于旨在支持创造力而不削弱个人声音的非游戏界面。exaggerates these dynamics through a game framing, similar principles may apply to non-game interfaces that aim to support creativity without eroding individual voice.

这项研究受到样本量小、响应长度短以及浏览器内推理所施加的技术限制的限制。能够访问游戏的人群偏向于拥有兼容硬件和浏览器的用户，并且有趣的框架可能无法推广到专业写作环境。由于系统被设计为游戏，一些行为也可能反映了完成任务或避免惩罚的策略，而不是对创造性声音的稳定偏好。因此，结果应被解释为指示性的而不是确定性的。该方法的优势不在于代表性，而在于其能够呈现在传统人工智能辅助写作环境中难以观察到的行为。This study is limited by its small sample size, short response lengths, and the technical constraints imposed by in-browser inference. The population able to access the game was skewed toward users with compatible hardware and browsers, and the playful framing may not generalize to professional writing contexts. Because the system was framed as a game, some behaviors may also reflect strategies for completing the task or avoiding penalties rather than stable preferences about creative voice. As such, the results should be interpreted as indicative rather than definitive. The strength of the approach lies not in representativeness but in its ability to surface behaviors that are difficult to observe in conventional AI-assisted writing environments.

VI 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了This paper introduced防滑Nonslop，一款基于网络的小型游戏，旨在研究当人工智能生成的单词建议可用但不鼓励时，人们如何响应这些建议。通过将浏览器内下一个单词预测与建议使用的明确反馈相结合，游戏创建了一个设置，玩家必须决定采用人工智能生成的语言是否值得潜在的惩罚。, a small web-based game designed to study how people respond to AI-generated word suggestions when those suggestions are available but discouraged. By combining in-browser next-word prediction with explicit feedback on suggestion use, the game creates a setting where players must decide whether adopting AI-generated language is worth the potential penalty.

在 74 名参与者提交的 214 份意见中，我们发现大多数用户很少尝试采用建议的词语。在近四分之三的提交内容中，根本没有提出任何人工智能建议的违规行为。当我们汇总用户层面的行为时，出现了一个清晰的模式：大多数玩家都是“极简主义者”，他们给出了简短的回复，几乎没有人工智能采用的违规行为，而一小部分“选择性采用者”则写了较长的回复并偶尔使用建议，而一小部分“积极采用者”互动更频繁，更频繁地使用建议。这表明，即使在相同的界面和激励结构下，用户也会采取不同的策略来参与人工智能支持。Across 214 submissions from 74 participants, we found that most users rarely attempted to adopt suggested words. In nearly three quarters of submissions, no AI suggestion transgressions were made at all. When we aggregated behavior at the user level, a clear pattern emerged: the majority of players were “minimalists” who produced short responses with almost no AI adoption transgressions, while a smaller group of “selective adopters” wrote longer responses and used suggestions occasionally, and an even smaller group of “active adopters” interacted more frequently and used suggestions more often. This suggests that, even under the same interface and incentive structure, users adopt distinct strategies for how to engage with AI support.

我们还观察到提示之间的系统差异。解释性提示显示，人工智能采用的平均违规率比创造性或观察性提示要高得多，而且最常回答的提示的建议使用率往往略低。总之，这些结果表明任务类型和提示吸引力决定了用户利用人工智能生成的延续的可能性。We also observed systematic differences across prompts. Explanatory prompts showed much higher average AI adoption transgressions than creative or observational prompts, and the most frequently answered prompts tended to have slightly lower suggestion use. Together, these results indicate that both task type and prompt appeal shape how likely users are to draw on AI-generated continuations.

这些发现受到我们样本的大小和组成以及 Web-LLM 设置的技术限制的限制，该设置排除了许多移动和锁定系统。回复本身很短，而且提示涵盖的任务类型范围很窄。尽管如此，该研究表明，一个简单的游戏可以围绕人工智能建议的违规行为做出可观察和可分析的隐藏决策。These findings are limited by the size and composition of our sample, as well as by the technical constraints of the Web-LLM setup, which excluded many mobile and locked-down systems. The responses themselves were short, and the prompts covered a narrow range of task types. Nonetheless, the study demonstrates that a simple game can make otherwise hidden decisions around AI suggestion transgressions observable and analyzable.

未来的工作可以通过测试不同的激励结构，比较阻止、警告或鼓励人工智能使用的界面，以及检查更长或更开放式的写作任务来扩展这种方法。更广泛地说，这项工作支持使用类似游戏的环境作为测试平台，以了解人们在实践中如何实际使用、避免和协商人工智能写作工具。未来的工作还应该将当前的本地推理实现与技术上更强大的版本（例如服务器端或基于 API 的建议系统）进行比较，以将游戏设计的行为影响与访问失败、延迟和浏览器兼容性问题分开。Future work could extend this approach by testing different incentive structures, comparing interfaces that block, warn about, or encourage AI use, and examining longer or more open-ended writing tasks. More broadly, this work supports the use of game-like environments as testbeds for understanding how people actually use, avoid, and negotiate with AI writing tools in practice. Future work should also compare the current local-inference implementation with a more technically robust version, such as a server-side or API-based suggestion system, to separate behavioral effects of the game design from access failures, latency, and browser compatibility issues.
