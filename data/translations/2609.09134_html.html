1 简介Introduction

代理人的能力由底层 LLM 和 围绕它的工具——系统提示符、工具集、执行挂钩， 以及确定模型观察和观察内容的上下文管理脚手架 它的行动空间。最近的工作表明，该安全带是一个强大的 杠杆，并将其视为可编辑程序并自动搜索它 可以将小型开放权重模型提升到编码和近前沿精度 企业任务The capability of an agent is determined jointly by the underlying LLM and by the harness that surrounds it—the system prompt, tool set, execution hooks, and context-management scaffolding that determine what the model observes and its action space. Recent work has shown that the harness is a powerful lever, and treating it as an editable program and searching over it automatically can lift a small open-weight model to near-frontier accuracy on coding and enterprise tasks(Yang et al., 2026; Agrawal et al., 2025; Lee et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2026b)。 这对于涉及针对内部 API 的工具调用、代码重构和长期数据审计的企业代理任务尤其有价值， 其中推理成本也很重要，并且特定于任务的工具可以提供较小模型所需的脚手架，以便以低得多的成本可靠地执行。. This is especially valuable for enterprise agentic tasks involving tool calling against internal APIs, code refactoring, and long-horizon data auditing, where inference cost also matters and a task-specific harness can provide the scaffolding needed for a smaller model to perform reliably at substantially lower cost.

然而，利用进化只是使代理适应环境的两个杠杆之一。 域；另一个是模型权重。在这项研究中，我们的兴趣在于 特定领域体系中的工具和模型的共同进化，而不是 旨在提高模型一般代理能力的更广泛的后期培训。 因此，我们主要尝试参数高效的微调，例如LoRA-SFTHarness evolution, however, is only one of two levers for adapting an agent to a domain; the other is the model weights. In this study, our interest is in the co-evolution of the harness and model in the domain-specific regime, rather than in broader post-training aimed at raising a model’s general agentic ability. Therefore, we mainly experiment with parameter-efficient fine-tuning, such as LoRA-SFT(Hu et al., 2022)这是广泛 用于在几个 GPU 小时内使小型或中型模型适应狭窄的任务。我们提出的核心问题是：在合理的预算下针对特定任务的领域适应， 应该如何将线束进化和轻量级模型微调结合起来，以及 它们能否共同进化以实现协同收益而不是相互干扰？which is widely used to adapt a small or mid size model to a narrow task in a few GPU-hours. The central question we ask is: for task-specific domain adaptation under a reasonable budget, how should harness evolution and lightweight model fine-tuning be combined, and can they be co-evolved to achieve synergistic gains rather than interfere with one another?

A natural training signal for closing this model capability gap is therefore to imitate the stronger expert, and this suggests a simple recipe: evolve the harness first, then fine-tune the weaker model under the expert model’s guidance. Surprisingly, we find that fine-tuning on expert trajectories collected under the evolved harness consistently degrades task success, even though the same recipe yields substantial gains under the unevolved baseline harness, indicating that the failure arises from the interaction between imitation and harness evolution rather than from imitation itself. 2 相关工作Related Work

先前的工作表明，对单个提示或更广泛的工具实施进行优化可以使较小的模型执行复杂的、多步骤的、使用工具的代理任务Prior work has shown that optimizing over individual prompts or broader harness implementations can enable smaller models to perform complex, multi-step, tool-using agentic tasks(Yang et al., 2026; Agrawal et al., 2025; Lee et al., 2026)。然而，利用和模型适应之间的界限是多孔的：通过外部脚手架引入的行为随后可以被吸收到模型权重中. However, the boundary between harness and model adaptation is porous: behaviors introduced through external scaffolding can subsequently be absorbed into model weights(Dennis et al., 2026; Lu et al., 2026)。因此，最近的工作已经开始优化这两个杠杆。 SIA 交错分类和科学优化任务的利用和模型更新； Co-Harness 根据经过改进的数学推理工具生成的经过验证的成功轨迹对模型进行微调； HarnessForge 和 HarnessX 联合优化模型策略和不断发展的线束. Recent work has therefore begun to optimize both levers. SIA interleaves harness and model updates on classification and scientific optimization tasks; Co-Harness fine-tunes a model on its own verified successful trajectories generated under an improved harness for mathematical reasoning; and HarnessForge and HarnessX jointly optimize model policies and evolving harnesses(Hebbar et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2026c; Chen et al., 2026a; Chen et al., 2026b)。尽管这些研究报告了各自领域的进展，但仍不清楚一旦安全带专门用于特定模型，这两个杠杆应如何协调，以及在什么条件下后续重量更新将复合而不是破坏这种配合。我们在异构、长期的企业代理任务中研究了这个问题，特别关注由更强的模型生成的监督作为额外的教学信号。 智能体后期训练通常对成功的专家轨迹进行监督微调，这可以显着提高工具的使用和任务的完成度. Although these studies report gains in their respective domains, it remains unclear how the two levers should be coordinated once a harness has become specialized to a particular model, and under what conditions subsequent weight updates will compound rather than disrupt that fit. We investigate this question in heterogeneous, long-horizon enterprise agent tasks, focusing specifically on supervision generated by a stronger model as an additional teaching signal. Agent post-training commonly uses supervised fine-tuning on successful expert trajectories, which can substantially improve tool use and task completion(Wang et al., 2026)。最近的方法超越了大规模轨迹模仿，通过在学生政策引起的状态下获得教师的延续或干预. Recent approaches move beyond wholesale trajectory imitation by obtaining teacher continuations or interventions at states induced by the student policy(Lauffer et al., 2025; Ye et al., 2026)。然而，这些方法在固定的代理接口下调整模型，并且没有检查它们的监督如何与进化的、特定于模型的工具进行交互。我们的工作将这些线索联系起来，表明模仿完整的专家轨迹可以破坏较弱模型对此类线束的拟合，而对较弱模型访问的状态进行局部专家校正则保留了这种拟合，并允许线束和模型适应组合，为它们的共同进化提供了实用的原则。. However, these methods adapt the model under a fixed agent interface and do not examine how their supervision interacts with an evolved, model-specific harness. Our work connects these lines by showing that imitating complete expert trajectories can break a weaker model’s fit to such a harness, whereas localized expert correction at states visited by the weaker model preserves this fit and allows harness and model adaptation to compose, providing a practical principle for their co-evolution.

3.4 策略内专家校正使 Harness 和模型适应组合起来On-Policy Expert Correction Makes Harness and Model Adaptation Compose

为了在不破坏规划拟合的情况下引入专家的教学信号，我们在策略上纠正较弱的模型，而不是模仿整个专家轨迹。我们设计了一个数据合成管道，由自我导向的 MLE 代理端到端驱动，保留较弱模型自己的规划分布。从较弱的模型在进化的工具下自己的推出开始，代理定位每个失败的推出出错的单个回合，然后专家模型仅重写该回合。通过这种方式，我们只引入必要的校正，而模型生成的每个周围步骤都保持不变。然后，我们在这组经过最少编辑的轨迹上构建较弱的 LoRA-SFT 模型，并在相同的进化工具下对其进行评估。To introduce the expert’s teaching signal without breaking planning fit, we instead correct the weaker model on-policy rather than imitating entire expert trajectories. We design a data-synthesis pipeline, driven end-to-end by a self-directed MLE agent, that preserves the weaker model’s own planning distribution. Starting from the weaker model’s own rollouts under the evolved harness, the agent localizes the single turn at which each failed rollout goes wrong, and an expert model then rewrites only that one turn in place. In this way we introduce only the necessary correction, leaving every surrounding step the model produced untouched. We then LoRA-SFT the weaker model on this set of minimally edited trajectories and evaluate it under the same evolved harness.

我们的结果表明，这使得利用演化和模型微调这两个杠杆得以结合。在策略修正在每项任务上都匹配或击败了进化线束基础（表Our results show that this makes the two levers—harness evolution and model fine-tuning—compose. On-policy correction matches or beats the evolved-harness base on every task (Table1，第 7 行）：它将平均测试成功率从 78.0% 提高到 79.7% (+1.7)，实现了七项任务中的五项——网站管理 (+5.6)、库存警报 (+2.2)、代码重构 (+2.2)、异常检测 (+1.7) 和浏览器自动化 (+1.2)——并且在安全带可能已经饱和的两项任务上保持在噪音范围内（预算批准）, row 7): it raises mean test success from 78.0% to 79.7% (+1.7), gaining on five of seven tasks—website management (+5.6), stock alerting (+2.2), code refactoring (+2.2), anomaly detection (+1.7), and browser automation (+1.2)—and staying within noise on the two tasks where the harness has likely already saturated (budget approval−-0.6、工资审核0.6, payroll auditing−-0.4）。这与直接模仿形成鲜明对比，直接模仿在所有七项任务上都出现了退化（0.4). This contrasts sharply with direct imitation, which regressed on all seven tasks (−-平均 14.9）：根据自身轨迹校正模型可增加安全带离开净空的能力，并在安全带已经接近天花板的位置保留该功能。14.9 on average): correcting the model on its own trajectories adds capability where the harness left headroom and preserves it where the harness was already near ceiling.

故障分析显示了为什么增益是安全的（表The failure analysis shows why the gain is safe (Table3）。在政策修正下，规划桶保持在基本模型的底线，仅将故障率从 1.1% 移动到 1.8% (+0.7)，这与仿制品跳跃到 14.6% 形成鲜明对比，因此模型与线束的拟合得以保留。与此同时知识桶进一步下降，从46.2%下降到43.2%（). Under on-policy correction, the planning bucket stays at the base-model floor, moving only from 1.1% to 1.8% of failures (+0.7)—in sharp contrast to imitation’s jump to 14.6%—so the model–harness fit is preserved. At the same time the knowledge bucket falls further, from 46.2% to 43.2% (−-3.0），总体故障率也下降（28.9％至26.8％）。因此，政策修正提供了与模仿相同的知识改进，但没有计划崩溃，从而破坏了较弱模型和进化工具之间的配合。它并没有完全缩小较弱模型和专家模型之间的差距，这可能是由于轻量级 LoRA 方法的限制，但它提供了一种有原则且低成本的方法，可以最大限度地发挥进化工具和模型训练之间的联合协同作用，而不会引入适得其反的回归。由于校正完全由自引导 MLE 代理生成，因此可以安全地将其堆叠到线束和模型的迭代协同进化中。3.0), and the overall failure rate drops as well (28.9% to 26.8%). On-policy correction therefore delivers the same knowledge improvement that imitation did, but without the planning collapse that breaks the fit between the weaker model and the evolved harness. It does not fully close the gap between the weaker and the expert model, likely due to a limit of the lightweight LoRA approach, but it offers a principled and low-cost way to maximize the joint synergy between an evolved harness and model training, without introducing counterproductive regression. Because the correction is generated entirely by the self-directed MLE agent, it can be safely stacked into an iterative co-evolution of both harness and model.

4 结论Conclusion

可靠地解决复杂的、长期的企业任务具有挑战性，而优化的工具已显示出有望帮助较弱的模型完成此任务，而成本只是较强的前沿模型的一小部分。在这项研究中，我们询问通过轻量级微调来共同优化模型权重和线束进化是否可以进一步提升性能。我们首先发现，即使该工具是为较弱的模型而进化的，但较强的专家模型可以很好地使用它，这表明专家可以向较弱的模型展示如何缩小差距。然而，在进化的控制下，简单地模仿专家的轨迹就会回归较弱的模型（Solving complex, long-horizon enterprise tasks reliably is challenging, and an optimized harness has shown promise in helping a weaker model do so at a fraction of the cost of a stronger frontier model. In this study we asked whether co-optimizing the model weights alongside harness evolution, through lightweight fine-tuning, can push performance further still. We first find that even though the harness is evolved for a weaker model, a stronger expert model uses it well, which suggests the expert could show the weaker model how to close the gap. However, under the evolved harness, simply imitating the expert’s trajectories regresses the weaker model (−-平均 14.9）。我们的分析说明了原因：模仿改变了模型的规划风格，从而破坏了其与安全带的配合并抵消了安全带的优势。然后，我们开发了一个用于策略校正的自引导 MLE 管道，该管道综合训练数据并在其自身的推出时微调模型。这种方法在不损害通过线束进化已经实现的收益的情况下提供了进一步的增益，并提供了模型和线束共同进化的良好方法。该配方也是轻量且经济的：训练时间不到一个小时，因此可以安全地堆叠到共同进化循环中。更广泛地说，我们的结果表明，在线束模型设置中保持策略一致是最重要的：教学信号只有在尊重模型与其所操作的线束之间的配合时才有用。14.9 on average). Our analysis shows why: imitation shifts the model’s planning style, which disrupts its fit with the harness and cancels out the harness’s benefits. We then develop a self-directed MLE pipeline for on-policy correction, which synthesizes training data and fine-tunes the model on its own rollouts. This approach delivers a further gain without eroding the gains already achieved through harness evolution, and offers a sound way to co-evolve the model and the harness. The recipe is also lightweight and economical: training takes under an hour, so it can be stacked safely into a co-evolution loop. More broadly, our results show that staying on-policy is what matters in the harness–model setting: the teaching signal is only useful when it respects the fit between the model and the harness it operates on.

模型和线束的共同进化是一个令人兴奋的方向。在未来的工作中，我们计划将强化学习与政策专家指导相结合，在仍有空间的情况下进一步推动较弱的模型。我们还希望让线束进化意识到模型稍后将进行微调，因此两个手臂可以联合优化，而不是按顺序运行。Co-evolution of both models and harnesses is an exciting direction. In future work, we plan to combine reinforcement learning with on-policy expert guidance to push the weaker model further where headroom remains. We also want to make harness evolution aware that the model will later be fine-tuned, so the two arms can be jointly optimized rather than run in sequence.

我们提供了两次展示，展示了专家模仿如何打破模型与模型之间的契合度。 进化后的安全带。在这两种情况下，微调模型都具有所需的领域知识；什么 它失去的是调整安全带的计划节奏。We give two rollouts that show how expert imitation breaks the fit between the model and the evolved harness. 案例1：工资审计（Qwen）。Case 1: payroll audit (Qwen).

Under the base model this scaffold works cleanly—on one example all three rollouts score1.01.0只需 14-20 个步骤，因为该工具的“计算、验证 “一次，完成”的节奏适合模型自己的逐步规划。模仿后 微调模型仍然会写出正确的输出（记分员甚至注意到正确的输出） 部门汇总），但随着其规划节奏的剥离，它变成了单一验证 进入无限循环：它重新发出相同的视图 29 次，重新读取提示 大约 20 次，累积了工具错误，并且在 78 个步骤中从未完成，因此 推出分数in 14–20 steps, because the harness’s “compute, verify once, finish” cadence was fit to the model’s own step-by-step planning. After imitation fine-tuning the model still writes the correct output (the scorer even notes the correct department aggregates), but with its planning cadence stripped it turns the single verify step into an unbounded loop: it re-issues the same view 29 times, re-reads the prompt about 20 times, accumulates tool errors, and never emits a finish across 78 steps, so the rollout scores00。一样的知识，一样的驾驭——只是后仿方案风格 不同了，它不再落在脚手架所期望的终点上。. 案例 2：Webarena 评论计数（Gemma）。Case 2: Webarena review count (Gemma).

这里进化后的安全带增加了 “Magento 管理约定”块的承载部分是网格过滤器过程： 找到状态列，应用过滤器，然后读取过滤后的总计，然后返回 通过结构化精加工工具。对于“已批准评论的总数”（真相 346）， 基本模型遵循“过滤，然后计数”的过程并正确回答。的 杰玛模仿精调，模仿了专家简洁、早回答的风格，但不是 其背后的能力：它导航到评论网格，但从未形成“过滤器，然后 count”子目标，直接从网格中读取未过滤的“找到 351 条记录”总数， 并以 351 结束。在其他复习题中也会出现同样的反应，其中 未经过滤的总数不可能是正确的。因为 Gemma 已经分享了专家的简洁输出 形状，这个案例表明问题不在于表面风格，而在于自信和简洁 较弱的模型无法备份，并且它完全绕过了安全带的多步骤过程 依靠。The evolved harness here adds a “Magento Admin Conventions” block whose load-bearing piece is a grid-filter procedure: locate the status column, apply the filter, then read the filtered total, and return it through a structured finish tool. For “total count of Approved reviews” (truth 346), the base model follows this “filter, then count” procedure and answers correctly. The imitation-fine-tuned Gemma copies the expert’s terse, answer-early style but not the competence behind it: it navigates to the review grid but never forms the “filter, then count” sub-goal, reads the unfiltered “351 records found” total straight off the grid, and finishes with 351. The same reflex recurs on other review questions where the unfiltered total cannot be right. Because Gemma already shares the expert’s terse output shape, this case shows the problem is not surface style but a confidence and brevity the weaker model cannot back up—and it bypasses exactly the multi-step procedure the harness relied on.
