iARCS：用于可控 3D 场景生成的迭代代理强化学习iARCS: Iterative Agentic RL for Controllable 3D Scene Generation

摘要Abstract

合成 3D 场景生成越来越多地用作计算机视觉和嵌入式 AI 的数据源，但现有的生成器通常会优化感知真实感，而无法可靠地满足关键任务的功能约束。这种不匹配限制了合成数据在下游训练中的有用性，在下游训练中，可访问性、可遍历性和空间规则合规性通常至关重要。我们提出了 iARCS，这是一种迭代代理强化学习框架，它使预训练的场景生成器适应自然语言任务要求。 iARCS 采用两阶段策略：通用奖励预训练以提高物理合理性和布局质量，然后使用 LLM 生成的奖励计划（根据训练反馈迭代完善）进行针对特定任务的微调。实验表明，步行性、可达性和以间隙为中心的任务的约束保真度有所提高，有效的特定任务约束优化和竞争场景多样性。我们进一步表明，iARCS 生成的数据改进了基础生成器，支持其作为实用的合成数据生成工具的价值，而不仅仅是可控的场景编辑方法。Synthetic 3D scene generation is increasingly used as a data source for computer vision and embodied AI, but existing generators often optimize perceptual realism without reliably satisfying task-critical functional constraints. This mismatch limits the usefulness of synthetic data for downstream training, where accessibility, traversability, and spatial rule compliance are often essential. We present iARCS, an iterative agentic reinforcement learning framework that adapts a pretrained scene generator to natural-language task requirements. iARCS uses a two-stage strategy: universal-reward pretraining to improve physical plausibility and layout quality, followed by task-specific fine-tuning with LLM-generated reward programs that are iteratively refined from training feedback. Experiments show improved constraint fidelity on walkability, reachability, and clearance-focused tasks, effective task-specific constraint optimization, and competitive scene diversity. We further show that data generated by iARCS improves a base generator, supporting its value as a practical synthetic data generation tool rather than only a controllable scene editing method.

Consequently, generated training data can appear realistic while remaining weakly aligned with downstream functional utility.

单独的文本条件通常不足以满足严格的功能场景要求：语言引导的管道改进了指令跟踪，但它们不能可靠地强制执行精确的几何约束，并且通常在生成过程中缺乏显式验证Text conditioning alone is usually insufficient for strict functional scene requirements: language-guided pipelines improve instruction following, but they do not reliably enforce precise geometric constraints and typically lack explicit verification during generation[42, 20]。奖励引导的优化提高了可控性，但现有实践仍然依赖于手动设计的目标和大量的奖励设计工作，这限制了对任意新约束的干净扩展. Reward-guided optimization improves controllability, yet existing practice still depends on manually engineered objectives and substantial reward-design effort, which limits clean scaling to arbitrary new constraints[41, 23, 2, 26].

我们将合成场景生成制定为预训练场景的训练后适应，先于满足用户定义的功能约束，同时保留真实性和多样性。这种观点与最近的训练后和基于强化学习的生成模型控制相一致，后者在预训练的扩散先验之上优化特定于任务的目标，而不是从头开始重建生成器We formulate synthetic scene generation as the post-training adaptation of a pretrained scene prior to satisfy user-defined functional constraints while preserving realism and diversity. In第一阶段Stage 1，我们优化通用奖励（例如，物理合理性和设计先验）以增强基线场景质量，并且, we optimize universal rewards (e.g., physical plausibility and design priors) to strengthen baseline scene quality, and in第二阶段Stage 2，LLM 代理将用户提示转化为特定于任务的奖励计划，并使用培训反馈迭代地完善它们。我们使用去噪扩散策略优化（DDPO）对扩散发生器进行微调, an LLM agent translates user prompts into task-specific reward programs and iteratively refines them using training feedback. 这种设计解决了两种故障模式：它保留了学习场景分布的真实性和多样性，同时通过奖励驱动的适应来明确执行功能约束。根据经验，两阶段优化计划比单阶段训练更有效：首先优化通用奖励，然后联合针对特定任务和通用奖励进行微调，产生更强的身体和功能表现。总结如下图所示。This design addresses both failure modes: it retains the realism and diversity of learned scene distributions while enabling explicit enforcement of functional constraints through reward-driven adaptation. Empirically, the two-stage optimization schedule is substantially more effective than single-stage training: first optimizing universal rewards, then fine-tuning with task-specific and universal rewards jointly, yields stronger physical and functional performance. As summarized in Fig.2，我们发现 iARCS 可以作为一种有效的合成数据引擎：使用 iARCS 生成的合成数据训练 MiDiffusion 可以提高强大的最先进的室内场景生成器，同时保留多样性和真实性。, we find that iARCS functions as an effective synthetic-data engine: training MiDiffusion with iARCS-generated synthetic data improves a strong state-of-the-art indoor scene generator while preserving diversity and realism.

3 方法Method

我们的方法是一个两阶段框架。在第一阶段，我们使用强化学习（RL）通过优化物理合理性（避免碰撞和入界放置）和功能实用性（步行性和可达性）来纠正基于基础数据分布训练的扩散模型中的偏差。在第二阶段，根据用户提示，LLM 模块在第一阶段约束的基础上生成特定于任务的奖励函数，并通过迭代奖励反映进一步优化模型。在我们的实验中，我们使用 GeminiOur method is a two-stage framework. In Stage 1, we use reinforcement learning (RL) to correct biases in a diffusion model trained on the base data distribution by optimizing for physical plausibility (collision avoidance and in-bound placement) and functional utility (walkability and reachability). In Stage 2, given a user prompt, an LLM module generates task-specific reward functions on top of Stage 1 constraints, and the model is further optimized through iterative reward reflection. In our experiments, we use Gemini[37]模型作为 LLM 函数生成器，并在 RL 训练循环的每 10 个周期执行奖励反射。model as LLM function generator and perform reward reflections every 10 epochs of RL training loop.

4 实验Experiments

为了证明我们的框架作为可扩展的合成数据生成器的可行性，我们的实验重点关注三个核心问题：（1）我们的方法能否提高物理和设计约束满意度，同时保留原始数据分布的多样性？ (2) 使用 3D-FRONT + 我们生成的数据训练的 MiDiffusion 是否优于仅使用 3D-FRONT 训练的 MiDiffusion？ (3) 代理框架处理特定的、面向任务的约束的效果如何？To demonstrate our framework’s viability as a scalable synthetic data generator, our experiments focus on three core questions: (1) Can our method improve physical and design constraint satisfaction while preserving the diversity of the original data distribution? (2) Does MiDiffusion trained with 3D-FRONT + our generated data outperform MiDiffusion trained with only 3D-FRONT? (3) How well does the agentic framework handle specific, task-oriented constraints?
