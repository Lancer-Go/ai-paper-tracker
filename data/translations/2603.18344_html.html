HRI-SA：用于远程人机协作期间人类态势感知在线评估的多模态数据集HRI-SA: A Multimodal Dataset for Online Assessment of Human Situational Awareness during Remote Human-Robot Teaming

Hashini Senaratne1, Richard Attfield2, Samith Widhanapathirana1, David Howard1, Cecile Paris1,
Dana Kulic1,2, Leimin Tian1 1CSIRO Robotics: www.csiro.au/en/research/technology-space/robotics2Monash Robotics: www.monash.edu/engineering/robotics
摘要Abstract

保持态势感知对于人机团队至关重要。然而，在高工作量和动态条件下，操作员经常会遇到态势感知差距。 自动检测态势感知差距可以为操作员提供及时的帮助。然而，传统的态势感知措施要么会破坏任务流程，要么无法捕获实时波动，从而限制了其操作效用。据我们所知，目前没有公开的数据集支持对人机协作中的在线人类态势感知评估进行系统评估。为了推进在线态势感知评估工具的开发，我们引入了 HRI-SA，这是一个多模态数据集，由 30 名参与者在现实的搜索和救援人类机器人团队环境中收集，包含眼球运动、瞳孔直径、生物信号、用户交互和机器人数据。实验协议包括需要及时操作员援助的预定义事件，通过测量援助需求开始和解决之间的时间来系统地获得两种类型（感知和理解）的地面真实态势感知延迟。我们通过评估标准机器学习模型来说明该数据集的实用性，该模型用于使用通用眼动追踪特征和上下文特征来检测感知态势感知延迟。结果表明，仅使用眼球追踪特征就可以使用留一组交叉验证有效地对感知态势感知延迟进行分类（召回率=88.91%，F1=67.63%），并通过上下文数据融合提高性能（召回率=91.51%，F1=80.38%）。本文贡献了第一个公共数据集，支持在整个人机协作任务中对态势感知进行系统评估，同时还展示了通用眼动追踪功能在远程人机协作中连续感知态势感知延迟检测的潜力。Maintaining situational awareness is critical in human–robot teams. Yet, under high workload and dynamic conditions, operators often experience situational awareness gaps. Automated detection of situational awareness gaps could provide timely assistance for operators. However, conventional situational awareness measures either disrupt task flow or cannot capture real-time fluctuations, limiting their operational utility. To the best of our knowledge, no publicly available dataset currently supports the systematic evaluation of online human situational awareness assessment in human-robot teaming. To advance the development of online situational awareness assessment tools, we introduce HRI-SA, a multimodal dataset from 30 participants in a realistic search-and-rescue human-robot teaming context, incorporating eye movements, pupil diameter, biosignals, user interactions, and robot data. The experimental protocol included predefined events requiring timely operator assistance, with ground truth situational awareness latency of two types (perceptual and comprehension) systematically obtained by measuring the time between assistance need onset and resolution. We illustrate the utility of this dataset by evaluating standard machine learning models for detecting perceptual situational awareness latencies using generic eye-tracking features and contextual features. Results show that eye-tracking features alone effectively classified perceptual situational awareness latency (recall=88.91%, F1=67.63%) using leave-one-group-out cross-validation, with performance improved through contextual data fusion (recall=91.51%, F1=80.38%). This paper contributes the first public dataset supporting the systematic evaluation of situational awareness throughout a human-robot teaming mission, while also demonstrating the potential of generic eye-tracking features for continuous perceptual situational awareness latency detection in remote human-robot teaming.

I 简介INTRODUCTION

人类操作员的态势感知（SA）是人机团队绩效和任务成功的关键决定因素Human operators’ Situational Awareness (SA) is a critical determinant of performance and mission success in human–robot teams[26, 29, 21]。随着机器人系统能力的不断提高以及人机团队规模的扩大，维持操作员所需的 SA 水平变得越来越具有挑战性. As robotic systems continue to advance in capability and the scale of human–robot teams expands, maintaining required levels of SA for operators has become increasingly challenging[20, 22]。这一挑战在现场机器人技术中尤为明显，其中一个人经常在高工作量、环境不确定性和动态条件下远程引导一组半自主机器人。例子包括 DARPA 地下挑战. This challenge is particularly evident in field robotics, where one human often remotely guides a fleet of semi-autonomous robots under high workload, environmental uncertainty and dynamic conditions. Examples include the DARPA Subterranean Challenge[20]，多无人机（UAV）任务, multi-Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) missions[6]和农业机器人, and agricultural robotics[24]。最近的一项访谈研究发现，在这种情况下反复出现和频繁的 SA 差距，反映了操作员的 SA 与特定于任务和时间的 SA 要求之间的间歇性不一致. A recent interview study identifies recurring and frequent SA gaps in such contexts, reflecting intermittent misalignment between operators’ SA and the task- and time-specific SA requirements[25]。这些 SA 差距会导致任务表现不佳，增加机器人、旁观者和环境的风险，并增加操作员的挫败感和压力。根据这项研究，运营商表达了对积极支持缓解 SA 差距的机器人系统的强烈偏好。. These SA gaps lead to poor mission performance, heightened risks to robots, bystanders and the environment, and increased operator frustration and stress. According to this study, operators expressed a strong preference for robotic systems that actively support the mitigation of SA gaps.

目前，大多数机器人系统为操作员维持或恢复所需的 SA 水平提供有限的支持或不提供支持Currently, most robotic systems offer limited or no support for operators to maintain or regain required levels of SA[25]。在人机交互 (HRI) 过程中测量 SA 对于提供有意义的帮助至关重要。传统上，SA 是使用冻结探针方法（例如 SAGAT：态势感知全球评估技术）和试验后主观测量（例如 SART：态势感知评级技术）进行评估的。. Measuring SA during Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) is crucial for meaningful assistance. Traditionally, SA has been assessed using freeze-probe methods (e.g., SAGAT: the Situation Awareness Global Assessment Technique) and post-trial subjective measures (e.g., SART: Situational Awareness Rating Technique)[11, 8, 10]。虽然这些方法在受控研究环境中得到了很好的应用，但它们需要中断任务流程，无法捕获细粒度的 SA 波动，并且容易出现回忆、过度概括和反应性偏差. While these approaches are well-established in controlled research environments, they require interruptions to task flow, do not capture fine-grained SA fluctuations and are subject to recall, overgeneralization and reactivity biases[9]。因此，这些措施不适合实时HRI场景. Therefore, these measures are unsuitable for real-time HRI scenarios[1, 25].

HRI 中自动化和在线 SA 差距评估的进步从根本上受到缺乏公开共享、系统注释的数据集的限制。先前的研究主要通过基于探针的技术和间接性能指标探索了 SA 的定量代理，并且偶尔结合了信息丰富的眼动追踪或生理信号The advancement of automated and online SA gap assessment in HRI is fundamentally constrained by the absence of publicly shared, systematically annotated datasets. Prior studies have explored quantitative proxies of SA, predominantly through probe-based techniques and indirect performance metrics, and only occasionally incorporating informative eye-tracking or physiological signals[27, 4, 8, 1, 18, 24]。然而，这些数据集很少发布，并且通常除了原始研究团队之外仍然无法访问。因此，更广泛的研究界缺乏基准数据来系统地评估、比较和复制在线检测 SA 差距的方法。据我们所知，迄今为止，还没有公开可用的数据集能够对整个 HRT 任务中的连续 SA 差距检测进行严格评估。这种有限的透明度和可重复性限制了方法论的进展，阻碍了方法之间的公平比较，并减缓了新的 SA 评估技术的发现以及将有前途的方法转化为可操作的 HRT 系统。. However, these datasets are rarely released and typically remain inaccessible beyond the original research teams. Consequently, the broader research community lacks benchmark data to systematically evaluate, compare, and replicate methods for detecting SA gaps in an online manner. To the best of our knowledge, to date, no openly available dataset enables rigorous evaluation of continuous SA gap detection across an entire HRT mission. This limited transparency and reproducibility restrict methodological progress, impede fair comparisons across approaches, and slow both the discovery of new SA assessment techniques and the translation of promising methods into operational HRT systems.

HRT 中一个常见且对操作有影响的 SA 差距是 SA 延迟 (SAL)，即识别机器人系统需要协助或任务相关环境发生变化时的延迟One frequent and operationally consequential SA gap in HRT is SA Latency (SAL)—a delay in recognizing a robotic system’s need for assistance or changes in the mission-relevant environment[25]。实时检测 SAL 的能力可以有效帮助操作员在复杂的 HRT 任务中满足动态 SA 需求。之前的一项研究报告了某些眼动追踪功能与两种类型的 SAL（感知型和理解型）之间存在很强的相关性，但该研究仅涉及单个参与者，并且仅分析了 SAL 持续时间，而不是连续的任务时间线. The ability to detect SAL in real-time could meaningfully aid operators to meet dynamic SA demands during complex HRT missions. A previous study reported strong correlations between certain eye-tracking features and two types of SALs: perceptual and comprehending, but it involved only a single participant and analyzed only SAL durations rather than the continuous mission timeline[24]。据我们所知，目前不存在来自实际 HRT 场景的数据集并支持对 SAL 进行连续的、全任务的评估，并且之前的工作也没有进行过此类评估。. To our knowledge, no dataset from realistic HRT scenarios currently exists and supports continuous, mission-wide assessment of SAL, and no prior work has conducted such an evaluation.

为了开发 SA 评估的在线方法，本文提供了在现实的搜索和救援 HRT 实验中连续收集的多模式数据集，其中发生了各种 SAL 情况。具体来说，需要操作员协助的事件是根据地图位置系统嵌入的，但 SA 延迟的发生和时间是自发出现的，并且在参与者之间有所不同，具体取决于机器人到达每个位置、它们的方向和接近顺序以及操作员的响应能力。我们通过评估一组机器学习 (ML) 模型来说明该数据集的实用性，这些模型用于在整个实验过程中每 5 秒对感知 SAL (PSAL) 的存在和不存在进行分类。本文的主要贡献是：To enable the development of online approaches for SA assessment, this paper contributes a multimodal dataset collected continuously during a realistic search-and-rescue HRT experiment, where various SAL situations occur. Specifically, events requiring operator assistance were systematically embedded based on the map location, yet the occurrence and timing of SA latency emerged spontaneously and differed across participants, depending on the robots’ arrival at each location, their direction and order of approach and the operator’s responsiveness. We illustrate the utility of this dataset by evaluating a set of Machine Learning (ML) models for classifying the presence and absence of Perceptual SAL (PSAL) every 5 seconds throughout the experiment. II 相关工作Related Work

情境意识被定义为三个层次：感知（识别环境中的元素）、理解（理解其含义）和预测（预测其近期状态）Situational awareness has been defined using three levels: perception (recognizing elements in the environment), comprehension (understanding their meaning), and projection (anticipating their near future status)[10]。 HRI 中感知和理解 SA 需求的示例分别包括及时识别机器人错误或界面通知，以及检测非最佳机器人行为. Examples of perceptual and comprehending SA demands in HRI include timely recognition of robot errors or interface notifications, and detecting non-optimal robot behavior, respectively[24].

实时测量操作员 SA 的能力将使系统能够提醒操作员仅在需要时提供及时干预，从而减少警报疲劳并提高团队绩效The ability to measure operator SA in real-time would enable the system to alert the operator to provide timely interventions only when needed, reducing alert fatigue and improving team performance[25, 3]。它还将使机器人能够与人类有效地适应和协调。然而，人机交互 (HCI) 和 HRI 研究中常用的主观自我评估和探测技术并不适合实时应用. It would also allow robots to adapt and coordinate effectively with humans. However, subjective self-assessment and probing techniques commonly used in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and HRI research are unsuitable for real-time applications[25, 23]。这些措施通常需要冻结任务，这在现实世界的 HRI 场景中可能是不可能的，而且它们也可能容易产生偏差，特别是在长期任务后执行时。此外，它们无法以高时间分辨率捕获 SA；即，它们没有指定 SA 更改何时发生以及它们如何与特定任务相关。它们仅提供总体 SA 分数，通常是在执行任务后或在有限的时间点获得的。这些限制突出表明需要对运营商 SA 进行客观、持续的衡量。然而，关于 HRT 中客观在线 SA 评估的研究仍然很少，并且基本上不存在能够系统发现和验证此类措施的综合数据集，如下所述。. These measures often require freezing the task, which may not be possible in real-world HRI scenarios, and they may also be prone to bias, especially if administered after a long mission. In addition, they fail to capture SA at a high temporal resolution; i.e., they do not specify when SA changes occur and how they relate to specific tasks. They provide only an overall SA score, typically obtained after a mission or at limited time points. These limitations highlight the need for objective, continuous measures of operator SA. However, research on objective online SA assessment in HRT remains sparse, and comprehensive datasets enabling the systematic discovery and validation of such measures are largely nonexistent, as reviewed below.

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于情境数据的人类 SA 评估Contextual Data Based Human SA Assessments

从上下文数据导出的任务级或基于事件的绩效衡量标准通常用于评估操作员 SA。例如，任务完成时间的增加和每个任务的错误数量增多（例如，无人机与危险物相撞的事件以及操作任务期间的接触错误）可以作为 SA 较差的代理指标Task-level or event-based performance measures derived from contextual data are often used to assess operator SA. For example, increased task completion times and a higher number of mistakes per task (e.g., incidents of UAVs colliding with hazards and contact errors during manipulation tasks) can be proxy indicators of poor SA[18, 27]。尽管这些措施可能与 SA 相关，但它们也可能受到其他因素的影响；例如，机器人传感和数据集成. Although these measures may correlate with SA, they can also be influenced by other factors; e.g., robot sensing and data integration[25]。此外，对于任务持续时间不可预测、任务重叠和工作流程灵活的复杂动态 HRT 任务来说，计算这些测量值可能是不可行的。此外，其中许多措施取决于特定的机器人系统数据和上下文，这使得跨应用程序的泛化具有挑战性。. Furthermore, calculating these measures can be infeasible for complex and dynamic HRT missions with unpredictable task durations, overlapping tasks, and flexible workflows. Additionally, many of these measures depend on specific robotic system data and context, making generalization across applications challenging.

Ⅱ-BII-B 基于眼动追踪数据的人类 SA 评估Eye-tracking Data Based Human SA Assessments

眼动追踪数据提供操作员注意力分配、信息处理和认知负荷的非侵入式详细视图Eye-tracking data provide a non-intrusive, detailed view of operator attention allocation, information processing and cognitive load[19, 2]。眼球追踪可以实时连续进行，而不会中断任务流程。此外，从眼球追踪数据导出的指标可以与有关机器人、其环境和界面（例如，界面中感兴趣的区域、机器人姿势）的一般上下文数据集成，从而能够跨各种 HRT 应用进行 SA 估计。. Eye-tracking can be done continuously in real-time without disrupting task flow. Additionally, metrics derived from eye-tracking data can be integrated with general contextual data about robots, their environment and interface (e.g., areas of interest in an interface, robot pose), enabling SA estimation across various HRT applications.

为评估操作员 SA 提供信息的眼动追踪指标包括注视点（即眼睛聚焦的稳定位置）、注视持续时间、扫视模式（注视之间的快速眼球运动）和瞳孔直径Eye-tracking metrics that are informative for assessing operator SA include fixation points (i.e., stable locations where the eyes are focused), fixation duration, saccade patterns (rapid eye movements between fixations), and pupil diameter[31]。从这些指标得出的特征（例如，感兴趣区域（AOI）的平均注视持续时间或首次注视关键元素的延迟）提供了注意力分配和信息吸收的定量指标. Features derived from these metrics—e.g., mean fixation duration on an Area of Interest (AOI) or latency to first fixation on a critical element—provide quantitative indicators of attention allocation and information uptake[24]。当映射到任务相关事件或系统状态时，这些特征可以转化为 SA 的估计，特别是在感知和理解层面。. When mapped to mission-relevant events or system states, such features can be translated into estimates of SA, particularly at the perceptual and comprehension levels.

许多人机交互研究Many HCI studies[7, 28]和 HRI 研究，使用单个有限移动机器人and HRI studies with a single, limited mobility robot[4]依靠固定 AOI 来获取眼球追踪功能。虽然固定 AOI（例如通知面板周围）可用于检测某些感知 SA 差距，但它们很难转移到多机器人和动态 HRI 设置。只有少数研究检查了移动实体（例如飞机、机器人）的动态 AOI，以使用眼动追踪功能评估操作员 SArely on fixed AOIs for deriving eye-tracking features. While fixed AOIs—e.g., around notification panels—can be useful for detecting certain perceptual SA gaps, they transfer poorly to multi-robot and dynamic HRI settings. Only a few studies have examined dynamic AOIs for moving entities (e.g., aircrafts, robots) to evaluate operator SA using eye-tracking features[16, 24].

多项人机交互研究Several HCI studies[31]以及一些 HRI 研究（例如，and a few HRI studies (e.g.,[4, 8]）报告高和低 SA 水平之间眼动追踪特征的差异。然而，大多数都无法捕捉细粒度和精确的 SA 状态，因为它们的基本事实来自整体任务表现、研究后调查问卷或不频繁的探索) report differences in eye-tracking features between high- and low-SA levels. However, most do not capture fine-grained and precise SA states, as their ground truths are derived from overall task performance, post-study questionnaires, or infrequent probing[4, 12, 8, 7, 28].

很少有研究在 HRT 任务的特定干预事件中检验 SA，在这些事件中理解 SAL 很可能会出现。例如，在使用两辆无人地面车辆 (UGV) 进行的侦察任务模拟中，Ali 等人。Few studies have examined SA at specific intervention events in HRT missions, where comprehending SALs are likely to emerge. For example, in a reconnaissance mission simulation with two Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs), Ali et al.[1]根据干预点发生的帮助请求将 SA 分为高或低，准确率达到 74%。同样，拉特瓦尼等人。classified SA as high or low based on the occurrence of help requests at intervention points, achieving 74% accuracy. Similarly, Ratwani et al.[18]研究了五架无人机的监管，根据操作员是否及时采取行动防止危险碰撞对 SA 进行分类，报告 F1 分数为 62.79%（召回率=84.37%，精度=50%）。两者都使用具有眼动追踪和次要任务交互指标的逻辑回归模型。 Ratwani 等人的模型是针对特定环境的，不太适用于未知环境，因为它需要在地图上预先标记危险和目标。值得注意的是，这两项研究都分析了 SAL 事件之前的数据，而不是潜伏期间的数据，这对于实际对 SAL 的存在进行分类至关重要。此外，两者都使用 10 倍交叉验证，由于训练集和测试集之间来自同一参与者的数据点重叠，这可能会高估性能studied the supervision of five UAVs, categorizing SA based on whether or not the operator acted in time to prevent hazard collisions, reporting an F1 score of 62.79% (recall=84.37%, precision=50%). Ⅱ-CII-C 数据可用性Data Availability

迄今为止，上述审查的研究均未提供公开可用的数据集，其中系统注释了 HRI（例如 SAL）中的 SA 或 SA 差距。现有的 HRI 数据集是出于其他目的而收集的，缺乏连续或特定事件 SA 状态的地面真实标签。因此，研究人员无法对 HRT 中的在线 SA 评估方法进行基准测试、复制或系统评估。To date, none of the above reviewed studies provide publicly available datasets with systematic annotations of SA or SA gaps in HRI (e.g., SAL). Existing HRI datasets have been collected for other purposes and lack ground truth labels for continuous or event-specific SA states. Consequently, researchers cannot benchmark, replicate, or systematically evaluate methods for online SA assessment in HRT.

V 讨论Discussion

我们提出了 HRI-SA，这是一个多模式数据集，用于调查远程 HRT 期间的态势感知 (SA) 差距。除了眼球追踪和生理数据外，它还提供感知和理解 SA 延迟 (SAL) 的真实注释。据我们所知，这是第一个提供关于 SA 的真实任务范围数据的数据集，涵盖需要和不需要人工干预的时间点。它还包括过度意识、混乱（即不完全意识）、压力、无聊和认知负荷的主观报告，从而能够调查 SAL 之外的 SA 差距和用户状态We presented HRI-SA, a multimodal dataset for investigating Situational Awareness (SA) gaps during remote HRT. Along with eye-tracking and physiological data, it provides ground truth annotations of perceptual and comprehending SA Latencies (SALs). To the best of our knowledge, this is the first dataset offering realistic mission-wide data on SA, covering time points where human intervention is needed as well as not. It also includes subjective reports of excessive awareness, confusion (i.e., incomplete awareness), stress, boredom, and cognitive load, enabling investigation of SA gaps and user states beyond SAL[25].

然而，这个数据集有局限性。它是在模拟任务期间收集的，由于风险较低，操作员的警觉性可能低于实际任务。此外，模拟环境比真实世界的设置更简单。因此，眼动追踪数据可能无法完全代表现实世界的 HRT。该数据集还提供了基本事实或可用于导出感知 SAL (PSAL) 和理解 SAL (CSAL) 的基本事实，仅适用于特定任务（但可转移到其他上下文），并且不考虑预测性 SAL（即预测机器人近期需求的延迟），如果不深入研究操作员的思维过程，很难捕获预测性 SAL。However, this dataset has limitations. It was collected during simulated missions, where operators may exhibit lower alertness than in physical missions, due to lower risks. Also, simulated environments are less complex than real-world settings. Thus, the eye-tracking data may not fully represent real-world HRT. The dataset also provides ground truths or data useful for deriving ground truths for Perceptual SAL (PSAL) and Comprehending SAL (CSAL) only for specific tasks (but transferrable to other contexts) and does not account for predictive SALs (i.e., delays in predicting the robot’s near-future needs), which are challenging to capture without delving into the operators’ thought processes.

除了主要数据集的贡献之外，我们还通过初步的机器学习实验来说明其实用性，该实验调查了眼动追踪和上下文特征对 PSAL 分类的功效。与单独的上下文特征 (F1=71.65%) 相比，通用眼动追踪功能在对操作员是否正在经历 PSAL 进行分类方面取得了中等性能 (F1=67.63%)。组合特征集显着提高了分类性能（F1=80.38%）。这些有希望的结果证明了 HRI-SA 数据集有潜力支持在线技术的开发，以自动监测远程 HRT 中操作员的 PSAL。In addition to the primary dataset contribution, we illustrate its utility through a preliminary ML experiment investigating the efficacy of eye-tracking and contextual features for PSAL classification. Generic eye-tracking features achieved moderate performance (F1=67.63%) in classifying whether or not an operator is experiencing a PSAL, comparable to contextual features alone (F1=71.65%). Combining feature sets significantly improved classification performance (F1=80.38%). These promising results demonstrate the HRI-SA dataset’s potential to support the development of online techniques for the automatic monitoring of operators’ PSALs in remote HRT.

我们观察到单峰 PSAL 分类（即仅使用眼动追踪或仅使用上下文特征）的误报率很高。之前针对需要干预的事件的研究也注意到了类似的观察结果We observed high false positive rates in unimodal PSAL classification (i.e., using eye-tracking only or contextual only features). Fusing eye movement and contextual features improved precision by 8.6-28.4%, yet additional improvements are still necessary, with current precision at 72.89%. Feature-importance analysis and sample RF decision trees we visualized (with F8 near the root) indicate that contextual features contributed most to PSAL detection, while eye-tracking features played an important role in reducing false positives. To improve detection performance, future work could explore additional features. Examples include eye movement features focused on individual notifications, contextual features such as the time a request was first sent to the interface, operator expertise, and the number of robots involved in the mission.

LOGO holds out all data from one participant group in each fold, ensuring model evaluation on unseen participants[30]。 K 折交叉验证可以混合来自同一参与者的训练和测试集数据，可能会高估模型性能. K-fold cross-validation can mix data from the same participants across training and test sets, potentially overestimating model performance[15]。因此，我们的研究结果提供了对性能的现实估计。然而，由于数据限制，我们没有使用单独的保留测试集。. Our findings thus provide a realistic estimate of performance. However, we did not use a separate hold-out test set due to data limitations.

VI 结论Conclusion

为了实现在线 SA 评估，本文引入了 HRI-SA，这是一种在真实模拟 HRT 环境中的新型多模态数据集。它作为 SA 计算分析的基准，有利于 HRT 研究。此外，我们的工作是第一个在整个动态 HRT 任务中评估感知 SA 延迟 (PSAL) 的工作。机器学习实验证明了使用通用眼动追踪特征结合有限的上下文特征来检测（召回率=88.91%，F1=67.63%）PSAL的可行性。集成眼球追踪功能对于减少与纯粹上下文检测相关的高误报至关重要。使用 HRI-SA 数据集的未来评估可以进一步探索在线 SA 差距评估的潜力，以实现 SA 感知的用户自适应机器人行为和支持维护操作员 SA 的监控界面。To enable online SA assessment, this paper introduced HRI-SA, a novel multimodal dataset in a realistic simulated HRT context. It serves as a benchmark for computational analysis of SA, benefiting HRT research. Additionally, our work is the first to evaluate Perceptual SA Latency (PSAL) throughout a dynamic HRT mission. Machine learning experiments demonstrate the feasibility of detecting (recall=88.91%, F1=67.63%) PSAL using generic eye-tracking features combined with limited contextual features. Integrating eye-tracking features was crucial to reduce high false positives associated with purely contextual detection. Future evaluations using the HRI-SA dataset could further explore the potential for online SA gap assessment to enable SA-aware user-adaptive robot behaviors and supervisory interfaces that support maintaining operator SA.
