1. 简介Introduction

研究界越来越多地采用综合表格数据生成Synthetic tabular data generation is increasingly adopted by the research community(Hu2024; jordon2022synthetic; liu2024tabular), 监管机构, regulators(fsa; cnil)和工业, and industry(tumult_labs; ydata)作为一种有前途的方法，可以促进数据共享和下游分析，同时降低披露风险。 这些方法旨在近似真实数据集的经验分布并生成保留关键统计特性的人工记录。 然而，合成数据默认并不能保证隐私或公平。 一方面，如果没有正式的保护，生成模型可能会记住或泄漏训练数据中的敏感信息，从而使其容易受到成员资格推断、属性推断或重建攻击as a promising approach to facilitate data sharing and downstream analysis while reducing disclosure risks. These methods aim to approximate the empirical distribution of real datasets and generate artificial records that preserve key statistical properties. However, synthetic data does not guarantee privacy or fairness by default. On the one hand, without formal protections, generative models may memorize or leak sensitive information from the training data, making them vulnerable to membership inference, attribute inference, or reconstruction attacks(Ganev2025; yao2025dcr; Stadler2022; giomi2023unified)。 此外，合成数据可以复制甚至放大数据中存在的社会偏见，导致下游预测模型产生不公平的结果. 为了评估公平意识学习机制对 DP 合成数据是否仍然有效，我们考虑了三个主要干预类别的代表性方法：To evaluate whether fairness-aware learning mechanisms remain effective on DP synthetic data, we consider representative methods from the three main categories of interventions:预处理pre-processing, 处理中in-processing, 和, and后处理post-processing。 这些方法已在公平性文献中得到广泛研究，并在 AIF360 等开源库中实现. These methods have been widely studied in the fairness literature and are implemented in open-source libraries such as AIF360(fair:AIF360)，使它们成为我们研究的合适基准。, making them suitable benchmarks for our study.

我们故意依赖基于 AIF360 的方法，因为它们在管道的三个主要阶段提供了标准化、广泛使用的规范公平干预措施的实现。 这种选择使我们能够在通用实验方案下比较干预阶段，同时避免由于异构实现、不兼容的假设或特定于方法的工程选择而导致的额外混杂因素。 因此，我们的目标不是耗尽公平算法的全部空间，而是评估具有代表性和可重复性的机制，使阶段级比较有意义。We deliberately rely on AIF360-based methods because they provide standardized, widely used implementations of canonical fairness interventions across the three main stages of the pipeline. This choice allows us to compare intervention stages under a common experimental protocol, while avoiding additional confounding factors due to heterogeneous implementations, incompatible assumptions, or method-specific engineering choices. Our goal is therefore not to exhaust the full space of fairness algorithms, but to evaluate representative and reproducible mechanisms that make the stage-level comparison meaningful.

预处理Pre-Processing

这些方法在模型学习之前修改训练数据，以减少对受保护属性的依赖。These approaches modify the training data before model learning to reduce dependence on the protected attribute.

    重新称重 (RW)Reweighing (RW)(fair:Reweighing-paper).RW 依靠对输入样本进行重采样和计算权重来减少歧视。RW relies on resampling and computing weights for the input samples to decrease discrimination.

  • •

    异质冲击消除器 (DIR)Disparate Impact Remover (DIR)(fair:DIR).该方法对原始数据集进行转换，以减少特权子组和非特权子组之间的不同影响。它首先检测是否存在不同的影响，然后消除未受保护的特征对受保护特征的依赖，最后调整未受保护的特征的分布，使得特权组和非特权组具有相似的分布。This method transforms the original dataset to reduce disparate impact between privileged and unprivileged subgroups. It first detects whether disparate impact exists, then removes the dependence of unprotected features on the protected feature, and finally adjusts the distributions of unprotected features so that both privileged and unprivileged groups have similar distributions.

  • •

The objective is to approximate statistical parity across groups while balancing fairness with the predictive utility of the data.

这些方法通常通过解决平衡准确性和公平性的约束优化问题来修改训练过程本身。These methods modify the training procedure itself, typically by solving constrained optimization problems that balance accuracy with fairness.

Essentially, it builds a grid of Lagrangian multipliers and exhaustively searches for the best solution considering the fairness-accuracy trade-off.

这些方法对训练模型的输出进行操作，调整预测以满足公平性约束。These methods operate on the outputs of a trained model, adjusting predictions to satisfy fairness constraints.

影响Implications

我们的研究结果更广泛的含义是，公平干预措施并不能一律从非私人机构转移到 DP 机构。 虽然后处理仍然可靠，但在 DP 引起的分布变化下，预处理和处理中方法更加脆弱。 此外，效用-公平权衡与合成器密切相关：对 AIM 有效的机制在 MST 下可能效果有限。 对于从业者来说，这提出了两条指导方针。 首先，当效用保留是首要任务时，将 AIM 等高效用 DP 合成器与后处理干预措施（特别是 ROC 或 EqOdds）相结合会产生最有利的权衡。 其次，当执行更强的奇偶约束至关重要，并且一些效用损失是可以接受的时，可以考虑预处理或处理中方法，例如 RW 或 EGR，前提是仔细评估其成本和稳定性限制。The broader implication of our findings is that fairness interventions are not uniformly transferable from non-private to DP settings. While post-processing remains reliable, pre- and in-processing methods are more fragile under DP-induced distributional shifts. Moreover, the utility–fairness trade-off interacts strongly with the synthesizer: mechanisms that are effective with AIM may have a limited effect under MST. For practitioners, this suggests two guidelines. First, when utility preservation is a priority, combining a high-utility DP synthesizer such as AIM with post-processing interventions (in particular ROC or EqOdds) yields the most favorable trade-offs. Second, when enforcing stronger parity constraints is critical, and some utility loss is acceptable, pre- or in-processing methods such as RW or EGR may be considered, provided their costs and stability limitations are carefully evaluated.
