6 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 ADC-GNN，这是一种注意力引导的扩散对比图神经网络，用于标签稀缺和类别不平衡情况下的少样本图欺诈检测。 ADC-GNN 结合了特征空间扩散引导增强、对比表示学习和关系感知多跳谱注意来改进异常敏感的节点表示。 三个公共基准的实验提供了 ADC-GNN 在稀疏监督下有效的主要可重复证据；专有的 CM 数据集被保留作为私人案例研究。其他实验进一步检查分割稳定性、训练比行为、过采样替代方案、灵敏度和效率。This paper presents ADC-GNN, an attention-guided diffusion-contrastive graph neural network for few-shot graph fraud detection under label scarcity and class imbalance. ADC-GNN combines feature-space diffusion-guided augmentation, contrastive representation learning, and relation-aware multi-hop spectral attention to improve anomaly-sensitive node representations. Experiments on three public benchmarks provide the main reproducible evidence that ADC-GNN is effective under sparse supervision; the proprietary CM dataset is retained as a private case study. Additional experiments further examine split stability, training-ratio behavior, oversampling alternatives, sensitivity, and efficiency.

尽管取得了这些结果，ADC-GNN 仍存在一些局限性。 首先，扩散模块是一种特征空间去噪增强机制，而不是一个全图生成扩散模型；它不会显式生成或重建图拓扑。 因此，当欺诈模式主要由边缘重新布线而不是节点特征偏差主导时，拓扑感知图扩散或结构增强可能是必要的。 其次，虽然所提出的方法是针对低标签和不平衡设置而设计的，但当有丰富的高质量标签可用时或者当更简单的不平衡感知 GNN 已经可以分离欺诈节点时，其优势可能会降低。 第三，尽管我们提供了模式级详细信息和分割协议来提高可重复性，但由于隐私限制，专有的 CM 数据集无法完全发布。 未来的工作将探索合成或部分匿名图形统计的隐私保护发布、拓扑感知扩散机制以及用于实时交易筛选的更有效的部署策略。Despite these results, ADC-GNN has several limitations. First, the diffusion module is a feature-space denoising augmentation mechanism rather than a full graph generative diffusion model; it does not explicitly generate or reconstruct graph topology. Therefore, when fraud patterns are dominated by edge rewiring rather than node-feature deviations, topology-aware graph diffusion or structural augmentation may be necessary. Second, although the proposed method is designed for low-label and imbalanced settings, its advantage may decrease when abundant high-quality labels are available or when simpler imbalance-aware GNNs can already separate fraudulent nodes. Third, the proprietary CM dataset cannot be fully released due to privacy constraints, although we provide schema-level details and split protocols to improve reproducibility. Future work will explore privacy-preserving release of synthetic or partially anonymized graph statistics, topology-aware diffusion mechanisms, and more efficient deployment strategies for real-time transaction screening.
