摘要Abstract

深度研究 (DR) 已成为一种新的代理范式，用于解决复杂的、开放式的研究任务，要求系统能够迭代地构建问题、获取证据、验证来源并合成长篇报告。 然而，在实践中，当前的灾难恢复系统受到四个相互关联的限制：未指定范围内的长期规划、在单个代理内分解和调度此类任务的瓶颈、长格式合成中的幻觉风险以及有限的过程可审计性。本技术报告介绍了Deep Research (DR) has emerged as a new agentic paradigm to tackle complex, open-ended research tasks, demanding systems that can iteratively frame problems, acquire evidence, verify sources, and synthesize long-form reports. In practice, however, current DR systems are constrained by four interrelated limitations: long-horizon planning over an underspecified scope, the bottleneck of decomposing and scheduling such tasks within a single agent, hallucination risk in long-form synthesis, and limited process auditability. This technical report presents杜马特深度研究DuMate-DeepResearch，一个基于Qianfan Agent Foundry构建的多Agent灾难恢复框架。该框架将代理核心（处理任务理解、规划和调度）与可扩展的工具生态系统解耦，用于检索、证据获取和报告呈现，使每个中间决策和工具调用都可明确追踪。在此基础设施的基础上，DuMate-DeepResearch 进一步引入了三种机制：(i), a multi-agent DR framework built on the Qianfan Agent Foundry. The framework decouples the Agent Core—which handles task understanding, planning, and scheduling—from an extensible Tool Ecosystem for retrieval, evidence acquisition, and report rendering, making every intermediate decision and tool invocation explicitly traceable. Building on this infrastructure, DuMate-DeepResearch further introduces three mechanisms: (i) a基于图的动态规划graph-based dynamic planning策略将研究路线图由粗到细扩展，并通过反思、重新规划、回溯和并行分支不断修正； (二)strategy expands the research roadmap coarse-to-fine and continuously revises it through reflection, re-planning, backtracking, and parallel branching; (ii) a递归两级执行recursive two-level execution设计将每个复杂的搜索子任务委托给一个内部搜索代理，该代理运行自己的规划循环，隔离嘈杂的检索并稳定长期执行； (三)design delegates each complex search sub-task to an inner Search Agent that runs its own planning loop, isolating noisy retrieval and stabilizing long-horizon execution; (iii) a基于评分标准的测试时间优化rubric-based test-time optimization机制动态生成特定于任务的质量标准，并将其用作基于证据的合成和自适应停止的实时推理支架。 在两项深度研究基准测试中，DuMate-DeepResearch 建立了新的最先进结果：在 DeepResearch Bench 上获得最佳总分（58.03%），在 DeepResearch Bench II 上获得最佳总分（61.95%），同时在信息召回和分析方面排名第一。这些结果证明了将可审计的多代理基础设施与自适应规划和标题引导推理相结合以实现高质量深度研究的价值。mechanism dynamically generates task-specific quality criteria and uses them as live reasoning scaffolds for evidence-grounded synthesis and adaptive stopping. Across two deep research benchmarks, DuMate-DeepResearch establishes new state-of-the-art results: the best overall score (58.03%) on DeepResearch Bench, and the best overall score (61.95%) on DeepResearch Bench II while ranking first in information recall and analysis. 1 简介Introduction

人工智能的快速发展促进了从被动、单轮问答系统到自主、代理系统的范式转变The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence has catalyzed a paradigm shift from passive, single-turn question-answering systems to autonomous, agentic systems(Yao et al., 2023b; Wang et al., 2024)，使用户能够从研究问题启动复杂的研究工作流程。在此背景下，, enabling users to initiate complex research workflows from a research question. In this context,深度研究（DR）Deep Research (DR) (Zheng et al., 2025; Shi et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025; Du et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025)已经成为弥合人类探究和系统知识发现之间差距的一个至关重要且极具挑战性的前沿领域。 虽然传统的检索增强工作流程仅限于 对静态语料库的单次或基于规则的检索has emerged as a crucial and highly challenging frontier to bridge the gap between human inquiry and systematic knowledge discovery. While traditional retrieval-augmented workflows are confined to single-shot or rule-based retrieval over static corpora(Lewis et al., 2020; Gao et al., 2023)，DR 旨在复制人类研究人员严格、系统的研究方法。为了解决复杂的、开放性的问题，灾难恢复需要复杂的长期推理、战略决策和大规模信息综合, DR aims to replicate the rigorous, systematic investigative methodologies of human researchers. To address complex, open-ended problems, DR requires sophisticated long-horizon reasoning, strategic decision-making, and large-scale information synthesis(Shinn et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023a).

Early systems adopted整体架构monolithic architectures（例如 OpenAI 的 DeepResearch），它将所有模块紧密集成在一个中央推理引擎周围，确保统一的控制流，但限制了可扩展性和工具的可扩展性。或者，(e.g., OpenAI’s DeepResearch), which tightly integrate all modules around a central reasoning engine, ensuring unified control flow but limiting scalability and tool extensibility. Alternatively,管道架构pipeline architectures（例如，n8n 工作流程）将流程分解为顺序连接的阶段，促进组件重用，但难以应对复杂的迭代和全局反馈。作为回应，(e.g., n8n workflows) decompose the process into sequentially connected stages, facilitating component reuse but struggling with complex iteration and global feedback. In response,代理架构agentic architectures已成为灾难恢复系统的自然方向。通过分解总体研究任务并将其分配给具有专门角色的自主代理，这种协作范式提高了复杂研究场景的可扩展性、并行效率和功能专业化。have become a natural direction for DR systems. By decomposing overarching research tasks and distributing them among autonomous agents with specialized roles, this collaborative paradigm improves scalability, parallel efficiency, and functional specialization for complex research scenarios.

Given a complex, open-ended research question, such a system transforms the high-level request into a comprehensive report through a set of tightly coupled capabilities that typically include, but are not limited to, the following:

  1. 1.

    问题框架和适应性规划：Problem Framing and Adaptive Planning:该系统将未明确的研究问题解析为结构化目标，并制定动态研究路线图，随着证据的积累，通过子目标细化、查询重新制定和从信息死胡同回溯，不断修改其策略。The system parses an underspecified research question into structured objectives and formulates a dynamic research roadmap, continuously revising its strategy as evidence accrues through sub-goal refinement, query reformulation, and backtracking from informational dead-ends.

  2. 2.

    证据获取和验证：Evidence Acquisition and Verification:在此路线图的驱动下，系统调用异构工具包（例如网络搜索引擎、学术数据库、特定领域的 API）来获取信息，同时评估来源可信度并跨来源交叉验证声明，以维护事实完整性。Driven by this roadmap, the system invokes a heterogeneous toolkit (e.g., web search engines, scholarly databases, domain-specific APIs) to acquire information, while assessing source credibility and cross-validating claims across sources to safeguard factual integrity.

  3. 3.

    综合和报告生成：Synthesis and Report Generation:经过验证的证据最终被整合成一份有凝聚力、逻辑结构的报告，该报告将多源发现编织成一个连贯的叙述，并进行细致入微的分析和可验证的引用。The validated evidence is finally integrated into a cohesive, logically structured report that weaves multi-source findings into a coherent narrative with nuanced analysis and verifiable citations.

主要挑战The Key Challenges

然而，在实践中实现这种理想化的工作流程还远未解决。当前的代理灾难恢复系统仍然面临着限制其实际部署可靠性的开放挑战：However, realizing this idealized workflow in practice remains far from solved. Current agentic DR systems still confront open challenges that limit their reliability for real-world deployment:

  • •

    长期规划和动态范围定义：Long-Horizon Planning and Dynamic Scope Definition:一个研究问题会展开为由数十个相互依赖的子问题组成的长期视野，这些子问题的范围在一开始就没有明确规定，只有随着证据的积累才会具体化。致力于单个下一步行动的反应式、分步策略（如 ReAct 风格的智能体）本质上是短视的：它们在没有轨迹的全局表示的情况下进行局部优化，在无限探索和过早收敛之间摇摆，并且当工具失败或新检索到的证据使先前的前提无效时，无法连贯地修改其策略。因此，有效的灾难恢复需要一种规划形式，维护整个路线图的全局、有远见的模型，并随着信息状态的发展不断重新划定范围和重新规划。A research question unfolds into a long horizon of dozens of interdependent sub-questions whose scope is underspecified at the outset and only crystallizes as evidence accrues. Reactive, step-by-step policies that commit to a single next action—as in ReAct-style agents—are inherently myopic: they optimize locally without a global representation of the trajectory, oscillate between unbounded exploration and premature convergence, and cannot coherently revise their strategy when a tool fails or newly retrieved evidence invalidates an earlier premise. Effective DR therefore demands a planning formalism that maintains a global, far-sighted model of the entire roadmap and continuously re-delineates scope and re-plans as the information state evolves.

  • •

    复杂任务分解与调度：Complex Task Decomposition and Scheduling:即使有一个合理的计划，分解并安排其执行也是长期轨迹最容易失败的地方。单个平面代理很少能够将高级任务分解与每个子任务依次要求的更精细的子任务分解、调度和噪声处理相协调，因为每个子问题本身可能需要在充满死链接、API 故障和不相关或矛盾的返回的随机网络上进行许多多步骤检索操作。将全局策略和低级检索合并到一个策略中会使两者纠缠在一起，并使单个局部故障传播并级联到全局轨迹中。因此，可靠的灾难恢复需要一种执行方案，将高级分解和调度与局部子任务完成分开，将噪声和错误限制在子任务边界内，并在不破坏整个过程稳定性的情况下稳健地执行每个子任务。Even given a sound plan, decomposing and scheduling it for execution is where long trajectories most often break down. A single flat agent can rarely reconcile high-level task decomposition with the finer sub-task decomposition, scheduling, and noise handling that each sub-task in turn demands, since every sub-question may itself entail many multi-step retrieval actions over a stochastic web rife with dead links, API failures, and irrelevant or contradictory returns. Folding global strategy and low-level retrieval into one policy entangles the two and lets a single local failure propagate and cascade into the global trajectory. Reliable DR thus requires an execution scheme that separates high-level decomposition and scheduling from local sub-task completion, confines noise and errors within sub-task boundaries, and robustly carries out each sub-task without destabilizing the overall process.

  • •

    幻觉缓解和事实基础：Hallucination Mitigation and Factual Grounding:在动态、多源证据流的长格式综合过程中维持严格的事实保真度是众所周知的困难，并且代理还必须拥有一个原则性标准，以确定何时积累的证据足以停止探索。这就需要严格的推理时间支架，在生成时根据可验证的证据来校准每个显着的断言，并在证据明显足够时准确地终止检索，而不是依赖事后验证或固定的探索预算。Sustaining strict factual fidelity during long-form synthesis over dynamic, multi-source evidence streams is notoriously difficult, and the agent must additionally possess a principled criterion for when accumulated evidence is sufficient to halt exploration. This calls for rigorous inference-time scaffolds that calibrate every salient assertion against verifiable evidence as it is generated, and that terminate retrieval precisely when—and only when—the evidence demonstrably suffices, rather than relying on post-hoc verification or fixed exploration budgets.

  • •

    流程可解释性和可审计性：Process Explainability and Auditability:为了让灾难恢复在高风险领域得到信任，其自主推理必须变得可检查。系统应该将其决策跟踪、工具调用和操作路径外部化为明确的、可审计的工件——就像严格研究的方法附录一样透明——以便用户不仅可以仔细审查最终报告，还可以审查其生成的过程。For DR to be trusted in high-stakes domains, its autonomous reasoning must be rendered inspectable. Systems should externalize their decision traces, tool invocations, and action paths as explicit, auditable artifacts—as transparent as the methodology appendix of a rigorous study—so that users can scrutinize not only the final report but the very process by which it was produced.

4 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

4.1 检索增强生成和代理搜索Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Agentic Search

在深度研究系统出现之前，将法学硕士与外部知识联系起来的主导范式是检索增强生成（RAG），其中系统检索一小组相关段落并根据它们条件生成器以产生简洁的答案。早期的 RAG 式系统表明，非参数检索可以显着改善知识密集型生成Before deep research systems, the dominant paradigm for connecting LLMs with external knowledge was retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), where a system retrieves a small set of relevant passages and conditions the generator on them to produce a concise answer. Early RAG-style systems showed that non-parametric retrieval can substantially improve knowledge-intensive generation(Lewis et al., 2020)，后来的工作进一步将检索集成到语言模型预训练和小样本学习中, and later work further integrated retrieval into language model pre-training and few-shot learning(Guu et al., 2020; Borgeaud et al., 2022; Izacard et al., 2023)。在这些系统中，搜索组件通常针对简答题进行优化：检索证据，选择性地重新排序或过滤它，并生成基于检索到的上下文的答案。检索器本身是从词汇检索（例如 BM25）演变而来的. In these systems, the search component is usually optimized for short-answer question answering: retrieve evidence, optionally rerank or filter it, and generate an answer grounded in the retrieved context. The retriever itself has evolved from lexical retrieval such as BM25(Robertson and Zaragoza, 2009)密集段落检索to dense passage retrieval(Karpukhin et al., 2020)，而更广泛的 RAG 调查将这条线总结为缓解法学硕士静态知识限制的标准方法, while broader RAG surveys summarize this line as a standard way to mitigate the static-knowledge limitation of LLMs(Gao et al., 2023).

传统 RAG 的一个主要限制是检索质量在很大程度上取决于输入查询。为了解决这个问题，许多系统引入了基于 LLM 的查询重写、分解或检索前规划A central limitation of conventional RAG is that retrieval quality depends heavily on the input query. To address this, many systems introduce LLM-based query rewriting, decomposition, or planning before retrieval(Li et al., 2025c; Chen et al., 2025a; Li et al., 2026c)。 Rewrite-Retrieve-Read 通过强化学习训练查询重写器，以便重写的查询提高下游答案的准确性. Rewrite-Retrieve-Read trains a query rewriter with reinforcement learning so that the rewritten query improves downstream answer accuracy(Ma et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2026)。后续工作通过使用更丰富的奖励信号或多智能体训练来优化面向检索的规划，例如 DeepRetrieval 和多智能体 RAG 优化，从而扩展了这一想法. Subsequent work extends this idea by optimizing retrieval-oriented planning with richer reward signals or multi-agent training, such as DeepRetrieval and multi-agent RAG optimization(Jiang et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025b)。除了一次性重写之外，迭代系统还将复杂的问题分解为多个相关子查询。当当前证据验证失败时，LLatrieval重复生成补充查询. Beyond one-shot rewriting, iterative systems decompose complex questions into multiple dependent sub-queries. LLatrieval repeatedly generates supplementary queries when current evidence fails verification(Li et al., 2023)，而 DRAGIN 使用模型的生成状态动态地重新构造检索查询, while DRAGIN uses the model’s generation state to dynamically reformulate retrieval queries(Su et al., 2024)。基于树或图的方法进一步扩展了搜索空间：RAG-Star 在深思熟虑的推理路径上使用检索增强验证和细化. Tree- or graph-based methods further expand the search space: RAG-Star uses retrieval-augmented verification and refinement over deliberative reasoning paths(Jiang et al., 2024)，DeepRAG逐步决定是依赖参数知识还是依赖检索, DeepRAG decides step by step whether to rely on parametric knowledge or retrieval(Guan et al., 2025)，MAO-ARAG通过多代理自适应RAG框架协调多个检索模块, and MAO-ARAG orchestrates multiple retrieval modules through a multi-agent adaptive RAG framework(Chen et al., 2025c).

另一条工作重点是Another line of work focuses on当whenLLM 应该进行搜索。固定检索可能效率低下，并且可能会引入不相关或误导性的证据，因此自适应检索方法让模型决定是否需要额外的证据。 IR-CoT 将检索与多步骤问题的思维链推理交织在一起LLMs should search. Fixed retrieval can be inefficient and may introduce irrelevant or misleading evidence, so adaptive retrieval methods let the model decide whether additional evidence is needed. IR-CoT interleaves retrieval with chain-of-thought reasoning for multi-step questions(Trivedi et al., 2022)，而 FLARE 根据生成过程中的不确定性触发检索, while FLARE triggers retrieval based on uncertainty during generation(Jiang et al., 2023)。 Self-RAG 训练模型通过自我反思标记来检索、生成和批评其输出. Self-RAG trains models to retrieve, generate, and critique their outputs through self-reflection tokens(Asai et al., 2024)。其他自适应方法通过模型置信度、内部状态或一致性来估计检索必要性，包括 DRAGIN、Rowen 和 SEAKR. Other adaptive methods estimate retrieval necessity through model confidence, internal states, or consistency, including DRAGIN, Rowen, and SEAKR(Su et al., 2024; Ding et al., 2024; Yao et al., 2024)。这个方向自然地与使用工具的代理联系在一起：ReAct 将搜索框架视为与推理交织的动作. This direction connects naturally to tool-using agents: ReAct frames search as an action interleaved with reasoning(Yao et al., 2023b)、Search-o1引入大型推理模型的代理搜索, Search-o1 introduces agentic search for large reasoning models(Li et al., 2025a)，和 Search-R1/R1-Searcher 通过强化学习优化搜索时间和搜索内容, and Search-R1/R1-Searcher optimize when and what to search through reinforcement learning(Jin et al., 2025; Song et al., 2025).

总体而言，LLM 增强搜索和代理 RAG 构成了深度研究的简答基础。他们通过检索、查询规划、自适应搜索计时和工具增强推理来改进证据获取。然而，他们的主要目标通常仍然是本地化答案准确性或多跳问答效率。深入研究将这一基础从简短的、基于证据的答案扩展到长篇、报告级的综合，需要更广泛的工具编排、持久内存、全局规划、源校准和结构化报告生成。Overall, LLM-augmented search and agentic RAG form the short-answer foundation of deep research. They improve evidence acquisition through retrieval, query planning, adaptive search timing, and tool-augmented reasoning. However, their primary objective is still usually localized answer accuracy or multi-hop question answering efficiency. Deep research extends this foundation from short, evidence-grounded answers to long-form, report-level synthesis, requiring broader tool orchestration, persistent memory, global planning, source calibration, and structured report generation.

4.2 深入研究Deep Research

超越简答 RAG 和代理搜索，最近的深度研究系统旨在为复杂和开放式的用户查询生成长篇的、基于证据的报告。与传统的RAG系统相比，它们通常需要更广泛的信息探索、更长期的规划、迭代反思、源级验证和结构化报告编写。因此，核心挑战从检索足够的证据来提供本地化答案，转变为协调可以跨多个步骤获取、组织和综合信息的端到端研究工作流程。Moving beyond short-answer RAG and agentic search, recent deep research systems aim to generate long-form, evidence-grounded reports for complex and open-ended user queries. Compared with conventional RAG systems, they usually require broader information exploration, longer-horizon planning, iterative reflection, source-level verification, and structured report writing. Therefore, the core challenge shifts from retrieving sufficient evidence for a localized answer to coordinating an end-to-end research workflow that can acquire, organize, and synthesize information across multiple steps.

DR-Tulu(Shao et al., 2025)通过引入不断发展的评估标准，解决了静态评估指标在优化开放式和长篇深度研究任务中的缺点。评分细则为强化学习提供可衡量的奖励信号，并动态适应策略模型的行为。 TTD-DRaddresses the drawback of static evaluation metrics in optimizing open-ended and long-form deep research tasks by introducing evolving rubrics. 总体而言，现有的深度研究系统突出了几个互补的方向：扩展工具增强的探索、分离问题解决和报告生成模式、使用标题和验证器作为优化信号、通过迭代细化改进测试时的编写、综合特定于能力的训练数据、开源可重复的训练管道以及引入外部工作空间作为持久存储器。这些研究表明，深度研究不仅仅是 RAG 的较长版本，而且是一个更广泛的代理工作流程，将搜索、规划、验证、记忆和长格式综合结合起来。Overall, existing deep research systems highlight several complementary directions: scaling tool-augmented exploration, separating problem-solving and report-generation modes, using rubrics and verifiers as optimization signals, improving test-time writing through iterative refinement, synthesizing capability-specific training data, open-sourcing reproducible training pipelines, and introducing external workspaces as persistent memory. These studies demonstrate that deep research is not merely a longer version of RAG, but a broader agentic workflow that couples search, planning, verification, memory, and long-form synthesis.

5 结论Conclusions

在这份技术报告中，我们介绍了In this technical report, we presented杜马特深度研究DuMate-DeepResearch，一个基于Qianfan Agent Foundry构建的多智能体深度研究框架。通过将处理任务理解、规划和调度的代理核心与用于检索、证据获取和报告呈现的可扩展工具生态系统解耦，该框架将每个规划决策和工具调用作为可检查的工件公开，直接解决代理深度研究的透明度和可审计性挑战。在此基础设施之上，我们引入了三种针对任务的开放挑战量身定制的认知机制：基于图的动态规划器，支持从粗到细的探索、反射、重新规划、回溯和并行分支，以进行有远见的长期研究；递归两级执行设计，将每个复杂的搜索子任务委托给运行自己的规划循环的内部搜索代理，隔离噪声检索，使全局轨迹保持稳定；基于标题的测试时间优化机制，可动态生成特定于任务的质量标准，并将其用作基于证据的合成和自适应停止的实时推理支架。, a multi-agent deep research framework built on the Qianfan Agent Foundry. By decoupling the Agent Core, which handles task understanding, planning, and scheduling, from an extensible Tool Ecosystem for retrieval, evidence acquisition, and report rendering, the framework exposes every planning decision and tool invocation as an inspectable artifact, directly addressing the transparency and auditability challenge of agentic deep research. On top of this infrastructure, we introduced three cognitive mechanisms tailored to the open challenges of the task: a graph-based dynamic planner that supports coarse-to-fine exploration, reflection, re-planning, backtracking, and parallel branching for far-sighted long-horizon research; a recursive two-level execution design that delegates each complex search sub-task to an inner Search Agent running its own planning loop, isolating noisy retrieval so that the global trajectory stays stable; and a rubric-based test-time optimization mechanism that dynamically generates task-specific quality criteria and uses them as live reasoning scaffolds for evidence-grounded synthesis and adaptive stopping.

DeepResearch Bench 和 DeepResearch Bench II 上的实验显示，互补评估协议的收益一致，其中 DuMate-DeepResearch 在两个基准测试中均取得了最佳总体得分。这些结果证明了将可审计的多智能体基础设施与自适应规划和标题引导推理相结合以实现高质量深度研究的有效性。在未来的工作中，我们计划将评估扩展到其他实时和多模式深度研究基准，以更丰富的特定领域功能拓宽工具生态系统，并进一步研究基于评估标准的优化作为训练时间和测试时间信号。Experiments on DeepResearch Bench and DeepResearch Bench II show consistent gains across complementary evaluation protocols, with DuMate-DeepResearch achieving the best overall scores on both benchmarks. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of combining auditable multi-agent infrastructure with adaptive planning and rubric-guided reasoning for high-quality deep research. In future work, we plan to extend the evaluation to additional live and multimodal deep research benchmarks, broaden the Tool Ecosystem with richer domain-specific capabilities, and further investigate rubric-based optimization as a training-time as well as test-time signal.
