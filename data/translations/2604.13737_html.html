1. 简介Introduction

推荐系统是现代数字经济的基础设施，为在线广告、信息流排名、电子商务和短视频平台提供动力。它们的实际重要性在货币化规模和用户参与强度上都很明显。 IAB/普华永道互联网广告收入报告显示，2024年美国互联网广告收入达2586亿美元，同比增长14.9%Recommender systems are a foundational infrastructure of the modern digital economy, powering online advertising, feed ranking, e-commerce, and short-video platforms. Their practical importance is evident in both monetization scale and user engagement intensity. According to the IAB/PwC Internet Advertising Revenue Report, U.S. internet advertising revenue reached $258.6 billion in 2024, up 14.9% year over year(Interactive Advertising Bureau and PricewaterhouseCoopers, 2025)。与此同时，社交和短视频平台持续快速增长：DataReportal报告称，2025年初全球社交媒体用户身份达52.4亿. At the same time, social and short-video platforms continue to grow rapidly: DataReportal reports 5.24 billion global social media user identities in early 2025(Kemp, 2025a)，而仅 TikTok 就通过其广告工具吸引了至少 15.9 亿用户，其 Android 用户在 2024 年 11 月在该应用上花费了近 35 小时, while TikTok alone reached at least 1.59 billion users via its advertising tools and its Android users spent almost 35 hours on the app in November 2024(Kemp, 2025c, b)。这种规模的背后隐藏着一个核心建模问题：给定高度稀疏的用户、项目和上下文信号以及用户不断变化的行为历史，我们如何构建既具有表现力又可扩展的推荐模型？. Behind this scale lies a core modeling problem: given highly sparse user, item, and context signals together with users’ evolving behavior histories, how can we build recommendation models that are both expressive and scalable?

2.2. 推荐中的代表性崩溃Representation Collapse in Recommendation

表示崩溃是指学习嵌入占据低维子空间的趋势，失去项目或用户之间的可区分性。在推荐的背景下，这个问题对于低基数或弱信息特征（例如人口统计桶或粗略上下文字段）尤其严重。最近的工作系统地证明了嵌入崩溃成为扩展推荐模型时的关键障碍Representation collapse refers to the tendency of learned embeddings to occupy a low-dimensional subspace, losing discriminability across items or users. In the context of recommendation, this problem is especially acute for low-cardinality or weak-information features such as demographic buckets or coarse context fields. Recent work has systematically demonstrated that embedding collapse becomes a critical barrier when scaling up recommendation models(Guo et al., 2024)，并且崩溃和特征纠缠共同降低了大规模工业系统中的推荐质量, and that collapse and feature entanglement jointly degrade recommendation quality in large-scale industrial systems(Pan et al., 2024)。从模型设计的角度来看，特征生成范式表明非线性交互算子可以在特征交叉期间显式地保留表示等级.
