摘要Abstract

However, the abundance of small, densely distributed, and visually similar objects creates high visual redundancy, while repetitive local configurations give rise to strong topological ambiguity. Existing approaches mainly focus on visual–language feature alignment or dense contextual interaction, yet they struggle to distinguish subtle inter-instance differences and effectively exploit spatial topological structures, leading to inaccurate grounding in highly crowded scenarios. To address these challenges, we propose抓取VGGrabVG，一种受人类视觉搜索启发的新颖的视觉基础框架。 GrabVG 将基础明确分解为两个连续的阶段：, a novel visual grounding framework inspired by human visual search. GrabVG explicitly decomposes grounding into two sequential stages:前注意假设搜索preattentive hypothesis search和and图关注特征绑定graph-attentive feature binding。 具体来说，我们首先通过蒸馏引导的提议归纳和文本感知假设过滤生成一组紧凑的可靠对象假设，从而大大减少背景干扰和语义不匹配。 然后将这些假设组织成一个稀疏图，其中语言引导的实例内视觉线索和实例间拓扑关系通过图注意力共同绑定和传播，从而实现高效的空间推理和准确的目标定位。 在 AerialVG 和 AerialSense 上进行的大量实验表明，GrabVG 实现了良好的精度与速度权衡，在 Acc@0.5 下达到了 67.31% 和 80.34%，分别比相应的基线高出 10.55 和 8.76 个百分点。. Specifically, we first generate a compact set of reliable object hypotheses through distillation-guided proposal induction and text-aware hypothesis filtering, substantially reducing background distractions and semantic mismatches. These hypotheses are then organized into a sparse graph, where language-guided intra-instance visual cues and inter-instance topological relationships are jointly bound and propagated via graph attention, enabling efficient spatial reasoning and accurate target localization. Extensive experiments on AerialVG and AerialSense show that GrabVG achieves a favorable accuracy–speed trade-off, reaching 67.31% and 80.34% Acc@0.5 and outperforming the corresponding baselines by 10.55 and 8.76 percentage points, respectively.

1 简介Introduction

无人机（UAV）图像中的视觉接地旨在根据自然语言表达在鸟瞰场景中定位目标物体Visual grounding in Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) imagery aims to localize a target object in a bird’s-eye-view scene according to a natural-language expression[19, 9, 4, 15]。 与传统的地面图像相比，无人机场景通常包含大量小型且密集分布的物体，其中许多物体表现出高度相似的外观. Compared with conventional ground-level imagery, UAV scenes typically contain numerous small and densely distributed objects, many of which exhibit highly similar appearances[31, 46, 42, 19]。 这些实例之间有限的视觉差异造成. The limited visual distinctions among these instances create高视觉冗余度high visual redundancy，仅凭外观线索不足以区分所指对象与附近的干扰物, making appearance cues alone insufficient for distinguishing the referred object from nearby distractors[19]。 此外，较高的视角会削弱透视和深度线索，同时将大量物体带入视野。 因此，对象通常被压缩为密集且重复的二维排列，导致不同的目标假设共享相似的局部邻域和相对空间配置。 这种结构相似的邻域使得仅靠拓扑线索不足以唯一地识别所指对象，从而产生. Moreover, the elevated viewpoint weakens perspective and depth cues while bringing a larger number of objects into view simultaneously. As a result, objects are often compressed into dense and repetitive two-dimensional arrangements, causing different target hypotheses to share similar local neighborhoods and relative spatial configurations. Such structurally similar neighborhoods make topological cues alone insufficient to uniquely identify the referent, giving rise to强拓扑模糊性strong topological ambiguity.

现有的视觉基础方法主要通过更强的视觉-语言对齐和上下文关系建模来解决这些挑战。 视觉-语言对齐方法增强表达条件的视觉表征Existing visual grounding approaches address these challenges primarily through stronger vision–language alignment and contextual relation modeling. Vision–language alignment methods enhance expression-conditioned visual representations[7, 29]，而基于关系的方法则结合了实例之间的空间配置, while relation-based approaches incorporate spatial configurations among instances[19]。 然而，跨模式对齐通常在大量中间查询中执行，这可能无法充分编码细微的候选级别差异。 同时，密集关系模型捕获了广泛的成对交互，可能允许遥远或弱相关的关系干扰信息丰富的本地上下文。 因此，现有的方法可能仍然难以在拥挤的无人机场景中获得有区别的候选表示和可靠的上下文证据。 这些限制源于建模证据和基础过程的组织。. However, cross-modal alignment is often performed across numerous intermediate queries, which may insufficiently encode subtle candidate-level differences. Meanwhile, dense relation modeling captures extensive pairwise interactions, potentially allowing distant or weakly relevant relations to interfere with informative local context. Consequently, existing approaches may still struggle to obtain discriminative candidate representations and reliable contextual evidence in crowded UAV scenes. These limitations arise from both the modeled evidence and the organization of the grounding process.

人类视觉搜索为组织这个过程提供了有用的原则。 引导搜索表明，前注意特征信号会将视觉注意力偏向一组有限的可能目标位置Human visual search offers a useful principle for organizing this process. Guided Search suggests that preattentive feature signals bias visual attention toward a restricted set of likely target locations[30]，而特征集成理论认为需要集中注意力才能将分布式视觉特征绑定到连贯的对象表示中, while Feature Integration Theory posits that focused attention is required to bind distributed visual features into coherent object representations[25]。 受这一渐进过程的启发，我们将无人机视觉接地制定为. Inspired by this progressive process, we formulate UAV visual grounding as前注意假设搜索preattentive hypothesis search随后followed by图关注特征绑定graph-attentive feature binding：前者将搜索空间限制为合理的目标假设，而后者将每个幸存的假设与判别性证据相关联。: the former constrains the search space to plausible target hypotheses, whereas the latter associates each surviving hypothesis with discriminative evidence.

2 相关工作Related Work

视觉接地。Visual Grounding.

视觉基础根据自然语言表达来定位图像区域。 早期基于提议的方法使用外观、位置和上下文线索对检测到的区域进行排名Visual grounding localizes an image region according to a natural-language expression. Early proposal-based methods rank detected regions using appearance, location, and contextual cues[39, 38]，而单阶段和基于变压器的方法直接从跨模态表示中回归所涉及的区域, while one-stage and transformer-based approaches directly regress the referred region from cross-modal representations[36, 18, 7, 13, 45]。 随后的研究通过语言引导的特征细化来改善视觉-语言对应性. Subsequent studies improve visual–language correspondence through language-guided feature refinement[37, 24]、动态视觉采样, dynamic visual sampling[22]，与语言无关的标记删除, language-irrelevant token removal[23]，以及解耦的多模态融合, and decoupled multimodal fusion[6]。 扎根预训练进一步将语言条件定位与开放词汇检测相结合. Grounded pre-training further unifies language-conditioned localization with open-vocabulary detection[17, 20]，而 PropVG 重新审视提案驱动的接地, while PropVG revisits proposal-driven grounding[5]最近的工作探索了小物体 REC 的渐进细化and recent work explores progressive refinement for small-object REC[8]。 然而，这些方法大多数要么对密集的多模态特征进行操作，要么对通用区域提案进行排名，而没有明确地将候选筛选与实例级关系推理分开。. However, most of these methods either operate on dense multimodal features or rank generic region proposals, without explicitly separating candidate screening from instance-level relational reasoning.

无人机和遥感视觉接地。UAV and Remote-Sensing Visual Grounding.

遥感视觉基础面临尺度变化、杂乱背景、高分辨率图像和小目标的挑战。 现有研究改善了跨模式对齐Remote-sensing visual grounding is challenged by scale variation, cluttered backgrounds, high-resolution imagery, and small targets. Existing studies improve cross-modal alignment[40, 14, 29], 关系推理, relational reasoning[19, 44]，以及语义几何建模, and semantic–geometric modeling[41, 3]。 ProVG进一步采用渐进式. ProVG further adopts a progressive调查-定位-验证survey–locate–verify将全局、关系和属性线索依次注入密集视觉特征的策略strategy that sequentially injects global, relational, and attribute cues into dense visual features[16]。 相比之下，GrabVG 首先筛选明确的候选对象，然后对其局部视觉属性和几何邻域进行推理。. In contrast, GrabVG first screens explicit object candidates and then reasons over their local visual attributes and geometric neighborhoods.

以推理为中心的遥感基础。Reasoning-Centric Remote-Sensing Grounding.

最近的方法，例如 RSGround-R1 和 Geo-R1，通过监督推理痕迹和强化微调来改善接地Recent methods such as RSGround-R1 and Geo-R1 improve grounding through supervised reasoning traces and reinforcement fine-tuning[11, 43]。 这些方法提供了明确的文本原理和改进的低样本推理，但需要额外的推理监督或基于推出的优化，并会产生自回归推理开销。 相反，GrabVG 在稀疏候选图上执行固定深度的可微分推理，提供直接可检查的节点-边缘交互，而无需生成文本推理轨迹。. These approaches offer explicit textual rationales and improved low-shot reasoning, but require additional reasoning supervision or rollout-based optimization and incur autoregressive inference overhead. GrabVG instead performs fixed-depth differentiable reasoning over sparse candidate graphs, offering directly inspectable node–edge interactions without generating textual reasoning trajectories.

视觉基础的关系建模。Relation Modeling for Visual Grounding.

关系上下文通常用于区分视觉上相似的候选者。 早期的方法将目标提案与周围对象进行比较或将表达式分解为主题、位置和关系组件Relational context is commonly used to distinguish visually similar candidates. Early methods compare target proposals with surrounding objects or decompose expressions into subject, location, and relation components[39, 38]。 后来的基于图的方法采用语言引导的节点和边缘注意. Later graph-based methods employ language-guided node and edge attention[27]，跨模态关系图, cross-modal relation graphs[33]、动态多步推理, dynamic multi-step reasoning[34]，或对齐的语言和视觉场景图, or aligned language and visual scene graphs[35]。 然而，这些关系通常是在通用建议或广泛的语义结构上建模的。 AerialVG 明确捕获空中物体之间的位置关系. However, these relations are typically modeled over generic proposals or broad semantic structures. AerialVG explicitly captures positional relations among aerial objects[19]，但密集的交互可能会引入来自遥远或弱相关实例的上下文。 相反，GrabVG 将语言条件图注意力应用于通过局部几何邻近度连接的过滤候选者，从而限制来自遥远或弱相关实例的消息传递。, but dense interaction may introduce context from distant or weakly relevant instances. GrabVG instead applies language-conditioned graph attention to filtered candidates connected by local geometric proximity, thereby limiting message passing from distant or weakly relevant instances.
