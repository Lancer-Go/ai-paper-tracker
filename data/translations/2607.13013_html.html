离散扩散语言模型Discrete-Diffusion Language Model

Harsha Vardhan Khurdula1  Abhinav Kumar Singh2  Yoeven D Khemlani2  Vineet Agarwal2
Interfaze AI
San Francisco, CA, USA

摘要Abstract

自动语音识别主要由一次发出一个标记的自回归解码器主导。我们询问离散扩散语言模型是否可以转录语音，通过少量的去噪步骤并行完善整个转录。我们为 DiffusionGemma 训练了一个音频原生接口，DiffusionGemma 是一个 26B 专家混合模型，通过均匀、随机令牌离散扩散而不是最近扩散语言模型常见的吸收掩模方案来生成文本。冻结的 Whisper 编码器提供声学特征，轻型投影仪将它们映射到模型嵌入空间，低阶适配器让冻结的骨干网参与新的模态。Automatic speech recognition is dominated by autoregressive decoders that emit one token at a time. We ask whether a discrete diffusion language model can transcribe speech instead, refining a whole transcript in parallel over a small number of denoising steps. We train an audio-native interface for DiffusionGemma, a 26B mixture-of-experts model that generates text by uniform, random-token discrete diffusion rather than the absorbing-mask scheme common to recent diffusion language models. A frozen Whisper encoder supplies acoustic features, a lightweight projector maps them into the model embedding space, and low-rank adapters let the frozen backbone attend to the new modality.

训练了大约 42M 参数，占主干网的 0.16%。我们发现自然训练目标无法使音频接地，因为它们的梯度只能通过已经忽略它的注意力到达投影仪。通过冻结输出头应用的联结主义时间分类损失打破了这一僵局。最终的模型在 LibriSpeech test-clean 上达到了 6.6% 的单词错误率，无论话语长度如何，都以大约八个并行步骤进行转录，并使用经过六种语言训练的单个适配器，我们在这里对英语、印地语和普通话进行评估。About 42M parameters are trained, which is 0.16 percent of the backbone. We find that the natural training objectives fail to ground the audio, because their gradient reaches the projector only through attention that has already dismissed it. A connectionist temporal classification loss applied through the frozen output head breaks this deadlock. The resulting model reaches 6.6 percent word error rate on LibriSpeech test-clean, transcribes in roughly eight parallel steps regardless of utterance length, and uses a single adapter trained on six languages, which we evaluate here on English, Hindi, and Mandarin.

1 简介Introduction

语音识别长期以来一直以相同的方式进行解码。编码器读取音频，注意力解码器一次拼写一个记号，将每个记号反馈给自身以生成下一个记号Speech recognition has been decoded the same way for a long time. An encoder reads the audio, and an attention decoder spells the transcript one token at a time, feeding each token back to itself to produce the next(Chan et al., 2016; Radford et al., 2023)。编码器已经变得非常强大，但解码器在结构上是串行的，因此无论有多少硬件可用，三十秒的剪辑都会花费几百次连续的前向传递。扩散语言模型的解码方式不同，从噪声画布开始，通过少量去噪步骤并行细化整个序列；他们现在达到了与类似大小的自回归模型相媲美的质量. The encoders have grown very strong, but the decoder is serial by construction, so a thirty second clip costs a few hundred sequential forward passes no matter how much hardware is available. Diffusion language models decode differently, starting from a canvas of noise and refining the whole sequence in parallel over a small number of denoising steps; they now reach quality competitive with autoregressive models of similar size(Nie et al., 2025)。自然的问题是语音是否可以以同样的方式转录，以便识别的成本由去噪步骤的数量而不是转录的长度决定。. The natural question is whether speech can be transcribed the same way, so that the cost of recognition is set by the number of denoising steps and not by the length of the transcript.

两个系统表明扩散可以转录。使用多项式扩散进行 TransFusion 去噪字符序列Two systems have shown that diffusion can transcribe. TransFusion denoised character sequences with multinomial diffusion(Baas et al., 2022)，Whisfusion 在冻结的 Whisper 特征之上训练了掩蔽扩散解码器, and Whisfusion trained a masked-diffusion decoder on top of frozen Whisper features(Kwon et al., 2025)。两者都专门针对该任务训练解码器，并且两者都只是英语。我们提出一个不同的问题：是否可以教会现有的通用扩散语言模型去听，以便识别通过它自己的解码器运行，而根本不需要训练新的解码器？. Both train a decoder specifically for the task, and both are English only. We ask a different question: can an existing, general-purpose diffusion language model be taught to hear, so that recognition runs through its own decoder with no new decoder trained at all?

我们采用 DiffusionGemma，一种开放权重 26B 专家混合模型，通过离散扩散生成文本（没有音频支持），并为其提供本机音频输入。我们保持脊椎冻结。我们添加一个冻结的 Whisper 编码器作为声学特征提取器We take DiffusionGemma, an open-weight 26B mixture-of-experts model that generates text by discrete diffusion (does not have audio support), and give it a native audio input. We keep the backbone frozen. We add a frozen Whisper encoder as an acoustic feature extractor(Radford et al., 2023)，一个小型可训练投影仪，将这些特征映射到模型嵌入空间，以及低阶适配器, a small trainable projector that maps those features into the model embedding space, and low-rank adapters(Hu et al., 2022)因此冻结的模型可以学习关注新的模式。投影到语言模型的配方遵循为纯文本模型提供视觉和听觉的连接器so the frozen model can learn to attend to the new modality. The projector-into-language-model recipe follows the connectors that gave sight and hearing to text-only models(Liu et al., 2023; Chu et al., 2023; Rubenstein et al., 2023)，在这里首次应用于扩散解码器。仅训练约 42M 参数，仅占主干网的 0.16%。在 LibriSpeech test-clean 上，结果达到 6.6% 的单词错误率，无论话语长度如何，都会在大约八个并行步骤中收敛，并使用经过六种语言训练的单个适配器。, applied here for the first time to a diffusion decoder. Only about 42M parameters train, which is 0.16 percent of the backbone. The result reaches 6.6 percent word error rate on LibriSpeech test-clean, converges in about eight parallel steps regardless of utterance length, and uses a single adapter trained on six languages.

挑战在于基础，冻结的语言模型从未见过频谱图，因此其嵌入空间没有共振峰或音素的概念，并且两个明显的训练目标都通过交叉注意力来路由信号。如果注意力已经确定音频是噪音，则没有梯度到达投影仪以使其有用，并且投影仪保持噪音，这反过来又使注意力没有理由改变。我们通过直接通过冻结输出头应用联结主义时间分类损失来打破这种僵局，这迫使音频嵌入对转录本进行线性预测，而不需要首先注意信任它们。The challenge lied in grounding, a frozen language model has never seen a spectrogram, so its embedding space has no notion of formants or phonemes, and the two obvious training objectives both route their signal through cross-attention. If attention has already decided the audio is noise, no gradient reaches the projector to make it useful, and the projector stays noise, which in turn gives attention no reason to change. We break this deadlock with a connectionist temporal classification loss applied directly through the frozen output head, which forces the audio embeddings to be linearly predictive of the transcript without asking attention to trust them first.

2 背景Background

2.1 扩散主干The diffusion backbone

DiffusionGemma 是一个 26B 专家混合模型，具有 128 位专家、top-8 路由以及每个令牌大约 4B 的活动参数。它有 30 个转换器层，隐藏大小为 2816，词汇量为 262144。它通过离散扩散而不是自回归生成文本。DiffusionGemma is a 26B mixture-of-experts model with 128 experts, top-8 routing, and roughly 4B active parameters per token. It has 30 transformer layers, a hidden size of 2816, and a vocabulary of 262144. It generates text by discrete diffusion rather than autoregression.

最近的扩散语言模型使用吸收状态，其中腐败用特殊的代币代替Most recent diffusion language models use an absorbing state, where corruption replaces tokens with a special<掩码><mask>符号和生成填充掩码symbol and generation fills masks in(Austin et al., 2021; Lou et al., 2024; Nie et al., 2025)。 DiffusionGemma 相反使用均匀的随机令牌扩散。它从固定长度的画布开始，最多 256 个槽，填充从词汇表中抽取的随机标记。在每个去噪步骤中，模型都会立即读取整个画布，保留其有信心的低熵预测，并将剩余位置重新降噪回新的随机标记。经过几步后，噪声退火成文本（图. DiffusionGemma instead uses uniform, random-token diffusion. It starts from a fixed-length canvas, up to 256 slots, filled with random tokens drawn from the vocabulary. At each denoising step the model reads the whole canvas at once, keeps the low-entropy predictions it is confident about, and renoises the remaining positions back to fresh random tokens. After a few steps the noise anneals into text (Figure1）。培训反映了这个过程。干净的序列因替换一小部分而被破坏). Training mirrors this process. A clean sequence is corrupted by replacing a fractionγ\gamma具有统一随机令牌的位置，并且训练模型以在损坏的位置恢复原始数据。我们在第节中正式化了该过程of positions with uniform random tokens, and the model is trained to recover the originals at the corrupted positions. We formalize the process in Section3.4.

2.2 编码器-解码器结构Encoder-decoder structure

从架构上来说，该模型是一个具有绑定变压器权重的编码器-解码器。编码器将提示按因果关系读取到只读键值缓存中。解码器通过双向自注意力优化画布，同时交叉关注该缓存。通过将投影特征分散到编码器输入嵌入的占位符标记位置来注入多模态输入。现有的视觉路径为此使用图像标记标识符。该模型开箱即用，接受文本、图像和视频，并生成文本。它不接受音频，并提供该模式是我们工作中的主要贡献之一。Architecturally the model is an encoder-decoder with tied transformer weights. The encoder reads the prompt causally into a read-only key-value cache. The decoder refines the canvas with bidirectional self-attention while cross-attending to that cache. Multimodal inputs are injected by scattering projected features into placeholder token positions of the encoder input embeddings. The existing vision path uses an image token identifier for this. Out of the box the model accepts text, images, and video, and produces text. It does not accept audio and providing that modality is one of our key contributions in our work.

2.3 将编码器连接到语言模型Connecting an encoder to a language model

通过训练一个小型连接器，可以为冻结的语言模型提供新的输入模态，该连接器将模态编码器的特征映射到模型嵌入空间，而主干保持固定或仅进行轻微调整。该配方通过视觉编码器上的投影仪为纯文本模型添加了视觉A frozen language model can be given a new input modality by training a small connector that maps features from a modality encoder into the model embedding space, while the backbone stays fixed or is only lightly adapted. This recipe added vision to text-only models through a projector over a vision encoder(Liu et al., 2023)，以及通过语音编码器和适配器将音频转换为语言模型, and audio to language models through speech encoders and adapters(Chu et al., 2023; Rubenstein et al., 2023)，通常位于开放模型系列（例如 Gemma）之上, usually on top of open model families such as Gemma(Gemma Team, 2024)。我们的音频路径遵循相同的模式。新的是它提供的解码器。我们知道的每个连接器都提供一个自回归模型，而这里的连接器条件是一个并行生成转录本的扩散解码器，这改变了模型的接地方式和解码方式。. Our audio pathway follows the same pattern. What is new is the decoder it feeds. Every connector we are aware of feeds an autoregressive model, whereas here the connector conditions a diffusion decoder that produces the transcript in parallel, which changes both how the model must be grounded and how it decodes.

5.5 瓶颈是什么What is the bottleneck

我们进行消融来定位残差的来源，因为答案决定了该方法是否被破坏或只是训练不足。我们一次排除了一名嫌疑人。We ran ablations to locate the source of the residual error, because the answer decides whether the method is broken or simply undertrained. We ruled out one suspect at a time.

首先，编码器质量。我们将 Whisper-small 前端替换为 Whisper-large-v3，并在相同的 100 小时集上进行重新训练。匹配数据的字错误率从 3 个 epoch 的 14.75% 上升到 6 个 epoch 的 13.15% 和 8 个 epoch 的 8.95，这与在相同数据规模下运行的小型编码器大致持平。更强的编码器在此数据预算下不会移动下限，因此编码器不是当前限制。First, encoder quality. We swapped the Whisper-small front end for Whisper-large-v3 and retrained on the same 100 hour set. Word error rate at matched data moved from 14.75 percent at three epochs to 13.15 at six and 8.95 at eight, which is roughly even with the small-encoder run at the same data scale. A stronger encoder did not move the floor at this data budget, so the encoder is not the current limit.

第二，音频分辨率。我们将子采样因子降低到八以下，以便为投影仪提供每秒更多的音频令牌。这使字错误率下降到 44.7%，而不是有所改善，因此标记分辨率也不是限制，并且八倍子样本不会让解码器感到饥饿。Second, audio resolution. We reduced the subsample factor below eight to give the projector more audio tokens per second. This regressed to 44.7 percent word error rate rather than improving, so token resolution is not the limit either, and the eight-fold subsample is not starving the decoder.

排除编码器质量、分辨率和步数后，最可能的剩余解释是数据规模和适配器对齐。 Whisper-large-v3 的训练时间为数百万小时，Whisper-small 的训练时间为约 68 万小时。该模型的运行时间约为 219 小时，减少了三个数量级，并且在具有 100 个干净小时的单个域上已达到 6.6%。前端消融也以同样的方式进行，因为更强大的编码器对这个数据预算没有帮助，而当瓶颈是数据而不是特征时，这是人们所期望的。证据与数据瓶颈一致，而不是破碎的方法，但如果没有缩放曲线，我们就无法确定这一点，我们将其留给未来的工作。With encoder quality, resolution, and step count ruled out, the most likely remaining explanation is data scale and adapter alignment. Whisper-large-v3 was trained on a few million hours and Whisper-small on about 680 thousand. The frontend ablation points the same way, since a stronger encoder did not help at this data budget, which is what one expects when the bottleneck is data rather than features. The evidence is consistent with a data bottleneck rather than a broken method, but we cannot establish this without a scaling curve, which we leave to future work.

6 相关工作Related Work

语音识别如何解码。How speech recognition decodes.

端到端的认可源于两个想法。连接主义时间分类将帧序列与较短的标签序列对齐，无需显式分割End-to-end recognition grew from two ideas. Connectionist temporal classification aligns a frame sequence to a shorter label sequence without explicit segmentation(Graves et al., 2006)。基于注意力的序列到序列模型让解码器负责编码的音频并拼写文字记录. Attention-based sequence-to-sequence models instead let a decoder attend over the encoded audio and spell the transcript(Chan et al., 2016)，变压器推广的模式, a pattern the transformer generalized(Vaswani et al., 2017)。此后的大部分进步都来自更强大的编码器，包括自我监督表示. Most progress since then has come from stronger encoders, including self-supervised representations(Baevski et al., 2020)和卷积增强变压器and convolution-augmented transformers(Gulati et al., 2020)、从规模和监管薄弱来看, and from scale and weak supervision(Radford et al., 2023)。在所有这些中，记录一次生成一个标记。. In all of these the transcript is produced one token at a time.

非自回归和扩散文本生成。Non-autoregressive and diffusion text generation.

并行生成从非自回归翻译开始，立即发出整个目标Parallel generation began with non-autoregressive translation, which emits the whole target at once(Gu et al., 2018)。为连续数据开发的扩散模型. Diffusion models, developed for continuous data(Sohl-Dickstein et al., 2015; Ho et al., 2020)，通过在连续空间中嵌入标记而被带入语言, were brought to language both by embedding tokens in a continuous space(Li et al., 2022; Gong et al., 2023)并直接在离散令牌上定义损坏and by defining the corruption directly on discrete tokens(Austin et al., 2021; Lou et al., 2024)。自调节通过将先前的预测反馈到下一步来改进离散扩散. Self-conditioning improved discrete diffusion by feeding the previous prediction back into the next step(Chen et al., 2023)，我们的采样器使用的一种技术。大型掩蔽扩散语言模型现在的质量可与类似大小的自回归模型相媲美, a technique our sampler uses. Large masked-diffusion language models now reach quality competitive with autoregressive models of similar size(Nie et al., 2025)。 DiffusionGemma 属于这个家族，但会腐蚀为统一的随机标记，而不是吸收掩模。. DiffusionGemma sits in this family but corrupts toward uniform random tokens rather than an absorbing mask.

扩散语音识别和音频语言模型。Diffusion speech recognition and audio language models.

两个系统直接对语音进行扩散解码。 TransFusion 使用多项式扩散转录字符作为概念证明Two systems put diffusion decoding directly on speech. TransFusion transcribed characters with multinomial diffusion as a proof of concept(Baas et al., 2022)，Whisfusion 在冻结的 Whisper 特征上训练了掩蔽扩散解码器, and Whisfusion trained a masked-diffusion decoder over frozen Whisper features(Kwon et al., 2025)。两者都是从头开始构建解码器，而我们则重复使用已经存在的解码器。另外，大量工作通过训练有素的连接器为冻结或轻微调整的语言模型提供了新的模式，以实现视觉. Both build that decoder from scratch, whereas we reuse one that already exists. Separately, a large body of work gives new modalities to frozen or lightly adapted language models through a trained connector, for vision(Liu et al., 2023)和音频and for audio(Chu et al., 2023; Rubenstein et al., 2023)，通常位于开放模型系列之上，例如 Gemma, often on top of open model families such as Gemma(Gemma Team, 2024)。我们的工作将这两条线索结合起来。我们重复使用现成的扩散语言模型，无需新的解码器，通过投影仪和低阶适配器连接冻结的语音编码器. Our work joins these two threads. We reuse an off-the-shelf diffusion language model with no new decoder, connect a frozen speech encoder through a projector and low-rank adapters(Hu et al., 2022)，以联结主义时间分类为基础, ground it with connectionist temporal classification(Graves et al., 2006)，使用统一的随机令牌扩散而不是吸收掩模进行解码，并用六种语言训练一个适配器。, decode with uniform random-token diffusion rather than absorbing masks, and train one adapter on six languages.

7 局限性Limitations

该模型在我们测试的每个基准上都落后于自回归 Whisper，在多语言朗读语音上的领先优势最大。证据表明数据规模是原因，而不是架构，但这仍然是一个假设，直到我们训练更多的音频。 Whisper 编码器将输入窗口固定在三十秒，因此不可能实现真正的流式传输，并且长音频是通过静音分段处理的，当语音没有明显的停顿时，这可能会出错。扩散解码器可以在短话语上产生重复伪影，我们目前通过后处理通道而不是在源头来删除这些伪影。最后，我们与 Whisper 的比较使用相同归一化器下发布的范围，而不是每个基线的单个受控重新运行，因此 Whisper 列应被视为参考点，而不是精确的头对头数字。The model trails autoregressive Whisper on every benchmark we tested, by the largest margin on multilingual read speech. The evidence points to data scale as the cause rather than architecture, but that remains a hypothesis until we train on substantially more audio. The Whisper encoder fixes the input window at thirty seconds, so real streaming is not possible and long audio is handled by silence segmentation, which can err when speech has no clear pauses. The diffusion decoder can produce repetition artifacts on short utterances, which we currently remove with a post-processing pass rather than at the source. Finally, our comparisons to Whisper use published ranges under the same normalizer rather than a single controlled re-run of every baseline, so the Whisper columns should be read as reference points rather than exact head-to-head numbers.

8 结论Conclusion

如果直接监督音频投影仪而不是仅通过注意力来监督，冻结的语言模型可以学习聆听。在模型实际落地之前，损失曲线和令牌准确性看起来很健康，因此在训练期间手动解码很重要。一旦落地，扩散解码就会使说话者所说内容的长度不再影响成本，因为识别会收敛于大约八个并行步骤。接地配方、CTC 解锁和扩散解码器今天都可以使用；该模型仍然缺乏的是接触更多时间的音频。A frozen language model can learn to hear if the audio projector is supervised directly rather than only through attention. Loss curves and token accuracy will look healthy well before the model is actually grounded, so manual decoding matters during training. Once grounded, diffusion decoding makes the length of what a speaker said stop mattering for cost, because recognition converges in about eight parallel steps. The grounding recipe, the CTC unlock, and the diffusion decoder all work today; what the model still lacks is exposure to more hours of audio.
