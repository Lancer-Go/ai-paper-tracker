摘要Abstract

传统上，抗癌疗法的发现将有机小分子和金属基配位化合物视为单独的化学领域，尽管它们具有共同的生物学目标，但限制了知识转移。这种差异在现有数据中尤其明显，与只有几千个特征金属配合物相比，有大量的有机化合物筛选数据库。在这里，我们介绍了 ChemCLIP，这是一个双编码器对比学习框架，它通过学习基于共享抗癌活性而不是结构相似性的统一表示来弥合这种有机-无机鸿沟。我们编制了补充数据集，其中包含 44,854 种独特的有机化合物和 5,164 种独特的金属络合物，并在 60 种癌细胞系中进行了标准化。通过使用活动感知硬负挖掘训练并行编码器，我们将结构不同的化合物映射到共享的 256 维嵌入空间中，在该空间中，无论化学类别如何，生物学上相似的化合物都会聚集在一起。我们通过定量比对指标、嵌入可视化和下游分类任务系统地评估了四种分子编码策略——摩根指纹、ChemBERTa、MolFormer 和 Chemprop。摩根指纹实现了卓越的性能，平均比对率为 0.899，下游分类 AUC 为 0.859（无机）和 0.817（有机）。使用冻结嵌入的高分类性能（无需针对特定任务的微调）表明，具有生物学意义的特征被编码在学习的表示中，从而实现了计算药物筛选的实际应用。这项工作将对比学习确立为统一不同化学领域的有效策略，并为多模式化学应用中编码器的选择提供了经验指导，其影响超出了抗癌药物的发现范围，扩展到任何需要跨领域化学知识转移的场景。The discovery of anticancer therapeutics has traditionally treated organic small molecules and metal-based coordination complexes as separate chemical domains, limiting knowledge transfer despite their shared biological objectives. This disparity is particularly pronounced in available data, with extensive screening databases for organic compounds compared to only a few thousand characterized metal complexes. Here, we introduce ChemCLIP, a dual-encoder contrastive learning framework that bridges this organic-inorganic divide by learning unified representations based on shared anticancer activities rather than structural similarity. We compiled complementary datasets comprising 44,854 unique organic compounds and 5,164 unique metal complexes, standardized across 60 cancer cell lines. By training parallel encoders with activity-aware hard negative mining, we mapped structurally distinct compounds into a shared 256-dimensional embedding space where biologically similar compounds cluster together regardless of chemical class. We systematically evaluated four molecular encoding strategies—Morgan fingerprints, ChemBERTa, MolFormer, and Chemprop—through quantitative alignment metrics, embedding visualizations, and downstream classification tasks. Morgan fingerprints achieved superior performance with an average alignment ratio of 0.899 and downstream classification AUCs of 0.859 (inorganic) and 0.817 (organic). The high classification performance using frozen embeddings—without task-specific fine-tuning— demonstrates that biologically meaningful features are encoded in the learned representations, enabling practical applications in computational drug screening. This work establishes contrastive learning as an effective strategy for unifying disparate chemical domains and provides empirical guidance for encoder selection in multi-modal chemistry applications, with implications extending beyond anticancer drug discovery to any scenario requiring crossdomain chemical knowledge transfer.

简介Introduction

传统上，抗癌疗法的发展沿着两条平行但基本上独立的路径进展：有机小分子和金属配位配合物[1, 2]。虽然经过数十年的系统筛选和优化，有机化合物在药物领域占据主导地位，但金属药物（以铂类化疗药物的临床成功为例）在解决耐药性方面提供了独特的作用机制和潜在优势 [3, 4]。然而，这些领域之间可用数据的巨大差异给计算药物发现带来了重大挑战。的The development of anticancer therapeutics has traditionally progressed along two parallel but largely independent paths: organic small molecules and metal-based coordination complexes [1, 2]. While organic compounds dominate the pharmaceutical landscape due to decades of systematic screening and optimization, metal-based drugs—exemplified by the clinical success of platinum-based chemotherapeutics—offer distinct mechanisms of action and potential advantages in addressing drug resistance [3, 4]. However, the vast disparity in available data between these domains presents a significant challenge for computational drug discovery. The

美国国家癌症研究所的 NCI60 筛选计划已生成超过 44,000 种有机化合物的活性数据 [5]，而金属配合物的综合数据库仅包含几千种化合物。这种数据不平衡，加上有机分子和配位络合物之间的基本结构差异，传统上需要单独的计算模型以及有机和无机领域之间有限的知识转移[6-10]。National Cancer Institute's NCI60 screening program has generated activity data for over 44,000 organic compounds [5], whereas comprehensive databases of metal complexes contain only a few thousand compounds. This data imbalance, combined with the fundamental structural differences between organic molecules and coordination complexes, has traditionally necessitated separate computational models and limited knowledge transfer between organic and inorganic domains [6-10].

对比学习的最新进展——最引人注目的是视觉语言领域的 CLIP（对比语言图像预训练）[11] 所证明的——提出了一种有前途的方法来桥接这种不同的模式 [12]。通过学习根据共享功能属性而不是结构相似性来对齐表示，对比学习框架可以发现根本不同的数据类型之间有意义的对应关系。在抗癌药物发现的背景下，这种范式转变提供了创建统一计算模型的机会，该模型利用有机化合物数据库中嵌入的广泛知识来为基于金属的疗法的预测提供信息，同时能够根据其生物活性对结构不同的候选药物进行直接比较和排名。Recent advances in contrastive learning—most notably demonstrated by CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training) [11] in the vision-language domain—suggest a promising approach to bridging such disparate modalities [12]. By learning to align representations based on shared functional properties rather than structural similarity, contrastive learning frameworks can discover meaningful correspondences between fundamentally different data types. In the context of anticancer drug discovery, this paradigm shift offers an opportunity to create unified computational models that leverage the extensive knowledge embedded in organic compound databases to inform predictions about metal-based therapeutics, while simultaneously enabling direct comparison and ranking of structurally diverse candidates based on their biological activities.

本研究引入了 ChemCLIP，这是一种双编码器对比学习框架，专门用于弥合抗癌化合物空间中的有机-无机鸿沟。我们编制了补充数据集，其中包括来自 NCI60 [5] 筛选计划的 44,854 种独特有机化合物和来自 MetalCytoToxDB [13] 的 5,164 种独特金属络合物，并在 60 个共享癌细胞系中进行了标准化。通过训练并行编码器将这些结构不同的化合物类别映射到统一的 256 维嵌入空间中，我们假设表现出相似生物活性的化合物无论其化学类别如何都会聚集在一起。为了严格评估这种方法，我们比较了四种分子编码策略——Morgan指纹[14]、ChemBERTa[15]、MolFormer[16]和Chemprop[17]——评估它们通过定量比对指标、嵌入空间可视化和下游分类性能学习活动相关表示的能力。This study introduces ChemCLIP, a dual-encoder contrastive learning framework specifically designed to bridge the organic-inorganic divide in anticancer compound space. We compiled complementary datasets comprising 44,854 unique organic compounds from the NCI60 [5] screening program and 5,164 unique metal complexes from MetalCytoToxDB [13], standardized across 60 shared cancer cell lines. By training parallel encoders to map these structurally distinct compound classes into a unified 256-dimensional embedding space, we hypothesized that compounds exhibiting similar biological activities would cluster together regardless of their chemical class. To rigorously evaluate this approach, we compared four molecular encoding strategies—Morgan fingerprints [14], ChemBERTa [15], MolFormer [16], and Chemprop [17]—assessing their ability to learn activity-relevant representations through quantitative alignment metrics, embedding space visualizations, and downstream classification performance.

我们的工作解决了三个基本问题：（1）对比学习能否根据共同的生物活动成功地调整有机和无机化学空间？ (2) 在金属配合物和有机分子的背景下，哪种分子编码策略最能支持跨域泛化？ （3）学习的嵌入空间是否为复合活动分类等下游任务提供有效的表示？这些问题的答案对计算药物筛选流程和化学中多模式学习的更广泛应用具有直接影响，其中桥接不同的化学领域可以加速多个治疗领域的发现。Our work addresses three fundamental questions: (1) Can contrastive learning successfully align organic and inorganic chemical spaces based on shared biological activities? (2) Which molecular encoding strategies best support cross-domain generalization in the context of metal complexes and organic molecules? (3) Does the learned embedding space provide effective representations for downstream tasks such as compound activity classification? The answers to these questions have direct implications for computational drug screening pipelines and broader applications of multi-modal learning in chemistry, where bridging diverse chemical domains could accelerate discovery across multiple therapeutic areas.

模型架构和训练方法Model Architecture and Training Methodology

ChemCLIP 实现了改编自 CLIP（对比语言-图像预训练）的双编码器对比学习架构，用于桥接无机和有机化合物空间。该模型学习了一个共享的嵌入空间，其中在癌细胞系中表现出相似生物活性的化合物实现了高度相似性，无论其化学类别（有机与无机）如何。这种方法可以实现跨域检索、从较大的有机化合物数据库到金属药物的知识转移以及下游活动预测任务。ChemCLIP implements a dual-encoder contrastive learning architecture adapted from CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training) for bridging inorganic and organic chemical compound spaces. The model learns a shared embedding space where compounds exhibiting similar biological activities across cancer cell lines achieve high similarity regardless of their chemical class (organic versus inorganic). This approach enables cross-domain retrieval, knowledge transfer from the larger organic compound database to metal-based drugs, and downstream activity prediction tasks.

双编码器架构。Dual-Encoder Architecture.该模型包含两个并行的编码分支，分别处理无机金属配合物和有机化合物，然后将它们投影到统一的 256 维嵌入空间中。无机分支结合了专门的金属特征集成，而有机分支则处理标准分子表示。两个分支都采用相同的投影架构来确保学习的嵌入空间的对称性。The model comprises two parallel encoding branches that process inorganic metal complexes and organic compounds separately before projecting them into a unified 256-dimensional embedding space. The inorganic branch incorporates specialized metal feature integration, while the organic branch processes standard molecular representations. Both branches employ identical projection architectures to ensure symmetry in the learned embedding space.

我们评估了四种不同的分子编码策略，以确定我们的对比学习框架的最佳表示。摩根指纹（半径=2，2048 位）提供了具有快速计算但固定表示的化学信息学基线。 ChemBERTa 和 MolFormer 代表了在大型化学数据库上预先训练的基于 Transformer 的方法。对于基于图的分子编码，我们实现了 Chemprop 的定向消息传递神经网络 (D-MPNN) [18]。We evaluated four distinct molecular encoding strategies to identify the optimal representation for our contrastive learning framework. Morgan fingerprints (radius=2, 2048 bits) provided a cheminformatics baseline with fast computation but fixed representations. ChemBERTa and MolFormer represented transformer-based approaches pre-trained on large chemical databases. For graph-based molecular encoding, we implemented Chemprop's Directed Message Passing Neural Network (D-MPNN) [18].

无机编码器通过金属特异性信息增强了基础分子表征，这对于理解金属络合物药理学至关重要。对于每种金属配合物，配体 SMILES 通过基本编码器进行处理，而金属特征则单独编码。金属类型通过 10 种过渡金属（Au、Co、Cu、Ir、Os、Pt、Re、Rh、Ru、Ti）的 one-hot 编码来表示，并辅以氧化态和原子序数的标量特征。这种架构使模型能够学习金属特定的药理学模式，同时保持与有机化合物表示的兼容性。The inorganic encoder enhanced base molecular representations with metal-specific information critical for understanding metal complex pharmacology. For each metal complex, the ligand SMILES was processed through the base encoder, while metal features were separately encoded. Metal type was represented through one-hot encoding across 10 transition metals (Au, Co, Cu, Ir, Os, Pt, Re, Rh, Ru, Ti), supplemented by scalar features for oxidation state and atomic number. This architecture enabled the model to learn metal-specific pharmacological patterns while maintaining compatibility with organic compound representations.

The first component was the bidirectional InfoNCE contrastive loss [19]. Within each training batch, we computed a similarity matrix between all inorganic and organic compound embeddings using normalized dot products scaled by a temperature parameter (τ = 0.07). Each inorganic compound was paired with exactly one organic compound from the same cell line, forming the positive pairs represented by diagonal elements in the similarity matrix. All other inorganic-organic combinations within the batch served as negative samples. The loss

第二个组成部分通过三元组保证金损失引入了活动感知的硬负挖掘[20]。与对比损失中的随机负例不同，硬负例是根据活动标签专门选择的，以创建更具挑战性的学习场景。对于每种活性无机化合物，我们选择来自同一细胞系的活性有机化合物作为正例，并选择来自同一细胞系的非活性有机化合物作为硬负例。这种配对策略迫使模型在相同的生物背景下区分活性和非活性化合物，而不是依赖于更容易区分来自不同细胞系的化合物。三重态损失强制无机化合物与其活性有机匹配物之间的相似性应超过与其非活性有机对应物的相似性。最终的训练目标将对比损失和三重损失以相等的权重相加，使模型能够学习广泛的化学相似性模式和细粒度的特定活动区分。The second component introduced activity-aware hard negative mining through triplet margin loss [20]. Unlike the random negatives in the contrastive loss, hard negatives were specifically selected based on activity labels to create more challenging learning scenarios. For each active inorganic compound, we selected an active organic compound from the same cell line as the positive example and an inactive organic compound from the same cell line as the hard negative. This pairing strategy forced the model to distinguish between active and inactive compounds within the same biological context, rather than relying on easier distinctions between compounds from different cell lines. The triplet loss enforced that the similarity between an inorganic compound and its active organic match should exceed the similarity to its inactive organic counterpart. The final training objective summed the contrastive loss and triplet loss with equal weighting, enabling the model to learn both broad chemical similarity patterns and fine-grained activity-specific discriminations.

We implemented compound-based data splitting to ensure rigorous evaluation of generalization to unseen chemical structures. Unique compounds from both datasets were randomly partitioned into 70% training, 15% validation, and 15% test sets. All compound-cell line pairs were assigned to their respective compound's split, preventing data leakage through compound replication across cell lines. This strategy provided stricter evaluation of model performance on completely novel chemical structures compared to random splitting of compound-cell line pairs. Model training employed the AdamW optimizer with learning rate 1×10⁻³, default weight decay (0.01), and beta parameters (0.9, 0.999). Training proceeded for 100 epochs with batch size 128, gradient clipping at maximum norm 1.0, and dropout rate 0.1. This configuration balanced training stability with efficient convergence across all encoder architectures evaluated.

讨论Discussion

主要发现和贡献Principal Findings and Contributions

这项研究引入了 ChemCLIP，这是一种对比学习框架，它通过统一的嵌入空间弥合了有机和无机抗癌化合物 [25] 传统上独立的领域。我们的结果表明，金属配合物和有机分子之间的跨模式排列可以成功捕获共享的生物活性模式，从而使知识从广泛研究的有机化学领域转移到相对尚待探索的无机领域。在四种经过评估的分子编码器中，MorganFingerprint 成为最佳选择，在嵌入质量（平均比对比：0.899）、可视化清晰度和下游分类任务（平均 AUC：0.838）方面实现了卓越的性能。使用冻结嵌入的强大分类性能（无需针对特定任务进行微调）验证了生物学相关特征已编码在学习的表示中，为标记训练数据可能有限的药物发现应用提供了实际优势。This study introduces ChemCLIP, a contrastive learning framework that bridges the traditionally separate domains of organic and inorganic anticancer compounds [25] through a unified embedding space. Our results demonstrate that cross-modal alignment between metal complexes and organic molecules can successfully capture shared biological activity patterns, enabling knowledge transfer from the extensively studied organic chemical space to the comparatively under-explored inorganic domain. Among four evaluated molecular encoders, MorganFingerprint emerged as the optimal choice, achieving superior performance across embedding quality (average alignment ratio: 0.899), visualization clarity, and downstream classification tasks (average AUC: 0.838). The strong classification performance using frozen embeddings—without task-specific fine-tuning—validates that biologically relevant features are encoded in the learned representations, offering practical advantages for drug discovery applications where labeled training data may be limited.

活动感知的硬负面挖掘及其在学习歧视中的作用Activity-Aware Hard Negative Mining and Its Role in Learning Discrimination

事实证明，通过三元组边缘损失结合活动感知的硬负挖掘对于实现观察到的分类性能至关重要。带有随机负采样的标准对比学习鼓励嵌入将化合物与不同的细胞系分开，这可能与活动差异相关，但并没有明确强制基于活动的歧视。通过将每种活性无机化合物与来自同一细胞系的活性有机化合物（阳性）和非活性有机化合物（硬阴性）专门配对，三联体损失迫使模型学习相同生物背景下活性和非活性化合物之间的微妙区别。The incorporation of activity-aware hard negative mining through triplet margin loss proved essential for achieving the observed classification performance. Standard contrastive learning with random negative sampling encourages embeddings to separate compounds from different cell lines, which may correlate with activity differences but does not explicitly enforce activitybased discrimination. By specifically pairing each active inorganic compound with an active organic compound (positive) and an inactive organic compound (hard negative) from the same cell line, the triplet loss forces the model to learn subtle distinctions between active and inactive compounds within identical biological contexts.

这种设计选择解决了多标签生物活性预测中的一个基本挑战：化合物可能表现出细胞系特异性效应，并且简单的交叉熵损失可以利用细胞系身份作为捷径，而不是学习可概括的活动模式。硬阴性挖掘策略通过确保阳性和阴性对共享相同的细胞系来消除这种捷径，迫使模型发现预测独立于测试环境的活动的化学特征。 t-SNE 可视化中活性和非活性化合物之间的清晰分离，特别是对于 MorganFingerprint，验证了This design choice addresses a fundamental challenge in multi-label biological activity prediction: compounds may exhibit cell line-specific effects, and simple cross-entropy losses can exploit cell line identity as a shortcut rather than learning generalizable activity patterns. The hard negative mining strategy eliminates this shortcut by ensuring that positive and negative pairs share the same cell line, compelling the model to discover chemical features that predict activity independent of the testing context. The clear separation between active and inactive compounds in the t-SNE visualizations, particularly for MorganFingerprint, validates

这种方法成功地将对比学习过程引导到具有生物学意义的表征。that this approach successfully guides the contrastive learning process toward biologically meaningful representations.

对比损失和三重损失的同等权重代表了两个互补目标之间的平衡：双向 InfoNCE 损失建立了广泛的跨模式对齐，而三重损失则细化了这种对齐以区分活性化合物和非活性化合物。替代权重方案或课程学习方法（其中每个损失分量的相对重要性在训练期间发生变化）可能会进一步提高嵌入质量，特别是在早期训练阶段，建立基本的跨模式对应可能比细粒度的活动区分更重要。The equal weighting of contrastive and triplet losses represents a balance between two complementary objectives: the bidirectional InfoNCE loss establishes broad cross-modal alignment, while the triplet loss refines this alignment to distinguish active from inactive compounds. Alternative weighting schemes or curriculum learning approaches—where the relative importance of each loss component varies during training—might further improve embedding quality, particularly in the early training phases where establishing basic crossmodal correspondence may be more important than fine-grained activity discrimination.

结论Conclusion

这项研究表明，对比学习可以成功地弥合有机和无机化学空间，创建统一的表示，尽管存在根本的结构差异，但仍能捕获共同的生物活动。 MorganFingerprint 的与领域无关的结构编码成为此任务的最佳选择，实现了卓越的跨模式对齐、嵌入质量和下游分类性能。通过冷冻嵌入训练的简单分类器获得的强有力的结果验证了这种方法在药物发现应用中的实用性，使知识从广泛研究的有机化合物转移到相对而言探索不足的金属药物。这些发现将对比学习确立为多领域化学应用的一种有前途的策略，并为需要跨结构不同化学类别平衡性能的场景中的编码器选择提供指导。This study demonstrates that contrastive learning can successfully bridge organic and inorganic chemical spaces, creating unified representations that capture shared biological activities despite fundamental structural differences. MorganFingerprint's domain-agnostic structural encoding emerges as the optimal choice for this task, achieving superior cross-modal alignment, embedding quality, and downstream classification performance. The strong results obtained with simple classifiers trained on frozen embeddings validate the practical utility of this approach for drug discovery applications, enabling knowledge transfer from extensively studied organic compounds to comparatively under-explored metal-based drugs. These findings establish contrastive learning as a promising strategy for multi-domain chemistry applications and provide guidance for encoder selection in scenarios requiring balanced performance across structurally diverse chemical classes.
