摘要Abstract

远视机器人操作需要逻辑连贯且几何基础的计划。现有的视觉-语言-行动策略通常隐藏潜在状态中的规划或仅暴露一种模态：纯文本思维链编码因果顺序，但错过空间约束，而视觉预测提供几何线索，但通常仍然局部和语义上受限。我们引入了交错视觉语言推理（IVLR），这是一个围绕 IVLR-Trace 构建的策略框架，IVLR-Trace 是一种显式中间表示，可在整个任务范围内将文本子目标与视觉关键帧交替使用。在测试时，单个本机多模态转换器根据初始观察和指令自行生成全局语义几何轨迹，将其缓存，并根据轨迹、原始指令和当前观察调节闭环动作解码器。由于标准机器人数据集缺乏此类痕迹，因此我们通过对演示进行时间分段并使用视觉语言模型为每个阶段添加字幕来构建伪监督。在长视界操纵和视觉分布偏移的模拟基准中，IVLR 在 LIBERO 上达到 95.5% 的平均成功率，其中在 LIBERO-Long 上达到 92.4%，在 SimplerEnv-WidowX 上达到 59.4% 的总体成功率。消融表明两种方式都是必要的：没有痕迹，LIBERO-Long 的成功率下降到 37.7%；仅文本和仅视觉痕迹达到 62.0% 和 68.4%，而完整交错痕迹达到 92.4%。具有执行扰动和屏蔽跟踪内容的压力测试显示出适度的退化，这表明跟踪可以容忍局部损坏和适度的执行漂移，但在过时或不正确的全局计划下仍然受到限制。我们的结果将交错的视觉语言轨迹定位为机器人策略中显式多模态推理的可扩展表示，同时将静态、完全观察的环境和初始规划延迟识别为当前的限制。Long-horizon robotic manipulation requires plans that are both logically coherent and geometrically grounded. Existing Vision-Language-Action policies usually hide planning in latent states or expose only one modality: text-only chain-of-thought encodes causal order but misses spatial constraints, while visual prediction provides geometric cues but often remains local and semantically underconstrained. We introduce Interleaved Vision–Language Reasoning (IVLR), a policy framework built around IVLR-Trace, an explicit intermediate representation that alternates textual subgoals with visual keyframes over the full task horizon. At test time, a single native multimodal transformer self-generates this global semantic-geometric trace from the initial observation and instruction, caches it, and conditions a closed-loop action decoder on the trace, original instruction, and current observation. Because standard robot datasets lack such traces, we construct pseudo-supervision by temporally segmenting demonstrations and captioning each stage with a vision-language model. Across simulated benchmarks for long-horizon manipulation and visual distribution shift, IVLR reaches 95.5% average success on LIBERO, including 92.4% on LIBERO-Long, and 59.4% overall success on SimplerEnv-WidowX. Ablations show that both modalities are necessary: without traces, LIBERO-Long success drops to 37.7%; text-only and vision-only traces reach 62.0% and 68.4%, while the full interleaved trace reaches 92.4%. Stress tests with execution perturbations and masked trace content show moderate degradation, suggesting that the trace can tolerate local corruption and moderate execution drift, but remains limited under stale or incorrect global plans. Our results position interleaved vision-language traces as a scalable representation for explicit multimodal reasoning in robot policies, while identifying static, fully observed environments and initial planning latency as current limitations.

1 简介Introduction

长视距机器人操纵不仅仅是选择下一个运动命令的问题。机器人必须保留任务的因果顺序，同时将每个抽象子目标扎根于当前场景的几何结构中。在诸如在操作第二个对象之前放置杯子之类的任务中，策略必须记住哪个对象首先重要，它应该放置在哪里，以及场景在每次中间交互之后应该如何演变。因果一致性和几何基础这两个要求很难用纯粹的反应性观察语言动作映射来满足。Long-horizon robotic manipulation is not only a problem of choosing the next motor command. A robot must preserve the causal order of a task while grounding each abstract subgoal in the geometry of the current scene. In a task such as placing a mug before manipulating a second object, the policy must remember which object matters first, where it should be placed, and how the scene should evolve after each intermediate interaction. These two requirements, causal coherence and geometric grounding, are difficult to satisfy with a purely reactive observation-language action mapping.

最近的视觉-语言-行动（VLA）政策通过扩展机器人数据和基础模型主干取得了快速进展Recent Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies have made rapid progress by scaling robot data and foundation-model backbones[3, 33, 12, 2, 18, 19]。然而，大多数政策仍然将其长期推理隐含在隐藏状态中。这种设计对于许多短期任务是有效的，但它没有为学习者提供明确的结构来表示中间任务状态、因果依赖性或可恢复计划。因此，当指令跨越多个阶段时，直接策略可能会暂时变得短视：它们可能会贪婪地执行视觉上显着的后续步骤，或者无法维持遥远目标所需的状态。. However, most policies still keep their long-horizon reasoning implicit in hidden states. This design is effective for many short-horizon tasks, but it gives the learner no explicit structure for representing intermediate task state, causal dependencies, or recoverable plans. As a result, when the instruction spans multiple stages, direct policies can become temporally myopic: they may greedily execute a visually salient later step or fail to maintain the state needed for a distant goal.

显式推理方法暴露了这个隐藏过程的一部分，但通常通过单一模式。文本思想链和分层语言计划为因果分解提供了有用的支架Explicit reasoning methods expose part of this hidden process, but usually through a single modality. Textual chain-of-thought and hierarchical language plans provide a useful scaffold for causal decomposition[1, 29, 31, 15]，但仅靠语言并不能指定低级控制器所需的空间姿态、接触几何形状或目标放置。视觉预测和世界模型政策提供几何线索, yet language alone does not specify the spatial pose, contact geometry, or target placement needed by a low-level controller. Visual prediction and world-model policies provide geometric cues[9, 4, 6]，但局部未来帧在语义上可能不明确：合理的图像不一定是用户预期因果序列中的正确步骤。这种互补性激发了在​​整个任务中携带两种模式的表示。, but local future frames can be semantically ambiguous: a plausible image need not be the correct step in the user’s intended causal sequence. This complementarity motivates a representation that carries both modalities over the whole task.

At the beginning of an episode, the model generates the complete trace from the initial observation and instruction. During execution, the generated trace is cached as context; at every control step, the action decoder receives the current observation, original instruction, and cached trace. The policy is therefore not an open-loop replay of generated keyframes: it remains closed-loop with respect to the live observation, while using the trace as long-horizon semantic-geometric context. We do not impose an explicit stage pointer; instead, the current observation conditions which parts of the cached trace are relevant.

首先，我们使用显式的 IVLR-Trace 制定长视野 VLA 控制，IVLR-Trace 是文本子目标和视觉关键帧的全视野序列，表示单个中间结构中的语义逻辑和几何基础。 其次，我们在本机多模态转换器中实例化此表示，该转换器生成一次跟踪，然后对缓存的跟踪、原始指令和当前观察进行闭环动作解码。 第三，我们引入了一种可扩展的伪迹构建管道，该管道通过分阶段视觉语言监督来改造非结构化机器人演示。 最后，我们在模拟操纵基准上表明，交错跟踪可显着提高长期成功，文本和视觉组件都是必要的，并且跟踪条件策略在受控执行扰动和跟踪损坏下仍然有效。First, we formulate long-horizon VLA control with an explicit IVLR-Trace, a full-horizon sequence of textual subgoals and visual keyframes that represents semantic logic and geometric grounding in a single intermediate structure. Second, we instantiate this representation in a native multimodal transformer that generates the trace once and then conditions closed-loop action decoding on the cached trace, original instruction, and current observation. Third, we introduce a scalable pseudo-trace construction pipeline that retrofits unstructured robot demonstrations with staged vision-language supervision. 机器人的文本推理。Textual reasoning for robots.

基于语言的机器人规划器将指令分解为可执行的子目标、代码、可供性评分的动作或思维链原理Language-based robot planners decompose instructions into executable subgoals, code, affordance-scored actions, or chain-of-thought rationales[1, 10, 22, 29, 31, 15]。它们的优势在于因果抽象：文本可以命名对象、描述顺序并解释意图。它们的弱点是文本本身不能解决操作的详细几何问题。 IVLR-Trace 保留了这个因果支架，但将每个阶段与视觉关键帧配对，为动作解码器提供空间锚点而不是纯粹的语言指令。. Their strength is causal abstraction: text can name objects, describe order, and explain intent. Their weakness is that text does not by itself resolve the detailed geometry of manipulation. IVLR-Trace keeps this causal scaffold but pairs every stage with a visual keyframe, giving the action decoder a spatial anchor rather than a purely linguistic instruction.

视觉预测和世界模型。Visual prediction and world models.

视觉预测策略和世界模型 VLA 学习想象未来的观察或潜在的动态Visual predictive policies and world-model VLAs learn to imagine future observations or latent dynamics[9, 4, 6]。这些方法自然地以几何学为基础，并且可以表示与接触相关的场景变化。然而，这种预测大部分是局部的或后退的地平线，没有语言的视觉未来可能会转向看似合理但任务不正确的状态。在长期操纵中，这种模糊性很重要，因为视觉上可行的未来可能对应于错误的子目标顺序。 IVLR 的不同之处在于生成语义字幕和 RGB 关键帧的全视野交错，因此预测的视觉状态受到预期任务序列的约束，而不仅仅是短期视觉合理性的约束。. These methods are naturally grounded in geometry and can represent contact-relevant scene changes. However, much of this prediction is local or receding-horizon, and visual futures without language can drift toward plausible but task-incorrect states. In long-horizon manipulation, this ambiguity matters because a visually feasible future may correspond to the wrong subgoal order. IVLR differs by generating a full-horizon interleaving of semantic captions and RGB keyframes, so the predicted visual states are constrained by the intended task sequence rather than only by short-term visual plausibility.

Chameleon、Transfusion 和 Show-o/Show-o2 等原生多模态模型统一了单个转换器中文本和图像的理解和生成Native multimodal models such as Chameleon, Transfusion, and Show-o/Show-o2 unify understanding and generation across text and images in a single transformer[5, 32, 26, 27]。机器人工作已经开始将这一想法应用到动作生成中，包括UniVLA、dVLA、EO-1以及相关的统一政策. 5 讨论和限制Discussion and limitations

结果支持表示级结论：暴露全视野交错轨迹使得长视野 VLA 推理比将所有中间结构留在潜在激活中更可控。痕迹不仅仅是一个计划，也不仅仅是一个记忆。它是一个语义几何接口，让策略根据实时观察和预期未来状态的结构化描述来调节每个闭环操作。The results support a representation-level conclusion: exposing a full-horizon interleaved trace makes long-horizon VLA reasoning more controllable than leaving all intermediate structure in latent activations. The trace is not merely a plan and not merely a memory. It is a semantic-geometric interface that lets the policy condition each closed-loop action on both the live observation and a structured description of intended future states.

范围被故意限制。我们在模拟操纵基准中进行评估，包括 SimplerEnv 作为视觉分布转移的代理，但我们不声称真实的机器人部署。当生成跟踪时，该方法假设有一个静态的、完全观察到的工作空间。当前的压力测试涵盖了丢失的本地跟踪内容和适度的执行位移，但没有涵盖导致全局跟踪无效的不利的错误阶段顺序、对象删除或场景更改。如果场景在规划后发生变化，如果目标最初未被观察到，或者如果另一个代理介入，则缓存的跟踪可能会变得过时。未来的工作应该通过滑动窗口重新规划或自触发重新规划来扩展 IVLR，从而能够在动态场景中修改轨迹，以及不确定性感知轨迹修正和真实机器人验证。The scope is deliberately limited. We evaluate in simulated manipulation benchmarks, including SimplerEnv as a proxy for visual distribution shift, but we do not claim real-robot deployment. The method assumes a static, fully observed workspace when the trace is generated. The current stress tests cover missing local trace content and moderate execution displacement, but not adversarially wrong stage order, object removal, or scene changes that invalidate the global trace. If the scene changes after planning, if targets are initially unobserved, or if another agent intervenes, the cached trace may become stale. Future work should extend IVLR with sliding-window replanning or self-triggered replanning, enabling the trace to be revised in dynamic scenes, along with uncertainty-aware trace revision and real-robot validation.
