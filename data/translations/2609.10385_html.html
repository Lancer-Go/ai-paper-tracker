摘要Abstract

动画和视觉特效预制作审查要求团队翻译松散指定的内容 创意意图——简报、不断发展的规范、异质参考文献和口头表达 决策——进行修改，初级艺术家可以执行而无需重复澄清。 在实践中，标准会随着迭代而变化，审查判断会失去证据基础， 而且请求背后的理由很少能在高级与初级交接后幸存下来。我们 贡献一个Animation and VFX pre-production review requires teams to translate loosely specified creative intent—briefs, evolving specifications, heterogeneous references, and verbal decisions—into revisions that junior artists can execute without repeated clarification. In practice, criteria drift across iterations, review judgments lose their evidential basis, and the reasoning behind a request rarely survives the senior–junior handoff. We contribute a意图-证据-行动一致性的设计框架design framework for intent–evidence–action alignment：意图被阐明到共享的项目记录中，判断以有根据的证据为基础，授权的决定被转换为直接与参考笔记相关的明确的修订任务。我们实例化这个框架: intent is articulated into a shared project record, judgments are anchored to grounded evidence, and authorized decisions are converted into clear revision tasks tied directly to reference notes. We instantiate this framework in月球漫步MOONWALK, 专业人士 生产前审查系统，包括共享意图记录、参考/规范 锚定、结构化的进行中工作比较和主管授权的行动 规划。在此工作流程中，人工智能负责管理协调——标记缺失的上下文并组织笔记——而艺术家则保留完整的创作方向。安, a professional pre-production review system comprising a shared intent record, reference/specification anchoring, structured work-in-progress comparison, and supervisor-authorized action planning. In this workflow, AI handles administrative coordination—flagging missing context and organizing notes—while artists retain full creative direction. 1 简介INTRODUCTION

2D 动画和视觉特效中的预制作审查是一个重复的协调过程：Pre-production review in 2D animation and VFX is a repeated coordination process: a导演director提供简介、参考资料或视觉目标；一个provides a brief, references, or visual goals; a主管supervisor在审查后续版本时解释该方向；和一个interprets that direction while reviewing successive versions; and a初级 艺术家junior artist修改工作。现有的制作工具已经组织了镜头、资源、 版本、注释和批准，最近的人工智能系统可以生成评论或比较 视觉材料。然而工作室审查的困难部分通常不是存储反馈 也不会生产更多。它保留了主管的意思、证据 定义了该意图，以及初级美术师应如何在迭代交接中对此采取行动revises the work. Existing production tools already organize shots, assets, versions, notes, and approvals, and recent AI systems can generate comments or compare visual material. Yet the difficult part of studio review is often neither storing feedback nor producing more of it. It is preserving what the supervisor meant, what evidence defines that intent, and how the junior artist should act on it across iterative handoffs[1, 2].

Supervisors must make missing specifications, references, and the relevant object, region, or property explicit; junior artists must be able to inspect those anchors, resolve ambiguity, and record intentional deviations. That interpretation then travels with the work into the next review. AI supports this exchange through evidence-bounded checks and clarification prompts, abstaining when the project record does not specify a criterion.

月球漫步MOONWALK使用仅限于协调的人工智能实例化该框架：请求缺少的参考或规范，提取相应的参考图像或规范条款，对照提交的工作检查规定的要求，提出未解决的要求，以及组织主管授权的修订。人工智能不会引入审美标准，也不会超出项目记录和明确的人类判断之外独立判断灯光、颜色、构图或风格。instantiates this framework with AI limited to coordination: requesting missing references or specifications, pulling up the corresponding reference image or specification clause, checking stated requirements against submitted work, surfacing unresolved requirements, and organizing supervisor-authorized revisions. AI does not introduce aesthetic criteria or independently judge lighting, color, composition, or style beyond the project record and explicit human judgment.

7.1 意图-证据-行动作为协调问题Intent–Evidence–Action as a Coordination Problem

在形成性和总结性研究中，参与者反复强调相同的价值 协调属性：在期间保持活动规范和参考可用 审查，追踪判断的理由，并将优先行动记录返回给 艺术家。我们的评估表明，生产前审查应围绕 交接过程中意图、证据和行动的连续性。类似协调 游戏开发中可能出现的问题Across the formative and summative studies, participants repeatedly valued the same coordination properties: keeping the active specification and references available during review, tracing a judgment to its rationale, and returning a prioritized action record to the artist. Our evaluations indicate that pre-production review should be designed around the continuity of intent, evidence, and action across a handoff. Similar coordination problems may arise in game development[51]和其他 角色差异化的创意生产设置，但这些模式是否延伸到游戏开发等相邻领域还需要进一步的实证检验。and other role-differentiated creative production settings, but whether these patterns extend to adjacent domains like game development requires further empirical testing.

7.2 通过共享审阅记录进行协作Collaboration Through a Shared Review Record

协作问题是角色之间的信息不对称。监事持有 项目历史、客户背景和工艺知识可以压缩为几个部分 反馈的话；初级艺术家需要足够的背景才能进行表演，而无需重复 打断小组领导（第 6.1 节）。The collaboration problem is an information asymmetry between roles. Supervisors hold project history, client context, and craft knowledge that may be compressed into a few words of feedback; junior artists need enough of that context to act without repeatedly interrupting the group lead (Section 6.1).

原型还存储了艺术家对提交作品的诠释， 创造更丰富的合作机会：主管可以了解初级员工为什么能够取得成功 响应工件之前的选择。该研究没有单独测试明确的 确认步骤，两个角色在审核之前协商该解释。 未来的工作应该明确隔离添加相互确认步骤是否可以改善审核结果。The prototype also stored the artist’s interpretation with the submitted work, which creates an opportunity for richer collaboration: a supervisor can see why a junior made a choice before responding to the artifact. The study did not separately test an explicit confirmation step in which the two roles negotiated that interpretation before review. Future work should explicitly isolate whether adding a mutual confirmation step improves review outcomes.

7.3 同一系统内不同的角色需要不同的支持Different Roles Need Different Support Within the Same System

共享记录并不意味着相同的帮助。年轻艺术家重视明确 要求、相关参考、有界的下一步行动和完成条件。 资深从业者已经牢记了大部分标准，而是希望直接 识别缺失的证据以及需要注意的物体或区域；几个 发现笼统的总结“过于外交化”。A shared record does not imply identical assistance. Junior artists valued explicit requirements, relevant references, bounded next actions, and completion conditions. Senior practitioners already held much of the standard in mind and instead wanted direct identification of missing evidence and the object or region requiring attention; several found broad summaries “too diplomatic.”

相同的意图-证据-行动记录可以在不分裂的情况下支持这些差异 将工作流程转移到单独的系统中。对于一个The same intent–evidence–action record can support these differences without splitting the workflow into separate systems. For a高级senior，AI可以请求失踪 参考/规范，帮助识别分析目标，检索先前的决策，以及 整理注释。对于一个, AI can request a missing reference/specification, help identify the analysis target, retrieve prior decisions, and organize annotations. For a初级junior，它可以提醒他们规定的限制， 将 WIP 与引用的证据进行比较，表面客观遗漏，帮助找到 适用的简介/规格，并了解他们故意偏离的原因。那么这个解释 与 WIP 一起进入主管审查。虽然两个角色都访问相同的项目记录，但主管需要高级别的差异标记，而初级艺术家需要具体的、逐步的修订指导, it can remind them of stated constraints, compare the WIP with cited evidence, surface objective omissions, help locate the applicable brief/spec, and capture why they intentionally deviated. That explanation then travels with the WIP into supervisor review. While both roles access the same project record, supervisors require high-level discrepancy flags, whereas junior artists need specific, step-by-step revision guidance[2, 9].

7.4 同一协作循环中的代理和指导Agency and Mentorship in the Same Collaboration Loop

根据特定角色定制 UI 功能直接决定了在自动化协助期间如何维持创意代理。第 6.3 节中的过度依赖问题与之前的调查结果相呼应： 更多的人工智能参与会降低认知参与度和多样性Tailoring UI features to specific roles directly dictates how creative agency is maintained during automated assistance. The over-reliance concern in Section 6.3 echoes prior findings that greater AI involvement can reduce cognitive engagement and diversity[33, 52]。人工授权有助于保持 与从业者一起做出最终的生产决定，但该决定的质量仍然 取决于模型输出是否基于项目证据以及 艺术家可以质疑它。. Human authorization helps keep the final production decision with practitioners, but the quality of that decision still depends on whether the model output is grounded in project evidence and whether the artist can question it.

这种担忧与指导直接相关。减少例行澄清可以释放 高年级的时间用于更高价值的教学，但自动化也会减少互动 后辈们通过它学习默会标准。因此，生产部署应该 将例行证据检查与指导分开：前者可以得到协助，而后者则可以 后者需要持续的高年级与低年级的互动和解释。This concern connects directly to mentorship. Reducing routine clarification could free senior time for higher-value teaching, but automation could also reduce the interactions through which juniors learn tacit standards. A production deployment should therefore separate routine evidence checking from mentorship: the former can be assisted, while the latter requires continued senior–junior interaction and explanation.

7.5 视觉原生审查和现实世界采用Visual-Native Review and Real-World Adoption

参与者发现以文本为主的输出与视觉制作不匹配，并建议 清单旁边的修订后预览图像。这样的预览可以提出建议 改变可以目视检查，但这里没有实现。他们还期望 适合较大项目的工作流程以及创意指导后的领导到初级边界 成立（P3、P4、P9、P18）。主要的采用障碍是工具摩擦：并行 ShotGrid、Zulip 和现有管道工具之外的平台将难以维持 （P9、P10、P19）。因此，与生产管理基础设施的集成更加重要 比维护单独的审核平台更合理的部署路径。Participants found text-heavy output poorly matched to visual production and suggested a post-revision preview image beside the checklist. Such a preview could make a proposed change visually inspectable, but it was not implemented here. They also expected the workflow to fit larger projects and the lead-to-junior boundary after creative direction was established (P3, P4, P9, P18). The main adoption barrier was tool friction: a parallel platform beside ShotGrid, Zulip, and existing pipeline tools would be difficult to sustain (P9, P10, P19). Integration with production-management infrastructure is therefore a more plausible deployment path than maintaining a separate review platform.

7.6 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

评估范围和方法权衡。Evaluation Scope and Methodological Trade-offs.我们的评估被设计为单次探索性研究，以验证 MOONWALK 工作流程的直接可用性和可行性。虽然从业者的估计（例如，解决 70-80% 的基础问题）强调了强烈的主观效用，但它们代表了感知的功效而不是纵向生产日志。此外，为了评估整体的端到端交互，该研究优先将 MOONWALK 与基线聊天界面进行比较，而不是隔离单个系统组件（例如持久结构与人工智能功能）。未来的对照研究应纳入非人工智能结构化基线、平衡条件顺序以消除顺序效应，并跟踪多周生产管道的长期指标（例如实际返工时间和澄清周期）。Our evaluation was designed as a single-session, exploratory study to validate the immediate usability and feasibility of the MOONWALK workflow. While practitioner estimates (e.g., resolving 70–80% of foundational issues) highlight strong subjective utility, they represent perceived efficacy rather than longitudinal production logs. Furthermore, to evaluate the holistic end-to-end interaction, the study prioritized comparing MOONWALK against a baseline chat interface rather than isolating individual systemic components (such as persistent structure vs. AI capabilities). Future controlled studies should incorporate non-AI structured baselines, counterbalance condition orders to eliminate sequence effects, and track long-term metrics (e.g., actual rework hours and clarification cycles) across multi-week production pipelines.

证据记录的范围。Scope of the Evidential Record.MOONWALK 的分析能力在结构上受到其共享项目记录完整性的限制。除非明确导入，否则默认决策、未记录的口头交流和外部客户消息对系统仍然不可见。在纵向部署中，未捕获的上下文可能会因切换而变得复杂。未来的工作应优先考虑低摩擦上下文摄取，例如审核线程解析、会议记录摄取和管道管理工具（例如 ShotGrid）网络钩子，同时严格维护资产来源。MOONWALK’s analytical capabilities are structurally bounded by the completeness of its shared project record. Tacit decisions, unrecorded verbal exchanges, and external client messages remain invisible to the system unless explicitly imported. In longitudinal deployments, uncaptured context may compound across handoffs. Future work should prioritize low-friction context ingestion—such as review thread parsing, meeting transcript ingestion, and pipeline management tools (e.g., ShotGrid) webhooks—while rigorously maintaining asset provenance.

从原型实例化到生产部署。From Prototype Instantiation to Production Deployment.MOONWALK 通过一组特定的接口、模型和审查检查点来操作其框架。为了概括这些见解，未来的工作应该评估这种意图-证据-行动循环如何在不同的工作室文化、制作阶段和工具生态系统中扩展。有前景的技术扩展包括基于扩散的视觉修订预览和特定领域的感知模型；然而，这种扩展必须保留核心边界：人工智能应该浮现候选人的观察结果，而可操作的审查决策仍然可以严格追溯到共享证据并得到人类从业者的授权。MOONWALK operationalizes its framework through a specific set of interfaces, models, and review checkpoints. To generalize these insights, future work should evaluate how this intent–evidence–action loop scales across different studio cultures, production stages, and tool ecosystems. Promising technical extensions include diffusion-based visual revision previews and domain-specific perceptual models; however, such extensions must preserve the core boundary: AI should surface candidate observations, while actionable review decisions remain strictly traceable to shared evidence and authorized by human practitioners.

8 结论CONCLUSION

我们通过 MOONWALK（一个有效的预制作审查系统）为专业初级美术师工作流程提出了意图-证据-行动设计框架。通过形成性研究和受试者内部工作室评估，从业者重视集成系统的持续规范和参考、与证据相关的审查记录以及可供执行者使用的清单；我们的评估揭示了工具使用中基于角色的差异、感知控制的限制以及过度依赖的风险。这些发现支持 MOONWALK 作为评估任务和材料中非结构化对话人工智能的结构化替代方案。他们没有孤立单个机制的贡献，没有确定人工智能在结构化审查支持之外是必要的，也没有评估明确的主管-艺术家解释检查点。更广泛地说，这项工作为协作创意系统提供了一个设计机会：保留明确的意图如何以证据为基础，并转化为主管批准的可操作的修订任务，同时保持专业人士的审美权威和最终判断。We presented an intent–evidence–action design framework for professional junior–supervisor artists workflow by MOONWALK, a working pre-production review system. Across a formative study and a within-subject in-studio evaluation, practitioners valued the integrated system’s persistent specifications and references, evidence-linked review records, and executor-ready checklists; our evaluation revealed role-based differences in tool usage, limits on perceived control, and risks of over-reliance. These findings support MOONWALK as a structured alternative to unstructured conversational AI within the evaluated tasks and materials. They do not isolate the contribution of individual mechanisms, establish that AI is necessary beyond structured review support, or evaluate an explicit supervisor–artist interpretation checkpoint. More broadly, the work identifies a design opportunity for collaborative creative systems: preserve how articulated intent is grounded in evidence and translated into supervisor-approved actionable revision tasks, while keeping aesthetic authority and final judgment with professionals.
