1 简介Introduction

\IEEEPAR开始\IEEEPARstart

在交会、邻近操作、在轨服务和空间态势感知中需要对非合作航天器进行精确的 6D 位姿感知。单目相机在这些任务中很有吸引力，因为它以有限的硬件复杂性提供紧凑的测量，但图像仍然必须支持目标旋转和平移的恢复。航天器与许多地面姿态估计目标不同：大的光滑面板、薄的结构元素、重复或对称的部分、硬阴影和高对比度背景经常一起出现。因此，从单个 RGB 图像中获得的视觉证据可能很弱、分布不均匀或部分被遮挡。Accurate 6D pose sensing of non-cooperative spacecraft is required in rendezvous, proximity operation, on-orbit servicing, and space situational awareness. A monocular camera is attractive in these tasks because it provides compact measurements with limited hardware complexity, but the image must still support recovery of the target rotation and translation. Spacecraft differ from many terrestrial pose-estimation targets: large smooth panels, thin structural elements, repeated or symmetric parts, hard shadows, and high-contrast backgrounds often appear together. As a result, the visual evidence available from a single RGB image can be weak, unevenly distributed, or partially occluded.

基于学习的 RGB 姿态估计器使密集的特定于对象的几何结构可在单目设置中使用。几何引导的直接回归在这里特别重要，因为它保留了密集的中间预测，同时避免了测试时的外部 PnP 求解器。 GDR-Net 遵循以下路线：它根据 RoI 图像预测几何表示并将其输入 Patch-PnP 进行最终姿态回归Learning-based RGB pose estimators have made dense object-specific geometry usable in monocular settings. Geometry-guided direct regression is particularly relevant here because it retains dense intermediate predictions while avoiding an external PnP solver at test time. GDR-Net follows this route: it predicts geometric representations from the RoI image and feeds them to Patch-PnP for final pose regression[1]。对于航天器图像来说，仍然存在两个限制。密集坐标和掩模预测必须利用粗糙的航天器布局和小边界线索，但照明变化和弱纹理可以抑制后者。 Patch-PnP 还在空间编码后聚合几何证据，其中遥远但相关的区域（例如主体和扩展面板）可能无法仅通过局部卷积充分交互。. For spacecraft imagery, two limitations remain. Dense coordinate and mask prediction must draw on both the coarse spacecraft layout and small boundary cues, yet illumination changes and weak texture can suppress the latter. Patch-PnP also aggregates geometric evidence after spatial encoding, where distant but related regions, such as the main body and extended panels, may not interact sufficiently through local convolutions alone.

GAP-GDRNet 保留 GDR-Net 输入输出设置并更改内部表示路径。在密集预测之前，基于注意力的特征细化（AFR）将全局分组坐标注意力分支与中值增强的局部特征分支相结合。前者强调长程结构；后者保留边界、轮廓和弱纹理响应。在 Patch-PnP 内部，补丁级几何自注意力（PGSA）在最终回归层之前将下采样的几何标记关联起来。这些变化的目标是特征形成和块级几何聚合，而推理仍然仅使用单目 RGB 图像、相机内在函数和目标 RoI。GAP-GDRNet keeps the GDR-Net input-output setting and changes the internal representation path. Before dense prediction, attention-based feature refinement (AFR) combines a global grouped coordinate attention branch with a median-enhanced local feature branch. The former emphasizes long-range structure; the latter preserves boundary, contour, and weak-texture responses. Inside Patch-PnP, patch-level geometric self-attention (PGSA) relates downsampled geometric tokens before the final regression layers. These changes target feature formation and patch-level geometric aggregation, while inference still uses only the monocular RGB image, camera intrinsics, and target RoI.

由于具有准确 6D 位姿和像素级几何形状的真实航天器图像很少，因此所需的密集监督是通过基于 Blender 的渲染和注释过程获得的。生成的数据集包含一个目标航天器模型的 50,000 个渲染图像，其中姿态、照明、背景和非目标遮挡存在变化。训练、验证和测试图像是通过场景因素的组合来分割的，而不是通过纯粹的图像级随机洗牌来分割。这个单目标合成航天器数据集是主要的评估设置。 BOP 基准测试中的 T-LESS 和 LM-O 仅用作补充公共数据集，以检查相同的模块是否改进了无纹理和遮挡非航天器物体上再现的 GDR-Net 基线The required dense supervision is obtained through a Blender-based rendering and annotation process, since real spacecraft images with accurate 6D pose and pixel-level geometry are scarce. The resulting dataset contains 50,000 rendered images of one target spacecraft model, with variations in pose, illumination, background, and non-target occlusion. Training, validation, and test images are split by combinations of scene factors rather than by a purely image-level random shuffle. This single-target synthetic spacecraft dataset is the main evaluation setting. T-LESS and LM-O from the BOP benchmark are used only as supplementary public datasets to check whether the same modules improve the reproduced GDR-Net baseline on textureless and occluded non-spacecraft objects[2].

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 基于单目 RGB 的 6D 物体姿态估计Monocular RGB-Based 6D Object Pose Estimation

单目 6D 物体姿态估计从单个 RGB 图像中寻求目标物体的 3D 旋转和平移。基于学习的方法通常直接回归姿势、估计 2D 关键点或预测密集对应关系。 PoseCNN 将杂乱场景中的对象分割和姿势预测结合起来Monocular 6D object pose estimation seeks the 3D rotation and translation of a target object from a single RGB image. Learning-based methods typically regress pose directly, estimate 2D keypoints, or predict dense correspondences. PoseCNN combines object segmentation and pose prediction in cluttered scenes[3]。 Single Shot Pose 和 PVNet 依赖于 2D 关键点或基于投票的定位. Single Shot Pose and PVNet rely on 2D keypoints or voting-based localization[4, 5]。基于坐标和对应关系的方法，包括 CDPN、Pix2Pose 和 DPOD，在恢复姿势之前预测密集的几何表示. Coordinate- and correspondence-based approaches, including CDPN, Pix2Pose, and DPOD, predict dense geometric representations before recovering the pose[6, 7, 8]。他们的共同点是在最终姿态估计之前将 RGB 外观转换为中间几何形式。. Their common thread is that RGB appearance is converted into an intermediate geometric form before the final pose estimate.

弱纹理、对称性和遮挡下的鲁棒性已在后来的几个系统中得到解决。 EPOS 显式处理对象对称性Robustness under weak texture, symmetry, and occlusion has been addressed in several later systems. EPOS explicitly handles object symmetries[9]; ZebraPose 和 SurfEmb 以不同形式编码表面信息; ZebraPose and SurfEmb encode surface information in different forms[10, 11]; PFA 使用透视流聚合进行数据有限的姿态估计; PFA uses perspective-flow aggregation for data-limited pose estimation[12]。 GDR-Net 最接近目前的工作，因为它可以预测密集的几何表示，并在完全学习的管道中使用 Patch-PnP 回归姿势. GDR-Net is closest to the present work because it predicts dense geometric representations and regresses the pose with Patch-PnP in a fully learned pipeline[1]。后来的几何引导变体进一步支持在基于 RGB 的姿态估计中使用密集的中间几何体. Later geometry-guided variants further support the use of dense intermediate geometry in RGB-based pose estimation[13]。 GAP-GDRNet 保留了这种范例，但修改了弱纹理航天器目标的特征细化和补丁级关系建模。. GAP-GDRNet keeps this paradigm but modifies feature refinement and patch-level relation modeling for weakly textured spacecraft targets.

2.2 航天器姿态估计和综合数据Spacecraft Pose Estimation and Synthetic Data

非合作航天器位姿估计已被研究用于交会、邻近操作和在轨服务。视觉目标可能包括光滑的面板、薄的附属物、镜面或阴影区域以及大的视点变化，因此几何形状和外观变化都很重要。基于神经网络的姿态估计已被探索用于非合作交会Non-cooperative spacecraft pose estimation has been studied for rendezvous, proximity operation, and on-orbit servicing. The visual target may include smooth panels, thin appendages, specular or shadowed regions, and large viewpoint changes, so both geometry and appearance variation matter. Neural-network-based pose estimation has been explored for non-cooperative rendezvous[14]。 SPEED+ 提供跨域间隙的航天器图像. SPEED+ provides spacecraft images across a domain gap[15]，SPNv2研究领域转移下的多任务学习和在线细化, and SPNv2 studies multi-task learning and online refinement under domain shift[16]。这些研究激发了航天器特定的姿态估计器，这些估计器不会将目标视为通用的纹理对象。. These studies motivate spacecraft-specific pose estimators that do not treat the target as a generic textured object.

对于真实的航天器图像来说，很难获得准确的 6D 位姿标签和像素级几何形状，这使得合成渲染成为实用的监督来源。域随机化和图像转换已用于减少机器人感知中的模拟与真实差距Accurate 6D pose labels and pixel-level geometry are difficult to obtain for real spacecraft images, which makes synthetic rendering a practical source of supervision. Domain randomization and image translation have been used to reduce simulation-to-real gaps in robotic perception[17, 18, 19]。在这里，渲染的使用范围更窄：它提供 GDR-Net 式训练目标所需的目标蒙版、可见区域蒙版、密集模型坐标图、相机内在函数和 6D 姿态标签。没有使用真实捕获的航天器图像，因此该论文没有声称模拟真实的航天器性能。. Here, rendering is used more narrowly: it supplies target masks, visible-region masks, dense model-coordinate maps, camera intrinsics, and 6D pose labels needed by the GDR-Net-style training objective. No real captured spacecraft images are used, and the paper therefore does not claim sim-to-real spacecraft performance.

2.3 特征注意力和几何引导姿势回归Feature Attention and Geometry-Guided Pose Regression

注意力模块通常用于在预测之前重塑特征响应。 SE 和 ECA 重新校准通道Attention modules are often used to reshape feature responses before prediction. SE and ECA recalibrate channels[20, 21]; CBAM、协调注意力和相关的空间通道模块将区域重点与通道交互结合起来; CBAM, Coordinate Attention, and related spatial-channel modules combine regional emphasis with channel interaction[22, 23, 24, 25]。非本地操作提供了一种对远程依赖关系进行建模的方法. Non-local operations provide a way to model long-range dependencies[26]，ConvNeXt 提供了现代卷积主干, and ConvNeXt offers a modern convolutional backbone[27]。对于航天器姿态感测，姿态模糊性与全局结构相关，而密集坐标取决于局部轮廓和弱纹理区域。 AFR 是围绕这种分割而设计的，通过在几何头之前细化全局和局部特征。. For spacecraft pose sensing, attitude ambiguity is tied to global structure, while dense coordinates depend on local contours and weak-texture regions. AFR is designed around this split by refining global and local features before the geometric heads.

最终姿态恢复通常由 EPnP 等 PnP 解算器的 2D-3D 对应关系制定，通常与 RANSAC 配对Final pose recovery is commonly formulated from 2D–3D correspondences with PnP solvers such as EPnP, often paired with RANSAC[28, 29]。 GDR-Net 使用 Patch-PnP 从密集几何预测和 RoI 坐标编码中回归姿势. GDR-Net instead uses Patch-PnP to regress pose from dense geometric predictions and RoI coordinate encoding[1]。一旦以这种形式学习姿势恢复，块级几何证据的聚合就成为模型设计的一部分。 PGSA 通过在回归之前关联补丁特征来解决该步骤，而旋转输出遵循连续的 6D 表示. Once pose recovery is learned in this form, the aggregation of patch-level geometric evidence becomes part of the model design. PGSA addresses that step by relating patch features before regression, while the rotation output follows the continuous 6D representation[30].

5 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 GAP-GDRNet，用于在单目标合成航天器数据集上进行基于单目 RGB 的姿态感知。该框架保留了 GDR-Net 的几何引导回归管道并修改了两个阶段。 AFR 通过将 GGCA 的远程结构线索与 MECS 的局部轮廓和弱纹理响应相结合，在密集几何预测之前细化特征。 PGSA 被插入到 Patch-PnP 中，以便在最终姿态回归之前关联面片级几何特征。在推理时，该方法仅使用 RGB 图像、相机内在函数和目标 RoI；深度、掩模和真实几何注释不需要作为输入。This article introduced GAP-GDRNet for monocular RGB-based pose sensing on a single-target synthetic spacecraft dataset. The framework keeps the geometry-guided regression pipeline of GDR-Net and modifies two stages. AFR refines features before dense geometric prediction by combining long-range structural cues from GGCA with local contour and weak-texture responses from MECS. PGSA is inserted into Patch-PnP to relate patch-level geometric features before final pose regression. At inference time, the method uses only the RGB image, camera intrinsics, and target RoI; depth, masks, and ground-truth geometric annotations are not required as inputs.
