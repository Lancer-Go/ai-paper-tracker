StartupBench：根据市场验证的端到端工作流程对通用代理进行基准测试StartupBench: Benchmarking General-Purpose Agents on Market-Validated End-to-End Workflows

2026 年 8 月 18 日August 18, 2026
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）和代理的最新进展极大地提高了人工智能系统执行复杂任务的能力。然而，现有的基准在很大程度上依赖于研究人员选择的任务，因此不确定这种进展是否会扩展到现实世界用户实际要求人工智能系统完成的工作。我们介绍Recent advances in Large Language Models(LLMs) and agents have substantially improved the ability of AI systems to execute complex tasks. Yet existing benchmarks largely rely on researcher-selected tasks, leaving uncertain whether such progress extends to the work that real-world users actually demand from AI systems. We introduce启动台StartupBench，一个基于经过市场验证的人工智能初创公司产品的端到端代理基准测试。我们不是根据关于有用代理能力的预定义假设来定义任务，而是系统地研究已被采用的人工智能产品及其产品工作流程和用户，以确定人工智能在不同专业领域已建立实际需求的现实世界任务。我们将这些工作流程转化为完整的以可交付成果为导向的任务，并使用捕捉其复杂需求的细粒度标准对其进行评估。在统一代理工具下评估的代表性模型中，即使是最强的模型也只能成功完成 StartupBench 的大约 30%，尽管在许多任务上取得了实质性的部分进展。进一步的分析将复杂的指令遵循和特定领域的专业知识等方面确定为失败的主要来源。我们的结果表明，许多经过市场验证的工作流程仍然超出了当前通用代理的可靠能力，将 StartupBench 建立为现实世界用户任务端到端完成进度的实证衡量标准。, an E2E agent benchmark grounded in market-validated AI startup products. Rather than defining tasks from pre-defined assumptions about useful agent capabilities, we systematically study AI products with demonstrated adoption, together with their product workflows and users, to identify real-world tasks for which AI has established practical demand across diverse professional domains. We translate these workflows into complete deliverable-oriented tasks and evaluate them with fine-grained rubrics capturing their complex requirements. Across representative models evaluated under a unified agent harness, even the strongest model successfully completes only approximately 30% of StartupBench, despite making substantial partial progress on many tasks. Further analysis identifies aspects like complex instruction following and domain-specific expertise as major sources of failure. Our results reveal that many market-validated workflows remain beyond the reliable capabilities of current general-purpose agents, establishing StartupBench as an empirical measure of progress toward E2E completions of real-world user tasks.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地从主要检索信息或回答问题的系统演变而来Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly evolving from systems that primarily retrieve information or answer questions[5, 12, 26]成为能够完成复杂的现实世界任务的代理。当前的人工智能系统预计将执行端到端（E2E）工作流程，协调多种功能，并在现实约束下生成满足实际需求的可交付成果。随着人工智能越来越多地融入专业工作，自主完成用户委托的任务已成为衡量模型能力的重要指标。因此，评估人工智能系统是否能够可靠地将其功能转化为专业可用的交付成果是下一代基准测试的核心挑战。into agents capable of completing complex real-world tasks. Current AI systems are expected to execute end-to-end (E2E) workflows, coordinate multiple capabilities, and produce deliverables that satisfy practical requirements under realistic constraints. As AI becomes increasingly integrated into professional work, autonomously completing user-delegated tasks has become an important measure of model capability. Evaluating whether AI systems can reliably translate their capabilities into professionally usable deliverables is therefore a central challenge for the next generation of benchmarks.

最近的基准测试在评估现实设置下的端到端任务执行方面取得了重要进展。然而，仍然存在一些重要的限制。首先，许多基准任务仍然很大程度上是从研究人员的角度定义的，而不是基于通过现实世界的人工智能采用证明了实际需求的工作流程Recent benchmarks have made important progress toward evaluating E2E task execution under realistic settings. Nevertheless, several important limitations remain. First, many benchmark tasks are still largely defined from researchers’ perspectives rather than grounded in workflows that have demonstrated practical demand through real-world AI adoption[19, 18, 12]，限制了他们反映真实用户需求和可交付要求的能力。其次，尽管有几个基准评估了完整的可交付成果, limiting their ability to reflect authentic user needs and deliverable requirements. Second, although several benchmarks evaluate complete deliverables[24, 23]，他们的评估协议通常比可交付成果本身要粗糙得多。现实世界的工作产品通常涉及大量功能、结构、格式和特定​​领域的要求，而整体评估无法忠实地评估这些要求。因此，现有基准仍然只提供有限的证据, their evaluation protocols are often substantially coarser than the deliverables themselves. Real-world work products typically involve numerous functional, structural, formatting, and domain-specific requirements that holistic evaluations cannot faithfully assess. Consequently, existing benchmarks still provide only limited evidence of当前模型和代理如何满足复杂的现实用户需求并可靠地生成专业可用的交付成果how well current models and agents can satisfy complex real-world user requirements and reliably produce professionally usable deliverables.

为了解决这些限制，我们观察到人工智能原生初创公司提供了现实世界人工智能工作流程的天然来源：他们的产品已经通过现实世界的采用和商业需求得到验证，提供了用户重视并寻求将这些任务委托给人工智能的直接证据。因此我们介绍To address these limitations, we observe that AI-native startups offer a natural source of real-world AI workflows: their products have been validated through real-world adoption and commercial demand, providing direct evidence that users value and seek to delegate these tasks to AI. We therefore introduce启动台StartupBench，一个由调查和访谈驱动的基准，利用这些工作流程来捕获跨领域的实际 E2E 用户需求。 StartupBench 涵盖 6 个主要领域的 97 项任务，并围绕以下设计原则构建：, a survey- and interview-driven benchmark that draws on these workflows to capture realistic E2E user requirements across domains. StartupBench spans 97 tasks across 6 primary domains and is built around the following design principles:

  • •

    以采用为基础的任务采购。Adoption-grounded task sourcing.我们系统地调查人工智能原生初创公司及其产品，并辅以产品演示和用户访谈，以确定具有实际需求的工作流程。然后，我们将这些工作流程构建为基准任务，以保留其核心用户目标和实际需求。We systematically survey AI-native startups and their products, complemented by product demonstrations and user interviews, to identify workflows with demonstrated real-world demand. We then construct these workflows into benchmark tasks that preserve their core user objectives and practical requirements.

  • •

    E2E 可交付成果。E2E deliverables.每项任务都需要生成用户最终在实际工作流程中期望的完整可交付成果，而不是中间输出或简化的演示。Each task requires producing the complete deliverables that users ultimately expect in realistic workflows, rather than intermediate outputs or simplified demonstrations.

  • •

    基于细粒度的评估。Fine-grained rubric-based evaluation.每个 StartupBench 任务都使用多个独立的评估标准进行评估，涵盖预期交付成果的不同维度，包括功能、结构、格式和特定领域的质量，确保对现实世界任务完成情况进行全面、忠实的评估。Each StartupBench task is evaluated using multiple independent rubrics covering distinct dimensions of the expected deliverable, including functionality, structure, formatting, and domain-specific quality, ensuring a comprehensive and faithful assessment of real-world task completion.

我们在统一代理工具下评估 StartupBench 上的代表性模型。结果表明，即使是最强大的模型也只能成功完成约 30% 的基准任务，这表明当前的通用系统距离可靠地生成专业可用的交付成果还很遥远。不同领域的表现差异很大，其中金融、STEM 和计算机科学以及教育和人文学科尤其具有挑战性。更深入的分析进一步将这些失败主要归因于复杂指令遵循和特定领域专业知识的限制，突出了未来基础模型和代理系统的有希望的方向。We evaluate representative models on StartupBench under unified agentic harness. Results show that even the strongest model successfully completes only about 30% of benchmark tasks, indicating that current general-purpose systems remain far from reliably producing professionally usable deliverables. Performance varies substantially across domains, with Finance, STEM & Computer Science and Education & Humanities proving particularly challenging. Deeper analysis further attributes these failures primarily to limitations in complex instruction following and domain-specific expertise, highlighting promising directions for future foundation models and agent systems.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 商业人工智能代理和垂直工作流程Commercial AI agents and Vertical Workflows

基础模型的最新进展使人工智能代理逐渐获得规划、工具使用、文件处理和多步骤执行能力Recent advances in foundation models have enabled AI agents to gradually acquire planning, tool-use, file-processing, and multi-step execution capabilities(Yao et al. 2023; Schick et al. 2023)，将人工智能系统从一般的会话助理推向以工作流程为导向的产品。在实践中，商业人工智能产品越来越多地将这些能力打包到文档处理、数据分析、金融研究、企业运营和知识工作等垂直工作流程中。这一趋势表明，现实世界的人工智能价值不仅取决于孤立的模型功能，还取决于这些功能是否可以产品化为有用的工作产品。然而，现有的学术基准很少使用商业采用本身作为任务发现的来源。 StartupBench 通过从经过市场验证的 AI 原生初创公司及其深度用户那里派生任务，将任务构建从研究人员定义的设置转向需求驱动的工作流程构建，从而解决了这一差距。, pushing AI systems from general conversational assistants toward workflow-oriented products. In practice, commercial AI products increasingly package these capabilities into vertical workflows such as document processing, data analysis, financial research, enterprise operations, and knowledge work. This trend suggests that real-world AI value is not determined only by isolated model capabilities, but also by whether these capabilities can be productized into useful work products. However, existing academic benchmarks rarely use commercial adoption itself as a source of task discovery. StartupBench addresses this gap by deriving tasks from market-validated AI-native startups and their deep users, moving task construction from researcher-defined settings toward demand-driven workflow construction.

2.2 AI代理的端到端评估End-to-end evaluation of AI agents

代理基准已从能力评估发展到端到端任务完成情况。早期的基准测试主要关注工具使用、多步推理和一般助理能力，如 AgentBench 和 GAIAAgent benchmarks have evolved from capability evaluation toward end-to-end task completion. Early benchmarks mainly focus on tool use, multi-step reasoning, and general assistant abilities, as in AgentBench and GAIA(Liu et al. 2024; Mialon et al. 2024)。后来的工作将评估扩展到更现实的交互环境，包括网络交互和计算机使用任务. Later work extends evaluation to more realistic interactive environments, including web interaction and computer-use tasks(Zhou et al. 2024; Xie et al. 2024)。最近的基准进一步转向专业工作和具有经济意义的任务，涵盖企业软件、模拟工作场所、职业任务和长期工作流程. More recent benchmarks further move toward professional work and economically meaningful tasks, covering enterprise software, simulated workplaces, occupational tasks, and long-horizon workflows(Drouin et al.; Xu et al. 2026; Patwardhan et al. 2025; Hu et al. 2026; Zhu et al. 2026; Meng et al. 2026)。这些工作反映了从孤立的能力测试到现实的任务完成评估的明显转变。 StartupBench 遵循这个方向，但其任务来源有所不同：它不是主要依赖公共环境、职业分类或模拟工作场所，而是从 AI 原生初创公司产品、ToB 用户需求和预期业务交付成果中派生任务。. These works reflect a clear transition from isolated capability tests to realistic task-completion evaluation. StartupBench follows this direction, but differs in its task source: rather than relying primarily on public environments, occupational taxonomies, or simulated workplaces, it derives tasks from AI-native startup products, ToB user needs, and expected business deliverables.

2.3 代理作为法官进行复杂的可交付成果评估Agent-as-a-Judge for complex deliverable evaluation

随着代理任务从简短答案转向报告、电子表格、幻灯片、代码补丁和结构化文件等异构可交付成果，评估必须从以能力为中心的评分转向以可交付成果为中心的评估As agent tasks shift from short answers to heterogeneous deliverables such as reports, spreadsheets, slides, code patches, and structured files, evaluation must move from capability-centric scoring to deliverable-centric assessmentYou et al. 2026。 LLM-as-a-Judge 已广泛用于开放式反应评估，MT-Bench 和 Prometheus 研究基于偏好或标题条件的判断. LLM-as-a-Judge has been widely used for open-ended response evaluation, with MT-Bench and Prometheus studying preference-based or rubric-conditioned judging(Zheng et al. 2023; Kim et al. 2024)。然而，对于代理工作流程，判断通常需要证据检索、文件检查、状态验证以及对最终工件的约束检查。 Agent-as-a-Judge通过为评估者配备工具和环境交互来扩展LLM评审，而AJ-Bench则进一步研究法官代理在信息获取、状态验证和过程验证方面的能力. For agentic workflows, however, judging often requires evidence retrieval, file inspection, state verification, and constraint checking over final artifacts. Agent-as-a-Judge extends LLM judging by equipping evaluators with tools and environment interaction, while AJ-Bench further studies judge agents on information acquisition, state verification, and process verification(Zhuge et al. 2025; Shi et al. 2026)。 StartupBench 采用这种以可交付成果为中心的评估范式，将专家评分标准与检查原始工件和证据视图的自动判断代理相结合，评估代理是否可以将模型功能转化为可用的工作产品。. StartupBench adopts this deliverable-centric evaluation paradigm by combining expert rubrics with automated judge agents that inspect original artifacts and evidence views, evaluating whether agents can transform model capabilities into usable work products.

3 启动台StartupBench

StartupBench 旨在评估模型和代理是否可以完成用户已经委托给 AI 产品的实际工作流程。这一目标需要数据能够反映真实的用户需求，同时支持受控评估。因此，我们要求每项任务都是现实的、可负责的、可评估的和有区别的：它应该源于实际的人工智能产品使用，为有效的解决方案提供足够的信息，指定明确的输出要求和评估标准，并保持足够的挑战性以区分当前的模型和代理。StartupBench is designed to evaluate whether models and agents can complete realistic workflows that users already delegate to AI products. This objective requires data that reflects authentic user demand while supporting controlled evaluation. We therefore require each task to be realistic, answerable, evaluable, and discriminative: it should originate from actual AI product usage, provide sufficient information for a valid solution, specify clear output requirements and assessment criteria, and remain challenging enough to distinguish current models and agents.

手动任务设计或直接从产品演示中提取都不足以获取此类数据。前者是可控的，但可能脱离实际需求，而后者是现实的，但往往缺乏背景、成功标准或可复制的规范。因此，我们采用了调查和访谈驱动的多阶段流程，如图所示Neither manual task design nor direct extraction from product demonstrations is sufficient to obtain such data. The former is controllable but may be detached from real demand, while the latter is realistic but often lacks context, success criteria, or reproducible specifications. We therefore adopt a survey- and interview-driven multi-stage pipeline shown in Figure2。我们首先调查经过市场验证的人工智能原生初创公司，采访深度用户以确定使用环境、任务目标和预期交付成果，招募领域专家将经过验证的场景转换为基准实例，最后应用质量控制来实现现实性、可回答性、可评估性和区分难度。. We first survey market-validated AI-native startups, interview deep users to identify usage contexts, task goals, and expected deliverables, recruit domain experts to convert validated scenarios into benchmark instances, and finally apply quality control for realism, answerability, evaluability, and discriminative difficulty.

3.1 数据集构建Dataset Construction

3.1.1 针对经过市场验证的代理场景的初创公司调查Startup Survey for Market-Validated Agent Scenarios

我们首先调查了跨不同领域构建代理产品的人工智能原生初创公司。为了确定经过市场验证的工作流程，我们保留了资金超过 100 万美元的初创公司，并需要通过付费使用或大量用户吸引力来证明其真正采用的证据。资金提供了市场预期的粗略信号，而支付或用户规模证据表明该产品的使用超出了探索性演示。此阶段的输出是用于用户访谈的候选产品和工作流程池。本阶段我们收集20+初创代理作为后续阶段的基础。We first survey AI-native startups that build agent products across diverse domains. To identify workflows with market validation, we retain startups with more than USD 1M in funding and require evidence of real adoption, either through paid usage or substantial user traction. Funding provides a coarse signal of market expectation, while payment or user-scale evidence indicates that the product is used beyond exploratory demonstrations. The output of this stage is a pool of candidate products and workflows for user interviews. During this stage we collect 20+ start-up agents as the basis for subsequent stage.

3.1.2 场景和需求发现的用户访谈User Interviews for Scenario and Requirement Discovery

对于每个候选初创产品，我们都会采访来自不​​同领域的相应代理的深层用户，以恢复公开产品描述之外的实际用途。访谈引出了使用环境、用户目标、输入信息、预期交付成果、成功标准和常见限制。我们将这些发现总结为场景规范和代表性演示案例，作为后续注释阶段的锚点。 在这一阶段，我们采访了 30 多名深度用户，并为每个候选 Agent 收集了至少一个演示案例。For each candidate Startup-product, we interview deep users of the corresponding agent from different domains to recover practical usage beyond public product descriptions. The interviews elicit the usage context, user goal, input information, expected deliverable, success criteria, and common constraints. We summarize these findings into scenario specifications and representative demo cases, which serve as anchors for later annotation stage. During this stage we interview over 30 deep users and collected at least one demo case for each candidate Agent.

3.1.3 领域任务的专家构建Expert Construction of Domain Tasks

4.4.2 行为级故障分析Behavior-level Failure Analysis

复杂的指令遵循。Complex Instruction Following.复杂的现实工作流程通常会对最终交付成果施加许多限制，包括结构组织、计算逻辑、格式和可执行正确性。尽管这些要求在任务描述中明确指定，但当前模型经常表现出部分合规性：它们满足最明显的要求，同时忽略其他同样重要的约束。因此，从表面检查来看，生成的工件基本上是完整的，但无法满足直接使用所需的完整验收标准。这种行为表明模型通常会优化以产生看起来正确的输出，而不是确保管理最终可交付成果的每条指令都得到忠实执行。Complex real-world workflows typically impose numerous constraints on the final deliverable, including structural organization, computation logic, formatting, and executable correctness. Although these requirements are explicitly specified in the task description, current models frequently exhibit partial compliance: they satisfy the most visible requirements while overlooking other equally critical constraints. As a result, the generated artifact appears largely complete from a superficial inspection, yet fails to satisfy the complete acceptance criteria required for direct use. This behavior indicates that models often optimize for producing outputs that look correct, rather than ensuring that every instruction governing the final deliverable has been faithfully executed.

Specifically, key cached values required for downstream verification are missing, rendering the workbook unverifiable despite its polished appearance. This case illustrates a common pattern of partial compliance, where the model satisfies the most visible requirements while overlooking less obvious but equally essential deliverable constraints.

自我验证幻觉。Self-Verification Hallucination.我们还观察到，许多模型失败并不是因为它们无法完成所需的工作流程，而是因为它们错误地认为成功执行意味着成功交付。在生成看似合理的工件后，模型通常会总结预期的推理过程或已完成的步骤，作为任务已得到验证的证据，而不根据原始用户需求独立检查最终可交付成果。因此，尽管输出令人信服，但微妙但严重的错误（例如不正确的单元格值、不匹配的标识符、缺少约束或格式不一致）仍然未被检测到。这种行为反映了一种自我验证幻觉，即模型错误地相信自己的推理过程，认为生成的工件满足验收标准。然而，在现实世界的办公工作流程中，成功完成需要验证最终可交付成果本身，而不仅仅是确认执行过程，这使其成为部署关键失败的常见根源。典型案例见附录We also observe that many models fail not because they cannot complete the required workflow, but because they incorrectly assume that successful execution implies successful delivery. After producing a plausible artifact, the model often summarizes the intended reasoning process or completed steps as evidence that the task has been verified, without independently inspecting the final deliverable against the original user requirements. Consequently, subtle yet critical errors—such as incorrect cell values, unmatched identifiers, missing constraints, or formatting inconsistencies—remain undetected despite an otherwise convincing output. This behavior reflects a form of self-verification hallucination, where the model mistakes confidence in its own reasoning process for evidence that the generated artifact satisfies the acceptance criteria. In real-world office workflows, however, successful completion requires validating the final deliverable itself rather than merely confirming the execution process, making this a common source of deployment-critical failures. 特定领域的专业知识。Domain-Specific Expertise.失败的另一个主要来源是特定领域专业知识不足，这与特定领域合规性维度上相对较差的表现一致。与一般的指令遵循错误不同，这些失败是由不同专业领域的独特操作要求驱动的。尽管模型通常生成看起来流畅且专业格式的输出，但它们经常无法满足确定可交付成果是否实际可用的特定于领域的约束。因此，主要的故障模式在不同领域存在很大差异，反映了现实世界工作流程所需的正确性、证据、精确性和可操作性的不同概念。Another major source of failure arises from insufficient domain-specific expertise, consistent with the relatively poor performance on the Domain-Specific Compliance dimension. Unlike general instruction-following errors, these failures are driven by the unique operational requirements of different professional domains. Although models often generate outputs that appear fluent and professionally formatted, they frequently fail to satisfy the domain-specific constraints that determine whether a deliverable is actually usable. As a result, the dominant failure patterns vary substantially across domains, reflecting different notions of correctness, evidence, precision, and actionability required by real-world workflows.

从领域的角度来看，不同的专业领域暴露出不同的失败特征。业务和管理任务主要强调工件的正确性，特别是电子表格、仪表板、公式和结构化报告。财务任务强调来源归属、数值精度、估值假设和时间一致性，其中错误通常是由于调节不充分、数值自我验证不充分或会计惯例不一致而引起的。法律任务较少受到法律写作风格的限制，而更多地受到法律推理的完整性和正确性的限制，包括引用缺失、事实覆盖不完整、法定支持不足或事实与适用规则之间的映射不正确。医疗任务获得相对较高的平均分数，但带来更大的安全风险，因为用药时机、延续标准或出院计划的错误直接损害了临床可用性。相反，STEM 和计算机科学任务需要精确的对象级基础，要求代码、工程图纸、图像、物料清单和系统组件之间的准确关系。最后，教育和人文学科任务更加强调细粒度的规则遵循，例如课程要求、学分分配、文档组织和文本连贯性。造成故障的典型例子 附录显示了特定领域专业知识不足From a domain perspective, different professional areas expose distinct failure characteristics. Business and management tasks primarily stress artifact correctness, particularly for spreadsheets, dashboards, formulas, and structured reports. Finance tasks emphasize source attribution, numerical precision, valuation assumptions, and temporal consistency, where errors commonly arise from insufficient reconciliation, inadequate numerical self-verification, or inconsistent accounting conventions. Legal tasks are less constrained by legal writing style than by the completeness and correctness of legal reasoning, including missing citations, incomplete factual coverage, insufficient statutory support, or incorrect mapping between facts and applicable rules. Finally, education and humanities tasks place greater emphasis on fine-grained rule following, such as curriculum requirements, credit allocation, document organization, and textual coherence. Unlike the previous experiment, this comparison evaluates each task using its corresponding production startup agent, which has been specifically optimized for its target workflow through domain-specific model selection, prompting strategies, tool orchestration, and workflow design.

5 结论Conclusion

我们介绍We introduce启动台StartupBench，一个评估端到端专业工作流程模型的基准，这些模型源自经过市场验证的人工智能原生产品和现实世界的用户需求。在跨越不同领域的多项任务中，我们的评估揭示了在专业工作上取得有意义的进展和产生完全满足实际验收标准的可交付成果之间存在巨大差距。这种差距主要是由复杂指令遵循、特定领域专业知识、专业惯例和长期工作流程执行方面的限制造成的。通过对用户已经寻求委托给人工智能的工作流程进行基础评估，StartupBench 提供了对实际代理能力的现实衡量，并强调了未来改进的具体方向。我们希望 StartupBench 支持模型和代理的开发，这些模型和代理不仅可以执行有价值的专业工作，而且可以可靠地提供可供直接使用的结果。, a benchmark for evaluating models on end-to-end professional workflows derived from market-validated AI-native products and real-world user demands. Across multiple tasks spanning diverse domains, our evaluation reveals a substantial gap between making meaningful progress on professional work and producing deliverables that fully satisfy practical acceptance criteria. This gap is primarily driven by limitations in complex instruction following, domain-specific expertise, professional conventions, and long-horizon workflow execution. By grounding evaluation in workflows that users already seek to delegate to AI, StartupBench provides a realistic measure of practical agent capabilities and highlights concrete directions for future improvement. We hope StartupBench supports the development of models and agents that can not only perform valuable professional work, but reliably deliver results ready for direct use.
