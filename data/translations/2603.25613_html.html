摘要Abstract

多模态大语言模型（MLLM）最近被探索作为面部验证系统，确定两个面部图像是否属于同一个人。与专用人脸识别系统不同，MLLM 通过视觉提示并依赖一般视觉和推理能力来完成此任务。然而，这些模型的人口公平性在很大程度上仍未得到探索。在本文中，我们提出了一项基准研究，该研究评估了来自六个模型系列的九个开源 MLLM，参数范围从 2B 到 8B，涉及四个种族群体和两个性别群体的 IJB-C 和 RFW 人脸验证协议。我们在每个人口群体的多个操作点上使用等错误率和真实匹配率来衡量验证准确性，并使用四个基于 FMR 的公平性指标来量化人口差异。我们的结果表明，FaceLLM-8B（我们研究中唯一的面部专用模型）在两个基准测试中都远远优于通用 MLLM。我们观察到的偏差模式与传统人脸识别中常见的偏差模式不同，不同的群体受到的影响最大，具体取决于基准和模型。我们还注意到，最准确的模型不一定是最公平的，总体准确度较差的模型可能看起来很公平，因为它们在所有人口群体中产生一致的高错误率。 项目页面：Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have recently been explored as face verification systems that determine whether two face images are of the same person. Unlike dedicated face recognition systems, MLLMs approach this task through visual prompting and rely on general visual and reasoning abilities. However, the demographic fairness of these models remains largely unexplored. In this paper, we present a benchmarking study that evaluates nine open-source MLLMs from six model families, ranging from 2B to 8B parameters, on the IJB-C and RFW face verification protocols across four ethnicity groups and two gender groups. We measure verification accuracy with the Equal Error Rate and True Match Rate at multiple operating points per demographic group, and we quantify demographic disparity with four FMR-based fairness metrics. Our results show that FaceLLM-8B, the only face-specialised model in our study, substantially outperforms general-purpose MLLMs on both benchmarks. The bias patterns we observe differ from those commonly reported for traditional face recognition, with different groups being most affected depending on the benchmark and the model. We also note that the most accurate models are not necessarily the fairest and that models with poor overall accuracy can appear fair simply because they produce uniformly high error rates across all demographic groups. 1 简介Introduction

人脸识别是应用最广泛的生物识别技术之一。 现代人脸识别系统通常通过使用在大量标记人脸图像集合上训练的深度神经网络将人脸图像映射到固定长度的数字表示（称为嵌入）来工作。Face recognition is one of the most widely deployed biometric technologies. Modern face recognition systems typically work by mapping a face image to a fixed-length numerical representation, called an embedding, using a deep neural network trained on large collections of labelled face images[30]。 为了确定两个图像是否描绘同一个人，系统计算它们嵌入之间的距离（或相似性）并将其与决策阈值进行比较。 这种基于嵌入的方法在标准基准上达到了很高的准确度. To determine whether two images depict the same person, the system computes the distance (or similarity) between their embeddings and compares it to a decision threshold. This embedding-based approach has reached high levels of accuracy on standard benchmarks[38, 7, 26, 17]，它现在用于从边境控制和执法到移动设备身份验证的各种应用。, and it is now used in applications ranging from border control and law enforcement to mobile device authentication.

近年来，大型语言模型 (LLM) 通过单一通用架构在广泛的任务上展示了强大的性能，从而改变了自然语言处理In recent years, Large Language Models (LLMs) have transformed natural language processing by demonstrating strong performance on a broad range of tasks through a single, general-purpose architecture[37, 44]。 这一成功促进了多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM) 的发展，该模型扩展了法学硕士的处理视觉输入和文本的能力. This success has led to the development of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), which extend LLMs with the ability to process visual inputs alongside text[43, 23, 36, 14, 2]。 Qwen-VL等型号. Models such as Qwen-VL[28], 拉瓦, LLaVA[23, 22]、和 Idefics, and Idefics[21]可以接受一张或多张图像以及文本提示，并生成自由格式的文本响应。 这一功能开启了使用 MLLM 进行人脸验证的可能性：给定两张人脸图像，可以提示模型判断它们是否属于同一个人，并且其响应可以转换为相似度得分。 与需要对人脸身份标签进行专门训练的基于嵌入的系统不同，MLLM 依靠预训练期间获得的一般视觉和推理能力，通过视觉问答来完成任务。 这种方法是否能够产生准确可靠的验证分数是一个悬而未决的问题。can accept one or more images together with a text prompt, and produce a free-form textual response. This capability opens up the possibility of using MLLMs for face verification: given two face images, the model can be prompted to judge whether they belong to the same person, and its response can be converted into a similarity score. Unlike embedding-based systems, which require specialised training on face identity labels, MLLMs approach the task through visual question answering by relying on the general visual and reasoning abilities acquired during pretraining. Whether this approach can produce accurate and reliable verification scores is an open question.

一个单独但同样重要的问题是不同人口群体的人脸识别系统的公平性A separate but equally important concern is the fairness of face recognition systems across different demographic groups[8, 20]。 事实证明，人脸识别系统在由种族和性别等属性定义的人口群体中表现不均衡，并且研究一再发现某些群体的错误率往往高于其他群体。. Face recognition systems have been shown to perform unevenly across demographic groups defined by attributes such as ethnicity and gender, and studies have repeatedly found that certain groups tend to have higher error rates than others.[13]。 当人脸识别用于高风险应用时，这会引起严重担忧，因为错误可能会对个人产生直接后果. This raises serious concerns when face recognition is used in high-stakes applications, because errors can have direct consequences for individuals[9]。 在这种情况下，公平意味着系统应该在某个操作点上对不同人口群体产生相似的错误率；如果一个组的错误匹配率或错误不匹配率明显高于另一组，则称该系统对该组有偏见。. Fairness in this context means that the system should produce similar error rates across demographic groups at an operating point; if one group has a substantially higher false match rate or false non-match rate than another, the system is said to be biased against that group.[10].

虽然针对基于嵌入的人脸识别的人口统计公平性进行了广泛的研究，但对于基于 MLLM 的人脸验证，不存在可比较的分析。 在传统系统中观察到的偏差模式是否会延续到 MLLM 上并不明显，因为这两种方法在处理人脸图像和得出相似性判断的方式上有根本的不同。 了解 MLLM 的公平性非常重要，因为这些模型越来越多地被考虑用于以前由专门系统处理的任务。While demographic fairness has been studied extensively for embedding-based face recognition, no comparable analysis exists for MLLM-based face verification. It is not obvious that the bias patterns observed in traditional systems will carry over to MLLMs, since the two approaches differ fundamentally in how they process face images and arrive at a similarity judgment. Understanding the fairness properties of MLLMs is important, because these models are increasingly being considered for tasks that were previously handled by specialised systems.

在本文中，我们提出了用于人脸验证的 MLLM 的系统人口统计公平性评估。 我们根据 IJB-C 和 RFW 验证协议评估了四个种族群体和两个性别群体的九个 MLLM。 我们在标准操作点使用等错误率 (EER) 和真实匹配率 (TMR) 来衡量验证准确性，并在多个阈值下使用四个基于 FMR 的互补公平性指标来量化人口差异。 我们的结果表明，当前的 MLLM 无法达到专用的基于嵌入的系统的准确性，并且 MLLM 中的偏差模式与传统人脸识别中观察到的偏差模式不同。In this paper, we present a systematic demographic fairness evaluation of MLLMs for face verification. We evaluate nine MLLMs on the IJB-C and RFW verification protocols across four ethnicity groups and two gender groups. We measure verification accuracy with the Equal Error Rate (EER) and True Match Rate (TMR) at standard operating points, and we quantify demographic disparity with four complementary FMR-based fairness metrics at multiple thresholds. Our results show that no current MLLM approaches the accuracy of dedicated embedding-based systems, and that the bias patterns in MLLMs differ from those observed in traditional face recognition.

2.2 生物识别系统的公平性指标Fairness Metrics for Biometric Systems

根据生物识别系统中人口统计偏差的证据，格罗瑟等人。Following the evidence of demographic bias in biometric systems, Grother et al.[9]作为人脸识别供应商测试的一部分，NIST 对 100 多个商用人脸识别系统进行了大规模评估，并报告了不同人口群体的 FMR 和 FNMR 值。这促使人们致力于将每组错误率降低为一个标量，将生物识别系统的公平性总结为测量组内 FMR 和通过设置全局阈值获得的 FNMR 值“分布”的值的组合。德弗雷塔斯·佩雷拉和马塞尔at NIST conducted a large-scale evaluation of over 100 commercially available face recognition systems as part of the Face Recognition Vendor Test, and reported FMR and FNMR values for various demographic groups. This prompted work on metrics that reduce per-group error rates into a scalar summarizing the fairness of a biometric system as a combination of values measuring the “spread” of in-group FMR and FNMR values obtained by setting a global threshold. De Freitas Pereira and Marcel[6]提出了公平差异率（FDR），它基于组间 FMR 和 FNMR 的最大差异。格罗瑟随后发表的 NIST 报告proposed the Fairness Discrepancy Rate (FDR), which is based on the maximum differential of FMR and FNMR across groups. A subsequent NIST report by Grother[10]确认不同算法中存在巨大的人口差异，并引入了基于最大 FMR 和 FNMR 比率的线性组合的不公平率 (IR)，以及最坏情况错误率指标 (WERM)，该指标采用最大 FMR 和 FNMR 比率的指数加权乘积与所有组的几何平均值。霍华德等人。confirmed that large demographic differentials persist across algorithms and introduced the Inequity Rate (IR), based on linear combinations of maximum FMR and FNMR ratios, and the Worst-case Error-Rate Metric (WERM), which takes the exponent-weighted product of the maximum FMR and FNMR ratios to the geometric mean across all groups. Howard et al.[12]提出了生物特征公平性的基尼聚合率（GARBE），它使用每组 FMR 和 FNMR 值的基尼指数，并表明它比以前的措施更好地满足一组可解释性标准。科特瓦尔和马塞尔proposed the Gini Aggregation Rate for Biometric Equitability (GARBE), which uses the Gini index of per-group FMR and FNMR values and showed that it better satisfies a set of interpretability criteria than previous measures. Kotwal and Marcel[19]进一步提出了基于决策前分数分布而不是决策后错误率的公平性度量。这些努力最终被整合到 ISO/IEC 19795-10:2024 中further proposed fairness measures based on pre-decision score distributions rather than post-decision error rates. The standard specifies that FMR and FNMR differentials and ratios should be reported per group, but it does not prescribe a single scalar fairness metric. In this paper, we mainly use metrics equivalent to FMR-based metrics described in the ISO standard.

5 结论Conclusion

本文首次提出了用于人脸验证的 MLLM 的人口统计公平性评估。我们根据 RFW 和 IJB-C 1:1 验证协议（子样本）评估了来自 6 个模型系列的 9 个 MLLM，参数范围从 2B 到 8B，涉及四个种族群体和两个性别群体。我们报告了四个基于 FMR 的公平性指标以及多个操作点的标准准确性指标。结果表明，当前的 MLLM 仍然达不到实际人脸验证所需的精度和速度。 FaceLLM-8B 是我们研究中唯一的面部专用模型，在两个基准测试中都优于所有通用模型。公平性分析得出了一些与基于嵌入的人脸识别中常见模式不同的发现：在 IJB-C 上，白人群体在八个模型中的五个中处于最不利的地位，这与传统人脸识别系统的通常报告相反。在所有模型和操作点中，性别差异始终小于种族差异，FaceLLM-8B 显示出较低的性别不平等（This paper presented the first demographic fairness evaluation of MLLMs for face verification. We evaluated nine MLLMs from six model families, ranging from 2B to 8B parameters, on the RFW and (a sub-sample of) IJB-C 1:1 verification protocols with respect to four ethnicity groups and two gender groups. We reported four FMR-based fairness metrics alongside standard accuracy measures at multiple operating points. The results indicate that current MLLMs still fall short of the accuracy and speed needed for practical face verification. FaceLLM-8B, the only face-specialised model in our study, outperforms all general-purpose models on both benchmarks. The fairness analysis produced several findings that differ from common patterns in embedding-based face recognition: On IJB-C, the Caucasian group is the most disadvantaged in five of eight models, which is the opposite of what is typically reported for traditional face recognition systems.
