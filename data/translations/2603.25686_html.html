摘要Abstract

跨视图地理定位 (CVGL) 是通过将街景图像与地理参考俯视图像进行匹配来估计摄像机位置的任务，从而实现 GPS 拒绝的定位和导航。现有方法几乎普遍将 CVGL 视为对比训练的嵌入空间中的图像检索问题。这种表述将性能与大批量和硬负挖掘联系在一起，并且忽略了地图的几何结构以及街景和俯视图像之间的覆盖范围不匹配。特别是，从街景中可见的显着地标可能会落在固定卫星作物之外，从而使检索目标模糊不清并限制地图上的显式空间推断。 我们建议Cross-view geo-localization (CVGL) is the task of estimating a camera’s location by matching a street-view image to geo-referenced overhead imagery, enabling GPS-denied localization and navigation. Existing methods almost universally pose CVGL as an image-retrieval problem in a contrastively trained embedding space. This formulation ties performance to large batches and hard negative mining, and it ignores both the geometric structure of maps and the coverage mismatch between street-view and overhead imagery. In particular, salient landmarks visible from the street-view can fall outside a fixed satellite crop, making retrieval targets ambiguous and limiting explicit spatial inference over the map. We propose只需放大Just Zoom In, 一个, an另类alternative通过在城市规模俯视图上进行自回归缩放来执行 CVGL 的公式。该模型从粗略的卫星视图开始，采取一系列简短的放大决策来选择formulation that performs CVGL via autoregressive zooming over a city-scale overhead map. 1 简介Introduction

视觉定位通过将捕获的图像与已知环境进行匹配来估计相机的位置。具体来说，跨视图地理定位（CVGL）通过使用地理参考卫星图像来定位街景查询图像来解决这个问题Visual localization estimates a camera’s location by matching a captured image to a known environment. Specifically, cross-view geo-localization (CVGL) tackles this problem by using geo-referenced satellite imagery to localize a street-view query image[Lin2014CVGL, Workman2015WideArea]。卫星图像可提供密集、大范围的室外环境覆盖，并广泛应用于许多地区，这使得 CVGL 对于 GPS 无法实现的定位和导航具有吸引力。 CVGL 的核心困难是弥合街景和卫星图像之间的极端视点差异。. Satellite imagery offers dense, large-scale coverage of outdoor environments and is widely available for many regions, making CVGL attractive for GPS-denied localization and navigation. The core difficulty in CVGL is bridging the extreme viewpoint differences between street-view and satellite imagery.

为了处理这些观点差异，现代 CVGL 系统主要依赖于对比训练的检索模型；给定街景图像，系统从大型 GPS 标记参考数据库中检索最相似的卫星图块To handle these viewpoint differences, modern CVGL systems predominantly rely on contrastively trained retrieval models; given a street-view image, the system retrieves the most similar satellite tile from a large GPS-tagged reference database[Shi2019SAFA, Deuser2023Sample4Geo, Zhu2021VIGOR, Zhu2022TransGeo]。在实践中，他们学习一个嵌入空间，其中街景查询和卫星图块如果对应于同一位置，则被拉在一起，否则被分开，通常依赖于许多信息负数（通过大的有效批量大小和/或硬负数挖掘）。在推理时，通过在大型卫星数据库的嵌入空间中进行最近邻搜索来执行定位，如图所示. In practice, they learn a single embedding space in which street-view queries and satellite tiles are pulled together if they correspond to the same location and pushed apart otherwise, commonly relying on many informative negatives (via large effective batch sizes and/or hard negative mining). At inference time, localization is performed by nearest-neighbor search in the embedding space over a large satellite database shown in Figure1.a.

尽管性能强劲，但这种对比检索公式对于可扩展、真实的 CVGL 来说存在三个实际限制。 首先，对比检索性能通常受益于许多信息丰富的负例，这些负例是通过大有效批次和/或硬负例挖掘 (HNM) 实现的Despite strong performance, this contrastive–retrieval formulation has three practical limitations for scalable, realistic CVGL. First, contrastive retrieval performance often benefits from many informative negatives, implemented via large effective batches and/or hard negative mining (HNM)[Deuser2023Sample4Geo, Zhu2022TransGeo]。维护和刷新硬底片会显着增加训练计算和工程复杂性. Maintaining and refreshing hard negatives can add significant training compute and engineering complexity[Berton2022CosPlace].

其次，基于检索的 CVGL 必须在推理时存储和搜索带有 GPS 标签的大型参考数据库。城市规模区域的细粒度定位需要许多密集采样的卫星图块，因此内存占用量与图块数量大致呈线性增长Second, retrieval-based CVGL must store and search a large GPS-tagged reference database at inference time. Fine-grained localization over a city-scale region requires many densely sampled satellite tiles, so memory footprint grows roughly linearly with the number of tiles[Zhu2021VIGOR, Zhu2022TransGeo, Deuser2023Sample4Geo]，使得扩展到更大区域的成本高昂。, making it expensive to extend to larger areas.

第三，检索公式将地图视为独立的固定大小图块的非结构化集合，相邻区域之间或较粗的缩放级别之间没有明确的链接。这种以图块为中心的视图忽略了底层的地理层次结构，可能会加剧Third, the retrieval formulation treats the map as an unstructured collection of independent, fixed-size tiles, without explicit links between neighboring regions or coarser zoom levels. This tile-centric view ignores the underlying geographic hierarchy and can exacerbate a覆盖范围不匹配coverage mismatch模式之间：由于透视和平铺框架/错位，街景图像中可见的歧视性上下文可能位于候选卫星补丁之外，即使该补丁在地理位置上很接近（图between modalities: due to perspective and tile framing/misalignment, discriminative context visible in the street-view image may lie outside a candidate satellite patch even when the patch is geographically close (Figure2）。因此，现有方法被优化以对孤立的斑块进行排名，而不是对卫星图像中的空间背景和多尺度结构进行推理。). As a result, existing methods are optimized to rank isolated patches rather than to reason over spatial context and multi-scale structure in the satellite imagery.

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 Just Zoom In，这是一种用于跨视图地理定位的自回归框架，它用顺序的、从粗到细的定位过程取代了单次对比检索。通过将街景和多尺度卫星图像联合编码为共享表示并使用因果变换器预测缩放动作，我们的方法逐步缩小搜索区域，同时保留全局空间上下文。这种设计能够实现与地理区域成对数缩放的高效推理，避免对比匹配的计算和表示瓶颈。我们进一步策划了一个新的多尺度数据集，由政府来源的正射摄影和众包街景图像组成，为跨视图定位提供了现实且可重复的基准。大量的实验和消融表明，与最先进的对比基线相比，我们的自回归公式始终能产生更高的定位精度和鲁棒性，特别是在具有挑战性的现实条件下。我们希望这项工作能够推动跨视图定位中从基于检索的匹配向顺序空间推理的转变。We introduced Just Zoom In, an autoregressive framework for cross-view geo-localization that replaces single-shot contrastive retrieval with a sequential, coarse-to-fine localization process. By jointly encoding street-view and multi-scale satellite imagery into a shared representation and predicting zoom actions with a causal transformer, our method progressively narrows the search region while preserving global spatial context. This design enables efficient inference that scales logarithmically with geographic area, avoiding the computational and representation bottlenecks of contrastive matching. We further curated a new multi-scale dataset composed of government-sourced orthophotography and crowd-sourced street-view imagery, providing a realistic and reproducible benchmark for cross-view localization. Extensive experiments and ablations demonstrate that our autoregressive formulation yields consistently higher localization accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art contrastive baselines, particularly in challenging real-world conditions. We hope this work motivates a shift from retrieval-based matching toward sequential spatial reasoning in cross-view localization.
