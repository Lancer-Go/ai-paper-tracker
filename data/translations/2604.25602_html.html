摘要Abstract

由于可扩展性、可观察性和自主进化方面的限制，在复杂的工业环境中部署生产就绪的多代理系统 (MAS) 仍然具有挑战性。我们提出了 OxyGent，一个开源框架，它通过统一的 Oxy 抽象实现模块化、可观察和可进化的 MAS，其中代理、工具、LLM 和推理流被封装为可插入原子组件。这种类似乐高的装配范例支持可扩展的系统组合和非侵入式监控。为了增强可观察性，OxyGent 引入了权限驱动的动态规划，用运行时生成的执行图取代了严格的工作流程，从而提供了自适应可视化。为了支持持续进化，该框架集成了OxyBank，这是一个支持自动化数据回流、注释和联合进化的AI资产管理平台。实证评估和现实案例研究表明，OxyGent 为 MAS 提供了强大且可扩展的基础。 OxyGent 可在以下位置公开获取：Deploying production-ready multi-agent systems (MAS) in complex industrial environments remains challenging due to limitations in scalability, observability, and autonomous evolution. We present OxyGent, an open-source framework that enables modular, observable, and evolvable MAS via a unified Oxy abstraction, in which agents, tools, LLMs, and reasoning flows are encapsulated as pluggable atomic components. This Lego-like assembly paradigm supports scalable system composition and non-intrusive monitoring. To enhance observability, OxyGent introduces permission-driven dynamic planning that replaces rigid workflows with execution graphs generated at runtime, which provide adaptive visualizations. To support continuous evolution, the framework integrates OxyBank, an AI asset management platform that supports automated data backflow, annotation, and joint evolution. Empirical evaluations and real-world case studies show that OxyGent provides a robust and scalable foundation for MAS. OxyGent is publicly available athttps://oxygent.jd.com/.

3 制氧机OxyGent

OxyGent 旨在将多代理编排从静态、硬编码的工作流程转变为弹性的、类似乐高的原子组件组装。本节详细介绍了该框架的基础设施、动态规划机制以及由 OxyBank 支持的进化循环。OxyGent aims to transform multi-agent orchestration from static, hard-coded workflows into an elastic, Lego-like assembly of atomic components. This section details the framework’s infrastructure, the dynamic planning mechanisms, and the evolutionary loop powered by OxyBank.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 OxyGent，一个用于构建可扩展和自我改进的多代理系统的开源框架。通过采用统一的原子抽象，OxyGent 实现了类似乐高的构建过程，用权限驱动的动态规划取代严格的工作流程，通过实时执行可视化提供高可观察性。该框架与 OxyBank 集成用于人工智能资产管理，为持续的智能体进化建立了强大的管道，我们对 GAIA 基准和行业应用的广泛评估就证明了这一点。In this paper, we present OxyGent, an open-source framework for building scalable and self-improving multi-agent systems. By adopting a unified atomic abstraction, OxyGent enables a Lego-like construction process that replaces rigid workflows with permission-driven dynamic planning, offering high observability through real-time execution visualizations. Integrated with OxyBank for AI asset management, the framework establishes a robust pipeline for continuous agent evolution, as evidenced by our extensive evaluations on the GAIA benchmark and industrial applications.

局限性。Limitations.

尽管OxyBank支持自我进化，但大规模训练目前仍依赖于手动资源配置。我们正在积极开发智能资源调度，以实现完全自动化的生命周期，包括代理构建、部署和细化。这将最大限度地减少人为干预，并进一步加速复杂环境中集体智慧的进化。Although OxyBank supports self-evolution, large-scale training currently depends on manual resource configuration. We are actively developing intelligent resource scheduling to achieve a fully automated lifecycle, including agent construction, deployment, and refinement. This will minimize human intervention and further accelerate the evolution of collective intelligence in complex environments.
