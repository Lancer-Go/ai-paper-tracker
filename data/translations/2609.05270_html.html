摘要Abstract：人工智能（AI）不仅开始改变信息系统研究人员的设计内容，而且开始改变他们进行设计研究的方式。然而，现有文献对于人工智能如何积极参与计算设计科学（CDS）研究的关键阶段（包括问题表述、资源构建、设计搜索、评估和知识抽象）提供的指导有限。本研究开发了计算设计科学人工智能（AI4CDS），这是一个五阶段方法框架，用于在人工智能成为研究过程的基本参与者时进行 CDS。 AI4CDS 详细说明了人工智能如何扩展对问题和设计的搜索，而研究人员则保留对领域基础、可接受性、验证和科学判断的责任。他们的合作受到分级信任、可逆性和可审计性的约束，差异化的再现性为进行验证和维护可审计性提供了实用的框架。 AI4CDS 通过 ChildRiskGuard 的开发进行实例化和检查，ChildRiskGuard 是一种可解释的计算工件，用于检测不适合儿童的短片视频；相应的 AI 交互、被拒绝的替代方案、更正和审计跟踪记录在在线附录 A 中。继 AI4CDS 之后，本案例研究将依赖于受众的安全性和解释的忠实性转化为三个技术挑战，并通过 AI 扩展和研究人员控制的设计搜索，开发了一种工件，可将一般风险与儿童特定风险区分开来，代表不同的发展风险机制，并使概念级解释成为预测计算的一部分。 ChildRiskGuard 的 F1 分数为 0.769，大大优于通用内容安全模型在儿童适当性检测中的直接应用，同时在强大的替代基准中保持竞争力。这项工作的主要贡献是 AI4CDS 作为 AI 支持的 CDS 的负责任的方法框架； ChildRiskGuard 提供了相应的过程和工件证据，显示该框架如何生成、评估和抽象新颖的计算设计知识。: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is beginning to transform not only what information systems researchers design, but also how they conduct design research. Yet existing literature offers limited guidance on how AI actively participates in the key stages of computational design science (CDS) research, including problem formulation, resource construction, design search, evaluation, and knowledge abstraction. This study develops AI for Computational Design Science (AI4CDS), a five-phase methodological framework for conducting CDS when AI becomes a constitutive participant in the research process. AI4CDS specifies how AI expands the search for problems and designs, while researchers retain responsibility for domain grounding, admissibility, verification, and scientific judgment. Their collaboration is governed by graduated trust, reversibility, and auditability, with differentiated reproducibility providing a practical framework for conducting verification and maintaining auditability. AI4CDS is instantiated and examined through the development of ChildRiskGuard, an interpretable computational artifact for detecting short-form videos that are inappropriate for children; the corresponding AI interactions, rejected alternatives, corrections, and audit trails are documented in Online Appendix A. Following AI4CDS, this case study translates audience-dependent safety and explanation faithfulness into three technical challenges and, through AI-expanded and researcher-governed design search, develops an artifact that separates generic from child-specific risk, represents distinct developmental-risk mechanisms, and makes concept-level explanations part of the predictive computation. ChildRiskGuard achieves an F1 score of 0.769, substantially outperforming the direct application of a general-purpose content-safety model to child-appropriateness detection while remaining competitive with strong alternative benchmarks. The primary contribution of this work is AI4CDS as a responsible methodological framework for AI-enabled CDS; ChildRiskGuard provides consequential process and artifact evidence showing how the framework can generate, evaluate, and abstract novel computational design knowledge.

一、简介1. Introduction

最近的信息系统（IS）学术认识到人工智能（AI）不仅可以重塑研究成果，还可以重塑科学探究本身的行为，包括面向设计的研究（Gopal et al. 2025）。对于通过新颖的计算模型、算法和方法解决随之而来的商业和社会问题的计算设计科学（CDS）（Rai 2017，Fang et al. 2025），这提出了一个基本的方法论问题：当人工智能成为生产工件和设计知识的基本参与者时，应该如何进行CDS？现有的 CDS 指南解释了研究人员如何激发特定领域的问题、构建和评估计算工件以及抽象设计知识，但没有具体说明如何在整个 CDS 生命周期中组织人工智能参与（Hevner et al. 2004、Gregor and Hevner 2013、Padmanabhan et al. 2022、Abbasi et al. 2024、Fang et al. 2025）。人类-人工智能研究将与日益代理的人工智能的交互视为授权问题，并表明有效的协作取决于责任分配和能力评估（Baird 和 Maruping 2021，Fügener 等人 2022），但没有解释如何在 CDS 相互依赖的活动中构建此类授权。人工智能可以扩展问题表述、探索替代方法、构建计算资源、支持评估和扩大设计搜索，而人类研究人员仍然负责领域基础、社会技术和伦理判断、验证和科学问责。人工智能还带来了幻觉、随机性、信息泄露和来源减弱等风险。因此，由此产生的问题不仅仅是如何设计人工智能制品，而是如何在 CDS 中组织人类和人工智能的能力、权限和责任。Recent Information Systems (IS) scholarship recognizes that Artificial Intelligence (AI) may reshape not only research outputs but also the conduct of scientific inquiry itself, including design-oriented research (Gopal et al. 2025). For computational design science (CDS), which addresses consequential business and societal problems through novel computational models, algorithms, and methods (Rai 2017, Fang et al. 2025), this raises a fundamental methodological question: how should CDS be conducted when AI becomes a constitutive participant in producing artifacts and design knowledge? Existing CDS guidance explains how researchers motivate domain-specific problems, construct and evaluate computational artifacts, and abstract design knowledge, but does not specify how AI participation should be organized across the CDS lifecycle (Hevner et al. 2004, Gregor and Hevner 2013, Padmanabhan et al. 2022, Abbasi et al. 2024, Fang et al. 2025). Human–AI research frames interaction with increasingly agentic AI as a delegation problem and shows that effective collaboration depends on responsibility allocation and capability assessment (Baird and Maruping 2021, Fügener et al. 2022), but does not explain how such delegation should be structured across the interdependent activities of CDS. AI can expand problem formulations, explore methodological alternatives, construct computational resources, support evaluation, and broaden design search, while human researchers remain responsible for domain grounding, sociotechnical and ethical judgment, verification, and scientific accountability. AI also introduces risks of hallucination, stochasticity, information leakage, and weakened provenance. The resulting question is therefore not simply how to design AI artifacts, but how human and AI capabilities, authority, and accountability should be organized across CDS.

2.1.针对特定受众的视频安全和不当视频检测2.1. Audience-Specific Video Safety and Inappropriate-Video Detection

大多数内容审核系统主要根据内容的特征来实施伤害，而对儿童的适宜性本质上取决于受众。一般观众可以接受的内容可能不适合儿童，因为接触的后果取决于观看者的发育特征（Papadamou et al. 2020）。发展研究为这种区别提供了实质性基础：幼儿在传递和解释介导信息以及对屏幕内容做出反应的方式方面与成人不同（Barr 2010，Guellai et al. 2022）。Most content-moderation systems operationalize harm primarily from characteristics of the content, whereas appropriateness for children is inherently audience-dependent. Content that is acceptable for a general audience may nevertheless be inappropriate for children because the consequences of exposure depend on developmental characteristics of the viewer (Papadamou et al. 2020). Developmental research provides a substantive basis for this distinction: young children differ from adults in how they transfer and interpret mediated information and respond to screen content (Barr 2010, Guellai et al. 2022).

这种区别产生了重要的计算要求。即使视频不包含一般安全系统针对的明确信号，也可能会出现针对儿童的风险，并且此类内容可能会故意采用与儿童媒体相关的视觉惯例（Balanzategui 2019，Papadamou et al. 2020）。因此，现有的审核系统提供了有关一般安全性的有用信息，但不能作为儿童适当性的代理（Ahmed 等人，2023）。面向儿童的探测器在识别时必须保留有用的通用安全信息This distinction creates an important computational requirement. Child-specific risk may arise even when a video contains none of the explicit signals targeted by general safety systems, and such content may deliberately adopt visual conventions associated with children’s media (Balanzategui 2019, Papadamou et al. 2020). Existing moderation systems therefore provide useful information about generic safety but cannot serve as proxies for child appropriateness (Ahmed et al. 2023). 2.2.可解释的视频预测2.2. Interpretable Video Prediction

可解释的视听视频分类方法将解释放在与预测相关的不同点上，包括前向传递注意力权重（Nagrani et al. 2021）、事后显着性图（Selvaraju et al. 2017）、局部视觉或音频基础（Arandjelović and Zisserman 2018）、概念加性分解（Kumar et al. 2025）和自然语言理由（Park 等人，2018）。这些方法之间的关键区别不在于它们是否提供解释，而在于该解释是否反映了模型实际用于进行预测的信息。基于注意力和梯度的方法暴露了模型行为的各个方面，但暴露的权重或显着性模式不需要构成预测的充分证据（Adebayo 等人，2018 年；Jain 和 Wallace，2019 年）。接地方法更直接地将表征与本地化证据联系起来，但物体或声音的本地化并不一定能解释为什么该证据会产生特定的下游风险Interpretable audio-visual video classification methods place explanations at different points relative to prediction, including forward-pass attention weights (Nagrani et al. 2021), post-hoc saliency maps (Selvaraju et al. 2017), localized visual or audio grounding (Arandjelović and Zisserman 2018), conceptor additive-decomposition (Kumar et al. 2025), and natural-language justifications (Park et al. 2018). The key distinction among these approaches is not whether they provide an explanation, but whether the explanation reflects the information that the model actually used to make its prediction. Attention and gradient-based methods expose aspects of model behavior, but the exposed weights or saliency patterns need not constitute the evidence sufficient for the prediction (Adebayo et al. 2018, Jain and Wallace 2019). Grounding methods more directly connect representations to localized evidence, but localization of an object or sound does not necessarily explain why that evidence produced a particular downstream risk

Natural-language methods can express domain-relevant reasons in an accessible form, but generated rationales may remain computationally decoupled from the prediction they describe. Concept-based methods offer a different possibility: named concepts can be placed directly on the predictive pathway so that prediction and explanation operate over the same intermediate variables.

对于面向儿童的节制而言，有两个要求尤其重要。首先，解释应该确定预测背后的具体发育风险机制，而不是仅仅突出像素、框架或模式。例如，一段涉及可模仿危险行为的视频和一段涉及自残的视频可能需要不同形式的审查或干预。其次，在模型中简单地包含具有预定义含义的人类可解释的概念（例如皮肤暴露、危险行为或自残证据）并不能保证准确的解释。概念仍可能受到与其预期含义无关的信息的影响，或者可能捕获有关目标标签的附加信息（Margeloiu 等人，2021 年；Havasi 等人，2022 年）。因此，可解释的架构必须控制通过命名概念输入的信息以及该概念如何对最终预测做出贡献。Two requirements are especially important for child-oriented moderation. First, the explanation should identify the specific developmental-risk mechanism underlying the prediction, rather than only highlighting pixels, frames, or modalities. For example, a video involving imitable dangerous actions and one involving self-harm may require different forms of review or intervention. Second, simply including human-interpretable concepts with predefined meanings (such as skin exposure, risky actions, or self-harm evidence) in the model does not guarantee faithful explanations. A concept may still be influenced by information unrelated to its intended meaning or may capture additional information about the target label (Margeloiu et al. 2021, Havasi et al. 2022). Thus, an interpretable architecture must control both what information enters through a named concept and how that concept contributes to the final prediction.

这些要求使得基于概念的预测成为我们研究的最合适的基础。与注意力或显着性不同，概念可以直接代表发展机制；与事后自然语言原理不同，它们可以参与产生决策的计算。因此，我们采用基于概念的架构，其中视觉语言表示提供语义测量，而命名的发展概念构建特定于儿童的风险评估。概念瓶颈模型为此设计提供了最相关的方法基础。These requirements make concept-based prediction the most appropriate foundation for our study. Unlike attention or saliency, concepts can represent developmental mechanisms directly; unlike post-hoc natural-language rationales, they can participate in the computation that produces the decision. We therefore adopt a concept-based architecture in which vision-language representations provide semantic measurements while named developmental concepts structure the child-specific risk assessment. Concept bottleneck models provide the most relevant methodological foundation for this design.

2.3.概念瓶颈模型2.3. Concept Bottleneck Models

概念瓶颈模型 (CBM) 将人类可理解的概念置于输入表示和最终预测之间，使概念层成为预测路径的一部分 (Koh et al. 2020)。随后的变体丰富了概念的表示方式，包括基于嵌入和概率公式（Zarlenga et al. 2022，Kim et al. 2023）。这种结构对我们的设置很有吸引力，因为用于解释调节决策的变量可以直接参与生成它。Concept bottleneck models (CBMs) place human-understandable concepts between the input representation and the final prediction, making the concept layer part of the predictive pathway (Koh et al. 2020). Subsequent variants enrich how concepts are represented, including embedding-based and probabilistic formulations (Zarlenga et al. 2022, Kim et al. 2023). This structure is attractive for our setting because variables used to explain a moderation decision can participate directly in producing it.

5.1. AI4CDS 作为规范性方法框架5.1. AI4CDS as a Prescriptive Methodological Framework

AI4CDS 是一种规范性方法框架，用于在人工智能参与整个研究生命周期时进行 CDS。 AI4CDS 以人类与人工智能委托研究为基础，将委托问题从任务分配扩展到任务和阶段相关的人工智能自由裁量权，涵盖问题制定、资源构建、设计搜索、评估和知识抽象（Baird 和 Maruping 2021，AI4CDS is a prescriptive methodological framework for conducting CDS when AI participates across the research lifecycle. Building on human–AI delegation research, AI4CDS extends the delegation problem from task allocation to task- and phase-contingent AI discretion across problem formulation, resource construction, design search, evaluation, and knowledge abstraction (Baird and Maruping 2021,

AI4CDS also makes domain requirements traceable across the research process. Domain knowledge helps define the consequential problem and technical challenges, constrains the resources and designs considered admissible, and determines the evidence required for evaluation. The framework therefore treats AI-enabled CDS as a governed research process in which AI expands scientific search but does not determine scientific commitments. Its contribution is to make these roles, transitions, and responsibilities explicit so that AI-enabled CDS processes can be examined, documented, and refined across future studies.

5.5.边界条件和未来 CDS 研究5.5. Boundary Conditions and Future CDS Research

AI4CDS 由一个密集的计算设计项目支持，因此应被视为一种规范性框架，需要跨设置进行进一步检查。该研究并没有证明它的五个阶段是人工智能 CDS 的唯一有效组织，或者 AI4CDS 减少了研究工作，比替代工作流程更有效地扩展了搜索，或者产生了优质的工件。它还无法确定不同的人工智能交互序列是否会产生相同的设计。未来的研究应该将 AI4CDS 与跨 CDS 领域的替代人类人工智能和纯人类流程进行比较，研究人工智能如何影响所考虑的替代方案，研究人员的干预在哪里最重要，分级信任、可逆性和可审计性何时提高研究质量，以及哪些差异化验证标准适合不同的人工智能研究活动。 ChildRiskGuard 的工件级别边界更窄。它的评估涉及一个短片AI4CDS is supported by one intensive computational design project and should therefore be treated as a prescriptive framework that requires further examination across settings. 随之而来的科学转型的治理Governance at consequential scientific transitions

支持人工智能的 CDS 需要区分生成可能的研究成果和授权该成果作为科学记录的一部分。我们将科学承诺定义为研究人员授权的决定，以限制后续工作或支持科学主张的方式促进研究过程中的临时输出、替代方案、分析或解释。示例包括承认资源、保留相应的设计选择、确定评估规范、接受计算结果作为证据，或将观察到的模式抽象为可转让的权利要求。人工智能可以为任何这些目的产生候选者，但产生本身并不构成科学承诺。AI-enabled CDS requires a distinction between generating a possible research output and authorizing that output as part of the scientific record. Lee and See (2004) argue that trust is consequential because it guides reliance when automation is complex and cannot be completely understood, whereas Fügener et al. 可逆性Reversibility

设计固定描述了暴露于初始解决方案以限制后续设计搜索并鼓励继续坚持一组有限的想法的趋势（Jansson 和 Smith 1991）。当人工智能能够快速生成合理且看似完整的设计，然后将其嵌入到资源、代码、评估和手稿声明中时，这种风险尤其重要。设计科学也将构建和评估视为迭代：评估为构建提供反馈，当后续证据暴露缺陷或条件变化时，可能需要修改假设或设计选择（Hevner et al. 2004）。Design fixation describes the tendency for exposure to an initial solution to constrain subsequent design search and encourage continued adherence to a limited set of ideas (Jansson and Smith 1991). This risk is particularly relevant when AI can rapidly produce plausible and seemingly complete designs that are then embedded in resources, code, evaluations, and manuscript claims. Design science also treats construction and evaluation as iterative: evaluation provides feedback to construction, and assumptions or design choices may need to be revised when subsequent evidence exposes deficiencies or changed conditions (Hevner et al. 2004).

AI4CDS 通过可逆性做出回应：早期的科学承诺必须保持可重新开放性AI4CDS responds through Reversibility: an earlier scientific commitment must remain reopenable

当后来的可信证据对承诺所依赖的假设、要求、测量或证据提出实质性挑战时。触发因素不仅仅是新替代方案的可用性，而是对早期理由提出质疑的证据。当这种情况发生时，研究人员会回到最早的实质性承诺，并明确决定是否保留、修改、替换或拒绝它。when credible later evidence materially challenges the assumptions, requirements, measurements, or evidence on which that commitment depended. The trigger is not simply the availability of a new alternative but evidence that calls the earlier justification into question. When this occurs, researchers return to the earliest materially implicated commitment and explicitly decide whether to retain, revise, replace, or reject it.

可逆性并不禁止下游修饰。相反，下游修改不得取代重新考虑上游承诺，而上游承诺的合理性已受到重大矛盾。经过重新考虑，研究人员可能会确定最初的承诺仍然合理，并且下游修改是适当的。可逆性也是双向的：它可以防止保留缺乏支持的选择，但也可以防止由于薄弱、不稳定或孤立的相反证据而过早放弃选择。Reversibility does not prohibit downstream modification. Rather, a downstream modification must not substitute for reconsidering an upstream commitment whose justification has been materially contradicted. After that reconsideration, researchers may determine that the original commitment remains justified and that a downstream modification is appropriate. Reversibility is also bidirectional: it protects against retaining a poorly supported choice, but it also protects against prematurely abandoning a choice because of weak, unstable, or isolated contrary evidence.

计算机化的决策会分散责任，并且很难确定后果如何产生以及谁应对这些结果负责（Nissenbaum 1996）。当人工智能参与研究时，这些担忧变得尤其突出，因为作者身份、转换和决策边界可能变得难以重建。因此，最近针对人工智能支持的信息系统研究的指南强调了来源和验证以及披露，包括人工智能贡献的记录、工作流程历史、模型和数据状态以及后果点的人类决策门（Gopal 等人，2025）。Computerized decision making can diffuse responsibility and make it difficult to identify how consequential outcomes arose and who remains accountable for them (Nissenbaum 1996). These concerns become especially salient when AI participates in research because authorship, transformation, and decision boundaries may become difficult to reconstruct. Recent guidance for AI-enabled IS research accordingly emphasizes provenance and verification alongside disclosure, including records of AI contributions, workflow histories, model and data states, and human decision gates at consequential points (Gopal et al. 2025).

AI4CDS 将这些问题转化为可审计性：随之而来的转变应该留下足够的可检查记录，以便知情的第三方确定人工智能的贡献、研究人员的决定、支持该决定的证据或标准，以及该决定如何影响最终的工件或声明。至少，记录应确定相关背景或输入、人工智能的实质性贡献、人类倾向、决策基础和科学结果。当模型或工具版本、提示、执行规范、数据状态和验证程序对于重建转换至关重要时，还应予以保留。AI4CDS translates these concerns into Auditability: consequential transitions should leave an inspectable record sufficient for an informed third party to determine what AI contributed, what researchers decided, what evidence or criteria supported the decision, and how that decision affected the resulting artifact or claim. At minimum, the record should identify the relevant context or input, the substantive AI contribution, the human disposition, the decision basis, and the scientific consequence. Model or tool versions, prompts, execution specifications, data states, and verification procedures should additionally be preserved when they are material to reconstructing the transition.

因此，可审计性将文档扩展到人工智能支持的交互的科学结果。常规格式、未使用的建议和其他不改变科学承诺的帮助不需要收到与保留的设计决策、报告的结果或可转让权利要求相同的文档。可审计性也不需要访问模型隐藏的内部推理。可审计的科学对象是可观察的研究过程：提供给人工智能的任务和输入、其外部可用的贡献、研究人员的决定、支持证据以及下游结果。Auditability therefore scales documentation to the scientific consequence of an AI-supported interaction. Routine formatting, unused suggestions, and other assistance that does not alter a scientific commitment need not receive the same documentation as a retained design decision, reported result, or transferable claim. Auditability also does not require access to a model’s hidden internal reasoning. The auditable scientific object is the observable research process: the task and inputs supplied to AI, its externally available contribution, the researcher’s decision, the supporting evidence, and the downstream consequence.

D.4。原理间关系、张力和边界条件D.4. Inter-Principle Relationships, Tensions, and Boundary Conditions

这三个原则是互补的，但并非没有代价。首先，分级信任和可逆性共同防止累积的依赖变成不可逆转的承诺。人工智能先前的成功表现可能证明在可比较的、明确指定的任务上有更大的自由裁量权是合理的，但这并不能免除早期决策受到后来相互矛盾的证据的影响。当可靠的证据挑战科学承诺时，可逆性触发器优先于对人工智能或现有设计积累的信心。The three principles are complementary but not costless. First, Graduated Trust and Reversibility jointly prevent accumulated reliance from becoming irreversible commitment. Successful prior AI performance may justify greater discretion on comparable, well-specified tasks, but it does not immunize an earlier decision from later contradictory evidence. When credible evidence challenges a scientific commitment, the Reversibility trigger takes precedence over accumulated confidence in either the AI or the existing design.

其次，可逆性取决于可审计性。重新考虑之前的承诺需要了解做出该承诺的原因、支持该承诺的证据以及随后的决策取决于该承诺。因此，重新提出相应的承诺会产生相应的审计义务：记录应识别新的证据以及该承诺是否被保留、修改、替换或拒绝。此要求并不意味着记录每个较小的迭代；完整的文档适用于随后的逆转。Second, Reversibility depends on Auditability. Reconsidering an earlier commitment requires knowing why it was made, what evidence supported it, and what subsequent decisions depend on it. Reopening a consequential commitment therefore creates a corresponding audit obligation: the record should identify the new evidence and whether the commitment was retained, revised, replaced, or rejected. This requirement does not imply documenting every minor iteration; full documentation applies to consequential reversals.

这些原则在明确的边界条件下定义了 AI4CDS 的规范性治理。它们的应用取决于任务的结果、独立验证的可用性、人工智能系统的特性以及研究环境。 ChildRiskGuard 证明这三个原则可以在相应的计算设计项目中实例化，并连接到可检查的过程记录。需要对不同的 CDS 问题、人工智能能力、研究团队和认知环境进行进一步研究，以测试、完善和界定这些原则提供有效治理的条件。These principles define prescriptive governance for AI4CDS within explicit boundary conditions. Their application depends on the consequence of the task, the availability of independent verification, the properties of the AI system, and the research setting. ChildRiskGuard demonstrates that the three principles can be instantiated within a consequential computational design project and connected to an inspectable process record. Further studies across different CDS problems, AI capabilities, research teams, and epistemic settings are needed to test, refine, and delimit the conditions under which these principles provide effective governance.
