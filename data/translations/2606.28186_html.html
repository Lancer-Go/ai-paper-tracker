Existing approaches typically rely on costly human calibration or item-level textual representations, offering limited interpretable evidence about the cognitive processes that make an item difficult. We argue that item difficulty should be viewed not only as a property of item text, but also as an observable consequence of the problemsolving burden induced by the item. Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) offer scalable process evidence through reasoning traces, but this evidence must be structured to support interpretable modeling of problem-solving dynamics. Cognitive episodes provide this structure by grouping trace segments into functional problem-solving states, enabling difficulty to be interpreted through the reasoning scale, effort allocation, and transitions across states. Building on this formulation, we introduceEpi2Diff（Episode to Difficulty），一个框架，将 LRM 推理轨迹映射到基于认知的情节序列，并提取紧凑的情节动态特征以用于人类项目难度预测。这些功能与语义项目表示相结合，以支持准确的预测和基于过程的解释。对四个真实世界人类难度数据集的实验表明，Epi2Diff 始终优于强大的基线，包括微调的 SLM、LLM 上下文学习和有监督的 LLM 适应。在现实世界中 SAT 衍生的分类基准中，Epi2Diff 比 LLM 监督的微调基准实现了 8.1% 的平均相对增益。进一步的分析表明，较难的项目会引发更加努力、迭代和以实施为中心的事件动态，而不仅仅是更长的反应。这些发现表明，LRM 推理轨迹中的认知事件为人类项目难度提供了预测性和可解释的过程表示，为推理模型的教育测量提供了新的视角。(Episode to Difficulty), a framework that maps LRM reasoning traces into cognitively grounded episode sequences and extracts compact episode-dynamic features for human item difficulty prediction. Across the real-world SAT-derived classification benchmarks, Epi2Diff achieves an 8.1% average relative gain over LLM supervised fine-tuning baselines. Further analyses reveal that harder items induce more effortful, iterative, and implementation-centered episode dynamics, rather than merely longer responses. These findings show that cognitive episodes in LRM reasoning traces provide a predictive and interpretable process representation for human item difficulty, offering a new lens for educational measurement with reasoning models.

Although these traces should not be treated as direct observations of human cognition, they can serve as scalable, model-generated proxies for solution processes.

3.3 项目表示3.3 Item Representation

先前的工作表明，推理痕迹表现出结构化的情节级动态，这表明项目解决问题的负担可能会反映在情节属性和情节间转换中。基于这一见解，我们从标记的推理轨迹构建了基于情节的表示，以对此类负担进行建模并改进项目难度预测。为了确定最有效的表示结构，我们系统地探索了多个级别的情节统计特征，从基本情节分布到多跳转换模式，并凭经验评估了它们在下游项目难度预测中的单独贡献和组合。Prior work has shown that reasoning traces exhibit structured episode-level dynamics, suggesting that the problem-solving burden of an item may be reflected in both episode-wise properties and inter-episode transitions. Building on this insight, we construct an episode-based representation from labeled reasoning traces to model such burden and improve item difficulty prediction. To determine the most effective representation construction, we systematically explored episode-statistic features at multiple levels, ranging from basic episode distributions to multi-hop transition patterns, and empirically evaluated their individual contributions and combinations in downstream item difficulty prediction.

基于此特征搜索和性能比较，我们采用两部分项目表示：（1）项目语义表示，对项目内容进行编码； （2）事件引发的过程表示，用于捕获推理痕迹中反映的解决问题的负担。具体来说，情节引发的过程表示结合了三组互补的特征：捕获推理过程整体规模的长度特征，描述其跨情节类型的组成的情节分布特征，以及通过情节间转换编码其局部顺序组织的转换特征。Based on this feature search and performance comparison, we adopt a two-part item representation: (1) an item semantic representation, which encodes the item content; and (2) episode-induced process representation for capturing the problem-solving burden reflected in reasoning traces. Concretely, the episode-induced process representation combines three complementary groups of features: length features capturing the overall scale of the reasoning process, episode-distribution features characterizing its composition across episode types, and transition features encoding its local sequential organization through inter-episode transitions.

6 结论6 Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个用于项目难度预测的结构化框架，该框架通过解决问题的负担来模拟难度。通过将 LRM 推理跟踪抽象为情节级过程表示并将其与语义项目表示相结合，该框架捕获了项目内容及其倾向于引发的推理工作。对四个基准的实验表明，所提出的方法在分类和回归设置上始终优于广泛的基准。总体而言，结果表明，结构化推理动态为预测人类项目难度提供了有用且可解释的信号。In this work, we proposed a structured framework for item difficulty prediction that models difficulty through problem-solving burden. By abstracting LRM reasoning traces into episode-level process representations and combining them with semantic item representations, the framework captures both item content and the reasoning effort it tends to elicit. Experiments on four benchmarks show that the proposed method consistently outperforms a broad range of baselines across both classification and regression settings. Overall, the results suggest that structured reasoning dynamics provide a useful and interpretable signal for predicting human item difficulty.

局限性Limitation

尽管所提出的框架使用结构化的情节级表示来对项目难度进行建模，但本研究中考虑的推理轨迹是由一组有限的 LRM 生成的。中间推理的冗长性、分解粒度、痕迹组织和明确性的差异可能会影响诱导的情节分布、转换模式和下游预测。因此，虽然我们的结果证明了所提出的表示在评估设置下的有效性，但在更广泛的推理跟踪样式中进行进一步验证将是有价值的。最后，尽管我们对跨越多种评估设置的四个基准进行评估，包括 SAT 数学和阅读、剑桥英语资格和 USMLE，但涵盖的领域、项目格式和标签设置仍然有限。未来的工作可以将该框架扩展到更多样化的评估设置，探索更丰富的事件抽象，并减少生成多个推理轨迹的计算成本，同时保持预测性能。Although the proposed framework uses structured episode-level representations to model item difficulty, the reasoning traces considered in this study are generated by a finite set of LRMs. Differences in verbosity, decomposition granularity, trace organization, and explicitness of intermediate reasoning could affect the induced episode distributions, transition patterns, and downstream predictions. Thus, while our results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed representation under the evaluated settings, further validation across a broader range of reasoning-trace styles would be valuable. Finally, although we evaluate on four benchmarks spanning multiple assessment settings, including SAT math & reading, Cambridge English Qualifications, and USMLE, the covered domains, item formats, and label settings remain limited. Future work can extend the framework to more diverse assessment settings, explore richer episode abstractions, and reduce the computational cost of generating multiple reasoning traces while maintaining predictive performance.

A.1 题目难度预测A.1 Item Difficulty Prediction

估计项目难度传统上依赖于 CTT 或 IRT 下的响应数据校准，这仍然是校准评估的核心，但对于新编写的项目来说成本高昂，因为它通常需要在项目投入运行之前进行现场测试（DeMars，2010；Hsu 等人，2018；AlKhuzaey 等人，2024；Peters 等人，2025）。专家判断也被认为是一种成本较低的替代方案，但先前的工作报告了专家评级与经验心理测量难度估计之间的有限或可变一致性，限制了其作为可扩展替代方案的可靠性（Conejo 等人，2014 年；Wauters 等人，2012 年）。为了减少对响应数据的依赖，早期的 NLP 工作将项目难度预测视为基于文本的监督学习问题。例如，珀金斯等人。 (1995) 使用具有语言、命题和认知过程特征的人工神经网络来处理阅读理解项目，而 Loukina 等人。 (2016) 提取了 100 多个手工制作的文本复杂性特征，例如字长、句法复杂性和可读性指数，以预测语言评估的难度。随后的工作结合了更丰富的语义表示和项目组件相似性，包括基于 Word2Vec 的特征（Hsu 等人，2018），并表明 NLP 派生的语言和句法特征可以预测高风险的医学多项选择题（Yaneva 等人，2019）。神经模型进一步扩展了这条线；例如，何等人。 (2021) 探索了基于 CNN 的阅读理解难度预测模型。总体而言，这些文献已逐渐从手工制作的语言和心理测量代理转向学习的文本表示（AlKhuzaey 等人，2024；Peters 等人，2025）。Estimating item difficulty has traditionally relied on response-data calibration under CTT or IRT, which remains central for calibrated assessment but is costly for newly authored items because it typically requires field testing before items can be operationalized (DeMars, 2010; Hsu et al., 2018; AlKhuzaey et al., 2024; Peters et al., 2025). Expert judgment has also been considered as a lower-cost alternative, but prior work reports limited or variable agreement between expert ratings and empirical psychometric difficulty estimates, limiting its reliability as a scalable substitute (Conejo et al., 2014; Wauters et al., 2012). To reduce this reliance on response data, early NLP work treated item difficulty prediction as a text-based supervised learning problem. For instance, Perkins et al. (1995) used artificial neural networks with linguistic, propositional, and cognitiveprocess features for reading-comprehension items, while Loukina et al. (2016) extracted over 100 hand-crafted textual-complexity features, such as word length, syntactic complexity, and readability indices, to predict difficulty in language assessment. Subsequent work incorporated richer semantic representations and itemcomponent similarities, including Word2Vec-based features (Hsu et al., 2018), and showed that NLP-derived linguistic and syntactic features are predictive for high-stakes medical multiple-choice questions (Yaneva et al., 2019). Neural models further extended this line; for example, He et al. (2021) explored CNN-based models for reading-comprehension difficulty prediction. Overall, this literature has gradually shifted from hand-crafted linguistic and psychometric proxies toward learned textual representations (AlKhuzaey et al., 2024; Peters et al., 2025).

最近，基于 Transformer 的表示已成为项目难度预测的强大基线。麦卡锡等人。 (2021) 在多任务广义线性模型中使用 BERT 特征来快速启动自适应语言测试的项目参数估计，Benedetto (2023) 发现基于 Transformer 的模型在问题难度估计方面可以优于传统的基于特征、TF-IDF 和 Word2Vec 基线（Devlin 等人，2019）。塔克等人。 （2024）结合了统计、机器学习和语言模型方法，同时还表明，更简单的基于特征的模型在某些情况下可以保持竞争力。最近，李等人。 (2025c) 比较了一系列基于 SLM 和 LLM 的方法，发现 BERT 和 RoBERTa 等经过微调的编码器模型在医疗评估基准上可以优于更大但任务专业性较低的 LLM 替代方案（Liu 等人，2019）。总之，这些研究为项目难度预测建立了强大的文本表示基线，但它们主要根据项目文本、元数据、注释、嵌入或其他项目级表示来建模难度，从而较少直接探索过程级解决负担。More recently, transformer-based representations have become strong baselines for item difficulty prediction. McCarthy et al. (2021) used BERT features in a multi-task generalized linear model to jump-start itemparameter estimates for adaptive language testing, and Benedetto (2023) found that Transformer-based models can outperform traditional feature-based, TF-IDF, and Word2Vec baselines for question difficulty estimation (Devlin et al., 2019). Tack et al. (2024) combined statistical, machine-learning, and language-model approaches, while also showing that simpler feature-based models can remain competitive in some settings. Most recently, Li et al. (2025c) compared a broad family of SLM- and LLM-based approaches and found that fine-tuned encoder models such as BERT and RoBERTa can outperform larger but less task-specialized LLM alternatives on a medical assessment benchmark (Liu et al., 2019). In conclusion, these studies establish strong text-representation baselines for item difficulty prediction, but they largely model difficulty from item text, metadata, annotations, embeddings, or other item-level representations, leaving process-level solving burden less directly explored.

A.2 基于 LLM 的项目难度预测方法A.2 LLM-Based Methods for Item Difficulty Prediction

随着法学硕士的出现，最近的工作探索使用模型输出和行为作为项目难度预测的附加信号。其中一项工作要求法学硕士回答项目、模拟考生般的反应、生成理由或直接从项目内容估计难度。例如，Rogoz 和 Ionescu (2024) 使用零样本 LLM 生成的答案和答案可变性来支持多项选择题难度和响应时间预测。杜尼亚斯等人。 (2024) 模拟医学候选人在提示场景中的反应，并从这些反应中提取特征以进行下游预测。更广泛地说，李等人。 (2025a)表明，强大的项目解决能力并不一定转化为对人类难度的准确估计，这表明直接的法学硕士判断可能反映以模型为中心的难度，而不是经验性的人类难度。同样，陈等人。 （2026）发现法学硕士与人类校准的项目歧视仅表现出有限的一致性，这表明难度并不是对基于法学硕士的评估仍然具有挑战性的唯一心理测量属性。另一项工作较少使用法学硕士作为端到端的判断者，而更多地使用法学硕士作为辅助预测信号的生成器。这些信号包括基于LLM的管道，使用语义和语言特征，例如命名实体和语义角色（Veeramani等人，2024）、基于推理和采样的特征（Feng等人，2025a）、不确定性感知特征（Zotos等人，2024）、直接评级和LLM提取的认知或语言特征与基于树的机器学习模型相结合（Razavi和Powers， 2025），以及针对项目难度建模的微调 SLM/LLM 表示（Li 等人，2025c）。With the emergence of LLMs, recent work has explored using model outputs and behavior as additional signals for item difficulty prediction. One line of work prompts LLMs to answer items, simulate examinee-like responses, generate rationales, or directly estimate difficulty from item content. For example, Rogoz and Ionescu (2024) uses zero-shot LLM-generated answers and answer variability to support multiple-choice item difficulty and response-time prediction. Dueñas et al. (2024) simulates medical-candidate responses across prompting scenarios and extracts features from these responses for downstream prediction. More broadly, Li et al. (2025a) shows that strong item-solving ability does not necessarily translate into accurate estimates of human difficulty, suggesting that direct LLM judgments may reflect model-centric difficulty rather than empirical human difficulty. Similarly, Chen et al. (2026) finds that LLMs show only limited alignment with human-calibrated item discrimination, indicating that difficulty is not the only psychometric property that remains challenging for LLM-based assessment. Another line of work uses LLMs less as end-to-end judges and more as generators of auxiliary predictive signals. These signals include LLM-based pipelines using semantic and linguistic features such as named entities and semantic roles (Veeramani et al., 2024), reasoning- and sampling-based features (Feng et al., 2025a), uncertainty-aware features (Zotos et al., 2024), direct ratings and LLM-extracted cognitive or linguistic features combined with tree-based machine-learning models (Razavi and Powers, 2025), and fine-tuned SLM/LLM representations for item difficulty modeling (Li et al., 2025c).

这些研究表明，LLM 的输出可以为项目难度预测提供有用的辅助线索，但它们并不完全优于强大的专业基线，并且它们提取的信号通常被视为预测特征，而不是与人类心理测量难度一致的解决过程的结构化、可解释的证据。These studies indicate that LLM outputs can provide useful auxiliary cues for item difficulty prediction, but they do not uniformly outperform strong specialized baselines, and their extracted signals are usually treated as predictive features rather than structured, interpretable evidence of solving processes aligned with human psychometric difficulty.

一项相关但独特的工作研究了更广泛的法学硕士学习和评估环境中的困难。例如，先前的研究探讨了难度如何形成从易到难的泛化、跨难度转移以及基准设计，其中难度按所需的推理长度进行缩放（Hase 等人，2024；Kordi 等人，2026；Ding 等人，2025）。这项工作是相关的，因为它表明难度会影响模型行为和评估，但它主要并不涉及预测教育评估中经经验校准的人类项目难度。A related but distinct body of work studies the difficulty in broader LLM learning and evaluation settings. For example, prior studies examine how difficulty shapes easy-to-hard generalization, cross-difficulty transfer, and benchmark design where difficulty is scaled by required reasoning length (Hase et al., 2024; Kordi et al., 2026; Ding et al., 2025). This work is relevant because it shows that difficulty can affect model behavior and evaluation, but it is not primarily concerned with predicting empirically calibrated human item difficulty in educational assessment.

A.3 将轨迹推理为过程信号A.3 Reasoning Traces as Process Signals

并行的研究问题不是法学硕士是否可以直接判断难度，而是所生成的推理轨迹是否包含有关模型如何处理问题的结构化信息。思想链提示使中间推理步骤变得明确（Wei et al., 2022），而更新的推理导向模型通过产生更长的、人类可读的痕迹进一步放大了这一趋势，这些痕迹可能会在推理过程中暴露自我反思、验证和策略适应（Guo et al., 2025）。最近关于数学推理和推理语言模型的工作进一步将这些痕迹置于更广泛的努力中，以分析模型推理行为和组织（Ahn 等人，2024；Besta 等人，2025）。这表明推理痕迹可能反映了超越冗长的模型端过程结构，尽管它们不应被解释为对人类认知的直接观察。A parallel line of research asks not whether LLMs can directly judge difficulty, but whether generated reasoning traces contain structured information about how models process problems. Chain-of-thought prompting made intermediate reasoning steps explicit (Wei et al., 2022), and newer reasoning-oriented models further amplified this trend by producing longer, human-readable traces that may expose self-reflection, verification, and strategy adaptation during inference (Guo et al., 2025). Recent work on mathematical reasoning and reasoning language models further situates such traces within broader efforts to analyze model reasoning behavior and organization (Ahn et al., 2024; Besta et al., 2025). This suggests that reasoning traces may reflect model-side process structure beyond verbosity, although they should not be interpreted as direct observations of human cognition.

最近的工作已经开始通过更明确的结构抽象来实施这个想法。例如，李等人。 (2025d) 将 Schoenfeld 的情节理论应用于推理模型轨迹，并表明模型生成的解决方案可以分解为可解释的功能情节。更一般地说，对推理效率和跟踪质量的研究表明，有效的推理不能仅仅简化为响应长度；相反，它取决于模型如何在探索、执行、审查和验证之间分配工作量，并且较长的跟踪可能包含冗余或低效的推理（Chen 等人，2024；Feng 等人，2025b；Bogdan 等人，2025；Aggarwal 和 Welleck，2025；Fan 等人，2025）。尽管如此，大多数先前基于痕迹的工作都集中在可解释性、行为诊断或推理效率分析上，而不是使用推理痕迹作为下游人类项目难度估计的结构化预测信号。这就留下了我们工作的核心问题：LRM 推理轨迹是否可以转化为可扩展和可解释的过程级信号，以估计人类处理不可见项目的难度。Recent work has started to operationalize this idea through more explicit structural abstractions. For example, Li et al. (2025d) applies Schoenfeld’s Episode Theory to reasoning-model traces and shows that model-generated solutions can be decomposed into interpretable functional episodes. More generally, studies on reasoning efficiency and trace quality suggest that effective reasoning cannot be reduced to response length alone; rather, it depends on how models allocate effort across exploration, execution, review, and verification, and longer traces may contain redundant or inefficient reasoning (Chen et al., 2024; Feng et al., 2025b; Bogdan et al., 2025; Aggarwal and Welleck, 2025; Fan et al., 2025). Still, most prior trace-based work has focused on interpretability, behavioral diagnosis, or reasoning-efficiency analysis, rather than using reasoning traces as structured predictive signals for downstream human item difficulty estimation. This leaves open the central question of our work: whether LRM reasoning traces can be transformed into scalable and interpretable process-level signals for estimating the human difficulty of unseen items.
