摘要Abstract

近端策略优化（PPO）由于其跨领域的可扩展性和经验鲁棒性，已成为策略强化学习的主要算法。然而，信任域方法的底层基础与 PPO 中使用的启发式裁剪目标之间存在显着脱节。 在本文中，我们通过介绍来弥补这一差距Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) has become the predominant algorithm for on-policy reinforcement learning due to its scalability and empirical robustness across domains. However, there is a significant disconnect between the underlying foundations of trust region methods and the heuristic clipped objective used in PPO. In this paper, we bridge this gap by introducing the有界比率强化学习 (BRRL)Bounded Ratio Reinforcement Learning (BRRL)框架。 我们提出了一个新颖的正则化和约束策略优化问题，并推导出其解析最优解。我们证明这个解决方案保证了单调性 性能改进。为了处理参数化策略类，我们开发了一种策略优化算法，称为framework. We formulate a novel regularized and constrained policy optimization problem and derive its analytical optimal solution. We prove that this solution ensures monotonic performance improvement. 1 简介Introduction

深度强化学习（DRL）在不同领域取得突破Deep reinforcement learning (DRL) has achieved breakthroughs across diverse domainssilver2017mastering ; lee2020learning ; ouyang2022training ; radosavovic2024real 。 DRL方法中，近端策略优化（PPO）. Among DRL methods, Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)schulman2017proximal 仍然是最广泛采用的算法之一。 PPO 的核心设计受到信任区域策略优化（TRPO，remains one of the most widely adopted algorithms. The core design of PPO is motivated by Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO,schulman2015trust ），将策略更新限制在“信任区域”内，以确保稳定的迭代。 通过利用 TRPO 目标的一阶近似，PPO 实现了训练现代大型模型所需的可扩展性。 因此，PPO 及其变体 GRPO 现在广泛应用于从机器人到大语言模型 (LLM) 微调的任务), which constrains policy updates within a “trust region” to ensure stable iterations. By utilizing a first-order approximation of the TRPO objective, PPO achieves the scalability necessary for training modern large-scale models. As a result, PPO and its variant GRPO are now widely applied to tasks ranging from robotics to large language model (LLM) fine-tuningmiki2022learning ; andrychowicz2020learning ; shao2024deepseekmath .

尽管在经验上取得了成功，PPO 在很大程度上仍然是启发式的：它的修剪目标并不是直接源自它想要近似的信任域公式。相反，PPO 目标的设计主要是由实验驱动的Despite its empirical success, PPO remains largely heuristic: its clipped objective is not directly derived from the trust-region formulation it was intended to approximate. Instead, the design of the PPO objective was primarily driven by experimentationschulman2017proximal ; engstrom2020implementation 。 此外，现有的PPO绩效提升理论分析大多依赖于原始TRPO或政策梯度公式. Furthermore, most existing theoretical analyses of PPO’s performance improvement rely on the original TRPO or policy gradient formulationschulman2015trust ; liu2019neural ; doering2026approximate ，它们都没有完全捕捉到实践中使用的一阶损失的细微差别。, none of which fully capture the nuances of the first-order loss used in practice.

最近提出了许多变体来改善 PPO。 一些作品主要关注算法设计并报告经验性能增益，而没有正式的理论贡献Numerous variants have been recently proposed to improve PPO. Some works focus primarily on algorithm design and report empirical performance gains without formal theoretical contributionscobbe2021phasic ; ye2020mastering ; tan2024beyond ; fakoor2020p3o ; kobayashi2021proximal 。 其他工作将 PPO 扩展到特定领域（例如安全 RL、非平稳 RL），而不修改核心 PPO 损失函数. Other works extend PPO to specific domains (e.g., safe RL, non-stationary RL) without modifying the core PPO loss functionakgul2025overcoming ; milosevic2025central 。 还有一些 PPO 变体旨在改善理论镜头的 PPO 损失. There are also PPO variants aiming at improving the PPO loss from a theoretical lensxie2024simple ; wang2020truly ; wang2019trust ; qi2026rethinking 。 然而，与PPO类似，他们也利用TRPO理论，但没有引入新颖的理论框架或建立卓越的性能保证。 因此，理论基础和实际策略优化算法之间仍然存在很大差距。. However, similar to PPO, they also utilize TRPO theory without introducing novel theoretical frameworks or establishing superior performance guarantees. Consequently, there remains a substantial gap between the theoretical foundations and the practical policy optimization algorithms.
