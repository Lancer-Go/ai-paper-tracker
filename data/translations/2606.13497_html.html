1 简介Introduction

机器人演示的空间注释可以识别正在操纵的对象、它的位置以及它如何随时间移动。这些注释是现代机器人学习流程的关键组成部分，包括以对象为中心的推理Spatial annotations of robot demonstrations identify which object is being manipulated, where it is located, and how it moves through time. These annotations are a key ingredient in modern robot learning pipelines, including object-centric reasoning in视觉-语言-动作模型 (VLA)Vision-Language-Action Models (VLAs) [67, 70, 2]，视觉基础策略的像素级场景理解, pixel-level scene understanding for visually grounded policies[42]，并体现为, and embodied视觉语言模型 (VLM)Vision-Language Model (VLM)训练中期mid-training[68, 15, 32, 17, 12].

大规模可靠地生成此类注释仍然很困难。 真实世界的演示包含混乱、遮挡和较大的视点变化。 现有的自动化管道通常将检测器与跟踪器链接起来Producing such annotations reliably at scale remains difficult. Real-world demonstrations contain clutter, occlusion, and large viewpoint variation. Existing automated pipelines typically chain a detector with a tracker[67, 2, 42, 56]，使探测器成为物体身份的唯一来源。检测器置信度衡量的是识别置信度，而不是检测到的物体是否是实际被操纵的物体。 因此，在杂乱的场景中，此类管道可以高置信度地锁定看似合理但不正确的对象，并随时间传播该错误。 这种不匹配造成了直接的规模质量权衡。检测器置信度的硬阈值要么保留许多不正确的注释，要么丢弃许多有效的注释。 注释时的人工审查可靠但昂贵, making the detector the sole source of object identity. Detector confidence measures recognition confidence, not whether the detected object is the one actually being manipulated. In cluttered scenes, such pipelines can therefore lock onto a plausible but incorrect object with high confidence and propagate that error through time. This mismatch creates a direct scale-quality tradeoff. Hard thresholds on detector confidence either retain many incorrect annotations or discard many valid ones. Human review at annotation time is reliable but expensive[55, 39, 53, 41, 32]。模拟提供了真实情况. Simulation provides ground-truth[65, 72]但在现实世界的转移中遇到了困难。but struggles with real world transfer.

5 结论Conclusion

我们介绍We introduce斯帕克SPARC，一个自动空间注释管道，通过物理基础的运动线索而不是检测器置信度对候选者进行评分。 由此产生的可靠性评分使得质量和规模之间的权衡变得可控，无需任何人工审核。, an automatic spatial annotation pipeline that scores candidates with physically grounded motion cues rather than detector confidence. The resulting reliability score makes the trade-off between quality and scale controllable without any human review.斯帕克SPARC比基于检测的基线产生更准确的注释，并在固定精度目标处保留更多可用数据。根据我们的注释训练的模型超过了训练过的模型 基于对象基础和运动感知基准的人类注释数据，以及针对它们训练的推理策略在混乱的现实世界操作中更加稳健。yields more accurate annotations than detection-based baselines and retains more usable data at a fixed precision target. Models trained on our annotations surpass those trained on human-annotated data across object-grounding and motion-aware benchmarks, and reasoning policies trained on them are substantially more robust in cluttered real-world manipulation.

局限性。Limitations. 斯帕克SPARC受到其构建的现成模型的限制：检测器错误限制了零件级注释，跟踪器身份切换发生在视觉上相似的物体上，以及透明表面的 3D 提升困难。它假设相机大部分是静态的，因此跟踪在强烈运动下会降低，并且依赖于精确的相位间隔，从而使上游子任务分割错误向下游传播。最后，is bounded by the off-the-shelf models it builds on: detector errors limit part-level annotations, tracker identity switches occur for visually similar objects, and 3D lifting struggles with transparent surfaces. It assumes a mostly static camera, so tracking degrades under strong motion, and relies on accurate phase intervals, letting upstream subtask-segmentation errors propagate downstream.
