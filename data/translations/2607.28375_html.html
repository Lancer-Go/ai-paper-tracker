HyperClaim：用于视频错误信息检测的细粒度跨模态超图推理HyperClaim: Fine-Grained Cross-Modal Hypergraph Reasoning for Video Misinformation Detection

Xiangbo Wang1, Jiasheng Zhang2, Xingtong Yu3, Luoqiang Lei1, Delvin Ce Zhang4\corresponding
摘要Abstract

视频错误信息检测通常通过全局多模态融合或自由形式多模态推理来实现。这两种范式都不能充分体现局部真实性线索，这些线索是由查询短语、上下文文本和帧的短时间跨度之间的耦合交互产生的。由于这种相互作用本质上是高阶的，因此成对图公式不足以捕获多路跨模式依赖关系，而超图为这些关系提供了合适的表示。我们提出了 HyperClaim，一种用于样本级别真实性分类的判别性时间超图框架。使用标题或基准提供的配对文本作为类似声明的查询，HyperClaim 在查询标记、证据标记和采样帧上构建稀疏异构超图；应用置信感知过滤和源预算来形成紧凑的文本框架和短程时间证据单元；通过残余文本-视频校准执行自适应软关联推理；并通过差异感知读数聚合文本、视觉和超边缘状态。在不依赖生成的基本原理或外部工具调用的情况下，HyperClaim 保留了全局融合趋于扁平化的细粒度跨模式和时间结构。在 FactGuard 时间协议下，它在 FakeSV、FakeTT 和 FakeVV 上分别达到 83.7%、82.0% 和 87.3% 的准确率，优于强判别性和以推理为中心的基线。学习到的发生率和注意力权重进一步揭示了令牌和框架级别的结构证据路径。Video misinformation detection is often approached through global multimodal fusion or free-form multimodal reasoning. Both paradigms can under-represent localized authenticity cues that arise from coupled interactions among query phrases, contextual text, and short temporal spans of frames. Because such interactions are inherently higher-order, pairwise graph formulations are insufficient to capture multi-way cross-modal dependencies, whereas hypergraphs offer a suitable representation for these relations. We propose HyperClaim, a discriminative temporal hypergraph framework for sample-level authenticity classification. Using the title or benchmark-provided paired text as a claim-like query, HyperClaim constructs a sparse heterogeneous hypergraph over query tokens, evidence tokens, and sampled frames; applies confidence-aware filtering and source budgeting to form compact text–frame and short-range temporal evidence units; performs adaptive soft-incidence reasoning with residual text–video calibration; and aggregates textual, visual, and hyperedge states through a discrepancy-aware readout. Without relying on generated rationales or external tool calls, HyperClaim preserves fine-grained cross-modal and temporal structure that global fusion tends to flatten. Under the FactGuard temporal protocol, it achieves 83.7%, 82.0%, and 87.3% accuracy on FakeSV, FakeTT, and FakeVV, respectively, outperforming strong discriminative and reasoning-centric baselines. Learned incidence and attention weights further reveal token- and frame-level structural evidence paths.

简介Introduction

短视频已成为网络新闻和舆论的主要渠道Short-form video has become a major channel for online news and public discourse(Qi et al. 2023a; Bu et al. 2024)。它结合了语言、图像、音频衍生文本和时间编辑，也使得错误信息难以从任何单一模式中识别出来。误导性内容可能会在新的上下文中重复使用真实的镜头，修改随附文本中的实体或位置，或者在不受支持的叙述下呈现简短的片段. Its combination of language, imagery, audio-derived text, and temporal editing also enables misinformation that is difficult to identify from any single modality. Misleading content may reuse authentic footage in a new context, modify an entity or location in the accompanying text, or present a short segment under an unsupported narrative(Luo, Darrell, and Rohrbach 2021; Papadopoulos et al. 2024; Bu et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2025)。在这种情况下，大多数样本可能仍然可信，而决定性的证据仅限于标题短语、一小组文本提示或框架或短暂的时间过渡。其他情况依赖于更广泛的社交、情境或编辑信号. In such cases, most of the sample may remain plausible while the decisive evidence is confined to a title phrase, a small set of textual cues or frames, or a brief temporal transition. Other cases rely on broader social, contextual, or editing signals(Qi et al. 2023a, b; Bu et al. 2024)。因此，有效的检测应该保留局部的跨模式证据，而不是假设每个误导性样本都会减少到一个明确的矛盾。. Effective detection should therefore preserve localized cross-modal evidence without assuming that every misleading sample reduces to a single explicit contradiction.

现有的视频错误信息检测器利用多模态相关性、社会背景、创作过程线索、一致性建模和领域泛化Existing video misinformation detectors exploit multimodal correlation, social context, creative-process cues, consistency modeling, and domain generalization(Shang et al. 2021; Qi et al. 2023a, b; Bu et al. 2024; Zong et al. 2024; Guo et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2025b)。大多数将异质证据压缩为模态或样本级别的表示。虽然对于捕获全局语义有效，但这种聚合可以减少仅涉及特定标记、框架或短时间关系的不一致。以推理为中心的系统追求不同的方向：Fact-R1 通过指令调整、偏好优化和强化学习来学习长形式多模态推理. Most condense heterogeneous evidence into modality- or sample-level representations. Although effective for capturing global semantics, this aggregation can dilute inconsistencies involving only a specific token, frame, or short temporal relation. Reasoning-centric systems pursue a different direction: Fact-R1 learns long-form multimodal reasoning through instruction tuning, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning(Zhang et al. 2025)，而 FactGuard 通过选择性工具的使用执行模糊性感知验证, while FactGuard performs ambiguity-aware verification with selective tool use(Li et al. 2026c)。这些方法对于开放式推理和外部验证非常有价值。我们研究了一种互补的封闭输入设置，其中相关信号已经存在，但分布在标题、上下文文本和时间顺序帧中。核心挑战是表示和聚合这种局部证据结构，而又不会因全球融合而丢失它。. These approaches are valuable for open-ended inference and externally grounded verification. We study a complementary closed-input setting in which the relevant signal is already present but distributed across the title, contextual text, and temporally ordered frames. The central challenge is to represent and aggregate this localized evidence structure without losing it through global fusion.
