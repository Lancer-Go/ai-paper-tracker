摘要Abstract

随着大型语言模型 (LLM) 融入日常交流，捕捉区域语言差异对于可靠和公平的语言使用至关重要。 在葡萄牙语中，欧洲 (pt-PT) 和巴西 (pt-BR) 变体的代表性仍然不均衡，其中 pt-BR 在数据量上占主导地位，而法学硕士对葡萄牙语变体的偏好仍未得到充分探索。 为了解决这一差距，我们引入了 P3B3，这是一种专家策划的与语言多样性无关的对话提示基准，以及用于衡量多样性偏差和可控性的评估框架。 对多个模型的实验表明，大多数法学硕士对 pt-BR 表现出强烈的偏见，不同模型的可控性存在差异。这些结果凸显了跨语言品种更平衡的多语言表示的需要。As Large Language Models (LLMs) become embedded in everyday communication, capturing regional linguistic variation is essential for reliable and equitable language use. In Portuguese, European (pt-PT) and Brazilian (pt-BR) varieties remain unevenly represented, with pt-BR dominating in data quantity, while LLM preference for Portuguese variants remains underexplored. To address this gap, we introduce P3B3, an expert-curated language variety agnostic benchmark of conversational prompts, along with an evaluation framework for measuring variety bias and controllability. Experiments on several models show that most LLMs exhibit a strong bias toward pt-BR, with variation in controllability across models. These results highlight the need for more balanced multilingual representation across language varieties.111https://github.com/Amalia-LLM/p3b3-benchmark

1 简介Introduction

法学硕士越来越多地用于日常交流，支持客户服务、创意写作和教育工具等应用。随着这些系统得到更广泛的部署，它们捕捉区域语言差异的能力对于可靠和公平的语言使用以及语言和文化多样性的保护至关重要LLMs are increasingly used in everyday communication, supporting applications such as customer service, creative writing, and educational tools. As these systems become more widely deployed, their ability to capture regional linguistic variation is critical for reliable and equitable language use, as well as for linguistic and cultural diversity preservationAepli et al. (2023); Chiu et al. (2025).

葡萄牙语是一种多中心语言，全世界有超过 2.5 亿使用者。虽然它有一个共同的核心，但欧洲葡萄牙语 (pt-PT) 和巴西葡萄牙语 (pt-BR) 之间的差异很大，涵盖词汇选择（例如，Portuguese is a pluricentric language with over 250 million speakers worldwide. While it shares a common core, the divergence between European Portuguese (pt-PT) and Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR) is substantial, spanning lexical choices (e.g.,奥托卡罗autocarro与vs.奥尼布斯ônibus）、地址形式（例如，), forms of address (e.g.,tu与vs.沃塞você) 和语法（例如，附着词位置和动名词用法）), and syntax (e.g., clitic placement and gerund usage)Rio-Torto et al. (2022); Preda et al. (2024)。尽管存在这些差异，大多数培训和评估框架都将葡萄牙语视为单一、统一的语言. Despite these differences, most training and evaluation frameworks treat Portuguese as a single, uniform languageTeam (2024, 2025c); AI (2026)。鉴于巴西数据在大规模数据集中的普遍存在. Given the strong prevalence of Brazilian data in large-scale datasetsPenedo et al. (2024); Common Crawl (2026)，这通常会导致沉默的偏见，即使上下文表明是 pt-PT，模型也默认为 pt-BR, this often leads to a silent bias, where models default to pt-BR even when the context suggests pt-PTSimplício et al. (2026); Riley et al. (2023); González et al. (2026)。这种行为会降低用户体验并导致语言水平不均匀，而法学硕士中的区域差异代表性不足。. This behavior can degrade user experience and contribute to linguistic uneven leveling, where regional variation is underrepresented in LLMs.

这种不平衡提出了两个关键问题：(i) 目前的法学硕士在多大程度上表现出对一种葡萄牙语变体的偏好，以及 (ii) 模型是否可以转向目标变体。This imbalance raises two key questions: (i) to what extent do current LLMs exhibit a preference for one Portuguese variant, and (ii) can models be steered toward a target variant.

面对这些问题，我们的贡献是三重的。首先，我们介绍 pt-PT/pt-BR 偏差基准 (P3B3)，这是一个由 74 个专家策划的、与品种无关的多轮对话组成的基准，旨在在模型响应中引出葡萄牙语品种特定的语言标记。其次，我们提出了一个结合分类器的经过人类验证的评估框架Facing these questions, our contributions are threefold. First, we introduce the pt-PT/pt-BR Bias Benchmark (P3B3), a benchmark of 74 expert-curated, variety-agnostic multi-turn dialogues designed to elicit Portuguese variety-specific linguistic markers in model responses. Second, we propose a human validated evaluation framework combining classifiersSousa et al. (2025)和法学硕士作为法官的方法and LLM-as-Judge methodsGu et al. (2024)评估变异对齐和可控性。最后，我们对不同的法学硕士进行了广泛的评估，揭示了对 pt-BR 的偏见以及在转向 pt-PT 方面取得的不同成功。to assess variant alignment and controllability. Finally, we conduct an extensive evaluation of diverse LLMs, revealing a bias toward pt-BR and variable success in steering generation toward pt-PT.

3 P3B3 基准The P3B3 Benchmark

P3B3的新颖之处在于系统地评估了LLM是否表现出对葡萄牙语变体的偏好以及这种行为的可控性。具体来说，P3B3 是围绕三个准则设计的：(i)The novelty of P3B3 lies in systematically evaluating whether LLMs exhibit preferences toward Portuguese variants and the controllability of such behavior. Specifically, P3B3 is designed around three guidelines: (i)与品种无关Variety-agnostic，没有明确引用输入中的变体以防止启动效应； (二), with no explicit references to variants in the input to prevent priming effects; (ii)品种敏感性Variety Sensitivity，其中提示引出了葡萄牙语变体之间自然的词汇和语法差异； (三), where prompts elicit natural lexical and grammatical differences between Portuguese varieties; and (iii)会话型Conversational，具有积累对话历史的多轮示例。, with multi-turn examples that accumulate dialogue history.

这些指导方针确保变异是从生成行为中产生的，而不是来自明确的线索或孤立的输入。These guidelines ensure that variation emerges from generation behavior rather than explicit cues or isolated inputs.

3.1 基于专家的对话创建Expert-Based Dialogue Creation

关注 MT 工作台Following MT-BenchZheng et al. (2023)，P3B3由74个多回合对话组成（共203回合），每回合2到6回合。与 MT-Bench 一样，后来的用户输入建立在先前的对话上下文之上，无需在早期回合中做出特定响应，从而能够隔离生成行为，同时保持对话的连贯性。 这种设计可以实现累积的上下文效应，这可能会放大单轮提示中无法观察到的偏差。, P3B3 consists of 74 multi-turn dialogues (203 turns in total), each with 2 to 6 turns. As in MT-Bench, later user inputs build on prior dialogue context without requiring specific responses in earlier turns, enabling isolation of generative behavior while maintaining conversational coherence. This design enables cumulative context effects that may amplify biases not observable in single-turn prompts.

为了确保质量和有效性，P3B3由两位拥有语言学相关领域硕士学位且拥有葡萄牙语变体专业知识的语言专家手动构建。拥有这些专业知识对于查明微妙但系统的变化线索至关重要，特别是在词汇、拼写和称呼形式方面To ensure quality and validity, P3B3 was manually constructed by two language experts holding Master’s degrees in linguistics-related fields and with expertise in Portuguese variants. (2022)，同时保持与变量无关。, while remaining variant-agnostic.

6 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 P3B3，这是一个多轮基准，用于评估跨葡萄牙变体的 LLM 生成的偏差和可控性。 大多数模型都对 pt-BR 表现出强烈的偏见，虽然法学硕士可以转向不同的品种，但更大和更新的模型显示出更大的可控性。然而，不同模型之间的 pt-PT 表现仍然不太一致，这凸显了在训练和评估中更好地表示语言变化的需要。We introduced P3B3, a multi-turn benchmark for evaluating bias and controllability in LLM generation across Portuguese variants. Most models exhibit a strong bias toward pt-BR, and while LLMs can be steered toward different varieties, larger and newer models show greater controllability. However, pt-PT performance remains less consistent across models, highlighting the need for better representation of linguistic variation in training and evaluation.

局限性Limitations

P3B3 对法学硕士在会话领域对葡萄牙语变体的遵守情况进行了系统评估。当前的设置不包括技术或专业领域，专注于日常主题。此外，虽然 P3B3 支持对 pt-BR 和 pt-PT 的分析，但将其扩展到安哥拉、莫桑比克和佛得角等国家使用的其他葡萄牙语变体是未来工作的一个有前途的方向。P3B3 provides a systematic evaluation of LLM adherence to Portuguese varieties in conversational domains. The current setup does not include technical or specialized domains, focusing on everyday topics. Moreover, while P3B3 supports analysis of pt-BR and pt-PT, extending it to other Portuguese varieties spoken in countries such as Angola, Mozambique, and Cape Verde is a promising direction for future work.
