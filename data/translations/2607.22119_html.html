摘要Abstract

多流机器人操纵策略通过对相对于环境参考系的动作进行建模，实现了无与伦比的样本效率和泛化性。 然而，现有的方法通常假设这些框架是严格外生的。 这种因果假设在动态环境中崩溃，例如当单个机器人手臂操纵移动物体时或当两个手臂协调时，每个手臂实际上成为另一个手臂动态环境的一部分。 我们提出了 DynaMAC，一个轻量级的、与策略无关的框架，它解决了这种因果限制，同时保留了多流策略的样本效率、计算速度和灵活性， DynaMAC 将另一只手臂视为动态任务参数，从而为动态操作和双手协调提供统一的公式，而不需要明确的领导者-跟随者关系。 为了严格评估这些功能，我们引入了 DynaBench，这是动态环境中机器人操作的新颖基准。 在动态环境和双手操作任务中，DynaMAC 的性能优于领先的概率和生成基线超过 35 个百分点Multi-stream robot manipulation policies achieve unparalleled sample efficiency and generalization by modeling actions relative to environmental reference frames. However, existing approaches typically assume these frames to be strictly exogenous. This causal assumption collapses in dynamic settings, such as when a single robot arm manipulates a moving object or when two arms coordinate, where each arm effectively becomes part of the dynamic environment of the other. We propose DynaMAC, a lightweight, policy-agnostic framework that resolves this causal limitation while preserving the sample efficiency, computational speed, and flexibility of multi-stream policies, DynaMAC treats the opposite arm as a dynamic task parameter, thereby providing a unified formulation for dynamic manipulation and bimanual coordination without requiring an explicit leader-follower relationship. To rigorously evaluate these capabilities, we introduce DynaBench, a novel benchmark for robot manipulation in dynamic environments. Across both dynamic environments and bimanual manipulation tasks, DynaMAC outperforms leading probabilistic and generative baselines by over 35 percentage points同时while所需样品减少 20 倍。 至关重要的是，DynaMAC 将零样本从静态演示推广到动态环境，大大简化了数据收集，并为人机协作建立了一座优雅的桥梁。requiring 20 times fewer samples. Crucially, DynaMAC generalizes zero-shot from static demonstrations to dynamic environments, substantially simplifying data collection and establishing an elegant bridge toward human-robot collaboration.

I 简介Introduction

现实世界的机器人不能假设一个静态的世界。 他们必须对移动的物体做出反应并与其他代理协调。 模仿学习或从演示中学习，已成为机器人操作的主导范例Real-world robots cannot assume a static world. They must react to moving objects and coordinate with other agents. Imitation Learning or Learning from Demonstration, has emerged as a dominant paradigm for robotic manipulation[1]，但仍然受到严重的数据瓶颈的制约, but it remains constrained by a severe data bottleneck[2]。 长任务范围、高维动作空间和严格的精度要求通常需要大量的训练数据。 与此同时，高质量的演示的收集成本很高，而且在动态环境中尤其稀缺. Long task horizons, high-dimensional action spaces, and stringent precision requirements typically demands large amounts of training data. At the same time, high-quality demonstrations are expensive to collect and particularly scarce in dynamic environments[3]。 因此，强归纳偏差对于在不牺牲性能的情况下减少数据需求至关重要。. Strong inductive biases are therefore essential for reducing data requirements without sacrificing performance.

通过将工作空间的底层几何结构直接嵌入到策略架构中，多流学习已成为一种样本效率极高的解决方案。 多流策略不是学习从原始观察到动作的高度复杂的直接映射，而是将观察条件的动作分布分解为多个以对象为中心的坐标系Multi-stream learning has emerged as an exceptionally sample-efficient solution by embedding the underlying geometric structure of the workspace directly into the policy architecture. Instead of learning a highly complex direct mapping from raw observations to actions, multi-stream policies decompose the observation-conditioned action distribution into multiple object-centric coordinate frames[4, 5, 2, 6, 7]。 在推理时，局部模型，或. This explicit reliance on 3D geometry yields unparalleled data efficiency[2, 7]。 最初是为高斯混合模型开发的. Originally developed for Gaussian Mixture Models[4, 5]，此多流公式已成功扩展到高斯过程（的混合）, this multi-stream formulation has since been successfully extended to (Mixtures of) Gaussian Processes[8, 6]以及流量匹配等生成策略and generative policies such as Flow Matching[7]。 当通过自动技能细分进行增强时. 尽管有这些优点，标准多流学习从根本上受到其对严格外生任务参数的依赖的限制。 假设该策略使用的参考系由环境决定并且独立于机器人自身的动作。 这一假设分解为三类重要且相关的操纵问题：动态环境、多智能体协作和双手协调。 在动态环境中，例如当从移动的传送带上拾取工件时，对象框架最初可能是外源的，但一旦对象被抓取，就会刚性地耦合到机器人。继续将此帧视为独立的任务参数会导致不一致的流融合和策略失败。 多智能体协作使问题变得更加复杂，因为另一个智能体的动作不断改变相关的任务框架，例如在切换或协作提升期间。 最后，大多数多流策略都是为单手操作而设计的，而许多日常任务，例如打开罐子或搬运大物体，需要两只手臂协同工作。Despite these advantages, standard multi-stream learning is fundamentally limited by its reliance on strictly exogenous task parameters. The reference frames used by the policy are assumed to be determined by the environment and independent of the robot’s own actions. This assumption breaks down in three important and related classes of manipulation problems: dynamic environments, multi-agent collaboration, and bimanual coordination. In dynamic environments, such as when picking a workpiece from a moving conveyor belt, the object frame may initially be exogenous but becomes rigidly coupled to the robot once the object is grasped. Continuing to treat this frame as an independent task parameter leads to inconsistent stream fusion and policy failure. Multi-agent collaboration compounds the problem, since another agent’s actions continuously change the relevant task frames, for example during handovers or cooperative lifting. Finally, most multi-stream policies are designed for unimanual manipulation, whereas many everyday tasks, such as opening a jar or carrying a large object, require two arms to work in tandem.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 DynaMAC，一个在动态多流学习的框架下统一双手操作、多智能体合作以及与动态环境交互的框架。 作为一个轻量级的、与策略无关的层，它保留了多流策略的本机，例如出色的样本效率。 通过大量的实验，我们证明了 DynaMAC 可以有效地对环境动态做出反应，同时协调多个手臂，而不需要明确的层次结构或专用的协调模块。 至关重要的是，DynaMAC 通过静态演示的零样本概括在动态环境中运行，从而大大简化了数据收集。 此外，它将零样本推广到影响任一臂的扰动，展示了对以前未见过的相互作用动态的强大适应。We presented DynaMAC, a framework that unifies bimanual manipulation, multi-agent cooperation, and interaction with dynamic environments under the umbrella of dynamic multi-stream learning. As a lightweight, policy-agnostic layer, it preserves the native of multi-stream policies, such as outstanding sample efficiency. Through extensive experiments, we demonstrated that DynaMAC effectively reacts to environment dynamics while coordinating multiple arms without requiring an explicit hierarchy or dedicated coordination module. Crucially, DynaMAC operates in dynamic environments through zero-shot generalization from static demonstrations, substantially simplifying data collection. Furthermore, it generalizes zero-shot to perturbations affecting either arm, demonstrating robust adaptation to previously unseen interaction dynamics.

局限性和未来的工作：Limitations and Future Work:通过建立在多流学习的基础上，DynaMAC 继承了它的一些结构限制，即个人技能可以从长期任务中分割出来的要求By building on multi-stream learning, DynaMAC inherits some of its structural limitations, namely the requirement that individual skills can be segmented from long-horizon tasks[2]，以及对外部视觉编码器模块的依赖。 然而，这种模块化也是一种优势，因为它可以利用新颖的计算机视觉方法而无需策略再培训。 未来的工作可能会研究 DynaMAC 在移动操作中人机协作和全身协调的潜力。, and the reliance on an external vision encoder module. However, this modularity is also a strength, as it enables harnessing novel computer vision methods without policy retraining. Future work might investigate DynaMAC’s potential for human-robot collaboration and whole-body coordination in mobile manipulation.
