立场：机械解释必须披露因果关系的识别假设Position: Mechanistic Interpretability Must Disclose Identification Assumptions for Causal Claims

Zezheng Lin &Fengming Liu Correspondence to Zezheng Lin.
摘要Abstract

机械可解释性论文越来越多地使用因果词汇：电路、中介、因果抽象、单一语义。此类主张需要明确的识别假设。对四个方法论分支的 10 篇论文进行了有目的的审核，发现没有专门的识别假设部分和重复出现的模式：诸如忠实性、完整性、单义性、对齐或消融效应等验证指标被报告为因果支持，但没有说明使它们识别的假设。两人编码员审核Mechanistic interpretability papers increasingly use causal vocabulary: circuits, mediators, causal abstraction, monosemanticity. Such claims require explicit identification assumptions. A purposive audit of 10 papers across four methodological strands finds no dedicated identification-assumptions section and a recurring pattern: validation metrics such as faithfulness, completeness, monosemanticity, alignment, or ablation effects are reported as causal support without stating the assumptions that make them identifying. A two-human-coder audit onn=30n=30再现了主要发现的方向：缺少专用标识部分，并且验证度量替代很常见，尽管精确的 Dim B/D 计数对编码规则敏感。该论文提出了一种披露规范：说明主张是否有因果关系，命名识别策略，列举假设，强调至少一个，并解释如果假设失败，结论将如何变化。验证不是识别。reproduces the direction of the main finding: dedicated identification sections are absent, and validation-metric substitution is common, though exact Dim B/D counts are coding-rule sensitive. The paper proposes a disclosure norm: state whether the claim is causal, name the identification strategy, enumerate assumptions, stress at least one, and explain how conclusions shift if assumptions fail. Validation is not identification.

1 简介Introduction

位置。Position. 使用因果语言的机械可解释性研究必须揭示识别假设。提出因果主张的论文必须在摘要中说明该主张是否具有因果关系，命名识别策略，列举识别该主张所依据的假设，测试或强调至少一个核心假设，并说明如果关键假设失败，结论将如何变化。Mechanistic interpretability research using causal language must disclose identification assumptions. A paper making a causal claim must state in its abstract whether the claim is causal, name the identification strategy, enumerate the assumptions under which the claim is identified, test or stress at least one core assumption, and state how conclusions shift if key assumptions fail.

机械可解释性文献已经提供了自己的证据表明这一点很重要。The mechanistic interpretability literature has already produced its own evidence that this matters.Wang et al. (2023)发表了一篇著名的 ICLR 2023 论文，识别了使用激活补丁进行间接对象识别的 GPT-2 电路。该论文没有说明确定电路索赔所依据的假设。一年后，presented a prominent ICLR 2023 paper identifying a GPT-2 circuit for indirect object identification using activation patching. The paper did not state the assumptions under which the circuit claim was identified. A year later,Makelov et al. (2024)诊断了一种这样的故障模式，并表明幼稚的子空间修补可能会产生可解释性错觉：修补的方向可能不是行为实际依赖的方向。他们的论文很仔细并且有自我意识；它没有做的是将诊断概括为一个全领域的假设披露协议，其他论文可以在下游出现故障之前应用该协议。diagnosed one such failure mode and showed that naive subspace patching can produce interpretability illusions: the patched direction may not be the direction the behavior actually depends on. Their paper is careful and self-aware; what it does not do is generalize the diagnosis into a field-wide assumption-disclosure protocol that other papers can apply before failure shows up downstream.Leask et al. (2025)对稀疏自动编码器进行了类似的校正，表明较大的 SAE 中大约有三分之一的特征在相同数据上训练的较小的 SAE 中不存在——对基于字典的可恢复性假设的单义性工作的直接经验压力produced an analogous correction for sparse autoencoders, showing that approximately one-third of features in a larger SAE are absent from a smaller one trained on the same data—direct empirical pressure on the dictionary-basis-recoverability assumption that monosemanticity work(Bricken et al., 2023; Templeton et al., 2024)不言而喻地依赖。relied on without stating.Canby et al. (2024)因果探索的形式化完整性和选择性是相互竞争的需求，并提出了基础探索文献尚未声明的假设。formalized completeness and selectivity for causal probing as competing desiderata, surfacing assumptions that the underlying probing literature had not declared.

这种顺序并非少数案例所独有。因果词汇（电路、中介、因果抽象、单一语义）被广泛采用；确定因果关系的假设是隐含的；下游工作随后诊断出特定的识别失败；该领域将这种修正视为一种方法论风险，而不是作为披露应该首先进行的证据。此处报告的审计记录了四个主要方法论方面的情况。This sequence is not unique to a few cases. Causal vocabulary (circuits, mediators, causal abstraction, monosemanticity) is adopted enthusiastically; the assumptions under which the causal claim is identified are left implicit; downstream work later diagnoses a specific identification failure; and the field absorbs the correction as a methodological hazard rather than as evidence that disclosure should have come first. The audit reported here documents this across four major methodological strands.

验证不是识别。Validation is not identification.

审核中的重复动作是什么 本文称The repeated move in the audit is what this paper calls验证指标替代validation metric substitution：报告忠实性、完整性、单义性、行为一致性或一致性搜索准确性，以支持因果主张，而无需单独声明这些主张所需的识别假设。忠实、完整、单一语义或行为一致的描述与模型及其自身一致，但一致性并不能证明所提出的机制是原因而不是相关性。: faithfulness, completeness, monosemanticity, behavioral alignment, or alignment-search accuracy are reported in support of causal claims without a separate statement of the identification assumptions those claims require. A faithful, complete, monosemantic, or behaviorally aligned description is consistent with the model and with itself, but consistency does not establish that the proposed mechanism is the cause rather than a correlate.Makelov et al. (2024)和andCanby et al. (2024)，这两个样本都根据经验证明，高验证分数可以与失败的识别共存。, both in the sample, demonstrate empirically that high validation scores can co-exist with failed identification.

为什么要从因果推理中借用披露规范。Why borrow a disclosure norm from causal inference.

计量经济学没有 消除身份纠纷；它将通过既定假设评估因果关系的规范制度化Econometrics did not eliminate identification disputes; it institutionalized the norm that causal claims are evaluated through stated assumptions(Haavelmo, 1944; Heckman, 1979; Angrist et al., 1996; Imbens, 2020)。本文提出的学科不会将工具变量、回归不连续性或任何特定的因果框架导入 MI。它引入了元实践：当一篇论文提出因果断言时，它会陈述识别该断言所依据的假设，并为至少其中一个提供可证伪性测试或敏感性陈述。这是一个比致力于潜在结果、结构因果模型或 do-calculus 更弱的要求，而且它是生成性的而不是限制性的：明确的假设可以实现累积的批评。. The discipline this paper proposes does not import instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, or any specific causal framework into MI. It imports the meta-practice: when a paper makes a causal claim, it states the assumptions under which the claim is identified, and it offers a falsifiability test or sensitivity statement for at least one of them. This is a weaker requirement than committing to potential outcomes, structural causal models, or do-calculus, and it is generative rather than restrictive: explicit assumptions enable cumulative critique.

审核和模板。Audit and templates.

审计涵盖跨越四个方面的文件 NeurIPS、ICLR、CLeaR、BlackboxNLP 和 Anthropic Transformer Circuits 线程。总体模式是，没有论文包含专门的识别假设部分，大多数论文仅在摘要中提出隐含的因果关系，大多数不包含可证伪性测试，并且大多数论文用验证指标代替识别陈述。然后，本文提供了特定于方法的模板，作者可以将其复制到方法部分，用于激活修补、稀疏自动编码器、因果抽象和消融探测、列出假设、解决这些假设的证据、潜在的伪造者以及违反时的敏感性影响。The audit covers papers spanning four strands across NeurIPS, ICLR, CLeaR, BlackboxNLP, and the Anthropic Transformer Circuits Thread. The aggregate pattern is that no paper contains a dedicated identification-assumptions section, the majority make only implicit causal claims in abstracts, the majority contain no falsifiability test, and the majority substitute a validation metric for an identification statement. The paper then offers method-specific templates that authors can copy into their Methods sections for activation patching, sparse autoencoders, causal abstraction, and probing with ablation, listing assumptions, the evidence that addresses them, potential falsifiers, and the sensitivity implications if violated.

审计范围。Scope of the audit.

审计样本是有目的的、小规模的， 旨在支持这一立场，而不是估计更广泛的 MI 文献中的患病率。每篇论文的证据（摘要摘要、来源定位器、主文本搜索证据、理由文本）均已发布，以便每个编码都可以在已发布的标题上受到质疑。该位置并不取决于患病率统计数据；这取决于概念点，即披露识别假设是任何因果主张的逻辑要求。审计的作用是证明主要场馆和领域的差距足够广泛，以保证社区标准的存在。The audited sample is purposive and small, and is meant to support the position rather than to estimate prevalence in the broader MI literature. Per-paper evidence (abstract summaries, source locators, main-text search evidence, justification text) is released so that every coding can be challenged on the published rubric. The position does not depend on prevalence statistics; it depends on the conceptual point that disclosure of identification assumptions is a logical requirement of any causal claim. The audit functions as evidence that the gap is widespread enough across major venues and strands to warrant a community standard.

2 相关工作Related Work

最近的几篇论文分析了机器学习中的评估实践，尽管没有一篇论文专门关注机械可解释性中的识别假设披露。Several recent papers have analyzed evaluation practices in machine learning, though none have focused specifically on identification assumption disclosure in mechanistic interpretability.Schaeffer et al. (2023)研究表明，在仔细评估下，所谓的新兴能力往往会消失，这表明机器学习社区的评估实践可能会得出在审查下失败的结论。showed that claimed emergent abilities often vanish under careful evaluation, demonstrating that the ML community’s evaluation practices can produce conclusions that fail under scrutiny.Pearl (2009); Pearl and Mackenzie (2018)建立了因果推理的正式基础，并认为因果主张需要明确的假设；这项工作为我们的主张提供了理论支柱，即法学硕士可解释性论文应该陈述其识别假设。established the formal foundations of causal inference and argued that causal claims require explicit assumptions; this work provides the theoretical backbone for our claim that LLM interpretability papers should state their identification assumptions.

关系更密切的是，More closely related,Leask et al. (2025)从经验上挑战稀疏自动编码器特征原子性，表明特征在 SAE 大小上的分解方式使关于单个特征因果关系的主张无效。他们在同一模型上训练了不同大小的稀疏自动编码器，并将较大 SAE 的潜在特征插入到较小的 SAE 中，发现较大 SAE 中大约三分之一的特征在较小的 SAE 中不存在，这表明较大的 SAE 捕获了较小 SAE 遗漏的信息。这一发现直接挑战了字典基础可恢复性假设：如果不存在规范基础，则 SAE 分解在旋转之前是任意的，并且将因果行为归因于单个特征是不合理的。这是唯一直接识别可解释性中未陈述的识别假设并对其进行测试的先前工作。我们的贡献是做出这种假设——检查标准实践，而不是事后发现。challenged sparse-autoencoder feature atomicity empirically, showing that features decompose across SAE sizes in ways that invalidate claims about individual feature causality. They trained sparse autoencoders of different sizes on the same model and inserted latents from a larger SAE into a smaller one, finding that approximately one-third of features in the larger SAE were absent from the smaller one, suggesting larger SAEs capture information the smaller ones miss. This finding directly challenges the dictionary-basis-recoverability assumption: if no canonical basis exists, the SAE decomposition is arbitrary up to rotation, and attributing causal behavior to individual features is unjustified. This is the only prior work that directly identifies an unstated identification assumption in interpretability and tests it. Our contribution is to make this kind of assumption-checking the standard practice rather than a post-hoc discovery.

3 计量经济学的先例The Econometrics Precedent

在计量经济学中，一篇声称治疗具有因果效应的论文In econometrics, a paper claiming a causal effect of treatmentDD关于结果on outcomeYY在开头部分说明了支持该主张的识别策略。这不是一种风格上的选择，而是一种方法论上的选择：如果没有明确的假设，科学界就无法评估结果是反映了真正的因果结构还是未说明条件的产物。states in its opening section the identification strategy supporting the claim. This is not a stylistic choice but a methodological one: without stated assumptions, the scientific community cannot evaluate whether a result reflects genuine causal structure or an artifact of unstated conditions.

在潜在成果框架中In the potential outcome framework(Rubin, 1974; Holland, 1986)，因果声明需要指定稳定单位治疗值假设 (SUTVA)，该假设指出一个单位的结果不受其他单位的治疗分配的影响，以及治疗分配机制，通常是无混杂性（可忽略性），它指出潜在的结果独立于以观察到的协变量为条件的治疗分配。在DAG框架中, a causal claim requires specifying the stable unit treatment value assumption (SUTVA), which states that the outcome for one unit is unaffected by the treatment assignment of other units, and the treatment assignment mechanism, typically unconfoundedness (ignorability), which states that potential outcomes are independent of treatment assignment conditional on observed covariates. In the DAG framework(Pearl, 2009)，因果识别需要指定因果图和马尔可夫假设，包括 d 分离标准。, causal identification requires specifying the causal graph and the Markov assumptions, including d-separation criteria.

这两个框架在技术细节上有所不同，但收敛于相同的元实践：无论框架如何，明确的假设披露是任何因果关系主张的先决条件。这不仅仅是一次纪律大会。这是因果推理的逻辑要求：如果不指定从可观察数据中识别因果量的条件，因果主张就没有任何意义。The two frameworks differ in technical detail but converge on the same meta practice: explicit assumption disclosure is a prerequisite for any causal claim, regardless of framework. This is not merely a disciplinary convention. It is a logical requirement of causal inference: a causal claim has no meaning without specifying the conditions under which the causal quantity is identified from observable data.

对于法学硕士的可解释性来说，这种平行在元意义上是直接的。激活补丁假设干预措施隔离了因果路径。稀疏自动编码器假设学习的特征对应于模型的真实表示基础。因果抽象假设分布式表示与更高级别的因果结构一致。探究假设表征空间允许局部因果干预。这些是识别假设：因果断言必须满足的条件。然而法学硕士的可解释性文献几乎从未阐述过它们。For LLM interpretability, the parallel is direct in the meta sense. Activation patching assumes interventions isolate causal pathways. Sparse autoencoders assume learned features correspond to the model’s true representational basis. Causal abstraction assumes distributed representations align with higher-level causal structures. Probing assumes the representational space admits localized causal interventions. These are identification assumptions: conditions that must hold for causal claims to follow. Yet the LLM interpretability literature almost never states them.

4 机械可解释性中的识别假设Identification Assumptions in Mechanistic Interpretability

本节列举了审计样本中每个方法论链最常见的识别假设。重点不是要规定一个固定的列表，而是要表明这种假设对于每种方法都存在并且可以明确表示。This section enumerates the most common identification assumptions per methodological strand in the audited sample. The point is not to prescribe a fixed list but to show that such assumptions exist for every method and can be made explicit.

激活补丁。Activation patching.

(1) 电路完整性Circuit completeness：目标组件 解释所有因果路径。 (2): the targeted components account for all causal pathways. (2)途径独家Pathway exclusivity：修补不会激活休眠的并行电路。 (3): patching does not activate dormant parallel circuits. (3)公制充分性Metric sufficiency：行为指标反映了兴趣的因果属性。: the behavioral metric reflects the causal property of interest.Wang et al. (2023)在 GPT-2 Small 中修补了 10 个注意力头，用于间接对象识别；他们并没有说所发现的电路耗尽了所有因果路径。patched 10 attention heads in GPT-2 Small for indirect object identification; they did not state that the discovered circuit exhausts all causal pathways.Makelov et al. (2024)在各种模型中修补子空间，并假设修补子空间是效果的唯一驱动因素。他们没有列举这个假设。patched subspaces in various models and assumed the patched subspace was the sole driver of the effect. They did not enumerate this assumption.

(1) 字典基础可恢复性Dictionary basis recoverability：美国汽车工程师学会 将激活分解为模型的真实特征基础，而不是任意旋转。 (2): the SAE decomposes activations into the model’s true feature basis rather than an arbitrary rotation. (2)特征原子性Feature atomicity：特征不可分解为更简单的子特征。 (3): features are non-decomposable into simpler sub-features. (3)单义性Monosemanticity：每个特征对应于一个连贯的概念，而不是多语义的混合。 (4): each feature corresponds to a single coherent concept rather than a polysemantic blend. (4)功能完整性Feature completeness：SAE 捕获所有因果相关的特征。: the SAE captures all causally relevant features.Bricken et al. (2023)将行为归因于 GPT-2 中的个体特征；他们假设这些特征是原子的和单一语义的。attributed behavior to individual features in GPT-2; they assumed those features were atomic and monosemantic.Templeton et al. (2024)将这种方法扩展到 Claude 3 Sonnet，假设较大模型的特征空间分解为规范基础。这些假设都没有被正式陈述。其后果在后续工作中显而易见：scaled this approach to Claude 3 Sonnet, assuming the larger model’s feature space decomposes into a canonical basis. 因果抽象。Causal abstraction.

(1) 对齐忠实度Alignment faithfulness： 结构 神经表征和更高层次的因果模型之间的对应关系在干预下得以保留。 (2): the structural correspondence between neural representations and higher-level causal models is preserved under intervention. (2)优化有效性Optimization validity：基于梯度的搜索过程发现真正的比对而不是统计伪影。: the gradient-based search procedure finds genuine alignments rather than statistical artifacts.Wu, Geiger et al. (2024)使用条件独立性和态射兼容性的形式化对齐。这两个假设都没有被声明为编号声明。formalized alignment using conditional independence and morphism compatibility. Neither assumption was declared as a numbered statement.

试探。Probing.

(1) 因果局部性Causal locality：相关代表性集中 在单个神经元或小子空间中而不是分布式编码。 (2): the relevant representation is concentrated in individual neurons or small subspaces rather than distributively encoded. (2)干预的完整性Intervention completeness：消融删除了目标概念的完整代表性内容。 (3): ablation removes the full representational content of the target concept. 5 审计方法论Audit Methodology

5.1 样本选择Sample Selection

审计涵盖十篇论文，涵盖四个方法论领域（电路发现、稀疏自动编码器、因果抽象、探测）以及 NeurIPS、ICLR、CLeaR、BlackboxNLP 和 Anthropic Transformer Circuits Thread。该样本包括开创性的作品（The audit covers ten papers spanning four methodological strands (circuit discovery, sparse autoencoders, causal abstraction, probing) and the venues NeurIPS, ICLR, CLeaR, BlackboxNLP, and the Anthropic Transformer Circuits Thread.   • •

8 披露协议The Disclosure Protocol

该协议将开头部分假设披露的因果推理惯例调整为机械可解释性。它没有规定因果框架；它要求无论论文采用什么框架，都必须明确说明其核心假设。对 NeurIPS、ICLR、ICML 和类似场所的建议是将协议纳入现有的提交清单中。五项要求：The protocol adapts the causal-inference convention of opening-section assumption disclosure to mechanistic interpretability. It does not prescribe a causal framework; it requires that whatever framework a paper adopts, its core assumptions be stated explicitly. The recommendation to NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, and comparable venues is to incorporate the protocol into existing submission checklists. The five requirements:

  1. 1.

    在摘要中说明论文是否提出因果主张或关联主张。 无论哪种方式都可以理解的含糊语言是不可接受的。State in the abstract whether the paper makes a causal claim or an association claim. Vague language that could be read either way is not acceptable.

  2. 2.

    如果摘要中出现因果关系主张，请指出支持它的识别策略。If a causal claim appears in the abstract, name the identification strategy supporting it.

  3. 3.

    在正文中包含编号的识别假设部分。每个假设 必须说明它的主张，为什么作者认为它合理，以及存在什么证据 为了它。Include a numbered Identification Assumptions section in the main text. Each assumption must state what it asserts, why the authors consider it plausible, and what evidence exists for it.

  4. 4.

    对于每个核心假设，至少提供以下一项： 直接可证伪性测试 实验设置，敏感性分析显示结论在违规情况下如何变化， 或者明确说明哪些实证结果会对其产生不利影响。For each core assumption, provide at least one of: a direct falsifiability test within the experimental setup, a sensitivity analysis showing how conclusions change under violation, or a clear statement of what empirical finding would count against it.

  5. 5.

    实质性讨论如果关键假设失败，结论将如何变化。Discuss substantively how conclusions change if key assumptions fail.

另类观点。Alternative views.

一种观点认为 MI 是工程而不是 因果科学。该协议仅适用于论文使用因果词汇的情况；非因果论文可以通过描述性地提出主张来避免这一要求。第二种观点是正式的假设披露还为时过早。回应是该协议没有强加计量经济学估计量或单一因果框架；它要求作者陈述他们自己的干预措施已经需要的假设。第三种观点是样板部分可能变得具有表演性。因此，审稿人指南应将实质性假设、证伪者和敏感性陈述与敷衍的清单区分开来。该补充提供了特定于方法的模板以使规范具体化。One view is that MI is engineering rather than causal science. The protocol applies only when papers use causal vocabulary; non-causal papers can avoid the requirement by framing claims descriptively. A second view is that formal assumption disclosure is premature. The response is that the protocol does not impose econometric estimators or a single causal framework; it asks authors to state the assumptions their own interventions already require. A third view is that boilerplate sections could become performative. Reviewer guidance should therefore distinguish substantive assumptions, falsifiers, and sensitivity statements from perfunctory checklists. The supplement provides method-specific templates to make the norm concrete.
