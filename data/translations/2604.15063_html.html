不再猜测：联邦学习中可验证的梯度反转攻击No More Guessing: a Verifiable Gradient Inversion Attack in Federated Learning

Francesco Diana    Chuan Xu    André Nusser    Giovanni Neglia
摘要Abstract

梯度反转攻击通过从客户的共享梯度重建训练样本来威胁联邦学习中的客户隐私。梯度聚合来自多个记录的贡献，而现有的攻击可能无法解开它们，从而产生不正确的重建，而没有内在的方法来证明成功。在视觉和语言方面，攻击者可能会依靠人工检查来判断重建的合理性，但这对于数字表格记录来说不太可行，从而加深了表格数据不易受到攻击的印象。Gradient inversion attacks threaten client privacy in federated learning by reconstructing training samples from clients’ shared gradients. Gradients aggregate contributions from multiple records and existing attacks may fail to disentangle them, yielding incorrect reconstructions with no intrinsic way to certify success. In vision and language, attackers may fall back on human inspection to judge reconstruction plausibility, but this is far less feasible for numerical tabular records , fueling the impression that tabular data is less vulnerable.

我们通过提出可验证的梯度反转攻击（VGIA）来挑战这种看法，该攻击为重建样本提供明确的正确性证书。我们的方法采用 ReLU 泄漏的几何视图：全连接层的激活边界定义输入空间中的超平面。 VGIA 引入了一种基于子空间的代数验证测试，可检测超平面分隔区域何时恰好包含一条记录。一旦隔离得到验证，VGIA 就会通过分析恢复相应的特征向量，并通过轻量级优化步骤重建目标。We challenge this perception by proposing a verifiable gradient inversion attack (VGIA) that provides an explicit certificate of correctness for reconstructed samples. Our method adopts a geometric view of ReLU leakage: the activation boundary of a fully connected layer defines a hyperplane in input space. VGIA introduces an algebraic, subspace-based verification test that detects when a hyperplane-delimited region contains exactly one record. Once isolation is certified, VGIA recovers the corresponding feature vector analytically and reconstructs the target via a lightweight optimization step.

在大批量的表格基准上进行的实验证明了在现有最先进的攻击失败或无法评估重建保真度的情况下精确的记录和目标恢复。与之前的几何方法相比，VGIA 可以更有效地分配超平面查询，从而以更少的攻击次数实现更快的重建。Experiments on tabular benchmarks with large batch sizes demonstrate exact record and target recovery in regimes where existing state-of-the-art attacks either fail or cannot assess reconstruction fidelity. Compared to prior geometric approaches, VGIA allocates hyperplane queries more effectively, yielding faster reconstructions with fewer attack rounds.

6 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Works

在这项工作中，我们介绍了一个In this work, we introduce a可验证的梯度反转攻击Verifiable Gradient Inversion Attack，联邦学习中的一种新颖的分析攻击，可以在没有限制性假设的情况下为重建样本提供明确的正确性证书。, a novel analytical attack in federated learning that provides explicit certificates of correctness for reconstructed samples without restrictive assumptions.
虽然全连接网络广泛用于表格学习，但将所提出的攻击扩展到更具表现力的架构仍然是未来工作的重要方向。例如，最近的研究表明，对 Transformer 模型进行有针对性的修改可以诱导实现直接输入恢复的行为While fully connected networks are widely used in tabular learning, extending the proposed attack to more expressive architectures remains an important direction for future work. For example, recent studies show that targeted modifications to Transformer models can induce behaviors enabling direct input recovery(Fowl et al., 2023)。 最后，在隐私增强机制下对攻击性能的系统评估在很大程度上仍未被探索，并且构成了未来研究的一个令人信服的途径。. Finally, a systematic evaluation of attack performance under privacy-enhancing mechanisms remains largely unexplored and constitutes a compelling avenue for future research.
