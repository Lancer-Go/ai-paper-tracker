摘要Abstract

解释机器学习模型对于决策和消费者信任越来越重要，但人们普遍认为这是有代价的：现有的可解释人工智能 (XAI) 方法始终存在准确性与可解释性之间的权衡。我们认为，这种权衡并不是根本性的，而是将解释和预测视为两个独立目标的产物；当两者正确耦合时，它们就会变得互补，这样配备一个模型来解释自身就可以提高而不是降低其预测准确性。然而，要实现这一好处，需要克服现有 XAI 方法的三个局限性：（1）在分布变化下没有单一的解释仍然有效； (2) 标量归因，例如SHAP，无法捕获条件或非线性关系； (3) 解释与其所提供的预测脱节。因此我们介绍Explaining machine-learning models is increasingly important for decision-making and consumer trust, yet it is widely believed to come at a cost: existing Explainable AI (XAI) methods suffer from a persistent accuracy–explainability trade-off. We argue that this trade-off is not fundamental, but rather an artifact of treating explanation and prediction as two separate objectives; when the two are properly coupled, they become complementary, such that equipping a model to explain itself improves, rather than degrades, its predictive accuracy. Realizing this benefit, however, requires overcoming three limitations of existing XAI methods: (1) no single explanation remains valid under distribution shift; (2) scalar attributions, such as SHAP, cannot capture conditional or non-linear relationships; and (3) explanations are disconnected from the predictions they inform. 意义建构理论：生产什么？Sensemaking Theory: What to Produce? Weick的（’s (1995）理论认为，意义建构产生合理的、指导行动的叙述，而不是单一的客观事实，并且本质上是多元的；因此，信息系统研究认为，决策支持应该适应解释性的多元化，而不是仅仅强加一种单一的解决方案) theory holds that meaning-construction generates plausible, action-guiding accounts rather than a single objective truth, and is inherently plural; IS research accordingly argues that decision support should accommodate interpretive plurality rather than impose only one single solution(Sharma and Yetton, 2003; Volkoff and Strong, 2013)。因此，根据解释的指导预测能力来生成一套解释至关重要，这正是定义 3 中罗生门解释的“有用性”标准。. 设计原则一：Design Principle 1: XAI 系统应该产生一套指导行动的解释，而不是针对单一解释进行优化，反映意义建构的多元化和务实本质。XAI systems should produce a set of action-guiding explanations rather than optimizing for a single explanation, reflecting the plural and pragmatic nature of sensemaking.

双环学习理论：如何产生？Double-Loop Learning Theory: How to Produce? Argyris and Schön的（’s (1978）理论将固定框架内的单环修正与双环学习区分开来，双环学习将持续性错误视为框架指定错误的证据，以便可以进行相应的修改) theory distinguishes single-loop correction within a fixed framework from double-loop learning, which treats persistent errors as evidence that the framework is misspecified, so that it can be revised accordingly(Sein et al., 2011; Kohli and Kettinger, 2004)。因此，我们产生的解释需要在学习过程中不断更新，以便我们可以最大限度地减少其错误指定的错误。. Therefore, our produced explanations need to be consistently updated in the learning process, so that we can minimize its misspecification error.

设计原则2：Design Principle 2: XAI 系统应采用双循环学习机制，不断修改解释结果以响应观察到的预测失败。XAI systems should incorporate a double-loop learning mechanism that continuously revises the explanation results in response to the observed prediction failures.

互补理论：如何组织？Complementarity Theory: How to Organize?互补理论Complementarity theory(Milgrom and Roberts, 1990)认为当两项活动的边际回报随另一项活动的水平上升时，两项活动是互补的，因此联合设计的系统优于独立模块holds that two activities are complements when the marginal return to each rises with the level of the other, so a jointly designed system outperforms independent modules(Tanriverdi, 2005)。在我们的框架中，由于我们假设预测任务和解释任务是互惠互利的，因此它们应该联合优化，而不是孤立优化。. In our framework, since we hypothesize that the prediction task and the explanation task are mutually beneficial for each other, they should be optimized jointly rather than in isolation.

将它们放在一起，意义制定设定目标（一组解释），双环学习修复过程（迭代修订），互补性决定架构（联合优化）。 RashomonLLM（将在本节的其余部分详细介绍）是这些设计原则的一个具体实例，可带来各种理论和经验效益。Putting them together, sensemaking sets the goal (a set of explanations), double-loop learning fixes the process (iterative revision), and complementarity determines the architecture (joint optimization). 4.2  解释代理——通过上下文学习生成解释4.2  Explanation Agent — Generating Explanations via In-Context Learning

我们框架中的第一个组件是解释代理，我们在其中提示法学硕士根据训练数据中观察到的结果生成解释，类似于现有的事后解释器。然而，挑战在于我们不知道先验的真实解释的结构，因此，我们不知道最好的解释提示是什么。例如，如果我们提示法学硕士只关注线性关系，那么解释就会忽略特征之间的非线性或条件关系；同时，如果我们推动法学硕士探索更复杂的关系，它在简单的线性案例上可能表现不佳。The first component in our framework is the Explanation Agent, where we prompt the LLM to generate explanations based on the observed outcomes in the training data, similar to existing post-hoc explainers. However, the challenge is that we do not know the structure of the ground-truth explanations a priori, and consequently, we do not know what the best explanation prompt is. For example, if we prompt the LLM to focus only on the linear relationships, the explanations would overlook non-linear or conditional relationships between features; meanwhile, if we push the LLM to explore more complex relationships, it might not perform well on simple linear cases.

4.3  预测代理——根据生成的解释预测模型结果4.3  Prediction Agent — Predicting Model Outcomes Based on Generated Explanations

一旦我们获得了解释代理产生的（初始）解释，我们接下来将构建预测代理以利用这些解释来预测模型结果。预测提示的格式使我们能够将解释与训练数据结合起来，以指导法学硕士的推理过程，从而做出准确的预测。虽然有许多不同的方法来制作适合我们框架的预测提示，但在我们的实证分析中，不同预测提示的性能保持相对稳定。这是因为预测代理的任务相对简单：只需执行所提供的解释中指定的指令即可产生预测结果，并不涉及复杂的推理过程；因此，我们不需要任何复杂的提示策略，这与以下发现一致：对于简单任务，提示敏感性较低，而对于复杂、推理密集型任务，提示敏感性较高Once we obtain the (initial) explanations produced by the Explanation Agent, we will next build the Prediction Agent to utilize these explanations for predicting the model outcomes. The prediction prompt is formatted in a manner that enables us to incorporate the explanation along with the training data to guide the reasoning process of LLMs in order to make accurate predictions. While there are many different ways to craft the prediction prompt that fit our framework well, the performance remains relatively steady across different prediction prompts in our empirical analysis. This is because the task of the Prediction Agent is relatively simple: it only needs to execute the instructions specified in the provided explanations to produce the prediction outcomes, and does not involve a complex reasoning process; as a result, we do not need any sophisticated prompting strategies, consistent with the finding that prompt sensitivity is low for simple tasks but high for complex, reasoning-intensive ones(Zhuo et al., 2024).

4.7  批量 LLM 学习——我们提出的增强可扩展性方法的扩展4.7  Batch LLM Learning — An Extension of Our Proposed Method to Enhance Scalability

虽然我们提出的 RashomonLLM 方法可以有效解决现有 XAI 方法中的问题，但从理论角度来看（正如我们在第 1 节中所说明的）While our proposed RashomonLLM method is effective in tackling the problems in existing XAI methods, both from the theoretical perspective (as we have illustrated in Section4.6）并从经验的角度来看（正如我们将在第 1 节中演示的那样）) and from the empirical perspective (as we will demonstrate in Section5接下来），我们提出的方法仍然存在一个重要的实际挑战：上下文窗口的限制。最先进的 LLM，例如 GPT 或 Claude，可以处理百万个令牌量级的上下文窗口，而 RashomonLLM 需要处理的数据大小对于工业平台来说通常是数百万甚至数十亿的规模。因此，我们无法以简单的方式将整个数据集输入 LLM。此外，之前的文献表明，当上下文历史变长时，LLM 更容易遭受长上下文退化next), an important practical challenge still exists for our proposed method: the limit of the context window. State-of-the-art LLMs, such as GPT or Claude, can handle context windows on the order of a million tokens, while the sizes of data that RashomonLLM needs to handle are typically at the scale of millions or even billions for industrial platforms. As a result, we cannot feed the entire dataset into the LLM in a trivial manner. In addition, previous literature has shown that when the context history gets longer, LLMs are more likely to suffer from long-context degradation(Liu et al., 2024)，模型不太可靠地关注埋藏在长上下文中的信息。, where the model attends less reliably to information buried deep in a long context.

为了应对这些挑战，我们开发了一种新的To address these challenges, we develop a new批量LLM学习Batch LLM Learning机制，我们将输入训练数据集分割成多个“批次”，并按顺序将它们输入 LLM，一次一批，以便每个批次增量更新提示中结转的数据的运行摘要。上下文批处理的一个实际问题是，随着运行上下文的增长，LLM 可能会丢失早期批次的信息。作为一种轻量级的保护措施，我们引入了“mechanism, where we segment the input training dataset into multiple “batches” and feed them into the LLM sequentially, one batch at a time, so that each batch incrementally updates a running summary of the data that is carried forward in the prompt. 4.9  罗生门LLM的技术新颖性4.9  Technical Novelty of RashomonLLM

最后，我们总结了 RashomonLLM 相对于现有方法的关键技术创新点。To conclude, we summarize the key technical novelties of RashomonLLM over existing methods.

  • •

    一系列解释而不是一个。A collection of explanations instead of one.先前的 XAI 方法致力于单一解释，这在分布偏移（定理 1）下是脆弱的。相反，罗生门LLM 构建并聚合了一组近乎最佳的、忠实的解释（罗生门解释）。Prior XAI methods commit to a single explanation, which is fragile under distribution shift (Theorem 1). RashomonLLM instead constructs and aggregates a set of near-optimal, faithful explanations (Rashomon Explanation).

  • •

    自然语言解释形式。Natural-language explanation form.现有的归因方法返回无法表示条件或非线性结构的标量特征权重（例如，SHAP、LIME），而 RashomonLLM 生成表达方向性、条件性和非线性结构的半结构化自然语言基本原理，同时保持利益相关者直接可读。Existing attribution methods return scalar feature weights (e.g., SHAP, LIME) that cannot represent conditional or non-linear structure, while RashomonLLM produces semi-structured natural-language rationales that express directional, conditional, and non-linear structures while remaining directly readable by stakeholders.

  • •

    解释与预测相结合。Explanation coupled with prediction.事后解释器与预测模型脱钩，无法改进它。通过 EPR 循环，RashomonLLM 将两者结合起来，从而产生忠实的解释，从而降低预测误差（定理 2），从而解决准确性与可解释性之间的权衡问题。Post-hoc explainers are decoupled from the prediction model and cannot improve it. Through the EPR loop, RashomonLLM couples the two, so that producing a faithful explanation lowers prediction error (Theorem 2), therefore resolving the accuracy–explainability trade-off.

  • •

    LLM 特色退学。LLM Feature Dropout.这项新技术通过扰乱暴露给模型的特征子集来使解释集多样化，使我们能够实现整体效果。This new technique diversifies the explanation set by perturbing the subset of features exposed to the model, enabling us to realize an ensemble effect.

  • •

    批量LLM学习。Batch LLM Learning.这种新机制通过上下文批处理将工作流程扩展到远远超出 LLM 上下文窗口的数据。This new mechanism scales the workflow to data far beyond the LLM context window through in-context batching.

据我们所知，RashomonLLM 是第一种在探索罗生门解释集时使解释和预测相辅相成的方法，每次运行的收敛性得到正式保证（定理 1-4）的支持，并在我们凭经验评估的条件下完全覆盖该集。我们现在展示其经验优势。To our knowledge, RashomonLLM is the first method to make explanation and prediction mutually reinforcing while exploring the Rashomon Explanation set, with per-run convergence backed by formal guarantees (Theorems 1–4) and full coverage of the set under the conditions we assess empirically. 7.3  边界条件7.3  Boundary Conditions

我们现在讨论该方法在什么条件下适用和不适用，以指导其在工业实践中负责任的采用。We now discuss the conditions under which the method is and is not appropriate, so as to guide its responsible adoption in industrial practices.

数据模态和特征可解释性。Data modality and feature interpretability.RashomonLLM 专为表格、结构化输入而设计，其特征带有有意义的语义，其解释只能与底层特征一样可解释。在 KuaiLive 研究中，我们故意排除不透明的输入，例如匿名的单热代码和学习的标题嵌入，因为没有可靠的自然语言原理可以附加到缺乏语义的维度。因此，以原始、非结构化信号（例如图像、音频或自由文本）为主的应用程序超出了我们目前的范围，需要将这些信号编码为可解释的中间特征，或者通过多模态功能扩展法学硕士主干，这是我们留给未来工作的方向。RashomonLLM is designed for tabular, structured inputs whose features carry meaningful semantics, and its explanations are only as interpretable as the underlying features. In the KuaiLive study, we deliberately exclude opaque inputs such as anonymized one-hot codes and learned title embeddings, because no faithful natural-language rationale can be attached to dimensions that lack semantics. Therefore, applications dominated by raw, unstructured signals, such as images, audio, or free text, fall outside our present scope and would require either encoding those signals into interpretable intermediate features or extending the LLM backbone with multimodal capability, a direction we leave to future work.

任务结构和特征维度。Task structure and feature dimensionality.当决策由可以用语言简洁表达的模式控制时，即中等数量的特征之间的交互，如流失和点击预测，该方法是最强的。相反，当预测信号分布在数百个具有高阶交互的弱信息特征时，简洁的自然语言描述可能无法捕获完整的决策面，而不受限制的专用模型可能在预测准确性方面具有优势。The method is strongest when the decision is governed by patterns that can be expressed compactly in language, i.e., interactions among a moderate number of features, as in churn and click prediction. When the predictive signal is instead distributed across hundreds of weakly informative features with high-order interactions, a concise natural-language description may not be able to capture the full decision surface, and a specialized model that is not constrained to remain verbalizable may hold an advantage on prediction accuracy.

7.4  局限性和未来研究7.4  Limitations and Future Research

以合乎道德的方式使用敏感属性。Ethical use of sensitive attributes.我们的几个数据集包含人口统计属性，比如快直播上的性别和年龄，HCT生存任务上的年龄和种族，RashomonLLM的解释可能会参考它们。我们注意到，我们的方法与属性无关，因为框架不需要受保护的功能，并且可以在不改变工作流程的情况下排除或屏蔽这些功能。我们计划在未来的工作中研究这样做对 RashomonLLM 的影响。Several of our datasets contain demographic attributes, such as gender and age on KuaiLive, and age and race on the HCT survival task, and RashomonLLM’s explanations may reference them. We note that our method is attribute-agnostic, as protected features are not required by the framework and can be excluded or masked without altering the workflow. We plan to study the impact of doing so on RashomonLLM in our future work.

超越表格数据。Beyond tabular data.我们将本文的范围限制为具有语义意义特征的表格、结构化输入。将框架扩展到文本和图像等非结构化数据，通过将其编码为可解释的中间特征或利用多模式主干，将是进一步增强其实用性的重要的下一步。We restrict the scope of this paper to tabular, structured inputs with semantically meaningful features. Extending the framework to unstructured data, such as text and images, either by encoding them into interpretable intermediate features or by leveraging multimodal backbones, will be an important next step to further enhance its practicality.

现场实验。Live experiment.实时在线 A/B 实验，以及提高实时、大规模服务的训练和推理效率，仍然是未来工作的重要方向。A live online A/B experiment, together with improving the efficiency of training and inference for real-time, large-scale serving, remains an important direction for future work.

从人类的解释中学习。Learning from human explanations.将法学硕士生成的解释与小样本学习与人类提供的真实解释联系起来可以进一步提高保真度，并实现我们框架的人机循环完善。Connecting the LLM-generated explanations to small-sample learning with human-provided ground-truth explanations could further improve fidelity and enable human-in-the-loop refinement of our framework.

上升至因果解释（第 4 级）。Ascending to causal explanation (Level 4).在整篇论文中，我们一直小心翼翼地不声称 RashomonLLM 的基本原理恢复了真正的因果机制（第 4 级）。缩小这一差距是最重要的延伸，它需要改变证据和成功标准。首先，验证必须从Throughout the paper, we have been careful not to claim that RashomonLLM’s rationales recover the true causal mechanism (Level 4). Closing that gap is the most consequential extension, and it requires changing both the evidence and the criterion of success. First, validation must shift from保持预测保真度held-out predictive fidelity to 干预一致性interventional consistency。其次，这需要干预数据，例如随机暴露 A/B 实验，以及明确的因果图及其识别假设. Second, this demands interventional data, such as a randomized-exposure A/B experiment, together with an explicit causal graph and its identification assumptions(Peters et al., 2016)。我们认为从解释模型到解释机制的上升是这项工作建立的研究管道的最终目标。. We regard this ascent from explaining the model to explaining the mechanism as the ultimate goal of the research pipeline built from this work.

8  结论8  Conclusion

XAI 是一个重要的研究领域，对业务应用具有重大影响，但现有方法存在严重局限性，导致解释不可靠，并且错过了提高模型性能的解释机会。本文从三个方面解决了这些限制。XAI is an important research area with significant implications for business applications, yet existing methods suffer from critical limitations that yield unreliable explanations and miss the opportunity for explanations to improve model performance. This paper addresses these limitations on three fronts.理论上Theoretically，我们确定没有任何单一的解释对于分布转移是稳健的，解释的保真度限制了它所指导的模型的预测误差，并且忠实且有用的解释集——罗生门解释集——是非空且可达的。, we establish that no single explanation is robust to distribution shift, that an explanation’s fidelity bounds the prediction error of the model it guides, and that the set of faithful-and-useful explanations—the Rashomon Explanation set—is non-empty and reachable.方法论上Methodologically，我们提出了 RashomonLLM，一种解释-预测-反射代理工作流程，具有针对工业规模数据的批量 LLM 学习扩展，并证明它在显式条件下在每次运行中收敛并恢复完整的 Rashomon 解释集。这些设计选择基于意义建构、双环学习和互补性的组织理论，它们共同产生了代理 XAI 的三个设计原则。, we propose RashomonLLM, an Explanation–Prediction–Reflection agentic workflow with a Batch LLM Learning extension for industrial-scale data, and prove that it, under explicit conditions, converges within each run and recovers the full Rashomon Explanation set. These design choices are grounded in the organizational theories of sensemaking, double-loop learning, and complementarity, which together yield three design principles for agentic XAI.经验上Empirically在两个 Kaggle 基准测试和直播平台的大规模 CTR 预测任务中，RashomonLLM 在准确性和解释忠实度方面均显着优于最先进的预测和 XAI 基准；最值得注意的是，让模型能够自我解释, across two Kaggle benchmarks and a large-scale CTR prediction task at a live-streaming platform, RashomonLLM significantly outperforms state-of-the-art prediction and XAI baselines on both accuracy and explanation faithfulness; most notably, equipping the model to explain itself改进其预测improves its predictions，它提供了直接证据表明解释和准确性是互补的而不是竞争的。通过使它们相辅相成，罗生门LLM使组织能够提高业务绩效，同时为消费者信任奠定基础。, which offers direct evidence that explanation and accuracy are complementary rather than competing. By making them mutually reinforcing, RashomonLLM enables organizations to advance business performance and lay the groundwork for consumer trust at the same time.
