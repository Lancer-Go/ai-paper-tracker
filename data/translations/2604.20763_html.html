覆盖范围，而不是平均值：可信检索评估的语义分层Coverage, Not Averages: Semantic Stratification for Trustworthy Retrieval Evaluation

Andrew Klearman    Radu Revutchi    Rohin Garg    Rishav Chakravarti    Samuel Marc Denton    Yuan Xue
摘要Abstract

检索质量是检索增强生成（RAG）准确性和鲁棒性的主要瓶颈。当前的评估依赖于启发式构造的查询集，这引入了隐藏的内在偏差。我们将检索评估形式化为统计估计问题，表明度量可靠性从根本上受到评估集构造的限制。我们进一步介绍Retrieval quality is the primary bottleneck for accuracy and robustness in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Current evaluation relies on heuristically constructed query sets, which introduce a hidden intrinsic bias. We formalize retrieval evaluation as a statistical estimation problem, showing that metric reliability is fundamentally limited by the evaluation-set construction. We further introduce语义分层semantic stratification，它通过将文档组织到基于实体的集群的可解释的全局空间中并系统地生成缺失层的查询来基于语料库结构进行评估。这产生了（1）跨检索机制的正式语义覆盖保证和（2）检索失败模式的可解释可见性。, which grounds evaluation in corpus structure by organizing documents into an interpretable global space of entity-based clusters and systematically generating queries for missing strata. This yields (1) formal semantic coverage guarantees across retrieval regimes and (2) interpretable visibility into retrieval failure modes.

跨多个基准和检索方法的实验验证了我们的框架。结果揭示了系统的覆盖范围差距，识别了解释检索性能差异的结构信号，并表明分层评估可以产生更稳定和透明的评估，同时支持比聚合指标更值得信赖的决策。Experiments across multiple benchmarks and retrieval methods validate our framework. The results expose systematic coverage gaps, identify structural signals that explain variance in retrieval performance, and show that stratified evaluation yields more stable and transparent assessments while supporting more trustworthy decision-making than aggregate metrics.

7 局限性Limitations

这项工作有一些局限性，为未来的研究指明了方向。首先，我们的评估是在一组有限的数据集和嵌入模型上进行的。需要跨更多样化的领域、其他语言、领域和基准套件进行更广泛的验证。其次，我们实验中使用的查询是由法学硕士综合生成和验证的。尽管这可以实现可扩展的评估，但它可能会引入偏差或忽略细微的错误。纳入人工评估将提供更强的校准并提高评估的可靠性。最后，提取性能取决于提示的设计，而提示的设计并未进行彻底的优化。及时的工程设计可以进一步提高提取质量。This work has several limitations that suggest directions for future research. First, our evaluation is conducted on a limited set of datasets and embedding models. Broader validation across more diverse domain, other languages, domains, and benchmark suites is necessary. Second, the queries used in our experiments are synthetically generated and verified by LLMs. Although this enables scalable evaluation, it may introduce biases or overlook subtle errors. Incorporating human evaluation would provide a stronger calibration and improve the reliability of the assessment. Finally, the extraction performance depends on the design of prompts, which were not exhaustively optimized. Prompt engineering could further improve extraction quality.

8 结论Conclusion

通过将检索评估形式化为统计估计问题 结构化异质性，我们解释了为什么聚合指标会产生偏差和 决策不稳定的评估。我们的语义分层框架依据 评估语料库结构，暴露以前隐藏的故障模式，以及 实现更加透明和值得信赖的系统比较。我们希望这项工作 鼓励转向覆盖范围感知评估协议以进行检索和 RAG 系统。为了支持可重复性和未来的研究，我们将 公开发布本工作中引入的分层数据集。By formalizing retrieval evaluation as a statistical estimation problem under structured heterogeneity, we explain why aggregate metrics can yield biased and decision-unstable assessments. Our semantic stratification framework grounds evaluation in corpus structure, exposes previously hidden failure modes, and enables more transparent and trustworthy system comparison. We hope this work encourages a shift toward coverage-aware evaluation protocols for retrieval and RAG systems. To support reproducibility and future research, we will publicly release the stratified datasets introduced in this work.
