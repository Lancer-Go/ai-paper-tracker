研究大型语言模型分析糖尿病食谱的能力Investigating the Ability of Large Language Models to Analyze Recipes for Diabetes

Revathy Venkataramanan    Aditya Luthra    Venkatesan Nadimuthu    Amit Sheth Affiliation: AI Institute, University of South Carolina Affiliation: 1112 Greene Street, Columbia, SC 29201 USA Email: revathycv24@gmail.com
摘要Abstract

多项研究评估了大型语言模型 (LLM) 制定膳食计划的能力，并取得了积极的成果。这些模型可以处理自然语言输入，并利用从预训练中学到的知识来生成膳食计划。在这项工作中，我们调查了法学硕士分析给定糖尿病食谱的适用性的能力。法学硕士面临的主要挑战是检索相关的糖尿病饮食指南，将食谱分解为成分和烹饪方法，并应用这些指南来确定食谱的适用性。为了研究这些挑战，我们采用了三种提示，即（i）直接查询提示（ii）上下文引导提示，以及（iii）包含来自医学来源的不同级别的糖尿病饮食指南的示例性上下文提示。我们引入了为本次调查策划的基准数据集，其中包含 7607 个食谱，其中包括 3807 个适合糖尿病的食谱和 3800 个不适合糖尿病的食谱。我们的结果表明，大多数法学硕士在预测适合的食谱以防止有害结果时持谨慎态度。此外，可以使用饮食指南进行推理的模型在预测糖尿病食谱的适用性方面表现更好。总体而言，Mistral-7B 和 Llama 70B 表现出了优于同类产品的性能。Several studies have evaluated the ability of Large Language Models (LLMs) for meal planning, yielding positive outcomes. These models can process natural language inputs and leverage learned knowledge from their pretraining to generate meal plans. In this work, we investigate the ability of LLMs to analyze the suitability of given recipes for diabetes. The primary challenge for LLMs is to retrieve relevant dietary guidelines for diabetes, decompose recipes into ingredients and cooking methods, and apply these guidelines to determine the recipe’s suitability. To study these challenges, we employ three kinds of prompts namely, (i) Direct Query Prompt (ii) Context-Guided Prompt, and (iii) Exemplary Context Prompt that incorporate different levels of diabetes dietary guidelines from medical sources. We introduce a benchmark dataset curated for this investigation consisting of 7607 recipes that include 3807 recipes suitable for diabetes and 3800 recipes not suitable for diabetes. Our results demonstrate that most LLMs are cautious in predicting recipes as suitable to prevent detrimental outcomes. Further, the models that can reason using the dietary guidelines performed better in predicting the suitability of recipes for diabetes. Overall, Mistral-7B and Llama 70B showed superior performance to their counterparts.

1 简介Introduction

世界卫生组织估计全球有 8.3 亿人患有糖尿病The World Health Organization estimates that 830 million people worldwide have diabetes[1]，这种情况可以通过饮食调整来控制。 MayoClinic 和疾病控制中心 (CDC) 等医疗机构已发布了全面的饮食指南。然而，它们内容广泛，难以记忆，并且难以应用于日常膳食计划。虽然指南包含示例，但并不详尽。例如，蔬菜和水果被认为是健康碳水化合物和丰富纤维的营养来源，适合糖尿病管理。然而，应避免食用某些葡萄糖含量高的水果。这强调了糖尿病饮食决策的复杂性。虽然人工智能驱动的膳食追踪工具, a condition manageable through dietary adjustments. Comprehensive dietary guidelines have been published by medical sources like MayoClinic and the Center for Disease Control (CDC). However, they are extensive, challenging to remember, and difficult to apply in daily meal planning. While the guidelines include examples, they are not exhaustive. For instance, vegetables and fruits are considered nutritious sources of healthy carbohydrates and rich fiber, suitable for diabetes management. However, certain fruits high in glucose should be avoided. This underscores the complexity of dietary decisions for diabetes. While AI-powered meal-tracking tools[2]尽管这些年来越来越受欢迎，但它们往往缺乏对糖尿病的针对性分析，这可能是由于责任问题。have gained popularity over the years, they often lack targeted analysis for diabetes, likely due to liability concerns.

ChatGPT、Bard 和 Claude 等通用法学硕士已成为公众的宝贵工具。他们经常用于日常活动，例如膳食计划General-purpose LLMs such as ChatGPT, Bard, and Claude have become invaluable tools for the general public. They are frequently employed for everyday activities like meal planning[3], 假期行程, vacation itineraries[4]、起草电子邮件并提供教育帮助。有几项研究展示了法学硕士在膳食计划方面的积极作用, drafting emails, and providing educational assistance. There have been several studies that showcase the positive aspect of LLMs in meal planning[5]。同时，建议根据临床医生的建议进行补充. At the same time, they are suggested to be augmented with the clinician’s advice[6]。另一方面，研究也表明使用法学硕士进行营养管理的局限性，特别是糖尿病. On the other hand, studies have also shown the limitations of using LLMs for nutrition management, specifically for diabetes[7, 8].

This process requires comprehensive knowledge of the full scope of dietary guidelines. (ii) 非糖尿病专用食谱：(ii) Non-Diabetes Specific Recipes:

(i) 符合医疗指南：(i) Alignment to Medical Guidelines:在分析和推理时，与医学指南的一致至关重要。这是一个挑战，因为众所周知，法学硕士会产生幻觉While analyzing and reasoning, consistent alignment with medical guidelines is critical. This is a challenge as LLMs are known to hallucinate[36]. (ii) 知识限制：(ii) Knowledge Limitation:糖尿病指南需要营养、血糖指数和其他因素的知识，这使得从巨大的搜索空间中整合这些信息变得具有挑战性。Diabetes guidelines require knowledge of nutrition, glycemic index, and other factors, making it challenging to integrate this information from vast search spaces.(iii) 专家验证：(iii) Expert Validation:该模型需要由领域专家进行验证，这涉及繁琐的人力劳动。The model needs to be validated by domain experts which involves tedious human labor.

4.3 道德挑战Ethical Challenges

(i) 安全：(i) Safety:法学硕士产生的任何不正确或误导性信息都会带来重大风险，特别是在饮食和慢性病等高风险领域。如果盲目遵循这一建议，可能会导致不良的健康后果。Any incorrect or misleading information generated by LLMs poses significant risks, particularly in a high-stakes domain such as diet and chronic conditions. It could lead to adverse health outcomes if this advice is blindly followed.(ii) 有偏见的表述：(ii) Biased Representation:膳食指南的代表性不平等，因为尽管有几个类别值得推荐或避免，但只有少数类别被用作推理。There is an unequal representation of dietary guidelines, as only a few categories are used as reasoning despite having several categories to recommend or avoid.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们调查了法学硕士分析给定糖尿病食谱的适用性的能力。与膳食计划不同的是，法学硕士可以采取谨慎的方法，通过他们可以检索或记住的指南推荐膳食，而评估适合性则需要全面的指南。 Mistral 7B、Llama3.1-70B 的整体性能优于同类产品。 Gemma2 类模型具有较高的精确度和较低的召回率。由于该领域的高风险性质，大多数模型在预测适合糖尿病的食谱时都是保守的。在所讨论的核心挑战中，大多数模型都能够从其巨大的嵌入空间中检索医学知识。然而，当提示中给出外部知识时，表现出更好的性能。相反，在 ChatGPT 的情况下，这种外部知识也与模型的内部知识发生冲突。这些模型对健康碳水化合物、优质脂肪、添加糖和精制碳水化合物等概念有很好的理解。另一方面，模型在推理中需要理解和利用蛋白质、脱脂、低脂、油炸食品和超加工食品等类别。此外，能够使用饮食概念进行演绎分析和推理的模型表现出更好的性能。在我们未来的工作中，我们的目标是评估法学硕士是否可以单独推理成分和烹饪方法，以得出糖尿病食谱适用性的结论。In this work, we investigated the ability of LLMs to analyze the suitability of a given recipe for diabetes. Unlike meal planning, where LLMs can take a cautious approach by recommending meals derived using guidelines that they can retrieve or remember, assessing suitability demands a full scope of guidelines. Mistral 7B, Llama3.1-70B showed superior performance overall to its counterparts. The Gemma2 class of models had high precision and low recall. Most models were conservative in predicting the recipes suitable for diabetes due to the high-stakes nature of this domain. Of the core challenges discussed, most models were able to retrieve the medical knowledge from their vast embedding space. However, better performance was shown when external knowledge was given in the prompt. Conversely, this external knowledge also conflicted with the internal knowledge of the model in the case of ChatGPT. The models had a good understanding of concepts such as healthy carbohydrates, good fat, added sugar, and refined carbohydrates. On the other hand, models need to understand and utilize categories such as protein, non-fat, low-fat, deep-fried food, and ultra-processed food in their reasoning. In addition, the models that were able to deductively analyze and reason using dietary concepts showed better performance. In our future work, we aim to evaluate if the LLMs can individually reason over ingredients and cooking methods to drive a conclusion on the suitability of recipes for diabetes.
