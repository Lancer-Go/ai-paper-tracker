通过随机播放对重叠语音进行建模Modeling Overlapped Speech with Shuffles

Matthew    Samuele    Alexander    Lucas    Lukáš Burget    Sanjeev 1 Johns Hopkins University, USA, 2 LISN, CNRS, France
3 Carnegie Mellon University, USA, 4 Brno University of Technology, Czechia {wiesner,khudanpur}@jhu.edu, lucas.ondel@cnrs.fr, samuele.cornell@ieee.org, {ipoloka,burget}@fit.vutbr.cz
摘要Abstract

我们建议使用随机播放来对并行数据流（例如重叠语音）进行建模。具体来说，本文展示了如何使用混洗积和偏序有限状态自动机 (FSA) 来对齐和重叠语音的说话人属性转录。我们使用这些 FSA 的总分作为损失函数进行训练，在子词、单词和短语级别上边缘化重叠序列的所有可能序列化。为了减小图的大小，我们通过构造偏序 FSA 来施加时间约束。我们通过直接建模（令牌，说话人）元组来解决说话人归因问题。维特比对准通过洗牌产品 FSA 直接实现一次性对准。我们评估合成 LibriSpeech 重叠的性能。据我们所知，这是第一个能够对多说话者录音进行单次对齐的算法。所有算法均使用 k2 / Icefall 实现。We propose to model parallel streams of data, such as overlapped speech, using shuffles. Specifically, this paper shows how the shuffle product and partial order finite-state automata (FSAs) can be used for alignment and speaker-attributed transcription of overlapped speech. We train using the total score on these FSAs as a loss function, marginalizing over all possible serializations of overlapping sequences at subword, word, and phrase levels. To reduce graph size, we impose temporal constraints by constructing partial order FSAs. We address speaker attribution by modeling (token, speaker) tuples directly. Viterbi alignment through the shuffle product FSA directly enables one-pass alignment. We evaluate performance on synthetic LibriSpeech overlaps. To our knowledge, this is the first algorithm that enables single-pass alignment of multi-talker recordings. All algorithms are implemented using k2 / Icefall.

The shuffle product, which generates all possible interleavings of sequences that preserve the internal order of each sequence, has been used to represent the space of possible serializations of concurrent events[restivo2015shuffle]。类似地，可以将序列子集上的偏序表示为所有可接受的序列化的集合。. To keep the approach tractable, we introduce partial order constraints that prune the shuffle FSA using approximate token timing, yielding a principled trade-off between flexibility and computational cost. We show that other approaches for multi-talker ASR such as utterance-level Serialized Output Training (SOT)[kanda2020serialized], 代币级SOT, token-level SOT[kanda2022streaming]，以及说话者可辨别的 SD-CTC, and Speaker-Distinguishable SD-CTC[sdctc]可以理解为这个框架的特殊情况或放宽。can be understood as special cases or relaxations of this framework.

这种公式的一个好处是，即使存在重叠的语音，它也能生成对齐的、说话者归属的转录本。 通过混洗产品 FSA 的维特比路径直接提供了重叠语音的单通道对齐，据我们所知，以前从未这样做过。我们通过将 CTC 输出增加到（令牌，说话者）元组来扩展此框架，以对说话者归因进行建模，并提出此分布的因子模型和联合模型。我们描述了使用 CTC argmax 解码对多说话者语音进行 1 遍说话者属性解码的方法和支持标准 WFST 语言模型集成的目标说话者解码方法。A benefit of this formulation is that it produces aligned, speaker-attributed transcripts even in the presence of overlapped speech. The Viterbi path through the shuffle product FSA directly provides a single-pass alignment of overlapped speech, which, to our-knowledge, has not previously been done. We extend this framework to also model speaker attribution by augmenting the CTC output to (token, speaker) tuples, and propose both factored and joint models of this distribution. We describe methods for 1-pass speaker-attributed decoding for multi-talker speech using CTC argmax decoding and a target-speaker decoding method that enables standard WFST language model integration.

这些贡献开辟了新的研究可能性，特别是与处理野外多说话者数据相关。来自网络视频These contributions open up new research possibilities particularly relevant for processing in-the-wild multi-talker data e.g. from webvideos[li2023yodas]，其中每个讲话者的音频流不可用，并且现有注释（例如字幕或讲话者转动的近似文字记录）通常在时间上错位，尤其是当讲话者重叠时。, where per-speaker audio streams are unavailable and existing annotations—such as subtitles or approximate transcripts with speakers turn—are often temporally misaligned especially when speakers overlap.

The formulation itself is also not specific to speech: it applies whenever multiple independent processes emit sequences that are observed interleaved through a single channel without a known ordering, as in polyphonic music transcription, multi-agent activity recognition, or multi-event detection.

本文首先介绍了混洗产品，并展示了如何包含偏序约束以使图大小易于管理，即使对于长重叠语音序列也是如此。然后我们展示如何将这些核心技术应用于训练、解码和对齐重叠语音的问题。在此过程中，我们展示了先前的工作（包括 tSOT、SOT 和 SD-CTC）如何与洗牌产品相关。最后，我们探索了我们提出的关于合成 LibriSpeech 重叠的训练、解码和对齐方法。This paper first introduces the shuffle product and shows how partial order constraints can be included to make the graph size manageable, even for long sequences of overlapped speech. Then we show how these core techniques can be applied to the problem of training, decoding, and aligning overlapped speech. In the process we show how prior work including tSOT, SOT, and SD-CTC relate to the shuffle product. Finally, we explore our proposed training, decoding, and alignment methods on synthetic LibriSpeech overlaps.

2 相关工作Related Work

已经提出了多种方法来处理多说话者 ASR 中的重叠语音。早期工作使用排列不变训练（PIT）Several approaches have been proposed for handling overlapped speech in multi-talker ASR. Early work used Permutation Invariant Training (PIT)[yu2017permutation, kolbaek2017multitalker]，其中多个输出头共享一个编码器，并且每个分支都使用 CTC 损失进行训练, where multiple output heads share an encoder and each branch is trained with a CTC loss[yu2017recognizing, seki2018purely, chang2020end]。然而，PIT 需要预先固定最大说话人数量，并且扩展性很差，因为每个额外的说话人都会引入一个新分支并组合地增加损失评估的数量。. However, PIT requires fixing the maximum number of speakers a priori and scales poorly, as each additional speaker introduces a new branch and increases the number of loss evaluations combinatorially.

串行输出训练 (SOT)Serialized Output Training (SOT)[kanda2020serialized]通过将多说话者转录物连接成单个输出序列来回避这个问题，从而实现标准的序列到序列训练。然而，它所施加的话语级 FIFO 排序可能无法反映信号的时间动态。在代币级 SOT (tSOT) 中sidesteps this by concatenating multi-speaker transcripts into a single output sequence, enabling standard sequence-to-sequence training. The utterance-level FIFO ordering it imposes, however, may not reflect the temporal dynamics of the signal. In token-level SOT (tSOT)[kanda2022streaming]，标记根据其开始时间进行排序，因此它们在时间上对齐，但这需要准确的字级对齐，而对于必须直接处理重叠语音的野外数据来说，这通常不存在。, tokens are ordered according to their start times, so they are temporally aligned, but this requires accurate word-level alignments which do not generally exist for in-the-wild data where overlapped speech must be handled directly.
