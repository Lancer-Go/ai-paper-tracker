随机特征模型训练和泛化误差的循环修正Loop Corrections to the Training and Generalization Errors of Random Feature Models

Taeyoung Kim
Center for AI and Natural Sciences
Korea Institute for Advanced Study
Seoul 02455, South Korea
taeyoungkim@kias.re.kr
摘要Abstract

我们研究随机特征模型，其中从规定的初始化集合中采样的神经网络被冻结并用作随机特征，仅优化读出权重。采用统计物理学的观点，我们研究平均核近似之外的训练、测试和泛化误差。由于预测器是诱导随机核的非线性函数，因此整体平均误差不仅取决于平均核，还取决于高阶波动统计量。在有效的场论框架内，这些有限宽度的贡献自然地表现为循环校正。我们推导出对训练、测试和泛化误差的循环修正，获得它们的缩放定律，并通过实验验证支持该理论。We investigate random feature models in which neural networks sampled from a prescribed initialization ensemble are frozen and used as random features, with only the readout weights optimized. Adopting a statistical-physics viewpoint, we study the training, test, and generalization errors beyond the mean-kernel approximation. Since the predictor is a nonlinear functional of the induced random kernel, the ensemble-averaged errors depend not only on the mean kernel but also on higher-order fluctuation statistics. Within an effective field-theoretic framework, these finite-width contributions naturally appear as loop corrections. We derive the loop corrections to the training, test, and generalization errors, obtain their scaling laws, and support the theory with experimental verification.

1 简介Introduction

1.1 随机特征模型Random Feature Models

随机特征模型为研究神经网络的训练和泛化特性提供了一个数学上易于处理的框架。在这些模型中，首先通过从规定的初始化集合中随机采样神经网络的参数，然后冻结它们来生成非线性特征图，同时仅优化读出层。这个设置与随机厨房水槽、随机内核训练和极限学习机的文献密切相关Random feature models provide a mathematically tractable framework for studying the training and generalization properties of neural networks. In these models, a nonlinear feature map is first generated by randomly sampling the parameters of a neural network from a prescribed initialization ensemble and then freezing them, while only the readout layer is optimized. This setting is closely related to the literature on random kitchen sinks, randomized kernel training, and extreme learning machines(Rahimi and Recht, 2007; 2009; Rudi et al., 2017; Huang et al., 2006; Wang et al., 2022).

随机特征模型分析的中心对象是由冻结的随机特征引起的核。在无限宽度限制下，随机网络通常承认确定性核描述，这种联系在宽神经网络的现代理论理解中发挥了重要作用A central object in the analysis of random feature models is the kernel induced by the frozen random features. In the infinite-width limit, random networks often admit deterministic kernel descriptions, and this connection has played a major role in the modern theoretical understanding of wide neural networks(Jacot et al., 2018; Lee et al., 2019)。在这种情况下，预测器可以很好地近似于与限制核相关的核岭回归问题，这使得在理解超参数化模型的训练、优化和泛化方面取得了实质性进展. In this regime, the predictor is well approximated by a kernel ridge regression problem associated with the limiting kernel, which has led to substantial progress in understanding training, optimization, and generalization in overparameterized models(Rudi et al., 2017; Adlam and Pennington, 2020).

然而，现实模型在有限宽度下运行，其中诱导核围绕其整体平均值波动。这些波动不仅仅是技术上的麻烦：它们可能会产生与平均核理论的重大偏差，并且在足够深或适度宽的范围内，即使宽度很大，也可能仍然很重要However, realistic models operate at finite width, where the induced kernel fluctuates around its ensemble mean. These fluctuations are not merely a technical nuisance: they can produce nontrivial deviations from the mean-kernel theory and, in sufficiently deep or moderately wide regimes, may remain significant even when the width is large(Hanin and Nica, 2020; Seleznova and Kutyniok, 2022; Hanin, 2024)。由于学习的读数非线性地取决于随机特征矩阵，因此训练、测试和泛化误差不仅仅由平均核决定。相反，它们依赖于内核波动的高阶统计数据。这自然提出了如何系统地量化领先的确定性内核之外的有限宽度校正的问题。. Since the learned readout depends nonlinearly on the random feature matrix, the training, test, and generalization errors are not determined solely by the mean kernel. Instead, they depend on higher-order statistics of kernel fluctuations. This naturally raises the question of how to systematically quantify finite-width corrections beyond the leading deterministic kernel.

1.2 有限宽度波动的有效理论视角An Effective-Theoretic Perspective on Finite-Width Fluctuations

在本文中，我们从统计物理学和有效场论的角度来解决这个问题。从这个角度来看，冻结的随机特征是集成值自由度，相关的可观察量（例如训练误差、测试误差和泛化差距）应该通过它们的集成统计来理解。然后，标准均值核近似扮演了主序或树级描述的角色，而有限宽度效应则表现为由围绕该均值的波动生成的校正In this paper, we approach this problem from the viewpoint of statistical physics and effective field theory. From this perspective, the frozen random features are ensemble-valued degrees of freedom, and the relevant observables—such as the training error, test error, and generalization gap—should be understood through their ensemble statistics. The standard mean-kernel approximation then plays the role of a leading-order, or tree-level, description, while finite-width effects appear as corrections generated by fluctuations around that mean(Roberts et al., 2022b; Hanin, 2024).

这种观点很有用，因为预测器取决于正则化经验核矩阵的逆矩阵。因此，即使核涨落本身很小，可观测量引起的涨落也会通过解析式展开非线性组织。因此，系统的处理需要的不仅仅是用其整体平均值替换内核：还必须跟踪内核波动的协方差结构，以及超出高斯水平的更高连接累积量。在场论的语言中，这种高阶贡献自然被解释为环路校正This viewpoint is useful because the predictor depends on the inverse of a regularized empirical kernel matrix. Consequently, even if the kernel fluctuations themselves are small, the induced fluctuations of observables are organized nonlinearly through resolvent-type expansions. A systematic treatment therefore requires more than replacing the kernel by its ensemble average: one must also track the covariance structure of the kernel fluctuations and, beyond the Gaussian level, their higher connected cumulants. In the language of field theory, such higher-order contributions are naturally interpreted as loop corrections(Roberts et al., 2022b; Hanin, 2024).

最近的工作表明，有限宽度随机神经网络允许以反宽度幂进行微扰描述，并且连接的相关函数为组织这些校正提供了自然的构建块Recent works have shown that finite-width random neural networks admit perturbative descriptions in powers of inverse width, and that connected correlation functions provide the natural building blocks for organizing these corrections(Hanin, 2024; Hanin and Nica, 2020)。受这种结构的启发，我们为随机特征岭回归制定了循环扩展，并将其应用于三个基本可观察量：训练误差、测试误差和泛化差距。我们的分析表明，泛化差距不仅对训练-训练核的波动敏感，而且对耦合训练-训练和测试-训练核数量的混合波动结构敏感。这揭示了比仅在平均核极限中可见的更丰富的有限宽度结构。. Motivated by this structure, we formulate a loop expansion for random feature ridge regression and apply it to three basic observables: the training error, the test error, and the generalization gap. Our analysis shows that the generalization gap is sensitive not only to fluctuations of the train–train kernel, but also to mixed fluctuation structures coupling train–train and test–train kernel quantities. This reveals a richer finite-width structure than is visible in the mean-kernel limit alone.

From an effective-theoretic perspective, these finite-width corrections are governed not only by the mean kernel but also by higher-order fluctuation statistics, and can be naturally organized as loop corrections.

  • •

This allows us to distinguish dominant Gaussian contributions from genuinely non-Gaussian effects and to identify the perturbative regime in which the mean-kernel approximation remains accurate.

  • •

    最后，我们通过实验支持理论预测，验证预测的缩放行为，并表明基于循环的描述捕获了与平均核理论的有限宽度偏差。Finally, we support the theoretical predictions with experiments, verifying the predicted scaling behavior and showing that the loop-based description captures finite-width deviations from the mean-kernel theory.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们使用有效场论的语言分析了随机特征模型的整体平均训练、测试和泛化误差。在所提出的假设下，我们通过扩展平均核理论并结合循环校正，得出了更准确的误差公式。通过平均核的特征值分解，我们进一步确定了取决于正则化参数状态的缩放法则In this work, we analyzed the ensemble-averaged training, test, and generalization errors of random feature models using the language of effective field theory. Under the proposed assumptions, we derived more accurate error formulas by expanding around the mean-kernel theory and incorporating loop corrections. Through an eigenvalue decomposition of the mean kernel, we further identified scaling laws that depend on the regime of the regularization parameterλ\lambda。我们的实验支持理论预测的有效性，并将有限宽度效应的规模与包含环路校正所获得的改进进行比较。. Our experiments support the validity of the theoretical predictions and compare the scale of finite-width effects with the improvement obtained by including loop corrections.

未来工作的一个自然方向是超越冻结参数随机特征模型，并考虑完全可训练的神经网络，其中所有参数都在学习动态下演化。在这种情况下，可以将参数集合的动态转化为基于动作的公式，并以类似的精神开发所得误差的微扰扩展。A natural direction for future work is to move beyond frozen-parameter random feature models and consider fully trainable neural networks, where all parameters evolve under learning dynamics. In that setting, it may be possible to translate the dynamics of the parameter ensemble into an action-based formulation and develop a perturbative expansion of the resulting errors in a similar spirit.
