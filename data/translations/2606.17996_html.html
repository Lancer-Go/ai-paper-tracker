用于长期时间序列预测的多重周期性和具有通道相关性的小波分解Multiple cyclicity and Wavelet Decomposition with Channel Correlation for Long-term Time Series Forecasting

Bin Wang1, Heming Yang1 and Jinfang Sheng1∗ 1Bin Wang, Heming Yang and Jinfang Sheng are all with School of Computer Science and Engineering, Central South University, HuNan, China. { wb_csut, 244712142, jfsheng}@csu.edu.cn*Corresponding author: Jinfang Sheng
摘要Abstract

周期性和趋势是时间序列数据的重要组成部分，许多基于周期性和趋势的研究在长期时间序列预测方面取得了良好的成果。然而，我们认为当前的工作忽略了时间序列数据中现实世界通道间相关性的影响，这导致了次优的预测。此外，这些模型依赖复杂的设计来捕获不同的信息，从而导致计算效率低下。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了 McWC，这是一种长期时间序列预测模型，可以分别对周期性、趋势和通道间相关性进行建模。具体来说，McWC 首先使用多层循环构造模块将循环信息与数据解耦。然后，它使用多层感知器提取通道间相关性。接下来，它使用多级小波分解模块对数据中的多层高频和低频信息进行建模和融合。最后，它聚合不同组件的结果以获得输出。同时，我们通过计算频域中的损失函数来解耦通道内自相关。对六个真实世界数据集的实验表明，McWC 实现了最先进的性能，表现出出色的计算效率和历史信息提取能力。Cyclicity and trend are important components of time series data and many studies based on cyclicity and trend have achieved good results in long-term time series forecasting. However, we believe that current work neglects the influence of real-world inter-channel correlations in time series data which leads to suboptimal predictions. Furthermore, these models rely on complex designs to capture diverse information so that resulting in low computational efficiency. To address this challenge, we propose McWC, a long-term time series forecasting model that separately models the cyclicity, trend, and inter-channel correlations. Specifically, McWC first decouples cyclical information from data using a multi-layer cyclicity construction module. Then, it extracts inter-channel correlations using multi-layer perceptron. Next, it models and fuses the multi-layer high-frequency and low-frequency information from data using a multi-level wavelet decomposition module. Finally, it aggregates the results of different components to obtain the output. Simultaneously, we decouple intra-channel autocorrelations by calculating a loss function in the frequency domain. Experiments on six real-world datasets demonstrate that McWC achieves state-of-the-art performance, exhibiting excellent computational efficiency and historical information extraction capabilities.

I 简介INTRODUCTION

长期时间序列预测（LTSF）已成为财务预测和分析等关键领域的重要组成部分Long-term time series forecasting(LTSF) has been an important part in some key areas, such as financial forecasting and analysis[20]、物联网设备信息预测, IoT device information prediction[21]、城市交通预测, urban traffic forecast[2, 1]，以及能源和资源分配预测, and energy and resource allocation forecasting[9]。然而，由于长期时间序列是现实世界的序列数据，具有复杂和非平稳的特征，这对研究人员从大量历史数据中捕获有意义的依赖关系提出了重大挑战。. However, because long-term time series are real-world sequential data with complex and non-stationary characteristics, it poses significant challenges for researchers to capture meaningful dependencies from extensive historical data.

随着深度学习的发展，人们提出了各种用于时间序列建模的架构。基于 CNN 的模型With the development of deep learning, various architectures have been proposed for time series modeling. CNN-based models[4]通过固定内核捕获局部时间和通道依赖性，但在建模全局依赖性方面表现不佳。基于变压器的模型capture local temporal and channel dependencies via fixed kernels, but perform poorly in modeling global dependencies. Transformer-based models[10]利用自注意力提取内在关系并实现准确的长期预测，但它们往往关注高频信息而忽略有用的低频成分，从而限制了表示多样性。基于线性的模型将时间序列分解为基本组成部分，并通过简单的线性运算对其进行建模。即使计算量和参数较少，趋势周期分解和基于傅立叶的下采样等方法也取得了有竞争力的结果。因此，我们认为正确建模时间序列中的依赖关系是高性能长期预测的关键。use self-attention to extract intrinsic relations and achieve accurate long-term forecasting, yet they tend to focus on high frequency information while ignoring useful low-frequency components, thus limiting representation diversity. Linear-based models decompose time series into basic components and model them with simple linear operations. Methods such as trend-cycle decomposition and Fourier-based downsampling have achieved competitive results even with low computation and parameters. Therefore, we argue that properly modeling dependencies in time series is the key to high performance long-term forecasting.

我们建议时间序列数据由周期性模式、趋势和通道间修正成分组成。受当前通道无关方法的影响，许多模型忽略了通道间修正，仅通过对周期性模式和趋势进行建模仍然取得了良好的结果。然而，我们认为，由于通道间修正客观存在，对它们进行建模可以在一定程度上提高模型的预测性能。同时，大多数现有模型要么直接捕获时间序列数据中的时间关系，要么构建简单的循环模式，从而无法很好地表示时间关系。因此，我们认为，基于时间序列数据的固有周期构建多种循环模式可以有效地对数据中的循环模式进行建模。We propose that time series data is composed of cyclical patterns, trend, and inter-channel corrections components. Influenced by the current channel-independent approach, many models ignore inter-channel corrections and still achieve good results by only modeling cyclical patterns and trend. However, we argue that since inter-channel corrections objectively exist, modeling them can improve the model’s forecasting performance to some extent. Meanwhile, most existing models either directly capture temporal relationships in the time series data or construct simple cyclical patterns which prevents temporal relationships from being well represented. Therefore, we believe that constructing multiple cyclical patterns based on the inherent periods of the time series data can effectively model the cyclical patterns in the data.

总之，我们提出了 McWC，它使用多周期构造块（McB）来提取循环模式，然后通过通道校正提取块（CEB）捕获通道间相关性，最后通过我们设计的多级小波分解块（MWB）提取趋势。通过我们的设计，时间序列数据的主要组成部分被分别提取​​并由三个相互关联但独立处理的部分建模，使每个组成部分都能被模型充分利用，从而获得更好的预测性能。我们的测试表明，McWC 在各种长期预测任务中都取得了出色的性能。我们在本文中的贡献可总结如下：In summary, we propose McWC, which uses the Multi-cycle Construction Block (McB) to extract cyclical patterns, then captures inter-channel correlations through the Channel-Correction Extraction Block (CEB) and finally extracts trend through our designed Multi-level Wavelet Decomposition Block (MWB). Through our design, the main components of the time series data are separately extracted and modeled by three interrelated yet independently handled parts, allowing each component to be fully utilized by the model, thereby achieving better forecasting performance. Our tests show that McWC achieves excellent performance on a wide range of long-term forecasting tasks. Our contributions in this paper can be summarized as follows:

  1. 1.

    我们提出了 McB，其思想是使用先验周期信息显式构建循环模式来捕获时间序列数据中不同频率的周期模式。我们提出了MWB模块，利用多级小波分解来提取趋势，并对不同尺度的频域信息进行建模，消除噪声干扰，同时有效捕获趋势变化。We proposed McB based on the idea of explicitly constructing cyclical patterns using prior periodic information to capture periodic patterns of different frequencies within the time series data. And we proposed MWB module which uses multi-level wavelet decomposition to extract trend and models frequency-domain information at different scales, eliminating noise interference while capturing trend changes effectively.

  2. 2.

    我们提出了CEB，它对不同尺度之间的动态交互进行建模，处理不同尺度的通道间校正，增强模型处理多尺度信息的能力。We proposed CEB, which models dynamic interactions between different scales, handling inter-channel corrections at various scales and enhancing the model’s ability to process multi-scale information.

  3. 3.

    我们设计的 McWC 使用三个相互关联但单独建模的组件进行时间序列预测。 McWC 在多个长期时间序列预测任务和数据集上展示了出色的性能和卓越的效率。Our designed McWC uses three interrelated but separately modeled components for time series forecasting. McWC demonstrates outstanding performance with remarkable efficiency across multiple long-term time series forecasting tasks and datasets.

Therefore, how to properly decompose the data into different components and extract inter-channel corrections to achieve accurate future predictions has always been a core concern for researchers.

IV 实验Experiments

IV-A 实验设置Experiment setting

实验数据集：Experimental Datasets:为了验证我们模式的预测准确性，我们选择了 LTSF 中六个常用的现实数据集（ETTh1、ETTh2、ETTm1、ETTm2、天气、电力）进行实验。To validate the forecasting accuracy of our mode, we selected six commonly used real-world datasets in LTSF (ETTh1, ETTh2, ETTm1, ETTm2, Weather, Electricity) for experimentation.

V 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 McWC，一种用于长期时间序列预测的计算高效模型。 McWC基于组件分解的思想，采用McB、CEB和MWB模块对周期性、趋势和通道相关性进行建模，提高了捕获复杂模式和数据突变的能力。我们进一步引入 FreDF 来优化损失函数，增强通道内自相关建模和预测性能。对现实世界数据集的大量实验表明，McWC 有效地利用了历史信息并实现了高质量的预测。In this paper, we propose McWC, a computationally efficient model for long-term time series forecasting. Based on the idea of component decomposition, McWC adopts the McB, CEB, and MWB modules to model periodicity, trend and channel correlation, improving its ability to capture complex patterns and data mutations. We further introduce FreDF to optimize the loss function, enhancing intra-channel autocorrelation modeling and prediction performance. Extensive experiments on real-world datasets show that McWC effectively exploits historical information and achieves high-quality forecasting.
