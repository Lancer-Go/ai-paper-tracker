摘要Abstract

少步蒸馏已成为加速高级视觉生成模型的有效策略，但之前的工作主要集中在蒸馏目标上。在这项工作中，我们从补充的角度重新审视了几步蒸馏，重点关注对学生表现至关重要的培训方案。以Qwen-Image-2.0为代表案例，我们系统地研究了统一文本到图像生成和指令引导图像编辑蒸馏的三个因素：Few-step distillation has become an effective strategy for accelerating advanced visual generative models, yet prior work has largely focused on distillation objectives. In this work, we revisit few-step distillation from a complementary perspective, focusing on the training recipe that critically shapes student performance. Using Qwen-Image-2.0 as a representative case, we systematically investigate three factors in unified text-to-image generation and instruction-guided image editing distillation:数据构成、教师指导和任务混合data composition, teacher guidance, and task mixture。我们的实证分析揭示了一些非显而易见的行为，这些行为激励了. Our empirical analysis reveals several non-obvious behaviors, which motivate the development ofQwen-图像-FlashQwen-Image-Flash。总的来说，我们的结果表明，有效的几步蒸馏不仅需要精心设计的目标，还需要对更广泛的培训渠道进行有原则的组织。. Overall, our results suggest that effective few-step distillation requires not only carefully designed objectives, but also principled organization of the broader training pipeline.

简介Introduction

视觉生成模型已经超越了传统的文本到图像（T2I）系统，并日益发展为通用视觉基础模型Visual generative models have moved beyond conventional text-to-image (T2I) systems and are increasingly developing into general-purpose visual foundation models(esser2024scaling; song2026awaking; liu2026ernieimagetechnicalreport; mao2026wan; song2026awaking)。现代模型可以根据复杂的提示生成高保真图像，生成密集且结构化的视觉文本，利用后训练来改善对齐和视觉偏好. Modern models can generate high-fidelity images from complex prompts, produce dense and structured visual text, leverage post-training to improve alignment and visual preference(liu2026flow)，并支持统一框架内指令引导的图像编辑, and support instruction-guided image editing within a unified framework(zhao2026qwen)。这些进步扩展了它们在内容创建、图形设计、交互式编辑和多模式应用程序中的适用性。. These advances expand their applicability to content creation, graphic design, interactive editing, and multimodal applications.

然而，这些模型的实际使用仍然受到采样成本的限制。扩散However, the practical use of these models is still constrained by their sampling cost. Diffusion(ho2020denoising; song2020score)和基于流量的and flow-based(lipman2022flow)视觉生成器通常通过迭代轨迹合成图像，在推理过程中需要进行许多函数评估，从而导致大量的延迟和计算。这使得在延迟敏感或资源有限的设置（包括交互式图像编辑）中的部署面临挑战visual generators typically synthesize images through iterative trajectories, requiring many function evaluations during inference and thereby incurring substantial latency and computation. This makes deployment challenging in latency-sensitive or resource-limited settings, including interactive image editing(meng2022sdedit; Brooks2022InstructPix2PixLT), 设备上生成, on-device generation(li2023snapfusion; zhao2024mobilediffusion)，以及大规模的视觉内容制作, and large-scale visual content production(azuma1997survey; yin2025causvid)。少步蒸馏通过将多步教师的采样行为压缩为只需几个步骤即可生成的学生模型来解决此限制。. Few-step distillation addresses this limitation by compressing the sampling behavior of a multi-step teacher into a student model that can generate with only a few steps.

这一观察自然地将我们的注意力从单独设计蒸馏目标转移到理解更广泛的培训方案，该方案决定了这些目标在实践中是否有效。这些考虑引导我们研究一个更实际的问题：This observation naturally shifts our attention from designing distillation objectives in isolation to understanding the broader training recipe that determines whether such objectives can be effective in practice. 我们的实证分析得出三个关键发现。首先，T2I 蒸馏对数据组合高度敏感：增加多样性或使用更多特定于目标的数据并不一定会提高性能，而来自单个类别的连贯数据可以意外地很好地传输。其次，从下游任务中具有互补优势的教师那里转移知识仍然具有挑战性。为此，我们提出了一种逐步的多教师指导策略，结合他们特定任务的专业知识，同时保持培训稳定性。第三，在联合T2I编辑蒸馏中，任务混合起着决定性作用，在平衡的T2I与编辑数据比率（T2I：Edit）下实现了最佳的统一性能。总之，这些观察结果表明，现代视觉生成模型的几个步骤的精炼不仅取决于目标，还取决于训练过程中数据、教师和任务的结构。Our empirical analysis leads to three key findings. First, T2I distillation is highly sensitive to data composition: increasing diversity or using more target-specific data does not necessarily improve performance, whereas coherent data from a single category can transfer unexpectedly well. Second, transferring knowledge from teachers with complementary strengths across downstream tasks remains challenging. To this end, we propose a step-wise multi-teacher guidance strategy that combines their task-specific expertise while preserving training stability. Third, in joint T2I-editing distillation, the task mixture plays a decisive role, with the best unified performance achieved under a balanced T2I-to-editing data ratio (T2I:Edit). Together, these observations suggest that few-step distillation of modern visual generative models is shaped not only by the objective, but also by how data, teachers, and tasks are structured during training.
