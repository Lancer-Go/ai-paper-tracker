G-CARL：基于检查表的奖励学习，用于以患者为导向的医疗报告解释G-CARL: Grounded Checklist-Aligned Reward Learning for Patient-Oriented Medical Report Interpretation

摘要Abstract

对医疗报告的个性化解读已成为患者日益重要的需求。满足这一需求需要以证据为基础的医学事实和依赖于上下文的患者沟通，但现有的医学视觉语言任务并不能充分满足这些双重要求。 为了弥补这一差距，我们引入Personalized interpretation of medical reports has emerged as an increasingly important need among patients. Addressing this need requires both evidence-grounded medical factuality and context-dependent patient communication, yet existing medical vision-language tasks do not adequately capture these dual requirements. To bridge this gap, we introduce以患者为中心的医疗报告解读Patient-oriented Medical Report Interpretation（PMRI），一种新颖的开放式多模式生成任务，需要模型根据用户的查询和对话历史以准确且易于理解的语言解释医疗报告。 这两个目标在可验证性方面存在根本差异，但仍然紧密耦合，使得它们难以在传统的监督微调和整体强化学习范式下联合优化。 为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了 G-CARL，这是一种扎根的、与清单一致的强化学习框架，它将用于原子声明验证的多源检索与用于响应覆盖的上下文感知、特定于实例的加权清单相结合，为事实性、用户需求满意度和表达质量提供结构化监督，而不限制响应多样性。 我们进一步构建(PMRI), a novel open-ended multimodal generation task that requires models to explain medical reports in accurate and accessible language based on a user’s query and dialogue history. These two objectives differ fundamentally in their verifiability, yet remain tightly coupled, making them difficult to optimize jointly under conventional supervised fine-tuning and holistic reinforcement learning paradigms. To address this challenge, we propose G-CARL, a grounded, checklist-aligned reinforcement learning framework that combines multi-source retrieval for atomic claim verification with context-aware, instance-specific weighted checklists for response coverage, providing structured supervision for factuality, user-demand satisfaction, and expression quality without constraining response diversity. We further construct医学硕士报告MMedReport，一个真实世界的 PMRI 基准，以及临床医生设计的三维评估协议。大量实验表明，G-CARL 在整体质量、声明级别精度和检查表召回率方面始终优于现有的训练后基线。临床医生的配对偏好评估进一步证实 G-CARL 产生的解释更准确且更符合患者需求。, a real-world PMRI benchmark, along with a clinician-designed three-dimensional evaluation protocol. Extensive experiments demonstrate that G-CARL consistently outperforms existing post-training baselines in overall quality, claim-level precision, and checklist recall. Pairwise preference evaluation by clinicians further confirms that G-CARL produces interpretations that are more accurate and better aligned with patient needs.

1 简介Introduction

医学报告是在专业医学背景下撰写的，通常会呈现异常值和描述性发现，而不会向非专业读者解释其含义。 这种差距在在线医疗保健场景中变得尤为突出，患者上传一张或多张报告图像，并根据他们的个人担忧提出开放式问题。 为了弥补这一差距，我们引入Written within a professional medical context, medical reports commonly present abnormal values and descriptive findings without explaining their implications for non-expert readers. This gap becomes particularly salient in online healthcare scenarios, where patients upload one or more report images and ask open-ended questions shaped by their personal concerns. To bridge this gap, we introduce以患者为中心的医疗报告解读Patient-oriented Medical Report Interpretation（PMRI），一种新颖的开放式多模式生成任务。 除了简单地识别报告结果之外，PMRI 还必须将专业医学知识与以患者为中心的沟通相结合，将报告证据转化为易于理解的解释，解决用户特定的问题并提供临床谨慎的指导。(PMRI), a novel open-ended multimodal generation task. Beyond simply recognizing report findings, PMRI must align professional medical knowledge with patient-centered communication by transforming report evidence into accessible explanations, addressing user-specific concerns, and offering clinically cautious guidance.

First, it is an证据有限的evidence-bounded生成任务，其反应不仅必须基于报告，而且必须基于可靠的临床知识和医学上连贯的诊断推理，特别是在解释异常情况或建议后续行动时。这一要求对于患者安全至关重要，因为不受支持的解释和不适当的建议可能会误导患者或延误必要的护理。其次，PMRI是generation task whose responses must be grounded not only in the reports but also in reliable clinical knowledge and medically coherent diagnostic reasoning, particularly when explaining abnormalities or suggesting follow-up actions. Accordingly, we model PMRI quality along three core dimensions: medical accuracy, demand satisfaction, and expression quality.

鉴于 PMRI 的性质，一个简单的策略是收集大规模医生撰写的解释并通过监督微调 (SFT) 训练多模态模型Given the nature of PMRI, a straightforward strategy is to collect large-scale physician-written interpretations and train multimodal models with supervised fine-tuning (SFT)(6; 28)。然而，SFT 可能会过度适应参考解释的具体措辞，并鼓励模仿特定答案而不是学习基本原理。这在 PMRI 中尤其受到限制，因为对于同一报告和用户查询，只要忠实于证据并解决用户的担忧，多个响应在临床上可能是可接受的。因此，医生参考资料就响应应涵盖的内容提供了有用的指导，而不是完全定义 PMRI 输出的质量空间。. However, SFT can overfit to the specific wording of the reference interpretation and encourage imitation of particular answers rather than learning the underlying principles. This is especially limiting in PMRI, where multiple responses may be clinically acceptable for the same report and user query as long as they remain faithful to the evidence and address the user’s concern. Physician references therefore provide useful guidance on what a response should cover, rather than fully defining the quality space of PMRI outputs.

具有基于奖励的后训练的强化学习 (RL)，例如组相对策略优化 (GRPO)Reinforcement learning (RL) with reward-based post-training, such as Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)(31)，为改进大型视觉语言模型超越参考模仿提供了一种有前途的替代方案, offers a promising alternative for improving large vision-language models beyond reference imitation(39)。 然而，将强化学习应用于 PMRI 需要反映不同目标的异构可验证性的奖励。医疗真实性可以根据上传的报告和临床知识进行外部验证，而需求满意度和表达质量更多地取决于患者的关注点和咨询背景。这激发了奖励信号，可以区分特定于目标的错误，而不是将整个响应分解为单个分数。. However, applying RL to PMRI requires rewards that reflect heterogeneous verifiability of different objectives. Medical factuality can be externally verified against the uploaded report and clinical knowledge, whereas demand satisfaction and expression quality depend more on the patient’s concern and the consultation context. This motivates reward signals that can distinguish objective-specific errors rather than collapsing the entire response into a single score.

现有的奖励设计并不能完全满足这一要求。整体 MLLM 作为法官评分Existing reward designs do not fully meet this requirement. Holistic MLLM-as-a-Judge scoring(42; 5)将整个解释压缩为单个奖励，使得监督变得粗糙并且高度依赖于法官模型的医学知识。因此，当整体反应显得流畅且可信时，局部幻觉可能会被忽视，尽管单个未经支持的医学主张可能从根本上误导患者。最近的工作引入了基于规则的奖励，以提供更有条理的监督compresses an entire interpretation into a single reward, making the supervision coarse and highly dependent on the judge model’s medical knowledge. As a result, localized hallucinations may be overlooked when the overall response appears fluent and plausible, despite the fact that a single unsupported medical claim can fundamentally mislead patients. Recent work has introduced rubric-based rewards to provide more structured supervision(2; 9)。然而，静态评估标准对于 PMRI 来说仍然不够，因为不同案例的评估优先级差异很大。 PMRI 错误通常并非源于完全错误的反应，而是源于忽略病例关键信息或强调次要细节，同时忽视最重要的临床建议和用户关注的问题。由于静态评分标准旨在捕获一般响应质量，因此它们通常对这些特定案例的遗漏和错误的重点不敏感。. However, static rubrics remain insufficient for PMRI because evaluation priorities vary substantially across cases. PMRI errors often stem not from entirely incorrect responses, but from omitting case-critical information or emphasizing secondary details while overlooking the most important clinical recommendations and user concerns. Since static rubrics are designed to capture generic response quality, they are often insensitive to these case-specific omissions and misplaced emphases.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 G-CARL，这是一种强化学习框架，具有基于检索和清单引导的奖励。 G-CARL根据每个目标的可验证性边界分配奖励机制。对于外部可验证的医疗事实，它将每个响应分解为原子医疗声明，从上传的报告和多源医疗数据存储中检索支持证据，并评估每个声明的事实支持和上下文相关性。这种声明级别的奖励为稀疏的事实错误提供了本地化监督，并阻止了不受支持的阐述。 对于需求满意度和表达质量等与上下文相关的目标，G-CARL 通过 MLLM 生成构建特定于实例的加权检查表，然后由临床医生细化。每个清单项目都会分配一个自动生成的权重，从而使奖励能够强调与当前报告和用户问题最相关的方面。这会产生一个明确的清单分数，为响应是否适当解决用户的担忧提供透明的监督。 总之，我们的贡献如下：To address these challenges, we propose G-CARL, a reinforcement learning framework with retrieval-grounded and checklist-guided rewards. G-CARL assigns reward mechanisms according to the verifiability boundary of each objective. For externally verifiable medical factuality, it decomposes each response into atomic medical claims, retrieves supporting evidence from the uploaded report and a multi-source medical datastore, and evaluates each claim for factual support and contextual relevance. This claim-level reward provides localized supervision for sparse factual errors and discourages unsupported elaboration. For context-dependent objectives such as demand satisfaction and expression quality, G-CARL constructs instance-specific weighted checklists through MLLM generation followed by clinician refinement. Each checklist item is assigned an automatically generated weight, allowing the reward to emphasize the aspects most relevant to the current report and user question. This yields an explicit checklist score that provides transparent supervision for whether the response addresses the user’s concern appropriately. In summary, our contributions are as follows:

与医学事实不同，需求满意度和表达质量等维度本质上是主观的，无法根据可参考的知识进行验证。 此外，这些维度高度依赖于实例： 响应是否被认为是完整的、有帮助的或表达良好的，取决于具体的报告、用户关注点和对话上下文。 不同的标准在临床重要性上也有很大差异。因此，分配单一的整体奖励对于应改进响应的哪些方面几乎没有提供任何指导，并且无法区分关键要求和理想但非必要的要求。Unlike medical factuality, dimensions such as demand satisfaction and expression quality are inherently subjective and cannot be verified against referenceable knowledge. 5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们将 PMRI 视为一项具有挑战性但尚未得到充分探索的任务，它需要以证据为基础的医学事实和依赖于背景的患者沟通。我们提出了 G-CARL，这是一种强化学习框架，通过基于检索的声明验证和特定案例的检查表指导来分解奖励监督。在新建的基础上进行了广泛的实验In this paper, we present PMRI as a challenging yet underexplored task that requires both evidence-grounded medical factuality and context-dependent patient communication. We propose G-CARL, a reinforcement learning framework that decomposes reward supervision through retrieval-grounded claim verification and case-specific checklist guidance. Extensive experiments on the newly constructed医学硕士报告MMedReport基准以及临床医生撰写的评估和人类偏好研究表明，G-CARL 持续提高以患者为导向的医疗报告解释的质量。我们希望这项工作为更可靠、以患者为中心的多模式医疗助理奠定基础。benchmark, together with clinician-authored evaluation and human preference studies, show that G-CARL consistently improves the quality of patient-oriented medical report interpretation.
