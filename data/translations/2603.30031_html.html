三元认知架构：通过时空和认知摩擦限制自主行动The Triadic Cognitive Architecture: Bounding Autonomous Action via Spatio-Temporal and Epistemic Friction

Davide Di Gioia
ucesigi@ucl.ac.uk
摘要Abstract

当前的自主人工智能代理主要由大型语言模型（LLM）驱动，在认知失重状态下运行：它们处理信息时没有内在的网络拓扑、时间节奏或认知限制。因此，启发式代理循环（例如，ReAct）可能会在交互环境中表现出故障模式，包括在拥塞情况下过度使用工具、在时间衰减情况下长时间审议以及在模糊证据下的脆弱行为。在本文中，我们提出了三元认知架构（TCA），这是一个统一的数学框架，为连续时间物理学中的机器推理奠定了基础。通过综合非线性滤波理论、黎曼路由几何和最优控制，我们正式定义了概念Current autonomous AI agents, driven primarily by Large Language Models (LLMs), operate in a state of cognitive weightlessness: they process information without an intrinsic sense of network topology, temporal pacing, or epistemic limits. Consequently, heuristic agentic loops (e.g., ReAct) can exhibit failure modes in interactive environments, including excessive tool use under congestion, prolonged deliberation under time decay, and brittle behavior under ambiguous evidence. In this paper, we propose the Triadic Cognitive Architecture (TCA), a unified mathematical framework that grounds machine reasoning in continuous-time physics. By synthesizing nonlinear filtering theory, Riemannian routing geometry, and optimal control, we formally define the concept of认知摩擦Cognitive Friction。我们将智能体的审议过程映射到耦合随机控制问题，其中信息获取是路径依赖的且受到物理约束。 TCA 不依赖于任意启发式停止标记，而是使用 HJB 驱动的停止边界，并实例化具有网络效用停止条件的信念相关信息值的基于推出的近似。通过模拟紧急医疗诊断网格（EMDG）中的实证验证，我们证明，虽然贪婪基线在延迟和拥塞成本下过度考虑，但三元策略减少了采取行动的时间，同时提高了患者的生存能力，而不会降低这种环境下的诊断准确性。. We map the agent’s deliberation process to a coupled stochastic control problem where information acquisition is path-dependent and physically constrained. Rather than relying on arbitrary heuristic stop-tokens, the TCA uses an HJB-motivated stopping boundary and instantiates a rollout-based approximation of belief-dependent value-of-information with a net-utility halting condition. Through empirical validation in a simulated Emergency Medical Diagnostic Grid (EMDG), we demonstrate that while greedy baselines over-deliberate under latency and congestion costs, the triadic policy reduces time-to-action while improving patient viability without degrading diagnostic accuracy in this environment.

1 简介Introduction

对自主机器智能的追求在很大程度上集中在围绕自回归语言模型的迭代、提示驱动的推理循环上。 ReAct、Tree-of-Thoughts 和 AutoGPT 等框架已成功证明 LLM 可以模拟顺序规划The pursuit of autonomous machine intelligence has largely converged on iterative, prompt-driven reasoning loops wrapped around autoregressive language models. Frameworks such as ReAct, Tree-of-Thoughts, and AutoGPT have successfully demonstrated that LLMs can emulate sequential planningyao2023react ; yao2023tot ; autogpt2023 。然而，这些系统从根本上仍然脱离了现实世界执行的物理原理。他们在隐含的、灾难性的假设下运作. However, these systems remain fundamentally disembodied from the physics of real-world execution. They operate under the implicit, catastrophic assumption of a无摩擦的认知环境frictionless cognitive environment，其中收集信息被认为是瞬时的、结构自由的且一致可靠的。, where gathering information is presumed to be instantaneous, structurally free, and uniformly reliable.

当部署在静态文本生成基准之外并部署到动态、物理或高度网络化的环境中时，这种假设在数学上可能会导致系统故障模式。由于没有空间拓扑、时间节奏或认知分辨率的内在界限，不受约束的智能体表现出三种关键的故障模式：When deployed outside of static text-generation benchmarks and into dynamic, physical, or highly networked environments, this assumption mathematically can lead to systematic failure modes. Without intrinsic bounds on spatial topology, temporal pacing, or epistemic resolution, unconstrained agents exhibit three critical failure modes:

  1. 1.

    拓扑饱和（空间）：Topological Saturation (Space):由于代理无法对通过多代理群或分布式数据库路由信息的结构成本进行定价，不加区别的 API 查询会导致网络拥塞和每次查询负载成本上升。Indiscriminate API querying cascades into network congestion and rising per-query load costs, as agents fail to price the structural cost of routing information through a multi-agent swarm or distributed database.

  2. 2.

    无限的深思熟虑（时间）：Infinite Deliberation (Time):由于缺乏连续时间的节奏机制，智能体会陷入递归思维循环，无法认识到最优决策的效用会随着时间的流逝而呈指数衰减。Lacking a continuous-time pacing mechanism, agents get trapped in recursive thought loops, failing to recognize that the utility of an optimal decision decays exponentially as time elapses.

  3. 3.

    认知崩溃（真相）：Epistemic Collapse (Truth):当面对无法解决的、相互矛盾的证据时，代理人缺乏严格的数学框架来应对怀疑。他们没有揭露歧义，而是对相互矛盾的主张进行平均，并自信地幻想出一种混合综合。When faced with unresolvable, contradictory evidence, agents lack a rigorous mathematical framework for doubt. Instead of exposing the ambiguity, they average conflicting claims and confidently hallucinate a hybrid synthesis.

新颖性和贡献。Novelty and contributions.

现有的基于 LLM 的代理框架通常将审议视为无成本并依赖于启发式停止规则，而三元认知架构 (TCA) 与此不同，它通过明确定价时空摩擦和认知维护来形式化有界推理。 我们贡献：（i）理想化的连续时间随机控制公式，其中最佳停止以 HJB 自由边界为特征，以及（ii）可计算的离散实例，通过蒙特卡罗推出估计信念相关的信息值（VOI）并应用净效用停止规则。 根据经验，在 EMDG 中，TCA 在延迟和拥塞成本下表现出更早的停止和改进的可行性，同时保持诊断准确性，说明了从原则性停止到可实施代理控制器的实用路径。Unlike existing LLM-based agent frameworks that typically treat deliberation as cost-free and rely on heuristic stopping rules, the Triadic Cognitive Architecture (TCA) formalizes bounded reasoning by explicitly pricing spatio-temporal friction alongside epistemic maintenance. 为什么这是新的。Why this is new.

与之前基于 VOI 或有限理性的方法（通常将工具成本视为静态或外部成本）不同，TCA 联合建模 (i) 拓扑和拥塞相关的信息访问成本（空间）， (ii) 延迟（时间）下的时间相关机会成本，以及 (iii) 依赖于信念的信息价值（真相）， 在由最佳停止驱动的单一停止框架内。 关键贡献是将这三个术语耦合到一个统一的决策规则中，并为生成的停止边界提供可计算的基于转出的近似值。Unlike prior VOI-based or bounded-rationality approaches that typically treat tool costs as static or external, TCA jointly models (i) topology- and congestion-dependent information access costs (Space), (ii) time-dependent opportunity costs under delay (Time), and (iii) belief-dependent value-of-information (Truth), within a single stopping framework motivated by optimal stopping. The key contribution is coupling these three terms in a unified decision rule and providing a computable rollout-based approximation to the resulting stopping boundary.

5.4 结论Conclusion

三元认知架构提供了一个统一的视角：强大的自主性需要结构化空间、时间动态和认知真理维护的明确耦合。通过将深思熟虑视为受约束的物理轨迹，TCA 为使用工具的代理进行有限推理提供了原则框架。当我们评估 EMDG 中基于离散推出的实例时，底层公式自然地推广到异构环境，在这些环境中，信息获取会产生真正的时空成本。The Triadic Cognitive Architecture offers a unifying perspective: robust autonomy requires explicit coupling of structured space, temporal dynamics, and epistemic truth maintenance. By casting deliberation as a constrained physical trajectory, TCA provides a principled framework for bounded reasoning in tool-using agents. While we evaluate a discrete rollout-based instantiation in EMDG, the underlying formulation generalizes naturally to heterogeneous environments where information acquisition incurs real spatio-temporal cost.

三元认知架构 (TCA) 通过明确耦合认知确定性、时间动态和空间/拓扑约束，重新构建了人工智能中安全且可扩展的自主性。在此过程中，TCA 解决了现代代理人工智能中的一个关键差距：当前由法学硕士驱动的推理系统在“认知真空”中运行，忽略了信息获取和审议的现实成本。通过形式化认知摩擦并强制执行有原则的停止边界（由 HJB 最佳停止驱动，并在我们的实验中通过可计算的基于 rollout 的 VOI 进行实例化），TCA 将计算从无限的抽象转换为可测量、可优化的资源。这对多个领域都有影响：在医疗保健领域，它可以帮助自主诊断代理做出及时、可靠的决策，而无需过度深思熟虑；在机器人技术中，它支持在物理和时间限制下更安全地执行任务；在人工智能研究中，它提供了一个可复制的闭环模型，将随机控制理论与法学硕士驱动的认知联系起来。除了这些直接应用之外，TCA 还为下一代原 AGI 式系统奠定了基础，说明智能系统如何自主推理、行动和停止，同时降低认知崩溃、时间溢出和系统超载的风险。The Triadic Cognitive Architecture (TCA) reframes safe and scalable autonomy in AI by explicitly coupling epistemic certainty, temporal dynamics, and spatial/topological constraints. In doing so, TCA addresses a critical gap in modern agentic AI: current LLM-driven reasoning systems operate in a “cognitive vacuum,” ignoring the real-world costs of information acquisition and deliberation. By formalizing Cognitive Friction and enforcing a principled stopping boundary (motivated by HJB optimal stopping and instantiated via computable rollout-based VOI in our experiments), TCA transforms computation from an unlimited abstraction into a measurable, optimizable resource. This has implications across multiple domains: in healthcare, it can help autonomous diagnostic agents make timely, reliable decisions without over-deliberation; in robotics, it supports safer task execution under physical and temporal constraints; and in AI research, it provides a replicable, closed-loop model bridging stochastic control theory with LLM-driven cognition. Beyond these immediate applications, TCA offers a foundation for next-generation proto-AGI-style systems, illustrating how intelligent systems can reason, act, and halt autonomously while reducing the risk of epistemic collapse, temporal overrun, and systemic overloading.
