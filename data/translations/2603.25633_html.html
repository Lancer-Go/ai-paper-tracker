摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在数学教育中越来越多地使用，不仅作为问题解决者，而且作为学习者推理的评估者。然而，尚不清楚更强的数学问题解决能力是否与更强的阶梯评估表现相关。本研究使用 PROCESSBENCH 的 GSM8K 和 MATH 子集来检验这种关系，PROCESSBENCH 是一个人工注释的基准，用于识别数学推理中最早的错误步骤。我们在同一数学问题的两个独立任务中评估两个基于 LLM 的数学导师代理设置（使用 GPT-4 和 GPT-5 实例化）：解决原始问题并通过预测最早的错误步骤来评估基准提供的解决方案。结果显示出一致的模型内模式：同一模型正确解决的数学问题项目的评估准确性比错误解决的问题项目的评估准确性要高得多，并且模型和数据集之间具有统计上显着的关联。与此同时，评估仍然比直接解决问题更困难，尤其是在存在错误的解决方案上。这些发现表明，解决数学问题的专业知识支持更强的评估性能，但可靠的步骤级诊断还需要额外的功能，例如步骤跟踪、监控和精确的错误定位。研究结果对用于数学教育形成性评估的人工智能支持的自适应教学系统（AIS）的设计和评估具有影响。Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used in math education not only as problem solvers but also as assessors of learners’ reasoning. However, it remains unclear whether stronger math problem-solving ability is associated with stronger step-level assessment performance. This study examines that relationship using the GSM8K and MATH subsets of PROCESSBENCH, a human-annotated benchmark for identifying the earliest erroneous step in mathematical reasoning. We evaluate two LLM-based math tutor agent settings, instantiated with GPT-4 and GPT-5, in two independent tasks on the same math problems: solving the original problem and assessing a benchmark-provided solution by predicting the earliest erroneous step. Results show a consistent within-model pattern: assessment accuracy is substantially higher on math problem items the same model solved correctly than on items it solved incorrectly, with statistically significant associations across both models and datasets. At the same time, assessment remains more difficult than direct problem solving, especially on error-present solutions. These findings suggest that math problem-solving expertise supports stronger assessment performance, but reliable step-level diagnosis also requires additional capabilities such as step tracking, monitoring, and precise error localization. The results have implications for the design and evaluation of AI-supported Adaptive Instructional Systems (AISs) for formative assessment in math education.

1 简介Introduction

数学教育中越来越多地探索大型语言模型 (LLM)，以提供教学支持和评估Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly being explored in math education for both instructional support and assessment[11, 17]。最近的工作表明，法学硕士正在将自适应教学系统（AIS）扩展到基于规则的支持之外，转向更丰富的对话、生成性教学辅助和更灵活的评估工作流程. Recent work suggests that LLMs are extending Adaptive Instructional Systems (AISs) beyond rule-based support toward richer dialogue, generative instructional assistance, and more flexible assessment workflows[6, 18]。在数学相关的环境中，法学硕士已被研究用于基于对话的辅导. In math-related settings, LLMs have been studied for dialog-based tutoring[7], 提示生成, hint generation[12]、个性化反馈和错误诊断, personalized feedback and error diagnosis[13]，构建响应的自动评分, automated scoring of constructed responses[9], 校样分级, proof grading[19]，以及学生推理的步骤级验证, and step-level verification of student reasoning[5]。这些发展使法学硕士不仅成为数学问题的解决者，而且成为学生推理的潜在评估者，因此了解这两种能力之间的关系非常重要。. These developments position LLMs not only as math problem solvers, but also as potential assessors of students’ reasoning, making it important to understand how these two capabilities are related.

在人类数学教育中，评估学生的推理能力不仅取决于教学技能，还取决于对基本数学任务的充分理解以及解释学生思维所需的诊断专业知识In human math education, assessing students’ reasoning depends not only on pedagogical skill, but also on sufficient understanding of the underlying math task and the diagnostic expertise needed to interpret students’ thinking[14, 1]。我们用这种直觉作为类比，而不是直接等价，来激发法学硕士提出类似的问题：更强的数学问题解决能力是否支持更强的数学推理评估？利用 Nelson 和 Narens 对对象级认知和元级监控之间的区别，我们将问题解决视为对象级推理任务，而识别所提供的解决方案中最早的错误步骤是元级监控任务. We use this intuition as an analogy, rather than a direct equivalence, to motivate a similar question for LLMs: does stronger math problem-solving ability support stronger assessment of math reasoning? Drawing on Nelson and Narens’ distinction between object-level cognition and meta-level monitoring, we view problem solving as an object-level reasoning task, whereas identifying the earliest erroneous step in a provided solution is a meta-level monitoring task[10]。从这个角度来看，这两种能力应该相关但不相同，因为评估需要的不仅仅是解决问题，还包括跟踪中间步骤、检查一致性以及定位推理首先失败的位置。尽管之前的工作已经将法学硕士作为数学问题的解决者进行了考察. From this perspective, the two capabilities should be related but not identical, because assessment requires more than solving alone, including tracking intermediate steps, checking consistency, and localizing where reasoning first breaks down. Although prior work has examined LLMs both as math problem solvers[16, 15, 3]并作为评估工具对学生的反应进行评分、识别推理错误以及生成反馈或补救措施and as assessment tools for grading student responses, identifying reasoning errors, and generating feedback or remediation[2]，这两种能力很大程度上是分开研究的。, these two capabilities have largely been studied separately.

基于这种区别，我们研究了法学硕士解决数学问题的成功是否与其分级评估表现相关。这个问题对于步骤级评估尤其重要，因为识别多步骤解决方案中最早的错误不仅仅是结果判断，而且是结果判断。它需要理解原始问题并确定推理首先偏离有效路径的位置。在教育环境中，与仅结果评分相比，此类诊断可以支持更具可解释性和指导意义的反馈。使用 PROCESSBENCH 的 GSM8K 和 MATH 子集，PROCESSBENCH 是数学推理中最早错误识别的人工注释基准Building on this distinction, we examine whether an LLM’s math problem-solving success is associated with its step-level assessment performance. This question is especially important for step-level assessment, because identifying the earliest error in a multi-step solution is not merely an outcome judgment; it requires understanding the original problem and determining where the reasoning first departs from a valid path. In educational settings, such diagnosis can support more interpretable and instructionally useful feedback than outcome-only scoring. Using the GSM8K and MATH subsets of PROCESSBENCH, a human-annotated benchmark for earliest-error identification in math reasoning[20]，我们在针对相同基础问题定义的两个独立任务上评估相同的基于 LLM 的数学导师代理。在解题任务中，导师智能体回答原问题；在评估任务中，它识别基准提供的解决方案中最早的错误步骤。更广泛地说，这项研究将基于法学硕士的数学评估重新定义为两种相关能力之间的关系，并为人工智能支持的评估工具的设计提供信息，这些工具可以提供更具可解释性和教学上有用的反馈。, we evaluate the same LLM-based math tutor agent on two independent tasks defined over the same underlying problems. In the problem-solving task, the tutor agent answers the original problem; in the assessment task, it identifies the earliest erroneous step in a benchmark-provided solution. More broadly, this study reframes LLM-based math assessment as a relationship between two related capabilities and informs the design of AI-supported assessment tools that can provide more interpretable and pedagogically useful feedback.

2 方法Methods

数据集。Dataset.我们使用 PROCESSBENCH 的 GSM8K 和 MATH 子集We use the GSM8K and MATH subsets of PROCESSBENCH[20] (https://huggingface.co/datasets/Qwen/ProcessBench），一个人工注释的基准，用于识别数学推理中最早的错误步骤。每个项目都包含一个原始数学问题、一个基准分步解决方案跟踪以及一个金色标签，该标签使用基于 0 的索引指示最早的错误步骤，或者), a human-annotated benchmark for identifying the earliest erroneous step in math reasoning. Each item includes an original math problem, a benchmark step-by-step solution trace, and a gold label indicating the earliest incorrect step using 0-based indexing, or−1-1如果解决方案完全正确。为了创建平衡的评估设置，我们使用所有 400 个 GSM8K 项目和随机抽样的 400 个 MATH 项目子集，总共产生 800 个评估问题项目。查看所有处理过的数据和实验结果if the solution is fully correct. To create a balanced evaluation setting, we use all 400 GSM8K items and a randomly sampled 400-item subset of MATH, resulting in 800 evaluation problem items in total. See all processed data and experimental results athttps://github.com/LiangZhang2017/math-assessment-transfer.

模型和任务设置。Models and task setup.在这两项任务中，系统都通过共享系统提示被实例化为单个专家数学导师，该系统提示将模型构建为擅长解决数学问题和评估错误的逐步解决方案。我们使用 GPT-4 和 GPT-5 在两个独立任务（问题解决和评估）中的同一组项目上评估两种基于 LLM 的数学导师配置。在解决问题的任务中，模型仅接收原始问题，并被要求生成解决方案和最终答案。在评估任务中，模型接收相同的原始问题以及基准解决方案轨迹，并被要求识别最早的错误步骤。对于每个配置，两个任务都使用相同的模型部署。In both tasks, the system is instantiated as a single expert math tutor through a shared system prompt that frames the model as skilled in solving math problems and evaluating step-by-step solutions for errors. We evaluate two LLM-based math tutor configurations, using GPT-4 and GPT-5, on the same set of items in two independent tasks: problem solving and assessment. In the problem-solving task, the model receives only the original problem and is asked to generate a solution and final answer. In the assessment task, the model receives the same original problem together with the benchmark solution trace and is asked to identify the earliest erroneous step. For each configuration, the same model deployment is used across both tasks.

评估与分析。Evaluation and analysis.每个法学硕士模型下的两项任务都重复三次，并报告平均结果，以减少运行之间的变异性。解决问题的表现通过最终答案的准确性来衡量，评估表现则通过最早错误预测的精确匹配准确性来衡量；在 PROCESSBENCH 之后，我们还报告了存在错误和无错误项目的评估准确性及其调和平均值 (F1)。我们进一步计算解决-评估差距，即解决问题的准确性减去评估 F1。为了测试解决和评估之间的模型内关联，我们使用 2 来比较已解决的正确项目和已解决的不正确项目之间的评估准确性Both tasks under each LLM model were repeated three times, and the mean results were reported to reduce run-to-run variability. Problem-solving performance was measured by final-answer accuracy, and assessment performance by exact-match accuracy on earliest-error prediction; following PROCESSBENCH, we also report assessment accuracy for error-present and no-error items and their harmonic mean (F1). We further compute a solve–assess gap as problem-solving accuracy minus assessment F1. To test the within-model association between solving and assessing, we compare assessment accuracy between solved-correct and solved-incorrect items using 2×\times2 个列联表2 contingency tables withχ2\chi^{2}费舍尔精确检验，以及评估准确性差异和 95% 置信区间。最后，我们对代表性的一致性和不匹配案例进行了简短的定性分析，以检查常见的故障模式和两种功能之间的部分重叠。and Fisher’s exact tests, together with assessment-accuracy differences and 95% confidence intervals. Finally, we conduct a brief qualitative analysis of representative agreement and mismatch cases to examine common failure modes and partial overlap between the two capabilities.

3.1 整体任务表现Overall Task Performance

On the problem-solving task, both models perform very strongly on GSM8K, with GPT-4 achieving 94.9% accuracy and GPT-5 achieving 97.4%, whereas performance drops sharply on MATH, where GPT-4 reaches 29.8% and GPT-5 reaches 30.5%. 4 讨论Discussion

我们的结果提供了一致的证据，表明法学硕士数学导师的数学问题解决专业知识与更强的阶梯评估表现相关，尽管这种关系是有条件的而不是确定性的。在模型和数据集中，同一模型正确解决的项目的评估准确性始终高于其错误解决的项目。总体而言，基于 GPT-5 的数学导师在解决问题和分步评估任务方面也优于基于 GPT-4 的数学导师。这些发现直接支持了本研究的中心问题，并且与论文中介绍的框架一致：问题解决和步骤级评估依赖于重叠的能力，但评估还涉及监控提供的推理过程，而不是单独生成答案Our results provide converging evidence that mathematical problem-solving expertise in an LLM-based math tutor is associated with stronger step-level assessment performance, although the relationship is conditional rather than deterministic. Across both models and datasets, assessment accuracy was consistently higher on items the same model solved correctly than on items it solved incorrectly. At the aggregate level, the GPT-5-based math tutor also outperformed the GPT-4-based math tutor on both problem-solving and step-level assessment tasks. These findings directly support the central question of this study and are consistent with the framing introduced in the paper: problem solving and step-level assessment rely on overlapping capabilities, but assessment additionally involves monitoring a provided reasoning process rather than generating an answer alone[10]。同时，这种关系显然不是一对一的。成功解决问题会增加准确评估的可能性，但并不能保证准确评估。. At the same time, the relationship is clearly not one-to-one. Solving success increases the likelihood of accurate assessment, but does not guarantee it.

结果还表明，步骤级评估仍然比解决问题困难得多，特别是当基准解决方案实际上包含错误时。较大的解决-评估差距、较低的 F1 值以及在无错误项上的表现比有错误项上的表现要强得多，这表明核心挑战不仅仅是确定解决方案看起来是否可以接受，而是确定推理首先在哪里变得无效。这种区别在教育上很重要。在实践中，确认解决方案是否正确不如诊断推理失败的步骤有用，因为有针对性的反馈、补救和自适应脚手架依赖于精确的错误定位，而不仅仅是结果判断The results also show that step-level assessment remains substantially more difficult than problem solving, especially when the benchmark solution actually contains an error. The large solve–assess gaps, low F1 values, and much stronger performance on no-error than error-present items suggest that the core challenge is not simply deciding whether a solution looks acceptable, but identifying where the reasoning first becomes invalid. This distinction is educationally important. In practice, confirming that a solution is correct is less useful than diagnosing the step at which reasoning breaks down, because targeted feedback, remediation, and adaptive scaffolding depend on precise error localization rather than outcome judgment alone[20, 5, 9]。因此，我们的研究结果表明，基于法学硕士的数学评估的评估应优先考虑错误敏感的步骤级指标，而不是仅依赖于整体准确性或最终答案一致性。. Our findings therefore suggest that evaluations of LLM-based math assessment should prioritize error-sensitive, step-level metrics, rather than relying only on overall accuracy or final-answer agreement.

定性不匹配案例进一步阐明了为什么解决和评估应被视为相关但部分不同的能力。在某些情况下，模型正确地解决了原始问题，但仍然错误地识别了基准跟踪中最早的错误步骤，这表明生成有效的解决方案比以细粒度监控另一个解决方案更容易。在其他情况下，模型无法解决原始问题，但仍然正确判断基准解决方案，特别是当提供的跟踪是连贯或完全正确时。这些不对称性表明评估需要利用答案生成之外的其他功能，包括步骤对齐、局部一致性检查和精确的错误定位。更广泛地说，研究结果表明，更强的数学求解者并不自动成为可靠的评估者，并且除了更强大的生成之外，改进人工智能支持的数学导师可能还需要对批评、验证和过程监督的有针对性的支持The qualitative mismatch cases further clarify why solving and assessment should be viewed as related but partially distinct capabilities. In some cases, the model solved the original problem correctly but still misidentified the earliest erroneous step in the benchmark trace, suggesting that generating a valid solution can be easier than monitoring another solution at fine granularity. In other cases, the model failed to solve the original problem but still correctly judged the benchmark solution, especially when the provided trace was coherent or fully correct. These asymmetries indicate that assessment draws on additional capabilities beyond answer generation, including step alignment, local consistency checking, and precise error localization. More broadly, the findings imply that stronger math solvers are not automatically reliable assessors, and that improving AI-supported math tutors may require targeted support for critique, verification, and process supervision in addition to stronger generation[4, 8, 20].

这些结论有几个局限性。我们仅评估两个 LLM 部署和两个 PROCESSBENCH 子集，基准由精心策划的推理轨迹组成，而不是真实的学生作业。精确匹配最早错误指标也很严格，可能会低估部分正确的批评，这些批评识别正确的问题，但将其分配给附近的步骤。此外，由于解决和评估是在相同的项目上进行测量的，因此观察到的关联不应被解释为纯粹的因果转移效应，因为共享项目的难度可能会影响这两个结果。未来的工作应该测试更多的模型，使用真实的学生推理数据，纳入批评质量的分级衡量标准，并检查随着时间的推移整合解决、评估、反馈和修订的交互式辅导设置。Several limitations qualify these conclusions. We evaluate only two LLM deployments and two PROCESSBENCH subsets, and the benchmark consists of curated reasoning traces rather than authentic student work. The exact-match earliest-error metric is also strict and may undercount partially correct critiques that identify the correct issue but assign it to a nearby step. In addition, because solving and assessment were measured on the same items, the observed association should not be interpreted as a purely causal transfer effect, since shared item difficulty may influence both outcomes. Future work should test more models, use authentic student reasoning data, incorporate graded measures of critique quality, and examine interactive tutoring settings that integrate solving, assessment, feedback, and revision over time.

5 结论Conclusion

这项研究检验了法学硕士数学导师代理的数学问题解决专业知识是否与更强的阶梯评估表现相关。在 PROCESSBENCH 的 GSM8K 和 MATH 子集上使用 GPT-4 和 GPT-5，我们发现了一致的模型内模式：正确解决同一模型的项目的评估性能明显高于解决错误的项目的评估性能。这表明解决和评估依赖于重叠的推理能力。与此同时，巨大的解决-评估差距、存在错误的解决方案的低性能以及定性不匹配的情况表明两者并不等效。可靠的最早错误诊断需要的不仅仅是生成正确的最终答案；它还需要步骤跟踪、一致性监控和精确的错误定位。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，更强的数学问题解决能力支持但并不完全决定阶梯级评估表现。这些结果凸显了需要评估人工智能支持的数学导师，不仅作为问题解决者，而且作为推理过程评估者，可以提供更具可解释性和教学有用的反馈。This study examined whether math problem-solving expertise in LLM-based math tutor agents is associated with stronger step-level assessment performance. Using GPT-4 and GPT-5 on the GSM8K and MATH subsets of PROCESSBENCH, we found a consistent within-model pattern: assessment performance was substantially higher on items the same model solved correctly than on items it solved incorrectly. This suggests that solving and assessing rely on overlapping reasoning capabilities. At the same time, large solve–assess gaps, low performance on error-present solutions, and qualitative mismatch cases show that the two are not equivalent. Reliable earliest-error diagnosis requires more than generating a correct final answer; it also requires step tracking, consistency monitoring, and precise error localization. Overall, our findings show that stronger math problem-solving ability supports, but does not fully determine, step-level assessment performance. These results highlight the need to evaluate AI-supported math tutors not only as problem solvers, but also as reasoning-process assessors that can provide more interpretable and instructionally useful feedback.
