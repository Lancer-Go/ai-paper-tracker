摘要Abstract

人工智能 (AI) 在高等教育中的日益融合引发了关于学生报告人工智能辅助工作的透明度的重要问题。本研究通过应用认知-情感-意动（CAC）框架来调查大学生愿意公开人工智能使用的心理机制。采用了顺序解释性混合方法设计。在定量阶段，收集了 546 名大学生的调查数据，并使用结构方程模型进行分析，以检验认知感知、情感反应和披露意图之间的关系。在定性阶段，对 22 名学生进行了半结构化访谈，以进一步解释定量结果。结果表明，心理安全感显着提高了学生披露人工智能使用的意愿，并且受到感知公平、感知教师支持和感知组织支持的积极影响。相反，评估忧虑会降低披露意愿和心理安全感，并因感知到的耻辱、感知到的不确定性和隐私担忧而增强。定性研究结果进一步表明，制度明确性和支持性教学实践鼓励开放，而政策模糊性和对负面评价的恐惧往往导致学生采取谨慎或战略性的披露实践。总体而言，该研究强调了促进和抑制心理机制在塑造人工智能使用披露方面的双重作用，并强调了支持性制度环境和明确指导对于促进高等教育中负责任的人工智能透明度的重要性。The increasing integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in higher education has raised important questions regarding students’ transparency in reporting AI-assisted work. This study investigates the psychological mechanisms underlying university students’ willingness to disclose AI use by applying the Cognition–Affect–Conation (CAC) framework. A sequential explanatory mixed-methods design was employed. In the quantitative phase, survey data were collected from 546 university students and analysed using structural equation modelling to examine the relationships among cognitive perceptions, affective responses, and disclosure intention. In the qualitative phase, semi-structured interviews with 22 students were conducted to further interpret the quantitative findings. The results indicate that psychological safety significantly increases students’ willingness to disclose AI use and is positively shaped by perceived fairness, perceived teacher support, and perceived organisational support. Conversely, evaluation apprehension reduces disclosure intention and psychological safety, and is strengthened by perceived stigma, perceived uncertainty, and privacy concern. Qualitative findings further reveal that institutional clarity and supportive instructional practices encourage openness, whereas policy ambiguity and fear of negative evaluation often lead students to adopt cautious or strategic disclosure practices. Overall, the study highlights the dual role of enabling and inhibitory psychological mechanisms in shaping AI-use disclosure and underscores the importance of supportive institutional environments and clear guidance for promoting responsible AI transparency in higher education.

一、简介1. Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 技术越来越多地融入高等教育，改变了学生获取信息、生成内容和完成学术任务的方式。基于生成式人工智能和大型语言模型的工具使学生能够获得解释、起草书面材料并支持解决问题的活动，从而嵌入日常学习实践中（Kasneci 等人，2023 年；Lee 等人，2026 年）。虽然这些技术提供了提高学习效率和获取知识的机会，但它们也引起了与学术诚信、透明度和负责任使用相关的担忧（Doğan 等人，2025；Jensen 等人，2025）。Artificial intelligence (AI) technologies are increasingly integrated into higher education, transforming how students access information, generate content, and complete academic tasks. Tools based on generative AI and large language models enable students to obtain explanations, draft written material, and support problem-solving activities, thereby becoming embedded in everyday learning practices (Kasneci et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2026). While these technologies offer opportunities to enhance learning efficiency and access to knowledge, they also raise concerns related to academic integrity, transparency, and responsible use (Doğan et al., 2025; Jensen et al., 2025).

在这种背景下出现的一个新问题是学生在完成学术作业时愿意披露他们对人工智能工具的使用情况。信息披露，例如在作业中承认人工智能的帮助或向教师报告人工智能支持的工作，越来越被视为教育中道德人工智能使用的一个重要方面（Ans 等人，2026 年；Wiese 等人，2025 年）。然而，由于担心负面评价、政策模糊或社会判断，学生可能会犹豫是否披露人工智能的使用。现有的研究主要集中在人工智能的采用或人工智能生成内容的检测上，很少关注影响学生披露人工智能使用决定的心理因素（Yan et al., 2025）。One emerging issue in this context is students’ willingness to disclose their use of AI tools when completing academic work. Disclosure, such as acknowledging AI assistance in assignments or reporting AI-supported work to instructors, is increasingly viewed as an important aspect of ethical AI use in education (Ans et al., 2026; Wiese et al., 2025). However, students may hesitate to disclose AI use due to concerns about negative evaluation, policy ambiguity, or social judgement. Existing research has largely focused on AI adoption or detection of AI-generated content, with less attention given to the psychological factors that influence students’ decisions to disclose AI use (Yan et al., 2025).

为了解决这一差距，本研究探讨了大学生愿意通过认知-影响-意动（CAC）框架披露人工智能使用的促成和抑制机制。To address this gap, this study examines the enabling and inhibitory mechanisms underlying university students’ willingness to disclose AI use through the Cognition–Affect–Conation (CAC) framework.

这种观点将行为概念化为一个过程，在这个过程中认知评估塑造情绪反应，从而影响行为意图（Zhou＆Zhang，2024）。利用这个框架，该研究调查了制度观念，包括公平、教师支持、组织支持、耻辱、不确定性和隐私问题，如何影响心理安全和评估忧虑等情感状态，最终影响学生披露人工智能使用的意愿。This perspective conceptualises behaviour as a process in which cognitive evaluations shape emotional responses that subsequently influence behavioural intentions (Zhou & Zhang, 2024). Drawing on this framework, the study investigates how institutional perceptions, including fairness, teacher support, organisational support, stigma, uncertainty, and privacy concerns, influence affective states such as psychological safety and evaluation apprehension, which ultimately shape students’ willingness to disclose AI use.

2.文献综述2. Literature Review

2.1 人工智能在高等教育中的应用2.1 AI in Higher Education

人工智能 (AI) 技术在高等教育领域迅速扩展，重塑了教学、学习和评估实践（Qian，2025）。基于自然语言处理、机器学习和生成模型的工具越来越多地支持自动反馈、个性化学习路径和内容生成等活动（Lee et al., 2026）。 ChatGPT 等基于大型语言模型的系统的出现特别加速了人工智能与学术环境的融合，使学生能够获得解释、起草文本并协助解决问题（Li et al., 2025）。因此，人工智能正在嵌入跨学科的常规学术工作流程中（Kasneci 等人，2023）。Artificial intelligence (AI) technologies have rapidly expanded within higher education, reshaping teaching, learning, and assessment practices (Qian, 2025). Tools based on natural language processing, machine learning, and generative models increasingly support activities such as automated feedback, personalised learning pathways, and content generation (Lee et al., 2026). The emergence of large language model–based systems such as ChatGPT has particularly accelerated the integration of AI into academic contexts, allowing students to obtain explanations, draft texts, and assist with problem-solving tasks (Li et al., 2025). Consequently, AI is becoming embedded in routine academic workflows across disciplines (Kasneci et al., 2023).

学术研究探讨了与高等教育中采用人工智能相关的教学机会和挑战。一方面，人工智能工具可以提高学习效率、提供自适应支持并促进信息获取（Jensen et al., 2025）。另一方面，人们开始担心学术诚信、过度依赖自动化辅助以及人工智能介导的工作的透明度（Doğan 等人，2025）。因此，机构和教师越来越多地探索监管人工智能工具使用方式以及如何在学术作业中报告此类使用的政策（Francis et al., 2025）。Scholarly research has examined both the pedagogical opportunities and the challenges associated with AI adoption in higher education. On one hand, AI tools can enhance learning efficiency, provide adaptive support, and facilitate access to information (Jensen et al., 2025). On the other hand, concerns have emerged regarding academic integrity, overreliance on automated assistance, and the transparency of AI-mediated work (Doğan et al., 2025). Institutions and instructors are therefore increasingly exploring policies that regulate how AI tools may be used and how such use should be reported in academic assignments (Francis et al., 2025).

在这种不断发展的背景下，学生关于人工智能使用的行为已成为一个重要的研究焦点（Nzenwata 等人，2024）。虽然许多学生尝试使用人工智能工具来支持学习任务，但他们对合法性、风险和机构期望的看法差异很大（Kasneci 等人，2023）。这些看法可能会影响学生如何将人工智能融入学术工作以及他们是否公开承认这种用途（Çerkini 等人，2025）。理解这些动态不仅需要检查技术的采用，还需要检查学生做出披露决定的心理过程。Within this evolving context, student behaviour regarding AI use has become an important research focus (Nzenwata et al., 2024). While many students experiment with AI tools to support learning tasks, their perceptions of legitimacy, risk, and institutional expectations vary considerably (Kasneci et al., 2023). These perceptions could shape how students integrate AI into academic work and whether they openly acknowledge such use (Çerkini et al., 2025). Understanding these dynamics requires examining not only technological adoption but also the psychological processes underlying students’ decisions about disclosure.

2.2 披露人工智能使用的意愿2.2 Willingness to Disclose AI Use

披露人工智能使用的意愿是指学生在完成学术任务时自愿报告或承认自己使用人工智能工具的程度（Garcia Ramos，2025）。披露可能以不同的形式进行，例如在作业中引用人工智能的协助，向教师通报人工智能产生的贡献，或遵循要求人工智能辅助工作透明的机构政策（Ans et al., 2026）。在新兴人工智能技术的背景下，披露行为越来越被视为教育中负责任和道德的人工智能使用的关键指标（Wiese 等人，2025）。Willingness to disclose AI use refers to the extent to which students voluntarily report or acknowledge their use of AI tools when completing academic tasks (Garcia Ramos, 2025). Disclosure may occur in different forms, such as citing AI assistance in assignments, informing instructors about AI-generated contributions, or following institutional policies that require transparency in AI-assisted work (Ans et al., 2026). In the context of emerging AI technologies, disclosure behaviour is increasingly viewed as a key indicator of responsible and ethical AI use in education (Wiese et al., 2025).

现有文献表明，披露行为受到多种心理和情境因素的影响（Zhou & Wu，2025b）。学生可能会权衡透明度带来的好处与潜在风险，例如学业处罚或负面讲师评价（Stone，2025）。认知评估（例如，对政策清晰度或公平性的看法）、情感反应（例如，焦虑或信任）以及学术环境中的社会规范都可以影响学生披露人工智能使用的意图（Zhou & Wu，2025a）。这些因素反映了与学术环境中的道德报告和自我监管相关的更广泛的决策过程（Ans 等人，2026）。Existing literature suggests that disclosure behaviour is influenced by multiple psychological and contextual factors (Zhou & Wu, 2025b). Students may weigh perceived benefits of transparency against potential risks, such as academic penalties or negative instructor evaluations (Stone, 2025). Cognitive evaluations (e.g., perceptions of policy clarity or fairness), affective responses (e.g., anxiety or trust), and social norms within academic environments can all shape students’ intentions to disclose AI use (Zhou & Wu, 2025a). These factors reflect broader decision-making processes associated with ethical reporting and self-regulation in academic contexts (Ans et al., 2026).

尽管人们对人工智能在教育中的应用越来越感兴趣，但专门针对学生披露人工智能援助意愿的实证研究仍然有限。当前的许多文献都关注学术诚信违规或检测机制，而不是自愿披露背后的心理过程（Yan 等人，2025）。随着人工智能工具变得越来越普遍，检查影响披露决策的启用和抑制途径，特别是从集成的Despite growing interest in AI use in education, empirical research specifically addressing students’ willingness to disclose AI assistance remains limited. 三、理论框架3. Theoretical Framework

本研究利用认知-影响-意动（CAC）框架来解释大学生愿意在学术环境中披露他们对人工智能的使用。 CAC 框架将行为概念化为一个连续的心理过程，其中个体的认知评估影响他们的情感反应，进而塑造行为意图（Zhou & Wang，2025）。该框架已广泛应用于行为和教育研究，因为它捕获了个体如何解释情境线索、体验情绪反应并最终决定是否从事特定行为（Zhou & Zhu，2024）。在高等教育中使用人工智能的背景下，披露决策不太可能是纯粹理性或纯粹感性的；相反，它们是学生对学术环境的看法与他们对潜在评估或判断的情绪反应之间相互作用的结果。因此，CAC 框架为理解学生如何形成披露人工智能使用的意图提供了适当的理论视角。This study draws on the Cognition–Affect–Conation (CAC) framework to explain university students’ willingness to disclose their use of AI in academic contexts. The CAC framework conceptualises behaviour as a sequential psychological process in which individuals’ cognitive evaluations influence their affective responses, which subsequently shape behavioural intentions (Zhou & Wang, 2025). This framework has been widely used in behavioural and educational research because it captures how individuals interpret situational cues, experience emotional reactions, and ultimately decide whether to engage in a particular behaviour (Zhou & Zhang, 2024). In the context of AI use in higher education, disclosure decisions are unlikely to be purely rational or purely emotional; instead, they result from the interaction between students’ perceptions of their academic environment and their emotional responses to potential evaluation or judgement. The CAC framework therefore provides an appropriate theoretical lens for understanding how students form intentions to disclose AI use.

应用 CAC 框架使本研究能够检查影响披露行为的促进和抑制心理途径。具体来说，与学术环境相关的认知感知可能会产生情感反应，从而促进或阻碍人工智能使用的开放性。积极的看法可能会创造支持透明度的情绪条件，而消极的看法可能会引起对潜在后果或社会判断的担忧，从而阻止披露。基于这个视角，概念模型提出认知因素、感知公平、感知教师支持、感知组织支持、感知耻辱、感知不确定性和隐私关注塑造情感状态，即心理安全和评价忧虑，进而影响学生披露人工智能使用的意愿。图 1 给出了说明这些关系的概念模型。Applying the CAC framework allows this study to examine both enabling and inhibiting psychological pathways that influence disclosure behaviour. Specifically, cognitive perceptions related to the academic environment may generate affective responses that either facilitate or discourage openness about AI use. Positive perceptions may create emotional conditions that support transparency, whereas negative perceptions may evoke concerns about potential consequences or social judgement, thereby discouraging disclosure. Based on this perspective, the conceptual model proposes that cognitive factors, perceived fairness, perceived teacher support, perceived organisational support, perceived stigma, perceived uncertainty, and privacy concern, shape affective states, namely psychological safety and evaluation apprehension, which in turn influence students’ willingness to disclose AI use. 4. 研究问题和假设发展4. Research Questions and Hypothesis Development

4.1 开启愿意披露人工智能使用的途径4.1 Enabling Pathway of Willingness to Disclose AI Use

基于概念模型，第一个研究问题（RQ1）询问心理安全、感知公平、感知教师支持和感知组织支持如何影响大学生披露人工智能使用的意愿。心理安全是指认为个人可以表达自己的行为、想法或担忧，而不必担心惩罚、尴尬或不公平判断等负面后果（Edmondson & Bransby，2023）。先前在组织和教育背景下的研究表明，心理安全有助于开放沟通、信息共享和行为透明度（Vella 等人，2024）。在学习环境中，体验到较高心理安全感的学生更有可能参与讨论、提出问题并认可他们的学习实践（Somerville 等，2023）。在人工智能辅助学习的背景下，感到心理安全的学生可能更愿意在完成学术任务时披露他们对人工智能工具的使用（McGuire，2025）。Based on the conceptual model, the first research question (RQ1) asks how psychological safety, perceived fairness, perceived teacher support, and perceived organisational support influence university students’ willingness to disclose AI use. Psychological safety refers to the perception that individuals can express their behaviours, ideas, or concerns without fear of negative consequences such as punishment, embarrassment, or unfair judgement (Edmondson & Bransby, 2023). Prior research in organisational and educational contexts shows that psychological safety facilitates open communication, information sharing, and behavioural transparency (Vella et al., 2024). In learning environments, students who experience higher psychological safety are more likely to engage in discussions, ask questions, and acknowledge their learning practices (Somerville et al., 2023). In the context of AIassisted learning, students who feel psychologically safe may be more willing to disclose their use of AI tools when completing academic tasks (McGuire, 2025).

感知公平是影响学生心理安全的重要认知因素。感知公平是指学生对制度政策和评估实践是否公平和透明的评价（Lünich et al., 2024）。组织正义研究表明，公平观念显着影响个人的情绪反应和对机构的信任（Malhotra 等，2022）。当个人相信程序和决策是公平的时，他们在与当局互动时更有可能感到安全和自信（Chambers 等，2023）。在高等教育中，如果学生认为有关人工智能使用和学术评估的政策是公平的，他们可能不会那么担心因使用人工智能工具而受到不公平的评判，从而增强他们的心理安全（Stone，2025）。Perceived fairness is an important cognitive factor that may influence students’ psychological safety. Perceived fairness refers to students’ evaluations of whether institutional policies and assessment practices are equitable and transparent (Lünich et al., 2024). Research on organisational justice suggests that fairness perceptions significantly influence individuals’ emotional responses and trust in institutions (Malhotra et al., 2022). When individuals believe that procedures and decisions are fair, they are more likely to feel secure and confident when interacting with authorities (Chambers et al., 2023). In higher education, if students perceive policies regarding AI use and academic evaluation as fair, they may feel less concerned about being unfairly judged for using AI tools, thereby enhancing their psychological safety (Stone, 2025).

感知到的教师支持也可能有助于心理安全感的发展。感知到的教师支持反映了学生相信教师提供指导的程度，Perceived teacher support may also contribute to the development of psychological safety. Perceived teacher support reflects the extent to which students believe that instructors provide guidance,

学习过程中的鼓励和理解（Tao et al., 2022）。教育心理学的实证研究表明，支持性的师生关系可以增强学生的信任、参与度以及交流其学术经历的意愿（Held & Mori，2024）。当教师对新兴技术的使用表现出开放和建设性的态度时，学生可能会更轻松地讨论他们的学习实践，包括他们对人工智能工具的使用（Prananto 等人，2025）。encouragement, and understanding in the learning process (Tao et al., 2022). Empirical studies in educational psychology indicate that supportive instructor–student relationships can enhance students’ trust, engagement, and willingness to communicate about their academic experiences (Held & Mori, 2024). When instructors demonstrate openness and constructive attitudes toward the use of emerging technologies, students may feel more comfortable discussing their learning practices, including their use of AI tools (Prananto et al., 2025).

感知的组织支持是指学生相信大学重视他们的学习需求并提供足够的指导和资源（Orpina et al., 2022）。在组织行为研究中，感知到的组织支持已被证明可以增强个人对机构的信任并培养积极的情感体验（Anton，2025）。在高等教育背景下，提供有关人工智能使用的明确政策、指导和支持的大学可以减少歧义并创造更具支持性的学习环境（Jeilani & Abubakar，2025）。这种机构支持可以增强学生的信心，让他们相信自己的行为会得到理解和公平对待，从而增强他们的心理安全（Dalban et al., 2025）。据此，提出以下假设：Perceived organisational support refers to students’ beliefs that the university values their learning needs and provides adequate guidance and resources (Orpina et al., 2022). In organisational behaviour research, perceived organisational support has been shown to strengthen individuals’ trust in institutions and foster positive emotional experiences (Anton, 2025). In the context of higher education, universities that provide clear policies, guidance, and support regarding AI use may reduce ambiguity and create a more supportive learning environment (Jeilani & Abubakar, 2025). Such institutional support may enhance students’ confidence that their actions will be understood and treated fairly, thereby strengthening their psychological safety (Dalban et al., 2025). 4.2 愿意披露人工智能使用的抑制途径4.2 Inhibitory Pathway of Willingness to Disclose AI Use

基于概念模型，第二个研究问题（RQ2）考察了评估忧虑、感知耻辱、感知不确定性和隐私担忧如何影响大学生披露人工智能使用的意愿。评价忧虑是指当个人的行为受到他人评价时，个人对自己的行为受到负面评价的焦虑（Ismail & Heydarnejad，2023）。研究表明，评价忧虑会阻碍个人表达想法、承认错误或揭露可能具有社会敏感性的行为（McGaghie，2018）。在学术背景下，担心教师或同学负面评价的学生可能会避免披露可能影响其学术声誉的行为（Prasad et al., 2023）。就人工智能辅助学习而言，这种担忧可能会降低学生公开报告其使用人工智能工具的意愿。此外，先前的研究表明，评估忧虑可能会破坏个人的心理安全感，因为对判断或批评的担忧会降低人际安全感和开放感（Geerts et al., 2021）。当学生预期负面评价时，他们在交流自己的学习行为时可能会感到不那么安全，从而削弱了心理安全感。Based on the conceptual model, the second research question (RQ2) examines how evaluation apprehension, perceived stigma, perceived uncertainty, and privacy concern influence university students’ willingness to disclose AI use. Evaluation apprehension refers to individuals’ anxiety about being negatively judged when their behaviour is evaluated by others (Ismail & Heydarnejad, 2023). Research suggests that evaluation apprehension can discourage individuals from expressing ideas, admitting mistakes, or revealing behaviours that might be socially sensitive (McGaghie, 2018). In academic contexts, students who worry about negative judgement from instructors or peers may avoid disclosing behaviours that could affect their academic reputation (Prasad et al., 2023). In the case of AIassisted learning, such apprehension may reduce students’ willingness to openly report their use of AI tools. In addition, prior studies indicate that evaluation apprehension can undermine individuals’ sense of psychological safety because concerns about judgement or criticism reduce feelings of interpersonal security and openness (Geerts et al., 2021). When students anticipate negative evaluation, they may feel less safe communicating their learning behaviours, thereby weakening psychological safety.

感知到的耻辱可能会导致评价忧虑的发展。耻辱是指一种特定行为在社会上不被认可或与负面道德判断相关的看法（Troup et al., 2022）。在高等教育中，人工智能在学术工作中的使用有时可能被视为不适当或在学术上存在问题，特别是在机构政策尚不明确的情况下（Yan 等人，2025）。对社会和教育背景下的耻辱感的研究表明，当个人认为某种行为受到耻辱时，他们会对他人的评价变得更加敏感，并且可能会对自己如何被看待感到更加焦虑（Orta‐Aleman 等人，2026）。因此，如果学生认为使用人工智能工具在他们的学术环境中带有社会耻辱，他们在考虑是否披露这种使用时可能会经历更强烈的评估忧虑。Perceived stigma may contribute to the development of evaluation apprehension. Stigma refers to the perception that a particular behaviour is socially disapproved of or associated with negative moral judgement (Troup et al., 2022). In higher education, AI use in academic work may sometimes be viewed as inappropriate or academically questionable, especially when institutional policies remain unclear (Yan et al., 2025). Research on stigma in social and educational contexts shows that when individuals believe a behaviour is stigmatised, they become more sensitive to others’ evaluations and may experience greater anxiety about how they will be perceived (Orta‐Aleman et al., 2026). Consequently, if students perceive that using AI tools carries social stigma within their academic environment, they may experience stronger evaluation apprehension when considering whether to disclose such use.

感知到的不确定性也可能会增加评估忧虑。当个人缺乏关于规则、期望或其行为可能产生的后果的明确信息时，就会出现不确定性（Usher & Barak，2024）。在许多大学，有关人工智能使用的政策仍在不断发展，学生可能无法完全理解何时可以接受人工智能的使用或应如何报告（Garcia Ramos，2025）。先前对组织和教育环境中的不确定性的研究表明，模糊的政策会增加焦虑和谨慎行为，因为个人担心犯错误或违反期望（Zhu et al., 2025）。在这种情况下，学生可能会更加关心他们的行为将如何被评估，从而增加了评估忧虑（Stone，2025）。Perceived uncertainty may also increase evaluation apprehension. Uncertainty arises when individuals lack clear information about rules, expectations, or possible consequences of their actions (Usher & Barak, 2024). In many universities, policies regarding AI use are still evolving, and students may not fully understand when AI use is acceptable or how it should be reported (Garcia Ramos, 2025). Prior research on uncertainty in organisational and educational settings suggests that ambiguous policies can increase anxiety and cautious behaviour because individuals fear making mistakes or violating expectations (Zhu et al., 2025). Under such conditions, students may become more concerned about how their behaviour will be evaluated, thereby increasing evaluation apprehension (Stone, 2025).

隐私问题是可能影响评估忧虑的另一个因素。隐私担忧是指个人对个人信息暴露或滥用的担忧（Herriger et al., 2025）。对技术使用的研究表明，当个人与数字系统交互或披露个人信息时，隐私问题可能会产生焦虑和防御行为（Zheng et al., 2025）。在人工智能使用披露的背景下，学生可能担心报告他们的人工智能使用可能会让他们的学习过程受到教师和机构的更严格的审查或监控（Hu & Min，2023）。这种担忧可能会提高对评估的敏感性，并导致更强的评估忧虑（Carmody 等人，2021）。据此，提出以下假设：Privacy concern represents another factor that may influence evaluation apprehension. Privacy concern refers to individuals’ worries about the exposure or misuse of personal information (Herriger et al., 2025). Research on technology use indicates that privacy concerns can generate anxiety and defensive behaviour when individuals interact with digital systems or disclose personal information (Zheng et al., 2025). In the context of AI use disclosure, students may worry that reporting their AI use could expose their learning processes to closer scrutiny or monitoring by instructors and institutions (Hu & Min, 2023). Such concerns may heighten sensitivity to evaluation and contribute to stronger evaluation apprehension (Carmody et al., 2021). Accordingly, the following hypotheses are proposed:

H5：评估忧虑与披露人工智能使用的意愿呈负相关。 H6：评价忧虑与心理安全感负相关。H5: Evaluation apprehension is negatively associated with willingness to disclose AI use. H6: Evaluation apprehension is negatively associated with psychological safety.

5.1 研究设计5.1 Research Design

本研究采用序贯解释性混合方法设计，在定量数据收集和分析之后进行定性调查，以进一步解释定量结果（Cohen et al., 2018）。在第一阶段，进行了问卷调查，以检查所提出的学生愿意披露人工智能使用的概念模型中各个结构之间的关系，重点关注认知因素、情感反应和行为意图之间的相互作用。定量结果提供了学生感知的整体情况以及这些变量之间的假设关系。在第二阶段，我们对有意选择的调查参与者子集进行了半结构化访谈，以更深入地了解学生对学术环境中人工智能使用和披露的经验和观点。定性研究结果用于阐述定量结果并将其置于背景中，从而更全面地了解影响学生披露人工智能使用意愿的因素。This study adopted a sequential explanatory mixed-methods design, in which quantitative data collection and analysis were followed by qualitative inquiry to further interpret the quantitative findings (Cohen et al., 2018). In the first phase, a questionnaire survey was administered to examine the relationships among the constructs in the proposed conceptual model of students’ willingness to disclose AI use, focusing on the interplay between cognitive factors, affective responses, and behavioural intention. The quantitative results provided an overall picture of students’ perceptions and the hypothesised relationships among these variables. In the second phase, semi-structured interviews were conducted with a purposively selected subset of survey participants to gain deeper insights into students’ experiences and perspectives regarding AI use and disclosure in academic contexts. The qualitative findings were used to elaborate and contextualise the quantitative results, thereby providing a more comprehensive understanding of the factors influencing students’ willingness to disclose AI use.

进行定性访谈是为了加深对影响学生披露人工智能使用意愿的因素的理解，并将定量结果置于背景中。对访谈数据的专题分析确定了三个主要主题：(1) 制度清晰度和支持促进开放，(2) 耻辱和不确定性引起对评估的担忧，以及 (3) 有条件和战略性的披露实践。这些主题说明了学生如何解释他们的学术环境，以及这种解释如何影响他们是否披露人工智能工具的使用的决定。The qualitative interviews were conducted to deepen understanding of the factors influencing students’ willingness to disclose AI use and to contextualise the quantitative findings. These findings help explain why students’ willingness to disclose AI use varies across contexts and academic environments.

7. 讨论7. Discussion

7.1 开启愿意披露人工智能使用的途径7.1 Enabling Pathway of Willingness to Disclose AI Use

定量研究结果为认知-情感-意动框架中提出的促成途径提供了明确的支持，表明心理安全是塑造学生披露人工智能使用意愿的核心机制。结构模型表明，心理安全感显着预测披露意图，而感知公平、教师支持和组织支持均对心理安全感产生正向影响。这些结果表明，学生对制度实践的认知评估转化为塑造行为意图的情感状态，这与 CAC 理论一致（Zhou＆Zhang，2024）。当学生认为政策和评估实践是公平的时，他们不太可能预期惩罚性或有偏见的评估，这会增强他们的人际安全感和开放感（Chambers 等，2023；Malhotra 等，2022）。同样，支持性的教师行为和机构指导似乎起到了使人工智能辅助学习合法化的关系信号的作用，从而营造了一个让披露在心理上变得更安全的环境。这一发现与之前的研究结果一致，表明支持性的教育氛围可以提高学生交流学习实践和参与透明学术行为的意愿（Held & Mori，2024；McGuire，2025）。然而，这些影响的中等程度表明，仅靠机构支持可能无法完全使人工智能披露正常化，这表明学生对更广泛学术文化的看法仍然是一个重要的背景因素。The quantitative findings provide clear support for the enabling pathway proposed in the Cognition– Affect–Conation framework, demonstrating that psychological safety is a central mechanism shaping students’ willingness to disclose AI use. Structural modelling showed that psychological safety significantly predicted disclosure intention, while perceived fairness, teacher support, and organisational support all positively influenced psychological safety. These results indicate that students’ cognitive evaluations of institutional practices translate into affective states that shape behavioural intentions, consistent with CAC theory (Zhou & Zhang, 2024). When students perceive policies and assessment practices as fair, they are less likely to anticipate punitive or biased evaluation, which strengthens their sense of interpersonal security and openness (Chambers et al., 2023; Malhotra et al., 2022). Similarly, supportive instructor behaviour and institutional guidance appear to function as relational signals that legitimise AI-assisted learning, thereby fostering an environment in which disclosure becomes psychologically safer. This finding aligns with prior work showing that supportive educational climates increase students’ willingness to communicate learning practices and engage in transparent academic behaviour (Held & Mori, 2024; McGuire, 2025). Nevertheless, the moderate magnitude of these effects suggests that institutional support alone may not fully normalise AI disclosure, indicating that students’ perceptions of the broader academic culture remain an important contextual factor.

定性研究结果进一步阐明了这些有利条件在实践中如何运作，同时也揭示了重要的局限性。受访者一致将制度清晰度和支持性教师态度描述为增加他们承认人工智能使用的舒适度的因素，强化了公平和支持增强心理安全的定量证据。当教师明确解释可接受的人工智能实践或将人工智能指导纳入作业指导时，学生会将此类信号解释为机构对负责任的人工智能使用的认可，从而降低与披露相关的感知风险。这一观察结果与研究相一致，研究表明明确的制度指导和道德框架可以使教育领域的新兴技术正常化The qualitative findings further illuminate how these enabling conditions operate in practice while also revealing important limitations. Interview participants consistently described institutional clarity and supportive instructor attitudes as factors that increase their comfort in acknowledging AI use, reinforcing the quantitative evidence that fairness and support enhance psychological safety. When instructors explicitly explain acceptable AI practices or integrate AI guidance into assignment instructions, students interpret such signals as institutional endorsement of responsible AI use, reducing perceived risk associated with disclosure. This observation is consistent with research suggesting that clear institutional guidance and ethical framing can normalise emerging technologies in educational

设置（Francis 等人，2025；Jensen 等人，2025）。然而，访谈还表明，这些促成机制常常因课程和教师之间的不一致而受到破坏。学生们报告说，他们收到了关于人工智能的使用是否可以接受的相互矛盾的信息，这削弱了感知公平和支持的稳定作用。这表明心理安全可能高度依赖于环境，不仅来自支持性行为者，还来自机构政策的一致性。因此，虽然定量模型中确定的促成途径得到了经验支持，但定性证据表明，其有效性取决于关于人工智能使用的一致的机构沟通和教学协调。settings (Francis et al., 2025; Jensen et al., 2025). However, the interviews also reveal that these enabling mechanisms are often undermined by inconsistency across courses and instructors. Students reported receiving conflicting messages about whether AI use is acceptable, which weakens the stabilising effect of perceived fairness and support. This suggests that psychological safety may be highly context-dependent, emerging not only from supportive actors but also from the coherence of institutional policies. Consequently, while the enabling pathway identified in the quantitative model is empirically supported, the qualitative evidence indicates that its effectiveness depends on consistent institutional communication and pedagogical alignment regarding AI use.

7.2 愿意披露人工智能使用的抑制途径7.2 Inhibitory Pathway of Willingness to Disclose AI Use

定量结果也为CAC框架中提出的抑制途径提供了强有力的支持。评估忧虑显着降低了学生披露人工智能使用的意愿，同时破坏了心理安全，这表明对负面判断的担忧是透明度的关键情感障碍。这一发现与之前的研究一致，即评估忧虑会阻止个人揭露可能使他们面临批评或声誉风险的行为（Ismail & Heydarnejad，2023；McGaghie，2018）。在本研究中，对耻辱、不确定性和隐私问题的认知认知显着增加了评估忧虑，这表明学生对学术环境的解释可能会引发对他们的人工智能使用将如何被评判的预期焦虑。当人工智能辅助的工作被认为在社会上有问题或道德上模棱两可时，学生可能会避免披露，作为一种保护策略，以尽量减少潜在的学术后果。这一结果与新兴文献相一致，表明人工智能政策和规范的模糊性可能会造成心理紧张，阻碍人工智能使用的公开报告（Stone，2025；Yan 等人，2025）。此外，评价忧虑与心理安全感之间的负相关凸显了这种情感因素的双重作用：它不仅直接抑制披露意图，而且间接削弱透明行为所需的支持性情感条件。The quantitative results also provide strong support for the inhibitory pathway proposed in the CAC framework. Evaluation apprehension significantly reduced students’ willingness to disclose AI use and simultaneously undermined psychological safety, indicating that concerns about negative judgement function as a critical emotional barrier to transparency. This finding is consistent with prior research suggesting that evaluation apprehension discourages individuals from revealing behaviours that may expose them to criticism or reputational risk (Ismail & Heydarnejad, 2023; McGaghie, 2018). In the present study, cognitive perceptions of stigma, uncertainty, and privacy concern significantly increased evaluation apprehension, suggesting that students’ interpretation of the academic environment can activate anticipatory anxiety about how their AI use will be judged. When AI-assisted work is perceived as socially questionable or ethically ambiguous, students may avoid disclosure as a protective strategy to minimise potential academic consequences. This result aligns with emerging literature indicating that ambiguity surrounding AI policies and norms can create psychological tension that discourages open reporting of AI use (Stone, 2025; Yan et al., 2025). Moreover, the negative association between evaluation apprehension and psychological safety highlights the dual role of this affective factor: it not only directly suppresses disclosure intentions but also indirectly weakens the supportive emotional conditions required for transparent behaviour.

定性研究结果进一步阐明了这些抑制机制如何在学生的决策过程中体现。许多受访者表示，人工智能的使用在学术背景下带有隐性的耻辱，特别是当教师将人工智能的帮助等同于学术不诚实或智力努力减少时。这种看法加剧了学生的担忧，即披露可能会导致教师质疑其作业的原创性或质量，从而加剧对评估的担忧。与会者还强调了政策不确定性的作用，指出快速发展的制度指导方针使得很难确定人工智能的使用何时可以接受或应如何报告。研究中也发现了类似的担忧，这些研究表明，模糊的技术法规可能会增加感知风险并阻碍对新兴实践的公开交流（Usher & Barak，2024；Zhu et al.，2025）。与隐私相关的担忧进一步加剧了这些担忧，因为一些学生担心披露人工智能的使用可能会引发对其作业的更严格审查或将他们的学习过程暴露于机构监控之下（Carmody 等，2021；Hu & Min，2023）。重要的是，访谈表明，这些抑制压力往往会导致学生采取谨慎或战略性的披露做法，而不是完全不披露，这表明人工智能使用的透明度是由动态风险效益评估决定的，而不是纯粹的规范性决策。The qualitative findings further illuminate how these inhibitory mechanisms manifest in students’ decision-making processes. Many interview participants described AI use as carrying implicit stigma within academic contexts, particularly when instructors equate AI assistance with academic dishonesty or reduced intellectual effort. Such perceptions intensified students’ concerns that disclosure might lead instructors to question the originality or quality of their work, thereby reinforcing evaluation apprehension. Participants also emphasised the role of policy uncertainty, noting that rapidly evolving institutional guidelines make it difficult to determine when AI use is acceptable or how it should be reported. Similar concerns have been identified in studies showing that ambiguous technological regulations can heighten perceived risk and discourage open communication about emerging practices (Usher & Barak, 2024; Zhu et al., 2025). Privacy-related worries further compounded these concerns, as some students feared that disclosing AI use could trigger closer scrutiny of their assignments or expose their learning processes to institutional monitoring (Carmody et al., 2021; Hu & Min, 2023). Importantly, the interviews suggest that these inhibitory pressures often lead students to adopt cautious or strategic disclosure practices rather than complete non-disclosure, indicating that transparency about AI use is shaped by a dynamic risk–benefit assessment rather than a purely normative decision.

7.3 理论和实践意义7.3 Theoretical and Practical Implications

这项研究通过扩展认知-影响-意动（CAC）框架来解释学生披露人工智能在高等教育中使用的意愿，从而在理论上做出了贡献。虽然教育领域人工智能的先前研究主要考察了采用或道德问题，但对披露行为背后的心理机制的关注有限（Yan et al., 2025；Zhou & Wu, 2025b）。研究结果表明，披露意图是通过认知影响过程产生的，在这个过程中，制度观念塑造了情绪反应，从而影响了行为意图。特别是，结果强调了心理安全和评价忧虑的中介作用，强化了CAC视角在技术相关教育行为中的解释价值（Zhou＆Zhang，2024）。This study contributes theoretically by extending the Cognition–Affect–Conation (CAC) framework to explain students’ willingness to disclose AI use in higher education. While prior research on AI in education has mainly examined adoption or ethical concerns, limited attention has been given to the psychological mechanisms underlying disclosure behaviour (Yan et al., 2025; Zhou & Wu, 2025b). The findings demonstrate that disclosure intentions emerge through a cognition–affect process in which institutional perceptions shape emotional responses that subsequently influence behavioural intentions. In particular, the results highlight the mediating role of psychological safety and evaluation apprehension, reinforcing the explanatory value of the CAC perspective in technology-related educational behaviour (Zhou & Zhang, 2024).

实际上，研究结果表明，大学和教师在提高人工智能使用透明度方面发挥着核心作用。明确和一致的制度政策对于减少歧义并加强学生对公平和安全的看法是必要的（Francis 等人，2025 年；Stone，2025 年）。此外，支持性的讲师沟通和对可接受的人工智能实践的明确指导可能会减少评估问题并使披露正常化。将人工智能素养和道德指导纳入课程可能会进一步帮助减少与人工智能辅助学习相关的耻辱，并鼓励负责任的披露实践（Wiese 等人，2025）。Practically, the findings suggest that universities and instructors play a central role in fostering transparency about AI use. Clear and consistent institutional policies are necessary to reduce ambiguity and strengthen students’ perceptions of fairness and security (Francis et al., 2025; Stone, 2025). In addition, supportive instructor communication and explicit guidance on acceptable AI practices may reduce evaluation concerns and normalise disclosure. Integrating AI literacy and ethical guidance into curricula may further help reduce stigma associated with AI-assisted learning and encourage responsible disclosure practices (Wiese et al., 2025).

7.4 局限性和未来方向7.4 Limitations and Future Directions

这项研究有一些局限性，在解释研究结果时应考虑这些局限性。首先，数据是使用便利抽样策略从中国大学生中收集的，这可能会限制结果在其他教育背景或文化环境中的普适性（Y. Du et al., 2024；Y. Du, 2025；Y. Du, Yuan, et al., 2026）。对于人工智能使用的制度规范和态度可能因国家和大学而异，可能会影响学生的看法和披露行为。其次，虽然该研究采用了混合方法设计，但定量数据是横截面的，这限制了在结构之间建立因果关系的能力（Y. Du等，2025；Zhang等，2026）。最后，这些措施依赖于自我报告的看法和意图，这可能会受到社会期望或受访者对人工智能在学术任务中使用的主观解释的影响（Y. Du & He，2026a，2026b；He & Du，2024）。This study has several limitations that should be considered when interpreting the findings. First, the data were collected from university students in China using a convenience sampling strategy, which may limit the generalisability of the results to other educational contexts or cultural settings (Y. Du et al., 2024; Y. Du, 2025; Y. Du, Yuan, et al., 2026). Institutional norms and attitudes toward AI use may vary across countries and universities, potentially influencing students’ perceptions and disclosure behaviour. Second, although the study employed a mixed-methods design, the quantitative data were cross-sectional, which restricts the ability to establish causal relationships among the constructs (Y. Du et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2026). Finally, the measures relied on self-reported perceptions and intentions, which may be influenced by social desirability or respondents’ subjective interpretations of AI use in academic tasks (Y. Du & He, 2026a, 2026b; He & Du, 2024).

未来的研究可以通过多种方式解决这些限制。不同国家或机构背景的比较研究将有助于研究文化和政策环境如何影响学生披露人工智能使用的意愿（Y. Du, 2026；Y. Du, Li, et al., 2026；Wang et al., 2026）。纵向或实验设计还可以提供关于认知感知、情感反应和披露行为之间的因果机制的更有力证据（C. Du et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024; Zou et al., 2023, 2024）。此外，未来的研究可能会探索具体的教学实践，例如人工智能集成评估设计或明确的披露指南，如何塑造学生对人工智能辅助工作的透明度。此类研究可以更深入地了解教育机构如何有效促进学术环境中负责任和开放的人工智能使用。Future research could address these limitations in several ways. Comparative studies across different countries or institutional contexts would help examine how cultural and policy environments influence students’ willingness to disclose AI use (Y. Du, 2026; Y. Du, Li, et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026). Longitudinal or experimental designs could also provide stronger evidence regarding the causal mechanisms linking cognitive perceptions, affective responses, and disclosure behaviour (C. Du et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024; Zou et al., 2023, 2024). In addition, future studies may explore how specific pedagogical practices, such as AI-integrated assessment design or explicit disclosure guidelines, shape students’ transparency regarding AI-assisted work. Such research could provide deeper insights into how educational institutions can effectively promote responsible and open AI use in academic environments.

八、结论8. Conclusion

这项研究通过识别促进性和抑制性心理途径，调查了大学生通过认知-影响-意动框架披露人工智能使用的意愿。研究结果表明，心理安全在鼓励披露方面发挥着核心作用，受到学生对公平、教师支持和组织支持的看法的影响。与此同时，由于与人工智能使用相关的耻辱感、不确定性和隐私问题，评估忧虑成为了一个关键障碍。总之，结果表明，学生的披露决定受到支持性制度条件和负面评价感知风险之间相互作用的影响。通过整合定量和定性证据，这项研究对高等教育中人工智能使用透明度背后的心理机制提供了更全面的理解，并强调了明确政策、支持性教学实践和减少耻辱感在促进负责任地披露人工智能辅助工作方面的重要性。This study examined university students’ willingness to disclose AI use through the Cognition–Affect– Conation framework by identifying both enabling and inhibitory psychological pathways. The findings show that psychological safety plays a central role in encouraging disclosure, shaped by students’ perceptions of fairness, teacher support, and organisational support. At the same time, evaluation apprehension acts as a key barrier, emerging from perceived stigma, uncertainty, and privacy concerns associated with AI use. Together, the results suggest that students’ disclosure decisions are influenced by the interaction between supportive institutional conditions and perceived risks of negative evaluation. By integrating quantitative and qualitative evidence, this study provides a more comprehensive understanding of the psychological mechanisms underlying AI-use transparency in higher education and highlights the importance of clear policies, supportive teaching practices, and reduced stigma in promoting responsible disclosure of AI-assisted work.
