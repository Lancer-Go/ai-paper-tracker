摘要Abstract

遥感深度学习的最新进展在很大程度上依赖于大型注释数据集，但获取几何、辐射和多领域任务的高质量地面实况仍然成本高昂且往往不可行。特别是，缺乏准确的深度注释、受控的照明变化和多尺度配对图像限制了单目深度估计、域适应和航空场景超分辨率的进展。我们呈现Recent advances in deep learning for remote sensing rely heavily on large annotated datasets, yet acquiring high-quality ground truth for geometric, radiometric, and multi-domain tasks remains costly and often infeasible. In particular, the lack of accurate depth annotations, controlled illumination variations, and multi-scale paired imagery limits progress in monocular depth estimation, domain adaptation, and super-resolution for aerial scenes. 1 简介Introduction

遥感领域的深度学习突破已经通过大型注释数据集进行了深入研究，但为不同任务创建高质量的地面事实仍然是一个主要瓶颈Deep learning breakthroughs in remote sensing have been highly researched by large annotated datasets, still creating high-quality ground truth for diverse tasks remains a major bottleneck[30, 39]。几何深度估计、跨域适应和超分辨率等任务都需要精确的标签，而在现实世界的航空图像中大规模获取这些标签成本高昂或不可行. Tasks such as geometric depth estimation, cross-domain adaptation, and super-resolution all require precise labels that are costly or infeasible to obtain at scale in real-world aerial imagery[30, 2]。例如，无人机 (UAV) 图像中的单目深度估计需要密集的 3D 地面实况。最近的研究强调，现实世界的无人机数据集很少能提供准确的像素级深度，因此研究人员依赖仅提供相对深度图的合成数据或自监督方法. For example, monocular depth estimation in unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) imagery requires dense 3D ground truth. Recent studies emphasize that very few real-world UAV datasets provide accurate pixel-level depth, hence why researchers rely on synthetic data or self-supervised methods that provide only relative depth maps[38, 13]。综合模拟平台已用于生成具有多模态地面实况的航空数据集（例如，Mid-Air 和 TartanAir 数据集），但这仍然留下了显着的模拟与真实域差距有待解决. Synthetic simulation platforms have been used to generate aerial datasets (e.g. the Mid-Air and TartanAir datasets) with multi-modal ground truth, but this still leaves a significant sim-to-real domain gap to be addressed[28]。即使可以从模拟中轻松获得 RGB 图像和姿势或语义标签，捕获航空场景的高保真深度图也很麻烦，并且仅在少数特殊情况下实现. Even when RGB images and pose or semantic labels can be readily obtained from simulations, capturing high-fidelity depth maps for aerial scenes is cumbersome and has only been achieved in a few special cases[45, 25].

计算机视觉中的深度学习受益于跨越多个任务的大规模注释数据集，特别是在地面图像中。相比之下，遥感数据集的范围通常较窄，通常只处理单一任务，缺乏大规模的多模式监督。在本节中，我们回顾了地面和遥感两个领域的重要数据集，并强调了我们提出的 SyMTRS 数据集旨在填补的空白。Deep learning in computer vision has benefited from large-scale annotated datasets spanning multiple tasks, particularly in ground-level imagery. A first result is that both models preserve the global scene geometry well including road layout, building footprints, lane markings, and bright window patterns remain spatially aligned with the input views. The visual comparison also highlights a difference between paired and unpaired training. In the day-to-night direction, pix2pix generally produces outputs that are closer to the target night domain, with darker global illumination, more localized light sources, and better preservation of scene-specific contrast transitions. CycleGAN, by contrast, often applies a more global dark surface and tends to brighten some surfaces excessively, especially roads and rooftops. The night-to-day direction appears more challenging for both methods, due to nighttime containing less visible information in dark regions. In the examples shown, CycleGAN often recovers daytime structure more clearly, producing a more readable road network and facade layout, albeit sometimes with a cooler tone than the ground truth. Pix2pix, despite the advantage of paired supervision, occasionally yields underexposed outputs with limited recovery in shadowed areas. This asymmetry suggests that translating from information-poor nighttime observations back to daytime appearance remains a difficult inverse problem even in a controlled synthetic setting.

因此，SyMTRS 可以支持配对和不配对翻译模式，同时保持场景内容足够一致，以进行有意义的视觉比较。此外，他们表明数据集具有足够的挑战性，足以暴露特定于模型的故障模式：CycleGAN 可能会为了风格一致性而牺牲特定于样本的保真度，而 pix2pix 可以更好地利用配对对齐，但当源图像缺乏太多纹理信息时，可能仍然会遇到困难。Therefore, SyMTRS can support both paired and unpaired translation modes while keeping the scene content consistent enough for meaningful visual comparison. Additionally, they indicate that the dataset is challenging enough to expose model-specific failure modes: CycleGAN may sacrifice sample-specific fidelity for style consistency, whereas pix2pix can better exploit paired alignment but may still struggle when the source image lacks too much texture information.

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了In this paper, we introduced系统MTRSSyMTRS，一个合成的多任务遥感数据集，旨在通过完美对齐的高分辨率图像、多尺度低分辨率对应数据以及在统一模拟管道中生成的配对跨域样本，实现航空图像超分辨率和昼/夜图像转换的受控研究。报告的基准测试表明 SyMTRS 既可靠又具有足够的挑战性：超分辨率性能从, a synthetic multi-task remote sensing dataset designed to enable controlled research in aerial image super-resolution and day/night image translation through perfectly aligned high-resolution imagery, multi-scale low-resolution counterparts, and paired cross-domain samples generated within a unified simulation pipeline. The reported benchmarks show that SyMTRS is both reliable and sufficiently challenging: super-resolution performance degrades consistently from×2\times 2 to ×8\times 8，证实了高倍重建的难度不断增加，而比较的基线表现出明显且稳定的差异，自动编码器实现了最强的基于失真的性能，而对抗性翻译模型则暴露了感知真实性和像素保真度之间的预期权衡。图像到图像的转换结果表明，该数据集保留了跨域的场景几何形状，并且可以支持配对和不配对的适应研究。, confirming the increasing difficulty of high-magnification reconstruction, while the compared baselines exhibit clear and stable differences, with the autoencoder achieving the strongest distortion-based performance and adversarial translation models exposing the expected trade-offs between perceptual realism and pixel fidelity. The image-to-image translation results demonstrate that the dataset preserves scene geometry across domains and can support both paired and unpaired adaptation studies.
