1. 简介Introduction

顺序推荐（SR）旨在通过对历史行为的时间动态进行建模来预测用户的下一次交互Sequential recommendation (SR) aims to predict the next interaction of a user by modeling the temporal dynamics of historical behaviors(Kang and McAuley, 2018; Sun et al., 2019; Du et al., 2023)。然而，有几个因素使准确的预测变得复杂：用户序列可能跨越几个月，而购买决策通常是由决定其兼容性的项目的内在语义驱动的。此外，用户与大规模项目集中相对较少的项目进行交互，并且记录的序列经常由于意外点击而偏离真实的用户偏好，自然导致严重的数据稀疏和噪声. However, several factors complicate accurate prediction: user sequences may span multiple months, and purchase decisions are often driven by the intrinsic semantics of items that determine their compatibility. Furthermore, users interact with relatively few items from the large-scale item set, and the recorded sequences often diverge from true user preferences due to accidental clicks, naturally resulting in severe data sparsity and noise(Xie et al., 2022; Zhou et al., 2024)。在这种稀疏和嘈杂的环境中，纯粹的统计信号（例如共同购买频率）变得不可靠，因为它们经常将偶然的共现与真正的用户兴趣混为一谈。为了在这些条件下对用户意图进行稳健建模，利用对统计噪声不变的信号至关重要：. In such sparse and noisy environments, purely statistical signals (e.g., co-purchase frequency) become unreliable, as they frequently conflate accidental co-occurrences with genuine user interests. 6. 结论Conclusion

这项工作提出了 CAST，一个用于互补感知顺序推荐的框架，可对细粒度语义级别转换进行建模。我们的研究结果表明，在离散语义空间中显式建模动态依赖关系可以有效地揭示潜在的功能兼容性，而这在传统的基于 ID 的方法中常常被掩盖。通过对潜在语义依赖性进行建模，CAST 可以区分真正的互补模式和虚假相关性。尽管取得了这些进步，但仍然存在两个限制。首先，虽然功能互补假设适合电子商务，但它在主题相似性驱动的内容消费领域可能不太有效。其次，我们的语义代码仍然是潜在的，缺乏与显式属性（例如规范）的直接映射以实现可解释性。未来的工作将探索 (1)This work presents CAST, a framework for complementary-aware sequential recommendation that models fine-grained semantic-level transitions. Our findings demonstrate that explicitly modeling dynamic dependencies in a discrete semantic space effectively uncovers latent functional compatibility, which is often obscured in traditional ID-based approaches. By modeling latent semantic dependencies, CAST can distinguish true complementary patterns from spurious correlations. Despite these advancements, two limitations remain. First, while the functional complementarity assumption fits e-commerce, it may be less effective in content-consumption domains driven by topical similarity. Second, our semantic codes remain latent, lacking direct mapping to explicit attributes (e.g., specifications) for explainability.
