1 简介Introduction

训练后是预训练语言模型变得有用的阶段 针对一组特定的面向人类的行为。有监督微调（SFT） 教模型模仿演示，强化学习（RL） 根据奖励和蒸馏转移优化采样模型输出 从教师的行为模式到学生的行为模式。这些方法可不小 实现细节：它们确定模型是否遵循指令， 解决推理问题，拒绝不安全的请求，保留事实知识， 并且在训练数据的狭窄分布之外仍然保持稳健。Post-training is the stage at which a pretrained language model becomes useful for a particular set of human-facing behaviors. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) teaches the model to imitate demonstrations, reinforcement learning (RL) optimizes sampled model outputs against rewards, and distillation transfers behavior from a teacher model to a student. These methods are not minor implementation details: they determine whether a model follows instructions, solves reasoning problems, refuses unsafe requests, preserves factual knowledge, and remains robust outside the narrow distribution of its training data.

尽管具有实际重要性，但一些基本的培训后现象仍然存在 用通常的词汇来解释很尴尬。 SFT 简单且数据高效， 然而，在攻击性的情况下，它可能会导致灾难性的遗忘或脆弱的行为 专业化。强化学习通常通过稀疏或嘈杂的奖励进行优化，但它可以 产生令人惊讶的稳定改进。蒸馏通常被理解为 模仿老师，但学生有时会匹配或超越老师。这些 观察结果提出了一个共同的问题：训练过程的属性是什么 确定训练后的模型是局部改进还是破坏性漂移？Despite this practical importance, several basic post-training phenomena remain awkward to explain with the usual vocabulary. SFT is simple and data-efficient, yet it can cause catastrophic forgetting or brittle behavior under aggressive specialization. RL is often optimized with sparse or noisy rewards, yet it can produce surprisingly stable improvements. Distillation is usually understood as copying a teacher, yet students sometimes match or outperform teachers. These observations raise a shared question: what property of the training process determines whether a post-trained model improves locally or drifts destructively?

3.1 预测Predictions

该框架产生了一些定性预测。The framework yields several qualitative predictions.

P1：政策外压力会在压力下产生遗忘。P1: Off-policy pressure can create forgetting under stress.

The prediction is conditional: forgetting should appear when off-policy pressure becomes strong enough or misaligned enough.

P2：同策略方法应该保留局部性。P2: On-policy methods should preserve locality.

RL 和 OPD 通常应该比受压力的离策略更好地保留能力 训练，因为它们应用了学习者引起的状态的更新。这不 保证每个指标下的标量漂移较低，但它预测更新是 更有可能与模型实际可以达到和修复的状态相关。RL and OPD should often retain capabilities better than stressed off-policy training because they apply updates on learner-induced states. 基础部署和训练后部署之间的分布距离应该是有用的， 但它不能完全表征训练动态。两种方法可以产生 类似的测量漂移，但接受监督的州不同 这些状态在当地的可恢复程度如何。因此漂移应该被解释为 以及状态源和信号密度。Distribution distance between base and post-trained rollouts should be useful, but it cannot fully characterize training dynamics. 6 讨论Discussion

客观层面的分析是不完整的。Objective-level analysis is incomplete.

压力老师的压力SFT和OPD的对比很难区分 仅使用监督来源进行解释。两者最终都是由 同样退化的教师/数据行为，但 OPD 对各州实施监督 从学生身上取样。这改变了学习问题。The contrast between stress SFT and OPD from the stress teacher is difficult to explain using only the source of supervision. Both are ultimately shaped by the same degraded teacher/data behavior, but OPD applies supervision on states sampled from the student. This changes the learning problem.

按策略密集整形。On-policy dense shaping.

成功的 OPD 变体使用教师延续而不是一步 逻辑匹配。一步 OPD 在我们的运行中严重崩溃，达到 GSM8K 0.040。 基于连续性的 OPD 恢复了目标性能。这提出了一个实用的 秘诀：将策略采样与密集的轨迹级局部相结合 监督。The successful OPD variant used teacher continuations rather than one-step logit matching. One-step OPD collapsed badly in our runs, reaching GSM8K 0.040. Continuation-based OPD recovered target performance. This suggests a practical recipe: combine on-policy sampling with dense, trajectory-level local supervision.

局限性。Limitations.

这项研究是小规模的：一个基础模型、一个目标数据集、LoRA 适配器、 有限的保留任务和轻量级漂移估计器。 RL 训练器是 最小的政策 GRPO 式实施，而不是全面的 verl PPO 或 GRPO 设置。我们的漂移度量使用词汇特征而不是隐藏状态或 编码器嵌入。因此，结果应被视为证据 机制假设，不作为基准主张。This study is small-scale: one base model, one target dataset, LoRA adapters, limited retention tasks, and lightweight drift estimators. The RL trainer is a minimal on-policy GRPO-style implementation rather than a full-scale verl PPO or GRPO setup. Our drift metric uses lexical features rather than hidden-state or encoder embeddings. The results should therefore be read as evidence for a mechanistic hypothesis, not as a benchmark claim.

This explains why a student need not inherit all failures of a degraded teacher, especially when teacher supervision is queried on student states rather than copied from teacher trajectories.

灾难性的遗忘和保留。Catastrophic forgetting and retention.

灾难性遗忘在持续学习中被研究为损失 新任务训练时以前的能力Catastrophic forgetting has been studied in continual learning as the loss of previous capabilities when training on new tasks[16, 6, 13, 15]。 在法学硕士培训后，遗忘通常被衡量为广泛的退化 专业化后的保留任务。我们的实验遵循这个经验 传统方法是在 GSM8K 训练后测量 TruthfulQA 和 MMLU。的 结果表明，遗忘不仅仅是更新幅度或 数据集大小：激进的离策略 SFT 可能会损害保留，而同策略 RL OPD 可以在相当的目标任务压力下保留更多能力。. In LLM post-training, forgetting is often measured as degradation on broad retention tasks after specialization. Our experiments follow this empirical tradition by measuring TruthfulQA and MMLU after GSM8K post-training. The results suggest that forgetting is not only a matter of update magnitude or dataset size: aggressive off-policy SFT can damage retention, while on-policy RL and OPD can preserve more capability under comparable target-task pressure.

状态漂移测量。State drift measurement.

分布偏移通常使用嵌入距离、分类器来量化 两样本检验，MMDDistribution shift is often quantified using embedding distances, classifier two-sample tests, MMD[8]，或 Wasserstein 风格的指标, or Wasserstein-style metrics[18, 1]。我们使用 rollout-state MMD 作为状态漂移的紧凑代理，同时还跟踪其他词汇 分布统计。我们的结果显示了这种方法的价值和局限性 标量测量：来自压力老师的压力 SFT 和 OPD 几乎 相同的 MMD 漂移，但保留能力却截然不同。这会激励更多的富人 状态分布分析不仅考虑模型推出的进展情况， 但哪些培训国家接受监督以及它们是否是当地的 学习者可达。. We use rollout-state MMD as a compact proxy for state drift, while also tracking other lexical distribution statistics. Our results show both the value and limits of such scalar measures: stress SFT and OPD from the stress teacher have nearly identical MMD drift, but very different retention. This motivates richer state-distribution analyses that consider not only how far model rollouts move, but which training states receive supervision and whether they are locally reachable by the learner.

8 结论Conclusion

我们提出了训练后的状态分布观点。从这个角度来看，SFT 是 Off-policy状态拟合，RL是on-policy奖励引导改进，OPD是 教师指导的政策学习。我们的实验表明 OPD 可以超越 退化的 SFT 教师，并且策略 RL 几乎没有改善目标任务 保留损失。证据完善了最初的论点：培训后并不 仅关于代币目标或标量分布漂移；这是关于在哪里 监督应用于模型的状态空间。We proposed a state-distribution view of post-training. In this view, SFT is off-policy state fitting, RL is on-policy reward-guided improvement, and OPD is teacher-guided on-policy learning. Our experiments show that OPD can surpass a degraded SFT teacher and that on-policy RL improves a target task with little retention loss. The evidence refines the original thesis: post-training is not only about token objectives or scalar distribution drift; it is about where supervision is applied in the model’s state space.
