高铁市场动态定价的关系多智能体强化学习Relational Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Pricing in High-Speed Railway Markets

Enrique Adrian Villarrubia-Martin enrique.villarrubia@uclm.es David Muñoz-Valero david.munoz@uclm.es Luis Rodriguez-Benitez luis.rodriguez@uclm.es Giovanni Montana g.montana@warwick.ac.uk Luis Jimenez-Linares luis.jimenez@uclm.es
摘要Abstract

在自由化的铁路系统中，运营商必须在具有部分可观察性的环境中动态设定价格，因为他们保留有关其目标和绩效的私人信息，而监管限制禁止竞争对手之间的沟通或直接信息交换，以防止明显的共谋。因此，智能体必须学会仅根据可观察的市场数据推断战略交互，这对多智能体强化学习提出了重大挑战，其中标准方法通常将观察结果视为非结构化向量，忽略了控制战略交互的潜在市场拓扑。为了解决这个问题，提出了一种实体图建模方法，它将环境表示为操作单元图，而不是决策代理或静态基础设施，编码实体之间的竞争、协调和连接关系。然后，使用基于图的表示学习的多智能体孪生延迟深度确定性策略梯度算法的扩展通过多层关系图卷积网络处理实体的特征，并通过学习的注意力机制聚合它们。铁路定价强化学习环境中的实验结果表明，与关系和非关系基线的代表性选择相比，这种新颖的框架在市场复杂性不断增加的两种不同环境中实现了更高的收入和稳定性。该代码可在以下位置公开获取：In liberalised railway systems, operators must set prices dynamically in an environment with partial observability, as they retain private information about their objectives and performance, where regulatory constraints prohibit communication or direct information exchange between competitors to prevent explicit collusion. Consequently, agents must learn to infer strategic interactions only from observable market data which presents a significant challenge for multi-agent reinforcement learning, where standard approaches typically treat observations as unstructured vectors, ignoring the underlying market topology that governs strategic interactions. To address this, an entity graph modelling approach is proposed, which represents the environment as a graph of operational units, rather than decision-making agents or static infrastructure, encoding competition, coordination, and connectivity relations between entities. Then, an extension of the multi-agent twin delayed deep deterministic policy gradient algorithm with graph-based representation learning processes the features of the entities through a multi-layer relational graph convolutional network and aggregates them via a learnt attention mechanism. Experimental results in a rail pricing reinforcement learning environment show that this novel framework achieves higher revenue and stability in two different settings of increasing market complexity compared to a representative selection of relational and non-relational baselines. 1 简介Introduction

多智能体强化学习 (MARL) 社区越来越多地采用基于图的方法来对复杂环境进行建模The Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) community has increasingly adopted graph-based approaches to model complex environments[21, 14]。图是一种常见且非常强大的实践，可以自然有效地捕获对于多智能体交互和推理至关重要的结构关系. Graphs are a common and very powerful practice to naturally and efficiently capture the structural relations that are fundamental to multi-agent interaction and reasoning[46]。环境的特点是高度动态，代理不断移动，它们的邻里关系迅速发展，这就需要能够捕获抽象关系表示的架构。图神经网络（GNN）被认为是理想的解决方案，正是因为它们在图结构上本地运行，允许学习过程自动适应不断变化的环境拓扑. The environments are characterised by a highly dynamic nature, where agents are constantly moving and their neighbourhood relations evolve rapidly, which creates a need for architectures capable of capturing abstract relational representations. Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) are framed as the ideal solution, precisely because they operate natively on graph structures, allowing the learning process to automatically adapt to the changing topology of the environment[6]。当代理修改其连接时，GNN 保持通过消息传递操作对交互进行建模的能力，这将关系可变性从限制转变为优势，而无需调整模型的架构。. As agents modify their connections, GNNs maintain the ability to model interactions through message passing operations, which transforms relational variability from a limitation into an advantage, without the need to adapt the architecture of the model.

专注于铁路系统（选择进行实验的应用领域），基于图形的表示已成为复杂交通网络建模的标准方法。基于车站的图，其中顶点编码物理车站，边表示直接铁路连接，仍然是最近铁路优化文献中的主要范例Focusing on railway systems, the field of application selected for experimentation, graph-based representations have emerged as the standard approach for modelling complex transportation networks. Station-based graphs, where vertices encode physical stations and edges denote direct rail connections, remain the predominant paradigm in recent railway optimisation literature[30]。该方法广泛用于网络拓扑分析、漏洞评估和容量规划. This approach is widely used for network topology analysis, vulnerability assessment, and capacity planning[26]。然而，这些将车站编码为节点的模型通常不适用于捕捉运营方面，例如自由化高速客运市场中的服务差异化、多运营商动态以及市场层面的互动. However, these models, where stations are encoded as nodes, are typically not designed to capture operational aspects, such as service differentiation, multi-operator dynamics, and market-level interactions in liberalised high-speed passenger markets[15]。为了弥补这一差距，这项工作引入了一种基于实体图的建模方法，其中操作实体构成顶点，竞争、协调和连接关系被编码为异构边。通过将这些关系编码在图结构中，GNN 层在网络中传播消息，而注意力机制可以有选择地聚合每个代理最相关的特征。这会产生可学习的关系表示，可用于支持多代理环境中的战略决策。. To bridge this gap, this work introduces an entity graph-based modelling approach, where operational entities constitute the vertices, and competitive, coordination, and connectivity relations are encoded as heterogeneous edges. With these relations encoded in the graph structure, GNN layers propagate messages across the network while attention mechanisms can selectively aggregate the most relevant features for each agent. This produces learnable relational representations that can be used to support strategic decision-making in multi-agent environments.

Then, it enriches them by allowing entities to exchange contextual information with their neighbours through the network of relations. These relational representations are subsequently integrated into an actor-critic architecture augmented with graphs that operates under the Centralised Training with Decentralised Execution (CTDE)[11, 32]范式。当构建和完善基于图的嵌入时，该架构允许代理在训练期间访问全局信息，同时维护在运行时保护隐私的去中心化策略。所提出的方法扩展了多智能体孪生延迟深度确定性策略梯度（MATD3）paradigm. In contrast to single-agent settings, MARL environments are inherently non-stationary, as each agent’s policy evolves during training, thus altering the environment for other agents. Under CTDE, agents share global information during training to stabilise learning, while retaining independent policies at execution time. 动态定价，也称为定价情报，涉及根据供需情况实时调整价格，以实现利润最大化并与市场状况保持一致。在此背景下，对于电动汽车充电，Dynamic pricing, also known as pricing intelligence, involves adjusting prices in real time based on supply and demand to maximise profits and align with market conditions. Similarly, for hydrogen fuel cell vehicle refuelling,[12]采用多代理系统 (MAS) 来协调加油时间表并确定价格，从而提高微电网的需求满意度和交通流量。转向其他领域，例如电信领域，employed a Multi-Agent System (MAS) to coordinate refuelling schedules and determine prices, improving demand satisfaction and traffic flow in microgrids. Shifting to other domains, in telecommunications,[43]将移动虚拟网络运营商和用户之间的交互构建为 Stackelberg 游戏，并使用多代理深度 Q 网络 (MADQN) 来解决它。此外，在智能电网中，framed the interaction between mobile virtual network operators and users as a Stackelberg game, solving it using a Multi-Agent Deep Q-Network (MADQN). Moreover, in smart grids,[33]提出了一种分布式多智能体优化方法，用于解决供需失衡，同时加强隐私和自治。proposed a distributed multi-agent optimisation approach for resolving supply-demand imbalances while strengthening privacy and autonomy.

尽管取得了这些进步，高速铁路的动态定价引入了复杂的关系依赖关系，例如多运营商服务的动态合作和重叠的市场竞争。此外，该领域的部分可观察性和代理之间通信的禁止要求代理只能从可观察的市场数据中推断战略交互。这激发了基于图的表示的使用，其中交互的实体及其关系被自然地编码为图结构中的节点和边。Despite these advancements, dynamic pricing in high-speed railways introduces complex relational dependencies, such as cooperative dynamics with multi-operator services and overlapping market competition. Furthermore, the partial observability and the prohibition of communication between agents in this domain demand that agents infer strategic interactions only from observable market data. This motivates the use of graph-based representations, where interacting entities and their relationships are naturally encoded as nodes and edges in a graph structure.

By modelling the environment as a graph, where vertices denote decision-making entities and edges encode their relationships, agents are able to reason explicitly about their interdependencies. 5.6.2 消息传递深度如何影响代理性能？How does message passing depth affect agent performance?

为了评估网络深度对代理性能的影响，进行了一项消融研究，比较了一层、两层和三层 R-GCN 层的变体。 GNN 的深度决定了如何聚合每个服务的信息：单层 GNN 仅聚合来自相邻服务的信息，而两层或三层 GNN 分别将其扩展到两跳或三跳邻域。To evaluate the impact of network depth on agent performance, an ablation study was conducted comparing variants with one, two, and three R-GCN layers. The depth of the GNN determines how information is aggregated for each service: a single-layer GNN aggregates information only from adjacent services, whereas a two-layer or three-layer GNN expands it to two-hop or three-hop neighbourhoods, respectively.

图Figure10说明了每个深度设置训练期间的平均总收入。两层配置在两种情况下都实现了最佳性能，其次是一层和三层变体。这可以归因于这样一个事实：它通过一次换乘即可捕获直达和转机服务，而单层变体仅捕获直达服务，因为如果总票价较低，对价格敏感的乘客愿意接受更长的旅程或换乘。此外，三层变体表现出最差的性能，这可能表明对顶点特征的平滑作用，使它们更难以区分并且对决策的意义较小illustrates the average total revenue during training for each depth setting. In addition, the three-layer variant exhibits the worst performance, which can suggest a smoothing effect on the features of vertices, making them harder to distinguish and less meaningful for decision-making[25].

5.6.3 基于注意力的池化与统一聚合相比如何？How does attention-based pooling compare to uniform aggregation?

To assess the contribution of the learnt attention mechanism to agent performance, the framework with learnt attention weights was compared against a variant employing uniform attention weights. In the uniform attention variant, all services receive an equal weight during the aggregation process, resulting in the computation of a mean service embedding, rather than a weighted combination.

6 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and future work

这项工作引入了一个实体关系演员-评论家框架，用于具有混合竞争-合作设置的部分可观察的 MARL。它将环境编码为具有异构边缘的操作单元图，而不是决策代理或静态基础设施，通过竞争、协调和连接关系连接。每个智能体通过多层 R-GCN 学习关系状态表示，然后通过学习的注意力机制将其聚合为智能体级观察，并通过扩展 MATD3 在 CTDE 范式下训练其策略。该框架已在市场复杂性不断增加的两种不同场景下的铁路定价强化学习环境中进行了评估，与考虑的其他 MARL 基准相比，该框架获得了更高的总收入。此外，与 MATD3 相比，它通过更小的价格增量和更高的乘客比例实现了这一目标。This work introduces an entity relational actor-critic framework for partially observable MARL with mixed competitive-cooperative settings. It encodes the environment as graph of operational units with heterogeneous edges, rather than decision-making agents or static infrastructure, connected by competition, coordination, and connectivity relations. Each agent learns a relational state representation through a multi-layer R-GCN, which is then aggregated into an agent-level observation via a learnt attention mechanism, and trains its policy under the CTDE paradigm by extending MATD3. The framework has been evaluated in a railway pricing reinforcement learning environment in two different scenarios of increasing market complexity, where it obtained a higher total revenue compared to the other MARL baselines considered. Moreover, it achieved this with smaller price increments and a higher percentage of passengers travelling compared to MATD3.

该方法的一个潜在好处是相对于市场结构的可扩展性，这是由于紧凑的固定大小代理嵌入和消息传递范式使模型大小独立于服务数量。这与基于代理的图基线 GA-AC 形成鲜明对比，其中每个算子都被编码到单个节点中，并且其有效性在更大的场景中崩溃。根据进一步的研究，竞争和连接关系对性能的贡献最大，而最接近基于代理的建模的协调关系的影响最小。此外，就消息传递的深度而言，两层配置实现了最高的收入，因为它通过一次传输捕获直接服务和连接服务。此外，注意力机制提高了训练早期阶段的样本效率，并且将表示模块与参与者梯度分离已被证明可以稳定个体代理的训练动态，尽管它会减少总收入。One potential benefit of the approach is the scalability with respect to the market structure, due to the compact fixed-size agent embeddings and the message passing paradigm that keep the model size independent of the number of services. This contrasts with the agent-based graph baseline GA-AC, in which each operator is encoded into a single node and whose effectiveness collapses in the larger scenario. According to the further studies, the competition and connectivity relations contribute the most to performance, while the coordination relation, which is the closest to the agent-based modelling, has the smallest impact. In addition, regarding the depth of the message passing, the two-layer configuration achieves the highest revenue, since it captures direct and connecting services with one transfer. Furthermore, the attention mechanism improves sample efficiency in the early stages of training, and detaching the representation module from the actor gradients has been shown to stabilise the individual agent training dynamics, even though it can reduce the total revenue.

作为未来的工作，建议探索代理可以直接发现哪些实体相互连接的方法，而不是依赖于预定义的边类型。例如，这可以通过使用注意力机制来实现，该机制根据观察到的状态转换和奖励动态学习关系结构。该框架还将受益于对图结构本身内的时间方面进行建模。目前，实体图是根据静态规则在每个时间步重建的，但许多现实世界的系统呈现出随时间演变的关系。为此，时间图网络As future work, it is suggested to explore methods where agents could directly discover which entities are connected to each other, rather than relying on predefined edge types. For instance, this could be achieved by using attention mechanisms that dynamically learn a relational structure based on observed state transitions and rewards. The framework would also benefit from modelling the temporal aspect within the graph structure itself. Currently, the entity graph is reconstructed at each time step based on static rules, but many real-world systems present relations that evolve over time. To this end, temporal graph networks[41]可以将其纳入模型中以捕获随着环境演变而发生的关系变化。最后，本工作仅考虑离策略方法，因此未来的方向可以将框架扩展到在策略算法，以评估它们在这种情况下的潜在优势。could be incorporated into the model to capture relational changes as the environment evolves. Finally, only off-policy methods are considered in this work, so future directions could extend the framework to on-policy algorithms in order to assess their potential advantages in this setting.
