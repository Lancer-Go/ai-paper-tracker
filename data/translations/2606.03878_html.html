1 简介Introduction

增量测量询问广告是否导致了原本不会发生的结果。这个问题对于广告排名、广告客户报告、广告活动优化、预算分配和产品实验至关重要。干净的统计解决方案是一个随机实验，具有明确的治疗、控制、结果和随访窗口。然而，在实践中，广告衡量越来越受到隐私和身份限制的影响。用户可以选择退出跟踪，设备标识符可能丢失或不稳定，可能仅汇总报告转化，可能会抑制小小区，并且隐私保护系统可能会在报告转化计数之前添加随机报告噪音。结果是实验旨在识别的因果估计值与分析人员可用的降级信号之间存在差距。Incrementality measurement asks whether advertising caused outcomes that would not otherwise have occurred. This question is central to ads ranking, advertiser reporting, campaign optimization, budget allocation, and product experimentation. The clean statistical solution is a randomized experiment with well-defined treatment, control, outcomes, and follow-up windows. In practice, however, advertising measurement is increasingly shaped by privacy and identity constraints. Users may opt out of tracking, device identifiers may be missing or unstable, conversions may be reported only in aggregate, small cells may be suppressed, and privacy-preserving systems may add randomized reporting noise before reporting conversion counts. The result is a gap between the causal estimand that the experiment was designed to identify and the degraded signal available to the analyst.

本文研究了这一差距。我们将隐私受限的广告测量制定为信号丢失情况下的鲁棒因果决策问题。起点是具有治疗指标、结果标签和协变量的随机增量实验。我们假设测量系统通过 6 个信号丢失层发布了该实验的降级版本：(i) 匹配率丢失降低了可报告的结果，(ii) 可链接性丢失破坏了事件之间的身份连续性，(iii) 报告窗口之外的归因窗口丢失审查器转换，(iv) 聚合阈值抑制消除了稀疏的报告单元，(v) 随机报告噪声扰乱了发布的聚合，以及 (vi) 分段异构信号丢失使这些降级因客户或活动而异组。这些层对应于现代报告系统中的常见原语，包括事件级归因报告、摘要报告、聚合服务、归因窗口、贡献预算和可互操作的私有归因协议This paper studies that gap. We formulate privacy-constrained advertising measurement as a robust causal decision problem under signal loss. The starting point is a randomized incrementality experiment with a treatment indicator, outcome labels, and covariates. We assume the measurement system releases a degraded version of this experiment through 6 signal-loss layers: (i) match-rate loss drops reportable outcomes, (ii) linkability loss breaks identity continuity across events, (iii) attribution-window loss censors conversions outside reporting windows, (iv) aggregation-threshold suppression removes sparse reporting cells, (v) randomized reporting noise perturbs released aggregates, and (vi) segment-heterogeneous signal loss makes these degradations vary across customer or campaign groups. These layers correspond to common primitives in modern reporting systems, including event-level attribution reports, summary reports, aggregation services, attribution windows, contribution budgets, and interoperable private-attribution protocols(Google Privacy Sandbox, 2025a, b; Case et al., 2023; Aksu et al., 2024; Tholoniat et al., 2024)。图. The analyst observes the degraded report and must decide whether the evidence is strong enough to certify positive incrementality.

主要区别在于估计提升和验证决策之间。当高价值转化更有可能未被观察到、低容量细分被抑制或随机报告噪声主导信号时，点估计可能是乐观的。因此，可部署的测量系统应该说明在合理的信号丢失机制下仍然合理的内容。我们提出了一个框架，将观察到的隐私退化实验映射为三组。第一个包含增量价值下限超过业务阈值的活动或细分。第二个包含上限低于阈值的情况。第三个包含未解决的案例，其中可用信号无法支持更强有力的主张。The key distinction is between estimating lift and certifying a decision. A point estimate can be optimistic when high-value conversions are more likely to be unobserved, when low-volume segments are suppressed, or when randomized reporting noise dominates the signal. A deployable measurement system should therefore state what remains justified under plausible signal-loss mechanisms. We propose a framework that maps an observed privacy-degraded experiment into three sets. The first contains campaigns or segments whose lower-bound incremental value exceeds the business threshold. The second contains cases whose upper bound is below the threshold. The third contains unresolved cases where the available signal cannot support a stronger claim.

本质上，本文的动机是广告测量实践中的四个观察结果。首先，随机增量数据仍然是因果主张最强有力的经验锚，而缺失测量仍然是一个单独的障碍。其次，隐私退化改变了可识别的因果主张，因此它属于决策规则的范围。第三，公共数据集很少同时包括隐私前的身份图和隐私后的报告，因此实证工作必须诚实地对待实际观察到的内容。第四，商业决策需要保守的认证，而不仅仅是排名或归因。In essence, the paper is motivated by four observations from advertising measurement practice. First, randomized incrementality data remain the strongest empirical anchor for causal claims, while missing measurement remains a separate obstacle. Second, privacy degradation changes which causal claims are identifiable, so it belongs inside the decision rule. Third, public datasets rarely include both the pre-privacy identity graph and post-privacy reports, so empirical work must be honest about what is actually observed. And fourth, business decisions require conservative certification, beyond ranking or attribution alone.

总体而言，研究贡献有五倍。Overall, the research contributions are fivefold.第一First，我们定义了一个用于隐私受限广告测量的信号丢失模型，涵盖匹配率丢失、可链接性丢失、归因窗口丢失、聚合阈值抑制、随机报告噪声和分段异构信号丢失。, we define a signal-loss model for privacy-constrained ads measurement that covers match-rate loss, linkability loss, attribution-window loss, aggregation-threshold suppression, randomized reporting noise, and segment-heterogeneous signal loss.第二Second，我们引入了隐私降级报告的兼容性光纤视图，并将尖锐的部分识别带专门用于转换和支出方面的隐私受限增量决策。, we introduce a compatibility-fiber view of privacy-degraded reports and specialize sharp partial-identification bands to privacy-constrained incrementality decisions over conversions and spend.第三Third，我们表明，未解决的区域是信息论的，因为如果业务阈值位于锐带内，则观察相同降级报告的一致有效程序无法证明或拒绝该声明。, we show that the unresolved region is information-theoretic in the sense that if the business threshold lies inside the sharp band, no uniformly valid procedure observing the same degraded report can certify or reject the claim.第四Fourth，我们证明了样本复杂度和最小最大下限结果，将采样误差、报告噪声误差和不可约信号丢失宽度分开，并将有限样本认证作为直接同时覆盖决策规则处理。并且, we prove sample-complexity and minimax lower-bound results that separate sampling error, reporting-noise error, and irreducible signal-loss width, with finite-sample certification handled as the direct simultaneous-coverage decision rule. And第五fifth，我们定义了报告粒度诊断，该诊断在随机报告噪声降低与分段异质信号丢失之间进行权衡，并使分段级未解决的索赔可见。, we define reporting-granularity diagnostics that trade off randomized reporting-noise reduction against segment-heterogeneous signal loss and make segment-level unresolved claims visible.

8 讨论Discussion

所提出的框架将隐私保护广告测量视为不完全观察下的因果决策问题。这种框架不同于纯粹的归因和纯粹的隐私协议设计。归因询问如何在接触点之间分配功劳。隐私协议询问如何在保护个人的同时报告测量结果。隐私稳健的增量询问在测量信号降级后什么因果关系仍然是合理的。The proposed framework treats privacy-preserving ads measurement as a causal decision problem under incomplete observation. This framing differs from both pure attribution and pure privacy-protocol design. Attribution asks how to allocate credit among touchpoints. Privacy protocols ask how to report measurements while protecting individuals. Privacy-robust incrementality asks what causal claim remains justified after the measurement signal has been degraded.

这种区别对于平台决策很重要。隐私保护报告在技术上可能有效，但决策不足。随机实验在设计上可能是因果有效的，而可链接性损失、归因窗口损失或随机报告噪声仍然会妨碍细粒度的提升认证。该框架通过输出未解决的声明而不是过度自信的估计来明确这种不足。This distinction matters for platform decisions. A privacy-preserving report can be technically valid yet decision-insufficient. A randomized experiment can be causally valid in design while linkability loss, attribution-window loss, or randomized reporting noise still prevent fine-grained lift certification. The framework makes this insufficiency explicit by outputting unresolved claims instead of overconfident estimates.

10 结论Conclusion

隐私限制现在已成为广告衡量统计环境的一部分。随机化仍然至关重要，当存在匹配率丢失、可链接性丢失、归因窗口丢失、聚合阈值抑制、随机报告噪声和分段异构信号丢失时，隐私降级报告需要额外的认证层。本文提出了一种隐私稳健的增量框架，可将随机营销证据转换为经过认证、被拒绝和未解决的决策主张。实证结果强化了理论信息。一旦包含有限样本不确定性和随机报告噪声，积极的清洁提升和有利的人口下限仍然可能达不到部署认证。贡献是现代广告系统的基于前沿的认证层。它保留了随机实验的强度，同时在隐私退化数据不足以做出因果决策时做出明确说明。Privacy constraints are now part of the statistical environment for advertising measurement. Randomization remains essential, and privacy-degraded reports require an additional certification layer when match-rate loss, linkability loss, attribution-window loss, aggregation-threshold suppression, randomized reporting noise, and segment-heterogeneous signal loss are present. This paper proposes a privacy-robust incrementality framework that converts randomized marketing evidence into certified, rejected, and unresolved decision claims. The empirical results reinforce the theoretical message. Positive clean lift and favorable population lower bounds can still fall short of deployment certification once finite-sample uncertainty and randomized reporting noise are included. The contribution is a frontier-based certification layer for modern ads systems. It preserves the strength of randomized experiments while making explicit when privacy-degraded data are insufficient for a causal decision.
