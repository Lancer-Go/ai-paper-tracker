Zilin Xiao    Qi Ma    Jason Chen    Xintao Chen    Avinash Atreya    Hanjie Chen    Vicente Ordonez [ [ zilin@meta.com

摘要Abstract

检索增强生成（RAG）已成为将语言模型建立在外部知识基础上的标准机制，但基于词汇或语义相似性的传统检索不太适合复杂的推理任务：语义相似的问题可能需要完全不同的解决策略，而表面不同的问题可能共享相同的底层推理模式。 我们建议Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) has become a standard mechanism for grounding language models in external knowledge, yet conventional retrieval based on lexical or semantic similarity is poorly suited for complex reasoning tasks: a semantically similar problem may demand an entirely different solution strategy, while a superficially different problem may share the same underlying reasoning pattern. We propose检索增强强化微调 (RA-RFT)Retrieval-Augmented Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RA-RFT)，一个训练语言模型通过类比推理的后训练框架。 RA-RFT 使用黄金相关性蒸馏来训练检索器，该检索器根据预期推理收益而不是语义重叠对上下文进行排名，然后通过强化微调方法和检索到的类似演示来微调策略模型，以便模型学会在可验证的结果奖励下利用推理痕迹。 我们进一步分析检索上下文的多样性，发现推理感知检索表面互补的解决方案策略，为单个问题提供独特的推理支架。 在具有挑战性的数学推理基准中，RA-RFT 始终优于标准强化微调方法。例如，与 Qwen3-1.7B 和 Qwen3-4B 的 GRPO 相比，它分别将 AIME 2025 平均@32 准确率提高了 7.1 和 2.8 个百分点，这表明推理感知检索是改进的补充轴，并且与奖励设计或培训课程的进步正交。, a post-training framework that teaches language models to reason by analogy. RA-RFT uses gold-relevance distillation to train a retriever that ranks contexts by expected reasoning benefit rather than semantic overlap, and then fine-tunes the policy model via reinforcement fine-tuning methods with retrieved analogous demonstrations, so the model learns to leverage reasoning traces under verifiable outcome rewards. We further analyze the diversity of retrieved contexts and find that reasoning-aware retrieval surfaces complementary solution strategies that provide distinct reasoning scaffolds for individual problems. Across challenging mathematical reasoning benchmarks, RA-RFT consistently outperforms standard reinforcement fine-tuning methods. For example, it improves AIME 2025 average@32 accuracy by 7.1 and 2.8 points over GRPO for Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-4B respectively—suggesting that reasoning-aware retrieval is a complementary axis of improvement and orthogonal to advances in reward design or training curricula.

1 简介Introduction

可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 已成为复杂推理的强大后训练范例Reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards (RLVR) has emerged as a powerful post-training paradigm for complex reasoning(deepseekr1; openai_o1)。 通过优化结果的正确性而不是模仿参考解决方案，RLVR 引发了复杂的思想链推理，可以概括不同的问题类型. By optimizing for outcome correctness rather than imitating reference solutions, RLVR elicits sophisticated chain-of-thought reasoning that generalizes across diverse problem types(deepseekmath)。 然而，这些方法完全依赖于模型的参数知识：当遇到新问题时，其解决方案需要推理模式在预训练中没有得到很好的体现，模型没有机制来利用外部解决问题的专业知识。. However, these approaches rely entirely on the parametric knowledge of a model: when confronted with novel problems whose solutions require reasoning patterns not well-represented in pre-training, the model has no mechanism to draw on external problem-solving expertise.

检索增强生成 (RAG) 提供了一个自然的补充，将模型输出置于外部检索的内容中Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) offers a natural complement, grounding model outputs in externally retrieved content(lewis2020rag; guu2020realm)。 然而，标准检索不太适合推理任务：传统检索器根据词汇或语义相似性对候选者进行排名，这与推理任务的相关性很弱。. Yet standard retrieval is poorly suited for reasoning tasks: conventional retrievers rank candidates by lexical or semantic similarity, which correlates weakly with推理效用reasoning utility，理解为检索到的内容是否真正有助于解决目标问题, understood as whether the retrieved content actually helps solve the target problem(su2025bright)。 语义上相似的问题可能需要完全不同的解决策略，而表面上不同的问题可能共享相同的底层推理模式。 因此，使用检索到的内容天真地增强推理模型通常会产生边际或不一致的收益. A semantically similar problem may demand an entirely different solution strategy, while a superficially different problem may share the same underlying reasoning pattern. As a result, naively augmenting reasoning models with retrieved content often yields marginal or inconsistent gains(arabzadeh2025restructuring).

我们通过以下视角来解决这个问题We approach this problem through the lens of类比推理analogical reasoning (gentner1983structure)：专家问题解决者回忆起以前解决过的问题不是因为表面细节匹配，而是因为底层推理结构发生了转移。 这激发了一种检索范式，该范式选择的示例: expert problem-solvers recall previously solved problems not because surface details match, but because the underlying reasoning structure transfers. This motivates a retrieval paradigm that selects examples whose推理痕迹reasoning traces, 即i.e.分步解决策略可以为目标问题提供最大程度的信息。 基于这一见解，我们建议step-by-step solution strategies are maximally informative for the target problem. Building on this insight, we propose检索增强强化微调 (RA-RFT)Retrieval-Augmented Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RA-RFT)，一个由三个阶段组成的后培训框架： (1), a post-training framework consisting of three stages: (1)黄金相关性蒸馏gold-relevance distillation，通过使用判断模型直接评估候选推理轨迹是否与目标问题共享可转移推理模式，构建基于推理效用的检索监督； (2), which constructs retrieval supervision grounded in reasoning utility by using a judge model to directly assess whether candidate reasoning traces share transferable reasoning patterns with the target problem; (2)推理意识检索训练reasoning-aware retriever training，它使用这些基于实用的注释的对比学习来训练一个密集的检索器，该检索器可以呈现结构上类似的问题，并且 (3), which uses contrastive learning on these utility-based annotations to train a dense retriever that surfaces structurally analogous problems, and (3)通过检索到的演示进行强化微调reinforcement fine-tuning with retrieved demonstrations，它将检索到的推理痕迹注入训练提示中，并通过 RLVR 优化目标模型。 我们进一步分析了检索上下文的多样性和推理感知检索器的质量，发现推理感知检索产生结构上不同的痕迹，这些痕迹呈现出针对个体问题的不同解决策略，这表明检索质量是有效上下文增强的关键因素。, which injects retrieved reasoning traces into training prompts and optimizes the target model via RLVR. We further analyze the diversity of retrieved contexts and the quality of the reasoning-aware retriever and find that reasoning-aware retrieval produces structurally varied traces that surface distinct solution strategies for individual problems, suggesting that retrieval quality is a key factor for effective context augmentation.

我们在竞赛级数学推理基准上评估 RA-RFT，包括 AIME 2024 和 2025We evaluate RA-RFT on competition-level mathematical reasoning benchmarks, including AIME 2024 and 2025(aime)，HMMT 2025 年 2 月, HMMT February 2025(hmmt)和 BrUMO 2025, and BrUMO 2025(brumo)。 在 Qwen3-1.7B 和 Qwen3-4B 中，RA-RFT 始终优于独立 RLVR 和强基线：它比 GRPO 将 AIME 2025 平均@32 精度提高了 7.1 和 2.8 个点，并在所有四个基准中实现了 4.1 和 2.6 个点的总体平均增益。 这些结果证实，推理实用性的基础检索，而不是表面水平的相似性，对于释放检索增强强化微调的全部潜力至关重要。. Across Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-4B, RA-RFT consistently outperforms both standalone RLVR and strong baselines: it improves AIME 2025 average@32 accuracy by 7.1 and 2.8 points over GRPO, and achieves 4.1 and 2.6 points of overall average gain across all four benchmarks. These results confirm that grounding retrieval in reasoning utility, rather than surface-level similarity, is critical for unlocking the full potential of retrieval-augmented reinforcement fine-tuning.
