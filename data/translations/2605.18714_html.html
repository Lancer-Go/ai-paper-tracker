摘要Abstract

统一多模态模型 (UMM) 致力于将视觉理解和视觉生成整合到单一架构中。然而，流行的训练范例通过稀疏文本信号独立优化理解，并通过密集像素目标生成。这种解耦策略会产生不对齐的表示空间，将视觉理解与生成隔离开来，并阻碍它们的相互强化。这项工作首次对生成后训练进行系统研究，其中我们将分层视觉任务制定为生成代理，以弥合 UMM 中的隔离。我们的实证研究表明，高级语义任务，特别是图像分割，可以作为最佳代理。与用纹理细节分散模型注意力的低级任务不同，分割提供的结构语义可以显着增强以视觉为中心的感知和生成布局保真度。基于这些见解，我们介绍Unified multimodal models (UMMs) strive to consolidate visual understanding and visual generation within a single architecture. However, prevailing training paradigms independently optimize understanding via sparse text signals and generation through dense pixel objectives. While these specialized architectures exhibit significant proficiency within their respective domains, the emergent trend toward UMMs seeks to consolidate both visual comprehension and generation within a single streamlined framework[umms:li2025uniworldv2, umms:MetaQueries, umms:janus, umms:pan2025transfer, umms:wang2025skywork, umms:yang2025mmar]。这种架构融合具有促进双向知识转移并促进理解和生成之间相互加强的潜力. Consequently, this deep integration unlocks advanced capabilities, including interleaved image-text generation and in-context visual editing, establishing a robust foundation for general-purpose multimodal systems[umms:lmfusion, umms:wise].

This decoupled training strategy isolates two capabilities and hinders the model from capturing the inherent dependencies between visual understanding and generation. Consequently, UMMs often fail to achieve true mutual reinforcement, leaving the framework with a shared architecture but disjointed optimization processes.

6 结论Conclusion

这项工作提出了 UMM 的微调范例，以减轻视觉理解和生成之间的优化分歧。之前的尝试利用像素空间重建来改善多模态对齐，但无意中引入了颗粒视觉噪声，最终导致性能不佳。为了克服这个限制，我们引入语义生成调整作为一种新颖的范例，将对齐代理从像素空间转移到语义空间。机制分析表明，这种语义集成从根本上提高了特征线性可分离性，并优化了注意力分配，以直接减轻表征错位。跨主流架构的广泛实证评估表明，SGT 在视觉理解准确性和生成布局保真度方面始终取得显着改进。该范式建立的原则强调，在语义层面上协调多模式功能是开发有凝聚力和多功能的 UMM 的重要基础。This work proposes a fine-tuning paradigm for UMMs to mitigate the optimization divergence between visual understanding and generation. Previous attempts leverage pixel space reconstruction to improve multimodal alignment but inadvertently introduce granular visual noise that ultimately leads to suboptimal performance. To overcome this limitation, we introduce Semantic Generative Tuning as a novel paradigm that shifts the alignment proxy from the pixel space to the semantic space. Mechanistic analyses reveal that this semantic integration fundamentally improves feature linear separability and optimizes attention allocation to directly mitigate representational misalignment. Extensive empirical evaluations across mainstream architectures demonstrate that SGT consistently yields significant improvements in both visual understanding accuracy and generative layout fidelity. The principles established by this paradigm highlight that aligning multimodal capabilities at the semantic level serves as a crucial foundation for developing cohesive and versatile UMMs.
