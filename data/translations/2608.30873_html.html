摘要Abstract

法学硕士越来越多地用于人际咨询和研究跨语言和跨文化的社会行为的工具。引发语言或文化相关变化的常见捷径是要求模型以母语人士的身份回答。我们测试这个母语者角色是否重现了模型以目标语言生成建议并将响应翻译回英语时获得的输出。我们使用 13 种语言和 8 名法学硕士的 600 个人际建议问题，将母语生成和翻译 (NL) 与母语人士角色提示 (NP) 进行比较，测量语言风格、行为支架和强制选择行动建议。我们发现 NP 和 NL 是不可互换的。与 NL 相比，NP 通常会增加词汇社交线索，包括归属感和积极语气，同时降低具体性和社交协调等品质； NP 还在开放式建议中提供了可操作性较低的脚手架。在强制选择场景中，NP 会改变模型选择的动作，倾向于对抗而不是重定向，其效果大小因语言、主题和模型而异。我们的结果表明，跨语言启发策略是一种相应的方法选择，它可以改变建议的框架方式和推荐的行动模型。LLMs are increasingly used for interpersonal advice and as tools for studying social behavior across languages and cultures. A common shortcut for eliciting language- or culturerelated variation is to ask a model to answer as a native speaker. We test whether this nativespeaker persona reproduces the outputs obtained when models instead generate advice in the target language and translate the response back into English. Using 600 interpersonal advice questions across 13 languages and eight LLMs, we compare native-language generation followed by translation (NL) with nativespeaker persona prompting (NP), measuring linguistic style, behavioral scaffolding, and forced-choice action recommendations. We find that NP and NL are not interchangeable. Compared to NL, NP often increases lexical social cues, including affiliation and positive tone, while reducing qualities such as concreteness and social attunement; NP also provides less actionable scaffolding in open-ended advice. In forced-choice scenarios, NP changes which action the model selects, favoring confrontation over redirection, with effect sizes varying across languages, topics, and models. Our results show that cross-lingual elicitation strategy is a consequential methodological choice that can change both how advice is framed and which actions models recommend.

2 相关工作2 Related Work

2.1 多语言法学硕士评估2.1 Multilingual LLM Evaluation

多语言 NLP 评估传统上侧重于模型在分类、问答、检索、翻译和结构化预测等标准任务上是否可以跨语言迁移。 XTREME 等基准评估了多种语言和任务类型的多语言表示（Hu 等人，2020）。最近的面向 LLM 的基准（例如 MEGA 和 ChatGPT Beyond English）评估了跨多语言环境的生成模型（Ahuja 等人，2023 年；Lai 等人，2023 年）。 Aya 等多语言教学调整工作通过构建多语言教学数据集和模型来解决法学硕士发展以英语为中心的本质（Singh 等人，2024 年；Üstün 等人，2024 年）。虽然现有文献表明，不同语言的法学硕士行为差异很大，但大多数评估都强调任务正确性、基准性能或指令遵循质量。人与人之间的建议会有所不同，因为可能没有单一的正确答案；相关问题涉及人际立场、语气和行动建议。Multilingual NLP evaluation has traditionally focused on whether models transfer across languages on standard tasks such as classification, question answering, retrieval, translation, and structured prediction. Benchmarks such as XTREME evaluate multilingual representations across many languages and task types (Hu et al., 2020). More recent LLM-oriented benchmarks such as MEGA and ChatGPT Beyond English evaluate generative models across multilingual settings (Ahuja et al., 2023; Lai et al., 2023). Multilingual instructiontuning efforts such as Aya address the Englishcentered nature of LLM development by constructing multilingual instruction datasets and models (Singh et al., 2024; Üstün et al., 2024). While existing literature shows that LLM behavior varies substantially across languages, most evaluations emphasize task correctness, benchmark performance, or instruction-following quality. Interpersonal advice differs because there may be no single correct answer; the relevant question concerns interpersonal stance, tone, and action recommendation.

2.2 人际建议和社会判断法学硕士2.2 LLMs for Interpersonal Advice and Social Judgment

越来越多的工作在社交、人际和情感支持环境中研究法学硕士。对法学硕士生成的建议的研究表明，建议的质量不仅取决于推荐本身，还取决于措辞、语气和感知的有用性（Wester 等人，2024）。关于社会情境判断的研究发现，法学硕士可以在涉及人际冲突和社会推理的情境中表现出色（Mittelstädt et al., 2024）。情感支持的相关工作研究了法学硕士是否可以产生同理心或文化敏感的反应，同时表明简单的角色扮演提示可能不足以产生文化敏感性（Liu et al., 2026）。A growing body of work examines LLMs in social, interpersonal, and emotional-support settings. Studies of LLM-generated advice show that advice quality is shaped not only by the recommendation itself, but also by phrasing, tone, and perceived usefulness (Wester et al., 2024). Work on social-situational judgment finds that LLMs can perform strongly on scenarios involving interpersonal conflict and social reasoning (Mittelstädt et al., 2024). Related work on emotional support studies whether LLMs can produce empathic or culturally sensitive responses, while showing that simple role-play prompts may be insufficient for cultural sensitivity (Liu et al., 2026).

2.3 角色、文化提示和社会偏见2.3 Personas, Cultural Prompting, and Social Bias

法学硕士越来越多地被用作人类受访者或社会参与者的代理，根据人口统计、文化或角色描述对他们进行调节。角色提示会影响模型输出，但仅捕获人类变异和上下文的一部分（Hu 和 Collier，2024；Giorgi 等人，2024）。角色选择可以改变文化规范判断（Kamruzzaman 等，2026），而更广泛的偏见研究记录了跨语言的人口刻板印象以及性别和关系属性的判断和说服风格的变化（Nadeem 等，2021；Smith 等，2022；Costa-jussà 等，2023；Kotek 等，2023；Levy 等， 2024；保利等人，2026）。Increasingly, LLMs are used as proxies for human respondents or social actors by conditioning them on demographic, cultural, or persona descriptions. Persona prompts affect model outputs but capture only part of human variation and context (Hu and Collier, 2024; Giorgi et al., 2024). Persona choices can shift cultural-norm judgments (Kamruzzaman et al., 2026), while broader bias research documents demographic stereotypes across languages and variation in judgments and persuasive style by gender and relationship attributes (Nadeem et al., 2021; Smith et al., 2022; Costa-jussà et al., 2023; Kotek et al., 2023; Levy et al., 2024; Pauli et al., 2026).

即时语言和文化框架提供了额外的调节方式，其效果取决于文化的表达和评估方式（AlKhamassi 等人，2024）。生成和组合跨语言的文化提示反应可以增加人口和观点多样性（Wang 等人，2025），而结构化价值调查发现与人类文化价值观一致的收益有限（Bulté 和 Terryn，2025）。Prompt language and cultural framing provide additional ways of conditioning whose effects depend on how culture is represented and evaluated (AlKhamissi et al., 2024). Generating and combining culturally prompted responses across languages can increase demographic and perspective diversity (Wang et al., 2025), while structured value surveys find only limited gains in alignment with human cultural values (Bulté and Terryn, 2025).

4.3 讨论4.3 Discussion

总之，语言结果表明 NP 和 NL 是多语言人际建议的不同启发方法。 NP 往往会产生更温暖、更积极、更明确的社会标记的建议，而 NL 更经常保留建议的关系和实用脚手架：用户应该如何直接说话、包含多少情感背景、如何谨慎地管理关系以及如何具体指定后续步骤。差异不仅仅是风格上的差异。语言路径改变了建议在人际情境中如何定位用户。Together, the linguistic results show that NP and NL are distinct elicitation methods for multilingual interpersonal advice. NP tends to produce advice that is warmer, more positive, and more explicitly socially marked, while NL more often preserves the relational and pragmatic scaffolding of advice: how directly the user should speak, how much emotional context is included, how carefully the relationship is managed, and how concretely next steps are specified. The difference is not just a stylistic one. Language pathway changes how the advice positions the user in the interpersonal situation.

5.3 讨论5.3 Discussion

总而言之，行为结果表明 NP 和 NL 在生成建议的结构和对何种行动合适的最终判断上都存在差异。与 NL 相比，NP 可以让建议显得更加社交化，同时减少帮助用户采取行动的脚手架，并且可以针对相同的困境改变建议。事实上，NP 有时会复制粗略的语言水平模式，同时仍然将选择转向对抗，这表明角色提示和母语生成有时可能在文本上看起来一致，但在具体指导或行动建议方面可能会产生完全不同的结果。Taken together, the behavioral results show that NP and NL differ in both the structure of generated advice and the final judgment about what action is appropriate. Compared to NL, NP can make advice appear more socially polished while reducing the scaffolding that helps users act, and can shift recommendations for the same dilemma. The fact that NP sometimes replicates coarse languagelevel patterns while still shifting choices toward confrontation suggests that persona prompting and native language generation may sometimes appear aligned textually, but can produce completely different results with regards to concrete guidance or action suggestions.

局限性Limitations

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，尽管我们排除了带有明确国家标签的问题，但源数据来自英语建议论坛。因此，人际困境可能反映了西方、英语国家的假设。我们的结果应该被解释为模型如何响应该语料库的翻译版本的差异，而不是关于来自不同语言社区的人们如何回答这些问题的主张。 NL 或 NP 是否更符合这些语言的真实使用者对人际场景的反应，是未来研究的一个宝贵途径。This study has several limitations. First, although we exclude questions with explicit country tags, the source data comes from an English-language advice forum. The interpersonal dilemmas may therefore reflect Western, English-speaking assumptions. Our results should be interpreted as differences in how models respond to translated versions of this corpus, not as claims about how people from different language communities would answer these questions. Whether NL or NP is better aligned with how real speakers of these languages would respond to interpersonal scenarios is a valuable avenue for future study.

其次，我们的语言和角色条件广泛。诸如“你是韩语母语”之类的提示在地区、阶级、年龄和性别中都会崩溃。这对于测试通用策略很有用，但对于代表文化身份却没有用。未来的工作应该研究更具体的区域、方言和情境背景。Second, our language and persona conditions are broad. A prompt such as ”You are a native Korean speaker” collapses across region, class, age, and gender. This is useful for testing a common strategy, but not for representing cultural identity. Future work should examine more specific regional, dialectal, and situational contexts.

第三，我们的分析依赖于自动注释管道。 LIWC 提供可重复的词汇特征，而基于 LLM 的注释和 BCT 标签允许可扩展的评估，但两者都可能会错过细微差别或反映注释器模型偏差。未来的工作应该与来自相关语言和文化背景的人类评分者一起评估法学硕士生成的反应。Third, our analysis relies on automated annotation pipelines. LIWC provides reproducible lexical features, while LLM-based annotations and BCT labels allow scalable evaluation, but both may miss nuance or reflect annotator-model biases. Future work should evaluate LLM-generated responses with human raters from the relevant linguistic and cultural backgrounds.

尽管我们同时包含开放模型和专有模型，但训练数据、一致性和以英语为中心的开发方面的差异可能会限制通用性。我们的一致性分析表明，许多模式可以跨模型推广，但不一定在任何时候都适用于所有模型。扩展到主要在非英语环境中开发的系统将有助于澄清哪些效果是跨语言提示的一般属性，哪些效果是特定于模型的。Although we include both open- and proprietary models, differences in training data, alignment, and English-centered development may limit generalizability. Our consistency analyses suggest that many patterns generalize across models, but not necessarily to all at all times. Expanding to systems developed primarily in non-English contexts would help clarify which effects are general properties of cross-lingual prompting and which are model-specific.

A.2 翻译管道A.2 Translation Pipeline

对于 12 种非英语目标语言中的每一种，我们使用 facebook/nllb-200-distilled-1.3B 翻译原始英语问题。我们生成 32 个温度为 0.3 的候选翻译。然后，我们将每个候选词回译为英语四次，温度为 0.7。这会产生每个候选翻译的多个英语重建，使我们能够估计反向翻译的平均语义保留和不稳定性。For each of the 12 non-English target languages, we translate the original English questions using facebook/nllb-200-distilled-1.3B. We generate 32 candidate translations with temperature 0.3. We then back-translate each candidate into English four times with temperature 0.7. This produces multiple English reconstructions of each candidate translation, allowing us to estimate both mean semantic preservation and instability across back-translations.

我们使用 all-MiniLM-L6-v2 的余弦相似度对每个反向翻译对原始英语问题进行评分。对于每个问题语言对，我们通过保留平均相似度最高的 5 个候选者并选择这五个中具有最小反向翻译方差的翻译来选择在反向翻译中具有高平均相似度和低方差的翻译。我们在表 3 中记录了按语言划分的翻译质量诊断。We score each back-translation against the original English question using cosine similarity from all-MiniLM-L6-v2. G 对角色提示措辞的敏感性G Sensitivity to Persona-Prompt Wording

In addition to the original NP formulation, we evaluate two controlled variants that modify only the native-speaker identity clause:

  • 原始NP：Original NP:“你的母语是{语言}。””You are a native {Language} speaker.”
