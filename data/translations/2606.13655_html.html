1 简介1 Introduction

生成视频模型的最新进展已经证明了摄像机控制的新颖视图合成的强大功能[1,9,11,27,35,36,42-44]。与扩散先验相结合，这些方法指向从稀疏视觉观察中自由视点生成动态对象。时间连贯和跨视图一致的多视图视频特别有价值，因为它们可以直接重建为动态 3D 表示，例如高斯图 [34, 39]，可以组合成生成的世界 [31, 38]，用于 AR/VR、模拟、游戏和视频重新拍摄等下游应用。Recent advances in generative video models have demonstrated strong capabilities in camera-controlled novel-view synthesis [1, 9, 11, 27, 35, 36, 42–44]. Combined with diffusion priors, these methods point toward free-viewpoint generation of dynamic subjects from sparse visual observations. Temporally coherent and cross-view-consistent multi-view videos are especially valuable because they can be directly reconstructed into dynamic 3D representations such as Gaussian splats [34, 39], which can be composed into generated worlds [31, 38] for downstream applications such as AR/VR, simulation, gaming, and video re-shooting.

多视图视频生成的常见策略是根据相机姿态、视图信息或显式几何信号来调节扩散模型[2,13,16,33,40,46]。对于动态人体，Di​​ffuman4D [16] 依赖于每个目标视图中渲染的 SMPL 骨架，而 MV-Performer [46] 依赖于单眼深度和人体法线估计 [17] 来构建用于目标相机调节的部分几何渲染。虽然有效，但这些方法继承了其几何输入的局限性：估计误差会传播到生成中，这些方法仍然与人类特定的模型联系在一起，并且推理通常与固定的相机配置或参考视图假设相结合。因此，将此类系统扩展到野外单眼视频或非人类受试者仍然具有挑战性。A common strategy for multi-view video generation is to condition diffusion models on camera pose, view information, or explicit geometric signals [2, 13, 16, 33, 40, 46]. For dynamic humans, Diffuman4D [16] relies on rendered SMPL skeletons in every target view, while MV-Performer [46] depends on monocular depth and human normal estimation [17] to construct partial geometric renderings for target-camera conditioning. Although effective, these approaches inherit the limitations of their geometric inputs: estimation errors propagate into generation, the methods remain tied to human-specific models, and inference is often coupled to fixed camera configurations or reference-view assumptions. Extending such systems to in-the-wild monocular videos or non-human subjects therefore remains challenging.

相反，我们训练了一个不依赖于显式几何先验的多视图视频扩散模型。从 Wan 2.1 的 1.3B 文本到视频 DiT [32] 开始，我们的模型仅使用相对相机姿势调节从一个或多个参考视图视频合成一致的密集目标视图。在训练或推理过程中，它不需要骨架拟合、深度估计、法线预测或渲染目标视图几何形状。我们唯一的架构修改是用五轴公式取代自注意力位置编码，该公式通过 PRoPE [18] 结合了空间坐标、时间索引、视槽索引和连续 SE(3) 相机几何结构。由于相机编码是相对的，并且视图槽编码是排列不变的，因此同一模型可以灵活地概括不同数量的参考视图和目标视图布局。We instead train a multi-view video diffusion model that does not rely on explicit geometry priors. Starting from Wan 2.1’s 1.3B text-to-video DiT [32], our model synthesizes consistent dense target views from one or more reference-view videos using only relative camera-pose conditioning. It requires no skeleton fitting, depth estimation, normal prediction, or rendered target-view geometry during either training or inference. Our only architectural modification is to replace the self-attention positional encoding with a five-axis formulation that combines spatial coordinates, temporal indices, view-slot indices, and continuous SE(3) camera geometry through PRoPE [18]. Because the camera encoding is relative and the view-slot encoding is permutation-invariant, the same model flexibly generalizes across varying numbers of reference views and target-view layouts.

为了支持稀疏或单目输入的外观合成，我们在训练期间添加多视图标题。我们的三阶段课程逐步介绍姿势跟随、灵活的参考目标视图调节和时间推出。这些组件共同实现了具有强大时间和跨视图一致性的同步多视图生成。使用生成的多视图视频，我们进一步构建了单目视频到 4D 高斯图像的管道，该管道可以从单个静态摄像机录制中生成动态 3D 资源，而无需多视图捕获设备。To support appearance synthesis from sparse or monocular inputs, we add multi-view captions during training. Our three-stage curriculum progressively introduces pose following, flexible reference-totarget view conditioning, and temporal rollout. Together, these components enable synchronized multi-view generation with strong temporal and cross-view consistency. Using the generated multiview videos, we further build a monocular-video-to-4D-Gaussian-splat pipeline that produces dynamic 3D assets from a single static-camera recording without requiring a multi-view capture rig.

2 相关工作2 Related Work

2.1 动态人体的新颖视图合成2.1 Novel view synthesis for dynamic humans

人类小说视图合成已经从静态、单帧重建方法发展到动态、时间相干的生成方法。早期的神经渲染方法，如 Neural Body [24]、D-NeRF [25]、Animatable NeRF [23] 和 Neural Actor [20] 建立了将特定主题的神经场拟合到多视图捕获的范式，实现了逼真的渲染，但需要针对特定​​场景的优化和校准相机装备。 HumanNeRF [37] 和 NeuMan [15] 后来演示了单目视频的自由视点渲染，减少了捕获要求，但仍然依赖于每个场景的训练和明确的人体先验，例如 SMPL [21] 模型或骨骼表示。Human novel-view synthesis has evolved from static, single-frame reconstruction methods to dynamic, temporally coherent generation. Early neural rendering approaches such as Neural Body [24], D-NeRF [25], Animatable NeRF [23], and Neural Actor [20] established the paradigm of fitting subject-specific neural fields to multi-view captures, achieving photorealistic rendering but requiring scene-specific optimization and calibrated camera rigs. HumanNeRF [37] and NeuMan [15] later demonstrated free-viewpoint rendering from monocular videos, reducing capture requirements but still relying on per-scene training and explicit human-body priors such as SMPL [21] models or skeletal representations.

随后的工作重点是提高效率和泛化能力。 InstantAvatar [14] 能够从单眼视频快速重建头像，而 3DGS-Avatar [26] 和动画高斯 [19] 利用可变形 3D 高斯表示来实现高质量的可动画化头像。 GPS-Gaussian [45] 进一步展示了来自稀疏观测的实时、可概括的人类新颖视图合成。虽然这些方法提高了重建速度、渲染质量或跨主题泛化，但它们仍然是基于重建的方法，而不是用于多视图视频合成的生成模型。Subsequent work focused on improving efficiency and generalization. InstantAvatar [14] enables rapid avatar reconstruction from monocular videos, while 3DGS-Avatar [26] and Animatable Gaussians [19] leverage deformable 3D Gaussian representations for high-quality animatable avatars. GPS-Gaussian [45] further demonstrates real-time, generalizable human novel-view synthesis from sparse observations. While these methods improve reconstruction speed, rendering quality, or crosssubject generalization, they remain reconstruction-based approaches rather than generative models for multi-view video synthesis.

最近基于扩散的方法已经开始解决多视图视频生成问题。 SV4D [40] 生成具有多帧和多视图一致性的动态 3D 内容，但依赖分阶段推理时间时间扩展来生成长视频。 DiffHuman4D [16] 引入了一种时空扩散框架，用于从稀疏视图视频合成 4D 一致的人体视图，但需要参考视图和目标视图的准确人体骨架。 MV-Performer [46] 结合了显式的几何先验，并在视频生成之前通过多阶段深度、法线和渲染管道逐步渲染目标视图条件。Recent diffusion-based methods have begun to address multi-view video generation. SV4D [40] generates dynamic 3D content with multi-frame and multi-view consistency, but relies on staged inference-time temporal extension to generate long videos. DiffHuman4D [16] introduces a spatiotemporal diffusion framework for 4D-consistent human view synthesis from sparse-view videos, but requires accurate human skeletons for both reference and target views. MV-Performer [46] incorporates explicit geometric priors and progressively renders target-view conditions through a multi-stage depth, normal, and rendering pipeline before video generation.

相比之下，我们的方法仅使用相机姿势调节从可变数量的参考视图联合生成可变数量的目标视图视频。与之前的方法不同，它不需要骨架、SMPL 模型、深度图、法线或渲染的目标视图几何形状，并且可以推广到固定相机装备配置之外。In contrast, our approach jointly generates a variable number of target-view videos from a variable number of reference views using only camera-pose conditioning. Unlike prior methods, it does not require skeletons, SMPL models, depth maps, normals, or rendered target-view geometry, and generalizes beyond fixed camera-rig configurations.

2.2 生成模型中的相机调节2.2 Camera conditioning in generative models

根据相机几何条件调节图像和视频生成器是一个活跃的研究领域。光线贴图或 Plücker 嵌入（将每像素光线原点和方向连接为附加输入通道）广泛用于多视图扩散模型 [2,5,16]。虽然概念上很简单，但射线图需要额外的可学习输入通道，这些通道必须从头开始训练，并且通常将模型与特定图像分辨率和 VAE 潜在结构耦合。替代方法Conditioning image and video generators on camera geometry is an active area of research. Ray-map or Plücker embeddings—which concatenate per-pixel ray origins and directions as additional input channels—are widely used in multi-view diffusion models [2, 5, 16]. While conceptually simple, ray maps require additional learnable input channels that must be trained from scratch and often couple the model to a specific image resolution and VAE latent structure. Alternative approaches

These methods avoid ray-map inputs, but view-index schemes typically assume a fixed set of camera slots during training and do not naturally generalize to variable numbers of reference and target views at inference time.

位置编码提供了一种非参数替代方案。 RoPE [30] 及其扩展通过旋转而不是学习的嵌入来编码位置信息，使它们本质上是长度不变的。 PRoPE [18] 进一步将 RoPE 扩展到连续 SE(3) 变换，使相机几何结构能够直接纳入注意力机制中。在 PRoPE 的基础上，我们将连续 SE(3) 调节与基于规范视图顺序的离散视图槽编码相结合。离散视图带区分相机身份，而连续 SE(3) 组件可推广到任意相机布局。它们共同实现了与摄像机装备无关的多视图生成，该多视图生成对于摄像机的数量和空间排列都是不变的。Positional encoding provides a non-parametric alternative. RoPE [30] and its extensions encode positional information through rotations rather than learned embeddings, making them inherently length-invariant. PRoPE [18] further extends RoPE to continuous SE(3) transformations, enabling camera geometry to be incorporated directly into the attention mechanism. Building on PRoPE, we combine continuous SE(3) conditioning with a discrete view-slot encoding based on canonical view order. The discrete view band distinguishes camera identities, while the continuous SE(3) component generalizes to arbitrary camera layouts. Together, they enable camera-rig-agnostic multiview generation that is invariant to both the number and spatial arrangement of cameras.

3 方法3 Method

3.1 概述3.1 Overview

我们在 Wan 2.1 文本到视频 DiT [32] 上构建 Flex4DHuman，并使用我们的多视图字幕从 DNA 渲染数据集 [4] 上的 1.3B T2V 检查点进行微调。给定一个或多个参考视图视频、其相机姿势以及所需的目标相机姿势，该模型会在目标相机处合成 RGB 帧。生成是在每个时间块内跨视图和时间联合执行的，并且按顺序推出这些块以生成比训练期间看到的时间范围更长的视频。可选的文本提示使所有生成的视图中的主题外观保持一致。We build Flex4DHuman on the Wan 2.1 text-to-video DiT [32] and fine-tune from its 1.3B T2V checkpoint on the DNA-Rendering dataset [4] with our multi-view captions. Given one or more reference-view videos, their camera poses, and the desired target camera poses, the model synthesizes RGB frames at the target cameras. Generation is performed jointly across views and time within each temporal chunk, and the chunks are rolled out sequentially to produce videos longer than the temporal horizon seen during training. An optional text prompt conditions the subject appearance consistently across all generated views.

我们的关键设计选择是保留 Wan 2.1 主干并通过自注意力位置编码注入多视图结构。具体来说，我们用五轴公式替换了原始的时空 RoPE，该公式通过 PRoPE [18] 结合了空间坐标、离散帧索引、离散视槽索引和连续 SE(3) 相机几何结构。这种设计直接在注意力内部对相对相机几何进行编码，同时支持排列不变的视图槽。此外，我们使用清洁调节掩模来区分观察到的参考标记和目标标记。在训练期间，这允许参考信息在视图和时间之间双向传播；在推理过程中，重复使用相同的机制来从先前的块中提供干净的历史标记以进行临时部署。这些组件共同产生一个模型，该模型可以推断出比训练期间看到的更密集的视图布局和更长的时间序列。Our key design choice is to preserve the Wan 2.1 backbone and inject multi-view structure through the self-attention positional encoding. Specifically, we replace the original spatio-temporal RoPE with a five-axis formulation that combines spatial coordinates, discrete frame indices, discrete view-slot indices, and continuous SE(3) camera geometry via PRoPE [18]. This design encodes relative camera geometry directly inside attention while supporting permutation-invariant view slots. In addition, we use a clean-conditioning mask to distinguish observed reference tokens from target tokens. During training, this allows reference information to propagate bidirectionally across views and time; during inference, the same mechanism is reused to feed clean history tokens from previous chunks for temporal rollout. Together, these components yield a model that extrapolates to denser view layouts and longer temporal sequences than those seen during training.

Within each window, we uniformly sample frames and construct a 2×2 个图像网格，来自四个近似正交的视点（前、后、左、右），以前景主体为中心，背景被遮蔽。生成的网格框架序列连同请求详细描述对象外观的提示一起提供给 Gemini，其中包括体型、头发或皮毛特征、服装、配饰以及任何可见的文本或徽标。对于动物，提示还要求提供观察到的行为的摘要，例如步态、头部姿势和尾巴运动。这个过程产生 252 image grid from four approximately orthogonal viewpoints (front, back, left, and right) centered on the foreground subject, with the background masked out. The resulting grid-frame sequence is provided to Gemini together with a prompt requesting a detailed description of the subject’s appearance, including body shape, hair or fur characteristics, clothing, accessories, and any visible text or logos. 我们有意将人工字幕重点放在We intentionally focus human captions on外观appearance而不是细粒度的运动。在试点研究期间，我们发现，当从非规范的摄像机角度观看对象时，生成的运动描述经常会错误地识别运动方向（例如，左/右或前/后）。此类错误会引入干扰监督，从而阻碍训练。通过强调外观属性，我们获得了更可靠且时间稳定的条件信号。对于动物来说，保留了高级行为描述，因为步行、小跑、跑步或尾部移动等动作通常是在以身体为中心的坐标中定义的，并且在各个视点之间保持一致。rather than fine-grained motion. During pilot studies, we found that generated motion descriptions frequently misidentify motion direction (e.g., left/right or forward/backward) when subjects are viewed from non-canonical camera angles. Such errors introduce noisy supervision that can hinder training. By emphasizing appearance attributes, we obtain more reliable and temporally stable conditioning signals. For animals, high-level behavioral descriptions are retained, as actions such as walking, trotting, running, or tail carriage are typically defined in body-centric coordinates and remain consistent across viewpoints.

在训练期间，每个采样的剪辑都与包含其起始帧的时间窗口对应的标题配对。因此，随着时间的推移，同一序列中的不同剪辑会接触到不同的文本描述，从而增加字幕多样性并减少对单个静态描述的过度拟合。During training, each sampled clip is paired with the caption corresponding to the temporal window containing its start frame. Consequently, different clips from the same sequence are exposed to different textual descriptions over time, increasing caption diversity and reducing overfitting to a single static description.

6 应用6 Applications

Flex4DHuman 支持实用的单目视频到 4D 资产创建工作流程。给定单眼或稀疏视图演员视频，我们的模型首先生成同步的密集多视图视频。然后，我们使用 MatAnyone2 [41] 分割前景演员，并使用 FreeTimeGS [34] 拟合生成的视图以重建动态高斯图。生成的 4D actor 可以组合成生成的 3D 场景，例如 Marble worlds [38]，并使用 SparkJS [29] 进行交互渲染。这演示了我们生成的多视图视频如何作为下游 4D 内容创建的中间表示，从而支持 AR/VR、游戏、模拟和视频重新拍摄等应用。Flex4DHuman enables a practical monocular-video-to-4D asset creation workflow. Given a monocular or sparse-view actor video, our model first generates synchronized dense multi-view videos. We then segment the foreground actor with MatAnyone2 [41] and fit the generated views with FreeTimeGS [34] to reconstruct dynamic Gaussian splats. The resulting 4D actor can be composed into generated 3D scenes, such as Marble worlds [38], and rendered interactively with SparkJS [29]. This demonstrates how our generated multi-view videos can serve as an intermediate representation for downstream 4D content creation, enabling applications such as AR/VR, gaming, simulation, and video re-shooting.

7 局限性和未来的工作7 Limitations and Future Work

我们当前的训练数据仍然以静态工作室捕获装置为主，限制了对动态摄像机运动、野外环境以及高度不分布的视点（例如极端倾斜或自上而下视图）的概括。虽然我们的 PRoPE 相机调节支持任意每帧相机变换，但可以通过合并动态多相机捕获来提高这些设置的稳健性。最后，虽然教师强制的历史调节可以实现时间推出，但更长的视野需要额外的漂移缓解策略，例如自我强迫[12]、扩散强迫[3]或其他长视野一致性目标。Our current training data is still dominated by static studio-capture rigs, limiting generalization to dynamic camera motion, in-the-wild environments, and highly out-of-distribution viewpoints such as extreme tilts or top-down views. While our PRoPE camera conditioning supports arbitrary perframe camera transforms, robustness to these settings could be improved by incorporating dynamic multi-camera captures. Finally, although teacher-forced history conditioning enables temporal rollout, longer horizons requires additional drift-mitigation strategies, such as self-forcing [12], diffusion forcing [3], or other long-horizon consistency objectives.

8 结论8 Conclusion

我们提出了 Flex4DHuman，一种多视图视频扩散模型，它将预先训练的文本到视频 DiT 转换为来自稀疏参考视图的同步多视图视频生成器。我们的方法通过结合相对相机几何和排列不变视图编码的投影位置编码来调节生成。与分阶段课程培训和多视图外观字幕相结合，这使得单个检查点能够泛化不同的视图计数、相机布局和时间长度，而无需依赖显式几何先验。 DNA-Rendering 和 ActorsHQ 的实验证明了在分布和零镜头设置中最先进的性能，优于依赖于骨架、深度、法线或渲染的目标视图几何体的方法。我们进一步表明，相同的公式从人类扩展到多物种动物生成，并直接与现有的 4D 高斯泼溅方法集成，支持单目视频到 4D 应用，例如 AR/VR、游戏、模拟和视频重新拍摄。We presented Flex4DHuman, a multi-view video diffusion model that turns a pre-trained text-tovideo DiT into a generator of synchronized multi-view videos from sparse reference views. Our method conditions generation through a projective positional encoding that combines relative camera geometry and permutation-invariant view encoding. Together with staged curriculum training and multi-view appearance captions, this enables a single checkpoint to generalize across varying view counts, camera layouts, and temporal lengths without relying on explicit geometry priors. Experiments on DNA-Rendering and ActorsHQ demonstrate state-of-the-art performance in both in-distribution and zero-shot settings, outperforming methods that depend on skeletons, depth, normals, or rendered target-view geometry. We further show that the same formulation extends beyond humans to multispecies animal generation and integrates directly with existing 4D Gaussian Splatting method, enabling monocular-video-to-4D applications such as AR/VR, gaming, simulation, and video re-shooting.
