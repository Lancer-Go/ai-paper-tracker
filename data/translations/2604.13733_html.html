通过视觉-语言-动作正则化快速启动强化学习Jump-Start Reinforcement Learning with Vision-Language-Action Regularization

Angelo Moroncelli12, Roberto Zanetti3, Marco Maccarini12, Loris Roveda13
摘要Abstract

强化学习 (RL) 可以实现机器人操作的高频闭环控制，但由于探索效率低下和信用分配不佳，扩展到奖励稀疏或不完善的长期任务仍然很困难。视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型利用大规模多模态预训练来提供通用的任务级推理，但当前的限制阻碍了它们在快速和精确的操作中的直接使用。在本文中，我们建议Reinforcement learning (RL) enables high-frequency, closed-loop control for robotic manipulation, but scaling to long-horizon tasks with sparse or imperfect rewards remains difficult due to inefficient exploration and poor credit assignment. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models leverage large-scale multimodal pretraining to provide generalist, task-level reasoning, but current limitations hinder their direct use in fast and precise manipulation. In this paper, we propose视觉-语言-行动快速启动 (VLAJS)Vision-Language-Action Jump-Starting (VLAJS)，一种将稀疏 VLA 指导与同策略 RL 连接起来的方法，以提高探索和学习效率。 VLAJS 将 VLA 视为高级行动建议的短暂来源，这些建议会偏向早期探索并改善信用分配，同时保留对 RL 的高频、基于状态的控制。我们的方法通过定向动作一致性正则化增强了近端策略优化（PPO），该正则化可以在早期训练期间将 RL 代理的动作与 VLA 指导柔和地对齐，而无需强制严格模仿，不需要演示或依赖持续的教师查询。 VLA 指导被稀疏地应用并随着时间的推移而退火，允许代理在线适应并最终超越指导策略。我们在模拟中评估了 VLAJS 六种具有挑战性的操作任务（提升、拾取和放置、钉重新定向、钉插入、戳和推），并在真实的 Franka Panda 机器人上验证了一个子集。 VLAJS 在样本效率方面始终优于 PPO 和蒸馏式基线，在多项任务中将所需的环境交互减少了 50% 以上。现实世界的实验证明了零样本模拟到真实的传输以及在杂乱、对象变化和外部扰动下的稳健执行。, a method that bridges sparse VLA guidance with on-policy RL to improve exploration and learning efficiency. VLAJS treats VLAs as transient sources of high-level action suggestions that bias early exploration and improve credit assignment, while preserving the high-frequency, state-based control of RL. Our approach augments Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with a directional action-consistency regularization that softly aligns the RL agent’s actions with VLA guidance during early training, without enforcing strict imitation, requiring demonstrations, or relying on continuous teacher queries. VLA guidance is applied sparsely and annealed over time, allowing the agent to adapt online and ultimately surpass the guiding policy. We evaluate VLAJS on six challenging manipulation tasks—lifting, pick-and-place, peg reorientation, peg insertion, poking, and pushing—in simulation, and validate a subset on a real Franka Panda robot. VLAJS consistently outperforms PPO and distillation-style baselines in sample efficiency, reducing required environment interactions by over 50% in several tasks. Real-world experiments demonstrate zero-shot sim-to-real transfer and robust execution under clutter, object variation, and external perturbations.

I 简介Introduction

强化学习（RL）提供了一个强大的框架，可以直接从交互中学习闭环控制策略Reinforcement Learning (RL) provides a powerful framework for learning closed-loop control policies directly from interaction[40]。在机器人技术中，强化学习可实现高频、基于状态的控制器，利用丰富的本体感受和几何反馈，实现精确的运动行为和在线适应。这些特性使强化学习对于现实世界的操纵特别有吸引力，其中抗干扰能力、紧密的反馈环和可靠性至关重要. In robotics, RL enables high-frequency, state-based controllers that exploit rich proprioceptive and geometric feedback, enabling precise motor behaviors and online adaptation. These properties make RL particularly attractive for real-world manipulation, where robustness to disturbances, tight feedback loops, and reliability are critical[24].

尽管有这些优势，强化学习仍面临着众所周知的挑战。学习复杂的操纵行为通常需要较长的训练时间、仔细的奖励工程和大量的交互数据Despite these strengths, RL faces well-known challenges. Learning complex manipulation behaviors often requires long training times, careful reward engineering, and large amounts of interaction data[3, 21, 41]。这些问题在长期或次优奖励任务中尤其明显，其中延迟奖励导致信用分配薄弱和政策改进缓慢. These issues are especially pronounced in long-horizon or suboptimally rewarded tasks, where delayed rewards lead to weak credit assignment and slow policy improvement[31].

大型语言模型 (LLM) 和视觉语言模型 (VLM) 的最新进展提供了一个互补的方向Recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) offer a complementary direction[13, 9, 8, 2]。视觉-语言-动作 (VLA) 模型通过将多模态观察直接映射到机器人动作来统一感知、语言理解和控制. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models unify perception, language understanding, and control by mapping multimodal observations directly to robot actions[6, 5]。大型系统，例如 Octo. Large-scale systems such as Octo[29], 开放VLA, OpenVLA[18], π0\pi_{0} [14]、RDT-1B, and RDT-1B[23]表现出强大的语义理解和广泛的泛化能力，通常表现出零样本和少样本能力。demonstrate strong semantic understanding and broad generalization, often exhibiting zero-shot and few-shot capabilities.

然而，当前的 VLA 模型并不是为了取代基于 RL 的控制器来实现精确的机器人操作。他们对大型专家数据集的依赖限制了可扩展性和适应性However, current VLA models are not designed to replace RL-based controllers for precise robotic manipulation. Their reliance on large expert datasets limits scalability and adaptation[30, 46, 16, 27]，而基于变压器的架构通常在低控制频率下运行，限制了它们处理精度要求、干扰和长视野闭环行为的能力, while transformer-based architectures typically operate at low control frequencies, restricting their ability to handle precision requirements, disturbances, and long-horizon closed-loop behaviors[26]。因此，尽管有很强的语义先验，但独立 VLA 在现实世界操作中的性能仍然有限。. As a result, despite strong semantic priors, standalone VLA performance in real-world manipulation remains limited.

在这项工作中，我们将 RL 和 VLA 模型视为训练机器人操作策略的补充组件。 RL 提供可部署的高频控制主干，而 VLA 模型提供稀疏的高级动作先验，用于编码语义知识和任务结构。我们不去对比这些方法，而是提出以下问题：In this work, we view RL and VLA models as complementary components for training robotic manipulation policies. Rather than contrasting these approaches, we ask:
