摘要Abstract

跨芯片设计、GPU 优化和嵌入式系统的工业软件开发缺乏专家推理轨迹来显示工程师如何推理硬件约束和时序语义。在这项工作中，我们提出了 InCoder-32B-Thinking，它使用工业代码世界模型 (ICWM) 对来自错误驱动思想链 (ECoT) 综合框架的数据进行训练，以生成推理轨迹。具体来说，ECOT通过综合多轮对话的思维内容和环境错误反馈来生成推理链，明确地建模纠错过程。 ICWM 根据来自 Verilog 仿真、GPU 分析等的特定领域执行跟踪进行训练，了解代码如何影响硬件行为的因果动态，并通过在实际编译之前预测执行结果来实现自我验证。所有合成的推理轨迹都通过领域工具链进行验证，创建与工业任务的自然推理深度分布相匹配的训练数据。对 14 个通用基准（LiveCodeBench v5 上的 81.3%）和 9 个工业基准（CAD-Coder 上的 84.0%，KernelBench 上的 38.0%）的评估显示，InCoder-32B-Thinking 在所有领域都取得了顶级的开源结果。Industrial software development across chip design, GPU optimization, and embedded systems lacks expert reasoning traces showing how engineers reason about hardware constraints and timing semantics. In this work, we propose InCoder-32B-Thinking, trained on the data from the Error-driven Chain-of-Thought (ECoT) synthesis framework with an industrial code world model (ICWM) to generate reasoning traces. Specifically, ECoT generates reasoning chains by synthesizing the thinking content from multi-turn dialogue with environmental error feedback, explicitly modeling the error-correction process. ICWM is trained on domain-specific execution traces from Verilog simulation, GPU profiling, etc., learns the causal dynamics of how code affects hardware behavior, and enables self-verification by predicting execution outcomes before actual compilation. All synthesized reasoning traces are validated through domain toolchains, creating training data matching the natural reasoning depth distribution of industrial tasks. Evaluation on 14 general (81.3% on LiveCodeBench v5) and 9 industrial benchmarks (84.0% in CAD-Coder and 38.0% on KernelBench) shows InCoder-32B-Thinking achieves top-tier open-source results across all domains.

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 InCoder-32B-Thinking，这是一种思维增强的代码模型，它弥合了通用代码智能与工业软件开发的严格要求之间的差距。通过将错误驱动的思想链 (ECoT) 综合（通过对迭代纠错过程进行显式建模来生成推理轨迹）与工业代码世界模型 (ICWM)（从特定领域的执行轨迹中学习代码和硬件行为之间的因果动态）相结合，InCoder-32B-Thinking 获得了有关硬件约束的深度推理能力，而无需牺牲一般编程性能。对 14 个通用基准和 9 个工业基准的广泛评估表明，InCoder-32B-Thinking 在 LiveCodeBench V5 上达到了 81.3%，同时在芯片设计、GPU 优化、嵌入式系统和 3D 建模方面建立了最强的开源结果。我们的分析进一步表明，将思维数据从 1.8 亿代币扩展到 5.4 亿代币可以带来持续的工业改进，证实基于世界模型指导的思维链综合是在现实世界工程场景中实现推理的关键。In this paper, we have presented InCoder-32B-Thinking, a thinking-augmented code model that bridges the gap between general code intelligence and the rigorous demands of industrial software development. By combining Error-driven chain-of-thought (ECoT) synthesis, which generates reasoning traces by explicitly modeling the iterative error-correction process, with an industrial code world model (ICWM) that learns causal dynamics between code and hardware behavior from domain-specific execution traces, InCoder-32B-Thinking acquires deep reasoning capabilities about hardware constraints without sacrificing general programming performance. Extensive evaluations across 14 general and 9 industrial benchmarks demonstrate that InCoder-32B-Thinking achieves 81.3% on LiveCodeBench V5, while establishing the strongest open-source results across chip design, GPU optimization, embedded systems, and 3D modeling. Our analysis further reveals that scaling thinking data from 180M to 540M tokens yields consistent industrial improvements, confirming that chain-of-thought synthesis grounded in world model guidance is key to enabling reasoning in real-world engineering scenarios.
