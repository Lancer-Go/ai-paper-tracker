摘要Abstract

重新排序器在细化检索增强生成的检索结果方面发挥着关键作用。 然而，当前的重新排名模型通常是在静态的人工注释相关性标签上单独优化的，与下游生成过程分离。 这种隔离导致了根本性的失调： 通过信息检索指标确定为主题相关的文档通常无法提供法学硕士生成精确答案所需的实际效用。 为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了重新排名偏好优化（RRPO），这是一种强化学习框架，可以直接将重新排名与法学硕士的生成质量保持一致。 通过将重新排名制定为顺序决策过程，RRPO 使用 LLM 反馈优化上下文效用，从而消除了对昂贵的人工注释的需求。 为了确保训练稳定性，我们进一步引入了参考锚定的确定性基线。 对知识密集型基准的大量实验表明，RRPO 显着优于强大的基准，包括强大的列表重排序器 RankZephyr。 进一步的分析强调了我们框架的多功能性：它可以无缝地推广到不同的读者（例如 GPT-4o），与 Query2Doc 等查询扩展模块正交集成，并且即使在嘈杂的监督者的训练下也能保持稳健。Rerankers play a pivotal role in refining retrieval results for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. However, current reranking models are typically optimized on static human annotated relevance labels in isolation, decoupled from the downstream generation process. This isolation leads to a fundamental misalignment: documents identified as topically relevant by information retrieval metrics often fail to provide the actual utility required by the LLM for precise answer generation. To bridge this gap, we introduce ReRanking Preference Optimization (RRPO), a reinforcement learning framework that directly aligns reranking with the LLM’s generation quality. By formulating reranking as a sequential decision-making process, RRPO optimizes for context utility using LLM feedback, thereby eliminating the need for expensive human annotations. To ensure training stability, we further introduce a reference-anchored deterministic baseline. Extensive experiments on knowledge-intensive benchmarks demonstrate that RRPO significantly outperforms strong baselines, including the powerful list-wise reranker RankZephyr. Further analysis highlights the versatility of our framework: it generalizes seamlessly to diverse readers (e.g., GPT-4o), integrates orthogonally with query expansion modules like Query2Doc, and remains robust even when trained with noisy supervisors.

1 简介Introduction

检索增强生成 (RAG)Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)Lewis et al. (2020); Borgeaud et al. (2022); Izacard and Grave (2020); Gao et al. (2023)已成为通过动态整合外部信息来增强法学硕士的强大范例，从而提高知识密集型任务的事实准确性和上下文相关性。 在典型的管道中，系统首先根据用户查询检索文档，然后将它们传递给 LLM 阅读器以生成响应。 因此，整体性能在很大程度上取决于这些检索到的文档的质量。 为了优化这一点，通常会引入重新排序模型（reranker）来细化初始文档集。 通过对最相关的文档进行优先排序，重新排序器可确保法学硕士获得高质量的上下文，从而获得更精确和连贯的答案。has emerged as a powerful paradigm to enhance LLMs by dynamically incorporating external information, thereby improving factual accuracy and contextual relevance for knowledge-intensive tasks. In a typical pipeline, the system first retrieves documents based on a user query and then passes them to an LLM Reader for response generation. Consequently, the overall performance depends heavily on the quality of these retrieved documents. To optimize this, a reranking model (reranker) is often introduced to refine the initial document set. By prioritizing the most relevant documents, the reranker ensures the LLM receives high-quality context, leading to more precise and coherent answers.

然而，当前 RAG 架构中仍然存在一个根本性的脱节：重新排序器优化通常与 RAG 管道隔离。 大多数重新排序器都是在使用静态信息检索指标（例如 NDCG）的监督范式下进行优化的，这是一个与下游 LLM 完全解耦的过程。 如图However, a fundamental disconnect persists in current RAG architectures: reranker optimization is typically isolated from the RAG pipeline. Most rerankers are optimized under a supervised paradigm using static Information Retrieval metrics (e.g., NDCG), a process that is entirely decoupled from the downstream LLM. As shown in Figure1，这会产生严重的错位： 重新排序器学到的相关性概念并不一定转化为法学硕士生成精确答案所需的有用性, this creates a critical misalignment: the notion of relevance learned by the reranker does not necessarily translate to the usefulness required by the LLM to generate precise answersLee et al. (2025)。 例如，假设查询“谁发现了青霉素？”，标准重新排序可能会偏向于一篇长历史文章（主题相关性高），而法学硕士可能会从简洁的时间线条目中受益更多（法学硕士有用性高）。 通过将重新排序器视为独立模块而不是 RAG 管道的集成组件，标准方法无法针对系统的最终目标（生成质量）进行优化。. For instance, given the query “Who discovered penicillin?”, a standard reranker might favor a long historical article (high topic relevance), whereas the LLM might benefit more from a concise timeline entry (high LLM usefulness). By treating the reranker as an independent module rather than an integrated component of the RAG pipeline, standard approaches fail to optimize for the system’s ultimate objective—generation quality.

为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了重新排名偏好优化（RRPO），这是一种强化学习框架，它使用 LLM 的生成质量作为奖励信号直接优化 RAG 管道内的重新排名器。 与依赖静态、人工注释的相关性标签的监督方法不同，RRPO 利用下游 LLM 本身提供可扩展的监督，从而消除了昂贵的手动注释的需要。 RRPO 将重新排序重新构建为一个顺序决策过程，允许检索和生成之间进行端到端对齐。 为了解决离散、高方差的 LLM 奖励引起的不稳定问题，我们引入了参考锚定的确定性基线。 该组件通过强制重新排序器优先考虑文档的“有用性”而不是主题的“相关性”，有效地稳定了训练，避免了与训练单独的批评者网络相关的计算复杂性和不稳定性。To bridge this gap, we introduce ReRanking Preference Optimization (RRPO), a reinforcement learning framework that directly optimizes the reranker within the RAG pipeline using the LLM’s generation quality as the reward signal. Unlike supervised approaches that rely on static, human-annotated relevance labels, RRPO leverages the downstream LLM itself to provide scalable supervision, thereby eliminating the need for expensive manual annotation. RRPO reframes reranking as a sequential decision-making process, allowing for end-to-end alignment between retrieval and generation. To address the instability caused by discrete, high-variance LLM rewards, we introduce a reference-anchored deterministic baseline. This component effectively stabilizes training by compelling the reranker to prioritize document “usefulness” over mere topic “relevance”, avoiding the computational complexity and instability associated with training a separate critic network.

我们通过对标准知识密集型基准进行大量实验来验证 RRPO 的有效性。 实证结果表明，RRPO 通过显式优化重新排序器以符合读者需求，实现了一致的改进，显着优于强监督基线。 值得注意的是，我们的方法超越了最先进的列表式 LLM 重新排序器 RankZephyrWe validate the effectiveness of RRPO through extensive experiments on standard knowledge-intensive benchmarks. 除了性能提升之外，我们的分析还强调了 RRPO 作为强大解决方案的三个关键特性。 首先，它表现出很强的泛化性：学习的策略不仅可以处理复杂的多跳推理，而且可以无缝地传输到不同的 LLM 读者，甚至有效地增强了 GPT-4o 等专有模型。 其次，它提供正交集成：作为“即插即用”模块，它补充了高级检索策略，与 Query2Doc 等查询扩展方法结合使用时可提供附加收益Beyond performance gains, our analysis highlights three critical properties that distinguish RRPO as a robust solution. 3 方法论Methodology

我们提出 RRPO，一个从根本上将文档重新排序重新定义为顺序决策过程的框架。 这种方法旨在弥合传统重新排序器预测的相关性分数与下游法学硕士文档的实际有用性之间的偏差。 与依赖静态标签的监督方法不同，RRPO 利用强化学习 (RL) 根据冻结的 LLM 读者的直接反馈来优化文档选择。 这允许检索管道与最终生成质量的端到端对齐。 在本节中，我们将重新排序问题表述为马尔可夫决策过程 (MDP)，并详细介绍我们的训练算法的组成部分，重点介绍我们的参考锚定基线。We propose RRPO, a framework that fundamentally redefines document reranking as a sequential decision-making process. This approach is designed to bridge the misalignment between the relevance scores predicted by traditional rerankers and the actual usefulness of documents for the downstream LLM. Unlike supervised methods that rely on static labels, RRPO leverages Reinforcement Learning (RL) to optimize document selection based on direct feedback from the frozen LLM Reader. This allows for end-to-end alignment of the retrieval pipeline with the final generation quality. 5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出 RRPO 来解决 RAG 系统中静态检索指标与 LLM 读者的动态需求之间的根本不一致问题。 通过将重新排名制定为由确定性基线锚定的顺序决策过程，我们成功地稳定了强化学习训练，消除了对昂贵的人工注释或不稳定的批评者网络的依赖。 我们广泛的实验结果提供了重要的见解： 与仅仅针对语义相关性进行优化相比，针对上下文效用进行优化可显着提高 RAG 性能。 除了性能提升之外，RRPO 还表现出非凡的稳健性——它可以推广到不同的 LLM 读者（包括 GPT-4o 等专有模型），甚至可以从规模较小、吵闹的主管那里提炼出有效的策略。 这使得 RRPO 不仅可以作为性能增强器，而且可以作为构建任务感知 RAG 系统的可扩展、经济高效的范例。In this work, we propose RRPO to address the fundamental misalignment between static retrieval metrics and the dynamic needs of LLM readers in RAG systems. By formulating reranking as a sequential decision-making process anchored by a deterministic baseline, we successfully stabilize the reinforcement learning training, eliminating the dependency on expensive human annotations or unstable critic networks. Our extensive experimental results offer a critical insight: optimizing for context utility yields significantly better RAG performance than merely optimizing for semantic relevance. Beyond performance gains, RRPO demonstrates remarkable robustness—it generalizes across diverse LLM readers (including proprietary models like GPT-4o) and distills effective policies even from smaller, noisy supervisors. This establishes RRPO not just as a performance booster, but as a scalable, cost-efficient paradigm for constructing task-aware RAG systems.

局限性Limitations

作为重新排序模型，RRPO 对初始检索模块提供的候选文档集进行操作。如果初始检索器未能检索到足够数量的相关文档（即顶部的召回率较低）As a reranking model, RRPO operates on a candidate set of documents provided by an initial retrieval module. 缺少黄金标签：SFT 依赖于高质量的段落级相关性注释（例如，标记为正/负的查询-段落对）。然而，我们实验中使用的数据集（DPR 检索设置中的 HotpotQA 和 AmbigNQ）提供了真实答案，但本质上并不为检索到的前 100 个候选者提供黄金标准排名标签。Absence of Gold Labels: SFT relies on high-quality, passage-level relevance annotations (e.g., query-passage pairs labeled as positive/negative).
