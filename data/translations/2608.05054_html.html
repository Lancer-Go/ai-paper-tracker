MarsCast：人工智能天气基础模型向行星大气层的迁移学习MarsCast: Transfer Learning of AI Weather Foundation Models to Planetary Atmospheres

摘要Abstract

我们通过将 GraphCast 图神经天气预报模型应用于火星，研究了地球天气基础模型到行星大气的可移植性。虽然 GraphCast 在陆地预测方面实现了最先进的性能，但其对非地球环境的适用性仍有待探索。火星气候数据库 (MCD) 提供了垂直高度水平（类似于地球压力水平）的全球大气场，我们使用火星气候数据库 (MCD) 评估火星温度和风场的零样本和微调 GraphCast 预测。零样本预测对当前状况进行了令人惊讶的准确描述，但无法重现昼夜变化并迅速衰减到气候平均状态。为了解决这个限制，我们使用 MCD 变量和大气层顶部太阳辐射强迫来微调 GraphCast，同时保持湿度恒定。微调可以快速了解火星的热变化。在短短 10 个训练周期内，该模型就开始捕获昼夜周期，并预测长达 10 天的季节性和垂直温度结构。预测质量随着训练样本大小的增加而提高，并对季节性初始化表现出敏感性。这些结果表明，经过地球训练的人工智能天气模型可以适应模拟火星大气动力学，为快速行星天气预报提供途径，以支持任务操作、沙尘暴风险缓解和未来人类探索。We investigate the transferability of Earth weather foundation models to planetary atmospheres by adapting the GraphCast graph neural weather forecasting model to Mars. While GraphCast achieves state-of-the-art performance for terrestrial forecasting, its applicability to non-Earth environments remains unexplored. Using the Mars Climate Database (MCD), which provides global atmospheric fields across vertical altitude levels (similar to Earth pressure levels), we evaluate zero-shot and fine-tuned GraphCast predictions of Martian temperature and wind fields. Zero-shot forecasts produce a surprisingly accurate depiction of current conditions but fail to reproduce diurnal variability and rapidly decay toward climatological mean states. To address this limitation, we fine-tune GraphCast using MCD variables and top-of-atmosphere solar radiation forcing while holding humidity constant. Fine-tuning enables rapid learning of Martian thermal variability. Within as few as 10 training epochs, the model begins to capture the diurnal cycle and forecasts up to 10 days reproduce seasonal and vertical temperature structure. Prediction quality improves with training sample size and exhibits sensitivity to seasonal initialization. These results demonstrate that Earth-trained AI weather models can be adapted to simulate Martian atmospheric dynamics, providing a pathway toward rapid planetary weather prediction to support mission operations, dust storm risk mitigation, and future human exploration.

一、简介1. Introduction

基于机器学习的天气预报的最新进展表明，在陆地全球大气再分析数据集上训练的基础模型可以与传统的数值天气预报（NWP）系统相媲美，同时使计算成本降低几个数量级（Bi等人，2023年；Bodnar等人，2025年；Lam等人，2023年）。 GraphCast 是一种图神经网络 (GNN) 天气预报模型，在测地网格上表示大气状态，并通过跨球域传递的消息来学习时空演化（Lam 等人，2023）。通过直接从数据中学习大气动力学，此类模型为天气预报提供了一种根本不同的范例。Recent advances in machine learning–based weather prediction have demonstrated that foundation models trained on terrestrial global atmospheric reanalysis datasets can rival traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP) systems while offering orders-of-magnitude reductions in computational cost (Bi et al., 2023; Bodnar et al., 2025; Lam et al., 2023). GraphCast, a graph neural network (GNN) weather forecasting model, represents atmospheric states on a geodesic grid and learns spatiotemporal evolution through message passing across the spherical domain (Lam et al., 2023). By learning atmospheric dynamics directly from data, such models offer a fundamentally different paradigm for weather prediction.

火星为这项研究提供了一个引人注目且科学丰富的测试平台。火星大气层稀薄，主要成分是二氧化碳，表面压力比地球低大约两个数量级。大气动力学受到强烈的昼夜温度变化、极冠和大气之间的季节性质量交换、塔尔西斯和希腊等特征的明显地形强迫以及悬浮尘埃气溶胶驱动的辐射加热的影响。这些过程会产生热潮、斜压波、区域性沙尘暴，偶尔还会产生环绕行星的沙尘事件。准确预测火星大气条件对于任务规划、进入-下降-着陆 (EDL) 安全、表面操作和未来人类探索至关重要。Mars presents a compelling and scientifically rich testbed for this investigation. The Martian atmosphere is thin and CO₂-dominated, with surface pressure roughly two orders of magnitude lower than Earth’s. Atmospheric dynamics are governed by strong diurnal temperature variability, seasonal mass exchange between polar caps and the atmosphere, pronounced topographic forcing from features such as Tharsis and Hellas, and radiative heating driven by suspended dust aerosols. These processes produce thermal tides, baroclinic waves, regional dust storms, and occasionally planet-encircling dust events. Accurate prediction of Martian atmospheric conditions is critical for mission planning, entry–descent–landing (EDL) safety, surface operations, and future human exploration.

火星大气的数值模拟传统上是使用火星全球气候模型（MGCM）进行的，该模型求解大气运动、热力学和辐射传输的控制方程（Forget 等，1999；Haberle 等，1993；Richardson 等，2007）。虽然物理上很全面，但 MGCM 模拟计算量大，不太适合快速预测或基于集合的不确定性量化。基于人工智能的大气预测提供了一种补充方法，经过训练后能够快速生成预测。Numerical simulation of the Martian atmosphere is traditionally performed using Mars Global Climate Models (MGCMs), which solve the governing equations of atmospheric motion, thermodynamics, and radiative transfer (Forget et al., 1999; Haberle et al., 1993; Richardson et al., 2007). Although physically comprehensive, MGCM simulations are computationally intensive and not well suited for rapid forecasting or ensemble-based uncertainty quantification. AI-based atmospheric prediction offers a complementary approach capable of producing rapid forecasts once trained.

一个关键的悬而未决的问题是“根据地球大气再分析数据训练的天气基础模型能否转移到大气成分和物理性质明显不同的行星环境中？”。陆地模型隐含地编码了有关大气成分、辐射平衡、环流状况和边界强迫的假设，这些假设与火星条件有很大不同。评估可转移性可以深入了解大气动力学的普遍性与特定行星的程度。A key open question is “Can weather foundation models trained on Earth atmospheric reanalysis data be transferred to planetary environments with distinctly different atmospheric composition and physics?”. Terrestrial models implicitly encode assumptions about atmospheric composition, radiative balance, circulation regimes, and boundary forcing that differ substantially from Martian conditions. Evaluating transferability provides insight into the degree to which atmospheric dynamics are universal versus planet-specific.

在本研究中，我们利用火星气候数据库 (MCD) 中的大气场研究 GraphCast 天气基础模型到火星的可移植性。我们首先评估零样本自回归预测，以评估地球训练模型是否可以重现火星大气变化。然后，我们制定了一个有针对性的微调策略，重点关注温度、风的变化和太阳强迫，以实现火星热动力学的预测。In this study, we investigate the transferability of the GraphCast weather foundation model to Mars using atmospheric fields from the Mars Climate Database (MCD). We first evaluate zero-shot autoregressive forecasts to assess whether the Earth-trained model can reproduce Martian atmospheric variability. We then develop a targeted fine-tuning strategy focused on temperature and wind variability and solar forcing to enable prediction of Martian thermal dynamics.

3. 方法3. Methods

3.1 GraphCast架构3.1 GraphCast Architecture

GraphCast 是一种基于图神经网络的天气预报系统，它在测地网格上表示大气以保留球形几何形状（Lam 等人，2023）。每个网格单元表示为包含大气状态变量的节点，而边则编码相邻单元之间的空间关系。这种图形表示可以实现全球范围内物理上一致的信息传播。GraphCast is a graph neural network–based weather forecasting system that represents the atmosphere on a geodesic grid to preserve spherical geometry (Lam et al., 2023). Each grid cell is represented as a node containing atmospheric state variables, while edges encode spatial relationships between neighboring cells. This graph representation enables physically consistent propagation of information across the globe.

该模型采用编码器-处理器-解码器架构。编码器将大气状态变量转换为潜在嵌入。处理器应用跨图传递的迭代消息，实现空间信息交换和大规模大气动态的表示。解码器将潜在状态投射回物理变量以产生预测。这种架构可以对全球大气演化进行有效建模，同时保留空间结构和尺度相互作用。The model employs an encoder–processor–decoder architecture. The encoder transforms atmospheric state variables into latent embeddings. The processor applies iterative message passing across the graph, enabling spatial information exchange and representation of large-scale atmospheric dynamics. The decoder projects latent states back into physical variables to produce forecasts. This architecture allows efficient modeling of global atmospheric evolution while preserving spatial structure and scale interactions.

3.2 零样本预测实验3.2 Zero-Shot Forecast Experiment

为了评估可转移性，GraphCast 被自回归地应用于火星大气状态，而无需针对火星进行特定训练。通过检查时间稳定性、空间梯度和昼夜变化来评估预测技能。To evaluate transferability, GraphCast was applied autoregressively to Martian atmospheric states without Mars-specific training. Forecast skill was evaluated by examining temporal stability, spatial gradients, and diurnal variability.

零样本预测迅速衰减到气候平均条件，并表现出完全不存在昼夜变化。热梯度随着预测提前时间的推移而减弱，表明地球训练的动力学不能推广到火星辐射强迫和大气结构（图1）。Zero-shot predictions rapidly decayed toward climatological mean conditions and exhibited a complete absence of diurnal variability. Thermal gradients weakened with forecast lead time, indicating that Earth-trained dynamics do not generalize to Martian radiative forcing and atmospheric structure ( Figure 1).

5. 讨论5. Discussion

使用 MCD 初始化的 GraphCast 的零样本运行性能凸显了基础模型作为构建其他模型的“基础”的价值。该模型仅根据地球大气数据进行训练，捕获了初始化时的模式，但无法产生昼夜周期。这凸显了行星特定的辐射强迫、大气成分和热惯性在塑造大气动力学方面的重要性。地球天气模型编码了适合稠密、富含水蒸气的大气层的动力学，因此无法重现火星的辐射平衡和昼夜强迫。The performance of zero-shot run of GraphCast initialized with MCD highlights the value of Foundation Models as a “base” upon which other models can be built. The model, trained only on Earth atmosphere data, captured the patterns from the initialization but was unable to produce the diurnal cycle. This highlights the importance of planetary-specific radiative forcing, atmospheric composition, and thermal inertia in shaping atmospheric dynamics. Earth weather models encode dynamics appropriate for a dense, water-vapor-rich atmosphere and therefore fail to reproduce Mars’ radiative balance and diurnal forcing.

通过少量数据、整个火星年中仅 30 天的样本以及仅 10 个时期的训练，GraphCast 模型开始学习变量的模式和大小。经过约 300 个 epoch 后，模型已有所稳定，但继续改进直至约 1000 个 epoch。微调的成功很大程度上是因为该模型保留了一般时空学习能力，同时适应了火星特定的强迫条件。这表明大气基础模型学习可转移的动态结构，可以通过适度的再训练进行调整。Fine-tuning with a small amount of data, samples from just 30 days throughout the Martian year, and training for only 10 epochs, the GraphCast model begins to learn the patterns and magnitudes of the variables. After ~300 epochs the model has stabilized somewhat but continues to improve all the way out to ~1000 epochs. The success of fine-tuning is largely because the model retains general spatiotemporal learning capacity while adapting to Mars-specific forcing conditions. This suggests that atmospheric foundation models learn transferable dynamical structures that can be adapted with modest retraining.

结果不仅在温度方面取得了成功，而且在垂直风和水平风方面也取得了成功，这证明了将地球人工智能天气基础模型应用于行星大气的可行性，并提供了对所学习的大气动力学的普遍性的见解。地球和火星大气之间的根本区别在于表面和大气中的水量。出于本示例的目的，表面被认为是所有陆地，并且大气湿度保持在全球平均值不变，这是不现实的。The results were successful not only for temperature but also for vertical and horizontal winds which demonstrates the feasibility of adapting Earth AI weather foundation models to planetary atmospheres and provide insight into the universality of learned atmospheric dynamics. A fundamental difference between Earth and Mars atmospheres lies in the amount of water both on the surface and in the atmosphere. For purposes of this example the surface was considered all land and the atmospheric humidity was held constant at the global mean, which is unrealistic.原位In situ观测表明，大部分溶胶的相对湿度<10%，黎明前气温最低时会短暂升高到较高值（有时甚至达到饱和）（Martínez 等人，2017 年；Polkko 等人，2023 年）。表面的相对湿度似乎高度依赖于表面特性，同时受到全球水循环的调节（例如，Pál 等人，2019）。observations indicate relative humidity is <10% for most of the sol, with brief excursions to higher values (occasionally even reaching saturation) just before dawn when air temperatures are lowest (Martínez et al., 2017; Polkko et al., 2023). 在火星上使用基于人工智能的天气基础模型的内在价值有三倍。 1) 预测时间比运行完整物理模型要低几个数量级。这可能会在未来的火星行动（人类和机器人）中发挥作用。 2) 基础模型的 10 天预测的计算成本要低得多：1 个 GPU 大约 2 分钟才能获得 1° 分辨率的预测，而 1 个 CPU 30 分钟才能从物理模型获得 5° 分辨率的预测（即基础模型在 2 分钟内处理的数据比物理模型在 30 分钟内处理的数据多 25 倍）。计算成本的降低增加了运行集成的选项，这也可以改善预测。 3) GraphCast 在 0.25° 空间分辨率下进行训练，在此应用中，它以 1° 空间分辨率运行，这比 MCD 的 3.75° x 5.625° 原始空间分辨率要精细得多。使用双线性插值对基础模型进行初始化，将 MCD 缩小至 1°，并且输出在视觉上似乎捕获了原始模型中的一些更精细的分辨率细节。这意味着粗分辨率输入的基本缩小可以产生更详细的预测输出。The intrinsic value of using an AI based weather foundation model on Mars is three-fold. 1) the time to forecast is orders of magnitude lower than running the full physics model. This could come into play in future operations on Mars (both human and robotic). 2) computational cost for the 10 day forecast from the foundation model is substantially lower: 1 GPU for ~2 minutes, to get a 1° resolution forecast compared to 1 CPU for 30 minutes to get a 5° resolution forecast from the physics model (i.e. Foundation Model is processing 25x more data in 2 minutes than the physics model is in 30 minutes). Reduced computation cost increases the options for running ensembles which could also improve the forecast. 3) GraphCast was trained at 0.25° spatial resolution and in this application it was run at 1° spatial resolution which is substantially finer than the 3.75° x 5.625° native spatial resolution of the MCD. The MCD was downscaled to 1° using bilinear interpolation for initialization of the foundation model and the outputs visually appear to capture some of the finer resolution details from the original model. This implies that a basic downscaling of the coarser resolution inputs can result in a more detailed forecast output.

MarsCast 模型以自回归方式运行，以 6 小时的时间步长获得 10 天的预测。在此示例中，我们正在运行历史数据，以便我们可以将预测与预测天数的 MCD 数据进行比较。比较显示预测相对于 MCD 随着时间的推移而降低，这与任何预测都是一致的。需要做额外的工作来量化，包括与观测结果进行比较，以量化随时间推移的退化情况，但据传闻，至少在 96 小时内，预测看起来不错（按照面向公众的陆地天气预报准确性标准）。The MarsCast model was run autoregressively to get a 10-day forecast at 6 hour time steps. 为了了解模型的稳健性，我们初始化了所有四个季节的许多不同日期的预测，并为每个季节生成了 10 天的预测（补充材料中的数字）。结果表明，MarsCast 正确地呈现了与火星上季节变化一致的模式，并在经常变化的 Ls = 240° 季节中保持了一致的性能。To understand the robustness of the model we initialized forecasts from many different days in all four seasons and produced 10-day forecasts for each (figures in supplemental materials). 六、结论6. Conclusion

基础模型的想法是，它是可以构建其他模型或扩展到其他领域的基础。这项研究表明，经过地球训练的人工智能天气基础模型（GraphCast）可以通过有针对性的微调来预测火星天气。即使是“零射击”结果也显示出与火星条件相关的模式。微调现有基础模型是为不同任务（在本例中为火星天气）生成新模型的一种经济有效的方法。由此产生的微调模型，我们称之为 MarsCast，可以捕获温度和风的昼夜和季节变化，并实现快速大气预测，为人工智能增强的行星天气预报提供一条途径。The idea of a foundation model is that it is a base upon which additional models can be built or extended to other areas. This study demonstrates that an Earth trained AI weather foundation model (GraphCast) can be adapted to forecast Martian weather through targeted fine-tuning. Even the “zero-shot” result show a pattern that is relevant to the conditions on Mars. Fine-tuning an existing foundation model is a cost-effective way to generate a new model for a different task, in this case Mars weather. The resulting fine-tuned model, we called MarsCast, captures diurnal and seasonal variability in both temperature and winds and enables rapid atmospheric prediction, providing a pathway toward AI-augmented planetary weather forecasting.
