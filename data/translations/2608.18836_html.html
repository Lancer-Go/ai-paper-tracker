摘要Abstract

公用事业公司因泄漏而损失了大量经过处理的水，但很少信任人工智能定位器来派遣工作人员：到处猜测并不能证明挖掘是合理的。差距在于问责制，而不是准确性：没有任何方法可以证明何时不应该采取行动。在这里，我们将泄漏定位重新定义为可验证弃权的决策。基于物理的执行代理针对数字孪生伪造假设（泄漏、需求、传感器、阀门）；独立的主管代理与大语言模型 (LLM) 审计员一起根据可代码验证的合同检查证据，然后认证调度、请求证据或弃权。在现场级噪声下，32% 的强制基线对于所发生的事件具有 96% 的决策精度。在独立生成的基准测试中，它仅对 33 个泄漏中的 4 个起作用，全部正确。通过双模拟压力和流量对经过审核的真实泄漏位置进行 194 个事件登记，得出 5 次挖掘调度，3 次正确，以及全区精度 44% 的调查回收率。负责任的弃权为水基础设施自主运营提供了一条可行的途径。Utilities lose a substantial share of treated water to leakage, yet rarely trust artificial-intelligence localizers to dispatch crews: guessing everywhere cannot justify excavation. The gap is accountability, not accuracy: no method proves when it should not act. Here we recast leak localization as decision-making under verifiable abstention. A physics-grounded executor agent falsifies hypotheses (leak, demand, sensor, valve) against a digital twin; an independent supervisor agent, with a large-language-model (LLM) auditor, checks evidence against a code-verifiable contract, then certifies a dispatch, requests evidence or abstains. Under field-grade noise, a 32% forced baseline becomes 96% decision precision on acted events. On an independently generated benchmark it acts on only 4 of 33 leaks, all correct. A 194-event register of audited real leak locations with twin-simulated pressures and flows yields five excavation dispatches, three correct, and 44% survey recovery at full district precision. Accountable abstention offers a defensible route to autonomous water-infrastructure operation.

简介Introduction

无收益水，即生产出来但从未计费的处理水，主要是物理泄漏，每年浪费很大一部分城市生产和数十亿美元Non-revenue water, the treated water that is produced but never billed, is dominated by physical leakage and wastes a large share of urban production and billions of dollars each year[1, 2]。因此，快速、可靠的泄漏定位是智能水管理的核心目标. Rapid, reliable leak localization is therefore a central goal of smart water management[3]，并且数据驱动的定位器的能力越来越强，来自图神经网络, and data-driven localizers are increasingly capable, from graph neural networks[4]突发检测序列模型to burst-detecting sequence models[5, 6, 7], 迁移学习, transfer learning[8]和基于物理的模型and physics-informed models[9, 10, 11]，通过 BattLeDIM 等基准进行整合, consolidated by benchmarks such as BattLeDIM[12]。然而，这样的系统很少被信任在真实网络上进行自主行动，并且仅靠准确性并不能解释这种差距：一次错误的挖掘会花费数千美元，并且在现场级噪声下以 30% 至 40% 的准确度对每个事件发出排名猜测的定位器不能被信任来派遣工作人员。逆问题是不适定的，候选位置的数量远远超过传感器的数量. Yet such systems are seldom trusted with autonomous action on real networks, and accuracy alone does not explain the gap: a wrong excavation costs thousands of dollars, and a localizer that emits a ranked guess for every event, at 30 to 40% accuracy under field-grade noise, cannot be trusted to dispatch a crew. The inverse problem is ill-posed, with candidate locations vastly outnumbering sensors[12];真正的异常不仅仅是泄漏，因为需求激增、传感器漂移和阀门误操作都会产生类似泄漏的偏差; real anomalies are not only leaks, since demand surges, sensor drift and valve mis-operation all produce leak-like deviations[13];并且没有流行的方法可以说明; and no prevailing method can state当它不应该采取行动时when it should not act，并向运营商或监管机构证明弃权。, and prove that abstention to an operator or regulator.

Selective classification adds a reject option[14, 15]并学会将经过校准的交接工作推迟给人类专家and learning-to-defer a calibrated hand-off to a human expert[16]，但深度模型往往校准不佳, but deep models are often poorly calibrated[17, 18]，保角方法证明标量覆盖保证, and conformal methods certify a scalar coverage guarantee[19, 20, 21]而不是受监管调度所需的可审计理由。因此，我们将渗漏定位重新定义为选择性的、有证据支持的决策，反映临床鉴别诊断：列举相互竞争的原因，安排最能区分它们的测试，并在诊断确定之前暂停治疗。rather than the auditable justification a regulated dispatch requires. We therefore recast leak localization as selective, evidence-backed decision-making, mirroring clinical differential diagnosis: enumerate the competing causes, order the test that best discriminates them, and withhold treatment until the diagnosis is secure.

这项工作做出了三个贡献。首先，它将泄漏诊断的目标从预测转变为问责：在可验证的弃权下，“不采取行动”成为可证明、可审计的结果，而不是无声的失败。其次，它作为执行者-监督者代理对实现了这一目标：对竞争原因的双重伪造，由可代码验证的目标合约和独立的语言模型审计员控制，该审计员可以收紧但绝不放松大门。第三，它提供了可部署的两层现场响应：在公用事业公司经过审计的 2025 年维修登记册上，可证明的压力信息上限限制了挖掘调度所能达到的目标，质量平衡调查层以 100% 的区域精度恢复了 44% 的事件。该架构在四个计算机网络（EXA7、KY4、H 市市政模型和 D 市地区模型）、第三方 BattLeDIM L-Town 基准和经过审计的 194 阶 D 市寄存器中应用不变的一组测量电池进行了验证，将 31.7% 的强制定位器转换为针对所发生事件的 96.1% 决策精度，对其接受的四个 L-Town 泄漏以 100% 精度进行操作，并进行标记每个操作都带有可机器检查的证书，其数字证据由独立的法学硕士在 32 个包损坏压力测试中进行审核。This work makes three contributions. First, it changes the objective of leak diagnosis from prediction to accountability: under verifiable abstention, “do not act” becomes a provable, auditable outcome rather than a silent failure. Second, it realises this objective as an executor–supervisor agent pair: twin-grounded falsification of competing causes, gated by a code-verifiable goal contract and an independent language-model auditor that can tighten but never loosen the gate. Third, it delivers a deployable two-tier field response: on a utility’s audited 2025 repair register, a provable pressure-information ceiling bounds what excavation dispatch can ever achieve, and a mass-balance survey tier recovers 44% of events at 100% district precision. Validated under one measurement battery applied unchanged across four in-silico networks (EXA7, KY4, the City H municipal model and the City D district model), the third-party BattLeDIM L-Town benchmark and the audited 194-order City D register, the architecture converts a 31.7% forced localizer into 96.1% decision precision on acted events, acts at 100% precision on the four L-Town leaks it accepts, and stamps every action with a machine-checkable certificate whose numeric evidence an independent LLM was shown to audit in a 32-package corruption stress test.
