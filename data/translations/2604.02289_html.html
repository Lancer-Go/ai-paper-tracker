1  简介Introduction

生成式人工智能的最新突破促进了向能够跨语言和视觉领域联合推理的统一多模态模型的转变，例如 GPT-4o 等模型Recent breakthroughs in generative AI have catalyzed a shift toward unified multimodal models capable of jointly reasoning across linguistic and visual domains, with models like GPT-4o[53]和纳米香蕉and Nano-Banana[23]启用高保真合成和指令引导编辑的本机功能。随着社区推动更加身临其境的数字环境，自然的演变是对“3D Nano-Banana”的追求：一个能够进行原生 3D 生成和编辑的统一框架，这对于推进具体人工智能和自主虚拟世界合成的应用程序至关重要。然而，在 3D 中实现这种原生功能仍然是一个持续的挑战。enabling native capabilities for high-fidelity synthesis and instruction-guided editing. As the community pushes toward more immersive digital environments, the natural evolution is the quest for a "3D Nano-Banana": a unified framework capable of native 3D generation and editing, which is essential for advancing applications in embodied AI and autonomous virtual world synthesis. However, achieving such native capability in 3D remains a persistent challenge.

与利用近乎无限的网络规模数据的 2D AIGC 不同，3D 生成面临严重的数据稀缺。这种差异使得 3D 合成本质上受到限制，尤其是在文本或单一视图的稀疏监督下。为了弥补这一缺陷，当前的方法通常将 2D 图像视为中间代理，在图像空间中执行编辑并通过迭代优化将其提升为 3D。这些间接方法虽然有效，但经常损害几何一致性，并且缺乏 2D 工作流程的直接性。这表明 3D 学习需要比稀疏信号所能提供的更强的结构约束。至关重要的是，2D 视觉数据隐式编码深层 3D 结构信息，包括物体形状Unlike 2D AIGC, which leverages near-infinite web-scale data, 3D generation faces acute data scarcity. This disparity leaves 3D synthesis inherently under-constrained, especially under sparse supervision from text or single views. To compensate, current methods often treat 2D images as an intermediate proxy—performing edits in the image space and lifting them into 3D through iterative optimization. While effective, these indirect approaches frequently compromise geometric consistency and lack the straightforwardness of 2D workflows. This suggests that 3D learning requires stronger structural constraints than sparse signals can provide. Crucially, as 2D visual data implicitly encodes deep 3D structural information, including object shape[44], 外观, appearance[74]和空间关系, and spatial relationships[3]，它提出了一个关键假设：我们能否通过统一的多模态框架利用丰富的 2D 观测来强化 3D 生成？, it prompts a pivotal hypothesis: Can we leverage the abundance of 2D observations to fortify 3D generation via a unified multimodal framework?

7  结论Conclusion

我们提出了 Omni123，一个统一的多模态生成模型 通过统一文本到 2D 和文本到 3D 解决 3D 数据稀缺问题 在单个自回归框架内生成。我们的三阶段 训练方法——异构对的跨模态预训练， 使用可学习的视图标记继续进行培训，以提高观点意识， 和文本-图像-3D 交错微调，关闭 “语义-视觉-几何”循环——实现丰富的二维观察 作为 3D 学习的隐式结构约束。 文本引导 3D 生成和基于指令的 3D 实验 编辑以验证这种统一方法是否显着改进 几何保真度和语义对齐，提供可扩展的途径 走向多模式 3D 世界模型。目前的限制包括 修复了 3D 分词器的网格分辨率以及对规范的依赖 观点；未来的工作将探索自适应分辨率标记化， 场景级生成以及材质和物理建模。We presented Omni123, a unified multimodal generative model that addresses 3D data scarcity by unifying text-to-2D and text-to-3D generation within a single autoregressive framework. Our three-stage training recipe—cross-modal pre-training over heterogeneous pairs, continued training with learnable view tokens for viewpoint awareness, and Text–Image–3D interleaved fine-tuning that closes the “semantic–visual–geometric” loop—enables abundant 2D observations to serve as an implicit structural constraint for 3D learning. Experiments on text-guided 3D generation and instruction-based 3D editing to validate that this unified approach significantly improves geometric fidelity and semantic alignment, offering a scalable pathway toward multimodal 3D world models. Current limitations include the fixed mesh resolution of the 3D tokenizer and reliance on canonical viewpoints; future work will explore adaptive-resolution tokenization, scene-level generation, and material and physics modeling.
