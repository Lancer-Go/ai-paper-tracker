Speech-based screening meets these requirements which relies on natural, low-burden data collected without specialized equipment. 一、简介1. Introduction

阿尔茨海默病（AD）是老年人痴呆的主要原因，其特征是记忆、语言和认知功能进行性恶化[1]。它约占全球所有痴呆病例的 60-70% [2, 3]。尽管目前 AD 无法治愈，但早期诊断对于及时采取干预措施至关重要，从而减缓疾病进展并改善患者的生活质量 [4]。Alzheimer’s disease (AD) is the leading cause of dementia among older adults and is characterized by the progressive deterioration of memory, language, and cognitive function [1]. It accounts for approximately 60–70% of all dementia cases worldwide [2, 3]. Although there is currently no cure for AD, early diagnosis plays a crucial role in enabling timely interventions that may slow disease progression and improve patients’ quality of life [4].

传统的 AD 诊断依赖于临床评估、神经心理学测试和先进的神经影像技术（如磁共振成像 (MRI) 和正电子发射断层扫描 (PET)）的结合 [5,6,7]。虽然这些方法提供了有价值的诊断和预后信息，但它们通常资源密集且成本高昂，需要专门的设备和训练有素的临床人员，并且通常仅限于医院或专门的临床环境。特别是，PET 成像涉及暴露于放射性示踪剂，并且会带来巨大的财务和后勤负担，限制了其大规模筛查或频繁监测的适用性。 MRI 虽然是非侵入性且无电离辐射的，但仍然昂贵、耗时，并且在社区或家庭环境中不太容易获得。与全面的神经心理学评估一起，这些限制限制了传统诊断途径的可扩展性和可访问性[8]。因此，这些传统方法不太适合大规模筛查、频繁监测或在家庭和社区环境中部署。这些限制凸显了对 AD 检测方法的迫切需要，这些方法必须是非侵入性的、具有成本效益的，并且能够适应现实临床环境中遇到的可变性，在现实临床环境中，患者特征和数据收集条件可能存在很大差异。这些限制还凸显了对智能、可部署临床系统的需求，该系统可以支持传统医疗保健环境之外的可扩展筛查和早期决策。Conventional AD diagnosis relies on a combination of clinical evaluations, neuropsychological testing, and advanced neuroimaging techniques such as magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and positron emission tomography (PET) [5, 6, 7]. While these approaches provide valuable diagnostic and prognostic information, they are often resource-intensive and costly, require specialized equipment and trained clinical personnel, and are typically confined to hospital or specialized clinical settings. In particular, PET imaging involves exposure to radioactive tracers and is associated with substantial financial and logistical burdens, limiting its suitability for large-scale screening or frequent monitoring. MRI, although non-invasive and free of ionizing radiation, remains expensive, time-consuming, and less accessible in community or home-based contexts. Together with comprehensive neuropsychological assessments, these constraints restrict the scalability and accessibility of conventional diagnostic pathways [8]. As a result, these conventional methods are not well suited for large-scale screening, frequent monitoring, or deployment in home and community environments. These limitations highlight a pressing need for AD detection approaches that are non-invasive, cost-effective, and robust to the variability encountered in real-world clinical settings, where patient characteristics and data collection conditions can differ substantially. These limitations also highlight the need for intelligent, deployable clinical systems that can support scalable screening and early-stage decision-making outside traditional healthcare settings.

为了应对传统诊断方法的局限性，最近的研究越来越关注可在专业临床环境之外部署的非侵入性且具有成本效益的替代方法。这些方法利用反映认知和神经功能的多模态信号，同时减轻患者负担和资源需求。代表性方法包括脑电图 (EEG)，它可以实时测量大脑活动 [9,10,11]；眼球追踪，捕捉与记忆和注意力缺陷相关的注视行为异常 [12, 11]；面部表情分析，反映 AD 患者面部肌肉活动减少和情感反应改变 [13, 14]；和语音分析，检查伴随认知衰退的声学特征和语言特征的变化[15]。In response to the limitations of conventional diagnostic approaches, recent research has increasingly focused on non-invasive and cost-effective alternatives that can be deployed outside specialized clinical settings. These methods leverage multimodal signals that reflect cognitive and neurological function while reducing patient burden and resource requirements. Representative approaches include electroencephalography (EEG), which enables real-time measurement of brain activity [9, 10, 11]; eye-tracking, which captures abnormalities in gaze behavior associated with deficits in memory and attention [12, 11]; facial expression analysis, which reflects diminished facial muscle activity and altered affective responses in individuals with AD [13, 14]; and speech analysis, which examines changes in acoustic features and linguistic characteristics that accompany cognitive decline [15].

在这些非侵入性技术中，基于语音的分析已成为早期 AD 检测最有前途的方法之一。语音是一种自然、低负担的信号，无需专用设备即可轻松收集，因此特别适合可扩展、远程和重复的筛选。重要的是，临床研究中的语音数据通常是使用结构化图片描述任务来引出的，其中要求参与者描述标准化图像。该范例提供了一种受控且可重复的启发协议，可最大程度地减少个体之间主题、内容和任务需求的可变性[16]。图片描述任务广泛用于神经心理学和临床评估，因为它们同时涉及语义记忆、词汇检索、句法规划和话语组织——已知在 AD 早期会受到影响的认知过程 [17, 18]。这些任务的受控性质确保了不同数据集之间语音样本的可比性，同时也允许出现细微的语言障碍。总的来说，这些特性将基于图片描述任务的语音分析定位为现实临床和社区环境中非侵入性认知筛查的实用、可访问且经济高效的解决方案。Among these non-invasive techniques, speech-based analysis has emerged as one of the most promising approaches for early AD detection. Speech is a natural, low-burden signal that can be collected unobtrusively without specialized equipment, making it particularly well suited for scalable, remote, and repeated screening. Importantly, speech data in clinical research are typically elicited using structured picture description tasks, in which participants are asked to describe a standardized image. This paradigm provides a controlled and reproducible elicitation protocol that minimizes variability in topic, content, and task demands across individuals [16]. Picture description tasks are widely used in neuropsychological and clinical assessments because they simultaneously engage semantic memory, lexical retrieval, syntactic planning, and discourse organization—cognitive processes known to be affected early in AD [17, 18]. The controlled nature of these tasks ensures the comparability of speech samples across different datasets, while also allowing subtle linguistic impairments to emerge. Collectively, these properties position speech analysis based on picture description tasks as a practical, accessible, and cost-effective solution for non-invasive cognitive screening in real-world clinical and community settings.

基于这些进步，大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新发展为开发用于基于语音的 AD 检测的智能和可扩展系统提供了新的机会。法学硕士能够提取丰富的高级语言表示，而这些表示通常很难使用传统的手工特征或浅层学习方法进行建模[19]。经过广泛数据集的训练，法学硕士可以执行从文本生成到复杂问题解决的广泛任务，从而实现更丰富的语言特征表示和改进的模型泛化[20]。Building on these advances, recent developments in large language models (LLMs) provide new opportunities for developing intelligent and scalable systems for speech-based AD detection. LLMs enable the extraction of rich, high-level linguistic representations that are often difficult to model using traditional hand-crafted features or shallow learning approaches [19]. Trained on extensive datasets, LLMs can perform a wide range of tasks, from text generation to complex problem-solving, which allows for richer linguistic feature representation and improved model generalization [20].

通过处理自发语音或转录对话，法学硕士可以检测可能表明早期认知能力下降的语言异常和纵向变化，从而提高非侵入性 AD 风险评估的敏感性和稳健性 [21]。除了检测之外，法学硕士驱动的系统还可以利用大量医学知识提供定制信息和建议，从而支持个性化教育和指导，从而扩展By processing spontaneous speech or transcribed dialogue, LLMs can detect linguistic anomalies and longitudinal changes that may indicate early cognitive decline, thereby improving the sensitivity and robustness of non-invasive AD risk assessment [21]. In addition to detection, LLM-driven systems can support personalized education and guidance by drawing on large bodies of medical knowledge to deliver tailored information and recommendations, extending

2、相关工作2. Related work

人类语音分析通常涉及声学特征（例如共振峰、音调和音素）和语言特征（包括语素、单词、句子结构和上下文含义）[23]。由于言语产生反映了物理机制和高级语言过程，因此它为检测认知障碍提供了丰富的线索。然而，声学特征本身并不总是可靠的医疗状况指标，特别是对于老年人[19]，因为与年龄相关的生理和外周变化可以显着改变60岁后的声学特征，而与认知能力下降无关[24]。Human speech analysis generally involves both acoustic features, such as formants, pitch, and phonemes, and linguistic features, including morphemes, words, sentence structures, and contextual meaning [23]. Because speech production reflects both physical mechanisms and higher-level language processes, it provides rich cues for detecting cognitive impairment. However, acoustic features alone are not always reliable indicators of medical conditions, particularly in older adults [19], since age-related physiological and peripheral changes can significantly alter acoustic characteristics after the age of 60, independent of cognitive decline [24].

随着法学硕士的最新进展，研究越来越关注认知障碍检测的语言特征。由于其强大的自然语言理解能力，法学硕士可以有效捕捉与AD相关的语法、词语选择和话语模式的细微变化[25]。这一进展很大程度上是由基于 Transformer 的架构和强大的嵌入技术推动的，这些技术使深度神经网络能够对复杂的语言表示进行建模 [19]。尽管由于教育和生活经历的差异，语言特征可能因人而异，但在大型语音语料库上训练的深度学习方法已证明能够识别不同人群中一致的损伤相关模式[26]。With recent advances in LLMs, research has increasingly focused on linguistic features for cognitive impairment detection. Owing to their strong natural language understanding capabilities, LLMs can effectively capture subtle changes in grammar, word choice, and discourse patterns associated with AD [25]. This progress is largely driven by transformer-based architectures and robust embedding techniques that enable deep neural networks to model complex language representations [19]. Although linguistic features can vary across individuals due to differences in education and life experience, deep learning approaches trained on large speech corpora have demonstrated the ability to identify consistent impairment-related patterns across diverse populations [26].

语言分析中的一个关键步骤是语音转录，特别是对于不提供手动转录的数据集，因为转录质量对下游 AD 检测性能有直接且可测量的影响 [27]。因此，自动语音识别 (ASR) 系统通常用于将录制的语音转换为文本，形成基于语音的 AD 检测管道中的基础预处理阶段 [28, 29]。先前的工作表明，即使在全自动临床筛查环境中，基于端到端语音的系统也可以有效地利用 ASR 生成的转录本进行 AD 评估 [30]。 ADReSS 挑战赛等大规模基准测试工作进一步标准化了转录和评估协议，实现了基于语音的 AD 检测方法的公平比较，并强调了自发语音在揭示认知障碍中的作用 [18]。A critical step in linguistic analysis is speech transcription, particularly for datasets that do not provide manual transcripts, as transcription quality has a direct and measurable impact on downstream AD detection performance [27]. As a result, Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) systems are commonly employed to convert recorded speech into text, forming a foundational preprocessing stage in speech-based AD detection pipelines [28, 29]. Prior work has shown that end-to-end voice-based systems can effectively leverage ASR-generated transcripts for AD assessment, even in fully automated clinical screening settings [30]. Large-scale benchmarking efforts such as the ADReSS Challenge further standardized transcription and evaluation protocols, enabling fair comparison of speech-based AD detection methods and highlighting the role of spontaneous speech in revealing cognitive impairment [18].

一旦获得转录本，文本数据就会转换为词嵌入，在多维向量空间中编码语义和上下文信息[31]。许多研究研究了从预训练语言模型（例如 BERT [32] 和 GPT [33]）中提取的嵌入，通常应用相同的分类器来隔离语言表示对 AD 检测性能的贡献 [34, 35]。最近的工作通过利用法学硕士从转录语音中提取更高级别的语言和评价特征来扩展这一范式，证明基于自发语音的 AD 识别的鲁棒性和诊断准确性得到了提高[36]。其他研究重点是在保持嵌入表示不变的情况下比较不同的检测策略，进一步强调语言特征建模在基于语音的 AD 诊断中的核心作用 [37, 38]。Once transcripts are obtained, textual data are transformed into word embeddings that encode semantic and contextual information in multi-dimensional vector spaces [31]. Numerous studies have investigated embeddings extracted from pretrained language models such as BERT [32] and GPT [33], often applying identical classifiers to isolate the contribution of linguistic representations to AD detection performance [34, 35]. More recent work has extended this paradigm by leveraging LLMs to extract higher-level linguistic and evaluative features from transcribed speech, demonstrating improved robustness and diagnostic accuracy in spontaneous speech-based AD recognition [36]. Other studies have focused on comparing different detection strategies while holding embedding representations constant, further emphasizing the central role of linguistic feature modeling in speech-based AD diagnosis [37, 38].

尽管基于法学硕士的方法在分析转录语音方面的前景越来越广阔，但它们与现实世界临床系统的集成仍然受到隐私和部署挑战的限制。许多高性能商业法学硕士（例如 ChatGPT）都是闭源的，并且依赖于不完全符合健康保险流通和责任法案 (HIPAA) 的基于云的基础设施，这引发了对敏感患者数据处理的严重担忧 [39, 40]。即使符合 HIPAA 的解决方案可用，它们通常也是专有的，限制了数据治理的透明度、适应性和控制 [41]。与此同时，医院 IT 环境通常要求将人工智能系统部署在安全且隔离的基础设施中，而依赖云的商业法学硕士无法可靠地满足这一要求 [42]。总的来说，这些限制对临床采用先进的法学硕士技术进行基于语音的 AD 检测构成了重大障碍。Despite the growing promise of LLM-based approaches for analyzing transcribed speech, their integration into real-world clinical systems remains constrained by privacy and deployment challenges. Many high-performing commercial LLMs, such as ChatGPT, are closed-source and rely on cloud-based infrastructures that are not fully compliant with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), raising significant concerns regarding the handling of sensitive patient data [39, 40]. Even when HIPAA-compliant solutions are available, they are typically proprietary, limiting transparency, adaptability, and control over data governance [41]. At the same time, hospital IT environments often require AI systems to be deployed locally within secure and isolated infrastructures, a requirement that cloud-dependent commercial LLMs cannot reliably satisfy [42]. Collectively, these limitations pose a significant barrier to the clinical adoption of advanced LLM technologies for speech-based AD detection.

3.2.方法3.2. Method

本研究提出了 LSEAD，这是一种基于语音的临床筛查框架，旨在将原始患者语音记录转换为紧凑且语义信息丰富的表示形式，以实现可靠的 AD 检测。该框架被设计为一个端到端、可部署的系统，支持现实临床环境中的非侵入性和保护隐私的认知评估。如图 1 所示，系统遵循从患者录音开始的顺序处理流程。首先使用 ASR 系统将原始语音信号转录为文本形式。这种设计选择有意优先考虑语言内容而不是声学特征，其动机是先前的研究表明文本信息捕获了与 AD 相关的最显着的认知和语义障碍 [36, 48]。该转录步骤构成了端到端临床流程的关键组成部分，使下游语言模型能够在标准化和可解释的文本输入上运行。This study proposes LSEAD, a speech-based clinical screening framework designed to transform raw patient speech recordings into compact and semantically informative representations for reliable AD detection. The framework is designed as an end-to-end, deployable system that supports non-invasive and privacy-preserving cognitive assessment in real-world clinical environments. As illustrated in Figure 1, the system follows a sequential processing pipeline beginning with patient audio recordings. The raw speech signals are first transcribed into textual form using an ASR system. This design choice intentionally prioritizes linguistic content over acoustic features, motivated by prior studies demonstrating that textual information captures the most salient cognitive and semantic impairments associated with AD [36, 48]. This transcription step forms a critical component of the end-to-end clinical pipeline, enabling downstream language models to operate on standardized and interpretable textual inputs.

然后将生成的成绩单输入法学硕士，其中与认知衰退相关的语义和句法模式被隐式建模。我们不依赖最终的预测层，而是从 LLM 的倒数第二层提取高级特征表示。这些嵌入利用模型学到的深层上下文理解，捕捉与 AD 相关的细微语言特征。然而，由于它们固有的高维度，直接使用这些嵌入可能会引入冗余并阻碍有效的分类[49]。为了解决这个问题，应用 PCA 将嵌入投影到低维潜在空间，同时保留最具辨别力的方差。The resulting transcripts are then fed into an LLM, where semantic and syntactic patterns associated with cognitive decline are implicitly modelled. Rather than relying on the final prediction layer, we extract high-level feature representations from the penultimate layer of the LLM. These embeddings capture nuanced linguistic characteristics relevant to AD, leveraging the deep contextual understanding learned by the model. However, due to their inherently high dimensionality, directly using these embeddings may introduce redundancy and hinder efficient classification [49]. To address this, PCA is applied to project the embeddings into a lower-dimensional latent space while preserving the most discriminative variance.

最后，减少的嵌入作为下游分类器的输入提供，该分类器经过训练以区分 CN 和 AD 参与者。分类器输出二元决策以及相关的置信度分数，指示 AD 存在的可能性。Finally, the reduced embeddings are provided as input to a downstream classifier, which is trained to distinguish between CN and AD participants. The classifier outputs a binary decision along with an associated confidence score, indicating the likelihood of AD presence.

4.1.实验装置4.1. Experimental setup

4.5. ASR研究4.5. ASR study

为了系统地检查 ASR 质量对下游 AD 分类的影响，我们评估了两种广泛采用且经过实证验证的 ASR 系统：Wav2Vec 2.0 和 Whisper，同时保持管道的所有其他组件（包括 LLM、PCA 配置和分类器）固定。选择这两个 ASR 模型是出于互补的原因。 Wav2Vec 2.0 是一种自我监督的 ASR 框架，通过直接从原始音频学习鲁棒的语音表示而不依赖于大型标记数据集，在不同的语音任务中表现出了强大的性能，特别是在资源匮乏和临床环境中 [69]。相比之下，Whisper 是一种大规模、弱监督的 ASR 模型，在大量多语言和多任务数据上进行训练，并且已被证明对自发临床语音中常见的说话者变异、背景噪音、口音和录音条件表现出卓越的稳健性 [70]。To systematically examine the impact of ASR quality on downstream AD classification, we evaluated two widely adopted and empirically validated ASR systems, Wav2Vec 2.0 and Whisper, while keeping all other components of the pipeline, including the LLM, PCA configuration, and classifiers, fixed. These two ASR models were selected for complementary reasons. Wav2Vec 2.0 is a self-supervised ASR framework that has demonstrated strong performance across diverse speech tasks, particularly in low-resource and clinical settings, by learning robust speech representations directly from raw audio without reliance on large labelled datasets [69]. In contrast, Whisper is a large-scale, weakly supervised ASR model trained on massive multilingual and multitask data, and has been shown to exhibit exceptional robustness to speaker variability, background noise, accents, and recording conditions commonly encountered in spontaneous clinical speech [70].

研究多个 ASR 系统的动机是双重的。首先，先前的研究表明，ASR 错误在说话者之间的分布并不均匀，可能会对认知受损的言语产生不成比例的影响，当依赖单一转录系统时，可能会给下游临床预测带来偏差或不稳定[71]。其次，在现实世界的临床部署中，ASR 系统可能因机构而异，随着时间的推移而发展，或者由于实际限制而被替换。因此，评估多个 ASR 可以对 LSEAD 在实际操作条件下的稳健性和可重复性进行重要评估。The motivation for investigating multiple ASR systems is twofold. First, prior studies have shown that ASR errors are not uniformly distributed across speakers and may disproportionately affect cognitively impaired speech, potentially introducing bias or instability into downstream clinical predictions when relying on a single transcription system [71]. Second, in real-world clinical deployment, ASR systems may vary across institutions, evolve over time, or be replaced due to practical constraints. Evaluating multiple ASRs, therefore, provides an important assessment of the robustness and reproducibility of LSEAD under realistic operational conditions.

这种分析对于基于文本的 AD 检测管道尤其重要，其中转录质量直接决定后续语言模型可用的语言信息。即使很小的转录差异也可以通过管道传播并影响提取的嵌入和最终分类结果 [69, 70]。因此，在不同的 ASR 系统中展示稳定的性能对于确保临床可靠性至关重要。This analysis is particularly critical for text-based AD detection pipelines, where transcription quality directly determines the linguistic information available to subsequent language models. Even minor transcription differences can propagate through the pipeline and influence the extracted embeddings and final classification outcomes [69, 70]. Demonstrating stable performance across different ASR systems is thus essential for ensuring clinical reliability.

如表 6 所示，Wav2Vec 2.0 和 Whisper 在所有评估的分类器中都具有出色的性能，这证实了所提出的框架并未与特定的 ASR 系统紧密耦合。通过 Wav2Vec 2.0 转录，LR 的准确率达到 88.2%，F1 达到 87.7%，优于 SVC、XGBoost 和 NN。这一结果与之前的研究结果一致，即自我监督的 ASR 模型产生语言连贯的转录本，非常适合下游临床语言分析 [69]。As shown in Table 6, both Wav2Vec 2.0 and Whisper yield strong performance across all evaluated classifiers, confirming that the proposed framework is not tightly coupled to a specific ASR system. With Wav2Vec 2.0 transcriptions, LR achieves 88.2% accuracy and 87.7% F1, outperforming SVC, XGBoost, and the NNs. This result is consistent with prior findings that self-supervised ASR models produce linguistically coherent transcripts that are well suited for downstream clinical language analysis [69].

基于 Whisper 的转录进一步提高了整体性能，特别是对于线性分类器。使用 Whisper，LR 在所有 ASR 分类器组合中取得了最好的结果，准确率为 90.0%，F1 分数为 89.7%，而 SVC 也受益匪浅，准确率达到 87.4%。这些收益可能归因于 Whisper 对说话者变异、背景噪声和异构录音条件的鲁棒性，这些在自发临床语音数据集中很常见 [70]。 ASR 阶段转录稳健性的提高有助于保留与早期认知障碍相关的微妙语言标记，从而实现更有效的嵌入提取和下游分类。Whisper-based transcriptions further improve overall performance, particularly for linear classifiers. Using Whisper, LR achieves the best results among all ASR-classifier combinations, with an accuracy of 90.0% and an F1 score of 89.7%, while SVC also benefits, reaching an accuracy of 87.4%. These gains are likely attributable to Whisper’s robustness to speaker variability, background noise, and heterogeneous recording conditions, which are common in spontaneous clinical speech datasets [70]. Improved transcription robustness at the ASR stage helps preserve subtle linguistic markers associated with early cognitive impairment, thereby enabling more effective embedding extraction and downstream classification.

5 结论和未来工作5. Conclusions and future work

本研究提出了 LSEAD，这是一种用于基于语音的 AD 筛选的隐私保护和可部署框架，它将 LLM 派生的文本嵌入与降维和轻量级分类集成在一起。通过多种评估设置下的广泛实验，所提出的系统表现出强大的性能、鲁棒性和泛化能力，凸显了其作为现实临床环境中非侵入性认知评估实用工具的潜力。This study presents LSEAD, a privacy-preserving and deployable framework for speech-based AD screening that integrates LLM-derived textual embeddings with dimensionality reduction and lightweight classification. Through extensive experiments under multiple evaluation settings, the proposed system demonstrates strong performance, robustness, and generalization capability, highlighting its potential as a practical tool for non-invasive cognitive assessment in real-world clinical environments.

通过利用预训练的法学硕士，LSEAD 捕获了与 AD 相关的微妙认知语言模式，而这些模式很难使用传统的手工制作或浅层特征来表示。 PCA 的结合通过减少特征冗余、抑制噪声和生成非常适合小样本临床数据集的紧凑表示，在提高系统稳定性方面发挥着关键作用。这种设计可以实现高效学习，同时保持强大的判别能力。By leveraging pretrained LLMs, LSEAD captures subtle cognitive-linguistic patterns associated with AD that are difficult to represent using traditional hand-crafted or shallow features. The incorporation of PCA plays a critical role in improving system stability by reducing feature redundancy, suppressing noise, and producing compact representations that are well suited for small-sample clinical datasets. This design enables efficient learning while maintaining strong discriminative capability.

在所有评估的分类器中，LR 在交叉验证和独立测试场景中始终实现最佳性能。该结果与降维特征空间一致，其中线性决策边界足以区分认知正常和 AD 语音模式。虽然 SVC 和 XGBoost 也表现出有竞争力的性能，但在相同条件下它们仍然略显逊色。相比之下，神经网络的有效性降低，可能是由于过度拟合和有限的数据可用性。这些发现表明，在临床实际数据限制内，精心设计的低复杂性模型可以提供可靠且稳健的性能。Across all evaluated classifiers, LR consistently achieves the best performance in both cross-validation and independent testing scenarios. This result aligns with the reduced-dimensional feature space, where linear decision boundaries are sufficient to separate cognitively normal and AD speech patterns. While SVC and XGBoost also demonstrate competitive performance, they remain slightly inferior under the same conditions. In contrast, NNs exhibit reduced effectiveness, likely due to overfitting and limited data availability. These findings suggest that, within clinically realistic data constraints, carefully designed low-complexity models can provide reliable and robust performance.

所提出的框架进一步展示了强大的跨数据集泛化能力，在 ADReSS20 和 ADReSSo2021 数据集上进行训练和测试时，仅观察到适度的性能下降。这表明学习到的表征捕获了稳定的与疾病相关的语言特征，这些特征能够适应队列组成和记录条件的变化。此外，对多个 ASR 系统的评估表明，LSEAD 在不同的转录模型中保持一致的性能，增强了其稳健性和在异构临床环境中部署的适用性。The proposed framework further demonstrates strong cross-dataset generalization, with only modest performance degradation observed when training and testing across ADReSS20 and ADReSSo2021 datasets. This indicates that the learned representations capture stable disease-related linguistic characteristics that are resilient to variations in cohort composition and recording conditions. In addition, the evaluation of multiple ASR systems shows that LSEAD maintains consistent performance across different transcription models, reinforcing its robustness and suitability for deployment in heterogeneous clinical environments.

尽管取得了这些有希望的结果，但仍应承认一些局限性。首先，该研究依赖于相对较小的基准数据集，可能无法完全捕捉现实世界患者群体的多样性，包括语言、方言和合并症的差异。其次，当前的框架仅关注从语音导出的文本表示，而没有结合可以提供额外诊断信息的补充声学特征。第三，所提出的框架目前缺乏可解释性，因为法学硕士衍生的嵌入和下游分类器的决策过程并不明确透明，这可能会限制临床信任和采用。Despite these promising results, several limitations should be acknowledged. First, the study relies on relatively small benchmark datasets, which may not fully capture the diversity of real-world patient populations, including variations in language, dialect, and comorbid conditions. Second, the current framework focuses exclusively on textual representations derived from speech, without incorporating complementary acoustic features that may provide additional diagnostic information. Third, the proposed framework currently lacks interpretability, as the decisionmaking process of the LLM-derived embeddings and downstream classifiers is not explicitly transparent, which may limit clinical trust and adoption.

未来的工作将通过将该框架扩展到更大、更多样化的数据集（包括多语言和自发对话语音）来解决这些限制，以进一步验证其普遍性。此外，还将研究整合声学和语言特征的多模态方法，以提高诊断性能。最后，提高可解释性仍然是临床采用的一个关键优先事项；未来的研究将侧重于可解释的人工智能技术，以识别驱动模型预测的语言标记，从而提高透明度，支持临床医生的信任，并促进融入临床决策工作流程。Future work will address these limitations by extending the framework to larger and more diverse datasets, including multilingual and spontaneous conversational speech, to further validate its generalizability. In addition, multimodal approaches that integrate acoustic and linguistic features will be investigated to enhance diagnostic performance. Finally, improving interpretability remains a key priority for clinical adoption; future research will focus on explainable AI techniques to identify the linguistic markers driving model predictions, thereby enhancing transparency, supporting clinician trust, and facilitating integration into clinical decision-making workflows.
