1 简介Introduction

视觉语言预训练从根本上重塑了表征学习的格局，超越了固定类别数据集上的监督学习。图像领域的开创性工作，特别是 CLIPVision-Language Pre-training has fundamentally reshaped the landscape of representation learning, moving beyond supervised learning on fixed category datasets. Seminal work in the image domain, notably CLIP[42]，展示了直接从自然语言监督中学习可转移视觉表示的力量。通过对从网络上收集的数亿个图像文本对采用对比学习目标，CLIP 学习到了能够跨不同视觉概念进行令人印象深刻的零样本泛化的表示，与传统的基于分类的预训练相比，显着拓宽了范围。这一成功激起了人们对将 VLP 扩展到视频领域的浓厚兴趣，视频领域自然更丰富，但实质上更复杂。, demonstrated the power of learning transferable visual representations directly from natural language supervision. By employing contrastive learning objectives on hundreds of millions of image-text pairs harvested from the web, CLIP learned representations capable of impressive zero-shot generalization across diverse visual concepts, significantly broadening the scope compared to traditional classification-based pre-training. This success spurred intense interest in extending VLP to the video domain, a naturally richer but substantially more complex modality.

大多数现有方法侧重于捕获整个视频与其标题之间的全局粗粒度对应关系Most existing approaches focus on capturing global, coarse-grained correspondences between an entire video and its caption[57, 4, 50, 62, 27, 49, 34, 66]，常常忽略细粒度的实例级语义。这留下了一个关键的差距：模型很难识别和区分文本中提到的特定对象或实体。例如，给定一个标题, often neglecting fine-grained instance-level semantics. This leaves a critical gap: models struggle to identify and distinguish specific objects or entities mentioned in text. For example, given a caption“孩子扔红球，狗跳”“a child throws a red ball while a dog jumps”，仅在全局对齐上训练的模型可能会掌握整个事件，但无法定位对应于哪个视觉区域, a model trained only on global alignments might grasp the overall event but fail to localize which visual region corresponds to the“球”“ball”或or the“狗”“dog”。实例级理解中的此类缺陷可能会限制下游任务的性能，这些任务需要视频中语言的精确基础，包括细粒度检索、时空基础和以对象为中心的问答。. Such shortcomings in instance-level understanding can limit performance on downstream tasks that require precise grounding of language in video, including fine-grained retrieval, spatial-temporal grounding, and object-centric question answering.

学习细粒度、实例感知的表示并非易事。一方面，大多数大规模视频文本数据集仅提供高级描述，缺乏学习实例-单词对应关系所需的基础注释。另一方面，普遍的预训练目标奖励整体视频文本对齐，几乎没有激励模型关注微妙的、特定于实例的细节。虽然最近的工作试图通过事后将实例级线索移植到模型上来解决这个问题，但它们通常依赖于预先训练的对象检测器Learning fine-grained, instance-aware representations is non-trivial. On one hand, most large-scale video-text datasets provide only high-level descriptions, lacking the grounded annotations necessary to learn instance-word correspondences. On the other hand, prevailing pre-training objectives reward holistic video-text alignment, providing little incentive for the model to attend to subtle, instance-specific details. While recent works have attempted to address this by grafting instance-level cues onto models post-hoc, they often rely on pre-trained object detectors[32, 69]或辅助专业负责人or auxiliary specialization heads[55, 65]。这些信号通常被视为辅助特征，而不是集成到核心表示学习中，继承检测器错误并且无法实现真正​​的实例级对齐。因此，实例感知视频预训练的通用且有效的解决方案仍然难以实现。. These signals are often treated as auxiliary features rather than being integrated into the core representation learning, inheriting detector errors and failing to achieve true instance-level alignment. Consequently, a general and effective solution for instance-aware video pre-training remains elusive.

6 结论Conclusion

我们介绍 InstAP，一个用于细粒度视频语言理解的实例感知预训练框架。 InstAP 基于具有双粒度注释的大规模 InstVL 数据集构建，通过实例感知对齐目标学习将文本置于特定的时空轨迹中。实验表明，它的收益来自训练范式，而不仅仅是来自数据，因为它始终优于在同一数据集上训练的强大基线。重要的是，这种实例级预训练还改善了全局表示，从而在标准基准上实现强大的泛化。总体而言，InstAP 推动了 VLP 模型在整体和实例层面上更深入地理解复杂的视觉场景。We introduce InstAP, an instance-aware pre-training framework for fine-grained video-language understanding. Built on the large-scale InstVL dataset with dual-granularity annotations, InstAP learns to ground text in specific spatial-temporal trajectories through an instance-aware alignment objective. Experiments show that its gains come from the training paradigm rather than from data alone, as it consistently outperforms strong baselines trained on the same dataset. Importantly, this instance-level pre-training also improves global representations, leading to strong generalization across standard benchmarks. Overall, InstAP advances VLP models toward more robust understanding of complex visual scenes at both holistic and instance levels.
