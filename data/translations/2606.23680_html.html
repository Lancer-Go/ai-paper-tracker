CoorDex：协调身体和手部先验以实现连续灵巧的人形机器人操作CoorDex: Coordinating Body and Hand Priors for Continuous Dexterous Humanoid Loco-Manipulation

Sikai Li1, Shuning Li1, Zhenyu Wei1, Yunchao Yao1, Chenran Li2, Mingyu Ding1
1University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill  2University of California, Berkeley
摘要Abstract

人形机器人的运动操纵通常被简化为一个走走停停的过程：走到一个物体前，停下来操纵它，然后恢复运动。它还通常依赖于低自由度 (DoF) 末端执行器，其行为类似于开闭抓取基元。我们介绍Humanoid loco-manipulation is often simplified into a stop-and-go process: walking to an object, stopping to manipulate it, and then resuming locomotion. It also commonly relies on low degree-of-freedom (DoF) end effectors that behave like an open-close grasp primitive. We introduce坐标索引CoorDex，一种学习管道，可将高维身体和灵巧手控制转换为协调的潜在残差控制，从而实现移动中的高自由度灵巧局部操纵。 从模拟全身和手部演示开始，CoorDex 为类人身体和灵巧手训练特权运动跟踪教师，将其提炼为本体感觉条件的潜在先验，并使用冻结的先验作为下游残差强化学习的动作空间。协调的潜在残差策略通过共享任务上下文和单独的身体-手残差头来组合这些先验，保留自然的全身运动，同时提高手指级接触可靠性。 CoorDex 使具有 20 自由度无极手的 Unitree G1 人形机器人能够在运动中执行灵巧的操作，包括不间断地抓握和搬运瓶子、在移动中打开冰箱门以及立方体拾取和转动。对行走-抓取-携带任务的消融表明，在相同的奖励预算下，关节空间 PPO、关节空间手控制和整体潜在预测都失败了，而潜在先验接口和协调残差结构使得高维接触丰富的局部操作可训练。项目页面：, a learning pipeline that converts high-dimensional body and dexterous hand control into coordinated latent residual control, enabling high-DoF dexterous loco-manipulation on the move. Starting from simulated whole-body and hand demonstrations, CoorDex trains privileged motion tracking teachers for the humanoid body and dexterous hand, distills them into proprioception-conditioned latent priors, and uses the frozen priors as the action space for downstream residual reinforcement learning. A coordinated latent residual policy composes these priors through shared task context and separate body-hand residual heads, preserving natural whole-body motion while improving finger-level contact reliability. CoorDex enables a Unitree G1 humanoid with a 20-DoF WUJI hand to execute dexterous manipulation while in motion, including non-stop bottle grasping and carrying, fridge door opening on the move, and cube pick-and-turn. Ablations on the walk-grasp-carry task show that joint-space PPO, joint-space hand control, and monolithic latent prediction all fail under the same reward budget, while the latent-prior interface and coordinated residual structure make high-dimensional contact-rich loco-manipulation trainable. 1 简介Introduction

配备高自由度灵巧手的人形机器人有望成为既可以在人类环境中导航又可以操纵日常物体的单一实施例。实现这一承诺需要的不仅仅是将末端执行器放置在目标附近。 机器人必须保持平衡，保持物体可触及，在接触前协调手腕和手指的姿势，在不干扰物体的情况下合上手，并在手与物体的相互作用扰乱全身动力学的同时继续运输物体。 直接在所有驱动关节上学习此类行为会产生具有挑战性的高维探索问题，其中运动和灵巧的手控制必须在整个执行过程中同时协调。Humanoid robots equipped with high-DoF dexterous hands promise a single embodiment that can both navigate human environments and manipulate everyday objects. Realizing this promise requires more than placing an end effector near a target. The robot must maintain balance, keep the object reachable, coordinate wrist and finger poses before contact, close the hand without disturbing the object, and continue transporting the object while hand-object interactions perturb whole-body dynamics. Learning such behaviors directly over all actuated joints creates a challenging high-dimensional exploration problem, where locomotion and dexterous hand control must be simultaneously coordinated throughout execution.

最近的进展已经推进了人形机器人操纵的补充部分。全身跟踪可实现稳定的运动和富有表现力的上半身运动Recent progress has advanced complementary parts of humanoid loco-manipulation. Whole-body tracking enables stable locomotion and expressive upper-body motion[24, 19, 22, 7, 3, 11, 9]。基于物理的先验减少了通过结构化潜在动作空间的探索. Physics-based priors reduce exploration through structured latent action spaces[26, 25, 31, 21, 30]。远程操作、剩余学习、分层控制、力自适应策略和视觉条件系统进一步改善任务级交互和模拟到真实的迁移. Teleoperation, residual learning, hierarchical control, force-adaptive policies, and vision-conditioned systems further improve task-level interaction and sim-to-real transfer[6, 5, 10, 47, 4, 14, 44, 29, 8, 39, 12]。然而，许多系统仍然使用以末端执行器为中心的、低维的或基于基元的操作界面，使得连续运动期间的高自由度多指接触仍然不太孤立和难以理解。. However, many systems still use end-effector-centric, low-dimensional, or primitive-based manipulation interfaces, leaving high-DoF multi-finger contact during continuous locomotion remains less isolated and less understood.

这种限制在移动灵巧的局部操作中尤其明显。 当前以手为中心的方法在抓取合成、手对象表示、跨实体迁移和演示生成方面取得了长足进步This limitation is especially pronounced in on-the-move dexterous loco-manipulation. Current hand-centric methods have made strong progress in grasp synthesis, hand-object representation, cross-embodiment transfer, and demonstration generation[34, 17, 42, 35, 36, 16, 13, brüdigam2024jactaversatileplannerlearning]。然而，他们经常假设手腕或手臂的轨迹是由远程操作、规划、固定底座设置或固定全身控制器提供的。 因此，现有的人形运动操纵系统通常被简化为一个走走停停的过程：走到一个物体处，停下来操纵它，然后恢复运动。 然而，在行走的人形机器人上，手腕姿势是由足部计时、根部运动、躯干姿势和全身伸展产生的。因此，之前还必须解释 6D 手腕运动的手将其大部分能力花费在身体一侧的放置上，而不是手指协调上。关键的挑战是在连续运动过程中分解和协调身体侧手腕放置与手侧手指运动。. Yet they often assume that the wrist or arm trajectory is provided by teleoperation, planning, a fixed-base setup, or a stationary whole-body controller. As a result, existing humanoid loco-manipulation systems are often simplified into a stop-and-go process: walking to an object, stopping to manipulate it, and then resuming locomotion. On a walking humanoid, however, the wrist pose emerges from foot timing, root motion, torso posture, and whole-body reaching. A hand prior that must also explain 6D wrist motion therefore spends much of its capacity on body-side placement rather than on finger coordination. The key challenge is to decompose and coordinate body-side wrist placement with hand-side finger motion during continuous locomotion.

It targets dexterous manipulation on the move, where wrist placement emerges from whole-body motion, by separating whole-body wrist placement from finger coordination, modeling them with a body prior and a wrist-stabilized hand prior, and coupling them through a coordinated latent residual policy. Specifically, CoorDex first constructs separate latent priors for the humanoid body and dexterous hand. Each prior is trained from a privileged motion tracking teacher and distilled into a proprioception conditioned student prior and decoder using a variational bottleneck[21]。 身体优先支持运动、伸展和手腕放置，而手腕稳定的手优先仅控制活动的手指关节并捕获可重复使用的手指协调。这种因式分解用对两个紧凑潜在空间的剩余控制取代了直接的全关节控制，将身体侧的放置与手侧的灵活性分开，同时保留了它们的下游协调。. The body prior supports locomotion, reaching, and wrist placement, while the wrist-stabilized hand prior controls only the active finger joints and captures reusable finger coordination. This factorization replaces direct full-joint control with residual control over two compact latent spaces, separating body-side placement from hand-side dexterity while preserving their downstream coordination.

为了组成这些先验，CoorDex 使用协调的潜在残差策略。在每个控制步骤中，冻结的身体和手部先验首先将它们各自的本体感觉编码为潜在手段。这些手段与对象相关的几何形状、接触特征和当前本体感受状态一起被传递到共享协调主干以产生任务级协调特征。然后，两个残差头预测身体和手部潜在校正，在解码之前将其添加到相应的先验平均值中。身体残差调整迈步、躯干运动、伸展和手腕放置，而手残差则细化手指预成型、闭合和接触。该设计通过共享任务状态将两个子系统耦合起来，而无需将它们折叠成单个整体动作头。它保留了全身运动和手指灵活性之间的结构分离。To compose these priors, CoorDex uses a coordinated latent residual policy. At each control step, the frozen body and hand priors first encode their respective proprioception into latent means. These means, together with object-relative geometry, contact features, and the current proprioceptive state, are passed to a shared coordination trunk to produce a task-level coordination feature. Two residual heads then predict body and hand latent corrections, which are added to the corresponding prior means before decoding. The body residual adjusts stepping, torso motion, reaching, and wrist placement, whereas the hand residual refines finger preshape, closure, and contact. This design couples the two subsystems through shared task state without collapsing them into a single monolithic action head. It preserves the structural separation between whole-body motion and finger-level dexterity.

5 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

我们推出了 CoorDex，一种用于灵巧局部操作的模块化管道。 CoorDex 没有将运动和操作视为两个独立的阶段，而是为人形身体和灵巧的手提供了一个结构化的潜在动作空间。在这个空间内，下游强化学习可以共同适应迈步、手腕放置和手指接触。在瓶子抓取和携带、冰箱门打开和立方体拾取和转动方面，结果表明，灵巧的人形技能受益于提供任务相关运动支持的先验知识，以及保留身体手协调结构的策略。这为未来的机器人操纵系统指明了一个实用的方向。可重复使用的运动先验应该与任务感知的潜在协调相结合，将全身活动性和灵巧接触视为耦合行为而不是缝合在一起的模块。We present CoorDex, a modular pipeline for dexterous loco-manipulation. Rather than treating locomotion and manipulation as two separate phases, CoorDex exposes a structured latent action space for the humanoid body and the dexterous hand. Within this space, downstream RL can jointly adapt stepping, wrist placement, and finger-level contact. Across bottle grasping and carrying, fridge door opening, and cube pick-and-turn, the results indicate that dexterous humanoid skills benefit from priors that provide task-relevant motion support, and from policies that preserve the structure of body-hand coordination. This points to a practical direction for future loco-manipulation systems. Reusable motion priors should be paired with task-aware latent coordination, treating whole-body mobility and dexterous contact as coupled behaviors rather than stitched-together modules.

The experiments also focus on a fixed G1 platform with a WUJI hand, and broader deployment will require evaluation across more objects, hands, and hardware configurations.
