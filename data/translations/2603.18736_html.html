1 简介1 Introduction

在大语言模型（LLM）时代，人类反馈强化学习（RLHF）已成为使LLM与人类价值观保持一致的基石技术（Ouyang et al., 2022），并在当前人工智能系统中得到广泛应用（Li et al., 2026a,b; Wang et al., 2026），例如ChatGPT（Achiam et al., 2023）、Gemini（Comanici et al., 2026）。 2025）和 DeepSeek（Guo 等人，2025）。大多数 RLHF 策略采用两阶段流程：首先，根据人类反馈数据训练奖励模型以近似用户偏好；其次，通过强化学习（RL）优化策略模型（即LLM）以最大化估计奖励。此过程的核心挑战是开发一个准确捕获用户偏好的奖励模型，因为任何错误指定都会直接误导 RL 阶段并产生次优的对齐性能（Wang 等人，2024a；Chen 等人，2024）。In the era of large language models (LLMs), reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) has emerged as a cornerstone technique for aligning LLMs with human values (Ouyang et al., 2022), with widespread application in current AI systems (Li et al., 2026a,b; Wang et al., 2026), such as ChatGPT (Achiam et al., 2023), Gemini (Comanici et al., 2025), and DeepSeek (Guo et al., 2025). Most RLHF strategies adopt a two-stage pipeline: first, a reward model is trained on human feedback data to approximate user preferences; second, the policy model (i.e., the LLM) is optimized by reinforcement learning (RL) to maximize the estimated rewards. The central challenge in this process is developing a reward model that accurately captures user preferences, as any misspecification directly misleads the RL stage and yields suboptimal alignment performance (Wang et al., 2024a; Chen et al., 2024).

In contrast, observational feedback data (e.g., user clicks, upvotes, copies, and regenerates) offers a compelling alternative (Wang et al., 2025c; Li et al., 2024a). They are passively gathered from user interactions with deployed AI systems and thus requires no explicit labeling effort (Liu et al., 2025b), rendering them more abundant and low-cost than experimental data (Wang et al., 2025b).
