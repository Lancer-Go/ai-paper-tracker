实时多方语音代理的自适应轮流Adaptive Turn-Taking for Real-time Multi-Party Voice Agents

摘要Abstract

对于基于语音的座席来说，多方语音对话中的轮流仍然是一个基本挑战，特别是在动态的发言权竞争和不同的用户期望下。我们建议Turn-taking in multi-party spoken conversations remains a fundamental challenge for voice-based agents, particularly under dynamic floor competition and varying user expectations. We propose版主LMModeratorLM，一种角色扮演语音代理，可在多方设置中对明确分配的角色进行轮流行为。该系统建立在以块式流方式运行的语音大语言模型之上。我们进一步引入了一种推理增强变体，它结合了对对话上下文和指定角色的思想链推理。我们构建, a role-playing voice agent that conditions turn-taking behavior on an explicitly assigned role in multi-party settings. The system is built on a speech large language model operating in chunk-wise streaming manner. We further introduce a reasoning-augmented variant that incorporates chain-of-thought reasoning over conversational context and the assigned role. We construct角色扮演会议RolePlayConv，具有不同助理角色的多方口头对话的大规模综合数据集。真实世界会议数据的实验, a large-scale synthetic dataset of spoken multi-party conversations with diverse assistant roles. Experiments on real-world meeting data and角色扮演会议RolePlayConv与非角色条件基线相比，轮流精确度提高了 40% 以上，召回率提高了 70% 以上，同时大大减少了误报中断。show improved turn-taking precision by over 40% and recall by more than 70%, while substantially reducing false-positive interruptions compared to non-role-conditioned baselines.

1 简介Introduction

大语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展推动了基于语音的会话代理的开发取得快速进展Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have driven rapid progress in the development of voice-based conversational agents[wang2024freeze, xie2024mini, chen2025minmo, ding2025kimi]。现代口语对话系统通常将低延迟流式语音处理模块与负责对话管理和响应生成的核心会话组件相结合。能够同时听和说的全双工代理的出现，通过支持更自然的交互模式（例如反向通道响应和用户打断）进一步先进了基于语音的会话系统. Modern spoken dialogue systems typically combine low-latency streaming speech processing modules with a core conversational component responsible for dialogue management and response generation. The emergence of full-duplex agents, capable of listening and speaking simultaneously, has further advanced voice-based conversational systems by enabling more natural interaction patterns such as backchannel responses and user barge-ins[defossez2024moshi, ma2025language, yu2024salmonn]。 在二元（两方）对话中，轮流通常由声音和语义提示控制，例如停顿持续时间、沉默检测和句子完成标记。最近的基准已经正式化了二元环境中轮流行为的评估. In dyadic (two-party) conversations, turn-taking is commonly governed by acoustic and semantic cues such as pause duration, silence detection, and sentence completion markers. Recent benchmarks have formalized the evaluation of turn-taking behavior in dyadic settings[arora2025talking, lin2025full]。然而，这些假设并不能很好地推广到多方对话，其中交互涉及重叠语音、动态发言权竞争以及参与者之间协商的轮次分配. However, these assumptions do not generalize well to multi-party conversations, where interactions involve overlapping speech, dynamic floor competition, and negotiated turn allocation among participants[sapkota2025multi]。在这种情况下，对话代理必须不断地决定，不仅. In such settings, a conversational agent must continuously decide not only当when说话，也to speak, but also是否whether根本不干预。 与此同时，用户对对话代理的期望因环境而异，从被动的倾听者到主动的促进者to intervene at all. At the same time, user expectations for conversational agents vary across contexts, ranging from passive listeners to active facilitators[zhou2020design]。这推动了角色扮演语言代理（RLPA）的研究，其中助手采用明确的角色或角色. This has motivated research on role-playing language agents (RLPA), where assistants adopt explicit personas or roles[chen2024persona, wang2024rolellm]。之前的工作主要集中在基于文本的对话设置、语言风格建模. Prior work has primarily focused on text-based dialogue settings, modeling linguistic style[zhou2024characterglm], 性格特征, character traits[ran2024capturing]和决策模式, and decision-making patterns[xu2024character]。然而，在基于语音的多方交互中，指定角色对实时轮流行为的影响在很大程度上被忽视了。. However, the effect of an assigned role on real-time turn-taking behavior in voice-based, multi-party interaction has been largely overlooked.

在这项工作中，我们介绍In this work, we introduce版主LMModeratorLM，一种用于多方对话的角色扮演语音代理，它根据明确分配的角色来决定轮流决策和生成响应。我们的系统建立在语音法学硕士的基础上，该法学硕士以块式流方式处理多说话者音频流和相应的文本假设。据我们所知，这是第一个专为多方对话设置而设计的角色调节语音代理。为了支持培训，我们使用现成的法学硕士和文本转语音 (TTS) 模型构建了一个涵盖不同助理角色的大规模多方对话合成语料库。实验结果表明, a role-playing voice agent for multi-party conversations that conditions both turn-taking decisions and response generation on an explicitly assigned role. Our system is built on a speech LLM that processes multi-speaker audio streams and corresponding text hypotheses in chunk-wise streaming manner. To our knowledge, this is the first role-conditioned voice agent designed for multi-party conversational settings. To support training, we construct a large-scale synthetic corpus of multi-party conversations spanning diverse assistant roles using off-the-shelf LLMs and a text-to-speech (TTS) model. Experimental results show that版主LMModeratorLM与非角色条件基线相比，可以更好地与分配的角色保持一致，从而改善角色一致的轮流和响应生成。我们进一步介绍achieves substantially better alignment with the assigned roles than non-role-conditioned baselines, improving both role-consistent turn-taking and response generation. We further introduce版主LM-ThinkModeratorLM-Think，其中结合了思维链推理, which incorporates chain-of-thought reasoning[wei2022chain]在对话上下文和分配的角色上，在角色一致的轮流行为方面产生额外的改进。over conversational context and the assigned role, yielding additional improvements in in role-aligned turn-taking behavior.

2 方法论Methodology

2.1 主持人LM：系统架构ModeratorLM: System Architecture

版主LMModeratorLM由语音编码器和主干LLM组成。语音编码器独立处理每个传入的音频块并产生块级嵌入。根据之前的工作，这些嵌入通过可训练的线性投影层投影到 LLM 嵌入空间中consists of a speech encoder and a backbone LLM. The speech encoder processes each incoming audio chunk independently and produces chunk-level embeddings. These embeddings are projected into the LLM embedding space via a trainable linear projection layer, following prior work[pandey2025sift, jia2025efficient]。多通道音频输入在编码之前被缩混为单通道信号。 为了支持实时交互，块级语音嵌入以流方式顺序附加到 LLM 上下文，类似于. The multi-channel audio inputs are downmixed into a single-channel signal before encoding. To support real-time interaction, chunk-level speech embeddings are appended sequentially to the LLM context in a streaming fashion, similar to[ding2025kimi, zhang2024omniflatten, xu2025qwen2]。然而，与依赖单独的语音活动检测模块来确定转弯边界的方法不同. However, unlike approaches that rely on a separate voice activity detection module to determine turn boundaries[ding2025kimi]，我们的系统将轮流决策完全委托给演讲法学硕士本身。为了提高对块持续时间变化的鲁棒性，我们采用动态大小的训练块。对于每个块，带有说话者注释的相应文本转录与语音嵌入一起提供作为 LLM 的输入。, our system delegates turn-taking decisions entirely to the speech LLM itself. To improve robustness to variability in chunk duration, we adopt dynamically sized training chunks. For each chunk, the corresponding text transcription with speaker annotations is provided alongside the speech embeddings as input to the LLM.

We first curate 125 detailed assistant roles (e.g., ``a 42-year-old Indian CEO with a confident and assertive communication style who enjoys strategic planning and mentoring''), specifying attributes such as conversational style and tone. 5 结论Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们引入了一种用于多方对话的角色扮演语音代理，它根据分配的角色来调节轮流行为。实验结果表明，角色条件微调可以产生更符合配置偏好的轮流决策，并且结合思想链推理进一步提高角色保真度。我们的分析还强调了训练期间动态分块的重要性；如果没有它，模型往往会过度适应块长度而不是对话上下文。未来的工作可能会探索全双工多方语音代理和同时听和说下的轮流。In this work, we introduced a role-playing voice agent for multi-party conversations that modulates turn-taking behavior based on an assigned role. Experimental results show that role-conditioned fine-tuning yields turn-taking decisions better aligned with configured preferences, and that incorporating chain-of-thought reasoning further improves role fidelity. Our analysis also highlights the importance of dynamic chunking during training; without it, models tend to overfit to chunk length rather than conversational context. Future work may explore full-duplex multi-party voice agents and turn-taking under simultaneous listening and speaking.
