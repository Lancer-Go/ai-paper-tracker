摘要Abstract

虽然基于流的生成模型在广泛的领域中表现出了强大的性能，但由于严格的约束要求，将它们部署在安全关键的物理系统中仍然具有挑战性。现有的方法通常通过事后校正来加强安全性，这会产生大量的计算开销，并且可能会扭曲学习到的分布。我们建议While flow-based generative models have demonstrated strong performance across a wide range of domains, deploying them in safety-critical physical systems remains challenging due to strict constraint requirements. Existing approaches typically enforce safety through post-hoc corrections, which incur substantial computational overhead and may distort the learned distribution. We propose多流PolyFlow，一个多面体约束流匹配框架，将约束直接嵌入到模型和流动力学中。 PolyFlow 引入了离散时间流公式和无投影架构，消除了离散化误差并保证严格满足任意多面体约束，而不需要昂贵的迭代求解器。实验结果表明，PolyFlow 实现了零约束违规，同时在一系列规划和控制任务中保持高分布保真度。与最先进的约束生成基线相比，PolyFlow 显着减少了推理延迟，并展示了安全性、效率和生成质量之间的有利权衡。, a polytope-constrained flow matching framework that embeds constraints directly into the model and flow dynamics. PolyFlow introduces a discrete-time flow formulation and a projection-free architecture, which eliminate the discretization error and guarantee strict satisfaction of arbitrary polyhedral constraints, without the need for expensive iterative solvers. Experimental results show that PolyFlow achieves zero constraint violation while maintaining high distributional fidelity across a range of planning and control tasks. Compared to state-of-the-art constrained generation baselines, PolyFlow significantly reduces inference latency and demonstrates a favorable trade-off between safety, efficiency, and generative quality.

1 简介Introduction

基于流的生成模型在高保真图像生成等广泛领域中表现出了卓越的性能和灵活性Flow-based generative models have demonstrated remarkable performance and flexibility across a wide range of fields, ranging from high-fidelity image generation(Lipman et al., 2023)和视频合成and video synthesis(Jin et al., 2025)复杂的决策任务to complex decision-making tasks(Zheng et al., 2023)。这些进步促使人们越来越多地转向在根深蒂固于物理世界的领域（例如科学发现）部署这些强大的模型. These advances have motivated a growing transition toward deploying these powerful models in domains deeply rooted in the physical world, such as scientific discovery(Chen and Lipman, 2024), 轨迹规划, trajectory planning(Tan et al., 2025)，和机器人控制, and robotic control(Black et al., 2024).

与传统的无约束生成任务不同，物理应用面临着根本性的挑战：Unlike traditional unconstrained generation tasks, physical applications are confronted with a fundamental challenge:安全safety和and约束满足constraint satisfaction。例如，在机器人规划中，环境受到严格的几何和动态约束的控制，包括关节限制、避障. While an out-of-distribution pixel in image generation merely results in a visual artifact, a constraint violation in physical system can lead to hardware damage or catastrophic failure. This disparity highlights the need for generative models with explicit safety and constraint awareness.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了PolyFlow，一种样本高效的流匹配框架，旨在为约束生成建模提供严格的安全保证。通过重新表达离散时间的流动动力学并结合无投影架构，所提出的方法消除了数值离散误差和事后投影的计算负担。In this work, we introduce PolyFlow, a sample-efficient flow matching framework designed to provide strict safety guarantees for constrained generative modeling. By reformulating flow dynamics in discrete time and incorporating a projection-free architecture, the proposed method eliminates numerical discretization errors and the computational burden of post-hoc projections.

虽然 PolyFlow 表现出强大的性能，但它也受到一些限制。目前，该框架要求将约束表示为凸多面体。此外，它需要初始分布位于可行区域内，这会在采样期间产生额外的计算开销。进一步将 PolyFlow 推广到非凸约束几何形状（例如通过凸分解策略）仍然是未来工作的一个重要且有前途的方向。While PolyFlow demonstrates strong performance, it is subject to several limitations. Currently, the framework requires constraints to be formulated as convex polytopes. Additionally, it necessitates that the initial distribution resides within the feasible region, which incurs additional computational overhead during sampling. Further generalizing PolyFlow to non-convex constraint geometries—such as through convex decomposition strategies—remains an important and promising direction for future work.
