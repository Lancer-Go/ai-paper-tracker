摘要Abstract

平衡序列长度、表征能力和长范围稳定性是自回归 (AR) 语音和音频生成中的核心问题。具有更高帧速率或更大容量的表示可以保留更多信号细节，但它们也使流生成更容易受到分布漂移和 AR 误差积累的影响。相反，更短和更压缩的表示简化了 AR 建模，但其有限的带宽可能会丢弃重要的组件并限制重建保真度和生成质量的上限。我们询问是否可以与流生成框架共同设计低帧率、高维、高带宽连续表示，以支持鲁棒的高保真重建、强大的单标记可预测性和卓越的长范围稳定性。我们将这个目标分解为两个耦合问题：高维表示空间应该具有哪些几何和统计特性，以及 AR 连续令牌生成器应该如何构造以抵抗错误累积。据此，我们建议Balancing sequence length, representational capacity, and long-horizon stability is a central problem in autoregressive (AR) speech and audio generation. Representations with higher frame rates or greater capacity can preserve more signal detail, but they also make streaming generation more vulnerable to distribution drift and AR error accumulation. Conversely, shorter and more compressed representations simplify AR modeling, but their limited bandwidth may discard important components and constrain the upper bound of reconstruction fidelity and generation quality. We ask whether a low-frame-rate, high-dimensional, high-bandwidth continuous representation can be co-designed with a streaming generation framework to support robust high-fidelity reconstruction, strong single-token predictability, and superior long-horizon stability. We decompose this goal into two coupled problems: what geometric and statistical properties a high-dimensional representation space should have, and how an AR continuous-token generator should be structured to resist error accumulation. Accordingly, we propose洛编解码器Locodec，一种本地编码的编解码器，它塑造其表示空间，以提高低维核心流形的可插值性和本地高维坐标的可识别性，从而提高高维高带宽令牌的可预测性。我们还建议, a locally encoded codec that shapes its representation space to improve the interpolatability of a lower-dimensional core manifold and the identifiability of the native high-dimensional coordinates, thereby improving the predictability of high-dimensional high-bandwidth tokens. We also propose电致发光二极管MP-ELD，一个单令牌 AR 流匹配框架，使用多路径信息路由和无残差分类器指导来减轻错误累积。实验与, a single-token AR flow-matching framework that uses multi-path information routing and residual classifier-free guidance to mitigate error accumulation. 1 简介Introduction

音频是人机交互最自然的界面之一，执行稳定的流理解和生成的能力对于现代音频和多模态基础模型变得越来越重要Audio is one of the most natural interfaces for human–machine interaction, and the ability to perform stable streaming understanding and generation has become increasingly important for modern audio and multimodal foundation models[wu2023next, hurst2024gpt, gemmateam2026gemma4technicalreport]。从此类系统的输入端来看，保留更多信息的表示通常为下游理解提供了更高的上限。如果表示已经通过积极的有损压缩丢弃了与任务相关的线索，则后续模型无法可靠地恢复它们。从这个意义上说，丰富或近乎完整的表示允许模型了解哪些因素对给定任务有用，而不是强迫它对可能不包含这些因素的表示进行操作。然而，从输出方面来看，对高信息表示的相同偏好会产生不同的困难。更高带宽或更高维度的目标空间通常更难预测，自回归 (AR) 系统中的预测误差会作为未来上下文反馈。因此，小的局部预测误差可能会随着时间的推移而累积，导致响度、音色、语速或频谱质量的漂移，在严重的情况下会导致音频输出不稳定或崩溃。这造成了一种基本的紧张：一般理解和高保真重建所需的表示可能很难用作稳定的生成目标。因此，平衡表示能力、AR 稳定性和计算效率是流音频系统的核心设计问题。. From the input side of such systems, a representation that preserves more information generally provides a higher ceiling for downstream understanding. If the representation has already discarded task-relevant cues through aggressive lossy compression, no subsequent model can recover them reliably. In this sense, rich or near-complete representations allow the model to learn which factors are useful for a given task, rather than forcing it to operate on a representation that may not contain those factors in the first place. From the output side, however, the same preference for high-information representations creates a different difficulty. A higher-bandwidth or higher-dimensional target space is usually harder to predict, and prediction errors in an autoregressive (AR) system are fed back as future context. Small local prediction errors may therefore accumulate over time, leading to drift in loudness, timbre, speaking rate, or spectral quality, and in severe cases to unstable or collapsed audio outputs. This creates a basic tension: representations that are desirable for general understanding and high-fidelity reconstruction can be difficult to use as stable generation targets. Balancing representation capacity, AR stability, and computational efficiency is therefore a central design problem in streaming audio systems.

随着文本语言模型和音文本数据集的不断改进，转录、字幕和其他面向理解的能力在大型音频模型中取得了稳步进展As text language models and audio–text datasets continue to improve, transcription, captioning, and other understanding-oriented capabilities have made steady progress in large audio models[chu2023qwen, defossez2024moshi, ding2025kimi, zhang2025mimo, wu2025step, xu2025qwen3, ghosh2026audio, yang2026moss]。相比之下，稳定高效的高保真流生成仍然是一个瓶颈：它直接决定了人机交互的感知质量，也影响了闭环系统中后续的机器端理解。随着交互场景从秒级响应转变为分钟或小时级会话，而实际上下文长度和计算预算仍然有限，该问题变得更加明显。理想情况下，人们希望使用一种表示形式，其信息量足以支持高保真重建，足够短以减少 AR 范围，并且足够简单以无需昂贵的本地预测器或后处理模块即可进行建模。然而，这些要求似乎形成了一个不利的三角：高信息含量、低AR误差累积和低模型复杂度很难同时满足。. By contrast, stable and efficient high-fidelity streaming generation remains a bottleneck: it directly determines the perceptual quality of human-facing interaction, and it also affects subsequent machine-side understanding in closed-loop systems. The problem becomes more pronounced as interactive scenarios move from second-scale responses to minute- or hour-scale sessions, while practical context length and computation budgets remain finite. Ideally, one would like to use a representation that is informative enough to support high-fidelity reconstruction, short enough to reduce the AR horizon, and simple enough to model without expensive local predictors or post-processing modules. These requirements, however, seem to form an unfavorable triangle: high information content, low AR error accumulation, and low model complexity are difficult to satisfy simultaneously.

本文探讨是否可以通过联合设计表示空间和AR生成模型来放松这个三角形。受到原始信号空间预测最新进展的推动This paper explores whether this triangle can be relaxed by jointly designing the representation space and the AR generative model. Motivated by recent progress in raw signal-space prediction[wang2025pixnerd, li2026back]、表示空间分析, representation-space analysis[yu2024representation, zheng2025diffusion]、AR流量匹配系统, and AR flow-matching systems[li2024autoregressive, jia2025ditar]，我们研究了一种低帧率、高维连续令牌公式及其相应的 AR 生成框架。我们不依赖外部预训练的自监督学习（SSL）或自动语音识别（ASR）模型来定义“语义”空间作为塑造表示空间的监督信号，而是训练一个重建优先的分词器，其潜在几何形状是直接塑造的。得到的分词器，, we study a low-frame-rate, high-dimensional continuous-token formulation together with its corresponding AR generation framework. Instead of relying on externally pretrained self-supervised learning (SSL) or automatic speech recognition (ASR) models to define a “semantic” space as a supervision signal for shaping the representation space, we train a reconstruction-first tokenizer whose latent geometry is shaped directly. The resulting tokenizer,洛编解码器Locodec，产生球形连续标记，其高维空间围绕较低维空间组织, produces spherical continuous tokens whose high-dimensional space is organized around a lower-dimensional可插值的interpolatable核心流形，同时鼓励其原生坐标开发一个能量层次结构，以改善每个代币core manifold, while its native coordinates are encouraged to develop an energy hierarchy that improves per-token可识别性identifiability。这种设计的目的是保持高容量表示的带宽和重构上限，同时使生成模型的单标记预测变得更加容易。. This design aims to keep the bandwidth and reconstruction ceiling of a high-capacity representation, while making single-token prediction substantially easier for the generative model.

为了解决高信息 AR 生成中剩余的长视野不稳定性，我们进一步研究了引导流合成期间如何出现错误累积。我们的经验观察表明，累积漂移的一个主要来源是无分类器引导（CFG）下的不稳定性，特别是当部分重叠的声学线索由不同的引导路径携带并且不一致地放大时。我们的目标不是在全球范围内压制指导，而是通过鼓励不同类型的信息通过功能不同的途径传递来减少此类冲突。因此我们建议To address the remaining long-horizon instability of high-information AR generation, we further examine how error accumulation arises during guided streaming synthesis. Our empirical observations suggest that a major source of accumulated drift is instability under classifier-free guidance (CFG), especially when partially overlapping acoustic cues are carried by different guidance paths and are amplified inconsistently. Rather than suppressing guidance globally, we aim to reduce such conflicts by encouraging different types of information to be routed through functionally distinct pathways. We therefore propose电致发光二极管MP-ELD，用于 AR 流匹配的多路径编码器 - LM - 解码器框架。通过显式信息路由和训练时路径丢失，MP-ELD 鼓励音频状态的不同方面由不同的调节路径表示。这允许将指导用作结构化残差校正，以便可以单独加强不同的因素，例如声学一致性和外部条件对准。在实践中，即使没有预先训练的文本语言模型提供强大的语义归纳偏差，这种设计也允许从头开始训练生成模型，以形成可区分的声学状态和对齐相关路径。这种分离使得独立控制它们的 CFG 音阶成为可能，从而减轻累积的声学漂移并提高长格式合成的稳定性。, a multi-path encoder–LM–decoder framework for AR flow matching. Through explicit information routing and training-time path dropout, MP-ELD encourages different aspects of the audio state to be represented by different conditioning pathways. This allows guidance to be applied as structured residual corrections, so that different factors, such as acoustic consistency and external-condition alignment, can be strengthened separately. In practice, even without pretrained text language models providing a strong semantic inductive bias, this design allows a generative model trained from scratch to form distinguishable acoustic-state and alignment-related pathways. This separation makes it possible to control their CFG scales independently, thereby mitigating accumulated acoustic drift and improving stability in long-form synthesis.

2 主要假设和设计原则Main Assumptions and Design Principles

在本节中，我们将重新审视标记器和生成模型的几种现有设计选择，讨论它们的优点和局限性，然后激发指导我们整体设计的原则。In this section, we revisit several existing design choices for tokenizers and generative models, discuss their advantages and limitations, and then motivate the principles that guide our overall design.

2.1 帧速率、比特率和重建保真度Frame rate, bitrate, and reconstruction fidelity

由于音频信号的性质，音频处理长期以来面临着对极长序列进行建模的挑战Due to the nature of audio signals, audio processing has long been confronted with the challenge of modeling extremely long sequences[van2016wavenet, mehri2016samplernn, huang2018music, narayanan2019recognizing, dhariwal2020jukebox, luo2020dual, mao2020speech, battenberg2020location, shi2021emformer, raj2021integration, han2021continuous, evans2024long, ning2025diffrhythm, yuan2025yue]。对于固定的音频持续时间，令牌序列长度由令牌帧速率决定，令牌帧速率直接控制每个令牌覆盖的时间跨度和粒度。与每个令牌的信息容量一起，还决定了单位时间内可以分配的信息量，即带宽。虽然标记器和生成模型设计已针对中等比特率配置进行了广泛探索. For a fixed audio duration, the token sequence length is determined by the token frame rate, which directly controls the temporal span and granularity covered by each token. Together with the information capacity of each token, it also determines the amount of information that can be allocated per unit time, i.e., the bandwidth. While tokenizer and generative model designs have been extensively explored for moderate bitrate configurations[defossez2022high, kumar2023high, wang2023neural, chen2024vall, zhang2024speechtokenizer, defossez2024moshi, ji2025wavtokenizer, liao2026fish]近年来的一个主要研究方向是在大幅降低帧速率和比特率设置的情况下实现尽可能高的重建保真度, a major line of research in recent years has focused on achieving the highest possible reconstruction fidelity under aggressively reduced frame rate and bitrate settings[du2024cosyvoice, guo2024fireredtts, ju2024naturalspeech, jiang2025diffrhythm, parker2025scaling, wang2026scaling]。降低帧率是降低下游序列模型复杂度最直接的方法之一，同时降低码率可以进一步简化生成建模问题，降低计算成本。例如，替换残差矢量量化（RVQ）. Lowering the frame rate is one of the most direct ways to reduce the complexity of the downstream sequence model, while lowering the bitrate can further simplify the generative modeling problem and reduce computational cost. For example, replacing residual vector quantization (RVQ)[zeghidour2021soundstream]采用单级矢量量化 (VQ)with single-stage vector quantization (VQ)[van2017neural]消除了对残差层次结构进行建模的需要，从而减少了所需的计算和有效的参数预算。removes the need to model a residual hierarchy, thereby reducing both the required computation and the effective parameter budget.

然而，从无损压缩的角度来看，低比特率和高重建保真度从根本上来说很难，而且往往不可能同时实现。因此，现有的分词器通常仅保留最重要信号属性的子集，例如内容、音色或音高，同时（有意或无意）丢弃更难以忠实保留的其他因素。在这样的训练目标下，许多低比特率标记器通常更接近于However, from the perspective of lossless compression, low bitrate and high reconstruction fidelity are fundamentally difficult, and often impossible, to achieve simultaneously. As a result, existing tokenizers typically preserve only a subset of the most important signal attributes, such as content, timbre, or pitch, while (intentionally or unintentionally) discarding other factors that are more difficult to retain faithfully. Under such a training objective, many low-bitrate tokenizers are often closer to a再合成resynthesis制度胜于严格system than to a strict编解码器codec。因此，在某些机制或任务中，例如高保真生成或本地编辑，性能上限可能直接受到分词器比特率和相应训练范例的约束。. Consequently, in certain regimes or tasks, such as high-fidelity generation or local editing, the upper bound of performance may be directly constrained by the tokenizer bitrate and by the corresponding training paradigm.

相反，当人们为了提高离散令牌的信息容量而增加比特率时，例如通过更深的 RVQ 层次结构或更大的有限标量量化 (FSQ) 比特率Conversely, when one increases bitrate in order to improve the information capacity of discrete tokens, for instance via deeper RVQ hierarchies or larger finite scalar quantization (FSQ) bitrates[mentzer2024finite]，由此产生的潜在空间逐渐变得更接近于连续的。从建模的角度来看，当 RVQ 等离散标记化方案使用许多残差级别时，生成模型必须在预测期间分层展开这些级别，例如通过延迟模式建模, the resulting latent space gradually becomes closer to a continuous one. From the modeling perspective, when a discrete tokenization scheme such as RVQ uses many residual levels, and the generative model must unroll these levels hierarchically during prediction, e.g., via delay-pattern modeling[copet2023simple]或残差量化变换器 (RQTransformer)or residual-quantization transformers (RQTransformer)[lee2022autoregressive]，模型复杂度大幅增加。因此，最近的工作越来越多地探索连续表示作为实现更高带宽建模的手段, the model complexity increases substantially. Recent work has therefore increasingly explored continuous representations as a means of enabling higher-bandwidth modeling[eskimez2024e2, liu2024autoregressive, peng2025vibevoice, meng2025autoregressive, chen2025f5, xin2026longcat]。一方面，在相同的模型架构、帧速率和标记维度下，只需完全删除量化步骤，通常就可以显着提高重建保真度。另一方面，从迭代预测的角度来看，高比特率离散标记化中的残差层次在某种程度上类似于基于流的连续生成中的迭代去噪或函数评估过程。从这个意义上说，一旦比特率充分提高，连续标记化就成为自然的建模方向。. On the one hand, under the same model architecture, frame rate, and token dimensionality, one can often significantly improve reconstruction fidelity simply by removing the quantization step altogether. On the other hand, from the perspective of iterative prediction, the residual hierarchy in high-bitrate discrete tokenization is, to some extent, analogous to the iterative denoising or function evaluation process in flow-based continuous generation. In this sense, once bitrate is increased sufficiently, continuous tokenization becomes a natural modeling direction.

同时，高比特率离散令牌和连续令牌都比低比特率令牌具有更大的目标空间自由度，这在实践中可能使 AR 系统更容易受到累积预测误差和曝光偏差的影响At the same time, both high-bitrate discrete tokens and continuous tokens have substantially more target-space degrees of freedom than low-bitrate tokens, which in practice can make AR systems more vulnerable to accumulated prediction errors and exposure bias[schmidt2019generalization]。此外，当分词器帧速率较高并且AR序列相应较长时，可以进一步放大这种累积。因此，对于高带宽标记化，降低帧率，或者更普遍地降低AR模型所看到的有效帧率，成为控制AR误差积累的必要手段。. Moreover, when the tokenizer frame rate is high and the AR sequence is correspondingly long, this accumulation can be further amplified. Therefore, for high-bandwidth tokenization, reducing the frame rate, or more generally reducing the effective frame rate seen by the AR model, becomes a necessary means of controlling AR error accumulation.

2.2 单一高维空间与产品结构空间Single high-dimensional space versus product-structured space

In other words, if the generative model must reshape a high-frame-rate, low-dimensional token sequence into a low-frame-rate, high-dimensional latent space, and then invert that reshaping during prediction, does this not suggest that one may instead train a tokenizer that directly produces such a native low-frame-rate, high-dimensional token space? Ideally, such a tokenizer would preserve sufficient bandwidth to maintain strong reconstruction fidelity, while eliminating the need for additional local encoding and decoding modules inside the generative model, especially prediction-time modules such as local DiTs that substantially increase computational cost and architectural complexity.

从生成模型输入的角度来看，压缩一组低维标记可以在分词器内部实现，也可以在生成模型内部实现，这两种选择并没有本质上的不同。然而，从生成模型输出的角度来看，差异是巨大的。本地标记组本身就是一个短序列，可以通过本地序列模型进行处理，该本地序列模型具有足够的能力来逐步提升和重塑本地表示，从而使相应的产品结构表示更容易建模。相比之下，单个高维标记本身并不是序列，因此无法从这种顺序提升中受益；它在模型内部的表征扩展更加有限，因为过大的隐藏维度会直接增加模型大小、训练难度和计算成本。因此，关键的挑战不是简单地使用高维空间，而是构建这样一个空间，使得生成模型能够稳定高效地执行From the viewpoint of the generative model input, compressing a group of low-dimensional tokens can be implemented either inside the tokenizer or inside the generative model, and these two choices are not fundamentally different. 2.3 解开的表示空间Disentangled representation space

先前工作中用于降低生成建模难度的最常见策略之一（适用于离散和连续标记）是显式构建从粗到细的表示空间，最常见的是语义声学解缠结的形式One of the most common strategies in prior work for reducing generative modeling difficulty, applicable to both discrete and continuous tokens, is to explicitly construct a coarse-to-fine representation space, most often in the form of semantic–acoustic disentanglement[borsos2023audiolm, mousavi2024should, du2024cosyvoice, liu2024semanticodec, bai2024seed, ye2025codec, wang2025spark, chen2026sac]。在此类表示中，语义组件通常充当紧凑的核心表示，保留信号的主要属性，并且可能来自预训练 SSL 模型的隐藏空间. In such representations, the semantic component typically serves as a compact core representation that preserves the main attributes of the signal, and may come from the hidden space of a pretrained SSL model[baevski2020wav2vec, chen2022wavlm, hsu2021hubert, chiu2022self]或 ASR 模型or an ASR model[radford2023robust, chen2025minmo]。然后，声学组件通常通过残留或分层结构提供额外的细节来补充这种语义表示。通过向语言模型提供更聚类、更受约束或自由度较低的语义表示，可以在输入端有效减少 AR 错误累积；在输出方面，语义嵌入本身可以简化和稳定预测。类似的趋势也越来越多地出现在图像生成中，其中大规模预训练的 SSL 嵌入要么用作标记器训练期间的对齐监督，要么直接用作标记本身，从而提高了连续表示（尤其是高维连续表示）的生成性能. The acoustic component then complements this semantic representation by providing additional detail, often through a residual or hierarchical structure. By feeding the language model with a more clustered, more constrained, or otherwise lower-freedom semantic representation, AR error accumulation can be effectively reduced on the input side; and on the output side, the semantic embedding itself can simplify and stabilize the prediction. Similar trends have also appeared increasingly in image generation, where large-scale pretrained SSL embeddings have been used either as alignment supervision during tokenizer training or directly as tokens themselves, improving the generative performance of continuous, especially high-dimensional continuous, representations[leng2025repa, zheng2025diffusion, tong2026scaling].

然而，依赖这种预定义或外部训练的语义嵌入并不是免费的午餐。一方面，从针对特定领域或属性训练的模型（例如 ASR 模型）派生的语义嵌入不可避免地继承了对相应目标属性的强烈偏差，例如单词或音素结构，甚至依赖于语言的偏差。这种偏差可能会降低分词器对其他类型信息（例如非语义或非语音内容）进行编码的能力，从而迫使这些属性仅在声学成分中表示。如果生成模型严重依赖语义组件来减少 AR 错误累积，则其他类型的信息可能在其输入中代表性不足，从而限制了模型的理解和生成能力。另一方面，从 SSL 模型派生的语义嵌入在很大程度上受到 SSL 训练数据、模型规模和预训练目标的影响。不同的生成任务，例如语音合成与音乐生成，可能更喜欢不同的表示空间属性。如果希望构建单个任务通用表示空间，训练一个足够大且足够通用的 SSL 模型的成本甚至可能超过训练生成模型本身的成本。However, relying on such predefined or externally trained semantic embeddings is not a free lunch. On the one hand, semantic embeddings derived from models trained for specific domains or attributes, such as ASR models, inevitably inherit strong biases toward the corresponding target attributes, for example, word or phoneme structure, or even language-dependent biases. Such biases may reduce the capacity of the tokenizer to encode other types of information, such as non-semantic or non-vocal content, forcing those properties to be represented only in the acoustic component. If the generative model then relies heavily on the semantic component in order to reduce AR error accumulation, other types of information may be underrepresented in its input, limiting the model’s understanding and generation capabilities. On the other hand, semantic embeddings derived from SSL models are heavily shaped by the SSL training data, model scale, and pretraining objective. Different generative tasks, e.g., speech synthesis versus music generation, may prefer different representation-space properties. If one wishes to build a single task-general representation space, the cost of training a sufficiently large and sufficiently universal SSL model may even exceed that of training the generative model itself.

因此，从简单性和通用性的角度来看，我们宁愿不依赖于明确解开的语义-声学表示，也不依赖于外部大规模SSL训练来塑造或约束高维令牌空间。相反，我们的目标是让分词器主要针对高保真重建进行优化，同时使其解码器足够鲁棒，以覆盖生成模型产生的预测错误类型，并使用较低成本的机制来诱导生成模型更容易预测的高维空间。Therefore, from the perspectives of simplicity and generality, we prefer not to rely on explicitly disentangled semantic–acoustic representations, nor on external large-scale SSL training to shape or constrain the high-dimensional token space. Instead, we aim to let the tokenizer optimize primarily for high-fidelity reconstruction, while making its decoder sufficiently robust to cover the types of prediction errors produced by the generative model, and to use lower-cost mechanisms to induce a high-dimensional space that is itself easier for the generative model to predict.

2.4 原始输入空间建模和流形假设Raw input space modeling and the manifold hypothesis

最近引起越来越多关注的另一项工作是直接在高维原始信号空间中执行生成建模，而不需要任何单独训练的分词器。此类方法的动机通常是Another line of work that has recently attracted increasing attention is to perform generative modeling directly in the high-dimensional raw signal space, without any separately trained tokenizer. Such approaches are often motivated by the流形假设manifold hypothesis，即假设许多现实世界的高维信号实际上位于低维潜在流形附近, namely the assumption that many real-world high-dimensional signals in fact lie near a lower-dimensional latent manifold[chapelle2006discussion, gorban2018blessing]。根据这种观点，直接对此类信号进行建模可能比对不继承此类结构的任意高维向量（例如自由学习的高维标记）进行建模更容易。这一系列工作在图像生成方面显示出了强大的潜力. Under this view, directly modeling such signals may be easier than modeling an arbitrary high-dimensional vector that does not inherit such structure, such as a freely learned high-dimensional token. This line of work has shown strong potential in image generation[hoogeboom2025simpler, yu2026pixeldit, li2026back]，并且也开始出现在音频生成中, and has also begun to appear in audio generation[zhou2026wavflow, chen2026wavtts, fan2026barewave].

然而，与图像像素空间中的直接建模不同，波形空间中的直接建模不一定是音频的自然选择。在波形空间中，当在正交时频变换下观察时，各向同性高斯损坏过程对应于频域中的广泛全带白噪声。相比之下，现实世界的音频信号往往在中频和高频频段表现出明显较低的自然能量。因此，如果生成模型无法完全消除注入的噪声，这些频带中的残余误差在人类听觉感知下可能会变得明显However, unlike direct modeling in image pixel space, direct modeling in waveform space might not necessarily be a natural choice for audio. In waveform space, an isotropic Gaussian corruption process, when viewed under an orthonormal time–frequency transform, corresponds to broadly full-band white noise in the frequency domain. Real-world audio signals, by contrast, tend to exhibit substantially lower natural energy in the mid- and high-frequency bands. As a consequence, if the generative model fails to remove the injected noise completely, the residual error in these bands may become perceptually salient under human auditory perception[miller1947sensitivity, zwicker2013psychoacoustics]。这可能与图像情况不同，在图像情况下，人类对此类噪声的敏感性和耐受性可能具有不同的机制. This may differ from the image case where human sensitivity and tolerance to such noises might have a different mechanism[campbell1968application, barten1999contrast, chen2023importance]，并且加性噪声的掩蔽效果也可能与音频中的掩蔽效果有质的不同, and where the masking effects of additive noise might also be qualitatively different from those in audio[watson1997model]。因此，相同类型的预测误差可能在音频和图像生成中产生显着不同的感知结果。此外，尽管图像生成中的像素空间建模已被经验证明可以实现与潜在空间建模相当的性能，并且在某些情况下优于潜在空间建模，但由于缺乏可以细化或补偿预测误差的抗噪声分词器解码器，它仍然可能表现出伪影或噪声。因此，我们寻求的是一个仍然受益于流形假设的高维空间，但同时拥有有意义程度的重构鲁棒性，使得生成模型可以由于结构化和可插值的低维流形的存在而更容易地学习它，同时还保留足够的鲁棒性以防止生成预测误差直接变成感知上的严重扭曲。. Therefore, the same type of prediction error may have significantly different perceptual consequences in audio and image generation. In addition, although pixel-space modeling in image generation has been empirically shown to achieve performance comparable to, and in some cases better than, latent-space modeling, it may still exhibit artifacts or noise due to the lack of a noise-robust tokenizer decoder that can refine or compensate for prediction errors. Therefore, what we seek is a high-dimensional space that still benefits from the manifold hypothesis, but at the same time possesses a meaningful degree of reconstruction robustness, so that the generative model can learn it more easily due to the presence of a structured and interpolatable low-dimensional manifold, while also retaining enough robustness to prevent generative prediction errors from directly becoming perceptually severe distortions.

在本节中，我们提供了极简分词器模型和极简 AR 流匹配模型的具体架构实例。对于标记器，我们有意将自己限制在简单和标准的构建块上，以表明一旦标记空间被适当塑造，整体模型结构及其基本单元都不需要过于复杂，以在保真度和可预测性之间实现良好的平衡。对于AR流量匹配模型，我们采用模块化设计，针对AR流量系统中容易出现的误差累积和曝光偏差问题。通过显式的功能解开，系统可以通过动态训练自动优化不同的信息提取路径，同时在推理时可以使用适当的多路径CFG来选择性地增强不同的功能组件，从而减轻AR生成过程中的顺序错误累积。In this section, we provide concrete architecture instantiations of a minimalist tokenizer model and a minimalist AR flow-matching model. 5 实验与结果Experiments and Results

在本节中，我们从两个互补的角度评估 Locodec 和 MP-ELD。首先，我们研究所提出的令牌空间整形机制是否在改变潜在空间的几何形状和统计数据的同时保持重建质量。其次，我们评估这些形状表示是否更容易预测，以及 MP-ELD 是否在保持短形式生成质量的同时提高了长期稳定性。In this section, we evaluate Locodec and MP-ELD from two complementary perspectives. 6 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

在本文中，我们研究了低帧率、高维连续标记是否可以作为自回归语音生成的稳定目标。我们的主要发现是，当共同设计表示空间和生成框架时，此类代币是可行的。我们提出了 Locodec，一种本地编码的标记器，它围绕低维核心流形形成球形高维标记空间，并通过 PDD 引入坐标式能量层次结构，从而提高可预测性，而不会明显降低全维标记重建质量。我们进一步提出了 MP-ELD，它将局部连续性、自一致性和对齐一致性信息路径分开，允许残差 CFG 更明确地控制声学一致性和内容对齐。实验表明，仅重建质量不足以表征生成表示，并且令牌空间几何形状强烈影响生成器训练损失、CFG 行为和长形式稳定性。适度的核心尺寸与 PDD 相结合可提供最佳的整体平衡，In this paper, we studied whether low-frame-rate, high-dimensional continuous tokens can serve as stable targets for autoregressive speech generation. Our main finding is that such tokens are viable when the representation space and the generative framework are designed jointly. We proposed Locodec, a locally encoded tokenizer which shapes a spherical high-dimensional token space around a lower-dimensional core manifold and induces a coordinate-wise energy hierarchy through PDD, improving predictability without noticeably degrading full-dimensional token reconstruction quality. We further proposed MP-ELD, which separates local-continuity, self-consistency, and alignment-consistency information pathways, allowing residual CFG to control acoustic consistency and content alignment more explicitly. The experiments show that reconstruction quality alone is not sufficient to characterize a generative representation, and token-space geometry strongly affects generator training loss, CFG behavior, and long-form stability. At the same time, the remaining SIM gap to the strongest prior systems suggests a semantic–acoustic trade-off: low frame rate appears to favor content aggregation and AR stability, but may make fine-grained acoustic and speaker-similarity modeling harder. We therefore view token frame rate not only as an efficiency parameter, but also as a semantic–acoustic resolution knob.

The same principles may also be relevant beyond audio: low-rate high-dimensional continuous tokens with shaped latent geometry could be useful for image, video, or other sequence generation problems where reconstruction capacity and AR stability must be balanced.

其次，我们故意避免预训练的 SSL、ASR 和语言模型是一种隔离令牌空间整形和信息路由影响的设计选择，而不是对方法的限制。 Locodec 和 MP-ELD 天然兼容预训练组件。例如，对齐一致性 LM 可以由预训练的文本 LM 初始化或替换，这可能会加强对齐路径的语义偏差，从而改变自一致性路径的作用。类似地，不同 MP-ELD 路径的令牌编码器可以进行不同的初始化，或者一个路径可以由预训练的 SSL 或 ASR 表示来引导。更一般地说，信息路由可以从训练动态中产生，如本文所示，但它也可以通过架构和初始化先验来加强。在分词器内部和生成器内部设计更好的信息路径可能是进一步减轻甚至消除 AR 错误累积的重要方向。Second, our deliberate avoidance of pretrained SSL, ASR, and language models is a design choice for isolating the effects of token-space shaping and information routing, not a restriction of the method. Locodec and MP-ELD are naturally compatible with pretrained components. For example, the alignment-consistency LM could be initialized from or replaced by a pretrained text LM, which may strengthen the semantic bias of the alignment path and thereby alter the role of the self-consistency path. Similarly, the token encoders for different MP-ELD pathways could be initialized differently, or one pathway could be guided by a pretrained SSL or ASR representation. More generally, information routing can emerge from training dynamics, as shown in this paper, but it can also be strengthened by architectural and initialization priors. Designing better information pathways, both inside the tokenizer and inside the generator, can potentially be an important direction to further mitigate or even eliminate AR error accumulation.

第三，提高说话人的相似度和声音保真度仍然是一个核心挑战。目前的结果表明，低帧率令牌自然有利于内容聚合，这有助于 WER，但可能会使细粒度声学建模变得更加困难。一种可能的方向是将语义声学分解引入代币空间，但仍然不依赖显式的 SSL 或 ASR 监督。更广泛地说，对于一般音频生成，相关因素可能超出语义和声学范围，包括空间属性、源身份或时间事件动态。开发能够以可控方式组织这些因素的分词器，同时保持重建保真度和 AR 可预测性，是一个关键的开放问题。Third, improving speaker similarity and acoustic fidelity remains a central challenge. The current results suggest that low-frame-rate tokens naturally favor content aggregation, which helps WER but may make fine-grained acoustic modeling harder. One possible direction is to introduce semantic–acoustic factorization into the token space, still without relying on explicit SSL or ASR supervision. More broadly, for general audio generation, the relevant factors may go beyond semantics and acoustics to include spatial attributes, source identity, or temporal event dynamics. Developing tokenizers that can organize these factors in a controllable way, while preserving reconstruction fidelity and AR predictability, is a key open problem.

第四，下一步自然是扩展所提议框架的各个组件。特别是，由于解码器是估计路径速度场的实际下一个令牌预测模块，因此了解其缩放行为尤为重要。与产品结构令牌方法相比，每个 LM 步骤可能需要额外的本地 DiT 来解码一组短程令牌，MP-ELD 中的 FFN 解码器需要明显更少的 MAC 来预测一个本地高维令牌。这为扩展解码器或用更具表现力的架构替换它留下了巨大的计算空间，同时仍然保持整体推理成本的竞争力。Fourth, scaling the individual components of the proposed framework is a natural next step. In particular, since the decoder is the actual next-token prediction module that estimates the pathwise velocity field, understanding its scaling behavior is particularly important. Compared with product-structured token approaches, where each LM step may require an additional local DiT to decode a group of short-range tokens, the FFN decoder in MP-ELD requires significantly fewer MACs to predict one native high-dimensional token. This leaves substantial computational headroom for scaling the decoder or replacing it with more expressive architectures while still keeping the overall inference cost competitive.
