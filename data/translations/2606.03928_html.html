摘要Abstract

推理分两个阶段进行。 在预填充阶段，模型处理Inference proceeds in two phases. In the decode phase, the model generates one token at a time and appends the corresponding KV pairs to the cache. 对 KV 缓存量化的影响。Implications for KV cache quantization.

我们表明，大We show that large-Range\mathrm{Range}值状态不仅对于维持 KV 缓存逐出精度至关重要，而且也是量化误差的主要来源。 因此，一个有前途的未来方向是研究混合精度方法value states are not only critical for maintaining KV cache eviction accuracy but also a major source of error under quantization. Therefore, a promising future direction is to investigate a mixed-precision approach(Liu et al., 2024b)以保持大的精度to maintain the precision of large-Range\mathrm{Range}值状态。 例如，可以将它们放置在高精度保留缓存中，而剩余的KV缓存则量化为较低位宽。一旦高精度预算用完，该保留缓存中最早的值状态可以被量化并“驱逐”到低精度缓存中。value states. For example, they can be placed in a high-precision reserved cache, while the remaining KV cache is quantized to a lower bit-width. Once the high-precision budget is used up, the earliest value state in this reserved cache can be quantized and “evicted” into the low-precision cache.

大数值状态对于推理进程至关重要。Large-magnitude value states are crucial for reasoning progression.

我们观察图中Our observation in Figure9表明大数值状态在维持推理进程中发挥着特殊作用。在这些值被驱逐后，世代通常会退化为重复循环，其中模型重复重述中间推理而没有取得进展。一种假设是，这些值有助于潜在推理步骤之间的转换，防止模型崩溃为自我强化路径。 因此，删除它们会使模型陷入重复循环。 未来一个潜在的方向是分析大量值状态携带哪些信息以及它们如何影响长式推理。suggests that large-magnitude value states play a special role in maintaining reasoning progression. After these values are evicted, generations often degenerate into repetitive loops, where the model repeatedly restates intermediate reasoning without making progress. One hypothesis is that these values help transition between latent reasoning steps, preventing the model from collapsing into self-reinforcing paths. Therefore, their removal traps the model in repetitive loops. One potential future direction is to analyze what information large-magnitude value states carry and how they influence long-form reasoning.

结论。Conclusion.

我们呈现We present瓦斯VaSE，一个免训练的KV缓存驱逐框架，旨在解决推理模型的内存瓶颈。我们确定了在 KV 缓存压缩期间保持准确性的两个关键因素：保护大数值状态免遭驱逐，以及引入随机性以提高 KV 缓存多样性。综合考虑这两个因素，, a training-free KV cache eviction framework designed to address the memory bottleneck of reasoning models. We identify two critical factors for maintaining accuracy during KV cache compression: protecting large-magnitude value states from eviction, and introducing stochasticity to improve KV cache diversity. Taking both factors into consideration,瓦斯VaSE为改进现有的驱逐方法提供了一个简单而有效的方法。更广泛地说，我们的研究结果强调了长形式推理中值状态的重要性，并为设计内存高效推理方法提出了新的方向。provides a simple and effective recipe for improving existing eviction methods. More broadly, our findings highlight the importance of value states in long-form reasoning and suggest new directions for designing memory-efficient inference methods.

附录EAppendix E局限性Limitations

我们的评估重点是推理模型和生成的解码阶段，其中驱逐方法提供最大的好处。因此，我们不对预填充阶段压缩基准进行评估。 然而，由于我们的方法是建立在对值状态分布和随机多样性的基本观察之上的，因此我们相信核心方法可以扩展到预填充阶段以进行长提示压缩。Our evaluation focuses on reasoning models and the decode phase of generation, where eviction methods offer the greatest benefit. As a result, we do not evaluate on prefill-phase compression benchmarks. However, because our method is built on fundamental observations of value-state distributions and stochastic diversity, we believe the core methodology can be extended to the prefill phase for long-prompt compression.

最后，虽然我们的研究结果将值状态大小与量化误差联系起来，但我们关注的是稀疏注意力，并且量化方法的开发超出了本文的范围。 未来的研究可以探索一个集成框架，将 KV 缓存驱逐与异常值感知量化相结合，以突破压缩比的极限。Lastly, while our findings link value-state magnitude to quantization errors, we focus on sparse attention, and quantization method development is beyond the scope of this paper.
