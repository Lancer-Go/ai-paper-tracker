1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的代理后培训越来越受到两种互补范式的影响Agentic post-training for Large Language Models (LLMs) is increasingly shaped by two complementary paradigms(Yao et al., 2023; Schick et al., 2023; Jin et al., 2025b)：强化学习（RL），它优化环境反馈的任务结果，以及教师指导的蒸馏，它提供更密集的令牌级监督。然而，在长视野代理中，天真地组合这些信号会造成严重的信用分配不匹配。一个稀疏的奖励必须监督数十个决策，尽管成功往往只取决于其中的一两个。: Reinforcement Learning (RL), which optimizes task outcomes from environment feedback, and teacher-guided distillation, which supplies denser token-level supervision. In long-horizon agents, however, naively combining these signals creates a severe credit-assignment mismatch. A single sparse reward must supervise dozens of decisions, even though success often hinges on only one or two of them.

这种不匹配有两个结构性根源。This mismatch has two structural sources.首先，失败是高度局部化的。First, failure is highly localized.格式错误的查询、无效的操作或过早的答案可能会破坏跨越数千个令牌的部署，但标准强化学习会在整个响应中广播相同的轨迹级信号。A malformed query, an invalid action, or a premature answer can ruin a rollout spanning thousands of tokens, yet standard RL broadcasts the same trajectory-level signal across the full response.其次，代理轨迹是异构的。Second, agent trajectories are heterogeneous.与静态推理轨迹不同，它们将不可变的环境观察与模型生成的动作跨度交织在一起。将完整轨迹视为统一的字符串会浪费对不可控令牌的监督，并模糊了使事后反馈有用的因果边界。Unlike static reasoning traces, they interleave immutable environment observations with model-generated action spans. Treating the full trajectory as a uniform string wastes supervision on non-controllable tokens and blurs the causal boundaries that make hindsight feedback useful.

这些观察表明，关键问题不仅仅是如何注入更强的教师，而是These observations suggest that the key problem is not merely how to inject a stronger teacher, but哪里where一旦获得事后信息，就应重新分配信用。对于多回合智能体，自然单位不是整个响应，而是以行动为中心的步骤。因此，有用的蒸馏信号应该尊重交互的步骤结构，并将优化质量集中在实际决定下游结果的局部决策上。credit should be redistributed once hindsight information is available. For multi-turn agents, the natural unit is not the whole response but the action-centered step. A useful distillation signal should therefore respect the step structure of interaction and concentrate optimization mass on the local decisions that actually determine downstream outcomes.

This is fundamentally misaligned with the nature of long-horizon tasks. A rollout spanning thousands of tokens often fails due to a single localized error—a malformed query, an invalid action, or a premature conclusion. Standard RL blindly smears the penalty across the entire sequence, forcing the agent to guess which step was fatal. Instead of learning a dense value model (which is notoriously unstable and prone to hallucination in agentic domains), StepOPSD addresses this bottleneck through post-rollout hindsight distillation. Rather than introducing a standalone objective, StepOPSD acts as a surgical intervention within the existing pipeline:

rollout→reward→step extraction→teacher–student rescoring→advantage shaping→policy update.\begin{split}&\text{rollout}\rightarrow\text{reward}\rightarrow\text{step extraction}\rightarrow\\ &\text{teacher--student rescoring}\rightarrow\\ &\text{advantage shaping}\rightarrow\text{policy update}.\end{split} (1)

Our contribution is to make the supervision unit explicit by extracting action-centered step segments and injecting their teacher-student comparison directly into advantage shaping.

5 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 StepOPSD，这是在线偏好蒸馏的一种步骤感知扩展，用于针对在线策略蒸馏 (OPD) 的多轮代理强化学习。 StepOPSD 不是统一提取整个轨迹，而是隔离以行动为中心的跨度，这些跨度因果决定代理行为，并将后见之明的教师信号反馈回令牌级别的优势塑造中。该方法忠实于底层 GRPO 训练堆栈，实现模块化，并且自然地与代理轨迹的异构结构保持一致。This paper presents StepOPSD, a step-aware extension of Online Preference Distillation for multi-turn agent reinforcement learning against Online Policy Distilation (OPD). Instead of distilling an entire trajectory uniformly, StepOPSD isolates the action-centered spans that causally determine agent behavior and feeds hindsight teacher signals back into token-level advantage shaping. The method is faithful to the underlying GRPO training stack, modular in implementation, and naturally aligned with the heterogeneous structure of agent trajectories.

根据经验，我们的结果表明，StepOPSD 的主要价值不是每个基准平均值的统一增益，而是对对局部决策错误最敏感的子集的更精确的校正。在 ALFWorld 和 Search-QA 中，StepOPSD 始终如一地实现了多个最佳和次佳结果，同时揭示了更清晰的双旋钮定律：通过更小的Empirically, our results show that the main value of StepOPSD is not a uniform gain on every benchmark average, but a more precise correction of the subsets most sensitive to local decision errors. 局限性Limitations

虽然 StepOPSD 展示了步骤感知学分分配的强大潜力，但我们承认当前研究存在两个主要局限性。While StepOPSD demonstrates strong potential for step-aware credit assignment, we acknowledge two main limitations in the current study.

Exploring how to use the step-level distillation signal to guide real-time decoding remains an important direction for future work.
