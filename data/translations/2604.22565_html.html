9.4 训练设置Training Settings

亚马逊-美容。Amazon-Beauty.

对于亚马逊美容推荐任务，参与者仅将用户的历史评论作为输入。 候选集（以及每个项目的元数据，例如标题/品牌/类别/价格带和短语）被视为下游求解器使用的查询的一部分，而不是输入到参与者中。通过这种方式，参与者专注于从用户历史中选择显着的偏好信号，而重新排序条件则取决于强调的历史和候选描述。For the Amazon-Beauty recommendation task, the actor takes only the user’s historical reviews as input. The candidate set (along with each item’s metadata, e.g., title/brand/category/price band and short phrases) is treated as part of the query used by the downstream solver, rather than being fed into the actor. In this way, the actor focuses on selecting salient preference signals from the user history, while the re-ranker conditions on both the emphasized history and the candidate descriptions.

问答。Question answering.

对于 QA 任务，我们向参与者提供一系列问题和证据， 但我们仅将突出显示应用于证据范围。这种设计鼓励参与者识别回答的最小支持证据，同时保持问题完整，作为下游 QA 模型的条件提示。For QA tasks, we feed the actor a concatenation of the question and the evidence, but we apply highlighting only to the evidence span. This design encourages the actor to identify the minimal supporting evidence for answering, while keeping the question intact as a conditioning prompt for the downstream QA model.
