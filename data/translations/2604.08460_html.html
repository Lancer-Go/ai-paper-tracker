通过反问题公式进行涡轮风扇健康估计的机器学习框架A Machine Learning Framework for Turbofan Health Estimation via Inverse Problem Formulation

Milad Leyli-Abadi Corresponding author    Lucas Thil    Sébastien Razakarivony    Guillaume Doquet    Jesse Read
摘要Abstract

估计涡轮风扇发动机的健康状态是一个具有挑战性的不适定反问题，受到稀疏传感和复杂非线性热力学的阻碍。该领域的研究仍然分散，由于使用不切实际的数据集和对时间信息利用的探索不足，比较受到限制。这项工作研究了如何在现实的退化和维护模式下从运行传感器数据中恢复组件级健康指标。为了支持这项研究，我们引入了一个新的数据集，其中包含面向行业的复杂性，例如维护事件和使用变化。使用该数据集，我们建立了一个初始基准，比较稳态和非稳态数据驱动模型以及贝叶斯过滤器（用于解决此问题的经典方法系列）。除了这个基准之外，我们还引入了自我监督学习（SSL）方法，该方法可以在不访问真实健康标签的情况下学习潜在表示，这是反映现实世界操作限制的场景。通过将这些无监督表示的下游估计性能与直接预测基线进行比较，我们建立了解决该逆问题的难度的实际下限。我们的结果表明，传统过滤器仍然具有强大的基线，而 SSL 方法揭示了健康估计的内在复杂性，并强调需要更先进和可解释的推理策略。为了可重复性，生成的数据集和本工作中使用的实现都是可访问的。Estimating the health state of turbofan engines is a challenging ill-posed inverse problem, hindered by sparse sensing and complex nonlinear thermodynamics. Research in this area remains fragmented, with comparisons limited by the use of unrealistic datasets and insufficient exploration of exploitation of temporal information. This work investigates how to recover component‑level health indicators from operational sensor data under realistic degradation and maintenance patterns. To support this study, we introduce a new dataset that incorporates industry-oriented complexities such as maintenance events and usage changes. Using this dataset, we establish an initial benchmark that compares steady‑state and nonstationary data‑driven models, and Bayesian filters –classic families of methods used to solve this problem. In addition to this benchmark, we introduce self-supervised learning (SSL) approaches that learn latent representations without access to true health labels, a scenario reflective of real-world operational constraints. By comparing the downstream estimation performance of these unsupervised representations against the direct prediction baselines, we establish a practical lower bound on the difficulty to solve this inverse problem. Our results reveal that traditional filters remain strong baselines, while SSL methods reveal the intrinsic complexity of health estimation and highlight the need for more advanced and interpretable inference strategies. 1 简介Introduction

监测涡轮机的健康状况对于确保可靠的飞机发动机监测和实现预测性维护至关重要。然而，现代飞机在机械、热和重量受限的环境中运行，这严重限制了机载传感器的数量和位置。因此，可用的测量仅提供发动机内部状态的部分视图，使得健康估计成为一个不适定且不确定的问题：几种不同的健康状态可能会产生几乎相同的传感器值。Monitoring the health of turbomachines is critical for ensuring reliable aircraft engine monitoring and enabling predictive maintenance. However, modern aircraft operate in mechanical, thermal, and weight constrained environments, which severely limit the number and placement of onboard sensors. As a consequence, available measurements provide only a partial view of the engine’s internal state, making health estimation an ill-posed and under-determined problem: several distinct health states may yield nearly identical sensor values.

发动机健康状况通常以热力学模型为特征，该模型根据发动机每个部件（压缩机、涡轮机）的效率和质量流量，将发动机内外的不同测量温度和压力联系起来。这两个量的变化可表示为Engine health is commonly characterized by a thermodynamic model that relates the different measured temperatures and pressures in and out of the engine, depending on the efficiencies and mass flows of each component of the engine (compressors, turbines). Variations in these two quantities serve as健康指标health indicators，反映了每个组件的降解水平。因此，健康监测需要解决一个具有挑战性的逆问题：从一组稀疏的测量中检索效率和质量流量。, reflecting the level of degradation in each of these components. Health monitoring therefore needs to solve a challenging inverse problem : retrieving the efficiencies and mass flows from a sparse set of measurements.

推进这一领域的一个关键障碍是缺乏捕捉现实世界涡轮机械中复杂退化模式的公共基准。现有数据集通常缺乏验证算法所需的保真度，主要是因为它们无法为逆问题提供可验证的地面实况健康指标。因此，仍然很难比较不同的方法实践并衡量实用、可部署解决方案的进展。在本文中，我们研究了基于稀疏传感器数据的涡轮风扇部件健康估计的逆问题。我们的主要贡献是：A critical barrier to advancing this field is the lack of a public benchmark that captures the complex degradation patterns found in real-world turbomachinery. 5.1.4 评价标准Evaluation criteria

为了评估和比较模型的性能，我们采用了三个互补标准：对称平均绝对百分比误差 (SMAPE)、均方根误差 (RMSE) 以及在真实健康指标和预测值之间计算的皮尔逊相关系数 (P.Corr.)。选择这些指标是因为它们捕获了估计质量的不同但同样重要的方面。 SMAPE 提供了一种与尺度无关的相对误差测量方法，使其适合比较不同量级健康指标的偏差。 RMSE 由于其二次公式而强调较大的误差，从而深入了解模型避免大偏差的能力，这在预测应用中可能至关重要。最后，皮尔逊相关性衡量预测与真实情况之间的线性关联，反映模型捕捉潜在趋势和退化动态的程度。To evaluate and compare the performance of the models, we employ three complementary criteria: the Symmetric Mean Absolute Percentage Error (SMAPE), the Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and the Pearson correlation coefficient (P.Corr.) computed between the ground‑truth health indicators and the predicted values. These metrics were selected because they capture different and equally important aspects of estimation quality. SMAPE provides a scale‑independent measure of relative error, making it suitable for comparing deviations across health indicators with different magnitudes. RMSE emphasizes larger errors due to its quadratic formulation, thereby offering insight into the model’s ability to avoid large deviations that may be critical in prognostics applications. Finally, Pearson correlation measures the linear association between predictions and ground truth, reflecting how well the models capture the underlying trends and degradation dynamics.

总的来说，这些结果突出了三个关键见解。首先，涡扇逆问题仍然部分不可观测，某些指标系统性地难以估计。其次，时间信息有助于解决这一难题，但没有一种算法能够完全解决不同的挑战。第三，无监督表示学习捕获了全局结构，但仍然达不到精确健康估计的监督反转，这表明未来的工作应该研究将物理信息建模与表示学习相结合的混合策略。Overall, these results highlight three key insights. First, the turbofan inverse problem remains partially non-observable, with certain indicators systematically harder to estimate. Second, temporal information helps to deal with this difficulty, but none of the algorithms completely solve the different challenges. Third, unsupervised representation learning captures global structure but still falls short of supervised inversion for precise health estimation, suggesting that future work should investigate hybrid strategies combining physics-informed modeling with representation learning.

6 结论Conclusion

从稀疏的传感器测量中估计涡轮风扇部件的健康状况从根本上来说是一个不适定的逆问题。在这项工作中，我们引入了一个基准设置，旨在在更现实的操作条件下研究这一挑战，包括异构降解动态、维护干预和多种操作方案，其中有数百个样本跨越 1000 多个时间步长。在此框架内，我们比较了几种建模范式，从直接监督回归和时间序列模型到状态空间过滤和自监督表示学习。Estimating turbofan component health from sparse sensor measurements is a fundamentally ill-posed inverse problem. In this work, we introduced a benchmark setting designed to study this challenge under more realistic operational conditions, including heterogeneous degradation dynamics, maintenance interventions, and multiple operating regimes, with hundreds of samples spanning more than 1000 time steps. Within this framework, we compared several modeling paradigms ranging from direct supervised regression and temporal sequence models to state-space filtering and self-supervised representation learning.

我们的实证研究强调了该问题的三个结构特征。首先，虽然监督方法可以恢复部分健康状态，但不同指标的表现差异很大， 确认逆映射中的模糊性在整个系统中并不统一。其次，建模策略的比较表明时间结构有助于降低系统的不可观测性。然而，没有一种方法能够准确地恢复所有 HI。 最后，SSL 方法捕获传感器空间中的全局结构，但仍然不足以在没有监督的情况下进行准确的组件级估计。从这个意义上说，这些方法为仅从传感器观测中推断出的内容提供了有用的参考点，并强调了特定任务的监督或基于物理的约束必须弥合的差距。Our empirical study highlights three structural properties of the problem. First, while supervised approaches can recover a portion of the health state, performance varies substantially across indicators, confirming that ambiguity in the inverse mapping is not uniform across the system. Second, the comparison of modeling strategies suggests that temporal structure can help reduce the non-observability of the system. However, no single method is able to accurately recover all HI. Finally, SSL methods capture global structure in the sensor space but remain insufficient for accurate component-level estimation without supervision. In this sense, these approaches provide a useful reference point for what can be inferred from sensor observations alone, and highlight the gap that task-specific supervision or physics-based constraints must bridge.

总而言之，这些结果强调涡轮风扇健康估计是一个由部分可观测性、时间动态和物理约束形成的结构化推理问题。因此，未来工作的一个有希望的方向是设计联合利用这三种结构来源的混合方法。除了我们的方法比较之外，这项工作还旨在为社区提供一个实用的实验框架，用于在面向行业的数据集上研究涡轮风扇的健康估计。Taken together, these results emphasize that turbofan health estimation is a structured inference problem shaped by partial observability, temporal dynamics, and physical constraints. A promising direction for future work is therefore to design hybrid approaches that jointly leverage these three sources of structure. Beyond our methodological comparison, this work also aims to provide the community with a practical experimental framework for studying turbofan health estimation on an industry-oriented dataset.
