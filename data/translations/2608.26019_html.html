DualOPSD：自适应特权教师用于政策自我蒸馏DualOPSD: Adaptive Privileged Teachers for On-Policy Self-Distillation

摘要Abstract

按策略自我蒸馏 (OPSD) 使用学生模型的特权副本来提供密集监督，无需外部教师。即使学生分布和输出风格在训练过程中发生变化，OPSD 也能保持这位特权教师的固定。我们提出 DualOPSD，这是一种适应两种政策的非对称交替框架。学生首先向有特权的老师学习。然后，教师按照相同的学生轨迹转向更新的学生分布。此更新使后续监督能够响应学习者的需求，并且不需要再次推出。在非思考模式下的 Qwen3-8B 上，DualOPSD 在 AIME 2024、AIME 2025 和 HMMT 2025 上将 avg@12 比 OPSD 提高了 23.61、13.89 和 10.00 个点。1.7B 和 4B 的结果表明，精度增益取决于模型规模。在所有三个尺度上，DualOPSD 都减少了截断。 4B 诊断还显示教师和学生之间两个方向的 KL 较低。On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) uses a privileged copy of the student model to provide dense supervision without an external teacher. OPSD keeps this privileged teacher fixed, even though the student distribution and output style change during training. We propose DualOPSD, an asymmetric alternating framework that adapts both policies. The student first learns from the privileged teacher. The teacher then moves toward the updated student distribution on the same student trajectory. This update makes later supervision responsive to the learner and does not require another rollout. On Qwen3-8B in non-thinking mode, DualOPSD improves avg@12 over OPSD by 23.61, 13.89, and 10.00 points on AIME 2024, AIME 2025, and HMMT 2025. Results at 1.7B and 4B show that the accuracy gain depends on model scale. Across all three scales, DualOPSD reduces truncation. The 4B diagnostic also shows lower KL in both directions between the teacher and student.

1 简介Introduction

后训练对于提高大型语言模型 (LLM) 的推理能力至关重要。常见的方法包括对高质量解决方案进行监督微调（SFT）、具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）以及从教师模型中提取知识。 SFT 提供代币层面的直接监管。然而，它在固定的专家轨迹上进行训练，因此模型看到的前缀与其自己的推理前缀不同Posttraining is central to improving the reasoning ability of large language models (LLMs). Common approaches include supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on high quality solutions, reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR), and knowledge distillation from a teacher model. SFT provides direct supervision at the token level. However, it trains on fixed expert trajectories, so the model sees prefixes that differ from its own inference prefixes(Bengio et al., 2015; Kim and Rush, 2016)。 RLVR 在模型样本上进行训练。它的最终答案验证器仅在序列级别提供稀疏反馈，并且每个提示通常需要多次推出. RLVR trains on model samples. Its final answer verifier provides only sparse feedback at the sequence level, and each prompt usually requires several rollouts(Shao et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025)。在政策蒸馏 (OPD) 将学生轨迹与每个下一个标记的密集教师反馈结合起来. On-policy distillation (OPD) combines student trajectories with dense teacher feedback at each next token(Gu et al., 2023; Agarwal et al., 2024; Zhao et al., 2026).

按策略自蒸馏 (OPSD) 消除了外部教师On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) removes the external teacher(Zhao et al., 2026)。它根据同一基本模型创建两个条件策略。学生只看到问题。老师收到一份. It creates two conditional policies from the same base model. The student sees only the problem. The teacher receives a特权背景privileged context，例如仅在训练期间可用的经过验证的参考解决方案或答案。学生做出回应。特权教师对每个学生前缀的下一个令牌分配进行评分。因此，该参考在优化过程中提供了密集的监督，无需教师轨迹或验证者调用。, such as a verified reference solution or answer that is available only during training. The student generates a response. The privileged teacher scores the next token distribution at every student prefix. The reference therefore provides dense supervision without a teacher trajectory or a verifier call during optimization.

现有的 OPSD 仅优化学生，并将特权政策视为固定的预言。这个假设是强有力的，因为老师是同一模型的另一个条件视图，而不是独立训练的专家。 OPSD 还发现一些与格式和风格相关的词汇条目可以对前向 KL 做出异常大的贡献。拟合这些条目可以支配有用的推理信号和损伤训练。因此，OPSD 在求和之前限制了每个大的贡献。这种逐点剪裁可以保护学生，但它只会拒绝不相容的监督。固定教师不会收到有关被拒绝的风格信号的反馈，并且可以在后续步骤中再次呈现它。Existing OPSD optimizes only the student and treats the privileged policy as a stationary oracle. This assumption is strong because the teacher is another conditional view of the same model, not an independently trained expert. OPSD also finds that a few vocabulary entries related to format and style can make unusually large contributions to forward KL. Fitting these entries can dominate the useful reasoning signal and damage training. OPSD therefore caps each large contribution before summation. This pointwise clipping protects the student, but it only rejects incompatible supervision. A fixed teacher receives no feedback about the rejected style signal and can present it again in later steps.

这引出了我们的核心问题。This leads to our central question.特权教学政策本身是否应该作为在政策自我蒸馏的一部分来学习？Should the privileged teaching policy itself learn as part of on-policy self-distillation?

我们的答案是 DualOPSD，这是一种学习蒸馏相互作用两侧的交替过程。学生的更新仍然简洁而保守。然后，有特权的老师会最小化完整的词汇量Our answer is DualOPSD, an alternating procedure that learns both sides of the distillation interaction. The student update remains clipped and conservative. The privileged teacher then minimizes the full vocabularyDKL(sg[pS]∥pT)D_{\text{KL}}(\operatorname{sg}[p_{S}]\|p_{T})以相同的轨迹朝向更新的学生。适应后的教师为后续的学生步骤提供目标。因此，它改变了未来学生的梯度。这种不对称的闭环既不需要教师推出，也不需要正确性验证者。toward the updated student on the same trajectory. The adapted teacher supplies the targets for later student steps. It therefore changes future student gradients. This asymmetric closed loop requires neither a teacher rollout nor a correctness verifier.

2 背景Background

2.1 示范督导、奖励奖励Supervision from demonstrations and rewards

SFT 通过从固定参考解决方案中预测标记来进行学习。它提供密集的监督，不需要抽样或验证者。然而，它是离策略的，因为该模型是根据专家前缀而不是它自己的前缀进行训练的。 RLVR 而是从当前策略中进行采样，并通过验证者对完整的响应进行评分。这使得 RLVR 符合策略。它的信号很稀疏，并且当一组中的每个响应都获得相同的奖励时可能会消失SFT learns by predicting tokens from fixed reference solutions. It provides dense supervision and does not require sampling or a verifier. However, it is off-policy because the model trains on expert prefixes rather than its own. RLVR instead samples from the current policy and scores completed responses with a verifier. This makes RLVR on-policy. Its signal is sparse and can vanish when every response in a group receives the same reward(Shao et al., 2024)。这两种方法将密集的代币反馈与政策性的国家覆盖进行交换。. The two approaches trade dense token feedback against on-policy state coverage.

6 相关工作Related Work

6.1 有监督的微调和强化学习Supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning

SFT 直接在专家解决方案或模型解决方案上进行训练，是教授推理轨迹的标准方法SFT trains directly on expert solutions or model solutions and is a standard way to teach reasoning traces(Zelikman et al., 2022)。其密集代币损失简单且稳定。然而，它的前缀来自固定的演示而不是部署分发。 GRPO 等 RLVR 方法优化了策略样本组的可验证奖励. Its dense token loss is simple and stable. However, its prefixes come from fixed demonstrations rather than the deployment distribution. RLVR methods such as GRPO optimize verifiable rewards over groups of on-policy samples(Shao et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025)。他们直接以答案正确性为目标，不需要令牌级别的教师概率。他们的反馈很少，而且多次长期部署的成本可能会很高。 DualOPSD 将 RLVR 使用的学生状态分布与 SFT 使用的密集监督相结合。它在训练期间使用特权上下文中的参考而不是奖励信号。. They directly target answer correctness and do not need teacher probabilities at the token level. Their feedback is sparse, and several long rollouts can be expensive. DualOPSD combines the student state distribution used by RLVR with the dense supervision used by SFT. It uses a reference in the privileged context during training instead of a reward signal.

6.2 蒸馏和适应性教学政策Distillation and adaptive teaching policies

经典知识蒸馏训练学生软教师概率Classical knowledge distillation trains a student on soft teacher probabilities(Hinton et al., 2015)。序列级别 KD 将这种传输扩展到自回归输出. MiniLLM uses reverse KL and policy gradient optimization for generative distillation(Gu et al., 2023)。 GKD 结合了策略内和策略外采样，但存在一些分歧. GKD combines on-policy and off-policy sampling with several divergences(Agarwal et al., 2024)。这些方法显示了监督学生前缀的价值，但它们通常假设有外部教师。深度相互学习和在线蒸馏允许同伴目标在训练期间改变. These methods show the value of supervising student prefixes, but they usually assume an external teacher. Deep Mutual Learning and online distillation allow peer targets to change during training(Zhang et al., 2018; Chen et al., 2020)。 DualOPSD 也使用变化的目标，但只有老师才能看到参考。学生使用前向截断 KL。老师用完全反向 KL 跟随更新后的学生。 PiDistill 和 BRTS 为特权教师提供了另外两种机制。他们的教师展示可以搜索多个候选人。表格. 6.3 特权上下文自蒸馏Privileged context self-distillation

使用特权信息进行学习可以为教师提供额外的训练功能，但不会为部署的预测器提供额外的训练功能。广义蒸馏将此设置连接到软目标传输Learning with privileged information gives extra training features to a teacher but not to the deployed predictor. Generalized distillation connects this setting to soft target transfer(Lopez-Paz et al., 2016)。 OPSD 将这一想法应用于根据同一模型创建的教师. OPSD applies this idea to a teacher created from the same model(Zhao et al., 2026)。教师以参考解决方案为条件，并根据学生的反应提供密集的反馈。 OPSD 避免了外部教师和验证者，但其特权目标保持固定。 DualOPSD 保留了密集的策略信号，并使目标对学习器做出响应。此更新还与交互式模仿学习相关，因为监督会根据学习者访问的状态进行调整. The teacher conditions on a reference solution and provides dense feedback along a student response. OPSD avoids an external teacher and verifier, but its privileged target stays fixed. DualOPSD preserves the dense on-policy signal and makes the target responsive to the learner. The update is also related to interactive imitation learning because supervision adapts around states visited by the learner(Ross et al., 2011)。我们的方法直接在下一个代币分配上运行。. Our method operates directly on next token distributions.

7 结论和局限性Conclusion and Limitations

DualOPSD 将特权上下文自蒸馏转变为不对称闭环。学生从一个有限的特权目标中保守地学习。然后，教师按照相同的轨迹适应更新后的学生。在三个 Qwen3 音阶中，此更新减少了截断，而无需再次推出。其准确性效果很大程度上取决于模型大小。它在 4B 和 8B 处比 OPSD 有所改进，但在 1.7B 处则没有。 4B 诊断还显示两个方向上的 KL 较低，这证实了策略耦合在训练期间发生了变化。DualOPSD turns privileged context self-distillation into an asymmetric closed loop. The student learns conservatively from a clipped privileged target. The teacher then adapts to the updated student on the same trajectory. Across three Qwen3 scales, this update reduces truncation without another rollout. Its accuracy effect depends strongly on model size. It improves over OPSD at 4B and 8B but not at 1.7B. The 4B diagnostic also shows lower KL in both directions, which confirms that policy coupling changes during training.

证据涵盖一个模型家庭、一个训练种子以及每个规模的 90 个相同的竞赛问题。大型 8B 结果需要跨种子复制和更广泛的答案审核。 1.7B 逆转排除了与规模无关的主张。教师目标预期 KL 较低。它本身并不能建立更好的推理或特权引用的继续使用。固定前缀 KL 诊断也仅涵盖八个 4B 示例。这些限制使得模型规模成为未来适应性特权教学工作的中心变量。The evidence covers one model family, one training seed, and the same 90 competition problems at each scale. The large 8B result needs replication across seeds and broader answer auditing. The 1.7B reversal rules out a claim that is independent of scale. Lower KL is expected from the teacher objective. It does not by itself establish better reasoning or continued use of the privileged reference. The fixed prefix KL diagnostic also covers only eight 4B examples. These limits make model scale a central variable for future work on adaptive privileged teaching.
