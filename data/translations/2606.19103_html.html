摘要Abstract

基于指令的图像编辑的最新进展使模型能够根据自然语言指令执行复杂的视觉编辑。然而，在以产品为中心的场景中，保留产品功能、品牌和文本元素至关重要，当前的开源和闭源模型通常难以维护这种细粒度的对象身份。由于缺乏具有文本保真度约束的基于指令的产品图像编辑数据集，这个问题进一步复杂化，使其在很大程度上被视为基于指令的图像编辑模型的隐式功能。Recent advances in instruction-based image editing have enabled models to perform complex visual edits from natural language instructions. However, in product-centric scenarios where preserving product features, branding, and textual elements are critical, current open and closed source models often struggle to maintain this fine-grained object identity. This issue is further compounded by the lack of datasets for instruction-based product image editing with text fidelity constraints, leaving it largely treated as an implicit capability of instruction-based image editing models.

1 简介Introduction

扩散模型在文本到图像生成以及最近基于指令的图像编辑方面取得了实质性进展。系统已经从早期基于掩码的管道发展到基于指令的框架，从而实现更细粒度的控制。最初的基于扩散的编辑器直接源自文本到图像的对应编辑器，并依赖于反转Diffusion models have made substantial progress in text-to-image generation and, more recently, instruction-based image editing. Systems have evolved from early mask-based pipelines to instruction based frameworks that enable more fine-grained control. The Initial diffusion-based editors were derived directly from their text-to-image counterparts and relied on inversion[9, 18, 31]和用于本地编辑的空间掩蔽and spatial masking for localized edits[41, 12, 3]。随后的方法引入了指令引导的图像编辑，无论有没有明确的空间掩模. Subsequent approaches introduced instruction-guided image editing, both with and without explicit spatial masks[12, 3, 2]，显着提高可用性和通用性。最近，视觉语言模型和基于变压器的架构的集成, significantly improving usability and generalization. More recently, the integration of vision language models and transformer-based architectures[45, 4, 2, 26]进一步改善了指令跟随、全局结构保存和整体视觉质量。因此，这些模型越来越多地被纳入需要高质量图像输出的现实工作流程中。has further improved instruction following, global structure preservation, and overall visual quality. As a result, these models are increasingly being incorporated into real-world workflows that demand high-quality image output.

尽管取得了这些进步，当前的图像编辑模型仍然不适合需要严格视觉正确性的生产设置。在广告、电子商务和产品营销等领域，编辑后的图像必须完美：即使是品牌元素、产品几何形状、徽标或生成的文本中的微小错误也会导致图像无法使用。如图所示Despite these advances, current image editing models remain ill-suited for production settings that require strict visual correctness. In domains such as advertising, e-commerce, and product marketing, edited images must be perfect: even minor errors in branding elements, product geometry, logos, or generated text render an image unusable. As we can see in Figure1，现有的基于扩散的编辑器经常难以在编辑过程中保留此类细粒度的特征。他们经常扭曲或产生幻觉对象上的文本，巧妙地改变品牌徽标，或在执行其他合理编辑时修改产品几何形状。即使闭源模型也难以完成这项任务，并且经常呈现带有拼写错误和/或幻觉文本的文本。一个关键原因是缺乏以产品为中心的数据集和基准，这些数据集和基准在图像编辑过程中明确针对品牌一致性和文本一致性，这使得研究、诊断或系统地改进这些故障模式变得困难。, existing diffusion-based editors frequently struggle to preserve such fine-grained features during editing. They often distort or hallucinate text on objects, subtly alter brand logos, or modify product geometry when performing otherwise reasonable edits. Even closed source models struggle with this task and often render text with spelling errors and/or hallucinated text. A key reason is the absence of product-centric datasets and benchmarks that explicitly target brand consistency and text consistency during image editing, making it difficult to study, diagnose, or systematically improve these failure modes.

最近的工作在相关问题上取得了进展，但没有直接解决产品和品牌的一致性。以文本为中心的生成模型Recent works have made progress on related problems, but do not directly address product and brand consistency. Text-focused generative models[45, 14]提高新生成文本的易读性，同时基于推理的编辑器improve the legibility of newly generated text, while reasoning-based editors[15, 19, 57, 38, 52, 53]引入多步骤计划和反思，以更好地遵循复杂的指示。然而，这两项工作都没有明确目标是在编辑过程中保留现有的对象文本或品牌标识。参考引导和基于适配器的方法introduce multi-step planning and reflection to better follow complex instructions. However, neither line of work explicitly targets the preservation of existing on-object text or brand identity during editing. Reference-guided and adapter-based methods[54, 17, 32, 51, 55, 37]通过调节辅助输入来提高全局视觉一致性，但它们仍然在实际产品图像的像素级保真度和文本一致性方面遇到困难，因为它们强制输入和输出之间的空间约束。强化学习方法improve global visual consistency by conditioning on auxiliary inputs, but they still struggle with pixel-level fidelity and textual consistency on real product images, as they enforce spatial constraints between input and output. Reinforcement learning approaches[11, 24, 16]使用学习的奖励模型使模型与人类偏好保持一致，但这些奖励模型是为通用指令遵循和审美质量而设计的，并且不直接捕获产品的一致性。因此，当前模型在应用于现实产品场景时经常会改变或产生幻觉品牌元素和文本。现有的基准测试忽略渲染的文本，或者专注于文档样式的布局而不是拍摄的对象，从而导致产品编辑最关键的故障模式在很大程度上无法测量align models with human preferences using learned reward models, yet these reward models are designed for general-purpose instruction following and aesthetic quality, and do not directly capture product consistency. As a result, current models frequently alter or hallucinate branding elements and text when applied to realistic product scenes. Existing benchmarks ignore rendered text, or focus on document-style layouts rather than photographed objects, leaving the most critical failure modes for product editing largely unmeasured[7, 1, 23].

尽管 ProductConsistency 数据集和培训框架显着提高了基于指令的图像编辑中的产品保真度和文本保存，但未来的工作仍然存在一些机会。首先，该管道主要关注具有直接且清晰可见的文本布局的产品。扩展框架以支持弯曲、风格化或装饰性文本将提高鲁棒性，因为这些情况对于当前的检测和 OCR 系统仍然具有挑战性。其次，数据集主要包含正面产品图像，不包含同一产品实例的多角度视图。未来的工作可以将其扩展到多视图产品数据集，以实现跨视图的一致产品标识。最后，扩展数据集以包含其他产品类别、包装样式和品牌变化可以进一步提高稳健性和泛化性。Although the ProductConsistency dataset and training framework significantly improve product fidelity and text preservation in instruction-based image editing, several opportunities remain for future work. First, the pipeline primarily focuses on products with straight and clearly visible text layouts. Extending the framework to support curved, stylized, or decorative text would improve robustness, as these cases remain challenging for current detection and OCR systems. Second, the dataset mainly contains front-facing product images and does not include multi-angle views of the same product instance. Future work could extend this to multi-view product datasets to enable consistent product identity across viewpoints. Finally, expanding the dataset to include additional product categories, packaging styles, and branding variations could further improve robustness and generalization.
