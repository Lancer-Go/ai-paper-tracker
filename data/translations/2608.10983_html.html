1. 简介Introduction

多模式推荐系统为电子商务市场和短视频应用程序等大型平台提供支持，其中来自图像、文本和音频的丰富项目内容有助于克服稀疏的用户项目反馈Multi-modal recommender systems power large-scale platforms such as e-commerce marketplaces and short-video applications, where rich item content from images, text, and audio helps overcome sparse user-item feedback(32; 6)。最近基于图的方法通过图神经网络将特定模态信号与协同过滤融合在一起，在各个基准测试中实现了强大的性能. 2. 相关工作Related Work

基于图的多模态推荐Graph-based Multi-Modal Recommendation

多模态推荐利用图像、文本和音频等项目内容来减轻交互稀疏性并提高鲁棒性。早期的方法将模态视为辅助信息，将内容特征融合到矩阵分解或排序模型中； VBPR 通过将视觉特征注入贝叶斯个性化排名以获得隐式反馈来体现这一点Multi-modal recommendation leverages item content such as images, text, and audio to alleviate interaction sparsity and improve robustness. Early approaches treated modalities as side information, fusing content features into matrix factorization or ranking models; VBPR exemplifies this line by injecting visual features into Bayesian personalized ranking for implicit feedback(2)。在此基础上，基于注意力的方法（例如 ACF）在用户-项目-组件级别上跨模式重新加权用户偏好. Building on this, attention-based methods such as ACF reweight user preferences across modalities at the user-item-component level(1)。最近的工作采用图神经网络作为骨干：MMGCN 和 GRCN 在用户-项目图上传播模态信号以捕获高阶依赖关系. More recent work adopts graph neural networks as the backbone: MMGCN and GRCN propagate modality signals over user-item graphs to capture high-order dependencies(25; 24)，LATTICE 通过从原始模态特征中挖掘潜在内容引发的结构来扩展这一想法, LATTICE extends this idea by mining latent content-induced structures from raw modality features(30)，并且 BM3 采用自举式自我监督来从不完美的多模态信号中学习, and BM3 employs bootstrap-style self-supervision to learn from imperfect multi-modal signals(35).

尽管取得了这些进展，基于图的多模态推荐中的模态融合通常被视为时间不变的：学习单个融合权重并在用户和时间上统一应用。该表述与第 5 节中确定的模态时间尺度不匹配不一致Despite this progress, modality fusion in graph-based multi-modal recommendation is typically treated as time-invariant: a single fusion weight is learned and applied uniformly across users and time. This formulation is at odds with the modality time-scale mismatch identified in Section1，其中具有不同时间行为特征的用户需要不同的模态比例。为了解决这个限制，我们的时间感知模态路由器根据每个用户的时间行为配置文件生成个性化模态权重，用用户和时间自适应权重取代全局共享融合。, where the users with different temporal behavioral profiles require different modality proportions. To address this limitation, our temporally aware modality router generates personalized modality weights from each user’s temporal behavior profile, replacing globally shared fusion with user- and time-adaptive weighting.

用于推荐的时间建模Temporal Modeling for Recommendation

时间建模长期以来被认为对于推荐系统至关重要。 Koren 首先通过对用户偏好和项目流行度的漂移进行建模，将时间动态引入协同过滤Temporal modeling has long been recognized as essential for recommender systems. Koren first introduced temporal dynamics into collaborative filtering by modeling drift in user preferences and item popularity(7)。基于这个想法，TiSASRec 等顺序推荐器将时间间隔信息注入自注意力中，以区分顺序接近但时间较远的事件. Building on this idea, sequential recommenders such as TiSASRec inject time-interval information into self-attention to distinguish events that are sequentially close but temporally distant(8)。最近的工作面临已部署系统中的时间分布变化，采用检索和提取范式和概率泛化框架，旨在随着数据漂移保持预测稳定. More recent work confronts temporal distribution shift in deployed systems, with retrieval-and-distill paradigms and probabilistic generalization frameworks designed to keep predictions stable as data drifts(37; 34)。在多模式设置中，XSMoE 通过添加可扩展的侧专家来处理流媒体推荐中的分布变化，这些专家在新模式或项目到达时吸收它们. In multi-modal settings, XSMoE handles distribution shifts in streaming recommendation by adding expandable side experts that absorb new modalities or items as they arrive(15).

然而，这些时间模型在很大程度上仍然与多模态融合脱节。时间协同过滤和顺序推荐器对用户和项目动态进行建模，但通常在单峰交互序列上运行。最近关于时间分布变化的工作将预测视为单个不断变化的信号，而不是多种模态的融合。同样，XSMoE 的每种模式专家旨在随着时间的推移吸收新出现的模式，而不是随着现有模式在用户中的相关性发生变化而重新调整其权重。我们的工作占据了这个交叉点：模态路由器使用每个用户的时间配置文件来驱动每个用户的多模态融合，而双流长期和短期头在每个模态降噪器中添加时间调制。Yet these temporal models remain largely disconnected from multi-modal fusion. Temporal collaborative filtering and sequential recommenders model user and item dynamics, but typically operate on unimodal interaction sequences. Recent work on temporal distribution shift treats prediction as a single evolving signal rather than a fusion of multiple modalities. Likewise, XSMoE’s per-modality experts are designed to absorb newly arriving modalities over time, rather than reweight existing modalities as their relevance changes within a user. Our work occupies this intersection: the modal router uses each user’s temporal profile to drive per-user multi-modal fusion, while dual-stream long- and short-term heads add temporal modulation within each per-modality denoiser.

推荐扩散模型Diffusion Models for Recommendation

生成模型提供了歧视性排名的替代方案。早期工作使用 GAN 和 VAE 进行稀疏性下的交互建模、表示学习和数据增强Generative models offer an alternative to discriminative ranking. Early work used GANs and VAEs for interaction modeling, representation learning, and data augmentation under sparsity(19; 9)。随后，扩散模型因其训练稳定性和模拟复杂交互分布的能力而被采用，并已应用于交互生成、顺序推荐和上下文信号下的条件去噪. Diffusion models were subsequently adopted for their training stability and capacity to model complex interaction distributions and have since been applied to interaction generation, sequential recommendation, and conditional denoising under contextual signals(20; 22; 11)。在此基础上，DiffMM 通过重建模态感知的用户项目图并将其与跨模态对比学习相结合以进行对齐，将扩散扩展到多模态推荐. Building on this line, DiffMM extends diffusion to multi-modal recommendation by reconstructing modality-aware user-item graphs and combining them with cross-modal contrastive learning for alignment(6).

DiffMM 和相关的多模态扩散推荐器将降噪器置于用户上下文中，而用户上下文本身对于时间上下文是不变的：无论用户是否已转变为新的行为模式或保持稳定的长期模式，相同的上下文向量都会驱动重建。因此，重建的图无法反映模态的相关性何时发生变化，并且过时的模态引起的边缘继续通过 GCN 消息传递传播。我们通过使用双流长期和短期头的 FiLM 调制对时间信号调节每模态降噪器来解决这个问题，以便重建图响应每个用户时间上下文的变化。DiffMM and related multi-modal diffusion recommenders condition the denoiser on a user context that is itself invariant to temporal context: the same context vector drives reconstruction whether the user has shifted into a new behavioral regime or remains in a stable long-term pattern. The reconstructed graphs therefore cannot reflect when a modality’s relevance has changed, and outdated modality-induced edges continue to propagate through GCN message passing. We address this by conditioning per-modality denoisers on temporal signals through FiLM modulation with dual-stream long- and short-term heads, so that reconstructed graphs respond to shifts in each user’s temporal context.

TimeRoute combines a temporal-aware modal router, which maps each user’s temporal context to personalized modality weights, with time-conditioned diffusion reconstruction, which uses FiLM-modulated dual-stream denoisers to suppress outdated graph-level signals. Across TikTok, Amazon-Baby, and Amazon-Sports, 10-seed paired tests show consistent improvements over strong baselines. Controlled ablations confirm that the gains come from temporal signals rather than extra router parameters, while quartile analysis shows systematic per-user routing differentiation. Noise-robustness experiments further show effectiveness under uneven modality quality.

TimeRoute 有局限性。路由取决于时间戳覆盖范围；在Amazon-Sports上，重建级组件的贡献比路由器更大。当一种模态占主导地位时，就像 TikTok 上的视觉特征一样，高度活跃的用户可能会趋向于接近单一模态的权重。自适应路由减少了对低权重噪声模式的暴露，但增加了对主导模式的依赖。未来的工作可以增加模态质量估计和顺序推荐。TimeRoute has limitations. Routing depends on timestamp coverage; on Amazon-Sports, reconstruction-level components contribute more than the router. When one modality dominates, as visual features do on TikTok, highly active users may converge toward near-single-modality weights. Adaptive routing reduces exposure to down-weighted noisy modalities but increases reliance on the dominant one. Future work could add modality-quality estimation and sequential recommendation.
