摘要Abstract

传统的像素级损失函数无法在冠状血管分割中强制实施拓扑约束，尽管像素级精度很高，但仍会产生支离破碎的血管树。我们提出了 ARIADNE，一个两阶段框架，将偏好对齐感知与基于 RL 的诊断推理相结合，用于拓扑相干的狭窄检测。感知模块采用 DPO 来微调 Sa2VA 视觉语言基础模型，使用 Betti 数约束作为偏好信号，将策略调整为几何完整的血管结构，而不是像素级的重叠度量。推理模块将狭窄定位制定为马尔可夫决策过程，具有明确的拒绝机制，可自主推迟不明确的解剖候选对象，例如分叉和血管交叉，从覆盖最大化转向可靠性优化。在 1,400 张临床血管造影照片中，ARIADNE 实现了最先进的中心线 Dice 0.838，与几何基线相比，误报率降低了 41%。多中心基准 ARCADE 和 XCAD 的外部验证证实了跨采集协议的通用性。这代表了 DPO 在医学成像中拓扑对齐的首次应用，表明基于偏好的结构约束学习可以减轻拓扑违规，同时保持介入心脏病学工作流程中的诊断灵敏度。代码可在 https://github.com/qimingfan10/ARIADNE 获取。Conventional pixel-wise loss functions fail to enforce topological constraints in coronary vessel segmentation, producing fragmented vascular trees despite high pixel-level accuracy. We present ARIADNE, a two-stage framework coupling preference-aligned perception with RL-based diagnostic reasoning for topologically coherent stenosis detection. The perception module employs DPO to fine-tune the Sa2VA vision-language foundation model using Betti number constraints as preference signals, aligning the policy toward geometrically complete vessel structures rather than pixel-wise overlap metrics. The reasoning module formulates stenosis localization as a Markov Decision Process with an explicit rejection mechanism that autonomously defers ambiguous anatomical candidates such as bifurcations and vessel crossings, shifting from coverage maximization to reliability optimization. On 1,400 clinical angiograms, ARIADNE achieves state-of-the-art centerline Dice of 0.838, reduces false positives by 41% compared to geometric baselines. External validation on multi-center benchmarks ARCADE and XCAD confirms generalization across acquisition protocols. This represents the first application of DPO for topological alignment in medical imaging, demonstrating that preference-based learning over structural constraints mitigates topological violations while maintaining diagnostic sensitivity in interventional cardiology workflows. 1 简介Introduction

冠状动脉疾病 (CAD) 仍然是全球发病率和死亡率的主要原因Coronary Artery Disease (CAD) remains a leading cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide[GBD2023]，需要提供准确、可重复和有效评估的诊断方式。有创 X 射线冠状动脉造影 (XCA) 是 CAD 诊断和指导经皮冠状动脉介入治疗 (PCI) 的主要工具, requiring diagnostic modalities that provide accurate, reproducible, and efficient assessment. Invasive X-ray Coronary Angiography (XCA) serves as the primary tool for CAD diagnosis and guidance of Percutaneous Coronary Interventions (PCI)[Jennifer2022]，提供可视化血流动力学所需的高时间分辨率, offering high temporal resolution necessary for visualizing hemodynamic flow[Ramos2025lightweight]。然而，当前的临床工作流程严重依赖人工解释，这一过程的特点是观察者之间存在显着的差异并且容易导致临床医生疲劳. However, current clinical workflows rely heavily on manual interpretation, a process characterized by significant inter-observer variability and susceptibility to clinician fatigue[Miguel2022]。随着医疗机构普遍采用图片存档和通信系统 (PACS)，被动图像存储与主动、自动化临床解读之间仍然存在着重大差距。虽然医院已经实施了数字图像存储，但他们缺乏能够将原始成像数据转化为可操作的临床见解的自动化系统。介入手术数量的不断增加使得纯粹的手动解释越来越不可持续，从而产生了对计算机辅助诊断系统的需求，该系统可以弥合数据采集和临床决策之间的差距。. As healthcare institutions universally adopt Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS), a critical gap persists between passive image storage and active, automated clinical interpretation. While hospitals have implemented digital image storage, they lack automated systems capable of transforming raw imaging data into actionable clinical insights. The growing volume of interventional procedures makes purely manual interpretation increasingly unsustainable, creating demand for Computer-Aided Diagnosis systems that can bridge the gap between data acquisition and clinical decision-making.

More recently, Vision Transformers (ViTs) have been introduced to capture global spatial relationships[Chen2021TransUNet]。尽管实现了高像素级性能指标，但这些模型在保留血管拓扑方面面临着关键限制。传统损失函数，包括交叉熵和骰子损失. Despite achieving high pixel-level performance metrics, these models face a critical limitation in preserving vascular topology. Traditional loss functions, including Cross-Entropy and Dice Loss[Milletari2016VNet]，独立优化像素级精度，无需明确惩罚拓扑错误, optimize pixel-level accuracy independently without explicitly penalizing topological errors[Shit2021CVPR]。因此，这些模型经常产生支离破碎的血管树，其中远端分支显得断开，特别是由于下采样操作期间细血管中的信号丢失. Consequently, these models frequently produce fragmented vessel trees where distal branches appear disconnected, particularly due to signal loss in thin vessels during downsampling operations[Chang2024optimizing]。在冠状动脉血流动力学分析中，拓扑连接至关重要；如果不连续性妨碍了准确的中心线提取和随后的几何分析，则具有高 Dice 分数的分割仍然不足以用于临床。. In coronary hemodynamics analysis, topological connectivity is essential; a segmentation with high Dice score remains insufficient for clinical use if discontinuities prevent accurate centerline extraction and subsequent geometric analysis.

最近出现的基础规模视觉语言模型（VLM）为医学图像分割引入了一种补充方法。 SAM3等型号The recent emergence of foundation-scale Vision-Language Models (VLMs) has introduced a complementary approach to medical image segmentation. Models such as SAM3[Carion2025SAM3]和 MedSAM3and MedSAM3[Liu2025MedSAM3]利用大型语言模型来实现基于提示的分割，其中文本描述指导掩模生成。这些架构展示了令人印象深刻的语义理解，能够根据学习到的视觉语言对应关系正确识别容器的构成。然而，他们对通用自然图像数据集的训练造成了基本的语义拓扑差距：虽然 VLM 理解血管结构的概念类别，但它们缺乏在低对比度、基于投影的 X 射线血管造影中强化结构连续性所需的特定领域的解剖先验。实证评估表明，通用 VLM 始终能够产生语义正确但拓扑碎片化的分割，即正确地将像素分类为血管，但无法维持血流动力学建模所必需的连接树结构。这种失败源于他们的优化目标：VLM 最大化像素级重叠（Dice、IoUleverage large language models to enable prompt-based segmentation, where textual descriptions guide mask generation. These architectures demonstrate impressive semantic understanding, correctly identifying what constitutes a vessel based on learned visual-linguistic correspondences. However, their training on generic natural image datasets creates a fundamental semantic-topological gap: while VLMs comprehend the conceptual category of a vascular structure, they lack the domain-specific anatomical priors necessary to enforce structural continuity in low-contrast, projection-based X-ray angiography. Empirical evaluation reveals that general-purpose VLMs consistently produce semantically correct but topologically fragmented segmentations—correctly classifying pixels as vessel while failing to maintain the connected tree structure essential for hemodynamic modeling. In coronary angiography, where vessel diameters approach image resolution limits and contrast variability is substantial, the absence of explicit topological constraints results in high-confidence predictions of isolated vessel fragments that are clinically unusable for stenosis quantification or flow analysis.

分割的这种限制直接影响狭窄检测系统的准确性。当前的自动化框架主要遵循顺序方法，其中分割和狭窄检测作为独立任务执行This limitation in segmentation directly impacts the accuracy of stenosis detection systems. Current automated frameworks predominantly follow a sequential approach where segmentation and stenosis detection are performed as independent tasks[Liu2023AQC, Huang2025]。在这些系统中，几何算法遍历分段的中心线来识别变窄的区域。然而，这些确定性算法缺乏区分病理性狭窄和常见解剖伪影（包括血管交叉）的能力. In these systems, geometric algorithms traverse the segmented centerline to identify regions of narrowing. However, these deterministic algorithms lack the ability to distinguish pathological stenosis from common anatomical artifacts, including vessel crossings[Hannink2014vesselness]、分叉和透视, bifurcations, and foreshortening[Yang2020CVPR]，导致假阳性率升高。相反，虽然YOLO等深层物体检测器已被应用于直接病变识别, resulting in elevated false positive rates. Specifically, generic object detectors treat lesions as isolated bounding boxes, failing to validate whether a detected stenosis actually resides within a continuous, hemodynamically relevant vascular segment. These limitations have hindered clinical adoption due to the high rate of false alarms that reduce system reliability.

5 讨论Discussion

本研究评估了一个分层框架，该框架将拓扑约束分割与基于强化学习的狭窄检测相结合，用于自动冠状动脉造影分析。结果表明，感知模块中血管连接性的改善直接使得检测模块中的诊断推理更加可靠，解决了结构表示和临床决策之间的相互依赖性，这限制了先前的自动化方法。This study evaluated a hierarchical framework integrating topologically-constrained segmentation with RL-based stenosis detection for automated coronary angiography analysis. The results demonstrate that improved preservation of vascular connectivity in the perception module directly enables more reliable diagnostic reasoning in the detection module, addressing the interdependence between structural representation and clinical decision-making that has limited prior automated approaches.

当代的血管分割方法（包括传统的损失函数和基础模型架构）主要针对像素级精度进行优化，而没有明确强制拓扑连续性，从而导致了我们所说的语义拓扑差距。标准分割损失（交叉熵、骰子损失）可最大限度地减少局部预测误差，但对血管碎片和较小的边界误差给予同等的惩罚。更关键的是，MedSAM3 等基础模型Contemporary approaches to vessel segmentation—including both conventional loss functions and foundation model architectures—optimize primarily for pixel-level accuracy without explicitly enforcing topological continuity, resulting in what we term the Semantic-Topological Gap. Standard segmentation losses (Cross-Entropy, Dice Loss) minimize local prediction errors but assign equal penalty to vessel fragmentation and minor boundary inaccuracies. More critically, foundation models such as MedSAM3[Liu2025MedSAM3]尽管进行了大规模的预训练，但仍面临着这种限制：虽然它们在语义上识别突出的结构，但它们严重无法在专门的医学环境中保持几何连续性。我们的定量分析直接强调了这一现象——尽管规模庞大，MedSAM3 的 clDice 仅为 0.7105，远远低于传统的、小得多的 U-Net, despite large-scale pretraining, struggle even more with this limitation: while they recognize prominent structures semantically, they severely fail to maintain geometric continuity in specialized medical contexts. Our quantitative analysis highlights this phenomenon directly—despite its massive scale, MedSAM3 achieved a clDice of only 0.7105, substantially underperforming the conventional, much smaller U-Net[Ronneberger2015unet]（0.7987）。这种鲜明的对比证明，简单地缩放通用模型容量并不能解决这一差距，因为这两种方法都没有固有地编码冠状血管必须形成连接的管状网络之前的特定领域解剖结构。(0.7987). 通过对解剖学上具有挑战性的病例（占数据集的 20.8%）进行有针对性的训练所证明的计算效率表明，在具有不同数据可用性的机构中资源受限的部署场景是可行的。虽然需要扩展到多视图分析以及与互补成像模式的集成来解决 2D 血管造影中固有的投影模糊性，但当前的结果为在结构一致性对于临床解释至关重要的领域中开发自动分析系统奠定了方法基础。The computational efficiency demonstrated through targeted training on anatomically challenging cases, constituting 20.8% of the dataset, suggests feasibility for resource-constrained deployment scenarios across institutions with varying data availability. 这项工作表明，弥合被动图像存档和自动诊断洞察之间的差距需要的不仅仅是先进的模式识别，它还需要计算模型与控制临床决策的解剖学和生理学原理明确一致。所提出的框架有助于开发能够在介入心脏病学工作流程中充当可靠决策支持工具的自动化系统，将传统信息学范式从回顾性存储转变为前瞻性临床情报。通过确定拓扑有效性可以通过偏好优化来学习和转移，本研究提供了一种将通用视觉基础模型适应几何完整性不可协商的安全关键医疗应用的途径。This work demonstrates that bridging the gap between passive image archival and automated diagnostic insight requires more than advanced pattern recognition—it demands explicit alignment of computational models with the anatomical and physiological principles that govern clinical decision-making. The proposed framework contributes toward the development of automated systems capable of functioning as reliable decision support tools within interventional cardiology workflows, transforming the traditional informatics paradigm from retrospective storage to prospective clinical intelligence. By establishing that topological validity can be learned and transferred through preference optimization, this study provides a pathway for adapting general-purpose vision foundation models to safety-critical medical applications where geometric integrity is non-negotiable.
