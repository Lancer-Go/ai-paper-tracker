1 简介Introduction

文本到图像的生成正在从生成合理的图像转向实现用户指定的视觉意图。早期的进展通常通过生成的图像看起来是否令人信服并与粗略的提示保持一致来衡量Text-to-image generation is shifting from producing plausible images toward realizing user-specified visual intent. Earlier progress was often measured by whether generated images looked convincing and aligned with prompts at a coarse levelSaharia et al. (2022); Betker et al. (2023); Esser et al. (2024)。然而，随着这些系统进入实用和创造性的工作流程，提示越来越多地描述场景，其定义细节必须保持，结果才能忠实. As these systems enter practical and creative workflows, however, prompts increasingly describe scenes whose defining details must hold for the result to be faithfulHu et al. (2023); Huang et al. (2023); Ghosh et al. (2023); Li et al. (2024)。这改变了生成的标准：图像不仅应该显得自然，而且还应该正确地实例化用户想要描绘的场景。. This changes the standard for generation: an image should not only appear natural, but also correctly instantiate the scene the user intended to depict.

忠实地实现复杂的视觉意图具有挑战性，因为提示所施加的要求并不同时全部变得可行。有些要求从一开始就是明确的，而另一些要求只有在系统根据外部背景或场景暗示的原因后才变得确定Faithfully realizing complex visual intents is challenging because the requirements imposed by a prompt do not all become actionable at the same time. Some requirements are explicit from the beginning, while others become determinate only after the system grounds external context or reasons about what the scene impliesLi et al. (2025a); Son et al. (2025); He et al. (2026a); Feng et al. (2026); Chen et al. (2026)。我们将这些要求称为. We refer to these requirements as语义承诺semantic commitments：最终图像必须满足才能满足用户意图的条件。因此，挑战不仅在于发现这些承诺，还在于保持每一项承诺的可识别性，直到可以直观地实现和检查。: conditions that the final image must satisfy for the user’s intent to be fulfilled. The challenge is therefore not only to discover these commitments, but also to keep each one identifiable until it can be visually realized and checked.

最近的工作逐渐超越了一次性提示图像生成的范畴，引入了检索、规划和迭代细化等多步骤干预措施Recent work has increasingly moved beyond one-shot prompt-to-image generation by introducing multi-step interventions such as retrieval, planning, and iterative refinementLi et al. (2025a); Son et al. (2025); Feng et al. (2026); Chen et al. (2025a); Li et al. (2025b); Wu et al. ; Ye et al. (2025)。这些干预措施改善了复杂生成的不同方面，从解决丢失的信息到纠正可见的故障。然而，使生成多步骤本身并不能确保生命周期的连续性：承诺可以在生成之前解决，在生成之后检查，并在稍后的步骤中修改，但这些操作可能不会仍然与相同的可识别单元相关联。因此，即使系统检索到相关信息或识别出真正的故障，生成目标仍然可能被稀释，错误可能被错误归因，或者修复操作可能缺乏针对性。我们将这种生命周期不连续性称为. These interventions improve different aspects of complex generation, from resolving missing information to correcting visible failures. However, making generation multi-step does not by itself ensure lifecycle continuity: a commitment may be resolved before generation, checked after generation, and revised in a later step, yet these operations may not remain tied to the same identifiable unit. As a result, even when a system retrieves relevant information or identifies a real failure, the generation target may still be diluted, the error may be misattributed, or the repair action may be poorly targeted. We refer to this lifecycle discontinuity as the概念裂痕Conceptual Rift。这就提出了一个核心问题：. This raises a central question:语义承诺如何在整个发电生命周期中作为统一的操作单元保持可表示、可验证和可操作？how can semantic commitments remain representable, verifiable, and actionable as unified operational units throughout the generation lifecycle?

为了应对这一挑战，我们建议To address this challenge, we propose范围SCOPE，一个用于复杂图像生成的规范引导的技能编排框架。 SCOPE 在不断发展的结构化规范中明确了语义承诺，该规范充当整个生成生命周期的共享接口。在此规范的指导下，SCOPE 有条件地调用检索、推理和修复技能，以查明缺失的外部信息、推断隐含需求并修改违反的承诺。它还根据当前规范中表示的承诺来验证生成的图像。通过将技能输出和验证结果写回规范，SCOPE 保持复杂的视觉意图可跨阶段操作，从而允许生成过程通过统一的生命周期进行，而不是一系列互不相关的本地操作。, a specification-guided skill orchestration framework for complex image generation. SCOPE makes semantic commitments explicit in an evolving structured specification, which serves as a shared interface across the generation lifecycle. Guided by this specification, SCOPE conditionally invokes retrieval, reasoning, and repair skills to ground missing external information, infer implicit requirements, and revise violated commitments. It also verifies generated images against the commitments represented in the current specification. By writing skill outputs and verification results back to the specification, SCOPE keeps complex visual intents actionable across stages, allowing generation to proceed through a unified lifecycle rather than a sequence of disconnected local operations.

By linking constraints to their prerequisite entities, Gen-Arena supports entity-first evaluation and enables diagnosis of missing entities, violated constraints, and downstream failures caused by unmet prerequisites.

为了在整个生成生命周期中保持语义承诺的可识别性，SCOPE 将每个请求表示为不断发展的结构化语义规范To keep semantic commitments identifiable across the generation lifecycle, SCOPE represents each request as an evolving structured semantic specificationz=(E,C,U)z=(E,C,U)。在这里，. We group constraints inCC分为三种类型。属性约束指定实体级要求，例如身份、外观、数量和可见文本。关系约束指定实体之间的交互或语义关系。布局约束指定场景中实体的放置及其与周围环境的组合。重要的是，未知数into three types. Attribute constraints specify entity-level requirements such as identity, appearance, quantity, and visible text. Relation constraints specify interactions or semantic relationships between entities. Importantly, unknowns inUU不被视为独立问题。每个未知数都附加到提示级、实体级或约束级所有者，指示它要解决哪个承诺。该所有权链接允许检索或推理结果更新规范的相应部分，并随后允许将验证失败映射回相同的实体或约束。当已验证的故障不再与未解决的未知相关联时，映射的实体或约束将成为修复的目标。因此，该规范提供了用于在迭代中携带解析信息、验证结果和修复目标的操作接口。are not treated as independent questions. Each unknown is attached to a prompt-, entity-, or constraint-level owner, indicating which commitment it is meant to resolve. Thus, the specification provides the operational interface for carrying resolved information, verification outcomes, and repair targets across iterations.

3.1.3 条件技能编排Conditional Skill Orchestration

SCOPE 通过当前规范协调检索、推理和修复技能，而不是将它们作为固定的管道步骤应用。当承诺依赖于缺失的外部证据（例如事实信息或参考身份线索）时，就会调用检索。当在可靠综合之前必须解决隐含的或未指定的承诺时，就会调用推理。当验证将视觉故障映射回不再需要额外基础或推理的实体或约束时，将调用修复。每项技能都锚定于具体的未知或违反的承诺，其输出更新相同的规范：检索和推理接近未解决的未知数，而修复记录如何修改违反的项目。通过这种方式，SCOPE 根据当前规范中尚未解决或违反的问题来调整生成生命周期。SCOPE orchestrates retrieval, reasoning, and repair skills through the current specification rather than applying them as fixed pipeline steps. Retrieval is invoked when a commitment depends on missing external evidence, such as factual information or reference identity cues. Reasoning is invoked when an implicit or underspecified commitment must be resolved before reliable synthesis. Repair is invoked when verification maps a visual failure back to an entity or constraint that no longer requires additional grounding or reasoning. Each skill is anchored to a concrete unknown or violated commitment, and its output updates the same specification: retrieval and reasoning close unresolved unknowns, while repair records how violated items are revised. In this way, SCOPE adapts the generation lifecycle according to what remains unresolved or violated in the current specification.

3.2 Gen竞技场Gen-Arena

Gen-Arena 评估复杂图像生成是否满足结构化视觉意图，而不是仅在粗略级别上匹配提示。每个实例将自然语言提示与评估规范配对，该评估规范标识所需的实体及其必须满足的约束，从而能够对生成的图像进行承诺级评估。Gen-Arena evaluates whether complex image generation fulfills structured visual intents rather than only matching prompts at a coarse level. Each instance pairs a natural-language prompt with an evaluation specification that identifies the required entities and the constraints they must satisfy, enabling commitment-level evaluation of generated images.

Gen-Arena是通过人工注释管道手动构建的，涵盖卡通、游戏、体育、娱乐、竞赛和仪式六大类。当身份或外观无法仅通过文本可靠地指定时，注释者首先编写自然的用户提示并收集参考图像。然后，他们识别每个提示中的可见目标实体，并注释这些目标的原子约束，包括属性、关系和布局。每个约束都链接到它所依赖的实体，允许评估区分丢失的实体故障和正确实现的实体上未满足的约束。生成的基准包含 300 个实例、1,954 个实体、2,533 个约束和 310 个参考图像。数字Gen-Arena is manually constructed through a human annotation pipeline covering six categories: cartoon, game, sports, entertainment, competition, and ceremony. Annotators first write natural user prompts and collect reference images when identity or appearance cannot be specified reliably by text alone. They then identify visible target entities in each prompt and annotate atomic constraints over these targets, including attributes, relations, and layouts. Each constraint is linked to the entities it depends on, allowing evaluation to distinguish missing-entity failures from unsatisfied constraints over correctly realized entities. The resulting benchmark contains 300 instances, 1,954 entities, 2,533 constraints, and 310 reference images. 4.4 分析Analysis

我们通过询问两个有关生成生命周期的问题来进一步分析 SCOPE。We further analyze SCOPE by asking two questions about the generation lifecycle.

Q1：仅结构化分解就足够了吗？Q1: Is structured decomposition alone sufficient?消融结果表明，结构化规范本身不应被理解为更强的提示。它的主要功能是跨阶段保存可寻址的语义单元。如果没有检索和推理，未解决的未知数仍然暴露在规范中；如果不进行修复，则无法针对生成后检测到的违规行为。因此，该规范很有用，因为它提供了一个稳定的接口，通过该接口，不同的技能可以对相同的承诺进行操作。The ablation results show that the structured specification should not be understood as a stronger prompt by itself. Its main function is to preserve addressable semantic units across stages. Without retrieval and reasoning, unresolved unknowns remain exposed in the specification; without repair, violations detected after generation cannot be targeted. Thus, the specification is useful because it provides a stable interface through which different skills can operate on the same commitments.

Q2：为什么SCOPE要改进EGIP？Q2: Why does SCOPE improve EGIP?表Table4有助于解释 SCOPE 收益从何而来。直接生成基线通过了许多单独的实体检查，但 EGIP 要求实例中的每个必需实体都正确，然后才能记入其约束。因此，即使 Nano Banana Pro 的项目级实体通行证为 0.82，许多实例仍然会留下至少一个缺失或错误接地的实体，从而阻止依赖属性、关系或布局。 Qwen-Image 和 Z-Image-Turbo 显示了类似的模式：它们的 Entity Pass 超过 0.83，但它们的 EGIP 仍然接近于零，因为实体错误和下游约束违规在实例级别累积。 SCOPE 通过在整个生成生命周期中将实体及其相关约束与相同的结构化规范相关联来改进 EGIP。这减少了级联实体到约束的失败，将实体传递提高到 0.92，门控约束传递提高到 0.83，整体 EGIP 提高到 0.60。helps explain where SCOPE gains come from. Direct generation baselines pass many individual entity checks, but EGIP requires every required entity in an instance to be correct before its constraints can be credited. Thus, even an item-level Entity Pass of 0.82 for Nano Banana Pro still leaves many instances with at least one missing or incorrectly grounded entity, which then blocks dependent attributes, relations, or layouts. Qwen-Image and Z-Image-Turbo show a similar pattern: their Entity Pass exceeds 0.83, yet their EGIP remains near zero because entity errors and downstream constraint violations accumulate at the instance level. SCOPE improves EGIP by keeping entities and their associated constraints tied to the same structured specification across the generation lifecycle. This reduces cascading entity-to-constraint failures, raising Entity Pass to 0.92, Gated Constraint Pass to 0.83, and overall EGIP to 0.60.

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 SCOPE，一个用于复杂图像生成的规范引导的技能编排框架。 SCOPE 通过在不断发展的结构化规范中表示复杂视觉意图所施加的语义承诺来解决概念裂痕。然后，它围绕尚未解决或违反的承诺协调检索、推理和修复技能。我们进一步引入了 Gen-Arena，这是一个用于承诺级别评估的人工注释基准，以及 EGIP 作为严格的实体优先通过指标。实验表明，SCOPE 大大优于 Gen-Arena 上所有评估的基线，并在外部基准上取得了强劲的结果，这表明在整个生成生命周期中保持承​​诺可识别是实现更忠实的复杂图像生成的实用途径。We introduced SCOPE, a specification-guided skill orchestration framework for complex image generation. SCOPE addresses the Conceptual Rift by representing the semantic commitments imposed by complex visual intents in an evolving structured specification. It then orchestrates retrieval, reasoning, and repair skills around commitments that remain unresolved or violated. We further introduced Gen-Arena, a human-annotated benchmark for commitment-level evaluation, together with EGIP as a strict entity-first pass metric. Experiments show that SCOPE substantially outperforms all evaluated baselines on Gen-Arena and achieves strong results on external benchmarks, suggesting that keeping commitments identifiable across the generation lifecycle is a practical path toward more faithful complex image generation.

局限性Limitations

虽然 SCOPE 改善了承诺级意图的实现，但它也有一些局限性。首先，SCOPE 需要在每个实例最多三次迭代中多次调用 MLLM、图像生成器、验证器和可选的检索服务，从而导致比一次性生成更高的推理时间成本和延迟。可以通过自适应提前停止或更选择性的技能调用来减少这种开销。其次，SCOPE 依赖于项目级验证来安排后续维修操作。因此，验证错误可以在整个生命周期中传播：误报可能会触发对正确实现的承诺进行不必要的修复，而误报可能会导致真正的故障无法解决。因此，改进验证器校准对于使验证引导修复更加可靠非常重要。While SCOPE improves commitment-level intent realization, it also has several limitations. First, SCOPE requires multiple calls to the MLLM, image generator, verifier, and optionally retrieval services across up to three iterations per instance, leading to higher inference-time cost and latency than one-shot generation. This overhead could be reduced through adaptive early stopping or more selective skill invocation. Second, SCOPE depends on item-level verification to route subsequent repair actions. Verification errors can therefore propagate through the lifecycle: a false negative may trigger unnecessary repair of a correctly realized commitment, while a false positive may leave a genuine failure unresolved. Improving verifier calibration is therefore important for making verification-guided repair more reliable.
