删除什么，保留什么：用于一体式图像恢复的双重模糊校正What to Remove, What to Preserve: Dual-Ambiguity Rectification for All-in-One Image Restoration

摘要。Abstract.

一体化图像恢复旨在在统一框架内处理各种退化。现有方法通常在共享潜在空间中对异构退化条件进行编码，其中与退化相关的线索和场景内容可能保持纠缠在一起。我们将由此产生的挑战描述为双重歧义：通道调制中的语义歧义和恢复响应中的空间歧义，这可能导致内容损坏和残留伪影。为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了 DAR-Net，一种用于一体式图像恢复的双模糊校正网络。 DAR-Net 首先引入退化原型表示（DAR）模块，通过单纯形约束原型混合建模构建结构化退化状态。基于此状态，语义歧义纠正 (SeAR) 模块会生成降级感知提示，以改进解码器中的通道调节。空间模糊校正 (SpAR) 模块进一步规范正交响应子空间的退化感知和互补特征，减少删除和保留线索之间的空间干扰。对标准一体式恢复基准的大量实验表明，DAR-Net 在三级降级和五级降级设置下均实现了最佳整体性能，平均 PSNR 比最强竞争对手分别提高了 0.14 dB 和 0.34 dB；它还在 CDD-11 和 WeatherBench 上显示出卓越的性能。All-in-one image restoration aims to handle diverse degradations within a unified framework. Existing methods commonly encode heterogeneous degradation conditions in a shared latent space, where degradation-related cues and scene content can remain entangled. We characterize the resulting challenge as dual ambiguity: semantic ambiguity in channel-wise modulation and spatial ambiguity in restoration responses, which can lead to content corruption and residual artifacts. To mitigate this issue, we propose DAR-Net, a Dual-Ambiguity Rectification Network for all-in-one image restoration. DAR-Net first introduces a Degradation Archetype Representation (DAR) module to construct a structured degradation state through simplex-constrained archetype mixture modeling. Based on this state, a Semantic Ambiguity Rectification (SeAR) module generates degradation-aware prompts to improve channel-wise conditioning in the decoder. A Spatial Ambiguity Rectification (SpAR) module further regularizes degradation-aware and complementary features toward orthogonal response subspaces, reducing spatial interference between removal and preservation cues. Extensive experiments on standard all-in-one restoration benchmarks show that DAR-Net achieves the best overall performance under both three-degradation and five-degradation settings, improving the average PSNR over the strongest competitor by 0.14 dB and 0.34 dB, respectively; it additionally shows superior performance on CDD-11 and WeatherBench.

1. 简介Introduction

图像恢复旨在从受噪声、雾霾和降雨条件等因素影响的退化观测中恢复清晰的视觉内容。作为现实世界多媒体内容捕获和增强管道的前端步骤，图像恢复也与多媒体内容质量密切相关。大多数现有的恢复方法都是在预定义的退化设置下开发的，并且许多代表性模型Image restoration aims to recover clean visual content from degraded observations affected by factors such as noise, haze, and rain conditions. As a front-end step in real-world multimedia content capture and enhancement pipelines, image restoration is also closely related to multimedia content quality. Most existing restoration methods are developed under a predefined degradation setting, and many representative models(Chen et al., 2020, 2022; Zamir et al., 2022)实践中仍按任务具体部署。这种范例会导致相当大的计算和存储开销，并限制复杂环境中的泛化。为了解决这一限制，一体式图像恢复（AIR）已经作为一个统一的框架出现，用于处理单个模型中的各种退化are still deployed in a task-specific manner in practice. Such a paradigm leads to considerable computational and storage overhead and limits generalization in complex environments. To address this limitation, all-in-one image restoration (AIR) has emerged as a unified framework for handling diverse degradations within a single model(Li et al., 2022; Potlapalli et al., 2023; Conde et al., 2024).

早期的 AIR 方法通常依赖于多分支设计，其中不同的退化类型由单独的分支或特定于任务的模块处理。尽管此类设计提高了退化专业化，但它们的参数成本通常随着退化类型的数量而变化，这限制了可扩展性，并且随着恢复场景变得更加多样化，模型部署效率越来越低Early AIR methods often rely on multi-branch designs, where different degradation types are handled by separate branches or task-specific modules. Although such designs improve degradation specialization, their parameter cost typically scales with the number of degradation types, which limits scalability and makes model deployment increasingly inefficient as restoration scenarios become more diverse(Li et al., 2020; Han et al., 2021)。为了提高可扩展性，后续研究越来越多地采用共享主干条件恢复，其中统一网络由退化线索进行调制. To improve scalability, subsequent studies increasingly adopt shared-backbone conditional restoration, where a unified network is modulated by degradation cues(Li et al., 2022; Potlapalli et al., 2023; Tian et al., 2025)。在这个范式中，已经探索了不同的调节机制。基于提示的方法通过从输入中导出轻量级调节信号并将其注入共享主干，从而提供了具有代表性且高效的解决方案，从而能够使用有限的附加参数进行输入自适应恢复. Within this paradigm, different conditioning mechanisms have been explored. Prompt-based methods provide a representative and efficient solution by deriving lightweight conditioning signals from the input and injecting them into a shared backbone, thereby enabling input-adaptive restoration with limited additional parameters(Ai et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025b)。与此同时，其他替代方案（例如基于 MoE 的路由）通过动态专家选择来增强模型容量，从而允许在必要时由不同的专家处理不同的恢复模式. In parallel, other alternatives such as MoE-based routing enhance model capacity through dynamic expert selection, allowing different restoration patterns to be handled by different experts when necessary(Yu et al., 2024; Zamfir et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2024)。然而，尽管这些方法在引入退化感知条件方面有所不同，但它们仍然主要依赖于统一恢复管道内的共享潜在表示。因此，一个常见的困难仍然没有得到充分解决：与退化相关的线索和与内容相关的表示通常以纠缠的方式编码在共享的潜在空间中。. However, although these methods differ in how they introduce degradation-aware conditioning, they still predominantly rely on shared latent representations within a unified restoration pipeline. As a result, a common difficulty remains insufficiently addressed: degradation-related cues and content-related representations are often encoded in an entangled manner within the shared latent space.

因此，现有的统一修复模型仍然面临着区分哪些内容应去除、哪些内容应保留的困难。这一挑战通常体现在两种典型的故障模式中，如图 1 所示。Therefore, existing unified restoration models still face difficulty in distinguishing what should be removed from what should be preserved. 2.1. 特定任务的图像恢复Task-Specific Image Restoration

传统的图像恢复方法通常是针对单一退化类型设计的，例如去噪Traditional image restoration methods are typically designed for a single degradation type, such as denoising(Liang et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2022; Shen et al., 2022), 去模糊, deblurring(Kong et al., 2022; Whang et al., 2021; Cho et al., 2021), 排水, deraining(Chen et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2020; Yi et al., 2021; Xiao et al., 2022)和去雾, and dehazing(Chen et al., 2023b; Shen et al., 2023; Song et al., 2022; Cai et al., 2016)。他们的目标是在预定义的降级设置下学习从降级图像到干净图像的直接映射。随着恢复架构的发展，许多方法逐渐从高度定制的特定任务设计转向更通用的恢复主干。代表型号如IPT. Their goal is to learn a direct mapping from degraded images to clean images under a predefined degradation setting. With the development of restoration architectures, many methods have gradually moved from heavily customized task-specific designs toward more general restoration backbones. Representative models such as IPT(Chen et al., 2020), 斯温红外, SwinIR(Liang et al., 2021), 乌弗默, Uformer(Wang et al., 2021), 雷斯特, Restormer(Zamir et al., 2022), 全国广播电视网, NAFNet(Chen et al., 2022)，和马克西姆, and MAXIM(Tu et al., 2022)通过更强大的特征建模和更广泛的上下文交互来提高恢复质量，体现了这一趋势。最近的图像超分辨率方法进一步探索混合 Mamba-Transformer 建模，以提高高效的远程交互exemplify this trend by improving restoration quality through stronger feature modeling and broader contextual interaction. Recent image super-resolution methods further explore hybrid Mamba–Transformer modeling to improve efficient long-range interaction(Liu et al., 2025a)。虽然特定于任务的图像恢复方法对于单个退化类型是有效的，但它们对单独的特定于退化模型的依赖导致可扩展性差，并限制了它们在统一的现实世界恢复设置中的适用性。. While task-specific image restoration methods are effective for individual degradation types, their reliance on separate degradation-specific models leads to poor scalability and limits their applicability in unified real-world restoration settings.

2.2. 多合一图像修复All-in-one Image Restoration

为了提高统一现实世界恢复场景中的可扩展性，一体式图像恢复旨在使用单个统一模型处理各种退化，使其更适合退化类型未知或混合的实际设置。现有方法的主要区别在于如何将退化信息合并到共享恢复管道中。代表性的早期方向包括退化表示学习，其中 AirNet 学习对比退化表示To improve scalability in unified real-world restoration scenarios, all-in-one image restoration aims to handle diverse degradations with a single unified model, making it more suitable for practical settings where the degradation type is unknown or mixed. Existing methods mainly differ in how degradation information is incorporated into a shared restoration pipeline. Representative early directions include degradation representation learning, where AirNet learns contrastive degradation representations(Li et al., 2022);基于提示的调节，其中 PromptIR; prompt-based conditioning, where PromptIR(Potlapalli et al., 2023), 指导IR, InstructIR(Conde et al., 2024)和 UP-Restorer, and UP-Restorer(Liu et al., 2025b)将学习到的提示或指令注入恢复网络；和多模式指导，其中 DA-CLIPinject learned prompts or instructions into the restoration network; and multimodal guidance, where DA-CLIP(Luo et al., 2024)和MPerceiverand MPerceiver(Ai et al., 2024)利用大规模视觉语言先验进行恢复。后续工作通过加强退化建模和共享表示学习进一步改进统一恢复leverage large-scale vision-language priors for restoration. Subsequent works further improve unified restoration either by strengthening degradation modeling and shared representation learning(Chen et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023; Tang et al., 2026)或者通过引入降解专业专家来更好地处理不同的降解模式or by introducing degradation-specialized experts to better handle diverse degradation patterns(Zamfir et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025)。尽管有这些不同的设计，大多数现有方法仍然依赖于退化线索来调节、组织或路由统一恢复网络内的共享特征。相比之下，我们的方法侧重于减少特征调制过程中的退化内容纠缠，以便模型能够更好地区分应该删除的内容和应该保留的内容。. Despite these different designs, most existing methods still rely on degradation cues to condition, organize, or route shared features within a unified restoration network. In contrast, our method focuses on reducing degradation-content entanglement during feature modulation, so that the model can better distinguish what should be removed from what should be preserved.

5. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 DAR-Net，一种用于一体式图像恢复的双模糊校正网络。我们发现现有的统一恢复方法经常遭受通道表示中的语义模糊性和空间响应中的空间模糊性的困扰。为了解决这个问题，我们引入了 DAR 来学习结构化退化状态，引入 SeAR 来改进通道方面的退化辨别，引入 SpAR 来减少退化和内容之间的空间纠缠。大量实验表明，DAR-Net 在不同的退化情况下实现了强大且平衡的恢复性能。这些结果表明，明确建模应该删除的内容和应该保留的内容是统一图像恢复的有效方向。In this paper, we presented DAR-Net, a dual-ambiguity rectification network for all-in-one image restoration. We identify that existing unified restoration methods often suffer from semantic ambiguity in channel-wise representations and spatial ambiguity in spatial responses. To address this, we introduced DAR to learn structured degradation states, SeAR to improve channel-wise degradation discrimination, and SpAR to reduce spatial entanglement between degradation and content. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DAR-Net achieves strong and balanced restoration performance across diverse degradations. These results suggest that explicitly modeling what should be removed and what should be preserved is an effective direction for unified image restoration.
