1 简介Introduction

许多现代信息系统都会生成连续的事件日志，作为与具体案例相关的按时间排序的活动。 例如，患者入院、服务票的签发或证书到期。 日志通常包含允许人们回答有关相应流程的核心问题的信息。 过程挖掘的学科Many modern information systems produce continuous event logs as time-ordered activities associated with concrete cases. Examples include a patient admitted to a hospital, the issuing of a service ticket, or the expiration of a certificate. The logs often contain information that allows one to answer central questions about the corresponding process. The discipline of process mining[Aalst16]研究从这些日志中系统地提取模型来描述和预测过程行为。 因此，经典方法对离线数据进行操作，studies the systematic extraction of models from these logs to describe and predict process behavior. Thereby, classical approaches operate on offline data,例如e.g.，评估并建议优化流程。 相比之下，最近的方法还针对生成事件日志时有关流程的流式（或在线）问题，从而实现了离线方法无法实现的新型应用领域, evaluating and suggesting an optimized process. By contrast, recent approaches also target streaming (or online) questions about the process while it is generating the event log, thereby enabling new types of application fields that were not feasible with offline methods[Burattin22]。 此类支持流的问题之一是在事件发生之前从事件日志中预测下一个活动。 应用包括患者和基础设施的健康监测、通过预测性资源分配改进响应时间以及针对威胁采取有针对性的对策等。. One such streaming-enabled question is the prediction of the next activities from the event log before they happen. Applications are in health monitoring of patients and infrastructure, improved response-times by predictive resource allocations, and targeted countermeasures to threats, among many others.

3 核心参数：窗口大小对精度的影响A Central Parameter: Impact of Window Size on Accuracy

同时Whilenn-gram 和 LSTM 由于其顺序性质（具有增量状态转换）本质上适合流式处理，基于 Transformer 的模型需要进行修改才能有效处理流式数据。 由于 Transformer 会立即处理整个序列，因此每次新令牌到达时，它们都需要重新处理整个序列，从而导致任意长的上下文大小，并随着序列长度的增加而迅速增加计算成本。-grams and LSTMs are inherently suitable for streaming due to their sequential nature (with incremental state transitions), Transformer-based models require modifications to handle streaming data effectively. Since Transformers process entire sequences at once, they require reprocessing the entire sequence every time a new token arrives, inducing arbitrarily long context sizes and rapidly increasing computational cost with sequence length.

常见的解决方案是使用滑动窗口将输入序列截断为固定长度（也称为上下文大小），类似于A common solution is to use sliding windows to truncate the input sequence to a fixed length (also referred to as context size), similarly tonn-克。 尽管这种方法接近批量学习中使用的填充技术，但它给流媒体带来了新的挑战，例如确定适当的窗口大小和学习准确反映窗口内事件时间顺序的位置嵌入。 直观上，太小的窗口大小将不允许语言模型将旧的但重要的动作合并到其预测中，从而导致整体准确性较低。 同样，太大的窗口大小预计会导致未使用可用数据进行训练的大量参数，从而再次降低准确性。 此外，过于复杂的语言模型具有较大的延迟和较低的吞吐量，使得它们对事件日志的实时使用无效。-grams. Although this approach is close to padding techniques used in batch learning, it brings new challenges in streaming, such as determining the appropriate window size and learning positional embeddings that accurately reflect the temporal order of events within the window. Intuitively, a too small window size will not allow the language model to incorporate old, but important actions into its prediction, resulting in a low overall accuracy. Likewise, a too large window size is expected to lead to large parameter numbers that are not trained with the available data, and thus, again a degradation of accuracy. Further, overly complex language models have large latencies and low throughput, invalidating their real-time use for event logs.
