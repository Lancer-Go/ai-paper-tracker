自适应实用程序驱动的弹性 AI 资源编排 (AURORA-AI)Adaptive Utility driven Resource Orchestration for Resilient AI (AURORA-AI)

Rahul Umesh Mhapsekar, Ilias Cherkaoui, Lizy Abraham, and Indrakshi Dey
摘要Abstract

现代人工智能系统越来越多地在非平稳条件下部署 计算、人口统计和操作条件 静态资源分配策略会降低预测能力 绩效和以人为本的属性，例如公平性和 可解释性。本文介绍了 AURORA-AI，一种自适应 用于弹性人工智能的实用程序驱动的资源编排框架 统一了 Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman 反馈控制， 基于李亚普诺夫的稳定性监控和公平感知复合材料 将效用纳入单一闭环策略。框架不断 在人群中重新分配计算预算 异构人工智能模型，以便共同定义全局效用 超过预测性能、人口统计、成本、延迟、 鲁棒性和可解释性在破坏情况下仍保持最大化。 该框架在压力丰富的离散时间模拟中进行评估 同时注入人口偏见冲击、渐进概念 漂移和突然的黑天鹅中断，并与 五个已建立的控制器，包括 Static、Round Robin、Greedy、 LinUCB，以及基于 Proximal 的深度强化学习代理 政策优化。 AURORA-AI 实现立即恢复 黑天鹅事件与静态的八十八个时间步相比 基线和近端政策优化的二十二，提升了 α 分位数和二十九和二十五的超分位数 分别百分比，同时减少平均值和最大值 人口均等差距，并增加了 李亚普诺夫稳定的操作步骤。这些结果表明 基于稳定性理论的公平感知自适应编排 是一条具有弹性的实践和理论驱动的道路 以人为本的人工智能部署。Modern AI systems are increasingly deployed under non-stationary computational, demographic, and operational conditions in which static resource allocation strategies degrade both predictive performance and human-centric properties such as fairness and explainability. This paper presents AURORA-AI, an Adaptive Utility-driven Resource Orchestration framework for Resilient AI that unifies Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman feedback control, Lyapunov-based stability monitoring, and a fairness-aware composite utility into a single closed-loop policy. The framework continuously redistributes computational budget across a population of heterogeneous AI models so that the global utility, defined jointly over predictive performance, demographic parity, cost, latency, robustness, and interpretability, remains maximised under disruption. The framework is evaluated in a stress-rich discrete-time simulation that concurrently injects demographic bias shocks, gradual concept drift, and abrupt black-swan disruptions, and is compared against five established controllers including Static, Round Robin, Greedy, LinUCB, and a deep reinforcement-learning agent based on Proximal Policy Optimisation. AURORA-AI achieves immediate recovery from the black-swan event compared to eighty-eight time steps for the Static baseline and twenty-two for Proximal Policy Optimisation, lifts the alpha-quantile and the super-quantile by twenty-nine and twenty-five percent respectively, simultaneously reduces the mean and maximum demographic parity gap, and increases the fraction of Lyapunov-stable operating steps. These results indicate that fairness-aware adaptive orchestration grounded in stability theory is a practical and theoretically motivated path toward resilient human-centric AI deployment.

I 简介Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 系统已从研究领域迅速发展 原型进入生产环境，改变行业，例如 医疗保健、农业、工业自动化和自治系统Artificial intelligence (AI) systems have moved rapidly from research prototypes into production environments, transforming sectors such as healthcare, agriculture, industrial automation, and autonomous systems[1]。最新的生成人工智能和大语言浪潮 模型通过设定语言新标准加速了这一趋势 理解、内容生成和决策支持. The most recent wave of generative-AI and large language models has accelerated this trend by setting new standards in language understanding, content generation, and decision support[2], 支持从自动化客户交互到各种应用程序 高级研究助理, enabling applications that range from automated customer interaction to advanced research assistants[3].

尽管势头强劲，但部署在现场的人工智能系统仍表现出 反复出现的一系列弱点。他们的内部代表依然存在 不透明且只能勉强解释，这限制了人们的信任 实时自主决策Despite this momentum, AI systems deployed in the field exhibit a recurring set of weaknesses. Their internal representations remain opaque and only marginally interpretable, which limits trust in real-time autonomous decision-making[4]。他们对 从训练数据继承的人口统计和统计偏差， 这会放大社会差距并产生不稳定的行为 在人口层面. They are sensitive to demographic and statistical biases inherited from training data, which can amplify societal disparities and produce unstable behaviour at the population level[5]。标准训练管道可以 不能防止在顺序或顺序的情况下发生灾难性遗忘 非固定任务. Standard training pipelines do not protect against catastrophic forgetting under sequential or non-stationary tasks[6]，以及 由此产生的模型仍然容易受到对抗性和 分布外扰动, and the resulting models remain vulnerable to adversarial and out-of-distribution perturbations[7].

这些弱点因操作环境而被放大 现代人工智能系统已实际部署。边缘、联合和 分布式设置强加了严格的计算、网络和 能源限制These weaknesses are amplified by the operating environments in which contemporary AI systems are actually deployed. Edge, federated, and distributed settings impose tight computational, networking, and energy constraints[8]，同时实时自主 部署需要安全保证传统学习 算法本身不提供, while real-time autonomous deployments demand safety guarantees that conventional learning algorithms do not natively provide[9]。在这样的情况下 在不同的条件下，不同的人工智能模型可能会表现出截然不同的权衡 涵盖预测准确性、公平性、鲁棒性、延迟性和 可解释性。传统的静态资源分配策略 无法适应不断变化的工作负载、人口结构变化或意外情况 干扰，从而降低效率和运营 可靠性. Under such conditions, different AI models may exhibit very different trade-offs across predictive accuracy, fairness, robustness, latency, and interpretability. Conventional static resource-allocation strategies cannot adapt to evolving workloads, demographic shifts, or unexpected disturbances, and therefore degrade both efficiency and operational reliability[10].

因此，越来越多的证据表明需要A growing body of evidence therefore points to the need for自适应资源编排adaptive resource orchestration持续不断的框架 在异构群体中重新分配计算预算 人工智能模型响应其不断变化的效用、弹性和 以人为本的行为。为了在实践中有用，编排 策略必须是 (i) 闭环，以便控制器在之前做出反应 性能下降； (ii) 具有稳定性意识，因此有界 骚乱不会破坏全球体系的稳定； (三) 以人为本，公平性和可解释性被视为 一流的数量，而不是事后的限制。frameworks that continuously reallocate computational budget across a population of heterogeneous AI models in response to their evolving utility, resilience, and human-centric behaviour. 本文介绍了 AURORA-AI，一种自适应实用驱动资源 统一 Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman 反馈的编排框架 控制、基于 Lyapunov 的稳定性监控和公平意识 将综合效用整合到单个闭环策略中，以实现弹性 非静止条件下以人为中心的人工智能部署。的 所提出的框架在压力丰富的离散时间中进行了评估 模拟同时注入人口统计学偏差冲击，渐进 概念漂移和突然的黑天鹅中断，并进行了比较 针对五个已建立的控制器：静态、循环、贪婪、 LinUCB，以及基于 Proximal 的深度强化学习代理 政策优化。报告中的经验证据 部分This paper introduced AURORA-AI, an adaptive utility-driven resource orchestration framework that unifies Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman feedback control, Lyapunov-based stability monitoring, and a fairness-aware composite utility into a single closed-loop policy for resilient human-centric AI deployment under non-stationary conditions. The proposed framework was evaluated in a stress-rich discrete-time simulation that concurrently injects demographic bias shocks, gradual concept drift, and abrupt black-swan disruptions, and was compared against five established controllers : Static, Round-Robin, Greedy, LinUCB, and a deep reinforcement-learning agent based on Proximal Policy Optimisation.
