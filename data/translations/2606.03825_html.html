4 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

我们的结果表明，动态短卷积是改进基于 Transformer 的语言模型的有用原语。与在每个位置施加相同的局部聚合规则的静态短卷积不同，动态卷积允许每个标记选择依赖于输入的局部组合函数。综合实验支持了这样一个层的实用性，显示了在执行召回之前需要解析可变长度局部结构的任务的增益。语言建模结果进一步表明，这些好处不仅限于玩具设置：动态卷积改进了密集 Transformer、MoE Transformer 和线性注意力变体，并且在参数匹配和计算匹配比较下，这种改进仍然存在。Our results suggest that dynamic short convolutions are a useful primitive for improving Transformer-based language models. Unlike static short convolutions, which impose the same local aggregation rule at every position, dynamic convolutions allow each token to choose an input-dependent local composition function. The synthetic experiments support the utility of such a layer, showing gains on tasks that require resolving variable-length local structure before performing recall. The language-modeling results further suggest that these benefits are not limited to toy settings: dynamic convolutions improve dense Transformers, MoE Transformers, and linear attention variants, and the improvements persist under parameter-matched and compute-matched comparisons.

在局限性方面，我们的扩展研究达到了 2B 个密集参数和具有 1B 个活动参数的 7B 参数 MoE，这足以建立一致的趋势，但其本身并不能证明相同的计算优势将在前沿规模、更长的训练下或跨不同的数据混合和标记器保持。此外，虽然我们的 Triton 实现使动态卷积在 H100 GPU 上变得实用，但仍需要额外的工程来全面优化推理、支持更广泛的硬件以及更积极地融合动态和静态组件。最后，我们只探索可能的参数化、布局和内核宽度的一小部分。On the limitations side, our scaling study reaches 2B dense parameters and a 7B-parameter MoE with 1B active parameters, which is sufficient to establish consistent trends but does not by itself demonstrate that the same compute advantage will hold at frontier scale, under substantially longer training, or across different data mixtures and tokenizers. Moreover, while our Triton implementations make dynamic convolutions practical on H100 GPUs, additional engineering would be needed to fully optimize inference, support a broader range of hardware, and fuse the dynamic and static components more aggressively. Finally, we only explore a small subset of possible parameterizations, placements, and kernel widths.

5 相关工作Related Work

用于序列建模的卷积网络。Convolutional networks for sequence modeling.

在基于注意力的架构兴起之前，卷积网络是一类流行的神经序列模型。早期的神经 NLP 系统使用词嵌入上的一维卷积来进行标记和句子分类Convolutional networks were a popular class of neural sequence models before the rise of attention-based architectures. More recently, long-convolution models such as S4 and Hyena have revisited convolutions as subquadratic alternatives to attention by using implicit long filters and gating(Gu et al., 2022; Poli et al., 2023).

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了动态短卷积作为一种依赖于输入、局部性偏向的原语，用于改进基于 Transformer 的语言模型。通过从当前隐藏状态生成卷积滤波器，动态卷积以更大的表现力扩展静态短卷积，同时保留高效的局部序列混合。在综合联想回忆任务、从 150M 到 2B 参数的密集语言建模实验、7B 参数 MoE 模型和两个线性注意力架构中，动态卷积在标准 Transformer 和静态卷积基线上持续改进。我们的缩放定律分析表明了有意义的计算优势，而我们的定制 Triton 内核表明，可以通过适度的端到端训练开销来获得这些收益。总而言之，这些结果表明动态短卷积是一种可扩展且实用的架构原语，是对注意力的补充，并且对法学硕士来说很有前景。We introduced dynamic short convolutions as an input-dependent, locality-biased primitive for improving Transformer-based language models. By generating convolutional filters from the current hidden state, dynamic convolutions extend static short convolutions with greater expressivity while retaining efficient local sequence mixing. Across synthetic associative-recall tasks, dense language-modeling experiments from 150M to 2B parameters, a 7B-parameter MoE model, and two linear attention architectures, dynamic convolutions consistently improve over both standard Transformers and static-convolution baselines. Our scaling-law analysis indicates a meaningful compute advantage, and our custom Triton kernels show that these gains can be obtained with modest end-to-end training overhead. Taken together, these results suggest that dynamic short convolutions are a scalable and practical architectural primitive, complementary to attention and promising for LLMs.
