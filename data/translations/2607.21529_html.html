摘要Abstract

预训练扩散模型的测试时间调整 (TTT) 已成为视频编辑的强大范例。然而，生成模型的分布映射性质与标准 TTT 的单点优化之间存在根本性的不匹配。在本文中，我们证明了这种不匹配会触发Test-Time Tuning (TTT) on pretrained diffusion models has emerged as a powerful paradigm for video editing. However, there exists a foundational mismatch between the distribution-mapping nature of generative models and the single-point optimization of standard TTT. In this paper, we demonstrate that this mismatch triggers先前崩溃Prior Collapse，一种退化状态，其中模型丢弃文本条件和空间潜伏，将世代折叠为源视频，或纠缠不同区域的特征。为了解决这个问题，我们建议, a degenerate state where the model discards the text conditions and spatial latents, collapsing generations to the source video, or entangling the features of distinct regions. To resolve this, we propose弹性TTTElasticTTT，一个保留先前生成分布并挽救生成弹性的新颖框架。具体来说，我们建议, a novel framework that preserves the prior generative distribution and rescues generative elasticity. Specifically, we propose目标分布正则化Target Distribution Regularization为了防止急剧的记忆最小值，to prevent sharp memorization minima,对比CFGContrastive CFG引导推理远离来源偏差，以及to guide inference away from source biases, and异步噪声表Asynchronous Noise Schedule保留未编辑的区域。在理论分析的支持下进行的广泛评估表明，ElasticTTT 成功保留了基本模型的生成先验，在一次性视频编辑方面实现了最先进的性能。to preserve unedited regions. Extensive evaluations, supported by theoretical analysis, demonstrate that ElasticTTT successfully preserves the generative prior of the base model, achieving state-of-the-art performance on one-shot video editing.

1 简介Introduction

去噪扩散模型的进展Advancements in denoising diffusion models[18, 62]在文本到视频生成方面取得了重大进展have enabled significant progress in text-to-video generation[32, 64, 21, 74]。这些强大的视频生成主干还通过利用学习的生成先验为可控视频编辑开辟了新的机会. These powerful video generation backbones also open new opportunities for controllable video editing by leveraging learned generative priors[71, 50, 24]。然而，用户提供的源视频可能不属于现成模型的固定训练分布. However, user-provided source videos may fall outside the fixed training distribution of off-the-shelf models[75, 2, 78, 70, 17, 38, 5]，特别是考虑到互联网上新概念的迅速出现，导致编辑性能下降。最近，另一项研究探索了测试时调整（TTT）, particularly given the rapid emergence of new concepts across the internet, resulting in degraded editing performance. Recently, another line of research has explored Test-Time Tuning (TTT)[63, 65, 66, 69, 24, 16, 67]，其目的是使现成模型的权重适应用户提供的源视频的领域。, which aims to adapt the weights of off-the-shelf models to the domain of user-provided source videos.

与直接使用预训练模型执行推理的视频编辑方法相比，TTT 特别适合于源视频与预训练分布存在较大域差距的挑战性场景。这是因为 TTT 明确地使模型适应每个源视频的特定内容和动态，从而改善其外观、结构和运动的保留。除了作为视频编辑的实用范例之外，基于 TTT 的方法还可以作为有效的数据引擎，用于合成配对编辑数据以训练大规模前馈视频编辑模型Compared with video editing approaches that perform inference directly with pre-trained models, TTT is particularly well suited to challenging scenarios where the source video exhibits a large domain gap from the pretraining distribution. This is because TTT explicitly adapts the model to the specific content and dynamics of each source video, thereby improving the preservation of its appearance, structure, and motion. Besides serving as a practical paradigm for video editing, TTT-based methods also function an effective data engine for synthesizing paired editing data to train large-scale feed-forward video editing models[70, 77].

尽管 TTT 提供了个性化用户体验的自然机制Although TTT provides a natural mechanism for personalized user experiences[58, 4, 12, 35]，它引入了扩散过程的内在张力：TTT 有意鼓励特定于实例的过度拟合，而扩散过程本质上是随机的，旨在保持分布多样性。值得注意的是，仅在这种零方差奇点上优化高度参数化的扩散模型很容易导致我们所说的故障模式, it introduces an inherent tension with the diffusion process: TTT intentionally encourages instance-specific overfitting, while diffusion processes are inherently stochastic and intended to preserve distributional diversity. Notably, optimizing a highly parameterized diffusion model exclusively on this zero-variance singularity can easily lead to a failure mode that we term先前崩溃prior collapse。如图所示. 为了规避这些挑战，以前的方法提出了一系列干预措施。为了减轻条件崩溃，先前的工作通常依赖于低秩适应（LoRA）To circumvent these challenges, previous methods have proposed a spectrum of interventions. However, these approaches are fundamentally sub-optimal, their complexity determines that they heavily rely on delicate architectural tuning, require auxiliary datasets, or remain dangerously sensitive to hyperparameter choices.

更重要的是，虽然测试时调整 (TTT) 通过直接在源图像上微调模型在图像编辑方面显示出巨大的前景，但将 TTT 适应视频却非常稀缺且充满困难。视频扩散变压器More importantly, while Test-Time Tuning (TTT) has shown immense promise in image editing by fine-tuning models directly on the source image, adapting TTT to video is remarkably scarce and fraught with difficulty. Video Diffusion Transformers[64, 53]拥有高度复杂的时空先验，并且天真地将基于图像的 TTT 优化技术转移到视频模型中，严重加速了先验崩溃。网络很快就会过度适应源视频的确定性运动和外观，失去精确、准确编辑所需的生成弹性。possess highly complicated spatial-temporal priors, and naively transferring image-based TTT optimization techniques to video models severely accelerates Prior Collapse. The network quickly overfits to the deterministic motion and appearance of the source video, losing its generative elasticity required for precise, accurate editing.

为了解决这种根本性的优化——抽样紧张，我们建议To resolve this fundamental optimization–sampling tension, we propose弹性TTTElasticTTT，一个旨在挽救生成弹性并明确治愈先前崩溃症状的原则框架。 ElasticTTT 没有将这些故障视为孤立的工件，而是对整个生成管道进行干预：首先，为了防止优化过程中的条件崩溃，我们引入了, a principled framework designed to rescue generative elasticity and explicitly cure the symptoms of Prior Collapse. Rather than treating these failures as isolated artifacts, ElasticTTT intervenes across the entire generative pipeline: First, to prevent Conditioning Collapse during optimization, we introduce目标分布正则化Target Distribution Regularization。通过以受控随机性轻轻放松刚性单点目标，我们从物理上防止模型记忆源视频，保留其弹性去噪先验。然而，由于调整后的权重仍然表现出向源概念的残余转变，我们随后应用. By softly relaxing the rigid, single-point target with controlled stochasticity, we physically prevent the model from memorizing the source video, preserving its elastic denoising priors. However, as the tuned weights still exhibit a residual shift toward the source concept, we subsequently apply无对比分类器指导Contrastive Classifier-Free Guidance在推理过程中强制将轨迹转向目标条件。最后，为了解决空间纠缠，我们在采样过程中引入了异步噪声计划，它动态地将独立的扩散轨迹分配给编辑和锚定的区域，澄清潜在的表示并赋予模型渲染新概念的局部自由，同时安全地保留未编辑的环境。during inference to forcefully steer the trajectory toward the target condition. Finally, to resolve Spatial Entanglement, we introduce an Asynchronous Noise Schedule in the sampling process, which dynamically assign independent diffusion trajectories to the edited and anchored regions, clarifying latent representations and granting the model the localized freedom to render new concepts while safely preserving the unedited surroundings.

为了验证我们框架的有效性，我们在各种编辑任务中进行了广泛的实验。我们的结果表明，ElasticTTT 成功解决了先前的崩溃问题，表现出强大的生成弹性、精确的指令遵守性以及源视频的高保真度保存。在定量和定性评估中，ElasticTTT 始终优于竞争基准（涵盖仅推理、测试时间调整和基于训练的方法），最终在视频编辑方面实现了最先进的性能。To validate the effectiveness of our framework, we conduct extensive experiments across a diverse range of editing tasks. Our results demonstrate that ElasticTTT successfully resolves the prior collapse issue, exhibiting robust generative elasticity, precise instruction adherence, and high-fidelity preservation of the source video. In both quantitative and qualitative evaluations, ElasticTTT consistently outperforms competitive baselines—spanning inference-only, test-time-tuning, and training-based approaches—ultimately achieving state-of-the-art performance in video editing.

Diffusion models learn mappings between data distributions and rely on diverse samples to preserve generative flexibility. 在敏感应用中，特别是涉及人脸的应用中，源保存是最重要的问题；即使是微小的结构改变也可能导致严重不自然的文物或身份的丧失。虽然调整 TTT 步骤计数是控制编辑程度和源保留的有效机制，但存在固有的权衡：强制与参考视频进行更严格的对齐不可避免地会降低语义对齐和整体编辑质量。结合我们的 TDR 模块可以显着缓解这个问题，从而以最小的语义牺牲实现强大的参考对齐。根据经验，当 TTT 步长从 100 增加到 300 时，采用 TDR 会产生高效的权衡：OVL 下降 1 点，SP 大幅提高 5 点。相比之下，如果没有 TDR，相同的语义成本只会使 SP 提高 2.7 点。In sensitive applications, particularly those involving human faces, source preservation is a paramount concern; even minor structural alterations can result in severely unnatural artifacts or loss of identity. While adjusting the TTT step count serves as an effective mechanism to control the degree of editing and source preservation, an inherent trade-off exists: enforcing stricter alignment with the reference video inevitably degrades semantic alignment and overall editing quality. Incorporating our TDR module significantly mitigates this issue, enabling robust reference alignment with minimal semantic sacrifice. Empirically, when increasing the TTT steps from 100 to 300, employing TDR yields a highly efficient trade-off: a 1-point drop in OVL translates to a substantial 5-point gain in SP. In contrast, without TDR, the identical semantic cost yields a mere 2.7-point improvement in SP.

一、1I.1局限性Limitation

尽管我们的模型可以在所有编辑任务中实现最先进的性能，但它仍然存在一些限制。 首先，与那些免训练方法相比，我们模型的 TTT 性质导致了更多的计算成本。 其次，在某些情况下，我们发现视频中某个区域的过度规范也会限制其他区域的编辑能力。我们推断这种现象可能是由于 Test-Time-Tuning 过程中 self-attention 层的过度优化造成的。Although our model can achieve state-of-the-art performance in all editing tasks, it still suffers from a few limitations. Secondly, in some situation, we found that over-specification of a certain area in the video can also limit the editing capabilities of other areas. We infer that this phenomenon may be caused by the over-optimization of the self-attention layers during the Test-Time-Tuning process.

一、2I.2未来的工作Future Work

目前，我们的异步噪声调度依赖于由外部模型（例如 Grounded-SAM2）生成的显式掩码。未来的工作可以通过在初始 TTT 步骤中直接从扩散模型的内部交叉注意力图中动态提取空间掩模来消除这种依赖性，从而创建一个完全端到端的框架。Currently, our Asynchronous Noise Scheduling relies on explicit masks generated by external models like Grounded-SAM2. Future work could eliminate this dependency by dynamically extracting spatial masks directly from the diffusion model’s internal cross-attention maps during the initial TTT steps, creating a fully end-to-end framework.

此外，我们的目标是扩展 ElasticTTT 以处理长上下文或多镜头视频。通过引入时间分块或滑动窗口 TTT，我们可以确保数百个帧之间的时间一致性，而不会超出标准 VRAM 限制。Also, we aim to extend ElasticTTT to handle long-context or multi-shot videos. By introducing temporal chunking or sliding-window TTT, we can ensure temporal consistency across hundreds of frames without exceeding standard VRAM constraints.

K.1 社会影响Social Impact

K.1.1 积极的社会影响Positive Social Impact

ElasticTTT 是一个可访问且高效的框架，可以为广大用户群实现视频编辑的民主化。通过仅通过自然语言指令实现直观修改，它消除了传统上与 DaVinci Resolve 等专业级软件相关的陡峭学习曲线。此外，我们的模型通过自动化日常编辑任务为专业创作者提供了巨大的价值，从而显着加快了工作流程并降低了时间和财务生产成本。ElasticTTT serves as an accessible and highly effective framework that democratizes video editing for a broad user base. By enabling intuitive modifications solely through natural language instructions, it eliminates the steep learning curve traditionally associated with professional-grade software such as DaVinci Resolve. Furthermore, our model offers substantial value to professional creators by automating routine editing tasks, thereby significantly accelerating workflows and reducing both temporal and financial production costs.

K.1.2 潜在风险Potential Risk

尽管我们提出的 ElasticTTT 实现了高质量视频编辑的大众化，但它本身就存在与恶意视频合成相关的风险。精确编辑视频特定区域的能力可能会被滥用来创建令人信服的伪造内容，例如未经个人同意将其置于虚构的场景中。这导致了深度造假和错误信息传播带来的更广泛的社会挑战。我们严厉谴责使用我们的方法生成欺骗性内容的行为。为了防止这种滥用，我们建议我们框架的下游应用程序包含隐形水印，并将该技术与先进的合成媒体检测算法配对。Although our proposed ElasticTTT democratizes high-quality video editing, it inherently carries risks related to malicious video synthesis. The ability to precisely edit specific regions of a video could be misused to create convincing counterfeit content, such as placing individuals in fabricated scenarios without their consent. This contributes to the broader societal challenge of deepfakes and misinformation propagation. We strictly condemn the use of our method for generating deceptive content. To safeguard against such misuse, we suggest that downstream applications of our framework incorporate invisible watermarking and pair the technology with advanced synthetic media detection algorithms.

K.2 人类评估的伦理问题Ethical Concerns for Human Evaluation

我们的人类评估框架是严格按照伦理研究标准设计的。首先，所有评估人员严格自愿参与评估，并保留在任何阶段无条件退出的权利，而不会面临任何负面影响。其次，我们执行严格的数据隐私政策；所有收集到的反馈都是完全匿名的，确保不会记录任何个人身份信息。此外，该研究纯粹是观察性的。核心任务仅要求人类受试者查看标准合成视频，这完全保护他们免受身体危害、心理压力或任何令人反感的视觉刺激。因此，我们的方法避免了任何可能损害参与者福祉的侵入性干预措施。Our human evaluation framework is designed in strict compliance with ethical research standards. First, all evaluators engaged in the assessment strictly on a voluntary basis, retaining the unconditional right to opt out at any stage without facing any negative repercussions. Second, we enforced a rigorous data privacy policy; all collected feedback was entirely anonymized, ensuring that no personally identifiable details were ever recorded. Furthermore, the study is purely observational. The core task merely requires human subjects to review standard synthesized videos, which completely shields them from physical hazards, psychological stress, or any offensive visual stimuli. Consequently, our methodology avoids any invasive interventions that might compromise participant well-being.

K.3 保障Safeguard

在开发用户友好的视频编辑软件或平台时，实施适当的保护措施非常重要。为了确保为人类评估提供经过彻底审查和安全的评估环境，我们严格从我们精心策划的数据集中对视频进行采样，该数据集是根据著名的 DAVIS 基准构建的。这种经过深思熟虑的选择过程可确保所有视觉内容都是良性的，从而积极防止无意中包含互联网上常见的不适当、敏感或未经审查的媒体。It is quite essential to implement proper safeguards when developing user-friendly video editing software or platform. To ensure a thoroughly vetted and secure assessment environment for our human evaluation, we strictly sampled videos from our curated dataset, which is constructed based on the renowned DAVIS benchmark. This deliberate selection process guarantees that all visual content is benign, thereby actively preventing the inadvertent inclusion of inappropriate, sensitive, or unvetted media commonly found on the internet.
