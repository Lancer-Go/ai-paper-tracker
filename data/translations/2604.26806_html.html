摘要Abstract

基于 Transformer 的架构已经建立了全局语义感知的主导范式；然而，它们仍然从根本上受到自然图像固有的深刻空间异质性的限制。具体来说，在不同信息密度的区域施加统一的全局感受野不可避免地会导致局部特征退化，特别是在微观目标密集的冲突区域。为了解决这种机械限制，我们提出了 ViCrop-Det，这是一种无需训练的推理框架，引入了自适应空间信任区域收缩。受到异常分割中注意力熵的启发，ViCrop-Det 利用检测解码器的交叉注意力分布作为内源探针。通过利用空间注意力熵 (SAE) 启发式评估局部空间模糊性，该框架执行动态空间路由，将固定的计算预算专门分配给同时表现出高目标显着性和高认知不确定性的区域。通过缩小空间信任区域并注入高频局部观测，ViCrop-Det 主动解决空间模糊性并恢复细粒度特征，而无需进行架构修改。对 VisDrone 和 DOTA-v1.5 的广泛评估表明，ViCrop-Det 产生了具有竞争力的性能增强，始终为 RT-DETR-R50 和 Deformable DETR 添加了 +1-3 mAP@50，延迟开销仅为 20-23%。在 MS COCO 上，Transformer-based architectures have established a dominant paradigm in global semantic perception; however, they remain fundamentally constrained by the profound spatial heterogeneity inherent in natural images. Specifically, the imposition of a uniform global receptive field across regions of varying information density inevitably leads to local feature degradation, particularly in dense conflict zones populated by microscopic targets. To address this mechanistic limitation, we propose ViCrop-Det, a training-free inference framework that introduces adaptive spatial trust region shrinkage. Inspired by the use of attention entropy in anomaly segmentation, ViCrop-Det leverages the detection decoder’s cross-attention distribution as an endogenous probe. By utilizing Spatial Attention Entropy (SAE) to heuristically evaluate local spatial ambiguity, the framework executes dynamic spatial routing, allocating a fixed computational budget exclusively to regions exhibiting both high target saliency and high cognitive uncertainty. By shrinking the spatial trust region and injecting high-frequency localized observations, ViCrop-Det actively resolves spatial ambiguity and recovers fine-grained features without requiring architectural modifications. Extensive evaluations on VisDrone and DOTA-v1.5 demonstrate that ViCrop-Det yields competitive performance enhancements, consistently adding +1-3 mAP@50 to RT-DETR-R50 and Deformable DETR with a marginal 20-23% latency overhead. 1 简介Introduction

尽管在标准基准测试中具有强大的性能，但全局 Transformer 架构在不受约束的密集环境中定位微观目标时仍然面临着根本瓶颈。这种限制通常根据经验被诊断为“注意力稀释”，其中全局感受野不成比例地将 softmax 归一化权重分布在广阔的、低信息背景块上Despite their robust performance across standard benchmarks, global Transformer architectures continue to face a fundamental bottleneck in localizing microscopic targets within unconstrained, dense environments. This limitation is typically diagnosed empirically as "attention dilution," wherein the global receptive field disproportionately distributes softmax-normalized weights across expansive, low-information background patches[22, 5]。当模型遇到严重下采样、纹理退化的目标时，查询嵌入无法提取明确的局部空间证据。因此，交叉注意力概率质量扩散，在空间参数空间上接近平坦、均匀的分布。这种均匀的空间分布本质上具有高香农熵. When a model encounters heavily downsampled, texture-degraded targets, the query embeddings fail to extract definitive localized spatial evidence. Consequently, the cross-attention probability mass diffuses, approaching a flat, uniform distribution across the spatial parameter space. This uniform spatial distribution inherently possesses a high Shannon entropy[16]，作为网络认知模糊性和局部空间不确定性的强有力的经验指标。从如此高熵的状态强制进行明确的边界框预测不可避免地会导致持续的类别混乱和极端的定位漂移。而免训练的均匀切片方法（例如 SAHI）尝试通过人为扩大像素区域来恢复这些目标, serving as a strong empirical indicator of the network’s cognitive ambiguity and local spatial uncertainty. Forcing a definitive bounding box prediction from such a high-entropy state inevitably leads to persistent category confusion and extreme localization drift. While training-free uniform slicing methodologies (e.g., SAHI) attempt to recover these targets by artificially enlarging the pixel area[1]，它们基于空间同质性的假设进行操作。通过盲目地将计算资源分配给低不确定性、平滑的背景区域，这些强力方法会产生严重的计算开销，并且无法利用自然图像固有的空间异质性。, they operate on an assumption of spatial homogeneity. By blindly allocating computational resources to low-uncertainty, smooth background regions, these brute-force approaches incur severe computational overhead and fail to exploit the intrinsic spatial heterogeneity of natural images.

为了解决这一机制差距，我们提出了一种植根于熵引导的自适应空间路由的免训练推理框架。受到最近发现的启发，表明空间注意力熵可以通过测量注意力分散来成功分割未知物体To address this mechanistic gap, we propose a training-free inference framework rooted in entropy-guided adaptive spatial routing. Inspired by recent findings demonstrating that spatial attention entropy can successfully segment unknown objects by measuring attention dispersion[2]，我们通过分析 Transformer 解码器的内生交叉注意力分布，将此原理应用于边界框检测, we adapt and expand this principle to bounding-box detection by analyzing the endogenous cross-attention distribution of the Transformer decoder[22]。我们的框架不依赖于强力空间切片，而是使用空间注意力熵（SAE）与注意力强度相结合来创建联合模糊性-显着性启发式得分。这使得模型能够动态隔离遭受峰值空间不确定性但包含潜在目标证据的局部冲突区域。将推理过程视为受限计算资源分配问题，我们的方法执行. 5 局限性Limitations

ViCrop-Det 依赖于固定阈值，这可能无法适应每个数据集，并且会错过零注意力对象（未来：集成显着性）。计算开销（吞吐量下降 20-25%）是有限的，但对于密集场景来说会更高。更广泛的数据集显示大物体的增益较小，强调小物体的焦点。ViCrop-Det relies on fixed thresholds, which may not adapt per-dataset, and misses zero-attention objects (future: integrate saliency). Compute overhead (20-25% throughput drop) is bounded but higher for dense scenes. Broader datasets reveal less gain on large objects, emphasizing small-object focus.

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 ViCrop-Det，这是一种新颖的免训练推理框架，旨在增强基于 Transformer 的架构中的小对象检测。摆脱严格的统一切片策略，我们证明了解码器的内生交叉注意力分布可以作为局部细化的强大工具。通过提出一种基于空间注意力熵（SAE）的联合模糊性-显着性启发式评分机制，我们的方法有效地隔离了密集的、容易发生冲突的区域。这使得 ViCrop-Det 能够专门针对严重认知模糊的区域执行有针对性的高分辨率自适应空间路由，而无需模型重新训练或架构修改。In this paper, we introduced ViCrop-Det, a novel training-free inference framework designed to enhance small object detection in Transformer-based architectures. Moving away from rigid uniform slicing strategies, we demonstrated that the decoder’s endogenous cross-attention distribution can serve as a powerful tool for localized refinement. By proposing a joint ambiguity-saliency heuristic scoring mechanism based on Spatial Attention Entropy (SAE), our method effectively isolates dense, conflict-prone regions. This allows ViCrop-Det to perform targeted, high-resolution adaptive spatial routing exclusively on areas suffering from severe cognitive ambiguity, without requiring model retraining or architectural modifications.

对 VisDrone 和 DOTA-v1.5 等具有挑战性的基准的实验评估表明，这种熵引导方法持续提高了不同基线检测器（包括 RT-DETR-R50 和 Deformable DETR）的性能。它产生显着的 mAP 增益，特别是对于小型和严重遮挡的物体，与传统的强力切片方法相比，实现了高度优化的精度-效率权衡。虽然 ViCrop-Det 引入了有限的计算开销，并且本质上依赖于基础检测器的初始注意力质量，但其即插即用的多功能性凸显了在高分辨率、混乱的现实环境中部署鲁棒感知模型的高度实用的方向。未来的工作将探索用于特定领域优化的动态阈值机制以及视频级时空路由的扩展。Experimental evaluations on challenging benchmarks like VisDrone and DOTA-v1.5 demonstrate that this entropy-guided approach consistently improves performance across diverse baseline detectors, including RT-DETR-R50 and Deformable DETR. It yields significant mAP gains particularly for small and heavily occluded objects, achieving a highly optimized accuracy-efficiency trade-off compared to conventional brute-force slicing methods. While ViCrop-Det introduces a bounded computational overhead and inherently relies on the base detector’s initial attention quality, its plug-and-play versatility highlights a highly pragmatic direction for deploying robust perception models in high-resolution, cluttered real-world environments. Future work will explore dynamic thresholding mechanisms for domain-specific optimization and extensions to video-level spatial-temporal routing.
