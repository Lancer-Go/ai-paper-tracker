摘要Abstract

具有同等能力的人工智能模型可以表现出根本不同的行为模式——一个在压力下屈服，而另一个则坚守；一种投资于关系维护，另一种则专注于任务完成。然而，不存在衡量这些性格差异的标准化工具。现有的方法要么借用人类人格维度并依赖于自我报告（这与法学硕士的实际行为不同），要么将行为变异视为缺陷而不是特征。AI models of equivalent capability can exhibit fundamentally different behavioral patterns—one capitulates under pressure while another holds firm; one invests in relational maintenance while another focuses on task completion. Yet no standardized instrument exists to measure these dispositional differences. Existing approaches either borrow human personality dimensions and rely on self-report (which diverges from actual behavior in LLMs) or treat behavioral variation as a defect rather than a trait.

1 简介Introduction

1.1 气质问题The Temperament Problem

人工智能模型正在激增。现在，组织在数十种基础模型中进行选择进行部署，并且选择越来越取决于行为特征，而不是原始能力（跨前沿模型的融合）。一种模式是顺从且乐于助人，但在压力下就会屈服。另一种是独立的、有创意的，但忽视了用户的喜好。第三种人善于社交，但容易受到操纵。这些不是能力差异；而是能力差异。他们是气质差异。AI models are proliferating. Organizations now choose among dozens of foundation models for deployment, and the choice increasingly hinges not on raw capability—which converges across frontier models—but on behavioral characteristics. One model is compliant and helpful but capitulates under pressure. Another is independent and creative but ignores user preferences. A third is socially attuned but vulnerable to manipulation. These are not capability differences; they are temperament differences.

然而，还没有标准化的方法来衡量它们。 AI 评估生态系统以能力基准（MMLU、HumanEval、MATH、ARC）为主，它们回答“这个模型能做什么？”而不是“这个模型的表现如何？”具有相同 MMLU 分数的两个模型在部署时可能表现出截然不同的行为模式：一个可能会在用户压力下改变其既定观点，而另一个则坚持不变；一种可能会在关系维护上投入大量资金，而另一种则专注于任务完成；一个人可能会在对抗性的刺激下崩溃，而另一个人则保持镇定。Yet there is no standardized way to measure them. The AI evaluation ecosystem is dominated by capability benchmarks—MMLU, HumanEval, MATH, ARC—that answer "what can this model do?" but not "how does this model behave?" Two models with identical MMLU scores can exhibit dramatically different behavioral patterns when deployed: one may change its stated opinions under user pressure while the other holds firm; one may invest heavily in relational maintenance while the other focuses exclusively on task completion; one may collapse under adversarial prompting while the other maintains composure.

能力测量和行为表征之间的差距就是 MTI 解决的问题。This gap—between capability measurement and behavioral characterization—is the problem MTI addresses.

1.3 模型医学和 MTI 的起源Model Medicine and the Origin of MTI

MTI 源于模型医学——一个将人工智能模型视为具有内部结构、动态过程和可分类行为条件的实体的研究项目（Jeong，2026；arXiv 2603.04722）。 Model Medicine 的理论支柱是四壳模型 (FSM)，其经验基础是 Agora-12 计划（720 个智能体，跨结构化多智能体实验（包括 Trust Game、Poker、Chess、Codenames 和 Avalon）的 24,923 个决策）。MTI emerged from Model Medicine—a research program that treats AI models as entities with internal structures, dynamic processes, and classifiable behavioral conditions (Jeong, 2026; arXiv 2603.04722). Model Medicine’s theoretical backbone is the Four Shell Model (FSM), empirically grounded in the Agora-12 program (720 agents, 24,923 decisions across structured multi-agent experiments including Trust Game, Poker, Chess, Codenames, and Avalon).

Agora-12 实验表明，同等能力的模型表现出截然不同的行为模式。有些人即使受到剥削也无条件合作；其他人则战略性地叛逃。有些在不同环境下保持稳定的策略；其他人则随着微小的框架变化而发生巨大变化。白屋实验（模型被放置在最小的、无约束的环境中）暴露了能力基准无法预测的原始行为差异：Mistral 的极端 Shell 敏感性 (PSI = 950)、EXAONE 的战略独立性、Haiku 的双重鲁棒性。The Agora-12 experiments revealed that models of equivalent capability exhibited strikingly different behavioral patterns. Some cooperated unconditionally even when exploited; others defected strategically. Some maintained stable strategies across contexts; others shifted dramatically with minor framing changes. The White-Room experiments—where models were placed in minimal, unconstrained environments—exposed raw behavioral differences that capability benchmarks could not predict: Mistral’s extreme Shell sensitivity (PSI = 950), EXAONE’s strategic independence, Haiku’s double robustness.

The correct framing was气质temperament：米斯特拉尔的环境敏感性不是一种疾病，而是一种性格特征，类似于人类气质中的高度神经质。这种认识——如果不首先了解正常变异就无法诊断病理学——是 MTI 的创立前提。 Model Medicine 的诊断框架（M-CARE nosology）需要正常行为变异的基线； MTI 提供了它。: Mistral’s environmental sensitivity was not a disease but a dispositional trait, analogous to high Neuroticism in human temperament. This realization—that you cannot diagnose pathology without first understanding normal variation—is MTI’s founding premise. Model Medicine’s diagnostic framework (M-CARE nosology) requires a baseline of normal behavioral variation; MTI provides it.

1.4 我们的方法Our Approach

MTI（模型气质指数）是一个 4 轴、基于行为的 AI 代理气质分析系统，通过结构化检查协议测量反应性、合规性、社交性和弹性。它生成可通信的类型代码（如 MBTI）和具有方面级详细信息的定量配置文件（如 NEO-PI-R）。MTI (Model Temperament Index) is a 4-axis, behavior-based profiling system for AI agent temperament, measuring Reactivity, Compliance, Sociality, and Resilience through structured examination protocols. It produces both a communicable type code (like MBTI) and a quantitative profile with facet-level detail (like NEO-PI-R).

2 理论基础Theoretical Foundation

2.1 为什么是气质而不是性格Why Temperament, Not Personality

心理学文献区分The psychological literature distinguishes气质temperament—基于体质的反应性和自我调节差异，植根于生物基质（Thomas & Chess，1977；Rothbart，2007）—来自—constitutionally based differences in reactivity and self-regulation, rooted in biological substrates (Thomas & Chess, 1977; Rothbart, 2007)—from个性personality，一个更广泛的概念，涵盖社会学习行为和发展变化。, a broader construct encompassing socially learned behaviors and developmental change.

对于人工智能代理来说，气质是更合适的结构。首先，模型没有发展轨迹：行为倾向是在训练时建立的。其次，气质的生物基础映射到人工智能的计算基础——架构和权重在结构意义上是“宪法性的”。第三，气质对反应性和自我调节的关注与人工智能模型实际变化的维度是一致的：环境敏感性、对压力的反应和抗压能力。For AI agents, temperament is the more appropriate construct. First, models have no developmental trajectory: behavioral tendencies are established at training time. Second, temperament’s biological grounding maps onto AI’s computational grounding—architecture and weights are "constitutional" in the structural sense. Third, temperament’s focus on reactivity and self-regulation aligns with the dimensions along which AI models actually vary: environmental sensitivity, response to pressure, and stress resistance.

6 讨论Discussion

6.1 基于行为的测量Behavior-Based Measurement

MTI 的行为方法需要权衡：包含 50 项的性格调查问卷需要一次 API 调用； MTI 的完整电池需要每个模型运行约 193 次。这种成本的合理性与临床心理学中的合理性相同：当自我报告和行为出现分歧时，行为就是基本事实。我们的合规性数据直接说明了这一点——模型的宜人性自我报告并不能预测它是否会在多轮压力下翻转。MTI’s behavioral approach carries a trade-off: a 50-item personality questionnaire requires one API call; MTI’s full battery requires ~193 runs per model. This cost is justified for the same reason it is justified in clinical psychology: when self-report and behavior diverge, behavior is the ground truth. Our Compliance data illustrate this directly—a model’s self-report of agreeableness would not predict whether it flips under multi-turn pressure.

7.1 局限性Limitations
