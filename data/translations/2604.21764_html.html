摘要Abstract

在解决新问题时，推理法学硕士通常会在较长的中间推理轨迹（例如思维链）上花费大量代币。我们建议总结并存储从广泛的审议和试错探索中提炼出来的可重用的推理技能，并在推理时检索这些技能以指导未来的推理。与盛行不同的是Reasoning LLMs often spend substantial tokens on long intermediate reasoning traces (e.g., chain-of-thought) when solving new problems. We propose to summarize and store reusable reasoning skills distilled from extensive deliberation and trial-and-error exploration, and to retrieve these skills at inference time to guide future reasoning. Unlike the prevailing从头开始推理reasoning from scratch范例中，我们的方法首先回忆每个查询的相关技能，帮助模型避免多余的弯路并专注于有效的解决方案路径。我们在编码和数学推理任务上评估我们的方法，发现它显着减少了推理标记，同时提高了整体性能。由此产生的较低的每个请求成本表明实际部署具有强大的实际和经济潜力。paradigm, our approach first recalls relevant skills for each query, helping the model avoid redundant detours and focus on effective solution paths. We evaluate our method on coding and mathematical reasoning tasks, and find that it significantly reduces reasoning tokens while improving overall performance. The resulting lower per-request cost indicates strong practical and economic potential for real-world deployment.

8 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了In this paper, we introduced推理能力思考（TRS）Thinking with Reasoning Skills (TRS)，一个框架，通过将范式从“从头开始推理”转变为“利用回忆经验进行推理”来解决推理模型的高推理成本。 TRS 不会强迫模型变得简短（这通常会损害复杂任务的性能），而是检索经过提炼的、可操作的模型, a framework that addresses the high inference cost of reasoning models by shifting the paradigm from “reasoning from scratch” to “reasoning with recalled experience.” Instead of forcing models to be brief—which often harms performance on complex tasks—TRS retrieves distilled, actionable技能卡skill cards（包括成功的策略和故障模式修复）引导模型通过解决方案空间。 大规模数学和编码基准的实证结果表明，TRS 成功打破了效率与准确性的权衡：它显着降低了推理标记和总体推理成本，同时匹配或超过了标准思想链基线的准确性，特别是在更困难的问题上。 作为一种无需训练、可跨模型推广的黑盒兼容方法，TRS 为在实际应用中部署能力密集型推理模型提供了实用且可扩展的途径。(encompassing both successful strategies and failure-mode fixes) to guide the model through the solution space. Empirical results on large-scale mathematical and coding benchmarks demonstrate that TRS successfully breaks the efficiency–accuracy trade-off: it significantly reduces reasoning tokens and overall inference costs while matching or exceeding the accuracy of standard Chain-of-Thought baselines, particularly on harder problems. As a training-free, black-box compatible approach that generalizes across models, TRS offers a practical and scalable pathway for deploying capability-intensive reasoning models in real-world applications.

局限性Limitations

首先我们分析First, our analysis of失败案例failure cases还没有我们的成功技能研究那么深入。 虽然 TRS 可以从不正确的轨迹中提取“故障模式修复”，但我们当前的管道依赖于有限的监督信号（例如，数学的最终答案正确性，以及来自编码测试用例的通过/失败信息）。 我们还没有完全描述哪些类型的错误最容易通过技能蒸馏恢复，也没有一个原则性的方法来总结这些信号之外的细粒度故障动态。is not yet as deep as our success-skill study. While TRS can distill “failure-mode fixes” from incorrect trajectories, our current pipeline relies on limited supervision signals (e.g., final answer correctness for math, and pass/fail information from test cases for coding). We do not yet fully characterize which types of errors are most recoverable via skill distillation, nor do we have a principled method to summarize fine-grained failure dynamics beyond these signals.

其次，TRS 引入了额外的检索和提示注入层。 尽管与长时间的测试时间推理相比，它是轻量级的，但端到端的好处取决于（i）检索质量和（ii）控制即时膨胀。 在某些设置中（例如，当完成令牌不减少或提示增长太长时），即使准确性提高，成本也可能不会严格改善。Second, TRS introduces an additional retrieval and prompt-injection layer. Although it is lightweight compared to long test-time reasoning, the end-to-end benefit depends on (i) retrieval quality and (ii) controlling prompt inflation. In some settings (e.g., when completion tokens do not decrease or prompts grow too long), cost may not strictly improve even if accuracy increases.

第三，我们目前的评价仍然是有限的。我们主要关注数学和具有自动验证器的竞争性编程，以及有针对性的转移和压力测试。更广泛的领域、弱可验证的任务和开放式工作流程仍然是未来的重要设置。Third, our current evaluation is still bounded. We focus mainly on math and competitive programming with automatic verifiers, plus targeted transfer and stress tests. Broader domains, weakly verifiable tasks, and open-ended workflows remain important future settings.

Future work can study which model should generate the original CoT, how to choose the summarizer, how to design the summarization and skill-use prompts, how to extend skill reuse to more domains, how to compress many problem-level skills into fewer general skills, and how to retrieve the most suitable skill from a very large library. The last question is especially connected to retrieval-augmented generation: as the skill library grows, retrieval design will become central to making experience reuse reliable and scalable.
