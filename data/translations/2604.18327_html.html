However, how to design and train reward models that can effectively guide such pipeline tasks remains underexplored. In this work, we systematically investigate the role of reward models in LLM-based pipelines, focusing on code generation for combinatorial optimization problems as a representative task. Our results demonstrate that PARM consistently improves pipeline output quality and stability across all datasets. Further analysis provides new insights into the interplay between reward modeling and multi-stage LLM reasoning. This work highlights the importance of reward model adaptation in complex pipelines and provides a practical framework for their integration, paving the way for more robust LLM applications in real-world, multi-stage tasks.

I 简介Introduction

最近，奖励模型因其在使大型语言模型（LLM）与人类偏好保持一致方面的核心作用而受到广泛关注，特别是在以推理为中心的任务中。奖励模型现在是人类反馈强化学习 (RLHF) 的组成部分Recently, reward models have garnered significant attention for their central role in aligning large language models (LLMs) with human preferences, especially in reasoning-centric tasks. Reward models are now integral to both reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)[28, 25]和先进的解码策略，使法学硕士能够生成不仅在语法上合理而且在语义上与期望目标一致的输出。作为评分模型，奖励模型已被广泛采用来评估和选择法学硕士的候选输出，形成了 Best-of-N (BoN) 采样、波束搜索和蒙特卡洛树搜索 (MCTS) 等技术的支柱。这些策略通过整合奖励信号，持续产生更高事实准确性、连贯性和任务相关性的输出。 例如，Best-of-N 采样在问答和网络搜索等开放域任务中表现出了显着的有效性and advanced decoding strategies, enabling LLMs to generate outputs that are not only syntactically plausible but also semantically aligned with desired objectives. As scoring models, reward models have been widely adopted to evaluate and select among candidate outputs from LLMs, forming the backbone of techniques such as Best-of-N (BoN) sampling, Beam Search, and Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS). These strategies, by integrating reward signals, consistently yield outputs of higher factual accuracy, coherence, and task relevance. For instance, Best-of-N sampling has demonstrated remarkable effectiveness in open-domain tasks like question answering and web search[27, 33]，提供了一种简单而强大的方法来选择高质量的响应。在机器翻译中，Beam Search（通常与修改后的评分函数配合使用）仍然是提高翻译质量的标准技术, offering a simple yet powerful way to select high-quality responses. In machine translation, Beam Search—often paired with modified scoring functions—remains a standard technique for enhancing translation quality[40, 26]。最近，将 MCTS 与策略驱动的法学硕士结合使用，可以自主生成高质量的训练数据，从而无需进行详尽的人工注释. More recently, the use of MCTS with policy-driven LLMs has enabled the autonomous generation of high-quality training data, bypassing the need for exhaustive human annotation[46]。总的来说，这些进步已经建立了奖励模型，作为在各种单阶段语言任务中优化法学硕士输出的不可或缺的工具。. Collectively, these advances have established reward models as indispensable tools for optimizing LLM outputs in a diverse range of single-stage language tasks.

与此同时，法学硕士的快速进步促进了它们在更复杂、流程驱动的场景中的采用。在自动化编程、多步骤问题解决和工作流程编排等当代应用中，LLM 越来越多地作为管道框架内的模块化组件进行部署At the same time, the rapid progress of LLMs has catalyzed their adoption in more complex, process-driven scenarios. In contemporary applications such as automated programming, multi-step problem solving, and workflow orchestration, LLMs are increasingly deployed as modular components within pipeline frameworks[10, 48]。这种管道的每个阶段都可以解决不同的子任务，例如问题制定、代码综合或解决方案验证，而整体系统性能取决于这些阶段的协调交互。. Each stage of such a pipeline may address a distinct subtask—such as problem formulation, code synthesis, or solution validation—with the overall system performance depending on the coordinated interaction of these stages.

虽然这些基于管道的系统在扩展法学硕士能力以解决现实世界的多阶段问题方面前景广阔，但它们也给质量控制带来了新的挑战。在实践中，由于复合错误和缺乏整体评估信号，当前基于法学硕士的管道通常无法提供实际部署所需的可靠性和输出质量。借鉴单一模型设置中奖励模型的成功经验，一个自然的问题出现了：While these pipeline-based systems hold great promise for scaling LLM capabilities to tackle real-world, multi-stage problems, they also introduce new challenges for quality control. In practice, current LLM-based pipelines often fall short of delivering the reliability and output quality required for practical deployment, due to compounding errors and the lack of holistic evaluation signals. Drawing on the proven success of reward models in single-model settings, a natural question arises:可以利用奖励模型来指导和增强整个 LLM 驱动的管道的输出吗？can reward models be leveraged to guide and enhance the output of entire LLM-driven pipelines?然而，尽管奖励模型具有潜力，但其在管道框架中的应用在很大程度上仍未得到探索。现有的研究主要集中在单个模型输出的奖励引导优化上，忽略了多级管道中存在的复杂依赖性和累积效应。这一差距凸显了迫切需要系统地研究为基于管道的法学硕士系统量身定制的奖励模型设计、培训和集成策略，以释放其在复杂的、面向流程的应用中的全部潜力。However, despite their potential, the application of reward models in pipeline frameworks remains largely unexplored. Existing research predominantly focuses on reward-guided optimization of individual model outputs, neglecting the intricate dependencies and cumulative effects present in multi-stage pipelines. This gap highlights an urgent need to systematically investigate reward model design, training, and integration strategies tailored for pipeline-based LLM systems, in order to unlock their full potential in complex, process-oriented applications.

为了具体研究奖励模型在管道框架中的作用，本文以数学优化问题的代码生成作为代表性案例研究To concretely investigate the role of reward models in pipeline frameworks, this paper takes code generation for mathematical optimization problems as a representative case study[34, 17, 41]。在我们的设置中，管道接收以自然语言描述的问题作为输入，并将解决过程分解为两个连续的阶段。第一阶段侧重于将问题陈述转换为精确的数学公式，而第二阶段生成可执行的Python代码来解决公式化的问题。每个阶段都由专门的法学硕士组件处理，总体输出质量取决于两个阶段的协调成功。. In our setting, the pipeline receives a problem described in natural language as input and decomposes the solution process into two sequential stages. The first stage focuses on transforming the problem statement into a precise mathematical formulation, while the second stage generates executable Python code to solve the formulated problem. Each stage is handled by a dedicated LLM component, and the overall output quality depends on the coordinated success of both stages.

This modular structure highlights the dependencies between each stage and the importance of effective coordination for achieving high-quality end-to-end solutions.

虽然奖励模型已被证明可以提高各个管道阶段的输出质量，但它们在提高整体管道输出方面的有效性并不能得到保证。一个关键的挑战在于，现有的奖励模型通常经过训练来评估孤立组件输出的质量，而不考虑它们对最终解决方案的下游影响。这可能会导致阶段输出获得高奖励分数，但对于全局任务而言仍然不是最佳的情况。 例如，数学上正确但过于复杂的公式可能在第一阶段得分很高，但会在后续阶段导致代码生成失败或运行时错误，最终降低管道的端到端性能。While reward models have been shown to enhance output quality within individual pipeline stages, their effectiveness in improving the overall pipeline output is not guaranteed. A key challenge lies in the fact that existing reward models are typically trained to assess the quality of isolated component outputs, without considering their downstream impact on the final solution. This can result in situations where a stage output receives a high reward score, but is nonetheless suboptimal for the global task. For example, a mathematically correct but overly complicated formulation may score highly in the first stage, yet lead to code generation failure or runtime errors in the subsequent stage—ultimately degrading the pipeline’s end-to-end performance.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一个简单而有效的框架，该框架采用奖励模型来解决基于管道的问题。与将奖励分配给各个组件输出的传统方法不同，我们的方法使用来自最终管道结果的反馈。这使得奖励模型能够了解早期决策（例如问题制定或中间解决方案）如何影响总体结果。通过关注端到端的性能，奖励模型可以更好地指导生成和选择的每个阶段实现真正的任务目标。这种适应管道的策略不仅弥合了局部和全局评估之间的差距，而且原则上可以扩展到其他复杂的多阶段任务，其中协调优化至关重要，尽管本文仅凭经验验证了两阶段实例化。To address these challenges, we propose a simple and effective framework that adapts reward models for pipeline-based problem solving. Unlike traditional methods that assign rewards to individual component outputs, our approach uses feedback from the final pipeline result. This allows the reward model to learn how early-stage decisions—such as problem formulation or intermediate solutions—affect the overall outcome. By focusing on the end-to-end performance, the reward model can better guide each stage of generation and selection towards the true task objective. This pipeline-adapted strategy not only bridges the gap between local and global evaluation, but may in principle extend to other complex, multi-stage tasks where coordinated optimization is essential, although this paper empirically validates only a two-stage instantiation.

我们根据四个不同的管道基准系统地评估我们的方法。实验结果表明，与传统的阶段式奖励模型相比，我们的管道适应奖励模型在端到端输出质量方面产生了持续的改进。此外，我们的分析提供了对奖励模型行为、指导机制及其与非基于训练的管道增强功能的集成的关键见解。我们强调数学优化的代码生成作为一个代表性用例。We systematically evaluate our approach on four diverse pipeline benchmarks. Experimental results demonstrate that our pipeline-adapted reward model yields consistent improvements in end-to-end output quality compared to traditional stage-wise reward models. Furthermore, our analysis provides key insights into reward model behaviors, guidance mechanisms, and their integration with non-training-based pipeline enhancements. We highlight code generation for mathematical optimization as one representative use case.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于管道的法学硕士系统Pipeline-based LLM Systems

经典深度学习中长期确立的管道范式The pipeline paradigm, long established in classical deep learning[12, 11, 6]，在大语言模型（LLM）时代变得越来越突出, has become increasingly prominent in the era of large language models (LLMs)[39, 23]。在此框架中，复杂的问题被分解为一系列子任务，每个子任务由专用模块或操作员解决。从历史上看，此类管道由一系列手工制作的函数或轻量级神经网络组成，每个函数负责特定的转换或分析步骤。随着法学硕士的出现，管道系统现在是通过链接多个法学硕士组件来构建的，每个组件专门负责数据解释、推理、生成或验证等阶段。. In this framework, a complex problem is decomposed into a sequence of subtasks, each addressed by a dedicated module or operator. Historically, such pipelines consisted of a series of hand-crafted functions or lightweight neural networks, each responsible for a specific transformation or analysis step. With the advent of LLMs, pipeline systems are now constructed by chaining multiple LLM components, each specializing in a stage such as data interpretation, reasoning, generation, or verification.

最近的研究表明，基于管道的 LLM 系统擅长处理多步骤推理、程序综合、代码生成和其他结构化任务，其中模块化分解提高了可解释性和可管理性Recent research demonstrates that pipeline-based LLM systems excel at handling multi-step reasoning, program synthesis, code generation, and other structured tasks where modular decomposition improves interpretability and manageability[10, 48, 41]。这些系统通常将不同的法学硕士或专门的提示策略分配给不同的阶段，从而能够更有效地利用模型能力和特定于任务的适应。例如，在程序综合中，LLM 管道可能首先将自然语言规范转换为形式表示，然后生成可执行代码，最后验证输出。. These systems often assign different LLMs or specialized prompting strategies to distinct stages, enabling more effective use of model capacity and task-specific adaptation. For example, in program synthesis, an LLM pipeline may first translate a natural language specification into a formal representation, then generate executable code, and finally validate the output.

从概念上讲，基于管道的 LLM 系统可以被视为更通用的多智能体或工具增强框架的子集，其中多个智能组件协作解决复杂问题Conceptually, pipeline-based LLM systems can be viewed as a subset of more general multi-agent or tool-augmented frameworks, where multiple intelligent components collaborate to solve complex problems[5, 20, 21]。在这种设置中，管道、工作流编排和多代理系统之间的界限变得不固定：管道强调顺序、分阶段处理，而基于代理和工具增强的方法可能涉及组件之间更加动态、交互或并行的协调。工作流编排框架通过启用条件分支、反馈循环和基于中间结果的自适应模块选择，进一步概括了这一想法。. In such settings, the boundaries between pipeline, workflow orchestration, and multi-agent systems become fluid: pipelines emphasize sequential, stage-wise processing, while agent-based and tool-augmented approaches may involve more dynamic, interactive, or parallel coordination among components. Workflow orchestration frameworks further generalize this idea by enabling conditional branching, feedback loops, and adaptive module selection based on intermediate results.

尽管有这样的承诺，基于管道的法学硕士系统面临着一些开放的挑战。跨管道阶段的错误传播、缺乏整体监督以及联合优化端到端性能的困难仍然是重大瓶颈。虽然模块化提高了可解释性和可扩展性，但它也增加了协调和质量控制的复杂性。此类系统的有效奖励建模和评估策略仍未得到充分探索，特别是与单阶段 LLM 优化的大量文献相比。Despite their promise, pipeline-based LLM systems face several open challenges. Error propagation across pipeline stages, the lack of holistic supervision, and difficulties in jointly optimizing for end-to-end performance remain significant bottlenecks. While modularization improves interpretability and scalability, it also increases the complexity of coordination and quality control. Effective reward modeling and evaluation strategies for such systems are still underexplored, especially when compared to the extensive literature on single-stage LLM optimization.

Ⅱ-BII-B 面向流程的人工智能的奖励模型Reward Models for Process-oriented AI

奖励模型已成为监督和选择大型语言模型输出的基本组成部分，特别是在输出质量评估非常简单的单阶段设置中Reward models have become a fundamental component for supervising and selecting the outputs of large language models, especially in single-stage settings where the evaluation of output quality is straightforward[28]。例如，奖励引导采样和来自人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）显着提高了开放域生成任务的事实性和用户满意度。. For example, reward-guided sampling and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) have significantly improved factuality and user satisfaction in open-domain generation tasks.

相比之下，将奖励模型应用于面向流程的人工智能系统（例如管道或多阶段工作流程）仍然是一个很大程度上尚未解决的挑战。在这些系统中，最终的输出质量取决于多个相互依赖的组件的协调性能。传统的基于结果的奖励模型通常被训练来评估单个阶段的输出，忽略中间结果对下游阶段和端到端解决方案的影响In contrast, applying reward models to process-oriented AI systems,such as pipelines or multi-stage workflows, remains a largely unsolved challenge. In these systems, the final output quality depends on the coordinated performance of multiple interdependent components. Ⅲ-CIII-C 管道奖励模型Pipeline Reward Models

设计动机：Design Motivation:在我们的管道中引入奖励模型的动机源于对模块化、高效和可扩展的多阶段生成的渴望。我们不是依赖单个大型模型来完成端到端任务，也不是使用大型带注释的数据集进行昂贵的微调，而是将管道分解为多个阶段，每个生成器都与一个轻量级奖励模型配对，该模型专门为该阶段评分和选择候选者。这种模块化方法降低了注释成本，并使奖励模型能够使用从管道本身自动收集的反馈信号来发展。The motivation for introducing reward models in our pipeline stems from the desire for modular, efficient, and scalable multi-stage generation. Instead of relying on a single large model for end-to-end task completion or expensive fine-tuning with large annotated datasets, we decompose the pipeline into phases, with each generator paired with a lightweight reward model that specializes in scoring and selecting candidates for that phase. This modular approach reduces annotation cost and enables the reward models to evolve using automatically collected feedback signals from the pipeline itself.

奖励模型评价标准：Reward Model Evaluation Criteria:对于制定和解决方案阶段，奖励模型旨在通过联合考虑输入提示和生成的候选来评估候选的质量。我们在提示中包含特定于任务的示例，以帮助奖励模型学习相关的评估线索。对于公式而言，奖励模型隐含地受到数学正确性和结构完整性的指导；对于解决方案，该模型进一步以代码正确性和执行可行性为指导。 为了量化最终解决方案的质量，我们使用两个指标：For both the formulation and solution stages, the reward models are designed to evaluate the quality of candidates by jointly considering the input prompt and the generated candidate. We include task-specific examples in the prompt to help the reward model learn relevant evaluation cues. For formulations, the reward model is implicitly guided by mathematical correctness and structural completeness; for solutions, the model is further guided by code correctness and execution feasibility. To quantify final solution quality, we use two metrics:

  • •

    执行率（ER）：Execution Rate (ER):执行无错误并产生有效输出的生成代码解决方案的比例。the proportion of generated code solutions that execute without error and produce valid outputs.

  • •

    求解精度（SA）：Solving Accuracy (SA):最佳值与任何提供的基本事实相匹配的已执行解决方案的比例。the proportion of executed solutions whose optimal values match any provided ground truth.

这些指标还为奖励模型的训练数据的构建提供信息。These metrics also inform the construction of training data for the reward models.

为了进一步增强管道输出的鲁棒性和正确性，我们将自调试机制纳入解决方案生成阶段（详细信息参见附录：提示设计）。当生成的候选代码无法正确编译或执行时，相关的编译错误和诊断信息以及原始问题陈述将在自调试循环中反馈到解决方案生成器中。然后，解决方案生成器尝试根据此反馈修改和重新生成代码，从而生成多个新的候选代码。与初始生成阶段一样，奖励模型会评估这些候选者并选择得分最高的候选者来执行。To further enhance the robustness and correctness of pipeline outputs, we incorporate a self-debugging mechanism into the solution generation stage (details in Appendix: Prompts Design). The solution generator then attempts to revise and regenerate code based on this feedback, producing multiple new candidates. As in the initial generation phase, the reward model evaluates these candidates and selects the highest-scoring one for execution.

这个过程可能会迭代多次：如果所选的纠正仍然失败，则执行进一步的错误反馈和代码重新循环，直到获得有效的解决方案或达到预定义的重试限制。然后将最终的执行结果与问题的真实解决方案进行比较This process may iterate multiple times: if the selected correction still fails, further cycles of error feedback and code regeneration are performed until a valid solution is obtained or a predefined retry limit is reached. The final execution result is then compared against the ground-truth solution for the problemPP，确保仅接受正确且可执行的代码作为管道输出。, ensuring that only correct and executable code is accepted as the pipeline output.

IV-D4 跨域影响Cross-Domain Implications

GSM8K 实验提供了 PARM 转移到一个根本不同的领域的补充证据。任务语义（小学数学与运筹学）、中间表示（解决方案与形式化表述）和评估协议（精确的数值匹配与可行性和最优性检查）都与优化实验不同，但 PARM 仍然通过相同的阶段特定贡献补充模式来改进 ER 和 SA。我们将 GSM8K 作为有针对性的概念验证来呈现；未来的工作仍是对其他非优化任务系列进行更广泛的验证。The GSM8K experiment provides supplementary evidence that PARM transfers to a fundamentally different domain. The task semantics (grade-school math vs. operations research), intermediate representation (solution plan vs. formal formulation), and evaluation protocol (exact numerical match vs. feasibility and optimality checking) all differ from the optimization experiments, yet PARM still improves both ER and SA with the same pattern of complementary stage-specific contributions. We present GSM8K as a targeted proof-of-concept; broader validation across additional non-optimization task families remains future work.

V 讨论Discussion

在这项工作中，我们表明由自动训练的奖励模型引导的管道框架是复杂推理管道的有前途的范例。在评估的两阶段设置中，我们的管道机制可以实现轻量级专家模型的模块化集成，并利用直接从执行反馈而不是手动注释派生的奖励信号。这种设计产生了强大且可重复的工作流程，可以超越所研究任务中的整体大型模型基线，而更广泛的可扩展性以更丰富的管道结构仍有待建立。In this work, we show that pipeline frameworks guided by automatically trained reward models are a promising paradigm for complex reasoning pipelines. In the evaluated two-stage setting, our pipeline mechanism enables modular integration of lightweight expert models and leverages reward signals derived directly from execution feedback rather than manual annotation. This design yields robust and reproducible workflows that can surpass monolithic large-model baselines in the studied tasks, while broader scalability to richer pipeline structures remains to be established.

我们方法的一个关键创新在于自动构建用于奖励模型训练的偏好数据集。通过直接从管道执行中收集监督信号而无需人工标记，我们可以在研究环境中实现自动奖励模型适应。这也保持了框架的模块化：原则上，可以在不改变核心训练配方的情况下纳入新的专家模型或奖励目标，尽管随着引入额外的真实阶段，数据构建的成本可能会大幅增长。A key innovation of our approach lies in the automated construction of preference datasets for reward model training. By collecting supervision signals directly from pipeline execution without human labeling, we enable automatic reward-model adaptation in the studied setting. This also keeps the framework modular: new expert models or reward objectives can, in principle, be incorporated without changing the core training recipe, although the cost of data construction can grow substantially as additional true stages are introduced.

在通用性方面，我们在优化基准上的主要结果以及补充的 GSM8K 实验表明，管道框架可以超越单个任务域。然而，跨其他领域（例如开放域推理或多模式任务）的更广泛验证仍然是未来研究的开放方向。我们还注意到，虽然我们的奖励模型在没有手动注释数据的情况下运行，但纳入少量高质量的人类反馈（特别是对于制定任务）可以进一步提高整体性能和可靠性。In terms of generalizability, our main results on optimization benchmarks, together with the supplementary GSM8K experiment, suggest that the pipeline framework can transfer beyond a single task domain. However, broader validation across additional domains—such as open-domain reasoning or multi-modal tasks—remains an open direction for future investigation. We also note that while our reward models operate without manually annotated data, incorporating a small amount of high-quality human feedback (especially for formulation tasks) could further improve overall performance and reliability.

展望未来，未来的工作可能会探索轻量级或在线奖励模型适应、混合人机循环监督、更可解释的奖励信号以及蒙特卡罗树搜索等高级搜索策略的集成。Looking ahead, future work may explore lightweight or online reward model adaptation, hybrid human-in-the-loop supervision, more interpretable reward signals, and integration of advanced search strategies such as Monte Carlo Tree Search.

Furthermore, although our method eliminates the need for manual annotation, a small amount of high-quality labeled data might further enhance reward model performance, particularly for subtle or domain-specific tasks. VI 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 PARM，这是一种用于复杂优化任务的模块化管道框架，它将轻量级专家模型与通过自动偏好数据收集训练的奖励模型相结合。通过将问题分解为子任务并集成专业组件（例如 Formulator 和 Coder），PARM 可实现解决方案的灵活组合和迭代细化。我们的框架利用完全无需手动注释即可训练的奖励模型，使用基于实际执行的反馈来指导高质量中间输出的选择。In this paper, we introduced PARM, a modular pipeline framework for complex optimization tasks that combines lightweight expert models with reward models trained through automated preference data collection. By decomposing problems into sub-tasks and integrating specialized components such as the Formulator and Coder, PARM enables flexible composition and iterative refinement of solutions. Our framework leverages reward models that are trained entirely without manual annotation, using real execution-based feedback to guide the selection of high-quality intermediate outputs.

跨多个基准的大量实验表明，PARM 不仅在求解精度和执行率方面优于直接大型模型基线，而且在评估的两阶段设置中表现出鲁棒性和有效性。奖励模型和自调试机制的加入进一步提高了性能，确认了模块化架构内奖励引导的迭代优化的价值。重要的是，我们的自动化数据收集过程表明，在此设置中无需手动注释即可进行奖励模型适应。Extensive experiments across multiple benchmarks demonstrate that PARM not only outperforms direct large-model baselines in solving accuracy and execution rate, but also exhibits robustness and effectiveness in the evaluated two-stage setting. The inclusion of reward models and a self-debugging mechanism further boosts performance, confirming the value of reward-guided, iterative optimization within a modular architecture. Importantly, our automated data collection process shows that reward-model adaptation can be carried out without manual annotation in this setting.

我们的工作强调了可扩展和可解释的基于管道的推理框架的潜力。对于未来的研究，我们的目标是将 PARM 推广到代码生成之外的更广泛领域，研究高级奖励建模策略，并进一步探索人类反馈和高级搜索技术的集成。我们相信，管道范式与自动奖励学习相结合，是解决更稳健、更透明的问题的一个有希望的方向，而跨其他领域和真正的 3+ 阶段管道的更广泛验证仍然是未来的工作。Our work highlights the potential of pipeline-based reasoning frameworks that are both extensible and interpretable. For future research, we aim to generalize PARM to broader domains beyond code generation, investigate advanced reward modeling strategies, and further explore the integration of human feedback and advanced search techniques. We believe that the pipeline paradigm, coupled with automated reward learning, is a promising direction for more robust and transparent problem solving, while broader validation across additional domains and true 3+ stage pipelines remains future work.
