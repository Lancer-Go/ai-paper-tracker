摘要Abstract

背景BACKGROUND
慢性疼痛会降低功能能力，从而降低生活质量，但客观地衡量这种功能影响，而不是仅仅依赖主观疼痛报告，在现实环境中仍然具有挑战性。虽然光学运动捕捉为评估改变的运动质量提供了高精度，但它成本高昂且仅限于实验室环境。我们的目标是开发和验证定量运动测试 (QMT)，这是一种计算机视觉管道，可从标准单眼智能手机视频中提取 3D 运动生物标记，平衡临床可及性与生物力学准确性。Chronic pain diminishes quality of life by decreasing functional ability, yet objectively measuring this functional impact, rather than relying solely on subjective pain reporting, remains challenging in real-world settings. While optical motion capture provides high precision for assessing altered movement quality, it is costly and restricted to laboratory environments. We aimed to develop and validate Quantitative Movement Testing (QMT), a computer vision pipeline extracting 3D kinematic biomarkers from standard monocular smartphone video, balancing clinical accessibility with biomechanical accuracy.
方法METHODS
我们利用基于深度学习的 3D 姿势估计，针对健康对照 (N=13) 中的黄金标准光学运动捕捉，在三个任务中验证了 QMT 流程：前屈、原地行进和小蹲。在留一受试者校准以纠正系统偏差后，我们在两个前瞻性临床队列中部署了 QMT 来评估现实世界的效用：一项针对纤维肌痛患者的干预前和干预后试验（PainLESS；N=80 入组，N=57 捕获 QMT 数据），以及一项针对慢性坐骨神经痛患者和健康对照的 30 天纵向在家监测研究（BeADS；N=97 入组，N=83 分析）。We validated the QMT pipeline, utilising deep learning-based 3D pose-estimation, against gold-standard optical motion capture in healthy controls (N=13) across three tasks: forward bending, marching on the spot, and the minisquat. Following leave-one-subject-out calibration to correct systematic bias, we deployed QMT in two prospective clinical cohorts to assess real-world utility: a pre- and post-intervention trial for fibromyalgia patients (PainLESS; N=80 enrolled, N=57 QMT data captured), and a 30-day longitudinal at-home monitoring study of chronic sciatica patients and healthy controls (BeADS; N=97 enrolled, N=83 analysed).
结果RESULTS
在使用健康参与者的实验室验证中，QMT 提取了与光学运动捕捉高度一致的临床运动学指标，产生了很强的相关性（In laboratory validation using healthy participants, QMT extracted clinical kinematic metrics with high agreement to optical motion capture, yielding strong correlations (r>0.85r>0.85）并且大多数指标的平均绝对误差较低。与传统欧拉 (3D) 推导（例如平面腰椎弯曲）相比，QMT 输出更好匹配的平面 (2D) 代理角度（例如腰椎和膝关节弯曲）) and low mean absolute errors for most metrics. QMT outputs better matched planar (2D) surrogate angles (e.g., lumbar and knee flexion) than traditional Euler (3D) derivations (e.g., planar lumbar flexionr=0.97r=0.97与欧拉vs. Eulerr=0.92r=0.92小深蹲期间）。 QMT 表现出较高的重测可靠性（during the minisquat). QMT demonstrated high test-retest reliability (r>0.86r>0.86）在纤维肌痛患者中，并成功追踪慢性坐骨神经痛的日常运动波动。虽然现实世界的家庭环境比实验室环境引入了更高的测量方差，但 QMT 完全基于远程记录发现坐骨神经痛患者的健康对照之间存在群体水平差异。) in fibromyalgia patients and successfully tracked day-to-day movement fluctuations in chronic sciatica. While real-world home settings introduced higher measurement variance than lab settings, QMT found group-level differences between healthy controls from sciatica patients based entirely on remote recordings.
结论CONCLUSIONS
通过建立经过验证的开源协议并根据当前的黄金标准光学运动捕捉对其进行基准测试，单目 3D 姿态估计为传统评估提供了一种可扩展的替代方案。 QMT 提供了一种客观、可访问的生物标志物，用于在大规模临床试验中跟踪疾病进展和治疗反应，但需要进一步研究来优化家庭环境中的可靠性。By establishing a validated, open-source protocol and benchmarking it against current gold-standard optical motion capture, monocular 3D pose estimation offers a scalable alternative to traditional assessments. QMT provides an objective, accessible biomarker for tracking disease progression and treatment response in large-scale clinical trials, though further research is needed to optimise reliability in home-based environments.

简介Introduction

肌肉骨骼疾病和慢性疼痛综合症影响着全球约 17.1 亿人，是全球残疾的主要原因Musculoskeletal conditions and chronic pain syndromes affect approximately 1.71 billion people globally, representing the leading contributor to disability worldwide(Cieza et al., 2020)。 对于这些患者来说，生活质量的下降是由于功能能力下降所致；这些情况的一个共同特征是运动质量改变，表现为运动范围缩小、运动变异性或保护性防护。至关重要的是，这些功能变化并不总是与机械组织损伤相关或与存在的任何结构损伤的程度成比例。例如，由损伤性或神经病理性机制驱动的病症，如纤维肌痛和坐骨神经痛，表现出独特的运动特征，通常因运动恐惧症（对运动的恐惧）而加剧. For these patients, the decline in quality of life is driven by decrease in functional ability; a common feature of these conditions is altered movement quality, manifesting as reduced range of motion, motor variability, or protective guarding. Crucially, these functional changes are not always associated with mechanical tissue injury or proportional to the degree of any structural damage present. For instance, conditions driven by nociplastic or neuropathic mechanisms, such as fibromyalgia and sciatica, display distinct motor signatures often exacerbated by kinesiophobia (fear of movement)(Vlaeyen and Linton, 2000; Vlaeyen et al., 2016)和中枢敏化and central sensitisation(Woolf, 2011; Kosek et al., 2021)。然而，测量这些运动变化仍然是一个挑战。正如定量感官测试 (QST) 提供体感功能的半客观评估. Yet, measuring these motor changes remains a challenge. Just as Quantitative Sensory Testing (QST) provides a semi-objective assessment of somatosensory function(Rolke et al., 2006)该领域缺乏同等的“定量运动测试”（QMT）来客观地描述患者的功能能力，可以负担得起且方便地在家中进行。目前，临床医生主要依靠主观视觉评估或静态测角测量，这两种方法都无法捕获现实世界中监测疾病进展或治疗反应所需的动态运动质量。, the field lacks an equivalent "Quantitative Movement Testing" (QMT) to objectively profile a patient’s functional capacity affordably and conveniently from their home. Currently, clinicians predominantly rely on subjective visual appraisal or static goniometry, neither of which captures the dynamic quality of movement required to monitor disease progression or treatment response in the real world.

尽管建立这些现实世界的生物标记刻不容缓，但当前的黄金标准——光学动作捕捉——仍然局限于实验室。虽然高度精确，但这些系统价格昂贵（需要专门的实验室基础设施和大量资本投资），需要专家长时间的劳动，并且缺乏生态有效性，因为在实验室环境中观察时患者可能会使用改变的运动策略Despite the urgency to establish these real-world biomarkers, the current gold standard, optical motion capture, remains tethered to the laboratory. While highly precise, these systems are prohibitively expensive (requiring dedicated laboratory infrastructure and substantial capital investment), demand hours of expert labour, and lack ecological validity, as patients may use altered movement strategies when observed in laboratory-based environments(Parsons, 2015)。此外，对于患有感觉过敏或触觉异常性疼痛的慢性疼痛人群来说，将反射标记附着到皮肤上的标准方案可能会造成身体不适，从而使得该方法不切实际且不公平。此外，实验室访问的运营后勤使得对患者群体进行系统的纵向跟踪变得困难。由于功能损伤对日常波动很敏感，纵向监测已成为数字医学的核心要素。正如所指出的. Further, for chronic pain populations with sensory hypersensitivity or tactile allodynia, the standard protocol of attaching reflective markers to the skin can be physically uncomfortable, rendering the method impractical and inequitable. Furthermore, the operational logistics of laboratory visits make systematic longitudinal tracking difficult in patient populations. Because functional impairment is sensitive to daily fluctuations, longitudinal monitoring has emerged as a core ingredient for digital medicine. As noted byKimmel (2025)，将运动作为生物标记物的障碍现在正在从纯粹的技术转变为操作，需要能够测量高保真运动学的工具，而无需在步态实验室进行光学运动捕捉。, the hurdles to deploying movement as a biomarker are now transitioning from purely technological to operational, necessitating tools that can measure high-fidelity kinematics without the friction of optical motion capture at a gait lab.

Conversely, single-camera approaches(Kidziński et al., 2020)可以成功地从标准视频估计基于临床运动学的指标。然而，这些方法通常依赖于端到端深度学习架构来映射 2D 关键点can successfully estimate clinical kinematic-based metrics from standard video. Importantly, prior approaches have not been applied to measure the functional impact of chronic pain. These prior works focus predominantly on gait and not discrete functional tasks, such as lumbar flexion or mini-squats, where the movement signatures of chronic pain are most evident.

Finally, to accelerate the adoption of objective movement profiling, we open-source the QMT software pipeline and protocol details.

方法Methods

参与者筛选和招募Participant Screening and Recruitment

这项研究评估了三个不同数据集的 QMT 管道：一个用于技术验证的主要实验室队列，以及两个用于评估现实世界效用的前瞻性临床队列。This study evaluated the QMT pipeline across three distinct datasets: a primary laboratory-based cohort for technical validation, and two prospective clinical cohorts to assess real-world utility.

验证数据集Validation Dataset

2025 年 2 月至 5 月期间，招募了 13 名健康成年人进行实验室验证。所有参与者均提供了书面知情同意书。该研究获得了牛津大学医学科学跨部门研究伦理委员会 (MS IDREC - 631993) 的伦理批准。Between February and May 2025, 13 healthy adults were recruited for laboratory validation. All participants provided written informed consent. The study received ethical approval from the University of Oxford Medical Sciences Interdivisional Research Ethics Committee (MS IDREC - 631993).

纳入标准为：年龄 18 岁或以上、会说英语、能独立行动。排除标准是：任何影响运动的肌肉骨骼或神经系统疾病（例如，腰背、臀部、膝盖或脚踝疼痛，或帕金森病）；过去六个月内接受过肌肉骨骼损伤或手术；使用关节支架或支撑物；以及当前怀孕情况。Inclusion criteria were: aged 18 years or older, English-speaking, and independently mobile. Exclusion criteria were: any musculoskeletal or neurological condition affecting movement (e.g., low back, hip, knee, or ankle pain, or Parkinson’s disease); musculoskeletal injury or surgery within the preceding six months; use of a joint brace or support; and current pregnancy.

Exclusion criteria were: known neurological conditions; major neuropsychiatric disorders (excluding depression and anxiety); epilepsy; cognitive impairment or neurodegenerative disorders; recent or planned surgery; current or planned night-shift work; specific untreated sleep disorders (e.g., sleep apnoea, restless leg syndrome); use of prescribed sleep medications on more than two nights in the past two weeks; currently receiving other psychological therapy for insomnia; and pregnancy or lactation.

在参加干预前访视的 80 名纤维肌痛参与者中，有 63 人参加了干预后访视，其中还有 9 人拒绝在研究访问当天进行部分或全部视频录制。因此，最终的分析样本包括 54 名受试者，具有干预前后配对的前屈记录，以及 57 名受试者，具有干预前后配对的原地行进和小深蹲记录。Of the 80 participants with fibromyalgia who attended a pre-intervention visit, 63 attended a post-intervention visit, and of these, a further 9 declined to perform some or all of the video recordings on the day of their research visit. Thus, the final analytical sample comprised 54 subjects with paired pre- and post-intervention forward bend recordings, and 57 subjects with paired pre- and post-intervention marching on the spot and minisquat recordings.

研究程序和方案设计Study Procedures and Protocol Design

临床方案围绕四种功能运动类别建立：前屈、小蹲、原地行进和脚后跟抬高。这些动作经过专门选择并标准化为可重复的协议，因为它们代表了日常活动中使用的动作，例如弯腰捡起东西、蹲下坐下、上楼梯或走一步。它们也是突显慢性疼痛病症中常见的功能缺陷的运动，包括慢性腰痛、坐骨神经痛、纤维肌痛和骨关节炎。The clinical protocols were built around four functional movement categories: forward bend, minisquat, marching on the spot and heel raise. These movements were specifically selected and standardised into a reproducible protocol because they represent actions that are used in day-to-day activities such as bending to pick something up, squatting to sit down, going up stairs or a step. They are also movements that highlight functional deficits commonly observed in chronic pain conditions, including chronic low back pain, sciatica, fibromyalgia, and osteoarthritis.

运动学度量推导Kinematic Metric Derivation

QMT 管道从 17 个关节的 VideoPose3D 骨骼中得出功能指标，包括骨盆、双侧臀部、膝盖、脚踝、脊柱、上躯干、颈基、头部、肩膀、肘部和手腕。根据这些预测的 3D 关节轨迹，我们计算了运动特定的平面 (2D) 角度和空间距离，以最大限度地提高几何稳定性。The QMT pipeline derived functional metrics from a 17-joint VideoPose3D skeleton comprising the pelvis, bilateral hips, knees, ankles, spine, upper torso, neck base, head, shoulders, elbows, and wrists. From these predicted 3D joint trajectories, we computed movement-specific planar (2D) angles and spatial distances to maximise geometric stability.

腰椎屈曲定义为脊柱-骨盆矢量相对于固定垂直参考轴的角度。膝关节屈曲计算为髋膝向量和膝踝向量之间的角度。髋部屈曲被定义为脊柱-骨盆和髋-膝向量之间的角度，而躯干摇摆是脊柱-骨盆和髋-骨盆向量之间的角度。空间功能指标包括前屈时的伸展范围（平均手腕位置和平均脚踝位置之间的垂直距离）和小蹲时的骨盆深度（骨盆节点和平均脚踝位置之间的垂直距离）。Lumbar flexion was defined as the angle of the spine-pelvis vector relative to a fixed vertical reference axis. Knee flexion was calculated as the angle between the hip-knee and knee-ankle vectors. Hip flexion was defined as the angle between the spine-pelvis and hip-knee vectors, while trunk sway was the angle between the spine-pelvis and hip-pelvis vectors. Spatial functional metrics included reach during the forward bend (the vertical distance between the mean wrist position and the mean ankle position) and pelvis depth during the minisquat (the vertical distance between the pelvis node and the mean ankle position).

为了根据黄金标准光学运动捕捉验证这些 QMT 推导，我们将每个 QMT 指标与其最接近的可用生物力学匹配（等效平面角）配对。由于低维姿势估计骨架不保留可用于运动捕捉的完整标记集几何形状，因此需要从 C3D 运动捕捉文件计算传统 3D 欧拉角和平面 (2D) 代理角。主要参考目标包括躯干欧拉角、功能性膝关节和髋关节欧拉角以及空间标记距离（例如手腕到脚踝的距离）。为了生成在解剖学上匹配 QMT 表示的平面 (2D) 代理角度，我们将特定的运动捕捉标记映射到 VideoPose3D 节点；例如，在比较步态实验室 T2、T6 和 T12 标记后，选择 T6 标记，因为它在解剖学上最适合映射到 QMT“脊柱”节点，用于计算平面腰椎弯曲和摇摆。To validate these QMT derivations against the gold standard optical motion capture, we paired each QMT metric with its closest available biomechanical match (the equivalent planar angle). Because the lower-dimensional pose-estimation skeleton does not retain the full marker-set geometry available to motion capture, this required calculating both traditional 3D Euler angles and planar (2D) surrogate angles from the C3D motion capture files. Primary reference targets included trunk Euler angles, functional knee and hip Euler angles, and spatial marker distances (e.g., wrist-to-ankle reach). To generate planar (2D) surrogate angles that anatomically matched the QMT representations, we mapped specific motion capture markers to the VideoPose3D nodes; for example, after comparing the gait laboratory T2, T6, and T12 markers, the T6 marker was selected as it best anatomically mapped to the QMT ’spine’ node for calculating planar lumbar flexion and sway.

Despite the inherent challenges of remote recording, the pipeline successfully differentiated between healthy controls and patients with chronic sciatica based entirely on remote, self-captured video, establishing its value in identifying group-level differences capturing functional impairment. Importantly, the operational feasibility and patient acceptability of this remote tracking approach were encouraging; only two of the 45 enrolled patients withdrew from upload longitudinal videos, and 8.2% of the unconstrained home recordings required manual exclusion due to severe framing errors. However, absolute measurement noise was approximately two to three times higher during at-home monitoring compared to the controlled validation study. This degradation is attributable to several factors. Environmentally, patients recorded movements under highly variable lighting conditions, at inconsistent distances and angles relative to their cameras, and occasionally with partial occlusions from furniture, other humans, or animals, and included reflections from mirrors. Methodologically, relying on a single daily trial intrinsically amplifies measurement variance compared to multi-trial laboratory averages.

这些发现揭示了指导我们未来工作的重要局限性。首先，我们的光学动作捕捉验证仅在受控实验室环境中的健康对照（N=13）中进行。未来的研究必须通过在实验室中重建嘈杂的、类似家庭的环境并直接在患者群体中测试可变的记录角度和距离来验证针对光学运动捕捉的管道。其次，我们在预训练状态下部署了 VideoPose3D 模型；对临床运动数据集上的底层姿势估计架构进行特定领域的微调可能会进一步提高准确性。总之，将 PainLESS 实验室记录中观察到的高保真度与 BeADS 家庭研究的可变数据进行比较表明，QMT 目前在基于实验室或基于诊所的受控环境中表现最可靠。尽管如此，开发用于实时帧反馈的智能用户界面并调整协议以要求每个家庭会话重复三次或多次，可以大大消除未来的算法噪音。These findings expose important limitations that direct our future work. First, our optical motion capture validation was conducted exclusively in a healthy controls (N=13) within a controlled laboratory setting. Future studies must validate the pipeline against optical motion capture by recreating noisy, home-like settings in the lab and testing variable recording angles and distances, directly within patient populations. Second, we deployed the VideoPose3D model in its pre-trained state; domain-specific fine-tuning of the underlying pose-estimation architecture on clinical movement datasets would likely yield further accuracy improvements. In summary, comparing the high fidelity observed in the PainLESS laboratory recordings to the variable data from the BeADS at-home study reveals that QMT currently performs most reliably within lab-based or clinic-based controlled environments. Nevertheless, developing intelligent user interfaces for real-time framing feedback and adjusting protocols to require three or more repetitions per home session could substantially smooth future algorithmic noise.

除了远程监控之外，QMT 还可以部署在一系列自由运动的实验室实验中，涉及实验诱发的疼痛和研究疼痛相关行为（例如，Looking beyond remote monitoring, QMT can be deployed in a range of free-motion in-lab experiments involving experimentally induced pain and studying pain-related behaviour (e.g.,Tong et al. (2025); Mahajan et al. (2025); Eccleston et al. (2022)）。最终，收集高频、客观的运动学数据为预测计算建模开辟了有希望的途径). Ultimately, collecting high-frequency, objective kinematics opens promising avenues for predictive computational modelling(Mahajan and Seymour, 2025; Wiech, 2016; Jepma et al., 2018; Mahajan et al., 2026)应用于简单实验室实验之外的纵向真实世界数据。 QMT 在 PainLESS 试验中的实用性测试了基于睡眠的干预措施的效果，这为研究睡眠和慢性疼痛相互影响的复杂方式提供了一条途径to be applied outside of simple lab experiments to longitudinal real-world data. The utility of QMT in the PainLESS trial testing the effect of a sleep-based intervention highlights an avenue for studying the complex ways in which sleep and chronic pain affect each other(Kelleher et al., 2026)。此外，QMT 和类似测量工具的可行性为应用上述预测计算模型提供了基础，以潜在地预测哪些患者可能康复以及哪些患者可能转变为恶化的慢性疼痛状态. Furthermore, the feasibility of QMT and similar measurement tools provides a foundation for applying the aforementioned predictive computational modelling to potentially forecast which patients may recover and which may transition into worsening chronic pain states(Schmid et al., 2023).

总之，QMT 证明单眼 3D 姿势估计可以作为一种可扩展的客观工具来评估与慢性疼痛相关的功能运动。尽管在不受约束的家庭环境中仍然存在挑战，但该管道显着降低了传统基于动作捕捉的跟踪的操作障碍，为慢性疼痛人群更容易和更公平的功能评估奠定了基础。In conclusion, QMT demonstrates that monocular 3D pose estimation can serve as a scalable, objective tool for assessing functional movement relevant to chronic pain. While challenges remain in unconstrained domestic environments, this pipeline significantly lowers the operational barriers of traditional motion capture-based tracking, laying the groundwork for more accessible and equitable functional assessment in chronic pain populations.
