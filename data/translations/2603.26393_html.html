通过与对比度无关的实例优化调整冻结的单模态主干以进行多模态配准Adapting Frozen Mono-modal Backbones for Multi-modal Registration via Contrast-Agnostic Instance Optimization

Yi Zhang    Yidong Zhao    Qian Tao
摘要Abstract

可变形图像配准仍然是医学图像分析中的一个核心挑战，特别是在扫描强度分布显着变化的多模态场景下。虽然深度学习方法提供了有效的前馈预测，但它们通常无法在测试时的分布变化下稳健地泛化。一个简单的补救措施是全面的网络微调，但对于 Transformer 或深度 U-Net 等现代架构来说，在 3D 中运行时，这种调整在内存和运行时上都非常昂贵。与此同时，在存在剧烈的域变化的情况下，天真的微调更容易面临性能潜在下降的问题。在这项工作中，我们提出了一个注册框架，集成了冻结的预训练Deformable image registration remains a central challenge in medical image analysis, particularly under multi-modal scenarios where intensity distributions vary significantly across scans. While deep learning methods provide efficient feed-forward predictions, they often fail to generalize robustly under distribution shifts at test time. A straightforward remedy is full network fine-tuning, yet for modern architectures such as Transformers or deep U-Nets, this adaptation is prohibitively expensive in both memory and runtime when operating in 3D. Meanwhile, the naive fine-tuning struggles more with potential degradation in performance in the existence of drastic domain shifts. In this work, we propose a registration framework that integrates a frozen pretrained单模态mono-modal具有轻量级适应管道的注册模型registration model with a lightweight adaptation pipeline for多式联运multi-modal图像注册。具体来说，我们采用基于与对比无关的表示生成和细化模块的风格迁移，以在测试时通过实例优化来弥合模态和领域差距。这种设计与主干单模态模型的选择正交，从而避免了完全微调的计算负担，同时保留了适应未知领域的灵活性。我们在 Learn2Reg 2025 LUMIR 验证集上评估我们的方法，并观察到相对于预训练的最先进的单模态主干网的持续改进。特别是，该方法在多模态子集上排名第二，在域外子集上排名第三，在 Dice 得分中总体排名第四。这些结果表明，将冻结单模态模型与模态适应和轻量级实例优化相结合，为稳健的多模态配准提供了有效且实用的途径。image registration. Specifically, we employ style transfer based on contrast-agnostic representation generation and refinement modules to bridge modality and domain gaps with instance optimization at test time. This design is orthogonal to the choice of backbone mono-modal model, thus avoids the computational burden of full fine-tuning while retaining the flexibility to adapt to unseen domains. We evaluate our approach on the Learn2Reg 2025 LUMIR validation set and observe consistent improvements over the pretrained state-of-the-art mono-modal backbone. In particular, the method ranks second on the multi-modal subset, third on the out-of-domain subset, and achieves fourth place overall in Dice score. These results demonstrate that combining frozen mono-modal models with modality adaptation and lightweight instance optimization offers an effective and practical pathway toward robust multi-modal registration.††脚注文本：footnotetext:本文是 MICCAI 2025 Learn2Reg 挑战赛的一部分。This paper was prepared as part of the Learn2Reg Challenge of MICCAI 2025.

1 简介Introduction

医学图像配准建立了医学图像之间的解剖对应关系，并且仍然是治疗计划和纵向研究等应用的先决条件Medical image registration establishes anatomical correspondences between medical images and remains a prerequisite for applications such as treatment planning and longitudinal studies[26, 12, 25]。经典方法针对每个图像对的参数化变换优化相似性正则化目标. Classical approaches optimize a similarity-regularization objective with respect to a parameterized transformation for each image pair[13]。机器学习的最新进展通过用数据驱动的预测取代逐例优化来重塑医学图像配准. Recent advances in machine learning have reshaped medical image registration by replacing per-case optimization with data-driven prediction[23]。无监督框架通常保留经典目标，仍然将图像相似性项与变形正则化相结合，但通过在群体中的许多对上训练的神经网络对其进行优化. Unsupervised frameworks typically retain the classical objective which still combines an image similarity term with deformation regularization but optimize it through neural networks trained across many pairs in a population[1, 5]。自从U网出现以来. Since the advent of U-Net[22]，一个丰富的无监督注册架构家族已经出现, a rich family of unsupervised registration architectures has emerged[1, 4]。 最近，Transformers 等替代骨干网. More recently, alternative backbones such as Transformers[2]，隐式神经表征, implicit neural representations[30, 29]和双编码器, and dual encoders[16]已经被探索来捕获长程依赖性和连续变形场。have been explored to capture long-range dependencies and continuous deformation fields.

一个持续的挑战是A persistent challenge is跨模态cross-modality配准，其中不同的 MRI 对比度（registration, where varying MRI contrasts (例如e.g.、T1w、T2w、FLAIR）破坏了简单的强度对应关系。 2025 Learn2Reg LUMIR 赛道强调了这个问题，该赛道评估对比度变化和零样本设置下的鲁棒性, T1w, T2w, FLAIR) disrupt simple intensity correspondences. This issue has been emphasized in the 2025 Learn2Reg LUMIR track, which evaluates robustness under contrast shifts and zero-shot settings[3, 27, 17, 8]。经典的跨模态注册通常采用互信息（MI）及其变体. Classical cross-modality registration often employs mutual information (MI) and its variants[6]，或与模态无关的局部描述符，例如 MIND, or modality-agnostic local descriptors such as MIND[9]。基于学习的策略包括针对多模态相似性的深度度量学习. Learning-based strategies include deep metric learning for multi-modal similarity[20, 18]，图像到图像的转换以合成类似目标的对比度, image-to-image translation to synthesize target-like contrast[21]和对比不变的训练方案, and contrast-invariant training regimes[10]。尽管取得了这些进步，但现代预训练主干网在其原生单模态域之外进行测试时，性能仍然大幅下降。. Despite these advances, modern pretrained backbones still suffer substantial performance drops when tested outside their native mono-modal domain.

实例优化 (IO) 已成为通过更新每个图像对的参数来进行测试时适应的轻量级方法Instance optimization (IO) has emerged as a lightweight means of test-time adaptation by updating parameters for each image pair[1, 19, 28]。然而，对大型 3D 模型（例如 U-Net 或 Transformers）的全面微调仍然非常昂贵，而且在严重的模态差距下，简单的 IO 可能会不稳定。随着最近基础注册骨干的可用性. However, full fine-tuning of large 3D models such as U-Nets or Transformers remains prohibitively expensive, and naive IO may be unstable under severe modality gaps. With the recent availability of foundational registration backbones[28, 7]，关键问题是如何使这些强大的单模态模型有效地适应多模态或域外场景。, the key question is how to adapt these powerful yet mono-modal models efficiently to multi-modal or out-of-domain scenarios.

在这项工作中，我们引入了一个与对比无关的实例优化框架，该框架充当预训练注册主干的通用测试时适配器。通过将主干输出作为初始化并应用具有轻量级细化的门控样式传输模块，该方法与主干选择保持正交并产生较小的开销。除了提高跨模态鲁棒性之外，它还持续增强了最先进的单模态基线的域内性能。我们的方法在 Learn2Reg 2025 LUMIR 基准上进行了验证，展示了域内、域外和多模态轨道的竞争准确性，为稳健的多模态变形配准建立了轻量级且广泛适用的适配器。In this work, we introduce a contrast-agnostic instance optimization framework that acts as a general test-time adaptor for pretrained registration backbones. By taking the backbone output as initialization and applying a gated style-transfer module with lightweight refinement, the method remains orthogonal to backbone choice and incurs minor overhead. Beyond improving cross-modality robustness, it consistently enhances in-domain performance of state-of-the-art mono-modal baselines. Validated on the Learn2Reg 2025 LUMIR benchmark, our approach demonstrates competitive accuracy across in-domain, out-of-domain, and multi-modal tracks, establishing a lightweight and broadly applicable adaptor for robust multi-modal deformable registration.

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了一个灵活的测试时适应框架，该框架通过对比不可知的传输和轻量级实例优化，重新调整预训练的单模态主干的用途，以实现稳健的多模态配准。 该方法仍然与主干选择正交，避免了全面微调的高昂成本，并且在 Learn2Reg 2025 LUMIR 基准测试中名列前茅。 我们的消融研究进一步证实了基于 Brain-ID 的转移、多级成分细化和适度预训练的好处。 结果表明，即使是冻结的主干网也可以有效地适应具有挑战性的跨模态设置，作为使用所提出的框架进行完整网络微调的稳健替代方案。We introduced a flexible test-time adaptation framework that repurposes pretrained mono-modal backbones for robust multi-modal registration through contrast-agnostic transfer and lightweight instance optimization. The approach remains orthogonal to the backbone choice, avoids the prohibitive cost of full fine-tuning, and ranks among the top-performing methods on the Learn2Reg 2025 LUMIR benchmark. Our ablation studies further confirm the benefit of Brain-ID based transfer, multi-level compositional refinement, and moderate pretraining. The results demonstrate that even frozen backbones can be effectively adapted to challenging cross-modality settings as a robust alternative to full network finetuning with the proposed framework.
