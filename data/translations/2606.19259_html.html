摘要Abstract

富含文本的图像通常包含隐私敏感、事务或决策相关信息。随着最近的多模态图像生成模型越来越能够合成真实的文本内容和结构化视觉设计，检测人工智能生成的富含文本的图像已成为数字信任和内容真实性的重要挑战。然而，现有的基准主要关注以对象为中心的图像，并且对以文本语义和布局组织为中心的场景提供有限的覆盖。在本文中，我们介绍了一个多域基准测试，用于检测由 OpenAI 的 GPT Image 2 生成的富含文本的图像。该基准测试包含 8,602 张图像，涵盖六个代表性类别：商业海报、信息图表、学术海报、收据、表格和 UI 屏幕截图。使用此基准，我们在零样本设置下评估了五个具有代表性的人工智能生成图像检测器，并分析了它们的整体、类别和后处理鲁棒性。我们的结果表明，检测器的性能高度依赖于领域：在某些类别中表现良好的方法通常在其他类别中表现不佳，甚至最强的传统检测器也对 JPEG 压缩表现出严重的敏感性。我们进一步使用多模态视觉语言模型进行探索性评估，揭示其在结构化格式上的前景和局限性。这些发现凸显了现代人工智能生成图像需要文本和布局感知的检测方法。我们的数据集于 XXX 发布。Text-rich images often contain privacy-sensitive, transactional, or decision-relevant information. As recent multimodal image generation models become increasingly capable of synthesizing realistic textual content and structured visual designs, detecting AI-generated text-rich images has become an important challenge for digital trust and content authenticity. Existing benchmarks, however, largely focus on object-centric images and provide limited coverage of scenarios where textual semantics and layout organization are central. In this paper, we introduce a multi-domain benchmark for detecting text-rich images generated by OpenAI’s GPT Image 2. The benchmark contains 8,602 images across six representative categories: commercial posters, infographics, academic posters, receipts, tables, and UI screenshots. Using this benchmark, we evaluate five representative AI-generated image detectors in a zero-shot setting and analyze their overall, category-wise, and post-processing robustness. Our results show that detector performance is highly domain-dependent: methods that perform well in some categories often fail on others, and even the strongest conventional detector exhibits severe sensitivity to JPEG compression. We further conduct an exploratory evaluation with a multimodal vision-language model, revealing both its promise and its limitations on structured formats. These findings highlight the need for text- and layout-aware detection methods for modern AI-generated images. Our dataset is released at XXX.

1 简介Introduction

生成真实的文本内容长期以来一直是图像生成模型的一个具有挑战性的问题。早期的系统经常产生扭曲的字符、不一致的单词结构和语义上无意义的文本，使得人工智能生成的图像在文本丰富的场景中相对容易识别。多模态图像生成的最新进展极大地提高了这种能力Generating realistic textual content has long been a challenging problem for image generation models. Earlier systems frequently produced distorted characters, inconsistent word structures, and semantically meaningless text, making AI-generated images relatively easy to identify in text-rich scenarios. Recent advances in multimodal image generation have substantially improved this capability[17, 2]。现代图像生成模型现在能够呈现连贯且有意义的文本，同时保持与周围视觉内容的一致性。. Modern image generation models are now able to render coherent and meaningful text while maintaining consistency with surrounding visual content.

生成高质量文本的能力将人工智能生成图像的范围扩展到传统艺术和创意应用之外。如今，图像生成系统可以生成各种富含文本的内容，包括用户界面（UI 屏幕截图）、收据、表格、海报和其他类似文档的图像。随着这些图像变得越来越真实和易于理解，人们开始担心创建误导性或看起来真实的内容The ability to generate high-quality text has expanded the scope of AI-generated images beyond traditional artistic and creative applications. Today, image generation systems can produce a wide range of text-rich content, including User Interfaces (UI screenshots), receipts, tables, posters, and other document-like images. As these images become increasingly realistic and accessible, concerns have emerged regarding the creation of misleading or authentic-looking content[26]。因此，可靠的图像真实性评估在文本丰富的场景中变得越来越重要。. Consequently, reliable image authenticity assessment is becoming increasingly important in text-rich scenarios.

这一趋势的最新例子是 OpenAI 的 GPT Image 2，以下简称 GPT-Image-2，它展示了生成具有可读文本和结构化布局的图像的强大能力A recent example of this trend is OpenAI’s GPT Image 2, hereafter referred to as GPT-Image-2, which demonstrates strong capability in generating images with readable text and structured layouts[16]。此类系统生成的图像可以与现实世界的视觉文档非常相似，这为人工智能生成的图像检测带来了新的挑战。与传统的面向对象的图像相比，富含文本的图像通常涉及文本信息、空间排列和结构化布局，这些共同影响图像语义，使得真实性评估更具挑战性。. Images generated by such systems can closely resemble real-world visual documents, creating new challenges for AI-generated image detection. Compared with conventional object-oriented images, text-rich images often involve textual information, spatial arrangements, and structured layouts that jointly contribute to image semantics, making authenticity assessment more challenging.

尽管这个问题越来越重要，但现有数据集为研究富含文本的人工智能生成图像提供的支持有限Despite the growing importance of this problem, existing datasets provide limited support for studying text-rich AI-generated images[29, 9, 20]。通用人工智能生成的图像数据集主要关注视觉内容，几乎不包含有意义的文本，而现有的文本相关数据集通常仅限于特定的应用领域. General-purpose AI-generated image datasets primarily focus on visual content and contain little meaningful text, while existing text-related datasets are often restricted to specific application domains[26, 27, 11]。此外，使用最新的多模态图像生成模型（例如 GPT-Image-2）构建的数据集相对较少。因此，仍然缺乏同时涵盖多种文本丰富场景和现代图像生成系统的数据集。此外，现有的人工智能生成图像检测器对此类内容的性能尚未得到系统评估。. Moreover, relatively few datasets are constructed using recent multimodal image generation models such as GPT-Image-2. As a result, there remains a lack of datasets that simultaneously cover diverse text-rich scenarios and modern image generation systems. In addition, the performance of existing AI-generated image detectors on such content has not been systematically evaluated.

为了解决这些差距，我们引入了使用 GPT-Image-2 生成的新的多域文本丰富图像数据集，涵盖六个代表性类别：UI 屏幕截图、收据、表格、信息图表、学术海报和商业海报。基于该数据集，我们建立了富含文本的人工智能生成图像检测的基准，并对不同场景下的几种代表性检测方法进行了统一评估。To address these gaps, we introduce a new multi-domain dataset of text-rich images generated using GPT-Image-2, covering six representative categories: UI Screenshot, Receipt, Table, Infographics, Academic Poster, and Commercial Poster. Based on this dataset, we establish a benchmark for text-rich AI-generated image detection and conduct a unified evaluation of several representative detection methods across different scenarios.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 AI 生成的图像数据集AI-Generated Image Datasets

人工智能生成的图像数据集已成为开发和评估图像真实性检测器的重要基础。 GenImage、ArtiFact 和 WildFake 等代表性数据集包含由各种图像生成模型生成的图像，包括生成对抗网络 (GAN)、扩散模型 (DM) 和其他早期的图像生成框架AI-generated image datasets have become an important foundation for developing and evaluating image authenticity detectors. Representative datasets such as GenImage, ArtiFact, and WildFake contain images generated by a variety of image generation models, including Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Diffusion Models (DMs), and other earlier image generation frameworks[29, 20, 9]。这些数据集为研究人工智能生成的图像检测和检测器泛化提供了大规模资源。然而，它们主要关注面向对象的视觉内容，并且包含很少或不包含有意义的文本信息。此外，这些数据集中生成的大多数图像都源自早期生成的模型，并且对最新多模态图像生成器的覆盖范围有限。. These datasets provide large-scale resources for studying AI-generated image detection and detector generalization. However, they mainly focus on object-oriented visual content and contain little or no meaningful textual information. Moreover, most generated images in these datasets originate from earlier generation models and provide limited coverage of recent multimodal image generators.

认识到文本内容在人工智能生成的图像中日益增长的重要性，已经提出了几个用于文本相关伪造检测的数据集。 Tampered-IC13、OSTF 和 AIForge-Doc 专注于场景文本和面向文档的图像中的本地化文本操作Recognizing the growing importance of textual content in AI-generated images, several datasets have been proposed for text-related forgery detection. Tampered-IC13, OSTF, and AIForge-Doc focus on localized text manipulation in scene-text and document-oriented images[24, 19, 26]。相比之下，GPT4o-Receipt 和 SciFigDetect 完全考虑人工智能生成的收据和科学数据，反映了现代图像生成系统向合成文本丰富的图像生成的转变. In contrast, GPT4o-Receipt and SciFigDetect consider fully AI-generated receipts and scientific figures, reflecting a shift toward synthetic text-rich image generation by modern image generation systems[27, 11].

尽管取得了这些进步，现有数据集在各个应用程序领域中仍然分散，并且对各种文本丰富的场景的覆盖范围有限Despite these advances, existing datasets remain fragmented across individual application domains and provide limited coverage of diverse text-rich scenarios[6, 7]。因此，学术海报、商业海报、表格和移动用户界面等重要类别的代表性仍然不足。此外，大多数现有数据集都是使用早期的生成模型构建的，并没有反映最新多模态图像生成系统（例如 GPT-Image-2）的功能，该系统可以生成高度连贯的文本和结构化布局。我们的数据集通过覆盖 GPT-Image-2 生成的多个具有代表性的富含文本的图像类别来补充现有资源。. As a result, important categories such as academic posters, commercial posters, tables, and mobile user interfaces remain underrepresented. Moreover, most existing datasets are built using earlier generative models and do not reflect the capabilities of recent multimodal image generation systems such as GPT-Image-2, which can produce highly coherent text and structured layouts. Our dataset complements existing resources by covering multiple representative text-rich image categories generated by a GPT-Image-2.

2.2 人工智能生成的图像检测方法AI-Generated Image Detection Methods

近年来，人工智能生成的图像检测引起了广泛关注。现有的方法利用不同的视觉伪像、统计模式和学习的表示来区分真实图像和合成图像。AI-generated image detection has attracted significant attention in recent years. Existing methods exploit different visual artifacts, statistical patterns, and learned representations to distinguish real images from synthetic ones.

早期研究主要依赖于训练为二元分类器的卷积神经网络 (CNN)。 CNNSpot 证明，当使用适当的数据增强进行训练时，基于 ResNet 的标准检测器可以实现强大的跨生成器泛化Early studies primarily relied on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) trained as binary classifiers. CNNSpot demonstrated that a standard ResNet-based detector can achieve strong cross-generator generalization when trained with appropriate data augmentation[23]。另一条研究重点是图像合成过程中引入的伪影。 NPR 表明，生成模型中的上采样操作留下了特征性的相邻像素依赖性，可以作为有效且可推广的检测信号. Another line of research focuses on artifacts introduced during image synthesis. NPR showed that upsampling operations in generative models leave characteristic neighboring-pixel dependencies that can serve as effective and generalizable detection signals[22].

其他方法试图识别图像生成过程本身留下的痕迹。例如，DIRE 通过基于扩散的重建误差来检测扩散生成的图像Other methods attempt to identify traces left by the image generation process itself. For example, DIRE detects diffusion-generated images through diffusion-based reconstruction errors[25]。最近，预训练的视觉和视觉语言模型已被用于人工智能生成的图像检测。 UnivFD 利用从预训练的 CLIP 模型中提取的表示，并在不同图像生成器之间展示了强大的泛化能力. More recently, pretrained vision and vision-language models have been adopted for AI-generated image detection. UnivFD leverages representations extracted from the pretrained CLIP model and demonstrates strong generalization across different image generators[15]。最近基于 CLIP 和 Vision Transformer (ViT) 的方法也报告了类似的发现，强调了大规模视觉预训练对于鲁棒检测的好处. Similar findings have also been reported by recent CLIP- and Vision Transformer (ViT)-based approaches, highlighting the benefits of large-scale visual pretraining for robust detection[3].

尽管这些方法已经在现有的人工智能生成图像基准上证明了有效性，但它们在由最近的多模态图像生成系统生成的富含文本的图像上的性能仍未得到充分探索。因此，评估所提出的数据集上的代表性检测器可以提供有用的见解，了解它们在不同的文本丰富场景中的泛化能力。Although these methods have demonstrated effectiveness on existing AI-generated image benchmarks, their performance on text-rich images generated by recent multimodal image generation systems remains underexplored. Evaluating representative detectors on the proposed dataset can therefore provide useful insights into their generalization ability across diverse text-rich scenarios.

3 数据集构建Dataset Construction

3.1 数据集概述Dataset Overview

为了表征现实世界中富含文本的图像，我们沿二维组织富含文本的图像。第一个维度是文本布局的规律性程度，范围从自由形式的视觉构图到高度结构化的文本排列。第二个维度是图像使用的功能语境，包括视觉传达、信息呈现、学术交流、交易记录、结构化数据表示、人机交互等。这些背景还与实际风险密切相关，其中生成或操纵的富含文本的图像可能会影响信息可信度、隐私敏感记录和面向用户的界面。To characterize real-world text-rich images, we organize text-rich images along two dimensions. The first dimension is the degree of text-layout regularity, ranging from free-form visual composition to highly structured textual arrangements. The second dimension is the functional context in which the image is used, including visual communication, information presentation, academic communication, transaction records, structured data representation, and human-computer interaction. These contexts are also closely related to practical risks, where generated or manipulated text-rich images may affect information trustworthiness, privacy-sensitive records, and user-facing interfaces.

基于这些考虑，我们在数据集中包含了六个代表性类别：商业海报、信息图表、学术海报、收据、表格和 UI 屏幕截图。 这六类的代表性例子如下所示：Based on these considerations, we include six representative categories in our dataset: commercial posters, infographics, academic posters, receipts, tables, and UI screenshots. 3.2 与现有数据集的比较Comparison with Existing Datasets

随着人工智能系统生成嵌入文本内容的图像的能力越来越强，富含文本的数据集对于人工智能生成的图像检测和评估文本图像生成场景中的未来多模态模型变得越来越重要。在这种情况下，有用的基准应该反映现实世界中文本丰富的视觉内容的复杂性。具体来说，它应该涵盖具有不同布局和实用视觉格式的多个代表性类别，包括用于检测器评估的真实样本和生成样本，并考虑全图像生成，其中合成整个图像而不是仅修改局部文本区域。As AI systems become increasingly capable of generating images with embedded textual content, text-rich datasets are becoming important for AI-generated image detection and for evaluating future multimodal models in text-image generation scenarios. In this setting, a useful benchmark should reflect the complexity of real-world text-rich visual content. Specifically, it should cover multiple representative categories with diverse layouts and practical visual formats, include both real and generated samples for detector evaluation, and consider full-image generation, where the entire image is synthesized rather than only local text regions being modified.

Existing datasets have made valuable contributions to text-rich image understanding and AI-generated text-rich image forensics. However, many of them were not originally designed to jointly cover multiple text-rich categories, real and generated samples, and full-image generation settings. Our benchmark complements these resources by providing a unified multi-category evaluation setting for detecting AI-generated text-rich images.

3.3 数据集构建Dataset Construction

4 实验Experiments

在构建了具有六个丰富文本类别的拟议数据集后，我们继续评估现有的人工智能生成的图像检测器。我们选择一组有代表性的检测方法和模型来对性能进行基准测试，并分析结果以了解它们的优势、局限性以及在处理文本丰富的视觉域方面需要改进的领域。Having constructed the proposed dataset with six text-rich categories, we proceed to evaluate existing AI-generated image detectors. We select a set of representative detection methods and models to benchmark performance, and analyze the results to understand their strengths, limitations, and areas for improvement in handling text-rich visual domains.

CNNSpot 是一种基于 CNN 的取证检测器，经过训练可识别基于 CNN 的生成模型生成的图像CNNSpot is a CNN-based forensic detector trained to identify images produced by CNN-based generative models[23]。其主要发现是，当使用适当的预处理和数据增强时，对来自一个生成器的图像进行训练的分类器可以泛化到来自看不见的生成器架构的图像。这使得 CNNSpot 成为用于跨生成器评估的代表性的基于工件的检测器。. 4.2 实施细节Implementation Details

所有检测器均使用从其官方版本获得的公开预训练检查点进行评估。没有任何检测器经过微调、重新训练或以其他方式适应我们的数据集。所有模型都是在零样本评估设置下使用官方推理管道执行的。All detectors were evaluated using publicly available pretrained checkpoints obtained from their official releases. No detector was fine-tuned, retrained, or otherwise adapted to our dataset. All models were executed using their official inference pipelines under a zero-shot evaluation setting.

输入预处理遵循官方实现中提供的特定于检测器的程序。由于不同的检测器依赖于不同的主干架构和预处理管道，因此图像大小调整、标准化和输入格式由相应的官方代码库处理。没有应用额外的预处理、手动裁剪或特定于数据集的图像转换。此外，没有使用测试时间增加。Input preprocessing followed the detector-specific procedures provided in the official implementations. Since different detectors rely on different backbone architectures and preprocessing pipelines, image resizing, normalization, and input formatting were handled by the corresponding official codebases. No additional preprocessing, manual cropping, or dataset-specific image transformations were applied. Furthermore, no test-time augmentation was used.

在推理过程中，每个图像仅被评估一次并产生单个预测。分类决策是使用检测器输出分数的阈值 0.5 生成的。没有采用重复采样、投票策略或集成方法。During inference, each image was evaluated exactly once and produced a single prediction. Classification decisions were generated using a threshold of 0.5 on the detector output score. No repeated sampling, voting strategy, or ensemble method was employed.

4.3 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

我们使用准确度、精确度、召回率和 F1 分数来评估检测器的性能，将 AI 生成的图像视为正类。在此设置中，真阳性 (TP) 表示生成的图像被正确分类为假图像，而真阴性 (TN) 表示正确分类为真实图像的真实图像。精确度衡量虚假预测的可靠性，而召回率则反映有多少生成的图像被成功检测到。由于基准测试同时包含真实样本和生成样本，并且一些检测器表现出很强的预测偏差，因此我们主要使用 F1 分数来总结精度和召回率之间的平衡。在分析召回率与 FPR 权衡时，我们还会报告误报率 (FPR)，因为它反映了真实图像被错误标记为假图像的频率。We evaluate detector performance using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score, treating AI-generated images as the positive class. In this setting, true positives (TP) denote generated images correctly classified as fake, while true negatives (TN) denote real images correctly classified as real. Precision measures how reliable the fake predictions are, while recall reflects how many generated images are successfully detected. Since the benchmark contains both real and generated samples and some detectors show strong prediction bias, we mainly use F1 score to summarize the balance between precision and recall. We also report the false positive rate (FPR) when analyzing the recall-FPR trade-off, as it reflects how often real images are incorrectly flagged as fake.

4.5 稳健性分析Robustness Analysis

实际设置中的探测器性能可能会受到图像后处理操作（例如调整大小和重新压缩）的影响。在我们的数据集中，生成的图像以 PNG 格式存储，而一些真实图像（例如 UI 屏幕截图和商业海报）以 JPG 或 JPEG 格式收集。此外，先前的研究表明，在线平台在图像分发过程中通常会进一步压缩和调整大小Detector performance in practical settings may be affected by image post-processing operations such as resizing and recompression. In our dataset, generated images are stored in PNG format, while some real images (e.g., UI screenshots and commercial posters) are collected in JPG or JPEG format. In addition, prior studies show that online platforms often apply further compression and resizing during image distribution[4, 8]。受这些观察的启发，我们进行了鲁棒性实验，以评估此类后处理对检测性能的影响。在评估的方法中，选择 NPR 进行本次分析是因为其强大的整体性能和代表性的基于工件的设计。我们研究了其检测性能在常见图像后处理操作下的变化，包括质量级别为 95、85、75 和 50 的 JPEG 压缩，大小调整为 512 或 1024 像素，然后进行 JPEG 压缩，以及 PNG 重新编码。. Motivated by these observations, we conduct robustness experiments to evaluate the impact of such post-processing on detection performance. Among the evaluated methods, NPR is selected for this analysis due to its strong overall performance and representative artifact-based design. We examine how its detection performance changes under common image post-processing operations, including JPEG compression with quality levels of 95, 85, 75, and 50, resizing to 512 or 1024 pixels followed by JPEG compression, and PNG re-encoding.

为了进一步解释其行为，我们手动审查了 GPT-5.5 生成的解释，并识别了不同类别中重复出现的推理模式。 这些解释表明，除了视觉外观之外，GPT-5.5 还经常依赖于文本、语义和结构一致性线索。这可能部分解释了其在拟议基准测试中的强劲表现。为了说明 GPT-5.5 引用的提示类型，To further interpret its behavior, we manually reviewed the explanations generated by GPT-5.5 and identified recurring reasoning patterns across different categories. The explanations suggest that GPT-5.5 frequently relies on textual, semantic, and structural consistency cues in addition to visual appearance. This may partially explain its strong performance on the proposed benchmark. 这种行为在收据中尤其明显，其中 GPT-5.5 经常提到合成地址、邮政编码、电话号码、产品描述和算术关系之间的不一致。这些信息为交叉检查提供了明确的机会，这可以解释该模型在这一类别中近乎完美的表现。 在商业海报和信息图表中也可以观察到类似的模式。尽管它们的视觉多样性和灵活的布局，这些图像仍然包含丰富的文本和语义信息，可以检查其合理性和内部一致性。在这些类别中，GPT-5.5 经常引用本地文本扭曲、格式错误的设计元素、不一致的数字信息以及内容级别的不一致。 一致性检查的作用在学术海报中显得更加明显。与收据或海报不同，学术海报结合了多种信息源，包括技术文本、图表、方程式、表格、作者信息和机构关系。 GPT-5.5 通常指的是乱码文本、捏造的学术细节、格式错误的表格、扭曲的标签和类似占位符的联系信息。丰富的交叉引用机会可以为真实性评估提供额外的证据。 然而，当这种一致性线索受到限制时，这种优势就变得不那么明显。这一挑战在 UI 屏幕截图中最为明显，现代图像生成模型通常可以生成具有连贯布局和可读文本的视觉上令人信服的界面，从而减少可利用的明显不一致之处。 统计表也存在类似的困难。 GPT-5.5 有时会通过样本大小、置信区间和汇总统计数据之间的不一致来识别生成的样本。然而，当生成的表格包含真实的布局和数字上合理的值时，模型经常将它们分类为真实的，从而导致大量的漏报。This behavior is particularly evident in receipts, where GPT-5.5 frequently referenced inconsistencies among synthesized addresses, ZIP codes, phone numbers, product descriptions, and arithmetic relationships. Such information provides explicit opportunities for cross-checking, which may explain the model’s near-perfect performance in this category. A similar pattern can also be observed in commercial posters and infographics. Despite their visual diversity and flexible layouts, these images still contain abundant textual and semantic information that can be examined for plausibility and internal consistency. In these categories, GPT-5.5 often cited local text distortions, malformed design elements, inconsistent numerical information, and content-level inconsistencies. The role of consistency checking appears even more pronounced in academic posters. Unlike receipts or posters, academic posters combine multiple information sources, including technical text, figures, equations, tables, author information, and institutional affiliations. GPT-5.5 commonly referred to garbled text, fabricated academic details, malformed tables, distorted labels, and placeholder-like contact information. The availability of rich cross-referencing opportunities may provide additional evidence for authenticity assessment. However, this advantage becomes less apparent when such consistency cues are limited. This challenge is most visible in UI screenshots, where modern image generation models can often produce visually convincing interfaces with coherent layouts and readable text, leaving fewer obvious inconsistencies to exploit. Statistical tables present a similar difficulty. GPT-5.5 occasionally identified generated samples through inconsistencies among sample sizes, confidence intervals, and aggregated statistics. However, when generated tables contained realistic layouts and numerically plausible values, the model frequently classified them as authentic, resulting in a large number of false negatives.

总的来说，这些观察结果表明 GPT-5.5 受益于文本丰富的图像中包含的丰富文本、语义和结构信息。当多种模式中出现生成错误时，它们会提供额外的真实性线索，可以通过多模式推理来利用。然而，这些发现来自模型生成的解释，而不是其内部决策过程，并且这些解释在多大程度上忠实地反映了实际的推理机制仍然是一个需要进一步研究的悬而未决的问题。Overall, these observations suggest that GPT-5.5 benefits from the abundance of textual, semantic, and structural information contained in text-rich images. When generation errors emerge across multiple modalities, they provide additional authenticity cues that can be exploited through multimodal reasoning. However, these findings are derived from the model’s generated explanations rather than its internal decision-making process, and the extent to which the explanations faithfully reflect the actual reasoning mechanism remains an open question requiring further investigation.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了一个全面的多领域基准测试，包含 6 个不同类别的 8,602 张图像，以解决检测由 GPT-Image-2 等现代多模态生成器合成的全图像、人工智能生成的丰富文本内容的关键挑战。我们广泛的零样本评估表明，当前最先进的取证探测器存在严重的域依赖性，在商业海报等流体成分上表现良好，但在表格和收据等高度结构化的文档格式上性能急剧下降。此外，我们证明基于像素和基于工件的分类器在不同类别中表现出不同的故障模式，并且缺乏后处理鲁棒性，在标准平台端有损 JPEG 压缩下遭受灾难性的性能下降。探索性测试表明，通用多模态视觉语言模型提供了一种有前途的替代方案，而具有复杂空间布局和精确文本对齐的图像仍然是传统和多模态范式的共同瓶颈。通过建立这一基准，我们强调未来取证研究迫切需要转向文本感知检测策略，以在日益复杂的文本图像环境中保护内容的真实性。In this paper, we presented a comprehensive, multi-domain benchmark containing 8,602 images across six distinct categories to address the critical challenge of detecting full-image, AI-generated text-rich content synthesized by modern multimodal generators such as GPT-Image-2. Our extensive zero-shot evaluations reveals that current state-of-the-art forensic detectors suffer from severe domain dependencies, performing well on fluid compositions like commercial posters but degrading sharply on highly structured document formats like tables and receipts. Furthermore, we demonstrate that pixel- and artifact-based classifiers exhibit distinct failure modes across categories and lack post-processing robustness, suffering catastrophic performance drops under standard platform-side lossy JPEG compression. exploratory testing reveals that general-purpose multimodal vision-language models provide a promising alternative, while images with complex spatial layouts and precise text alignments remain a shared bottleneck for both traditional and multimodal paradigms. By establishing this benchmark, we highlight the urgent need for future forensics research to shift toward text-aware detection strategies to safeguard content authenticity in increasingly sophisticated text-image environments.
