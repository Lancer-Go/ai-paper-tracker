抽象的战争：科学传播中军事化语言的兴起及其后果War in the Abstract: The Rise and Consequences of Militarized Language in Scientific Communication

科学家们并不发动战争，但他们却开始像发动战争一样写作。他们谈到针对癌症和痴呆症的“战争”，谈到在体内“巡逻”寻找“入侵者”的免疫细胞，谈到对抗肿瘤的“灵丹妙药”，以及宿主和病原体之间的“军备竞赛”。直观上，战争隐喻可能会持续存在于科学写作中，因为作者将其视为有效的修辞工具Scientists do not wage war, yet they have come to write as if they did. They speak of “wars” on cancer and dementia, of immune cells that “patrol” the body for “invaders,” of “magic bullets” against tumours, and of “arms races” between hosts and pathogens. Intuitively, war metaphors may persist in scientific writing because authors perceive them as effective rhetorical instruments53。他们可以使抽象目标具体化. They can make abstract targets concrete34; 35、发出紧迫性和利害关系信号, signal urgency and stakes18，并且历史上一直被部署来围绕科学优先事项动员公众关注和政策支持, and have been deployed historically to mobilize public attention and policy support around scientific priorities45; 55。尽管人们认为军国主义语言具有说服力，但越来越多的证据表明，使用战争隐喻可能会损害信息的有效性。桑塔格在 1978 年的评论中. Despite perceptions that militaristic language will be persuasive, there is increasing evidence that the use of war metaphors may undermine message effectiveness. In her 1978 critique, Sontag51认为将癌症视为需要“战斗”的敌人，将患者视为“战士”，这给病人带来了道德负担，使那些未能康复的人蒙上了耻辱。随后的实验工作支持了桑塔格的担忧：癌症的战争框架减少了对预防的参与argued that the framing of cancer as an enemy to be ‘fought’ and patients as ‘fighters’ imposes a moral burden on the ill, stigmatizing those who fail to recover. Subsequent experimental work has supported Sontag’s concerns: war framings of cancer reduce engagement with prevention26并增加患者的负罪感and increase patient guilt27。在其他领域也观察到了战争框架的平行影响：在犯罪方面，它们将政策偏好转向惩罚性执法. Parallel effects of war framings have been observed across other domains: in crime, they shift policy preferences toward punitive enforcement55;在气候变化方面，他们将公共话语缩小到一个二元的输赢框架; in climate change, they narrow public discourse to a binary win-or-lose frame18;在应对大流行病时，他们鼓励对外群体的指责和污名化; and in pandemic response, they encourage blame and stigmatization of outgroups60; 50。尽管战争框架在科学工作中产生了意想不到的影响，但先前的学术研究已经记录了免疫学中持续存在的战争隐喻. Despite the unintended effects of war framings in scientific work, prior scholarship has documented persistent war metaphors in immunology39, 肿瘤学, oncology45和微生物学and microbiology43。这些先前的叙述都没有衡量战争语言在更广泛的科学语料库中的轨迹、最近武装冲突的升级或 2022 年后大型语言模型的出现。鉴于越来越多的理由相信使用与战争相关的语言会降低可信度，现在是检验科学写作是否进一步倾向于这种词汇还是在数十年的批评中保持稳定的重要时刻。. None of these prior accounts measures the trajectory of war language across the broader scientific corpus, the recent escalations of armed conflict, or the post-2022 emergence of large language models. Given increasing reason to believe that the use of war-related language will reduce credibility, it is an important moment to examine whether scientific writing has tilted further toward this vocabulary or held steady against decades of critique remains unmeasured29; 42.

战争隐喻在科学作品中的盛行是很重要的，因为隐喻不仅作用于文本，还作用于读者The prevalence of war metaphors in scientific work is consequential because metaphor acts on the reader, not just on the text35。隐喻采用熟悉的源领域（例如战争）并将其结构投射到抽象目标（例如疾病）上. Metaphors take a familiar source domain (such as warfare) and project its structure onto an abstract target (such as a disease)34; 23，并且源域的选择会在目标推理中产生可测量的变化, and the choice of source domain produces measurable shifts in reasoning about the target7。这些转变很大程度上是在读者意识之外发生的. These shifts operate largely outside the reader’s awareness4：实验参与者将他们的结论归因于潜在的证据而不是隐喻: experimental participants attribute their conclusions to underlying evidence rather than to the metaphor55。即使框架被明确识别，这些框架效应仍然存在，使得仅通过警告来消除读者偏见的尝试变得复杂化. These framing effects persist even when the frame is explicitly identified, complicating attempts to debias readers through warnings alone11。在所有实验文献中，隐喻对态度和信念产生一致的说服作用，元分析证据支持该机制的普遍性. Across the experimental literature, metaphor produces consistent persuasive effects on attitudes and beliefs, with meta-analytic evidence supporting the generality of the mechanism53。隐喻的效果不需要刻意的修辞使用；融入日常词汇的传统隐喻与深思熟虑的隐喻一样有效地形成解释. Metaphor’s effects do not require deliberate rhetorical use; conventional metaphors woven into routine vocabulary shape interpretation as effectively as deliberate ones54。尤其是战争隐喻，具有与其他来源领域不同的认知特征。它们主要体现竞争、紧迫性以及胜利或失败的二元结果框架. War metaphors, in particular, carry a cognitive profile distinct from other source domains. They prime competition, urgency, and a binary frame of outcomes as wins or losses18。它们提高了演讲者的感知能力，同时降低了感知的热情. They elevate the perceived competence of a speaker while depressing the perceived warmth17。在非专业观众研究中，感知的温暖是专家来源的信息是否被接受为合法的最有力的预测因素之一. In lay-audience research, perceived warmth is among the strongest predictors of whether information from an expert source is accepted as legitimate28。这些个体实验的影响是有限的。然而，综合数以百万计的科学文本，外行推理的累积后果是巨大的。. These individual-experiment effects are modest; yet, compounded across millions of scientific texts, the cumulative consequences for lay reasoning are substantial.

2010 年代和 2020 年代的三个趋同转变与科学战争框架的轨迹相交叉，使得任何崛起的时机都成为分析的中心。一、2014年以来全球武装冲突升级Three convergent shifts in the 2010s and 2020s intersect with the trajectory of war framing in science, making the timing of any rise analytically central. First, armed conflict has escalated globally since 201446，提高了公共话语中字面战争的重要性。其次，在公共卫生和社交媒体讨论中，COVID-19 大流行被广泛定义为一场战争, raising the importance of literal warfare in public discourse. Second, the COVID-19 pandemic was widely framed as a war in both public-health and social-media discourse60; 50，随之而来的是公众对科学机构的信任度明显下降，这增加了框架的风险, and has been followed by a measurable decline in public trust in scientific institutions that raises the stakes of framing29; 42。三、2022年后大语言模型的出现. Third, the post-2022 emergence of large language models6已经开始改变科学写作的词汇范围has begun to alter the lexical register of scientific writing36; 31; 22。这些驱动因素在不同层面上发挥作用：冲突和流行病框架改变了科学家工作的话语环境，而法学硕士则改变了写作本身的表面形式。无论科学家是故意引入战争框架还是从周围的话语中吸收它，都会提出一个流行率测量无法完全解决的归因问题。尽管如此，每个驱动程序都具有时序分析可以利用的独特时间签名。. These drivers operate at different levels: conflict and pandemic framing shift the discursive environment in which scientists work, while LLMs shift the surface form of the writing itself. Whether scientists deliberately import the war frame or absorb it from the surrounding discourse raises an attribution question that prevalence measurement cannot fully resolve. Each driver nonetheless has a distinct temporal signature that timing analysis can exploit.

之前的工作还没有在语料库规模上测量科学中战争词汇的轨迹。计算科学的科学文献计量传统No prior work has measured the trajectory of war vocabulary in science at corpus scale. The bibliometric tradition of computational science-of-science19; 37跟踪主题漂移、引用模式和作者动态，但很少跟踪科学写作的修辞语域。最近的人口规模词汇工作主要集中在法学硕士标记词汇上tracks topical drift, citation patterns and authorship dynamics, but rarely the rhetorical register of scientific writing. 通过单词边界正则表达式匹配，对清理后的标题和摘要分别检查每一层。每个基本术语都会自动扩展以接受规则的尾随复数（因此武器与武器和武器都匹配），并且明确的不规则复数形式（例如军队、敌人、伤亡、战略）被单独列出，因此它们在没有语法变形的情况下进行匹配。将每篇论文的标题和摘要计数相加，得出每层的每篇论文计数。Each tier was checked separately on the cleaned title and abstract via word-boundary regex matching. Each base term was automatically expanded to admit a regular trailing plural (so weapon matches both weapon and weapons), and explicit irregular plural forms (e.g., armies, enemies, casualties, strategies) were listed separately so they were matched without grammatical inflection. The per-paper title and abstract counts were summed to give a per-paper count for each tier.

特定层级的流行度是指包含该层级中至少一个术语的论文的百分比；我们使用二进制每篇论文存在而不是每篇论文计数，这样一篇包含五个二级术语的论文与一篇只有一个二级术语的论文对流行率的贡献相同，从而防止术语密集的评论主导该指标。本文报告的总体患病率是三层特定患病率的总和。The tier-specific prevalence is the percentage of papers containing at least one term from that tier; we use binary per-paper presence rather than per-paper count so that a paper with five Tier-2 terms contributes the same to prevalence as a paper with one, preventing term-dense reviews from dominating the metric. The overall prevalence reported throughout this paper is the sum of the three tier-specific prevalences.
