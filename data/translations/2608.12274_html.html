摘要Abstract

背景：Background:在 3D 自由呼吸、非造影 CT 中准确分割左前降支 (LAD) 动脉对于胸部放射治疗中节省心脏剂量至关重要。这项任务本身就很困难，因为 LAD 非常小，软组织对比度差，并且患者之间差异很大。即使是手动轮廓也显示观察者间在非造影 CT 上的一致性有限，这凸显了血管边界的模糊性。Accurate segmentation of the Left Anterior Descending (LAD) artery in 3D free-breathing, non-contrast CT is critical for cardiac dose sparing in thoracic radiotherapy. The task is inherently difficult because the LAD is extremely small, exhibits poor soft-tissue contrast, and varies substantially across patients. Even manual contours show limited inter-observer agreement on non-contrast CT, underscoring the ambiguity of the vessel boundaries.

目的：Purpose:开发基于变压器的分割框架，使用局部-全局上下文建模和不确定性引导优化来增强低对比度、不平衡 CT 中的 LAD 描绘。To develop a transformer-based segmentation framework that enhances LAD delineation in low-contrast, imbalanced CT using local-global context modeling and uncertainty-guided optimization.

方法：Methods:我们建议We proposeNA-UNETR，一种基于 3D 变压器的分割模型，具有邻域注意力 (NA) 和扩张 NA (DiNA) 块，可共同捕获精细结构细节和远程上下文。为了解决带注释的 LAD 动脉数据可用性有限的问题，该模型在代表一般冠状动脉解剖结构的 1,000 个 CTA 体积上进行了预训练，然后使用参数有效的适应策略对 20 个机构的 LAD 动脉自由呼吸 CT 扫描进行微调。复合 Dice-Focal 和 Hausdorff 损失通过同方差不确定性进行动态平衡，提高了重叠和边界精度。预处理包括强度剪切、对比度增强和以动脉为中心的采样，以及后处理精细形态。, a 3D transformer-based segmentation model with Neighborhood Attention (NA) and Dilated NA (DiNA) blocks that jointly capture fine structural detail and long-range context. To address the limited availability of annotated LAD artery data, the model is pretrained on 1,000 CTA volumes representing general coronary anatomy and then fine-tuned using a parameter-efficient adaptation strategy on 20 free-breathing institutional CT scans of the LAD artery. A composite Diceâ€“Focal and Hausdorff loss, dynamically balanced via homoscedastic uncertainty, improves overlap and boundary accuracy. Preprocessing included intensity clipping, contrast enhancement, and artery-centric sampling, and postprocessing refined morphology.

结果：Results:NA-UNETR 实现了与 SOTA 相当的性能，并提高了效率和边界特性。它达到了 45.64% Dice、38.16 mm HD95 和 10.01 mm ASD，比 nnU-Net 提高了 Dice 3.10 个百分点，相对于 Swin UNETR 降低了 HD95 2.96 mm，同时表现出所有模型中最强的边界精度，并表现出改进的几何和中心线稳定性。在 ImageCAS 上，它实现了 79.49% Dice、8.89 mm HD95 和 1.02 mm ASD。 Ablations 证实，残差块、可变内核和不确定性加权损失都有助于性能稳定性和边界精度。NA-UNETR achieved comparable performance to SOTA with improved efficiency and boundary properties. It reached 45.64% Dice, 38.16 mm HD95, and 10.01 mm ASD, improving Dice by 3.10 percentage points over nnU-Net and reducing HD95 by 2.96 mm relative to Swin UNETR, while showing the strongest boundary accuracy among all models and demonstrating improved geometric and centerline stability. On ImageCAS, it achieved 79.49% Dice, 8.89 mm HD95, and 1.02 mm ASD. Ablations confirmed that residual blocks, variable kernels, and uncertainty-weighted loss each contributed to performance stability and boundary precision.

结论：Conclusions:NA-UNETR 有效地平衡了局部精度和全局上下文，从而能够准确分割薄的、低对比度的 LAD 结构。它集成了邻域注意力、不确定性加权损失和基于 LoRA 的微调，为放射治疗计划中的子结构级心脏分割提供了一个强大且计算高效的框架。NA-UNETR effectively balances local precision and global context, enabling accurate segmentation of thin, low-contrast LAD structures. Its integration of Neighborhood Attention, uncertainty-weighted loss, and LoRA-based fine-tuning demonstrates a robust and computationally efficient framework for substructure-level cardiac segmentation in radiotherapy planning.

II. 方法Methods

我们的任务被表述为 3D 二元分割问题，其中模型根据输入 CT 体积预测 LAD 动脉的体素概率。Our task is formulated as a 3D binary segmentation problem, where the model predicts voxel-wise probabilities of the LAD artery from input CT volumes.

V. 讨论Discussion

在非造影 CT 上准确描绘 LAD 很困难，因为血管经常与邻近结构混合，降低了其边界的可见度。先前的研究表明，非造影 CT 上的手动 LAD 注释表现出显着的观察者间差异，报告的 Dice 值范围为 0.10 至 0.53Accurately delineating the LAD on non-contrast CT is difficult because the vessel often blends with adjacent structures, reducing the visibility of its boundaries. Prior studies have shown that manual LAD annotations on non-contrast CT exhibit substantial inter-observer variability, with reported Dice values ranging from 0.10 to 0.5319, 20, 21, 17, 18，自动化方法通常达到 0.09–0.30，通常需要手动校正, and automated methods typically achieving 0.09–0.30, often requiring manual correction19, 20, 21, 17。在这种情况下，NA-UNETR 在 LAD-SEG 上实现了 45.6% 的平均 DSC，这与之前的报告和本研究中评估的基线相比是有利的。定制的预处理和训练管道提高了测试控制设置的性能。在这个共同的管道下，NA-UNETR 实现了最高的整体准确度，这与其通过 Neighborhood Attention 整合本地和全球背景所带来的好处一致。. In this setting, NA-UNETR achieved a mean DSC of 45.6% on LAD-SEG, which compares favorably to prior reports and to the baselines evaluated in this study. The tailored preprocessing and training pipeline improved performance in the tested control settings. Under this common pipeline, NA-UNETR achieved the highest overall accuracy, consistent with a benefit from its integration of local and global context through Neighborhood Attention.

CTA 上的预训练提供了冠状动脉树的清晰可视化，并允许编码器学习稳定的血管特征，为后续微调奠定了有用的基础。尽管 CTA 与非造影 CT 不同，但解码器和适应 LoRA 的 MLP 层有助于减轻模态转变，并使模型能够在适应目标域的同时传输结构信息。基于 Transformer 的模型受益于对 MLP 编码器层与解码器的微调，能够更好地适应非造影 CT，并产生比基于 CNN 的基线更高的 DSCPretraining on CTA provides a clear visualization of the coronary tree and allows the encoder to learn stable vessel features that form a useful foundation for subsequent fine-tuning. Although CTA differs from non-contrast CT, the decoder and the LoRA-adapted MLP layers help mitigate the modality shift and enable the model to transfer structural information while adapting to the target domain. Transformer-based models benefited from fine-tuning the MLP encoder layers together with the decoder, enabling better adaptation to non-contrast CT and yielding higher DSC than the CNN-based baselinesTable 1，而 clDice 受到的影响较小，因为无需额外的编码器微调即可保持拓扑连续性。未来的工作将纳入明确的领域适应或模态对齐策略，以进一步缩小 CTA 与非造影 CT 领域的差距。, while clDice was less influenced since topological continuity can be maintained without additional encoder fine-tuning. Future work will incorporate explicit domain-adaptation or modality-alignment strategies to further reduce the CTA–non-contrast CT domain gap.

NA-UNETR 在我们的 LAD-SEG 数据集上超越了基线，并在直接根据该基准进行训练时在公共动脉数据集上实现了有竞争力的性能，从而表现出了良好的泛化能力。在这两个数据集中，NA-UNETR 除了更高的基于 Dice 的性能（DSC、clDice）之外，还实现了更强的基于边界的指标（HD95、ASD）。尽管由于船舶口径小且航线细长，边界精度对于 LAD 分割尤为重要，但之前以 LAD 为重点的研究NA-UNETR showed favorable generalization by outperforming baselines on our LAD-SEG dataset and achieving competitive performance on the public artery dataset when trained directly on that benchmark. Across both datasets, NA-UNETR achieved stronger boundary-based metrics (HD95, ASD) in addition to higher Dice-based performance (DSC, clDice). Although boundary accuracy is particularly important for LAD segmentation due to the vessel’s small caliber and thin, elongated course, prior LAD-focused studies19, 20, 21, 17没有报告基于边界的指标，限制了他们对轮廓质量和拓扑连续性的评估。did not report boundary-based metrics, limiting their assessment of contour quality and topological continuity.

尽管取得了这些进展，基于边界的指标在 LAD-SEG 上仍然具有挑战性。细血管口径和弱边界通常会产生小的、碎片化的预测，即使准确捕获中心线，也会使 HD95 和 ASD 膨胀，所有模型中的模式一致，反映了 LAD 在小型非对比 CT 数据集上的内在可见性限制。 NA-UNETR 在 ImageCAS 上实现了更好的 HD95 和 ASD，其中 CCTA 图像提供了更清晰的血管清晰度，突出了边界精度对成像模式的强烈依赖性。在 LAD-SEG 上，鉴于报告的观察者间 Dice 分数范围为 0.10 到 0.53Despite these gains, boundary-based metrics remain challenging on LAD-SEG. The thin vessel caliber and weak boundaries often produce small, fragmented predictions that inflate HD95 and ASD even when the centerline is captured accurately, a pattern consistent across all models reflecting the intrinsic visibility limits of the LAD on a small non-contrast CT dataset. NA-UNETR achieved substantially better HD95 and ASD on ImageCAS, where the CCTA images provide clearer vessel definition, highlighting the strong dependence of boundary accuracy on imaging modality. On LAD-SEG, given that reported inter-observer Dice scores range from 0.10 to 0.5318，所实现的 DSC 取决于专家的可变性，这表明在减少手动编辑工作和提高小型细长血管结构的一致性方面具有潜在的用途。需要进行前瞻性工作流程研究来评估时间节省、编辑负担和用户接受度，以进一步确定其临床影响。, the achieved DSC lies within expert variability, suggesting potential utility in reducing manual editing effort and improving consistency for small, elongated vascular structures. Prospective workflow studies evaluating time savings, edit burden, and user acceptance will be required to further establish its clinical impact.

目视检查Visual inspection inFigure 5和andFigure 6结果表明，当血管缩小到只有几个体素或局部对比度非常弱时，就会出现大多数错误，从而导致预测中出现小间隙或较小的边界偏移。尽管 NA-UNETR 在多个指标上显示出相对于基线方法的改进，但非造影 CT 上的 LAD 分割仍然从根本上受到解剖结构可见性的限制，并且模型性能最终受到底层图像中可用信息的限制。这些结果代表着向前迈出了一步，但该方法尚不适合临床部署。进一步细化、更大的注释数据集以及跨多个机构的验证对于建立常规临床使用所需的可靠性和稳健性至关重要。showed that most errors arose when the vessel narrowed to only a few voxels or when local contrast was very weak, leading to small gaps or minor boundary offsets in the predictions. Although NA-UNETR shows improvements over baseline methods across multiple metrics, LAD segmentation on non-contrast CT remains fundamentally limited by the visibility of the anatomy, and model performance is ultimately bounded by the information available in the underlying images. These results represent a step forward, but the approach is not yet suitable for clinical deployment. Further refinement, larger annotated datasets, and validation across multiple institutions will be essential to establish the reliability and robustness required for routine clinical use.

VI. 结论Conclusion

LAD 动脉口径小、软组织对比度差以及注释数据有限，使其成为非造影 CT 自动分割最具挑战性的目标之一。这项工作通过邻域关注来解决这些困难，以进行几何连贯的局部全局上下文建模，并通过不确定性引导的损失平衡来实现精确的边界描绘。通过对更广泛的冠状动脉数据集进行预训练，然后进行参数高效的微调，进一步增强了小数据体系的通用性。这些贡献共同推进了自动 LAD 勾画的可行性，并为其整合到胸部放射治疗计划中的心脏剂量评估工作流程奠定了基础。The LAD artery’s small caliber, poor soft-tissue contrast, and limited annotated data make it one of the most challenging targets for automated segmentation in non-contrast CT. This work addresses these difficulties through neighborhood attention for geometrically coherent local-global context modeling and uncertainty-guided loss balancing for precise boundary delineation. Generalizability to the small-data regime is further strengthened through pretraining on a broader coronary dataset followed by parameter-efficient fine-tuning. Together, these contributions advance the feasibility of automated LAD delineation and lay groundwork for its integration into cardiac dose assessment workflows in thoracic radiotherapy planning.
