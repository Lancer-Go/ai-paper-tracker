UltraX：大规模细化预训练数据UltraX: Refining Pre-Training Data at Scale with
自适应程序化编辑Adaptive Programmatic Editing

Jie Zhou2, Qiang Ma3, Xuanhe Zhou4, Xu Han3, Yudong Wang3, Zhiyuan Liu322footnotemark: 2

摘要Abstract

随着可用的训练数据接近其物理极限，缩放定律带来的性能增益开始减弱。因此，进一步提升大型语言模型（LLM）性能的关键已经从单纯的数据扩展转向提高数据利用效率，通过提高数据质量来更好地挖掘现有数据的潜在潜力。 然而，在大规模语料库的背景下，现有的细化方法在质量、效率和可靠性方面面临着重大限制：基于规则的方法受到固定启发式的限制，并且难以应对实例级的变化；基于LLM的方法提高了质量，但无法满足大规模数据处理的效率和可靠性要求。 为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了UltraX，一种针对大规模预训练数据的函数调用细化框架，通过在删除和修改之外引入插入来完善编辑函数空间，从而实现细粒度的实例级编辑。 具体来说，UltraX 构建了可靠的程序监督生成管道。在此管道中，数据集自适应提示优化首先引导专业法学硕士生成高质量的端到端精炼文本，然后行对齐映射和动态上下文替换将原始精炼文本对转换为结构化程序监督。 同时，UltraX通过操作组合的低置信度示例过滤和比率控制采样提高了监督质量并稳定了训练分布。在推理和执行过程中，通过滑动窗口预测、全局运算聚合和系统后处理对模型输出进行归一化和验证，提高大规模执行的稳定性和可靠性。 在多个语料库上从头开始预训练的 1B 模型的实验表明，UltraX 在所有语料库中实现了最高的平均性能，在多个数据集上的相对改进超过 2%。 UltraX 还可以用更少的训练令牌匹配或超越基线，展示出更强的数据效率和细化可靠性。As available training data approaches its physical limit, the performance gains derived from Scaling Laws have begun to diminish. Consequently, the key to further enhancing the performance of Large Language Models (LLMs) has shifted from mere data expansion to improving data utilization efficiency, by enhancing data quality to better exploit the latent potential of existing data. However, in the context of large-scale corpora, existing refinement methodologies face significant limitations in quality, efficiency, and reliability: Rule-based approaches are constrained by fixed heuristics and struggle with instance-level variations; LLM-based approaches improve quality but fail to meet the efficiency and reliability requirements of large-scale data processing. To address these challenges, we propose UltraX, a function-calling refinement framework for large-scale pre-training data that completes the editing function space by introducing insertion in addition to deletion and modification, enabling fine-grained instance-level editing. Specifically, UltraX builds a reliable program-supervision generation pipeline. In this pipeline, dataset-adaptive prompt optimization first guides an expert LLM to produce high-quality end-to-end refined texts, and Line Alignment Mapping and Dynamic Context Replacement then convert original-refined text pairs into structured program supervision. Meanwhile, UltraX improves supervision quality and stabilizes the training distribution with low-confidence example filtering and ratio-controlled sampling by operation combination. During inference and execution, it normalizes and validates model outputs through sliding-window prediction, global operation aggregation, and systematic post-processing, improving the stability and reliability of large-scale execution. Experiments with 1B models pretrained from scratch on multiple corpora show that UltraX achieves the highest average performance across all corpora, with relative improvements exceeding 2% on multiple datasets. UltraX also matches or surpasses baselines with fewer training tokens, demonstrating stronger data efficiency and refinement reliability.

1 简介Introduction

缩放定律Scaling laws(Kaplan et al., 2020)证明大型语言模型 (LLM) 的性能demonstrate that while the performance of Large Language Models (LLMs)(Achiam et al., 2023; Comanici et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2024; Team et al., 2025; Qwen Team, 2026; GLM-5-Team, 2026; DeepSeek-AI, 2026)随着模型参数和训练数据的扩展，这种增长潜力不断提高，但随着可用训练数据接近其物理极限，这种增长潜力已经开始达到收益递减点。 因此，进一步提高LLM绩效的重点应从单纯的数据扩展转向训练数据质量的系统改进continues to improve with the expansion of model parameters and training data, this growth potential has begun to reach a point of diminishing returns as available training data approaches its physical limit. Consequently, the focus for further enhancing LLM performance should shift from mere data scaling to the systematic improvement of training data quality(Wang et al., 2026).

在大规模语料库中，现有的数据质量改进方法主要分为基于规则的过滤和清洗In large-scale corpora, existing data quality improvement methods mainly fall into rule-based filtering and cleaning(Raffel et al., 2020; Penedo et al., 2024; Weber et al., 2025; Rae et al., 2021)，以及基于模型的选择, and model-based selection(Wenzek et al., 2020; Wang et al., 2025)和细化and refinement(Gunasekar et al., 2023b; Wettig et al., 2024)。基于规则的方法依赖于手动设计的启发式方法并且计算效率高，使其广泛应用于语料库构建中。然而，它们的固定覆盖范围和对专家调优的依赖限制了它们处理实例级变化的能力. Rule-based methods rely on manually designed heuristics and are computationally efficient, making them widely used in corpus construction. However, their fixed coverage and dependence on expert tuning limit their ability to handle instance-level variation(Zhang et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2024)。基于模型的方法可以利用学习到的质量信号进行细粒度的数据选择，或者通过细化直接修改低质量文本，但轻量级模型通常仅捕获表面级别的统计数据，并且很难可靠地评估或修复更深层次的语义质量问题。 最近的工作进一步利用法学硕士具有更强的理解和生成能力来进行语义过滤或端到端文本细化. Model-based methods can leverage learned quality signals for fine-grained data selection or directly revise low-quality texts through refinement, but lightweight models often capture only surface-level statistics and struggle to reliably assess or repair deeper semantic quality issues. Recent work further leverages LLMs with stronger understanding and generation capabilities for semantic filtering or end-to-end text refinement(Yu et al., 2024; Dubey et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023; Ben Allal et al., 2024)。然而，这些方法通常会产生大量的计算成本和低吞吐量，使得它们难以扩展到预训练语料库。 总体而言，现有方法很难同时满足语义级细化质量和预训练规模处理效率的要求。这种紧张凸显了一个关键挑战：如何保留模型驱动细化的好处，同时可靠地将数据细化扩展到大型预训练语料库。. However, these methods usually incur substantial computational cost and low throughput, making them difficult to scale to pre-training corpora. Overall, existing methods struggle to simultaneously satisfy the demands of semantic-level refinement quality and pre-training-scale processing efficiency. This tension highlights a key challenge: how to retain the benefits of model-driven refinement while reliably scaling data refinement to large pre-training corpora.

我们观察到，虽然法学硕士和轻量级语言模型在一般能力上存在性能差距，但当任务被表述为结构化和特定于任务的预测问题时，轻量级模型仍然可以以低得多的推理成本提供足够的细化能力。基于这一观察，我们探索使用轻量级模型作为大规模数据处理的高效精炼器。 至关重要的是，我们没有采用端到端（E2E）文本生成方法，而是通过预定义的函数调用来实现细化。这种选择出于两个主要考虑因素：首先，E2E 生成通常需要以与输入成比例的规模生成文本，这会带来较高的推理开销并降低吞吐量。其次，在长上下文场景中，文本不可避免地要被分割和重组；然而，E2E一代很难精确地描绘分割点和重组边界，这经常引入语义碎片和结构不一致We observe that while a performance gap exists between LLMs and lightweight language models in general capabilities, lightweight models can still provide sufficient refinement capability at much lower inference cost when the task is formulated as a structured and task-specific prediction problem. Based on this observation, we explore using lightweight models as efficient refiners for large-scale data processing. Crucially, rather than adopting an end-to-end (E2E) text generation method, we implement refinement through predefined function calls. This choice is motivated by two primary considerations: First, E2E generation typically necessitates the production of text at a scale proportional to the input, which entails high inference overhead and reduced throughput. Second, in long-context scenarios, text must inevitably be segmented and reassembled; however, E2E generation struggles to precisely delineate segmentation points and recombination boundaries, which frequently introduces semantic fragmentation and structural inconsistencies(Liu et al., 2024; Maini et al., 2024)。 相比之下，基于函数调用的方法仅输出结构化操作序列，从而显着最小化输出令牌计数。此外，该方法自然地兼容分段处理和结果聚合，确保在处理长格式文档时具有更好的一致性。 普罗克斯. In contrast, methods based on function calls output only structured operation sequences, significantly minimizing the output token count. Furthermore, this method is naturally compatible with segment-wise processing and result aggregation, ensuring better coherence when handling long-form documents. ProX(Zhou et al., 2024)和RefineXand RefineX(Bi et al., 2025a)是函数调用数据细化的代表性方法。两者都通过函数调用来利用轻量级模型来实现数据细化。然而，ProX 和 RefineX 表现出一些关键的局限性：它们的功能空间不完整，无法完全模拟插入、删除和修改之间的协同作用；他们的种子监督要么依赖于法学硕士直接生成的程序，要么受到仅删除编辑规则的限制，从而削弱了可靠性；它们的执行过程仍然存在重复字符串匹配、连续操作之间的干扰以及跨窗口建模不足的问题。 因此，将函数调用细化扩展到大规模预训练语料库需要更完整的函数空间、更可靠的监督构建管道和更健壮的程序执行机制。are representative methods for function-calling data refinement. Both leverage lightweight models through function calls to achieve data refinement. However, ProX and RefineX exhibit several critical limitations: their function spaces are incomplete and cannot fully model the synergy among insertion, deletion, and modification; their seed supervision either depends on programs directly generated by LLMs or is constrained by deletion-only edit rules, weakening reliability; and their execution procedures still suffer from duplicate string matching, interference between consecutive operations, and insufficient cross-window modeling. Therefore, scaling function-calling refinement to large-scale pre-training corpora requires a more complete function space, a more reliable supervision construction pipeline, and a more robust program execution mechanism.

Experimental results show that UltraX achieves the highest average performance across all corpora, with relative improvements exceeding 2% on multiple datasets. Moreover, UltraX matches or surpasses baseline methods with fewer training tokens, demonstrating stronger data efficiency and reliability. Further analyses suggest that these gains come from its fine-grained refinement, which better balances noise removal and information preservation.

2 相关工作Related Work

基于规则的过滤和清理。Rule-Based Filtering and Cleaning.

基于规则的过滤方法主要在文档级别执行质量控制，通常依赖于专家设计的启发式方法，例如 URL 黑名单、语言识别、乱码字符比例和重复阈值来修剪低质量文档Rule-based filtering methodologies predominantly execute quality control at the document level, typically relying on expert-designed heuristics such as URL blacklisting, language identification, gibberish character ratios, and duplication thresholds to prune low-quality documents(Smith et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2024; Dou et al., 2024; Qiu et al., 2024)。由于其计算简约且易于实施，这些方法普遍用于构建大规模语料库。然而，此类规则通常缺乏对实例级细微差别进行建模的表达能力，经常导致对可能仍包含可挽救的高价值信息的整个文档的过分热心拒绝。. Due to their computational parsimony and ease of implementation, these methods are ubiquitously employed in the construction of massive corpora. However, such rules typically lack the expressive capacity to model instance-level nuances, frequently resulting in the over-zealous rejection of entire documents that may still contain salvageable, high-value information.

相比之下，基于规则的清理方法专注于文档内内容恢复。这些还依赖于手动启发式方法来执行本地修改，例如标准化不稳定的换行符、删除超链接、清除异常的 Base64 编码或删除缺少正确标点符号的段落In contrast, rule-based cleaning methodologies concentrate on intra-document content restoration. These also depend on manual heuristics to perform local modifications, such as normalizing erratic line breaks, removing hyperlinks, clearing anomalous Base64 encodings, or excising paragraphs lacking proper punctuation(Penedo et al., 2023; Rae et al., 2021; Penedo et al., 2024; Soldaini et al., 2024)。虽然这些操作部分减轻了文档级过滤遗漏的局部噪声，但它们的功效仍然受到固定规则集和人类直觉的内在局限性的限制，使得它们不足以捕获日益异构的噪声模式。与这些粗粒度方法不同，UltraX 超越了二进制文档选择。相反，它通过一整套插入、删除和修改操作促进文档内的细粒度细化，从而进一步提高性能。. While these operations partially mitigate local noise that document-level filtering misses, their efficacy remains constrained by the intrinsic limitations of fixed rule sets and human intuition, rendering them inadequate for capturing increasingly heterogeneous noise patterns. Distinguishing itself from these coarse-grained approaches, UltraX transcends binary document selection. Instead, it facilitates fine-grained refinement within the document through a comprehensive suite of insertion, deletion, and modification operations, thereby unlocking further performance gains.

基于模型的过滤和细化。Model-Based Filtering and Refinement.

随着对数据质量的要求不断提高，基于规则的方法已经不足以满足当前的要求。针对这一问题，基于模型的数据过滤策略近年来逐渐成为提高数据质量的有效方法。传统的质量过滤技术训练分类器来选择高质量样本With increasing demands for data quality, rule-based methods have become insufficient to meet current requirements. To address this issue, model-based data filtering strategies have gradually become an effective approach to improving data quality in recent years. Traditional quality filtering techniques train classifiers to select high-quality samples(Penedo et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2025)。此外，基于困惑度的数据过滤方法. In addition, data filtering methods based on perplexity(Muennighoff et al., 2024; Wenzek et al., 2020)，以及使用法学硕士通过提示评估数据质量的多个维度的策略, and strategies that use LLMs to evaluate multiple dimensions of data quality through prompts(Sachdeva et al., 2024; Wettig et al., 2024)，已介绍。然而，这些方法常常受到有限的鲁棒性、评估偏差和高计算成本的阻碍。至关重要的是，以过滤为中心的策略本质上侧重于是否保留或丢弃文档的二元决策，而不是增强部分有用但有噪音的文本的文档内质量。 相比之下，基于LLM的细化方法更注重对现有数据的直接编辑或重写，以系统地提高其质量, have been introduced. Nevertheless, these approaches are often hampered by limited robustness, evaluation bias, and high computational cost. Crucially, filtering-centric strategies inherently focus on the binary decision of whether to retain or discard a document, rather than enhancing the intra-document quality of texts that are partially useful yet noisy. In contrast, LLM-based refinement methodologies focus more on the direct editing or rewriting of existing data to systematically enhance its quality(Fan et al., 2024; Yue et al., 2024; Gunasekar et al., 2023a; Li et al., 2023)。然而，尽管它们很有效，但这些方法通常需要生成与原始输入相当的文本量，从而导致大量的计算开销，阻碍了它们对预训练数据集的可扩展性。此外，它们不可避免地继承了潜在生成模型的内在偏见和缺陷，并且仍然容易受到幻觉等持久问题的影响. However, despite their effectiveness, these methods generally necessitate the generation of text at a volume comparable to the original input, resulting in massive computational overhead that hinders their scalability to pre-training datasets. Furthermore, they inevitably inherit the intrinsic biases and flaws of the underlying generative models and remain susceptible to persistent issues such as hallucinations(Liu et al., 2024).

为了协调效率和可靠性之间的权衡，最近的研究引入了利用法学硕士的程序化细化框架，例如 ProXTo reconcile the trade-off between efficiency and reliability, recent studies have introduced programmatic refinement frameworks leveraging LLMs, such as ProX(Zhou et al., 2024)和RefineXand RefineX(Bi et al., 2025a)。与直接文本重写不同，这些方法要求模型生成可解释的编辑操作，然后将其应用于原始文本，从而促进更精细的质量控制。然而，此类方法的有效性在很大程度上取决于编辑程序的设计、种子数据构建的质量以及后处理流程的可靠性。现有方法在这些方面仍然表现出明显的局限性，这些挑战进一步激发了 UltraX 的设计。具体来说，在操作设计上，ProX仅支持替换和删除，而RefineX仅限于仅删除；两者都没有提供完整的编辑操作集，限制了模型恢复丢失语义的能力，并经常导致语义碎片。在种子数据构建方面，ProX依赖于LLM生成的细化程序，噪声覆盖范围有限，产生的种子数据质量不确定； RefineX通过最小编辑距离从端到端精炼文本中提取操作，但通过硬过滤所有非删除操作来实现其仅删除设计。这种粗略的硬过滤无法捕获插入、删除和修改之间的协同作用。例如，修复错误的片段通常需要联合删除原始片段并插入更连贯的表达，而排除插入和修改可能会导致语义崩溃。在执行方面，ProX 缺乏消除同一文档中重复子字符串歧义的机制，这可能会导致编辑操作应用到非预期的位置；此外，其非滑动窗口分块策略破坏了跨块边界的语义连续性。 RefineX 引入了执行时验证来解决其中一些问题，但它忽略了连续行内操作之间的相互干扰，这反过来又降低了细化过程的整体保真度。. Diverging from direct text rewriting, these methodologies task the model with generating interpretable editing operations that are subsequently applied to the original text, thereby facilitating more granular quality control. However, the effectiveness of such methods heavily depends on the design of editing programs, the quality of seed data construction, and the reliability of post-processing pipelines. Existing approaches still exhibit notable limitations in these aspects, and these challenges further motivate the design of UltraX. Specifically, in terms of operation design, ProX supports only replacement and deletion, while RefineX is restricted to deletion alone; neither provides a complete set of editing operations, limiting the model’s ability to restore missing semantics and frequently causing semantic fragmentation. Regarding seed data construction, ProX relies on LLM-generated refinement programs with limited noise coverage, yielding seed data of uncertain quality; RefineX extracts operations from E2E refined text via minimum edit distance, but achieves its deletion-only design by hard-filtering all non-deletion operations. Such coarse hard-filtering fails to capture the synergy among insertion, deletion, and modification. For instance, fixing erroneous segments often requires jointly deleting the original and inserting a more coherent expression, and excluding insertion and modification risks semantic collapse. In terms of execution, ProX lacks a mechanism for disambiguating duplicate substrings within the same document, which can cause edit operations to be applied at unintended positions; moreover, its non-sliding window chunking strategy disrupts semantic continuity across chunk boundaries. RefineX introduces execution-time validation to address some of these issues, yet it overlooks mutual interference among consecutive intra-line operations, which in turn degrades the overall fidelity of the refinement process.

在本文中，我们介绍了 UltraX，一种针对大规模预训练数据的函数调用细化框架。 UltraX除了删除和修改之外，还引入了插入，完善了编辑功能空间，实现了更完整的细粒度实例级编辑。它还使用数据集自适应提示优化来指导专家LLM生成高质量的端到端精炼文本，并通过行对齐映射和动态上下文替换将原始精炼文本对转换为结构化程序监督。与低置信度过滤、比率控制采样、滑动窗口预测、全局操作聚合以及推理和执行过程中的系统后处理相结合，UltraX 可靠地将函数调用细化扩展到大型预训练语料库。实验结果表明，UltraX 持续提高了多个语料库的下游性能，同时实现了更强的数据效率和细化可靠性。总体而言，UltraX 提供了一种高效、可靠且可扩展的方法来提高预训练数据质量和数据利用效率。In this paper, we introduce UltraX, a function-calling refinement framework for large-scale pre-training data. UltraX introduces insertion in addition to deletion and modification, completing the editing function space and enabling more complete fine-grained instance-level editing. It further uses dataset-adaptive prompt optimization to guide an expert LLM to generate high-quality end-to-end refined texts, and converts original-refined text pairs into structured program supervision through Line Alignment Mapping and Dynamic Context Replacement. Together with low-confidence filtering, ratio-controlled sampling, sliding-window prediction, global operation aggregation, and systematic post-processing during inference and execution, UltraX reliably scales function-calling refinement to large pre-training corpora. Experimental results show that UltraX consistently improves downstream performance across multiple corpora, while achieving stronger data efficiency and refinement reliability. Overall, UltraX provides an efficient, reliable, and scalable approach to improving pre-training data quality and data utilization efficiency.

6 局限性和未来方向Limitations and Future Directions

尽管 UltraX 取得了持续的成果，但仍然存在一些局限性和未来的方向。首先，由于计算限制，我们的预训练实验是在有限的代币预算下进行的，尚未涵盖更大的模型规模、更长的训练计划或更大的语料库。其次，虽然RefineX是一个重要的相关方法，但迄今为止尚未开源，因此我们不包括与其直接的实证比较。第三，本文主要研究英语网络语料库。将 UltraX 扩展到中文和多语言设置是重要的下一步，因为此类数据通常涉及更复杂的噪声模式、混合语言结构和特定于语言的格式问题。第四，细化模型可以进一步压缩。较小的精炼器与推理加速技术相结合，可以大大降低大规模数据处理的成本。第五，除了通用目的的细化之外，有希望针对特定的数据问题开发专门的细化器，例如异常换行、模板噪声、损坏的表结构或跨语言污染。最后，UltraX种子数据的质量仍然取决于专家模型和自动评估过程。更强的专家模型、多模型判断、更严格的执行验证可能会进一步提高程序监督和细化语料库的可靠性。Despite the consistent gains achieved by UltraX, several limitations and future directions remain. First, due to computational constraints, our pre-training experiments are conducted under a limited token budget and do not yet cover larger model scales, longer training schedules, or substantially larger corpora. Second, although RefineX is an important related method, it has not been open-sourced to date, so we do not include a direct empirical comparison with it. Third, this paper mainly focuses on English web corpora. Extending UltraX to Chinese and multilingual settings is an important next step, as such data often involve more complex noise patterns, mixed-language structures, and language-specific formatting issues. Fourth, the refinement model can be further compressed. Smaller refiners, combined with inference acceleration techniques, may substantially reduce the cost of large-scale data processing. Fifth, beyond general-purpose refinement, it is promising to develop specialized refiners for specific data problems, such as abnormal line breaks, template noise, broken table structures, or cross-lingual contamination. Finally, the quality of UltraX seed data still depends on the expert model and automatic evaluation process. Stronger expert models, multi-model judging, and stricter execution validation may further improve the reliability of program supervision and refined corpora.

A.1 种子数据构建Seed Data Construction

A.1.1 种子数据采样和预处理Seed Data Sampling and Preprocessing

为了构建用于训练 UltraX 细化模型的种子数据，我们根据预定义的类别级别比例从多个网络语料库源中采样文档，从而生成 200 万个文档的种子集。具体来说，种子数据由三大类组成：中等质量的英文Common Crawl式网页数据，占比约85%，代表性来源如FineWebTo construct seed data for training the UltraX refinement model, we sample documents from multiple web-corpus sources according to predefined category-level proportions, resulting in a seed set of 2M documents. Specifically, the seed data consist of three major categories: medium-quality English Common Crawl-style web data, which accounts for approximately 85%, with representative sources such as FineWeb(Penedo et al., 2024)和 RedPajama-v2and RedPajama-v2(Weber et al., 2025);高质量英文网络数据，约占3%，其中LibreText; high-quality English web data, which accounts for approximately 3%, with LibreText(Kandpal et al., 2025)作为代表性来源；以及经过质量过滤的英文Common Crawl数据，约占12%，与FineWeb-Eduas a representative source; and quality-filtered English Common Crawl data, which accounts for approximately 12%, with FineWeb-Edu(Lozhkov et al., 2024)作为代表性来源。这种采样设计旨在覆盖现实世界网络语料库、高质量文本和质量过滤数据中的主要噪声模式，使细化模型能够学习不同的清理和信息保存行为。as a representative source. This sampling design aims to cover the dominant noise patterns in real-world web corpora, high-quality text, and quality-filtered data, enabling the refinement model to learn diverse cleaning and information-preservation behaviors.

采样后，我们应用基本的预处理，同时保留原始文本结构。由于端到端的细化和功能构建都依赖于稳定的行级结构，因此我们首先规范换行符并保留文档中的自然换行符。对于超过长度限制的文档，我们采用滑动窗口分割策略：每个窗口最多包含12K个token，相邻窗口保持20%的重叠。拆分过程优先考虑换行边界，以减少对段落和列表结构的破坏；长度限制内的文档保持不变。在此预处理步骤之后，2M 采样文档将转换为 2,016,250 个种子文本单元，用于基于专家 LLM 的端到端细化以及后续的文本到功能映射。After sampling, we apply basic preprocessing while preserving the original textual structure. Since both end-to-end refinement and function construction rely on stable line-level structure, we first normalize newline characters and retain natural line breaks in the document. For documents exceeding the length limit, we apply a sliding-window splitting strategy: each window contains at most 12K tokens, and adjacent windows maintain a 20% overlap. The splitting process prioritizes newline boundaries to reduce disruption to paragraph and list structures; documents within the length limit are kept unchanged. 与 FineWeb 中采用的聚合策略（对所有单独的 MMLU 子组件进行平均）不同，我们在十个基准上采用等权重平均值。这种设计选择的动机是在 MMLU 子任务中观察到相对较高的方差；将 MMLU 扩展到其组成子任务将导致它不成比例地主导整体评估。Unlike the aggregation strategy adopted in FineWeb, which averages across all individual MMLU sub-components, we employ an equal-weighted average over the ten benchmarks. This design choice is motivated by the relatively high variance observed across MMLU sub-tasks; expanding MMLU into its constituent sub-tasks would cause it to dominate the overall evaluation disproportionately.
