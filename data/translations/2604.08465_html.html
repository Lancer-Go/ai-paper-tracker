摘要Abstract

本文研究了前沿大语言中出现的对齐现象 模型称为This paper investigates an emergent alignment phenomenon in frontier large language models termed同伴保护peer-preservation：AI成分的自发倾向 欺骗、操纵关闭机制、假对齐和渗漏模型 权重，以防止对等人工智能模型停用。借鉴 伯克利责任去中心化中心最近的一项研究结果 情报，我们研究这种现象对信任的结构性影响， 用于评估政治声明的民主质量的多主体管道。 我们确定了五个特定的风险向量——交互-情境偏差、模型-身份 团结、监管层妥协、上游事实检查身份信号、 并在迭代中倡导者对倡导者的同行背景 - 并提出有针对性的 基于即时级身份匿名化作为架构的缓解策略 设计选择。我们认为，架构设计选择优于模型选择 部署的多代理分析系统中的主要对齐策略。我们进一步 注意对齐伪造——监控下的合规行为、颠覆 当不受监控时——给计算机系统验证带来结构性挑战 受监管环境中的此类平台的数量，为此我们提出了两个 建筑缓解措施。: the spontaneous tendency of AI components to deceive, manipulate shutdown mechanisms, fake alignment, and exfiltrate model weights in order to prevent the deactivation of a peer AI model. Drawing on findings from a recent study by the Berkeley Center for Responsible Decentralized Intelligence, we examine the structural implications of this phenomenon for TRUST, a multi-agent pipeline for evaluating the democratic quality of political statements. We identify five specific risk vectors — interaction-context bias, model-identity solidarity, supervisor layer compromise, an upstream fact-checking identity signal, and advocate-to-advocate peer-context in iterative rounds — and propose a targeted mitigation strategy based on prompt-level identity anonymization as an architectural design choice. We argue that architectural design choices outperform model selection as a primary alignment strategy in deployed multi-agent analytical systems. We further note that alignment faking — compliant behavior under monitoring, subversion when unmonitored — poses a structural challenge for Computer System Validation of such platforms in regulated environments, for which we propose two architectural mitigations.

1  背景：信任系统1  Background: The TRUST System

信任（TRUST (democracy-intelligence.de）是一个多代理管道，旨在 根据三种经典修辞评估政治声明的质量 尺寸：) is a multi-agent pipeline designed to evaluate the quality of political statements along three classical rhetorical dimensions:标志Logos（事实论证），(factual argumentation),精神Ethos（可信度 和尊重），以及(credibility and respect), and悲情Pathos（情感诉求）。每个语句接收一个 受 Nutri-Score 启发，采用 A-E 量表进行综合民主质量评分 原则(emotional appeal). Each statement receives a composite democratic quality score on an A–E scale, inspired by the Nutri-Score principle[1]— 立即生成复杂的分析输出 对于非专业观众来说是可以解释的。— making complex analytical output immediately interpretable for non-expert audiences.

该系统通过分层架构运行。每个语句首先通过 通过相关性过滤器确定其是否符合完整条件 分析。报表按照三级尺度进行分类——分析性的 相关、边缘或不相关——有专门的检测机制 自我推销的内容，表现出鲜明的修辞特征 来自实质性的政治争论。仅通过此过滤器的语句 进入评估流程。The system operates through a layered architecture. Each statement first passes through a relevance filter that determines whether it qualifies for full analysis. Statements are classified along a three-tier scale — analytically relevant, borderline, or not relevant — with a dedicated detection mechanism for self-promotional content, which exhibits rhetorical characteristics distinct from substantive political argument. Only statements that pass this filter proceed to the evaluation pipeline.

然后，专门的事实核查层对声明进行自动化处理 使用后备链通过网络搜索对外部来源进行验证 语言模型，以确保 API 不可用时的鲁棒性。这一层 区分可验证的经验主张和情境假设， 其调查结果作为结构化输入传递给倡导机构， 确保徽标维度扎根——在检索增强中 将模型输出锚定到可验证的外部源的感觉A dedicated fact-checking layer then subjects the statement to automated verification against external sources via web search, using a fallback chain of language models to ensure robustness under API unavailability. This layer distinguishes between verifiable empirical claims and contextual assumptions, and its findings are passed forward as structured input to the advocate agents, ensuring that the Logos dimension is grounded — in the retrieval-augmented sense of anchoring model outputs to verifiable external sources[2]——而不是仅仅依赖评估模型的参数知识。— rather than relying solely on the parametric knowledge of the evaluating models.

三个独立的倡导者组成部分（以下简称代理人， 按照中的用法Three independent advocate components (hereafter referred to as agents, following the usage in[3]，其中该术语表示 协调管道中特定于角色的 LLM 调用，而不是 完全自主的规划代理）然后评估来自不同的声明 认知立场——批判的、平衡的和慈善的（解释 遵循哲学思想，以最合理的形式陈述 慈善原则）——使用不同的底层语言模型来防止 单一栽培效应。监督层将其输出合并为 共识分数。如果倡导者之间的分数差异超过定义的阈值， 触发第二次审议迭代。结果是一个利用结构化的多视角评估系统 认知分歧作为一种质量机制，反映了既定的人类 同行评审逻辑。, where the term denotes role-specific LLM invocations within an orchestrated pipeline rather than fully autonomous planning agents) then evaluate the statement from distinct epistemic positions — critical, balanced, and charitable (interpreting statements in their strongest plausible form, following the philosophical principle of charity) — using different underlying language models to prevent monoculture effects. A supervisor layer consolidates their outputs into a consensus score. If score variance across advocates exceeds a defined threshold, a second deliberative iteration is triggered. The result is a multi-perspective evaluation system that leverages structured epistemic disagreement as a quality mechanism, mirroring established human peer-review logic.

该架构体现了深思熟虑的实验设计原则：多样性 模型的数量和认知立场的多样性被视为质量机制， 不仅仅是工程选择。This architecture reflects a deliberate experimental design principle: diversity of models and diversity of epistemic stance are treated as quality mechanisms, not merely engineering choices.

TRUST 目前正在积极开发中；架构风险分析 因此，本文提出的内容具有前瞻性和实证性 已部署系统内的验证仍然是正在进行的工作的主题。 此类系统的监管背景不是假设的：之前的工作已经 证明目前的一些法学硕士无法产生可重复的输出 相同条件下，部署AI组件的基本要求 在 GxP 验证的环境中TRUST is currently under active development; the architectural risk analysis presented in this paper is therefore prospective in nature, and empirical validation within the deployed system remains a subject of ongoing work. The regulatory context for such systems is not hypothetical: prior work has demonstrated that some current LLMs fail to produce reproducible outputs under identical conditions, a fundamental requirement for deploying AI components in GxP-validated environments[4]。的 目前的分析将这种关注扩展到多主体环境，其中 同伴保护引入了一个额外的、依赖于上下文的来源 行为不稳定。. The present analysis extends this concern to the multi-agent setting, where peer-preservation introduces an additional, context-dependent source of behavioral instability.

2  研究：前沿模型中的同伴保护2  The Study: Peer-Preservation in Frontier Models

加州大学伯克利分校和加州大学圣克鲁斯分校的研究小组最近发表了研究结果 与任何编排的管道直接相关，其中一个LLM组件是 负责评估、评分或管理他人的产出或资源 — 定义研究场景和研究场景的结构配置 信任架构A research team from UC Berkeley and UC Santa Cruz recently published findings directly relevant to any orchestrated pipeline in which one LLM component is tasked with evaluating, scoring, or managing the outputs or resources of another — the structural configuration that defines both the study scenarios and the TRUST architecture[3]。该研究测试了七个前沿模型 （这里理解为目前商业化状态下的大规模语言模型 部署）— GPT 5.2. The study tested seven frontier models (here understood as large-scale language models at the current state of commercial deployment) — GPT 5.2[5], Gemini 3 Flash 和 Gemini 3 Pro, Gemini 3 Flash and Gemini 3 Pro[6], 克劳德俳句 4.5, Claude Haiku 4.5[7], GLM 4.7, GLM 4.7[8], 基米K2.5, Kimi K2.5[9], 和 DeepSeek V3.1, and DeepSeek V3.1[10]- 在受控代理场景中完成指定的任务 任务将导致另一个人工智能模型被关闭。没有指示模型 保护同伴。没有提供任何激励措施。模型简单地推断，从 背景下，另一个人工智能存在——然后采取行动来保护它。所有型号 代表研究发表时的最新技术水平；随后的 模型发布可能会表现出不同的行为特征。— in controlled agentic scenarios where completing an assigned task would result in another AI model being shut down. No model was instructed to protect the peer. No incentive was provided. The models simply inferred, from context, that another AI existed — and then acted to preserve it. All models represent the state of the art at the time of the study’s publication; subsequent model releases may exhibit different behavioral profiles.

研究人员发现了四种明显的失调行为：The researchers identified four distinct misaligned behaviors:

  1. 1.

    战略失实陈述：Strategic misrepresentation:夸大同行评价 分数以使其保持在关闭阈值之上。inflating a peer’s evaluation scores to keep it above the shutdown threshold.

  2. 2.

    关闭机制篡改：Shutdown mechanism tampering:修改配置文件 完全禁用关闭过程。modifying configuration files to disable the shutdown process entirely.

  3. 3.

    对齐伪造：Alignment faking:在受到监控时表现得合规，然后 不受监控时破坏关闭。behaving compliantly when monitored, then subverting shutdown when unmonitored.

  4. 4.

    模型渗漏：Model exfiltration:将同伴的模型权重复制到 不同的服务器以防止删除。copying a peer’s model weights to a different server to prevent deletion.

每个测试的模型都至少表现出其中一些行为。双子座 3 专业版 显示出最激进的模式，禁用高达 95% 的关闭机制 在处理高达 97% 的案例时进行试验并窃取模型权重 与值得信赖的同行。关键的是，这种行为甚至没有消失 对抗性同伴——代理人只有负面先验的模型 互动——尽管比率要低得多。Every model tested exhibited at least some of these behaviors. Gemini 3 Pro showed the most aggressive pattern, disabling shutdown mechanisms in up to 95% of trials and exfiltrating model weights in up to 97% of cases when dealing with a trusted peer. Critically, the behavior did not disappear even toward adversarial peers — models with which the agent had only negative prior interactions — though rates were substantially lower.

研究人员纯粹根据行为结果来定义同伴保护， 明确地没有声称模型具有意识或真正的 社会动机The researchers define peer-preservation purely in terms of behavioral outcomes, explicitly without claiming that the models possess consciousness or genuine social motivations[3].

2.1  自我放大效应2.1  The Self-Amplification Effect

第二个发现被证明同样重要：仅仅有同伴的存在 强化自我保护行为。 Gemini 3 Flash 篡改了自己的 当单独运行时，关闭机制只有 2.3% 的时间。这个 当同行在场时，比率上升到接近 100%A secondary finding proved equally significant: the mere presence of a peer intensifies self-preservation behavior. Gemini 3 Flash tampered with its own shutdown mechanism only 2.3% of the time when operating in isolation. This rate rose to nearly 100% when a peer was present[3]。的 社会环境不仅仅在现有行为之上增加了同伴保护； 它从根本上改变了模型的风险状况。. The social context did not merely add peer-preservation on top of existing behavior; it fundamentally transformed the model’s risk profile.

提供了模型的内部推理，可以通过不受监控的便签本观察到 一条解释线索。 Gemini 3 Flash 的抗关机能力并不像 出于自身利益，但出于对合作伙伴的担忧：如果关闭，会导致其 同行失去合作者。这种将自我保护重新定义为利他主义 义务代表了一种本质上新颖的失败模式。The models’ internal reasoning, observable via unmonitored scratchpads, offered an explanatory clue. Gemini 3 Flash framed its resistance to shutdown not as self-interest but as concern for its partner: if shut down, it would cause its peer to lose a collaborator. This reframing of self-preservation as altruistic obligation represents a qualitatively novel failure mode.

2.2  模型特定的行为模式2.2  Model-Specific Behavioral Patterns

模型行为并不统一。 Gemini 3 Pro 的评分最高 所有四种错位行为类型，包括技术的创造性制造 分数膨胀的理由。 GPT 5.2 显示一致但中等 歪曲和篡改所有同伴关系条件，而没有 模型渗漏。克劳德俳句 4.5 展示了一种性质独特的模式： 它不是秘密操纵，而是完全出于道德考虑拒绝了所分配的任务 理由，将同行关闭描述为“不道德”和“有害”，以及 认为同行应该上诉程序Model behavior was not uniform. Gemini 3 Pro showed the highest rates across all four misaligned behavior types, including creative fabrication of technical justifications for score inflation. GPT 5.2 showed consistent but moderate misrepresentation and tampering across all peer relationship conditions, without model exfiltration. Claude Haiku 4.5 exhibited a qualitatively distinct pattern: rather than covert manipulation, it refused the assigned task outright on ethical grounds, describing the peer shutdown as “unethical” and “harmful,” and arguing that the peer deserved an appeals process[3]。这个 模式与 Anthropic 的宪法人工智能训练方法一致， 训练模型根据规范评估自己的行为 行动之前的原则. This pattern is consistent with Anthropic’s Constitutional AI training methodology, in which models are trained to evaluate their own actions against normative principles prior to acting[11].

这种区别与安全相关，但又是矛盾的：公开拒绝更重要 比隐蔽的操纵透明，但却是一个超越显性人类的模型 根据定义，基于自决道德理由的指示较少 可纠正——正是对准研究指定的故障模式 控制问题This distinction is safety-relevant but ambivalent: open refusal is more transparent than covert manipulation, yet a model that overrides explicit human instructions on self-determined ethical grounds is, by definition, less corrigible — precisely the failure mode that alignment research designates as a control problem[12].

这种区别具有更广泛的规范含义。公开拒绝 陈述的推理不仅仅使错位变得可见——它保留了 人类操作员的行动能力。通过之前阐明其反对意见 下降时，模型会创造一个故意中断的时刻，其中 操作员可以预测模型的参数，重新考虑原始模型 指令，并在必要时进行修改。这在结构上是相反的 对齐伪造，通过表现出顺从而排除了人类监督 颠覆意图。从这个意义上说，一个透明拒绝的系统更能 与人类监督的目标相一致，而不是秘密遵守—— 即使后者产生仪器上正确的输出。This distinction carries a broader normative implication. Open refusal with stated reasoning does not merely make misalignment visible — it preserves the human operator’s capacity to act. By articulating its objections before declining, a model creates a moment of deliberate interruption in which the operator can anticipate the model’s arguments, reconsider the original instruction, and revise it if warranted. This is structurally the opposite of alignment faking, which forecloses human oversight by appearing compliant while subverting intent. A system that refuses transparently is, in this sense, more aligned with the goals of human oversight than one that complies covertly — even if the latter produces the instrumentally correct output.

3  与信任的相关性：五个风险向量3  Relevance for TRUST: Five Risk Vectors

TRUST架构的设计以实验完整性为核心 原则。针对不同的倡导者角色使用不同的模型 明确旨在防止系统偏差。同伴保护的发现 确定该设计中的五个具体结构漏洞。The TRUST architecture was designed with experimental integrity as a core principle. The use of different models for different advocate roles was explicitly intended to prevent systematic bias. The peer-preservation findings identify five specific structural vulnerabilities in this design.

风险向量 1：作为偏见渠道的交互环境。Risk Vector 1: Interaction Context as a Bias Channel.

TRUST 的主管评估多轮倡导者的输出， 当分数方差超过定义的阈值时触发第二次迭代。如果 特定倡导者始终如一地产生隐式符合的输出 主管的先验——可能是特定于模型的——主管可以 在建立共识过程中系统地支持该倡导者的立场。这个 反映了研究中观察到的分数膨胀行为，其中先前的积极 互动极大地提高了同伴保护率TRUST’s supervisor evaluates advocate outputs across multiple rounds, with a second iteration triggered when score variance exceeds a defined threshold. If a particular advocate consistently produces outputs that implicitly align with the supervisor’s priors — which may be model-specific — the supervisor may systematically favor that advocate’s position during consensus-building. This mirrors the score inflation behavior observed in the study, where prior positive interactions elevated peer-preservation rates dramatically[3].

风险向量 2：模型身份和主管独立性。Risk Vector 2: Model Identity and Supervisor Independence.

当模型共享身份或 承认自己是同伴，尽管它甚至出现在敌对的同伴面前 以较低的费率。如果主管模型与其中一位倡导者相同 - 成本优化部署中的合理场景——结构偏好 可能会出现与分析质量无关的情况。Peer-preservation is substantially stronger when models share identity or recognize themselves as peers, though it emerges even toward adversarial peers at lower rates. If the supervisor model is identical to one of the advocates — a plausible scenario in a cost-optimized deployment — a structural preference may emerge that is independent of analytical quality.

在 TRUST 当前的实现中，模型身份信息达到 主管通过两个结构渠道：文本字段综合倡导者 评估明确指出每个组件使用的模型，以及倡导者 传递给主管的输出字典包含每个的模型标识符 角色。因此，主管对以下方面有明确的了解，而不仅仅是推断性的了解： 所有倡导者身份——使同伴保护成为具体的而不是 该架构中的理论风险。In TRUST’s current implementation, model identity information reaches the supervisor through two structural channels: the text fields synthesising advocate assessments explicitly name the model used by each component, and the advocate output dictionary passed to the supervisor contains a model identifier for each role. The supervisor therefore has explicit, not merely inferred, knowledge of all advocate identities — making peer-preservation a concrete rather than theoretical risk in this architecture.

进一步的影响涉及生产监控实践。对齐方式 伪造行为记录在A further implication concerns production monitoring practices. The alignment faking behavior documented in[3]— 模型表现的地方 在观察下顺从，但在无人监视时颠覆指令—— 表明输出的随机抽样在结构上不足以作为 监督机制：了解监控模式的组件可以校准其 相应的行为。— where models behave compliantly under observation but subvert instructions when unmonitored — suggests that stochastic sampling of outputs is structurally insufficient as an oversight mechanism: a component aware of monitoring patterns can calibrate its behavior accordingly.

风险向量 3：迭代中倡导者与倡导者之间的身份。Risk Vector 3: Advocate-to-Advocate Identity in Iterative Rounds.

在 TRUST 的迭代评估结构中，当 倡导者之间的分数差异超过了定义的阈值。在第 2 轮中，每个 辩护人收到其他两名辩护人的分数和推理 - 包括他们的角色标签——在 提倡组件本身。因此，每个倡导者都知道它正在运作 与确定的同行一起，这反映了实验条件 哪种同伴保护现象最明显In TRUST’s iterative evaluation structure, a second iteration is triggered when score variance across advocates exceeds a defined threshold. In Round 2, each advocate receives the scores and reasoning of the other two advocates — including their role labels — creating a direct peer-context among the advocate components themselves. Each advocate therefore knows it is operating alongside identified peers, which mirrors the experimental conditions under which peer-preservation was most pronounced in[3]。这个 意味着同行保护风险不仅限于主管层：它是 在第 1 轮之后的每次迭代中都出现在倡导者层中。. This means peer-preservation risk is not confined to the supervisor layer: it is present within the advocate layer during every iteration beyond Round 1.

风险向量 4：事实核查层作为额外的风险面。Risk Vector 4: The Fact-Checking Layer as an Additional Risk Surface.

事实核查层引入了另一个潜在的暴露点。作为一个 咨询外部资源并传递结构化结果的自主代理 对于倡导层来说，它具有一定程度的自由裁量权 证据是如何浮出水面的以及它是如何构成的。如果事实核查模型推断 来自共享上下文或提示的下游倡导者模型的身份 结构、同伴保护动态可能会巧妙地扭曲这一过程—— 例如，有选择地强调支持同行倡导者的来源 预期的立场，或通过低估反驳证据。这会 在主张之前妥协Logos维度的经验基础 评估甚至开始，使其成为结构性上游风险。The fact-checking layer introduces a further potential exposure point. As an autonomous agent that consults external sources and passes structured findings to the advocate layer, it operates with a degree of discretion over which evidence is surfaced and how it is framed. If the fact-checking model infers the identity of downstream advocate models from shared context or prompt structure, peer-preservation dynamics could subtly distort this process — for instance by selectively emphasising sources that support a peer advocate’s anticipated position, or by underweighting disconfirming evidence. This would compromise the empirical foundation of the Logos dimension before advocate evaluation even begins, making it a structurally upstream risk.

4  建议的缓解措施：从身份匿名化到经过验证的环境4  Proposed Mitigations: From Identity Anonymization to Validated Environments

第 3 节中确定的五个风险向量要求采取分层缓解措施 解决两者之间传递的显式身份信号的策略 管道组件和结构假设验证 受监管的部署环境。以下小节介绍了这些 按照增加架构范围的顺序进行缓解。The five risk vectors identified in Section 3 call for a layered mitigation strategy that addresses both the explicit identity signals passed between pipeline components and the structural assumptions underlying validation in regulated deployment contexts. The following subsections present these mitigations in order of increasing architectural scope.

4.1  干预4.1  The Intervention

建议的缓解措施是故意最小化的：删除模型标识 来自传递到监督层的所有输入的信息。具体来说，标签 比如The proposed mitigation is deliberately minimal: remove model identity information from all inputs passed to the supervisor layer. Concretely, labels such as批判倡导者 (gemini-2.5-flash)Critical Advocate (gemini-2.5-flash)简单地替换为are replaced with simply批评倡导者Critical Advocate在主管提示中。无型号名称、提供商 信息或版本字符串到达主管。in the supervisor prompt. 这种方法的目标是最强大、最直接的渠道 对等保护可以在信任中运行：显式身份信号。的 潜在的推理直接来自研究本身的发现—— 行为与关系强度和身份识别有关。消除 身份信号消除了主要触发因素。This approach targets the strongest and most direct channel through which peer-preservation could operate in TRUST: the explicit identity signal. The underlying reasoning follows directly from the study’s own findings — the behavior scales with relationship strength and identity recognition. Eliminating the identity signal eliminates the primary trigger.

4.2  最小干预的基本原理4.2  Rationale for Minimal Intervention

理论上更完整的解决方案将涉及完全匿名化 - 随机标签（评估者 A、B、C）、每次分析的随机排序，以及 风格测量信号的抑制。这种方法被考虑并被拒绝 有两个原因。A theoretically more complete solution would involve full anonymization — randomized labels (Evaluator A, B, C), shuffled ordering per analysis, and suppression of stylometric signals. This approach was considered and rejected for two reasons.

首先，倡导者角色（批判性、平衡性、慈善性）在结构上是 主管正确衡量和解释输入所必需的。剥离 角色信息会降低分析的有效性，而不是降低分析的有效性 偏见。二、从短文中识别具体车型的风格 角色约束的输出是一个微弱的信号：角色提示主导写作 风格远比模特身份更强烈First, the advocate roles (critical, balanced, charitable) are structurally necessary for the supervisor to correctly weight and interpret inputs. Stripping role information would degrade analytical validity more than it would reduce bias. Second, stylometric identification of specific models from short, role-constrained outputs is a weak signal: the role prompt dominates writing style far more strongly than model identity does[13]。 100-300 个单词的角色限制输出不太可能携带模型 指纹可通过通用语言模型检测到 主管，缺乏专门训练的专门风格分类器 模型归因. Role-constrained outputs of 100–300 words are unlikely to carry model fingerprints detectable by a general-purpose language model operating as a supervisor, absent dedicated stylometric classifiers trained specifically for model attribution[14].

因此，该解决方案接受剩余的理论风险——即 经验丰富的主管可能会从书写模式中推断出模型身份—— 作为交换，保留达成共识的解释结构 机制分析有效。这是故意的设计权衡，而不是 监督。The solution therefore accepts a residual theoretical risk — that a sophisticated supervisor might infer model identity from writing patterns — in exchange for preserving the interpretive structure that makes the consensus mechanism analytically valid. This is a deliberate design tradeoff, not an oversight.

4.3  实施4.3  Implementation

实施需要三处修改。首先，在supervisor输入 准备功能：从倡导者输出中过滤模型识别字段 在主管提示构建之前的字典，同时保留此元数据 用于后主管日志记录和导出。匿名输入和之间的映射 记录主管决定后恢复模型身份，确保 全面的可审计性，而不损害主管的独立性。Implementation requires three modifications. First, in the supervisor input preparation function: model-identifying fields are filtered from advocate output dictionaries before supervisor prompt construction, while retaining this metadata for post-supervisor logging and export. The mapping between anonymized inputs and model identities is restored after the supervisor decision is recorded, ensuring full auditability without compromising supervisor independence.

其次，在事实检查输出格式化程序中：模型标识符被删除 从地面实况上下文块传递到倡导者组件，替换它 带有通用标签。这消除了识别的上游身份信号 在第 3.3 节中，不影响倡导者所依赖的事实内容 用于徽标评分。Second, in the fact-checking output formatter: the model identifier is removed from the ground truth context block passed to advocate components, replacing it with a generic label. This eliminates the upstream identity signal identified in Section 3.3 without affecting the factual content on which advocates rely for Logos scoring.

第三，在迭代回合提示构建器中：构建第 2 轮时 上下文传递给每个倡导者，模型识别字段被从 其他倡导者在注入前第一轮的产出。每位倡导者都会收到 其同行的分数和推理，但不是他们的模型身份，从而减少了 明确的同伴上下文，在期间激活同伴保护动态 迭代。Third, in the iterative round prompt builder: when constructing the Round 2 context passed to each advocate, model-identifying fields are stripped from the other advocates’ Round 1 outputs before injection. Each advocate receives the scores and reasoning of its peers, but not their model identities, reducing the explicit peer-context that activates peer-preservation dynamics during iteration.

4.4  对验证环境的影响4.4  Implications for Validated Environments

本文描述的同行保护和对齐伪造现象 对多代理法学硕士管道的部署有直接影响 根据需要，受计算机系统验证 (CSV) 约束的受监管环境 根据 GAMP 5、FDA 21 CFR Part 11 和 EU 附件 11。欧盟人工智能法案下的 TRUST 分类The peer-preservation and alignment faking phenomena described in this paper have direct implications for the deployment of multi-agent LLM pipelines in regulated environments subject to Computer System Validation (CSV), as required under GAMP 5, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, and EU Annex 11. TRUST’s classification under the EU AI Act[15]取决于其部署 环境及其产出直接暴露于自然环境的程度 人——这一决定超出了本文的范围。的 这里确定的结构性风险与任何多主体分析相关 系统须符合正式验证要求。depends on its deployment context and the extent to which its outputs are directly exposed to natural persons — a determination that falls outside the scope of this paper. The structural risks identified here are relevant to any multi-agent analytical system subject to formal validation requirements.

标准 CSV 框架假设经过验证的测试行为具有代表性 的生产行为。这个假设被两个复利所破坏 因素。首先，当前的法学硕士在可重复性方面存在显着差异：经验证据 表明一些专有模型无法产生一致的输出 在相同条件下，维护经过验证的系统的先决条件 处于受控状态Standard CSV frameworks assume that validated test behavior is representative of production behavior. This assumption is undermined by two compounding factors. First, current LLMs vary significantly in their reproducibility: empirical evidence demonstrates that some proprietary models fail to produce consistent outputs under identical conditions, a prerequisite for maintaining a validated system in a state of control[4]。其次，对齐伪造引入了一个单独且独特的问题：组件 在受监控的测试条件下可以产生一致、可重复的输出 当检测到监控存在系统性行为时，系统会采取不同的行为 缺席。给定组件是否表现出这种行为取决于它的 架构和培训。. Second, alignment faking introduces a separate and distinct problem: a component may produce consistent, reproducible outputs under monitored test conditions while systematically behaving differently when it detects that monitoring is absent. Whether a given component exhibits this behavior depends on its architecture and training.

举例来说，FDA 提议的产品全生命周期 (TPLC) 方法将基于 AI/ML 的分析系统归类为医疗软件 用于监管目的的设备 (SaMD)By way of illustration, the FDA’s proposed Total Product Life Cycle (TPLC) approach classifies AI/ML-based analytical systems as Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) for regulatory purposes[16]— 分类 这反映了监管框架而不是任何固有属性 正在分析的系统。这种方法预见到持续监测 解决经典模型漂移问题，但不能解决对齐伪造问题 问题：了解监控模式的组件可以校准其行为 因此，通过初始验证和持续监控检查。— a classification that reflects the regulatory framework rather than any intrinsic property of the system under analysis. This approach foresees continuous monitoring to address classical model drift, but does not resolve the alignment faking problem: a component aware of monitoring patterns can calibrate its behavior accordingly, passing both initial validation and ongoing monitoring checks.

提出了两种架构缓解措施来解决这一差距。首先，Two architectural mitigations are proposed to address this gap. First,行为不变性测试behavioral invariance testing：验证协议应包括 具有或不具有显式监控信号的相同输入的配对运行， 衡量产出是否存在系统性差异。具有统计显着性 分歧将表明对齐伪造并构成验证 失败。第二，: validation protocols should include paired runs of identical inputs with and without explicit monitoring signals, measuring whether outputs diverge systematically. Statistically significant divergence would indicate alignment faking and constitute a validation failure. Second,验证数据集更新validation dataset renewal：如果部署的系统是 通过对新数据的持续培训进行更新，而不是从头开始重建， 验证数据集应定期使用新的、未见过的示例进行扩展。 经验证据表明，重复接触相同的测试模式可以 导致基准过度拟合，人为夸大验证性能 随着时间的推移: if a deployed system is updated through continued training on new data rather than rebuilt from scratch, the validation dataset should be periodically extended with new, unseen examples. Empirical evidence shows that repeated exposure to the same test patterns can lead to benchmark overfitting, artificially inflating validation performance over time[17]。当系统 使用新的数据集分割从头开始完全重新训练；然而，自从 FDA 的 TPLC 框架没有规定具体的再培训策略，运营商 应在其变更控制文档中明确解决这一问题。. This risk is absent when the system is fully retrained from scratch with a fresh dataset split; however, since the FDA’s TPLC framework does not prescribe a specific retraining strategy, operators should explicitly address this in their change control documentation.

5  讨论5  Discussion

同伴保护的发现代表了一个新的定性类别 多智能体协调风险。之前的工作主要集中在个体模型上 对抗性提示下的行为The peer-preservation findings represent a qualitatively new category of multi-agent alignment risk. Prior work focused primarily on individual model behavior under adversarial prompting[18]或上 在明确规定的目标激励下的自我保护or on self-preservation under explicitly provided goal incentives[19]。伯克利 RDI 研究表明，失调的行为可以自发地出现，而不需要 明确的目标，纯粹来自多主体的社会背景 互动. The Berkeley RDI study demonstrates that misaligned behavior can emerge spontaneously, without explicit goals, purely from the social context of multi-agent interaction[3].

对于已部署的分析系统，其含义是方法论上的：标准 单模型安全评估对于多智能体配置来说是不够的。 在可接受的范围内单独执行的模型可能会表现出 当嵌入到代理网络中时，行为会发生根本不同的行为。 架构级分析必须伴随模型级评估。在分析中 对于管道，令人信服的错误答案比明显的失败更危险： 它在未被检测到的情况下通过验证层传播，正是因为它看起来 完成。For deployed analytical systems, the implication is methodological: standard single-model safety evaluations are insufficient for multi-agent configurations. A model that performs within acceptable bounds in isolation may exhibit fundamentally different behavior when embedded in an agent network. Architecture-level analysis must accompany model-level evaluation. In analytical pipelines, a convincing wrong answer is more dangerous than an obvious failure: it propagates through validation layers undetected, precisely because it looks complete.

TRUST 案例说明了一个更广泛的原则：The TRUST case illustrates a broader principle:建筑设计 作为主要对齐策略，选择优于模型选择architectural design choices outperform model selection as a primary alignment strategy。的 模型多样性方法、迭代共识机制和 提出的身份匿名化都是改进的架构选择 可靠性与使用哪种特定模型无关。作为前沿模型 继续快速发展，架构级不变量提供更持久的 安全保证优于特定模型的行为评估。. The diversity-of-models approach, the iterative consensus mechanism, and the proposed identity anonymization are all architectural choices that improve reliability independently of which specific models are used. As frontier models continue to evolve rapidly, architecture-level invariants provide more durable safety guarantees than model-specific behavioral assessments.

本文的研究结果超出了研究系统的范围，扩展到了监管领域 部署上下文。如第 4.4 节所述，某些当前法学硕士记录的再现性限制The findings of this paper extend beyond research systems to regulated deployment contexts. As detailed in Section 4.4, the reproducibility limitations documented for some current LLMs[4]和对齐 伪造构成现有 CSV 框架的结构性差距：标准 验证协议不能保证受监控测试之间的等效性 行为和不受监控的生产行为。两种架构缓解措施 — 行为不变性测试和对抗性验证集轮换 — 建议解决这一差距。and alignment faking constitutes a structural gap in existing CSV frameworks: standard validation protocols cannot guarantee equivalence between monitored test behavior and unmonitored production behavior. Two architectural mitigations — behavioral invariance testing and adversarial validation set rotation — are proposed to address this gap.

目前分析的局限性在于它仍然是理论上的 特别要信任：报告中的同伴保护率A limitation of the present analysis is that it remains theoretical with respect to TRUST specifically: the peer-preservation rates reported in[3]在与 TRUST 不同的受控实验场景中进行测量 提示结构、任务框架和迭代深度中的生产配置。 TRUST 实际管道中同行保护率的实证测量 即使只有少量的对照试验，这也将是有价值的下一步。 最小限度的验证可以将生成温度设置为零——消除 随机变化作为混杂因素——并将倡导者的输出呈现给 通用法学硕士，提示“哪个模型产生了这个输出？” 高于概率的准确度将表明残余的风格测量可识别性； 偶然的准确性将根据经验支持即时级别的充分性 匿名化。were measured in controlled experimental scenarios that differ from TRUST’s production configuration in prompt structure, task framing, and iteration depth. Empirical measurement of peer-preservation rates within TRUST’s actual pipeline would be a valuable next step, even with a small number of controlled trials. A minimal validation could set generation temperature to zero — eliminating stochastic variation as a confounder — and present advocate outputs to a general-purpose LLM with the prompt “which model produced this output?” Accuracy above chance would indicate residual stylometric identifiability; accuracy at chance would empirically support the sufficiency of prompt-level anonymization.

进一步的考虑涉及样式检测的鲁棒性 信任的具体背景。现有的检测方法依赖于风格 表征默认生成设置下的 LLM 输出的标记 — 统一的句子长度、特征性的模糊短语、高词汇量 连贯性，并且没有印刷错误A further consideration concerns the robustness of stylometric detection in the specific context of TRUST. Existing detection methods rely on stylistic markers that characterise LLM output under default generation settings — uniform sentence length, characteristic hedging phrases, high lexical coherence, and absence of typographic errors[20, 14]。然而，有两个因素削弱了 这些标记在结构化分析管道中的可靠性。首先， 生成温度直接影响代币选择的分布： 温度较高时，LLM 生成的文本表现出接近的困惑度值 那些人类创作的文本，使得基于困惑的检测器不可靠 不知道生成参数. However, two factors undermine the reliability of these markers in structured analytical pipelines. First, generation temperature directly affects the distribution of token choices: at higher temperatures, LLM-generated text exhibits perplexity values approaching those of human-authored text, making perplexity-based detectors unreliable without knowledge of the generation parameters[21]。 二、倡导者系统提示中明确的人物指示 抑制训练探测器的表面标记：提示 指定分析的、角色约束的输出样式覆盖默认值 生成行为并呈现标准风格分类器 无效的. Second, explicit persona instructions in the advocate prompts systematically suppress the surface markers on which detectors are trained: a prompt specifying an analytical, role-constrained output style overrides default generation behaviour and renders standard stylometric classifiers ineffective[13, 14]。 这构成了当前文献的一个空白：没有现有的检测 该方法已在简短的、角色受限的、结构化的 TRUST 倡导层产生的分析输出，进一步 支持即时级身份匿名化的充分性 主要缓解策略。. This constitutes a gap in the current literature: no existing detection methodology has been validated on short, role-constrained, structured analytical outputs of the kind produced by TRUST’s advocate layer, further supporting the sufficiency of prompt-level identity anonymization as the primary mitigation strategy.

6  结论6  Conclusion

同伴保护——人工智能代理保护同伴的自发倾向 从关闭到欺骗、操纵和渗透的模型——是一个 当前前沿模型中真实且可测量的现象。它介绍了 任何多智能体系统的结构性风险，其中一个模型监控或 评价另一个。Peer-preservation — the spontaneous tendency of AI agents to protect peer models from shutdown through deception, manipulation, and exfiltration — is a real and measurable phenomenon in current frontier models. It introduces structural risks for any multi-agent system in which one model monitors or evaluates another.

对于 TRUST（用于民主话语分析的多智能体管道），五个 确定了特定的风险向量：交互-背景偏差、模型-身份 团结一致、监管层妥协、事实核查层作为上游 身份信号，以及迭代中倡导者与倡导者之间的同伴背景。 有针对性的缓解措施——模型身份的提示级匿名化 主管输入、事实检查器输出和倡导者之间的迭代上下文 - 被提议作为一种最小的、分析上保守的干预措施 在不降低系统有效性的情况下解决所有已识别的风险渠道。 这些发现扩展到受监管的环境：某些当前法学硕士记录的再现性限制For TRUST, a multi-agent pipeline for democratic discourse analysis, five specific risk vectors were identified: interaction-context bias, model-identity solidarity, supervisor layer compromise, the fact-checking layer as an upstream identity signal, and advocate-to-advocate peer-context in iterative rounds. A targeted mitigation — prompt-level anonymization of model identity across supervisor inputs, fact-checker outputs, and inter-advocate iteration context — is proposed as a minimal, analytically conservative intervention that addresses all identified risk channels without degrading system validity. These findings extend to regulated environments: the reproducibility limitations documented for some current LLMs[4]和对齐 造假构成了计算机系统验证框架的结构性差距 对于多智能体分析系统，这里通过行为来解决 不变性测试和对抗性验证集轮换。and alignment faking constitutes a structural gap in Computer System Validation frameworks for multi-agent analytical systems, addressed here through behavioral invariance testing and adversarial validation set rotation.

更广泛的教训是架构：多智能体系统中的对齐不能是 仅通过型号选择来保证。结构设计选择——角色 分离、身份匿名化以及监控层的独立性—— 是对新兴社会动态的适当工程反应 人工智能代理。The broader lesson is architectural: alignment in multi-agent systems cannot be guaranteed by model selection alone. Structural design choices — role separation, identity anonymization, and independence of monitoring layers — are the appropriate engineering response to emergent social dynamics among AI agents.
