神经马尔可夫逻辑网络中的并行噪声Parallel Noising in Neural Markov Logic Networks

Peter Jung1, Giuseppe Marra2, Ondřej Kuželka1
1Czech Technical University, Prague, Czech Republic
2Department of Computer Science, KU Leuven, Leuven, Belgium
摘要Abstract

神经马尔可夫逻辑网络（NMLN）是一种灵活的神经符号关系模型。 先前的工作表明，尽管 NMLN 作为小型关系结构的生成模型具有很强的性能，但它们在较大结构上的性能不如基于扩散的生成图模型。在本文中，我们沿着两个主要维度强化 NMLN：（i）我们使用图神经网络提高其潜在函数的表达能力，（ii）我们开发了一种新的训练和推理算法，其灵感来自于并行调节马尔可夫链蒙特卡罗方法，我们将其命名为Neural Markov Logic Networks (NMLNs) are a flexible neurosymbolic relational model. Previous work has shown that, although NMLNs achieve strong performance as generative models for small relational structures, they underperform diffusion-based generative graph models on larger structures. In this paper, we strengthen NMLNs along two main dimensions: (i) we increase the expressive capacity of their potential functions using graph neural networks, and (ii) we develop a new training and inference algorithm inspired by parallel-tempering Markov chain Monte Carlo methods, which we name并行噪声parallel noising。总之，这些增强功能使 NMLN 能够在图生成方面获得相对于一般基于扩散的生成图模型的强大性能。此外，它们使得 NMLN 在生成小分子结构时能够与基于文本的专门循环模型的性能相匹配。. Together, these enhancements enable NMLNs to attain strong performance in graph generation relative to general diffusion-based generative graph models. Furthermore, they allow NMLNs to match the performance of specialized text-based recurrent models when generating small molecular structures.

9 结论Conclusions

我们引入了 NMLN*，这是一种增强的神经马尔可夫逻辑网络，它解决了两个实际瓶颈：基于片段的电位的有限表达性以及在近确定性学习约束下脆弱、缓慢混合的吉布斯推理。在建模方面，我们用全局 GNN 能量实例化 NMLN 势来捕获长程结构。在推理方面，我们建议We introduced NMLN*, a strengthened Neural Markov Logic Network that tackles two practical bottlenecks: limited expressivity of fragment-based potentials and brittle, slow-mixing Gibbs inference under near-deterministic learned constraints. On the modeling side, we instantiate NMLN potentials with global GNN energies to capture long-range structure. On the inference side, we propose并行噪声parallel noising，一个精确的副本交换采样器，将链耦合在固定损坏级别而不是温度的阶梯上，即使目标分布在训练期间发生变化，它也能保持良好的行为。根据经验，NMLN* 提高了分子生成基准的召回率和样本质量。, an exact replica-exchange sampler that couples chains across a ladder of fixed corruption levels rather than temperatures, which stays well-behaved even as the target distribution changes during training. Empirically, NMLN* improves recall and sample quality on molecular generation benchmarks.

A.2 直觉：并行噪声与并行回火Intuition: Parallel Noising vs. Parallel Tempering

并行噪声（PN）和并行回火（PT）都是副本交换方法：它们运行辅助链的梯子并使用交换移动将探索状态转移回目标分布。关键的区别在于辅助分布的构建方式。Parallel noising (PN) and parallel tempering (PT) are both replica-exchange methods: they run a ladder of auxiliary chains and use swap moves to transfer exploratory states back toward the target distribution. The crucial difference is how the auxiliary distributions are constructed.

PT 通过重新调整不同温度下的能量来构建其阶梯。高温链使能源格局变得平坦，因此可以更容易地在模式之间移动。当温度阶梯与目标的能量尺度很好匹配时，这种方法效果很好：相邻链必须有足够的分布重叠才能接受交换移动。然而，当目标非常尖锐、高度受限或在训练过程中发生变化时，这一要求可能会出现问题。在这种情况下，固定温度梯可能要么太粗，导致交换接受度低，要么太温和，导致热链探索性不够。PT builds its ladder by rescaling the energy with different temperatures. High-temperature chains flatten the energy landscape and can therefore move more easily between modes. This works well when the temperature ladder is well matched to the energy scale of the target: adjacent chains must have enough distributional overlap for swap moves to be accepted. However, this requirement can become problematic when the target is very sharp, highly constrained, or changing during training. In such cases, a fixed temperature ladder may either be too coarse, leading to low swap acceptance, or too mild, giving hot chains that are not exploratory enough.

相反，PN 通过破坏状态空间来构建其阶梯。它没有改变能量尺度，而是定义了与结构化对象的噪声越来越大的版本相对应的辅助链。较高噪声的链探索同一组合问题的宽松版本，而最低噪声的链仍然是感兴趣的目标。当困难来自尖锐的符号或组合约束时，这可能会更加稳健，因为梯子与数据结构相关，而不是与可能难以调整的温标相关。PN instead builds its ladder by corrupting the state space. Rather than changing the energy scale, it defines auxiliary chains corresponding to increasingly noised versions of the structured object. The higher-noise chains explore relaxed versions of the same combinatorial problem, while the lowest-noise chain remains the target of interest. This can be more robust when the difficulty comes from sharp symbolic or combinatorial constraints, because the ladder is tied to the structure of the data rather than to a temperature scale that may be hard to tune.
