By injecting PII into specific model parameters via masked continual pretraining, we obtain a ground-truth for knowledge localization. This enables the first quantitative assessment of whether unlearning targets the right parameters. Our findings underscore that high performance in terms of traditional unlearning metrics (particularly in case of模拟非营利组织SimNPO）可以在不实际针对这些权重的情况下实现，从而使遗忘方法更容易受到重新浮现攻击。 相比之下，) can be achieved without actually targeting those weights, leaving unlearning methods more susceptible to resurfacing attacks. 这些发现为未来研究提出了两个重要方向。首先，遗忘方法的设计和测试不仅应考虑输出水平的功效，还应考虑其针对适当参数的能力。其次，需要更精确的知识定位技术，这可以极大地有利于忘却学习的发展。These findings suggest two important directions for future research. Firstly, unlearning methods should be designed and tested not only for output-level efficacy, but also for their ability to target the appropriate parameters. Secondly, there is a need for more precise knowledge localization techniques, which could greatly benefit the development of unlearning.缺口Lacuna对于这两个研究方向来说，这是一个有价值的工具，我们希望它将鼓励社区超越产出水平评估。同时，我们认识到知识本地化可能并不总是现实的，并且在密集模型中内存存储可能并不总是非常本地化。因此，当训练涉及敏感的现实世界数据时，最好将记忆限制在特定的参数或模块，而不是让它遍布整个模型。is a valuable tool for both these research directions, and we hope it will encourage the community to move beyond output-level evaluation.
