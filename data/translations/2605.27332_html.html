II 背景Background

在本研究中，我们广泛使用图表来涵盖需求工程（例如 UML）中使用的各种图形模型。然而，我们提出的方法专门针对流程图，流程图是图表的基本子集，其特征是表示顺序控制流逻辑的有向图。In this study, we use the diagrams broadly to encompass the wide variety of graphical models used in requirements engineering (e.g., UML). Our proposed approach, however, specifically targets flowcharts, a fundamental subset of diagrams characterized by directed graphs that represent sequential control-flow logic.

Ⅱ-AII-A 视觉语言模型Vision Language Models

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 将图像编码器与 LLM 相结合，以支持需要联合解释视觉内容并以自然语言生成或推理的任务。Vision Language Models (VLMs) combine an image encoder with an LLM to support tasks that require jointly interpreting visual content and generating or reasoning in natural language.

许多最先进的 VLM 使用 Vision Transformer (ViT) 作为其视觉支柱。 ViT 架构将图像表示为一系列固定大小的补丁，将 Transformer 自注意力机制应用于模型这些补丁之间的交互。虽然这支持全局上下文化视觉特征，但固定大小的补丁标记化通常过于粗糙，无法保留对流程图拓扑至关重要的高频几何信号。具体来说，编码控制流方向的细连接器和小箭头经常在补丁边界上变得支离破碎。这种离散化会导致合并的视觉特征失去显着性，从而使生成的图模型容易丢失或不正确的边缘。Many state-of-the-art VLMs utilize a Vision Transformer (ViT) as their visual backbone. ViT architectures represent an image as a sequence of fixed-size patches, applying Transformer self-attention to model interactions among these patches. While this supports globally contextualized visual features, fixed-size patch tokenization is often too coarse to preserve the high-frequency geometric signals critical for flowchart topology. Specifically, thin connectors and small arrowheads, which encode control-flow direction, frequently become fragmented across patch boundaries. This discretization leads to a loss of salience in the pooled visual features, making the resulting graph models prone to missing or incorrect edges.

Ⅱ-BII-B Canny 边缘检测和结构先验Canny Edge Detection and Structural Priors

边缘检测是一种经典的计算机视觉操作，可突出显示强烈强度变化的位置，生成强调边界和细线结构的边缘图。 Canny 检测器是一种广泛采用的方法，它 (i) 平滑图像，(ii) 计算强度梯度，(iii) 应用非极大值抑制来定位边缘，以及 (iv) 使用滞后阈值保留相干的边缘轮廓，同时抑制噪声Edge detection is a classical computer vision operation that highlights locations of strong intensity change, producing an edge map that emphasizes boundaries and thin line structures. The Canny detector is a widely adopted method that (i) smooths the image, (ii) computes intensity gradients, (iii) applies non-maximum suppression to localize edges, and (iv) uses hysteresis thresholding to retain coherent edge contours while suppressing noise[6].

由于流程图主要通过线段（连接器）和箭头对控制流进行编码，Canny 边缘图可以将这些拓扑关键特征与周围的视觉内容（颜色、填充、文本）隔离开来。在计算机视觉和机器学习文献中，这种与偏差模型推理的主要输入一起提供的确定性、领域信息信号被称为As flowcharts encode control-flow primarily through line segments (connectors) and arrowheads, Canny edge maps can isolate these topology-critical features from the surrounding visual content (colors, fills, text). In the computer vision and machine learning literature, such a deterministic, domain-informed signal that is supplied alongside a primary input to bias model inference is called a结构先验structural prior [23]。 EdgeFlow 将相同的原理应用于 RE 上下文中的流程图处理。部分. EdgeFlow adapts the same principle to flowchart processing in an RE context.
