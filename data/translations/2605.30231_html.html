超越 3D VQA：将 3D 空间先验注入视觉语言模型以增强几何推理Beyond 3D VQAs: Injecting 3D Spatial Priors into Vision-Language Models for Enhanced Geometric Reasoning

Chun-Hsiao Yeh    Shengyi Qian    Manchen Wang    Yi Ma    Joseph Tighe    Fanyi Xiao [ [ [
(2026 年 5 月 28 日May 28, 2026)
摘要Abstract

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 常常难以应对强大的 3D 空间推理。依赖于 3D 视觉问答 (VQA) 数据集微调的流行方法可能会过度拟合数据集特定的偏差，而集成专门的 3D 视觉编码器通常不灵活且繁琐。在本文中，我们认为真正的空间理解应该来自学习基本的几何先验，而不仅仅是来自高级 VQA 监督。我们建议Vision-Language Models (VLMs) often struggle with robust 3D spatial reasoning. Prevailing methods that rely on fine-tuning with 3D visual question-answering (VQA) datasets may overfit dataset-specific biases, while integrating specialized 3D visual encoders is often inflexible and cumbersome. In this paper, we argue that genuine spatial understanding should emerge from learning fundamental geometric priors, not only from high-level VQA supervision. We proposeGASP（几何感知空间先验）GASP (Geometric-Aware Spatial Priors)，一个将这些先验直接注入到 LLM 的转换器层中的框架。 GASP 采用小型对应头，作为跨所有层的深度监督信号，并利用大型视频场景中的地面实况几何进行双重目标训练：地面实况点对应的对比损失增强了 2D 视图不变性，而深度一致性监督则解决了 3D 几何模糊性。我们的分析首先提供了一个诊断结果，显示标准 VLM 的内部对应匹配准确度非常低（通常低于 5%）。然后，我们证明我们的训练极大地改善了这种行为，将峰值逐层对应性提高到 70% 以上，并保持超过 85% 的时间鲁棒性，而基线仍低于 5%。这些内部改进转化为下游空间基准的显着提升，包括 All-Angles Bench 上的 +18.2% 和 VSI-Bench 上的 +29.0%，所有这些都无需对任何 3D VQA 数据进行训练。我们的研究结果表明，从基本几何先验中学习是实现具有更可靠 3D 空间推理的 VLM 的一条有前途且可推广的途径。, a framework that injects these priors directly into the LLM’s transformer layers. GASP employs a small correspondence head, applied as a deep supervision signal across all layers, and is trained with a dual objective leveraging ground-truth geometry from large-scale video scenes: a contrastive loss on ground-truth point correspondences enforces 2D view-invariance, while a depth consistency supervision resolves 3D geometric ambiguities. Our analysis first provides a diagnostic showing that standard VLMs’ internal correspondence matching accuracy is very low (often below 5%). We then demonstrate that our training substantially improves this behavior, boosting peak layer-wise correspondence to over 70% and maintaining over 85% temporal robustness while baselines remain below 5%. These internal improvements translate to significant gains on downstream spatial benchmarks including +18.2% on All-Angles Bench and +29.0% on VSI-Bench, all without training on any 3D VQA data. Our findings indicate that learning from fundamental geometric priors is a promising and generalizable pathway towards VLMs with more reliable 3D spatial reasoning.

1 简介Introduction

执行强大的空间推理的能力是人工智能的基石，使智能体能够理解、导航复杂的现实世界并与之交互The ability to perform robust spatial reasoning is a cornerstone of artificial intelligence, enabling agents to understand, navigate, and interact with the complex real world[41, 43]。近年来，视觉语言模型（VLM）在多模态理解和推理方面表现出了卓越的能力. In recent years, Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in multimodal understanding and reasoning[28, 21, 1, 45, 3, 8]，然而他们对空间概念的掌握仍然是一个重大挑战, yet their grasp of spatial concepts remains a significant challenge[20, 11, 63, 26, 36, 31]。 解决这一限制的主要范例是在广泛的 3D 视觉问答 (VQA) 数据集上微调这些模型. A dominant paradigm to address this limitation involves fine-tuning these models on extensive 3D visual question-answering (VQA) datasets[37, 44, 9, 51, 53, 64, 69, 54]。 尽管在一定程度上有效，但这些 VQA 对上的监督微调 (SFT) 和强化学习 (RL) 等训练后策略通常会鼓励模型学习表面相关性并记住数据集特定的偏差， 导致对未见过的场景的概括性较差。例如，如实验所示. Although effective to some extent, post-training strategies such as supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) on these VQA pairs often encourage models to learn superficial correlations and memorize dataset-specific biases, leading to poor generalization on unseen scenarios. For example, as the experiments shown in[44]，像 VILASR 这样的专业模型, specialized models like VILASR[54], 空间MLLM, SpatialMLLM[53]和 VG-LLM, and VG-LLM[67]在 VSI-Bench 等域内基准测试中显示出巨大的性能提升show huge performance boosts on in-domain benchmark like VSI-Bench[60]微调后。然而，这些模型在域外空间基准（例如 MMSI-Bench）上表现出一致的性能下降after fine-tuning. However, these models show a consistent performance drop on out-of-domain spatial benchmarks such as MMSI-Bench[61], STI-基准, STI-Bench[29]、和太空远景, and SpaceVista[44]。另类作品. An alternative line of works[12, 67, 53]寻求通过集成专门的视觉编码器（例如 VGGT）来提取 3D 空间信息seek to extract 3D spatial information by integrating specialized visual encoders such as the VGGT[49]模型，或使用点云等直接 3D 输入model, or by using direct 3D inputs like point clouds[7], 预先分割的对象, pre-segmented objects[52, 19]或 BEV 地图or BEV maps[39]。 然而，这条道路存在很大的实际局限性。这些预先训练的空间编码器很麻烦，会增加模型大小和推理延迟。此外，它们通常必须“按原样”使用（即冻结权重），因为它们的专门 3D 训练数据和管道与标准 VLM 训练不兼容。这就产生了一个具有挑战性的集成问题，迫使模型将其本机视觉表示与这些严格的、预先计算的 3D 特征对齐。. However, this path presents significant practical limitations. These pre-trained spatial encoders are cumbersome, increasing model size and inference latency. Furthermore, they must typically be used “as is” (i.e., with frozen weights) because their specialized 3D training data and pipelines are incompatible with standard VLM training. This creates a challenging integration problem, forcing the model to align its native visual representations with these rigid, pre-computed 3D features.

在这项工作中，我们背离了这两种流行的范式。In this work, we depart from both of these prevailing paradigms.我们认为，强大的空间智能源于学习 3D 几何的基本感知信号。We argue that robust spatial intelligence emerges from learning the fundamental perceptual signals of 3D geometry.我们假设真正的空间理解是以在不断变化的视角之间建立视觉对应的能力为基础的。我们的目标不是教模型将文本与视觉模式关联起来，而是教它世界本身的基本几何一致性。学习这种对象恒常性会鼓励模型构建内部的、视图不变的表示We hypothesize that true spatial understanding is underpinned by the ability to establish visual correspondences across changing viewpoints. Rather than teaching a model to associate text with visual patterns, our goal is to teach it the underlying geometric consistency of the world itself. Learning this object constancy encourages the model to build an internal, view-invariant representation[27, 38, 34]，为下游空间推理任务提供更通用的基础。, providing a more generalizable foundation for downstream spatial reasoning tasks.

Our method introduces a lightweight correspondence head inserted across all transformer layers to receive deep supervision signal. This forces geometric consistency to be maintained at every stage of the model’s feature representation. This head is trained with a dual objective leveraging ground-truth geometric priors from the large-scale video scenes[30]：首先，跨帧点对应数据的对比学习目标向模型传授以下核心原理：: First, a contrastive learning objective on point correspondence data across frames teaches the model the core principle of物体恒常性object constancy，迫使它学习视图不变的二维表示。其次，深度一致性损失利用真实深度图作为关键的几何正则化器，通过匹配深度值来解决 3D 模糊性（即前景-背景匹配混乱）。至关重要的是，该对应头仅在训练阶段有效，并且在推理时被完全丢弃。, forcing it to learn view-invariant 2D representations. Second, a depth consistency loss leverages ground-truth depth maps as a crucial geometric regularizer to resolve 3D ambiguities (i.e., foreground-background matching confusion) through matching depth values. Crucially, this correspondence head is only active during the training phase and is discarded entirely for inference.

我们通过广泛的消融实验验证了我们方法的有效性。 我们为 VLM 的主干提供了一种新颖的视觉对应匹配分析，并揭示了 GASP 显着改善了 VLM 的内部几何表示，无论是显着提高的对应匹配分数，还是在长时间范围内保持鲁棒匹配的能力。 此外，我们还证明这些内部改进可以推广到高级推理。我们的 GASP 通过将全角度基准上的相机姿态估计提高了 18.2%，在下游空间推理基准上实现了显着的性能提升We validate the effectiveness of our approach through extensive ablation experiments. We provide a novel visual correspondence matching analysis for VLM’s backbones, and reveal that GASP dramatically improves the VLM’s internal geometric representations, in terms of both the significantly improved correspondence matching scores, as well as its capability to maintain robust matching across long temporal range. Moreover, we also demonstrate that these internal improvements generalize to high-level reasoning. Our GASP achieves significant performance gains on downstream spatial reasoning benchmarks by improving camera pose estimation by +18.2% on the All-Angles Bench[62]，VSI-Bench 上的对象计数增加了 29.0%, object counting by +29.0% on VSI-Bench[60]，以及 BLINK 上的多视图推理 +15.0%, and multi-view reasoning by +15.0% on BLINK[15]。我们的贡献总结如下：. 2 相关作品Related Works

3D 感知 VLM。3D-Aware VLMs.最近的工作重点是使 MLLM 能够理解 3D 场景Recent efforts have focused on enabling MLLMs to understand 3D scenes[7, 52, 19, 18, 68, 17, 14, 39, 23, 59]。主要方法处理显式 3D 数据，例如点云特征. A dominant approach processes explicit 3D data, such as point cloud features[7]或预先分割的 3D 对象or pre-segmented 3D objects[52, 19]。另一种策略是投射多视图图像. Another strategy projects multi-view images[56]进入明确的空间表示，例如体素空间into explicit spatial representations, like voxel space[68]或 BEV 地图or BEV maps[39]。其他工作使用双编码器架构或接地代理将 3D 几何特征与 2D 语义特征融合. Other work uses dual-encoder architectures or grounding agents to fuse 3D geometry features with 2D semantic features[12, 67, 58]。一个共同点是它们对显式 3D 数据的依赖，这带来了重大的对齐挑战，因为法学硕士必须集成新的、严格的特征流。相比之下，我们的工作提出了一种更轻量级的替代方案，避免显式 3D 数据输入和双编码器融合。相反，我们将几何先验直接注入现有 LLM 主干的中间层，以在其自身表示中找到 3D 一致性。. A common thread is their reliance on explicit 3D data, which poses a significant alignment challenge, as the LLM must integrate a new, rigid feature stream. In contrast, our work proposes a more lightweight alternative, avoiding explicit 3D data inputs and dual-encoder fusion. We instead inject geometric priors directly into the intermediate layers of the existing LLM backbone to find 3D consistency within its own representations.

VLM 中的空间推理。Spatial Reasoning in VLMs.VLM 在复杂空间推理中面临重大挑战VLMs face significant challenges in complex spatial reasoning[12, 37, 67, 44, 9, 51, 53, 69, 54, 6]。由 VSI-Bench 等基准测试推动. Catalyzed by benchmarks like VSI-Bench[60]，出现了一个主导范式：创建大规模、3D 相关的 VQA 数据集, a dominant paradigm emerged: creating large-scale, 3D-related VQA datasets[37, 51, 12, 64, 44]为专用模型提供燃料to fuel specialized models[54, 67, 53, 12]通过微调。这种依赖可能会鼓励 VLM 学习表面相关性并记住数据集特定的偏差，从而导致泛化能力较差。相比之下，我们的工作背离了这种基于 VQA 的监督，而是将基本几何先验（对应性和深度一致性）直接注入到 VLM 的内部表示中。via fine-tuning. This reliance may encourage VLMs to learn superficial correlations and memorize dataset-specific biases, leading to poor generalization. In contrast, our work departs from this VQA-based supervision, instead injecting fundamental geometric priors (correspondence and depth consistency) directly into the VLM’s internal representations.
