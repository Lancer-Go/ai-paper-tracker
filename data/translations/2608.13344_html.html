LongEarth-R1：用于长视野地球观测推理的基准测试和调整视觉语言模型LongEarth-R1: Benchmarking and Aligning Vision-Language Models for Long-Horizon Earth Observation Reasoning

Yupan Ding    Jing Xiao    Zhenyuan Zhang    Chaofeng Chen    Liang Liao    Gui-Song Xia    Mi Wang
摘要Abstract

长视距地球观测推理需要模型来组织多阶段地理演化、定位空间变化、检测时间异常并从扩展图像序列推断未来。然而，现有的遥感视觉语言模型主要关注孤立图像、图像对或短序列，限制了相关帧和区域的可靠基础。我们引入了 LongEarth-Bench，这是一个基准，包含从 117k 个独特图像中提取的大约 120k 个问答样本。其序列平均 15.14 帧，扩展到 30 帧，涵盖进化总结、空间推理、异常识别和逻辑预测等 12 个任务。 30k 样本子集进一步提供将关键帧和更改区域与最终答案联系起来的结构化推理轨迹。我们通过显式序列标识符和结构化思想链监督的监督微调来开发 LongEarth。 LongEarth-R1 以 LongEarth 为基础，应用了具有格式、时间和空间奖励的群体相对策略优化。 LongEarth-R1 在所有 12 项长序列任务上均取得了最佳结果，同时在标准遥感基准上保持竞争力。Long-horizon Earth observation reasoning requires models to organize multi-stage geographic evolution, localize spatial changes, detect temporal anomalies, and infer future from extended image sequences. However, existing remote sensing vision-language models mainly focus on isolated images, image pairs, or short sequences, limiting reliable grounding in the relevant frames and regions. We introduce LongEarth-Bench, a benchmark containing approximately 120k question-answering samples derived from 117k unique images. Its sequences average 15.14 frames and extend to 30 frames, covering 12 tasks across evolution summarization, spatial reasoning, anomaly identification, and logical prediction. A 30k-sample subset further provides structured reasoning traces linking key frames and changed regions to final answers. We develop LongEarth through supervised fine-tuning with explicit sequence identifiers and structured chain-of-thought supervision. Building on LongEarth, LongEarth-R1 applies group relative policy optimization with format, temporal, and spatial rewards. LongEarth-R1 achieves the best results on all 12 long-sequence tasks while remaining competitive on standard remote sensing benchmarks.

简介Introduction

遥感分析日益超越孤立的观测，转向了解地理区域如何随时间演变Remote sensing analysis is increasingly moving beyond isolated observations toward understanding how geographic regions evolve over time(37)。随着高重访地球观测图像的不断增加，现在可以观察城市建设、灾害演变和生态系统恢复等多阶段过程. With the growing availability of high-revisit Earth observation imagery, it is now possible to observe multi-stage processes such as urban construction, disaster evolution, and ecosystem recovery(19; 10; 11)。例如，根据洪水之前、期间和之后的观测结果，模型应识别其爆发和受影响区域，跟踪其演变并推断恢复阶段。这需要的不仅仅是成对的变化检测：模型必须恢复时间轨迹，定位支撑框架和区域，并将地理演化与季节性和采集引起的变化分开. For example, given observations before, during, and after a flood, a model should identify its onset and affected regions, track its evolution, and infer the recovery stage. This requires more than pairwise change detection: the model must recover a temporal trajectory, localize supporting frames and regions, and separate geographic evolution from seasonal and acquisition-induced variations(17; 29; 23)。我们将此功能称为. We refer to this capability as长视距地球观测推理long-horizon Earth observation reasoning：从扩展的地球观测序列中推理多阶段地理演化。: reasoning about multi-stage geographic evolution from extended Earth observation sequences.

最近的遥感视觉语言模型（RSVLM）已逐步将语言引导的解释从个体观察扩展到图像对和时间序列。单时间模型通过图像描述、视觉问答和视觉基础将个体观察与语言联系起来Recent remote sensing vision-language models (RSVLMs) have progressively extended language-guided interpretation from individual observations to image pairs and temporal sequences. Single-temporal models connect an individual observation with language through image description, visual question answering, and visual grounding(45; 9)。双时态模型进一步比较配对观测值以描述和定位地理变化. Bi-temporal models further compare paired observations to describe and localize geographic changes(40; 18)。最近的时间 RSVLM 接受多个观察并支持序列级对话或改变理解. More recent temporal RSVLMs accept multiple observations and support sequence-level dialogue or change understanding(10; 29; 39)。然而，这些能力仍然不足以进行长期推理。单个图像不包含进化轨迹，图像对主要暴露变化的端点，当前的序列模型通常强调识别或描述性问题回答，而不是重建完整的多阶段过程。因此，它们在追踪关键转变、跨框架和区域得出结论以及推断演化过程如何继续方面仍然受到限制。. Nevertheless, these capabilities are still insufficient for long-horizon reasoning. A single image contains no evolution trajectory, an image pair exposes mainly the endpoints of change, and current sequence models generally emphasize recognition or descriptive question answering rather than reconstructing a complete multi-stage process. They consequently remain limited in tracing critical transitions, grounding conclusions across frames and regions, and inferring how an evolving process may continue.

为了解决这些限制，我们将长视野地球观测推理制定为对扩展序列的推理。这种能力需要模型来识别地理实体如何演化、定位和表征其变化、评估观察到的演化是否在时间上一致，并推断未观察到的状态。因此，我们将其分为四个渐进的认知维度：1）To address these limitations, we formulate long-horizon Earth observation reasoning as reasoning over extended sequences. This capability requires models to identify how geographic entities evolve, localize and characterize their changes, assess whether the observed evolution is temporally consistent, and infer unobserved states. We therefore organize it into four progressive cognitive dimensions: 1)进化总结Evolution summarization将主要阶段组织成连贯的时间轨迹； 2）organizes major stages into coherent temporal trajectories; 2)空间推理Spatial reasoning定位变化并对其不断变化的空间关系进行建模； 3）localizes changes and models their evolving spatial relations; 3)异常识别Anomaly identification检测时间顺序违规、重复和上下文干扰；和 4)detects chronological violations, repetitions, and contextual interference; and 4)逻辑预测Logical prediction从积累的证据推断后续或缺失的状态。图infers subsequent or missing states from accumulated evidence. 方法Method

本节介绍了长时空推理的两阶段框架。如图This section presents a two-stage framework for long-horizon spatiotemporal reasoning. As shown in Figure4，框架基于Qwen2.5-VL-7B构建, the framework is built on Qwen2.5-VL-7B(2)。第一阶段应用有监督微调（SFT）和两种互补形式的监督。序列感知答案监督使用显式序列标识符来建立稳定的帧级时间锚点，而结构化 CoT 监督则教导模型选择相关观察并整合整个序列的时间和空间证据，从而产生 LongEarth。第 2 阶段从 LongEarth 初始化，并应用具有互补格式、时间和空间奖励的 GRPO 来改进推理结构和时空一致性，产生 LongEarth-R1。. Stage 1 applies supervised fine-tuning (SFT) with two complementary forms of supervision. Sequence-aware answer supervision uses explicit sequence identifiers to establish stable frame-level temporal anchors, while structured CoT supervision teaches the model to select relevant observations and integrate temporal and spatial evidence across the sequence, yielding LongEarth. Stage 2 initializes from LongEarth and applies GRPO with complementary format, temporal, and spatial rewards to improve reasoning structure and spatiotemporal consistency, yielding LongEarth-R1.

有监督的序列基础和推理Supervised Sequence Grounding and Reasoning

第一阶段包含两个监督组件。第一个组件执行序列感知答案监督，使基本模型适应长期遥感输入并建立帧级时间锚点。第二个组件注入结构化推理痕迹，进一步指导模型在产生最终答案之前组织跨框架证据。The first stage contains two supervised components. The first component performs sequence-aware answer supervision, which adapts the base model to long-term remote sensing inputs and establishes frame-level temporal anchors. The second component injects structured reasoning traces, which further guides the model to organize cross-frame evidence before producing the final answer.

结论Conclusion

长期的遥感理解需要对不断变化的地理证据进行推理，而不是孤立的观察。我们提出了 LongEarth-Bench，它用四个认知维度和结构化推理监督来定义这种设置。我们通过监督序列基础进一步开发 LongEarth，并通过奖励驱动的时空对齐进一步开发 LongEarth-R1。结果提高了 12 个长序列任务的性能，同时保留了向传统遥感任务的转移。这些发现支持长期地球观测推理的时间顺序、中间证据和空间一致性的明确建模。Long-term remote sensing understanding requires reasoning over evolving geographic evidence rather than isolated observations. We present LongEarth-Bench, which defines this setting with four cognitive dimensions and structured reasoning supervision. We further develop LongEarth through supervised sequence grounding and LongEarth-R1 through reward-driven spatiotemporal alignment. Results improve performance across the 12 long-sequence tasks while retaining transfer to conventional remote sensing tasks. These findings support explicit modeling of temporal order, intermediate evidence, and spatial consistency for long-horizon Earth observation reasoning.
