包含集合的不确定性建模Set-Inclusive Uncertainty Modeling
用于稳健的脑肿瘤分割for Robust Brain Tumor Segmentation

Seunghun Baek∗    Jihwan Park∗    Jaeyoon Sim    Hoseok Lee   
Seungjoo Lee    Won Hwa Kim
摘要Abstract

多模态 MRI 对于准确的脑肿瘤分割至关重要。 然而，在实践中获取所有推理模式往往具有挑战性， 由于不可避免的信息丢失而导致内在的不确定性。 如果不对这种不确定性进行建模，现有方法会将不完整的证据编码为看似合理但缺乏可靠性的确定性表示。 在这种情况下，我们提出了一个概率表示框架，将表示建模为高斯分布， 他们的均值捕获任务信息，方差衡量缺失证据的不确定性。 为了使方差反映信息的缺乏， 我们将每个部分配置的均值正规化为其全模态对应物， 同时根据对齐均值之间的差异来缩放方差。 我们进一步引入了一种包含集合的策略，该策略利用模态子集的层次结构并强制执行排序约束以维持其一致的不确定性关系。 BraTS 2018 和 2020 的大量实验表明，我们的方法在不同的缺失模态场景中提供了优于基线的性能。 代码和模型检查点可在 https://github.com/atlas-sky/SIUM 获取。Multimodal MRI is essential for accurate brain tumor segmentation. However, acquiring all modalities at inference is often challenging in practice, which causes intrinsic uncertainty due to unavoidable information loss. Without modeling this uncertainty, existing methods encode incomplete evidence into deterministic representations that appear plausible but lack reliability. In this regime, we propose a probabilistic representation framework that models representations as Gaussian distributions, where their mean captures task information and their variance measures uncertainty from missing evidence. To make variance reflect information deficiency, we regularize the mean from each partial configuration toward its full-modality counterpart, while scaling the variance with the discrepancy between their aligned means. We further introduce a set-inclusive strategy that exploits the hierarchical structure of modality subsets and enforces an ordering constraint to maintain their consistent uncertainty relationships. Extensive experiments on BraTS 2018 and 2020 demonstrate that our approach offers superior performance over baselines across diverse missing-modality scenarios. Code and model checkpoint are available at https://github.com/atlas-sky/SIUM.

1 简介Introduction

脑肿瘤分割对于评估疾病进展和治疗计划至关重要Brain tumor segmentation is critical for assessing disease progression and treatment planning[3, 5, 17]。 在临床实践中， 使用四种标准磁共振成像 (MRI) 模式：T1、T1c、T2 和 FLAIR. In clinical practice, four standard magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) modalities are utilized: T1, T1c, T2, and FLAIR[10, 11], 为描绘肿瘤亚区域提供补充信息， 包括全肿瘤（WT）、肿瘤核心（TC）和增强肿瘤（ET）, which provide complementary information for delineating tumor subregions, including whole tumor (WT), tumor core (TC), and enhancing tumor (ET)[4, 6, 11, 17]。 由于没有一种方法可以完全捕获每个肿瘤亚区域， 准确划定需要各种方式的联合整合. Since no single modality fully captures every tumor subregion, accurate delineation requires the joint integration of all modalities[12, 18]。 然而， 由于成本和患者负担，获得所有这些通常是不切实际的. However, acquiring all of them is often impractical due to cost and patient burden[1, 2]。 由于脑肿瘤分割依赖于多种模式的补充证据， 当某种方式缺失时， 其独特的信息变得无法访问。. As brain tumor segmentation relies on the complementary evidence from multiple modalities, when a modality goes missing, its unique information becomes inaccessible.

为了解决潜在的不完整性， 以前的作品To address potential incompleteness, previous works[8, 14, 15, 21]训练他们的框架在缺失模态的情况下保持稳健。 他们将每种模态配置编码为确定性嵌入，以表示与任务相关的信息。 虽然缺失会在最终的嵌入中引入不可避免的信息丢失， 确定性方法仍然更新其预测模型，而不考虑信息不完整性（即不确定性）。 因此，模型可能依赖于缺乏证据的幻觉特征。train their frameworks to be robust across missing-modality scenarios. They encode each modality configuration into a deterministic embedding to represent task-relevant information. While the absence introduces unavoidable information loss in the resulting embedding, deterministic approaches still update their prediction models without considering informational incompleteness (i.e., uncertainty). Consequently, the models may rely on hallucinated features that lack evidence.

在这种情况下，我们将每个配置建模为高斯分布，其中均值编码与任务相关的信息，方差捕获不确定性。 该设计承认缺失的模式会导致内在信息缺乏， 这应该反映为不确定性，而不是隐藏在确定性嵌入中。 为了使方差编码不确定性，我们利用模态子集的层次结构， 其中较小的集合嵌套在较大的集合中。在这个包含集合的层次结构中，当子集的均值偏离其全模态对应物时，方差就会增加， 因为偏差表明证据缺失。 它被进一步正则化，以保持子集比其超集更高，这反映了它们降低的信息完整性。 通过不确定性指导，该模型可以实现可靠的预测，优先考虑跨配置的有充分支持的证据。In this regime, we model each configuration as a Gaussian distribution, where the mean encodes task-relevant information and the variance captures uncertainty. This design acknowledges that missing modalities cause intrinsic information deficiency, which should be reflected as uncertainty rather than concealed within a deterministic embedding. To make the variance encode uncertainty, we exploit the hierarchical structure of modality subsets, where smaller sets are nested within larger ones. Within this set-inclusive hierarchy, the variance is encouraged to increase when the mean of a subset deviates from its full-modality counterpart, since deviation indicates missing evidence. It is further regularized to remain higher for subsets than for their supersets, which reflects their reduced informational completeness. Through uncertainty guidance, the model enables reliable predictions that prioritize well-supported evidence across configurations.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们通过不完整的多模态脑肿瘤分割的概率框架来解决由缺失模态引起的内在不确定性。 这种设计明确减少了对虚假线索的依赖，从而产生更可靠的预测。 BraTS 2018 和 2020 的大量实验证明了其在缺失模态场景下的鲁棒性。 理论和实证分析都支持我们的不确定性建模的必要性。In this work, we address the intrinsic uncertainty induced by missing modalities through a probabilistic framework for incomplete multimodal brain tumor segmentation. This design explicitly reduces reliance on spurious cues, thereby yielding more reliable predictions. Extensive experiments on BraTS 2018 and 2020 demonstrate robustness across missing-modality scenarios. Both theoretical and empirical analyses support the necessity of our uncertainty modeling.
