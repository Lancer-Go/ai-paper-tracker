摘要Abstract

自然的计算机使用痕迹、被动记录的屏幕截图以及鼠标或键盘操作，是导出日常工作如何完成的符号、可审计和可重用模型的宝贵资源。 当计算机使用的代理进入实际工作时，此类模型很重要，代理需要了解任务的实际执行方式，而组织需要审核和重用这些知识。然而，引入这样的任务模型具有挑战性，因为活动仅作为低级事件被观察到，而现实世界的工作是具有交错目标的多线程。现有方法假设给定的任务或单个工作流程，并生成步骤级摘要而不是结构化任务模型。 我们介绍Naturalistic computer-use traces, passively recorded screenshots and mouse or keyboard actions, are a valuable resource for deriving symbolic, auditable, and reusable models of how everyday work is done. Such models matter as computer-use agents enter real work, where agents need to learn how tasks are actually performed, and organizations need to audit and reuse that knowledge. However, inducing such task models is challenging, as activity is observed only as low-level events and real-world work is multi-threaded with interleaved goals. Existing methods assume a given task or a single workflow, and produce step-level summaries rather than structured task models. We introduce任务模型归纳Task Model Induction(TMI)，它 (i) 在不受约束的跟踪中发现潜在任务，解开并发活动，并且 (ii) 对于每个潜在任务，引入一个任务模型，将递归目标分解的分层目标模型与组织执行的控制流的过程模型配对。本质上，在受控的人类和代理轨迹上，TMI 恢复了交错任务，与真实分组的一致性为 0.974，并重建了 74.9% 的观察到的执行步骤，远远超过了最强的工作流归纳基线。从外部来看，源自 TMI 任务模型的技能将保留任务的准确性比最强基线提高了 30.0%。(TMI), which (i) discovers the latent tasks in an unconstrained trace, disentangling concurrent activity, and (ii) for each latent task, induces a task model pairing a hierarchical objective model of recursive goal decomposition with a procedure model of the control flow that organized the execution. Intrinsically, on controlled human and agent trajectories, TMI recovers interleaved tasks with 0.974 agreement against ground-truth groupings and reconstructs 74.9% of the observed execution steps, far more than the strongest workflow induction baseline. 从行为中诱导表征Inducing representation from behavior

计划识别从观察到的行动推断目标，但前提是计划库或领域理论Plan recognition infers goals from observed actions but presupposes a plan library or domain theory(Kautz et al. 1986)，而流程挖掘则从已经包含类型化活动和案例标识符的事件日志中发现程序模型, while process mining discovers procedural models from event logs that already contain typed activities and case identifiers(Van Der Aalst et al. 2011)。最近的计算机使用跟踪工作将像素融入语义操作. We drop these assumptions, inducing semantic actions, latent tasks, objective hierarchies, and procedures jointly from raw screen-and-input traces.

多任务处理和交错Multitasking and interleaving

实地和认知研究表明，知识工作者不断地在工作领域中交叉目标，并且在转换时会产生可衡量的成本Field and cognitive studies show that knowledge workers continuously interleave goals across working spheres and incur measurable costs when switching(Czerwinski et al. 2004; González and Mark 2004; Mark et al. 2008; Iqbal and Horvitz 2007; Salvucci et al. 2009)，事件分割理论将活动框架为从目标和状态变化推断出的分层组织的边界, and event segmentation theory frames activity as hierarchically organized boundaries inferred from goal and state changes(Zacks and Tversky 2001)。关于推断用户意图中的基础代理记忆的研究进一步表明，必须对潜在意图进行建模才能理解交错的活动. Work on grounding agent memory in inferred user intent further argues that latent intent must be modeled to make sense of interleaved activity(Yang et al. 2026)。这些发现为我们选择将任务身份视为潜在的并允许非连续的任务跨度奠定了基础，以便在任务归纳期间解决交错活动，而不是将其带入过程模型。. These findings ground our choice to treat task identity as latent and to allow non-contiguous task spans, so that interleaved activity is resolved during task induction rather than carried into the procedure model.

7 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced任务模型归纳Task Model Induction，它将原始计算机使用事件抽象为活动，发现它们实现的潜在任务，并将分别归纳的目标和过程模型协调为每个任务的一个任务模型。它将日常计算机使用活动痕迹转化为持久的、可审计的工作执行记录。实验表明，这种表示忠实地恢复了交错执行，并且比原始跟踪或工作流摘要产生更好的下游代理技能。 TMI 有潜力促进跨领域工作如何进行的研究，并使这些记录可作为人员和代理的知识重复使用。, which abstracts raw computer-use events into activities, discovers the latent tasks they realize, and reconciles separately induced objective and procedure models into one task model per task. It turns everyday computer-use activity traces into durable, auditable records of how work are carried out. Experiments show that this representation recovers interleaved execution faithfully and yields better downstream agent skills than raw traces or workflow summaries. TMI has the potential to facilitate the study of how work is carried out across domains and to make these records reusable as knowledge for both people and agents.

局限性Limitations

TMI 基于自然计算机使用痕迹进行操作，其中可能包含个人身份信息。将此方法应用于原始计算机使用跟踪的未来部署应考虑在归纳之前对屏幕截图和键盘事件进行隐私编辑，以便私人和敏感内容不会传播到为下游用途（例如技能学习）分发的诱导工件中。研究这种编辑对归纳质量的影响留待未来的工作。TMI operates on naturalistic computer-use traces that may contain personally identifiable information. Future deployments that apply this method to raw computer-use traces should consider privacy redaction of screenshots and keyboard events before induction, so that private and sensitive content does not propagate into induced artifacts distributed for downstream uses such as skill learning. Studying the effect of such redaction on the induction quality is left to future work.
