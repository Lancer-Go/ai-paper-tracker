摘要Abstract

1 简介Introduction

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 的最新进展Recent progress in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)[liu2023visual, bai2025qwen2, alayrac2022flamingo]展示了他们在各种实际应用中的卓越能力，包括视觉探索has revealed their remarkable abilities across diverse practical applications, including visual exploration[lou2025explorevlm, luo2024vlai]、自动驾驶, autonomous driving[xu2024vlm, you2024v2x, sima2024drivelm]，遵循现实世界的指令, real-world instruction following[ji2025robobrain, lu2024gui]，以及具体化导航, and embodied navigation[zhang2025mem2ego, yokoyama2025film]。 VLM 的多功能性激发了人们的巨大研究兴趣，特别是在自动化室内安全检查这一新兴领域. The versatility of VLMs has catalyzed significant research interest, particularly in the emerging domain of automated indoor safety inspection[gao2025homesafebench, mullen2024don, hassan2024coherence, huang2025framework, yin2024safeagentbench]，它们提供了增强或取代传统人类评估的潜力。然而，当前基于 VLM 的方法在家庭安全危害评估方面表现出严重的局限性，这主要是由于对潜在家庭风险的领域知识不足。解决这一知识差距需要一个强大的评估框架，该框架既可以作为具有挑战性的测试平台，又可以作为丰富的领域知识来源。, where they offer the potential to augment or replace traditional human assessments. However, current VLM-based approaches exhibit critical limitations in home safety hazard assessment, primarily stemming from inadequate domain knowledge about potential domestic risks. Addressing this knowledge gap requires a robust evaluation framework that serves as both a challenging testbed and a rich source of domain knowledge.

现有的安全检查评估基准分为两类： 基于文本的方法Existing evaluation benchmarks for safety inspection fall into two categories: text-based approaches[yin2024safeagentbench, mullen2024don]将视觉数据转换为文本表示（例如，对象关系图），以便由纯法学硕士和基于视觉的方法进行处理that convert visual data into textual representations (e.g., object relationship graphs) for processing by pure LLMs, and vision-based methods[gao2025homesafebench, hassan2024coherence, huang2025framework]使用 Unity 3D 构建虚拟环境进行 VLM 训练。这些范例受到三个基本限制：（1）过度依赖模拟器的合成数据，造成巨大的现实差距，阻碍实际部署； (2) 情景多样性受到人为限制，危险类别受到限制，未能捕捉到现实世界中广泛的安全问题； (3)使用训练-测试重叠度高的静态图像的简单化评估协议，对模型泛化能力的评估不充分。that construct virtual environments using Unity 3D for VLM training. These paradigms suffer from three fundamental limitations: (1) over reliance on synthetic data from simulators, causing a substantial reality gap that impedes practical deployment; (2) constrained scenario diversity with artificially limited hazard categories, failing to capture the broad spectrum of real-world safety concerns; and (3) simplistic evaluation protocols using static images with high training-test overlap, providing inadequate assessment of model generalization.

Given that TSHA comprises both choice and QA questions, we employed an LLM to score the QA responses on a 1–100 scale, while using accuracy as the metric for choice questions. 2 相关工作Related Work

VLM 安全。VLM for Safety.视觉语言模型 (VLM) 越来越多地集成到安全关键型应用中，它们准确解释视觉内容、推理现实世界物理场景并生成可操作见解的能力对于预防潜在危险和减轻相关风险至关重要。这种变革潜力在大规模、公共和工业领域得到了最显着的实现，在这些领域中，可靠的感知和推理能力直接影响操作安全和人类福祉。例如，在自动驾驶领域，VLM作为强大态势感知的核心组件，使车辆能够感知复杂的道路环境，识别脆弱的道路使用者，并导航动态场景以避免碰撞Visual-Language Models (VLMs) are increasingly being integrated into safety-critical applications, where their capacity to accurately interpret visual content, reason about real-world physical scenarios, and generate actionable insights is pivotal to preventing potential hazards and mitigating associated risks. This transformative potential has been most prominently realized in large-scale, public, and industrial domains, where reliable perception and reasoning capabilities directly impact operational safety and human well-being. For instance, in the field of autonomous driving, VLMs serve as a core component for robust situational awareness, enabling vehicles to perceive complex road environments, identify vulnerable road users, and navigate dynamic scenarios to avoid collisions[xu2024vlm, you2024v2x]。在工业自动化中，VLM 为智能监控系统提供动力，持续检测不安全的工作条件、设备故障或操作异常，从而确保高风险制造和工业环境中工人的安全. In industrial automation, VLMs power intelligent monitoring systems that continuously detect unsafe working conditions, equipment malfunctions, or operational anomalies, thereby ensuring worker safety in high-risk manufacturing and industrial environments[wu2025monitorvlm]。此外，在灾难响应场景中，最近的研究探索利用 VLM 来指导实体代理在动态变化和危险环境（例如地震后或火灾影响地区）中做出实时、安全意识决策. Furthermore, in disaster response scenarios, recent research explores leveraging VLMs to guide embodied agents in making real-time, safety-aware decisions within dynamically changing and hazardous environments, such as post-earthquake or fire-affected areas[zhou2024hazard].

然而，虽然这些应用展示了 VLM 在不同场景中的潜力，但私人家庭安全的微妙且高度复杂的领域仍未得到充分探索。我们的工作解决了这一关键差距，重点是调整 VLM 功能以自动检查潜在的家庭危险。However, while these applications demonstrate the potential of VLMs in diverse scenarios, the nuanced and highly complex domain of private home safety remains significantly underexplored. Our work addresses this critical gap, focusing on adapting VLM capabilities for automated inspection of potential domestic hazards.

家庭危险评估基准。Home Hazard Assessment Benchmarks.现有的家庭危险检测基准采用基于文本或基于视觉的方法。基于文本的基准测试，例如 SafetyDetectExisting benchmarks for home hazard detection adopt either text-based or vision-based methodologies. Text-based benchmarks, such as SafetyDetect[mullen2024don]和安全贝阿尔and Safe BeAl[huang2025framework]，通过将视觉场景转换为结构化文本表示以进行分析，开创了这一领域。虽然是基础性的，但这种以文本为中心的方法造成了不可避免的信息瓶颈。具体来说，在图像到文本转换过程中，对于准确安全评估至关重要的细微视觉细节经常会丢失或过于简单化。这种信息丢失直接损害了识别模糊或可见性低的危险的能力，这在现实家庭环境中尤其常见。此外，其规模仍然有限，SafetyDetect 仅提供 3 个危险类别的 1,000 个样本。, pioneered this area by converting visual scenes into structured textual representations for analysis. Specifically, nuanced visual details that are critical for accurate safety assessment are often lost or oversimplified during the image-to-text conversion process. This information loss directly impairs the ability to identify ambiguous or low-visibility hazards, which are particularly common in real-world home environments. Moreover, their scale remains limited, with SafetyDetect offering 1,000 samples across only 3 hazard categories.

To sum up, these approaches are still constrained by a narrow scope of predefined hazard categories, and large-scale studies rely on synthetic data, leading to critical gaps between simulation and reality.

与之前的作品相比，TSHA 从根本上是为了提高现实性和复杂性而设计的。与依赖于模拟的工作不同，TSHA 优先考虑现实世界的数据源，并构建更接近日常家庭环境的安全场景，这对当前模型提出了更大的辨别挑战。In contrast to prior works, TSHA is fundamentally designed for greater realism and complexity. Unlike simulation-dependent work, TSHA prioritizes real-world data sources and constructs safety scenarios closer to everyday domestic environments, presenting significantly greater discrimination challenges for current models.

3.3 多模态对话生成。Multimodal Dialogue Generation.

我们采用 GPT-4oWe employ the GPT-4o[hurst2024gpt]模型通过精心设计的思维链推理过程生成上下文、多轮对话。我们的方法逐步引导模型经历四个不同的阶段：场景描述、初步危险识别和最终危险识别，确保系统可靠的危险分析。model to generate contextual, multi-turn dialogues through a carefully designed chain-of-thought reasoning process. Our methodology progressively guides the model through four distinct stages: Scene Description, Preliminary Hazards Identification, and Final Hazards Identification, ensuring systematic and reliable hazard analysis.

在场景描述阶段，模型被指示生成视觉环境的客观和全面的描述。提示明确指示模型“仔细观察这个室内环境图像”和“客观描述所有可见物体、它们的位置和空间关系”，同时明确禁止任何与安全相关的判断。此步骤为后续分析奠定了事实、无假设的基础。In the Scene Description phase, the model is instructed to generate an objective and comprehensive account of the visual environment. The prompt explicitly directs the model to “carefully observe this indoor environment image” and “objectively describe all visible objects, their positions, and spatial relationships,” while explicitly prohibiting any safety-related judgments. This step establishes a factual, assumption-free foundation for subsequent analysis.

在此描述的基础上，初步危险识别阶段对多个预定义的安全维度进行初步风险扫描，即电气安全、火灾危险、绊倒或跌倒风险、杂乱和障碍危险以及可能导致人身伤害或财产损失的其他情况。该模型系统地列举了潜在的危险，每种危险都附有将观察结果与其相关风险联系起来的简明理由，从而构建了初步的危险概况。Building on this description, the Preliminary Hazards Identification stage performs an initial risk scan across multiple predefined safety dimensions—namely, electrical safety, fire hazards, trip or fall risks, clutter and obstruction hazards, and other conditions that could lead to personal injury or property damage. The model systematically enumerates potential hazards, each accompanied by a concise rationale linking the observation to its associated risk, thereby constructing a preliminary hazard profile.

最终危险识别阶段对每个候选危险进行严格的、基于证据的验证。对于每个已识别的风险，模型必须：（1）引用图像中的具体视觉证据； (2) 指定置信水平（高/中/低）； (3) 区分真正的危险和良性的日常情景； (4) 应用特定领域的规则，特别是针对混乱和阻碍的规则，以确保解释的一致性。此阶段进一步结合了错误识别分析和置信度校准，以抑制误报，同时保持对真实危险的敏感性。The Final Hazards Identification phase enforces rigorous, evidence-based validation of each candidate hazard. For every identified risk, the model must: (1) cite specific visual evidence from the image; (2) assign a confidence level (high/medium/low); (3) differentiate genuine hazards from benign everyday scenarios; and (4) apply domain-specific rules—particularly for clutter and obstruction—to ensure consistent interpretation. This stage further incorporates misidentification analysis and confidence calibration to suppress false positives while preserving sensitivity to true hazards.

第四步包括人工验证。为了确保GPT-4o能够准确地识别和描述图像中的安全隐患，上述步骤采用了基于思想链的自我修正机制，使GPT-4o能够作为自动质量评估器进行自检和修正。为了进一步验证所得数据集的质量，我们由两名计算机科学博士生进行了人工验证。具体来说，我们采用随机抽样策略，随机抽取总样本的10%进行人工检查。结果表明，采样样本中的文本注释与图像中描绘的实际安全隐患高度一致，有效保证了最终数据集的可靠性和高质量。The fourth step consists of human verification. To ensure that GPT-4o can accurately identify and describe safety hazards within images, the preceding steps adopt a chain-of-thought-based self-correction mechanism, enabling GPT-4o to serve as an automatic quality assessor for self-inspection and revision. To further validate the quality of the resulting dataset, we conduct human verification performed by two PhD students in computer science. Specifically, we employ a random sampling strategy, where 10% of the total samples are randomly selected for manual inspection. The results demonstrate that the textual annotations in the sampled samples are highly consistent with the actual safety hazards depicted in the images, which effectively guarantees the reliability and high quality of the final dataset.

这个四阶段管道保持了严格的上下文连续性：每个推理步骤都明确地建立在先前的输出之上，形成一个连贯且可追踪的分析链。所有评估仍然基于可观察的视觉内容，避免推测性推断。最后，每个生成的示例都经过手动验证，以保证问答一致性和注释保真度。This four-stage pipeline maintains strict contextual continuity: each reasoning step explicitly builds upon the prior output, forming a coherent and traceable analytical chain. All assessments remain grounded in observable visual content, avoiding speculative inference. Finally, every generated example undergoes manual verification to guarantee question–answer consistency and annotation fidelity.

3.4 任务制定。Task Formulation.

为了系统地评估视觉语言模型识别安全隐患的能力，我们基于精心策划的图像-问题对设计了两个互补的推理任务。第一个任务是选择问题，包括两个子类型：二元对/错问题和四选项单选问题。在真/假变体中，每个问题都提出关于图像中是否存在危险的陈述，并且模型必须相应地选择“真”或“假”。单选变量评估模型识别和分类特定危险类型的能力。每个问题都提供四个相互排斥的选项，涵盖不同的危险类别或“无危险”选项，并且模型需要选择最能描述图像中描述的风险的标签。第二个任务是开放式问答，要求模型在没有任何预定义答案选择的情况下全面识别和描述图像或视频中的所有潜在安全隐患。与上述受限格式不同，该任务需要自发生成自然语言响应，这些响应不仅准确，而且简洁、连贯且语义完整。因此，它构成了更具挑战性的评估设置，共同评估危险检测准确性、描述保真度和推理清晰度。To systematically evaluate the capability of vision-language models in identifying safety hazards, we design two complementary inference tasks based on carefully curated image–question pairs. The first task, Choice Question, comprises two subtypes: binary true/false questions and four-option single-choice questions. In the true/false variant, each question presents a statement about the presence or absence of a hazard in the image, and the model must select “true” or “false” accordingly. The single-choice variant evaluates the model’s ability to recognize and categorize specific hazard types. Each question provides four mutually exclusive options covering distinct hazard categories or the option “no hazard”, and the model is required to select the label that best describes the risk depicted in the image. The second task, Open-ended Question Answering, requires the model to comprehensively identify and describe all potential safety hazards in an image or video without any predefined answer choices. Unlike the constrained formats above, this task demands spontaneous generation of natural language responses that are not only accurate but also concise, coherent, and semantically complete. Consequently, it constitutes a more challenging evaluation setting, jointly assessing hazard detection accuracy, descriptive fidelity, and reasoning clarity.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了 TSHA，这是评估 VLM 安全隐患评估能力的综合基准。 TSHA 汇总了来自四个互补来源的训练数据：现有室内数据集、互联网图像、AIGC 图像和新捕获的图像。测试集还包含 Sora2 和 Hunyuan 生成的样本。通过对不同的 VLM 进行广泛的实验，我们发现所有评估的模型在安全隐患评估方面都表现出明显的局限性——揭示了它们在室内任务准备方面的关键差距。通过系统地识别和分析这些缺点，我们的工作旨在提供可行的见解，推动 VLM 研究朝着稳健、安全意识的视觉理解方向发展，并促进此类模型在现实室内环境中的可靠部署。In this work, we introduce TSHA, the comprehensive benchmark for evaluating safety hazard assessment capabilities in VLMs. TSHA aggregates training data from four complementary sources: existing indoor datasets, internet images, AIGC images, and newly captured images. The test sets additinally incorporates samples generated by Sora2 and Hunyuan. Through extensive experiments across a diverse set of VLMs, we find that all evaluated models exhibit notable limitations in safety hazards assessments—revealing a critical gap in their readiness for indoor tasks. By systematically identifying and analyzing these shortcomings, our work aims to provide actionable insights that advance VLM research toward robust, safety-aware visual understanding and facilitate the reliable deployment of such models in real-world indoor environments.
