摘要Abstract

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 Sat3DGen，这是一种从卫星图像生成 3D 室外场景的新颖算法。我们的算法专门针对 GPS 对齐卫星和全景街景图像数据进行训练。通过结合本文提出的基于重力的密度变化损失和空间令牌等新颖方法，我们的实验表明 Sat3DGen 有效提高了生成的 3D 资产的质量并增强了生成视频的质量。 我们希望我们的方法能够启发未来 3D 户外场景生成的研究，并且我们的模型能够有效支持数字地球和场景模拟等下游任务，从而提高户外 3D 相关应用的效率和性能。In this work, we introduce Sat3DGen, a novel algorithm for generating a 3D outdoor scene from a satellite image. Our algorithm is trained exclusively on GPS-aligned satellite and panorama street view image data. By incorporating the novel approaches such as gravity-based Density Variation Loss and spatial tokens proposed in this paper, our experiments demonstrate that Sat3DGen effectively improves the quality of the generated 3D assets and enhances the quality of the generated videos. We hope that our approach inspires future research in 3D outdoor scene generation and that our model can effectively support downstream tasks such as digital Earth and scene simulation, thereby enhancing the efficiency and performance of outdoor 3D-related applications.
