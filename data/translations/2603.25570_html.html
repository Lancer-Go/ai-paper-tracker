TAAC：通往可信音频情感计算的大门: A gate into Trustable Audio Affective Computing

Xintao Hu    Feng-Qi Cui
摘要Abstract

随着抑郁症诊断人工智能技术的出现，抑郁症筛查高需求与有限供给的矛盾得到显着缓解。在各种模态数据中，由于音频是最常见的情感传递载体，基于音频的抑郁症诊断越来越受到学术界和工业界的关注。不幸的是，音频数据还包含用户敏感的身份信息（ID），该信息非常容易受到攻击，并且可能在智能诊断过程中被恶意使用。在以往的方法中，抑郁特征和敏感特征之间的明确一直是一个障碍。引入仅对敏感特征进行加密的安全加密方法和能够正确诊断抑郁症的强大分类器对于该问题也至关重要。 为了跟踪这些挑战，通过利用基于对抗性损失的子空间分解，我们提出了第一个实用框架With the emergence of AI techniques for depression diagnosis, the conflict between high demand and limited supply for depression screening has been significantly alleviated. Among various modal data, audio-based depression diagnosis has received increasing attention from both academia and industry since audio is the most common carrier of emotion transmission. Unfortunately, audio data also contains User-sensitive Identity Information (ID), which is extremely vulnerable and may be maliciously used during the smart diagnosis process. Among previous methods, the clarification between depression features and sensitive features has always serve as a barrier. It is also critical to the problem for introducing a safe encryption methodology that only encrypts the sensitive features and a powerful classifier that can correctly diagnose the depression. To track these challenges, by leveraging adversarial loss-based Subspace Decomposition, we propose a first practical frameworkTAAC提出了可信音频情感计算，以在可信环境中通过音频执行自动抑郁症检测。 的关键推动因素presented for Trustable Audio Affective Computing, to perform automated depression detection through audio within a trustable environment. The key enablers ofTAAC是区分特征子空间分解器（DFSD）、灵活噪声加密器（FNE）和阶段训练范式，分别用于分解、ID加密和性能增强。 具体来说，为了分离不同类型的特征以保护用户隐私，通过对双端自动编码器采用基于子空间的解散损失，我们提出DFSD来最大限度地分解身份相关和抑郁相关的特征。 然后，通过采用渐进噪声添加，我们提出了确定性扩​​散（加密）过程并引入FNE，用于以可调整的加密强度加密与id相关的数据。 然后，通过面向目标的多功能分段协作训练，设计了一种新颖的分阶段训练范式，使不同模块能够专门从事各自的预定义功能。 对现有加密方法的大量实验证明了我们的框架在抑郁症检测、ID 保留和音频重建方面的卓越性能。同时，各种设置下的实验证明了我们的模型在不同加密强度下的稳定性。从而证明我们的框架在保密性、准确性、可追溯性和可调整性方面的卓越性。are Differentiating Features Subspace Decompositor (DFSD), Flexible Noise Encryptor (FNE) and Staged Training Paradigm, used for decomposition, ID encryption and performance enhancement, respectively. Specifically, to separate different types of features for protecting user privacy, by employing subspace-based distenglement loss to dual-end Auto Encoder, we propose DFSD to maximally decompose the features between id-related and depression-related. Then, by employing progressive noise addition, we propose a Deterministic Diffusion (Encryption) process and introduce FNE, for encrypting the id-related data with adjustable encryption strength. Then, by goal-oriented mulfunctional segment-based collaborative training, a noval staged training paradigm that enables different modules to specialize in their respective predefined functions is designed. Extensive experiments with existing encryption methods demonstrate our framework’s preeminent performance in depression detection, ID reservation and audio reconstruction. Meanwhile, the experiments across various setting demonstrates our model’s stability under different encryption strengths. Thus proving our framework’s excellence in Confidentiality, Accuracy, Traceability, and Adjustability.

4.5 各个训练阶段Various Training Phases

该框架通过三个连续的训练阶段来执行。在第一阶段，我们重点训练区分特征子空间分解器（DFSD），将原始音频样本分解为两个正交子空间——抑郁特征和非抑郁特征——同时严格执行正交性和重建约束。加密器作为确定性模块仍然未经训练，其输出完全由预定义的加密密钥和强度参数确定。在第二阶段，VPM 分类器接受未加密数据的训练，这些数据直接从两个子空间重建，为原始音频处理建立基线诊断性能。最后，第三阶段使分类器适应加密输入：首先对非抑郁特征进行加密，然后与抑郁特征重新组合以合成保护隐私的音频样本。 VPM 分类器通过反向传播对这些加密样本进行微调。The framework is executed through three sequential training phases. In Phase I, we focus on training the Differentiating Features Subspace Decompositor (DFSD) to decompose raw audio samples into two orthogonal subspaces—depressive and non-depressive features—while strictly enforcing orthogonality and reconstruction constraints. The encryptor remains untrained as a deterministic module, where its output is fully determined by predefined encryption keys and strength parameters. During Phase II, the VPM Classifier is trained on unencrypted data, which is reconstructed directly from the two subspaces, establishing baseline diagnostic performance for raw audio processing. Finally, Phase III adapts the classifier to encrypted inputs: non-depressive features are first encrypted and then recombined with depressive features to synthesize privacy-preserving audio samples. The VPM Classifier undergoes fine-tuning via backpropagation on these encrypted samples.

第一阶段Phase I在这个训练阶段，我们使用三个损失函数：正交性损失、重建损失和分类损失。正交性损失和重建损失确保 DFSD 实现其核心功能，而分类损失则强化抑郁特征和抑郁检测之间的强相关性。正交性损失和分类损失的综合作用最大限度地减少了其他解码器特征的诊断影响。In this training phase, we utilize three loss functions: Orthogonality Loss, Reconstruction Loss, and Classification Loss. The Orthogonality Loss and Reconstruction Loss ensure the DFSD fulfills its core functionality, while the Classification Loss enforces strong relevance between depressive features and depression detection. The combined effect of Orthogonality Loss and Classification Loss minimizes the diagnostic influence of features from the other decoder.

在此阶段，使用 DFSD 和 VPM 分类器。与抑郁症相关的特征直接输入 VPM 分类器以计算分类损失。虽然分类损失在整个网络中反向传播，但正交损失和重建损失梯度仅更新 DFSD。为了优先考虑 DFSD 训练，我们引入了一个超参数来增强对正交性/重建损失的重视并减弱分类损失的影响。During this phase, the DFSD and VPM Classifier are employed. The depression-related features are fed directly into the VPM Classifier to compute the Classification Loss. While the Classification Loss is backpropagated through the entire network, the Orthogonality Loss and Reconstruction Loss gradients only update the DFSD. To prioritize DFSD training, we introduce a hyperparameter to amplify the emphasis on Orthogonality/Reconstruction Losses and attenuate the Classification Loss influence.

第二阶段Phase II随后，我们进入第二阶段。在此阶段和后续阶段，DFSD 的参数被冻结以保持其正交性和重建约束。 DFSD 提取的抑郁和非抑郁特征被重新整合并输入 VPM 分类器（未加密）。此训练阶段致力于训练能够处理未加密数据的模型。Subsequently, we proceed to Phase II. During this and subsequent phases, DFSD’s parameters are frozen to preserve its orthogonality and reconstruction constraints. The depression and non-depressive features extracted by DFSD are reintegrated and fed into the VPM Classifier (without encryption). This training phase is dedicated to training a model capable of processing unencrypted data.

第三阶段Phase III在最后的第三阶段，DFSD 的参数保持冻结。加密的非抑郁特征与抑郁特征融合，并输入VPM分类器进行训练，建立一个针对加密临床音频数据处理优化的模型。In the final Phase III, the DFSD’s parameters remain frozen. The encrypted non-depressive features are fused with depression features and fed into the VPM Classifier for training, establishing a model optimized for processing encrypted clinical audio data.
