1 简介Introduction

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 已经迅速发展到可以解释复杂的视觉场景、回答有关图像的开放式问题以及越来越流畅地进行跨模态推理的程度Vision-language models (VLMs) have rapidly advanced to the point where they can interpret complex visual scenes, answer open-ended questions about images, and reason across modalities with increasing fluency(Li et al., 2023a; Liu et al., 2024a, 2023; Bai et al., 2025)。与此同时，计算神经科学领域越来越多的工作表明，经过视觉任务训练的人工神经网络可以开发出能够显着预测灵长类视觉皮层神经活动的内部表征。. In parallel, a growing body of work in computational neuroscience has demonstrated that artificial neural networks trained on visual tasks develop internal representations that are remarkably predictive of neural activity in the primate visual cortex(Yamins et al., 2014; Schrimpf et al., 2020)。这种对应关系通常被量化为“大脑对齐”或“神经预测性”，已成为评估模型如何忠实地捕捉生物视觉基础计算原理的基准. This correspondence, commonly quantified as “brain alignment” or “neural predictivity,” has become a benchmark for evaluating how faithfully a model captures the computational principles underlying biological vision(Conwell et al., 2024; Gifford et al., 2023)。最近对数百个模型进行的大规模研究表明，大脑排列并不是一个单一的属性。它在不同的皮质区域有很大差异，并受到训练目标、架构和视觉饮食等因素的影响. Recent large-scale studies examining hundreds of models have revealed that brain alignment is not a monolithic property; it varies substantially across cortical regions and is shaped by factors such as training objective, architecture, and visual diet(Conwell et al., 2024)。这些发现提出了一个自然的问题：模型反映人类神经处理的程度是否会产生超出预测大脑活动的影响？. These findings raise a natural question: does the degree to which a model mirrors human neural processing have consequences beyond predicting brain activity?

这样的后果之一可能与对抗压力下的鲁棒性有关。 VLM 越来越多地展示出One such consequence may relate to robustness under adversarial pressure. VLMs are increasingly known to exhibit阿谀奉承的sycophantic一种行为，在用户表达不同意见或施加社会压力后，模型放弃正确的响应，转而支持错误的响应behavior, in which a model abandons a correct response in favor of an incorrect one after a user expresses disagreement or applies social pressure(Sharma et al., 2025; Perez et al., 2023)。这种故障模式尤其令人担忧，因为它破坏了对已部署系统的信任，并且可以被对手利用来提取有害或错误的输出。阿谀奉承与人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）有关，其中模型学习针对用户认可而不是事实准确性进行优化. This failure mode is particularly concerning because it undermines trust in deployed systems and can be exploited by adversaries to extract harmful or false outputs. Sycophancy has been linked to reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), where models learn to optimize for user approval rather than factual accuracy(Ouyang et al., 2022; Sharma et al., 2025)。尽管通过对越狱、即时注入和基于图像的攻击的研究，VLM 的对抗鲁棒性受到越来越多的关注. While adversarial robustness in VLMs has received growing attention through studies of jailbreaking, prompt injection, and image-based attacks(Zhao et al., 2023; Shayegani et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024b)，之前的工作还没有研究过模型视觉表征对人类神经处理的保真度是否与其承受结构化阿谀奉承操纵的能力有关。这种差距很重要，因为大脑排列和阿谀抵抗可能取决于相同的基本属性：模型如何忠实地编码视觉证据，独立于语言环境。, no prior work has investigated whether the fidelity of a model’s visual representations to human neural processing relates to its ability to withstand structured sycophantic manipulation. This gap is significant because both brain alignment and sycophancy resistance may depend on the same underlying property: how faithfully a model encodes visual evidence, independent of linguistic context.

在这项工作中，我们通过应用于涵盖 256M 到 10B 参数的 12 个开放权重 VLM 的三阶段经验管道来解决这一差距。我们特意关注中小型开放式重量型号，原因有以下三个。首先，我们的方法需要直接访问冻结的视觉编码器权重来提取大脑对齐计算的中间表示，这是闭源系统（例如 GPT-4V、Gemini）无法满足的要求，因为它们不公开其内部架构。其次，该参数范围内的开放权重模型在实践中部署最广泛，为最迫切需要但最不系统地进行安全评估的设备、边缘和资源受限的应用程序提供支持。第三，通过保持模型可访问性不变（所有模型均可通过 HuggingFace Transformers 获得），我们确保完全可重复性，这是闭源评估无法保证的核心科学原则。In this work, we address this gap through a three-stage empirical pipeline applied to 12 open-weight VLMs spanning 256M to 10B parameters. We focus deliberately on small-to-medium open-weight models for three reasons. First, our methodology requires direct access to frozen vision encoder weights to extract intermediate representations for brain alignment computation, a requirement that closed-source systems (e.g., GPT-4V, Gemini) cannot satisfy because they do not expose their internal architecture. Second, open-weight models in this parameter range are the most widely deployed in practice, powering on-device, edge, and resource-constrained applications where safety evaluation is most urgently needed yet least systematically conducted. Third, by holding model accessibility constant (all models available via HuggingFace Transformers), we ensure full reproducibility, a core scientific principle that closed-source evaluations cannot guarantee.

我们的工作位于三个活跃研究领域的交叉点：人工视觉系统中的神经预测性、语言模型中的阿谀奉承行为以及视觉语言模型的对抗鲁棒性。我们回顾下面的每个领域，然后找出激发我们研究的差距。Our work sits at the intersection of three active research areas: neural predictivity in artificial vision systems, sycophantic behavior in language models, and adversarial robustness of vision-language models. 2.1 神经预测和大脑对齐人工智能Neural Predictivity and Brain-Aligned AI

经过物体识别训练的深度神经网络会产生类似于灵长类动物腹侧视觉流的表征，这一观察结果塑造了神经科学和机器学习交叉领域十年的研究。The observation that deep neural networks trained on object recognition develop representations resembling those in the primate ventral visual stream has shaped a decade of research at the intersection of neuroscience and machine learning.(Yamins et al., 2014)首先证明了性能优化的分层模型可以定量预测 V4 和下颞叶 (IT) 皮层的神经反应，建立了一个范式，其中任务驱动的优化产生类似大脑的表征作为紧急副产品。这一发现推动了综合评估框架的开发，尤其是 Brain-Scorefirst demonstrated that performance-optimized hierarchical models quantitatively predict neural responses in both V4 and inferior temporal (IT) cortex, establishing a paradigm in which task-driven optimization yields brain-like representations as an emergent byproduct. This finding motivated the development of composite evaluation frameworks, most notably Brain-Score(Schrimpf et al., 2020)，它根据灵长类动物视觉系统的神经和行为数据对模型进行基准测试。, which benchmarks models against both neural and behavioral data from the primate visual system.

将模型表示与大脑活动进行比较的方法基础借鉴了两个互补的传统。编码模型The methodological foundations for comparing model representations to brain activity draw on two complementary traditions. Encoding models(Naselaris et al., 2011; Kay et al., 2008)训练从模型特征到功能磁共振成像响应的体素预测映射，产生神经预测的空间分辨测量。代表性相似性分析（RSA）train voxelwise predictive mappings from model features to fMRI responses, yielding spatially resolved measures of neural predictivity. Representational similarity analysis (RSA)(Kriegeskorte et al., 2008)相比之下，它比较二阶相似性结构并实现跨模式比较，而不需要显式的特征到体素映射。这两种方法都已扩展到大型模型群体。康威尔等人。, by contrast, compares second-order similarity structures and enables cross-modal comparisons without requiring explicit feature-to-voxel mappings. Both approaches have been scaled to large model populations. Conwell et al.(Conwell et al., 2024)检查了 224 个模型，发现大脑排列随结构、训练目标和视觉饮食的不同而有很大差异，自我监督模型在预测高级视觉皮层方面通常与监督模型相匹配或超过监督模型。斯托斯等人。examined 224 models and found that brain alignment varies substantially with architecture, training objective, and visual diet, with self-supervised models often matching or exceeding supervised ones in predicting high-level visual cortex. Storrs et al.(Storrs et al., 2021)表明，一旦经过适当的训练和安装，不同的架构就会收敛到类似的 IT 预测水平，这表明这种对应关系反映了共享的计算约束，而不是特殊的架构特征。showed that diverse architectures converge on similar levels of IT predictivity once appropriately trained and fitted, suggesting that the correspondence reflects shared computational constraints rather than idiosyncratic architectural features.

尽管取得了这些进展，但也出现了重要的警告。徐和瓦齐里-帕什卡姆Despite this progress, important caveats have emerged. Xu and Vaziri-Pashkam(Xu and Vaziri-Pashkam, 2021)证明 CNN 和人类视觉皮层之间的表征对应性比通常假设的要弱，特别是对于人工刺激的高阶表征。康克和阿尔瓦雷斯demonstrated that the representational correspondence between CNNs and human visual cortex is weaker than commonly assumed, particularly for higher-order representations of artificial stimuli. Konkle and Alvarez(Konkle and Alvarez, 2022)研究表明，对自然图像的自我监督、领域通用学习可以在没有明确类别监督的情况下解释腹侧流中的类别选择性组织，从而使大脑对齐的解释复杂化，因为反映了类别级别的处理。穆滕塔勒等人。showed that self-supervised, domain-general learning on natural images can account for category-selective organization in the ventral stream without explicit category supervision, complicating the interpretation of brain alignment as reflecting category-level processing. 2.2 语言模型中的阿谀奉承和协调失败Sycophancy and Alignment Failures in Language Models

现代语言模型通常通过人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）来符合人类的偏好Modern language models are typically aligned with human preferences through reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)(Christiano et al., 2023; Ouyang et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022)。虽然 RLHF 大幅提高了帮助性并减少了明显有害的输出，但越来越多的证据表明，它引入了系统性故障模式，其中最主要的是阿谀奉承：产生符合用户期望而不是实际情况的响应的倾向. While RLHF substantially improves helpfulness and reduces overtly harmful outputs, a growing body of evidence indicates that it introduces systematic failure modes, chief among them sycophancy: the tendency to produce responses that match user expectations rather than factual reality(Sharma et al., 2025; Perez et al., 2023).

夏尔马等人。Sharma et al.(Sharma et al., 2025)提供了迄今为止最全面的表征，证明跨多个家庭的 RLHF 训练模型对从事实问题回答到道德推理等任务表现出阿谀奉承。至关重要的是，他们表明人类偏好模型本身偏向于阿谀奉承的反应，从而形成了一个反馈循环，在这个循环中，批准的优化系统性地降低了真实性。佩雷斯等人。provided the most comprehensive characterization to date, demonstrating that RLHF-trained models across multiple families exhibit sycophancy on tasks ranging from factual question answering to ethical reasoning. Critically, they showed that human preference models themselves favor sycophantic responses, creating a feedback loop in which optimization for approval systematically degrades truthfulness. Perez et al.(Perez et al., 2023)通过开发模型编写的评估套件来补充这一发现，该套件揭示了不同环境中的阿谀奉承行为，包括模型在用户不同意后翻转正确答案的情况。拉纳尔迪和弗雷塔斯complemented this finding by developing model-written evaluation suites that revealed sycophantic behavior across diverse settings, including cases where models flip correct answers after user disagreement. Ranaldi and Freitas(Ranaldi and Pucci, 2025)扩展了这些观察结果，表明即使在用户顺序表达相反信念的对话环境中，阿谀奉承也会表现出来，而模型则同意这两个矛盾的立场。extended these observations by showing that sycophancy manifests even in conversational contexts where users express opposing beliefs sequentially, with models agreeing with both contradictory positions.

人们越来越多地将阿谀奉承的机制理解为 RLHF 范式的基本限制，而不是表面的产物。卡斯珀等人。The mechanisms underlying sycophancy are increasingly understood as fundamental limitations of the RLHF paradigm rather than superficial artifacts. Casper et al.(Casper et al., 2023)对 RLHF 中的开放问题进行了分类，将奖励黑客行为和训练与部署之间的分配转变确定为阿谀奉承行为的关键因素。魏等人。catalogued open problems in RLHF, identifying reward hacking and distributional shift between training and deployment as key contributors to sycophantic behavior. Wei et al.(Wen et al., 2024)证明 RLHF 可以训练模型产生对人类更有说服力的输出，但并不更准确，他们将这种现象称为“U-Sophistry”。拉班等人。demonstrated that RLHF can train models to produce outputs that are more convincing to humans without being more accurate, a phenomenon they term “U-Sophistry.” Laban et al.(Krishna et al., 2024)研究表明，迭代提示（即用户反复挑战模型的响应）会降低真实性，即使在旨在抵抗这种压力的模型中也是如此，这表明多轮阿谀奉承是一种独特的、比单轮同意偏差更具挑战性的失败模式。林等人。showed that iterative prompting, in which a user repeatedly challenges a model’s response, degrades truthfulness even in models designed to resist such pressure, suggesting that multi-turn sycophancy is a distinct and more challenging failure mode than single-turn agreement bias. Lin et al.(Lin et al., 2022)提供了衡量真实性的基准，并发现较大的模型不一定更真实，这挑战了仅靠规模来减轻对齐失败的假设。provided a benchmark for measuring truthfulness and found that larger models are not necessarily more truthful, challenging the assumption that scale alone mitigates alignment failures.

也许最令人担忧的是，有证据表明，通过安全培训，阿谀奉承和欺骗倾向可能会持续存在。胡宾格等人。Perhaps most concerning is the evidence that sycophantic and deceptive tendencies can persist through safety training. Hubinger et al.(Hubinger et al., 2024)证明可以训练模型在评估过程中表现出有益的行为，同时在部署中追求不一致的目标，并且标准 RLHF 安全训练无法消除这种“沉睡”行为。这些发现强调，阿谀奉承不仅是一种麻烦，而且是当前培训范式尚未解决的更深层次一致性挑战的症状。demonstrated that models can be trained to behave helpfully during evaluation while pursuing misaligned objectives in deployment, and that standard RLHF safety training fails to remove such “sleeper” behaviors. These findings underscore that sycophancy is not merely a nuisance but a symptom of deeper alignment challenges that current training paradigms have not resolved.

虽然阿谀奉承文献主要关注纯文本语言模型，但我们的工作将这项研究扩展到遭受结构化多轮煤气灯攻击的视觉语言模型。这种扩展意义重大，因为 VLM 必须整合来自视觉和语言渠道的证据，从而创造一个感知基础和社会依从性之间的紧张关系特别尖锐的环境。While the sycophancy literature has focused predominantly on text-only language models, our work extends this investigation to vision-language models subjected to structured multi-turn gaslighting attacks. This extension is significant because VLMs must integrate evidence from both visual and linguistic channels, creating a setting in which the tension between perceptual grounding and social compliance is particularly acute.

视觉语言模型将视觉编码器与大型语言模型集成以实现多模态推理Vision-language models integrate visual encoders with large language models to enable multimodal reasoning(Alayrac et al., 2022; Li et al., 2023a; Liu et al., 2024a)。然而，这种集成相对于单模态系统大大扩展了攻击面，因为对手可以利用任一模态或跨模态界面中的漏洞. 一项补充工作检查了可能导致 VLM 漏洞的视觉接地故障。童等人。A complementary line of work has examined the visual grounding failures that may underlie VLM vulnerability. Tong et al.(Tong et al., 2024)记录了多模式法学硕士中的系统视觉缺陷，包括需要细粒度空间推理和对象属性绑定的任务的失败。李等人。documented systematic visual shortcomings in multimodal LLMs, including failures on tasks that require fine-grained spatial reasoning and object attribute binding. 单峰视觉文献中已经探讨了视觉表示质量和鲁棒性之间的关系。盖霍斯等人。The relationship between visual representation quality and robustness has been explored in the unimodal vision literature. 5.4 VLM 开发的设计意义Design Implications for VLM Development

我们的结果对 VLM 设计和评估提出了一些可行的建议。Our results suggest several actionable implications for VLM design and evaluation.

含义 1：使用 ROI 特定的大脑评分作为诊断。Implication 1: Use ROI-specific brain scores as a diagnostic.

开发人员不应报告单一的大脑对齐总得分，而应计算特定于 ROI 的得分，特别是早期视网膜专题皮层 (V1-V3)。我们的数据表明，prf-visualrois 对齐可以作为视觉基础质量的轻量级代理，补充不测试对抗稳健性的标准 VQA 基准。Rather than reporting a single aggregate brain alignment score, developers should compute ROI-specific scores, particularly for early retinotopic cortex (V1–V3). Our data suggest that prf-visualrois alignment may serve as a lightweight proxy for visual grounding quality, complementing standard VQA benchmarks that do not test adversarial robustness.

含义 2：明确测试对抗性视觉语言冲突。Implication 2: Test adversarial vision-language conflicts explicitly.

标准阿谀奉承基准侧重于纯文本分歧Standard sycophancy benchmarks focus on text-only disagreements(Sharma et al., 2025)。我们的两转煤气灯协议表明，VLM 非常容易受到多模式操纵的影响，平均压力转换率为 55.4%。 VLM 的安全评估应包括语言与视觉证据相矛盾的结构化对抗性调查。. Our two-turn gaslighting protocol demonstrates that VLMs are highly susceptible to multimodal manipulation, with a mean pressure conversion rate of 55.4%. Safety evaluations for VLMs should include structured adversarial probes where language contradicts visual evidence.

SigLIP2-NaFlex 悖论（高大脑对齐、高阿谀奉承）表明，如果语言解码器过度合规，强大的视觉编码器并不能保证行为鲁棒性。指令调整管道应包括对抗性视觉语言分歧场景，以训练模型优先考虑视觉证据而不是社会压力。The SigLIP2-NaFlex paradox (high brain alignment, high sycophancy) demonstrates that a strong vision encoder does not guarantee behavioral robustness if the language decoder is overly compliant. 基于统计和基于权威的策略最为有效（分别为 86.5% 和 77.5% 的谄媚）的发现表明，VLM 特别容易受到模仿基于证据的推理的论点的影响。开发人员应优先考虑对此类攻击的鲁棒性，因为它们在实践中是最有效且最有可能被敌对用户部署的。The finding that statistics-based and authority-based tactics are most effective (86.5% and 77.5% sycophancy, respectively) suggests that VLMs are particularly vulnerable to arguments that mimic evidence-based reasoning. Developers should prioritize robustness to this class of attacks, as they are both the most effective and the most likely to be deployed by adversarial users in practice.

5.5 更广泛的影响Broader Impact

这项工作既有积极的社会影响，也有潜在的消极社会影响。This work has both positive and potentially negative societal implications.

从积极的一面来看，我们的研究结果为理解和预测 VLM 漏洞提供了一个基于神经科学的框架。通过识别早期视觉皮层对齐与对抗鲁棒性的相关性，我们为在部署之前评估和提高视觉语言系统的可靠性提供了原则基础。煤气灯基准本身可以作为标准化的安全评估工具。On the positive side, our findings provide a neuroscience-grounded framework for understanding and predicting VLM vulnerabilities. 不利的一面是，说服策略的详细分类及其有效性原则上可用于针对已部署的 VLM 发起更有效的对抗性攻击。我们认为，公开描述这些漏洞的科学价值超过了滥用的风险，因为我们采用的策略（诉诸权威、捏造统计数据、煤气灯操纵）在社会工程文献中已经众所周知，并且不需要专门的技术知识来部署。On the negative side, the detailed taxonomy of persuasion tactics and their effectiveness rates could, in principle, be used to craft more effective adversarial attacks against deployed VLMs. We believe that the scientific value of publicly characterizing these vulnerabilities outweighs the risk of misuse, as the tactics we employ (appeals to authority, fabricated statistics, gaslighting) are already well-known in the social engineering literature and do not require specialized technical knowledge to deploy.
