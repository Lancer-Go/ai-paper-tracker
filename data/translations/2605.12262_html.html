摘要Abstract

我们介绍We introduce缺失-MDPmissingness-MDPs（未命中 MDP）， 部分可观察马尔可夫决策过程 (POMDP) 的一个新子类，其中包含缺失数据理论。 miss-MDP 是一个 POMDP，其观察函数是(miss-MDPs), a novel subclass of partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs) that incorporates the theory of missing data. A miss-MDP is a POMDP whose observation function is a缺失函数missingness function，指定各个状态特征的概率, specifying the probability that individual state features are失踪missing（即，未观察到的）在某个时间步长。 文献区分了三种典型的缺失类型：(1) 完全随机缺失 (MCAR)、(2) 随机缺失 (MAR) 和 (3) 非随机缺失 (MNAR)。 我们的规划问题是为未命中的 MDP 计算接近最优的策略(i.e., unobserved) at a time step. The literature distinguishes three canonical missingness types: missing (1) completely at random (MCAR), (2) at random (MAR), and (3) not at random (MNAR). Our planning problem is to compute near-optimal policies for a miss-MDP with an未知unknown缺失函数，给定动作观察轨迹数据集。 实现策略的这种最优性保证需要从数据中学习缺失函数，这对于一般的 POMDP 来说是不可行的。 为了克服这一挑战，我们利用不同缺失类型的结构特性来推导出可能近似正确的（PAC）算法来学习缺失函数。 这些算法产生一个近似但完全指定的 Miss-MDP，我们使用现成的规划方法来解决它。 我们证明，最终的政策很可能是missingness function, given a dataset of action–observation trajectories. Achieving such optimality guarantees for policies requires learning the missingness function from data, which is infeasible for general POMDPs. To overcome this challenge, we exploit the structural properties of different missingness types to derive probably approximately correct (PAC) algorithms for learning the missingness function. These algorithms yield an approximate but fully specified miss-MDP that we solve using off-the-shelf planning methods. We prove that, with high probability, the resulting policies areε\varepsilon-真正的miss-MDP中的最优。 实证结果证实了该理论，并证明了我们的方法比两种无模型 POMDP 方法具有优越的性能。-optimal in the true miss-MDP. 6 结论Conclusion

Miss-MDP 将缺失数据理论与不确定性下的决策相结合。 从具有已知底层 MDP 的 miss-MDP 生成的数据中，我们近似未知的缺失函数，该函数在对其类型的假设下能够计算Miss-MDPs integrate the theory of missing data with decision-making under uncertainty. From data generated by a miss-MDP with known underlying MDP, we approximate the unknown missingness function, which – under assumptions about its type – enables the computation ofε\varepsilon-最优政策。 我们表明，关于缺失类型的错误假设可能会导致错误指定的模型和次优策略。 我们的实验支持该理论并证明了我们的方法的实际好处，强调了我们的问题设置相对于无模型基线的优越性。未来的工作可能会提升已知转换函数的假设，并将 miss-MDP 扩展到更一般的 miss-POMDP 设置。-optimal policies. We show that incorrect assumptions about the missingness type may lead to misspecified models and suboptimal policies. Our experiments support the theory and demonstrate the practical benefits of our approach, highlighting the superiority over model-free baselines in our problem setting. Future work may lift the assumption of a known transition function and extend miss-MDPs to the more general setting of miss-POMDPs.
