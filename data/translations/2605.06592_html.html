1 简介Introduction

对比语言-图像预训练Contrastive Language–Image Pretraining[13]重新定义了可迁移视觉表征学习的标准配方。然而，InfoNCE 目标的两个结构性副作用现已得到充分记录。首先，对称损失将每个不匹配的批内对视为一致负数，并丢弃它们之间的相对顺序has redefined the standard recipe for transferable visual representation learning. Yet two structural side effects of the InfoNCE objective are now well documented. First, the symmetric loss treats every unmatched in-batch pair as uniformly negative and discards the relative ordering among them[19]。其次，视觉编码器上的全局池化步骤将表示折叠成语义瓶颈，该瓶颈对细粒度的部分整体组合、密集的局部对应或纹理级分布偏移不太敏感. Second, the global pooling step on the visual encoder collapses the representation into a semantic bottleneck that is poorly sensitive to fine-grained part–whole composition, dense local correspondence, or texture-level distribution shift[3, 10, 18]。这两个问题是相互关联的：视觉方面缺乏结构性先验放大了成对损失的弱点，而损失的钝性消除了保留密集局部性的任何压力。. The two problems are coupled: the absence of a structural prior on the visual side amplifies the weakness of the pair-wise loss, while the bluntness of the loss removes any pressure to retain dense locality.

2 相关工作Related Work

对比视觉-语言预训练。Contrastive vision–language pretraining.

剪辑CLIP[13]通过对称 InfoNCE 在匹配的图像-文本对上学习联合嵌入。由于损失基本上是成对的，后续工作试图注入更丰富的信号：SLIPlearns a joint embedding via symmetric InfoNCE on matched image–text pairs. Because the loss is fundamentally pair-wise, follow-up work has tried to inject richer signals: SLIP[10]添加仅图像的自监督分支；环CLIPadds an image-only self-supervised branch; CyCLIP[3]增加模态内和跨模态循环一致性损失；阿利普adds in-modal and cross-modal cyclic-consistency losses; ALIP[18]用合成描述替换或增强嘈杂的网页标题，并根据标题质量自适应地重新加权样本。这些方法中的每一种都保持视觉编码器上的全局池化步骤不变，因此不能直接弥补激励我们工作的密集局部结构的损失。replaces or augments noisy web captions with synthetic descriptions and adaptively reweights samples by caption quality. Each of these methods leaves the global pooling step on the visual encoder untouched and therefore does not directly remedy the loss of dense local structure that motivates our work.

排名剪辑RankCLIP [19]通过批内相似性矩阵上的一阶 Plackett-Luce 模型将对比度从成对扩展到列表；它从成对 InfoNCE 丢弃的不匹配条目中恢复有意义的监督信号，无需外部注释。extends contrast from pair-wise to list-wise via a first-order Plackett–Luce model on the in-batch similarity matrix; it recovers a meaningful supervision signal from the unmatched entries that pair-wise InfoNCE discards, without external annotations.DinoRankCLIP继承了这种列表方式的观点并将其概括：我们将排名列表提升到更高的顺序，以便一致性约束传播到多步邻居关系，并且我们测试的每个基准的最佳顺序是inherits this list-wise perspective and generalises it: we lift the rank list to higher orders so the consistency constraint propagates to multi-step neighbour relations, and the optimal order on every benchmark we tested isR∗=3R^{*}\!=\!3.

7 讨论、局限性和结论Discussion, Limitations, and Conclusion

范围。Scope.

我们并不声称高阶排序和残差注入取代了更大的预训练语料库或完全消除了模态差距。我们声称，给定固定的对比配方和固定的语料库，（a）将一阶 Plackett-Luce 模型推广到具有注意参数化成对和元组转换的三阶模型，可以产生与从无排名一致性转变为一阶的价值相媲美的一致改进； (b) 通过冲突感知多尺度融合模块注入冻结的 DINOv3 教师，产生了额外的改进，这在强调局部结构推理的细粒度和 OOD 基准上是最大的。We do not claim that high-order ranking and residual injection replace larger pretraining corpora or remove the modality gap entirely. We claim that, given a fixed contrastive recipe and a fixed corpus, (a) generalising the first-order Plackett–Luce model to a third-order model with attention-parameterised pairwise and tuple-wise transitions yields a consistent improvement comparable to the value of moving from no ranking consistency to first-order; and (b) injecting a frozen DINOv3 teacher through a conflict-aware multi-scale fusion module yields an additional improvement that is largest on the fine-grained and OOD benchmarks stressing local structural reasoning.
