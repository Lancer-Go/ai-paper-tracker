摘要Abstract

当个人通过共享环境进行协调时，集体智慧就会出现，从而使当地行动积累成持久的社会组织。语言模型代理为此过程提供了新的基础，但大多数多代理系统依赖于直接对话、预定义角色或集中式工作流程。目前尚不清楚去中心化代理是否可以构建功能技术并超越独立搜索。在这里，SwarmWorld 中最初同质的 LLM 代理在没有分配角色或配方的情况下自组织成不断发展的技术社会。代理探索空间环境、处理资源、测试材料、构建持久工件，并编写可执行控制器，并在代理被移除后，由确定性模拟器在不可见的干扰下进行评估。 SwarmWorld 将认知与结果分开：代理在固定的动作和材料模式中提出架构和控制器，而模拟世界则决定功能。与强大的最佳技术相比，共享社会开发出更广泛、更具弹性的技术组合Collective intelligence can emerge when individuals coordinate through a shared environment, allowing local actions to accumulate into durable social organization. Language-model agents offer a new substrate for this process, yet most multi-agent systems rely on direct conversation, predefined roles, or centralized workflows. It remains unclear whether decentralized agents can build functional technologies and outperform independent search. Here, initially homogeneous LLM agents in SwarmWorld self-organize without assigned roles or recipes into evolving technological societies. Agents explore a spatial environment, process resources, test materials, construct persistent artifacts, and write executable controllers evaluated by a deterministic simulator under unseen disturbances after the agents are removed. SwarmWorld splits cognition from consequence: agents propose architectures and controllers within fixed action and material schemas, while the simulated world determines function. Explicit cultural mechanisms amplify collaboration and organization, but functional benefits depend on outcome and timescale. Physical stigmergy alone supports capable societies, while interaction drives persistent technological ecologies rather than universally superior individual inventions.

仍然缺少的是一个结合了四个属性的受控测试：最初等效的代理、保留其修改的共享世界、独立于代理声明评估其功能的可执行技术，以及衡量交互是否改变能力而不是仅仅增加样本数量的匹配的孤立搜索基线。 TerraLingua 为持续的文化积累提供了密切的比较，但其主要文物是文本，其终点涉及生态生存和解释的文物属性。这种区别是重要的：持久性信息可以证明文化积累，而持久性技术也可以接受外部功能分析。因此，技术传承应该有两个独立可观察的后果——后来的智能体可以继承和修改早期的构造，并且所产生的工件可以在不依赖于法学硕士对其价值的描述的动态下成功或失败。科学代理系统提供功能评估，但通常在设计的分解或工作流程中进行。悬而未决的问题不仅仅是LLM代理是否可以沟通、专业化或创造工件，而是它们的相互作用是否能够产生比相同计算群体独立发现的功能更强大的技术生态。What remains missing is a controlled test that combines four properties: initially equivalent agents, a shared world that retains their modifications, executable technologies whose function is evaluated independently of agent claims, and a matched isolated-search baseline that measures whether interaction changes capability rather than merely increasing the number of samples. Scientific-agent systems provide functional evaluation, but commonly within designed decompositions or workflows. The unresolved question is not simply whether LLM agents can communicate, specialize, or create artifacts, but whether their interaction can produce a functionally stronger technological ecology than the same computational population can discover independently.

2 结果与讨论Results and Discussion

SwarmWorld 提供了一个可以通过多种渠道评估集体智慧的环境，包括代理行为、通信和群体留下的技术（在共享世界中）。这种设计使我们能够询问交互如何改变技术的发现、积累、继承和功能鲁棒性，以及这些影响是否可以与并行独立搜索的预期收益区分开来。结果追踪了这一过程，从受控的交互机制和新兴的行为分化到可执行的技术谱系、通过持久性工件的扩散，以及由此产生的代理工件生态的长期组织和稳健性。SwarmWorld provides a setting in which collective intelligence can be evaluated through multiple channels including agent behavior, communication and the technologies that a population leaves behind (in a shared world). This design allows us to ask how interaction changes the discovery, accumulation, inheritance, and functional robustness of technology, and whether these effects can be distinguished from the gains expected from parallel independent search. The results trace this process from controlled interaction mechanisms and emergent behavioral differentiation to executable technological lineages, diffusion through persistent artifacts, and the long-horizon organization and robustness of the resulting agent-artifact ecology.

Visual presentation is kept separate from simulation state: terrain meshes, object assets, textures, lighting, particles, and shader parameters may be assigned by a renderer, but they cannot alter physics, resource quantities, agent observations, action legality, or measured outcomes. Thus, the same simulated world may be rendered at different levels of visual fidelity while remaining scientifically identical. Further details are provided in SectionS2.2.
