1 简介Introduction

人形机器人最近在包括网球在内的动态球类运动中展示了有前途的能力Humanoid robots have recently demonstrated promising capabilities in dynamic ball sports, including tennis[49], 乒乓球, table tennis[36, 12, 30]，和羽毛球, and badminton[19, 4]。现有的工作主要关注任务表现（例如击球和回球），而对职业风格的关注较少。事实证明，这种风格不仅美观，而且反映了结构化的全身协调性，这对于人类运动生物力学的有效力量产生和快速恢复至关重要. Existing work mainly focuses on task performance (e.g., hitting and ball return) while paying less attention to professional styles. Such styles have been proven not merely aesthetic, but reflect structured whole-body coordination that is critical for efficient force generation and rapid recovery in human sports biomechanics[6, 16]。对于人形机器人，我们设想可以利用这些风格来提高运动效率和任务性能，并接受其时间有限的驱动和能量限制。. For humanoid robots, we envision that these styles can be leveraged to improve motion efficiency and task performance, embracing their temporally limited actuation and energy constraints.

在这项工作中，我们研究网球，这是一项具有挑战性的球类运动，涉及多样化的击球、高速互动和全身协调。我们首先构建一个轻量级管道，从广播视频中提取职业球员的动作，并注释击球类型、旋转类型和发球时间。为了在确保任务鲁棒性的同时保留运动风格，我们采用了解耦的规划和跟踪框架In this work, we study tennis, a challenging ball sport involving diverse strokes, high-speed interaction, and full-body coordination. We first construct a lightweight pipeline to extract professional-player motions from broadcast videos and annotate stroke type, spin type, and serving toss timing. To preserve motion style while ensuring task robustness, we adopt a decoupled planning-and-tracking framework[18, 47, 13, 43]，将风格化的高级动作生成与低级执行分开。对于拉力赛，我们以 Vid2Player3D 为基础, separating stylistic high-level motion generation from low-level execution. For rallying, we built on Vid2Player3D[47]并训练基于 MVAE 的运动生成器and train an MVAE-based motion generator[18, 47]以及一个从预测的未来球轨迹推断运动潜力和跟踪速度的规划器。对于服务，我们以 AdaMimic 为基础and a planner that infers motion latents and tracking speed from predicted future ball trajectories. For serving, we build on AdaMimic[13]并训练残差跟踪器以平衡任务性能和风格保真度。 虽然这种解耦设计在仿真中是有效的，但在真实的人形系统上部署时，会出现很大的仿真与真实差距。特别是，由于仿真中不完善的执行器模型，跟踪性能不可避免地会下降and train a residual tracker to balance task performance and stylistic fidelity. 5 结论和局限性Conclusion and Limitations

我们提出了 AdaPT，一种风格化人形网球比赛的解决方案。 AdaPT 可以从职业球员的动作中学习，实现对打和发球。通过解耦的规划和跟踪，AdaPT 实现了强大的任务性能，同时保留了现实世界中的人形机器人 G1 和 Atom 中玩家特定的风格。We proposed AdaPT, a solution for stylized humanoid tennis playing. AdaPT learns from professional player motions and enables both rallying and serving. Through decoupled planning and tracking, AdaPT achieves strong task performance while preserving player-specific styles in real-world humanoid robots, G1 and Atom.

局限性。Limitations.所提出的系统仍然主要依靠运动捕捉来获取机器人根部和球的位置。未来的工作应该探索更强大的基于摄像头的感知，以进行野外部署。同时，观察到的响应能力和风格保真度之间的权衡表明需要更有效的学习算法，可以更好地平衡实时适应性和运动一致性。此外，开发用于鲁棒球和机器人状态估计的无运动捕捉定位管道仍然是现实世界部署的重要方向。在硬件方面，更强大的手腕驱动可以进一步实现更稳定灵巧的发球动作，从而提高击球精度。我们在附录中留下更多讨论The proposed system still relies primarily on motion capture to obtain the robot root and ball positions. Future work should explore more robust camera-based perception for in-the-wild deployment. Meanwhile, the observed trade-off between responsiveness and style fidelity suggests the need for more effective learning algorithms that can better balance real-time adaptability with motion consistency. Additionally, developing a motion capture-free localization pipeline for robust ball and robot state estimation remains an important direction for real-world deployment. On the hardware side, more capable wrist actuation may further enable more stable dexterous serving motions and subsequently improve strike precision. We leave more discussion in AppendixC.

B.3 系统识别System Identification

These parameters govern the ball’s post-impact energy loss and in-plane velocity decay, respectively. Parameter estimation is performed by minimizing the trajectory discrepancy between the observed motion capture data and the model-predicted rollout under identical initial conditions.
