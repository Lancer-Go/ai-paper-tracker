摘要Abstract

音频智能涉及理解、推理和生成音频和语音。在这项工作中，我们介绍了 Nemotron-Labs-Audex-30B-A3B (Audex)，这是一个基于 Nemotron-Cascade-230B-A3B（强大的纯文本 MoE LLM）构建的统一音频文本 LLM。 Audex 采用简单的统一设计，只有一个 Transformer 解码器：音频输入被编码并投影到文本嵌入空间中，而文本标记和量化音频输出标记在生成过程中被统一处理。该架构可实现强大的音频文本融合、无缝多模式生成以及与标准法学硕士培训和推理基础设施的兼容性。对于训练，我们精心策划了包含 157.4B 音频标记和 320.5B 文本标记的音频文本数据集。我们对这些数据集应用多阶段监督训练，然后是纯文本 Cascade RL 和多域策略蒸馏。 Audex 提供最先进的音频理解、语音识别和翻译、文本到语音、音频生成和语音到语音生成，同时保留其纯文本 LLM 骨干的非常引人注目的推理、对齐、知识、长上下文和代理功能，并且具有边际或无回归。我们发布模型检查点以促进开放研究。Audio intelligence involves understanding, reasoning about, and generating both audio and speech. In this work, we introduce Nemotron-Labs-Audex-30B-A3B (Audex), a unified audio-text LLM built on Nemotron-Cascade-230B-A3B, a strong text-only MoE LLM. Audex adopts a simple unified design with a single Transformer decoder: audio inputs are encoded and projected into the text embedding space, while text tokens and quantized audio output tokens are treated uniformly during generation. This architecture enables strong audio-text fusion, seamless multimodal generation, and compatibility with standard LLM training and inference infrastructure. For training, we meticulously curate audio-text datasets comprising 157.4B audio tokens and 320.5B text tokens. We apply multi-stage supervised training on these datasets, followed by text-only Cascade RL and multi-domain on-policy distillation. Audex delivers state-of-the-art audio understanding, speech recognition and translation, text-to-speech, audio generation, and speech-to-speech generation, while preserving very compelling reasoning, alignment, knowledge, long-context, and agentic capabilities of its text-only LLM backbone with marginal or no regression. We release the model checkpoints to facilitate open research.

Nemotron-Labs-Audex-30B-A3B：音频文本到音频文本模型。 Nemotron-Labs-Audex-2B：较小的 2B 模型使用与 30B-A3B 模型相同的训练方案。Nemotron-Labs-Audex-30B-A3B: the audio-text to audio-text model. Nemotron-Labs-Audex-2B: the smaller 2B model uses the same training recipe as the 30B-A3B model.

一、简介1. Introduction

音频，包括语音、音乐和环境声音，是人类感知、交流和体验世界的核心，从理解物理环境和进行口语对话，到表达书面语言之外的情感和欣赏音乐。因此，涉及理解、推理和生成音频和语音的音频智能是构建通用人工智能 (AGI) 不可或缺的模式。Audio, including speech, music and environmental sound, is central to how humans perceive, communicate, and experience the world, from understanding the physical environment and engaging in spoken conversation to expressing emotions beyond written language and appreciating music. As such, audio intelligence, which involves understanding, reasoning about, and generating both audio and speech, is an indispensable modality for building artificial general intelligence (AGI).

近年来，人们致力于构建擅长音频和语音理解的音频法学硕士，包括 Audio Flamingo (Ghosh et al., 2025b, 2026; Goel et al., 2025; Kong et al., 2024a)、Qwen2-Audio (Chu et al., 2024)、Kimi-Audio (Ding et al., 2025)、 Voxtral（Liu 等人，2025a）和 Step-Audio 2（Wu 等人，2025a）。其他作品探索了能够进行音频理解和生成的统一 LLM，包括 Qwen-Omni（Team，2026；Xu 等人，2025a，b）、MiMo-Audio（Zhang 等人，2025a）和 UALM（Tian 等人，2026）。In recent years, substantial efforts have been devoted to building audio LLMs that excel at audio and speech understanding, including Audio Flamingo (Ghosh et al., 2025b, 2026; Goel et al., 2025; Kong et al., 2024a), Qwen2-Audio (Chu et al., 2024), Kimi-Audio (Ding et al., 2025), Voxtral (Liu et al., 2025a), and Step-Audio 2 (Wu et al., 2025a). Other works have explored unified LLMs capable of both audio understanding and generation, including Qwen-Omni (Team, 2026; Xu et al., 2025a,b), MiMo-Audio (Zhang et al., 2025a), and UALM (Tian et al., 2026).

值得注意的是，能够处理多模式输入和输出（包括音频和视觉）的法学硕士，尤其是那些支持多模式生成的法学硕士，通常在文本基准上表现出明显的回归。对于仅视觉输入，NVLM 研究（Dai 等人，2024）记录了这个问题，并通过对高质量纯文本和视觉文本数据的混合进行训练来解决该问题。对于具有多模态输出能力的模型，这个问题更具挑战性。例如，即使多模态输出域仅限于语音生成，Qwen3-Omni（Xu 等人，2025b）和 Qwen3.5-Omni（团队，2026）也显示出关键文本的退化It is worth noting that LLMs capable of handling multimodal inputs and outputs, including audio and vision, especially those supporting multimodal generation, often exhibit noticeable regressions on text benchmarks. For vision-only inputs, the NVLM study (Dai et al., 2024) documented this issue and addressed it by training on a mixture of high-quality text-only and vision-text data. For models with multimodal output capabilities, the issue is even more challenging. Although Qwen3.5 (Qwen-Team, 2026) and Kimi-2.5 (Team et al., 2026) incorporate vision inputs, they do not yet support multimodal outputs such as audio.

Speech is tokenized using a speech-focused codec and non-speech sound is tokenized using a general-purpose audio codec with separate vocabularies. 4.4.1.监督微调4.4.1. Supervised Fine-Tuning

在这项工作中，我们发现无需进行音频或语音相关的预训练就可以构建一流的音频法学硕士。我们从纯文本预训练的 LLM 开始，研究两种训练后策略（见图 3（上））。第一个是多阶段SFT策略，我们将不同阶段的不同任务解耦，并逐渐添加更多任务。In this work, we find that it is possible to build a best-in-class audio LLM without audio- or speech-related pretraining. We start with the text-only pretrained LLM and study two post-training strategies (see Figure 3 (upper)). The first is the multi-stage SFT strategy, where we decouple different tasks in different stages and gradually add more tasks.

  • 我们首先应用与 Nemotron-Cascade 2 SFT 相同的纯文本 SFT（Yang 等人，2026）。事实上，我们直接重用 Nemotron-Cascade 2 中 SFT 后的检查点。We first apply text-only SFT same as Nemotron-Cascade 2 SFT (Yang et al., 2026). Indeed, we directly reuse the checkpoint after SFT from Nemotron-Cascade 2.

  • 然后，我们扩展词汇表以包含音频和语音标记，添加音频编码器和初始化的 MLP 适配器，并预热这些组件。我们称之为音频预热阶段；在此阶段，我们仅训练 MLP 适配器和音频令牌嵌入。我们冻结文本标记嵌入，因为对这些标记的训练会导致文本质量下降（参见附录 C.1 中的分析）。We then extend the vocabulary to include audio and speech tokens, add the audio encoder and initialized MLP adapters, and warmup these components. We call this the audio warmup stage; in this stage, we only train on the MLP adapters and audio token embeddings. We freeze text token embeddings because training on these leads to degraded text quality (see analysis in Appendix C.1).

  • 音频预热后，我们进行音频生成 SFT。我们解冻整个法学硕士并训练音频/语音生成数据和纯文本数据以保持文本能力。我们引入这个阶段时没有音频到文本的任务，因为音频和语音标记生成对于模型来说是全新的任务，因此需要更多的训练才能有效地学习。请注意，音频编码器和 MLP 不涉及音频/语音生成和纯文本数据。After audio warmup, we conduct audio generation SFT. We unfreeze the entire LLM and train on audio/speech generation data plus text-only data to maintain the text abilities. We introduce this stage without audio-to-text tasks as audio and speech token generation are entirely new tasks for the model, and thus require more training to learn effectively. Note that audio encoder and MLP are not involved with audio/speech generation and text-only data.

  • 最后，我们进一步解冻 MLP 适配器和 LLM，包括音频理解、ASR 和 AST 任务，以及音频生成和纯文本数据。音频编码器保持冻结状态。Finally, we further unfreeze the MLP adapters and the LLM, and include audio understanding, ASR, and AST tasks, along with audio generation and text-only data. The audio encoder remains frozen.

6.1.统一理解和生成模型6.1. Unified Understanding and Generation Models

将理解和生成结合到一个法学硕士中仍然是一项具有挑战性的任务。这是由这些任务之间显着区别的本质造成的：生成需要综合细粒度的细节，而理解通常可以通过简洁的语义特征来实现。此外，如何在添加统一的多模式功能的同时保持强大的文本推理质量仍然是一个新兴的、悬而未决的问题。It remains a challenging task to combine understanding and generation into a single LLM. This is caused by the nature of significant distinctions between these tasks: generation requires synthesizing fine-grained details, while understanding is generally achievable with succinct semantic features. Furthermore, it remains a nascent, open question on how to preserve strong text reasoning quality while adding unified multimodal capabilities.

在视觉领域已经进行了许多研究（Chen et al., 2025b; Tang et al., 2025; Team, 2024; Wang et al., 2024c; Wu et al., 2026, 2025b; Ye et al., 2026; Zhuang et al., 2025b; Zhuang et al., 2025）。总之，虽然可以将所有文本和多模态功能集成到单个模型中，但同时优化所有这些功能具有挑战性 - 某些任务的收益可能会导致其他任务（尤其是文本推理质量）的下降。几项工作表明，早期融合多模态数据A number of studies have been conducted in the vision domain (Chen et al., 2025b; Tang et al., 2025; Team, 2024; Wang et al., 2024c; Wu et al., 2026, 2025b; Ye et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2025b; Zhuang et al., 2025). 6.2.演讲法学硕士6.2. Speech LLMs

语音法学硕士是一类专门用于语音应用的音频法学硕士，包括自动语音识别 (ASR)、自动语音翻译 (AST)、语音理解、文本到语音 (TTS) 生成、语音到语音 (S2S) 生成以及这些任务的组合（Chen 等人，2024b；Défossez 等人，2024；Huang 等人，2023b；Kharitonov 等人）等人，2023；Nguyen 等人，2025；Rubenstein 等人，2023；Wang 等人，2023a，b，2025d；Speech LLMs are a class of audio LLMs specialized for speech applications, including automatic speech recognition (ASR), automatic speech translation (AST), speech understanding, text-to-speech (TTS) generation, speech-to-speech (S2S) generation, and combinations of these tasks (Chen et al., 2024b; Défossez et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2023b; Kharitonov et al., 2023; Kim et al., 2024; Nguyen et al., 2025; Radford et al., 2022; Rubenstein et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023a,b, 2024b, 2025d; Zhang et al., 2023a,b).

语音LLM的语音输出通常是从编解码器模型获得的离散语音标记（Défossez等人，2022、2024；Gong等人，2026；Ji等人，2025；Kumar等人，2023；Yang等人，2023；Ye等人，2025a,b；Zeghidour等人，2021；Zhang等人）等，2024）。编解码器模型经过训练，将语音波形编码为离散标记（或码本），并根据这些标记重建波形，并且通常使用 VQ-VAE 风格（Van Den Oord 等人，2017）编码器-量化器-解码器架构进行训练。为了实现高效表示、更高质量和更好的延迟，语音 LLM 采用各种量化方法，例如残差矢量量化 (RVQ)（Zeghidour 等人，2021）和有限标量量化（FSQ）（Mentzer 等人，2023），以及与之相关的不同码本配方和并行化令牌模式（Copet 等人，2024）。有些作品还使用单独的说话者来实现更好的可扩展性和控制（Xu et al., 2025a,b）。The speech outputs of speech LLMs are usually discrete speech tokens obtained from codec models (Défossez et al., 2022, 2024; Gong et al., 2026; Ji et al., 2025; Kumar et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2023; Ye et al., 2025a,b; Zeghidour et al., 2021; Zhang et al., 2024). Codec models are trained to encode speech waveforms into discrete tokens (or codebooks) and reconstruct waveform from these tokens, and are usually trained with the VQ-VAE style (Van Den Oord et al., 2017) Encoder-Quantizer-Decoder architectures. For efficient representation, higher quality, and better latency, speech LLMs adopt various quantization methods such as residual vector quantization (RVQ) (Zeghidour et al., 2021) and finite scalar quantization (FSQ) (Mentzer et al., 2023), and different codebook recipes and parallelized token patterns associated with them (Copet et al., 2024). Some works also use a separate talker for better scalability and control (Xu et al., 2025a,b).

语音 LLM 的输入通常采用各种格式，包括来自语音编解码器模型的离散标记（Chen 等人，2024b；Wang 等人，2023a；Zhang 等人，2025a）、音频编码器的连续表示（Goel 等人，2025；Liu 等人，2025a；Lu 等人，2026）或混合格式（Ding 等人，2026）。 2025）。这些方法通常在提高识别/理解质量与减少输入输出空间不匹配之间进行不同的权衡。The inputs to speech LLMs are usually in various formats, including discrete tokens from speech codec models (Chen et al., 2024b; Wang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2025a), continuous representations of an audio encoder (Goel et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025a; Lu et al., 2026), or a hybrid format (Ding et al., 2025). These approaches usually have different trade-offs between improving the recognition/understanding quality versus reducing input-output space mismatch.

还有另一类研究称为双工模型（ByteDance Seed Team，2026；Casanova et al.，2025；Chien et al.，2026；Défossez et al.，2024；Hu et al.，2025；Leviathan and Matias，2018；Roy et al.，2026）。这些模型专注于用户和代理之间的实时交互，双方可以同时边听边说。双工模型基于语音 LLM，并引入了额外的交互通道，这超出了本文的范围。There is another line of research called duplex models (ByteDance Seed Team, 2026; Casanova et al., 2025; Chien et al., 2026; Défossez et al., 2024; Hu et al., 2025; Leviathan and Matias, 2018; Roy et al., 2026). These models focus on real-time interaction between the user and the agent, where both can speak-while-listen at the same time. Duplex models are based on speech LLMs and introduce additional channels for interaction, which is outside the scope of this paper.

音频理解模型将语音法学硕士扩展到一般非语音和音乐领域，并专注于这些声音的语义理解。这些任务包括字幕、问答和多种角度的分类。特别是，一些作品侧重于非语音声音理解（Deshmukh et al., 2023, 2026b; Ghosh et al., 2024, 2025b; Kong et al., 2024a）、音乐理解（Deng et al., 2024; Gardner et al., 2023; Ghosh et al., 2025a; Liu et al., 2024c；Ma 等人，2024；Won 等人，2024），或包括语音在内的所有类型的声音（Ding 等人，2025；Ghosh 等人，2026；Goel 等人，2025；Gong 等人，2023；Liu 等人，2025a；Tang 等人，2024；Team，2026；田等人，2025；吴等人，2025a；徐等人，2025a，b；张等人，2025a）。有些作品还具有上一节中讨论的语音输出（Ding et al., 2025; Team, 2026; Tian et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a; Xu et al., 2025a,b; Zhuang et al., 2025a）。Audio understanding models extend speech LLMs to the domain of general non-speech sound and music, and focus on semantic understanding of these sound. Especially, some works focus on non-speech sound understanding (Deshmukh et al., 2023, 2026b; Ghosh et al., 2024, 2025b; Kong et al., 2024a), music understanding (Deng et al., 2024; Gardner et al., 2023; Ghosh et al., 2025a; Liu et al., 2024c; Ma et al., 2024; Won et al., 2024), or all types of sound including speech (Ding et al., 2025; Ghosh et al., 2026; Goel et al., 2025; Gong et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2025a; Tang et al., 2024; Team, 2026; Tian et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a; Xu et al., 2025a,b; Zhang et al., 2025a). There are many types of audio encoders used based on the focus and tasks of the audio understanding model (Chen et al., 2022a,b; Chu et al., 2024; Dhariwal et al., 2020; Elizalde et al., 2023; Goel et al., 2025; Gong et al., 2021; Li et al., 2024b; Radford et al., 2022; Sekoyan et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2023; Xu et al., 2025b). Besides continuous representations, several works investigated audio understanding with discrete audio codecs, but there is still a gap from the state-of-the-art models in terms of audio understanding quality (Yang et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025a).

C.1.音频预热C.1. Audio Warmup

在本节中，我们比较多阶段 SFT 中的不同音频预热方法。第一个（在 Audex 上使用）是冻结文本标记嵌入并仅更新音频标记嵌入。这是通过文本标记嵌入的梯度掩码来实现的。由于LLM也被冻结，因此这种方法不会影响模型的文本部分。第二种方法是简单地解冻文本和音频标记嵌入，并对包括文本在内的所有任务进行训练。我们假设这种方法可以联合优化所有嵌入，以实现更好的多模式训练。第三种方法是将前两种方法结合起来：我们应用第一种方法，然后应用第二种方法。In this section, we compare different audio warmup methods in multi-stage SFT. The first (used on Audex) is to freeze text token embeddings and only update audio token embeddings. This is implemented by gradient masking of text token embeddings. Since LLM is also frozen, this approach does not affect the text part of the model. The second approach is to simply unfreeze both text and audio token embeddings and train on all tasks including text. We hypothesized this approach could jointly optimize all embeddings for better multi-modal training. The third approach is to combine the previous two: we apply the first and then the second.

音频预热后的结果如表 16 所示。我们发现解冻文本标记嵌入在任何情况下都没有任何好处，并且与第一种方法的差距巨大。在表 5 中，我们绘制了不同预热方法后 Audio Gen.SFT 阶段的文本评估结果。如果我们在音频预热中冻结文本嵌入，则模型以高文本分数开始，并在音频生成 SFT 开始时有较小的降级，以补偿音频生成任务。如果我们解冻文本嵌入，则模型从较低的文本分数开始，在训练 10%-20% 后恢复，但与第一种方法不具有可比性。The results after audio warmup are shown in Table 16. We find unfreezing text token embeddings has no benefit in any situation, and the gap to the first approach is huge. In Table 5, we plot the text evaluation results of the Audio Gen.
