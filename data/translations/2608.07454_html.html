摘要Abstract

复杂分子的全合成是化学中要求最高的智力和实验壮举之一：化学家必须提前计划许多步骤，以了解如何将简单的构建块组装成复杂的目标，设计备用策略并预测程序挑战。这也是一项极具创造性的活动。半个世纪以来，天然产物和其他复杂分子的逆合成设计自动化的努力一直在利用编目反应，而由此产生的工具现在报告在基于同一来源构建的基准上几乎完全成功。但这些工具的形状适合基准化学，并且它们在许多天然产物上表现不佳，这些前沿领域的密集功能化、多环结构恰恰需要记录中包含最少的创造性化学。机器是否能够像专业化学家那样合理地设计此类合成仍不清楚。在这里，我们展示了 SynthEx，一个基于大型语言模型构建的代理框架，它规划了传统设计算法无法企及的复杂自然产品的路线。 SynthEx 摆脱了限制规划人员的固定反应库，提出了竞争策略，将一系列常规和关键步骤组装成一条有凝聚力的路线，并批评和改进了自己的设计；它所支持的化学反应比现有工具产生的更加收敛和优雅，并且跨越了基于目录的工具无法比拟的反应空间区域。最值得注意的是，在盲法评估中，化学专家判断其关键步骤与已发表的人类合成方法相当，并将其作为真正的合成计划，这是以前从未完成的响应算法路线预测。我们以 SynthAtlas 的形式发布了 1000 多种天然产物的路线，这是一个开放的交互式路线图，并预计它将成为缺乏当前相关文献设计的复杂目标分子集合的共享资源。The total synthesis of a complex molecule is among the most demanding intellectual and experimental feats in chemistry: a chemist must plan many steps ahead for how to assemble simple building blocks into an intricate target, devise backup strategies, and anticipate procedural challenges. It is also a profoundly creative activity. For half a century, efforts to automate the retrosynthetic design of natural products and other complex molecules have drawn on catalogued reactions, and the resulting tools now report near-complete success on benchmarks built from that same source. But these tools were shaped to fit benchmarked chemistry, and they falter on many natural products, the frontier of the field, whose densely functionalized, polycyclic architectures demand precisely the inventive chemistry the record contains least. Whether a machine could reasonably design such syntheses like an expert chemist does has remained unclear. Here, we show that SynthEx, an agentic framework built on large language models, plans routes to complex natural products that lie beyond the reach of conventional design algorithms. Freed from the fixed reaction libraries that confine those planners, SynthEx proposes competing strategies, assembles a sequence of routine and key steps into a cohesive route, and critiques and improves its own design; the chemistry it favors is more convergent and elegant than existing tools produce, and spans a region of reaction space that catalogue-based tools cannot match. Most notably, in blinded assessments, expert chemists judged its key steps comparable to those of published human syntheses and engaged with them as genuine synthesis plans, a response algorithmic route prediction has not previously accomplished. We release routes to more than a thousand natural products as SynthAtlas, an open, interactive roadmap, and anticipate it will become a shared resource for a collection of complex target molecules that lack relevant current literature designs.

1 主要Main

天然产物的全合成是化学领域最具挑战性和创造性的工作之一。面对错综复杂的分子结构和复杂的立体化学关系，化学家开发了逐个反应的配方（合成路线），决定形成哪些键以及以什么顺序，何时引入和去除保护基团，以及如何控制创建目标分子的每一步的三维结果。科里的逆向综合分析为这一推理提供了一种形式语言，将一个艰巨的目标简化为逐步断开连接，从而产生一系列既定的、可行的转换The total synthesis of natural products is among the most challenging and creative endeavors in chemistry. Faced with an intricate molecular architecture and complex stereochemical relationships, a chemist develops a reaction-by-reaction recipe (synthetic route), deciding which bonds to forge and in what order, when to introduce and remove protective groups, and how to control the three-dimensional outcome of every step to create the target molecule. Corey’s retrosynthetic analysis gave this reasoning a formal language, reducing a formidable target to stepwise disconnections, resulting in a sequence of established, feasible transformations[corey1964total, Corey_Cheng_LogicofChemicalSynthesis, nicolaou1996classics, nicolaou2000]。该学科已经多次回报了我们的努力：全合成是新反应证明其价值的地方，许多现在标准的转化都可以追溯到天然产物的组装. The discipline has repaid the effort many times over: total synthesis is where new reactions prove their worth and many, now-standard, transformations trace their origins to the assembly of a natural product[trost1991comprehensive, nicolaou1997wittig, heravi2021recent]。然而，设计一条通往高要求目标的路线仍然是一门艺术，只有一小部分专家能够熟练地实践. Yet designing a route to a demanding target remains an art that only a small community of experts practice fluently[bran2026chemical]，事实证明，最难表达和模仿的是专业知识，而不是它所利用的化学反应。, and it is that expertise, not the chemistry it draws on, that proves hardest to articulate and imitate.

半个世纪以来，化学家一直在寻求自动化合成规划。计算机辅助综合规划 (CASP) 由 Corey 和 Wipke 在 60 年代开始For half a century, chemists have sought to automate synthesis planning. Computer-Assisted Synthesis Planning (CASP) began with Corey and Wipke in the 60s[corey1969computer, corey1972computer, corey1985computer]，使用手动编码的反应逻辑，然后转向反应模板——从文献和专利中大规模挖掘的确定性规则, with manually coded reaction logic, before shifting to reaction templates—deterministic rules mined at scale from literature and patents[lowe2011chemical, lowe2012extraction, Lowe2017]。经过训练的逆向合成模型现在可以在几分钟内提出多步路线，并且在从这些相同语料库中提取的标准基准上，它们取得了令人印象深刻的成功. Trained retrosynthesis models can now propose multistep routes in minutes, and on standard benchmarks drawn from these same corpora, they succeed impressively[Segler2017neural, coley2017computer, segler2018planning, Schwaller2018found, Jin2017predicting, chen2020retro, Sacha2021molecule, schwaller2020predicting, pdvn, maziarz2024chimera]。然而，这一成功反映了基准和培训数据的共享分布，这些数据主要由简化的药物和过程化学的反复、常规转换所主导。复杂目标所需要的高度特定于上下文的脱节正是这些工具难以捕捉的. However, this success reflects the shared distribution of the benchmarks and training data, which are dominated by the recurring, routine transformations of streamlined medicinal and process chemistry. The highly context-specific disconnections that complex targets demand are precisely what these tools struggle to capture[3aizynthtrain, schwaller2019molecular, tanovic2026exploration].

这种限制通过两种方式变得显而易见。首先， 基准本身已经饱和：在该领域长期使用的专利衍生测试集上，最先进的规划者现在报告成功接近完整。就像早期的编码基准在该领域转向代理软件工程任务之前就已经饱和一样This limitation has become visible in two ways. First, the benchmarks themselves have saturated: on the patent-derived test sets that the field has long used, state-of-the-art planners now report success approaching completeness. Like early coding benchmarks that saturated before the field shifted to agentic software engineering tasks[jimenez2024swe, yang2024swe]，现有的综合基准不再探测模型能力正在改进的轴, existing synthesis benchmarks no longer probe the axes on which model capabilities are improving[genheden_bjerrum_2022, maziarz2023re, tripp2024retro, maziarz2024chimera, morgunov2025procrustean]。其次，更明显的是，综合规划工具一旦离开化学空间就会动摇. Second, and more tellingly, synthetic planning tools falter the moment they leave that chemical space[xuan2025tempre, reactionspace_hassen, tran2026quantifying]。复杂的天然产物具有密集融合、立体化学丰富的结构，对于这些工具来说是无法分布的。这一弱点可能是反应规则方法本身的结构性问题。规则，无论是作为显式模板，还是隐含在神经网络权重中，都依赖于应用来自频繁发生的反应的记忆模式。然而，天然产物的合成通常需要定制的、创造性的转化——例如复杂的闭环级联或复杂的结构重排——这完全取决于分子的特定整体背景。因为这些反应很少发生，无法形成可靠的模板. Complex natural products, with their densely fused, stereochemically rich architectures, are out-of-distribution for these tools. This weakness is likely structural to the reaction rule approach itself. Rules, whether as explicit templates, or implicit in neural network weights, rely on applying memorized patterns from frequently occurring reactions. Natural product synthesis, however, routinely requires bespoke, inventive transformations—such as complex ring-closing cascades or intricate structural rearrangements—that depend entirely on the specific, global context of the molecule. Because these reactions occur too rarely to form robust templates[3aizynthtrain]，或者太罕见而无法被神经网络召回, or are too rare to be recalled by a neural network[schwaller2019molecular, maziarz2024chimera]，一名仅限于历史反应库的规划者从根本上很难驾驭天然产物独特的化学环境。因此，该领域已经不再判断规划者是否能够成功检索路径，而是判断化学家是否会真正使用所得到的路径, a planner confined to a historical reaction library fundamentally struggles to navigate the unique chemical environments of natural products. The field has accordingly moved past judging a planner on whether it can successfully retrieve a pathway, to whether a chemist would actually use the resulting route[morgunov2026syntax].

大型语言模型（LLM）提出了一种突破这一限制的方法。通过预训练吸收了大量化学知识和背景，远远超出任何单一反应数据库，它们可以充当化学推理引擎，而不是检索引擎Large language models (LLMs) suggest a way past this limitation. Having absorbed, through pretraining, a vast body of chemical knowledge and context that extends well beyond any single reaction database, they can act less as retrieval engines than as chemical reasoning engines[jablonka202314, jablonka2023gpt, alampara2024probing, bran2023chemcrow, boiko2023autonomous, mirza2025framework, armstrong2025synthstrategy]。布兰等人。表明法学硕士可以判断合成，将化学家规定的策略与候选路线结合起来，并根据人类专家的意见对它们进行排名. Bran et al. showed that an LLM can judge a synthesis, aligning a chemist’s stated strategy with candidate routes and ranking them in agreement with human experts[bran2026chemical]。 LARC 等框架. Frameworks such as LARC[baker2025larc]和MMORFand MMORF[baker2026mmorf]使用法学硕士作为评估者，根据用户定义的标准选择节点并对其进行评分，而传统规划器则扩展搜索空间。合成物use LLMs as evaluators, selecting and scoring nodes against user-defined criteria while a traditional planner expands the search space. Synthelite[xuan2025synthelite]证明法学硕士可以直接规划，将模型转换为基于语言的策略先验，以自然语言提出每个断开连接，从而直接引导树搜索，代替训练有素的反应策略网络；然而，这些建议仍然以针对固定模板库的检索为基础。每一个都提升了法学硕士可以发挥的作用，但都有一个上限：无论模型对综合战略或战术进行排名还是提出下一步，它都会在传统规划器已经固定的反应空间内进行选择，并且无法在该空间之外实例化反应。这就是使复杂分子合成成为化学推理最相关的可用测试的原因：因为所需的断开通过构建与任何反应数据库分离，所以成功不仅限于反应检索，并且规划这些路线的系统必须推理反应性、策略和立体控制，而不是通过模板调用它们。以这种方式规划合成需要规划者使化学原理适应任何给定的反应，并且是化学推理自动化的必要先决条件。demonstrates that an LLM can directly plan, casting the model as a language-based policy prior that proposes each disconnection in natural language and so steers the tree search directly, in place of a trained reaction policy network; these proposals, however, are still grounded by retrieval against a fixed template library. Each advances the role an LLM can play, yet all share one ceiling: whether the model ranks synthetic strategy or tactics or proposes the next step, it selects within a reaction space a conventional planner has already fixed, and has no ability to instantiate reactions outside that space. This is what makes complex-molecule synthesis the most relevant available test of chemical reasoning: because the required disconnections are, by construction, decoupled from any reaction database, success is not limited to reaction retrieval, and a system that plans these routes must reason about reactivity, strategy, and stereocontrol rather than recall them through a template. Planning a synthesis in this way requires the planner to adapt chemistry principles to any given reaction and is a necessary precondition for the automation of chemical reasoning.

Rather than selecting disconnections from a fixed template library, SynthEx expresses each as an ordered list of atom-level graph edits, a format we call ReactionJSON. By letting the language model write these edits directly, SynthEx transitions from a critic that ranks known templates to a policy that generates novel expansions. Around this representation, SynthEx builds the iterative reasoning of a human chemist: it proposes competing high-level strategies, expands each into a complete pathway, and critiques and repairs its own chemistry step by step (Fig.1a).

SynthEx 应用于超过一千种有记录的天然产物，打开了一个最先进的搜索无法到达的合成空间区域。它为绝大多数目标设计路线，而领先的基于模板的规划器只能解决一小部分，及其优势Applied to a collection of over a thousand documented natural products, SynthEx opens a region of synthesis space that a state-of-the-art search does not reach. It designs routes for the large majority of targets for which a leading template-based planner solves only a small fraction, and its advantage扩大widens随着目标变得更加复杂，而不是缩小：导致传统搜索失败的额外环、连接和功能组对 SynthEx 的影响较小。它的键结构也明显比现有工具所提出的更加收敛，其中大多数将两个独立的片段结合在一起，并且它们占据了最先进的单步模型很少提出的独特反应空间, rather than narrows, as targets grow more complex: the additional rings, connectivities, and functional groups that cause a conventional search to fail have a lower impact on SynthEx. Its bond constructions are also markedly more convergent than those existing tools propose, most of them uniting two independent fragments, and they occupy a distinct reaction space that state-of-the-art single-step models rarely propose[3aizynthtrain, maziarz2024chimera, lowe2011chemical, reaxys]。这不是在新环境中产生的相同化学反应，而是完全不同的化学反应：SynthEx 更倾向于建设性的成键步骤、环结构和交叉偶联，而不是主导基于模板的输出的官能团操作，这是 SynthEx 战略偏好的定性特征。 NPAtlas 中没有一个基准目标被标记为合成的，因此 SynthEx 提出的路线不是对现有文献的检索。在盲审中，化学专家认为 SynthEx 的关键步骤与已发表的人类合成方法相当，此外，还指出了单独的断开，例如 Grob 碎片、[3+2] 偶极环加成或分子内级联 Michael 加成，这些都是真正优雅的（图 1）。. This is not the same chemistry produced in new contexts but different chemistry altogether: SynthEx favors constructive, bond-forming steps, ring constructions and cross-couplings, over the functional group manipulations that dominate template-based output, a qualitative signature of SynthEx’s strategic preferences. None of the benchmark targets are flagged as synthesized in NPAtlas, so the routes SynthEx proposes are not retrievals of existing literature.
