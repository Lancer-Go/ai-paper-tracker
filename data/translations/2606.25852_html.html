摘要Abstract

基于群体的强化学习通过从轨迹结果中获取步骤级信用，有效地对 LLM 代理进行长期、稀疏奖励任务的后训练。 然而，这将步骤的功劳与其推出的最终结果联系起来：语义上几乎相同的中间步骤获得相反的功劳，具体取决于它们的轨迹最终是成功还是失败。 这种语义信用不一致会给类似的操作带来冲突的梯度，并浪费失败的部署中部分正确的进度。受此启发，我们建议Group-based reinforcement learning effectively post-trains LLM agents for long-horizon, sparse-reward tasks by deriving step-level credit from trajectory outcomes. However, this ties a step’s credit to its rollout’s final outcome: semantically near-identical intermediate steps receive opposite credit depending on whether their trajectory eventually succeeded or failed. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）越来越多地充当Large language models (LLMs) increasingly act as代理人agents在开放式互动环境中感知、推理和行动that perceive, reason, and act in open-ended, interactive environments(Achiam and others, OpenAI; Gemini Team, 2024)。 代表性应用包括模拟家庭中的具体助理. Representative applications include embodied assistants in simulated households(Shridhar et al., 2021)，完成多步骤浏览任务的网络导航器, web navigators that complete multi-step browsing tasks(Yao et al., 2022)，以及使用工具进行搜索和推理的代理, and tool-using agents for search and reasoning(Qian et al., 2025; Jin et al., 2025)。 除了单轮语言理解之外，此类智能体还必须执行长期规划和稳健的决策，经常在观察到任何奖励之前反复观察环境、采取行动并从早期错误中恢复。. Beyond single-turn language understanding, such agents must perform long-horizon planning and robust decision-making, repeatedly observing the environment, acting, and recovering from earlier mistakes, often before any reward is observed.

强化学习（RL）已成为培训后法学硕士的关键范式Reinforcement learning (RL) has become a key paradigm for post-training LLMs(Ouyang et al., 2022; DeepSeek-AI, 2025)。 特别是基于组、无价值的方法，例如 RLOO. In particular, group-based, value-free methods such as RLOO(Kool et al., 2019), GRPO (Shao et al., 2024)，和DAPO, and DAPO(Yu et al., 2025)估计一组采样的推出中的相对优势，而不是训练单独的批评者，以较低的计算成本实现强大的性能。 这些方法大多建立在单轮任务上，例如数学推理和代码生成，其中奖励是立即的并且信用分配estimate relative advantages within a group of sampled rollouts instead of training a separate critic, achieving strong performance at low computational cost. These methods are most established on single-turn tasks such as mathematical reasoning and code generation, where reward is immediate and credit assignment(Pignatelli et al., 2024)很简单。 然而，在多轮代理设置中，奖励通常是单个终端信号——通常是在数十步的长轨迹之后才传递的二元成功/失败——因此应用这些算法天真地为每一步分配相同的轨迹级优势，并消除了步级差异。 为了在没有批评者或额外部署的情况下恢复细粒度信用，GiGPOis straightforward. In multi-turn agentic settings, however, the reward is typically a single terminal signal—often a binary success/failure delivered only after a long trajectory of tens of steps—so applying these algorithms naively assigns every step the same trajectory-level advantage and collapses step-level distinctions. To recover fine-grained credit without a critic or additional rollouts, GiGPO(Feng et al., 2025)组步骤在推出过程中重新访问相同的环境状态，并估计这些状态一致组内的步骤级别相对优势，后续工作进一步丰富了基于组的代理强化学习（例如 HGPO）groups steps that revisit the same environment state across rollouts and estimates step-level relative advantages within these state-aligned groups, and subsequent work has further enriched group-based agentic RL (e.g., HGPO(He et al., 2026),HCAPO, HCAPO(Tan et al., 2026)和 Context-Lite, and Context-Lite(Chen et al., 2025)).

然而，这些步骤级别的补救措施仍然从其轨迹的最终结果中获得步骤的信用：每当其在后续步骤中推出失败时，本地正确的步骤就会受到惩罚，即使它与成功的推出共享一个阶段。 因此，两个语义上几乎相同的步骤（一个来自成功的推出，一个来自后来才出错的推出）会收到相反的训练信号，这些信号由其轨迹结果而不是步骤本身决定。 我们称之为故障模式However, these step-level remedies still derive a step’s credit from its trajectory’s eventual outcome: a locally correct step is penalised whenever its rollout fails at a later step, even when it shares a stage with successful rollouts. Two semantically near-identical steps—one from a successful rollout, one from a rollout that erred only later—therefore receive opposite training signals, decided by their trajectories’ outcomes rather than by the step itself. 4 实验Experiments

在本节中，我们将在具有挑战性的代理基准上对 SCPO 进行实证评估。具体来说，我们的目标是证明：（1）SCPO 在培养强大的 LLM 代理人方面的有效性及其与最先进的基于群体的基线的竞争力； (2) 收益出现在哪里，特别是集中在较难的多步骤任务系列中； (3) SCPO 设计选择的贡献，重点是通过消融研究分配顺序； (4) 冻结交叉编码器整形器引入的计算开销。In this section, we present empirical evaluations of SCPO on challenging agentic benchmarks. Specifically, we aim to demonstrate: (1) the effectiveness of SCPO in training strong LLM agents and its competitiveness with state-of-the-art group-based baselines; (2) where the gains arise, in particular their concentration in the harder multi-step task families; (3) the contribution of SCPO’s design choices, with a focus on allocation order, through ablation studies; and (4) the computational overhead introduced by the frozen cross-encoder shaper.

局限性Limitations

依赖成功的兄弟姐妹。Dependence on successful siblings.

SCPO 只能在包含至少一个的组中恢复学分 成功的兄弟。当所有推出失败时，该组将被留下 不变。这使得 SCPO 成为轨迹级的补充 基于群体的强化学习而不是替代品；极其稀疏的 或早期训练制度，其益处可能只有在基础训练之后才会显现 政策有时可以解决任务。SCPO can recover credit only in groups that contain at least one successful sibling. When all rollouts fail, the group is left unchanged. This makes SCPO complementary to trajectory-level group-based RL rather than a replacement for it; in extremely sparse or early-training regimes, its benefit may appear only after the base policy occasionally solves the task.

近似语义匹配，而非精确验证。Approximate semantic matching, not exact verification.

SCPO 将语义相似性的失败归功于成功的步骤 兄弟姐妹，而不是通过精确的状态-动作等价。冷冻的 交叉编码器返回软对齐分数，因此匹配器进行操作 模糊语义重叠，并且可以过度信用和信用不足：两个 即使在以下情况下，具有相似表面形状的台阶也可以被评分为匹配： 它们的效果不同，而真正等效的步骤却表述为 不同的情况可能会被错过。这非常适合体现和 网络导航任务，例如 ALFWorld 和 WebShop，其中进度为 反映在粗略的、可观察的状态变化（位置、物体、 页面转换），通用重新排序器可靠地捕获。 它不太适合需要精确、符号匹配的领域： 在初步实验中，应用相同的方法来推理 RL 数学和代码数据集，我们没有观察到明显的改进，这 我们归因于现成的交叉编码器——从未使用过训练 此类内容的对比目标——仅产生粗略的 无法可靠地分离正确中间体的相似性信号 从一个稍微错误的推导步骤。使记分员适应 目标域（例如，经过对比训练或基于验证者的 matcher）是我们留给未来工作的自然扩展。SCPO credits a failed step by semantic similarity against a successful sibling rather than by exact state–action equivalence. The frozen cross-encoder returns a soft alignment score, so the matcher operates on fuzzy semantic overlap and can both over- and under-credit: two steps with similar surface form may be scored as matching even when their effects differ, while genuinely equivalent steps phrased differently may be missed. This is well suited to embodied and web-navigation tasks such as ALFWorld and WebShop, where progress is reflected in coarse, observable state changes (locations, objects, page transitions) that a general-purpose reranker captures reliably. It is a poorer fit for domains that require exact, symbolic matching: in preliminary experiments applying the same recipe to reasoning RL on mathematics and code datasets, we observed no clear improvement, which we attribute to the off-the-shelf cross-encoder—never trained with a contrastive objective on such content—producing only a coarse similarity signal that cannot reliably separate a correct intermediate derivation step from a subtly incorrect one. Adapting the scorer to the target domain (e.g., a contrastively trained or verifier-based matcher) is a natural extension that we leave to future work.

局部语义信用不是因果验证。Local semantic credit is not causal verification.

SCPO 避免强迫失败的轨迹模仿准确的时间 成功推出的路径，但它也不验证是否匹配 局部行为形成了全球一致的解决方案。失败的步骤可能 语义上类似于成功的参考槽，但不相关或 在上下文中有害。每个参考位置最多记入一次限制 此类错误的严重程度，但不保证政策 改进；实现的更新还是要看优势 归一化、PPO 裁剪、KL 正则化和优化 动态。SCPO avoids forcing failed trajectories to imitate the exact temporal path of a successful rollout, but it also does not verify that matched local behaviours form a globally coherent solution. A failed step may semantically resemble a success reference slot while being irrelevant or harmful in context. Crediting each reference position at most once limits the magnitude of such errors, but does not guarantee policy improvement; the realised update still depends on advantage normalisation, PPO clipping, KL regularisation, and optimisation dynamics.
