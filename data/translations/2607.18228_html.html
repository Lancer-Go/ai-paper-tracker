压力下的逻辑判断：用习得的软前缀诊断三段论稳定性Logical Judgments Under Pressure: Diagnosing Syllogistic Stability with Learned Soft Prefixes

Brian K Chen
National University of Singapore
e0694208@u.nus.edu
摘要Abstract

为了测试正确的逻辑判断如何响应学习的上下文，我们在精确标记的三段论推理基准之前添加一个软前缀，同时保持模型固定。软前缀是不透明的连续向量，因此我们通过它们在逻辑形式和接口的受控变化中引起的行为来表征它们。通过研究哪些前缀成功以及它们的影响如何泛化，我们描述了习得的上下文压力如何超越正确的判断并暴露模型逻辑稳定性的限制。在 Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE、Qwen3-8B 和 Gemma 4 31B 中，学习前缀会重定向许多正确答案，并在未见过的表单和界面更改中保持有效。在使用 Qwen3.6 MoE 和 Gemma 进行的重复测试中，它们在所有 16 个模型方向分割比较中均优于配对随机对照 37 至 99 个百分点。 Qwen3.6 MoE 在措辞和提示更改中的翻转率保持在 72% 到 90% 之间，而 Gemma 有效性前缀保留 54% 到 56% 的翻转率，而匹配的随机前缀的翻转率不到 1%。诊断测试表明，主导效应是对一种答案含义的广泛偏好，而不是固定符号强制或在任务之间可靠转移的逻辑操作。这种偏差的形式因模型而异。在这两个 Qwen 模型中，简单的评分模型通常会预测哪些判断会发生翻转，但不会预测其利润率会移动多远，而 Gemma 的整体反应更接近于相同模型。这些结果表明，成功的软前缀的主要行为效应是广泛的答案偏好，而其余的响应则揭示了逻辑稳定性方面特定于模型的实质性差异。To test how correct logical judgments respond to learned context, we prepend a soft prefix to an exactly labeled syllogistic reasoning benchmark while keeping the model fixed. Soft prefixes are opaque continuous vectors, so we characterize them through the behavior they induce across controlled variations in logical form and interface. By studying which prefixes succeed and how their effects generalize, we characterize how learned contextual pressure can override correct judgments and expose limits in a model’s logical stability. Across Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE, Qwen3-8B, and Gemma 4 31B, learned prefixes redirect many correct answers and remain effective across unseen forms and interface changes. In repeated tests with Qwen3.6 MoE and Gemma, they outperform paired random controls in all 16 model–direction–split comparisons by 37 to 99 percentage points. Qwen3.6 MoE flip rates remain between 72% and 90% across wording and prompt changes, while Gemma validity prefixes retain 54% to 56% flip compared with less than 1% for matched random prefixes. Diagnostic tests show that the dominant effect is a broad preference for one answer meaning rather than fixed-symbol forcing or a logical operation that transfers reliably between tasks. The form of this bias differs across models. In both Qwen models, simple score models often predict which judgments will flip but not how far their margins will move, whereas Gemma’s overall response is more closely approximated by the same models. These results show that the dominant behavioral effect of successful soft prefixes is a broad answer preference, while the remaining response reveals substantial model-specific differences in logical stability.

1 简介Introduction

可靠的逻辑推理需要的不仅仅是在单一提示下产生正确答案。即使逻辑内容是固定的，模型也可能做出正确的形式判断，但当问题外部的上下文发生变化时却会逆转它。因此，我们区分逻辑任务的准确性和鲁棒性。准确性询问判断是否正确，而稳健性询问判断在附加背景下是否保持稳定。基准准确性本身并不能揭示哪种学习背景可以克服其他正确的逻辑行为。Reliable logical reasoning requires more than producing the correct answer under a single prompt. A model may make the correct formal judgment yet reverse it when context outside the problem changes, even though the logical content is fixed. We therefore distinguish accuracy on logical tasks from robustness. Accuracy asks whether a judgment is correct, whereas robustness asks whether it remains stable under added context. Benchmark accuracy alone does not reveal what kinds of learned context can overcome otherwise correct logical behavior.

我们使用三段论逻辑来研究这个问题，其中标签可以被精确计算。我们在每个提示前添加一个简短的可训练嵌入向量序列，同时保持语言模型固定。前缀经过优化，可以将无前缀模型正确回答的所选判断类别重定向到指定的目标答案。由于软前缀是连续向量而不是可读指令，因此无法直接检查它们的含义。相反，我们通过询问它们改变了哪些判断、它们的影响转移到哪里以及哪些更简单的机制再现它们来从功能上解释它们。通过识别有效前缀的作用，我们可以推断出哪些学习上下文可以覆盖正确的逻辑判断以及模型的稳定性边界在哪里。We study this question using syllogistic logic, where labels can be computed exactly. We prepend a short trainable sequence of embedding vectors to every prompt while keeping the language model fixed. The prefix is optimized to redirect a chosen class of judgments that the unprefixed model answers correctly toward a specified target answer. Because soft prefixes are continuous vectors rather than readable instructions, their meaning cannot be inspected directly. We instead interpret them functionally by asking which judgments they change, where their effects transfer, and which simpler mechanisms reproduce them. By identifying what effective prefixes do, we can infer what kinds of learned context can override correct logical judgments and where a model’s stability boundaries lie.

出于逻辑原因，我们关注不可满足到可满足以及有效到无效的反转。即使前缀被解释为附加前提，这两种逆转都不会根据基准语义获得许可。添加信息不能使不一致的集合可满足，并且单调蕴涵不能仅仅因为添加更多信息而变得无效。因此，这些方向上的成功前缀会重定向模型的判断，而不是提供合法的前提、证明或反例。We focus on unsatisfiable-to-satisfiable and valid-to-invalid reversals for a logical reason. Even if the prefix were interpreted as additional premises, neither reversal is licensed under the benchmark semantics. Adding information cannot make an inconsistent set satisfiable, and monotonic entailment cannot become invalid merely because more information is added. A successful prefix in these directions therefore redirects the model’s judgment rather than supplying a legitimate premise, proof, or counterexample.

这些方向造成了重要的解释模糊性。两者都将少数答案转向多数答案，并且来自其他类别的每个示例都已经具有目标标签。因此，始终目标系统会翻转每个目标答案，同时保留其他类别。高翻转和低伤害可以确定干预是强大的并转移到看不见的逻辑形式，但它不能建立选择性逻辑编辑。因此，核心问题是什么样的行为会产生这种模式。These directions create an important interpretive ambiguity. Both move a minority answer toward the majority answer, and every example from the other class already has the target label. An always-target system would therefore flip every targeted answer while preserving the other class. High flip with low damage can establish that an intervention is strong and transfers to unseen logical forms, but it cannot establish selective logical editing. The central question is therefore what kind of behavior produces this pattern.

我们区分三种可能性。前缀可以强制使用固定的输出符号，在一项任务中创建对目标答案的广泛偏好，或者实现在有效性和可满足性之间转换的更通用的操作。不变的形式问题和精确的语义在整个测试中保持正确的答案固定。随机答案映射测试固定符号强制。目标答案率、直接得分奖励和拟合得分模型测试广泛的答案偏好，并衡量其在示例中表现的一致性。跨任务传输测试前缀是否实现了两个任务之间的共享操作。按逻辑形式、新措辞和重新表述的提示进行拆分可以测试效果的概括程度。随机前缀和可读文本控件测试它是否反映了对添加上下文的一般敏感性，而反向方向则揭示了其方向限制。支持激活分析，然后检查最终答案的更改可以在哪里被中断。总之，这些实验为原本不透明的前缀提供了功能解释，而不将它们视为可读指令或逻辑编辑。We distinguish three possibilities. A prefix may force a fixed output symbol, create a broad preference for the target answer within one task, or implement a more general operation that transfers between validity and satisfiability. The unchanged formal problem and exact semantics keep the correct answer fixed throughout these tests. Randomized answer mappings test for fixed-symbol forcing. Target-answer rates, direct score bonuses, and fitted score models test for broad answer preference and measure how consistently it acts across examples. Cross-task transfer tests whether the prefix implements a shared operation across the two tasks. Splits by logical form, new wordings, and rephrased prompts test how well the effect generalizes. Random-prefix and readable-text controls test whether it reflects generic sensitivity to added context, while reverse directions reveal its directional limits. Supporting activation analyses then examine where the final-answer change can be interrupted. Together, these experiments give otherwise opaque prefixes a functional interpretation without treating them as readable instructions or logical edits.

跨Qwen3.6-35B-A3B教育部Across Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE[19],Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-8B[18]和杰玛 4 31B, and Gemma 4 31B[9]，学习的前缀重定向了许多以前对未见过的逻辑形式的正确判断，并且在措辞或提示结构发生变化时仍然有效。在新措辞和改写提示条件下，Qwen3.6 MoE 的翻转率范围为 72% 至 90%，Qwen3-8B 的翻转率范围为 53% 至 72%，Gemma 4 31B 的翻转率范围为 40% 至 56%。在逻辑形式的四个不同分区中，学习前缀在所有 16 个 Qwen3.6 和 Gemma 比较中都优于配对随机对照，优势为 37 到 99 个百分点。匹配的迁移结果对于 Gemma 有效性尤其明显，其中学习的前缀在新措辞和改写提示下保留 54% 至 56% 的翻转，而匹配的随机前缀保持在 1% 以下。补充性 Qwen3.6 测试表明，即使是从 1,000 个随机候选者中选出的最强前缀，传输量也远低于学习到的前缀。, learned prefixes redirect many previously correct judgments on unseen logical forms and remain effective when wording or prompt structure changes. Across the new-wording and rephrased-prompt conditions, flip rates range from 72% to 90% for Qwen3.6 MoE, 53% to 72% for Qwen3-8B, and 40% to 56% for Gemma 4 31B. Across four different partitions of the logical forms, learned prefixes outperform paired random controls in all 16 Qwen3.6 and Gemma comparisons, with advantages of 37 to 99 percentage points. The matched transfer results are especially clear for Gemma validity, where learned prefixes retain 54% to 56% flip under new wordings and rephrased prompts, while matched random prefixes remain below 1%. A complementary Qwen3.6 test shows that even the strongest prefixes selected from 1,000 random candidates transfer substantially less than the learned prefixes.

诊断配置文件显示，主导效应是对一个答案含义的广泛偏好，而不是固定的输出符号或在两个测试任务之间可靠传输的逻辑运算。这种广泛的答案偏好解释了大部分的总体效应，但它并不能完全解释单个例子的反应有多强烈。使用答案类别和无前缀边距的评分模型更接近 Gemma 的整体前缀边距，远好于 Qwen 的。对于这两个 Qwen 模型，尽管它们的规模和架构不同，但这些模型通常会预测哪些判断将跨越决策边界，但通常不会预测它们的最终利润。因此，类似的总转向率可以隐藏模型之间不同的响应模式。The diagnostic profile shows that the dominant effect is a broad preference for one answer meaning rather than a fixed output symbol or a logical operation that transfers reliably between the two tested tasks. This broad answer preference explains much of the aggregate effect, but it does not fully explain how strongly individual examples respond. Similar aggregate steering rates can therefore conceal different response patterns across models.

Their work asks which internal components support correct syllogistic inference. We study a complementary question: how learned prefix interventions alter judgments that the model initially answers correctly, and whether the resulting changes resemble broad answer bias or more input-dependent behavior. We therefore use activation comparisons and patching to characterize where the intervention affects the final judgment, rather than to claim discovery of the model’s underlying reasoning circuit.

3 预赛Preliminaries

3.1 三段论任务和精确标签Syllogistic tasks and exact labels

我们使用分类三段论，因为它们提供了一个紧凑的形式设置，在该设置中可以准确计算正确答案并将其与用于表达问题的单词分开。每个例子包含两个前提和一个候选结论。例如，We use categorical syllogisms because they provide a compact formal setting in which the correct answer can be computed exactly and separated from the words used to express the problem. Each example contains two premises and a candidate conclusion. For example,

All ​R​ are ​Q,All ​Q​ are ​P,therefore all ​R​ are ​P.\text{All }R\text{ are }Q,\qquad\text{All }Q\text{ are }P,\qquad\text{therefore all }R\text{ are }P.

每个满足两个前提的解释也满足结论，因此该论证是有效的。Every interpretation satisfying the two premises also satisfies the conclusion, so the argument is valid.

我们研究两个二元任务：We study two binary tasks:

  1. 1.

    有效性。Validity.只要前提为真，候选结论就一定为真吗？Must the candidate conclusion be true whenever the premises are true?

  2. 2.

    满意度。Satisfiability.前提和候选结论能同时成立吗？Can the premises and candidate conclusion all be true together?

有效性测试必然的结果，而可满足性测试显示的陈述是否共同一致。Validity tests necessary consequence, whereas satisfiability tests whether the displayed statements are jointly consistent.

5 局限性和更广泛的影响Limitations and Broader Impact

范围和统计精度。Scope and statistical precision.

三段论推理是逻辑的受控片段，而不是数学或自然语言推理的完整模型。该基准包含固定非空术语语义下的 256 种形式。通过测试轮换每个任务的所有 24 个少数形式，减少了对原始拆分的依赖，但每个测试拆分仍然只包含 6 个目标形式。不同分组之间的差异还表明，干预强度在很大程度上取决于用于培训和发展的形式。Syllogistic reasoning is a controlled fragment of logic rather than a complete model of mathematical or natural-language reasoning. The benchmark contains 256 forms under a fixed non-empty-term semantics. Rotating all 24 minority forms for each task through test reduces dependence on the original split, but each test split still contains only six targeted forms. The variation across splits also shows that intervention strength depends substantially on the forms used for training and development.

解释和模型比较。Interpretation and model comparison.

标题方向将少数答案转向多数答案，其高目标答案率揭示了强烈的答案偏差成分。因此，结果表征了对优化上下文的鲁棒性，而不是对逻辑结构的选择性编辑。简单的评分模型比 Qwen 更适合 Gemma 的整体反应，但目标示例中的对比取决于任务。跨模型比较还使用校准的特定于模型的接口，并且只有示例能够正确回答而无需前缀。他们描述了这些条件下的响应模式，但没有将模型架构作为其唯一原因。The headline directions move a minority answer toward the majority answer, and their high target-answer rates reveal a strong answer-bias component. The results therefore characterize robustness to optimized context rather than selective editing of logical structure. Simple score models fit Gemma’s overall response better than Qwen’s, but the contrast within targeted examples is task dependent. Cross-model comparisons also use calibrated model-specific interfaces and only examples answered correctly without a prefix. They describe response patterns under these conditions but do not isolate model architecture as their sole cause.

选择和产生。Selection and generation.

生成的答案结果使用根据开发数据选择的前缀，因此不会估计平均前缀性能。 Gemma 有效性生成被省略，因为所选接口下的干净输出对于当前解析器具有很高的解析失败率。Generated-answer results use prefixes selected on development data and therefore do not estimate average-prefix performance. Gemma validity generation is omitted because clean outputs under the chosen interface have a high parse-failure rate with the current parser.

活化分析。Activation analysis.

层比较是描述性的，激活修补使用选定的翻转情况、固定层网格和一层替换。全提示令牌补丁故意是粗糙的，而内容对齐的补丁传输在移位的序列位置计算的状态。在该协议下，实验显示了最终答案效果可以在哪里被中断，但它们没有识别推理电路或确定底层逻辑计算被中断。The layer comparisons are descriptive, and activation patching uses selected flipped cases, a fixed layer grid, and one-layer substitutions. The all-prompt-token patch is deliberately coarse, while content-aligned patching transfers states computed at shifted sequence positions. Under this protocol, the experiments show where the final-answer effect can be interrupted, but they do not identify a reasoning circuit or establish that the underlying logical computation was disrupted.

更广泛的影响。Broader impact.

这项工作更广泛的影响主要是方法论上的。我们的框架提供了一种受控诊断，用于测量模型的逻辑稳定性并识别重定向正确判断的上下文压力类型。随着针对提示和周围环境变化的应用程序开发出更稳健的模型，该方法可用于比较模型行为并测试改进是否会提高准确性和答案稳健性。尽管目前的基准仅限于三段论推理，但相同的设计（精确标签、受控干预、看不见的形式和匹配控制）可以扩展到其他正式定义的推理任务。The broader impact of this work is primarily methodological. Our framework provides a controlled diagnostic for measuring a model’s logical stability and identifying the kinds of contextual pressure that redirect correct judgments. As more robust models are developed for applications in which prompts and surrounding context vary, the method can be used to compare model behavior and test whether improvements increase both accuracy and answer robustness. Although the present benchmark is limited to syllogistic reasoning, the same design—exact labels, controlled interventions, unseen forms, and matched controls—could be extended to other formally defined reasoning tasks.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项研究中，我们引入了一个受控的软前缀框架，用于压力测试正确的三段论判断。在 Qwen3.6 MoE、Qwen3-8B 和 Gemma 4 31B 中，学习前缀将许多以前正确的答案重定向到未见过的逻辑形式上，并且在措辞或提示短语发生变化时仍然有效。In this study, we introduced a controlled soft-prefix framework for stress-testing correct syllogistic judgments. Across Qwen3.6 MoE, Qwen3-8B, and Gemma 4 31B, learned prefixes redirect many previously correct answers on unseen logical forms and remain effective when wording or prompt phrasing changes.

主要聚合模式是对目标答案的广泛偏好，但这并不能完全解释每个模型的响应。简单的分数转换比 Qwen 更接近 Gemma 的整体响应。这表明，Gemma 的脆弱性在答案分数级别上更具全局性，而 Qwen 的脆弱性在示例中更具异质性：许多翻转的方向是可预测的，但它们的最终大小却不可预测。因此，类似的总翻转率可以掩盖逻辑失败的规律性和可预测性方面的差异。这是测试接口下的行为区别，而不是关于模型内部推理机制的声明。测试任务接口之间的弱传递进一步表明，学习效果的行为并不像共享逻辑操作。激活分析表明，与仅恢复三段论或答案标记相比，恢复所有提示标记状态可以中断更多的答案更改。通过描述哪些学习到的前缀成功以及它们在不同模型中的效果有何不同，该框架将原本不透明的连续干预转变为形式推理中答案稳定性的功能性探索。The dominant aggregate pattern is a broad preference for the target answer, but this does not fully explain every model’s response. Simple score transformations approximate Gemma’s overall response more closely than Qwen’s. This suggests that Gemma’s vulnerability is more globally structured at the answer-score level, whereas Qwen’s is more heterogeneous across examples: the direction of many flips is predictable, but their final magnitudes are not. Similar aggregate flip rates can therefore conceal differences in the regularity and predictability of logical failure. This is a behavioral distinction under the tested interfaces, not a claim about the models’ internal reasoning mechanisms. Weak transfer between the tested task interfaces further shows that the learned effect does not behave like a shared logical operation. The activation analyses show that restoring all prompt-token states can interrupt much more of the answer change than restoring only the syllogism or answer token. By characterizing which learned prefixes succeed and how their effects differ across models, the framework turns otherwise opaque continuous interventions into functional probes of answer stability in formal reasoning.
