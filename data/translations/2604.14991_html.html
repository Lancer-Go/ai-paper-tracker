使用基础模型预测电力系统动态轨迹Predicting Power-System Dynamic Trajectories with Foundation Models

Haoran Li, Lihao Mai, Chenhan Xiao, Erik Blasch, and Yang Weng Haoran Li, Lihao Mai, Chenhan Xiao, and Yang Weng are with the School of Electrical, Computer and Energy Engineering at Arizona State University, Tempe, AZ 85281 USA, e-mail: {lhaoran, lmai7, cxiao20, yang.weng}@asu.edu. Erik Blasch is with the MOVEJ Analytics, Dayton, OH 45324 USA, e-mail: erik.blasch@gmail.com.
摘要Abstract

随着电力系统向富含可再生能源和逆变器主导的运行过渡，准确的时域动态分析变得越来越重要。此类分析支持关键操作任务，包括暂态稳定性评估、动态安全分析、意外事件筛选和故障后轨迹评估。在实践中，这些任务可能会面临多种挑战，包括未知且时变的系统参数、数据共享的隐私限制以及快速在线推理的需求。基于非线性微分代数方程 (DAE) 的传统高保真预测需要详细的领域知识，并且计算成本可能很高。与此同时，现有的基于学习的方法通常是针对单个系统进行训练的，因此缺乏对操作条件和物理参数的概括。 因此，本文提出了 LArge Scale Small ODE (LASS)-ODE-Power，一种通用时域预测的学习框架。所提出的方法利用超过 40 GB 的 DAE 或常微分方程 (ODE) 轨迹的大规模预训练来学习可转移表示。由此产生的模型支持跨不同动态状态（包括机电和逆变器驱动系统）的短测量前缀的轨迹预测。因此，该模型可以直接使用，无需在零样本设置中共享数据。此外，所提出的架构结合了并行和线性化计算来实现快速推理。此外，为了增强电力系统的特定任务性能，基于大约1GB的异构电力系统动态数据开发了专门的微调策略。针对不同电力系统仿真场景的大量实验表明，LASS-ODE-Power 在轨迹预测精度和高效推理方面始终优于现有的基于学习的模型。As power systems transition toward renewable-rich and inverter-dominated operations, accurate time-domain dynamic analysis becomes increasingly critical. Such analysis supports key operational tasks, including transient stability assessment, dynamic security analysis, contingency screening, and post-fault trajectory evaluation. In practice, these tasks may operate under several challenges, including unknown and time-varying system parameters, privacy constraints on data sharing, and the need for fast online inference. Conventional high-fidelity prediction based on nonlinear differential–algebraic equations (DAEs) requires detailed domain knowledge and can be computationally expensive. Meanwhile, existing learning-based approaches are typically trained for individual systems and therefore lack generalization across operating conditions and physical parameters. Hence, this paper proposes LArge Scale Small ODE (LASS)-ODE-Power, a learning framework for general-purpose time-domain prediction. The proposed approach leverages large-scale pretraining on more than 40 GB of DAE or ordinary differential-equation (ODE) trajectories to learn transferable representations. The resulting model supports trajectory prediction from short measurement prefixes across diverse dynamic regimes, including electromechanical and inverter-driven systems. Hence, the model can be directly used without data sharing in a zero-shot setting. In addition, the proposed architecture incorporates parallel and linearized computation to achieve fast inference. Moreover, to enhance task-specific performance in power systems, a specialized fine-tuning strategy is developed based on approximately 1 GB of heterogeneous power-system dynamic data. Extensive experiments over diverse power-system simulation scenarios demonstrate that LASS-ODE-Power consistently outperforms existing learning-based models in trajectory prediction accuracy with efficient inference.

I 简介Introduction

在基于逆变器的资源（IBR）的高渗透率的推动下，电网正在经历结构转型The electric power grid is undergoing a structural transition driven by high penetrations of inverter-based resources (IBRs)[2]，扩展AC/DC互连, expanding AC/DC interconnections[41]，以及日益变化的操作条件, and increasingly variable operating conditions[20]。这些变化降低了运营利润并增加了对准确时域动态分析的需求。此类分析支持电力系统运行中的多项关键任务，包括暂态稳定性评估. These changes reduce operational margins and increase the need for accurate time-domain dynamic analysis. Such analysis supports several critical tasks in power system operation, including transient stability assessment[19, 46]、动态安全评估（DSA）, dynamic security assessment (DSA)[44]，和动态状态估计, and dynamic state estimation[35, 37, 23]。对于应急筛查也很重要. It is also important for contingency screening[51]和故障后轨迹分析and post-fault trajectory analysis[25, 26, 24]。在现代电网运行中，必须在严格的时间限制下跨多个运行点、拓扑配置和扰动场景重复执行此类模拟. In modern grid operations, such simulations must be executed repeatedly across many operating points, topology configurations, and disturbance scenarios under strict time constraints[44, 19, 27]。因此，可扩展且高效的时域模拟和预测对于规划和实时作战研究变得越来越重要。. As a result, scalable and efficient time-domain simulation and prediction have become increasingly important for both planning and real-time operational studies.

在实践中，这一目标带来了一些挑战。首先，由于运行条件的变化、控制器更新等，动态模型参数通常是不确定的或时变的。此外，故障事件、保护动作和开关操作会引起离散模式转换，从而产生分段混合轨迹，而使用单个固定参数模型很难捕获这些轨迹In practice, this goal introduces several challenges. First, dynamic-model parameters are often uncertain or time-varying due to changing operating conditions, controller updates, etc. In addition, fault events, protection actions, and switching operations induce discrete mode transitions that produce piecewise hybrid trajectories that are difficult to capture using a single fixed-parameter model[49, 21]。其次，测量数据质量问题普遍存在，包括噪声、缺失通道、异步采样和部分可观测性，这些问题可能会降低数值稳定性和学习代理的泛化性能. Second, measurement data-quality issues are pervasive, including noise, missing channels, asynchronous sampling, and partial observability, which can degrade numerical stability and the generalization performance of learned surrogates[52, 29, 36]。第三，隐私和专有限制通常会阻止运营商和供应商在集中式培训环境中共享测量结果和设备参数. Third, privacy and proprietary constraints often prevent operators and vendors from sharing measurements and device parameters in centralized training environments[9]。最后，实际研究需要在严格的延迟预算内评估大量的干扰场景和操作条件，因此高吞吐量和快速推理至关重要. Finally, practical studies require evaluating large ensembles of disturbance scenarios and operating conditions within tight latency budgets, making high-throughput and fast inference essential[8, 6].

从数学上讲，时域动态预测对应于求解由微分代数方程 (DAE) 描述的非线性初始值问题，微分代数方程将设备级微分动力学与代数网络约束耦合起来。在可再生能源丰富且以逆变器为主的电网中，由此产生的动态表现出明显的多时间尺度行为和混合切换事件。因此，实际研究可能依赖于具有显式事件处理的电磁瞬态 (EMT) 仿真或 EMT-瞬态稳定性 (TS) 混合/协同仿真Mathematically, time-domain dynamic prediction corresponds to solving nonlinear initial-value problems described by differential-algebraic equations (DAEs) that couple device-level differential dynamics with algebraic network constraints. In renewable-rich and inverter-dominated grids, the resulting dynamics exhibit pronounced multi-timescale behavior and hybrid switching events. Practical studies may therefore rely on electromagnetic-transient (EMT) simulation or EMT-transient-stability (TS) hybrid/co-simulation with explicit event handling[47, 31]。此类模拟越来越多地与现场测量相结合，以捕获实时系统动态并使用具有不同采样率和分辨率的异构监控流来校准模型. Increasingly, such simulations are aligned with field measurements to capture real-time system dynamics and calibrate models using heterogeneous monitoring streams with different sampling rates and resolutions[38, 22].

当精确的参数和模型可用时，经典的高保真数值模拟和预测仍然是分析电力系统动力学的黄金标准。例如，EMT 仿真和 EMT-TS 联合仿真提供了动态行为的高度详细的表示Classic high-fidelity numerical simulation and prediction remain the gold standard for analyzing power-system dynamics when accurate parameters and models are available. Consequently, evaluating large ensembles of disturbance scenarios can become throughput-limited even when accelerated using GPU or high-performance computing techniques[50]。此外，不确定的参数、重复的初始化过程和随时间变化的操作条件会进一步降低仿真保真度。 为了降低计算成本并改进不确定参数下的建模，基于学习的动态预测方法最近引起了人们的关注。符号回归方法尝试直接从轨迹数据恢复控制方程，例如使用遗传编程. 低秩适应 (LoRA) 是一种标准的参数高效微调方法，它通过将可训练的低秩更新注入选定的权重矩阵来适应预训练的基础模型，从而避免全参数重新训练。我们专注于 LoRA 来微调 LASS-ODE。在这项工作中，微调信号是从特定领域的数据集（即电力系统 ODE/DAE 轨迹）中提取的。然而，电力系统动力学形成了一个广泛且内部异构的域：可用的轨迹可能来自实质上不同的子域，包括 EMT 信号、同步相量尺度响应以及具有不同时间分辨率和动态特性的其他模拟机制。这种异质性会在微调过程中引起专业化与泛化的权衡。纳入多样化的轨迹集合可以提高领域覆盖范围并支持更广泛的泛化，但过度的异质性可能会掩盖子组特定的动态结构并削弱专业化。相反，对狭窄限制的子集进行微调可以提高特定机制的专业化，同时减少跨更广泛的电力系统域的转移。为了解决专业化-泛化挑战，我们提出了一种混合 LoRA 机制，用于使 LASS-ODE 适应异构电力系统数据集。中心思想是自动识别轨迹中的子组并将它们与不同的低秩适应专家相关联，以便微调保留异构子组之间的多样性，同时确保每个子组贡献一致的适应信号。Low-rank Adaptation (LoRA) is a standard parameter-efficient fine-tuning method that adapts a pretrained foundation model by injecting trainable low-rank updates into selected weight matrices, thereby avoiding full-parameter retraining. We focus on LoRA to fine-tune LASS-ODE. In this work, the fine-tuning signals are drawn from domain-specific datasets, namely, power-system ODE/DAE trajectories. However, power-system dynamics form a broad and internally heterogeneous domain: the available trajectories may arise from substantially different subdomains, including EMT signals, synchrophasor-scale responses, and other simulation regimes with distinct temporal resolutions and dynamic characteristics. This heterogeneity induces a specialization-generalization tradeoff during fine-tuning. Incorporating a diverse collection of trajectories can improve domain coverage and support broader generalization, but excessive heterogeneity may obscure subgroup-specific dynamic structure and weaken specialization. Conversely, fine-tuning on a narrowly restricted subset can improve specialization to a particular regime while reducing transfer across the broader power-system domain. To address the specialization-generalization challenge, we propose a mixture-of-LoRA mechanism for adapting LASS-ODE to heterogeneous power-system datasets. The central idea is to automatically identify subgroups in the trajectories and associate them with different low-rank adaptation experts, so that fine-tuning preserves diversity across heterogeneous subgroups while ensuring that each subgroup contributes a coherent adaptation signal.

八VIII结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 LASS-ODE-Power，这是通用电力系统时域仿真的基础模型。给定在线测量的前缀，LASS-ODE-Power 可以预测未来的系统演变。它是一个大规模预训练模型，仅使用数据即可适应不同的仿真任务，例如频率稳定性、电压稳​​定性和 EMT 仿真，而无需了解底层 ODE/DAE 参数。具体来说，我们展示了如何通过在电力系统数据集上微调我们的先前模型 LASS-ODE 来获得 LASS-ODE-Power。为了处理这些数据集的多时间尺度和异构性质，我们提出了一种新颖的 LoRA 混合策略，该策略可以跨不同的电力系统动态组进行适应。最后，对不同电力系统任务的大量实验表明，LASS-ODE-Power 始终能够实现最低的预测误差和快速推理。This paper introduces LASS-ODE-Power, a foundation model for general-purpose power-system time-domain simulation. Given a prefix of online measurements, LASS-ODE-Power predicts the future system evolution. It is a large-scale pretrained model that can be adapted, using only data, to different simulation tasks, such as frequency stability, voltage stability, and EMT simulation, without requiring knowledge of the underlying ODE/DAE parameters. Specifically, we show how to obtain LASS-ODE-Power by fine-tuning our prior model, LASS-ODE, on power-system datasets. To handle the multi-timescale and heterogeneous nature of these datasets, we propose a novel mixture-of-LoRA strategy that performs adaptation across different groups of power-system dynamics. Finally, extensive experiments on diverse power-system tasks demonstrate that LASS-ODE-Power consistently achieves the lowest prediction error and fast inference.
