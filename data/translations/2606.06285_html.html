TRACE：多模态时间序列基础模型的时间条件估计TRACE: A Temporal Conditional Estimation for Multimodal Time Series Foundation Models

Ziwen Kan    Yishuo Chen    Kecheng Li    Andrew Wen    Xiaomeng Wang    Liwei Wang    Jihao Duan    Song Wang    Hongfang Liu    Tianlong Chen
摘要Abstract

时间序列基础模型（TS-FM）旨在学习可泛化的时间表示 可以适应广泛的下游任务。 在现实世界的多模式环境中，时间序列经常受到时间错位的影响 和部分模态缺失，其中在异构处观察到不同的模态 时间尺度或部分缺失。 现有的方法通常依赖于朴素的插补或掩蔽策略， 无法考虑跨模式依赖性，并且经常导致错位 或退化的表示。 我们建议Time series foundation models (TS-FMs) aim to learn generalizable temporal representations that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks. In real-world multimodal settings, time series are frequently affected by temporal misalignment and partial modality missingness, where different modalities are observed at heterogeneous time scales or are partially absent. Existing approaches typically rely on naive imputation or masking strategies, which fail to account for cross-modal dependencies and often lead to misaligned or degraded representations. We propose追踪TRACE，多模态时间序列基础模型管道的条件估计范例 在缺失和不规则抽样的情况下， 允许从可用的辅助模式中系统地推断出不完整的目标模式。 我们在涵盖医疗保健和情感计算的多种多模式基准上评估 TRACE， 包括 MIMIC-IV 临床数据集以及用于多模态情感分析的 CMU-MOSI 和 CMU-MOSEI 基准。 在一系列下游预测任务和缺失模态设置中， TRACE 始终优于先前的多模态融合方法， 证明了对严重模态缺失的鲁棒性提高和更可靠的跨模态表示。, a conditional estimation paradigm for multimodal time series foundation model pipelines under missingness and irregular sampling, allowing incomplete target modalities to be systematically inferred from available auxiliary modalities. We evaluate TRACE on diverse multimodal benchmarks spanning healthcare and affective computing, including the MIMIC-IV clinical dataset and the CMU-MOSI and CMU-MOSEI benchmarks for multimodal sentiment analysis. Across a range of downstream prediction tasks and missing-modality settings, TRACE consistently outperforms prior multimodal fusion approaches, demonstrating improved robustness to severe modality missingness and more reliable cross-modal representations.

2.1 多模态时间序列基础模型Multimodal Time Series Foundation Models

大多数现有的多模态 TS-FM 隐含地假设模态特定的表示是完全观察到的并且易于对齐以进行融合，从而限制了它们在缺失和不规则采样下的适用性。 例如，时代FMMost existing multimodal TS-FMs implicitly assume that modality-specific representations are fully observed and readily alignable for fusion, limiting their applicability under missingness and irregular sampling. For example, TimesFM(Das et al., 2024)是一种仅解码器的预测 FM，需要在预处理期间过滤缺失值，从而将其使用限制为具有完整观测值的精选设置。 即时播报, a decoder-only forecasting FM, requires filtering missing values during preprocessing, restricting its use to curated settings with complete observations. PromptCast(Xue and Salim, 2023)结合语言信号进行预测，但同样假设融合之前有完整的观察结果。 其他方法依赖于特定于任务的插值；例如，节奏incorporates language signals for forecasting but similarly assumes complete observations prior to fusion. Other approaches rely on task-specific interpolation; for instance, TEMPO(Cao et al., 2023)在公司投资分析中应用零填充，这取决于领域知识，并且不能很好地跨多模式设置进行概括。 达姆applies zero-filling in company investment analysis, which depends on domain knowledge and does not generalize well across multimodal settings. DAM(Darlow et al., 2024)将时间序列建模为时间的连续函数，自然支持不规则观测和无水平预测。 然而，它主要针对预测和信号级回归，并且不解决跨异构模式的表示级对齐和融合。models time series as continuous functions of time, naturally supporting irregular observations and horizon-free forecasting. However, it primarily targets forecasting and signal-level regression, and does not address representation-level alignment and fusion across heterogeneous modalities.

最近的 TS-FM 开始明确考虑缺失。 弹性教育部More recent TS-FMs begin to explicitly consider missingness. FlexMoE(Yun et al., 2024)通过灵活的专家路由提高了鲁棒性，但仍然假设模态表示在融合之前可用。 时间迪特improves robustness through flexible expert routing, yet still assumes that modality representations are available prior to fusion. TimeDiT(Cao et al., 2024)通过掩蔽和基于扩散的去噪来解决缺失问题，但仅在单模态设置中运行，并且不包含跨模态调节。 熔丝MoEaddresses missingness via masking and diffusion-based denoising, but operates exclusively in unimodal settings and does not incorporate cross-modal conditioning. FuseMoE(Han et al., 2024)通过混合专家融合，进一步将这条线延伸到模态层面的缺失；然而，它依赖于确定性插补在融合之前填充缺失的模态表示，将估计视为预处理步骤而不是学习的条件过程。 总体而言，现有的 TS-FM 要么忽略缺失，要么依赖确定性或单模态估计，而不在多模态融合之前执行跨模态条件估计。further extends this line to modality-level missingness through mixture-of-experts fusion; however, it relies on deterministic imputation to populate missing modality representations before fusion, treating estimation as a preprocessing step rather than a learned, conditional process. Overall, existing TS-FMs either ignore missingness or rely on deterministic or unimodal estimation, without performing cross-modal conditional estimation prior to multimodal fusion.

5 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

范式通用性与领域专业化。Paradigm Generality vs. Domain Specialization.

我们注意到，在某些 MIMIC-IV 任务中，TRACE 的表现并不总是优于 HAIM。 这一观察结果是预期的，并且反映了建模范围的根本差异，而不是所提出的范式的限制。 特别是，对于 25-PHE 任务，HAIM 实现了强大的 F1 性能，因为该任务强调整个 ICU 住院期间的事件级表型检测，较少依赖于细粒度的时间动态，同时对 F1 指标下的罕见标签敏感。 HAIM 是通过全停留特征聚合和基于 XGBoost 的分类，明确为这种聚合主导的目标而设计的，因此在 25-PHE 等有利于全局聚合而不是时间分辨率的任务上表现强劲。 从不同的角度来看，性能差距凸显了领域专用架构和范式级泛化之间固有的权衡。 虽然 HAIM 等特定于任务的管道在其归纳偏差与目标紧密匹配时可以表现出色，但 TRACE 等通用框架旨在跨异构模式、缺失模式和下游任务稳健运行。 通过 TRACE 中的轻量级领域先验或任务感知调节来弥合这一差距，代表了未来工作的一个有希望的方向。We note that on certain MIMIC-IV tasks, TRACE does not consistently outperform HAIM. This observation is expected and reflects a fundamental difference in modeling scope rather than a limitation of the proposed paradigm. In particular, for the 25-PHE task, HAIM achieves strong F1 performance, as this task emphasizes episode-level phenotype detection over the entire ICU stay and is less dependent on fine-grained temporal dynamics, while being sensitive to rare labels under the F1 metric. HAIM is explicitly designed for this aggregation-dominated objective through full-stay feature aggregation and XGBoost-based classification, and accordingly performs strongly on tasks such as 25-PHE that favor global aggregation over temporal resolution. Viewed from a different perspective, the performance gap highlights an inherent trade-off between domain-specialized architectures and paradigm-level generalization. While task-specific pipelines such as HAIM can excel when their inductive biases closely match the objective, general frameworks like TRACE are designed to operate robustly across heterogeneous modalities, missingness patterns, and downstream tasks. Bridging this gap through lightweight domain priors or task-aware conditioning within TRACE represents a promising direction for future work.

缺失建模的范围。Scope of Missingness Modeling.

虽然 TRACE 被制定为多模态缺失下条件表示估计的通用范例，但当前的实例化侧重于以下设置： 模式被部分观察到，而不是完全不存在。 具体来说，我们的目标是模态内缺失，其中每种模态可能都是稀疏的 或不规则采样但至少保留一些观察结果。 对于模态级别的缺失，我们的框架继承了 FuseMoE 的骨干能力，其路由机制已经可以适应融合过程中不可用的模态。 因此，TRACE 旨在当模态存在但仅部分观察到时加强表示估计。 这一重点也与我们的经验成果一致：当一种模态保持部分观察并且补充模态提供足够的背景来恢复丢失的片段时，TRACE 是最有效的。 当整个模态不存在时，问题就会从条件细化转变为完全替代。 将条件估计从部分观察扩展到完全不存在的模式的显式生成仍然是未来工作的一个有趣的方向。While TRACE is formulated as a general paradigm for conditional representation estimation under multimodal missingness, the current instantiation focuses on settings where modalities are partially observed rather than entirely absent. Specifically, we target within-modality missingness, where each modality may be sparsely or irregularly sampled but retains at least some observations. For modality-level absence, our framework inherits the backbone capability of FuseMoE, whose routing mechanism can already adapt to unavailable modalities during fusion. Accordingly, TRACE is intended to strengthen representation estimation when a modality is present but only partially observed. This focus is also consistent with our empirical gains: TRACE is most effective when a modality remains partially observed and complementary modalities provide enough context to recover missing segments. When an entire modality is absent, the problem shifts from conditional refinement to full substitution. Extending conditional estimation from partial observation to explicit generation for entirely absent modalities remains an interesting direction for future work.

两阶段培训的基本原理。Rationale for Two-Stage Training.

TRACE 采用解耦训练策略，因为条件扩散阶段的目的是在施加任何下游目标之前，在部分可观测性下学习任务无关的表示估计。 这两个阶段还优化了不同的目标：扩散模块专注于在生成损失下重建合理的缺失组件，而融合阶段则专注于判别性下游预测，直接耦合这些目标可能会使估计质量偏向特定于任务的捷径。 此外，将迭代去噪纳入端到端优化会大大增加训练成本和优化不稳定性，特别是当扩散范围很长时。TRACE adopts a decoupled training strategy because the conditional diffusion stage is intended to learn task-agnostic representation estimation under partial observability before any downstream objective is imposed. The two stages also optimize different targets: the diffusion module focuses on reconstructing plausible missing components under a generative loss, whereas the fusion stage focuses on discriminative downstream prediction, and directly coupling these objectives may bias estimation quality toward task-specific shortcuts. In addition, incorporating iterative denoising into end-to-end optimization would substantially increase training cost and optimization instability, especially when the diffusion horizon is long.

更广泛的影响。Broader Impacts.

这项工作可以提高多模式缺失设置的稳健性，这对于医疗保健分析等应用可能是有益的。 同时，错误的估计可能会引入下游风险，因此在高风险环境中应仔细评估该方法。This work may improve robustness in multimodal missing settings, which could be beneficial in applications like healthcare analysis. Meanwhile, incorrect estimation may introduce downstream risks, so the method should be used with careful evaluation in high-stakes settings.

6 结论Conclusions

这项工作解决了时间错位和部分模态缺失的挑战 在多模态时间序列基础模型中。 我们介绍This work addresses the challenge of temporal misalignment and partial modality missingness in multimodal time series foundation models. We introduce追踪TRACE，跨模式条件条件的范例 缺失和不规则抽样下的估计。 通过条件扩散对缺失进行建模， TRACE 提供了朴素插补的原则性替代方案 并实现更一致的多模式表示。 多个多模式时间序列设置的实证结果表明 TRACE 提高了鲁棒性和下游性能， 强调显式条件估计的重要性 作为时间序列基础的核心设计原则。, a paradigm for cross-modal conditional estimation under missingness and irregular sampling. By modeling missing through conditional diffusion, TRACE provides a principled alternative to naive imputation and enables more consistent multimodal representations. Empirical results across multiple multimodal time series settings demonstrate that TRACE improves robustness and downstream performance, highlighting the importance of explicit conditional estimation as a core design principle for time series foundation.
