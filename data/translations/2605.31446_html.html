1. 简介Introduction

基于方面的情感分析（ABSA）是一种细粒度的情感分析任务，旨在识别对给定文本中特定方面的情感意见Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) is a fine-grained sentiment analysis task that aims to identify sentiment opinions toward specific aspects in a given text(Schouten and Frasincar, 2016; Lai et al., 2025d)。作为 ABSA 中的代表性复合任务，方面情感三元组提取（ASTE）进一步要求模型提取方面术语、意见术语以及针对方面表达的情感极性作为结构化三元组。与句子级情感分析或方面级情感分类相比，ASTE通过对多个情感相关元素及其关系进行联合建模，提供了更完整、更可解释的观点信息表示。. As a representative compound task in ABSA, Aspect Sentiment Triplet Extraction (ASTE) further requires models to extract aspect terms, opinion terms, and the sentiment polarities expressed toward the aspects as structured triplets. Compared with sentence-level sentiment analysis or aspect-level sentiment classification, ASTE provides a more complete and interpretable representation of opinion information by jointly modeling multiple sentiment-related elements and their relations(Peng et al., 2020; Naglik and Lango, 2024)。这种结构化的情感信息是下游信息系统应用的关键输入，例如意见挖掘、可解释推荐、分面产品分析和评论总结，所有这些都需要可靠的信息检索. Such structured sentiment information is a critical input to downstream information system applications such as opinion mining, explainable recommendation, faceted product analysis, and review summarization, all of which require reliable information retrieval(Lai et al., 2025b; Cui et al., 2023).

最近的研究通过端到端提取模型在 ASTE 方面取得了实质性进展，该模型从不同的角度制定任务，包括序列标记Recent studies have made substantial progress in ASTE through end-to-end extraction models, which formulate the task from different perspectives, including sequence tagging(Yan et al., 2021; Xu et al., 2020)、基于机器阅读理解（MRC）的提取, machine reading comprehension (MRC)-based extraction(Mao et al., 2021; Zhai et al., 2022; Zou et al., 2024)，基于跨度的提取, span-based extraction(Xu et al., 2021; Zhao et al., 2024), 表格填充, table filling(Li et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2024; Peng et al., 2025)，和生成建模, and generative modeling(Gao et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2021b)。 尽管方法上存在差异，但这些方法主要经过优化，以端到端的方式提取准确的三元组预测。 在实践中，他们的推理通常涉及部分解耦的子决策，例如元素提取、方面观点配对和情感分类. Despite their methodological differences, these approaches are primarily optimized to extract accurate triplet predictions in an end-to-end manner. In practice, their inference often involves partially decoupled sub-decisions, such as element extraction, aspect-opinion pairing, and sentiment classification(Chen et al., 2026; Bodke et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024b)。 一些方法通过简单的后处理步骤（例如启发式过滤或多数投票）进一步细化提取管道内的预测. Some methods further refine predictions within the extraction pipeline through simple post-processing steps, such as heuristic filtering or majority voting(Bodke et al., 2025; Gou et al., 2023)。 然而，这些步骤通常依赖于一组受限的提取信号，例如模型概率、解码分数或预测频率。 尽管此类信号对于选择最终预测很有用，但它们仅提供候选可靠性的粗略证据，并且对候选有效性的重视有限，这涉及预测的三元组在语义上是否被支持为完整的结构。 当这些信号与特定模型架构或解码过程相关联时，这种限制会进一步放大. However, these steps typically rely on a constrained set of extraction signals, such as model probabilities, decoding scores, or prediction frequencies. Although such signals are useful for selecting final predictions, they provide only coarse evidence for candidate reliability and place limited emphasis on candidate validity, which concerns whether a predicted triplet is semantically supported as a complete structure. This limitation is further amplified when these signals are tied to specific model architectures or decoding procedures(Li et al., 2024a)，这限制了它们作为三元组有效性的一般指标的使用，并使下游应用程序评估预测可靠性的基础有限。, which restricts their use as general indicators of triplet validity and leaves downstream applications with a limited basis for assessing prediction reliability.

Variants of a gold triplet may appear locally plausible while still exhibiting hierarchical errors, ranging from simple boundary errors to single element errors (e.g., opinion term error) and compound errors involving multiple triplet components. These cases are difficult to resolve solely through the extraction process and motivate a candidate verification mechanism that complements conventional ASTE extraction. Such a mechanism verifies each candidate triplet against the input sentence to assess whether it is semantically supported as a complete structure and to provide diagnostic signals about candidate usability. However, effective verification for ASTE remains challenging. First, candidate invalidity is fine-grained and structured, involving possible errors in span boundaries, triplet elements, element pairings, sentiment polarity, or multiple components simultaneously. Binary validity supervision is too coarse to characterize the nature and severity of candidate errors. Second, candidate usability is inherently graded, since candidates may be fully supported, partially supported, weakly supported, or contradicted by the input sentence. Without fine-grained verification, valid candidates with low extraction confidence may be suppressed, while candidates that appear locally plausible but are not semantically supported by the sentence may still be retained. Therefore, a verifier that produces graded and interpretable signals can better capture both triplet validity and the quality of candidate support.

Instead of replacing existing extractors, FiVeD learns a plug-and-play verifier that assesses the reliability of candidate triplets produced by a base extractor, and operates in two stages. In the first stage, we construct verification supervision from gold ASTE annotations. Specifically, we perform counterfactual candidate mining to synthesize plausible invalid triplets under semantic and syntactic constraints, exposing the verifier to realistic extraction errors. In the second stage, we train the verifier with multi-task learning and automatically learned task weights. The verifier jointly learns validity classification and quality score estimation as main tasks, together with error type classification and rationale generation as auxiliary tasks. During inference, it estimates the validity and quality of candidate triplets and uses the predicted scores to rescore, filter, or rerank extractor outputs.

In this work, we focus on improving generative extractors, which operate in a more open output space than sequence labeling, table filling, and span-based methods and can naturally produce diverse candidate triplets. These properties make them particularly suitable for post-hoc verification through filtering and reranking. We evaluate FiVeD across multiple generative ASTE extractors to assess its effectiveness and flexibility relative to state-of-the-art baselines. Experimental results show that FiVeD consistently improves extraction performance across baselines. 5.1. 方面情感三元组提取Aspect Sentiment Triplet Extraction

Aspect Sentiment Triplet Extraction (ASTE) 是一项细粒度的情感分析任务 提取方面术语、意见术语以及表达的情感极性 作为结构化三元组的方面Aspect Sentiment Triplet Extraction (ASTE) is a fine-grained sentiment analysis task that extracts aspect terms, opinion terms, and the sentiment polarities expressed toward the aspects as structured triplets(Schouten and Frasincar, 2016; Zhang et al., 2024)。 与方面术语提取、观点术语提取、方面级别情感分类、方面观点对提取等子任务相比，ASTE 需要模型联合识别所有情感元素并确定 他们的语义关联。因此，预测的三元组仅在以下情况下才有效： 它的三个组成部分被正确识别并且在上下文中相互一致. Compared with subtasks such as aspect term extraction, opinion term extraction, aspect-level sentiment classification, and aspect-opinion pair extraction, ASTE requires models to jointly identify all sentiment elements and determine their semantic associations. 现有的研究主要集中在改进端到端提取模型。 早期工作将 ASTE 表述为具有专门设计的标记方案的序列标记问题Existing studies have primarily focused on improving end-to-end extraction models. Early work formulates ASTE as a sequence labeling problem with specially designed tagging schemes(Peng et al., 2020; Yan et al., 2021; Xianlong et al., 2023)。为了更好地捕获方面意见交互作用，后续研究采用了明确建模其结构依赖关系的公式，例如基于跨度的提取. Another line of work formulates ASTE as a machine reading comprehension (MRC) task(Mao et al., 2021; Zhai et al., 2022; Zou et al., 2024)。这些方法不是一次性提取三元组，而是将 ASTE 分解为几个查询条件预测步骤，并在推理过程中组装最终的三元组。最近，生成方法将 ASTE 视为文本到文本问题，其中预训练的序列到序列模型以结构化格式生成三元组. Instead of extracting a triplet in a single pass, these methods decompose ASTE into several query-conditioned prediction steps and assemble the final triplets during inference. More recently, generative methods have cast ASTE as a text-to-text problem, where pretrained sequence-to-sequence models generate triplets in a structured format(Zhang et al., 2021a, b)。这些方法受益于预训练语言模型强大的表示和生成能力，并且可以灵活地产生可变长度的结构化输出。基于提示和指令调整的方法进一步利用大型预训练模型的知识和泛化能力进行结构化情感提取. These approaches benefit from the strong representation and generation abilities of pretrained language models and can flexibly produce variable-length structured outputs. Prompt-based and instruction-tuned methods further exploit the knowledge and generalization capabilities of large pretrained models for structured sentiment extraction(Gou et al., 2023).

6. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 FiVeD，一种细粒度验证框架，具有用于方面情感三元组提取的诊断推理。 FiVeD 通过评估提取后候选三元组的有效性和质量来解决三元组验证的挑战性问题。通过多目标监督，包括有效性分类、质量得分估计、错误类型分类和理由生成，FiVeD 通过鼓励验证者联合评估三元组有效性并诊断潜在错误源，实现更细粒度和可解释的验证。 跨多个 ASTE 基线的实验表明，FiVeD 持续提高碱基提取器的提取性能，实现可控的精确率与召回率权衡，并实现卓越的结果。这些发现证明了 FiVeD 作为灵活的即插即用验证模块的有效性。更广泛地说，他们强调了细粒度验证对于增强 ASTE 系统可靠性的价值，并提出了其对更广泛的情感分析和结构化信息提取任务的潜在适用性。In this paper, we propose FiVeD, a fine-grained verification framework with diagnostic reasoning for aspect sentiment triplet extraction. FiVeD addresses the challenging problem of triplet verification by assessing the validity and quality of candidate triplets after extraction. Through multi-objective supervision, including validity classification, quality score estimation, error type classification, and rationale generation, FiVeD enables more fine-grained and interpretable verification by encouraging the verifier to jointly assess triplet validity and diagnose potential error sources. Experiments across multiple ASTE baselines show that FiVeD consistently improves extraction performance for base extractors, enables controllable precision-recall trade-offs, and achieves superior results. These findings demonstrate the effectiveness of FiVeD as a flexible plug-and-play verification module. More broadly, they highlight the value of fine-grained verification for enhancing the reliability of ASTE systems and suggest its potential applicability to broader sentiment analysis and structured information extraction tasks.
