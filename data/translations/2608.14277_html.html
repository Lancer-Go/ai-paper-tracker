SimpleOPD：用于长上下文推理的简单分词器无关的策略蒸馏SimpleOPD: Simple Tokenizer-Agnostic On-Policy Distillation for Long-Context Reasoning

摘要Abstract

在策略蒸馏（OPD）提供了一种从更强的教师模型转移推理能力的有前途的方法，但将其应用于长上下文推理教师和短上下文学生会带来实际挑战，包括分词器不匹配、师生分布不匹配、响应长度爆炸和训练不稳定。在这项工作中，我们通过将证明推理能力从长上下文推理模型 SU-01 转移到短上下文学生模型来研究这种设置。为了处理标记器差异，我们在共享文本空间中执行 OPD，并仅对齐在学生和教师标记器下占据相同文本范围的标记。 为了缓解生成长度过长和频繁截断的问题，我们引入了学生参考 KL 损失并掩盖了特殊终止标记的优点，例如On-policy distillation (OPD) offers a promising way to transfer reasoning capabilities from stronger teacher models, but applying it to long-context reasoning teachers and short-context students introduces practical challenges, including tokenizer mismatch, teacher-student distribution mismatch, response length explosion, and training instability. In this work, we study this setting by transferring proof-reasoning capabilities from the long-context reasoning model SU-01 to short-context student models. To handle tokenizer differences, we perform OPD in a shared text space and align only tokens that occupy identical text spans under the student and teacher tokenizers. This strategy constrains the student from drifting excessively from its initial policy, thereby mitigating the teacher-student distribution mismatch problem and fostering steady length growth. Experiments on both same-family and different-family student models, including Qwen3, Qwen3.5, Intern-S2, GLM-4.7, Gemma-4, show consistent gains in mathematical reasoning, especially natural-language math proving. Notably, Intern-S2-Preview improves by 21.2 points on ProofBench, reaching 55.2 and surpassing Gemini-2.5-Pro. 1 简介Introduction

在政策蒸馏 (OPD)On-policy distillation (OPD)(11; 1)在法学硕士的培训后环境中，针对强到弱蒸馏等任务，提出了一个令人信服的范例presents a compelling paradigm in the post-training landscape of LLMs for tasks such as strong-to-weak distillation(18)、多师蒸馏, multi-teacher distillation(22)和自蒸馏, and self-distillation(12)。与从教师生成的固定数据中提取知识的离策略方法不同，OPD 主动询问教师以评估学生生成轨迹中的令牌，从而提供基于学生自己的策略的密集令牌级监督. Unlike off-policy approaches that distill knowledge from fixed teacher-generated data, OPD actively queries the teacher to evaluate tokens in student-generated trajectories, thereby providing dense token-level supervision grounded in the student’s own policy(11; 1), 能够有效提炼复杂的推理行为，因为与离策略设置相比，它会减少遗忘并实现更好的泛化。 然而，之前的工作主要研究共享相同模型家庭和词汇的师生对。将长语境教师的推理能力提炼到短语境学生的推理能力，特别是在不同的模型家庭中，仍然在很大程度上尚未得到充分探索, enabling efficient distillation of complex reasoning behaviors, as it incurs less forgetting and achieves better generalization than off-policy settings. However, prior work has primarily studied teacher–student pairs that share the same model family and vocabulary. Distilling reasoning capabilities from long-context teachers into short-context students, especially across different model families, remains largely underexplored(30).

应用 OPD 将长语境推理教师提炼为短语境推理学生，这带来了一些挑战。首先，教师和学生模型可能使用不同的分词器，使得他们的分词级别分布之间的直接对齐是不可行的Applying OPD to distilling long-context reasoning teachers to short-context reasoning students presents several challenges. First, teacher and student models may use different tokenizers, making direct alignment between their token-level distributions infeasible(36; 5; 29)。其次，模型容量和上下文长度的差异导致了师生分布的严重不匹配. Second, differences in model capacity and context length introduce a substantial teacher–student distribution mismatch(17)。特别是，长语境推理教师通常倾向于超出学生语境预算的答案。通过实验，我们观察到直接 OPD 会导致响应长度快速增长、频繁截断和推理轨迹不完整，最终导致训练不稳定。这个问题对于终止令牌尤其明显，例如. In particular, long-context reasoning teachers often favor responses that exceed the student’s context budget. Through experiments, we observe that direct OPD can cause rapid response-length growth, frequent truncation, and incomplete reasoning trajectories, ultimately destabilizing training. This issue is especially pronounced for termination tokens such as</think>{\scriptstyle<}\,\mathrm{/think}{\scriptstyle>}和and<|im​_​end|>{\scriptstyle<}\,\mathrm{|im\_end|}\,{\scriptstyle>}，因为教师的监督可能会反复阻止学生终止其思考过程或最终反应。, as teacher supervision may repeatedly discourage the student from terminating its thinking process or final response.

在这项工作中，我们重点关注长上下文推理模型 SU-01 的推理能力迁移In this work, we focus on the transfer of reasoning capabilities from the long-context reasoning model SU-01(16)到短上下文学生模型。如图to short-context student models. As shown in Figure2，为了能够跨不同模型系列进行蒸馏，我们在共享文本空间中执行 OPD，并且仅当教师和学生标记占据相同的文本范围时才对齐它们。这提供了可靠的代币级监管，而不需要在不兼容的代币化之间进行人为对应。为了缓解过度的响应长度增长并稳定优化，我们在学生与其初始策略之间引入了参考 KL 散度损失。我们还掩盖了特殊终止令牌的优点，防止教师监督直接抑制终止。这些技术共同限制了过度的政策漂移，缓解了教师与学生分配的不匹配，并促进了学生背景预算内响应长度的稳定增长。, to enable distillation across different model families, we perform OPD in a shared text space and align teacher and student tokens only when they occupy identical text spans. This provides reliable token-level supervision without requiring an artificial correspondence between incompatible tokenizations. To mitigate excessive response-length growth and stabilize optimization, we introduce a reference KL-divergence loss between the student and its initial policy. We also mask the advantages of special termination tokens, preventing teacher supervision from directly suppressing termination. Together, these techniques constrain excessive policy drift, alleviate teacher–student distribution mismatch, and promote steady response-length growth within the student’s context budget.

我们使用 Qwen3、Qwen3.5、Intern-S2-Preview、GLM-4.7 和 Gemma-4 的学生模型在同族和跨族蒸馏设置中评估 SimpleOPDWe evaluate SimpleOPD across both same-family and cross-family distillation settings, using student models from Qwen3, Qwen3.5, Intern-S2-Preview, GLM-4.7, and Gemma-4(32; 27; 13; 35; 9)。在各个模型系列中，SimpleOPD 不断提高数学推理性能，尤其是在自然语言数学证明任务上取得了显着的进步。最值得注意的是，Intern-S2-Preview 在 ProofBench 上提高了 21.2 点. Across model families, SimpleOPD consistently improves mathematical reasoning performance, with particularly strong gains on natural-language mathematical proof tasks. Most notably, Intern-S2-Preview improves by 21.2 points on ProofBench(20)，从 34.0 到 55.2，超越了 Gemini-2.5-Pro，接近了强得多的推理模型的性能（图, from 34.0 to 55.2, surpassing Gemini-2.5-Pro and approaching the performance of substantially stronger reasoning models (Figure1）。蒸馏学生还取得了以科学为导向的基准的改进，包括 HLE 和 HiPhO). The distilled students also achieve improvements on science-oriented benchmarks, including HLE and HiPhO(26; 34)，尽管专门接受了数学推理数据的培训。 这些结果表明，稳定的跨分词器 OPD 可以迁移长上下文教师的推理能力，并且所获得的能力可以推广到训练领域之外。, despite being trained exclusively on mathematical reasoning data. These results demonstrate that stable cross-tokenizer OPD can transfer reasoning capabilities from long-context teachers, and that the acquired capabilities generalize beyond the training domain.

总之，我们的观察和贡献是：In summary, our observations and contributions are:

  • •

    交叉分词器 OPD 可以通过使用学生生成的文本作为共享空间并将标记与相同的文本范围对齐来改进学生模型，而无需完全标记级别对齐。Cross-tokenizer OPD can improve student models without full token-level alignment by using student-generated text as a shared space and aligning tokens with identical text spans.

  • •

    直接OPD转移了长上下文推理能力，但往往会导致长度过度增长、频繁截断和训练不稳定。Direct OPD transfers long-context reasoning capabilities, but it often causes excessive length growth, frequent truncation, and training instability.

  • •

    终止令牌优势屏蔽和学生参考 KL 损失稳定训练并减少截断。较大的师生差距通常需要更强的 KL 正则化。Termination-token advantage masking and student reference KL loss stabilize training and reduce truncation. Larger teacher–student gaps generally require stronger KL regularization.

  • •

    SimpleOPD 在模型系列中表现出一致的性能增益，无需对教师轨迹进行监督微调，同时适度增加蒸馏长度可以进一步改进长式推理。SimpleOPD shows consistent performance gains across model families without supervised fine-tuning on teacher trajectories, while moderately increasing the distillation length can further improve long-form reasoning.
