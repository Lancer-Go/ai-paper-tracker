Piper：可编程分布式训练系统Piper: A Programmable Distributed Training System

Megan Frisella University of WashingtonSeattleWAUSA , Shubham Tiwari University of WashingtonSeattleWAUSA , Andy Ruan University of WashingtonSeattleWAUSA , Yi Pan University of Washington and Shanghai Jiao Tong UniversitySeattleWAUSA , Parker Gustafson University of WashingtonSeattleWAUSA , Mat Jacob University of WashingtonSeattleWAUSA , Gilbert Bernstein University of WashingtonSeattleWAUSA and Stephanie Wang University of WashingtonSeattleWAUSA
摘要。Abstract.

大规模模型训练越来越依赖于组合多种并行策略，例如数据并行、管道并行和专家并行，以及 ZeRO 等节省内存的优化。 用于基础模型预训练的已部署系统通常依赖人类专家手动设计高级并行策略，然后实施相应的低级执行策略，使得系统难以适应新策略。 与此同时，许多通用框架更加灵活，但它们的实现仍然依赖于一组固定的常见并行策略，这使得集成最先进的策略具有挑战性。Large-scale model training increasingly relies on composing multiple parallelism strategies, such as data, pipeline, and expert parallelism, together with memory-saving optimizations like ZeRO. Deployed systems for foundation model pretraining often rely on human experts to manually design a high-level parallelism strategy then implement the corresponding low-level execution strategy, making it difficult to adapt the system to new strategies. Meanwhile, many general-purpose frameworks are more flexible but their implementations are still tied to a fixed set of common parallelism strategies, making it challenging to integrate state-of-the-art strategies.

我们推出 Piper，一个用户可控的分布式训练系统，它将策略与运行时实现解耦。 Piper 允许用户使用一小组模型注释和调度指令来声明全面的分布式训练策略。 每个指令都对 Piper 的中间表示 (IR) 进行转换，IR 是表示所有计算和通信的统一全局训练 DAG。 使用此 IR，Piper 编译每个设备的执行计划，并使用与策略无关的分布式运行时来执行它们。 我们表明，组合系统在 ZeRO 等常用策略上保持了性能平价，同时还通过在 DeepSeek-V3 的 DualPipe 等组合并行策略中联合调度计算和通信来实现额外的性能和内存效率增益。We present Piper, a user-controllable distributed training system that decouples the strategy from the runtime implementation. Piper allows users to declare a comprehensive distributed training strategy with a small set of model annotations and scheduling directives. Each directive applies a transformation on Piper’s intermediate representation (IR), a unified global training DAG that represents all computation and communication. Using this IR, Piper compiles per-device execution plans and executes them with a distributed runtime agnostic to the strategy. We show that the combined system maintains performance parity on commonly available strategies such as ZeRO, while also enabling additional performance and memory efficiency gains through joint scheduling of compute and communication in composed parallelism strategies such as DeepSeek-V3’s DualPipe.

1. 简介Introduction

随着机器学习 (ML) 模型规模的增大，预训练现在需要扩展到数百到数千个加速器。 这是具有挑战性的，因为有很多方法可以分片和复制模型，并且每种方法都会引入不同且可能相互作用的通信开销。 例如，现代工作负载现在使用数据 (DP)、张量 (TP)、专家 (EP)、上下文 (CP) 和管道 (PP) 并行性以及 ZeRO 等内存节省优化的组合As machine learning (ML) models have increased in size, pretraining now requires scaling across hundreds to thousands of accelerators. This is challenging because there are many ways to shard and replicate the model, and each introduces different and possibly interacting communication overheads. For example, modern workloads now use combinations of data (DP), tensor (TP), expert (EP), context (CP) and pipeline (PP) parallelism together with memory-saving optimizations such as ZeRO(Rajbhandari et al., 2019)。没有一种万能的解决方案，因为正确的策略取决于工作负载和硬件。. There is no one-size fits-all solution, as the right strategy depends on the workload and hardware.

工作负载的分布式训练策略可以分解为：(1) 高级并行策略，例如分片和复制模型和激活的维度，以及 (2) 每个设备用于执行高级并行策略规定的计算和通信操作的低级策略。 前一种策略确定每个设备的最小内存、计算和通信负载，这反过来又限制了整个系统吞吐量的上限。 后一种策略决定了通过有效调度每个设备的资源，系统能够接近上限的程度。 这两种策略共同决定了工作负载的实际吞吐量。A workload’s distributed training strategy can be decomposed into: (1) the high-level parallelism strategy, such as the dimensions along which to shard and replicate the model and activations, and (2) each device’s low-level strategy for executing the compute and communication operations dictated by the high-level parallelism strategy. The former strategy determines the minimum memory, compute, and communication load per-device, which in turn upper-bounds the overall system throughput. The latter strategy determines how close the system can get to the upper bound by effectively scheduling each device’s resources. The two strategies together determine the workload’s actual throughput.

迄今为止，策略组合空间的巨大规模使得完全自动化变得棘手。 因此，虽然某些解决方案可以在特定子空间内找到最优策略The sheer size of the combined space of strategies has so far made full automation intractable. Thus, while some solutions can find an optimal strategy within a particular subspace(Chen et al., 2024a; Peng et al., 2019)，实际部署的系统仍然绝大多数依赖人类专家。 例如，DeepSeek-V3 引入了 DualPipe，这是一种自定义 PP 调度，当与 EP 组合时，每个设备都可以使用本地微批量重叠来隐藏 EP 通信开销, the systems deployed in practice still rely overwhelmingly on human experts. For example, DeepSeek-V3 introduced DualPipe, a custom PP schedule that when composed with EP enables each device to use local micro-batch overlapping to hide EP communication overheads(DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025)。 该解决方案需要对高级并行策略进行人为设计，并使用手动实施的每设备执行策略来管理 GPU 内资源，例如分配给计算与通信的流式多处理器 (SM)。 这样的系统需要付出很大的努力；专家必须设计. This solution required human-engineered codesign of the high-level parallelism strategy with a hand-implemented per-device execution strategy to manage intra-GPU resources, such as the streaming multiprocessors (SMs) allocated to compute vs. communication. Such systems require high effort; experts must design并实施and implement专门针对特定模型和集群的固定策略。 这导致系统难以适应新策略。a fixed strategy that is specialized to a particular model and cluster. This results in a system that is hard to adapt to new strategies.

另一方面，通用框架如 MegatronOn the other hand, general-purpose frameworks such as Megatron(Shoeybi et al., 2019), 深速, DeepSpeed(dee, 2025)，和火炬泰坦, and TorchTitan(Liang et al., 2025)提供更灵活且与模型无关的界面，并带有用于调整分布式训练策略的旋钮。 然而，这些框架急切地为每个高级并行维度调度操作，就好像这些维度是独立的一样，这使得从组合策略联合调度操作变得具有挑战性。 例如，从概念上讲，DualPipe 在两个 PP 微批次之间共享一个 GPU；在假设每个微批次都分配了完整 GPU 的现有框架中实现这一点具有挑战性。 虽然基于编译器的框架（例如 JAX/XLA）提供了更通用的张量放置抽象offer a more flexible and model-agnostic interface, with knobs for tuning the distributed training strategy. However, these frameworks eagerly dispatch operations for each high-level parallelism dimension as if the dimensions are independent, making it challenging to jointly schedule operations from composed strategies. For example, conceptually DualPipe shares a GPU between two PP microbatches; this is challenging to implement in existing frameworks that assume that each microbatch is allocated the full GPU. While compiler-based frameworks such as JAX/XLA present a more generic tensor placement abstraction(Xu et al., 2021)它们不是一组固定的旋钮，无法轻松支持任意 PP 计划，也无法控制每个设备的资源。 因此，引入 DualPipe 等新颖策略也需要付出很大的努力。instead of a fixed set of knobs, they cannot easily support arbitrary PP schedules nor control over each device’s resources. Thus, again it requires high effort to introduce novel strategies such as DualPipe.

我们解决的关键问题是分布式训练系统的可扩展性。 我们的目标是构建一个系统，最大限度地减少指定和实施任意分布式训练策略所需的工作量。 因此，我们构建了一个系统，可以在需要更高性能时为通用策略和控制提供良好的性能，使其适合专家工程和自动搜索。 我们的关键见解是解耦The key problem we address is extensibility in distributed training systems. Our goal is to build a system that minimizes the effort needed to specify and implement an arbitrary distributed training strategy. Thus, we build a system that provides good performance for common strategies and control when needed for higher performance, making it amenable to both expert engineering and automated search. Our key insight is to decouple什么what整体的执行策略应该是从the overall execution strategy should be from如何how它是由运行时实现的。it is realized by the runtime.

面临的挑战是：(1) 设计一个用户调度 API，使其能够实现简单的规范，同时对分布式训练策略进行充分的控制；(2) 设计一个中间表示 (IR) 来连接用户 API 和系统运行时；(3) 构建一个高效的系统运行时来执行任意策略。The challenges are to: (1) design a user scheduling API that enables simple specification yet sufficient control over the distributed training strategy, (2) design an intermediate representation (IR) to interface between the user API and system runtime, and (3) build an efficient system runtime to execute an arbitrary strategy.

This can be used to specify high-level parallelism strategies such as the pipeline-parallel stage boundaries or whether to use expert parallelism.

接下来，我们设计一个API，用于为每个设备上所有算子的执行顺序指定相应的低级执行策略。 稻草人解决方案是要求用户通过为 GPU 内资源（例如 CUDA 流）上的运算符编写自己的调度程序来完全指定策略。 这为用户提供了完全控制权，但不切实际。 另一方面，API 还必须具有足够的表达能力，以捕获 GPU 内资源分配的新颖策略，例如 DualPipe。Next, we design an API for specifying the corresponding low-level execution strategy for the execution order of all operators on each device. A strawman solution is to ask the user to fully specify the strategy by writing their own scheduler for operators over intra-GPU resources such as CUDA streams. This provides users with full control but is impractical. On the other hand, the API must also be expressive enough to capture novel strategies for intra-GPU resource allocation such as DualPipe.

为了应对这一挑战，我们公开了一组指令，用于将系统的 IR 降低到每设备执行策略。 我们设计了一个 IR，它捕获全局训练 DAG 中的所有计算和通信操作。 编译器首先从模型代码中提取非分布式 DAG。 然后，用户指定的每个指令都会转换 IR。 指令包括转换，例如将批次拆分为微批次以增加重叠机会，以及将设备流等资源分配给块。 为了在需要时为用户提供更多控制，我们允许用户指定计算节点之间的排序约束。 然后，我们使用通用调度策略来决定每个设备上的执行顺序。 这使得用户能够控制调度的粒度并做出一些决策，而系统则填写其余的决策。 我们保证转换的安全性，即每个用户指令都应该与原始的高层策略兼容。To address this challenge, we expose a set of directives for lowering the system’s IR to a per-device execution strategy. We design an IR that captures all compute and communication operations in a global training DAG. The compiler begins by extracting a non-distributed DAG from the model code. Then, each directive specified by the user transforms the IR. Directives include transforms such as splitting batches into microbatches to increase overlap opportunity and assigning resources such as device streams to chunks. To provide the user more control when needed, we allow the user to specify ordering constraints between compute nodes. We then use a generic scheduling policy to decide the execution order on each device. This enables the user to control the granularity of scheduling and make some decisions while the systems fills in the rest. We guarantee the safety of transformations, i.e. each user directive should be compatible with the original high-level strategy.

最后，我们构建了一个高效、灵活的分布式运行时来执行组合策略。 我们使用集中式调度程序来生成和分发每个设备的本地执行策略。 每个工作线程加载其关联的模型权重，然后执行本地调度以分配共享设备资源，例如内存、通信器和 GPU 流。 与原始模型定义相比，张量算子的执行结果可能是分布式、重叠、乱序等； Piper 保证全局执行计划仍然遵循模型定义和用户指令指定的数据和时间依赖性。Finally, we build an efficient and flexible distributed runtime to execute the combined strategy. We use a centralized scheduler to produce and distribute each device’s local execution strategy. Each worker loads its associated model weights, then performs local scheduling to allocate shared device resources such as memory, communicators, and GPU streams. Compared to the original model definition, the resulting execution of tensor operators may be distributed, overlapped, out-of-order, etc.; Piper guarantees that the global execution plan still respects the data and temporal dependencies specified by the model definition and user directives.

While other generic training frameworks fail to support certain combinations, Piper can support all strategies, enabling 3-8x larger batch sizes. Our contributions include

2. 背景Background

我们首先概述高级并行策略中的一些可能选择。We first overview some possible choices in high-level parallelism strategy.

DP 和 ZeRODP and ZeRO

在DP中In DP(Abadi et al., 2016; Li et al., 2020)，每个工作人员持有模型权重的副本，计算局部梯度，然后使用 allreduce（或 allgather 和 reduce-scatter）对所有副本的梯度进行平均。零度, each worker holds a replica of the model weights, computes a local gradient, then averages the gradients across all replicas using an allreduce (or allgather and reduce-scatter). ZeRO(Rajbhandari et al., 2019)，也称为全分片DP, also known as fully sharded DP(Zhao et al., 2023)，是一种 DP 变体，通过在 DP 等级之间划分优化器、梯度和/或模型权重来减少工作人员之间的冗余状态。需要时，状态会通过 allgather 重新实现，并通过丢弃远程分片或在梯度情况下减少分散来重新分片。通信操作可以与之前或之后的计算操作的计算重叠。, is a DP variant that reduces redundant state across workers by sharding the optimizer, gradients, and/or the model weights across DP ranks. States are rematerialized with allgather when needed, and resharded by either dropping the remote shards or reduce-scatter in the case of gradients. Communication operations can be overlapped with computation of the preceding or following compute operations.

TP、EP 和 CPTP, EP, and CP

每个工作人员都持有模型权重的一个分片，例如 EP 中的专家子集Each worker holds a shard of either the model weights, e.g., a subset of experts in EP(Lepikhin et al., 2020)或 TP 中行/列的子集or a subset of rows/columns in TP(Shoeybi et al., 2019)，或激活，例如 CP 中上下文的子序列, or the activations, e.g., a subsequence of the context in CP(Yang et al., 2025)。 Workers根据分片计划和张量操作执行集体通信，以确保所有激活被正确聚合。与 DP 相比，这些集合在批次计算的关键路径上执行。. Workers execute collective communication according to the sharding plan and tensor operations to ensure that all activations are correctly aggregated. Compared to DP, these collectives execute on the critical path of a batch’s computations.

PP

每个工作人员都持有模型层的一个碎片。由于模型层之间的顺序依赖性，PP 调度使用多个微批次来跨设备重叠执行。 PP 策略的性能取决于一系列复杂的因素，包括设备之间的气泡和通信开销的量。因此，许多 PP 时间表被提出来平衡这些因素Each worker holds a shard of the model layers. Because of the sequential dependency between model layers, PP schedules use multiple microbatches to overlap execution across devices. The performance of a PP strategy depends on a complex set of factors including bubbles between devices and the amount of communication overhead. Thus, many PP schedules have been proposed to balance between such factors(Huang et al., 2019; Narayanan et al., 2021a; Qi et al., 2023)。当激活在“阶段”（即放置在不同设备上的分片）之间传递时，PP 会添加发送-接收通信。与上述并行策略不同，并程最容易使用 MPMD（多程序多数据）方法执行，因为每个等级需要执行不同的操作集和顺序。. PP adds send-receive communications when activations are passed between “stages”, i.e. shards placed on different devices. Unlike the above parallelism strategies, PP is most easily executed with an MPMD (multiple program multiple data) approach due to each rank needing to execute a different set and order of operations.

分布式运行时。Distributed runtime.

分布式运行时负责在每个设备上执行已编译的低级执行策略。 因此，它必须：（1）对策略保持不可知，（2）添加最小的调度开销，以避免在基于 CPU 的调度上阻塞加速器，以及（3）有效地分配每个加速器的共享资源，而不需要要求用户指定低级张量运算符的确切调度。The distributed runtime is responsible for executing the compiled low-level execution strategy on each device. Thus, it must: (1) remain agnostic to the strategy, (2) add minimal scheduling overhead, to avoid blocking accelerators on CPU-based scheduling, and (3) allocate the shared resources of each accelerator efficiently, without needing to ask the user to specify the exact schedule of low-level tensor operators.

4. 设计Design

Piper 有两个组件（Piper has two components (Figure 5）。首先，编译器将带注释的用户模型和用户调度意图转换为分布式执行计划。其次，运行时以与特定执行策略无关的方式在分布式工作线程上执行计划。这些组件一起让 Piper 能够表示并实现一组丰富的训练计划，而无需将特定的并行策略组合或优化硬编码到运行时中。). First, the compiler translates annotated user models and user scheduling intent into a distributed execution plan. Tasks are dispatched asynchronously, as soon as their upstream tasks have been scheduled, to avoid blocking the GPU on CPU scheduling. When two adjacent tasks execute on different streams, Piper inserts synchronization between their streams to preserve correctness using CUDA events and stream-wait. This lets the runtime support arbitrary execution patterns without hard-coding a fixed mapping from operator type to stream.

例如，对于重叠的 PP 通信，应为发送和接收操作符分配与其上游和下游计算操作符不同的流。在这种情况下，通信流在发出发送之前必须与生产者节点的流同步，下游计算节点在消费通信值之前必须与接收节点的流同步。For example, for overlapping PP communication, send and receive operators should be assigned a different stream from their upstream and downstream compute operators. In this case, the communication stream must synchronize with the producer node’s stream before issuing the send, and the downstream compute node must synchronize with the receive node’s stream before consuming the communicated value.

尽管 Piper 必须插入同步以保持正确性，但它仅在不同流之间的任务间依赖关系需要时才插入同步，从而允许在独立资源上运行的没有数据依赖关系的任务同时进行。 这也最大限度地减少了调度和 CUDA 事件创建的 CPU 开销。Although Piper must insert synchronization to preserve correctness, it inserts synchronization only when required by inter-task dependencies across different streams, allowing tasks without data dependencies running on independent resources to proceed concurrently. This also minimizes CPU overhead from scheduling and CUDA event creation.

沟通管理Communication management

由于设备间和设备内依赖关系之间的复杂交互，避免不必要的同步产生的气泡可能具有挑战性。 GPU 集体通信通常需要一个“通信器”，它代表给定集体组中一个级别的上下文。 同一组内所有Rank的通信器上的通信操作必须以相同的顺序执行，否则可能会因为GPU集体通信同步而导致设备挂起Avoiding bubbles from unnecessary synchronization can be challenging due to complex interactions between inter- and intra-device dependencies. GPU collective communication typically requires a “communicator”, which represents one rank’s context in a given collective group. Communication operations must be executed in the same order on all ranks’ communicators in the same group, or else the device may hang because GPU collective communications are synchronous(NVIDIA, [n. d.]).

当与共享 CUDA 流创建的设备内依赖性相结合时，设备间通信顺序可能会产生气泡。 例如，在 PP 中，大多数工作人员不断地向另一个工作人员发送和接收微批次。 因此，如果我们使用一个集体组进行 PP 调度，那么发送和接收操作需要在所有工作人员的通信器上进行全局排序。 然而，在编译时选择最佳串行顺序很困难，因为它需要准确预测每个微批次阶段的执行时间以及通信时间。 例如，在发送和接收之间进行选择时，工作人员需要选择是先发送（这将解锁下游工作人员的计算），还是先接收（这将解锁在上游工作人员发送之后排队的通信操作）。The inter-device communication order can create bubbles when combined with the intra-device dependencies created by shared CUDA streams. For example, in PP, most workers are continually sending and receiving microbatches to and from another worker. Thus, if we use one collective group for a PP schedule, then the send and receive operations need to be globally ordered across all workers’ communicators. However, choosing an optimal serial order at compile time is hard because it would require exact predictions of the execution time per microbatch stage as well as communication time. For example, when choosing between sending and receiving, a worker needs to choose whether to send first, which would unblock the downstream worker’s compute, or receive first, which would unblock communication operations queued after the upstream worker’s send.

为了解决这个问题，Piper 为点对点通信分配单独的流，一个用于发送，一个用于接收。 为了避免死锁，Piper 还为每个流分配一个通信器。 使用单独的通信器可以减轻调度负担。系统不需要要求每对worker的所有P2P操作都按照一致的顺序排序，而只需要保证每个方向的P2P操作对于每对worker的排序都是一致的。 这不需要知道实际的运行时间，并且已经通过常见的并程调度自然地满足了。 特别是，对 PP 调度的要求是下游工作程序按照上游工作程序生成数据的顺序处理数据，例如，如果排名 0 在 2 之前执行微批次 1，则排名 1 也会在 2 之前执行微批次 1。 Piper 目前会拒绝不满足此要求的计划。To resolve this problem, Piper allocates separate streams for point-to-point communications, one for sending and one for receiving. To avoid deadlock, Piper also allocates one communicator per stream. Using separate communicators reduces scheduling burden. Instead of requiring that all P2P operations are ordered consistently for every pair of workers, the system only needs to guarantee that P2P operations in each direction are ordered consistently for every pair of workers. This does not require knowing actual run times and is already naturally satisfied by common PP schedules. In particular, the requirement on the PP schedule is that downstream workers process data in the same order that it is produced by upstream workers, e.g., if rank 0 executes microbatch 1 before 2, then rank 1 also executes microbatch 1 before 2. Piper currently rejects schedules that do not meet this requirement.

8. 结论Conclusion

我们推出 Piper，这是一个 PyTorch 的分布式训练系统，它使用统一的 IR（全局训练 DAG）将分布式执行策略规范与模型和运行时解耦。 通过明确计算和通信操作的放置、粒度和顺序，Piper 允许用户表达各种将 PP、DP 和 EP 与任意 ZeRO 内存优化相结合的分布式训练策略。 Piper 提供了一个有用的接口，用于对专家设计的时间表和未来在丰富的组合策略空间中进行自动搜索进行编码。We present Piper, a distributed training system for PyTorch that decouples the distributed execution strategy specification from the model and runtime using a unified IR: the global training DAG. By making the placement, granularity, and ordering of computation and communication operations explicit, Piper allows users to express a wide range of distributed training strategies combining PP, DP, and EP with arbitrary ZeRO memory optimizations. Piper provides a useful interface for encoding both expert-designed schedules and future automated search over a rich space of composed strategies.
