摘要。Abstract.基于 LLM 的多代理系统越来越多地部署用于支付工作流程，但主流指标——任务成功率 (TSR) 和代理移交 F1 分数 (HF1)——仅捕获最终结果或无序路由决策。我们介绍LLM-based multi-agent systems are increasingly deployed for payment workflows, yet prevailing metrics—Task Success Rate (TSR) and Agent Handoff F1-Score (HF1)—capture only final outcomes or unordered routing decisions. We introduce the代理成功率Agentic Success Rate（ASR），一种轨迹保真度指标，用于比较观察到的和预期的代理执行序列(ASR), a trajectory-fidelity metric that compares observed and expected agent execution sequences at the过渡transition（二元组）级别，将性能分解为 Transition Recall（完整性）和 Transition Precision（效率）。将 ASR 应用于 18 个 LLM 和 90,000 个任务实例（每个模型场景对 5 次重复）的分层多代理支付系统 (HMASP) 中，ASR 显示 18 个模型中有 10 个模型在付款结帐期间系统地跳过确认检查点（对于 TSR 和 HF1 来说都是不可见的），而 8 个模型则完美地执行了检查点。值得注意的是，(bigram) level, decomposing performance into Transition Recall (completeness) and Transition Precision (efficiency). Applied to the Hierarchical Multi-Agent System for Payments (HMASP) across 18 LLMs and 90,000 task instances (5 repeats per model–scenario pair), ASR reveals that 10 of 18 models systematically skip a confirmation checkpoint during payment checkout—invisible to both TSR and HF1—while 8 models enforce the checkpoint perfectly. Notably,GPT-4.1尽管实现了完美的 TSR 和 HF1，但仍展现出隐藏的工作流程快捷方式，同时exhibits hidden workflow shortcuts despite achieving perfect TSR and HF1, whileGPT-5.2实现完美的 ASR。由 ASR 诊断引导的即时改进和确定性路由保护可显着提高 TSR，对于之前陷入困境的模型而言，收益高达 +93.8 pp，这表明轨迹级评估在监管领域至关重要。achieves perfect ASR. Prompt refinements and deterministic routing guards guided by ASR diagnostics yield substantial TSR improvements, with gains up to +93.8 pp for previously struggling models, demonstrating that trajectory-level evaluation is essential in regulated domains.

1 简介1 Introduction

基于法学硕士的自主代理人的出现推动了快速采用The emergence of autonomous LLM-based agents has fueled rapid adoption of代理付款agentic payments—使用LLM代理来自动化端到端支付工作流程[16]。主要支付网络都推出了代理功能：万事达卡的代理支付[12]和Visa的智能商务[17]，预计到2030年将达到1.7万亿美元[16]。在研究中，HMASP [5] 等框架已经证明了分层多代理系统支付的可行性。—using LLM agents to automate end-to-end payment workflows [16]. Major payment networks have launched agentic capabilities: Mastercard’s Agent Pay [12] and Visa’s Intelligent Commerce [17], with projections reaching $1.7 trillion by 2030 [16]. In research, frameworks like HMASP [5] have demonstrated the feasibility of hierarchical multi-agent systems for payments.

However, TSR captures only final outcomes, and HF1 evaluates routing in a bag-of-edges fashion, ignoring temporal order. Neither captures工作流程保真度workflow fidelity——代理遵守预期的步骤顺序。在支付系统中，可审计性至关重要 [14]，结果的顺序路径与结果本身同样重要 [4, 3]。—the agent’s adherence to the expected sequence of steps. In payment systems, auditability is critical [14], and the sequential path to the result is as important as the outcome itself [4, 3].

Our contributions are: (1) ASR as a multi-agent payment metric; (2) evaluation of 18 LLMs on 90,000 instances; (3) discovery of workflow shortcuts invisible to TSR/HF1; and (4) demonstration that ASR-guided engineering yields dramatic TSR improvements for previously struggling models.

低过渡精度（TP）表示冗余（“对数移动”）。由于 ASR 在转换上运行，而不是在单个代理访问或无序边缘集上运行，因此它直接测量每个代理是否将控制权移交给正确的下一个代理（这正是合规性审计所需的属性），并推广到任意代理架构。low Transition Precision (TP) signals redundant ones (“log moves”). 模型。Models.我们评估了 18 个模型，涵盖开放式法学硕士和专有法学硕士。根据最初的 HMASP 研究 [5]，我们包括 Qwen3（8B、14B、32B）、Qwen2.5（7B、14B、32B）、Mistral-Small-3.2（24B）、Magistral（24B）、Llama 3.1（8B、70B）和 GPT-4.1。此外，我们还评估了原始研究中未包含的七个模型：GPT-5.2、GPT-OSS（20B、120B）和 Gemma4（E2B、E4B、26B、31B）。专有模型（GPT-4.1、GPT-5.2）可通过官方 OpenAI API 访问，而所有开放权重模型均使用 Ollama v0.20.0 在 Apple M5 Max 上本地运行，具有 128 GB 统一内存和 4 位量化（GPT-OSS 模型为 MXFP4；所有其他模型为 Q4_K_M）。We evaluated 18 models spanning both open-weight and proprietary LLMs. From the original HMASP study [5], we include Qwen3 (8B, 14B, 32B), Qwen2.5 (7B, 14B, 32B), Mistral-Small-3.2 (24B), Magistral (24B), Llama 3.1 (8B, 70B), and GPT-4.1. 我们引入 ASR 作为基于 LLM 的多代理支付系统的轨迹级评估指标。在 18 个法学硕士和 90,000 个实例中，ASR 暴露了系统性程序偏差：尽管 TSR 和 HF1 近乎完美，但 18 个模型中有 10 个在付款结帐期间跳过了 CPA 介导的确认检查点（T3 ASR 差距为 0.01-5.45%），而 8 个模型完美地执行了检查点。值得注意的是，We introduced ASR as a trajectory-level evaluation metric for LLM-based multiagent payment systems. Across 18 LLMs and 90,000 instances, ASR exposes a systematic procedural deviation: 10 of 18 models skip a CPA-mediated confirmation checkpoint during payment checkout (T3 ASR gaps of 0.01–5.45%) despite near-perfect TSR and HF1, while 8 models enforce the checkpoint perfectly. Notably,GPT-4.1尽管实现了完美的 TSR 和 HF1，但仍展现出隐藏的工作流程快捷方式，同时exhibits hidden workflow shortcuts despite achieving perfect TSR and HF1, whileGPT-5.2实现完美的 ASR。 ASR 引导的系统工程为之前陷入困境的模型带来了巨大的改进（高达 +93.8 pp）。随着支付网络大规模部署代理功能 [12, 17]，ASR 提供​​了确保程序保真度所需的可见性，这在合规性、可审计性和责任归属受到威胁时至关重要。未来的工作包括验证生产系统中的 ASR、安全关键型转换的加权变体、T3 检查点的架构实施以及跨架构评估 [17]。achieves perfect ASR. ASR-guided system engineering yields dramatic improvements for previously struggling models (up to +93.8 pp). As payment networks deploy agentic capabilities at scale [12, 17], ASR provides the visibility needed to ensure procedural fidelity—essential where compliance, auditability, and liability attribution are at stake. Future work includes validating ASR in production systems, weighted variants for security-critical transitions, architectural enforcement of the T3 checkpoint, and cross-architecture evaluations [17].
