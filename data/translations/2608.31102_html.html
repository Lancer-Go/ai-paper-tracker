摘要Abstract

工业后培训是一种棕地制度。团队继承已部署的检查点，并且必须在固定的计算和混合预算下进行有针对性的改进，而不会影响其余部分。维护的神器越来越多Industrial post-training is a brownfield regime. Teams inherit a deployed checkpoint and must land targeted improvements under fixed compute and mixture budgets without regressing the rest. The maintained artifact is increasingly数据件dataware：行为由精心设计的训练后混合物控制，通过有界混合物补丁而不是全新的再训练进行更新。从工业代码生成改进工作中，我们从维护者的角度来解释为什么这项工作在实践中很难，并提炼出三个反复出现的挑战，: behavior governed by a curated post-training mixture, updated via bounded mixture patches rather than clean-slate retraining. 2 数据编程的挑战The Challenges of Data Programming

在棕地制度中，培训后感觉不太像“构建模型”，而更像是在发布列车上维护关键任务代码库。社区已经拥有强大的合成、过滤和混合分配工具In the brownfield regime, post-training feels less like “building a model” and more like maintaining a mission-critical codebase on a release train. The community already has strong tools for synthesis, filtering, and mixture allocation(Wang et al., 2023; Xie et al., 2023; Fan et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025b)。维护者的痛苦在于这些部分以难以预测的方式交互，并且端到端验证成本高昂。我们依次采用 C1-C3，展示每种方法在实践中的表现，以及为什么简单的修复在生产中的约束下会失败。. The maintainer’s pain is that these pieces interact in ways that are hard to anticipate and expensive to validate end-to-end. We take C1–C3 in turn, showing how each manifests in practice and why naive fixes fail under the constraints that bind in production.

6 结论Conclusion

固定预算下的培训后是棕地软件维护：通过预算内的回归安全补丁改进部署的模型，而不是从头开始重建。我们采用的技术都不是新的，这就是要点：开放的问题是研究与实践之间的差距，以及在实时系统上安全地采用已发布方法的限制。我们提供此案例研究作为缩小这一差距的证据，而不是处方：Post-training under fixed budgets is brownfield software maintenance: a deployed model improved by budgeted, regression-safe patches, not clean-slate rebuilds. None of the techniques we adopted is new, and that is the point: the open problem is the gap between research and practice, the constraints of adopting published methods safely on a live system.
