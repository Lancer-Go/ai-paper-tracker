摘要Abstract

无线基础模型已成为为每个无线任务构建单独模型的有前途的替代方案。然而，现有方法依赖于屏蔽输入重建，这可能会使表示偏向低级信号细节。在本文中，我们提出了 LatentWave，这是一种使用联合嵌入预测架构 (JEPA) 在不同无线频谱图和信道状态信息 (CSI) 上进行预训练的无线基础模型。通过预测潜在空间中的屏蔽区域，LatentWave 学习到的表示形式在不同的下游任务中更容易转移。 所提出的架构在预训练期间采用每通道补丁嵌入和随机通道采样，使其能够处理可变天线数量并提高跨异构无线配置的可用性。 我们在四个下游任务上评估 LatentWave：RF 信号分类、5G NR 定位、波束预测和 LoS/NLoS 分类，并与在相同数据上预训练的掩模建模基线 (WavesFM) 进行比较。此外，我们表明掩蔽几何引入了与任务相关的归纳偏差：频率掩蔽强烈支持与通道相关的任务，例如定位和波束预测，而区域掩蔽更好地保留了信号分类的可辨别性。Wireless foundation models have emerged as a promising alternative to building separate models for each wireless task. However, existing approaches rely on masked input reconstruction, which can bias representations toward low-level signal details. In this paper, we propose LatentWave, a wireless foundation model pretrained using a Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) on diverse wireless spectrograms and channel state information (CSI). By predicting masked regions in latent space, LatentWave learns representations that are more transferable out of the box across diverse downstream tasks. The proposed architecture employs per-channel patch embeddings with stochastic channel sampling during pretraining, allowing it to process variable antenna counts and improving usability across heterogeneous wireless configurations. We evaluate LatentWave on four downstream tasks: RF signal classification, 5G NR positioning, beam prediction, and LoS/NLoS classification, comparing against a masked-modeling baseline (WavesFM) pretrained on the same data. Additionally, we show that the masking geometry introduces a task-dependent inductive bias: frequency masking strongly favors channel-related tasks such as positioning and beam prediction, while region masking better preserves discriminability for signal classification.

I 简介Introduction

传统的无线人工智能系统需要为每个任务和环境构建单独的模型，从而创建越来越多的专用管道集合Conventional wireless AI systems require building a separate model for each task and environment, creating a growing collection of specialized pipelines[3]开发、部署和维护成本高昂。 基础模型和自我监督学习that are expensive to develop, deploy, and maintain. Foundation models and self-supervised learning[15]提供了一种引人注目的替代方案：通过从不同的、未标记的数据中学习通用模式，单个预训练模型可以快速适应新的下游任务，用一个通用且适应性强的架构取代许多专用系统。 在网络软件化的背景下，这种架构可以被视为可重用的软件智能层，它将异构无线测量映射到与任务无关的表示。无线基础模型不是为每个无线服务部署单独的 AI 模型，而是提供一个共享底层，可以支持多种下游功能，例如传感、定位和波束管理。offer a compelling alternative: by learning general patterns from diverse, unlabeled data, a single pretrained model can be rapidly adapted to new downstream tasks, replacing many specialized systems with one general and adaptable architecture. In the context of network softwarization, such an architecture can be viewed as a reusable software intelligence layer that maps heterogeneous wireless measurements into task-agnostic representations. Rather than deploying separate AI models for each wireless service, the Wireless Foundation Model provides a shared substrate that can support multiple downstream functions such as sensing, localization, and beam management.

最近在无线领域朝着这种范式取得了进展，包括Recent advances have been made toward this paradigm in the wireless domain, including the works in[2, 14, 8, 1, 10]。无线基础模型的主要预训练策略是通过像素空间重建进行掩蔽建模。然而，这种方法有一个重要的限制——通过要求模型重建原始输入值，模型被迫将表示能力专用于低级频谱细节、噪声纹理和细粒度的幅度变化，这些对下游任务来说可能几乎没有区分价值。 结果表明，通过像素空间掩蔽和重建进行预训练的无线基础模型需要对最后几层进行微调或参数有效的适应，以在与预训练分布有很大不同的任务上实现有竞争力的性能。 这表明仅冻结表示并不能完全捕获不同无线任务所需的高级语义结构。. The dominant pretraining strategy for Wireless Foundation Models has been masked modeling with pixel-space reconstruction. However, this approach has an important limitation - by requiring the model to reconstruct raw input values, the model is forced to dedicate representational capacity to low-level spectral details, noise textures, and fine-grained amplitude variations that may carry little discriminative value for downstream tasks. Results indicate that Wireless Foundation Models pretrained with pixel-space masking and reconstruction require either fine-tuning of several last layers or parameter-efficient adaptation to achieve competitive performance on tasks that differ substantially from the pretraining distributions. This suggests that the frozen representations alone do not fully capture the high-level semantic structure needed for diverse wireless tasks.

联合嵌入预测架构框架The Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture framework[5]提供了一种替代方案 - 联合嵌入预测架构不是重建丢失的像素，而是训练一个模型来预测offers an alternative - instead of reconstructing missing pixels, Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture trains a model to predict the representations of遮蔽区域masked regions在一个习得的潜在空间中。由于预测目标本身就是一个学习的抽象，因此鼓励模型捕获更高级别的语义特征，同时丢弃不相关的低级别变化。然而，对于利用 CSI 和频谱图的无线模式和任务，尚未探索这种方法的有效性。in a learned latent space. Because the prediction target is itself a learned abstraction, the model is encouraged to capture higher-level semantic features while discarding irrelevant low-level variation. However, the effectiveness of such an approach has not been explored for wireless modalities and tasks that leverage CSI and spectrograms.

Ⅲ-AIII-A 拟议的预训练框架和架构Proposed Pretraining Framework and Architecture

我们的基础模型使用联合嵌入预测架构框架在多样化、未标记的无线信号频谱图和 CSI 数据集上进行预训练Our foundation model is pretrained on a diverse, unlabeled dataset of wireless signal spectrograms and CSI using the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture framework[5]。核心原理是通过在学习的潜在空间中进行预测来学习表示，而不是重建原始输入数据，如图 1 所示。. The core principle is to learn representations by predicting in a learned latent space rather than reconstructing raw input data, as shown in Fig.1。联合嵌入预测架构是一个自我监督框架，通过三个交互组件对屏蔽输入进行操作：. Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture is a self-supervised framework that operates on masked inputs through three interacting components: a上下文编码器context encoder, 一个, a目标编码器target encoder，和一个, and a预测器predictor。给定一个输入，其内容的子集被屏蔽。上下文编码器仅处理可见（未屏蔽）部分并将它们映射到潜在表示。共享相同架构的目标编码器处理完整的输入并生成潜在目标。然后，预测器仅使用上下文编码器的输出来预测目标编码器对屏蔽区域的表示。预测发生在潜在空间而不是原始输入空间。此过程详细说明于. Given an input, a subset of its content is masked. The context encoder processes only the visible (unmasked) portions and maps them to latent representations. The target encoder, which shares the same architecture, processes the complete input and produces latent targets. The predictor then forecasts the target encoder’s representations of the masked regions using only the context encoder’s output. Predictions occur in the latent space rather than in the raw input space. This process is illustrated in detail in[5].

V 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 LatentWave，这是一种基于 JEPA 的无线基础模型，通过预测潜在空间中的屏蔽区域进行学习。该架构通过每通道补丁嵌入和随机通道采样将编码器与固定天线数量解耦，使单个预训练模型能够处理具有不同通道数量的频谱图和 CSI 输入。我们还表明，掩蔽几何引入了与任务相关的归纳偏差：频率掩蔽有利于与通道相关的任务，例如定位和波束预测，而区域掩蔽更好地保留了信号分类的可辨别性。这些发现将掩蔽策略设计视为构建更多可转移无线基础模型的有前途的方向。This paper presented LatentWave, a JEPA-based wireless foundation model that learns by predicting masked regions in latent space. The architecture decouples the encoder from a fixed antenna count through per-channel patch embedding and stochastic channel sampling, enabling a single pretrained model to handle both spectrogram and CSI inputs with varying numbers of channels. We also showed that the masking geometry introduces a task-dependent inductive bias: frequency masking favors channel-related tasks such as positioning and beam prediction, while region masking better preserves discriminability for signal classification. These findings position masking strategy design as a promising direction for building more transferable wireless foundation models.
