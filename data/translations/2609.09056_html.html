摘要Abstract

现代科学和工程越来越依赖于时变数据，但用于模拟时间现象的数学工具通常是在不同学科内开发的，从而模糊了共同原理并限制了思想跨领域的转移。本章介绍了叙事理论，这是一个针对任何数学类型的时变对象的抽象框架，支持理论研究和应用。为了说明这一观点，本章提出了三个小插图，每个小插图说明了不同的研究方向。第一个解决了普遍关注的问题：Modern science and engineering increasingly rely on time-varying data, yet the mathematical tools used to model temporal phenomena are often developed within separate disciplines, obscuring common principles and limiting the transfer of ideas across fields. This chapter presents the theory of narratives, an abstract framework for time-varying objects of any mathematical kind that supports both theoretical investigations and applications. To illustrate this perspective, the chapter develops three vignettes, each illustrating a different research direction. The first addresses a general concern:在时态数据的不同表示之间切换时会发生哪些信息丢失？What information loss can occur when switching between different representations of temporal data?第二个涉及结构和算法方法：The second concerns structural and algorithmic approaches:我们如何系统地将时变数据分解为简单的片段并获得描述其结构复杂性的不变量？How can we systematically decompose time-varying data into simple pieces and obtain invariants describing its structural complexity?第三个是控制理论的应用：The third is an application to control theory:我们如何对具有交换通信拓扑的多智能体系统进行建模？How can we model multi-agent systems with switching communication topologies?比任何单独的小插图更重要的是，此邀请的中心信息是，合适的抽象视角可以组织和指导跨不同数学和科学领域的研究。More important than any individual vignette, the central message of this invitation is that a suitable abstract perspective can organize and guide research across remarkably diverse mathematical and scientific domains.

1 简介1 Introduction

现代科学和工程越来越受到时变数据分析的驱动。从社会动力学 [1]、环境科学 [2] 和公共卫生 [3, 4] 到多智能体系统的分布式控制 [5, 6]、语言进化 [7] 和科学计算史 [8, 9]，观察很少描述静态对象；相反，它们捕捉了现象随时间的演变[10]。尽管此类数据无处不在，但用于分析它们的数学工具仍然很大程度上分散[11]，不同的社区开发了时态数据挖掘、时态数据库、时态网络和相关领域的专门理论[11-14]。许多现有方法将时间信息表示为测量序列或演化系统的快照，通常没有连贯的数学结构来描述这些观察结果如何与时间相关。因此，整合时态数据的不同表示或系统地推理它们的转换仍然是一个重大挑战。 [15]中引入的叙述理论通过将时变数据表示为时间间隔类别上的滑轮和副滑轮来解决这个问题。持续性叙述记录了在整个时间间隔内保持有效的信息，而累积性叙述则记录了在该时间间隔内累积的信息。由于叙述是分类对象，因此该框架不仅描述了时变数据，还描述了它们之间的映射，并且它适用于在集合、图、拓扑空间、元胞滑轮和许多其他结构化对象类别中有价值的数据。本章旨在邀请人们了解叙事理论。通过对最近发展的调查，我们希望阐明叙述如何提供一个共同的框架，在这个框架中，来自许多数学领域的思想可以通过抽象理论和具体应用为时变数据的研究做出贡献。Modern science and engineering are increasingly driven by the analysis of time-varying data. From social dynamics [1], environmental science [2], and public health [3, 4] to distributed control of multi-agent systems [5, 6], language evolution [7], and the computational history of science [8, 9], observations rarely describe static objects; rather, they capture the evolution of phenomena through time [10]. Despite the ubiquity of such data, the mathematical tools used to analyze them remain largely fragmented [11], with different communities developing specialized theories for temporal data mining, temporal databases, temporal networks, and related domains [11–14]. Many existing approaches represent temporal information as sequences of measurements or snapshots of evolving systems, often without a coherent mathematical structure describing how these observations relate across time. As a result, integrating different representations of temporal data, or reasoning systematically about their transformations, remains a significant challenge. The theory of narratives introduced in [15] addresses this problem by representing time-varying data as sheaves and cosheaves over categories of time intervals. A persistent narrative records information that remains valid throughout an interval, while a cumulative narrative records information that accumulates over it. Because narratives are categorical objects, the framework describes not only timevarying data but also maps between them, and it applies to data valued in categories of sets, graphs, topological spaces, cellular sheaves, and many other structured objects. This chapter is intended as an invitation to the theory of narratives. Through a survey of recent developments, we hope to illustrate how narratives provide a common framework in which ideas from many areas of mathematics can contribute to the study of time-varying data, through both abstract theory and concrete applications.

叙事理论旨在解决当人们寻求一种用于时变数据的通用数学语言时出现的五个要求：The theory of narratives was designed to address five requirements that arise when one seeks a general mathematical language for time-varying data:

  • (D1) ( 时态数据的类别Categories of Temporal Data）任何时态数据理论不仅应该定义时变数据，还应该定义其适当的态射。) Any theory of temporal data should define not only time-varying data, but also appropriate morphisms thereof.

  • (D2) ( 累积和持续的观点Cumulative and Persistent Perspectives）与单纯的序列相反，时间数据应该明确记录是否要累积地查看或持续地查看。而且，这两种观点之间应该有转换的方法。) In contrast to being a mere sequence, temporal data should explicitly record whether it is to be viewed cumulatively or persistently. Furthermore, there should be methods of conversion between these two viewpoints.

  • (D3) ( 系统化“时间化”Systematic “Temporalization”）任何时态数据理论都应该配备系统方法来获取与静态数据相关的概念的时态类似物。) Any theory of temporal data should come equipped with systematic ways of obtaining temporal analogues of notions relating to static data.

  • (D4) ( 对象不可知论Object Agnosticism）时态数据理论应该与对象无关，并且适用于源自给定基础动态的任何类型的数据。) Theories of temporal data should be object agnostic and applicable to any kinds of data originating from given underlying dynamics.

  • (D5) ( 取样Sampling）由于时态数据源自底层动力系统，因此任何时态数据理论都应该与动力系统理论具有互操作性。) Since temporal data arises from an underlying dynamical system, any theory of temporal data should be interoperable with theories of dynamical systems.

本章收集了叙事理论的三个最新发展。每一个都以一个小插图的形式呈现，围绕一个不同的问题：（1）当一个人在持久性和累积性观点之间变化时，会保留多少信息，（2）如何测量时变数据的结构复杂性，以及（3）如何使用叙述来建模交互结构随时间变化的多智能体系统。这些例子共同说明了我们研究计划的更广泛目标：通过研究时变数据This chapter collects three recent developments in the theory of narratives. Each is presented as a vignette centered on a different question: (1) how much information is preserved when one changes between persistent and cumulative viewpoints, (2) how the structural complexity of time-varying data can be measured, and (3) how narratives can be used to model multi-agent systems whose interaction structure changes through time. Together, these examples illustrate the broader goals of our research program: by studying time-varying data

第一个小插曲涉及持久性叙事和累积性叙事之间的关联。由于这种附加通常不是等价的，因此将叙述从一种观点转换到另一种观点并返回可能会丢失信息。我们引入了在共同扭曲的箭头类别中有价值的叙述，该类别编码持久组件、累积组件以及它们之间的比较。然后，我们使用这个类别来分解持久性-积累附加因素，并根据叙述是否可以在两个可能的往返之后恢复来对叙述进行分类。The first vignette concerns the adjunction between persistent and cumulative narratives. Since this adjunction is not, in general, an equivalence, converting a narrative from one viewpoint to the other and back may lose information. We introduce narratives valued in a cotwisted arrow category, which encode a persistent component, a cumulative component, and a comparison between them. We then use this category to factorize the persistence– accumulation adjunction and to classify narratives according to whether they can be recovered after either of the two possible round trips.

第二个小插图涉及时变数据的分解。复杂的物体通常通过将其表达为较小部分的组合并记录这些部分如何重叠来研究。这种分解也产生了结构复杂性的度量。在时间设置中，片段本身必须随时间变化：独立分解每个快照不会记录片段如何持续、合并、分裂或消失。在最近的工作[16]的基础上，我们解释了如何将静态对象的结构化分解理论提升为持久的叙述。由此产生的框架产生分解，其部分是随时间变化的对象，并产生结构不变量（例如树宽度）的时间类似物。The second vignette concerns decompositions of time-varying data. Complex objects are often studied by expressing them as composites of smaller pieces and recording how these pieces overlap. Such decompositions also give rise to measures of structural complexity. In the temporal setting, the pieces must themselves vary through time: decomposing each snapshot independently does not record how the pieces persist, merge, split, or disappear. Building on recent work [16], we explain how theories of structured decompositions for static objects can be lifted to persistent narratives. The resulting framework yields decompositions whose pieces are time-varying objects and yields temporal analogues of structural invariants such as tree-width.

第三个小插曲将叙述应用于通信拓扑随时间变化的多智能体系统。通过将细胞滑轮作为值的类别，叙述可以记录不断变化的交互图以及其顶点和边所承载的局部状态空间、感知图和兼容性约束。交换系统的最终模型包含的信息比一系列通信图要多得多。它还提供了一种设置，可以使用相同的分类语言来研究底层动力系统与从中采样的时态数据之间的关系。The third vignette applies narratives to multi-agent systems whose communication topology changes through time. By taking cellular sheaves as the category of values, a narrative can record both the changing interaction graph and the local state spaces, sensing maps, and compatibility constraints carried by its vertices and edges. The resulting model of a switching system contains substantially more information than a sequence of communication graphs. It also provides a setting in which the relationship between an underlying dynamical system and the temporal data sampled from it can be studied using the same categorical language.

需要时间化。Need for temporalization.

结构化分解仅涉及静态图，即传统意义上的图，仅由一组顶点和一组连接它们的边组成。然而，当将现有的概念和结果应用于时变数据时，这种传统的图概念就不再足够了。许多感兴趣的系统随着时间的推移而发展，传统的图论技术忽略了这个时间维度。为了捕捉这种演变，引入了各种时间图的概念。它们的不同之处在于它们如何表示时间信息和底层图的演变。对于现有方法多样性的概述，我们参考[34-39]。Structured decompositions are concerned only with static graphs, meaning graphs in the traditional sense, consisting merely of a set of vertices together with a set of edges connecting them. When applying the existing concepts and results to time-varying data, however, this conventional notion of a graph is no longer sufficient. Many systems of interest evolve over time, and traditional graph-theoretical techniques ignore this temporal dimension. To capture such evolution, a variety of notions of temporal graphs have been introduced. These differ in how they represent temporal information and the evolution of the underlying graph. 4 结论和未来方向4 Conclusion and future directions

叙事框架的优势之一是它在很大程度上独立于随时间演变的物体的性质。一旦确定了适当的目标类别，相同的理论就会立即产生一个连贯的框架，用于对广泛应用中的时间现象进行建模。本章提出的三个研究方向从互补的角度说明了这种灵活性：第一个通过叙述类别及其诱导的附加因子分解研究持久-积累附加的分类结构，第二个探索结构化分解的时间类比，第三个实例化细胞滑轮类别中的框架，以对具有交换通信拓扑的多智能体系统进行建模。One of the strengths of the narrative framework is that it is largely independent of the nature of the objects evolving through time. Once an appropriate target category has been identified, the same theory immediately yields a coherent framework for modeling temporal phenomena across a wide range of applications. The three research directions presented in this chapter illustrate this flexibility from complementary perspectives: the first investigates the categorical structure of the persistence–accumulation adjunction through the category of narratives and its induced factorization of the adjunction, the second explores temporal analogues of structured decompositions, and the third instantiates the framework in the category of cellular sheaves to model multi-agent systems with switching communication topologies.

附件不动点的表征Towards a characterization of the fixed points of the adjunction

通过叙述范畴对持久性-积累附加物的分解引入了一种背景，在这种环境中，持久性和积累不再被视为单独的结构，而是对同一时间对象的两种互补描述。由此产生的刚性分类衡量了这些描述相互确定的程度。僵化的叙述与两个方向的附加所引起的规范完成相一致。左刚性叙事在持久性累积往返下保留了其持久性描述，右刚性叙事保留了其累积描述，而松散叙事则在两个方向上都丢失了信息。The factorization of the persistence–accumulation adjunction through the category of narratives introduces a setting in which persistence and accumulation are no longer viewed as separate constructions, but as two complementary descriptions of the same temporal object. The resulting rigidity classification measures the extent to which these descriptions determine one another. Rigid narratives coincide with the canonical completions induced by the adjunction in both directions. Left-rigid narratives preserve their persistent description under the persistence–accumulation round trip, right-rigid narratives preserve their cumulative description, while loose narratives lose information in both directions.

时间细胞滑轮展示了叙事框架如何自然地应用于具有切换通信拓扑的异构多智能体系统。选择蜂窝滑轮作为目标类别使框架不仅可以编码通信架构的演变，还可以编码与之相关的异构传感、通信和信息处理结构。这为研究时变通信网络上的分布式控制问题奠定了分类基础。Temporal cellular sheaves demonstrate how the narrative framework applies naturally to heterogeneous multi-agent systems with switching communication topologies. This establishes a categorical foundation for studying distributed control problems on time-varying communication networks.

自然的下一步是赋予时间细胞滑轮以在分配给它们的细胞的向量空间上进化的动力系统。这就提出了理解动态如何与拓扑变化以及叙事所编码的持久和累积结构相互作用的问题。特别令人感兴趣的是用于交换通信网络的层理论描述的稳定性理论的发展，导致分布式协调目标（例如共识、编队维护或目标跟踪）在通信拓扑发生变化时仍保持稳定的条件。该研究计划目前正在[47]中开发。A natural next step is to endow temporal cellular sheaves with dynamical systems evolving on the vector spaces assigned to their cells.
