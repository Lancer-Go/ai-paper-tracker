计算机视觉中的常识推理：基础、最新进展和未来方向Commonsense Reasoning in Computer Vision: Foundations, Recent Advancements, and Future Directions

计算机视觉中的常识推理包括整合视觉数据和上下文知识，这对于增强人工智能对日常场景的理解至关重要。这种理解不仅改进了机器学习模型，还增强了它们与人类和环境进行有意义交互的能力。基于 CNN 的传统视觉模型旨在识别特定图像中的对象，与此不同的是，结合常识知识使模型能够以更全面的方式解释场景，从而提高其推理对象和动作之间关系的空间能力。这种集成不仅增强了对象识别，还有助于更深入地理解上下文因素，最终在现实应用中实现更精确的预测和交互。本文对将常识知识整合到计算机视觉任务中的最新进展进行了全面调查。我们系统地回顾了基于知识图、场景图、神经符号模型和常识增强变压器的方法。我们还概述了当前与数据集偏差、知识不完整性和集成挑战相关的局限性。最后，我们重点介绍了跨模式推理、可扩展常识知识注入和神经符号混合架构方面的前瞻性研究轨迹，以开发真正的智能视觉系统。Commonsense reasoning in computer vision encompasses integrating visual data and contextual knowledge, crucial for enhancing AI’s understanding of everyday scenarios. This understanding not only improves machine learning models but also enhances their ability to interact meaningfully with humans and the environment. Unlike CNN-based conventional vision models, which are designed to identify objects within a specific image, incorporating commonsense knowledge enables models to interpret scenes in a more holistic manner, thereby improving their spatial ability to reason about relationships among objects and actions. This integration not only enhances object recognition but also facilitates a deeper understanding of the contextual factors, ultimately leading to more precise predictions and interactions in real-world applications. This paper presents a comprehensive survey of recent developments that integrate commonsense knowledge into computer vision tasks. We systematically review approaches based on knowledge graphs, scene graphs, neuro-symbolic models, and commonsenseaugmented transformers. We also outline current limitations related to dataset bias, knowledge incompleteness, and integration challenges. Finally, we highlight prospective research trajectories in cross-modal reasoning, scalable commonsense knowledge injection, and neuro-symbolic hybrid architectures to develop truly intelligent visual systems.

2.3 检索增强推理作为结构创新2.3 Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning as a Structural Innovation

虽然检索增强生成（RAG）在自然语言处理中已经很成熟（Lewis et al. 2020），但它在视觉语言常识推理中的应用构成了一种性质独特的架构范式。While retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is well-established in natural language processing (Lewis et al. 2020), its application to vision–language commonsense reasoning constitutes a qualitatively distinct architectural paradigm.

与纯文本 RAG 系统不同，基于视觉的检索面临几个独特的挑战：Unlike text-only RAG systems, vision-based retrieval faces several unique challenges:

  • 多模式接地Multimodal grounding：检索查询必须基于空间区域、时间片段或抽象视觉概念；: retrieval queries must be grounded in spatial regions, temporal segments, or abstract visual concepts;

  • 知识来源异质性Knowledge source heterogeneity：系统必须集成结构化知识图、非结构化网络语料库和参数化模型知识；: systems must integrate structured knowledge graphs, unstructured web corpora, and parametric model knowledge;

  • 推理时间适应性Inference-time adaptability：检索可以访问最新的常识而无需重新训练，解决了纯参数模型的主要限制。: retrieval enables access to up-to-date commonsense without retraining, addressing a major limitation of purely parametric models.

我们在第 6.1 节中的分析首次系统地将检索增强视觉推理作为一个独特的架构类进行处理，检查查询制定策略、检索源选择、融合机制以及多模态特定的故障模式（例如幻觉放大和上下文不匹配）。Our analysis in Section 6.1 provides the first systematic treatment of retrieval-augmented vision reasoning as a distinct architectural class, examining query formulation strategies, retrieval source selection, fusion mechanisms, and multimodal-specific failure modes such as hallucination amplification and context mismatch.

3.1 论文选择与分类3.1 Paper Selection and Categorization

在这项调查中，选择审查的文章是根据它们将常识推理整合到计算机视觉（CV）任务中的贡献来选择的。范围主要集中在 2018 年至 2025 年期间发表的作品，因为这些作品代表了该领域最新、最有影响力的研究。具体的纳入标准指导了选择，以确保相关性和影响力。In this survey, the articles selected for review were chosen based on their contributions to integrating commonsense reasoning into computer vision (CV) tasks. The scope was focused on works published between 2018 and 2025, as these represent the latest and most impactful research in the field. Specific inclusion criteria guided the selection to ensure relevance and impact.

首先，仅包含明确解决将常识知识纳入计算机视觉模型的论文。这些模型通常集成来自外部来源的知识，例如结构化知识图或将神经网络与逻辑推理相结合的神经符号系统。这种方法确保所选的研究与常识推理和视觉任务的交叉点直接相关。First, only papers that explicitly address the incorporation of commonsense knowledge into computer vision models were included. These models typically integrate knowledge from external sources, such as structured knowledge graphs or neuro-symbolic systems that combine neural networks with logical reasoning. This approach ensures that the selected studies are directly related to the intersection of commonsense reasoning and vision tasks.

Scene graphs are instrumental in image captioning, object detection, and visual reasoning tasks, where understanding the interactions between objects is key. For example, in a typical picture with a dog and a ball, a scene graph might represent the relationship as "dog → playing with → ball," helping the model to understand not just the presence of objects but their contextual interactions.

此外，神经符号模型开始在小样本学习中显示出潜力。通过将符号推理的泛化能力与神经网络的数据高效学习能力相结合，这些模型可以推断关系或通过有限的示例进行预测。Moreover, neuro-symbolic models are beginning to show potential in few-shot learning. By combining the generalization power of symbolic reasoning with the data-efficient learning capabilities of neural networks, these models can infer relationships or make predictions with limited examples.

尽管神经符号系统前景广阔，但其发展仍面临一些挑战。主要限制之一是难以有效地将神经网络的灵活性与符号系统的刚性结合起来。虽然神经网络具有高度适应性并且能够从数据中学习，但符号系统通常依赖于预定义的逻辑和规则，这可能并不总是符合现实场景所需的灵活性。当应用于复杂的动态环境时，两种范式之间的这种不一致可能会导致推理不一致或泛化能力受到限制。Despite the promise of neuro-symbolic systems, several challenges remain in their development. One of the key limitations is the difficulty in effectively combining the flexibility of neural networks with the rigidity of symbolic systems. While neural networks are highly adaptable and capable of learning from data, symbolic systems often rely on predefined logic and rules, which may not always align with the flexibility required for real-world scenarios. This dissonance between the two paradigms can lead to inconsistent reasoning or limitations in generalization when applied to complex, dynamic environments.

此外，神经符号模型的可扩展性仍然是一个重大挑战。尽管小规模实验已显示出希望，但扩展这些模型以处理现实世界的大规模数据（尤其是具有不同常识知识库的数据）仍然是一个持续的研究问题。在推理过程中，特别是在实时场景中，相关符号知识的查询和检索仍然是这些模型在实际应用中更广泛采用的瓶颈。Furthermore, the scalability of neuro-symbolic models remains a significant challenge. While small-scale experiments have shown promise, scaling these models to handle real-world, large-scale data — especially with diverse commonsense knowledge bases — is still an ongoing research problem. The querying and retrieval of relevant symbolic knowledge during inference, particularly in real-time scenarios, remains a bottleneck for the broader adoption of these models in practical applications.

此外，常识推理与符号系统的整合也受到知识库不完整的限制。虽然 ATOMIC 或 ConceptNet 等知识图提供了大量关系，但它们仍然存在差距，特别是在需要动态推理或复杂的多步骤推理的领域。例如，许多现实世界的任务需要理解微妙的上下文线索，而这些线索并不总是被现有的常识知识库捕获。Moreover, integrating commonsense reasoning with symbolic systems is also limited by the incompleteness of knowledge bases. While knowledge graphs such as ATOMIC or ConceptNet provide a large set of relations, they still have gaps, particularly in areas that require dynamic reasoning or complex, multi-step inferences. For instance, many real-world tasks require understanding subtle contextual clues that are not always captured by existing commonsense knowledge bases.

5.3 常识增强型视觉变压器5.3 Commonsense-Enhanced Vision Transformers

视觉 Transformers (ViT) 从传统的卷积神经网络 (CNN) 转向基于 Transformer 的架构，彻底改变了计算机视觉领域。 ViT 能够捕获图像中的远程依赖性，使其对于图像分类、对象检测和分割等任务非常有效。Vision Transformers (ViTs) have revolutionized the field of computer vision by moving away from traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to transformer-based architectures. ViTs are capable of capturing longrange dependencies in images, making them highly effective for tasks such as image classification, object detection, and segmentation.

5.3.1 常识知识在视觉转换器中的作用。5.3.1 The Role of Commonsense Knowledge in Vision Transformers.将常识知识纳入 ViT 可以采取多种形式，例如利用知识图、知识库或预训练的语言模型。主要目标是使模型不仅能够从数据中学习视觉表示，而且还能结合上下文知识，从而能够推理场景中对象的合理性和交互性。常识增强的 ViT 可用于预测更好的可供性、对象关系以及需要了解对象在现实世界中行为方式的预期操作和任务。Incorporating commonsense knowledge into ViTs can take several forms, such as leveraging knowledge graphs, knowledge bases, or pretrained language models. The primary objective is to enable the model to not only learn visual representations from the data but also incorporate contextual knowledge that allows for reasoning about the plausibility and interactions of objects within the scene. Commonsense-enhanced ViTs can be used to predict better affordances, object relationships, and intended actions, tasks that require an understanding of how objects behave in the real world.

该领域最近的工作包括使用结合视觉和语言表示的多模式转换器。 LXMERT（从 Transformers 中学习跨模态编码器表示）（Tan 和 Bansal 2019a）和 ViLT（用于语言理解的视觉 Transformers）（W. Kim 等人 2021a）是将基于语言的常识知识与视觉理解相结合的杰出模型。这些模型在多模态数据集上进行训练，可以推理视觉输入（图像）和文本描述之间的关系，这显着增强了它们的常识推理能力。Recent work in this area includes the use of multi-modal transformers that combine both visual and language representations. LXMERT (Learning Cross-Modality Encoder Representations from Transformers) (Tan and Bansal 2019a) and ViLT (Vision Transformers for Language Understanding) (W. Kim et al. 2021a) are prominent models that integrate language-based commonsense knowledge with visual understanding. These models are trained on multi-modal datasets and can reason about the relationships between visual inputs (images) and textual descriptions, which significantly enhances their commonsense reasoning capabilities.

常识增强型 ViT 的一项关键创新是集成预训练语言模型 (PLM)，例如 BERT（Devlin 等人，2019b）或 GPT（Brown 等人，2020），这些模型已被证明可以在训练过程中捕获丰富的常识知识。例如，ViLT 不仅将 Transformer 用于视觉任务，还用于图像字幕和视觉问答 (VQA) 等语言任务。通过使用大量语言数据对 Transformer 进行预训练，ViLT 学会将视觉表示与文本表示和常识知识关联起来，使模型能够推断出图像中更合理的关系和对象交互。A key innovation in commonsense-enhanced ViTs is the integration of pretrained language models (PLMs) like BERT (Devlin et al. 2019b) or GPT (Brown et al. 2020), which have been shown to capture rich commonsense knowledge during training. For instance, ViLT uses transformers not only for vision tasks but also for language tasks such as image captioning and Visual Question Answering (VQA). By pretraining the transformer on vast amounts of language data, ViLT learns to associate visual representations with textual representations and commonsense knowledge, enabling the model to infer more plausible relationships and object interactions in images.

另一种方法是使用查询结构化知识库的常识推理模块来增强 ViT 架构。例如，Zellers 等人最近的一项工作。 (2021) (Zellers et al. 2019) 引入了一种用于图像字幕任务的常识增强变压器，其中模型从 ATOMIC 等常识知识库中动态检索相关知识，并使用这些信息来提高为图像生成的字幕的连贯性和上下文。这种方法表明，在推理阶段将结构化知识注入 ViT 可以产生上下文更加准确且常识一致的输出。Another approach has been augmenting the ViT architecture with commonsense reasoning modules that query structured knowledge bases. For example, a recent work by Zellers et al. (2021) (Zellers et al. 2019) introduced a commonsense-augmented transformer for image captioning tasks, where the model dynamically retrieves relevant knowledge from a commonsense knowledge base such as ATOMIC and uses this information to improve the coherence and context of the captions generated for images. This approach demonstrated that injecting structured knowledge into ViTs during the inference phase can lead to more contextually accurate and commonsense-consistent outputs.

尽管将常识知识整合到 ViT 中已显示出巨大的前景，但仍然存在一些挑战。主要障碍之一是知识检索：模型必须能够在推理过程中有效地查询外部知识库，而不会引入显着的延迟或计算开销。常识知识的动态性质（根据上下文、对象关系和任务而变化）为每项任务选择最相关的专业知识带来了额外的挑战。While the integration of commonsense knowledge into ViTs has shown significant promise, several challenges remain. One of the primary obstacles is knowledge retrieval: models must be able to query external knowledge bases efficiently during inference, without introducing significant delays or computational overhead. The dynamic nature of commonsense knowledge — which varies depending on the context, object relationships, and task — presents additional challenges in selecting the most relevant expertise for each task.

此外，知识库中的数据稀疏仍然是一个关键问题。虽然大规模知识库涵盖了广泛的关系和事实，但它们仍然缺乏对许多领域的全面覆盖，特别是那些需要高阶推理或特定领域知识的领域。这种知识差距可能会导致模型做出不正确或有偏见的推论，特别是在不熟悉或边缘情况的情况下。此外，整合常识推理通常需要调整符号和视觉表示，由于每种模态中知识的表示方式存在差异，这项任务已被证明是困难的。Additionally, data sparsity in knowledge bases remains a critical issue. While large-scale knowledge bases cover a wide range of relationships and facts, they still lack comprehensive coverage in many domains, particularly those that require high-order reasoning or domain-specific knowledge. This knowledge gap can lead to models making incorrect or biased inferences, particularly in unfamiliar or edge-case scenarios. Furthermore, integrating commonsense reasoning often requires aligning symbolic and visual representations, a task that has proven difficult due to the differences in how knowledge is represented in each modality.

6.3.1 SENet（语义丰富网络）。6.3.1 SENet (Semantic Enrichment Network).SENet 通过合并来自知识图（例如 ConceptNet）的外部语义信息来增强视觉表示（Speer 等人，2017a）。它通过利用上下文知识消除视觉特征的歧义，实现了对象检测和分割任务的高精度。然而，模型对静态外部图的依赖可能会在推理过程中引入延迟，从而影响实时系统的可扩展性。尽管存在这些限制，SENet 在医学成像、自动驾驶汽车和辅助技术等领域表现出了强大的适用性，在这些领域，情境意识至关重要。SENet enhances visual representations by incorporating external semantic information from knowledge graphs such as ConceptNet (Speer et al. 2017a). It achieves high accuracy in object detection and segmentation tasks by disambiguating visual features with contextual knowledge. However, the model’s reliance on static external graphs can introduce latency during inference, affecting scalability in realtime systems. Despite these limitations, SENet demonstrates strong applicability in medical imaging, autonomous vehicles, and assistive technologies where contextual awareness is critical.

6.3.2 NSCL（神经符号概念学习器）。6.3.2 NSCL (Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learner).神经符号概念学习器 (NSCL) 将感知模块（深度卷积网络）与符号推理引擎集成在一起，从而能够对视觉场景进行组合和关系推理（Mao 等人，2019b）。 NSCL 擅长于需要结构化逻辑推理的任务，实现卓越的常识集成。然而，由于符号执行的计算开销，该模型在可扩展性方面遇到了困难，限制了其在时间敏感或大规模场景中的适用性。The Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learner (NSCL) integrates perceptual modules (deep convolutional networks) with symbolic reasoning engines, enabling compositional and relational reasoning over visual scenes (Mao et al. 2019b). NSCL excels in tasks requiring structured logical inference, achieving superior commonsense integration. Nevertheless, the model struggles with scalability due to the computational overhead of symbolic execution, limiting its applicability in time-sensitive or large-scale scenarios.

6.3.3 LXMERT。6.3.3 LXMERT.LXMERT（Tan 和 Bansal 2019b）利用跨模态转换器对视觉和文本输入进行联合编码，促进强大的多模态理解。尽管 LXMERT 最初并不是为显式常识集成而设计的，但其语言主干隐式编码从大型文本语料库中学习的常识先验。它在 VQA 和图像文本检索任务上实现了高精度，并且由于其模块化变压器架构而具有高度可扩展性，使其适合实际部署。LXMERT (Tan and Bansal 2019b) utilizes cross-modality transformers to jointly encode visual and textual inputs, facilitating strong multimodal understanding. Although LXMERT was not originally designed for explicit commonsense integration, its language backbone implicitly encodes commonsense priors learned from large text corpora. It achieves high accuracy on VQA and image-text retrieval tasks and is highly scalable owing to its modular transformer architecture, making it suitable for real-world deployment.

6.3.4 ViLT。6.3.4 ViLT.ViLT（W. Kim 等人，2021b）通过消除繁重的视觉特征提取阶段，直接将转换器应用于图像补丁和文本标记，进一步简化了多模态学习。通过利用 BERT（Devlin 等人，2019a）等预训练语言模型，ViLT 无需显式外部图即可捕获常识知识。它在多个基准测试中取得了最先进的结果。然而，训练和推理成本仍然很高，而且模型对密集数据的依赖导致边缘部署效率较低。ViLT (W. Kim et al. 2021b) further streamlines multimodal learning by removing heavy visual feature extraction stages, directly applying transformers to image patches and text tokens. By leveraging pretrained language models such as BERT (Devlin et al. 2019a), ViLT captures commonsense knowledge without explicit external graphs. It achieves state-of-the-art results on multiple benchmarks. However, training and inference costs remain high, and the model’s reliance on dense data makes it less efficient for edge deployment.

6.3.5 图 R-CNN。6.3.5 Graph R-CNN.Graph R-CNN（Yang et al. 2018b）利用场景图对图像中的对象-对象关系进行显式建模，从而增强关系推理能力。虽然这种方法通过空间和语义关系提供了强大的常识性建模，但生成和维护场景图会产生大量的计算成本。因此，Graph R-CNN 表现出有限的可扩展性和较慢的推理速度，降低了其在实时或高度动态环境中的适用性。Graph R-CNN (Yang et al. 2018b) leverages scene graphs to explicitly model object-object relationships in images, enhancing relational reasoning capabilities. While this method provides strong commonsense modeling through spatial and semantic relationships, generating and maintaining scene graphs incurs significant computational costs. Consequently, Graph R-CNN exhibits limited scalability and slower inference, reducing its applicability for real-time or highly dynamic environments.

8.1 超越可观察的推理8.1 Reasoning Beyond Observables

当前的许多模型擅长对视觉内容进行表面推理，但很难推断出不可观察的因素，例如意图、目标、情感或因果关系。例如，即使像 OmniVL (X. Wang et al. 2023) 这样的高级模型也主要依赖于可观察的模式，而不是潜在的因果结构。人类常识推理从根本上依赖于推断隐藏状态并根据部分观察预测未来结果。使模型能够进行超越可见的推理，特别是关于隐性人类活动，仍然是一个未解决的问题，需要因果建模和溯因推理方面的进步。Many current models excel at surface-level reasoning over visual content but struggle to infer unobservable factors such as intentions, goals, emotions, or causality. For instance, even advanced models like OmniVL (X. Wang et al. 2023) primarily rely on observable patterns rather than latent causal structures. Human commonsense reasoning fundamentally depends on inferring hidden states and predicting future outcomes based on partial observations. Enabling models to reason beyond the visible, particularly regarding implicit human activities, remains an unresolved problem that requires advances in causal modeling and abductive inference.

Designing lightweight architectures or retrieval-augmented modules that operate under strict computational budgets without sacrificing reasoning depth remains a critical open problem.

8.3 检索到的常识中的偏见和幻觉8.3 Biases and Hallucinations in Retrieved Commonsense

知识库和预训练模型不可避免地继承了训练语料库的偏差。更重要的是，检索增强视觉语言系统可以放大而不是减轻这些偏见。从基于网络的大型语料库中检索进一步引入了幻觉——看似合理但实际上不正确的信息，在推理过程中被视为权威信息（Ji 等人，2023 年；S. Lin 等人，2022 年）。Knowledge bases and pretrained models inevitably inherit biases from their training corpora. More critically, retrieval-augmented vision–language systems can amplify rather than mitigate these biases. Retrieval from large, web-based corpora further introduces hallucinations—plausible yet factually incorrect information that is treated as authoritative during inference (Ji et al. 2023; S. Lin et al. 2022).

Empirical studies report a 15–20% accuracy drop for images depicting female surgeons or male nurses compared to visualonly baselines (J. Zhao et al. 2017). In these cases, commonsense retrieval exacerbates bias rather than correcting it.

为什么检索会放大偏差：Why Retrieval Amplifies Bias:偏差放大源于多种系统机制：Bias amplification arises from several systemic mechanisms:

  • (1) 确认偏差。Confirmation Bias.模型优先生成强化现有先验的检索查询，呈现与学到的关联一致的证据（Nickerson 1998）。Models preferentially generate retrieval queries that reinforce existing priors, surfacing evidence consistent with learned associations (Nickerson 1998).

  • (2) 缺乏来源验证。Lack of Source Verification.检索到的段落被视为事实，允许单个不正确或过时的来源通过许多下游推论传播（Petroni 等人，2019）。Retrieved passages are treated as factual, allowing a single incorrect or outdated source to propagate through many downstream inferences (Petroni et al. 2019).

  • (3) 偏差复合。Bias Compounding.多跳推理链会增加检索步骤中的偏差，放大每个阶段的错误（Qi 等人，2021）。Multi-hop reasoning chains multiply biases across retrieval steps, magnifying errors at each stage (Qi et al. 2021).

当常识先验降低性能时：When Commonsense Priors Degrade Performance:当常识性先验与视觉证据相冲突时，可能会严重损害性能：Commonsense priors can actively harm performance when they conflict with visual evidence:

  • 医学影像。Medical Imaging.强大的解剖学先验（例如，“心脏位于中心偏左”）会导致右位心等罕见变异的错误分类，与基线模型相比，准确率降低高达 12%（Oakden-Rayner et al. 2020）。Strong anatomical priors (e.g., “the heart is left-of-center”) cause misclassification of rare variants such as dextrocardia, reducing accuracy by up to 12% compared to baseline models (Oakden-Rayner et al. 2020).

  • 自动驾驶。Autonomous Driving.检索到的将交通与制动联系起来的先验覆盖了矛盾的视觉线索，导致不必要的防御动作（Bojarski、Yeres 等人，2017 年）。Retrieved priors linking traffic to braking override contradictory visual cues, leading to unnecessary defensive maneuvers (Bojarski, Yeres, et al. 2017).

  • VQA 刻板印象。VQA Stereotypes.性别刻板印象主导了上下文推理，在启用检索时降低了反刻板印象图像的准确性（Hendricks 等人，2018 年；J.Zhao 等人，2017 年）。Gender stereotypes dominate contextual reasoning, lowering accuracy for counterstereotypical images when retrieval is enabled (Hendricks et al. 2018; J. Zhao et al. 2017).

8.4 跨模态统一推理8.4 Unified Reasoning Across Modalities

尽管视觉语言预训练取得了成功，但现有模型仍然难以跨多种模式（包括图像、视频、音频和文本）进行深度统一的常识推理。像 OmniVL 这样的模型通过对混合模式进行预训练来尝试实现这一点，但动态视觉场景和叙事文本之间完全连贯的推理仍然难以实现。构建能够执行时间感知、多模态常识推理的架构（例如理解视频场景并将其与看不见的文本常识相关联）是一项重大的开放挑战，需要新的融合技术和预训练范例。Despite the success of vision-language pretraining, existing models still struggle with deeply unified commonsense reasoning across multiple modalities, including images, videos, audio, and text. Models like OmniVL attempt this by pretraining on mixed modalities, yet fully coherent reasoning across dynamic visual scenes and narrative text remains elusive. Building architectures capable of performing temporally aware, multimodal commonsense reasoning—such as understanding a video scene and relating it to unseen textual commonsense—is a significant open challenge that demands new fusion techniques and pre-training paradigms.

8.5 可解释性和可解释性8.5 Interpretability and Explainability

随着模型集成更多的外部知识并执行复杂的推理步骤，它们的决策变得越来越不透明。深层常识推理的黑箱性质引起了医疗保健、教育和法律等需要透明度的领域的担忧。当前很少有模型提供可解释的推理链或可追踪的常识应用程序。开发具有内置可解释性的常识性视觉模型，用户可以检查检索到的知识、推理步骤和最终预测，这对于信任、问责制和更广泛的社会接受度至关重要。As models integrate more external knowledge and perform complex reasoning steps, their decision-making becomes increasingly opaque. The black-box nature of deep commonsense reasoning raises concerns in domains that require transparency, such as healthcare, education, and law. Few current models offer interpretable reasoning chains or traceable commonsense application. Developing commonsense-enabled vision models with built-in explainability, where users can inspect retrieved knowledge, reasoning steps, and final predictions, is critical for trust, accountability, and broader societal acceptance.

8.6 故障级联和错误传播8.6 Failure Cascades and Error Propagation

常识增强的视觉系统很容易受到Commonsense-enhanced vision systems are vulnerable to故障级联failure cascades，其中感知过程中引入的错误通过检索和推理阶段传播和放大（Amodei et al. 2016；H. Jiang et al. 2020）。在大多数当前的管道中，每个模块都假设上游输出的正确性，从而导致复合故障而不是纠正推理。, in which errors introduced during perception propagate and amplify through retrieval and reasoning stages (Amodei et al. 2016; H. Jiang et al. 2020). In most current pipelines, each module assumes the correctness of upstream outputs, leading to compounding failures rather than corrective reasoning.

级联示例：医疗诊断：Cascade Example: Medical Diagnosis:典型的级联发生在医疗决策支持系统中。由于对比度低，分割模型无法检测到小肺结节。然后，检索模块查询“小肺结节”并检索表明良性的人群水平常识（Ardila 等人，2019）。推理阶段放大了这一假设，并得出结论：该发现与临床无关。因此，系统建议不进行随访，错过早期癌症。根本原因是缺乏跨阶段的不确定性传播。A representative cascade occurs in medical decision support systems. A segmentation model fails to detect a small lung nodule due to low contrast. The retrieval module then queries for “small lung nodules” and retrieves population-level commonsense suggesting benignity (Ardila et al. 2019). The reasoning stage amplifies this assumption and concludes that the finding is not clinically concerning. As a result, the system recommends no follow-up, missing an early-stage cancer. The root cause is the absence of uncertainty propagation across stages.

级联示例：自动驾驶：Cascade Example: Autonomous Driving:在自动驾驶中，物体检测器可能会将塑料袋错误地分类为小动物。常识检索将道路上的动物与紧急制动行为联系起来。规划模块执行硬制动操作，增加追尾碰撞风险（Bojarski、Del Testa 等人，2016 年；Bojarski、Yeres 等人，2017 年）。尽管感知证据微弱或模糊，但不正确的常识被放大而不是被纠正。In autonomous driving, an object detector may misclassify a plastic bag as a small animal. Commonsense retrieval associates animals on the roadway with emergency braking behavior. The planning module executes a hard braking maneuver, increasing rear-end collision risk (Bojarski, Del Testa, et al. 2016; Bojarski, Yeres, et al. 2017). Incorrect commonsense is amplified rather than corrected, despite weak or ambiguous perceptual evidence.

为什么会发生级联故障：Why Failure Cascades Occur:由于当前系统缺乏关键保障措施，级联故障持续存在：Failure cascades persist because current systems lack key safeguards:

  • 无不确定性传播No uncertainty propagation：置信度估计不会在感知、检索和推理模块之间转移（Gal 和 Ghahramani 2016；Guo 等人 2017）。: Confidence estimates are not transferred between perception, retrieval, and reasoning modules (Gal and Ghahramani 2016; Guo et al. 2017).

  • 无冲突检测No conflict detection：系统无法识别检索到的常识和视觉证据之间的矛盾。: Systems cannot identify contradictions between retrieved commonsense and visual evidence.

  • 无回溯No backtracking：一旦选择了假设，模型就无法修改之前的决策。: Once a hypothesis is selected, models cannot revise earlier decisions.

Building on the strengths and challenges outlined in recent work, this section highlights key directions for future research.

10 结论10 Conclusion

常识推理代表了计算机视觉系统从模式识别到准确的上下文理解能力的关键前沿。在这项调查中，我们系统地分析了视觉常识整合的演变，从早期的场景图和神经符号模型到现代的检索增强和基础模型方法。通过详细的比较分析，我们强调了不同的范式如何应对可扩展性、推理深度和现实世界适用性的挑战。Commonsense reasoning represents a critical frontier in advancing the capabilities of computer vision systems beyond pattern recognition toward accurate contextual understanding. In this survey, we systematically analyzed the evolution of commonsense integration in vision, from early scene graphs and neuro-symbolic models to modern retrieval-augmented and foundation model approaches. Through detailed comparative analysis, we highlighted how different paradigms address the challenges of scalability, reasoning depth, and real-world applicability.

2022 年至 2025 年间的最新创新，例如检索增强常识推理 (REVIVE)、统一多模态预训练 (BEiT-3、OmniVL) 和小样本适应 (Flamingo)，标志着向能够实时利用外部知识的灵活、动态推理架构的范式转变。然而，仍然存在重大的开放挑战，包括动态知识检索、超越可观察的因果推理、计算可扩展性、偏差缓解、多模态统一和透明可解释性的需求。Recent innovations between 2022 and 2025, such as retrieval-augmented commonsense reasoning (REVIVE), unified multimodal pretraining (BEiT-3, OmniVL), and few-shot adaptation (Flamingo), signal a paradigm shift toward flexible, dynamic reasoning architectures capable of leveraging external knowledge in real time. However, significant open challenges remain, including the need for dynamic knowledge retrieval, causal inference beyond observables, computational scalability, bias mitigation, multimodal unification, and transparent explainability.

未来的研究必须结合符号推理、检索增强架构和基础模型的优势，以构建像人类一样灵活、符合情境和合乎道德的推理的视觉系统。动态常识检索、轻量级因果推理模块、偏差感知学习和可解释决策对于实现可信、稳健和可概括的常识推理至关重要。Future research must bridge the strengths of symbolic reasoning, retrieval-augmented architectures, and foundation models to build vision systems that reason like humans do, flexibly, contextually, and ethically. Dynamic commonsense retrieval, lightweight causal reasoning modules, bias-aware learning, and interpretable decision-making are essential to achieve trustworthy, robust, and generalizable commonsense reasoning.

随着该领域的发展，基于常识的视觉不仅将成为识别和字幕等经典任务的基础，而且还将成为自主代理、具体人工智能、辅助机器人和人机协作等新兴领域的基础。通过解决当前的局限性并采用跨学科方法，视觉界有望实现人工智能中类人常识推理的全部前景。As the field advances, commonsense-enabled vision will become foundational not only for classical tasks such as recognition and captioning but also for emerging domains like autonomous agents, embodied AI, assistive robotics, and human-AI collaboration. By addressing the current limitations and embracing interdisciplinary approaches, the vision community stands poised to realize the full promise of human-like commonsense reasoning in artificial intelligence.
