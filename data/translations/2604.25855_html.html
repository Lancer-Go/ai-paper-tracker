SIEVES：选择性预测通过视觉证据评分进行推广SIEVES: Selective Prediction Generalizes through Visual Evidence Scoring

Hector G. Rodriguez    Marcus Rohrbach
摘要Abstract

多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 在视觉语言任务上实现了更强的性能。 即使传统的视觉问答基准接近饱和，可靠的部署也需要在现实世界的分布外 (OOD) 场景中满足低容错能力。 准确地说，选择性预测旨在提高覆盖范围，即系统回答的输入份额，同时遵守用户定义的风险级别。 这通常是通过为每个答案分配一个置信度分数并放弃那些低于特定阈值的答案来实现的。 为了实现可靠的泛化，我们需要推理模型在回答时产生局部视觉证据， 并设计一个选择器，明确地学习估计推理器提供的本地化质量。 我们表明，与非接地基线相比，SIEVES（通过视觉证据评分进行选择性预测）在具有挑战性的 OOD 基准（V* Bench、HR-Bench-8k、MME-RealWorld-Lite、VizWiz 和 AdVQA）上将覆盖率提高了三倍。 除了更好地泛化到 OOD 任务之外，SIEVES 选择器的设计还可以传输到专有推理机，而无需访问其权重或分对数，例如 o3 和 Gemini-3-Pro，从而提供超出仅归因于准确性的覆盖范围提升。 我们强调，SIEVES 可以泛化所有五个经过测试的 OOD 数据集和推理器模型（Pixel-Reasoner、o3 和 Gemini-3-Pro），无需特定于基准或推理器的训练或适应。Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) achieve ever-stronger performance on visual-language tasks. Even as traditional visual question answering benchmarks approach saturation, reliable deployment requires satisfying low error tolerances in real-world out-of-distribution (OOD) scenarios. Precisely, selective prediction aims to improve coverage, i.e. the share of inputs the system answers, while adhering to a user-defined risk level. This is typically achieved by assigning a confidence score to each answer and abstaining on those that fall below a certain threshold. To enable reliable generalization, we require reasoner models to produce localized visual evidence while answering, and design a selector that explicitly learns to estimate the quality of the localization provided by the reasoner. We show that SIEVES (Selective Prediction through Visual Evidence Scoring) improves coverage by up to three times on challenging OOD benchmarks (V* Bench, HR-Bench-8k, MME-RealWorld-Lite, VizWiz, and AdVQA), compared to non-grounding baselines. Beyond better generalization to OOD tasks, the design of the SIEVES selector enables transfer to proprietary reasoners without access to their weights or logits, such as o3 and Gemini-3-Pro, providing coverage boosts beyond those attributable to accuracy alone. We highlight that SIEVES generalizes across all five tested OOD datasets and reasoner models (Pixel-Reasoner, o3, and Gemini-3-Pro), without benchmark- or reasoner-specific training or adaptation.

1 简介Introduction

最近几代大型语言模型 (LLM)Recent generations of large language models (LLMs)[1, 21]和视觉语言模型 (VLM)and vision-language models (VLMs)[33, 3]通过对文档的理解，能够自主执行越来越多具有经济价值的任务are capable of performing an increasing number of economically valuable tasks autonomously, from document understanding[42, 29]辅助视觉回答to assistive visual answering[13, 17]。 通常，在这些任务中，错误可能会在人力和财务方面造成高昂的代价。 然而，决定哪些任务超出了这些模型的能力范围，因此模型应该放弃或服从人类专家，这是具有挑战性的。 选择性预测，即在给定的风险承受能力下知道何时回答和何时放弃的能力，远未成为一个已解决的问题. Often in these tasks, mistakes can be costly, both in human and financial terms. However, deciding which tasks are beyond the reach of these models, and hence the model should abstain or defer to a human expert, is challenging. Selective prediction, the ability to know when to answer and when to abstain at a given risk tolerance, is far from being a solved problem[44].

视觉问答 (VQA) 一直是 VLM 的良好测试平台。 尽管如此，即使作为传统的 VQA 基准Visual Question Answering (VQA) has been a good testbed for VLMs. Nonetheless, even as traditional VQA benchmarks[2, 18]从精度角度来看已饱和become saturated from an accuracy perspective[36]，选择性预测仍然是一个挑战, selective prediction remains a challenge[11]。 选择性预测仍然困难的原因有几个。 最重要的是，机器学习 (ML) 系统的主要设计目的是尽可能正确地回答尽可能多的问题，很少或根本没有培训来在有疑问时放弃. There are several reasons why selective prediction continues to be difficult. Most importantly, machine learning (ML) systems are predominantly designed to answer as many questions correctly as possible, with little or no training to abstain when in doubt[25]。 由于用户的风险承受能力事先未知，因此真正的二元弃权/答复决策过于严格。 因此，选择性预测为每个答案分配一个数字置信度分数，这反过来又使任务变得更加困难：选择器现在必须对答案-问题对的完整分布进行排名。. Since the user’s risk tolerance is unknown in advance, a truly binary abstain/answer decision is too rigid. Selective prediction, therefore, assigns each answer a numerical confidence score, which in turn makes the task harder: the selector must now rank the full distribution of answer-question pairs.

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 SIEVES（通过视觉证据评分进行选择性预测），用于在具有挑战性的视觉问答任务中进行通用选择性预测。 通过对视觉证据的质量进行明确评分，SIEVES 可以过滤掉过于自信但基础薄弱的答案。 在一系列具有挑战性的 OOD VQA 基准上，与非接地基线相比，SIEVES 在低风险下实现了高达三倍的覆盖率。 此外，由于 SIEVES 选择器仅消耗模型输出，而不消耗对数概率或隐藏状态等内部结构，因此我们表明它可以从在较弱的开源模型上进行训练推广到使用 o3 和 Gemini-3-Pro 等专有前沿模型进行评估。 选择性预测对于安全地自动化大部分端到端任务将变得越来越重要，我们希望我们的工作能够激发视觉证据的使用，以提高视觉任务的可靠性。We introduce SIEVES (Selective Prediction through Visual Evidence Scoring) for generalizable selective prediction in challenging visual question answering tasks. By explicitly scoring the quality of visual evidence, SIEVES filters out overconfident but weakly grounded answers. On a diverse set of challenging OOD VQA benchmarks, SIEVES achieves up to three times higher coverage at low risk when compared with non-grounding baselines. Additionally, because the SIEVES selector only consumes model outputs, not internals such as log-probabilities or hidden states, we showed that it can generalize from being trained on a weaker open-source model to evaluated using a proprietary frontier model such as o3 and Gemini-3-Pro. Selective prediction will be increasingly important for safely automating a larger share of end-to-end tasks, and we hope our work can inspire the use of visual evidence to improve reliability in visual tasks.
