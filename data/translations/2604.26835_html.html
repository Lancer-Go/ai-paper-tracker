HalluCiteChecker：人工智能科学家时代幻觉引文检测和验证的轻量级工具包: A Lightweight Toolkit for Hallucinated Citation Detection and Verification in the Era of AI Scientists

Yusuke Sakai,    Hidetaka Kamigaito,    Taro Watanabe
Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), Japan
{sakai.yusuke.sr9, kamigaito.h, taro}@is.naist.jp
摘要Abstract

我们介绍We introduceHalluCiteChecker，一个用于检测和验证科学论文中的幻觉引用的工具包。 虽然人工智能辅助技术改变了学术写作过程，包括引文推荐，但它们也导致了与任何现有作品不对应的幻觉引文的出现。 此类引用不仅损害了科学论文的可信度，而且还给审稿人和作者带来了额外的负担，他们必须在审稿过程中手动验证其有效性。 在本研究中，我们将幻觉引文检测形式化为 NLP 任务，并提供相应的工具包作为解决该问题的实践基础。我们的软件包重量轻，可以在标准笔记本电脑上在几秒钟内执行验证。 它还可以完全离线执行，并且仅使用 CPU 即可高效运行。 我们希望, a toolkit for detecting and verifying hallucinated citations in scientific papers. While AI assistant technologies have transformed the academic writing process, including citation recommendation, they have also led to the emergence of hallucinated citations that do not correspond to any existing work. Such citations not only undermine the credibility of scientific papers but also impose an additional burden on reviewers and authors, who must manually verify their validity during the review process. In this study, we formalize hallucinated citation detection as an NLP task and provide a corresponding toolkit as a practical foundation for addressing this problem. Our package is lightweight and can perform verification in seconds on a standard laptop. It can also be executed entirely offline and runs efficiently using only CPUs. We hope thatHalluCiteChecker将通过系统化的预审和出版检查来帮助减少审稿人的工作量并支持组织者。 我们的代码根据 Apache 2.0 许可证在 GitHub 上发布will help reduce reviewer workload and support organizers by enabling systematic pre-review and publication checks. Our code is released under the Apache 2.0 license on GitHub111 : yusuke1997/HalluCiteChecker并通过 PyPI 作为可安装包分发and is distributed as an installable package via PyPI222 : pip install hallucitechecker。 YouTube 上提供了演示视频. 1 简介Introduction

引用是科学论文的基本组成部分Citations are a fundamental component of scientific papersWang and Barabási (2021)。它们的作用是用证据证实主张，承认先前的工作，并阐明研究领域内新贡献的定位和新颖性。除了支持科学论点之外，引用次数还可以作为评估科学影响力的关键定量指标。引用计数广泛用于评估论文、研究人员和出版场所的影响力，构成 h 指数和影响因子等指标的基础。因此，引文不仅可以作为学术参考，而且可以作为分配学术学分的机制。. They serve to substantiate claims with evidence, acknowledge prior work, and clarify the positioning and novelty of new contributions within a research area. Beyond supporting scientific arguments, citations also function as a key quantitative metric for evaluating scientific impact. Citation counts are widely used to assess the influence of papers, researchers, and publication venues, forming the basis of metrics such as the h-index and impact factor. As a result, citations serve not only as scholarly references but also as a mechanism for allocating academic credit.

最近，由大语言模型 (LLM) 提供支持的写作辅助改变了学术写作过程，包括自动起草、释义和引文推荐，这反映了科学人工智能和人工智能科学家技术的进步。这些工具提高了工作效率和可访问性，特别是对于非英语母语人士、初级研究人员和学生而言。然而，它们也带来了新的风险。 幻觉引用，即与任何真实作品不对应或包含不正确的书目信息的参考文献，已经开始越来越多地出现在正在审稿的论文、预印本，甚至同行评审的出版物中。最近的报告表明其患病率迅速增加Recently, reflecting the advancement of AI for Science and AI Scientist technologies, writing assistance powered by large language models (LLMs) has transformed the academic writing process, including automated drafting, paraphrasing, and citation recommendation. These tools improve productivity and accessibility, particularly for non-native English speakers, junior researchers, and students. However, they have also introduced new risks. Hallucinated citations, which are references that do not correspond to any real work or contain incorrect bibliographic information, have begun to appear increasingly in papers under review, preprints, and even peer-reviewed publications. Recent reports indicate that their prevalence has increased rapidlySakai et al. (2026)。这种趋势对科学传播的完整性和可靠性构成了严重威胁，科学传播传统上依赖于隐含的善意假设，即作者准确引用现有的相关工作. 幻觉引用的不断增加将额外的验证责任转移给审稿人，审稿人现在必须手动确认引用参考文献的有效性以维持出版物质量。 这一验证过程给审稿人和出版组织者带来了重大的单方面负担。 因此，包含幻觉引用的论文可能会面临桌面拒绝或其他纠正措施。 尽管做出了这些努力，系统调查显示，包含幻觉引用的论文已被多个场所接受。 这些发现凸显了手动验证的局限性，并强调了对自动检测方法的迫切需要。The growing presence of hallucinated citations has shifted additional verification responsibilities onto reviewers, who must now manually confirm the validity of cited references to maintain publication quality. This verification process imposes a significant and unilateral burden on reviewers and publication organizers. Consequently, papers containing hallucinated citations may face desk rejection or other corrective actions. Despite these efforts, systematic investigations have revealed that papers containing hallucinated citations have been accepted to several venues. These findings highlight the limitations of manual verification and underscore the urgent need for automated detection methods.

2.1 引文提取Citation Extraction

科学论文通常作为结构化文档分发，例如 PDF 文件Scientific papers are typically distributed as structured documents, e.g., PDF files555科学研究也以各种格式呈现，包括物理打印输出和 HTML 页面（例如博客文章）。在本文中，为了简单说明，我们重点关注 PDF 文档。这些替代格式仅仅是输入格式的变体。此外，尽管文档解析研究取得了重大进展，但可靠地提取完整且准确的引文信息仍然具有挑战性。Scientific research is also presented in various formats, including physical printouts and HTML pages such as blog posts. In this paper, we focus on PDF documents for simplicity of exposition. These alternative formats are merely variations in input format. Moreover, reliably extracting complete and accurate citation information remains challenging, despite significant progress in document parsing research.。在此约束下，第一步是从文档中提取引文信息。引文提取涉及从原始文档中识别和解析书目条目，由于布局、格式和文档质量的变化，这带来了挑战。 该子任务提供了几个改进和扩展的方向。通过解析、OCR、后期校正等方式可以提高识别准确率和效率。此外，有必要隔离特定引文信息，例如识别参考文献部分和分隔各个书目记录。. Under this constraint, the first step is to extract citation information from the document. Citation extraction involves identifying and parsing bibliographic entries from raw documents, which presents challenges due to variations in layout, formatting, and document quality. This subtask offers several directions for improvement and extension. Recognition accuracy and efficiency can be improved through parsing, OCR, and post-correction. Moreover, it is necessary to isolate citation-specific information, such as identifying reference sections and separating individual bibliographic records.

此外，该子任务可以扩展到提取外围信息，包括结构信息，例如边界框坐标以及与引文相关的上下文信息。此类扩展可以实现引文使用的结构理解和可视化，并为下游识别和验证提供额外的信号，包括评估引文是否适当和有效。总之，此子任务侧重于从文档中提取与引文相关的信息，包括书目条目以及相关的结构和上下文元数据。In addition, this subtask can be extended to extract peripheral information, including structural information, e.g., bounding-box coordinates, and contextual information associated with citations. Such extensions enable a structural understanding and visualization of citation usage, and provide additional signals for downstream recognition and verification, including assessing whether citations are appropriate and valid. In summary, this subtask focuses on extracting citation-related information, including bibliographic entries and associated structural and contextual metadata, from documents.

2.2 引文识别Citation Recognition

引文信息通常表示为文档内的内联文本。因此，有必要对各个引文组成部分进行识别和分类，例如标题、作者姓名、出版年份、地点和页码。引文风格差异很大，包括 APA 和 MLA 等标准格式，以及会议和期刊特定的格式约定。此外，引文条目可能包含缺失或不完整的字段。因此，基于规则的简单提取方法在现实场景中是不够的。Citation information is typically represented as inline text within documents. Therefore, it is necessary to identify and classify individual citation components, such as the title, author names, publication year, venue, and page numbers. Citation styles vary widely, including standard formats such as APA and MLA, as well as conference- and journal-specific formatting conventions. In addition, citation entries may contain missing or incomplete fields. Consequently, naive rule-based extraction approaches are insufficient in real-world scenarios.

该子任务旨在通过确定哪些跨度对应于特定的书目组件来识别野外引文文本的语义结构。这可以被视为命名实体识别（NER）任务的特殊形式，其目标是从原始引文字符串中提取结构化书目字段。尽管现有的 NER 技术可以适用于科学文档，但由于实体类型数量众多以及跨场所和学科的引文格式的多样性，挑战仍然存在。This subtask aims to identify the semantic structure of citation text in the wild by determining which spans correspond to specific bibliographic components. This can be regarded as a specialized form of named entity recognition (NER) task, where the goal is to extract structured bibliographic fields from raw citation strings. Although existing NER techniques can be adapted for scientific documents, challenges remain due to the large number of entity types and the diversity of citation formats encountered across venues and disciplines.

2.3 引文匹配Citation Matching

给定结构化引文信息，我们通过将其与书目数据库（例如本地数据库和基于网络的学术搜索引擎）进行匹配来确定每个引文是否有效。虽然可以为此目的使用各种引文字段，但先前对幻觉引文验证的研究表明，标题信息可作为可靠的匹配密钥Given structured citation information, we determine whether each citation is valid by matching it against bibliographic databases, such as local databases and web-based scholarly search engines. While it is possible to use various citation fields for this purpose, prior studies on hallucinated citation verification have shown that title information serves as the reliable matching keySakai et al. (2026); Bienz et al. (2026); Xu et al. Therefore, in this paper, we primarily focus on title-based citation matching. Note that if structured bibliographic data, such as BibTeX entries, are available, citation verification can be conducted directly starting from this subtask. However, such structured metadata is rarely available in practice. Therefore, it is desirable to consider the entire pipeline, including citation extraction, recognition, and matching.

7 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introducedHalluCiteChecker，一个用于检测科学论文中的幻觉引用的工具包。我们将引文验证形式化为一项结构化任务，并提出了一个模块化框架，将流程分解为三个子任务，为未来的研究提供了明确的基础。此外，如部分所示, a toolkit for detecting hallucinated citations in scientific papers. We formalized citation verification as a structured task and presented a modular framework that decomposes the process into three subtasks, providing a clear foundation for future research. Moreover, as shown in Section6，该工具包可以细粒度测量每个组件的处理时间，便于未来开发中的系统分析和优化。 除了技术贡献之外，我们的目标是支持更加透明和建设性的科学审查过程。通过实现系统性和可重复的引文验证，该工具包可以帮助减少意外的案头拒绝和不必要的审稿人工作量，从而促进作者、审稿人和组织者之间负责任的研究实践。, the toolkit enables fine-grained measurement of processing time for each component, facilitating systematic analysis and optimization in future development. Beyond its technical contributions, our goal is to support a more transparent and constructive scientific review process. By enabling systematic and reproducible citation verification, the toolkit can help reduce unintended desk rejections and unnecessary reviewer workload, thereby promoting responsible research practices among authors, reviewers, and organizers.
