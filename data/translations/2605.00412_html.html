摘要Abstract

世界模型最近重新出现，成为实体智能、机器人技术、自动驾驶和基于模型的强化学习的中心范式。然而，当前的世界模型研究通常由三个部分独立的路线主导：强调视觉未来合成的 2D 视频生成模型、强调空间重建的以 3D 场景为中心的模型以及强调抽象预测表示的类似 JEPA 的潜在模型。尽管每条路线都取得了重要进展，但它们仍然难以为具体决策提供物理可靠、动作可控且长期稳定的预测。在本文中，我们认为世界模型的瓶颈不再仅仅是它们是否能够生成现实的未来，而是这些未来是否具有物理意义和对行动有用。我们建议World models have recently re-emerged as a central paradigm for embodied intelligence, robotics, autonomous driving, and model-based reinforcement learning. However, current world model research is often dominated by three partially separated routes: 2D video-generative models that emphasize visual future synthesis, 3D scene-centric models that emphasize spatial reconstruction, and JEPA-like latent models that emphasize abstract predictive representations. While each route has made important progress, they still struggle to provide physically reliable, action-controllable, and long-horizon stable predictions for embodied decision making. In this paper, we argue that the bottleneck of world models is no longer only whether they can generate realistic futures, but whether those futures are physically meaningful and useful for action. We propose哈密顿世界模型Hamiltonian World Models作为世界建模的基于物理的视角。关键思想是将观测值编码到结构化的潜在相空间中，通过受哈密顿量启发的动力学（具有控制、耗散和残差项）演化潜在状态，将预测轨迹解码为未来的观测值，并使用生成的结果进行规划。我们讨论哈密顿结构如何提高可解释性、数据效率和长期稳定性，同时还注意到现实世界机器人场景中涉及摩擦、接触、非保守力和可变形物体的实际挑战。as a physically grounded perspective on world modeling. The key idea is to encode observations into a structured latent phase space, evolve the latent state through Hamiltonian-inspired dynamics with control, dissipation, and residual terms, decode the predicted trajectory into future observations, and use the resulting rollouts for planning. We discuss how Hamiltonian structure may improve interpretability, data efficiency, and long-horizon stability, while also noting practical challenges in real-world robotic scenes involving friction, contact, non-conservative forces, and deformable objects.

1 简介Introduction

学习内部模拟器的想法作为跨具体人工智能、机器人技术和决策的统一目标重新受到重视 制作。这种新的兴趣是由两种融合趋势驱动的。首先，大规模基础模型在语言、视觉和多模态推理方面表现出强大的语义能力The idea of learning internal simulators has regained prominence as a unifying objective across embodied AI, robotics, and decision making. This renewed interest is driven by two converging trends. First, large-scale foundation models have demonstrated strong semantic competence in language, vision, and multimodal reasoning(Radford et al., 2021; Alayrac et al., 2022; OpenAI, 2023; Driess et al., 2023; Brohan et al., 2023)。其次，实体智能体需要的预测模型不仅仅局限于识别当前场景：它们必须预测世界在自己的行动下将如何变化。例如，机器人不仅应该识别物体，还应该预测推动、抓握或释放物体是否会导致稳定的操作、碰撞或任务失败。这使得世界建模成为感知、预测和决策之间的天然桥梁。. Second, embodied agents require predictive models that go beyond recognizing the current scene: they must anticipate how the world will change under their own actions. A robot, for example, should not only identify an object, but also predict whether pushing, grasping, or releasing it will lead to stable manipulation, collision, or task failure. This makes world modeling a natural bridge between perception, prediction, and decision making.

世界模型的经典动机是提供环境的内部模拟器，使代理能够在行动之前想象可能的未来。早期的工作表明，学习的潜在模型可以通过将观察结果压缩为紧凑的预测表示来支持控制The classical motivation for world models is to provide an internal simulator of the environment, enabling agents to imagine possible futures before acting. Early work showed that learned latent models can support control by compressing observations into compact predictive representations(Ha and Schmidhuber, 2018)。基于模型的强化学习进一步证明，智能体可以在学习到的潜在动态中规划或优化策略，从而减少昂贵的真实环境交互的需要. Model-based reinforcement learning further demonstrated that agents can plan or optimize policies inside learned latent dynamics, reducing the need for costly real environment interaction(Hafner et al., 2019, 2020, 2023)。在机器人技术中，视觉预见和视频预测已用于在执行前评估候选动作. In robotics, visual foresight and video prediction have been used to evaluate candidate actions before execution(Finn and Levine, 2017; Ebert et al., 2018)。在自动驾驶和交互式模拟中，GAIA-1 和 Genie 等生成世界模型表明，未来的场景生成可以以动作、文本或潜在控制为条件. In autonomous driving and interactive simulation, generative world models such as GAIA-1 and Genie suggest that future scene generation can be conditioned on actions, text, or latent controls(Hu et al., 2023; Bruce et al., 2024)。总之，这些发展使得“创造未来”成为越来越重要的研究方向。. Together, these developments have made “generating the future” an increasingly important research direction.

然而，生成未来视频并不等于理解物理世界。现代视频生成模型可以合成视觉上合理的序列，但视觉合理性本身并不能保证物理有效性。生成的轨迹可能在短时间内看起来很真实，但逐渐违反了物体持久性、接触一致性、动量守恒或动作效果因果关系。这种区别对于机器人和自主系统尤其重要，因为不正确的未来预测可能会直接误导规划和执行。对于面向娱乐的视频合成，感知现实主义可能就足够了；对于具体决策来说，预测的未来也必须是动态可行、可控和可靠的。However, generating future video is not equivalent to understanding the physical world. Modern video generative models can synthesize visually plausible sequences, but visual plausibility alone does not guarantee physical validity. A generated trajectory may look realistic over a short horizon while gradually violating object permanence, contact consistency, momentum conservation, or action-effect causality. This distinction is especially important for robotics and autonomous systems, where an incorrect future prediction can directly mislead planning and execution. For entertainment-oriented video synthesis, perceptual realism may be sufficient; for embodied decision making, the predicted future must also be dynamically feasible, controllable, and reliable.

The more fundamental question is whether the generated future is physically coherent and useful for action. A world model should not merely predict what the next frames look like; it should represent how objects, agents, and their interactions evolve under interventions. Prior work on model-based control and robotic video prediction has already shown the practical value of imagined rollouts for planning(Finn and Levine, 2017; Ebert et al., 2018; Hafner et al., 2020, 2023)。然而，为了支持可靠决策的此类推出，潜在的动态必须是稳定的、可解释的，并且受到物理世界的规律性的约束。. Yet for such rollouts to support reliable decision making, the latent dynamics must be stable, interpretable, and constrained by the regularities of the physical world.

因此，我们认为世界模型研究的下一阶段应该从视觉生成转向物理结构建模。从这个角度来看，视频生成是一个观察级的界面，而世界模型的核心是其潜在的动态。内部状态应该编码实体、关系、可控变量和有物理意义的结构；过渡应反映几何、能量、接触和动作条件因果关系等约束；所生成的未来应该被视为底层状态演变的呈现。这种观点与结构化动态学习、以对象为中心的表示学习和物理信息机器学习方面的更广泛努力是一致的We therefore argue that the next stage of world model research should move from visual generation toward physical structure modeling. In this view, video generation is an observation-level interface, while the core of a world model is its latent dynamics. The internal state should encode entities, relations, controllable variables, and physically meaningful structure; the transition should reflect constraints such as geometry, energy, contact, and action-conditioned causality; and the generated future should be treated as a rendering of an underlying state evolution. This perspective is consistent with broader efforts in structured dynamics learning, object-centric representation learning, and physics-informed machine learning(Battaglia et al., 2016; Greydanus et al., 2019; Toth et al., 2020; Raissi et al., 2019; Sanchez-Gonzalez et al., 2020; Kipf et al., 2020).

受这一观点的启发，我们建议通过哈密顿动力学的视角重新思考世界模型。哈密​​顿力学提供了一个原则框架，用于通过广义坐标、动量和能量函数来描述相空间中的物理演化。哈密​​顿世界模型不是要求视频模型纯粹从数据中发现物理定律，而是利用学习的相空间中基于能量的动力学来控制潜在的演化。这为世界模型提供了一条道路，该模型不仅具有生成性，而且在物理上可解释、数据高效且在长期部署下保持稳定。Motivated by this perspective, we propose to rethink world models through the lens of Hamiltonian dynamics. Hamiltonian mechanics provides a principled framework for describing physical evolution in phase space through generalized coordinates, momenta, and an energy function. Rather than asking a video model to discover physical laws purely from data, a Hamiltonian world model leverages energy-based dynamics in a learned phase space to govern latent evolution. This offers a path toward world models that are not only generative, but also physically interpretable, data-efficient, and stable under long-horizon rollout.

Its limitation is that a video may look realistic while still violating contact, momentum, action-effect consistency, or long-horizon stability.

3D scene-centric world models.

第二条路线强调明确的空间结构。以 3D 场景为中心的模型不是主要将世界表示为一系列 2D 帧，而是旨在恢复支持新颖视图合成、重建和空间推理的几何感知表示。神经辐射场将场景建模为连续体积辐射场，并已成为视图一致场景表示的基础The second route emphasizes explicit spatial structure. Instead of representing the world primarily as a sequence of 2D frames, 3D scene-centric models aim to recover geometry-aware representations that support novel view synthesis, reconstruction, and spatial reasoning. Neural Radiance Fields model scenes as continuous volumetric radiance fields and have become a foundation for view-consistent scene representation(Mildenhall et al., 2020)。 3D Gaussian Splatting 使用显式 3D 高斯基元进一步提高实时渲染效率和视觉质量. 3D Gaussian Splatting further improves real-time rendering efficiency and visual quality using explicit 3D Gaussian primitives(Kerbl et al., 2023)。这条路线很重要，因为具体代理必须在三维空间中操作，而纯粹的 2D 视频预测可能无法捕获视图一致性、遮挡结构和度量几何。然而，仅 3D 重建还不是完整的世界模型。许多 3D 场景表示更适合静态或准静态重建，而不是动作条件的物理演化。它们可以回答物体在哪里以及从另一个角度看场景如何，但它们不一定回答物体如何移动、碰撞、变形或对机器人动作的响应。. This route is important because embodied agents must operate in three-dimensional space, and purely 2D video prediction may fail to capture view consistency, occlusion structure, and metric geometry. However, 3D reconstruction alone is not yet a complete world model. Many 3D scene representations are better suited to static or quasi-static reconstruction than to action-conditioned physical evolution. They can answer where objects are and how a scene looks from another view, but they do not necessarily answer how objects move, collide, deform, or respond to robot actions.

第三条路线侧重于潜在空间中的预测表示学习。联合嵌入预测架构不是重建像素，而是学习预测丢失或未来输入的抽象表示The third route focuses on predictive representation learning in latent space. Instead of reconstructing pixels, Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures learn to predict abstract representations of missing or future inputs(Assran et al., 2023; Bardes et al., 2024)。这种设计的动机是智能系统应该预测高级特征而不是每个低级像素。与像素级生成相比，潜在预测可以更紧凑、更具可扩展性，并且更少受不相关视觉细节的支配。这使得类似 JEPA 的模型作为通向抽象世界表示的途径而具有吸引力。然而，由于这些模型主要是为表示学习而不是显式物理模拟而设计的，因此它们的潜在变量不一定与位置、速度、动量、能量或接触约束等物理量一致。因此，虽然类似 JEPA 的模型可以提供强大的预测抽象，但如果此类表示要支持物理基础的规划和控制，则需要额外的结构。. This design is motivated by the idea that intelligent systems should predict high-level features rather than every low-level pixel. Compared with pixel-level generation, latent prediction can be more compact, more scalable, and less dominated by irrelevant visual details. This makes JEPA-like models attractive as a route toward abstract world representations. However, because these models are primarily designed for representation learning rather than explicit physical simulation, their latent variables are not necessarily aligned with physical quantities such as position, velocity, momentum, energy, or contact constraints. Therefore, while JEPA-like models may provide strong predictive abstractions, additional structure is needed if such representations are to support physically grounded planning and control.

3 Limitations of Current World Models

尽管取得了快速进展，当前的世界模型在用于具体决策时仍然面临一些基本限制。这些限制不仅仅反映了模型规模或视觉质量不足；相反，它们可能揭示了生成合理的观察结果与模拟物理上可靠的世界动态之间更深层次的不匹配。Despite rapid progress, current world models still face several fundamental limitations when used for embodied decision making. These limitations do not simply reflect insufficient model scale or visual quality; rather, they may reveal a deeper mismatch between generating plausible observations and modeling physically reliable world dynamics.

This becomes critical in robotics and autonomous driving, where a generated future is useful only if it corresponds to a dynamically feasible outcome.

当物理先验不存在时，数据效率仍然有限。Data efficiency remains limited when physical priors are absent.

挑战和界限。Challenges and boundaries.

尽管哈密顿世界模型提供了吸引人的物理视角，但应明确说明其范围和局限性。首先，真实的具体环境并不是理想的哈密顿系统：它们涉及摩擦、控制输入、影响、耗散、部分可观察性，并且通常是非刚性相互作用。因此，实用模型必须将哈密顿动力学视为结构先验，而不是对整个世界的字面描述Although Hamiltonian World Models offer an appealing physical perspective, their scope and limitations should be stated clearly. First, real embodied environments are not ideal Hamiltonian systems: they involve friction, control inputs, impacts, dissipation, partial observability, and often non-rigid interactions. 8 未来的方向Future Directions

更广泛的机会是从The broader opportunity is to move from视频世界模型video world models朝向toward物理世界基础模型physical world foundation models。在这一转变中，哈密顿世界模型并没有取代其他范式，而是被视为提供了一个物理接地轴，沿着这个轴世界模型可以成熟。未来的系统可能会结合 2D 视频生成的感知丰富性、3D 场景表示的空间基础、类似 JEPA 的潜在预测的紧凑抽象以及基于能量的相空间动力学的稳定性。未来的一个关键方向是从场景级物理部署转向开放式物理泛化。未来的哈密顿世界模型可能不仅仅对固定场景中实体之间的交互进行建模，而且应该学习可重用的物理机制，这些机制可以由看不见的物体、材料、实施例和任务组成。从这个意义上说，长期目标不仅是一个更稳定的世界模型，而且是一个能够不断进行物理抽象、组合和自我进化的物理原生基础模型。. In this transition, rather than replacing other paradigms, Hamiltonian World Models are viewed as providing a physically grounded axis along which world models can mature. Future systems may combine the perceptual richness of 2D video generation, the spatial grounding of 3D scene representations, the compact abstraction of JEPA-like latent prediction, and the stability of energy-based phase-space dynamics. A critical future direction is to move from scene-level physical rollout to open-ended physical generalization. Future Hamiltonian World Models may not merely model the interactions among entities in a fixed scene, but should learn reusable physical mechanisms that can be composed across unseen objects, materials, embodiments, and tasks. In this sense, the long-term goal is not only a more stable world model, but a physically native foundation model capable of continual physical abstraction, composition, and self-evolution.
