1 简介Introduction

自回归语言模型Autoregressive language modelsBrown et al. (2020)有一个用于开放式生成的简单接口：它们一次发出一个令牌，并在生成序列结束令牌时停止。掩蔽扩散语言模型have a simple interface for open-ended generation: they emit one token at a time and stop when an end-of-sequence token is produced. Masked diffusion language models(Austin et al., 2021a; Campbell et al., 2022; Zheng et al., 2023; Lou et al., 2023; Sahoo et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024)相比之下，通过迭代地对屏蔽标记画布进行去噪来生成。该范式实现了并行令牌更新、双向条件和任意顺序细化等有吸引力的属性，并且最近已扩展到有竞争力的指令跟踪模型，例如 LLaDA, by contrast, generate by iteratively denoising a canvas of masked tokens. This paradigm enables attractive properties such as parallel token updates, bidirectional conditioning, and arbitrary-order refinement, and has recently scaled to competitive instruction-following models such as LLaDANie et al. (2026)和梦想and DreamYe et al. (2025)。然而，它暴露了一个基本的推理问题：生成长度通常必须在解码开始之前固定。. However, it exposes a basic inference problem: the generation length must typically be fixed before decoding begins.

这种固定画布的假设与现实生成不太匹配。如果画布太短，模型可能会截断代码、推理或解释。如果画布太长，推理会浪费对最终答案可能不需要的屏蔽位置的计算，甚至可能通过强制模型对超出有用响应的去噪来损害质量This fixed-canvas assumption is poorly matched to realistic generation. If the canvas is too short, the model may truncate code, reasoning, or explanations. If the canvas is too long, inference wastes computation on masked positions that may not be needed for the final answer, and can even harm quality by forcing the model to denoise beyond the useful responseRossi et al. (2026)。适当的长度还取决于实例：来自同一任务的两个提示可能需要非常不同的响应长度，即使它们使用相同的解码设置也是如此。因此，可变长度解码并不是一个次要的工程细节，而是使扩散 LM 可用于开放式生成的主要障碍。. The appropriate length is also instance-dependent: two prompts from the same task may require very different response lengths, even when they use the same decoding setup. As a result, variable-length decoding is not a minor engineering detail, but a central obstacle to making diffusion LMs practical for open-ended generation.

一个自然的解决方案是在去噪期间调整画布。之前的工作，例如 DAEDALA natural solution is to adapt the canvas during denoising. Prior work such as DAEDALLi et al. (2025)通过使用内部置信信号来决定何时分配额外的屏蔽标记，提出了扩散语言模型的无训练长度扩展。这说明解码前不需要固定响应长度 开始，并且可以在推理过程中进行调整。然而，扩展引入了第二个很容易被忽视的问题：添加掩模会改变去噪问题本身。在双向屏蔽扩散模型中，插入新的屏蔽位置可能会扰乱模型在其他尚未解决的位置的预测。因此，适当的长度扩展规则应该询问额外的空间是否有用，以及插入该空间是否使现有的预测足够稳定。proposes training-free length expansion for diffusion language models by using internal confidence signals to decide when to allocate additional masked tokens. This shows that response length need not be fixed before decoding begins and can instead be adjusted during inference. Yet expansion introduces a second question that is easy to overlook: adding masks changes the denoising problem itself. In a bidirectional masked diffusion model, inserting new masked positions may perturb the model’s predictions at other still-unresolved positions. An appropriate length expansion rule should therefore ask both whether additional space might be useful and whether inserting that space leaves the existing predictions sufficiently stable.

CARVE augments a standard diffusion sampler with a verified expansion step. Before certain reveal steps, CARVE proposes inserting a number of additional masked tokens into the current canvas. It then runs the model once on the expanded canvas and compares the predictive distributions of the original and expanded canvases at aligned still-masked positions. The insertion is accepted only when the mean Jensen–Shannon divergence between these distributions remains below a fixed threshold. Otherwise, the expanded branch is rejected and we continue with the original canvas. In this way, the canvas grows only when the proposed expansion leaves the predictive distributions at aligned unresolved positions sufficiently stable under this criterion.

这将长度扩展转变为稳定性检查，而不是纯粹的置信启发式。每个提议的插入都会提出一个本地反事实问题：如果画布包含额外的空间，模型是否会对未解析的标记做出基本相同的预测？当答案为是时，可以在不显着改变去噪状态的情况下提交添加的掩模。如果答案是否定的，该提案将被视为破坏稳定的编辑并被丢弃。此标准不同于仅基于绝对置信度或序列结束 (EOS) 令牌行为的扩展规则This turns length expansion into a stability check rather than a pure confidence heuristic. Each proposed insertion asks a local counterfactual question: would the model make essentially the same predictions for the unresolved tokens if the canvas contained additional space? When the answer is yes, the added masks can be committed without substantially changing the denoising state. When the answer is no, the proposal is treated as a destabilizing edit and discarded. The same principle applies across different masked diffusion backends; in this work, we instantiate CARVE for both full-canvas Dream decoding and blockwise LLaDA decoding.

Recent large-scale models, such as LLaDA and Dream, demonstrate that this paradigm scales effectively to complex instruction-following and reasoning tasks(Nie et al., 2026; Ye et al., 2025)。然而，由于这些模型预测并揭示预定义屏蔽画布上的标记子集，因此它们在解码开始之前本质上施加了严格的长度约束。. However, because these models predict and reveal subsets of tokens across a predefined masked canvas, they inherently impose a strict length constraint before decoding begins.

为了克服固定长度的瓶颈，有几种方法修改了底层的生成公式或状态空间。现有方法实现了代码填充的动态伸缩To overcome the fixed-length bottleneck, several approaches modify the underlying generative formulation or state space. While these strategies successfully enable dynamic length adjustment, they require specialized architectural modifications or costly retraining. CARVE, by contrast, applies directly to pretrained masked dLLMs.

仅当建议的插入在对齐的未解决位置产生较小的均值分布偏移时，CARVE 才会扩展画布。该稳定性标准仍然足够宽松，以允许评估设置中的有用增长。在各个模型中，CARVE 持续提高了固定长度基线的平均准确度，同时通常比固定长度解码使用更少的计算量。CARVE expands the canvas only when the proposed insertion produces a small mean distribution shift at aligned unresolved positions. This stability criterion remains permissive enough to allow useful growth in the evaluated settings. Across models, CARVE consistently improves average accuracy over fixed-length baselines while often using less compute than fixed-length decoding.

结果表明，掩模扩散 LM 的有效长度控制不仅在于添加更多掩模，而且还在于在不破坏去噪状态稳定性的情况下添加它们。 JS 分歧通过仅当模型的预测在插入后保持稳定时才接受扩展分支来提供此检查。消融支持了这一观点：阈值太小会拒绝有用的增长，而阈值太大则使 CARVE 几乎等同于始终扩张。The results suggest that effective length control for masked diffusion LMs is not only about adding more masks, but about adding them without destabilizing the denoising state. JS divergence provides this check by accepting an expanded branch only when the model’s predictions remain stable after insertion. The ablations support this view: too small a threshold rejects useful growth, while too large a threshold makes CARVE nearly equivalent to always expanding.

最后，计算结果表明，分支引入的额外前向传播不一定会转化为更高的总成本。在 CARVE 中，分支与 EOS 裁剪配对：一旦模型提交序列结束标记，解码就会在有用的前缀上停止，而不是继续细化将被丢弃的后缀位置。与自适应展示计划一起，这抵消了扩展尝试的大部分成本。因此，CARVE 仅使用推理时间变化来提高准确性-计算权衡，而无需重新训练或修改底层扩散模型。Finally, the compute results show that the extra forward passes introduced by branching do not necessarily translate into higher total cost. In CARVE, branching is paired with EOS crop: once the model commits an end-of-sequence token, decoding stops on the useful prefix instead of continuing to refine suffix positions that would be discarded. Together with the adaptive reveal schedule, this offsets much of the cost of expansion attempts. As a result, CARVE improves the accuracy–compute trade-off using only inference-time changes, without retraining or modifying the underlying diffusion model.

我们推出了 CARVE (We introduced CARVE (C反事实-ounterfactual-A洁具wareR评估与eveal withV埃里化erifiedExpansion），一种用于掩蔽扩散语言模型的免训练变长解码算法。 CARVE 通过分支到候选扩展画布并仅在保留模型对对齐的未解决位置的预测时接受扩展来解决 dLLM 的固定画布限制。这将长度控制变成了反事实的稳定性测试，而不是纯粹的置信度或 EOS 启发式。xpansion), a training-free variable-length decoding algorithm for masked diffusion language models. CARVE addresses the fixed-canvas limitation of dLLMs by branching into candidate expanded canvases and accepting an expansion only when it preserves the model’s predictions on aligned unresolved positions. This turns length control into a counterfactual stability test rather than a pure confidence or EOS heuristic.

在代码生成和数学推理基准测试中，CARVE 提高了每个评估的扩散 LM 相对于固定长度基线的平均性能。尽管分支增加了前向传递，但自适应显示和 EOS 裁剪通常会抵消这一成本，从而产生比固定长度解码更好的精度-计算权衡。总体而言，我们的结果表明，预训练的掩模扩散 LM 已经包含用于安全长度适应的有用信号，可以在推理时直接利用这些信号，而无需重新训练或架构更改。Across code-generation and mathematical-reasoning benchmarks, CARVE improves average performance over fixed-length baselines for each evaluated diffusion LM. Although branching adds forward passes, adaptive reveal and EOS cropping often offset this cost, yielding a better accuracy–compute trade-off than fixed-length decoding. Overall, our results show that pretrained masked diffusion LMs already contain useful signals for safe length adaptation, which can be exploited directly at inference time without retraining or architectural changes.

局限性Limitations

CARVE 目前在每个接受的扩展处插入固定数量的掩码标记。在我们的主要配置中，该值设置为CARVE currently inserts a fixed number of mask tokens at each accepted expansion. In our main configurations, this value is set tok=16k=16。虽然这在经验上效果很好，但它不能适应每个提示的不确定性或长度要求。未来工作的一个自然方向是使插入大小自适应，不仅决定在哪里扩展画布，还决定应添加多少新的蒙版标记。. While this works well empirically, it does not adapt to the uncertainty or length requirements of each prompt. A natural direction for future work is to make insertion size adaptive, deciding not only where to expand the canvas but also how many new mask tokens should be added.

第二个限制是 JS 散度计算使用的对齐方式。 CARVE 仅比较插入前已存在的未解析位置的预测，而新插入的蒙版位置则被排除在外，因为它们在基础画布中没有直接对应的位置。这使得验证步骤简单且定义明确，但可能过于严格。未来的工作可以探索更软的对齐或替代的发散标准，这些标准也可以解释新插入位置的行为。A second limitation is the alignment used by the JS divergence computation. CARVE compares predictions only on unresolved positions that already existed before insertion, while the newly inserted mask positions are excluded because they have no direct counterpart in the base canvas. This makes the verification step simple and well-defined, but it may be overly rigid. Future work could explore softer alignment or alternative divergence criteria that also account for the behavior of the newly inserted positions.
