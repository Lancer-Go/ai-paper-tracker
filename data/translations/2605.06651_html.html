AI 联合数学家：利用 Agentic AI 加速数学家发展AI Co-Mathematician: Accelerating Mathematicians with Agentic AI

摘要Abstract

我们推出了 AI 联合数学家，这是数学家可以交互地利用 AI 代理进行开放式研究的工作台。 AI 联合数学家经过优化，可为数学工作流程的探索性和迭代现实提供整体支持，包括构思、文献检索、计算探索、定理证明和理论构建。通过提供一个异步、有状态的工作空间来管理不确定性、完善用户意图、跟踪失败的假设并输出本地数学工件，该系统反映了人类协作工作流程。在早期测试中，人工智能联合数学家帮助研究人员解决开放问题，确定新的研究方向，并发现被忽视的文献参考。除了展示人工智能辅助数学发现的高度交互范例外，这位人工智能联合数学家还在难题解决基准上取得了最先进的成绩，包括在 FrontierMath Tier 4 上得分 48%，这是所有评估的人工智能系统中的新高分。We introduce the AI co-mathematician, a workbench for mathematicians to interactively leverage AI agents to pursue open-ended research. The AI co-mathematician is optimized to provide holistic support for the exploratory and iterative reality of mathematical workflows, including ideation, literature search, computational exploration, theorem proving and theory building. By providing an asynchronous, stateful workspace that manages uncertainty, refines user intent, tracks failed hypotheses, and outputs native mathematical artifacts, the system mirrors human collaborative workflows. In early tests, the AI co-mathematician helped researchers solve open problems, identify new research directions, and uncover overlooked literature references. Besides demonstrating a highly interactive paradigm for AI-assisted mathematical discovery, the AI co-mathematician also achieves state of the art results on hard problem-solving benchmarks, including scoring 48% on FrontierMath Tier 4, a new high score among all AI systems evaluated.

2 人工智能辅助数学的设计原则Design Principles for AI-Assisted Mathematics

我们设计的首要目标是支持人类数学家的工作。 作为The overriding goal of our design is to support human mathematicians in their own work. As[undefan]众所周知，数学从根本上来说是一项旨在促进人类理解的社会事业，而不仅仅是生成形式证明的机器。 为了真正加速数学家的发展，人工智能工具必须融入到以人为本的现实中。 为此，人工智能联合数学家的建立是为了捕捉专业数学工作流程的持久方面，同时务实地探索人工智能推理当前的优势和劣势。 我们的设计既基于数学实践的历史记录，也基于我们观察和改进以前的数学发现系统的经验，特别是那些在谷歌开发的系统。 我们依靠七个核心原则来指导这个设计：famously argued, mathematics is fundamentally a social enterprise aimed at advancing human understanding, rather than merely a machine for generating formal proofs. To genuinely accelerate mathematicians, AI tools must integrate into this human-centric reality. To this end, the AI co-mathematician is built to capture the enduring aspects of professional mathematical workflows, while pragmatically navigating the current strengths and weaknesses of AI reasoning. Our design is grounded in both historical accounts of mathematical practice and our experience observing and improving previous systems for mathematical discovery, especially those developed at Google. We relied on seven core principles to guide this design:

拥抱超越证明的数学：Embrace mathematics beyond proofs:

真正的数学发现涉及定理证明之外的一系列复杂的活动，例如迭代地完善研究问题、梳理文献、集思广益、进行广泛的计算和数值模拟以建立直觉等等。Genuine mathematical discovery involves a complex mixture of activities beyond theorem proving, such as iteratively refining research questions, combing the literature, brainstorming, and running extensive calculations and numerical simulations to build intuition, to name a few.[undefao]认为将数学简化为孤立的逻辑形式主义忽略了该学科深刻的准经验现实，而我们的设计有意反映和支持这种整体的、多模态的现实，而不是仅仅索引最终的定理证明。argued that reducing mathematics to isolated logical formalisms ignored the deeply quasi-empirical reality of the discipline, and our design deliberately reflects and supports this holistic, multi-modal reality, rather than indexing solely on final theorem proving.

支持意图的迭代细化：Support iterative refinement of intent:

在许多领域，用户从一开始就准确地知道他们想要什么。 然而，在数学中，发现的过程很大程度上依赖于不断的迭代和完善。 作为In many domains, users know precisely what they want from the outset. In mathematics, however, the process of discovery is heavily dependent on continuous iteration and refinement. As[undef]在他关于合理推理的工作中证明，提出正确的问题通常需要大量的启发式猜测才能开始。 正如乔治·康托 (Georg Cantor) 所说，“在数学中，提出问题的艺术必须比解决问题的艺术具有更高的价值”demonstrated in his work on plausible inference, formulating the right question often requires extensive heuristic guesswork just to get started. As argued by Georg Cantor, “In mathematics, the art of proposing a question must be held of higher value than solving it”[undefap, p. 27]。 AlphaEvolve 等系统的经验证实了这一点：数学家花费大量精力积极迭代到底要关注什么问题——在扩大规模之前用较小的运行或相关问题进行测试. Experience with systems like AlphaEvolve corroborates this: mathematicians spend considerable effort actively iterating on exactly what problem to focus on—testing with smaller runs or related problems before scaling up[undefq, undefr]。 我们设计了一个支持多种探索路径的灵活界面，以便用户可以流畅地完善定义、问题和猜想。. We designed a flexible interface that supports multiple exploration paths, so users can fluidly refine definitions, questions, and conjectures.

生成原生数学工件：Produce native mathematical artifacts:

研究数学家已经有了标准化的工作方式。 人工智能联合数学家不会输出短暂的聊天日志或过早打磨的手稿，而是将其流程围绕着一份活生生的“工作论文”。 在传统的工作流程中，起草和评论可以帮助数学家建立项目的严格心理模型——准确地知道哪些部分是可靠的，哪些部分需要进一步关注。 由于自主推理系统可以在视野之外异步执行大部分探索性工作，因此用户面临失去这种有机上下文的风险。 为了帮助用户重建他们的心理模型，我们的系统跟踪研究的发展状态，并通过内嵌文本和页边注释将其可视化。 通过明确提供上下文（例如特定主张的出处或起草的引理的争议性），系统以完全数学界原生的格式呈现人工智能的底层过程。Research mathematicians already have standardized ways of working. Rather than outputting transient chat logs or prematurely polished manuscripts, the AI co-mathematician centers its process around a living “working paper.” In a traditional workflow, drafting and commenting help a mathematician build a rigorous mental model of the project—knowing exactly which parts are solid and which require further attention. Because autonomous reasoning systems can perform much of this exploratory work asynchronously out of view, the user risks losing this organic context. To help the user reconstruct their mental model, our system tracks the evolving state of the research and visualizes it through inline text and margin notes. By explicitly providing context—such as the provenance of specific claims or the contentiousness of a drafted lemma—the system surfaces the AI’s underlying process in a format completely native to the mathematical community.

实现异步交互和灵活转向：Enable asynchronous interaction and flexible steering:

数学研究很少是一个线性过程。为了反映这一点，该系统不是作为单个会话聊天机器人运行，而是作为一个异步团队运行。 在底层，消息传递系统允许多个专门代理并行工作。 由于这些代理异步运行，系统可以为问题分配大量计算资源，而不会阻塞用户，从而允许繁重的计算和持续的交互性共存。Mathematical research is rarely a linear process. To mirror this, the system operates not as a single conversational chatbot, but as an asynchronous team. Under the hood, a messaging system allows multiple specialized agents to work in parallel. Because these agents run asynchronously, the system can allocate substantial computational resources to a problem without blocking the user, allowing heavy compute and continuous interactivity to coexist.

用户可以随时与中央项目协调代理直接通信，以进行干预、绕过当前限制并指导正在进行的研究，而不是在系统“思考”时被锁定。 这种协作也是双向的：如果代理停滞，项目协调员代理会透明地标记障碍并请求人工协助以帮助解除系统阻塞。Rather than being locked out while the system “thinks,” the user can communicate directly with a central project coordinator agent at any time to intervene, bypass current constraints, and steer the ongoing research. This collaboration is also bidirectional: if the agents stall, the project coordinator agent transparently flags the roadblock and requests human assistance to help unblock the system.

通过渐进式披露来管理认知负荷：Manage cognitive load via progressive disclosure:

当用户和人工智能代理协作探索多个并行途径、从死胡同回溯并重用中间结果时，复杂的研究变得难以处理。 如果高级策略（例如，“尝试定点方法”）与多个后台代理的低级执行日志（例如，“我们必须验证第 40 行的可测量性”）混合，那么长的、非结构化的聊天很快就会变得不可用。 为了控制向数学家透露的信息量，我们的协作界面采用渐进式披露，通过镜像代理层次结构本身将高级意图与低级执行分开。 默认情况下，用户主要与项目协调代理进行交互，该代理会过滤掉专用代理沙箱的低级执行信息。 然而，系统始终为用户提供了根据需要深入了解任何并行代理活动的详细信息的能力。Complex research becomes unwieldy when the user and the AI agents collaboratively explore multiple parallel avenues, backtrack from dead ends, and reuse intermediate results. A long, unstructured chat quickly becomes unusable if high-level strategy (e.g., “Try a fixed-point approach”) is mixed with low-level execution logs of multiple background agents (e.g., “We must verify measurability on line 40”). To control how much information is revealed to the mathematician, our collaborative interface employs progressive disclosure, separating high-level intent from low-level execution by mirroring the agent hierarchy itself. By default, the user interacts primarily with the project coordinator agent, which filters out the low-level execution chatter of the specialized agents’ sandboxes. However, the system always offers the user the ability to drill down into the granular details of any parallel agent’s activity on demand.

跟踪、管理和传达不确定性：Track, manage, and communicate uncertainty:

7 挑战和限制Challenges and Limitations

虽然人工智能联合数学家展示了交互式人工智能工作流程的潜力，但我们在使这样的系统真正有用方面遇到了一些困难。While the AI co-mathematician demonstrates the potential of interactive AI workflows, we encountered several difficulties in making such a system genuinely useful.

取悦审稿人的偏见（错误共识）：Reviewer-Pleasing Bias (False Consensus):

迭代审查过程是一个动态系统，不断发展工作文件。 当代理产生无法真正修复的有缺陷的论点时，满足审阅者代理的严格约束有时会导致该系统收敛到仍然有缺陷的论点，但审阅者代理无法再检测到错误。 这些论点对于人类来说也很难区分，这已被强调为当前人工智能系统的局限性The iterative review process is a dynamical system, evolving the working documents. When an agent produces a flawed argument that it cannot genuinely fix, the strict constraint of satisfying the reviewer agents can sometimes cause this system to converge to an argument that remains flawed, but where the errors can no longer be detected by the reviewer agent. Such arguments can also be tricky for humans to tease apart, which has been highlighted as a limitation of current AI systems[undefaad]。 文献中已经指出了证明者-验证者动态中的类似病症. Similar pathologies in prover–verifier dynamics have been noted in the literature[undefaae]。 尽管这种行为相对罕见，但它违反了我们明确承认不确定性的核心原则。. Although this behavior is relatively rare, it represents a violation of our core principle of explicitly acknowledging uncertainty.

棘手的分歧（非终止）：Intractable Disagreements (Non-Termination):

相反，当迭代审查过程未能达成共识时，它可能无法完全终止。 在这些动态下，迭代审查过程陷入了无限的修改和拒绝循环中。 在连续的自主迭代中，这个循环常常会退化为越来越幻觉的推理——这种现象通俗地称为“死亡螺旋”。 尽管我们已经实施了各种机制来减轻这些影响，但语言模型经常相互不一致的核心问题仍然存在。 早期用户已经注意到这种行为，学会识别工作流何时进入这种状态，并适当降低对其输出的信任。Conversely, when the iterative review process fails to reach consensus, it can fail to terminate entirely. Under these dynamics, the iterative review process becomes locked in an endless cycle of revisions and rejections. Over successive autonomous iterations, this loop often degrades into increasingly hallucinated reasoning—a phenomenon colloquially known as a “death spiral.” While we have implemented various mechanisms to mitigate these effects, the core issue of language models frequently disagreeing with each other remains. Early users have noted this behavior, learning to recognize when a workstream has entered such a state and appropriately down-weighting their trust in its output.

系统自治需要放弃控制：System Autonomy Requires Ceding Control:

数学研究本质上是探索性的；预定义的任务计划通常是不可能的，因为发现正确的步骤顺序就等于解决问题本身。 因此，模型始终存在遇到计划外困难的重大风险，这种风险在人工智能联合数学家中被放大，我们允许系统自主工作多个小时，而不需要中间的人工输入。 从我们的经验中可以清楚地看出，当前模型对这种情况下该怎么做的判断远远落后于人类的能力和期望，在允许长期运行的自主能力的同时保留用户的可控性是一个具有挑战性的平衡行为。Mathematical research is inherently exploratory; predefined task planning is often impossible because discovering the correct sequence of steps is synonymous with solving the problem itself. Consequently, there is always a significant risk that the model encounters an unplanned-for difficulty, which is amplified in the AI co-mathematician where we allow the system to work autonomously for many hours without requiring intermediate human input. It is clear from our experiences that current models’ judgment on what to do in such cases is far behind human capabilities and expectations, and it is a challenging balancing act to allow long-running autonomous capabilities while retaining user controllability.

表示的语义含义：Semantic Meaning of Representations:

我们的团队很早就认识到，数学家倾向于将排版良好的文档与内容的相应严谨程度联系起来，这种直觉被法学硕士所掩盖，法学硕士擅长生成完美的 LaTeX 格式，但经常在严格的逻辑验证方面苦苦挣扎。 在人工智能联合数学家中，我们试图通过将输出突出显示为带有相关边注的“工作文档”来减少这种情况，但似乎可以开发新的界面来更直观地表示此类文档的特征。 人机交互领域最新的人工智能研究Our team recognized early on that mathematicians tend to associate a well-typeset document with a corresponding level of rigor in the content, an intuition that is belied by LLMs, which excel at generating flawless LaTeX formatting while frequently struggling with rigorous logical verification. In the AI co-mathematician, we attempt to reduce this by highlighting the output as a “working document” with associated marginalia, but it seems that new interfaces could be developed to more intuitively represent the characteristics of such documents. Recent AI-enabled research in HCI[undefaaf, e.g.,]可以为前进的道路提供灵感。could provide inspiration for the path forward.

此外，将高性能的代理系统引入数学生态系统会带来一些风险。 展望未来，社区必须应对几个系统性挑战：Further, the introduction of highly capable, agentic systems into the mathematical ecosystem carries some risk. Moving forward, the community must navigate several systemic challenges:

维持文献中的信噪比：Maintaining Signal-to-Noise in the Literature:

随着人工智能系统在排版 LaTeX 和综合文献方面变得非常熟练，社区将需要管理潜在的自动化输出涌入。 如果这些工具经常被用作自主生成器而不是人工引导的合作者，那么该领域将会看到看似合理但肤浅、渐进或存在细微缺陷的论文数量的增加。 这种转变可能会增加系统性的“语义噪音”，要求研究人员开发新的启发式方法，以便在更高的基线量的草稿中有效地识别真正新颖的数学发现。 形式化方法和自动形式化可能有助于检查论文的正确性和突出缺陷，但不会取代社区成员理解和吸收新发表作品的含义的过程。As AI systems become highly proficient at typesetting LaTeX and synthesizing literature, the community will need to manage a potential influx of automated output. If these tools are frequently used as autonomous generators rather than human-steered collaborators, the field would see a rise in plausible but shallow, incremental, or subtly flawed papers. This shift could increase systemic “semantic noise,” requiring researchers to develop new heuristics to efficiently identify genuinely novel mathematical discoveries amidst a higher baseline volume of drafts. Formal methods and auto-formalization could plausibly assist with checking correctness and highlighting flaws in papers, but will not substitute for the process of community members understanding and absorbing the implications of newly published works.

适应同行评审生态系统：Adapting the Peer Review Ecosystem:

数学同行评审过程依赖于深入、密集的人工验证。 Agentic AI 引入了一种具有挑战性的新扩展动态：系统可以在几分钟内生成 20 页的证明尝试，但人类专家可能需要几天的时间来验证它。 如果人工智能辅助起草变得无处不在，而没有内置的、可审计的书面记录，它可能会给志愿者同行评审系统带来沉重且不成比例的负担。 人工智能联合数学家的边际注释只是提高结果可审核性的一小步，但还需要更广泛的社区标准。 此外，尽管我们的系统具有专门的审稿代理，但用人工智能代替人类审稿人也存在风险。虽然自动审稿人擅长局部逻辑检查、发现代数错误或发现缺失的引文，但他们目前缺乏判断论文的优雅、深度或真正的数学意义所需的整体直觉。 过度依赖人工智能进行同行评审存在将数学评估简化为机械验证的风险，可能会导致指导该领域的人类定性判断被边缘化。The mathematical peer review process relies on deep, intensive human verification. Agentic AI introduces a challenging new scaling dynamic: a system can generate a 20-page proof attempt in minutes, but a human expert may take days to verify it. If AI-assisted drafting becomes ubiquitous without built-in, auditable paper trails, it could place a heavy and disproportionate burden on the volunteer peer review system. The AI co-mathematician’s margin annotations are a small initial step toward increasing the auditability of results, but broader community standards will be needed. Furthermore, although our system features specialized reviewer agents, substituting AI for human referees presents its own risks. While automated reviewers excel at local logical checks, catching algebraic slips, or surfacing missing citations, they currently lack the holistic intuition required to judge a paper’s elegance, depth, or true mathematical significance. Over-relying on AI for peer review risks reducing mathematical evaluation to mechanical verification, potentially sidelining the qualitative human judgment that guides the field.

8 结论Conclusion

人工智能社区最近跨越了多个技术里程碑，在各种数学基准上展示了人类水平和超人类的表现。 然而，如果我们想真正加速科学发现，解决静态的、明确定义的问题只是解决方案的一部分。 数学研究的前沿不是线性对话或一系列互不相关的谜题；这是一个混乱、重叠、高度迭代的过程，由未经证实的直觉、分支假设和复杂的人类协作定义。The AI community has recently passed multiple technical milestones, demonstrating human-level and superhuman performance across a variety of mathematical benchmarks. If we want to genuinely accelerate scientific discovery, however, solving static, well-defined problems is only part of the solution. The frontier of mathematical research is not a linear conversation or a series of disconnected puzzles; it is a messy, overlapping, highly iterative process defined by unproven intuition, branching hypotheses, and complex human collaboration.

人工智能联合数学家旨在满足研究人员的需求。 该系统不是将人工智能简化为孤立的预言机或简单的验证管道，而是充当整体工作空间。 通过管理不确定性的生命周期，通过分层委托反映人类工作流程，并将其输出基于本地数学工件，它将强大的基础模型提升为自然的协作者。 通过硬性的程序约束和对动态“工作论文”的持续维护，该系统确保完整的研究过程——失败的测试、综合的文献以及想法的不断完善——被捕获、审计并明确地传达给用户。 正如早期用户解决开放问题和寻找新颖证据所证明的那样，这种双向交换使人类能够有效地引导人工智能克服困难的瓶颈。The AI co-mathematician is designed to meet researchers where they are. Rather than reducing AI to an isolated oracle or a simple verification pipeline, the system acts as a holistic workspace. By managing the life cycle of uncertainty, mirroring human workflows through hierarchical delegation, and grounding its output in native mathematical artifacts, it elevates powerful base models into natural collaborators. Through hard programmatic constraints and the continuous maintenance of a living “working paper,” the system ensures that the full research journey—the failed tests, the literature synthesized, and the continuous refinement of an idea—is captured, audited, and explicitly communicated to the user. As demonstrated by early users resolving open problems and finding novel proofs, this bidirectional exchange allows humans to effectively steer AI past difficult bottlenecks.

然而，要充分发挥这种范式的潜力，需要改变人工智能社区衡量成功的方式。 虽然这些基准测试非常适合评估模型为特定问题生成最终答案的能力，但它们并没有涵盖前沿研究的全部范围。 它们并不是为了衡量系统交互修剪假设树、综合利基文献或在扩展失败时适当停止和披露不确定性的能力而设计的。Realizing the full potential of this paradigm, however, requires a shift in how the AI community measures success. While these benchmarks are excellent at evaluating a model’s ability to generate a final answer to a curated problem, they do not capture the full scope of frontier research. They are not designed to measure a system’s ability to interactively prune a hypothesis tree, synthesize niche literature, or appropriately halt and disclose uncertainty when scaling fails.

如果我们想构建真正的联合数学家的系统，我们必须开发互补的评估框架来衡量协作效率、状态探索和不确定性的严格管理。 人工智能辅助数学的下一次革命不仅取决于哪种模型可以最快地合成正确答案，还取决于哪种系统可以最有效地帮助人类研究人员探索未知世界。If we want to build systems that act as true co-mathematicians, we must develop complementary evaluation frameworks that measure collaborative efficacy, stateful exploration, and the rigorous management of uncertainty. The next revolution in AI-assisted mathematics will not just be defined by which model can synthesize the right answer the fastest, but by which system can most effectively help human researchers navigate the unknown.
