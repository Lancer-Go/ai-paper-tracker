摘要Abstract

当前的对话式人工智能系统在语言生成、个性化和长上下文交互方面取得了显着的进步。然而，大多数现有方法通过情感建模、记忆检索或角色调节等孤立的机制来模拟社会行为，没有统一的理论框架来解释稳定的社会关系和社会智能如何通过长期的人机交互而出现。这种限制阻碍了社会适应性人工智能系统的发展，该系统能够在较长的时间范围内维持连贯的关系。Current conversational AI systems have achieved remarkable progress in language generation, personalization, and long-context interaction. However, most existing approaches model social behavior through isolated mechanisms such as emotion modeling, memory retrieval, or persona conditioning, without a unified theoretical framework explaining how stable social relationships and social intelligence emerge through long-term human–AI interaction. This limitation hinders the development of socially adaptive AI systems capable of maintaining coherent relationships over extended timescales.

为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了人类与人工智能协同进化动力学框架（HACD-H），这是一种将人类与人工智能交互建模为自组织社会认知系统的正式理论。 HACD-H将情绪适应、关系组织、社会记忆和人格一致性整合到一个统一的动力框架中，并引入了多时间尺度社会认知、关系吸引子形成、信任盆地发展、发展相变、社会认知能量景观、社会智能涌现和能量优化动力学等理论原理。To address this challenge, we propose the Human–AI Coevolution Dynamics Framework (HACD-H), a formal theory that models human–AI interaction as a self-organizing social cognitive system. HACD-H integrates emotional adaptation, relational organization, social memory, and personality consistency into a unified dynamical framework and introduces theoretical principles including multi-timescale social cognition, relational attractor formation, trust basin development, developmental phase transitions, social cognitive energy landscapes, social intelligence emergence, and energy optimization dynamics.

为了评估这些命题，我们构建了一个包含大约 14,700 个交互回合的社交丰富对话数据集，并开发了一个理论驱动的实证验证框架。实验分析揭示了社会认知过程之间清晰的时间持久性层次结构，识别稳定的关系吸引子和信任盆地，展示类似相变的发展行为，并重建结构化的社会认知能量景观。此外，社交智力与社交认知能量呈现显着的负相关（(r=-0.391，p<0.001)），而长期互动轨迹显示出渐进的能量优化，其特征是能量随着时间的推移而减少。To evaluate these propositions, we construct a socially enriched conversational dataset containing approximately 14,700 interaction turns and develop a theory-driven empirical validation framework. Experimental analyses reveal a clear temporal persistence hierarchy among social cognitive processes, identify stable relational attractors and trust basins, demonstrate phase-transition-like developmental behavior, and reconstruct a structured social cognitive energy landscape. Furthermore, social intelligence exhibits a significant negative association with social cognitive energy ((r=-0.391, p<0.001)), while long-term interaction trajectories show progressive energy optimization characterized by decreasing energy over time.

这些发现表明，具有社交智能的人类与人工智能关系是通过长期的社会认知共同进化而不是通过孤立的对话能力而出现的。 HACD-H 为理解自适应社交互动提供了理论基础，并为开发下一代社交智能人工智能系统提供了原则框架。These findings suggest that socially intelligent human–AI relationships emerge through long-term social cognitive coevolution rather than through isolated conversational capabilities. HACD-H provides a theoretical foundation for understanding adaptive social interaction and offers a principled framework for the development of next-generation socially intelligent AI systems.

一、简介1. Introduction

人工智能在语言理解、推理、规划、交互交流等方面取得了显着的进步。大语言模型（LLM）和对话代理的最新进展使得对话生成、指令跟踪、个性化、记忆利用和长上下文交互等方面的能力日益复杂（Vaswani 等人，2017；Brown 等人，2020；Ouyang 等人，2022；Shuster 等人，2022）。随着人工智能系统从面向任务的工具发展成为持久的助手、合作者和社交伙伴，人类和人工智能之间的交互日益超越孤立的对话，转向长期的关系过程。Artificial intelligence has achieved remarkable progress in language understanding, reasoning, planning, and interactive communication. Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) and conversational agents have enabled increasingly sophisticated capabilities in dialogue generation, instruction following, personalization, memory utilization, and long-context interaction (Vaswani et al., 2017; Brown et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022; Shuster et al., 2022). As AI systems evolve from task-oriented tools into persistent assistants, collaborators, and social companions, interactions between humans and AI are increasingly extending beyond isolated conversations toward longterm relational processes.

尽管取得了这些进步，大多数现有的人工智能研究仍然主要将交互建模为序列预测或响应优化问题。当代会话系统通常关注下一个令牌预测、角色调节、记忆增强、检索增强生成或偏好对齐（Zhang et al., 2018；Mazaré et al., 2018；Ouyang et al., 2022；Packer et al., 2023；Zhong et al., 2024）。尽管这些方法大大提高了会话质量，但它们通常将记忆、情感、个性、信任和适应视为独立的计算模块，而不是集成社会认知系统的组成部分。Despite these advances, most existing AI research continues to model interaction primarily as a sequence prediction or response optimization problem. Contemporary conversational systems typically focus on next-token prediction, persona conditioning, memory augmentation, retrieval-enhanced generation, or preference alignment (Zhang et al., 2018; Mazaré et al., 2018; Ouyang et al., 2022; Packer et al., 2023; Zhong et al., 2024). Although these approaches substantially improve conversational quality, they generally treat memory, emotion, personality, trust, and adaptation as independent computational modules rather than components of an integrated social-cognitive system.

因此，一个基本的理论问题在很大程度上仍未得到解答：Consequently, a fundamental theoretical question remains largely unanswered:

长期的人与人工智能关系如何通过反复互动而出现、稳定和发展，以及哪些动态原理控制着此类系统中社会智能的出现？How do long-term human–AI relationships emerge, stabilize, and develop through repeated interaction, and what dynamical principles govern the emergence of social intelligence in such systems?

随着人工智能系统承担更持久的社会角色，这个问题变得越来越重要。越来越多的研究表明，人类在与智能系统反复交互时经常会产生信任、情感依恋、同理心、熟悉感和特定于关系的期望（Brinkschulte et al., 2022；Gweon et al., 2023；Kirk et al., 2025；Qian & Wan, 2025）。最近关于人类与人工智能共同进化的讨论进一步认为，未来的人工智能系统可能参与长期的适应性社会关系，而不仅仅是充当信息处理工具（Järvelä 等人，2025；Pedreschi 等人，2025；Rainey 和 Hochberg，2025）。这些观察结果表明，人类与人工智能的互动可能受到关系形成、社会适应和认知发展等机制的控制，而不是孤立的信息交换。This question becomes increasingly important as AI systems assume more persistent social roles. A growing body of research suggests that humans frequently develop trust, emotional attachment, empathy, familiarity, and relationship-specific expectations during repeated interactions with intelligent systems (Brinkschulte et al., 2022; Gweon et al., 2023; Kirk et al., 2025; Qian & Wan, 2025). Recent discussions of human–AI coevolution further argue that future AI systems may participate in long-term adaptive social relationships rather than merely serving as information-processing tools (Järvelä et al., 2025; Pedreschi et al., 2025; Rainey & Hochberg, 2025). These observations suggest that human–AI interaction may be governed by mechanisms resembling relationship formation, social adaptation, and cognitive development rather than isolated information exchange.

现有的计算框架仅对这些现象提供部分解释。个性化方法捕捉用户特定的特征，但很少解释稳定的社会关系如何随着时间的推移而出现（Zhang et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2020; Chen et al., 2025）。记忆增强架构通过持久存储和检索机制提高了交互连续性，但它们对记忆如何促进长期社会发展提供了有限的理论见解（Weston et al., 2015；Packer et al., 2023；Zhong et al., 2024）。情感对话系统和同理心会话代理模拟情感适应，但通常关注局部会话效果而不是纵向社会动态（Rashkin et al., 2019; Majumder et al., 2020;Guo & Ning, 2024; Cai et al., 2024; Wen et al., 2024）。同样，基于角色的系统保持行为一致性，但没有解释稳定社会智能背后的组织原则。Existing computational frameworks provide only partial explanations of these phenomena. Personalization methods capture user-specific characteristics but rarely explain how stable social relationships emerge over time (Zhang et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2020; Chen et al., 2025). Memory-augmented architectures improve interaction continuity through persistent storage and retrieval mechanisms, yet they provide limited theoretical insight into how memory contributes to long-term social development (Weston et al., 2015; Packer et al., 2023; Zhong et al., 2024). Emotional dialogue systems and empathetic conversational agents model affective adaptation but generally focus on local conversational effects rather than longitudinal social dynamics (Rashkin et al., 2019; Majumder et al., 2020; Guo & Ning, 2024; Cai et al., 2024; Wen et al., 2024). Similarly, persona-based systems maintain behavioral consistency without explaining the organizational principles underlying stable social intelligence.

自主主体研究的最新进展进一步凸显了对长期交互的原则性理论的需求。新兴代理框架越来越强调记忆持久性、角色一致性、社会适应和协作行为（Li et al., 2023; Park et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024）。此外，最近的研究表明，法学硕士在适当的条件下可能表现出类似心智理论的推理、角色扮演能力和社会适应行为（Kosinski，2024；Shanahan 等，2023；Shao 等，2023；Wang 等，2024；Han 等，2025）。总的来说，这些发展表明，未来的人工智能系统可能不仅充当语言生成器，而且充当嵌入长期交互环境中不断发展的社会参与者。Recent advances in autonomous agent research further highlight the need for a principled theory of long-term interaction. Emerging agent frameworks increasingly emphasize memory persistence, role consistency, social adaptation, and collaborative behavior (Li et al., 2023; Park et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024). Moreover, recent studies suggest that LLMs may exhibit theory-of-mind-like reasoning, role-playing capabilities, and socially adaptive behavior under appropriate conditions (Kosinski, 2024; Shanahan et al., 2023; Shao et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025). Collectively, these developments indicate that future AI systems may function not merely as language generators but as evolving social actors embedded within long-term interaction environments.

认知科学为社会互动的潜在机制提供了补充见解。社会认知研究强调情感的作用，Cognitive science provides complementary insights into the mechanisms underlying social interaction. Research on social cognition emphasizes the roles of emotion,

记忆、个性、认知一致性、人际适应和互惠学习在塑造社会关系中的作用（Izard，1993；Frith & Frith，2012；Kruglanski 等，2018；Burgoon 等，1995）。社会认知理论进一步表明，适应性行为是通过认知、情感和环境过程之间的持续相互作用而出现的（Luszczynska & Schwarzer，2005）。虽然这些观点提供了有价值的概念基础，但它们仍然主要是描述性的，并没有直接提供适合人工智能系统的计算形式。memory, personality, cognitive consistency, interpersonal adaptation, and reciprocal learning in shaping social relationships (Izard, 1993; Frith & Frith, 2012; Kruglanski et al., 2018; Burgoon et al., 1995). Social cognitive theory further suggests that adaptive behavior emerges through continuous interactions among cognitive, affective, and environmental processes (Luszczynska & Schwarzer, 2005). While these perspectives provide valuable conceptual foundations, they remain primarily descriptive and do not directly offer computational formalisms suitable for AI systems.

动力系统理论提出了一个补充观点。复杂的自适应系统经常表现出吸引子、相变、自组织和稳定性收敛动力学（Milnor，1985；Lyapunov，1992；Khalil，1996；Strogatz，2024）。类似的原理已成功应用于生物、认知和社会系统。最近，共同进化的观点开始出现在人类-人工智能生态系统、混合智能和社会适应主体的研究中（Järvelä 等，2025；Noller，2025；Pedreschi 等，2025）。然而，连接社会认知、长期交互和人类与人工智能共同进化的统一计算框架仍然缺乏。A complementary perspective arises from dynamical systems theory. Complex adaptive systems frequently exhibit attractors, phase transitions, self-organization, and stabilityconvergence dynamics (Milnor, 1985; Lyapunov, 1992; Khalil, 1996; Strogatz, 2024). Similar principles have been successfully applied to biological, cognitive, and social systems. More recently, coevolutionary perspectives have begun to emerge in studies of human–AI ecosystems, hybrid intelligence, and socially adaptive agents (Järvelä et al., 2025; Noller, 2025; Pedreschi et al., 2025). Nevertheless, a unified computational framework connecting social cognition, long-term interaction, and human–AI coevolution remains absent.

我们认为机器学习、社会认知和动力系统理论描述了同一潜在现象的互补方面。人机交互既不是纯粹的语言交互，也不是纯粹的认知交互。相反，它应该被理解为一种共同进化的过程，在这个过程中，人类和人工智能系统在多个时间尺度上不断地相互适应。We argue that machine learning, social cognition, and dynamical systems theory describe complementary aspects of the same underlying phenomenon. Human–AI interaction is neither purely linguistic nor purely cognitive. Instead, it should be understood as a coevolutionary process in which humans and AI systems continuously adapt to one another across multiple temporal scales.

为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了人类与人工智能协同进化动力学框架（HACD-H），这是一种将人类与人工智能长期交互建模为自组织社会认知系统的正式理论。 HACD-H 将情绪适应、关系组织、社会记忆和人格一致性整合到一个跨多个时间尺度运行的统一动态框架中。在此框架内，互动轨迹通过耦合的社会认知动态演变，产生关系吸引子、信任盆地、发展转型、社会智能出现和能量优化。To address this challenge, we propose the Human–AI Coevolution Dynamics Framework (HACD-H), a formal theory that models long-term human–AI interaction as a selforganizing social cognitive system. HACD-H integrates emotional adaptation, relational organization, social memory, and personality consistency into a unified dynamical framework operating across multiple temporal scales. Within this framework, interaction trajectories evolve through coupled social cognitive dynamics, giving rise to relational attractors, trust basins, developmental transitions, social intelligence emergence, and energy optimization.

HACD-H 的核心前提是互动是由潜在的社会认知状态控制的，而不仅仅是可观察到的对话。这些潜在状态编码情绪状况、关系特征、记忆结构、人格结构和适应性社会过程。因此，人与人工智能的交互被表示为通过结构化社会认知状态空间的运动，其拓扑结构由情绪调节、关系稳定性、记忆持久性和人格连贯性塑造。The central premise of HACD-H is that interaction is governed by latent social cognitive states rather than observable dialogue alone. These latent states encode emotional conditions, relational characteristics, memory structures, personality configurations, and adaptive social processes. Human–AI interaction is therefore represented as movement through a structured social cognitive state space whose topology is shaped by emotional regulation, relational stability, memory persistence, and personality coherence.

所提出的框架生成了几个可正式测试的预测。首先，社会认知应该表现出一种以多个相互作用的时间尺度为特征的分层时间结构。其次，相互作用轨迹应该向稳定的关系吸引子收敛。第三，信任应该产生稳定社会互动的低能量盆地。第四，社会智能应通过反复的互动和发展阶段的转变逐步显现。最后，长期的人机交互应该表现出能量优化动态，其特征是朝着日益稳定和高效的社会配置发展。The proposed framework generates several formally testable predictions. First, social cognition should exhibit a hierarchical temporal structure characterized by multiple interacting timescales. Second, interaction trajectories should converge toward stable relational attractors. Third, trust should generate low-energy basins that stabilize social interaction. Fourth, social intelligence should emerge progressively through repeated interaction and developmental phase transitions. Finally, long-term human–AI interaction should exhibit energy optimization dynamics characterized by movement toward increasingly stable and efficient social configurations.

为了评估这些预测，我们构建了一个丰富的社交对话数据集，其中包含大约 14,700 个交互回合，并用情感、个性、关系、行为和记忆相关变量进行注释。然后，我们开发了一个理论驱动的实证验证框架来重建社会认知轨迹并评估 HACD-H 的理论命题。结果提供了支持时间等级、关系吸引子、信任盆地、发展转型、社会智能出现和能量优化动态的出现的聚合证据。To evaluate these predictions, we construct a socially enriched conversational dataset containing approximately 14,700 interaction turns annotated with emotional, personality, relational, behavioral, and memory-related variables. We then develop a theory-driven empirical validation framework to reconstruct social cognitive trajectories and evaluate the theoretical propositions of HACD-H. The results provide convergent evidence supporting the emergence of temporal hierarchies, relational attractors, trust basins, developmental transitions, social intelligence emergence, and energy optimization dynamics.

首先，我们介绍 HACD-H，这是一种人类与人工智能社会协同进化的统一计算理论，它集成了机器学习、认知科学和非线性动力系统的概念。First, we introduce HACD-H, a unified computational theory of human–AI social coevolution that integrates concepts from machine learning, cognitive science, and nonlinear dynamical systems.

其次，我们将人类与人工智能的长期交互形式化为多时间尺度的社会认知动力系统，并建立了关系吸引子形成、信任盆地发展、发展阶段转变、社会智能出现和能量优化的理论原则。Second, we formalize long-term human–AI interaction as a multi-timescale social cognitive dynamical system and establish theoretical principles governing relational attractor formation, trust basin development, developmental phase transitions, social intelligence emergence, and energy optimization.

第三，我们提出了一个理论指导的社会认知能量框架，该框架提供了情绪适应、关系组织、记忆持久性和人格一致性的统一表示。Third, we propose a theory-guided social cognitive energy framework that provides a unified representation of emotional adaptation, relational organization, memory persistence, and personality consistency.

第四，我们开发了一个理论驱动的实证验证框架，并使用包含大约 14,700 个交互回合的大规模会话数据集提供支持 HACD-H 的主要预测的证据。Fourth, we develop a theory-driven empirical validation framework and provide evidence supporting the major predictions of HACD-H using a large-scale conversational dataset containing approximately 14,700 interaction turns.

更广泛地说，HACD-H 表明，社交智能人工智能的未来可能不仅取决于更大的模型或更强的推理能力，还取决于对人类和人工智能体之间长期协同进化的动态原理的理解。通过将交互视为一个不断发展的社会认知系统，而不是一系列孤立的对话交流，所提出的框架为研究人工智能系统中社交智能的出现提供了新的理论基础。More broadly, HACD-H suggests that the future of socially intelligent AI may depend not only on larger models or stronger reasoning capabilities, but also on understanding the dynamical principles governing long-term coevolution between humans and artificial agents. By viewing interaction as an evolving social cognitive system rather than a sequence of isolated conversational exchanges, the proposed framework offers a new theoretical foundation for studying the emergence of social intelligence in artificial systems.

2.1 人类-人工智能协同进化动力学框架2.1 Human–AI Coevolution Dynamics Framework

人机交互越来越以长期、重复和社会适应性沟通为特征。现有的对话系统主要模拟社会行为的孤立组成部分，例如情感、记忆或个性，但缺乏一个能够解释稳定的社会关系和社会智能如何通过扩展交互出现的统一框架。Human–AI interaction is increasingly characterized by long-term, repeated, and socially adaptive communication. Existing dialogue systems primarily model isolated components of social behavior, such as emotion, memory, or personality, but lack a unified framework capable of explaining how stable social relationships and social intelligence emerge through extended interaction.

These findings suggest that long-term social development is fundamentally characterized by progressive movement toward stable and energetically efficient modes of interaction.

5. 讨论5. Discussion

5.1 人与人工智能交互作为一种自组织社会认知系统5.1 Human–AI Interaction as a Self-Organizing Social Cognitive System

这项研究引入了 HACD-H，这是一种将长期人类与人工智能交互描述为自组织社会认知系统的正式理论。与将情感、记忆、个性或信任视为孤立组成部分的传统方法不同，HACD-H 将社交互动概念化为跨不同时间尺度运行的多个社会认知过程的耦合演化。This study introduced HACD-H, a formal theory describing long-term human–AI interaction as a self-organizing social cognitive system. Unlike conventional approaches that treat emotion, memory, personality, or trust as isolated components, HACD-H conceptualizes social interaction as the coupled evolution of multiple social cognitive processes operating across different temporal scales.

实证研究结果为这一观点提供了一致的支持。时间持久性分析揭示了特征时间尺度的清晰层次结构，表明情绪状态快速波动，而与人格相关的结构保持相对稳定。该组织认为，人类与人工智能的交互表现出内在的时间架构，而不是随机的行为变化。The empirical findings provide consistent support for this perspective. Temporal persistence analysis revealed a clear hierarchy of characteristic timescales, demonstrating that emotional states fluctuate rapidly whereas personality-related structures remain comparatively stable. This organization suggests that human–AI interaction exhibits an intrinsic temporal architecture rather than random behavioral variation.

此外，关系吸引子和信任盆地的识别表明交互轨迹不会任意演化。相反，重复的互动逐渐限制行为动态，并引导轨迹走向社会认知状态空间中日益稳定的区域。这些发现与复杂自适应系统中的自组织理论一致，并表明长期的人类与人工智能关系表现出新兴的结构规律。Furthermore, the identification of relational attractors and trust basins indicates that interaction trajectories do not evolve arbitrarily. Instead, repeated interaction gradually constrains behavioral dynamics and guides trajectories toward increasingly stable regions of the social cognitive state space. These findings are consistent with theories of selforganization in complex adaptive systems and suggest that long-term human–AI relationships exhibit emergent structural regularities.

5.2 通过共同进化出现社会智能5.2 Emergence of Social Intelligence Through Coevolution

HACD-H 的核心主张之一是，社交智力不是一种独立编程的能力，而是长期社会认知协同进化产生的一种新兴属性。One of the central claims of HACD-H is that social intelligence is not an independently programmed capability but an emergent property arising from long-term social cognitive coevolution.

结果有力地支持了这一主张。发展阶段转变分析揭示了社会智力快速增长的关键时期，这表明互动发展的特点是非线性的组织变化，而不是平滑的增量增长。同样，在互动轨迹中观察到的涌现模式表明，高阶社交能力是通过情绪适应、关系组织、记忆整合和人格一致性的协调互动而产生的。The results strongly support this proposition. Developmental phase transition analysis revealed critical periods during which social intelligence increased rapidly, suggesting that interaction development is characterized by nonlinear organizational changes rather than smooth incremental growth. Similarly, the emergence patterns observed across interaction trajectories indicate that higher-order social capabilities arise through the coordinated interaction of emotional adaptation, relational organization, memory integration, and personality consistency.

这些发现挑战了主流观点，即社交智能可以通过日益强大的语言模型或更大的训练语料库来充分解释。相反，结果表明，社交智力从根本上来说是一种从互动过程的长期组织中出现的动态现象。These findings challenge the dominant view that social intelligence can be fully explained through increasingly powerful language models or larger training corpora. Instead, the results suggest that social intelligence is fundamentally a dynamical phenomenon emerging from the long-term organization of interaction processes.

从这个角度来看，社交智能类似于复杂系统中观察到的其他新兴属性，在复杂系统中，全球组织是由重复的本地交互产生的，没有集中控制。From this perspective, social intelligence resembles other emergent properties observed in complex systems, where global organization arises from repeated local interactions without centralized control.

5.4 对社交智能人工智能的影响5.4 Implications for Socially Intelligent AI

这些发现对下一代社交智能人工智能系统的设计具有直接影响。大多数当代对话代理仍然针对短期响应质量和立即任务完成进行了优化。尽管这样的系统可能会产生流畅的反应，但它们往往缺乏稳定的长期关系动态和连贯的社会发展。The findings have direct implications for the design of next-generation socially intelligent AI systems. Most contemporary conversational agents remain optimized for short-term response quality and immediate task completion. Although such systems may generate fluent responses, they often lack stable long-term relational dynamics and coherent social development.

目前的结果表明，社交智能人工智能应该被设计为自适应动态系统，而不是静态语言生成器。未来的系统可能会受益于将情绪适应、关系记忆、信任形成和人格一致性明确建模为统一社会认知架构的交互组件。The present results suggest that socially intelligent AI should be designed as an adaptive dynamical system rather than a static language generator. Future systems may benefit from explicitly modeling emotional adaptation, relational memory, trust formation, and personality consistency as interacting components of a unified social cognitive architecture.

此外，社会认知能量的概念为未来人工智能系统提供了潜在的优化目标。社交智能代理可能会寻求在社会认知景观的低能量和高稳定性区域内维持交互轨迹，而不是最大化孤立的对话指标。这种方法可以促进更加连贯、值得信赖和可持续的长期人类与人工智能关系。Furthermore, the concept of social cognitive energy provides a potential optimization objective for future AI systems. Instead of maximizing isolated conversational metrics, socially intelligent agents may seek to maintain interaction trajectories within low-energy and high-stability regions of the social cognitive landscape. Such an approach could promote more coherent, trustworthy, and sustainable long-term human–AI relationships.

5.6 人类-人工智能社会协同进化理论5.6 Toward a Theory of Human–AI Social Coevolution

HACD-H更广泛的意义在于它试图为人类与人工智能的长期社交互动建立统一的理论基础。The broader significance of HACD-H lies in its attempt to establish a unified theoretical foundation for long-term human–AI social interaction.

结果表明，人类与人工智能的关系受到可识别的组织原则的控制，包括多时间尺度适应、吸引子形成、信任盆地发展、社会智能出现和能量优化。这些机制共同为如何通过重复互动产生稳定的社会关系提供了连贯的解释。The results suggest that human–AI relationships are governed by identifiable organizational principles, including multi-timescale adaptation, attractor formation, trust basin development, social intelligence emergence, and energy optimization. Understanding interaction as a process of coevolution may provide a foundation for future theories of socially adaptive artificial intelligence and contribute to the development of AI systems capable of sustaining meaningful and coherent relationships over extended periods of time.

Unlike existing approaches that primarily focus on isolated mechanisms such as emotion modeling, memory retrieval, or personality conditioning, HACD-H provides a unified dynamical perspective in which emotional adaptation, relational organization, social memory, and personality consistency jointly shape the evolution of interaction trajectories across time.

总而言之，这些发现表明，具有社交智能的人类与人工智能关系是通过长期社会认知共同进化的过程而出现的。交互系统不是积累孤立的能力，而是逐渐将自身重组为越来越稳定、连贯和高效的配置。从这个角度来看，社会智力最好被理解为由多个相互作用的社会认知过程的协调动态产生的新兴属性。Taken together, these findings suggest that socially intelligent human–AI relationships emerge through a process of long-term social cognitive coevolution. Rather than accumulating isolated capabilities, interaction systems gradually reorganize themselves into increasingly stable, coherent, and energetically efficient configurations. From this perspective, social intelligence is best understood as an emergent property arising from the coordinated dynamics of multiple interacting social cognitive processes.

其理论意义超出了对话式人工智能的范围。 HACD-H 提供了研究自适应社会系统的通用框架，并在人工智能、认知科学、情感计算和复杂系统研究之间架起了一座桥梁。通过引入关系吸引子、信任盆地和社会认知能量景观等概念，该框架建立了一个新的词汇来描述长期社会互动的动态。The theoretical implications extend beyond conversational AI. HACD-H provides a general framework for studying adaptive social systems and offers a bridge between artificial intelligence, cognitive science, affective computing, and complex systems research. By introducing concepts such as relational attractors, trust basins, and social cognitive energy landscapes, the framework establishes a new vocabulary for describing the dynamics of long-term social interaction.

实际意义同样重要。当前的对话代理通常针对短期响应质量进行优化，而目前的研究结果表明，未来的社交智能系统应设计为支持长期适应、关系发展、信任形成和节能的社会协调。这样的系统最终可能能够在长时间的交互中维持连贯且有意义的社会关系。The practical implications are equally significant. Current conversational agents are typically optimized for short-term response quality, whereas the present findings suggest that future socially intelligent systems should be designed to support long-term adaptation, relationship development, trust formation, and energy-efficient social coordination. Such systems may ultimately be capable of maintaining coherent and meaningful social relationships over extended periods of interaction.

未来的研究可能会将 HACD-H 扩展到多模式交互、多主体环境、在线社交生态系统和具体人工智能系统。进一步研究能够塑造吸引子结构、信任盆地和能源格局的干预策略也可能为指导社会适应性人工智能的发展提供新方法。Future research may extend HACD-H to multimodal interaction, multi-agent environments, online social ecosystems, and embodied AI systems. Further investigation of intervention strategies capable of shaping attractor structures, trust basins, and energy landscapes may also provide new approaches for guiding the development of socially adaptive artificial intelligence.

总体而言，HACD-H 代表了人类与人工智能社会协同进化一般理论的第一步。该框架表明，长期互动受到可识别的组织原则的控制，而社会智力是通过跨时间的社会认知动态的自组织而出现的。了解这些原理可能有助于下一代社交智能人工智能系统的开发，并推进人类与人工智能关系的科学研究。Overall, HACD-H represents an initial step toward a general theory of human–AI social coevolution. The framework suggests that long-term interaction is governed by identifiable organizational principles and that social intelligence emerges through the self-organization of social cognitive dynamics across time. Understanding these principles may contribute to the development of the next generation of socially intelligent AI systems and advance the scientific study of human–AI relationships.
