QAROO：人工智能驱动的在线任务卸载，实现节能和可持续的 MEC 网络QAROO: AI-Driven Online Task Offloading for Energy-Efficient and Sustainable MEC Networks

Yongtao Yao, Yao Yang, Haorui Shi, Canglu Zhu, Miaojiang Chen, Ahmed Farouk Corresponding author: Miaojiang Chen (email: mjchen_cs@gxu.edu.cn).Manuscript received Date Month Year; revised Date Month Year.
摘要Abstract

随着人工智能（AI）和智能科学的快速发展，智能边缘计算已被广泛采用。然而传统方法的适应性差、启发式算法收敛速度慢等局限性日益明显。为了实现可持续且资源高效的边缘应用，本文提出了一种用于无线供电移动边缘计算（MEC）网络的在线任务卸载框架，称为基于量子注意力的在线卸载强化学习（QAROO）。该系统采用二进制卸载策略，旨在在动态信道环境中共同优化计算和能源资源。针对传统方法适应性差和启发式算法收敛速度慢的问题，该框架集成了量子神经网络和注意力机制，引入了三个关键改进：使用循环神经网络增强时间建模能力，提出不确定性引导量化方法以提高探索效率，以及将注意力机制纳入量子网络以加强特征表示。实验表明，该方法在归一化计算速度和处理时间方面优于对比方案，为大规模物联网（IoT）动态环境中的在线任务卸载提供了高效稳定的解决方案。With the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) and intelligent science, intelligent edge computing has been widely adopted. However, the limitations of traditional methods, such as poor adaptability and the slow convergence of heuristic algorithms, are becoming increasingly evident. To enable sustainable and resource-efficient edge applications, this paper proposes an online task offloading framework for wireless powered mobile edge computing (MEC) networks, called Quantum Attention-based Reinforcement learning for Online Offloading (QAROO). The system employs a binary offloading strategy with the aim of co-optimizing computing and energy resources in dynamic channel environments. In response to the issues of poor adaptability in traditional approaches and the slow convergence of heuristic algorithms, the framework integrates quantum neural networks and attention mechanisms, introducing three key improvements: using recurrent neural networks to enhance temporal modeling capability, proposing an uncertainty-guided quantization method to improve exploration efficiency, and incorporating attention mechanisms into quantum networks to strengthen feature representation. Experiments demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms comparative schemes in terms of normalized computation speed and processing time, offering an efficient and stable solution for online task offloading in large-scale Internet of Things (IoT) dynamic environments.

I 简介Introduction

尺寸受限的设备面临电池寿命和计算能力的瓶颈。无线功率传输 (WPT) 可通过空中持续射频 (RF) 能量充电，而 MEC 技术允许设备将计算密集型任务卸载到附近的边缘服务器 (ES)，从而显着减少计算延迟和本地能源消耗Size-constrained devices face bottlenecks in battery life and computational capability. Wireless power transfer (WPT) enables sustained radio frequency (RF) energy charging over the air, while MEC technology allows devices to offload computation-intensive tasks to nearby edge servers (ES), significantly reducing computational latency and local energy consumption[24]。这两种技术的集成被称为无线移动边缘计算，可以解决这些瓶颈。在此类系统中，接入点 (AP) 同时向设备传输射频能量以维持其运行，并接收其卸载的计算任务以进行远程处理。设备通常采用二进制卸载策略. The integration of these two technologies, known as wireless-powered mobile edge computing, can address these bottlenecks. In such systems, an access point (AP) simultaneously transmits RF energy to devices to sustain their operation and receives their offloaded computational tasks for remote processing. Devices typically employ a binary offloading policy[19]，这意味着任务要么在本地计算，要么完全卸载到 MEC 服务器。, meaning a task is either computed locally or fully offloaded to the MEC server.

在多用户MEC网络中，由于二进制卸载变量的存在，机会计算卸载优化需要制定混合整数规划（MIP）模型来共同确定二进制卸载决策以及无线能量传输和任务卸载时间的资源分配。然而，解决此类问题极其复杂，尤其是在大规模网络中。许多研究都集中在设计低复杂度次优算法，例如基于局部搜索的启发式算法In multi-user MEC networks, due to the presence of binary offloading variables, opportunistic computation offloading optimization requires the formulation of a mixed-integer programming (MIP) model to jointly determine the binary offloading decisions and the allocation of resources for wireless energy transfer and task offloading time. However, solving such problems is extremely complex, especially in large-scale networks. Numerous studies have focused on designing low-complexity suboptimal algorithms, such as heuristic algorithms based on local search[19]、面向分解的搜索算法, decomposition-oriented search algorithms[3]，以及二元变量的凸松弛方法, and convex relaxation methods for binary variables[12]等。然而，除了性能损失之外，这些算法通常需要大量的数值迭代才能获得满意的解决方案。因此，它们不适合在快速衰落的信道环境中进行实时决策，在这种环境中，需要不断更新卸载策略以响应信道状态的重大变化。, among others. Nonetheless, besides performance loss, these algorithms often require extensive numerical iterations to obtain satisfactory solutions. Consequently, they are unsuitable for real-time decision-making in fast-fading channel environments, where the offloading strategy needs to be constantly updated in response to significant changes in the channel state.

在原有的保序量化方法ref alg:ugq的基础上，本文提出了一种基于不确定性引导的新颖量化方法（Uncertainty-Guided Quantization，UGQ）。该方法的核心思想是：对于深度神经网络输出的每个卸载决策概率值，越接近0.5，模型对该维度决策的不确定性越大。因此，在生成多个候选卸载动作时，应优先考虑调整这些不确定维度，从而实现对动作空间更加有效和多样化的探索。具体来说，UGQ方法每次生成K个候选动作时，首先选择一个基线动作。然后，它依次选择概率最接近 0.5 的几个维度，使用它们的概率值作为枢轴点。通过引入小的随机噪声来调整阈值，它生成不同的候选动作。如果新生成的动作与现有动作重复，则强制翻转特定位以保持动作多样性。该方法不仅保留了原始保序方法的高效率，而且通过强调不确定维度和引入噪声扰动，显着增强了动作探索能力，从而加速模型收敛并提高最终性能。Building upon the original Order-Preserving Quantization method ref alg:ugq, this paper proposes a novel quantization method based on uncertainty guidance (Uncertainty-Guided Quantization, UGQ). The core idea of this method is: for each offloading decision probability value output by the deep neural network, the closer it is to 0.5, the more uncertain the model is regarding that dimension’s decision. If a newly generated action duplicates an existing one, a specific bit is forcibly flipped to maintain action diversity. This method not only retains the high efficiency of the original order-preserving approach but also significantly enhances action exploration capability by emphasizing uncertain dimensions and incorporating noise perturbation, thereby accelerating model convergence and improving final performance.

VI 结论CONCLUSION

本文提出了一种名为 QAROO 的在线任务卸载框架，用于无线供电的移动边缘计算网络。该框架通过集成RNN、UGQ和混合QNN+Attention，有效解决了动态信道环境中计算资源和能源资源的协调优化问题。具体来说，RNN的引入增强了系统对时间通道状态建模的能力； UGQ策略通过动态阈值调整和随机扰动提高动作探索多样性；量子注意力混合架构显着增强了特征表示和决策鲁棒性。实验表明，RNN+UGQ在归一化计算速率、收敛速度和稳定性方面均优于DROO等传统方法，尤其在大规模设备场景中表现出色。未来的工作将将该框架扩展到多小区 MEC 网络、部分任务卸载和移动场景，并探索在具有能效优化的真实量子硬件上的部署。This paper proposes an online task offloading framework called QAROO for wireless-powered mobile edge computing networks. By integrating RNN, UGQ, and a hybrid QNN+Attention,the framework effectively addresses the coordinated optimization of computational and energy resources in dynamic channel environments. Specifically, the introduction of RNN enhances the system’s capability to model temporal channel states; the UGQ strategy improves action exploration diversity through dynamic threshold adjustment and stochastic perturbation; and the quantum-attention hybrid architecture significantly strengthens feature representation and decision robustness. Experiments demonstrate that RNN+UGQ outperforms traditional methods such as DROO in terms of normalized computation rate, convergence speed, and stability, particularly excelling in large-scale device scenarios. Future work will extend this framework to multi-cell MEC networks, partial task offloading, and mobility scenarios, and explore deployment on real quantum hardware with energy efficiency optimization.
