1. 简介Introduction

音频到乐谱转录 (A2S) 可以被视为以下领域的一个子领域： 自动音乐转录（AMT）。事实上，正如定义在Audio-to-Score Transcription (A2S) can be considered as a subfield of Automatic Music Transcription (AMT). Indeed, as defined in(Benetos et al., 2019)，AMT 在于“将原声音乐信号转换为某种形式的乐谱的计算算法的设计”。 然而，AMT 模型的输出通常是 MIDI 或钢琴卷帘表示形式, AMT consists in “the design of computational algorithms to convert acoustic music signals into some form of musical notation”. However, more often than not, the output of AMT models is a MIDI or piano-roll representation(Benetos et al., 2019; Jamshidi et al., 2024)，而不是完整的乐谱。 A2S 研究通过将所需输出设置为人类可读的分数来解决这一限制。 在本节中，我们首先介绍 A2S 领域的相关工作以及现有方法的局限性，然后再介绍我们提出的方法和贡献。, and not a full-fledged musical score. A2S research addresses this limitation, by setting the desired output as a human-readable score. In this section, we first introduce related work in the field of A2S and limitations of existing approaches, before presenting our proposed approach and contributions.

6.1. 用户注释变量User-Annotation Variabilities

SheetSage-A2S 数据集上较高 SER 的一个促成因素是用户生成内容的固有可变性。转录者在微妙的风格选择上略有不同，模型无法从音频中辨别出来。我们选择以最少的预处理来发布核心数据集。然而，缺乏标准化可能会带来性能损失。A contributing factor to the higher SER on the SheetSage-A2S dataset is the inherent variability of user-generated content. Transcribers slightly differ in subtle stylistic choices, which the model has no way of discerning from the audio. We choose to release the core dataset with minimal preprocessing. However, this lack of standardisation is likely to introduce performance penalties.

首先，用户以不同的标准转录旋律和和弦。一些注释者仅在和声发生变化时才对和弦进行一次标记，而另一些注释者则为每个节奏扫弦重复注释同一和弦，或者每个小节重复注释一次。 此外，我们还观察到转录不一致的问题，其中一些用户在音频剪辑正式结束之前停止转录，因为剩余的（通常很小）部分不属于当前音乐部分，这对正确转录剩余音频的模型进行了惩罚。 当人声无声时，一些用户还会转录一小部分伴奏，我们的模型没有发现这种行为，因为它在训练数据中并不普遍。First, users transcribe melodies and chords with varying standards. Some annotators label a chord only once when the harmony changes, while others annotate the same chord repeatedly for every rhythmic strum, or once per bar. Furthermore, we observe issues with inconsistent transcriptions, where some users stop transcribing before the audio clip officially ends as the remaining (usually small) part does not belong to the current music section, which penalises the model for correctly transcribing the remaining audio. Some users also transcribe small parts of the accompaniment when vocals are silent, a behaviour our model did not catch on as it is not prevalent in the training data.

其次，旋律需要不同程度的节奏粒度。 这部分是由于用户注释不一致造成的：一些用户为每个单词注释单独的注释，一些用户为每个音节注释，而另一些用户仅在音高变化时注释。此外，歌手经常采用富有表现力的声音技巧，例如微妙的弯音或装饰音。一些用户对弯音的开始和结束都进行了注释，而其他用户则仅对核心音符进行了注释。所有上述情况都是训练和评估过程中的不一致，从而影响了我们的模型在 SheetSage-A2S 数据集上的性能。Second, melodies expect different levels of rhythmic granularity. This is partly due to user annotation inconsistencies: some users annotate separate notes for every word, some for every syllable, and others only annotate when the pitch changes. Furthermore, singers often employ expressive vocal techniques, such as subtle pitch bends or grace notes. Some users annotate both the start and end of the bend, while others only annotate the core note. All the aforementioned cases serve as inconsistencies in the training and evaluation processes that compromise the performances of our model on the SheetSage-A2S dataset.
