细粒度的视角：使用注释者特定的原理进行建模解释Fine-Grained Perspectives: Modeling Explanations with Annotator-Specific Rationales

摘要Abstract

除了探索建模视角的分类标签之外，注释者的基本原理还提供了各个视角的细粒度信号。在这项工作中，我们提出了一个框架，用于联合建模特定于注释者的标签预测和相应的解释，并根据注释者提供的基本原理进行微调。使用具有分类自然语言推理 (NLI) 注释和注释者提供的解释的数据集，我们通过表示级用户护照机制对注释者身份和人口统计元数据进行预测。我们进一步介绍了两种解释器架构：基于事后提示的解释器和前缀桥解释器，它将注释器条件的分类器表示直接传输到生成模型中。这种设计使得解释生成能够与各个注释者的观点保持一致。我们的结果表明，结合解释模型大大提高了基线注释器感知分类器的预测性能，前缀桥方法实现了更稳定的标签对齐和更高的语义一致性，而事后方法产生了更强的词汇相似性。这些发现表明，将解释建模为细粒度观点的表达可以提供更丰富、更忠实的分歧表示。所提出的方法通过将注释者特定的基本原理集成到预测和生成组件中来推进透视主义建模。Beyond exploring disaggregated labels for modeling perspectives, annotator rationales provide fine-grained signals of individual perspectives. In this work, we propose a framework for jointly modeling annotator-specific label prediction and corresponding explanations, fine-tuned on the annotators’ provided rationales. Using a dataset with disaggregated natural language inference (NLI) annotations and annotator-provided explanations, we condition predictions on both annotator identity and demographic metadata through a representation-level User Passport mechanism. We further introduce two explainer architectures: a post-hoc prompt-based explainer and a prefixed bridge explainer that transfers annotator-conditioned classifier representations directly into a generative model. This design enables explanation generation aligned with individual annotator perspectives. Our results show that incorporating explanation modeling substantially improves predictive performance over a baseline annotator-aware classifier, with the prefixed bridge approach achieving more stable label alignment and higher semantic consistency, while the post-hoc approach yields stronger lexical similarity. These findings indicate that modeling explanations as expressions of fine-grained perspective provides a richer and more faithful representation of disagreement. The proposed approaches advance perspectivist modeling by integrating annotator-specific rationales into both predictive and generative components.

1.  简介1.  Introduction

透视主义者 NLP 认为注释应该反映个体注释者的具体判断，而不是集中在单一的共识标签上Perspectivist NLP argues that annotations should reflect the specific judgments of individual annotators rather than converge on a single consensus label(Pavlick and Kwiatkowski, 2019)。在自然语言推理 (NLI)、立场检测和仇恨言论分类等任务中. In tasks such as natural language inference (NLI), stance detection, and hate speech classification(Xu et al., 2024)，注释者由于背景、解释或社会人口观点的差异而产生不同意见是合理的。对此类分歧进行建模已成为近期共享任务的一个重要焦点, it is legitimate for annotators to disagree due to differences in background, interpretation, or socio-demographic perspective. Modeling such disagreement has become an important focus of recent shared tasks(Leonardelli et al., 2023; Uma et al., 2021)和研究举措，将重点从多数投票转向保留变异。and research initiatives, shifting the emphasis away from majority voting toward preserving variation.

大多数观点主义方法使用语言和上下文信号（例如社会人口统计信息、用户 ID 和群体归属）隐式地对观点进行建模Most perspectivist approaches implicitly model perspectives using linguistic and contextual signals such as sociodemographic information, user IDs, and group affiliations(Davani et al., 2022; Plepi et al., 2022)。这些信号用于推断注释中变异和多样性的潜在来源。然而，除了分类标签之外，注释者的观点通常仍然是抽象的，并且仅间接地在模型中表示。尽管某些数据集需要注释者提供选择特定标签的基本原理. These signals are used to infer the potential sources of variation and diversity in annotations. However, beyond disaggregated labels, annotators’ perspectives often remain abstracted and only indirectly represented in the model. Although some datasets require annotators to provide rationales for selecting a particular labelWeber-Genzel et al. (2024)，这些解释很少明确地集成到透视主义建模中。结合注释者的基本原理可以实现更细致、更细粒度的视角表示。, these explanations are rarely integrated explicitly into perspectivist modeling. Incorporating annotator rationales enables more nuanced and fine-grained representations of perspective.

视角主义方法中的可解释性已逐渐成为值得信赖的 NLP 系统的关键组成部分，因为它通过分析用于证明预测的注意力模式或内部结构来支持模型级可解释性Explainability in perspectivist approaches has gradually emerged as a critical component of trustworthy NLP systems, as it supports model-level interpretability through the analysis of attention patterns or internal structures used to justify predictions(Mastromattei et al., 2022a)。例如，在推荐系统中，人们提出了自然语言生成（NLG）方法来根据用户生成的内容生成灵活的自由文本解释. In recommendation systems, for example, natural language generation (NLG) methods have been proposed to generate flexible, free-text explanations based on user-generated content(Li et al., 2021b)。虽然这些方法展示了生成模型产生流畅且多样化的解释的潜力，但它们也暴露了局限性：生成的内容可能偏离主题、输入的基础不够充分、重复. While such approaches demonstrate the potential of generative models to produce fluent and varied explanations, they also expose limitations: generated content may be off-topic, insufficiently grounded in the input, repetitiveLi et al. (2021a)，或不够个性化。这些挑战凸显了对可控且忠实的解释生成的需求，特别是当解释预计反映特定用户或注释者的观点时。, or insufficiently personalized. These challenges highlight the need for controllable and faithful explanation generation, particularly when explanations are expected to reflect specific user or annotator viewpoints.

在透视主义 NLP 中，可解释性以不同的方式被处理。一些研究将其视为事后模型解释Within perspectivist NLP, explainability has been approached in different ways. Some studies treat it as post-hoc model interpretationMastromattei et al. (2022a)，识别影响视角感知预测的语言特征或结构模式, identifying linguistic features or structural patterns that influence perspective-aware predictions(Muscato et al., 2025)。其他人依靠提示策略来模拟大型语言模型中的用户视角. Others rely on prompting strategies to simulate user perspectives in large language models(Hayati et al., 2024)。然而，相对较少的工作明确地对特定于注释者的解释和分类标签进行建模，部分原因是很少有数据集同时包含分歧和不同的理由。. However, relatively little work has explicitly modeled annotator-specific explanations alongside disaggregated labels, partly because few datasets contain both disagreement and distinct rationales.

在这项研究中，我们通过明确地根据注释者特定的表示来调节解释生成，将透视主义建模与透视主义解释相结合。使用具有分类 NLI 标签和注释者提供的解释的数据集，我们对标签预测和基本原理生成中的视角进行建模。我们探索了一个基于提示的事后解释器和一个表示前缀桥，它将用注释器信息丰富的分类器表示转移到生成模型中。在此过程中，我们将解释视为观点的表达，而不仅仅是模型决策的事后论证。In this study, we integrate perspectivist modeling with perspectivist explanation by explicitly conditioning explanation generation on annotator-specific representations. Using a dataset with disaggregated NLI labels and annotator-provided explanations, we model perspective in both label prediction and rationale generation. We explore a prompt-based post-hoc explainer and a representation-prefix bridge that transfers classifier representations enriched with annotator information into a generative model. In doing so, we treat explanations as expressions of perspective rather than merely post-hoc justifications of a model’s decisions.

3.  方法与数据3.  Methods and data

我们使用 VariErrNLI 数据集研究生成可解释性中的视角主义We study perspectivism in generative explainability using the VariErrNLI datasetWeber-Genzel et al. (2024)，其中包含分类的注释器标签和注释器特定的基本原理。与大多数现有的分类数据集不同，VariErrNLI 保留了标签不一致和解释多样性，使其适合建模细粒度的观点。, which contains disaggregated annotator labels and annotator-specific rationales. Unlike most existing disaggregated datasets, VariErrNLI preserves both label disagreement and explanation diversity, making it suitable for modeling fine-grained perspectives.

我们的框架由两个组件组成：（i）一个注释器感知分类器，用于预测每个注释器的标签集；（ii）一个注释器条件解释器，用于生成相应的基本原理。我们使用学习的嵌入和元数据特征对注释者身份进行显式建模，这些特征与输入的上下文表示（上下文和语句）融合以产生特定于注释者的预测。Our framework consists of two components: (i) an annotator-aware classifier that predicts label sets for each annotator, and (ii) an annotator-conditioned explainer that generates corresponding rationales. We explicitly model annotator identity using learned embeddings and metadata features, which are fused with the contextual representation of the input (context and statement) to produce annotator-specific predictions.

我们比较两种解释方法。第一个是事后基于提示的解释器，它根据文本输入生成解释。第二个是前缀桥解释器，它根据分类器的内部注释器特定表示来生成条件。这使得模型能够合并预测标签和底层注释器特定的推理信号。We compare two explanation approaches. The first is a post-hoc, prompt-based explainer that generates explanations from textual inputs. The second is a prefixed bridge explainer that conditions generation on the classifier’s internal annotator-specific representations. This allows the model to incorporate both predicted labels and underlying annotator-specific reasoning signals.

3.1.  VariErrNLI 数据集3.1.  VariErrNLI Dataset

我们使用 VariErrNLI（变化与错误），这是一个透视 NLI 数据集，旨在将人类标签变化与注释错误分开。 VariErrNLI 包含从 ChaosNLI（MNLI 子集）采样的大约 500 个 NLI 项目，并由四个独立注释器分两轮进行注释。We use VariErrNLI (Variation vs. Error), a perspectivist NLI dataset designed to disentangle human label variation from annotation error. VariErrNLI contains approximately 500 NLI items sampled from ChaosNLI (MNLI subset) and annotated in two rounds by four independent annotators.

在第一轮中，注释者为每个项目分配一个或多个 NLI 标签、蕴涵 (E)、中性 (N) 或矛盾 (C)，并为分配的每个标签提供一句话解释，保留细粒度的推理多样性。这一轮注释产生了 1,933 个标签-解释对。In Round 1, annotators assigned one or more NLI labels, Entailment (E), Neutral (N), or Contradiction (C) to each item and provided a one-sentence explanation for each label assigned, preserving fine-grained reasoning diversity. This round of annotation produced 1,933 label-explanation pairs.

在第二轮中，注释者通过判断解释是否合理地支持指定的标签来独立评估每个标签-解释对（包括他们自己的）的有效性。第二阶段能够区分合理的人类标签变化和注释错误。因此，该数据集不仅提供分类标签和基本原理，还提供对其有效性的元判断。In Round 2, annotators independently evaluated the validity of each label–explanation pair (including their own) by judging whether the explanation plausibly supports the assigned label. The variation observed across metrics suggests that annotators may express similar decisions through different explanatory structures, levels of detail, or linguistic forms. By incorporating explanation generation, both models move beyond label prediction and provide additional insight into how annotator perspectives are represented. The Prefixed Bridge Explainer, in particular, better preserves the relationship between predictions and underlying reasoning, especially when explanations are less consistent in form.

值得注意的是，这些观察结果是基于少数注释者、有限的人口多样性和相对较小的测试集。因此，我们不进行统计显着性检验，而是依赖描述性分析。因此，观察到的模式应被解释为指示性趋势，而不是普遍的发现。It is important to note that these observations are based on a small number of annotators, with limited demographic diversity and a relatively small test set. As such, we do not perform statistical significance testing and instead rely on descriptive analysis. The patterns observed should therefore be interpreted as indicative trends rather than generalizable findings.

总体而言，每个注释器的分析表明，合并解释模型可以改善注释器视角的表示，并且预测和解释之间更紧密的集成（如前缀桥解释器）可以在不同的注释器行为中提供更一致和语义上一致的输出。Overall, the per-annotator analysis suggests that incorporating explanation modeling improves the representation of annotator perspectives, and that tighter integration between prediction and explanation, as in the Prefixed Bridge Explainer, provides more consistent and semantically aligned outputs across diverse annotator behaviors.

6.  结论6.  Conclusion

这项工作证明了在自然语言推理中对细粒度注释者视角进行建模以及解释生成的重要性。我们没有将解释视为事后合理化，而是证明将注释者表达的基本原理集成到预测架构中可以对人类多样性进行更稳健的建模。通过利用与单个注释相关的解释级监督，该模型不仅捕获标签结果，还捕获它们背后的推理模式，从而更忠实地表示分歧和解释的细微差别。This work demonstrates the importance of Modeling fine-grained annotator perspectives jointly with explanation generation in natural language inference. Rather than treating explanations as post-hoc rationalizations, we show that integrating annotator-expressed rationales into the predictive architecture enables more robust modeling of human diversity. By leveraging explanation-level supervision tied to individual annotations, the model captures not only label outcomes but also the reasoning patterns underlying them, allowing for more faithful representation of disagreement and interpretative nuance.

在方法上，我们实现了一个编码器到编码器的桥接架构，它显式地连接预测和解释模块。这种结构耦合使模型能够根据驱动分类决策的相同信号来调节其解释性表示，从而提高宏观层面的稳定性和注释器之间的推理一致性。我们的结果表明，通过注释器原理对视角进行建模可以增强语义一致性和预测鲁棒性，特别是在语义复杂的类别中。总的来说，这项工作强调了将解释模型集成到注释器感知架构中的价值，以开发更透明和视角敏感的 NLP 系统。Methodologically, we implement an encoder-to-encoder bridge architecture that explicitly connects prediction and explanation modules. This structural coupling enables the model to condition its explanatory representations on the same signals that drive classification decisions, thereby improving macro-level stability and inferential alignment across annotators. Our results show that Modeling perspectives through annotator rationales strengthens semantic consistency and predictive robustness, particularly in semantically complex categories. Overall, this work highlights the value of integrating explanation modeling into annotator-aware architectures for developing more transparent and perspective-sensitive NLP systems.

7.  限制7.  Limitation

这项工作的主要限制是所使用的数据集，该数据集最初是为了研究人类标签变异中的注释错误而构建的。尽管包含注释者特定的基本原理代表着朝着保留个体推理模式迈出了实质性的一步，但这些解释并不是为了系统地捕获人口、语言或文化背景中的受控变化而设计的，而是针对注释错误检测。因此，范围仍然有限，这可能会限制所提出的编码器到编码器桥接框架的通用性。A primary limitation of this work is the dataset used, which was originally constructed to investigate annotation errors in human label variation. Although the inclusion of annotator-specific rationales represents a substantial step toward preserving individual reasoning patterns, the explanations were not designed to systematically capture controlled variations in demographic, linguistic, or cultural background, but were instead targeted toward annotation error detection. As a result, the scope remains limited, which may constrain the generalizability of the proposed encoder-to-encoder bridge framework.

我们最初建议用英语为母语的人编写的解释来扩展 VariErrNLI 中使用的 ChaOSNLI 实例，以系统地检查双语与母语注释者的基本原理如何影响建模结果。这一扩展将允许对语言背景对解释可信度和预测性能的影响进行更可控的调查。未来的工作将集中于在这个方向上扩展数据集，以加强透视建模的经验基础。We initially proposed extending the ChaOSNLI instances used in VariErrNLI with explanations written by native English speakers to systematically examine how bilingual versus native annotator rationales affect modeling outcomes. This extension would allow a more controlled investigation of linguistic background effects on explanation faithfulness and predictive performance. Future work will focus on expanding the dataset in this direction to strengthen the empirical foundation of perspective modeling.

此外，事后和前缀桥方法的集成为未来的工作提供了一个有趣的方向，因为它可以利用各个模型的优势来产生更全面的输出。Additionally, an ensemble of the Post-hoc and Prefixed Bridge approaches presents an interesting direction for future work, as it could leverage the strengths of the individual models to produce a more well-rounded output.
