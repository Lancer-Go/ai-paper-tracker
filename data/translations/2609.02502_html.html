摘要Abstract

To bridge this gap, we introduceVMetaphor-长凳VMetaphor-Bench，第一个评估 T2I 模型中视觉隐喻生成的基准。它包含 1,500 个从现实世界的创意图像中挑选出来的视觉隐喻，分为三个级别和十个类别，每个样本都配有两个不同特异性的提示。为了进行评估，我们在 MLLM 作为法官范例中开发了一个混合框架，将基于多项选择问题 (MCQ) 的协议（涵盖四个隐喻保真度级别的 9,594 个问题）与沿三个感知维度的基于维度的评分协议相结合。对 11 个代表性 T2I 模型的广泛评估表明，即使是最强大的专有模型，也难以应对构成结构和跨域映射（隐喻表达的关键方面），这凸显了视觉隐喻生成是未来 T2I 研究的重要前沿。, the first benchmark for evaluating visual metaphor generation in T2I models. It comprises 1,500 visual metaphors curated from real-world creative imagery, organized into three levels and ten categories, with each sample paired with two prompts of differing specificity. For evaluation, we develop a hybrid framework within an MLLM-as-judge paradigm, combining a multiple-choice question (MCQ) based protocol of 9,594 questions across four levels of metaphorical fidelity with a dimension-based scoring protocol along three perceptual dimensions. Extensive evaluation of 11 representative T2I models reveals that even the strongest proprietary models struggle with compositional structuring and cross-domain mapping, key aspects of metaphorical expression, highlighting visual metaphor generation as an important frontier for future T2I research.

3.2 评估框架Evaluation Framework

评估视觉隐喻生成需要评估隐喻保真度（生成的图像是否忠实地捕捉预期的跨域映射）和表达质量（隐喻的传达效果和美观程度）。为此，我们采用以 MLLM 为评判者的混合评估框架Evaluating visual metaphor generation requires assessing both metaphorical fidelity — whether the generated image faithfully captures the intended cross-domain mapping — and expressive quality — how effectively and aesthetically the metaphor is conveyed. To this end, we adopt a hybrid evaluation framework with MLLM-as-judgeZhang et al. (2025b)，包括两种互补的方法：(1) 基于 MCQ 的评估，通过多项选择题衡量隐喻元素的正确性；(2) 基于维度的评分，以 1-5 Likert 量表对生成的隐喻的整体质量进行评分。评估框架概述如图所示, comprising two complementary approaches: (1) MCQ-based evaluation that measures the correctness of metaphorical elements through multiple-choice questions, and (2) dimension-based scoring that rates the overall quality of the generated metaphor on a 1-5 Likert scale. 基于 MCQ 的评估。MCQ-based Evaluation.为了客观地评估隐喻结构每个级别生成图像的正确性，我们为数据集中的所有 1,500 个样本自动构建了一组多项选择题 (MCQ)。具体来说，我们利用 Gemini 3.1 Pro 根据每个样本的结构化注释及其描述性提示生成 MCQ。生成的 MCQ 评估隐喻保真度的四个层次：To objectively assess the correctness of generated images at each level of the metaphorical structure, we automatically construct a set of multiple-choice questions (MCQs) for all 1,500 samples in the dataset. Specifically, we leverage Gemini 3.1 Pro to generate MCQs based on each sample’s structured annotations along with its descriptive prompt. The generated MCQs assess four hierarchical levels of metaphorical fidelity:

  • •

    L1 – 域存在L1 – Domain Presence：图像中目标域和源域是否在视觉上可识别（每个域一个问题）。: Whether the target and source domains are visually recognizable in the image (one question per domain).

  • •

    L2 – 结构类型L2 – Structure Type：视觉组合策略是否与真实结构类型匹配（替换、融合或并置）。: Whether the visual combination strategy matches the ground-truth structure type (replacement, fusion, or juxtaposition).

  • •

    L3 – 含义L3 – Meaning：图像是否传达了预期的隐喻含义。: Whether the image conveys the intended metaphorical meaning.

  • •

    L4 – 元素映射L4 – Element Mapping：每个源元素是否正确对应于其预期目标元素（每个映射对一个问题；得分取平均值）。: Whether each source element correctly corresponds to its intended target element (one question per mapping pair; scores are averaged).

此过程在所有样本中总共生成 9,594 个 MCQ，并由相同的注释器手动验证以确保正确性。每个问题由四个选项组成：一个从注释中得出的真实答案、两个看似合理但不正确的干扰项，以及一个“无法确定”选项。所有期权订单都是随机的，以消除仓位偏差。对于每个图像，我们计算每个级别的准确性：L1 是两个领域问题的平均值，L2 是单个二进制分数，L3 是单个二进制分数，L4 是所有映射问题的平均值。 MCQ 总体得分是所有四个级别的平均值。This process yields a total of 9,594 MCQs across all samples, which are manually verified by the same annotators to ensure correctness. Each question consists of four options: one ground-truth answer derived from the annotation, two plausible but incorrect distractors, and a "cannot be determined" option. All option orders are randomized to eliminate positional bias. For each image, we compute per-level accuracy: L1 averages over the two domain questions, L2 is a single binary score, L3 is a single binary score, and L4 averages over all mapping questions. The overall MCQ score is the mean across all four levels.

基于维度的评分。Dimension-based Scoring.虽然基于 MCQ 的评估衡量隐喻元素的正确性，但它并不能捕获生成图像的整体感知质量。因此，我们用基于维度的评分对其进行补充，其中向 MLLM 评估者提供生成的图像及其结构化注释，并要求在三个独立维度上以 1-5 Likert 量表对图像进行评分：While MCQ-based evaluation measures the correctness of metaphorical elements, it does not capture the overall perceptual quality of the generated image. We therefore complement it with dimension-based scoring, where an MLLM evaluator is presented with the generated image along with its structured annotations and asked to rate the image on a 1–5 Likert scale across three independent dimensions:

  • •

    隐喻功效Metaphoric Efficacy：在没有任何文字解释的情况下，图像如何清楚地向观看者传达预期的隐喻含义。: How clearly the image communicates the intended metaphorical meaning to a viewer without any textual explanation.

  • •

    隐喻逻辑Metaphor Logic：源和目标之间的跨域映射在概念上是否一致且逻辑上是否合理。: Whether the cross-domain mapping between source and target is conceptually coherent and logically sound.

  • •

    感性和谐Perceptual Harmony：考虑到混合、构图和整体视觉凝聚力，这两个领域在视觉上呈现的自然和连贯程度。: How naturally and coherently the two domains are visually presented, considering blending, composition, and overall visual cohesion.

总体维度得分是所有三个维度的平均值。与基于 MCQ 的评估一起，该混合框架提供了对生成的视觉隐喻的隐喻保真度和表达质量的全面评估。附录中提供了完整的评估提示The overall dimension score is the average across all three dimensions. 5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 VMetaphor-Bench，第一个 评估文本到图像模型中视觉隐喻生成的基准。我们的基准测试以 1,500 个精心策划的视觉隐喻和结构化注释为基础，提供了三个级别和十个类别的分层分类法，以及一个将基于 MCQ 的保真度评估与基于维度的评分相结合的混合评估框架。对 11 个代表性 T2I 模型的广泛评估表明，专有模型的性能大大优于开源模型，但即使是最强大的模型仍然在组合结构和跨域映射（隐喻表达的关键方面）方面遇到困难。我们希望 VMetaphor-Bench 能够成为未来弥合文字之间差距的研究的基础。 渲染和创造性的、意义丰富的视觉生成。In this paper, we introduce VMetaphor-Bench, the first benchmark for evaluating visual metaphor generation in text-to-image models. Built upon 1,500 carefully curated visual metaphors with structured annotations, our benchmark provides a hierarchical taxonomy of three levels and ten categories and a hybrid evaluation framework that combines MCQ-based fidelity assessment with dimension-based scoring. Extensive evaluation of 11 representative T2I models reveals that proprietary models substantially outperform open-source counterparts, yet even the strongest models continue to struggle with compositional structuring and cross-domain mapping—key aspects of metaphorical expression. We hope VMetaphor-Bench serves as a foundation for future research on bridging the gap between literal rendering and creative, meaning-rich visual generation.

In contrast, fusion requires disentangling two co-present elements, while juxtaposition simply pairs them side by side. Our results extend this ordering from comprehension to generation: T2I models exhibit the same complexity gradient when producing visual metaphors. This suggests that the cognitive demands of replacement, which require preserving context while substituting a localized element, pose a fundamental challenge for current models. This finding points to compositional precision, the ability to perform localized, structure-aware modifications, as a key direction for advancing visual metaphor generation.

A.10 局限性Limitations

我们承认有一些限制。首先，样本在主题类别中的分布不均匀，反映了现实世界创意图像中不同主题中视觉隐喻的自然流行。其次，我们的评估依赖于 MLLM 作为判断框架，尽管与人类注释者有很强的一致性，但它继承了自动视觉推理对最主观判断的基本限制。最后，我们的提示和源图像主要是英语和西方创意传统，将多语言提示和跨文化隐喻作为未来的方向。We acknowledge several limitations. First, the distribution of samples across thematic categories is uneven, reflecting the natural prevalence of visual metaphors in different topics in real-world creative imagery. Second, our evaluation relies on an MLLM-as-judge framework, which, despite strong alignment with human annotators, inherits the fundamental limitations of automatic visual reasoning on the most subjective judgments. Finally, our prompts and source imagery are predominantly English and from Western creative traditions, leaving multilingual prompts and cross-cultural metaphors as future directions.

A.11 更广泛的影响Broader Impacts

这项工作推进了视觉隐喻生成的评估，这是文本到图像模型的一个具有挑战性的方面，需要连贯的跨域推理和富有表现力的视觉构成。通过提供具有细粒度评估标准的结构化基准，它可以支持开发能力更强、可解释的生成模型。这种进步可能有利于现实世界的应用，包括创意设计、教育和广告，其中视觉隐喻被广泛用于有效地传达信息并吸引受众。This work advances the evaluation of visual metaphor generation, a challenging aspect of text-to-image models that requires coherent cross-domain reasoning and expressive visual composition. By providing a structured benchmark with fine-grained evaluation criteria, it can support the development of more capable and interpretable generative models. Such progress may benefit real-world applications including creative design, education, and advertising, where visual metaphors are widely used to communicate messages effectively and engage audiences.

我们也承认潜在的负面影响。改进的视觉隐喻生成可能会被滥用，产生有说服力的误导性图像（例如，操纵性广告或政治宣传），利用视觉类比的强烈情感和修辞效果。我们鼓励未来的工作考虑防止此类滥用的保障措施，并且我们的基准本身旨在评估而不是直接部署。We also acknowledge potential negative implications. Improved visual metaphor generation could be misused to produce persuasive misleading imagery (e.g., manipulative advertising or political propaganda) that exploits the strong emotional and rhetorical effects of visual analogies. We encourage future work to consider safeguards against such misuse, and our benchmark itself is intended for evaluation rather than direct deployment.
