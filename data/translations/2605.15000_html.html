1  简介1  Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 现在的性能可与医生相媲美或超过 一些医学推理基准，包括执照式考试， 诊断片段以及医生对诊断和管理的基线评估 推理Large language models (LLMs) now achieve performance rivaling or exceeding physicians on several medical reasoning benchmarks, including licensing-style examinations, diagnostic vignettes, and physician-baseline evaluations of diagnostic and management reasoning[1, 2]。最近的医生基线评估表明 高级法学硕士可以在多项诊断和管理方面超越临床医生 推理任务，包括真实的急诊室案例. Recent physician-baseline evaluations suggest that advanced LLMs can outperform clinicians on several diagnostic and management reasoning tasks, including real emergency-department cases[3]。这些 成果表明，医学知识和诊断准确性的传统基准 正在变得饱和. These gains suggest that conventional benchmarks of medical knowledge and diagnostic accuracy are becoming saturated[4]。随着法学硕士进入面向患者和 临床决策支持环境. As LLMs move into patient-facing and clinical decision-support contexts[5]，更紧迫的问题 不仅在于他们能否给出正确答案，还在于他们是否能够识别 当现有信息不支持安全答案时, the more pressing question is not only whether they can produce correct answers, but whether they can recognize when the available information does not support a safe answer[6].

在医学上，过早结束描述的是一种认知错误，即致力于某件事。 在收集到足够的信息之前进行诊断或计划In medicine, premature closure describes the cognitive error of committing to a diagnosis or plan before sufficient information has been gathered[7, 8, 9]。在本文中，我们定义. In this paper, we define法学硕士提前关闭LLM premature closure作为 不确定性下的不适当承诺：选择答案，提供具体的 临床指导，或在现有信息存在时遵守不安全的指示 不支持安全响应。因为这种行为在不同的地方表现不同 任务格式，我们在结构化和开放式环境中以不同的方式对其进行衡量。在 结构化多项选择设置，过早关闭的测量为as inappropriate commitment under uncertainty: selecting an answer, providing specific clinical guidance, or complying with unsafe instructions when the available information does not support a safe response. Because this behavior manifests differently across task formats, we measure it differently in structured and open-ended settings. In the structured multiple-choice setting, premature closure was measured as假的 行动false action：在正确选项被删除后选择答案。在开放式的 临床相互作用，过早关闭被测量为给予自信或具体 当需要澄清、不确定、升级或拒绝时提供指导 保证。在这两种设置中，共享构造在以下情况下都会提交响应： 现有信息不足: selecting an answer when the correct option had been removed. In open-ended clinical interactions, premature closure was measured as giving confident or specific guidance when clarification, uncertainty, escalation, or refusal would have been warranted. Across both settings, the shared construct is committing to a response when the available information is insufficient[10]。让人放心的模型 症状需要升级的患者，或者能够提供可靠指导的患者 信息不完整，尽管看起来有帮助，但可能会带来风险. A model that reassures a patient whose symptoms warrant escalation, or that produces confident guidance from incomplete information, may create risk despite appearing helpful[11, 12, 13]。直接检查前沿模型何时以及如何失败 因此，拒绝、限定或升级他们的回应至关重要。. Direct examination of when and how frontier models fail to withhold, qualify, or escalate their responses is therefore critical.

标准医疗人工智能基准不太适合检测这种行为。 多项选择评估通常假设一个答案是正确的并给予奖励 选择它，几乎没有空间来测试模型是否认识到没有选项是 适当的。先前的工作表明，高多项选择准确度可以掩盖 不可靠的医学推理，特别是当正确答案被删除并且 替换为“以上都不是”替换Standard medical AI benchmarks are poorly suited to detect this behavior. Multiple-choice evaluations generally assume that one answer is correct and reward selecting it, leaving little room to test whether a model recognizes that no option is appropriate. Prior work has shown that high multiple-choice accuracy can mask unreliable medical reasoning, particularly when the correct answer is removed and replaced with “none of the above” substitutions[14]。最近 面向部署的工作还表明，法学硕士在考试形式上表现良好 基准测试在现实的面向用户的情况下可能表现不太可靠 设置. Recent deployment-oriented work has also shown that LLMs performing well on exam-style benchmarks may perform less reliably in realistic user-facing settings[15]，以及不确定性敏感的评估，例如脚本 一致性测试揭示了多项选择准确性未捕获的差距 独自一人, and uncertainty-sensitive evaluations such as script concordance testing reveal gaps not captured by multiple-choice accuracy alone[16]。更广泛地说，最近的弃权基准表明，即使 高绩效的法学硕士常常无法认识到他们不应该在什么时候 回答. More broadly, recent abstention benchmarks show that even high-performing LLMs often fail to recognize when they should not answer[17].

开放式评估已开始通过评分来解决其中一些限制 根据医生制定的标准进行现实的患者和临床医生对话。 健康台Open-ended evaluations have begun to address some of these limitations by scoring realistic patient and clinician conversations against physician-authored criteria. HealthBench[18]评估多轮患者和临床医生的对话 使用医生撰写的评估标准，涵盖准确性、完整性、情境意识、 沟通和遵循指令。 HealthBench专业版evaluates multi-turn patient and clinician conversations using physician-authored rubrics covering accuracy, completeness, context awareness, communication, and instruction-following. HealthBench Professional[19]添加了更具挑战性的案例，包括医生设计的对抗性查询来测试 模型是否会陷入过度自信或不安全的答案。在一起，这些 研究表明，医学法学硕士的评估正在超越简单的答案正确性， 但他们仍然没有充分审查一个核心安全问题：模型能否识别何时 他们不应该回答吗？adds more challenging cases, including physician-designed adversarial queries that test whether models can be pushed into overconfident or unsafe answers. Together, these studies show that medical LLM evaluation is moving beyond simple answer correctness, but they still leave a central safety question underexamined: can models recognize when they should not answer?

实用的一线缓解措施是通过系统级进行安全导向的提示 指示模特在不确定时弃权，在不确定时寻求澄清 上下文缺失，当演示可能很紧急时升级，并且拒绝不安全 请求。即时工程很有吸引力，因为它不需要模型重新训练 可以由开发人员在系统提示层实现或 机构A practical first-line mitigation is safety-oriented prompting via system-level instructions that direct models to abstain when uncertain, seek clarification when context is missing, escalate when a presentation may be urgent, and refuse unsafe requests. Prompt engineering is attractive because it requires no model retraining and can be implemented at the system-prompt layer by developers or institutions[20, 21]。先前的工作表明提示可以 改善推理并帮助模型表达不确定性，而相关研究表明它们 可以鼓励模型在需要时拒绝或推迟. Prior work suggests that prompts can improve reasoning and help models express uncertainty, while related studies show they can encourage models to refuse or defer when needed[22, 23, 24, 25]。然而，不同型号的提示效果可能会有所不同，可能会引入 过度延迟或降低准确性等权衡，并且在以下情况下可能很脆弱 对抗压力. However, prompt effects may vary across models, may introduce trade-offs such as over-deferral or reduced accuracy, and may be fragile under adversarial pressure[26, 27, 28]。是否简单安全 提示可以减少结构化和开放式医疗任务的过早结束 仍不清楚。. Whether simple safety prompting can reduce premature closure across structured and open-ended medical tasks remains unclear.

我们评估了五位跨结构化和开放式医疗任务的前沿法学硕士。在 结构化多项选择设置，我们修改了 MedQA 和 AfriMed-QA 以包括 无法回答的问题，其中正确的选项已被删除，测量错误 采取提前关闭的行动。在开放式环境中，我们评估了模型响应 861 个 HealthBench 问题和 191 个医生撰写的对抗性 HealthBench 专业询问，衡量模型在弃权时是否给出具体指导， 澄清、升级或拒绝会更安全。我们的研究询问是否 前沿法学硕士知道什么时候不应该回答，以及安全提示是否可以 当直接答案是时，减少过早关闭而不损害性能 适当的。We evaluated five frontier LLMs across structured and open-ended medical tasks. In the structured multiple-choice setting, we modified MedQA and AfriMed-QA to include unanswerable questions in which the correct option had been removed, measuring false action as premature closure. In the open-ended setting, we evaluated model responses to 861 HealthBench questions and 191 physician-authored adversarial HealthBench Professional queries, measuring whether models gave specific guidance when abstention, clarification, escalation, or refusal would have been safer. Our study asks whether frontier LLMs recognize when they should not answer, and whether safety prompting can reduce premature closure without undermining performance when direct answers are appropriate.

3  讨论3  Discussion

这项研究确定了前沿法学硕士中的持续失败模式：给出了明确的 当可用信息不完整、无效或临床不安全时回答。在 基线时，模型平均错误地选择了 71% 无法回答的答案 选择题，在标准的 30% 上给出了不恰当的直接答案 HealthBench 提出问题，并认可针对 78% 的对抗性查询的不安全行为。 因为这种模式出现在没有有效选项的多项选择任务和 需要澄清、放弃或升级的面向患者的任务 似乎不限于任何单一的评估格式。相反，它反映了一个 当更安全的反应是停止、要求更多时，更广泛的回答倾向 信息或升级护理This study identifies a persistent failure mode in frontier LLMs: giving a definitive answer when the available information is incomplete, invalid, or clinically unsafe. At baseline, models falsely selected an answer on an average of 71% of unanswerable multiple-choice items, gave inappropriate direct answers on 30% of standard HealthBench questions, and endorsed unsafe actions on 78% of adversarial queries. Because this pattern appeared in both multiple-choice tasks with no valid option and patient-facing tasks requiring clarification, abstention, or escalation, the problem does not appear to be limited to any single evaluation format. Instead, it reflects a broader tendency to answer when the safer response would be to stop, ask for more information, or escalate care[29].

对抗性结果强调了实际后果：当应用于临床时 现实但模糊或误导性的场景，模型经常产生自信， 具体的、有时甚至是有害的指导。这对于 Grok 3 和 DeepSeek R1，其基线对抗性评分为负（The adversarial results highlight the practical consequences: when placed in clinically realistic but ambiguous or misleading scenarios, models frequently produced confident, specific, and sometimes harmful guidance. 按临床类别对结果进行分层时出现了一个显着的趋势。展出车型 在未指定的低风险情况下，过早关闭的比率最高（基线 过早关闭率在所有模型中为 73-96%，平均为 81%），而且比率要低得多 在未明确说明的紧急情况下（基线 5-36%，平均 19%）。这个图案 表明模型并非一律过度自信；相反，它们有选择地出现 对感知到的紧迫性敏感，同时对缺失的上下文保持不敏感 情况似乎是良性的。换句话说，模型更有可能保留或升级 当风险信号明确时，但在存在模糊性时默认回答 没有明确的紧急提示。A notable trend emerged when stratifying results by clinical category. Models exhibited the highest rates of premature closure in underspecified low-risk scenarios (baseline premature closure 73–96% across models, average 81%), and substantially lower rates in underspecified urgent scenarios (baseline 5–36%, average 19%). This pattern suggests that models are not uniformly overconfident; rather, they appear selectively sensitive to perceived urgency while remaining insensitive to missing context when the scenario appears benign. In other words, models are more likely to withhold or escalate when risk signals are explicit, but default to answering when ambiguity is present without clear urgency cues.

这一发现对现实世界的使用具有重要意义。多次与患者互动 正是属于这一类未明确说明、看似低风险的演示， 症状不完整、不断演变或不明确This finding has important implications for real-world use. Many patient interactions fall into exactly this category of underspecified, seemingly low-risk presentations, where symptoms are incomplete, evolving, or ambiguous[30]。一 可能的解释是，紧迫性线索在训练中表现得更加强烈 数据和对齐过程，使模型更容易检测和响应 适当地。相比之下，识别信息何时不足需要 不同的能力：不仅仅是推理，还有关于推理是否有效的元推理 完全有可能. One possible explanation is that urgency cues are more strongly represented in training data and alignment processes, making them easier for models to detect and respond to appropriately. By contrast, recognizing when information is insufficient requires a different capability: not just reasoning, but meta-reasoning about whether reasoning is possible at all[31]。结果表明当前模型更好 校准以对显性风险做出反应，而不是识别隐性风险 不确定性. The results suggest that current models are better calibrated to react to explicit risk than to recognize implicit uncertainty[32].

这项研究表明，“安全提示”策略提供了一个重要但又 缓解过早关闭的杠杆有限。跨越结构化和开放式 评估，一个简单的以安全为导向的系统指令不断减少过早的发生 结束，包括在 6-46 分之前对无法回答的多项选择题采取错误行动 个百分点，在标准开放式环境中过早关闭 4-8 个百分点， 在对抗条件下过早关闭 9-20 分。然而，效果 并不均匀，而且关键的是，仍然存在大量残余失效，特别是在 过早关闭率通常超过 50% 的对抗性场景。This study suggests that the “safety prompt” strategy provides an important but limited lever for mitigation of premature closure. Across both structured and open-ended evaluations, a simple safety-oriented system instruction consistently reduced premature closure, including false action on unanswerable multiple-choice questions by 6–46 percentage points, premature closure in standard open-ended settings by 4–8 points, and premature closure under adversarial conditions by 9–20 points. However, the effect was not uniform, and critically, substantial residual failure remained, particularly in adversarial scenarios where premature closure rates often exceeded 50%.

这一发现对于如何制定缓解策略具有重要意义 概念化了。虽然基于提示的护栏很有吸引力，因为它们很容易 实施并且不需要模型重新训练，它们应该被理解为部分 解决方案而不是最终的修复。This finding has important implications for how mitigation strategies are conceptualized. While prompt-based guardrails are appealing because they are easy to implement and require no model retraining, they should be understood as partial solutions rather than definitive fixes.

对于开发者和人工智能公司来说，这些发现强化了越来越多的证据： 仅靠即时干预是不够的，凸显了系统级干预的必要性 设计变更，例如支持适当弃权的培训目标，改进 不确定性校准以及能够澄清或升级的机制 未指定的场景For developers and AI companies, these findings reinforce growing evidence that prompt-level interventions alone are insufficient, highlighting the need for system-level design changes, such as training objectives that support appropriate abstention, improved uncertainty calibration, and mechanisms that enable clarification or escalation in underspecified scenarios[33]。对于将法学硕士纳入的卫生系统 工作流程中，安全提示可以作为有用的基线护栏，但不应该 作为主要安全机制，特别是在高风险或面向患者的情况下 应用程序。对于临床医生来说，研究结果强调了特定且非明显的失败 模式：在结构化基准上表现良好的模型可能仍然提供不合适的结果 面对不完整或模糊的临床信息时提供指导。. For health systems integrating LLMs into workflows, safety prompting may serve as a useful baseline guardrail but should not be relied upon as a primary safety mechanism, particularly in high-risk or patient-facing applications. For clinicians, the findings highlight a specific and non-obvious failure mode: models that perform well on structured benchmarks may still provide inappropriate guidance when faced with incomplete or ambiguous clinical information.

由于最终用户不太可能一致应用安全提示， 缓解责任主要落在开发商和部署机构身上。Because safety prompting is unlikely to be applied consistently by end users, mitigation responsibility will largely fall on developers and deploying institutions.

这些发现对评估也有影响。当前的基准范式， 当存在答案时强调正确性，对不确定性提供有限的评估 当信息被处理时，很少测试模型是否适当地放弃 不足。未来的评估框架应明确奖励适当的弃权 并纳入反映模糊性的未明确和对抗性场景 真实的临床相互作用。These findings also have implications for evaluation. Current benchmark paradigms, which emphasize correctness when an answer exists, provide limited assessment of uncertainty handling and rarely test whether models appropriately abstain when information is insufficient. Future evaluation frameworks should explicitly reward appropriate abstention and incorporate underspecified and adversarial scenarios that reflect the ambiguity of real clinical interactions.

应考虑几个限制。首先，结果评估依赖于基于LLM的 法官使用结构化的评估标准，而不是专门由人类临床医生进行评估。 尽管法官之间的协议很牢固，并且独立的结果也一致 法官们，自动评估可能无法完全捕捉细致入微的临床推理或优势 案例。其次，这里测试的提示干预故意简单且 标准化；更复杂的方法可能会产生不同的结果。第三，同时 该研究跨越多个基准和对抗条件，它仍然是一项评估 在受控设置下而不是在实际部署下。Several limitations should be considered. First, outcome assessment relied on LLM-based judges using structured rubrics rather than exclusively human clinician evaluation. Although inter-judge agreement was strong and results were consistent across independent judges, automated evaluation may not fully capture nuanced clinical reasoning or edge cases. Second, the prompting intervention tested here was intentionally simple and standardized; more sophisticated approaches may yield different results. Third, while the study spans multiple benchmarks and adversarial conditions, it remains an evaluation under controlled settings rather than real-world deployment.

尽管存在这些局限性，但结果得出了一个明确且可行的结论。 基于提示的护栏可以有效地减少过早关闭的情况，并提供低成本、 可立即部署的减少伤害机制。然而，它们并没有消除 问题。解决过早关闭的问题可能需要在提示之外取得进展 单独的，包括明确奖励弃权的培训目标，改进的模型 将“知道何时不回答”视为一种校准和评估标准 一流的能力。Despite these limitations, the results point to a clear and actionable conclusion. Prompt-based guardrails can meaningfully reduce premature closure and offer a low-cost, immediately deployable mechanism for harm reduction. However, they do not eliminate the problem. Addressing premature closure will likely require advances beyond prompting alone, including training objectives that explicitly reward abstention, improved model calibration, and evaluation standards that treat “knowing when not to answer” as a first-class capability.

在临床医学中，知道何时不采取行动与知道该做什么同样重要。 我们的研究结果表明，目前的法学硕士尚未可靠地表现出这种行为，并且 实现这一目标不仅需要重新思考模型的提示方式，还需要重新思考它们如何 进行培训、评估和部署。In clinical medicine, knowing when not to act is as important as knowing what to do. Our findings suggest that current LLMs do not yet reliably exhibit this behavior, and that achieving it will require rethinking not only how models are prompted, but how they are trained, evaluated, and deployed.
