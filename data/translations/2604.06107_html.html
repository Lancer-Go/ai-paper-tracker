摘要Abstract

人工智能 (AI) 的最新进展正在释放数学的变革能力。人工智能有望帮助解决重大开放问题并自主发现新的数学概念。在这篇文章中，我们进一步思考人工智能如何通过开辟一条与数学互补的新途径来开启数学的宏大视野。Recent progress in artificial intelligence (AI) is unlocking transformative capabilities for mathematics. There is great hope that AI will help solve major open problems and autonomously discover new mathematical concepts. In this essay, we further consider how AI may open a grand perspective on mathematics by forging a new route, complementary to mathematical逻辑，logic,理解正式的全球结构to understanding the global structure of formal证明proofs。我们首先根据普遍证明和结构超图提供数学形式结构的草图，并讨论由此提出的有关数学基础结构的问题。然后，我们概述了主要成分，并为能够自动数学发现的人工智能模型提供了一组需要满足的标准。当我们派遣人工智能代理穿越柏拉图式的数学世界时，我们期望它们能够教会我们数学的本质：无论是作为一个整体，还是有利于人类理解的小丝带。或许他们会解答这个老问题：“数学是被发现还是发明的？”我们能否了解这些地区的地形？. We begin by providing a sketch of the formal structure of mathematics in terms of universal proof and structural hypergraphs and discuss questions this raises about the foundational structure of mathematics. We then outline the main ingredients and provide a set of criteria to be satisfied for AI models capable of automated mathematical discovery. As we send AI agents to traverse Platonic mathematical worlds, we expect they will teach us about the nature of mathematics: both as a whole, and the small ribbons conducive to human understanding. Perhaps they will shed light on the old question: "Is mathematics discovered or invented?" Can we grok the terrain of these柏拉图式的世界Platonic worlds?
