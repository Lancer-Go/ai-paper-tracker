ATOM-Bench：操作策略中原子技能和组合泛化的现实基准ATOM-Bench: A Real-World Benchmark for Atomic Skills and Compositional Generalization in Manipulation Policies

摘要Abstract

通才操纵策略越来越多地被提出作为机器人控制的基础模型，但它们在现实世界中的普遍性仍然难以诊断。一项策略可能在已展示的任务上取得成功，但仍然无法执行细粒度的原子技能或在新的任务结构中重新组合学到的技能。我们介绍Generalist manipulation policies are increasingly presented as foundation models for robotic control, but their real-world generalization remains difficult to diagnose. A policy may succeed on demonstrated tasks while still failing to execute fine-grained atomic skills or recombine learned skills in new task structures. We introduceATOM 工作台ATOM-Bench，用于评估操作策略中的原子技能和组合泛化的现实世界基准。 ATOM-Bench 将桌面操作分解为运动原子和指令原子，并包含 30 个原子任务和 24 个跨配对单臂和双臂机器人轨道的保留组合任务。我们收集了 3,000 个用于原子微调的人体演示，并发布了演示数据和评估推出数据，以支持可重复的现实世界评估。策略针对原子任务进行微调，并针对原子技能获取和保留的组合任务进行评估。我们进一步引入原子分数（AS）和组合失败份额（CFS）来区分由弱原子技能引起的失败和由有限组合重用引起的失败。通过对五种代表性操纵策略进行 2,700 次物理部署，我们发现当前的策略可以获得简单的指令基础技能，但仍然难以处理细粒度的运动原子、计数和逻辑过滤。更重要的是，强大的原子性能并不能可靠地转移到保留的组合任务中。 ATOM-Bench 提供了一个诊断测试平台，用于研究故障是否是由电机执行能力弱、指令接地不良或组合重用有限引起的。, a real-world benchmark for evaluating both atomic skills and compositional generalization in manipulation policies. ATOM-Bench factorizes tabletop manipulation into motor atoms and instruction atoms, and contains 30 atomic tasks and 24 held-out compositional tasks across paired single-arm and dual-arm robot tracks. We collect 3,000 human demonstrations for atomic fine-tuning and release both the demonstration data and evaluation rollout data to support reproducible real-world evaluation. Policies are fine-tuned on atomic tasks and evaluated on both atomic skill acquisition and held-out compositional tasks. We further introduce Atomic Score (AS) and Compositional Failure Share (CFS) to distinguish failures caused by weak atomic skills from failures caused by limited compositional reuse. Through 2,700 physical rollouts on five representative manipulation policies, we find that current policies can acquire simple instruction-grounding skills, but still struggle with fine-grained motor atoms, counting, and logical filtering. More importantly, strong atomic performance does not reliably transfer to held-out compositional tasks. ATOM-Bench provides a diagnostic testbed for studying whether failures arise from weak motor execution, poor instruction grounding, or limited compositional reuse.

1 简介Introduction

近期操纵政策Recent manipulation policies(Black et al., 2025; Physical Intelligence et al., 2025b; Wu et al., 2026; Cai et al., 2026; Bi et al., 2025; Ye et al., 2026)越来越多地作为机器人控制的基础模型出现。他们在常见操作、长期指令遵循和多任务学习方面表现出了令人印象深刻的行为。这些系统提出了一种有吸引力的可能性：机器人策略可以获取可重用的物理和语义技能，然后重新组合它们来解决新任务。然而，这一承诺仍然很难在真实的机器人上得到验证。强大的演示、高模拟分数或成功的特定于任务的微调并不一定表明策略已经学习了可组合的技能，而不是任务模板或特定于数据集的规律。are increasingly presented as foundation models for robotic control. They have demonstrated impressive behaviors across common manipulation, long-horizon instruction following, and multi-task learning. These systems suggest an appealing possibility: a robot policy may acquire reusable physical and semantic skills, and then recombine them to solve new tasks. However, this promise remains difficult to verify on real robots. Strong demonstrations, high simulation scores, or successful task-specific fine-tuning do not necessarily show that a policy has learned composable skills rather than task templates or dataset-specific regularities.

现有的基准使这种验证变得困难，揭示了操纵基础模型的评估压力越来越大。模拟基准提供了规模、可控性和快速迭代，但它们以编程方式生成的大型任务分布可以被高容量策略快速吸收，而它们的物理挑战仍然不如现实世界的接触、感知和执行要求严格。因此，高仿真成功率越来越多地为可部署的真实机器人泛化提供了有限的证据。现实世界的评估提供了更有力的证据，但成本昂贵、难以自动化且难以扩展。最近的真实机器人研究取得了重要进展：RoboArenaExisting benchmarks make this verification difficult, revealing a growing evaluation tension for manipulation foundation models. Simulation benchmarks provide scale, controllability, and fast iteration, but their large programmatically generated task distributions can be rapidly absorbed by high-capacity policies, while their physical challenges remain less demanding than real-world contact, perception, and execution. As a result, high simulation success increasingly provides limited evidence of deployable real-robot generalization. Real-world evaluation gives stronger evidence, but it is expensive, difficult to automate, and hard to scale. Recent real-robot efforts make important progress: RoboArena(Atreya et al., 2025)研究分布式成对政策排名，AutoEvalstudies distributed pairwise policy ranking, AutoEval(Zhou et al., 2025b)减少人类对学习成功分类器的参与并重置策略，RoboChallengereduces human involvement with learned success classifiers and reset policies, RoboChallenge(Yakefu et al., 2025)构建可扩展的真实机器人测试的在线评估系统，以及 GM-100builds an online evaluation system for scalable real-robot testing, and GM-100(Wang et al., 2026)通过 100 个注重细节的任务扩展了真实机器人任务的多样性。然而，这些基准主要评估任务套件或排名协议的策略。它们对策略在适应过程中看到的技能提供有限的控制，使得很难确定模型是否已经学习了可重用的原子技能，这些技能可以转移到看不见的组合任务。 ATOM-Bench 通过控制原子训练分布并评估真实机器人上的原子技能获取和保留的组合概括来解决这一诊断差距。expands real-robot task diversity with 100 detail-oriented tasks. However, these benchmarks primarily evaluate policies over task suites or ranking protocols. They provide limited control over the skills a policy has seen during adaptation, making it difficult to determine whether a model has learned reusable atomic skills that can be transferred to unseen compositional tasks. ATOM-Bench targets this diagnostic gap by controlling the atomic training distribution and evaluating both atomic skill acquisition and held-out compositional generalization on real robots.

3.1 任务分解和任务组Task Factorization and Task Groups

ATOM-Bench 使用原子作为受控真实机器人评估的实用抽象。 我们通过总结先前操作基准中重复出现的任务因素来选择原子ATOM-Bench uses atoms as a practical abstraction for controlled real-robot evaluation. The selected atoms are common in manipulation tasks, expose different physical or semantic failure modes, can be reliably instantiated on real robots, and can be recombined into held-out compositional tasks. The full taxonomy and design rationale are provided in AppendixE.

我们将这些因素分为两类：We divide these factors into two types:运动原子motor atoms和and指令原子instruction atoms。 电机原子指定任务所需的物理操作，包括拾取和放置、重新定向、推动、堆叠、浇注或铰接式物体访问。 指令原子指定如何用语言描述目标对象或目的地，包括颜色、形状、大小、源关系、目标目的地、计数或排除。 指令原子始终通过可执行的机器人任务进行评估，通常使用拾取和放置或其双手对应物作为载体动作。. Motor atoms specify the physical operation required by the task, including pick-and-place, reorientation, pushing, stacking, pouring, or articulated-object access. Instruction atoms specify how the target object or destination is described in language, including color, shape, size, source relation, goal destination, counting or exclusion. Instruction atoms are always evaluated through executable robot tasks, typically with pick-and-place or its bimanual counterpart as the carrier action.

ATOM-Bench 使用五个指标：成功率 (SR)、流程成功率 (PSR)、原子分数 (AS)、组合失败份额 (CFS) 和转移差距 (TG)。 每个任务都通过 10 个共享物理测试种子进行评估，并且结果是跨任务的宏观平均。ATOM-Bench uses five metrics: Success Rate (SR), Process Success Rate (PSR), Atomic Score (AS), Compositional Failure Share (CFS), and Transfer Gap (TG). 4 实验Experiments

我们设计实验来评估两个轴上的通才操纵策略：原子技能获取和组合概括，在共享的现实世界适应协议下。我们的实验回答了四个问题： (1) 当前的政策在真实机器人上获得原子运动和指令技能的效果如何？ (2) 这些获得的原子技能如何很好地组合成看不见的悬而未决的任务？ (3) 当前政策的主要失败模式是什么？ （4）原子传输微调与单任务微调相比如何？We design our experiments to evaluate generalist manipulation policies on two axes: atomic skill acquisition and compositional generalization, under a shared real-world adaptation protocol. Our experiments answer four questions: (1) How well do current policies acquire atomic motor and instruction skills on real robots? (2) How well do those acquired atomic skills compose into unseen held-out tasks? (3) What are the dominant failure modes of current policies? (4) How does atomic-transfer fine-tuning compare with single-task fine-tuning?

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 ATOM-Bench，这是一个用于评估操作策略中的原子技能和组合泛化的现实基准。 ATOM-Bench 包含 30 个原子任务和 24 个跨配对单臂和双臂轨道的保留组合任务，从而能够诊断电机执行、指令基础和组合重用。通过对五种代表性策略的 2,700 次物理推广，我们发现当前策略可以获得一些有用的原子技能，特别是简单的指令基础技能。 然而，需要从集合中选择对象（例如计数和排除）的细粒度运动原子和指令原子仍然很困难，并且强大的原子性能不能可靠地转移到保留的组合任务。 我们希望 ATOM-Bench 帮助社区更好地了解操纵策略失败的地方，并支持在更可靠的现实世界推广方面取得进展。We introduced ATOM-Bench, a real-world benchmark for evaluating atomic skills and compositional generalization in manipulation policies. ATOM-Bench contains 30 atomic tasks and 24 held-out compositional tasks across paired single-arm and dual-arm tracks, enabling diagnosis of motor execution, instruction grounding, and compositional reuse. Through 2,700 physical rollouts on five representative policies, we find that current policies can acquire some useful atomic skills, especially simple instruction-grounding skills. However, fine-grained motor atoms and instruction atoms that require selecting objects from a set, such as counting and exclusion remain difficult, and strong atomic performance does not reliably transfer to held-out compositional tasks. We hope ATOM-Bench helps the community better understand where manipulation policies fail and supports progress toward more reliable real-world generalization.

6 局限性Limitations

ATOM-Bench 受到真实机器人评估和模型微调成本的限制。 我们评估了五种代表性策略，但并未涵盖所有可用的 VLA 和 WAM 模型。 我们的任务套件侧重于可以在真实机器人上可靠实例化的常见桌面操作因素，因此不涵盖许多长尾技能，例如可变形物体操作、工具使用、移动操作或高度长视野的任务。 此外，评估仍然是劳动密集型的：每次部署都需要场景设置、安全监控和手动评分点注释。 未来的工作将扩大任务覆盖范围，并探索更多自动化重置、成功检测和注释工具，以扩展现实世界的诊断评估。ATOM-Bench is limited by the cost of real-robot evaluation and model fine-tuning. We evaluate five representative policies, but do not cover all available VLA and WAM models. Our task suite focuses on common tabletop manipulation factors that can be reliably instantiated on real robots, and therefore does not cover many long-tail skills such as deformable-object manipulation, tool use, mobile manipulation, or highly long-horizon tasks. In addition, evaluation remains labor-intensive: each rollout requires scene setup, safety monitoring, and manual score-point annotation. Future work will extend the task coverage and explore more automated reset, success detection, and annotation tools to scale real-world diagnostic evaluation.
