摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地用于根据自然语言问题描述制定优化模型，但实际的运筹学 (OR) 请求通常是不完整的：缺少目标、约束或业务规则可能会改变生成的数学程序。现有的评估很大程度上假设了一个完整的规范，因此忽略了代理是否知道在建模之前何时需要澄清。 我们推出 OR-Clarify，这是一种制剂前澄清的基准。每个任务都提供部分公共问题描述，保留结构化隐藏槽，并通过与模拟用户的有界交互来评估代理。该基准支持开放式和基于选择的澄清，并测量槽恢复、停止行为、沉默假设和交互成本。 我们进一步提出了交互式优化（InterOPT），这是一个两阶段框架，可识别未解决的配方关键差距，并使用它们来指导是否提出下一个问题或停止。在我们基于选择的实验中，InterOPT 在精确时隙恢复方面远远优于所有基线；在开放式环境中，它与先前的强大方法相比仍然具有竞争力。 OR-Clarify 和 InterOPT 共同将 OR 援助重新定义为选择性完整性决策：需要时澄清，准备好时停止，并量化仍然缺失的内容。Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used to formulate optimization models from natural-language problem descriptions, yet realistic operations research (OR) requests are often incomplete: missing objectives, constraints, or business rules can change the resulting mathematical program. Existing evaluations largely assume a complete specification and therefore overlook whether an agent knows when clarification is needed before modeling. We introduce OR-Clarify, a benchmark for pre-formulation clarification. Each task presents a partial public problem description, withholds structured hidden slots, and evaluates agents through bounded interaction with a simulated user. The benchmark supports both open-ended and choice-based clarification, and measures slot recovery, stopping behavior, silent assumptions, and interaction cost. We further propose Interactive Optimization (InterOPT), a two-stage framework that identifies unresolved formulation-critical gaps and uses them to guide whether to ask the next question or to stop. In our choice-based experiments, InterOPT substantially outperforms all baselines in exact slot recovery; in the open-ended setting, it remains competitive with strong prior methods. Together, OR-Clarify and InterOPT reframe OR assistance as a selective completeness decision: clarify when needed, stop when ready, and quantify what remains missing.

1. 简介1. Introduction

运筹学 (OR) 模型的结构（包括其目标函数、约束、决策变量和可行区域）由业务问题规范确定。 借助大型语言模型 (LLM)，非专家现在可以用自然语言描述问题并获得候选数学公式The structure of an operations research (OR) model—including its objective function, constraints, decision variables, and feasible region—is determined by the business problem specification. With large language models (LLMs), non-experts can now describe problems in natural language and receive a candidate mathematical formulation[15, 18]，降低 OR 建模的障碍。 然而，现实世界的业务请求很少以完整的教科书式规范的形式提供。 用户可以在不指定目标的情况下描述容量和需求，或者在不明确时间窗口是硬的还是软的情况下陈述路由规则。 这些遗漏并不是表面的：它们可以从根本上改变问题的数学结构。, lowering the barrier to OR modeling. However, real-world business requests are rarely delivered as complete textbook-like specifications. A user may describe capacities and demands without specifying the objective, or state a routing rule without clarifying whether the time windows are hard or soft. These omissions are not superficial: they can fundamentally alter the mathematical structure of the problem.

此设置中的中心故障模式是The central failure mode in this setting is过早制定premature formulation。 大多数基于 LLM 的优化代理的评估都假设有足够的规范并衡量代理是否可以解决或表达给定的模型. Most evaluations of LLM-based optimization agents assume a sufficient specification and measure whether the agent can solve or express a given model[1, 7]，忽略了先前的问题，即现有信息是否支持有意义的表述。 我们发现，强大的法学硕士代理人经常在核心业务事实仍不清楚的情况下宣布准备就绪，或者默默地用不受支持的默认值填充缺失的事实。 例如，在车辆路线上下文中，如果用户忽略了快递员是否必须返回原点，则默默地假定封闭行程的代理会更改约束结构而不要求确认。, missing the earlier question of whether the available information supports a meaningful formulation. We find that strong LLM agents often declare readiness while core business facts remain unclarified, or silently fill missing facts with unsupported defaults. For example, in a vehicle-routing context, if a user omits whether a courier must return to the origin, an agent that silently assumes a closed tour changes the constraint structure without asking for confirmation.

最近的交互式手术室系统已经开始纳入用户交互。或飞行员Recent interactive OR systems have begun to incorporate user interaction. ORPilot[20]在端到端建模流程中使用访谈，而 Drossman 等人。uses interviews within an end-to-end modeling pipeline, while Drossman et al.[6]通过迭代解决方案细化来研究利益相关者效用的会话优化。总而言之，这些研究证明了交互在 OR 问题解决中的价值。然而，交互是嵌入在更广泛的交互过程中的，其有效性并不是孤立的作为一个明确的评估目标。特别是，目前尚不清楚代理是否真正恢复了配方关键缺失的要求，以及它是否知道何时获得了足够的信息来继续进行。这些功能很重要，因为未解决的需求可能会改变最终的优化公式。因此，我们将配制前澄清作为一个独特的研究问题进行研究，并针对该问题开发了专门的评估框架和澄清方法。study conversational optimization through iterative solution refinement toward stakeholder utility. Taken together, these studies demonstrate the value of interaction in OR problem solving. However, interaction is embedded within a broader interactive process, and its effectiveness is not isolated as an explicit evaluation target. In particular, it remains unclear whether an agent actually recovers formulation-critical missing requirements and whether it knows when enough information has been obtained to proceed. These capabilities matter because unresolved requirements can change the resulting optimization formulation. We therefore study pre-formulation clarification as a distinct research problem and develop both a dedicated evaluation framework and clarification methods tailored to this problem.

7. 结论7. Conclusion

我们引入了 OR-Clarify（运筹学中预先阐明的基准）和 InterOPT（一个两阶段框架，可保持表述间隙以指导提问和停止）。 OR-Clarify 评估代理是否可以在建模之前恢复对配方至关重要的隐藏需求，同时还审核交互成本、停止行为和沉默假设。We introduced OR-Clarify, a benchmark for pre-formulation clarification in operations research, and InterOPT, a two-stage framework that maintains formulation gaps to guide questioning and stopping. OR-Clarify evaluates whether agents can recover formulation-critical hidden requirements before modeling, while also auditing interaction cost, stopping behavior, and silent assumptions.

根据经验，InterOPT 在选择环境中最有效，其中结构化差距引导澄清可以提高比无培训基线的精确恢复。在开放/自由形式的环境中，它仍然具有竞争力，但并没有统一主导强大的基线，这表明它的收益并没有统一转移到自由形式的澄清。消融进一步表明差距跟踪和指导行动选择都有助于恢复。Empirically, InterOPT is most effective in the Choice setting, where structured gap-guided clarification improves exact recovery over training-free baselines. In the open/free-form setting, it remains competitive but does not uniformly dominate strong baselines, showing that its gains do not transfer uniformly to free-form clarification. Ablations further indicate that both gap tracking and guided action selection contribute to recovery.

这些结果也暴露了开放的挑战。 InterOPT 可能需要更多的交互，停止仍然不完美，并且没有消除沉默的假设。未来的工作应该扩大基准，提高问题效率，并开发更好的停止校准。总体而言，优化代理不仅应该在生成模型之后进行评估，还应该在建模之前进行评估：评估它们是否知道要问什么、何时询问以及规范何时足够完整以进行建模。These results also expose open challenges. InterOPT can require more interaction, stopping remains imperfect, and silent assumptions are not eliminated. Future work should expand the benchmark, improve question efficiency, and develop better stopping calibration. Overall, optimization agents should be evaluated not only after they produce a model, but also before modeling: on whether they know what to ask, when to ask, and when the specification is complete enough to model.
