QGP 作为运行可靠性。QGP as operational reliability.

Agents often find or complete valid units, but lose the run by repeating accepted work, stopping before the target count, or failing to use verifier feedback to choose the next unit. QGP makes this gap measurable: success requires accumulated verifier-accepted progress, not a plausible final claim or a handful of correct local steps. 10 结论Conclusion

我们引入了定量目标持久性，这是一种用于评估长期代理是否持续存在直到某个目标的公式。 显式定量完成不变量在外部得到满足。 我们的实验表明，高计数持久性 即使对于更强大的黑匣子前沿代理安全带来说，这仍然是不平凡的。 我们希望 QGP 式的指标成为代理评估的标准部分，通过直接衡量代理是否保留验证的进度直到请求的工作实际完成来补充端到端任务的成功。We introduced Quantitative Goal Persistence, a formulation for evaluating whether long-horizon agents persist until an explicit quantitative completion invariant is externally satisfied. Our experiments suggest that high-count persistence remains nontrivial even for stronger black-box frontier-agent harnesses. We hope QGP-style metrics become a standard part of agent evaluation, complementing end-to-end task success with a direct measure of whether agents preserve verified progress until the requested work is actually complete.

11 局限性Limitations

这项工作有五个主要局限性。This work has five main limitations.

  1. 1.

    首先，QGP-RepoScan 和 QGP-DataOps-lite 是受控的 QGP 基准测试，而不是通用软件代理 排行榜。所包含的模型测试持久性故障在更强的情况下是否仍然可见 政策。它们旨在隔离受控条件下的定量持久性，而不是对通用代理进行排名或评估其整体软件工程能力。First, QGP-RepoScan and QGP-DataOps-lite are controlled QGP benchmarks, not general software-agent leaderboards. The included models test whether persistence failures remain visible under stronger policies. They are designed to isolate quantitative persistence under controlled conditions, not to rank general-purpose agents or estimate their overall software-engineering ability.

  2. 2.

    其次，任务覆盖面仍然有限。 QGP-RepoScan使用三个存储库和36个任务 实例，而 QGP-DataOps-lite 使用公共数据片段和存储库派生的固定装置。更广泛的 在对软件或软件做出声明之前需要存储库、数据源和任务类型 一般数据代理工作负载。Second, the task coverage is still limited.
