1 简介Introduction

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 RVSD，这是一种免训练、即插即用的解码框架，首次解决了现有稀疏解码器中先前被忽视的稀疏化-幻觉悖论。通过将语义引导的标记选择与 SSVR 机制集成在单个解码过程中，RVSD 将不可逆的视觉标记修剪转变为可逆且按需的跨模态检索，从而仅在必要时才恢复视觉基础。对三个 LVLM 主干网的五个代表性基准进行的广泛实验表明，RVSD 在 VH 缓解方面实现了 SOTA 性能，同时提高了解码效率并在长上下文生成下保持鲁棒性，而现有的稀疏和基于解码的方法通常会恶化。In this paper, we have proposed RVSD, a training-free and plug-and-play decoding framework that, for the first time, resolves the previously overlooked sparsification-hallucination paradox in existing sparse decoders. By integrating semantics-directed token selection with the SSVR mechanism within a single decoding pass, RVSD transforms irreversible visual token pruning into reversible and on-demand cross-modal retrieval, enabling visual grounding to be restored only when necessary. Extensive experiments on five representative benchmarks across three LVLM backbones demonstrate that RVSD achieves SOTA performance in VH mitigation while simultaneously improving decoding efficiency and maintaining robustness under long-context generation, where existing sparse and decoding-based methods often deteriorate.

对于未来的工作，我们将把 RVSD 扩展到更具挑战性的多模态设置，例如长格式视频理解，其中扩展的时间依赖性和更丰富的模态交互进一步加剧了稀疏-幻觉悖论。我们希望这项工作为实现可信且高效的 LVLM 推理提供一个有希望的方向。For future work, we will extend RVSD to more challenging multimodal settings, such as long-form video understanding, where extended temporal dependencies and richer modality interactions further exacerbate the sparsification-hallucination paradox. We hope this work provides a promising direction toward trustworthy and efficient LVLM inference.

局限性Limitations

虽然 RVSD 显示出有希望的结果，但仍然存在一些局限性。首先，SSVR 中的不确定性触发检索门依赖于预定义的熵阈值来确定何时应激活跨模态检索。尽管 RVSD 在合理的阈值范围内保持稳健，但根据输入复杂性动态调整触发器的更具适应性的门控策略可以进一步减少不必要的检索调用并提高更简单查询的效率。While RVSD shows promising results, several limitations remain. Firstly, the uncertainty-triggered retrieval gate in SSVR relies on a predefined entropy threshold to determine when cross-modal retrieval should be activated. Although RVSD remains robust across a reasonable range of threshold values, a more adaptive gating strategy that dynamically adjusts the trigger according to input complexity can further reduce unnecessary retrieval calls and improve efficiency on simpler queries.

此外，当前的实现使用简单的点积相似性从延迟视觉记忆库中检索证据。更先进的检索机制，例如学习的语义投影或多头跨模式注意力，可以进一步提高细粒度任务的性能。尽管我们的评估已经涵盖了两个不同的架构系列，即 LLaVA 系列和 Qwen-VL，但我们还计划将 RVSD 扩展到更大规模和更异构的 LVLM，以进一步评估其通用性。Additionally, the current implementation uses a simple dot-product similarity to retrieve evidence from the deferred visual memory bank. More advanced retrieval mechanisms, such as learned semantic projections or multi-head cross-modal attention, may further improve performance on fine-grained tasks. Although our evaluation already spans two distinct architectural families, i.e., the LLaVA series and Qwen-VL, we also plan to extend RVSD to larger-scale and more heterogeneous LVLMs to further evaluate its generality.

道德考虑Ethical Considerations

这项工作旨在通过减少 VH 来提高 LVLM 的可信度，从而有助于更安全地部署多模态人工智能系统。 RVSD 是一个免训练且仅推理的框架，不需要额外的数据收集或人工注释。本研究中使用的所有数据集（即 POPE、MME、AMBER、CHAIR、MM-Vet、MSCOCO、A-OKVQA 和 GQA）和骨干模型（LLaVA-1.5、LLaVA-NEXT 和 Qwen-VL）均可供公开用于学术研究，并严格按照其原始许可使用。虽然 RVSD 大大减少了 VH，但并没有消除它们。因此，我们建议在将其部署到医疗诊断或辅助技术等安全关键型应用之前，将其与下游验证或人工监督配对。This work aims to improve the trustworthiness of LVLMs by mitigating VHs, thereby contributing to the safer deployment of multimodal artificial intelligence systems. RVSD is a training-free and inference-only framework that requires no additional data collection or human annotation. All datasets (i.e., POPE, MME, AMBER, CHAIR, MM-Vet, MSCOCO, A-OKVQA, and GQA) and backbone models (LLaVA-1.5, LLaVA-NEXT, and Qwen-VL) used in this study are publicly available for academic research and are used strictly in accordance with their original licenses. While RVSD substantially reduces VHs, it does not eliminate them. Therefore, we recommend pairing it with downstream verification or human oversight before deployment in safety-critical applications such as medical diagnosis or assistive technologies.

B.2 基线Baselines

我们将 RVSD 与跨越三种范式的四种代表性免训练解码器进行比较。所有基线均遵循推荐的超参数，并在与 RVSD 相同的解码预算下进行评估，以进行公平比较。We compare RVSD against four representative training-free decoders spanning three paradigms. 光盘VCD（视觉对比解码）(Visual Contrastive Decoding)(Leng et al., 2024)通过添加扩散式高斯噪声引入扰动的视觉输入，并在干净和损坏的视觉条件之间执行对比解码，从而抑制单峰语言先验。introduces a perturbed visual input by adding diffusion-style Gaussian noise and performs contrastive decoding between the clean and corrupted visual conditions, thereby suppressing unimodal language priors.
