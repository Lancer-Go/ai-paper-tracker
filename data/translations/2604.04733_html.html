摘要Abstract

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 尽管在多模态基准上取得了出色的性能，但经常会误解人类轻松识别的简单视觉概念，例如计数、空间推理和视点理解。之前的研究Vision-language Models (VLMs), despite achieving strong performance on multimodal benchmarks, often misinterpret straightforward visual concepts that humans identify effortlessly, such as counting, spatial reasoning, and viewpoint understanding. Previous studies[29, 9]手动识别这些弱点并发现它们通常源于特定技能的缺陷。然而，这种手动工作成本高昂、不可扩展，并且容易受到人为偏见的影响，往往会为了突出的对象而忽略微妙的细节，从而导致对模型漏洞的不完全理解。为了解决这些限制，我们提出了一种基于强化学习（RL）的框架，可以自动发现给定数据分布上任何“候选 VLM”的故障模式或盲点，无需人工干预。我们的框架训练一个提问者代理，该代理根据候选 VLM 的响应自适应地生成查询，以引出错误的答案。随着训练的进行，我们的方法通过关注细粒度的视觉细节和独特的技能组合来增加问题的复杂性，从而识别出 VLM 难以应对的 36 种新颖的故障模式。我们通过展示其在各种模型组合中的通用性来证明我们的框架的广泛适用性。manually identified these weaknesses and found that they often stem from deficits in specific skills. However, such manual efforts are costly, unscalable, and subject to human bias, which often overlooks subtle details in favour of salient objects, resulting in an incomplete understanding of a model’s vulnerabilities. To address these limitations, we propose a Reinforcement Learning (RL)-based framework to automatically discover the failure modes or blind spots of any “candidate VLM” on a given data distribution without human intervention. Our framework trains a questioner agent that adaptively generates queries based on the candidate VLM’s responses to elicit incorrect answers. Our approach increases question complexity by focusing on fine-grained visual details and distinct skill compositions as training progresses, consequently identifying 36 novel failure modes in which VLMs struggle. We demonstrate the broad applicability of our framework by showcasing its generalizability across various model combinations.

1 简介Introduction

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 已展现出通过回答人类查询来推理视觉世界的卓越能力Vision Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated remarkable ability to reason about the visual world by answering human queries[23, 1]。这些能力归功于强大的视觉编码器的有效集成. These capabilities are attributed to the effective integration of strong visual encoders[25, 35]大型语言模型 (LLM)with Large Language Models (LLMs)[20, 4]，使他们能够解决复杂的任务。尽管 VLM 在多模式基准测试中表现出色，但它很容易出现人类很少犯的错误。例如，他们无法理解观点和观点, enabling them to solve complex tasks. Despite their impressive performance on multimodal benchmarks, VLMs are prone to errors that humans rarely make. For example, they fail to understand viewpoints and perspectives[29]、幻觉物体存在, hallucinate object existence[17]，并与低级视觉概念作斗争, and struggle with low-level visual concepts[26].

识别 VLM 的故障模式是一个活跃的研究领域， 过去的研究已成功识别出故障模式的子集。例如，一项研究发现，VLM 很难完成涉及方向、观点和视角的任务Identifying failure modes of VLMs is an active area of research, where past research has successfully identified a subset of failure modes. For instance, one study found that VLMs struggle with tasks involving orientation, viewpoint, and perspective[29]，另一个人指出了深度感知、反射和多视图推理方面的挑战, and another identified challenges in depth perception, reflectance, and multi-view reasoning[9]。尽管这些研究很有价值，但由于两个原因而受到限制。首先，他们手动设计故障模式识别基准，这既费时又费力。其次，人类在发现此类故障模式方面发挥着重要作用，由于人类的认知偏差，这限制了已发现故障模式的覆盖范围. Despite their value, these studies are limited for two reasons. First, they manually design benchmarks for failure mode identification, which is time-consuming and labour-intensive. Second, humans play a major role in discovering such failure modes, which limits the coverage of discovered failure modes due to their cognitive biases[3]。为了克服这些挑战，最近的工作利用法学硕士作为提问者，自动生成用于探索 VLM 的问题和数据集. To overcome these challenges, recent work leverages LLMs as questioners to automatically generate questions and datasets for probing VLMs[24]。这些方法提供了一个可扩展的解决方案，因为法学硕士可以生成多样化的高质量查询，而不会像人类那样受到疲劳或认知偏差的限制。然而，他们将提问者视为静态组件。这些系统采用“开环”生成，其中提问者不会适应候选 VLM 的响应。此外，发现的故障模式是通用的，无法捕捉特定模型故障模式的细微差别。. These methods offer a scalable solution, as LLMs can generate diverse, high-quality queries that are not limited by fatigue or cognitive biases, as humans are. However, they treat the questioner as a static component. These systems employ “open-loop” generation, in which the questioner does not adapt to the candidate VLM’s responses. Moreover, the discovered failure modes are generic and do not capture the nuances of a specific model’s failure modes.

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了一个基于强化学习的框架，用于自动发现 VLM 中的故障模式。与之前需要大量手动干预的方法不同，我们的方法使用基于 VLM 的验证器完全自动地训练提问者代理。我们的实验表明，经过强化学习训练的提问者在各种指标上的表现都远远优于竞争基线。特别是，SFT 微调的 RL 变体产生的问题引发了大约 100% 的问题。目标 VLM 的故障比次佳基准多 13%。它还确定了 36 项以前的工作和其他方法未能识别的新技能，同时在生成的问题中保持了显着的词汇和语义多样性。We introduced an RL-based framework for automatically discovering failure modes in VLMs. Unlike previous approaches that require significant manual intervention, ours trains a questioner agent fully automatically using a VLM-based verifier. Our experiments demonstrate that the RL-trained questioner substantially outperforms competitive baselines across various metrics. In particular, the SFT fine-tuned RL variant generated questions that elicited approx. 13% more failures from the target VLM than the next-best baseline. It also identified 36 new skills that previous work and other approaches failed to identify, while maintaining significant lexical and semantic diversity in the generated questions.

我们的分析进一步表明，随着时间的推移，基于强化学习的方法会产生越来越复杂的问题，从更简单的问题开始。我们还观察到，在利用模型的基本漏洞后，一些较新的技能是在训练后期发现的。这些发现概括了模型规模和架构。与静态基准不同，我们的框架动态地针对给定 VLM 的特定弱点，为发现特定于模型的故障模式提供可扩展的替代方案。Our analysis further revealed that the RL-based approaches generate increasingly complex questions over time, starting with simpler ones. We also observed that some of the newer skills are discovered later in the training, after exploiting the model’s basic vulnerabilities. These findings generalize across model scales and architectures. Unlike static benchmarks, our framework dynamically targets the specific weaknesses of a given VLM, offering a scalable alternative for discovering model-specific failure modes.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and Future Work.虽然我们的框架有效地发现了故障模式，但仍有几个方向尚待解决。首先，当前的方法侧重于单图像、单轮交互；扩展到多轮对话或视频理解可能会揭示更多的失败模式。其次，将发现的故障模式整合到微调管道中可以闭合从诊断到补救的循环。最后，扩展框架以探测更大的专有模型仍然是全面 VLM 评估的一个重要挑战。总之，我们的工作代表了从静态、手动策划的基准到自适应、强化学习驱动的故障发现的范式转变。通过将 VLM 评估转变为持续学习问题，我们能够系统地识别原本隐藏的认知盲点，最终有助于开发更强大、更可靠的视觉语言系统。While our framework effectively discovers failure modes, several directions remain open. First, the current approach focuses on single-image, single-turn interactions; extending to multi-turn dialogues or video understanding could reveal additional failure patterns. Second, integrating the discovered failure modes into fine-tuning pipelines could close the loop from diagnosis to remedy. Finally, scaling the framework to probe larger proprietary models remains an important challenge for comprehensive VLM evaluation. In summary, our work represents a paradigm shift from static, manually curated benchmarks toward adaptive, RL-driven failure discovery. By transforming VLM evaluation into a continual learning problem, we enable systematic identification of cognitive blind spots that would otherwise remain hidden, ultimately contributing to the development of more robust and reliable vision-language systems.
