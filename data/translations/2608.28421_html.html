Supervised fine-tuning adjusts weights toward demonstrated behaviour; reinforcement learning adjusts them toward rewarded outcomes(Guo et al., 2025)。两者都存储在中学到的内容 它已经存储在同一位置，并继承该选择的三个属性。 获得的能力是不透明的：权重中没有任何内容说明了哪一步 多步骤任务模型现在表现更好。正在进行中无法验证： 轨迹只有完成后才能判断。并且它绑定到一个模型： 12B 检查点学习到的能力不会转移到 30B 检查点 重复该过程。. Both store what was learned in the same place it was already stored, and inherit three properties of that choice. The acquired capability is opaque: nothing in the weights says which step of a multi-step task the model now performs better. It is unverifiable in progress: a trajectory can only be judged once complete.
