摘要Abstract

区块链平台上模因币的快速扩散增加了欺诈活动的风险，特别是地毯拉动。虽然之前的研究主要集中在基于以太坊的代币上，但本文将焦点转向了 Solana，它是交易量和代币数量领先的 memecoin 区块链。与以太坊不同，地毯拉动通常利用智能合约后门，Solana memecoin 地毯拉动主要由流动性操纵和社会动态驱动。这项研究通过在 7 个月内收集了包含 640 万个代币的数据集，开创了 Solana 生态系统中大规模早期检测的先河。市场分析显示，绝大多数模因币在发布后一小时内表现出拉动特征，凸显了短期预测的紧迫性。尽管缺乏代码级功能，我们证明了经典的机器学习模型，特别是梯度提升 (XGBoost)，仅使用前 5 分钟的交易数据即可在检测潜在的拉动方面实现稳健的性能。此外，我们评估了 PumpFun 和 Raydium 之间的跨平台泛化，表明多源数据融合显着减轻了域转移并提高了检测可靠性。这项研究增进了对高吞吐量链上 DeFi 欺诈的理解，并为保护投资者提供了实用的框架。The rapid proliferation of memecoins on blockchain platforms has increased the risk of fraudulent activities, particularly rug pulls. While previous studies have focused on Ethereum-based tokens, this paper shifts the spotlight to Solana, the leading blockchain for memecoins by trading volume and token count. Unlike Ethereum, where rug pulls often exploit smart contract backdoors, Solana memecoin rug pulls are predominantly driven by liquidity manipulation and social dynamics. This research pioneers large-scale rug pull early detection in the Solana ecosystem by assembling a dataset of 6.4 million tokens over 7 months. Market analysis reveals that a vast majority of these memecoins exhibit rug pull characteristics within one hour of launch, highlighting the urgency of short-horizon prediction. Despite the absence of code-level features, we demonstrate that classic machine learning models, particularly Gradient Boosting (XGBoost), achieve robust performance in detecting potential rug pulls using only the first 5 minutes of trading data. Furthermore, we evaluate cross-platform generalization between PumpFun and Raydium, revealing that multi-source data fusion significantly mitigates domain shift and improves detection reliability. This study advances the understanding of DeFi fraud on high-throughput chains and provides a practical framework for protecting investors.

I 简介Introduction

区块链技术、Web3创新和去中心化金融（DeFi）的快速发展极大地推动了加密货币市场的扩张The rapid advancement of blockchain technology, Web3 innovations, and decentralized finance (DeFi) has significantly fueled the expansion of the cryptocurrency market[31]。到2024年5月，加密货币市值突破2万亿美元. By May 2024, the market capitalization of cryptocurrencies surpassed 2 trillion US dollars[14]，凸显了这些技术的深远影响。为了支持代币交易并提供点对点交易所的替代方案，出现了各种交易平台和加密货币交易所。这些平台分为中心化交易所（CEX）, highlighting the profound influence of these technologies. To support token transactions and provide alternatives to peer-to-peer exchanges, various trading platforms and cryptocurrency exchanges have emerged. These platforms are categorized into centralized exchanges (CEXs)[28]，其运作方式与传统金融机构和去中心化交易所（DEX）类似, which operate similarly to traditional financial institutions, and decentralized exchanges (DEXs)[17]，利用智能合约和密码学方法保证资产安全。, which utilize smart contracts and cryptographic methods to ensure asset security.

DEX 活动的增加对 memecoin 市场产生了重大影响。 2024 年，memecoin 成为加密货币世界的主导趋势，虽然没有产生最大的总体交易量，但推动了 DEX 的大量活动The rise in DEX activity has significantly impacted the memecoin market. In 2024, memecoins emerged as a leading trend in the crypto world, not generating the largest trade volume overall, but driving substantial activity on DEXs[13]。 Memecoin 是一种加密资产，通常作为笑话或利用互联网上的 meme 和趋势而创建. Memecoins are a type of crypto asset often created as a joke or to capitalize on memes and trends on the Internet[34]。与比特币或以太坊等具有特定用例和底层技术的传统加密货币不同，模因币通常缺乏内在的实用性或严肃的目的。相反，它们通过社交网络、社区参与和病毒式营销而受到欢迎。现代模因币是部署在已建立的区块链（例如 Solana、以太坊）上的代币，而不是具有专用共识层的本机代币。然而，这种增长导致了欺诈行为的增加，例如拉地毯. Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin or Ethereum, which have specific use cases and underlying technologies, memecoins typically lack inherent utility or serious purpose. Instead, they gain popularity through social networks, community engagement, and viral marketing. Modern memecoins are tokens deployed on established blockchains (e.g., Solana, Ethereum) rather than native coins with dedicated consensus layers. However, this growth has led to an increase in fraudulent schemes, such as rug pulls[9, 43]，开发商推广代币以吸引投资者，然后撤回流动性，导致代币价值暴跌。 DEX 的去中心化性质和用户匿名性进一步使此类计划的检测变得复杂。随着 DeFi 的不断扩展，开发有效的方法来检测 Solana 和其他区块链上的拉动对于保护用户和维持对生态系统的信任至关重要。, where developers promote a token to attract investors and then withdraw liquidity, causing the token’s value to plummet. The decentralized nature of DEXs and user anonymity further complicate the detection of such schemes. As DeFi continues to expand, developing effective methods to detect rug pulls on Solana and other blockchains is crucial to safeguard users and maintain trust in the ecosystem.

去中心化交易所的强势对投资者信心和市场稳定构成重大威胁。目前，DEX 上缺乏实时检测的专门工具，使得欺诈者可以利用此漏洞。因此，迫切需要能够在潜在的地毯拉动发生之前识别它们的机制Rug pulls on decentralized exchanges pose a significant threat to investor confidence and market stability. Currently, there is a lack of specialized tools for real-time rug pull detection on DEXs, allowing fraudsters to exploit this vulnerability. Consequently, there is an urgent need for mechanisms capable of identifying potential rug pulls before they occur[29, 33].

II 背景：Solana 与以太坊 MemecoinBackground: Solana vs Ethereum Memecoins

Memecoin 已成为加密货币领域的重要组成部分，其特点是融合了幽默、投机吸引力和社区参与度Memecoins have emerged as a significant segment within the cryptocurrency landscape, characterized by their blend of humor, speculative appeal, and community engagement[42]。本节探讨了 Solana 和以太坊这两个支持 memecoin 的著名区块链平台的社区和技术生态系统的对比。. This section explores the contrasting community and technological ecosystems of Solana and Ethereum, two prominent blockchain platforms that support memecoins.

Solana memecoin 本质上受益于平台的高吞吐量和低交易成本，吸引了对快速且具有成本效益的交易感兴趣的交易者Solana memecoins inherently benefit from the high throughput and low transaction costs of the platform, attracting traders interested in rapid and cost-effective transactions[39]。相比之下，以太坊模因币利用了该平台已建立的网络效应和广泛的用户群。以太坊区块链. In contrast, Ethereum memecoins leverage the platform’s established network effects and extensive user base. The Ethereum blockchain[6]已转变为权益证明共识并采用模块化架构，利用了既定的网络效应。尽管这种方法提供了灵活性和独立可升级性，但它引入了层协调的复杂性，可能会影响用户体验。, which has transitioned to a proof-of-stake consensus and adopted a modular architecture, leverages established network effects. Although this approach offers flexibility and independent upgradability, it introduces complexity in layer coordination, potentially affecting user experience.

这两个生态系统的一个关键问题是地毯拉动的普遍存在A critical issue in both ecosystems is the prevalence of rug pulls[29]是开发商在获得投资者资金后放弃项目的一种欺诈行为。以太坊强大的 DeFi 基础设施以及通过 ERC-20 标准轻松发行代币, a type of fraud in which developers abandon a project after securing investor funds. Ethereum’s robust DeFi infrastructure and the ease of launching tokens via the ERC-20 standard[35, 15]使其成为此类诈骗的主要目标。以太坊模因币，例如 Shiba Inu (SHIB) 和 Pepe (PEPE)，由于社区支持和战略营销而获得了巨大的关注。尽管以太坊上的智能合约主要用 Solidity 编写，可以实现去中心化、去信任化和透明的交易，但它们需要强大的安全措施（例如白名单）来管理访问并减少欺诈风险。have made it a prime target for such scams. Ethereum memecoins, such as Shiba Inu (SHIB) and Pepe (PEPE), have gained significant traction due to community support and strategic marketing. Although smart contracts on Ethereum, primarily written in Solidity, enable decentralized, trustless, and transparent transactions, they require robust security measures such as whitelisting to manage access and reduce fraud risks.

区块链技术将易于使用的可审计性引入数字系统Blockchain technology has introduced easy-to-use auditability into digital systems[5]。然而，为了充分受益，智能合约的源代码应该开源。虽然大多数 DeFi 应用程序都遵循这一原则，但以太坊上的许多 memecoin 却没有。尽管智能合约的字节码始终是公开可用的并且通常可以进行逆向工程. However, to fully benefit from this, the source code of smart contracts should be open source. While most DeFi applications adhere to this principle, many memecoins on Ethereum do not. Although the bytecode of the smart contract is always publicly available and can often be reverse-engineered[16, 3]在某些著名的骗局案例中，研究人员有时无法找到源代码，这给以太坊 memecoin 生态系统蒙上了阴影。, researchers sometimes fail to find the source code in certain notable scam cases, casting a shadow on the Ethereum memecoin ecosystem.

This ensures proper access control within the contract, providing a layer of security against potential fraud.

IV-C 滚动时间序列交叉验证Rolling Time Series Cross-Validation

训练集和测试集的构建直接影响模型评估的可靠性。由于地毯拉力检测是一项依赖于时间的任务，随机分区或标准 K 折交叉验证会引入前瞻偏差，使模型在训练过程中暴露于未来市场信息，并导致评估结果过于乐观。因此，本研究采用基于代币发行时间的前向滚动窗口交叉验证，保留最后一个时间窗口作为独立的测试集。The construction of training and test sets directly impacts the reliability of model evaluation. Since Rug Pull detection is a time-dependent task, random partitioning or standard K-fold cross-validation introduces look-ahead bias, exposing the model to future market information during training and leading to overly optimistic evaluation results. Therefore, this study employs forward rolling window cross-validation based on token issuance time, reserving the last time window as an independent test set.

使用时间划分的另一个原因是，Rug Pull欺诈表现出概念漂移特征，其链上特征随着市场不断变化。与混合不同时间段数据的标准 K 折交叉验证相比，固定长度滚动窗口仅利用最近 3 个月的数据进行训练。Another reason for using time partitioning is that Rug Pull fraud exhibits concept drift characteristics, with its on-chain features constantly changing with the market. Compared to standard K-fold cross-validation, which mixes data from different time periods, a fixed-length rolling window utilizes only the most recent 3 months of data for training.

此外，由于Rug Pulls是小众类赛事，本研究实施了正样本保证机制。当验证集中的正样本数量不足时，自动扩大验证窗口，保证每个验证折叠至少包含5个Rug Pull样本，从而保证评估结果的统计显着性。Furthermore, because Rug Pulls are a minority class event, this study implements a positive sample guarantee mechanism. When the number of positive samples in the validation set is insufficient, the validation window is automatically expanded to ensure that each validation fold contains at least 5 Rug Pull samples, thereby guaranteeing the statistical significance of the evaluation results.

这种划分方法遵循“历史训练，未来验证”的流程，避免数据泄露，保证最终测试集仅用于一次性评估，从而提高实验结果的可信度。This partitioning method follows the process of "historical training, future verification" to avoid data leakage and ensure that the final test set is used only for one-time evaluation, thereby improving the credibility of the experimental results.
