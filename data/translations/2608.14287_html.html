战场场景中的声学无人机检测：处理噪声、域转移和弱标签Acoustic UAV Detection in Battlefield Scenarios: Handling Noise, Domain Shift, and Weak Labels

Vadym Vilhurin1,2, Volodymyr Sydorskyi1,2,3, Andrii Shevtsov2,3 Affiliation: 1Institute for Applied System Analysis, Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute, Kyiv, Ukraine Affiliation: 2Zvook, Lviv, Ukraine Affiliation: 3Computer Science Department, Kyiv School of Economics, Kyiv, Ukraine
摘要Abstract

被动声学传感为检测小型无人机提供了一种关键的、经济高效的、最重要的是被动的替代方案。然而，极端环境噪声和异构硬件引起的传感器引起的域转移阻碍了声学系统的实际部署。本文通过引入针对现实战场条件优化的强大框架来解决这些挑战。我们提出将每通道能量归一化（PCEN）和基于注意力的池化相结合，以增强低信噪比场景下的特征提取。我们进一步提出了一种领域感知训练策略，利用辅助类和多麦克风数据来减轻跨域性能下降。通过对乌克兰前线战区记录的独特数据集进行评估，我们的方法显着优于现有基线，将 F1 分数从 55.4% 提高到 78.6%。Passive acoustic sensing offers a critical, cost-efficient, and, crucially, passive alternative for detecting small unmanned aerial vehicles. However, the practical deployment of acoustic systems is discouraged by extreme environmental noise and sensor-induced domain shift caused by heterogeneous hardware. This paper addresses these challenges by introducing a robust framework optimized for real-world battlefield conditions. We propose the integration of Per-Channel Energy Normalization (PCEN) and attention-based pooling to enhance feature extraction under low signal-to-noise ratio scenarios. We further propose a domain-aware training strategy that leverages auxiliary classes and multi-microphone data to mitigate cross-domain performance degradation. Evaluated on a unique dataset of combat-zone recordings from the Ukrainian frontlines, our approach significantly outperforms existing baselines, increasing the F1 score from 55.4% to 78.6%.

I 简介Introduction

小型无人机 (UAV) 的快速发展和大规模部署对军事和安全关键环境中的可靠检测提出了越来越大的挑战。在战场和争议地区场景中，无人机对关键基础设施、人员安全和操作保密构成直接威胁。在以强环境干扰、杂乱和异构传感硬件为特征的现实条件下进行早期和稳健的检测对于估计无人机轨迹、识别作战区域和采取及时对策至关重要The rapid development and mass deployment of small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) pose a growing challenge for reliable detection in military and security-critical environments. In battlefield and contested-area scenarios, UAVs represent a direct threat to critical infrastructure, personnel safety, and operational secrecy. Early and robust detection under realistic conditions characterized by strong environmental interference, clutter, and heterogeneous sensing hardware is essential for estimating UAV trajectories, identifying operational zones, and enabling timely countermeasures[8, 38].

人们已经探索了多种无人机检测方法，包括使用 RGB 或热图像的基于视觉的方法A wide range of approaches have been explored for UAV detection, including vision-based methods using RGB or thermal imagery[15, 17, 3], 射频 (RF) 传感, radio-frequency (RF) sensing[27, 38]，以及基于雷达或红外的系统, and radar- or infrared-based systems[5]。虽然这些技术在受控环境中有效，但在实际操作环境中面临着重大限制。基于视觉的检测需要清晰的视线、良好的照明、大量的存储和计算资源，这使得大规模部署变得复杂。基于射频的方法依赖于通信协议的先验知识，并且容易受到战场环境中常见的其他射频发射设备的干扰。红外和雷达系统需要专门的、昂贵的硬件，并且可能受到环境或操作因素的限制。 相比之下，声学无人机检测是被动的、低成本的、与协议无关的，并且在很大程度上不受视觉遮挡的影响，这使得它对于杂乱和有争议的环境中的早期检测特别有吸引力。这些特性在战场场景中尤其有价值，其中隐身、快速部署和对部分遮挡的鲁棒性至关重要. While effective in controlled settings, these techniques face significant limitations in real operational environments. Vision-based detection requires clear line-of-sight, favorable lighting, large storage and computational resources, which complicates large-scale deployment. RF-based methods depend on prior knowledge of communication protocols and are vulnerable to interference from other RF-emitting devices common in battlefield settings. Infrared and radar systems require specialized, costly hardware and may be constrained by environmental or operational factors. In contrast, acoustic UAV detection is passive, low-cost, protocol-agnostic, and largely invariant to visual occlusion, making it particularly attractive for early detection in cluttered and contested environments. These properties are especially valuable in battlefield scenarios, where stealth, rapid deployment, and robustness to partial occlusion are critical[38, 27].

尽管有这些优点，实用的声学无人机检测仍然具有挑战性。现实环境中包含来自风、车辆、机械和人类活动的强烈且高度可变的背景噪声，这些噪声可能会掩盖无人机特征并显着降低检测性能Despite these advantages, practical acoustic UAV detection remains challenging. Real-world environments contain strong and highly variable background noise from wind, vehicles, machinery, and human activity, which can obscure UAV signatures and significantly degrade detection performance[8]。此外，由于传感器频率响应、放置和环境条件的变化，从不同麦克风和部署位置收集的声学数据通常会表现出显着的域偏移. Moreover, acoustic data collected from different microphones and deployment locations often exhibit substantial domain shift, driven by variations in sensor frequency response, placement, and environmental conditions[2]。这些挑战因操作领域的严重数据稀缺而进一步加剧，其中通常只有有限的标记记录可用。. These challenges are further compounded by severe data scarcity in the operational domain, where only limited labeled recordings are typically available.

II 军事应用的原理Rationale to Military Application

声学无人机探测器为现代小型无人机传感问题提供有效且高效的解决方案。作为无源系统，它们不易受到无线电干扰或电子对抗的影响，从而增强了它们在竞争环境中的可靠性和隐身性。这使得它们在以无人机为中心的俄罗斯-乌克兰战争或北约边境监视的真实战争场景中特别有价值，它们可以集成到针对无人机群的多层防御中。它们的低成本和易于大规模生产进一步使其能够在大规模战场行动中部署。此外，声学传感器可在中短程距离内提供高性能和准确性。Acoustic UAV detectors offer effective and efficient solutions to the modern small UAV sensing problem. As passive systems, they are far less susceptible to radio jamming or electronic countermeasures, enhancing their reliability and stealth in contested environments. This makes them particularly valuable in real warfare scenarios of drone-centric Russia-Ukraine war, or NATO’s border surveillance, where they can integrate into a multi-layered defense against drone swarms. Their low cost and ease of mass production further enable deployment in large-scale battlefield operations. Additionally, acoustic sensors deliver high performance and accuracy at short- to medium-range distances.

在声学探测中，域转移缓解对于维持现代战争中的作战有效性至关重要。由于环境条件随季节变化，并且新的无人机模型引入了新颖的声学特征，为每种可能的场景收集大量数据集变得资源密集型且不切实际In acoustic detection, domain shift mitigation is essential for maintaining operational effectiveness in modern warfare. As environmental conditions fluctuate with seasons and new UAV models introduce novel acoustic signatures, gathering vast datasets for every possible scenario becomes resource-intensive and impractical[2]。未解决的域转移可能会导致严重故障，例如漏检或误报，尤其是在声音模仿等对抗性策略下。因此，任何基于机器学习的军事系统都必须从一开始就适应不断变化的条件，确保动态和高风险战区的弹性。. Unaddressed domain shifts could result in critical failures, such as missed detections or false alarms, especially under adversarial tactics like sound mimicry. Therefore, any machine learning-based military system must incorporate adaptability to changing conditions from the outset, ensuring resilience in dynamic and high-stakes theaters.

As a result, current literature does not fully capture the challenges of real-world UAV monitoring, where models must generalize across heterogeneous sensors and operate reliably with very limited operational data.

在低信噪比场景下，标准池化策略可能会导致破坏性聚合：高能量低频噪声可能与无人机信号重叠，从而有效掩盖了判别性特征。为了解决这个问题，我们删除了广义均值（GeM）频率池层，并将其替换为作为卷积块实现的可学习频率投影仪，并沿频率维度应用卷积。与会破坏频谱分辨率的池化不同，此投影保留了卷积频率结构，并使模型能够学习非线性、加权的频段组合。In low-SNR scenarios, the standard pooling strategy can lead to destructive aggregation: high-energy low-frequency noise may overlap with the UAV signal, effectively masking discriminative features. To address this issue, we remove the Generalized Mean (GeM) frequency pooling layer and replace it with a learnable frequency projector implemented as a convolutional block, with convolutions applied along the frequency dimension. Unlike pooling, which collapses spectral resolution, this projection preserves convolved frequency structure and enables the model to learn non-linear, weighted combinations of frequency bands.

对于多类分类，我们删除For multiclass classification, we remove the正值tanh来自注意机制的约束，允许无限制的分数来锐化时间焦点。我们还在聚合输出上将最终的 sigmoid 替换为按类的 softmax，以强制类之间的互斥性。constraint from the attention mechanism, allowing unbounded scores that sharpen temporal focus. We also replace the final sigmoid with a class-wise softmax on the aggregated output to enforce mutual exclusivity between classes.

IX 结论Conclusion

跨异构记录设备的声学无人机检测在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索。我们提出了一个领域感知声学分类框架，结合了 PCEN 表示、基于注意力的池化和噪声驱动的课程增强。针对多个开源和专有基线的评估表明，跨设备泛化和检测准确性得到了持续改进。Acoustic UAV detection across heterogeneous recording devices remains largely underexplored. We proposed a domain-aware acoustic classification framework combining PCEN representations, attention-based pooling, and noise-driven curriculum augmentations. Evaluations against multiple open-source and proprietary baselines have shown consistent improvements in cross-device generalization and detection accuracy.

尽管取得了这些进步，但仍有一些改进的途径：Despite these advancements, several avenues for refinement remain:

  • •

    当前模型将无人机视为一个整体类别。开发细粒度的分类法（例如区分 DJI Mavics 等多旋翼无人机和高速 FPV 无人机）对于应对战术问题至关重要。Current models treat UAVs as a monolithic class. Developing a fine-grained taxonomy (e.g., distinguishing between multi-rotors like DJI Mavics and high-speed FPV drones) is essential for facing tactical problems.

  • •

    我们的方法以独立的片段处理音频。整合顺序预测的信息可以提高检测稳定性并降低误报率。Our approach processes audio in independent segments. Integrating information from sequential predictions could improve detection stability and reduce false-positive rates.

  • •

    缺乏明确的空间数据（距离和高度）限制了我们执行详细错误分析和性能报告的能力。The lack of explicit spatial data (distance and altitude) limits our ability to perform detailed error analysis and performance reporting.

因此，未来的研究应重点关注：Consequently, future research should focus on:

  • •

    将其他元数据纳入数据集中，包括距离指标、无人机类型和其他相关属性。Incorporating additional metadata into the dataset, including distance metrics, UAV type, and other relevant attributes.

  • •

    实现内存模块或循环架构以利用声学流中的长期依赖性。Implementing memory modules or recurrent architectures to leverage long-term dependencies in acoustic streams.

  • •

    探索基于 Transformer 的主干和自监督预训练，以更好地利用大规模未标记声学数据。Exploring Transformer-based backbones and self-supervised pre-training to better utilize large-scale unlabeled acoustic data.

  • •

    开发半监督管道，以快速使模型适应标记数据稀缺的新前线位置。Developing semi-supervised pipelines to rapidly adapt models to new frontline locations where labeled data is scarce.
