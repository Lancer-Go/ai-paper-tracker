通过视觉重新校准和实体重建进行免培训隧道缺陷检测和工程解释Training-Free Tunnel Defect Inspection and Engineering Interpretation via Visual Recalibration and Entity Reconstruction

Shipeng Liu lsp@xauat.edu.cn [    Liang Zhao zhaoliang@xauat.edu.cn [    Dengfeng Chen chdengf@xauat.edu.cn [    Zhanping Song songzhpyt@xauat.edu.cn [
摘要Abstract

隧道检查需要能够支持缺陷定位、测量、严重程度分级和工程文档的输出。现有的免训练基础模型管道通常停留在粗略的开放词汇建议上，很难直接在干扰严重的隧道场景中使用。我们提出了一个免训练框架 TunnelMIND。具体来说，语言引导的缺陷建议不被视为最终输出；相反，它们的空间支持在推理时通过密集的视觉一致性进行重新校准，以便粗略的语义锚可以在特定于隧道的硬负例下转化为更可靠的提示。生成的掩模进一步被重建为具有类别、位置、几何形状、严重性和上下文属性的结构化缺陷实体，然后在专家知识约束下映射到基于检索的解释和工程可读的报告生成。在可见光、GPR 和道路缺陷任务上，TunnelMIND 的 F1 分数分别为 0.68、0.78 和 0.72。总体而言，TunnelMIND 表明，免培训隧道检查可以超越粗略定位，转向用于工程评估的结构化缺陷证据。Tunnel inspection requires outputs that can support defect localization, measurement, severity grading, and engineering documentation. Existing training-free foundation-model pipelines usually stop at coarse open-vocabulary proposals, which are difficult to use directly in interference-heavy tunnel scenes. We propose a training-free framework TunnelMIND. Specifically, language-guided defect proposals are not treated as final outputs; instead, their spatial support is recalibrated at inference time through dense visual consistency, so that coarse semantic anchors can be transformed into more reliable prompts under tunnel-specific hard negatives. The resulting masks are further reconstructed into structured defect entities with category, location, geometry, severity, and context attributes, which are then mapped to retrieval-grounded explanation and engineering-readable report generation under expert knowledge constraints. On visible, GPR, and road defect tasks, TunnelMIND achieves F1 scores of 0.68, 0.78, and 0.72, respectively. Overall, TunnelMIND shows that training-free tunnel inspection can move beyond coarse localization toward structured defect evidence for engineering assessment.

1 简介Introduction

隧道检查需要的不仅仅是类别级别的认可。在实际的工程工作流程中，检查输出预计会表明可能存在什么缺陷、缺陷的位置、缺陷的大小以及其严重程度是否足以需要进一步关注。这一要求使得隧道检查与一般视觉任务有根本的不同。即使模型可以指示潜在的相关区域，如果空间支撑不稳定、边界不可靠，或者结果无法转换为可测量和可追溯的缺陷记录以供后续评估、维护计划和检查记录，其输出仍然可能不适合工程使用Tunnel inspection requires more than category-level recognition. In practical engineering workflows, inspection outputs are expected to indicate what defect may be present, where it is located, how large it is, and whether its severity is sufficient to require further attention. This requirement makes tunnel inspection fundamentally different from generic vision tasks. Even when a model can indicate potentially relevant regions, its outputs may still be unsuitable for engineering use if the spatial support is unstable, the boundaries are unreliable, or the results cannot be converted into measurable and traceable defect records for subsequent evaluation, maintenance planning, and inspection documentationcho2026bim; ou2025towards; attard2017image.

此类要求贯穿隧道工程的整个生命周期。在施工过程中，需要视觉感知来完成开挖面观察、岩渣评估、工人安全监控、设备合规性检查等任务Such requirements arise throughout the tunnel engineering lifecycle. During construction, visual perception is needed for tasks such as excavation-face observation, rock slag assessment, worker safety monitoring, and equipment compliance checkingshi2014advance; vukicevic2022generic。在运营和维护过程中，必须以支持状态评估和后续干预的形式检测和解释可见的衬砌缺陷、探地雷达（GPR）发现的隐藏缺陷以及路面损坏. During operation and maintenance, visible lining defects, hidden defects revealed by ground-penetrating radar (GPR), and road-surface damage must be detected and interpreted in a form that supports condition assessment and follow-up interventionou2025towards。在这些场景中，隧道图像经常受到密集硬负片的影响，包括阴影、结构接缝、污渍、反射和重复纹理。这些干扰不仅仅降低视觉清晰度。更重要的是，它们使得很难将真正的缺陷与视觉上相似的背景响应区分开来，这直接限制了工程量化和记录生成的可靠性. Across these scenarios, tunnel images are frequently affected by dense hard negatives, including shadows, structural joints, stains, reflections, and repetitive textures. These disturbances do not merely reduce visual clarity. More importantly, they make it difficult to distinguish true defects from visually similar background responses, which directly limits the reliability of engineering quantification and record generationsjolander2023towards; zhang2024reactive.

当特定任务注释足够并且部署条件保持相对稳定时，最近的监督深度学习方法在隧道裂缝检测、渗流分割、探地雷达缺陷解释、路面损坏检测和安全相关监测方面取得了强大的性能Recent supervised deep learning methods have achieved strong performance on tunnel crack detection, seepage segmentation, GPR defect interpretation, pavement damage detection, and safety-related monitoring when task-specific annotations are sufficient and deployment conditions remain relatively stablezhou2024tunnel; feng2023deep; zhao2025multimodal。然而，仍然存在三个实际限制。首先，收集和精细注释隧道数据的成本很高，特别是当必须涵盖不同的隧道断面、施工阶段、传感方式和成像条件时。其次，生成的模型通常与训练分布紧密耦合，如果不进一步进行再训练，就很难转移到新地点、新任务或新采集条件。第三，每个任务一个模型的范式在工程部署中越来越难以维护，其中检查需求在施工和运营阶段不断变化，但仍然需要连贯且可重用的技术接口. However, three practical limitations still remain. First, collecting and finely annotating tunnel data is expensive, especially when different tunnel sections, construction stages, sensing modalities, and imaging conditions must all be covered. Second, the resulting models are often tightly coupled to the training distribution, making transfer to new sites, new tasks, or new acquisition conditions difficult without further retraining. Third, the one-model-per-task paradigm is increasingly difficult to maintain in engineering deployment, where inspection needs evolve across construction and operation stages but still require a coherent and reusable technical interfacesu2024review.

基础模型和多模态大型模型为这种传统工作流程提供了一种有前途的替代方案。及时的细分模型Foundation models and multimodal large models provide a promising alternative to this conventional workflow. Promptable segmentation modelskirillov2023segment、自监督密集视觉编码器和视觉语言模型在有限甚至零特定任务优化下表现出了强大的可迁移性。对于隧道工程来说，这个方向很有吸引力，因为它可以减轻收集特定于任务的注释的负担，同时提高跨任务的部署灵活性。然而，当前的免训练管道通常停留在粗略的开放词汇基础上, self-supervised dense visual encoders, and vision-language models have demonstrated strong transferability under limited or even zero task-specific optimization. For tunnel engineering, this direction is attractive because it may reduce the burden of collecting task-specific annotations while increasing deployment flexibility across tasks. However, current training-free pipelines often stop at coarse open-vocabulary groundingzhang2024vision; myers2024foundation。在隧道场景中，这还不够。语言引导模型可能会返回合理的缺陷类别或大致相关的区域，但仍然无法提供测量、位置分配、严重程度分级和工程文档所需的空间可靠性。换句话说，关键的瓶颈不仅仅是模型能否发现可疑区域，而是粗略的提案能否转换为稳定的、可测量的、可追溯的缺陷实体进行工程解释。. In tunnel scenes, this is not enough. A language-guided model may return a plausible defect category or a roughly relevant region, yet still fail to provide the spatial reliability needed for measurement, location assignment, severity grading, and engineering documentation. In other words, the key bottleneck is not merely whether a model can find suspicious regions, but whether coarse proposals can be converted into stable, measurable, and traceable defect entities for engineering interpretation.

这个瓶颈在干扰严重的隧道环境中变得尤其重要。阴影可能类似于裂缝或渗漏边界，施工缝可能会与结构缺陷混淆，污渍和污染可能会引发误报，重复的隧道纹理可能会导致建议漂移或重复。类似的困难也出现在弱语义模式中，例如 GPR，其中异常反应在视觉上是有意义的，但并不总是与明确的语言描述自然地一致。因此，直接将视觉语言模型的输出作为最终的检测结果往往不足以满足工程应用的需要。我们需要的是一种中间机制，它可以保留免训练感知的语义灵活性，同时提高结果输出的空间可靠性和工程可解释性。This bottleneck becomes especially important in interference-heavy tunnel environments. Shadows may resemble cracks or seepage boundaries, construction joints may be confused with structural defects, stains and contamination may trigger false positives, and repetitive tunnel textures may cause proposal drift or duplication. Similar difficulties also arise in weak-semantic modalities such as GPR, where abnormal responses are visually meaningful but not always naturally aligned with explicit language descriptions. As a result, directly treating the output of a vision-language model as the final inspection result is often insufficient for engineering use. What is needed is an intermediate mechanism that can preserve the semantic flexibility of training-free perception while improving the spatial reliability and engineering interpretability of the resulting outputs.

2 相关工作Related work

2.1 隧道视觉感知Tunnel visual perception

隧道建设和维护涉及广泛的视觉感知任务，包括开挖面分析、岩渣识别、个人防护装备合规性监控、工人行为理解以及运营过程中裂缝、渗漏、剥落和路面损坏等可见缺陷的检测Tunnel construction and maintenance involve a wide range of visual perception tasks, including excavation-face analysis, rock slag recognition, PPE compliance monitoring, worker action understanding, and the detection of visible defects such as cracks, seepage, spalling, and pavement damage during operationvukicevic2022generic; yu2024road。最近的评论表明，光学隧道检测必须共同考虑缺陷的可观测性、成像条件和面向评估的数据采集，而不是将隧道缺陷视为孤立的识别目标. Recent reviews have shown that optical tunnel inspection must jointly consider defect observability, imaging conditions, and assessment-oriented data acquisition, rather than treating tunnel defects as isolated recognition targetssjolander2023towards。大多数现有研究将这些问题表述为特定任务的监督学习任务，并通过专用的检测、分割或实例分割模型来解决它们. Most existing studies formulate these problems as task-specific supervised learning tasks and address them with dedicated detection, segmentation, or instance segmentation modelszhao2020deep; zhou2024tunnel; feng2023deep。例如，可见缺陷检测通常使用 YOLO 来解决. For example, visible defect detection is commonly tackled with YOLOredmon2016you,DETR, DETRcarion2020end或相关的基于检测器的框架，而面向区域的目标（例如裂缝和渗漏）通常由 U-Net 处理, or related detector-based frameworks, while region-oriented targets such as cracks and seepage are often handled by U-Netronneberger2015u具有注意力、功能融合或轻量级设计的变体。对于隐藏的隧道缺陷，探地雷达（GPR）解译也逐渐从人工检测转向基于深度学习的自动识别和分割variants with attention, feature fusion, or lightweight designs. For hidden tunnel defects, ground-penetrating radar (GPR) interpretation has also gradually shifted from manual inspection to deep learning-based automatic recognition and segmentationzhao2025multimodal.

当注释足够、场景条件受控并且任务边界明确定义时，这些方法可以实现强大的性能。然而，它们的有效性通常依赖于丰富的标记数据和相对稳定的部署环境。在隧道工程中，随着时间的推移，这种假设很难维持，因为不同地点和阶段的地质条件、照明布置、施工方法和摄像机视角差异很大。此外，将多任务感知分解为多个孤立的模型会导致部署、维护和升级成本增加。因此，现有的监督隧道检测方法更适合单任务优化，而不是跨阶段、跨站点和不断变化的工程检测需求These approaches can achieve strong performance when annotations are sufficient, scene conditions are controlled, and task boundaries are clearly defined. However, their effectiveness typically relies on abundant labeled data and relatively stable deployment environments. In tunnel engineering, this assumption is difficult to maintain over time because geological conditions, lighting arrangements, construction methods, and camera viewpoints vary substantially across sites and stages. In addition, decomposing multi-task perception into multiple isolated models leads to increasing deployment, maintenance, and upgrade costs. As a result, existing supervised tunnel inspection methods are more suitable for single-task optimization than for cross-stage, cross-site, and continuously evolving engineering inspection demandssu2024review; zhang2024reactive.

2.2 感知基础模型Foundation models for perception

Beyond generic promptable segmentation, subsequent studies such as SEEM and Semantic-SAM further extended the idea toward more universal and semantically aware segmentation interfaces, broadening the range of prompt types and granularity levels that can be handled within a unified frameworkzou2023segment; li2024segment。自监督视觉表示模型提供具有强可迁移性的密集特征，可以支持区域匹配、相似性检索和推理时间约束，而无需显式任务监督. Self-supervised visual representation models provide dense features with strong transferability, which can support region matching, similarity retrieval, and inference-time constraints without explicit task supervisionchen2023mixed; oquab2023dinov2。早期的无监督发现方法（例如 LOST 和 TokenCut）进一步表明，自监督 Transformer 特征已经可以仅通过特征一致性来支持无标签定位和区域分组，即使没有特定于任务的训练. 这些进展表明，轻训练甚至无需训练的感知正在变得可行。然而，在隧道环境中，有两个限制仍然很重要。首先，隧道图像包含密集的硬底片，例如接缝、阴影、污点和重复纹理，因此语言对齐的开放词汇提案很容易出现误报、漏检和重复。其次，工程应用不仅需要语义发现，还需要稳定的空间边界和可测量的几何属性，以支持长度、面积、位置和严重性估计等下游分析。因此，当前的基础模型提供了开放词汇感知和基于提示的分割的潜力，但仍然缺乏将语义建议转换为工程就绪实体的可靠中间步骤。These advances suggest that training-light, or even training-free, perception is becoming feasible. However, two limitations remain critical in tunnel environments. First, tunnel imagery contains dense hard negatives such as joints, shadows, stains, and repetitive textures, so language-aligned open-vocabulary proposals are prone to false positives, missed detections, and duplicates. Second, engineering applications require not only semantic discovery but also stable spatial boundaries and measurable geometric attributes to support downstream analysis such as length, area, location, and severity estimation. Current foundation models therefore provide the potential for open-vocabulary perception and prompt-based segmentation, but still lack a reliable intermediate step that converts semantic proposals into engineering-ready entities.

2.3 用于检查的视觉语言模型Vision-language models for inspection

视觉语言模型和多模态大型模型进一步降低了构建复杂视觉系统的障碍Vision-language models and multimodal large models further lower the barrier to building complex visual systemswu2023multimodal; zhang2024vision。与传统的特定任务模型相比，这些方法使用自然语言作为统一界面，可以从类别短语或任务描述生成候选区域、坐标或结构化响应。对于隧道场景，任务多种多样，需求变化频繁，注释昂贵，这种语言驱动的候选生成特别有吸引力. Compared with conventional task-specific models, these methods use natural language as a unified interface and can generate candidate regions, coordinates, or structured responses from category phrases or task descriptions. For tunnel scenarios, where tasks are diverse, requirements change frequently, and annotations are expensive, such language-driven candidate generation is particularly appealingyang2025qwen3.

然而，统一的接口并不自动意味着工程可靠性。在隧道场景中，主要的挑战通常不是多模态模型是否可以产生一些候选响应，而是该响应在硬负片、低对比度和纹理干扰条件下是否具有足够稳定的空间支持。语言模型可以为当前存在的内容提供语义答案，但它们不一定能提供关于它在哪里以及其边界对于工程量化是否足够可信的可靠答案。因此，面向工程的检查系统不应停留在语言引导的候选生成上，而应进一步将语义锚与密集视觉一致性相结合，以在推理时重新校准和约束开放词汇提案。大多数现有的视觉语言管道仍然将语义基础视为可用定位的几乎直接代理，而工程场景通常需要一个额外的阶段，在几何测量之前将语义线索转换为空间上更稳定的支持。However, a unified interface does not automatically imply engineering reliability. In tunnel scenes, the main challenge is often not whether a multimodal model can produce some candidate response, but whether the response has sufficiently stable spatial support under hard-negative, low-contrast, and texture-interference conditions. Language models may provide a semantic answer to what is present, but they do not necessarily provide a reliable answer to where it is and whether its boundary is trustworthy enough for engineering quantification. Therefore, an engineering-oriented inspection system should not stop at language-guided candidate generation, but should further combine semantic anchors with dense visual consistency to recalibrate and constrain open-vocabulary proposals at inference time. Most existing vision-language pipelines still treat semantic grounding as an almost direct proxy for usable localization, whereas engineering scenarios often require an additional stage that converts semantic cues into spatially more stable support before geometric measurement.

2.4 基于检索的报告协助Retrieval-grounded report assistance

除了感知本身之外，工程部署还需要将模型输出转换为检查解释、风险解释和工程可读的报告。检索增强生成的核心思想是将参数生成与显式非参数记忆结合起来，从而提高知识密集型任务中的事实基础、出处和可更新性Beyond perception itself, engineering deployment also requires model outputs to be converted into inspection explanations, risk interpretation, and engineering-readable reporting. The core idea of retrieval-augmented generation is to couple parametric generation with explicit non-parametric memory, thereby improving factual grounding, provenance, and updateability in knowledge-intensive taskslewis2020retrieval; gao2023retrieval。近年来，基于检索的生成和知识约束推理越来越多地应用于特定领域的报告辅助. Their basic idea is to map structured observations or language queries to retrieval requests, recall relevant evidence from standards, manuals, reports, and technical documents, and then generate constrained summaries and recommendations with the support of retrieved evidence. For infrastructure inspection and structural health monitoring, the value of such methods lies not in replacing professional engineers, but in improving traceability, consistency, and standard alignment during the explanation process.

然而，现有的研究通常集中于视觉识别或基于知识的问答，而对它们之间的中间表示的关注有限。如果感知输出保持在方框、掩模或自由格式文本的水平，则很难将它们与工程文档中的几何阈值、严重性级别和干预条件保持一致。因此，从实际部署的角度来看，关键步骤不仅仅是将视觉结果输入到大型语言模型（LLM）中However, existing studies often focus on either visual recognition or knowledge-based question answering, with limited attention to the intermediate representation between them. If perception outputs remain at the level of boxes, masks, or free-form text, it is difficult to align them with geometric thresholds, severity levels, and intervention conditions in engineering documents. From the perspective of practical deployment, the key step is therefore not simply to feed visual results into a large language model (LLM)chang2024survey，而是首先将感知输出转换为具有类别、位置、几何和严重性属性的结构化工程实体，然后在检索约束下生成基于证据的解释和咨询建议。, but to first convert perception outputs into structured engineering entities with category, location, geometry, and severity attributes, and then generate evidence-grounded explanations and advisory suggestions under retrieval constraints.

2.5 研究差距Research gap

总之，之前的工作从三个相关但仍然不相关的方向推进了隧道检查。当注释足够并且部署条件保持相对稳定时，特定于任务的监督模型可以提供很高的准确性。基础模型和开放词汇方法可实现轻训练候选生成和基于提示的分割。基于检索的推理进一步为证据感知解释和报告生成提供了一个有前景的界面。然而，对于以密集的硬负片、弱边界和不断变化的任务要求为特征的隧道检查场景，三个差距仍然没有得到充分解决。In summary, prior work has advanced tunnel inspection from three related but still disconnected directions. Task-specific supervised models provide strong accuracy when annotations are sufficient and deployment conditions remain relatively stable. Foundation models and open-vocabulary methods enable training-light candidate generation and prompt-based segmentation. Retrieval-grounded reasoning further offers a promising interface for evidence-aware explanation and report generation. However, for tunnel inspection scenarios characterized by dense hard negatives, weak boundaries, and evolving task requirements, three gaps remain insufficiently addressed.

首先，存在空间可靠性差距：隧道场景中的语言引导或开放词汇候选者在其空间支持方面通常仍然太不稳定，无法直接用于工程量化。其次，存在实体重建差距：当前的免训练输出通常停留在框、掩码或自由格式描述上，而工程工作流程需要具有类别、位置、几何形状、严重性和上下文属性的结构化实体。第三，存在感知与报告之间的差距：基于检索的解释方法通常假设标准化的结构化观测结果已经可用，但很少解决如何以免训练的方式直接从原始隧道图像生成此类观测结果。数字First, there is a spatial reliability gap: language-guided or open-vocabulary candidates in tunnel scenes often remain too unstable in their spatial support to be used directly for engineering quantification. Second, there is an entity reconstruction gap: current training-free outputs usually stop at boxes, masks, or free-form descriptions, while engineering workflows require structured entities with category, location, geometry, severity, and context attributes. Third, there is a perception-to-reporting gap: retrieval-grounded explanation methods typically assume that standardized structured observations are already available, but rarely address how such observations can be produced directly from raw tunnel imagery in a training-free manner. 4 实验Experiments

本节从三个角度评估 TunnelMIND：检查可靠性、工程实体质量和基于检索的报告实用性。由于所提出的框架以跨模型视觉重新校准而不是特定于任务的监督优化为中心，因此实验是围绕机制验证而不是基准枚举来组织的。我们首先报告代表性隧道检查任务的主要结果，然后检查硬负条件下的鲁棒性和跨任务接口可转移性，最后评估以实体为中心的表示和基于检索的报告层是否提高了下游工程的实用性。This section evaluates TunnelMIND from three perspectives: inspection reliability, engineering entity quality, and retrieval-grounded reporting utility. Since the proposed framework is centered on cross-model visual recalibration rather than task-specific supervised optimization, the experiments are organized around mechanism validation instead of benchmark enumeration. We first report the main results on representative tunnel inspection tasks, then examine robustness under hard-negative conditions and cross-task interface transferability, and finally evaluate whether the entity-centric representation and retrieval-grounded reporting layer improve downstream engineering usefulness.

为了在真实的隧道检查条件下评估 TunnelMIND，我们从三个互补的角度设计了实验：代表性任务性能、跨任务接口可转移性和面向工程的下游实用性。我们没有将所有六个任务视为同等权重的基准，而是选择几个代表性任务进行初步评估，并使用其余任务来评估界面级可迁移性。这种设计与这项工作的目标是一致的，即不是为每个任务构建单独的最先进模型，而是检查所提出的免训练管道是否可以跨异构隧道检查设置可靠地将开放词汇提案转换为工程就绪实体。此外，由于隧道图像经常受到阴影、接缝、污渍、重复纹理和低对比度目标的影响，我们进一步构建了一个硬负子集来评估在干扰严重的条件下的鲁棒性。To evaluate TunnelMIND under realistic tunnel inspection conditions, we design the experiments from three complementary perspectives: representative-task performance, cross-task interface transferability, and engineering-oriented downstream utility. 5 讨论Discussion

结果表明，TunnelMIND 提供了一条从免培训视觉基础到面向工程的隧道检查的实用路径。该框架的主要贡献不仅在于它在一个管道中结合了视觉语言模型、DINOv3、SAM 和基于检索的生成，而且在于它在语义发现和工程解释之间引入了一种明确的中间机制。在隧道场景中，语言引导的建议通常在语义上是合理的，但在空间上不稳定，尤其是在阴影、接缝、污渍、反射和重复纹理等硬底片的情况下。跨模型视觉重新校准模块通过将粗语义锚转移到密集的视觉支持空间中并在分割和测量之前用局部视觉一致性约束它们来解决这个问题。代表性任务和硬阴性结果共同表明，如果期望免训练感知超越粗略的开放词汇基础，转向更可用的检查输出，那么这个中间步骤是必要的。The results indicate that TunnelMIND provides a practical path from training-free visual grounding to engineering-oriented tunnel inspection. The main contribution of the framework is not simply that it combines a vision-language model, DINOv3, SAM, and retrieval-grounded generation within one pipeline, but that it introduces an explicit intermediate mechanism between semantic discovery and engineering interpretation. In tunnel scenes, language-guided proposals are often semantically plausible but spatially unstable, especially under hard negatives such as shadows, joints, stains, reflections, and repetitive textures. The cross-model visual recalibration module addresses this problem by transferring coarse semantic anchors into a dense visual support space and constraining them with local visual consistency before segmentation and measurement. The representative-task and hard-negative results jointly show that this intermediate step is necessary if training-free perception is expected to move beyond coarse open-vocabulary grounding toward more usable inspection outputs.

这项工作的第二个意义是，工程可用性不仅取决于目标是否可以粗略定位，还取决于结果是否可以转换为可测量、可追溯和工程兼容的结构化实体。在TunnelMIND中，这是通过将重新校准的分割与以实体为中心的工程重建相结合来实现的，从而可以将类别、结构位置、几何属性和严重性组织成统一的表示。这种设计对于面向缺陷和区域的任务特别有价值，其中下游解释直接取决于长度、面积、位置和分级信息。它还解释了为什么该框架在可见缺陷、探地雷达隐藏缺陷和道路缺陷上比在更依赖于专用结构建模的姿势类任务上显示出更大的价值。从这个角度来看，TunnelMIND 的贡献不仅仅是在免训练环境下改善感知，而是在语义提案和工程记录之间建立了一座可用的桥梁。A second implication of this work is that engineering usability depends not only on whether a target can be roughly localized, but also on whether the result can be converted into measurable, traceable, and engineering-compatible structured entities. In TunnelMIND, this is achieved by combining recalibrated segmentation with entity-centric engineering reconstruction, so that category, structural location, geometric attributes, and severity can be organized into a unified representation. This design is particularly valuable for defect- and region-oriented tasks, where downstream interpretation depends directly on length, area, position, and grading information. It also explains why the framework shows greater value on visible defects, GPR hidden defects, and road defects than on pose-like tasks that rely more heavily on dedicated structural modeling. From this perspective, the contribution of TunnelMIND is not merely improved perception under a training-free setting, but the establishment of a usable bridge between semantic proposals and engineering records.

基于检索的报告组件应该在同一边界内进行理解。它的作用不是自动化工程决策，而是提供面向部署的界面，提高检查输出的可读性、可追溯性和知识一致性。通过使用结构化实体而不是原始框、掩码或自由形式的视觉描述作为检索输入，报告模块能够更好地将缺陷观察与标准、手册和先前案例保持一致。报告质量的实验结果支持了这种解释，但它们仍然应被视为报告效用的比较证据，而不是作为正式工程审查的替代品。换句话说，报告阶段扩展了感知结果的实用价值，但并没有将框架重新定义为自主诊断系统。The retrieval-grounded reporting component should be understood within this same boundary. Its role is not to automate engineering decision-making, but to provide a deployment-facing interface that improves the readability, traceability, and knowledge alignment of inspection outputs. By using structured entities rather than raw boxes, masks, or free-form visual descriptions as retrieval inputs, the reporting module is better able to align defect observations with standards, manuals, and prior cases. The experimental results on report quality support this interpretation, but they should still be viewed as comparative evidence of reporting utility rather than as a substitute for formal engineering review. In other words, the reporting stage extends the practical value of the perception results, but it does not redefine the framework as an autonomous diagnosis system.

同时，当前的框架仍然存在一些局限性。首先，TunnelMIND 不太适合严重重叠、交叉或视觉纠缠的缺陷。在这种情况下，以锚点为中心的检索和基于提示的分割可能会合并相邻实例或无法保留细粒度的分离，这反过来会影响后续的几何量化和严重性评估。其次，当前的探地雷达评估遵循固定的视觉提示协议，每个类别有一个参考范例。尽管这种设计提高了弱语义雷达图像中基线的可比性，但它并没有完全表征对样本选择的敏感性。第三，实际部署仍然需要大量的计算资源，因为系统依赖于按顺序执行的多个冻结基础模型。在当前的硬件设置下，每个图像的感知阶段可以在几秒钟内完成，而下游报告生成占总延迟的很大一部分。因此，目前的框架更适合离线协助检查、起草报告和辅助审查，而不是严格的实时控制。这些限制并不会使所提出的设计无效，但它们澄清了免培训隧道检查的当前部署边界。At the same time, the current framework still has several limitations. First, TunnelMIND is not well suited to heavily overlapping, intersecting, or visually entangled defects. In such cases, anchor-centered retrieval and prompt-based segmentation may merge adjacent instances or fail to preserve fine-grained separation, which in turn affects subsequent geometric quantification and severity assessment. Second, the current GPR evaluation follows a fixed visual prompting protocol with one reference exemplar per category. Although this design improves comparability across baselines in weak-semantic radar imagery, it does not fully characterize sensitivity to exemplar choice. Third, practical deployment still requires nontrivial computational resources because the system depends on multiple frozen foundation models executed in sequence. Under the current hardware setting, the perception stages can be completed within a few seconds per image, whereas downstream report generation contributes a large fraction of the total latency. Therefore, the present framework is better suited to offline inspection assistance, report drafting, and assisted review than to strict real-time control. These limitations do not invalidate the proposed design, but they clarify the current deployment boundary of training-free tunnel inspection.

总体而言，TunnelMIND 应被视为数据稀缺、面向缺陷的隧道检查场景的灵活且面向部署的替代方案，而不是特定于任务的监督模型的通用替代品。它的主要价值在于表明，当语义建议得到密集视觉重新校准和结构化工程重建的补充时，免训练感知变得更加有用。未来的工作将侧重于重叠缺陷条件下更强大的实例解缠，对探地雷达等弱语义模式中的样本敏感性进行更系统的分析，以及提高部署效率的轻量级加速策略。Overall, TunnelMIND should be viewed as a flexible and deployment-oriented alternative for data-scarce, defect-oriented tunnel inspection scenarios, rather than as a universal replacement for task-specific supervised models. Its main value lies in showing that training-free perception becomes substantially more useful when semantic proposals are complemented by dense visual recalibration and structured engineering reconstruction. Future work will focus on more robust instance disentanglement under overlapping-defect conditions, more systematic analysis of exemplar sensitivity in weak-semantic modalities such as GPR, and lightweight acceleration strategies for improving deployment efficiency.

6 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 TunnelMIND，这是一种免培训的隧道检查框架，它将语言引导的语义锚定、跨模型视觉重新校准、基于提示的几何实现和以实体为中心的工程重建集成在统一的检查管道中。 TunnelMIND 没有将视觉语言模型视为最终检测器，而是使用语义建议作为中间锚点，并在密集的 DINOv3 特征空间中细化其空间支持，以便视觉上一致的证据可以转换为更可靠的分割和下游量化提示。然后将生成的掩模组织成结构化工程实体，并进一步链接到基于检索的解释和报告帮助。This paper presented TunnelMIND, a training-free tunnel inspection framework that integrates language-guided semantic anchoring, cross-model visual recalibration, prompt-based geometric realization, and entity-centric engineering reconstruction within a unified inspection pipeline. Instead of treating a vision-language model as a final detector, TunnelMIND uses semantic proposals as intermediate anchors and refines their spatial support in the dense DINOv3 feature space, so that visually consistent evidence can be converted into more reliable prompts for segmentation and downstream quantification. The resulting masks are then organized into structured engineering entities and further linked to retrieval-grounded explanation and report assistance.

对代表性隧道检查任务的实验表明，所提出的框架提高了免训练感知的可用性，在硬负条件下保持更稳健，并为下游解释生成更高质量的工程实体。这些发现表明，免训练隧道检查的主要障碍不仅在于模型是否能够响应开放词汇任务描述，还在于其输出是否可以转化为稳定且可测量的工程结果。 TunnelMIND 通过在语义候选生成和工程解释之间引入显式中间层来解决这一障碍。Experiments on representative tunnel inspection tasks show that the proposed framework improves the usability of training-free perception, remains more robust under hard-negative conditions, and produces higher-quality engineering entities for downstream interpretation. These findings suggest that the main obstacle in training-free tunnel inspection is not only whether a model can respond to open-vocabulary task descriptions, but whether its outputs can be transformed into stable and measurable engineering results. TunnelMIND addresses this obstacle by introducing an explicit intermediate layer between semantic candidate generation and engineering interpretation.

尽管在重叠缺陷分离、弱语义模式中的范例敏感性和部署效率方面仍然存在挑战，但本研究表明，免训练感知可以超越粗略定位，转向更可追溯和工程兼容的隧道检查输出。从这个意义上说，TunnelMIND 为数据稀缺、面向缺陷的隧道检查和面向部署的基于检索的检查辅助提供了实用基础。Although challenges remain in overlapping-defect separation, exemplar sensitivity in weak-semantic modalities, and deployment efficiency, the present study demonstrates that training-free perception can move beyond coarse localization toward more traceable and engineering-compatible tunnel inspection outputs. In this sense, TunnelMIND provides a practical basis for data-scarce, defect-oriented tunnel inspection and deployment-facing retrieval-grounded inspection assistance.
