算法漫画：审计法学硕士在危机事件中生成的政治话语The Algorithmic Caricature: Auditing LLM-Generated Political Discourse Across Crisis Events

Gunjan    Sidahmed Benabderrahmane    Talal Rahwan
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）越来越有能力生成流畅的政治文本，这引发了人们对社会冲突期间大规模生成合成话语的担忧。虽然之前的检测工作经常强调本地语言线索，例如复杂性、突发性或标记级不规则性，但随着生成系统的改进，这些信号可能会变得不太可靠。在本文中，我们采用计算社会科学（CSS）的视角，在人口层面而不是句子层面研究综合政治话语。Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly capable of generating fluent political text, raising concerns about the large-scale production of synthetic discourse during periods of social conflict. While prior detection work has often emphasized local linguistic cues such as perplexity, burstiness, or token-level irregularities, these signals may become less reliable as generative systems improve. In this paper, we adopt a Computational Social Science (CSS) perspective and study synthetic political discourse at the population level rather than the sentence level.

Wade, the 2020 BLM protests, the US midterm elections, the Utah shooting, and the US-Iran war. 在整个事件中，合成话语比观察到的话语更消极，情绪更分散，结构更规则，词汇更抽象。相比之下，观察到的话语表现出更广泛的情感分散、更长尾的结构分布以及更多的上下文特定和口语词汇标记。这些差异在不同事件中并不统一：对于快速发展、分散的危机，这些差异通常较大，而对于正式的、机构调解的事件，差异通常较小。我们通过一个简单的事件级差异度量来总结这些差异，我们将其称为Across events, synthetic discourse is more negative and less dispersed in sentiment, structurally more regular, and lexically more abstract than observed discourse. In contrast, observed discourse exhibits broader emotional dispersion, longer-tailed structural distributions, and more context-specific and colloquial lexical markers. These differences are not uniform across events: they are typically larger for fast-moving, decentralized crises and smaller for formal, institutionally mediated events. We summarize these differences through a simple event-level divergence measure, which we term the漫画差距Caricature Gap.

我们的研究结果表明，综合政治话语的主要限制不是语法流畅性，而是人口现实主义的降低。我们认为，这种人口层面的视角为传统文本检测方法提供了有用的补充，并为审核生成话语的社会现实性提供了 CSS 框架。Our findings suggest that the main limitation of synthetic political discourse is not grammatical fluency, but reduced population realism. We argue that this population-level perspective offers a useful complement to traditional text-detection approaches and provides a CSS framework for auditing the social realism of generated discourse.

1 简介Introduction

社交媒体平台已成为政治表达、动员和争论的核心基础设施Social media platforms have become central infrastructures for political expression, mobilization, and contestation[Hasan and Paul, 2025]。它们也是操纵的关键场所，包括协调影响力行动、错误信息活动和自动放大. They are also key sites of manipulation, including coordinated influence operations, misinformation campaigns, and automated amplification[Orgeret et al., 2026],[Ahmed, 2026]。大型语言模型（LLM）的快速传播在这一领域引发了一个新的担忧：产生大量流畅的、充满政治色彩的合成话语的可能性，这些话语可以大规模模仿草根表达. The rapid diffusion of Large Language Models (LLMs) raises a new concern within this landscape: the possibility of generating large volumes of fluent, politically charged synthetic discourse that can imitate grassroots expression at scale[Lu et al., 2026].

最近关于人工智能生成文本检测的许多文献都集中在本地文本签名上，包括标记的可预测性、突发性、重复性或基于困惑的不规则性Much of the recent literature on AI-generated text detection has focused on local textual signatures, including token predictability, burstiness, repetition, or perplexity-based irregularities[Kehkashan et al., 2025]。这些方法可能很有用，但它们越来越容易受到模型改进、释义和文体适应的影响。在政治沟通等语言嘈杂、情绪化且与事件相关的环境中，狭隘地关注句子层面的线索可能会错过更广泛的人口层面的扭曲. Such methods can be useful, but they are increasingly vulnerable to model improvements, paraphrasing, and stylistic adaptation. In settings such as political communication, where language is noisy, emotional, and event-dependent, a narrow focus on sentence-level cues may miss broader population-level distortions[Wolfsfeld, 2022].

在本文中，我们采用计算社会科学（CSS）的视角并提出一个不同的问题：In this paper, we adopt a Computational Social Science (CSS) perspective and ask a different question:当法学硕士被用来模拟围绕危机事件的政治话语时，所产生的话语与观察到的在线人群有何不同？when LLMs are used to simulate political discourse around crisis events, how does the resulting discourse differ from observed online populations?我们没有将合成文本仅仅视为作者问题，而是将其视为社会语言人口层面的现象。本文的贡献不是人工智能生成文本的新检测器，而是一个计算社会科学框架，用于审核合成话语群体是否再现了观察到的在线公众的总体行为特征。这种区别很重要，因为政治影响力不仅仅通过孤立的文本发挥作用；它还通过情绪、毒性、重复、词汇框架和特定事件变异性的人群水平分布来运作。通过将话语群体而不是单个帖子作为分析单位，所提出的框架将生成式人工智能评估与CSS的核心关注点联系起来：集体行为、政治沟通、危机应对和在线公众的衡量。Rather than treating synthetic text solely as an authorship problem, we treat it as a socio-linguistic population-level phenomenon. The contribution of this paper is not a new detector of AI-generated text, but a computational social science framework for auditing whether synthetic discourse populations reproduce the aggregate behavioral signatures of observed online publics. This distinction is important because political influence does not operate only through isolated texts; it also operates through population-level distributions of emotion, toxicity, repetition, lexical framing, and event-specific variability. By treating the discourse population rather than the individual post as the unit of analysis, the proposed framework connects generative AI evaluation to central CSS concerns: collective behavior, political communication, crisis response, and the measurement of online publics.

为了研究这个问题，我们建立了一个配对的多事件语料库，其中包含九个危机事件中观察到的和综合的话语。然后，我们从四个维度比较这两种话语来源：情感强度、结构规律、词汇意识形态框架和跨事件依赖。在这些维度上，我们发现观察到的话语和综合话语之间存在一致的差异。合成文本通常比观察到的话语情感更加集中，结构更加同质，词汇更加抽象或戏剧化。同时，这些差异的大小取决于事件类型：对于分散且快速发展的危机，差异往往会扩大，而对于更加制度化或正式化的事件，差异往往会缩小。To investigate this question, we build a paired multi-event corpus containing both observed and synthetic discourse across nine crisis events. We then compare these two sources of discourse along four dimensions: emotional intensity, structural regularity, lexical-ideological framing, and cross-event dependency. Across these dimensions, we find consistent differences between observed and synthetic discourse. Synthetic text is typically more emotionally concentrated, structurally more homogeneous, and lexically more abstract or dramatized than observed discourse. At the same time, the magnitude of these divergences depends on the event type: differences tend to widen for decentralized and fast-moving crises and narrow for more institutional or formalized events.

我们的贡献有四重：Our contributions are fourfold:

  1. 1.

    我们引入了配对的多事件基准，用于比较九个危机事件中观察到的和综合的政治话语。We introduce a paired multi-event benchmark for comparing observed and synthetic political discourse across nine crisis events.

  2. 2.

    我们提出了一个用于审核人口层面的合成话语的四大支柱 CSS 框架：情感强度、结构规律、词汇意识形态框架和跨事件依赖。We propose a four-pillar CSS framework for auditing synthetic discourse at the population level: emotional intensity, structural regularity, lexical-ideological framing, and cross-event dependency.

  3. 3.

    我们形式化了事件层面的社会学类型学，将危机事件视为不同的话语环境而不是可互换的主题。We formalize an event-level sociological typology that treats crisis events as different discourse environments rather than interchangeable topics.

  4. 4.

    我们表明，综合话语与观察到的话语在具有社会意义和事件依赖性方面存在系统性差异，并且我们通过一种简单的分歧度量来总结这些差异，我们将其称为We show that synthetic discourse differs systematically from observed discourse in ways that are socially meaningful and event-dependent, and we summarize these differences through a simple divergence measure that we term the漫画差距Caricature Gap.

总体而言，该论文主张重点的转变：从询问单个文本是否看起来是机器生成的，转变为询问生成的话语群体的行为是否像真实的话语群体。Overall, the paper argues for a shift in emphasis: from asking whether a single text looks machine-generated to asking whether a generated discourse population behaves like a real one.

1.1 研究问题和假设Research Questions and Hypotheses

这项研究以三个研究问题为指导：This study is guided by three research questions:

RQ1。RQ1.法学硕士生成的政治话语群体与危机事件中观察到的在线话语群体是否有所不同？Do LLM-generated political discourse populations differ from observed online discourse populations across crisis events?

RQ2。RQ2.话语现实主义的哪些维度受到的影响最大：情感强度、结构规律、词汇意识形态框架或跨事件依赖？Which dimensions of discourse realism are most affected: emotional intensity, structural regularity, lexical-ideological framing, or cross-event dependency?

RQ3。RQ3.综合观察到的分歧在事件中是否稳定，或者它们是否受到事件的社会学特征的调节？Are synthetic–observed divergences stable across events, or are they moderated by the sociological character of the event?

基于先前关于合成社会数据、政治传播和机器生成文本的工作，我们提出了三个描述性假设。首先，我们预计合成话语的群体异质性低于观察到的话语，这反映在更窄的情感、长度和词汇分布上。其次，我们预计对于快速发展和分散的危机，分歧会更大，因为观察到的话语更加非正式和异构。第三，我们预计毒性分歧是事件相关的而不是统一的，因为模型安全行为和提示框架可能与每个事件的主导修辞语域相互作用。Based on prior work on synthetic social data, political communication, and machine-generated text, we formulate three descriptive hypotheses. First, we expect synthetic discourse to exhibit lower population heterogeneity than observed discourse, reflected in narrower sentiment, length, and lexical distributions. Second, we expect divergence to be larger for fast-moving and decentralized crises, where observed discourse is more informal and heterogeneous. Third, we expect toxicity divergence to be event-dependent rather than uniform, because model safety behavior and prompt framing may interact with the dominant rhetorical register of each event.

2 相关工作Related Work

本文涵盖三个研究方向：机器生成文本的自动检测、在线政治话语的计算社会科学研究以及使用大型语言模型来模拟或合成社会数据。我们依次回顾每一个，并找出一个差距，促使我们对危机时代的话语进行人群层面的处理。This paper intersects three lines of research: automated detection of machine-generated text, computational social science studies of online political discourse, and the use of large language models to simulate or synthesize social data. We review each in turn and identify a gap that motivates a population-level treatment of crisis-era discourse.

机器生成文本的检测已成为对大型语言模型快速扩散的核心响应，之前的调查将该领域组织为统计、文体测量、监督、基于扰动和水印方法The detection of machine-generated text has emerged as a central response to the rapid proliferation of large language models, with prior surveys organizing the field into statistical, stylometric, supervised, perturbation-based, and watermarking approaches[Crothers et al., 2023],[Wu, 2025]。早期的统计方法利用生成的文本占据模型概率分布的高似然、低秩区域的趋势，如 GLTR 所示. Early statistical methods exploited the tendency of generated text to occupy high-likelihood, low-rank regions of a model’s probability distribution, as exemplified by GLTR[Gehrmann et al., 2019]，而后来的基于扰动的检测器，例如 DetectGPT, while later perturbation-based detectors such as DetectGPT[Mitchell et al., 2023]和双筒望远镜and Binoculars[Hans et al., 2024]通过曲率和交叉困惑标准形式化了这种直觉，实现了强大的零样本性能。基于 Transformer 主干的监督分类器（例如 RoBERTa）同样通过 Grover 等系统展示了较高的域内准确度formalized this intuition through curvature and cross-perplexity criteria, achieving strong zero-shot performance. Supervised classifiers built on transformer backbones such as RoBERTa have likewise demonstrated high in-domain accuracy, with systems like Grover[Zellers et al., 2019]和捉鬼敢死队and Ghostbuster[Verma et al., 2024]以近乎完美的 F1 分数将人类编写的内容与机器编写的内容区分开来。水印方案在生成时嵌入伪随机信号以实现事后验证separating human- from machine-written content with near-perfect F1 scores. Watermarking schemes embed pseudorandom signals at generation time to enable post-hoc verification[Kirchenbauer et al., 2023]，尽管它们以生成器的合作为前提，并且对对手提供的保护很少 部署无水印的开源模型。针对简短内容（尤其是推文）量身定制的文体测量技术通过利用措辞、标点符号和词汇多样性特征来补充这些方法, though they presuppose cooperation from the generator and offer little protection against adversaries who deploy unwatermarked open-source models. Stylometric techniques tailored to short-form content, particularly tweets, complement these methods by leveraging phraseology, punctuation, and lexical-diversity features[Fagni et al., 2021], [Kumarage et al., 2023]。然而最近的压力测试表明，递归释义和对抗性扰动急剧降低了所有四个系列的可靠性. Yet recent stress tests have shown that recursive paraphrasing and adversarial perturbations sharply degrade the reliability of all four families[Sadasivan et al., 2023], [Dugan et al., 2024]，揭示了当代探测器主要在本地文本层面上运行，而对更广泛的话语和社会模式仍然视而不见，而生成的内容通过这些模式在冲突驱动的在线环境中传播和施加影响。, revealing that contemporary detectors operate primarily at the local textual level and remain blind to the broader discursive and social patterns through which generated content circulates and exerts influence in conflict-driven online environments.

第二条工作来自计算社会科学和网络政治话语的研究The second line of work comes from Computational Social Science and the study of online political discourse[Theocharis and Jungherr, 2024]。这些文献研究了两极分化、情感语言、错误信息、意识形态集群、抗议传播以及事件驱动的在线行为转变。我们的工作建立在这一传统的基础上，将话语分析为人口层面的社会信号，而不仅仅是孤立文本的集合。跨平台分析表明，在线政治互动聚集成同质社区，其结构决定了信息传播和对立观点的暴露. This literature has examined polarization, affective language, misinformation, ideological clustering, protest communication, and event-driven shifts in online behavior. Our work builds on this tradition by analyzing discourse as a population-level social signal rather than merely a collection of isolated texts. Cross-platform analyses have established that online political interactions cluster into homophilic communities whose structure shapes information diffusion and exposure to opposing views[Cinelli et al., 2021]，多国证据进一步表明，群体外的互动始终比群体内的互动更具毒性，这种规律现在被视为情感两极分化的一个决定性特征, and multinational evidence has further shown that out-group interactions are consistently more toxic than in-group ones, a regularity now treated as a defining signature of affective polarization[Falkenberg et al., 2024]。这种测量传统已经通过通过回复互动的情感和毒性来操作情感极化的方法得到了扩展，表明网络中的意识形态接近度与表达语言的效价和礼貌密切相关. This measurement tradition has been extended through methods that operationalize affective polarization via the emotion and toxicity of reply interactions, demonstrating that ideological proximity in the network correlates strongly with the valence and civility of expressed language[He et al., 2024]。危机驱动的事件为此类分析提供了特别丰富的背景，纵向研究追踪了 COVID-19 大流行期间的党派动态. Crisis-driven events have provided especially rich settings for such analysis, with longitudinal studies tracking partisan dynamics during the COVID-19 pandemic[Chen et al., 2020]、1月6日国会大厦袭击事件, the January 6th Capitol attack[Lee et al., 2022]，推翻罗伊诉韦德案, the overturning of Roe v. Wade[Chang et al., 2023]，以及 2024 年美国总统大选, and the 2024 U.S. presidential election[Balasubramanian et al., 2024]，揭示了对立的用户社区之间尖锐的情感、词汇和意识形态的不对称。然而，在整个工作中，分析仅针对观察到的人类创作的内容进行，留下了当话语不再存在时相同的人口水平规律如何表现的开放性。 完全是人类起源的。, revealing sharp emotional, lexical, and ideological asymmetries between opposing user communities. Yet across this body of work, analyses are conducted on observed human-authored content alone, leaving open how the same population-level regularities behave when discourse is no longer exclusively human in origin.

第三条工作涉及合成社会数据和模拟。最近的研究探索了使用法学硕士来模拟个人、生成调查回复或模拟社交互动。我们的工作的不同之处在于，我们特别关注危机驱动的政治话语，并询问生成的话语是否再现了观察到的在线人群的情感、结构和词汇变化。在这个方向上的早期努力确立了以下概念：The third line of work concerns synthetic social data and simulation. Recent studies have explored the use of LLMs to emulate individuals, generate survey responses, or approximate social interactions. Our work differs by focusing specifically on crisis-driven political discourse and by asking whether generated discourse reproduces the emotional, structural, and lexical variability of observed online populations. Early efforts in this direction established the notion of算法保真度algorithmic fidelity，证明以社会人口背景故事为条件的法学硕士可以重现跨政治小组的真实调查参与者的反应分布, demonstrating that LLMs conditioned on socio-demographic backstories can reproduce response distributions of real survey participants across political subgroups[Argyle et al., 2023]。此后，这种洞察力已扩展到更丰富的环境，包括由生成代理填充的沙箱环境，这些生成代理可以计划、交谈和形成社会关系. This insight has since been extended to richer settings, including sandbox environments populated by generative agents that plan, converse, and form social ties[Park et al., 2023]，以及模拟微博平台，其中具有政治代表性的法学硕士群体被用来评估提要排名干预措施和跨党派毒性, and simulated micro-blogging platforms in which politically representative LLM populations are used to evaluate feed-ranking interventions and cross-partisan toxicity[Törnberg et al., 2023]。与此同时，越来越多的证据引起了人们对合成内容的行为和意识形态特性的担忧：法学硕士生成的推文在准确信息和虚假信息方面至少与人类推文一样可信. The key question is whether generated discourse exhibits narrower and more homogeneous distributions than observed discourse.

The goal is not topic modeling in the strict sense, but identification of salient lexical markers that differentiate observed and synthetic discourse within each event. We use these lexical profiles to assess whether synthetic discourse is more abstract, formalized, or ideologically compressed.

6 鲁棒性和灵敏度的考虑Robustness and Sensitivity Considerations

我们对采样的丰富数据集进行了三项轻量级稳健性检查。首先，由于稳健性子集包含每个事件 1,500 个观察帖子和 1,500 个合成帖子的平衡队列，因此等量抽样使情绪和毒性差距保持不变，从而证实所报告的漫画差距不是由样本量不平衡造成的。其次，在删除重复的文本条目后，签名情绪和毒性差距的变化几乎可以忽略不计，通常小于 0.005。这表明观察到的差异不是重复合成生成、主题标签垃圾邮件或重复平台内容的产物。第三，我们将基于均值的结构差异与中位数和四分位数范围摘要进行了比较。观察到的话语的中位长度为 23 个单词，IQR 为 26 个单词，而合成话语的中位长度为 21 个单词，IQR 为 15 个单词。因此，合成字数 IQR 比观察到的 IQR 低约 42%，支持了 LLM 生成的话语向更窄的结构范围崩溃的解释。We conducted three lightweight robustness checks on the sampled enriched dataset. First, because the robustness subset contains balanced cohorts of 1,500 observed and 1,500 synthetic posts per event, equal-size sampling leaves the sentiment and toxicity gaps unchanged, confirming that the reported Caricature Gap is not driven by sample-size imbalance. Second, after removing duplicate text entries, the signed sentiment and toxicity gaps changed only negligibly, typically by less than 0.005. This indicates that the observed divergences are not artifacts of repeated synthetic generations, hashtag spam, or duplicated platform content. Third, we compared mean-based structural differences with median and interquartile-range summaries. Observed discourse has a median length of 23 words and an IQR of 26 words, whereas synthetic discourse has a median length of 21 words and an IQR of 15 words. Thus, the synthetic word-count IQR is approximately 42% lower than the observed IQR, supporting the interpretation that LLM-generated discourse collapses toward a narrower structural range.

对于这些检查，推理的重点应该放在合成话语是否比观察到的话语更同质且更少的事件特定性上。由于数据集非常大，因此微小的数值差异预计具有统计显着性；因此，鲁棒性应该通过效果方向的稳定性、事件级间隙的相对排名以及基于分散的压缩的持续性来评估。For these checks, the inferential emphasis should be on whether synthetic discourse remains more homogeneous and less event-specific than observed discourse. Because the dataset is very large, minor numeric differences are expected to be statistically significant; robustness should therefore be evaluated by the stability of the effect direction, the relative ranking of event-level gaps, and the persistence of dispersion-based compression.

7 讨论Discussion

我们的结果表明综合政治话语的人口水平有限。主要问题不在于生成的文本语法很差。相反，大部分内容都很流畅。相反，问题在于它往往低估了观察到的在线人群的异质性。与观察语篇相比，合成语篇情感更加集中，结构更加规则，词汇更加抽象。Our results point to a population-level limitation of synthetic political discourse. The main issue is not that generated text is grammatically poor. On the contrary, much of it is fluent. Rather, the issue is that it tends to underrepresent the heterogeneity of observed online populations. Compared with observed discourse, synthetic discourse is more affectively concentrated, structurally more regular, and lexically more abstract.

8 局限性和道德考虑Limitations and Ethical Considerations

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，观察到的语料库不能保证只包含人类创作的内容；它可能包括机器人、协调的活动或机器辅助的帖子。因此，我们的比较是在观察到的话语和合成话语之间进行的，而不是在经过验证的人类文本和经过验证的人工智能文本之间进行的。其次，合成语料库取决于用于生成它的提示、模型选择和生成程序。不同的提示、解码设置、安全过滤器或角色假设可能会改变观察到的差距的大小。第三，犹他州枪击事件和美伊战争语料库与其他事件的直接可比性可能较差，因为它们在规模、新近时间、平台构成和地缘政治背景方面有所不同。因此，它们应该被解释为有用的压力案例，而不是直接等同于长期发生的美国国内事件。This study has several limitations. First, the observed corpus is not guaranteed to contain only human-authored content; it may include bots, coordinated campaigns, or machine-assisted posts. Our comparison is therefore between observed and synthetic discourse, not between verified human and verified AI text. Second, the synthetic corpus depends on the prompts, model choices, and generation procedures used to produce it. Different prompts, decoding settings, safety filters, or persona assumptions may change the magnitude of the observed gaps. Third, the Utah shooting and US-Iran war corpora may be less directly comparable to the other events because they differ in scale, recency, platform composition, and geopolitical context. They should therefore be interpreted as useful stress cases rather than as directly equivalent to the longer-running US domestic events.

第四，我们的措施是代理措施。 VADER 没有捕捉到政治影响的全部细微差别，毒性分类器依赖于模型，TF-IDF 词汇分歧是描述性的，而不是因果性的。最后，我们对这些模式出现的原因的解释（例如，安全约束、训练数据组成或提示注册的可能作用）应被视为与证据一致的假设，而不是直接识别的机制。Fourth, our measures are proxies. VADER does not capture the full nuance of political affect, toxicity classifiers are model-dependent, and TF–IDF lexical divergence is descriptive rather than causal. Finally, our interpretations of why these patterns emerge—for example, the possible role of safety constraints, training-data composition, or prompt register—should be treated as hypotheses consistent with the evidence rather than directly identified mechanisms.

该研究还提出了伦理和双重用途的考虑。它涉及政治敏感的话语和综合政治文本，如果在没有上下文的情况下重新分发，可能会被滥用。因此，我们将数据集视为用于聚合分析的研究工件，而不是作为重复用于说服、模仿或放大的内容。我们不会尝试识别个人用户、推断私人属性或将意图归因于特定帐户。在可能的情况下，发布应优先考虑汇总统计数据、特征表示、可复制代码和记录提示，同时限制有害生成示例或原始政治敏感内容的重新分发。The study also raises ethical and dual-use considerations. It involves politically sensitive discourse and synthetic political text that could be misused if redistributed without context. We therefore treat the dataset as a research artifact for aggregate analysis rather than as content to be reused for persuasion, imitation, or amplification. We do not attempt to identify individual users, infer private attributes, or attribute intent to specific accounts. Where possible, release should prioritize aggregate statistics, feature representations, reproducible code, and documented prompts while limiting the redistribution of harmful generated examples or raw politically sensitive content.

9 可重用的审计协议A Reusable Audit Protocol

除了这里分析的特定事件之外，所提出的框架还可以用作未来生成系统和模拟社交媒体环境的可重用审计协议。该协议由八个步骤组成：定义危机事件和收集窗口；构建透明的种子词典；在记录的平台限制下收集观察到的话语；根据记录的模型和提示设置生成对齐的合成语料库；使用相同的管道对两个语料库进行标准化；计算四个分歧支柱；使用基于均值和分散意识的措施总结差距；并通过事件类型和稳健性检查来解释结果。这使得该框架不仅可用于回顾性分析，还可用于审核新模型、提示、安全设置或模拟在线公众。Beyond the specific events analyzed here, the proposed framework can be used as a reusable audit protocol for future generative systems and simulated social-media environments. The protocol consists of eight steps: define the crisis event and collection window; construct a transparent seed lexicon; collect observed discourse under documented platform constraints; generate an aligned synthetic corpus under documented model and prompt settings; normalize both corpora using the same pipeline; compute the four divergence pillars; summarize gaps using mean-based and dispersion-aware measures; and interpret the results through an event typology and robustness checks. This makes the framework useful not only for retrospective analysis, but also for auditing new models, prompts, safety settings, or simulated online publics.

10 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一个计算社会科学框架，用于审核危机事件中的综合政治话语。使用跨越九个事件的配对语料库，我们表明合成话语与观察到的话语在四个维度上有系统的不同：情感强度、结构规律性、词汇意识形态框架和跨事件依赖性。We presented a Computational Social Science framework for auditing synthetic political discourse across crisis events. Using a paired corpus spanning nine events, we showed that synthetic discourse differs systematically from observed discourse along four dimensions: emotional intensity, structural regularity, lexical-ideological framing, and cross-event dependency.

这些发现表明，合成话语的关键限制不是句子层面的流畅性，而是群体现实性的降低。因此，拟议的漫画差距和分散意识审计协议将评估从孤立的帖子转移到话语群体。我们认为，未来的合成文本审计工作应该超越本地语法线索，并询问生成的话语是否像它声称代表的在线公众那样表现。These findings suggest that the key limitation of synthetic discourse is not sentence-level fluency but reduced population realism. The proposed Caricature Gap and dispersion-aware audit protocol therefore shift evaluation from the isolated post to the discourse population. We argue that future work on synthetic-text auditing should move beyond local grammatical cues and ask whether generated discourse behaves like the online publics it claims to represent.
