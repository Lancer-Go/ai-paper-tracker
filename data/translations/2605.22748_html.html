多智能体强化学习Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

摘要Abstract

自治系统在隔离或模拟中已经实现了超人的性能，但它们在共享、动态的现实世界空间中仍然脆弱。这种失败源于物理应用中占主导地位的单代理范例，其中其他参与者被忽略或被视为环境噪音，从而阻碍了有效的协调。 在这里，我们展示了多智能体强化学习提供了现实世界交互所需的基本安全支架。使用高速四旋翼赛车作为高风险测试平台，我们训练智能体与不同数量的赛车手进行复杂的空气动力相互作用和战略机动。通过基于联盟的自我对战，智能体进化出复杂的预期行为，包括主动避免碰撞、超车和处理多智能体物理交互，包括空气动力学下洗。 我们的智能体在多人竞赛中以超过 22 米/秒的速度超越冠军级人类飞行员，同时与最先进的单智能体基线相比，碰撞率降低了 50%。 至关重要的是，使用不同的人工智能体进行训练可以实现零样本泛化，从而实现更安全的人机交互。 这些结果表明，稳健的机器人共存之路不在于孤立的安全约束，而在于多智能体交互的严格要求。 多媒体材料可在以下位置获取：Autonomous systems have achieved superhuman performance in isolation or simulation, yet they remain brittle in shared, dynamic real-world spaces. This failure stems from the dominant single-agent paradigm for physical applications, where other actors are ignored or treated as environmental noise, preventing effective coordination. Here we show that multi-agent reinforcement learning provides the essential safety scaffolding required for real-world interaction. Using high-speed quadrotor racing as a high-stakes testbed, we train agents to navigate complex aerodynamic interactions and strategic maneuvering with a variable number of racers. Through league-based self-play, agents evolve sophisticated anticipatory behaviors, including proactive collision avoidance, overtaking, and handling multi-agent physical interactions, including aerodynamic downwash. Our agents outperform a champion-level human pilot in multi-player races at speeds exceeding 22 m/s, while simultaneously reducing collision rates by 50% compared to state-of-the-art single-agent baselines. Crucially, training with diverse artificial agents enables zero-shot generalization to safer human interaction. These results suggest that the path to robust robotic co-existence lies not in isolated safety constraints, but in the rigorous demands of multi-agent interaction. 简介Introduction

自主机器人越来越多地在现实世界中运行。学习策略现在使步行机器人能够穿越具有挑战性的地形Autonomous robots are increasingly operating in the physical world. Learned policies now enable walking robots to traverse challenging terrain[29, 25]、移动机械手进行清理, mobile manipulators to clean up[44]或做饭or cook[14]和飞行器高速导航复杂环境, and aerial vehicles to navigate complex environments at high speed[28, 23, 37, 1, 15]。 然而，显着的不对称性仍然存在：虽然单智能体系统已经从实验室演示成熟到实际部署，但敏捷的多智能体协调仍然主要局限于模拟. Yet, a striking asymmetry persists: while single-agent systems have matured from laboratory demonstrations to practical deployment, agile multi-agent coordination remains largely confined to simulation[16, 12]，即使是在高度现实和竞争激烈的领域，例如自动驾驶赛车, even in highly realistic and competitive domains such as autonomous car racing[45].

在现实世界中，与人类对手的正面交锋也无法解决这一挑战：它从根本上来说仍然是一场决斗，一种有限的互动，其中策略很大程度上简化为单圈时间优化。一旦自主智能体取得领先，与单个对手发生碰撞的风险实际上就消失了，正如在之前与冠军级人类的正面交锋中所观察到的那样，自主智能体在其完成的每场比赛中从头到尾都处于领先地位In the physical world, head-to-head racing against a human opponent does not resolve this challenge either: it remains fundamentally a duel, a bounded interaction in which strategy largely reduces to lap time optimization. Once the autonomous agent establishes a lead, the risk of collision with its single opponent effectively vanishes, as empirically observed in prior head-to-head racing against champion-level humans, where the autonomous agent led from start to finish in every race it completed[23]。当更多的竞争对手进入同一空域时，这种简化就会失效。随着智能体数量的增加、碰撞的增加和比赛完成率的下降，针对单人或面对面比赛优化的策略会遭受性能急剧下降。掌握决斗的策略，在多人互动和部署中失败。这一差距对扩展自主系统构成了根本障碍，因为从仓库物流到搜索救援再到城市空中交通等许多关键应用，本质上需要多个机器人与人类一起在共享物理空间中运行。解决这一挑战需要智能体对共享同一物理空间的多个其他智能体的行为进行建模、预测和适应。. When additional competitors enter the same airspace, this simplification breaks down. Strategies optimized for solo or head-to-head racing suffer sharp performance degradation as the number of agents grows, with increasing collisions and deteriorating race-completion rates. Strategies that master a duel, lose in multi-player interaction and deployment. This gap presents a fundamental barrier to scaling autonomous systems, as many critical applications, from warehouse logistics to search and rescue to urban air mobility, inherently require multiple robots operating in shared physical space alongside humans. Addressing this challenge demands agents which model, anticipate, and adapt to the behavior of multiple other agents sharing the same physical space.

人工智能之前就遇到过这种从单对手到多对手设置的转变，每个实例都需要新的方法而不是增量扩展。在扑克领域，人工智能首次在两人零和设置中实现了超人的玩法Artificial intelligence has encountered this transition from single- to multi-opponent settings before, and each instance has required new methods rather than incremental scaling. In poker, AI first achieved superhuman play in two-player zero-sum settings[8, 9]，其中纳什均衡提供了强有力的最坏情况保证并且在计算上仍然易于处理。事实证明，向六人扑克的过渡无疑更加困难：均衡策略不再保证有限的可利用性，对手互动的组合增长需要本质上不同的搜索方法来实现超人的表现, where the Nash equilibrium provides strong worst-case guarantees and remains computationally tractable. The transition to six-player poker proved categorically harder: equilibrium strategies no longer guarantee bounded exploitability, and the combinatorial growth of opponent interactions demands qualitatively different search methods to achieve superhuman performance[10]。 正如玩游戏的人工智能必须从两人竞赛发展到多人游戏设置一样，机器人控制也必须从优化单独轨迹发展到共享物理环境中多个智能体的动态共存。. Just as game-playing AI had to advance from two-player contests to multiplayer settings, robotic control must advance from optimizing solo trajectories to dynamic co-existence of multiple agents in shared physical environments.

强化学习有着悠久的超人游戏历史Reinforcement learning has a long history of superhuman game play[39]，来自双陆棋和围棋, from backgammon and Go[34, 35]星际争霸 II 的部分可观察性和广阔的策略空间to the partial observability and vast strategy spaces of StarCraft II[41], 刀塔2, Dota 2[7]，以及多人夺旗, and multi-player capture-the-flag[21]。这些系统的一个关键见解是，针对不同群体的对手而不是单一的固定对手进行训练，可以防止策略过度拟合并产生更普遍的行为. A key insight from these systems is that training against a diverse population of opponents, rather than a single fixed adversary, prevents strategic overfitting and produces more generalizable behaviors[24, 3, 12]，包括与人类的互动, including interaction with humans[34, 41, 7]。除了游戏之外，强化学习还在模拟机器人环境中产生了新兴的团队行为. Beyond game play, reinforcement learning has produced emergent team behaviors in simulated robotics settings[27, 2, 4]。因此，最近的工作已经开始通过模拟到真实的迁移来弥合与物理系统的差距，展示机器人足球中的竞争行为. Consequently, recent work has begun to bridge the gap to physical systems through sim-to-real transfer, demonstrating competitive behaviors in robot soccer[17, 40]、小型赛车, small-scale car racing[43]，以及具有稀疏竞争奖励的两人无人机竞赛, and two-player drone racing with sparse competitive rewards[30].

然而，这些系统要么运行速度低且安全挑战有限，要么仅限于两人交互，无法捕捉多智能体协调的组合复杂性。 将多智能体学习转化为更敏捷的物理平台，加剧了非平稳性、指数状态空间增长和部分可观测性等经过充分研究的挑战However, these systems either operate at low speeds and limited safety challenges, or are restricted to two-player interactions that do not capture the combinatorial complexity of multi-agent coordination. Analysis of race trajectories reveals a consistent pattern: the human pilot, typically trailing the autonomous agents, attempted increasingly aggressive maneuvers to close the gap, often resulting in gate collisions or loss of control. The learned policies, by contrast, maintained consistent safety margins regardless of competitive standing while using the given opportunities to overtake. These results suggest that interaction-aware training produces policies that internalize robust collision avoidance independent of race dynamics, whereas human pilots modulate risk-taking based on competitive pressure. This property is essential for the future deployment of autonomous agents in shared environments, where predictable and conservative behavior around human operators is a prerequisite for safe integration.

讨论Discussion

这项工作表明，基于多样化的自我游戏和物理交互建模的多智能体强化学习可以在共享环境中实现安全的高速协调。通过与具有不同策略的对手联盟进行训练，我们的策略开发了强大的防撞功能，可以推广到训练条件之外，从而实现与冠军级人类飞行员的竞争表现，同时保持一致的安全裕度。这些结果将自动四旋翼飞行器赛车确立为必须与人类一起运行的多智能体系统的试验场。 一个核心发现是，交互意识培训从根本上改变了政策在竞争压力下的表现。当落后于竞争对手时，人类飞行员会采取越来越激进的策略，从而增加碰撞风险。相比之下，我们学到的政策无论是领先还是落后，都保持一致的安全边际。这种行为差异具有超越赛车的重要影响：在人类和自主系统交互的任何共享环境中，自主代理的可预测和保守行为对于安全集成至关重要。在没有明确的安全约束的情况下，通过自我博弈来训练将此类行为内化的政策的能力，表明了一条通过设计而不是通过限制实现安全的自治系统的有前途的道路。This work demonstrates that multi-agent reinforcement learning, grounded in diverse self-play and physical interaction modeling, can achieve safe high-speed coordination in shared environments. By training against a league of opponents with varied strategies, our policies develop robust collision avoidance that generalizes beyond training conditions, enabling competitive performance against champion-level human pilots while maintaining consistent safety margins. These results establish autonomous quadrotor racing as a proving ground for multi-agent systems that must operate alongside humans. A central finding is that interaction-aware training fundamentally changes how policies behave under competitive pressure. Human pilots, when trailing competitors, adopt increasingly aggressive strategies that elevate collision risk. Our learned policies, by contrast, maintain consistent safety margins, whether leading or trailing. This behavioral difference has important implications beyond racing: in any shared environment where humans and autonomous systems interact, predictable and conservative behavior from autonomous agents is essential for safe integration. The ability to train policies that internalize such behavior through self-play, without explicit safety constraints, suggests a promising path toward autonomous systems that are safe by design rather than by restriction.

联赛训练政策的泛化特性值得特别关注。针对不同对手的训练，包括忽略竞争对手的单代理策略和过度适应特定合作伙伴的独立学习者，可以产生针对新颖交互模式的强大策略。这种多样性至关重要：虚拟的自我对战在较高的代理密度下会表现出性能下降，这可能是由于单代理策略所采用的高风险策略的暴露程度有限，这些策略优先考虑速度而不是安全。独立学习的策略进一步扩大了行为分布，因为联合优化产生了具有与自博动态显着不同的独特赛车线和交互模式的智能体。即使有八名同时参赛的选手，联赛比赛也能保持竞技表现，这是训练期间遇到的最多选手的两倍。对于现实世界的部署来说，可能的交互空间是巨大且不可预测的，这种概括不仅是可取的，而且是必要的。尽管部署的策略共享单一网络架构，但训练联盟和大规模评估都使框架与跨越多种学习范式的异构策略群体直接接触，提供了经验证据，证明交互感知行为来自行为不同的代理，而不仅仅是同质副本。The generalization properties of league-play trained policies merit particular attention. Training against diverse opponents, including single-agent policies that ignore competitors and independent learners that overfit to specific partners, produces robust strategies to novel interaction patterns. This diversity is essential: fictitious self-play alone exhibits degraded performance at higher agent densities, likely due to limited exposure to high-risk strategies employed by single-agent policies that prioritize speed over safety. Independently learned policies further expand the behavioral distribution, as joint optimization produces agents with distinct racing lines and interaction patterns that differ substantially from self-play dynamics. League-play maintains competitive performance even with eight simultaneous competitors, twice the maximum encountered during training. For real-world deployment, where the space of possible interactions is vast and unpredictable, such generalization is not merely desirable but necessary. Although the deployed policies share a single network architecture, both the training league and the large-scale evaluation place the framework in direct contact with heterogeneous policy populations spanning multiple learning paradigms, providing empirical evidence that interaction-aware behavior emerges from behaviorally distinct agents rather than only among homogeneous copies.

课程学习Curriculum learning
