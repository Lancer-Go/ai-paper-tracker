一、简介1. Introduction

通过卫星图像进行的地球观测 (EO) 改变了我们监测全球环境过程和人类活动的能力。最近出现的自监督基础模型标志着地理空间人工智能（GeoAI）和地球观测的关键进步[36]，使得能够从大量未标记的空中图像档案中进行可扩展的表示学习。这些模型采用了专门的架构，可以解释地理空间数据的独特属性，包括多分辨率观测、时间序列和多光谱通道 [10, 38]。通过对 PB 级数据集进行预训练，它们产生了强大的特征表示，可以有效地转移到各种下游应用，例如土地覆盖分类、灾害评估、作物田地划分和气候监测 [5, 15]。Earth observation (EO) through satellite imagery has transformed our ability to monitor global environmental processes and human activities. The recent emergence of self-supervised foundation models marks a pivotal advance in geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI) and earth observations [36], enabling scalable representation learning from vast unlabeled archives of overhead imagery. These models incorporate specialized architectures that account for the unique properties of geospatial data, including multi-resolution observations, temporal sequences, and multi-spectral channels [10, 38]. Through pretraining on petabyte-scale datasets, they produce robust feature representations that transfer effectively to diverse downstream applications such as land cover classification, disaster assessment, crop field delineation, and climate monitoring [5, 15].

尽管取得了成功，当前的地球观测基础模型（EOFM）通常根据卫星或航空栅格数据等地球观测图像进行训练，而更广泛的地理空间基础模型（GeoFM）可能还包含矢量和结构化数据等其他地理空间模式，但仍然主要局限于基于栅格的表示[28]，忽视了编码在可公开访问的矢量数据中的丰富的结构化地理空间知识。 OpenStreetMap 和 Overture 等矢量数据捕获明确的空间实体，包括建筑物、道路和土地使用地块，及其几何、拓扑和语义关系 [2, 3]。这些表示反映了人类的解释和组织Despite their success, current Earth Observation Foundation Models (EOFMs), typically trained on Earth observation imagery such as satellite or aerial raster data, and broader Geospatial Foundation Models (GeoFMs), which may additionally encompass other geospatial modalities including vector and structured data, remain largely confined to raster-based representations [28], overlooking the rich, structured geospatial knowledge encoded in openlyaccessible vector data. Vector data such as OpenStreetMap and Overture capture explicit spatial entities, including buildings, roads, and land use parcels, along with their geometry, topology, and semantic relationships [2, 3]. 这种碎片化限制了能够整体理解的地理空间模型的发展。许多现实世界的应用，从城市分析到气候影响评估，都需要对物理观测和人类定义的空间结构进行同步推理[36]。然而，现有的基于深度学习的方法无法充分利用这些互补信号，因为这两种范式需要不同的神经编码和表示方式，而这些方式很难以细粒度的方式对齐和集成[3]，例如，将一个基于矢量的建筑多边形与卫星图像中相应的一组像素对齐。值得注意的是，单独使用栅格或矢量数据具有内在的局限性。虽然基于栅格的模型在语义上仍然不够明确，但基于矢量的方法缺乏上下文基础[30]。结合这两种数据模型可以提供更全面的物理世界视图，如图 1 所示，并可以更深入地了解塑造我们星球的物理过程和人类过程之间的相互联系。This fragmentation constrains the development of geospatial models capable of holistic understanding. Many real-world applications, from urban analysis to climate impact assessment—demand simultaneous reasoning over physical observations and human, defined spatial structure [36]. However, existing deep learning-based approaches cannot fully exploit these complementary signals, since these two paradigms require distinct ways for neural encoding and representations which are hard to align and integrate in a fine-grained manner[3], e.g., alignment of one vectorbased building polygon to its corresponding set of pixels in a satellite image. Notably, using raster or vector data alone has intrinsic limitations. While raster-based models remain semantically under-specified, vector-based methods lack contextual grounding [30]. Combining both data models could provide a more comprehensive view of the physical world, as shown in Figure 1, and allow for a deeper understanding of the interconnections between the physical and human processes shaping our planet.

我们设想，解决这一限制需要转向联合 SRL，其中栅格和矢量数据（及其相互依赖性）是在共享嵌入空间中学习的。这种表示将统一感知和结构信息，使模型能够将视觉模式与语义实体及其关系保持一致。这篇透视论文概述了这种统一方法的概念基础、技术挑战和新兴方向，并将联合栅格矢量学习定位为迈向下一代以人为中心的地理空间基础模型的关键一步[30, 34]。We envision that addressing this limitation requires a shift toward joint SRL, in which raster and vector data (and their inter-dependencies) are learned within shared embedding spaces. Such representations would unify perceptual and structural information, enabling models to align visual patterns with semantic entities and their relationships. This perspective paper outlines the conceptual foundations, technical challenges, and emerging directions for such unified approaches, and positions joint raster–vector learning as a critical step toward next-generation, human-centric geospatial foundation models [30, 34].

2.1.栅格表示和基于图像的方法2.1. Raster Representations and Imagery-based Approaches

在过去的几年里，EO 和遥感 (RS) 数据已经变得丰富且达到 PB 级，提供了对地球上自然和人类动态的近乎实时的洞察。与此同时，计算机视觉和表示学习取得了重大进展，特别是模型预训练和自监督学习，其中通用数据表示成功地从 ImageNet 以及后来的特定领域 RS 数据集获得[25, 38]。遥感图像将地理空间捕获为离散单元的规则网格，构建连续地理图层的理想表示，例如海拔剖面、植被指数和土地覆盖信息。通过利用大量 EO 数据的力量，先进的基于图像的 EOFM 通常采用针对地理空间特征定制的自我监督预训练目标 [45]。先进的人工智能架构，如屏蔽自动编码器（MAE），将模型训练视为自监督重建，随机屏蔽补丁，同时保持多尺度上下文连贯性，导致对局部纹理和全局场景结构敏感的潜在特征[19, 46]。此外，对比框架进一步对齐空间相邻斑块的嵌入，同时排斥远处区域，将遥感图像的空间自相关特征嵌入到光电数据的通用 SRL 中。Over the last years, EO and Remote Sensing (RS) data have become abundant and available at the petabyte scale, providing almost real-time insights into natural and human dynamics on Earth. At the same time, computer vision and representation learning have seen major advances, especially w.r.t model pre-training and self-supervised learning, where general-purpose data representations were successfully obtained from ImageNet and, later on, domain-specific RS datasets [25, 38]. Remote sensing imagery captures geographic space as regular grids of discrete cells, constructing an ideal representation of continuous geographical layers such as elevation profiles, vegetation indices, and land cover information. By harnessing the power of massive EO data, advanced imagery-based EOFMs often employ self-supervised pretraining objectives tailored to geospatial characteristics [45]. Advanced AI architectures, like Masked autoencoder (MAE), treat the model training as self-supervised reconstruction which randomly masks patches while maintaining multi-scale contextual coherence, results in latent feature sensitive to both local textures and global scene structure [19, 46]. Moreover, contrastive frameworks further align embeddings of spatially adjacent patches while repelling distant regions, embedding the spatial autocorrelation characteristic of RS imagery toward general-purposed SRL of EO data.

最近，越来越多的工作大规模证明了这些策略的有效性。 SkySense [58]、SpectralGPT [25]、SatCLIP [28]、TerraFM [12]、CopernicusFM [49] 和 Prithvi-EO-2.0 [45] 等模型利用多模态和多传感器 EO 数据生成强大的神经表示，这些神经表示可以在地理区域、传感器模式和下游地理空间任务中稳健执行。 AlphaEarth [6] 的出现进一步扩展了这一景观，它展示了多传感器和多时相观测大规模集成到统一的地球表示中。此外，最近的发展，例如 THOR [20]、DOFACLIP [54] 和 TerraMind [26]，进一步引入了计算自适应架构并结合文本对齐来处理传感器异构性和可变分辨率以及实现零样本泛化 [20]。除了光学 RS 图像之外，地球系统 FM（例如 Prithvi WxC [42] 和 Aurora [5]）将 SRL 范式扩展到大气和气候变量，展示了 EOFM 在 PB 级数据集上的潜力和可扩展性 [48]。Recently, a growing body of work demonstrates the effectiveness of these strategies at scale. Models such as SkySense [58], SpectralGPT [25], SatCLIP [28], TerraFM [12], CopernicusFM [49], and Prithvi-EO-2.0 [45] leverage multi-modal and multi-sensor EO data to generate powerful neural representations that perform robustly across geographic regions, sensor modalities, and downstream geospatial tasks. This landscape has been further expanded by the emergence of AlphaEarth [6], which demonstrates large-scale integration of multi-sensor and multi-temporal observations into a unified Earth representation. Moreover, recent developments, for example THOR [20], DOFACLIP [54] and TerraMind [26], further introduce computeadaptive architectures and incorporate textual alignment to handle sensor heterogeneity and variable resolutions as well as to enable zero-shot generalization [20]. Beyond optical RS imagery, Earth system FMs (e.g., Prithvi WxC [42] and Aurora [5]) extend the SRL paradigms to atmospheric and climate variables, demonstrating the potential and scalability of EOFMs across petabyte-scale datasets [48].

2.2.向量表示和基于多态性的方法2.2. Vector Representations and Polymorphicbased Approaches

为了从矢量数据中提取有意义的特征以用于下游任务，许多研究人员要么基于领域知识执行特征工程，要么将空间数据从其原始格式（例如点、折线和多边形）转换为更容易神经网络处理的格式（例如，点云到体素，或将矢量文件映射到光栅图像图块[4]。To extract meaningful features from vector data for downstream tasks, many researchers either perform feature engineering based on domain knowledge, or convert spatial data from their original formats (e.g., points, polylines, and polygons) into formats that are easier for neural networks to handle (e.g., point clouds to voxels, or map vector files into raster image tiles [4].

由于多态矢量数据缺乏像素的规则网格结构，因此被认为是 SRL 学习更具挑战性的数据源（例如点、折线、多边形、不规则三角网络、3D LiDAR 点云、3D 建筑模型等）。幸运的是，SRL 技术的最新发展开始为多态矢量数据（例如点、线和多边形）开发新的 SRL 方法。具体来说，基于多态的 SRL 方法将非欧几里得向量几何映射为低维、密集嵌入向量，同时保留其空间、结构和拓扑属性 [44]。As polymorphic vector data lacks the regular grid structure of pixels, it is considered as a more challenging data source (e.g., points, polylines, polygons, triangulated irregular networks, 3D LiDAR point clouds, 3D building models, etc.) for SRL to learn from. Fortunately, recent developments of SRL techniques start to develop new SRL methods for polymorphic vector data (e.g., point, line and polygon). Specifically, polymorphic-based SRL approaches map nonEuclidean vector geometries into low-dimensional, dense embedding vectors while preserving their spatial, structural, and topological properties [44].

对于地理标记点数据，基于正弦函数和球谐函数的位置编码等先进方法提供了地理位置的连续表示[40]，可以聚合为任意多边形几何形状，尽管将位置嵌入或全局像素级嵌入（例如 Alpha Earth Foundations）聚合到多边形几何形状上的系统研究仍然很少。除了点数据编码之外，全球尺度空间索引的进步，例如基于 S2 几何的 S2Vec [8]，进一步促进了鲁棒的全球尺度地理特征编码。除了点级输入之外，GeoBind [13] 和主要 TOM 项目 [11] 等框架还提供高维抽象，用于将各个地理层映射到共享潜在空间中，以实现 SRL 的目的。最近的几何感知方法，例如 Poly2Vec [44] 和 Geo2Vec [9] 能够同时对点、形状和距离进行编码，同时保留几何结构。最近，像 AETHER [30] 这样的多模态 FM 开始强调 POI 作为语义锚的作用，利用多模态对齐通过 RS 和 SVI 将基于矢量的人类活动模式与基于图像的城市环境观察联系起来。最近的评估还强调For geotagged point data, advanced approaching such as location encoding based on sinusoid functions and spherical harmonics provide continuous representations of geographic position [40], which may be aggregated into arbitrary polygon geometries, although systematic studies on aggregating either location embeddings or global pixellevel embeddings such as Alpha Earth Foundations onto a polygon geometry remain scarce. Besides point data encoding, advances in global-scale spatial indexing, such as the S2 Geometry-based S2Vec [8], further facilitate robust global-scale geographic feature encoding. Beyond pointlevel inputs, frameworks like GeoBind [13] and the Major TOM project [11] provide high-dimensional abstractions for mapping individual geographic layers into shared latent spaces for the purposed of SRL. Recent geometryaware approaches such as Poly2Vec [44] and Geo2Vec [9] is able to encode points, shapes, and distances simultaneously while preserving geometric structure. More recently, multimodal FMs like AETHER [30] start to emphasize the role of POIs as semantic anchors, leveraging multimodal alignment to link vector-based human activity patterns with imagery-based urban environment observation via RS and SVI. 3. 将向量语义集成到基于栅格的空间表示学习中3. Toward Integrating Vector Semantics into Raster-Based Spatial Representation Learning

栅格网格擅长表示连续场和视觉模式，但可以近似对象边界并模糊拓扑关系，具体取决于空间分辨率和比例。矢量基元保留了精确的几何形状、连通性和语义标识，但缺乏卫星观测中可用的密集物理上下文。当前的 GeoAI 模型通常将这些模式视为具有不同 SRL 神经架构的独立孤岛，仅通过有损、特定于任务的转换进行连接，而牺牲结构保真度 [22]。接下来，我们将系统地研究现有集成策略的局限性，强调原生多模式架构的新兴方向，并主张统一的表示学习范式，将栅格和矢量数据视为共享嵌入空间中的同等组件。Raster grids excel at representing continuous fields and visual patterns, but can approximate object boundaries and obscure topological relationships, depending on the spatial resolutions and scales. Vector primitives preserve exact geometry, connectivity, and semantic identity but lack the dense physical context available from satellite observations. Current GeoAI models usually treat these modalities as separate silos with distinct neural architectures for SRL, connected only through lossy, task-specific conversions that sacrifice structural fidelity [22]. In the following, we will systematically examine the limitations of existing integration strategies, highlight emerging directions for native multimodal architectures, and argue for a unified representation learning paradigm that treats raster and vector data as coequal components within shared embedding spaces.

3.2.当前的整合策略：间接方法的局限性3.2. Current Integration Strategies: Limitations of Indirect Approaches

栅格和矢量联合学习的一个主要挑战在于栅格和矢量数据表示之间的根本差异。栅格数据（例如卫星图像）代表连续的空间场，并自然地与深度学习架构的归纳偏差保持一致，特别是设计用于处理密集网格结构的卷积和基于变压器的模型。相比之下，矢量数据本质上是离散的、稀疏的和结构化的，由几何形状、拓扑关系和语义属性组成。由于这些差异，为矢​​量数据设计有效的 SRL 模型仍然具有挑战性。因此，矢量到光栅的转换是一种常见的策略，它在将矢量数据输入神经网络之前将其转换为光栅图像。由于缺乏直接将矢量数据编码到神经网络中的专门方法，通常采用这种做法[35]。作为一种传统的GIS方法，它仍然广泛应用于最近的许多GeoAI研究中，例如建筑/建筑群分类和泛化（将矢量建筑多边形转换为地图图像）[60]、空气污染预测（即将基于点的空气污染测量值转换为插值空气污染场）[57]和交通模型识别（将GPS轨迹转换为轨迹图像）[39]。这种方法的缺点是有据可查的：1）转换可能导致形状细节和拓扑信息的大量信息丢失，并且基于矢量到栅格函数的损失在大多数情况下是不可逆的，因为转换过程是不可微分的； 2）当待建模过程根据空间尺度或层次结构以不同方式表示时，任意尺度决策（例如像素大小）可能会导致可修改面积单位问题（MAUP）。A major challenge in raster and vector joint learning lies in the fundamental differences between raster and vector data representations. Raster data, such as satellite imagery, represent continuous spatial fields and naturally align with the inductive biases of deep learning architectures, particularly convolutional and transformer-based models designed to process dense grid structures. In contrast, vector data are inherently discrete, sparse, and structured, consisting of geometries, topological relationships, and semantic attributes. Because of these differences, designing effective SRL models for vector data remains challenging. As a result, vector-to-raster conversion is a common strategy that converts vector data into raster images before feeding it into neural networks. Such practice is usually adopted due to the lack of specialized approaches directly encoding vector data into the neural networks [35]. As a traditional GIS approach, it is still widely used in many recent GeoAI studies, such as building/building group classification and generalization (converting vector building polygons into map images) [60], air pollution forecasting (i.e., converting pointbased air pollution measures into interpolated air pollution field) [57], and transportation model identification (converting GPS trajectories into trajectory images) [39]. The drawbacks of such an approach are well-documented: 1) conversions can lead to substantial information loss of shape details and topological information, and losses based on vector-to-raster functions are, in most cases, irreversible since the conversion process is non-differentiable; 2) arbitrary scale decisions, e.g., the pixel size, can lead to a modifiable areal unit problem (MAUP), when the to-bemodeled process is represented differently depending on spatial scales or hierarchies.

3.4.统一空间基础模型的研究议程3.4. Research Agenda for Unified Spatial Foundation Models

我们确定了加速 SRL 研究统一栅格矢量空间基础模型的四个关键方面：（1）方法创新，（2）空间基础模型的公平性，（3）针对栅格和以矢量为中心的地理空间应用的评估，以及（4）不确定性量化和可解释性的方法。We identify four key aspects for accelerating SRL research towards unified raster-vector spatial foundation models: (1) methodological innovations, (2) fairness in spatial foundation models, (3) evaluations that target both raster and vector-centric geospatial applications, and (4) approaches for uncertainty quantification and interpretability.

方法论创新：Methodological innovations:实现统一的空间表示学习需要解决几个架构和经验挑战。首先，为了选择模型预训练目标，跨模态对齐需要预训练课程，将每种模态双向调节到另一种模态。联合对比目标可用于将像素邻域与相应的地理空间对齐的地理实体进行注册，并且相互重建损失可以促进信息共生。除了预训练目标之外，栅格和矢量表示之间的集成机制提出了另一个基本挑战。由于栅格和矢量数据不同的空间结构和语义特征，简单地通过共享编码器传递栅格和矢量数据可能不是最有效的解决方案。相反，集成可能发生在学习管道的不同阶段，具体取决于下游应用程序。虽然联合编码策略代表了一种可能的方法，但栅格和矢量表示也可以在解码、推理或生成阶段发挥补充作用。例如，栅格观测可以充当矢量嵌入的上下文地理先验，而矢量表示可以为基于栅格的预测提供语义约束或验证信号。其次，为了捕获地理空间数据的多尺度和层次性质，多分辨率空间转换器必须将跨倍频程尺度的栅格观测与对象层次向量表示相协调。分层注意力在表征粒度上双向传播上下文线索，平衡全球地理一致性与局部边界保真度。第三，为了解决全球环境中的空间异质性，拍字节级数据集管理需要卫星档案和动态矢量存储库之间系统的时空对齐，考虑观测节奏差异和区域完整性异质性。最后但并非最不重要的一点是，为了保留每种模态的详细信息，这样一个统一的 SRL 框架应该支持特定于模态的编码器和解码器 [35]，以支持无损数据重建。Realizing unified spatial representation learning demands resolution of several architectural and empirical challenges. Joint contrastive objectives can be utilized to register pixel neighborhoods with corresponding geospatially aligned geo-entities, and reciprocal reconstruction losses can foster informational symbiosis. Beyond pretraining objectives, the integration mechanism between raster and vector representations presents another fundamental challenge. Due to their distinct spatial structures and semantic characteristics, simply passing raster and vector data through a shared encoder may not be the most effective solution. While joint encoding strategies represent one possible approach, raster and vector representations may also play complementary roles during decoding, reasoning, or generation stages. For example, raster observations may serve as contextual geopriors for vector embeddings, whereas vector representations may provide semantic constraints or validation signals for raster-based predictions. Second, to capture the multiscale and hierarchical nature of the geospatial data, multiresolution spatial transformers must reconcile raster observations across octave-spaced scales with object-hierarchical vector representations. Hierarchical attention propagates contextual cues bidirectionally across representational granularity, balancing global geographic coherence with localized boundary fidelity. Third, in order to tackle spatial heterogeneity in a global setting, petabyte-scale dataset curation demands systematic spatial-temporal alignment between satellite archives and dynamic vector repositories, accounting for observational cadence disparities and regional completeness heterogeneity. Last but not least, to preserve the detailed information of each modality, such a unified SRL framework should support modality-specific encoders and decoders [35] to support lossless data reconstruction.

栅格和矢量表示为地理现实提供了互补的优势：连续的感知密度面对离散的结构语义。当代地理空间人工智能通过脱节、信息退化的转换来处理这些模式，从而破坏了表征的连贯性。原生统一空间学习综合了共享架构中的两个领域，利用跨模式注意力和协作预训练来打造基于上下文的地理空间智能。Raster and vector representations furnish complementary vantages upon geographic reality: continuous perceptual density confronts discrete structural semantics. Contemporary geospatial artificial intelligence processes these modalities through disjointed, information-degrading transformations that undermine representational coherence. Native unified spatial learning synthesizes both domains within shared architectures, harnessing cross-modal attention and collaborative pretraining to forge contextually grounded geospatial intelligence.

这种视角描绘了从栅格感知到矢量结构的概念基础，仔细审视了流行集成范式的局限性，并绘制了统一空间基础模型的精确研究轨迹。通过感知结构综合，后续的地理空间系统有望在环境监测、城市系统分析、应急响应等领域增强鲁棒性、可解释性和现实世界的实用性。研究界面临着一个明确的当务之急：构建空间人工智能，既理解地球的可观测表面，又理解人类强加于其上的故意结构。This perspective delineates the conceptual foundations from raster perception through vector structure, scrutinizes limitations of prevailing integration paradigms, and charts a precise research trajectory toward unified spatial foundation models. Through perceptual-structural synthesis, subsequent geospatial systems promise enhanced robustness, interpretability, and real-world utility across environmental monitoring, urban systems analysis, emergency response, and beyond. The research community confronts an unambiguous imperative: to construct spatial artificial intelligence that comprehends both Earth’s observable surface and the deliberate structure humanity imposes upon it.
