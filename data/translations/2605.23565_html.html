摘要Abstract

强化学习代理经常在其训练分布之外表现出非预期的目标导向行为，但我们目前对此类代理如何根据其训练历史泛化到新环境缺乏原则性的理解。 我们为在一项或多项任务上顺序训练的智能体解决了这一差距。 我们研究了 100 多个连续训练管道，评估了 250 多个分布外环境中的行为。我们发现显着的特征可以促进泛化，并且早期训练中学到的目标可以持续存在并影响后来获得的目标。 为了解释这些现象，我们引入Reinforcement learning agents often exhibit unintended goal-directed behaviour outside their training distribution, but we currently lack a principled understanding of how such agents will generalise to novel environments based on their training history. We address this gap for agents trained sequentially on one or more tasks. We study over 100 sequential training pipelines, evaluating behaviour across over 250 out-of-distribution environments. We find that salient features drive generalisation, and that goals learnt early in training can persist and influence those acquired later. To explain these phenomena, we introduce潜在的政策梯度latent policy gradients，一种预测训练管道可能引发的分布外行为的方法。 我们的方法根据潜在变量如何映射到行为的简单模型，根据训练目标上获得高回报的方式来模拟训练期间低维潜在变量的演化。 它具有很强的预测准确性，可推广到看不见的训练流程类型，并且是可解释的。 我们的研究结果表明，虽然分布外 RL 代理的行为依赖于整个训练流程，但这种依赖性具有我们可以捕获的潜在结构，为从发展角度理解目标泛化奠定了基础。, a method that predicts what out-of-distribution behaviour a training pipeline will likely induce. Our method simulates the evolution of low-dimensional latent variables during training according to what would achieve high reward on the training objective with respect to a simple model of how the latent variables map to behaviour. It achieves strong predictive accuracy, generalises to unseen types of training pipeline, and is interpretable. Our findings demonstrate that while out-of-distribution RL agent behaviour is dependent on the whole training pipeline, this dependence has an underlying structure we can capture, laying groundwork for understanding goal generalisation from a developmental perspective.

2 背景Background

RL 特工展示An RL agent exhibits目标错误概括goal misgeneralisation当它表现出色但追求分配外（OOD）投入的意外目标时when it performs competently but pursues unintended goals on out-of-distribution (OOD) inputs(Shah et al., 2022; Langosco et al., 2023)——代理人表现得称职，但目标却错误。 目标错误概括给我们安全部署 RL 智能体的能力带来了巨大差距，因为它可能导致智能体在训练和评估期间表现良好，但在部署后表现出意外。 在本文中，我们研究了更广泛的问题—the agent behaves competently, but toward the wrong objective. Goal misgeneralisation poses a significant gap in our ability to safely deploy RL agents, as it can lead to agents that perform well during training and evaluation but behave unexpectedly once deployed. In this paper, we investigate the broader problem of目标概括goal generalisation：在了解智能体训练流程的情况下，我们能否预测智能体的学习行为将如何推广到未见过的环境？: given knowledge of an agent’s training pipeline, can we predict how the agent’s learnt behaviour will generalise to unseen environments?

6 讨论Discussion

本文将目标泛化问题表述为从训练管道中预测 OOD 行为，并引入潜在策略梯度作为解决方案。 性能最佳的参数化使用显着性矩阵来捕获不同的特征学习率和跨特征混淆——我们在实证分析中观察到的现象。 除了经验拟合之外，潜在策略梯度还可以解释：它们的动态对应于超平面上的迭代投影，并且它们的超参数定义了特征相似性度量。 与描述给定模型所学内容的标准可解释性方法不同，潜在策略梯度预测训练期间偏好如何形成。This paper formulates the goal generalisation problem as predicting OOD behaviour from training pipelines, and introduces latent policy gradients as a solution. The best-performing parametrisation uses a saliency matrix to capture differing feature learning rates and cross-feature confusion—phenomena observed in our empirical analysis. Beyond empirical fit, latent policy gradients admit interpretation: their dynamics correspond to iterated projection onto hyperplanes, and their hyperparameters define a feature similarity metric. Unlike standard explainability methods, which characterise what a given model has learned, latent policy gradients predict how preferences form during training.

神经网络可塑性。Neural Network Plasticity.

我们报告的两种现象——价值持久性和价值抑制——与持续学习中的可塑性效应一致Two of the phenomena we reported—value persistence and value inhibition—are consistent with plasticity effects in continual learning(Nikishin et al., 2022, 2023; Dohare et al., 2024; Lyle et al., 2024)，梯度饥饿, gradient starvation(Pezeshki et al., 2021)，以及休眠神经元的出现, and the emergence of dormant neurons(Sokar et al., 2023)。 我们展示了这些影响对多阶段目标导向强化学习中目标泛化的影响，并进一步表明它们可以通过具有可解释结构的简单潜在模型来捕获。. We show the impact these effects have on goal generalisation in multi-stage goal-directed RL, and further show that they can be captured by a simple latent model with interpretable structure.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and future work.

Our saliency findings (例如e.g.，形状优于颜色）反映了 CNN 归纳偏差，尽管 LPG 框架本身与架构无关。 我们的每个代理都使用单个随机种子进行训练，尽管大量的管道排列提供了起到类似作用的对称性。 我们使用的偏好函数参数化假设目标值在特征上线性分解。这非常适合我们领域的阶乘结构，但可能不适用于目标具有更复杂、非组合结构的环境。该框架支持替代参数化，但我们尚未探索它们。 最后，我们对代理偏好的预测并不完美，我们欢迎未来的工作，以提高我们数据集上目标泛化问题的性能。, shape over colour) reflect CNN inductive biases, though the LPG framework itself is architecture-agnostic. Our agents were each trained with a single random seed, though the large number of pipeline permutations provides symmetries that serve a similar role. The preference function parametrisation we used assumes that goal values decompose linearly over features. This is well-suited to the factorial structure of our domain, but may not hold in settings where goals have more complex, non-compositional structure. The framework accommodates alternative parametrisations, but we have not yet explored them. Finally, our predictions of agent preferences were not perfect, and we welcome future work which improves performance on the goal generalisation problem on our dataset.

更广泛的影响。Broader impacts.

这项工作的动机是人工智能安全：预测训练管道如何塑造 OOD 行为对于安全部署 RL 系统是必要的。通过提供可解释的方法来预测目标泛化，我们的目标是为构建更易于理解的人工智能系统做出贡献。我们预计这项基础研究不会产生负面的社会后果。This work is motivated by AI safety: predicting how training pipelines shape OOD behaviour is necessary for safe deployment of RL systems. By providing interpretable methods for predicting goal generalisation, we aim to contribute to building more understandable AI systems. We do not foresee negative societal consequences from this foundational research.

结论。Conclusion.

强化学习代理的值是路径相关的，并且先前的训练可以显着影响模型推广 OOD 的方式。 然而，这种路径依赖具有潜在的结构。潜在策略梯度提供了一种通过少量潜在变量的演化来预测目标泛化的方法。 我们的最终动机是了解多阶段训练流程如何塑造前沿人工智能系统的价值，我们将这里建立的问题表述、评估方法和基线方法视为实现这一目标的基础。 潜在策略梯度的数学结构是通用的，我们相信它为将这项工作扩展到更复杂的系统提供了坚实的基础。The values of RL agents are path dependent, and prior training can significantly influence how a model generalises OOD. However, this path-dependence has underlying structure. Latent policy gradients provide a means of predicting goal generalisation via the evolution of a small number of latent variables. Our ultimate motivation is understanding how multi-stage training pipelines shape the values of frontier AI systems, and we see the problem formulation, evaluation methodology, and baseline method established here as a foundation for this goal. The mathematical structure of latent policy gradients is general, and we believe it provides a strong foundation for extending this work to more complex systems.

迁移学习和人工智能对齐。Transfer learning and AI alignment.

迁移学习是现代机器学习的常见范例Transfer learning is a common paradigm in modern machine learning[Iman et al., 2023]。 最值得注意的例子是使用在大型文本语料库上预先训练的法学硕士，然后对强化学习进行微调以构建有用的助手. The most notable example is the use of LLMs pre-trained on large text corpora and then RL fine-tuned to build helpful assistants[Bai et al., 2022a]或强大的推理家or powerful reasoners[DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025]，但基础模型在机器人领域的应用也正在探索中, but applications of foundation models are also being explored in robotics[Firoozi et al., 2025]。 通过迁移学习训练的系统处于人工智能发展的前沿，人们担心强大的人工智能系统的安全性. Systems trained via transfer learning are at the frontier of AI developments, with concerns over the safety of powerful AI systems[Amodei et al., 2016, Hubinger et al., 2021, Ngo et al., 2025, Hendrycks et al., 2023]激发各种复杂的方法来尝试更好地使这些模型与人类价值观保持一致motivating a variety of complex approaches to try and better align these models with human values[Bai et al., 2022b, Guan et al., 2025, Maiya et al., 2025, Rafailov et al., 2023].

然而，人们对这些复杂的训练流程（从预训练到训练后的对齐和推理）如何最终塑造其生成的模型的最终值和行为了解甚少。 尽管看起来在表面上有效，但对越狱的研究However, little is understood about how these complex training pipelines, from pre-training to alignment and reasoning post-training, end up shaping the final values and behaviours of the models they produce. Despite seeming to work at the surface level, research on jailbreaks[Yi et al., 2024], 紧急失准, emergent misalignment[Betley et al., 2026, Taylor et al., 2025]，以及在更代理环境中的错位, and misalignment in more agentic settings[Lynch et al., 2025, Andriushchenko et al., 2025, Naik et al., 2025]表明还有很多工作要做。 也就是说，考虑整个训练流程的对齐方法正在开始探索show there is still lots more work to be done. That said, alignment approaches that consider the entire training pipeline are starting to be explored[Tice et al., 2026]，尽管我们缺乏原则理论来理解经验观察到的影响。, though we lack a principled theory to understand the empirically observed effects.

In our paper we try to better understand goal generalisation, and how it is influenced by multi-stage training pipelines.

推断代理的值。Inferring the values of agents.

在我们的工作中，我们尝试从行为上推断人工智能代理的价值，然后尝试仅利用训练流程的知识来预测它们。 虽然我们的方法很简单，但逆强化学习 (IRL) 子领域的广泛工作已经详细分析了更多推断支持代理行为的潜在价值的方法，以及可能激励代理行为的奖励结构In our work, we try to infer the values of AI agents behaviourally, and then try to predict them with just knowledge of the training pipeline. Whilst our methods are simple, extensive work in the subfield of inverse RL (IRL) has detailed and analysed many more approaches for inferring latent values underpinning agentic behaviour, and the reward structures that might incentivise it[Wulfmeier et al., 2016, Skalse and Abate, 2023, Ramachandran and Amir, , Hadfield-Menell et al., 2016, Adams et al., 2022, Ziebart et al., 2008, Abbeel and Ng, 2004, Brown et al., 2025]。 然而，这主要局限于机器人或游戏任务，并且侧重于构建奖励函数，而不是理解代理价值形成的动态。. However, this has mostly been restricted to robotics or game-playing tasks, and focuses on building reward functions, rather than understanding the dynamics of agent value formation.

机器学习中的泛化。Generalisation in machine learning.

除了价值观和目标之外，还有大量关于 RL 系统如何泛化的现有文献Beyond values and goals, there is a large body of existing literature on how RL systems generalise[Cobbe et al., 2019, Zhao et al., 2019, Kirk et al., 2023]，尽管这主要关注代理能力的概括，而不是他们的价值。 著名的作品强调了过度拟合的问题以及如何处理它, though this is mostly focussed on generalisation of agent capabilities, rather than their values. Prominent works highlight the issue of overfitting, and how to deal with it[Cobbe et al., 2019, 2020]。 这促使我们在任务中使用程序生成，迫使我们的智能体尽可能地将他们的能力推广到具有不熟悉目标的新迷宫。.
