I 简介Introduction

智能电表现已在全球范围内广泛部署，定期记录家庭用电量并生成用于能源分析的大规模数据集Smart meters are now widely deployed worldwide, recording household electricity consumption at regular intervals and generating large-scale datasets for energy analytics[1]。 通常每 10-30 分钟收集一次，具体取决于国家/地区. Typically collected every 10–30 minutes, depending on the country[2]，这些测量提供了对住宅用电的详细了解。 公用事业公司依靠这些数据进行需求预测、计费以及个性化反馈工具、分时电价和需求响应计划等服务的开发, these measurements provide detailed insights into residential electricity use. Utilities rely on such data for demand forecasting, billing, and the development of services such as personalized feedback tools, time-of-use tariffs, and demand-response programs[2, 3]。 在这个粒度上，智能电表数据还支持广泛的数据驱动应用，包括客户细分、非侵入式负载监控以及家庭节能行动评估. At this granularity, smart-meter data also support a wide range of data-driven applications, including customer segmentation, non-intrusive load monitoring, and the evaluation of household energy-efficiency actions[4, 5, 6, 7]。 因此，智能电表已成为现代能源分析和电力系统规划的基石。. Thus, smart meters have become a cornerstone of modern energy analytics and power-system planning.

然而，根据 GDPR，单个智能电表负载曲线属于个人数据However, individual smart-meter load curves are personal data under GDPR[8]，可能会泄露占用时间表、工作习惯、假期或电器使用情况, potentially revealing occupancy schedules, work habits, holidays, or appliance use[9]。因此，访问受到严格的法律和技术保障，而高级分析通常仍局限于公用事业基础设施内，限制了外部研究人员、监管机构和第三方创新者的访问. As a result, access is subject to strict legal and technical safeguards, and advanced analytics often remain confined within utility infrastructures, limiting access for external researchers, regulators, and third-party innovators[10].

因此，合成数据生成已成为一种有前途的替代方案：人工负载曲线可以再现真实用电量的统计特性，但不对应于任何真实家庭。 早期的方法依赖于将设备模型与占用模式相结合的参数化或基于规则的模拟器Synthetic data generation has therefore emerged as a promising alternative: artificial load curves that reproduce the statistical properties of real electricity consumption while not corresponding to any real household. Early approaches relied on parametric or rule-based simulators combining appliance models with occupancy patterns[11].

最近，深度生成模型，例如变分自动编码器More recently, deep generative models, e.g., variational autoencoders[12]，生成对抗网络, generative adversarial networks[13]和扩散模型and diffusion models[14]，已被用来直接从数据中学习现实的消费模式。 然而，现有的方法存在一些局限性：它们通常关注短期行为（例如，一天或一周）而不是长期行为, have been used to learn realistic consumption patterns directly from data. IV LoaDiff 方法The LoaDiff approach

本节介绍This section introduces负载差异LoaDiff，智能电表负荷曲线的条件生成模型。, a conditional generative model for smart-meter load curves.负载差异LoaDiff学习单个家庭用电轨迹的分布，捕获多尺度时间模式，同时允许对日历特征、室外温度和电器所有权等外生因素进行调节。 以下小节描述了我们方法的主要组成部分：learns a distribution over individual household electricity trajectories that captures multi-scale temporal patterns, while allowing conditioning on exogenous factors such as calendar features, outdoor temperature, and appliance ownership. The following subsections describe the main components of our approach:

基于扩散的生成。Diffusion-based generation. 负载差异LoaDiff依赖于去噪扩散框架，在该框架中，模型经过训练以逐步从损坏的时间序列中去除噪声。在推理时，通过迭代地对随机噪声序列进行去噪来生成真实的负载曲线。relies on a denoising diffusion framework, in which the model is trained to progressively remove noise from corrupted time series. VI 结论和局限性Conclusions and Limitations

我们介绍了We introduced负载差异LoaDiff，一种条件扩散模型，用于在隐私约束下生成真实的智能电表负荷曲线。在三个独立的数据集中，, a conditional diffusion model for generating realistic smart-meter load curves under privacy constraints. Across three independent datasets,负载差异LoaDiff在保真度（每种设置中最好或第二好的 FID 和 ACD）和隐私（三个数据集中的两个上最低 NNDR）方面始终名列前茅，并且提供了所有比较方法中最强的下游实用性，赢得了 18 个分类器、设备组合中的 16 个，并且在大多数情况下匹配或超过了真实数据预测性能，使其成为个人智能电表数据的实用且保护隐私的替代品。这种优势并不是绝对的：性能因数据集和指标而异，并且没有单一的方法，consistently ranks among the top methods on fidelity (best or second-best FID and ACD in every setting) and privacy (lowest NNDR on two of three datasets), and delivers the strongest downstream utility of any method compared, winning 16 of 18 classifier, appliance combinations and matching or exceeding real-data forecasting performance in most cases, making it a practical, privacy-preserving substitute for individual smart-meter data. This strength is not absolute: performance varies across datasets and metrics, and no single method,负载差异LoaDiff包括在内，在每一项上都占主导地位，强调了为什么评估应该依赖于多个互补指标而不是任何单一分数。included, dominates on every one, underscoring why evaluation should rely on multiple complementary metrics rather than any single score.

我们的三个数据集中有两个是专有的 EDF 数据，无法发布；只有 CER 和我们的代码是公开的，但这意味着我们的结果是在独立人群而不是单一基准上进行验证的。我们的隐私评估依赖于基于距离的启发式方法，而不是正式的保证或基于攻击的评估，并且我们的温度敏感性分析仅涵盖两个反事实转变；两者都应被理解为指示性的而非详尽无遗的。Two of our three datasets are proprietary EDF data and cannot be released; only CER and our code are public, though this means our results are validated on independent populations rather than a single benchmark. Our privacy assessment relies on distance-based heuristics rather than formal guarantees or attack-based evaluations, and our temperature-sensitivity analysis covers only two counterfactual shifts; both should be read as indicative rather than exhaustive.
