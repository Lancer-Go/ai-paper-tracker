摘要。Abstract.

随着智能体策略的发展，技能扮演着不同的角色：它们首先应该提供可学习的知识，然后支持能力的形成，最后只有在改进个人决策时才被调用。现有的方法很少对这个生命周期进行建模。他们要么将技能保留在模型之外，将其完全内化，要么通过嘈杂的任务级成功率在内化和利用目标之间进行选择。这种设计将训练分割开来，并对同一轨迹内的行动赋予统一的重要性，尽管技能指导可能有助于某些决策，同时分散其他决策的注意力。为了解决这些问题，我们引入Skills play different roles as an agent’s policy evolves: they should first provide learnable knowledge, then support capability formation, and finally be invoked only when they improve individual decisions. Existing methods rarely model this lifecycle. They either keep skills outside the model, fully internalize them, or select among internalization and utilization objectives through noisy task-level success rates. Such designs fragment training and assign uniform importance to actions within the same trajectory, even though skill guidance may help some decisions while distracting others. To solve these problems, we introduceAUSO（动作级统一技能优化）AUSO (Action-level Unified Skill Optimization)，它通过渐进的、行动感知的优化过程将技能学习和技能使用统一起来。在培训开始时，AUSO 共同从教师指导和环境结果中学习，使政策能够在不失去以任务为导向的反馈的情况下获得基础技能。随后强调基于结果的政策优化，以巩固自主解决问题的能力。随着政策的成熟，AUSO 会在技能条件和无技能环境下评估每个样本行动。由此产生的动作级信息信号与轨迹结果优势相结合，允许有益的技能敏感动作接收更强的更新，并抑制有害的动作。因此，技能逐渐从外部监督来源转变为决策知识，其使用适应其行动层面的效益，而强化学习仍然是所有阶段的共同支柱。 ALFWorld、WebShop 和 SearchQA 上的实验表明，与竞争基线相比，AUSO 持续提高了代理性能和分布外泛化能力。我们的代码位于, which unifies skill learning and skill use through a progressive, action-aware optimization process. At the beginning of training, AUSO jointly learns from teacher guidance and environmental outcomes, enabling the policy to acquire foundational skills without losing task-oriented feedback. It subsequently emphasizes outcome-based policy optimization to consolidate autonomous problem-solving ability. As the policy matures, AUSO evaluates each sampled action under both skill-conditioned and skill-free contexts. The resulting action-level information signal is coupled with the trajectory outcome advantage, allowing beneficial skill-sensitive actions to receive stronger updates and harmful ones to be suppressed. Therefore, skills gradually transition from an external source of supervision into decision knowledge whose utilization is adapted to its action-level benefit, while reinforcement learning remains the shared backbone across all stages. Experiments on ALFWorld, WebShop, and SearchQA show that AUSO consistently improves agent performance and out-of-distribution generalization over competitive baselines. 1. 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 日益从静态文本生成器发展为交互式代理，可在复杂的长期环境中做出顺序决策。这些代理不是产生单一响应，而是交织推理、行动和观察：它们调用外部工具、检索信息、导航 Web 界面、操纵具体环境并根据反馈修改计划Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly evolving from static text generators into interactive agents that make sequential decisions in complex, long-horizon environments. Rather than producing a single response, these agents interleave reasoning, acting, and observation: they call external tools, retrieve information, navigate web interfaces, manipulate embodied environments, and revise plans based on feedback(Yao et al. 2023b; Schick et al. 2023; Zhou et al. 2023; Huang et al. 2025a)。这种以交互为中心的设置是现代信息访问和决策任务的核心，包括网络购物、具体家庭执行和基于搜索的问答. Such interaction-centric settings are central to modern information access and decision-making tasks, including web shopping, embodied household execution, and search-based question answering(Yao et al. 2022; Shridhar et al. 2021; Dunn et al. 2017; Lou et al. 2025)。然而，随着任务范围的扩大，智能体不仅必须反复决定要采取什么行动，而且还要决定哪些程序知识、工具使用策略或环境启发应该指导该行动。. However, as task horizons grow, agents must repeatedly decide not only what action to take, but also which procedural knowledge, tool-use strategy, or environmental heuristic should guide that action.

代理技能已经成为解决这个问题的一个有前途的抽象。它们对可重用的程序知识、任务策略、工具使用模式或特定环境的指导进行编码，从而为代理提供可转移的先验以进行长期决策Agent skills have emerged as a promising abstraction for this problem. They encode reusable procedural knowledge, task strategies, tool-use patterns, or environment-specific guidance, thereby providing agents with transferable priors for long-horizon decision-making(Wang et al. 2023; Huang et al. 2026)。在基于提示的代理中，技能被检索、组合或注入作为明确的指导，以支持复杂的交互式任务解决. In prompt-based agents, skills are retrieved, composed, or injected as explicit guidance to support complex interactive task solving(Xia et al. 2026)。 最近的训练后方法通过细粒度的环境反馈进一步优化各种技能条件行为，将技能使用从静态提示转向代理策略的可学习组件. Recent post-training methods further optimize diverse skill-conditioned behaviors through fine-grained environment feedback, shifting skill usage from static prompts toward learnable components of agent policies(Shi et al. 2026; Zhu et al. 2026).

现有的基于技能的代理学习方法可以大致分为三种范式。第一个范式完全外化技能：代理在模型外部保留持久的技能库，并在任务执行期间检索或更新技能，如 Voyager 中那样Existing skill-based agent learning methods can be broadly organized into three paradigms. The first paradigm fully externalizes skills: the agent keeps a persistent skill library outside the model and retrieves or updates skills during task execution, as in Voyager(Wang et al. 2023), 技能RL, SkillRL(Xia et al. 2026)，和技能1, and Skill1(Shi et al. 2026)。这种设计保留了可解释性、模块化和组合重用，但也引入了上下文开销和检索噪声。第二范式将技能完全内化到模型参数中，旨在消除推理时技能提示并实现自主执行，如 SKILL0 所示. This design preserves interpretability, modularity, and compositional reuse, but also introduces context overhead and retrieval noise. The second paradigm fully internalizes skills into the model parameters, aiming to eliminate inference-time skill prompts and enable autonomous execution, as in SKILL0(Lu et al. 2026), 潜在技能, LatentSkill(Yu et al. 2026)以及相关的技能到参数方法, and related skill-to-parameter approaches(Zhang and Qi 2026)。这提高了令牌效率并减少了对外部记忆的依赖，但可能会失去显性技能的可控性和特定于任务的适应性。第三范式共同外化和内化技能。技能0.5. This improves token efficiency and reduces dependence on external memory, but may lose the controllability and task-specific adaptability of explicit skills. The third paradigm jointly externalizes and internalizes skills. Skill0.5(Zhu et al. 2026)将通用技能内化到模型中，同时保留明确的特定于任务的技能利用，证明这种混合策略可以更好地平衡不同环境中的跨任务转移、推理效率和任务级专业化。internalizes generalizable skills into the model while retaining explicit task-specific skill utilization, demonstrating that such a hybrid strategy can better balance cross-task transfer, inference efficiency, and task-level specialization across diverse environments.

尽管取得了这些进展，现有的混合方法仍然存在关键的优化差距。 Skill0.5 依靠任务级别的成功率将样本划分为难度级别，然后将不同的目标应用于困难、中等和简单的任务。这种路由使得内化和利用之间的边界对噪声聚合结果敏感：具有非常相似的经验成功率的样本可能会接受不同的训练处理，仅仅因为它们落在阈值的两侧。如图Despite this progress, existing hybrid methods still leave a key optimization gap. Skill0.5 relies on task-level success rates to route samples into difficulty tiers and then applies different objectives to hard, medium, and easy tasks. Such routing makes the boundary between internalization and utilization sensitive to noisy aggregate outcomes: samples with very similar empirical success rates may receive different training treatment simply because they fall on opposite sides of a threshold. As shown in Figure1，这个问题在实践中并不罕见：在 ALFWorld、WebShop 和 SearchQA 中，大量样本位于中等/简单边界附近，它们的通过率最多相差一个 rollout 单元，但它们被分配给不同的优化目标。这一观察结果表明，轨迹级路由引入的固有边界促使我们超越明确的技能选择，追求更统一的学习范式，其中技能首先内化到策略中，然后在后续交互中自然地利用。更重要的是，轨迹级技能路由广泛且稳定，但对于技能指导改变特定决策质量的关键状态来说可能过于粗糙。相比之下，动作级技能信号更精确，但更难获得，因为环境通常只提供轨迹级奖励。这种粗略的结果反馈无法直接揭示哪些个体行为受益于技能调节，哪些行为受到技能调节的干扰，这与强化学习中的学分分配挑战相呼应, this issue is not rare in practice: across ALFWorld, WebShop, and SearchQA, a substantial number of samples lie around the medium/easy boundary, where their pass rates differ by at most one rollout unit but they are assigned to different optimization objectives. This observation suggests that the inherent boundaries introduced by trajectory-level routing motivate us to move beyond explicit skill selection and pursue a more unified learning paradigm, where skills are first internalized into the policy and then naturally utilized during subsequent interaction. More importantly, trajectory-level skill routing is broad and stable, but may be too coarse for pivotal states where skill guidance changes the quality of a specific decision. In contrast, action-level skill signals are more precise, but are harder to obtain because the environment usually provides only trajectory-level rewards. Such coarse outcome feedback cannot directly reveal which individual actions benefit from skill conditioning and which actions are distracted by it, echoing the credit-assignment challenge in reinforcement learning(Schulman et al. 2017; Shao et al. 2024)。因此，现有的方法很难在技能内化和利用方面以代理决策的实际粒度来优化技能的使用。. As a result, existing methods struggle to optimize skill use at actual granularity of agent decision making both in skills internalization and utilization.

为了解决这些限制，我们建议To address these limitations, we proposeAUSO（动作级统一技能优化）AUSO (Action-level Unified Skill Optimization)，一种渐进式强化学习框架，通过行动感知优化将技能内化和技能利用统一起来。 AUSO 的核心是采用詹森-香农散度 (JSD) 作为统一的信息增益信号来量化技能指导如何改变政策的行动分布，从而支持行动层面的技能内化和利用。 AUSO 首先允许策略从教师提供的技能指导和环境结果中学习，然后通过基于结果的策略优化来巩固自主解决问题的能力，最后在技能条件和无技能环境下评估每个样本动作。通过将这种动作级技能敏感性信号与轨迹级优势结合起来，AUSO 加强了对真正受益于技能的动作的更新，同时抑制了那些技能指导有害的动作。通过这种方式，技能逐渐从外部监督转变为根据其行动层面收益使用的决策知识，而强化学习仍然是整个智能体政策演变的共同支柱。大量实验表明，AUSO 在 WebShop、ALFWorld 和 Search-QA 上始终优于 Skill0.5 等强基线，在 WebShop 和 ALFWorld 上的 ID 和 OOD 设置下，以及在 Search-QA 上的单跳和多跳任务上显示出稳健的改进，这显示了 AUSO 在不同代理任务和技能设置中的有效性和泛化能力。我们的贡献如下：, a progressive reinforcement learning framework that unifies skill internalization and skill utilization through action-aware optimization. At its core, AUSO employs Jensen–Shannon divergence (JSD) as a unified information-gain signal to quantify how skill guidance changes the policy’s action distribution, supporting both skill internalization and utilization at the action level. AUSO first allows the policy to learn from both teacher-provided skill guidance and environment outcomes, then consolidates autonomous problem-solving ability through outcome-based policy optimization, and finally evaluates each sampled action under both skill-conditioned and skill-free contexts. By coupling this action-level skill-sensitivity signal with trajectory-level advantage, AUSO strengthens updates for actions that genuinely benefit from skills while suppressing those where skill guidance is harmful. In this way, skills gradually transition from external supervision into decision knowledge utilized according to their action-level benefits, while reinforcement learning remains the shared backbone throughout the agent’s policy evolution. Extensive experiments demonstrate that AUSO consistently outperforms strong baselines such as Skill0.5 across WebShop, ALFWorld, and Search-QA, showing robust improvements under both ID and OOD settings on WebShop and ALFWorld, as well as across single-hop and multi-hop tasks on Search-QA which shows effectiveness and generalization ability of AUSO across diverse agent tasks and skill settings. 2. 相关工作Related Work

2.1. 法学硕士代理LLM Agents

大型语言模型越来越多地被研究为交互式代理，通过迭代推理、动作执行和外部环境的反馈来解决长期任务。思想链、ReAct 和思想树等提示方法表明，中间推理和深思熟虑的搜索可以显着改善复杂的决策Large language models have increasingly been studied as interactive agents that solve long-horizon tasks through iterative reasoning, action execution, and feedback from external environments. Prompting methods such as chain-of-thought, ReAct, and Tree-of-Thoughts show that intermediate reasoning and deliberate search can substantially improve complex decision making(Wei et al. 2022; Yao et al. 2023b; Yao et al. 2023a)。工具增强代理通过启用 API 调用、搜索、计算和其他外部操作，进一步将 LLM 扩展到纯文本生成之外. Tool-augmented agents further extend LLMs beyond text-only generation by enabling API calls, search, calculation, and other external operations(Schick et al. 2023; Li et al. 2023)。这些功能激发了一系列广泛的代理基准，涵盖网络导航、在线购物、具体家务、软件工程和一般代理评估. These capabilities have motivated a broad set of agent benchmarks covering web navigation, online shopping, embodied household tasks, software engineering, and general agent evaluation(Yao et al. 2022; Shridhar et al. 2021; Zhou et al. 2023; Deng et al. 2023; Liu et al. 2023; Chen et al. 2026b).

另一项工作是通过记忆来改进 LLM 代理Another line of work improves LLM agents through memory(Cong et al. 2026; Ye et al. 2026)、反思、经验重用或交互强化学习。反射使用以前失败的口头反馈来指导未来的决策，而无需更新模型参数, reflection, experience reuse, or reinforcement learning from interaction. Reflexion uses verbal feedback from previous failures to guide future decisions without updating model parameters(Shinn et al. 2023)，而 Voyager 在开放式的具体环境中构建可重用的程序和课程驱动的探索, while Voyager constructs reusable programs and curriculum-driven exploration in open-ended embodied environments(Wang et al. 2023)。最近关于代理强化学习的工作进一步强调了交互式环境的训练后法学硕士不同于标准静态偏好优化. Recent work on agentic reinforcement learning further emphasizes that post-training LLMs for interactive environments differs from standard static preference optimization(Huang et al. 2025b; Gao et al. 2026)，因为智能体必须收集轨迹，与环境状态交互，并在延迟结果下优化行为, because agents must collect trajectories, interact with environment states, and optimize behavior under delayed outcomes(Zhang et al. 2025; Chen et al. 2026b)。然而，大多数LLM代理方法仍然将外部指导、记忆和可重用知识视为辅助模块，而不是对这些知识如何逐渐过渡到策略本身进行建模。 AUSO 通过在统一的强化学习周期过程中优化技能学习和技能使用来解决这一差距。. However, most LLM-agent methods still treat external guidance, memory, and reusable knowledge as auxiliary modules rather than modeling how such knowledge should gradually transition into the policy itself. AUSO addresses this gap by optimizing skill learning and skill use in a unified reinforcement learning cycle process.

2.2. 代理技巧Agent Skills

代理技能为解决复杂任务提供可重用的程序知识。在具体化和机器人环境中，技能通常对应于可执行原语、可供感知的子例程或将高级语言指令转化为可行动作的编程任务计划Agent skills provide reusable procedural knowledge for solving complex tasks. In embodied and robotic settings, skills often correspond to executable primitives, affordance-aware subroutines, or programmatic task plans that ground high-level language instructions into feasible actions(Ahn et al. 2022; Singh et al. 2023)。在 LLM 代理中，技能通常表示为自然语言策略、特定于环境的启发法、工具使用模式或存储在外部库中的可执行程序.
