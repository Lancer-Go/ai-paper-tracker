摘要Abstract

1 简介Introduction

基础模型（FM）的最新进展极大地扩展了机器直接解释世界的能力Recent advances in foundation models (FMs) have substantially expanded the ability of machines to interpret the world directly[1]。现代模型可以对文本、图像、表格、图表、代码和日益复杂的多模式环境进行处理和推理. Contemporary models can process and reason over text, images, tables, diagrams, code, and increasingly complex multimodal environments[2, 3, 4, 5, 6]。在许多设置中，机器不再局限于预先设计的符号管道，而是可以摄取原始信息流并生成端到端决策。随着机器智能越来越接近直接理解世界，一个更深层次的问题出现了：智能是否仍然受益于人类设计的抽象，或者仅原始数据就足够了？这个问题与空间信息尤其相关，人类长期以来依赖地图来外化地理推理。. In many settings, machines are no longer limited to pre-designed symbolic pipelines, but can ingest raw information streams and generate decisions end-to-end. As machine intelligence moves closer to direct world understanding, a deeper question emerges: Does intelligence still benefit from human-designed abstractions, or can raw data alone suffice? This question is especially relevant for spatial information, where humans have long relied on maps to externalize geographic reasoning.

在人类设计的抽象概念中，地图是组织空间知识最持久、最有影响力的工具之一Among human-designed abstractions, maps are one of the most enduring and influential tools for organizing spatial knowledge[7]。地图不是将世界呈现为碎片化的坐标、表格或文本描述，而是将地理复杂性转化为结构化的视觉形式，支持有关邻近性、层次结构、拓扑、区域差异和空间模式的推理. Rather than presenting the world as fragmented coordinates, tables, or textual descriptions, maps transform geographic complexity into structured visual forms that support reasoning about proximity, hierarchy, topology, regional disparity, and spatial pattern[15, 16, 17, 18]。在城市规划、环境管理、流行病学、交通和教育等领域，地图长期以来不仅充当插图，而且充当认知界面，减少推理负担并揭示仅从原始记录难以推断的关系. Across domains such as urban planning, environmental management, epidemiology, transportation, and education, maps have long functioned not merely as illustrations, but as cognitive interfaces that reduce reasoning burden and reveal relationships difficult to infer from raw records alone[19, 20, 21, 22]。对于人类来说，地图的价值是众所周知的。然而，类似的好处是否也适用于机器，目前仍不得而知。. For humans, the value of maps is well established. Whether similar benefits extend to machines, however, remains largely unknown.

这种不确定性在FM时代变得更加重要。现代模型可以直接使用结构化地理输入，例如区域统计数据、表格属性、坐标列表、文本报告和机器可读格式（例如 JSON）This uncertainty has become more important in the FM era. Modern models can directly consume structured geographic inputs such as regional statistics, tabular attributes, coordinate lists, textual reports, and machine-readable formats such as JSON[24, 27]。原则上，此类功能可能会减少对传统制图调解的需求。与此同时，越来越多的文献评估了图表、文档、自然图像、具体场景和导航环境的机器推理. In principle, such capabilities may reduce the need for traditional cartographic mediation. At the same time, growing literature has evaluated machine reasoning on charts, documents, natural images, embodied scenes, and navigation environments[10, 11, 12, 9]。然而，大多数先前的研究都集中在感知或通用多模态推理上，而不是询问地图本身是否仍然是机器有用的表示界面。. Yet most prior studies focus on perception or generic multimodal reasoning, rather than asking whether maps themselves remain useful representational interfaces for machines.如果模型已经可以直接处理原始地理数据，那么将这些数据转换为地图是否仍然具有优势？If a model can already process raw geographic data directly, does converting such data into maps still provide an advantage?令人惊讶的是，这个看似简单的问题并没有得到系统的探讨。This seemingly straightforward question has, surprisingly, not been explored systematically.

2 相关工作Related work

2.1 基础模型中的空间理解Spatial Understanding in Foundation Models

近年来，基础模型（FM）取得了快速进展，其功能现已从语言生成扩展到多模态感知、推理和交互式决策Recent years have witnessed rapid progress in foundation models (FMs), whose capabilities now extend beyond language generation to multimodal perception, reasoning, and interactive decision-making[2, 3, 4]。随着这些模型越来越多地部署在各地，评估它们理解空间结构、布局推理以及在部分可观察性下运行的能力已成为一个重要的研究方向。. As these models are increasingly deployed everywhere, evaluating their ability to understand spatial structure, reason about layouts, and operate under partial observability has become an important research direction.

早期研究主要考察静态视觉场景中的空间推理。视觉语言模型在自然图像中的相对位置、物体排列、透视理解和几何关系上进行了测试Early studies mainly examined spatial reasoning in static visual scenes. Vision-language models were tested on relative position, object arrangement, perspective understanding, and geometric relationships in natural images[13, 6]。与此同时，图表和图表基准证明模型可以对结构化视觉抽象进行部分推理，尽管在需要多步骤推理或精确定量比较时性能通常会下降. In parallel, chart and diagram benchmarks demonstrated that models can partially reason over structured visual abstractions, although performance often declines when multi-step reasoning or precise quantitative comparison is required[10, 46, 36, 9]。这些发现表明，现代模型可以从视觉输入中提取空间线索，但它们的成功在很大程度上取决于表征清晰度和任务复杂性。. These findings suggest that modern models can extract spatial cues from visual inputs, but their success depends strongly on representational clarity and task complexity.

第二个研究方向侧重于动态或具体空间环境，其中模型被要求解释以自我为中心的视频、跟踪运动、回答有关场景变化的问题或完成面向导航的任务。代表性基准测试如 OpenEQAA second research line focuses on dynamic or embodied spatial environments, in which models are asked to interpret egocentric videos, track motion, answer questions about changing scenes, or complete navigation-oriented tasks. Representative benchmarks such as OpenEQA[12]揭示长视记忆、自我定位和基于行动的推理仍然具有挑战性。具身人工智能和交互代理的相关工作进一步表明，空间能力不仅需要感知，还需要规划、记忆和顺序决策reveal that long-horizon memory, self-localization, and action-grounded reasoning remain challenging. Related work in embodied AI and interactive agents further shows that spatial competence requires not only perception, but also planning, memory, and sequential decision-making[30, 5, 14, 1].

FM 的快速发展也吸引了 GIS 科学界越来越多的关注，特别是在 GeoAI 的新兴范式下。因此，第三条研究线已经开始探索地理空间智能，其中FM应用于遥感解释、地理问答、地点理解、空间知识检索和地球观测分析等任务The rapid development of FMs has also attracted growing attention from the GIScience community, particularly under the emerging paradigm of GeoAI. As a result, a third research line has begun to explore geospatial intelligence, where FMs are applied to tasks such as remote sensing interpretation, geographic question answering, place understanding, spatial knowledge retrieval, and Earth observation analytics[43, 44, 49, 45, 48, 47]。这些研究凸显了基础模型在地理环境中日益增长的潜力。然而，大多数现有工作主要强调原始图像、文本知识或特定于任务的预测管道。相对而言，很少有人关注制图表示本身是否为机器空间理解的结构化接口提供独特的优势。. These studies highlight the growing potential of foundation models in geographic contexts. However, most existing work primarily emphasizes raw imagery, textual knowledge, or task-specific predictive pipelines. Comparatively little attention has been paid to whether cartographic representations themselves provide unique advantages as structured interfaces for machine spatial understanding.

总体而言，现有文献表明，FM 跨图像、视频、具体场景和地理空间数据进行空间推理的能力越来越强。然而，之前的工作主要关注模型可以做什么，而不是不同的表征形式如何影响它们可以做什么。特别是，地图作为机器推理结构化接口的作用仍未得到充分研究。Overall, existing literature establishes that FMs are increasingly capable of spatial reasoning across images, videos, embodied scenes, and geospatial data. However, prior work has primarily focused on what models can do, rather than how different representational forms influence what they can do. In particular, the role of maps as structured interfaces for machine reasoning remains insufficiently examined.

2.2 地图作为情报的外部空间表示Maps as External Spatial Representations for Intelligence

早在机器智能出现之前，地图就已经成为人类组织空间知识最成功的外部表征之一Long before the emergence of machine intelligence, maps had already become one of humanity’s most successful external representations for organizing spatial knowledge[7]。通过有选择地将现实世界抽象为符号、几何和视觉结构，地图可以有效地推理距离、邻接、区域、方向和空间模式. By selectively abstracting the real world into symbols, geometry, and visual structure, maps enable efficient reasoning about distance, adjacency, region, direction, and spatial pattern[19, 15, 17]。从这个意义上说，地图不仅仅是描述性图形，而且是将地理复杂性转化为可解释形式的认知工具. In this sense, maps are not merely descriptive graphics, but cognitive tools that transform geographic complexity into interpretable forms[22].

制图学和空间认知的研究早已记录了地图如何支持人类推理。认知研究表明，地图设计会影响空间记忆、搜索效率、路线规划和模式识别Research in cartography and spatial cognition has long documented how maps support human reasoning. Cognitive studies show that map design influences spatial memory, search efficiency, route planning, and pattern recognition[16, 20, 34, 23, 26]。地理可视化研究进一步表明，适当设计的地图可以揭示复杂数据中的隐藏结构，特别是当用户必须比较区域、识别集群或检测跨空间趋势时. Geovisualization research further demonstrates that appropriately designed maps can reveal hidden structures in complex data, especially when users must compare regions, identify clusters, or detect trends across space[31, 21]。这些发现表明，地图充当外部认知支架，而不是被动展示。. These findings suggest that maps function as external cognitive scaffolds rather than passive displays.

在地图类型中，专题地图与信息推理尤其相关，因为它们将空间结构与统计属性结合起来。尤其是等值线地图，通过基于区域的颜色编码来表示区域价值，并广泛应用于人口学、流行病学、经济学、气候科学和选举传播Among map types, thematic maps are especially relevant for information reasoning because they integrate spatial structure with statistical attributes. Choropleth maps, in particular, represent regional values through area-based color encoding and are widely used in demography, epidemiology, economics, climate science, and election communication[28, 18, 24]。广泛的制图研究调查了分区统计图设计中的分类方法、配色方案、感知偏差、归一化效应和不确定性通信. Extensive cartographic research has investigated classification methods, color schemes, perceptual bias, normalization effects, and uncertainty communication in choropleth design[35, 29, 50, 37, 38]。该文献主要关注于提高人类的可解释性，表明视觉设计选择强烈影响推断聚类、梯度和异常值的能力。. This literature largely focuses on improving human interpretability, showing that visual design choices strongly affect the ability to infer clusters, gradients, and outliers.

随着人工智能的兴起，地图也开始作为机器输入出现在地图解析、道路提取、地图图像检索和多模态地理系统等应用中With the rise of artificial intelligence, maps have also begun to appear as machine inputs in applications such as map parsing, road extraction, map-image retrieval, and multimodal geographic systems[32, 40, 41, 42, 48]。然而，这些研究通常将地图视为下游任务的另一种数据模式，而不是提出一个更基本的问题：当机器已经可以直接处理原始结构化数据时，地图是否仍然是有用的抽象？这个区别很重要。如果现代 FM 可以同样很好地根据表格、坐标或 JSON 记录进行推理，那么地图对于机器来说可能是多余的。相反，如果基于地图的抽象仍然可以提高性能，那么地图不仅可以继续充当人类的有效外部接口，也可以继续充当机器智能的有效外部接口。据我们所知，这一代表性问题尚未在受控基准设置下进行系统研究。我们的工作通过基于分区统计图的比较研究来解决这一差距。. However, these studies typically treat maps as another data modality for downstream tasks, rather than asking a more fundamental question: do maps remain useful abstractions when machines can already process raw structured data directly? This distinction is important. If modern FMs can reason equally well from tables, coordinates, or JSON records, maps may be redundant for machines. Conversely, if map-based abstraction still improves performance, then maps continue to serve as efficient external interfaces not only for humans, but also for machine intelligence. To our knowledge, this representational question has not been systematically investigated under controlled benchmark settings. Our work addresses this gap through a comparative study based on choropleth maps.

5.6 局限性和未来的工作Limitation and future work

在这项研究中，我们提供的经验证据表明，分区统计图仍然为 FM 提供明显的好处，特别是当视觉地图与符号数据相结合以进行空间理解时。通过比较多个前沿模型中的仅数据、仅地图和数据+地图条件的受控基准，我们表明地图仍然是机器智能的有效外部表示。然而，应该承认一些局限性并指出未来的重要方向。In this study, we provide empirical evidence that choropleth maps still offer clear benefits for FMs, especially when visual maps are combined with symbolic data for spatial understanding. Through a controlled benchmark comparing Data Only, Map Only, and Data + Map conditions across multiple frontier models, we show that maps remain effective external representations for machine intelligence. Nevertheless, several limitations should be acknowledged and point to important future directions.

首先，我们的基准测试重点关注等值区域图作为受控测试平台。虽然分区统计图是最广泛使用的专题地图类型之一，但没有考虑许多其他制图表示形式，例如比例符号图、流量图、等高线图、地图和交互式网络地图。未来的工作应该将分析扩展到更广泛的地图形式，以检验所观察到的制图抽象的好处是否可以推广到分区统计图之外。First, our benchmark focuses on choropleth maps as a controlled testbed. While choropleth maps are one of the most widely used thematic map types, many other cartographic representations, such as proportional symbol maps, flow maps, contour maps, cartograms, and interactive web maps, were not considered. Future work should extend the analysis to a broader range of map forms to examine whether the observed benefits of cartographic abstraction generalize beyond choropleth maps.

其次，尽管我们的基准测试系统地控制地图类型、色调和空间结构，但生成的地图仍然是简化的合成环境。现实世界的地图通常包含更丰富的地理语义、不规则布局、噪声、不确定性、注释和混合视觉变量。在真实的作战地图上评估基础模型将为实用性提供更有力的证据。此外，虽然我们的研究没有明确改变地理区域（例如不同的国家），但我们承认预训练的基础模型可能会编码特定于区域的先验知识，这可能会影响模型的性能和泛化，并且我们将系统的跨区域分析留作未来的工作。Second, although our benchmark systematically controls map type, hue, and spatial structure, the generated maps remain simplified synthetic environments. Real-world maps often contain richer geographic semantics, irregular layouts, noise, uncertainty, annotations, and mixed visual variables. Evaluating foundation models on authentic operational maps would provide stronger evidence of practical utility. In addition, while our study does not explicitly vary geographic regions (e.g., different countries), we acknowledge that pretrained foundation models may encode region-specific prior knowledge, which could influence model performance and generalization, and we leave systematic cross-region analysis as future work.

第三，我们的评估主要使用多项选择推理任务的准确性。虽然这种受控设置可以实现公平比较，但它并不能完全捕获开放式地理分析、决策支持、交互式推理或长期规划。未来的基准可以结合自由形式的响应、工具增强推理和人机交互任务，以更好地反映实际应用。Third, our evaluation mainly uses accuracy on multiple-choice reasoning tasks. While this controlled setting enables fair comparison, it does not fully capture open-ended geographic analysis, decision support, interactive reasoning, or long-horizon planning. 第四，我们评估当前的前沿多模式模型，但基础模型继续快速发展。通过更强大的视觉编码器、更大的上下文窗口或专门的地理空间训练，今天观察到的性能差距可能会缩小。因此，随着新一代模型的出现，有必要不断进行重新评估。Fourth, we evaluate current frontier multimodal models, but foundation models continue to evolve rapidly. Performance gaps observed today may narrow with stronger visual encoders, larger context windows, or specialized geospatial training. Continuous re-evaluation will therefore be necessary as new generations of models emerge.

最后，我们的研究结果表明，地图仍然是机器有价值的外部表示，但这种优势背后的机制仍未完全理解。未来的工作可能会探索地图如何作为人工智能的认知压缩接口，如何优化地图设计以提高机器可读性，以及是否应该专门为基础模型开发新形式的面向人工智能的制图。Finally, our findings suggest that maps remain valuable external representations for machines, but the mechanisms behind this advantage are still not fully understood. Future work may explore how maps function as cognitive compression interfaces for AI, how map design can be optimized for machine readability, and whether new forms of AI-oriented cartography should be developed specifically for foundation models.

FM 的最新进展极大地增强了机器处理原始多模态输入（例如文本、图像和结构化数据）的能力。在这种背景下，出现了一个重要的问题：如果机器越来越多地直接根据原始信息进行推理，那么传统的人类设计的抽象（例如地图）仍然重要吗？为了解决这个问题，我们通过受控基准重新审视了等值区域图在 FM 空间理解中的作用。我们构建了 ChoroplethMap-Bench，它系统地比较了三个输入条件：Recent advances in FMs have greatly strengthened machines’ ability to process raw multimodal inputs such as text, images, and structured data.
