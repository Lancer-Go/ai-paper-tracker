结论Conclusion

代理服务将 LLM 调度的目标转移到集群范围的 TPS 并使 KV$ 重用占主导地位。我们对两个现实世界痕迹的研究 显示现有调度程序以负载平衡换取 KV$ 重用， 并且这种交易是不必要的：全球级 KV$ 商店 将重用与请求放置和工作负载分离 会话内局部性意味着平衡每个会话的第一个请求 足以平衡集群。Agentic serving shifts the goal of LLM scheduling to cluster-wide TPS and makes KV$ reuse dominant. Our study of two real-world traces shows that existing schedulers trade load balance for KV$ reuse, and that this trade is unnecessary: the global-tier KV$ store decouples reuse from request placement, and the workload’s intra-session locality means balancing each session’s first request suffices to balance the cluster.S公制SMetric将这些见解转化为 平衡的以会话为中心的调度——调度的第一个请求 平衡，后续请求路由缓存感知 - 并且性能优于 最先进的调度程序：TPS 提高 10–16% 与全局存储进行预填充解码并置，提高 2–34 % 在跨全局层配置的分解下预填充 TPS。turns these insights into balanced session-centric scheduling—first requests scheduled for balance, follow-up requests routed cache-aware—and outperforms state-of-the-art schedulers: 10–16 % higher TPS under prefill-decode colocation with a global store and 2–34 % higher prefill TPS under disaggregation across global-tier provisionings.
