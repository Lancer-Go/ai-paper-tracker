摘要Abstract

头戴式设备 (HMD) 的人体动作捕捉提供了一种可扩展的方式来获取真实世界的人体动作和交互数据， 这对于嵌入式人工智能和 VR/AR 的应用至关重要。 现有的方法侧重于以自我为中心的身体跟踪，估计佩戴设备的主体的运动，或以外心为中心的跟踪，捕获佩戴者周围环境中的人的运动。到目前为止，这两种范式在很大程度上是孤立地进行探索的。 在本文中，我们提出了一种新颖的分布式框架，该框架联合利用自心和外心多模态信号进行 HMD 人体运动估计。 与需要笨重的多摄像头设置或引人注目的动作捕捉套装的传统动作捕捉系统不同，我们的方法 EgoExoMoCap 非常简单，只需两个（或更多）人，每个人都戴着一副智能眼镜。 该方法利用头部（可能还包括手腕）跟踪信号来准确估计 3D 世界中的全局运动，并结合基于 DINOv3 的上下文感知图像特征，以在存在噪声和遮挡的情况下实现鲁棒性。 对两个野外数据集的广泛实验表明，即使在具有挑战性的场景中，我们的方法也可以稳健地重建运动。Human motion capture from head-mounted devices (HMDs) offers a scalable way to acquire real-world human motion and interaction data, which is crucial for applications in embodied AI and VR/AR. Existing approaches focus on either egocentric body tracking, estimating the motion of the subject wearing the device, or exocentric tracking, capturing the movements of people in the wearer’s surroundings. So far, these two paradigms have largely been explored in isolation. In this paper, we propose a novel distributed framework that jointly leverages ego- and exocentric multi-modal signals for human motion estimation from HMDs. Unlike traditional motion capture systems requiring bulky multi-camera setups or obtrusive mocap suits, our approach, EgoExoMoCap, is as simple as two (or more) people, each wearing a pair of smart glasses. The method leverages head (plus potentially wrist) tracking signals for accurate estimation of global motion in the 3D world and combines context-aware image features based on DINOv3 to achieve robustness in the presence of noise and occlusions. Extensive experiments on two in-the-wild datasets show that our approach can robustly reconstruct motion even in challenging scenarios.

4.4 讨论Discussion

我们的方法侧重于使用分布式 HMD 设置进行便携式、轻量级人体动作捕捉。我们假设摄像机已通过最新的 Aria 工具进行校准和同步Our approach focuses on portable, lightweight human motion capture using a distributed HMD setup. We assume cameras are calibrated and synchronized by recent Aria tooling[5]。虽然我们专注于野外真实运动采集而不是在线和实时跟踪，但当硬件和工具可以支持时，探索实时应用程序是未来工作的一个令人兴奋的方向。. While we focus on in-the-wild ground-truth motion acquisition rather than online and real-time tracking, exploring real-time applications is an exciting direction for future work when hardware and tooling can support it.

目前，我们主要关注运动重建，并假设在计算关节位置时提供了主体形状；它可能会利用图像或基于传感器的校准来估计Currently, we mainly focus on motion reconstruction and assume subject shape is provided when calculating the joint positions; it could potentially be estimated leveraging images or sensor-based calibrations[69]。 我们观察到当佩戴者被另一个主体遮挡时会出现故障，因为这种情况可能会混淆基于 DINO 的可见度分数。长时间的视野外间隔或持续不可靠的 exo 信号（. 5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 EgoExoMoCap，这是一种新颖的可扩展方法，有效地将自我中心和外中心跟踪结合起来，用于野外人体动作捕捉。 与依赖大量硬件和复杂设置的传统动作捕捉系统不同，我们的方法仅利用一组 HMD，提出了一种轻量级分布式解决方案，可在现实环境中进行灵活且不引人注目的跟踪。 EgoExoMoCap 只需要两个受试者，每个受试者都戴着一副眼镜，并且可以自然地扩展到多受试者设置。对两个野外数据集的大量实验表明，该方法在自我中心和外中心基线方面表现良好，可以处理遮挡和视野外运动等具有挑战性的场景。We presented EgoExoMoCap, a novel scalable approach effectively combining egocentric and exocentric tracking for in-the-wild human motion capture. Unlike traditional motion capture systems that rely on extensive hardware and complex setups, our method only leverages a set of HMDs, proposing a lightweight distributed solution for flexible and unobtrusive tracking in real-world environments. EgoExoMoCap requires just two subjects, each wearing a pair of glasses, and can naturally scale to multi-subject setups. Extensive experiments on two in-the-wild datasets show that the approach performs favorably with respect to egocentric and exocentric baselines, handling challenging scenarios like occlusions and out-of-view motions.
