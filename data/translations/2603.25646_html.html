摘要Abstract

本文提出了一个用于研究非人形机器人的意向状态归因的实验平台。该系统结合了模拟机器人、现实任务环境和基于大型语言模型的解释层，可以用心灵论、目的论或机械术语表达相同的行为。通过在改变解释框架的同时保持行为不变，该平台提供了一种受控的方法来研究语言和框架如何影响机器人技术中有意立场的采用。This paper presents an experimental platform for studying intentional-state attribution toward a non-humanoid robot. The system combines a simulated robot, realistic task environments, and large language model-based explanatory layers that can express the same behavior in mentalistic, teleological, or mechanistic terms. By holding behavior constant while varying the explanatory frame, the platform provides a controlled way to investigate how language and framing shape the adoption of the intentional stance in robotics.

I 简介Introduction

人类通过讲述有关信仰、欲望和意图的故事来解释行为Humans explain behavior by telling stories about beliefs, desires, and intentions[9, 10]。日常的解释模式——有意的立场——是人们理解他人思想的方式，但在机器人技术中，它很少被视为可以故意改变和以受控方式研究的东西。研究要么依赖于通过外观引发读心术的人形外形因素，要么依赖于突出内部状态和控制信息的机械解释层，而不是民间心理语言. That everyday mode of explanation — the intentional stance — is how people make sense of other minds, but in robotics it is rarely treated as something that can be deliberately varied and studied in a controlled way. Research either relies on humanoid form factors that invite mind-reading by appearance, or on mechanistic explanation layers that foreground internal states and control information rather than folk-psychological language[18]。本文提出了一个翻转脚本的平台：它不是问人们是否会将机器人拟人化，而是故意用心智术语来解决这些问题，并提供一种方法来研究这些术语如何容易被视为行为的解释。 中心思想是建立一个代理，使用信念、欲望和意图来进行自我交流，并使用该交流层作为实验杠杆来探测合规性——. This paper proposes a platform that flips that script: rather than asking whether people will anthropomorphize a robot, it deliberately addresses them in mentalistic terms and provides a way to study how readily those terms are taken as explanations for behavior. The central idea is to build an agent that communicates about itself using beliefs, desires, and intentions, and use that communicative layer as an experimental lever to probe compliance —当一个明显非人类的设备坚持以这种方式解释时，人们有多容易对它采取有意的立场？how readily do people adopt an intentional stance toward a clearly non-human device when it insists on being interpreted that way?这样看来，意向性并不是简单地从机器人的身体形状或其他拟人化线索中推断出来的；相反，它是一种由语言和社会框架塑造的互动产品。这种区别很重要，因为意向立场比拟人论更狭窄，具体指心理状态的归因，而拟人论是 HRI 研究中更广泛且方法论上不太稳定的结构。因此，最近在机器人技术和 HRI 方面的工作表明，将故意立场归因与人类相似性分开，以及研究框架和解释性语言如何独立于具体化而影响心理感知是有价值的。Framed this way, intentionality is not simply inferred from a robot’s body shape or other anthropomorphic cues; rather, it is an interactional product shaped by language and social framing. This distinction is important because the intentional stance is narrower than anthropomorphism, referring specifically to the attribution of mental states, whereas anthropomorphism is a broader and methodologically less stable construct in HRI research. Recent work in robotics and HRI therefore suggests the value of separating intentional-stance attribution from human-likeness, and of studying how framing and explanatory language influence mind perception independently of embodiment[13, 14, 8]。衡量主体对自己的评价与观察者对其的看法之间的差距，可以让我们将修辞的说服力与真实的心理状态区分开来。. Measuring the gap between what the agent says about itself and what observers believe about it lets us separate rhetorical persuasiveness from genuine mental-state.

为什么这很重要？首先，它将可解​​释性重新定义为一种社会心理现象，而不仅仅是一种技术现象：解释的形式很重要，语言可以成为制造能动性的工具。正如最近有关通用人工智能（AGI）的文献中指出的那样Why is this important? First, it reframes interpretability as a social-psychological phenomenon, not merely a technical one: the form of explanation matters, and language can be an instrument that manufactures agency. As recently pointed out in the literature about Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)[1]，对基于人工智能的代理的有意立场的有效采用可能是可解释性的高水平衡量标准。其次，它为心理学家和 HRI 研究人员提出了一些易于处理的问题：在什么情况下，人们会将口头报告视为思想的证据，或者至少视为一个方便的故事？哪些语言框架（主体论、目的论、机械论）与三种立场（意图、设计、物理）相对应，会引发行为预测模型，而哪些只会增加信任而不增进理解？第三，将民间心理报告视为自变量，为道德严格的实验创造了一条清晰的道路，这些实验可以在不依赖欺骗性实施例的情况下测试归因、说服和责任判断。, the fruitful adoption of the intentional stance towards AI based agents is likely to be a high-level measure of interpretability. Second, it opens tractable questions for psychologists and HRI researchers: under what circumstances do people treat a verbal report as evidence of a mind, or at least as a convenient story? Which linguistic frames (agentive, teleological, mechanistic) that correspond to the three stances (intentional, design, physical) invite predictive models of behavior, and which merely increase trust without improving understanding? Third, treating folk-psychological reporting as an independent variable creates a clear path for ethically rigorous experiments that test attribution, persuasion, and responsibility judgments without relying on deceptive embodiments.

本文提出了研究非人形机器人中的意向姿态作为设计选择的想法、规范性论证，以及使这些实验成为可能的研究平台和刺激集的具体建议。 简而言之：与其在机器中寻找思想，不如让机器提供思想，然后看看人们是否接受这个提议。这将科学问题从“这个机器人看起来像心灵吗？”转移了。 “这个机器人的说话和行为是否像大脑一样？”This paper presents the idea, the normative argument for studying intentional stance in non-humanoid robotics as a design choice, and a concrete proposal for a research platform and stimuli set that make those experiments possible. In short: rather than searching for minds in the machinery, let the machine offer the mind and see whether people take the offer. This shifts the scientific question from “Does this robot look like a mind?” to “Does this robot speak and behave like a mind?”

这项工作的目的是提供一个实验平台，用于测试非人形机器人的意图状态的归因。选择非人形系统有意减少拟人化和移情参与的混淆影响。引入大语言模型（LLM）使我们能够引出对机器人行为的民间心理学解释，同时系统地改变心理承诺的水平。据作者所知，这种受控体现和法学硕士介导的解释的结合在机器人学意向立场的研究中是新颖的，并为未来研究解释性语言、体现和观点如何塑造意向归因提供了严格的基础。The aim of this work is to present an experimental platform for testing the attribution of intentional states to a non-humanoid robot. Selecting a non-humanoid system intentionally reduces confounding effects from anthropomorphism and empathic engagement. Introducing large language models (LLMs) enables us to elicit folk-psychological explanations of the robot’s behavior while systematically varying the level of mentalistic commitment. To the best of the authors’ knowledge, this combination of controlled embodiment and LLM-mediated explanation is novel in studies of the intentional stance in robotics and provides a rigorous foundation for future research into how explanatory language, embodiment, and viewpoint shape intentional attributions.

II 相关作品Related works

最近的工作探讨了以人为中心的人工智能在机器人技术中的更广泛作用，描绘了自主系统的当前趋势，并强调了在机器人设计中平衡性能、可靠性和人类控制的重要性Recent work has examined the broader role of human-centered AI in robotics, mapping current trends in autonomous systems and highlighting the importance of balancing performance, reliability, and human control in robotic design[5]。补充研究表明，语言模型可以利用具体环境中的自然语言反馈，使用“内心独白”来改进机器人控制中的规划和交互. Complementary research has shown that language models can leverage natural-language feedback in embodied settings, using an “inner monologue” to improve planning and interaction in robotic control[12]。总之，这些研究表明，以语言为媒介的界面和以人为中心的设计原则与机器人技术的相关性越来越高，这强化了研究解释性框架如何塑造机器人行为解释的必要性。 目前，对人类对人工智能体采取有意立场的条件的调查已经发展成为一个多方面的领域，采用不同的方法来区分行为的机械（设计）和心理（有意）解释. Together, these studies suggest that language-mediated interfaces and human-centered design principles are becoming increasingly relevant for robotics, reinforcing the need to examine how explanatory framing shapes the interpretation of robot behavior. Currently, the investigation into the conditions under which humans adopt the intentional stance toward artificial agents has evolved into a multifaceted field, employing diverse methodologies to distinguish between mechanistic (design) and mentalistic (intentional) interpretations of behavior[18, 20, 24, 17]，非常关注机器人平台的拟人化线索。人形研究主要利用 iCub 平台，经常使用实例测试 (IST) 来衡量日常活动的图画场景如何引发归因心理状态的倾向, with great attention on the antropomorphic cues of the robotic platform. Humanoid research, primarily utilizing the iCub platform, frequently employs the InStance Test (IST) to measure how pictorial scenarios of daily activities elicit the tendency to attribute mental states[18, 28, 17, 19]。这些研究利用社会体现的刺激来探索共享体验如何调节从机械设计立场到有意设计立场的转变，例如与机器人一起观看视频，机器人做出类似人类的情感反应或参与协作任务. These studies use socially embodied stimuli to explore how shared experiences, such as watching videos alongside a robot reacting with human-like emotions or participating in collaborative tasks, can modulate the transition from a mechanistic design stance to an intentional one[3, 17, 19]。在方法论上，人形平台还用于将意向立场与联合注意范式（例如凝视提示）相结合，并分析错误行为（将类人错误与机械循环进行比较）对意向性感知的影响. Methodologically, humanoid platforms are also used to integrate the intentional stance with joint attention paradigms, such as gaze cueing, and to analyze the effects of erring behavior—comparing human-like mistakes to mechanical loops—on the perception of intentionality[7, 17, 19]。除了身体互动之外，最近的作品还利用语言和叙事刺激，包括心理文本插图和定格动画电影，来展示想象或虚构的场景如何帮助人们模拟机器人的体验. Beyond physical interaction, recent works utilize linguistic and narrative stimuli, including mentalistic text-vignettes and stop-motion films, to demonstrate how imagined or fictional scenarios can prime individuals to simulate a robot’s experience[21, 22]。相反，涉及非人形平台的文献通常利用抽象机械臂或虚拟 3D 角色来隔离运动质量与物理外观的影响. Conversely, literature involving non-humanoid platforms typically utilizes abstract robotic arms or virtual 3D characters to isolate the influence of motion qualities from physical appearance[4, 24]。这些研究重点关注表达性、自我驱动或令人惊讶的行为如何触发意图的归属，即使是在缺乏类人形态的物体中. These studies focus on how expressive, self-propelled, or surprising behavior can trigger the ascription of intentions even in objects lacking human-like morphology[4, 25, 23]。最后，涉及“石头剪刀布”等竞技游戏中的非实体计算机对手的研究采用神经影像学来隔离有意立场的神经关联，发现仅仅相信对手的理性就会激活心智化网络. Finally, research involving non-embodied computer opponents in competitive games like Rock, Paper, Scissors employs neuroimaging to isolate the neural correlates of the intentional stance, finding that the mere belief in an opponent’s rationality activates the mentalizing network[2, 23].

三、III意向立场、心智理论和民间心理学Intentional Stance, Theory of Mind and folk psychology

想象一下，看着一个小型非人形机器人暂停、转动并走向充电器，并立即构建一个关于它相信和想要什么的因果故事。从运动学到心智叙事的日常转变取决于三个相互关联的概念。丹尼尔·C·丹尼特 (Daniel C. Dennett) 阐述的有意立场Imagine watching a small, non-humanoid robot pause, pivot on, and head for a charger — and immediately constructing a causal story about what it believed and wanted. That everyday move from kinematics to mentalistic narrative rests on three interlocking concepts. The intentional stance, as articulated by Daniel C. Dennett[9, 10]，是选择最有用的解释模型的一种方式：人们采用心理状态假设，因为它提供了对行为的简洁有效的描述，允许以简单直观的方式预测行为。心智理论是指认知能力，允许观察者表征和推理其他主体的心理状态（例如信念、欲望、意图、感知和情感），并使用这些推断的状态来解释和预测行为，包括另一个主体持有错误信念的情况, is a way of choosing the most useful explanatory model: one adopts a mental-state hypothesis because it offers a concise and effective account of behavior, allowing actions to be predicted in a simple and intuitive way. Theory of mind refers to the cognitive capacity that allows observers to represent and reason about other agents’ mental states — such as beliefs, desires, intentions, perceptions, and emotions — and to use those inferred states to explain and predict behavior, including cases in which another agent holds a false belief[27]。民间心理学提供了理解行为的概念和因果框架，围绕信念、欲望和导致行动的意图组织解释。它还提供用于表达和传达这些推论的日常语言. Folk psychology provides the conceptual and causal framework through which behavior is understood, organizing explanations around beliefs, desires, and intentions that lead to action. It also supplies the everyday language used to express and communicate these inferences[6].

这些框架的共同基础是心理状态的概念：观察者为解释和预测系统行为而提出的归因结构。这些状态的特征不在于它们的物质实现，而在于它们在观察者眼中所扮演的角色以及它们编码的内容The common foundation of these frameworks is the notion of mental states: attributed constructs that observers posit to explain and anticipate a system’s behavior. Such states are characterized not by their material implementation but by the role they play in the eye of the observer and by the content they encode[10]。因为它们是按功能定义的，所以心理状态可以在从生物有机体到软件架构的各种基础上实现。它们的合法性并不依赖于对内部机制的特权访问，而是依赖于它们的解释性和预测性效用：当一个归属连贯地捕捉到可观察到的规律并能够对未来行为进行可靠预测时，它就赢得了自己的地位。. Because they are defined functionally, mental states are multiply realizable across substrates, from biological organisms to software architectures. Their legitimacy does not rest on privileged access to internal mechanisms, but on their explanatory and predictive utility: an ascription earns its place when it coherently captures observable regularities and enables reliable forecasts of future behavior[29, 26].

与心理状态的定义不同，人们可以认为这样的语言是在不同的基质之间共享的，并且可以作为允许不同主体进行交流的通用媒介。从这个角度来看，并依靠AGI上的工作Departing from this definition of mental states, one can argue that such a language is shared across different substrates and can function as a common medium allowing diverse agents to communicate. From this view, and relying on the work on AGI[1]，我们在这里提出了社交机器人行为可解释性的新概念：, we propose here a new notion of interpretability for social-robot behavior:当社交机器人以民间心理学术语表达自己时，它就可以被解释a social robot is interpretable when it expresses itself in folk-psychological terms。 然而，故意立场经常与其他不同的立场相混淆. However, the intentional stance is often confused with other distinct stances[11]：道德立场，其中至少一个相互作用的系统表现出对另一个系统的道德承诺，以及语言立场，它将意向性降低为用可理解的语言表达心理状态的能力。: the moral stance, in which at least one interacting system manifests a moral commitment toward the other, and the linguistic stance, which reduces intentionality to the ability to express mental states in comprehensible language.

在这项工作中，我们提供了一个平台，有可能将真实的意图归属与道德/拟人归属分开，并评估语言故事讲述在民间心理心理叙事中的作用。In this work we provide a platform that has the potential to isolate true intentional ascriptions by moral/antropomorphic ones, and evaluate the role of linguistic storytelling in folk psychological mental narratives.

IV 设计基于法学硕士的非人形意向平台Designing an LLM-based Non-Humanoid intentional platform

这项工作的中心思想是设计一个实验装置，旨在测试模拟的非人形机器人的意图状态的归因。在此设置中，机器人有效地定位在基于大语言模型（LLM）的两个语言界面之间，这使得系统的技术和设计相关方面在民间心理框架内对用户来说不透明。其中一个 LLM 层用于向系统发出命令，而另一层则提供有关机器人当前状态及其计划操作的报告。 法学硕士生成的响应可以进行调整，以便使用心理、目的论或机械术语来表达报告，这些术语对应于物理、设计和意图这三种立场。这种设置使得语言立场能够与意向性的归属分开。在实践中，每个数据集项可能由显示机器人执行任务的裸视频序列组成，也可能由相同的序列组成，并附有以各种解释风格生成的不同 LLM 生成的自我解释。模拟机器人在现实环境（例如公寓、书店）中运行，并执行设计师分配的任务。模拟视频是从两个视点（平面图和前视图）录制的，以说明个人观点在归因意向性方面可能具有的优势。每个任务视频都链接到与用户的整个聊天，包括以各种解释风格提供的 LLM 响应。将裸露视频中的精神状态归因与法学硕士增强版本得出的精神状态归因进行比较，将有助于将这些归因分类为故意、语言或道德立场。The central idea of this work is to design an experimental setup aimed at testing the attribution of intentional states to a simulated, non-humanoid robot. In this setup, the robot is effectively positioned between two linguistic interfaces based on large language models (LLMs), which render the technical and design-related aspects of the system opaque to the user within a folk-psychological frame. One LLM layer is used to issue commands to the system, while another provides reports about the robot’s current state and its planned actions. The responses generated by the LLMs can be modulated so that the reports are expressed using mentalistic, teleological, or mechanistic terminology, which correspond to the three stances, physical, design and intentional. This setup allows the linguistic stance to be disentangled from the attribution of intentionality. In practice, each dataset item may consist either of a bare video sequence showing the robot performing a task or of the same sequence accompanied by different LLM-generated self-explanations produced in the various explanatory styles. The simulated robot operates within realistic environments (e.g. a flat, a bookshop) and carries out tasks assigned by the designer. A video of the simulation is recorded from two viewpoints — the plan view and the front view — to account for a possible advantage of a personal point of view in attributing intentionality. Each task video is linked to the entire chat with the user, including the LLM responses delivered in the various explanatory styles. Comparing mental-state attributions made from the bare video with those elicited by the LLM-enhanced versions will help classify those attributions as falling under the intentional, linguistic, or moral stances.

V 实施Implementation

所提出的平台被设计为一种计算架构，使移动机器人能够根据信念、愿望和意图产生并传达对其行为的解释。该系统将内部状态的符号表示与能够在不同解释框架下表达该状态的语言生成层集成在一起。该架构遵循信念-欲望-意图（BDI）推理范式，不是作为机器人拥有心理状态的主张，而是作为一种操作结构，用于以可以通过语言向人类观察者报告的方式组织内部信息。这与机器人甚至不是自主的，而是由远程操作员部分控制的研究相一致The proposed platform is designed as a computational architecture that enables a mobile robot to produce and communicate explanations of its behavior in terms of beliefs, desires, and intentions. The system integrates a symbolic representation of internal state with a language generation layer capable of expressing that state under different explanatory frames. 七VII结论Conclusion

通过将可解释性重新定义为一种交互的、语言介导的现象，并为非人形智能体提供一个具体的法学硕士介导的实验平台，这项工作表明，有意归因可以被视为可操纵的变量，而不是不可避免的形式副产品。基于丹尼尔·C·丹尼特（Daniel C. Dennett）阐述的有意立场，该平台系统地改变了解释框架（心理论、目的论、机械论）、观点和法学硕士生成的自我报告，以将修辞说服力与真正的信仰归因分开。由此产生的协议和刺激集为严格的实验提供了一条易于处理的途径，这些实验可以量化人们何时以及为何采用民间心理学解释，探讨语言与具体化的相对影响，并为正确校准信任、责任和可解释性的设计选择提供信息。简而言之，本文不是在机器中寻找思想，而是提供了工具来测试何时以及通过什么语言和社会手段，机器自己的账户引导人类将其视为拥有自己的账户。By reframing interpretability as an interactional, language-mediated phenomenon and delivering a concrete LLM-mediated experimental platform for non-humanoid agents, this work shows that intentional attributions can be treated as manipulable variables rather than inevitable byproducts of form. Building on the intentional stance as articulated by Daniel C. Dennett, the platform systematically varies explanatory frames (mentalistic, teleological, mechanistic), viewpoints, and LLM-generated self-reports to separate rhetorical persuasiveness from genuine belief attribution. The resulting protocol and stimulus set offer a tractable route for rigorous experiments that quantify when and why people adopt folk-psychological explanations, probe the relative influence of language versus embodiment, and inform design choices that properly calibrate trust, responsibility, and interpretability. In short, rather than hunting for minds in the machinery, this paper supplies the tools to test when—and by what linguistic and social means—a machine’s own accounts lead humans to treat it as if it had one.
