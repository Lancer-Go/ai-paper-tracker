1 简介Introduction

大多数受监督的 NLP 系统通常通过多数投票将多个人类判断分解为单个“真实事实”标签来解决注释者的分歧Most supervised NLP systems resolve annotator disagreement by collapsing multiple human judgments into a single “ground-truth” label, typically via majority vote(Snow et al., 2008; Raykar and Yu, 2012)。虽然方便，但这种聚合策略丢弃了人类解释变异中的结构化变异，这些变异通常是系统性的、有社会基础的、信息丰富的，而不是噪音. While convenient, this aggregation strategy discards structured variation in human interpretation variation that is often systematic, socially grounded, and informative rather than noise(Aroyo and Welty, 2014; 2013; Homan et al., 2022)。分歧可以反映真正的语言歧义. Disagreement can reflect genuine linguistic ambiguity(Pavlick and Kwiatkowski, 2019; Dumitrache et al., 2018a)，源于注释者身份的视角差异, perspectival differences rooted in annotator identity(Davani et al., 2022; Denton et al., 2021)，或解释中的人口统计差异, or demographic variation in interpretation(Prabhakaran et al., 2024; Davani et al., 2023; Pei and Jurgens, 2023; Al Kuwatly et al., 2020)。将此类差异视为错误可能会导致构建的模型过度拟合多数观点，同时掩盖少数观点. Treating such variation as error risks building models that overfit majority viewpoints while obscuring minority perspectives(Basile et al., 2021; Plank, 2022; Hovy and Spruit, 2016)，最近的立场文件认为应该将这一问题视为内在的设计原则，而不是偶然的建模选择, a concern that recent position papers have argued should be treated as an intrinsic design principle rather than an incidental modeling choice(Xu et al., 2026; Fleisig et al., 2024).

最近的透视主义方法认为，NLP 系统反而保留并模拟了人类反应的完整分布Recent perspectivist approaches argue that NLP systems instead preserve and model the full distribution of human responses(Xu and Jurgens, 2026; Gordon et al., 2022; Uma et al., 2021; Fornaciari et al., 2021; Rodrigues and Pereira, 2018)。这些模型不是预测单个标签，而是旨在捕获可能具有不同背景、价值观和生活经历的不同注释者如何解释相同的输入. Rather than predicting a single label, these models aim to capture how different annotators, potentially with different backgrounds, values, and lived experiences, interpret the same input(Weerasooriya et al., 2023d; Heinisch et al., 2023)。这种转变对于社会敏感或主观任务（例如攻击性检测）尤其重要. This shift is especially critical for socially sensitive or subjective tasks such as offensiveness detection(Leonardelli et al., 2021; Almanea and Poesio, 2022; Weerasooriya et al., 2023c)、仇恨言论分类, hate speech classification(Davani et al., 2021; Sap et al., 2019)、讽刺解读, sarcasm interpretation(Casola et al., 2024)，以及自然语言推理, and natural language inference(Pavlick and Kwiatkowski, 2019)，其中分歧普遍存在且有意义。, where disagreement is pervasive and meaningful.

建立在这些基础上的数据集，例如 VOICEDBuilding on these foundations, datasets such as VOICED(Weerasooriya et al., 2023a)将透视主义范式作为分歧感知建模的基准。 LeWiDi 共享任务系列operationalize the perspectivist paradigm as benchmarks for disagreement-aware modeling. The LeWiDi shared task series(Leonardelli et al., 2026)要求系统 (i) 预测注释者的软标签分布，(ii) 模拟单个注释者的响应，以及 (iii) 保持对项目级分歧模式的忠实。这些目标带来了巨大的建模挑战：注释器监督稀疏，人口统计效应与内容特征纠缠在一起，评估指标明确地衡量与观察到的方差和熵的一致性，而不仅仅是分类准确性。requires systems to (i) predict soft label distributions over annotators, (ii) simulate individual annotator responses, and (iii) remain faithful to item-level disagreement patterns. These objectives introduce substantial modeling challenges: annotator supervision is sparse, demographic effects are entangled with content features, and evaluation metrics explicitly measure alignment with observed variance and entropy, not merely classification accuracy.

在这项工作中，我们引入了 DiADEM（分歧和人口统计感知分布建模），这是一种人口统计感知注释器学习方法，它通过可学习的每个人口统计重要性权重和改进的注释器项目混合策略来解决这些挑战。In this work, we introduce DiADEM (Disagreement- and Demographic-Aware Distribution Modeling), a demographic-aware annotator learning method that addresses these challenges through learnable per-demographic importance weights and improved annotator item mixing strategies.

6 讨论Discussion

6.1 为什么人口统计和观点很重要？Why Demographics and Perspectives Matter?

毒性注释本质上是透视主义的：不同的注释者可以 合理地为同一内容分配不同的标签。这在 VOICED 中尤其明显，其中标签与注释者的政治背景相关；而在 DICES 中，分歧是数据的第一类属性，而不是注释噪声。在这种情况下，将监管压缩为单一硬标签会消除有意义的变化，并可能掩盖少数群体的观点。人口统计元数据的作用在以下方面最为明显：Toxicity annotation is inherently perspectivist: different annotators can legitimately assign different labels to the same content. This is especially visible in VOICED, where labels are tied to annotator political affiliation,and in DICES, where disagreement is a first-class property of the data rather than annotation noise. In such settings, collapsing supervision to a single hard label removes meaningful variation and can obscure minority perspectives. The role of demographic metadata is most evident in the注释者级别annotator-level分裂。在此拆分下，测试实例注释为split. 7 结论Conclusion

人类的分歧不是需要纠正的缺陷，而是可以用来对智能系统进行建模的信号。 DiADEM 通过可学习的按人口统计权重和改进的项目注释器融合来解决这个问题，因此模型可以学习Human disagreement is not a flaw that needs to be corrected, but a signal that can be used to model intelligent systems. We also use an improved item-level disagreement loss that directly penalizes mismatch between actual and predicted annotator disagreement.

根据我们的结果，DiADEM 在注释器级别设置中比神经网络和 LLM 基线表现更好，并且表明从以前的注释器行为中学习有助于预测新的注释器。它还从项目级设置中的现有注释器中学习。同时，DiADEM 并不完美：性能取决于可用的人口统计特征、其质量以及每个数据集的评级设置。Based on our results, DiADEM performs better than neural and LLM baselines in annotator-level settings, and shows that learning from previous annotator behavior helps prediction for new annotators. It also learns from existing annotators in the item-level setting. At the same time, DiADEM is not perfect: performance depends on the available demographic features, their quality, and the rating setup of each dataset.

除了原始表现之外，学习权重还提供了跨群体分歧结构的社会视角。对于我们的数据集（DICES 和 VOICED），种族和年龄等人口统计因素通常会产生影响，尽管确切的重要性模式取决于数据集。这意味着 DiADEM 还可以帮助确定哪些人口统计属性与给定环境中的分歧建模最相关。Beyond raw performance, learned weights provide an social view of disagreement structure across groups. For our datasets (DICES and VOICED), demographic factors such as race and age are often influential, though the exact importance pattern is dataset-dependent. This means DiADEM can also help identify which demographic attributes are most relevant for disagreement modeling in a given setting.

A.1 局限性Limitations

数据规模和人口稀疏性。Data scale and demographic sparsity.

尽管 DICES 和 VOICED 提供多注释者监督，但一些人口统计群体可能仍然不平衡。这可能会限制对亚组特定效应的稳定估计，并可能使学习到的人口统计权重偏向于更好代表的集群。 DiADEM 基于基于语言的任务，但是不同模式之间存在分歧。Although DICES and VOICED provide multi-annotator supervision, several demographic group may remain imbalanced. This can limit stable estimation of subgroup specific effects and may bias learned demographic weights toward better represented clusters. DiADEM is based on language based tasks, however disagreement exists across modalities.

基线覆盖率。Baseline coverage.

我们的比较包括分歧意识基线和 LLM 提示基线，但基准集仍然有限。额外的比较（例如，更新的多注释器基础模型适配器和以校准为中心的分歧模型）将增强外部有效性。Our comparisons include disagreement aware baselines and LLM prompting baselines, but the benchmark set is still limited. Additional comparisons (e.g., newer multi-annotator foundation-model adapters and calibration-focused disagreement models) would strengthen external validity.

拆分和度量相关性。Split- and metric-dependence.

注释者级别和项目级别拆分之间的性能趋势有所不同，并且某些指标对类不平衡和少数类稀疏性敏感。虽然这在透视主义环境中是预料之中的，但结论应该跨硬标签、软标签和透视主义指标联合解释，而不是从单一分数来解释。Performance trends vary between annotator-level and item-level splits, and some metrics are sensitive to class imbalance and minority-class sparsity. While this is expected in perspectivist settings, conclusions should be interpreted jointly across hard-label, soft-label, and perspectivistic metrics rather than from a single score.

人口统计数据作为上下文代理。Demographics as contextual proxies.

人口统计元数据信息丰富，但从注释者的角度来看并不完整。它们不应被解释为个人判断的唯一原因，未来的工作应该整合人口统计属性之外的更丰富的信号。Demographic metadata are informative but incomplete for annotator perspective. They should not be interpreted as the only causes of individual judgments, and future work should integrate richer signals beyond demographic attributes.
