用于可解释抑郁症症状注释的自我进化的以人为中心的框架Self-Evolving Human-Centered Framework for Explainable Depression Symptom Annotation

Hoang-Loc Cao153, Van Pham13, Truong Thanh Hung Nguyen52, Phuc Truong Loc Nguyen4,
Phuc Ho3, Veronica Whitford2, Hung Cao2 1These authors contributed equally.
摘要Abstract

注释质量是为心理健康研究构建可靠且可解释的人工智能（XAI）系统的主要瓶颈。在抑郁症相关数据集中，标签的分配通常没有结构化证据、症状水平论证或与《精神疾病诊断和统计手册》第五版文本修订版 (DSM-5-TR) 标准的可追踪一致性，从而限制了透明度和下游模型的可解释性。我们提出了一种针对重度抑郁症（MDD）的自我进化、专家循环注释框架，该框架将大语言模型（LLM）辅助标记与专家验证相结合。该框架旨在支持构建可解释的、符合 DSM-5-TR 的数据集，而不是执行临床诊断。它分三个阶段运行：从文本记录中选择候选证据、标准级 DSM-5-TR 分析以及生成标签级诊断和严重性注释的病例级综合。由示例内存和反射内存组成的双内存架构，旨在内化专家反馈并迭代改进未来的注释，而无需重新训练；我们描述了这种机制，并将其跨多个反馈周期的评估留给未来的工作。除了最终标签之外，该框架还导出临床证据、推理痕迹和编辑历史，从而实现全面的可审计性。在使用专家评审样本的试点研究中，所提出的方法提高了注释的一致性和可解释性，同时减少了手动修改工作。Annotation quality is a major bottleneck in building reliable and explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) systems for mental health research. In depression-related datasets, labels are often assigned without structured evidence, symptom-level justification, or traceable alignment to the criteria of the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR), limiting both transparency and downstream model interpretability. We propose a self-evolving, expert-in-the-loop annotation framework for Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) that combines large language model (LLM)–assisted labeling with expert verification. The framework is intended to support the construction of explainable, DSM-5-TR-aligned datasets rather than to perform clinical diagnosis. It operates in three stages: candidate evidence selection from textual records, criterion-level DSM-5-TR analysis, and case-level synthesis producing label-level diagnostic and severity annotations. A dual-memory architecture, composed of Example Memory and Reflection Memory, is designed to internalize expert feedback and iteratively improve future annotations without retraining; we describe this mechanism and leave its evaluation across multiple feedback cycles to future work. In addition to final labels, the framework exports clinical evidence, reasoning traces, and edit histories, enabling comprehensive auditability. In a pilot study with expert-reviewed samples, the proposed approach improves annotation consistency and explainability while lowering manual revision effort.

I 简介Introduction

心理健康状况对全球健康挑战做出了重大贡献，部分原因是它们的症状往往是主观的、重叠的、依赖于背景的，并且难以一致地定义Mental health conditions contribute substantially to global health challenges, partly because their symptoms are often subjective, overlapping, context-dependent, and difficult to define consistently[28]。重度抑郁症 (MDD) 由于其患病率、功能影响和异质性表现而尤为突出. Major depressive disorder (MDD) is especially prominent due to its prevalence, functional impact, and heterogeneous presentation[27]。 MDD 不是反映单一标记，而是涉及情感、认知和躯体体验，包括情绪低落、兴趣丧失、烦躁、绝望、注意力难以集中、沉思、疲劳、睡眠和食欲变化、精神运动变化和精力下降. Rather than reflecting a single marker, MDD involves affective, cognitive, and somatic experiences, including low mood, loss of interest, irritability, hopelessness, concentration difficulties, rumination, fatigue, sleep and appetite changes, psychomotor changes, and reduced energy[8]。这些经验被记录在不同的来源中，例如日记、生态瞬时评估、问卷、患者叙述、电子健康记录、临床医生笔记和非正式数字通信，甚至在引入计算模型之前就为一致的解释带来了挑战. These experiences are documented across varied sources such as diaries, ecological momentary assessments, questionnaires, patient narratives, electronic health records, clinician notes, and informal digital communication, creating challenges for consistent interpretation even before computational modeling is introduced[17, 7, 11]。 临床实践通过标准化诊断框架和经过验证的评估工具解决了这种复杂性。的. Clinical practice addresses this complexity through standardized diagnostic frameworks and validated assessment tools. The精神障碍诊断与统计手册，第五版Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition（DSM-5）建立了一个标准化诊断框架，使用基于症状的标准对精神障碍进行分类，而其(DSM-5) established a standardized diagnostic framework for classifying mental disorders using symptom-based criteria, while its文本修改Text Revision(DSM-5-TR) 更新了描述性文本、术语、编码指南和选定的说明(DSM-5-TR) updates descriptive text, terminology, coding guidance, and selected clarifications[3, 9, 10]。 SCID-5 等结构化访谈支持标准级专家评估. Structured interviews such as the SCID-5 support criterion-level expert assessment[4]，PHQ-9 提供了一个简短的、经过验证的抑郁症筛查和严重程度测量方法, and the PHQ-9 offers a brief validated measure for depression screening and severity[16]。这些工具提供了强大的临床基础并促进一致性，但它们主要是为临床评估而不是自由文本的细粒度注释而设计的，例如证据链接、标准级基本原理或用于计算重用的结构化决策跟踪. These tools provide strong clinical grounding and promote consistency, but they are designed primarily for clinical assessment rather than fine-grained annotation of free text, such as evidence links, criterion-level rationales, or structured decision traces for computational reuse[25].

与此同时，人工智能（AI）方法越来越多地应用于与抑郁症相关的语言，特别是通过自然语言处理（NLP）方法来处理非结构化文本In parallel, artificial intelligence (AI) methods have increasingly been applied to depression-related language, particularly through natural language processing (NLP) approaches for unstructured text[12]。它们的主要优势在于可扩展性：它们可以处理大量叙述数据并识别可能难以在长记录或异构记录中手动跟踪的模式。然而，在精神病学应用中，仅预测输出是不够的。临床医生、注释者和下游模型开发人员还必须能够检查支持决策的文本证据，了解该证据如何与公认的临床结构相关，并确定最终的判断在临床上是否站得住脚。因此，可解释的人工智能（XAI）在心理健康环境中尤其重要，其中透明度和可解释性与信任、问责制和实际可用性密切相关. Their primary advantage lies in scalability: they can process large volumes of narrative data and identify patterns that may be difficult to track manually across long or heterogeneous records. However, in psychiatric applications, predictive output alone is insufficient. Clinicians, annotators, and downstream model developers must also be able to inspect the textual evidence supporting a decision, understand how that evidence relates to recognized clinical constructs, and determine whether the resulting judgment is clinically defensible. For this reason, explainable AI (XAI) is especially important in mental health settings, where transparency and interpretability are closely linked to trust, accountability, and practical usability[13, 19]。这些考虑因素表明，临床上最有用的方向不是用不透明的自动化取代既定的评估实践，而是将 DSM-5/DSM-5-TR 指导的临床结构、AI 辅助的可扩展性和面向 XAI 的透明度集成到单个证据感知注释框架中。. These considerations suggest that the most clinically useful direction is not to replace established assessment practices with opaque automation, but to integrate DSM-5/DSM-5-TR-guided clinical structure, AI-assisted scalability, and XAI-oriented transparency within a single evidence-aware annotation framework.

在本文中，我们提出了一种用于 MDD 预测的自适应 DSM-5-TR 标准化注释框架。我们特别关注 MDD，因为它有明确定义的 DSM-5-TR 标准、广泛的临床评估工具以及丰富的叙述文本表达。该框架旨在通过符合 DSM-5-TR 的标签标准化、自适应建议生成和专家在环修订来支持多标签注释。除了生成结构化注释之外，该框架还可以通过专家编辑和证据感知审查来分析系统行为。我们在试点研究中使用专家审查的样本来评估该框架，重点关注系统标签质量和所需的手动校正量。In this paper, we propose an Adaptive DSM-5-TR Standardized Annotation Framework for MDD prediction. We focus specifically on MDD because of its well-defined DSM-5-TR criteria, widespread clinical assessment tools, and rich expression in narrative text. The framework is designed to support multi-label annotation through DSM-5-TR-aligned label standardization, adaptive suggestion generation, and expert-in-the-loop revision. In addition to producing structured annotations, the framework enables the analysis of system behavior through expert edits and evidence-aware review. We evaluate the framework in a pilot study with expert-reviewed samples, focusing on both system label quality and the amount of manual correction required.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 心理健康预测的可解释性Explainability in Mental Health Prediction

可解释性是心理健康预测的核心要求，因为临床相关信号通常是微妙的、依赖于上下文的并且难以单独解释Explainability is a core requirement in mental health prediction because clinically relevant signals are often subtle, context-dependent, and difficult to interpret in isolation[13]。对于抑郁症尤其如此，NLP 模型越来越多地用于从语言推断症状或障碍状态，但仅预测性能并不能说明模型为何做出决定. This is especially true for depression, where NLP models are increasingly used to infer symptoms or disorder status from language, but predictive performance alone does not show why a model reached its decision[12]。因此，根据之前的工作，心理健康模型应该做的不仅仅是返回准确的标签。他们应该支持专家对预测背后的证据进行检查，并将模型输出与具有临床意义的构造联系起来. Thus, based on prior work, mental health models should do more than return accurate labels. They should support expert inspection of the evidence underlying a prediction and relate model outputs to clinically meaningful constructs[13, 22]。这一要求还扩展到最近的大型语言模型（LLM）方法，它可以在预测的同时生成解释；然而，这些解释仍然需要仔细评估质量、可靠性和临床实用性，然后才能支持专家审查. This requirement also extends to recent large language model (LLM) approaches, which can generate explanations alongside predictions; however, these explanations still require careful evaluation of quality, reliability, and clinical usefulness before they can support expert review[29, 19, 21].

现有的工作以多种方式解决这个问题。事后方法Existing work addresses this problem in several ways. Post-hoc methods[20]例如 Shapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP)such as SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP)[18]和局部可解释的与模型无关的解释（LIME）and Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations (LIME)[24]通常用于显示哪些特征对抑郁症检测或严重程度预测影响最大are commonly used to show which features most influenced depression detection or severity prediction[1]。其他研究通过预测症状或问卷项目而不仅仅是最终标签来提高临床可解释性，从而使输出能够更直接地与既定的评估实践进行比较. Other studies improve clinical interpretability by predicting symptoms or questionnaire items rather than only a final label, enabling outputs to be compared more directly with established assessment practice[31, 26]。对临床医生与心理健康人工智能互动的研究进一步表明，当解释清楚地说明预测的基础并自然地适合专家评审时，解释是最有用的. Research on clinician interaction with mental-health AI further shows that explanations are most useful when they clearly state the basis of a prediction and fit naturally into expert review[14]。最近的可解释法学硕士研究遵循相同的方向，将预测与生成的解释配对，同时也表明必须评估解释质量而不是假设. Recent interpretable LLM studies follow the same direction by pairing predictions with generated explanations, while also showing that explanation quality must be evaluated rather than assumed[29]。然而，大多数现有的工作仍然集中在使预测在事后变得更容易解释，而如何组织解释性内容以供系统的专家审查。. Most existing work, however, still centers on making predictions more interpretable after the fact, leaving open how explanatory content should be organized for systematic expert review.

Ⅱ-BII-B 可解释的心理健康注释框架Explainable Mental Health Annotation Frameworks

可解释的心理健康预测的注释框架越来越多地超越文档级标签，转向临床结构化监督。 PsySym 推出了一个基于 DSM 的症状类别和临床问卷构建的多疾病症状注释框架，具有针对性的检索和质量控制程序，以支持症状辅助、可解释的检测Annotation frameworks for explainable mental health prediction increasingly move beyond document-level labels toward clinically structured supervision. PsySym introduced a multi-disease symptom annotation framework built from DSM-based symptom classes and clinical questionnaires, with targeted retrieval and quality-control procedures to support symptom-assisted, interpretable detection[30]。 DepreSym 通过构建与 21 Beck 抑郁量表-II（BDI-II 症状）一致的句子级语料库，使用专家评估员和详细的相关性指南将文本与特定抑郁标记链接起来，将重点缩小到抑郁症. DepreSym narrowed this focus to depression by constructing a sentence-level corpus aligned with 21 Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II symptoms), using expert assessors and detailed relevance guidelines to link text to specific depressive markers[23]。最近的抑郁症数据集更进一步，注释了抑郁跨度和症状类别，从而能够在文本证据和症状对齐水平上进行评估. A more recent depression dataset goes one step further by annotating both depressive spans and symptom categories, enabling evaluation at the level of textual evidence and symptom alignment[6]。总之，这些研究表明了从症状类别到句子级相关性和跨度级证据的更精细注释的发展。. Together, these studies point to a move toward finer annotation, from symptom categories to sentence-level relevance and span-level evidence.

并行的工作涉及如何生成和审查此类注释。在临床丰富的心理健康数据中，法学硕士已被用来协助注释和数据收集，但可靠的输出仍然取决于专家定义的变量和临床医生的监督A parallel line of work addresses how such annotations are produced and reviewed. In clinically enriched mental-health data, LLMs have been used to assist annotation and data collection, but reliable outputs still depend on expert-defined variables and clinician oversight[2]。更通用的人类-法学硕士注释系统通过结合提示控制、注释管理、自动标记和选择性人工验证，从工作流程的角度得出了相同的观点. More general human–LLM annotation systems make the same point from a workflow perspective by combining prompt control, annotation management, automatic labeling, and selective human verification[15]。这些系统演示了如何使用人工智能生成、管理和检查标签，但它们为与心理健康环境中的临床结构化症状数据更紧密地集成留下了空间。剩下的差距不是人工智能是否可以协助注释，而是如何将基于症状的证据注释和人工智能专家协作审查统一到一个单一的管道中，以进行可解释的心理健康预测。. These systems demonstrate how labels can be generated, managed, and checked using AI, but they leave room for closer integration with clinically structured symptom data in mental health settings. The remaining gap is not whether AI can assist with annotation, but how symptom-grounded evidence annotation and collaborative AI–expert review can be unified into a single pipeline for explainable mental health prediction.

Ⅲ-CIII-C3 自我进化机制The Self-Evolution Mechanism

该框架的核心创新在于它能够向The core innovation of this framework is its ability to learn from the审计追踪audit trail无需重新训练模型参数：without retraining model parameters:

Expert-approved “gold cases” populate the示例内存Example Memory，而提炼的基本原理存储在, while the distilled rationales are stored in the反思记忆Reflection Memory。这种闭环演化确保了智能体未来的建议变得越来越准确，使该框架成为真正可扩展、自我改进的临床工具。. This closed-loop evolution ensures that the agent’s future proposals become increasingly accurate, making the framework a truly scalable, self-improving clinical tool.

在实现中，专家修订被转换为两次内存更新。首先，通过最终专家批准的案例存储为示例内存条目，其中包含源文本、可接受的证据范围、标准标签和最终诊断。其次，重复出现的纠正模式被总结为反射记忆条目，例如用于区分 DSM-5-TR A1 证据中的弱情感线索或拒绝不受支持的证据到标准链接的规则。在后续注释期间，代理使用当前案例表示检索相关示例和反思，并将它们合并到提示上下文中。没有更新模型参数；适应只能通过检索增强的记忆更新来实现。In implementation, expert revisions are converted into two memory updates. First, cases that pass final expert approval are stored as Example Memory entries containing the source text, accepted evidence spans, criterion labels, and final diagnosis. Second, recurring correction patterns are summarized into Reflection Memory entries, such as rules for distinguishing weak affective cues from DSM-5-TR A1 evidence or for rejecting unsupported evidence-to-criterion links. During subsequent annotation, the agent retrieves relevant examples and reflections using the current case representation and incorporates them into the prompting context. No model parameters are updated; adaptation occurs only through retrieval-augmented memory updates.

IV 实验与结果Experiment and Results

IV-A 实验装置Experimental Setup

我们对从 ReDSM5 抑郁症相关基准数据集中抽取的 10 个复杂临床病例进行了试点评估We conduct a pilot evaluation on 10 complex clinical cases sampled from the ReDSM5 depression-related benchmark dataset[5]。每个案例都包含支持 MDD 九项 DSM-5-TR 标准的叙述性证据。为了构建专家金标签，五位心理学及相关领域的专家在共享的 DSM-5-TR 协议下独立注释了案例。注释包括临床相关的句子选择、标准级别标签、证据到标准的链接以及最终的 MDD 诊断。分歧经过交叉检查并通过共识裁决解决，为评估拟议框架提供了黄金注释。. Each case includes narrative evidence supporting the nine DSM-5-TR criteria for MDD. To construct expert gold labels, five experts in psychology and related fields independently annotated the cases under a shared DSM-5-TR protocol. The annotation includes clinically relevant sentence selection, criterion-level labels, evidence-to-criterion links, and final MDD diagnosis. Disagreements were cross-checked and resolved through consensus adjudication, producing gold annotations for evaluating the proposed framework.

建立黄金标签后，我们的框架使用三个 LLM 主干处理相同的案例：Gemini-3.5-Flash-Lite、GPT-4o-mini 和 GPT-5.4-mini。我们在专家校正之前评估自主输出，以评估人工智能生成注释的内在质量。评估涵盖四个层面：句子级证据筛选、DSM-5-TR标准分类、证据对对齐、案例级诊断。此外，我们还衡量专家的修订工作和注释效率，以量化人类人工智能工作流程的实际效用。After gold labels were established, the same cases were processed by our framework using three LLM backbones: Gemini-3.5-Flash-Lite, GPT-4o-mini, and GPT-5.4-mini. We evaluate the autonomous outputs before expert correction to assess the intrinsic quality of AI-generated annotations. The evaluation covers four levels: sentence-level evidence screening, DSM-5-TR criterion classification, evidence-pair alignment, and case-level diagnosis. In addition, we measure expert revision effort and annotation efficiency to quantify the practical utility of the human-AI workflow.

IV-B 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

我们使用基于共识的指标和人力指标来评估该框架。对于自主注释质量，我们报告句子级别和 DSM-5-TR 标准级别的精确度、召回率和 F1 分数，与专家黄金注释相比，衡量系统识别临床相关证据和分配症状标签的效果。我们还计算证据对 F1，其中仅当模型将正确的 DSM-5-TR 标准链接到正确的支持句子时，每个预测才被认为是正确的。病例级 MDD 诊断准确性用于衡量与专家最终诊断的一致性。We evaluate the framework using both consensus-based and human-effort metrics. For autonomous annotation quality, we report precision, recall, and F1-score at the sentence level and DSM-5-TR criterion level, measuring how well the system identifies clinically relevant evidence and assigns symptom labels compared with expert gold annotations. We also compute evidence-pair F1, where each prediction is considered correct only if the model links the correct DSM-5-TR criterion to the correct supporting sentence. Case-level MDD diagnosis accuracy is used to measure agreement with expert final diagnoses.

为了评估以人为本的效用，我们报告了节省的时间、总编辑量、标准翻转和证据编辑量，这些数据量化了使用人工智能生成的注释而不是手动注释时所需的专家纠正量。这些基于工作量的指标在案例级别进行计算，然后对 10 个评估样本进行平均。节省的时间是通过将平均手动注释时间与平均人工智能辅助审阅时间进行比较来计算的。总编辑衡量每个案例的专家更正的平均数量，而标准翻转和证据编辑分别捕获 DSM-5-TR 标准决策和支持证据链接的更改。To assess human-centered utility, we report time saved, total edits, criterion flips, and evidence edits, which quantify the amount of expert correction required when using AI-generated annotations instead of manual annotation. These effort-based metrics are computed at the case level and then averaged across the 10 evaluated samples. Time saved is calculated by comparing the average manual annotation time with the average AI-assisted review time. Total edits measure the average number of expert corrections per case, while criterion flips and evidence edits separately capture changes to DSM-5-TR criterion decisions and supporting evidence links.

IV-E 调查结果摘要Summary of Findings

我们的试点结果表明，所提出的框架实现了强大的人类人工智能一致性，生成可解释的基于证据的 DSM-5-TR 注释，并减少了专家注释工作。结果还揭示了法学硕士骨干之间的互补优势。 Gemini-3.5-Flash-Lite在句子精度、证据基础、效率方面表现最好； GPT-4o-mini在标准级别分类方面最强； GPT-5.4-mini 提供高召回率和诊断准确性。总的来说，这些发现表明该框架与模型无关，并且支持一致的专家循环临床注释工作流程。Our pilot results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves strong human-AI agreement, produces explainable evidence-grounded DSM-5-TR annotations, and reduces expert annotation effort. The results also reveal complementary strengths across LLM backbones. Gemini-3.5-Flash-Lite performs best in sentence precision, evidence grounding, and efficiency; GPT-4o-mini is strongest in criterion-level classification; and GPT-5.4-mini provides high recall and diagnostic accuracy. Overall, these findings suggest that the framework is model-agnostic and support a consistent expert-in-the-loop clinical annotation workflow.

V 结论Conclusion

本文提出了一个自我进化的 DSM-5-TR 注释框架，用于可解释的 MDD 预测。通过结合基于 LLM 的证据筛选、标准级 DSM-5-TR 推理和专家在环验证，该框架可生成结构化标签、支持证据和可追溯的修订历史。试点结果显示了跨多个注释级别的人类与人工智能的强烈一致性，并证明人工智能辅助标记可以大大减少专家修订时间，同时保留专家控制。通过This paper presents a self-evolving DSM-5-TR annotation framework for explainable MDD prediction. By combining LLM-based evidence screening, criterion-level DSM-5-TR reasoning, and expert-in-the-loop verification, the framework produces structured labels, supporting evidence, and traceable revision histories. The pilot results show strong human-AI agreement across multiple annotation levels and demonstrate that AI-assisted labeling can substantially reduce expert revision time while preserving expert control. Through示例内存Example Memory和and反思记忆Reflection Memory，专家修正进一步转化为可重用的反馈，以改进未来的注释，而无需模型重新训练。未来的工作将把评估扩展到更大、更多样化的数据集；检查整个反馈周期的长期改进；并使该框架适应其他心理健康状况和更细粒度的评估任务。, expert corrections are further converted into reusable feedback for improving future annotations without model retraining. Future work will extend the evaluation to larger, more diverse datasets; examine long-term improvement across feedback cycles; and adapt the framework to both other mental health conditions and finer-grained assessment tasks.
