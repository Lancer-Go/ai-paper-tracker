用于耦合需求预测和供应链优化的混合深度学习方法Hybrid Deep Learning Approach for Coupled Demand Forecasting and Supply Chain Optimization

Nusrat Yasmin Nadia Md Habibul Arif Habibor Rahman Rabby Md Iftekhar Monzur Tanvir Md. Jakir Hossen M. F. Mridha
摘要Abstract

对于需求波动和供应不确定的行业，例如纺织品和个人防护装备 (PPE)，供应链的弹性和效率至关重要。传统的预测和优化方法通常单独运作，限制了它们在现实世界中的有效性。Supply chain resilience and efficiency are vital in industries characterized by volatile demand and uncertain supply, such as textiles and personal protective equipment (PPE). Traditional forecasting and optimization approaches often operate in isolation, limiting their real-world effectiveness.本文提出了一种供需预测和优化的混合人工智能框架（HAF-DS），它将基于长短期记忆（LSTM）的需求预测模块与混合整数线性规划（MILP）优化层集成在一起。 LSTM 捕获时间和上下文需求依赖性，而优化层则规定具有成本效益的补货和分配决策。This paper proposes a Hybrid AI Framework for Demand–Supply Forecasting and Optimization (HAF-DS), which integrates a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)–based demand forecasting module with a mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) optimization layer. The LSTM captures temporal and contextual demand dependencies, while the optimization layer prescribes cost-efficient replenishment and allocation decisions. 该框架通过基于嵌入的特征表示和循环神经架构共同最小化预测误差和运营成本。对纺织品销售和供应链数据集的实验表明，与统计和深度学习基线相比，性能显着提升。在组合数据集上，HAF-DS 将平均绝对误差 (MAE) 从 15.04 降低到 12.83 (14.7%)，将均方根误差 (RMSE) 从 19.53 降低到 17.11 (12.4%)，将平均绝对百分比误差 (MAPE) 从 9.5% 降低到 8.1%。库存成本下降5.4%，缺货下降27.5%，服务水平从95.5%上升到97.8%。The framework jointly minimizes forecasting error and operational cost through embedding-based feature representation and recurrent neural architectures. Experiments on textile sales and supply chain datasets show significant performance gains over statistical and deep learning baselines. On the combined dataset, HAF-DS reduced Mean Absolute Error (MAE) from 15.04 to 12.83 (14.7%), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) from 19.53 to 17.11 (12.4%), and Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) from 9.5% to 8.1%. Inventory cost decreased by 5.4%, stockouts by 27.5%, and service level rose from 95.5% to 97.8%. 这些结果证实，将预测性预测与规范性优化相结合可以提高准确性和效率，为现代纺织品和个人防护用品供应链提供可扩展且适应性强的解决方案。These results confirm that coupling predictive forecasting with prescriptive optimization enhances both accuracy and efficiency, providing a scalable and adaptable solution for modern textile and PPE supply chains.

1 简介Introduction

由于快速全球化、产品生命周期缩短和需求波动加剧，供应链管理的全球格局变得越来越复杂。 纺织品和个人防护装备 (PPE) 等行业面临着消费者需求波动、流行病等危机以及物流和供应商网络中断带来的挑战The global landscape of supply chain management has become increasingly complex due to rapid globalization, shorter product life cycles, and heightened demand volatility. Industries such as textiles and personal protective equipment (PPE) face challenges from fluctuating consumer demand, crises such as pandemics, and disruptions in logistics and supplier networks[3, 28]。 最近的研究强调数字化转型和混合人工智能以实现实时适应和恢复力. Recent studies emphasize digital transformation and hybrid AI for real-time adaptation and resilience[23].

这里解决的主要研究问题是需求预测和供应优化之间的脱节。包括统计时间序列和机器学习方法在内的传统预测模型侧重于预测准确性，但并不将预测转化为可操作的供应链政策The main research problem addressed here is the disconnect between demand forecasting and supply optimization. Conventional forecasting models including statistical time-series and machine learning methods focus on prediction accuracy but do not convert forecasts into actionable supply chain policies[24]。同样，优化模型通常依赖于无法捕捉现实世界变化的简化假设，从而使其建议不太可靠. Likewise, optimization models often rely on simplifying assumptions that fail to capture real-world variability, making their recommendations less reliable[15]。预测分析和运营决策之间的差距在纺织品和个人防护用品供应链中至关重要，预测不准确或补货效率低下会导致库存过剩、短缺和服务水平问题。. This gap between predictive analytics and operational decision-making is critical in textile and PPE supply chains, where inaccurate forecasts or inefficient replenishment lead to overstocking, shortages, and service-level issues.

本研究旨在设计和评估一个混合人工智能框架，将预测和优化统一到一个集成系统中。目标不仅是提高预测准确性，还要确保预测洞察力推动经济高效且以服务为导向的决策。所提出的框架处理大规模数据，集成库存水平、交货时间和供应商可变性等上下文变量，并提供可提高准确性和效率的可行结果。This study aims to design and evaluate a hybrid AI framework that unifies forecasting and optimization into an integrated system. The objective is not only to improve forecast accuracy but also to ensure that predictive insights drive cost-efficient and service-oriented decisions. The proposed framework handles large-scale data, integrates contextual variables such as inventory levels, lead times, and supplier variability, and delivers actionable outcomes that enhance both accuracy and efficiency.

这项研究除了方法创新之外还具有更广泛的重要性。在纺织品和个人防护用品供应链中，准确的预测和有效的优化不仅对于成本效率至关重要，而且对于社会和经济弹性也至关重要。例如，在全球健康危机期间，个人防护装备短缺凸显了强大供应链的重要性。通过将基于深度学习的预测与优化相结合，该方法可以满足预测性和规范性需求，提高决策质量，降低成本波动并提高服务水平The research holds broader importance beyond methodological innovation. In textile and PPE supply chains, accurate forecasting and effective optimization are essential not only for cost efficiency but also for social and economic resilience. For instance, during global health crises, PPE shortages underscore the importance of robust supply chains. By combining deep learning–based forecasting with optimization, this approach addresses both predictive and prescriptive needs, improving decision quality, reducing cost volatility, and enhancing service levels[10].

与传统的混合或两阶段供应链模型将需求预测和供应优化视为顺序且松散耦合的过程不同，所提出的框架引入了完全集成的预测-规定性学习架构。关键的新颖之处在于预测和优化模块之间的端到端、可微耦合，其中供应方的运营目标通过共享梯度直接影响需求预测模型的学习。 具体来说，提出的供需预测和优化混合人工智能框架（HAF-DS）使用统一的损失函数共同最小化预测误差和运营成本，允许下游成本、服务水平和约束违规在训练期间主动指导预测参数的更新。这种设计确保预测准确性的提高始终如一地转化为切实的运营效益，解决了传统预测然后优化和两阶段混合方法的关键限制。Unlike conventional hybrid or two-stage supply chain models that treat demand forecasting and supply optimization as sequential and loosely coupled processes, the proposed framework introduces a fully integrated predictive–prescriptive learning architecture. The key novelty lies in the end-to-end, differentiable coupling between the forecasting and optimization modules, where supply-side operational objectives directly influence the learning of the demand forecasting model through shared gradients. Specifically, the proposed Hybrid AI Framework for Demand–Supply Forecasting and Optimization (HAF-DS) jointly minimizes forecasting error and operational cost using a unified loss function, allowing downstream cost, service-level, and constraint violations to actively guide the update of forecasting parameters during training. This design ensures that improvements in predictive accuracy consistently translate into tangible operational benefits, addressing a critical limitation of traditional predict-then-optimize and two-stage hybrid approaches.

为了实现这些目标，该研究开发了一种基于深度学习的混合框架，将循环神经架构与供应方优化模块集成在一起。该方法涉及预处理来自纺织品销售和供应链运营的异构数据集，应用基于嵌入的特征表示，以及联合训练预测和优化组件。使用平均绝对误差 (MAE)、均方根误差 (RMSE)、平均绝对百分比误差 (MAPE)、库存成本、缺货率、服务水平和总成本来评估模型To achieve these objectives, the study develops a deep learning–based hybrid framework that integrates recurrent neural architectures with supply-side optimization modules. The methodology involves preprocessing heterogeneous datasets from textile sales and supply chain operations, applying embedding-based feature representations, and jointly training forecasting and optimization components. 2 相关工作Related Work

供应链预测和优化的研究沿着两条基本独立但互补的轨迹发展：使用统计和机器学习技术的需求预测，以及使用启发式和数学编程方法的供应链优化。 ARIMA、指数平滑和回归模型等传统预测方法长期以来一直作为预测制造业和零售业需求趋势的基线Research on supply chain forecasting and optimization has evolved along two largely independent but complementary trajectories: demand forecasting using statistical and machine learning techniques, and supply chain optimization using heuristic and mathematical programming approaches. Traditional forecasting methods such as ARIMA, exponential smoothing, and regression models have long served as baselines for predicting demand trends in manufacturing and retail[12]。虽然这些方法对于稳定环境中的短期预测是可解释且有效的，但它们常常难以应对非线性、季节性和需求突然变化，而这些是纺织品和个人防护用品供应链等动态市场的特征。. While these methods are interpretable and effective for short-term prediction in stable environments, they often struggle with nonlinearities, seasonality, and abrupt demand shifts, which are characteristic of dynamic markets such as textile and PPE supply chains.

为了解决这些限制，最近的研究采用了机器学习模型，包括支持向量回归、随机森林和梯度提升，它们更适合捕获非线性依赖性。然而，这些方法仍然严重依赖手动特征工程，并且通常无法充分利用需求时间序列中固有的时间依赖性。To address these limitations, more recent studies have employed machine learning models, including support vector regression, random forests, and gradient boosting, which are better suited for capturing nonlinear dependencies. However, these approaches still rely heavily on manual feature engineering and often fail to fully exploit temporal dependencies inherent in demand time series.

深度学习的出现使得时间序列需求预测取得了重大进展。循环神经网络 (RNN)、长短期记忆 (LSTM) 网络和门控循环单元 (GRU) 等模型已被广泛采用来捕获顺序依赖性和长期时间模式The advent of deep learning has led to significant progress in time-series demand forecasting. Models such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs), long short-term memory (LSTM) networks, and gated recurrent units (GRUs) have been widely adopted to capture sequential dependencies and long-term temporal patterns[27]。这些架构已应用于多个行业，并且始终表现出比传统统计和机器学习方法更高的预测准确性。. These architectures have been applied across multiple industries and consistently demonstrate improved predictive accuracy over traditional statistical and machine learning approaches.

最近的进展结合了注意力机制和基于变压器的模型，进一步增强了对远程依赖关系和异构特征交互进行建模的能力。尽管有这些改进，许多深度学习方法仍然主要关注预测准确性，与运营决策过程的直接集成有限More recent advancements incorporate attention mechanisms and transformer-based models, which further enhance the ability to model long-range dependencies and heterogeneous feature interactions. Despite these improvements, many deep learning approaches remain focused primarily on forecasting accuracy, with limited direct integration into operational decision-making processes[5, 14, 32, 13, 29].

最近的基准测试证实，基于 Transformer 的架构在时间序列预测方面优于循环模型，提供了改进的泛化性和可扩展性Recent benchmarks confirm that Transformer-based architectures outperform recurrent models in time-series forecasting, offering improved generalization and scalability[31]。这些模型能够在波动的市场中实现数据驱动的适应，使其适用于个人防护用品和纺织品制造等高方差领域。. These models enable data-driven adaptation in volatile markets, making them suitable for high-variance domains such as PPE and textile manufacturing. 最近的物流实证基准测试还表明，统计模型、基于树的模型和深度模型的相对排名取决于噪声和需求复杂性，从而激发了在混合框架中使用经验证据和理论驱动的设计选择Recent empirical benchmarking in logistics also shows that the relative ranking of statistical, tree-based, and deep models depends on noise and demand complexity, motivating the use of both empirical evidence and theory-driven design choices in hybrid frameworks[25].

与预测文献并行，供应链优化传统上依赖于运筹学方法，例如线性规划、混合整数规划和随机优化Parallel to the forecasting literature, supply chain optimization has traditionally relied on operations research methods such as linear programming, mixed-integer programming, and stochastic optimization[22]。这些方法已应用于库存控制、生产计划、运输调度和供应商选择等问题。经济订单数量 (EOQ) 模型和再订购点启发式等经典优化框架仍然是实践中的基础工具。尽管这些模型提供了易于处理的解决方案，但它们通常依赖于简化的假设，例如固定需求或确定性的交付时间，这限制了它们在不稳定和不确定环境中的适用性。包含随机元素或强大的优化框架的扩展提高了现实性，但可扩展性和对高维现实世界供应链的适应性仍然是挑战。. These methods have been applied to problems including inventory control, production planning, transportation scheduling, and supplier selection. Classical optimization frameworks, such as the economic order quantity (EOQ) model and reorder point heuristics, remain foundational tools in practice. Although these models provide tractable solutions, they often rely on simplifying assumptions, such as stationary demand or deterministic lead times, which limit their applicability in volatile and uncertain environments. Extensions that incorporate stochastic elements or robust optimization frameworks have improved realism, but scalability and adaptability to high-dimensional, real-world supply chains remain challenges.

最近，研究转向整合机器学习和优化来创建预测规范系统。这些混合方法利用需求预测作为优化模型的输入，从而实现更准确和适应性更强的决策More recently, research has turned toward integrating machine learning and optimization to create predictive–prescriptive systems. These hybrid approaches leverage demand forecasts as inputs to optimization models, enabling more accurate and adaptive decision-making[16]。示例包括将 LSTM 预测与随机库存控制策略相结合，或将神经预测嵌入基于模拟的优化中。强化学习也已成为一个有前途的方向，代理通过与模拟供应链环境的交互来学习库存和补货决策的策略。这些方法超越了静态优化，转向了自适应决策，但通常仍然受到计算复杂性和跨行业通用性有限的限制. Examples include coupling LSTM forecasts with stochastic inventory control policies or embedding neural forecasts within simulation-based optimization. Reinforcement learning has also emerged as a promising direction, where agents learn policies for inventory and replenishment decisions through interaction with simulated supply chain environments. These methods move beyond static optimization toward adaptive decision-making but often remain constrained by computational complexity and limited generalizability across industries[6, 11].

6 结论Conclusions

本文提出了一个混合人工智能框架，该框架集成了纺织和个人防护用品行业的需求预测和供应链优化，解决了将预测准确性与运营效率保持一致的关键挑战。通过将基于深度学习的序列模型与上下文供应方特征相结合，与经典统计方法和强大的神经基线相比，该框架表现出卓越的预测准确性，同时降低了库存成本、缺货率和总运营成本，同时提高了服务水平。统一设计弥合了预测分析和规范决策之间的差距，提供了一种更强大、适应性更强的方法来处理动态和不确定的供应链环境。对纺织品销售数据、供应链记录及其组合的实验评估证实，混合模型始终优于传统预测技术和独立优化策略，其收益在统计和操作上均有意义。从实用的角度来看，所提出的框架可以通过模块化 API 或中间件接口集成到现有的企业资源规划 (ERP) 或供应链管理 (SCM) 平台中。预测模块可以作为生成需求预测的服务层运行，而优化组件可以充当采购或生产计划系统内的决策支持引擎。这种设计可以在不破坏现有工作流程的情况下逐步采用。此外，该架构支持批处理和流数据模式，允许在定期计划和实时控制设置中进行部署。然而，实施方面可能会出现挑战。集成需要跨采购、生产和物流数据库的干净、标准化的数据管道。训练和推理需要适度的 GPU 资源，并且保持联合优化稳定性可能涉及针对每个企业环境的超参数调整。为了确保采用，应合并用户界面设计和可解释性模块，使管理人员能够以成本或服务水平术语解释模型输出。解决这些因素将简化实际部署并提高决策者对人工智能驱动建议的信任。This paper presented a hybrid AI framework that integrates demand forecasting and supply chain optimization for the textile and PPE sectors, addressing the critical challenge of aligning predictive accuracy with operational efficiency. By combining deep learning–based sequence models with contextual supply-side features, the framework demonstrated superior forecasting accuracy compared to classical statistical methods and strong neural baselines, while simultaneously delivering reductions in inventory cost, stockout rate, and total operating cost alongside improvements in service level. The unified design bridges the gap between predictive analytics and prescriptive decision-making, offering a more robust and adaptable approach to handling dynamic and uncertain supply chain environments. Experimental evaluations on textile sales data, supply chain records, and their combination confirmed that the hybrid model consistently outperforms both traditional forecasting techniques and standalone optimization policies, with gains that are both statistically and operationally meaningful. From a practical standpoint, the proposed framework can be integrated into existing Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) or Supply Chain Management (SCM) platforms through a modular API or middleware interface. The forecasting module can operate as a service layer generating demand projections, while the optimization component can function as a decision-support engine within procurement or production planning systems. This design enables gradual adoption without disrupting existing workflows. Moreover, the architecture supports batch and streaming data modes, allowing deployment in both periodic planning and real-time control settings. However, implementation challenges may arise. Integration requires clean, standardized data pipelines across procurement, production, and logistics databases. Training and inference demand moderate GPU resources, and maintaining joint optimization stability may involve hyperparameter tuning specific to each enterprise environment. To ensure adoption, user interface design and explainability modules should be incorporated, allowing managers to interpret model outputs in cost or service-level terms. Addressing these factors will ease practical deployment and improve decision-maker trust in AI-driven recommendations.
