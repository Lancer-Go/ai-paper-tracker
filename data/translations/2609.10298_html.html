摘要Abstract

针对监控和控制工业流程的运营技术 (OT) 系统的网络攻击对基本社会服务构成了越来越大的威胁。因此，开发自动入侵响应策略非常重要。在本文中，我们使用 POMDP 框架提出了 OT 入侵响应用例的正式模型。它包括基于流量测量的部分可观测性的现实模型。这种方法使我们能够开发基于 PPO 的易于处理的、基于学习的自动入侵响应解决方案。我们在模拟 OT 系统上评估了获得的响应策略，发现它们可以有效抵御所研究用例的几种类型的 MITRE 攻击。Cyberattacks against Operational Technology (OT) systems, which monitor and control industrial processes, pose an increasing threat to essential societal services. For this reason, developing automated intrusion response strategies is highly important. In this paper, we present a formal model of an OT intrusion response use case using the POMDP framework. It includes a realistic model of partial observability that is based on traffic measurements. This approach allows us to develop tractable, learning-based solution methods for automated intrusion response, which are based on PPO. We evaluate the obtained response strategies on an emulated OT system and find that they are effective against several types of MITRE attacks for the studied use case.

最近，已经开发出 OT 入侵响应方法，这些方法不依赖于预定义的规则，而是基于从系统测量中学习。这些方法使用强化学习（RL）作为主导概念。一些强化学习方法在没有正式系统模型的情况下学习启发式策略Recently, methods for OT intrusion response have been developed that do not rely on predefined rules, but are based on learning from system measurements. These methods use Reinforcement Learning (RL) as the dominant concept. Some RL approaches learn heuristic policies without a formal system model[14, 15, 16]，这妨碍了对可实现的最优策略的理解。其他作品应用正式模型，例如马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）, which precludes the understanding of an achievable optimal strategy. Other works apply formal models, such as Markov Decision Process (MDP)[17, 18, 19], 部分可观察马尔可夫决策过程 (POMDP), Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP)[20, 21]，和随机博弈, and stochastic game[22, 23]。 大多数这些研究方向都假设系统状态或攻击者行为是完全可观察的，这是不现实的，并且限制了结果的实际相关性。或者，一些研究假设特定的观察模型，但没有详细说明如何在实际系统中获得此类观察结果. Most of these lines of research assume full observability of the system state or attacker actions, which is unrealistic and limits the practical relevance of the results. Alternatively, some studies assume specific observation models, without detailing how such observations can be obtained in real systems[20, 21].

我们认为这项工作的主要局限性如下。首先，像所有形式模型一样，我们的模型依赖于简化假设。例如，我们假设攻击者和防御者的行动是在离散时间同时采取的，并且具有立竿见影的效果。实际上，这些动作在连续时间内异步发生，具有不同的持续时间，并对系统产生延迟。捕捉这种动态将使模型更加真实，但代价是增加学习防御策略的计算复杂性。此外，我们可以以更高的粒度对状态空间进行建模，但也有同样的缺点。其次，我们的 POMDP 模型对于特定用例、系统配置和攻击类型有效。在这项工作中，我们没有研究我们的模型在这些方面的泛化特性。第三，这项工作目前没有考虑操作安全性，这限制了防御者的策略。未来我们也会这样做。We see the main limitations of this work as follows. First, like all formal models, our model relies on simplifying assumptions. For instance, we assume that attacker and defender actions are taken simultaneously at discrete times and have immediate effects. In reality, such actions occur asynchronously in continuous time, have different durations, and affect the system with delays. Capturing such dynamics would make the model more realistic, at the expense of increasing the computational complexity of the learning defender strategies. Also, we could have modeled the state space with higher granularity, with the same drawback. Second, our POMDP model is valid for the specific use case, system configuration and attack types. In this work, we did not study the generalization property of our model with respect to those aspects. Third, this work currently does not consider operational safety, which constrains defender strategy. We will do so in the future.

VI 构建仿真环境和模拟环境来训练和评估防御者策略Constructing an Emulation Environment and a Simulation Environment to Train and Evaluate Defender Strategies

使用我们的解决方案方法学习防御者策略并对其进行评估需要重复的 POMDP 事件。此类事件无法直接在运行的 OT 基础设施中执行，由于训练需要大量事件，这既效率低下，又因为需要在系统中执行攻击者操作而不切实际。基于这些原因，我们开发了仿真环境和模拟环境。仿真环境旨在紧密匹配目标基础设施的运行条件。它有两个目的：收集 IDS 测量结果以识别 POMDP 的观测模型，并在现实条件下评估防御者策略。模拟环境生成 POMDP 事件以有效训练防御者策略。Learning defender strategies using our solution methods and evaluating them requires repeated POMDP episodes. Such episodes cannot be performed directly in an operating OT infrastructure, which is both inefficient due to the large number of episodes required for training and impractical because it requires performing attacker actions in the system. For these reasons, we develop an emulation environment and a simulation environment. The emulation environment is designed to closely match the operational conditions of the target infrastructure. It serves two purposes: collecting IDS measurements to identify the observation model of the POMDP, and evaluating defender strategies under realistic conditions. The simulation environment generates POMDP episodes to efficiently train the defender strategies.

基于用例的 POMDP 模型，我们定义了最佳防御者策略，并提出了两种学习该策略近似值的解决方法。该解决方法使我们能够以合理的成本和较低的样本复杂度来学习防御者策略。仿真环境中的评估表明，通过这些方法可以学习有效的防守策略。特别是，BF-PPO 接近具有完全可观察性的基线性能。Based on the POMDP model of the use case, we have defined an optimal defender strategy and proposed two solution methods for learning the approximation of this strategy. The solution methods allow us to learn the defender strategies at a reasonable cost and with low sample complexity. The evaluation in the emulation environment shows that effective defender strategies can be learned with these methods. In particular, BF-PPO approaches the performance of a baseline with full observability.

至于未来的工作，我们计划与工业合作伙伴合作，在工业测试台上评估所提出的模型和解决方案方法，以实现类似于本文研究的入侵响应用例。该评估将使我们能够研究在不同的现实环境中建模和计算防御者策略的方法，并进一步包括安全约束。As for future work, we plan to evaluate the proposed model and solution methods on an industrial testbed in collaboration with an industrial partner, for an intrusion response use case similar to the one studied in this paper. This evaluation will allow us to study our approach to modeling and computing defender strategies in a different and realistic environment and further to include safety constraints.
