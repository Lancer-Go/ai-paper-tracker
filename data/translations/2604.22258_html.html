治疗心脏时保护大脑：用于检测栓子的卷积神经网络Protect the brain when treating the heart: A convolutional Neural Network for Detecting Emboli

Andrea Angino1,3, Ken Trotti2, Diego Ulisse Pizzagalli1, Rolf Krause1,2, Tiziano Torre4, Stefanos Demertzis4,5 1 Università della Svizzera Italiana (USI), Center for Computational Medicine in Cardiology (CCMC)2 King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), AMCS/CEMSE Division3 Unidistance Suisse4 Cardiac Surgery Department, Cardiocentro Ticino Institute, Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale (EOC)5 Università della Svizzera Italiana (USI), Faculty of Biomedical Sciences
摘要Abstract

气体微栓子（GME）是手术和经导管方法心脏结构干预的常见并发症。经胸心脏超声成像是一种可视化循环 GME 存在的便捷方法。然而，由于依赖于操作员的视图、高速度以及背景中具有相似结构的物体，它们的检测和量化远非微不足道。 在这里，我们提出了一种基于 2.5D U-Net 架构的方法来分割时空连接数据中的 GME。这种方法可以产生稳健的背景检测和高分割精度，同时保持实时执行速度。这些特性有利于将拟议的管道集成到患者监测手术方案中，从而随着时间的推移提供 GME 面积的量化。Gaseous microemboli (GME) represent a common complication of cardiac structural interventions across both surgical and transcatheter approaches. Trans thoracic cardiac ultrasound imaging represents a convenient methodology to visualize the presence of circulating GME. However, their detection and quantification are far from trivial due to operator-dependent view, high velocity, and objects with similar structure in the background. Here, we propose an approach based on a 2.5D U-Net architecture to segment GME in space-time connected data. Such an approach yields robust detection vs. background and high segmentation accuracy while retaining real-time execution speed. These properties facilitated the integration of the proposed pipeline in patient-monitoring surgical protocols, providing the quantification of GME area over time.

I 简介Introduction

由于无意中将栓塞、固体或气体颗粒引入血流，心脏结构干预（手术和/或经导管）会带来严重的神经风险。气体微栓子 (GME)，主要是气泡，尤其令人担忧，因为它们可以穿过循环系统，可能滞留在脑血管中，导致无声梗塞和认知缺陷Cardiac structural interventions (surgical and/or transcatheter) pose significant neurological risks due to the inadvertent introduction of emboli, solid or gaseous particles, into the bloodstream. Gaseous microemboli (GME), primarily air bubbles, are especially concerning as they can travel through the circulatory system, potentially lodging in cerebral vessels and leading to silent infarctions and cognitive deficits[5]。 GME 引起的神经系统并发症已被广泛记录，包括心脏手术后患者的短暂损伤和长期认知能力下降. Neurological complications arising from GME have been documented extensively, ranging from transient impairments to long-term cognitive decline in patients following cardiac procedures[11]。现有的解决方案，如经颅多普勒超声和弥散加权磁共振成像，提供了一种术后栓塞检测的方法，但它们缺乏促进手术期间实时干预所需的即时性，限制了它们在减轻术中神经系统风险方面的有效性. Existing solutions, such as transcranial Doppler ultrasound and diffusion-weighted MRI, provide a means of post-operative emboli detection, yet they lack the immediacy necessary to facilitate real-time intervention during surgery, limiting their effectiveness in mitigating intraoperative neurological risks[16]。因此，在手术工作流程中提供实时反馈可以通过提供及时的置信度估计和改进术中决策来支持手术团队. Providing real-time feedback within the surgical workflow can therefore support the operating team by offering timely confidence estimates and improving intraoperative decision-making[12].

我们专注于经胸心脏超声心动图，这是一种标准化且易于实施的方式，可以集成到临床实践中以可视化心腔中的 GME。尽管它具有广泛的可用性并且适用于术中监测，但仍然缺乏用于自动 GME 检测和量化的可靠软件。这种差距很大程度上是由于栓子尺寸小、外观不规则且多变、与周围组织和常见超声心动图伪影高度相似，这使得纯粹基于外观的检测变得不可靠。We focus on transthoracic cardiac echocardiography, a standardized and easily implementable modality that can be integrated into clinical practice to visualize GMEs in cardiac chambers. Despite its wide availability and suitability for intraoperative monitoring, reliable software for automatic GME detection and quantification is still lacking. This gap is largely explained by the small size of emboli, their irregular and variable appearance, and their strong similarity to surrounding tissue and common echocardiographic artifacts, which makes purely appearance-based detection unreliable.

基于学习的细分提供了解决这些挑战的自然方法。最先进的架构，例如全卷积网络 (FCN)Learning-based segmentation provides a natural way to address these challenges. State-of-the-art architectures such as Fully Convolutional Networks (FCNs)[14]和 Mask R-CNNand Mask R-CNN[8]已成功应用于医学图像分割，而 U-Net 仍然是广泛采用的参考模型，因为它能够通过跳跃连接保留精细的空间细节have been successfully applied to medical image segmentation, while U-Net remains a widely adopted reference model due to its ability to preserve fine spatial details through skip connections[17]。除了静态分割之外，最近的工作表明基于 U-Net 的模型还可以分割分辨率有限的灰度图像中的运动目标. Beyond static segmentation, recent work has shown that U-Net-based models can also segment motile targets in grayscale images with limited resolution[3]。然而，在 GME 的特定情况下，仅凭外观通常是不够的，因为栓子可能与单帧中的静态散斑图案和瞬态伪影无法区分，而它们在连续帧中的快速运动提供了区分的关键线索。这激发了除了空间特征之外还利用短期时间上下文的方法。. In the specific case of GME, however, appearance alone is often insufficient as emboli may be indistinguishable from static speckle patterns and transient artifacts in single frames, while their rapid motion across consecutive frames provides a key cue for discrimination. This motivates approaches that exploit short-term temporal context in addition to spatial features.

基于这一证据，我们提出了一种基于 2.5D U-Net 架构的实时检测系统，用于超声心动图视频中的 GME 分割。该模型处理连续帧的短序列以引入有限的时间上下文，同时保持在线部署的计算可行性。通过利用时间连续性，所提出的方法旨在区分实时术中约束下的移动栓塞与动态解剖背景。Building on this evidence, we propose a real-time detection system based on a 2.5D U-Net architecture for GME segmentation in echocardiographic video. The model processes short sequences of consecutive frames to introduce limited temporal context while remaining computationally feasible for online deployment. By leveraging temporal continuity, the proposed approach is designed to distinguish moving emboli from the dynamic anatomical background under real-time intraoperative constraints.

II 数据集构建Dataset construction

分割 GME 需要训练有素的专家进行细致、耗时的工作，这些专家能够将微栓子与周围的心脏结构区分开来。Segmenting GME requires meticulous, time-intensive work by trained specialists capable of distinguishing microemboli from surrounding cardiac structures.

在这种情况下的分割提出了独特的挑战：虽然最初在每个帧内描绘无回声区域似乎很简单，但由于心肌和栓子在超声心动图评估中通常具有相似的灰度强度，因此任务变得复杂。这种相似性造成了模糊性，尤其是在边缘附近，运动是区分快速移动的栓塞与缓慢移动或静止的心脏组织的关键指标。手动分析通常需要移动视频帧来观察运动中的这些细微差异，因为与相对稳定的心脏背景相比，GME 的特点是位置变化迅速。Segmentation in this context presents unique challenges: while it may initially seem straightforward to delineate iperechogenic regions within each frame, the task is complicated by the fact that cardiac muscle and emboli often share similar grayscale intensities at echocardiographic evaluation. This similarity creates ambiguity, especially near edges, where motion is the key indicator distinguishing rapidly moving emboli from the slower-moving or stationary cardiac tissue. Manual analysis typically requires moving through video frames to observe these subtle differences in movement, as GMEs are characterized by rapid positional changes compared to the relatively stable cardiac background.

U-Net 架构的选择源于其编码器-解码器结构在生物医学成像任务中取得的成功The choice of a U-Net architecture stems from its well-documented success in biomedical imaging tasks due to its encoder-decoder structure[18]，它可以通过利用上下文信息来实现详细的细分。 U-Net 的跳跃连接允许模型保留下采样过程中丢失的空间信息，这对于在复杂背景下准确分割 GME 边界至关重要。, which enables detailed segmentation by leveraging contextual information. The U-Net’s skip connections allow the model to retain spatial information lost during down-sampling, crucial for accurately segmenting GME boundaries against a complex background.

Ⅲ-AIII-A 细分绩效指标Segmentation Performance Metrics

为了全面评估分割性能，我们考虑三个互补指标：频率偏差指数（FBI）、并交交集（IoU）和 Dice 系数。每个都捕获分割质量的不同方面，从而可以对检测 GME 区域时的模型行为进行细致入微的评估。To comprehensively evaluate segmentation performance, we consider three complementary metrics: the Frequency Bias Index (FBI), the Intersection over Union (IoU), and the Dice coefficient. Each captures different aspects of segmentation quality, allowing for a nuanced assessment of model behavior in detecting GME regions.
在本节中，我们使用以下符号：Throughout this section, we use the following notation:

  • •

    TP\mathrm{TP}（真阳性）：正确预测为 GME 的 GME 像素数，(true positives): number of GME pixels correctly predicted as GME,

  • •

    FP\mathrm{FP}（误报）：错误预测为 GME 的背景像素数，(false positives): number of background pixels incorrectly predicted as GME,

  • •

    FN\mathrm{FN}（漏报）：模型错过的 GME 像素数，(false negatives): number of GME pixels missed by the model,

  • •

    TN\mathrm{TN}（真负数）：正确预测为背景的背景像素数。(true negatives): number of background pixels correctly predicted as background.

频率偏差指数Frequency Bias Index

频率偏差指数 (FBI) 是我们分析中的一个关键绩效指标。它通常用于预测，以衡量模型过度预测或低估积极事件的趋势。在我们的上下文中，它被定义为预测为 GME 的像素数与真实 GME 像素总数之间的比率，出于可视化目的重新调整为百分比：The Frequency Bias Index (FBI) is a key performance metric in our analysis. It is commonly used in forecasting to measure the tendency of a model to over- or under-predict positive events. In our context, it is defined as the ratio between the number of pixels predicted as GME and the total number of ground-truth GME pixels, rescaled to a percentage for visualization purposes:

并交交集 (IoU)Intersection over Union (IoU)

并交交集 (IoU) 或杰卡德指数以像素计数的形式量化预测 GME 区域与真实 GME 区域之间的重叠。它被定义为The Intersection over Union (IoU), or Jaccard index, quantifies the overlap between predicted and true GME regions in terms of pixel counts. It is defined as

IoU=TPTP+FP+FN×100%\mathrm{IoU}\;=\;\frac{\mathrm{TP}}{\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FP}+\mathrm{FN}}\times 100\% (2)

IoU 为 100% 意味着完全一致，而 0% 意味着没有重叠。由于每个 FP 和 FN 都会同等地降低分数，因此 IoU 是分割质量的平衡衡量标准。在实践中，IoU 值高于 50% 被认为是可以接受的，高于 75% 的为良好，而值超过 90% 则表明在具有挑战性的医学成像任务中表现出色。An IoU of 100% means perfect agreement, while 0% means no overlap. Because every FP and FN reduces the score equally, IoU is a balanced measure of segmentation quality. In practice, IoU values above 50% are considered acceptable, above 75% good, and values exceeding 90% indicate excellent performance in challenging medical imaging tasks.

Typical benchmarks consider Dice above 70% moderate, above 85% strong, and above 90% near perfect for fine grained segmentation.

V 结论Conclusions

这项工作确定 2.5D U-Net 是一种实用且计算高效的方法，用于实时检测术中心脏超声中的气体微栓子。通过利用短期时间背景，该模型将移动的栓塞与心脏背景区分开来，同时保持实时性能。所选配置提供了准确性、GME 区域稳定性和推理速度之间的最佳权衡，从而实现了临床集成。This work identified the 2.5D U-Net as a practical and computationally efficient method for real-time detection of gaseous microemboli in intraoperative cardiac ultrasound. By leveraging short-term temporal context, the model distinguishes moving emboli from the cardiac background while maintaining real-time performance. The selected configuration provides the best trade-off between accuracy, GME area stability, and inference speed, enabling clinical integration.

尽管该研究是使用单一成像设备对有限数量的受试者进行的，但基于 U-Net 的架构在医学成像任务中表现出了强大的适应性Although the study was conducted on a limited number of subjects using a single imaging device, U-Net-based architectures have shown strong adaptability across medical imaging tasks[15]。因此，拟议的协议预计将推广到具有类似采集设置的其他中心。. Therefore, the proposed protocol is expected to generalize to other centers with similar acquisition settings.

总体而言，该框架代表了向实时人工智能辅助栓塞负荷监测迈出的一步，对术中决策和患者安全具有潜在的好处。Overall, the framework represents a step toward real-time AI-assisted monitoring of embolic load, with potential benefits for intraoperative decision-making and patient safety.

V-A 未来的作品Future Works

未来的发展将侧重于扩展模型功能、提高泛化能力并探索更先进的学习范式以更好地支持临床整合。Future developments will focus on extending model capabilities, improving generalization, and exploring more advanced learning paradigms to better support clinical integration.

当前架构的自然扩展涉及采用基于 TransUNet、SwinUNet 和 TransBTSV2 等基于 Transformer 的模型，这些模型在 ACDC 和 BraTS 等大规模基准测试中表现出了具有竞争力或卓越的性能A natural extension of the current architecture involves the adoption of transformer-based models such as TransUNet, SwinUNet, and TransBTSV2, which have demonstrated competitive or superior performance in large-scale benchmarks like ACDC and BraTS[10]。这些架构擅长捕获远程依赖性，并且在时间和空间上下文都至关重要的医疗视频分割任务中可能特别有价值。然而，它们的高数据要求和对过度拟合的敏感性给我们这样的低数据制度带来了挑战。随着我们的数据集的增长，针对特定领域的用例有效地微调或预训练此类模型将变得越来越可行。. These architectures excel at capturing long-range dependencies and could prove particularly valuable in medical video segmentation tasks, where temporal and spatial context are both critical. However, their high data requirements and sensitivity to overfitting pose challenges in low-data regimes like ours. As our dataset grows, it will become increasingly feasible to fine-tune or pre-train such models effectively for our domain-specific use case.

另外，无监督学习策略预计将在未来的发展中发挥越来越重要的作用Alternatively, unsupervised learning strategies are expected to play an increasingly central role in future developments[9]。医学视频的手动注释非常耗时并且需要专业知识，使得监督学习难以扩展。一致性正则化、伪标记和对比学习等方法提供了利用大量未标记数据的有前途的途径，减少了对昂贵注释的依赖，同时仍然提高了泛化能力. Manual annotation of medical videos is time-consuming and requires expert knowledge, making supervised learning difficult to scale. Approaches such as consistency regularization, pseudo-labeling, and contrastive learning offer promising avenues to leverage large amounts of unlabeled data, reducing the dependency on costly annotations while still improving generalization[4, 13].

因此，我们相信当前的 2.5D U-Net 架构仍然是一个可靠且计算高效的选择，非常适合我们当前的数据限制。它可以作为未来训练迭代的坚实基础，以及随着数据集成熟评估更复杂架构的优势的基线。Therefore, we believe the current 2.5D U-Net architecture remains a reliable and computationally efficient choice, well-suited to our current data constraints. It can serve as a strong foundation for future training iterations and a baseline for assessing the benefits of more complex architectures as the dataset matures.

除了架构方面的考虑之外，未来的工作还将侧重于提高模型在不同患者群体和成像条件下的稳健性和泛化性。事实证明，结合先进的数据增强策略可以通过生成合理的训练示例和减轻过度拟合来有效提高性能Beyond architectural considerations, future work will also focus on improving model robustness and generalization across diverse patient populations and imaging conditions. Incorporating advanced data augmentation strategies has proven effective in enhancing performance by generating plausible training examples and mitigating overfitting[6]。 此外，将实时反馈机制集成到临床工作流程中，包括系统 GME 评分的估计，可以通过提供及时的置信度估计和增强干预期间的决策来为临床医生提供支持. Additionally, integrating real-time feedback mechanisms into the clinical workflow, including the estimation of a systemic GME score, can support clinicians by providing timely confidence estimates and enhancing decision-making during interventions[12].
