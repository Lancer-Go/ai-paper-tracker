1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）和视觉语言模型（VLM）的快速发展促进了The rapid progress of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) has catalyzed机器人基础模型robotic foundation models，特别是视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型, particularly Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models(Sapkota et al., 2025)。 通过将感知、语言理解和动作生成结合起来，VLA 有望将自然语言意图转化为物理行为。 然而，在严格控制的实验室设置之外部署 VLA 仍然具有挑战性：体现智能是一个闭环. By coupling perception, language understanding, and action generation, VLAs promise to turn natural-language intent into physical behaviors. Yet, deploying VLAs beyond carefully controlled lab setups remains challenging: embodied intelligence is a closed-loop观察-推断-行动observe–infer–act过程中，推理阶段必须满足严格的实时要求，以避免延迟引起的卡顿、不安全振荡或任务失败process, and the inference stage must meet strict real-time requirements to avoid latency-induced stuttering, unsafe oscillations, or task failure(Brohan et al., 2023b; Fu et al., 2024).

5.3.2 跨硬件Across Hardware

由于 DP-Cache 跳过一组固定的中间扩散步骤， 它的加速在不同的硬件平台上保持一致。 另一方面，V-AEFusion 试图平衡 VLM 和 Action Expert 的计算利用率， 因此它的加速因硬件平台而异。Since DP-Cache skips a fixed set of intermediate diffusion steps, its speedup remains consistent across different hardware platforms. V-AEFusion, on the other hand, tries to balance the computational utilization of the VLM and the Action Expert, so its speedup varies across hardware platforms.
