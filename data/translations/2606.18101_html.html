用于 GUI 接地的自蒸馏Self-Distillation for GUI Grounding

摘要Abstract

图形用户界面 (GUI) 基础需要视觉语言模型 (VLM) 来识别高分辨率屏幕截图中的小目标元素并预测精确的屏幕坐标。在策略自蒸馏（OPSD）对于这种坐标敏感任务来说是一种很有前途的训练后方法，因为它提供了超出硬坐标标签的密集标记级教师信号。然而，朴素的 OPSD 不太适合 GUI 基础：OPSD 根据学生生成的前缀来评估教师，当前缀已经偏离目标坐标时，坐标标记教师信号的质量可能会下降，从而导致教师信号不可靠。为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了基于 VLM 的 GUI 接地的质量感知自蒸馏，它通过软正确性感知门控和教师概率缩放来提高坐标令牌教师信号质量。软正确性感知门检查教师当前的坐标标记预测是否仍然可以完成到学生生成的前缀下的地面实况框中。如果不是，则相应的教师信号被降低权重。然后，教师概率缩放使用教师的置信度作为轻量级因素来进一步校准门控监督的强度。一个关键的实证发现是，任何一个组件都不能单独提高整体性能，而将它们组合起来可以持续提高性能。这表明这两种机制发挥着互补作用：正确性感知门控抑制不可靠的坐标令牌监督，而教师概率缩放则校准剩余信号的强度。六个 GUI 基础基准测试的实验表明，我们的方法持续改进了基本模型并优于强大的基线。Graphical user interface (GUI) grounding requires vision-language models (VLMs) to identify small target elements in high-resolution screenshots and predict precise screen coordinates. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) is a promising post-training approach for this coordinate-sensitive task, since it provides dense token-level teacher signals beyond hard coordinate labels. However, naive OPSD is not well suited to GUI grounding: OPSD evaluates the teacher on student-generated prefixes, the quality of coordinate-token teacher signals can degrade when the prefix has already deviated from the target coordinate, leading to unreliable teacher signal. To mitigate this, We propose quality-aware self-distillation for VLM-based GUI grounding, which improves coordinate-token teacher-signal quality through soft correctness-aware gating and teacher-probability scaling. The soft correctness-aware gate checks whether the teacher’s current coordinate-token prediction can still be completed into the ground-truth box under the student-generated prefix. If not, the corresponding teacher signal is down-weighted. Teacher-probability scaling then uses the teacher’s confidence as a lightweight factor to further calibrate the strength of the gated supervision. A key empirical finding is that neither component alone improves overall performance, whereas combining them consistently improves performance. This suggests that the two mechanisms play complementary roles: correctness-aware gating suppresses unreliable coordinate-token supervision, while teacher-probability scaling calibrates the strength of the remaining signals. Experiments across six GUI grounding benchmarks show that our method consistently improves the base model and outperforms strong baselines.

1 简介Introduction

GUI 接地是操作计算机、移动设备和 Web 应用程序的 VLM 和代理的基本功能GUI grounding is a fundamental capability for VLMs and agents that operate computers, mobile devices, and web applications(Cheng et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Gou et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2024b)。 给定屏幕截图和指令，模型必须识别预期的界面元素并输出其屏幕坐标. Given a screenshot and an instruction, the model must identify the intended interface element and output its screen coordinates(Cheng et al., 2024; Gou et al., 2025; Park et al., 2025)。 此任务在高分辨率屏幕截图和复杂的 GUI 场景中尤其具有挑战性，其中目标元素可能很小、视觉上相似且排列密集. This task is especially challenging in high-resolution screenshots and complex GUI scenes, where target elements can be small, visually similar, and densely arranged(Hong et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025; Park et al., 2025)。 现有的训练后方法对这种坐标敏感任务提供的监督有限. Existing post-training methods provide limited supervision for this coordinate-sensitive task(Park et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025; Tang et al., 2025)。 SFT 简单且稳定，但它将带注释的坐标视为硬目标，除了最终答案之外提供的信息很少. SFT is simple and stable, but it treats the annotated coordinate as a hard target, providing little information beyond the final answer(Park et al., 2025)。 它不会利用教师对合理坐标标记的不确定性或其他“黑暗知识”. It does not exploit the teacher’s uncertainty or other “dark knowledge” over plausible coordinate tokens(Hinton et al., 2015)，限制了细粒度本地化监管的丰富性。 GRPO 等强化学习方法可以优化任务结果，但它们需要多次部署并依赖稀疏奖励, limiting the richness of supervision for fine-grained localization. Reinforcement learning methods such as GRPO optimize task outcomes, but they require multiple rollouts and rely on sparse rewards(Shao et al., 2024; Zhao et al., 2026a)。 这种只注重结果的监管成本高昂，并且对细粒度本地化的指导作用薄弱. Such outcome-only supervision is costly and provides weak guidance for fine-grained localization(Tang et al., 2025; Park et al., 2025).

按策略自蒸馏 (OPSD) 是 GRPO 和 SFT 的一个有前途的替代方案。 通过沿着学生生成的轨迹对教师分布进行训练，OPSD 提供了密集的令牌级教师信号，而不需要 GRPO 式方法使用的大量推出On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) is a promising alternative to both GRPO and SFT. By training on teacher distributions along student-generated trajectories, OPSD provides dense token-level teacher signals without requiring the large number of rollouts used by GRPO-style methods(Zhao et al., 2026a)。 原则上，这种软监督可以比硬标签 SFT 携带更丰富的信息，包括合理位置之间的偏好. In principle, such soft supervision can carry richer information than hard-label SFT, including preferences among plausible locations(Hinton et al., 2015; Zhao et al., 2026a)。 然而，OPSD 的简单实例化不太适合 GUI 基础。 OPSD 的有效性主要取决于教师信号的质量。 OPSD 向老师询问学生生成的前缀. However, a naive instantiation of OPSD is not well suited to GUI grounding. The effectiveness of OPSD depends critically on the quality of teacher signals. OPSD queries the teacher on prefixes generated by the student(Zhao et al., 2026a)。 由于 GUI 坐标是自回归生成的，因此错误的学生前缀可能已经编码了错误的空间假设。 以这样的前缀为条件，教师的后续逻辑可能会成为错误坐标的合理延续，而不是教师向真实目标发出的有用信号。 因此，直接将 OPSD 应用于 GUI 接地可能会导致坐标令牌教师信号不可靠。. Since GUI coordinates are produced autoregressively, an incorrect student prefix can already encode a wrong spatial hypothesis. Conditioned on such a prefix, the teacher’s subsequent logits may become a plausible continuation of the wrong coordinate rather than a useful teacher signal toward the true target. Thus, directly applying OPSD to GUI grounding can lead to unreliable coordinate-token teacher signals.

Specifically, soft correctness-aware gating exploits a special property of GUI grounding: coordinate predictions are spatially verifiable against the ground-truth bounding box(Cheng et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024b; Tang et al., 2025)。 在当前学生生成的前缀下，如果教师当前的坐标标记预测仍然可以落入地面实况边界框内，则我们认为坐标标记教师信号是可靠的；如果它不能再落入框内，则我们认为坐标标记教师信号不可靠。 软正确性感知门控将完整的门值分配给可靠的信号，并降低不可靠信号的权重，而不是丢弃它们。 此外，教师概率缩放使用顶部坐标标记预测的教师概率作为轻量级缩放因子来进一步细化门控教师信号，为较高概率的教师信号分配较大的蒸馏权重并软化较低概率的教师信号。 这种组合使得坐标令牌监督既具有正确性感知又具有确定性感知，保留有用的分布信息，同时减少不可靠的教师信号的负面影响。 本文的贡献如下：. Under the current student-generated prefix, we regard a coordinate-token teacher signal as reliable if the teacher’s current coordinate-token prediction can still fall inside the ground-truth bounding box, and as unreliable if it can no longer fall inside the box. Soft correctness-aware gating assigns the full gate value to reliable signals and down-weights unreliable ones rather than discarding them. In addition, teacher-probability scaling uses the teacher probability of the top coordinate-token prediction as a lightweight scaling factor to further refine the gated teacher signal, assigning larger distillation weights to higher-probability teacher signals and softening lower-probability ones. This combination makes coordinate-token supervision both correctness-aware and certainty-aware, preserving useful distributional information while reducing the negative impact of unreliable teacher signals. 2 相关工作Related Work

GUI 接地。GUI Grounding.

GUI 基础需要视觉语言模型来本地化目标界面元素，并在给定屏幕截图和自然语言指令的情况下输出精确的屏幕坐标。 最近的工作通过 GUI 特定数据的后期培训改进了 GUI 基础。 监督微调方法使用带注释的指令坐标对训练模型，如 SeeClick、CogAgent、UGround、OS-Atlas 和 RVLMGUI grounding requires vision-language models to localize target interface elements and output precise screen coordinates given a screenshot and a natural-language instruction. Recent work has improved GUI grounding through post-training on GUI-specific data. Supervised fine-tuning methods train models with annotated instruction-coordinate pairs, as in SeeClick, CogAgent, UGround, OS-Atlas, and RVLM(Cheng et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Gou et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2024b; Park et al., 2025)。 最近基于强化学习的方法通过可验证的奖励信号进一步优化 GUI 基础. More recent reinforcement-learning-based methods further optimize GUI grounding with verifiable reward signals(Yang et al., 2026b)，包括GUI-G1和GUI-G2, including GUI-G1 and GUI-G2(Zhou et al., 2025; Tang et al., 2025)。 一些作品进一步为 GRPO 风格的 GUI 基础设计了距离感知或连续空间奖励，根据预测坐标与目标区域的接近程度提供更密集的反馈. Some works further design distance-aware or continuous spatial rewards for GRPO-style GUI grounding, providing denser feedback according to how close the predicted coordinate is to the target region(Shi et al., 2026; Zeng et al., 2026; Tang et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2026b).

自蒸馏和教师信号可靠性。Self-Distillation and Teacher-Signal Reliability.

OPSD 根据从其自己的策略采样的轨迹来训练学生，而教师模型则在相同的学生生成的前缀上提供令牌级教师信号OPSD trains the student on trajectories sampled from its own policy, while a teacher model provides token-level teacher signals on the same student-generated prefixes(Zhao et al., 2026a; Yuan et al., 2026)。 先前的按策略蒸馏 (OPD)/OPSD 分析指出，教师信号并非一致可靠. Prior On-Policy Distillation (OPD)/OPSD analyses point out that teacher signals are not uniformly reliable(Zhu et al., 2026; Zheng et al., 2026; Ke et al., 2026)。 特别是，当教师以学生生成的前缀为条件时，这些前缀可能不完美或分布不匹配，导致教师信号变得嘈杂或信息量较少. In particular, when the teacher is conditioned on student-generated prefixes, these prefixes may be imperfect or distributionally mismatched, causing the teacher signal to become noisy or less informative(Zhu et al., 2026; Zheng et al., 2026)。 其他工作进一步观察到，直接暴露完整的正确答案可能会使特权教师的信号过于尖锐或近乎确定性，从而削弱软蒸馏的好处. Other work further observes that directly exposing the full correct answer can make the privileged teacher signal overly sharp or near-deterministic, weakening the benefit of soft distillation(Tan and Hong, 2026; Zhang et al., 2026a).

提高教师信号可靠性的方法。Methods to Improve Teacher-Signal Reliability.

为了缓解这些问题，几种可靠性感知的 OPD/OPSD 方法通过基于代理的加权或特权教师输入来改善教师信号。 例如，基于熵的自蒸馏方法利用教师不确定性来调整令牌级别的更新权重To mitigate these problems, several reliability-aware OPD/OPSD methods improve teacher signals through proxy-based weighting or privileged teacher inputs. For example, entropy-based self-distillation methods use teacher uncertainty to adjust token-level update weights(Ke et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026a); 基于困惑的 OPD 方法减轻了看似不可靠的教师指导; perplexity-based OPD methods down-weight teacher guidance that appears unreliable(Zheng et al., 2026); 将自蒸馏与可验证反馈相结合的方法使用结果正确性来锚定更新方向或路由监督路径; and methods that combine self-distillation with verifiable feedback use outcome correctness to anchor update directions or route supervision paths(Yang et al., 2026a; Zheng et al., 2026)。 在视觉语言设置中，Vision-OPD 构建了一个裁剪条件教师，为全图像学生提供更集中的教师信号. In vision-language settings, Vision-OPD constructs a crop-conditioned teacher to provide more focused teacher signals for a full-image student(Yuan et al., 2026)。 GUI-SD 通过构建视觉丰富的特权教师输入和保留布局的区域掩码，进一步将这一想法适应 GUI 基础，同时使用坐标令牌加权和基于熵的缩放来改进 GUI 自蒸馏. GUI-SD further adapts this idea to GUI grounding by constructing a visually enriched privileged teacher input with layout-preserving regional masking, while using coordinate-token weighting and entropy-based scaling to improve GUI self-distillation(Zhang et al., 2026a)。 这些作品确立了一个重要的观点：天真的自我蒸馏不应将所有教师信号视为同样值得信赖。 然而，现有标准仍然主要依靠间接代理来提高教师信号的质量。 平均而言，熵、教师概率或困惑度等间接代理可能与信号可靠性相关，但它们不能直接保证所选或强调的信号实际上是可靠的。. These works establish an important point: naive self-distillation should not treat all teacher signals as equally trustworthy. However, existing criteria still mainly rely on indirect proxies to improve teacher signals’ quality. Indirect proxies such as entropy, teacher probability, or perplexity may correlate with signal reliability on average, but they do not provide a direct guarantee that the selected or emphasized signals are actually reliable.

GUI 基础提供了一个自然的机会来弥补这一差距，因为坐标预测在空间上是可验证的。 我们的工作利用了这种结构，并使用真实框作为坐标标记教师信号的直接训练时间可靠性标准。 我们的方法不是盲目模仿不可靠的教师信号，而是在蒸馏之前轻柔地降低权重并进一步缩放其损失贡献。GUI grounding provides a natural opportunity to bridge this gap, because coordinate predictions are spatially verifiable. Our work leverages this structure and uses the ground-truth box as a direct training-time reliability criterion for coordinate-token teacher signals. Instead of blindly imitating unreliable teacher signals, our method softly down-weights and further scales their loss contribution before distillation.

5 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

GUI 基础提供了一个具体的设置，用于检查当可以明确验证教师信号的质量时，应如何在策略蒸馏中使用教师信号。与一般的令牌预测任务不同，GUI 接地具有空间可检查的结构：在给定的解码前缀下，可以通过是否仍然有可能将其完成到地面实况目标区域来测试坐标令牌预测。这使得教师监督不仅可以通过置信度或不确定性等间接指标进行评估，还可以通过其与目标约束的兼容性进行评估。GUI grounding provides a concrete setting for examining how teacher signals should be used in on-policy distillation when their quality can be explicitly verified. Unlike general token prediction tasks, GUI grounding has a spatially checkable structure: under a given decoding prefix, a coordinate-token prediction can be tested by whether it remains possible to complete it into the ground-truth target region. This allows teacher supervision to be assessed not only through indirect proxies such as confidence or uncertainty, but also through its compatibility with the target constraint.

根据这种观点，不可靠的教师信号不应以纯粹的二元方式处理。与地面实况区域不兼容的信号不应像兼容的信号那样强烈地模仿，因为这样做可能会强化不正确的空间预测。然而，这样的信号并不一定缺乏有用的信息；它们可能仍然反映了教师学到的当地偏好或分配结构。因此，我们的研究探索了一种在可验证可靠性下使用教师监督的软方法：使用目标兼容性来调整对教师信号的信任，而概率校准控制监督的强度。这提供了一个初步的尝试，使在政策蒸馏在 GUI 基础中更加具有可靠性意识，其中不完美的教师信号被削弱和重塑，而不是简单地丢弃。Under this view, unreliable teacher signals should not be treated in a purely binary manner. A signal that is incompatible with the ground-truth region should not be imitated as strongly as a compatible one, since doing so may reinforce incorrect spatial predictions. However, such signals are not necessarily devoid of useful information; they may still reflect local preferences or distributional structure learned by the teacher. Therefore, our study explores a soft way of using teacher supervision under verifiable reliability: target compatibility is used to adjust the trust placed in the teacher signal, while probability calibration controls the strength of the supervision. This provides an initial attempt to make on-policy distillation more reliability-aware in GUI grounding, where imperfect teacher signals are weakened and reshaped rather than simply discarded.

我们的方法也有局限性。首先，正确性感知门在训练过程中依赖于真实边界框，因此当空间注释可用时它最直接适用。其次，当前的可靠性标准是为 GUI 基础中的坐标标记预测而设计的。将相同的想法扩展到没有明确空间坐标的任务可能需要不同形式的可验证的教师信号评估。未来的工作还可以研究类似的可靠性感知自蒸馏策略是否可以跨模型规模和其他基于视觉的代理任务转移。Our method also has limitations. First, the correctness-aware gate relies on ground-truth bounding boxes during training, so it is most directly applicable when spatial annotations are available. Second, the current reliability criterion is designed for coordinate-token prediction in GUI grounding. Extending the same idea to tasks without explicit spatial coordinates may require different forms of verifiable teacher-signal assessment. Future work could also study whether similar reliability-aware self-distillation strategies transfer across model scales and other visually grounded agent tasks.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了用于 GUI 基础的质量感知自蒸馏，旨在提高策略自蒸馏中坐标令牌教师信号的可靠性。我们的方法使用软正确性感知门控来降低与学生生成的前缀下的目标区域不兼容的教师预测的权重，并进一步应用教师概率缩放来细化坐标令牌监督的强度。对六个 GUI 基础基准的实验表明，所提出的方法持续改进了基础模型，并且优于强大的训练后基线，包括 SFT、GRPO、naive OPSD 和 GUI-SD。这些结果强调空间可验证性是提高 GUI 接地自蒸馏中教师信号可靠性的有效信号。We presented quality-aware self-distillation for GUI grounding, aiming to improve the reliability of coordinate-token teacher signals in on-policy self-distillation. Our method uses soft correctness-aware gating to down-weight teacher predictions that are incompatible with the target region under the student-generated prefix, and further applies teacher-probability scaling to refine the strength of coordinate-token supervision. Experiments on six GUI grounding benchmarks show that the proposed method consistently improves the base model and outperforms strong post-training baselines, including SFT, GRPO, naive OPSD, and GUI-SD. These results highlight spatial verifiability as an effective signal for improving teacher-signal reliability in GUI grounding self-distillation.
