It is important to be able to explain the decisions of an AI system to ensure trust on the system. Explainable AI (XAI) algorithms play a vital role in achieving this objective. The proposed algorithm is based on a graph-theoretic formulation of the feature set termed as Feature Association Map (FAM). The foundation of the modelling is based on association between features. This definitely shows that FAMeX is a promising algorithm in explaining the predictions from an AI system.

1 简介Introduction

在过去的几十年里，基于人工智能（AI）的系统已被应用于日常生活的多个方面。无论是天气预报、股票价格预测等常见应用，还是通过医学图像早期检测疾病、分析文本情绪等更高级的应用，人工智能系统的作用都相当突出。正如我们所看到的，人工智能系统的主要应用是事件预测，为此采用了某些复杂的学习算法。这些复杂的学习算法可以提供卓越的、几乎接近准确的预测。For the last few decades, artificial intelligence (AI) based systems have been adopted in multiple facets of daily life. Whether we look at frequent applications like weather forecasting, prediction of stock prices, or we look at more advanced applications like early detection of diseases from medical images, analyse sentiment of texts, etc., the role of AI systems is quite predominant. Major applications of AI systems, as we can see, are in the prediction of events, for which certain complex learning algorithms are employed. These complex learning algorithms can give superior, almost close to accurate, predictions.

人工智能系统的成功也带来了一些深层次的挑战。当我们研究复杂的人工智能算法时，尽管包括复杂的学习算法，但有时很难理解算法如何做出决策。有时无法理解人工智能系统做出的决定是基于科学事实还是虚假巧合。当我们使用非关键系统（例如向用户推荐书籍、电影或音乐的系统）时，这很好。但当我们寻求人工智能的关键实施，例如疾病检测、预测天气或预测股票价格时，后果可能是致命的。因此，对于基于人工智能的关键决策系统，需要打破黑盒人工智能系统的不透明性，并且对系统所做决策的解释应该是显而易见的。为了培养人们内部的信任，必须保持可靠性、问责制等，以保持计算系统和人类之间的透明度[1]。The success of AI systems has also brought forward some deep-lying challenges. When we look at sophisticated AI algorithms, that albeit includes the complex learning algorithms, it is sometimes very difficult to understand how the algorithms are making decisions. It is sometimes impossible to understand whether the decision made by the AI system is based on scientific facts or spurious coincidences. This is fine when we work with non-critical systems like one which recommends books, movies or music to users. But when we go for critical implementations of AI like detection of diseases, forecasting weather, or predicting stock prices, the consequences might be fatal. So, for critical AI based decision systems, the opacity of the black-box AI system needs to be broken and an explanation of the decisions taken by the system should be evident. To foster trust within people, it is essential to conserve reliability, accountability, etc. to maintain transparency between computational systems and human beings [1].

为了使人工智能系统透明并解释为什么特定算法会做出特定决策，人工智能系统的可解释性概念已经发展。在过去的几年里，人工智能研究社区做了很多研究，重点是建立人工智能系统的透明度。可解释的人工智能（或通常称为 XAI）算法 [2,3,4] 旨在对特定人工智能系统如何做出决策提出某些解释或理解。近年来，XAI 算法达到了新的顶峰 [5, 6]，因为人工智能决策系统的传统预测需要用人类可理解的原理来补充。由于系统的预测性越高，其可解释性就越低，这一事实进一步变得必要[7]。To make an AI system transparent and to explain why a particular algorithm takes a specific decision, the concept of explainability of AI systems has evolved. In the last few years, a lot of research has been done by the AI research community which is focused on establishing transparency of AI systems. Explainable AI (or popularly termed as XAI) algorithms [2, 3, 4] are intended to come up with certain explanations or understanding about how a particular AI system is taking decisions. XAI algorithms have seen a new apex in recent years [5, 6] because conventional forecasting of AI decision-making systems need to be complimented with human-understandable rationale. This has been further necessitated by the fact that the more predictive a system is, the less is its interpretability [7].

总的来说，XAI 算法基于几个关键原理。第一个基于可感知的解释。这些算法试图利用人类基于感知的直觉来解释人工智能系统的决策。另一种方法是通过对系统进行数学建模来解释人工智能系统。一种流行的可解释性方法是基于特征重要性。在这种方法中，XAI 算法尝试以特征排名的形式表示特征重要性。研究人员提出了一些算法来得出人工智能系统决策背景下的特征重要性 [12, 13]。但他们还没有对这些特征的关键方面——相关性和冗余——进行全面分析。我们早期的作品 [27, 28] 中提出的基于图的特征集公式，称为特征关联图（FAM），已经能够成功地对特征的各个方面（即特征相关性和冗余）进行建模。我们设想 FAM 的相同公式也可用于解释特征重要性，以更全面的方式理解人工智能系统所做的预测。Broadly, XAI algorithms are based on few key philosophies. The first one is based on perceptible interpretation. These algorithms try to explain the decisions by AI systems using human perception-based intuitions. The other approach is to explain the AI systems by doing mathematical modelling of the system. A popular approach for explainability is based on feature importance. In this approach the XAI algorithm tries to represent feature importance in the form of ranking of features. There are a few algorithms proposed by the researchers to come up with the feature importance in context of decision-making of an AI system [12, 13]. But they have not done a holistic analysis of the critical aspects of the features - relevance and redundancy. A graph-based formulation of the feature set, termed as Feature Association Map (FAM), proposed in our earlier works [27, 28] has been able to successfully model the aspects of features namely feature relevance and redundancy. We envisage that the same formulation of FAM can also be used to interpret feature importance in context of understanding a prediction done by an AI system in a more holistic way.

我们在本文中提出的算法的新颖之处在于，它在相关性和冗余性的背景下考虑特征重要性。因此，它能够评估该特征在人工智能系统决策过程中贡献的重要性。 FAM 是特征集的可视化建模，在提出特征重要性时已被使用。The novelty of the algorithm that we have proposed in this paper is that it looks at the feature importance in context of both relevance and redundancy. Hence, it is able to evaluate the criticality of contribution of the feature in the decision-making process of the AI system. FAM, which is a visual modelling of the feature set, has been used while coming up with the feature importance.

2 相关作品Related Works

用户采用XAI技术来建立对AI解决方案的信任。 LIME [8] 使用局部模型来解释其预测，Deep LIFT [9] 借助网络神经元的反向传播来解释输出，部分依赖图 [10] 解释了模型的预测如何部分依赖于感兴趣的输入变量的值。XAI techniques are adopted by users to establish trust on AI solutions. LIME [8] uses local models to explain its prediction, Deep LIFT [9] explains the output with the help of backpropagation through the neurons of a network, Partial Dependence Plot [10] explains how the model’s prediction partially depends on values of the input variables of interest.

排列特征重要性（PFI）[11]计算排列（即删除特征）后模型预测误差的增加或减少。这给出了特征重要性的度量。在所有与模型无关的局部解释技术中，最流行的统一方法是以 SHapley Additive exPlanations 为代表的 SHAP [13,14,15,16]。它根据每个特征的边际贡献来估计其重要性。 SHAP 使用博弈论的概念来计算一个群体（联盟）中参与者各自的贡献。为了解释学习模型，它估计每个特征对模型决策的贡献。在一项工作[17]中，SHAP被用来解释和分析芝加哥都会高速公路事故发生所获得的特征。Permutation Feature Importance (PFI) [11] calculates the increase or decrease of the model’s prediction error after permuting i.e. removing a feature. This gives a measure of the feature importance. Among all the model agnostic local explanation techniques the most popular unified approach is SHAP typified for SHapley Additive exPlanations [13, 14, 15, 16]. It estimates the importance of each feature based on its marginal contribution. SHAP uses the concept of game theory that calculates the respective contribution of the players in a group (coalition). To interpret the learning models it estimates the contribution of each feature to a model’s decision. In a work [17], SHAP is used to interpret and analyze the features obtained by the occurrence of accidents in Chicago Metropolitan Expressways.

4 提议的方法Proposed Methodology

不透明的人工智能系统对输入输出关系的理解有限。我们试图通过我们提出的算法来解决这种缺乏可解释性的问题。所提出的基于特征关联图的可解释性算法（或 FAMeX）提出了基于特征重要性的系统输出解释。 FAMeX 算法的基础是对特征关联进行建模的图论概念，称为特征关联图（FAM）。如图 1 所示，FAM 是一个图，其特征建模为顶点，特征之间的关联表示为边。这种关联有两个方面——一是相关性，二是冗余性。相关性表示特定特征在监督学习问题的背景下有助​​于决定类别值的信息量。因此，某个特定功能提供的信息越多，它就越重要或相关。同样，多个特征可以在决定类别值方面提供信息。但某些功能可能会提供类似的信息。这些功能可以被视为潜在的冗余。在人工智能系统做出的决策的背景下，潜在的冗余特征可以被认为不太重要。The opaque AI systems furnish limited understanding about the input-output relationship. This lack of interpretability is attempted to be addressed with our proposed algorithm. The proposed algorithm Feature Association Map based eXplainability (or FAMeX) comes up with a feature importance based explanation of the system output. The foundation of the FAMeX algorithm is the graph-theoretic concept to model feature association, which is termed as Feature Association Map (FAM). FAM, as shown in Fig. 1, is a graph that has features modelled as vertices and the association between the features represented as edges. There are two facets of this association - one is relevance and the other is redundancy. Relevance signifies the amount of information a particular feature contributes to decide the class value in context of a supervised learning problem. So more information a particular feature contributes, the more significant or relevant it is. Again, multiple features can contribute information in terms of deciding the class value. But certain features might be contributing similar information. Those features can be regarded as potentially redundant. Potentially redundant features can be can be considered to be less important in context of the decision taken by an AI system.

为了计算每个特征的特征重要性得分，将相似性得分和相关性得分以这样的方式集成：如果相关性得分较高，则特征的得分较高，如果相似性得分较高，则特征的得分较低。因此，如等式 11 所示，相似性得分具有反比关系，而相关性得分与特征重要性得分具有直接关系。To calculate feature importance score for each feature, similarity score and the relevance score are integrated in such a way that the score of the feature is higher if the relevance score is higher and the score of the feature is lower if the similarity score is higher. So similarity score, as shown in equation 11, has an inverse relation whereas relevance score has a direct relation with the feature importance score.

F​e​a​t​u​r​e​I​m​p​o​r​t​a​n​c​e​S​c​o​r​e=R​e​l​e​v​a​n​c​e​S​c​o​r​eS​i​m​i​l​a​r​i​t​y​S​c​o​r​eFeature\;Importance\;Score=\frac{Relevance\;Score}{Similarity\;Score} (11)

因此，人工智能系统或学习模型的解释是基于特征的特征重要性得分得出的。顶级特征是最重要的特征，即具有最高的特征重要性得分。这些特征预计在决定特定类别值时最为重要。因此，最重要的特征是决定预测的特征，这些特征可以用来解释模型结果。Thus, an explanation of the AI system or the learning model is derived based on the feature importance score of the features. The top features are the ones that are the most important features i.e. having highest feature importance score. Those features are expected to be most important in deciding a particular class value. Thus the most important features are the features that decide on the prediction, and these are the features which can be used to explain the model outcome.

所提出的 FAMeX 算法的结果与可解释 AI 的两种基准算法（即 PFI 和 SHAP）进行了比较。这项比较研究可以更好地理解所提出的 FAMeX 算法与相同数据集的现有基准算法相比的表现。对竞争算法 PFI 和 SHAP 以及 FAMeX 均进行了 10 倍交叉验证。Results of the proposed FAMeX algorithm have been compared with two benchmark algorithms of Explainable AI, namely PFI and SHAP. This comparative study can provide a better understanding of how the proposed FAMeX algorithm performs in comparison to the existing benchmark algorithms with the same datasets. 10-fold cross-validation has been performed both for the competing algorithms PFI and SHAP as well as for FAMeX.

6.2 评估指标Evaluation metrics

为了评估所提出的 FAMeX 算法与竞争的 SHAP 和 PFI 算法相比的性能，考虑了基于特征重要性的排序。为了找到算法的相对功效，每个算法识别的最重要的特征已被用来导出数据集的子集。然后使用标准分类器计算分类精度。显然，能够根据特征重要性选择产生更高分类精度的特征集的 XAI 算法是性能最好的算法。我们还通过选择最不重要的特征来进行观察。对于给定的算法，期望由最重要的特征生成的数据子集比由最不重要的特征生成的数据子集具有显着更高的分类精度。很少有鲁棒的分类器，即支持向量机 (SVM) [18, 19]、决策树 (DT) [20, 21]、随机森林 (RF) [22] 和朴素贝叶斯 (NB) [23, 24] 用于证明算法的性能并检查应用于数据集时结果的准确性。所有实验均已重复 100 次迭代。To evaluate the performance of the proposed FAMeX algorithm compared to the competing SHAP and PFI algorithms, feature importance based ranking has been considered. To find the relative efficacy of the algorithms, most important features identified by each algorithm have been used to derive a subset of the dataset. Then classification accuracy has been calculated using a standard classifier. It is obvious that the XAI algorithm which is able to select the set of features generating higher classification accuracy based on feature importance, is the best performing algorithm. We have also taken a view by selecting the least important feature. For a given algorithm, it is expected that the data subset generated by most important features should have a significantly higher classification accuracy than the data subset generated by least important features. Few of the robust classifiers, namely Support Vector Machine (SVM) [18, 19], Decision Tree (DT) [20, 21], Random Forest (RF) [22] and Naive Bayes (NB) [23, 24] are used to justify the performance of the algorithms and check how accurate the outcomes are when applied on the datasets. All the experiments have been repeated for 100 iterations.

对于本文提出的实验，提取并分类了前 30% 的 FAMeX 特征和后 30% 的 FAMeX 特征。如前所述，预计前 30% 的特征的分类精度将明显优于后 30% 的特征。这将表明算法的可信度。这也已应用于每个竞争的 XAI 算法。For the experiments presented in this paper, the top 30% of the FAMeX features and bottom 30% of the FAMeX features are extracted and classified. As already stated, it is expected that the classification accuracy for the top 30% of the features will be significantly better than the bottom 30% of the features. This will indicate the trustworthiness of the algorithm. This has been applied on each of the competing XAI algorithms too.

6.4 基于图形用户界面的工具GUI based tool

我们开发了基于图形用户界面 (GUI) 的工具，该工具集成了 FAMeX 算法的实现。该工具允许用户浏览数据集。该工具生成所选数据集特征的相似性得分、相关性得分和特征重要性得分，以及 4 个标准分类器（SVM、RF、NB 和 DT）的分类准确性。顶部或底部特征的百分比可以作为用户输入。该工具与平台无关。该工具将有助于理解预测背景下特定数据集的重要特征。该原型工具已上传至Github存储库A Graphical User Interface (GUI) based tool has been developed which integrates the implemention of the FAMeX algorithm. The tool allows the user to browse a dataset. The tool generates the similarity score, relevance score and feature importance score of the features of the dataset selected along with the classification accuracy for the 4 standard classifiers - SVM, RF, NB and DT. The percentage of top or bottom features can be taken as user input. This tool is platform-independent. This tool will assist in understanding the important features of a particular dataset in context of a prediction. 7 结论Conclusion

在本文中，提出了一种新算法 FAMeX，用于解释人工智能系统最重要的特征。该算法已经过验证，可以为前 30% 的特征生成更高的准确度，而为后 30% 的特征生成相对较低的准确度。它还被发现优于两种竞争的 XAI 算法 - PFI 和 SHAP。实验结果足以证明 FAMeX 的功效优于 PFI 和 SHAP 算法。作为未来的工作，FAMeX 算法可以应用于更复杂和大型的数据集，并与更先进的 XAI 算法进行比较。In this paper, a new algorithm FAMeX has been proposed for explaining AI systems in terms of most important features. The algorithm has been validated to generate greater accuracy for the top 30% features and comparatively lesser for the bottom 30% features. It is also found to outperform two competing XAI algorithms - PFI and SHAP. The experimental results are promising enough to establish the efficacy of FAMeX ahead of PFI and SHAP algorithms. As a future work, the FAMeX algorithm can be applied on more complex and large datasets and compared with more state-of-the-art XAI algorithms.
