摘要。Abstract.目前，事件响应由安全操作员使用预定义的剧本进行管理，导致安全决策过程缓慢且耗费大量人力。因此，对自动化事件响应计划的需求日益增长。基于控制、优化和强化学习的决策理论方法已经被提出，可以通过扎实的方法来自动化此类规划任务，但其中大多数在保证强大性能的同时，仅限于抽象模型，不能直接应用于操作系统。缓解这一限制的一种有前景的方法是使用嵌入大型语言模型 (LLM) 中的安全知识来开发代理响应系统。然而，当前的代理方法依赖于重复调用法学硕士来生成响应计划，这是不可靠的，并且由于幻觉而限制了计划范围。在本文中，我们通过将决策理论规划与 LLM 生成的响应命令相结合，开发了一种原则性的基于 LLM 的规划方法。所提出的代理事件响应方法使用推出规划器来计算分配安全资源（战术规模）的高级响应策略，然后由轻量级 LLM 代理将其转换为可执行命令（操作规模）。在此架构中，我们使用数字孪生，通过模拟支持战术规划，并通过仿真支持运营执行。在三种攻击场景中，我们的代理方法比前沿 LLM 基线平均减少了 15.1% 的恢复执行时间，并将恢复率提高了 33.6%。Incident response is currently managed by security operators using predefined playbooks, resulting in slow, labor-intensive security decision-making processes. Consequently, there is a growing need for automated incident response planning. Decision-theoretic approaches based on control, optimization, and reinforcement learning have been proposed to automate such planning tasks with well-grounded approaches, yet most of which, while guaranteeing strong performance, are limited to abstract models and cannot be directly applied to operational systems. A promising approach to mitigate this limitation is to use the security knowledge embedded in large language models (LLMs) to develop agentic response systems. However, current agentic approaches rely on repeated invocations of the LLM to generate a response plan, which is unreliable and limits the planning horizon due to hallucination. In this paper, we develop a principled LLM-based planning method by combining decision-theoretic planning with LLM-generated response commands. The proposed agentic incident response approach uses a rollout planner to compute a high-level response strategy that allocates security resources (the tactical scale), which is then translated into executable commands by a lightweight LLM agent (the operational scale). Within this architecture, we use a digital twin that supports tactical planning through simulation and operational execution through emulation. Across three attack scenarios, our agentic approach reduces recovery execution time by 15.1% on average and increases the recovery rate by 33.6% over frontier LLM baselines.

1 简介1 Introduction

事件响应是指为遏制、减轻网络攻击并从中恢复而采取的协调行动。如今，事件响应主要是由安全操作员执行的手动过程。尽管这种方法很有效，但通常很慢并且需要专门的技能。例如，IBM 最近的一份报告表明，60% 的受访组织需要 100 多天的时间才能响应网络系统中的安全事件并从中恢复[24]。Incident response refers to the coordinated actions taken to contain, mitigate, and recover from cyberattacks. Today, incident response is largely a manual process carried out by security operators. Though this approach can be effective, it is often slow and requires specialized skills. For instance, a recent report by IBM indicates that 60% of the surveyed organizations take more than 100 days to respond and recover from security incidents in networked systems [24].

决策理论事件响应。Decision-theoretic incident response.由于事件响应可以被视为一个连续的决策过程，因此人们一直在努力将控制、优化以及最近的强化学习 (RL) 方法应用于事件响应。一般的方法可以追溯到[26]，首先将响应过程建模为离散时间控制系统，其中系统状态封装了网络安全态势，控制操作是安全响应；例子包括马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）[49]和马尔可夫博弈[2,32]等。然后，最优响应计划对应于顺序决策模型的最优策略。Since incident response can be viewed as a sequential decision-making process, there have been considerable and ongoing efforts to apply control, optimization, and, most recently, reinforcement learning (RL) methods to incident response. The general recipe, which can be traced back to [26], is to first model the response process as a discrete-time control system, where the system state encapsulates the network security posture and control actions are security responses; examples include the Markov decision process (MDP) [49] and the Markov game [2,32], among others. Then, the optimal response plan corresponds to the optimal policy of the sequential decision-making models.

最近的发展[23,41]积极致力于鲁棒控制和最优控制，以在离线设计中实现最优响应规划，而在线学习[21,20]、元学习[15]和多智能体学习[29,34]最近也被探索用于在线自适应规划。与控制理论响应规划相关，当网络系统的封闭式建模未实现时，强化学习已成为数据驱动最优控制的一种有前途的范例。Recent developments [23,41] have actively engaged with robust control and optimal control to achieve optimal response planning in the offline design, while online learning [21,20], meta learning [15] and multi-agent learning [29,34] have also been explored recently for online adaptive planning. 基于法学硕士的事件响应方法。LLM-based approaches for incident response.解决决策理论方法缺点的一种有前途的方法是使用大型语言模型（LLM）从系统日志自动生成有效的响应操作。这种方法不限于一组预定义的操作，并且不需要模拟器。这个方向的早期研究包括[42,37,36,50]。这些方法可以分为两类：基于提示的 LLM 编排和 LLM-RL 混合方法。A promising approach to address the drawbacks of decision-theoretic approaches is to use large language models (LLMs) to automatically generate effective response actions from system logs. 第一类将事件响应分解为几个子任务，并为法学硕士开发定制提示来处理每个任务[42,37]。虽然这些工作报告了令人鼓舞的结果，但它们具有三个关键局限性：它们没有利用有原则的规划技术，它们依赖于广泛的即时调整，并且大多数需要将事件数据上传到外部法学硕士提供商。The first category decomposes incident response into several subtasks and develops tailored prompts for LLMs for tackling each task [42,37]. While these works report encouraging results, they have three key limitations: they do not leverage principled planning techniques, they rely on extensive prompt tuning, and most require uploading incident data to external LLM providers.

第二类通过结合 RL 和 LLM 代理来解决其中一些限制，其中 RL 代理监督 LLM 生成 [52]，LLM 代理通过知识共享 [38] 增强 RL 代理，并且两个代理相互通信 [6]。尽管代理交互的性质不同，但这些工作需要在模拟环境中进行额外的 RL 训练和 LLM-RL 联合操作，从而在模拟和实际实现之间留下了差距。与我们的工作最相关的是最近一系列关于将决策理论规划与法学硕士生成相结合的工作[19,13]。然而，这些方法使用规划方法，例如推出和蒙特卡罗树搜索，来指导法学硕士生成的操作响应行动。The second category addresses some of these limitations by combining RL and LLM agents, where RL agents supervise the LLM generation [52], LLM agents augment RL agents through knowledge sharing [38], and two agents communicate with each other [6]. Despite the different nature of agentic interactions, these works require additional RL training and LLM-RL joint operations in a simulated environment, leaving a gap between simulation and practical implementation. Most relevant to our work is a line of recent work on integrating decision-theoretic planning with LLM generation [19,13]. However, these approaches use planning methods, e.g., rollout and Monte Carlo tree search, to guide LLM-generated operational response actions.

网络安全中的数字孪生。Digital twins in cybersecurity.数字孪生 (DT) 的概念起源于制造、航空和物理人工智能领域 [16,30]，其中数字副本与物理过程并行运行，提供实时态势感知 [16,28]。在网络安全的背景下，DT 提供两种主要功能：仿真和模拟；参见例如[9,12,1,10,45,46,11]。仿真旨在在虚拟副本中重现目标系统的功能和计时行为。这种虚拟副本可用于在现实世界取证发生之前进行数字取证 [9] 以及可视化漏洞 [12]。更重要的是，它为虚拟操作提供了一个受控环境，其结果可用于优化目标系统中的操作[46]，例如事件管理[10]和事件预测[45]。相比之下，模拟是一种轻量级抽象，它对选定的系统方面进行建模以探索安全场景，而无需再现完整目标系统的行为[18]。The concept of digital twins (DTs) originated in the manufacturing, aviation, and physical AI sectors [16,30], where a digital replica runs in parallel with the physical process, offering real-time situational awareness [16,28]. In the context of cybersecurity, DTs provide two main kinds of functionalities: emulation and simulation; see e.g., [9,12,1,10,45,46,11]. Emulation aims to reproduce the target system’s functions and timing behavior in a virtual replica. Such a virtual replica can be used for digital forensics before real-world forensics takes place [9] and for visualizing vulnerabilities [12]. More importantly, it provides a controlled environment for virtual operations, with outcomes that can be used to optimize operations in the target system [46], such as event management [10] and incident prediction [45]. In contrast, simulation is a lightweight abstraction that models selected system aspects to explore security scenarios without reproducing the behavior of the full target system [18].

事件响应计划。Incident Response Planning.事件响应涉及选择一系列操作，以在网络攻击后将网络系统恢复到安全的运行状态。这些行动应该分析攻击的范围，保护取证证据，遏制并驱逐攻击者，强化系统以防止再次发生，并恢复关键服务。响应操作的示例包括重定向网络流、更新访问控制策略、修补漏洞、关闭受感染的系统以及重新启动运营服务。Incident response involves selecting a sequence of actions to restore a networked system to a secure, operational state after a cyberattack. These actions should analyze the scope of the attack, secure forensic evidence, contain and evict the attacker, harden the system to prevent recurrence, and restore critical services. Examples of response actions include redirecting network flows, updating access control policies, patching vulnerabilities, shutting down compromised systems, and restarting operational services.

防御者的目标是找到一系列行动，驱动受影响的系统尽快通过这些响应阶段，同时最大限度地降低运营成本。选择此类操作的一个关键挑战是，有关攻击的可用信息通常仅限于部分危害指标，例如 IDS 警报。而且，攻击者的策略通常是未知的。The goal of the defender is to find a sequence of actions that drives the affected system through these response stages as quickly as possible while minimizing operational costs. A key challenge in selecting such actions is that the available information about the attack is often limited to partial indicators of compromise, e.g., IDS alerts. Moreover, the attacker’s tactics are generally unknown.

6.4 讨论6.4 Discussion

实验结果表明，我们的代理多尺度响应计划方法在三种攻击场景中始终优于前沿 LLM 和基于 LLM 的事件响应的先前工作。与前沿法学硕士的比较表明，由决策理论规划支持的定制轻量级模型为事件响应提供了足够的代理方法。我们将此结果归因于基于数字孪生的验证，这导致我们的评估中恢复率更高。此外，与之前基于 LLM 的事件响应方法相比，我们方法中的战术规模规划通过战略性地选择恢复顺序来提高性能。The experimental results demonstrate that our method for agentic multiscale response planning consistently outperforms the frontier LLMs and prior works on LLM-based incident response across three attack scenarios. The comparisons with frontier LLMs suggest that a tailored, lightweight model powered by decision-theoretic planning provides a sufficient agentic approach for incident response. We attribute this result to the digital twin-based verification, which leads to higher recovery rates in our evaluation. Moreover, compared with prior LLM-based incident response approaches, the tactical-scale planning in our method improves performance by strategically selecting the recovery order.局限性。Limitations.我们方法的一个局限性是事件评估期间攻击策略的错误指定。如果推断的攻击猜想偏离真实的战术，攻击图就会变得不准确，战术规模的规划就会变得不那么有效。虽然之前的工作已经开始解决安全设置中的错误指定问题 [21,20]，但当 LLM（即黑盒生成模型）用作推理模型时，这一挑战仍未得到探索。我们方法的另一个限制是基于法学硕士的运营规划推出的成本。由于再生和数字孪生重新初始化，失败的验证会增加额外的成本。通过并行法学硕士课程和数字孪生仿真可以部分缓解这一问题。One limitation of our method is the misspecification of attack tactics during incident assessment. If the inferred attack conjecture deviates from the true tactics, the attack graph becomes inaccurate, and tactical-scale planning becomes less effective. While prior work has begun to address misspecification in security settings [21,20], this challenge remains unexplored when an LLM (i.e., a black-box generative model) serves as the inference model. Another limitation of our method is the cost of LLM-based rollout in operational planning. Failed verification adds extra cost due to regeneration and digital twin reinitialization. This can be partially mitigated through parallel LLM sessions and digital twin emulations.

与响应剧本的连接。Connections to response playbooks.我们的代理响应计划方法与事件响应手册[4]的作用类似，但侧重于生成可执行的操作操作。与传统的剧本相比，它有两个优点。首先，它产生针对感兴趣的系统量身定制的具体且针对具体情况的行动。其次，它通过多尺度规划对受损节点进行优先级排序，充当动态剧本。Our agentic response planning method serves a similar role to incident response playbooks [4], but focuses on generating executable operational actions. Compared with conventional playbooks, it offers two advantages. First, it produces concrete and context-specific actions tailored to the system of interest. Second, it acts as a dynamic playbook by prioritizing compromised nodes through multiscale planning.

7 结论7 Conclusion

我们提出了一种代理方法，集成了决策理论规划、大语言模型 (LLM) 和用于事件响应规划的数字孪生。所提出的方法使用基于数字孪生仿真的推出规划器来计算在战术规模上分配安全资源的高级响应策略。轻量级法学硕士在公共事件数据集上进行了微调，将抽象策略转换为由运营规模的数字孪生仿真验证的可执行命令。我们根据文献中报告的日志和测试台上的三种不同的攻击场景评估代理响应计划方法。在三种场景中，我们的代理方法保持了 90% 左右的恢复率，平均将恢复操作执行时间减少了 15.1%，并将恢复率比前沿 LLM 提高了 33.6%。We present an agentic approach that integrates decision-theoretic planning, large language models (LLMs), and digital twins for incident response planning. The proposed method uses a rollout planner based on digital twin simulation to compute a high-level response strategy that allocates security resources at the tactical scale. A lightweight LLM, fine-tuned over public incident datasets, translates the abstract strategies into executable commands verified by the digital twin emulation at the operational scale. We evaluate the agentic response planning approach on logs reported in the literature and three distinct attack scenarios on a testbed. Across three scenarios, our agentic approach maintains a recovery rate of around 90%, reduces recovery-action execution time by 15.1% on average, and increases the recovery rate by 33.6% over the frontier LLMs.

未来工作的主要方向是解决基于法学硕士的事件评估中的攻击策略错误指定，这在战术规划中发挥着重要作用。关键是利用法学硕士的情境学习能力，通过将观察到的警报与错误指定策略下的预测警报进行比较，来校准他们对网络中潜在策略的推断。A primary direction for future work is to address the attack tactics misspecification in the LLM-based incident assessment, which plays an instrumental role in tactical planning. The key is to leverage LLMs’ in-context learning ability to calibrate their inference about potential tactics in the network by comparing observed alerts with predicted alerts under misspecified tactics.
