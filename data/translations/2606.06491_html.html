摘要Abstract

1 简介Introduction

视觉-语言-动作模型（VLA）已成为通用机器人操纵的主流范例Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) have emerged as a mainstream paradigm for general-purpose robot manipulation[7, 26, 4, 39, 50, 29]。 通过在机器人演示中训练大型视觉语言骨干，VLA 遵循语言指令并在从机器人手臂到四足动物和类人机器人的各种具体平台上执行操作。. By training large vision-language backbones on robot demonstrations, VLAs follow language instructions and act across diverse embodied platforms, from robot arms to quadrupeds and humanoids.

部署这些政策的一个核心但不受控制的维度是执行速度。 真正的操纵在应该快速运行的低风险运输阶段和应该放慢速度以获得精确度的高风险接触阶段之间交替。 然而，今天的 VLA 默默地从其训练演示中继承了单一的固定执行速度。 改变这种速度的现有努力位于推理或控制器端，通过模型压缩、KV 缓存重用、异步操作分块或强化学习 (RL) 部署来加速策略A core but under-controlled dimension in deploying these policies is the execution speed. Real manipulation alternates between low-risk transit phases that should run fast and high-risk contact phases that should slow down for precision. Yet today’s VLAs silently inherit a single fixed execution speed from their training demonstrations. Existing efforts to alter this speed sit at the inference or controller side, accelerating policies through model compression, KV-cache reuse, asynchronous action chunking, or Reinforcement-Learning (RL) rollouts[52, 46, 55, 40, 25, 5, 30, 57, 31, 54, 47, 38, 53]。 然而，这些方法只是将策略从一种固定速度转变为另一种固定速度，而不是提供明确的按需速度控制。 他们还专门关注加速，而减速对于精确插入、脆弱的切换和其他接触丰富的行为仍然至关重要，却很少受到关注。 因此，公开的挑战是为单个 VLA 提供显式的双向速度控制，而无需从头开始重新训练其基础架构。. However, these methods merely shift the policy from one fixed speed to another rather than offering explicit, on-demand speed control. They also focus exclusively on acceleration, while deceleration, which remains essential for precision insertion, fragile handover, and other contact-rich behaviors, receives little attention. The open challenge is therefore to give a single VLA explicit, bidirectional speed control without retraining its base architecture from scratch.

G.1 与以控制者为中心的作品的关系Relationship with Controller-focused Works

改变机器人执行速度的一个自然替代方案是对低级控制器进行操作，例如在策略产生输出后缩放目标速度或延长步长周期。 我们将此类控制器端方法视为与 TempoVLA 正交，而不是与之竞争：控制器位于策略的下游，只能重新调整或重新定时策略已经预测的内容，而变速训练会在策略级别本身更改这些预测的内容。 因此，这两种方法介入控制堆栈的不同层，并且可以直接组合，因此当需要更精细的执行端调整时，TempoVLA 策略仍然可以与任何控制器端调制配对。A natural alternative to changing the execution speed of a robot is to act on the low-level controller, for example by scaling target velocities or stretching step periods after the policy has produced its outputs. We view such controller-side methods as orthogonal to TempoVLA rather than competing with it: controllers sit downstream of the policy and can only rescale or retime what the policy has already predicted, while variable-speed training changes the content of those predictions at the policy level itself. The two approaches therefore intervene at different layers of the control stack and can be composed directly, so a TempoVLA policy can still be paired with any controller-side modulation when finer execution-side tuning is desired.

G.2 VSTA对EEF和联合行动空间影响的差异The difference between effect of VSTA on EEF and Joint Action Space

对于操纵任务，成功是由末端执行器和被操纵对象之间的相对姿态来定义的，因此末端执行器轨迹是我们在评估重新定时的演示如何忠实地保留原始任务语义时最终关心的量。 EEF 和联合命令都满足Section 的线性可组合性要求For manipulation tasks, success is defined by the relative pose between the end effector and the manipulated objects, so the end-effector trajectory is the quantity we ultimately care about when evaluating how faithfully a re-timed demonstration preserves the original task semantics. Both EEF and joint commands satisfy the linear-composability requirement of Section3.2，因此无论哪种情况，VSTA 的累加然后分割操作在数学上都是明确定义的。 因此，相关的问题不是VSTA在关节上是否有效，而是重新定时后所产生的末端执行器偏差有多大，并且三个结构原因使得这种偏差在关节空间中比在EEF空间中更大。, so VSTA’s accumulate-then-split operation is mathematically well-defined in either case. The relevant question is therefore not whether VSTA is valid on joints, but how large the resulting end-effector deviation becomes after re-timing, and three structural reasons make this deviation larger in joint space than in EEF space.

单关节处的几何放大。Geometric amplification at single joints.近端关节上的微小重新定时误差在末端执行器处会按照其杠杆臂的比例被几何放大，这是关节空间重新定时无法避免的结构性损失。A small re-timing error on a proximal joint is geometrically magnified at the end effector in proportion to its lever arm, a structural penalty that joint-space re-timing cannot avoid.

整个运动链的非线性耦合。Non-linear coupling across the kinematic chain.正向运动学通过非线性函数将关节角度映射到末端执行器姿势，因此线性插值的关节增量转换为不再线性插值的末端执行器运动，而直接在 EEF 平移和轴角度增量上重新计时使线性插值与定义任务成功的数量保持一致。Forward kinematics maps joint angles to end-effector pose through a non-linear function, so linearly interpolated joint increments translate into end-effector motions that no longer interpolate linearly, while re-timing directly on EEF translations and axis-angle increments keeps the linear interpolation aligned with the quantity that defines task success.

速度变化下控制器的可实现性。Controller realizability under speed change.对于现代手臂上常用的操作空间控制器，关节空间重定时将策略提交给特定的关节轨迹，而 EEF 空间重定时让控制器使用其逆运动学分辨率来找到在新速度下动态可行的关节轨迹，有助于吸收惯性、摩擦和扭矩饱和等影响。For operational-space controllers commonly used on modern arms, joint-space re-timing commits the policy to a specific joint trajectory, whereas EEF-space re-timing lets the controller use its inverse-kinematics resolution to find a joint trajectory dynamically feasible at the new speed, helping absorb effects such as inertia, friction, and torque saturation.

总而言之，这些原因解释了为什么当两种表示形式都可用时，我们更喜欢 VSTA 的 EEF 操作，尽管 VSTA 仍然可以直接应用在仅公开联合命令的平台上，而不会损害我们设置中的任务相关性能。Taken together, these reasons explain why we prefer EEF actions for VSTA when both representations are available, although VSTA can still be applied directly on platforms that expose only joint commands without harming task-relevant performance in our setup.
