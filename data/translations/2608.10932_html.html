1 简介Introduction

视频包含场景中的运动以及观察场景的摄像机的运动。镜头运动有自己的表达词汇和电影语法的悠久传统A video contains motion in the scene and motion of the camera observing it. Camera motion has its own expressive vocabulary and a long tradition in film grammar(Spottiswoode, 1959; Yilmaz et al., 2023)：平移可以改变观看者的视线，移动车可以通过视差显示深度，而变焦可以在相机保持静止时重新构图。识别这些运动需要将观察者引起的变化与场景中发生的变化分开。这种能力对于空间智能很重要: a pan redirects the viewer’s gaze, a dolly reveals depth through parallax, and a zoom reframes the shot while the camera remains stationary. Recognizing these movements requires separating changes caused by the observer from those occurring in the scene. This capability is important for spatial intelligence(Zhang et al., 2026)，可控视频生成, controllable video generation(Bai et al., 2025a; Xing et al., 2025)，以及电影分析, and cinematic analysis(Wang et al., 2026b)。尽管如此，大多数相机运动基准测试仍然将问题表述为整个剪辑上的单标签或多标签分类. Nevertheless, most camera-motion benchmarks still formulate the problem as single- or multi-label classification over an entire clip(Lin et al., 2026; Liu et al., 2026; Feng et al., 2026)。这种表述并不反映真实的镜头：即使在不间断的镜头中，摄像机也可能在运动之间转换或同时执行多个运动。因此，剪辑级标签会丢失时间结构和物理成分。. That formulation does not reflect real footage: even within an uninterrupted shot, the camera may transition between movements or execute several movements at once. Clip-level labels consequently lose both temporal structure and physical composition.

Given a video, the model partitions each shot into motion-consistent intervals and predicts the complete set of direction-aware movements active in each interval. 6 结论Conclusion

我们将相机运动作为一个基于时间的构图问题进行研究，其中模型必须在每次运动发生时进行本地化，并恢复镜头中同时发生的运动。建设We studied camera motion as a temporally grounded, compositional problem, in which a model must localize when each movement occurs and recover the movements that co-occur within a shot. Building theCamChoreo这项任务的基准表明，这种时间和成分结构是真实视频中的规则而不是例外，并且当前的 MLLM 在这方面举步维艰，因为它们的视觉编码器缺乏所需的几何基础。为了缩小这一差距，我们引入了benchmark for this task showed that such temporal and compositional structure is the rule rather than the exception in real video, and that current MLLMs struggle on it because their visual encoders lack the geometric grounding it requires.
