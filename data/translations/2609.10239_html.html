摘要Abstract

基于图的检索可以改进多跳问答，但现有方法通常会产生较高的查询时间成本，并产生分散的、超大的上下文，从而降低生成效率。我们提出了 LiteRAG，一种基于图的检索方法，用查询条件算法探索和推理链上下文构建取代了昂贵的检索时间 LLM 控制。在分布式系统论文多跳检索基准 DistComp 上，LiteRAG 在评估的方法中获得了最高的整体质量 (0.798)，同时将每次查询延迟减少了 100 以上Graph-based retrieval can improve multi-hop question answering, but existing approaches often incur high query-time costs and produce diffuse, oversized contexts that reduce generation efficiency. We present LiteRAG, a graph-based retrieval method that replaces expensive retrieval-time LLM control with query-conditioned algorithmic exploration and reasoning-chain context construction. On DistComp, a benchmark for multi-hop retrieval over distributed-systems papers, LiteRAG attains the highest overall quality among the evaluated methods (0.798) while reducing per-query latency by over 100×\times相对于 GraphRAG Global 和 DRIFT，成本降低了 99% 以上。在 UltraDomain 上，它在整体质量上与 LinearRAG 相匹配，同时使用了大约 14 个and cost by over 99% relative to GraphRAG Global and DRIFT. 1 简介Introduction

检索增强生成（RAG）已成为将大型语言模型（LLM）建立在外部知识基础上的标准方法Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has become a standard approach for grounding Large Language Models (LLMs) in external knowledgeLewis et al. (2020); Fan et al. (2024)。在最常见的形式中，RAG 通过密集相似度检索文本块，并将它们传递给生成器作为支持上下文。这种设计对于许多查找式查询是有效的，但对于需要跨文档组合证据、跟踪关系依赖性或通过多个跃点合成信息的问题仍然很薄弱。. In its most common form, RAG retrieves text chunks by dense similarity and passes them to the generator as supporting context. This design is effective for many lookup-style queries, but it remains weak on questions that require combining evidence across documents, tracing relational dependencies, or synthesizing information over multiple hops(Gao et al., 2023; Jiang et al., 2023b).

基于图的 RAG 方法通过将语料库表示为结构化图（其中实体、关系和社区被显式建模）来解决此限制Graph-based RAG methods address this limitation by representing corpora as structured graphs in which entities, relations, and communities are explicitly modeled(Edge et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025a; Zhuang et al., 2026)。然而，许多现有系统在查询时仍然严重依赖 LLM 来遍历图表、总结社区或跨检索区域聚合证据。这种依赖性增加了延迟和成本，随着语料库大小的扩展性很差，并且可能产生广泛的上下文，迫使生成器筛选大量不相关或弱相关的信息. However, many existing systems still rely heavily on the LLM at query time to traverse the graph, summarize communities, or aggregate evidence across retrieved regions. This dependence increases latency and cost, scales poorly with corpus size, and can produce broad contexts that force the generator to sift through substantial irrelevant or weakly relevant informationLiu et al. (2024).

因此，对于多跳问答，挑战不仅在于检索足够的证据，而且在于以保留高密度查询相关信息的形式呈现它。当检索返回松散过滤的文本或广泛的摘要时，生成器仍然必须推断实体如何连接。在现实的代币预算下，这可能会稀释有用的证据并降低结构化检索的价值。For multi-hop question answering, the challenge is therefore not only to retrieve enough evidence, but to present it in a form that preserves a high density of query-relevant information. When retrieval returns loosely filtered text or broad summaries, the generator must still deduce how entities connect. Under realistic token budgets, this can dilute useful evidence and reduce the value of structured retrieval.

因此，我们认为 RAG 的图检索应该优化证据选择以及如何为生成器准备检索的证据。我们引入了 LiteRAG，这是一种从检索循环中删除生成模型的方法。 LiteRAG 不依赖于顺序 LLM 调用进行导航，而是执行查询条件算法图探索：它首先选择语义和词汇锚节点，然后使用动态阈值和社区感知集线器惩罚来扩展有界子图。We therefore argue that graph retrieval for RAG should optimize both evidence selection and how retrieved evidence is prepared for the generator. We introduce LiteRAG, a method that removes the generation model from the retrieval loop. Rather than relying on sequential LLM calls for navigation, LiteRAG performs query-conditioned algorithmic graph exploration: it first selects semantic and lexical anchor nodes, then expands a bounded subgraph using dynamic thresholding and community-aware hub penalization.

最后阶段在上下文层面解决相同的目标。 LiteRAG 不是将大型文本集合或广泛的摘要传递给生成器，而是将检索到的子图转换为紧凑的推理链，直接在提示中表达保留的关系。这会产生一个更紧凑、以查询为中心的上下文，其中相关连接已经是明确的，从而保留了在有限的令牌预算下生成所需的结构。The final stage addresses the same objective at the context level. Rather than passing large text collections or broad summaries to the generator, LiteRAG converts the retrieved subgraph into compact reasoning chains that express the retained relations directly in the prompt. This yields a more compact, query-focused context in which relevant connections are already explicit, preserving the structure needed for generation under restricted token budgets.

Within this trend, graph-based methods explicitly encode entities and relations, enabling retrieval over relational structure rather than isolated text chunks.

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 LiteRAG，一种基于图的 RAG 检索方法，其建立在一个简单的前提上：对于多跳生成，检索不仅应该找到相关证据，还应该以紧凑、结构明确的形式将其传递给 LLM。 LiteRAG 通过用查询条件算法探索和推理链上下文构建取代 LLM 介导的导航来实现这一目标。特别是，该方法结合了词汇和社区感知的锚点选择、查询自适应阈值、社区感知的集线器惩罚以及最终生成之前的紧凑推理链构建。In this paper, we presented LiteRAG, a graph-based retrieval method for RAG built on a simple premise: for multi-hop generation, retrieval should not only find relevant evidence, but deliver it to the LLM in a compact, structurally explicit form. LiteRAG addresses this objective by replacing LLM-mediated navigation with query-conditioned algorithmic exploration and reasoning-chain context construction. In particular, the method combines lexical and community-aware anchor selection, query-adaptive thresholding, community-aware hub penalization, and compact reasoning-chain construction before final generation.

在报告的实验中，这种设计产生了有利的质量效率权衡。论分布式计算Across the reported experiments, this design yields a favorable quality-efficiency trade-off. On DistCompD1280D_{1280}, LiteRAG 达到报告的最高值, LiteRAG attains the highest reportedQtotalQ_{\mathrm{total}}在比较的方法中，同时还使用最少的令牌、最低的延迟和最低的每次查询成本。更广泛的扩展结果显示，在评估的语料库范围内，延迟成本概况相对平坦，固定预算分析表明，LiteRAG 的优势不仅仅是检索较少的上下文，而是以更高的有效信息密度检索上下文，并在较小的预算下保持有效。在 UltraDomain 上，LiteRAG 在总体质量方面保持竞争力，同时使用比最强比较器小得多的检索上下文和显着较低的执行成本。among the compared methods while also using the fewest tokens, the lowest latency, and the lowest per-query cost. The broader scaling results show a comparatively flat latency-cost profile over the evaluated corpus range, and the fixed-budget analysis suggests that LiteRAG’s advantage is not merely retrieving less context, but retrieving context with higher effective information density and remaining effective under a small budget. On UltraDomain, LiteRAG remains competitive in aggregate quality while using a substantially smaller retrieved context and markedly lower execution cost than the strongest comparator.

总的来说，这些发现支持了本文的三个主要贡献。比较结果确定了总体质量效率收益；固定预算和消融结果表明，推理链上下文构建有助于在紧张的代币预算下保持整体质量；消融研究进一步表明，词汇和社区意识锚点选择支持检索质量，而查询自适应阈值和社区意识中心惩罚是令牌效率的主要驱动因素。更广泛地说，将图探索与 LLM 推理分开，并将上下文构建视为检索的核心部分，可以为生成器产生更小、信息更密集的最终上下文。未来的工作可以改进检索控制，并在其他领域测试相同的上下文管理原则。Taken together, these findings support the paper’s three main contributions. The comparative results establish the overall quality-efficiency gains; the fixed-budget and ablation results show that reasoning-chain context construction helps preserve overall quality under tight token budgets; and the ablation study further shows that lexical and community-aware anchor selection supports retrieval quality, while query-adaptive thresholding and community-aware hub penalization are the main drivers of token-efficiency. More broadly, separating graph exploration from LLM inference and treating context construction as a core part of retrieval yields a smaller, more information-dense final context for the generator. Future work can refine retrieval controls and test the same context-curation principle across additional domains.

局限性Limitations

这项研究有一些局限性。首先，DistComp 是一个以分布式系统文献为中心的专门基准测试，因此不应假设此处报告的绝对性能水平不会改变到每个域； UltraDomain 结果旨在检查主要质量效率模式是否仅限于该语料库。其次，LiteRAG 侧重于检索和上下文构建，而不是图索引，因此当索引成本本身是主要关注点时，本文没有解决完整的端到端优化问题。第三，所有系统都在共享生成和嵌入设置下进行评估，以隔离检索行为，这提高了可比性，但为未来的工作留下了跨模型的稳健性。最后，可扩展性分析是经验性的而不是形式性的，并且一些基线架构没有公开允许跨方法完美匹配硬代币预算的控制。这些限制主要影响范围和通用性，而不是论文的中心主张，即 LiteRAG 通过查询条件图探索和紧凑上下文构建提供强大的质量效率权衡。This study has some limitations. First, DistComp is a specialized benchmark centered on distributed-systems literature, so the absolute performance levels reported here should not be assumed to transfer unchanged to every domain; the UltraDomain results are intended to check that the main quality-efficiency pattern is not confined to that corpus. Second, LiteRAG focuses on retrieval and context construction rather than graph indexing, so the paper does not address the full end-to-end optimization problem when indexing cost is itself a primary concern. Third, all systems are evaluated under a shared generation and embedding setup to isolate retrieval behavior, which improves comparability but leaves cross-model robustness for future work. Finally, the scalability analysis is empirical rather than formal, and some baseline architectures do not expose controls that permit perfectly matched hard token budgets across methods. These limitations primarily affect scope and generality, rather than the paper’s central claim that LiteRAG delivers a strong quality-efficiency trade-off through query-conditioned graph exploration and compact context construction.
