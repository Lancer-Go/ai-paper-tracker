1. 简介Introduction

深度神经网络 (DNN) 擅长复杂的模式识别和决策任务，彻底改变了各个行业。然而，随着这些技术越来越多地应用于自动驾驶和医疗诊断等安全关键领域，其固有的漏洞也变得更加明显。这些应用中潜在故障的严重后果包括致命的交通事故Deep neural networks (DNNs) have revolutionized various industries by excelling at complex pattern recognition and decision-making tasks. However, as these technologies are increasingly adopted in safety-critical domains such as autonomous driving and medical diagnostics, their inherent vulnerabilities have become more apparent. The severe consequences of potential failures in these applications, ranging from fatal traffic accidents(Stewart, 2018)对于危及生命的医疗误诊，强调迫切需要强大的测试框架。to life-threatening medical misdiagnoses, highlight the urgent need for robust test frameworks.

由于测试预算通常是有限的，一个有效的策略是测试用例优先级，它对候选测试集进行排序，以便尽早发现错误的输入。大量工作研究了 DNN 测试的优先级标准As testing budgets are typically limited, an effective strategy is test case prioritization, which ranks a candidate test set so that faulty inputs are revealed as early as possible. A large body of work has studied prioritization criteria for DNN testing(Li et al., 2024; Shen et al., 2024; Hwang et al., 2023)，包括覆盖率驱动的启发法, including coverage-driven heuristics(Ma et al., 2018; Yin et al., 2025), 基于不确定性的分数, uncertainty-based scores(Feng et al., 2020; Bao et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2025)，以及学习排名措施, and learning-to-rank measures(Wang et al., 2021; Dang et al., 2024; Shen et al., 2024).

然而，大多数测试优先级方法假设基于置信度的指标可以可靠地将正确的预测与错误的预测区分开来，正确的预测通常具有较高的置信度，而错误的预测则表现出较低的置信度。最近的研究However, most test prioritization methods assume that confidence-based metrics can reliably separate correct from incorrect predictions, with correct predictions typically assigned higher confidence, whereas mispredictions exhibit low confidence. Recent studies(Bao et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024)研究人员检验了这一假设，结果表明 DNN 可能确实是错误的，并且即使对于正确的情况，预测类与竞争类之间的置信差距也往往很小。这种置信度和准确性的不匹配会影响优先级：高置信度错误可能会被排在后面，而低置信度但正确的输入可能会被过度优先排序，从而消耗有限的测试预算。have examined this assumption, showing that DNNs can be confidently wrong and that the confidence gap between the predicted class and competing classes is often marginal even for correct cases. This confidence and accuracy mismatch affects prioritization: high-confidence errors may be ranked late, while low-confidence yet correct inputs may be prioritized excessively, consuming limited testing budgets.

Second, we measure cross-checkpoint prediction variability by quantifying, for each test input, the instability of its predicted probability distribution across the selected checkpoints via Total Variation Distance (TVD), which captures sensitivity to checkpoint-induced decision boundary shifts. Third, we apply boundary uncertainty integration by incorporating the final checkpoint’s prediction margin to emphasize samples close to the decision boundary, where small perturbations are more likely to flip the predicted label. 6. 有效性的威胁Threats to Validity

实施和评估因素。Implementation and Evaluation Factors.我们的发现可能会受到实施和配置因素的影响。检查点索引、预处理一致性或相似性/裕度计算中的数值稳定性中的任何不准确都可能使优先级分数产生偏差。此外，训练和评估的随机性（例如随机初始化、数据改组和 GPU 不确定性）可能会引入方差。此外，对抗性鲁棒性评估对攻击配置很敏感；尽管我们使用广泛采用的攻击（FGSM/PGD/CW/BIM）以及标准库实现和默认设置，但替代超参数可能会改变绝对 RAUC 值，在某些情况下还会改变相对排名。为了减轻这些威胁，我们在所有方法中实施统一的培训和评估协议，并使用相同的测试集和预算。我们还发布了代码和配置以支持可重复性。Our findings may be influenced by implementation and configuration factors. Any inaccuracies in checkpoint indexing, preprocessing consistency, or numerical stability in similarity/margin computations may bias the prioritization scores. In addition, training and evaluation stochasticity (e.g., random initialization, data shuffling, and GPU nondeterminism) can introduce variance. Moreover, adversarial robustness evaluation is sensitive to attack configurations; although we use widely adopted attacks (FGSM/PGD/CW/BIM) with standard library implementations and default settings, alternative hyperparameters may change the absolute RAUC values and, in some cases, relative rankings. To mitigate these threats, we enforce a unified training and evaluation protocol across all methods and use identical test sets and budgets. We also release our code and configuration to support reproducibility.

基于模型分歧的方法之间的差异。Difference Between Model Disagreement-Based Methods.构造有效性威胁是不同的方法对模型分歧的定义不同。 Dropout 和 EffiMAP 从一个检查点周围的随机扰动中得出分歧，而 NCIP 使用 NC 引导的训练检查点之间的分歧并将其与最终利润信息相结合。与通过模型扰动测量预测变化的基于分歧的方法相比，NCIP 从自然可用的训练检查点估计预测变化。这两个系列依赖于不同的预测不稳定来源，这可能在不同的部署环境中提供互补的观点。A construct-validity threat is that different methods define model disagreement differently. Dropout and EffiMAP derive disagreement from stochastic perturbations around one checkpoint, whereas NCIP uses disagreement across NC-guided training checkpoints and combines it with final-margin information. Compared with disagreement-based methods that measure prediction variation through model perturbation, NCIP estimates prediction variability from naturally available training checkpoints. The two families rely on different sources of prediction instability, which may offer complementary perspectives in different deployment contexts.

实用性和部署限制。Practicality and Deployment Constraints.由于加载检查点和执行多检查点推理来估计预测变异性，NCIP 会产生相当大的运行时开销。这种成本可能会阻碍在吞吐量和延迟至关重要的工业管道中的采用。 NCIP 主要用于保留训练或微调检查点的模型开发和托管服务设置，不适用于没有检查点访问权限的第三方部署模型。即使有这样的开销，NCIP 在回归测试中仍然有用，在回归测试中，在每个周期中运行每个测试通常是不可行的，并且在有限的预算下重新排序测试很重要。这个范围也反映在我们的 LLM 设置中：我们只使用微调检查点（图 1）。NCIP incurs considerable runtime overhead due to loading checkpoints and performing multi-checkpoint inference to estimate prediction variability. This cost can hinder adoption in industrial pipelines where throughput and latency are critical. NCIP is mainly intended for model development and managed serving settings where training or fine-tuning checkpoints are retained, not for third-party deployed models without checkpoint access. Even with this overhead, NCIP is still useful in regression testing, where running every test in every cycle is often infeasible and reordering tests under a limited budget matters. 7.2. 测试输入优先级Test Input Prioritization

测试优先级已经成为一种特别有效的方法Test prioritization has become a particularly effective method(Al-Qadasi et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2020; Hwang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023; Huang et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025b; Abbasishahkoo et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2025)。这涉及根据失败的可能性对测试输入进行排名，这样可以通过关注高风险样本，在有限的时间和计算资源内及早检测到关键错误。现有的 DNN 测试输入优先级排序技术主要可分为五类：基于覆盖的方法、基于惊喜充分性的方法、基于置信度的方法、基于模型扰动的方法和学习排序方法。. This involves ranking test inputs based on their likelihood of failure, which allows for the early detection of critical errors within limited time and computational resources by focusing on high-risk samples. Existing DNN test-input prioritization techniques can be mainly divided into five categories: coverage-based methods, surprise adequacy-based methods, confidence-based methods, model-perturbation-based methods and learning-to-rank methods.

基于覆盖范围。Coverage-Based.基于覆盖的方法通过量化内部激活行为（例如神经元覆盖）并优先考虑最大化此类标准的输入，将传统的代码覆盖思想应用于 DNNCoverage-based methods adapt traditional code-coverage ideas to DNNs by quantifying internal activation behaviors (e.g., neuron coverage) and prioritizing inputs that maximize such criteria(Ma et al., 2018; Zhang et al., 2020a; Guo et al., 2025; Yin et al., 2025)。基于覆盖的方法侧重于内部激活多样性，而 NCIP 侧重于整个训练轨迹的预测稳定性。. Coverage-based methods focus on internal activation diversity, while NCIP focuses on prediction stability across the training trajectory.

基于惊喜充分性。Surprise Adequacy-Based.惊喜充分性 (SA) 方法通过测量输入的激活轨迹相对于训练分布的新颖程度来确定输入的优先级Surprise adequacy (SA) methods prioritize inputs by measuring how novel an input’s activation trace is relative to the training distribution(Kim et al., 2023, 2020; Li et al., 2025a)。代表性实例包括基于 KDE 的似然估计和基于距离的 SA (DSA). Representative instantiations include KDE-based likelihood estimation and distance-based SA (DSA)(Kim et al., 2019)。 SA已作为量化目标指导系统边界探索. SA has been used as a quantitative target to guide systematic boundary exploration(Kang et al., 2024)。基于 SA 的方法量化相对于训练分布参考的输入新颖性。相比之下，NCIP 在检查点级预测一致性上运行，无需在优先级排序时访问训练数据。. SA-based methods quantify input novelty relative to training distribution references. In contrast, NCIP operates on checkpoint-level prediction consistency without accessing training data at prioritization time.

基于信心。Confidence-Based.这些方法根据输出概率估计错误可能性，例如 DeepGiniThese methods estimate error likelihood from output probabilities, e.g., DeepGini(Feng et al., 2020), 熵, entropy(Byun et al., 2019),PCS, PCS(Zhang et al., 2020b)和 MSP, and MSP(Weiss and Tonella, 2022)。最近的工作通过结合邻域不确定性（NNS）改进了不确定性估计. Recent works improve uncertainty estimation by incorporating neighborhood uncertainty (NNS)(Bao et al., 2023)，通过特征选择调整概率向量（FAST）, adjusting probability vectors via feature selection (FAST)(Chen et al., 2024)，或联合优化不确定性和多样性（SETS）, or jointly optimizing uncertainty and diversity (SETS)(Wang et al., 2025)。基于置信度的方法从单个训练状态下的每个样本输出概率中得出排名信号。 NCIP 通过测量多个训练检查点的预测变异性来补充这一观点，针对仅靠点估计置信度可能无法捕获的不稳定性。. Confidence-based methods derive ranking signals from per-sample output probabilities at a single training state. NCIP complements this perspective by measuring prediction variability across multiple training checkpoints, targeting instability that may not be captured by point-estimate confidence alone.

基于模型扰动。Model-Perturbation-Based.这些方法通过测量模型端扰动下的预测变化来对输入进行优先级排序。辍学These methods prioritize inputs by measuring prediction changes under model-side perturbations. Dropout(Hu et al., 2023)估计重复随机前向传播的不确定性，而 EffiMAPestimates uncertainty from repeated stochastic forward passes, while EffiMAP(Wei et al., 2022)扰乱模型和输入，并根据结果预测变化对样本进行排名。模型扰动方法通过在固定检查点重复扰动来估计预测变化。相反，NCIP 从自然发生的训练检查点中汲取变化，提供了预测不稳定的不同来源。perturbs both model and input and ranks samples by the resulting prediction variation. Model-perturbation methods estimate prediction variation through repeated perturbation at a fixed checkpoint. NCIP instead draws variation from naturally occurring training checkpoints, providing a different source of prediction instability.

学习排名。Learning-to-Rank.学习排名方法从标记的监督中学习评分函数，以便在预算范围内尽早出现错误揭示的输入。示例包括基于突变的标记以提高优先级 (PRIMA)Learning-to-rank methods learn a scoring function from labeled supervision so that bug-revealing inputs appear early under a budget. Examples include mutation-based labeling to improve prioritization (PRIMA)(Wang et al., 2021)，基于特征的经典模型排名（MLPrior）, feature-based ranking for classical models (MLPrior)(Dang et al., 2024)，以及利用训练动态来区分错误揭示轨迹的方法（TDPR）, and approaches exploiting training dynamics to distinguish bug-revealing trajectories (TDPR)(Shen et al., 2024)。学习排名方法从标记监督中训练专用的排名模型，有效利用失败信息。 NCIP 专为可访问训练模型及其检查点但无法访问标记的测试数据的设置而设计，使用交叉检查点变异性作为无监督优先级信号。. Learning-to-rank methods train a dedicated ranking model from labeled supervision, making effective use of failure information. NCIP is designed for settings where a trained model and its checkpoints are accessible but labeled test data is not, using cross-checkpoint variability as an unsupervised prioritization signal.

8. 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了This paper presentsNCIP，一种用于深度分类器的无标签错误优先级方法，在训练期间利用几何稳定性。 NCIP 使用分类器权重等角度分数选择 NC 引导的代表性子集，然后通过将 (i) 这些检查点上的时间预测变异性与 (ii) 最终模型预测裕度相结合来确定测试输入的优先级，以捕获决策边界邻近度。通过将错误分类风险与跨检查点预测变异性联系起来，NCIP 为仅置信启发式方法提供了一种有原则的替代方案，同时在优先级划分期间不需要训练数据。大量实验表明，NCIP 在早期故障检测中实现了具有竞争力的性能，并在数据集、架构和评估设置中提供了强大的优先级。未来的工作将探索将 NC 引导选择扩展到更广泛的模型系列，并开发自适应检查点选择策略以进一步减少计算开销。, a label-free error prioritization approach for deep classifiers that leverages geometric stability during training. NCIP selects an NC-guided representative subset using classifier-weight equiangularity scores, and then prioritizes test inputs by combining (i) temporal prediction variability across these checkpoints with (ii) the final model prediction margin to capture decision boundary proximity. By tying misclassification risk to cross-checkpoint prediction variability, NCIP offers a principled alternative to confidence-only heuristics while requiring no training data during prioritization. Extensive experiments show that NCIP achieves competitive performance in early fault detection and delivers robust prioritization across datasets, architectures, and evaluation settings. Future work will explore extending NC-guided selection to broader model families and developing adaptive checkpoint selection strategies to further reduce computational overhead.
