1 简介Introduction

如今，大型语言模型 (LLM) 广泛用于各种写作工作流程，包括起草、重写、摘要、润色和风格转换。它们的下游用途不仅限于自由形式生成，还包括辅助合成和现有文本的转换Large language models (LLMs) are nowadays used in a broad range of writing workflows, including drafting, rewriting, summarization, polishing, and style transfer. Their downstream use is not limited to free-form generation but also includes assisted composition and the transformation of existing text(Yang et al., 2024)。然而，人工智能文本检测仍然经常被评估为二元问题：区分人类编写的文本和模型直接生成的文本. AI-text detection, however, is still often evaluated as a binary problem: distinguishing human-written text from text generated directly by a model(Gehrmann et al., 2019; Ippolito et al., 2020; Mitchell et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024)。该标准基准是必要的基线，但作为检测器稳健性的经验测试，它并不完整。因此，本文解决的是基准有效性问题，而不仅仅是释义稳健性问题。. This standard benchmark is a necessary baseline, but it is incomplete as an empirical test of detector robustness. This paper therefore addresses a benchmark-validity question rather than only a paraphrase-robustness question.

The second is语言表面linguistic surface：最终的措辞是人工编写的、由法学硕士自由生成的，还是由法学硕士通过重写调解的。这些因素在实际工作流程中可能会有所不同。学生、记者、分析师或软件工程师可以编写初稿，然后使用法学硕士来提高流畅性。在这种情况下，最终的文本具有人类起源的内容，但具有法学硕士介导的表面。相反，LLM 生成的文本可以再次通过相同的 LLM，同时保留 LLM 来源。因此，检测器分数可以反映直接机器作者身份、机器介导的重写、域伪影、解码伪影、生成器特定线索或这些信号之间的交互。: whether the final wording is human-written, freely generated by an LLM, or mediated by an LLM through rewriting. These factors can diverge in realistic workflows. A student, journalist, analyst, or software engineer may write an initial draft and then use an LLM to improve fluency. In that case, the final text has human-origin content but an LLM-mediated surface. Conversely, an LLM-generated text may be passed again through the same LLM while remaining LLM-origin. A detector score may therefore reflect direct machine authorship, machine-mediated rewriting, domain artifacts, decoding artifacts, generator-specific cues, or interactions among these signals.

3 相关工作Related Work

本节将这项研究置于人工智能文本检测、重写鲁棒性、基准设计和低误报评估的先前工作中。目标不是彻底调查所有检测器变体，而是确定标准人类与法学硕士基准的经验局限性，并激发对匹配的四体系评估的需求。This section positions the study within prior work on AI-text detection, rewriting robustness, benchmark design, and low-false-positive evaluation. 3.1 AI文本检测系列AI-text detection families

人工智能文本检测已经通过多个检测器系列进行了研究。最近的一项调查沿着被动/主动轴组织这个空间：被动检测器仅根据文本推断作者身份，而主动方法（主要是水印和生成日志检索）需要生成管道本身的合作AI-text detection has been studied through several detector families. A recent survey organizes this space along a passive/active axis: passive detectors infer authorship post hoc from a text alone, whereas active approaches, principally watermarking and generation-log retrieval, require cooperation from the generation pipeline itself(Xiang et al., 2026). 受体阻滞剂ARB仅限于被动式事后探测器；水印超出了范围，因为它针对不同的部署设置，其中检测器控制或具有对生成器的特权访问权限。在被动检测中，GLTR 等统计检测器会暴露令牌排名的不规则性is restricted to passive, post-hoc detectors; watermarking is out of scope, as it targets a different deployment setting in which the detector controls or has privileged access to the generator. Within passive detection, statistical detectors such as GLTR expose token-rank irregularities(Gehrmann et al., 2019);监督检测器微调编码器，例如 BERT 或 RoBERTa; supervised detectors fine-tune encoders such as BERT or RoBERTa(Ippolito et al., 2020; Zhuang et al., 2021; Li et al., 2024);基于似然和曲率的零样本检测器使用参考模型中的概率结构，如 DetectGPT 和 FastDetectGPT; likelihood- and curvature-based zero-shot detectors use probability structure from a reference model, as in DetectGPT and FastDetectGPT(Mitchell et al., 2023; Bao et al., 2024);对比零样本探测器（例如双筒望远镜）比较配对观察者/表演者的可能性; contrastive zero-shot detectors such as Binoculars compare paired observer/performer likelihoods(Hans et al., 2024);以及面向鲁棒性的监督检测器，例如针对对抗性释义的 RADAR 训练; and robustness-oriented supervised detectors such as RADAR train against adversarial paraphrasing(Hu et al., 2023)。较新的“重写即探针”系列使用 LLM 自己的候选文本重写或校正作为检测信号，利用 LLM 编辑已由 LLM 生成的文本少于人工编写文本的发现：RAIDAR 测量由 LLM 重写输入引起的编辑距离. A newer rewriting-as-probe family instead uses an LLM’s own rewrite or correction of a candidate text as the detection signal, exploiting the finding that LLMs edit already-LLM-generated text less than human-written text: RAIDAR measures the edit distance induced by an LLM rewrite of the input(Mao et al., 2024); MAGRET 相反使用重写相似性来检测和归属作者身份，而无需日志概率访问; MAGRET instead uses rewrite similarity to detect and attribute authorship without log-probability access(Huang et al., 2025b); L2R微调重写器以放大RAIDAR编辑距离差距，提高跨域AUROC; L2R fine-tunes the rewriter to amplify the RAIDAR edit-distance gap, improving cross-domain AUROC(Hao et al., 2025); GECScore 对文本的语法校正版本的相似性进行评分，报告对跨域和释义攻击的鲁棒性; and GECScore scores similarity to a grammar-corrected version of the text, reporting robustness to cross-domain and paraphrase attacks(Wu et al., 2025)。最近的工作还探索了更专业的零样本、可解释或域自适应检测器，包括逆提示和分布对齐方法. Recent work also explores more specialized zero-shot, interpretable, or domain-adaptive detectors, including inverse-prompt and distribution-alignment approaches(Chen et al., 2025a, b).

这些检测器系列不可互换：受监督的编码器可以学习数据集或生成器特定的线索，并在分布偏移下降级；基于似然的检测器取决于参考模型和评估分布之间的拟合；对比方法在直接生成上可能很强，但在重写下表现不同；面向鲁棒性的监督检测器仍然需要跨域、生成器和操作点进行评估。最近的两项大规模比较直接证实了这种异质性：一项跨越经典、神经、融合和基于提示的检测器，在域和生成器转换下，每个系列都会损失 5-30 个 AUROC 点These detector families are not interchangeable: supervised encoders may learn dataset- or generator-specific cues and degrade under distribution shift; likelihood-based detectors depend on the fit between the reference model and the evaluated distribution; contrastive methods can be strong on direct generation but behave differently under rewriting; and robustness-oriented supervised detectors still need evaluation across domains, generators, and operating points. Two recent large-scale comparisons confirm this heterogeneity directly: one spanning classical, neural, fusion, and prompting-based detectors under domain and generator shift, with every family losing 5–30 AUROC points(Baidya et al., 2026);另一种将样式特征与变压器表示融合，改善了跨域传输，但留下了骨干网特定的故障模式; the other fusing stylometric features with transformer representations, which improves cross-domain transfer but leaves backbone-specific failure modes(Mady et al., 2026)。因此，基准测试应将检测器视为评估对象并报告家庭级别的模式，而不仅仅是单个总分。. For this reason, a benchmark should treat detectors as the objects of evaluation and report family-level patterns rather than only a single aggregate score.

7.3 理论和实践意义Theoretical and Practical Implications

理论意义。Theoretical implications.核心理论贡献是将释义稳健性重新定义为基准转移问题，而不是单个稳健性标量。先前的释义稳健性研究通常会报告在日益激进的重写器下每个检测器的一条退化曲线The core theoretical contribution is reframing paraphrase robustness as a benchmark-transfer problem rather than a single robustness scalar.
