摘要Abstract

本文研究了基于 GPT 的工具如何帮助构建可重用的分析电子表格模型。经过筛选，我们评估了五个 GPT 扩展，并选择 pulsrai.com 的 Excel AI 进行详细测试。通过对简单问题陈述进行结构化实验，我们根据 ERFR 标准（单元格中的每个输入；单元格公式；无硬连线数字；标签；准确）评估 Excel AI 的性能。结果表明，虽然 Excel AI 可以生成结构良好的模型，但它是不一致的并且通常不可重现。我们确定了两个核心挑战——“信心问题”和“工作流程问题”——这凸显了熟练用户验证和调整 GPT 生成的电子表格的需要。尽管 GPT 有望生成草稿模型，从而缩短开发时间或降低技能要求，但当前的工具对于专业用途来说仍然不可靠。最后，我们对未来的快速工程、可重复性和大规模建模任务的研究提出了建议。This paper investigates how GPT-based tools can assist in building reusable analytical spreadsheet models. After a screening, we evaluate five GPT extensions, and select Excel AI by pulsrai.com for detailed testing. Through structured experiments on simple problem statements, we assess Excel AI’s performance against the ERFR criteria (each input in a cell; cell formulas; no hardwired numbers; labels; accurate). Results show that while Excel AI can produce well-structured models, it is inconsistent and often non-reproducible. We identify two central challenges—“the problem of confidence” and “the problem of workflow”—which highlight the need for skilled users to verify and adapt GPT-generated spreadsheets. Though GPTs show promise for generating draft models that may reduce development time or lower skill requirements, current tools remain unreliable for professional use. We conclude with recommendations for future research into prompt engineering, reproducibility, and larger-scale modeling tasks.

1. 简介1. INTRODUCTION

人们普遍认为，人工智能通用预训练变压器（GPT）有望对商业活动的许多方面产生巨大影响。分析被认为是 GPT 特别有吸引力的候选者，包括电子表格分析。快速搜索互联网就会发现无数资源，旨在展示 GPT 如何帮助分析数据集；可视化数据；设计细胞公式；和其他有用的活动。尽管可靠性仍然是一个悬而未决的问题，但显然已经取得了一些成功。It is widely understood that Artificial Intelligence general pre-trained transformers (GPTs) are poised to have a large impact on many aspects of business activity. Analytics is perceived to be a particularly attractive candidate for GPTs, including spreadsheet analytics. A quick search of the internet reveals countless resources purporting to show how GPTs can help with analyzing datasets; visualizing data; devising cell formulas; and other useful activities. There are clearly some successes, although reliability remains an open question.

我们试图了解 GPT 如何协助当今的电子表格分析，特别是电子表格模型的创建。我们的兴趣是创建可重复使用的电子表格模型，该模型接受输入值并计算输出值。我们的最终愿望是“工业质量分析电子表格模型”（Grossman、Mehrotra 和 Sander 2011；Grossman 和 Mehrotra 2023），它满足六个标准：适合高效分析；可读；可转让；准确的;可重复使用的;可修改的。因为这些标准微妙而复杂，所以我们将研究一个更简单的目标，即可重用性。We seek to understand the reality of how GPTs can assist in spreadsheet analytics today, particularly in the creation of spreadsheet models. Our interest is the creation of reusable spreadsheet models that take input values and calculate output values. Our ultimate desire is “industrial quality analytical spreadsheet models” (Grossman, Mehrotra and Sander 2011; Grossman and Mehrotra 2023) which meet six criteria: suitable for efficient analysis; readable; transferable; accurate; reusable; modifiable. Because these criteria are subtle and complex, we will instead investigate a simpler goal, of reusability.

在没有明确指示的情况下，GPT（或就此而言，人类）必然会对输出的性质和形式做出假设。因此，用户有责任提供明确的指示。事实上，如果没有提供明确的指示，期望预期的结果是不合理的。In the absence of clear instructions, a GPT (or for that matter, a human being) will necessarily make assumptions as to the nature and form of the output. Hence, it is incumbent upon the user to provide clear instructions. Indeed, it is unreasonable to expect the desired results if clear instructions are not provided.

5.2 工作流程问题5.2 The Problem of Workflow

在某些商业领域，人们认为使用 GPT 生成包含不完美甚至完全错误的“草稿”是一个好主意。例如，撰写（并回复）征求建议书 (RFP) 是一个活动较多且有多项可行业务的领域（例如 rohirrim.ai）。In some fields of business, people argue that using a GPT to generate a “rough draft” that contains imperfections or even outright errors is a good idea. This is grounded in the logic of technology adoption: when a new technology arrives, it is wise to change one’s business processes to leverage the technology’s capabilities in order to achieve desired outcomes; old problems are reduced, new problems emerge, and a new workflow evolves. This obviously requires effort, learning, disruption, and a willingness to release past practices. And it doesn’t always work.

将这种“粗略的思维”探索到分析电子表格模型似乎是明智的。这就提出了一个问题，不完美的电子表格模型有什么关系？人们可以研究它以获得洞察力，然后重新开始。或者可以在此基础上尝试纠正任何缺陷或错误。这不是一个微不足道的问题，也是一个没有被很好理解的问题。It seems wise to explore this “rough draft thinking” to analytical spreadsheet models. Which raises the question, what is one to do with an imperfect spreadsheet model? One can study it for insight, then start over. Or one can build on and try to correct any imperfections or errors. This is not a trivial question and one that is not well understood.

答案无疑会因用例而异。目前，一些用例似乎并不适合，例如高风险、大规模的财务规划模型（Grossman 和 Özlük 2010 年进行的调查）。但也许在某些用例中这确实有意义，特别是对于不太复杂的建模者。The answer will undoubtedly differ by use case. Some use cases do not seem fertile ground at this time, for example high-stakes, large-scale financial planning models (surveyed in Grossman and Özlük 2010). But perhaps there are use cases where this does make sense, especially for lesssophisticated modelers.

我们看到两种可能的价值主张。首先是总时间。二是人员技能水平。总时间是提示 GPT 所花费的时间，加上理解和评估生成的草稿电子表格所花费的时间，加上将草稿电子表格重构为最终产品所花费的时间（或在收到草稿的通知和启发后重新开始）。如果这个总时间少于从头开始编写电子表格所需的时间，那么草稿就会增加价值。We see two possible value propositions. First is total time. Second is the level of personnel skill. Total time is the time spent on prompting the GPT, plus the time spent on understanding and evaluating the resulting rough draft spreadsheet, plus the time spent on refactoring the rough draft spreadsheet into a final product (or starting over after being informed and inspired by the rough draft). If this total time is less than the time needed to code a spreadsheet from scratch, then the rough draft would add value.

人员技能水平是领域知识、电子表格知识和（对于具有电子表格标准的组织）设计标准知识和快速可靠地满足标准的能力的组合。如果技能水平较低（因此工资较低）的人员可以开发可行的电子表格，那么草稿就会增加价值。The level of personnel skill is the combination of domain knowledge, spreadsheet knowledge, and (for organizations with spreadsheet standards) knowledge of the design standards and ability to quickly and reliably meet the standard. If it is possible for personnel with a lower level of skill (and hence pay) to develop a viable spreadsheet, then the rough draft would add value.

这似乎是一个富有成果的研究领域。许多不同的参与者在这个领域开展了大量活动，我们预计未来几年将会出现可行的用例。This seems an area of fruitful research. There is much activity in this space by many different players, and we anticipate that viable use cases will be emerging in the next few years.

6. 结论6. CONCLUSIONS

本文总结了使用 GPT 构建电子表格模型的简单入门级实验。有大量 GPT 声称能够构建电子表格模型。我们发现这些工具不稳定且不可靠。然而，它们有时效果很好，尽管结果通常不可重复。初步筛选表明，一个工具（pulsrai.com 的 ChatGPT 自定义 GPT Excel AI）是一个不错的选择。This paper provides a summary of simple, entry-level experiments into using GPTs to build spreadsheet models. There is a large space of GPTs that claim an ability to build spreadsheet models. We found that the tools are temperamental and unreliable. However, they sometimes work very well, although the results are often not reproducible. Initial screening suggested that one tool (the ChatGPT custom GPT Excel AI by pulsrai.com) was a good choice.
