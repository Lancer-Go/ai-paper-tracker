Xiongwei Zhu    Xiaojian Liao    Tianyang Jiang    Yusen Zhang    Liang Wang    Limin Xiao
摘要Abstract

However, in memory-constrained inference scenarios, only a small set of experts can be cached. Experts not in the cache must be fetched from slow external storage (e.g., UFS), leading to frequent evictions and substantial I/O overhead. We propose ReMoE, a router fine-tuning framework designed to boost token-wise expert reuse. ReMoE biases the router toward recently selected experts, producing temporally stable routing that better matches cache locality constraints. By increasing short-horizon expert reuse, ReMoE reduces expert fetches from storage without adding inference-time computation. Experiments on DeepSeek and Qwen models show that ReMoE improves expert reuse by 26% while maintaining downstream task performance. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的边缘部署是一种新兴趋势Edge-side deployment of Large Language Models (LLMs) is an emerging trend(Xu et al., 2024; Zheng et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025)，实时翻译和高级照片编辑等设备端人工智能应用已经展示了其实用价值, with on-device AI applications such as real-time translation and advanced photo editing already demonstrating its practical value(Xue et al., 2024)。虽然专家混合 (MoE) 架构对于扩展 LLM 能力至关重要，但迄今为止，其部署仍然主要基于云，在 边缘设备。最近在设备上 MoE 模型、面向边缘的推理运行时和移动 MoE 应用程序方面的努力表明，这种情况正在开始改变，使得 MoE 与边缘和内存受限部署越来越相关. While Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures are central to scaling LLM capacity, their deployment has so far remained predominantly cloud-based, with limited use on edge devices. Recent efforts on on-device MoE models, edge-oriented inference runtimes, and mobile MoE applications suggest that this situation is beginning to change, making MoE increasingly relevant to edge and memory-constrained deployments(OPPO, 2024; MediaTek, 2024; Nvidia, 2026; Liquid AI, 2025; Ai2, 2025; Google DeepMind, 2026).

这一趋势受到两个因素的支持。首先，MoE 的稀疏激活在推理过程中仅使一部分专家处于活动状态，从而保留较大的模型容量，同时限制激活的参数足迹。其次，移动存储的进步使得本地重量存储变得越来越可行。例如，三星的 UFS 4.0 提供高达 4 GB/s 的读取速度和高达 1 TB 的容量，使得大型模型重量的本地存储变得越来越可行This trend is supported by two factors. First, MoE’s sparse activation keeps only a subset of experts active during inference, preserving large model capacity while limiting the activated parameter footprint. Second, advances in mobile storage make local weight storage increasingly feasible. For example, Samsung’s UFS 4.0 offers read speeds up to 4 GB/s and capacities up to 1 TB, making local storage of large model weights increasingly feasible(Samsung Semiconductor, )。然而，在边缘设备上部署 MoE LLM 会带来频繁专家切换的挑战。在解码阶段，每个令牌可能激活一组不同的专家，这会导致频繁、不规则的 I/O 请求从存储中加载所需的专家权重，从而延长推理延迟. However, deploying MoE LLMs on edge devices introduces the challenge of frequent expert switching. During the decoding phase, where each token may activate a different set of experts, this results in frequent, irregular I/O requests to load the required expert weights from storage, prolonging the inference latency(Qu et al., 2025).

3 教育部卸载中的地方性差距The Locality Gap in MoE Offloading

我们研究了边缘设备上内存受限、单请求推理下的细粒度 MoE 解码，其中快速内存有限。由于现代 MoE LLM 仅权重就需要数十 GB，而边缘 DRAM 还必须容纳运行时缓冲区和 KV 缓存，因此只有一小部分专家可以驻留在快速内存中。剩余的专家必须按需从较慢的存储（例如 UFS）中获取，这使得专家权重移动成为一阶瓶颈。与数据中心服务相比，这种制度通常与We study fine-grained MoE decoding under memory-constrained, single-request inference on edge devices, where fast memory is limited. Since modern MoE LLMs can require tens of GB for weights alone, while edge DRAM must also accommodate runtime buffers and KV cache, only a small subset of experts can remain resident in fast memory. The remaining experts must be fetched from slower storage (e.g., UFS) on demand, making expert-weight movement a first-order bottleneck. In contrast to datacenter serving, this regime typically operates withB=1B{=}1（交互式使用），几乎没有机会分摊跨批次的 I/O 延迟。为了清楚起见，我们的缓存分析采用请求隔离设置，其中每个提示都从冷专家缓存开始。因此，路由专家的逐步稳定性成为端到端延迟的主要决定因素。(interactive usage), leaving little opportunity to amortize I/O latency across batches. For clarity, our cache analysis adopts a request-isolated setting where each prompt starts from a cold expert cache. As a result, the step-to-step stability of routed experts becomes a primary determinant of end-to-end latency.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 ReMoE，一种仅用于路由器的微调方法，可以改进 短期专家重用内存受限的 MoE 推理，无需 更改模型架构或推理图。跨越 DeepSeek 和 Qwen MoE模型，ReMoE改进了路由局部性，缓存友好性， 和实际系统解码效率，同时很大程度上保留下游 能力。We propose ReMoE, a router-only fine-tuning method that improves short-horizon expert reuse for memory-constrained MoE inference without changing the model architecture or inference graph. Across DeepSeek and Qwen MoE models, ReMoE improves routing locality, cache friendliness, and real-system decoding efficiency while largely preserving downstream capability.
