敏捷感知多技能运动Agile perceptive multi-skill locomotion
用于野外四足机器人for quadrupedal robots in the wild

摘要Abstract

要使四足机器人能够穿越复杂的地形（从崎岖的户外环境到城市景观），需要无缝集成多种运动技能、步态之间的平滑过渡以及仅使用机载传感器的高速感知运动。 我们提出了 APT-RL（基于动作预训练变压器的强化学习），这是一个统一的框架，使多技能运动能够通过仅利用机载感知和计算的自主技能转换来实现复杂环境中的高速遍历。 我们的方法通过简化动力学的轨迹优化来生成大规模、功能丰富的二维运动数据集。这些数据集可以训练多样化、可重复使用的运动技能，这些技能可以有效地转移到在复杂不平坦的地形上运行的真正的四足机器人。由此产生的高质量技能可以作为有效学习复杂下游任务的强大先验，并自然地扩展到 3D 环境，从而在部署的策略中实现平稳、高速的多技能移动。 现实世界的实验证明了该框架的功能：机器人可以通过复杂的室内障碍物和室外野外环境进行灵活的机动，包括动态下降机动，瞬时峰值速度高达每秒 6 米。单一的机载策略可以强劲地穿越各种障碍，包括楼梯、障碍物、踏脚石、间隙和倒下的树枝，这证明了我们方法的多功能性和有效性。Enabling quadrupedal robots to traverse complex terrains—from rugged outdoor environments to urban landscapes—requires seamless integration of multiple motor skills, smooth transitions between gaits, and high-speed perceptive locomotion using only onboard sensors. We present APT-RL (Action Pretrained Transformer-based Reinforcement Learning), a unified framework that enables multi-skill locomotion to achieve high-speed traversal in complex environments through autonomous skill transitions utilizing only onboard perception and computation. Our approach generates large-scale, feature-rich 2D motion datasets through trajectory optimization with simplified dynamics. These datasets enable training of diverse, reusable locomotion skills that transfer effectively to a real quadruped robot operating on complex uneven terrains. The resulting high-quality skills serve as strong priors for efficient learning of complex downstream tasks and extend naturally to 3D environments, enabling smooth, high-speed multi-skill locomotion in deployed policy. Real-world experiments demonstrate the framework’s capabilities: the robot performs agile maneuvers through complex indoor obstacles and outdoor wild environments, including dynamic drop-down maneuvers that reach instantaneous peak speeds of up to 6 meters per second. A single onboard policy enables robust traversal of diverse obstacles, including stairs, hurdles, stepping stones, gaps, and fallen branches, demonstrating the versatility and effectiveness of our approach.

1 简介Introduction

四足动物会自然地调整步态和运动技能以适应其生理、环境、地形和运动速度Quadrupedal animals naturally adapt their gaits and motor skills to their physiology, environment, terrain, and locomotion speed[23, 76]。 这种适应性行为包括通过快速奔跑和跳跃接近障碍物，以及将各种运动技能结合成精确定时和协调的序列。因此，四足动物通过使用多种运动技能灵活地穿越复杂的地形，同时保持稳定性和敏捷性。同样，腿式机器人有潜力通过高速动态、高效地穿越复杂地形（包括瓦砾、台阶和原木）来到达其他难以到达的位置。实现这种动态运动不仅对于在森林和山区等自然户外环境中导航至关重要，而且对于提高搜救任务等高风险应用中的机器人性能也至关重要. This adaptive behavior includes approaching obstacles through fast running and jumping, as well as combining various motor skills into precisely timed and coordinated sequences. Consequently, quadrupeds agilely traverse complex terrains by using multiple locomotion skills while maintaining stability and agility. Likewise, legged robots have the potential to reach otherwise inaccessible locations by dynamically and efficiently traversing complex terrains, including rubble, steps, and logs, at high speeds. Enabling such dynamic locomotion is crucial not only for navigating natural outdoor environments such as forests and mountainous regions, but also for advancing robotic performance in high-stakes applications such as search-and-rescue missions[51, 14, 34].

为了实现这一目标，腿式机器人必须配备几个关键组件：首先，强大的控制架构，支持多种步态和各种速度下相应的运动技能；其次，能够自主执行平滑、灵活、稳定的步态和技能转换，以适应难以到达的地形条件；第三，一个高效实用的学习框架，能够在现实世界的机器人平台上获取和部署复杂的运动行为。这些要素必须和谐地整合并适应不同的环境，包括多种敏捷运动技能的精确协调。开发能够穿越不同地形的四足控制系统长期以来一直是腿式机器人社区的核心追求To realize this goal, legged robots must be equipped with several key components: first, a robust control architecture that supports multiple gaits and corresponding motor skills across a broad range of speeds; second, the ability to autonomously perform smooth, flexible, stable gait and skill transitions that adapt to hard-to-reach terrain conditions; and third, an efficient and practical learning framework that enables the acquisition and deployment of complex locomotion behaviors on real-world robotic platforms. These elements must be harmoniously integrated and adapted to diverse environments, including the precise coordination of multiple agile motor skills. Developing quadrupedal control systems capable of traversing diverse terrains has long been a central pursuit within the legged robotics community[28, 8]，激发了基于模型的控制和强化学习（RL）方法的广泛研究。, motivating extensive research in both model-based control and reinforcement learning (RL) approaches.

3 讨论Discussion

我们的工作扩展了四足机器人的能力，使其能够在现实的户外环境中执行快速、感知和自适应的多技能运动。我们提出了 APT-RL 架构，该架构利用从 2D 轨迹优化数据导出的学习状态表示和先验扭矩动作。该先前数据集捕获与各种 3D 运动行为相关的紧凑且基于物理的运动特征。为了充分利用 TO 数据集中的状态和动作信息，我们首先执行表示学习，然后对动作（扭矩）解码器进行监督训练。通过这个过程，来自多种步态的信息被嵌入到统一的潜在空间中，使模型能够捕获不同运动行为的共同结构。这个预训练过程是我们的 Action Pretrained Transformer (APT) 架构的初始阶段，后来通过强化学习重新调整其用途。我们联合训练辅助动作以及由潜在表示结构的固定预训练动作解码器。这使得该策略能够适应以前未见过的高速 3D 障碍路线，在共享潜在空间中表达多种步态，并以提高的样本效率泛化到不同的地形。我们的系统利用感知、地形条件和命令速度自主选择多种运动步态和相应的运动技能，支持在不同地形条件下的可靠运动。为了支持现实条件下的部署，我们提炼了一个学生外感受编码器网络，该网络利用以自我为中心的深度图像和稀疏的远程 LiDAR 到统一的外感受嵌入中，从而实现超出 RGB-D 相机有限范围的可靠地形感知控制。我们在室内和室外环境中评估了四足机器人，包括高台阶、森林小径、树叶覆盖的路径和大学校园的阶梯结构。Our work extends the capabilities of quadrupedal robots to perform fast, perceptive, and adaptive multi-skill locomotion in real-world outdoor environments. We propose the APT-RL architecture that leverages a learned state representation and torque action prior derived from 2D trajectory optimization data. This prior dataset captures compact and physically grounded motion features relevant to diverse 3D locomotion behaviors. To fully leverage both state and action information from the TO dataset, we first perform representation learning followed by supervised training of an action (torque) decoder. Through this process, information from multiple gaits is embedded into a unified latent space, allowing the model to capture common structure across diverse locomotion behaviors. This pretraining process serves as the initial stage of our Action Pretrained Transformer (APT) architecture, which is later repurposed through reinforcement learning. We jointly train an auxiliary action alongside a fixed pretrained action decoder structured by a latent representation. This enables the policy to adapt to previously unseen 3D obstacle courses at high speeds, express multiple gaits in shared latent space, and generalize across diverse terrains with improved sample efficiency. Our system autonomously selects multiple locomotion gaits and corresponding motor skills by utilizing perception, terrain conditions, and command speed, supporting reliable locomotion across diverse terrain conditions. 4 材料与方法Materials and Methods

在本节中，我们介绍 APT-RL 管道，它由四个阶段组成。我们首先使用轨迹优化来生成不同的平坦地形跑步运动，然后学习基于 TVAE 的统一潜在空间用于小跑和弹跳。接下来，我们训练了一种强化学习策略，该策略通过预训练的步态特定解码器输出解码为关节扭矩的潜在动作。最后，我们通过蒸馏进行感知适应，以实现深度图像和 2D LiDAR 的控制。In this section, we present the APT-RL pipeline, which consists of four stages. We first used trajectory optimization to generate diverse flat-terrain running motions, and then learned a TVAE-based unified latent space for trotting and bounding. Next, we trained a reinforcement learning policy that outputs latent actions decoded into joint torques via pretrained gait-specific decoders. Finally, we performed perceptual adaptation through distillation to enable control from depth images and 2D LiDAR.

演员和评论家神经网络Actor and critic neural network

策略网络被实现为完全连接的前馈神经网络，具有大小分别为 512、256 和 128 的三层。执行者网络采用 203 维输入向量并输出 29 维动作向量。整个网络使用的激活函数是修正线性单元（ReLU）。The policy network is implemented as a fully connected feedforward neural network with three layers of sizes 512, 256, and 128. 29 维动作向量由 16 维潜在动作、12 维辅助动作向量和一维步态选择逻辑组成。潜在动作从学习的高斯分布中采样，并通过预训练的解码器以生成扭矩命令。辅助作用细化了解码的扭矩。步态选择 logit 通过 sigmoid 函数来计算步态选择输出，该输出用于在两个离散步态之间进行选择。The 29-dimensional action vector consists of a 16-dimensional latent action, a 12-dimensional auxiliary action vector, and a one-dimensional gait-selection logit.
