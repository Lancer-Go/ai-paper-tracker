We show that this approach systematically fails to generalize, and trace the failure to模拟器崩溃simulator collapse：因为模拟器 LLM 是模式崩溃的，所以针对它训练的 LLM 策略会过度拟合利用模拟器主导模式的狭窄策略，并且这种策略很难迁移到看不见的模拟器和真实用户。我们从理论上形式化了这种崩溃，并提出了两种互补的解决方案，一种在推理时，一种在训练时。推理时间解决方案，: because the simulator LLM is mode-collapsed, an LLM policy trained against it overfits to narrow strategies that exploit the simulator’s dominant mode, and such a policy transfers poorly to unseen simulators and real users.
