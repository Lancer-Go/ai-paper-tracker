1 简介Introduction

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 在理解从自然场景分析到医学成像等不同领域的视觉内容方面表现出了令人印象深刻的能力Vision Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated impressive capabilities in understanding visual content across diverse domains, from natural scene analysis to medical imagingRadford et al. (2021); Yu et al. (2022); Liu et al. (2023b, 2024); Bai et al. (2025); Li et al. (2025); Liu et al. (2025)。最近，它们与文化遗产应用程序的集成速度迅速加快，部署范围涵盖数字博物馆藏品、教育平台和自动编目系统. Recently, their integration into cultural heritage applications has accelerated rapidly, with deployments spanning digital museum collections, educational platforms, and automated cataloging systemsLi et al. (2024a); Hwang et al. (2025); Trichopoulos (2023); Arnold and Tilton (2024); Ghaboura et al. (2025)。然而，随着这些模型越来越多地在公众和文化文物之间发挥中介作用，我们发现了一个关键的差距：它们倾向于通过不适当的时间镜头来解释历史文物，我们将这种现象定义为. However, as these models increasingly mediate between the public and cultural artifacts, we identify a critical gap: their tendency to interpret historical artifacts through inappropriate temporal lenses, a phenomenon we define as文化不合时宜cultural anachronism.

VLM 中的文化时代错误代表了概念、技术、材料或解释框架对不存在或与文化无关的时期的文物的系统性错误归因。例如，当 VLM 使用 19 世纪欧洲背景中出现的艺术词汇描述公元前 3 世纪的雕塑时，将制造技术归因于几千年后才发展起来的古代陶器，或者通过当代而非适合时期的符号框架来解释宗教图像Cultural anachronism in VLMs represents the systematic misattribution of concepts, techniques, materials, or interpretive frameworks to artifacts from time periods where they did not exist or were not culturally relevant. For example, when a VLM describes a sculpture from the 3rd century BCE using artistic vocabulary that emerged in 19th century European contexts, attributes manufacturing techniques to ancient pottery that were only developed millennia later, or interprets religious iconography through contemporary rather than period-appropriate symbolic frameworksLiang et al. (2022); Bhatia et al. (2024); Gallegos et al. (2024); Ko et al. (2023)。这种时间概念的位移对博物馆数字化、教育技术和文化遗产保护的应用提出了重大挑战，其中历史再现的准确性至关重要. Such temporal-conceptual displacement poses a significant challenges for applications in museum digitization, educational technologies, and cultural heritage preservation, where accuracy in historical representation is paramountSiliutina et al. (2024); Hwang et al. (2025); Trichopoulos (2023); Arnold and Tilton (2024)。与可能造成轻微不便的一般物体识别错误不同，不合时宜的解释从根本上歪曲了文物的文化和历史意义，可能会强化历史叙述中的殖民或当代偏见，并破坏文化遗产文献的完整性. Unlike general object recognition errors that may cause minor inconveniences, anachronistic interpretations fundamentally misrepresent the cultural and historical significance of artifacts, potentially reinforcing colonial or contemporary biases in historical narratives and undermining the integrity of cultural heritage documentationBirhane et al. (2021); Thylstrup (2022).

VLM 中时间推理的挑战超出了简单的日期识别范围，涵盖了对跨历史时期的技术演变、艺术发展和文化背景的复杂理解。虽然现有基准评估 VLM 在当代视觉理解任务上的表现The challenge of temporal reasoning in VLMs extends beyond simple date recognition to encompass complex understanding of technological evolution, artistic development, and cultural context across historical periods. While existing benchmarks evaluate VLMs on contemporary visual understanding tasksLi et al. (2025); Chang et al. (2024)，没有人系统地评估他们在解释历史材料时保持时间连贯性的能力。鉴于文化遗产应用越来越依赖人工智能系统，这种差距尤其巨大，其中误解的风险远远超出了技术性能指标，还包括文化代表性、教育准确性和历史知识的保存。由于 VLM 主要接受当代视觉数据的训练，因此在遇到历史文物时会对现代解释框架产生固有的偏见，这一事实使问题变得更加复杂。, none systematically assess their ability to maintain temporal coherence when interpreting historical materials. This gap is particularly substantial given the increasing reliance on AI systems for cultural heritage applications, where the stakes of misinterpretation extend far beyond technical performance metrics to encompass cultural representation, educational accuracy, and preservation of historical knowledge. The problem is compounded by the fact that VLMs are predominantly trained on contemporary visual data, creating inherent biases toward modern interpretive frameworks when encountering historical artifacts.

3 TAB-VLM

我们的基准 TAB-VLM 重点关注印度文化遗产。我们整理了多样化的工件数据集，并构建了时间推理任务，揭示了六个维度上的不合时宜的响应：(1) 时间顺序，(2) 周期入侵检测（奇数周期），(3) 材料可用性，(4) 制造技术，(5) 基于周期的分组，以及 (6) 风格周期归因。 TAB-VLM 提供了一个用于评估多模式模型中的历史意识的结构化框架。Our benchmark TAB-VLM focuses on Indian cultural heritage. We curate a diverse dataset of artifacts and construct temporal reasoning tasks that uncover anachronistic responses across six dimensions: (1) Chronological Sequence, (2) Period Intrusion Detection (Odd-One-Out Period), (3) Material Availability, (4) Manufacturing Technique, (5) Period-Based Grouping, and (6) Style Period Attribution. TAB-VLM offers a structured framework for assessing historical awareness in multimodal models.

The model must identify the artifact that does not fit, based on subtle visual cues that indicate differences in era or context. Find the one that belongs to different time period."任务结构：Task structure:4 个选项，1 个正确答案。4 options with one correct answer.

4.2 评估指标Evaluation Metric

我们使用准确性作为主要指标来评估模型性能，计算为每个任务类别和总体中正确回答问题的比例。对于单选问题（风格周期归因、奇数周期检测），准确性表示预测答案与真实答案之间的精确匹配。对于多项选择题（制造技术、材料可用性），我们需要精确的设置匹配，必须选择所有正确的选项，并且不能选择错误的选项。对于基于序列的任务（时间顺序），准确性衡量精确的顺序匹配，而对于分组任务（周期分组），我们需要精确识别来自同一历史时期的所有三个工件。通过多次评估运行计算标准差，以评估结果的稳定性。We evaluate model performance using accuracy as the primary metric, calculated as the proportion of correctly answered questions within each task category and overall. For single-choice questions (Style-Period Attribution, Odd-One-Out Period Detection), accuracy represents exact match between predicted and ground truth answers. For multi-choice questions (Manufacturing Technique, Material Availability), we require exact set matching where all correct options must be selected and no incorrect options chosen. For sequence-based tasks (Chronological Sequencing), accuracy measures exact ordering match, while for grouping tasks (Period Grouping), we require precise identification of all three artifacts from the same historical period. Standard deviation is computed across multiple evaluation runs to assess result stability.

5 讨论Discussion

我们的评估揭示了当前 VLM 对历史文物进行时间推理的能力的根本局限性。我们通过定量结果和定性误差分析来解决我们的三个研究问题，为多模式人工智能系统中文化不合时宜的本质提供诊断见解。Our evaluation reveals fundamental limitations in current VLMs’ ability to reason temporally about historical artifacts. We address our three research questions through quantitative results and qualitative error analysis, providing diagnostic insights into the nature of cultural anachronism in multimodal AI systems.

5.1 模型架构中文化不合时宜的程度Extent of Cultural Anachronism Across Model Architectures

针对 RQ1，当前最先进的 VLM 在解释历史文物时表现出严重的文化过时性，即使是性能最好的模型 (GPT-5.2) 也只能实现 58.7% 的总体准确率，比 12.8% 的随机基线略有改善。这个 45.9 个百分点的差距虽然很大，但表明即使是前沿模型在时间推理方面也存在很大困难。不同模型架构和规模之间表现不佳的一致性表明，文化不合时宜代表了根本性限制，而不是规模问题。值得注意的是，GPT-4o 相对于 GPT-4o-mini 的边际优势（50.4% vs 47.2%），以及 Qwen2.5-VL-3B 在多个类别中与更大模型的竞争表现，表明架构创新和训练方法可能比原始参数计数更重要。这些发现与 VLM 在当代视觉理解基准上的普遍强劲表现形成鲜明对比，表明对历史背景的时间推理需要与当前训练范式所强调的能力根本不同的能力。Addressing RQ1, current state-of-the-art VLMs exhibit substantial cultural anachronism when interpreting historical artifacts, with even the best-performing model (GPT-5.2) achieving only 58.7% overall accuracy, a modest improvement over the random baseline of 12.8%. 5.3 对开发和部署的影响Implications for Development and Deployment

Expert annotations, though rigorously validated, reflect particular scholarly perspectives and discrete temporal classifications that may not accommodate transitional or culturally mixed artifacts.
