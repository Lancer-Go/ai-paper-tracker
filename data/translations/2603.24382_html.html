MolEvolve：法学硕士引导的可解释分子优化的进化搜索MolEvolve: LLM-Guided Evolutionary Search for Interpretable Molecular Optimization

Xiangsen Chen    Ruilong Wu    Yanyan Lan    Ting Ma    Yang Liu
摘要Abstract

尽管深度学习在化学领域取得了成功，但其影响力因缺乏可解释性和无法解决“活动悬崖”而受到阻碍，在“活动悬崖”中，微小的结构细微差别会引发剧烈的性质变化。当前的表示学习受相似性原则的约束，通常无法捕获这些结构活动的不连续性。为了解决这个问题，我们引入了 MolEvolve，这是一个进化框架，它将分子发现重新表述为一个自主的、前瞻性的规划问题。 与依赖于人工设计特征或严格先验知识的传统方法不同，MolEvolve 利用大型语言模型 (LLM) 积极探索和发展可执行化学符号操作库。通过利用 LLM 冷启动和蒙特卡罗树搜索 (MCTS) 引擎通过外部工具（例如 RDKit）进行测试时间规划，系统可以自主发现最佳轨迹。这个过程形成了透明的推理链，将复杂的结构转变转化为可操作的、人类可读的化学见解。实验结果表明，MolEvolve 的自主搜索不仅能够形成透明的、人类可读的化学见解，而且在性质预测和分子优化任务方面都优于基线。Despite deep learning’s success in chemistry, its impact is hindered by a lack of interpretability and an inability to resolve ’activity cliffs’, where minor structural nuances trigger drastic property shifts. Current representation learning, bound by the similarity principle, often fails to capture these structural-activity discontinuities. To address this, we introduce MolEvolve, an evolutionary framework that reformulates molecular discovery as an autonomous, look-ahead planning problem. Unlike traditional methods that depend on human-engineered features or rigid prior knowledge, MolEvolve leverages a Large Language Model (LLM) to actively explore and evolve a library of executable chemical symbolic operations. By utilizing the LLM to cold start and an Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) engine for test-time planning with external tools (e.g. RDKit), the system self-discovers optimal trajectories autonomously. This process evolves transparent reasoning chains that translate complex structural transformations into actionable, human-readable chemical insights. Experimental results demonstrate that MolEvolve’s autonomous search not only evolves transparent, human-readable chemical insights, but also outperforms baselines in both property prediction and molecule optimization tasks.

1 简介Introduction

深度学习已经成为分子相关任务从手动实验到以数据为中心的发现的范式转变。在这个领域，深度学习模型，特别是图神经网络（GNN），通过对分子的几何拓扑进行建模，表现出了卓越的性能Deep learning has become a paradigm shift in molecule-related tasks from manual experimentation to data-centric discovery. In this domain, deep learning models, especially Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), have demonstrated remarkable performance by modeling the geometric topology of molecules(Stärk et al., 2022; Li et al., 2023)。 分子数据（例如原子和键）预训练等技术进一步巩固了 GNN 作为各种属性预测任务的标准. Techniques such as pre-training on molecular data (e.g., atoms and bonds) have further solidified GNNs as the standard for diverse property prediction tasks(Stuyver and Coley, 2022; Cremer et al., 2023)。最近，大型语言模型（LLM）在科学任务中显示出巨大的前景。特定领域的法学硕士. More recently, Large Language Models (LLMs) show great promise in scientific tasks. Domain-specific LLMs(Taylor et al., 2022; Liu et al., 2024)和检索增强生成（RAG）方法and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) methods(Xian et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025)利用参数化领域知识和大量外部文献来捕获深度学习模型可能错过的知识和语义理解。leverage parametric domain knowledge and vast corpora of external literature to capture knowledge and semantic understanding that deep learning models may miss.

尽管这些现有方法具有多种能力，但仍面临很大的局限性。尽管 GNN 等深度学习模型在分子数据挖掘方面具有强大的功能，但它们仍以黑匣子的形式运行In spite of their capabilities, these existing methods face significant limitations. Deep learning models like GNNs operate as black boxes despite their powerful capabilities in molecular data mining(Li et al., 2023; Lavecchia, 2025; Stegmann et al., 2023)。他们通常无法提供准确的特征属性来解释哪些特定子结构有助于给定的分子特性。这降低了模型的科学可解释性。此外，基于 GNN 的解释（例如，注意力权重）充当事后近似，而不是内在逻辑. They are often unable to provide accurate feature attributions to explain which specific substructures contribute to a given molecular property. This reduces the scientific interpretability of the models. In addition, GNN-based explanations (e.g., attention weights) serve as post-hoc approximations rather than intrinsic logic(Stegmann et al., 2023; He et al., 2024)。另一方面，法学硕士即使在特定领域的知识上进行了微调，也容易产生幻觉. On the other hand, LLMs, even when fine-tuned on domain-specific knowledge, are prone to hallucination(Suzgun et al., 2025)。法学硕士很容易产生看似合理但实际上不正确的科学知识或属性. LLMs are prone to generate plausible but factually incorrect scientific knowledge or properties(Truhn et al., 2023)，并且他们经常难以预测准确的定量结果, and they often struggle to predict accurate quantitative results(Wang et al., 2025).

以前的方法通常将预测精度和可解释性视为单独的目标Previous approaches often treat predictive precision and interpretability as separate objectives(Oviedo et al., 2022)。因此，仍然存在一个关键差距：科学家不仅需要知道最终结果，还需要知道使分子拥有某种特性的明确因果规则. Consequently, a critical gap remains: scientists need to know not just the final results, but the explicit causal rules that enable a molecule to possess a certain property(Mengaldo, 2024)。 我们认为，实现这种可解释精度的困难源于基本的表示精度悖论。 如附录所示. We argue that the difficulty in achieving such interpretable precision arises from a fundamental representation-precision paradox. As demonstrated in AppendixB和图and Figure1，分子特性通常表现出高利普希茨常数，称为“活性悬崖”，其中微小的结构细微差别会引发剧烈的特性变化。 这一挑战源于分子流形和连续语义流形之间严重的拓扑不匹配。最近的工作表明，LLM 内部表示最好表征为流形，其中接近度反映语义相似性，而不是物理属性所需的精确欧几里得距离, molecular properties often exhibit high Lipschitz constants, known as ”activity cliffs,” where minor structural nuances trigger drastic property shifts. This challenge arises from the severe topological mismatch between molecular manifolds and continuous semantic manifolds. Recent work suggests that LLM internal representations are best characterized as manifolds where proximity reflects semantic similarity, rather than the precise Euclidean distances required for physical properties(Modell et al., 2025)。法学硕士试图将离散的化学标记映射到连续的物理流形上，这导致他们难以完成精确的定量任务. LLMs attempt to map discrete chemical tokens onto continuous physical manifolds, which causes them to struggle with precise quantitative tasks(Wang et al., 2025; Truhn et al., 2023)。 虽然 GNN 可以拟合这些流形，但它们的推理仍然是一个不透明的黑匣子. While GNNs can fit these manifolds, their reasoning remains an opaque black box(Stegmann et al., 2023)。 为了解决这一悖论，我们认为必须超越连续嵌入并在可执行符号逻辑的空间内进行操作。与平滑向量不同，离散符号规则可以自然地对非线性不连续性（例如逻辑分支）进行建模。 一个基本的缺失部分是主动搜索和验证显式化学规则的机制，从而将化学特征工程转变为动态的、目标导向的推理任务。. To resolve this paradox, we argue that one must transcend continuous embeddings and operate within a space of executable symbolic logic. Unlike smooth vectors, discrete symbolic rules can naturally model non-linear discontinuities (e.g., logic branches). A fundamental missing piece is a mechanism to actively search for and verify explicit chemical rules, thereby transforming chemical feature engineering into a dynamic, goal-directed reasoning task.
