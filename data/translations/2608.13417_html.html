超越最终分数：对长期人工智能研究和开发的代理进行系统评估Beyond Final Scores: A Systematic Evaluation of Agents for Long-Horizon AI Research and Development

摘要Abstract

自主代理越来越有能力通过长期实验来改进模型、系统和其他技术工件。 然而，要了解这种能力的当前状态，评估必须超越最终分数，最终分数既不能揭示取得或失去的进展，也不能表明积累的经验是否会改善以后的决策。 因此，我们基于一个新框架，对 36 个长期任务的七个前沿模型进行了系统评估，该框架使用基于规则的指标，通过解决方案框架、执行和反馈控制来描述运行内行为，并进行受控比较，以评估任务内和任务间的经验重用。 结果表明，当前智能体的运作方式更像是工程优化器，而不是完全自主的研究人员：它们可以制定和实施实用的解决方案，但它们的性能在不同的运行中差异很大，它们最强的解决方案主要适应或结合现有的技术，真正的方法论新颖性仍然很少。 详细分析表明，观察到的性能是由多种因素决定的，包括相似最终结果背后的不同流程瓶颈、可能有助于或误导后续决策的经验重用，以及影响性能稳定性的利用设计。 这些发现为改进模型训练、推理时间策略、经验管理和线束设计提出了具体方向。Autonomous agents are increasingly capable of improving models, systems, and other technical artifacts through long-horizon experimentation. To understand the current state of this capability, however, evaluation must go beyond final scores, which neither reveal where progress is gained or lost nor indicate whether accumulated experience improves later decisions. We therefore present a systematic evaluation of seven frontier models on 36 long-horizon tasks based on a new framework that uses rule-based metrics to characterize within-run behavior through Solution Framing, Execution, and Feedback Control and controlled comparisons to assess experience reuse within and across tasks. The results show that current agents operate more like engineering optimizers than fully autonomous researchers: they can formulate and implement practical solutions, but their performance varies substantially across runs, their strongest solutions mainly adapt or combine established techniques, and genuine methodological novelty remains rare. Detailed analysis reveals that observed performance is shaped by multiple factors, including distinct process bottlenecks behind similar final outcomes, experience reuse that can help or mislead subsequent decisions, and harness designs that affect performance stability. These findings suggest concrete directions for improving model training, inference-time strategies, experience management, and harness design.

1 简介Introduction

前沿语言模型越来越有能力进行长期自动化研究、反复提出更改、运行实验、解释反馈和完善可执行工件Frontier language models are increasingly capable of conducting long-horizon automated research, repeatedly proposing changes, running experiments, interpreting feedback, and refining executable artifacts(12; 29; 30)。 通过要求智能体优化模型、算法或计算系统，这些任务可以提供一种可衡量的 AI-for-AI 形式，并提供一个早期窗口来了解前沿语言模型与实现递归自我改进的接近程度. By requiring agents to optimize models, algorithms, or computing systems, these tasks can provide a measurable form of AI-for-AI and an early window into how close frontier language models are to enabling recursive self-improvement(3; 23; 18)。 因此，系统地评估当前智能体对于了解其研究能力并指导模型和智能体系统的有针对性的改进至关重要. Systematically evaluating current agents is therefore essential for understanding their research capabilities and guiding targeted improvements to both models and agent systems(29; 20).

然而，自动化研究的过程与其评估之间仍然存在根本性的不匹配：智能体从事长期、闭环的实验和改进周期，但现有的基准主要使用单一的最终分数来评估它们，无法捕捉模型行为的根本原因或提供细粒度的诊断信息However, there remains a fundamental mismatch between the process of automated research and its evaluation: agents engage in long-horizon, closed-loop cycles of experimentation and refinement, yet existing benchmarks primarily evaluate them using a single final score, which fails to capture the underlying reasons for model behavior or provide fine-grained diagnostic information(12; 3; 29)。 例如，在一次跑步中，相同的最终得分可能来自早期确定的有效方向，也可能来自经过大量尝试和错误后发现的方向。 它还没有显示提出的想法是否被转化为可靠的实施，或者反馈是否被有效地利用来保持进展并从失败中恢复。 除了基于最终分数的评估的局限性之外，传统评估独立对待每次运行，使能力显得静态，并模糊了积累的经验是否会改善或误导后续决策。 它还不清楚周围的安全带是否有助于智能体在长期研究过程中维持有效的行为。 这些差距使得很难确定当前智能体在自主研究方面取得了多大进展，以及进一步的改进是否应该针对模型功能、经验重用或利用设计。. Within a run, for example, the same final score may come from an effective direction identified early or from one found after extensive trial and error. It also does not show whether proposed ideas are translated into reliable implementations or whether feedback is used effectively to retain progress and recover from failures. Beyond the limitations of final-score-based evaluation, conventional evaluation treats each run independently, making capability appear static and obscuring whether accumulated experience improves or misleads subsequent decisions. It also leaves unclear whether the surrounding harness helps the agent sustain effective behavior over a long research run. These gaps make it difficult to determine how far current agents have progressed toward autonomous research and whether further improvements should target model capabilities, experience reuse, or harness design.

为了弥补这些差距，并为从事长期人工智能研发的代理商提供系统的评估框架，我们围绕四个问题组织评估：To address these gaps and provide a systematic evaluation framework for agents engaged in long-horizon AI R&D, we organize the evaluation around four questions: ❶ 当前代理商产生的最终结果有多强？How strong are the final results produced by current agents? ❷ 研究循环中的进展在哪里获得或失去？Where is progress gained or lost within the research loop? ❸ 积累的经验能否改善后续决策？Can accumulated experience improve subsequent decisions? ❹ 线束选择如何影响代理性能？How does harness choice affect agent performance?

具体来说，最终表现是直接根据任务分数来衡量的。 为了诊断运行中的行为，我们将研究过程分解为三个互补的功能：解决方案框架 (C1)、执行 (C2) 和反馈控制 (C3)。 对于每项功能，我们设计了一个基于规则的指标，该指标根据验证者的结果和记录的轨迹信号而不是法学硕士的判断来确定性地计算。 除了这些运行内能力之外，我们还将使用累积经验的能力视为元能力（M），并进行受控比较，以衡量其对任务内和任务间设置中后续决策的影响。 图Specifically, final performance is measured directly from task scores. To diagnose behavior within a run, we decompose the research process into three complementary capabilities: Solution Framing (C1), Execution (C2), and Feedback Control (C3). For each capability, we design a rule-based metric computed deterministically from verifier outcomes and recorded trajectory signals rather than LLM judgments. Beyond these within-run capabilities, we treat the ability to use accumulated experience as a meta-capability (M) and conduct controlled comparisons to measure its effect on subsequent decisions in intra- and inter-task settings. 受益于我们的评估设计，可以对模型能力进行细粒度诊断，我们可以对当前能力阶段进行整体评估：Benefiting from our evaluation design, which enables fine-grained diagnosis of model capabilities, we reach an overall assessment of the current capability stage:当前的自动化研究代理的运作方式更像是工程优化器，而不是完全自主的研究人员。Current automated research agents operate more like engineering optimizers than fully autonomous researchers.在有限的研究循环内，他们可以制定实用方向、实施工作解决方案并改进技术工件。 然而，它们的成功因运行而异，真正的算法创新仍然很少，实现的性能是由流程瓶颈、积累的经验和线束设计决定的。 这个结论的证据有三个：Within bounded research loops, they can formulate practical directions, implement working solutions, and improve technical artifacts. 2 评估设置和结果层面的景观Evaluation Setting and Outcome-Level Landscape

我们首先使用共享工具和协议评估相同任务的七个前沿模型，以建立最终性能和资源使用的受控比较。 这种结果层面的景观锚定了我们对研究行为、经验重用、利用效果和解决方案新颖性的后续分析。We first evaluate seven frontier models on the same tasks with a shared harness and protocol to establish a controlled comparison of final performance and resource use. This outcome-level landscape anchors our subsequent analyses of research behavior, experience reuse, harness effects, and solution novelty.

3 过程级评估Process-Level Evaluation

3.1 过程评估设计Process Evaluation Design

自动化研发通过重复轮次进行，其中代理提出方向，实施相应的改变，观察结果，并决定如何进行。仅凭最终分数无法确定此循环在哪里成功或失败。因此，我们将流程分解为解决方案框架 (C1)、执行 (C2) 和反馈控制 (C3)。这种分解遵循循环的因果结构：C1 评估智能体选择追求的内容，C2 评估该选择是否转化为有效结果，C3 评估后续决策如何使用实验反馈。这些阶段代表不同的故障源，因此需要不同形式的改进。Automated R&D proceeds through repeated rounds in which an agent proposes a direction, implements the corresponding change, observes the result, and decides how to proceed. A final score alone cannot identify where this loop succeeds or fails. We therefore decompose the process into Solution Framing (C1), Execution (C2), and Feedback Control (C3). This decomposition follows the causal structure of the loop: C1 evaluates what the agent chooses to pursue, C2 evaluates whether that choice is translated into a valid result, and C3 evaluates how subsequent decisions use experimental feedback. These stages represent distinct failure sources and therefore require different forms of improvement.

与仅提供最终输出反馈的传统一次性任务不同，这里研究的迭代研究任务在每个评估的检查点公开明确的验证者反馈。这些阶梯级信号提供了进展和失败的直接证据，使我们能够在不依赖主观模型判断的情况下评估研究过程。因此，我们根据记录的评估信号确定性地计算所有三个分数，如下所述。生成的指标是可重复且可审计的。Unlike conventional one-shot tasks that provide feedback only on the final output, the iterative research tasks studied here expose explicit verifier feedback at each evaluated checkpoint. These step-level signals provide direct evidence of progress and failure, allowing us to evaluate the research process without relying on subjective model judgments. We therefore compute all three scores deterministically from recorded evaluation signals, as detailed below. The resulting metrics are reproducible and auditable.

C1：解决方案框架。C1: Solution Framing.C1 询问智能体追求的方向是否能快速产生强有力的解决方案。 它不是判断一个提案听起来有多复杂，而是使用运行中的最佳验证者分数作为迄今为止发现的方向质量的客观代理。 轨迹被映射到一个共同的地平线，较短的跑步将其最后一次跑步的最佳成绩向前推进，而较长的跑步则使用共享的截止点。 我们总结了这一视野的早期、中期和晚期的进展，因此分数既奖励达到高分，又奖励尽早达到目标，同时防止后来的失败抹掉早期的发现。C1 asks whether the directions an agent pursues lead quickly to a strong solution. Rather than judging how sophisticated a proposal sounds, it uses the running best verifier score as an objective proxy for the quality of the directions discovered so far. Trajectories are mapped to a common horizon, with shorter runs carrying their last running best forward and longer runs using a shared cutoff. We summarize progress across the early, middle, and late parts of this horizon, so the score rewards both reaching a high score and reaching it early while preventing later failures from erasing an earlier discovery.

C2：执行。C2: Execution.C2 询问代理是否可靠地将提议的更改转换为可执行且正确的结果。 在每个非初始评估的检查点，交付门首先检查工件是否运行，并且当任务提供正确性判定时，检查它是否正确。 失败的交付不会获得任何积分，而成功的交付则会根据在该检查点之前观察到的与代码相关的构建失败进行折扣。 折扣是有限的，因此提供有效的结果仍然是主要要求，并且排除了由环境引起的失败。C2 asks whether an agent reliably translates proposed changes into executable and correct results. At each non-initial evaluated checkpoint, a delivery gate first checks whether the artifact runs and, when the task provides a correctness verdict, whether it is correct. Failed delivery receives no credit, while successful delivery is discounted according to the code-related build failures observed before that checkpoint. The discount is bounded so that delivering a valid result remains the primary requirement, and failures caused by the environment are excluded.

C3：反馈控制。C3: Feedback Control.C3 询问代理是否保留成功的发现，并在尝试的更改使结果变得更糟时是否做出有效响应。 其保留部分将最终得分与跑步期间达到的最高步数得分进行比较。 对于每个有意义的回归，其恢复组件测量恢复了多少丢失的分数以及恢复需要多少个评估的转换，以及额外的自我评估尝试，对隐藏的试验和错误应用有界惩罚。 这两个部分共同奖励保持强劲成果和纠正挫折；当没有发生回归时，C3 仅使用保留，因为未测试恢复。C3 asks whether an agent preserves successful discoveries and responds effectively when an attempted change makes the result worse. Its retention component compares the final score with the highest step score reached during the run. For each meaningful regression, its recovery component measures how much of the lost score is recovered and how many evaluated transitions the recovery requires, with additional self-evaluated attempts applying a bounded penalty for hidden trial and error. The two components jointly reward preserving strong results and correcting setbacks; when no regression occurs, C3 uses retention alone because recovery was not tested.

7 讨论Discussion

我们的结果表明，当前代理的局限性无法通过单一优化策略来解决。 不同的故障模式需要对模型训练、推理时间策略、长期系统设计或评估目标本身进行相应的改变。Our results suggest that the limitations of current agents cannot be addressed through a single optimization strategy. Different failure patterns require corresponding changes to model training, inference-time strategies, long-horizon system design, or the evaluation objective itself.

培训可以改进什么What Training Can Improve

我们的流程分析表明，培训应针对每个模型和任务类别的具体弱点进行定制。 执行已经很强大并且跨模型紧密聚集，因此单独的通用代码执行训练不太可能成为主要的全领域机会。 解决方案框架和反馈控制的差异更大，这表明在方向选择和反馈使用方面特定于模型的改进范围更大。 同样，根据主要瓶颈是否在于框架、实施或反馈控制，不同任务类别的培训优先级也应有所不同。 这些过程指标可以指导有针对性的培训数据、过程奖励和课程的构建，而不仅仅是最终奖励本身提供的内容。 经验实验提供了额外的训练信号：正迁移和负迁移的配对案例可以帮助模型学习何时适用先前的经验以及何时应重新考虑。Our process analysis suggests that training should be tailored to the specific weaknesses of each model and task category. Execution is already strong and tightly clustered across models, so generic code-execution training alone is unlikely to be the primary field-wide opportunity. Solution Framing and Feedback Control vary more widely, indicating greater scope for model-specific improvements in direction selection and feedback use. Training priorities should likewise vary across task categories according to whether the main bottleneck lies in framing, implementation, or feedback control. These process metrics can guide the construction of targeted training data, process rewards, and curricula beyond what final reward alone provides. The experience experiments offer an additional training signal: paired cases of positive and negative transfer could help models learn when prior experience is applicable and when it should be reconsidered.

推理时间搜索可以恢复什么What Inference-Time Search Can Recover

除了训练之外，推理时间策略还提供了一种直接方法来提高模型实现其现有功能的可靠性。 平均和最佳运行性能之间的对比表明，一些模型可以达到有竞争力的解决方案，但不能一致地重现它们。 这创造了一个机会来产生更多样化的推出并使用验证者反馈来识别有希望的轨迹。 不必为每个轨迹分配固定预算，而是可以通过从有希望的检查点分支并终止反复失败或停滞的轨迹来重定向计算。 流程诊断可以通过在解决方案框架薄弱时鼓励更广泛的探索以及在执行或反馈控制受到限制时鼓励更深入的实施或恢复来进一步指导这种分配。 轨迹选择可以将验证者的结果与执行有效性和进度保留结合起来，以避免单独依赖最终奖励。Beyond training, inference-time strategies offer a direct way to improve how reliably models realize their existing capabilities. The contrast between average and best-run performance shows that several models can reach competitive solutions but do not reproduce them consistently. This creates an opportunity to generate more diverse rollouts and use verifier feedback to identify promising trajectories. Instead of assigning every trajectory a fixed budget, compute could be redirected by branching from promising checkpoints and terminating trajectories that repeatedly fail or stagnate. Process diagnostics could further guide this allocation by encouraging broader exploration when Solution Framing is weak and deeper implementation or recovery when Execution or Feedback Control is limiting. Trajectory selection could combine verifier outcomes with execution validity and progress retention to avoid relying on final reward alone.

什么内存和线束设计可以稳定What Memory and Harness Design Can Stabilize

有些失败是由于在长期研究过程中保留和应用信息而产生的，而不是由于孤立地采取有效行动而产生的。 积累的经验通常有助于代理保留有用的发现并避免已知的失败，但它也可能会产生误导性的结论或将探索锚定到局部最优。 因此，一个有效的记忆系统需要的不仅仅是简单地存储额外的上下文。它必须支持根据当前任务选择性检索、验证、修改和删除经验。Some failures arise from retaining and applying information over a long research process rather than from generating an effective action in isolation. Accumulated experience usually helps agents preserve useful discoveries and avoid known failures, but it can also carry forward misleading conclusions or anchor exploration to a local optimum. An effective memory system therefore requires more than simply storing additional context. It must support the selective retrieval, validation, revision, and removal of experience according to the current task.

线束解决了长视野控制的补充问题。 原生安全带提高了运行间的稳定性，但并没有显着提高观察到的性能上限。 这种稳定性增益可能来自于在扩展实验期间减少可避免故障的机制，包括错误恢复、任务管理和最佳状态保护。 自动化线束优化提供了进一步的空间，包括针对不同研究瓶颈的特定任务线束和考虑工具使用、规划和恢复行为差异的模型自适应线束。Harnesses address a complementary aspect of long-horizon control. Native harnesses improve run-to-run stability but do not substantially raise the observed performance ceiling. This stability gain likely comes from mechanisms that reduce avoidable failures during extended experimentation, including error recovery, task management, and best-state protection. Automated harness optimization offers further headroom, including task-specific harnesses that target distinct research bottlenecks and model-adaptive harnesses that account for differences in tool use, planning, and recovery behavior.

需要新的目标和基准What Requires New Objectives and Benchmarks

当奖励捕获任务绩效而不是方法质量时，一些限制无法通过训练、推理时间策略、记忆或工具设计来解决。 当前的代理可以有效地执行和优化，但他们最强大的解决方案主要由现有技术组成，而经过验证的新颖方法仍然很少。 此外，当代理偏离标准解决方案时，特定于评估者的捷径比新方法更常见。 因此，对相同奖励进行更积极的优化可能会强化寻找捷径，而不是提高研究质量。 朝着更加开放式的研究和科学发现取得进展将需要任务和反馈，不仅奖励任务绩效，而且奖励新颖性、有效性和通用性。Some limitations cannot be resolved through training, inference-time strategies, memory, or harness design when the reward captures task performance but not methodological quality. Current agents execute and optimize effectively, yet their strongest solutions primarily compose established techniques, while validated novel approaches remain rare. Moreover, evaluator-specific shortcuts are substantially more common than novel approaches when agents depart from standard solutions. More aggressive optimization of the same reward may therefore reinforce shortcut-seeking rather than improve research quality. Progress toward more open-ended research and scientific discovery will require tasks and feedback that reward not only task performance, but also novelty, validity, and generality.

9 结论Conclusion

我们对长期汽车研究代理进行了系统评估，通过检查解决方案框架、执行、反馈控制、想法层面的新颖性、经验重用和利用效果，超越了最终分数。 结果使当前系统处于部分研究循环自动化阶段。 代理人可以识别实用的方法并实施它们，有时甚至可以达成有竞争力的解决方案，但他们最强大的行为并没有一致地再现，真正的创新仍然很少。 流程瓶颈因模型和工作负载而异，经验传递仍然不稳定，而利用主要提高现有能力的可靠实现。 这些区别将观察到的局限性映射到有针对性的训练、推理时间选择、选择性记忆、工具设计或更强大的任务和验证器。 由此产生的评估为改进汽车研究代理和跟踪更可靠和自我改进的研究系统的进展提供了具体的基础。We presented a systematic evaluation of long-horizon auto research agents that goes beyond final scores by examining Solution Framing, Execution, Feedback Control, idea-level novelty, experience reuse, and harness effects. The results place current systems at a stage of partial research-loop automation. Agents can identify practical approaches, implement them, and sometimes reach competitive solutions, but their strongest behavior is not reproduced consistently, and genuine innovation remains rare. Process bottlenecks vary across models and workloads, experience transfer remains unstable, and harnesses mainly improve the reliable realization of existing capability. These distinctions map observed limitations to targeted training, inference-time selection, selective memory, harness design, or stronger tasks and verifiers. The resulting evaluation provides a concrete basis for improving auto research agents and tracking progress toward more reliable and self-improving research systems.

局限性Limitations

过程度量范围。Process-metric scope.C1-C3 是基于验证者分数和执行信号的可重复代理，而不是基础研究能力的详尽定义。 它们无法捕获轨迹中不可靠可见的方面，例如未实现想法的语义质量或代理的潜在推理。 它们的证据强度还取决于运行期间观察到的事件：特别是，当轨迹包含很少或没有回归时，C3 无法有意义地衡量恢复。 因此，短或近乎单调的轨迹可能会获得较高的反馈控制分数，而不会表现出从重复挫折中恢复的能力。 这三个指标应与行为诊断和轨迹证据一起解释，而不是作为一般研究能力的独立衡量标准。C1–C3 are reproducible proxies grounded in verifier scores and execution signals rather than exhaustive definitions of the underlying research capabilities. They do not capture aspects that are not reliably visible in the trajectory, such as the semantic quality of an unrealized idea or the agent’s latent reasoning. Their evidential strength also depends on the events observed during a run: in particular, C3 cannot meaningfully measure recovery when a trajectory contains few or no regressions. A short or nearly monotone trajectory may therefore receive a high Feedback Control score without demonstrating recovery from repeated setbacks. The three metrics should be interpreted together with the behavioral diagnostics and trajectory evidence rather than as standalone measures of general research ability.

受控实验依赖性。Controlled-experiment dependence.我们的自我改进估计取决于用于隔离经验的干预措施，包括任务内擦除点、选定的源-目标对以及转移经验的表示。 这些控件支持评估设置内的因果比较，但它们并没有涵盖代理在较长的部署中可能积累、检索、修改或忘记经验的所有方式。 因此，不同的记忆系统或任务序列可能会产生对经验驱动的改进的不同估计。Our self-improvement estimates depend on the interventions used to isolate experience, including the intra-task erasure point, the selected source–target pairs, and the representation of transferred experience. These controls support causal comparisons within the evaluated settings, but they do not cover every way an agent might accumulate, retrieve, revise, or forget experience over a longer deployment. Different memory systems or task sequences may therefore produce different estimates of experience-driven improvement.

基准和线束依赖性。Benchmark and harness dependence.我们的结论是基于 AutoLab 的任务分配、资源预算、验证者和执行环境，并使用通用工具进行主要的跨模型比较。 尽管利用实验表明哪些结果在几种替代支架下是稳定的，但它们并没有耗尽提示、工具、上下文管理策略或模型-利用组合的空间。 在其他研究领域或系统配置下，绝对分数和一些相对排名可能会发生变化。Our conclusions are based on the task distribution, resource budgets, verifiers, and execution environment of AutoLab, with a common harness used for the main cross-model comparison. Although the harness experiments show which findings are stable under several alternative scaffolds, they do not exhaust the space of prompts, tools, context-management policies, or model–harness combinations. Absolute scores and some relative rankings may change under other research domains or system configurations.

成本可比性。Cost comparability.估计的推理成本取决于提供商定价、令牌会计和服务配置，所有这些都可能随着时间的推移而变化，或者因部署而异。 挂钟的使用还受到特定任务的预算和基础设施条件的影响。 因此，在我们的受控设置下进行比较时，成本结果最为可靠，不应被解释为通用部署价格。Estimated inference cost depends on provider pricing, token accounting, and serving configurations, all of which can change over time or differ across deployments. Wall-clock use is also affected by task-specific budgets and infrastructure conditions. Cost results are therefore most reliable for comparisons under our controlled setup and should not be interpreted as universal deployment prices.
