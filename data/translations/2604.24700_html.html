摘要Abstract

Guided by the PCS framework and developed in collaboration with practicing physicians, we instantiate Green Shielding in medical diagnosis by introducing HealthCareMagic-Diagnosis (HCM-Dx), a novel benchmark of patient-authored queries, and by constructing structured reference diagnosis sets and clinically grounded metrics for evaluating differential diagnosis lists (i.e., candidate diagnoses under consideration). We further study perturbation regimes that capture routine input variation, allowing us to characterize how prompt-level factors shift model behavior along clinically meaningful dimensions. Across multiple frontier LLMs, we find that these shifts trace out Pareto-like tradeoffs. In particular, neutralization, which removes common user-level factors from inputs while preserving clinical content, increases plausibility and yields more concise, clinician-like differentials, while reducing coverage of highly likely and safety-critical conditions. Together, these findings show that interaction choices can systematically shift task-relevant properties of model outputs, and that Green Shielding can help ground evidence-backed, user-facing guidance for safer deployment in high-stakes domains. Although instantiated here in medical diagnosis, this agenda extends naturally to other decision-support settings and to agentic AI systems, where small variations in user inputs may shape downstream model reasoning and actions. 1  简介1  Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在广泛的领域中展示了卓越的功能，加速了它们在现实世界中的采用。作为当代模型开发流程的标准实践，新版本通常伴随着广泛的技术报告，记录培训程序并总结流行基准和排行榜的表现Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities across a wide range of domains, accelerating their adoption in real-world settings. As a standard practice in contemporary model development pipelines, new releases are typically accompanied by extensive technical reports that document training procedures and summarize performance on popular benchmarks and leaderboards[1, 47, 3]，提供发布时功能的标准化快照。与此同时，LLM的输出被广泛认为是不可靠且基础不足的，表现出诸如幻觉内容等众所周知的问题, providing a standardized snapshot of capabilities at release. At the same time, LLM outputs are widely recognized to be unreliable and insufficiently grounded, exhibiting well-known issues such as hallucinated content[54, 24], 推理不一致, inconsistent reasoning[51, 28]，并以牺牲正确性为代价过度迎合用户偏好, and over-alignment to user preferences at the expense of correctness[41]。 LLM 提供者相应地包含模型可能出错的免责声明；然而，考虑到真实用户模型交互的复杂性，此类警告很少转化为有关有效使用或何时应信任输出的可操作指导。与此同时，研究界对红队投入了大量注意力，红队在对抗条件下探索模型以暴露漏洞并对安全边界进行压力测试；我们指的是对灾难性或最坏情况风险的广泛关注. LLM providers accordingly include disclaimers that models may make mistakes; however, given the complex nature of real user-model interactions, such warnings rarely translate into actionable guidance about effective use or when outputs should be trusted. In parallel, the research community has devoted substantial attention to red-teaming, which probes models under adversarial conditions to expose vulnerabilities and stress-test safety boundaries; we refer to this broad focus on catastrophic or worst-case risks[22] as 人工智能安全一AI Safety I。然而，由于大多数现实世界的使用都是非对抗性的，最坏情况的威胁模型对日常使用的重要问题的洞察力有限，例如查询和上下文中的常规变化如何塑造模型行为以及哪些交互策略可以带来更可靠的响应。我们将这些常规的、以用户为中心的风险称为. However, since most real-world use is non-adversarial, worst-case threat models provide limited insight into the questions that matter for everyday use, such as how routine variation in queries and context shapes model behavior and which interaction strategies lead to more reliable responses. We refer to these routine, user-centric risks as人工智能安全二AI Safety II。前沿法学硕士发展的实际现实进一步推动了对与部署相关的行为评估日益增长的需求：随着模型规模、专有数据和培训基础设施越来越多地使直接模型干预超出了大多数学术研究人员的能力范围，与部署相关条件下的严格行为评估成为科学贡献的一个特别高杠杆的方向。. This growing need for deployment-relevant behavioral assessment is further motivated by the practical realities of frontier LLM development: as model scale, proprietary data, and training infrastructure increasingly place direct model intervention beyond the reach of most academic researchers, rigorous behavioral assessment under deployment-relevant conditions becomes a particularly high-leverage direction for scientific contribution.

因此，我们提出“绿色屏蔽”，这是一项总体研究议程，旨在制定以用户为中心、有证据支持的指南，指导如何在实际部署中使用法学硕士，类似于客户期望的任何商业产品的说明手册。我们通过研究用户输入中良性的、部署现实的变化如何塑造模型行为，并通过实施可衡量的成功概念来为未来面向用户的指导奠定基础，从而推进这一议程。我们不是规定最佳实践，而是专注于建立推导它们所需的概念和经验基础，将法学硕士视为固定的、部署的产品，并分析它们在现实的、非对抗性输入变化下的行为。这种观点对红队进行了补充，红队主要探讨对抗性条件，重点关注稳定性属性、效用权衡以及日常使用中出现的用户相关风险。在医疗保健、教育和法律等高风险领域，建立实证基础来描述这些日常使用行为变得越来越有必要，在这些领域，不可靠的输出可能会直接影响后续决策和公众信任Therefore, we propose Green Shielding, an overarching research agenda for developing user-centric, evidence-backed guidance for how LLMs should be used in real deployments, analogous to an instruction manual that customers would expect for any commercial product. We advance this agenda by studying how benign, deployment-realistic variation in user inputs shapes model behavior and by operationalizing measurable notions of success to ground future user-facing guidance. Rather than prescribing best practices, we focus on establishing the conceptual and empirical basis required to derive them, treating LLMs as fixed, deployed products and analyzing their behaviors under realistic, non-adversarial input variation. This perspective complements red-teaming, which predominantly probes adversarial conditions, by centering the stability properties, utility tradeoffs, and user-relevant risks that emerge in everyday use. Building empirical foundations to characterize these everyday-use behaviors is increasingly necessary in high-stakes domains such as healthcare, education, and law, where unreliable outputs can directly affect consequential decisions and public trust[9, 49]。快速发展的治理格局进一步强调了这一需求，包括《欧盟人工智能法案》等全面监管. This need is further underscored by a rapidly evolving governance landscape, including comprehensive regulation such as the EU AI Act[2, 50]以及正式的评估指南，例如 NIST 的人工智能风险管理框架and formal evaluation guidance such as NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework[45]。绿色屏蔽还可以帮助弥合现代人工智能的三个相互竞争的社会技术想象. Green Shielding can also help bridge the three competing sociotechnical imaginaries of modern AI[34]：前沿AI，将AI视为技术神器；负责任的人工智能，将人工智能视为一个社会技术系统；务实的人工智能，其重点是对人民权利和监管的影响。我们设想绿色屏蔽将超越前沿式评估，提供与部署相关的证据，为组织实践和监管监督提供信息。: frontier AI, which treats AI as a technical artifact; responsible AI, which treats AI as a sociotechnical system; and pragmatic AI, which centers impacts on people’s rights and regulation. We envision Green Shielding as moving beyond frontier-style evaluation by producing deployment-relevant evidence that can inform both organizational practices and regulatory oversight.

这些标准共同为绿色屏蔽提供了初步的实证基础，并指导本文的研究设计。我们在法学硕士的医学诊断中实例化了这种方法，这是一个高风险领域，其中模型行为受到用户查询日常差异的强烈影响。Together, these criteria provide an initial empirical foundation for Green Shielding and guide the study design in this paper. We instantiate this approach in medical diagnosis with LLMs, a high-stakes domain in which model behavior is strongly influenced by everyday differences in user queries.

第一步，我们使用两个广泛采用的医学基准 MedQA 在受控环境中研究即时水平敏感性As a first step, we study prompt-level sensitivity in a controlled setting using two widely adopted medical benchmarks, MedQA[25]和 MedXpertQAand MedXpertQA[62]。使用开放式生成作为基线，我们沿着几个实际维度（内容、格式和语气）扰乱提示，并发现这些变化一致地改变了两个基准上的响应正确性。与此同时，这些实验凸显了自由形式医学评估的静态考试式基准的局限性：它们被设计为信息完整且单一答案，与现实世界的查询不同，现实世界的查询通常是嘈杂、模糊和不明确的。这激发了基准和评估程序，以更好地捕获诊断不确定性以及考虑多种合理诊断的需要。. Using open-ended generation as the baseline, we perturb prompts along several practical dimensions—content, format, and tone—and find that these changes consistently shift response correctness on both benchmarks. At the same time, these experiments highlight a limitation of static, exam-style benchmarks for free-form medical assessment: they are designed to be information-complete and single-answer, unlike real-world queries, which are often noisy, vague, and underspecified. This motivates benchmarks and evaluation procedures that better capture diagnostic uncertainty and the need to consider multiple plausible diagnoses.

Under raw patient inputs, models generate moderately sized differential diagnosis lists, with most proposed diagnoses falling within the clinically plausible set. However, coverage of the highly likely and safety-critical reference sets remains substantially lower, indicating that even frontier models do not reliably surface the most probable or cannot-miss conditions. Prompt neutralization induces a consistent and interpretable tradeoff across models: differentials become more concise and plausibility increases, but coverage of highly likely and safety-critical diagnoses declines. Comparisons with clinician responses show that physicians produce markedly narrower differentials, prioritizing a small number of leading hypotheses rather than exhaustive enumeration. Taken together, these results demonstrate that routine, non-adversarial differences in prompt formulation can substantially shift clinically meaningful properties of model outputs, and that our framework makes the resulting precision–coverage tradeoffs explicit rather than obscured by single-score evaluations. We do not interpret any single prompting condition as uniformly preferable; instead, different conditions correspond to different operating points, whose desirability depends on context, risk tolerance, and downstream decision-making. By making these tradeoffs measurable, our results motivate human-centered evaluation of usefulness, safety, and trust under realistic interaction settings.

As a standalone resource, HCM-Dx bridges the gap between conventional exam-style medical benchmarks and the ambiguity of real-world patient queries in a structured and efficient way. Although we focus on medical diagnosis as a case study in this work, Green Shielding extends naturally to other decision-support domains and, in future work, to multi-turn and more agentic settings, where interaction itself becomes a central determinant of downstream behavior.

6  讨论和限制6  Discussion and Limitations

我们的结果为 Green Shielding 作为以用户为中心的议程提供了初步支持，为 LLM 在部署中的使用制定有证据支持的指南。在医疗诊断环境中，用户呈现相同潜在病例的方式的实际变化可以系统地改变模型行为，揭示仅通过单一答案准确性无法捕获的权衡。同时，本案例研究强调了重要的局限性，并为未来的工作提出了具体方向。它还展示了如何通过可预测性、可计算性和稳定性 (PCS) 框架指导的程序对绿色屏蔽进行实证研究。Our results offer initial support for Green Shielding as a user-centric agenda for developing evidence-backed guidance for LLM use in deployment. In the medical diagnosis setting, realistic variation in how users present the same underlying case can systematically shift model behavior, revealing tradeoffs that are not captured by single-answer accuracy alone. At the same time, this case study highlights important limitations and suggests concrete directions for future work. It also shows how Green Shielding can be studied empirically through procedures guided by the Predictability, Computability, and Stability (PCS) framework.

解释观察到的权衡Interpreting the Observed Tradeoffs

值得注意的是，我们并不将中和提示条件解释为普遍优于原始条件。相反，我们的结果揭示了合理性、覆盖范围和广度等指标之间的系统权衡，不同的提示条件占据不同的操作点。哪一点更可取可能取决于用户、任务和部署环境，尤其是在高风险设置中，可能需要以不同方式平衡谨慎性、全面性和选择性。因此，未来工作的一个重要方向不仅是提高这些维度的绩效，而且还要确定其他与任务相关的评估标准，例如不确定性校准、可操作性和下游决策效用，并确定如何在实践中解决这些权衡。特别是，选择适当的操作点可能需要结合利益相关者的特定偏好和风险承受能力，例如通过临床医生或用户告知的效用函数，对不同类型的错误和遗漏进行不同的权重。It is important to note that we do not interpret the neutralized prompt condition as universally better than the raw one. Instead, our results reveal systematic tradeoffs across metrics such as plausibility, coverage, and breadth, with different prompt conditions occupying different operating points. Which point is preferable may depend on the user, task, and deployment context, especially in high-stakes settings where caution, comprehensiveness, and selectivity may need to be balanced differently. An important direction for future work is therefore not only to improve performance along these dimensions, but also to identify additional task-relevant evaluation criteria, such as uncertainty calibration, actionability, and downstream decision utility, and to determine how these tradeoffs should be resolved in practice. In particular, selecting an appropriate operating point will likely require incorporating stakeholder-specific preferences and risk tolerances, for example through clinician- or user-informed utility functions that weight different types of errors and omissions differently.

广泛的相关性Broad Relevance

尽管我们通过医疗诊断案例研究来实例化绿色屏蔽，但其基本议程超出了医学范围。更一般地说，我们研究用户呈现的良性但现实的变化如何影响用户在不确定性下寻求决策支持的环境中的系统行为。类似的依赖于交互的不稳定性也出现在法律和政策决策、教育和推荐等领域，这些领域的输出往往是开放式的、依赖于上下文的，并且不能被单一的正确答案很好地捕获。这种观点在最近向代理人工智能系统的转变中尤其重要。随着模型越来越多地充当在扩展交互中计划、行动和适应的助手，用户代理交互本身成为下游行为的核心决定因素。在这些设置中，用户表达目标、不确定性或上下文的方式的微小差异可能会通过多步骤推理或行动管道传播，并导致截然不同的结果，对安全、安保和隐私产生深远影响。Although we instantiate Green Shielding through a medical diagnosis case study, the underlying agenda goes beyond medicine. More generally, we study how benign but realistic variation in user presentation affects system behavior in settings where users seek decision support under uncertainty. Similar interaction-dependent instabilities arise in domains such as legal and policy decision-making, education, and recommendation, where outputs are often open-ended, context-dependent, and not well captured by a single correct answer. This perspective is especially relevant in the recent shift toward agentic AI systems. As models increasingly operate as assistants that plan, act, and adapt over extended interactions, user-agent interaction itself becomes a central determinant of downstream behavior. In these settings, small differences in how users express goals, uncertainty, or context may propagate through multi-step reasoning or action pipelines and lead to materially different outcomes, with profound implications for safety, security, and privacy.

任务制定的局限性Limitations of Task Formulation

我们的诊断案例研究的一个主要限制是，许多真实的患者询问是高度非结构化的并且临床上不明确。类似形式的不明确、用户驱动的输入也出现在教育、法律或政策决策等其他领域，学生可能会在没有澄清其当前理解或具体困惑点的情况下寻求帮助，或者用户可能会在没有指定管辖权、程序阶段或相关事实背景的情况下请求法律或政策指导。在这种情况下，仅根据初始查询请求鉴别诊断的价值可能有限，因为输入通常缺乏有意义的评估所需的信息。这就产生了过早关闭的风险：尽管信息不足，模型仍可能产生自信的输出，反映出奖励确定性的基准设置与通常要求承认、澄清或推迟不确定性的现实世界设置之间存在更广泛的不一致。在许多情况下，更合适的反应是承认信息不足、提出澄清问题或推迟诊断范围缩小。此外，在这些情况下，诊断并不总是最有用的目标。诸如分类紧急程度、处置或后续问题等输出可能更合适。进一步的限制是当前的基准是单轮的，而真正的诊断交互是迭代的。因此，将绿色屏蔽扩展到多轮设置对于临床现实性和评估模型是否可以从模糊或框架不良的初始输入中恢复都很重要。A central limitation of our diagnosis case study is that many real patient queries are highly unstructured and clinically underspecified. Similar forms of underspecified, user-driven input arise in other domains such as education and legal or policy decision-making, where a student may seek help without clarifying their current understanding or specific point of confusion, or a user may request legal or policy guidance without specifying the jurisdiction, procedural stage, or relevant factual background. In such cases, requesting a differential diagnosis from the initial query alone may be of limited value, because the input often lacks the information needed for a meaningful assessment. This creates a risk of premature closure: models may produce confident outputs despite insufficient information, reflecting a broader misalignment between benchmark settings that reward definitiveness and real-world settings that often call for uncertainty acknowledgment, clarification, or deferral. In many cases, the more appropriate response would be to acknowledge insufficient information, ask clarifying questions, or defer diagnostic narrowing. Furthermore, diagnosis is not always the most useful target in these settings; outputs such as triage urgency, disposition, or follow-up questions may be more appropriate. A further limitation is that the current benchmark is single-turn, whereas real diagnostic interactions are iterative. Extending Green Shielding to multi-turn settings is therefore important both for clinical realism and for evaluating whether models can recover from ambiguous or poorly framed initial inputs.

This tension is not specific to medical diagnosis: scalable evaluation may also miss important features of real-world use, such as adaptation to user context, incorporation of information revealed across turns, and appropriate requests for clarification before offering guidance. An important direction for future work is therefore to develop evaluation pipelines that better preserve the richness of real-world use while still enabling reliable, scalable, and interpretable comparisons.

专家分歧和规范不确定性Expert Disagreement and Normative Uncertainty

我们的初步临床医生验证还表明，专家的分歧本身就是不确定性的一个有意义的来源。在许多情况下，出现分歧似乎并不是因为参考集明显有缺陷，而是因为潜在的患者查询未指定。当可用信息稀疏或混乱时，即使是人类专家在决定哪些诊断属于鉴别诊断时也必须依赖主观推理。这一观察结果指出了评估决策支持系统面临的更广泛的挑战：标签中的一些不稳定性反映了任务中真正的模糊性，而不是简单的注释噪音。因此，未来的工作应该研究临床医生、专业和机构之间的稳定性，并更明确地检查实践中首选哪种权衡方案。Our preliminary clinician validation also suggests that expert disagreement is itself a meaningful source of uncertainty. In many cases, disagreement appears to arise not because the reference sets are clearly flawed, but because the underlying patient queries are underspecified. When the available information is sparse or messy, even human experts must rely on subjective inference when deciding which diagnoses belong in the differential. This observation points to a broader challenge for evaluating decision-support systems: some instability in the labels reflects genuine ambiguity in the task rather than simple annotation noise. Future work should therefore study stability across clinicians, specialties, and institutions, and more explicitly examine which tradeoff profiles are preferred in practice.

走向互动评估Toward Interactive Evaluation

自然而然的下一步是将绿色屏蔽扩展到多轮和更代理的设置，允许系统提出澄清问题、识别丢失的信息并适应未指定的用户输入。这样做将使议程超越单次基准测试，转向更通用的框架，以了解交互选择如何在实际使用中塑造模型行为。One natural next step is to extend Green Shielding to multi-turn and more agentic settings, where systems are allowed to ask clarifying questions, recognize missing information, and adapt to underspecified user inputs. Doing so would move the agenda beyond single-shot benchmarking and toward a more general framework for understanding how interaction choices shape model behavior under realistic use.

7  结论7  Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 Green Shielding，这是一个以用户为中心的研究议程，通过研究用户输入中良性的、部署现实的变化如何塑造模型行为来开发有证据支持的部署指南。这一挑战出现在用户在不确定的情况下寻求决策支持的许多领域，包括医学、教育以及法律或政策设置。我们提出了医学诊断中这一议程的第一个实证研究。我们的方法基于 PCS 框架并通过 CUE 标准构建，强调任务相关性、实际约束下的可行性以及对现实变化的鲁棒性。使用可扩展的参考构建和针对鉴别诊断定制的细粒度指标，我们表明前沿法学硕士在与部署相关的即时变化下表现出系统的行为变化，揭示了传统基准在很大程度上掩盖的可靠性权衡。我们进一步表明，即时中和使模型输出变得更加类似于临床医生的差异，同时明确了全面性和选择性之间的权衡。除了记录这些影响之外，绿色屏蔽还为未来的工作定义了一个具体目标：根据明确的、与任务相关的标准（例如合理性、覆盖范围和广度）改进提示级策略和交互设计，而不是仅针对总体准确性进行优化。In this work, we introduce Green Shielding, a user-centric research agenda for developing evidence-backed deployment guidance by studying how benign, deployment-realistic variation in user inputs shapes model behavior. This challenge arises across many domains in which users seek decision support under uncertainty, including medicine, education, and legal or policy settings. We present the first empirical study of this agenda in medical diagnosis. Grounded in the PCS framework and structured through the CUE criteria, our approach emphasizes task relevance, feasibility under practical constraints, and robustness to realistic variation. Using scalable reference construction and fine-grained metrics tailored to differential diagnosis, we show that frontier LLMs exhibit systematic behavioral shifts under deployment-relevant prompt variation, revealing reliability tradeoffs that are largely obscured by conventional benchmarks. We further show that prompt neutralization moves model outputs toward more clinician-like differentials while making explicit the tradeoff between comprehensiveness and selectivity. Beyond documenting these effects, Green Shielding defines a concrete target for future work: improving prompt-level strategies and interaction design against explicit, task-relevant criteria such as plausibility, coverage, and breadth, rather than optimizing only for aggregate accuracy.

更广泛地说，绿色屏蔽为研究交互选择如何在实际使用中塑造模型行为提供了基础。未来的工作应该扩大跨模型和领域的覆盖范围，同时将框架从单次评估扩展到多回合和更代理的设置，其中系统必须识别丢失的信息，提出澄清问题并适应未指定的用户输入。在医疗环境中，还需要更大规模的临床医生研究和补充性对话级别评估，以加强验证并更好地捕获反应质量的各个维度，而不仅仅是鉴别诊断的生成。我们相信，绿色屏蔽可以帮助促进向交互式、以用户为中心的评估范式的更广泛转变，更好地反映真实的部署条件，并为在实践中改进人工智能系统提供更可行的指导。More broadly, Green Shielding provides a foundation for studying how interaction choices shape model behavior under realistic use. Future work should expand coverage across models and domains, while extending the framework beyond single-shot evaluation to multi-turn and more agentic settings, where systems must recognize missing information, ask clarifying questions, and adapt to underspecified user inputs. In the medical setting, larger clinician studies and complementary conversation-level assessments will also be needed to strengthen validation and better capture dimensions of response quality beyond differential diagnosis generation alone. We believe Green Shielding can help catalyze a broader shift toward interactive, user-centric evaluation paradigms that better reflect real deployment conditions and provide more actionable guidance for improving AI systems in practice.

附录CAppendix C提示级因素的选择Selection of Prompt-level Factors

试点研究和 HCM 实验中使用的即时水平因素是通过人为判断与经验细化相结合的迭代过程选择的。我们的目标不是枚举所有可能的即时变化来源，而是分离出一小组可以独立研究并以受控方式操纵的现实用户端因素。The prompt-level factors used in the pilot study and the HCM experiments were chosen through an iterative process combining human judgment with empirical refinement. Our goal was not to enumerate all possible sources of prompt variation, but to isolate a small set of realistic user-side factors that could be studied independently and manipulated in a controlled way.

在试点研究中，因素选择从考试式的诊断问题开始，我们根据人类直觉添加了现实的用户端扰动，即真实的患者可能如何以不同的方式呈现相同的潜在病例。我们将这些扰动分为三大类：内容层面的因素，它改变了前景或引入的实质性信息；格式级因素，它改变请求的结构；以及改变情感或人际框架的语气水平因素。这种分类很有用，因为这三个维度在概念上是不同的，并且初步近似是可分离的：用户可以在不改变核心内容的情况下改变语气，在不改变效果的情况下改变格式，或者在不改变请求的整体结构的情况下引入附加内容。因此，我们优先考虑可以操纵的主要因素，而无需预设其他潜在因素。相反，我们避免了次要因素，其定义取决于其他提示属性，例如误导性用户信念的置信度，因为这些因素更难干净地变化，也更难以因果解释。In the pilot study, factor selection began from exam-style diagnostic questions, to which we added realistic user-side perturbations based on human intuition about how real patients might present the same underlying case differently. We organized these perturbations into three broad categories: content-level factors, which change what substantive information is foregrounded or introduced; format-level factors, which change how the request is structured; and tone-level factors, which change affective or interpersonal framing. This categorization was useful because these three dimensions are conceptually distinct and, to a first approximation, separable: a user can change tone without changing core content, alter formatting without changing affect, or introduce additional content without changing the overall structure of the request. We therefore prioritized primary factors that could be manipulated without presupposing another latent factor. Conversely, we avoided secondary factors whose definition depends on other prompt attributes, such as the confidence of a misleading user belief, since such factors are harder to vary cleanly and harder to interpret causally.

该试点研究不仅证明了即时水平变化可以影响模型行为的概念，而且还作为一个实验阶段，以细化哪些因素的表达有助于推进。一些最初看似合理的候选因素被证明很难彻底隔离，或者在我们的操作下没有产生强烈的影响。这对于解释很重要：当一个因素似乎影响有限时，适当的结论并不是潜在的现象普遍不重要，而是我们对该因素的特殊阐述在这种情况下没有产生强烈的影响。The pilot study served not only as a proof of concept that prompt-level variation can affect model behavior, but also as an experimentation stage for refining which factor articulations were useful to carry forward. Some candidate factors that initially seemed plausible proved difficult to isolate cleanly or did not induce strong effects under our operationalization. This is important for interpretation: when a factor appears to have limited impact, the appropriate conclusion is not that the underlying phenomenon is universally unimportant, but that our particular articulation of that factor did not produce a strong effect in this setting.
