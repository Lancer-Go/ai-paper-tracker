基于事件的多模态自我运动估计中学习表示的几何On the Geometry of Learned Representations in Event-Based Multi-Modal Egomotion Estimation

Stefano Silvestrini Corresponding author. E-Mail: stefano.silvestrini@polimi.it Politecnico di Milano, Via Giuseppe La Masa, 34, 20156, Milan, Italy Michele Ceresoli Politecnico di Milano, Via Giuseppe La Masa, 34, 20156, Milan, Italy
摘要Abstract

基于事件的自我运动估计的经典方法，包括 ELOPE 挑战赛中表现最好的团队所采用的方法，依赖于几何优化框架，例如对比度最大化、单应性估计或与分析运动反演相结合的密集光流。这项工作研究了用于自我运动估计的多模态网络中出现的几何结构。事件张量、惯性测量和距离信号通过跨模式注意力架构融合，并在批量设置中进行训练。我们分析了潜在空间几何和注意力动态，表明（i）嵌入位于与运动变量对齐的低维流形上，（ii）注意力权重适应角度激励和视觉可靠性，以及（iii）融合表示恢复了经典的可观察性线索。这些结果将分析估计理论和现代数据驱动融合联系起来。Classical approaches to event-based egomotion estimation, including those adopted by the top-performing teams of the ELOPE challenge, rely on geometric optimization frameworks such as contrast maximization, homography estimation, or dense optical flow combined with analytic motion inversion. This work investigates the geometric structure that emerges inside a multi-modal network for egomotion estimation. Event tensors, inertial measurements, and range signals are fused through a cross-modal attention architecture and trained in a batch setting. We analyze the latent space geometry and attention dynamics, showing that (i) embeddings lie on low-dimensional manifolds aligned with motion variables, (ii) attention weights adapt with angular excitation and visual reliability, and (iii) the fused representation recovers classical observability cues. These results bridge analytical estimation theory and modern data-driven fusion.

1 简介Introduction

自我运动估计传统上是通过刚体运动学和射影几何来制定的。在基于帧的视觉中，运动估计通常源自亮度恒定假设，而基于事件的方法通常利用时间对比度和事件对齐。 ELOPE 数据集、竞赛设计和提交的方法的全面概述见Egomotion estimation is traditionally formulated through rigid-body kinematics and projective geometry. In frame-based vision, motion estimation is often derived from brightness-constancy assumptions, whereas event-based methods usually exploit temporal contrast and event alignment. A comprehensive overview of the ELOPE dataset, competition design, and submitted approaches is provided in[2]，作为将这项工作背景化的主要参考。对比度最大化技术, which serves as the primary reference for contextualizing this work. Contrast maximization techniques[4]通过根据候选运动假设扭曲事件并最大化图像清晰度或频域能量来估计运动参数。这些方法直接对异步事件流进行操作，并根据事件密度自适应地选择时间窗口，从而实现高时间保真度。该范式的扩展已应用于月球着陆和太空导航场景estimate motion parameters by warping events according to a candidate motion hypothesis and maximizing image sharpness or frequency-domain energy. These methods operate directly on asynchronous event streams and adaptively select temporal windows based on event density, enabling high temporal fidelity. Extensions of this paradigm have been applied to lunar landing and space navigation scenarios[10, 12]，表现出出色的准确性，但需要在推理时进行迭代优化。在事件张量上运行的深度网络, demonstrating excellent accuracy but requiring iterative optimization at inference time. Deep networks operating on event tensors[13, 5]或融合事件和惯性数据or fusing event and inertial data[11]直接回归运动量，将复杂性从推理转移到训练。这就提出了一个核心问题：directly regress motion quantities, shifting complexity from inference to training. This raises a central question:几何形状是否在学习的融合模型中消失了，或者它是否隐式编码在它们的表示中？does geometry disappear in learned fusion models, or is it encoded implicitly in their representations?本文通过分析模拟月球下降期间用于垂直自我运动估计的自监督多模态网络的潜在空间来解决这个问题。我们使用新颖的端到端自监督模型作为学习的多模态案例研究，以研究几何结构是否出现在其内部表示中。 这些结果不会取代经典的几何估计器，但明确了其几何推理的一部分如何在学习的表示中摊销。该分析提出了潜在空间诊断在可靠性监控、故障检测和未来混合学习优化管道中的实际用途。因此，这项工作的贡献不是最先进的性能声明，而是对新开发的基于事件的多模态模型学习的几何形状的诊断研究。我们特别提供：This paper addresses this question by analyzing the latent space of a self-supervised multi-modal network for vertical egomotion estimation during simulated lunar descent. We use the novel end-to-end self-supervised model as a learned multi-modal case study to investigate whether geometric structure emerges in its internal representation. These results do not replace classical geometric estimators, but make explicit how part of their geometric reasoning is amortized inside the learned representation. The analysis suggests practical uses of latent-space diagnostics for reliability monitoring, failure detection, and future hybrid learned–optimization pipelines. The contribution of this work is therefore not a state-of-the-art performance claim, but a diagnostic study of the geometry learned by a newly developed multi-modal event-based model. In particular, we provide:

  • •

    状态充分性测试表明瞬时潜在向量包含速度预测所需的几乎所有信息；a state-sufficiency test showing that the instantaneous latent vector contains almost all information needed for velocity prediction;

  • •

    潜在空间可靠性诊断表明，可以通过嵌入规范和马哈拉诺比斯距离检测到高误差窗口；latent-space reliability diagnostics showing that high-error windows can be detected from embedding norms and Mahalanobis distance;

  • •

    物理对齐分析表明，主要潜在方向与速度相关变量相关；a physical-alignment analysis showing that dominant latent directions correlate with velocity-related variables;

  • •

    将跨模式注意力解释为隐式可靠性调制机制。an interpretation of cross-modal attention as an implicit reliability modulation mechanism.

这些分析使得与经典的基于优化的方法的比较变得明确：虽然经典方法在线解决几何问题，但学习模型似乎将该几何结构的一部分分摊到其潜在表示中。These analyses make the comparison with classical optimization-based methods explicit: while classical methods solve a geometric problem online, the learned model appears to amortize part of this geometric structure into its latent representation.

This can be exploited to identify regimes where event-based visual information becomes less reliable and where inertial or range measurements dominate the estimate. Therefore, the latent-space analysis is not only descriptive. It points toward monitorable and actionable quantities that could support safety checks in future learned guidance, navigation, and control systems.

4 讨论Discussion

所提出的架构通过采用批量制定的摊销推理范式来脱离增量对比度最大化管道The proposed architecture departs from incremental contrast-maximization pipelines by adopting a batch-formulated, amortized inference paradigm[6, 7]。该模型不是在每个时间步显式优化几何目标，而是在单个前向传递中学习从同步多模态输入到速度估计的映射。这种设计体现了两个核心原则。. Instead of explicitly optimizing a geometric objective at each time step, the model learns a mapping from synchronized multi-modal inputs to velocity estimates in a single forward pass. This design reflects two central principles.

  1. 1.

    首先，多模态融合是在表示级别而不是在输出级别执行的。事件衍生的时空特征、惯性测量和测距信号被投影到一个公共的嵌入空间中，并通过跨模态注意力进行融合。这使得网络能够根据上下文学习异构传感模式的自适应加权，而不是依赖于固定的分析融合规则。First, multi-modal fusion is performed at the representation level rather than at the output level. Event-derived spatial-temporal features, inertial measurements, and rangemeter signals are projected into a common embedding space and fused through cross-modal attention. This enables the network to learn adaptive weighting of heterogeneous sensing modalities depending on context, rather than relying on fixed analytical fusion rules.

  2. 2.

    其次，监督是跨目标的。速度估计提供了明确的几何监督，而辅助光流头引入了自我监督约束来规范事件编码器。这种双重监督鼓励潜在表示对运动一致和光度相干结构进行编码。Second, supervision is distributed across objectives. Velocity estimation provides explicit geometric supervision, while the auxiliary optical flow head introduces self-supervised constraints that regularize the event encoder. This dual supervision encourages the latent representation to encode both motion-consistent and photometrically coherent structures.

  3. 3.

    选择基于注意力的融合机制不仅是因为架构新颖，而且是因为它能够在不强加硬结构假设的情况下对跨模式交互进行建模。与基于级联的融合相比，注意力允许模型根据事件衍生的特征来调节惯性或范围线索的影响，这在具有挑战性的照明或低纹理条件下尤其相关。The attention-based fusion mechanism was selected not merely for architectural novelty but for its capacity to model cross-modal interactions without imposing hard structural assumptions. In contrast to concatenation-based fusion, attention allows the model to modulate the influence of inertial or range cues depending on event-derived features, which is particularly relevant under challenging illumination or low-texture conditions.

4.1 学习表示的几何形状Geometry of the Learned Representation

推动这项工作的一个核心问题是学习的中间表示是否捕获与显式状态估计器相当的几何结构。A central question motivating this work is whether the learned intermediate representation captures geometric structure comparable to explicit state estimators.

尽管速度在输出层受到监督，但多模态融合产生的中间嵌入在组件级别仍然不受约束，因此构成了潜在表示。经验分析表明，这种表示以结构化方式编码运动相关信息：Although velocity is supervised at the output layer, the intermediate embedding produced by multi-modal fusion remains unconstrained at the component level and therefore constitutes a latent representation. Empirical analyses indicate that this representation encodes motion-related information in a structured manner:

  • •

    线性探测表明，可以从嵌入中以高保真度恢复速度分量，这表明运动的近似线性编码。Linear probing reveals that velocity components can be recovered with high fidelity from the embedding, suggesting approximate linear encoding of motion.

  • •

    嵌入轨迹的时间平滑度与物理运动连续性相关，表明潜在空间中的结构化动态。Temporal smoothness of the embedding trajectory correlates with physical motion continuity, indicating structured dynamics in latent space.

  • •

    维数分析表明，有效的内在维数显着低于嵌入维数，这表明该模型沿着低维流形组织几何信息。Dimensionality analysis shows that the effective intrinsic dimensionality is significantly lower than the embedding dimension, suggesting that the model organizes geometric information along a low-dimensional manifold.

这些观察结果支持这样的假设：网络学习隐式几何表示而不是任意特征编码。These observations support the hypothesis that the network learns an implicit geometric representation rather than an arbitrary feature encoding.

4.2 隐式几何与显式几何Implicit versus Explicit Geometry

尽管有这些结构化属性，但在将性能与经典管道进行比较时，隐式几何和显式几何之间的差异变得显而易见。经典方法显式参数化运动变量并通过优化强制几何一致性。相比之下，学习模型在分布式嵌入中隐式编码几何。虽然这允许灵活性和摊销推理，但它可能会限制全局一致性和长期指标稳定性，特别是在分布变化的情况下。特别是，基于优化的方法可以通过直接最大化几何一致性目标来重新估计每个测量窗口的运动参数，而学习的表示必须仅依赖于训练期间内化的模式。这可以解释经典管道优于学习方法的情况，尽管后者具有表示灵活性。Despite these structured properties, differences between implicit and explicit geometry become apparent when comparing performance against classical pipelines. Classical methods explicitly parameterize motion variables and enforce geometric consistency through optimization. The learned model, by contrast, encodes geometry implicitly in a distributed embedding. While this allows flexibility and amortized inference, it may limit global consistency and long-term metric stability, particularly under distribution shift. In particular, optimization-based methods can re-estimate motion parameters for each measurement window by directly maximizing a geometric consistency objective, whereas the learned representation must rely solely on patterns internalized during training. This may explain scenarios where classical pipelines outperform the learned approach despite the latter’s representational flexibility.

4.3 自我监督的作用Role of Self-Supervision

辅助光流目标通过鼓励事件编码器中的空间相干运动表示来发挥正则化作用。它不是仅仅充当辅助预测任务，而是通过强制执行运动一致的特征组织来塑造潜在空间的几何形状。这种多任务结构强调了一个更广泛的原则：当不存在显式几何约束时，自监督目标可以充当几何先验。监督速度回归和自监督流量估计之间的相互作用促成了结构化嵌入的出现。The auxiliary optical flow objective plays a regularizing role by encouraging spatially coherent motion representations in the event encoder. Rather than serving solely as an auxiliary prediction task, it shapes the geometry of the latent space by enforcing motion-consistent feature organization. This multi-task structure highlights a broader principle: self-supervised objectives can act as geometric priors when explicit geometric constraints are absent. The interplay between supervised velocity regression and self-supervised flow estimation contributes to the emergence of structured embeddings.

4.4 局限性Limitations

所提出的分析是诊断性的，并没有建立正式的可观察性或稳定性保证。所识别的潜在空间结构是根据模拟月球下降数据进行经验测量的，因此应被解释为测试分布内学习几何组织的证据。此外，尽管潜在距离和注意力统计数据提供了有用的可靠性指标，但它们并不是经过校准的不确定性估计。未来的工作应该在更强的分布变化、传感器退化和闭环引导条件下评估这些诊断。总体而言，结果表明，学习的基于事件的自我运动估计器并不是简单地用不透明的回归图替换几何图形。相反，经典管道使用的部分几何结构似乎被摊销到潜在表示中：瞬时嵌入总结了测量窗口，主要潜在方向与物理速度变量一致，潜在偏差与估计误差相关。这一观察的实用价值在于可以监控潜在空间。在未来的混合系统中，此类诊断可用于触发后备优化、调整传感器权重或在不可靠的估计影响下游引导和控制之前拒绝它们。The proposed analysis is diagnostic and does not establish formal observability or stability guarantees. The identified latent-space structure is measured empirically on simulated lunar descent data and should therefore be interpreted as evidence of learned geometric organization within the tested distribution. Moreover, although latent distance and attention statistics provide useful reliability indicators, they are not calibrated uncertainty estimates. Future work should evaluate these diagnostics under stronger distribution shifts, sensor degradation, and closed-loop guidance conditions. Overall, the results suggest that learned event-based egomotion estimators do not simply replace geometry with an opaque regression map. Instead, part of the geometric structure used by classical pipelines appears to be amortized into the latent representation: instantaneous embeddings summarize the measurement window, dominant latent directions align with physical velocity variables, and latent deviations correlate with estimation error. The practical value of this observation is that the latent space can be monitored. In future hybrid systems, such diagnostics could be used to trigger fallback optimization, adapt sensor weighting, or reject unreliable estimates before they affect downstream guidance and control.
