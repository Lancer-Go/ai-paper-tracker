摘要Abstract

Existing work has introduced Learned Sparse Retrieval (LSR) to push beyond exact lexical matching toward richer semantics. 2.1 学习稀疏检索Learned sparse retrieval

学习稀疏检索用神经模型取代了固定的词汇统计（例如 BM25），该模型既可以重新加权观察到的术语，又可以使用原始文本中不存在的语义相关术语来扩展表示。基于 SPLADE 的方法表明，上下文标记加权和词汇扩展可以使稀疏检索与强密集基线竞争Learned sparse retrieval replaces fixed lexical statistics, such as BM25, with neural models that both reweight observed terms and expand the representation with semantically related terms absent from the original text. SPLADE-based methods demonstrate that contextual token weighting and vocabulary expansion can make sparse retrieval competitive with strong dense baselines(spladev1; spladev2; spladev3)。最近的工作利用法学硕士骨干和更强大的培训方法扩展了这一研究领域. Recent work extends this line of research using LLM backbones and stronger training recipes(laconic)。虽然相关工作要么使解码器骨干适应双向行为以进行检索. While related efforts either adapt decoder backbones toward bidirectional behavior for retrieval(laconic)或者在一个模型内联合训练密集和稀疏检索or jointly train dense and sparse retrieval within one model(bgem3; sparsejoint)，我们的工作的不同之处在于在统一主干内的因果关注下本地研究稀疏检索。, our work differs by studying sparse retrieval natively under causal attention within a unified backbone.

2.2 多模态检索Multimodal Retrieval

最近，多模态大型语言模型（MLLM）Recently, Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)(gemini2; qwen3vl2025; gpt5)在广泛的任务中表现出了卓越的表现。因此，通用的多模态嵌入模型have demonstrated exceptional performance across a wide range of tasks. Consequently, universal multimodal embedding models(vlm2vec; thinkthenembed2025; qwenvlembedding)建立在这些 MLLM 主干之上，在 MMEB-v2 等基准上取得了极具竞争力的结果have been built upon these MLLM backbones, achieving highly competitive results on benchmarks such as MMEB-v2(vlm2vecv2)。然而，多模态稀疏检索的探索仍然很少。现有的 LSR 工作通常引入辅助模块或额外模型来解释非文本模式. However, multimodal sparse retrieval remains much less explored. Existing LSR work typically introduces auxiliary modules or an extra model to interpret non-text modalities(visualsparta; stair; blipsparse; frozen; splare)。这些辅助模块将稀疏模型限制为特定的模态对，并使其扩展到新模态变得复杂。我们的工作通过将解码器本机稀疏检索、统一的密集和稀疏建模以及单个因果主干内的多模态支持相结合来解决这一差距。. Such auxiliary modules restrict the sparse model to a specific modality pair and complicate its extension to new modalities. Our work addresses this gap by combining decoder-native sparse retrieval, unified dense and sparse modeling, and multimodal support within a single causal backbone.
