摘要Abstract

通用人工智能最终需要能够在物理世界中学习、推理和行动的代理。行动模式、愿景-语言-行动政策和世界模式已经使这一雄心变得具体。世界行动模型（WAM）特别有前途，因为它们将候选干预措施与预测的身体后果联系起来，支持后果意识决策。然而，进步并不是一蹴而就就能积累起来的。许多以动作为中心的系统将输出绑定到动作空间和控制器。预测系统暴露不同的变量；数据集、任务和运行时使用不兼容的约定。单独缩放参数或轨迹并不能解决这些接口。我们调查了 WAM 的演变，并将这些限制分为模型角色和表示、目标和标准化以及系统组成方面的三个耦合差距。然后，我们提出了可扩展物理智能的共同进化路线图。其中心是Artificial general intelligence ultimately requires agents that can learn, reason, and act in the physical world. Action models, vision-language-action policies, and world models have made this ambition concrete. World Action Models (WAMs) are especially promising because they connect candidate interventions to predicted physical consequences, supporting consequence-aware decisions. Yet progress does not accumulate readily. Many action-centric systems bind outputs to action spaces and controllers. Predictive systems expose different variables; datasets, tasks, and runtimes use incompatible conventions. Scaling parameters or trajectories alone does not resolve these interfaces. We survey the evolution toward WAMs and organize these limitations into three coupled gaps in model roles and representations, objectives and standardization, and system composition. We then propose a co-evolution roadmap for scalable physical intelligence. At its center is the具身脑embodied brain，物理推理的长期模型目标。它集成了多模式上下文，比较可能的干预，并传达预期的状态转换或功能请求而不是执行器命令。当前的 WAM 为该模型可能需要的预测功能提供了有前途的原型，而无需修复其最终架构。物理线束将大脑输出接地、解析工具和控制器、验证执行情况并记录结果跟踪。共享合约使异构数据、任务和组件具有可比性，同时保留实施例细节。闭环后培训将经过验证的交互转化为可重用的体验。通过将一般物理推理与本地执行分开，该路线图使组件可以独立测试、相互兼容和共同改进。由此产生的物理智能堆栈为实现自适应、自我改进的代理和基于现实世界交互的更通用智能提供了一条可行的路径。, a long-term model target for physical reasoning. It integrates multimodal context, compares possible interventions, and communicates an intended state transition or capability request instead of actuator commands. Current WAMs provide promising prototypes for the predictive capabilities this model may require without fixing its final architecture. A physical harness grounds the brain output, resolves tools and controllers, verifies execution, and records the resulting trace. Shared contracts make heterogeneous data, tasks, and components comparable while preserving embodiment details. Closed-loop post-training turns verified interaction into reusable experience. By separating general physical reasoning from local execution, the roadmap makes components independently testable, mutually compatible, and jointly improvable. The resulting physical-intelligence stack offers an actionable path toward adaptive, self-improving agents and more general intelligence grounded in real-world interaction.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能的长期目标是构建能够感知、推理、行动和改进开放式任务的通用代理。大型语言模型和多模态基础模型的最新进展使这一雄心在数字领域变得越来越具体。模型现在可以总结知识、编写代码、使用工具、遵循指令并参与多步骤工作流程。然而，纯粹数字化的智能概念仍然不完整。人类智能不仅是语言或符号的；它以空间、时间、物体、力量、可供性和后果为基础。一个渴望成为通用机构的系统最终必须超越预测文本或图像，并获得理解和干预物理世界的能力。从这个意义上来说，物理智能并不是AGI的外围应用，而是智能系统能否在现实世界约束下连接感知、预测、决策和行动的核心测试。A long-standing ambition of artificial intelligence is to build general-purpose agents that can perceive, reason, act, and improve across open-ended tasks. Recent progress in large language models and multimodal foundation models has made this ambition increasingly concrete in digital domains. Models can now summarize knowledge, write code, use tools, follow instructions, and participate in multi-step workflows. Yet a purely digital notion of intelligence remains incomplete. Human intelligence is not only linguistic or symbolic; it is grounded in space, time, objects, forces, affordances, and consequences. A system that aspires to general-purpose agency must eventually move beyond predicting text or images and acquire the ability to understand and intervene in the physical world. In this sense, physical intelligence is not a peripheral application of AGI, but a central test of whether intelligent systems can connect perception, prediction, decision making, and action under real-world constraints.

这一观点与对人工智能未来的几个有影响力的观点是一致的。 “惨痛的教训”强调继续受益于计算和数据的通用方法This view is consistent with several influential perspectives on the future of AI. The “bitter lesson” emphasizes general methods that continue to benefit from computation and data(sutton2019bitterlesson)。最近关于“体验时代”的讨论进一步认为，未来的智能体不仅应该从静态的人类生成的语料库中学习，还应该从它们自己的交互痕迹和后果中学习. Recent discussions of an “era of experience” further argue that future agents should learn not only from static human-generated corpora, but also from their own interaction traces and consequences(silver2025eraexperience)。与此同时，世界模型和空间智能观点认为，智能行为需要支持预测、规划和控制的外部世界的内部表征. In parallel, world-model and spatial-intelligence perspectives argue that intelligent behavior requires internal representations of the external world that support prediction, planning, and control(lecun2022path; li2025spatialintelligence)。这些观点共同指出了一个共同的要求：人工智能的下一阶段不仅应该从观察中学习，而且应该在感知、干预、反馈和自我完善的闭环中运行。. Together, these perspectives point to a common requirement: the next stage of AI should not only learn from observations, but also operate in closed loops of perception, intervention, feedback, and self-improvement.

实体人工智能和机器人学习使这一要求变得具体。在物理环境中，一个行为会改变世界，而成功的行为通常取决于对这种变化的预期。最近的系统涵盖基于语言的规划、多模式目标规范以及将视觉、语言和本体感受上下文映射到机器人动作的视觉-语言-动作策略Embodied AI and robot learning make this requirement concrete. In a physical environment, an action changes the world, and successful behavior often depends on anticipating that change. Recent systems span language-grounded planning, multimodal goal specification, and Vision-Language-Action policies that map visual, linguistic, and proprioceptive context to robot actions(ahn2022saycan; driess2023palme; jiang2022vima; brohan2022rt1; brohan2023rt2; kim2024openvla; octo2024; black2024pi0)。这些系统扩大了任务和实施范围，但观察到行动的预测只是物理机构的一部分。实体代理还必须保持与任务相关的状态，比较可能的干预措施，估计可行性和不确定性，并在需要专门控制时委托执行。这反映了运动控制和可供性理论的经典见解：当感知围绕行动和结果而不是被动描述组织时，就会支持行为。. These systems broaden task and embodiment coverage, but observation-to-action prediction is only one part of physical agency. An embodied agent must also maintain task-relevant state, compare possible interventions, estimate feasibility and uncertainty, and delegate execution when specialized control is required. This reflects a classical insight from motor control and affordance theory: perception supports behavior when it is organized around action and consequence rather than passive description(wolpert1998multiple; gibson1979ecological).

面向操作的界面还暴露了扩展问题。物理命令是相对于机器人的形态、坐标系、控制模式和传感配置来定义的。 Open X-Embodiment 将异构动作粗略地对齐为通用的末端执行器格式，同时保留数据集相关的标准化和控制约定The action-oriented interface also exposes a scaling problem. Physical commands are defined relative to a robot’s morphology, coordinate frames, control mode, and sensing configuration. Open X-Embodiment coarsely aligns heterogeneous actions into a common end-effector format while retaining dataset-dependent normalization and control conventions(oneill2023openx)。 Octo 通过可配置的输入、输出头和下游微调来适应新的观察和操作空间. Octo accommodates new observations and action spaces through configurable inputs, output heads, and downstream fine-tuning(octo2024)。这些是跨实体学习的重要进展。他们还表明，重用取决于共享模型表示和本地控制之间的显式映射。当这种映射在策略中保持隐式时，改变夹具、控制器或形态可以改变执行界面和学习的预测目标。. These are important advances in cross-embodiment learning. They also show that reuse depends on an explicit mapping between shared model representations and local control. When this mapping remains implicit inside a policy, changing a gripper, controller, or morphology can alter both the execution interface and the learned prediction target.

根据新兴文献，我们使用Informed by the emerging literature, we use世界行动模式World Action Model（WAM）表示一种行动条件预测模型，该模型使干预结果明确用于规划、验证、控制或学习(WAM) to denote an action-conditioned predictive model that makes intervention outcomes explicit for planning, verification, control, or learning(wang2026wam)。根据此定义，WAM 会估计执行候选操作、技能或工具调用时可能发生的情况。现有系统通过联合图像动作预测、统一视频动作建模、3D 场景预测、世界语言动作建模和结构化世界动作堆栈来实例化此功能. Under this definition, a WAM estimates what may happen if a candidate action, skill, or tool call is executed. Existing systems instantiate this capability through joint image–action prediction, unified video–action modeling, 3D scene prediction, world–language–action modeling, and structured world–action stacks(wu2023gr1; guo2024predictionactionvisualpolicy; li2025unifiedvideoactionmodel; lu2025gwm; yang2026wla; kairos2026)。这种多样性证明了结果建模的前景。它还保留了 WAM 应该预测什么、应该如何表示该信息以及另一个系统应该如何使用它。. This diversity demonstrates the promise of consequence modeling. It also leaves open what a WAM should predict, how that information should be represented, and how another system should consume it.

这些观察揭示了更广泛的扩展问题。身体智力必须在至少三种互补的意义上进行扩展。模型必须获得更强的空间和物理表征；学到的能力必须跨任务、工具和实施例转移；来自不同数据集和系统的进步必须不断积累。增加参数或轨迹主要解决第一个要求。后两者还需要能够跨异构系统保留预测、行动和结果含义的接口。多样性本身并不是障碍。障碍是多样性，其语义仍然是隐藏的。These observations reveal a broader scaling problem. Physical intelligence must scale in at least three complementary senses. Models must acquire stronger spatial and physical representations; learned capabilities must transfer across tasks, tools, and embodiments; and advances from different datasets and systems must accumulate. Increasing parameters or trajectories primarily addresses the first requirement. The latter two also require interfaces that preserve the meaning of predictions, actions, and outcomes across heterogeneous systems. Diversity is not itself the obstacle. The obstacle is diversity whose semantics remain hidden.

我们将这个问题分为三个耦合的挑战。第一个是We organize this problem into three coupled challenges. The first is a模型与表征的差距model and representation gap。当前的系统结合了语言和视觉语言模型、动作标记器、扩散策略、视频生成器和多模式主干。这些组件增强了语义基础或生成能力，但它们不会自动提供物理状态的持久且可操作的说明。空间结构、接触敏感推理、校准不确定性和恢复仍然是开放的挑战；最近的评估报告了空间推理、接触预测和物理一致性方面的失败. Current systems combine language and vision-language models, action tokenizers, diffusion policies, video generators, and multimodal backbones. These components strengthen semantic grounding or generative capacity, but they do not automatically provide a persistent and actionable account of physical state. Spatial structure, contact-sensitive reasoning, calibrated uncertainty, and recovery remain open challenges; recent evaluation reports failures in spatial reasoning, contact prediction, and physical consistency(robowmbench2026)。中心模特的角色也不稳定。该领域尚未就应学习哪些物理能力、应暴露哪些后果或应保留哪些责任达成一致。. The central model role is also unsettled. The field has not converged on which physical capabilities it should learn, which consequences it should expose, or which responsibilities should remain downstream.

第二个挑战是The second challenge is an目标和标准化差距objective and standardization gap。最终任务的成功本身并不能揭示系统是否预测了正确的结果、利用了狭窄的基准规律或依赖于强大的控制器。现有的评估涉及不同层面，包括可执行性、具体推理、基准构建和安全性. End-task success cannot by itself reveal whether a system predicted the right consequence, exploited a narrow benchmark regularity, or relied on a strong controller. Existing evaluations address different layers, including executability, embodied reasoning, benchmark construction, and safety(robowmbench2026; luo2025robobench; zhang2026a2eval; cui2026liberosafety; yin2026roboshackles)。不同项目的数据收集、任务定义、监督和评估协议也有所不同。如果没有干预措施、预测变化和结果的共享语义，这些资源就无法提供完全可比较的监督。更多数据会增加容量，但不一定会增加可重复使用的物理体验。. Data collection, task definitions, supervision, and evaluation protocols also differ across projects. Without shared semantics for interventions, predicted changes, and outcomes, these resources cannot provide fully comparable supervision. More data then increases volume without necessarily increasing reusable physical experience.

第三个挑战是The third challenge is an生态系统和系统差距ecosystem and systems gap。单个模型不需要拥有通用推理、低级控制、模拟、验证、内存、安全性和每个执行服务。数字代理通过管理工具调用、状态、护栏、跟踪和多步骤执行的工具将这些角色分开. A single model need not own general reasoning, low-level control, simulation, verification, memory, safety, and every execution service. Digital agents separate these roles through harnesses that manage tool calls, state, guardrails, tracing, and multi-step execution(openai2025agentsguide; openai2025agentssdk; anthropic2024buildingeffectiveagents)。语言、代码和结构化 API 提供了它们的通信媒介。物理代理缺乏同样成熟的空间关系、坐标系、预期状态转换、不确定性和控制器约束的接口。因此，每个项目通常通过特定于系统的运行时重新连接感知、推理和控制，从而限制组件替换和跟踪重用。. Language, code, and structured APIs provide their communication medium. Physical agents lack an equally mature interface for spatial relations, coordinate frames, intended state transitions, uncertainty, and controller constraints. Consequently, each project often reconnects perception, reasoning, and control through a system-specific runtime, limiting component substitution and trace reuse.

该路线图通过明确的模型和系统契约解决了这些差距。对于当前的 WAM 研究，我们提出了预测合约而不是强制输出模式。给定物理背景和候选干预，模型应该揭示与决策相关的结果及其范围、参考框架、不确定性和有效性条件。结果可以通过视频、几何、结构化状态、预测潜伏或混合来表示。模型也可以预测动作，但动作向量本身并不能明确预期的结果。The roadmap addresses these gaps through explicit model and system contracts. For current WAM research, we propose a prediction contract rather than a mandatory output modality. Given physical context and a candidate intervention, the model should expose a decision-relevant consequence together with its horizon, reference frame, uncertainty, and validity conditions. The consequence may be represented through video, geometry, structured state, predictive latents, or a hybrid. A model may also predict actions, but an action vector alone does not make the expected consequence explicit.

在系统路线图的中心，我们定义了At the center of the systems roadmap, we define the具身脑embodied brain作为长期模型目标。它集成了物理观察和任务上下文、可能干预的原因，并发出中间意图表示。该表示传达预期的状态转换或能力请求而不是实施例特定的致动器命令。具身大脑与完整大脑不同as a long-term model target. It integrates physical observations and task context, reasons over possible interventions, and emits an intermediate intent representation. This representation communicates an intended state transition or capability request rather than embodiment-specific actuator commands. The embodied brain is distinct from the complete身体智力堆栈physical-intelligence stack，其中还包含物理线束、工具模型、控制器、验证器、传感器、主体和环境。当前的 WAM 是该模型可能需要的预测能力的有前景的原型；它们既不等同于实体大脑，也不要求保持独立的模块。, which also contains a physical harness, tool models, controllers, verifiers, sensors, bodies, and environments. Current WAMs are promising prototypes for predictive capabilities that this model may require; they are neither equivalent to the embodied brain nor required to remain a separate module.

这种分离的目的是使物理推理可重用。夹具、控制器或形态的变化应该主要改变所声明的功能及其适配器，而不是强制重新学习所有模型级推理。这是一个设计假设，而不是模块化的保证结果。应通过表示基础、工具替换和跨实施例适应来对其进行测试。当中间职责和转变仍然可以检查时，联合或端到端培训仍然与路线图兼容。This separation is intended to make physical reasoning reusable. A change of gripper, controller, or morphology should primarily alter the declared capability and its adapter instead of forcing all model-level reasoning to be learned again. This is a design hypothesis, not a guaranteed consequence of modularity. It should be tested through representation grounding, tool substitution, and cross-embodiment adaptation. Joint or end-to-end training remains compatible with the roadmap when the intermediate responsibilities and transformations remain inspectable.

现有系统解决了该观点的补充部分。最初的RoboBrain是一个大规模的知识引擎，而最近的RoboBrain工作提出了一个统一的操作模型Existing systems address complementary parts of this view. The original RoboBrain is a large-scale knowledge engine, while recent RoboBrain work proposes a unified manipulation model(saxena2014robobrain; ji2025robobrain)。 RoboOS定义了分层体现框架，Embodied.cpp专注于跨异构机器人的可移植推理. RoboOS defines a hierarchical embodied framework, and Embodied.cpp focuses on portable inference across heterogeneous robots(tan2025roboos; xu2026embodiedcpp)。我们并不将这些系统视为等同的。相反，它们的不同目标说明了为什么仍然需要共享模型角色和显式接口。. We do not treat these systems as equivalent. Their different objectives instead illustrate why a shared model role and explicit interfaces are still needed.

因此，本文将 WAM 研究视为一个起点，而不是一个独立的终点。共同进化路线图将实体大脑与物理工具、工具功能、数据和任务契约以及闭环学习连接起来。物理交互被记录为可重播的跟踪，保留决策上下文、翻译过程、执行状态、验证器输出和结果。此类跟踪可以训练和诊断 WAM 原型、未来大脑模型、线束适配器和工具模型。然后评估确定哪些痕迹足够可靠以返回到训练后。Accordingly, this paper treats WAM research as a starting point rather than a self-contained endpoint. The co-evolution roadmap connects the embodied brain to a physical harness, tool capabilities, data and task contracts, and closed-loop learning. Physical interaction is recorded as a replayable trace that preserves the decision context, translation process, execution state, verifier outputs, and outcome. Such traces can train and diagnose WAM prototypes, future brain models, harness adapters, and tool models. Evaluation then determines which traces are reliable enough to return to post-training.

这种观点也改变了 WAM 的评估方式。像素重建可以奖励与动作弱耦合的外观因素。最终任务的成功可以隐藏性能是否来自物理远见、反应鲁棒性、控制器质量或基准结构。因此，评估应该暴露将预测与行动联系起来的中间信号。代表性的例子包括状态和接触变化、前提条件满足、不确定性、干预效果、失败原因和恢复结果。拟议的This perspective also changes how WAMs should be evaluated. Pixel reconstruction can reward appearance factors that are weakly coupled to action. 2 体现模型向 WAM 的演变Evolution of Embodied Models Toward WAMs

引言将 WAM 视为从被动感知和直接行动预测到干预级物理预见的一个步骤。本节使这种转变变得明确。这种演变并不是动作模型变成 VLA 模型然后变成 WAM 的单一路线。更准确的解释是三个轨迹的汇合。一个学习可执行的机器人动作，另一个学习语言和多模式任务上下文中的动作，第三个预测世界在干预下如何变化。当这些轨迹围绕一个共同的问题相遇时，WAM 就变得有意义：The introduction frames WAMs as a step from passive perception and direct action prediction toward intervention-level physical foresight. This section makes that transition explicit. The evolution is not a single line in which action models become VLA models and then become WAMs. A more accurate account is a convergence of three trajectories. One learns executable robot actions, another grounds action in language and multimodal task context, and a third predicts how the world changes under intervention. WAMs become meaningful when these trajectories meet around a shared question:如果这个具体系统在当前的物理限制下采取这个动作，调用这个技能，或者调用这个工具，会发生什么？what will happen if this embodied system takes this action, invokes this skill, or calls this tool under the current physical constraints?

2.1 动作预测作为第一个界面Action Prediction as the First Interface

现代机器人学习的一个基本轨迹解决了硬接口问题：将感觉和本体感受环境转化为时间连贯的动作。行为变压器对多模式行为进行建模，ACT 预测细粒度双手操作的动作块，扩散策略将视觉运动控制表示为条件动作扩散One foundational trajectory in modern robot learning addresses a hard interface problem: transforming sensory and proprioceptive context into temporally coherent actions. 局限性在于行动的预期结果通常是隐含的。一项政策可能会选择一个好的动议，而无需暴露它所依赖的物理先决条件、预期的状态转换、不确定性如何影响决策，或者为什么附近的替代方案不安全。这对于狭隘的反应技能来说是可以接受的，但它成为长期操作、工具使用、恢复、安全验证和从失败中学习的瓶颈。一个只发出下一个动作的系统很难作为实体大脑进行检查、比较和改进。The limitation is that the expected consequence of an action is often implicit. A policy may select a good motion without exposing which physical preconditions it relies on, which state transition it expects, how uncertainty affects the decision, or why a nearby alternative is unsafe. This is acceptable for narrow reactive skills, but it becomes a bottleneck for long-horizon manipulation, tool use, recovery, safety verification, and learning from failures. A system that only emits the next action is difficult to inspect, compare, and improve as an embodied brain.

A latent action is therefore not automatically a robot action: it may encode motion, viewpoint change, or other visual variation, and its correspondence to an embodiment-specific control space must be learned and evaluated.

在这三个家族中，动态学习和行动基础有不同程度的分离。一些方法首先从无动作或弱标记的视频中学习预测结构，然后通过动作标记的数据、几何结构或现有控制器将该表示连接到机器人。其他人则联合优化预测和行动。因此，界面的范围可以从显式像素、流或文本到预测潜伏和专用转换代码。此接口选择与完整架构是模块化还是联合训练无关。Across the three families, dynamics learning and action grounding are separated to different degrees. Some methods first learn predictive structure from action-free or weakly labeled video and later connect that representation to a robot through action-labeled data, geometry, or an existing controller. Others optimize prediction and action jointly. The interface can therefore range from explicit pixels, flow, or text to predictive latents and purpose-built transition codes. This interface choice is orthogonal to whether the complete architecture is modular or jointly trained.

3 当前通往实体大脑的障碍Current Barriers on the Path to an Embodied Brain

上一节追溯了从行动策略、语义基础和世界建模到面向 WAM 的预测控制的重叠轨迹。这些发展提供了有用的功能，但它们也暴露了三种形式的纠缠。模型级推理通常与特定的预测或操作接口相关联。学习信号与特定于项目的数据和任务协议相关联。执行与自定义运行时和实施例相关联。我们通过引言中介绍的三个差距来组织这些限制：The previous section traced overlapping trajectories from action policies, semantic grounding, and world modeling toward WAM-oriented predictive control. These developments provide useful capabilities, yet they also expose three forms of entanglement. Model-level reasoning is often tied to a particular prediction or action interface. Learning signals are tied to project-specific data and task protocols. Execution is tied to a custom runtime and embodiment. We organize these limitations through the three gaps introduced in the Introduction: a模型与表征的差距model and representation gap, 一个, an目标和标准化差距objective and standardization gap，和一个, and an生态系统和系统差距ecosystem and systems gap。目的是解释为什么进展仍然难以比较、重用和积累。. The purpose is to explain why progress remains difficult to compare, reuse, and accumulate.

3.1 三个差距是耦合的The Three Gaps Are Coupled

这三个缺口构成了一个系统问题。如果不指定表示应该支持哪个决策，则无法对表示进行评估。如果没有产生其监督的数据模式、操作界面和实施例，就无法解释目标。当执行基础设施决定如何转换输出、哪些操作可行以及哪些失败返回学习时，它并不是中立的。因此，模型设计、数据构建、任务定义、执行和评估通过它们的接口进行耦合。The three gaps form one system problem. A representation cannot be evaluated without specifying which decision it should support. An objective cannot be interpreted without the data schema, action interface, and embodiment that produced its supervision. Execution infrastructure is not neutral when it determines how outputs are translated, which actions are feasible, and which failures return to learning. Model design, data construction, task definition, execution, and evaluation are therefore coupled through their interfaces.

这种耦合解释了为什么快速进步并不总是成为累积能力。 VLA 政策将丰富的多式联运背景映射到行动。面向 WAM 的方法揭示了视觉、潜在、几何或联合世界动作预测。数据集扩大了任务和实施例的覆盖范围，而基准测试则测试推理、可执行性和安全性的不同方面。当变量和系统假设是隐含的时，这些进步仍然很难结合起来。因此，路线图必须指定正在开发什么功能以及如何重用该功能。This coupling explains why rapid progress does not always become cumulative capability. VLA policies map rich multimodal context to actions. WAM-oriented methods expose visual, latent, geometric, or joint world–action predictions. Datasets broaden task and embodiment coverage, while benchmarks test different aspects of reasoning, executability, and safety. These advances remain difficult to combine when their variables and system assumptions are implicit. A roadmap must therefore specify both what capability is being developed and how that capability can be reused.

3.2 模型和表征的差距Model and Representation Gap

模型和表示差距涉及一般物理推理模型应该学习、公开并留给下游执行的内容。当前的系统结合了语言和视觉语言模型、动作标记器、扩散策略、视频生成器、世界模型和多模式主干。这些组件改进了语义基础、动作生成或预测建模。它们不会自动产生持久且可操作的物理状态表示。模型可能会模仿行为或生成可信的视频，但仍无法跟踪几何形状、接触、不确定性或恢复条件The model and representation gap concerns what a general physical reasoning model should learn, expose, and leave to downstream execution. Current systems combine language and vision-language models, action tokenizers, diffusion policies, video generators, world models, and multimodal backbones. These components improve semantic grounding, action generation, or predictive modeling. They do not automatically yield a persistent and actionable representation of physical state. A model may imitate behavior or generate plausible video while still failing to track geometry, contact, uncertainty, or recovery conditions(liang2022seeg; liang2019vrr; liu2025uniinter; wang2026wam; robowmbench2026).

以操作为中心的界面使所有权问题变得具体。 Open X-Embodiment 对齐来自许多机器人的数据，但保留依赖于实施例的标准化和控制语义Action-centric interfaces make the ownership problem concrete. Open X-Embodiment aligns data from many robots but retains embodiment-dependent normalization and control semantics(oneill2023openx)。 Octo 通过适应性输出头和微调支持新的动作空间. Octo supports new action spaces through adaptable output heads and fine-tuning(octo2024)。这些系统展示了有价值的跨实体传输。它们还表明，可执行动作仅相对于声明的实施例和控制器才有意义。当模型输出与本地操作接口密不可分时，更改硬件可能需要更改模型头、训练数据或两者。. These systems demonstrate valuable cross-embodiment transfer. They also show that an executable action remains meaningful only relative to a declared embodiment and controller. When the model output is inseparable from that local action interface, changing the hardware can require changes to the model head, training data, or both.

WAM 研究通过揭示预测结果开辟了一个补充方向。 GR-1 和 GR-2 使用视频生成学习进行操作；动作预测和 UVA 将视觉未来与动作推理结合起来； WLA 结合了世界建模、语言推理和动作合成WAM research opens a complementary direction by exposing predicted consequences. GR-1 and GR-2 use video-generative learning for manipulation; Prediction with Action and UVA couple visual futures with action inference; and WLA combines world modeling, language reasoning, and action synthesis(wu2023gr1; cheang2024gr2generativevideolanguageactionmodel; guo2024predictionactionvisualpolicy; li2025unifiedvideoactionmodel; yang2026wla)。他们的产出和培训安排各不相同。因此，该领域尚未收敛于另一个模型、规划器、验证器或控制器可以使用的共享结果接口。. Their outputs and training arrangements differ. The field has therefore not converged on a shared consequence interface that another model, planner, verifier, or controller can consume.

这个差距并不等同于渲染视频的能力。视频提供密集的时间监督和可解释的预测，但视觉保真度并不与决策相关。有用的物理推理可能需要姿势、接触、可遍历性、先决条件、不确定性或预期进展。当 WAM 原型的表现可以改善决策时，即使没有逼真的输出，WAM 原型也可能很有价值。相反，如果看似合理的部署不能改善行动选择、验证、恢复或学习，那么它提供的证据就有限。This gap is not equivalent to the ability to render video. Video offers dense temporal supervision and an interpretable forecast, but visual fidelity is not decision relevance. Useful physical reasoning may instead require pose, contact, traversability, preconditions, uncertainty, or expected progress. A WAM prototype can be valuable without photorealistic output when its representation improves decisions. Conversely, a plausible rollout provides limited evidence when it does not improve action selection, verification, recovery, or learning.

实体大脑提供模型级目标，而不是规定的 WAM 模块。它应该整合任务上下文和物理证据，比较可能的干预措施，并传达预期的状态转换或能力请求。当前的 WAM 为干预条件预测提供了易于处理的原型，而无需确定最终的架构。特定于实施例的执行仍然位于下游：线束为大脑输出提供基础，而工具模型和控制器则实现所请求的功能。这个角色边界允许通过声明的接口来提高模型能力和执行能力。The embodied brain provides the model-level target, not a prescribed WAM module. It should integrate task context and physical evidence, compare possible interventions, and communicate an intended state transition or capability request. Current WAMs provide tractable prototypes for intervention-conditioned prediction without determining the final architecture. Embodiment-specific execution remains downstream: the harness grounds the brain output, while tool models and controllers realize the requested capability. This role boundary allows model capacity and execution capacity to improve through a declared interface.

3.3 目标和标准化差距Objective and Standardization Gap

目标和标准化差距涉及该领域优化的内容以及学习信号在整个系统中是否仍然有意义。孤立的任务成功不足以实现此目的。系统可能会成功，因为任务分配窄、控制器强或不需要恢复。它可能会因状态跟踪不正确、接触预测较弱、校准不良或工具先决条件不可用而失败。最终的成功率并不能区分这些原因。The objective and standardization gap concerns what the field optimizes and whether learning signals remain meaningful across systems. Isolated task success is insufficient for this purpose. A system may succeed because the task distribution is narrow, the controller is strong, or recovery is unnecessary. It may fail because of incorrect state tracking, weak contact prediction, poor calibration, or an unavailable tool precondition. A final success rate does not distinguish these causes.

培训目标还针对不同的能力。模仿学习可以在不暴露远见的情况下产生有效的行动。未来的预测可以对合理的动态进行建模，而无需选择更好的干预措施。强化学习可以在利用捷径的同时提高奖励，验证者标签取决于其反馈源的质量。这些信号不应被分解为单一措施。表示学习、结果预测、接口接地、工具链监督、验证器对齐和闭环后训练需要不同的证据。Training objectives also target different capabilities. Imitation learning can produce competent actions without exposing foresight. Future prediction can model plausible dynamics without selecting a better intervention. Reinforcement learning can improve reward while exploiting shortcuts, and verifier labels depend on the quality of their feedback source. These signals should not be collapsed into a single measure. Representation learning, consequence prediction, interface grounding, tool-chain supervision, verifier alignment, and closed-loop post-training require different evidence.

更深层次的扩展问题是缺乏共享语义。如果一个系统预测像素，另一个系统预测潜在运动，第三个系统发出机器人命令，则不能仅通过采用相同的文件格式来比较它们的数据。有用的标准必须声明干预、预测或预期的变化、时间范围、参考框架、不确定性、实施假设和观察到的结果。这些字段提供了通用的决策上下文，同时允许底层编码有所不同。The deeper scaling problem is the absence of shared semantics. If one system predicts pixels, another predicts latent motion, and a third emits robot commands, their data cannot be compared merely by adopting the same file format. A useful standard must declare the intervention, predicted or intended change, time horizon, reference frame, uncertainty, embodiment assumptions, and observed outcome. These fields provide a common decision context while allowing the underlying encoding to differ.

3.4 生态系统和系统差距Ecosystem and Systems Gap

生态系统和系统差距涉及大脑模型如何成为物理代理，而不将完整的代理吸收到一个模型中。一般推理、低级稳定性、专业感知、验证、记忆和执行以不同的速度发展。当这些功能隐藏在一个特定于项目的堆栈中时，更换控制器或工具可能需要重新培训和重新验证整个系统。因此，实体大脑应该与其运行的更广泛的物理智能堆栈区分开来。The ecosystem and systems gap concerns how a brain model becomes a physical agent without absorbing the complete agent into one model. General reasoning, low-level stabilization, specialist perception, verification, memory, and execution evolve at different rates. When these functions are hidden inside one project-specific stack, replacing a controller or tool can require retraining and revalidating the entire system. The embodied brain should therefore be distinguished from the broader physical-intelligence stack in which it operates.

数字代理使这种分离变得可见。语言模型在管理工具调用、状态、护栏、跟踪、批准和多步骤执行的工具内部运行Digital agents make this separation visible. Language models operate inside harnesses that manage tool calls, state, guardrails, tracing, approval, and multi-step execution(openai2025agentsguide; openai2025agentssdk; anthropic2024buildingeffectiveagents)。语言、代码和结构化API提供下游服务可以解析的接口。物理代理面临着更困难的通信问题。学习的视觉或多模式令牌可能无法被控制器使用，除非它共享该表示或具有经过训练的适配器。几何形状、坐标系、触觉状态、时序、不确定性和实施例约束在执行期间也必须保持一致。. Language, code, and structured APIs provide interfaces that downstream services can parse. Physical agents face a harder communication problem. Learned visual or multimodal tokens may be unusable by a controller unless it shares that representation or has a trained adapter. Geometry, coordinate frames, tactile state, timing, uncertainty, and embodiment constraints must also remain consistent during execution.

因此，物理线束的功能必须不仅仅是工具。它必须为大脑模型输出奠定基础，调整表示和框架，解决声明的功能，验证先决条件，同步控制器，监控结果并保留出处。其中一些功能是可以学习的。其他可以通过确定性系统或控制软件来实现。它们的共同目的是防止特定于实施例的执行层定义通用推理模型。A physical harness must consequently do more than call a tool. It must ground the brain-model output, align representations and frames, resolve a declared capability, verify preconditions, synchronize controllers, monitor outcomes, and preserve provenance. Some of these functions may be learned. Others may be implemented through deterministic systems or control software. Their common purpose is to prevent the embodiment-specific execution layer from defining the general reasoning model.

生态系统的差距不仅限于建筑。数据集可能会省略解释预期转换所需的帧。机器人可能不会声明比较所需的控制器假设。基准测试可能会丢弃训练后所需的故障，并且工具可能不会暴露其先决条件或故障模式。在一个封闭的项目中，每个遗漏都是合理的。总的来说，它们阻止独立开发的工作贡献于共享堆栈。The ecosystem gap extends beyond architecture. A dataset may omit the frame needed to interpret an intended transition. A robot may not declare the controller assumptions required for comparison. A benchmark may discard failures needed for post-training, and a tool may not expose its preconditions or failure modes. Each omission is reasonable within a closed project. Collectively, they prevent independently developed work from contributing to a shared stack.

所需的响应不是一种通用机器人、文件格式或基准。它是使异构系统可解释的最小契约集。实施例配置文件、大脑输出模式、坐标约定、工具功能、线束跟踪、验证程序协议、任务定义和评估层都发挥着这一作用。长期共享的 3D/4D 表示可能会进一步降低翻译成本，但其编码仍然是一个研究问题。标准化语义可以在该领域收敛于该表示之前开始。The required response is not one universal robot, file format, or benchmark. It is a minimum set of contracts that makes heterogeneous systems interpretable. Embodiment profiles, brain-output schemas, coordinate conventions, tool capabilities, harness traces, verifier protocols, task definitions, and evaluation layers serve this role. A longer-term shared 3D/4D representation may reduce translation costs further, but its encoding remains a research question. Standardizing semantics can begin before the field converges on that representation.

3.5 从差距到路线图要求From Gaps to Roadmap Requirements

这三个差距支持一个结论：物理智能需要可扩展的接口以及可扩展的模型。模型差距询问什么样的预测能力应该成为可重用的物理推理。标准化差距要求异构任务和经验如何监督该能力。系统差距询问模型级意图如何在不将模型绑定到一个控制器或主体的情况下变得可执行。这些要求相互制约。模型输出确定必须翻译和记录的内容，而任务协议确定可用的监督。工具契约决定哪些意图可执行，评估决定哪些痕迹可以回归学习。The three gaps support one conclusion: physical intelligence requires scalable interfaces as well as scalable models. The model gap asks what predictive capability should become reusable physical reasoning. The standardization gap asks how heterogeneous tasks and experience can supervise that capability. The systems gap asks how model-level intent becomes executable without binding the model to one controller or body. These requirements constrain one another. The model output determines what must be translated and logged, while the task protocol determines which supervision is available. The tool contract determines which intentions are executable, and evaluation determines which traces can return to learning.

因此，路线图是从系统合同而不是独立议程列表出发的。它首先定义了完整物理智能堆栈的角色和接口。然后开发具身大脑和 WAM 预测合约、物理装备和工具生态系统、数据和任务记录、闭环学习和社区标准。每个阶段都会回答一个障碍，同时产生下一阶段所需的信息。预期的回报是累积的进步：模型推理可以转移，特定于实施例的适应可以保留在本地，并且经过验证的交互可以成为可重用的体验。The Roadmap therefore proceeds from a system contract rather than a list of independent agendas. It first defines the roles and interfaces of the complete physical-intelligence stack. It then develops the embodied brain and WAM prediction contract, the physical harness and tool ecosystem, data and task records, closed-loop learning, and community standards. Each stage answers one barrier while producing information needed by the next. The intended payoff is cumulative progress: model reasoning can transfer, embodiment-specific adaptation can remain local, and verified interaction can become reusable experience.

4.2 Embodied Brain 和 WAM 预测合约Embodied Brain and the WAM Prediction Contract

物理智能系统不应简化为预测每个可能的未来并直接控制每个执行器的模型。具身脑是系统内的通用推理模型。它消耗多模式观察、任务上下文、检索的内存、实施例信息和可用功能的描述。它的作用是整合这些证据，比较可能的干预措施，并确定预期的身体变化。持久存储、高频稳定和特定于硬件的执行可以保留在模型之外。A physical-intelligence system should not be reduced to a model that predicts every possible future and directly controls every actuator. The embodied brain is the general reasoning model within the system. It consumes multimodal observations, task context, retrieved memory, embodiment information, and descriptions of available capabilities. Its role is to integrate this evidence, compare possible interventions, and determine an intended physical change. Persistent storage, high-frequency stabilization, and hardware-specific execution can remain outside the model.

定义输出是中间意图表示而不是执行器轨迹。它应该识别预期的状态转换或功能请求。根据任务的不同，它还可能包括相关实体或区域、空间和时间框架、约束、不确定性以及完成或恢复条件。编码保持开放。语言或代码可能足以满足某些功能；其他人可能需要结构化几何、学习标记或混合表示。要求是该安全带可以解释和验证输出，而无需将大脑绑定到一个夹具、控制器或形态。The defining output is an intermediate intent representation rather than an actuator trajectory. It should identify an intended state transition or capability request. Depending on the task, it may also include relevant entities or regions, spatial and temporal frames, constraints, uncertainty, and completion or recovery conditions. The encoding remains open. Language or code may be sufficient for some capabilities; structured geometry, learned tokens, or hybrid representations may be required for others. The requirement is that the harness can interpret and validate the output without binding the brain to one gripper, controller, or morphology.

WAM 研究使该目标的预测部分可以通过实验实现。拟议的预测合同条件是关于物理环境和候选人干预的。它返回一个与决策相关的结果，具有声明的范围和不确定性。未来的观察、潜在的转变、几何状态、接触变化、可供性或任务进展在改善决策时可以满足这个角色。因此，RGB 视频是可选的，并且必须评估其物理内容而不是假设。WAM research makes the predictive part of this target experimentally accessible. The proposed prediction contract conditions on physical context and a candidate intervention. It returns a decision-relevant consequence with declared scope and uncertainty. Future observations, latent transitions, geometric states, contact changes, affordances, or task progress can satisfy this role when they improve decision making. RGB video is therefore optional, and its physical content must be evaluated rather than assumed.

一个动作可以与结果一起预测，但单独的动作向量并不能满足这个契约。该动作说明特定实施例应该执行什么；结果表明系统期望干预改变什么。保持这两种语义可见，即使下游控制器发生变化，也可以评估模型的前瞻性。An action may be predicted jointly with the consequence, but an action vector alone does not satisfy this contract. The action states what a particular embodiment should execute; the consequence states what the system expects that intervention to change. Keeping both semantics visible allows a model to be evaluated for foresight even when the downstream controller changes.

这种区别保留了当前 WAM 的价值，而无需修复未来的架构。 WAM 原型提供了从视频、机器人轨迹、模拟和交互中学习干预条件预测的实用途径。未来的实体大脑可能会吸收这些功能，重新组织它们，或者取代当前的 WAM 抽象。路线图指定了应该学习和交流的内容，同时保留架构选择的余地。This distinction preserves the value of present WAMs without fixing the future architecture. WAM prototypes offer practical routes to learning intervention-conditioned prediction from video, robot trajectories, simulation, and interaction. A future embodied brain may absorb these capabilities, reorganize them, or replace the current WAM abstraction. The roadmap specifies what should be learned and communicated while leaving that architectural choice open.

角色分离也不禁止联合或端到端训练。单个网络可以实现多个功能，并且梯度可以跨越声明的边界。需求是一个可检查的合同：预期的变更、表示翻译、选择的功能、执行的操作和观察到的结果应保持可归因。这使得泛化声明是可测试的，而不是假设模块化单独产生转移。Role separation also does not prohibit joint or end-to-end training. A single network may implement several functions, and gradients may cross the declared boundaries. The requirement is an inspectable contract: the intended change, representation translation, selected capability, executed action, and observed outcome should remain attributable. This makes generalization claims testable rather than assuming that modularity alone produces transfer.

4.4 数据构建和任务构建：学习和比较的契约Data Construction and Task Building: Contracts for Learning and Comparison

数据构建应该遵循模型和系统需要学习的接口。一旦 WAM 预测声明其干预、范围、框架和结果，数据集就可以记录该结果是否发生。一旦大脑输出声明了预期的转换或功能请求，跟踪就可以记录线束如何解释和执行它。这种对齐将数据收集从信号积累转变为可重用决策上下文的构建。Data construction should follow the interfaces that models and systems need to learn. Once a WAM prediction declares its intervention, horizon, frame, and consequence, a dataset can record whether that consequence occurred. Once a brain output declares an intended transition or capability request, a trace can record how the harness interpreted and executed it. This alignment turns data collection from signal accumulation into the construction of reusable decision contexts.

该提案不需要一种机器人、传感器套件、存储格式或控制接口。它需要足够的元数据来区分共享语义和本地实现。没有校准、参考系、动作界限、控制器假设或故障注释的操纵轨迹可以支持模仿学习。它为结果建模、接口转移或工具替代提供了更弱的证据。The proposal does not require one robot, sensor suite, storage format, or control interface. It requires enough metadata to distinguish shared semantics from local implementation. A manipulation trace without calibration, reference frames, action bounds, controller assumptions, or failure annotations may support imitation learning. It provides much weaker evidence for consequence modeling, interface transfer, or tool substitution.

我们提出了三个轻量级记录。安We propose three lightweight records. A任务卡Task Card描述目标、场景背景、允许的观察和能力、约束、成功标准和有意义的失败模式。一个describes the goal, scene context, permitted observations and capabilities, constraints, success criteria, and meaningful failure modes. A追踪卡Trace Card将同步观察结果链接到大脑请求、表示版本、选定的工具链、验证者决策、控制器状态、执行结果、修正和数据质量标志。links synchronized observations to the brain request, representation version, selected tool chain, verifier decisions, controller state, execution outcome, corrections, and data-quality flags.

这些卡定义了可重播的决策上下文，而不是新的强制文件格式。机器人、人类、模拟和线束数据可以保留其原始工件，同时将相关字段映射到合同中。这保留了特定于实施例的信息，而不允许隐藏那些细节。These cards define a replayable decision context rather than a new mandatory file format. Robot, human, simulation, and harness data can retain their original artifacts while mapping relevant fields into the contract. This preserves embodiment-specific information without allowing those details to remain hidden.

任务构建也应遵循同样的原则。基准应该指定哪些物理变量很重要，哪些观察和工具可用，以及应该诊断哪些故障。它还应该说明评估哪些中间预测或界面转换。然后，数据和任务共享一个语义契约：训练记录包含评估协议稍后需要的证据。Task construction should follow the same principle. A benchmark should specify which physical variables matter, which observations and tools are available, and which failures should be diagnosed. Data and tasks then share one semantic contract: the training record contains the evidence that the evaluation protocol later requires.

4.6 生态系统路线图：累积进步的共享标准Ecosystem Roadmap: Shared Standards for Cumulative Progress

路线图的最后部分涉及社区的累积进展。如果每个模型、机器人、数据集、基准测试和工具都隐藏不同的界面，那么物理智能的扩展成本仍然很高。共享协议可以使这些工件兼容，而无需强加一种硬件设计或学习方法。他们的目的不是统一。他们的目的是使专业化可重用。The final part of the roadmap concerns cumulative community progress. Physical intelligence will remain expensive to scale if each model, robot, dataset, benchmark, and tool hides a different interface. Shared agreements can make these artifacts compatible without imposing one hardware design or learning method. Their purpose is not uniformity. Their purpose is to make specialization reusable.

第一批协议应该可以实施。机器人声明其实施例和控制器假设。大脑模型声明其输出的语义。线束声明其表示适配器和路由决策。工具声明其功能、先决条件和故障模式。数据集记录决策上下文，基准则说明它评估哪一层。实施例、任务和跟踪卡为这些声明提供了一个轻量级的起点。The first agreements should be operational. A robot declares its embodiment and controller assumptions. A brain model declares the semantics of its output. A harness declares its representation adapters and routing decisions. A tool declares its capabilities, preconditions, and failure modes. A dataset records the decision context, and a benchmark states which layer it evaluates. Embodiment, Task, and Trace Cards provide a lightweight starting point for these declarations.

此类合同改变了工作积累的方式。可以在现有意图接口下评估新控制器。可以在同一决策协议中测试新的预测模型。数据集可以支持未来的目标，因为它的框架、干预措施和结果仍然是可解释的。基准测试可以定位改进，而不是仅报告端到端分数。当可以比较和组合独立的贡献而不消除其本地假设时，生态系统就会成功。Such contracts change how work accumulates. A new controller can be evaluated beneath an existing intent interface. A new predictive model can be tested inside the same decision protocol. A dataset can support future objectives because its frames, interventions, and outcomes remain interpretable. A benchmark can localize improvement instead of reporting only an end-to-end score. The ecosystem succeeds when independent contributions can be compared and combined without erasing their local assumptions.

4.7 近期研究议程：从 WAM 原型到实体大脑Near-Term Research Agenda: From WAM Prototypes to an Embodied Brain

该路线图并未将 WAM 视为等待插入具体大脑的已解决模块。它将 WAM 研究视为未来大脑模型可能需要的预测和干预能力的实验途径。三个近期优先事项使这条路线变得可衡量。The roadmap does not treat a WAM as a solved module waiting to be inserted into an embodied brain. It treats WAM research as an experimental route toward predictive and interventional capabilities that a future brain model may require. Three near-term priorities make this route measurable.

第一个优先事项是与决策相关的 WAM 预测合约。当前系统涵盖未来状态和动作预测、视频动作建模、3D 世界建模以及级联或联合预测控制设计The first priority is a decision-relevant WAM prediction contract. Current systems span future-state and action prediction, video–action modeling, 3D world modeling, and cascaded or joint predictive-control designs(guo2024predictionactionvisualpolicy; cheang2024gr2generativevideolanguageactionmodel; li2025unifiedvideoactionmodel; lu2025gwm; wang2026wam)。视频预测可以提供密集的时间目标和可检查的未来. Video prediction can provide dense temporal targets and inspectable futures(finn2017deepvisualforesightplanning; ebert2018visualforesightmodelbaseddeep; zhou2024robodreamerlearningcompositionalworld; liang2022simple)。它的有用性应该通过几何、接触、不确定性和决策改进来测试，而不是从视觉质量推断. Its usefulness should be tested through geometry, contact, uncertainty, and decision improvement rather than inferred from visual quality(robowmbench2026)。具体的里程碑是一个基准，模型在其中声明范围、框架、结果语义和置信度，然后证明这些预测可以改善干预选择或验证。. A concrete milestone is a benchmark in which models declare horizon, frame, consequence semantics, and confidence, then demonstrate that these predictions improve intervention selection or verification.

第二个优先事项是接地的大脑线束表示。近期工作应该比较一种语义契约下的结构化状态、语言、学习标记、3D/4D 表示和混合接口。相关测试是线束是否恢复预期的实体、框架、约束和状态更改。更有力的证据来自于替换工具或实施例，同时保留大脑级别的请求。应报告适配器成本和剩余性能损失，而不是隐藏在端到端微调中。The second priority is a grounded brain–harness representation. Near-term work should compare structured state, language, learned tokens, 3D/4D representations, and hybrid interfaces under one semantic contract. The relevant tests are whether the harness recovers the intended entities, frames, constraints, and state changes. Stronger evidence comes from substituting a tool or embodiment while retaining the brain-level request. Adapter cost and residual performance loss should be reported rather than hidden inside end-to-end fine-tuning.

第三个优先事项是自适应物理安全带和追踪生态系统。能力注册、表示适配器、多模式记忆、验证器集成、监控和恢复都可以在通用实体大脑存在之前进行评估。线束应支持跨控制器、身体部位、外部工具和数字服务的分层工具组合。一个具体的里程碑是一个可重播的跟踪卡，它将失败归因于意图、翻译、工具选择、控制或环境响应。然后，候选更新应在升级之前通过回归和安全门。The third priority is an adaptive physical harness and trace ecosystem. Capability registries, representation adapters, multimodal memory, verifier integration, monitoring, and recovery can each be evaluated before a general embodied brain exists. The harness should support hierarchical tool composition across controllers, body parts, external implements, and digital services. A concrete milestone is a replayable Trace Card that attributes failure to intent, translation, tool selection, control, or environment response. Candidate updates should then pass regression and safety gates before promotion.

这些优先事项共同定义了从 WAM 原型到实体大脑及其周围物理智能堆栈的可实现过渡。他们本地化特定于实施例的适应，同时保留共享的推理目标。他们还将数据收集、界面设计、执行和评估转化为相互提供信息的阶段。长期目标仍然雄心勃勃，但结果契约、接地表示、可替代工具、可重播跟踪和门控更新现在可以构建和测试。Together, these priorities define an achievable transition from WAM prototypes to an embodied brain and its surrounding physical-intelligence stack. They localize embodiment-specific adaptation while preserving a shared reasoning objective. They also convert data collection, interface design, execution, and evaluation into mutually informative stages. The long-term destination remains ambitious, but consequence contracts, grounded representations, substitutable tools, replayable traces, and gated updates can be built and tested now.

5 结论Conclusion

本文调查了以行动为中心的政策、语言调节的机器人学习和 WAM 的预测世界模型的重叠轨迹。他们的共同机会是干预条件的物理推理：估计在行动下可能发生的变化，并使用该估计来改进决策。当前的 WAM 为该功能提供了有前途的原型。它们并不能确定通用物理代理的最终架构。This paper has surveyed the overlapping trajectories from action-centric policies, language-conditioned robot learning, and predictive world models toward WAMs. Their common opportunity is intervention-conditioned physical reasoning: estimating what may change under an action and using that estimate to improve a decision. Current WAMs provide promising prototypes for this capability. They do not determine the final architecture of a general physical agent.

核心限制不仅仅是模型容量。动作输出仅相对于实施例和控制器才有意义。预测接口公开不同的结果变量，而数据、任务和运行时则编码不同的假设。即使单个系统有所改进，这些依赖性也会限制重用。我们通过模型和表示、目标和标准化以及生态系统和系统差距来描述它们。The central limitation is not model capacity alone. Action outputs remain meaningful only relative to an embodiment and controller. Predictive interfaces expose different consequence variables, while data, tasks, and runtimes encode different assumptions. These dependencies restrict reuse even when individual systems improve. We described them through model and representation, objective and standardization, and ecosystem and systems gaps.

共同进化路线图通过明确的责任来解决这些差距。 WAM 预测合约揭示了与决策相关的后果及其范围和不确定性。具身大脑作为长期模型目标，根据上下文和干预进行推理，然后传达预期的状态转换或能力请求。物理线束通过声明的工具模型、控制器和验证器来实现该意图。实施例、任务和跟踪卡保留重建和评估所产生的交互所需的信息。The co-evolution roadmap addresses these gaps through explicit responsibilities. A WAM prediction contract exposes a decision-relevant consequence with its scope and uncertainty. The embodied brain serves as the long-term model target that reasons over context and interventions, then communicates an intended state transition or capability request. A physical harness grounds that intent through declared tool models, controllers, and verifiers. Embodiment, Task, and Trace Cards preserve the information required to reconstruct and evaluate the resulting interaction.

这个组织改变了身体智力的扩展方式。无需将一个执行器空间视为其永久输出，即可改进一般物理推理。特定于实施例的适配可以在工具和适配器中保持可见。兼容的跟踪可以支持比较、故障归因和回归门控后训练。这些好处仍然是通过基于决策的预测、表示基础、工具替代、跨实施例适应和安全学习进度来测试的假设。This organization changes how physical intelligence can scale. General physical reasoning can be improved without treating one actuator space as its permanent output. Embodiment-specific adaptation can remain visible in tools and adapters. Compatible traces can support comparison, failure attribution, and regression-gated post-training. These benefits remain hypotheses to test through decision-grounded prediction, representation grounding, tool substitution, cross-embodiment adaptation, and safe learning progress.

该路线图并没有声称接口本身就可以产生 AGI，或者应该提前修复一种表示形式。它提供了一条技术上可行的路径，模型、数据、工具和系统可以通过该路径分别改进，同时为共享的物理智能堆栈做出贡献。如果这些接口成熟，异构的物理体验可以累积起来，而不是特定于项目。这种转变可能会让适应性和自我改进的智能体更接近基于与物理世界持续互动的通用智能。The roadmap does not claim that interfaces alone produce AGI or that one representation should be fixed in advance. It provides a technically actionable path by which models, data, tools, and systems can improve separately while contributing to a shared physical-intelligence stack. If these interfaces mature, heterogeneous physical experience can become cumulative rather than project-specific. That shift may bring adaptive and self-improving agents closer to general intelligence grounded in sustained interaction with the physical world.
