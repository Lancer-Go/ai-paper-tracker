AnnoBench：可视化注释生成的基准AnnoBench: A Benchmark for Visualization Annotation Generation

摘要Abstract

注释是自动化中要求最高的可视化任务之一，因为它同时需要正确导航视觉、语义和风格约束。不满足任何这些条件都会严重损害注释的实用性，使其难以阅读、不准确或视觉上不一致。尽管注释工具和自动化越来越多，但没有现有的基准或评估框架测试是否满足这些条件，因为它们的范围和注释不是他们研究的重点。 我们推出 AnnoBench，这是可视化注释的基准，它以结构化和可测试的方式具体化了该领域的固有挑战。 AnnoBench 将专业数据新闻和可视化库的可视化与注释任务配对，涵盖四种表示格式、五种图表描述条件和两种提示规范级别。 该基准测试是通过 VLM 作为法官执行的，使用与人工评估一致的模型。 我们通过四个一次单因素实验来评估基准，探索输入表示、语义上下文、提示特异性以及模型选择对注释质量的影响。 这项工作为推进注释自动化、工具和可视化生成管道奠定了基础。Annotation is among the most demanding visualization tasks to automate, as it simultaneously requires correctly navigating visual, semantic, and stylistic constraints. Failure to meet any of these conditions severely undermines the utility of an annotation, rendering it challenging to read, inaccurate, or visually discordant. Despite a growing body of annotation tools and automations, no existing benchmark or evaluation framework tests whether these conditions are met because of their scope and annotation not being the focus of their studies. We introduce AnnoBench, a benchmark for visualization annotation that materializes the inherent challenges of this domain in a structured and testable manner. AnnoBench pairs visualizations from professional data journalism and visualization galleries with annotation tasks, spanning four representation formats, five chart description conditions, and two prompt specification levels. The benchmark is executed via VLM-as-a-judge, using models aligned with manual human assessment. We evaluate the benchmark via four one-factor-at-a-time experiments, exploring the effects of input representation, semantic context, and prompt specificity, and model selection on annotation quality. This work provides a foundation for advancing annotation automation, tooling, and visualization-generation pipelines.

1 简介Introduction

注释是可视化中最关键的组成部分之一Annotations are among the most critical components of a visualization[43]，通过仔细的布局和上下文来引导读者浏览内容，这些内容必须在图形和语义上都适当。 由于这些原因，注释是要求自动化的最苛刻的可视化任务之一。注释必须针对正确的视觉元素, guiding readers through content via careful placement and contextualization that must be both graphically and semantically appropriate. For these reasons, annotation is among the most demanding visualization tasks to automate. An annotation must target the correct visual element[27, 50]，仅断言底层编码支持的内容, assert only what the underlying encoding supports[43, 20]，并适合可视化的现有布局，而不遮挡证据, and fit within the visualization’s existing layout without occluding evidence[24]。这些条件以多种方式复合，使得故障难以预测。例如，如果某个条形图编码了第二高值，则将该条形图标记为“峰值收入”的注释是不正确的，即使注释文本措辞良好且位置清晰。相反，事实上正确的注释可能在不遮挡其引用的数据的情况下在密集标记的区域中没有可行的位置。 注释在视觉上锚定到它们所描述的元素，因此此类错误可能很难检测到. These conditions compound in ways that make failures hard to anticipate. For example, an annotation labeling a bar as “peak revenue” is incorrect if that bar encodes the second-highest value, even if the annotation text is well-phrased and clearly positioned. Conversely, a factually correct annotation may have no viable placement in a densely labeled region without occluding the data it references. Annotations are visually anchored to the elements they describe, and so such errors can be difficult to detect[30, 14]并可以通过其他准确的可视化来改变读者的结论and can shift reader conclusions in otherwise accurate visualizations[55].

一系列工具已经应对了这一挑战，包括交互式环境A range of tools have taken up this challenge, including interactive environments[50]、半自动洞察外化系统, semi-automatic insight externalization systems[9]、自动生成管道, automated generation pipelines[20, 15]和规范语法, and specification grammars[48, 10]。先前的工作还描述了注释形式和目标的特征. Prior work has also characterized annotation forms and targets[50, 45, 27]，注释所服务的交际目的, the communicative purposes annotations serve[45, 20, 47]，以及它们对读者理解和偏见感知的可测量影响, and their measurable effects on reader comprehension and bias perception[54, 55, 39, 13, 14]。然而，在工具和研究文献中，注释正确性的评估条件仍然没有定义. Yet across both the tools and the research literature, evaluable conditions for annotation correctness remain undefined[43, 53]。如果没有这样的条件，注释质量只能被非正式地判断，并且无法系统地识别或跨系统比较故障。图表相关任务的现有基准评估图表理解力. Without such conditions, annotation quality can only be judged informally, and failures cannot be systematically identified or compared across systems. Existing benchmarks for chart-related tasks evaluate chart comprehension[28, 4, 18], 文字答案, text answers[33, 35]，自然语言描述, natural-language descriptions[56, 22]，或图表规格, or chart specifications[8, 49]，但没有考虑注释质量。 随着我们越来越多地利用生成式人工智能模型来支持可视化构建，这些问题变得更加复杂, but none considers annotation quality. These problems further compound as we increasingly draw on generative AI models for visualization construction support[29, 58].

2 相关工作Related Work

我们将 AnnoBench 置于两个工作的交叉点： 注释结构和效果的可视化研究， 以及 LLM 和 VLM 在可视化任务上的基准评估。We situate AnnoBench at the intersection of two bodies of work: visualization research on the structure and effects of annotations, and benchmark evaluation of LLMs and VLMs on visualization tasks.

2.1 可视化中的注释Annotations in Visualization

注释研究主要涉及三个方面 与 AnnoBench 相关：注释的结构如何，它们如何 形式化，以及它们的设计如何影响读者。Research on annotations addresses three aspects relevant to AnnoBench: how annotations are structured, how they are formalized, and how their design affects readers.

注释结构和形式化。Annotation structure and formalization.注释是图表绑定的附加内容，附加文本或图形 对特定图表元素进行解释、强调、比较或的提示 将数据特征置于上下文中Annotations are chart-bound additions that attach text or graphical cues to specific chart elements to explain, emphasize, compare, or contextualize data features[27, 45, 46]。两种结构尺寸 组织这个空间——注释形式和注释目标。形式是指图形载体 注释，例如文本标签、连接器、附件和 亮点。目标是指图表元素的注释 地址，例如单个数据项、区域或更广泛的图表 结构. Two structural dimensions organize this space—annotation forms and annotation targets. Form refers to the graphical vehicle of the annotation, such as text labels, connectors, enclosures, and highlights. Target refers to the chart element the annotation addresses, such as individual data items, regions, or broader chart structures[27, 50]。几个 系统和语法使这些维度变得明确。例如，ChartAccent 围绕配对形式和目标组织注释设计 选择. Several systems and grammars make these dimensions explicit. For instance, ChartAccent organizes annotation design around paired form and target choices[50]。上下文语境区分 重述可见模式的注释 外部环境和模型锚定在多个层面 可视化. Contextifier distinguishes annotations that restate visible patterns from those that introduce external context and models anchoring at multiple levels of the visualization[20]。最近基于语法 工作将注释语义、目标和位置视为 一流的可视化组件 规格. Recent grammar-based work treats annotation semantics, targets, and placement as first-class components of visualization specifications[48, 10]。在 在实践中，单一形式往往不足以明确 确定其目标。因此，专业图形经常 将表单组合成整体，例如连接器+文本和 外壳+连接器+文本，提示选择因任务而异 显示上下文. In practice, a single form is often insufficient to unambiguously identify its target. Professional graphics, therefore, frequently combine forms into ensembles such as connector+text and enclosure+connector+text, with cue selection varying by task and display context[45, 46, 25]。观察到的实践表明，形式目标的选择仍然取决于任务、显示密度和交流目的. Observed practice suggests that form-target choices remain dependent on task, display density, and communicative purpose[45, 46, 25].

Whether an annotation restates a pattern visible in the chart or introduces external context constrains both the appropriate form and the claims it can legitimately make[20, 45]。 演示文稿的选择更广泛地影响记忆力， 理解力，以及 回忆. Presentation choices more broadly influence memorability, comprehension, and recall[6, 5, 3], 在带注释的视图中，具体而言，放置、链接和布局会影响注视行为、对引用证据的理解， 和认知负荷, and within annotated views, specifically, placement, linking, and layout affect gaze behavior, comprehension of referenced evidence, and cognitive load[7, 39, 63, 2]。这些效果是 取决于设计。增强选择因形式和提示而异 整个演示的有效性发生变化 设置. These effects are design-dependent. Augmentation choices vary by form, and cue effectiveness changes across presentation settings[11, 17, 26]。 同样的机制也会扭曲解释。即使基础图表准确，文本数量、框架和措辞也会改变读者的看法和感知偏差. The same mechanisms can also distort interpretation. Text amount, framing, and wording shift reader takeaways and perceived bias even when the underlying chart is accurate[55, 54, 13, 14, 30]。因为这些影响 都是设计相关的和必然的，注释错误是 不仅仅是风格上的。不正确的位置、误导性的措辞或语义上不恰当的上下文可能会改变读者的结论 来自准确的图表。. Because these effects are both design-dependent and consequential, annotation errors are not merely stylistic. Incorrect placement, misleading phrasing, or semantically inappropriate context can change what readers conclude from an otherwise accurate chart.

先前的工作提供了用于描述注释的词汇表， 用于指定它们的正式工具，以及注释的证据 设计塑造读者的结果。但它并没有确定是否 特定注释对于给定的图表和任务是正确的。 这样做需要可验证的每个实例条件：是否 注释针对预期的视觉元素，仅断言什么 编码支持，可以无遮挡放置 证据。设计指南、读者成果衡量标准以及 编辑惯例在更广泛的层面上运作，因此 不提供这样的条件。拉赫曼等人。Prior work provides a vocabulary for describing annotations, formal tools for specifying them, and evidence that annotation design shapes reader outcomes. But it does not establish whether a particular annotation is correct for a given chart and task. Doing so requires verifiable per-instance conditions: whether an annotation targets the intended visual element, asserts only what the encoding supports, and can be placed without occluding evidence. Design guidelines, reader outcome measures, and editorial conventions operate at a broader level and therefore do not provide such conditions. Rahman et al.[43]以及斯皮瓦克和托里and Spivak & Tory[53]识别这种缺席 作为一个开放的问题。在identify this absence as an open problem. 越来越多的基准测试与图表相关的 LLM 和 VLM 任务，涵盖图表理解、自然语言描述以及图表生成和编辑。A growing body of benchmarks evaluates LLMs and VLMs on chart-related tasks, spanning chart comprehension, natural language description, and chart generation and editing.

7 讨论与结论Discussion & Conclusion

图表注释是一项多维挑战，共同取决于表示、指令特异性和语义上下文。没有任何单一因素可以单独决定成功；相反，注释质量来自图表理解与执行精确视觉修改的能力之间的相互作用，我们使用五维评估框架对其进行了评估。在评估各种模型的注释时，我们遇到了一些关键的观察结果。Chart annotation is a multi-dimensional challenge that depends jointly on representation, instruction specificity, and semantic context. No single factor alone determines success; rather, annotation quality emerges from the interaction between chart understanding and the ability to execute precise visual modifications, which we evaluated with a five-dimensional evaluation framework. In such cases, annotations appear visually plausible but no longer correspond to the correct data, leading to low human scores in precise, relevant, and preservant (see supplementary document for examples). While automated judging aligns reasonably well with human evaluation for structured representations, it performs less reliably on raster inputs, where it struggles to detect input corruption and may overestimate semantically incorrect results. These findings suggest that, although VLM-based judging is useful for scaling evaluation, it should be applied with caution in settings involving high design flexibility, such as annotation tasks.

AnnoBench 浏览器的可扩展性。Extensibility of AnnoBench Browser.AnnoBench 浏览器作为 Python 库发布，旨在 可沿多个维度进行扩展。用户可以按照文档和标准化数据架构添加新的可视化数据集。对于不支持的图表表示，可以通过 API 注册自定义渲染函数，从而实现与其他可视化框架的集成。此外，该系统支持语义描述策略和注释提示级别的扩展，允许研究人员引入新的图表描述方法或任务变体。重要的是，AnnoBench 不限于 LLM 或 VLM；它可用于评估任何采用图表和任务提示作为输入并生成带注释的图表作为输出的系统，包括基于规则的方法、程序合成方法或混合人类人工智能工作流程。AnnoBench Browser is released as a Python library and is designed to be extensible along several dimensions. Users can add new visualization datasets by following the documentation and standardized data schema. For unsupported chart representations, custom rendering functions can be registered through the API, enabling integration with additional visualization frameworks. Furthermore, the system supports extension of semantic description strategies and annotation prompt levels, allowing researchers to introduce new chart description methodologies or task variations. Importantly, AnnoBench is not limited to LLMs or VLMs; it can be used to evaluate any system that takes a chart and a task prompt as input and produces an annotated chart as output, including rule-based methods, program synthesis approaches, or hybrid human-AI workflows.

局限性。Limitations.尽管 AnnoBench 的范围很广，但它也有一些局限性。 首先，尽管我们考虑了多种图表表示形式和数据集，但由于 API 成本和人工评估工作的实际限制，我们的评估仅涵盖了可能配置的子集。我们还评估了一组有限的模型；虽然足以进行比较分析，但更广泛的模型覆盖范围将加强普遍性。 其次，虽然我们包含了多种注释类型，但我们的任务受到可用数据集中存在的分布的限制，并且可能无法完全捕获所有现实世界的注释场景。第三，我们的评估一次改变一个维度，这可以实现受控分析，但可能会错过维度之间的高阶相互作用。 第四，我们的评估依赖于固定的评估标准和有限数量的评估人员。尽管评估者间的可靠性很高，但某些维度，特别是风格一致性，本质上仍然是主观的。当我们将基于 VLM 的判断纳入量表评估时，我们观察到明显的失败模式，并且此类方法不应被视为人类评估的替代品。为视觉任务设计更可靠的自动判断器仍然是一个开放的挑战。 第五，我们的实验侧重于静态图表，不考虑交互式或动画可视化，这给注释设计和评估带来了额外的复杂性。 最后，模型功能快速发展，这可能会降低本文报告的特定模型性能结果的长期相关性。因此，我们不将模型输出包含在基准数据集中，而是将其作为时间点评估来展示 AnnoBench 在分析可视化注释维度方面的实用性。Despite the breadth of AnnoBench, it has several limitations. First, although we consider multiple chart representations and datasets, our evaluation covers only a subset of possible configurations due to practical constraints on API cost and human assessment effort. We also evaluate a limited set of models; while sufficient for comparative analysis, broader model coverage would strengthen generality. Second, while we include a diverse set of annotation types, our tasks are constrained by the distributions present in available datasets and may not fully capture all real-world annotation scenarios. Third, our evaluation varies one dimension at a time, which enables controlled analysis but may miss higher-order interactions between dimensions. Fourth, our evaluation relies on a fixed rubric and a limited number of human evaluators. Although inter-rater reliability is high, some dimensions, particularly stylistic consistency, remain inherently subjective. While we incorporate VLM-based judging to scale evaluation, we observe clear failure modes, and such methods should not be treated as a substitute for human assessment. Designing more reliable automated judges for visual tasks remains an open challenge. Fifth, our experiments focus on static charts and do not consider interactive or animated visualizations, which introduce additional complexities for both annotation design and evaluation. Finally, model capabilities evolve rapidly, which may reduce the long-term relevance of the specific model performance results reported in this paper. For this reason, we do not include model outputs in the benchmark dataset and instead present them as a point-in-time evaluation to demonstrate the utility of AnnoBench for analyzing visualization annotation dimensions.

结论。Conclusion.我们提出了 AnnoBench，这是一个通过每个实例的正确性标准和图表表示、任务规范和语义上下文的受控变化来评估图表注释的基准。在我们的实验中，当前的模型显示出理解图表内容与正确编辑图表以表达这种理解之间存在明显的差距。当模型接收结构化、可编辑的图表表示和明确的执行级别指令时，性能会显着提高，但栅格输入、未指定的提示和图表保存失败仍然是长期存在的弱点。这些发现表明，注释自动化的进步需要的不仅仅是更强的图表理解。它还需要支持忠实的图表编辑、更强的注释策略推理以及不奖励视觉上合理但语义上不正确的输出的评估程序的方法。 AnnoBench 为这项工作提供了具体的基础。We present AnnoBench, a benchmark for evaluating chart annotation through per-instance correctness criteria and controlled variation in chart representation, task specification, and semantic context. Across our experiments, current models show a clear gap between understanding what a chart says and correctly editing it to express that understanding. Performance improves substantially when models receive structured, editable chart representations and explicit execution-level instructions, but raster inputs, underspecified prompts, and chart-preservation failures remain persistent weaknesses. These findings suggest that progress in annotation automation will require more than stronger chart comprehension alone. It will also require methods that support faithful chart editing, stronger inference of annotation strategies, and evaluation procedures that do not reward visually plausible but semantically incorrect outputs. AnnoBench provides a concrete basis for that work.

AnnoBench 是作为一个 Python 库实现的，具有基于 Web 的界面，遵循数据驱动的设计。每个数据集都组织成图表和任务，其中图表包含多种表示形式（例如，光栅图像、SVG、Vega/Vega-Lite 规范或 D3 代码），任务定义注释目标、提示变量和评估设置。这种结构允许以不同的方式呈现相同的图表，同时保持不同表示形式的任务一致。AnnoBench is implemented as a Python library with a web-based interface, following a data-driven design. Each dataset is organized into charts and tasks, where charts include multiple representations (e.g., raster images, SVG, Vega/Vega-Lite specifications, or D3 code), and tasks define annotation objectives, prompt variants, and evaluation settings. This structure allows the same chart to be presented in different modalities while keeping tasks consistent across representations.

基准管道通过组合所选的图表表示、语义描述和任务提示来自动构建模型输入。根据配置，模型可以接收视觉输入、结构化规范或代码表示。然后对输出进行标准化，并在必要时将其渲染成图像，以便人类和自动化方法可以跨表示一致地评估结果。The benchmark pipeline automatically constructs model inputs by combining the selected chart representations, semantic descriptions, and task prompts. Depending on the configuration, models may receive visual inputs, structured specifications, or code representations. Outputs are then standardized and, when necessary, rendered into images so that both humans and automated methods can evaluate the results consistently across representations.

AnnoBench支持多种评估策略，包括基于规则的检查、基于相似性的比较、人工评估和VLM作为法官的方法。该系统旨在处理生成或渲染期间的错误，并允许重新运行失败的案例，将模型推理失败与执行问题分开。AnnoBench supports multiple evaluation strategies, including rule-based checks, similarity-based comparisons, human evaluation, and VLM-as-a-judge methods. The system is designed to handle errors during generation or rendering and to allow rerunning failed cases, separating model reasoning failures from execution issues.

为了支持大规模实验，该系统管理基准测试运行，以结构化格式存储结果，并支持跨不同配置的可重复分析。 Web 界面提供了用于启动实验、检查模型输出以及跨数据集、模型和评估标准分析结果的工具。总体而言，该实现被设计为灵活且可扩展，使研究人员能够在统一的框架内评估不同的模型、图表表示和注释任务。To support large-scale experimentation, the system manages benchmark runs, stores results in a structured format, and enables reproducible analysis across different configurations. The web interface provides tools for launching experiments, inspecting model outputs, and analyzing results across datasets, models, and evaluation criteria. Overall, the implementation is designed to be flexible and extensible, allowing researchers to evaluate diverse models, chart representations, and annotation tasks within a unified framework.
