摘要Abstract

语音大语言模型 (SpeechLLM) 可减少延迟并保留副语言的细微差别，而这些细微差别通常在级联自动语音识别 (ASR) 和基于文本的 LM 架构中丢失。然而，它们在复杂推理任务上仍然落后于纯文本法学硕士，而实时语音交互则施加了严格的延迟限制。尽管先前的工作采用思想链（CoT）和并发推理来增强推理能力，而不会引起令人望而却步的延迟，但固有的准确性与延迟之间的权衡仍然存在。在本文中，我们研究了流式 SpeechLLM 是否可以动态地动态修改其推理轨迹。我们介绍Speech large language models (SpeechLLMs) offer reduced latency and retain paralinguistic nuances that are typically lost in cascaded automatic speech recognition (ASR) and text-based LM architectures. However, they continue to lag behind text-only LLMs on complex reasoning tasks, while real-time spoken interaction imposes strict latency constraints. Although prior works employ Chain-of-Thought (CoT) and concurrent reasoning to enhance reasoning capabilities without inducing prohibitive delays, an inherent accuracy-latency trade-off persists. In this paper, we investigate whether a streaming SpeechLLM can dynamically revise its reasoning traces on the fly. We introduce复古思想家RetroThinker，一个多阶段后训练框架，使 Moshi 模型能够在推理过程中自我验证和前向纠正 CoT 步骤。 RetroThinker 将针对策划的回顾性思维数据的监督微调 (SFT) 与基于长度的直接偏好优化 (DPO) 结合起来，以在早期推理过程中优化回顾（即在用户说话的同时进行推理）。根据 GSM8K 基准进行评估，RetroThinker 显着改善了非回顾性基线的准确性与延迟权衡，在相当的延迟下实现了 11% 的绝对准确性增益。, a multi-stage post-training framework that equips the Moshi model to self-verify and forward-correct CoT steps during inference. RetroThinker combines supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on curated retrospective thinking data with length-based direct preference optimization (DPO) to optimize retrospective during early reasoning (i.e., reasoning concurrently while the user speaks). Evaluated on the GSM8K benchmark, RetroThinker significantly improves the accuracy-latency trade-off over non-retrospective baselines, achieving an 11% absolute accuracy gain at a comparable latency.

I 简介Introduction

近年来，人工智能代理已变得普遍，提供无缝集成到智能手机和智能眼镜等个人设备中的各种功能。 在所有方式中，语音是人机交互最直接、最自然的界面。 与文本不同，语音传达的信息要多得多，可以捕获说话者的身份、韵律和情感细微差别，而这些细微差别通常在转录中丢失。In recent years, AI agents have become pervasive, offering diverse capabilities seamlessly integrated into personal devices like smartphones and smart glasses. Among all modalities, speech serves as the most direct and natural interface for human-agent interaction. Unlike text, speech conveys substantially more information, capturing speaker identity, prosody, and emotional nuances typically lost in transcription.

大多数当代人工智能代理都依赖大型语言模型（LLM）作为其支柱，并接受海量文本语料库的训练。 因此，使它们能够处理语音通常遵循两种范式之一。 第一种是级联方法Most contemporary AI agents rely on Large Language Models (LLMs) as their backbone and are trained on massive text corpora. Consequently, enabling them to process speech generally follows one of the two paradigms. The first is a cascaded approach[1, 2, 3]，它连接了模块化组件：自动语音识别 (ASR)、基于文本的 LLM 和文本到语音合成 (TTS)。 第二个是预训练和微调 LLM 以直接使用和生成语音令牌。 这种方法，我们称之为 SpeechLLM, which concatenates modular components: Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), a text-based LLM, and Text-to-Speech synthesis (TTS). The second is to pretrain and fine-tune LLMs to directly consume and generate speech tokens. This approach, which we term SpeechLLMs[4, 5, 6]，绕过级联系统固有的模块化约束和信息瓶颈。 SpeechLLM 的主要优点包括较低的推理延迟、简化的部署以及处理副语言信号的卓越能力。 因此，它们具有独特的能力来促进人与机器之间的自然对话。, bypasses the modular constraints and information bottlenecks inherent in cascaded systems. The primary advantages of SpeechLLMs include lower inference latency, simplified deployment, and a superior capacity to process paralinguistic signals. As a result, they are uniquely equipped to facilitate naturalistic conversations between humans and machines.

尽管 SpeechLLM 具有固有的优势，但仍然存在两个重大挑战。首先，最近的研究表明，与类似规模和训练数据量的基于文本的对应物相比，它们在推理和一般智能方面往往表现不佳Despite the inherent advantages of SpeechLLMs, two significant challenges remain. First, recent studies reveal that they often underperform in reasoning and general intelligence when compared to text-based counterparts of similar scale and training data volume[7, 8]。 与简单的事实检索相比，这种性能差异在复杂的推理任务中尤其明显。 第二个挑战是响应延迟比基于文本的法学硕士受到的限制要大得多，因为用户期望与语音代理进行近乎实时的交互。. This performance disparity is particularly pronounced in complex reasoning tasks, as opposed to simple factual retrieval. The second challenge is that response latency is far more constrained than in text-based LLMs, as users expect near real-time interactions with voice agents.

受到思想链 (CoT) 提示的激励Motivated by Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting[9], 近期作品, recent works[7, 10, 11, 12]将 CoT 集成到 SpeechLLM 中以增强其会话推理能力。 CoT 最初是在 NLP 社区中引入的，现在被广泛采用，通过引发逐步推理来提高模型性能。 通过将复杂问题分解为中间逻辑步骤，CoT 显着提高了不同基准的准确性。 然而，当应用于 SpeechLLM 时，生成额外的 CoT 跟踪会显着增加延迟。integrate CoT into SpeechLLMs to enhance their conversational reasoning capabilities. Originally introduced in the NLP community, CoT is widely adopted to boost model performance by eliciting step-by-step reasoning. By decomposing complex problems into intermediate logical steps, CoT significantly improves accuracy across diverse benchmarks. However, when applied to SpeechLLMs, generating the additional CoT trace substantially increases latency.

为了缓解这个问题，之前的工作通过启用并发推理来减少延迟：模型要么在用户仍在说话时启动推理过程To mitigate this, prior work reduces latency by enabling concurrent reasoning: models either initiate the reasoning process while the user is still speaking[7, 10]，或者在生成语音输出的同时进行推理, or simultaneously reason while generating speech output[11, 12]。 这种范式转变允许 SpeechLLM 在推理的同时处理音频。 虽然这改进了推理并减少了响应延迟，但固有的准确性-延迟帕累托权衡仍然存在. This paradigm shift allows SpeechLLMs to process audio concurrently with reasoning. While this improves reasoning and reduces response latency, an inherent accuracy-latency Pareto trade-off remains[13].

为了进一步推动这种准确性-延迟帕累托前沿，我们探索了一种并行范例：To push this accuracy-latency Pareto frontier further, we explore a parallel paradigm:回顾性思考retrospective thinking。 在人类对话中，当解决复杂的问题时，个人在内部推理过程中偶尔会犯逻辑错误。 然而，人类拥有认知灵活性，可以在对话过程中反思这些想法并进行实时修正. In human conversation, when addressing complex inquiries, individuals occasionally commit logical errors during their internal reasoning processes. However, humans possess the cognitive flexibility to reflect on these thoughts and perform real-time revisions during the dialogue[14]。 受这种行为的激励，我们向 SpeechLLM 引入了回顾性思维的概念。 与人类同行非常相似，SpeechLLM 在推理过程中不可避免地会在推理轨迹中引入错误，特别是在严格的实时约束下。 因此，我们认为回顾性思维可以通过允许模型动态识别和纠正此类错误来提高推理准确性。. Motivated by this behavior, we introduce the concept of retrospective thinking to SpeechLLMs. Much like their human counterparts, it is inevitable that SpeechLLMs will introduce errors within their reasoning traces during inference, particularly under strict real-time constraints. Consequently, we posit that retrospective thinking can improve reasoning accuracy by allowing the model to identify and rectify such errors dynamically.

我们建议We propose复古思想家RetroThinker，一个训练后框架，为 SpeechLLM 提供实时回顾性思维能力。 调整回顾性抽样, a post-training framework that equips SpeechLLMs with real-time retrospective thinking capabilities. Adapting retrospective sampling[15]，我们将专门的微调技术应用于 SpeechLLM，使它们能够识别不正确的 CoT 步骤并随后进行修改。 正在关注, we apply specialized fine-tuning techniques to SpeechLLMs, enabling them to identify incorrect CoT steps and subsequently revise them. Following[7]，我们选择莫西, we select Moshi[4]作为我们的基础演讲法学硕士。 其原生多流架构提供了将 CoT 推理注入文本流的灵活性，而不会中断同步的音频输入和输出流。 此外，为了减轻回顾性思维过程引入的额外延迟，我们研究了它与先前工作中的两种延迟减少技术的相互作用as our foundational SpeechLLM. Its native multistream architecture offers the flexibility to inject CoT reasoning into the text stream without disrupting the synchronized audio input and output streams. Furthermore, to mitigate the additional latency introduced by the retrospective thinking process, we investigate its interplay with two latency-reduction techniques from prior work[7]：早期推理（即“边听边思考”）和直接偏好优化（DPO）: Early Reasoning (i.e., “thinking while listening”) and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)[16]。 对 TTS 语音 GSM8K 的评估表明，结合所有三种技术可以改善受控多步推理设置中的准确性与延迟权衡。 此外，我们还进行了深入分析，以阐明这些技术如何相互影响。 总而言之，我们的主要贡献如下：. Additionally, we conduct an in-depth analysis to elucidate how these techniques influence one another. In summary, our key contributions are as follows:

II 背景Background

Ⅱ-AII-A 莫希Moshi

莫希Moshi[4]是一个多流、全双工 SpeechLLM。 在每次前向传递期间，模型同时处理三个不同的流：用户音频、系统文本（也称为内部独白）和系统音频。 为了将这些异构输入处理为离散序列，Moshi 利用 Mimi 编解码器使用 8 个码本，以 80 毫秒的间隔将连续语音波形离散化为音频标记。 内部独白流提供与系统语音输出同步的单词级、时间对齐的转录本。 在架构上，Moshi 使用了时间转换器和深度转换器。 在每个时间步，时间转换器都会消耗所有三个输入流来预测系统文本流中的下一个标记。 随后，深度转换器采用时间转换器的输出来预测系统音频流的 8 个音频标记。 时间转换器在集成到 Moshi 之前经过初始纯文本预训练。 最后，完整的模型在大规模音频数据集上进行预训练，并在多流口语对话数据上进行微调。is a multi-stream, full-duplex SpeechLLM. During each forward pass, the model concurrently processes three distinct streams: User Audio, System Text (a.k.a., inner monologue), and System Audio. To process these heterogeneous inputs as discrete sequences, Moshi leverages the Mimi codec to discretize continuous speech waveforms into audio tokens at 80 ms intervals using 8 codebooks. The inner monologue stream provides word-level, time-aligned transcripts synchronized with the system’s speech output. Architecturally, Moshi utilizes a Temporal Transformer coupled with a Depth Transformer. At each timestep, the Temporal Transformer consumes all three input streams to predict the next token in the system text stream. Subsequently, the Depth Transformer takes the Temporal Transformer’s output to predict the 8 audio tokens for the system audio stream. The temporal transformer undergoes initial text-only pre-training before being integrated into Moshi. Finally, the complete model is pre-trained on large-scale audio datasets and fine-tuned on multi-stream spoken conversational data.

Ⅱ-CII-C 回顾性抽样Retrospective Sampling

反向In REVERSE[15]，作者通过回顾性采样能力训练视觉语言模型（VLM）在图像字幕任务中减轻幻觉。 在他们的框架内，字幕首先被划分为多个片段。 随后，采用基于规则的方法和LLM提示相结合来合成错误字幕片段。 然后将这些错误片段随机插入到相应的真实片段之前。 在每个片段的末尾，附加一个专门的自验证标记来指示前一个片段的准确性。 在训练阶段，对注入的错误段屏蔽下一个令牌预测损失，以防止模型学习错误的生成模式。 在推理过程中，作者引入了一种称为“回顾性抽样”的机制。 如果自验证令牌识别出某个段不正确，系统会将生成回滚到特定点，并采用各种策略重新生成该段，以确保事实一致性。, the authors mitigate hallucinations in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) on image captioning task by training them with retrospective sampling abilities. Within their framework, captions are first partitioned into multiple segments. Subsequently, a combination of rule-based methods and LLM prompting is employed to synthesize error caption segments. These error segments are then randomly inserted before the corresponding ground-truth segments. At the end of each segment, a specialized self-validation token is appended to indicate the accuracy of the preceding segment. During the training phase, the next-token prediction loss is masked for the injected error segments to prevent the model from learning incorrect generation patterns. During inference, the authors introduce a mechanism termed “Retrospective Sampling.” If the self-verification token identifies a segment as incorrect, the system rolls back the generation to a specific point and employs various strategies to regenerate the segment, ensuring factual consistency.

Therefore, we structure our data such that the model learns to identify, revise, and proceed with subsequent CoT steps even when interleaved with previous errors. We perform Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on Moshi using this data, masking the loss on the synthesized erroneous CoT tokens while retaining loss on the verification markers and corrected continuation, similarly to[15].

第 2 阶段：基于样本的分布对齐。Stage 2: Sample-based Distribution Alignment.虽然第一阶段建立了回顾性思维的结构格式，但基于规则的错误通常无法反映推理过程中模型失败的实际分布。 为了减轻这种分布不匹配的情况，我们提出了第二阶段基于样本的微调。 我们从第一阶段检查点生成推理轨迹，并使用法学硕士作为法官来评估给定问题和先前推理上下文的每个步骤。 每当法官识别出错误的步骤时，它就会返回从该点到最终答案的正确延续。 然后，我们使用模型生成的误差作为屏蔽的负步骤，并使用法官提供的延续作为正校正，构建回顾性训练示例。 这保持了与第一阶段相同的损失掩蔽策略，同时使校正行为与真实的模型故障保持一致。While Stage 1 establishes the structural format for retrospective thinking, rule-based errors often fail to reflect the actual distribution of model failures during inference. To mitigate this distribution mismatch, we proposed a second stage of sample-based fine-tuning. We generate reasoning traces from the Stage 1 checkpoint and use an LLM-as-a-judge to evaluate each step given the question and preceding reasoning context. Whenever the judge identifies an incorrect step, it returns a corrected continuation from that point to the final answer. We then construct retrospective training examples by using the model-generated error as the masked negative step and the continuation provided by the judge as the positive correction. This keeps the same loss-masking strategy as Stage 1 while aligning the correction behavior to authentic model failures.

第 3 阶段：基于长度的 DPO 微调。Stage 3: Length-based DPO Fine-tuning.虽然回顾性思维可以显着提高准确性，但包含修订步骤却会显着增加总思维长度，从而增加响应延迟。 为了解决这个问题，我们采用 LengthDPOWhile retrospective thinking significantly enhances accuracy, the inclusion of revision steps non-trivially increases the total thinking length, thereby escalating response latency. To address this, we employ LengthDPO[7]在不牺牲准确性的情况下压缩推理轨迹。 具体来说，我们对第二阶段模型的输出进行采样，并构建偏好对，其中首选示例正确且简洁，而不首选示例不正确。 除了减少延迟之外，DPO 微调还可以作为减少固有分布不匹配的关键机制。to compress the reasoning trace without sacrificing accuracy. Specifically, we sample outputs from the Stage 2 model and construct preference pairs where preferred examples are correct and concise, while dispreferred examples are incorrect. Beyond latency reduction, DPO fine-tuning serves as a critical mechanism for reducing the distribution mismatch inherent in早期推理early reasoning [7]。 在第二阶段，推理痕迹由法学硕士进行判断和修改，而不会受到用户查询可能尚未完全接收的约束。 通过应用 DPO. In Stage 2, the reasoning traces are judged and revised by an LLM without exposure to the constraint that the user’s query may not yet be fully received. By applying DPO in the早期推理early reasoning设置中，模型学习使其回顾能力适应不完整用户查询的不确定性。setting, the model learns to adapt its retrospective capabilities to the uncertainties of incomplete user queries.

推理策略。Inference Strategy.背离回滚机制Departing from the rollback mechanism in[15]，我们的推理过程遵循 SpeechLLM 的流约束。 我们的模型执行“仅向前”的自我校正：它继续生成后续的 CoT 步骤和最终响应，同时在文本流中保留整个推理历史记录，包括不正确和正确的步骤。 如果模型发出否定验证标记，则会像普通的后续文本一样生成更正。 此设置与我们微调模型的方式相匹配。, our inference procedure adheres to the streaming constraints of SpeechLLMs. Our model performs “forward-only” self-correction: it continues generating subsequent CoT steps and the final response while preserving the entire reasoning history, including both incorrect and corrected steps, in the text stream. If the model emits a negative verification marker, correction is produced as ordinary subsequent text. This setup matches the way we finetune the model.

VI 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了In this work, we introduced复古思想家RetroThinker，一种新颖的训练后框架，为流式 SpeechLLM 配备了仅前向回顾性推理功能。我们在语音 GSM8K 基准测试上的实验表明，可以有效地微调 Moshi 等模型，以自我验证思想链 (CoT) 步骤并生成纠正性延续，而无需跟踪回滚。 至关重要的是，虽然回顾性训练以增加延迟为代价提高了准确性，但我们通过集成 Early Reasoning 和 LengthDPO 成功地减轻了这种开销。 此外，我们全面的消融研究验证了我们三阶段管道的有效性并证实了我们的核心假设。通过分析当前的故障模式，我们强调了未来研究的有希望的途径，最终推动口语模型中回顾性、低延迟推理的发展。, a novel post-training framework that equips streaming SpeechLLMs with forward-only retrospective reasoning capabilities. Our experiments on the spoken GSM8K benchmark demonstrate that models such as Moshi can be effectively fine-tuned to self-verify Chain-of-Thought (CoT) steps and generate corrective continuations without requiring trace rollback. Crucially, while retrospective training improves accuracy at the cost of increased latency, we successfully mitigate this overhead by integrating Early Reasoning and LengthDPO. Furthermore, our comprehensive ablation studies validate the efficacy of our three-stage pipeline and substantiate our core hypotheses. By analyzing current failure modes, we highlight promising avenues for future research, ultimately advancing the development of retrospective, low-latency reasoning in spoken language models.

七VII局限性Limitations

我们的评估仅限于 GSM8K 的 TTS 版本。 该基准非常有用，因为它提供了逐步的基本原理和客观答案，但它没有捕获自然口语对话的声学变化、中断、不流畅或任务多样性。 因此，我们的主张应该被解释为流式语音数学推理的证据，而不是广泛的会话概括。Our evaluation is limited to a TTS version of GSM8K. This benchmark is useful because it provides step-level rationales and objective answers, but it does not capture the acoustic variability, interruptions, disfluencies, or task diversity of natural spoken dialogue. Therefore, our claims should be interpreted as evidence for streaming speech math reasoning rather than broad conversational generalization.

我们的准确性指标还取决于法学硕士法官的 Whisper 转录。 我们使用此管道来评估实际面向用户的波形并与之前的工作保持可比性，但 ASR 或判断错误仍然会影响绝对精度。 由于每个系统都使用相同的管道进行评估，因此相对比较比绝对分数更可靠。Our accuracy metric also depends on Whisper transcription followed by an LLM judge. We use this pipeline to evaluate the actual user-facing waveform and to remain comparable with prior work, but ASR or judge errors can still affect absolute accuracy. Because every system is evaluated with the same pipeline, the relative comparisons are more reliable than the absolute scores.

最后，虽然 RetroThinker 显着提高了性能，但它并没有完全消除推理的延迟成本。在 DPO 之前，回顾性修正会增加明显的延迟，即使在 DPO 之后，QC 阈值也不再像非回顾性早期推理那样精确地控制延迟。此外，解决高度复杂的多步骤问题仍然是我们模型面临的一个公开挑战，反映了流式语音推理中更广泛的困难。未来的工作将侧重于测试自然和噪声语音基准，以及开发明确控制回顾性纠正的数量和长度的方法。Finally, while RetroThinker significantly improves performance, it does not entirely eliminate the latency cost of reasoning. Before DPO, retrospective corrections add noticeable delay, and even after DPO, the QC threshold no longer controls latency as precisely as in non-retrospective early reasoning. Furthermore, resolving highly complex, multi-step problems remains an open challenge for our model, reflecting a broader difficulty in streaming speech reasoning. Future work will focus on testing natural and noisy speech benchmarks, as well as developing methods to explicitly control the number and length of retrospective corrections.
