摘要Abstract

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 已成为现代人工智能系统的基础设施，但它们经常反映和放大训练数据中潜在的社会偏见Large Language Models (LLMs) have become foundational infrastructure for modern artificial intelligence systems, yet they often reflect and amplify social biases latent in the training data(Zhao et al., 2023; Huang and Chang, 2023; Gallegos et al., 2024; Navigli et al., 2023)。这些偏差不仅仅是表面级的伪影，而且深深地编码在内部表示和计算过程中. These biases are not merely surface-level artifacts but are deeply encoded within internal representations and computational processes(Chandna et al., 2025; Cheng et al., 2023)。现有的去偏工作可分为（i）. Existing debiasing work can be divided into (i)模型外部方法model-external methods，调整数据、推理或输出行为，但不限制内部偏差表示, which adjust data, inference, or output behavior but do not constrain internal bias representations(Zmigrod et al., 2019; Li et al., 2024; Kiashemshaki et al., 2025; Kim et al., 2025; Gallegos et al., 2025; Chandna et al., 2025); (二); and (ii)模型内部方法model-internal methods，主要侧重于定位或理解局部组件，例如注意力头或 FFN 向量, which primarily focus on locating or understanding local components like attention heads or FFN vectors(Meng et al., 2022; Geva et al., 2021; Prakash and Roy, 2024; Zhou et al., 2024)。然而，现有的这些方法仍然难以从根本上削弱模型内部存在的偏差. However, these existing methods are still difficult to fundamentally weaken the biases present inside the model(Fang et al., 2025; Vijjini et al., 2025).

我们的研究揭示了模型内部机制中偏差的规模依赖性。通过对照实验，我们观察到，当仅对注意力机制施加约束时，在像 GPT-2 这样的小参数规模的模型中，偏差指标可以显着降低，但当模型规模扩展到十亿参数级别时，这种效果会显着减弱甚至失效（图Our investigation reveals the scale-dependence of bias within the internal mechanisms of the model. Through controlled experiments, we observe that when constraints are imposed only on the attention mechanism, bias metrics can be significantly reduced in models with small parameter scales like GPT-2, but this effect is significantly weakened or even fails when the model scale is extended to the billion-parameter level (Figure1）。这表明偏见不仅仅由注意力决定；相反，前馈网络（FFN）作为语义和事实知识的主要存储单元，也会编码和放大偏差). This indicates that bias is not determined solely by attention; instead, feed-forward networks (FFNs), serving as primary storage units for semantic and factual knowledge, also encode and amplify bias(Vig et al., 2020a; Meng et al., 2022)。因此，仅限制注意力路由不足以从根本上削弱模型中嵌入的偏差。. Thus, constraining attention routing alone is insufficient to fundamentally weaken the bias embedded within the model.
