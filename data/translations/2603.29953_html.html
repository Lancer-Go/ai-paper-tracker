摘要Abstract

结构化意图表示在不同的人工智能模型、语言和提示框架之间传输用户目标的可靠性如何？先前的工作表明，PPS（提示协议规范）是一种基于 5W3H 的结构化意图框架，可以改善中文的目标一致性，并推广到英语和日语。本文将研究扩展到三个新的维度：(1) 三个前沿模型（Claude、GPT-4o、Gemini 2.5 Pro）的跨模型鲁棒性，(2) 与两个广泛采用的结构化提示框架（CO-STAR 和 RISEN）进行系统比较，以及 (3) 一项用户研究 (N=50)，检查在生态有效的环境中人工智能辅助的意图扩展。How reliably can structured intent representations transmit user goals across different AI models, languages, and prompting frameworks? Prior work established that PPS (Prompt Protocol Specification), a 5W3H-based structured intent framework, improves goal alignment in Chinese and generalizes to English and Japanese. This paper extends the investigation along three new dimensions: (1) cross-model robustness across three frontier models (Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5 Pro), (2) systematic comparison with two widely adopted structured prompting frameworks (CO-STAR and RISEN), and (3) a user study (N=50) examining AI-assisted intent expansion in ecologically valid settings.

总而言之，这些发现支持了结构化意图表示作为人机交互的稳健、类协议层的实用价值，同时也激励了未来在外部黄金意图基准、更细粒度的评估以及编码结构和模型能力之间关系的正式建模方面的工作。Taken together, these findings support the practical value of structured intent representation as a robust, protocollike layer for human-AI interaction, while also motivating future work on external gold-intent benchmarks, finergrained evaluation, and formal modeling of the relationship between encoding structure and model capacity.

1.1 人机通信的可靠性问题1.1 The Reliability Problem in Human-AI Communication

大型语言模型 (LLM) 已成为知识工作的通用接口，但基本的可靠性问题仍然存在：通过非结构化自然语言表达的相同用户意图，会在不同模型、语言和会话之间产生不一致的输出。这种不一致——我们之前将其描述为Large language models (LLMs) have become general-purpose interfaces for knowledge work, yet a fundamental reliability problem persists: the same user intent, expressed through unstructured natural language, produces inconsistent outputs across different models, languages, and sessions. This inconsistency — which we have previously characterized as意图传输损失intent transmission loss[25, 26]——不仅仅是质量问题，更是沟通问题：人类意图与人工智能执行之间的通道缺乏可靠信息传输的结构保证。[25, 26] — is not merely a quality problem but a communication problem: the channel between human intent and AI execution lacks the structural guarantees that enable reliable information transmission.

先前在即时工程方面的工作已经产生了许多用于改进个人输出的技术——思想链推理 [2]、角色分配 [22]、少数样本示例 [1]——但这些技术的运作方式是Prior work in prompt engineering has produced numerous techniques for improving individual outputs — chainof-thought reasoning [2], role assignment [22], few-shot examples [1] — but these operate at the执行execution层（模型应如何处理信息）而不是layer (how the model should process information) rather than the意图intent层（用户应该如何编码他们的目标）。区别很重要：执行层技术需要专业知识才能应用，并且不会跨任务转移，而意图层协议可以提供系统的、可重用的可靠性改进。layer (how the user should encode their goals). The distinction matters: execution-layer techniques require expertise to apply and do not transfer across tasks, while an intent-layer protocol could provide systematic, reusable reliability improvements.

1.2 从即时工程到意图协议1.2 From Prompt Engineering to Intent Protocol

在我们之前的工作 [25, 26] 中，我们提出了 PPS（提示协议规范），这是一种围绕新闻 5W3H 框架的八个维度组织的结构化意图表示：In our prior work [25, 26], we proposed PPS (Prompt Protocol Specification), a structured intent representation organized around eight dimensions from the journalistic 5W3H framework:什么What（任务目标），(task objective),为什么Why（目的和动机），(purpose and motivation),谁Who（目标受众和利益相关者），(target audience and stakeholders),当When（时间背景），(temporal context),哪里Where（环境背景），(environmental context),操作方法How-to-do（执行方法），(execution method),多少钱How-much（定量要求），以及(quantitative requirements), and感觉如何How-feel（语气和情感质量）。 PPS 将意图表示构建为结构化通信层，而不是一种提示技术——类似于应用层协议如何标准化跨异构系统的数据传输 [14]。(tone and emotional quality). PPS frames intent representation not as a prompting technique but as a structured communication layer — analogous to how application-layer protocols standardize data transmission across heterogeneous systems [14].

2.2 结构化提示框架2.2 Structured Prompting Frameworks

一些框架提出了提示的结构化组件。 CO-STAR [5] 将提示组织为六个维度：上下文、目标、风格、语气、受众和响应格式。 RISEN 将提示结构为角色、说明、步骤、最终目标和缩小限制（未确定正式的学术出版物；记录在从业者社区和提示工程指南中）。 CRISPE、ICIO 和其他框架提供类似的维度分解 [5]。Several frameworks have proposed structured components for prompts. CO-STAR [5] organizes prompts into six dimensions: Context, Objective, Style, Tone, Audience, and Response format. RISEN structures prompts as Role, Instructions, Steps, End goal, and Narrowing constraints (no formal academic publication identified; documented in practitioner communities and prompt engineering guides). CRISPE, ICIO, and other frameworks offer similar dimensional decompositions [5].

这些框架有一个共同的见解——将意图分解为显式维度可以提高输出质量——但维度覆盖范围有所不同。表 2 将 CO-STAR 和 RISEN 的维度映射到 5W3H 框架，揭示了 5W3H 提供了更广泛的显式维度覆盖：CO-STAR 缺乏“为什么”、“何时”、“何地”和“多少”的显式维度； RISEN 缺少时间、地点和感觉。这种维度分析激发了我们的实验比较。These frameworks share a common insight — that decomposing intent into explicit dimensions improves output quality — but differ in dimensional coverage. 八、结论8. Conclusion

本文提出了对人机交互结构化意图编码的大规模实证评估：跨 3 个模型、3 种语言、6 种条件和 3 个任务领域的 3,240 个评估输出，并辅以 50 人用户研究。据我们所知，现有的评估还没有提供这种规模的跨语言覆盖、多框架比较和意图一致性评分的组合。This paper presents a large-scale empirical evaluation of structured intent encoding for human-AI interaction: 3,240 evaluated outputs across 3 models, 3 languages, 6 conditions, and 3 task domains, supplemented by a 50person user study. To our knowledge, no existing evaluation provides this combination of cross-language coverage, multi-framework comparison, and intent-alignment scoring at this scale.

核心发现是The central finding is that结构化意图编码表现出强大的、很大程度上与语言和模型无关的行为structured intent encoding exhibits robust, largely language- and model-agnostic behavior能够可靠地改善测试条件下的目标一致性。三个独立框架（5W3H、CO-STAR、RISEN）在当前评估分辨率下实现了类似的高性能，这表明是维度分解的行为（使隐含的意图变得明确）推动了对齐改进，而不是任何特定的维度集。that reliably improves goal alignment across the conditions tested. Three independent frameworks (5W3H, CO-STAR, RISEN) achieve similarly high performance under the current evaluation resolution, suggesting that it is the act of dimensional decomposition — making implicit intent explicit — that drives alignment improvement, rather than any specific set of dimensions.

弱模型补偿效应表明，结构化编码恰恰在最需要的地方最有价值：对于较弱的模型和经验不足的用户。跨语言方差减少 24 倍表明结构化意图超越了语言表面形式。The weak-model compensation effect reveals that structured encoding is most valuable precisely where it is most needed: for weaker models and less experienced users. The 24× reduction in cross-language variance demonstrates that structured intent transcends linguistic surface form.

与信息论原理。正如第 7.6 节中所讨论的，观察到的模式与信息论解释兼容：结构化编码可以被视为在给定提示的情况下减少用户意图的残余不确定性（条件熵减少）。将这种解释形式化为一个预测理论框架——一个可以将最佳编码策略描述为模型容量和任务复杂性函数的框架——代表了这一研究领域自然而然的下一步。这些发现并没有将 PPS 确立为工程意义上的通信协议，但它们确实支持这样的观点：结构化意图表示可以作为人类与人工智能交互的类似协议的通信层。with information-theoretic principles. As discussed in Section 7.6, the observed patterns are compatible with an information-theoretic interpretation: structured encoding can be viewed as decreasing the residual uncertainty of user intent given the prompt (conditional entropy reduction). Formalizing this interpretation into a predictive theoretical framework — one that can characterize optimal encoding strategies as a function of model capacity and task complexity — represents a natural next step for this line of research. These findings do not establish PPS as a communication protocol in the engineering sense, but they do support the view that structured intent representation may serve as a protocol-like communication layer for human-AI interaction.

在解释这些结果时，有两个局限性值得一提。首先，用户研究采用固定的A→D_raw→D_mod序列，没有平衡；观察到的满意度改善部分反映了实践和学习效果以及结构性收益。其次，框架比较（D≈E≈F）应该被理解为“所有三个都使当前指标饱和”，而不是被确认为真正的等价性——更细粒度的评估可能会揭示框架间的差异。前沿模型（对照实验）和中国国内人工智能模型（用户研究）的结构化提示效益的一致性提供了令人鼓舞的证据，表明该机制可以在模型生态系统中泛化，但跨生态系统泛化应该在未来的工作中进行更严格的测试。Two limitations warrant mention when interpreting these results. First, the user study employed a fixed A → D_raw → D_mod sequence without counterbalancing; the observed satisfaction improvement partly reflects practice and learning effects alongside structural benefits. Second, the framework comparison (D≈E≈F) should be read as "all three saturate the current metric" rather than as confirmed true equivalence — finer-grained evaluation may yet reveal inter-framework differences. The consistency of structured prompting benefits across both frontier models (controlled experiment) and domestic Chinese AI models (user study) provides encouraging evidence that the mechanism generalizes across model ecosystems, but cross-ecosystem generalization should be tested more rigorously in future work.
