摘要Abstract

组合优化中的算法性能对参数设置高度敏感，而单个全局调整的配置通常无法利用问题实例的异构性。这种限制在电动电容车辆路径问题中尤其明显，其中实例在结构、需求模式和能量约束方面存在很大差异。本文研究了 Bilevel Late Acceptance Hill Climbing 的实例感知参数配置，这是一种针对电动电容车辆路径问题的最先进的元启发式方法。离线调整过程用于获取特定于实例的参数标签，然后通过回归模型从实例特征映射这些标签，以便在执行之前启用对未见过的实例的参数预测。 IEEE WCCI 2020 基准及其扩展的实验结果表明，所提出的方法实现了平均目标值降低Algorithm performance in combinatorial optimisation is highly sensitive to parameter settings, while a single globally tuned configuration often fails to exploit the heterogeneity of problem instances. This limitation is particularly evident in the Electric Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem, where instances differ substantially in structure, demand patterns, and energy constraints. This paper investigates instance-aware parameter configuration for Bilevel Late Acceptance Hill Climbing, a state-of-the-art metaheuristic for the Electric Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem. An offline tuning procedure is used to obtain instance-specific parameter labels, which are then mapped from instance features via a regression model to enable parameter prediction for unseen instances prior to execution. Experimental results on the IEEE WCCI 2020 benchmark and its extensions show that the proposed approach achieves an average objective value reduction of0.28%0.28\%相对于全局调整的配置，跨八个保留的测试实例。这相当于数百万美元运输业务成本的大幅降低。across eight held-out test instances relative to a globally tuned configuration. This corresponds to a significant cost reduction in multimillion-dollar transportation operations.

I 简介Introduction

在设计组合优化问题的算法时，一个根本的挑战在于确定适当的参数设置When designing algorithms for combinatorial optimisation problems, a fundamental challenge lies in determining appropriate parameter settings[11, 3, 14, 17]。在实践中，算法性能通常对这些配置高度敏感。更重要的是，不同的问题实例通常表现出异构的结构特性，这反过来又导致不同的搜索景观。因此，在一个实例上表现良好的参数配置可能会导致在另一实例上表现不佳。. In practice, algorithm performance is often highly sensitive to these configurations. More importantly, different problem instances typically exhibit heterogeneous structural properties, which in turn induce distinct search landscapes. Consequently, a parameter configuration that performs well on one instance may lead to unsatisfactory performance on another.

为了在不同的问题实例中获得一致的良好性能，可以确定两个有代表性的研究方向。一个方向聚焦To achieve consistently good performance across diverse problem instances, two representative research directions can be identified. One direction focuses on适应机制adaptive mechanisms [23, 15, 27, 21]，其中算法参数或行为根据不断变化的搜索动态进行在线调整。虽然此类机制可以有效地对运行期间观察到的反馈做出反应，但它们主要依赖于在线信息。, in which algorithm parameters or behaviours are adjusted online in response to the evolving search dynamics. While such mechanisms are effective in reacting to feedback observed during the run, they primarily rely on online information.

另一个方向是An alternative direction is实例感知参数配置 (IAPC)instance-aware parameter configuration (IAPC) [11, 14, 26]，旨在确定合适的参数设置, which aims to determine suitable parameter settings先验的a priori基于给定问题实例的内在特征。该范例假设实例级特征和算法参数之间存在映射，允许在搜索开始之前专门化配置。based on the intrinsic characteristics of a given problem instance. This paradigm assumes that a mapping exists between instance-level features and algorithm parameters, allowing the configuration to be specialised before the search starts.

Bilevel Late Acceptance Hill Climbing (b-LAHC) 是一种针对电动电容车辆路径问题 (E-CVRP) 开发的元启发式算法Bilevel Late Acceptance Hill Climbing (b-LAHC) is a metaheuristic developed for the Electric Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (E-CVRP)[21]。在固定的评估预算下，它在 IEEE WCCI 2020 EVRP 基准测试中展现了强劲的性能. Under a fixed evaluation budget, it has demonstrated strong performance on the IEEE WCCI 2020 EVRP benchmark[19]，在七个小规模实例上获得极具竞争力的解决方案并改进, attaining highly competitive solutions on seven small-scale instances and improving99出于out of1010大规模基准测试中最著名的结果，平均改进为best-known results on large-scale benchmarks, with an average improvement of1.23%1.23\%。在目前的形式中，b-LAHC 涉及五个算法参数，这些参数是全局配置的，并且在之前的研究中的所有基准实例中都是固定的。. In its current form, b-LAHC involves five algorithmic parameters, which were configured globally and fixed across all benchmark instances in previous studies.

尽管这样的全局配置提供了强大而可靠的基线，但它隐含地假设单个参数设置可以在具有不同结构特征的实例中表现良好。然而，E-CVRP 实例在网络拓扑、客户需求分布和能源相关约束方面表现出明显的异构性。因此，这一假设可能会限制 b-LAHC 在某些类别的实例上的全部潜力。Although such a global configuration provides a strong and reliable baseline, it implicitly assumes that a single parameter setting can perform well across instances with diverse structural characteristics. However, E-CVRP instances exhibit pronounced heterogeneity in terms of network topology, customer demand distributions, and energy-related constraints. As a result, this assumption may limit the full potential of b-LAHC on certain categories of instances.

受此观察的启发，本文研究了 b-LAHC 的实例感知算法配置，旨在根据各个 E-CVRP 实例的特征定制其参数设置。通过利用实例级信息来指导参数选择，所提出的方法不仅寻求进一步提高算法性能，而且还提供对 b-LAHC 算法行为和问题实例特征之间相互作用的更深入的了解。Motivated by this observation, this paper investigates instance-aware algorithm configuration for b-LAHC, aiming to tailor its parameter settings to the characteristics of individual E-CVRP instances. By exploiting instance-level information to guide parameter selection, the proposed approach seeks not only to further improve algorithm performance but also to provide deeper insights into the interaction between b-LAHC’s algorithmic behaviour and problem instance characteristics.

三、III背景Background

Ⅲ-AIII-A 电动电容车辆路径问题Electric Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem

E-CVRP 涉及同质电动汽车 (EV) 车队的路线，以服务于一组地理上分散的客户。目标是最大限度地减少总行驶距离，同时满足客户需求并尊重车辆负载能力和电池能量限制。每辆车从中央停车场出发，拜访一部分客户，并在完成其路线后返回停车场。The E-CVRP concerns the routing of a homogeneous fleet of electric vehicles (EVs) to serve a set of geographically distributed customers. The objective is to minimise the total travelled distance while satisfying customer demands and respecting both vehicle load capacity and battery energy constraints. Each vehicle departs from a central depot, visits a subset of customers, and returns to the depot upon completion of its route.

与经典的容量车辆路径问题 (CVRP) 不同，E-CVRP 明确考虑了能源消耗和充电决策。车辆可能会前往充电站补充电池，可能会沿着路线多次充电，以便在能源限制方面保持可行。因此，路线的可行性不仅取决于客户排序，还取决于充电站访问的安排和顺序。Unlike the classical Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP), the E-CVRP explicitly accounts for energy consumption and recharging decisions. Vehicles may visit charging stations to replenish their batteries, potentially multiple times along a route, in order to remain feasible with respect to energy constraints. As a result, route feasibility depends not only on customer sequencing but also on the placement and ordering of charging station visits.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 双层延迟接受爬山Bilevel Late Acceptance Hill Climbing

b-LAHC 是一种元启发式算法，旨在解决 E-CVRP 问题。它建立在基于路径的双层优化模型之上，其中上层决策决定车辆路线，而下层决策优化每条路线的充电策略，反映了路线决策和充电决策之间的内在耦合。The b-LAHC is a metaheuristic designed for solving the E-CVRP. It is built upon a path-based bilevel optimisation model, in which upper-level decisions determine vehicle routes, while lower-level decisions optimise charging strategies along each route, reflecting the intrinsic coupling between routing and recharging decisions.

IV 实例感知参数预测框架Instance-aware Parameter Prediction Framework

IV-A 框架概述Overview of the Framework

本节概述了所提出的 b-LAHC 实例感知参数预测框架。该框架的目标是仅根据实例特征自动推断出未见过的 E-CVRP 实例的有效算法参数配置，而不需要在执行时进行任何额外的参数调整。This section provides an overview of the proposed instance-aware parameter prediction framework for b-LAHC. The goal of the framework is to automatically infer effective algorithm parameter configurations for unseen E-CVRP instances based solely on their instance characteristics, without requiring any additional parameter tuning at execution time.

如图所示As illustrated in Fig.2，该框架由离线训练阶段和随后的在线推理阶段组成。在离线训练期间，每个E-CVRP实例经历两个并行过程。首先，对每个训练实例独立进行特定于实例的参数调整，以获得接近最优的参数配置，作为监督标签。二、一组描述性特征，详见表, the framework consists of an offline training phase followed by an online inference phase. During offline training, each E-CVRP instance undergoes two parallel processes. First, instance-specific parameter tuning is conducted independently for each training instance to obtain near-optimal parameter configurations, which serve as supervisory labels. Second, a set of descriptive features, detailed in TableII，被提取来表征每个实例的结构和需求相关属性。基于生成的特征-标签对，训练回归模型来学习从实例特征到有效 b-LAHC 参数的映射。, is extracted to characterise the structural and demand-related properties of each instance. Based on the resulting feature–label pairs, a regression model is trained to learn the mapping from instance characteristics to effective b-LAHC parameters.

经过训练后，回归模型可以为新实例进行简单的参数配置。对于未见过的 E-CVRP 实例，应用相同的特征提取过程，预测实例特定的参数配置并将其直接提供给 b-LAHC，b-LAHC 在与基线配置相同的停止标准下执行，无需任何进一步的参数自适应。Once trained, the regression model enables straightforward parameter configuration for new instances. For an unseen E-CVRP instance, the same feature extraction procedure is applied and an instance-specific parameter configuration is predicted and directly supplied to b-LAHC, which is executed under the same stopping criterion as the baseline configuration, without any further parameter adaptation.

重要的是，仅在离线训练阶段才需要进行特定于实例的参数调整，从而在解决新的 E-CVRP 实例时实现高效且可扩展的实例感知参数配置。Importantly, instance-specific parameter tuning is required only during the offline training stage, enabling efficient and scalable instance-aware parameter configuration when solving new E-CVRP instances.

IV-B 离线实例特定参数调优Offline Instance-specific Parameter Tuning

离线调整过程的目标是为各个 E-CVRP 实例获得高质量、接近最优的 b-LAHC 参数配置。这些配置随后用作监督参数预测的参考标签。The objective of the offline tuning procedure is to obtain high-quality, near-optimal configurations of the b-LAHC parameters for individual E-CVRP instances. 需求相关特征描述了客户需求的分布及其与车辆容量的关系，包括汇总统计、容量紧张指标和分布形状描述符。此外，还包括到最近充电站的需求加权距离，以捕获需求模式和充电基础设施之间的相互作用。Demand-related features characterise the distribution of customer demands and their relationship to vehicle capacity, including summary statistics, capacity tightness indicators, and distributional shape descriptors. Furthermore, a demand-weighted distance to the nearest charging station is also included to capture the interaction between demand patterns and charging infrastructure.

VI 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

本文研究了应用于 E-CVRP 的 b-LAHC 实例感知算法配置。虽然现有的 E-CVRP 算法主要依赖于全局调整的参数设置，但我们表明，明确考虑实例级异构性可以带来一致的性能改进。This paper investigated instance-aware algorithm configuration for b-LAHC applied to the E-CVRP. While existing E-CVRP algorithms predominantly rely on globally tuned parameter settings, we showed that explicitly accounting for instance-level heterogeneity can lead to consistent performance improvements.

提出了一种实例感知参数预测框架，其中通过基于描述性实例特征的回归模型来学习通过离线自动调整获得的高质量实例特定配置。这使得能够对未见过的实例进行有效的参数推断，而无需任何在线调整开销。An instance-aware parameter prediction framework was proposed, in which high-quality instance-specific configurations obtained via offline automated tuning are learned through a regression model based on descriptive instance features. This enables effective parameter inference for unseen instances without any online tuning overhead.

IEEE WCCI 2020 EVRP 基准及其扩展实例的实验结果表明，特定于实例的调优始终优于全局配置，并且所提出的模型预测的参数在保留的测试实例上实现了接近预言机配置的性能。特征分析进一步揭示，需求异质性和基于图的结构属性在确定有效的后期接受行为中发挥着关键作用。Experimental results on the IEEE WCCI 2020 EVRP benchmark and its extended instances demonstrate that instance-specific tuning consistently outperforms a global configuration, and that parameters predicted by the proposed model achieve performance close to oracle configurations on held-out test instances. Feature analysis further reveals that demand heterogeneity and graph-based structural properties play a key role in determining effective late acceptance behaviour.

未来的工作将探索将自适应机制集成到 b-LAHC 中，通过将邻域算子选择建模为马尔可夫决策过程，允许搜索行为由当前搜索状态动态引导。Future work will explore the integration of adaptive mechanisms into b-LAHC, by modelling neighbourhood operator selection as a Markov Decision Process, allowing search behaviour to be guided dynamically by the current search state.
