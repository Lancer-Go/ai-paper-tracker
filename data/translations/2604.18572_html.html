1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）的成功引起了计算机视觉界的极大关注：我们是否需要像素来构建理解我们世界的机器，或者语言是“你所需要的一切”吗？The success of Large Language Models (LLMs) is causing much hand-wringing in the computer vision community: do we even need pixels to build machines that understand our world, or is language “all you need”?

This resonates with the suggestion that text data may make other modalities redundant[83]，前提是世界上与人类相关的部分是通过语言表现出来的。 另一方面，有人认为仅靠语言数据无法产生真正的理解, on the premise that the part of the world that is relevant to humans is manifest in language. On the other hand, it is argued that linguistic data alone cannot yield genuine understanding[8]或者允许实际的实施例。毕竟，我们参观艺术博物馆而不是仅仅阅读目录中的绘画描述是有原因的。 这就提出了一个核心问题：在不同模式上训练的模型如何代表现实？or allow actual embodiment. After all, there is a reason we visit art museums rather than just read descriptions of paintings in a catalogue. This raises a central question: how do models trained on different modalities represent reality?

4.3 当数据不是双射时会发生什么？What happens when the data is not bijective?

在实践中，图像和文本等模态之间的关系本质上是多对多的：单个图像可以由无数文本描述来描述，单个文本标题可以对应于一大组视觉上不同的图像。更根本的是，模式通常在信息内容上有所不同。In practice, the relationship between modalities, such as image and text, is inherently many-to-many: a single image can be described by countless text descriptions, and a single text caption can correspond to a large set of visually distinct images. More fundamentally, modalities often differ in information content.

具体来说，图像对空间、纹理和感知结构进行编码，而文本只能在有限的范围内捕获这些结构。另一方面，文本编码抽象、否定和组合语义，而图像则不然。 原则上，我们可以轻松地弥合这一差距。例如，可以将像素值编码为文本或将标题渲染为图像，并在它们之间建立双射。这些保留了信息，但每种模态的归纳结构（使每种模态有用的模态特定属性）都丢失了。Specifically, images encode spatial, textural, and perceptual structure that text captures only to a limited extent. On the other hand, text encodes abstraction, negation, and compositional semantics that images do not. One could, in principle, bridge this gap trivially. For instance, one could encode pixel values as text or render captions as images and establish a bijection between those. Those preserve the information, but the inductive structure (the modality-specific properties that make each modality useful) of each modality is lost.

5 讨论和未来的工作Discussion and future work

柏拉图表示假说表明，在不同模态上训练的模型随着规模的扩大会趋向于共享现实的表示。我们的结果表明了对这一说法的更有条件的解释。互助The Platonic Representation Hypothesis suggests that models trained on different modalities converge toward a shared representation of reality as they scale. Our results indicate a more conditional interpretation of this claim. Moreover, the previously reported trend that stronger language models yield higher alignment does not consistently hold for recent models.

这些发现并不排除跨模式的共享结构。总的来说，我们的结果表明，小规模的相互最近邻评估可能会通过依赖限制性画廊大小和一对一配对来夸大收敛程度。互低These findings do not rule out shared structure across modalities. 然而，现实世界的图像文本数据本质上是多对多的，并且在表示级别上存在任何近似双射的程度仍不清楚。未来工作的一个关键方向是直接测试这一假设，例如通过研究语言是否可以作为图像重建（即图像文本图像自动编码器）的无损瓶颈。如果正如我们怀疑的那样，这对于现实设置来说是不切实际的（例如，少于一千个单词的文本瓶颈），那么识别和建模联合文本图像空间中形成双射（维恩图的交集）的部分，并将其与不形成双射的部分分开，将会非常有趣。However, real-world image–text data is fundamentally many-to-many, and the extent to which any approximate bijection exists at the level of representations remains unclear. A key direction for future work is to directly test this assumption, for example by studying whether language can serve as a lossless bottleneck for image reconstruction (i.e. an image-text-image autoencoder). If, as we suspect, this proves illusive for realistic settings (e.g. text bottlenecks of under a thousand words), it would be very interesting to identify and model the part of the joint text-image space forming a bijection (the intersection of the Venn diagram), and disentangle it from the parts that do not.
