摘要Abstract

城市规划者需要最新的、全球性的、一致的街道网络模型和指标来衡量弹性和绩效、建模可达性并针对当地生活质量干预措施。本文介绍了世界上每个城市地区的最新街道网络模型和指标。它使用全球人类住区图层中的 2025 年城市区域边界，允许用户将这些数据连接到数百个其他城市属性。其工作流程摄取了 189 个国家/地区 10,351 个城市地区的 1.8 亿个 OpenStreetMap 节点和 3.6 亿个 OpenStreetMap 边缘。代码、模型和指标可公开供重复使用。这些资源解锁了全球城市街道网络科学，超越了样本以及资源贫乏地区的本地分析，否则模型和指标很难获得。Urban planners need up-to-date, global, and consistent street network models and indicators to measure resilience and performance, model accessibility, and target local quality-of-life interventions. This article presents up-to-date street network models and indicators for every urban area in the world. It uses 2025 urban area boundaries from the Global Human Settlement Layer, allowing users to join these data to hundreds of other urban attributes. Its workflow ingests 180 million OpenStreetMap nodes and 360 million OpenStreetMap edges across 10,351 urban areas in 189 countries. The code, models, and indicators are publicly available for reuse. These resources unlock worldwide urban street network science beyond samples as well as local analyses in under-resourced regions where models and indicators are otherwise less-accessible.

1. 简介1. Introduction

学者和从业者使用空间图对街道网络进行建模，以理解或预测城市现象，包括交通动态、日常生活需求的可达性以及城市形态的弹性和可持续性Scholars and practitioners use spatial graphs to model street networks to understand or predict urban phenomena including traffic dynamics, accessibility to daily living needs, and the resilience and sustainability of the urban form(Barthelemy,, 2022)。这些网络由其拓扑（例如连接和配置）和几何形状（例如位置、长度、面积和角度）定义. These networks are defined by both their topology—e.g., connections and configuration—and geometry—e.g., positions, lengths, areas, and angles(Barthelemy,, 2011)。因此，文献中存在各种拓扑和几何指标来衡量重要的街道网络特征。例如，节点度揭示了街道的连通性. Accordingly, various topological and geometric indicators exist throughout the literature to measure important street network characteristics. For instance, node degrees reveal streets’ connectedness(Barrington-Leigh and Millard-Ball,, 2020)，加权介数中心性确定网络中相对重要的部分, weighted betweenness centralities identify relatively important parts of the network(Barthelemy and Boeing,, 2025)，而电路则表明其效率或缺乏, and circuity indicates its efficiency or lack thereof(Giacomin and Levinson,, 2015)。然后，这些指标可以为下游城市分析提供信息，以制定规划干预措施或基准并监控城市实现既定可持续发展目标的进展情况. Such indicators can then inform downstream urban analytics to target planning interventions or benchmark and monitor cities’ progress toward stated sustainability goals(Higgs et al.,, 2024).

由于规划者面临世界各地城市相互交织的可持续性和公共卫生危机，现在比以往任何时候都更需要最新的、全球性的、一致的城市街道网络模型和指标Up-to-date, global, consistent urban street network models and indicators are needed now more than ever before as planners face intertwined sustainability and public health crises in cities around the world(Giles-Corti et al.,, 2022)。与此同时，城市科学寻求超越传统抽样的限制，建立普遍的理论并更好地了解研究不足的地区，例如南半球. Meanwhile, urban science seeks to expand beyond the limits of traditional sampling to build universal theory and better understand under-studied regions, such as the Global South(Lobo et al.,, 2020)。然而，传统数据源和方法的局限性给这些努力带来了阻力。城市街道数据的数字化往往因地而异，阻碍了全球同类比较，并使资源贫乏地区的分析变得尤其困难. Yet the limitations of traditional data sources and methods present headwinds to these efforts. Data on urban streets are often digitized inconsistently from place to place, thwarting apples-to-apples global comparisons and making analyses particularly difficult in under-resourced regions(Liu et al.,, 2022)。 OpenStreetMap 等流行数据源提供了世界各地相当高质量的数据，但不将其打包为图论形式，也不提供指标. Popular data sources such as OpenStreetMap offer reasonably high quality data around the world, but do not package it in graph-theoretic form nor provide indicators(Boeing, 2025a, )。像 OSMnx 这样的工具会有所帮助，但仍然需要编码知识来进行分析，并且对于尝试进行全球城市科学的人来说仍然需要大量的计算资源。. Tools like OSMnx help, but still require coding knowledge to conduct the analysis and potentially extensive computational resources for someone trying to conduct global urban science.

本文提供了填补这一空白的资源：全世界的街道网络模型和指标，以及用于重新生成它们的可复制代码，供学者和从业者轻松重用，而无需重新发明轮子。本研究使用 OpenStreetMap 的数据和 2025 年全球人类住区层 (GHSL) 的边界，对世界上每个城市地区的街道网络进行建模和分析，获取了 189 个国家/地区 10,351 个城市地区的 1.8 亿个 OpenStreetMap 节点和 3.6 亿个 OpenStreetMap 边缘。本文的其余部分描述了这个街道网络模型和指标的开放数据存储库，以及包含生成它们的代码的开源软件存储库。This article presents a resource to fill this gap: street network models and indicators worldwide—plus reproducible code to regenerate them—for scholars and practitioners to easily reuse without reinventing the wheel. Using data from OpenStreetMap and boundaries from the 2025 Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL), this study models and analyzes the street networks of every urban area in the world, ingesting 180 million OpenStreetMap nodes and 360 million OpenStreetMap edges across 10,351 urban areas in 189 countries. The rest of this article describes this open data repository of street network models and indicators, as well as the open-source software repository containing the code to generate them.

4. 实际应用4. Practical Applications

就下游应用而言，这些模型和指标可用于估计网络拓扑与出行模式或土地利用之间的关系In terms of downstream applications, these models and indicators can be used to estimate relationships between network topology and travel patterns or land use(Wang et al.,, 2020, 2018)，通过城市内介数中心性的经验分布来识别网络中相对重要的部分, identify relatively important parts of a network via empirical distributions of within-city betweenness centrality(Barthelemy and Boeing,, 2025), 测量蔓延, measure sprawl(Gervasoni et al.,, 2017)，模拟灾害对世界各地街道网络恢复能力的影响, simulate disasters’ effects on street network resilience around the world(Boeing and Ha,, 2024)，改善物流的最后一英里行程预测, improve logistics’ last-mile trip predictions(Merchán et al.,, 2020)，并估计温室气体排放与街道网络设计之间的关系, and estimate relationships between greenhouse gas emissions and street network design(Boeing et al.,, 2024)。迄今为止，该存储库的模型和指标数据已被用户下载超过 70,000 次。. To date, this repository’s models and indicator data have been downloaded over 70,000 times by users.

Dyer et al., (2024)认为城市学者和实践者需要能够跟上快速城市化时代转型变革步伐的模型和指标。该项目旨在通过在 2019-2020 年最初进行的先前工作（生成数据存储库的初步版本）的基础上解决这一问题argue that urban scholars and practitioners need models and indicators that keep up with the pace of transformational change in an era of rapid urbanization. This project seeks to address this by building on prior work initially conducted in 2019–2020 that generated a preliminary version of the data repository(Boeing,, 2022)。该初始版本基于 2015 版 GHSL UCD 和 2020 年 OpenStreetMap 数据。这个新版本使用 2025 GHSL UCD 和 2025 OpenStreetMap 数据做出了多项新贡献。. That initial version was based on the 2015 version of the GHSL UCD and 2020 OpenStreetMap data. This new version uses the 2025 GHSL UCD and 2025 OpenStreetMap data to make several new contributions.

首先，它比早期版本增加了 1,400 多个城市地区和 11 个国家/地区。在城市快速扩张的时代，这需要显着扩大全球覆盖范围。也就是说，这些新模型使我们能够研究比以前更多且最近城市化的城市。First, it includes over 1,400 more urban areas and 11 more countries than the earlier version. This entails significantly more worldwide coverage in an era of rapid urban expansion. That is, these new models allow us to study more—and more-recently urbanized—cities than before.

其次，这些新模型将最近 10 年的城市化纳入其更新的城市区域边界，并将 5 年的新社区添加到 OpenStreetMap 中。因此，与早期版本相比，此工作流的建模包括大约 2000 万个街道网络节点和 4000 万个边缘。新的城市边界允许用户将这些街道网络模型和指标与数百个关于城市气候、土地利用、经济状况等的新的、最新的 GHSL 属性联系起来。例如，这使我们能够模拟极热或极冷背景下的城市出行和通行，生成城市网络和土地利用的综合模型，或估计经济活动与基础交通基础设施之间的关系。Second, these new models incorporate 10 years of recent urbanization into their updated urban area boundaries and 5 years of new community additions to OpenStreetMap. As such, this workflow’s modeling includes approximately 20 million more street network nodes and 40 million more edges than the earlier version. The new urban boundaries allow users to link these street network models and indicators to hundreds of new, up-to-date GHSL attributes on urban climate, land use, economic conditions, etc. For instance, this allows us to simulate urban travel and access in the context of extreme heat or cold, generate integrated models of urban networks and land uses, or estimate relationships between economic activity and underlying transport infrastructure.

第三，它向存储库添加了新的属性和指标，最重要的是每个城市地区街道网络中每个节点的介数中心性，计算起来非常耗时和资源密集，但可以对网络结构和弹性进行有用的分析。例如，在模拟网络中断和恢复能力时，介数中心性可以为我们提供阻塞点指标Third, it adds new attributes and indicators to the repository—most consequentially the betweenness centrality of every node in every urban area’s street network, which is time- and resource-intensive to calculate, yet unlocks useful analyses of network structure and resilience. For example, betweenness centrality can provide us with indicators of chokepoints when simulating network disruptions and resilience(Boeing and Ha,, 2024)以及网络要素相对重要性的指标and indicators of the relative importance of network elements(Barthelemy and Boeing,, 2025)。第四，它使用更细粒度的SRTM数据（30m而不是之前的90m分辨率）以获得更精确的高程属性值。这在对主动出行行为进行建模时特别有用，该行为对剧烈的海拔变化很敏感. Fourth, it uses finer-grained SRTM data (30m instead of the previous 90m resolution) for more precise elevation attribute values. This is especially useful when modeling active travel behavior, which is sensitive to arduous elevation changes(Barthelemy et al.,, 2024).

第五，从一Fifth, from a代码产品code product从角度来看，工作流的代码库已经从头开始完全重构和重写，以显着降低其圈复杂度、内存使用和运行时间。这使得工作流程更加可维护、可持续，并且将来更容易重新运行，以便在发布新的 GHSL 数据时定期更新数据存储库。这也使下游用户更容易重新运行代码来建模他们想要的任何网络类型，例如步行网络或自行车网络。perspective, the workflow’s code base has been wholly refactored and rewritten from the ground-up to significantly reduce its cyclomatic complexity, memory use, and runtime. This makes the workflow more maintainable, sustainable, and easier to re-run in the future to periodically update the data repository whenever new GHSL data are released. This also makes it easier for downstream users to re-run the code to model any network types they wish—including pedestrian networks or cycling networks, for example.

最后，这些模型和指标本身可以解锁其他研究人员的工作，例如通过上面讨论的示例应用程序。该项目提供了一个全球数据集，可进行样本之外的全球城市街道网络科学以及本地分析，特别是在最需要此类模型和指标但又最稀缺的资源匮乏地区Finally, these models and indicators themselves unlock other researchers’ work, such as through the example applications discussed above. This project provides a global dataset to conduct both worldwide urban street network science beyond samples as well as local analyses particularly in less-resourced regions where such models and indicators are most needed, yet most scarce(Giles-Corti et al.,, 2022).
