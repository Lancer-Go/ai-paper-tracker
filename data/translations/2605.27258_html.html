1 简介1 Introduction

随着生成神经网络的快速发展，文本转语音（TTS）合成在自然度、表现力和说话人保真度方面取得了显着的进步With the rapid advancement of generative neural networks, text-to-speech (TTS) synthesis has achieved remarkable progress in naturalness, expressiveness, and speaker fidelity[chen2025neural, shen2024naturalspeech2, seed-tts]。一个特别具有变革性的发展是大规模零样本 TTS，其中在广泛的多说话者语料库上训练的模型可以从仅几秒钟的参考音频中克隆任意声音。基于大型语言模型 (LLM) 架构的最新系统. A particularly transformative development is large-scale zero-shot TTS, where models trained on extensive multi-speaker corpora can clone arbitrary voices from only a few seconds of reference audio. Recent systems built upon large language model (LLM) architectures[cosyvoice, cosyvoice2, seed-tts, fireredtts2]或非自回归基于流的生成or non-autoregressive flow-based generation[f5tts, ditar, naturalspeech3]展示了令人印象深刻的品质，为该领域树立了新标准。然而，这一进步伴随着数据集越来越大（从数十万小时到数百万小时）的持续趋势。have demonstrated impressive quality, establishing new standards for the field. However, this progress has been accompanied by a persistent trend toward ever-larger datasets (from hundreds of thousands to millions of hours[qwen3tts, minimax-speech]）以及日益复杂的架构，涉及多码本分词器、多阶段训练管道和多个专用子模型。) and increasingly complex architectures involving multi-codebook tokenizers, multi-stage training pipelines, and multiple specialized sub-models.

这一轨迹虽然产生了最先进的结果，但也给更广泛的研究界带来了重大挑战。首先，获取和处理数百万小时的语音数据需要专有的基础设施和大量的计算资源，而可用于构建高质量训练集的标准化开源工具很少。其次，架构复杂性，例如多码本残差量化、分层预测模块和流媒体特定设计，增加了工程工作量和部署难度。第三，先进的可控性功能（情感合成、副语言合成、方言合成）通常作为单独的专业系统开发，进一步分散了所需的专业知识和资源。虽然这些方向无疑推动了语音合成表现力的上限并代表了有效的研究目标，但大多数商业部署场景都需要一种不太复杂但仍能满足实际要求的替代方案。在这种情况下，追求极端表现力的努力可能会矛盾地成为快速部署的障碍，并显着增加应用程序成本。这些障碍共同限制了资源有限的团队构建有竞争力的 TTS 系统或扩展和完善他们已经可以使用的方法。This trajectory, while yielding state-of-the-art results, poses significant challenges for the broader research community. First, acquiring and processing millions of hours of speech data requires proprietary infrastructure and substantial computational resources, with few standardized open-source tools available for constructing high-quality training sets. Second, architectural complexity, such as multi-codebook residual quantization, hierarchical prediction modules, and streaming-specific designs, increases engineering effort and deployment difficulty. Third, advanced controllability features (emotion synthesis, paralinguistic synthesis, dialect synthesis) are typically developed as separate specialized systems, further fragmenting the required expertise and resources. While these directions undoubtedly push the upper bound of speech synthesis expressiveness and represent valid research goals, most commercial deployment scenarios call for a less complex alternative that still meets practical requirements. In such cases, efforts toward extreme expressiveness can paradoxically become an obstacle to rapid deployment and significantly inflate application cost. These barriers collectively restrict resource-constrained teams from building competitive TTS systems or from extending and refining the approaches they already have access to.

在本报告中，我们介绍了 PilotTTS，这是一种快速组装的自回归 TTS 框架，可以满足紧迫的实际需求，通过将成熟的开源组件与细致的数据工程相结合，而不是通过架构新颖性或数据规模来实现具有竞争力的性能。系统采用Qwen3语言模型In this report, we present PilotTTS, an autoregressive TTS framework rapidly assembled to meet pressing practical demand, achieving competitive performance by combining well-established open-source components with meticulous data engineering, rather than through architectural novelty or data scale. The system employs a Qwen3 language model[qwen3]作为基于 Q-Former 的自回归主干as the autoregressive backbone with a Q-Former-based[blip2]用于说话人和风格表示的调节模块，并通过条件流匹配 (CFM) 生成语音conditioning module for speaker and style representation, and generates speech through a Conditional Flow Matching (CFM)[flowmatching]带有扩散变压器 (DiT) 的解码器decoder with a Diffusion Transformer (DiT)[dit]主干后面是 HiFi-GANbackbone followed by a HiFi-GAN[hifigan]声码器。 PilotTTS 使用从公共来源收集的大约 20 万小时的语音数据进行训练，并使用完全由公共可用工具构建的管道进行处理，其性能与使用多一个数量级的数据进行训练的系统相比，具有竞争力。vocoder. Trained on approximately 200K hours of speech data collected from public sources and processed using a pipeline built entirely from publicly available tools, PilotTTS delivers performance competitive with systems trained on an order of magnitude more data.

2 数据处理管道2 Data Processing Pipeline

高质量的训练数据对于竞争性语音合成至关重要，但现有的大规模 TTS 系统通常依赖于限制再现性的专有数据处理管道。为了解决这个问题，我们设计了一个完全基于公开可用模块构建的多阶段数据处理管道，由三个连续阶段组成：质量评估和增强、标签注释和质量过滤（图High-quality training data is critical for competitive speech synthesis, yet existing large-scale TTS systems often rely on proprietary data processing pipelines that limit reproducibility. To address this, we design a multi-stage data processing pipeline built entirely upon publicly available modules, composed of three sequential stages: quality assessment and enhancement, label annotation, and quality filtering (Figure2）。从从异构现实世界源收集的原始波形数据开始，该管道通过声学预处理、结构化注释和基于可靠性的样本选择逐步细化音频，最终为下游模型训练生成干净且标记丰富的数据集。). Starting from raw waveform data collected from heterogeneous real-world sources, the pipeline progressively refines the audio through acoustic preprocessing, structured annotation, and reliability-based sample selection, ultimately producing a clean and richly labeled dataset for downstream model training.

2.1  质量评估与提升2.1  Quality Assessment and Enhancement

第一阶段评估并提高输入音频的音质。由于来自不同来源的原始录音可能在录音条件、背景噪声、扬声器转换和信号质量方面表现出很大的变化，因此该阶段结合了音频标准化、语音结构分析和质量感知增强。The first stage evaluates and improves the acoustic quality of the input audio. Since raw recordings from diverse sources may exhibit substantial variability in recording conditions, background noise, speaker transitions, and signal quality, this stage combines audio standardization, speech structure analysis, and quality-aware enhancement.

我们首先将所有录音标准化为统一的格式和采样率，以减少源级别的异质性。然后，标准化音频由语音活动检测 (SAD) 模块和说话者变化检测 (SCD) 模块一起进行分段，以识别有效的语音区域并检测说话者的转换We first standardize all recordings to a unified format and sampling rate to reduce source-level heterogeneity. The standardized audio is then segmented by a speech activity detection (SAD) module together with a speaker change detection (SCD) module to identify valid speech regions and detect speaker transitions[pyannote-powerset, pyannote].

接下来，并行估计三个与质量相关的属性。我们使用 DNSMOSNext, three quality-related attributes are estimated in parallel. We use DNSMOS[dnsmos]为了预测感知语音质量，语音/非语音分类器to predict perceptual speech quality, a speech/non-speech classifier111https://www.modelscope.cn/models/iic/SenseVoiceSmall以确定片段是否包含有效语音而不是音乐、环境噪声或其他非语音事件，以及 SNR 估计模块来测量信噪比。根据这些指标，每个部分都被分配一个质量状态。预测感知质量较低的片段（例如，预测的 MOSto determine whether a segment contains valid speech rather than music, environmental noise, or other non-speech events, and an SNR estimation module to measure the signal-to-noise ratio. Based on these indicators, each segment is assigned a quality status. Segments with low predicted perceptual quality (e.g., predicted MOS≤\leq3.5）、非语音标签或信噪比不足被视为声学缺陷。3.5), non-speech labels, or insufficient SNR are regarded as acoustically deficient.

对于识别为低质量的片段，我们应用去噪和增强模块For segments identified as low quality, we apply a denoising and enhancement module222https://github.com/resemble-ai/resemble-enhance抑制背景噪音并提高语音清晰度。这种双重评估和增强设计为后续注释提供了更清晰的输入。to suppress background noise and improve speech intelligibility. This dual assessment-and-enhancement design provides cleaner inputs for subsequent annotation.

2.2  标签标注2.2  Label Annotation

第二阶段为每个处理后的语音片段生成结构化注释，包括文本、时间、说话者相关和韵律相关信息。The second stage generates structured annotations for each processed speech segment, including textual, temporal, speaker-related, and prosody-related information.

每个音频片段首先由多个自动语音识别（ASR）系统转录以获得文本内容。我们从多个开源系统收集转录结果，包括 ParaformerEach audio segment is first transcribed by multiple automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems to obtain textual content. We collect transcription results from several open-source systems, including Paraformer[paraformer], 火红ASR, FireRedASR[fireredasr]，和耳语, and Whisper[whisper]，以及内部 ASR 模型。多系统转录可实现跨系统一致性检查，提高最终文本标签的可靠性。, as well as internal ASR models. Multi-system transcriptions enable cross-system consistency checking, improving the reliability of the final text labels.

接下来，每个音频-文本对由重叠语音检测 (OSD) 模块进行处理Next, each audio-text pair is processed by an overlapping speech detection (OSD) module333https://huggingface.co/pyannote/segmentation-3.0和强制对准模块。 OSD 组件识别包含多个说话者同时发出的语音的片段，而对齐器则在转录文本和声音信号之间建立时间对应关系。基于对齐结果，我们进一步应用基于Qwen3-Force-Alignment构建的韵律注释系统and a forced alignment module. The OSD component identifies segments containing simultaneous speech from multiple speakers, while the aligner establishes temporal correspondence between the transcribed text and the acoustic signal. Based on the alignment results, we further apply a prosody annotation system built upon Qwen3-Force-Alignment[qwen3asr]注释分层韵律结构。to annotate hierarchical prosodic structures.

然后，我们使用 3D-Speaker-Toolkit 执行说话者标记We then perform speaker tagging using 3D-Speaker-Toolkit[3dspeaker]为每个片段分配说话人身份元数据，有助于构建说话人一致的训练样本。此外，我们还进行频谱滚降分析，以识别高频内容不足的低带宽录音，防止有效采样率过低的音频进入训练集。to assign speaker identity metadata to each segment, facilitating the construction of speaker-consistent training samples. In addition, we perform spectral rolloff analysis to identify low-bandwidth recordings with insufficient high-frequency content, preventing audio with excessively low effective sampling rates from entering the training set.

2.3  质量过滤2.3  Quality Filtering

最后阶段根据前一阶段积累的质量信号和注释去除不可靠或不需要的样本。The final stage removes unreliable or undesirable samples based on the quality signals and annotations accumulated in the previous stages.

我们首先应用截断检测器来识别开头或结尾不完整的话语。此类样本通常是由不准确的分割或突然剪切引起的，可能会对模型训练产生不利影响。然后，我们使用合成语音检测器来识别可能是人工生成的而不是自然说出的片段。此步骤对于大规模爬取数据尤其重要，其中合成语音可能与真实录音混合。We first apply a truncation detector to identify utterances with incomplete beginnings or endings. Such samples are typically caused by inaccurate segmentation or abrupt clipping and can adversely affect model training. We then employ a synthetic speech detector to identify segments that are likely to be artificially generated rather than naturally spoken. This step is particularly important for large-scale crawled data, where synthesized speech may be mixed with authentic recordings.

在这些专门的检测步骤之后，所有可用的元数据和质量指标都被聚合在最终的过滤模块中。该模块通过共同考虑声学质量、语音有效性、转录可靠性、重叠条件、说话者一致性、截断风险、合成可能性和频谱质量来执行样本选择。仅保留满足所有过滤标准的样本。After these dedicated detection steps, all available metadata and quality indicators are aggregated in a final filtering module. This module performs sample selection by jointly considering acoustic quality, speech validity, transcription reliability, overlap condition, speaker consistency, truncation risk, synthesis likelihood, and spectral quality. Only samples that satisfy all filtering criteria are retained.

我们没有丢弃排除的样本，而是保留所有处理过的数据及其质量标签和注释元数据，从而能够灵活构建具有不同质量要求的数据集以用于未来的任务。经过过滤后，我们保留了大约20万小时的中英文语音数据用于预训练。该管道中使用的所有组成模块都是公开访问的，允许任何团队轻松获取它们并快速组装适合自己的数据准备需求的类似管道。Rather than discarding excluded samples, we preserve all processed data together with their quality tags and annotation metadata, enabling the flexible construction of datasets with different quality requirements for future tasks. After filtering, we retain approximately 200,000 hours of Chinese and English speech data for pre-training. All constituent modules used in this pipeline are openly accessible, allowing any team to readily obtain them and rapidly assemble a comparable pipeline tailored to their own data preparation needs.

3.2  语音标记器3.2  Speech Tokenizer

3.3.1 解耦说话人和风格编码3.3.1 Decoupled Speaker and Style Encoding

自回归模块根据参考音频的文本输入生成离散语义标记。一个关键的设计问题是如何从参考信号中提取与说话人相关的信息。现有方法遵循两个范例： 音频令牌延续The autoregressive module generates discrete semantic tokens from text input conditioned on reference audio. A key design question is how to extract speaker-related information from the reference signal. Existing approaches follow two paradigms: audio token continuation[chen2025neural, cosyvoice]和扬声器嵌入。前者直接利用细粒度的声学提示进行高保真克隆，但其质量会因嘈杂或短的提示而降低，而长的提示会大大增加推理成本；后者通过将参考压缩为固定维度的向量来提高鲁棒性，但不可避免地会丢弃音色细节，并且无法捕获动态风格特征，例如语速和韵律轮廓。 为了解决这两种范式的局限性，我们的调节机制旨在（1）从任意长度、质量和口音的参考音频中稳健地提取说话者信息，以及（2）共同捕获全局音色和动态说话风格。and speaker embedding. The former directly leverages fine-grained acoustic cues for high-fidelity cloning, but its quality degrades with noisy or short prompts, and long prompts substantially increase inference cost; the latter improves robustness by compressing the reference into a fixed-dimensional vector, but inevitably discards timbral details and fails to capture dynamic style features such as speaking rate and prosodic contours. To address the limitations of both paradigms, our conditioning mechanism is designed to (1) extract speaker information robustly from reference audio of arbitrary length, quality, and accent, and (2) jointly capture global timbre and dynamic speaking style.

为此，我们采用基于 Q-Former 的To this end, we adopt a Q-Former-based[blip2, qformer-efficient]调节器通过交叉注意力提取风格条件。具体来说，调节器的输入是冻结的 -Bert 2.0 编码器的输出conditioner to extract style condition through cross-attention. Specifically, the input to the conditioner is the output of a frozen -Bert 2.0 Encoder[chung2021w2v]，它是在大规模数据上进行预训练的，可产生语义丰富的表示。为了补充全局音色信息，我们另外引入了冻结的 CAMPPlus, which is pretrained on large-scale data that produces semantically rich representations. To supplement global timbre information, we additionally introduce a frozen CAMPPlus[campp]提取静态说话人嵌入的编码器。由于说话人嵌入已经携带了说话人身份，因此它允许 Q-Former 调节器专注于提取动态说话风格。encoder that extracts a static speaker embedding. Since the speaker embedding already carries speaker identity, it allows the Q-Former conditioner to focus on extracting dynamic speaking style.

3.3.3 情绪控制3.3.3 Emotion Control

所提出的调节器从参考音频中捕获丰富的与说话者相关的属性，包括情感特征。 预训练的语言模型主干进一步贡献了从文本上下文中进行隐式情感推断。 然而，隐式控制缺乏精度和稳定性。因此，我们通过对大约 2,200 小时的情绪标记数据进行后训练，引入了明确的情绪控制。 统一的标签系统支持七个主要类别，即快乐、悲伤、愤怒、恐惧、蔑视、严重和惊讶，以及四个扩展类别：关注、蓝色（忧郁）、厌恶和心理（内心独白）。The proposed conditioner captures rich speaker-related attributes from the reference audio, including emotional characteristics. The pretrained language model backbone further contributes implicit emotion inference from textual context. However, implicit control lacks precision and stability. We therefore introduce explicit emotion control via post-training on approximately 2,200 hours of emotion-labeled data. The unified label system supports seven primary categories, namely happy, sad, angry, fear, contempt, serious, and surprise, together with four extended categories: concern, blue (melancholy), disgust, and psychology (inner monologue).

3.3.4 副语言生成3.3.4 Paralinguistic Generation

在我们的环境中，我们关注四种我们认为对自然语音生成最重要的副语言现象：笑声、呼吸、哭泣和咳嗽。我们的模型支持这四种行为以及一种包裹式笑声模式（LAUGH_SPAN），其中笑声在整个话语中暂时与语音结合在一起。这种设计可以更精确地控制笑声同时发生的时间和方式，从而提高合成的准确性和一致性。In our setting, we focus on four paralinguistic phenomena that we consider most important for our natural speech generation: laughter, breathing, crying, and coughing. Our model supports these four behaviors plus a wrapped laughter mode (LAUGH_SPAN), in which laughter is temporally coupled with speech throughout an utterance. This design enables more precise control of when and how laughter co-occurs, thereby improving synthesis accuracy and consistency.

与仅限于固定实现模式的先前方法不同，我们的模型以两种模式运行。在Unlike prior approaches limited to fixed realization patterns, our model operates in two modes. In隐含的implicit模式下，它从文本上下文中推断出适当的副语言行为，根据设置产生不同的笑声类型（克制的笑声、轻柔的笑声、衷心的笑声）。在mode, it infers appropriate paralinguistic behavior from textual context, producing varied laughter types (restrained chuckle, soft giggle, hearty laughter) depending on the setting. In明确的explicit模式下，用户通过文本拟声词指定行为。两种模式都支持具有任意扬声器提示的零样本合成。mode, users specify behaviors through textual onomatopoeia. Both modes support zero-shot synthesis with arbitrary speaker prompts.

这些功能是通过对来自数据处理输出和内部集合的大约 200 小时的副语言数据进行监督微调来实现的。These capabilities are realized through supervised fine-tuning on approximately 200 hours of paralinguistic data from the data processing outputs, and internal collections.

3.3.5 方言综合3.3.5 Dialect Synthesis

方言合成使用普通话音频作为说话者提示和方言标签来生成目标方言语音（Dialect synthesis generates target-dialect speech using Mandarin audio as the speaker prompt and a dialect tag (\lvert​lang\rvert\lvert\text{lang}\rvert) 来指定品种。主要挑战是数据稀缺：方言录音的数量远不如普通话丰富。) to specify the variety. The central challenge is data scarcity: dialect recordings are far less abundant than Mandarin.

我们观察到，经过双语预训练后，即使提示包含方言特征，模型也能稳健地生成普通话。利用这一点，我们通过使用预训练模型合成每个方言使用者的三个普通话话语来构建并行数据，产生大规模的“方言-普通话”对，从而缓解稀疏性。We observe that after bilingual pre-training, the model generates Mandarin robustly even when the prompt contains dialectal features. Exploiting this, we construct parallel data by synthesizing three Mandarin utterances per dialect speaker using the pretrained model, yielding large-scale “dialect–Mandarin” pairs that alleviate sparsity.

在对大约 16,000 小时的方言数据进行方言微调时，我们采用混合提示采样：目标始终是方言话语，而条件提示以相同概率从同一说话人的普通话或方言话语中抽取。这迫使模型从风格多样的提示中提取说话者身份并将其映射到目标方言，而不是直接复制提示样式。During dialect fine-tuning on approximately 16,000 hours of dialect data, we employ mixed-prompt sampling: the target is always a dialect utterance, while the conditioning prompt is drawn with equal probability from either a Mandarin or dialect utterance of the same speaker. This forces the model to extract speaker identity from stylistically diverse prompts and map it to the target dialect, rather than copying prompt style directly.

3.4  语音解码器3.4  Speech Decoder

语音解码器使用条件流匹配 (CFM) 将离散语义代码转换为梅尔谱图The speech decoder converts discrete semantic codes into mel spectrograms using Conditional Flow Matching (CFM)[flowmatching]与 DiTwith a DiT[dit]主干300M参数。调节输入为backbone of 300M parameters. The conditioning input is[𝐌ref,𝐬,𝐞Audiotgt][\mathbf{M}_{\text{ref}},\;\mathbf{s},\;\mathbf{e}_{\text{Audio}}^{\text{tgt}}]，包括参考梅尔频谱图、CAMPPlus 说话人嵌入和预测语义特征。通过 10 步迭代去噪，解码器逐步将噪声样本转换为以这些输入为条件的目标梅尔频谱图。然后生成的梅尔谱图由 HiFi-GAN 转换为波形, comprising the reference mel spectrogram, CAMPPlus speaker embedding, and predicted semantic features. The generated mel spectrogram is then converted to a waveform by HiFi-GAN[hifigan].

5 结论和未来工作5 Conclusion and Future Work

我们推出了 PilotTTS，这是一个基于公开可用模块构建的文本转语音系统，其设计理念是优先考虑集成和数据工程而不是架构新颖性。完全由公开可用的工具组装而成的数据处理管道使系统只需大约 200,000 小时的训练数据即可实现具有竞争力的零样本性能。在Seed-TTS Eval基准测试中，PilotTTS在中英文测试集上获得了最高的说话者相似度，并且在英语测试集上获得了最低的单词错误率。解耦调节机制进一步实现了情绪控制、副语言生成和跨方言合成，所有这些都是通过有针对性的后训练实现的。We have presented PilotTTS, a text-to-speech system built upon publicly available modules with a design philosophy that prioritizes integration and data engineering over architectural novelty. A data processing pipeline assembled entirely from publicly available tools enables the system to achieve competitive zero-shot performance with only approximately 200,000 hours of training data. On the Seed-TTS Eval benchmark, PilotTTS attains the highest speaker similarity on both Chinese and English test sets and the lowest word error rate on the English set. The decoupled conditioning mechanism further enables emotion control, paralinguistic generation, and cross-dialect synthesis, all through targeted post-training.

尽管取得了这些结果，但仍然存在一些局限性并指出了未来的方向：Despite these results, several limitations remain and point to future directions:

  1. 1.

    明确的风格建模不足。Insufficient explicit style modeling.我们的架构不包含具有强大表征能力的专用风格建模模块；相反，它依赖于 Q-Former 条件来隐式捕获与风格相关的因素。因此，合成质量可能会受到表达细节粒度的限制。为了解决这个限制，我们正在开发一个表示模块，联合建模全局风格和局部（细粒度）风格，旨在增强生成音频的表现力。Our architecture does not include a dedicated style modeling module with strong representational capacity; instead, it relies on the Q-Former condition to implicitly capture style-related factors. As a result, the synthesis quality may be limited in the granularity of expressive details. To address this limitation, we are developing a representation module that jointly models global style and local (fine-grained) style, aiming to enhance the expressiveness of generated audio.

  2. 2.

    单码本量化上限。Single-codebook quantization ceiling.尽管单码本 FSQ 分词器在架构上更简单并且通常足以满足语音需求，但它比多码本残差矢量量化或连续潜在表示具有较低的信息容量上限。因此，将模型扩展到更复杂的场景（例如唱歌和背景音乐）变得更具挑战性。Although the single-codebook FSQ tokenizer is architecturally simpler and is generally sufficient for speech, it possesses a lower information-capacity ceiling than multi-codebook residual vector quantization or continuous latent representations. As a result, it becomes more challenging to extend the model to more complex scenarios such as singing and background music.

  3. 3.

    有损梅尔谱图重建。Lossy mel-spectrogram reconstruction.解码管道依赖于梅尔频谱图和单独的声码器，这是一种间接重建过程，与端到端波形生成相比，可能会引入额外的失真。The decoding pipeline relies on mel spectrograms and a separate vocoder, an indirect reconstruction process that may introduce additional distortion compared to end-to-end waveform generation.

未来的工作将集中在引入显式编码器、探索更高容量的量化策略以及采用端到端波形生成架构。所有代码和模型权重将继续作为开源发布。Future work will focus on introducing an explicit style encoder, exploring higher-capacity quantization strategies, and adopting end-to-end waveform generation architectures. All code and model weights will continue to be released as open source.
