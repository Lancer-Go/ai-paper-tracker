摘要Abstract

大型推理模型（LRM）在英语和其他语言之间仍然表现出巨大的性能差距，但当前的许多工作假设这些差距可以通过使每种语言的推理类似于英语推理来缩小这些差距。这项工作通过提出以下问题来挑战这一假设：多语言环境中有效推理的实际特征是什么，以及英语派生推理功能在多大程度上真正对其他语言有帮助？我们首先定义一套可测量的推理特征，涵盖推理轨迹的多语言对齐、推理步骤和推理流程方面，并使用逻辑回归来量化每个特征与最终答案准确性的关联。我们进一步在多语言轨迹上训练稀疏自动编码器，以自动发现实例化或扩展这些特征的潜在推理概念。最后，我们使用这些特征作为测试时选择策略来检查它们是否可以引导模型进行更强大的多语言推理。在两个数学推理基准、四种 LRM 和 10 种语言中，我们发现大多数特征与准确性呈正相关，但关联强度在不同语言中差异很大，甚至在某些语言中可能相反。我们的研究结果挑战了以英语为中心的奖励设计，并指出了适应特定语言推理模式的适应性目标，对多语言基准和奖励设计具有具体影响。 [1]Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) still exhibit large performance gaps between English and other languages, yet much current work assumes these gaps can be closed simply by making reasoning in every language resemble English reasoning. This work challenges this assumption by asking instead: what actually characterizes effective reasoning in multilingual set- tings, and to what extent do English derived reasoning features genuinely help in other languages? We first define a suite of measurable reasoning features spanning multilingual alignment, reasoning step, and reasoning flow aspects of reasoning traces, and use logistic regression to quantify how each feature associates with final answer accuracy. We further train sparse autoencoders over multilingual traces to automatically discover latent reasoning concepts that instantiate or extend these features. Finally, we use the features as test-time selection policies to examine whether they can steer models toward stronger multilingual reasoning. Across two mathematical reasoning benchmarks, four LRMs, and 10 languages, we find that most features are positively associated with accuracy, but the strength of association varies considerably across languages and can even reverse in some. Our findings challenge English-centric reward designs and point toward adaptive objectives that accommodate language-specific reasoning patterns, with concrete implications for multilingual benchmark and reward design.[1]

1 简介1 Introduction

推进多语言推理对于跨多种语言部署大型推理模型 (LRM) 和改善全球用户体验至关重要（Shi 等人，2022 年；Ghosh 等人，2025 年）。然而，当用英语以外的语言查询 LRM 时，仍然存在巨大的性能和行为差距（Huang et al., 2025; Tam et al., 2025），导致推理准确性较低（Wang et al., 2025; Luo et al., 2025）以及推理轨迹和最终答案之间的系统性不匹配（Ovalle et al., 2026; Reddy et al., 2026）。Advancing multilingual reasoning is critical for deploying Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) across diverse languages and improving user experiences worldwide (Shi et al., 2022; Ghosh et al., 2025). Yet substantial performance and behavioral gaps remain when LRMs are queried in languages other than English (Huang et al., 2025; Tam et al., 2025), leading to lower reasoning accuracy (Wang et al., 2025; Luo et al., 2025) and systematic mismatches between reasoning traces and final answers (Ovalle et al., 2026; Reddy et al., 2026).

弥补这些差距的最新方法通常将其他语言的推理投射到英语空间中：它们将查询翻译成英语（Zhu et al., 2024; Ko et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2026a），或者奖励结构上（Lai & Nissim, 2024）或语义上与英语推理相似的痕迹（She et al., 2024; Faisal et al., 2025; Zhuang et al., 2025）等人，2026；Sutawika 等人，2026）。虽然这些方法可以减少准确性差距，但它们隐含地假设英语中表示“良好”推理的特征不会改变到其他语言。这种假设是脆弱的：新出现的证据表明，模型有时可以更有效地用原始语言进行推理，而不是翻译成英语（Gao 等人，2025），并且用其他语言查询时的痕迹可能遵循不同但同样有效的推理轨迹，特别是对于类型上相距较远的语言（Tam 等人，2025）。因此，盲目奖励类似英语的推理可能会掩盖甚至惩罚支持正确答案的特定语言推理行为。Recent approaches to closing these gaps often project reasoning in other languages into English space: they translate queries into English (Zhu et al., 2024; Ko et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2026a), or reward traces that are structurally (Lai & Nissim, 2024) or semantically similar to English reasoning (She et al., 2024; Faisal et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2026; Sutawika et al., 2026). While these methods can reduce accuracy gaps, they implicitly assume that the features signaling “good” reasoning in English transfer unchanged to other languages. This assumption is fragile: emerging evidence suggests models can sometimes reason more effectively in the original language instead of translating to English (Gao et al., 2025), and that traces when queried in other languages may follow distinct but equally valid reasoning trajectories, particularly for typologically distant languages (Tam et al., 2025). Blindly rewarding English-like reasoning therefore risks obscuring or even penalizing language-specific reasoning behaviors that support correct answers.

2.2 解开推理痕迹2.2 Disentangling Reasoning Traces

最近的工作将 LRM 痕迹分解为中间步骤，以表征其结构和行为特性（Lanham 等人，2023；Paul 等人，2024）。人们提出了一套多样化的阶梯评估标准，包括事实性（Golovneva et al., 2022）、信息性（Prasad et al., 2023）、相关性（Jacovi et al., 2024）、实用性和有效性（Lee & Hockenmaier, 2025）以及连贯性（Do et al., 2025）。结构分析通过相关方法进一步将图结构（Jiang et al., 2025；Gandhi et al., 2025；Li et al., 2025a）和自我修正模式（Feng et al., 2025）与性能联系起来。另外，稀疏自动编码器（SAE；Cunningham 等人（2023））已被用于提取可解释的潜在特征，这些特征既可以解释推理行为（Galichin 等人，2025），又可以引导模型走向更高的性能（Ma 等人，2026）。然而，这些文献几乎完全局限于英语，在英语数据集和特征空间上运行，使得多语言环境的泛化在很大程度上尚未得到探索。我们的工作通过研究这些特征如何跨语言转移以及与多语言推理性能的关系来弥补这一差距。Recent work decomposes LRM traces into intermediate steps to characterize their structural and behavioral properties (Lanham et al., 2023; Paul et al., 2024). A diverse set of steplevel evaluation criteria has been proposed, including factuality (Golovneva et al., 2022), informativeness (Prasad et al., 2023), relevance (Jacovi et al., 2024), utility and validity (Lee & Hockenmaier, 2025), and coherence (Do et al., 2025). Structural analyses further link graph structure (Jiang et al., 2025; Gandhi et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025a) and self-revision patterns (Feng et al., 2025) to performance through correlational methods. Separately, sparse autoencoders (SAEs; Cunningham et al. (2023)) have been used to extract interpretable latent features that both explain reasoning behavior (Galichin et al., 2025) and steer models toward higher performance (Ma et al., 2026). However, this literature is almost entirely confined to English, operating on English datasets and feature spaces, leaving generalization to multilingual settings largely unexplored. Our work bridges this gap by examining how these features transfer across languages and relate to multilingual reasoning performance.

我们的目标是描述多语言环境中有效推理的特征。为此，我们首先用每种目标语言的查询独立提示每个 LRM，以产生推理轨迹和最终答案，作为所有后续分析的输入。然后，我们 ➊ 定义一组可测量的推理特征（第 3.1 节），通过 ➋ 基于回归的特征分析（第 3.2 节）和 ➌ SAE 分析（第 3.3 节）量化每个特征与准确性的关系，并使用 ➍ 测试时间选择来探测特征是否可以引导模型走向更高的准确性（第 3.4 节），如图 1 所示。所有提示均在附录 C 中提供。Our goal is to characterize what constitutes effective reasoning in multilingual settings. 3.3 稀疏自编码器（SAE）分析3.3 Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) Analysis

我们通过在多语言推理轨迹上训练稀疏自动编码器（SAE），用更细粒度的表示驱动方法来补充手工设计的特征分析。继最近使用 SAE 从文本生成假设的工作（Movva 等人，2025a；b）之后，我们将每个推理轨迹视为输入，将最终答案的准确性视为目标，并学习一组稀疏的、可解释的潜在概念来解释准确性的变化。We complement the hand-designed feature analysis with a finer-grained, representationdriven approach by training sparse autoencoders (SAEs) over multilingual reasoning traces. Following recent work using SAEs for hypothesis generation from text (Movva et al., 2025a;b), we treat each reasoning trace as input and final answer accuracy as the target, and learn a set of sparse, interpretable latent concepts that explain variation in accuracy.

对于每种模型和语言，我们将每个推理轨迹分成最多 400 个单词的片段，使用 LABSE 嵌入对每个块进行编码（Feng 等人，2022），并训练 Batch TopK SAE（Bussmann 等人，2024）来重建它们。每个块都会继承其父跟踪的准确性标签。然后，我们通过基于相关性的标准来识别预测性 SAE 神经元：对于每个神经元，我们计算其激活向量和二进制精度标签之间的 Pearson 相关性，并通过绝对相关性保留前 20 个神经元。For each model and language, we chunk each reasoning trace into segments of up to 400 words, encode each chunk with LABSE embeddings (Feng et al., 2022), and train a Batch TopK SAE (Bussmann et al., 2024) to reconstruct them. Each chunk inherits the accuracy label of its parent trace. We then identify predictive SAE neurons via a correlation-based criterion: for each neuron, we compute the Pearson correlation between its activation vector and the binary accuracy label, and retain the top-20 neurons by absolute correlation.

为了解释每个神经元，我们向 GPT-4O 提示最强烈激活它的 10 个块以及 10 个随机采样的非激活块，并要求对区分概念进行简短的自然语言描述。对于每个概念，我们报告其分离分数（概念存在与不存在时的准确度差异）及其普遍性（出现该概念的示例的比例）。最后，我们将学习到的概念与我们的特征进行比较，以评估 SAE 恢复相同底层模式的程度。 [4]To interpret each neuron, we prompt GPT-4O with the 10 chunks that most strongly activate it alongside 10 randomly sampled non-activating chunks, and ask for a short natural language description of the distinguishing concept. For each concept, we report its separation score (the difference in accuracy when the concept is present vs. absent) and its prevalence (the fraction of examples in which it appears). Finally, we compare the learned concepts to our features to assess the extent to which SAEs recover the same underlying patterns.[4]

总之，我们的特征分析表明，大多数推理特征具有一致的方向效应，但它们的大小差异很大，甚至可能在不同语言之间发生冲突。Together, our feature analysis shows that most reasoning features have consistent directional effects, but their magnitudes vary substantially and can even conflict across languages.

我们讨论了我们的发现对于推进多语言推理的重要意义。We discuss important implications of our findings for advancing multilingual reasoning.

推理基准设计。Reasoning Benchmark Design.(1) 与 COMET-QE 相关的正 ΔAcc 强调，应该准确呈现英语以外语言的基准查询，最好通过人工翻译（Shi 等人，2022）或对代表性子集进行人工验证（Chen 等人，2024；Wang 等人，2025；Dobler 等人，2026）。 (2) 我们的研究结果表明(1) The positive ∆Acc associated with COMET-QE underscores that benchmark queries in languages other than English should be accurately rendered, ideally via human translation (Shi et al., 2022) or human verification on a representative subset (Chen et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025; Dobler et al., 2026). 奖励模型设计。Reward Model Design.(1) 我们的特征分析（第 5.1 节）和测试时间选择实验（第 5.3 节）都表明，奖励与英语痕迹的语义相似性虽然具有竞争力，但并不是普遍有效的转向信号。替代信号，例如直接和间接效用或结果整合，可以产生更强的多语言推理性能，尤其是在 AIME 等具有挑战性的基准上。这激发了明确合并这些信号的训练目标，而不是仅仅依赖语义相似性。 (2) 如图 3 所示，相同的特征可能会导致跨语言的冲突效应，这凸显了鼓励特定于语言的推理模式的必要性。结合最近的自适应推理工作（Wu et al., 2025；Gao et al., 2026），我们提出了自适应奖励设计，可以灵活地决定何时保留原始语言、何时翻译以及优先考虑哪些特征信号。(1) Both our feature analysis (§5.1) and test-time selection experiments (§5.3) show that rewarding semantic similarity to English traces, while competitive, is not a universally effective steering signal. 7 局限性7 Limitations

范围有限。Limited scope.我们的分析仅限于当前的实验设置。首先，我们手工设计的特征集包括 16 个可测量的推理特征，这不一定是详尽的，并且没有涵盖多语言推理的潜在信息信号的全部空间。其次，我们专门关注数学推理基准，它具有以下几个优点：（i）跨一组代表性语言的并行查询，（ii）清晰的可验证正确性信号（准确性），以及（iii）典型的多步骤问题，使它们成为我们的自然测试平台。Our analysis is constrained to the current experimental setup. First, our hand-designed feature set includes 16 measurable reasoning features, which is necessarily non-exhaustive and does not cover the full space of potentially informative signals for multilingual reasoning. Second, we focus exclusively on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, which offer several advantages: (i) parallel queries across a representative set of languages, (ii) a clear verifiable correctness signal (accuracy), and (iii) typically multi-step problems that make them a natural testbed for our推理流程Reasoning Flow特点。然而，我们的发现可能不会直接转移到非数学领域，例如常识、法律或多跳问答，其中推理的本质和信息量最大的特征空间可能有所不同。最后，我们的实验仅限于四个 LRM，因此观察到的模式可能不适用于其他架构或训练方案；我们认为我们的工作是迈向更广泛调查的第一步。features. However, our findings may not directly transfer to non-mathematical domains such as commonsense, legal, or multi-hop QA, where both the nature of reasoning and the most informative feature space may differ. Finally, our experiments are limited to four LRMs, so the observed patterns may not hold for other architectures or training regimes; we view our work as an initial step toward broader investigations.
