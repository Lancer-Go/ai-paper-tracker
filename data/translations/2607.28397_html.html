摘要Abstract

基于知识图的检索增强生成（RAG）需要能够有效捕获图结构和语义信息的检索器。最近的方法探索了基于图神经网络（GNN）的检索器来对多跳推理任务中的图拓扑进行建模。与此同时，图语言模型（GLM）已经成为一种有前途的范式，它集成了图推理和语言模型的语义功能。在这项工作中，我们介绍了一种基于 GLM 的检索器，并研究了基于 GLM、基于 GNN 和传统的基于向量搜索的检索器在单跳和多跳 RAG 设置中的比较优势，并特别关注到未见域的可转移性。我们的研究结果表明，经过微调的 GLM 检索器在域外具有更好的泛化能力，在两个多跳基准上实现了 SOTA。在域内多跳 QA 数据集上，它们仍然与之前的工作相当，随着参数和子图覆盖范围的增加，有望进行扩展。基于 GNN 的检索器通过高效的训练设置实现了更高的图覆盖率，而矢量搜索基线在单跳数据集上表现出色。Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over knowledge graphs requires retrievers that can effectively capture both graph structure and semantic information. Recent approaches have explored graph neural network (GNN)-based retrievers to model graph topology in multi-hop reasoning tasks. In parallel, graph language models (GLMs) have emerged as a promising paradigm that integrates graph reasoning and the semantic capabilities of language models. In this work, we introduce a GLM-based retriever and investigate the comparative strengths of GLM-based, GNN-based, and traditional vector-search-based retrievers in single- and multi-hop RAG settings, and with a particular focus on transferability to unseen domains. Our findings suggest that finetuned GLM retrievers generalize better out of domain, achieving SOTA on two multi-hop benchmarks. On in-domain multi-hop QA datasets they remain comparable to prior work, with promising scaling as parameters and subgraph coverage increase. 3 初步：图语言模型Preliminary: Graph Language Models

图语言模型 (GLM) 通过将预训练的 LM 转换为图转换器来连接语言模型和图转换器Graph Language Models (GLMs) bridge language models and graph transformers by converting a pretrained LM into a graph transformer(Plenz and Frank, 2024)。这种设计使模型能够利用来自 LM 的预训练语言理解，同时通过架构设计纳入结构图推理。. This design enables the model to leverage pretrained language understanding from the LM while incorporating structural graph reasoning through the architectural design.

这种设计使得 GLM 非常适合处理文本属性图，例如知识图。该方法涉及两个主要步骤。首先，知识（子）图经过预处理：边和节点标签被单独标记并根据原始图结构连接。这种转换允许每个三元组表示为类似于自然文本的标记序列，同时仍然在多个三元组之间共享节点标记，就像在标准图形结构中一样。其次，LM 的自注意力机制被修改为使用相对位置编码来捕获同一三元组内令牌对之间的距离，从而使模型能够This design makes GLMs ideal for processing text-attributed graphs, such as knowledge graphs. The method involves two main steps. First, the knowledge (sub)graph undergoes preprocessing: edge and node labels are tokenized individually and connected according to the original graph structure. This transformation allows each triplet to be represented as a token sequence resembling natural text, while still sharing node tokens across multiple triplets, as in a standard graph structure. Second, the LM’s self-attention mechanism is modified to use relative positional encodings that capture distances between token pairs within the same triple, thereby enabling the model to将三元组读取为连续文本read triplets as sequential text– 就像语言模型一样。虽然这些对于 LM 来说是典型的注意力模式应用于三元组，但 GLM 还使用类似 GNN 的注意力模式来捕获整体图结构。这些调整共同产生了一个模型，该模型能够像连续语言一样读取标记图，从而实现强大的 (i) 语言理解能力，同时 (ii) 非常适合图 RAG 的基于图的推理能力。我们参考原始出版物以获取有关该方法以及实验验证的更多详细信息。– just as a language model would. While these attention patterns, which are typical for LMs, are applied to triplets, the GLM also uses GNN-like attention patterns, which capture the overall graph structure. Together, these adaptations yield a model that is able to read a labeled graph much like contiguous language, thereby achieving strong (i) language understanding abilities and simultaneously, (ii) graph-based reasoning abilities that are well-suited for graph RAG. We refer to the original publications for more details on the method as well as experimental validation.

当种子实体使用查询嵌入进行初始化时，所有其他节点都使用零向量进行初始化。相比之下，关系是用句子嵌入来初始化的。因此，GNN 生成的节点表示是由它们与种子实体的相对图距离驱动的，而不是由它们的语义特征驱动的。节点文本专门用于识别种子实体，并且在 GNN 检索器中不起进一步的作用。鉴于这个缺点，我们还探索了 GFM-RAG 的一种变体，GFM-RAG+，其中所有节点都用它们的句子嵌入进行初始化（参见第 2 节）。While seed entities are initialized with the query embeddings, all other nodes are initialized with zero vectors. By contrast, relations are initialized with their sentence embeddings. Consequently, the resulting node representations from the GNN are driven by their relative graph distance to the seed entities rather than their semantic features. Node texts are used exclusively to identify seed entities and play no further role in the GNN retriever. Given this shortcoming, we also explore a variant of GFM-RAG, GFM-RAG+, where all nodes are initialized with their sentence embeddings (see Sec.5.1).

在最后一层，每个节点的表示与查询嵌入相连接，并由 MLP 进行评分，以找到最相关的节点。At the final layer, the per-node representations are concatenated with the query embedding and scored by an MLP to find the most relevant nodes.

4.2.2 基于 GLM 的检索器GLM-based Retriever

我们提出的基于 GLM 的检索器旨在采用图 RAG 方法，最大限度地发挥文本属性图的潜力。Our proposed GLM-based retriever aims for a graph RAG approach that maximizes the potential of text-attributed graphs.

GLM 检索器的内部工作原理与基于 GNN 的检索器有着根本的不同。最值得注意的是，基于 GNN 的检索器没有利用图中丰富的文本特征，而是依赖于查询和原始图结构，而我们的 GLM 检索器通过 GLM 的文本编码能力充分利用了图中的语义（§The inner workings of the GLM retriever fundamentally differ from those of the GNN-based one. Most notably, the GNN-based retriever does not make use of the rich textual features in the graph and instead relies on the query and the raw graph structure, whereas our GLM retriever fully utilizes the semantics in the graph, through the GLM’s text encoding abilities (§3).

GLM 检索器提取种子实体周围的局部子图，将其转换为文本三元组序列，并通过具有结构感知相对位置的图编码器对单独标记化的边和节点表示进行编码（参见图 1 的左侧）。The GLM retriever extracts a local subgraph around the seed entities, converts it into a sequence of textual triplets, and encodes the individually tokenized edge and node representation through a graph encoder with structure-aware relative positions (see left side of Fig.2）。使用底层 LM 自己的标记器和标记嵌入层对节点和关系进行标记和嵌入。令牌通过 LM 层传递，使用注意力掩码对图结构进行编码。这与 GNN 检索器形成鲜明对比，其中只有种子实体使用非零嵌入进行初始化。因此，查询图交互发生在更早的阶段，即当问题文本与 GLM 第一个注意力层内提取的子图结构融合时。得到的每个实体嵌入按元素与投影问题向量相乘，并通过评分头。). Nodes and relations are tokenized and embedded using the underlying LM’s own tokenizer and token embedding layer. The tokens are passed through the LM’s layers, using attention masking to encode the graph structure. This stands in contrast to the GNN retriever, where only the seed entities are initialized with non-zero embeddings. As a result, the query-graph interaction happens at a much earlier stage, namely when the question text gets fused with the extracted subgraph structure inside the GLM’s first attention layer. The resulting per-entity embeddings are element-wise multiplied with a projected question vector and passed through a scoring head.

7 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了一种通过训练基于 GLM 的检索器来处理图 RAG 设置中的多跳问题的替代方法。我们的实验表明，GLM-RAG 是一种更通用的方法，具有更好的可扩展性，与构建图基础模型的工作一致。通过集成文本属性图中托管的语义知识，基于 GLM 的检索器可以更好地理解和选择相关文档，更多地依赖于评估文档实体和问题之间的相似性，而并发的基于 GNN 的检索器更多地依赖于结构信号。我们对普通 RAG、基于 GNN 和基于 GLM 的检索器的广泛比较表明：(i) 对于不需要多跳推理的单跳问题，普通 RAG 基线就足够了，(ii) 对于多跳问题，使用节点嵌入初始化的微调 GFM-RAG 模型 (GFM-RAG+) 显示了与 GLM-RAG 的竞争性能，(iii) 对于零样本多跳问题，GLM-RAG 始终表现出更好的性能与基于 GNN 的同类产品相比，可转移能力。In this paper, we present an alternative approach to handle multi-hop questions in graph RAG settings by training a GLM-based retriever. Our experiments show that GLM-RAG is a more generalizable method with better scalability, in line with works on building a graph foundational model. By integrating the semantic knowledge hosted in a text-attributed graph, a GLM-based retriever can better understand and select relevant documents, relying more on assessing the similarity between document entities and the question, whereas concurrent GNN-based retrievers rely more on structural signals. Our extensive comparison of vanilla RAG, GNN-based and GLM-based retrievers reveal that: (i) for single-hop questions, which do not require multi-hop reasoning, a vanilla RAG baseline is sufficient, (ii) for multi-hop questions a finetuned GFM-RAG model initialized with node embeddings (GFM-RAG+) shows competitive performance with GLM-RAG, and (iii) for zero-shot multi-hop questions, GLM-RAG shows consistently better transferability capabilities compared to its GNN-based counterparts.

Future work could scale up GLM-based retrievers to combine their strong semantic capabilities with broader graph coverage.
