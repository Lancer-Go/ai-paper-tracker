ADEPT pretrains a dexterous policy on a generic object reposing task, then post-trains downstream policies with this pretrained behavior as a prior. 1 简介Introduction

高自由度机器人系统的灵巧操作，例如配备拟人多指手的手臂，仍然是机器人学习的主要挑战Dexterous manipulation with high degrees-of-freedom robotic systems, such as arms equipped with anthropomorphic multi-fingered hands, remains a major challenge for robot learning[1, 9, 16, 4]。多指手臂系统将高维状态和动作空间与丰富的接触交互相结合，使得很难从稀疏的任务特定奖励中发现有用的行为。即使使用大规模并行 GPU 模拟. Multi-fingered arm-hand systems combine high-dimensional state and action spaces with contact-rich interactions, making useful behaviors hard to discover from sparse task-specific rewards. Even with massively parallel GPU simulation[18]，为一项任务从头开始训练的强化学习策略很少转移到另一项任务；每项新任务都会重新开始，在出现任何特定于任务的行为之前，重新发现相同的低级技能，例如伸手、抓握、举起和重新定向。, RL policies trained from scratch for one task rarely transfer to another; each new task starts over, rediscovering the same low-level skills like reaching, grasping, lifting, and reorientation before any task-specific behavior can emerge.

这激发了不同的范式。我们建议首先获得一个通用的灵巧基础，然后将其适应特定的下游任务，而不是从头开始为每个新的灵巧任务训练强化学习专家This motivates a different paradigm. The policy’s initialization in parameter space is as consequential as the downstream objective it must adapt to. A generic pretraining task that exercises the underlying motor primitives lands the network in a region from which task-specific behaviors build on, rather than overwrite, the pretrained skills[13].

3 方法论Methodology

我们提出了一个框架，用于对通用任务目标进行基础灵活性预训练，并对接触丰富的下游任务进行后训练，如图 1 所示。We propose a framework for pretraining foundational dexterity on a generic task objective and post-training for contact-rich downstream tasks as outlined in Fig.2。我们的方法包括三个阶段：(1) 在通用对象操作任务的模拟中预训练灵巧的策略，(2) 通过蒸馏转移预训练的行为，并通过大规模并行策略 RL 后期训练进行适应，同时通过稳定的策略更新防止灾难性遗忘，以及 (3) 师生蒸馏，使用任务专家教师将其提炼成基于立体 RGB 视觉的学生，可以在现实世界中进行零样本部署。. Our approach consists of three stages: (1) pretraining a dexterous policy in simulation on a generic object manipulation task, (2) transferring the pretrained behavior through distillation and adapting through massively parallel on-policy RL post-training while preventing catastrophic forgetting through stable policy updates, and (3) teacher-student distillation using a task specialist teacher distilled into a stereo RGB vision-based student that can be zero-shot deployed in the real-world.

5 局限性Limitations

尽管现实世界的政策表现出的行为与模拟中观察到的行为非常匹配，但感知仍然是蒸馏的主要瓶颈。失败通常与咬合情况下不对称钉子方向的错误估计有关，而在手臂快速运动下，圆形 Allegro 指尖与钉子圆形侧面之间的小接触斑可能会引起抓握不稳定。 Flexiv-Sharpa 上的指尖触觉感应减轻了接触模糊性，但在遮挡情况下强大的以对象为中心的感知仍然开放。腕上安装的摄像头和其他平台上的触觉传感可能有助于解决这些限制。Although the real-world policies exhibit behaviors closely matching those observed in simulation, perception remains the primary bottleneck for distillation. Failures frequently coincide with incorrect estimates of the asymmetric peg’s orientation under occlusion, while grasp instability can arise from the small contact patch between the rounded Allegro fingertips and the rounded side of the peg under fast arm motion. Fingertip tactile sensing on Flexiv-Sharpa mitigates contact ambiguity, but robust object-centric perception under occlusion remains open. A wrist-mounted camera and tactile sensing on additional platforms may help address these limitations.

6 结论与讨论Conclusion and Discussion

我们提出了 ADEPT，一个训练前和训练后框架，用于通过强化学习学习灵巧的手臂操作。通用的重置任务为下游操作提供了可重用的初始化，而结构化的后训练则在策略适应并适应新的接触丰富的任务目标时保留了有用的预训练行为。在 Kuka-Allegro 和 Flexiv-Sharpa 中，相同的方法支持长视野行为，包括抓取、重新定向、运输和插入，以及通过视觉和视觉触觉学生策略转移到真实的机器人。我们的结果表明，可重复使用的灵巧预训练可以减少每个新操作问题必须重新发现的量，同时仍然为下游 RL 适应和获取特定于任务的行为留下空间。下一步重要的一步是将预训练分布扩展到更多样化的交互，包括手动操作、工具使用、杂乱和双手操作，并研究所得先验可以在多大程度上转移到与预训练有更大差异的任务。We presented ADEPT, a pre-training and post-training framework for learning dexterous arm-hand manipulation with reinforcement learning. A generic reposing task provides a reusable initialization for downstream manipulation, while structured post-training preserves useful pretrained behaviors as the policy adapts and aligns to new contact-rich task objectives. Across Kuka-Allegro and Flexiv-Sharpa, the same approach supports long horizon behaviors including grasping, reorientation, transport, and insertion, and transfers to real robots through vision and visuo-tactile student policies. Our results suggest that reusable dexterous pre-training can reduce how much must be rediscovered for each new manipulation problem, while still leaving room for downstream RL to adapt and acquire task-specific behavior. An important next step is to broaden the pre-training distribution to more diverse interactions, including in-hand manipulation, tool use, clutter, and bimanual manipulation, and study how far the resulting priors can transfer to tasks that differ more substantially from pre-training.
