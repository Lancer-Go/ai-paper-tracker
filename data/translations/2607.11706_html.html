VoxENES 2026：针对 LLM-Era TTS 和语音转换的语音欺骗检测器的基准通用化VoxENES 2026: Benchmarking Generalization of Speech Spoofing Detectors Against LLM-Era TTS and Voice Conversion

Aastha    Guangjing University of South Florida, Tampa, FL, USA aasthasharma@usf.edu, guangjingwang@usf.edu
摘要Abstract

现代 LLM 驱动的文本到语音 (TTS) 和语音转换 (VC) 系统生成的合成语音与许多传统欺骗基准中代表的生成器不同。这种不匹配会产生时间泛化差距，可能会高估现实世界后处理条件下检测器的鲁棒性。我们通过引入 VoxENES 2026 来弥补这一差距，这是一个双语（英语和西班牙语）基准，包含使用 10 种当代语音合成方法生成的 53,628 个音频样本，并在 10 种标准化后处理条件下进行评估。使用 VoxENES 2026，我们对八个预训练探测器进行了基准测试，无需进行微调，并观察到性能大幅下降：最佳模型总体 EER 为 28.98%，而大多数模型在现代生成器和扰动中的性能接近或低于随机机会。我们的结果强调了当前探测器对脆性伪影的依赖，并将 VoxENES 2026 确立为开发强大的音频欺骗对策的实用测试平台。Modern LLM-driven text-to-speech (TTS) and voice conversion (VC) systems produce synthetic speech that differs from the generators represented in many legacy spoofing benchmarks. This mismatch creates a temporal generalization gap that can overestimate detector robustness under real-world post-processing conditions. We bridge this gap by introducing VoxENES 2026, a bilingual (English and Spanish) benchmark of 53,628 audio samples generated using 10 contemporary speech synthesis methods and evaluated under 10 standardized post-processing conditions. Using VoxENES 2026, we benchmark eight pretrained detectors without fine-tuning and observe substantial performance degradation: the best model achieves 28.98% EER overall, while most perform near or below random chance across modern generators and perturbations. Our results highlight the reliance on brittle artifacts in current detectors and establish VoxENES 2026 as a practical testbed for developing robust audio spoofing countermeasures.

1 简介Introduction

强大的语音欺骗和深度伪造检测对于维护基于语音的身份验证的信任至关重要Robust speech spoofing and deepfake detection are essential to preserve trust in speech-based authentication[wang2025clearmask, guo2024wavepurifier, guo2023phantomsound, wang2023vsmask]。随着语音成为生物识别技术以及语音驱动代理和辅助技术的控制通道，这种需求正在增长。如果合成语音与真实语音无法区分，检测失败不仅会损害安全协议，还会削弱对音频证据的信任。. This need is growing as voice becomes a biometric and a control channel for speech-driven agents and assistive technologies. If synthetic speech becomes indistinguishable from genuine speech, detection failures not only compromise security protocols but also erode trust in audio evidence.

许多基准数据集被提出用于语音欺骗和深度伪造检测评估。例如，ASVspoof 挑战系列一直是欺骗对策开发的主要推动力。 ASV恶搞 2019Many benchmark datasets are proposed for speech spoofing and deepfake detection evaluation. 随着语音合成技术的改进，对暂时过时的基准进行评估可能会高估现实世界的鲁棒性。 LLM 驱动的 TTS 和 VC 系统产生的合成音频具有与早期欺骗语料库显着不同的声学特性，从而创建了Evaluation on temporally stale benchmarks can overestimate real-world robustness as speech synthesis techniques improve. LLM-driven TTS and VC systems produce synthetic audio with acoustic characteristics that differ substantially from earlier spoofing corpora, creating a数据漂移问题data drifting issue对于现有探测器。在旧基准上表现良好的 Deepfake 检测模型在针对较新的 Deepfake 生成器部署时可能会失败。这种不匹配反映了一种猫捉老鼠的动态，其中检测器学习与先前的合成伪影相关的线索，而生成器和后处理步骤逐渐抑制或隐藏这些线索。因此，法学硕士驱动的 TTS 和 VC 的快速发展推动了评估基准的更新。for existing detectors. Deepfake detection models that perform well on older benchmarks may fail when deployed against newer deepfake generators. This mismatch reflects a cat-and-mouse dynamic in which detectors learn cues tied to previous synthesis artifacts, while generators and post-processing steps progressively suppress or conceal those cues. As a consequence, the rapid LLM-driven TTS and VC evolution motivates updated evaluation benchmarks.

为了研究LLM时代数据漂移问题下的deepfake检测器的泛化能力，我们引入了现代双语基准数据集To study the generalization ability of deepfake detectors under the data drifting issue in the era of LLM, we introduce a modern bilingual benchmark dataset沃克斯尼斯 2026VoxENES 2026。我们通过 10 个 LLM 驱动的合成方法（7 个 TTS、3 个 VC）生成合成音频，涵盖英语和西班牙语音频。此外，现实世界的音频经常受到压缩、噪声和重采样等转换，这可能会严重影响检测器的行为. We generate synthetic audios from 10 LLM-driven synthesis methods (7 TTS, 3 VC), covering English and Spanish audios. In addition, real-world audios are frequently subjected to transformations such as compression, noise, and resampling, which can substantially affect detector behavior[yamagishi2021asvspoof, yan2024voicewukong, muller2024mlaad]。 因此，VoxENES 2026 通过应用一组标准化的音频增强方法来显式模拟后处理设置，这些方法模拟常见的传输和操作效果，包括编解码器压缩、加性噪声、重采样、速度扰动和响度归一化。. Therefore, VoxENES 2026 explicitly models the post-processing setting by applying a standardized set of audio augmentation methods that simulate common transmission and manipulation effects, including codec compression, additive noise, resampling, speed perturbation, and loudness normalization.

通过 VoxENES 2026，我们评估了八个预训练的 Deepfake 检测器，无需进行微调来测量分布外泛化。我们的结果表明，相对于传统基准测试报告的性能，现有检测器的性能大幅下降。最好的检测器总体 EER 仅为 28.98%，而大多数检测器的性能接近或低于随机机会。这些发现表明，许多当前的检测器依赖于脆弱的伪影线索，这些线索可能无法在新的 TTS 和 VC 代以及现实的后处理中推广。通过提供现代基准和受控的分布外评估，我们的工作建立了一个必要的测试平台，用于测量现实世界的语音欺骗检测器的鲁棒性，并推动检测器的开发，与不断发展的合成前沿保持同步。With VoxENES 2026, we evaluate eight pretrained deepfake detectors without fine-tuning to measure out-of-distribution generalization. Our results show that existing detectors suffer from substantial degradation relative to reported performance on legacy benchmarks. The best detector achieves only 28.98% EER overall, while most detectors perform near or below random chance. These findings suggest that many current detectors rely on brittle artifact cues that may not generalize across new TTS and VC generations and realistic post-processing. By providing a modern benchmark and a controlled out-of-distribution evaluation, our work establishes a necessary testbed for measuring real-world speech spoofing detector robustness and for driving detector development that keeps pace with the evolving synthesis frontier.

4.2 后处理的影响Impact of Post-Processing

5 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 VoxENES 2026，这是一个用于语音欺骗检测的现代双语基准，反映了 LLM 时代的 TTS 和 VC 生成以及真实的后处理。使用 VoxENES 2026，我们评估了 8 个未经微调的预训练检测器，并观察到了巨大的泛化差距：最佳模型总体 EER 仅为 28.98%，而许多检测器的表现接近或低于机会。 这些结果表明，当前的许多对策依赖于脆弱的、特定于基准测试的工件，并且对现代生成器和常规后处理仍然敏感。我们鼓励未来开展面向部署的欺骗检测工作，以提高数据漂移下的泛化能力，并改进持续跟踪快速发展的 TTS 和 VC 前沿的评估协议。We presented VoxENES 2026, a modern bilingual benchmark for speech spoofing detection that reflects LLM-era TTS and VC generation and realistic post-processing. Using VoxENES 2026, we evaluated eight pretrained detectors without fine-tuning and observed a substantial generalization gap: the best model achieves only 28.98% EER overall, while many detectors perform near or below chance. These results suggest that many current countermeasures rely on brittle, benchmark-specific artifacts and remain sensitive to modern generators and routine post-processing. We encourage future work on deployment-oriented spoofing detection that improves generalization under data drift and advances evaluation protocols that continuously track the fast-evolving TTS and VC frontier.
