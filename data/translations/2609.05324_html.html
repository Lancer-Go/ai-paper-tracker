摘要Abstract

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型在语言调节机器人操作方面取得了可喜的进展。然而，现有的数据集和基准主要评估预定义设置下的任务完成情况，在空间和程序复杂性不断增加的情况下对模型推理的洞察力有限。我们介绍Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have shown promising progress in languageconditioned robotic manipulation. However, existing datasets and benchmarks mainly evaluate task completion under predefined settings, offering limited insight into model reasoning under increasing spatial and procedural complexity. We introduce机器人SPARoboSPA ( 机器人Robo t S帕蒂亚-patial-P程序性的roceduralAssessment），一个大规模机器人操作数据集和诊断 VLA 模型中的体现推理的基准。 RoboSPA聚焦细粒度空间推理和长视野程序规划两个核心维度，涵盖10个任务类别、56个基础任务。每个任务都通过五个难度级别进行实例化，产生 280 个变体，空间模糊性和程序复杂性不断增加。我们收集了跨多个实施例和不同场景的 527K 轨迹。除了二进制成功率之外，RoboSPA 还引入了诊断指标以进行更详细的评估。对代表性 VLA 模型的实验表明，当前的系统仍然在复杂的空间关系、精确的低级执行和内存密集型规划方面遇到困难。这些结果将 RoboSPA 确立为具有挑战性的诊断基准，用于开发更强大、更可靠和更通用的实体代理。我们的数据和代码可以在https://github.com/fanzhenxuan/RoboSPA获取。ssessment), a large-scale robotic manipulation dataset and benchmark for diagnosing embodied reasoning in VLA models. RoboSPA focuses on two core dimensions, Fine-Grained Spatial Reasoning and Long-Horizon Procedural Planning, covering 10 task categories and 56 base tasks. Each task is instantiated across five difficulty levels, yielding 280 variants with increasing spatial ambiguity and procedural complexity. We collect 527K trajectories across multiple embodiments and diverse scenes. Beyond binary success rate, RoboSPA introduces diagnostic metrics for more detailed evaluation. Experiments on representative VLA models show that current systems still struggle with complex spatial relations, precise low-level execution, and memory-intensive planning. These results establish RoboSPA as a challenging diagnostic benchmark for developing more capable, reliable, and generalizable embodied agents. Our data and code are available at https: //github.com/fanzhenxuan/RoboSPA.

Each task supports step-level evaluation beyond final success rates. Across five embodiments, RoboSPA contains 527K trajectories and 997 hours of videos, forming a comprehensive benchmark for embodied reasoning under increasing task complexity.

我们在 RoboSPA 上评估了代表性的 VLA 模型（Liu 等人，2025a；AgiBot-World-Contributors 等人，2025；Black 等人，2025a；Zheng 等人，2026），发现它们随着任务复杂性的增加而陷入困境。在最困难的任务上，所有模型的平均成功率都低于 25%，有些任务甚至下降到 0%。诊断分析揭示了目标定位、低级操作、长视野跟踪和基于记忆的推理方面的失败，凸显 RoboSPA 作为具身推理的具有挑战性的诊断测试平台。We evaluate representative VLA models (Liu et al., 2025a; AgiBot-World-Contributors et al., 2025; Black et al., 2025a; Zheng et al., 2026) on RoboSPA and find that they struggle as task complexity increases. On the hardest tasks, all models achieve an average success rate below 25%, with some tasks dropping to 0%. Diagnostic analyses reveal failures in target grounding, low-level manipulation, long-horizon tracking, and memory-based reasoning, highlighting RoboSPA as a challenging diagnostic testbed for embodied reasoning.

机器人操作数据集和基准。Robotic Manipulation Datasets and Benchmarks.VLA模型的快速发展也加速了机器人操纵数据集和评估基准的开发。大规模数据集，包括 BridgeData V2（Walke 等人，2023）、Open X- Implementment（O’Neill 等人，2024）、DROID（Khazatsky 等人，2024）和 AgiBot World（AgiBotWorld-Contributors 等人，2025），扩大了现实世界的跨实体操作覆盖范围。 RLBench (James et al., 2020)、CALVIN (Mees et al., 2022)、ManiSkill2 (Gu et al., 2023) 和 LIBERO (Liu et al., 2023a) 等基准套件提供了用于评估不同技能、语言条件控制和顺序操作的结构化设置。最近的基准测试进一步检查了场景和能力泛化、模拟到真实的转换、双手操作和依赖于记忆的任务（Pumacay 等人，2024；Nasiriany 等人，2024；Li 等人，2025；Sedlacek 等人，2026；Garcia 等人，2025；Zhang 等人，2025c；Zhou 等人， 2025；Zhang 等人，2025a；Chen 等人，2026a；Chen 等人，2026b）。然而，他们很少通过步骤级诊断来系统地评估细粒度空间推理和长期程序规划。 RoboSPA 通过围绕这两种功能专门设计的任务来弥补这一差距。The rapid development of VLA models has also accelerated the development of robotic manipulation datasets and evaluation benchmarks. Large-scale datasets, including BridgeData V2 (Walke et al., 2023), Open X- Embodiment (O’Neill et al., 2024), DROID (Khazatsky et al., 2024), and AgiBot World (AgiBotWorld-Contributors et al., 2025), expand realworld, cross-embodiment manipulation coverage. Benchmark suites such as RLBench (James et al., 2020), CALVIN (Mees et al., 2022), ManiSkill2 (Gu et al., 2023), and LIBERO (Liu et al., 2023a) provide structured settings for evaluating diverse skills, language-conditioned control, and sequential manipulation. Recent benchmarks further examine scene and capability generalization, sim-to-real transfer, bimanual manipulation, and memory-dependent tasks (Pumacay et al., 2024; Nasiriany et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025; Sedlacek et al., 2026; Garcia et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025c; Zhou et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025a; Chen et al., 2026a; Cherepanov et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2026b). However, they rarely systematically evaluate fine-grained spatial reasoning and long-horizon procedural planning with step-level diagnostics. RoboSPA addresses this gap with tasks specifically designed around these two capabilities.

3.2.1 能力分类3.2.1 Capability Taxonomy

为了系统地评估 VLA 模型中的体现推理，我们构建了分层能力分类法，而不是将操作任务视为孤立的实例。如图2（a）所示，它包括两个核心维度：细粒度空间推理和长视野程序规划。To systematically evaluate embodied reasoning in VLA models, we build a hierarchical capability taxonomy instead of treating manipulation tasks as isolated instances. As shown in Fig. 2(a), it includes two core dimensions: Fine-Grained Spatial Reasoning and Long-Horizon Procedural Planning.

细粒度空间推理。Fine-Grained Spatial Reasoning.该维度评估复杂空间配置中的教学基础。任务需要在复杂场景中通过细粒度的空间推理来选择正确的目标。它包括五类：This dimension assesses instruction grounding in complex spatial configurations. Tasks require selecting the correct target through fine-grained spatial reasoning in complex scenes. It includes five categories:

  • 几何属性认知（GAC）：Geometric Attribute Cognition (GAC):评估模型是否可以通过超出类别级别识别的几何或形状相关属性来识别和操作对象。Evaluates whether the model can identify and manipulate objects by geometric or shape-related attributes beyond category-level recognition.

  • 空间距离估计（SDE）：Spatial Distance Estimation (SDE):衡量模型是否可以比较之间的距离Measures whether the model can compare distances among

对象或参考实体，推理邻近度、远程度和相对距离。objects or to a reference entity, reasoning about proximity, remoteness, and relative distance.

  • 规范位置索引 (CPI)：Canonical Position Indexing (CPI):评估模型在不同计数方向和空间扫描顺序下是否能够通过行列索引来识别对象。Evaluates whether the model can identify objects by rowcolumn indices under different counting directions and spatial scanning orders.

  • 参照关系推理（RRR）：Referential Relational Reasoning (RRR):评估模型是否可以通过与参考对象的方向关系来定位目标，涵盖基本位置和组合位置。Assesses whether the model can locate targets through directional relations to reference objects, covering basic and compositional positions.

  • 交叉视图推理（CVR）：Cross-View Reasoning (CVR):通过跨视角转换空间参考，检查模型是否可以从非自我中心的角度解释空间指令。Examines whether the model can interpret spatial instructions from non-egocentric viewpoints by transforming spatial references across perspectives.

长期程序规划。Long-Horizon Procedural Planning.该维度评估模型完成具有不同动作、多个子目标、状态和程序约束的扩展操作任务的能力。它还包括五个类别：This dimension evaluates a model’s ability to complete extended manipulation tasks with diverse actions, multiple subgoals, states, and procedural constraints. It also consists of five categories:

  • 重复程序如下 (RPF)：Repetitive Procedure Following (RPF):评估模型是否可以重复指定操作所需的次数，同时跟踪进度并正确停止。Assesses whether the model can repeat a specified operation the required number of times while tracking progress and stopping correctly.

  • 无订单执行（OFE）：Order-Free Execution (OFE):衡量模型是否能够以灵活的顺序完成多个子目标，覆盖所有目标，没有遗漏或不必要的重复。Measures whether the model can complete multiple subgoals in flexible order, covering all targets without omission or unnecessary repetition.

5 结论5 Conclusion

我们推出 RoboSPA，一个以推理为中心的大规模机器人操作数据集和基准。 RoboSPA以细粒度空间推理和长视野程序规划为核心，涵盖十个任务类别、五个难度级别、多种实施方式和多样化场景，具有细粒度的空间基础和子任务完成指标。实验表明，现有的 VLA 模型仍然难以应对复杂的空间关系、精确的执行和内存密集型规划，尤其是在较高难度级别上。我们希望RoboSPA能够推动开发出更有能力、更可靠的实体代理。We present RoboSPA, a reasoning-focused, largescale robotic manipulation dataset and benchmark. Centered on Fine-Grained Spatial Reasoning and Long-Horizon Procedural Planning, RoboSPA covers ten task categories, five difficulty levels, multiple embodiments, and diverse scenes, with finegrained metrics for spatial grounding and subtask completion. Experiments show that existing VLA models still struggle with complex spatial relations, precise execution, and memory-intensive planning, especially at higher difficulty levels. We hope RoboSPA can advance the development of more capable and reliable embodied agents.

局限性Limitations

尽管我们的数据集涵盖了各种细粒度空间推理和长期程序规划任务，但仍然存在一些局限性。首先，所有任务都是在模拟中构建的，模拟与现实之间的差距可能会限制直接转移到物理机器人。其次，我们的基准测试侧重于桌面操作，这可能无法完全捕捉现实世界环境的多样性和开放性。第三，虽然我们包括多个机器人实施例和域随机场景，但更广泛的设置（例如可变形对象操作、人机交互和开放式任务指令）仍未得到充分探索。Although our dataset covers diverse fine-grained spatial reasoning and long-horizon procedural planning tasks, several limitations remain. First, all tasks are constructed in simulation, and the sim-toreal gap may limit direct transfer to physical robots. Second, our benchmark focuses on tabletop manipulation, which may not fully capture the diversity and open-endedness of real-world environments. Third, while we include multiple robotic embodiments and domain-randomized scenes, broader settings such as deformable object manipulation, human-robot interaction, and open-ended task instructions remain underexplored.

For fine-grained spatial reasoning tasks, we define three error types to distinguish target selection failures from execution failures:

  • 目标执行错误：Target Execution Error:模型选择了正确的目标，但无法正确执行抓取或拾取动作。The model selects the correct target but fails to execute the grasp or pick action properly.

培训。Training.对于每个基本任务，我们在每个难度级别使用 50 条轨迹，从而在五个级别中产生 250 条轨迹。对于每个任务变体，我们生成 60 个模板，其中 50 个用于训练，10 个用于评估。这些模板使用特定于任务的对象描述进行实例化，以形成 100 个不同的训练指令和 100 个不重叠的评估指令。在训练期间，从每个轨迹的相应任务变体指令池中随机采样一条指令。For each base task, we use 50 trajectories per difficulty level, resulting in 250 trajectories across five levels. For each task variant, we generate 60 templates, with 50 used for training and 10 held out for evaluation. These templates are instantiated with task-specific object descriptions to form 100 distinct training instructions and 100 non-overlapping evaluation instructions. During training, one instruction is randomly sampled from the corresponding task-variant instruction pool for each trajectory.
