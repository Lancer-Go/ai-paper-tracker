使用公理进行规划的逻辑回归Logical Regression for Planning with Axioms

Connor Little, Christian Muise
摘要Abstract

在自动规划中，逻辑回归是一种返回操作实现特定公式所需的最一般条件的操作。它有许多应用，例如允许更强大的计划执行以及为非确定性计划提供紧凑的策略。虽然在基本规划设置中计算相对简单，但当存在其他因素（例如公理）时，逻辑回归会变得更加复杂。我们引入了一种方法来近似包含公理的域中的动作的逻辑回归；将条件限制为部分状态的近似值。我们的方法产生最小的部分状态，同时避免重新计算公理。为了证明我们的方法的影响，我们将回归形式嵌入到执行监控上下文中，这是一个完善的设置，可以从逻辑回归中受益匪浅。我们的结果表明，这种形式的回归可以显着概括跨多个域的部分状态，将执行监控所考虑的变量数量减少多达 70%，并证明生成的执行监控器足够强大，可以在发生意外变化的环境中频繁恢复：在我们的测试中，多个域的恢复时间超过 50%。In automated planning, logical regression is an operation that returns the most general condition necessary for an action to achieve a particular formula. It has many applications, such as allowing for more robust plan execution and providing compact policies for non-deterministic planning. Although relatively simple to calculate in basic planning settings, logical regression becomes significantly more complex when additional factors, such as axioms, are present. We introduce a methodology for approximating the logical regression of an action in a domain that includes axioms; an approximation that limits conditions to partial states. Our method produces minimal partial states while avoiding the recalculation of axioms. To demonstrate the impact of our methods, we embed our form of regression in an execution monitoring context, a well-established setting that can benefit greatly from logical regression. Our results show that this form of regression can dramatically generalize partial states across multiple domains, reducing the number of variables considered for execution monitoring by up to 70%, and demonstrate that the resulting execution monitor is robust enough to recover frequently in an environment with unexpected changes: several domains recover over 50% of the time in our tests.

1 简介Introduction

在经典规划中，规划者从完整的初始状态和一个或多个目标条件开始。通过查找从初始状态到目标状态的一系列操作来创建计划。规划器的目标是在给定域描述的情况下生成规划。人们提出了许多强大的语言功能和技术来帮助提高规划者的能力。 这样的语言特征之一是公理的使用）。公理是定义派生变量为真的条件的规则。这将这些变量与基本变量区分开来，基本变量是通过应用运算符来赋值的。公理提供了多种好处，例如允许更自然地表达现象，以及无法有效编译的复杂性的跳跃In classical planning, a planner begins with a complete initial state and one or more goal conditions. A plan is created by finding a sequence of actions from the initial state to a goal state. The aim of a planner is to generate a plan given a description of a domain. Many powerful language features and techniques have been proposed to help improve planner capabilities. One such language feature is the use of axioms). Axioms are rules that define the conditions in which a derived variable would be true. This distinguishes these variables from basic variables, which are assigned values through the application of operators. Axioms offer multiple benefits, such as allowing for more natural expressions of phenomena, and a jump in complexity that cannot be efficiently compiled away(Thiébaux_Hoffmann_Nebel_2005)。当通过最佳优先搜索算法进行探索时，公理有利于更小的搜索空间，并且可以递归计算。尽管如此，许多规划者并不支持它们，因为它们会带来复杂性和额外的实施工作。. Axioms facilitate a smaller search space when exploring through best-first search algorithms and can be computed recursively. Despite this, many planners do not support them due to the complexity and additional implementation work they would entail.

虽然公理的表达能力已经在计划生成的背景下得到了探索，但自动化规划中的其他相关设置却很少受到关注。我们特别有兴趣将这种表达能力While the expressive power of axioms has been explored in the context of plan generation, other related settings in automated planning have had far less focus. We are specifically interested in bringing this expressive power to逻辑回归logical regression，一种通常用于广义执行监控的技术, a technique typically used for generalized execution monitoring(Fritz and McIlraith 2007)或更丰富的规划设置or richer planning settings(Muise et al. 2024)。逻辑回归是确保操作实现给定公式的最一般条件的计算。结合回归允许一些规划器，特别是非确定性规划器，例如 PR2，通过允许在多个状态上调用操作符来变得更加通用和更加健壮。当执行计划的环境不可预测时，这会很有用。如果在执行期间状态发生意外变化，代理必须决定如何恢复。在这些情况下，完全按照给定的计划执行可能会导致意外状态并不可避免地失败。. Logical regression is the calculation of the most general condition that ensures that an action achieves a given formula. Incorporating regression allows some planners, notably non-deterministic planners such as PR2, to become more generalized and more robust by permitting operators to be called on more than a single state. This can be useful when the environment in which the plan will be executed is unpredictable. If, during execution, the state changes unexpectedly, the agent must decide how to recover. Under these conditions, executing the plan exactly as given can lead to unexpected states and inevitably fail.

在许多情况下，完整回归公式的计算是复杂且昂贵的。因此，我们引入了该回归的近似值。完全回归提供了一个双条件公式：当且仅当该公式对某个操作的回归由该状态引发时，该公式将在执行某个操作后保持给定的状态。近似的限制较少。它只是说明如果应用该运算符，该公式将成立。我们考虑的近似形式是将回归限制为仅由部分状态表示。Calculation of the complete regression formula is complex and expensive in many cases. As such, we introduce an approximation of this regression. The complete regression provides a formula that is biconditional: A formula will hold given a state after the execution of an action if and only if the regression of that formula over an action is entailed by that state. The approximation is less restrictive. It merely states that the formula will hold if the operator is applied. The form of approximation we consider is to restrict the regression to only be represented by partial states.

我们引入了一种新颖的方法，可以在存在公理的情况下有效地计算动作的近似逻辑回归。我们以部分状态的形式来研究回归的特殊情况，因为它维护了重复回归的计算有效表示。我们的算法提供了三种不同的方法来计算不同粒度级别的近似值，从简单的方法到基于搜索的方法。此外，我们在附录中研究了一种详尽的方法和完整的回归。We introduce a novel method to efficiently compute an approximate logical regression over actions in the presence of axioms. We look at a specialized case of regression in the form of partial states, as it maintains a computationally efficient representation for repeated regression. Our algorithm offers three different approaches for calculating the approximation with varying levels of granularity, from naive to a search based method. Additionally, we look into an exhaustive method and with full regression in the Appendix.

为了探索其带来的好处，我们将这些方法应用于执行监控框架。我们发现，在生成逻辑回归的近似值时，我们能够大大减少在存在公理的情况下执行计划所需的已定义基本变量的数量。该算法能够泛化所有领域的计划，其中一些领域能够泛化计划执行期间超过 70% 的状态。我们希望证明执行监视器可以在许多场景中恢复而无需重新规划。总的来说，我们提供了三种不同的方法来在存在公理的情况下逼近回归，并探索每种方法的优点。To explore the benefits that this provides, we apply these methods to an execution monitoring framework. We found that in generating approximations of logical regression, we are able to greatly reduce the number of defined basic variables required to execute a plan in the presence of axioms. The algorithm was able to generalize plans across all domains, with some domains being able to generalize states over 70% during a plan’s execution. We look to demonstrate that the execution monitor can recover in many scenarios without replanning. Overall, we offer three different approaches to approximating regression under the presence of axioms and explore the benefits of each method.

Therefore, by only checking a specific subset of axioms through a subset of basic variables in the context, we are not able to conclusively say that a derived variable is false. The previous two methods are able to handle negated derived variables as every axiom is considered.

7 结论Conclusion

公理是一个强大的工具，可以提供许多好处并可以简化复杂的现象Axioms are a powerful tool that offer many benefits and can simplify complex phenomena(Thiébaux_Hoffmann_Nebel_2005)。尽管它们很实用，但许多规划的扩展和应用并没有利用它们。我们着手通过引入域中公理的动作回归近似来改进这一点。我们已经为带有公理的完全可观察的确定性域实现了近似回归算法。. Despite their utility, many extensions and applications of planning do not take advantage of them. We set out to improve this by introducing an approximation for regression over actions with axioms in the domain. We have implemented an approximate regression algorithm for fully observable deterministic domains with axioms.

创建了该方法的三个不同版本：朴素方法，保留所有基本变量以确保公理计算的执行与用于近似回归的状态完全相同；相关变量方法，仅保留特定公理所需的状态；后处理方法，选择一组可以实现所需结果的特定公理。此外，附录还包含其他潜在的方法。Three different versions of this method were created: a naive method which keeps all basic variables to ensure that axiom calculation is performed exactly the same as the state used to approximate regression, a relevant variables method which only keeps the states required for specific axioms, and a post processing method which chooses a specific set of axioms which can achieve the desired result. Additionally, the appendix contains other potential methods.

使用我们新回归的部分状态，我们引入了一个执行监控框架。这通常可以避免在出现问题时重新计划，从而在计划执行期间实现稳健的行为。我们还用它来测试回归方法的统计特性。Using our newly regressed partial states, we introduce an execution monitoring framework. This enables robust behaviour during plan execution by often avoiding replanning if something goes awry. We also use it to test statistical properties of our regression methodology.

在八个领域，我们已经确定了许多关键见解。首先，在当前的基准测试中，仅对目标状态应用后处理会产生对计划的近似回归，这与在每个步骤中应用后处理一样好。如果情况并非如此，则可能会出现问题，这引发了这样的问题：这是否是现实世界领域的属性，或者现有基准测试的范围是否过于有限。在所有领域中，大约一半的时间，执行监控系统都避免了重新规划。这使得规划应用程序可以节省计算时间。此外，我们的方法能够极大地概括所有领域的计划，领域之间基本变量数量的差异会产生很大的影响。Across eight domains, we have identified many key insights. The first is that in current benchmarks, only applying post-processing to the goal state produces an approximate regression over the plan that is just as good as applying post-processing at every step. Problems may be constructed where this is not the case, which raises the question of whether this is a property of real-world domains or if existing benchmarks are simply too limited in scope.
