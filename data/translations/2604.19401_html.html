摘要Abstract

知识图嵌入（KGE）支持知识图（KG）上的各种下游任务。在实践中，知识图谱随着新实体和事实的添加而不断发展，从而激发了持续知识图嵌入（CKGE）方法，随着时间的推移更新嵌入。当前的 CKGE 方法主要通过限制对现有嵌入的更改来解决灾难性遗忘（即先前学习任务的性能下降）。Knowledge Graph Embeddings (KGEs) support a wide range of downstream tasks over Knowledge Graphs (KGs). In practice, KGs evolve as new entities and facts are added, motivating Continual Knowledge Graph Embedding (CKGE) methods that update embeddings over time. Current CKGE approaches address catastrophic forgetting (i.e., the performance degradation on previously learned tasks) primarily by limiting changes to existing embeddings.

然而，我们表明这种观点是不完整的。当引入新实体时，它们的嵌入可能会干扰之前学习的实体，导致模型预测它们来代替之前正确的答案。这种现象，我们称之为However, we show that this view is incomplete. When new entities are introduced, their embeddings can interfere with previously learned ones, causing the model to predict them in place of previously correct answers. This phenomenon, which we call实体干扰entity interference，在很大程度上被忽视了，并且在当前的 CKGE 评估协议中没有得到考虑。结果，对灾难性遗忘的评估变得具有误导性，并且 CKGE 方法的性能被系统性高估。, has been largely overlooked and is not accounted for in current CKGE evaluation protocols. As a result, the assessment of catastrophic forgetting becomes misleading, and CKGE methods performance is systematically overestimated.

为了解决这个问题，我们引入了一个修正的 CKGE 评估协议，该协议考虑了实体干扰。通过对多个基准的实验，我们表明，忽略这种影响可能会导致性能高估高达 25%，特别是在实体显着增长的情况下。我们进一步分析了不同的 CKGE 方法和 KGE 模型如何受到不同遗忘来源的影响，并引入了针对 CKGE 量身定制的灾难性遗忘度量。To address this issue, we introduce a corrected CKGE evaluation protocol that accounts for entity interference. Through experiments on multiple benchmarks, we show that ignoring this effect can lead to performance overestimation of up to 25%, particularly in scenarios with significant entity growth. We further analyze how different CKGE methods and KGE models are affected by the different sources of forgetting, and introduce a catastrophic forgetting metric tailored to CKGE.

1 简介Introduction

知识图嵌入（KGE）将知识图（KG）的实体和关系表示为连续空间中的向量。通过捕获图的底层结构和语义属性，KGE 可以在 KG 上实现广泛的下游任务。然而，现实世界的知识图谱是动态的，并且通过添加新的实体、关系和事实而不断发展Knowledge Graph Embeddings (KGEs) represent the entities and relations of a Knowledge Graph (KG) as vectors in a continuous space. By capturing the underlying structural and semantic properties of the graph, KGEs enable a wide range of downstream tasks over KGs. However, real-world KGs are dynamic and continuously evolve over time through the addition of new entities, relations, and facts[polleres_hal-04373234]而且，随着图的发展，嵌入很快就会过时。因此，必须通过学习新实体和关系的嵌入以及更新现有嵌入以纳入新知识来更新嵌入以反映这些变化。在这种不断变化的条件下有效维持高质量的嵌入仍然是一个关键挑战。and, as the graph evolves, embeddings quickly become outdated. Consequently, embeddings must be updated to reflect these changes, both by learning embeddings for new entities and relations and by updating existing embeddings to incorporate new knowledge. Efficiently maintaining high-quality embeddings under such evolving conditions remains a key challenge.

持续知识图嵌入（CKGE）领域最近受到关注The Continual Knowledge Graph Embedding (CKGE) field has recently gained traction[LKGE, incDE, fastKGE, FMR, CMKGE, ETT-CKGE, SAGE, STCKGE, LGD, DebiasedKGE, DyGM]，建立在持续学习技术的基础上, building upon continual learning techniques[continualSurvey]并将它们扩展到 KGE 设置。现有的 CKGE 方法的设计目标是能够获取新知识，同时减少灾难性遗忘，灾难性遗忘是指在使用新数据进行训练时先前学习的任务的性能下降。然而，当前的 CKGE 评估协议在评估现有嵌入时不考虑新引入的实体。因此，无法捕获这些实体引起的潜在预测错误，从而导致对 CKGE 性能的高估。and extending them to the KGE setting. Existing CKGE approaches are designed with the goal of enabling the acquisition of new knowledge while mitigating catastrophic forgetting, which refers to the degradation of performance on previously learned tasks when training with new data. However, current CKGE evaluation protocols do not consider newly introduced entities when evaluating existing embeddings. As a result, potential prediction errors caused by these entities are not captured, leading to an overestimation of CKGE performance.

Entity interference arises from a fundamental characteristic of CKGE: the need to incorporate new entity embeddings resulting from the evolution of the underlying KG. 2 KGE的持续学习Continual Learning of KGE

在标准机器学习中，模型通常是在静态数据集的假设下进行训练的。然而，许多现实世界的应用程序在动态环境中运行，其中数据随着时间的推移按顺序到达。持续学习通过研究模型如何逐步获取和提炼知识，同时限制先前学到的信息的退化（称为灾难性遗忘）来解决这一问题。In standard machine learning, models are typically trained under the assumption of a static dataset. However, many real-world applications operate in dynamic environments where data arrive sequentially over time. Continual learning addresses this setting by studying how models can incrementally acquire and refine knowledge while limiting the degradation of previously learned information, known as catastrophic forgetting.

这种动态设置也存在于 KG 中，因为它们随着时间的推移通过添加新的实体、关系和事实而演变。因此，通过机器学习模型学习的 KGE 也必须不断更新，以保留和完善现有知识，同时整合新信息。在以下小节中，我们介绍 CKGE 的预备知识，并将问题定位在已建立的持续学习场景中。This dynamic setting is also found in KGs, as they evolve over time through the addition of new entities, relations, and facts. Consequently, their KGEs, which are learned via a machine learning model, must also undergo continual updates to preserve and refine existing knowledge while integrating new information. In the following subsections, we introduce the preliminaries of CKGE and position the problem within the established continual learning scenarios.

7 结论Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们对 CKGE 中的灾难性遗忘进行了深入分析，证明现有观点是不完整的，因为它侧重于先前学习的嵌入的表示漂移，而忽略了新引入实体的影响。我们确定In this work, we present an in-depth analysis of catastrophic forgetting in CKGE, demonstrating that the existing view is incomplete, as it focuses on representation drift of previously learned embeddings while overlooking the impact of newly introduced entities. We identify实体干扰entity interference作为遗忘的第二个来源，证明一旦新实体合并到嵌入空间中，即使完美保存旧实体的嵌入也会产生降级的预测。as a second source of forgetting, demonstrating that even perfectly preserved embeddings of old entities can yield degraded predictions once new entities are incorporated to the embedding space.

基于这些观察，我们表明标准 CKGE 评估协议通过将候选实体限制为学习每个快照时存在的实体来系统地排除实体干扰。通过对广泛使用的基准进行大量实验，我们证明这会导致对 CKGE 性能的高估，从而导致有关现有 CKGE 方法有效性的误导性结论。在评估灾难性遗忘时，这个问题尤其重要，因为一旦适当考虑新实体的干扰，在标准评估下可能显得稳健的方法实际上会遭受性能下降。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一个在 CKGE 设置中正确评估灾难性遗忘的指标，并分析了不同遗忘来源的贡献。我们的结果表明，在实体数量大幅增长的情况下，实体干扰是遗忘的主要来源，而当实体增长有限时，表示漂移仍然是主要因素。此外，对完整知识图谱进行再训练始终会产生对干扰更鲁棒的嵌入，突显了当前 CKGE 方法的学习过程与它们旨在近似的理想持续学习行为之间的差距。Based on these observations, we showed that the standard CKGE evaluation protocol systematically excludes entity interference by restricting candidate entities to those present at the time each snapshot is learned. Through extensive experiments on widely used benchmarks, we demonstrate that this leads to an overestimation of CKGE performance and, consequently, to misleading conclusions about the effectiveness of existing CKGE methods. This issue is particularly critical when assessing catastrophic forgetting, as approaches that may appear robust under the standard evaluation in reality suffer from performance degradation once interference from new entities is properly considered. To address this, we propose a metric for properly assessing catastrophic forgetting in the CKGE setting, and analyze the contribution of the different sources of forgetting. Our results reveal that entity interference is the dominant source of forgetting in scenarios with substantial growth in the number of entities, whereas representation drift remains the primary factor when entity growth is limited. Moreover, retraining on the full KG consistently yields embeddings that are more robust to interference, highlighting a gap between the learning process of current CKGE methods and the ideal continual learning behavior they aim to approximate.

最后，我们分析了不同 KGE 模型在持续学习环境中的行为，发现翻译模型在两种遗忘来源下都更加稳定，而语义模型以经历更高水平的遗忘为代价更好地适应新知识。Finally, we analyze the behavior of different KGE models in the continual learning setting, finding that translational models are more stable under both sources of forgetting, while semantic models adapt better to new knowledge in the expense of experiencing higher levels of forgetting.

总的来说，我们的研究结果强调需要重新审视 CKGE 中的评估协议和灾难性遗忘缓解策略。特别是，未来的工作应该明确考虑实体干扰，例如通过设计对干扰更鲁棒的学习目标或架构，或者探索在推理过程中处理不断增长的候选实体集的替代方法。我们相信，所提出的评估协议和遗忘度量将实现更可靠的评估和比较，支持 CKGE 方法开发的进一步进展。Overall, our findings highlight the need to revisit both evaluation protocols and catastrophic forgetting mitigation strategies in CKGE. In particular, future work should explicitly account for entity interference, for example by designing learning objectives or architectures that are more robust to interference, or by exploring alternative ways to handling the growing candidate entity set during inference. We believe that the proposed evaluation protocol and forgetting metric will enable more reliable assessment and comparison, supporting further progress in the development of CKGE methods.
