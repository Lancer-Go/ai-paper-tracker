学习自我修改和巩固记忆Learning to Self-Modify and Consolidate Memories

摘要Abstract

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）的发展标志着机器学习研究的一个关键里程碑：从特定任务模型到具有各种新兴功能的更通用系统的范式转变The development of Large Language Models (LLMs) marks a pivotal milestone in machine learning research: a paradigm shift from task-specific models to more general-purpose systems with various emergent capabilities[brown2020language, schaeffer2023emergent]。尽管法学硕士在不同的任务中具有卓越的能力. Despite LLMs’ remarkable capabilities in diverse sets of tasks[wang2023visionllm, nijkamp2023codegen, comanici2025gemini]，它们在初始部署后基本上是静态的，这意味着它们成功地执行了在训练前或训练后学到的任务，但无法, they are largely static after their initial deployment, meaning that they successfully perform tasks learned during pre- or post-training, but are unable to不断获得continually acquire超出其直接环境的新功能。这种固有的静态性质造成了一个严重的漏洞：模型的知识和技能逐渐变得陈旧，以固定的“知识截止”日期运行，超过该日期它就无法意识到新的事实、事件和不断变化的信息new capabilities beyond their immediate context. This inherent static nature creates a crucial vulnerability: The model’s knowledge and skills become progressively stale, operating with a fixed "knowledge cutoff" date beyond which it is unaware of new facts, events, and evolving information[cheng2024dated].

克服这一限制的努力主要集中在：（1）对扩展数据集进行重新预训练，尽管其有效，但计算成本昂贵且对于频繁更新来说不切实际Efforts to overcome this limitation have primarily focused on: (1) re-pretraining on an expanded dataset, which despite its effectiveness, is computationally expensive and impractical for frequent updates[ibrahim2024simple]; (2) 使用昂贵的连续参数更新或其他轻量级替代方案，例如微调或低秩适应; (2) using expensive continual parameter updates or other lightweight alternatives, such as fine-tuning or low-rank adaption[hu2022lora, akyureksurprising]，迭代更新通常会导致灾难性遗忘（CF）, which with iterative updates often results in Catastrophic Forgetting (CF)[kemker2018measuring, shi2024continual]– 一个众所周知的现象，模型在学习新任务时对原始任务的熟练程度会急剧下降。这种两难境地——一方面是知识过时和灾难性遗忘，另一方面是高昂的成本或更新的破坏性——强调了一个关键的、尚未解决的挑战：使法学硕士能够在整个生命周期中增量和高效地学习。–a well-known phenomenon where the model’s proficiency on original tasks degrades catastrophically as it learns new ones. This dilemma—between knowledge obsolescence on one hand and catastrophic forgetting as well as the prohibitive cost or destructive nature of updates on the other—underscores a critical, unresolved challenge: enabling LLMs to learn incrementally and efficiently throughout their lifecycle.

近年来，情境学习（ICL）In recent years, In-Context Learning (ICL)[brown2020language]作为一种高效且成功的持续学习形式而受到关注has gained attention as a highly efficient and successful form of continual learning[akyurek2022learning, dong2024survey, akyurek2024context, li2025longcontext]。最初，ICL 被认为是法学硕士的一项新兴能力，它接受过大规模数据的训练，使他们能够快速适应环境，从而执行零次或几次任务. Initially, ICL was known as an emergent ability of LLMs that is trained on large scale data, enabling them to adapt fast to the context and so perform zero- or few-shot tasks[brown2020language]。后来，更多的研究揭示并形式化了 ICL 作为元学习过程的作用，其中模型沿着序列执行内部计算，通过将上下文知识保留或压缩到短期记忆中，将其合并到其输出中. Later, more studies revealed and formalized the role of ICL as a meta-learning process in which the model performs internal computations along the sequence to incorporate context knowledge to its output by keeping or compress it into a short-term memory[behrouz2025nested, dherin2025learning]。尽管 ICL 作为一种持续学习形式具有有效性/效率，但它仅限于序列模型的上下文窗口，这意味着任何新获得的知识都将在会话/上下文结束时从模型中删除。这种观点提出了一个关键问题：. Despite the effectiveness/efficiency of ICL as a form of continual learning, it is limited to the context-window of sequence models, meaning that any new acquired knowledge will be removed from the model at the end of the session/context. This perspective raises a critical question:模型如何有效地将脆弱的短期记忆转化为更稳定的长期知识How the model can effectively transfer the fragile short-term memories into more stable long-term knowledge?

Such conditions can limit the person’s knowledge to immediate present that fits in the short-term memory and long past, before the onset of the disorder, resulting in continuously experiencing the immediate present as if it were always new. One might notice a similar pattern in the memory processing of current Transformer-based LLMs. The knowledge of LLMs are limited in either: (1) the immediate context that fits into their context window (a.k.a. in-context learning), or (2) MLP and projection layers, storing long-past, before the onset of “end of pre-training.” This similarity in pattern motivates us to ask,人类学习过程中巩固记忆的关键组成部分是什么？What is the critical component in human learning process that consolidates memories?

人脑在巩固记忆方面效率很高，虽然有损耗，但很有效，这通常归因于神经可塑性——大脑根据新的经历、记忆和学习而改变自身的能力The human brain is highly efficient, lossy but effective when it comes to consolidating memory, which is often attributed to neuroplasticity—the brain’s ability to change itself in response to new experiences, memories, and learning[pascual2005plastic, johnston2009plasticity]。最近的研究表明，长期记忆的形成至少涉及两个不同但互补的巩固过程. Recent studies support that long-term memory formation involves at least two distinct but complementary consolidation processes[Stepwise-consolidation2021, frey1997synaptic, yang2024selection]：（1）学习后立即或不久就会发生快速的“在线”巩固阶段，甚至在清醒时也是如此。这是新的脆弱记忆痕迹稳定下来并开始从短期存储转移到更长期存储的时候； (2)“离线”巩固（也称为系统巩固）过程在睡眠期间重复重放最近编码的模式，并重新组织记忆并支持转移到皮质部位: (1) A rapid “online” consolidation phase occurs immediately or soon after learning, even during wakefulness. This is when new fragile memory traces are stabilized and begin transferring from short-term to more longer-term storage; (2) An “offline” consolidation (also known as systems consolidation) process repeats the replay of the recently encoded patterns during sleep and reorganizes the memory and supports transfer to cortical sites[ji2007coordinated, peyrache2009replay, foster2006reverse].

睡眠在人类学习过程中的作用The Role of Sleep in Human Learning Process

睡眠不是一种被动状态，而是对认知功能至关重要的大脑活动的动态且高度结构化的时期Sleep is not a passive state but a dynamic and highly structured period of brain activity essential for cognitive function[rasch2013sleep, goldstein2014role]。在睡眠期间，大脑协调对学习、神经可塑性、自我完善和记忆巩固至关重要的复杂过程. During sleep, the brain orchestrates complex processes fundamental to learning, neural plasticity, self-improvement, and memory consolidation[wamsley2011memory, rasch2013sleep, goldstein2014role]。在人类中，这些过程主要由两个关键且交替的睡眠阶段控制：快速眼动睡眠 (REM) 和非快速眼动睡眠 (NREM)。. In humans, these processes are primarily governed by two critical and alternating stages of sleep: Rapid Eye Movement (REM) and Non-REM (NREM) sleep.

非快速眼动睡眠（慢波睡眠）：Non-Rapid Eye Movement Sleep (Slow-Wave Sleep):这个阶段，特别是被称为慢波睡眠的最深阶段，其特点是同步、高幅度、低频神经活动。慢波睡眠与对学习至关重要的两个主要功能相关：第一个是突触稳态，这是一个全局性降低突触强度的过程，以抵消清醒经历带来的连通性净增加，从而维持代谢平衡并防止神经饱和This stage, particularly its deepest phase known as slow-wave sleep, is characterized by synchronized, high-amplitude, low-frequency neural activity. Slow-wave sleep is associated with two primary functions crucial for learning: The first is synaptic homeostasis, a process that globally downscales synaptic strengths to counteract the net increase in connectivity from waking experiences, thereby maintaining metabolic balance and preventing neural saturation[tononi2006sleep].

第二个核心功能是记忆巩固，将脆弱的近期经验转化为稳定的长期知识The second core function is memory consolidation, the transformation of fragile, recent experiences into stable, long-term knowledge[squire1995retrograde]。这个过程是通过海马体和新皮质之间复杂的对话精心策划的. This process is orchestrated through a sophisticated dialogue between the hippocampus and the neocortex[squire2015memory]。海马体作为一个高保真临时存储系统，能够快速编码特定的日常经历。相比之下，新皮质是一个巨大的长期存储库，更适合从这些经验中逐步学习通用规则和语义知识. The hippocampus serves as a high-fidelity temporary storage system, capable of rapidly encoding specific daily experiences. In contrast, the neocortex is a vast, long-term repository better suited for the gradual learning of generalized rules and semantic knowledge from these experiences[squire1995retrograde, squire2015memory]。在慢波睡眠期间，大脑会在这些结构之间发起夜间对话，从而促进复杂的信息传递。值得注意的是，这种传输并不是简单地重放原始数据；而是简单地重放原始数据。相反，它重新构建了醒着时获得的知识，提取抽象概念并将其整合到一个有凝聚力的语义网络中。. During slow-wave sleep, the brain initiates a nightly dialogue between these structures that facilitates an intricate transfer of information. Notably, this transfer does not simply replay raw data; instead, it re-architects the knowledge acquired during waking hours, extracting abstractions and integrating them into a cohesive semantic network.

快速眼动睡眠：Rapid Eye Movement Sleep:快速眼动睡眠的特点是类似于清醒状态的高频、低幅度脑电波，通常与做梦有关。从功能上讲，这个阶段与新形成的突触的选择性强化以及新信息与预先存在的情感和语义网络的整合有关。此外，假设它在模拟未来场景以改善适应性行为方面发挥作用。Characterized by high-frequency, low-amplitude brain waves that resemble an awake state, REM sleep is most commonly associated with dreaming. Functionally, this stage is linked to the selective strengthening of newly formed synapses and the integration of new information with pre-existing emotional and semantic networks. Furthermore, it is hypothesized to play a role in simulating future scenarios to improve adaptive behavior.

总之，整个晚上 NREM 和 REM 阶段之间的周期性交替至关重要。 NREM 睡眠似乎可以巩固和修剪当天的经历，以建立更有效的知识库。随后，快速眼动睡眠似乎在这个精致的基础上运作，探索新的联系并加强显着的神经通路。In summary, the cyclical alternation between NREM and REM stages throughout the night is crucial. NREM sleep appears to consolidate and prune the day’s experiences to build a more efficient knowledge base. Subsequently, REM sleep seems to operate on this refined base, exploring novel connections and strengthening salient neural pathways.

贡献Contributions

受人类记忆处理的启发，我们认为对于持续学习者来说，没有训练或测试时间。相反，模型需要定期具有“活动”或“睡眠”两个阶段；在活动状态下，模型接收新数据并对其进行处理，而在睡眠阶段，重点是内部知识和最近记忆的巩固。为此，我们为法学硕士引入了一个“睡眠”范式的实例，它由两个集成阶段组成：Inspired by the memory processing in humans, we argue that for a continual learner, there is no train or test time. Instead, the model needs to periodically have two phases of being "active" or "Sleep"; In the active state, the model receives new data and processes it, while in the sleep phase the focus is on the internal knowledge and the consolidation of recent memories. To this end, we introduce an instance of “sleep” paradigm for LLMs that consists of two integrated phases:

  1. i

    记忆巩固Memory Consolidation：为了减轻灾难性遗忘（CF）并捕获更高级别的抽象，在内存巩固阶段，模型使用周期性过程，在该过程中激活/解锁新参数，并将知识从高频（即更新速度更快）层/模块中提取到更稳定（低频）层/模块中新解锁的参数。这个过程允许新参数有足够的可塑性，同时保证旧参数的稳定性，保留旧知识。: To mitigate catastrophic forgetting (CF) and to capture higher-levels of abstraction, in the memory consolidation phase, the model uses a periodic process in which it activates/unlocks new parameters and distills the knowledge from higher-frequency (i.e., faster updating) layers/modules to the newly unlocked parameters in more stable (lower-frequency) layers/modules. This process allows enough plasticity for new parameters while ensuring the stability of old parameters, preserving the old knowledge.

  2. ii

    通过梦想提升自我Self-Improvement via Dreaming：虽然前面的睡眠第一阶段确保将知识抽象转移到长期记忆，但该阶段负责递归自我完善的过程。特别是，考虑到模型的当前状态，它会生成一组梦想（即综合自我生成的数据）来提高其自身性能，特别注重对最近添加的知识更加熟练。: While the previous first stage of sleep ensures transferring the knowledge abstraction to longer-term memories, this stage is responsible for the process of recursive self-improvement. In particular, given the current state of the model, it generates a set of dreams (i.e., synthetically self-generated data) to improve its own performance with particular focus on the acquiring more proficiency on the recently added knowledge.

从技术角度来看，我们对上述各个阶段的贡献是：From the technical point of view, our contributions to each of the above phases are:

  1. 1.

    周期性参数（去）激活Periodic Parameter (De)Activation：基于嵌套学习（NL）范式构建: Building on the Nested Learning (NL) paradigm[behrouz2025nested]为了允许每个组件有自己的更新频率，我们建议采用周期性和渐进的参数（停用）激活过程，其中给定一个块，对于每个睡眠步骤，我们停用更快块（即更高频率块）中的一组参数，并将它们替换为当前块中一组新激活的参数。这允许保持可塑性，同时避免知识干扰先前的参数。that allows each component to have its own frequency of update, we suggest a periodic and gradual parameter (de)activation process, where given a block and for each sleep step, we deactivate a set of parameters in a faster block (i.e., higher frequency block) and replace them with a set of newly activated parameters in the current block.
