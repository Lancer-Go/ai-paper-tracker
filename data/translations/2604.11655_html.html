摘要Abstract

交互式系统中大型语言模型 (LLM) 的快速采用使得动态、开放式角色扮演代理 (RPA) 的创建成为可能。然而，评估这些代理仍然是一个重大挑战，因为标准 NLP 指标无法捕捉角色依从性、逻辑一致性和长期叙事稳定性的细微差别。本文介绍了 RPA-Check，这是一种多阶段自动化评估框架，旨在客观评估基于 LLM 的 RPA 在复杂、约束繁重的环境中的性能。我们的方法基于四步流程：（1）维度定义，建立高水平的定性行为标准； (2) 增强，将这些要求扩展到细粒度的布尔检查表指标； （3）语义过滤，保证指标客观性、无冗余和代理隔离； (4) 法学硕士作为法官评估，采用思维链验证来对代理人的忠诚度进行评分。 我们通过应用它来验证这个框架The rapid adoption of Large Language Models (LLMs) in interactive systems has enabled the creation of dynamic, open-ended Role-Playing Agents (RPAs). However, evaluating these agents remains a significant challenge, as standard NLP metrics fail to capture the nuances of role adherence, logical consistency, and long-term narrative stability. This paper introduces RPA-Check, a multi-stage automated evaluation framework designed to objectively assess the performance of LLM-based RPAs in complex, constraints-heavy environments. Our methodology is based on a four-step pipeline: (1) Dimension Definition, establishing high-level qualitative behavioral criteria; (2) Augmentation, where these requirements are expanded into granular boolean checklist indicators; (3) Semantic Filtering, to ensure indicator objectivity, no redundancy and agent isolation; and (4) LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation, which employs chain-of-thought verification to score agent fidelity. We validate this framework by applying it to法学硕士法庭LLM Court，一个严肃的法医训练游戏，涉及多个量化的局部模型。五个不同法律场景的实验结果表明，该框架能够识别模型大小、推理深度和操作稳定性之间的微妙权衡。值得注意的是，研究结果揭示了参数规模和程序一致性之间的反比关系，表明较小的、经过充分指令调整的模型 (8-9B) 可以胜过容易出现用户对齐偏差或阿谀奉承的较大架构。因此，RPA-Check 为专业领域内生成代理评估的未来研究提供了标准化且可重复的指标。, a serious game for forensic training involving several quantized local models. Experimental results across five distinct legal scenarios demonstrate the framework’s ability to identify subtle trade-offs between model size, reasoning depth, and operational stability. Notably, the findings reveal an inverse relationship between parametric scale and procedural consistency, showing that smaller, adequately instruction-tuned models (8-9B) can outperform larger architectures prone to user-alignment bias or sycophancy. RPA-Check thus provides a standardized and reproducible metric for future research in generative agent evaluation within specialized domains.

1 简介Introduction

交互式系统中越来越多地采用大型语言模型 (LLM)，从而能够创建动态且开放的角色扮演代理 (RPA)，无需预定义脚本即可维持自然语言对话The increasing adoption of Large Language Models (LLMs) in interactive systems has enabled the creation of dynamic and open Role-Playing Agents (RPAs) capable of sustaining natural language dialogues without predefined scripts(Chen2024; Wang2026)。然而，评估这些代理仍然是一个重大挑战：标准自然语言处理指标（例如 BLEU、ROUGE 或困惑度）无法捕获关键维度，例如对指定角色的遵守、内部逻辑一致性以及扩展对话中的叙述稳定性。这种方法论差距在高度专业化的环境中尤其明显，其中正确性与语言流畅性和一般事实性并不相符，而是取决于对特定领域的程序和语义约束的遵守，并且玩家可能会遇到不自然的对话流程. However, evaluating these agents remains a significant challenge: standard Natural Language Processing metrics, such as BLEU, ROUGE or perplexity, do not capture critical dimensions such as adherence to the assigned role, internal logical consistency, and narrative stability over extended conversations. This methodological gap is particularly evident in highly specialized contexts, where correctness does not coincide with linguistic fluency and general factuality, but depends on compliance with procedural and semantic constraints specific to the domain, and players might experience unnatural conversational flow(Zargham2026).

在严肃的游戏中，目标之一是通过交互式模拟来传达专业技能，LLM 的集成为自适应和沉浸式培训环境提供了机会In serious games, where one of the goals is to convey professional skills through interactive simulations, the integration of LLM opens opportunities for adaptive and immersive training environments(Bonetti2025; Ozkaya2025)。然而，缺乏客观和可重复的评估框架限制了比较模型和确定不同性能维度之间权衡的能力。现有方法，例如 MMLU 基准. However, the lack of objective and reproducible evaluation frameworks limits the ability to compare models and identify trade-offs between different performance dimensions. Existing methodologies, such as the MMLU benchmark(Hendrycks2021)或聊天机器人竞技场or Chatbot Arena(Chiang2024)，侧重于一般知识或会话实用性，这使得它们不适用于必须体现严格定义的角色的代理，例如公正的法官或模拟审判中的敌对证人。, focus on general knowledge or conversational utility, making them inadequate for agents who must embody rigidly defined roles, such as an impartial judge or a hostile witness in a mock trial.

所提出的 RPA-Check 框架在“LLM Court”（本文中也介绍了）上进行了测试，该游戏使用本地量化模型作为实现非游戏角色 (NPC) 的 LLM 来模拟法庭，并在 Unity 中开发。该架构允许执行审判模拟，其中法学硕士控制的代理人体现法律角色（法官、检察官、证人），并通过开放的、即兴的对话与玩家互动。模拟环境施加了严格的程序限制，要求代理保持叙述的一致性，尊重干预层次结构，并在扩展的对话会话中提供逻辑一致的响应。此外，量化模型的采用允许在消费类硬件上完全本地执行The proposed RPA-Check framework is tested on “LLM Court” (also introduced in this paper), a game simulating a court using local quantized models as the LLMs implementing the Non-Playing Characters (NPCs) and developed in Unity. The simulation environment imposes strict procedural constraints, requiring agents to maintain narrative consistency, respect intervention hierarchies, and provide logically consistent responses over extended dialogue sessions. Moreover, the adoption of quantized models allows for completely local execution on consumer hardware(Lang2024)，消除对云服务的依赖，确保敏感数据的隐私，并显着降低与推理相关的计算成本和环境影响, eliminating dependence on cloud services, ensuring the privacy of sensitive data, and significantly reducing the computational costs and environmental impact associated with inference(Bai2024)。这种架构选择可实现可持续且可访问的部署，这对于资源有限或机密性要求较高的环境中的教育应用程序尤其重要。. 3 方法论：多阶段自动评估框架Methodology: The Multi-Stage Automated Evaluation Framework

在约束繁重的环境中评估 RPA 提出了一个独特的挑战：标准 NLP 指标（例如 BLEU、ROUGE、Perplexity）无法捕获高级语义要求，例如角色依从性、叙述稳定性或程序合规性。模型可能会生成语言流畅的文本，但在程序上无效或逻辑上不连贯。 为了解决这些限制，我们提出了 RPA-Check，这是一种多阶段自动化评估框架，通过“法学硕士作为法官”的方法将定性行为要求转化为细粒度的、可验证的布尔指标，以进行最终评估。Evaluating RPAs in constraints-heavy environments presents a unique challenge: standard NLP metrics (e.g., BLEU, ROUGE, Perplexity) fail to capture high-level semantic requirements like role adherence, narrative stability, or procedural compliance. A model might generate linguistically fluent text that is nonetheless procedurally invalid or logically incoherent. To address these limitations, we propose RPA-Check, a multi-stage automated evaluation framework that transforms qualitative behavioral requirements into granular, verifiable boolean indicators via an “LLM-as-a-Judge” approach for the final evaluation.

7 结论Conclusions

本文提出了 RPA-Check，这是一个多阶段自动化评估框架，它解决了在严格的程序和语义约束下运行的基于 LLM 的角色扮演代理评估中的关键方法论差距。该框架将定性行为要求转换为细粒度的、可验证的布尔指标，从而实现跨异构模型架构的可重复和客观的比较。通过 LLM Court（一款法医模拟环境的严肃游戏）进行的验证证实了该框架在三个主要评估维度上的判别能力：行为角色保真度、程序收敛性和稳定性以及语言形式主义和正字法。This paper presented RPA-Check, a multi-stage automated evaluation framework that addresses a critical methodological gap in the assessment of LLM-based Role-Playing Agents operating under strict procedural and semantic constraints. The framework transforms qualitative behavioral requirements into granular, verifiable Boolean metrics, enabling reproducible and objective comparison across heterogeneous model architectures. Validation through LLM Court, a serious game that is a forensic simulation environment confirmed the framework’s discriminative capacity across three primary evaluation dimensions: Behavioral Role Fidelity, Procedural Convergence and Stability, and Linguistic Formalism and Orthography.

具体来说，使用 RPA-Check 从 LLM 法庭收集的实验证据建立了一个原则性的、基于经验的对立论，反对普遍的假设，即参数规模足以代表受限生成环境中的代理能力。模型规模和程序一致性之间的反比关系，最明显地体现在较大架构的阿谀奉承驱动的角色偏差中，强调指令调优质量和行为对齐方法比单独的参数计数更能决定操作可靠性。同样重要的是在语言形式主义维度的所有评估模型中观察到的近乎一致的天花板效应，这证实了专门角色扮演环境中的区分性评估能力不在于表面流畅性，而在于在扩展的、对抗性结构的交互中维持程序和语义连贯性的能力。此外，在架构（例如 Phi-4-14B 和 Gemma-3-12B-it）中检测到的高场景间差异表明，单场景基准测试构成了 RPA 结构上不足的评估协议，从而促使使用此处介绍的程序生成管道构建更大的自动生成的案例语料库。Specifically, the experimental evidence collected from LLM court using RPA-Check establishes a principled and empirically grounded counterpoint to the prevailing assumption that parametric scale is a sufficient proxy for agent capability in constrained generative environments. The inverse relationship between model scale and procedural consistency, most sharply manifested in the sycophancy-driven role deviations of the larger architectures, underscores that instruction tuning quality and behavioral alignment methodology are more determinative of operational reliability than parameter count alone. Equally significant is the near-uniform ceiling effect observed across all evaluated models on the linguistic formalism dimension, which confirms that discriminative evaluation power in specialized role-playing contexts resides not in surface fluency but in the capacity to sustain procedural and semantic coherence across extended, adversarially structured interactions. Moreover, the high inter-scenario variance detected in architectures (such as Phi-4-14B and Gemma-3-12B-it) indicates that single-scenario benchmarking constitutes a structurally inadequate evaluation protocol for RPAs, motivating the construction of larger, automatically generated case corpora using the procedural generation pipeline introduced here.

更广泛地说，RPA-Check 的模块化架构（其中维度、增强策略和过滤标准是域可配置的）使该框架成为一个可转移的工具，用于评估跨专业领域的受限生成代理，可能超出取证环境（例如在医学模拟、合规培训和受监管的谈判环境中）。More broadly, the modular architecture of RPA-Check (where dimensions, augmentation strategies, and filtering criteria are domain-configurable) makes the framework a transferable tool for evaluating constrained generative agents across specialized domains, potentially beyond the forensic context (such as in medical simulation, compliance training, and regulated negotiation environments).
