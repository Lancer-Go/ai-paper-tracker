摘要Abstract

智力通常被理解为获取和应用知识、适应不熟悉的情况和解决新问题的能力Intelligence is commonly understood as the ability to acquire and apply knowledge, adapt to unfamiliar situations and solve new problemsrussell2021artificial, legg2007universal。大型语言模型通过从文本上下文中推断与任务相关的知识并将其应用于新任务来展示这种能力. Large language models exhibit this capacity by inferring task-relevant knowledge from textual context and applying it to new tasksbrown2020language。然而智力不必局限于语言。对于科学和社会系统，我们需要能够从数字环境中获取和应用知识的模型——我们称之为数字智能的能力。在这里，我们介绍统一上下文运营商网络（UNICON），这是一个展示跨学科数字智能的基础模型。 UNICON 使用系统中基于图形的示例作为上下文，推断出它们之间共享的预测关系，并将其应用于来自同一系统的查询。在科学和社会系统中，包括那些没有接受培训的学科，相同的模型无需再培训即可达到专业绩效。将 UNICON 与语言模型代理相结合会产生进一步的收益，使其能够超越训练中未见过的学科中最先进的专家。我们进一步表明，训练语料库的多样性提高了对未知学科的泛化能力。总之，这些结果将 UNICON 确立为数值智能的基础模型，并将其定位为更广泛的人工智能生态系统的构建模块。. Yet intelligence need not be confined to language. For scientific and social systems, we need models that acquire and apply knowledge from numerical context–an ability we call numerical intelligence. Here we introduce UNified In-Context Operator Networks (UNICON), a foundation model that exhibits numerical intelligence across disciplines. Using graph-based examples from a system as context, UNICON infers the predictive relation shared across them and applies it to queries from the same system. Across scientific and social systems, including those from disciplines absent from training, the same model approaches specialist performance without retraining. Combining UNICON with language-model agents yields further gains, enabling it to surpass state-of-the-art specialists in a discipline unseen in training. We further show that training-corpus diversity improves generalization to unseen disciplines. Together, these results establish UNICON as a foundation model of numerical intelligence and position it as a building block for a broader ecosystem of artificial intelligence.

Traffic flows across road networks, water moves through river basins, and atmospheric and geomagnetic states evolve over space and time. We encounter these systems through numerical observations organized as graphs, fields and multivariate sequences. These observations contain relations that may be learned before they are named, formalized or distilled into a specialist model.

实现数字智能很困难，因为数字系统不共享明显的词汇。交通图和大气场的变量、单位、采样率、几何形状和预测目标可能有所不同。因此，数值模型往往一次只针对一个系统进行设计。迁移到新系统通常需要安装新模型。最近的上下文运算符学习朝着这个目标迈出了一步：给定输入和输出函数配对的示例，冻结模型推断它们表达的关系并将其应用于新查询Realizing numerical intelligence is difficult because numerical systems do not share an obvious vocabulary. A traffic graph and an atmospheric field may differ in their variables, units, sampling rates, geometry and prediction targets. Numerical models therefore tend to be designed for one system at a time; moving to a new system usually entails fitting a new model. Recent in-context operator learning takes a step towards this goal: given examples that pair input and output functions, a frozen model infers the relation they express and applies it to a new queryyang2023context, yang2024pde, yang2025fine, cao2026vicon, wu2026graph, yang2026chop。然而，现有的演示很大程度上仍停留在单个操作员家族或密切相关的物理系统内。相同的原理是否可以统一跨学科的数值系统仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。. Existing demonstrations, however, have largely remained within individual operator families or closely related physical systems. Whether the same principle can unite cross-disciplinary numerical systems remains open.

在这里，我们介绍统一上下文算子网络（UNICON），一种具有跨学科泛化的数值智能基础模型。 UNICON 接受过水文、交通、电力系统、天气、土地、海洋、土壤、太阳能资源和人员流动性数据方面的培训，所有系统均以基于共享图形的上下文格式呈现。在这种格式中，上下文由将观察到的历史与所选预测范围内的未来状态配对的示例组成，而查询是新的观察到的历史，其同一范围内的状态将被预测。推断时，UNICON 通过使用该系统中的示例作为上下文来适应新系统，同时其权重保持固定。Here we introduce UNified In-Context Operator Network (UNICON), a foundation model of numerical intelligence with cross-disciplinary generalization. UNICON is trained on data spanning hydrology, traffic, power systems, weather, land, ocean, soil, solar resources, and human mobility, with all systems presented in a shared graph-based context format. Within this format, the context consists of examples pairing observed histories with future states at a chosen forecast horizon, while the query is a new observed history whose state at the same horizon is to be predicted. At inference, UNICON adapts to a new system by using examples from that system as context, while its weights remain fixed.

我们在跨越科学和社会系统的测试任务上评估相同的冻结模型，包括完全没有参加培训的三个系统（空气质量、网络活动和太空天气）。无需任何再训练，UNICON 就超越了 k 最近邻 (kNN) 基线，并接近专门针对每个任务训练的模型。值得注意的是，在网络活动中，UNICON 推理达到了专家级别的性能，即使在训练期间既没有代表数据集也没有代表学科。由语言模型代理精心策划的即时构建和重复调用可产生进一步的收益，使 UNICON 在网络活动任务中超越最先进的专家。We evaluate the same frozen model on testing tasks spanning scientific and social systems, including three systems from disciplines entirely absent from training (air quality, web activity and space weather). Without any retraining, UNICON beats k-nearest neighborhood (kNN) baseline and approaches models trained specifically for each task. Remarkably, on web activity, UNICON inference reaches specialist-level performance even though neither the dataset nor the discipline was represented during training. Prompt construction and repeated calls, orchestrated by language-model agents, yield further gains that allow UNICON to surpass state-of-the-art specialists in the web activity task.

为了理解上下文适应如何支持这种性能，我们系统地干预提供的示例，表明 UNICON 推断并使用上下文示例表达的预测关系，而不是仅仅从上下文的存在或长度中受益。此外，语料库多样性实验表明，跨不同数值系统的训练可以改善向未知系统和学科的迁移，同时保持训练语料库系统的强劲性能。To understand how contextual adaptation supports this performance, we systematically intervene on the supplied examples, showing that UNICON infers and uses the predictive relations expressed by contextual examples, rather than benefiting from the mere presence or length of context. Moreover, corpus-diversity experiments show that training across diverse numerical systems improves transfer to unseen systems and disciplines while retaining strong performance on training-corpus systems.

总之，这些结果将 UNICON 确立为数字智能的基础模型：一个单一的冻结模型，可以从数字上下文中推断出系统特定的预测关系，并将其应用于跨科学和社会系统的新查询。更广泛地说，他们认为通用人工智能可能不是来自单一的整体模型，而是来自集成语言和数字智能的协作生态系统。Together, these results establish UNICON as a foundation model for numerical intelligence: a single frozen model that infers system-specific predictive relations from numerical context and applies them to new queries across scientific and social systems. More broadly, they suggest that artificial general intelligence may emerge not from a single monolithic model, but from a collaborative ecosystem integrating linguistic and numerical intelligence.

将学科与图形上下文统一起来Unifying disciplines with graph contexts

跨学科推广单一模型面临两个挑战。首先， 模型必须适应在数值尺度、变量数量和集合、几何形状、观测历史长度和预测范围方面不同的系统和任务。其次，它必须适应系统的实体和变量如何交互以及观察到的历史如何映射到所请求的未来状态。 UNICON 通过专门设计的网络架构和情境学习范例来应对这些挑战。Generalizing a single model across disciplines poses two challenges. First, the model must accommodate systems and tasks that differ in numerical scale, numbers and sets of variables, geometries, observation-history length, and forecast horizon. Second, it must adapt to how the entities and variables of a system interact and how observed histories map to the requested future state. UNICON addresses these challenges with a specially designed network architecture and in-context learning paradigm.

在 UNICON 中，每个系统都表示为一个图，其节点表示系统特定的实体，例如河流流域、地磁网格单元或测量站以及网页。每个节点携带随着时间的推移观察到的一个或多个系统特定变量，例如流域的气象强迫和水流、地磁站点的多个场分量或网页的页面浏览活动。该图对实体之间的关系进行编码，而变量轴表示与每个实体关联的数量。In UNICON, each system is represented as a graph whose nodes denote system-specific entities, such as river catchments, geomagnetic grid cells or measurement stations, and webpages. Each node carries one or more system-specific variables observed over time, for example, meteorological forcings and streamflow for a catchment, multiple field components at a geomagnetic site, or page-view activity for a webpage. The graph encodes relations among entities, whereas the variable axis represents the quantities associated with each entity.

该图提供了实体之间关系的通用表示，但它没有完全指定系统或预测任务的动态。构建一个跨学科词汇表来明确描述这些信息是不切实际的；更根本的是，潜在的动态往往只能被部分理解，并且本身无法被完全指定。因此，UNICON 通过数值示例提供此信息，允许冻结模型适应上下文，而无需系统或任务标识符或参数更新。The graph provides a common representation of relations among entities, but it does not fully specify the dynamics of a system or the prediction task. Constructing a cross-disciplinary vocabulary for explicitly describing this information would be impractical; more fundamentally, the underlying dynamics are often only partially understood and cannot themselves be fully specified. UNICON therefore supplies this information through numerical examples, allowing the frozen model to adapt in context without system or task identifiers or parameter updates.

讨论Discussion

这项工作的核心发现是，固定的数字智能模型可以通过从上下文中学习来适应跨学科。在 UNICON 中，训练使模型能够从基于图形的数值示例中学习，而上下文则传达每个查询所需的系统和任务特定知识。该模型推断出示例之间共享的预测关系，并将其应用于来自同一系统的查询。无需再培训，即使在未经过培训的学科系统上，它也能达到专业性能，并且可以在变量、单位、几何形状和预测目标不同的系统上运行。这种从数字环境中获取和应用知识的能力是 UNICON 数字智能的基础。The central finding of this work is that a fixed numerical intelligence model can adapt across disciplines by learning from context. In UNICON, training equips the model to learn from graph-based numerical examples, whereas context conveys the system- and task-specific knowledge needed for each query. The model infers the predictive relation shared across the examples and applies it to queries from the same system. Without retraining, it approaches specialist performance even on systems from disciplines absent from training and operates across systems that differ in their variables, units, geometry and prediction targets. This ability to acquire and apply knowledge from numerical context is the basis of UNICON’s numerical intelligence.

情境干预阐明了这种适应是如何发生的。与没有上下文或包含不匹配的键值对或损坏的数值的上下文相比，随机采样但正确配对的示例的性能更高；检索与查询相关的示例提供了额外的改进。总之，这些控制表明 UNICON 推断并使用上下文示例表达的预测关系，而不是仅仅从上下文的存在或长度中受益。Contextual interventions clarify how this adaptation occurs. Performance is higher with randomly sampled but correctly paired examples than with no context or with contexts containing mismatched key-value pairs or corrupted numerical values; retrieving examples for their relevance to the query provides an additional improvement. Together, these controls show that UNICON infers and uses the predictive relations expressed by contextual examples, rather than benefiting from the mere presence or length of context.

在固定的培训预算下，对更广泛的数值系统进行培训可以提高未见过的系统和学科的性能，同时在很大程度上保持培训中包含的系统的性能。这种模式表明语料库多样性增强了 UNICON 从新系统中的上下文示例中学习的能力。Under a fixed training budget, training on a broader range of numerical systems improves performance on unseen systems and disciplines while largely maintaining performance on systems included in training. This pattern indicates that corpus diversity strengthens UNICON’s ability to learn from contextual examples in new systems.

本研究有几个局限性。它通过预测来评估数字智能；同样的方法是否扩展到插补、异常检测、模拟或控制仍有待测试。语料库多样性实验在固定的训练预算下比较特定的语料库组成，因此没有建立与数据量、模型大小和计算相关的性能的一般缩放法则。最后，为不同系统和任务选择和组织上下文示例的系统方法需要进一步研究。The present study has several limitations. It evaluates numerical intelligence through forecasting; whether the same approach extends to imputation, anomaly detection, simulation or control remains to be tested. The corpus-diversity experiments compare particular corpus compositions under a fixed training budget and therefore do not establish a general scaling law relating performance to data volume, model size and compute. Finally, systematic methods for selecting and organizing contextual examples for different systems and tasks require further study.

在这项工作中，代理辅助推理提供了语言智能和数字智能如何相互补充的具体示例。语言模型代理使用任务描述和可用观察来构建提示并组织重复调用，而 UNICON 提供解决跨学科问题所需的数值能力。这种角色分工表明，通用人工智能可能会从集成语言和数字智能的协作生态系统中出现，而 UNICON 为这种生态系统提供了数字构建模块。In this work, agent-assisted inference provides a concrete example of how linguistic and numerical intelligence can complement one another. Language-model agents use task descriptions and available observations to construct prompts and organize repeated calls, while UNICON provides the numerical capability needed to address problems across disciplines. This division of roles suggests that artificial general intelligence may emerge from a collaborative ecosystem integrating linguistic and numerical intelligence, with UNICON providing a numerical building block for such an ecosystem.

D.1 培训目标Training objective

训练目标是标准化未来状态的均方误差。 在混合源训练期间，解码器为每个键和每个序列中的查询生成预测。这些预测与 相应的值和查询的未来状态。对于报道的 模型，第一个密钥的预测被排除在损失之外， 因为前面没有例子可以用来推断潜在的动态。当数据集提供结构观察掩模时，损失仅限于观察到的条目；否则，它是对未来状态的所有条目进行计算的。The training objective is mean squared error on normalized future states. During mixed-source training, the decoder produces a prediction for every key and for the query in each sequence. These predictions are compared with the corresponding values and the query’s future state. For the reported model, the prediction for the first key is excluded from the loss, because no preceding example is available from which to infer the underlying dynamics. When a dataset provides structural observation masks, the loss is restricted to observed entries; otherwise, it is computed over all entries of the future state.

The agent can query the same frozen UNICON model multiple times and use the returned predictions to develop a task-specific prediction procedure.
