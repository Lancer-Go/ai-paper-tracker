摘要：Abstract:

背景和目的：Background and Purpose:局灶性皮质发育不良 (FCD) 的自动检测需要大量体素病变描绘的 MRI 数据，而这些数据很难获取。本研究旨在生成显示 FCD 的合成 MRI 数据，评估其真实性，并评估其对自动 FCD 检测的影响，特别是在减少手动注释的需求方面。Automated detection of focal cortical dysplasia (FCD) requires large volumes of voxelwise-lesion delineated MRI data, which are difficult to acquire. This study aims to generate synthetic MRI data exhibiting FCD, assess their realism, and evaluate their impact on automated FCD detection—particularly in reducing the need for manual annotations.

方法：Methods:回顾性研究了来自多个 (3) 个部位的 131 名 FCD 患者和 90 名健康对照者的 T1 加权 (T1w) 和 T2 加权流体衰减反转恢复 (FLAIR) MRI 扫描。通过在二元 FCD 掩模上调节生成网络来生成合成 MRI。两名神经放射学家从一组随机的 14 个真实扫描和 14 个合成扫描中识别出真实图像。训练三个 nnU-Net 模型来检测 FCD，使用：（i）纯真实（35-FCD/35-对照），（ii）真实（35-FCD/35-对照）+合成增强，以及（iii）扩展真实数据（70-FCD/70-对照）。T1-weighted (T1w) and T2-weighted-Fluid-Attenuated Inversion Recovery (FLAIR) MRI scans from 131 FCD patients and 90 healthy controls from multiple (3) sites, were retrospectively studied. Synthetic MRIs were generated by conditioning a generative network on binary FCD mask. Two neuroradiologists identified real images from a random set of 14 real and 14 synthetic scans. Three nnU-Net models were trained to detect FCD using: (i) real-only (35-FCD/35-controls), (ii) real (35-FCD/35-controls) + synthetic augmentation, and (iii) expanded real data (70-FCD/70-controls).

结果：Results:专家们区分真实图像和合成图像的能力有限，T1w 的分类准确度为 60%，FLAIR 的分类准确度为 70%（评估者间一致性 κ = 0.86）。使用合成数据增强自动 FCD 检测可将灵敏度提高 8.14% (p = 0.12)，并提高真实病变部位的模型置信度（0.83 ± 0.11 至 0.89 ± 0.12；p = 0.02）。扩展的真实数据模型进一步将灵敏度提高至 73.8% (p < 0.001)，将置信度提高至 0.90 ± 0.14 (p = 0.01)。Experts showed limited ability to distinguish real from synthetic images, with classification accuracy of 60% for T1w and 70% for FLAIR (inter-rater agreement κ = 0.86). Augmenting automated FCD detection with synthetic data increased sensitivity by 8.14% (p = 0.12) and improved model confidence at true lesion sites (0.83 ± 0.11 to 0.89 ± 0.12; p = 0.02). The expanded real-data model further improved sensitivity to 73.8% (p < 0.001) and confidence to 0.90 ± 0.14 (p = 0.01).

结论：Conclusion:条件生成网络可以生成真实的合成 FCD-MRI，将标记数据需求减少约 20%，同时保持同等的灵敏度。等量的真实数据（如果可用）仍然比合成增强更有效。Conditional generative networks can generate realistic synthetic FCD-MRIs, reducing labeled data needs by ~20% while maintaining equivalent sensitivity. Equivalent amounts of real data, when available, remain more effective than synthetic augmentation.

简介INTRODUCTION

局灶性皮质发育不良 (FCD) 是一种常见的皮质畸形，在手动 MRI 检查中经常被忽视，它是儿童耐药性癫痫 (DRE) 的主要原因，也是成人中第三常见的原因 [1-3]。早期发现导致耐药性癫痫（DRE）的皮质畸形对于及时进行手术治疗和获得更好的结果至关重要[1]。通常，放射科医生通过目视检查 MRI 扫描来识别异常区域。当通过 MRI 检测时，FCD 与高达 70% 的无癫痫发作率相关，而 MRI 阴性病例的无癫痫发作率约为 40% [4]。然而，由于细微特征、感知错误或 MRI 扫描质量差，放射科医生漏掉了近 30% 的局灶性皮质发育不良 (FCD) 病例 [4-5]。Focal Cortical Dysplasia (FCD), a common cortical malformation often overlooked during manual MRI review, is the leading cause of drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE) in children and the third most common cause in adults [1–3]. Early detection of cortical malformations causing drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE) is critical for timely surgical treatment and better outcomes [1]. Typically, expert radiologists visually inspect MRI scans to identify abnormal regions. When detected on MRI, FCD is associated with seizure freedom rates of up to 70%, compared to ~40% in MRI-negative cases [4]. However, nearly 30% of Focal Cortical Dysplasia (FCD) cases are missed by radiologists due to subtle features, perceptual errors, or poor MRI scan quality [4–5].

最近提出了机器学习（ML）和深度学习（DL）方法来协助从 MRI 进行手动 FCD 检测，充当辅助读者来支持放射科医生并减少感知错误 [6-10,14]。这些模型通常依赖于使用 MRI 图像与每个体素病变描绘配对的监督学习。由放射科专家和神经外科医生手动逐个体素地描绘病灶，以指示表现出 FCD 的异常区域，在本工作中将其称为 FCD 标签。 FCD 表现出以下 MRI 成像特征：灰白质连接模糊、皮质增厚、跨幔征、异常回旋以及 T2 加权或 FLAIR 序列上的信号强度增加 [4]。然而，手动体素注释既耗时又费力，限制了大型注释数据集的可用性。大型数据集的收集需要多站点数据共享，这会带来后勤和监管障碍，通常需要大量时间和精力[4]。因此，当前方法的发展受到标记数据的稀缺性和可访问性的限制[4]。Machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) approaches have recently been proposed to assist in manual FCD detection from MRI, acting as secondary readers to support radiologists and reduce perceptual errors [6–10,14]. These models typically rely on supervised learning using MRI images paired with delineation of lesions for each voxel. The lesions were manually delineated voxel-by-voxel by expert radiologists and neurosurgeons to indicate abnormal regions exhibiting FCD, which are referred to as FCD labels in this work. FCDs exhibit the following MRI imaging characteristics: gray-white matter junction blurring, cortical thickening, transmantle sign, abnormal gyration, and increased signal intensity on T2-weighted or FLAIR sequences [4]. However, manual voxel-wise annotation is time-consuming and labor-intensive, limiting the availability of large annotated datasets. The collection of large dataset demands for multi-site data sharing which introduces logistical and regulatory barriers, often requiring significant time and effort [4]. Consequently, the development of current methods is constrained by both the scarcity and limited accessibility of labeled data [4].

生成深度学习是一种提高图像分割和异常检测等任务性能的方法，其中带注释的数据集有限[19]。这种方法估计训练数据的基本分布，以合成新的、真实的数据样本。然后将这些合成数据与有限的带注释的数据集相结合，以扩展训练数据的大小和多样性，而不需要额外的手动注释[11]。最近的研究证明了使用生成模型来创建带注释的病理脑图像，有效地增强了脑肿瘤和白质高信号检测等应用中的小型数据集[12-13,17]。Generative deep learning is an approach that improves the performance of tasks such as image segmentation and anomaly detection - where annotated datasets are limited [19]. This approach estimates the underlying distribution of training data to synthesize new and realistic data samples. These synthetic data are then combined with limited annotated datasets to expand both the size and diversity of training data, without requiring additional manual annotation [11]. Recent studies have demonstrated the use of generative models to create annotated pathological brain images, effectively augmenting small datasets in applications such as brain tumor and white matter hyperintensity detection [12–13,17].

然而，这种生成方法尚未被探索用于生成展示 FCD 的合成注释图像或评估其对数据稀缺场景中 FCD 检测的影响。生成模型在 FCD 检测中的直接应用受到标记 FCD 病例数量有限的挑战。例如，在先前的基于生成的研究中检测脑肿瘤或白质高信号需要大约 800 个带注释的 MRI 扫描 [13]。这可与迄今为止用于 FCD 检测的最大的精选多站点数据集相媲美，该数据集包含约 762 个受试者 [9]——这是一项重大工作，凸显了此类数据集不易获得或访问的原因。 [12-13, 33-34] 中对大型注释数据集的需求源于使用高度参数化的潜在扩散模型和变分编码器-解码器网络，经过训练以生成组织概率图和二进制 FCD 标签。However, this generative approach has not yet been explored for producing synthetic annotated images exhibiting FCD or evaluating its impact on FCD detection in data-scarce scenarios. The direct application of generative modeling in FCD detection is challenged by the limited availability of labeled FCD cases. For instance, approximately 800 annotated MRI scans were required in prior generative based studies detecting brain tumors or white matter hyperintensities [13]. This is comparable to the largest curated multi-site dataset for FCD detection to date, comprising around 762 subjects [9]—a significant effort that highlights how such datasets are not readily available or accessible. The need for large annotated datasets in [12–13, 33-34] arises from the use of highly parameterized latent diffusion models and variational encoder-decoder networks, trained to generate both tissue probability maps and binary FCD labels.

此外，当前的方法与群论框架相一致，其中增强充当不变数据轨道上的方差减少工具[31]。虽然标准增强技术（例如旋转、缩放或翻转）确保模型对于方向不变，但它们无法模拟 FCD 病变复杂的、空间自适应的强度变化特征 [33]。相反，域随机化策略利用解剖图来确保模型针对跨站点扫描仪变异性的鲁棒性[32]。因此，仍然存在一个Furthermore, current methodologies align with group-theoretic frameworks where augmentation acts as a variance-reduction tool over invariant data orbits [31]. While standard augmentation techniques—such as rotation, scaling, or flipping— ensure the model is invariant to orientation, they cannot simulate the complex, spatially-adaptive intensity changes characteristic of FCD lesions [33]. 为了解决上述挑战，我们建议在健康的大脑扫描中嵌入类似 FCD 的区域，提供显示 FCD 的 MRI 图像以及相应的二进制 FCD 标签。这种策略消除了从头开始生成脑部扫描的需要——之前是使用[12-13]中的扩散模型完成的。扩散模型的消除减少了可训练参数（减少了 95%），从而降低了对大型注释数据集的依赖。据我们所知，这是第一项在低数据场景下引入 FCD 检测生成框架的研究，解决了文献中的一个关键空白。这项工作的目标是：(i) 生成显示 FCD 的合成 MRI 体积，(ii) 用训练数据对其进行增强，以及 (iii) 评估这种增强对自动 FCD 检测的影响。我们的主要贡献包括：(i) 一种基于 GMM 的新颖方法，从健康的大脑扫描中生成解剖学上合理的 FCD 标签，(ii) 应用条件生成模型将 MRI 与 FCD 合成，以及 (iii) 评估这种增强对自动 FCD 检测的影响。我们假设用合成的 FCD 标记体积丰富训练数据将提高检测准确性。To address the above challenge, we propose embedding FCD like regions in healthy brain scans providing MRI images exhibiting FCD along with corresponding binary FCD labels. This strategy eliminates the need to generate brain scan from scratch - previously done using diffusion models in [12-13]. The elimination of diffusion models reduces trainable-parameters (by 95%), and thus lowers dependence on large annotated datasets. To our knowledge, this is the first study to introduce a generative framework for FCD detection in low-data scenarios, addressing a critical gap in the literature. The objectives of this work are to: (i) generate synthetic MRI volumes exhibiting FCD, (ii) augment it with training data, and (iii) evaluate the impact of this augmentation on automated FCD detection. Our key contributions include: (i) a novel GMM-based method to generate anatomically plausible FCD labels from healthy brain scans, (ii) application of a conditional generative model to synthesize MRI with FCD, and (iii) evaluate impact of this augmentation on automated FCD detection. We hypothesize that enriching training data with synthetic FCD-labeled volumes will improve detection accuracy.

讨论DISCUSSION

准确检测局灶性皮质发育不良 (FCD) 仍然具有挑战性，特别是在数据稀缺的环境和次优成像的情况下。这项研究表明，即使注释有限，合成数据增强也可以改进自动化 FCD 检测。合成图像与真实的 MRI 非常相似，并且能够检测到之前遗漏的其他 FCD 病例。虽然敏感性提高不大，但病变水平的置信度显着提高。然而，增强也增加了误报——漏掉了九个病例Accurate detection of Focal Cortical Dysplasia (FCD) remains challenging, particularly in data-scarce settings and suboptimal imaging. This study shows that synthetic data augmentation can improve automated FCD detection, even with limited annotations. The synthetic images closely resembled real MRIs and enabled detection of additional FCD cases previously missed. While sensitivity gains were modest, lesion-level confidence improved significantly. Compared to existing methods, our approach performs competitively with fewer training samples.

正如 MELD [7] 中观察到的那样，增加训练样本数量并不总是能提高 FCD 检测性能，并且最近的研究报告了 MELD [25-26] 的类似性能趋势。之前的工作 [22,27] 也表明，在不增加数据的情况下切换到 nnU-Net 时，检测效果得到了改善。在这项研究中，我们表明，对于固定的 nnU-Net 模型，性能随着真实标记数据的减少而下降。此外，我们的方法证明了使用合成数据来减少训练数据需求，同时提高低数据设置中的性能的可行性。这开辟了一个新的研究方向，利用新兴技术进一步改进 FCD 检测。Increasing the number of training samples does not always improve FCD detection performance, as observed in MELD [7], and recent studies have reported similar performance trends for MELD [25-26]. Previous work [22,27] also showed improved detection when switching to nnU-Net without increasing data. In this study, we showed that for a fixed nnU-Net model, performance decreased with reduced real labeled data. Additionally, our method demonstrated the feasibility of using synthetic data to reduce training data requirements while improving performance in low-data settings. This opens a new research direction that leverages emerging technologies to further improve FCD detection.

我们提出的框架利用公开的健康数据集，这本质上消除了收集额外患者数据或手动病变注释的需要。与其他数据生成策略相比，这种方法具有显着的优势。例如，利用以合成病变为条件的语义分割的方法，例如用于生成肿瘤图像的扩散模型或 GAN，通常需要大量训练集（例如 850-1500 个受试者），并且通常依赖于不稳定的技术，例如移动和翻转真实肿瘤标签 [13, 29]。同样，像 SynthSeg [30] 这样的框架已经证明，对纯合成图像（使用高斯混合模型（GMM）从标签图生成）进行训练可以在不同的图像对比度下实现高解剖分割精度。另一种趋势涉及使用分割网络生成伪标签，然后在超低数据状态下训练单独的生成网络[28]，这仍然是未来研究的一个有价值的领域。重要的是，所提出的工作提供了一种可控的方法，通过控制 GMM 的超参数来提供病变位置和病变大小的多样性（如图 6 所示），这与基于扩散模型的方法不同[13]。Our proposed framework leverages publicly available healthy datasets, which inherently eliminates the need for collecting additional patient data or manual lesion annotations. This approach offers a significant advantage over other data generation strategies. For instance, methods that utilize semantic segmentation conditioned on synthetic lesions, such as diffusion models or GAN used to generate tumor images, typically require large training sets (e.g., 850-1500 subjects) and often rely on unstable techniques like shifting and flipping real tumor labels [13, 29]. Similarly, frameworks like SynthSeg [30] have demonstrated that training on purely synthetic images—generated from label maps using Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM)—can achieve high anatomical segmentation accuracy across diverse image contrasts. An alternative trend involving using a segmentation network to generate pseudo-labels which then train a separate generation network in ultra low data regimes[28], remains a valuable area for future research. Importantly, the proposed work provides a controllable way to provide diversity on lesion location and lesion size (as shown in Figure 6) by controlling the hyperparameters of GMM unlike diffusion models based methods [13].

这项工作展示了清晰的性能层次结构：真实数据优于同等数量的合成数据，但在低资源设置中，包含合成数据比非增强基线显着提高了性能。未来工作的一个关键领域是确定通过大幅增加合成数据量（例如，高达真实数据的 100 倍，与计算机视觉的趋势一致）可实现的改进上限。与当前的工作不同，当前的验证仅限于将 FCD 嵌入少量的真实健康扫描中，未来的研究将利用大型公共健康数据集。This work demonstrated a clear performance hierarchy: real data outperforms an equivalent volume of synthetic data, yet the inclusion of synthetic data significantly improves performance over a non-augmented baseline in low resource settings. A crucial area for future work is determining the upper bound of improvement achievable by substantially increasing the synthetic data volume (e.g., up to 100 times the real data, consistent with trends in computer vision). Unlike the current work, where validation was limited to embedding FCD only in small number of real healthy scans, the future investigation will utilize large public healthy datasets.

当前的研究使用了两个不同的评估框架，即测试集 1 和测试集 2，来确定基于 GAN 的数据增强是否能够提供性能优势，而不仅仅是缓解主题级别的过度拟合。具体来说，测试集 1 评估了跨个体受试者变异性的模型稳健性，而测试集 2 评估了框架对多中心扫描仪效应的​​弹性。我们的观察表明，一旦真实世界数据达到足够的规模，使用合成样本增强真实数据对检测性能的影响最小。在我们的设置中（包括 70 名健康对照者和 70 名患者），该模型捕获了足够的生物学差异，以至于额外的合成数据没有为未见过的测试队列提供可测量的益处。这表明，虽然合成增强对于在低数据情况下跨不同成像站点进行泛化至关重要，但随着现实世界的训练数据集达到足够的规模，其效用就会减弱。The current study utilized two distinct evaluation frameworks, Test Set 1 and Test Set 2, to determine if GAN-based data augmentation offers performance benefits beyond merely mitigating subject-level overfitting. Specifically, Test Set 1 assessed model robustness across individual subject variability, while Test Set 2 evaluated the framework’s resilience to multi-center scanner effects. Our observations indicate that augmenting real data with synthetic samples has a minimal effect on detection performance once the real-world data reaches a sufficient scale. In our setup—comprising 70 healthy controls and 70 patients—the model captured enough biological variance that additional synthetic data provided no measurable benefit for the unseen test cohorts. This suggests that while synthetic augmentation is critical for generalizing across disparate imaging sites in low-data regimes, its utility diminishes as the real-world training dataset achieves sufficient scale.

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，生成图像的质量取决于 PVE 图的准确性，在低质量扫描中 CSF 估计通常不可靠，导致模糊。其次，仅使用有限数量的合成示例（来自同一数据集 (Dataset-1) 的 35 名健康受试者的子集）来生成合成病变图像，与用于训练 FCDD-3 的 35 个额外真实病变病例相匹配。虽然这种受控设计支持公平评估，但它限制了对更大规模合成和多站点采集多样性的探索。在未来的工作中，我们的目标是增加合成量的数量，并且This study has several limitations. First, the quality of generated images depends on the accuracy of PVE maps, with CSF estimation often unreliable in low-quality scans, causing blurring. Second, only a limited number of synthetic examples—a subset of 35 healthy subjects from the same dataset (Dataset-1)—was used to generate synthetic lesional images, matching the 35 additional real lesional cases used to train FCDD-3. While this controlled design supported fair evaluation, it limited exploration of larger-scale synthesis and multi-site acquisition diversity.
