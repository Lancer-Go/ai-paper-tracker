Yet most niching-based evolutionary algorithms rely on distances or density estimators without explicitly recovering the underlying peak–basin organization in the decision space, which can lead to pseudo-multimodality: many distinct individuals ultimately collapse into only a few basins. We introduce Chaotic Landscape-Decoding Evolution (CLDE), a decision-space-centric framework that turns multimodal search into a closed loop of decode–value–allocate–refine. An adaptive persistence threshold continuously tunes the decoding resolution online to avoid over-fragmentation and over-merging. 1. 简介Introduction

在机器人、药物发现和工程设计等领域，多模态优化问题 (MMOP) 旨在发现和维护一组多样化的高质量但结构不同的解决方案，而不是收敛到单个最优值。 形式上，单目标 MMOP (SOMMOP) 寻求一组在决策空间中分离良好的解决方案，同时每个解决方案都获得足够好的客观质量：In fields such as robotics, drug discovery, and engineering design, multimodal optimization problems (MMOPs) aim to discover and maintain a diverse set of high-quality yet structurally distinct solutions, rather than converging to a single optimum. Formally, a single-objective MMOP (SOMMOP) seeks a set of solutions that are well-separated in the decision space, while each solution attains sufficiently good objective quality:

(1) mini≠j∥x(i)−x(j)∥2≥εdec,f(x(i))≤f⋆+εobj,∀i.\min_{i\neq j}\,\lVert x^{(i)}-x^{(j)}\rVert_{2}\ \geq\ \varepsilon_{\mathrm{dec}},\qquad f(x^{(i)})\leq f^{\star}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{obj}},\ \ \forall i.

在具有已知最优值的基准（例如 CEC2013）上，该目标通常通过规定决策空间精度阈值下的峰值识别来实例化，并通过峰值比率 (PR) 进行总结。On benchmarks with known optima (e.g., CEC2013), this goal is commonly instantiated by peak identification under a prescribed decision-space accuracy threshold and summarized by the peak ratio (PR).

MMOP 的一个核心困难是决策空间和目标空间之间的信息差距。 由于选择压力是由目标值驱动的，优化器可能对决策空间拓扑视而不见：结构上不同的解决方案在目标空间中可能看起来是多余的，导致对一些主要盆地的过度开发和重要模式的丢失。 许多利基定位和多样性保护方法可以缓解模式崩溃，但它们通常作用于个体，并且缺乏明确的机制来识别稳定的决策空间区域并量化哪些区域值得更多的搜索工作。 结果，评估被浪费在多余或停滞的地区，而较小但有希​​望的盆地仍未得到充分勘探。A central difficulty in MMOPs is the information gap between the decision space and the objective space. Since selection pressure is driven by objective values, an optimizer can be blind to decision-space topology: structurally distinct solutions may look redundant in objective space, leading to over-exploitation of a few dominant basins and the loss of important modes. Many niching and diversity-preserving methods alleviate mode collapse, but they typically act on individuals and lack explicit mechanisms to identify stable decision-space regions and to quantify which regions deserve more search effort. As a result, evaluations are wasted on redundant or stagnant areas, while minor but promising basins remain under-explored.

为了解决这个根本原因，我们提出了 CLDE，这是一个以区域为中心的框架，它将多模态搜索转变为决策空间区域上的闭环过程（图 1）。To address this root cause, we propose CLDE, a region-centric framework that turns multimodal search into a closed-loop process over decision-space regions (Figure.1）。 CLDE 迭代三个步骤：它从采样解决方案中解码稳定区域，为每个区域分配显着性分数并相应地分配预算，并通过受控的混沌扰动执行区域感知变化。 该循环通过明确流域结构、使用区域评估进行原则性预算控制以及在不牺牲局部细化的情况下实现跨流域过渡来弥合结构性信息差距。). CLDE iterates three steps: it decodes stable regions from sampled solutions, assigns each region a saliency score and allocates budgets accordingly, and performs region-aware variation with controlled chaotic perturbations. This cycle bridges the structural information gap by making basin structure explicit, using region valuation for principled budget control, and enabling cross-basin transitions without sacrificing local refinement.

我们实例化 CLDE 以进行单目标和多目标多模态优化，并在标准基准上对其进行评估。 结果表明，CLDE 持续改善了不同多模态景观中的模式覆盖率、收敛稳定性和结构多样性保存。We instantiate CLDE for both single-objective and multi-objective multimodal optimization and evaluate it on standard benchmarks. Results demonstrate that CLDE consistently improves mode coverage, convergence stability, and structural diversity preservation across diverse multimodal landscapes.

2. 相关工作Related Work

所提出的 CLDE 框架与三个研究方向最密切相关：适应度景观分析和结构解码、单目标多模态进化算法和多模态多目标进化算法。尽管这些方向已经开发出了保留多种模式的有效机制，但（i）在有限的评估下在线解码稳定的决策空间区域和（ii）将此类结构信号转化为进化循环内的区域级资源分配仍然是不平凡的。The proposed CLDE framework is most closely related to three research lines: fitness landscape analysis and structure decoding, single-objective multimodal evolutionary algorithms, and multimodal multi-objective evolutionary algorithms. Although these directions have developed effective mechanisms for preserving multiple modes, it remains nontrivial to (i) decode stable decision-space regions online under limited evaluations and (ii) translate such structural signals into region-level resource allocation within an evolutionary loop.

2.1. 健身景观分析与结构解码Fitness landscape analysis and structure decoding

适应度景观分析（FLA）旨在表征优化问题的宏观属性，包括鲁棒性、相关长度和局部最优分布，通常用于诊断和算法比较Fitness landscape analysis (FLA) aims to characterize macroscopic properties of optimization problems, including ruggedness, correlation length, and the distribution of local optima, and is often used for diagnosis and algorithm comparison(Zou et al., 2022; Tan et al., 2021; Pitzer and Affenzeller, 2012)。经典的 FLA 工具（例如随机游走相关性和信息论指标）提供全局难度摘要，但通常不会明确重建盆地级结构。. Classical FLA tools (e.g., random-walk correlation and information-theoretic indicators) provide global summaries of difficulty but typically do not reconstruct basin-level structures explicitly.

为了获得更具结构性的视图，局部最优网络将局部最优表示为节点，将盆地之间的过渡表示为边缘，揭示组合景观上的漏斗结构和逃逸路径To obtain a more structural view, local optima networks represent local optima as nodes and transitions between their basins as edges, revealing funnel structures and escape pathways on combinatorial landscapes(Ochoa and Veerapen, 2018)。对于连续问题，一些研究通过从随机起点执行局部搜索并计算不同的局部最优值及其吸引域来估计盆地的数量和大小. For continuous problems, several studies estimate the number and size of basins by performing local search from random starting points and counting distinct local optima and their attraction domains(Preuss, 2012; Li et al., 2011)。基于图的方法在样本上构建邻域图并分析连通性或轮廓结构以推断多个盆地的存在. Graph-based approaches construct neighborhood graphs over samples and analyze connectivity or contour structures to infer the existence of multiple basins(Stoean et al., 2010).

与此同时，拓扑数据分析 (TDA) 提供了强大的工具来从标量场和点云中提取特征。持久同源性跟踪过滤过程中连接组件、环路和空隙的产生和消亡，并将短暂的噪声与持久结构分开In parallel, topological data analysis (TDA) offers robust tools to extract features from scalar fields and point clouds. Persistent homology tracks the birth and death of connected components, loops, and voids during a filtration and separates short-lived noise from persistent structures(Edelsbrunner and Harer, 2010; Chazal and Michel, 2017)。对于标量值适应度函数，. For scalar-valued fitness functions,0维持久同源性与偏移集中连通分量的持久性密切相关，这自然地反映了峰和盆的出现和消失。然而，上述大部分结构分析都是以离线方式使用的，并且通常假设相对密集的采样；在严格的评估预算下将这种结构解码紧密地集成到在线进化过程中仍然很少被探索。-dimensional persistent homology is closely related to the persistence of connected components in excursion sets, which naturally reflects the emergence and disappearance of peaks and basins. Nevertheless, much of the above structure analysis is used in an offline manner and often assumes relatively dense sampling; integrating such structural decoding tightly into an online evolutionary process under strict evaluation budgets remains less explored.

2.2. 单目标多模态进化算法Single-objective multimodal evolutionary algorithms

SOMMOP 旨在找到标量目标的多个局部和全局最优值，而不是单个最佳解决方案。经典的遗传利基方法包括健身共享SOMMOP aims to locate multiple local and global optima of a scalar objective instead of a single best solution. Classical genetic niching methods include fitness sharing(Goldberg and Richardson, 1987), Mahfoud 的一般利基框架, Mahfoud’s general niching framework(Mahfoud, 1995), 清算, clearing(Petrowski, 1996)，以及基于形态的遗传算法, and speciation-based genetic algorithms(Li, 2002)。这些方法引入了共享功能或限制竞争，从而对拥挤的区域进行惩罚，从而能够同时保留多个峰值；综合调查总结典型机制和失效模式. These approaches introduce sharing functions or restricted competition so that crowded regions are penalized, enabling several peaks to be preserved simultaneously; comprehensive surveys summarize typical mechanisms and failure modes(Li et al., 2011).

差分进化（DE）由于其简单的结构和自适应步骤也是多模态搜索的流行基础。拥挤DE应用拥挤选择，使每个后代与其最近的邻居竞争，促进生态位共存Differential Evolution (DE) is also a popular base for multimodal search due to its simple structure and self-adaptive steps. Crowding DE applies crowding selection so that each offspring competes against its nearest neighbor, promoting niche coexistence(Thomsen, 2004)。基于邻域的突变和局部搜索机制也被提出来增强 DE 跟踪多个盆地的能力. Neighborhood-based mutation and local search mechanisms have also been proposed to enhance the ability of DE to track multiple basins(Qu and Suganthan, 2012)。结构或拓扑感知的生态位进一步通过空间分布对个体进行聚类，并维护每个物种的代表. Structure- or topology-aware niching further clusters individuals by spatial distribution and maintains representatives for each species(Stoean et al., 2010; Preuss, 2012)。最近的多模态 DE 变体强调自适应生态位和多尺度搜索，包括自适应生态位半径、双策略设计、轮廓启发的边界推理和多阶段探索-细化方案. More recent multimodal DE variants emphasize adaptive niching and multi-scale search, including adaptive niche radii, dual-strategy designs, contour-inspired boundary inference, and multi-stage exploration–refinement schemes(Chen et al., 2019; Wang et al., 2018, 2019b, 2019a).

混沌驱动的动力学也被广泛用于通过引入确定性非周期性扰动来增强探索Chaos-driven dynamics have also been widely used to enhance exploration by introducing deterministic yet non-periodic perturbations(Caponetto et al., 2003; Zelinka et al., 2010; Peng et al., 2009; Pei, 2014; Alatas, 2010)。虽然这些方法可以减轻过早收敛和模式崩溃，但模式控制通常是通过个体级距离规则和局部竞争来实现的。在更严格的评估预算下，明确优先考虑稳定且信息丰富的盆地而不是冗余或停滞的盆地变得具有挑战性，从而激发了将结构解码与预算控制相结合的以区域为中心的设计。. While these methods can mitigate premature convergence and mode collapse, mode control is often implemented via individual-level distance rules and local competition. Under tighter evaluation budgets, it becomes challenging to explicitly prioritize stable and informative basins over redundant or stagnant ones, motivating region-centric designs that couple structure decoding with budget control.

2.3. 多模态多目标进化算法Multimodal multi-objective evolutionary algorithms

MOMMOP 旨在逼近目标空间中的帕累托前沿，同时在决策空间中保留多个结构不同的解决方案系列，以实现类似的权衡MOMMOP aims to approximate the Pareto front in the objective space while preserving multiple structurally distinct solution families in the decision space that realize similar trade-offs(Deb and Saha, 2010; Li et al., 2011)。经典 MOEA，例如 NSGA-II. Classical MOEAs such as NSGA-II(Deb et al., 2002), SPEA2 (Zitzler et al., 2001)，和经济部/D, and MOEA/D(Zhang and Li, 2007)主要强调目标空间的收敛性和多样性，因此即使目标空间前沿看起来被很好地覆盖，也可能崩溃为几个决策空间盆地mainly emphasize convergence and diversity in the objective space, and can therefore collapse to a few decision-space basins even when the objective-space front appears well covered(Coello, 2007).

为了更明确地解决多模态问题，第一族通过决策空间分组或局部前沿建模来增强基于帕累托的选择。 DN-NSGA-II 在决策空间中对解决方案进行聚类，并按聚类偏置交配和生存，以维持断开的局部前沿To address multimodality more explicitly, a first family augments Pareto-based selection with decision-space grouping or local-front modeling. DN-NSGA-II clusters solutions in the decision space and biases mating and survival by cluster to maintain disconnected local fronts(Liang et al., 2016)，而 HREA 会分层检测局部前沿并对其进行排名，并将此类几何/指标提示注入选择中, while HREA detects and ranks local fronts hierarchically and injects such geometry/indicator cues into selection(Li et al., 2022)。第二个系列依靠基于邻域或群体的机制来维持多个分支，包括多群体维护和环邻域交互. A second family relies on neighborhood- or swarm-based mechanisms to sustain multiple branches, including multi-swarm maintenance and ring-neighborhood interactions(Fieldsend, 2014; Yue et al., 2018; Liang et al., 2018)。第三类采用分解、协同进化和指标辅助选择来平衡收敛性和覆盖范围，例如CoMMEA. A third family adopts decomposition, coevolution, and indicator-assisted selection to balance convergence and coverage, such as CoMMEA(Li et al., 2023), 右心室EA, RVEA(Cheng et al., 2016), MMEA-WI (Li et al., 2021)和CMMO, and CMMO(Ming et al., 2023).

对于昂贵的目标，替代和分类辅助的 MOMMOP 也很受欢迎，并且在很大程度上与上述机制正交。分类/回归模型可以过滤候选人并减少评估For expensive objectives, surrogate- and classification-assisted MOMMOPs are also popular and largely orthogonal to the above mechanisms. Classification/regression models can filter candidates and reduce evaluations(Zhang et al., 2015; Pan et al., 2018)，而克里金法和集成决策模型可以与参考向量引导或分解相结合，以提高鲁棒性和覆盖范围, while Kriging and ensemble decision models can be combined with reference-vector guidance or decomposition to improve robustness and coverage(Chugh et al., 2016; Lin et al., 2021; Sonoda and Nakata, 2022)。总体而言，MOMMOP研究大大提高了决策空间的多样性，但盆地结构的维持往往由局部几何线索或静态分区规则驱动，与演化循环紧密同步的明确的区域评估和预算分配仍然相对有限。. Overall, MOMMOP research has substantially improved decision-space diversity, yet the maintenance of basin structure is often driven by local geometric cues or static partition rules, and explicit region-wise valuation and budget allocation tightly synchronized with the evolutionary loop remain relatively limited.
