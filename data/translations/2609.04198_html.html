清洁工程，不稳定的测量：预先注册的可靠性 共享端点上黑盒 LLM 观察者的故障Clean Engineering, Unstable Measurement: A Preregistered Reliability Failure of Black-Box LLM Observers on Shared Endpoints

School of Electronic and Electrical Engineering, University of Sheffield, Sheffield S10 2TN, UK Jie Zhang jie.zhang@ranplanwireless.com

摘要Abstract

语言模型评委现在负责控制训练数据、评分生成并推动排行榜。法官是一个测量仪器，基于一个很少提及的假设：相同的请求，发送到相同的型号名称，明天读取相同的结果。我们在两个预先注册的活动中审核了这一假设，这些活动旨在测试观察者是否可以从部分推理痕迹中读取解决方案的进度；每个门槛都是预先确定的，并且两个活动都没有通过验证其工具。在 52,988 次经审核的请求尝试中，同一窗口重复排名一致为 Spearman 0.400，而要求的为 0.90；字节相同的次日重播则一致为 0.78，而要求的为 0.99。这些尝试是审核量，而不是样本量：分析基于 31 个有效任务组、100 个重播对、每个补充臂 10 个窗口以及 3,060 个构造错误判断。工程不是问题：交付、模式有效性、请求哈希和记录的元数据都处于上限。三种机制解释了这种差距：标签到含义的映射，它对读数的偏差与信号本身一样强烈；候选者比仪器本身的本底噪声低七个数量级；和字节相同的输入返回不同的排名，这是精确排列读数复合的噪声。度量替换和采样都无法在测试网格上修复它（748,000 次调用的模拟设计通过了 500 次中的 0 次）。预先注册的后续行动解决了这个问题。等待对采样的日子没有帮助（同日 0.805 相对于跨日 0.800；在接下来的五天内重复，一天的下限还更低）。切换提供商并没有帮助：三个司法管辖区的四家提供商共享发言权（中位数 0.74 到 0.88），这是根据他们公开的元数据字段预测的。批处理不变内核上的自托管仅在服务器安静时才有帮助：并发负载将分歧提高了 8.4 倍，回到了共享端点的程度。并且读数不是幅度计：对于具有已知间隙的构造错误，其间隔跟踪错误类型，而不是大小。我们将证据提炼成三级快照身份阶梯、八项设计规则和报告清单；大约占研究呼叫量 2% 的试点将提前暴露两个无法到达的大门。所有结果都涉及共享服务基础设施上的外部测量行为；他们只字不提模型的内部结构，也不评估任何提供商的服务质量。在共享端点上，型号名称不是冻结仪器；预先注册的评估必须在冻结其上的任何门之前测量其仪器。Language-model judges now gate training data, score generations, and drive leaderboards. The judge is then a measurement instrument, resting on one rarely stated assumption: the same request, sent to the same model name, reads the same tomorrow. We audited that assumption in two preregistered campaigns built to test whether an observer can read solution progress from partial reasoning traces; every threshold was fixed in advance, and neither campaign got past validating its instrument. Across 52,988 audited request attempts, same-window repeat rankings agreed at Spearman 0.400 against a required 0.90, and byte-identical next-day replays agreed at 0.78 against a required 0.99. Those attempts are audit volume, not sample size: the analyses rest on 31 valid task groups, 100 replay pairs, ten windows per supplementary arm, and 3,060 constructed-error judgments. The engineering was not the problem: delivery, schema validity, request hashes, and recorded metadata all sat at ceiling. Three mechanisms explain the gap: a label-to-meaning mapping that biased readouts as strongly as the signal itself; candidate gaps seven orders of magnitude below the instrument’s own noise floor; and byte-identical inputs returning different rankings, a noise that exact-permutation readouts compound. Neither metric substitution nor sampling repaired it on the tested grid (a 748,000-call simulated design passed 0 of 500 times). Preregistered follow-ups bound the problem. Waiting did not help on the days sampled (0.805 same-day versus 0.800 cross-day; replicated over five further days, one day’s own floor lower still). Switching providers did not help: four providers in three jurisdictions share the floor (medians 0.74 to 0.88), predicted by none of the metadata fields they expose. Self-hosting on batch-invariant kernels helped only while the server was quiet: concurrent load raised disagreement 8.4-fold, back to shared-endpoint magnitude. And the readout is no magnitude meter: on constructed errors with known gaps, its separation tracks error type, not size. We distill the evidence into a three-level snapshot-identity ladder, eight design rules, and a reporting checklist; a pilot at roughly 2% of the study’s call volume would have exposed both unreachable gates in advance. All results concern externally measured behaviour on shared serving infrastructure; they say nothing about model internals and do not assess any provider’s service quality. On a shared endpoint, a model name is not a frozen instrument; a preregistered evaluation must measure its instrument before freezing any gate on it.

语言模型现在位于自己领域的测量循环中：作为评委，他们对候选答案进行排名，对推理轨迹进行评分，对训练数据进行门控，并推动排行榜。使用模型作为工具带有一个很少被提及的基本假设：该工具是Language models now sit inside the measurement loop of their own field: as judges they rank candidate responses, score reasoning traces, gate training data, and drive leaderboards. Using a model as an instrument carries an assumption so basic it is rarely stated: that the instrument is稳定stable。相同的请求，发送到相同的型号名称，明天应该会读取相同的内容；冻结的评价标准应该测试所研究的系统而不是其测量装置的波动状态。在共享推理端点上，这种假设并非无害的。系统文献记录了原因：服务基础设施批量并发请求，通用推理内核不是批量不变的，并且部署在固定模型名称后面发生变化（§2）；并且这些在评估者可以记录的响应元数据中都是不可见的，因此黑盒研究可以观察由此产生的不稳定性，而无法对其进行归因。本文回答的问题是判断范式大多忽略的计量问题：. The same request, sent to the same model name, should read the same tomorrow; a frozen evaluation criterion should test the system under study rather than the fluctuating state of its measuring device. On shared inference endpoints this assumption is not innocent. The systems literature documents why: serving infrastructure batches concurrent requests, common inference kernels are not batch-invariant, and deployments change behind fixed model names (§2); and none of this is visible in the response metadata an evaluator can record, so a black-box study can observe the resulting instability without being able to attribute it. And when it does not, can the platform’s own metadata even reveal why?

对共享端点上的黑盒 LLM 观察员的两次预注册审计在其仪器门口终止，执行记录达到上限，科学结论远低于阈值。解剖结构紧凑。传输完美并不意味着测量可靠性 (C1)。这些失败分解为有偏差的读数、近乎退化的分数分离以及字节相同输入无法跨越的不稳定性，并通过排列读数放大为一个可观察量（C2）。度量替代并没有修复它们（C3），测试的采样网格上的比例也没有修复它们（C4）。即使指纹字段已填充且稳定（C5），平台自己的元数据也无法证明可重复性声明所需的内容。每种机制都转化为可执行的设计规则，总结为报告清单，其每个项目都可追溯到本文（C6）中的测量证据。这些补充强化了测试配置中的归因：历史重放分歧位于其端点的当天非确定性底线内，在第二个五天回合中复制，其中一天自己的底线脱颖而出（A-S1）；在所测量的所有四个提供商中，下限的大小相当，未通过任何公开的元数据修复（A-S2）；读数按类型而非大小区分构造误差 (A-S3)。跨轨 L2 臂从上方关闭梯子，仅在一个堆栈和一种负载模式上（第 8.3 节）：自托管批处理不变服务在安静时击败每个共享端点，无论如何都会失去准确性，并在并发负载下回落到共享端点范围。共享端点层是否源于批处理、内核调度或部署轮换仍然无法从外部识别：批处理压力已得到证明Two preregistered audits of black-box LLM observers on shared endpoints terminated at their instrument gates, with execution records at ceiling and scientific verdicts far below threshold. The dissection is compact. Transport perfection implies nothing about measurement reliability (C1). The failures decompose into a biased readout, near-degenerate score separations, and an instability that byte-identical inputs could not cross, amplified by a permutation readout into one observable (C2). Metric substitution did not repair them (C3), nor did scale on the tested sampling grid (C4). The platform’s own metadata cannot certify what reproducibility claims need, even when the fingerprint field is populated and stable (C5).
