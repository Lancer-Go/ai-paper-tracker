1 简介Introduction

面部再老化——合成个人面部的衰老或去衰老版本的任务——是计算机图形和视觉领域的一个复杂而引人注目的问题，在娱乐、取证和生物识别领域有多种应用。近年来，在生成对抗网络（GAN）等深度生成模型的进步的推动下，二维面部再老化方面取得了重大进展Face re-aging – the task of synthesizing aged or de-aged versions of an individual’s face – is a complex and compelling problem in computer graphics and vision, with diverse applications in entertainment, forensics, and biometrics. In recent years, there has been significant progress in 2D face re-aging, driven by advances in deep generative models such as generative adversarial networks (GANs)[goodfellow2014generative]和扩散模型and diffusion models[ho2020denoising]。这些方法通常将输入图像反转为预训练的 GAN 或扩散模型，并通过潜在操作执行重新老化. These approaches typically either invert the input image into a pretrained GAN or diffusion model and perform re-aging via latent manipulation[li2021continuous, yao2021high, duong2019automatic, harkonen2020ganspace, shen2020interpreting, sam, chen2023fading, gomez2022custom]，或者训练一个专门的网络来直接进行年龄转换, or train a dedicated network for direct age transformation[zoss2022fran]。后一种方法，与 FRAN. The latter approach, with FRAN[zoss2022fran]就是一个突出的例子，它通过避免潜在空间投影通常引入的身份失真来生成高质量的重新老化图像。being the prominent example, produces high-quality re-aged images by avoiding the identity distortion commonly introduced by latent space projection.

尽管取得了这些进步，但 3D 面部重新老化，旨在修改使用 3D 高斯分布 (3DGS) 等技术表示的 3D 面部模型的表观年龄Despite these advances, 3D face re-aging, which aims to modify the apparent age of a 3D face model represented using techniques such as 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)[3dgaussian2023]或神经辐射场 (NeRF)or neural radiance fields (NeRFs)[Mildenhall2020nerf]，仍然相对未经探索。将 2D 重新老化技术扩展到 3D 的一个关键挑战是确保多视图一致性。虽然不是专门为再老化而设计的，但最近的几种方法, remains relatively unexplored. A key challenge in extending 2D re-aging techniques to 3D is ensuring multi-view consistency. While not specifically designed for re-aging, several recent methods[haque2023nerf2nerf, igs2gs, chen2024gaussianeditor, chen2024dge, li2024syncnoise, wu2024gaussctrl, luo2024trame, wang2024vcgauss]使用预训练的 2D 扩散模型执行常规 3D 场景编辑。具体来说，这些方法通过使用扩散模型编辑渲染或输入的 2D 图像，然后使用编辑后的图像来优化 3D 模型。为了鼓励多视图一致性，它们通常依赖于扩散模型中的特征空间约束perform general 3D scene editing using pretrained 2D diffusion models. Specifically, these approaches operate by editing rendered or input 2D images using the diffusion model and then use the edited images to optimize a 3D model. To encourage multi-view consistency, they typically rely on either feature-space constraints from the diffusion model[chen2024dge, wu2024gaussctrl, luo2024trame, wang2024vcgauss]，受控噪声预测, controlled noise prediction[li2024syncnoise]，或优化过程中的具体策略和/或损失, or specific strategies and/or losses during optimization[haque2023nerf2nerf, igs2gs, chen2024gaussianeditor].

尽管这些技术在一般编辑方面取得了令人印象深刻的结果（例如，“把他变成小丑”），但它们很难产生详细且与年龄一致的重新老化模型。这主要是因为与年龄相关的线索（例如皱纹和皮肤纹理）很微妙，但在感知上至关重要，甚至视图中这些特征的微小不一致也可能导致 3D 外观过于平滑。此外，其中许多方法依赖于 InstructPix2Pix 等编辑模型Although these techniques achieve impressive results for general edits (e.g., “turn him into a clown”), they struggle to produce detailed and age-consistent re-aged models. 6 结论、局限性和未来的工作Conclusion, Limitations, and Future Work

我们提出了一种用于真实 3D 面部再老化的新颖框架，该框架可产生具有细粒度年龄相关细节的身份保留结果。我们方法的核心是基于扩散的二维再老化模型，We have presented a novel framework for realistic 3D face re-aging that produces identity-preserving results with fine-grained age-related details. At the core of our approach is a diffusion-based 2D re-aging model,差分反应DiffReaging，对合成生成的重新老化图像对进行训练。为了生成多视图一致的重新老化图像，我们引入了一种传播策略，该策略重新老化单个枢轴视图并通过扭曲重建剩余视图，以及我们提出的, trained on synthetically generated re-aged image pairs. To produce multi-view consistent re-aged images, we introduce a propagation strategy that re-ages a single pivot view and reconstructs the remaining views through warping and our proposed掩蔽差分检测Masked-DiffReaging机制。mechanism.掩蔽差分检测Masked-DiffReaging利用迭代性质leverages the iterative nature of差分反应DiffReaging确保重建区域和现有扭曲内容之间的一致性。此外，我们提出了一种从中心向外的重新老化方法，通过从同心层中的枢轴逐步扩展来避免重叠区域的冗余重建。生成的再老化图像监督再老化 3D 表示的优化，从而实现年龄一致且保留身份的 3D 再老化结果。大量实验表明，我们的方法在视觉和数字方面均优于现有的 3D 编辑方法。to ensure coherence between the reconstructed regions and existing warped content. Moreover, we propose a center-out re-aging method to avoid redundant reconstruction of overlapping regions by progressively expanding from the pivot in concentric layers. The resulting re-aged images supervise the optimization of the re-aged 3D representation, enabling age-consistent and identity-preserving 3D re-aged results. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our method outperforms existing 3D editing approaches both visually and numerically.
