摘要Abstract

在热力学平衡下对分子系统进行有效采样是统计物理学中的一个标志性挑战。这一挑战推动了玻尔兹曼生成器 (BG) 的发展，它可以通过将生成模型与精确似然和重要性采样校正相结合来快速生成不相关的平衡样本。然而，现代 BG 主要依赖于归一化流（NF），由于严格的可逆性约束（离散时间）或计算昂贵的似然性（连续时间），其表达能力受到限制。在本文中，我们提出了自回归玻尔兹曼生成器（ARBG）——一种新颖的自回归建模框架——它通过脱离基于流的 BG 范式克服了这些限制。 ARBG 规避了流的拓扑约束并支持顺序推理时间干预，同时通过利用大型语言模型中有效的架构来提供增强的可扩展性。我们凭经验证明，ARBG 在所有基准测试中都比基于流的模型带来了显着改进，特别是在较大的肽系统（例如 10 残基 Chignolin）中。此外，我们还引入了 ROBIN，这是一个使用 ARBG 框架训练的 1.32 亿参数可转移模型，它比之前的最先进技术有所改进，减少了零样本能量误差，Efficient sampling of molecular systems at thermodynamic equilibrium is a hallmark challenge in statistical physics. This challenge has driven the development of Boltzmann Generators (BGs), which allow rapid generation of uncorrelated equilibrium samples by combining a generative model with exact likelihoods and an importance sampling correction. However, modern BGs predominantly rely on normalizing flows (NFs), which either suffer from limited expressivity due to strict invertibility constraints (discrete time) or computationally expensive likelihoods (continuous time). In this paper, we propose AUTOREGRESSIVE BOLTZMANN GENERATORS (ARBG)—a novel autoregressive modelling framework—that overcomes these limitations by departing from the flow-based BG paradigm. ARBG circumvents the topological constraints of flows and enables sequential inference-time interventions, while offering enhanced scalability by leveraging architectures effective in Large Language Models. We empirically demonstrate that ARBG leads to significant improvements over flow-based models across all benchmarks, but particularly in larger peptide systems such as the 10-residue Chignolin. 3.2. ARBG 支持的工具3.2. Tools Enabled by ARBG

ARBG 的自回归分解解锁了一个大型推理时间干预工具包，而这些工具是基于流的方法无法同时生成所有数据维度的。通过将生成分解为每个条件的离散步骤，我们可以利用现代法学硕士的标准技术，例如用于多样性控制的温度缩放，并将其扩展到分子领域。至关重要的是，这种自回归结构允许中间干预；在实现完整的分子之前，我们可以分析、纠正或丢弃部分构象。我们继续通过粒度重采样方案来演示此功能。ARBG’s autoregressive factorization unlocks a large toolkit of inference-time interventions unavailable to flow-based approaches that generate all data dimensions concurrently. 五、相关工作5. Related Work

玻尔兹曼发生器Boltzmann Generators。随着玻尔兹曼生成器 (BG) 的引入，深度生成模型在平衡采样中的使用得到了普及（Noe´ et al., 2019）。大多数后续工作都集中在改进 BG 中使用的 NF 架构，以提高表达性（Zhai et al., 2025；Draxler et al., 2024）、稳定性（Schopmans & Friederich, 2025；von Klitzing et al., 2025）和泛化性（Klein & Noe, 2024）。 CNF 提供卓越的表达能力和易于处理对称性（Kohler 等人，2020；Klein 等人，2023b），但在推理过程中进行似然评估会产生很高的计算成本，这只能通过近似少步模型（Rehman 等人，2026a）、架构约束（Gloy 和 Olsson，2025）或学习能量函数（Aggarwal 等人）部分改善。等人，2025；Akhound-Sadegh 等人，2025）。. The use of deep generative models in equilibrium sampling was popularized with the introduction of Boltzmann Generators (BGs) (Noe´ et al., 2019). Most subsequent work has focused on refining the NF architecture used in BGs to improve expressivity (Zhai et al., 2025; Draxler et al., 2024), stability (Schopmans & Friederich, 2025; von Klitzing et al., 2025), and generalization (Klein & Noe, 2024). CNFs offer superior expressivity and easy handling of symmetries (Kohler et al.¨ , 2020; Klein et al., 2023b), but incur a high computational cost for likelihood evaluation during inference, which is only partially ameliorated with approximate few-step models (Rehman et al., 2026a), architectural constraints (Gloy & Olsson, 2025), or learned energy functions (Aggarwal et al., 2025; Akhound-Sadegh et al., 2025).

连续空间上的自回归模型Autoregressive Models on Continuous Spaces。一些工作研究了基于变压器的分子生成模型，包括潜在空间（Murtada 等人，2025）和基于流的（Cheng 等人，2025；Team 等人，2025）方法。虽然可以设计自回归 SE(3) 不变架构（Gebauer 等人，2019），但这些架构的扩展性不如纯粹基于 Transformer 的方法。混合密度网络（Bishop，1994）已经被探索了数十年，作为一种在连续空间上参数化输出同时提供精确密度的方法（Razavi 等人，2024）。这些已用于生成草图（Ha & Eck，2018）、图像（Salimans 等人，2017）、世界模型（Ha & Schmidhuber，2018）和需求预测（Li 等人，2025）等。 AR MDN 最近也被重新引入（Tschannen 等人，2024 年；Li 等人，2024 年；Billera 等人，2024 年）；然而，所有这些方法都侧重于提案质量，而不是询问玻尔兹曼生成的可能性的使用。此外，MDN 在历史上很容易在其混合物中丢失模式（Deng 等人，2022），导致性能不佳——表 1 中的分子也观察到这一点。. Several works have investigated transformer-based models for generating molecules, including latent space (Murtada et al., 2025) and flow-based (Cheng et al., 2025; Team et al., 2025) approaches. While it is possible to design an autoregressive SE(3) invariant architecture (Gebauer et al., 2019), these have not scaled as well as purely transformer-based methods. Mixture Density Networks (Bishop, 1994) have been explored for decades as a way to parametrize outputs over continuous spaces while providing exact densities (Razavi et al., 2024). These have been employed for generating sketches (Ha & Eck, 2018), images (Salimans et al., 2017), world models (Ha & Schmidhuber, 2018), and demand forecasting (Li et al., 2025), to name a few. AR MDNs have also recently been reintroduced (Tschannen et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Billera et al., 2024); however, all these methods focus on proposal quality, rather than interrogating the use of likelihoods for Boltzmann Generation. Moreover, MDNs have historically been prone to dropping modes in their mixtures (Deng et al., 2022), leading to suboptimal performance—as also observed for molecules in Table 1.

六、结论6. Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了自回归玻尔兹曼生成器（ARBG），这是一种新颖的玻尔兹曼生成器自回归模型，它为基于流的方法的主流范例提供了一种有前景的替代方案。特别是，ARBG 提供的新工具可以规避阻碍基于流的离散架构的表达性和效率限制，同时在似然估计方面比 CNF 具有更高的计算效率。重要的是，ARBG 享有与法学硕士可用的相同工具包，该工具包具有丰富的优化功能，可实现语言的缩放法则、令牌级别控制，我们首次在玻尔兹曼生成器框架内的分子设置中演示了这一点。In this work, we introduced AUTOREGRESSIVE BOLTZMANN GENERATORS (ARBG), a novel autoregressive model for Boltzmann Generators that offers a promising alternative to the dominant paradigm of flow-based approaches. In particular, ARBG offers new tools that circumvent the expressivity and efficiency constraints that hinder discrete flow-based architectures while being more computationally efficient at likelihood estimation than CNFs. Importantly, ARBG enjoys the same toolkit available to LLMs that comes with feature rich optimizations that enable scaling laws for language, token level-steering, which we demonstrated in the molecular setting for the first time within a Boltzmann Generator framework.

局限性Limitations。虽然 ARBG 为 BG 提供了一种截然不同的方法，但它也有一些明显的局限性。首先，AR 模型在维度上施加了特定的排序，而分子本身不具有自然排序，因此，这种选择可能会影响性能，例如在小分子中（Cheng et al., 2025）。其次，使用统一分箱将模型的精度限制在 Δ 范围内，这可能会对具有更清晰能量分布的更大系统构成挑战。最后，基于流的 BG 可以受益于信息先验的使用，例如 TFEP（Wirnsberger 等人，2020）；我们将针对 AR 模型的调查作为未来工作的方向。. While ARBG enables a drastically different approach to BGs, it comes with a few notable limitations. Firstly, AR models impose a specific ordering over dimensions, while molecules themselves do not possess a natural ordering and as a result, this choice may affect performance, e.g., in small molecules (Cheng et al., 2025). Secondly, the use of uniform binning bounds the precision of the model by ∆, which may pose challenges on even larger systems with sharper energy profiles. Finally, flow-based BGs can benefit from the use of informative priors, such as in TFEP (Wirnsberger et al., 2020); an investigation of which for AR models we leave as a direction for future work.

C.2.关于基于几何似然的度量在高维中的使用C.2. On the use of Geometric over Likelihood-based metrics in High-dimensions

在这项工作中，我们优先考虑基于 Wasserstein 的指标（TICA、Torus、Energy）而不是基于可能性的指标。虽然 ESS 是 SNIS 估计器效率的标准诊断，但它被广泛认为是高维空间中样本质量的潜在误导性代理。在高维空间中，重要性采样容易受到“维数灾难”的影响，称为In this work, we prioritize Wasserstein-based metrics (TICA, Torus, Energy) over likelihood-based metrics. While ESS is a standard diagnostic for the efficiency of SNIS estimators, it is widely recognized as a potentially misleading proxy for sample quality in high-dimensional spaces. In high-dimensional spaces, importance sampling is susceptible to the “curse of dimensionality”, referred to as体重崩溃weight collapse在粒子过滤文献中（Snyder et al., 2008）。随着维度的增加，建议分布和目标分布的典型集合之间的重叠呈指数级消失。因此，重要性权重的方差由落在小重叠区域的稀有样本主导。这会导致估计量方差爆炸，使 ESS 成为具有数百个自由度的系统（例如十肽）中不可靠的性能指标，因为该指标对全局缩放因子而不是局部模式覆盖变得敏感。in the particle filtering literature (Snyder et al., 2008). As the dimensionality increases, the overlap between the typical sets of the proposal and target distributions vanishes exponentially. Consequently, the variance of the importance weights becomes dominated by rare samples that land in the small region of overlap. This results in an estimator variance that explodes, rendering ESS an unreliable metric for performance in systems with hundreds of degrees of freedom (e.g., Decapeptides), as the metric becomes sensitive to global scaling factors rather than local mode coverage.

为了让我们的离散模型生成连续坐标，我们探索了三种不同的采样策略：For our discrete model to generate continuous coordinates, we explored three different sampling strategies:

  1. 从模型选择的离散箱中均匀采样。这是我们合理的默认选择，定义了 R 中的分段常数连续密度Sampling uniformly from the discrete bin selected by the model. This is our reasonable default choice and defines a piecewise-constant continuous density in R[d] .

  2. 使用箱的中心值。这种策略减少了生成过程中的一些变异性，并且可能使键长稍微更加均匀。如果有足够的垃圾箱，则没有必要这样做。根据经验，我们观察到这种去量化策略相对于从箱中均匀采样的好处很小。Using the center value of the bin. This strategy reduces a bit of variability from generation and may make bond lengths slightly more uniform. With enough bins, this is not necessary. Empirically, we observed small benefits for this de-quantization strategy over uniformly sampling from the bin.

  3. 使用所选分子的有偏训练数据分布来确定我们在推理时应用于所选箱的经验偏移。特别是对于较大的 bin 大小，我们可能能够在 bin 内拟合有关训练数据集的更有趣的分布。在这里，我们对箱内训练数据集的经验平均值使用狄拉克分布。我们发现这对性能有小幅提升，尤其是当训练数据集中时。Using the biased training data distribution for the chosen molecule to determine empirical offsets that we apply to the chosen bin at inference time. Especially for larger bin sizes, we may be able to fit more interesting distributions concerning the training dataset within the bin. Here, we use a Dirac distribution over the empirical mean of the training dataset within the bin. We find this gives a small boost in performance, especially when the training data is centered.

E.5.2。 KV缓存E.5.2. KV-CACHING

KV 缓存是一种在推理过程中使用的技术，它在解码过程中存储来自先前标记的注意键和值，以防止在每一步都重新计算它们（Pope 等人，2023）。通过缓存这些张量，模型避免了整个前缀上的冗余注意计算，降低了每个标记的复杂性并降低了推理时的延迟。因此，我们采用它来减少从平衡分布生成样本时的推理时间成本。KV-caching is a technique used during inference that stores the attention keys and values from previous tokens during decoding to prevent recomputing them at every step (Pope et al., 2023). By caching these tensors, the model avoids redundant attention computations over the entire prefix, reducing per-token complexity and lowering latency at inference time. Consequently, we adopt it to reduce inference time cost when generating samples from the equilibrium distribution.
