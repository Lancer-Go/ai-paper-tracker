辣椒（Chilli (辣椒Capsicum annuum）是印度最具经济意义的作物之一，但其生产力持续受到疾病的威胁，如果没有专家干预，这些疾病很难识别。虽然视觉变换器 (ViT) 已经实现了较高的分类精度，但其庞大的计算量使得在资源受限的设备上的部署具有挑战性。现有的压缩方法通常单独解决修剪、量化和知识蒸馏问题，而没有充分探索其组合应用程序的潜在好处和相互作用。我们提出了一个统一的 Vision Transformer 压缩框架，该框架结合了 Hessian 平衡自适应块修剪（H-BAC），以二阶灵敏度估计为指导，与量化和基于注意力的知识蒸馏相结合。为了系统地确定每个压缩系列中最有效的配置，首先通过受控消融研究对每种技术进行独立评估，然后将性能最佳的组件集成到针对现实世界农业限制量身定制的顺序部署管道中。在具有真正的跨村庄、跨设备分布外测试分割的 chili 3 级村庄分割数据集上，生成的压缩模型匹配或超过 95.13% FP32 基线的准确度，同时模型大小减少了 74-98%，并且完全集成的压缩管道实现了 54.5) is one of India’s most economically significant crops, yet its productivity is persistently threatened by diseases that are difficult to identify without expert intervention. While Vision Transformers (ViTs) have achieved high classification accuracy, their large computational footprint makes deployment on resource constrained devices challenging. We propose a unified Vision Transformer compression framework that combines Hessian-Balanced Adaptive Block Pruning (H-BAC), guided by second-order sensitivity estimation, with quantization and attention-based knowledge distillation. What makes this even more challenging is that many of these diseases look the same visually in the very early stages.     我们提出了 H-BAC，一种用于 Vision Transformers 的曲率感知自适应块剪枝方法，它将 Hutchinson 估计的块级 Hessian 曲率与每个块内基于一阶泰勒的分量剪枝相结合，据我们所知，这是第一个作为农业病害检测联合压缩管道的一部分进行评估的此类方法。We propose H-BAC, a curvature-aware adaptive block pruning method for Vision Transformers that combines Hutchinson-estimated block-level Hessian curvature with first-order Taylor-based component pruning within each block, and is, to our knowledge, the first such method evaluated as part of a joint compression pipeline for agricultural disease detection.

2.3 研究差距和我们的贡献Research gaps and our contributions

尽管取得了这些进展，但在农业环境中 ViT 的修剪、量化和蒸馏的系统协同设计方面仍然存在重大研究空白。大多数现有的压缩工作都是针对通用基准，而忽略了特定领域的挑战，例如不断变化的光照条件、不均匀的类别分布以及面向农民的工具进行解释的需要Despite these advancements, there is still a major research gap in the systematic co-design of pruning, quantization, and distillation for ViTs in agricultural contexts. Most existing compression works are aimed at general-purpose benchmarks and overlook domain-specific challenges such as changing light conditions, uneven class distributions, and the need for explanation in farmer-facing tools[27, 19]。此外，传统的 ViT 修剪技术对所有变压器块的一阶重要性评分相同，从而忽略了块级敏感性的显着差异。当教师和学生在隐藏维度上存在显着差异时，传统的基于特征的蒸馏同样会失败，因为线性适配器无法充分弥合特征空间不匹配。. Furthermore, conventional ViT pruning techniques score first-order importance identically for all transformer blocks, thereby neglecting significant differences in block-level sensitivity. Conventional feature-based distillation similarly breaks down when teacher and student differ substantially in hidden dimension, as linear adapters cannot adequately bridge the feature-space mismatch.

这项工作通过围绕 ViT 几何图形共同设计的压缩管道来解决这些差距，而不是独立方法的顺序应用。块级曲率间隙由 H-BAC 解决，它采用二阶 Hessian 曲率来分配变压器块之间的非均匀修剪率。基于注意力的 KD 差距是基于注意力的知识蒸馏方案的目标，该方案通过多头注意力图传输关系推理，完全绕过特征维度不兼容。最后，通过将 H-BAC、基于注意力的 KD 和 PTQ-Dynamic INT8 量化作为单个部署管道而不是作为独立技术一起评估来填补联合评估空白。This work addresses these gaps through a compression pipeline co-designed around ViT geometry rather than a sequential application of independent methods. The block-level curvature gap is addressed by H-BAC, which employs second-order Hessian curvature to assign non-uniform pruning rates across transformer blocks. The attention-based KD gap is targeted by an Attention-Based Knowledge Distillation scheme that transfers relational reasoning through multi-head attention maps, bypassing feature-dimension incompatibility entirely. Finally, the joint evaluation gap is filled by evaluating H-BAC, Attention-Based KD, and PTQ-Dynamic INT8 quantization together as a single deployment pipeline rather than as independent techniques.

我们提出了 Hessian 平衡自适应块修剪 (H-BAC)，这是一种曲率感知修剪方法，可减少 Vision Transformer FLOP，同时保持边缘部署的分类准确性。与之前在所有变压器块上应用统一剪枝的工作不同，H-BAC 结合了二阶 Hessian 曲率信息来跨块分配非均匀的曲率加权剪枝率，并使用一阶泰勒级数计算每个块内组件级剪枝的重要性。该方法经历四个阶段：通过 Hessian 迹估计获得分块曲率、根据曲率分配剪枝资源、对注意力头和 MLP 神经元执行结构剪枝、以及执行微调以恢复精度。We present Hessian-Balanced Adaptive Block Pruning (H-BAC), the curvature-aware pruning method that reduces Vision Transformer FLOPs while preserving classification accuracy for edge deployment. Unlike prior work that applies uniform pruning across all transformer blocks, H-BAC incorporates second-order Hessian curvature information to allocate non-uniform, curvature-weighted pruning rates across blocks, and calculates importance using first-order Taylor series for component-level pruning within each block. The method proceeds through four phases: obtaining block-wise curvature via Hessian trace estimation, assigning pruning resources based on curvature, executing structural pruning on attention heads and MLP neurons, and performing fine-tuning for accuracy recovery.

3.6 综合压缩管道Integrated Compression Pipeline

所提出的压缩框架遵循两阶段优化策略，旨在确保管道集成之前原则性的组件选择。 最初，通过受控消融实验独立评估三个压缩系列——修剪、量化和知识蒸馏。此步骤确定可实现的最大性能以及与每种单独技术的部署相关的副作用，从而可以对压缩系列进行公平比较，而不会受到混杂相互作用的影响。The proposed compression framework follows a two-stage optimization strategy designed to ensure principled component selection before pipeline integration. Initially, the three compression families – pruning, quantization, and knowledge distillation, are evaluated independently through controlled ablation experiments. This step determines the maximum performance achievable and side-effects related to the deployment for each individual technique, which allows for a fair comparison of compression families without the influence of confounding interactions.

PTQ-Dynamic 用于管道的最后量化阶段。量化之前的额外微调仅对尚未经过充分训练的检查点起作用，并且到达管道此阶段的每个检查点都已经具有 KD 的 15 个蒸馏时期。 PTQ-Dynamic 直接应用，无需添加训练步骤。基于注意力的 KD 因其转移关系推理的能力而被选为蒸馏策略。 在第二阶段，按顺序组合选定的组件以构建最终的部署就绪模型。全精度 ViT-B/16 教师经过 H-BAC 结构修剪，以减少其计算占用量，同时保留重要的变压器块。随后应用基于注意力的知识蒸馏，将冻结修剪的教师的推理模式传递给学生模型。最后，将 PTQ-Dynamic 量化直接应用于蒸馏后的学生，以获得适合在资源受限的硬件上部署的 INT8 精度模型。这种顺序组合确保在每个阶段之后，工作表示都被进一步压缩，并且压缩技术交互的处理是以受控和可再现的方式完成的，而不是忽略它们的相互依赖性的联合优化。PTQ-Dynamic is used for the pipeline’s final quantization stage. An extra fine-tuning pass before quantization only matters on checkpoints that have not yet had adequate training, and every checkpoint reaching this stage of the pipeline already has KD’s 15 distillation epochs. PTQ-Dynamic is applied directly with no added training step. Attention-Based KD is selected among distillation strategies for its capacity to transfer relational reasoning. In the second stage, the selected components are composed sequentially to construct the final deployment-ready model. The full-precision ViT-B/16 teacher undergoes H-BAC structural pruning to reduce its computational footprint while preserving important transformer blocks. Attention-Based Knowledge Distillation is subsequently applied, imparting the reasoning patterns of the frozen pruned teacher to the student model. Finally, PTQ-Dynamic quantization is applied directly to the distilled student to obtain an INT8-precision model suitable for deployment on resource-constrained hardware. This sequential composition ensures that after each stage, the working representation is further compressed and that the handling of compression technique interactions is done in a way that is controlled and reproducible rather than the joint optimization disregarding their interdependencies.
