摘要Abstract

强大的感知和推理需要感官模式的一致性。然而，当前的多模态模型经常违反这一原则，对同一概念的视觉表示和文本表示产生矛盾的预测。我们没有用标准的投票机制来掩盖这些失败——这会放大系统偏差——而是证明跨模式的不一致为学习提供了丰富的、自然的信号。我们介绍Robust perception and reasoning requires consistency across sensory modalities. Yet, current multimodal models often violate this principle, yielding contradictory predictions for visual versus textual representations of the same concept. Rather than masking these failures with standard voting mechanisms—which amplify systematic biases—we demonstrate that cross-modal inconsistency provides a rich, natural signal for learning. We introduce电阻-CR-C2，一个强化学习框架，通过强制执行跨模式循环一致性来解决内部冲突。通过要求模型执行后向推理、切换模态并通过前向推理可靠地重建答案，我们建立了密集的、无标签的奖励。这种循环约束迫使模型自动对齐其表示。优化此结构可减少特定于模态的错误，并将推理准确性提高多达 7.6 个点。我们的结果表明，高级推理不仅来自扩展数据，还来自对世界的结构一致的理解。, a reinforcement learning framework that resolves internal conflicts by enforcing cross-modal cycle consistency. By requiring a model to perform backward inference, switches modalities, and reliably reconstruct the answer via forward inference, we establish a dense, label-free reward. This cyclic constraint forces the model to autonomously align its representations. Optimizing for this structure mitigates modality-specific errors and improves reasoning accuracy by up to 7.6 points. Our results suggest that advanced reasoning emerges not just from scaling data, but from enforcing a structurally consistent understanding of the world.

3 方法Method

5 结论Conclusion

我们的工作表明，强大的多模态推理需要模型在模态之间实现严格的双向对齐。 我们从这个领域的一个关键失败开始——一种模态差距，MLLM 在同一内容的视觉视图和文本视图上相互矛盾。 我们的框架不仅仅是汇总相互冲突的选票，Our work shows that robust multimodal reasoning requires models to achieve a rigorous, bidirectional alignment between modalities. We start from a key failure in this area—a modality gap where MLLMs contradict themselves on visual versus textual views of the same content. Instead of merely aggregating conflicting votes, our framework,电阻-CR-C2，通过跨模式循环利用这一差距，这是自我奖励学习的强大的未开发资源。, exploits this gap via a cross-modal cycle, which is a powerful untapped resource for self-reward learning.电阻-CR-C2执行后向推理来假设查询、切换模态并执行前向推理来重建答案。通过教导模型解决其自身的内部冲突，performs backward inference to hypothesize a query, switches modalities, and performs forward inference to reconstruct the answer. By teaching the model to resolve its own internal conflicts,电阻-CR-C2导致推理准确性和跨模式对齐的显着提高。 我们的工作表明，下一代强大的多模态推理不仅会产生于扩展数据，而且会产生对世界更严格、双向的理解。leads to substantial improvements in both reasoning accuracy and cross-modal alignment.
