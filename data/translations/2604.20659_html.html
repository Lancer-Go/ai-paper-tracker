摘要Abstract

具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 通过利用直接结果验证而不是学习奖励模型，提高了大型语言模型 (LLM) 的推理能力。在此范式的基础上，组相对策略优化（GRPO）消除了对批评模型的需求，但会受到中间步骤不加区别的信用分配的影响，这限制了其识别有效推理策略的能力并导致过度思考。在这项工作中，我们通过探索模型在整个推理轨迹中对正确答案的信念，引入了一种无模型且可验证的过程监督。通过将生成分段为离散步骤并跟踪附加在每个分段边界的正确答案的条件概率，我们有效地计算可解释的分段进度测量，以完善 GRPO 的轨迹级反馈。这种方法可以实现更具针对性和样本效率的政策更新，同时避免了因昂贵的蒙特卡罗推出或辅助模型而产生的中间监督的需要。数学和通用领域基准的实验表明，在不同模型中，GRPO 均取得了一致的收益：数学任务的精度提高了 2.6 点，推理长度减少了 13.7%，通用领域任务的精度提高了 2.4 点，推理长度减少了 4%，表现出了很强的泛化能力。Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has advanced the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) by leveraging direct outcome verification instead of learned reward models. Building on this paradigm, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) eliminates the need for critic models but suffers from indiscriminate credit assignment for intermediate steps, which limits its ability to identify effective reasoning strategies and incurs overthinking. In this work, we introduce a model-free and verifiable process supervision via probing the model’s belief in the correct answer throughout its reasoning trajectory. By segmenting the generation into discrete steps and tracking the conditional probability of the correct answer appended at each segment boundary, we efficiently compute interpretable segment-wise progress measurements to refine GRPO’s trajectory-level feedback. This approach enables more targeted and sample-efficient policy updates, while avoiding the need for intermediate supervision derived from costly Monte Carlo rollouts or auxiliary models. Experiments on mathematical and general‑domain benchmarks show consistent gains over GRPO across diverse models: up to 2.6‑point accuracy improvements and 13.7% reasoning‑length reductions on math tasks, and up to 2.4 points and 4% on general‑domain tasks, demonstrating strong generalization.

1 简介Introduction

通过具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 进行改进Advanced by Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)(Shao et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2025a)，大型语言模型（LLM）在复杂的推理任务中表现出了非凡的能力，从数学问题解决到, Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in complex reasoning tasks, ranging from mathematical problem solving(OpenAI, 2024; Team et al., 2025; Shao et al., 2024)多跳问答to multi-hop question answering(Huang et al., 2025; Song et al., 2025)。 RLVR 的成功很大程度上归功于通过直接结果验证来计算奖励，而不是依赖于使训练流程复杂化且容易受到奖励黑客攻击的奖励模型. The success of RLVR is largely attributed to computing rewards via direct outcome verification, rather than relying on reward models that complicate the training pipeline and are prone to reward hacking(Yu et al., 2025a)。同样，组相对策略优化 (GRPO). In a similar vein, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)(Shao et al., 2024)消除了用于令牌级优势估计的批评模型，而是将轨迹级优势统一传播到中间步骤。虽然这种简化避免了训练批评家模型的挑战并减少了相关的开销，但不加区别的信用分配阻碍了样本效率并限制了策略模型学习有效推理策略的能力eliminates critic models for token-level advantage estimation, instead uniformly propagating trajectory-level advantages to intermediate steps. While this simplification avoids the challenges of training a critic model and reduces associated overhead, indiscriminate credit assignment hinders sample efficiency and limits the policy model’s ability to learn effective reasoning strategies(Qu et al., 2025).

为了解决这一限制，我们探索通过从带注释的最终答案得出的无模型、可验证的过程监督来增强 GRPO。我们的主要见解是，中间推理步骤的贡献可以通过相应断点处附加的参考答案的概率增量来探测。图中的观察结果支持了这一点To address this limitation, we explore enhancing GRPO with model-free, verifiable process supervision derived from the annotated final answer. Our key insight is that the contribution of intermediate reasoning steps can be probed by the probability increment of the reference answer appended at corresponding breakpoints. This is supported by observations in Figure1：（1）在宏观层面，平均探测概率随着推理的进行而增加，最终达到正确答案的轨迹趋势更加明显； (2)在微观层面上，降低探测概率的推理片段往往质量较低。基于这些观察，我们的方法利用模型自身的推理轨迹来生成本地化的监督信号，从而实现更有针对性和更有效的政策更新。具体来说，我们将模型的响应分割为离散的推理部分，并在每个部分边界策略性地连接正确的最终答案。通过提取模型在这些位置的正确答案的条件概率，我们获得了其不断演变的信念状态的代理。相邻片段之间的这些概率的差异充当分段监督信号，补充轨迹级优势并量化每个推理片段对最终结果的贡献。: (1) at the macro level, the average probed probability increases as reasoning progresses, with a more pronounced trend for trajectories that ultimately reach the correct answer; and (2) at the micro level, reasoning segments that reduce the probed probability tend to be of low quality. Based on these observations, our method leverages the model’s own reasoning trace to generate localized supervision signals, enabling more targeted and effective policy updates. Specifically, we segment the model’s response into discrete reasoning segments and strategically concatenate the correct final answer at each segment boundary. By extracting the model’s conditional probability of the correct answer at these positions, we obtain a proxy for its evolving belief state. The differences in these probabilities between adjacent segments serve as segment-wise supervision signals, complementing trajectory-level advantages and quantifying the contribution of each reasoning segment toward the final outcome.

这种方法有两个主要优点：(1) 提供与模型内部决策流程一致的密集、可解释的反馈；(2) 避免对辅助模型的依赖This approach offers two key benefits: (1) it provides dense, interpretable feedback aligned with the model’s internal decision flow and (2) it avoids reliance on auxiliary models(Schulman et al., 2017; Zha et al., 2025; Cui et al., 2025; He et al., 2024b)或蒙特卡罗推出or Monte Carlo rollouts(Qu et al., 2025; Dai et al., 2025)，确保高效率和可扩展性，并遵循 RLVR 和 GRPO 制定的设计原则。通过这种细粒度的监督机制，我们的目标是提高强化学习训练的样本效率，为学习更有效和高效的推理行为铺平道路。, ensuring high efficiency and scalability and adhering to the design principles established by RLVR and GRPO. Through this fine-grained supervision mechanism, we aim to enhance the sample efficiency of RL training, paving the way for learning more effective and efficient reasoning behaviors.

组相关策略优化 (GRPO)Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)。 具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为微调 LLM 的重要范例，它使用来自基于规则的验证者的明确信号来规避对昂贵且可能存在偏见的奖励模型的需求. Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has become a prominent paradigm for fine-tuning LLMs, using definitive signals from rule-based verifiers to circumvent the need for costly and potentially biased reward models(Shao et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2025a)。在这个范式中，GRPO. Within this paradigm, GRPO(Shao et al., 2024)为 PPO 等基于 Critic 的算法提供轻量级且高效的替代方案offers a lightweight and efficient alternative to critic-based algorithms like PPO(Schulman et al., 2017)。通过比较一组采样轨迹的最终结果，GRPO 消除了对单独价值网络的需求。然而，这种简化是以不加区别的信用分配为代价的：单一的轨迹级奖励被统一传播到所有中间代币。这可能会无意中强化成功轨迹中的虚假推理步骤，或惩罚失败轨迹中有希望的部分逻辑。我们的工作通过引入细粒度的流程监督机制来解决这一限制，在不牺牲其轻量级性质的情况下增强其信用分配能力。. By comparing final outcomes across a group of sampled trajectories, GRPO eliminates the need for a separate value network. However, this simplification comes at the cost of indiscriminate credit assignment: a single, trajectory-level reward is uniformly propagated to all intermediate tokens. This can inadvertently reinforce spurious reasoning steps in a successful trajectory or penalize promising partial logic in a failed one. Our work addresses this limitation by introducing a fine-grained process supervision mechanism, enhancing its credit assignment capabilities without sacrificing its lightweight nature.

Recent work has explored injecting fine-grained supervision into the reasoning process to better guide long-form generation. This makes our approach more efficient while still providing the benefits of fine-grained, verifiable process feedback.

3 方法论：从可验证的结果进行过程监督Methodology: Process Supervisions from Verifiable Outcomes

在 GRPO 下培训的法学硕士仍然受到不加区别的学分分配的影响，基于结果的奖励稀疏，无法指导中间推理步骤。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一个可验证的流程监督框架，通过细粒度的信用分配来增强 GRPO。我们首先介绍一种分段策略，该策略使用标记级熵来识别高不确定性转换，并将轨迹划分为语义上有意义的推理步骤（第 1 节）LLMs trained under GRPO still suffer from indiscriminate credit assignment, where sparse outcome-based rewards fail to guide intermediate reasoning steps. To address this, we present a verifiable process supervision framework for enhancing GRPO with fine-grained credit assignment. We first introduce a segmentation strategy that uses token-level entropy to identify high-uncertainty transitions and partition trajectories into semantically meaningful reasoning steps (Section3.1）。然后，我们引入分段进度估计，根据模型置信度的变化来量化每个推理分段的贡献（第 1 节）). We then introduce segment-wise progress estimation to quantify the contribution of each reasoning segment based on changes in model confidence (Section3.2）。最后，我们将这种本地化反馈纳入 GRPO 的代币级更新中，形成融合基于结果和过程级信号的混合优势（第 1 节）). Finally, we incorporate this localized feedback into GRPO’s token-level updates, forming a hybrid advantage that fuses outcome-based and process-level signals (Section3.3).

我们提出了 GRPO-VPS，这是一种无模型和无验证者的方法，它通过从条件答案概率派生的分段级信用分配来增强 GRPO。我们的方法生成与模型内部决策流一致的密集且可解释的监督信号，从而实现更高效、更有针对性的政策优化。对四个数学推理基准的实验表明，与强大的 RL 基线和分段感知方法相比，我们的方法持续提高了准确性和推理简洁性，且无需依赖辅助模型或昂贵的部署。此外，对通用推理基准的广泛评估证实了该方法强大的泛化能力，证明了在稳健的多领域推理任务中的一致收益。这些发现强调了免验证者、信心驱动的奖励作为大型语言模型中未来对齐和推理优化的可扩展方向的潜力。We present GRPO-VPS, a model-free and verifier-free method that augments GRPO with segment-level credit assignment derived from conditional answer probabilities. Our method generates dense and interpretable supervision signals aligned with the model’s internal decision flow, enabling more efficient and targeted policy optimization. Experiments on four math reasoning benchmarks show that our method consistently improves both accuracy and reasoning conciseness over strong RL baselines and segment-aware methods, without relying on auxiliary models or costly rollouts. Furthermore, extensive evaluations on general reasoning benchmarks confirm the method’s strong generalization capabilities, demonstrating consistent gains in robust, multi-domain reasoning tasks. These findings underscore the potential of verifier-free, confidence-driven rewards as a scalable direction for future alignment and reasoning optimization in large language models.
