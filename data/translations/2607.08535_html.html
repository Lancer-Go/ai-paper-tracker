当法官改变时，衡量标准也会改变：审计法学硕士作为法官的可靠性When the Judge Changes, So Does the Measurement: Auditing LLM-as-Judge Reliability

Zongyou Yang Dyson School of Design Engineering, Imperial College LondonLondonUnited Kingdom zy2926@ic.ac.uk , Yinghan Hou Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College LondonLondonUnited Kingdom yh24@ic.ac.uk and Xiaokun Yang School of Electronic Information, Nanchang Institute of TechnologyNanchangChina yangxk@bupt.cn
摘要。Abstract.

即使候选人的回答保持不变，法学硕士作为评判的分数也可能会发生变化，仅仅是因为评估者发生了变化。我们将这种评估者替换的模糊性视为测量有效性问题。在四个判断数据集上，我们比较了实践中可用的两种升级路径：将 Qwen3 密集判断从 1.7B 参数扩展到 32B 参数，以及跨 MiniMax M2–M2.7 发布的 API 进行移动。主要模式是判断升级不可互换：仅限Qwen3 1.7BAn LLM-as-judge score can move even when the candidate responses stay fixed, simply because the evaluator has changed. We treat this evaluator-replacement ambiguity as a measurement-validity problem. Across four judgment datasets, we compare two upgrade paths available in practice: scaling Qwen3 dense judges from 1.7B to 32B parameters and moving across MiniMax M2–M2.7 released APIs. The main pattern is that judge upgrades are not interchangeable: only Qwen3 1.7B→\rightarrow4B 提供了强大的相邻增益，而 MiniMax 相邻版本则没有。更强的法官会减少但不会消除立场和冗长的偏见。当错误相关时，重复样本陪审团几乎不会增加任何内容。结构化辩论可以极大地改变决策，但如果没有解析器和后备日志，这些转变就不能归因于深思熟虑。我们认为，法学硕士作为法官的报告应包括数据集切片、偏差调查、错误依赖性估计和协议审计跟踪。4B gives a robust adjacent gain, while MiniMax adjacent releases do not. Stronger judges reduce but do not remove position and verbosity bias. Repeated-sample juries add little when errors are correlated. Structured debate can move decisions substantially, but without parser and fallback logs those shifts cannot be attributed to deliberation. We argue that LLM-as-judge reports should include dataset slices, bias probes, error-dependence estimates, and protocol audit trails.

1. 简介Introduction

法学硕士作为法官的评估现在经常被用作模型质量的衡量工具。困难在于仪器本身就是一个模型。当系统的分数在更换评估器后发生变化时，这种变化是不明确的：它可能表明新的评估器更有能力，它有不同的偏见，它在基准的不同部分失败，或者评估管道以不同的方式解析和聚合其输出。这种歧义并不是一个小的实现细节。它决定了法学硕士作为法官的分数是否可以被解释为有关候选系统的证据。LLM-as-judge evaluation is now often used as a measuring instrument for model quality. The difficulty is that the instrument is itself a model. When a system’s score changes after replacing the evaluator, the change is ambiguous: it may indicate that the new judge is more capable, that it is biased differently, that it fails on a different slice of the benchmark, or that the evaluation pipeline parsed and aggregated its outputs differently. This ambiguity is not a minor implementation detail. It determines whether an LLM-as-judge score can be interpreted as evidence about the candidate systems at all.

我们称之为We call this the评价者替换歧义evaluator-replacement ambiguity：当更换法官后测量的偏好结果发生变化时，仅从准确性无法识别变化的来源。本文通过评估实践中可用的两种可观察的干预措施来研究模糊性。第一个是参数缩放决策，以 Qwen3 密集判断从 1.7B 到 32B 参数为代表: when the measured preference outcome changes after replacing the judge, the source of the change is not identifiable from accuracy alone. This paper studies the ambiguity through two observable interventions available in evaluation practice. The first is a parameter-scaling decision, represented by Qwen3 dense judges from 1.7B to 32B parameters(Yang and others, 2025)。第二个是发布模型升级路径，以评估为已发布的 MiniMax M2–M2.7 API 为代表. The second is a released-model upgrade path, represented by MiniMax M2–M2.7 APIs evaluated as released(MiniMax, 2026)。这些轴是证据来源，而不是本文的解释对象。 MiniMax M2 报告记录了已发布的系列及其面向代理的训练管道，但它并没有使我们的 API 序列成为受控消融；因此，本文并未对 MiniMax 内部结构做出因果断言。. These axes are evidence sources rather than the paper’s object of explanation. The MiniMax M2 report documents the released series and its agent-oriented training pipeline, but it does not make our API sequence a controlled ablation; consequently, this paper does not make a causal claim about MiniMax internals.

这个表述引出了三个研究问题。 RQ1询问判断可靠性是否沿着参数轴和发布模型升级路径得到类似的提高。如果评估者能力是主导因素，则两个轴都应在数据集中显示一致的正相邻增益；如果可靠性取决于工作负载，则增益应因数据集而异，并且重要性不应统一转移。 RQ2 询问更高的总体准确度是否也会减少已知的判断偏差。能力较高的法官应该对偏差不太敏感，但非零翻转率将表明能力并不能消除测量伪影。 RQ3 询问陪审团和辩论等协议级别的升级是否会改变单一法官扩展之外的可靠性。当错误相关性很高时，陪审团的收益应该受到限制，而辩论的转变应该取决于能力不对称和协议的可审计性。This formulation leads to three research questions. RQ1 asks whether judge reliability improves similarly along a parameter axis and a released-model upgrade path. If evaluator capability is the dominant factor, both axes should show consistently positive adjacent gains across datasets; if reliability is workload-dependent, gains should vary by dataset and significance should not transfer uniformly. RQ2 asks whether higher aggregate accuracy also reduces known judge biases. Higher-capability judges should be less bias-sensitive, but nonzero flip rates would show that capability does not eliminate measurement artifacts. RQ3 asks whether protocol-level upgrades, such as juries and debate, change reliability beyond single-judge scaling. Jury gains should be limited when error correlation is high, while debate shifts should depend on capability asymmetry and protocol auditability.

中心论点是评估者的能力很重要但不完整：同一位法官在工作负载、偏见调查、相关投票和协议实现方面可能看起来有所不同。The central thesis is that evaluator capability is important but incomplete: the same judge can look different across workloads, bias probes, correlated votes, and protocol implementations.

本文为法学硕士作为法官的可靠性提供了一个可审计的测量框架，将模型能力与工作负载依赖性、偏差行为、相关投票和测量工件分开。首先，它提出了一项两轴评估器研究，将参数缩放与发布的模型生成进行比较，同时明确避免有关 MiniMax 内部的因果声明。其次，它将可靠性分解为单个法官的准确性、偏见调查、相关陪审团错误和辩论可审核性。第三，它提炼出报告标准：基准切片、解析共享测试、A/B 随机化、This paper contributes an auditable measurement framework for LLM-as-judge reliability, separating model capability from workload dependence, bias behavior, correlated voting, and measurement artifacts. First, it presents a two-axis evaluator study that compares parameter scaling with released-model generation while explicitly avoiding causal claims about MiniMax internals. Second, it decomposes reliability into single-judge accuracy, bias probes, correlated jury errors, and debate auditability. Third, it distills a reporting standard: benchmark slices, parse-shared tests, A/B randomization,ρ\rho估计和协议日志。estimates, and protocol logs.

该研究得出三个主要结果。首先，判断升级不可互换：参数缩放会产生强大的低端增益，而相邻的 MiniMax 版本则不会。其次，更高的准确性会减少但不能消除偏差。第三，聚合协议只有在其假设可审计时才可解释：陪审团投票受到相关错误的限制，并且辩论的转变不能归因于没有解析器日志的审议。The study yields three main results. First, judge upgrades are not interchangeable: parameter scaling produces one robust low-end gain, while adjacent MiniMax releases do not. Second, higher accuracy reduces but does not remove bias. Third, aggregation protocols are interpretable only when their assumptions are auditable: jury voting is bounded by correlated errors, and debate shifts cannot be attributed to deliberation without parser logs.

2. 相关工作Related Work

LLM评委和偏好评估。LLM judges and preference evaluation.MT-Bench、Chatbot Arena、PandaLM、JudgeLM、G-Eval、Auto-J、Prometheus 和 RewardBench 帮助建立了 LLM 法官作为开放式生成的实用评估者MT-Bench, Chatbot Arena, PandaLM, JudgeLM, G-Eval, Auto-J, Prometheus, and RewardBench have helped establish LLM judges as practical evaluators for open-ended generation(Zheng et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023c; Zhu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023; Kim et al., 2024a, b; Lambert et al., 2024)。这些研究表明，法学硕士法官可以与人类偏好相关联，但这种相关性还取决于任务、提示、评分量表和模型系列。我们的工作遵循这一经验传统，但将法官本身视为尺度分析的对象。差距在于，之前的报告通常将评估器性能呈现为一维模型排序，从而不清楚收益是否来自能力、基准切片、偏差减少或协议设计。. These studies show that LLM judges can correlate with human preferences, but also that correlations depend on task, prompt, scoring scale, and model family. Our work follows this empirical tradition but treats the judge itself as the object of scaling analysis. The gap is that prior reports often present evaluator performance as a one-dimensional model ordering, leaving unclear whether gains arise from capability, benchmark slice, bias reduction, or protocol design.

法官偏见和协议效应。Judge bias and protocol effects.先前的工作记录了位置偏差、冗长偏差、对评级格式的敏感性以及评估设计选择引起的可靠性变化Prior work documents position bias, verbosity bias, sensitivity to rating format, and reliability changes caused by evaluation design choices(Dubois et al., 2024; Saito et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023a; Yamauchi et al., 2025)。这些影响很重要，因为它们可以在不改变候选人反应的情况下改变排名。尚未明确的是偏差如何与评估者缩放共同变化，而不是在孤立的即时扰动下变化。与完整的提示因子研究不同，我们的实验使用固定的主提示并将提示敏感性视为稳健性检查；主要关注点是模型能力、偏见、聚合和辩论如何在受控报告协议下相互作用。. These effects matter because they can change rankings without changing candidate responses. What remains under-specified is how bias changes jointly with evaluator scaling rather than under isolated prompt perturbations. Unlike a full prompt-factorial study, our experiments use a fixed main prompt and treat prompt sensitivity as a robustness check; the main focus is how model capability, bias, aggregation, and debate interact under a controlled reporting protocol.

聚合和辩论。Aggregation and debate.古典孔多塞式的结果预测独立选民的收益，而对相关选民的研究表明，依赖性会急剧减少这些收益Classical Condorcet-style results predict gains from independent voters, while work on correlated voters shows that dependence can sharply reduce those gains(de Condorcet, 1785; Ladha, 1992)。在 LLM 评估中，自我一致性、多代理辩论和 ChatEval 风格的协议提供了类似的聚合或审议途径. In LLM evaluation, self-consistency, multi-agent debate, and ChatEval-style protocols offer analogous routes to aggregation or deliberation(Wang et al., 2023b; Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Chan et al., 2023; Khan et al., 2024)。现有的研究通常强调最终的准确性；我们的重点是这种收益所需的聚合假设是否在经验上得到满足。我们的陪审团实验直接估计误差相关性，我们的辩论实验被报告为可审计性案例研究。. Existing studies often emphasize final accuracy; our focus is whether the aggregation assumptions required for such gains are empirically satisfied. Our jury experiment directly estimates error correlation, and our debate experiment is reported as an auditability case study.

5. 讨论Discussion

原则。Principle.仅当联合报告评估的切片、扰动灵敏度、误差依赖性和协议状态时，判断分数才是可解释的。因此，实际建议很简单：法学硕士作为法官的结果不应被视为可靠，除非它报告了对哪个切片进行了判断，法官在简单扰动下的偏见程度，重复判断的相关性如何，以及协议级决策是否可审计。A judge score is interpretable only when the evaluated slice, perturbation sensitivity, error dependence, and protocol state are jointly reported. The practical recommendation is therefore simple: an LLM-as-judge result should not be accepted as reliable unless it reports what slice was judged, how biased the judge was under simple perturbations, how correlated repeated judgments were, and whether protocol-level decisions were auditable.

结果为法学硕士法官评估提供了两个方法学教训。首先，判断缩放应报告为设计空间而不是单个评估者排序。在此面板中，最清晰的参数轴增益出现在低端，而 MiniMax 释放生成轴在测量的相邻比较中变化很小。这不应被理解为声称所有版本都缺乏有意义的收益，或者 MiniMax 版本隔离了一个训练阶段。支持的结论更窄：在相同的数据集和提示下，观察到的两条升级路径具有不同的相邻对模式。The results suggest two methodological lessons for LLM-as-judge evaluation. First, judge scaling should be reported as a design space rather than a single evaluator ordering. In this panel, the clearest parameter-axis gain occurs at the low end, while the MiniMax release-generation axis changes little in the measured adjacent comparisons. This should not be read as a claim that all release generations lack meaningful gains, or that the MiniMax releases isolate one training stage. The supported conclusion is narrower: under the same datasets and prompts, the two observed upgrade paths have different adjacent-pair patterns.

其次，可靠性报告必须超越单一数字的准确性，并指定分析单位。偏见调查表明，更强的法官可以不那么脆弱，但它们也表明偏见仍然是可以衡量的。陪审团实验表明，聚合增益取决于误差相关性。辩论实验表明，协议变化可能大到足以与扩展效应相媲美，但前提是实现保留解析失败、平局打破和弃权的审计跟踪。对于成对数据集，这意味着可解析子集定义、解析共享显着性测试和 A/B 随机化。对于陪审团来说，这意味着报告Second, reliability reporting has to go beyond single-number accuracy and name the unit of analysis. Bias probes show that stronger judges can be less brittle, but they also show that bias remains measurable. Jury experiments show that aggregation gains depend on error correlation. Debate experiments show that protocol changes can be large enough to rival scaling effects, but only if the implementation preserves an audit trail for parse failures, tie-breaking, and abstentions. For pairwise datasets, this means parseable-subset definitions, parse-shared significance tests, and A/B randomization. For juries, it means reportingρ\rho除了in addition toKK。对于辩论或多代理协议，这意味着在解释最终分数变化之前存储原始响应、解析器状态和每个中间投票。这些要求操作起来很简单，但如果没有这些要求，法官的表面可靠性可能会成为评估流程的伪影。. For debate or multi-agent protocols, it means storing raw responses, parser status, and every intermediate vote before interpreting final-score shifts. These requirements are operationally simple, but without them the apparent reliability of a judge can be an artifact of the evaluation pipeline.

6. 有效性的威胁Threats to Validity

构建效度。Construct validity.可靠性通过选定的代理来实现：准确性和一致性、偏差探测、错误关联和可审核性日志。这些并没有穷尽所有可靠性维度，例如校准、长格式基本原理质量或特定领域的一致性。Reliability is operationalized through selected proxies: accuracy and agreement, bias probes, error correlation, and auditability logs. These do not exhaust all reliability dimensions, such as calibration, long-form rationale quality, or domain-specific consistency.

内部效度。Internal validity.Qwen3 轴更接近参数扫描，而 MiniMax 轴是观察到的释放序列而不是受控消融。辩论实验尤其受到缺少原始输出和解析成功日志的限制。The Qwen3 axis is closer to a parameter sweep, whereas the MiniMax axis is an observed release sequence rather than a controlled ablation. The debate experiment is especially limited by missing raw outputs and parse-success logs.

统计结论的有效性。Statistical conclusion validity.McNemar 测试是相邻对测试，不构成正式的轴间测试。多重比较是通过 Holm 校正来控制的，但样本大小和解析器覆盖范围因数据集而异。McNemar tests are adjacent-pair tests and do not constitute a formal between-axis test. Multiple comparisons are controlled through Holm correction, but sample sizes and parser coverage differ across datasets.

外部效度。External validity.结果以两个模型系列、两个参考判断、四个数据集、少量提示和每个实验的一个主要解码机制为条件。在将这些观察结果转化为一般的法官选择规则之前，需要额外的领域、语言和指令分布。Results are conditioned on two model families, two reference judges, four datasets, a small number of prompts, and one primary decoding regime per experiment. Additional domains, languages, and instruction distributions are needed before turning these observations into general judge-selection rules.

7. 结论Conclusion

本文提出了法学硕士作为法官可靠性的两轴实证研究。结果表明，评估者能力很重要，但并不完整：可靠性还受到数据集切片、偏差行为、相关错误和协议实现的影响。在测试的面板中，最强的鲁棒相邻效应是Qwen3 1.7B到4B的过渡； MiniMax 相邻释放步骤不重要；多数票评审团受到高误差相关性的限制；具有强参考性的辩论会极大地改变结果，但在当前日志下缺乏足够的证据来解释因果关系。这些发现将法学硕士作为法官的可靠性重新定义为测量有效性问题，而不仅仅是模型选择问题。This paper presents a two-axis empirical study of LLM-as-judge reliability. The results show that evaluator capability is important but incomplete: reliability is also shaped by dataset slice, bias behavior, correlated errors, and protocol implementation. In the tested panel, the strongest robust adjacent effect is the Qwen3 1.7B to 4B transition; no MiniMax adjacent release step is significant; majority-vote juries are limited by high error correlation; and debate with a strong reference changes outcomes substantially but lacks sufficient evidence for causal interpretation under the current logs. These findings recast LLM-as-judge reliability as a measurement-validity problem rather than a model-selection problem alone.
