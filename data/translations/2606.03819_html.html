2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 推测性解码Speculative Decoding

推测解码是一种无损范例，用于加速大型语言模型中的自回归生成。 早期工作采用小型、独立的语言模型作为起草者来提出候选标记，随后由目标模型并行验证Speculative decoding is a lossless paradigm for accelerating autoregressive generation in large language models. Early work employs a small, separate language model as a drafter to propose candidate tokens that are subsequently verified by the target model in parallelLeviathan et al. (2023).

这方面研究的一个关键进展是基于树的推测解码，其中起草者构建候选延续树而不是单个序列，从而大大提高了接受率。 早期的理论处理得出复杂的令牌接受标准，以最佳地利用起草的树A key advancement in this line of research is tree-based speculative decoding, in which the drafter constructs a tree of candidate continuations rather than a single sequence, substantially improving acceptance rates. Early theoretical treatments derive sophisticated token-acceptance criteria to optimally exploit the drafted treeMiao et al. (2024); Sun et al. (2023)。 最近最先进的方法发现，简单的基于等式的验收检查在实践中就足够了，因为它允许有效的非随机树构建策略. More recent state-of-the-art methods find that a simple equality-based acceptance check is sufficient in practice, as it admits efficient non-stochastic tree construction strategiesWang et al. (2025); Li et al. (2024).

除了树推测解码的改进之外，还付出了相当大的努力来降低起草者本身的成本。 虽然早期的工作依赖于完全独立的小型自回归模型，但最近的方法用轻量级模型取代了这些模型，这些模型重用了验证者的中间隐藏状态Alongside improvements to tree speculative decoding, considerable effort has been directed at reducing the cost of the drafter itself. Whereas early work relies on entirely separate small autoregressive models, more recent approaches replace these with lightweight models that reuse intermediate hidden states from the verifierLi et al. (2025b)。 然而，此类模型仍然从根本上受到自回归解码的限制，从而限制了起草序列的长度。. Nevertheless, such models remain fundamentally constrained by autoregressive decoding, limiting the length of the drafted sequence.

为了克服这个瓶颈，一些工作已经探索了完全并行的草稿生成。 Medusa 和 Hydra 将线性预测头直接连接到验证者主干上，以同时预测未来的代币To overcome this bottleneck, several works have explored fully parallel draft generation. Medusa and Hydra attach linear prediction heads directly to the verifier backbone to predict future tokens simultaneouslyCai et al. (2024); Ankner et al. (2024)。 最近，扩散模型被提议作为起草者. More recently, diffusion models have been proposed as draftersLi et al. (2025a); Sandler et al. (2025)。 虽然扩散方法消除了草稿生成的顺序性质，但它们仍然需要多次迭代才能产生最终草稿。. While diffusion methods eliminate the sequential nature of draft generation, they still require multiple iterations to produce a final draft.

一个补充方向 TiDAR 将并行绘图直接纳入模型预训练目标中，使单个模型能够并行生成草图标记并以自回归方式验证它们，相对于标准自回归模型，没有观察到输出质量下降A complementary direction, TiDAR, incorporates parallel drafting directly into the model pretraining objective, enabling a single model to generate draft tokens in parallel and verify them autoregressively, with no observed degradation in output quality relative to standard autoregressive modelsLiu et al. (2025).

5 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 TreeFlash，这是一种通过结合轻量级 AR 近似机制来改进单次分块绘图的方法，该机制可调节前一个标记上的每个绘图位置，从而保留This paper presents TreeFlash, a method to improve one-shot block-wise drafting by incorporating a lightweight AR-approximation mechanism that conditions each draft position on the preceding token, preserving the𝒪​(1)\mathcal{O}(1)解码并行绘图员的复杂性，同时解决随绘图深度而增长的分配差距。 TreeFlash 在广泛的任务、模型大小和解码机制上比 DFlash 和 DDTree 实现了一致且显着的性能改进，通过以下方式提高了块效率：decoding complexity of parallel drafters while addressing the distributional gap that grows with draft depth. TreeFlash achieves consistent and substantial performance improvements over both DFlash and DDTree across a wide range of tasks, model sizes, and decoding regimes, improving block efficiency by+12.4%+12.4\%在完全边际分布构建的树上。over trees constructed with fully marginal distributions.

局限性Limitations

TreeFlash 展示了所有评估任务、模型大小和解码机制的一致改进。TreeFlash demonstrates consistent improvements across all evaluated tasks, model sizes, and decoding regimes.

所有实验均使用 SDPA 注意力在单批次设置中进行。 然而，生产服务系统通常包含一系列优化，例如量化、多批次解码和自定义注意内核，这些优化可能与大树大小和动态树拓扑发生冲突All experiments are conducted in a single-batch setting using SDPA attention. Production serving systems, however, typically incorporate a range of optimizations, such as quantization, multi-batch decoding, and custom attention kernels, that can conflict with large tree sizes and dynamic tree topologiesDao et al. (2022); Lin et al. (2024); Kwon et al. (2023)。 本工作中未评估 TreeFlash 与此类服务级优化之间的交互。. The interaction between TreeFlash and such serving-level optimizations is not evaluated in this work.

TreeFlash 是从现有的 DFlash 检查点初始化的，而不是从头开始训练。 完整的预训练运行超出了本文的范围，并且可能需要在用于构建初始候选分布的辅助损失上TreeFlash is initialised from an existing DFlash checkpoint rather than trained from scratch. A full pretraining run is beyond the scope of this paper, and would likely require an auxiliary loss on the initial candidate distribution used to construct theMM-二叉树。 同样的担忧也适用于 AR 逼近器的扩展训练：DFlash 主干的长时间微调可能会降低用于构建-ary tree. The same concern applies to extended training of the AR-approximator: prolonged fine-tuning of the DFlash backbone may degrade the initial candidate distributions used to construct theMM-二叉树，导致最终的树构建不理想-ary tree, leading to suboptimal final tree construction

AR 逼近器使用教师强制输入嵌入进行训练，这是自回归模型的标准范例，但在这里可能不是最理想的：在推理时，逼近器条件是其自己先前起草的标记，而不是真实标记。 解决这种暴露偏差留待未来的工作。 最近针对树生成而不是顺序 AR 建模的训练模型的研究也可能为训练 TreeFlash 提供一个有前途的未来方向The AR-approximator is trained with teacher-forced input embeddings, which is the standard paradigm for autoregressive models but may be suboptimal here: at inference time, the approximator conditions on its own previously drafted tokens rather than ground-truth ones. Addressing this exposure bias is left to future work. Recent work on training models explicitly for tree generation rather than sequential AR modelling may also offer a promising future direction in training TreeFlashHu et al. (2026).

TreeFlash 仅在 Qwen 系列型号上进行评估。 效率提升是否会转移到其他型号系列仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。TreeFlash is evaluated exclusively on Qwen-family models. Whether the efficiency gains transfer to other model families remains an open question.
