从合成数据到真实修复：针对患者特定牙冠完成的扩散模型From Synthetic Data to Real Restorations: Diffusion Model for Patient-specific Dental Crown Completion

Dávid Pukanec  Tibor Kubík  Michal Španěl
Department of Computer Graphics and Multimedia,
Brno University of Technology, Czechia
{xpukanec, kubik, spanel}@fit.vut.cz
摘要Abstract

我们呈现We present牙齿工艺ToothCraft，一种基于扩散的牙冠上下文生成模型，在人工创建的不完整牙齿上进行训练。基于 3D 形状条件扩散模型的最新进展，我们开发了一种能够根据局部解剖环境自动完成牙冠的模型。为了解决此任务缺乏训练数据的问题，我们设计了一个增强管道，可以从公开的完整牙弓数据集（3DS、ODD）生成不完整的牙齿几何形状。通过综合一组不同的训练示例，我们的方法可以对各种牙齿缺陷进行稳健的学习。实验结果表明，我们的模型重建完整牙冠的强大能力，在综合损坏的测试修复体上实现了 81.8% 的交叉交集 (IoU) 和 0.00034 的倒角距离 (CD)。我们的实验表明，该模型可以直接应用于现实世界的案例，有效地填充不完整的牙齿，同时生成的牙冠与相对牙列的交叉最小化，从而降低了咬合干扰的风险。可以通过以下地址访问代码、模型权重和数据集信息：, a diffusion-based model for the contextual generation of tooth crowns, trained on artificially created incomplete teeth. Building upon recent advancements in conditioned diffusion models for 3D shapes, we developed a model capable of an automated tooth crown completion conditioned on local anatomical context. To address the lack of training data for this task, we designed an augmentation pipeline that generates incomplete tooth geometries from a publicly available dataset of complete dental arches (3DS, ODD). By synthesising a diverse set of training examples, our approach enables robust learning across a wide spectrum of tooth defects. Experimental results demonstrate the strong capability of our model to reconstruct complete tooth crowns, achieving an intersection over union (IoU) of 81.8% and a Chamfer Distance (CD) of 0.00034 on synthetically damaged testing restorations. Our experiments demonstrate that the model can be applied directly to real-world cases, effectively filling in incomplete teeth, while generated crowns show minimal intersection with the opposing dentition, thus reducing the risk of occlusal interference. Access to the code, model weights, and dataset information will be available at:https://github.com/ikarus1211/VISAPP_ToothCraft.

1 简介Introduction

修复受损或缺失的牙齿是旨在重建其原始形状的手术。通常，技术人员从模板中选择预定义的牙齿并对其进行雕刻，以满足功能和美学要求。 CAD/CAM 系统的引入极大地增强了这一过程，并提供了当天牙齿制造的能力Restoration of a damaged or missing tooth is a procedure that aims to reconstruct its original shape. Typically, a technician selects a predefined tooth from a template and sculpts it to meet both functional and aesthetic requirements. The introduction of CAD/CAM systems has greatly enhanced this process and provided the ability for same-day tooth fabrication[14]。然而，修复体的设计仍然非常耗时，需要专业技能，并且容易出现人为错误。因此，人们对自动化这一过程越来越感兴趣. However, the design of the restoration remains very time-consuming, requires specialised skills, and is prone to human error. Therefore, there is a growing interest in automating this process[5]。近年来，人们进行了各种研究来解决一个相关但更广泛的问题，即形状完成. In recent years, various studies have been conducted to tackle a related but broader issue known as shape completion[25, 13, 22, 3]。这促使我们探索深度学习方法来解决牙冠修复问题。. This motivates us to explore deep learning methods to address the problem of tooth crown restoration.

去噪扩散概率模型 (DDPM)Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPMs)[6]近年来已成为最有前途的生成模型之一。这些模型在各种 2D 任务中表现出了卓越的性能have emerged as one of the most promising classes of generative models in recent years. These models have shown remarkable performance across various 2D tasks[18, 17]，以及 3D 形状生成领域, as well as in the area of 3D shape generation[12]。为了引导生成过程获得所需的输出，可以使用辅助信息（例如部分输入、类标签或文本）来调节这些扩散模型. To steer the generative process toward the desired output, these diffusion models can be conditioned using auxiliary information, such as partial input, class labels, or text[29, 7, 18]。这激发了使用扩散模型进行牙齿重建的想法，以由相邻牙齿和相对牙列组成的局部环境为条件。. This inspires the idea of using diffusion models for tooth reconstruction, conditioned on the local context composed of neighbouring teeth and opposing dentition.

然而，一个关键的挑战是扩散模型需要大量数据集However, a key challenge is that diffusion models require large datasets[18, 16]，提出了如何获得如此广泛的数据的问题。由于成本高昂、隐私问题和道德责任，获取牙科数据集可能会出现问题。同时，基于扩散的方法通常需要数千个样本进行学习。然而，仅存在数量有限的公开可用的 3D 形状牙科数据集, raising the question of how such extensive data can be obtained. Acquiring datasets in dentistry can be problematic due to high costs, privacy concerns, and ethical liabilities. Simultaneously, diffusion-based approaches often require thousands of samples for learning. Nevertheless, only a limited number of publicly available 3D shape dental datasets exist[1, 21]。此外，这些数据集都没有专门包含用于牙齿完整的不完整或有缺陷的牙齿。为了解决这个问题，我们设计了一种增强管道，可以从公开的分段牙弓数据集中修改健康的牙齿，使我们能够训练一个统一的扩散模型，该模型可以以自我监督的方式恢复各种牙齿缺陷或缺失的牙齿，如图所示. Furthermore, none of these datasets specifically includes incomplete or defective teeth for the purpose of tooth completion. To address this issue, we devised an augmentation pipeline that modifies healthy teeth from publicly available datasets of segmented dental arches, allowing us to train a single, unified diffusion model that can, in a self-supervised fashion, restore a wide variety of tooth defects or missing teeth, as depicted on Figure1.

  • •

  • •

This enables training a conditional diffusion model on synthetic data in a self-supervised fashion for the tooth completion task.

  • •

    评估表明，在合成的不完整样本上训练的模型可以有效地将学到的知识转移到不完整牙齿数据的真实扫描中。The evaluation shows that the model trained on synthesised incomplete samples can effectively transfer the learned knowledge to real-world scans of incomplete tooth data.

3 方法Method

我们的目标是开发 ToothCraft，这是一种基于扩散的生成模型，能够从不完整或缺失的牙齿结构中生成解剖学上准确的牙齿，同时考虑局部解剖背景。如之前的工作所示Our goal was to develop ToothCraft, a diffusion-based generative model capable of producing anatomically accurate teeth from incomplete or missing tooth structures, while considering the local anatomical context. As shown in previous work[8]，上下文为牙齿的形态、样式和咬合关系提供了有价值的见解。为了指导我们的模型，我们借鉴了 ControlNet 的关键概念, the context offers valuable insights into the morphology, style, and occlusal relationship of the tooth. To guide our model, we draw on key concepts from ControlNet[29]和 DiffCompleteand DiffComplete[3]，两者都表现出很强的可控性。此外，我们的目标是使用从公开数据集导出的合成不完整牙齿数据来训练我们的网络, both of which demonstrate strong controllability. Additionally, our objective is to train our network using synthesised incomplete tooth data derived from publicly available datasets[1, 21]分段牙弓，同时确保模型在真实病例中表现良好。of segmented dental arches, while ensuring that the model performs well on real cases.

4 实验Experiments

虽然生成合成数据的过程非常适合训练，但我们认识到仅评估此类数据并不能充分反映实际的临床使用。因此，除了对合成样本进行测试之外，我们还根据临床医生创建的真实情况对 16 个牙齿缺陷的真实案例进行了评估。这些示例由 TESCAN 公司提供，显示了各种牙冠缺陷，从部分牙齿缺失到完全缺牙。例子还包括牙龈异常，例如用于残端和牙冠固定的螺钉和基台，以及牙龈退缩。使用这些示例，我们测试了网络将合成损伤知识转移到真实案例的能力及其临床应用的潜力。While the process of generating synthetic data is well-suited for training, we recognise that evaluating only such data would not adequately reflect real clinical use. Therefore, in addition to tests on synthetic samples, we also conducted an evaluation on 16 real-world cases of tooth defects, with ground truth created by clinicians. These examples were provided by the TESCAN company and show various crown defects, ranging from partial tooth loss to complete tooth absence. Using these examples, we tested the network’s ability to transfer knowledge from synthetic damage to real cases and its potential for clinical application.

5 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 ToothCraft，一个基于扩散的统一框架，用于修复各类部分损坏或缺失的牙齿。我们的方法利用从公开可用的分段牙弓数据集衍生的综合生成的不完整样本，从而实现自我监督训练，而无需精选的缺陷数据。实验结果表明，在合成测试集上表现出色，并成功转移到牙齿缺失或损坏的真实临床扫描中。We present ToothCraft, a unified diffusion-based framework for restoring various classes of partially damaged or missing teeth. Our approach leverages synthetically generated incomplete samples derived from publicly available datasets of segmented dental arches, enabling self-supervised training without the need for curated defect data. Experimental results demonstrate strong performance on synthetic test sets and successful transfer to real clinical scans with missing or damaged teeth.

尽管取得了这些有希望的结果，但仍然存在一些局限性。在存在异常牙龈解剖结构或非典型周围环境的情况下，该模型会遇到困难，并且由于内存限制，细粒度的几何细节受到 SDF 表示相对较低的分辨率的限制。应对这些挑战，特别是通过更高分辨率的表示和更多样化的训练数据，构成了未来工作的重要方向。Despite these promising results, several limitations remain. The model struggles in cases where abnormal gingival anatomy or atypical surrounding context is present, and fine-grained geometric details are limited by the relatively low resolution of the SDF representation due to memory constraints. Addressing these challenges, particularly through higher-resolution representations and more diverse training data, constitutes an important direction for future work.
