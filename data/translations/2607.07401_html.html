用于 PET 引导全身 MRI 平移的异质性自适应扩散薛定谔桥Heterogeneity-Adaptive Diffusion Schrödinger Bridge for PET-Guided Whole-Body MRI Translation

Chengbo Wang†\dagger    Jiacheng Yu†\dagger    Linjie Bian†\dagger    Ming Qi    Xiaosheng Liu    Tongtong Che    Jichang Zhang    Shuyu Li    Shaoli Song*and Xiuying Wang*
摘要Abstract

虽然全身多模态医学成像扫描仪已经 因更有效的医疗应用而受到越来越多的认可， PET-MR 扫描采集时间过长是一个主要障碍 更有效的临床实践。基于深度学习的 MRI 翻译 提供了减少扫描持续时间的潜在解决方案。然而，目前 模型通常专注于特定的解剖区域并面临挑战 对于由高度异质特征分布组成的全身扫描，主要是由于（1） 全身的不同解剖区域，以及（2）病变或病理组织。本文通过新颖的异质性自适应扩散薛定谔桥（HA-DSB）框架解决了这些挑战。通过将翻译显式建模为源分布和目标分布之间的随机传输，HA-DSB 结合了源自视觉语言模型 (VLM) 的区域上下文嵌入，以实现特定于区域的建模。为了增强病理组织的保真度，PET 之前的病变感知代谢通过双级引导机制直接集成到桥动力学中。具体来说，PET引导的噪声调制模块在正向过程中自适应地缩放空间扩散扰动，而在反向过程中利用PET特征通过注意机制选择性地放大与病变相关的结构。实验证明了我们的方法在全身 MRI 翻译中跨不同身体区域的优越性，并表明在 PET 引导下病变区域的翻译质量得到了提高。我们的代码位于While whole-body multimodal medical imaging scanners have been increasingly recognized for more effective medical applications, the excessive long acquisition time in PET–MR scanning is a major obstacle in more efficient clinical practice. Deep learning–based MRI translation provides a potential solution to reduce scan duration. However, current models often focus on specific anatomical regions and face challenges for whole-body scans that consists of highly heterogeneous feature distributions mainly due to (1) different anatomical regions across whole-body, and (2) lesions or pathological tissues. This paper tackles the challenges through a novel Heterogeneity-Adaptive Diffusion Schrödinger Bridge (HA-DSB) framework. By explicitly modeling translation as stochastic transport between source and target distributions, HA-DSB incorporates region context embeddings derived from a vision-language model (VLM) to enable region-specific modeling. To enhance fidelity of the pathological tissue, lesion-aware metabolic prior from PET is integrated directly into the bridge dynamics through a dual-stage guidance mechanism. Specifically, a PET-guided noise modulation module adaptively scales spatial diffusion perturbations during the forward process, while PET features are leveraged during the reverse process to selectively amplify lesion-relevant structures via an attention mechanism. Experiments demonstrate the superiority of our method across different body regions in whole-body MRI translation and show improved translation quality in lesion areas under PET guidance. Our code is available athttps://github.com/xyw-medical-research/HADSB.

1 简介Introduction

混合正电子发射断层扫描与磁共振成像 (PET/MR) 相结合是一种新兴的成像方式，能够同时采集代谢和高分辨率解剖信息，生成本质上共同配准的 PET 和多序列 MR 图像，同时与 PET/CT 相比减少辐射暴露Hybrid positron emission tomography combined with magnetic resonance imaging (PET/MR) is an emerging imaging modality that enables simultaneous acquisition of metabolic and high-resolution anatomical information, producing intrinsically co-registered PET and multi-sequence MR images while reducing radiation exposure compared with PET/CT[1]。全身PET/MR支持从头颈部到大腿中部的全面多床成像，在癌症转移评估等临床应用中变得越来越重要. Whole-body PET/MR supports comprehensive multi-bed imaging from the head and neck to the mid-thigh and has become increasingly important in clinical applications such as cancer metastasis evaluation[2]。然而，全身 PET/MR 扫描时间通常为 60-90 分钟，这主要是由于多个 MR 序列，这限制了临床效率并增加了患者的不适。基于深度学习的 MR 翻译通过从快速获取的序列中合成耗时的模态，提供了一种实用的解决方案，而最近基于扩散的方法已在 MR 图像翻译中展现出良好的性能. However, whole-body PET/MR scan time typically ranges from 60–90 minutes, largely due to multiple MR sequences, which limits clinical efficiency and increases patient discomfort. Deep learning–based MR translation offers a practical solution by synthesizing time-consuming modalities from fast-acquired sequences, and recent diffusion-based approaches have demonstrated promising performance in MR image translation[3, 4, 5, 6].
然而，大多数 MR 翻译都集中在特定的解剖区域，例如公共数据集中的大脑However, most MR translation focus on specific anatomical regions such as the brain in public datasets[7]，并将这些模型扩展到集成 PET/MR 设置中的全身 MR 翻译仍然具有挑战性。全身扫描表现出强烈的跨区域异质性，不同解剖区域的组织成分和信号特征存在很大差异, and extending these models to whole-body MR translation in integrated PET/MR settings remains challenging. Whole-body scans exhibit strong cross-region heterogeneity, with substantial variations in tissue composition and signal characteristics across anatomical regions[8]。这种非平稳和区域相关的强度分布显着增加了学习统一模态映射的难度. Such non-stationary and region-dependent intensity distributions significantly increase the difficulty of learning a unified modality mapping[9, 10]。例如，众所周知，GAN 在多模态分布中会遭受模式崩溃的影响. For example, GANs are known to suffer from mode collapse in multi-modal distributions[11, 12]，而扩散模型可能偏向于主导特征模式, while diffusion models may be biased toward dominant feature modes[13]。如果没有明确的区域调节，这些模型可能很难捕获全身 MR 翻译中的区域特定结构。. Without explicit region conditioning, these models may struggle to capture region-specific structures in the whole body MR translation.
MR 翻译中另一个关键但尚未探索的挑战是病理区域的准确重建Another critical yet underexplored challenge in MR translation is the accurate reconstruction of pathological regions[14, 15]，这通常具有主要的临床重要性。与正常组织相比，病变表现出高度异质性的外观和复杂的跨模态信号关系。大多数现有模型没有明确区分病理变化与正常组织，导致病变信号偏向于占主导地位的健康分布，并导致病理保真度下降。, which are often of primary clinical importance. Compared to normal tissues, lesions exhibit highly heterogeneous appearances and complex cross-modality signal relationships. Most existing models do not explicitly differentiate pathological changes from normal tissues, causing lesion signals to be biased toward the dominant healthy distribution and leading to degraded pathological fidelity.
在这项工作中，我们提出了一种采用 PET 引导的异质性自适应扩散薛定谔桥（HA-DSB），用于全身 MR 翻译（图 1）。In this work, we propose a Heterogeneity-Adaptive Diffusion Schrödinger Bridge (HA-DSB) with PET guidance for whole-body MR translation (Fig.1）。与依赖隐式噪声预测的传统基于扩散的翻译方法不同，HA-DSB 显式优化源和目标 MR 分布之间的随机传输计划). Unlike conventional diffusion-based translation methods that rely on implicit noise prediction, HA-DSB explicitly optimizes a stochastic transport plan between source and target MR distributions[3, 16]。这种显式传输公式更适合全身 MR 翻译，其中强烈的跨区域域移位使得隐式对齐容易出现过度平滑和模态模糊的情况。为了解决全身 MR 翻译中明显的区域相关分布变化，我们进一步引入了源自大型视觉语言模型生成的结构化描述的区域上下文嵌入，并将其与扩散时间嵌入融合，从而实现细粒度、特定于区域的桥建模，并持续提高跨解剖区域的翻译性能。此外，为了提高病理保真度，我们利用 PET 作为病变感知先验，由于内在的共同配准，这在集成 PET/MR 中自然可用。由于病变在 PET 中通常表现为局灶性高摄取信号，因此我们引入了一种 PET 引导的噪声调制机制，该机制在前向过程中应用空间自适应损坏。这种空间自适应扰动重新分配了随机传输难度，鼓励模型学习病理敏感的映射，而不是偏向占主导地位的健康结构。多尺度 PET 引导注意力模块进一步纳入逆向过程中，以放大病变相关特征，提高病理区域的翻译保真度。我们的主要贡献包括：. This explicit transport formulation is better suited for whole-body MR translation, where strong cross-region domain shifts make implicit alignment prone to over-smoothing and modality ambiguity. To address the pronounced region-dependent distribution shifts in whole-body MR translation, we further introduce region context embeddings derived from structured descriptions generated by the large vision-language model and fuse them with diffusion time embeddings, enabling fine-grained, region-specific bridge modeling and consistently improving translation performance across anatomical regions. Furthermore, to improve pathological fidelity, we exploit PET as a lesion-aware prior, which is naturally available in integrated PET/MR due to intrinsic co-registration. Since lesions typically manifest as focal high-uptake signals in PET, we introduce a PET-guided noise modulation mechanism that applies spatially adaptive corruption in the forward process. This spatially adaptive perturbation redistributes the stochastic transport difficulty, encouraging the model to learn pathology-sensitive mappings rather than being biased toward dominant healthy structures. A multi-scale PET-guided attention module is further incorporated into the reverse process to amplify lesion-relevant features, improving translation fidelity in pathological areas. 4 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 HA-DSB 用于 PET 引导的全身 MR 翻译。通过结合区域上下文嵌入、PET 引导的噪声调制和多尺度 PET 感知注意力，HA-DSB 解决了全身 PET/MR 中的解剖和病理异质性问题。实验表明，跨身体区域的翻译质量得到了提高，并且在含有病变的区域中也获得了额外的收益。这些结果表明，异质性自适应调节对于建模区域依赖性结构变异性和在具有挑战性的全身环境中恢复病变相关细节是有效的。更广泛地说，HA-DSB 为稳健的全身医学图像转换提供了一个实用的基于桥接的框架。In this work, we proposed HA-DSB for PET-guided whole-body MR translation. By combining region context embeddings, PET-guided noise modulation, and multi-scale PET-aware attention, HA-DSB addresses anatomical and pathological heterogeneity in whole-body PET/MR. Experiments show improved translation quality across body regions, with additional gains in lesion-containing areas. These results indicate that heterogeneity-adaptive conditioning is effective for modeling region-dependent structural variability and recovering lesion-relevant details in challenging whole-body settings. More broadly, HA-DSB provides a practical bridge-based framework for robust whole-body medical image translation.
