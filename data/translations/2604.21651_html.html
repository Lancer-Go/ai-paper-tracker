摘要Abstract

周期性信号的去噪和准确的波形估计是许多信号处理领域的核心任务，包括语音、音乐、医疗诊断、无线电和声纳。尽管深度学习方法最近显示出相对于经典方法的性能改进，但它们需要大量的计算资源，并且通常针对每个信号观察单独进行训练。本研究提出了一种基于 DCNN 和重采样的计算高效方法，称为 R-DCNN，专为在严格的功率和资源约束下运行而设计。该方法针对具有不同基频的信号，并且只需要一次观察即可进行训练。它通过轻量级重采样步骤推广到其他信号，该步骤对齐不同频率信号中的时间尺度，以重新使用相同的网络权重。尽管计算复杂度较低，但 R-DCNN 的性能可与最先进的经典方法相媲美，例如基于自回归 (AR) 的技术，以及针对每个观察单独训练的传统 DCNN。这种效率和性能的结合使得所提出的方法特别适合在资源受限的环境中部署，而无需牺牲去噪或估计精度。Denoising of periodic signals and accurate waveform estimation are core tasks across many signal processing domains, including speech, music, medical diagnostics, radio, and sonar. Although deep learning methods have recently shown performance improvements over classical approaches, they require substantial computational resources and are usually trained separately for each signal observation. This study proposes a computationally efficient method based on DCNN and Re-sampling, termed R- DCNN, designed for operation under strict power and resource constraints. The approach targets signals with varying fundamental frequencies and requires only a single observation for training. It generalizes to additional signals via a lightweight resampling step that aligns time scales in signals with different frequencies to re-use the same network weights. Despite its low computational complexity, R-DCNN achieves performance comparable to state-of-the-art classical methods, such as autoregressive (AR)-based techniques, as well as conventional DCNNs trained individually for each observation. This combination of efficiency and performance makes the proposed method particularly well suited for deployment in resource-constrained environments without sacrificing denoising or estimation accuracy.

1. 简介Introduction

This study proposes a new deep learning method fitted for periodical signals and optimized for minimum computational complexity and storage to be used in power limited devices like sensors or IoT devices. The method matches the performance of classical deep learning approaches with significantly reduced computational complexity. The proposed training-inference architecture requires a tiny network with very small number of parameters whose weights are trained on a single observation only and further used during inference.

当前的 DCNN 通常对每一层使用固定的、基于整数的膨胀因子（例如 2[𝑙−1] ）。这种限制源于数字化信号的离散性质，其中信号周期是恒定的。虽然这简化了训练过程中的实现，但它极大地限制了网络处理周期变化信号（如大多数现实应用中那样）的灵活性。在现实场景中，当信号周期变化时，信号分量的频率不是采样率乘以膨胀因子的整数倍。然后，由于错误的时间对齐，固定扩张限制了网络实现最佳性能。为了弥补这一点，现有的 DCNN 使用参数数量大幅增加的非线性激活函数，或者对每个信号进行连续重新训练。这些解决方案显着降低了收敛性并增加了计算开销，限制了 DCNN 在功率有限的边缘应用中的使用。Current DCNNs generally use fixed, integer-based dilation factors (e.g., 2[𝑙−1] ) for each layer. This constraint stems from the discrete nature of digitized signals, where signal period is constant. While this simplifies implementation during training, it significantly limits the network's flexibility to work with signals whose period changes like in most real-life applications. In real-world scenarios, when signal period varies the frequencies of signal components are not integer multiples of the sampling rate times dilation factor. Then the fixed dilation limits the network from achieving optimal performance due to the wrong temporal alignment. To compensate for it, existing DCNN use either non-linear activation functions with substantial increase in number of parameters or continuous re-training for every signal. The solutions significantly degrade convergence and increase computational overhead limiting the use of DCNN in powerlimited edge applications.

本文提出了一种使用带重采样的 DCNN（R-DCNN）对周期变化的周期信号进行去噪的新方法。该研究证明，当基频已知时，无需对新观测值进行重新训练即可进行去噪。该模型通过使用仅在重新采样的观测值上进行测试的单个观测值进行训练，该观测值的时间轴经过转换以匹配交易观测的时间轴。该论文证明，当信号基频变化时，唯一应该调整的参数是时间缩放，允许使用具有固定权重的模型。This paper proposes a novel method of using DCNN with Re-sampling (R-DCNN) for de-noising of periodical signals whose period varies. The study proves that the denoising can be performed without re-training on new observations when their fundamental frequency is known. The model is trained by using a single observation only tested on re-sampled observations whose time axis is transformed to match the time axis of the trading observation. The paper proves that when signal fundamental frequency varies the only parameter which should be adjusted is time scaling allowing to use the model with fixed weights.

实验表明，与深度学习 DCNN 甚至经典自回归（AR）等现有方法相比，新的 R-DCNN 提供了高精度的信号去噪，同时在不同周期的周期信号估计中显着降低了计算复杂度。The experiments demonstrate that the new R-DCNN provides high accuracy of signal denoising while significantly decreasing computational complexity compared to the existing methods including deep learning DCNN and even classical autoregression (AR) in the estimation of periodic signals with varying period.

3.3.周期性信号去噪3.3. Periodic Signal Denoising

周期性信号去噪的方法有很多，大致可以分为参数方法、非参数方法以及最近基于机器学习 (ML) 的方法。在非参数（谱）方法中，Lomb-Scargle 周期图 (LSP) 被广泛用于通过正弦曲线的最小二乘拟合来估计谱密度。对于实际应用，研究 [14] 将 LSP 扩展为还可以估计相位谱和幅度谱。研究[15]引入了SigSpec，它推导了白噪声下频域幅度的解析概率密度，结合了频率和相位。据报道，它比 LSP 或相位色散最小化更不易受混叠影响。一项比较研究 [16] 在偏差、标准偏差和计算复杂性方面对几种非参数频率估计器进行了基准测试。There are many methods for periodic signal denoising, which can be broadly categorized into parametric, nonparametric, and more recently, machine learning (ML) - based approaches. Among the nonparametric (spectral) methods, the Lomb–Scargle periodogram (LSP) is widely used to estimate spectral density via a least-squares fit of sinusoids. For practical applications, study [14] extends LSP to also estimate phase and amplitude spectra. Study [15] introduces SigSpec, which derives an analytical probability density for amplitude in frequency domain under white noise, incorporating both frequency and phase. It is reported to be less susceptible to aliasing than LSP or Phase Dispersion Minimization. A comparative study [16] benchmarks several nonparametric frequency estimators in terms of bias, standard deviation, and computational complexity.

参数化方法和基于模型的方法以不同的方式处理问题。研究 [17] 调查了噪声中正弦曲线的参数频率估计，涵盖通过 Yule-Walker 方程进行自回归 (AR) 建模、高分辨率子空间方法（Root-MUSIC、ESPRIT、截断 SVD）、最大似然和贝叶斯方法，以及模型阶数选择的关键问题。Parametric and model-based methods approach the problem differently. Study [17] surveys parametric frequency estimation for sinusoids in noise, covering autoregressive (AR) modeling via Yule–Walker equations, high-resolution subspace methods (Root-MUSIC, ESPRIT, truncated SVD), maximum likelihood, and Bayesian approaches, along with the critical issue of model order selection.

自适应和滤波方法处理频率缓慢变化的信号。论文[18]提出了一种基于折扣最小二乘的自适应算法，用于增强和跟踪固定或缓慢变化频率的周期信号。研究[19]推导了一种非线性观测器，用于估计多正弦（可能谐波相关）信号的参数，报告了扩展卡尔曼滤波器的性能改进。在[20]中，作者提出了一种非线性自适应时域方法，用于具有强抗噪性的瞬时频率估计。Adaptive and filtering methods address signals with slowly varying frequencies. Paper [18] proposes an adaptive algorithm based on discounted least squares for enhancement and tracking of periodic signals with fixed or slowly varying frequency. Study [19] derives a nonlinear observer for estimating parameters of multi-sinusoidal (possibly harmonically related) signals, reporting improved performance over the extended Kalman filter. In [20] the authors present a nonlinear adaptive time-domain method for instantaneous frequency estimation with strong noise immunity.

稀疏和压缩感知方法代表了一个更新的方向。研究[21]提出通过嵌套周期字典中的稀疏表示来恢复周期混合，并使用新引入的一致性度量来导出改进的支持恢复保证。在[22]中，作者表明，利用集群稀疏信号的周期性支持结构可以改进经典的压缩感知界限。Sparse and compressed sensing methods represent a more recent direction. 5.4.结论和未来的工作5.4. Conclusions and Future Work

本研究提出了一种新颖的 R-DCNN 方法，用于从具有不同基频的噪声观测中估计周期性确定性信号。一方面，该方法利用了 DCNN 的优势，在不增加参数数量的情况下实现了大的感受野。另一方面，它适用于低计算复杂度，使其适合功耗受限的应用，例如边缘物联网设备。This study presents a novel R-DCNN method for estimating periodic deterministic signals from noisy observations with varying fundamental frequency. On the one hand, the method leverages the advantages of DCNNs, enabling a large receptive field without increasing the number of parameters. On the other hand, it is adapted for low computational complexity, making it suitable for power-constrained applications such as edge IoT devices.

与需要为每个观测重新训练 DCNN 的传统方法不同，所提出的方法在单个参考观测上训练网络，并将其应用于具有不同基频的新观测。这是通过在推理之前对每个观测值重新采样以匹配参考信号的时间尺度来实现的。Unlike conventional approaches, which require retraining the DCNN for each observation, the proposed method trains the network on a single reference observation and applies it to new observations with different fundamental frequencies. This is achieved by resampling each observation to match the time scale of the reference signal prior to inference.

仿真结果表明，R-DCNN 的性能与经典方法（包括 DCNN 和 AR 方法）的性能相当，但由于每次观察再训练，这些方法的计算复杂度显着提高。这使得所提出的方法成为功率有限环境中周期性信号去噪的有前途的替代方案。Simulation results demonstrate that the performance of the R-DCNN is comparable to that of classical methods, including DCNN and AR approaches, which involve significantly higher computational complexity due to perobservation retraining. This makes the proposed method a promising alternative for periodic signal denoising in power-limited environments.

该方法的一个关键限制是需要准确了解每次观测的基频。未来的工作将集中于评估该方法对基频估计和重采样精度误差的鲁棒性。这种分析至关重要，因为在这些参数中实现高精度可能会带来额外的计算开销，而这在实际应用中必须保持最小。A key limitation of the method is the requirement for accurate knowledge of the fundamental frequency for each observation. Future work will focus on evaluating the robustness of the method to errors in fundamental frequency estimation and resampling precision. This analysis is essential, as achieving high accuracy in these parameters may introduce additional computational overhead, which must remain minimal in practical applications.
