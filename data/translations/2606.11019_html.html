摘要Abstract

基于学习的运动规划器尽管最近取得了进展，但经常遇到时间不一致的问题。框架上的小扰动可能会累积成不稳定的轨迹，从而降低闭环驾驶的舒适性和安全性。几种方法尝试将历史作为静态调节信号注入以稳定输出，结果只是诱导规划器复制历史模式而不是适应环境上下文。为了解决这个限制，我们提出了扩散强迫规划器（DFP），这是一种由历史引导控制驱动的基于扩散的规划框架。具体来说，DFP 将完整轨迹分解为历史、当前和未来段，并为每个段分配独立的噪声级别。该模型联合对历史和未来片段进行去噪，强制执行异构联合扩散过程。在推理时，无分类器引导（CFG）被应用于以可控方式使用退火历史来引导未来采样。 nuPlan上的闭环评估和综合消融表明，DFP在复杂驾驶场景下产生连续、稳定、可控的运动计划的同时，实现了具有竞争力的性能。Learning-based motion planners, despite recent progress, often suffer from temporal inconsistency. Small perturbations across frames can accumulate into unstable trajectories, degrading comfort and safety in closed-loop driving. Several methods attempt to inject history as a static conditioning signal to stabilize outputs, only to induce the planner to copy historical patterns instead of adapting to environment contexts. To address this limitation, we propose Diffusion Forcing Planner (DFP), a diffusion-based planning framework driven by history-guided control. Specifically, DFP decomposes the full trajectory into history, current and future segments, and assign independent noise levels to each segment. The model jointly denoises the historical and the future segments, enforcing a heterogeneous joint diffusion process. At inference, classifier-free guidance (CFG) is applied to steer future sampling using annealed history in a controllable manner. Closed-loop evaluation and comprehensive ablations on nuPlan show that DFP achieves competitive performance while producing continuous, stable, and controllable motion plans in complex driving scenarios.

扩散模型Diffusion models[17]由于它们能够代表多模式分布并生成高质量的长期输出，因此最近在自动驾驶方面表现出了强大的前景。它们自然适合需要多模式轨迹输出和长视野动作生成的规划任务have recently shown strong promise for autonomous driving thanks to their ability to represent multi-modal distributions and generate high-quality long-horizon outputs. They are naturally suited to planning tasks that require multi-modal trajectory outputs and long-horizon action generation[13, 4]。最近的工作. Recent work[29, 41, 39, 4, 3]将扩散模型集成到端到端（E2E）管道和视觉-语言-动作（VLA）框架中，以改善自主系统的类人驾驶行为。has integrated diffusion models into end-to-end (E2E) pipelines and vision–language–action (VLA) frameworks to improve the human-like driving behavior of autonomous systems.

尽管有这些优点，但通过模仿学习训练的基于扩散的策略仍然对演示和场景环境中的噪声敏感Despite the advantages, diffusion-based policies trained via imitation learning remain sensitive to noise in demonstrations and scene contexts[32]。即使在看似相似的环境中，该策略也可能会因场景环境中的小扰动而大幅漂移，从而导致轨迹中明显的帧到帧不稳定. Even in seemingly similar environments, the policy can drift substantially in response to small perturbations in the scene contexts, leading to noticeable frame-to-frame instability in trajectories[5]。因此，提高计划轨迹的时间一致性和鲁棒性已成为一个关键的开放挑战。. As a result, improving the temporal consistency and robustness of planned trajectories has become a key open challenge.

为了应对这一挑战，我们利用历史轨迹来指导未来计划的生成，从而提高帧与帧之间的一致性。从决策建模的角度来看，运动规划本质上是非马尔可夫的：合理的行动不仅取决于当前的观察，还取决于过去的观察和行动To address this challenge, we leverage the historical trajectory to guide the generation of future plans, thereby improving frame-to-frame consistency. From a decision-modeling perspective, motion planning is inherently non-Markovian: a reasonable action depends not only on the current observation, but also on past observations and actions[38]。然而，在复杂的交通场景中，如果将历史视为与环境同等的静态条件，则模型往往倾向于复制历史模式，而不是根据环境变化调整未来的决策. However, in complex traffic scenes, if history is treated as a static condition on equal footing with the environment, the model often tends to replicate historical patterns rather than adjust future decisions in response to environment changes[10, 16, 27, 12]。这催生了一种以可控方式使用历史的范式：它既不应该被忽视，也不应该被强加为无条件的硬约束。我们从视频扩散中的扩散强迫变压器（DFoT）中汲取灵感. This motivates a paradigm in which history is used in a controllable way: it should neither be ignored nor imposed as an unconditional hard constraint. We draw inspiration from Diffusion Forcing Transformer (DFoT) in video diffusion[34]，它使用噪声掩蔽机制来选择性地暴露和退火历史片段，从而平衡发电质量和稳定性。, which uses a noising-as-masking mechanism to selectively expose and anneal historical segments, thereby balancing generation quality and stability.

基于这一见解，我们提出了扩散强迫规划器（DFP），这是一种以历史为导向的分块扩散规划框架。核心思想是将完整轨迹划分为历史、当前和未来块，并对每个块采样独立的扩散时间步以实现噪声掩蔽。在训练过程中，我们共同预测历史和未来，迫使模型在各种配置下学习因果一致的条件生成，其中历史可能是可用的或被屏蔽的，而未来的不同部分可能是可见的或被遮挡的。与历史帧是真实内容的视频生成不同，驾驶历史中过时的运动模式可能会主动误导当前决策。为了解决这个问题，我们设计了一个历史退火的无分类器指导（CFG）Building on this insight, we propose Diffusion Forcing Planner (DFP), a history-guided, chunkwise diffusion framework for planning. 5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了扩散强迫规划器（DFP），这是一种基于扩散的规划策略，它明确利用历史指导，同时保持可控性和鲁棒性。我们分别详细介绍了 DFP 的两个核心模块：扩散强迫训练和历史引导的 CFG 推理。在训练期间，DFP 通过分块噪声掩蔽来学习因果一致的生成。在推理时，DFP 会退火历史指导并融合引导和非引导分支，以在测试时可控地影响未来轨迹。结果表明，DFP 显着提高了舒适度和帧间一致性，同时在 nuPlan 上保持有竞争力的闭环性能，而无需依赖繁重的后处理。尽管取得了这些成果，DFP 广告管理系统仍存在局限性。性能可能对指导超参数敏感。未来的工作将侧重于将自适应引导调度应用于端到端自动驾驶框架，并规划对更广泛基准的评估以评估泛化能力。In this work, we introduced Diffusion Forcing Planner (DFP), a diffusion‐based planning policy that explicitly leverages history guidance while preserving controllability and robustness. We separately detail two core modules of DFP: diffusion-forcing training and history-guided CFG inference. During training, DFP learns causally consistent generation via chunked noising-as-masking. At inference, DFP anneals historical guidance and fuses guided and unguided branches to controllably influence future trajectories at test time. The results demonstrate DFP markedly improves comfort and inter-frame consistency, while retaining competitive closed-loop performance on nuPlan without relying on heavy post-processing. Despite these gains, DFP has limitations. Performance can be sensitive to the guidance hyperparameters. Future work will focus on applying adaptive guidance scheduling to end-to-end autonomous driving framework, and planning evaluations on broader benchmarks to assess generalization.
