We study 4-bit KV-cache compression for this setting, using TurboQuant-style rotation and codebook quantization(Zandieh et al., 2026)作为质量锚和 vLLM FP8 KV 缓存作为部署锚点。我们报告三 贡献。首先，我们围绕多轮构建 4 位 KV 缓存 代理工作负载，其中任务质量、缓存驻留和服务 吞吐量必须联合测量。其次，我们描述一下实际情况 使 4 位路径稳健所需的设计选择，包括 不对称 K/V 处理、Walsh-Hadamard 旋转as a quality anchor and vLLM FP8 KV caching as the deployment anchor. 代理工作负载是具有较大共享前缀的长时间运行的会话 以及许多较短的后续转弯。在这种情况下，压缩很重要 因为它增加了保留的有用前缀的数量 在设备上。然而，单独压缩并不能保证更好的服务结果：量化和反量化引入的开销可能会降低令牌延迟，从而使端到端服务测量变得至关重要。我们通过两个指标来描述服务效率：首次令牌时间（TTFT）和每个输出令牌时间（TPOT）。Agentic workloads are long-running sessions with a large shared prefix and many shorter follow-up turns. 8 结论Conclusions

上下文密集的代理使 KV 缓存容量成为部署限制： 长前缀必须在许多回合中保持可重用性，并且服务 系统必须支持足够的并发会话以保持 GPU 的利用率。 我们围绕此设置提出了一项 4 位 KV 缓存研究。上 质量方面，TurboQuant 式旋转和码本方法提供了 强大的 4 位锚点，具有校准和块尺度选择 确定保留多少准确性。在系统方面， Ultra-TQ 表明，仔细的布局、LUT 和 MFMA 调度可以关闭 码书量化和生产服务之间的大部分差距。 UltraQuant 进一步近似旋转的码本路径 具有 FP4 微张量和 UE8M0 尺度，因此 CDNA4 矩阵核心可以执行 直接反量化。Context-heavy agents make KV-cache capacity a deployment constraint: long prefixes must remain reusable across many turns, and serving systems must support enough concurrent sessions to keep GPUs utilized. We presented a 4-bit KV-cache study centered on this setting. On the quality side, TurboQuant-style rotation and codebook methods provide a strong 4-bit anchor, with calibration and block-scale choices determining how much accuracy is retained. On the systems side, Ultra-TQ shows that careful layout, LUT, and MFMA scheduling can close much of the gap between codebook quantization and production serving. UltraQuant goes further by approximating the rotated codebook path with FP4 micro-tensors and UE8M0 scales so CDNA4 matrix cores perform the dequantization directly.

更广泛的教训是，低位 KV 缓存应评估为 端到端的服务机制，不仅仅是离线压缩 方法。对于代理工作负载，相关结果是联合效应 任务成功、缓存驻留、吞吐量和交互性。我们的 结果表明4位KV缓存可以保留上下文容量 在接近可部署性的同时积极压缩的好处 硬件本机 FP8 KV 缓存。The broader lesson is that low-bit KV caching should be evaluated as an end-to-end serving mechanism, not only as an offline compression method. For agent workloads, the relevant outcome is the joint effect on task success, cache residency, throughput, and interactivity. Our results indicate that 4-bit KV caching can retain the context-capacity benefits of aggressive compression while approaching the deployability of hardware-native FP8 KV caching.

9 讨论Discussion

有几个方向仍然开放。算法路径需要更完整 相同条件下固定旋转、校准旋转和学习旋转的比较 代理基准。系统路径需要更广泛地覆盖整个模型 系列、上下文长度和并发机制。最后， UltraQuant 近似提出了未来的一个重要问题 硬件感知量化：当稍微不太理想的码本映射时 直接到矩阵核心指令，端到端服务最优 可能更喜欢硬件本机格式而不是分析最佳格式 交涉。Several directions remain open. The algorithmic path needs a fuller comparison of fixed, calibrated, and learned rotations under the same agent benchmark. The systems path needs broader coverage across model families, context lengths, and concurrency regimes. Finally, the UltraQuant approximation raises an important question for future hardware-aware quantization: when a slightly less optimal codebook maps directly to matrix-core instructions, the end-to-end serving optimum may favor hardware-native formats over analytically optimal representations.
