摘要Abstract

人工智能生成的视觉媒体的快速扩散迫切需要高效、值得信赖的深度换脸检测系统。然而，现有的基于深度学习的检测方法依赖于计算密集型和耗能的推理算法，限制了其可扩展性。在这里，我们提出了一种混合数模深度伪造视频检测框架，该框架将轻量级数字前端与空间复用光学解码后端相结合，通过可编程空间光调制器进行大规模并行模拟推理。通过在单次光传播过程中同时处理 ≥15 个视频流，该系统能够以比纯数字方法更低的计算成本实现高吞吐量和准确的视频级真实性预测。我们使用不同的数据集验证了这种混合 Deepfake 视频处理器，这些数据集涵盖经典换脸、现实世界的 Deepfake 录音和完全 AI 生成的视频。使用在可见光谱中运行的空间复用实验装置，我们在 Celeb-DF 视频数据集上实现了平均深度伪造检测准确度、灵敏度和特异性，分别为 97.79%、99.86% 和 95.72%，其中每次推理在单次光学通道中并行测试了 15 个视频。多路复用光学解码器还表现出针对各种类型的视频降级、噪声、压缩、实验错位和黑盒对抗攻击的恢复能力。我们的结果表明，将光学计算集成到人工智能推理中可以同时提高吞吐量、能源效率和对抗鲁棒性，这三个特性在纯数字系统中很难同时实现。The rapid proliferation of AI-generated visual media has created an urgent need for efficient, trustworthy deepfake detection systems. However, existing deep learning–based detection methods rely on computationally intensive and energy-demanding inference algorithms, limiting their scalability. Here, we present a hybrid digital–analog deepfake video detection framework that combines a lightweight digital front-end with a spatially multiplexed optical decoding back-end for massively parallel analog inference through a programmable spatial light modulator. By simultaneously processing ≥15 video streams within a single opticalpropagation pass, the system enables high-throughput and accurate video-level authenticity prediction at reduced computational cost compared with purely digital methods. We validated this hybrid deepfake video processor using different datasets spanning classical faceswapping, real-world deepfake recordings, and fully AI-generated videos. Using a spatially multiplexed experimental set-up operating in the visible spectrum, we achieved average deepfake detection accuracy, sensitivity and specificity of 97.79%, 99.86% and 95.72%, respectively, on the Celeb-DF video dataset with 15 videos tested in parallel in a single optical pass per inference. The multiplexed optical decoder also demonstrates resilience against various types of video degradation, noise, compression, experimental misalignments and black-box adversarial attacks. Our results show that integrating optical computation into AI inference enables simultaneous gains in throughput, energy efficiency, and adversarial robustness—three properties that are difficult to achieve together in purely digital systems.

尽管取得了重大进展，但现有的基于深度学习的检测系统在可扩展性和效率方面面临着严重的限制。虽然深度神经网络在一系列 Deepfake 操作类型中表现出了强大的检测性能，但最先进的模型通常需要数百次千兆浮点运算 (GFLOP) 每次推理并按顺序处理视频，导致高延迟和功耗，阻碍大规模实时部署[[20-23]]。这些计算瓶颈激发了对替代推理范例的探索，这些范例能够在不完全依赖数字处理的情况下提供高吞吐量和节能检测。Despite significant progress made, existing deep learning-based detection systems face critical limitations in scalability and efficiency. While deep neural networks have demonstrated strong detection performance across a range of deepfake manipulation types, state-of-theart models often require hundreds of giga floating-point operations (GFLOPs) per inference and process videos sequentially, resulting in high latency and power consumption that hinders real-time deployment at scale[[20-23]] . These computational bottlenecks motivate the exploration of alternative inference paradigms capable of delivering high-throughput and energy-efficient detection without relying entirely on digital processing.

除了这些效率挑战之外，当前深度伪造检测器的一个重要限制是它们容易受到对抗性攻击。大量研究表明，基于深度学习的检测网络本质上容易受到目标扰动的影响，因此即使对人工智能生成的内容进行相对简单的操作也可能导致预训练的检测器完全失败，使它们无法区分合成媒体和真实内容[[24-26]]。这些挑战凸显了对根本不同的检测策略的需求。在这种情况下，本质上难以攻击的基于物理的检测范例可以提供一种特别引人注目的替代方案，有可能为验证人工智能生成的媒体提供强大而高效的第一道防线。Beyond these efficiency challenges, an important limitation of current deepfake detectors is their vulnerability to adversarial attacks. Extensive studies have shown that deep learning– based detection networks are inherently susceptible to targeted perturbations, such that even relatively simple manipulations of AI-generated content can cause pretrained detectors to fail entirely, rendering them unable to distinguish synthetic media from authentic content[[24-26]] . These challenges highlight the need for fundamentally different detection strategies. In this context, a physics-based detection paradigm that is intrinsically difficult to attack could offer a particularly compelling alternative, with the potential to provide a robust and efficient first line of defense for verifying AI-generated media.
