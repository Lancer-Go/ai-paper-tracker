电子离子对撞机科学文献检索增强问答系统Retrieval-Augmented Question Answering over Scientific Literature for the Electron-Ion Collider

Tina. J. Jat1, T. Ghosh1∗, Karthik Suresh2
摘要Abstract

为了利用语言模型的力量来回答特定领域的专业技术问题，检索增强生成（RAG）被广泛使用。 在这项工作中，我们开发了一款受检索增强生成 (RAG) 启发的问答应用程序，该应用程序由一个内部数据库组成，该数据库索引了与电子离子对撞机 (EIC) 实验（最大的国际科学合作之一）相关的 arXiv 文章，并纳入了用于生成答案的开源 LLaMA 模型。这是对其正在进行的应用程序的扩展，该应用程序基于 EIC 实验的专有模型和云托管的外部知识库而构建。这种本地部署的 RAG 系统提供了一种经济高效、资源受限的替代解决方案，用于构建 RAG 辅助的问答应用程序，以回答实验核物理领域的特定领域查询。这种设置有利于数据隐私，避免将任何出版前的科学数据和信息发送到公共领域。 未来的改进将扩展知识库以涵盖异构 EIC 相关出版物和报告，并将应用程序管道编排升级到 LangGraph 框架。To harness the power of Language Models in answering domain specific specialized technical questions, Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) is been used widely. In this work, we have developed a Q&A application inspired by the Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), which is comprised of an in-house database indexed on the arXiv articles related to the Electron-Ion Collider (EIC) experiment - one of the largest international scientific collaboration and incorporated an open-source LLaMA model for answer generation. This is an extension to it’s proceeding application built on proprietary model and Cloud-hosted external knowledge-base for the EIC experiment. This locally-deployed RAG-system offers a cost-effective, resource-constraint alternative solution to build a RAG-assisted Q&A application on answering domain-specific queries in the field of experimental nuclear physics. This set-up facilitates data-privacy, avoids sending any pre-publication scientific data and information to public domain. Future improvement will expand the knowledge base to encompass heterogeneous EIC-related publications and reports and upgrade the application pipeline orchestration to the LangGraph framework.

1 简介Introduction

在人工智能（AI）时代，自然语言处理（NLP）和语言模型（LM）是根据周围环境或上下文对语言含义的本质进行编码的领域，以促进人工智能驱动的推理和决策任务。该领域的任何进步都会对科学、技术、商业等领域的人类生活产生巨大影响。 因此，在这些应用程序中产生的任何偏见或听起来确实不正确的结果都会在各个利益相关者之间产生跨领域的多米诺骨牌效应。目前最先进的语言模型，无论是专有的还是开源的，都是在互联网上提供的大量文本上进行训练的，表现出生成流畅、自信但实际上不正确的反应（俗称幻觉）的问题In the era of Artificial Intelligence(AI), Natural Language Processing(NLP) and Language Models(LM) are the domains that encode the essence of the meaning of language based on its surrounding or context to facilitate AI-driven reasoning and decision making tasks. Any advancement in this domain has created an enormous implications to the human lives across domains including science, technology, commerce and so on. As a consequence, any bias or confident sounding factually incorrect generated outcome imputed in those applications will create a domino effects across domains among the various stakeholders. The present state-of-the art Language Models either proprietary or open-source which are trained on exorbitantly large amount of text available in the internet exhibit this problem of generating fluent, confident but factually incorrect responses popularly known as hallucination[1, 2]。 2021年，Meta研究团队引入了检索增强生成（Retrieval Augmented Generation）的概念，又名RAG. 3 方法论和应用架构Methodology and Application Architecture

问答应用程序实现了一个由五个主要步骤组成的 RAG 管道：上下文摄取和预处理、分块、嵌入生成、通过相似性搜索进行检索以及通过提示模板编码的 LLM 条件答案生成。架构示意图如图所示The Q&A application implements a RAG-pipeline consisting of five major steps: context ingestion and pre-processing, chunking, embedding generation, retrieval via similarity search and LLM-conditioned answer generation encoded through prompt template. The schematic of the architecture is demonstrated in Figure1.

这个受 RAG 启发的问答应用程序的知识库是由 arXiv 预印本存储库中的 178 篇 EIC 相关研究文章构建的。 这些学术文章涵盖现象学、软件开发、探测器设计、加速器物理等研究领域。为了增强推理和检索保真度，与每篇文章相关的多个元数据（例如 arXiv ID、作者、出版年份等）与分块文本连接，然后嵌入并存储在矢量数据库中。这些附加信息有助于模型区分来自不同来源的语义相似的块，从而提高上下文准确性和可追溯性。这种方法允许系统引用正确的引文，而不是给出孤立或模糊的文本片段。The knowledge base of this RAG-inspired Q&A application is constructed with 178 EIC-related research articles from the arXiv preprint repository. These scholarly articles span research domains across phenomenology, software development, detector design, accelerator physics etc. To enhance inference and retrieval fidelity, several metadata associated with each article such as arXiv ID, authors, year of publication etc, are concatenated with chunked text, then embedded and stored in the vector database. This additional information helps the model differentiate between semantically similar chunks from different sources, improving contextual accuracy and traceability. This approach allows the system to refer to the correct citations rather than giving isolated or ambiguous text snippets.

选定的 arXiv 文章被分为固定大小的较小文本段，称为“块”The selected arXiv articles are divided into fixed-sized smaller text segments, known as "chunks"[8]. 递归字符文本分割器RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter来自浪链from LangChain[9]将每个文档拆分为长度为 120 和 180 个字符的“块”，连续块之间有 20 个字符重叠。这种重叠保留了块之间的语义连续性，并减少了推理过程中的任何人为碎片。 我们遵循早期工作的分块哲学splits each document into "chunks" of lengths 120 and 180 characters with a 20 character overlap between consecutive chunks. This overlap preserves semantic continuity across chunks and mitigate any artificial fragmentation during inference. We followed the chunking philosophy of the earlier work[5]，但是另外还与块大小进行了交叉验证，例如120 和 180 个字符。 确定最佳块大小是提升基于 RAG 的应用程序性能的重要因素，因为较小的块可能会提高精度，但可能会丢失重要信息，而较大的块会捕获更多内容，但可能会损害相关性, however additionally cross-validated with chunk sizes e.g. 120 and 180 characters. Determining the optimal chunk size is an important factor in elevating the performance of a RAG-based application, since smaller chunks may increase precision but risk missing essential information, while larger chunks capture more content but may compromise relevance[10].

随后，使用以下方法将每个块嵌入到 1024 维密集向量表示中：Subsequently, each of these chunks are embedded into a 1024 dimensional dense vector representation using themxbai-嵌入-大mxbai-embed-large模型由 Mixedbread AI 提供model provided by Mixedbread AI[11]. mxbai-嵌入-大mxbai-embed-large是一种基于 Transformer 的模型，有助于本地部署，在大规模文本嵌入基准 (MTEB) 中拥有出色的性能记录is a transformer-based model that facilitates local deployability, has a strong performance record in the Massive Text Embedding Benchmark(MTEB)[12]并且没有 API 依赖性。外部知识库被保存到持久存储系统中。探索了多种数据库选项，包括 FAISSand no API-dependency. The external knowledge base is saved into a persistence storage system. Several database options were explored, including FAISS[13], 松果, Pinecone[7], 兰斯数据库, LanceDB[14]和 ChromaDB, and ChromaDB[15]，有效地存储嵌入的块。虽然 FAISS 提供高性能的相似性搜索； Pinecone 提供了具有可扩展性的托管、基于云的服务，选择 ChromaDB 是因为它通过本地部署结合了数据隐私的实际优势，并无缝集成到 LangChain 编排框架中，简化了 RAG 管道的构建和实施。, to efficiently store the embedded chunks. While FAISS offers high-performance similarity search; Pinecone provides a managed, cloud-based service with scalability, ChromaDB was chosen due to its combination of practical advantages of data privacy through local deployment and seamless integration into the LangChain orchestration framework, simplifying the construction and implementation of the RAG pipeline.

检索阶段提取与用户查询相关的语义最一致的文档。用户的查询被编码成一个 1024 维的向量，使用The retrieval stage extract the most semantically aligned documents related to the user’s query. The users’ query is encoded into a 1024-dimensional vector using themxbai-嵌入-大mxbai-embed-large嵌入模型，与向量数据库中存储的索引文档具有相同的维度。 检索阶段旨在检索最符合用户查询意图的文档。检索到的文档的有效性在很大程度上取决于嵌入的质量和编码语义相似性所采用的策略，因为它们直接影响最相关的文档。尽管之前的研究已经提出了许多相似性度量，但本研究采用了两种策略：余弦相似性和最大边际相关性（MMR）embedding model, the same dimension as the indexed documents stored in the vector database. The retrieval stage seeks to retrieve documents that best match the intent of the user’s query. The effectiveness of the retrieved documents heavily depends on both the quality of the embeddings and the strategy adopted to encode the semantic similarity, as they directly influence the most pertinent documents. Although many similarity metrics have been proposed in prior research, this study adopted two strategies: cosine similarity and Maximum Marginal Relevance(MMR)[16].

余弦相似度通过两个非零嵌入向量之间角度的余弦来衡量两个非零嵌入向量之间的角度对齐Cosine Similarity measures the angular alignment between two non-zero embedding vectors by the cosine of the angle between them[17]。它们之间的角度捕获了语义相似性，并且最相似的检索上下文获得了最高分。然而，它对于嵌入向量的大小是不变的，嵌入向量可以编码重要性或置信度等信息，从而能够产生冗余结果. The angle between them captures semantic similarity and the most similar retrieved contexts attains the top scores. However, it is invariant to the magnitude of the embedding vectors which may encode information such as importance or confidence, enabling to produce redundant results[18]。 MMR 通过平衡相关性和多样性来扩展这一点：在初始基于余弦的检索之后，它迭代地选择与之前选择的文档相关且最小相似的文档. MMR extends this by balancing relevance and diversity: after an initial cosine-based retrieval, it iteratively selects documents that are both relevant and minimally similar to previous chosen ones[16]，减少了冗余，提高了检索内容的多样性, which reduces the redundancy and improves the diversity of the retrieved content[19].

检索到的前 20 个块被连接到提示中，并通过 LLaMA 3.2 或 LLaMA 3.3 模型传递，这些模型部署在本地，不依赖任何 API。该提示提供了护栏，以限制仅根据所提供的上下文生成答案，并避免任何错误的响应。 LangSmith 精心策划了引文追踪机制Top 20 retrieved chunks are concatenated into a prompt and passed through either LLaMA 3.2 or LLaMA 3.3 model, which are deployed on-premises without any API-dependency. The prompt provides guardrails to restrict answer generation grounded on the provided context only and refrain from any erroneous responses. A citation tracing mechanism is orchestrated through LangSmith[6]，它跟踪生成的特定 arXiv 文章的答案，从中检索相关和支持上下文，从而为答案奠定基础。 LangSmith 有助于记录和跟踪推理管道的每个步骤：用户查询、检索内容以及相关元数据、将这些信息传播到 LM 的提示以及生成的答案。这使得问答系统成为透明的研究辅助系统。, which traces the generated answers to specific arXiv articles from which the relevant and supporting context is retrieved, thereby grounding the answers. LangSmith facilitates the recording and tracing of each step of the inference pipeline: the user query, retrieved content along with the associated metadata, the prompt which propagated these information to the LMs and the generated answer. This allows the Q&A system system into a transparent research assistance.

4 结果分析与讨论Results Analysis and Discussion

为了推断 RAG 系统的性能，通过与专家策划的数据集、EIC 相关问题及其最佳解决方案的集合进行比较来评估生成的答案。To infer of the performance of the RAG-system, the generated answers are evaluated by comparing with an expert curated dataset, a collection of the EIC-related questions and their optimal solutions.

4.1 基准数据集Benchmark Dataset

AI4EIC2023_DATASETS 是高质量基准数据集，包含一组 51 个问题的真实答案The AI4EIC2023_DATASETS is the high quality benchmark dataset that contains the ground truth answers of a set of 51 questions[5]。 为了评估 RAG 应用程序生成的输出，我们精心策划了这个黄金标准数据集。这些真实问题答案是使用 GPT-4.0 模型生成的，来自 arXiv 预印本存储库中跨领域的 EIC 相关出版物，例如高能现象学 (hep.ph)、核实验 (nucl.ex) 等。该数据集中的每个问题都映射到预定义数量的称为“声明”的子部分、针对每个声明的单独答案以及整个问题的综合答案。 AI 生成的 QA 对经过人类专家的仔细审查，以创建用于验证 RAG 生成的响应的黄金真理。 还可以由注释者直接根据用户选择的 arXiv 文章生成问答对，遵循类似的“声明”问答结构，支持来自基于网络的注释界面的响应等。. To evaluate the output generated from the RAG-application, this gold standard dataset is curated. These ground truth question-answers are generated using GPT-4.0 model, contextualized from the EIC-related publications from the arXiv pre-print repository across domains e.g., high energy phenomenology(hep.ph), nuclear experiments(nucl.ex) etc. Each question in this dataset is mapped to a pre-defined number of sub-parts called "claims", individual answer against each claim and a comprehensive answer of the entire question. The AI-generated QA-pairs are meticulously vetted by human experts to create a gold-truth for validating the RAG-generated responses. There is also provision to generate question-answers pairs by an annotator directly from user-chosen arXiv articles following the similar Q&A structure of "claims", supporting responses etc. from the web-based annotation interface.

4.2 评价及结果分析Evaluation and result analysis

使用 RAGAS 框架评估应用程序的性能The performance of the application is evaluated with the RAGAS framework[20]编码为一组 6 个评估指标；上下文实体召回、上下文精确度、上下文召回、答案相关性、答案正确性和忠实性。 前三个指标用于验证生成的答案相对于检索到的上下文的基础性和真实性，而后三个指标则对生成的响应的事实准确性和语义相似性进行编码。我们探索了四种不同配置的评估分数：块大小 120 和 180 以及用于上下文检索的相似性度量 cosine 和 MMR。所有这些组合的分布分数如图所示encoded into a set of 6 evaluation metrics; Context Entity Recall, Context Precision, Context Recall, Answer Relevancy, Answer Correctness and Faithfulness. The first three metrics are to validate the groundedness and factuality of the generated answers with respect to the retrieved context, where as the later three metrics encode the factual accuracy and semantic similarity of the generated response. We have explored the evaluation scores across four different configurations: chunk sizes 120 and 180 and similarity metrics cosine & MMR for context retrieval. The distributions scores for all these combinations are shown in Figure4和图and Figure5。 通过独立测量检索和答案生成步骤的延迟来评估端到端 RAG 管道的性能。检索系统的性能通过检索延迟来体现：从提交查询到返回从知识库中检索到的最相关的 20 个文档之间所花费的时间。. The performance of the end-to-end RAG-pipeline is evaluated by measuring latency of the retrieval and answer generation steps independently. The retrieval systems’ performance is encapsulated via the retrieval latency: the time elapsed between the query submission and the return of the most relevant 20 documents retrieved from the knowledge base.

5 结论Conclusion

这项工作提出了针对 EIC 实验相关出版物定制的基于 RAG 的问答系统的概念验证设计、实施和定量评估。该系统可在本地部署，并基于开源组件构建，为基于云的存储和专有模型提供了经济高效且安全的替代方案。该倡议旨在实现大型国际科学合作的数据优先性和运营独立性。 该管道是使用外部知识库构建的，该知识库由 arXiv 中发布的 PDF 文章、开源组件组成：This work presents proof-of-concept design, implementation and quantitative evaluation of a RAG-based Question-answering system tailored to publications related to the EIC-experiment. The system is deployable on-premises and built on open-source components which provide a cost-effective and secure alternative to Cloud-based storage and proprietary model. The initiative is aligned towards data primacy and operational independence of large international scientific collaboration. The pipeline is constructed with an external knowledge base consisting of PDF articles published in arXiv, open-source components: themxbai-嵌入-大mxbai-embed-large用于文本嵌入的模型、用于持久存储的 ChromaDB、用于生成答案的 LLaMA 模型。该管道通过 LangChain 进行协调，而引文追踪则由 LangSmith 启用。 LLaMA 3.3 导致推理延迟增加了一个数量级，并且某些查询存在更大的可变性和极端异常值。这个更大的模型可能会带来更好的推理能力，但是由于计算限制，这一点在这项工作中无法得到验证。 结果还强调，模型缩放对延迟的影响比块大小和相似性指标等设计选择更大。 RAGAS 评估表明，块大小 180 提供最佳配置；然而，在我们的研究中，MMR 机制并没有表现出比余弦相似性有任何额外的优势。未来的工作将侧重于将额外的资源（例如 PowerPoint 演示文稿、wiki、白皮书、报告等）纳入知识库。即将进行的重大管道升级计划是迁移到 LangGraphmodel for text embedding, ChromaDB for persistent storage, LLaMA models for answer generation. The pipeline is orchestrated through LangChain, while citation tracing is enabled by LangSmith. LLaMA 3.3 results an order-of magnitude increase in inference latency along with wider variability and extreme outliers for some queries. This larger model may lead to better reasoning ability, however this could not be verified in this work owing to the compute constraint. The result also highlights that the model scaling has greater impact on the latency than the design choices such as chunk size and similarity metrics. The RAGAS evaluation established that chunk-size 180 offers optimal configuration; however, in our study, MMR mechanism does not demonstrate any added advantage over cosine similarity. Future work will focus on incorporating additional resources, such as PowerPoint presentations, wiki, white paper, reports, etc. into the knowledge base. An imminent major planned upgrade of the pipeline is to migrate to LangGraph[27]编排框架。orchestration framework.
