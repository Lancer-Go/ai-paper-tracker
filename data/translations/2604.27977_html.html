4(a)比较 D3-Gym 和 AutoSDT-5K 数据的训练compares training on data from D3-Gym against AutoSDT-5K(Li et al., 2025)，5K 指令-解决方案对的静态数据集，缺乏可执行环境或验证信号。当用于标准 SFT 时，D3-Gym 的样本效率更高：使用从 D3-Gym 采样的解决方案进行训练的 Qwen3-4B-Instruct 使用不到一半的训练样本，比其 AutoSDT-5K 基线实现了大约 30% 的相对改进，这可能是由于保证 D3-Gym 中任务质量的严格过滤。除了样本效率之外，D3-Gym 的可执行环境和验证信号还可以对完整的推理轨迹进行采样，解锁思维模型的训练——这是静态指令解决方案数据集不支持的功能。例如，当 Qwen3-4B 在其自己经过验证的推理轨迹上进行训练时，SR@3 从 6.9% 提高到 11.8%。, a static dataset of 5K instruction–solution pairs that lacks executable environments or verification signals. When used for standard SFT, D3-Gym is more sample-efficient: Qwen3-4B-Instruct trained on solutions sampled from D3-Gym achieves roughly 30% relative improvement over its AutoSDT-5K baseline using less than half the training samples, likely due to the rigorous filtering that guarantees task quality in D3-Gym. Beyond sample efficiency, D3-Gym’s executable environment and verification signals enable sampling full reasoning trajectories, unlocking training of thinking models — a capability that static instruction–solution datasets do not support. For example, Qwen3-4B improves from 6.9% to 11.8% SR@3 when trained on its own verified reasoning trajectories.

较大的模型在训练规模上表现出更一致的改进。Larger models show more consistent improvement with training scale.

This suggests that larger models are better able to extract useful signal from increasing amounts of training data, consistent with previous studies(Li et al., 2025; Jain et al., 2025).

不同任务类别的绩效都有所提高。Performance improves across diverse task categories.

5 结论Conclusion

我们推出 D3-Gym，这是第一个自动构建的可验证环境数据集，用于科学数据驱动的发现。对从 D3-Gym 采样的轨迹进行训练可以为跨尺度的开放权重模型带来一致且实质性的改进，从而缩小与强大的专有系统的差距。总体而言，D3-Gym 代表了在科学数据驱动的发现中推进语言模型的多功能资源。We introduce D3-Gym, the first automatically constructed dataset of verifiable environments for scientific data-driven discovery. Training on trajectories sampled from D3-Gym yield consistent and substantial improvements for open-weight models across scales, narrowing the gap with strong proprietary systems. Overall, D3-Gym represents a versatile resource for advancing language models in scientific data-driven discovery.

附录AAppendix A局限性Limitations

我们认识到以下限制和未来的工作方向：We recognize the following limitations and future work directions:

D3-健身房环境量表。D3-Gym Environment Scale.

D3-Gym 目前包含 565 项任务，与软件工程等其他领域的培训环境相比仍然相对有限。然而，这反映了采购高质量、具有科学意义的任务的固有困难，而不是我们工作流程的限制，只要有足够的合适存储库可用，我们就可以轻松收集更多任务。此外，D3-Gym 的可执行和可验证性质可以对数千个训练轨迹进行采样，我们的结果证实这足以在模型大小上产生实质性改进。D3-Gym currently comprises 565 tasks, which remains relatively limited compared to training environments in other domains such as software engineering. However, this reflects the inherent difficulty of sourcing high-quality, scientifically meaningful tasks rather than a constraint of our workflow, which can readily collect more tasks provided that sufficient suitable repositories are available. Moreover, the executable and verifiable nature of D3-Gym enables sampling thousands of training trajectories, which our results confirm is sufficient to yield substantial improvements across model sizes.

训练方法。Training method.

我们专注于 RFT 作为我们的训练方法，因为它提供了密集的监督，当基础模型性能较弱时尤其有效，就像我们训练的开放权重模型的情况一样。我们的结果证实了这种方法在扩展所有规模的模型功能方面的有效性。虽然我们的可验证训练环境也将支持包括强化学习在内的其他技术，但模型的有限基线能力、数据驱动的发现任务的复杂性以及奖励信号的稀疏性使这成为我们未来工作的一个具有挑战性的方向。We focus on RFT as our training method, as it provides dense supervision that is particularly effective when base model performance is weak, as is the case for the open-weight models we train. Our results confirm the effectiveness of this approach in expanding model capabilities across all scales. While our verifiable training environment would also support other techniques including reinforcement learning, the limited baseline capabilities of the models, the complexity of data-driven discovery tasks, and the sparsity of the reward signal make this a challenging direction that we leave for future work.
