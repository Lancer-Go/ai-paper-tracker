通过可持续和弹性工业网络中的自适应阈值和自主决策进行联邦学习的代理信任协调Agentic Trust Coordination for Federated Learning through Adaptive Thresholding and Autonomous Decision Making in Sustainable and Resilient Industrial Networks

Paul Shepherd1,2, Tasos Dagiuklas1, Bugra Alkan1, Jonathan Rodriguez2
摘要Abstract

工业网络中的分布式智能越来越多地跨异构和资源受限的设备集成传感、通信和计算。联邦学习 (FL) 可以在此类环境中进行协作模型训练，但其可靠性会受到不一致的客户端行为、嘈杂的传感条件以及错误或对抗性更新的存在的影响。基于信任的机制通常用于减轻这些影响，但大多数仍然是统计和启发式的，依赖于固定参数或简单的自适应规则，难以适应不断变化的操作条件。Distributed intelligence in industrial networks increasingly integrates sensing, communication, and computation across heterogeneous and resource constrained devices. Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training in such environments, but its reliability is affected by inconsistent client behaviour, noisy sensing conditions, and the presence of faulty or adversarial updates. Trust based mechanisms are commonly used to mitigate these effects, yet most remain statistical and heuristic, relying on fixed parameters or simple adaptive rules that struggle to accommodate changing operating conditions.

本文提出了一种用于可持续和弹性工业网络中的 FL 的轻量级代理信任协调方法。所提出的代理信任控制层作为服务器端控制循环运行，观察信任相关信号和系统级信号，解释它们随时间的演变，并在检测到不稳定时应用有针对性的信任调整。该方法通过启用上下文感知干预决策来扩展先前的自适应信任机制，而不是依赖于固定或纯粹反应性的参数更新。通过明确分离观察、推理和行动，所提出的框架支持稳定的 FL 操作，而无需修改客户端训练或增加通信开销。This paper presents a lightweight agentic trust coordination approach for FL in sustainable and resilient industrial networks. The proposed Agentic Trust Control Layer operates as a server side control loop that observes trust related and system level signals, interprets their evolution over time, and applies targeted trust adjustments when instability is detected. The approach extends prior adaptive trust mechanisms by enabling context aware intervention decisions, rather than relying on fixed or purely reactive parameter updates. By explicitly separating observation, reasoning, and action, the proposed framework supports stable FL operation without modifying client side training or increasing communication overhead.

I 简介Introduction

工业网络越来越多地融入分布式智能，以支持地理上和操作上分散的资产的监控、控制和优化。基于学习的方法通常部署在网络边缘，其中传感质量、计算能力和通信可靠性因设备而异。在这种环境中，随着时间的推移保持稳定可靠的学习行为是操作连续性和长期系统可持续性的关键要求。Industrial networks increasingly incorporate distributed intelligence to support monitoring, control, and optimisation across geographically and operationally dispersed assets. Learning based methods are commonly deployed at the network edge, where sensing quality, computational capability, and communication reliability vary across devices. In such environments, maintaining stable and dependable learning behaviour over time is a key requirement for both operational continuity and long term system sustainability.

联邦学习已成为在工业环境中训练共享模型的实用方法，在工业环境中，由于隐私、监管或操作限制，数据集中化是不可行或不可取的。通过将训练数据保存在本地并在协调服务器上聚合模型更新，FL 减少了数据暴露和通信开销。然而，实际部署仍然对不一致的客户端行为、间歇性参与、不一致的数据分布和数据质量的变化敏感。这些因素会影响全局模型的准确性和训练过程的稳定性。Federated learning has emerged as a practical approach for training shared models in industrial settings where data centralisation is infeasible or undesirable due to privacy, regulatory, or operational constraints. By keeping training data local and aggregating model updates at a coordinating server, FL reduces data exposure and communication overhead. However, real deployments remain sensitive to inconsistent client behaviour, intermittent participation, non identical data distributions, and variations in data quality. These factors affect both global model accuracy and the stability of the training process.

引入基于信任的机制来应对这些挑战，方法是评估客户端更新的可靠性并调节其对全局模型的影响，补充聚合规则和其他稳健性技术。现有方法通常根据更新相似性、参与一致性或时间行为等统计属性来计算信任分数。虽然此类机制可以提高鲁棒性，但它们通常依赖于固定阈值或手动定义的适应规则。随着操作条件的变化，这些静态或弱自适应设计可能会导致响应延迟、过度排斥或振荡信任行为，这在长寿命工业系统中是不受欢迎的。Trust based mechanisms have been introduced to address these challenges by evaluating the reliability of client updates and regulating their influence on the global model, complementing aggregation rules and other robustness techniques. Existing approaches typically compute trust scores from statistical properties such as update similarity, participation consistency, or temporal behaviour. While such mechanisms can improve robustness, they often depend on fixed thresholds or manually defined adaptation rules. As operating conditions evolve, these static or weakly adaptive designs can lead to delayed responses, excessive exclusion, or oscillatory trust behaviour, which is undesirable in long lived industrial systems.

我们早期工作 [1,2] 的自适应信任评分缩放系统过滤 (ATSSSF) 的最新实现将 TOPSIS [1,3] 排名方法与 EMA 平滑 [1] 相结合，以识别和限制不可靠的贡献（权重），结果表明，固定阈值和恒定平滑参数限制了 ATSSSF 在动态变化的客户端可靠性条件下的灵活性，而动态调整信任参数可以提高变化条件下的稳定性。尽管如此，这些方法仍然将自适应视为参数调整问题而不是控制问题。决策通常是由局部指标偏差触发的，而没有对整体训练状态的明确解释。因此，适应在很大程度上仍然是反应性的，并且可能无法区分瞬态干扰和持续不稳定。Recent implementations of our earlier work [1,2], the Adaptive Trust Score Scaling System Filtering (ATSSSF) which combined the TOPSIS [1,3] ranking method with EMA smoothing [1] to identify and limit unreliable contributions (weights), has shown that fixed thresholds and constant smoothing parameters restrict ATSSSF’s flexibility under dynamically changing client reliability conditions, and that dynamically adjusting trust parameters can improve stability under changing conditions. Nevertheless, these methods still treat adaptation as a parameter tuning problem rather than a control problem. Decisions are commonly triggered by local metric deviations without an explicit interpretation of overall training state. As a result, adaptation remains largely reactive and may fail to distinguish transient disturbances from sustained instability.

本文通过引入 FL 的代理信任控制视角来解决这一限制[4]。所提出的方法不是将适应逻辑直接嵌入到信任更新规则中，而是引入了一个轻量级服务器端控制循环，该循环观察信任相关信号和系统级信号，解释它们随时间的演变，并选择适当的信任控制操作。通过明确分离观察、推理和行动，该方法可以实现上下文感知干预，同时保持简单性、可解释性和可部署性。This paper addresses this limitation by introducing an agentic trust control perspective for FL [4]. Instead of embedding adaptation logic directly into trust update rules, the proposed approach introduces a lightweight server side control loop that observes trust related and system level signals, interprets their evolution over time, and selects appropriate trust control actions. By explicitly separating observation, reasoning, and action, the approach enables context aware intervention while preserving simplicity, interpretability, and deployability.

Section II discusses background concepts and limitations of existing trust based FL approaches. Section III introduces the system model and design considerations. Section IV presents the proposed agentic trust control methodology. Section V discusses expected behaviour and implications for sustainability and resilience in industrial networks. Section VI concludes the paper and outlines directions for future work.

II 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

联邦学习 (FL) 支持跨分布式客户端进行协作模型训练，同时保留数据局部性，这使其对工业和边缘环境具有吸引力。然而，数据分布、设备功能、通信可靠性和参与模式的异构性会带来不稳定性，从而降低收敛性和模型可靠性[5,6]。联合平均 (FedAvg) 等经典聚合方案及其扩展（例如 FedProx）[7,8] 解决了系统异构性，但仍然与信任无关，隐含地假设参与者是良性且可靠的。在现实的工业部署中，这种假设越来越无效。Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training across distributed clients while preserving data locality, making it attractive for industrial and edge environments. However, heterogeneity in data distributions, device capabilities, communication reliability, and participation patterns introduces instability that degrades convergence and model reliability [5,6]. Classical aggregation schemes such as Federated Averaging (FedAvg) and its extensions (e.g., FedProx) [7,8] address system heterogeneity but remain trust agnostic, implicitly assuming benign and reliable participants. In realistic industrial deployments, this assumption is increasingly invalid.

为了解决鲁棒性限制，信任感知 FL 框架将每个客户端与在训练轮次中不断变化的信任评分相关联。信任分数通常源自结构化行为信号，包括更新相似性、训练稳定性、参与一致性和绩效指标。通过基于信任来加权或忽略客户贡献，这些方法将时间记忆引入聚合过程，并能够对不可靠或对抗性行为进行长期监管。To address robustness limitations, trust aware FL frameworks associate each client with a trust score that evolves across training rounds. Trust scores are typically derived from structured behavioural signals, including update similarity, training stability, participation consistency, and performance indicators. By weighting or omitting client contributions based on trust, these approaches introduce temporal memory into the aggregation process and enable longer term regulation of unreliable or adversarial behaviour.

ATSSSF 框架 [1,2] 代表了该范例的轻量级且可解释的实例。 ATSSSF 采用多标准决策，使用 TOPSIS 和基于熵的加权对客户进行排名，而指数移动平均 (EMA) 平滑引入了时间感知。这种设计可以有效缓解数据中毒和标签翻转攻击，而无需增加通信开销或修改客户端训练[1,2]。其服务器端操作以及与 Flower [9] 等框架的兼容性使其适合 MEC 和工业物联网部署。The ATSSSF framework [1,2] represents a lightweight and interpretable instantiation of this paradigm. ATSSSF employs multi-criteria decision making using TOPSIS with entropy based weighting to rank clients, while Exponential Moving Average (EMA) smoothing introduces temporal awareness. This design enables efficient mitigation of data poisoning and label flipping attacks without increasing communication overhead or modifying client-side training [1,2]. Its serverside operation and compatibility with frameworks such as Flower [9] make it suitable for MEC and industrial IoT deployments.

尽管有这些优点，ATSSSF 还是通过预定义的操作规则对适应进行编码。遗漏阈值和 EMA 参数是手动指定的，并根据本地指标偏差进行反应性调整。在高度动态的环境中，这种基于规则的适应可能会导致响应延迟、客户包含振荡或瞬态噪声与持续行为漂移之间的区分不足。在其他自适应信任和零信任聚合方案中也观察到类似的限制，这些方案要么依赖于静态控制逻辑，要么会产生过高的计算开销[11]。Despite these advantages, ATSSSF encodes adaptation through predefined operational rules. Omission thresholds and EMA parameters are manually specified and adjusted reactively in response to local metric deviations. In highly dynamic environments, such rule-based adaptation can lead to delayed responses, oscillatory client inclusion, or insufficient discrimination between transient noise and sustained behavioural drift. Similar limitations are observed in other adaptive trust and zero-trust aggregation schemes, which either rely on static control logic or incur prohibitive computational overhead [11].

代理和自主计算框架通过将适应框架视为控制问题而不是参数调整任务，提供了另一种视角[10]。在这些框架中，系统级信号被联合观察、解释以推断操作状态，并用于通过显式感知推理动作循环来选择上下文相关的控制动作。轻量级代理设计强调确定性、可解释性和有界决策逻辑，非常符合工业和安全关键系统的要求。Agentic and autonomic computing frameworks offer an alternative perspective by framing adaptation as a control problem rather than a parameter tuning task [10]. In these frameworks, system level signals are jointly observed, interpreted to infer an operating state, and used to select context dependent control actions through an explicit perception reasoning action loop. Lightweight agentic designs emphasise determinism, interpretability, and bounded decision logic, aligning well with the requirements of industrial and safety critical systems.

这项工作直接建立在 ATSSSF 之上，引入了代理信任控制循环 (ATCL)，该循环监督信任适应，而不是重新定义信任度量或聚合规则 [11,12]。 ATCL 保留了 ATSSSF 的结构化信任信号和效率，同时用明确的观察分析决策行动周期取代了预定义的适应规则。通过从联合信任和性能信号推断系统状态，ATCL 实现了上下文感知信任调节，区分瞬态干扰和持续不稳定，代表了 FL 中从自适应信任扩展到代理监督信任控制的自然演变。This work builds directly on ATSSSF by introducing an Agentic Trust Control Loop (ATCL) that supervises trust adaptation rather than redefining trust metrics or aggregation rules [11,12]. ATCL retains the structured trust signals and efficiency of ATSSSF while replacing predefined adaptation rules with an explicit observation–analysis decision action cycle. By inferring system states from joint trust and performance signals, ATCL enables context aware trust regulation that distinguishes between transient disturbances and sustained instability, representing a natural evolution from adaptive trust scaling to agent supervised trust control in FL.

三、III系统模型和设计目标System Model and Design Goals

我们考虑一个同步 FL 系统，其中中央协调服务器管理一组分布式工业客户端的协作模型训练。每个客户端对私有数据执行本地优化，并定期将模型更新传输到服务器，服务器聚合收到的更新以生成在后续轮次中重新分配的全局模型。该系统在部分和随时间变化的参与下运行，反映了现实的工业和 MEC 部署，其特点是异构计算资源、不同分布的数据、可变的传感质量和间歇性连接。假定服务器是可信的，但不对各个客户端的可靠性做出任何假设。We consider a synchronous FL system in which a central coordination server manages collaborative model training across a set of distributed industrial clients. Each client performs local optimisation on private data and periodically transmits model updates to the server, which aggregates the received updates to produce a global model that is redistributed in subsequent rounds. The system operates under partial and time varying participation, reflecting realistic industrial and MEC deployments characterised by heterogeneous computational resources, non-identically distributed data, variable sensing quality, and intermittent connectivity. The server is assumed to be trusted, while no assumptions are made regarding the reliability of individual clients.

在这种情况下，由于传感器噪声、数据漂移、资源限制和通信中断等良性因素，以及包括数据中毒或模型操纵在内的对抗性行为，客户端行为可能会偏离预期规范。这些影响可以在服务器上通过结构化信号观察到，包括异常梯度模式、更新波动性升高、各轮参与不一致以及全局损失趋势恶化。现有最先进 (SOTA) 信任感知 FL 机制利用这些信号的子集来调节聚合；然而，它们通常依赖于反应性的、特定于指标的规则，并且不明确地解释随时间变化的系统级行为。In this setting, client behaviour may deviate from expected norms due to benign factors such as sensor noise, data drift, resource constraints, and communication disruptions, as well as adversarial actions including data poisoning or model manipulation. These effects are observable at the server through structured signals, including abnormal gradient patterns, elevated update volatility, inconsistent participation across rounds, and degradation in global loss trends. Existing State-Of-The-Art (SOTA) trust aware FL mechanisms utilise subsets of these signals to regulate aggregation; however, they typically rely on reactive, metric-specific rules and do not explicitly interpret system level behaviour across time.

所提出的 ATCL 是作为一种轻量级的服务器端监督机制引入的，该机制在聚合之前调节信任适应。 ATCL 不会修改客户端训练程序、本地优化目标或通信协议。相反，它根据服务器上已有的行为和性能信号进行操作，从而能够与现有 FL 框架直接集成。这种定位使 ATCL 能够补充而不是取代已建立的信任感知聚合机制。The proposed ATCL is introduced as a lightweight, server-side supervisory mechanism that regulates trust adaptation prior to aggregation. ATCL does not modify client side training procedures, local optimisation objectives, or communication protocols. Instead, it operates on behavioural and performance signals already available at the server, enabling straightforward integration with existing FL frameworks. This positioning allows ATCL to complement, rather than replace, established trust aware aggregation mechanisms.

ATCL 的设计受到当前自适应信任方法的限制的启发，该方法通过预定义的更新规则和静态控制逻辑对自适应进行编码。虽然与固定阈值基线相比，此类方法提高了鲁棒性，但它们仍然是反应性的，并且可能对瞬态噪声和持续的行为退化做出类似的响应。 ATCL 通过将信任适应视为一个控制问题，超越了这一最先进的技术 (BSOTA)，其中联合解释多维信号以推断操作条件并选择适合上下文的控制操作。The design of ATCL is motivated by limitations in current adaptive trust approaches, which encode adaptation through predefined update rules and static control logic. While such methods improve robustness compared to fixed threshold baselines, they remain reactive and may respond similarly to transient noise and sustained behavioural degradation. ATCL moves Beyond this State-Of-The-Art (BSOTA) by framing trust adaptation as a control problem, in which multi-dimensional signals are jointly interpreted to infer operating conditions and select context-appropriate control actions.

因此，ATCL 旨在通过减轻不可靠或敌对的客户端影响而不破坏训练的稳定性来增强弹性，通过限制不必要的计算、通信开销和振荡客户端排除来支持可持续性，并通过轻量级、可解释和确定性控制逻辑保持可部署性。这些设计目标使 ATCL 与本次研讨会针对的可持续和弹性工业 FL 系统的实际要求保持一致。Accordingly, ATCL is designed to enhance resilience by mitigating unreliable or adversarial client influence without destabilising training, to support sustainability by limiting unnecessary computation, communication overhead, and oscillatory client exclusion, and to remain deployable through lightweight, interpretable, and deterministic control logic. These design goals align ATCL with the practical requirements of sustainable and resilient industrial FL systems targeted by this workshop.

IV 方法论methodology

代理信任控制环 (ATCL) 通过用在 FL 服务器上执行的连续观察分析决策-动作控制循环替换预定义和本地反应性自适应规则来扩展自适应 ATSSSF 机制 [1]。虽然 ATSSSF 引入了自适应阈值和时间信任演化，但其自适应逻辑仍然以规则驱动和参数为中心。 ATCL 通过引入显式监督层来推广这种方法，该监督层根据推断的系统状态而不是直接的度量响应来协调信任适应。The Agentic Trust Control Loop (ATCL) extends adaptive ATSSSF mechanisms [1] by replacing predefined and locally reactive adaptation rules with a continuous observation analysis decision–action control cycle executed at the FL server. While ATSSSF introduced adaptive thresholding and temporal trust evolution, its adaptation logic remains rule driven and parameter centric. ATCL generalises this approach by introducing an explicit supervisory layer that coordinates trust adaptation based on inferred system states rather than direct metric responses.

控制循环首先对一组信任相关信号进行建模，这些信号表征跨联合回合的客户端行为。这些信号包括从梯度相似性模式得出的统计信任分数、捕获本地更新中不稳定性的波动性指标、将个体客户行为与联盟规范概况进行比较的相似性指标、反映长期可靠性的参与一致性度量以及模型级指标（例如全局损失趋势或异常收敛行为）。 ATCL 没有将这些指标视为独立的启发式方法，而是将它们集成到代表 FL 流程当前健康状况的结构化且可解释的代理状态中。The control loop begins by modelling a set of trust relevant signals that characterise client behaviour across federated rounds. These signals include statistical trust scores derived from gradient similarity patterns, volatility indicators capturing instability in local updates, similarity metrics comparing individual client behaviour to the federation’s normative profile, participation consistency measures reflecting long term reliability, and model level indicators such as global loss trends or anomalous convergence behaviour. Rather than treating these indicators as independent heuristics, ATCL integrates them into a structured and interpretable agent state representing the current health of the FL process.

根据推断的代理状态，轻量级推理层执行控制循环的分析阶段。目前，该推理组件采用基于规则或最小代理决策逻辑，并且故意不基于 LLM，在从聚合信任和系统信号派生的低维结构化状态表示上运行。通过随着时间的推移联合评估多个信任和系统指标，代理可以检测新出现的行为模式、与名义操作的持续偏差以及恢复趋势。这使得 ATCL 能够区分瞬态噪声与持续不稳定或对抗行为，解决了阈值驱动信任适应的关键限制。Based on the inferred agent state, a lightweight reasoning layer executes the analysis phase of the control loop. For the moment, this reasoning component employs rule based or minimally agentic decision logic and is deliberately non–LLM-based, operating over a low dimensional, structured state representation derived from aggregated trust and system signals. By jointly evaluating multiple trust and system indicators over time, the agent detects emerging behavioural patterns, sustained deviations from nominal operation, and recovery trends. This enables ATCL to distinguish transient noise from persistent instability or adversarial behaviour, addressing a key limitation of threshold driven trust adaptation.

当推理阶段确定需要干预时，ATCL 会发出有针对性的、以状态为条件的信任控制操作。这些行动包括动态引导遗漏阈值、调整管理信任评分演变的指数移动平均 (EMA) 平滑因子、暂时排除不稳定或低信任度的客户，以及在稳定性恢复后逐步恢复之前排除的参与者。通过这种方式，ATCL 保留了 ATSSSF 的计算简单性和服务器端可部署性，同时实现了更多上下文感知、实时和细粒度的信任协调。When the reasoning phase determines that intervention is required, ATCL issues targeted and state conditioned trust control actions. These actions include dynamically steering omission thresholds, adapting exponential moving average (EMA) smoothing factors governing trust score evolution, temporarily excluding unstable or low trust clients, and progressively reinstating previously excluded participants once stability is restored. In this manner, ATCL preserves the computational simplicity and server side deployability of ATSSSF while enabling more context aware, real time, and fine grained trust coordination.

V 预期结果Expected Results

尽管计划在后期阶段进行全面的实验，但所提出的框架的预期行为是通过使用 ATSSSF [1,2] 获得的早期自适应 FL 结果来确定的。在这些研究中，自适应阈值和自适应 EMA 平滑提高了遗漏效率，减少了对抗期间的不稳定性，并产生了比固定参数基线更稳定的全局模型性能。基于这些发现，ATCL 预计将通过将代理行为 [3,4] 引入信任管道来提供额外的改进。代理观察不断变化的系统信号、分析行为趋势和自主选择干预措施的能力使信任监管从被动调整转变为主动模式识别。Although full experimentation is planned for later stages, the expected behaviour of the proposed framework is informed by earlier adaptive FL results obtained using ATSSSF [1,2]. In those studies, adaptive thresholding and adaptive EMA smoothing improved omission efficiency, reduced instability during adversarial periods, and produced more stable global model performance than fixed parameter baselines. Building on these findings, the ATCL is expected to provide additional improvements by introducing agentic behaviour [3,4] into the trust pipeline. The agent’s ability to observe evolving system signals, analyse behavioural trends, and autonomously select interventions allows trust regulation to shift from reactive adjustment to proactive pattern recognition.

通过其观察分析决策行动周期，ATCL 有望比基于阈值的机制更早地检测到新出现的不稳定性，并在模型退化变得显着之前应用有针对性的修正，例如阈值转向或平滑调整。由于这些操作是由上下文推理而不是静态统计规则驱动的，因此遗漏决策应该变得更准确、更具情境意识，并且对固定超参数不太敏感。Through its observation analysis decision action cycle, ATCL is expected to detect emerging instability earlier than threshold based mechanisms and apply targeted corrections such as threshold steering or smoothing adjustments before model degradation becomes significant. Because these actions are driven by contextual reasoning rather than static statistical rules, omission decisions should become more accurate, more situationally aware, and less sensitive to fixed hyperparameters.

ATCL 的代理性质也支持更稳定的信任评分演化。代理可以解释变化是否代表噪音、短暂影响或持续的行为漂移，而不是机械地对每个偏差做出反应，从而实现更加深思熟虑和有选择性的干预。这种行为预计将减少信任轨迹的波动性，并减轻静态过滤器中经常观察到的振荡排除周期。此外，代理整合多个行为线索的能力更新相似性、参与一致性、波动性指标和模型级别趋势，应该可以改善对不一致或敌对客户的检测，超出纯粹统计规则所能达到的效果。The agentic nature of ATCL also supports more stable trust score evolution. Instead of responding mechanically to each deviation, the agent can interpret whether changes represent noise, transient effects, or sustained behavioural drift, enabling more deliberate and selective interventions. This behaviour is expected to reduce volatility in trust trajectories and mitigate oscillatory exclusion cycles often observed in static filters. Furthermore, the agent’s ability to integrate multiple behavioural cues update similarity, participation consistency, volatility indicators, and model level trends should improve detection of inconsistent or adversarial clients beyond what purely statistical rules can achieve.

尽管有这些增强，计算开销仍然很低，因为代理使用聚合信号专门在服务器上运行，而不修改客户端执行。总而言之，预计结果表明，诸如 ATCL 之类的代理驱动信任机制可以为在异构和动态条件下运行的 FL 系统提供更高的鲁棒性、稳定性和可解释性。Despite these enhancements, computational overhead remains low because the agent operates exclusively at the server using aggregated signals, without modifying client side execution. Taken together, the projected outcomes suggest that an agent driven trust mechanism such as ATCL can deliver greater robustness, stability, and interpretability in FL systems operating under heterogeneous and dynamic conditions.

VI 结论和未来发展Conclusion and Future Developments

这项工作引入了 FL 的代理信任控制环 (ATCL)，将早期的自适应 ATSSSF 机制扩展到通用观察分析决策操作框架。通过对与信任相关的行为信号进行建模、应用轻量级推理并发出有针对性的干预措施，ATCL 为适合大规模和多域 FL 部署的代理信任协调提供了可行且可扩展的路径。尽管仍处于开发的早期阶段，但其预计的好处是基于先前的自适应 ATSSSF 结果，该结果证明了模型稳定性、遗漏准确性以及对不可靠或不一致客户的恢复能力方面的改进。 ATCL 预计将通过实现更多情境感知干预并防止短期噪音或对抗性扰动引起的波动来增强这些成果。This work introduces an agentic trust control loop (ATCL) for FL, extending earlier adaptive ATSSSF mechanisms into a general observation analysis decision action framework. By modelling trust relevant behavioural signals, applying lightweight reasoning, and issuing targeted interventions, ATCL provides a feasible and scalable path toward agentic trust coordination suited to large scale and multi domain FL deployments. Although still in an early stage of development, its projected benefits are grounded in prior adaptive ATSSSF results, which demonstrated improvements in model stability, omission accuracy, and resilience to unreliable or inconsistent clients. ATCL is expected to enhance these outcomes by enabling more context aware intervention and by preventing volatility caused by short term noise or adversarial perturbations.

拟议的创新轨迹遵循从 ATCL 的实际实施开始的分阶段进展。第一阶段用代理驱动的信任控制器取代了固定的统计阈值，该控制器能够识别不稳定性、解释训练状态信号并完全在服务器端执行有针对性的信任调整。除了这一直接贡献之外，该工作还概述了自组织联邦智能 (SOFI) 的长期研究方向：一个更广泛的、与 6G 一致的愿景，其中多个协调的代理模块自主管理信任动态、模型可靠性和系统范围的适应。 SOFI 代表架构端点，而 ATCL 则提供实现此类演进的可实施基础。The proposed trajectory of innovation follows a staged progression that begins with the practical implementation of ATCL. This first stage replaces fixed statistical thresholds with an agent driven trust controller capable of recognising instability, interpreting training state signals, and performing targeted trust adjustments entirely at the server side. Beyond this immediate contribution, the work outlines a longer term research direction toward Self Organising Federated Intelligence (SOFI): a broader, 6G aligned vision in which multiple coordinated agentic modules autonomously manage trust dynamics, model reliability, and system wide adaptation. While SOFI represents the architectural endpoint, ATCL provides the implementable foundation that enables such evolution.

未来的工作将侧重于在现实的联合环境中部署和评估 ATCL，评估其运营开销、与不同模型和客户端行为的交互以及其在现有 FL 平台中的集成要求。这将建立所提出框架的经验有效性，同时指导从代理信任控制逐步过渡到完全自组织的联邦智能系统。Future work will focus on deploying and evaluating ATCL in realistic federated environments, assessing its operational overhead, its interaction with diverse models and client behaviours, and its integration requirements within existing FL platforms. This will establish the empirical validity of the proposed framework while guiding the gradual transition from agentic trust control toward fully self organising federated intelligence systems.
