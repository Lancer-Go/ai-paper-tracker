简介Introduction

对数学人工智能（AI for Math）的追求代表了机器智能的一个重要前沿，其中平面几何是一个典型的巨大挑战。 成功解决几何问题需要视觉感知和数学推理的复杂相互作用——解析复杂的图表和文本，执行逻辑推论，甚至通过辅助结构展示创造力。 这一过程反映了从感知和认知到决策的过程，标志着通向通用人工智能（AGI）的关键途径。 因此，几何问题解决已成为前沿人工智能研究的焦点。The pursuit of Artificial Intelligence for Mathematics (AI for Math) represents a significant frontier in machine intelligence, with plane geometry standing out as a quintessential grand challenge. Successfully solving geometry problems requires a sophisticated interplay of visual perception and mathematical reasoning—parsing complex diagrams and text, performing logical deductions, and even exhibiting creativity through auxiliary constructions. This process mirrors the progression from perception and cognition to decision-making, marking a critical pathway toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). Consequently, geometry problem solving has become a focal point for cutting-edge AI research.

开创性的作品，例如 InterGPSPioneering works, such as InterGPS(Lu et al. 2021)和阿尔法几何and AlphaGeometry(Trinh et al. 2024; Chervonyi et al. 2025)，展示形式系统在实现高精度方面的力量。 然而，它们对形式化语言的依赖带来了巨大的开销，包括自然问题翻译过程中信息丢失的风险以及基于规则的系统固有的脆弱性。 此外，它们的符号性、非人类可读的输出对用户可读性提出了相当大的挑战。这些形式系统也总是不完整的，例如，interGPS 和 AlphaGeometry 都无法处理几何中的不等式或优化问题。, demonstrate the power of formal systems in achieving high precision. However, their reliance on formalized languages introduces significant overhead, including the risk of information loss during the translation from naturalistic problems and the inherent brittleness of rule-based systems. Furthermore, their symbolic, non-human-readable outputs pose considerable challenges for user readability. These formal systems are also invariably incomplete, for instance, neither interGPS nor AlphaGeometry can handle inequality or optimization problems in geometry.

以 Gemini 2.5 Pro 等系统为代表的大型多模态模型 (LMM) 的出现开辟了一条充满希望的新途径。 通过本地处理视觉语言信息，LMM 无需显式形式化，并提供自然的交互式用户体验。 然而，它们最先进的性能并非没有代价。 这些模型的计算成本很高，而且它们的推理可能不透明。 更重要的是，它们在几何等专业领域的表现通常受到其通用训练数据和几何图的特定符号逻辑之间的分布变化的限制。The advent of Large Multimodal Models (LMMs), exemplified by systems like Gemini 2.5 Pro, has opened a promising new avenue. By natively processing visuo-linguistic information, LMMs bypass the need for explicit formalization and offer a natural, interactive user experience. Nevertheless, their state-of-the-art performance is not without trade-offs. These models are computationally expensive, and their reasoning can be opaque. More critically, their performance on specialized domains like geometry is often constrained by distributional shifts between their generalist training data and the specific symbolic logic of geometric diagrams.

本文挑战了普遍的假设，即端到端 LMM 是复杂几何推理的最终解决方案。 我们证明，强大的大型语言模型（LLM）在加上专门的几何感知和演绎推理能力后，不仅可以匹配甚至超越领先的 LMM 的性能。 虽然像 DeepSeek-R1 这样的法学硕士拥有巨大的抽象推理能力，但它们固有的视觉处理能力的缺乏导致它们不适合此类任务。 我们的工作通过引入一种新颖的框架来直接解决这一限制，该框架旨在帮助法学硕士解决高级几何问题。其核心是两个关键组件：This paper challenges the prevailing assumption that end-to-end LMMs are the definitive solution for complex geometric reasoning. We demonstrate that a powerful Large Language Model (LLM), when augmented with specialized geometric perception and deductive reasoning capabilities, can not only match but surpass the performance of leading LMMs. While LLMs like DeepSeek-R1 possess immense abstract reasoning power, their inherent lack of visual processing has previously rendered them unsuitable for such tasks. Our work directly addresses this limitation by introducing a novel framework designed to empower LLMs for advanced geometric problem-solving. At its core are two critical components:

几何视觉解析器，将非结构化图表图像转换为符号、结构化表示。 通过集成 OCR 和几何基元检测，它为法学硕士提供了其天然缺乏的精确、高保真视觉信息。A Geometric Vision Parser, which translates unstructured diagram images into a symbolic, structured representation. By integrating OCR and geometric primitive detection, it provides the LLM with the precise, high-fidelity visual information it naturally lacks.

符号求解器模块，对角度关系执行有针对性的形式推导——这是法学硕士幻觉的常见来源A Symbolic Solver module, which performs targeted formal deduction on angular relationships—a frequent source of LLM hallucination(Wang et al. 2025a)。 这不仅提高了模型的准确性，而且还重要地引导其走向更优雅的演绎解决方案，使其远离困扰许多当前模型并降低可读性的脆弱、强力的坐标计算。. This not only enhances the model’s accuracy but also critically guides it towards more elegant, deductive solutions, steering it away from brittle, brute-force coordinate calculations that plague many current models and degrade readability.

更重要的是，它们的结构简单性无法挑战具有复杂的多步骤推导或需要辅助线路构建的模型。 为了解决这个问题，我们引入了一个新的、具有挑战性的基准，该基准源自 2025 年中国中考数学题——这是一个需要深度推理和创造性洞察力的难题的来源，同时也保证数据的新颖性。 由于这些问题来自最近的公开考试，因此它们不太可能包含在现有大型模型的预训练数据中，从而可以在不存在数据污染风险的情况下对几何推理能力进行公平评估。More importantly, their structural simplicity fails to challenge models with complex multi-step deductions or the necessity for auxiliary line constructions. To address this, we introduce a new, challenging benchmark derived from 2025 Chinese Zhongkao (national middle school examination) math problems—a source of difficult problems that demand deep reasoning and creative insight, while also guaranteeing data novelty. Since these questions are from recent public examinations, they are highly unlikely to have been included in the pre-training data of existing large models, thus enabling a fair evaluation of geometric reasoning capabilities without the risk of data contamination.

我们的贡献有三方面：Our contributions are threefold:

  • •

  • •

    新的几何视觉解析器和符号求解器模块，通过大幅减少模型对非直观、“强力”坐标几何的依赖，引导法学硕士进行类人的、可解释的推理，从而增强解决方案的可读性和用户友好性。New Geometric Vision Parser and Symbolic Solver modules that guide LLMs towards human-like, interpretable reasoning by substantially reducing the model’s reliance on non-intuitive, ”brute-force” coordinate geometry, thereby enhancing the readability and user-friendliness of the solutions.

相关工作Related Work

利用人工智能解决平面几何问题一直是一项长期挑战，需要对文本和图表进行复杂的多模式理解，以及严格的逻辑推导。 早期的努力依赖于符号系统，但符号系统已逐渐让位于数据驱动的神经网络和集成两种范式优势的强大混合架构。Solving plane geometry problems with AI has been a long-standing challenge, requiring both sophisticated multimodal understanding of text and diagrams, and rigorous logical deduction. Early efforts relied on symbolic systems, which have gradually given way to data-driven neural networks and powerful hybrid architectures that integrate the strengths of both paradigms.

基于符号的方法Symbolic-Based Methods

早期自动解决几何问题的方法主要是基于符号和规则的系统。 这些方法优先考虑逻辑健全性和可解释性。 他们通常使用精心设计的基于规则的解析器将自然语言文本转换为正式的符号语言。 图解基元还可以通过手动翻译或经过训练的图解析器转换为正式描述。 PGDP-网络Early approaches to automated geometry problem solving were dominated by symbolic and rule-based systems. These methods prioritize logical soundness and interpretability. They typically employ meticulously crafted rule-based parsers to convert the natural language text into a formal, symbolic language. The diagrammatic primitives are also converted into formal descriptions through manual translation or a trained diagram parser. PGDP-Net(Zhang et al. 2022)将几何元素识别建模为实体分割任务，并使用图神经网络（GNN）来识别元素类及其关系，输出正式的语言描述。 然后使用在这个正式系统中运行的符号推理器来解决问题。 Inter-GPS 和 Formal-Geo 等开创性工作models geometric element recognition as an entity segmentation task and uses a Graph Neural Network (GNN) to identify element classes and their relationships, outputting a formal language description. The problem is then solved using symbolic reasoners that operate within this formal system. Pioneering works like Inter-GPS and Formal-Geo(Zhang et al. 2024)是这种方法的典范。 虽然逻辑一致性强大，但它们对手工制定规则的依赖可能会限制其可扩展性。are exemplars of this approach. While powerful in logical consistency, their reliance on hand-crafted rules can limit their scalability.

基于神经网络的方法Neural Network-Based Methods

随着深度学习的兴起，研究人员开始探索端到端神经模型，学习直接从数据解决几何问题。 这些方法将问题编码为密集向量表示，利用神经网络根据学习到的特征来预测解决方案。 例如，NGSWith the rise of deep learning, researchers began exploring end-to-end neural models that learn to solve geometry problems directly from data. These methods encode the problem into dense vector representations, utilizing neural networks to predict solutions based on the learned features. For instance, NGS(Chen et al. 2021)使用文本编码器来学习问题的文本特征，并引入拼图游戏等辅助任务来学习几何图的稳健视觉表示。 在此基础上，PGPSNet 等模型employed a text encoder to learn textual features of the problem, and introduced auxiliary tasks like a jigsaw puzzle game to learn robust visual representations of geometric diagrams. Building on this, models like PGPSNet(Zhang et al. 2023)利用具有双向 GRU 的双流编码器来融合文本和视觉特征。 局域网utilized dual-stream encoders with bidirectional GRUs to fuse textual and visual features. LANS(Li et al. 2023)通过引入布局感知预训练模块并采用对比学习来增强视觉点和文本标记之间的对齐，进一步完善了该架构，从而在将融合表示馈送到序列到序列解码器之前实现更精确的多模态理解。further refined this architecture by introducing a layout-aware pre-training module and employing contrastive learning to enhance the alignment between visual points and textual tokens, leading to more precise multimodal understanding before feeding the fused representation to a sequence-to-sequence decoder.

混合方法Hybrid Methods

为了将符号系统的逻辑严谨性与神经网络的快速决策优势结合起来，大量的工作集中在混合或神经符号方法上。 该领域的主导范式涉及使用神经网络作为定理预测模块来加速推理，解决传统符号方法通常依赖于时间和计算成本高昂的搜索过程的事实。 例如，GeoDRLTo combine the logical rigor of symbolic systems with the rapid decision-making strengths of neural networks, a significant body of work has focused on hybrid, or neuro-symbolic, methods. A dominant paradigm in this area involves using neural networks as theorem prediction modules to accelerate inference—addressing the fact that traditional symbolic approaches often rely on time- and computationally expensive search processes. For example, GeoDRL(Peng et al. 2023)将问题表示为几何逻辑图，并通过强化学习训练 GNN 作为策略网络，在推理过程的每一步中选择最有希望的定理应用于形式推理系统中。represents the problem as a geometric logic graph and trains a GNN via reinforcement learning to act as a policy network, which selects the most promising theorem to apply within a formal reasoning system at each step of the reasoning process.

大型模型的最新趋势Recent Trends with Large Models

最近，LLM 已被采用为强大的后端推理机，采用 GeoX 等方法Recently, LLMs have been adopted as powerful backend reasoners, with methods like GeoX(Xia et al. 2024), DFE-GPS (Zhang et al. 2025)和 Pi-GPS, and Pi-GPS(Zhao et al. 2025)利用它们进行最终的解决阶段。 现在的重点已经转向使用专门的策略来增强这些大型基础模型，以增强其几何推理能力。 这些策略包括改进正式的问题表示leveraging them for the final solving stage. The focus has now shifted towards augmenting these large foundational models with specialized strategies to enhance their geometric reasoning capabilities. These strategies include refining the formal problem representation(Ping et al. 2025)，生成大规模带注释的数据集, generating large-scale annotated datasets(Wu et al. 2025)，利用先进的搜索算法改进推理时间推理, improving inference-time reasoning with advanced search algorithms(Wang et al. 2025c)，并使用强化学习来解决众所周知的困难子问题，例如辅助线建设, and using reinforcement learning to tackle notoriously difficult sub-problems like auxiliary line construction(Wang et al. 2025d).

方法Method

解决几何问题，尤其是那些严重依赖图表的几何问题，需要精确理解视觉几何关系。 与代数或纯粹基于文本的任务不同，几何问题的解决通常依赖于识别隐式约束，例如共线性、角度属性，这些约束以视觉方式编码在图表中，而不是在文本中明确说明。 然而，当前的多模态模型在精确解析和推理几何图方面表现出很大的局限性Solving geometry problems, especially those that heavily rely on diagrams, requires a precise understanding of visual geometric relationships. Unlike algebraic or purely text-based tasks, geometric problem solving often depends on recognizing implicit constraints, such as collinearity, angle properties, that are visually encoded in diagrams rather than explicitly stated in text. However, current multimodal models exhibit significant limitations in accurately parsing and reasoning about geometric diagrams(Wang et al. 2025b)，这对自动化几何问题解决提出了重大挑战。 我们提出了一种用于自动几何问题解决的混合框架，该框架集成了形式化问题解析、定理驱动推理和法学硕士推理。 与传统的符号几何求解器不同，我们的方法利用基于规则的定理应用和法学硕士的生成能力来生成人类可读的解决方案。 如图, which poses a major challenge for automated geometry problem solving. We propose a hybrid framework for automated geometry problem solving that integrates formalized problem parsing, theorem-driven reasoning, and LLM inference. Unlike traditional symbolic geometry solvers, our approach leverages both rule-based theorem application and the generative capabilities of LLMs to produce human-readable solutions. As shown in Figure1，框架由三个核心模块组成： (1) 几何问题形式化， (2) 几何定理推理，以及 (3) 基于法学硕士的答案生成。, the framework consists of three core modules: (1) Geometric Problem Formalization, (2) Geometric Theorem Reasoning, and (3) LLM-based Answer Generation.

评估指标Evaluation Metrics

对于 L1 和 L2 数据集，我们使用严格的准确性指标。 仅当生成的答案与基本事实完全匹配时，问题才会被标记为正确（得分为 1）。 任何偏差，无论多么微小，都会导致 0 分。 最终准确率计算为正确回答问题数与总问题数的比率。For the L1 and L2 datasets, we use a strict accuracy metric. A question is marked as correct (score of 1) only if the generated answer perfectly matches the ground truth. Any deviation, however minor, results in a score of 0. The final accuracy is calculated as the ratio of correctly answered questions to the total number of questions.

由于L3数据集直接来自今年举行的中考考试，因此我们采用基于中考官方评分标准的评分率指标。 问题中的每个子问题都分配有一个特定的分值。 如果模型的答案正确，则该模型将获得子问题的满分，否则获得零分。 问题的总分是其组成子问题的分数之和。 最终评分率是模型获得的总分与整个数据集的最大可能得分的比率。Since the L3 dataset is directly sourced from the Zhongkao examinations held this year, we adopt a Scoring Rate metric based on the official grading rubrics of the Zhongkao examinations. Each sub-question within a problem is assigned a specific point value. A model receives the full points for a sub-question if its answer is correct and zero points otherwise. The total score for a problem is the sum of scores from its constituent sub-questions. The final Scoring Rate is the ratio of the total score achieved by the model to the maximum possible score across the entire dataset.

For open-source models, specifically the Qwen series, since the official documentation states that in reasoning mode ”greedy decoding should not be used as it may lead to performance degradation and endless repetition,” we followed the official recommendations and used the default parameters, reporting the average performance over three runs. Additionally, since DeepSeek-R1’s reasoning mode does not support adjusting the Temperature parameter—meaning greedy decoding cannot be enabled—its single-run outputs are less stable compared to other closed-source models. 结论Conclusion

这项工作挑战了流行的范式，即越来越大的多模态模型是推进几何推理的主要解决方案。 我们已经证明，纯 LLM 在使用专门的几何视觉解析器和符号求解器进行增强时，可以达到甚至超越 Gemini 2.5 Pro 等最先进的 LMM 的性能。 我们推出的具有挑战性、无污染的ZhongkaoGeo基准为评估真实的演绎能力提供了严格的新标准。 更重要的是，通过促进类似人类的演绎推理而不是不透明的暴力方法，我们的框架促进了用于解决数学问题的透明且可解释的人工智能的发展。This work challenges the prevailing paradigm that ever-larger multimodal models are the predominant solution to advancing geometric reasoning. We have demonstrated that a pure LLM, when augmented with a specialized Geometric Vision Parser and a Symbolic Solver, can achieve and even surpass the performance of a state-of-the-art LMM like Gemini 2.5 Pro. Our introduction of the challenging, contamination-free ZhongkaoGeo benchmark provides a rigorous new standard for evaluating true deductive capabilities. More importantly, by promoting human-like deductive reasoning over opaque, brute-force methods, our framework advances the development of transparent and interpretable AI for mathematical problem-solving.
