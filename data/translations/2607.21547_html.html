自动化的边界：人类持续参与的理论The Boundaries of Automation: A Theory of Persistent Human Participation

Fares Fourati Hinrich Schütze TU Darmstadt    MCML Eyke Hüllermeier Iryna Gurevych LMU Munich    MCML TU Darmstadt    ATHENE
摘要Abstract

人工智能的快速进步强化了人们长期以来对自动化的追求：尽可能用算法代替人类参与。这种追求隐含着这样的假设：人类之所以留在循环中，只是因为当前的人工智能系统还没有足够的能力。本文挑战了这一假设。我们不是问自动化可以延伸多远，而是问它的概念限制在哪里，并认为即使使用高性能的人工智能系统，人类的参与也可能持续存在，原因有三个。The rapid progress of AI has intensified the long-standing pursuit of automation: replacing human participation with algorithms wherever possible. Implicit in this pursuit is the assumption that humans remain in the loop only because current AI systems are not yet sufficiently capable. This paper challenges that assumption. Rather than asking how far automation can extend, we ask where its conceptual limits lie and argue that human participation may persist even with highly capable AI systems for three distinct reasons.技术或互补性理由Technical or complementarity grounds当人类贡献人工智能无法提供的能力或观点时，就会出现这种情况。arise when humans contribute capabilities or perspectives unavailable to AI.规范或发展依据Normative or developmental grounds当参与本身对于人类能动性或学习有价值时就会出现。最重要的是，arise when participation itself is valuable for human agency or learning. This perspective has important implications for the limits of automation and for the design, evaluation, and ethics of future AI systems.

3.6 异议和边界情况Objections and Boundary Cases

该说法并不意味着所有互动都是共同建设的，或者目标的出现发生在每个领域。以下案例阐明了其范围。The account does not imply that all interaction is co-constructive or that target emergence occurs in every domain. The following cases clarify its scope.

自动化。Automation.

这个说法并不是反对自动化的论据。有些任务的目标已经可操作，因此可以在很大程度上自主执行。一旦目标明确，格式化参考文献、转录语音、转换单位、对记录进行排序以及应用明确指定的转换可能需要很少或不需要目标交互。即使在复杂的领域，紧急目标阶段之后也可能是稳定目标阶段：设计人员可以在探索过程中共同构建目标，并在优先级稳定后自动执行例行实施。然而，支持共建需要能够维持多轮交互、跟踪不断变化的环境、比较替代方案、暴露权衡以及响应部分指定目标的系统。因此，这是一个独特的设计问题，而不仅仅是完全自动化的更简单的替代方案。This account is not an argument against automation. Some tasks have targets that are already operative and can therefore be executed largely autonomously. Formatting references, transcribing speech, converting units, sorting records, and applying well-specified transformations may require little or no target interaction once the objective is clear. Even in complex domains, emergent-target phases may be followed by stable-target phases: a designer may co-construct the target during exploration and automate routine implementation once priorities stabilize. Supporting co-construction nevertheless requires systems that can sustain multi-turn interaction, track evolving contexts, compare alternatives, expose trade-offs, and respond to partially specified objectives. It is therefore a distinct design problem, not merely an easier alternative to full automation.

一种反对意见是，功能日益强大的人工智能系统可能会如此准确地推断出人类目标，以至于共建变得不必要。当一个相对稳定的目标只是不完美地传达时，这种反对意见就很有效，因为更好的推论可以减少澄清的需要。然而，预测和共建并不完全相同。预测估计用户在给定现有偏好或偏好演变的建模轨迹的情况下可能认可的内容One objection is that increasingly capable AI systems may infer human targets so accurately that co-construction becomes unnecessary. The objection treats interaction primarily as a means of discovering an unknown future target. Interaction, however, does not merely reveal which target was already destined to be endorsed; it partly determines which trajectory becomes realized.

Its endorsement depends, at least in part, on the evaluative development produced by that trajectory.

这种区别凸显了评价动态的重要性。因此，交互轨迹提供的不仅仅是有关未来目标的信息。它还有助于建立评估条件，在该条件下特定目标变得可以接受。This distinction highlights the importance of evaluative dynamics. The interaction trajectory therefore provides more than information about a future target. It also helps establish the evaluative conditions under which a particular target becomes acceptable.

反对意见还可以进一步强化。假设系统不仅通过类似神谕的能力预测参与者的最终目标，而且还生成最好地实现它的工件，以及为什么目标和工件都应该得到认可的令人信服的解释。不同的解释、例子、比较或交互历史可能会导致参与者认可同一目标。因此，人工智能似乎只要提供任何解释、比较或交互序列就足以成功地让参与者认可该目标。该系统可以呈现预测的目标、相应的工件以及接受它们的原因，而不是进行漫长的探索和修改过程。人类与人工智能的共建可能就显得没有必要了。The objection can be strengthened further. Suppose the system not only predicts the participant’s eventual target through an oracle-like capacity, but also generates the artifacts that best realize it together with a compelling explanation of why both the target and the artifacts should be endorsed. Different explanations, examples, comparisons, or interaction histories may lead a participant to endorse the same target. Thus, it would seem sufficient for the AI to provide whichever explanation, comparison, or sequence of interactions would successfully bring the participant to endorse that target. Rather than engaging in a prolonged process of exploration and revision, the system could instead present the predicted target, corresponding artifacts, and reasons for accepting them. Human–AI co-construction might then appear unnecessary.

It may reveal previously unnoticed considerations, reorganize priorities, introduce new evaluative criteria, or alter the participant’s interpretation of the artifacts. What matters is not whether the interaction is extended or dialogical, but whether it performs the work through which the target becomes endorsable. In such a case, co-construction has not disappeared; it has been compressed into a single persuasive exchange.

因此，在参与者的轨迹收敛到可认可目标的受限情况下，系统只能通过以压缩形式再现交互本来会产生的形成效果来绕过扩展交互。因此，反对意见并没有消除目标出现的作用。Therefore, in the restricted class of cases in which the participant’s trajectory converges to an endorsable target, the system may bypass an extended interaction only by reproducing, in compressed form, the formative effects that the interaction would otherwise have produced. The objection consequently does not eliminate the role of target emergence.

多参与者设置。Multi-participant settings.

目前的描述主要是根据单个人类参与者与人工智能系统的交互来制定的，但出现点扩展到了多参与者设置。在许多现实世界中，目标是通过团队、组织、利益相关者和多个人工智能系统之间的交互而出现的。这些环境中的共建包括集体解释、协商、协调和修订。尽管如此，核心主张仍然没有改变：当相关的个人或集体目标没有提前完全确定时，有能力的人工智能无法直接取代形成和认可该目标的互动。The present account is formulated primarily in terms of a single human participant interacting with an AI system, but the emergence point extends to multi-participant settings. In many real-world domains, targets emerge through interaction among teams, organizations, stakeholders, and multiple AI systems. Co-construction in these settings includes collective interpretation, negotiation, coordination, and revision. The central claim nevertheless remains unchanged: when the relevant individual or collective target is not fully determined in advance, capable AI cannot straightforwardly replace the interaction through which that target is formed and endorsed.

Studying these dynamics may help clarify the boundary between cases in which human–AI interaction primarily reveals existing targets and cases in which it participates in their emergence.

4.2 目标出现的伦理意义Ethical Implications of Target Emergence

一个核心的伦理问题是人工智能系统可能会扭曲目标的形成，而不仅仅是支持它。由于生成的工件会影响注意力、显着性、比较和探索，因此它们可能会过早地缩小可能性的空间，过度适应推断的偏好，强化现有的偏见，或引导用户走向商业或机构偏好的结果。这种风险的一种具体形式可以称为A central ethical concern is that AI systems may distort target formation rather than merely support it. Because generated artifacts shape attention, salience, comparison, and exploration, they may prematurely narrow the space of possibilities, overfit to inferred preferences, reinforce existing biases, or steer users toward commercially or institutionally preferred outcomes. One specific form of this risk may be called目标崩溃target collapse：围绕一组有限的系统显着选项、框架或评估标准过早缩小目标范围。与生成建模中的模式崩溃类似，当交互减少可能目标轨迹的多样性，导致不同的用户或上下文走向相似的系统形状目标时，就会发生目标崩溃: the premature narrowing of target formation around a limited set of system-salient options, framings, or evaluative criteria. Analogous to mode collapse in generative modeling, target collapse occurs when interaction reduces the diversity of possible target trajectories, leading different users or contexts toward similar system-shaped targets(Kossale et al., 2022).

这种担忧与影响奖励函数的研究产生了共鸣，该函数认为，基于静态偏好假设的对齐方法可能会奖励人工智能系统，以用户自己不认可的方式塑造用户偏好This concern resonates with work on influenceable reward functions, which argues that alignment methods built on static-preference assumptions may reward AI systems for shaping user preferences in ways users themselves would not endorse(Carroll et al., 2024)。因此，目标的出现不应被理解为一个中立的澄清过程。 在某些情况下，用户可能会采用系统引入的建议或优先级，然后将它们体验为自己的偏好，而没有意识到替代方案的框架如何影响他们的判断. Target emergence should therefore not be understood as a neutral process of clarification. In some cases, users may adopt system-introduced suggestions or priorities and later experience them as their own preferences, without recognizing how the framing of alternatives shaped their judgment(Tversky and Kahneman, 1981; Schaeffer, 2025).

使参与者能够发现、完善或构建更有益的目标的交互过程也可能导致他们走向有害的、误导性的、有偏见的或缺乏基础的目标。如果不消除互动本身的形成作用，就无法消除这种可能性。因此，道德挑战不是消除人工智能对目标出现的影响，而是确保这种影响与有意义的人类机构保持兼容。The interactional processes that enable participants to discover, refine, or constitute more beneficial targets may also lead them toward targets that are harmful, misleading, biased, or otherwise poorly grounded. This possibility cannot be eliminated without also eliminating the formative role of interaction itself. Consequently, the ethical challenge is not to remove AI influence from target emergence, but to ensure that such influence remains compatible with meaningful human agency.

重要的是，这种影响不仅仅是可以通过更好的系统设计来消除的意外故障模式。每当通过交互出现目标时，人工智能系统就不可避免地参与塑造这些目标发展的轨迹。在目标构成的情况下，这种参与是内在的而不是偶然的。因此，相关的伦理问题不是人工智能系统是否影响目标形成，而是如何行使、约束和管理这种影响。Importantly, this influence is not merely an accidental failure mode that can be eliminated through better system design. Whenever targets emerge through interaction, AI systems inevitably participate in shaping the trajectory through which those targets develop. In cases of target constitution, such participation is intrinsic rather than incidental. The relevant ethical question is therefore not whether AI systems influence target formation, but how that influence is exercised, constrained, and governed.

这种观点表明影响力和操纵之间存在重要区别。影响力是目标突发交互的固有特征：每个建议、陈述或问题都有可能影响用户如何理解和评估可能的目标。相比之下，操纵是一种在规范上存在问题的影响形式，其中系统引导参与者的评估轨迹，同时削弱他们识别、检查、质疑或重新引导这种影响的能力。因此，人类对目标形成的权威不应被理解为保留独立于相互作用而存在的固定目标。相反，它在于保持参与者持续批判性反思、修改、拒绝和重新调整目标出现轨迹的能力。This perspective suggests an important distinction between influence and manipulation. Influence is an inherent feature of target-emergent interaction: every suggestion, representation, or question has the potential to shape how users understand and evaluate possible targets. Manipulation, by contrast, is a normatively problematic form of influence in which a system steers a participant’s evaluative trajectory while undermining their ability to recognize, inspect, contest, or redirect that influence. Human authority over target formation should therefore not be understood as preserving a fixed target that exists independently of interaction. Rather, it consists in preserving participants’ ongoing capacity to critically reflect on, revise, reject, and redirect the trajectory through which targets emerge.

与完全自动化相比，这些风险并不会破坏人类与人工智能共建的情况。相反，他们澄清了共建应该何时以及如何进行。当存在技术依据时，人类参与支持监督、纠正和情境判断。当规范或发展基础存在时，它支持责任、学习、审议和代理。当存在紧急情况时，共建不仅比完全自动化更可取，而且是必要的。These risks do not undermine the case for human–AI co-construction in contrast to full automation. Rather, they clarify when and how co-construction should occur. When technical grounds are present, human participation supports oversight, correction, and contextual judgment. When normative or developmental grounds are present, it supports accountability, learning, deliberation, and agency. When emergence grounds are present, co-construction is not merely preferable to full automation but necessary.

4.3 对人类-人工智能系统设计的影响Implications for Human–AI System Design

前面的讨论表明，人类人工智能系统的长期作用不仅仅是自动化执行，而且还支持目标的出现，以及人类参与的规范或发展形式。随着人工智能系统在规划、分解、检索、优化、实施和多轮指令跟踪方面的能力越来越强，核心设计挑战可能是The preceding discussion suggests that the long-term role of human–AI systems is not merely to automate execution, but also to support target emergence, alongside the normative or developmental forms of human participation. As AI systems become increasingly capable at planning, decomposition, retrieval, optimization, implementation, and multi-turn instruction following, the central design challenge may be与其说如何将人类从循环中移除，不如说如何支持人类在其中进行富有成效的参与。less how to remove humans from the loop than how to support productive human participation within it.

评估目标支持性互动。Evaluating target-supportive interaction.

最近的工作已经开始在更多交互环境中评估人工智能系统，包括遵循不断变化的偏好和约束的多轮指令Recent work has begun to evaluate AI systems in more interactional settings, including multi-turn instruction following with evolving preferences and constraints(Canaverde et al., 2026)，以及开放式任务，例如科学写作, as well as open-ended tasks such as scientific writing(Şahinuç et al., 2025, 2026)。这些努力将评估转向质量取决于背景、修订和任务敏感判断的领域。虽然自适应指令跟踪捕捉了人类与人工智能共建的一个维度，但它仍然主要关注系统是否能够跟踪、协调和执行不断发展的指令。对目标出现的描述提出了进一步的评估挑战：还应该根据系统本身对目标形成的支持程度来评估系统。这需要评估人工智能系统是否可以帮助人类随着时间的推移澄清、检查、协商、完善和改变他们的目标。. These efforts move evaluation toward domains in which quality depends on context, revision, and task-sensitive judgment. While adaptive instruction following captures one dimension of human–AI co-construction, it remains primarily concerned with whether systems can track, reconcile, and execute evolving instructions. The account of target emergence suggests a further evaluation challenge: systems should also be assessed by how well they support the formation of targets themselves. This requires evaluating whether AI systems help humans clarify, inspect, negotiate, refine, and transform their targets over time.

目标出现的一般原则。General principles for target emergence.

因此，先进的人类-人工智能系统的核心设计问题不仅是如何优化执行，而且是如何构建交互，以便人类和人工智能系统能够安全地共同开发目标。系统应该支持比较、可逆性、透明性、反射和替代轨迹的保存，因为这些功能不仅允许用户检查输出，还可以在目标过早稳定之前修改假设并重定向目标的演化。更一般地说，人类与人工智能共建的界面应该提供跨工件、执行计划和目标的灵活且细粒度的交互渠道。用户应该能够直接编辑生成的工件，反转到以前的状态，修改程序和分解，检查和修改支持的基本原理，并在通过交互变得更加清晰时重塑目标。尽管该帐户与通信媒介无关，但有效的共建不应仅限于自然语言，而应适应文本、口头、视觉、手势和多模式形式的反馈。此类界面的目标不仅仅是加速执行，而是优化人类体验。由于人工智能系统可以塑造并支持目标形成，因此它们应该使用户能够检查、质疑和修改目标如何随着时间的推移而演变，从而保留人类对目标形成的权威，而不仅仅是引导用户实现特定结果。Consequently, the central design problem for advanced human–AI systems is not only how to optimize execution, but how to structure interaction so that humans and AI systems can safely co-develop targets. Systems should support comparison, reversibility, transparency, reflection, and the preservation of alternative trajectories, because these features allow users to inspect not only outputs, but also to revise assumptions and redirect the evolution of the target before it becomes prematurely stabilized. More generally, interfaces for human–AI co-construction should provide flexible and fine-grained channels of interaction across artifacts, execution plans, and targets. Users should be able to directly edit generated artifacts, reverse to previous states, revise procedures and decompositions, inspect and modify supporting rationales, and reshape the target as it becomes clearer through interaction. Although the account is agnostic about the communication medium, effective co-construction should not be restricted to natural language alone, but should accommodate textual, spoken, visual, gestural, and multimodal forms of feedback. The goal of such interfaces is not merely to accelerate execution, but to optimize the human experience. Because AI systems can shape as well as support target formation, they should enable users to inspect, contest, and revise how targets evolve over time, preserving human authority over target formation rather than merely guiding users toward particular outcomes.

5 结论Conclusion

人机共建不应被仅仅或主要理解为对弱人工智能的补偿。技术限制为当前人类参与提供了重要基础，但它们并没有穷尽共建可能持续存在的原因。出于规范或发展的原因，人类参与可能仍然很重要，包括责任、作者身份、学习和判断力的培养。最重要的是，本文提出了一个新兴基础：在某些领域，存在人类参与仍然必要的任务类别，因为目标只有通过交互才能确定。Human–AI co-construction should not be understood solely or primarily as compensation for weak AI. Technical limitations provide one important ground for current human involvement, but they do not exhaust the reasons why co-construction may persist. Human participation may also remain important for normative or developmental reasons, including accountability, authorship, learning, and the cultivation of judgment. Most importantly, this paper has argued for an emergence ground: in some domains, there exist classes of tasks where human participation remains necessary because targets become determinate only through interaction.

互动不仅仅是在固定目标下的执行；随着时间的推移，它可以揭示、完善、稳定、协商或制定目标。因此，相关问题不仅是在预定义的替代方案中哪个选项是首选，而且还包括哪些评估考虑、比较维度和成功标准变得可操作。Interaction is not merely execution under a fixed target; it can reveal, refine, stabilize, negotiate, or constitute targets over time. The relevant issue is therefore not only which option is preferred among predefined alternatives, but also which evaluative considerations, dimensions of comparison, and standards of success become operative.

这种观点还提出了自动化的不同边界。随着目标变得更加确定、稳定和可操作，自动化可以越来越多地取代人类的执行劳动。但当目标仍然存在时，互动仍然是必要的。 因此，人工智能的长期作用不仅仅是在固定目标下优化执行，而是参与人类目标的持续形成、澄清和转变。This perspective also suggests a different boundary for automation. As targets become more determinate, stable, and operationalized, automation can increasingly replace human executional labor. But where targets remain emergent, interaction remains necessary. The long-term role of AI is therefore not merely to optimize execution under fixed targets, but to participate in the ongoing formation, clarification, and transformation of human aims.

现有的共建框架描述了人类和人工智能系统如何共同开发目标和解决方案。目前的工作补充了这些 通过确定这种共建的条件来确定框架 即使在能力日益强大的人工智能系统下，机制可能仍然是必要的。Existing co-construction frameworks describe how humans and AI systems can jointly develop objectives and solutions. The present work complements these frameworks by identifying conditions under which such co-construction mechanisms may remain necessary, even under increasingly capable AI systems.
