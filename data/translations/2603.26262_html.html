摘要Abstract

图像到点云配准方法通常遵循从粗到细的流程，提取块级对应关系并将其细化为密集的像素到点匹配。然而，在具有重复图案的场景中，图像通常缺乏足够的 3D 结构线索以及与点云的对齐，从而导致不正确的匹配。此外，现有方法通常忽视结构一致性，限制了对应关系的充分利用。为了解决这些问题，我们提出了两个新颖的模块：局部几何增强（LGE）模块和图分布一致性（GDC）模块。 LGE 使用法向量增强图像和点云特征，将几何结构注入图像特征以减少不匹配。 GDC 从匹配点构建一个图来更新特征并显式约束相似性分布。对 RGB-D Scenes v2 和 7-Scenes 这两个基准进行的大量实验和消融表明，我们的方法在图像到点云配准方面实现了最先进的性能。Image-to-point cloud registration methods typically follow a coarse-to-fine pipeline, extracting patch-level correspondences and refining them into dense pixel-to-point matches. However, in scenes with repetitive patterns, images often lack sufficient 3D structural cues and alignment with point clouds, leading to incorrect matches. Moreover, prior methods usually overlook structural consistency, limiting the full exploitation of correspondences. To address these issues, we propose two novel modules: the Local Geometry Enhancement (LGE) module and the Graph Distribution Consistency (GDC) module. LGE enhances both image and point cloud features with normal vectors, injecting geometric structure into image features to reduce mismatches. GDC constructs a graph from matched points to update features and explicitly constrain similarity distributions. Extensive experiments and ablations on two benchmarks, RGB-D Scenes v2 and 7-Scenes, demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in image-to-point cloud registration.

I 简介Introduction

图像到点云配准旨在估计将 3D 点云与从同一场景捕获的 2D 图像对齐的刚性变换。这涉及建立像素和 3D 点之间的跨模式对应关系，然后进行姿势估计以恢复旋转和平移。该任务在 3D 重建等各种应用中发挥着关键作用Image-to-point cloud registration aims to estimate a rigid transformation that aligns a 3D point cloud with a 2D image captured from the same scene. This involves establishing cross-modal correspondences between pixels and 3D points, followed by pose estimation to recover rotation and translation. The task plays a critical role in various applications such as 3D reconstruction[35, 53, 55, 66], SLAM [32, 56, 57, 65]和视觉定位, and visual localization[33, 50, 67]。然而，图像和点云本质上不同的结构构成了重大挑战：图像是密集的且具有 2D 网格结构，而点云在 3D 空间中是稀疏、无序且不规则的。这种巨大的模态差距使有意义的跨模态对应的提取变得复杂，阻碍了两种数据类型之间的有效对齐。. However, the inherently different structures of images and point clouds pose a major challenge: images are dense and structured as 2D grids, while point clouds are sparse, unordered, and irregular in 3D space. This large modality gap complicates the extraction of meaningful cross-modal correspondences, hindering effective alignment between the two data types.

为了解决图像到点云的配准问题，现有的方法通常分为两类。第一种是检测然后匹配方法To tackle the image-to-point cloud registration problem, existing approaches generally fall into two categories. The first is the detect-then-match approach[23, 3, 38, 52, 54]，分别检测图像和点云中的2D和3D关键点，然后根据语义特征进行匹配。由于模态差距，这种方法经常遇到困难：图像关键点依赖于纹理，而点云关键点依赖于几何形状，使得可重复检测变得困难。此外，2D 和 3D 特征表示之间的巨大差异使得学习一致的描述符变得困难。为了缓解这些问题，最近的工作探索了免检测管道, which detects 2D and 3D key points in the image and point cloud separately and then matches them based on semantic features. This approach often struggles due to the modality gap: image keypoints depend on texture, while point cloud keypoints rely on geometry, making repeatable detection difficult. Moreover, the large discrepancy between 2D and 3D feature representations makes it hard to learn consistent descriptors. To mitigate these issues, recent works explore detection-free pipelines[40, 51, 48, 49]。代表性方法，2D3D-MATR. A representative method, 2D3D-MATR[2]，引入了一个从粗到细的框架，首先执行块级匹配，然后将对应细化到点级，然后通过 PnP-RANSAC 进行刚性变换估计, introduces a coarse-to-fine framework that first performs patch-level matching and then refines correspondences to the point level, followed by rigid transformation estimation via PnP-RANSAC[4, 5]。然而，在光照变化、低纹理区域或重复图案等具有挑战性的条件下，缺乏结构指导仍然可能导致跨模式匹配不准确。. However, under challenging conditions such as illumination changes, low-texture regions, or repetitive patterns, the lack of structural guidance can still lead to inaccurate cross-modal matching.

讨论LGE 和GDC 之间的相互作用。Discussion of the interaction between LGE and GDC.LGE和GDC分属不同层面，发挥着互补作用。 LGE模块专注于增强局部几何编码并提高表示级别的特征质量。通过结合局部几何背景，LGE 增强了对应关系的可辨别性，并减少了具有挑战性的区域中的模糊性（例如，弱纹理或部分结构相似性）。换句话说，LGE主要改进了执行匹配的特征基础。LGE and GDC operate at different levels and play complementary roles. The LGE module focuses on enhancing local geometric encoding and improving feature quality at the representation level. By incorporating local geometric context, LGE strengthens the discriminability of correspondences and reduces ambiguity in challenging regions (e.g., weak texture or partial structural similarity). In other words, LGE primarily improves the feature foundation on which matching is performed.

相比之下，GDC 在特征提取后在相似性分布级别上运行。它不是直接修改原始特征，而是规范全局相似性分布，以减轻跨模式偏差并重新校准对应置信度。因此，GDC主要细化候选匹配之间的置信度排名。当整体相似性结构相当可靠时，它对于加深真实对应和硬负例之间的分离特别有效。 关于系统失配，GDC 并非旨在完全纠正由严重损坏的特征或严重的几何失准引起的大规模结构错误。如果底层特征表示根本上不正确（例如，由于严重的深度伪影或极度稀疏），则仅靠分布对齐无法恢复正确的几何形状。然而，当系统不匹配源于模态引起的偏差或置信度错误校准（而不是不正确的几何编码）时，GDC 可以通过重塑相似性景观并抑制全局偏差的相似性模式来部分纠正它们。In contrast, GDC operates at the similarity-distribution level after feature extraction. Rather than modifying raw features directly, it regularizes the global similarity distribution to mitigate cross-modal bias and recalibrate correspondence confidence. As a result, GDC mainly refines the confidence ranking among candidate matches. It is particularly effective in sharpening the separation between true correspondences and hard negatives when the overall similarity structure is reasonably reliable. Regarding systematic mismatches, GDC is not designed to fully correct large-scale structural errors caused by severely corrupted features or gross geometric misalignment. If the underlying feature representation is fundamentally incorrect (e.g., due to severe depth artifacts or extreme sparsity), distribution alignment alone cannot recover the correct geometry. However, when systematic mismatches stem from modality-induced bias or confidence miscalibration (rather than from incorrect geometric encoding), GDC can partially correct them by reshaping the similarity landscape and suppressing globally biased similarity patterns.

因此，LGE主要改进特征判别性和几何编码，而GDC主要细化和校准相似性分布。它们的交互是互补的：LGE 提供更可靠的关系结构，GDC 利用这些结构来提高对应选择的稳健性。Therefore, LGE primarily improves feature discriminability and geometric encoding, while GDC mainly refines and calibrates similarity distributions. Their interaction is complementary: LGE provides more reliable relational structures, and GDC leverages these structures to improve correspondence selection robustness.

V 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了一种用于 2D-3D 配准的创新几何感知局部对齐和结构同步网络。 我们的方法有效地从二维图像中提取表面法线信息以捕获丰富的结构线索，从而增强了与点云特征的相关性并有效减少了跨模态失配。此外，使用基于图的特征传播和结构相似性约束可以提高匹配对应关系的一致性，从而显着提高配准准确性和鲁棒性。对 RGB-D Scenes v2 和 7-Scenes 数据集的大量实验表明，我们的 GLASS 方法在图像到点云配准方面实现了最先进的性能。In this paper, we introduce an innovative geometry-aware local alignment and structure synchronization network for 2D-3D registration. Our method efficiently extracts surface normal information from 2D images to capture rich structural cues, which enhances the correlation with point cloud features and effectively reduces cross-modal mismatches. Additionally, the use of graph-based feature propagation and structural similarity constraints improves the consistency of matched correspondences, significantly boosting registration accuracy and robustness. Extensive experiments on the RGB-D Scenes v2 and 7-Scenes datasets demonstrate that our GLASS approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in image-to-point cloud registration.
