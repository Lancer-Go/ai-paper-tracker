许多雇主使用少数算法供应商构建的算法来筛选求职者。我们假设算法单一文化会导致同一个人和同一种族群体的成员面临拒绝。我们获取并分析了一个新颖的数据集，其中包含 300 万申请人提交的 400 万份申请，其中所有申请均由同一供应商构建的算法进行筛选。我们发现申请人结果存在明显的种族差异。根据美国就业歧视标准，在亚裔和黑人申请人提交的所有申请中，分别有14.74%和25.87%提交给了对亚裔和黑人申请人产生不利影响的职位。个人也会得到相同的结果：在申请 10 个职位的所有申请人中，有 4% 的人被建议拒绝所有职位，这一比例高于偶然的预期。为了更好地理解这种同质性，我们利用招聘算法的确定性可复制性来生成申请人申请所有职位时将会收到的结果。我们表明，申请人需要广泛申请，以确保他们的申请得到人工考虑。Many employers screen job applicants with algorithms built by the same few algorithm vendors. We hypothesize that algorithmic monoculture leads to the same individuals and members of the same racial groups facing rejection. We acquire and analyze a novel dataset of 3 million applicants submitting 4 million applications where all the applications are screened by algorithms built by the same vendor. We find clear racial disparities in applicant outcomes. Of all applications submitted by Asian and Black applicants, 14.74% and 25.87% are submitted to positions that adversely impact Asian and Black applicants, respectively, according to U.S. employment discrimination standards. Individuals also receive homogeneous outcomes: 4% of all applicants who apply to 10 positions are recommended for rejection from all positions, a rate higher than expected by chance. To better understand this homogeneity, we leverage the deterministic replicability of hiring algorithms to generate the outcomes applicants would have received if they applied to all positions. We show that applicants would need to apply widely in order to ensure their applications are considered by a human.

数据访问限制了算法招聘的实证研究。单一雇主研究 [14, 58] 无法观察 A 公司的拒绝是否预示着 B 公司的拒绝，因为它们缺乏跨雇主申请人跟踪，函授研究 [7, 38] 无法观察潜在的决策过程来研究算法的作用，而招聘算法的静态审计 [60] 无法观察部署时间结果。我们的数据集是第一个观察多个雇主的同一申请人的真实算法结果的数据集。算法设置还允许我们研究新的研究问题：我们引入新方法，利用算法建议的确定性可复制性和算法决策的效率来回答传统社会科学方法难以解决的研究问题。如果申请人申请每个职位，我们会生成反事实结果，并因此接受每个 Pymetrics 模型的评估。这种模拟使我们能够确定候选人是否被系统性地拒绝，只是因为他们申请了自己不适合的工作，这样，如果他们申请了更多或不同的工作，他们就会被接受。我们的模拟显示，每个申请人都会被至少一个 Pymetrics 模型推荐，尽管实际上许多申请人在他们申请的任何职位上都没有被推荐。在更现实的申请人行为下，申请人的申请范围更广，但并非无处不在，我们发现一些申请人仍然被系统性地拒绝。为了保证系统拒绝率低于 0.1%，申请人需要提交 25 份申请，而独立基线下则需要提交 10 份申请。Data access limits empirical research on algorithmic hiring. Single-employer studies [14, 58] cannot observe whether rejection at Firm A predicts rejection at Firm B because they lack cross-employer applicant tracking, correspondence studies [7, 38] cannot observe the underlying decisionmaking process to study the role of algorithms, and static audits of hiring algorithms [60] cannot observe deployment-time outcomes. Our dataset is the first to observe real algorithmic outcomes for the same applicant across multiple employers. The algorithmic setting also allows us to study new research questions: we introduce new methods that capitalize on the deterministic replicability of algorithmic recommendations and the efficiency of algorithmic decision-making to answer a research question that would be intractable for traditional social science methods. We generate the counterfactual outcomes applicants would receive if they applied to every position and were, thereby, assessed by every pymetrics model. This simulation allows us to determine whether candidates are systemically rejected only because they apply to jobs for which they would be a poor fit, such that if they applied to more or different jobs they would be accepted. Our simulation shows that every applicant would be recommended by at least one pymetrics model, even though in reality many applicants were not recommended for any position to which they applied. Under more realistic applicant behavior, where applicants apply more broadly but not everywhere, we find that some applicants are still systemically rejected. To guarantee a systemic rejection rate below 0.1%, applicants would need to submit 25 applications compared to 10 under the baseline of independence.

有必要进行独立研究来阐明不透明的招聘算法。通过整合不同雇主之间的部分招聘决策流程，招聘算法会影响集体不利影响率和系统性拒绝的模式，考虑到歧视和长期失业的既定危害，这一点尤其突出[28,52,55]。随着算法招聘政策的进步（例如 2021 年纽约市地方法 144、2024 年欧盟人工智能法案），我们建议政策制定者努力提高算法招聘的透明度，并为独立研究创造新途径。Independent research is necessary to illuminate otherwise-opaque hiring algorithms. By consolidating part of hiring decision process across distinct employers, hiring algorithms impact collective adverse impact rates and patterns of systemic rejection, which are particularly salient given the established harms of discrimination and extended unemployment [28, 52, 55]. As algorithmic hiring policy advances (e.g. New York City Local Law 144 of 2021, the EU AI Act of 2024), we recommend that policymakers work to increase transparency into algorithmic hiring and create new pathways for independent research.

4.2 限制4.2 Limitations

我们确定的核心局限性涉及（i）整个算法招聘的普遍性，（ii）与申请人质量和模型有效性概念的关系，（iii）与美国就业法执行的相关性，以及（iv）下游招聘结果的确定性。此外，我们承认我们的工作可能会抑制未来对算法招聘的实证研究，因此可能会促使采取更强有力的政策反应形式，以确保研究人员获得数据。The core limitations we identify pertain to (i) generalizability to algorithmic hiring as a whole, (ii) relationships with notions of applicant quality and model validity, (iii) relevance to the enforcement of US employment law, and (iv) certainty about downstream hiring outcomes. In addition, we acknowledge that our work may suppress future empirical research into algorithmic hiring and may therefore prompt stronger forms of policy response to ensure researcher access to data.

我们的研究结果可能无法推广到所有算法筛选。我们没有来自其他供应商的数据，也不知道获取此类数据的可行机制。特别是，基于游戏的 Pymetrics 方法可能在质量上不同于简历筛选等替代方法。Our findings may not generalize to all algorithmic screening. We do not have data from other vendors nor are aware of viable mechanisms to acquire such data. In particular, the game-based approach of pymetrics may qualitatively differ from alternative approaches such as resume screening.

我们缺乏对申请人质量和模型有效性的外部衡量。由于我们无法衡量申请人的质量，因此我们无法预测被系统拒绝的申请人是否能够在他们所申请的职位上发挥作用。缺乏有关申请人质量的独立信息会影响我们对同质化的发现，但不会影响我们对不利影响的发现。开始就业歧视调查无需证明申请人的素质或适合工作；确定不利影响就足够了。我们还缺乏模型有效性的外部衡量标准。因此，我们不确定 Pymetrics 模型如何准确地反映相关雇主的偏好，或者模型在选择适合该职位的候选人方面是否成功。We lack external measures of both applicant quality and model validity. Because we cannot measure applicant quality, we cannot predict if applicants that were systemically rejected would have been effective at the positions to which they applied. Lack of independent information about applicant quality affects our finding of homogenization but not our finding of adverse impact. No demonstration of applicant quality or fit to job is necessary to begin an investigation of employment discrimination; establishing adverse impact can be sufficient. We also lack external measures of model validity. We are therefore uncertain of how accurately pymetrics models reflect the preferences of the associated employers or how successful the models are at selecting candidates who are good fits for the positions.

我们无法确定我们提供的不利影响的证据是否意味着存在满足相同业务需求的“歧视性较小”的算法或程序 [8, 78-9]。根据美国第七章的相关法律，虽然不利影响的证据可能足以启动调查并在诉讼中提供证明价值，但这并不一定意味着雇主的行为是非法的。特别是，我们无法了解 pymetrics 模型的开发过程，无法了解是否可以选择或以类似的成本生成替代模型。我们所做的最接近的比较是大规模对应研究数据[38]，它并不代表单一的替代筛选程序，因为接受和拒绝是由许多不同公司的人类和算法决策混合执行的。We cannot determine whether the evidence of adverse impact we present implies the existence of a “less discriminatory” algorithm or procedure that serves the same business need [8, 78-9]. Under the relevant US law for Title VII, while evidence of adverse impact may suffice to initiate an investigation and provide probative value in litigation, it does not necessarily imply an employer’s conduct is illegal. In particular, we do not have visibility into the development process for pymetrics models to understand whether alternative models could have been selected or generated at similar cost. The closest comparison we do make is to a large-scale correspondence study data [38], which does not represent a single alternative screening procedure, as the acceptances and rejections were performed by a mix of human and algorithmic decision-making at many different firms.

我们不知道雇主的招聘经理如何使用 Pymetrics 建议来做出最终的招聘决定。一些先前的工作表明，遵循招聘算法的建议可以识别非传统申请人和/或更好的申请人 [14, 23]；其他人发现，由算法建议介导的人类判断比单独的人类判断更具歧视性[11]。最终的人类决策可能会受到一套算法建议的影响，并且不同的公司可能在其筛选过程中使用不同的附加算法工具[53]。然而，我们认为，不被pymetrics模型推荐意味着下游雇主很可能拒绝申请人，这部分减轻了（系统性）拒绝背景下的这种不确定性。We do not know how the pymetrics recommendations are used by hiring managers at employers to make final hiring decisions. Some prior work suggests that following the recommendations of hiring algorithms leads to identifying non-traditional applicants and/or better applicants [14, 23]; others finds that human judgments mediated by algorithmic recommendations are more discriminatory than human judgments alone [11]. Final human decisions may be influenced by a suite of algorithmic recommendations, and different companies likely use different additional algorithmic tools in their screening processes [53]. However, we believe that not being recommended by a pymetrics model means the downstream employer is very likely to reject the applicant, which partially abates this uncertainty in the context of (systemic) rejections.

最后，我们希望这项工作能够鼓励对算法招聘的进一步独立研究，包括算法招聘供应商的透明度和审查。然而，我们的工作本身可能会阻碍自愿数据共享和人口统计数据收集，而正是这些数据使这些发现成为可能。因此，我们的工作可能需要作为政策的基础，以便能够对主要招聘算法供应商进行更深入的调查。这与社交媒体和在线平台研究的背景非常相似，后者促使欧盟数字服务法案强制研究人员访问数据[45]。Finally, we hope that this work encourages further independent research into algorithmic hiring, including transparency into and scrutiny of algorithmic hiring vendors. However, our work in itself may discourage the voluntary data sharing and demographic data collection that made these findings possible. Therefore, our work may need to serve as a foundation for policy that enables deeper inquiry into major hiring algorithm vendors. This bears a close resemblance to the context around social media and online platform research that prompted mandatory researcher data access under the European Union’s Digital Services Act [45].

算法招聘是算法决策的一种广泛部署的形式。为了实施算法招聘，许多公司依赖招聘算法供应商，例如 pymetrics。考虑到招聘/就业的风险以及算法决策可能存在的风险，自然存在公平和公正的问题：这些系统如何在不同的受保护类别和人口亚组中表现？Algorithmic hiring is a broadly deployed form of algorithmic decision-making. To implement algorithmic hiring, many firms rely on vendors of hiring algorithms, such as pymetrics. Given the stakes of hiring/employment, and the possible risks of algorithmic decision-making, naturally there are questions of fairness and equity: how do these systems perform across different protected categories and demographic subgroups?

在我们的研究中，我们的目标是进一步分析 pymetrics 算法的性质，以了解它们在不同部署中的表现。具体来说，我们的工作将集中在In our study, we aim to further analyze the nature of the pymetrics’ algorithms to see how they perform across different deployments. Concretely, our work will center on结果同质化outcome homogenization，一种系统性损害。也就是说，我们将分析 pymetrics 算法是否倾向于在向客户提供的所有系统中对相同的候选人给予较高的评分，而在所有系统中对相同的候选人给予较低的评分。除此之外我们还将进行各种群体层面的分析，更广泛地了解各种以个人为中心和以群体为中心的现象。, a type of systemic harm. That is, we will analyze whether the pymetrics algorithms have a tendency to rate the same candidates highly across all systems they would provide to clients and the same candidates lowly across all systems. We will also perform various group-level analyses in addition to this, and more generally understand a variety of individual-centric and group-centric phenomena.
