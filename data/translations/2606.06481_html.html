摘要Abstract

随着人工智能写作助手越来越多地融入现实世界的起草和修订工作流程，许多文档不再纯粹是人类编写或人工智能生成的，而是由渐进的人类与人工智能共同编辑的结果。然而，现有的人工智能文本检测基准主要关注最终输出，并且对人工智能作者信号在整个修订过程中如何出现、积累或消失的理解有限。我们介绍As AI writing assistants become increasingly integrated into real-world drafting and revision workflows, many documents are no longer purely human-written or AI-generated, but instead result from progressive human–AI co-editing. However, existing AI-text detection benchmarks largely focus on final outputs and provide limited understanding of how AI authorship signals emerge, accumulate, or disappear throughout the revision process. We introduceOpAI-Bench，一个操作引导的基准，用于研究跨文档、句子、标记和跨度粒度的渐进式人类到人工智能文本转换。从人类编写的文档开始，OpAI-Bench 在预定义的 AI 覆盖级别和五种代表性的 AI 编辑操作下为每个样本构建了九个连续修订的版本，涵盖四个领域，同时在多个粒度上保留完整的作者来源。该基准测试支持使用 8 个文档级检测器、7 个句子级检测器和 2 个细粒度 token/span 级检测器进行综合评估。实验表明，人工智能文本的可检测性不仅受到人工智能编辑内容的比例的影响，还受到编辑操作、领域和累积修订历史的影响。有趣的是，我们注意到混合作者的中间版本通常比完全人类和大量人工智能编辑的端点更难检测，暴露了现有基准测试遗漏的非单调检测模式。 OpAI-Bench 提供了一个用于分析的受控测试平台, an operation-guided benchmark for studying progressive human-to-AI text transformation across document, sentence, token, and span granularities. Starting from human-written documents, OpAI-Bench constructs nine sequentially revised versions for each sample under predefined AI coverage levels and five representative AI edit operations, covering four domains while preserving complete authorship provenance at multiple granularities. The benchmark supports comprehensive evaluation with 8 document-level detectors, 7 sentence-level detectors, and 2 fine-grained token/span-level detectors. Experiments reveal that AI-text detectability is governed not only by the proportion of AI-edited content, but also by edit operation, domain, and cumulative revision history. Interestingly, we notice that mixed-authorship intermediate versions are often harder to detect than both fully human and heavily AI-edited endpoints, exposing non-monotonic detection patterns missed by existing benchmarks. OpAI-Bench provides a controlled testbed for analyzing是否whether, 当when, 和, and如何how在现实的渐进式编辑场景下，人工智能辅助写作变得可检测。我们的代码和基准可在AI-assisted writing becomes detectable under realistic progressive editing scenarios. Our code and benchmark are available athttps://github.com/VILA-Lab/OpAI-Bench.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能辅助写作和编辑工作流程AI-assisted writing and editing workflows[17, 23, 25, 6, 7, 14]它们越来越多地嵌入到实际写作工作流程中，它们不仅用于生成完整的段落，还用于修改、完善、扩展、压缩和重组人工撰写的草稿。因此，许多现实世界的文档被更好地描述为渐进式人类与人工智能共同编辑的产物，而不是纯粹的人类编写或完全人工智能生成的文本。这种新兴的写作范式挑战了人工智能文本检测的传统二进制框架，其中假设文档属于两个端点类别之一。在实践中，人工智能的参与可能会出现在本地，逐渐积累，并在多轮修订中与先前的人类内容交互。are increasingly embedded in practical writing workflows, where they are used not only to generate complete passages, but also to revise, polish, expand, compress, and restructure human-written drafts. As a result, many real-world documents are better characterized as products of progressive human–AI co-editing rather than as purely human-written or fully AI-generated text. This emerging writing paradigm challenges the conventional binary framing of AI-text detection, where a document is assumed to belong to one of two endpoint categories. In practice, AI involvement may appear locally, accumulate gradually, and interact with prior human content across multiple rounds of revision.

现有的人工智能文本检测基准Existing AI-text detection benchmarks[5, 29, 28]为评估完整的文本是人类还是人工智能编写的提供了宝贵的资源，但它们为分析从人类写作到人工智能编辑文本的转变过程中可检测性如何演变提供了有限的支持。大多数基准测试都是根据静态最终输出构建的，并且不保留导致这些输出的中间修订状态。因此，他们无法回答几个重要的问题：人工智能的参与在什么阶段变得可以可靠地检测到，哪些类型的编辑引入了最强的检测信号，以及混合作者文本是否比端点案例更容易或更难检测。随着人工智能辅助写作变得更加增量、交互和操作特定，这些问题变得越来越重要。provide valuable resources for evaluating whether a completed text is human- or AI-written, but they offer limited support for analyzing how detectability evolves during the transformation from human writing to AI-edited text. Most benchmarks are constructed from static final outputs and do not preserve the intermediate revision states that lead to those outputs. Consequently, they cannot answer several important questions: at what stage does AI involvement become reliably detectable, which types of edits introduce the strongest detection signals, and whether mixed-authorship texts are easier or harder to detect than endpoint cases. These questions are increasingly important as AI-assisted writing becomes more incremental, interactive, and operation-specific.

评估不仅应该捕获人工智能编辑的文本量，还应该捕获文本的编辑方式以及编辑发生的位置。编辑操作可以留下不同的信号：润色可以保留词汇选择，同时提高流畅性，释义改变形式，同时保留含义，扩展添加解释性细节，压缩删除细节或简化结构。将这些操作折叠成一个人工智能编写的标签，掩盖了它们对检测的不同影响。同样，文档级标签支持全局筛选，但无法本地化人工智能的参与，而句子级标签可能会错过句子中混合的人类与人工智能片段。因此，需要令牌级和跨度级来源来评估细粒度本地化。Evaluation should capture not only how much text is AI-edited, but also how it is edited and where the edits occur. Edit operations can leave different signals: polishing may preserve lexical choices while improving fluency, paraphrasing changes form while retaining meaning, expansion adds explanatory detail, and compression removes details or simplifies structure. Collapsing these operations into a single AI-written label obscures their different effects on detection. Similarly, document-level labels support global screening but cannot localize AI involvement, while sentence-level labels may miss mixed human–AI fragments within a sentence. Token- and span-level provenance is therefore needed to evaluate fine-grained localization.

使用 OpAI-Bench，我们对文档级、句子级和细粒度的 AI 文本检测器进行了全面评估。我们的结果表明，人工智能文本的可检测性不仅仅取决于人工智能编辑内容的比例。相反，检测器性能在编辑操作、域和修订阶段之间存在很大差异。特别是，中间混合作者版本可能比纯人类和大量人工智能编辑的端点更具挑战性，揭示了现有静态基准忽略的非单调检测行为。这些发现表明，可靠的人工智能文本检测需要超越二进制端点分类，转向轨迹感知和操作感知评估。Using OpAI-Bench, we conduct a comprehensive evaluation of document-level, sentence-level, and fine-grained AI-text detectors. Our results show that AI-text detectability is not determined solely by the proportion of AI-edited content. Instead, detector performance varies substantially across edit operations, domains, and revision stages. In particular, intermediate mixed-authorship versions can be more challenging than both purely human and heavily AI-edited endpoints, revealing non-monotonic detection behavior that is overlooked by existing static benchmarks. These findings suggest that reliable AI-text detection requires moving beyond binary endpoint classification toward trajectory-aware and operation-aware evaluation.

3.1 概述及设计原则Overview and Design Principles

大多数先前的人工智能文本检测基准都会评估完整的文本并为其分配最终的作者标签，例如人类、人工智能生成或混合。这种表述对于静态检测很有价值，但对于研究随着通过修订引入人工智能的参与，可检测性如何演变，其信息量较少。之前的基准测试通常不会保留显式的中间版本，不会对累积的版本间编辑进行建模，并且不会在统一设置中共同控制 AI 覆盖范围、编辑类型和多粒度来源。因此，它们仅提供有限的支持来分析检测何时变得可靠、匹配的 AI 覆盖范围是否会在编辑类型之间产生不同的难度，以及检测器行为是否取决于修订历史记录以及最终编辑的文本。Most prior AI-text detection benchmarks evaluate a completed text and assign it a final authorship label, such as human, AI-generated, or mixed. This formulation has been valuable for static detection, but it is less informative for studying how detectability evolves as AI involvement is introduced through revision. Prior benchmarks generally do not preserve explicit intermediate versions, do not model cumulative version-to-version editing, and do not jointly control AI coverage, edit type, and multi-granularity provenance within a unified setup. 6 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introducedOpAI-Bench，一个操作指导的基准，用于评估渐进式人类人工智能修订下的人工智能文本检测。与基于端点的基准不同，OpAI-Bench 保留了中间修订状态和多粒度来源，使检测能够作为轨迹而不是单个二元决策进行研究。 我们的结果表明，人工智能文本的可检测性并不仅仅取决于人工智能的覆盖范围。在文档、句子、标记和跨度粒度上，检测器行为很大程度上取决于编辑操作、域、生成器和检测器系列。特别是，混合作者的中间版本可能比人工编写和大量人工智能编辑的端点更难检测，从而揭示了静态评估遗漏的非单调故障模式。受控消融进一步表明，在匹配的覆盖范围内，压缩通常比扩展更难检测。这些发现表明，可靠的人工智能文本检测需要超越人类与人工智能的端点分类，转向轨迹感知和操作感知评估。 OpAI-Bench 为此设置提供了一个受控测试平台，并强调了对能够定位和推理部分、增量和特定于操作的 AI 参与的检测器的需求。, an operation-guided benchmark for evaluating AI-text detection under progressive human–AI revision. Unlike endpoint-based benchmarks, OpAI-Bench preserves intermediate revision states and multi-granularity provenance, enabling detection to be studied as a trajectory rather than a single binary decision. Our results show that AI-text detectability is not governed by AI coverage alone. Across document, sentence, token, and span granularities, detector behavior depends strongly on edit operation, domain, generator, and detector family. In particular, mixed-authorship intermediate versions can be harder to detect than both human-written and heavily AI-edited endpoints, revealing non-monotonic failure modes that static evaluations miss. Controlled ablations further show that compression is often harder to detect than expansion at matched coverage. These findings suggest that reliable AI-text detection requires moving beyond endpoint human-versus-AI classification toward trajectory-aware and operation-aware evaluation. OpAI-Bench provides a controlled testbed for this setting and highlights the need for detectors that can localize and reason about partial, incremental, and operation-specific AI involvement.

附录AAppendix A局限性Limitations

OpAI-Bench 为研究渐进式人类到人工智能文本转换提供了一个受控基准，但它仍然存在一些局限性。首先，修订轨迹是在预定义的人工智能覆盖水平和编辑操作下构建的，这可能无法完全捕捉现实世界人类人工智能写作工作流程的多样性和不可预测性。其次，该基准测试侧重于一组固定的领域、检测器和人工智能编辑模型，因此结论可能无法直接推广到未见过的领域、新兴的写作助手或未来的检测器架构。第三，保留细粒度的作者来源需要受控的生成和对齐程序，这可能会引入与自然发生的协作写作不同的工件。OpAI-Bench provides a controlled benchmark for studying progressive human-to-AI text transformation, but it still has several limitations. First, the revision trajectories are constructed under predefined AI coverage levels and edit operations, which may not fully capture the diversity and unpredictability of real-world human–AI writing workflows. Second, the benchmark focuses on a fixed set of domains, detectors, and AI editing models, so conclusions may not directly generalize to unseen domains, emerging writing assistants, or future detector architectures. Third, preserving fine-grained authorship provenance requires controlled generation and alignment procedures, which may introduce artifacts that differ from naturally occurring collaborative writing.
