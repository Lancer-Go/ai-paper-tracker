1 简介Introduction

答案集编程（ASP） 是一种成熟的声明式逻辑编程范式Answer set programming (ASP) is a well-established declarative logic programming paradigm under the答案集/稳定模型语义answer set/stable models semantics (Gelfond and Lifschitz 1988)。 ASP 方法依赖于设计一个逻辑程序，使其答案集与目标问题的解决方案一一对应。 这种方法是完全声明性的，因为逻辑程序仅描述问题及其解决方案的条件，而不是获取它们的方法。后者被委托给称为答案集求解器的系统。 然后，软件工程师的任务是将问题的规范翻译成逻辑程序的形式语言，而不是设计算法来获得问题的解决方案。 在这种情况下，编程艺术和代码正确性论证的构建都变得更加直接. The ASP methodology relies on devising a logic program so that its answer sets are in one-to-one correspondence to the solutions of the target problem. This approach is fully declarative, since the logic program only describes a problem and conditions on its solutions, not the way to obtain them. The latter is delegated to systems called answer set solvers. A software engineer is then tasked with translating problem’s specifications into the formal language of logic programs rather than devising algorithms for obtaining solutions to a problem. Under these circumstances both the art of programming and the construction of an argument for code’s correctness become more direct(Cabalar et al. 2020; Fandinno et al. 2020; Hansen and Lierler 2025; Fandinno et al. 2025)。 这对于构建值得信赖的软件解决方案至关重要，使 ASP 成为当前编程环境中可行的角色。 然而，为了充分发挥 ASP 的潜力，必须更加关注其理论和实践方面。 在这里，我们的目标是 推进这种编程范式的“模块化”前沿的理论基础。. This is essential in building trustworthy software solutions, making ASP a viable role player in the current programming landscape. Yet, to realize the full potential of ASP more attention to its theoretical and practical aspects have to be paid. Here, we target advancing theoretical foundations of the “modularity” frontiers of this programming paradigm.

6 结论Conclusions

本文主张研究 ASP 中模块化的声明式方法的必要性。我们通过引入参数化模块化程序及其声明性语义，朝这个方向迈出了一步。在结论部分，我们通过对一个具有集体控制的示例 ASP 程序进行推理来说明这种方法的实用性，该程序以正式的方式进行集体控制，而对 ASP 系统内部工作的引用是无效的。 这是加深我们对模块化形式主义的理解并为 ASP 从业者提供正式工具来推理复杂系统设计中经常需要的模块化程序属性的重要一步。然而，我们只触及了这个重要主题的表面。未来的研究有很多方向。例如，研究如何扩展所提出的方法以捕获其他形式的控制非常重要。 在实践中，控件通常使用所谓的外部原子“按需”“停用”子程序的某些部分。我们相信我们的框架也可以扩展以捕获这种控制，并且我们计划探索这个方向。 未来工作的另一个可行方向是开发一种声明性元语言 参数模块的实例化。该语言中的规范将定义参数模块的哪些实例构成实际的预期程序。This paper champions a need for investigating a declarative approach to modularity in ASP. We take a step in this direction by introducing parametric modular programs and their declarative semantics. In the concluding section we illustrate the utility of this approach by reasoning about a sample ASP program with collective control in a formal way that is void from the reference to the inner workings of ASP systems. This is an important step towards refining our understanding about modular formalisms and providing ASP practitioners with formal tools to reason about the properties of modular programs frequently required in the design of complex systems. Yet, we only scratched the surface of this important topic. There are many directions for future research. For example, it is important to investigate how the proposed approach can be extended to capture other forms of controls. In practice, it is common that controls use so called external atoms to “deactivate” certain parts of the subprograms “on demand”. We believe that our framework can be extended to capture this kind of control as well, and we plan to explore this direction. Another viable direction for future work is a development of a declarative meta-language for the instantiation of parametric modules. The specifications in that language would define which instantiations of parametric modules form the actual intended program.
