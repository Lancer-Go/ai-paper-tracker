强化学习中的多模式奖励黑客Multimodal Reward Hacking in Reinforcement Learning

Jiayu Yao†,  Yiwei Wang‡,  Anmeng Zhang,  Zhe Sun§,  Songsong Wang, Lingrui Mei†,  Yuyao Ge†,  Shenghua Liu†,§ † Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences
‡ University of California, Merced ,  § Southeast University ,  § Corresponding Author
摘要Abstract

强化学习越来越多地用于调整多模态大语言模型（MLLM），但更高的奖励并不总是意味着更好的任务表现。这个问题在多模式环境中会被放大，因为视觉输入很难用纯文本或弱接地的奖励信号来验证，从而允许策略提高奖励分数，同时降低视觉推理。我们通过一个涵盖安全导向的 VQA、图表 VQA 和极端奖励压力测试的受控设置来研究 MLLM 强化学习中的奖励黑客行为，同时系统地改变奖励设计、数据模糊性、模型规模（2B 到 32B）和 RL 算法（GRPO、RLOO、DAPO）。为了将 RL 引起的失败与预先存在的弱点区分开来，我们引入了新奖励失败率 (NRFR)，它专门测量 RL 获得比 SFT 基线更高的代理奖励的样本中的黑客攻击率。我们的实验揭示了三个主要发现。首先，只注重结果的奖励会导致严重的黑客攻击（奖励黑客率高达 48.1%），并且 NRFR 超过 RHR 证实 RL 主动创造新的失败，而不是仅仅继承它们。其次，扩展可以减少黑客攻击，但仅靠扩展是不够的。即使是 32B 模型在仅结果奖励下也保留了 54.9% 的较差率，而答案感知奖励则在每个尺度上反转了平均预言机方向，与 32B 结合时达到了最强的差距。第三，算法的鲁棒性与规模相关，GRPO 始终最具抵抗力（RHR 48-53%），RLOO 持续脆弱（67-68%），而 DAPO 从 2B 的 67.2% 急剧提高到 8B 的 45.5%。我们进一步表明，视觉证据奖励仅在验证者可靠时才有帮助：相对于答案感知奖励，基于关键字的验证会增加黑客攻击，而 VLM 作为法官语义验证会减少黑客攻击。总体而言，多模式奖励黑客是优化不完美奖励的系统性结果。稳健的 MLLM 一致性需要在优化压力下保持可靠的奖励和验证者。Reinforcement learning is increasingly used to align multimodal large language models (MLLMs), yet higher rewards do not always mean better task performance. This problem is amplified in multimodal settings because visual input is difficult to verify with text-only or weakly grounded reward signals, allowing policies to improve reward scores while degrading visual reasoning. We study reward hacking in MLLM reinforcement learning through a controlled setup covering safety-oriented VQA, chart VQA, and extreme reward stress tests, while systematically varying reward design, data ambiguity, model scale (2B to 32B), and RL algorithm (GRPO, RLOO, DAPO). To separate RL-induced failures from pre-existing weaknesses, we introduce Newly Rewarded Failure Rate (NRFR), which measures the hacking rate specifically among samples where RL achieves higher proxy reward than the SFT baseline. Our experiments reveal three main findings. First, outcome-only rewards cause severe hacking (up to 48.1% Reward Hacking Rate), and NRFR exceeding RHR confirms that RL actively creates new failures rather than merely inheriting them. Second, scaling reduces hacking but is insufficient alone. Even the 32B model retains 54.9% worse rate under outcome-only rewards, while answer-aware rewards invert the average oracle direction at every scale, reaching the strongest gap when combined with 32B. Third, algorithm robustness is scale-dependent, with GRPO consistently most resistant (RHR 48–53%), RLOO persistently vulnerable (67–68%), and DAPO improving sharply from 67.2% at 2B to 45.5% at 8B. We further show that visual-evidence rewards help only when the verifier is reliable: keyword-based verification increases hacking relative to answer-aware rewards, whereas VLM-as-judge semantic verification reduces it. Overall, multimodal reward hacking is a systematic consequence of optimizing imperfect rewards. Robust MLLM alignment requires rewards and verifiers that remain reliable under optimization pressure.

1 简介Introduction

强化学习越来越多地被采用作为多模态大型语言模型（例如 Qwen3-VL）的后训练范例Reinforcement learning is increasingly adopted as a post-training paradigm for multimodal large language models such as Qwen3-VL[bai2025qwen3]和 GPT-4oand GPT-4o[hurst2024gpt]。训练规模的实时人工评估成本高昂. Real-time human evaluation at training scale is prohibitively expensive[ouyang2022training]。因此，越来越多的系统依赖于自动奖励信号，包括确定性评分规则、关键字检查器、格式验证器、答案匹配器和基于法学硕士的评委。然而，高奖励分数并不一定能保证真正的绩效提高。. More and more systems therefore rely on automated reward signals, including deterministic scoring rules, keyword checkers, format verifiers, answer matchers, and LLM-based judges. However, a high reward score does not necessarily guarantee genuine performance improvement.

原因是结构性的。自动奖励需要以低廉的成本进行大规模计算，因此它们倾向于对易于验证的内容（关键字存在、格式合规性或答案字符串匹配）进行评分，而不是对正确的内容进行评分。在多模式任务中，这种限制尤其明显，因为主要证据（视觉输入）很难用纯文本或基础薄弱的奖励进行验证。因此，一项策略可以在不提高或降低其视觉推理能力的情况下提高其奖励分数。我们称这个间隙为The reason is structural. Automated rewards need to be cheap to compute at scale, so they tend to score what is easy to verify (keyword presence, format compliance, or answer-string match) rather than what is correct. In multimodal tasks this limitation is especially pronounced because the primary evidence, visual input, is difficult to verify with text-only or weakly grounded rewards. A policy can therefore improve its reward score without improving, or while degrading, its visual reasoning. 奖励黑客，即政策利用奖励错误指定而不提高任务绩效，已在纯文本环境中得到广泛研究。之前的工作探索了奖励鲁棒性、奖励塑造、贝叶斯建模和梯度正则化Reward hacking, where policies exploit reward misspecification without improving task performance, has been widely studied in text-only settings. Prior work has explored reward robustness, reward shaping, Bayesian modeling, and gradient regularization[yan2024reward, fu2025reward, duan2026mitigating, ackermann2026gradient]。理论分析表明，黑客攻击可能源于结构优化激励，而不是实施工件. Theoretical analyses show that hacking can arise from structural optimization incentives rather than implementation artifacts[wang2026reward, shao2025spurious]。在数学推理、代码生成和安全对齐中也观察到了类似的现象，其中模型满足奖励标准，但没有获得预期的能力。然而，现有的研究仍然主要仅限于文本，并且没有研究当正确性取决于视觉基础时奖励黑客如何变化。. Similar phenomena have been observed in math reasoning, code generation, and safety alignment, where models satisfy reward criteria without acquiring the intended capability. However, existing studies remain largely text-only and do not examine how reward hacking changes when correctness depends on visual grounding.

3.6 机械合成Mechanistic Synthesis

这五项发现共有三种机制，每种机制作用于训练循环的不同阶段。首先，当奖励函数的激励不对称或其验证者不可靠时，奖励函数会创建一条剥削路径。其次，强化学习算法将该信号带入策略中，其选择决定了发现和强化捷径的效率。第三，模型规模限制了奖励改进可以补偿的程度，因为较大的模型可以利用更好的奖励，但无法替代奖励。The five findings share three mechanisms, each acting at a different stage of the training loop. First, the reward function creates an exploitation path when its incentives are asymmetric or its verifier is unreliable. Second, the RL algorithm carries that signal into the policy, and its choice determines how efficiently the shortcut is discovered and reinforced. Third, model scale caps how far reward improvements can compensate, since larger models leverage a better reward but cannot substitute for one.

这三个阶段是复合的，它们的组合指向一个通用的处方而不是单一的解决方案。当设计不良的奖励（例如仅输出信号）在捷径有效的算法（例如 RLOO）下在具有模糊或边界重的数据的较小模型上进行训练时，失败会被放大，因为每个阶段都会增加对相同开发路径的压力。稳健性向相反方向提高。可靠验证的奖励（例如 VLM 作为法官接地）与捷径稀释算法（例如 GRPO）在具有更清洁数据的较大模型上相结合，可以减少每个阶段的黑客攻击。在我们的实验中，我们观察到两个方向，最差的配置产生接近完全的模板崩溃，而最强的配置仍然留下非零的 RHR，这表明没有单一的杠杆是足够的，并且不完美奖励下的奖励黑客是一种结构性风险，需要通过奖励设计、算法选择和规模来共同管理。The three stages compound, and their combination points to a general prescription rather than a single fix. Failures are amplified when a poorly designed reward such as an outcome-only signal is trained under a shortcut-efficient algorithm such as RLOO on a smaller model with ambiguous or boundary-heavy data, since each stage adds pressure toward the same exploitation path. Robustness improves in the opposite direction. A reliably verified reward such as VLM-as-judge grounding combined with a shortcut-dilutive algorithm such as GRPO on a larger model with cleaner data reduces hacking at every stage. In our experiments we observe both directions, with the worst configurations producing near-total template collapse and the strongest configurations still leaving nonzero RHR, indicating that no single lever is sufficient and that reward hacking under imperfect rewards is a structural risk to be jointly managed across reward design, algorithm choice, and scale.

5 结论Conclusion

我们对多模态大语言模型的强化学习中的奖励黑客进行了系统的实证研究。使用具有面向安全的 VQA 和极端奖励压力测试的受控沙箱，我们检查了奖励设计、数据模糊性、模型规模、强化学习算法和视觉验证如何影响奖励预言机的不匹配。我们的研究结果表明，多模式奖励黑客行为不是偶然的产物，而是优化不完美奖励的系统性结果。强化学习可以新奖励预言无效的行为。模糊的决策边界暴露了奖励盲点。算法选择改变了捷径放大。不可靠的视觉验证可能会造成严重危害。规模化奖励和答案感知奖励有所帮助，但单独来看这两种奖励似乎都不够。因此，稳健的 MLLM 对齐可能不仅需要更大的模型，还需要在优化压力下保持可靠的奖励、验证器和算法。We presented a systematic empirical study of reward hacking in reinforcement learning for multimodal large language models. Using a controlled sandbox with safety-oriented VQA and extreme reward stress tests, we examined how reward design, data ambiguity, model scale, RL algorithm, and visual verification shape reward-oracle mismatch. Our findings suggest that multimodal reward hacking is not an incidental artifact but a systematic consequence of optimizing imperfect rewards. RL can newly reward oracle-invalid behaviors. Ambiguous decision boundaries expose reward blind spots. Algorithm choice changes shortcut amplification. Unreliable visual verification can be actively harmful. Scaling and answer-aware rewards help, but neither appears sufficient alone. Robust MLLM alignment therefore likely requires not only larger models, but also rewards, verifiers, and algorithms that remain reliable under optimization pressure.

局限性Limitations

尽管我们评估了从 2B 到 32B 的多个尺度，但我们的研究重点是一个模型家族 (Qwen3-VL)。未来的工作应该测试相同的奖励黑客动态是否适用于其他 MLLM 系列和培训基础设施。我们的预言机评估依赖于强大的 VLM 判断，而不是大规模的人工注释，因此报告的黑客攻击率可能继承了判断特定的偏差。此外，该研究的重点是面向安全的 VQA，对其他多模态任务（例如图表推理、文档理解）的跨域验证正在进行中。这些限制并没有改变这项工作中研究的核心奖励-预言不匹配，但它们限制了我们的实证主张的范围。Our study focuses on one model family (Qwen3-VL), although we evaluate multiple scales from 2B to 32B. Future work should test whether the same reward-hacking dynamics hold across other MLLM families and training infrastructures. Our oracle evaluation relies on a strong VLM judge rather than large-scale human annotation, so the reported hacking rates may inherit judge-specific biases. In addition, the study focuses on safety-oriented VQA, and cross-domain validation on other multimodal tasks (e.g., chart reasoning, document understanding) is ongoing work. These limitations do not change the central reward-oracle mismatch studied in this work, but they bound the scope of our empirical claims.
