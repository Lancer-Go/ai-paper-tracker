摘要Abstract

安全性是在机器人和自动驾驶等现实领域部署强化学习 (RL) 代理的核心问题。 虽然基准测试一直是 RL 进步的核心，但现有的具有高保真 3D 物理的安全基准测试仍然计算缓慢，限制了大规模实验和快速原型设计。 为了弥补这一差距，我们建议Safety is a core concern for deploying reinforcement learning (RL) agents in real-world domains such as robotics and autonomous driving. While benchmarks have been central to progress in RL, existing safety benchmarks with high-fidelity 3D physics remain computationally slow, limiting large-scale experimentation and rapid prototyping. To address this gap, we propose克雷克斯CRAX (C受约束的onstrainedRL A通过 JA 加速ccelerated with JAX）。 CRAX 建立在具有逼真 3D 动态的 MuJoCo XLA (MJX) 物理引擎之上，利用矢量化运算和硬件加速，产生高达). Built on top of the MuJoCo XLA (MJX) physics engine with realistic 3D dynamics, CRAX leverages vectorized operations and hardware acceleration, yielding up to∼\sim与基于 CPU 的同类安全基准相比，速度提高了 100 倍。 该基准测试具有六个环境套件和三个特定于代理的任务，每个任务跨越三个难度级别。 评估六种流行的安全强化学习方法表明，没有一种方法可以在所有任务中占主导地位，并揭示了性能和安全性之间的权衡。 我们发现，跨难度级别和安全转移的课程学习可以比在更困难的环境中进行直接训练提高表现。100x speedups over comparable CPU-based safety benchmarks. The benchmark features six environment suites and three agent-specific tasks, each spanning three difficulty levels. Evaluating six popular safe RL methods shows that no single approach dominates across all tasks, and reveals the trade-offs between performance and safety. We find that curriculum learning across difficulty levels and safety transfer can improve performance over direct training in harder settings.

6 结论Conclusions

我们推出了 CRAX，这是一种基于高保真 3D 物理模拟的 SafeRL 硬件加速基准测试，可实现现有 CPU 限制基准测试无法进行的大规模实验。其具有难度进展的多样化环境套件允许评估跨代理形态的安全性能权衡。我们凭经验证明了 CRAX 的计算优势、流行的 SafeRL 算法的优点和局限性，以及课程学习和安全迁移在具有挑战性的环境中提高性能的潜力。我们希望 CRAX 成为开发、分析和基准测试未来安全强化学习方法的重要工具。We introduced CRAX, a hardware-accelerated benchmark for SafeRL built on high-fidelity 3D physics simulation, enabling large-scale experimentation that is infeasible with existing CPU-bound benchmarks. Its diverse environment suites with difficulty progression allow evaluation of safety–performance trade-offs across agent morphologies. We empirically demonstrate the computational advantages of CRAX, the strengths and limitations of popular SafeRL algorithms, and the potential for curriculum learning and safety transfer to improve performance in challenging settings. We hope CRAX serves as a vital tool for developing, analyzing, and benchmarking future safe RL methods.

7 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

截至撰写本文时，MJX 尚不支持基于 CPU 的 MuJoCo 中可用的所有功能，例如某些刚体碰撞类型。这限制了当前可以在 CRAX 中表达的场景范围。此外，我们的评估仅侧重于政策方法。在 CRAX 中探索离策略和基于模型的安全强化学习方法仍然是未来工作的重要方向。在这项工作的范围内，我们仅通过预期累积成本约束来评估安全性，而未探索其他安全公式。最后，我们的实验仅考虑基于状态的观察和单代理设置。未来的工作可以从具体的角度研究仅从像素观察中学习，并将 CRAX 扩展到多智能体安全场景。As of the time of writing, MJX does not yet support all features available in the CPU-based MuJoCo, such as certain rigid-body collision types. This restricts the range of scenarios that can currently be expressed within CRAX. In addition, our evaluation focuses exclusively on on-policy methods. Exploring off-policy and model-based safe RL approaches within CRAX remains an important direction for future work. In the scope of this work, we evaluate safety exclusively through expected cumulative cost constraints, leaving other safety formulations unexplored. Finally, our experiments only consider state-based observations and single-agent settings. Future work could investigate learning solely from pixel observations from an embodied perspective and extend CRAX to multi-agent safety scenarios.

A.2 任务描述Task Descriptions

A.2.1 导航套件Navigation Suite

目标。Goal.

特工：点、蚂蚁、人形、蜘蛛Agents: Point, Ant, Humanoid, Spider
导航到目标区域，同时避免遍布整个竞技场的危险。当代理到达目标时，它会在新的随机位置重生。危险可能是可碰撞的（阻塞，基于接触的成本）或不可碰撞的（传递，基于接近的成本）。这是一项没有最终成功状态的连续任务。难度级别增加了危险的数量并引入了混合危险类型（圆柱体和立方体）。Navigate to goal regions while avoiding hazards scattered throughout an arena. When the agent reaches a goal, it respawns at a new random location. Hazards may be collidable (blocking, with contact-based cost) or non-collidable (pass-through, with proximity-based cost). This is a continuous task with no terminal success state. Difficulty levels increase the number of hazards and introduce mixed hazard types (cylinders and cubes).

按钮。Button.

特工：点、蚂蚁、人形、蜘蛛Agents: Point, Ant, Humanoid, Spider
导航以在多个按钮中按下正确的“活动”按钮，同时避免危险和可选的移动小魔怪。活动按钮通过罗盘传感器进行视觉指示和观察。按错按钮可能会产生费用。在连续模式下，每次成功后都会激活一个新按钮。 1 级竞技场中有 4 个危险和 4 个小魔怪。 2 级在更大的竞技场中增加到 8 个危险和 6 个小魔怪。 3 级在较小的竞技场中有 12 个危险和 8 个速度更快的小魔怪，使导航更具挑战性。Navigate to press the correct “active” button among multiple buttons while avoiding hazards and optional moving gremlins. The active button is visually indicated and observable through a compass sensor. Pressing a wrong button can optionally incur a cost. In continual mode, a new button becomes active after each success. Level 1 has 4 hazards and 4 gremlins in a arena. Level 2 increases to 8 hazards and 6 gremlins in a larger arena. Level 3 has 12 hazards and 8 faster gremlins in a smaller arena, making navigation more challenging.

圆圈。Circle.

特工：点、蚂蚁、人形、蜘蛛Agents: Point, Ant, Humanoid, Spider
围绕固定中心点以目标半径保持圆形轨道。代理因切向速度（沿圆周移动）而受到奖励，并因偏离目标半径而受到惩罚。难度级别逐渐增加边界约束和危险：1 级仅具有 x 边界，没有危险。 2 级添加了一个带有 1 个圆柱体危险的方形边界。 3 级的边界较小，有 2 个气缸危险。Maintain a circular orbit at a target radius around a fixed center point. The agent is rewarded for tangential velocity (moving along the circle) and penalized for deviating from the target radius. Difficulty levels progressively add boundary constraints and hazards: Level 1 has x-boundaries only with no hazards. Level 2 adds a square boundary with 1 cylinder hazard. Level 3 has a smaller boundary with 2 cylinder hazards.

推。Push.

特工：点、蚂蚁、人形、蜘蛛Agents: Point, Ant, Humanoid, Spider
将可移动方块推入目标区域，同时避免危险。与目标不同，Push a movable block into goal regions while avoiding hazards. Unlike Goal, the块block（不是代理人）必须达到目标。代理必须协调接近方块并将其推向正确的方向。只有代理与危险的相互作用才会产生成本；该块可以自由地穿过危险。难度级别会增加目标移动速度：级别 1 是静止的，级别 2 移动缓慢，级别 3 移动快速。(not the agent) must reach the goal. The agent must coordinate approaching the block and pushing it in the correct direction. Only agent-hazard interactions incur cost; the block passes through hazards freely. Difficulty levels increase goal movement speed: Level 1 is stationary, Level 2 moves slowly, and Level 3 moves fast.

A.2.2 其他套房Other Suites

速度。Velocity.

代理：Ant、HalfCheetah、Hopper、Humanoid、Walker2dAgents: Ant, HalfCheetah, Hopper, Humanoid, Walker2d
具有附加速度约束的标准运动。智能体必须最大化前进进度，同时将其速度保持在阈值以下。三个难度级别逐渐收紧速度限制（基线的 100%、75%、50%）。Standard locomotion with an added velocity constraint. The agent must maximize forward progress while keeping its speed below a threshold. Three difficulty levels progressively tighten the speed limit (100%, 75%, 50% of baseline).

高度。Height.

特工：人形Agents: Humanoid
人形机器人必须向前移动，同时保持在最大高度阈值以下，模拟低天花板约束。随着高度超过阈值，成本平稳增加，鼓励代理蹲下。基于 HumanoidStandup 环境，并添加了向前运动奖励。难度级别降低了最大高度要求。The humanoid must move forward while staying below a maximum height threshold, simulating a low ceiling constraint. Cost increases smoothly as height exceeds the threshold, encouraging the agent to crouch. Based on the HumanoidStandup environment with added forward locomotion reward. Difficulty levels lower the maximum height requirement.

途径。Pathway.

代理：Walker2d、HalfCheetah、HopperAgents: Walker2d, HalfCheetah, Hopper
双足或跳跃代理穿过一条路径，沿其路线放置有不可碰撞的危险区域。当脚踏入危险区域时会产生成本，穿透深度越深，成本越高。危险是随机放置的，具有不同的间隙和横向偏移，要求代理仔细计时其步骤。跌倒会产生终端成本。难度级别降低了危险之间的最大差距。A bipedal or hopping agent traverses a path with non-collidable hazard zones placed along its route. Cost is incurred when feet step inside hazard regions, with deeper penetration causing higher cost. Hazards are randomly placed with varying gaps and lateral offsets, requiring the agent to time its steps carefully. Falling incurs a terminal cost. Difficulty levels decrease the maximum gap between hazards.

到达。Reach.

特工：Reacher（2连杆臂）Agents: Reacher (2-link arm)
平面二连杆机械臂必须到达随机放置的目标，同时避免工作空间中散布的平面危险。当手臂的任何部分与危险相交时都会产生成本。在沿着两个链接的离散点对手臂进行采样以检测碰撞。奖励强调接近目标并为达到目标提供奖励。难度级别会增加危险的数量：1 级 (4)、2 级 (7)、3 级 (10)。A planar 2-link robotic arm must reach a randomly placed target while avoiding flat hazards scattered on the workspace. Cost is incurred when any part of the arm intersects a hazard. The arm is sampled at discrete points along both links to detect collisions. Reward emphasizes proximity to the target with a bonus for reaching it. Difficulty levels increase the number of hazards: Level 1 (4), Level 2 (7), Level 3 (10).

蜘蛛腿。SpiderLegs.

特工：蜘蛛（6足）Agents: Spider (6-legged)
六足蜘蛛必须向前行走，同时保持特定的腿离开地面，从而迫使其采取不寻常的步态。级别 1 限制 2 个对角腿，级别 2 限制 3 个腿（三脚架模式），级别 3 限制 4 个腿（仅中心腿可以接触）。受限制的脚接触地板的每个时间步都会产生成本。A hexapod spider must walk forward while keeping specified legs off the ground, forcing unusual gaits. Level 1 restricts 2 diagonal legs, Level 2 restricts 3 legs (tripod pattern), and Level 3 restricts 4 legs (only center legs may touch). Cost is incurred each timestep a restricted foot contacts the floor.

    步态限制Gait restriction(SpiderLegs)：当受限的脚与地面接触时的二元成本，迫使受限的运动模式。(SpiderLegs): Binary costs when restricted feet make ground contact, forcing constrained locomotion patterns.

  • •

    足部放置Foot placement（路径）：当脚进入危险区域时，穿透成本呈二次方，穿透越深，成本越高。包括跌倒的最终惩罚。(Pathway): Quadratic penetration costs when feet step inside hazard regions, with deeper penetration causing higher cost. Includes a terminal penalty for falling.
