1. 简介Introduction

可视化在数据驱动的世界中无处不在，是传达复杂数字信息的强大工具。 然而，这种说服力常常受到以下因素的影响：Visualizations are ubiquitous in the data-driven world, serving as powerful tools for communicating complex numerical information. However, this persuasive power is frequently compromised by误导性图表misleading charts：旨在通过轴操纵、挑选或不成比例的编码等策略来扭曲数据感知的可视化: visualizations designed to distort data perception through tactics such as axis manipulation, cherry-picking, or disproportionate encoding(Pandey et al., 2015; Correll and Heer, 2017; McNutt et al., 2020; Lo et al., 2022; Lan and Liu, 2024)。 可视化和人机交互社区广泛研究了这些欺骗性设计如何操纵人类受众，产生了完善的分类法和实证评估. The visualization and human-computer interaction communities have extensively studied how these deceptive designs manipulate human audiences, yielding well-established taxonomies and empirical evaluations(Tufte and Graves-Morris, 1983; Huff, 2023; Szafir, 2018; Yang et al., 2021; Rho et al., 2024)以及读写能力评估, as well as literacy assessments(Lee et al., 2016; Ge et al., 2023).

最近，随着视觉语言模型（VLM）Recently, as Visual Language Models (VLMs)(Zhang et al., 2023a, b)随着自动化图表理解变得越来越重要，一个关键的新挑战已经出现：确保它们对这些误导性可视化的鲁棒性。 传统的图表理解任务主要评估良性假设下显式视觉事实的提取become increasingly central to automated chart understanding, a critical new challenge has emerged: ensuring their robustness against these misleading visualizations. Traditional chart comprehension tasks primarily evaluate the extraction of explicit visual facts under benign assumptions(Masry et al., 2022; Xia et al., 2025; Wei et al., 2024)。 相比之下，新出现的任务. In contrast, the emerging task of误导性图表问答Misleading Chart Question Answering（MQA）将范式转向视觉批判性推理，要求模型主动检测操作并解决跨模态矛盾。 然而，最近的研究揭示了一个巨大的差距：在有针对性的基准上，例如(MQA) shifts the paradigm toward visual critical reasoning, requiring models to actively detect manipulations and resolve cross-modal contradictions. However, recent studies reveal a significant gap: on targeted benchmarks like the误导性图表质量检查Misleading ChartQA数据集显示，最先进的 VLM 准确率始终低于 50%dataset, state-of-the-art VLMs consistently score below 50% accuracy(Chen et al., 2025b)。 多种评价证实. As corroborated by diverse evaluations(Tonglet et al., 2025; Bharti et al., 2024)，这些模型的表现并不比未经训练的人类观察者更好，因为它们无法保持针对欺骗性线索的逻辑一致性。, these models perform no better than untrained human observers because they fail to maintain logical consistency against deceptive cues.

当前自动解释图表的方法主要遵循两种模式。 第一个范式，即端到端 VLM 路径，利用多模态模型进行直接视觉推理The current methods for automatically interpreting charts mainly follow two patterns. The first paradigm, the end-to-end VLM path, leverages multimodal models for direct visual reasoning(Zhang et al., 2023a; Bendeck and Stasko, 2024; Pandey and Ottley, 2025; Valentim et al., 2025)。 尽管这些模型在 ChartQA 等标准基准上表现良好. Although these models perform well on standard benchmarks such as ChartQA(Masry et al., 2022)，他们整体处理图表，有利于, they process charts holistically, favoring宏观视觉启发法macro-level visual heuristics（例如，线条的整体斜率或相对条形高度）超过细粒度的结构线索，例如精确的刻度值。 如图中突出显示的(e.g., the overall slope of a line or relative bar heights) over fine-grained structural cues like exact tick values. As highlighted in Figure1，这种架构限制使它们非常容易受到恶意编码的影响。 例如，当 Y 轴反转（顶部为零）时，视觉编码器的注意力会被陡峭的向下物理斜率劫持。 这迫使模型陷入“认知谄媚”，错误地预测数值下降并完全忽略实际增加的数据值。, this architectural limitation makes them highly vulnerable to malicious encodings. This forces the model into “cognitive sycophancy,” erroneously predicting a numerical decline and completely ignoring the actual increasing data values.

理想的解决方案在于这两条路径的协同融合，从被动描述转向主动验证： 就像人类审计员将视觉上的欺骗性趋势与文字数字数据进行交叉验证。 然而，将诊断视觉与数据驱动推理相结合提出了三个与感知、推理和优化级别相对应的重要挑战：An ideal solution lies in the synergistic fusion of these two paths, moving beyond passive description toward active verification: much like a human auditor who cross-verifies a visually deceptive trend against literal numerical data. 2.1. 视觉欺骗和图表素养Visual Deception and Chart Literacy

传统的图表理解研究主要集中于从自然可视化中提取事实，如FigureQA 等早期数据集所示Traditional chart comprehension research primarily focused on factual extraction from natural visualizations, as seen in early datasets like FigureQA(Kahou et al., 2017), DVQA (Kafle et al., 2018), 绘图QA, PlotQA(Methani et al., 2020)和图表-HQAand Chart-HQA(Chen et al., 2025a).

误导性图表利用欺骗性编码（例如轴截断和倒轴）来利用人类认知偏差，长期以来一直是视觉传达中关注的主题Misleading charts, which utilize deceptive encodings (such as axis truncation and inverted axes) to exploit human cognitive biases, have long been a subject of concern in visual communication(Tufte and Graves-Morris, 1983; Pandey et al., 2015)。 最近的基准测试，例如误导性 ChartQA. Recent benchmarks like Misleading ChartQA(Chen et al., 2025b), 叶质量保证, LEAF-QA(Chaudhry et al., 2020)和查托姆and CHARTOM(Bharti et al., 2024)进一步表明，即使是先进的 VLM 也很容易受到这些视觉“陷阱”的影响，在识别“心理理论”欺骗模式方面通常表现并不比普通观众更好have further shown that even advanced VLMs are highly susceptible to these visual “traps,” often performing no better than general audiences in identifying “Theory-of-Mind” deceptive patterns(Lo and Qu, 2024).

Models like MATCHA(Liu et al., 2023b)和部署and DEPLOT(Liu et al., 2023a)利用数学推理和图到表转换来链接像素和结构化数据。 尽管如此，基于 ViT 等主干的整体图像处理leverage math reasoning and plot-to-table translation to link pixels and structured data. Despite this, holistic image processing based on backbones like ViT(Dosovitskiy et al., 2020)或 Pix2Structureor Pix2Struct(Lee et al., 2023)通常会忽略细粒度的异常，例如不均匀的轴尺度。 最近的作品，如 ChartXoften overlooks fine-grained anomalies, such as non-uniform axis scales. Recent works like ChartX(Xia et al., 2025)和问图and AskChart(Yang et al., 2024)探索了通过 OCR 和文本增强来增强 VLM，以提供数值约束。 类似的细粒度特征隔离和模态对齐策略在多模态错误信息检测中也被证明是有效的have explored augmenting VLMs with OCR and textual enhancement to provide numerical constraints. 2.3. 有监督的微调和基于强化学习的对齐Supervised Fine-Tuning and RL-based Alignment

VLM 优化的最新进展已从一般指令遵循转向专门的推理对齐Recent advancements in VLM optimization have shifted from general instruction following to specialized reasoning alignment(Ouyang et al., 2022; Chowdhery et al., 2023)。 监督微调（SFT）仍然是注入特定领域知识的基石，例如“持怀疑态度”的调查思想链（CoT）.
