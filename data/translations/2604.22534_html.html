摘要Abstract

电子健康记录 (EHR) 的特征工程由于不规则的观察间隔、可变的测量频率以及临床时间序列固有的结构稀疏性而变得复杂。现有的自动化方法要么缺乏临床领域意识，要么假设干净、定期采样的输入，限制了它们对现实世界 EHR 数据的适用性。我们呈现Feature engineering for Electronic Health Records (EHR) is complicated by irregular observation intervals, variable measurement frequencies, and structural sparsity inherent to clinical time series. Existing automated methods either lack clinical domain awareness or assume clean, regularly sampled inputs, limiting their applicability to real-world EHR data. We present特征EHR-法学硕士FeatEHR-LLM，一个利用大型语言模型 (LLM) 从不规则采样的 EHR 时间序列生成具有临床意义的表格特征的框架。为了限制患者隐私暴露，法学硕士专门针对数据集模式和任务描述而不是原始患者记录进行操作。工具增强的生成机制为法学硕士配备了用于查询不规则时间数据的专用例程，使其能够生成可执行的特征提取代码，明确处理不均匀的观察模式和信息稀疏性。 FeatEHR-LLM 通过迭代、循环验证管道支持单变量和多变量特征生成。通过对四个 ICU 数据集的八个临床预测任务进行评估，我们的框架在 8 个任务中的 7 个上实现了最高的平均 AUROC，与强基线相比提高了高达 6 个百分点。代码可在, a framework that leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate clinically meaningful tabular features from irregularly sampled EHR time series. To limit patient privacy exposure, the LLM operates exclusively on dataset schemas and task descriptions rather than raw patient records. A tool-augmented generation mechanism equips the LLM with specialized routines for querying irregular temporal data, enabling it to produce executable feature-extraction code that explicitly handles uneven observation patterns and informative sparsity. FeatEHR-LLM supports both univariate and multivariate feature generation through an iterative, validation-in-the-loop pipeline. Evaluated on eight clinical prediction tasks across four ICU datasets, our framework achieves the highest mean AUROC on 7 out of 8 tasks, with improvements of up to 6 percentage points over strong baselines. 1 简介Introduction

电子健康记录 (EHR) 包含丰富的纵向数据，可捕获患者的健康轨迹。在这些记录中，时间序列测量（例如生命体征和实验室结果）构成了临床信息的支柱。然而，对这些数据进行建模具有挑战性，因为临床时间序列本质上是不规则采样的，表现为可变的观察频率和事件驱动的测量。由此产生的差距代表结构稀疏，仅在临床指示时记录测量结果，而不是统计缺失Electronic Health Records (EHRs) contain rich longitudinal data that capture patient health trajectories. Within these records, time series measurements, such as vital signs and laboratory results, form the backbone of clinical information. However, modeling this data is challenging because clinical time series are inherently irregularly sampled, manifesting as variable observation frequencies and event-driven measurements. The resulting gaps represent structural sparsity, with measurements recorded only when clinically indicated, rather than statistical missingness[14, 45, 13]。尽管存在这些复杂性，机器学习 (ML) 越来越多地用于分析 EHR，以支持关键的临床决策. Despite these complexities, machine learning (ML) is increasingly leveraged to analyze EHRs for critical clinical decision support[6, 41].

为了有效地利用这种复杂的时态数据，特征工程仍然是基础。虽然现代深度学习 (DL) 模型可以端到端处理纵向序列，但其广泛的临床部署往往受到不透明、高数据要求和繁重的计算占用的阻碍To effectively utilize this complex temporal data, feature engineering remains fundamental. While modern deep learning (DL) models can process longitudinal sequences end-to-end, their widespread clinical deployment is often hindered by opacity, high data requirements, and heavy computational footprints[8]。因此，显式特征工程仍然至关重要。这对于临床环境中青睐的可解释、高效的基于树的模型是绝对必要的. Consequently, explicit feature engineering remains critical. It is strictly necessary for interpretable, efficient tree-based models favored in clinical settings[3, 43]。此外，显式设计的特征可以作为静态协变量注入到高级深度学习架构中，补充学习到的表示，以提高整体预测性能和透明度. Furthermore, explicitly engineered features can be injected into advanced DL architectures as static covariates, complementing learned representations to improve overall predictive performance and transparency[24, 28].

Recently, Large Language Models (LLMs) have emerged to bridge this gap. By leveraging vast domain knowledge, LLMs inject zero-shot clinical context, improving sample efficiency and generating transparent, rule-based features[16, 31, 1]。然而，现有的基于法学硕士的框架主要关注静态表格数据并假设干净的结构化输入。将它们应用到纵向电子病历中，由于其复杂的时间依赖性、超出上下文的序列长度和结构稀疏性，仍然是一个重大的、尚未解决的挑战。. Applying them to longitudinal EHRs, with their complex temporal dependencies, context-exceeding sequence lengths, and structural sparsity, remains a significant, unaddressed challenge.

在本节中，我们将回顾表格和时间序列数据的特征工程方法，重点是生成显式、可检查特征的方法（包括法学硕士辅助管道）。我们有意排除基于端到端学习的表示方法（例如深度序列编码器），因为它们学习潜在嵌入而不是显式工程特征，因此属于与本工作目标不同的方法范式和评估设置。In this section, we review feature engineering methods for tabular and time series data, with emphasis on approaches that generate explicit, inspectable features (including LLM-assisted pipelines). We intentionally exclude end-to-end learning-based representation methods (e.g., deep sequence encoders) because they learn latent embeddings rather than explicit engineered features, and therefore belong to a different methodological paradigm and evaluation setting than the one targeted in this work.

基于变换的特征工程Transformation-Based Feature Engineering

基于转换的方法通过预定义的数学运算自动创建特征。对于表格数据，OpenFE 等框架Transformation-based methods automate feature creation through predefined mathematical operations. For tabular data, frameworks like OpenFE[42], 自动功能, AutoFeat[17]，和获取, and FETCH[23]依靠选择算法来过滤预测效用，成功生成大量候选空间。对于时间序列，库提取跨统计、谱和分形域的时间描述符（例如，tsfreshsuccessfully generate massive candidate spaces, relying on selection algorithms to filter for predictive utility. For time series, libraries extract temporal descriptors across statistical, spectral, and fractal domains (e.g., tsfresh[7], TSFEL [9], 凯特, Kats[18]和第二十二条军规, and Catch22[29]).

虽然在批量生成方面有效，但这些方法存在两个主要限制。首先，他们缺乏语义意识；他们盲目地应用数学变换而不了解变量的领域上下文，常常导致充满冗余或临床上无意义的特征的“维度诅咒”。其次，它们绝大多数是为定期采样信号而设计的。他们从根本上与临床 EHR 时间序列特征的高度不规则采样率、可变序列长度和信息缺失作斗争，需要可能破坏潜在时间模式的临时插补步骤。While effective at bulk generation, these methods suffer from two major limitations. First, they lack semantic awareness; they blindly apply mathematical transformations without understanding the domain context of the variables, often leading to a “curse of dimensionality” filled with redundant or clinically meaningless features. Second, they are overwhelmingly designed for regularly sampled signals. They fundamentally struggle with the highly irregular sampling rates, variable sequence lengths, and informative missingness that characterize clinical EHR time series, requiring ad-hoc imputation steps that can destroy underlying temporal patterns.

基于法学硕士的特征工程LLM-Based Feature Engineering

最近的工作利用大型语言模型（LLM）将语义领域知识注入特征工程中。当前的方法通常将其表述为代码生成或规则合成任务。类似 CAAFE 的方法Recent work has leveraged Large Language Models (LLMs) to inject semantic domain knowledge into feature engineering. Current approaches generally formulate this as a code-generation or rule-synthesis task. Methods like CAAFE[16]和法学硕士and FeatLLM[15]从数据集描述或少量示例生成人类可读的转换，而其他方法如 LLM-FEgenerate human-readable transformations from dataset descriptions or few-shot examples, while others like LLM-FE[1], FeRG-法学硕士, FeRG-LLM[22], 八叉树, OCTree[31], 医学壮举, MedFeat[43]、 和 名誉, and FAMOSE[5]集成迭代反馈机制，例如验证驱动的细化、进化搜索、记忆引导选择或 ReAct 式推理执行循环，以改进生成的特征integrate iterative feedback mechanisms such as validation-driven refinement, evolutionary search, memory-guided selection, or ReAct-style reasoning-execution cycles to improve generated features[40].

尽管取得了这些进步，综合当前的文献表明，基于 LLM 的特征工程仍然受限于静态的表格数据。使这些管道适应时间序列，特别是 EHR 数据，提出了尚未解决的挑战。首先，现有方法对行模式描述进行操作，缺乏生成复杂纵向聚合所需的时间推理（例如，跟踪患者的滚动健康状况恶化）。其次，上下文窗口限制阻止法学硕士直接摄取长的、原始的数值时间序列观察序列来导出模式。最后，现有的法学硕士生成的代码管道假设干净、结构化的输入，并且目前无法为临床数据固有的不规则间隔和缺失值自主编写强大的逻辑。弥合这一差距仍然是一个开放的研究挑战。Despite these advancements, synthesizing the current literature reveals that LLM-based feature engineering remains constrained to static, tabular data. Adapting these pipelines to time series, particularly EHR data, presents unresolved challenges. First, existing methods operate on row-wise schema descriptions and lack the temporal reasoning required to generate complex longitudinal aggregations (e.g., tracking a patient’s rolling health deterioration). Second, context window limits prevent LLMs from directly ingesting long, raw sequences of numerical time-series observations to derive patterns. Finally, existing LLM-generated code pipelines assume clean, structured inputs and are currently unequipped to autonomously write robust logic for the irregular intervals and missing values inherent to clinical data. Bridging this gap remains an open research challenge.

6 讨论Discussion

违规范围Scope of irregularity

FeatEHR-LLM 解决的不规则性是 EHR 时间序列中典型的观察不规则性：在不均匀的时间测量变量，在不同的时间戳观察到不同的变量，即使在固定的特定任务范围内，记录的观察数量也因患者而异The irregularity addressed by FeatEHR-LLM is the observational irregularity typical of EHR time series: variables are measured at nonuniform times, different variables are observed at different timestamps, and the number of recorded observations varies across patients even within a fixed task-specific horizon[25, 36]。我们的工具增强特征生成旨在直接推理这些不均匀的观察模式。然而，该框架没有明确模拟 MCAR、MAR 或 MNAR 等统计缺失机制，也没有推断临床医生选择不测量变量的原因. Our tool-augmented feature generation is designed to reason over these uneven observation patterns directly. However, the framework does not explicitly model statistical missingness mechanisms such as MCAR, MAR, or MNAR, nor does it infer why a clinician chose not to measure a variable[26, 2]。这种区别很重要，因为在我们的设置中，缺失的测量主要被视为观察过程中的结构稀疏性，而不是单独参数化的缺失数据过程。. This distinction is important because, in our setting, absent measurements are treated primarily as structural sparsity in the observation process rather than as separately parameterized missing-data processes.

冗余功能Redundant features

每个变量或临床问题生成多个候选特征提取脚本会产生一组相关特征，这些特征通常在阈值或聚合逻辑上仅略有不同。尽管这引入了冗余，但我们的消融结果表明这些变体是有益的而不是纯粹多余的。在基于树的预测器（例如梯度增强决策树）中，相关特征仍然可以贡献互补信号，因为它们会引起特征空间的不同分区，并在训练期间启用替代的分割规则。因此，多个阈值变体可以捕获数据分布的不同区域，并有助于发现与其他变量的信息交互。这种行为与之前的工作一致，表明树集成在相关变量组之间分配预测重要性，并且在构建决策树时可以在它们之间灵活选择Generating multiple candidate feature extraction scripts per variable or clinical question yields a set of related features that often differ only slightly in thresholds or aggregation logic. Although this introduces redundancy, our ablation results suggest that these variants are beneficial rather than purely superfluous. In tree-based predictors such as gradient boosted decision trees, correlated features can still contribute complementary signal because they induce different partitions of the feature space and enable alternative splitting rules during training. Consequently, multiple threshold variants may capture distinct regions of the data distribution and facilitate the discovery of informative interactions with other variables. This behavior is consistent with prior work showing that tree ensembles distribute predictive importance across groups of correlated variables and can flexibly select among them when constructing decision trees[30]。更广泛地说，机器学习文献表明，特征表示的多样性可以通过提供相同底层信号的互补视图来提高预测性能，这是与属性装袋和表示集成密切相关的想法. More broadly, the machine learning literature has shown that diversity in feature representations can improve predictive performance by providing complementary views of the same underlying signal, an idea closely related to attribute bagging and representation ensembles[4]。对树集成中特征选择的实证研究进一步表明，预测重要性通常分布在相关特征组中，这意味着过于积极地删除这些特征可能会消除有用的替代决策边界. Empirical studies of feature selection in tree ensembles further demonstrate that predictive importance is often spread across correlated feature groups, implying that removing such features too aggressively may eliminate useful alternative decision boundaries[27]。总之，这些发现表明 LLM 生成的特征变体可以作为临床信号的补充表示，增加下游模型可用的表示灵活性。. Together, these findings suggest that LLM-generated feature variants can act as complementary representations of clinical signals, increasing the representational flexibility available to the downstream model.

计算成本Computational cost

我们的框架增加了特征生成和提取期间的计算，因为它依赖于多轮和每个提示的多个候选程序。 LLM 调用的数量与变量数量、多元问题数量、轮数以及每个提示的样本数量成比例。然而，与需要将每个患者的时间序列序列化为文本的零样本或基于嵌入的方法相比，从法学硕士的角度来看，我们的方法在推理时间上效率更高。 LLM 仅接收元数据和任务描述，这使提示保持简短并使生成独立于患者数量。因此，主要成本集中在功能建议上，而不是患者层面的提示上。Our framework increases computation during feature generation and extraction because it relies on multiple rounds and multiple candidate programs per prompt. The number of LLM calls scales with the number of variables, the number of multivariate questions, the number of rounds, and the number of samples per prompt. Nevertheless, compared with zero-shot or embedding-based approaches that require serializing each patient’s time series into text, our method is substantially more efficient at inference time from the LLM perspective. The LLM only receives metadata and the task description, which keeps the prompt short and makes generation independent of the number of patients. The main cost is therefore concentrated in feature proposal rather than patient-level prompting.

临床可解释性Clinical interpretability

我们的方法的一个实际优点是生成的特征被表达为显式的、可执行的规则，而不是不透明的潜在表示。这使得它们比端到端表示学习方法生成的特征更容易检查A practical advantage of our approach is that the generated features are expressed as explicit, executable rules rather than opaque latent representations. This makes them easier to inspect than features produced by end-to-end representation learning methods[11]。然而，不应夸大可解释性：可理解的代码并不自动意味着临床有效性. However, interpretability should not be overstated: understandable code does not automatically imply clinical validity[10]。此外，由于这些特征是由法学硕士提出的，生成仍然可能受到幻觉的影响，并且这种风险可能会在有利于多样性和创造力的较高采样温度下增加. Moreover, because the features are proposed by an LLM, generation can still be affected by hallucination, and this risk may increase at higher sampling temperatures that favor diversity and creativity[44, 21, 35]。在临床环境中，这种看似合理但不正确的特征定义尤其令人担忧，因为它们可能编码不安全的假设，同时保持表面上的可解释性. In the clinical setting, such plausible but incorrect feature definitions are particularly concerning because they may encode unsafe assumptions while remaining superficially interpretable[32]。因此，生成的特征应在使用前进行经验验证并由临床医生或领域专家进行审查，特别是当它们可能影响高风险决策时. For this reason, generated features should be validated empirically and reviewed by clinicians or domain experts before use, especially when they may influence high-stakes decisions[44].

7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 FeatEHR-LLM，这是一个利用 LLM 从不规则采样的 EHR 时间序列中设计临床驱动的表格特征的框架。通过专门对数据集元数据而不是原始记录进行操作，该框架限制了患者隐私暴露，同时通过旨在处理结构稀疏性和可变观察频率的工具增强机制生成可执行的特征提取代码。多轮单变量和多变量生成，加上循环内语法和运行时验证，产生多样化且强大的特征集。在 4 个 ICU 数据集的 8 项临床预测任务中，FeatEHR-LLM 在 8 项任务中的 7 项上实现了最高的平均 AUROC，比基线提高了高达 6 个百分点。生成的功能仍然可以完全解释为可执行代码，并无缝集成到标准 ML 管道中。主要限制包括生成特征逻辑中法学硕士幻觉的风险以及缺乏明确的缺失数据机制建模，这两者都需要在临床部署之前进行专家审查。未来的工作可以将该框架扩展到其他 EHR 模式，并纳入自动化临床验证反馈。We presented FeatEHR-LLM, a framework that leverages LLMs to engineer clinically motivated tabular features from irregularly sampled EHR time series. By operating exclusively on dataset metadata rather than raw records, the framework limits patient privacy exposure while producing executable feature-extraction code through a tool-augmented mechanism designed to handle structural sparsity and variable observation frequencies. Multi-round univariate and multivariate generation, coupled with in-loop syntax and runtime validation, yields diverse and robust feature sets. Across eight clinical prediction tasks on four ICU datasets, FeatEHR-LLM achieves the highest mean AUROC on 7 out of 8 tasks, with gains of up to 6 percentage points over baselines. Generated features remain fully interpretable as executable code and integrate seamlessly into standard ML pipelines. Key limitations include the risk of LLM hallucinations in generated feature logic and the absence of explicit missing-data mechanism modeling, both of which warrant expert review before clinical deployment. Future work could extend the framework to additional EHR modalities and incorporate automated clinical validation feedback.
