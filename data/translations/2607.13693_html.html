摘要Abstract

社会模拟是一种计算方法，用于研究社会现象如何从个人、群体、组织及其环境之间的相互作用中产生。本文调查了从经典的基于代理的建模到基于大语言模型增强的代理的模拟和社交数字孪生的进展。它概述了它们的核心建模假设、架构组件、优点、局限性以及这些方法可以支持的声明范围。Social simulation is a computational approach for studying how social phenomena emerge from interactions among individuals, groups, organizations, and their environments. This article surveys the progression from classical agent-based modeling to large-language-model-enhanced agent-based simulations and social digital twins. It outlines their core modeling assumptions, architectural components, advantages, limitations, and the scope of claims that these approaches can support.

1 定义Definition

社会模拟是通过构建和分析代表个人、群体、组织及其相互作用的形式模型来对社会现象进行计算研究，目的是生成人工社会Social simulation is the computational study of social phenomena through the construction and analysis of formal models that represent individuals, groups, organizations, and their interactions, with the aim of generating artificial societies计算机模拟in silico观察、解释或预测集体行为的新模式。它是经典思想实验的算法延续，将社会理论的程式化“假设”场景转化为可执行模型，其结果可以被观察、测量和系统地改变。to observe, explain, or predict emergent patterns of collective behavior. It is the algorithmic continuation of classical thought experiments, translating stylized “what-if” scenarios of social theory into executable models whose outcomes can be observed, measured, and systematically varied.

2 社交模拟Social Simulations

社会系统本质上是复杂的：集体结果是由异质个体的相互作用产生的，这些个体的行为、信仰和关系随着时间的推移而演变。传统的分析方法往往难以捕捉这种复杂性，因为它们依赖于简化的假设或静态快照，而无法代表动态的、适应性的社会过程。Social systems are inherently complex: collective outcomes emerge from the interactions of heterogeneous individuals, whose behaviors, beliefs, and relationships evolve over time. Traditional analytical methods often struggle to capture this complexity, as they rely on simplified assumptions or static snapshots that cannot represent dynamic, adaptive social processes.社交模拟Social simulation是一种通过创建人工社会来应对这一挑战的计算方法is a computational approach that addresses this challenge by creating artificial societies计算机模拟in silico其中代理根据明确的规则进行交互。这些模拟使研究人员能够观察微观行为如何产生宏观现象，测试理论假设，并探索在现实世界中很难或不可能进行实验的反事实“假设”场景。 从本质上讲，社会模拟是社会理论中经典思想实验的算法延续。哲学家和社会科学家长期以来一直采用程式化的“假设”推理来探索社会现象：例如，即使个人具有温和的地点偏好，种族隔离也可能如何出现，或者小社区中的规范如何稳定。社会模拟将这些思想实验形式化为可执行模型，产生可系统分析的可衡量结果。 该领域利用了多种方法，包括基于代理的建模 (ABM)、系统动力学、网络模拟，以及最近的人工智能增强模型和数字孪生。特别是，ABM 通过在结构化环境中表示具有不同属性、状态和行为规则的个体代理，在理论和计算之间提供了一座天然的桥梁。这些模型使得以受控、可重复的方式研究合作、极化、创新扩散和社会影响等新兴动态成为可能。 在本章中，我们首先介绍经典的 ABM 作为社会模拟的基础，强调它们的结构、设计和局限性。然后，我们介绍了通过大型语言模型 (LLM) 增强的 ABM 方面的进展，该模型使代理能够使用自然语言进行交互并模拟复杂的认知和社交过程。最后，我们讨论将社会模拟集成到数字孪生中，从而实现现实世界社会技术系统的高保真、数据驱动的表示。这些观点共同说明了社会模拟从概念实验到能够解决社会科学中紧迫问题的计算框架的演变。in which agents interact according to explicit rules. These simulations allow researchers to observe how micro-level behaviors generate macro-level phenomena, to test theoretical assumptions, and to explore counterfactual “what-if” scenarios that are difficult or impossible to experiment with in the real world. At its core, social simulation is the algorithmic continuation of classical thought experiments in social theory. Philosophers and social scientists have long employed stylized “what-if” reasoning to explore social phenomena: for example, how segregation might emerge even if individuals have mild location preferences, or how norms stabilize in small communities. Social simulation formalizes these thought experiments into executable models, producing measurable outcomes that can be systematically analyzed. The field draws on diverse methods, including agent-based modeling (ABM), system dynamics, network simulations, and, more recently, AI-enhanced models and digital twins. ABMs, in particular, provide a natural bridge between theory and computation by representing individual agents with distinct attributes, states, and behavioral rules within structured environments. These models make it possible to study emergent dynamics such as cooperation, polarization, diffusion of innovations, and social influence in a controlled, repeatable manner. In this chapter, we first introduce classical ABMs as the foundation of social simulations, highlighting their structure, design, and limitations. We then present advances in ABM enhanced with Large Language Models (LLMs), which enable agents to interact using natural language and simulate complex cognitive and social processes. Finally, we discuss the integration of social simulations into digital twins, allowing high-fidelity, data-driven representations of real-world socio-technical systems. Together, these perspectives illustrate the evolution of social simulation from conceptual experiments to computational frameworks capable of addressing pressing questions in social science.

时间Time代表了基于代理的建模的一个关键但具有挑战性的方面。 现实世界的社会过程在连续的时间内发生，但计算模型必须将时间离散成可管理的步骤。 建模者必须决定适当的时间步持续时间，这决定了每个模拟周期内发生多少个动作或交互。 此外，他们必须在同步更新（所有代理同时操作）和异步更新（代理按顺序操作）之间进行选择，因为这种选择会由于路径依赖效应而显着影响模拟结果。represents a critical but challenging aspect of Agent-based modeling. Real-world social processes occur in continuous time, but computational models must discretize time into manageable steps. Modelers must decide on the appropriate time step duration, which determines how many actions or interactions occur within each simulation cycle. Additionally, they must choose between synchronous updating (where all agents act simultaneously) and asynchronous updating (where agents act sequentially), as this choice can significantly impact simulation outcomes due to path-dependence effects.

Spatially explicit models range from simple grid-based environments to complex geographic information system integrations that incorporate real-world spatial data. The spatial component becomes particularly important when modeling phenomena such as urban development or epidemic spread, where physical proximity and environmental characteristics play crucial roles.

然而，与任何技术承诺一样，SDT 的采用也面临着挑战。 首先也是最重要的是Yet, as with any technological promise, the adoption of SDTs comes with challenges. First and foremost are the成本costs：开发和维护高保真 SDT 是资源密集型的。 它需要细粒度的数据、计算基础设施和深厚的领域专业知识。 但更根本的问题是: developing and maintaining a high-fidelity SDT is resource-intensive. It requires fine-grained data, computational infrastructure, and deep domain expertise. But the more fundamental issue is验证validation。 为了成为推理和预测的可靠工具，SDT 必须至少在三个层面上得到验证。 首先，在一个. To be reliable tools for reasoning and prediction, SDTs must be validated on at least three levels. First, on a结构性的structural在层面上，研究人员应该评估数字孪生的架构是否准确反映了它所复制的现实世界系统的组成和组织。 这包括验证网络结构、代理类型、可供性和约束，确保组件及其关系与现实中观察到的相对应。 其次，关于一个level, researchers should assess whether the architecture of the digital twin accurately reflects the composition and organization of the real-world system it replicates. This includes validating the network structure, agent types, affordances, and constraints, ensuring that the components and their relationships correspond to those observed in reality. Second, on a行为的behavioral在水平上，有必要验证孪生中的代理以合理且异质的方式行事。 这意味着不仅要再现平均趋势，还要捕捉真实系统中出现的策略和响应的多样性。 尤其是在社会系统中，忽视行为异质性确实会导致误导性的预测。 第三，关于一个level, it is essential to verify that the agents within the twin behave in plausible and heterogeneous ways. This means not only reproducing average trends but capturing the diversity of strategies and responses that emerge in the real system. In social systems, especially, overlooking behavioral heterogeneity can indeed lead to misleading forecasts. Third, on a预测性的predictive水平上，SDT 的输出应与经验观察进行比较，以评估其预测能力。 这并不意味着在任何时候都完美对应，但双胞胎应该能够在已知条件下以合理的精度重现过去或正在进行的动态。level, the outputs of the SDT should be compared with empirical observations to evaluate its forecasting power. This does not imply perfect correspondence at all times, but the twin should be able to reproduce past or ongoing dynamics with reasonable accuracy under known conditions.

最后，更大的现实主义本质上并不是一种认知美德。虽然 SDT 等高保真模型可以提供更精细的洞察和预测，但它们也存在过度拟合所模拟系统特性的风险。 从这个意义上说，现实主义可以成为一把双刃剑，研究人员应该仔细权衡具体性和抽象性之间的权衡。 数字孪生和更抽象的 ABM 之间的选择应取决于研究目标：优先级是预测特定系统中的结果，还是发现适用于跨环境的通用机制。Finally, greater realism is not inherently an epistemic virtue. While high-fidelity models like SDTs allow more granular insights and predictions, they also risk overfitting to the peculiarities of the system they emulate. In this sense, realism can become a double-edged sword, and researchers should carefully weigh the trade-offs between specificity and abstraction. The choice between a digital twin and a more abstract ABM should depend on the research goals: whether the priority is to forecast outcomes in a particular system, or to uncover general mechanisms that apply across contexts.

Their预测性的predictive然而，使用必然更加有限：预测取决于数据质量、校准和行为假设，并且应被视为依赖于情景的预测，而不是精确的点预测。 过度解释是一种具体风险，尤其是当高保真输出造成准确性错觉时；表面的现实主义并不能保证认知的有效性。 因此，SDT 和 LLM 驱动代理的结果最好被理解为决策支持工具，为可能的未来进行推理，而不是作为明确的或与上下文无关的预测因子。use, however, is necessarily more limited: forecasts are conditional on data quality, calibration, and behavioral assumptions, and should be regarded as scenario-dependent projections rather than precise point predictions. Consequently, results from SDTs and LLM-driven agents are best understood as decision-support tools that inform reasoning about possible futures, not as definitive or context-independent predictors.
