链条的强度取决于其最薄弱的环节：人工智能系统集成审计的范围审查A Chain Is Only as Strong as Its Weakest Link: A Scoping Review of System Integration Audits in AI

Leah Davis1, Dominic Martin2, AJung Moon1
摘要Abstract

随着人工智能系统越来越多地集成到不同的接口和应用程序中，以模型为中心的审计不足以解决系统组件和部署环境之间的交互所产生的风险。长期以来，系统集成一直是航空航天等安全关键领域软件审计的核心。然而，它在人工智能审计中的作用仍未得到充分探索。通过扫描 4,259 份文件，我们对人工智能审计进行了范围审查，其中涉及As AI systems become increasingly integrated into diverse interfaces and applications, model-centric audits are insufficient to address risks arising from interactions among system components and deployment environments. System integration has long been central to software audits in safety-critical domains such as aerospace. However, its role in AI auditing remains underexplored. Scanning through 4,259 documents, we present a scoping review of AI audits that treat系统集成system integration作为评估的核心原则（n = 58）。使用反思性主题分析，我们分析其要素、参与者、推动因素和限制因素。我们发现该语料库代表了一种新兴但仍然分散的人工智能审计形式：很少有现有措施针对特定于集成的风险；在满足传统审计期望方面仍存在巨大差距；获取必要的信息和资源会极大地影响审计设计。尽管如此，集成可以分为三个站点（组件间、系统环境和多系统），每个站点都具有风险探索、风险确定、协调和程序规则性的功能。与其他类型的评估不同，这些审计评估特定于系统集成的质量，包括兼容性、完整性和监督。本次审查呼吁人工智能社区优先考虑系统集成作为解决人工智能风险的核心策略，并开发能够捕获组件级评估范围之外的跨组件、环境和系统故障的审计实践。as a core tenet of evaluation (n = 58). Using reflexive thematic analysis, we analyze their elements, actors, enablers, and constraints. We find that the corpus represents an emerging yet still fragmented form of AI auditing: few existing measures target integration-specific risks; large gaps remain in meeting traditional audit expectations; and access to necessary information and resources significantly influences audit design. Nonetheless, integration can be categorized across three sites (inter-component, system-environment, and multi-system), each serving the functions of risk exploration, risk determination, coordination, and procedural regularity. Deviating from other types of evaluations, these audits assess qualities specific to system integration, including compatibility, completeness, and oversight. This review calls on the AI community to prioritize system integration as a core strategy for addressing AI risk, and to develop audit practices capable of capturing failures across components, environments, and systems beyond the reach of component-level evaluation.

5 讨论Discussion

衡量系统的技术和负责任的人工智能性能是审计的核心目标也许并不奇怪。然而，我们还发现，特定于集成的质量，例如兼容性、完整性和监督，反映在审计的角色和四个职能中。在此基础上，我们现在阐述了系统集成审计制度化的三个要点。It is perhaps unsurprising that measuring the technical and responsible AI performance of a system is a core objective of the audits. 5.2 与传统审计预期的比较Comparison to Traditional Audit Expectations

鉴于我们的分析以审计为中心，审计比人工智能评估具有更高的保证期望，因此必须检查其合法性。在第 2.1 节中，我们确定了三个审核条件：独立审核员、系统地遵守标准或认证以及系统级风险的系统级评估。考虑到审查标准，第三个条件很容易满足；其他人则不然。Given that our analysis centres on audits, which carry higher assurance expectations than AI evaluations, their legitimacy must be examined. 5.4 研究局限性Study limitations

“系统集成”一词在人工智能审计界很少使用。为了弥补其缺失，我们使用更广泛的搜索标准来保留重要的贡献，同时缩小与系统集成相关的文档范围。由于查询面向学术机构和科学数据库，筛选过程可能无法充分代表行业、基层和社区工作。许多文件还引用了面向西方的法律、经合组织原则和欧盟人工智能法案，这些法律限制了全球南方部署的代表性。The term “system integration” is rarely used in the AI auditing community. To compensate for its absence, we used broader search criteria to keep important contributions, while narrowing the documents relevant to system integration. As queries were oriented to academic outlets and scientific databases, the screening process could under-represent industry, grassroots, and community work. Many documents also referenced Western-oriented laws, the OECD principles, and the EU AI Act, which constrain the representation of Global South deployments.

6 结论Conclusion

在过去的五年里，人工智能中出现了系统集成审计，涵盖管道元素、外部环境和多系统架构之间的交互。然而，这些审计揭示了谁承担责任以及如何获得保证的模糊性。鉴于语料库的经验证据有限，未来的工作应该使用设计民族志来调查实践者如何在实践中采用、参与和抵制这些结构。随着人工智能系统嵌入到新应用中，该领域必须优先考虑针对特定集成故障的专用方法。正如波音 737 MAX 8 事件所表明的那样，系统集成不是一个次要的问题；而是一个重要的问题。它可能是失败的关键根源。它需要针对组件、系统和参与者组合在一起时出现的风险进行审计。Over the last five years, system integration audits have emerged in AI, encompassing interactions among pipeline elements, external environments, and multi-system architectures. Yet these audits reveal ambiguity over who bears accountability and how assurance can be obtained. Given the corpus’s limited empirical evidence, future work should use design ethnography to investigate how practitioners adopt, engage with, and resist these structures in practice. As AI systems become embedded in new applications, the field must prioritize dedicated approaches for integration-specific faults. As the Boeing 737 MAX 8 incidents made clear, system integration is not a peripheral concern; it can be a critical source of failure. It requires audits designed for the risks that arise when components, systems, and actors are brought together.

附录CAppendix CAI系统要素描述Description of AI System Elements

我们最初确定了人工智能系统的四个核心阶段：数据/输入、模型、输出和用户交互。这些阶段按照 OECD 对人工智能系统的定义进行定义，并被演绎地包含在分析中。We initially identified four core stages of an AI system: data/input, model, output, and user-interaction. These stages, as defined by the OECD’s definition of an AI system, were deductively included in the analysis.

数据/输入：Data/input:在这些阶段中，87.9%的文章明确包含数据/输入阶段，包括数据集、数据源、数据表、数据字典、训练、测试和验证数据、元数据或来源数据、合成数据使用、数据结构以及输入提示和参数。此阶段还包括数据的特征，包括其格式、模式、属性、规模以及可用性和表示形式。还涵盖了其他领域，例如数据共享协议、数据存储和隐私，通常与数据保护法相关。这个阶段经常与其他阶段重叠，包括输出数据、生产数据、部署数据，甚至审计数据。围绕数据阶段的行动很广泛，包括数据收集和管理、注释和标签、预处理、导入和上传、维护、传输和传输、识别或去识别、共享、版本控制和更新、侵权、提取和一般处理。即时工程的许多方面也属于这个阶段。Among these stages, 87.9% of articles explicitly included the data/input stage, including datasets, data sources, data sheets, data dictionaries, training, testing, and validation data, metadata or provenance data, synthetic data use, data structures, and input prompts and parameters. This stage also comprises characteristics of the data, including its format, modalities, properties, scale, and availability and representation. Other areas, such as data sharing agreements, data storage, and privacy, were also covered, often related to data protection laws. This stage often overlapped with others, including output data, production data, deployment data, and even audit data. The actions surrounding the data stage were wide, including data collection and curation, annotation and labelling, pre-processing, importing and uploading, maintenance, transfer and transmission, identification or de-identification, sharing, versioning and updating, infringement, extraction, and general handling. Many aspects of prompt engineering also fell into this stage.

型号：Model:67.2%的文章明确讨论了模型阶段。此阶段的代码范围包括围绕模型权重、参数、特征、规范、决策阈值、代理、推论以及损失函数和目标函数的讨论。报告了各种模型类型和算法选择，以及更通用的设计、配置、决策结构和逻辑、架构、技术和权衡。除了模型创建（手动或自动）、选择、训练前和训练后、测试、欠拟合和过度拟合、微调、比较、传输、重用、提取、许可（例如版权和知识产权）、更新、版本控制和优化过程之外，还包括模型置信度、行为、约束、能力、限制、内部推理、推理、实现、校准和对齐。模型卡等工件以及模型插件、微服务、API 许可协议和模型周围的“外壳”等中介体也被标记。67.2% of articles explicitly discussed the model stage. The codes in this stage ranged from discussions surrounding model weights, parameters, features, specifications, decision thresholds, proxies, inferences, and both loss and objective functions. A variety of model types and algorithm choices were reported, along with more generalized designs, configurations, decision structures and logic, architectures, techniques, and trade-offs. Model confidence, behaviour, constraints, capabilities, limitations, internal reasoning, inference, realization, calibration, and alignment were also included, in addition to model creation (manual or automated), selection, pre- and post-training, testing, under- and over-fitting, fine-tuning, comparison, transmission, reuse, extraction, licensing (e.g., copyright and intellectual property), updating, versioning, and optimization processes. Artifacts such as model cards were also tagged, along with intermediaries such as model plug-ins, microservices, API licensing agreements, and the “housing” surrounding models.

输出：Output:在输出阶段，65.5% 的文章明确讨论了这一阶段，讨论了各种结果类型和格式、发现和证据，以最终支持进一步的决策。这种翻译有多种形式，包括指标、措施、阈值、目标、标准、目的、分数、绩效水平、干预点、标准、反事实、产出分布，以及更广泛的结果、确认和结论。此阶段还主要捕获反馈，包括预测、概率、潜在机制、条件、观察、响应、错误检查、影响以及全屏输出的使用。比较术语通常用于描述输出的相对定位，例如相似性、差异、依从性、响应变化、因果关系、相关性、聚合、“净效应”或测量过程的程度或影响。还使用了术语“声明验证”。输出中发生了各种动作，例如检测、揭示、解释、交流、量化或离散化、演示、展示、预测、产量，以及最终产生某种形式的产品的总结。使用各种工具来显示输出，包括仪表板和可视化。Within the output stage, which 65.5% of articles explicitly discussed, various result types and formats, findings, and evidence were discussed to ultimately support further decisions. This translation took many forms, including metrics, measures, thresholds, targets, standards, aims, scores, performance levels, intervention points, criteria, counterfactuals, output distributions, and more broadly, outcomes, confirmations, and conclusions. This stage also predominantly captured feedback, including predictions, probabilities, underlying mechanisms, conditions, observations, responses, error checks, implications, and the use of the output to full screen. Comparative terms were often used to describe the relative positioning of an output, such as similarities, differences, adherence, changes in response, causation, correlations, aggregations, “net effects,” or the extent or impact of the measurement process. The term “claim validation” was also used. Various actions took place within the output, such as detection, revealing, interpretation, communication, quantification or discretization, demonstrations, exhibitions, prognosis, yields, and ultimately, summarizations resulting in some form of a product. Various tools were used to display outputs, including dashboards and visualizations.

用户交互：User-interaction:48.3% 的文章提到了用户交互阶段。此阶段涉及用户参与，无论是主动实时应用还是被动牵连。它详细介绍了与审计相关的用户感知、行为、信念、反应、情绪、启发、偏好、愿望、期望、概况和特征，以及使用和知识工作流程。讨论了个人和集体用户行为。例如，一些审计通过用户主导的审计或联合审计来利用用户交互，其中用户自行或在人工智能系统的帮助下进行审计或生成审计数据。人类决策和人类与人工智能的协同也是突出的合作主题，包括人类脱离循环或人类指挥；还记录了关于拟人化的注释。关于信息收集和检索过程的对话，包括人类反馈和报告以及沟通；批准和交付渠道，无论是直接、有目的的还是间接的；用户体验如何从各种适应、定制、个性化、管理和优化方法（例如意图匹配）中受益也很常见。大多数讨论围绕用户披露、认知、曝光和洞察。其中一些是通过用户注册、入职、培训以及一般指导和指导来制定的。它还涉及用户界面之间的差异，包括网络、移动和物理应用程序（例如，具体化和机器人案例），以及用户如何呈现和随后摄取信息。48.3% of articles referenced the user-interaction stage. This stage concerns user involvement, whether actively applied in real time or passively implicated. It detailed user perceptions, behaviours, beliefs, reactions, sentiments, heuristics, preferences, desires, expectations, profiles, and characterizations relating to the audit, as well as usage and knowledge workflows. Both individual and collective user action were discussed. For instance, several audits leveraged user-interaction through user-led audits or co-audits, where users conducted an audit, or generated data for the audit, by themself or with assistance from an AI system. Human decision-making and human-AI synergy were also prominent collaboration themes, including human-out-of-the-loop or human-in-command; a note on anthropomorphism was also recorded. Conversations about information-gathering and retrieval processes, including human feedback and reporting, and communication; approval and delivery channels, whether direct and purposeful or indirect; and how user experience can benefit from various acclimation, customization, personalization, management, and optimization approaches, such as intent matching, were also common. Most of this discussion surrounded user disclosure, awareness, exposure, and insight. Some of this was enacted through user enrollment, onboarding, training, and general guidance and instruction. It also touched on differences among user interfaces, including web, mobile, and physical applications (e.g., embodied and robotic cases), and how information was presented and subsequently ingested by users.

还讨论了启用或允许用户采取特定操作的中介机构，例如 API 访问、包装器、微服务、仿真器或模拟器、各种输出模板、仪表板、视觉效果和显示，以及用户协议和合同。还讨论了网络流量监控、调查、评论、请求和历史记录等用户数据。注释、表达等动作词和浏览、切换、更新、上传或下载等常规计算活动都被记录下来。毫不奇怪，输出阶段之间存在重叠，因为大多数用户交互都集中在人工智能系统的输出上。这方面的明显例子包括以用户为中心的措施。Intermediaries that enabled or permitted users to take specific actions, such as API access, wrappers, microservices, emulators or simulators, various output templates, dashboards, visuals, and displays, as well as user agreements and contracts, were also addressed. User data, such as network traffic surveillance, surveys, comments, requests, and history, were also discussed. Action words such as interpretation, expression, and routine computing activities, such as browsing, toggling, updating, uploading, or downloading, were noted. Unsurprisingly, there was overlap between the output stage, given that most user-interaction centres on the AI system’s output. Clear examples of this included user-centred measures.

部署：Deployment:51.7% 的文章讨论的部署阶段是指人工智能系统安装、配置或发布到面向用户的环境后，持续、实时、主动地实施、操作、监控和维护。通常，此阶段被称为最终验证步骤。上下文契合性、适当性和可转移性被反复强调，以评估系统的行为在不同时间、设置和用户交互中是否按预期执行。豪尔等人。将部署描述为“需要检查嵌入决策的最终社会过程”The deployment stage, discussed in 51.7% of articles, refers to the continuous, real-time, and active implementation, operation, monitoring, and maintenance of an AI system, once it is installed, configured, or released into a user-facing environment. Often, this stage was referred to as the final verification step. Contextual fit, appropriateness, and transferability were repeatedly emphasized to assess whether a system’s behaviour performs as intended across different times, settings, and user-interactions. Hauer et al. describe deployment as “the final, social process in which the decision making is embedded needs to be checked”(Hauer et al. 2021)。由不断变化的数据、模型和用户交互引起的分布变化是核心，其次是对现实条件下可接受的评估偏差的讨论。因此，文章主要从回顾的角度讨论了部署阶段，详细说明了偏差变得太大时的补救、追索、恢复和回滚功能。还提到了对云计算等外部服务的依赖，以确保其可以部署到生产中。部署方法包括沙箱、上市后监测和分析、场景测试、人种学技术和可行性研究。. Distribution shifts arising from evolving data, models, and user interactions were central, followed by discussions of acceptable evaluation deviation under real-world conditions. As a result, articles primarily discussed the deployment stage in a retrospective sense, detailing remedy, recourse, restoration, and rollback functions if deviations became too significant. Dependencies on external services, such as cloud computing, were also mentioned to ensure it can be deployed to production. Deployment methods included sandboxing, post-market surveillance and analysis, scenario testing, ethnographic techniques, and feasibility studies.

生态系统：Ecosystem:我们还注意到一个更广泛的阶段，我们将其标记为生态系统。有 12 篇文章直接使用该术语来支持更加以人类和环境为中心的“生态系统观点”，例如 Groves 等人。 （2024 年，第 1115 页）。我们遵循 Barker 等人的定义，他们将生态系统描述为“ML 管道所在的社会技术领域”（2023 年，第 5 页）。这个阶段，或者说领域，是占主导地位的，反映在77.6%的文章中。讨论的范围从人工智能系统生态系统到审计生态系统，以及更多特定领域的生态系统（例如新闻或招聘生态系统）。这个阶段更加系统化，通常关注长期风险、危害以及与人工智能系统的生存能力、可持续性和寿命的大局相关的考虑因素。这次讨论包括复合影响和总结影响，以制定后续活动的路线图。通常，这会引发围绕人工智能系统的组织、管理和治理结构的讨论，以及标准和法律如何促进或阻碍程序规律性的讨论。权力、控制和访问动态被频繁提及，包括关系动态的子主题；信息和知识流动；更广泛地说，是负责任的人工智能文化。这些动态还包括对信息、知识和物质流的讨论，特别是围绕供应链及其参与者的讨论。因此，在此阶段确定了启用或阻止人工智能系统审计的因素，包括社会经济地位；成本、激励和市场结构；政治优先事项和气候；游说和众包努力；以及历史文化定位。人工智能系统如何定位（例如，谁的利益和代价）的问题是生态系统讨论的核心，包括所涉及的各种角色的假设、动机、意图和理由。民间理论被用来解释规范性价值决策和进一步的意义建构，成为许多生态系统领域的基础。We additionally note a broader stage, which we labelled as the ecosystem. Twelve articles directly used the term to position themselves in favour of a more human- and environment-centric “ecosystem view” such as Groves et al. (2024, p. 1115). We follow Barker et al.’s definition, who describe the ecosystem as “the socio-technical realm in which the ML pipeline lives” (2023, p. 5). This stage, or perhaps realm, was dominant, reflected in 77.6% of articles. The scope being discussed ranged from AI system ecosystems to auditing ecosystems, as well as more domain-specific ecosystems (e.g., news or hiring ecosystems). This stage was more systemic, often focusing on longer-term risks, harms, and considerations related to the bigger picture of an AI system’s viability, sustainability, and longevity. This discussion included compounding and summative impacts to produce a road map for subsequent activities. Oftentimes, this led to discussions of organizational, management, and governance structures surrounding the AI system, as well as of how standards and laws could promote or impede procedural regularity. Power, control, and access dynamics were frequently mentioned, including sub-themes of relational dynamics; information and knowledge flows; and, more generally, responsible AI culture. These dynamics also included discussions of information, knowledge, and material flows, especially surrounding supply chains and their actors. Factors enabling or blocking AI system audits were therefore identified in this stage, including socioeconomic status; cost, incentive, and market structures; political priorities and climates; lobbying and crowd-sourcing efforts; and historical and cultural positioning. The question of how the AI system was positioned (e.g., at whose benefit and expense) was central to the ecosystem discussion, including the assumptions, motives, intentions, and rationales of the various roles involved. Folk theories were used to explicate normative value decisions and further sense-making, acting as the foundation for many ecosystem spheres.
