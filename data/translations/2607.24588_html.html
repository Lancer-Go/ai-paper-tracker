However, expert-centered warning workflows are costly, labor-intensive, and difficult to scale throughout the warning-to-action process. 1. 简介Introduction

极端天气事件的频率、强度和复合影响日益增加，正在加剧人类社会、经济活动和生态系统面临的系统性风险The increasing frequency, intensity, and compound impacts of extreme weather events are amplifying systemic risks to human societies, economic activities, and ecosystems(Camps-Valls et al., 2025)。 为此，联合国 (UN) 和世界气象组织 (WMO) 已将早期预警系统 (EWS) 确定为减少灾害风险和气候适应的基石. In response, the United Nations (UN) and the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) have identified Early Warning Systems (EWS) as a cornerstone of disaster risk reduction and climate adaptation(United Nations, n.d.; World Meteorological Organization, n.d.)。 有效的 EWS 不仅仅是一个预测模块，而是一个集成了危险监测、事件定位、基于影响的风险评估、预警沟通和准备决策的操作性预警到行动链. An effective EWS is not merely a forecasting module but an operational warning-to-action chain that integrates hazard monitoring, event localization, impact-based risk assessment, warning communication, and preparedness decision-making(Reichstein et al., 2025)。 因此，其目标是超越以灾害为中心的预测，转向可操作、以影响为导向的预警，以便在危险天气发展成为严重灾害之前能够及时进行干预. Its objective is therefore to move beyond hazard-centered prediction toward actionable, impact-oriented warnings that enable timely interventions before hazardous weather develops into a severe disaster(De Perez et al., 2022).

实施这样的综合预警管道仍然具有挑战性。 在目前的实践中，极端天气预警在很大程度上依赖于领域专家来解释不断变化的大气条件，应用气象知识和先前的经验，协调异构数据源和分析工具，并将中间证据综合到预警和响应决策中Implementing such an integrated warning pipeline remains challenging. In current practice, extreme-weather warning relies heavily on domain experts to interpret evolving atmospheric conditions, apply meteorological knowledge and prior experience, coordinate heterogeneous data sources and analytical tools, and synthesize intermediate evidence into warning and response decisions(World Meteorological Organization, 2015; Potter et al., 2021)。 尽管有效，但这种以专家为中心的工作流程成本高昂、劳动力密集、难以扩展，并且容易在相互依赖的阶段中出现级联错误。 现有的软件系统和机器学习 (ML) 模型改进了检测、预测和诊断分析等各个组件，但通常只处理孤立的任务，需要人类专家来整合证据并管理端到端的警告过程。 LLM 的最新进展启用了数据驱动的 LLM 代理的新范式. Despite its effectiveness, this expert-centered workflow is costly, labor-intensive, difficult to scale, and vulnerable to cascading errors across interdependent stages. Existing software systems and machine learning (ML) models have improved individual components, such as detection, forecasting, and diagnostic analysis, but typically address isolated tasks, leaving human experts to integrate evidence and manage the end-to-end warning process. Recent advances in LLMs have enabled a new paradigm of data-driven LLM agents(Hong et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2026)，它可以自动编排多步骤推理、生成代码并使用科学工具将异构证据转化为有根据的分析。 这些进步促使我们将极端天气预警制定为一项代理任务，其中法学硕士代理人收集气象证据、推理灾害风险并支持运营决策。, which can automatically orchestrate multi-step reasoning, generate code, and use scientific tools to transform heterogeneous evidence into grounded analyses. These advances motivate us to formulate extreme-weather early warning as an agentic task in which LLM agents gather meteorological evidence, reason about hazard risks, and support operational decisions.

最近的研究调查了 LLM 代理在数据驱动的天气应用中的应用，包括极端天气诊断Recent studies have investigated LLM agents for data-driven weather applications, including extreme-weather diagnosis(Jiang et al., 2025; Tang et al., 2026)和自动化天气科学工作流程and automated weather-science workflows(Guo et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026; Varambally et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026)。 然而，这些代理主要关注早期任务，例如危害分析和预测，而很大程度上忽略了下游影响评估、预警通信和面向响应的决策支持。 因此，现有基准无法体现端到端极端天气预警的全部业务目标。 为了解决这个差距，我们构建. 2.2. 基准概述Benchmark Overview

2.2.1. 整体覆盖Overall Coverage

SIREN-Bench 重点关注 2021 年美国的极端天气事件。 它包含与 EWS 管道一致的 5 个极端天气分析类别的 600 个 QA 实例，涵盖 19 个子任务。 这些实例涵盖 12 个主要灾害类别，包括对流风暴、热带系统、龙卷风、洪水、冬季天气、大风事件、能见度灾害、高温、火灾、干旱、海洋灾害和寒冷天气事件。 它们还分布在一年中的所有月份，使基准能够捕获天气分析中的季节性变化。SIREN-Bench focuses on extreme-weather events in the U.S. in 2021. It contains 600 QA instances across five categories of extreme-weather analysis aligned with the EWS pipeline, covering 19 subtasks. These instances span 12 primary hazard families, including convective storms, tropical systems, tornadoes, floods, winter weather, high-wind events, visibility hazards, heat, fire, drought, marine hazards, and cold-weather events. They are also distributed across all months of the year, enabling the benchmark to capture seasonal variation in weather analysis.

2.3. 数据集构建Dataset Construction

2.3.2. 多源数据预处理和对齐Multi-source Data Preprocessing and Alignment

预处理管道首先过滤包含丢失或异常条目的事件记录，以获得原始数据的一致子集。 然后我们映射关键元数据（The preprocessing pipeline first filters event records containing missing or anomalous entries to obtain a consistent subset of the raw data. However, the spatiotemporal attributes of the same event often differ across sources. To address this issue, we design a soft alignment strategy that matches events of the same type while allowing controlled spatiotemporal tolerance. For example, two events are considered matched when the difference between their recorded timestamps is within a predefined threshold or when their recorded locations are in neighboring counties.

2.3.4. 质量控制Quality Control

为了确保基准质量，我们通过法学硕士和人类专家参与的三阶段流程审核 2021 年生成的实例。 首先，法学硕士根据预定义的标准审核实例：(1) 事件条件是否不完整或容易泄漏；(2) 真实答案或参考响应是否不可信、不明确或信息不足。 然后丢弃低质量的实例。 随后，为了防止频繁的灾害或灾害多发月份主导基准，我们对过滤后的实例进行重新采样，以促进极端事件类型和发生季节的多样性和平衡覆盖。 最后，专家手动检查其余实例，以验证它们是否合理且能够代表实际的极端天气场景。To ensure benchmark quality, we audit the generated instances from 2021 through a three-stage process involving LLMs and human experts. First, LLMs audit the instances according to predefined criteria: (1) whether the event conditions are incomplete or prone to leakage and (2) whether the ground-truth answers or reference responses are implausible, ambiguous, or insufficiently informative.
