通过大型语言模型的强化学习算法的进化发现Evolutionary Discovery of Reinforcement Learning Algorithms via Large Language Models

Alkis Sygkounas Machine Perception and Interaction Lab, Örebro UniversitySweden alkis.sygkounas@oru.se , Amy Loutfi Machine Perception and Interaction Lab, Örebro UniversitySweden amy.loutfi@oru.se and Andreas Persson Machine Perception and Interaction Lab, Örebro UniversitySweden andreas.persson@oru.se
摘要。Abstract.

强化学习算法由其学习更新规则定义，这些规则通常是手工设计和固定的。我们提出了一个进化框架，用于通过直接搜索实现完整训练过程的可执行更新规则来发现强化学习算法。该方法建立在 REvolve 的基础上，这是一个使用大型语言模型作为生成变异算子的进化系统，并将其从奖励函数发现扩展到算法发现。为了促进非标准学习规则的出现，搜索排除了规范机制，例如演员批评家结构、时间差异损失和价值引导。由于强化学习算法对内部标量参数高度敏感，因此我们引入了进化后细化阶段，其中大型语言模型为每个进化的更新规则提出可行的超参数范围。通过在多个 Gymnasium 基准上运行完整训练进行端到端评估，发现的算法相对于既定基准（包括 SAC、PPO、DQN 和 A2C）实现了具有竞争力的性能。Reinforcement learning algorithms are defined by their learning update rules, which are typically hand-designed and fixed. We present an evolutionary framework for discovering reinforcement learning algorithms by searching directly over executable update rules that implement complete training procedures. The approach builds on REvolve, an evolutionary system that uses large language models as generative variation operators, and extends it from reward-function discovery to algorithm discovery. To promote the emergence of nonstandard learning rules, the search excludes canonical mechanisms such as actor–critic structures, temporal-difference losses, and value bootstrapping. Because reinforcement learning algorithms are highly sensitive to internal scalar parameters, we introduce a post-evolution refinement stage in which a large language model proposes feasible hyperparameter ranges for each evolved update rule. Evaluated end-to-end by full training runs on multiple Gymnasium benchmarks, the discovered algorithms achieve competitive performance relative to established baselines, including SAC, PPO, DQN, and A2C.

1. 简介Introduction

在强化学习中，更新规则决定了经验如何转化为参数更新，从而直接塑造学习行为。虽然自动化发现已经应用于强化学习的某些方面，例如架构设计In reinforcement learning, the update rule determines how experience is transformed into parameter updates, which directly shape the learning behavior. Although automated discovery has been applied to some aspects of reinforcement learning, such as architecture design(Zoph and Le, 2017; Pham et al., 2018; Sato et al., 2021), 超参数调整, hyperparameter tuning(Paul et al., 2019; Xu et al., 2018; Eimer et al., 2023)和训练信号设计, and training-signal design(He et al., 2022; Zou et al., 2021)，更新规则通常保持固定。少数工作试图通过本质上探索更新规则的学习来进一步提升这一发现，或者通过优化可微更新函数, the update rule typically remains fixed. A small number of works have attempted to further elevate the discovery by exploring the learning of update rules intrinsically, either by optimizing differentiable update functions(Oh et al., 2020)，或者通过进化结构化损失表达式, or by evolving structured loss expressions(Co-Reyes et al., 2021)。然而，这些方法在学习规则的受限参数化表示上运行，例如可微更新网络或符号损失形式，因此不能生成表示为可执行训练逻辑的根本不同的更新规则。因此，更新规则仍然是强化学习的核心组成部分之一，但尚未成为完全自动化设计的主题。. However, these methods operate over restricted, parameterized representations of the learning rule, such as differentiable update networks or symbolic loss forms, and therefore cannot generate fundamentally different update rules expressed as executable training logic. As a result, the update rule remains one of the core components of reinforcement learning that has not yet been the subject of fully automated design.

搜索强化学习更新规则与基于梯度或其他本地引导的优化不兼容，因为搜索空间由实现完整训练过程的离散程序结构组成，而不是连续参数或可微更新模块。即使更新逻辑的微小变化也可能导致性质不同的学习动态，并且众所周知，强化学习算法对实现细节高度敏感Searching over reinforcement-learning update rules is incompatible with gradient-based or other locally guided optimization because the search space consists of discrete program structures implementing full training procedures, rather than continuous parameters or differentiable update modules. Even small changes to the update logic can induce qualitatively different learning dynamics, and reinforcement-learning algorithms are known to be highly sensitive to implementation details(Henderson et al., 2018; Engstrom et al., 2020)。稳定一个更新规则的超参数通常无法稳定另一个更新规则. Hyperparameters that stabilize one update rule often fail for another(Patterson et al., 2024)，通过小扰动阻止有效的局部细化。因此，每个更新规则必须被视为不同的算法，其质量只能通过完整的训练运行来评估。在这些条件下，基于群体的进化方法是一个自然的选择，因为它们使用端到端性能来比较候选算法，而不需要梯度或平滑的目标结构, preventing effective local refinement through small perturbations. As a result, each update rule must be treated as a distinct algorithm whose quality can only be assessed through complete training runs. Under these conditions, population-based evolutionary methods are a natural fit, since they compare candidate algorithms using end-to-end performance without requiring gradients or smooth objective structure(Jaderberg et al., 2017; Khadka and Tumer, 2018; Co-Reyes et al., 2021).

进化算法和遗传编程已应用于广泛的机器学习任务，包括神经网络进化、超参数优化、控制器综合和符号策略搜索Evolutionary algorithms and genetic programming have been applied to a wide range of machine-learning tasks, including neural network evolution, hyperparameter optimization, controller synthesis, and symbolic policy search(Franke et al., 2019; Jaderberg et al., 2017; Khadka and Tumer, 2018)。这些设置中的一个关键实际要求是能够生成语法上有效且可执行的后代，使进化算子能够探索搜索空间，而不会产生主要无效的变体. A key practical requirement in these settings is the ability to generate offspring that are both syntactically valid and executable, enabling evolutionary operators to explore the search space without producing predominantly invalid variants(Co-Reyes et al., 2021)。然而，强化学习更新规则违反了这一要求，因为它们是作为紧密耦合的训练过程而不是孤立的目标函数来实现的。因此，即使是很小的结构代码更改也可能会破坏组件之间的依赖关系或使所需的数据流无效，从而导致失败或未定义的训练行为. Reinforcement-learning update rules, however, violate this requirement because they are implemented as tightly coupled training procedures rather than isolated objective functions. As a result, even small structural code changes can break dependencies between components or invalidate required data flows, leading to failed or undefined training behavior(Henderson et al., 2018; Engstrom et al., 2020; Patterson et al., 2024)。因此，无引导的突变和交叉不太可能可靠地产生功能更新规则，这强调了对引导机制的需要，该机制提出连贯的、可执行的修改来完成训练逻辑。. Consequently, unguided mutation and crossover are unlikely to reliably produce functional update rules, underscoring the need for a guided mechanism that proposes coherent, executable modifications to complete training logic.

最近的工作表明，大型语言模型（LLM）可以充当进化管道中的此类提议机制，从而能够对复杂设计空间进行结构化探索。在强化学习中，这种方法已被用于通过生成奖励实现来优化奖励函数，其效用通过训练结果进行评估Recent work suggests that large language models (LLMs) can serve as such proposal mechanisms within evolutionary pipelines, enabling structured exploration of complex design spaces. In reinforcement learning, this approach has been used to optimize reward functions by generating reward implementations whose utility is assessed through training outcomes(Ma et al., 2024; Hazra et al., 2025)。还在遗传编程框架内探索了程序和代码进化的相关想法. Related ideas have also been explored for program and code evolution within genetic programming frameworks(Hemberg et al., 2024; Co-Reyes et al., 2021)，以及更广泛的进化计算，例如神经架构优化中基于群体和质量多样性的搜索, as well as for broader evolutionary computation, such as population-based and quality-diversity search in neural architecture optimization(Nasir et al., 2024)。然而，在这些工作中，进化搜索通常针对学习系统的各个组成部分，而强化学习更新规则本身仍然是固定的。. However, across these lines of work, evolutionary search typically targets individual components of learning systems, while the reinforcement-learning update rule itself remains fixed.

在这项工作中，我们研究了大型语言模型的进化搜索，通过直接搜索学习更新规则来发现新颖的强化学习算法。我们的方法建立在 REvolve 之上In this work, we investigate evolutionary search with large language models to discover novel reinforcement-learning algorithms by directly searching over learning update rules. Our approach builds on REvolve(Hazra et al., 2025)，它将奖励函数发现表述为使用岛屿种群的进化过程, which formulates reward-function discovery as an evolutionary process using island populations(Cantú-Paz and others, 1998)和基于语言模型的变体。我们将相同的进化结构应用于不同的搜索空间。然而，我们不是进化奖励函数，而是将学习更新规则进化为固定策略架构、优化器和训练配置下的可执行代码。在此设置中，每个候选更新规则定义了不同的强化学习算法。为了鼓励非规范学习规则的综合并评估是否可以出现真正的新更新规则，我们禁止使用标准机制，例如演员-评论家分解and language-model-based variation. We apply the same evolutionary structure to a different search space. However, instead of evolving reward functions, we evolve learning update rules as executable code under a fixed policy architecture, optimizer, and training configuration. In this setting, each candidate update rule defines a distinct reinforcement-learning algorithm. To encourage the synthesis of noncanonical learning rules and to assess whether genuinely new update rules can emerge, we prohibit the use of standard mechanisms such as actor–critic decomposition(Konda and Tsitsiklis, 1999)，引导价值目标, bootstrapped value targets(Sutton et al., 1999)，以及时差误差计算, and temporal-difference error computation(Sutton et al., 1999)。每个候选算法都是通过在一组固定环境上进行全面训练来评估的，并且选择基于汇总的经验性能。我们进一步扩展了 REvolve，将进化后超参数优化应用于性能最佳的算法，以评估固定训练预算下的鲁棒性并提高稳定性。所提出方法的概述如图所示. Each candidate algorithm is evaluated by full training on a fixed set of environments, and selection is based on aggregated empirical performance. We further extend REvolve with a post-evolution hyperparameter optimization applied to top-performing algorithms to evaluate robustness and improve stability under fixed training budgets. 2. 相关工作Related Work

有几项工作重点关注学习更新规则本身外部的强化学习系统组件的自动化。这包括强化学习代理的神经架构搜索Several lines of work have focused on automating components of reinforcement learning systems that are external to the learning update rule itself. This includes neural architecture search for reinforcement learning agents(Zoph and Le, 2017; Pham et al., 2018; Sato et al., 2021)，针对训练稳定性和样本效率的大规模超参数优化框架, large-scale hyperparameter optimization frameworks targeting training stability and sample efficiency(Jaderberg et al., 2017; Xu et al., 2018)，以及自动设计辅助目标或训练信号的方法, and methods that automate the design of auxiliary objectives or training signals(Jaderberg et al., 2016; He et al., 2022)。这些方法通过修改架构、超参数或训练信号来提高性能，而底层更新规则通常保持固定。. These approaches improve performance by modifying architectures, hyperparameters, or training signals, while the underlying update rule is typically held fixed.

进化和基于群体的方法提供了互补的工作线，通过在固定训练循环内操作策略、参数或架构来提高学习性能。这包括进化策略参数或网络结构的神经进化方法Evolutionary and population-based methods provide a complementary line of work that improves learning performance by operating over policies, parameters, or architectures within a fixed training loop. This includes neuro-evolution approaches that evolve policy parameters or network structures(Stanley and Miikkulainen, 2002; Real et al., 2019)，应用于政策优化的进化策略, evolutionary strategies applied to policy optimization(Salimans et al., 2017; Khadka and Tumer, 2018)，基于人群的训练方法，在学习过程中适应超参数, population-based training methods that adapt hyperparameters during learning(Jaderberg et al., 2017)，以及环境和课程的进化生成, and evolutionary generation of environments and curricula(Wang et al., 2019)。最近，大型语言模型已被纳入强化学习和相关领域的类似迭代优化流程中，包括生成或细化奖励规范和训练目标. More recently, large language models have been incorporated into similar iterative optimization pipelines for reinforcement learning and related domains, including generating or refining reward specifications and training objectives(Ma et al., 2024; Hazra et al., 2025)，并提出通过性能评估和选择的策略级代码或启发式方法, and proposing policy-level code or heuristics that are evaluated and selected through performance(Hemberg et al., 2024).

总的来说，现有方法探索了架构、超参数、奖励、环境和策略参数的自动化和进化优化。他们还引入了语言模型作为此类搜索过程中的生成器。但是，以受限形式学习或演化更新规则的工作除外Collectively, existing approaches have explored automated and evolutionary optimization of architectures, hyperparameters, rewards, environments, and policy parameters. They have additionally introduced language models as generators within such search processes. However, with the exception of work that learns or evolves update rules in restricted forms(Oh et al., 2020; Co-Reyes et al., 2021; Andrychowicz et al., 2016)，这些方法将强化学习更新规则本身视为固定的。相比之下，我们的工作目标是更新规则级别的算法发现，将可执行的学习逻辑视为进化搜索的对象。, these approaches treat the reinforcement learning update rule itself as fixed. In contrast, our work targets algorithm discovery at the level of the update rule, treating the executable learning logic as the object of evolutionary search.

6. 限制和未来的工作Limitation & Future Work

该框架的计算成本很高，因为每个候选更新规则必须通过跨多个环境和随机种子的全面强化学习训练来评估，这限制了进化搜索的规模。在当前设置中，所发现的算法源自现有强化学习机制的新颖重组，而不是源自全新的更新形式，反映了搜索空间的结构和用于生成候选规则的语言模型的表示限制。The framework is computationally expensive, since each candidate update rule must be evaluated through full reinforcement-learning training across multiple environments and random seeds, which restricts the scale of evolutionary search. In the current setting, the discovered algorithms arise from novel recombinations of existing reinforcement-learning mechanisms rather than from fundamentally new update forms, reflecting both the structure of the search space and the representational limits of the language model used to generate candidate rules.

因此，未来的工作将侧重于通过提高评估效率和替代适应度模型来扩展进化搜索。随着语言模型的改进，我们假设它们将生成更复杂和专门的更新规则，可能以任务结构为条件。一个重要的方向是探索规范强化学习组件与语言模型生成的新颖机制的更丰富的组合，并研究任务自适应算法发现。Future work will, therefore, focus on scaling evolutionary search by improving evaluation efficiency and surrogate fitness models. As language models improve, we hypothesize that they will generate more complex and specialized update rules, potentially conditioned on task structure. An important direction is to explore richer combinations of canonical reinforcement-learning components with novel mechanisms generated by language models, and to study task-adaptive algorithm discovery.

7. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个进化框架，用于通过直接搜索可执行更新规则来发现强化学习算法。在 REvolve 的基础上，候选训练程序由大型语言模型生成，并根据跨环境的经验性能进行选择，将优化从架构和超参数转移到学习程序本身。In this work, we presented an evolutionary framework for discovering reinforcement-learning algorithms by searching directly over executable update rules. Building on REvolve, candidate training procedures are generated by large language models and selected based on empirical performance across environments, shifting optimization from architectures and hyperparameters to the learning procedure itself.

进化的更新规则在不同的基准套件中实现了具有竞争力的性能，而不依赖于预定义的参与者批评家或时间差异结构。这些结果表明，更新规则空间是进化搜索的可行目标，并且语言模型可以作为算法代码的有效生成运算符，用于自动强化学习算法发现。The evolved update rules achieve competitive performance across a diverse benchmark suite without relying on predefined actor-critic or temporal-difference structures. These results demonstrate that update-rule space is a viable target for evolutionary search and that language models can serve as effective generative operators over algorithmic code for automated reinforcement-learning algorithm discovery.
