1 简介1 Introduction

摘要Abstract

我们的主要目标是在拥挤的场景中推进视频对象计数，旨在根据给定的文本或视觉提示对目标类别的所有实例进行稳健计数。现有方法依赖于 RGB 信息，限制了它们在拥挤和遮挡条件下的辨别能力。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了深度引导探测器（DG-Det）以及通用后处理流程。通过将深度线索与多尺度 RGB-D 交叉注意力和显式遮挡预测相结合，我们的方法增强了空间理解，并在拥挤和遮挡的场景中实现了稳健的检测。此外，我们引入了统一的重复数据删除框架来消除跨帧冗余计数。为了方便未来的研究，我们还发布了一个新的 RGB-D 视频对象计数数据集，具有深度信息和多个对象类别序列。大量实验表明，与现有基线相比，我们的方法实现了 MAE 降低 62.01%，并且 RMSE 也得到了一致的改进。我们在 https://github.com/streamer-AP/DG-Net 提供源代码，在 https://huggingface.co/datasets/aerospace123/RGBDVideoCount 提供数据集。Our primary objective is to advance video object counting in crowded scenes, aiming to robustly count all instances of a target category based on given text or visual prompts. Existing methods rely on RGB information, limiting their discriminative ability in crowded and occluded conditions. To address this, we propose a Depth-Guided Detector (DG-Det) along with a general post-processing pipeline. By integrating depth cues with multi-scale RGB-D cross-attention and explicit occlusion prediction, our method enhances spatial understanding and achieves robust detection in crowded and occluded scenes. Furthermore, we introduce a unified de-duplication framework to eliminate cross-frame redundant counting. To facilitate future research, we also release a new RGB-D Video Object Counting dataset featuring depth information and multiple object categories persequence. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our method achieves a 62.01% reduction in MAE compared to existing baselines, and also produces consistent improvements in RMSE. We provide the source code at https://github.com/streamer-AP/DG-Net and the dataset at https://huggingface.co/datasets/aerospace123/RGBDVideoCount.

我们专注于在拥挤场景中对视频对象进行计数的任务，其目标是使用文本或视觉提示枚举视频中指定目标类别的所有实例。然而，对拥挤和遮挡场景中视频计数的研究仍然非常有限。在货架库存检查和仓库检查等实际应用中，动态视频流的准确计数是自动化库存管理和缺货警告的关键先决条件，具有很大的实用价值。受此启发，我们专注于真实拥挤和遮挡物体场景中的视频计数，并首次对拥挤场景中的视频个体计数进行了研究。与现有视频任务中的常见目标（例如行人和车辆）不同，多类别遮挡场景中的同类别对象通常在包装、纹理和尺度上高度相似。同时，这些场景涉及密集的布置、频繁的遮挡和视点的变化。由于 RGB 主要捕获颜色和纹理等外观线索，因此仅依靠单帧内的 RGB 特征很难有效分离紧密相邻且相互遮挡的对象。在帧之间，视点变化和短期遮挡进一步扰乱了外观表示的稳定性，使得实例匹配更容易出现混乱。因此，现有的主要依赖于 RGB 信息的检测和关联策略在遮挡场景中面临明显的局限性（见图 1）。因此，该任务的核心挑战不仅在于视频层面的跨帧关联和去重，还在于准确地区分每帧内外观高度模糊的相邻对象。We focus on the task of video object counting in crowded scenes, where the goal is to enumerate all instances of a specified target category within a video using either text or visual prompts. However, research on video counting in crowded and occluded scenes remains very limited. In real-world applications such as shelf inventory checks and warehouse inspections, accurate counting from dynamic video streams is a key prerequisite for automated inventory management and out-of-stock warnings, which have substantial practical value. Motivated by this, we focus on video counting in real crowded and occluded object scenes, and conduct the first research on video individual counting in crowded scenes. Unlike common targets in existing video tasks, such as pedestrians and vehicles, same-category objects in multi-category occluded scenes are often highly similar in packaging, texture, and scale. At the same time, these scenes involve dense arrangements, frequent occlusions, and viewpoint changes. Since RGB mainly captures appearance cues such as color and texture, relying solely on RGB features within a single frame makes it difficult to effectively separate tightly adjacent and mutually occluded objects. Across frames, viewpoint variation and short-term occlusion further disturb the stability of appearance representations, making instance matching more prone to confusion. As a result, existing detection-and-association strategies that mainly rely on RGB information face clear limitations in occluded scenes(see Fig. 1). Therefore, the core challenge of this task lies not only in cross-frame association and deduplication at the video level, but also in accurately distinguishing neighboring objects with highly ambiguous appearances within each frame.

为了克服RGB特征在拥挤和遮挡场景中的局限性，引入深度信息来提供前后关系和相对距离是一种有效的解决方案。对于紧密排列或相互遮挡的同一类别的物体，它们在3D空间中仍然表现出可区分的深度差异和空间层次。这些信息不仅可以减轻单个帧内相邻对象之间的遮挡，而且还可以提供对跨帧的外观变化不太敏感的几何约束。然而，引入深度并不会自动带来一致的收益；关键在于如何有效融合。 RGB 和深度特征的直接通道串联，如图 1（b）所示，忽略了两种模态之间的分布间隙，因此导致许多漏检。传统的早期融合或后期融合策略也经常遭受特征交互不足的困扰。简单的融合方法通常无法在两种模态之间建立有效的互补性，并且无法强调关键区域中真正有用的几何信息。因此，要使对象级视频计数真正受益于深度，关键不在于简单地引入深度，而在于设计一种有效的融合机制，能够更好地处理遮挡、外观相似性和多尺度几何变化。To overcome the limitations of RGB features in crowded and occluded scenes, introducing depth information to provide front-back relations and relative distance is an effective solution. For tightly arranged or mutually occluded objects of the same category, they still exhibit distinguishable depth differences and spatial hierarchy in 3D space. Such information can not only alleviate occlusion among adjacent objects within a single frame, but also provide geometric constraints that are less sensitive to appearance variation across frames. However, introducing depth does not automatically lead to consistent gains; the key lies in how to fuse it effectively. Direct channel concatenation of RGB and depth features, as shown in Fig. 1(b), ignores the distribution gap between the two modalities and therefore leads to many missed detections. Conventional earlyfusion or late-fusion strategies also often suffer from insufficient feature interaction. Simple fusion methods usually fail to establish effective complementarity between the two modalities and cannot emphasize truly useful geometric information in critical regions. Therefore, for object-level video counting to truly benefit from depth, the key lies not in simply introducing depth, but in designing an effective fusion mechanism that can better handle occlusion, appearance similarity, and multi-scale geometric variation.

2 相关工作2 Related Work

本节回顾了从静态图像到视频的对象计数的发展，并进一步讨论了深度信息在检测和跟踪框架中的作用。This section reviews the development of object counting from static images to videos, and further discusses the role of depth information in detection and tracking frameworks.

现有的图像计数方法可大致分为两类：密度图回归方法[4,6,15,24]和基于检测的实例计数方法[5,9,22,23,25]。前者在高度拥挤和同质的场景中表现良好，但类别通常隐式编码在网络参数中，这限制了它们对不可见类别的泛化并阻碍了实例级本地化[10, 28]。后者提供了明确的本地化，但大多数依赖于预定义的类别，并且在拥挤的场景中通常会降级 [16, 31]。为了克服封闭类别的限制，开放世界计数受到越来越多的关注。少镜头计数使用视觉样本进行匹配，但它对样本质量和外观变化敏感[11,20,29]。零样本计数利用了视觉语言模型的语义迁移能力，但它仍然很容易受到细粒度目标上的语义歧义的影响[1,7,14]。最近的工作通过密度估计（Lin 等人[19]）或基于检测的框架（CountGD [2]）统一了开放世界计数的多模式提示。视频领域的相关研究仍然有限。作为一种代表性方法，CountVID [3]主要通过提示分割和伪轨迹过滤来增强时间计数的唯一性。然而，在现实世界的遮挡场景中，对象之间的高度视觉相似性和严重的空间重叠使得这种方法极易出现漏检和跟踪中断的情况。与以前的视频对象计数方法不同，我们的方法创新地结合了深度，以减轻依赖于模型的模型所面临的严重遮挡挑战Existing image counting methods can be broadly divided into two categories: density map regression methods [4, 6, 15, 24] and detection-based instance counting methods [5, 9, 22, 23, 25]. The former perform well in highly crowded and homogeneous scenes, but the category is usually implicitly encoded in the network parameters, which limits their generalization to unseen categories and prevents instance-level localization [10, 28]. The latter provides explicit localization, but most of them rely on predefined categories and often degrade in crowded scenes [16, 31]. To overcome the limitation of closed-set categories, open-world counting has received increasing attention. Few-shot counting uses visual exemplars for matching, but it is sensitive to exemplar quality and appearance variation [11, 20, 29]. Zero-shot counting leverages the semantic transfer ability of vision-language models, but it is still easily affected by semantic ambiguity on fine-grained targets [1, 7, 14]. Recent works unify multi-modal prompts for open-world counting, either through density estimation (Lin et al. [19]) or detection-based frameworks (CountGD [2]). Related research in the video domain remains limited. As a representative method, CountVID [3] primarily enhances temporal counting uniqueness via promptable segmentation and pseudo-track filtering. However, in real-world occluded scenes, the high visual similarity and severe spatial overlap among objects make this approach highly susceptible to missed detections and track interruptions. MonoDETR[34] further reformulates the Transformer into a depth-aware structure, allowing object queries to interact with scene depth and thus reducing reliance on local appearance cues. Unlike existing depth-aware detectors dedicated to 3D pose regression or salient object extraction, our depth-guided detector not only uses depth features to separate overlapping targets within a single frame, but also explicitly predicts occlusion to provide crucial cues for cross-frame trajectory de-duplication,making it highly suitable for the VIC task in real-world occluded scenes

在视频任务中，最近的研究已经开始在多目标跟踪的关联成本、轨迹细化和遮挡推理的设计中使用深度，以提高复杂场景中的时间稳定性。例如，DepthMOT[30]使用深度线索和相机姿态估计来减少由遮挡和相机运动引起的 ID 切换。 DepTR-MOT[8]将实例级深度监督和深度一致性引入到跟踪检测框架中，从而提高了遮挡和近距离交互下的轨迹鲁棒性。虽然MOT和我们的方法都使用深度来减少遮挡，但MOT侧重于现实复杂场景中的长期ID跟踪，而视频计数侧重于跨帧去重。因此，我们的方法适合这项任务，该方法在跨帧关联期间有效利用深度信息，并通过遮挡自适应时间投票动态评估轨迹有效性。In video tasks, recent studies have started to use depth in the design of association costs, trajectory refinement, and occlusion reasoning for multi-object tracking, in order to improve temporal stability in complex scenes. For example, DepthMOT[30]uses depth cues and camera pose estimation to reduce ID switches caused by occlusion and camera motion. DepTR-MOT[8] introduces instancelevel depth supervision and depth consistency into a tracking-bydetection framework, thereby improving trajectory robustness under occlusion and close-range interactions.Although both MOT and our method use depth to reduce occlusion, MOT focuses on long-term ID tracking in real-world complex scenes, whereas video counting focuses on cross-frame de-duplication. Therefore, our approach, which effectively utilizes depth information during crossframe association and dynamically evaluates trajectory validity via Occlusion-Adaptive Temporal Voting, is suited for this task.

6 结论6 Conclusion

我们研究拥挤场景中视频个体计数的深度信息。与主要依赖时间重复数据删除的基于 RGB 的方法不同，我们的方法引入了每帧检测和视频级推理的深度约束。深度引导融合和遮挡感知预测减少了漏检和重复计数，而稳定的深度线索则提高了跨帧关联和视频级计数准确性。We investigate depth information for video individual counting in crowded scenes. Unlike RGB-based methods that mainly rely on temporal deduplication, our approach introduces depth constraints for per-frame detection and video-level inference. Depth-guided fusion and occlusion-aware prediction reduce missed detections and duplicate counting, while stable depth cues improve cross-frame association and video-level counting accuracy.

我们通过采用深度增强检测器 YOLOv8 RGB-D 作为附加基线来进一步补充我们的比较。具体来说，YOLOv8 RGB-D用作检测前端，并与主要实验中相同的下游跟踪和计数管道集成，并在相同的数据分割和指标下进行评估。结果（见表6）表明，在检测阶段引入深度信息后，性能略好于RGB检测器获得的性能，这表明深度线索可以为密集排列和遮挡场景中的目标定位提供辅助优势。尽管如此，我们提出的模型仍然实现了最佳的整体性能。这表明视频实例计数的改进不仅仅依赖于扩展检测器输入模态，还依赖于相邻相似实例的有效区分，以及准确的跨帧关联和重复数据删除。We further supplement our comparisons by adopting the depthenhanced detector YOLOv8 RGB-D as an additional baseline. Specifically, YOLOv8 RGB-D is used as the detection front-end and integrated with the same downstream tracking and counting pipeline as in the main experiments, with evaluation conducted under the same data split and metrics. The results(see Tab 6) show that, after introducing depth information at the detection stage, the performance is slightly better than that obtained with the RGB detector, indicating that depth cues can provide auxiliary benefits for target localization in densely arranged and occluded scenes. Nevertheless, our proposed model still achieves the best overall performance. This suggests that improvements in video instance counting do not solely rely on expanding the detector input modality, but also depend on the effective discrimination of adjacent similar instances, as well as accurate cross-frame association and deduplication.

F 故障场景分析F Failure Scenes analysis

如图7所示，在有玻璃门的饮料场景中，玻璃表面引入反射、折射和局部高光，导致深度传感器获取的深度值出现噪声、区域缺失或结构扭曲。这个问题在柜门边界、反光玻璃区域以及密集排列的瓶罐周围尤为明显，原始深度图往往无法准确表示物体的真实空间层次，从而影响前景目标和背景结构之间的有效分离。在这种场景下，如果模型直接依赖于原始深度信息，很容易受到异常深度响应的干扰，从而进一步导致目标定位不稳定、相邻实例之间的边界模糊，甚至漏检或重复计数。As shown in the figure 7, in beverage scenes with glass doors, the glass surface introduces reflections, refractions, and local highlights, which cause depth values acquired by the depth sensor to exhibit noise, missing regions, or structural distortions. This issue is particularly pronounced around the cabinet door boundaries, reflective glass regions, and densely arranged bottles and cans, where the raw depth map often fails to accurately represent the true spatial hierarchy of the objects, thereby affecting the effective separation between foreground targets and background structures. In such scenarios, if the model directly relies on raw depth information, it is easily disturbed by abnormal depth responses, which further leads to unstable target localization, ambiguous boundaries between adjacent instances, and even missed detections or duplicate counting.

同时，预测的深度信息在此类场景中也表现不佳。虽然预测的深度图可以在某些区域保持相对平滑的全局结构，但玻璃的反射、透明遮挡和复杂的照明Meanwhile, the predicted depth information also performs poorly in this type of scene.
