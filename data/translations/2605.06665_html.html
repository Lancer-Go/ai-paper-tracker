This convention couples depth scaling with linear expert-parameter growth and assumes that every layer needs isolated expert capacity. 1 简介Introduction

专家混合 (MoE) 模型已成为扩展大型语言模型 (LLM) 的主流技术，可实现参数大幅增长，同时保持每个令牌计算几乎恒定Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models have become a mainstream technique for scaling large language models (LLMs), enabling substantial parameter growth while maintaining nearly constant per-token computation[13, 26, 18, 9]。 传统的 MoE 设计遵循严格的专家预算分配规则：每个变压器层拥有自己的一组专家 FFN，并且特定于层的路由器为每个令牌选择这些私人专家的稀疏子集。 这种设计广泛应用于最先进的 MoE 系统. Conventional MoE design follows a rigid expert-budget allocation rule: each transformer layer owns its own set of expert FFNs, and a layer-specific router selects a sparse subset of those private experts for each token. This design, widely adopted in state-of-the-art MoE systems[14, 6, 7, 5]，硬编码变压器深度和总专家参数之间的线性关系：添加层必然会分配新的私有专家容量。, hard-codes a linear relationship between transformer depth and total expert parameters: adding layers necessarily allocates new private expert capacity.
