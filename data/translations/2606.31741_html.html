STEB：样式文本嵌入基准STEB: Style Text Embedding Benchmark

Rafael Rivera Soto Johns Hopkins University Anna Wegmann Utrecht University Cristina Aggazzotti Johns Hopkins University
摘要Abstract

虽然语义嵌入是在 在大规模文本嵌入基准中，样式嵌入的评估仍然分散，每项工作都依赖于自己的一组任务和数据集。 为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了样式文本嵌入基准，这是一个全面的开源基准，旨在标准化样式嵌入的评估。While semantic embeddings are rigorously evaluated on the Massive Text Embedding Benchmark, the evaluation of style embeddings remains fragmented, with each work relying on their own set of tasks and datasets. To bridge this gap, we introduce the Style Text Embedding Benchmark, a comprehensive open-source benchmark intended to standardize the evaluation of style embeddings.STEB包含encompasses9696跨数据集datasets across77语言，涵盖作者身份验证、作者检索、人工智能文本检测、语言特征探测等应用。 我们发现语义嵌入在风格任务中始终失败，并且没有一种风格嵌入在所有评估的任务中普遍优于。 我们开源了languages, spanning applications such as authorship verification, authorship retrieval, AI-text detection, probing of linguistic features, and others. We find that semantic embeddings consistently fail in stylistic tasks, and that there is no style embedding that is universally superior across all tasks evaluated. 虽然许多方法会比较特定 n 元语法的频率，但仅仅计算它们在文档中的存在并不能揭示更广泛的上下文信息，例如 n 元语法通常有多常见。因此，可以使用 n-gram 的术语频率-逆文档频率 (TF-IDF) 进行加权，该术语考虑了 n-gram 在特定文档中出现的频率与其在整个语料库中的罕见程度。这种测量增加了稀有词的权重，这可能是作者的区别，并减少了常见词的权重。 PAN 竞赛的作者身份验证基线While many approaches compare the frequencies of particular n-grams, simply counting their presence in documents does not reveal broader contextual information, such as how common the n-gram is in general.
