Semantic consistency is assessed via an LLM-based judge (CriticLean-Qwen3-14B), and judgments remain sensitive to model versions and prompting details. We therefore report judge configurations and analyze judge/prover mismatch cases; under our deployed setup, the judge reaches 79.0% and 81.0% accuracy on CONSISTENCYCHECK and CRITICLEANBENCH, respectively (Appendix C.5.7).
