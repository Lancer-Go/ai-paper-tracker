摘要Abstract

强化学习 (RL) 已成为训练后和调整大型语言模型 (LLM) 的标准范式，但最近的证据表明它面临着持续的“能力上限”：与发现新策略的经典 RL 系统不同，LLM 的 RL 通常只是对预训练权重中已经隐藏的模式进行细化。在这项工作中，我们确定了一个基本的结构瓶颈：虽然经典强化学习依赖于紧凑、信息丰富的马尔可夫状态，但当前的法学硕士训练后公式与不断扩展的行动历史有关。Reinforcement learning (RL) has become a standard paradigm for post-training and aligning Large Language Models (LLMs), yet recent evidence suggests it faces a persistent “capability ceiling”: unlike classical RL systems that discover novel strategies, RL for LLMs often acts as a mere refiner of patterns already latent in pre-trained weights. In this work, we identify a fundamental structural bottleneck: while classical RL relies on compact, informative Markov states, current LLM post-training formulations are tethered to an ever-expanding history of actions.

我们重新审视了一个长期以来一直是强化学习的核心但在法学硕士训练后中却没有的经典原则：显式马尔可夫状态。理论上，我们提供严格的保证，证明利用估计的马尔可夫状态可以显着降低样本复杂性。根据经验，我们表明，引入马尔可夫状态始终打破了一系列复杂逻辑难题中标准 RL 后训练的性能界限。我们的研究结果表明，超越“历史即状态”模型，转而采用结构化马尔可夫表示，对于解锁生成人工智能中的开放式发现和真正的新推理能力至关重要。We revisit a classical principle long central to RL yet absent from LLM post-training: explicit Markov states. Theoretically, we provide rigorous guarantees demonstrating that leveraging estimated Markov states can significantly reduce sample complexity. Empirically, we show that introducing Markov states consistently breaks the performance boundaries of standard RL post-training across a suite of complex logic puzzles. Our findings suggest that moving beyond “history-as-state” modeling in favor of structured Markovian representations is essential for unlocking open-ended discovery and genuinely new reasoning capabilities in Generative AI.

1 简介Introduction

强化学习 (RL) 已成为训练后和调整大型语言模型 (LLM) 的权威范式，可在复杂推理、数学问题解决和代理行为方面取得突破Reinforcement learning (RL) has emerged as the definitive paradigm for post-training and aligning Large Language Models (LLMs), enabling breakthroughs in complex reasoning, mathematical problem-solving, and agentic behavior(Jaech et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025)。通过从静态监督微调转向动态环境交互，强化学习允许模型探索广阔的解决方案空间。在流行的 RL 训练后范例中，LLM 被表述为代理，其中操作空间由离散标记组成，状态由所有先前操作的串联定义. By shifting from static supervised fine-tuning to dynamic environment interaction, RL allows models to explore vast solution spaces. In the prevailing RL post-training paradigm, LLMs are formulated as agents where the action space consists of discrete tokens and the state is defined by the concatenation of all preceding actions(Guo et al., 2025).

尽管取得了这些成功，但越来越多的证据表明，强化学习主要是作为一种机制，在基本模型已经可达的区域内加强搜索，而不是从根本上扩展其解决方案空间Despite these successes, growing evidence suggests that RL primarily functions as a mechanism for sharpening search within regions already reachable by the base model, rather than fundamentally expanding its solution space(Yue et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a; Shao et al., 2025; Yeo et al., 2025)。虽然一些当代研究声称强化学习可以激发新的能力，但这些收益通常表现为预训练边界的适度扩展，或者取决于密集的奖励塑造或专门的特定领域设计. While some contemporary studies claim that RL can elicit novel capabilities, these gains typically manifest as modest extensions of the pre-training boundary or are contingent upon dense reward shaping or specialized domain-specific designs(Zhang et al., 2025; Sun et al., 2025; Yuan et al., 2025a). 关于 RL 训练后的争论Debate on RL Post-Training

关于强化学习是否能够赋予模型从根本上超越预训练期间获得的推理能力的争论已经展开。越来越多的工作表明，强化学习主要是细化、重新加权或有选择地放大基本模型中已经隐藏的推理模式，而不是引入真正新颖的功能An active debate has emerged regarding whether RL can endow models with reasoning capabilities that fundamentally exceed those acquired during pre-training. A growing body of work suggests that RL primarily refines, reweights, or selectively amplifies reasoning patterns already latent in the base model, rather than inducing genuinely novel capabilities[Shao et al., 2024, Yue et al., 2025, Wu et al., 2025a, Shao et al., 2025, Yeo et al., 2025]。最近的自我改进方法进一步强化了这种观点，这些方法完全消除了环境相互作用或外部奖励信号，但仍取得了可比较的收益. This view is further reinforced by recent self-improvement methods that eliminate environment interaction or external reward signals altogether, yet achieve comparable gains[Prabhudesai et al., 2025, Zuo et al., 2025]，表明改进通常来自概率质量的内部重新分配，而不是探索性发现。, indicating that improvement often arises from internal redistribution of probability mass rather than exploratory discovery.

相比之下，报告强化学习下的新兴能力的作品通常依赖于限制性的训练设计，例如先决领域知识In contrast, works reporting emergent capabilities under RL typically depend on restrictive training designs, such as prerequisite domain knowledge[Yuan et al., 2025a]，精心策划的任务难度, carefully curated task difficulty[Zhang et al., 2025]，或明确设计的热身阶段和部分奖励塑造, or explicitly designed warm-up phases and partial reward shaping[Sun et al., 2025]。这些机制极大地限制了优化景观并引导学习走向已知的解决方案流形，这表明观察到的收益反映了狭窄假设空间内的受控外推，而不是发现了根本上新的推理轨迹。. These mechanisms substantially constrain the optimization landscape and guide learning toward known solution manifolds, suggesting that the observed gains reflect controlled extrapolation within a narrow hypothesis space rather than the discovery of fundamentally new reasoning trajectories.

此外，Furthermore,Samineni et al. [2025]对强化学习训练后盛行的“历史即国家”表述进行了批判性的重新评估。将理论论证与经验证据相结合，他们表明主流强化学习后训练方法实际上等同于结果条件监督学习，这意味着在这种表述下，强化学习并没有显着超过监督学习的表征或优化能力。offers a critical reassessment of the prevailing “history-as-state” formulation in RL post-training. Combining theoretical arguments with empirical evidence, they show that mainstream RL post-training methods are effectively equivalent to outcome-conditioned supervised learning, implying that—under this formulation—RL has not meaningfully exceeded the representational or optimization capabilities of supervised learning.

上下文管理Context Management

越来越多的工作通过压缩减少传递的信息量来解决上下文长度爆炸的问题A growing body of work addresses context-length explosion by reducing the amount of information carried forward, either through compression[Li et al., 2023, 2025]或通过外部记忆机制or by external memory mechanisms[Packer et al., 2023, Chhikara et al., 2025, Xu et al., 2025, Zhong et al., 2024]。这些方法通常会丢弃不相关的细节，并将显着信息压缩成紧凑的摘要，以便在长上下文中运行。在代理人工智能中，相关的上下文管理技术旨在防止代理与其环境之间的交互历史无限制增长，从而减轻上下文限制并提高长期训练的稳定性. These methods typically discard irrelevant details and condense salient information into compact summaries to operate over long contexts.
