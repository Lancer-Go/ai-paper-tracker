摘要Abstract

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 具有显着先进的人工智能驱动的复杂医学成像解释和报告，例如计算机断层扫描 (CT)。然而，现有的方法在很大程度上使临床医生成为最终输出的被动观察者，没有为他们提供可解释的推理痕迹来检查、验证或完善。为了解决这个问题，我们引入Vision-language models (VLM) have markedly advanced AI-driven interpretation and reporting of complex medical imaging, such as computed tomography (CT). Yet, existing methods largely relegate clinicians to passive observers of final outputs, offering no interpretable reasoning trace for them to inspect, validate, or refine. To address this, we introduce辐射剂RadAgent，一种使用工具的人工智能代理，通过逐步且可解释的过程生成 CT 报告。每份生成的报告都附有中间决策和工具交互的完全可检查的痕迹，使临床医生能够检查报告的结果是如何得出的。在我们的实验中，我们观察到, a tool-using AI agent that generates CT reports through a stepwise and interpretable process. Each resulting report is accompanied by a fully inspectable trace of intermediate decisions and tool interactions, allowing clinicians to examine how the reported findings are derived. In our experiments, we observe that辐射剂RadAgent与 3D VLM 对应项 CT-Chat 相比，在三个维度上改进了胸部 CT 报告的生成。宏观 F1 中的临床准确性提高了 6.0 点（相对 36.4%），微观 F1 中的临床准确性提高了 5.4 点（相对 19.6%）。对抗条件下的稳健性提高了 24.7 个百分点（相对 41.9%）。此外，improves Chest CT report generation over its 3D VLM counterpart, CT-Chat, across three dimensions. Clinical accuracy improves by 6.0 points (36.4% relative) in macro-F1 and 5.4 points (19.6% relative) in micro-F1. Robustness under adversarial conditions improves by 24.7 points (41.9% relative). Furthermore,辐射剂RadAgent忠实度达到 37.0%，这是 3D VLM 同类产品中完全不具备的新功能。通过将胸部 CT 的解释构建为明确的、工具增强的和迭代的推理轨迹，achieves 37.0% in faithfulness, a new capability entirely absent in its 3D VLM counterpart. By structuring the interpretation of chest CT as an explicit, tool-augmented and iterative reasoning trace,辐射剂RadAgent让我们更接近透明、可靠的放射学人工智能。brings us closer toward transparent and reliable AI for radiology.

简介Introduction

尽管在报告生成和相关任务方面表现强劲，但最近的 3D 视觉语言模型 (VLM)Despite strong performance in report generation and related tasks, recent 3D vision language models (VLM)Bai et al. 2024; Hamamci et al. 2026; Wu et al. 2025; Shui et al. 2025; Blankemeier et al. 2026仍然在很大程度上产生最终报告，但没有透露如何识别报告的发现、支持这些发现的证据或如何将中间观察结果纳入最终结论。 CT 报告尤其需要大量劳动力，因为临床医生必须逐层解释 3D 数据，因此对自动化产生了强烈需求。然而，这也是一项高风险的任务，临床医生必须能够检查和验证系统达到输出的过程。因此，生成报告而不暴露其推理的模型仍然是透明度和可信度有限的黑匣子。still largely produce final reports without revealing how the reported findings were identified, what evidence supported them, or how intermediate observations were integrated into the final conclusion. CT reporting is particularly labor-intensive because clinicians must interpret 3D data slice-by-slice, creating a strong need for automation. However, it is also a high stakes task in which clinicians must be able to inspect and validate the process by which a system arrives at its output. Thus, models generating reports without exposing their reasoning remain blackboxes of limited transparency and trustworthiness.

为了解决这个问题，最近的医疗代理系统试图通过利用大语言 (LLM) 和 VLM 与外部工具交互的功能来模拟放射工作流程固有的多步骤和迭代推理过程Addressing this issue, recent medical agentic systems seek to emulate the inherently multi-step and iterative reasoning process of radiological workflows by leveraging the capabilities of large language (LLM) and VLMs to interact with external toolsYao et al. 2022; Fallahpour et al. 2025。对于生成 CT 报告的用例，CT-Agent. For the use case of CT report generation, CT-AgentMao et al. 2025提出了一个框架，其中规划模块同时将视觉数据分发到十个专门的推理工具，每个工具专用于分析特定的解剖区域（通过来自策划的查询池的预定义问题）。然后使用过去的示例汇总和完善信息以产生最终输出。同样，专门针对CT肺血管造影的CTPA-Agentproposes a framework where the planning module simultaneously distributes the visual data to ten specialized reasoning tools, with each tool dedicated to analyzing a specific anatomical region (through pre-defined questions from a curated query pool). The information is then aggregated and refined using past examples to produce the final output. Similarly, specifically targeting CT pulmonary angiography, CTPA-AgentZhong et al. 2025采用多步骤设置，首先分类模块识别与肺栓塞相关的 32 种异常，然后对预定义的 VLM 进行一系列预定义的特定区域查询，最后使用单独的重写代理汇总获取的信息。adopts a multi-step setup, where first a classification module identifies 32 abnormalities related to pulmonary embolism, followed by a series of predefined region-specific queries to a predefined VLM, before summarizing the acquired information with a separate rewriting agent.

重要的是，这些代理系统无需培训。在此范例中，代理策略由系统提示设计或通过预定义的工具调用序列来确定。然而，这有其固有的局限性。首先，它假设确定代理政策的法学硕士已经融入了所需的医学知识来设计相关的、以医学为基础的、完整的诊断计划。这一假设在实践中可能并不总是成立。因此，一些人建议在医疗指南中明确制定代理诊断计划。这种接地要么通过预先定义精确的固定诊断计划来实现Importantly, these agentic systems are training-free. In this paradigm, the agent policy is determined by the system prompt design, or via pre-defined tool call sequences. However, this comes with inherent limitations. First, it presumes that the LLM determining the agent policy has already incorporated required medical knowledge to design relevant, medically-grounded, and complete diagnosis plans. This assumption may not always hold in practice. For this reason, some have proposed to explicitly ground an agentic diagnosis plan in medical guidelines. This grounding either occurs through pre-defining a precise fixed diagnosis planZhong et al. 2025; Mao et al. 2025，或者通过为系统提供对外部医学知识源的访问, or by providing the system access to external medical knowledge sourcesWang et al. 2025b; Li et al. 2025a。免培训范式还假设法学硕士协调员本质上能够正确利用工具来完成手头的任务。在需要复杂的动态工具工作流程的设置中，这可能会失败。因此，无需培训的代理系统通常会难以完成需要高度详细地了解复杂工具规范和约束的任务. The training-free paradigm also presumes that the LLM orchestrator is inherently able to correctly leverage tools for the task at hand. This may fail in settings where complex dynamic tool workflows are necessary. As such, training-free agentic systems often struggle with tasks that require a highly detailed understanding of complex tool specifications and constraintsQi et al. 2025，在复杂的临床环境中经常遇到。, which are frequently encountered in complex clinical environments.

奖励设计Reward design

该训练流程成功的一个关键因素是定义合适的奖励函数。确实，A crucial contributing factor to the success of this training pipeline is the definition of a suitable reward function. Indeed,辐射剂RadAgent不仅应提供改进的报告生成功能，而且还应遵守所提供的诊断检查表，并生成连贯的工具调用序列。也就是说，should not only provide improved report generation capabilities, but also adhere to the provided diagnostic checklist, as well as produce coherent tool call sequences. That is,辐射剂RadAgent应该只调用最终分析所需的工具。例如，在分析跟踪的任何后续部分中调用分割工具而不使用生成的分割图被认为是不连贯的，因为这会导致不必要的高计算成本，而对最终报告没有任何优势。我们表明，将所有这些要求整合到奖励设计中对于实现最佳绩效至关重要。我们的最终奖励包括综合奖励课程，旨在仔细平衡新工具序列的探索、报告质量和工具序列的一致性。最终奖励设计详见should only call the tools that are necessary for its final analysis.
