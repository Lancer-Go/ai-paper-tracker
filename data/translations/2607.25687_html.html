摘要Abstract

空气污染全场重建对于评估污染暴露和支持公共卫生决策至关重要。然而，污染物之间复杂的相互作用、难以预测的天气模式以及有限的监测站覆盖范围使得这项任务变得复杂。我们应用深度学习技术，根据四种关键污染物的稀疏观测提供快速、准确的重建： NOFull-field reconstruction of air pollution is essential for evaluating pollution exposure and supporting public health decision-making. However, the complex interactions among pollutants, hard-to-predict weather patterns, and limited monitoring-station coverage make this a complex task. We apply deep learning techniques to provide fast and accurate reconstructions from sparse observations of four key pollutants: NO2, 氧, O3, 下午, PM2.5和下午and PM10。模型根据全场模拟数据进行训练，并根据从以下位置收集的真实世界观察结果进行评估. Models are trained on full-field simulation data and evaluated on real-world observations collected from99 to 2828巴黎市的监测站。我们引入了一种基于扩散的多污染物重建生成框架，并根据确定性深度学习模型对其性能进行了基准测试。尽管存在噪声观测和强烈的空间变异性，但这些模型在模拟验证数据上实现了高度的结构相似性，并根据真实世界的观测产生了真实的空间模式，如功率谱分析所示。我们引入了数据增强方法，无需重新训练即可转移到现实世界的观察结果，从而使模型能够在训练期之外进行推广。这些发现凸显了机器学习模型在现实世界中可靠部署空气污染重建任务的潜力。monitoring stations in the city of Paris. We introduce a diffusion-based generative framework for multi pollutant reconstruction and benchmark its performance against deterministic deep learning models. Despite noisy observations and strong spatial variability, the models achieve high structural similarity on simulated validation data and produce realistic spatial patterns on real-world observations, as indicated by power-spectrum analysis. We introduce data augmentation methods that enable transfer to real-world observations without retraining, allowing the models to generalise beyond the training period. These findings highlight the potential of ML models for reliable real-world deployment in air pollution reconstruction tasks.

Accurate estimation of pollutant concentrations is essential for assessing pollution exposure, addressing associated health impacts[brauer2016ambient]，并帮助政策制定者设计有效的措施, and helping policymakers design effective measures[barwick2024fog].

化学输运 (CT) 模型的确定性建模可通过提供空间和时间解析的浓度场来估计污染暴露Deterministic modelling with chemistry-transport (CT) models can be used to estimate pollution exposure by providing spatially and temporally resolved concentration fields[PASCAL2016, sartelet2025]。然而，这些估计仍然受到多个来源产生的不确定性的影响，包括排放清单、气象场以及大气化学和物理过程的表示。为了减少这些不确定性，可以通过基于空间插值的方法（例如克里金法）使用地面观测来校正模拟浓度. However, these estimates remain subject to uncertainties arising from several sources, including emission inventories, meteorological fields, and the representation of atmospheric chemical and physical processes. To reduce these uncertainties, simulated concentrations can be corrected using ground-based observations through spatial interpolation-based methods, such as kriging[SHUKLA2020]，或数据同化技术, or data assimilation techniques[tilloy2013, sartelet2025]。这些方法通常与较高的计算成本相关，并且依赖于误差分布及其空间相关性的精确先验知识，这限制了它们在实时监控场景中的适用性。. These approaches are generally associated with higher computational costs and rely on precise prior knowledge of error distributions and their spatial correlations, which limits their applicability in real-time monitoring scenarios.

为了解决这个问题，我们研究直接根据传感器观测重建空间场，而不是在推理过程中依赖 CT 模型或数据同化。虽然监测站的数据为我们提供了实时观测值，但这些监测站通常稀疏且分布不均匀。高精度地面站的部署和维护成本高昂。相比之下，更具可扩展性的低成本传感器通常精度有限、灵敏度较高，并且需要定期校准To address this, we investigate the reconstruction of spatial fields directly from sensor observations, without relying on CT models or data assimilation during inference. While data from monitoring stations provide us with real-time observation values, these stations are usually sparse and unevenly distributed. High-precision ground stations are expensive to deploy and maintain. By contrast, low-cost sensors that are more scalable often suffer from limited accuracy, higher sensitivity, and require regular calibration[hayward2024low, stowe2026assessing]。 此外，污染物浓度场可能会表现出突然的转变，导致不同污染物之间存在不同的非线性和非平稳空间模式. Additionally, pollutant concentration fields can exhibit abrupt transitions, leading to nonlinear and nonstationary spatial patterns that vary across pollutants[he2022spatial]。城市空气质量重建本质上仍然是一个不适定的逆问题. Urban air-quality reconstruction remains an intrinsically ill-posed inverse problem[hase2017atmospheric, rodgers2000inverse]无需事先进行模拟。稀疏的监测观测并不能唯一地确定潜在的污染场，因为多种大气状态可能与同一组测量结果一致。由于观测结果通常是稀疏且有噪声的，因此需要在这些条件下保持鲁棒性的重建方法。without prior simulations. Sparse monitoring observations do not uniquely determine the underlying pollution field because multiple atmospheric states can be consistent with the same set of measurements. Because observations are often sparse and noisy, there is a need for reconstruction methods that remain robust under these conditions.

近年来，利用机器学习技术在空气污染领域重建方面取得了快速进展In recent years, the use of machine learning techniques has shown rapid progress in the reconstruction of air pollution fields[le2020spatiotemporal, sasaki2022airex, feng2024spatio]。尽管这些方法比传统方法具有更高的重建精度，但仍有一些重要的局限性尚未解决。首先，大多数研究单独对污染物进行建模，或者为每种污染物训练单独的模型. Although these methods have higher reconstruction accuracy than traditional approaches, several important limitations remain unaddressed. First, most studies model pollutants individually or train separate models for each[le2020spatiotemporal]，忽略污染物间的强相关性, ignoring strong inter-pollutant correlations[seinfeld2016atmospheric]。其次，使用综合生成的全场数据训练的方法进行的研究很少评估现实世界观测的重建质量，因为噪声测量限制了模型向新数据的转移。此外，现实世界的传感器网络会随着时间的推移而打开和关闭，导致暂时不完整的观察。在现有的建模框架中，这一实际挑战经常被忽视. Second, studies using methods trained on synthetically generated full-field data rarely evaluate the reconstruction quality on real-world observations, as noisy measurements limit model transfer to new data. Moreover, real-world sensor networks switch on and off over time, leading to temporally incomplete observations. This practical challenge is often overlooked in existing modelling frameworks[santos2023development].

To provide a spatially consistent representation of the sensor network to the ML model, Voronoi tessellations are constructed. This also mitigates the issues arising from temporally missing observations.

在本文中，重建的评估是在观察到的真实世界传感器读数的独立数据集上进行的。为了提供公平的评估并评估模型在训练季节之外的普适性，模型在2014年1月至10月的模拟数据上进行了10个月的训练，并在2014年11月至12月的分布外真实观测数据上进行了测试。由于观测数据集没有全场空间数据，我们引入逐点交叉验证和谱图来评估模型的性能。我们的结果表明，扩散模型可以很好地推广到现实世界的数据。结合训练后实时生成重建的能力，这些发现凸显了扩散模型在城市空气质量监测和绘图方面的潜力。In this paper, the evaluation of the reconstruction is conducted on an independent dataset of observed real-world sensor readings. To provide a fair assessment and evaluate the model’s generalisability beyond the training seasons, the models are trained on simulation data for 10 months, from January to October 2014, and tested on out-of-distribution real observational data from November to December 2014. Since the full-field spatial data is not available for the observation dataset, we introduce point-wise cross-validation and spectral plots to evaluate the model’s performance. Our results demonstrate that diffusion models generalise well to real-world data. Combined with their ability to generate reconstructions in real-time after training, these findings highlight the potential of diffusion models for operational urban air quality monitoring and mapping.

Perlin 扰动生成随机定向向量的网格，定义局部方向变化。为了计算给定点的噪声值，该算法计算周围网格角的贡献并对它们进行插值。分形 Perlin 噪声通过在增加空间频率（“间隙”）和减小幅度（“持久性”）下对 Perlin 噪声的多层（“八度音阶”）求和来扩展这一概念。Perlin perturbation generates a grid of randomly oriented vectors that define local directional changes. To compute the noise value at a given point, the algorithm calculates contributions from the surrounding grid corners and interpolates them. Fractal Perlin noise extends this concept by summing multiple layers (”octaves”) of Perlin noise at increasing spatial frequencies (”lacunarity”) and decreasing amplitudes (”persistence”).

F.4 互相关扰动Cross-correlation perturbation

该方法根据训练数据集中像素之间的互相关结构生成扰动。通过估计像素间相关性并应用相应的协方差变换，该方法产生空间相关的噪声场，更好地反映空气污染模式的统计特性。由于互相关是图像特定的度量，因此计算时间维度上的平均值。使用互相关矩阵的 Cholesky 分解，将白噪声转换为互相关噪声。This method generates perturbations based on the cross-correlation structure among pixels in the training dataset. By estimating inter-pixel correlations and applying the corresponding covariance transformation, the method produces spatially correlated noise fields that better reflect the statistical properties of air-pollution patterns. As cross-correlation is an image-specific measure, the mean across the time dimension is computed. Using the Cholesky decomposition of the cross-correlation matrix, white noise is converted into cross-correlated noise.

在考虑的增强策略中，该方法生成最统计上真实的扰动，因为它明确保留了训练数据的空间相关结构。然而，这种对训练集统计数据的依赖可能会限制生成样本的多样性，从而可能降低其提高模型对分布外观测的鲁棒性的有效性。Among the augmentation strategies considered, this method generates the most statistically realistic perturbations, as it explicitly preserves the spatial correlation structure of the training data. However, this reliance on training-set statistics may limit the diversity of the generated samples, potentially reducing its effectiveness in improving model robustness to out-of-distribution observations.
