1. 简介Introduction

多模态大语言模型（MLLM）在以下方面取得了显着进展 单视图视觉理解，具有代表性的系统，例如 火烈鸟Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have achieved remarkable progress in single-view visual understanding, with representative systems such as Flamingo(Alayrac et al., 2022),BLIP-2, BLIP-2(Li et al., 2023), 拉瓦, LLaVA(Liu et al., 2023), 实习生VL, InternVL(Chen et al., 2024b), 和 Qwen3-VL, and Qwen3-VL(Bai et al., 2025)在字幕和一般方面表现出强劲的表现 视觉问答。基于这些进展，最近的研究已开始将 MLLM 扩展到更丰富的空间和 3D 推理能力demonstrating strong performance on captioning and general visual question answering. Building on these advances, recent research has begun to extend MLLMs toward richer spatial and 3D reasoning capabilities(Chen et al., 2024a; Cheng et al., 2024; Daxberger et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025a)，这对于实现现实世界的交互至关重要。, which are essential for enabling real-world interaction.

然而，现实世界的环境本质上是动态的和多视角的：智能体必须从不断变化的视角持续感知场景，而不是依赖于单个静态图像。从静态感知到动态多视图理解的转变带来了新的挑战，而这些挑战无法通过简单地扩展单视图功能来解决。 模型必须在视点变化和使用中识别相同的物理对象 对齐表示以支持有关可见性的下游推理， 几何和相互作用。在真实的多机位设置中，诸如 跨视图对应、遮挡、布局变化和身体接触 依赖于跨视图的稳定的对象级一致性。这个能力是 实体人工智能、多智能体协作、机器人和视频的基础 分析，但在当前的 MLLM 中仍然不发达。我们将此限制归因于以下主要挑战。However, real-world environments are inherently dynamic and multi-perspective: agents must continuously perceive scenes from changing viewpoints rather than relying on a single static image. This shift from static perception to dynamic, multi-view understanding introduces new challenges that cannot be addressed by simply scaling single-view capabilities. A model must identify the same physical object across viewpoint changes and use that aligned representation to support downstream reasoning about visibility, geometry, and interactions. In real multi-camera settings, judgments such as cross-view correspondence, occlusion, layout change, and physical contact all depend on stable object-level consistency across views. This capability is fundamental for embodied AI, multi-agent collaboration, robotics, and video analytics, but remains underdeveloped in current MLLMs. We attribute this limitation to the following key challenges.

6. 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 CrossView Suite，它结合了 CrossViewSet、CrossViewBench 和 CrossViewer 来实现多视图 空间智能。在 CrossViewBench 上，CrossViewer 的总体准确率达到 62.7%， 性能优于 Qwen3-VL-8B 20.0%。我们的分析表明 ART、对比对齐和跨视图注意力都是 重要。我们希望 CrossViewBench 能够支持未来更丰富的多视图工作 MLLM 中的设置和开放式空间推理。We introduced CrossView Suite, which combines CrossViewSet, CrossViewBench, and CrossViewer for multi-view spatial intelligence. On CrossViewBench, CrossViewer reaches 62.7% overall accuracy, outperforming Qwen3-VL-8B by 20.0%. Our analyses show that ART, contrastive alignment, and cross-view attention are all important. We hope CrossViewBench will support future work on richer multi-view settings and open-ended spatial reasoning in MLLMs.
