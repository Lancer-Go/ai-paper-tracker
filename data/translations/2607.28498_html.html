摘要Abstract

科学假设生成是新兴人工智能科学系统的关键组成部分。假设生成分两步进行：1)Scientific hypothesis generation is a key component of the emerging AI for Science systems. Hypothesis generation is performed in two steps: 1)科学灵感检索（SIR）Scientific Inspiration Retrieval (SIR)，识别现有的鼓舞人心的知识，这些知识可以为解决目标问题提出假设，并且 2), to identify the existing inspiring knowledge that can seed a hypothesis to address a target problem, and 2)假设构成Hypothesis Composition，根据检索到的灵感提出解决目标问题的假设。现有的 SIR 方法仅根据主题相似性对论文进行排名，而没有明确表示候选灵感如何转移到目标。这种方法特别忽视了远程灵感（即来自目标领域以外的知识领域的灵感），而远程灵感以生成最具创造性的假设而闻名。这些灵感的相关性并不取决于主题的相似性，而是取决于它们提供的可重复使用的解决问题的原则。受人类如何通过抽象源灵感的可转移方面并重新映射它们以解决目标问题来转移想法的启发，我们将 SIR 重新表述为, to compose the hypothesis for addressing the target problem based on the retrieved inspirations. Existing SIR methods rank papers merely based on topical similarity without explicitly representing how a candidate inspiration is transferable to the target. This approach particularly disregards remote inspirations (i.e., inspirations from knowledge domains other than the target domain), which are known for generation of most creative hypotheses. The relevance of such inspirations depends not on topical similarity but on reusable problem-solving principles they offer. Motivated by how humans transfer ideas by abstracting transferable aspects of source inspirations and remapping them to address a target problem, we reformulate SIR as目标条件抽象target-conditioned abstraction（TCA）。通过这种方法，检索到的对象是从与解决目标问题特别相关的候选灵感中提取的可转移的抽象原理。据此，我们提出(TCA). With this approach, the retrieved object is a transferable abstract principle extracted from a candidate inspiration specifically relevant for addressing the target problem. Accordingly, we presentTCA-SIR，一种新颖的基于 TCA 的 SIR 方法，它学习为候选灵感生成目标条件抽象，并使用它们学习到的表示来预测灵感到目标的可转移性。使用 ResearchBench 基准，我们证明 TCA-SIR 优于现有的 SIR 解决方案以及直接 LLM 检索，与最先进的 MOOSE-Chem 方法相比，HitRate@top4% 提高了 10 个百分点以上。使用未经训练的 TCA 提示与相同主干进行比较进一步表明，学习的抽象更清楚地恢复目标相关机制（包括化学杠杆和性能瓶颈），而不是主题口号或肤浅的事实。通过明确揭示将候选灵感与目标联系起来的可重用原则，TCA-SIR 不仅提高了检索性能，而且还提供了, a novel TCA-based SIR method that learns to generate target-conditioned abstractions for candidate inspirations and uses their learned representations to predict the transferability of the inspirations to the target. Using the ResearchBench benchmark, we demonstrate that TCA-SIR outperforms the existing SIR solutions as well as direct LLM retrieval, improving HitRate@top4% over the state-of-the-art MOOSE-Chem method by more than 10 percentage points. Comparisons with the same backbone using an untrained TCA prompt further show that learned abstractions more clearly recover target-relevant mechanisms (including chemical levers and performance bottlenecks) rather than topical slogans or superficial facts. By explicitly exposing the reusable principles connecting a candidate inspiration to the target, TCA-SIR not only improves retrieval performance, but also provides an可解释的interpretable科学灵感检索的基本原理。rationale for scientific inspiration retrieval.

1 简介Introduction

在大型语言模型 (LLM) 的科学机会和能力提升的推动下，科学人工智能正在迅速发展。 AI Scientist 系列等系统可自动完成发现流程的大部分工作，并且可以在有限的人工干预下起草论文AI for science is advancing rapidly, driven by both scientific opportunity and capability gains in large language models (LLMs). Systems such as the AI Scientist series automate substantial parts of the discovery pipeline and can draft papers with limited human intervention(Lu et al. 2024; Yamada et al. 2025; Lu et al. 2026)。 人工智能已经在科学发现过程的每个阶段做出了贡献. AI already contributes at each stage of the scientific discovery process(Wei et al. 2025)：(1) 观察和假设生成: (1) observation and hypothesis generation(Si et al. 2025; Romera-Paredes et al. 2024; Yang et al. 2026; Novikov et al. 2025; Liu et al. 2026a; Radensky et al. 2026; Baek et al. 2025), (2) 实验规划和执行, (2) experimental planning and execution(Boiko et al. 2023; Swanson et al. 2024), (3) 结果分析, (3) result analysis(Wang et al. 2025; Ding et al. 2024)和 (4) 合成、验证和进化, and (4) synthesis, validation, and evolution(Ou et al. 2025; Takagi et al. 2023)。 在这些阶段中，假设生成尤其重要：弱假设会浪费下游实验和分析工作，但稳健、可扩展的假设生成仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。. SIR asks systems to rank papers that can seed a hypothesis for a target research problem. This step is particularly difficult to implement for LLMs because useful inspirations often come from distant domains and share little topic similarity with the target problem. 7 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and Future Work

科学灵感检索（SIR）是基于法学硕士的假设生成的瓶颈。有用的灵感通常是遥远的话题，但却为目标提供了可转移的原则。 借鉴人类抽象和重新实例化远程类比的方式，我们将 SIR 重新定义为 TCA（目标条件抽象），其中检索对象是从候选者中提取的可转移原则，并以目标为条件，而不是作为原子文档的出版物。 我们提出了 TCA-SIR，它联合学习目标条件抽象和对候选进行排名的可转移性分数。 在 ResearchBench 上，TCA-SIR 比直接 LLM 提示和基于抽象的基线提高了命中率和排名指标，定性分析表明学习的抽象恢复了目标相关机制，而不是一般的主题相似性。 实际限制是计算成本。由于每个抽象都是目标条件的，TCA-SIR 必须为每个目标候选对生成一个 R/A 中间体，而不是单个源端摘要，这使得大型池在推理时成本高昂。 未来的工作将探索更有效的方法来扩展这种方法。Scientific inspiration retrieval (SIR) is a bottleneck for LLM-based hypothesis generation. Useful inspirations are often topically remote, yet carry a transferable principle for the target. Drawing on how humans abstract and reinstantiate remote analogies, we redefined SIR as TCA (Target-Conditioned Abstraction), where the retrieval object is a transferable principle extracted from a candidate and conditioned on the target rather than the publication as an atomic document. We proposed TCA-SIR, which jointly learns target-conditioned abstractions and a transferability score for ranking candidates. On ResearchBench, TCA-SIR improves HitRate and ranking metrics over direct LLM prompting and abstraction-based baselines, and qualitative analysis shows that learned abstractions recover target-relevant mechanisms rather than generic topical similarities. A practical limitation is computational cost. Because each abstraction is target-conditioned, TCA-SIR must generate an R/A intermediate for every target-candidate pair rather than a single source-side summary, making large pools expensive at inference. Future work will explore more efficient ways to scale this approach.
