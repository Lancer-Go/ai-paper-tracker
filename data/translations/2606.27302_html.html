摘要ABSTRACT

人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人越来越多地用于支持健康信息查找和自我管理，但其性能和对用户的影响仍有待研究。这项研究调查了来自 59 个人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人应用程序的 15,000 多个用户评论，以探索这些系统在日常信息和情感环境中的运作方式。主题建模和解释分析确定了三个反复出现的故障：访问障碍和服务不可靠性、用户体验和交互质量以及计费和客户支持问题。隐私和安全问题与最负面的经历相关。通过将人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人视为信息基础设施，我们的研究结果强调了访问、可用性和信任方面的失败如何影响用户，为旨在改善数字医疗系统的设计师、政策制定者和信息专业人员提供了可操作的见解。AI healthcare chatbots are increasingly used to support health information seeking and self-management, yet their performance and impact on users remains to be studied. This study examines over 15,000 user reviews from 59 AI healthcare chatbot apps to explore how these systems function in everyday informational and emotional contexts. Topic modeling and interpretive analysis identify three recurring breakdowns: access barriers and service unreliability, user experience and interaction quality, and billing and customer support issues. Privacy and security concerns are associated with the most negative experiences. By framing AI healthcare chatbots as information infrastructures, our findings highlight how failures in access, usability, and trust affect users, offering actionable insights for designers, policymakers, and information professionals aiming to improve digital health systems.

简介INTRODUCTION

支持人工智能的医疗保健聊天机器人已越来越多地嵌入数字健康生态系统中，影响着个人访问和参与健康相关信息的方式。皮尤研究中心最近的数据表明，22% 的美国人表示出于健康相关原因使用人工智能聊天机器人，而年轻人比年长的同龄人更有可能转向人工智能聊天机器人和社交媒体来满足医疗保健需求（皮尤研究中心，2026）。这些系统主要通过移动应用程序提供，利用智能手机的功能（例如推送通知、传感器和始终可用的界面）来提供对信息资源的持续按需访问、自我管理指导和超出正式临床环境的对话支持（Laranjo 等人，2018）。 Wysa、Youper 和 Woebot 等应用程序说明了会话代理如何被定位为管理情绪、症状和幸福感的日常工具（Inkster 等人，2018）。随着这些系统融入用户持续的信息实践中，检查个人如何在现场体验它们，特别是当用户的期望未得到满足时，成为一个问题。这种观点强调需要理解用户的担忧和不满，而不是将其视为孤立的故障，而是将其视为此类系统如何在日常用户的生活环境中运行的有意义的信号。AI-enabled healthcare chatbots have become increasingly embedded in digital health ecosystems, shaping how individuals access and engage with health-related information. Recent Pew Research Center data indicate that 22% of Americans report using AI chatbots for health-related reasons, and younger adults are more likely than older peers to turn to AI chatbots and social media for healthcare needs (Pew Research Center, 2026). Delivered primarily through mobile applications, these systems leverage smartphone affordance, such as push notifications, sensors, and always-available interfaces, to provide continuous and on-demand access to informational resources, selfmanagement guidance, and conversational support that extends beyond formal clinical settings (Laranjo et al., 2018). Applications such as Wysa, Youper, and Woebot illustrate how conversational agents are positioned as everyday tools for managing mood, symptoms, and well-being (Inkster et al., 2018). As these systems become integrated within users’ ongoing information practices, examining how individuals experience them in situ, particularly when users’ expectations are unmet, becomes a concern. This perspective highlights the need to understand user concerns and dissatisfaction not as isolated failure, but as a meaningful signal of how such systems operate within lived contexts of everyday users.

将人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人概念化为信息基础设施，强调了它们在跨交叉领域协调知识、支持和服务的获取方面的作用（Star & Bowker，1999）。当基础设施发生故障、访问受阻、交互失败或支付和支持等制度安排变得不明确时，它们往往变得最为明显（Star & Ruhleder，1996）。健康聊天机器人的运行跨越了上下文边界：临床和日常环境之间、机构医疗保健系统和商业平台之间、个人体验和分布式信息资源之间。因此，关注用户的负面体验为检查这些基础设施在实践中如何运作以及它们的不足之处提供了一个分析镜头。这种方法可以使人们更深入地了解基础设施如何运作。检查这些用户体验可以洞察这些系统在实践中的运作方式以及它们的不足之处。Conceptualizing AI healthcare chatbots as information infrastructures highlights their role in mediating access to knowledge, support, and services across intersecting domains (Star & Bowker, 1999). Infrastructures often become most visible when they break down, when access is blocked, interactions fail, or institutional arrangements such as payment and support become unclear (Star & Ruhleder, 1996. Health chatbots operate across contextual boundaries: between clinical and everyday environments, between institutional healthcare systems and commercial platforms, and between personal experiences and distributed informational resources. Attending to users’ negative experiences therefore offers an analytical lens for examining how these infrastructures function in practice and where they fall short. Such an approach enables a closer understanding of how infrastructural conditions shape, and at times constrain users’ experiences of informational support and well-being. Examining these user experiences offers insight into how these systems function in practice and where they fall short.

Taken together, these findings position AI healthcare chatbots within a broader landscape of evolving health information infrastructures, where questions of usability, trust, and governance remain central to how informational support is distributed and experienced in everyday life.

背景及相关工作BACKGROUND AND RELATED WORK

人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人作为信息系统AI Healthcare Chatbots as Information Systems

支持人工智能的医疗保健聊天机器人已发展成为广泛使用的数字健康工具，旨在支持健康生态系统，重塑个人访问和参与健康相关信息的方式。这些系统，从基于规则的会话代理到更新的机器学习和大语言模型 (LLM) 驱动工具，旨在通过会话交互支持症状检查、自我管理以及情感或心理健康支持（Laranjo 等人，2018 年；Inkster 等人，2018 年）。先前的研究已证明它们有潜力扩大健康信息的获取范围并提供可扩展的支持形式，特别是在传统护理有限或无法获得的情况下（Fitzpatrick 等，2017）。AI-enabled healthcare chatbots have evolved into widely used digital wellness tools designed to support health ecosystems, reshaping how individuals access and engage with health-related information. These systems, ranging from rule-based conversational agents to more recent machine learning and large language models (LLM)-driven tools, are designed to support symptom checking, self-management, and emotional or mental health support through conversational interaction (Laranjo et al., 2018; Inkster et al., 2018). Prior research has demonstrated their potential to extend access to health information and provide scalable forms of support, particularly in contexts where traditional care is limited or inaccessible (Fitzpatrick et al., 2017).

这些系统是社会技术安排，可调节临床和日常环境中知识、支持和服务的获取（Ocepek，2018）。用户与聊天机器人互动不仅是为了检索信息，也是为了解释症状、反思个人经历并寻求情感上的安慰。这将人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人置于更广泛的日常信息搜索和使用框架内，其中信息和情感需求往往相互交织（Savolinen，2005；Wardle 等人，2025；Liu 等人，2025）。从这个意义上说，聊天机器人不仅充当工具，而且充当中介，塑造健康信息在实践中的接触、解释和应用方式，既用于预期的健康支持，又用于更具探索性的用途，如 Chen 等人（2024）所讨论的。These systems are sociotechnical arrangements that mediate access to knowledge, support, and services across clinical and everyday settings (Ocepek, 2018). Users engage with chatbots not only to retrieve information but also to interpret symptoms, reflect on personal experiences, and seek emotional reassurance. This positions AI healthcare chatbots within broader frameworks of everyday information seeking and use, where informational and affective needs are often intertwined (Savolainen, 2005; Wardle et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025). In this sense, chatbots function not merely as tools, but as intermediaries that shape how health information is encountered, interpreted, and applied in practice, both for intended health support and more exploratory use as discussed by Chen et al, (2024).

与此同时，越来越多的工作引起了人们对这些系统的可靠性、安全性和局限性的担忧。研究发现了诸如不准确或不完整的反应、缺乏临床基础以及不一致的对话行为等问题（Miner et al., 2016；Bickmore & Giorgino, 2006）。在健康方面，担忧进一步延伸到包括潜在的不当反应、未能识别危机情况以及用户对自动化系统过度信任的风险（Vaidyam 等，2019）。这些挑战凸显出不仅需要检查聊天机器人的技术能力，还需要检查它们在实际使用中的表现，特别是与用户期望和福祉相关的表现。At the same time, a growing body of work has raised concerns about the reliability, safety, and limitations of these systems. Studies have identified issues such as inaccurate or incomplete responses, lack of clinical grounding, and inconsistent conversational behavior (Miner at al., 2016; Bickmore & Giorgino, 2006). In health contexts, concerns extend further to include the potential for inappropriate responses, failure to recognize crisis situations, and the risk of users placing undue trust in automated systems (Vaidyam et al., 2019). These challenges highlight the need to examine not only the technical capabilities of chatbots, but also how they perform in real-world use, particularly in relation to user expectations and well-being.

数字医疗系统中的信任、隐私和数据实践Trust, Privacy, and Data Practices in Digital Health Systems

信任是使用数字医疗技术的一个核心问题，特别是当系统处理敏感的个人和健康相关信息时。道德和法律领域的工作表明，对基于人工智能的系统的看法不仅取决于技术性能，还取决于数据实践和责任的定义有多清晰。米特尔施塔特等人。例如，（2016）确定了算法决策支持的关键道德挑战，包括数据使用中的不透明和责任不明确，而 Veale 等人。 （2018）研究了数据保护和非歧视法如何适用于自动决策，强调了解释和补救方面的不确定性。 Williamson 和 Prybutok (2024) 分析了用户对人工智能健康应用程序的反应，表明隐私、安全和透明度问题与用户是否认为此类工具值得信赖密切相关。总之，这项工作强调数据实践不是背景细节，而是数字卫生系统评估方式的可见组成部分。Trust is a central concern in the use of digital health technologies, particularly when systems handle sensitive personal and health-related information. Work in ethics and law has shown that perceptions of AI-based systems depend not only on technical performance but also on how clearly data practices and responsibilities are defined. Mittelstadt et al. (2016), for example, identify key ethical challenges of algorithmic decision support, including opacity and unclear accountability in data use, while Veale et al. (2018) examine how data-protection and non-discrimination law apply to automated decision-making, highlighting uncertainties around explanation and redress. Williamson and Prybutok (2024) analyse user responses to AI-enabled health applications, showing that privacy, security, and transparency concerns are closely tied to whether users perceive such tools as trustworthy. Together, this work underscores that data practices are not a background detail but a visible component of how digital health systems are evaluated.

对具体系统的实证研究通过展示用户如何面对有限的控制和部分信息（即使存在正式政策）来加深这些担忧。霍斯特曼等人。 (2024) 发现人们很难理解数据应用程序收集和共享哪些数据，从而使知情同意变得复杂，而 Winter 和 Davidson (2019) 表明，患者往往不清楚他们的数据如何跨平台流动，当涉及多个参与者时，提出了问责和治理问题。与此同时，现有的工作表明，人们在日常使用中并不总是明确表达隐私或安全问题：Ischen 等人。 （2020）表明，隐私担忧可能会一直存在，直到服务出现意外行为，而 Gumusel（2025）则展示了用户如何通过不适或不信任而不是明确的隐私语言来间接表达不安，强调应用程序评论中缺乏隐私术语并不一定意味着不存在与隐私相关的不适。Empirical studies of concrete systems deepen these concerns by showing how users face both limited control and partial information even where formal policies exist. Horstmann et al. (2024) find that people struggle to understand what data applications collect and share, complicating informed consent, while Winter and Davidson (2019) show that patients often lack clarity about how their data flow across platforms, raising questions of accountability and governance when multiple actors are involved. At the same time, existing work indicates that people do not always explicitly articulate privacy or security concerns in everyday use: Ischen et al. (2020) suggest that privacy worries may remain latent until a service behaves unexpectedly, and Gumusel (2025) shows how users express unease indirectly, through discomfort or distrust rather than explicit privacy language, underscoring that the absence of privacy terminology in app reviews does not necessarily imply the absence of privacy-related discomfort.

有关人工智能健康聊天机器人的文献强调了面向消费者的工具的数据敏感性和监管差距。 Haupt 和 Marks (2023) 表明，心理健康聊天机器人可能介于临床监管和消费者保护之间，从而在数据处理和安全方面产生模糊的保护，而 Hassan 和 Bashir (2023) 则发现应用程序规定的隐私政策与其数据流之间不匹配。与受既定健康数据框架管辖的临床系统不同，嵌入商业应用程序中的聊天机器人在定义不太明确的标准下运行，因此对隐私和数据使用的担忧常常与更广泛的信任问题交织在一起，包括支付实践和服务可靠性。与此同时，对话代理的研究强调了可靠性和安全性的局限性：Miner 等人。 (2016) 和 Bickmore 和 Giorgino (2006) 记录了不准确、薄弱的临床基础和不一致的对话，而 Vaidyam 等人。 （2019）表明，未能识别或适当应对危机情况可能会带来巨大风险。总之，这项工作表明不仅需要评估聊天机器人如何管理数据，还需要评估它们在现实世界中相对于用户期望和福祉的表现。Literature on AI health chatbots highlights both data sensitivity and regulatory gaps in consumer-facing tools. Haupt and Marks (2023) show that mental-health chatbots maybe between clinical regulation and consumer protection, creating ambiguous protections around data handling and safety, while Hassan and Bashir (2023) identify mismatches between apps stated privacy policies and their data flows. Unlike clinical systems governed by established health-data frameworks, chatbots embedded in commercial apps operate under less clearly defined standards, so worries about privacy and data use often intersect with broader issues of trust, including payment practices and service reliability. At the same time, studies of conversational agents underscore limitations in reliability and safety: Miner et al. (2016) and Bickmore and Giorgino (2006) document inaccuracies, weak clinical grounding, and inconsistent dialogue, and Vaidyam et al. (2019) show that failures to recognise or appropriately respond to crisis situations can pose substantial risk. Together, this work suggests the need to evaluate not only how chatbots manage data, but also how they perform in real-world use relative to user expectations and well-being.

在此文献的基础上，本研究调查了用户使用人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人的体验，并调查了安全、隐私和数据处理相关问题（SPR）作为用户体验的一个特定维度。我们研究了这些担忧如何在评论中明确表达，以及它们如何与更广泛的不满模式相关，包括访问、互动质量和财务信任方面的崩溃。这种方法使我们能够将与 SPR 相关的投诉置于更广泛的基础设施问题中，而不是将其视为孤立的合规或政策问题。Building on this literature, the present study investigates users’ experience with AI healthcare chatbots and investigates security-, privacy-, and data-handling-related concerns (SPR) as a specific dimension of user experience. We examine how such concerns are explicitly articulated within reviews and how they relate to broader patterns of dissatisfaction, including breakdowns in access, interaction quality, and financial trust. This approach allows us to situate SPR-related complaints within a wider landscape of infrastructural issues, rather than treating them as an isolated problem of compliance or policy.

方法论METHODOLOGY

这项研究将大规模语料库建设与计算建模和解释分析相结合。在本节中，我们将描述如何识别支持人工智能的医疗保健聊天机器人应用程序、收集和过滤应用程序商店评论以及分析生成的语料库。This study combines large-scale corpus construction with computational modelling and interpretive analysis. In this section, we describe how we identified AI-enabled healthcare chatbot applications, collected and filtered app-store reviews, and analyzed the resulting corpus.

第 1 步：数据收集Step 1: Data Collection

为了构建实证语料库，我们系统地识别了来自 Google Play Store (Android) 和 Apple App Store (iOS) 的支持 AI 的医疗保健聊天机器人应用程序。我们在收集过程中没有应用任何区域过滤器，因此生成的应用程序和评论可能反映全球用户群而不是区域背景。我们使用“人工智能健康”、“人工智能医疗保健”和“医疗保健聊天机器人”等术语进行基于关键字的搜索，结合健康相关和面向人工智能的术语来捕获广泛的应用。搜索结果根据应用程序名称和开发人员进行聚合和重复数据删除。To construct an empirical corpus, we systematically identified AI-enabled healthcare chatbot applications from the Google Play Store (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS). We did not apply any regional filters during collection, so the resulting apps and reviews are likely to reflect a global user base rather than a regional context. We conducted keyword-based searches using terms such as “AI health,” “AI healthcare,” and “healthcare chatbot,” combining health-related and AI-oriented terms to capture a broad range of applications. Search results were aggregated and deduplicated based on app name and developer.

我们应用了两个纳入标准来确保分析的相关性。首先，应用程序必须关注健康，如其商店类别、标题或描述所示。其次，它必须提供基于人工智能的对话或聊天机器人功能，无论是通过基于规则的对话、专有的机器学习模型还是通过移动 SDK 嵌入的基于 LLM 的组件来实现。两名研究人员使用公开的商店材料（包括描述、屏幕截图和界面提示）独立地将每个应用程序编码为“包含”或“排除”。使用 Cohen’s kappa (κ = 0.6773) 评估评估者间的可靠性，表明基本一致，并通过讨论解决差异，直至达成共识。此过程产生了 38 个 Android 应用程序和 21 个 iOS 应用程序的最终样本，其中 18 个应用程序可在两个平台上使用。We applied two inclusion criteria to ensure analytical relevance. First, the application had to focus on health, as indicated by its store category, title, or description. Second, it had to provide AI-based conversational or chatbot functionality, whether implemented via rule-based dialogue, proprietary machine-learning models, or LLM-based components embedded through mobile SDKs. Two researchers independently coded each app as “include” or “exclude” using publicly available store materials, including descriptions, screenshots, and interface cues. Inter-rater reliability was assessed using Cohen’s kappa (κ = 0.6773), indicating substantial agreement, and the discrepancies were resolved through discussion until consensus was reached. This process yielded a final sample of 38 Android applications and 21 iOS applications, including 18 applications available on both platforms.

第三步：分析Step 3: Analysis

为了在不强加预定义类别的情况下识别用户评论中的重复模式，我们采用主题建模作为归纳分析方法。我们使用词袋表示法在 15,090 条评论（使用 NLP 分类为负面评论）的语料库上训练了潜在狄利克雷分配 (LDA) 模型。选择 LDA 是因为它提供了一种可解释的方式来显示大型文本语料库中共现的术语和潜在主题，从而提供结构化的定性解释（Jelodar 等人，2019）。在对一系列主题编号 (10-35) 进行迭代评估后，我们根据最佳一致性得分 (0.53) 和结果主题的实质性可解释性选择了 10 个主题解决方案，模型参数设置为 100 次迭代和 20 次传递。To identify recurring patterns in user reviews without imposing predefined categories, we employed topic modeling as an inductive analytical approach. We trained a Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) model on the corpus of 15,090 reviews (classified as negative using NLP), using a bag-of-words representation. LDA was selected because it offers an interpretable way to surface co-occurring terms and latent themes in large text corpora, providing a structured qualitative interpretation (Jelodar et al., 2019). Following iterative evaluation across a range of topic numbers (10– 35), we selected a 10-topic solution based on the best coherence score (0.53) and the substantive interpretability of the resulting topics, with model parameters set to 100 iterations and 20 passes.

我们将主题模型视为启发式工具来指导而不是确定我们的分析。对于每个主题，我们检查了高概率术语，并审查了具有高主题比例的有目的的评论样本。通过对这些文本的比较阅读，我们确定了反复出现的问题、评价性语言以及对系统行为的参考，特别是与健康和人工智能交互相关的系统行为。基于这个过程，我们为每个主题分配了一个描述性标签和一个简洁的主题摘要。这一解释步骤从 Braun 和 Clarke (2006) 阐述的主题分析原则中汲取灵感，使用模型建议的模式作为起点，并通过与数据的密切接触来完善它们，而不是将主题视为固定的、纯粹的统计类别。We treated the topic model as a heuristic tool to guide, rather than determine, our analysis. For each topic, we examined high-probability terms and reviewed a purposive sample of reviews with high topic proportions. Through comparative reading of these texts, we identified recurrent issues, evaluative language, and references to system behaviour, particularly in relation to health and AI interaction. Based on this process, we assigned each topic a descriptive label and a concise thematic summary. This interpretive step draws inspiration from thematic analysis principles in the sense articulated by Braun and Clarke (2006), using patterns suggested by the model as a starting point and refining them through close engagement with the data, rather than treating topics as fixed, purely statistical categories.

Two authors collaboratively reviewed and refined these groupings, iteratively revisiting representative reviews to ensure conceptual alignment between topics and categories. This process positions computational modeling as a starting point for human-centered interpretation, allowing categories to emerge through engagement with the data rather than being imposed a priori.

讨论DISCUSSION

将负面体验解释为基础设施用户的担忧Interpreting Negative Experiences as Infrastructural User Concerns

本研究中确定的模式指出了人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人如何在日常使用中充当信息基础设施的反复出现的问题（Bowker 等人，1996 年；Wah，2025 年）。这三种关注类型并不是孤立的可用性问题，而是反映了用户使用这些系统的不同阶段的失败。计费和客户支持问题表明对管理访问的计费、透明度和客户支持的不满，这与之前关于数字医疗服务中信任和问责制中心地位的工作相呼应。访问障碍和服务不可靠性反映了进入点的故障，即用户无法到达或维持与系统的交互，这种模式与研究产生共鸣，这些研究表明基础设施的脆弱性如何使正式服务“无法获得”，即使这些服务名义上可用。反过来，当用户可以参与但发现系统反应迟钝、无益或与他们的需求不一致时，就会出现与交互相关的问题，通过展示在日常环境中如何大规模经历此类限制，扩展了早期关于健康聊天机器人中准确性有限和对话行为不一致的发现。The patterns identified in this study point to recurring issues in how AI healthcare chatbots function as information infrastructures in everyday use (Bowker et al., 1996; Wah, 2025). Rather than isolated usability problems, the three concern types reflect failures at different stages of users’ engagement with these systems. Billing and customer support issues indicate dissatisfaction with the billing, transparency and customer support that govern access, echoing prior work on the centrality of trust and accountability in digital health services. Access barriers and service unreliability reflect failures at the point of entry, where users are unable to reach or sustain interaction with the system, a pattern that resonates with studies showing how infrastructural fragility can keep formal services “out of reach” even when they are nominally available. In turn, interaction-related concerns arise when users can engage but find the system unresponsive, unhelpful, or misaligned with their needs, extending earlier findings on limited accuracy and inconsistent conversational behavior in health chatbots by demonstrating how such limitations are experienced at scale in everyday settings.

虽然付费墙、崩溃和计费纠纷并不是人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人所独有的，但它们在这种背景下获得了独特的特征。使用一般生产力或娱乐应用程序的用户若遇到付费专区，将无法获得便利；遇到同样障碍的心理健康聊天机器人用户可能在痛苦时刻无法获得情感支持（Zhang et al., 2024；Vaidyam et al., 2019；Torous et al., 2023）。同样，人工智能特定的失败、伪装成自适应对话的脚本对话、不响应用户输入的过时代理以及用户明确描述为“不是真正的人工智能”的交互，反映了用户与现代生成人工智能相关的可供性与他们遇到的系统的实际功能之间的差距（Zhang 等人，2024 年；Miner 等人，2016 年；Fitzpatrick 等人，2017 年）。这些不是一般的移动应用程序问题；它们反映了健康敏感环境中对话式人工智能未满足的特定期望。While paywalls, crashes, and billing disputes are not unique to AI healthcare chatbots, they acquire a distinctive character in this context. Users of general productivity or entertainment apps who encounter paywall are denied convenience; users of mental health chatbots who encounter the same barrier may be denied access to emotional support at a moment of distress (Zhang et al., 2024; Vaidyam et al., 2019; Torous et al., 2023). Similarly, AIspecific failures, scripted dialogue masquerading as adaptive conversation, outdated agents that do not respond to user input, and interactions that users explicitly describe as "not real AI", reflect a gap between the affordances users associate with modern generative AI and the actual capabilities of the systems they encounter (Zhang et al., 2024, Miner et al., 2016; Fitzpatrick et al., 2017). These are not generic mobile app problems; they reflect unmet expectations specific to conversational AI in health-sensitive contexts.

这些细分在用户体验和评估方式上也有所不同。与计费和访问相关的故障往往会立即产生一致的负面反应，因为它们完全阻止了有意义的使用，这与之前的研究一致，这些研究与缺乏财务和计费透明度以及访问受限导致信任度急剧下降有关。相比之下，与交互相关的问题表现出更大的差异：一些用户很快就脱离了，而另一些用户则继续交互足够长的时间以阐明特定的限制，例如缺乏个性化或浅薄的情感支持。在这一类别的某些部分中观察到的相对较高的评级表明，不满可能与部分参与共存，特别是当用户将这些系统作为持续的伴侣而不是一次性工具时。这种细微差别表明负面经历并不统一，从而丰富了之前的研究成果。有些表明完全失败，而另一些则揭示了持续依赖中的紧张局势。These breakdowns also differ in how users experience and evaluate them. Failures related to billing and access tend to produce immediate and uniformly negative responses, as they prevent meaningful use altogether, aligning with prior research that links to lack of financial and billing transparency and restricted access to sharp declines in trust. By contrast, interaction-related issues show greater variation: some users disengage quickly, while others continue interacting long enough to articulate specific limitations, such as lack of personalization or shallow emotional support. The relatively higher ratings observed in parts of this category suggest that dissatisfaction can coexist with partial engagement, particularly when users approach these systems as ongoing companions rather than one-off tools. This nuance adds to previous work by showing that negative experiences are not uniform; some signal complete failure, while others reveal tensions within continued reliance.

综合来看，这些发现将人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人视为基础设施，在访问、交互和治理维度上经常出现故障。这种观点将注意力从技术性能转移到更广泛的条件上，在这些条件下，此类系统在实践中变得可用、有意义和值得信赖。在此过程中，该研究通过强调用户自己如何描述信任、访问和日常互动中的故障，补充了对聊天机器人准确性和安全性的受控评估，强调了未来设计、监管和政策工作必须关注基础设施和界面层面问题的领域。Viewed together, these findings frame AI healthcare chatbots as infrastructures that often fail across access, interaction, and governance dimensions. This perspective shifts attention beyond technical performance to the broader conditions under which such systems become usable, meaningful, and trustworthy in practice. In doing so, the study complements controlled evaluations of chatbot accuracy and safety by stressing how users themselves describe breakdowns in trust, access, and everyday interaction, highlighting areas where future work on design, regulation, and policy must be attentive to infrastructural as well as interface-level concerns.

这些基础设施崩溃在卫生支持方面具有特别重要的意义。这些系统的用户经常寻求心理健康支持，例如焦虑、压力或情绪困扰。在这种情况下，访问、交互或计费方面的失败不仅仅是技术上的不便。相反，他们可能会打断或破坏寻求支持的尝试，有时是在脆弱性加剧的时刻（Vaidyam et al., 2019; Haque & Rubya, 2023）。描述沮丧、困惑或恶化情绪状态的用户帐户指出了敏感使用环境中不可靠或不一致的系统的潜在后果。These infrastructural breakdowns carry particular significance in the context of health support. Users of these systems are often seeking assistance for mental health support such as anxiety, stress, or emotional distress. 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

这项研究依赖于自我报告的应用商店评论，这可能无法完全代表更广泛的用户群体，并且依赖于解释性分析选择（例如主题建模、类别聚合），这些选择可能会在替代规范下产生略有不同的主题结构。基于关键字的搜索仅捕获对 SPR 问题的明确提及，可能会遗漏更多隐含的不适形式，并且分析未考虑可能影响用户如何体验这些系统的语言或人口统计变化。未来的工作可以将大规模审查分析与定性方法（访谈或调查）结合起来，将观察到的模式更直接地与用户福祉和信任联系起来，跨地区、语言或健康领域进行比较分析，并开发更多上下文敏感的方法来检测 SPR 问题，为设计和政策干预提供信息。This study relies on self-reported app-store reviews, which may not fully represent broader user populations, and on interpretive analytical choices (e.g., topic modeling, category aggregation) that could yield slightly different thematic structures under alternative specifications. The keyword-based search only captures only explicit mentions of SPR concerns, potentially missing more implicit forms of discomfort, and the analysis does not account for linguistic or demographic variation that may shape how users experience these systems. Future work could combine large-scale review analysis with qualitative methods (interviews or surveys) to link observed patterns more directly to user well-being and trust, conduct comparative analyses across regions, languages, or health domains, and develop more context-sensitive approaches for detecting SPR concerns to inform design and policy interventions.

结论CONCLUSION

这项研究通过对应用商店评论的大规模分析，调查了人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人的用户评论。我们展示了用户如何描述访问和可靠使用这些系统的障碍，强调了完全阻止交互的付费墙、不稳定和登录失败。我们还调查了用户如何描述交互质量和感知有用性，识别有限的情感支持、脚本化或“虚假”人工智能行为以及即使可以访问也难以持续使用的界面带来的挫败感。最后，我们记录了用户如何体验信任、公平、支持结构和明确的隐私/安全问题，计费纠纷和薄弱的客户支持削弱了对服务的信心，一小部分但重要的评论表达了对数据收集、跟踪和滥用的强烈担忧。This study examined user reviews of AI healthcare chatbots through large-scale analysis of app-store reviews. We showed how users describe barriers to accessing and reliably using these systems, highlighting paywalls, instability, and login failures that prevent interaction altogether. We also investigated how users characterise interaction quality and perceived usefulness, identifying frustrations with limited emotional support, scripted or “fake” AI behaviour, and interfaces that make ongoing use difficult even when access is available. Finally, we documented how users experience trust, fairness, support structures, and explicit privacy/security concerns, with billing disputes and weak customer support eroding confidence in the service and a small but important subset of reviews expressing strong worries about data collection, tracking, and misuse.

总而言之，这些发现将人工智能医疗保健聊天机器人视为信息基础设施，其价值取决于访问条件、交互质量和治理实践的一致性。应用商店评论成为观察这些动态的有用镜头，捕捉系统在日常环境中而不是仅在受控环境中的评估方式。随着人工智能医疗保健工具的不断发展，确保它们作为可靠、公平和值得信赖的信息系统运行，不仅需要关注对话性能，还需要关注计费透明度、支持流程以及强大、可理解的数据治理实践。Taken together, these findings frame AI healthcare chatbots as information infrastructures whose value depends on the alignment of access conditions, interaction quality, and governance practices. App-store reviews emerge as a useful lens on these dynamics, capturing how systems are evaluated in everyday contexts rather than only in controlled settings. As AI-enabled healthcare tools continue to evolve, ensuring that they function as reliable, equitable, and trustworthy information systems will require attention not only to conversational performance, but also to billing transparency, support processes, and robust, comprehensible data-governance practices.
