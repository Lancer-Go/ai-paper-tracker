用于泛化脑电图表征学习的多模态预训练Multimodal Pretraining for Generalizable EEG Representation Learning

Targol Bakhtiarvand, Jugal Kalita, Adham Atyabi Department of Computer Science, University of Colorado Colorado Springs, Colorado Springs, CO, USA {tbakhtia,jkalita,aatyabi}@uccs.edu
摘要Abstract

用于癫痫的脑电图 (EEG) 模型通常仅限于特定的数据集和任务。这种有限的方法可能使得在不同数据集或各种情况下应用这些模型变得具有挑战性。然而，最近对基础模型和自我监督学习的研究表明，适应性强的脑电图骨干可以支持一系列脑电图相关任务。在这项研究中，我们开发了一种多模态 EEG 基础模型，该模型结合了基于 Mamba 架构的原始信号编码器、用于时频数据的 Vision Transformer (ViT) 式编码器和用于文本的轻量级编码器，所有这些都在共享嵌入空间内。预训练过程依赖于多种创新技术，例如掩模建模、跨视图对比对齐和时间一致性损失。这些方法旨在创建丰富的、与癫痫发作相关的表示，而不需要标记数据。为了评估我们预训练模型的有效性和泛化性，我们在规范的 CHB-MIT 癫痫检测基准和其他癫痫检测数据集上对其进行了微调，并进行了广泛的实验来比较不同的模型变体。在标准 CHB-MIT 拆分中，我们最好的单一模型达到了 0.874 的 AUROC，而集成变体达到了 0.878 AUROC，代表了该基准测试中最先进的性能。除了标准的训练-测试分割之外，我们还评估了留一受试者排除 (LOSO) 协议下的表现，该协议在之前的脑电图癫痫发作建模工作中很少报道，并突显了独立于患者的癫痫发作检测的难度，19 名受试者的平均 LOSO 平衡准确度为 0.558。在数据集和评估设置中，我们的多模态基础模型实现了强大的癫痫发作检测并直接适应新的癫痫发作检测场景，同时还支持可解释的癫痫发作定位。这些发现凸显了脑电图特定基础模型作为多功能工具的潜力，不仅可以重复用于癫痫发作检测，还可以更广泛地分析与癫痫发作相关的脑电图数据。Electroencephalography (EEG) models used for epilepsy are often limited to specific datasets and tasks. This limited approach can make it challenging to apply these models across different datasets or in various situations. However, recent studies in foundation models and self-supervised learning suggest that an adaptable EEG backbone could support a range of EEG related tasks. In this study, we have developed a multimodal EEG foundation model that combines a raw signal encoder based on the Mamba architecture, a Vision Transformer (ViT)-style encoder for time-frequency data, and a lightweight encoder for text, all within a shared embedding space. The pretraining process relies on several innovative techniques, such as masked modeling, cross-view contrastive alignment, and temporal consistency losses. These methods are designed to create rich, seizure-relevant representations without requiring labeled data. To assess the efficacy and generalization of our pretrained model, we fine-tuned it on the canonical CHB-MIT seizure detection benchmark and additional seizure detection datasets, and conducted extensive experiments comparing different model variants. On the standard CHB-MIT split, our best single model achieved an AUROC of 0.874, and an ensemble variant reached 0.878 AUROC, representing state-of-the-art performance on this benchmark. In addition to standard train-test splits, we evaluated performance under a leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) protocol, which is rarely reported in prior EEG seizure modeling work and highlights the difficulty of patient-independent seizure detection, with a mean LOSO balanced accuracy of 0.558 across 19 subjects. Across datasets and evaluation settings, our multimodal foundation model enabled robust seizure detection and straightforward adaptation to new seizure detection scenarios, while also supporting interpretable seizure localization. These findings highlight the potential of EEG-specific foundation models as versatile tools that can be reused not just for seizure detection but also for broader analyses of EEG data related to seizures.

I 简介INTRODUCTION

脑电图 (EEG) 记录可以对大脑活动进行非侵入性高时间分辨率测量，但难以建模。这些信号的信噪比较低，容易出现生理和环境伪影，并且在受试者、记录蒙太奇、范式和记录设备之间存在很大差异Electroencephalography (EEG) recordings enable non‑invasive high‑temporal‑resolution measurements of brain activity but are difficult to model. The signals have low signal‑to‑noise ratio, are prone to physiological and environmental artifacts, and differ substantially between subjects, recording montages, paradigms, and recording devices[25]。公共癫痫数据集的采样率、通道布局、参考方案和注释质量的变化损害了学习模型的普遍性和可重复性. Variations in sampling rates, channel layouts, referencing schemes, and annotation quality across public epilepsy datasets impairs the generalizability and reproducibility of learned models[20]。受监督的脑电图编码器还依赖于昂贵的专家注释，并且经常学习不能推广到新任务和数据集的模型. Supervised EEG encoders also rely on costly expert annotations and often learn models that do not generalize to new tasks and datasets[10]。这些挑战推动了最近对大规模自监督脑电图基础模型的研究，这些模型使用基于掩模的重建和变压器主干对数百到数千个受试者进行预训练. These challenges have motivated recent work on large‑scale, self‑supervised EEG foundation models that pretrain on hundreds to thousands of subjects using mask‑based reconstruction and transformer backbones[26].

癫痫发作检测是在脑电图的发作（和近发作）窗口上进行的，其中已经存在明显的电图异常。因此，典型的任务涉及实时对短窗口进行癫痫发作与非癫痫发作的二元分类Seizure detection is performed on ictal (and near‑ictal) windows of EEG where there is already obvious electrographic abnormality. Typical tasks therefore involve binary classification of short windows as seizure vs. non‑seizure in real time[18]。性能通过灵敏度、假阳性率和检测延迟来衡量。最先进的头皮脑电图临床系统可在监测单元环境中实现约 75% 至 90% 的灵敏度和每小时 0.1 至 5 次误报. Performance is measured in terms of sensitivity, false‑positive rate, and detection delay. State-of-the-art clinical systems for scalp EEG achieve about 75 to 90% sensitivity and 0.1 to 5 false alarms per hour in the monitoring‑unit environment[2]。然而，这些表现数据主要来自不使用严格的留一受试者排除（LOSO）评估或真正的跨受试者泛化测试的研究，众所周知，这些测试可以对未见过的患者的表现做出更真实的估计. However, these performance figures largely come from studies that do not use strict leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) evaluation or true across‑subject generalization tests, which are known to give more realistic estimates of performance on unseen patients[19].

检测仍不完善。短暂、低幅度、局灶性或被伪影掩盖的癫痫发作可能不会被发现，而发作间期异常和伪影则是大多数误报的原因Detection is still imperfect. Seizures that are short, low-amplitude, focal, or obscured by artifacts may go undetected, while interictal abnormalities and artifacts account for most false positives[2]。此外，即使对于最近的深度学习方法，也缺乏对患者和现实的长期记录的模型泛化. Furthermore, model generalization to patients and realistic long‑term recordings is lacking, even for recent deep learning methods[13].

多模态表示学习将原始脑电图、时频视图（例如 CWT/STFT 频谱图）和/或文本信息（标签/临床描述）一起建模，从而允许检测器利用互补的发作特征。与单视图方法相比，通过双分支或多流架构将原始脑电图与类频谱图特征融合的联合模型始终能够实现更高的准确性、灵敏度和特异性Multimodal representation learning models raw EEG, time-frequency views (e.g. CWT/STFT spectrograms), and/or textual information (labels/clinical descriptions) together, allowing the detector to leverage complementary ictal signatures. Joint models that fuse raw EEG with spectrogram‑like features via dual‑branch or multi‑stream architectures consistently achieve higher accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity compared to single‑view approaches[3]。最近，CLIP 式脑电图文本模型表明，在联合潜在空间中对齐脑电图和文本可以产生鲁棒、高性能的癫痫检测器，可跨数据集推广. Recently, CLIP-style EEG-text models have shown that aligning EEG and text in a joint latent space produces robust, high‑performing seizure detectors generalizable across datasets[28]。总之，这些发现表明多模式学习可以提高灵敏度，同时减少基于脑电图的癫痫发作检测中的误报. Together, these findings suggest multimodal learning can increase sensitivity while decreasing false alarms in EEG‑based seizure detection[5].

这项工作引入了用于癫痫检测的特定领域基础模型，该模型共同从原始脑电图、基于 CWT 的时频尺度图和相关文本信息（标签/报告）中学习。与之前经过 LOSO 验证的纯信号系统不同This work introduces a domain‑specific foundation model for seizure detection that jointly learns from raw EEG, CWT‑based time-frequency scalograms, and associated textual information (labels/reports). V-B 评估协议Evaluation Protocol

我们通过三个互补协议对模型进行基准测试，并使用越来越多的数据来系统地理解泛化性，从零样本跨主题转移到完整的主题内适应。We benchmark our model across three complementary protocols with increasing amounts of data to systematically understand generalization, from zero-shot cross-subject transfer to complete within-subject adaptation.

留一主题（LOSO）：Leave-One-Subject-Out (LOSO):最严格、最符合临床实际的方案。对于每个测试对象，所有剩余的对象构成训练集，并且独立保留的对象作为早期停止的验证集。该模型在训练期间不接收来自测试对象的数据。该协议提供了对完全未见过的患者的跨受试者泛化的直接估计，这可以作为评估临床可部署性的适当基准。The strictest and most clinically realistic protocol. For each test subject, all remaining subjects form the training set, and an independent held-out subject serves as the validation set for early stopping. The model receives no data from the test subject during training. This protocol provides a direct estimate of cross-subject generalization to completely unseen patients, which serves as an appropriate benchmark for evaluating clinical deployability.

This involves using subjects 1-19 for training, subjects 20 and 21 for validation, and subjects 22 and 23 for testing. 七VII讨论DISCUSSION

我们对多模态脑电图基础模型进行了基准测试 指导未来癫痫检测工作的三个协议。 对 19 名受试者进行的严格 LOSO 评估表明 跨受试者癫痫发作泛化仍然具有挑战性 （平均 BAcc 0.558，平均 TPR 0.570），确认高 小儿癫痫的受试者间变异性；因此我们 提倡学科层面的评估而不是固定的跨学科评估 分裂。关于五个科目 (chb01–chb05)，科目内 使用 60% 的主题数据进行微调提高了平均平衡 准确度达到 0.643（比 LOSO +0.174），证实 针对特定主题的适应可显着提高性能。 少样本校准仅产生 5-40% 的主题数据 边际收益（平均 BAcc 0.490–0.506），表明 小校准窗口不足以捕获癫痫发作 形态多样性和大量标记数据 有效适应所需要的。参数高效调整 仅 7.5% 的参数达到了最先进的水平 性能，建议预训练的表示 很好地转移到脑电图癫痫发作检测；积极的微调 存在灾难性遗忘的风险。最后，GradCAM 和开始 本地化分析证实预测是有根据的 在已知的发作期脑电图生物标志物中，以及该模型的 45 秒 对受试者 chb22 的早期检测显示出癫痫发作的可能性 预期，尽管这必须在更多受试者中得到验证。We benchmark a multimodal EEG foundation model across three protocols to guide future work in seizure detection. A strict LOSO evaluation across 19 subjects demonstrates that cross-subject seizure generalization remains challenging (mean BAcc 0.558, mean TPR 0.570), confirming high inter-subject variability in pediatric epilepsy; we therefore advocate subject-level evaluation over fixed cross-subject splits. On five subjects (chb01–chb05), within-subject fine-tuning using 60% of subject data raises mean balanced accuracy to 0.643 (+0.174 over LOSO), confirming that subject-specific adaptation substantially improves performance. Few-shot calibration with 5–40% of subject data yields only marginal gains (mean BAcc 0.490–0.506), suggesting that small calibration windows are insufficient to capture seizure morphology diversity and that substantial labeled data is required for effective adaptation. Parameter-efficient tuning of only 7.5% of parameters achieves state-of-the-art performance, suggesting the pretrained representations transfer well to EEG seizure detection; aggressive fine-tuning risks catastrophic forgetting. Finally, GradCAM and onset localization analyses confirm that predictions are grounded in known ictal EEG biomarkers, and the model’s 45-second early detection on subject chb22 shows promise for seizure anticipation, though this must be validated across more subjects.

八VIII结论CONCLUSION

我们提出了一个多模态脑电图基础模型，该模型联合 从原始 EEG、CWT 尺度图和文本中学习，实现 最先进的平衡精度 (0.781) 和 AUC-PR (0.709) 在 BrainRVQ 固定分割协议下，只有 7.5% 参数微调，AUC-PR 比之前提高了 52.5% 大脑RVQ。我们对 19 个 CHB-MIT 科目进行的 LOSO 评估 构成第一个跨主题报告的下界 该基准的 EEG 基础模型（平均 BAcc 0.558， 平均 TPR 0.570）。限制包括：学科内和 仅涵盖 chb01 至 chb05 的少样本实验；依赖于 一般脑电图知识而不是患者特定的临床知识 报告；并对单个受试者进行早期检测验证 (chb22)，需要对实时部署进行更广泛的评估。 未来的工作将把特定主题的适应扩展到所有 24 主题并结合元学习以改进少数镜头 校准。We present a multimodal EEG foundation model that jointly learns from raw EEG, CWT scalograms, and text, achieving state-of-the-art balanced accuracy (0.781) and AUC-PR (0.709) under the BrainRVQ fixed-split protocol with only 7.5% of parameters fine-tuned, a 52.5% AUC-PR improvement over BrainRVQ. Our LOSO evaluation across 19 CHB-MIT subjects constitutes the first cross-subject lower bound reported for an EEG foundation model on this benchmark (mean BAcc 0.558, mean TPR 0.570). Limitations include: within-subject and few-shot experiments covering only chb01 to chb05; reliance on general EEG knowledge rather than patient-specific clinical reports; and early detection validated on a single subject (chb22), requiring broader evaluation for real-time deployment. Future work will extend subject-specific adaptation to all 24 subjects and incorporate meta-learning for improved few-shot calibration.
