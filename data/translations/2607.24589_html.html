人工智能和创新生态系统：演进发展、挑战和未来方向Artificial Intelligence and Innovation Ecosystem: Evolutionary Developments, Challenges, and Future Directions

 Zhimin Zhang Corresponding author (zhangzhimin@uest.edu.gr). 摘要Abstract

创新生态系统（IE）的发展呈现出经济融合、协同发展、共享成果的新范式。人工智能的兴起，显着加速了全球数字化、信息化、智能化进程。探索人工智能如何利用固有特性影响IE的发展轨迹是一个值得进一步研究的课题。鉴于人工智能在 IE 中的重要性和作用日益增强，本文分析了这种新形式，研究了人工智能对 IE 的独特贡献及其潜在挑战。首先，本文综合了围绕IE的概念框架，将其分解为物理、社会和思维空间的表现形式。此外，从空间角度引入了人工智能IE（AIIE）的概念，并探讨了AI对IE的贡献特征。随后，本文采用进化的视角分析了人工智能在AIIE不同发展时期所提供的角色。文章进而验证了AIIE定义的可行性、有效性和合理性，并结合企业发展实例从演化的角度分析了AIIE的发展。最后，在承认人工智能固有局限性的基础上，本文从四个角度审视了AIIE未来面临的潜在挑战，旨在为AIIE的进一步发展寻找新的研究途径。The development of the Innovative Ecosystem (IE) presents a new paradigm for economic integration, collaborative advancement, and shared achievements. The rise of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has significantly accelerated the global processes of digitization, informatization, and intelligence. Exploring how AI can leverage inherent characteristics to influence the development trajectory of IE is a topic that warrants further investigation. Given AI’s increasing prominence and role within IE, the paper analyzes this new form, examining both AI’s unique contributions to IE and its potential challenges. Firstly, the paper synthesizes the conceptual frameworks surrounding IE, decomposing them into manifestations in physical, social, and thinking spaces. Furthermore, the concept of Artificial Intelligence IE (AIIE) is introduced from a spatial perspective, with an exploration of the characteristics AI contributes to IE. Subsequently, the paper employs an evolutionary perspective to analyze the roles provided by AI during different development periods of AIIE. The paper then verifies the feasibility, effectiveness, and rationality of the AIIE’s definition and analyzes AIIE development from an evolutionary perspective using enterprise development examples. Finally, acknowledging AI’s inherent limitations, the paper examines potential challenges facing AIIE in the future from four perspectives, aiming to identify new research avenues for the further development of AIIE.

1 简介Introduction

创新生态系统（IE）是一个包括政府、学术机构、创新企业等参与者以及文化、法律、教育等外部环境的先进网络，旨在促进技术和商业模式创新。 这些参与者与外部环境互动、相互影响、通过资源共享、知识交流、协作合作促进创新。Innovation Ecosystem (IE) is an advanced network that includes participants such as governments, academic institutions, and innovation companies, as well as external environments like culture, law, and education, all aimed at fostering technological and business model innovation. These participants interact with the external environment, influencing each other and facilitating innovation through resource sharing, knowledge exchange, and collaborative cooperation (Mercier-Laurent (2020)）。 随着全球化的深入，IE为全球可持续发展做出了巨大贡献。 此外，IE的不断发展也让许多参与者认识到，在当前趋势下，合作与竞争的平衡可以推动互利创新和价值提升。). As globalization deepens, IE has significantly contributed to sustainable global development. Furthermore, the ongoing evolution of IE has led many participants to recognize that, in light of current trends, a balance of cooperation and competition can drive mutually beneficial innovation and value enhancement.

信息技术特别是人工智能的进步，加速了全球数字化、虚拟化、智能化进程，促进物理空间、社会空间、思维空间、网络空间的泛在连接和深度融合。The advancement of information technology, particularly Artificial Intelligence (AI), has accelerated the global processes of digitization, virtualization, and intelligence, fostering the ubiquitous connection and deep integration of physical space, social space, thinking space, and cyberspace (Ning et al. (2018)）。 从预设的规则和逻辑推理到强大的计算和自学习能力，人工智能不仅增强了数字流程的自动化，还深刻影响了医疗、教育、金融、交通等领域。 对人工智能的持续关注和探索将持续推动技术突破和应用拓展。 此外，人工智能还改善了跨领域协作，优化了需求分配的供应链并增强了国际互动。 因此，人工智能和全球化之间的相互作用极大地促进了经济一体化和市场扩张。). From preset rules and logical reasoning to powerful computing and self-learning capabilities, AI has not only enhanced the automation of digital processes but also profoundly impacted fields such as healthcare, education, finance, and transportation. Ongoing attention and exploration in AI will continue to drive technological breakthroughs and expand applications. Additionally, AI has improved cross-domain collaboration, optimized supply chains for demand allocation, and enhanced international interactions. Consequently, the interplay between AI and globalization has significantly contributed to economic integration and market expansion.

人工智能带来的前所未有的进步和变化，为IE的数字化转型奠定了坚实的基础，推动IE的快速发展，促进协同发展。 随着人工智能在 IE 中的作用不断扩大，参与者的决策将越来越多地受到人工智能的影响，有可能改变两者的主导地位。 然而，如果这项技术得不到有效利用或控制，IE可能无法按照预想发展。 因此，一些研究开始探讨人工智能在工业企业中的作用，特别是其在辅助决策、跨领域协作和价值共创方面的显着贡献。The unprecedented progress and changes brought by AI have laid a strong foundation for IE’s digital transformation, driving its rapid growth and facilitating collaborative development. As AI’s role within IE continues to expand, participants’ decisions will increasingly be shaped by AI, potentially altering the dominant positions of both. However, if this technology is not effectively utilized or controlled, IE may fail to develop as envisioned. Consequently, some studies have begun to explore AI’s role in IE, particularly its remarkable contributions to assisted decision-making, cross-domain collaboration, and value co-creation.

人工智能辅助IE的研究仍处于起步阶段，缺乏统一、公认、全面的定义，现有文献对此主题也不一致（Studies on AI-assisted IE are still in their infancy, lacking a unified, recognized, and comprehensive definition, and the existing literature on this topic is also inconsistent (Roundy and Asllani (2024)）。 这种模糊性阻碍了识别新兴工业互联网特征的努力，也阻碍了有效描述其如何在快速迭代和发展的人工智能时代为全球可持续发展做出独特贡献的努力。 此外，目前的研究往往从参与者（包括外部环境）构成、资源共享、创新合作等角度探讨人工智能对具有一定发展规模的工业企业的影响和作用。 但不同发展时期的IE在基本条件、环境因素、任务导向、价值目标等方面存在差异。 因此，当前的研究往往忽视了IE发展不同时期人工智能需求的动态变化。). 2 AIIE的定义及特点Definition and Characteristics of AIIE

2.1 AIIE的定义Definition of AIIE

IE类似于一个自然生态系统，强调各个参与者之间的相互依赖，通过合作、共享和交流来通过进化来适应技术进步、市场变化和政策调整。 在这种相互依存的关系中，以实现最终目标为指导的合作和良性竞争至关重要。 受领域、场景、范围等因素的影响，IE催生了几个相关概念，包括商业生态系统（IE is analogous to a natural ecosystem, highlighting the interdependence of various participants who cooperate, share, and communicate to adapt to technological advancements, market changes, and policy adjustments through evolution. In such interdependent relationships, cooperation and healthy competition are crucial, guided by ultimate goals of achievement. Influenced by factors such as field, scenario, and scope, IE has given rise to several related concepts, including business ecosystem (Adner (2006)）、工业4.0 IE（), industry 4.0 IE (Matt et al. (2021); Benitez et al. (2020)）、智能产品IE（), and smart product IE (Kahle et al. (2020)).

人工智能对于数据分析、预测建模和运营优化至关重要，是数字化转型的强大驱动力。 在人工智能技术的影响下，网络-物理-社会系统（CPSS）等系统级概念（AI is crucial for data analysis, predictive modeling, and operational optimization, serving as a powerful driving force of digital transformation. Under the influence of AI technology, system-level concepts such as Cyber-Physical-Social Systems (CPSS) (Zeng et al. (2020)）、一般网络空间（GC）（), General Cyberspace (GC) (Ning et al. (2018)) 和 PhiNet of Things (PoT) (), and PhiNet of Things (PoT) (Ning et al. (2020)）相继提出，强调通过提高生产力来优化和改进传统系统。综合分析这些概念，凸显出以人工智能为代表的网络空间对传统物理空间、社会空间乃至思维空间的深刻影响。物理空间是空间因素的基础，主要由空间实体（如人、物）和相应的环境组成。社会空间考虑实体的社会属性、行为和相互关系。因此，思维空间主要处理相关的大脑活动或观察思维领域的反应。) have been successively proposed to emphasize the optimization and improvement of traditional systems through enhanced productivity. A comprehensive analysis of these concepts underscores the profound impact of cyberspace, as represented by AI, on traditional physical, social, and even thinking spaces. Physical space is the foundation of spatial factors, mainly composed of spatial entities (such as people and objects) and corresponding environments. Social space considers the social attributes, behaviors, and interrelationships of entities. Consequently, thinking space primarily processes related brain activities or observes reactions in the realm of thinking.

IE作为生态系统的表现形式，也可以在三个空间中体现，并会受到面向AI的网络空间的影响。对于IE来说，它在物理空间的表现是参与者的组合；在社会空间中，是合作竞争关系的表现；而在思维空间中，则表现为终极目标。在此分类的基础上，本文对IE现有的概念进行了分解，总结如表As a manifestation of the ecosystem, IE can also be represented in three spaces and will be influenced by AI-oriented cyberspace. For IE, its manifestation in physical space is compositions of participants; in social space, it is the manifestation of cooperative-competitive relationships; and in thinking space, it is manifested as ultimate goals. 2.1.1 物理空间参与者的构成Compositions of Participants in Physical Space

参与者定义IE的构成和规模，代表IE在物理空间的具体表现。 具有项目经验的初创企业和科技公司是提升行业竞争力、促进信息资源交流的重要组成部分。 为提升创新能力，具有前沿知识和探索精神的行业研究机构、教育机构和高等教育机构为IE发展注入活力。Participants define the composition and scale of IE, representing IE’s concrete manifestation in the physical space. Startups and technology companies with project experience are vital components that enhance industry competitiveness and facilitate information resource exchange. To boost innovation capabilities, industry research institutes, educational institutions, and higher education entities with cutting-edge knowledge and an exploratory spirit invigorate the development of IE.

鉴于人工智能的颠覆性和不确定性，有效的监管对于保持工业互联网的活力和健康运行至关重要。 政府和决策机构在提供此类监管支持方面发挥着至关重要的作用。 与强制监管相比，动态监管和沙盒监管方式为IE内的AI开发提供了更大的灵活性（Given AI’s disruptive and uncertain nature, effective regulation is essential for maintaining IE’s vitality and healthy operation. Governments and decision-making bodies play a crucial role in providing such regulatory support. Compared to mandatory regulation, dynamic and sandbox regulatory ways offer greater flexibility for AI development within IE (Fenwick et al. (2018)).

为确保创新研究和技术开发等关键活动的成功实施，风险投资、私募股权和天使投资是重要的资金来源。 此外，孵化器还可以通过提供场地、基础设施和服务支持，帮助降低创新成本，促进成果转化，提高系统效率。To ensure the successful execution of key activities like innovative research and technological development, venture capital, private equity, and angel investments serve as important funding sources. Additionally, incubators can help reduce innovation costs, facilitate achievement transformation, and enhance system efficiency by providing venue, infrastructure, and service support.

最后，目标用户也是 IE 的重要组成部分。 他们的经验和反馈可以极大地影响IE的未来方向，帮助生态系统调整和做出符合市场和社会发展需求的决策。Finally, target users are also a critical component of IE. Their experiences and feedback can significantly influence IE’s future direction, aiding the ecosystem in adjusting and making decisions that align with market and social development needs.

通过人工智能的加入，该技术可以充当决策者或技术顾问，协助各种决策过程，而不是仅限于物理空间中的人类参与者。 通过利用人工智能技术，IE可以处理和分析海量数据，在发展趋势、资源分配和个性化推荐等方面提供有价值的见解。 结合这些建议，政府和机构等参与者可以做出更明智、更全面的发展决策。With the addition of AI, this technology can act as a decision-maker or technical advisor, assisting in various decision-making processes rather than being limited to human participants in the physical space. 3.3 AIIE成熟期表现The Performance of AIIE in the Mature Period

经过扩张式增长，AIIE在行业内的地位和声誉已经稳定，规模、关系、流程都达到了相对稳定的状态。 AIIE已经开始进入成熟发展期，这是进化角度的最高形态。这个时期，AIIE对整个海量生态系统的管理不应松懈，而应立足现有资源，明确未来发展愿景，不断实现自我更新，而不是衰落甚至消亡。因此，AIIE成熟期的工作是一个自我优化的过程。After expansionary growth, AIIE’s position and reputation have stabilized within the industry, with its scale, relationships, and processes reaching a relatively stable state. AIIE has begun to enter a mature period of development, which is the highest form in evolutionary perspective. At this period, AIIE should not slack off in managing the entire massive ecosystem but should build on existing resources, clarify a clear vision for future development, and continuously achieve self-renewal rather than decline or even death. Therefore, AIIE’s work in the mature period is a self-optimizing process.

人工智能是AIIE的核心技术，需要它的引领来完成整个AIIE的控制和管理。但随着生态系统不断升级、规模扩大，必须寻求高效的管理方法。集中管理可以将人工智能整合到单个或几个参与者中，进行统一管理和部署。这种方式可以实现资源的高效整合，通过标准化和统一规范促进协作，并集中安全和风险控制。然而，随着AIIE规模不断扩大，中心化管理的弊端也开始显现，包括单点故障风险、权力垄断、灵活性降低、资源失衡等。因此，凭借人工智能的分布式、去中心化、分级化的管理能力，需要认真审视成熟的AIIE管理模式，以维持整个生态系统的去中心化领导。AI is the core technology in AIIE, therefore its leadership is needed to complete the control and management of the entire AIIE. However, as the ecosystem continues to upgrade and expand in scale, efficient management methods must be sought. Centralized management can consolidate AI across a single or a few participants for unified management and deployment. This way can achieve efficient integration of resources, facilitate collaboration through standardization and unified norms, and centralize security and risk control. However, as the scale of AIIE continues to expand, the drawbacks of centralized management also begin to emerge, including single point of failure risk, power monopoly, reduced flexibility, and resource imbalance. Therefore, with the distributed, decentralized, and hierarchical management capabilities of AI, it is necessary to carefully examine the mature AIIE management model to maintain the decentralized leadership of the entire ecosystem.

近10年来，Owkin的发展无论是在空间构成还是演化过程上都与论文提出的AIIE表现出了良好的重叠性。一方面，Owkin的发展表明，在当前的技术发展路径下，可以有效构建以人工智能为核心的生态系统，并在增长过程中达到相对稳定的状态。另一方面，Owkin的例子也证明了论文中提出的AIIE概念的合理性，因为人工智能将对参与者的构成、合作竞争关系以及最终目标产生深远的影响。此外，人工智能在生态系统发展的不同时期所发挥的作用也有所不同，这为不同时期控制AIIE提供了有力的基础。Over the past 10 years, the development of Owkin has shown a good overlap with the AIIE proposed in the paper, both in terms of spatial composition and evolutionary process. On the one hand, Owkin’s development demonstrates that an AI-centered ecosystem can be effectively constructed and reach a relatively stable state during growth under the current technological development path. On the other hand, Owkin’s example also demonstrates the rationality of the conceptual AIIE proposed in the paper, as AI will have a profound impact on the compositions of participants, cooperative-competitive relationships, and ultimate goals. In addition, the role played by AI varies at different periods of the ecosystem’s development, providing a strong basis for controlling AIIE at various periods.

5 AIIE未来发展方向Future Development Direction of AIIE

人工智能可以发挥其先天优势，在AIIE各发展时期的演进中发挥积极作用。 但由于技术能力、学习方法、训练模式等因素的制约，人工智能也给生态系统的发展带来了可预见的风险。 这些风险主要体现在四个关键领域：可解释性、节能性、公平性、和谐性。 应对这些挑战对于推动AIIE的未来发展至关重要。AI can leverage its inherent advantages to play a positive role in the evolution of AIIE across various periods of its development. However, due to constraints imposed by factors such as technological capabilities, learning methodologies, and training modes, AI also introduces foreseeable risks to the ecosystem’s development. These risks primarily manifest in four key areas: interpretability, energy conservation, fairness, and harmony. Addressing these challenges will be crucial for advancing the future development of AIIE.

5.0.1 AIIE的可解释性发展The Interpretability Development of AIIE

人工智能通常被认为是一个黑匣子，其内部配置的变化主要由新的和改进的数据驱动（AI is often perceived as a black-box, with changes to its internal configurations primarily driven by new and improved data (de Lemos and Grześ (2019)）。 这种模式使得技术不好解释和透明，结果的说服力会降低，甚至难以理解（). This mode makes the technology not well interpretable and transparent, and the persuasiveness of the results will be reduced, even difficult to understand (Mercier-Laurent (2020)）。 此外，这一特性对于容易发生变化的生态系统不利，例如概念漂移。 尽管解释性人工智能（XAI）在学术研究中获得了关注，但大多数人工智能解决方案仍然以黑匣子的方式运行。). Furthermore, this characteristic is disadvantageous for ecosystems prone to change, such as concept drift. Although Explanatory AI (XAI) has gained traction in academic research, most AI solutions continue to operate as black-boxes.

随着AIIE的发展，参与者及其合作竞争关系将不断适应内部和外部的需求。 因此，增强AIIE的整体透明度和可解释性对其健康发展至关重要。 透明的决策过程使利益相关者能够了解决策是如何制定的，从而减少误解、增强信任并加强协作。As AIIE evolves, the participants and their cooperative-competitive relationships will continuously adapt to internal and external demands. Therefore, enhancing the overall transparency and interpretability of AIIE is crucial for its healthy development. A transparent decision-making process allows stakeholders to understand how decisions are made, thereby reducing misunderstandings, enhancing trust, and strengthening collaboration.

提高透明度和可解释性有助于跟踪、检测和解决潜在的偏见和歧视，促进人工智能决策的公平性、准确性和客观性。 此外，这些增强功能使参与者能够更好地控制、监控和分析人工智能行为，确保AIIE运行的整体稳定性、可靠性和安全性，使生态系统能够遵守合理的监管要求，并避免潜在的法律风险。 这一改进对于 AIIE 来说意义重大，因为任何危险的漏洞或异常都可能严重影响生态系统，甚至跨越道德和法律界限。Improving transparency and interpretability facilitates tracking, detecting, and addressing potential biases and discrimination, promoting fairness, accuracy, and objectivity in AI decision-making. Additionally, these enhancements empower participants to control better, monitor, and analyze AI behavior, ensuring the overall stability, reliability, and security of AIIE operation, enabling the ecosystem to comply with reasonable regulatory requirements, and avoiding potential legal risks. This improvement is significant for AIIE, as any dangerous vulnerability or anomaly could seriously affect the ecosystem, even crossing ethical and legal boundaries.

此外，增加透明度和可解释性可以改善人机融合，从而提高 AIIE 的效率和性能。 尽管人们越来越关注人工智能透明度和可解释性的解决方案，但在 AIIE 这样的大型复杂生态系统中实施这些功能仍然是一个重大挑战。Moreover, increased transparency and interpretability can improve human-machine fusion, thereby enhancing the efficiency and performance of AIIE. While there is growing attention to solutions for AI transparency and interpretability, implementing these capabilities in large and complex ecosystems like AIIE remains a significant challenge.

5.0.2 AIIE节能发展The Energy Conservation Development of AIIE

AIIE要求利用人工智能辅助决策、关系管理、资源整合等任务。虽然自动化程度有了显着提升，但其背后的能源消耗也不容忽视。这个消耗可以从两个方面来分析，即硬件制造消耗和AI运营消耗。AIIE requires the employ of AI to assist in decision-making, relationship management, resource integration, and other tasks. Although there has been a significant improvement in automation, the energy consumption behind it cannot be ignored. This consumption can be analyzed from two aspects, namely hardware manufacturing consumption and AI operation consumption.

从硬件制造的角度来看，依赖AI算力的芯片的生产需要大量的稀土资源和水电能源。例如，单个高端图形处理单元（GPU）的制造过程可能会产生大量碳排放。以AIIE的去中心化运营为例，这种方式要求参与者具备相应的数据处理和管理能力。这一要求显着扩大了AIIE对硬件的需求，同时也加剧了电子垃圾污染问题。此外，高昂的硬件成本也可能使中小型参与者难以承担运营和维护费用。在人工智能的操作层面，通用语言人工智能模型的单次训练可能会导致大量的能源消耗。推理阶段还面临着因算力积累而造成的能源浪费，可能导致水冷系统的建设和水资源的过度消耗。然而，这些人工智能模型是赋予 AIIE 独特创意能力的重要组成部分。From the perspective of hardware manufacturing, the production of chips that rely on AI computing power requires a large amount of rare earth resources and hydropower energy. For example, the manufacturing process of a single high-end Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) may generate significant carbon emissions. Taking AIIE’s decentralized operation as an example, this way requires participants to have corresponding data processing and management capabilities. This requirement has significantly expanded AIIE’s demand for hardware while also exacerbating the problem of electronic waste pollution. Additionally, high hardware costs may also make it difficult for small and medium-sized participants to afford operational and maintenance expenses. At the operational level of AI, a single training session for a general language AI model can result in significant energy consumption. The inference stage also faces energy waste due to the accumulation of computing power, which may lead to the construction of a water cooling system and excessive consumption of water resources. However, these AI models are essential components that give AIIE unique creative capabilities.

在这种情况下，AIIE需要考虑优化硬件制造和人工智能运营的整个生命周期的能源消耗，以实现可持续发展。在硬件制造方面，AIIE可以通过开发低功耗芯片、利用可再生材料进行封装、提高硬件能效来减少制造阶段的碳足迹和成本，从而为中小型参与者提供硬件部署优势。在AI运算方面，AIIE可以通过算法压缩、动态计算分配、清洁能源供应等方法进行创新，既降低了AI运算的能耗，又保持了模型性能。这种硬件与软件的双重创新，有利于实现AIIE的可持续发展，构建节能、绿色的运行环境。In this situation, AIIE needs to consider optimizing energy consumption throughout the entire life-cycle of hardware manufacturing and AI operations to achieve sustainable development. In terms of hardware manufacturing, AIIE can reduce its carbon footprint and costs during the manufacturing phase by developing low-power chips, utilizing renewable materials for packaging, and enhancing hardware energy efficiency, thereby providing hardware deployment advantages for small and medium-sized participants. In terms of AI operation, AIIE can innovate through algorithm compression, dynamic computation allocation, and clean energy supply, among other methods, which not only reduces the energy consumption of AI operations but also maintains model performance. This dual innovation, combining hardware and software, is beneficial for achieving the sustainable development of AIIE and constructing an energy-saving, green operating environment.

5.0.3 AIIE的公平发展The Fair Development of AIIE

AIIE的发展依赖于参与者、社会乃至国际组织等多方利益相关者的支持与协作。 然而，如果人工智能表现出偏见，它可能会产生不公平、极端或错误的结果。 在轻微的情况下，这种情况可能会扰乱 AIIE 的运营并削弱参与者的信心。 在严重的情况下，它可能会引起公众的强烈抗议并跨越道德或法律界限。The development of AIIE relies on the support and collaboration of multiple stakeholders, including participants, society, and even international organizations. However, if AI exhibits biases, it can produce unfair, extreme, or erroneous results. At a mild level, this situation can disrupt AIIE operations and undermine participant confidence. At a severe level, it may provoke public outcry and cross moral or legal boundaries.

尽管人工智能在医疗、金融、教育和法律等关键领域得到应用，但许多公众仍然对其决策能力持怀疑态度，对这项技术缺乏信任。 这种怀疑极大地影响了 AIIE 内部对人工智能的接受度，这对于该技术的更广泛采用至关重要。 如果利益相关者对人工智能抱有偏见或疑虑，人工智能的实施就会受到阻碍，不利于AIIE的和谐发展。Despite AI’s application in critical sectors such as healthcare, finance, education, and law, many members of the public remain skeptical of its decision-making capabilities, lacking trust in the technology. This skepticism significantly impacts the acceptance of AI within AIIE, which is crucial for the technology’s wider adoption. If stakeholders harbor biases or doubts about AI, its implementation will be hindered, jeopardizing the harmonious development of AIIE.

人工智能决策持续存在偏差，会导致创新资源配置不均，进而挫伤参与者的创新积极性，甚至导致相关合作竞争关系破裂，严重影响AIIE的整体有效性和最终目标。 此外，作为社会发展的重要组成部分，AIIE的不公平产出容易引发社会不满和冲突，阻碍公平、开放、包容的创新环境的建立。 这种情况不利于AIIE的长期可持续发展，并可能威胁社会和国家的稳定。Continued biased decision-making by AI can lead to uneven innovation resource allocation, which can dampen the innovation enthusiasm of participants and even cause the breakdown of relevant cooperative-competitive relationships, seriously affecting the overall effectiveness and ultimate goals of AIIE. Moreover, as an essential part of social development, unfair outputs from AIIE can result in social dissatisfaction and conflict, hindering the establishment of a fair, open, and inclusive innovation environment. Such conditions are detrimental to the long-term sustainable development of AIIE and could threaten societal and national stability.

因此，有必要尽可能消除对人工智能的偏见，保证产出的正确性和公平性，促进AIIE的顺利产生、发展、成熟和繁荣。 通过数据多样性、模型一致性、方法可解释性、审计多样性等多种措施，可以保证人工智能的公平性和偏差，进一步促进AIIE的可持续发展和普遍接受。Therefore, it is necessary to eliminate bias against AI as much as possible to ensure the outputs’ correctness and fairness and promote the smooth emergence, development, maturity, and prosperity of AIIE. Through various measures such as data diversity, model consistency, method interpretability, and audit diversity, AI fairness and bias may be ensured, further promoting the sustainable development and universal acceptance of AIIE.

5.0.4 AIIE和谐发展The Harmonious Development of AIIE

AIIE的出现和发展给就业市场带来了重大变化，既带来了新的挑战，也带来了新的机遇。 人工智能的集成，特别是通过提高自动化程度，已逐渐取代了工业工程中的许多传统角色，例如风险评估者、数据收集者和技术评估者。 这种趋势给这些职位的参与者带来了严重的失业风险，特别是那些具有一般技能水平的人，他们可能会发现适应 AIIE 中与人工智能一起工作的新协作需求具有挑战性。The emergence and development of AIIE have significantly changed to the job market, presenting both new challenges and opportunities. The integration of AI, particularly through increased automation, has gradually replaced many traditional roles in industrial engineering, such as risk assessors, data collectors, and technical evaluators. This trend poses a severe unemployment risk for participants in these positions, especially those with general skill levels who may find it challenging to adapt to the new collaborative demands of working alongside AI in AIIE.

这种需求的转变要求参与者不断提升技能，以便在 AIIE 不断变化的工作氛围和环境中茁壮成长。 然而，对于一些参与者来说，这可能是一个艰巨的挑战。 AIIE的发展可能会进一步加剧社会不平等。 技术红利可能只集中在少数参与者手中，而大多数参与者可能会因技术变革而被迫终止与AIIE的合作，甚至面临地区经济失衡、失业潮等严重的社会问题。This shift in demand requires participants to continuously upgrade their skills to thrive in the evolving work atmosphere and environment of AIIE. However, for some participants, this may represent a daunting challenge. The development of AIIE may further exacerbate social inequality. The technological dividend may only be concentrated in the hands of a few participants, while most participants may be forced to terminate their collaboration with AIIE due to technological changes, and even face serious social problems such as regional economic imbalances and unemployment waves.

针对这些挑战，AIIE有责任推动产业结构升级转型，加大对人工智能新兴技术的支持力度，为参与者提供更多展示价值的机会。 此外，AIIE还有义务与政府和机构合作制定相关法律法规，建立道德标准，规范人工智能应用行为。 通过促进人工智能与人类社会的和谐共处，AIIE可以为创造公平和可持续的就业环境做出贡献。 只有积极推动这种和谐发展，AIIE才能获得广泛认可，发挥创新潜力，最终为个人、社会、国家和国际社会做出重大贡献。In light of these challenges, AIIE is responsible for promoting the upgrading and transformation of industrial structures, enhancing support for emerging AI technologies, and providing participants with more opportunities to demonstrate their value. Additionally, AIIE has the obligation to collaborate with governments and institutions to formulate relevant laws and regulations, establish ethical standards, and regulate AI application behaviors. By fostering a harmonious coexistence between AI and human society, AIIE can contribute to creating a fair and sustainable employment landscape. Only by actively promoting this harmonious development can AIIE gain widespread recognition and realize its potential for innovation, ultimately making significant contributions to individuals, society, nations, and the global community.

Firstly, the paper summarized the concept of IE and its related terms and introduced the concept of AIIE from a spatial perspective. Secondly, the paper analyzed AIIE’s uniqueness compared to traditional IE in seven dimensions, emphasizing AI’s significant influence on this ecosystem. Thirdly, from an evolutionary perspective, the paper examined the distinct roles that AI plays in AIIE development across three critical periods, highlighting AI’s transformative impact. Subsequently, the paper adopts the development trajectory of the medical company Owkin to analyze and verify the feasibility, effectiveness, and rationality of the conceptual definition of AIIE, as well as the unique role of AI in the evolution and development of AIIE, drawing on relevant examples. Finally, the paper identified the current limitations of AI and outlined the potential challenges facing the further development of AIIE, which can guide future research directions and priorities. In summary, AI’s role in advancing IE is continuously expanding, giving rise to a new paradigm of AIIE. However, researchers must remain vigilant to the multidimensional risks associated with AIIE’s development.
