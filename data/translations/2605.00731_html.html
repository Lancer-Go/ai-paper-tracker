摘要Abstract

虽然图基础模型（GFM）在同构图方面取得了显着的成功，但由于跨类型特征转移和域内关系差距，将其扩展到多域异构图（MDHG）仍然是一个艰巨的挑战。现有的全局特征对齐方法（PCA或SVD）盲目地强制共享特征空间，这扭曲了特定于类型的语义并破坏了原始拓扑，不可避免地导致“类型崩溃”和“关系混乱”。为了解决这些基本限制，我们提出了解耦关系子空间对齐（DRSA），这是一种新颖的、即插即用的关系驱动对齐框架。 DRSA 通过将特征语义与关系结构解耦，从根本上改变了范式。具体来说，它引入了一种双关系子空间投影机制，以明确地协调共享低秩关系子空间内的跨类型交互。此外，特征结构解耦表示旨在将对齐特征分解为语义投影组件和结构残差项，自适应地吸收域内变化。 DRSA 通过基于块坐标下降的稳定交替最小化策略进行优化，构建了一个经过良好校准的、结构感知的潜在空间。对多个真实世界基准数据集的大量实验表明，DRSA 可以无缝集成为通用预处理模块，显着且持续地增强最先进的 GFM 的跨域和少量知识传输能力。该代码可在以下位置获取：While Graph Foundation Models (GFMs) have achieved remarkable success in homogeneous graphs, extending them to multi-domain heterogeneous graphs (MDHGs) remains a formidable challenge due to cross-type feature shifts and intra-domain relation gaps. Existing global feature alignment methods (PCA or SVD) enforce a shared feature space blindly, which distorts type-specific semantics and disrupts original topologies, inevitably leading to "Type Collapse" and "Relation Confusion". To address these fundamental limitations, we propose Decoupled relation Subspace Alignment (DRSA), a novel, plug-and-play relation-driven alignment framework. DRSA fundamentally shifts the paradigm by decoupling feature semantics from relation structures. Specifically, it introduces a dual-relation subspace projection mechanism to coordinate cross-type interactions within a shared low-rank relation subspace explicitly. Furthermore, a feature-structure decoupled representation is designed to decompose aligned features into a semantic projection component and a structural residual term, adaptively absorbing intra-domain variations. Optimized via a stable alternating minimization strategy based on Block Coordinate Descent, DRSA constructs a well-calibrated, structure-aware latent space. Extensive experiments on multiple real-world benchmark datasets demonstrate that DRSA can be seamlessly integrated as a universal preprocessing module, significantly and consistently enhancing the cross-domain and few-shot knowledge transfer capabilities of state-of-the-art GFMs. The code is available at:https://github.com/zhengziyu77/DSRA.

I 简介Introduction

图数据作为复杂实体关系建模的强大范例，已在包括学术网络在内的不同领域得到广泛采用Graph data, as a powerful paradigm for modeling complex entity relationships, has been extensively adopted across diverse domains, including academic networks[40]、推荐系统, recommendation systems[38, 39]、生物信息学分析, and bioinformatics analysis[24]。近年来，受到语言大规模预训练成功的启发. In recent years, inspired by the success of large-scale pre-training in the language[21]和视野and vision fields[2], 图基础模型 (GFM), Graph Foundation Models (GFMs)[16, 15]已成为图表示学习的关键方向。通过对多源图数据进行预训练并适应下游任务，GFM 旨在跨不同场景传递通用知识。最近的研究have emerged as a pivotal direction in graph representation learning. By pre-training on multi-source graph data and adapting to downstream tasks, GFMs aim to transfer generalizable knowledge across different scenarios. Recent studies[43, 49, 35, 46, 47]表明多域图的联合建模有助于获取通用结构模式和语义表示，从而提高分布变化下的泛化能力indicate that joint modeling over multi-domain graphs facilitates the acquisition of universal structural patterns and semantic representations, thereby improving generalization under distribution shifts[29].

尽管取得了显着的进展，目前多域 GFM 的研究主要集中在同质图上Despite remarkable progress, current research on multi-domain GFMs primarily focuses on homogeneous graphs[43, 49, 35, 46, 47, 17, 51, 8]，它假设统一的节点和边类型，并且只需要解决域间分布变化。相比之下，将这种范式扩展到多域异构图仍然是一个艰巨的挑战。异构图不仅表现出跨域分布变化，而且表现出由多类型节点和不同关系语义组成的复杂域内结构, which assume uniform node and edge types and merely require addressing inter-domain distribution shifts. In contrast, extending this paradigm to multi-domain heterogeneous graphs remains a formidable challenge. Heterogeneous graphs exhibit not only cross-domain distribution shifts but also complex intra-domain structures composed of multi-type nodes and diverse relation semantics[41, 52, 36, 37, 20]，这显着加剧了跨领域知识转移的难度。, which significantly exacerbates the difficulty of cross-domain knowledge transfer.

为了研究这种双层差异的影响，我们使用 ACM 数据集进行多域预训练To investigate the impact of this dual-level discrepancy, we conduct multi-domain pre-training using the ACM dataset[37]作为目标域。在源域上采用相同的预训练策略，我们分别利用元路径图和原始异构图作为输入。实验结果从两个角度都揭示了明显的负迁移现象，当直接在原始异构图上进行关系聚合时，这种现象变得更加严重。这一发现表明，固有的域内异质性显着加剧了跨域比对的难度。因此，一个关键的研究问题自然而然地出现了：as the target domain. Employing the same pre-training strategy on the source domains, we utilize meta-path graphs and raw heterogeneous graphs as inputs, respectively. Experimental results reveal a pronounced negative transfer phenomenon from both perspectives, which becomes even more severe when relation aggregation is directly performed on the raw heterogeneous graphs. This finding indicates that the inherent intra-domain heterogeneity significantly exacerbates the difficulty of cross-domain alignment. Consequently, a critical research question naturally arises:如何在不依赖手动定义的元路径的情况下构建多域异构图的通用基础模型？How can we construct a universal foundation model for multi-domain heterogeneous graphs without relying on manually defined meta-paths?

多领域预训练的核心前提是克服跨领域输入空间的不可通约性The core prerequisite for multi-domain pre-training is to overcome the incommensurability of input spaces across domains[4]。为了实现这一目标，现有的为同质图设计的多域 GFM 通常采用输入级全局对齐技术，例如主成分分析 (PCA). To achieve this, existing multi-domain GFMs designed for homogeneous graphs typically employ input-level global alignment techniques, such as Principal Component Analysis (PCA)[1]或奇异值分解 (SVD)or Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)[30]，在预训练之前将多域节点特征投影到统一的维度空间中。然而，该策略隐含地假设所有节点特征，无论其内在类型和关系上下文如何，都可以嵌入到共享度量空间中。这一假设在异构图中从根本上被违反，其中不同类型的节点具有根本不同的语义含义和结构角色, to project multi-domain node features into a unified dimensional space before pre-training. However, this strategy implicitly assumes that all node features, regardless of their intrinsic types and relation contexts, can be embedded within a shared metric space. This assumption is fundamentally violated in heterogeneous graphs, where different types of nodes possess fundamentally distinct semantic meanings and structural roles[48, 11]。为了实证验证这一限制，我们对两个异构数据集进行了分析：学术网络 ACM 和电影网络 IMDB. 上述分析揭示了一个重要的见解：与仅需要解决跨域分布变化的同构图不同，多域异构图中的节点语义由其属性和跨类型关系共同确定。仅仅依赖特征对齐会导致表示空间不匹配。这表明有效的对齐机制必须解决多类型节点和异构关系之间的域内不匹配问题。因此，核心挑战在于解决双层分配转变：❶The aforementioned analysis reveals a critical insight: unlike homogeneous graphs that merely need to address cross-domain distribution shifts, node semantics in multi-domain heterogeneous graphs are jointly determined by their attributes and cross-type relations. Consequently, the core challenge lies in tackling a dual-level distribution shift: ❶交叉型特征转移Cross-type Feature Shift，其中不同的节点类型拥有不同维度和分布的特征空间；和❷, where different node types possess feature spaces with varying dimensions and distributions; and ❷域内关系差距Intra-domain relation Gap，其中异构关系形成的混合分布在不同子空间中表现出不一致。因此，有效的跨域对齐机制需要对特征语义和关系结构进行同步建模。, where the mixed distribution formed by heterogeneous relations exhibits inconsistencies across different subspaces. Therefore, an effective cross-domain alignment mechanism necessitates the simultaneous modeling of feature semantics and relation structures.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一种新颖的即插即用关系驱动对齐框架——解耦关系子空间对齐（DRSA），它从根本上重新思考了跨域异构图中的对齐机制。与在特征空间内强制执行统一表示的传统方法不同，DRSA 将特征语义与关系结构解耦，并通过显式建模跨类型交互来实现关系子空间级别的对齐。具体来说，我们引入了一种双关系子空间投影机制，将跨类型交互分解为共享的低秩子空间，并通过双线性投影捕获关系依赖关系，从而确保统一潜在空间内关系的语义一致性。此外，我们提出了一种特征-结构解耦机制，其中对齐的特征被分解为语义投影组件和结构残差项。这种设计使模型能够保持特征一致性，同时自适应地捕获由关系结构引起的域内变化。为了优化模型，我们采用交替最小化策略To tackle these challenges, we propose a novel plug-and-play relation-driven alignment framework, Decoupled relation Subspace Alignment (DRSA), which fundamentally rethinks the alignment mechanism in cross-domain heterogeneous graphs. Unlike conventional methods that enforce a unified representation within the feature space, DRSA decouples feature semantics from relation structures and achieves alignment at the level of a relation subspace by explicitly modeling cross-type interactions. Specifically, we introduce a dual-relation subspace projection mechanism that factors cross-type interactions into a shared low-rank subspace and captures relation dependencies via bilinear projections, thereby ensuring semantic consistency of relations within a unified latent space. In addition, we propose a feature–structure decoupled mechanism, where aligned features are decomposed into a semantic projection component and a structural residual term. This design enables the model to preserve feature consistency while adaptively capturing intra-domain variations induced by relation structures. To optimize the model, we adopt an alternating minimization strategy[33]，它通过两阶段过程逐步对齐关系结构并细化特征表示。由于每个子问题都是凸的并且有闭式解，因此整体优化具有稳定的收敛性。, which progressively aligns relation structures and refines feature representations through a two-stage procedure. As each subproblem is convex and admits a closed-form solution, the overall optimization enjoys stable convergence.

七VII结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们系统地研究了多域异构图预训练中的“负迁移”现象，并揭示了传统全局特征对齐的根本局限性，在处理多类型节点时，它本质上会导致类型崩溃和关系混乱。为了解决这个瓶颈，我们引入了解耦关系子空间对齐（DRSA），一个通用的、即插即用的输入对齐框架。通过创新地采用双关系子空间投影以及特征结构解耦表示机制，DRSA 有效地从关系拓扑中解开语义特征。这使得模型能够在统一的潜在空间内协调异构交互，而无需牺牲特定于类型的语义完整性。跨不同多域基准的广泛实证评估证实，DRSA 成功克服了双层分布变化。当与现有的最先进的图基础模型集成时，它在跨域泛化和小样本适应任务中提供了显着且一致的性能增益。最终，我们的工作提供了一种高效且无文本的范例，用于推进多域异构图学习，而不依赖于手动制作的元路径。In this paper, we systematically investigate the "negative transfer" phenomenon in multi-domain heterogeneous graph pre-training and reveal the fundamental limitations of traditional global feature alignment, which inherently induces type collapse and relation confusion when handling multi-type nodes. To address this bottleneck, we introduce Decoupled relation Subspace Alignment (DRSA), a universal, plug-and-play input alignment framework. By innovatively employing a dual-relation subspace projection alongside a feature-structure decoupled representation mechanism, DRSA effectively untangles semantic features from relation topologies. This allows the model to harmonize heterogeneous interactions within a unified latent space without sacrificing type-specific semantic integrity. Extensive empirical evaluations across diverse multi-domain benchmarks confirm that DRSA successfully overcomes dual-level distribution shifts. When integrated with existing state-of-the-art graph foundation models, it delivers substantial and consistent performance gains in both cross-domain generalization and few-shot adaptation tasks. Ultimately, our work provides a highly effective and text-free paradigm for advancing multi-domain heterogeneous graph learning without the reliance on manually crafted meta-paths.
