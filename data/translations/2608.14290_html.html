Intern-S2-Mobius：知识与推理解耦的基础模型Intern-S2-Mobius: Foundation Model with Decoupled Knowledge and Reasoning

Intern-S2-Mobius Team, Shanghai AI Laboratory
摘要Abstract

我们介绍We introduce莫比乌斯-v0Mobius-v0，一种架构，由存储知识向量的全局共享内存（FFN）和迭代实现组合推理的多个推理器（Self-Attn）组成。使用隐藏状态作为缓存和载体，推理器重复查询内存以获取所需的知识向量，同时将知识传输回推理运算符。通过这种知识推理分离的架构，莫比乌斯实现了更好的知识压缩和推理效率。基于 Mobius-v0 架构构建：1) 我们从头开始训练的 7B 模型达到了与 7B Transformer 基线相似的下游分数，其中基线训练数据占 62.6%。 2) 我们的, an architecture that comprises a globally shared Memory (FFN) that stores knowledge vectors and multiple Reasoners (Self-Attn) that iteratively achieve compositional reasoning. Using hidden states as cache and carrier, reasoners repeatedly query memory for required knowledge-vectors, while the knowledge is transmitted back to reasoning operators. Through this knowledge-reasoning-separation architecture, Mobius achieves better knowledge compression and reasoning efficiency. 1 基础模型的发展瓶颈The development bottleneck of the Foundation Models

变形金刚The Transformer[67]是迄今为止最流行、最强大的架构范式，几乎渗透到每个领域——从语言和视觉，到视频和科学计算stands as the most prevalent and powerful architectural paradigm to date, permeating virtually every domain—from language and vision, to video and scientific computing[15, 16, 4, 79]。沿着其进化轨迹，出现了两个关键的研究方向。第一个保持模型架构完整，同时增加其他方面的支出以增强能力：缩放模型参数、扩展训练语料库和延长推理链通常赋予模型更丰富的知识和解决更复杂问题的能力. Along its evolutionary trajectory, two pivotal research directions have emerged. The first preserves the model architecture intact while increasing expenditure elsewhere to enhance capability: scaling model parameters, expanding training corpora, and elongating reasoning chains typically endow models with richer knowledge and the capacity to tackle more intricate problems[36, 33, 69, 44]。第二个是通过降低架构复杂度来减少训练和推理开销，以提高实用性：受到广泛持有的观点的启发，即自注意力的二次复杂度构成了超长上下文的瓶颈，因此提出了 SSM 和 GDN 等线性注意力机制. The second reduces training and inference overhead by lowering architectural complexity to improve practicality: motivated by the widely held view that the quadratic complexity of self-attention constitutes a bottleneck for ultra-long contexts, linear attention mechanisms such as SSM and GDN have been proposed[37, 10, 25, 76]，及其变体已被融入主流当代建筑中。, and their variants have since been integrated into mainstream contemporary architectures.

然而，这两种方法现在都达到了极限。Yet both approaches are now hitting their limits.一方面，缩放数据和参数已经产生了越来越强大的模型和高投资回报，但缩放定律已逐渐趋于稳定并正在接近边际收益递减On one hand, scaling data and parameters has yielded increasingly powerful models at high returns on investment, yet the Scaling Law has gradually plateaued and is approaching diminishing marginal returns[36, 33]。长长的思想链在数学、代码和物理等领域为我们提供了很好的帮助，但无论问题的难度如何，模型往往会产生冗长、离题的输出——这一特征在人类社会中通常被视为智力不足的标志. Long chains of thought have served us well in domains such as mathematics, code, and physics, yet models tend to produce verbose, tangential output regardless of problem difficulty—a trait typically regarded in human society as a sign of insufficient intelligence[53, 48]。另一方面，降低全注意力的计算复杂度确实会带来一定的效率提升，但越来越明显的是，以牺牲大部分模型能力为代价实现的效率提升并没有让模型真正能够负担得起商业部署的费用. On the other hand, reducing the computational complexity of full attention does bring certain efficiency gains, yet it has become increasingly apparent that the efficiency improvements achieved at the cost of sacrificing a substantial portion of model capability have not made models truly affordable enough for commercial deployment[3].

面对人工智能发展的这一瓶颈，我们寻求另一种路径：Confronted with this bottleneck in AI development, we pursue an alternative path:增加架构复杂性以提高模型智能的上限，从而减少端到端的开销。increasing architectural complexity to raise the ceiling of model intelligence, thereby reducing the end-to-end overhead.为此，我们建议To this purpose, we propose the莫比乌斯Mobius，解耦了知识向量（FFN）和推理算子（Self-Attn）之间的绑定，从而构建了所有推理算子都可以访问的共享知识向量数据库。尽管这种共享向量数据库的大规模使得每个推理激活高度稀疏，引入了更大的内存访问压力和较低的每遍转发效率，但我们发现，与具有相同参数的 Transformer 架构相比，该实现实现了近 4 倍的端到端推理加速，同时保持了同等的推理精度。, which decouples the binding between knowledge vectors (FFN) and reasoning operators (Self-Attn), thereby constructing a shared knowledge vector database accessible to all reasoning operators. Although the large scale of this shared vector database renders each inference activation highly sparse, introducing greater memory access pressure and lower per-pass forward efficiency, we find that this implementation achieves nearly 4× end-to-end inference speedup over the Transformer architecture with same parameters while maintaining equivalent reasoning accuracy.

Ultimately, Mobius completes the same reasoning task with markedly fewer high-quality tokens, achieving substantially higher end-to-end inference efficiency than Transformer.

2 莫比乌斯环的设计灵感是什么？What inspired the design of Mobius?

在基于 Transformer 的架构中，前馈网络通常被认为负责知识存储In Transformer-based architectures, the Feed-Forward Network is conventionally regarded as responsible for knowledge storage[20, 51]，用于信息传输的隐藏状态，以及用于组合推理的自注意力, Hidden States for information transmission, and Self-Attention for compositional reasoning[67, 17].

同时，由于 Transformer 的层次结构，每一层的 Self-Attention 主要处理从前一层接收到的知识输入，并诱导当前层的 FFN 产生下一层所需的输出。尽管残差连接建立了更灵活的跨层连接Concurrently, owing to the hierarchical structure of Transformer, Self-Attention at each layer primarily processes knowledge inputs received from the preceding layer and induces the FFN at the current layer to produce outputs required by the next layer. Although residual connections establish more flexible connections across layers[31, 64, 34]，只有浅层Hidden States才能访问深层知识，而深层Self-Attention只能处理源自浅层的知识；该模型仍然无法在相反方向上传递信息。, only shallow Hidden States can access deep-layer knowledge, while deep-layer Self-Attention can only process knowledge originating from shallow layers; the model remains incapable of information transfer in the opposite direction.

为了构建反向信息传递，当前模型主要依赖于思想链To construct information transfer in the reverse direction, current models predominantly rely on Chains of Thought[69, 26]。每次前向传递后，模型都会生成一个新标记，用于激活下一个推理步骤中的知识。通过迭代令牌生成，模型不断从 FFN 中提取有价值的知识，直到这些知识足以生成关键令牌甚至最终答案。. After each forward pass, the model generates a new token that serves to activate knowledge in the next inference step. Through iterative token generation, the model continuously extracts valuable knowledge from the FFN until this knowledge suffices to produce critical tokens or even the final answer.

仅使用分层结构、前向残差连接和以代币为中介的思想链来联合构建人工智能模型效率很低。为了让模型具备足够的知识来回答复杂的问题，需要花费大量的计算来生成冗长、冗余的推理链。为了缓解这个问题，我们重新设计了模型架构并提出了莫比乌斯环。The joint construction of artificial intelligence models using exclusively hierarchical structures, forward residual connections, and token-mediated chains of thought is inefficient. To equip models with sufficient knowledge for answering complex questions, an inordinate amount of computation is expended on producing lengthy, redundant reasoning chains. To mitigate this issue, we have redesigned the model architecture and propose Mobius.

2.1 莫比乌斯的第一个天赋——后向残差连接Mobius’ first innate talent —— Backward Residual Connection

残差连接已成为现代深度学习模型中最关键的组成部分之一Residual connections have become one of the most critical components in modern deep learning models[31]。看似微不足道，但它们承担着在前向传播期间传输层间信息和在反向传播期间稳定梯度幅度的关键责任。. Seemingly minor, they bear the crucial responsibility of transmitting inter-layer information during forward propagation and stabilizing gradient magnitudes during backpropagation.[32, 73]尽管此后出现了许多残差连接的变体，例如超连接和注意力残差Although numerous variants of residual connections have since emerged, such as Hyper-Connection and Attention-Residual[78, 72, 38]，迄今为止所有主流残差都保留其单向性质。例如，在前向传播期间，残差仅将浅层信息传递到更深的层。, all mainstream residual to date retain their unidirectional nature. For instance, during forward propagation, residual only convey shallow-layer information to deeper layers.[34]这种单向性带来了很大的缺陷：如果在浅层计算过程中某些关键知识未能被激活，模型可能会在该轮推理中难以解码有价值的标记，只能通过思想链生成低信息标记来进行下一个推理步骤。This unidirectionality entails significant drawbacks: if certain critical knowledge fails to be activated during shallow-layer computation, the model may struggle to decode valuable tokens in that reasoning round, and can only resort to generating low-information tokens via chains of thought to proceed to the next reasoning step.

为了缓解这种现象，有必要双向渲染残差连接，也就是说，使深层能够在前向传播过程中访问浅层知识。然而，这种向后剩余连接的直接实现对基础设施不友好，因为它可能引入更复杂的计算图和更难以并行化的异步性。为此，我们选择间接实现后向残差连接：不同层共享一个超大的知识库，使每一层都有机会访问模型内​​的所有知识。经验结果最终证实，这种形式的后向残差连接对于提高模型的端到端推理效率做出了有意义的贡献。To mitigate this phenomenon, rendering residual connections bidirectional is necessary—that is, enabling deep layers to access shallow-layer knowledge during forward propagation. However, a direct implementation of such backward residual connections is infrastructure-unfriendly, as it may introduce more complex computation graphs and harder-to-parallelize asynchrony. To this end, we opt for an indirect realization of backward residual connections: different layers share a single, oversized knowledge repository, granting every layer the opportunity to access all knowledge within the model. Empirical results ultimately corroborate that this form of backward residual connection contributes meaningfully to improving the model’s end-to-end inference efficiency.

2.2 莫比乌斯的第二个天赋——动态潜在推理Mobius’ second innate talent —— Dynamic Latent Reasoning

长思维链（Long CoT）已成为大语言模型推理中不可或缺的组成部分Long chains of thought (Long CoT) have become an indispensable component in large language model reasoning[69, 44, 26]。在这项技术的支持下，当代大型语言模型现在可以解决广泛的复杂推理问题，涵盖数学、物理和代码生成。然而，长期 CoT 的成本却非常高昂。一方面，较长的推理链需要生成更多的代币，生成成本随链长度非线性增长。另一方面，当前模型在处理复杂问题时经常采用试错和纠正的方法，导致扩展推理过程中出现大量的令牌冗余。此外，即使对于简单的问题，此类模型也往往会产生过于冗长的响应。这种冗长部分源于上面讨论的残余连接，部分源于我们当前推理的最小单位是令牌——一种离散的、低信息密度的存储。. Empowered by this technique, contemporary large language models can now solve a wide spectrum of complex reasoning problems, spanning mathematics, physics, and code generation. Yet the cost of Long CoT is exorbitant. On one hand, longer reasoning chains entail the generation of more tokens, with generation cost growing non-linearly with chain length. On the other hand, current models frequently adopt a trial-and-error-and-correct approach when tackling complex problems, resulting in substantial token redundancy during extended reasoning. Moreover, such models tend to produce excessively verbose responses even for simple problems. This verbosity stems partly from the residual connections discussed above, and partly from the fact that the minimal unit of our current reasoning is the token—a discrete, low-information-density storage[9].

莫比乌斯将深思熟虑、试错和细化等过程内化为连续向量的优化，并使模型能够动态地将计算预算分配给不同的代币。这为模型创造了产生更少多余标记的机会，同时实现更高效、更智能的推理。莫比乌斯可以看作是循环变压器和扩散语言模型的升级综合体Mobius internalizes processes such as deliberation, trial-and-error, and refinement into the optimization of a continuous vector, and enables the model to dynamically allocate computational budget to different tokens. This creates the opportunity for the model to produce fewer superfluous tokens while achieving more efficient and more intelligent reasoning. Mobius can be regarded as an upgraded synthesis of the Looped Transformer and the Diffusion Language Model[21, 42, 54]，采用更有效的潜在推理和并行预测。首先，莫比乌斯以更高的循环频率运行，用极少的层完成一次表示迭代。其次，在推理过程中，莫比乌斯通过更紧凑、高信息密度的连续向量同时获取多个标记的联合表示。这一方面减轻了多个隐藏状态并行的压力，降低了循环过程中KV-cache管理的复杂度，增强了向量迭代过程的连续可微性。同时，这种原生潜在推理赋予模型更加动态和不受约束的迭代过程，而不限制模型在特定迭代步骤解码固定数量的令牌。, employing more efficient latent reasoning and parallel prediction. First, Mobius operates at a higher loop frequency, completing one representation iteration with extremely few layers. Second, during inference, Mobius acquires joint representations of multiple tokens simultaneously through more compact, high-information-density continuous vectors. This, on one hand, alleviates the pressure from parallelizing multiple hidden states, reduces the complexity of KV-cache management during the looping process, and enhances the continuous differentiability of the vector iteration process. Meanwhile, this native latent reasoning endows the model with a more dynamic and unfettered iteration process, without constraining the model to decode a fixed number of tokens at specific iteration steps.

2.3 一石二鸟——解开知识向量与推理算子One Stone Two Birds —— Disentangling Knowledge Vectors and Reasoning Operators

总体而言，莫比乌斯通过将知识存储模块FFN与其分层绑定解耦，并构建全球共享的知识向量数据库来实现这两种天赋。首先，这授予所有推理运算符（自注意力）对整个知识体系的读取权限。此外，自注意力有机会在更少的层中访问所有知识，并通过隐藏状态的循环潜在推理来执行自适应多轮迭代，最终在一次突发中发出精致的、高信息密度的令牌。Overall, Mobius achieves these two innate talents by decoupling the knowledge storage module, the FFN, from its layer-wise binding, and constructing a globally shared knowledge-vector database. First, this grants all reasoning operators, the Self-Attention, read access to the entire body of knowledge. Further, the Self-Attention gains the opportunity to access all knowledge within significantly fewer layers, and performs adaptive multi-round iteration through recurrent latent reasoning via Hidden States, ultimately emitting refined, high-information-density tokens in a single burst.

在构建共享知识库时，我们选择了直接的水平串联，因为 FFN 通常被视为键值知识向量池，并且这种串联方法保留了这些知识向量之间的对应关系。尽管这种简单的串联可以带来性能优势，但过多的参数激活会降低模型的训练和推理效率。因此，在较大的参数尺度下，我们采用类似于 MoE 的分块分区技术In constructing the shared knowledge repository, we opted for a straightforward horizontal concatenation, as the FFN is conventionally regarded as a Key-Value Knowledge-Vector Pool, and this concatenation approach preserves the correspondence relationships among these knowledge vectors. Although such simple concatenation yields performance benefits, excessive parameter activation degrades the model’s training and inference efficiency. Therefore, at larger parameter scales, we employ a block-wise partitioning technique analogous to MoE[shazeer2017moe]分割 FFN，在前向传播期间使用稀疏激活。to partition the FFN, with sparse activation during forward propagation.

3 莫比乌斯的表现如何？How about Mobius’ performance?

为了验证 Mobius 的性能，我们进行了从头训练（TFS）和持续预训练（CPT）实验。对于 TFS 实验，我们在 1TB 代币上训练了 7B-A1B MoE 模型（一个使用 Mobius，一个使用 Transformer），并比较了它们的 MMLU 分数。对于CPT实验，我们使用Qwen3.5-35B-A3B作为起始检查点，继续对1TB令牌进行预训练，随后进行监督微调（SFT）和强化学习（RL）。To validate the performance of Mobius, we conducted both training-from-scratch (TFS) and continual pre-training (CPT) experiments. For the TFS experiments, we trained 7B-A1B MoE models — one with Mobius and one with Transformer — on 1TB tokens, and compared their MMLU scores. For the CPT experiments, we used Qwen3.5-35B-A3B as the starting checkpoint, continued pre-training on 1TB tokens, and subsequently performed supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL).

4 莫比乌斯与主流研究的关系Mobius’ relationship with mainstream research

4.1 潜在推理Latent Reasoning

思想链 (CoT) 将推理外化为中间标记Chain-of-thought (CoT) externalizes reasoning into intermediate tokens[69]，但会产生顺序解码开销。潜在推理改为在连续状态下执行计算，探索三个方向：连续思维、循环计算和并行细化。, but incurs sequential decoding overhead. Latent reasoning instead performs computation in continuous states, exploring three directions: continuous thought, looped computation, and parallel refinement.

4.1.1 持续思考Continuous Thought

连续思维方法用推理步骤之间传递的可微表示取代离散推理标记。 COCONUT 直接将最终的隐藏状态反馈回作为下一个输入嵌入，允许中间计算继续进行，而无需将每个状态解码为语言Continuous-thought approaches replace discrete reasoning tokens with differentiable representations passed between reasoning steps. COCONUT directly feeds the final hidden state back as the next input embedding, allowing intermediate computation to proceed without decoding each state into language[30]。 CODI通过自蒸馏将显式CoT监督压缩为连续状态. CODI compresses explicit CoT supervision into continuous states through self-distillation[62]，而 SoftCoT 通过轻量级助手生成特定于实例的软思想，并将其投影到目标语言模型的表示空间中, while SoftCoT generates instance-specific soft thoughts with a lightweight assistant and projects them into the representation space of a target language model[74]。这些方法表明，连续状态可以保留有用的推理信息，同时缩短或消除明确的基本原理。. These approaches demonstrate that continuous states can preserve useful reasoning information while shortening or eliminating explicit rationales.

这些方法公开了一小部分潜在状态，后续令牌可以处理这些状态并将其重用为共享上下文。莫比乌斯进一步使这种模式成为一种原生架构能力：它的循环状态根据共享内存进行细化，并共同支持多个未来令牌，从而产生更高密度的推理，而无需冗长的跟踪。These approaches expose a small set of latent states that subsequent tokens can attend to and reuse as shared context. Mobius further makes this pattern a native architectural capability: its recurrent states are refined against the shared Memory and jointly support multiple future tokens, yielding higher-density reasoning without verbose traces.

4.1.2 循环语言模型Looped Language Models

循环语言模型通过重复应用共享计算块来增加有效深度。 Universal Transformers 通过参数共享引入了跨深度的循环细化Looped language models increase effective depth by repeatedly applying shared computation blocks. Universal Transformers introduced recurrent refinement across depth with parameter sharing[12]，而随后的分析建立了 Looped Transformers 的计算表达能力, while subsequent analyses established the computational expressivity of Looped Transformers[21]。最近的工作将有效深度直接与推理联系起来：循环模型可以模拟多步潜在计算并在推理任务上接近更深的非循环模型. More recent work connects effective depth directly to reasoning: looped models can emulate multi-step latent computation and approach much deeper non-looped models on reasoning tasks[59]，而循环深度预训练通过增加潜在迭代数量来实现测试时计算扩展，而无需生成额外的推理标记, while recurrent-depth pre-training enables test-time compute scaling by increasing the number of latent iterations without generating additional reasoning tokens[19].

Mobius 还通过潜在循环扩展计算，但仅在几个层上执行每次更新，同时保留对完整共享内存的访问。这种更高的更新频率可以实现更多的迭代细化，并将推理集中到更高信息密度的潜在状态。Mobius also scales computation through latent recurrence, but performs each update over only a few layers while retaining access to the full shared Memory. This higher update frequency enables more iterative refinement and concentrates reasoning into higher-information-density latent states.

4.1.3 额外的潜在计算步骤Additional Latent Computation Steps

另一项工作是通过在生成之前引入额外的潜在计算步骤来提高推理能力。暂停令牌方法添加学习占位符以推迟预测并创建额外的计算空间Another line of work increases reasoning capacity by introducing extra latent computation steps before generation. Pause-token methods add learned placeholders to defer prediction and create additional computation space[24]。隐藏解码将每个令牌在单个前向传递中扩展为多个潜在流，使用中间键值状态作为可重用的计算轨迹. Hidden Decoding expands each token into multiple latent streams within a single forward pass, using intermediate key–value states as reusable computation traces[45]。扩散语言模型通过连续或屏蔽表示的迭代细化实现类似的效果. Diffusion language models achieve similar effects through iterative refinement of continuous or masked representations[42, 54]。这些方法将计算从显式 CoT 轨迹转移到潜在轨迹。. These methods shift computation from explicit CoT traces to latent trajectories.

莫比乌斯通过循环潜在细化扩展了这一想法，无需明确的占位符或扩散步骤。它的推理机反复从共享知识向量内存中检索，细化高密度潜在状态，并并行解码多个标记。Mobius extends this idea through recurrent latent refinement without explicit placeholders or diffusion steps. Its Reasoners repeatedly retrieve from a shared knowledge-vector Memory, refine high-density latent states, and decode multiple tokens in parallel.

4.2 高效推理Efficient Reasoning

从历史上看，语言模型推理的主要范式依赖于单个强大模型的自回归单标记生成，并通过长链思想进行增强。为了提高端到端推理效率，有两种方法越来越受欢迎：推测解码（增强推理并行性）和简洁 CoT（减少推理链的顺序长度）。Historically, the predominant paradigm for language model inference relied on autoregressive single-token generation from a single strong model, augmented with long chains of thought. To improve end-to-end inference efficiency, two approaches have gained increasing traction: Speculative Decoding, which enhances inference parallelism, and Concise CoT, which reduces the sequential length of reasoning chains.

4.2.1 推测性解码Speculative Decoding

推测性解码通过使用廉价的起草者提出未来令牌的块或树并与目标模型并行验证这些候选者来加速自回归生成Speculative decoding accelerates autoregressive generation by using a cheap drafter to propose a block, or a tree, of future tokens and verifying these candidates in parallel with the target model[66, 41, 8, 52]。通过拒绝采样校正，该验证保留了目标模型的输出分布。虽然经典方法采用轻量级外部草图模型，但后来的工作开发了基于辅助解码头或预测隐藏特征的内部绘图器. With rejection-sampling correction, this verification preserves the target model’s output distribution. While classical methods employ a lightweight external draft model, later work develops internal drafters based on auxiliary decoding heads or predicted hidden features[7, 43, 2]。 MTP 是一种内部起草机制，而不是解码协议本身：共享目标主干的辅助头预测多个未来令牌. MTP is such an internal drafting mechanism rather than a decoding protocol itself: auxiliary heads sharing the target backbone predict multiple future tokens[22, 46, 50].

无论是使用草稿模型还是其他策略，推测解码在标记预测之前仅遍历所有知识一次。相比之下，Mobius 在内部执行多轮知识遍历和多令牌迭代，在最终解码之前产生更高信息密度的隐藏状态，并原生支持多令牌预测。Whether employing a draft model or other strategies, Speculative Decoding traverses all knowledge only once before token prediction. In contrast, Mobius performs multiple rounds of knowledge traversal and multi-token iteration internally, yielding hidden states of higher information density prior to final decoding, with native support for multi-token prediction.

4.2.2 简洁的思路Concise Chain of Thought

长思想链 (CoT) 推理可以提高挑战性任务的性能，但其自回归生成会产生大量推理成本和延迟。因此，最近的工作通过几种互补的方法寻求简洁的 CoT：监督方法从修剪或配对的长短轨迹中学习，或者将显式基本原理内化为隐式或密集表示Long chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning can improve performance on challenging tasks, but its autoregressive generation incurs substantial inference cost and latency. Recent work therefore seeks concise CoT through several complementary approaches: supervised methods learn from pruned or paired long–short trajectories, or internalize explicit rationales into implicit or dense representations[35, 71, 13, 9, 14, 77];基于强化学习的方法通过奖励设计塑造推理长度，包括可控压缩和长度感知目标; RL-based methods shape reasoning length through reward design, including controllable compression and length-aware objectives[49, 18, 63, 11]。尽管强化学习可以引发自我纠正、验证和回溯等高级行为. Although RL can elicit advanced behaviors such as self-correction, verification, and backtracking[61, 26]，仅结果奖励通常有利于更长的轨迹，因为额外的代币可以进行进一步的探索和错误纠正, outcome-only rewards often favor longer trajectories because additional tokens enable further exploration and error correction[75]。因此，核心挑战是在不消除准确和稳健推理所需的深思熟虑的情况下减少 CoT 的冗长。. The central challenge is thus to reduce CoT verbosity without removing the deliberation needed for accurate and robust reasoning.

传统的长度正则化可能会通过减少深思熟虑来缩短 CoT，从而可能损害准确性和稳健性。相比之下，莫比乌斯通过几轮潜在思想迭代产生更精致和更短的响应。Conventional length regularization may shorten CoT by curtailing deliberation, potentially harming accuracy and robustness. In contrast, Mobius yields far more refined and shorter responses through several rounds of latent thought iteration.

4.3 建筑设计Architecture Design

Mobius通过将知识存储与推理计算解耦，重新设计了Transformer架构的内部信息流。 在本节中，我们讨论它与两个主要架构方向的关系：注意力机制和残差连接。Mobius redesigns the internal information flow of Transformer architectures by decoupling knowledge storage from reasoning computation. In this section, we discuss its relationship with two major architectural directions: attention mechanisms and residual connections.

4.3.1 注意力机制Attention Mechanism

自注意力是 Transformer 架构的核心组件，使模型能够通过令牌之间的成对交互来捕获全局依赖关系Self-attention is the core component of Transformer architectures, enabling models to capture global dependencies through pairwise interactions among tokens[67]。然而，充分注意力的二次复杂度已成为将 Transformer 模型扩展到长上下文场景的主要瓶颈。为了解决这个问题，广泛的研究探索了更有效的注意力机制。稀疏注意力通过限制注意力模式来减少不必要的标记交互，而线性注意力则重新制定注意力计算，以通过替代核化或分解策略来降低复杂性。最近，状态空间模型和门控状态空间变体，例如 Mamba 和门控 Delta 网络，用循环状态更新取代显式注意力计算，为长序列建模提供了有效的替代方案. However, the quadratic complexity of full attention has become a major bottleneck for scaling Transformer models to long-context scenarios. To address this issue, extensive studies have explored more efficient attention mechanisms. Sparse attention reduces unnecessary token interactions by restricting attention patterns, while linear attention reformulates the attention computation to reduce complexity through alternative kernelization or factorization strategies. More recently, state-space models and gated state-space variants, such as Mamba and Gated Delta Networks, replace explicit attention computation with recurrent state updates, providing efficient alternatives for long-sequence modeling[25, 76]。这些方法主要通过修改计算形式或降低信息交互成本来提高效率。. These approaches mainly improve efficiency by modifying the computation form or reducing the cost of information interaction.

我们的目标不是直接降低注意力复杂性，而是增加每个注意力操作提取的信息密度和价值。具体来说，通过将知识与推理解耦，构建全局共享的知识库，莫比乌斯带来了更高的检索成本，但为注意力提供了更灵活的选择空间和更高质量的输入，从而以更紧凑的计算轨迹完成推理。Rather than reducing attention complexity directly, we aim to increase the information density and value extracted per attention operation. Specifically, by decoupling knowledge from reasoning and constructing a globally shared knowledge repository, Mobius incur higher retrieval costs but provide attention with a more flexible selection space and higher-quality inputs, thereby completing reasoning with a more compact computation trajectory.

4.3.2 剩余连接Residual Connection

残差连接是扩展深度神经网络的基本组成部分，可通过捷径实现稳定的优化和有效的信息传播Residual connections are fundamental components for scaling deep neural networks, enabling stable optimization and effective information propagation through shortcut pathways[31]。随后的研究探索了更灵活的连接模式，以改善跨层的信息传输。高速公路网络引入可学习门来自适应控制信息流. Subsequent studies have explored more flexible connection patterns to improve information transmission across layers. Highway Networks introduce learnable gates to adaptively control information flow[65]，而 DenseNet 通过允许每一层直接访问先前的表示来增加连接密度, while DenseNet increases connection density by allowing each layer to directly access previous representations[huang2017densely]。最近，超连接和注意力残差进一步研究了如何通过增加连接容量或动态聚合前层表示来增强残差路径. More recently, Hyper-Connections and Attention Residuals further investigate how to enhance residual pathways by increasing connection capacity or dynamically aggregating previous-layer representations[78, 38]。尽管有这些改进，现有的残差增强方法主要集中在加强前向信息传播，即信息从较早的层流向较晚的层。. Despite these improvements, existing residual enhancement methods mainly focus on strengthening forward information propagation, where information flows from earlier layers to later layers.

莫比乌斯将残差设计从前向信息传播扩展到双向知识访问，而不是明确扩展残差路径。由于所有推理阶段都可以访问相同的知识库，因此更深的推理器可以检索超出其本地层层次结构的知识，从而实现更灵活的信息传输，同时保持高效的计算结构。Mobius extends residual design from forward information propagation toward bidirectional knowledge access, instead of explicitly expanding residual pathways. Since all reasoning stages can access the same knowledge repository, deeper Reasoners can retrieve knowledge beyond their local layer hierarchy, enabling more flexible information transmission while maintaining an efficient computation structure.

5 莫比乌斯在几个亮点主题上的潜力Mobius’ potential on several highlight topics

5.1 自我进化Self-Evolving

尽管模型和智能体的能力越来越强，但如何使他们不断从外部吸收新的知识和技能仍然是一个悬而未决的问题Although models and agents have grown increasingly capable, how to enable them to continuously absorb new knowledge and skills from external sources remains an open question[39, 55, 70, 58].

我们的立场是，Our position is that,目前的Transformer架构不满足自进化的先决条件。the current Transformer architecture does not satisfy the prerequisites for self-evolution.由于知识和推理在 Transformer 中紧密耦合，此类模型只能通过端到端训练来获取新技能，这不可避免地会同时改变知识和推理能力，从而导致对先前学到的技能的灾难性遗忘。适合自进化的架构应该表现出一定程度的知识推理解耦，这将支持知识存储的无界高效扩展，并使模型的推理能力能够跨多个领域泛化。我们提出的莫比乌斯架构具有初步的知识推理分离特性，与 Transformer 相比表现出更大的持续学习潜力。Because knowledge and reasoning are tightly coupled in Transformer, such models can only acquire new skills through end-to-end training, which inevitably alters both knowledge and reasoning capabilities simultaneously, thereby causing catastrophic forgetting of previously learned skills. An architecture suitable for self-evolution should exhibit a certain degree of knowledge-reasoning decoupling, as this would support unbounded and efficient expansion of knowledge storage, and enable the model’s reasoning capabilities to generalize across multiple domains. The Mobius architecture we propose possesses preliminary knowledge-reasoning separation characteristics, demonstrating greater potential for continual learning compared to Transformer.

尽管如此，莫比乌斯能否在现实的自我进化场景中表现更好，仍然需要基础模型、代理系统和环境反馈的联合设计，还有待在未来的工作中验证。Nevertheless, whether Mobius can perform better in real-world self-evolution scenarios still requires the joint design of foundation models, agentic systems, and environmental feedback, and remains to be validated in future work.

5.2 世界模特World Model

世界模型正在成为超越语言模型的下一个前沿领域，但如何使模型能够对连续空间输入进行理解和推理仍然是一个悬而未决的问题World models are emerging as the next frontier beyond language models, yet how to enable models to perform understanding and reasoning over continuous-space inputs remains an open question[27, 28, 29, 60, 6, 33].

我们的立场是，Our position is that,当前的 Transformer 架构不满足世界模型的先决条件。the current Transformer architecture does not satisfy the prerequisites for world models.主要基于 Transformer 构建的模型已被证明可以在 TB 参数规模上对离散模态（例如语言）进行建模，但使用 Transformer 对连续空间进行完美建模可能需要 PB 量级的参数规模。 TB 级语言模型的推理成本已经使现有硬件达到了极限； PB 级世界模型的推理成本将是天文数字。我们提出的 Mobius 架构本身就支持潜在推理，为连续空间建模提供了更强的先验，并展示了比 Transformer 更大的潜力。Models built primarily upon Transformer have been demonstrated to model discrete modalities (such as language) at the terabyte parameter scale, yet perfect modeling of continuous space with Transformer would likely require parameter scales on the order of petabytes. The inference cost of terabyte-scale language models already strains existing hardware to its limits; the inference cost of a petabyte-scale world model would be astronomical. The Mobius architecture we propose natively supports latent reasoning, offering a stronger prior for continuous-space modeling and demonstrating greater potential than Transformer.

然而，莫比乌斯先前引入的潜在推理可能被证明还远远不够。如何更好地重新设计注意力机制可能更为关键。Nevertheless, the latent reasoning prior introduced by Mobius may prove far from sufficient; how to better reengineer the attention mechanism may be of even greater criticality.

5.3 科学发现Scientific Discovery

当代人工智能在代码和数学方面已经取得了令人瞩目的熟练程度，并且最近的科学基础模型在解决程序问题方面表现出色Contemporary AI has achieved remarkable proficiency in code and mathematics, and recent scientific foundation models excel at procedural problem-solving[57, 5]。然而，使模型能够制定颠覆性的科学假设和想法仍然是一个开放的挑战. Nevertheless, enabling models to formulate disruptive scientific hypotheses and ideas remains an open challenge[47, 23, 68].

我们的立场是，Our position is that,当前 Transformer 架构的范式与长链思想相结合并不能满足科学发现的先决条件。the current paradigm of Transformer architecture combined with long chains of thought does not satisfy the prerequisites for scientific discovery.在目前的范式下，模型擅长解决程序问题。然而，科学发现不仅需要程序能力，还需要科学直觉以及知识的构成和概括。莫比乌斯架构从两个方面解决了这个问题：一方面，它将深思熟虑内化为一个连续可微的潜在空间Under the present paradigm, models excel at procedural problem-solving; however, scientific discovery demands not only procedural competence but also scientific intuition and the composition and generalization of knowledge. The Mobius architecture addresses this on two fronts: on one hand, it internalizes deliberation into a continuously differentiable latent space[30, 62]，为模型提供发展更强大直觉的机会；另一方面，它扁平化了模型中的所有知识，使更多的知识能够同时交互，并大大增加了跨知识领域组合泛化的可能性。, affording the model the opportunity to develop more powerful intuition; on the other hand, it flattens all knowledge within the model, enabling a greater volume of knowledge to interact simultaneously and substantially increasing the possibility of compositional generalization across knowledge domains.

尽管如此，莫比乌斯能否在现实科学发现场景中表现更好，可能需要数据、基础设施、训练算法和优化算法的联合优化，还有待在未来的工作中验证。Nevertheless, whether Mobius can perform better in real-world scientific discovery scenarios likely requires the joint optimization of data, infrastructure, training algorithms, and optimization algorithms, and remains to be validated in future work.

5.4 软硬件协同设计Hardware-Software Co-Design

模型正在扩展到越来越大的参数数量，这表明更大的规模确实会产生新兴的智能，同时突破物理硬件的极限Models are being scaled to ever-larger parameter counts, demonstrating that greater scale does yield emergent intelligence, while simultaneously pushing the limits of physical hardware[36, 33, 6, 40, 56].

我们的立场是，Our position is that,目前 Transformer 架构与现有硬件系统的组合不满足进一步扩展的先决条件。the current combination of Transformer architecture and existing hardware systems does not satisfy the prerequisites for further scaling.这是因为 Transformer 架构将知识和推理混合在同一参数集中，因此需要在部署时将几乎所有参数加载到 GPU 内存中。我们提出的莫比乌斯架构展示了知识推理分离，开启了在GPU内存中仅稳定保留推理专用参数的可能性，同时主要将知识参数存储在SSD上，并按需检索高优先级知识并将其加载到内存中。This is because the Transformer architecture commingles knowledge and reasoning within the same parameter set, necessitating the loading of nearly all parameters into GPU memory at deployment. The Mobius architecture we propose exhibits knowledge-reasoning separation, opening the possibility of stably retaining only reasoning-dedicated parameters in GPU memory while storing knowledge parameters predominantly on SSD, with high-priority knowledge retrieved and loaded into memory on demand.

从长远来看，Mobius 比 Transformer 更适合软硬件协同设计。然而，在实现这一愿景之前，还需要付出大量努力。In the long term, Mobius is more amenable to hardware-software co-design than Transformer. However, substantial effort remains before this vision can be realized.
