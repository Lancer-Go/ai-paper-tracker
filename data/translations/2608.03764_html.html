1 简介Introduction

在人工智能时代，一旦任务可以In the AI era, once a task can be自动化的automated和and评价的evaluated，这个问题距离大规模解决已经不远了。为了将这两种能力引入代理任务解决中，代理自我进化已成为一种有前途的范例。在这种范式下，代理根据先前的交互并在后续相关任务中重用结果状态，通过更新其内部状态（例如参数状态和非参数状态（例如，记忆、技能、提示或利用代码））来不断提高任务性能, it is rarely far from being solved at scale. To bring these two capabilities to agentic task solving, agent self-evolution has emerged as a promising paradigm. Under this paradigm, an agent continuously improves task performance by updating its internal state, such as parametric states and non-parametric states (e.g., memory, skills, prompts, or harness code), based on prior interactions and reusing the resulting state in subsequent related tasks(Gao et al., 2026a)。自动化包括技术，例如持续学习. Automation includes techniques, such as continual learning(Wang et al., 2024b), 体验式学习, experiential learning(Shinn et al., 2023; Zhao et al., 2024)，以及递归的自我改进, and recursive self-improvement(Schmidhuber, 2007; Zhang et al., 2025)，用于执行这些内部更新。另一方面，评估决定这些更新是否真正改善了后续行为。尽管现在文献提供了许多自动化自我进化的技术，但评估方法仍然不太成熟。, for performing these internal updates. Evaluation, on the other hand, determines whether these updates actually improve subsequent behavior. Although the literature now offers many techniques to automate self-evolution, evaluation methodology remains less mature.

First, evolution-native benchmarks are designed to test whether an agent learns from training experience and transfers what it learns to held-out tasks. They deliberately construct the train–test split so that the capabilities an agent acquires on the training tasks are genuinely required and exercised on the test tasks(Zhang et al., 2026; Jiang et al., 2026; Gao et al., 2026b; Huang et al., 2026)。其次，进化自适应基准重新调整了最初为静态、单集能力测量而设计的任务套件，例如交互式推理环境和软件工程任务. Second, evolution-adaptive benchmarks repurpose task suites originally designed for static, single-episode capability measurement, such as interactive reasoning environments and software-engineering tasks(Shinn et al., 2023; Zhao et al., 2024; Jimenez et al., 2024; Merrill et al., 2026)。他们没有引入刻意的设计，而是直接将套件分为训练集和测试集，报告解决训练集时获得的经验是否提高了测试集的性能。. 对自我进化的正确评估需要精心设计的训练-测试关系。如果训练和测试任务相同或几乎相同，那么改进可能反映的是记忆而不是概括。如果它们不相关，则培训期间获得的经验可能不会为测试表现提供相关支持。因此，这些基准必须使用不同但相关的任务，并有意构建训练-测试分割，以便在训练期间获得的能力既是需要的，又是在保留的测试任务中得到锻炼的。The proper evaluation of self-evolution requires a carefully designed train–test relationship. If the training and test tasks are identical or nearly identical, improvements may reflect memorization rather than generalization. If they are unrelated, experience gained during training may provide no relevant support for test performance. These benchmarks must therefore use distinct but related tasks and deliberately construct the train–test split so that capabilities acquired during training are both required and exercised on held-out test tasks.

2.3 进化基准的进一步要求Further Requirements for Evolution Benchmarks

除了具有经济价值的工作流程和围绕可转移规则设计的训练-测试分割之外，进化基准还需要四个额外的属性。首先，他们应该通过可以刷新暴露任务的管道来抵抗数据污染Beyond economically valuable workflows and train–test splits designed around transferable rules, evolution benchmarks require four additional properties. Second, they should use deterministic, rule-based graders rather than LLM judges, so that scores are reproducible and each error can be traced to a specific rule violation. Third, they should treat tokens, agent turns, and monetary cost as first-class metrics alongside accuracy, because a good self-evolving agent should not only become more accurate but also more efficient. Finally, they should provide diagnostic views, such as score breakdowns and transfer heatmaps, that reveal where and how evolution succeeds. 为了使智能体真正从训练任务中学习可迁移规则并将其应用到测试任务中，我们引入了To make agents genuinely learn transferable rules from the training tasks and apply them to the held-out test tasks, we introduce规则杂交rule hybridization。首先，对于每个场景（成为一个任务组），管道将其示例的业务逻辑分解为. First, for each scenario (which becomes one task group), the pipeline decomposes the business logic of its examples into原子规则atomic rules，这是最小且可独立检查的决策规则。模型的世界知识中故意不存在这些规则；它们是特定于特定企业的内部约定，例如一家公司的赞助商地位优先级、另一家公司的黑名单排除以及特定于公司的发票到期日期。其次，管道将规则分散在 5 个训练任务中，因此每个训练任务仅公开这些规则的子集。代理尝试这些训练任务；因为世界知识中不存在隐藏规则，它一开始无法知道它们，并且很可能会失败。在进化过程中，我们提供各种监督信号，让智能体推断这些规则并将其记录在其技能中。第三，管道重新组合了 5 个测试任务的规则：测试任务可以一起调用优先级和黑名单规则，即使没有训练任务包含该组合。只有在训练期间学习了规则并能够编写和应用规则的代理才能解决测试任务。, which are minimal and independently checkable decision rules. These rules are deliberately absent from the model’s world knowledge; they are internal conventions specific to a particular enterprise, such as one company’s sponsor-status priority, another company’s blacklist exclusion, and a company-specific invoice expiration date. Second, the pipeline scatters the rules across the 5 training tasks, so each training task exposes only a subset of these rules. 合理Reasonable基地base准确性是必要的，但还不够。任务组可能会很困难，因为信​​息丢失或者因为其测试任务依赖于训练中从未出现的规则。相反，近乎完美的进化后准确性可能表明训练和测试过于相似。因此，我们在训练后校准初始难度和适度的、非饱和的改进。当一个小组校准失败时，我们会修改其任务设计、证据或训练-测试关系，而不是改变评分标准以迫使分数进入目标范围。校准运行使用不继承构建器上下文的新代理进程。accuracy is necessary but not sufficient. A task group may be difficult because information is missing or because its test tasks depend on rules that never appear in training. Conversely, near-perfect post-evolution accuracy may indicate that training and test are too similar. We therefore calibrate both initial difficulty and moderate, non-saturating improvement after training. When a group fails calibration, we revise its task design, evidence, or train–test relationship rather than changing rubric fractions to force the score into a target range. Calibration runs use fresh agent processes that do not inherit the builder’s context.

G.4 保持评估的可比性和可审计性Keeping Evaluation Comparable and Auditable

评估代理人必须有能力并保持固定。The evaluation agent must be capable and remain fixed.

我们采用We adopt anAI-评估-AIAI-evaluates-AI其中一个单独的评估代理组织对每个被评估代理的评估的协议。它暂存文件、启动容器、将生成的技能与测试尝试配对、调用确定性评分器、处理故障并汇总结果。评估代理本身不会分配语义分数，但其编排决策仍然会影响测量的结果。特别是，扮演此角色的能力较差的模型可能会错误地暂存文件、技能和尝试不匹配、无法恢复中断的容器或错误地聚合输出，从而导致与所测量的能力无关的评估失败。因此，我们使用功能足够的评估代理，并在可比较的模型-工具组合中保持其模型、版本、推理设置、提示、工具权限、重试策略和聚合规则固定。更改评估的代理不应同时更改组织实验的代理。protocol in which a separate evaluation agent organizes the assessment of each evaluated agent. It stages files, launches containers, pairs generated skills with test attempts, invokes deterministic graders, handles failures, and aggregates results. The evaluation agent does not assign semantic scores itself, but its orchestration decisions can still affect the measured outcome. In particular, a less capable model in this role may mis-stage files, mismatch skills and attempts, fail to recover interrupted containers, or aggregate outputs incorrectly, causing evaluation failures unrelated to the capability being measured. We therefore use a sufficiently capable evaluation agent and keep its model, version, reasoning setting, prompt, tool permissions, retry policy, and aggregation rules fixed across comparable model–harness combinations. Changing the evaluated agent should not simultaneously change the agent that organizes the experiment.

应保留痕迹和成本。Traces and costs should be preserved.

每次演化和测试尝试都会收到一个唯一的运行标识符、一个独立的工作区和一个专用的原始跟踪。测试过程仅解决一项任务，并且每次非基础测试尝试都使用相应进化尝试产生的技能。失败或受污染的尝试将被保留以供审核，并根据原始协议进行替换，而不是默默地评分为零或丢弃。Every evolution and test attempt receives a unique run identifier, an independent workspace, and a dedicated raw trace. A test process solves only one task, and each non-base test attempt uses the skill produced by the corresponding evolution attempt. Failed or contaminated attempts are retained for audit and replaced under the original protocol rather than silently scored as zero or dropped.

我们还将技能生成成本与测试时间成本分开报告。前者衡量获得可重用技能所需的投资；后者衡量应用它的效率。将两个阶段分开可以确定监管类型是否支付较大的一次性成本以换取下游重复的节省。We also report skill-generation cost separately from test-time cost. The former measures the investment required to acquire a reusable skill; the latter measures the efficiency of applying it. Keeping the two stages separate makes it possible to determine whether a supervision type pays a larger one-time cost in exchange for repeated downstream savings.

总之，自动自我进化基准生成产生的不仅仅是一个任务集。它还需要设计好的训练-测试关系、独立的信息预算、确定性分级器、校准证据、独立审查和尝试级跟踪。这些控制将可转移的改进与答案泄漏、模板重用、评分工件和执行失败区分开来。In summary, automated self-evolution benchmark generation produces more than a task set. It also requires a designed train–test relationship, isolated information budgets, deterministic graders, calibration evidence, independent review, and attempt-level traces. These controls distinguish transferable improvement from answer leakage, template reuse, scoring artifacts, and execution failures.
