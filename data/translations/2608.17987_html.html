1. 简介Introduction

社交媒体重塑了政治话语，使个人能够大规模表达、强化和传播意识形态观点Social media has reshaped political discourse, enabling individuals to express, reinforce, and disseminate ideological views at scale(29)。在这种环境下，令人担忧的是回声室的形成和政治日益两极分化。这些影响在算法管理和社交集群放大现有偏见的平台上尤其明显。在美国，政治观点常常按照党派路线划分——通常被贴上“左”（民主党）和“右”（共和党）的标签。这些区别不仅影响选举行为，还影响数字平台上的参与模式. A concern in this environment is the formation of echo chambers and the increasing polarization of politics. These effects are particularly visible on platforms where algorithmic curation and social clustering amplify existing biases. In the United States, political opinions are often divided along partisan lines—commonly labeled as “left” (Democratic) and “right” (Republican). These distinctions influence not only electoral behavior but also patterns of engagement on digital platforms(12; 11)。这种情况强调了审查这些意识形态差异的重要性，这些差异对投票行为和社交媒体激进主义有重大影响。在本文中，我们的目标是提供一个全面的方法论框架，不仅阐明这些现象，而且有助于数字政治环境的明智决策，从而满足当代政治话语的重要需求。. This scenario underscores the importance of examining these ideological differences, which have a significant impact on voting behavior and social media activism. In this paper, we aim to contribute a comprehensive methodological framework that not only elucidates these phenomena but also aids in informed policy-making for digital political environments, thereby addressing a vital need in contemporary political discourse.

4.1. 政治意识形态检测网Political Ideology Detection Network

政治意识形态检测涉及分析社交媒体帖子，以确定 1) 它们是否具有政治相关性，如果是，2) 它们在多大程度上符合政治光谱。该任务不是不加区别地为所有输入分配分数，而是首先过滤掉非政治内容，然后估计被确定为政治的帖子的意识形态分数。我们提出的网络通过结合无监督域适应 (UDA)、使用 LLM 的文本样式传输 (TST) 和高效的模型架构来解决这些双重挑战。Political ideology detection involves analyzing social media posts to determine 1) whether they are politically relevant and, if so, 2) to what extent they align along the political spectrum. Rather than assigning scores to all inputs indiscriminately, the task first filters out non-political content and then estimates an ideology score for posts identified as political. Our proposed network addresses these dual challenges through a combination of unsupervised domain adaptation (UDA), text style transfer (TST) using LLMs, and an efficient model architecture.

虽然法学硕士表现出强大的 ICL 能力While LLMs show strong ICL capabilities(9; 22)，它们直接用于意识形态检测等大规模任务面临着高推理延迟。添加上下文示例可以延长输入并增加注意力计算, their direct use in large-scale tasks like ideology detection faces high inference latency. Adding in-context examples lengthens the input and increases attention computation(88; 63; 82)。法学硕士在定义明确、资源丰富的任务上也可能表现不佳. LLMs can also underperform fine-tuned models on well-defined, high-resource tasks(61)，限制了它们对实时或高吞吐量使用的适用性。 因此，我们采用基于 BERT 的架构，它在任务性能和效率之间提供了更强的权衡——特别是在推理速度和资源使用方面, limiting their suitability for real-time or high-throughput use. We therefore adopt a BERT-based architecture, which offers a stronger trade-off between task performance and efficiency—especially in inference speed and resource usage(68)。 LLM 保留用于离线 TST 阶段，其中延迟不太重要，而不是核心检测管道。. LLMs are reserved for the offline TST stage, where latency is less critical, rather than the core detection pipeline.

在此架构决策的基础上，我们的检测管道将基于离线 LLM 的风格适应与基于 BERT 的在线分类器集成在一起。具体来说，考虑到正式新闻文章和非正式社交媒体帖子之间存在显着的风格差异，我们应用 TST 模块将新闻内容转换为类似于 X 等平台的风格。这种风格对齐缩小了标记源域（新闻）和未标记目标域（社交媒体）之间的域差距，从而提高了可转移性。 为了进一步弥合这一差距，我们结合了 UDA 来调整政治新闻和未标记的社交媒体帖子的潜在表示。我们还通过混合政治新闻文章和非政治社交媒体内容来增强训练，使模型能够共同学习 1) 政治相关性分类和 2) 相关帖子的意识形态评分。这种统一的方法可以在嘈杂的现实社交媒体环境中进行稳健的检测，同时利用来自传统媒体的结构化意识形态信号。Building on this architectural decision, our detection pipeline integrates offline LLM-based style adaptation with an online BERT-based classifier. Specifically, given the substantial stylistic differences between formal news articles and informal social media posts, we apply a TST module to transform news content into a style resembling that of platforms such as X. This stylistic alignment narrows the domain gap between the labeled source domain (news) and the unlabeled target domain (social media), thereby improving transferability. To further bridge this gap, we incorporate UDA to align the latent representations of political news and unlabeled social media posts. We also augment training with a mixture of political news articles and non-political social media content, allowing the model to jointly learn 1) political relevance classification and 2) ideology scoring for relevant posts. This unified approach enables robust detection in noisy, real-world social media environments, while leveraging structured ideological signals from traditional media.

4.2. 政治意识形态预测网络Political Ideology Prediction Network

政治意识形态预测对用户的立场如何随着时间的推移而演变进行建模——由于缺乏未来的帖子，这项任务与静态检测根本不同。因此，传统的后级嵌入方法不适用。我们采用时态图神经网络（TGNN），它非常适合意识形态预测。这种选择的动机有两个：社交媒体平台自然地生成动态用户交互图，而 TGNN 旨在对这种不断发展的结构中的时间依赖性进行建模。Political ideology prediction models how users’ stances evolve over time—a task fundamentally different from static detection due to the absence of future posts. This choice is motivated by two factors: social media platforms naturally produce dynamic user interaction graphs, and TGNNs are designed to model temporal dependencies in such evolving structures.

A.1.1。A.1.1.数据隐私Data Privacy

鉴于政治意识形态的敏感性，隐私是我们数据处理的核心。所有新闻数据均来自公共网站。社交媒体数据已匿名，以满足道德标准。在 X 和 Truth Social 上，个人标识符（例如姓名、账号）被数字 ID 取代。只保留公共职位；没有使用私人用户数据。Given the sensitivity of political ideology, privacy is central to our data handling. All news data were sourced from public websites. Social media data were anonymized to meet ethical standards. On X and Truth Social, personal identifiers (e.g., names, handles) were replaced with numeric IDs. Only public posts were retained; no private user data was used.

数据发布格式。Data Release Format.为了尊重底层来源的条款，发布的数据集分布在To respect the terms of the underlying sources, the released datasets are distributed in a脱水的dehydrated形式。对于 X (Twitter) 和 Truth Social 语料库，我们发布帖子和用户标识符以及派生注释（政治相关性标签、意识形态分数和用于构建图表的时间交互边缘），而不是原始帖子文本；用户可以根据其服务条款通过相应平台 API 重新水化文本。对于新闻语料库，我们发布文章 URL、媒体名称、出版日期和每个媒体的 AllSides 意识形态得分，而不是完整的文章文本，后者仍然是出版媒体的财产。以这种方式重新分发的 AllSides 评级受 CC BY-NC 4.0 许可证保护，并提供该许可证所需的归属；因此，发布的收藏品仅供非商业研究用途。存储库中提供了用于再水化和复制论文中每个表格的脚本，网址为form.
