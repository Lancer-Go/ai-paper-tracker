1. 简介Introduction

多模式奖励模型 (MRM) 已成为现代训练流程中不可或缺的一部分，在过滤高质量数据方面发挥着关键作用Multimodal reward models (MRMs) have become indispensable in modern training pipelines, serving critical roles in filtering high-quality data(Chen et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2025a)，为强化学习提供奖励信号, providing reward signals for reinforcement learning(Ouyang et al., 2022; Lin et al., 2024)，并在测试时间扩展中实现可靠的评估, and enabling reliable assessment in test-time scaling(Zhou et al., 2025b; Venktesh et al., 2025)。虽然基于文本和图像的奖励模型取得了显着的进步.
