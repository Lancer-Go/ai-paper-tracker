告诉我为什么：设计一个可解释的基于法学硕士的对话系统Tell Me Why: Designing an Explainable LLM-based Dialogue System
用于学生问题行为诊断for Student Problem Behavior Diagnosis

Zhilin Fan    Deliang Wang    Penghe Chen    Yu Lu1,2(🖂)
摘要Abstract

诊断学生的问题行为需要教师综合多方面的信息，识别行为类别，并规划干预策略。尽管经过微调的大语言模型（LLM）可以通过多轮对话支持这一过程，但它们很少解释为什么推荐某种策略，从而限制了透明度和教师的信任。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一个基于微调的法学硕士的可解释对话系统。 该系统使用基于可解释人工智能（xAI）的分层归因方法来识别每个推荐的对话证据，并根据该证据生成自然语言解释。 在技​​术评估中，该方法在识别支持证据方面优于基线方法。在对 22 名职前教师进行的初步用户研究中，收到解释的参与者对系统的信任度更高。这些发现为提高教育对话系统中法学硕士的可解释性提供了一个有希望的方向。Diagnosing student problem behaviors requires teachers to synthesize multifaceted information, identify behavioral categories, and plan intervention strategies. Although fine-tuned large language models (LLMs) can support this process through multi-turn dialogue, they rarely explain why a strategy is recommended, limiting transparency and teachers’ trust. To address this issue, we present an explainable dialogue system built on a fine-tuned LLM. The system uses a hierarchical attribution method based on explainable AI (xAI) to identify dialogue evidence for each recommendation and generate a natural-language explanation based on that evidence. In technical evaluation, the method outperformed baseline approaches in identifying supporting evidence. In a preliminary user study with 22 pre-service teachers, participants who received explanations reported higher trust in the system. These findings suggest a promising direction for improving LLM explainability in educational dialogue systems.

1 简介Introduction

学生的问题行为，例如攻击性和违反规则，会对学生的心理健康和学业发展产生负面影响Student problem behaviors, such as aggression and rule breaking, can negatively affect students’ psychological well-being and academic development[10]。诊断这些行为需要教师综合有关学生、家庭和学校背景的信息，识别行为类别并计划干预措施. Diagnosing these behaviors requires teachers to synthesize information about the student, family, and school context, identify behavioral categories, and plan interventions[2]。这个过程具有挑战性，因为它借鉴了多个领域的知识，包括心理学和教育学. This process is challenging because it draws on knowledge from multiple domains, including psychology and pedagogy[1].

最近的研究表明，微调的大语言模型（LLM）可以通过多轮对话支持学生问题行为诊断，并生成更符合专家实践的干预策略Recent studies suggest that fine-tuned large language models (LLMs) can support student problem behavior diagnosis through multi-turn dialogue and generate intervention strategies more consistent with expert practice[1, 2]。然而，这些系统通常会推荐策略，但不会解释它们为何合适。因为教师对他们选择的策略负有专业责任. However, these systems typically recommend strategies without explaining why they are appropriate. Because teachers are professionally accountable for the strategies they choose[5]，缺乏解释可能会限制他们对建议的信任和使用。, this lack of explanation may limit their trust in and use of the recommendations.

为了解决这一差距，我们提出了一个基于微调的法学硕士的可解释对话系统。对于每个推荐的策略，系统都会识别教师提供的对话证据，并根据该证据生成自然语言解释。基于最新的法学硕士可解释人工智能 (xAI) 归因方法To address this gap, we present an explainable dialogue system built on a fine-tuned LLM. For each recommended strategy, the system identifies teacher-provided dialogue evidence and generates a natural-language explanation grounded in that evidence. Building on recent explainable AI (xAI) attribution methods for LLMs[9, 3]，我们采用分层归因方法，首先定位最有影响力的对话回合，然后识别该回合内的教师句子。我们解决两个研究问题（RQ）：, we adopt a hierarchical attribution method that first locates the most influential dialogue turn and then identifies the teacher sentence within that turn. We address two research questions (RQs):

RQ1。RQ1.分层归因方法可以在多大程度上识别支持推荐策略的对话证据？To what extent can the hierarchical attribution method identify dialogue evidence that supports recommended strategies?

RQ2。RQ2.解释如何影响教师对可解释诊断对话系统的信任？How do explanations influence teachers’ trust in the explainable diagnostic dialogue system?

2 系统设计与实现System Design and Implementation

2.1 系统设计System Design

图Figure1总结了可解释的诊断对话系统，它由summarizes the explainable diagnostic dialogue system, which consists of a对话模块dialogue module进行多轮诊断和干预策略推荐，以及for multi-turn diagnosis and intervention strategy recommendation, and an解释模块explanation module用于识别对话证据并生成自然语言解释。for identifying dialogue evidence and generating natural-language explanations.

该系统由两个设计决策决定。首先，因为通用法学硕士缺乏学生问题行为诊断所需的主动诊断探究能力The system is shaped by two design decisions. First, because general-purpose LLMs lack the proactive diagnostic inquiry capabilities required for student problem behavior diagnosis[2]，对话模块建立在经过微调的LLM之上，可以主动从教师那里收集和综合多方面的学生信息。其次，因为自己生成的法学硕士解释可能听起来似乎合理，但没有忠实反映推荐的基础, the dialogue module is built on a fine-tuned LLM that can proactively collect and synthesize multifaceted student information from teachers. Second, because self-generated LLM explanations may sound plausible without faithfully reflecting the basis of a recommendation[11]，解释模块首先通过 xAI 归因识别对话证据，然后提示经过微调的 LLM 生成自然语言解释。, the explanation module first identifies dialogue evidence via xAI attribution and then prompts the fine-tuned LLM to generate a natural-language explanation.

4 结论和未来方向Conclusions and Future Directions

本研究探讨了如何在学生问题行为诊断的背景下使基于法学硕士的对话系统对教师来说更容易解释。我们提出了一个可解释的对话系统，将基于微调 LLM 的对话模块与基于 xAI 归因方法的解释模块相结合。对于每个推荐的干预策略，解释模块都会识别最有影响力的对话回合，根据必要性和充分性对教师说出的句子进行排序，最后使用经过微调的法学硕士生成基于所选证据的自然语言解释。我们通过技术评估和对 22 名职前教师进行的初步用户研究来评估该方法。This study explores how to make LLM-based dialogue systems more explainable for teachers in the context of student problem behavior diagnosis. We present an explainable dialogue system, combining a dialogue module built on a fine-tuned LLM with an explanation module based on an xAI attribution method. For each recommended intervention strategy, the explanation module identifies the most influential dialogue turn, ranks teacher-uttered sentences within that turn by necessity and sufficiency, and finally uses the fine-tuned LLM to generate a natural-language explanation grounded in the selected evidence. We evaluate the approach through both a technical evaluation and a preliminary user study with 22 pre-service teachers.

结果表明，分层归因方法在识别与推荐策略相关的对话证据方面比基线方法表现更好。用户研究的结果进一步表明，提供解释可以提高教师对系统的信任。这种模式与之前针对教师基于 xAI 的解释的工作大致一致Results suggest that the hierarchical attribution method performs better than baseline approaches in identifying dialogue evidence associated with recommended strategies. Findings from the user study further suggest that providing explanations may improve teachers’ trust in the system. This pattern is broadly consistent with prior work on xAI-based explanations for teachers[12, 6, 13]。总之，这些发现表明，使用 xAI 归因方法对对话证据进行基础解释可能是开发更值得信赖的基于法学硕士的教育对话系统的一个有希望的方向。. Together, these findings suggest that grounding explanations in dialogue evidence using xAI attribution methods may be a promising direction for developing more trustworthy LLM-based educational dialogue systems.

然而，应该注意一些限制。用户研究涉及一小部分职前教师，并且仅测量了自我报告的信任度，因此研究结果并不能确定解释是否可以改善下游决策或干预质量。此外，当前系统仅解释推荐的干预策略，并且归因方法侧重于单个最有影响力的对话回合，这可能会忽略分布在多个回合中的证据。因此，未来的工作应该评估具有更大、更多样化的教师群体的系统，将解释覆盖范围扩展到其他诊断组件，探索多轮和更丰富的解释形式，例如反事实解释However, several limitations should be noted. The user study involved a small sample of pre-service teachers and measured only self-reported trust, so the findings do not establish whether explanations improve downstream decision making or intervention quality. In addition, the current system explains only recommended intervention strategies, and the attribution method focuses on a single most influential dialogue turn, which may overlook evidence distributed across multiple turns. Future work should therefore evaluate the system with larger and more diverse teacher populations, extend explanation coverage to other diagnostic components, explore multi-turn and richer explanation forms such as counterfactual explanations[7]，并检查对完整语料库和更大的法学硕士的普遍性。, and examine generalizability to the full corpus and larger LLMs.
