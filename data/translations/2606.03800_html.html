4 实验设计Experiment Design

4.1 经济替代框架Economic Substitution Framing

我们将人工任务创作和自动增强之间的选择视为替代问题。给定两个具有不同单位成本和单位训练价值的训练数据源，它们的替代弹性是多少，以及在什么成本比率下，其中一个占主导地位？这反映了混合 LLM 推理中的成本质量路由公式We frame the choice between human task authoring and automated augmentation as a substitution problem. Given two training data sources with different per-unit costs and per-unit training value, what is their substitution elasticity, and under what cost ratios does one dominate? This mirrors the cost-quality routing formulation in hybrid LLM inference[19].

    交易率Trade rate：在边际训练价值方面，有多少个增强任务等于一项人类任务，无论是训练集金丝雀上的每个任务还是保留泛化上的每个手臂？: how many augmented tasks equal one human task in marginal training value, both per-task on training-set canary and per-arm on held-out generalization?

  2. 2.

    成本当量Cost equivalence：在相同的数据管理成本下，增强在保留泛化方面是否匹配或优于人类管理？: at the same data-curation cost, does augmentation match or outperform human curation on held-out generalization?

  3. 3.

    收益递减Diminishing returns：额外增强变体的边际价值在哪里低于生成它们的成本？: where does the marginal value of additional augmented variants drop below the cost of generating them?

  4. 4.

    故障模式覆盖范围Failure-mode coverage：与纯人类训练相比，增强手臂是否会导致更广泛的特工失败模式分布？: do augmented arms elicit a wider distribution of agent failure modes than human-only training?
