各地电价预测 后危机时代挪威的五个申办区Electricity price forecasting across Norway’s five bidding zones in the post-crisis era

My Thi Diem Phan Trung Tuyen Truong Hoai Phuong Ha Dat Thanh Nguyen Independent researcher Department of Mathematics, University of Oslo Department of Computer Science, The Arctic University of Norway Faculty of Medicine, University of Oslo
摘要Abstract

挪威的电力市场主要以水电为主，但2021-2022年的能源危机以及与欧洲大陆的更紧密一体化从根本上改变了价格形成，降低了根据历史数据校准的预测模型的可靠性。尽管迫切需要更新模型，但仍然缺乏评估所有结构多样化的挪威投标区的功能贡献的统一基准。在这里，我们对所有五个挪威 Nord Pool 竞标区的电价预测进行了综合评估。我们构建了一个跨越 2019 年至 2025 年的多模式每小时数据集，并使用严格的因果测试集评估了 8 个预测模型系列，包括 LightGBM、ARX 和高级深度学习架构。我们实施了稳健的滚动原点回测、留一组特征消融和条件机制分析来剖析模型性能和特征效用。我们的结果表明，LightGBM 在每个区域都实现了最佳性能，MAE 范围为 1.64 至 5.74 欧元/MWh，而 ridge ARX 模型在北部区域仍然是极具竞争力的线性基准。特征消融表明，仅依赖滞后价格和日历变量的模型可以实现高精度，并且通常可以匹配或超过完整的多模式集成。然而，条件制度分析表明，水库水位和天然气价格等外部特征对于分层预测误差仍然至关重要，这些误差在压力市场制度下持续增加。这凸显了模型可解释性和制度意识对于面临市场动态结构性变化的决策者的实用价值。Norway’s electricity market is heavily dominated by hydropower, but the 2021–2022 energy crisis and stronger integration with Continental Europe have fundamentally altered price formation, reducing the reliability of forecasting models calibrated on historical data. Despite the critical need for updated models, a unified benchmark evaluating feature contributions across all structurally diverse Norwegian bidding zones remains lacking. Here we present a comprehensive evaluation of electricity price forecasting across all five Norwegian Nord Pool bidding zones. We constructed a multimodal hourly dataset spanning 2019–2025 and evaluated eight forecasting model families including LightGBM, ARX, and advanced deep learning architectures using a strictly causal test set. We implemented robust rolling-origin backtesting, leave-one-group-out feature ablation, and conditional regime analysis to dissect model performance and feature utility. Our results show that LightGBM achieves the best performance in every zone with MAE ranging from 1.64 to 5.74 EUR/MWh, while the ridge ARX model remains a highly competitive linear benchmark in northern zones. Feature ablation reveals that models relying solely on lagged prices and calendar variables achieve high accuracy and often match or exceed full multimodal integration. However, conditional regime analysis demonstrates that external features like reservoir levels and gas prices remain crucial to stratify forecast errors, which consistently increase under stressed market regimes. This highlights the practical value of model interpretability and regime awareness for decision makers facing structural changes in market dynamics.

1 简介Introduction

20 世纪 90 年代末欧洲电力市场自由化后，电价预测 (EPF) 变得重要Electricity price forecasting (EPF) became important after the liberalisation of European electricity markets in the late 1990s[5]。在日前市场，价格对短期供求反应迅速，并且可能在同一天内大幅波动. In day ahead markets, prices respond quickly to short term supply and demand and can move sharply within the same day[28]。多年来，这项任务相对稳定，自回归模型捕获了大部分可预测的每日和每周结构. For many years, this task was relatively stable, and autoregressive models captured much of the predictable daily and weekly structure[28, 13]。这种情况在 2021 年和 2022 年发生了变化。天然气储存量低、疫情后需求复苏以及俄罗斯天然气供应中断将产权转让设施 (TTF) 天然气价格推至极端水平，并改变了整个欧洲的电价形成. That setting changed in 2021 and 2022. Low gas storage, post-pandemic demand recovery, and the disruption of Russian gas supply pushed title transfer facility (TTF) gas prices to extreme levels and changed electricity price formation across Europe[6, 21, 2, 1]。因此，在新政权下，根据危机前数据训练的模型变得不太可靠。. Models trained on pre-crisis data therefore became less reliable in the new regime.

挪威是研究这个问题的一个有用的环境。挪威约 88% 的电力生产来自水力发电Norway is a useful setting for studying this problem. Around 88% of Norwegian electricity production comes from hydropower[18]，但市场分为NO1-NO5五个竞价区，结构差异明显。南部地区更容易受到欧洲大陆价格信号的影响，而北部地区仍然更多地以水电为主，并且通常价格水平较低。这种对比在2021年NordLink和North Sea Link全面运营并将外部价格压力传导至挪威南部后变得更加强烈, but the market is split into five bidding zones NO1–NO5, with clear structural differences. The southern zones are more exposed to Continental European price signals, while the northern zones remain more hydro dominated and usually have lower price levels. This contrast became stronger after NordLink and North Sea Link entered full operation in 2021 and expanded the transmission of external price pressure into southern Norway[23, 24]。因此，挪威为具有不同市场动态的地区的危机后电价预测提供了强有力的测试案例。. Norway therefore offers a strong test case for post-crisis electricity price forecasting across zones with different market dynamics.

更广泛的 EPF 文献非常广泛，但它并没有完全回答挪威的危机后问题。基础审查表明，没有单一类别的模型能够在整个市场中占据主导地位The broader EPF literature is extensive, but it does not fully answer this Norwegian post-crisis question. Foundational reviews showed that no single class of model dominates across markets[28]。后来的工作确立了正则化线性模型、仔细的特征设计和长期样本评估的重要性. Later work established the importance of regularised linear models, careful feature design, and long out of sample evaluation[25, 13, 16]。最近的评论还记录了机器学习和深度学习方法的发展. Recent reviews also documented the move toward machine learning and deep learning methods[9]。然而，大部分基准证据仍然来自危机前的数据或来自挪威以外的市场. However, much of the benchmark evidence still comes from pre-crisis data or from markets other than Norway[13].

对于挪威和北欧市场来说，证据仍然有限。早期的研究重点是 Nord Pool 系统价格、每周设置或结构性突破之前的跨境影响For Norway and the Nordic market, the evidence is still limited. Earlier studies focused on the Nord Pool system price, weekly settings, or cross border effects before the structural break[11, 12, 14]。其他工作考虑了北欧每月总体预测、短危机期窗口或未经严格因果评估的环境. Other work considered monthly Nordic aggregate forecasting, short crisis-period windows, or settings without strict causal evaluation[17, 26, 19]。最近一项关于 NO4 的研究考察了长期情景设置，而不是提前一天的基准. A recent study on NO4 examined a long term scenario setting rather than a day ahead benchmark[22]。因此，文献仍然缺乏针对挪威所有五个投标区的统一的危机后基准，以及对功能贡献的严格评估和分析。. As a result, the literature still lacks a unified post-crisis benchmark across all five Norwegian bidding zones with rigorous evaluation and analysis of feature contributions.

2.3 特征工程Feature engineering

功能分为六组，如补充表 S3 中详述。日历功能使用正弦余弦投影来保留圆形拓扑（例如，23 小时和 0 小时在编码空间中相邻）。所有滞后特征都使用因果变化，滚动统计数据在窗口化之前移动一个周期以排除当前小时。这可以防止交付时间或之后的任何信息进入特征集，从而确保整个特征矩阵中严格的因果完整性。更广泛地说，特征构造旨在反映实时信息集。所有输入仅基于预测时可用的信息，避免任何前瞻偏差。每周水库数据向前填充至每小时分辨率，以便每小时继承最近发布的每周释放数据。每日商品价格同样按小时分辨率向前填充，确保数据源之间的一致性。季节性特征，包括月均值、自相关函数 (ACF)、星期几分布和温度价格散布，在补充图 S1 中进行了可视化。Features are organised into six groups as detailed in Supplementary Table S3. Calendar features use sine cosine projections to preserve circular topology (e.g., hour 23 and hour 0 are adjacent in encoding space). All lag features use causal shifts, with rolling statistics shifted by one period prior to windowing to exclude the current hour. This prevents any information from the delivery hour or later from entering the feature set, ensuring strict causal integrity throughout the feature matrix. More broadly, the feature construction is designed to reflect the real time information set. All inputs are based solely on information available at the time of prediction, avoiding any look ahead bias. Weekly reservoir data are forward filled to hourly resolution, such that each hour inherits the most recently published weekly release. Daily commodity prices are similarly forward filled to hourly resolution, ensuring consistency across data sources. This design makes it possible to assess not only whether prediction errors increase under stressed hydro or gas conditions, but also whether the full feature set provides a meaningful advantage over the reduced autoregressive-seasonal model in those states. To summarize the contribution of each regime variable, we also compute two marginal contrasts from the four-cell decomposition. The marginal gas effect is defined as the average sMAPE in high-TTF cells minus the average sMAPE in low-TTF cells, so that positive values indicate reduced forecast accuracy when gas prices are high. This framework provides an interpretable way to evaluate whether model errors are concentrated in specific hydro-gas market states, and whether such regime dependence is already captured by lagged prices and calendar structure or requires the broader feature set used by the full LightGBM model.

4 讨论Discussion

4.1 危机前与危机后数据Pre-crisis versus post-crisis data

训练窗口比较表明，危机后数据是必要的，但还不够。仅在 2022-2023 年训练的模型通常优于仅在 2019-2021 年训练的模型，这证实了危机前的数据本身并不能很好地代表当前的状况。然而，整个 2019-2023 年窗口在表中的所有四个指标上给出了每个区域的最佳性能The training-window comparison shows that post-crisis data are necessary but not sufficient. Models trained only on 2022–2023 generally outperform models trained only on 2019–2021, which confirms that pre-crisis data alone do not represent the current regime well. However, the full 2019–2023 window gives the best performance in every zone across all four metrics in Table2。这表明，危机前的观察结果与危机后的数据相结合时仍然包含有用的信息。. This indicates that pre-crisis observations still contain useful information when they are combined with post-crisis data.

一种可能的解释是，危机前的样本有助于模型学习稳定的季节性和自回归结构，而危机后的样本则需要代表新的水力天然气价格机制。因此，主要含义并不是应丢弃较旧的数据，而是应将它们与来自当前市场状态的足够的最新数据一起使用。A likely explanation is that the pre-crisis sample helps the model learn stable seasonal and autoregressive structure, while the post-crisis sample is needed to represent the new hydro-gas price regime. The main implication is therefore not that older data should be discarded, but that they should be used together with enough recent data from the current market state.

4.2 自回归和外生信息Autoregression and exogenous information

特征消融结果表明自回归结构仍然是 挪威日前电价预测能力的主要来源 预测。消除滞后功能会使 MAE 增加 14.23–22.88 欧元/兆瓦时 NO1–NO5，而去除任何外源基团只会使 MAE 改变几个 大多数情况下为百分之一欧元/兆瓦时。日历变量是唯一的外生变量 组在所有区域提供清晰且一致的增益，但即使如此 相对于滞后价格的贡献，影响仍然不大。这种模式并不意味着水库、天气、商品和系统 预测变量没有提供信息。相反，这表明他们的大部分 短期预测内容已经编码在最近的价格中，这会起作用 作为市场对这些基本面反应的压缩摘要。在那 从某种意义上说，与滞后价格的内部相关性限制了增量价值 许多点预测准确性的外生预测因子。同时，制度分析表明，多模态变量仍然存在 对于诊断和解释很重要。储层异常和TTF天然气价格 除了滞后和日历之外，不会显着提高平均点精度 基线，但它们清楚地对预测的热液状态进行了分层 错误增加。因此，外生变量保留了大量的解释性 即使它们对测试集 MAE 的边际贡献很小，也具有价值。The feature ablation results show that autoregressive structure remains the dominant source of predictive power in Norwegian day-ahead electricity price forecasting. Removing lag features increases MAE by 14.23–22.88 EUR/MWh across NO1–NO5, whereas removing any exogenous group changes MAE by only a few hundredths of EUR/MWh in most cases. Calendar variables are the only exogenous group that provides a clear and consistent gain across all zones, but even this effect remains modest relative to the contribution of lagged prices. This pattern does not imply that reservoir, weather, commodity, and system forecast variables are uninformative. Rather, it suggests that much of their short-horizon predictive content is already encoded in recent prices, which act as a compressed summary of market responses to these fundamentals. In that sense, internal correlation with lagged prices limits the incremental value of many exogenous predictors for point forecasting accuracy. At the same time, the regime analysis shows that multimodal variables remain important for diagnosis and interpretation. Reservoir anomaly and TTF gas price do not materially improve average point accuracy beyond the lag and calendar baseline, but they clearly stratify the hydro-thermal states in which forecast errors increase. Exogenous variables therefore retain substantial explanatory value even when their marginal contribution to test-set MAE is small.

4.3 区域异质性Zone heterogeneity

基准和显着性结果揭示了两者之间的明显差异 南部区域（NO1、NO2、NO5）和北部区域（NO3、NO4）。光GBM 在每个区域中实现最低的 MAE，但它相对于 Ridge ARX 的优势是 仅在 NO1、NO2 和 NO5 中具有统计显着性。在NO3和NO4中，两者 模型的表现类似，表明已经有一个明确的线性模型 捕捉了这些市场中大部分可预测的结构。消融和制度结果强化了这种异质性。日历效果 NO1、NO2、NO5 最强，而水库的边际效应 州因区域而异。 NO1 中的低储层条件更难预测， NO2、NO5，但高库下预报误差略高 NO3 和 NO4 条件下。相比之下，高 TTF 价格增加了预测 所有五个区域都存在困难，使气体压力成为最一致的不利因素 整个系统的制度。总而言之，这些结果表明模型复杂性在以下方面最有用： 市场状况与非线性和非线性相关性更强的区域 外部耦合动力学。在价格形成较为规律的区域， LightGBM 相对于 Ridge ARX 的实际优势甚至变得更小 尽管 LightGBM 仍然在 MAE 中排名第一。The benchmark and significance results reveal a clear difference between the southern zones (NO1, NO2, NO5) and the northern zones (NO3, NO4). LightGBM achieves the lowest MAE in every zone, but its advantage over Ridge ARX is statistically significant only in NO1, NO2, and NO5. In NO3 and NO4, the two models perform similarly, indicating that a well-specified linear model already captures most of the predictable structure in those markets. The ablation and regime results reinforce this heterogeneity. Calendar effects are strongest in NO1, NO2, and NO5, while the marginal effect of reservoir state differs by zone. Low-reservoir conditions are harder to predict in NO1, NO2, and NO5, but forecast errors are slightly higher under high-reservoir conditions in NO3 and NO4. By contrast, high TTF prices increase forecast difficulty in all five zones, making gas stress the most consistent adverse regime across the system. Taken together, these results indicate that model complexity is most useful in zones where market conditions are more strongly associated with non-linear and externally coupled dynamics. In zones where price formation is more regular, the practical advantage of LightGBM over Ridge ARX becomes much smaller even though LightGBM still ranks first by MAE.

4.4 跨时间和制度的稳健性Robustness across time and regimes

滚动原点回测表明 LightGBM 在以下方面优于 Naïve-24h 2025 年的每周都会在所有五个申办区进行，确认其 优越性并不是由测试周期的一小部分驱动的。这个暂时的 稳定性强化了基准测试结果并支持模型的使用 在危机后的广泛市场条件下。体系分析为这一结果添加了重要的限定条件。虽然 简化的 Lags+Calendar 模型与完整的 LightGBM 模型非常匹配 机制单元通常表现稍好，但预测误差仍然增加 在特定的氢气状态下尤其明显，特别是在高 TTF 条件下 在南部地区的低水库州。这表明主要 预测信号已经被自回归和季节性结构捕获， 但市场状态变量对于理解预测仍然至关重要 变得更加困难。失效分析与这种解释是一致的。最大的 预测错误集中在价格急剧上涨的情况下，通常发生在周一或周五 周二早上，最近的滞后几乎没有提供突然政权的警告 转变。这些事件显示了滞后主导预测的实际边界。 模型平均表现强劲，同时仍然容易受到罕见情况的影响 和快速变化的价格飙升。The rolling-origin backtest shows that LightGBM outperforms Naïve-24h in every week of 2025 across all five bidding zones, confirming that its superiority is not driven by a narrow subset of the test period. This temporal stability strengthens the benchmark findings and supports the use of the model under a wide range of post-crisis market conditions. The regime analysis adds an important qualification to this result. Although the reduced Lags+Calendar model closely matches the full LightGBM model across regime cells and often performs slightly better, forecast errors still increase markedly in specific hydro-gas states, especially under high TTF conditions and in low-reservoir states in the southern zones. This indicates that the main forecast signal is already captured by autoregressive and seasonal structure, but market-state variables remain essential for understanding when prediction becomes more difficult. The failure analysis is consistent with this interpretation. The largest forecast errors cluster around sharp upward price movements, often on Monday or Tuesday mornings, when recent lags provide little warning of an abrupt regime shift. These events show the practical boundary of lag dominated forecasting. Models can perform strongly on average while still remaining vulnerable to rare and fast-moving price spikes.

4.5 局限性Limitations

应该注意几个限制。首先，深度学习模型是 经过基本优化训练；更精细的架构优化可能会缩小与LightGBM的差距。二、研究重点 点预测，而概率预测会提供更多信息 用于极端价格不确定条件下的运营决策。Several limitations should be noted. First, the deep learning models are trained with basic optimization; more delicate architectural optimisation may narrow the gap to LightGBM. Second, the study focuses on point forecasting, whereas probabilistic forecasts would be more informative for operational decision-making under extreme price uncertainty.

第三，天气输入使用有限的空间在区域级别表示 摘要，可能无法完全反映当地需求或供应相关的情况 条件。第四，虽然特征消融和状态分析是 扩展到所有五个区域，它们仍然是描述性诊断，并且不 它们本身建立了水力、天然气和预测之间的因果关系 错误。最后，本分析评估了一组固定的模型和 功能组；替代方案，包括针对具体制度的模型或 水力和天然气变量之间更明确的相互作用，可能会提供进一步的信息 收益。Third, weather inputs are represented at zone level using a limited spatial summary, which may not fully capture local demand- or supply-relevant conditions. Fourth, although the feature ablation and regime analyses are extended to all five zones, they remain descriptive diagnostics and do not by themselves establish causal relationships between hydro, gas, and forecast error. Finally, the present analysis evaluates a fixed set of models and feature groups; alternative formulations, including regime-specific models or more explicit interactions between hydro and gas variables, may provide further gains.

Its advantage over Naïve-24h is large and consistent in both static and rolling-origin evaluation, and it is statistically significant against Ridge ARX in the southern zones NO1, NO2, and NO5.

主要方法论发现是滞后价格主导预测 性能。特征消融表明，删除滞后特征会导致大量 每个区域的准确性都会损失，而大多数外源组只提供 一旦滞后价格已经包含在内，边际收益就会很小。日历 变量是最稳健的次要特征组，而水库， 商品、天气、负荷或可再生能源预测变量贡献甚微 平均点精度的进一步改进。与此同时，外生变量对于理解模型行为仍然很重要。条件体系分析表明预测误差增加 在高 TTF 天然气价格条件下最为一致，而 不同投标区的水库状态有所不同。这表明多式联运 特征可能会为预测增加有限的增量价值，但仍然发挥作用 通过识别水热市场状态在诊断中发挥重要作用 哪些模型最困难。The main methodological finding is that lagged prices dominate predictive performance. Feature ablation shows that removing lag features causes a large loss of accuracy in every zone, whereas most exogenous groups provide only small marginal gains once lagged prices are already included. Calendar variables are the most robust secondary feature group, while reservoir, commodity, weather, and load or renewable forecast variables contribute little additional improvement to average point accuracy. At the same time, exogenous variables remain important for understanding model behaviour. The conditional regime analysis shows that forecast errors increase most consistently under high TTF gas-price conditions, while the effect of reservoir state differs across bidding zones. This indicates that multimodal features may add limited incremental value for prediction, yet still play an important role in diagnosis by identifying the hydro-thermal market states in which models struggle most.

总体而言，结果表明功能之间存在实际的分工。 滞后的价格和日历效应携带了大部分所需的信息 准确的日前点预测，而更广泛的外生变量是 对于解释预测风险和模型故障模式最有价值。未来 因此，工作的重点可能不仅在于提高平均准确度，还在于 开发政权感知或概率模型来更好地表征 预测误差变大的条件。Overall, the results suggest a practical division of labour between features. Lagged prices and calendar effects carry most of the information needed for accurate day-ahead point forecasts, whereas broader exogenous variables are most valuable for interpreting forecast risk and model failure modes. Future work may therefore focus not only on improving average accuracy, but also on developing regime-aware or probabilistic models that better characterize the conditions under which forecast errors become large.
