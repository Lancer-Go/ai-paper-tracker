摘要Abstract

稀疏自动编码器（SAE）广泛用于从神经网络表示中提取可解释的特征，通常是在概念对应于独立线性方向的隐含假设下。 然而，越来越多的证据表明，许多概念是沿着编码连续几何关系的低维流形组织的。 这就提出了三个基本问题：SAE 捕获流形意味着什么？现有的 SAE 架构何时这样做以及如何这样做？ 我们开发了一个理论框架来回答这些问题，并表明 SAE 可以通过两种根本不同的方式捕获流形：Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) are widely used to extract interpretable features from neural network representations, often under the implicit assumption that concepts correspond to independent linear directions. However, a growing body of evidence suggests that many concepts are instead organized along low-dimensional manifolds encoding continuous geometric relationships. This raises three basic questions: what does it mean for an SAE to capture a manifold, when do existing SAE architectures do so, and how? We develop a theoretical framework that answers these questions and show that SAEs can capture manifolds in two fundamentally different ways:全球globally，通过分配一个 其线性跨度包含整个流形的紧密原子团，或, by allocating a compact group of atoms whose linear span contains the entire manifold, or本地locally，通过将其分布到每个特征上，每个特征有选择地, by distributing it across features that each selectively瓷砖tile底层几何形状的限制区域。 根据经验，我们发现 SAE 不能理想地恢复连续结构， 将全局子空间和局部平铺解决方案混合在我们称为的碎片体系中a restricted region of the underlying geometry. Empirically, we find that SAEs suboptimally recover continuous structures, mixing the global subspace and local tiling solutions in a fragmented regime we call稀释dilution。 这解释了为什么流形结构在单个概念的层面上很少可见，并激发了事后无监督发现方法，这些方法寻找连贯的原子组而不是孤立的方向。 更广泛地说，我们的结果表明，未来的表示学习方法应该将几何对象（而不仅仅是单个方向）视为可解释性的基本单位。. This explains why manifold structure is rarely visible at the level of individual concepts and motivates post-hoc unsupervised discovery methods that search for coherent groups of atoms rather than isolated directions. More broadly, our results suggest that future representation learning methods should treat geometric objects, not just individual directions, as the basic units of interpretability.

1 简介Introduction

受大型语言模型 (LLM) 能力前所未有的改进的推动，最近的工作试图理解为什么 LLM 会针对给定的输入产生特定的输出Motivated by unprecedented improvements in Large Language Models’ (LLMs) capabilities, recent work has sought to understand why an LLM produces a particular output for a given input(Sharkey et al., 2025)。 通常，此类工作会对神经网络表示的几何形状做出假设，特别认为数据生成过程背后的抽象概念（潜在因素）以“线性”方式表示. Often, such work makes assumptions about the geometry of neural network representations, arguing especially that abstract concepts (latent factors) underlying the data-generating process are represented in a “linear” fashion(Elhage et al., 2022; Olah, 2023; Arora et al., 2018; Jiang et al., 2024)。 称为. Called the线性表示假设 (LRH)Linear Representation Hypothesis (LRH) (Park et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2025)，这个几何模型认为神经网络的表示是多个方向的加法混合，每个方向编码一个特定的概念, this geometric model argues a neural network’s representations are an additive mixture of several directions, each encoding a specific concept(Elhage et al., 2022); 任何概念的值都可以通过线性映射从神经网络的隐藏表示中读出; any concept’s value can be read-out from a neural network’s hidden representations via a linear map(Belinkov, 2022); 线性代数运算足以操纵这个值; and linear algebraic operations suffice to manipulate this value(Mikolov et al., 2013; Korchinski et al., 2025; Karkada et al., 2025)。 因此，LRH 可以被视为神经网络表示的生成模型，其逆过程导致稀疏自动编码器（SAE）. LRH can thus be deemed as a generative model of neural network representations, the inverse of which leads to Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs)(Costa et al., 2025)——一种流行的工具，用于无监督地发现模型学到的概念—a popular tool used for unsupervised discovery of concepts learned by a model(Bricken et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2024; Bussmann et al., 2024; Rajamanoharan et al., 2024; Bussmann et al., 2025)，深深植根于稀疏编码的旧文献, with deep roots in the older literature on sparse coding(Olshausen and Field, 1996, 1997; Klindt et al., 2020, 2025)、稀疏子空间聚类, sparse subspace clustering(Elhamifar and Vidal, 2013; Abdolali and Gillis, 2021)和非线性流形学习, and nonlinear manifold learning(Tenenbaum et al., 2000; Roweis and Saul, 2000).

虽然 SAE 已大规模使用并取得了一些成功，例如调试大规模部署的神经网络While SAEs have been used at scale to some success, e.g., to debug neural networks deployed at scale(OpenAI, 2025; Nguyen et al., 2025)或识别通过表观遗传学模型学习的候选生物标志物or to identify candidate biomarkers learned by an epigenetics model(Wang et al., 2026)，最近越来越多的研究表明，神经网络表示的几何结构比 LRH 所暗示的更为复杂, a growing body of recent work has argued that geometry of neural network representations is more intricate than LRH suggests(Lubana et al., 2025; Fel et al., 2025b; Karkada et al., 2026; Dooms and Gauderis, 2025)：例如，周期性概念沿着圆形拓扑进行编码: e.g., periodic concepts are encoded along a circular topology(Modell et al., 2025; Kantamneni and Tegmark, 2025; Engels et al., 2024);沿着线性拓扑的开放式数值概念; open-ended numerical concepts along a linear topology(Gurnee et al., 2025; Yocum et al., 2025);上下文统计产生任意图结构表示; in-context statistics induce arbitrary graph structured representations(Park et al., 2025; Saanum et al., 2025; Sarfati et al., 2026);在基因组学中可以看到层次表示; hierarchical representations are seen in genomics(Pearce et al., 2025)和视觉语言模型and vision-language models(Costa et al., 2025);句法关系沿着极坐标系组织，该极坐标系共同编码依赖关系的存在和类型; syntactic relations are organized along a polar coordinate system that jointly encodes the existence and the type of dependencies(Diego-Simón et al., 2024);空间和时间上的平滑表示分别出现在视觉和语言模型中; and spatially and temporally smooth representations emerge in vision and language models, respectively(Chung et al., 2018; Cohen et al., 2020; Lubana et al., 2025; Hosseini et al., 2026; Dhimoila et al., 2026; Fel et al., 2025a; Gorton, 2024). 7 结论Conclusion

模型表示中结构化、非线性几何的存在表明解释的基本单位不必是孤立的方向。 虽然稀疏自动编码器原则上可以表示这种结构，但我们表明，在实践中它们以碎片化的方式这样做：流形不是被捕获为连贯的子空间，而是平铺在许多局部的、部分冗余的特征上。 这仅隐式地保留了几何形状，在单个特征的层面上模糊了几何形状并限制了基于方向的可解释性的可靠性。 展望未来，我们认为可解释性应该围绕几何结构的恢复和操纵而不是单个方向来重新构建。 这包括开发明确针对流形的特征化方法，以及设计将特征组作为连贯单元进行操作的分析工具。The presence of structured, nonlinear geometries in model representations suggests that the fundamental unit of interpretation need not be isolated directions. While sparse autoencoders can, in principle, represent such structures, we show that in practice they do so in a fragmented manner: manifolds are not captured as coherent subspaces, but are instead tiled across many localized, partially redundant features. This preserves geometry only implicitly, obscuring it at the level of individual features and limiting the reliability of direction-based interpretability. Moving forward, we argue that interpretability should be reframed around the recovery and manipulation of geometric structures rather than individual directions. This includes both developing featurization methods that explicitly target manifolds, and designing analysis tools that operate on groups of features as coherent units.

更广泛地说，我们的结果表明，理解神经网络需要从概念词典转变为表示的几何学——其中意义不是编码在单个原子中，而是编码在它们共同引发的结构中。More broadly, our results suggest that understanding neural networks requires shifting from a dictionary of concepts to a geometry of representations—where meaning is encoded not in single atoms, but in the structure they collectively induce.
