The gains are sharp on mathematical reasoning, competitive programming, and agentic benchmarks. The dominant paradigm extends the model’s chain of thought, either by encouraging longer traces(Jaech et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025)或者通过使用学习的价值函数搜索推理步骤or by searching over reasoning steps with a learned value function(Snell et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024)。 这两种方法本质上都是顺序的：额外的计算购买深度，而不是广度，并且单个早期失误会破坏其余的跟踪. 3 方法Method

3.1 设置Setup

设置问题。Problem setup.

我们研究了在三个同时约束下最大化 LLM 在硬推理任务上的准确性的问题：(i) 在 API 调用中测量的固定的每个问题计算预算，(ii) 挂钟时间限制，以及 (iii) 在推理时无法访问真实验证器或隐藏测试用例。仅提供问题陈述；求解器无法访问网络搜索、外部知识检索或工具使用。 挂钟约束是承载的：它排除了仅扩展单个推理链的方法，因为此类方法无法将额外的计算转换为减少的响应时间。We study the problem of maximizing an LLM’s accuracy on hard reasoning tasks under three simultaneous constraints: (i) a fixed per-problem compute budget measured in API calls, (ii) a wall-clock time limit, and (iii) no access to a ground-truth verifier or hidden test cases at inference time. Only the problem statement is available; the solver has no access to web search, external knowledge retrieval, or tool use. The wall-clock constraint is load-bearing: it rules out methods that merely extend a single reasoning chain, since such methods cannot convert additional compute into reduced response time.

6 结论Conclusion

OpenDeepThink 解决了测试时计算扩展并行化而不是深化时出现的选择瓶颈。该框架没有扩展单一的思想链，而是维护了一组OpenDeepThink addresses the selection bottleneck that emerges when test-time compute scaling is parallelized rather than deepened. Instead of extending a single chain of thought, the framework maintains a population ofnn候选人并不断发展他们candidates and evolves them overTT几代人。相同的 LLM 既充当生成器又充当成对判断器：Bradley-Terry 聚合产生软验证器，比较批评驱动反馈导向的突变。该管道以八个 LLM 调用的顺序深度运行。在 Codeforces 上，这将 Gemini 3.1 Pro 的有效 Elo 提升了generations. The same LLM acts as both generator and pairwise judge: Bradley–Terry aggregation yields a soft verifier, and the comparison critiques drive feedback-directed mutation. The pipeline runs at a sequential depth of eight LLM calls. On Codeforces, this lifts Gemini 3.1 Pro’s effective Elo by+405+405点，相同的超参数传输到 Gemini 3 Flash 和 Gemini 2.5 Pro，无需重新调整。在 HLE 上，当成对判断可靠时，增益保持不变，而当成对判断不可靠时，增益保持不变，因此软验证器的好坏取决于它汇总的比较。points, and the same hyperparameters transfer to Gemini 3 Flash and Gemini 2.5 Pro without retuning. On HLE, gains hold where pairwise judgment is reliable and reverse where it is not, so the soft verifier is only as good as the comparisons it aggregates.
