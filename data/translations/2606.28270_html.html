2.2  代理中的安全与健康融合2.2  The Security–Health Convergence in Agents

在经典的人工智能话语中，安全性（对抗鲁棒性、越狱抵抗性）和一致性（无害、诚实）通常被视为正交的。在主体范式中，这种区别崩溃了。攻击者可以将对齐的代理武器化，攻击者会毒害其内存存储或劫持其工具调用链。相反，具有强大外围防护措施但协调能力较差的特工可能会自主地追求其免疫系统无法识别为“非自我”的有害目标。In classical AI discourse, security (adversarial robustness, jailbreak resistance) and alignment (harmlessness, honesty) are often treated as orthogonal. In the agentic paradigm, this distinction collapses. An aligned agent can be weaponized by an attacker who poisons its memory store or hijacks its tool-calling chain. Conversely, an agent with robust perimeter safeguards but poor alignment may autonomously pursue harmful goals that its immune system fails to recognize as “non-self.”

ANIS 解决了保持代理认知循环完整性以防止恶意攻击（安全）和内源性目标漂移（健康）的统一问题。我们并不将安全和健康视为正交轴，而是将其视为单一免疫连续体的端点：安全是针对“非自我”（外部病原体）的防御；健康是“自我”的保存（目标稳定和内在完整性）。它们共同构成了个体代理人的福祉；当扩展到集体时，它们就构成了秩序。ANIS addresses the unified problem of preserving the integrity of the agent’s cognitive loop against both malicious attacks (security) and endogenous goal drift (health). We view security and health not as orthogonal axes, but as endpoints of a single immunological continuum: security is the defense against “non-self” (external pathogens); health is the preservation of “self” (goal stability and internal integrity).
