基本子空间合并Essential Subspace Merging for
多任务学习Multi-Task Learning

Longhua Li, Lei Qi, Xin Geng, , Qi Tian Longhua Li, Lei Qi and Xin Geng are with the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Southeast University, and Key Laboratory of New Generation Artificial Intelligence Technology and Its Interdisciplinary Applications (Southeast University), Ministry of Education, China, 211189 (e-mail: lhli@seu.edu.cn; qilei@seu.edu.cn; xgeng@seu.edu.cn).Qi Tian is with Huawei Inc., Shenzhen 518129, China (e-mail: tian.qi1@huawei.com).Corresponding authors: Lei Qi and Xin Geng.This is an extended version of the paper presented at CVPR 2026 [33].
摘要Abstract

模型合并旨在通过将从同一预训练检查点微调的多个模型的功能集成到单个模型中来实现多任务学习。其核心挑战是任务特定参数更新之间的任务间干扰。在本文中，我们分析了任务更新引起的输出变化，并观察到它们的能量集中在少数主要方向上。我们将这些方向所跨越的子空间称为本质子空间。相比之下，大多数剩余方向携带的与任务相关的能量很少，但它们在多个任务更新中的积累可能会在合并过程中造成严重干扰。受这一观察的启发，我们提出了基本子空间分解（ESD），它根据其激活转变的主要组成部分来分解每个任务更新。基于 ESD，我们引入了基本子空间合并（ESM），这是一种免训练的静态合并方法，可将基本组件正交并融合到一个紧凑的多任务模型中。我们进一步将 ESM 扩展到 ESM++，这是一种免训练的动态合并方法，可将特定于任务的残差分解为低等级专家，并在前向推理过程中通过基于原型的路由选择最相关的专家。 跨多个任务集和模型规模的大量实验表明，ESM 和 ESM++ 有效地保留了任务知识，同时减少了任务间干扰。Model merging aims to enable multi-task learning by integrating the capabilities of multiple models fine-tuned from the same pre-trained checkpoint into a single model. Its core challenge is inter-task interference among task-specific parameter updates. In this paper, we analyze the output shifts induced by task updates and observe that their energy is concentrated in a small number of principal directions. We call the subspace spanned by these directions the essential subspace. In contrast, most remaining directions carry little task-relevant energy, but their accumulation across multiple task updates can cause severe interference during merging. Motivated by this observation, we propose Essential Subspace Decomposition (ESD), which decomposes each task update according to the principal components of its activation shift. Based on ESD, we introduce Essential Subspace Merging (ESM), a training-free static merging method that orthogonalizes and fuses essential components into one compact multi-task model. We further extend ESM to ESM++, a training-free dynamic merging method that decomposes task-specific residuals into low-rank experts and selects the most relevant expert through prototype-based routing during forward inference. Extensive experiments across multiple task sets and model scales demonstrate that ESM and ESM++ effectively preserves task knowledge while reducing inter-task interference.

I 简介Introduction

近年来，预训练-微调范式产生了大量改编自相同预训练检查点的任务专用模型。模型合并In recent years, the pre-training–fine-tuning paradigm has produced large numbers of task-specialized models adapted from the same pre-trained checkpoint. Model merging[66, 24, 70, 56]旨在将这些经过微调的模型的功能集成到单个模型中，而无需额外的训练，从而为多任务学习提供免训练的途径。主要困难在于组合多个特定于任务的参数更新而不让它们相互干扰。aims to integrate the capabilities of these fine-tuned models into a single model without additional training, thereby providing a training-free route to multi-task learning. The central difficulty lies in composing multiple task-specific parameter updates without letting them interfere with one another.

出现任务间干扰是因为每个微调模型将其任务知识编码为相对于共享预训练模型的更新，并且这些更新可能包含对一项任务有用但对其他任务有害或无关的方向。简单的平均方法，例如 Model SoupInter-task interference arises because each fine-tuned model encodes its task knowledge as an update relative to the shared pre-trained model, and these updates may contain directions that are useful for one task but harmful or irrelevant for others. Simple averaging methods such as Model Soup[66]直接混合所有更新方向，因此有用的任务知识可能会被噪声或冲突的组件稀释。为了缓解这个问题，后续研究分析了任务向量，定义为微调模型和预训练模型之间的参数差异directly mix all update directions, so useful task knowledge can be diluted by noisy or conflicting components. To mitigate this issue, subsequent studies analyze task vectors, defined as the parameter differences between fine-tuned and pre-trained models[24, 69, 38, 25]。最近的方法进一步将奇异值分解（SVD）应用于任务向量，以识别低秩结构并删除冗余参数空间分量. More recent methods further apply Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) to task vectors to identify low-rank structures and remove redundant parameter-space components[54, 14, 37, 74]。然而，SVD 通过参数空间能量而不是它们对数据分布的功能影响来命令更新方向。尽管它截断了最小的奇异值，但它仍然可能会丢弃在频繁出现的输入上引起较大输出变化的方向，从而在输入标记与截断的奇异向量对齐时导致严重的功能错误，如方程中所量化. However, SVD orders update directions by parameter-space energy rather than by their functional effect on the data distribution. Although it truncates the smallest singular values, it may still discard directions that induce large output changes on frequently occurring inputs, leading to significant functional errors when input tokens align with truncated singular vectors, as quantified in Equation4。这种限制表明模型合并应该根据任务更新对输出激活的影响而不是单独的参数空间能量来分解任务更新。. This limitation suggests that model merging should decompose task updates according to their effect on output activations rather than parameter-space energy alone.

受这个观点的启发，我们通过任务更新引起的输出激活变化重新审视低秩现象。给定一个任务更新矩阵，我们对其引起的输出偏移进行主成分分析（PCA），并观察到能量仅高度集中在几个主方向上，如图 2 所示。Motivated by this perspective, we revisit the low-rank phenomenon through the output activation shifts caused by task updates. Given a task update matrix, we perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on its induced output shifts and observe that the energy is highly concentrated in only a few principal directions, as shown in Fig.1(a)。我们将这些主导方向跨越的子空间称为. We refer to the subspace spanned by these dominant directions as the基本子空间essential subspace，因为它捕获了对任务行为最负责的功能方向。相反，大多数剩余方向包含很少的激活转移能量，并且对任务本身贡献很小。然而，这些低能量方向在模型合并中并非无害：当在许多任务更新中累积时，它们可能会引入大量的跨任务干扰并同时降低所有任务的性能，如图 1 所示。, since it captures the functional directions most responsible for the task behavior. Conversely, most remaining directions contain very little activation-shift energy and contribute little to the task itself. Nevertheless, these low-energy directions are not harmless in model merging: when accumulated across many task updates, they can introduce substantial cross-task interference and degrade all tasks simultaneously, as illustrated in Fig.1(b).

在本文中，我们基于我们的初步工作，基本子空间合并（ESM）In this paper, we build on our preliminary work, Essential Subspace Merging (ESM)[33]，探索了 ViT 架构的激活转移感知模型合并。 ESM引入了一种静态免训练合并范式，其原则是有效的合并应将承载任务知识的必要方向与主要积累干扰的非必要方向分开，并应主要通过前者组成任务更新，而不需要重新训练或学习额外的参数。 为此，ESM 使用基本子空间分解 (ESD)。 ESD 对任务更新引起的输出激活变化执行 PCA，并使用主方向作为重要基础。然后将每个任务矩阵投影到该基础中，并且仅保留主要成分。由于 ESD 直接通过功能激活转移能量对方向进行排序，因此其截断误差仅取决于丢弃的特征值，从而使其更好地与在排序预算下保留任务行为保持一致。通过丢弃低能量的非必要方向，ESD 可以防止弱残留分量在任务中累积并成为主要干扰源。, which explored activation shift-aware model merging for ViT architectures. ESM introduces a static training-free merging paradigm based on the principle that effective merging should separate the essential directions carrying task knowledge from the non-essential directions that mainly accumulate interference, and should compose task updates primarily through the former without retraining or learning additional parameters. To this end, ESM uses Essential Subspace Decomposition (ESD). ESD performs PCA on the output activation shifts induced by a task update and uses the principal directions as an essential basis. Each task matrix is then projected into this basis, and only the dominant components are retained. Because ESD directly ranks directions by functional activation-shift energy, its truncation error depends only on the discarded eigenvalues, making it better aligned with preserving task behavior under a rank budget. By discarding the low-energy non-essential directions, ESD prevents weak residual components from accumulating across tasks and becoming a major source of interference.

我们进一步将 ESM 扩展到 ESM++，这是一种超越静态 ViT 模型融合的动态免训练合并框架。从静态合并的ESM模型开始，ESM++为每个任务构造特定于任务的动态合并参数：它用ESD分解每个微调专家与ESM基础模型之间的残差，将所得的低秩组件存储为特定于任务的专家，并通过基于原型的路由在前向推理过程中动态选择最相关的专家。此扩展保留了 ESM 学习到的紧凑共享表示，同时通过自适应组合恢复特定于任务的专业化。此外，我们将基本子空间合并的范围从 ViT 架构扩展到更广泛的设置，包括视觉模型、判别性语言模型和生成语言模型。We further extend ESM to ESM++, a dynamic training-free merging framework that goes beyond static ViT model fusion. Starting from the statically merged ESM model, ESM++ constructs task-specific dynamic merging parameters for each task: it decomposes the residual difference between each fine-tuned expert and the ESM base model with ESD, stores the resulting low-rank components as task-specific experts, and dynamically selects the most relevant experts during forward inference through prototype-based routing. This extension preserves the compact shared representation learned by ESM while recovering task-specific specialization through adaptive composition. Moreover, we broaden the scope of essential subspace merging from ViT architectures to a wider range of settings, including vision models, discriminative language models, and generative language models.

Ⅲ-DIII-D ESM++：动态基本子空间合并ESM++: Dynamic Essential Subspace Merging

ESM 生成一个单一的合并模型，捕获跨任务的共享知识。然而，静态组合过程不可避免地会淡化特定于任务的专业知识：特定任务特有的知识可能在正交化和任务间竞争期间被抑制。为了解决这个问题，我们引入了 ESM++，它将每个任务的低等级基本组件保留为单独的专家，并在推理时动态地组合它们，如图 1 所示。ESM produces a single merged model that captures shared knowledge across tasks. To address this, we introduce ESM++, which preserves the low-rank essential components of each task as separate experts and composes them dynamically at inference time, as shown in Fig.3。至关重要的是，ESM++ 保持免训练：它不学习额外的路由器，而是依赖离线收集的代理原型。 ESM++ 不是一种附加到 ESM 上的正交路由方法，而是同一基本子空间框架内的互补组合范式：两种范式共享相同的 ESD 分解原理，仅在分解知识的组合方式上有所不同 - 静态地组合到一个模型中，或通过每个输入的专家选择动态地组合。. V 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们从特定任务更新引起的输出激活变化的角度研究了模型合并。我们表明，这些变化集中在几个主要方向上，这些方向比参数空间分解更好地反映了功能变化，而累积的低能量方向可能导致合并干扰。受此启发，我们提出了基本子空间分解（ESD）来保留必要的更新组件，并开发了用于紧凑静态融合的ESM和用于动态低秩残差路由的ESM++。关于视觉和语言基准的大量实验表明，我们的框架实现了强大的性能和效率，为结构化和可靠的模型合并提供了原则性方法。In this paper, we studied model merging from the perspective of output activation shifts induced by task-specific updates. We showed that these shifts concentrate in a few principal directions that better reflect functional changes than parameter-space decomposition, while accumulated low-energy directions can lead to merging interference. Motivated by this, we proposed Essential Subspace Decomposition (ESD) to preserve essential update components, and developed ESM for compact static fusion and ESM++ for dynamic low-rank residual routing. Extensive experiments on vision and language benchmarks demonstrate that our framework achieves strong performance and efficiency, providing a principled approach to structured and reliable model merging.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and Future Work.尽管其有效性，当前的方法主要是为了合并共享相同架构并源自相同基础模型的模型而设计的，其中任务更新可以直接比较并在公共参数和激活空间中组合。将该框架扩展到更通用的场景，例如合并来自不同来源的模型、训练配方或架构，仍然是未来工作的重要方向。我们希望本质子空间视角能够激发更通用的模型融合方法，超越同质模型族。Despite its effectiveness, the current method is mainly designed for merging models that share the same architecture and originate from the same base model, where task updates can be directly compared and composed in a common parameter and activation space. Extending this framework to more general scenarios, such as merging models from different sources, training recipes, or architectures, remains an important direction for future work. We hope that the essential-subspace perspective can inspire more universal model fusion methods that operate beyond homogeneous model families.
