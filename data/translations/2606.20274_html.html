拉格朗日：一种开放词汇、基于能量的稀疏框架Lagrange: An Open-Vocabulary, Energy-Based Sparse Framework
用于广义的端到端驱动for Generalized End-to-End Driving

Shihao Ji, HongXi Li, Zihui Song, Mingyu Li
摘要Abstract

将端到端自动驾驶扩展到复杂的开放世界环境需要泛化到异常场景的感知模型和产生运动学有效轨迹的规划器。现有范式面临着表征效率和泛化能力之间的明显二分法。密集模型（例如占用网络）虽然在几何上具有鲁棒性，但会产生关键的计算瓶颈并难以进行高级语义推理。相反，基于查询的稀疏规划器虽然高效，但依赖于封闭集定义，这使得它们容易受到分布外 (OOD) 事件的影响。尽管最近的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型提供了开放词汇推理，但它们的自回归、离散标记生成从根本上与车辆动力学的连续、高频控制要求相冲突。Scaling end-to-end autonomous driving to complex, open-world environments requires perceptual models that generalize to anomalous scenarios and planners that produce kinematically valid trajectories. Existing paradigms face a distinct dichotomy between representational efficiency and generalization capacity. Dense models (e.g., occupancy networks), while geometrically robust, incur critical computational bottlenecks and struggle with high-level semantic reasoning. Conversely, sparse, query-based planners are efficient but reliant on closed-set definitions, rendering them vulnerable to out-of-distribution (OOD) events. Although recent Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models offer open-vocabulary reasoning, their autoregressive, discrete token generation fundamentally conflicts with the continuous, high-frequency control requirements of vehicle dynamics.

为了解决这个问题，我们提出了拉格朗日，一种基于掩蔽潜在场（MLF）的开放词汇、计算稀疏的驱动框架。拉格朗日不依赖密集体积重建或封闭集查询机制，而是利用视觉语言模型（VLM）将与类无关的对象建议编码为连续的语义视觉标记。我们引入了一个意图驱动的屏蔽交叉注意模块，该模块可以暂时过滤不相关的实体，将参与的标记解码为在空间坐标上定义的隐式连续能量场。通过将决策制定为跨越该能量场的拉格朗日动作最小化问题，我们在执行防撞时严格遵守车辆运动学。对标准（nuScenes）和长尾（CODA）基准的广泛离线评估表明，拉格朗日为稳健、可解释和运动学上可行的开放世界自治建立了一个有前途的框架。To address this, we propose Lagrange, an open-vocabulary, computationally sparse driving framework based on Masked Latent Fields (MLF). Rather than relying on dense volumetric reconstructions or closed-set query mechanisms, Lagrange exploits Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to encode class-agnostic object proposals into continuous semantic visual tokens. We introduce an intent-driven masked cross-attention module that temporally filters irrelevant entities, decoding the attended tokens into an implicit continuous energy field defined over spatial coordinates. By framing decision-making as a Lagrangian action minimization problem spanning this energy field, we enforce strict compliance with vehicle kinematics while executing collision avoidance. Extensive offline evaluations on both standard (nuScenes) and long-tail (CODA) benchmarks demonstrate that Lagrange establishes a promising framework for robust, interpretable, and kinematically feasible open-world autonomy.

1 简介Introduction

自动驾驶 (AD) 系统的设计逐渐从模块化、基于规则的管道转向端到端 (E2E) 神经架构，旨在最大限度地减少感知、预测和规划模块之间的复合错误。随着自主能力扩展到不受限制的开放世界环境，关于驾驶场景的最佳表示出现了基本的二分法：密集的体积网格与稀疏的实体公式。The design of autonomous driving (AD) systems has increasingly shifted from modular, rule-based pipelines toward end-to-end (E2E) neural architectures, aiming to minimize the compounding errors across perception, prediction, and planning modules. As autonomous capabilities scale to unrestricted open-world environments, a fundamental dichotomy has emerged regarding the optimal representation of the driving scene: dense volumetric grids versus sparse entity formulations.

主要通过 3D 占用网络和鸟瞰图 (BEV) 网格实例化的密集表示，在空间维度上连续地制定环境。尽管这些架构非常能够表示任意几何边界，但它们隐式地将语义视为黑匣子并产生大量计算开销。例如，密集网络从根本上难以区分结构相同但语义不同的体积之间的交互严重性，例如耗尽的烟羽与混凝土柱。Dense representations, predominantly instantiated through 3D Occupancy Networks and Bird’s-Eye View (BEV) grids, formulate the environment continuously across spatial dimensions. Although highly capable of representing arbitrary geometric boundaries, these architectures implicitly treat semantics as a black box and incur massive computational overhead. For example, dense networks fundamentally struggle to differentiate the interactive severity between structurally identical but semantically divergent volumes, such as an exhausted smoke plume versus a concrete pillar.

相反，稀疏范式通过离散对象查询来表示场景，从而规避了此类瓶颈，从而实现了显着的推理效率。然而，这些基于查询的方法将感知限制为闭集分类分布，定义了类标签的预定义白名单Conversely, the sparse paradigm circumvents such bottlenecks by representing the scene through discrete object queries, achieving significant inference efficiency. However, these query-based methods restrict perception to a closed-set categorical distribution, defining a predefined whitelist of class labelsci∈{Car,Pedestrian,Cyclist}c_{i}\in\{\text{Car},\text{Pedestrian},\text{Cyclist}\}。当遇到分布外（OOD）异常情况时，例如溢出的负载或无法识别的外来车辆，稀疏架构很容易出现频繁的漏报，从而使下游轨迹规划器在结构上对潜在危险视而不见。. When confronted with out-of-distribution (OOD) irregularities—such as spilled loads or unrecognized exotic vehicles—sparse architectures are prone to frequent false negatives, leaving the downstream trajectory planner structurally blind to potential hazards.

虽然最近对视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型的改编试图通过开放词汇推理来绕过这些限制，但它们在离散、自回归令牌生成中的架构基础与车辆动力学的连续、高频物理约束形成了根本的不匹配。直接语言到动作的翻译经常导致多模式状态空间中的不顺利执行或关键的均值寻求行为。While recent adaptations of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models attempt to bypass these limitations via open-vocabulary reasoning, their architectural basis in discrete, autoregressive token generation forms a fundamental mismatch with the continuous, high-frequency physical constraints of vehicular dynamics. Direct language-to-action translation frequently leads to non-smooth execution or critical mean-seeking behaviors in multimodal state spaces.

为了解决开放世界语义和连续动态控制之间的紧张关系，我们引入了拉格朗日。我们的框架以经典拉格朗日力学为概念根源，将大型视觉语言模型（VLM）的广泛开放词汇概括与基于能量的模型（EBM）的物理可解释性相结合。拉格朗日不是回归确定的轨迹矩阵或采样离散的 LLM 令牌，而是动态地将连续上下文投影到标量势能场中To resolve the tension between open-world semantics and continuous dynamical control, we introduce Lagrange. Taking conceptual root in classical Lagrangian mechanics, our framework integrates the vast open-vocabulary generalization of large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with the physical interpretability of Energy-Based Models (EBMs). Rather than regressing a determinate trajectory matrix or sampling discrete LLM tokens, Lagrange dynamically projects continuous context into a scalar potential energy fieldℰ​(x,y)\mathcal{E}(x,y), 其中, wherexx和andyy表示环境的平面空间坐标。在这个学习领域中，异常和语义上危险的实体天生映射到高潜力区域，而可航行空间则表现为能量谷。随后，驾驶任务被定义为解决最小动作原理优化问题。denote the planar spatial coordinates of the environment. In this learned field, anomalous and semantically hazardous entities innately map to regions of high potential, whereas navigable spaces manifest as energy valleys. The driving task is subsequently framed as solving a Principle of Least Action optimization problem.

具体来说，本文的主要贡献是围绕三个新颖的组成部分构建的。首先，我们设计了一个开放词汇表稀疏分词器，它提取在 VLM 派生的连续潜在空间中对齐的通用的、与类无关的区域建议。这建立了广泛的分布外鲁棒性，同时保留了以对象为中心的稀疏性的计算优势。在此标记器的基础上，我们开发了一个基于 Masked Latent Fields (MLF) 的意图驱动推理器。通过将时间运动状态查询注入屏蔽的交叉注意机制中，网络有效地隔离了上下文关键的语义实体，模仿了人类驾驶员的选择性注意优先级特征。最后，我们将驾驶轨迹生成公式化为拉格朗日动作最小化。通过将参与的语言视觉标记映射到物理连续能量场，我们严格限制车辆的语义目标与动能导数的界限，保证运动学上可行且可证明安全的轨迹。Specifically, the primary contributions of this paper are structured around three novel components. First, we design an open-vocabulary sparse tokenizer that extracts generic, class-agnostic region proposals aligned within VLM-derived continuous latent spaces. This establishes broad out-of-distribution robustness while preserving the computational edge of object-centric sparsity. Building upon this tokenizer, we develop an intent-driven reasoner based on Masked Latent Fields (MLF). By injecting a temporal kinematic state query into a masked cross-attention mechanism, the network effectively isolates context-critical semantic entities, mimicking the selective attention prioritization characteristic of human drivers. Finally, we formulate driving trajectory generation as Lagrangian Action Minimization. By mapping the attended linguistic-visual tokens into a physical continuous energy field, we strictly constrain the vehicle’s semantic objective with bounds on kinetic energy derivatives, guaranteeing kinematically feasible and provably safe trajectories.

2.3 基于能量的轨迹优化Energy-Based Trajectory Optimization

基于能量的模型 (EBM) 提供了数学上稳健的框架，用于描绘复杂的多模态概率分布。在机器人技术中，EBM 方法经常将轨迹生成视为实现避免碰撞和目标对齐的能量最小化任务。传统上，这些领域的综合需要预言机状态估计或大量密集的语义图表。基于掩蔽潜在场（MLF）的理论前提，我们的方法证明可以从稀疏的、非结构化的视觉标记中系统地导出综合隐式能量流形。拉格朗日将高维 VLM 语义投射到连续的 EBM 中，以实现反应式、全面的驾驶自主。Energy-Based Models (EBMs) offer mathematically robust frameworks for delineating complex, multi-modal probability distributions. Within robotics, EBM approaches frequently pose trajectory generation as an energy minimization task achieving collision avoidance and goal alignment. Traditionally, the synthesis of such fields necessitated either oracle state estimations or heavy dense semantic charting. Building upon the theoretical premise of Masked Latent Fields (MLF), our approach demonstrates that comprehensive implicit energy manifolds can be systematically derived from sparse, unstructured visual tokens. Lagrange projects high-dimensional VLM semantics into continuous EBMs for reactive, full-scale driving autonomy.

6 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

虽然拉格朗日在概念上弥合了语义开放世界泛化和连续动态控制之间的二分法，但结构限制仍然存在。对几何驱动的区域提议网络的依赖意味着纯粹的无定形或非几何危险（例如，广阔的黑冰块或缺乏离散边界的被淹没的主干道）可能会逃避视觉标记化。未来的开发将研究高度压缩、低分辨率自由空间分割令牌的集成。作为连续的能量底线，这将补充离散的 VLM 对象令牌，确保基本的可行驶区域边界。此外，探索将 VLM 对齐模块直接提炼到边缘设备图像信号处理器 (ISP) 中可以显着减少延迟。While Lagrange conceptually bridges the dichotomy between semantic open-world generalization and continuous dynamical control, structural limitations remain. The reliance on a geometry-driven region proposal network implies that purely amorphous or non-geometric hazards (e.g., expansive black ice patches, or flooded arterial roads lacking discrete boundaries) might evade visual tokenization. Future development will investigate the integration of a highly compressed, low-resolution free-space segmentation token. Serving as a continuous energy floor, this would complement the discrete VLM object tokens, securing foundational drivable area bounds. Furthermore, exploring the direct distillation of the VLM alignment module into edge-device Image Signal Processors (ISPs) could yield critical latency reductions.

7 结论Conclusion

在这份手稿中，我们提出了拉格朗日，一种端到端自动驾驶的新颖公式。我们挑战高保真语义控制需要密集像素级重建的假设。通过将视觉语言模型的开放词汇泛化属性注入高度优化的稀疏标记化框架，我们有效地解决了阻碍传统以对象为中心的规划器的分类白名单限制。此外，通过掩蔽潜在场将离散语义标记转换为隐式能量场，并通过拉格朗日动作最小化指示轨迹优化，我们将高级感知安全地绑定在严格的物理现实中。拉格朗日为实现稳健的 3 级和 4 级自治建立了数学上优雅、计算上可行且可严格解释的基础。In this manuscript, we present Lagrange, a novel formulation for end-to-end autonomous driving. We challenge the assumption that high-fidelity semantic control requires dense pixel-level reconstruction. By injecting the open-vocabulary generalization properties of Vision-Language Models into a highly optimized sparse tokenization framework, we effectively address the categorical whitelist limitation encumbering traditional object-centric planners. Furthermore, by translating discrete semantic tokens into an implicit energy field via Masked Latent Fields, and dictating trajectory optimization through Lagrangian action minimization, we securely bind high-level perception within strict physical reality. Lagrange establishes a mathematically elegant, computationally viable, and rigorously interpretable foundation for realizing robust Level 3 and Level 4 autonomy.
