QFedAgent：用于多智能体活动识别的量子增强个性化联合学习QFedAgent: Quantum-Enhanced Personalized Federated Learning for Multi-Agent Activity Recognition

Quoc Bao Phan and Tuy Tan Nguyen
摘要Abstract

联邦学习 (FL) 支持跨分布式设备进行协作模型训练，而无需共享原始数据，使其适合隐私敏感的机器人传感应用。然而，多智能体系统会生成异构、非独立且同分布（非 IID）的多模态传感器流，从而降低传统 FL 算法的性能，而经典融合模块会引入大量参数开销和通信成本。本文提出了 QFedAgent，一种用于多智能体活动识别的混合量子经典个性化 FL 框架。该方法集成了一个变分量子电路融合模块，通过量子态编码和纠缠来模拟加速度计-陀螺仪相互作用，仅需要 72 个量子旋转参数，而经典的基于多层感知器的融合需要 33K 个参数，实现了大约Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training across distributed devices without sharing raw data, making it suitable for privacy-sensitive robotic sensing applications. However, multi-agent systems generate heterogeneous and non-independent and identically distributed (non-IID) multimodal sensor streams that degrade conventional FL algorithms, while classical fusion modules introduce substantial parameter overhead and communication cost. This paper proposes QFedAgent, a hybrid quantum-classical personalized FL framework for multi-agent activity recognition. The approach integrates a variational quantum circuit fusion module that models accelerometer–gyroscope interactions through quantum state encoding and entanglement, requiring only 72 quantum rotation parameters versus 33K in classical multi-layer perceptron-based fusion, achieving approximately10×10\times总参数减少。在基于主题的非 IID 分区下对 OPPORTUNITY 数据集进行的实验表明，平均测试精度为 97.7%，这证实了参数高效的量子融合与传统的联合基线相比仍然具有竞争力。total parameter reduction. Experiments on the OPPORTUNITY dataset under subject-based non-IID partitions demonstrate 97.7% mean test accuracy, confirming that parameter-efficient quantum fusion remains competitive with conventional federated baselines.

I 简介Introduction

自主机器人系统越来越多地部署在工业自动化、医疗保健监控和智能基础设施中。在许多应用中，代理以分布式团队的形式运行，配备了惯性测量单元 (IMU)、摄像头和 LiDAR 等异构传感平台。当它们不断地与环境交互时，这些代理会生成大量的多模式感知数据，这些数据在地理上分布在各个设备上。然而，由于通信开销、延迟限制和隐私问题，集中此类数据进行模型训练通常是不切实际的，特别是在无法保证可靠连接的安全关键环境中Autonomous robotic systems are increasingly deployed in industrial automation, healthcare monitoring, and intelligent infrastructure. In many applications, agents operate as distributed teams equipped with heterogeneous sensing platforms such as inertial measurement units (IMUs), cameras, and LiDAR. As they continuously interact with their environments, these agents generate large volumes of multimodal sensory data that are geographically distributed across devices. However, centralizing such data for model training is often impractical due to communication overhead, latency constraints, and privacy concerns, particularly in safety-critical environments where reliable connectivity cannot be guaranteed[8].

为了应对这些挑战，联邦学习（FL）已成为分布式机器人系统中协作训练的一个有前途的框架To address these challenges, federated learning (FL) has emerged as a promising framework for collaborative training in distributed robotic systems[2]。在 FL 中，智能体根据自己的观察结果在本地训练模型，并与协调服务器而不是原始数据共享模型更新，从而在实现集体学习的同时保留数据局部性. In FL, agents train models locally on their own observations and share model updates with a coordinating server instead of raw data, preserving data locality while enabling collective learning[3]。然而，FedAvg 等经典方法通常假设相似的客户端数据分布，而机器人代理通常在不同的环境、运动模式和传感器特征下运行，导致数据非独立同分布（非 IID），从而降低收敛性并削弱个性化. However, classical methods such as FedAvg generally assume similar client data distributions, whereas robotic agents often operate under different environments, motion patterns, and sensor characteristics, leading to non-independent and identically distributed (non-IID) data that degrades convergence and weakens personalization[1]。这个问题在活动识别等多模态感知任务中变得更加明显，其中基于 IMU 的加速度计和陀螺仪信号表现出复杂的时间和统计依赖性。尽管注意力、门控和变压器等融合方法可以有效地对这些交互进行建模，但它们通常会引入大量参数开销，增加通信成本，并使跨异构客户端的聚合进一步复杂化. This issue becomes even more pronounced in multimodal perception tasks such as activity recognition, where IMU-based accelerometer and gyroscope signals exhibit complex temporal and statistical dependencies. Although fusion methods such as attention, gating, and transformers can model these interactions effectively, they usually introduce substantial parameter overhead, increasing communication cost and further complicating aggregation across heterogeneous clients[4].

为了减轻这些限制，多模态 FL 系统需要紧凑而富有表现力的融合机制。变分量子电路（VQC）最近作为可微分模型出现，可以使用基于梯度的方法进行优化To mitigate these limitations, compact yet expressive fusion mechanisms are desirable for multimodal FL systems. Variational quantum circuits (VQCs) have recently emerged as differentiable models that can be optimized using gradient-based methods[7]。通过将经典输入编码为量子态并利用纠缠操作，VQC 可以表示复杂的特征相关性，同时保持相对较少的可训练参数。因此，它们为分布式学习场景中参数高效的多模态融合提供了一个有前途的方向。然而，尽管有这些优势，将量子电路层集成到用于机器人传感的个性化 FL 系统中仍然很大程度上未被探索。. By encoding classical inputs into quantum states and exploiting entanglement operations, VQCs can represent complex feature correlations while maintaining relatively few trainable parameters. Consequently, they offer a promising direction for parameter-efficient multimodal fusion in distributed learning scenarios. However, despite these advantages, integrating quantum circuit layers into personalized FL systems for robotic sensing remains largely unexplored.

我们提出了 QFedAgent，一种混合量子经典个性化 FL 框架，用于从多模态 IMU 数据进行多智能体活动识别。该框架使用 VQC 作为参数高效的跨模态融合算子，同时在非 IID 联合训练下保持稳健的性能。我们的主要贡献总结如下：We propose QFedAgent, a hybrid quantum–classical personalized FL framework for multi-agent activity recognition from multimodal IMU data. The framework uses VQC as parameter-efficient cross-modal fusion operators while maintaining robust performance under non-IID federated training. Our main contributions are summarized as follows:

  • •

  • •

QFedAgent 的总体架构如图 1 所示。The overall QFedAgent architecture is illustrated in Fig.1。每个代理使用单独的 CNN 编码器处理加速度计和陀螺仪信号以生成模态嵌入。这些嵌入由共享 VQC 层融合，该层通过量子纠缠捕获跨模式相互作用。然后，生成的表示由特定于客户端的适配器进行细化，并馈送到本地分类器。在联合训练期间，仅将编码器和VQC参数传输到服务器进行聚合，而适配器和分类器对每个客户端保持私有。. Each agent processes accelerometer and gyroscope signals using separate CNN encoders to produce modality embeddings. These embeddings are fused by a shared VQC layer that captures cross-modal interactions through quantum entanglement. The resulting representation is then refined by a client-specific adapter and fed to a local classifier. During federated training, only the encoder and VQC parameters are transmitted to the server for aggregation, while the adapter and classifier remain private to each client.

IV 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 QFedAgent，这是一种个性化 FL 框架，用于多智能体系统中基于多模态 IMU 的活动识别。通过用 VQC 模块替换经典的 MLP 融合，QFedAgent 实现了大约This paper presents QFedAgent, a personalized FL framework for multimodal IMU-based activity recognition in multi-agent systems. By replacing classical MLP fusion with a VQC module, QFedAgent achieves approximately10×10\times总体融合参数减少，同时在 OPPORTUNITY 数据集上的 FedAvg、FedProx 和 MLP-FL 基线上获得 97.7% 的最高平均准确度。个性化架构平衡了非独立同分布分布下的全局知识转移和局部适应，证明参数高效的量子融合在联邦环境中仍然有效。尽管经典模拟会导致每轮训练时间较长，但在量子就绪硬件上，这种开销预计会减少，因为电路执行的扩展与参数计数无关。total fusion parameter reduction while attaining the highest mean accuracy of 97.7% over FedAvg, FedProx, and MLP-FL baselines on the OPPORTUNITY dataset. The personalized architecture balances global knowledge transfer with local adaptation under non-IID distributions, demonstrating that parameter-efficient quantum fusion remains effective in federated settings. Although classical simulation incurs higher per-round training time, this overhead is expected to diminish on quantum-ready hardware where circuit execution scales independently of parameter count.
