在教学结果之前评估学习者表现的差异Evaluating Learner Representations for Differentiation Prior to Instructional Outcomes

Junsoo Park    Youssef Medhat    Htet Phyo Wai    Ploy Thajchayapong    Ashok K. Goel
摘要Abstract

学习者表征在教育人工智能系统中发挥着核心作用，但当教学结果不可用或高度依赖于环境时，通常不清楚它们是否保留学生之间有意义的差异。这项工作研究了如何根据学习者表示是否在共享比较规则下保留学习者之间的分离来评估学习者表示。我们介绍Learner representations play a central role in educational AI systems, yet it is often unclear whether they preserve meaningful differences between students when instructional outcomes are unavailable or highly context-dependent. This work examines how to evaluate learner representations based on whether they retain separation between learners under a shared comparison rule. We introduce独特性distinctiveness，一种表征级别的度量，使用成对距离评估每个学习者与队列中其他学习者的差异，无需聚类、标签或特定于任务的评估。使用在线学习环境中通过对话式人工智能代理收集的学生撰写的问题，我们将基于单个问题的表示与随着时间的推移聚合学生交互模式的表示进行比较。结果表明，学习者级别的表示比交互级别的表示产生更高的分离度、更强的聚类结构和更可靠的成对区分。这些发现表明，学习者表征可以独立于教学结果进行评估，并提供实用的部署前标准，使用独特性作为诊断指标来评估表征是否支持差异化建模或个性化。, a representation-level measure that evaluates how each learner differs from others in the cohort using pairwise distances, without requiring clustering, labels, or task-specific evaluation. Using student-authored questions collected through a conversational AI agent in an online learning environment, we compare representations based on individual questions with representations that aggregate patterns across a student’s interactions over time. Results show that learner-level representations yield higher separation, stronger clustering structure, and more reliable pairwise discrimination than interaction-level representations. These findings demonstrate that learner representations can be evaluated independently of instructional outcomes and provide a practical pre-deployment criterion using distinctiveness as a diagnostic metric for assessing whether a representation supports differentiated modeling or personalization.

3 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

3.1 个性化学习中的学习者代表Learner Representations in Personalized Learning

个性化教学在掌握学习和差异化教学方面有着悠久的基础，强调对学习者之间有意义的差异做出反应Personalized instruction has longstanding foundations in mastery learning and differentiated instruction, which emphasize responding to meaningful differences among learners[3, 14]。在教育人工智能系统中，学习者的差异是通过总结参与、进步或需求模式的表征来捕捉的。适应性和个性化学习的先前工作依赖于学习者表征来支持教学适应. In educational AI systems, learner differences are captured through representations that summarize patterns of engagement, progress, or need. Prior work in adaptive and personalized learning has relied on learner representations to support instructional adaptation[4]，但对于如何评估这种表征是否在学习者之间保持有意义的差异（独立于教学结果）提供了有限的指导。, yet offers limited guidance on how to evaluate whether such representations preserve meaningful differentiation among learners, independent of instructional outcomes.

3.2 评估学习者表征的挑战Challenges in Evaluating Learner Representations

在教育环境中评估个性化仍然具有挑战性。教学反应本质上是依赖于情境的，并且不允许单一正确的反馈形式Evaluating personalization remains challenging in educational contexts. Instructional responses are inherently context-dependent and do not admit a single correct form of feedback[8]，而学习者表示提供可解释的近似值，而不是学习的真实模型, while learner representations provide interpretable approximations rather than ground-truth models of learning[5]。因此，基于结果的评估在早期系统设计期间提供的指导有限。为了解决这个问题，我们借鉴隐私研究的概念作为分析镜头。在差异隐私中，敏感度表征系统输出如何响应个体输入的变化而变化. As a result, outcome-based evaluation offers limited guidance during early system design. To address this, we draw on concepts from privacy research as analytical lenses. In differential privacy, sensitivity characterizes how system outputs vary in response to changes in individual inputs[9]。类似地，我们使用表征本身的结构特性而不是教学结果来检查学习者表征是否仍然对个体差异做出反应。. Analogously, we examine whether learner representations remain responsive to individual differences, using structural properties of the representation itself rather than instructional outcomes.

5 讨论Discussion

5.1 表述反映了什么What Representations Reflect

学习者水平的差异取决于学生在共同的相似性衡量标准下是否保持可区分性。当学习者看起来相似时，差异就变得难以解释。我们的结果表明，交互级问题嵌入很快就会失去这种分离：随着相似性标准的扩大，提出重叠问题的学生变得更难区分。相比之下，即使个别问题重叠，学习者层面的表征在更广泛的比较下也能保持分离。交互级表示强调孤立问题文本中的语义重叠，而学习者级签名则聚合学习者活动中的信号（例如，教学需求、时间模式和聚合嵌入特征）。因此，比较从个别问题转向更广泛的模式，揭示了孤立互动中不明显的区别。这些模式在本节中报告的所有分离指标中都是一致的Learner-level differentiation depends on whether students remain distinguishable under a common similarity measure. When learners appear similar, differences become difficult to interpret. Our results show that interaction-level question embeddings lose this separation quickly: as similarity criteria are broadened, students who ask overlapping questions become harder to distinguish. In contrast, learner-level representations preserve separation under broader comparisons, even when individual questions overlap. Interaction-level representations emphasize semantic overlap in isolated question text, whereas learner-level signatures aggregate signals across learner activity (e.g., instructional needs, temporal patterns, and aggregated embedding features). As a result, comparisons shift from individual questions to broader patterns, revealing distinctions not apparent from isolated interactions. These patterns are consistent across all separation indicators reported in Section4.4，包括独特性、轮廓得分和成对 ROC-AUC。, including distinctiveness, silhouette score, and pairwise ROC–AUC.

5.2 独特性衡量什么What Distinctiveness Measures

独特性Distinctiveness（等式。(Eq.1）测量每个学习者与所有其他学习者之间的平均距离。它评估学习者在共享距离规则下是否保持可区分性，并且应该被解释为表示的属性而不是学习者的模型。分离被视为必要但非充分条件：如果学习者不可区分，则没有区分推理的基础，尽管较大的分离也可能反映噪音。独特性不能区分有意义的变化和特征构建引入的变化，并且解释差异需要外部验证。与互补指标相比，独特性在不强加额外结构的情况下评估分离。轮廓取决于聚类和选择) measures the average distance between each learner and all others. It evaluates whether learners remain distinguishable under a shared distance rule and should be interpreted as a property of the representation rather than a model of the learner. Separation is treated as a necessary but not sufficient condition: if learners are not distinguishable, there is no basis for differentiated reasoning, though large separation may also reflect noise. Distinctiveness does not distinguish between meaningful variation and variation introduced by feature construction, and interpreting differences requires external validation. Compared to complementary indicators, distinctiveness evaluates separation without imposing additional structure. Silhouette depends on clustering and a choice ofkk，而 ROC-AUC 评估成对分类任务。相反，独特性将每个学习者与整个队列进行比较，合并所有成对关系，而不进行聚类或抽样。与方差等离散性度量不同，它总结了相对于中心点的分布，独特性直接度量学习者之间的分离。这与教学环境相一致，在教学环境中，学习者的解释是相对于课堂上的其他人而言的，而不是抽象的平均水平, while ROC–AUC evaluates a pairwise classification task. Distinctiveness instead compares each learner to the full cohort, incorporating all pairwise relationships without clustering or sampling. Unlike dispersion measures such as variance, which summarize spread relative to a central point, distinctiveness measures separation directly between learners. This aligns with instructional settings in which learners are interpreted relative to others in the classroom rather than to an abstract average[14]。独特性概括了平均的分离，而独特性则反映了最孤立的学习者。在我们的结果中，学习者层面的表征都得到了改善，表明全局和局部的分离程度都更强。未来的工作应该研究特征组成和维度如何造成这些差异。. Distinctiveness summarizes average separation, while uniqueness reflects the most isolated learner. In our results, learner-level representations improved both, indicating stronger separation both globally and locally. Future work should examine how feature composition and dimensionality contribute to these differences.

5.3 对个性化和学习者建模的影响Implications for Personalization and Learner Modeling

独特性表明表征是否提供了学习者差异化推理所需的信息。如果学习者没有被分开，就没有什么基础来区别对待他们，这与之前强调个性化取决于识别有意义的学习者差异的工作是一致的Distinctiveness indicates whether a representation provides the information necessary for differentiated reasoning about learners. If learners are not separated, there is little basis to treat them differently, consistent with prior work emphasizing that personalization depends on identifying meaningful learner differences[2, 3, 14]。它可以识别将学习者折叠成相似配置文件的表示以及保留学习者之间差异的表示。当外部措施可用时（例如评估或调查），此类表示更有可能包含与这些措施相关的变化，尽管这必须经过经验验证。在实践中，这些表示可以作为标准建模方法（例如聚类、分类、回归）的输入。例如，从业者可以比较候选表示，并在训练个性化学习模型之前选择那些保持更大分离的表示. It can identify representations that collapse learners into similar profiles and those that retain variation across learners. When external measures are available (e.g., assessments or surveys), such representations are more likely to contain variation that relates to those measures, though this must be verified empirically. In practice, these representations can serve as inputs to standard modeling approaches (e.g., clustering, classification, regression). For example, a practitioner could compare candidate representations and select those that preserve greater separation before training models for personalized learning[2, 4]。这并不建立指导意义，但确保表示保留可以支持个性化的变化。. This does not establish instructional meaning, but ensures that the representation retains variation that could support personalization.

6 结论Conclusion

这项工作研究了代表学习者的不同方式如何影响在教学评估之前学生之间的差异仍然可见。使用在线课堂上学生撰写的问题，我们将基于个人问题的表示与随着时间的推移学习者互动的聚合模式的表示进行比较。我们介绍This work examined how different ways of representing learners affect which differences between students remain visible prior to instructional evaluation. Using student-authored questions from an online classroom, we compared representations based on individual questions with representations that aggregate patterns across a learner’s interactions over time. We introduce独特性distinctiveness作为一种独立于任务的衡量标准，衡量学习者表征在基于结果的验证之前是否保留学习者之间的分离。与基于聚类或基于任务的方法不同，独特性在共享比较规则下直接评估整个队列中的分离，而不强加群体结构或将学习者减少到中心参考点。从这个意义上说，它反映了每个学习者相对于同类中的其他学习者如何脱颖而出。我们进一步凭经验表明，学习者级别的聚合相对于交互级别的表示改善了这一属性。在所有分析中，学习者级别的表示比交互级别的表示表现出更高的独特性、轮廓分数和成对 ROC-AUC，支持 H1。独特性不需要指导标签或性能数据，并且可以应用于从交互痕迹构建的表示。从概念上讲，它基于一个简单的几何原理：当没有假设分组或参考点时，必须相对于彼此来定义学习者之间的差异。独特性通过在共享距离测量下将每个学习者与整个队列进行比较来实现这一点。在实践中，这提供了一个预部署标准：学习者之间没有分离，差异化建模或个性化的基础有限。as a task-independent measure of whether learner representations preserve between-learner separation prior to outcome-based validation. Unlike clustering- or task-based approaches, distinctiveness evaluates separation directly across the full cohort under a shared comparison rule, without imposing group structure or reducing learners to a central reference point. In this sense, it captures how each learner stands out relative to others in the cohort. We further show empirically that learner-level aggregation improves this property relative to interaction-level representations. Across all analyses, learner-level representations exhibited higher distinctiveness, silhouette scores, and pairwise ROC–AUC than interaction-level representations, supporting H1. Distinctiveness does not require instructional labels or performance data and can be applied to representations constructed from interaction traces. Conceptually, it is grounded in a simple geometric principle: when no grouping or reference point is assumed, differences between learners must be defined relative to one another. Distinctiveness operationalizes this by comparing each learner to the full cohort under a shared distance measure. In practice, this provides a pre-deployment criterion: without separation between learners, there is limited basis for differentiated modeling or personalization.
