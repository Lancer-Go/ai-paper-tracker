Praval Sharma
College of Information Science & Technology
University of Nebraska Omaha, USA
摘要Abstract

事件提取对于事件理解和分析至关重要。支持紧急场景下的文档汇总、决策等任务。然而，现有的事件提取方法存在局限性：（1）闭域算法仅限于预定义的事件类型，因此很少泛化到看不见的类型；（2）能够处理不受约束的事件类型的开放域算法，尽管具有先进的能力，但在很大程度上忽视了大型语言模型（LLM）的潜力。此外，它们没有明确地对文档级上下文、结构和语义推理进行建模，这对于有效的事件提取至关重要，但由于中间迷失现象和注意力稀释，对法学硕士来说仍然具有挑战性。为了解决这些限制，我们提出了多模式开放域事件提取（MODEE），这是一种开放域事件提取的新方法，它将基于图的学​​习与法学硕士基于文本的表示相结合，以对文档级推理进行建模。对大型数据集的实证评估表明，MODEE 的性能优于最先进的开放域事件提取方法，并且可以推广到闭域事件提取，从而优于现有算法。Event extraction is essential for event understanding and analysis. It supports tasks such as document summarization and decision-making in emergency scenarios. However, existing event extraction approaches have limitations: (1) closed-domain algorithms are restricted to predefined event types and thus rarely generalize to unseen types and (2) open-domain algorithms, capable of handling unconstrained event types, have largely overlooked the potential of large language models (LLMs) despite their advanced abilities. Additionally, they do not explicitly model document-level contextual, structural, and semantic reasoning, which are crucial for effective event extraction but remain challenging for LLMs due to lost-in-the-middle phenomenon and attention dilution. To address these limitations, we propose multimodal open-domain event extraction, MODEE, a novel approach for open-domain event extraction that combines graph-based learning with text-based representation from LLMs to model document-level reasoning. Empirical evaluations on large datasets demonstrate that MODEE outperforms state-of-the-art open-domain event extraction approaches and can be generalized to closed-domain event extraction, where it outperforms existing algorithms.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项研究中，我们介绍了 MODEE，这是一种基于 GNN 和 LLM 的新型开放域事件提取方法，它结合了多种模态来建模文档级推理，这对于事件提取至关重要，但由于中间丢失现象和注意力稀释，对于 LLM 来说仍然具有挑战性。实验结果表明，MODEE 在开放域和闭域设置中均优于强基线。未来的工作将把 MODEE 扩展到多文档设置，并纳入图像、音频和视频等附加模式。In this research, we introduced MODEE, a novel GNN- and LLM-based open-domain event extraction approach that combines multiple modalities to model document-level reasoning, which is essential for event extraction but remains challenging for LLMs due to lost-in-the-middle phenomenon and attention dilution. Experimental results showed that MODEE outperformed strong baselines in both open-domain and closed-domain settings. Future work will extend MODEE to multi-document settings and incorporate additional modalities such as images, audio, and video.

局限性Limitations

我们使用新闻报道来评估 MODEE 的事件提取能力。虽然该领域非常适合事件 5W 提取，但需要在社交媒体、科学文章或金融文档等其他领域进行额外的实验，以全面评估不同文本类型的 MODEE。 MODEE 假定每个文档一个事件设置。当文档报告多个显着事件时，模型并未明确设计为提取所有事件，并且可能专注于单个主导事件。We evaluate MODEE’s event extraction capability using news reports. While this domain is well suited for event 5Ws extraction, additional experiments on other domains such as social media, scientific articles, or financial documents are necessary to fully assess MODEE across diverse text genres. MODEE assumes a one-event-per-document setting. When a document reports multiple salient events, the model is not explicitly designed to extract all events and may focus on a single dominant event.

文档级标记图是 MODEE 的关键组件，因为它使模型能够捕获事件相关标记之间的全局结构和语义关系。然而，为很长的文档构建和处理文档级标记图会引入额外的计算开销，这可能会限制 MODEE 的可扩展性而无需进一步优化。图稀疏化或边缘修剪等技术可以帮助降低这种复杂性。最后，由于其编码器-解码器架构，MODEE 使用 T5 作为其骨干。大多数现代 LLM 都是仅解码器模型，我们尚未评估具有更大和更新的 LLM 主干的 MODEE，这可能允许更好地利用现代 LLM 的功能。The document-level token graph is a critical component of MODEE as it enables the model to capture the global structure and semantic relationships among event-related tokens. However, constructing and processing a document-level token graph for a very long document introduces additional computational overhead, which may limit MODEE’s scalability without further optimization. Techniques such as graph sparsification or edge pruning could help reduce this complexity. Finally, MODEE uses T5 as its backbone due to its encoder-decoder architecture. Most modern LLMs are decoder-only models, and we have not evaluated MODEE with larger and more recent LLM backbones, which could potentially allow better utilization of the capabilities of modern LLMs.

创建大型手动注释数据集需要大量时间和精力。我们聘请了学生注释员，并按照大学为学生工作者规定的基本最低工资标准向他们支付报酬。参与注释过程是自愿的，为了保护隐私和机密，不会披露有关注释者的个人或身份信息。Creating a large, manually annotated dataset requires substantial time and effort. We employed student annotators and compensated them at the basic minimum pay rate set by the university for student workers. Participation in the annotation process was voluntary, and to protect privacy and confidentiality, no personal or identifying information about the annotators is disclosed.

What is present in all documents, while Where, When, Who, and Why appear in 9,390 (93.9%), 9,493 (94.9%), 7,391 (73.9%), and 4,451 (44.5%) documents, respectively. This distribution highlights the relative sparsity of causal information (Why) in news reports, which makes identifying and extracting it complex. In terms of lexical diversity, the dataset contains 5,469 (58.2%) unique Where values, 1,279 (13.5%) When, 7,462 (74.6%) What, 4,424 (59.9%) Who, and 3,944 (88.6%) Why.
