摘要Abstract

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）在数学和代码等领域具有先进的语言模型推理，这些领域的客观答案检查起来并不昂贵。 对复杂数据的诊断推理缺乏这种优势：确定异常的真正原因通常需要昂贵的专家调查，并且事后可能仍然含糊不清。 我们询问是否可以设计这种验证的不对称性。 我们对干预进行采样，将其注入受控模拟器，并生成它将产生的观察结果。 隐藏的干预提供了预言机标签和客观奖励，而代理仍然必须调查嘈杂、混乱和分布式的证据。Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has advanced language-model reasoning in domains such as mathematics and code, where objective answers are inexpensive to check. Diagnostic reasoning over complex data lacks this advantage: establishing the true cause of an anomaly often requires costly expert investigation and may remain ambiguous after the fact. We ask whether this asymmetry of verification can instead be engineered. We sample an intervention, inject it into a controlled simulator, and generate the observations it would produce. 1 简介Introduction

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）推动了法学硕士推理的最新进展。 数学推理工作Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has driven recent progress in LLM reasoning. Work on mathematical reasoning(Guo et al., 2025; Shao et al., 2024)和编码剂and coding agents(Wei et al., 2026; Luo et al., 2025b)表明针对客观验证者优化的策略可以获得多步推理和工具使用能力。 这些域提供了自然的shows that policies optimized against objective verifiers can acquire multi-step reasoning and tool-use capabilities. These domains provide a natural验证的不对称性asymmetry of verification：根据已知答案或测试套件检查解决方案比生成解决方案本身要容易得多: checking a solution against a known answer or test suite is considerably easier than producing the solution itself(Wei, 2025)。 RLVR 将这种不对称性转化为可扩展的训练信号。. RLVR turns this asymmetry into a scalable training signal.

许多实际推理任务缺乏这样的验证器。 对复杂数据的诊断推理是一个典型的例子：确定系统发生变化的原因可能需要昂贵的专家调查，而且即使在事后，所得到的归因也可能仍然不确定。 多个变化可能同时发生，观察结果是嘈杂的，并且合理的原因可能会产生类似的特征。 法学硕士作为法官的方法Many practical reasoning tasks lack such verifiers. Diagnostic reasoning over complex data is a representative example: determining why a system changed can require expensive expert investigation, and the resulting attribution may remain uncertain even after the fact. Multiple changes can occur concurrently, observations are noisy, and plausible causes may produce similar signatures. LLM-as-judge approaches(Zheng et al., 2023; Bai et al., 2022; Lee et al., 2024)不要消除这个瓶颈，因为目标本身仍然不明确，而博学的法官可以为奖励黑客带来额外的机会do not eliminate this bottleneck because the target itself remains ambiguous, while learned judges can introduce additional opportunities for reward hacking(Gao et al., 2023; Skalse et al., 2022)。 因此，在这些环境中应用 RLVR 的主要障碍是获取可扩展的、客观的地面事实。. The central obstacle to applying RLVR in these settings is therefore access to scalable, objective ground truth.

在本文中，我们询问验证的不对称性是否可以被In this paper, we ask whether the asymmetry of verification can be工程化的engineered。 我们不是观察数据并试图确定未知原因，而是首先对干预进行采样，将其注入受控模拟器，并生成干预将产生的观察结果。 隐藏的干预被保留为预言机标签，使得代理的最终归因成本低廉且确定性验证。 代理不遵守此标签：它仍然必须调查嘈杂、混乱和分散的证据以找出原因。 因此，模拟将解决诊断任务的难度与验证其答案的难度分开。. Rather than observing data and attempting to determine an unknown cause, we first sample an intervention, inject it into a controlled simulator, and generate the observations that the intervention would produce. The hidden intervention is retained as an oracle label, making the agent’s final attribution inexpensive and deterministic to verify. The agent does not observe this label: it must still investigate noisy, confounded, and distributed evidence to recover the cause. Simulation therefore separates the difficulty of solving a diagnostic task from the difficulty of verifying its answer.

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了一种模拟器 - 预言机 - 强化学习方法，用于在自然发生的验证者稀缺时训练诊断推理代理。 受控模拟器对隐藏的干预进行采样，生成该干预产生的数据，并将其保留为预言机标签。 这种结构使最终归因能够客观地验证，而无需消除使诊断任务变得困难的噪音、混杂因素、多表证据和探索性工具的使用。 我们在中实例化这种方法We introduced a simulator–oracle–RL approach for training diagnostic-reasoning agents when naturally occurring verifiers are scarce. A controlled simulator samples a hidden intervention, generates the data resulting from that intervention, and retains it as an oracle label. This construction makes the final attribution objectively verifiable without removing the noise, confounders, multi-table evidence, and exploratory tool use that make the diagnostic task difficult. We instantiate this approach in追踪TRACE，一个生成数字广告环境，包含整个活动范围、细分市场组合和细分市场特定的根本原因。, a generative digital-advertising environment containing campaign-wide, segment-mix, and segment-specific root causes.

关于保留的On the held-out追踪TRACEbenchmark，最强提示基线达到benchmark, the strongest prompted baseline reaches0.6860.686FullAttr@1。 SFT 将 Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 从FullAttr@1. SFT raises Qwen3.5-35B-A3B from0.1590.159 to 0.6370.637，随后带有综合奖励的 RL 进一步将其改进为, and subsequent RL with synthesized rewards further improves it to0.7570.757，超越了所有评估的提示基线，包括 Qwen3.5-122B-A10B。 这一结果提供的证据表明，对于这种诊断设置，绑定约束是获取有效的训练后信号（包括可扩展的客观奖励），而不是单独的模型规模。, surpassing every evaluated prompted baseline, including Qwen3.5-122B-A10B. This result provides evidence that, for this diagnostic setting, the binding constraint is access to an effective post-training signal—including a scalable, objective reward—rather than model scale alone.

有几个方向可以扩展这种方法。 更丰富的代理工具可以提供明确的规划、记忆、自适应工具选择和改进的停止机制，使策略能够进行更长时间、更结构化的调查。 模拟器-预言机构建还可以扩展到更广泛的干预系列、保留的根本原因以及软件操作和数据质量诊断等领域，在这些领域中可以注入故障并观察其下游影响。 最后，模拟器控制使系统化课程能够克服噪声、混杂、证据可用性和归因复杂性，提供一种研究哪些诊断程序跨环境转移的方法。Several directions could extend this approach. Richer agent harnesses could provide explicit planning, memory, adaptive tool selection, and improved stopping mechanisms, allowing the policy to conduct longer and more structured investigations. The simulator–oracle construction could also be expanded to broader intervention families, held-out root causes, and domains such as software operations and data-quality diagnosis, where failures can be injected and their downstream effects observed. Finally, simulator control enables systematic curricula over noise, confounding, evidence availability, and attribution complexity, providing a way to study which diagnostic procedures transfer across environments.

更广泛地说，我们的结果展示了一种为其他不明确的诊断任务构建客观训练信号的途径：当一个领域承认可控生成模型时，产生难题的干预也可以提供其验证者。More broadly, our results demonstrate a route to constructing objective training signals for otherwise ambiguous diagnostic tasks: when a domain admits a controllable generative model, the intervention that creates a difficult problem can also provide its verifier.

Appendix C Additional Evaluation Details

C.1 Evaluation Protocol

All models receive the same task specification, candidate-cause definitions, final-answer contract, and Python/SQL tool interface over the agent-visible fact store. The Python state persists across calls within an episode. Tool calls have a 60-second execution timeout, and tool outputs are truncated after 8,000 characters. A final-turn nudge asks the model to return its answer before exhausting the interaction budget.

Open-weight evaluations sample five trajectories per task at temperature 0.60.6 with a 60-step backstop. API-model evaluations use provider-supported stochastic sampling without an explicit temperature and a 30-turn backstop. The reported Claude and GPT baselines use the xhigh reasoning-effort setting. Claude models receive a maximum output-token budget of 32,768, while GPT models receive 16,384 output tokens. Every reported FullAttr@5 value is computed from five distinct samples per task. The interaction limits do not bind for the trained or frontier models; one Qwen3.5-35B base-model trajectory is truncated.

C.2 Answer-Parser Sensitivity

For the primary evaluation, we parse each model’s final answer to extract the decision fields needed to assess attribution: a non-empty root cause and any predicted segment assignment. Malformed report-only evidence, an omitted explanation, or benign extra JSON keys do not invalidate an otherwise scoreable decision. A malformed segment assignment is treated as absent and therefore cannot earn full attribution on a segment-specific episode. We report the decision-parse rate separately from diagnostic accuracy.

As a sensitivity analysis, we also use an exact-schema parser that requires the complete prescribed output structure. The parser choice has no effect on the SFT or SFT→\rightarrowRL results, but exact-schema parsing underestimates FullAttr@1 when a model produces a semantically scoreable decision with malformed or missing report-only fields.
