摘要Abstract

询问预训练的生物医学语言模型“皮质醇 28 µg/dL”和“股市波动性”是否相关，它会返回 0.83 的余弦相似度，其中 1.0 表示相同。两者没有共享机制。这不是一个极端案例。我们测试的每个现成的生物医学编码器——BioBERT、PubMedBERT、BioM-ELECTRA——当答案应该接近零时，来自不同领域的不相关对的得分在 0.76 到 0.92 之间。在六部分诊断中，跨域区分的准确度恰好为 0%。Ask a pretrained biomedical language model whether “cortisol 28 µg/dL” and “stock-market volatility” are related, and it returns a cosine similarity of 0.83 on a scale where 1.0 means identical. The two share no mechanism. This is not a corner case. Every off-the-shelf biomedical encoder we tested — BioBERT, PubMedBERT, BioM-ELECTRA — scores unrelated pairs from different domains between 0.76 and 0.92 when the answer should be near zero. On a six-part diagnostic, accuracy on cross-domain discrimination is exactly 0%.

大多数检索系统都能幸免于难，因为下游的语言模型会过滤噪音。大型行为模型（LBM）——一个以人而不是句子为主题的基础模型——则不然。 LBM 对用户生活图表进行推理，并将嵌入的邻近性读取为两个事件存在因果关系的证据。如果几何学认为皮质醇和股票市场是一体的，那么模型就会写出错误的因果边缘，下游的一切都会继承这个错误。对于这个系统，嵌入几何体不是一个调谐旋钮。这是正确性。Most retrieval systems survive this, because a language model downstream filters the noise. A Large Behavioural Model (LBM) — a foundation model whose subject is a person, not a sentence — does not. An LBM reasons over a graph of a user’s life and reads embedding proximity as evidence that two events are causally linked. If the geometry says cortisol and the stock market belong together, the model writes a false causal edge, and everything downstream inherits the mistake. For this system, embedding geometry is not a tuning knob. It is correctness.

我们报告修复情况及其价格。首次对比通过 72,034 对，将 PubMedBERT 的 BIOSSES 相关性从 0.633 提高到 0.828，并将其域内与跨域分离从 1.05× 提高到 1.63×。我们称之为 BODHI 的第二遍——它从生物医学知识图谱中不存在的边缘中挖掘硬负例——将分离度提高到 2.30×，将辨别差距提高到 +0.392，在 BIOSSES 上花费了 4.5%。然后我们让它运行。在配备 AMX 的 Intel Xeon 6737P 上，OpenVINO 将单查询延迟从 1,367 毫秒缩短至 10 毫秒 (133×)，并达到每秒 555 个句子。一个结果与标准部署建议相矛盾：在我们服务的每个批量大小上，FP16 在该芯片上都击败了 INT8，我们解释了原因。无 AMX Ice Lake 实例上的相同模型运行速度慢 13-27 倍。We report the fix and its price. A first contrastive pass over 72,034 pairs raises PubMedBERT’s BIOSSES correlation from 0.633 to 0.828 and its within-versus-across-domain separation from 1.05× to 1.63×. A second pass we call BODHI — which mines hard negatives from edges that are absent in a biomedical knowledge graph — pushes separation to 2.30× and the discrimination gap to +0.392, costing 4.5% on BIOSSES. Then we make it run. On an Intel Xeon 6737P with AMX, OpenVINO cuts single-query latency from 1,367 ms to 10 ms (133×) and reaches 555 sentences per second. One result contradicts standard deployment advice: FP16 beats INT8 on this silicon at every batch size we serve, and we explain why. The same model on a no-AMX Ice Lake instance runs 13–27× slower.

我们发布了基准测试套件、训练语料库、BODHI 硬负生成器和 OpenVINO 转换脚本。 LBM 对人类进行建模。本文构建了嵌入层，使其无需发明从未存在过的连接即可实现这一点。We release the benchmark suite, the training corpora, the BODHI hard-negative generator, and the OpenVINO conversion scripts. The LBM models the human. This paper builds the embedding layer that lets it do so without inventing connections that were never there.

1.问题，说清楚1. The problem, stated plainly

一个人不是一个段落。当今主导人工智能的模型是围绕语言构建的：单位是令牌，目标是预测下一个，成功是以流畅性来衡量的。他们擅长文字。这并不意味着他们善于与人相处。人是连续的、有状态的、因果纠缠的——生物喂养认知、喂养情绪喂养行为，跨越从几分钟到几十年的时间尺度。对人进行建模需要与令牌不同的原语、与下一个令牌预测不同的目标以及与上下文窗口不同的内存。A person is not a paragraph. The models that dominate AI today are built around language: the unit is the token, the objective is to predict the next one, and success is measured in fluency. They are good at text. That does not make them good at people. People are continuous, stateful, and causally tangled — biology feeding cognition feeding mood feeding behaviour, across timescales from minutes to decades. Modelling a person needs a different primitive than the token, a different objective than next-token prediction, and a different memory than a context window.

我们将该原语称为大型行为模型。 LBM 是一个基础模型，其主体是人类，代表跨临床、基因组、心理、行为、环境和生理信号以及跨时间。它不是聊天机器人，也不是语言模型的竞争对手。两人并肩坐着，做着不同的工作。语言模型模型语言； LBM 对语言所涉及的人进行建模。我们的配套论文 (arXiv:2605.27580) 中描述了该架构。这篇论文是关于它的一个组成部分，以及如果你做错了就会破坏整个事情的失败。We call that primitive a Large Behavioural Model. An LBM is a foundation model whose subject is a human, represented across clinical, genomic, psychological, behavioural, environmental, and physiological signals and across time. It is not a chatbot and it is not a competitor to language models. The two sit side by side and do different jobs. Language models model language; an LBM models the person the language is about. The architecture is described in our companion paper (arXiv:2605.27580). This paper is about one component of it, and about a failure that breaks the whole thing if you get it wrong.

LBM 保存每个用户的生活图表。节点是事件——实验室结果、日记条目、咨询笔记、可穿戴阅读器。边缘就是关系：这导致那，这先于那，这是那的重现。该模型通过遍历图来增长图，其遍历的信号之一是嵌入邻近度。当两个事件之间没有边缘但它们的嵌入紧密相连时，模型会将这种接近程度视为询问是否存在因果联系的理由，如果答案是肯定的，则会绘制边缘。几个月来，LBM 就是这样将一堆互不相关的记录变成单个生命的因果图的。The LBM keeps a graph of each user’s life. Nodes are events — a lab result, a journal entry, a counselling note, a wearable reading. Edges are relationships: this caused that, this preceded that, this is a recurrence of that. The model grows the graph by walking it, and one of the signals it walks on is embedding proximity. When two events have no edge between them but their embeddings sit close together, the model treats that closeness as a reason to ask whether a causal link exists, and if the answer is yes, it draws the edge. Over months, this is how the LBM turns a pile of disconnected records into a causal map of a single life.

现在失败已经显而易见了。整个循环信任几何形状。如果嵌入空间将不相关的事物放在一起，则模型会假设不存在的边缘，并且用户的因果图会充满虚构。如果它将真正关联的事物放在很远的地方，那么模型永远不会将它们连接起来，并且地图上也会有漏洞。不管怎样，图表之上的推理都是基于谎言的推理。因此，本文回答的问题是狭窄且承载的：嵌入模型是否可以将因果相关的事件放在一起，而将因果无关的事件放在远处，即使这些事件来自不同的领域并使用完全不同的词汇？对于今天可以下载的每个模型，诚实的起始答案是否定的。Now the failure becomes obvious. The whole loop trusts the geometry. If the embedding space puts unrelated things close together, the model hypothesises edges that do not exist, and the user’s causal map fills up with fiction. If it puts genuinely linked things far apart, the model never connects them and the map has holes. Either way, the reasoning that runs on top of the graph is reasoning on a lie. So the question this paper answers is narrow and load-bearing: can an embedding model place causally related events close together and causally unrelated events far apart, even when those events come from different domains and use completely different vocabularies? The honest starting answer, for every model you can download today, is no.

2. LBM 的嵌入需要什么2. What an LBM needs from its embeddings

检索系统使用嵌入作为相似性索引。你提出一个问题，你会得到看起来最相似的文档，然后语言模型决定如何处理它们。如果索引返回的结果略有偏差，语言模型就会对此不予理睬。嵌入错误的代价是更糟糕的搜索结果，系统会吸收它。Retrieval systems use embeddings as a similarity index. You ask a question, you get back the documents that look most like it, and a language model decides what to do with them. If the index returns something slightly off, the language model shrugs it off. The cost of an embedding error is a worse search result, and the system absorbs it.

图推理没有这样的缓冲。嵌入的使用者不是一个宽容的语言模型；这是一个遍历例程，将邻近度视为证据并将该证据写入永久图表中。错误的边不会被重新排名。它位于图表中并破坏了经过它的每一次未来行走。错误会复合而不是被消除。这个单一的差异就是为什么适合搜索的嵌入空间在这里可能毫无用处。Graph reasoning has no such cushion. The consumer of the embedding is not a forgiving language model; it is a traversal routine that treats proximity as evidence and writes that evidence into a permanent graph. A wrong edge does not get re-ranked away. It sits in the graph and corrupts every future walk that passes through it. Errors compound instead of washing out. That single difference is why an embedding space that is fine for search can be useless here.

LBM 真正需要的不是相似性，而是词汇变化后仍能保持的因果连贯性。四种行为将两者分开。该模型必须将位于同一因果链上的事件整合在一起，即使这些事件是用不同的语言写成的——一条写着“睡了四个小时，整个早上都焦虑”的日志行属于“皮质醇 28 µg/dL”的实验室值，因为睡眠不足会驱动皮质醇驱动焦虑，但一个是一种感觉，另一个是一个数字。它必须将那些仅仅具有表面词汇的事件分开——“皮质醇升高”和“市场波动”都会引发压力，但没有机制将它们连接起来，所以它们最终必定会相距甚远。它必须将看起来毫无相似之处的词汇联系起来——遗传学节点“BRCA1致病性变异”和临床节点“增加乳腺癌筛查”是因果关系，一个分子一个程序。而且它必须识别相隔数月以两种不同方式描述的相同基本状态，因此模型可以看到重复出现的事件，而不是两个不相关的条目。What the LBM actually needs is not similarity but causal coherence that survives a change of vocabulary. Four behaviours separate the two. The model has to pull together events that sit on the same causal chain even when they are written in different languages — a journal line that reads “slept four hours, anxious all morning” belongs next to a lab value of “cortisol 28 µg/dL,” because sleep loss drives the cortisol drives the anxiety, but one is a feeling and the other is a number. It has to push apart events that merely share surface words — “elevated cortisol” and “market volatility” both invoke stress, but there is no mechanism connecting them, so they must end up far apart. It has to link across vocabularies that look nothing alike — the genetics node “BRCA1 pathogenic variant” and the clinical node “increased breast-cancer screening” are cause and consequence, one molecular and one procedural. And it has to recognise the same underlying state described two different ways months apart, so the model can see a recurring episode rather than two unrelated entries.

经过 PubMed 训练来预测屏蔽标记的模型无法直接学习这些内容。它了解到“皮质醇”和“压力”同时发生。它永远不会知道睡眠不足会导致皮质醇升高，从而导致焦虑，因为这条链跨越三个领域和三个词汇，并且没有隐藏的客观奖励将它们缝合在一起。如果不去管，该模型默认为表面共现，这完全是错误的信号：它将不相关但听起来相似的配对评为接近，这就是我们测量的失败。A model trained to predict masked tokens over PubMed learns none of this directly. It learns that “cortisol” and “stress” co-occur. It never learns that sleep deprivation causes elevated cortisol causes anxiety, because that chain runs across three domains and three vocabularies and no masked-token objective rewards stitching them together. Left alone, the model defaults to surface co-occurrence, which is exactly the wrong signal: it rates unrelated-but-similar-sounding pairs as close, and that is the failure we measured.

5.他们失败的原因：各向异性5. Why they fail: anisotropy

B2、B3 和 B6 之间的模式是嵌入文献多年来记录的问题的特征。 BERT 系列句子向量不会散布在单位范围内。它们塌陷成一个狭窄的圆锥体，全部指向大致相同的方向，聚集在语料库均值周围。实际结果是任意两个随机句子之间的余弦远高于零。不相关的内容读起来是 0.4 到 0.7 相似，只是因为每个向量都位于同一个小空间区域中。The pattern across B2, B3, and B6 is the signature of a problem the embedding literature has documented for years. BERT-family sentence vectors do not spread out over the unit sphere. They collapse into a narrow cone, all pointing roughly the same way, clustered around the corpus mean. The practical consequence is that the cosine between any two random sentences sits well above zero. Unrelated content reads as 0.4 to 0.7 similar simply because every vector lives in the same small region of space.

搜索容忍这一点。语言模型读取最重要的结果并忽略垃圾，因此高噪声基底会损失一点精度，仅此而已。图推理并没有那么奢侈。 LBM 必须将其边缘假设阈值放在余弦轴上的某个位置，如果本底噪声为 0.75，而实际链路为 0.95，则没有阈值将它们分开数百万个节点。白化、对比句子训练和基于提示的池化都已被证明可以减少标准相似性基准上的各向异性。它们都没有在我们关心的事情上进行过测试，即空间是否将跨域因果对拉在一起，同时将跨域相似的因果对分开。因此，我们建立了直接针对这一目标的培训。Search tolerates this. A language model reads the top results and ignores the junk, so a high noise floor costs a little precision and nothing else. Graph reasoning does not get that luxury. The LBM has to put its edge-hypothesis threshold somewhere on the cosine axis, and if the noise floor is 0.75 while real links sit at 0.95, there is no threshold that separates them across millions of nodes. Whitening, contrastive sentence training, and prompt-based pooling have all been shown to reduce anisotropy on standard similarity benchmarks. None of them have been tested on the thing we care about, which is whether the space pulls cross-domain causal pairs together while pushing cross-domain look-alikes apart. So we built training that targets that directly.

6. 解决办法：两次通过，一个好主意6. The fix: two passes, and one good idea

6.1 第 1 轮 — 72,034 对的对比训练6.1 Pass 1 — contrastive training on 72,034 pairs

第 1 遍对来自 8 个公共数据集的 72,034 个（锚定、正例、硬负例）三元组上的每个模型进行微调，并补充了合成的跨域硬负例。损失是套娃损失（Matryoshka Loss）和多重负数排名损失（Multiple Negative Ranking Loss）。 MNRL 将 128 个批次中的所有其他示例都转换为负例，因此每个锚点都会立即从其他 127 个锚点中推开，再加上其显式的硬负例。 Matryoshka 包装器同时训练五个嵌套维度 - 768、512、256、128、64 - 这意味着一个模型可以为图形存储提供完整的 768 维向量，为遍历过程中的快速邻近扫描提供 256 维向量，以及为设备上 LBM 提供 64 维向量，无需重新训练。Pass 1 fine-tunes each model on 72,034 (anchor, positive, hard-negative) triplets drawn from eight public datasets and topped up with synthetic cross-domain hard negatives. The loss is Matryoshka Loss wrapped around Multiple Negatives Ranking Loss. MNRL turns every other example in a batch of 128 into a negative, so each anchor is pushed away from 127 others at once, plus its explicit hard negative. The Matryoshka wrapper trains five nested dimensions at the same time — 768, 512, 256, 128, 64 — which means one model serves the full 768-d vector for graph storage, a 256-d vector for fast proximity scans during traversal, and a 64-d vector for an on-device LBM, with no retraining.

6.2 第二关——菩提，从缺失中学习6.2 Pass 2 — BODHI, learning from what is missing

第 1 遍有帮助，但其硬否定的效果仅取决于产生它们的启发式，而词汇重叠启发式则错过了失败的点。这些模型不会混淆表面上相似的事物；他们混淆了概念上不相关的事物。我们需要的是硬否定的来源，它可以通过单词重叠之外的其他因素来编码两个概念是否确实相关。Pass 1 helps, but its hard negatives are only as good as the heuristics that made them, and lexical-overlap heuristics miss the point of the failure. The models do not confuse things that look alike on the surface; they confuse things that are conceptually unrelated. What we needed was a source of hard negatives that encodes whether two concepts are actually connected, judged by something other than word overlap.

知识图就是这个来源。在精心策划的生物医学图中，两个概念之间的边缘是领域专家断言它们是相关的，而没有边缘则表明它们不相关。我们就是这样使用BODHI生物医学知识图谱的。 PRESENT_IN、CHILD_OF 或 IMPACTS 类型的每个显式边都成为正对。每对都在图中但之间没有边缘的概念（没有长度为 2 或更少的路径）都会成为硬负数。因此，“BRCA1 → 遗传性癌症综合征”是一个积极因素，因为存在边缘，而“BRCA1 与抑郁症”是一个严格消极因素，因为没有专家将它们联系起来，无论预训练模型可能认为它们有多相似。 Pass 2 采用 Pass-1 PubMedBERT 检查点，并在大约 3,500 个本体三元组上再训练两个 epoch，产生一个独特的模型：PubMedBERT BODHI。A knowledge graph is exactly that source. In a curated biomedical graph, an edge between two concepts is a domain expert’s assertion that they are linked, and the absence of an edge is an assertion that they are not. We use the BODHI biomedical knowledge graph this way. Every explicit edge of type PRESENT_IN, CHILD_OF, or IMPACTS becomes a positive pair. Every pair of concepts that are both in the graph but have no edge between them — no path of length two or less — becomes a hard negative. So “BRCA1 → hereditary cancer syndrome” is a positive, because the edge exists, and “BRCA1 vs depression” is a hard negative, because no expert ever connected them, however similar a pretrained model might think they are. Pass 2 takes the Pass-1 PubMedBERT checkpoint and trains two more epochs on roughly 3,500 of these ontology triplets, producing a distinct model: PubMedBERT BODHI.

有一种对称性值得命名。 BODHI 在一张图中训练缺失边的嵌入，以便在部署时，嵌入可以帮助 LBM 假设另一张图中缺失边（用户的个人因果图）。我们训练模型来做它被要求做的工作。这就是整个想法，它比我们尝试过的任何其他方法都更有效。There is a symmetry worth naming. BODHI trains the embedding on absent edges in one graph so that, in deployment, the embedding can help the LBM hypothesise absent edges in another — the user’s personal causal graph. We train the model to do the job it will be asked to do. That is the whole idea, and it works better than anything else we tried.

LBM 拥有两家商店。一种是带有时间戳的测量结果（实验室、评估分数、生理读数）的结构化特征存储，模型将其直接摄取到其前向传播中。另一个是图存储，其中每个文本节点都携带本文模型生成的 768 维向量。 LBM 不会对该图运行最近邻搜索。它会对其进行推理，同时沿着三个信号从种子节点向外走：人类或先前通过的边缘已经绘制，模型自己学到的因果链似乎合理的感觉，以及嵌入邻近度。当邻近度表示两个未连接的节点属于在一起时，模型决定是否绘制边缘。The LBM keeps two stores. One is a structured feature store of timestamped measurements — labs, assessment scores, physiological readings — that the model ingests straight into its forward pass. The other is the graph store, where every text node carries the 768-d vector produced by the model in this paper. The LBM does not run nearest-neighbour search over that graph. It reasons over it, walking outward from a seed node along three signals at once: edges a human or a prior pass already drew, the model’s own learned sense of which causal chains are plausible, and embedding proximity. When proximity says two unconnected nodes belong together, the model decides whether to draw the edge.

所以嵌入质量直接就是图的质量。对于预训练的嵌入，LBM 是不可能实现的：在 0.76-0.92 的跨域相似度下，它几乎在每次行走时都会产生边缘的幻觉。第 1 遍，间隔为 1.63 倍，可用于真实的假边风险。 BODHI 为 2.30×，清除了 1.5× 条，在该条中接近成为您可以依靠的信号。我们测量的阈值（BODHI 为 0.40，Pass 1 为 0.50）是设置 LBM 绘制边缘的积极程度的刻度盘。当假边缘代价高昂并且您想要精度时，请使用 0.40；当覆盖范围更重要时使用 0.50。So the embedding quality is the graph quality, directly. With pretrained embeddings the LBM is a nonstarter: at 0.76–0.92 cross-domain similarity it would hallucinate an edge on nearly every walk. Pass 1, at 1.63× separation, is usable with real false-edge risk. BODHI, at 2.30×, clears the 1.5× bar where proximity becomes a signal you can lean on. The thresholds we measured — 0.40 for BODHI, 0.50 for Pass 1 — are the dials that set how aggressively the LBM draws edges. 菩提概括。这个秘诀——存在的边缘产生正值，不存在的边缘产生硬性负值——适用于任何具有精选本体的领域。人类表型本体论、DSM-5 结构、UMLS、基因本体论以及我们为 LBM 构建的行为本体论都是候选者。考虑到从 Pass 1 到 BODHI 跳跃的规模，第二个本体图传递看起来对于任何面向图推理的嵌入模型（而不仅仅是我们的模型）来说都是一个高杠杆的举动。BODHI generalises. The recipe — positives from edges that exist, hard negatives from edges that do not — works for any domain with a curated ontology. Human Phenotype Ontology, the DSM-5 structure, UMLS, Gene Ontology, and the behavioural ontologies we are building for the LBM are all candidates. Given the size of the Pass 1 to BODHI jump, a second ontology-graph pass looks like a high-leverage move for any embedding model headed for graph reasoning, not just ours.

我们对我们未声明的内容非常谨慎。We are careful about what we are not claiming.

  • ELECTRA 的架构不适合这种情况。微调后其差距为+0.066，比 BERT 模型低一个数量级。 QA 预训练不会重塑为嵌入器。负面结果是包含它的重点。ELECTRA is the wrong architecture for this. After fine-tuning its gap is +0.066, an order of magnitude short of the BERT models. QA pretraining does not reshape into an embedder. The negative result is the point of including it.

We are claiming that the embedding substrate underneath it can be made correct, that we measured how correct, and that it runs on hardware you can rent.

13. 结论13. Conclusion

我们从一个模型开始，认为皮质醇和股票市场有 83% 的相似度，最后得出一个模型，将不相关的部分分开，将真正的联系在一起，足够清晰地进行推理。预训练的编码器完全无法进行跨域区分，并且它们失败是因为它们的嵌入空间是各向异性的。双通道对比微调修复了这个问题：第一次通过 72,034 对，第二次 - BODHI - 在知识图的缺失边缘上，这是完成繁重工作的通道，将分离度提高到 2.30×，将 F1 提高到 93%。整个过程在商用 Intel CPU 上运行，通过 OpenVINO 速度提高了 133 倍，根据每个部署指南的建议，FP16 击败了 INT8。We started with a model that thinks cortisol and the stock market are 83% alike, and ended with one that keeps the unrelated apart and the genuinely linked together, cleanly enough to reason on. The pretrained encoders fail cross-domain discrimination completely, and they fail because their embedding spaces are anisotropic. A two-pass contrastive fine-tune fixes it: the first pass on 72,034 pairs, the second — BODHI — on the absent edges of a knowledge graph, which is the pass that does the heavy lifting, taking separation to 2.30× and F1 to 93%. And the whole thing runs on commodity Intel CPUs, 133× faster through OpenVINO, with FP16 beating INT8 against every deployment guide’s advice.

对于人群来说，从相关性到因果性需要随机试验和仔细的推理。对于一个人来说——这就是 LBM 所在的地方——事实证明，这在很大程度上是一个嵌入几何的问题。正确处理跨域拉和推，图遍历就可以完成剩下的工作；如果他们错了，下游的聪明才智也无法恢复。这一层可以让他们在硬件上进行正确的基准测试，并根据模型要服务的人群进行扩展。On populations, getting from correlation to causation is a matter of randomised trials and careful inference. On one person — which is where an LBM lives — it turns out to be, in large part, a matter of embedding geometry. Get the cross-domain pulls and pushes right and the graph traversal can do the rest; get them wrong and no amount of downstream cleverness recovers. This is the layer that gets them right, benchmarked honestly, on hardware that scales to the people the model is meant to serve.
