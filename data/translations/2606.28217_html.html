在完全授权的人工智能合作社中实现价值受限的信用分配Towards Value-Constrained Credit Assignment in Fully Delegated AI Cooperatives

Young Yoon    Jimin Kim    Soyeon Park
摘要Abstract

我们提出了一个在完全委托的人工智能合作社中进行奖励分配的框架，其中人类由在异构价值约束下贡献数据并参与模型更新的代理代表。关键的想法是只信任那些在根据每个委托人的价值概况进行筛选后仍然可接受的更新。我们在遍历学习（TL）基底中制定值条件梯度过滤、在线边际贡献信号和累积收入结算。 TL 在这里特别有吸引力，因为它执行去中心化反向传播，而不会出现与以聚合为中心的分布式学习相关的质量损失，并且我们认为，它通过保留显式遍历和梯度路径，提供了比 FedAvg 式联邦学习更精细的归因基础。该框架的定位是针对数据评估、联邦贡献估计、个性化联邦学习和多元对齐。We propose a framework for reward allocation in fully delegated AI cooperatives where humans are represented by agents that contribute data and participate in model updates under heterogeneous value constraints. The key idea is to credit only those updates that remain admissible after screening them against each principal’s value profile. We formulate value-conditioned gradient filtering, online marginal contribution signals, and cumulative revenue settlement within a traversal learning (TL) substrate. TL is especially attractive here because it performs decentralized backpropagation without the quality loss associated with aggregation-centric distributed learning and, we argue, offers a finer attribution substrate than FedAvg-style federated learning by preserving explicit traversal and gradient paths. The framework is positioned against data valuation, federated contribution estimation, personalized federated learning, and pluralistic alignment.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能合作社汇集多个成员的数据和计算来生产共享的人工智能服务。在完全授权的合作社中，每个成员都由一个自治代理代表，该代理代表该成员参与学习。那么两个要求同时出现：学习必须尊重成员的价值约束，并且由此产生的服务的经济利益必须按照实际贡献进行分配。AI cooperatives pool data and computation from multiple members to produce shared AI services. In a fully delegated cooperative, each member is represented by an autonomous agent that participates in learning on the member’s behalf. Two requirements then arise simultaneously: learning must respect the member’s value constraints, and the economic benefits of the resulting service must be distributed according to actual contribution.

这些要求是耦合的。如果一个成员不允许军事部署，另一个成员拒绝保险风险评分，另一个成员要求不同人口群体之间的公平性，那么并非其数据引起的每一次更新都应同等地计入通用模型。忽略此类约束的奖励规则可能会对成员不认可的更新进行补偿，而具有贡献会计的价值意识学习规则则提供了可持续的激励结构。These requirements are coupled. If one member disallows military deployment, another rejects insurance risk scoring, and another requires fairness across demographic groups, then not every update induced by their data should count equally toward a common model. A reward rule that ignores such constraints may compensate members for updates they would not endorse, while a value-aware learning rule with contribution accounting offers sustainable incentive structure.

遍历学习（TL）是这种设置的有用基础。 TL 是一种去中心化反向传播形式，通过在分布式节点上保留集中式学习式梯度计算，避免了 FL、SL 和 SplitFed 式方案中出现的性能退化Traversal learning (TL) is a useful substrate for this setting. TL is a form of decentralized backpropagation that avoids the degradation seen in FL, SL, and SplitFed-style schemes by preserving centralized-learning-style gradient computation across distributed nodes[1]。相对于 FedAvg 式 FL. Relative to FedAvg-style FL[2]，TL 还使归因结构更加清晰：因为学习是通过显式遍历和精心编排的梯度流进行的，而不仅仅是聚合客户端更新，因此可以更透明地跟踪从本地数据暴露到可接受的更新再到合作效果的路径。当只有在检查值的可接受性之后才必须测量贡献时，这一点尤其重要。, TL also makes attribution structurally clearer: because learning proceeds through explicit traversal and orchestrated gradient flow rather than only aggregated client updates, the path from local data exposure to admissible update to cooperative effect can be tracked more transparently. This is particularly important when contribution must be measured only after checking value admissibility.

拟议的管道有四个步骤：委托人将数据和价值概况委托给代理人；代理计算本地更新并通过该值配置文件对其进行过滤；合作社估计可接受的更新对共享验证质量的边际贡献；并根据这些贡献分数来分配收入。The proposed pipeline has four steps: a principal delegates data and a value profile to an agent; the agent computes a local update and filters it through that value profile; the cooperative estimates the marginal contribution of the admissible update to shared validation quality; and revenue is allocated according to these contribution scores.

4 相关工作及定位Related Work and Positioning

基于 Shapley 的数据评估和基于影响的归因量化数据的价值或模型行为的更新Shapley-based data valuation and influence-based attribution quantify the value of data or updates with respect to model behavior[7, 8, 9]。然而，这些方式并不是异构价值约束下的合作支付规则。 FL 中的贡献估计和激励已经成为活跃的研究领域. However, these approaches are not cooperative payment rules under heterogeneous value constraints. Contribution estimation and incentives in FL are already active research areas[10, 11]。然而，这些方法通常从基于聚合的 FL 开始，并询问如何在聚合后估计或支付贡献。与之前的方法不同，我们的框架在测量其贡献之前，会在训练循环中在线评估每个更新的价值可接受性。. Yet those methods typically start from aggregation-based FL and ask how to estimate or pay for contribution after aggregation. Unlike prior approaches, our framework evaluates each update for value admissibility online within the training loop before its contribution is measured.

个性化 FL 解决异构客户目标，但主要是为了预测个性化而不是规范可接受性Personalized FL addresses heterogeneous client objectives, but primarily for predictive personalization rather than normative acceptability[12]。多元联盟凸显服务多元化人类价值观仍然是一个悬而未决的问题. Pluralistic alignment highlights that serving diverse human values remains an open problem[13]。我们的框架通过将多元化纳入培训时间学分分配机制本身来补充该文献。因此，它的新颖之处不仅仅是一种新的归因指标，而是将价值条件更新过滤、基于TL的归因、在线贡献信号和累积合作结算集成在一种机制中。. Our framework complements that literature by moving pluralism into the training-time credit-allocation mechanism itself. Its novelty is therefore not a new attribution metric alone, but the integration of value-conditioned update filtering, TL-based attribution, online contribution signals, and cumulative cooperative settlement in one mechanism.

5 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

该框架并没有通过将异质价值观整合为一个目标来解决多元化问题。相反，它使多元化在学习动态中发挥作用。经典收敛到单一最优值不一定成立，因为代理可能会接受不同的更新方向；更现实的期望是在价值受限的可行区域内稳定或折衷均衡。重要的是，即使全球趋同不完善，本地信用信号在每一步仍然是可计算的，因此会计范围内的累积结算仍然是明确定义的。The framework does not solve pluralism by collapsing heterogeneous values into one objective. Instead, it makes pluralism operational inside learning dynamics. Classical convergence to a single optimum need not hold because agents may admit different update directions; the more realistic expectation is stabilization within a value-constrained feasible region or compromise equilibrium. Importantly, even when global convergence is imperfect, the local credit signal remains computable at each step, so cumulative settlement over an accounting horizon is still well-defined.

为了提高效率和隐私，TL 变体For greater efficiency and privacy, a TL variant111https://github.com/neouly-inc/TLplus可以在每个成员代理处仅放置一个剪切层或浅前块，将安全屏蔽或私有化的激活传输到协调器，并让协调器执行剩余的反向传播could place only a cut layer or shallow front block at each member agent, transmit safely masked or privatized activations to the orchestrator, and let the orchestrator execute the remaining backpropagation[16]。这个方向是由 SplitNN 式分区推动的. This direction is motivated by SplitNN-style partitioning[14]以及最近的隐私保护方法，通过掩蔽或噪声注入来保护被破坏的激活and recent privacy-preserving methods that protect smashed activations through masking or noise injection[15]。这样的设计可以保留 TL 的归因优势，同时降低客户端成本并限制中间表示的信息泄漏。我们并不声称在公平性或 FL 激励机制方面优于 Shapley 式估值。相反，我们认为 TL 提供了比基于聚合的 FL 更透明的归因基础，并且这种基础使得在单个分布式学习框架中连接价值可接受性、贡献核算和合作奖励分配成为可能。. Such a design could preserve the attribution benefits of TL while reducing client-side cost and limiting information leakage from intermediate representations. We do not claim to outperform Shapley-style valuation in fairness or FL incentive mechanisms. Rather, we argue that TL offers a more transparent attribution substrate than aggregation-based FL, and that this substrate makes it possible to connect value admissibility, contribution accounting, and cooperative reward allocation in a single distributed-learning framework.
