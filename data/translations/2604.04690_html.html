摘要Abstract

由于严重的混乱、遮挡以及传统 3D 传感设置的高成本，实际工业环境中的箱式拣选仍然具有挑战性。我们推出了 Pickalo，这是一种完全基于低成本硬件构建的模块化 6D 基于姿势的垃圾箱拾取管道。腕式 RGB-D 相机从多个视点主动探索场景，同时使用 BridgeDepth 处理原始立体流，以获得适合准确碰撞推理的精细深度图。对象实例使用纯粹基于真实感合成数据训练的 Mask-RCNN 模型进行分割，并使用零样本 SAM-6D 姿态估计器进行定位。姿势缓冲模块随着时间的推移融合多视图观察，处理对象对称性并显着减少姿势噪声。离线时，我们为每个对象生成并管理大量对映抓取候选对象；在线查询基于实用程序的排名和快速碰撞检查以进行抓取规划。 Pickalo 部署在配备平行爪抓手和英特尔实感 D435i 的 UR5e 上，每小时可实现多达 600 次平均抓取，抓取成功率高达 96-99%，并且在密集装满的 Eurobox 上运行 30 分钟后性能强劲。消融研究证明了增强深度估计和姿态缓冲区对于现实工业条件下的长期稳定性和吞吐量的好处。 视频可在Bin picking in real industrial environments remains challenging due to severe clutter, occlusions, and the high cost of traditional 3D sensing setups. We present Pickalo, a modular 6D pose-based bin-picking pipeline built entirely on low-cost hardware. A wrist-mounted RGB-D camera actively explores the scene from multiple viewpoints, while raw stereo streams are processed with BridgeDepth to obtain refined depth maps suitable for accurate collision reasoning. Object instances are segmented with a Mask-RCNN model trained purely on photorealistic synthetic data and localized using the zero-shot SAM-6D pose estimator. A pose buffer module fuses multi-view observations over time, handling object symmetries and significantly reducing pose noise. Offline, we generate and curate large sets of antipodal grasp candidates per object; online, a utility-based ranking and fast collision checking are queried for the grasp planning. Deployed on a UR5e with a parallel-jaw gripper and an Intel RealSense D435i, Pickalo achieves up to  600 mean picks per hour with 96-99% grasp success and robust performance over 30-minute runs on densely filled euroboxes. Ablation studies demonstrate the benefits of enhanced depth estimation and of the pose buffer for long-term stability and throughput in realistic industrial conditions. I 简介Introduction

尽管经过了数十年的研究，垃圾箱拣选仍然是工业自动化的一个核心挑战。该任务要求机器人在不同的物体姿态下从杂乱的容器中检测、定位和提取物体。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种用于工业垃圾箱拣选的模块化 6D 抓取管道，该管道依赖于多视图采集并利用最新的基础模型进行姿态和深度估计。Despite decades of research, bin picking remains a central challenge in industrial automation. The task requires a robot to detect, localize, and extract objects from a cluttered container under varying object poses. In this work, we propose a modular 6D grasping pipeline for industrial bin picking that relies on multi-view acquisition and leverages the latest foundation models for pose and depth estimation.

传统上，分箱系统采用经典的视觉技术，例如 2D-3D 特征匹配、模板匹配和基于投票的姿态估计Conventionally, bin-picking systems adopt classical vision techniques such as 2D-3D feature matching, template matching, and vote-based pose estimation[23, 4, 10, 26, 30]。这些方法要么不够稳健，要么假设可以访问高质量的深度数据，这些数据通常是从工业级立体相机获得的。此类传感器产生具有丰富几何细节的密集点云，使配准算法能够可靠地将对象模型与观察结果对齐，即使在部分遮挡的情况下也是如此。然而，这些解决方案需要复杂且昂贵的设置，对用户采用造成了障碍。. Ⅲ-AIII-A 姿势相机选择Pose Camera selection

与依赖固定摄像头的经典工业场景相比，我们采用了安装在机器人手腕上的手眼摄像头。根据我们的经验，视点变化有助于减轻遮挡并确保完整的箱扫描覆盖。 我们采用了一种策略，以最佳传感器距离从以 bin 原点为中心的半球对相机姿势进行采样。 为了确保均匀的覆盖范围和视点可变性，可到达的相机姿势被排序为最大化与先前选择的视点的距离。In contrast to classical industrial scenarios relying on fixed cameras, we adopted an eye-in-hand camera mounted on the robot’s wrist. In our experience, viewpoint variation helps to mitigate occlusion and ensure full bin scanning coverage. We employed a strategy that samples camera poses, at optimal sensor distance, from a hemisphere centered at the bin origin. To ensure uniform coverage and viewpoint variability, reachable camera poses are ordered maximizing the distance from previously selected viewpoints.

At the end of the release phase, the robot acquires a new image, and a new cycle begins.

V 结论Conclusions

我们的工作解决了在现实工业条件下部署可靠、高吞吐量和经济高效的垃圾箱拣选的挑战，在这些条件下，严重的混乱、遮挡和严格的周期时间限制使得许多现有的研究原型难以在实践中使用。我们推出了 Pickalo，这是一个完整的基于 6D 姿势的垃圾箱拣选系统，围绕低成本硬件和模块化开源软件组件而设计。通过围绕稳健的物体姿态估计而不是纯粹基于几何的抓取检测构建管道，系统可以保持关于物体身份和方向的明确推理，这在下游流程要求每个部分以预定义的姿态呈现时至关重要。Our work has addressed the challenge of deploying reliable, high-throughput, and cost-effective bin picking in realistic industrial conditions, where severe clutter, occlusions, and tight cycle-time constraints make many existing research prototypes difficult to use in practice. We presented Pickalo, a complete 6D pose–based bin-picking system designed around low-cost hardware and modular, open-source software components. By structuring the pipeline around robust object pose estimation rather than purely geometry-based grasp detection, the system maintains explicit reasoning about object identity and orientation, which is crucial when downstream processes require each part to be presented in a predefined pose.

在具有三个具有挑战性的对象类别的密集填充的 Eurobox 上进行的实验结果表明，这种设计选择可以转化为强大的现实性能。 Pickalo 部署在配备平行钳口夹具和现成 RGB-D 摄像头的 UR5e 上，每小时可实现大约 600 次平均抓取，同时在连续 30 分钟的运行中保持抓取成功率在 96-99% 范围内。多视图感知、增强的深度估计（通过深度立体匹配处理）和随着时间的推移融合观察结果的姿势缓冲区的组合显着提高了严重遮挡和部分视图下的鲁棒性。消融研究进一步证实，姿态缓冲模块和深度处理都是稳定姿态估计和防止垃圾箱清空时出现故障的关键。Experimental results on densely filled euroboxes with three challenging object categories demonstrate that this design choice translates into strong real-world performance. Deployed on a UR5e equipped with a parallel jaw gripper and an off-the-shelf RGB-D camera, Pickalo achieves up to roughly 600 mean picks per hour while maintaining grasp success rates in the 96–99% range over continuous 30-minute runs. The combination of multi-view perception, enhanced depth estimation (via deep stereo matching processing), and a pose buffer that fuses observations over time significantly improves robustness under heavy occlusion and partial views. Ablation studies further confirm that both the pose buffer module and the depth processing are key to stabilizing pose estimates and preventing failures as the bin empties.

从系统设计的角度来看，这项工作的一个重要贡献是表明工业相关的箱子拾取性能不需要专门或昂贵的传感硬件。相反，我们证明了现代 6D 姿态估计、合成数据驱动的实例分割和离线抓取集生成的仔细集成，再加上快速碰撞检查和简单的基于实用程序的排名，可以缩小学术方法和生产要求之间的大部分差距。系统结构保持模块化：感知、姿态融合、抓取选择和运动规划都可以随着新算法的出现而被替换或升级，而无需重新设计整个管道。From a system-design perspective, an important contribution of this work is showing that industrially relevant bin-picking performance does not require specialized or expensive sensing hardware. Instead, we demonstrate that careful integration of modern 6D pose estimation, synthetic-data–driven instance segmentation, and offline grasp set generation, combined with fast collision checking and simple utility-based ranking, can close much of the gap between academic methods and production requirements. The system structure remains modular: perception, pose fusion, grasp selection, and motion planning can each be replaced or upgraded as new algorithms become available, without redesigning the entire pipeline.
