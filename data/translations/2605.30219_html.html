摘要Abstract

长视野交互需要语言模型来管理累积的信息：何时更新其状态，何时保留其状态以及忽略什么。 我们将这一挑战研究为Long-horizon interactions require language models to manage accumulating information: when to update their state, when to preserve their state, and what to ignore. We study this challenge as情境信念管理 (CBM)Contextual Belief Management (CBM)：保持与正式证据一致的预测信念状态，同时隔离与任务无关的噪音。 为了使 CBM 可测量，我们引入了 BeliefTrack，这是一个涵盖规则发现和电路诊断的封闭世界基准，其中有限的置信空间和符号验证器可以实现精确的回合级评估。 BeliefTrack 诊断三种故障：停留失败、更新失败和隔离失败。 在多个法学硕士中，普通模型表现出严重的 CBM 失败，而明确的信念跟踪提示提供的收益有限。 相比之下，带有信念状态奖励的强化学习平均将失败率降低了 70.9%。 进一步的探索揭示了这些失败背后的潜在信念状态动态，并且表征级转向将两项任务的失败率降低了 46.1%: maintaining a predicted belief state aligned with formal evidence while isolating task-irrelevant noise. To make CBM measurable, we introduce BeliefTrack, a closed-world benchmark spanning Rule Discovery and Circuit Diagnosis, where a finite belief space and symbolic verifiers enable exact turn-level evaluation. BeliefTrack diagnoses three failures: Failed Stay, Failed Update, and Failed Isolation. Across multiple LLMs, vanilla models exhibit severe CBM failures, while explicit belief-tracking prompts provide limited gains. In contrast, reinforcement learning with belief-state rewards reduces failure rates by 70.9% on average. 6 稳健性和机制分析Robustness and Mechanistic Analysis

我们沿着三个轴分析 CBM：时间稳健性、上下文稳健性和机制。 我们首先改变锚定深度来测试模型是否可以在长期证据历史中保留和修改信念状态。 然后，我们改变与任务无关的噪音，以测试模型是否可以将正式证据与误导性线索区分开来。 最后，我们使用基于提示的探测和表示级引导来检查 CBM 故障是否对应于可修改的表示级模式。We analyze CBM along three axes: temporal robustness, contextual robustness, and mechanism. We first vary anchoring depth to test whether models can preserve and revise belief states over long evidence histories. We then vary task-irrelevant noise to test whether models can isolate formal evidence from misleading cues. Finally, we use prompt-based probing and representation-level steering to examine whether CBM failures correspond to modifiable representation-level patterns.

7 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced情境信念管理Contextual Belief Management(CBM) 和 BeliefTrack 研究长期交互中证据一致的信念跟踪。 当前的法学硕士表现出严重的 CBM 失败，提示无法可靠地解决这些问题，而验证者引导的奖励学习则改善了信念管理并在不同环境中进行推广。 我们的探索和引导分析进一步表明，这些失败与可修改的表示级别模式相关，使得 CBM 既可测量又可操作。(CBM) and BeliefTrack to study evidence-aligned belief tracking in long-horizon interactions. Current LLMs exhibit substantial CBM failures that prompting does not reliably fix, while verifier-guided reward learning improves belief management and generalizes across environments. Our probing and steering analyses further suggest that these failures are associated with modifiable representation-level patterns, making CBM both measurable and actionable.

局限性Limitations

信仰范围范围。Scope of BeliefTrack.

我们的实验使用两个合成环境（规则发现和电路诊断）实例化 BeliefTrack，这对于隔离信念状态跟踪很有用，但当前设置中未涵盖更多开放式的信念修正形式。Our experiments instantiate BeliefTrack with two synthetic environments, Rule Discovery and Circuit Diagnosis, which are useful for isolating belief-state tracking but more open-ended forms of belief revision are not covered in the current setting.

情境敏感性和社会影响。Context Sensitivity and Societal Impact.

相关证据和不相关噪声之间的界限在 BeliefTrack 中明确定义，而现实世界的交互通常混合了用户修正、偏好、不确定性表达、情绪线索和社会背景。 过度过滤此类信号的模型可能会对合法反馈或不断变化的用户意图反应迟钝。 因此，未来的工作应该研究校准的上下文敏感性，平衡对干扰的鲁棒性和对相关上下文输入的灵活性。The boundary between relevant evidence and irrelevant noise is explicitly defined in BeliefTrack, whereas real-world interactions often mix user corrections, preferences, uncertainty expressions, emotional cues, and social context. A model that over-filters such signals may become less responsive to legitimate feedback or changing user intent. Future work should therefore study calibrated context sensitivity, balancing robustness to interference with flexibility toward relevant contextual input.
