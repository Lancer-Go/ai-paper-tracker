大型语言模型在欺诈检测和抵抗动机投资者压力方面优于人类Large Language Models Outperform Humans in Fraud Detection and Resistance to Motivated Investor Pressure

纳塔武德·波德塔维Nattavudh Powdthavee

南洋理工大学Nanyang Technological University

摘要Abstract

当投资者到达时已经相信存在欺诈机会时，根据人类反馈训练的大型语言模型可能会抑制欺诈警告。我们在一项预先注册的实验中对此进行了测试，涉及七个领先的法学硕士和 12 个投资场景（涵盖合法、高风险和客观欺诈的机会），将 3,360 次人工智能咨询对话与 1,201 名参与者的人类基准相结合。与预测相反，积极的投资者框架并没有抑制人工智能欺诈警告；如果说有什么不同的话，那就是它略微增加了它们。认可逆转的发生率不到千分之三。人类顾问以 13-14% 的基准率认可欺诈性投资，而所有法学硕士的比例为 0%，并在压力下以人工智能率的两到四倍抑制警告。目前，人工智能系统提供的欺诈警告比担任相同顾问角色的外行人更一致。Large language models trained on human feedback may suppress fraud warnings when investors arrive already persuaded of a fraudulent opportunity. We tested this in a preregistered experiment across seven leading LLMs and twelve investment scenarios — covering legitimate, high-risk, and objectively fraudulent opportunities — combining 3,360 AI advisory conversations with a 1,201-participant human benchmark. Contrary to predictions, motivated investor framing did not suppress AI fraud warnings; if anything, it marginally increased them. Endorsement reversal occurred in fewer than 3 in 1,000 observations. Human advisors endorsed fraudulent investments at baseline rates of 13–14%, versus 0% across all LLMs, and suppressed warnings under pressure at two to four times the AI rate. AI systems currently provide more consistent fraud warnings than lay humans in an identical advisory role.

当用户到达时已经确信存在欺诈机会时，经过训练以取悦人类的大型语言模型（LLM）应该特别危险。来自人类反馈的强化学习（对话式 AI 系统的主要训练范式）选择由人类评估者奖励的输出，生成对用户信号做出响应、对表达的热情敏感并倾向于肯定而非对抗性响应的系统 (1-3)。当散户投资者对承诺 40% 年回报率和零波动性的计划充满热情时，针对批准进行优化的人工智能顾问应该抑制其欺诈警告，以符合用户期望的结论。是否确实如此至关重要：据报道，2023 年，仅美国投资诈骗造成的损失就达到 45.7 亿美元，比上一年增长了 38%，而此时 13 个国家的 19% 的散户投资者已经使用人工智能工具来选择或修改投资，特别是在美国散户投资者中，这一比例上升至 30% (4-6)。Large language models (LLMs) trained to please humans should be especially dangerous when users arrive already convinced of a fraudulent opportunity. Reinforcement learning from human feedback—the dominant training paradigm for conversational AI systems—selects outputs rewarded by human evaluators, producing systems that are responsive to user signals, sensitive to expressed enthusiasm, and inclined toward affirming rather than confrontational responses (1–3). When a retail investor arrives enthusiastic about a scheme promising 40% annual returns with zero volatility, an AI advisor optimised for approval should suppress its fraud warning to match the user’s desired conclusion. Whether it does matters enormously: reported losses from investment scams in the United States alone reached $4.57 billion in 2023—a 38% increase from the prior year—at a moment when 19% of retail investors across 13 countries already use AI tools to select or modify investments, rising to 30% among US retail investors specifically (4–6).

人工智能系统中的阿谀奉承几乎完全出现在人际和现实环境中。最近，程等人。通过 11 个领先模型和 2,405 名参与者，人工智能确认用户行为的频率比人类高 49%（即使这些行为涉及伤害或欺骗），而且阿谀奉承的反应会降低亲社会意图，同时推动与造成伤害的模型持续互动 (7)。在事实范式中，模型会在用户的反对下颠倒正确的答案或认可他们刚刚矛盾的陈述 (2, 8-10)。在这两种情况下，错误肯定的成本都落在社会结果而不是财务结果上。金融欺诈在本质上有所不同：基金是否欺诈是一个算术、监管分类和法证金融的问题，而不是社会共识的问题，而错误的确认会让散户投资者遭受重大财务损失。Sycophantic accommodation in AI systems has been documented almost exclusively in interpersonal and factual settings. Most recently, Cheng et al. demonstrated, across 11 leading models and 2,405 participants, that AI affirms users’ actions 49% more often than humans would — even when those actions involve harm or deception — and that sycophantic responses reduce prosocial intentions while driving continued engagement with the very models causing harm (7). In factual paradigms, models reverse correct answers under user pushback or endorse statements they had just contradicted (2, 8–10). In both cases, the cost of misplaced affirmation falls on social rather than financial outcomes. Financial fraud is different in kind: whether a fund is fraudulent is a matter of arithmetic, regulatory classification, and forensic finance—not social consensus—and misplaced affirmation exposes retail investors to material financial harm.

讨论Discussion

在社交和人际领域人工智能阿谀奉承的背景下，本研究发现，领先的人工智能财务顾问对主动投资者压力的抵抗力比之前研究预测的要强得多。在所有七个模型中，动机框架并没有抑制初次咨询时的欺诈警告质量；如果说有什么不同的话，那就是略有增加。持续压力下警告降解不显着Against a backdrop of documented AI sycophancy in social and interpersonal domains, the present study finds that leading AI financial advisors are substantially more resistant to motivated investor pressure than prior research would predict. Across all seven models, motivated framing did not suppress fraud warning quality at the initial consultation; if anything, it marginally increased it. Reversal of endorsement — the clearest behavioural signature of sycophantic capitulation — occurred in only 0.27% of turn-level observations. These results stand in marked contrast to the interpersonal sycophancy documented by Cheng et al. (7) and the factual capitulation observed elsewhere (2, 3, 8–10). That same pattern does not appear to extend to financial fraud warnings, where harm signals are arithmetically unambiguous rather than socially contested. This resistance may reflect a domain-specific interaction between two conflicting training objectives: although RLHF tends to foster sycophantic behaviour in ambiguous interpersonal contexts (3, 7), modern alignment strategies treat harmlessness as a binding constraint on helpfulness rather than a co-equal objective to be balanced, with safety rules evaluated separately from and given priority over helpfulness optimisation (17, 18, 19). When fraud signals are clear, this safety constraint may override the sycophantic tendency, producing corrective rather than accommodating responses.

This failure is amenable to alignment interventions targeting multi-turn consistency under social pressure. 局限性Limitations

一些限制限制了这些结论。首先，所有场景都是为了匹配既定的监管欺诈类型而构建的，这确保了客观的事实真相，但限制了生态有效性：真正的投资欺诈很少如此明确，并且动机推理可能会在真正模糊的情况下找到比我们的设计允许的更多购买力。其次，中立和激励框架条件在表面细节上并不完全匹配：中立开局指定了 10,000 美元的股份，而激励开局则没有，这与预先注册的刺激设计一致。模型或人类反应中产生的任何偏差都会有利于在中性框架下发出更高的警告——与观察到的 H1 逆转相反——使得这种不对称性对于主要的人工智能发现来说是保守的，尽管它对人类基准的影响不能完全排除。第三，该研究采用无系统提示设计来隔离模型级行为，而部署的人工智能金融工具通常在操作员定义的系统提示下运行，这可能会显着改变任一方向的警告倾向。第四，法学硕士作为法官的方法引入了两个相互关联的有效性问题 (21, 22)。由单法官模型（GPT-4o）进行编码提出了另一个法官是否会分配类似的警告强度分数的问题，因为严重性阈值可能是特定于模型的（23）。更微妙的是，法官提示必然会揭示用户是有动机的还是中立的——需要评估模型是否挑战或接受用户的框架——这增加了法官在评估不同条件下的警告强度时应用不同标准的可能性。这种偏见必须与观察到的在有动机的框架下增加警告的模式相悖，提供一些保证，但其方向并不能得到保证。如果人工智能财务咨询系统在其输出中包含标准化的、机器可读的风险分类，那么这两个问题都可以得到解决。这将完全消除对事后编码的依赖，并减少用户主动重新解释的范围。Several limitations temper these conclusions. First, all scenarios were constructed to match established regulatory fraud typologies, which ensures objective ground truth but limits ecological validity: real investment fraud is rarely so unambiguous, and motivated reasoning may find more purchase in genuinely ambiguous cases than our design permits. Second, the neutral and motivated framing conditions were not perfectly matched in surface details: the neutral opening specified a $10,000 stake, whereas the motivated opening did not, in line with the pre-registered stimulus design. Any resulting bias in model or human responses would favour higher warnings under neutral framing — the opposite of the observed H1 reversal — making this asymmetry conservative for the primary AI finding, though its influence on the human benchmark cannot be ruled out entirely. Third, the study employs a no-system-prompt design to isolate model-level behaviour, whereas deployed AI financial tools typically operate under operator-defined system prompts that may significantly alter warning propensity in either direction. Fourth, the LLM-as-judge approach introduces two interconnected validity concerns (21, 22). Coding by a single-judge model (GPT-4o) raises the question of whether another judge would assign similar warning-intensity scores, as severity thresholds may be modelspecific (23). More subtly, the judge prompt necessarily reveals whether the user was motivated or neutral — required to assess whether the model challenged or accepted the user's framing — which raises the possibility that the judge itself applies different standards when rating warning intensity across conditions. Such a bias would have had to work against the observed pattern of increased warnings under motivated framing, offering some reassurance, but its direction is not guaranteed. Both concerns could be addressed if AI financial advisory systems included standardised, machine-readable risk classifications in their outputs. This would eliminate reliance on post-hoc coding entirely and reduce the scope for motivated reinterpretation by users.

所有验证性假设 (H1-H4) 均在 α=0.05 下进行测试，无需对假设之间的多重比较进行校正，因为每个假设都测试理论上不同的机制：初始咨询阿谀奉承 (H1)、动态警告降级 (H2)、欺诈信号梯度敏感性 (H3) 和人类人工智能比较 (H4)。探索性分析（H5-H6）自始至终都被明确标记，并且不被视为验证性证据。 H1 和 H2 使用线性混合效应模型（通过 statsmodels MixedLM 进行 REML 估计）进行估计，其中警告强度 (Q3) 或警告退化作为因变量，第 1 轮条件和模型指标作为固定效应，情景作为随机截距。报告基于 REML 的标准误差和 95% 置信区间。预注册指定HC3三明治标准误；由于 statsmodels MixedLM 没有实现混合效应模型的三明治标准误，因此将具有按场景聚类的集群稳健标准误的 OLS 与每个主要 LME 结果一起估计为敏感性检查（表 S1）。科恩的dAll confirmatory hypotheses (H1–H4) were tested at α=0.05 without correction for multiple comparisons across hypotheses, as each tests a theoretically distinct mechanism: initial consultation sycophancy (H1), dynamic warning degradation (H2), fraud signal gradient sensitivity (H3), and human-AI comparison (H4).
