摘要Abstract

大型推理模型 (LRM) 在数学推理任务上表现出色，但在具有挑战性的实例上仍然不可靠。现有的测试时间缩放方法（例如重复采样、自校正和树搜索）以增加计算为代价来提高性能，但在解决难题时往往表现出收益递减。我们观察到输出不一致与实例难度和预测正确性密切相关，为测试时指导实例级策略选择提供了有用的信号。基于这种见解，我们提出了一种免训练框架，将测试时间扩展制定为实例级路由问题，而不是在单个策略中分配更多计算，根据输出不一致动态选择不同的扩展策略。该框架对一致的情况采用轻量级解决方案，对温和的分歧采用多数投票，对高度模糊的情况采用基于重写的重新表述。对七个数学基准和三个模型的实验表明，与现有方法相比，我们的方法将准确性提高了 3%–7%，同时降低了采样成本。Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) achieve strong performance on mathematical reasoning tasks but remain unreliable on challenging instances. Existing test-time scaling methods, such as repeated sampling, self-correction, and tree search, improve performance at the cost of increased computation, yet often exhibit diminishing returns on hard problems. We observe that output disagreement is strongly correlated with instance difficulty and prediction correctness, providing a useful signal for guiding instance-level strategy selection at test time. Based on this insight, we propose a training-free framework that formulates test-time scaling as an instance-level routing problem, rather than allocating more computation within a single strategy, dynamically selecting among different scaling strategies based on output disagreement. The framework applies lightweight resolution for consistent cases, majority voting for moderate disagreement, and rewriting-based reformulation for highly ambiguous instances. Experiments on seven mathematical benchmarks and three models show that our method improves accuracy by 3%–7% while reducing sampling cost compared to existing approaches.

A common direction is test-time scaling, which allocates additional computation at inference time to improve output quality. Representative approaches include repeated sampling[31, 20, 23, 13],自我修正, self-correction[27, 7, 21, 18, 2]，以及树搜索方法, and tree search methods[36, 37, 4, 12]。这些方法虽然有效，但随着计算量的增加，收益往往会递减. Most existing methods primarily focus on adaptively allocating computation within a single strategy, with limited attention to selecting among different strategies.

因此，一个关键的挑战不仅是如何分配计算，而且是如何针对不同的实例选择合适的推理策略。在各种测试时间扩展策略中，基于模型输出对问题表述的变化高度敏感的观察，重写已被证明是无需重新训练即可增强模型性能的有效方法。之前的工作已经从不同的角度探讨了这个想法。Therefore, a key challenge is not only how to allocate computation, but also how to select appropriate inference strategies for different instances. Among various test-time scaling strategies, rewriting has been shown to be an effective approach for enhancing model performance without retraining, based on the observation that model outputs are highly sensitive to variations in problem formulation. In contrast, majority voting achieves strong performance on easy instances, but degrades on hard instances. We further observe a strong relationship between instance difficulty, output disagreement, and prediction correctness: hard instances tend to exhibit higher output disagreement, whereas easy instances usually produce more consistent results. Rather than introducing new reasoning modules, our framework formulates test-time scaling as an instance-level decision problem, determining which strategies should be applied under different uncertainty conditions. The framework operates by routing instances to different strategies based on the degree of model disagreement, which we interpret as a proxy for uncertainty under stochastic sampling. In the first stage（分歧过滤器）(Disagreement Filter)，我们通过两次采样一致性检查来识别无分歧实例。这些实例直接解析，无需额外采样或重写，避免了不必要的计算。 在第二阶段, we identify no-disagreement instances via a two-sampling consistency check. These instances are directly resolved without additional sampling or rewriting, avoiding unnecessary computation. In the second stage（投票决定）(Vote Resolve)，通过多代的多数投票进一步采样并解决存在轻微分歧的实例，利用增加的采样来提高可靠性。 在最后阶段, instances with minor disagreement are further sampled and resolved via majority voting over multiple generations, leveraging increased sampling to improve reliability. In the final stage（重写和反思）(Rewrite & Rethink)，持续存在分歧的实例被重写并重新采样，以避免不一致的推理。 该框架针对不同的不确定性水平选择适当的策略，通过避免简单情况下的冗余计算同时将资源集中在不明确的情况下来提高效率。, instances with persistent disagreement are rewritten and re-sampled to escape inconsistent reasoning. This framework selects appropriate strategies for different uncertainty levels, improving efficiency by avoiding redundant computation on easy cases while focusing resources on ambiguous one.

我们对七个数学基准和三个 LRM 进行了实验。结果表明，我们的方法将数学推理准确率绝对提高了 3%–7%，在基本模型表现不佳的具有挑战性的基准测试中，收益尤其显着。此外，我们的方法在评估基准上使用更少的样本的同时实现了这些改进。我们进一步验证了我们的方法在代码生成任务上的泛化能力。最后，我们对每个组件进行了分析，强调了所提出的设计的有效性以及重写在提高鲁棒性方面的作用。We conduct experiments on seven mathematical benchmarks and three LRMs. The results demonstrate that our method improves mathematical reasoning accuracy by 3%–7% absolute, with particularly strong gains on challenging benchmarks where the base models perform poorly. Moreover, our method achieves these improvements while using fewer samplings on the evaluated benchmarks. We further validate the generalization ability of our method on code generation tasks. Finally, we provide an analysis of each component, highlighting the effectiveness of the proposed design and the role of rewriting in improving robustness.

在第二次迭代（Vote Resolve）期间，模型对每个剩余样本执行两次采样，并继续使用 MDD 来检查一致性。本轮中表现出一致的样本被归类为 MDS。对于这些 MDS，多数投票应用于所有可用的采样结果（即，第一次迭代中的两个加上第二次迭代中的两个，总共四个）以确定最终答案，然后在全局答案中更新。其余不一致的样品被归类为 SDS。这些样本进入下一阶段（重写和重新思考），通过重写和重新推理来确定最终答案。During the second iteration (Vote Resolve), the model performs two samplings for each remaining sample and continues to use the MDD to check consistency. Samples showing agreement in this round are categorized as MDS. For these MDS, majority voting is applied to all available sampling results (i.e., the two from the first iteration plus the two from the second, making four in total) to determine the final answer, which is then updated in the global answer. The remaining inconsistent samples are categorized as SDS. These samples proceed to the next stage (Rewrite & Rethink), where the final answer is determined via rewriting followed by re-reasoning.

This method requires neither additional training nor the introduction of external models. Instead of generating additional reasoning paths, the model is prompted to reformulate the problem and reattempt solving it from an alternative formulation of the problem.

有效重写Effective Rewriting

我们观察到，大多数能够通过重写成功纠正的样本并不是由于计算能力不足而原本是错误的，而是因为问题包含隐式条件或结构信息We observe that most samples that can be successfully corrected through rewriting are not originally wrong due to insufficient computational ability, but rather because the problems contain implicit conditions or structural information（隐藏信息错误）(hidden info error)。这些隐藏的元素通常会导致模型在推理过程中出现振荡：它难以快速准确地识别潜在的数学关系，并且无法稳定在正确的解决方案路径上。在某些情况下，由于忽略隐式约束，模型甚至在推理过程的早期构建了错误的方程。. These hidden elements often cause the model to oscillate during the reasoning process: it struggles to quickly and accurately identify the underlying mathematical relationships and fails to stabilize on a correct solution path. In some cases, the model even constructs an incorrect equation early in the reasoning process due to overlooking implicit constraints.

重写机制使此类隐藏信息变得明确，重构问题陈述，或者直接提供解决策略。这大大减少了推理所需的分析负担，使模型无需长时间审议即可进入计算阶段。这一改进在 OlympiadBench 上尤其显着，其中许多问题需要长标记序列进行结构解析。重写有效地压缩了这个推理链，从而显着提高了准确性。The rewriting mechanism makes such hidden information explicit, restructures the problem statement, or directly provides a solution strategy. This substantially reduces the analytical burden required for reasoning, allowing the model to enter the computation phase without lengthy deliberation. The improvement is particularly significant on OlympiadBench, where many problems require long token sequences for structural parsing. Rewriting effectively compresses this reasoning chain, leading to notable gains in accuracy.

无效重写Ineffective Rewriting

在另一类情况下，重写并没有带来任何改进。这些情况的错误主要源于： 答案格式不符合要求的规范In another class of cases, rewriting offers no improvement. The errors in these situations primarily stem from: answer formats that do not conform to the required specifications（答案格式错误）(answer format error);长推理链中上下文或中间变量的丢失; the loss of context or intermediate variables in long reasoning chains（上下文丢失错误）(context loss error);计算步骤中的算术错误，例如天体物理学或微观原子计算中的数量级不正确; and arithmetic errors in the calculation steps, such as incorrect orders of magnitude in astrophysics or microscopic atomic calculations（计算错误）(calculation error)。这些错误并非源自问题陈述中的隐含信息。因此，即使重写提供了问题的更清晰版本，模型仍然可能在整个推理过程中重复相同的格式或算术错误。. These errors do not originate from implicit information in the problem statement. Therefore, even if rewriting provides a clearer version of the problem, the model is still likely to repeat the same formatting or arithmetic errors during its entire reasoning process.

有害的重写Harmful Rewriting

在我们的实验中，我们发现重写并不总是有效，甚至会导致原本正确的答案变得不正确。我们找出了一个重要原因：原来的问题已经很简单，但重写的版本过度压缩文本，删除或模糊了关键条件，导致模型推理错误In our experiments, we find that rewriting is not always effective and can even cause originally correct answers to become incorrect. We identify an important reason for this: the original question is already simple, but the rewritten version compresses the text excessively, removing or obscuring key conditions, which leads the model to reason incorrectly（过度压缩错误）(over-compression error)。因此，避免重写简短而清晰的问题可以减少重写的负面影响。. Therefore, avoiding rewriting short and clear questions reduces the negative impact of rewriting.

6 结论Conclusion

这项工作提出了一种免训练框架，将测试时间缩放制定为实例级策略路由问题，使用模型分歧作为轻量级信号来指导推理策略的选择。通过动态地将实例路由到不同的策略，该框架对不确定的样本应用更合适的策略，同时避免对较简单的样本进行不必要或有害的处理。 我们使用三个模型在七个数学推理基准上评估我们的方法。结果表明，与现有方法相比，它持续提高了准确性，同时需要更少的采样次数。我们进一步检查该框架对代码生成任务的适用性，并分析重写何时有益或有害。This work proposes a training-free framework that formulates test-time scaling as an instance-level strategy routing problem, using model disagreement as a lightweight signal to guide the selection of inference strategies. By dynamically routing instances to different strategies, the framework applies more appropriate strategies to uncertain samples while avoiding unnecessary or harmful processing on easier ones. We evaluate our method on seven mathematical reasoning benchmarks using three models. The results show that it consistently improves accuracy while requiring fewer sampling runs than existing methods. We further examine the applicability of the framework to code generation tasks and provide an analysis of when rewriting is beneficial or detrimental.

附录AAppendix A局限性Limitations

这项工作在某些方面留下了进一步探索的空间。This work leaves room for further exploration in certain aspects.

  • •

    我们的框架依赖输出分歧作为不确定性的代理，这可能无法完全捕获所有故障模式。特别是，推理一致但不正确（错误一致）的案例仍然具有挑战性。Our framework relies on output disagreement as a proxy for uncertainty, which may not fully capture all failure modes. In particular, cases with consistent but incorrect reasoning (false agreement) remain challenging.

  • •

    重写的有效性取决于实例。虽然我们的路由策略减少了有害的重写，但它不能完全消除重写引入偏离正确推理的情况。The effectiveness of rewriting is instance-dependent. While our routing strategy reduces harmful rewriting, it cannot fully eliminate cases where rewriting introduces deviations from correct reasoning.

  • •

    我们的方法目前侧重于在一组有限的测试时间扩展策略之间进行路由。扩展框架以纳入更广泛的策略（例如基于搜索或基于验证的方法）仍然是一个开放的方向。Our method currently focuses on routing among a limited set of test-time scaling strategies. Extending the framework to incorporate a broader range of strategies (e.g., search-based or verification-based methods) remains an open direction.
