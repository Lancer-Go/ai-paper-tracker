1 简介Introduction

通过具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）对大型语言模型进行后训练已成为提高推理和解决问题能力的标准方法Post-training large language models through reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has emerged as a standard approach for improving reasoning and problem-solving capabilities(Jaech et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025; Team et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025)。 RLVR方法中，组相对策略优化. Among RLVR methods, Group Relative Policy Optimization(GRPO; Shao et al., 2024)因其简单性和稳定性而被广泛采用。 GRPO 通过标准化一组部署中的结果奖励来估计优势，产生统一应用于部署中的每个代币的单一标量优势。对于成功的部署，这种统一的分配通常是合适的，因为大多数中间步骤都支持正确的结果。相反，对于失败的推出，这种粗略的令牌信用分配会在整个序列中分配统一的惩罚。因此，政策更新缺乏解决特定偏差所需的重点，这最终会降低样本效率并减慢收敛速度is widely adopted for its simplicity and stability. GRPO estimates advantages by normalizing outcome rewards across a group of rollouts, producing a single scalar advantage that is applied uniformly to every token in a rollout. For successful rollouts, this uniform assignment is generally appropriate, as most intermediate steps support the correct outcome. Conversely, for failed rollouts, this coarse token credit assignment distributes a uniform penalty across the entire sequence. Consequently, the policy update lacks the focus needed to address specific deviations, which ultimately diminishes sample efficiency and slows convergence(Khandoga et al., 2026; Kumar et al., 2026; Parthasarathi et al., 2025).

Taken together, SRPO improves not only training efficiency and stability, but also computational efficiency in terms of response length and per-step compute time.

5 结论Conclusion

我们重新审视了 LLM 训练后奖励驱动强化和自蒸馏之间的权衡，并提出了 SRPO，这是一个统一的策略框架，它将成功的样本路由到 GRPO 进行奖励调整强化，将带有教师信息的失败样本路由到 SDPO 进行密集的逻辑级别校正，并结合熵感知动态加权来抑制不可靠的自蒸馏信号并强调自信的信号。在五个基准和两个模型尺度上，SRPO 始终优于纯 GRPO 和 SDPO，这表明样本级路由可以保持自蒸馏的早期效率，同时保持奖励驱动强化的长期稳定性。此外，SRPO 在较长的训练范围内可产生适中的响应长度和较低的每步计算时间。未来工作的一个重要方向是将这个框架扩展到反馈更丰富的环境中，以便自蒸馏分支能够更好地利用环境信息。We revisit the trade-off between reward-driven reinforcement and self-distillation in LLM post-training and propose SRPO, a unified on-policy framework that routes successful samples to GRPO for reward-aligned reinforcement and failed samples with teacher information to SDPO for dense logit-level correction, together with entropy-aware dynamic weighting to suppress unreliable self-distillation signals and emphasize confident ones. Across five benchmarks and two model scales, SRPO consistently outperforms both pure GRPO and SDPO, demonstrating that sample-level routing can preserve the early efficiency of self-distillation while maintaining the long-horizon stability of reward-driven reinforcement. Moreover, SRPO yields moderate response lengths and lower per-step compute time over long training horizons. An important direction for future work is to extend this framework to environments with richer feedback, so that self-distillation branch can better leverage environment information.

A.1 具有可验证奖励的强化学习Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards

具有可验证奖励的培训后已成为法学硕士调整和适应的中心范式，建立在 REINFORCE 和 PPO 等政策梯度基础上Post-training with verifiable rewards has become a central paradigm for LLM alignment and adaptation, building on policy-gradient foundations such as REINFORCE and PPO(Williams, 1992; Schulman et al., 2017)。越来越多的工作将这些想法应用于 LLM 后期培训，其中序列级结果奖励指导模型采样轨迹的优化. A growing body of work applies these ideas to LLM post-training, where sequence-level outcome rewards guide optimization on model-sampled trajectories(Guo et al., 2025; Shao et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025; Zhoubian et al., 2025)。其中，GRPO 无需单独的批评者即可估计群体相对奖励的优势，使其成为强大且可扩展的基线. Among them, GRPO estimates advantages from group-relative rewards without requiring a separate critic, making it a strong and scalable baseline(Shao et al., 2024).

然而，这些方法通常为每个代币统一分配一个标量优势，因此信用分配仍然很粗糙。最近的分析表明，这种统一分配稀释了因果不相关标记的梯度However, these methods typically assign a single scalar advantage uniformly to every token, so credit assignment remains coarse. Recent analyses have shown that this uniform assignment dilutes gradients across causally irrelevant tokens(Khandoga et al., 2026)，阻碍了近乎正确的程序中语义错误的定位, hinders localization of semantic errors in near-correct programs(Kumar et al., 2026)，并引入随序列长度而增长的偏差, and introduces bias that grows with sequence length(Parthasarathi et al., 2025)。互补的工作线旨在通过过程监督或过程奖励模型来改进信用分配，这些模型提供从中间状态或反馈得出的更密集的步骤级信号. A complementary line of work seeks to improve credit assignment through process supervision or process reward models, which provide denser step-level signals derived from intermediate states or feedback(Lightman et al., 2023; Setlur et al., 2025; Cui et al., 2025)。这些方法提供了更细粒度的指导，但通常需要额外的学习奖励估计器。这种权衡激发了在不引入额外奖励模型的情况下提供更密集监督的方法。. These approaches offer finer-grained guidance but usually require additional learned reward estimators. This trade-off motivates methods that provide denser supervision without introducing an additional reward model.

A.2 合规蒸馏和自蒸馏On-Policy Distillation and Self-Distillation

蒸馏通过匹配输出分布或中间表示将行为从教师转移到学生Distillation transfers behavior from a teacher to a student by matching output distributions or intermediate representations(Hinton et al., 2015; Kim and Rush, 2016; Sanh et al., 2019)。最近的策略蒸馏方法通过在学生自己的轨迹上训练学生同时接受教师对相同轨迹的指导来减少训练与测试的不匹配. More recent on-policy distillation methods reduce train-test mismatch by training the student on its own trajectories while receiving teacher guidance on those same trajectories(Agarwal et al., 2024; Gu et al., 2023; Lu and Lab, 2025)。相对于仅奖励强化学习，这些方法提供了更密集的监督，但它们通常依赖于独立且通常更强大的外部教师。. Relative to reward-only RL, these methods provide denser supervision, but they typically rely on a separate and often stronger external teacher.

自蒸馏通过使用自身的条件版本来监督模型，从而消除了对外部教师的需要。上下文蒸馏首先表明模型可以将特权上下文引发的行为内化到其参数中Self-distillation removes the need for an external teacher by supervising the model with a conditioned version of itself. Context distillation first showed that a model can internalize behavior induced by privileged context into its parameters(Snell et al., 2022)。最近的工作将这一想法扩展到自我改进和符合政策的自我蒸馏设置，包括从自我生成的轨迹或更丰富的调节信息中学习. More recent work extends this idea to self-improvement and on-policy self-distillation settings, including learning from self-generated trajectories or richer conditioning information(Mitra and Ulukus, 2025; Hübotter et al., 2025; 2026; Shenfeld et al., 2026; Zhao et al., 2026; Buening et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026)。一个代表性的例子是 SDPO. A representative example is SDPO(Hübotter et al., 2026)，它对当前策略的推出进行采样，并将反馈条件自教师的 Logit 级别分布提炼回相同的策略。然而，反馈条件下的策略自蒸馏可能会表现出后期退化：, which samples rollouts from the current policy and distills the logit-level distribution of a feedback-conditioned self-teacher back into the same policy. However, feedback-conditioned on-policy self-distillation can exhibit late-stage degradation: concurrent work byKim et al. (2026)将其归因于认知语言表达的抑制，而我们的分析（第 1 节）将其追溯到正确样本的模糊性和自学信号的逐渐退化。attributes this to the suppression of epistemic verbalization, while our analysis (Section 1) traces it to ambiguity on correct samples and progressive degradation of the self-teacher signal.

总体而言，先前基于强化学习的后训练方法提供了奖励对齐，但依赖于粗略的序列级监督。基于蒸馏的方法提供了更密集的逻辑级别指导，并且自蒸馏消除了对外部教师的需要，但反馈条件的策略自蒸馏可能会在以后的训练中遭受样本相关的模糊性和信号质量下降的影响。为了解决这一差距，我们的工作研究了如何将奖励驱动和基于自我蒸馏的监督结合在基于样本路由的统一框架内，从而利用这两种训练后范式的优势。Overall, prior RL-based post-training methods provide reward alignment but rely on coarse sequence-level supervision. Distillation-based methods provide denser logit-level guidance, and self-distillation removes the need for an external teacher, but feedback-conditioned on-policy self-distillation may suffer from sample-dependent ambiguity and degraded signal quality in later training. To address this gap, our work studies how reward-driven and self-distillation-based supervision can be combined within a unified framework based on sample routing, thereby leveraging the strengths of both post-training paradigms.
