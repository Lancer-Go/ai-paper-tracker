量子信息理论 (QIT) 描述了量子信息处理的能力和基本限制，支撑着量子通信、计算和纠错。形式化其编码定理需要在统一的机器检查框架内连接有限块协议、解析不等式和渐近极限。然而，现有的开发缺乏可重用的操作层来定义代码、错误标准、可实现的速率和独立于其信息论特征的容量。在这项工作中，我们提出Quantum information theory (QIT) characterizes the capabilities and fundamental limits of quantum information processing, underpinning quantum communication, computation, and error correction. Formalizing its coding theorems requires connecting finite-block protocols, analytic inequalities, and asymptotic limits within a unified machine-checked framework. Existing developments, however, lack a reusable operational layer that defines codes, error criteria, achievable rates, and capacities independently of their information-theoretic characterizations. In this work, we present精益QITLean-QIT，一个用于有限维 QIT 的 Lean 4 库。它为量子态和通道、源代码和通道代码、有限块性能标准、假设检验、一次性量和渐近率构造提供可组合的、经过内核检查的接口。利用这个基础设施，我们形式化了舒马赫的量子源编码定理、Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland 经典容量定理和纠缠辅助经典容量定理。通过将操作定义与分析特征分离并公开可重用的可实现性、逆向和渐近组件，, a Lean 4 library for finite-dimensional QIT. It provides composable, kernel-checked interfaces for quantum states and channels, source and channel codes, finite-block performance criteria, hypothesis testing, one-shot quantities, and asymptotic rate constructions. Using this infrastructure, we formalize Schumacher’s quantum source-coding theorem, the Holevo–Schumacher–Westmoreland classical capacity theorem, and the entanglement-assisted classical capacity theorem. By separating operational definitions from analytic characterizations and exposing reusable achievability, converse, and asymptotic components,精益QITLean-QIT为形式化 QIT 提供机器可读的基础，并为新兴的人工智能辅助形式化、自动证明搜索以及量子信息和计算中的代理推理提供组合知识基础。provides a machine-readable foundation for formal QIT and a compositional knowledge substrate for emerging AI-assisted formalization, automated proof search, and agentic reasoning in quantum information and computation.

1  简介1  Introduction

量子信息理论（QIT）研究信息在量子系统中如何表示和处理，为量子通信、量子计算和量子纠错提供基础。量子香农理论 (QST) 是 QIT 的核心。它研究量子系统上经典信息和量子信息的压缩、传输和操纵，并描述其可实现的速率和基本可靠性限制。其主要结果确定了通过有限块协议和误差标准以及熵或信息论公式定义的操作量。代表性结果涵盖源压缩和噪声量子通道上的通信Quantum information theory (QIT) studies how information is represented and processed in quantum systems, providing the foundation for quantum communication, quantum computing, and quantum error correction. Quantum Shannon theory (QST) is central to QIT. It studies the compression, transmission, and manipulation of classical and quantum information over quantum systems, and characterizes their achievable rates and fundamental reliability limits. Its principal results identify operational quantities, defined through finite-block protocols and error criteria, with entropic or information-theoretic formulae. Representative results cover source compression and communication over noisy quantum channels[1, 2, 3]、量子、私有和资源辅助通信, quantum, private, and resource-assisted communication[4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9]，以及通道模拟, and channel simulation[10, 11]。一次性机制研究有限资源设置，而不假设渐近重复限制。他们依赖于平滑熵、假设检验和解耦. One-shot regimes study finite-resource settings without assuming an asymptotic repetition limit. They rely on smooth entropies, hypothesis testing, and decoupling[12, 13, 14, 15]。这使得 QST 成为量子信息理论中正式基础设施的严格测试用例：量子态、通道、信息量和测量必须与编码对象、可靠性标准、正向和反向边界以及渐近极限相结合。. This makes QST a demanding test case for formal infrastructure in quantum information theory: quantum states, channels, information quantities, and measurements must compose with coding objects, reliability criteria, direct and converse bounds, and asymptotic limits.

形式化 QIT 的困难在于分析和组合。编码定理是 QST 的核心，必须将类型化的操作模型与跨多个尺度的估计保持一致。其中包括有限块码、错误准则、一次性不等式、张量幂簿记、直接论证和逆论证以及限制构造。现代证明将算子不等式与有限块编码、一次性边界以及对称性或渐近约简相结合The difficulty of formalizing QIT is both analytic and compositional. Coding theorems, which lie at the core of QST, must align a typed operational model with estimates across several scales. These include finite-block codes, error criteria, one-shot inequalities, tensor-power bookkeeping, direct and converse arguments, and limiting constructions. Modern proofs combine operator inequalities with finite-block coding, one-shot bounds, and symmetry or asymptotic reductions[15, 16]。有效性可以取决于支持和正性条件、标准化约定、寄存器标识、错误标准、扩展实值以及量词和限制的顺序。成熟的非正式证明通常会压缩这些依赖性。最近在广义量子斯坦因引理的证明中发现了一个空白. Validity can depend on support and positivity conditions, normalization conventions, register identities, error criteria, extended-real values, and the order of quantifiers and limits. Mature informal proofs often compress these dependencies. A recently identified gap in proofs of the generalized quantum Stein’s lemma[17]表明被抑制的假设可能会产生实质性的数学后果。因此，形式化必须确定分析接口、运算接口和渐近接口的正确组合。shows that suppressed assumptions can have substantive mathematical consequences. Formalization must therefore establish that analytic, operational, and asymptotic interfaces compose correctly.

随着人工智能系统越来越多地进入数学和理论物理研究工作流程，这种需求变得更加紧迫。他们在 QIT 中最可信的近期作用是在定理检索、引理选择、证明完成、假设审计以及将非正式论证转化为正式义务方面提供结构化协助This need becomes more pressing as AI systems increasingly enter mathematical and theoretical-physics research workflows. Such tasks require a digital knowledge substrate with typed objects, explicit side conditions, searchable dependencies, and kernel-checkable candidate derivations. Papers with unformalized proofs alone do not provide this substrate. A reusable formal library can instead support both human researchers and proof AI agents.

4.2  Classical Capacity

The second line moves from source compression to noisy-channel coding. For unassisted classical communication over a quantum channel 𝒩{\cal N}, the HSW theorem identifies the operational classical capacity with the regularized Holevo expression [2, 3]. This theorem is a demanding formalization target because the final equality depends on several distinct proof layers: finite-block message codes, Holevo information, Hayashi–Nagaoka decoding estimates, packing and expurgation, converse bounds, superadditivity, and the limiting regularized expression.

Lean-QIT treats HSW as a completed public capacity endpoint. The classical-capacity layer includes the capacity definition, the relevant Holevo quantities, the Holevo dimension bound, the Hayashi–Nagaoka operator inequality, a derandomized packing lemma, and the final HSW theorem [42, 16]. The library first defines what it means for a real number RR to be an achievable classical communication rate. Only after this predicate is in place does the library define the operational capacity as the supremum of achievable rates. The Holevo expression is then introduced as a separate information quantity, and the HSW theorem proves that the two coincide.

The formal source statements used below follow the Khatri–Lami–Wilde chapter [16] tracked by the Lean proof, with the original direct-coding theorem due to Holevo–Schumacher–Westmoreland and the regularized-capacity formulation presented in modern QST references [2, 3, 42].

4.3  Entanglement-assisted classical capacity

The third line keeps the noisy-channel setting but changes the communication resource. With unlimited shared entanglement, classical communication over a quantum channel is governed by a single-letter channel mutual information rather than by a regularized Holevo expression [8, 9]. The presentation below follows the modern Khatri–Lami–Wilde organization of the entanglement-assisted classical capacity theorem [16]. We define the one-shot protocol and error criterion, define asymptotic achievable and strong-converse rates, and only then assemble the capacity identity. This ordering matters for formalization because the final equality is not the primitive object in Lean. It is assembled from typed operational definitions and separately named direct and converse inputs.

The source proof route itself combines several strands of QST. The one-shot viewpoint for entanglement-assisted communication originates in the one-shot EA literature of Datta–Hsieh [43]. The Khatri–Lami–Wilde direct branch then uses position-based coding, sequential decoding, and the quantum union bound to obtain a hypothesis-testing lower bound [43, 44, 45, 46, 16]. In this organization, position-based coding is traced to Anshu–Jain–Warsi, the EA one-shot lower bound to Qi–Wang–Wilde, and the quantum-union-bound proof route to Oskouei–Mancini–Wilde [44, 45, 46, 16]. The Petz–Rényi lower bound used in the asymptotic direct proof is obtained from this hypothesis-testing lower bound by comparison, then tensorized and sent to the α→1−\alpha\to 1^{-} limit [45, 16]. The converse branch uses the Matthews–Wehner finite-blocklength meta-converse through hypothesis-testing mutual information, and the strong-converse upgrade uses the Gupta–Wilde sandwiched-Rényi and completely bounded norm route [47, 48, 16]. Lean-QIT preserves this decomposition: the lower, weak-converse, and strong-converse proof obligations are exposed as named theorems before the final strong-converse endpoint is stated.

5  Conclusion

This work presents Lean-QIT, a reusable finite-dimensional QIT substrate whose compositional design is validated through operational quantum Shannon theory. Its central contribution is a formal interface connecting analytic information quantities to finite-block protocol witnesses and asymptotic coding limits. By keeping operational objects independent of the formulae that later characterize them, the library turns capacity statements into kernel-checkable theorems relating separately defined mathematical structures.

The Schumacher, HSW, and entanglement-assisted theorem lines provide complementary stress tests across source and channel coding, direct and converse reasoning, one-shot and asymptotic methods, and regularized and single-letter formulae. Together, they demonstrate that theorem-scale QST formalization can be organized around reusable interfaces rather than theorem-specific proof scripts. The same substrate also identifies a concrete path toward further coding theorems: quantum and private capacities require coherent-information, privacy, and regularization layers; channel simulation and quantum reverse-Shannon theorems require operational simulation criteria and explicit resource accounting; feedback-assisted tradeoffs and resource inequalities require rate-region interfaces that track multiple resources simultaneously. These extensions can reuse the present state and channel substrate, information quantities, finite-resource bounds, and asymptotic APIs while exposing only their genuinely new obligations.

The architecture also gives AI-assisted formalization a disciplined point of entry. Its near-term value lies in formal retrieval, proof completion, assumption auditing, and maintenance of theorem chains. The present work does not implement or evaluate an autonomous theorem-discovery system. Rather, Lean-QIT supplies the typed objects, explicit side conditions, searchable dependencies, and public theorem endpoints needed for humans and proof agents to inspect, compose, and extend formal QIT. In this sense, Lean-QIT provides a digital foundation for auditable, scalable, and machine-checkable reasoning in quantum information theory.
