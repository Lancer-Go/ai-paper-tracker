1 简介Introduction

奖励是自主机器人改进的关键组成部分。理想的奖励函数应该提供密集、明确的反馈，并且应该捕获理想行为的所有方面。例如，对摆桌子这个简单任务的监督应包括餐具的配置、不打破易碎盘子的注意程度、附近人的舒适度（例如避免将刀指向人的动作）以及执行速度等方面。无论是在引起人们的监督时，还是在奖励函数和下游行为中表示所有这些因素时，准确捕获所有这些轴都是一项重大挑战。此外，捕获这些轴但过于稀疏或密集但不准确的奖励函数在优化时可能会导致不需要的下游行为。在本文中，我们研究如何利用人类监督来学习奖励函数，并最终学习捕获人意图所有维度的机器人行为。Rewards are a critical component for autonomous robot improvement. An ideal reward function should provide dense, unambiguous feedback and should capture all aspects of desirable behavior. For example, supervision for the simple task of setting a table should incorporate the configuration of the cutlery, the degree of care taken to not break fragile plates, the comfort of nearby people (e.g. to avoid motions that point a knife towards a person), and the speed of execution, among other aspects. Accurately capturing all of these axes presents a major challenge, both when eliciting supervision from people and when representing all of these factors in a reward function and downstream behavior. Moreover, a reward function that captures these axes but is too sparse, or dense but inaccurate, can lead to unwanted downstream behaviors when optimized against. In this paper, we study how to leverage human supervision to learn reward functions and ultimately robot behavior that captures all dimensions of a person’s intent.

However, this reward signal places significant burden on the reinforcement learning algorithm, making it hard to scale to more challenging tasks and to incorporate real-world constraints on behavior beyond basic task completion. Other works learn shaped scalar rewards[27, 24, 7]但仍然关注任务进度指标，忽略任务执行方式的重要标准。最后，偏好学习but still focus on task progress metrics, ignoring important criteria on how a task was performed. In long-horizon tasks this can make preferences difficult to provide and the resulting supervision ambiguous.

为了保留这些判断轴，我们的主要见解是允许注释者在他们选择的任何与任务相关的轴上提供偏好标签。然后可以使用这种多维监督来学习针对这些维度的组合而优化的策略。我们通过要求注释者用自然语言指定相关的判断维度并为每个轴提供二元偏好来实例化这个想法。可以预先或在注释过程中定义轴。然后，我们学习一个多轴奖励函数，当以轴的自然语言描述为条件时，该函数会产生标量奖励分数。最后，我们训练一个及时的策略来优化奖励训练期间描述的轴组合。值得注意的是，该框架同时提高了提供明确监管的便利性和下游政策优化的监管密度。To preserve these axes of judgment, our key insight is to allow annotators to provide preference labels on any task-relevant axes of their choosing. Finally, we train a promptable policy to optimize the combination of axes described during reward training. Notably, this framework simultaneously improves both the ease of providing unambiguous supervision and the density of supervision for downstream policy optimization.

3 从自由形式的偏好中学习Learning from Freeform Preferences

我们的方法背后的关键思想，The key idea behind our method,自由形式偏好学习Freeform Preference Learning (飞行计划FPL），就是从自然语言和开放式反馈中学习，而不是传统的二元偏好。我们不是要求注释者提供两个轨迹之间的总体偏好，而是要求他们描述比较它们的轴，例如速度、安全性、平滑度或子任务完成情况。这会产生更精细、更明确的反馈。我们使用这些自由形式的偏好来学习一种以语言为条件的多维奖励函数，该函数沿着每个指定的轴对轨迹进行评分。然后，我们训练一个以多个偏好维度为条件的策略，以优化每个轴的行为。我们在下面描述了我们提出的算法的每个组成部分。), is to learn from natural language and open-ended feedback instead of traditional binary preferences. Rather than asking annotators for one overall preference between two trajectories, we ask them to describe the axes along which to compare them, such as speed, safety, smoothness, or subtask completion. This yields feedback that is more granular and less ambiguous. We use these freeform preferences to learn a language-conditioned, multi-dimensional reward function that scores trajectories along each specified axis. We then train a policy conditioned on multiple preference dimensions to optimize the behavior with respect to each axis. We describe each component of our proposed algorithm below.

6 局限性与结论Limitations & Conclusion

最后，我们介绍了To conclude, we introduced飞行计划FPL，一种根据自由人类偏好学习机器人策略的方法。通过使用自然语言轴收集偏好，, a method for learning robot policies from freeform human preferences. By collecting preferences with natural-language axes,飞行计划FPL与单一二元偏好相比，提供更密集、更明确的监督。在四个现实世界任务和两个模拟设置中，provides denser and less ambiguous supervision than single binary preferences. Across four real-world tasks and two simulation settings,飞行计划FPL优于基线。我们表明，保留人类反馈的多维结构可以实现行为的组合性和学习策略的测试时可操纵性，以及具有更好的信用分配的定性学习奖励模型。outperforms the baselines. We show that preserving the multi-dimensional structure of human feedback enables compositionality of behaviors and test-time steerability of the learned policy as well as qualitatively learn reward models with better credit assignment.

仍然存在一些限制。偏好学习需要收集人类偏好，这比完全无监督的方法更昂贵。奖励条件策略学习需要在测试时选择适当的奖励值，自动化这种选择是未来工作的重要方向。最后，当前的策略以一组固定的偏好轴为条件，随着 VLA 变得更加强大，扩展此方法来处理可变轴是一个有希望的方向。Several limitations remain. Preference learning requires collecting human preferences, which is more expensive than fully unsupervised approaches. Reward-conditioned policy learning requires selecting appropriate reward values at test time, automating this selection is an important direction for future work. Finally, the current policy is conditioned on a fixed set of preference axes, and extending this method to handle variable axes is a promising direction as VLAs become more capable.
