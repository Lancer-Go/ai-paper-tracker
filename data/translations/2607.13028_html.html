TerraZero：程序驾驶模拟TerraZero: Procedural Driving Simulation
大规模零演示自我游戏for Zero-Demonstration Self-Play at Scale

Zhouchonghao Wu Applied IntuitionMountain ViewCAUSA , Akshay Rangesh Applied IntuitionMountain ViewCAUSA , Weixin Li Applied IntuitionMountain ViewCAUSA , Wei-Jer Chang Applied IntuitionMountain ViewCAUSA , Zachary Lee Applied IntuitionMountain ViewCAUSA , Tim Wang Applied IntuitionMountain ViewCAUSA and Wei Zhan Applied IntuitionMountain ViewCAUSA
摘要。Abstract.

训练强大的自动驾驶代理需要一个模拟器，该模拟器必须足够快以进行大规模强化学习，足够真实以在现实世界地图结构中提供基础行为，并且足够多样化以涵盖记录数据很少包含的安全关键长尾。 我们呈现Training robust autonomous driving agents requires a simulator that is fast enough for reinforcement learning at scale, realistic enough to ground behavior in real-world map structure, and diverse enough to cover the safety-critical long tail that logged data rarely contains. We present特拉零TerraZero，一个满足这些目标的程序驾驶模拟器和自玩训练堆栈。 可配置的 C 引擎通过零复制路径在 CPU 上运行模拟并在 GPU 上运行策略推理，在单个服务器级 GPU 上维持每秒 130 万个代理步骤，远快于现有的对象级模拟器，同时保持保真度较轻的单代理系统省略：异构代理、多个动态模型和完整的流量规则实施。 TerraZero 仅将记录的数据视为现实世界地图几何的来源，用基于规则的随机道路使用者和信号控制器填充每个地图，并随机化每个情节的代理动态、奖励和大小，因此一张地图会产生一组有效的无限场景。 每个报告的策略都是通过在 GPU 上扩展的计算高效的自玩配方上单独进行强化学习来从头开始训练的，其中, a procedural driving simulator and self-play training stack that meets these goals. A configurable C engine runs simulation on the CPU and policy inference on the GPU over a zero-copy path, sustaining 1.3M agent-steps per second on a single server-grade GPU, far faster than existing object-level simulators, while keeping fidelity lighter single-agent systems omit: heterogeneous agents, multiple dynamics models, and full traffic-rule enforcement. TerraZero treats logged data only as a source of real-world map geometry, populating each map with randomized rule-based road users and signal controllers and randomizing agent dynamics, rewards, and sizes per episode, so one map yields an effectively unbounded set of scenarios. Every reported policy trains from scratch by reinforcement learning alone on a compute-efficient self-play recipe scaled across GPUs, with零zero人类示范，没有模仿，没有记录轨迹，也没有推理时的后备规划器，由此产生的政策是通用的human demonstrations, no imitation, no logged trajectories, and no fallback planner at inference, and the resulting policies generalize零射击zero-shot跨城市和数据集，包括在没有明确监督的情况下突发的左侧交通驾驶。 据我们所知，作为一项自我策略，TerraZero 是第一个超越 InterPlan 长尾基准的完全学习策略，领先于更大的学习规划者；在常规驾驶 val14 中，它被列为最佳方法之一，也是最安全的，发布了最佳碰撞和碰撞时间分数。 在 Waymo Open Sim Agents 现实主义中，相同的配方优于其他免演示方法，并且与最强的参考锚定自我对弈方法具有竞争力。 一个堆栈具有两种角色：驱动汽车和卡车动态的策略，以及共同控制车辆、行人和骑自行车者的模拟代理，具有广泛的可配置性。across cities and datasets, including emergent left-hand-traffic driving without explicit supervision. As an ego policy, TerraZero is, to our knowledge, the first fully learned policy to top the InterPlan long-tail benchmark, ahead of larger learned planners; on routine-driving val14 it ranks among the best approaches and is the safest, posting the best collision and time-to-collision scores. On Waymo Open Sim Agents realism the same recipe outperforms other demonstration-free methods and is competitive with the strongest reference-anchored self-play method. One stack serves both roles: driving policies across dynamics for cars and trucks, and sim agents that jointly control vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists, with broad configurability.

1. 简介Introduction

现实世界的驾驶数据以日常驾驶为主。 大多数记录的里程包括稳态车道跟踪、平缓弯道和例行停车，这些场景是自主智能体从练习中获益最少的。 在很大程度上决定部署准备情况的安全关键情况的长尾情况（密集的高速公路交汇处、激进的切入、险些发生的行人互动、迎面驶来的车辆未受保护的左转）很少见，因此在现实世界的驾驶数据集中覆盖成本高昂。 对记录轨迹的模仿学习很难教授日志几乎不包含的行为，并且以舰队规模收集更多数据只能缓慢地缩小这种分布差距。 仿真提供了一条补充路径：模拟器可以系统地制造最重要的困难场景Real-world driving data is dominated by routine driving. Most logged miles consist of steady-state lane following, gentle curves, and routine stops, the scenarios an autonomous agent has least to gain from practicing. The long tail of safety-critical situations that largely determine deployment readiness (dense highway merges, aggressive cut-ins, near-miss pedestrian interactions, unprotected left turns through oncoming traffic) is rare, and therefore costly to cover, in real-world driving datasets. Imitation learning on logged trajectories struggles to teach behaviors that the logs barely contain, and collecting more data at fleet scale closes this distributional gap only slowly. Simulation offers a complementary path: a simulator can systematically manufacture the hard scenarios that matter most(dosovitskiy2017carla; vinitsky2022nocturne; kazemkhani2025gpudrive).

现有的模拟方法在吞吐量与保真度的权衡中占据了不同的位置，但没有一个能够完全解决这个问题。 为提高速度而构建的对象级模拟器，例如 PufferDriveExisting simulation approaches occupy different points in a throughput–fidelity trade-off, and none fully resolves it. Object-level simulators built for speed, such as PufferDrive(pufferdrive2025github; cornelisse2025reliable)，达到高吞吐量，但仅支持具有固定动态且无流量信号的单一代理类型。 SMARTS 等功能丰富的环境, reach high throughput but support only a single agent type with fixed dynamics and no traffic signals. Feature-rich environments like SMARTS(zhou2021smarts)和卡拉and CARLA(dosovitskiy2017carla)提供了实际评估所需的复杂性，但对于从头开始的大规模强化学习 (RL) 训练而言，速度太慢了几个数量级，其中数十亿个环境步骤是常规的。 单独的工作线构建学习流量模型（SimNetoffer the complexity needed for realistic evaluation but are orders of magnitude too slow for from-scratch reinforcement learning (RL) training at scale, where billions of environment steps are routine. A separate line of work builds learned traffic models (SimNet(bergamini2021simnet), 位, BITS(xu2023bits), 特拉杰格利什, Trajeglish(philion2024trajeglish)）为周围的智能体生成真实的轨迹，但产生的流量很大程度上是非反应性的，限制了它们对闭环策略改进的效用。 像 Gigaflow 这样的自我对弈方法) that generate realistic trajectories for surrounding agents but produce largely non-reactive traffic, limiting their utility for closed-loop policy improvement. Self-play approaches like Gigaflow(cusumano-towner2025gigaflow)研究表明，反应式多智能体训练可以产生稳健的驾驶策略，但它们在每个智能体类共享的单一动态模型下对一小部分合成地图进行训练，并且它们仅依赖随机化，而不是构建的场景内容来接触长尾。show that reactive multi-agent training can yield robust driving policies, but they train on a small family of synthetic maps under a single dynamics model shared by every agent class, and they rely on randomization alone, rather than constructed scenario content, for exposure to the long tail.

CPU/GPU分工。CPU/GPU division of labor.

低开销数据路径。Low-overhead data path.

两者之间的接口完全是零复制的：C 引擎将模拟状态直接写入训练循环作为 PyTorch 张量读回的缓冲区中，从而消除了 CPU 绑定模拟器中占主导地位的每步序列化和分配，而主机到设备的传输与内核启动重叠。 TerraZero 进一步根据实际存在的受控代理调整其缓冲区大小，而不是填充到固定计数，以 16 位精度发出观察结果，GPU 逐位重新解释以将观察带宽减半，并将每个等级的工作线程固定到其 GPU NUMA 节点本地的 CPU 核心。The interface between the two is fully zero-copy: the C engine writes simulation state directly into the buffers that the training loop reads back as PyTorch tensors, eliminating the per-step serialization and allocation that dominate CPU-bound simulators, while host-to-device transfers overlap with kernel launches. TerraZero further sizes its buffers to the controlled agents actually present rather than padding to a fixed count, emits observations in 16-bit precision that the GPU reinterprets bit-for-bit to halve observation bandwidth, and pins each rank’s workers to the CPU cores local to its GPU’s NUMA node.

4. 程序场景生成Procedural Scenario Generation

一张真实世界地图是数千个不同训练场景的种子。 TerraZero 按程序生成记录数据的超集，而不是受其限制：记录的地图几何形状和轨迹锚定道路的运行位置和智能体可能开始的位置，并且一堆可组合的随机化机制将每个种子扩展为策略实际训练的更广泛的分布。A single real-world map is the seed for thousands of distinct training scenarios. TerraZero procedurally generates a superset of the logged data rather than being bounded by it: recorded map geometry and trajectories anchor where roads run and where agents may begin, and a stack of composable randomization mechanisms expands each seed into the far broader distribution the policy actually trains on.

7. 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 TerraZero，这是一款高性能闭环驾驶模拟器，专为大规模自玩强化学习而设计。 TerraZero 解决了自动驾驶研究中持续存在的紧张局势：对于 RL 规模的训练来说足够快的模拟器历来缺乏有意义的政策转移所需的场景保真度，而功能丰富的环境仍然太慢了几个数量级。 通过结合可配置的 C 仿真引擎、程序场景生成器和计算高效的自玩配方，TerraZero 实现了对象级、支持自玩的驾驶模拟器的最高报告吞吐量，同时支持异构交通（车辆、行人、骑自行车者）、多个动态模型、交通规则执行以及来自 Waymo、nuPlan 和 CARLA 的多源地图数据。We have presented TerraZero, a high-performance closed-loop driving simulator designed for self-play reinforcement learning at scale. TerraZero addresses a persistent tension in autonomous driving research: the simulators fast enough for RL-scale training have historically lacked the scenario fidelity needed for meaningful policy transfer, while feature-rich environments remain orders of magnitude too slow.
