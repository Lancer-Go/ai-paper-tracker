从简单的奖励中揭示复杂的集体行为Unveiling Complex Collective Behaviors from Simple Rewards

摘要Abstract

多智能体强化学习（MARL）对于机器人群具有巨大的潜力，但神经策略的黑盒性质使策略分析变得复杂，限制了多机器人的应用。此外，在没有明确的聚集激励的情况下，简单的奖励也会令人惊讶地出现复杂的群体行为。揭示这种现象背后的机制至关重要，但简单奖励与集体行为之间的脱节加剧了可解释性挑战。本文旨在揭示这一过程中隐藏的机制。我们提出一个两阶段的 EEC（Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) holds great potential for robot swarms, but the black-box nature of neural policies complicates strategic analysis, limiting multi-robot applications. Furthermore, complex swarm behaviors can surprisingly emerge from simple rewards without explicit aggregation incentives. Unveiling the mechanisms behind this emergence is critical, but the disconnection between simple rewards and collective behaviors exacerbates interpretability challenges. This paper aims to reveal the hidden mechanisms in this process. We propose a two-stage EEC (自我观察Ego-observation  →\rightarrow  自我行为Ego-behavior  →\rightarrow  集体行为Collective-behavior）解释框架。其中包括一种称为智能体响应图（ARM）的新颖分析工具，它揭示了智能体跨空间的决策模式并识别聚集和回避区域。 ARM 透露，机器人隐式学习环境的几何场，并利用这些结构作为协调运动的所需目标。我们通过两个不同的任务验证了这一发现：协作的多机器人形状组装和竞争性的捕食者-猎物追击-逃避。 1）在协作任务中，ARM将未占用的目标内部识别为机器人导航的期望目的地。当中心被占据时，该目标区域会自动向边界移动，展示机器人自主探索未占据区域的能力。 2）在竞争性任务中，ARM 令人惊讶地将捕食者的 Voronoi 图的边界识别为猎物代理的收敛目的地。这两项任务共同证明了 ARM 发现机器人群中 MARL 策略背后隐藏几何结构的能力。我们进行定量分析来验证 ARM 的预测准确性。本文提出的见解和工具可能为弥合将黑盒 MARL 应用于多机器人系统的可解释性差距提供新的视角。) explanatory framework. This includes a novel analytical tool called the Agent Response Map (ARM), which reveals agents’ decision-making patterns across space and identifies regions of aggregation and avoidance. ARM reveals that the robots implicitly learn the geometric fields of the environment and utilize these structures as desired targets for coordinated movement. We validate this finding across two distinct tasks: a cooperative multi-robot shape assembly and a competitive predator-prey pursuit-evasion. 1) In the cooperative task, ARM identifies the unoccupied target interior as the desired destination for robot navigation. As the center becomes occupied, this target region automatically shifts toward the boundary, demonstrating the robots’ capacity to autonomously explore unoccupied areas. 2) In the competitive task, ARM surprisingly identifies the boundary of the predators’ Voronoi diagram as the convergence destination for prey agents. Together, these two tasks demonstrate the capability of ARM to discover the hidden geometric structures underlying MARL policies in robot swarms. We perform quantitative analysis to validate the predictive accuracy of ARM. The insights and tools presented in this paper may provide a new perspective on bridging the interpretability gap in applying black-box MARL to multi-robot systems.

I 简介Introduction

集体行为（例如八哥群、鱼群和羊群）是自然界中复杂而迷人的现象Collective behaviors (e.g., starling flocks, fish schools, and sheep herds) are complex and fascinating phenomenon in nature[21]，引起各领域的广泛关注。普遍的观点是，生存压力在驱使动物形成群体方面发挥着核心作用，因为集体行为可以增加个体在面对捕食或环境风险时的生存机会, attracting widespread attention across various fields. A prevailing view is that survival pressure plays a central role in driving animals to form groups, as collective behavior can increase individual survival chances in the face of predation or environmental risks[2]。然而严格量化和解释此类现象仍然具有挑战性。各种 人们提出了传统模型来解释这些集体行为，这些模型是根据手工设计的规则构建的. Yet rigorously quantifying and explaining such phenomena remains challenging. Various traditional models have been proposed to explain these collective behaviors, constructed from hand-designed rules[25, 19, 6]。虽然这些模型可以复制某些新兴模式，但它们简化的相互作用机制无法捕捉真实生物系统的复杂性。. While these models can replicate certain emergent patterns, their simplified interaction mechanisms fall short of capturing the complexity of real biological systems.

最近，强化学习（RL）已被用来模拟生物有机体的适应性策略Recently, reinforcement learning (RL) has been used to simulate adaptive strategies in biological organisms[8, 17]，通过多智能体强化学习 (MARL)，可以模拟复杂的智能体交互并通过奖励优化集体行为。例如，, with multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) enabling simulations of complex agent interactions and optimizing collective behavior via rewards. For example,[8]惩罚“失去邻居”的代理人，同时penalizes agents that “lose neighbors”, while[17]通过减少危险区域来鼓励猎物聚集。然而，这些方法通常依赖于明确的手工奖励，因此对于自然生物体为何遵循此类设计规则提供的基本见解有限。encourages prey to gather by reducing dangerous areas. However, these methods often rely on explicit hand-crafted rewards and therefore provide limited fundamental insights into why natural organisms would follow such designed rules.

相比之下，最近的一项研究In contrast, a recent study[13]采用唯一的生存压力奖励函数，并删除捕食者-猎物共同进化框架内的其他手工设计的奖励项。它展示了在简单激励下集体行为的自然出现。这自然提出了一个问题：如此简单的奖励如何产生复杂的集体行为？adopts a sole survival-pressure reward function and removes other hand-crafted reward terms within a predator-prey coevolution framework. 为了揭示自我行为如何扩展到集体行为，我们提出了 ARM，一个反事实的空间策略运算符。 ARM 探索反事实空间干预下的政策，因此可以解释局部决策如何组织成群体模式。这是通过引入虚拟代理来实现的，虚拟代理对真实代理来说是不可见的，因此不会影响他们的行为。虚拟代理充当探针，在当前时间步查询猎物在不同空间位置的响应，这可以避免代理之间相互干扰造成的非平稳性问题。通过这种方式，我们可以解释关键特征如何塑造主体的运动，从而导致集体行为。To reveal how ego-behavior scales to collective behavior, we propose ARM, a counterfactual spatial policy operator. ARM probes policy under counterfactual spatial intervention and thus, can explain how local decisions organize into swarm patterns. This is achieved by introducing a virtual agent which is invisible to real agents and therefore does not influence their behavior. The virtual agent acts as a probe to query the prey’s response at different spatial locations at the current timestep, which can avoid the non-stationarity issue caused by mutual interference among agents. In this way, we can explain how key features shape agents movement, leading to collective behaviors.

密闭环境中的集体行为分析：Analysis of Collective Behavior in Confined Environment:现实世界的机器人部署主要在有限的环境中运行。因此，验证智能体的策略是否能够适应边界约束，同时保持内聚性是实现模拟到真实迁移的关键一步。Real-world robotic deployments mainly operate within confined environments. Therefore, verifying that the agent’s policy can adapt to boundary constraints while maintaining cohesion is a critical step towards sim-to-real transfer.

然而，分析有限区域内的策略具有挑战性，因为猎物的行为是由耦合动力学驱动的：捕食者的排斥力和墙壁的碰撞惩罚。很难区分转弯是由捕食者的方向还是墙壁的接近引起的。为了解耦这些因素并严格验证边界回避机制，我们设计了一个受控实验：我们不使用动态捕食者，而是将猎物对捕食者相对位置的观察覆盖为固定值However, analyzing the policy in confined area is challenging because the prey’s behavior is driven by coupled dynamics: the repulsive force from the predator and the collision penalties from the walls. It is difficult to distinguish whether a turn is caused by the predator’s direction or the wall’s proximity. To decouple these factors and strictly verify the boundary-avoidance mechanism, we devise a controlled experiment: Instead of a dynamic predator, we override the prey’s observation of the predator’s relative position to a fixed value of(0,0)(0,0)，这意味着每个猎物都会感知到最近的捕食者恰好位于其当前位置。这消除了对手的所有方向线索，并使我们能够将最终的空间结构仅归因于环境边界。, which means each prey perceives the nearest predator to be located exactly at its own current position. This removes all directional cues from the adversary, and allows us to attribute resulting spatial structure solely to the environmental boundaries.

在这种受控设置下，我们观察到代理不会撞到墙壁，也不会随机移动。相反，它们会自发地产生稳定的旋转轨迹，如图所示Under this controlled setting, we observe that the agents do not crash into walls, nor do they move randomly. Instead, they spontaneously generate a stable swirling trajectory, shown inFig. 7（一）。 ARM 分析表明，随着智能体接近边界，临界值会减小。定量分析如图所示(a). The ARM analysis reveals that the critic value diminishes as the agent approaches the boundaries. The quantitative analysis shown inFig. 7(b) 进一步证明了这一发现：智能体的实际批评值高于投射到最近边界的假设智能体的批评值。这表明观察到的漩涡代表了在有限区域内避开墙壁所需的几何策略。(b) further demonstrates this finding: The agent’s actual critic value is higher than that of a hypothetical agent projected onto the nearest boundary. This indicates that the observed swirling represents a desired geometric strategy in confined area for wall avoidance.

七VII结论Conclusion

本文提出ARM来探索通过MARL训练的集体行为的隐藏机制。 ARM 揭示了智能体隐式学习几何结构并将其用作协调运动的所需目标。这些发现为 MARL 中的集体行为提供了新的见解。This paper proposed ARM to explore the hidden mechanisms of collective behavior trained through MARL. ARM reveals agents implicitly learn geometric structure and use it as desired targets for coordinated motion. These findings offer new insights into collective behavior in MARL.
