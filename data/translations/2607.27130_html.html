摘要Abstract

本体匹配（OM）传统上被表述为等价发现或包含匹配。现有的OM系统仅识别一种类型的语义对应，并且不能同时发现等价和包含映射。在本文中，我们介绍Ontology matching (OM) has traditionally been formulated as either equivalence discovery or subsumption matching. The existing OM systems identify only one type of semantic correspondence and cannot simultaneously discover equivalence and subsumption mappings. In this paper, we introduce混合本体匹配（HOM）Hybrid Ontology Matching (HOM)，一种新的OM任务，统一了等价性和包含发现，并相应提出了基于大语言模型（LLM）的多智能体OM框架, a new OM task that unifies equivalence and subsumption discovery, and accordingly propose a Large Language Model (LLM)-based multi-agent OM framework代理地图AgentMap这是通过一系列相互依赖的语义决策来实现的。 给定源本体中的概念，AgentMap 集成语义检索、层次搜索和协作多智能体 LLM 推理，以逐步探索目标本体，识别等效概念（如果存在）或最细粒度的包含者。 我们进一步扩展了 HOM 基准的四个 OM 数据集，并在混合、仅等价和仅包含设置下评估 AgentMap。实验结果表明，AgentMap 在混合设置上实现了良好的性能，同时在仅等价和仅包含设置上分别优于等价匹配和包含匹配基线。that is implemented by a series of interdependent semantic decisions. Given a concept in the source ontology, AgentMap integrates semantic retrieval, hierarchical search, and collaborative multi-agent LLM reasoning to progressively explore the target ontology, identifying either the equivalent concept, if one exists, or the most fine-grained subsumer. We further extend four OM datasets for a HOM benchmark and evaluate AgentMap under hybrid, equivalence-only, and subsumption-only settings. Experimental results show that AgentMap achieves promising performance on the hybrid setting, and at the same time outperforms equivalence matching and subsumption matching baselines on the equivalence-only and subsumption-only settings, respectively.

1 简介Introduction

本体已广泛用于许多领域的知识表示，例如用于医疗保健的 SNOMED CT 和用于食品和农业的 FoodOnOntologies have been widely used for knowledge representation in many domains, such as SNOMED CT for healthcare and FoodOn for food and agriculture[2, 4, 23]。 对于知识集成、重用和解释，本体匹配（OM），即发现跨本体的具有特定关系（如等价和包含）的概念对，已被广泛研究. For knowledge integration, reuse and interpretation, ontology matching (OM), which is to discover pairs of concepts across ontologies with a specific relationship like equivalence and subsumption, has been widely investigated[20]。 然而，不同的本体可能使用不同的术语并采用不同粒度级别的概念化，因此准确的OM具有挑战性，需要超越词汇和结构匹配的语义解释。. However, different ontologies may use different terminologies and adopt conceptualisations with different levels of granularity, and thus accurate OM is challenging, requiring semantic interpretation beyond lexical and structural matching.

OM 系统是从传统的词汇匹配和基于规则的方法（例如 LogMap）发展而来的OM systems have evolved from traditional lexical matching and rule-based approaches, such as LogMap[14]和AgreementMakerLight (AML)and AgreementMakerLight (AML)[6]，到基于编码器的预训练语言模型 (PLM) 方法，例如 BERTMap, to encoder-based pre-trained language model (PLM)-based approaches, such as BERTMap[8]，以及最近基于 LLM 的生成框架，例如 GenOM, and more recently to generative LLM-based frameworks, such as GenOM[22]。目前大多数 OM 系统都专注于等价匹配，尽管也有一些 OM 系统，例如 BERTSub. Most of the current OM systems focus on equivalence matching, although there are also some OM systems like BERTSub[3]旨在发现具有包含关系的概念对。 更重要的是，据我们所知，缺乏同时发现等价和包含映射的 OM 系统。that aim to discover concept pairs with subsumption relationships. More importantly, to the best of our knowledge, there is a shortage of OM systems that simultaneously discover equivalence and subsumption mappings.

为了缩小这一差距，我们引入To close this gap, we introduce混合本体匹配（HOM）Hybrid Ontology Matching (HOM)，一个新的 OM 任务，需要共同发现每个源概念的等价性和包含对应关系（在问题陈述中形式化）。, a new OM task that requires jointly discovering equivalence and subsumption correspondences for each source concept (formalised in the Problem Statement).

因此，我们提出了一个名为 AgentMap 的多智能体 OM 框架，它将 HOM 分解为一系列相互依赖的语义推理和决策步骤。该设计的灵感来自于 LLM 代理范式，将推理与行动交织在一起Accordingly, we propose a multi-agent OM framework named AgentMap, which decomposes HOM into a series of interdependent semantic reasoning and decision-making steps. This design is inspired by LLM agent paradigms that interleave reasoning with action[27]，使用后续验证步骤的反馈完善初始决策, refine an initial decision using feedback from subsequent verification steps[17]，并通过切换在专门代理之间进行路由控制, and route control between specialised agents via handoffs[19]。 AgentMap 将基于嵌入的候选检索、层次结构感知的 LLM 推理以及具有冲突解决的词汇匹配相结合，将推理分解为用于等价筛选、等价验证和包含发现的专用代理。. AgentMap combines embedding-based candidate retrieval, hierarchy-aware LLM reasoning, and lexical matching with conflict resolution, decomposing reasoning into specialised agents for equivalence screening, equivalence verification, and subsumption discovery.

We further extend four existing OM datasets involving ontologies of the food and biomedicine domains for a benchmark of the new HOM task. The main contributions of this work can be summarised as follows:

  • •

    We introduce the new task of Hybrid Ontology Matching (HOM), which requires simultaneously considering equivalence and subsumption mappings. It better reflects the requirements of real-world knowledge integration and reuse than conventional OM tasks.

  • •

    我们开发了 AgentMap 框架，它将复杂的 HOM 任务分解为一组由代理工作流程编排的子任务，有效地将已建立的本体匹配技术与基于 LLM 的推理相结合。We develop the framework AgentMap that decomposes the complex HOM task into a set of sub-tasks orchestrated by an agentic workflow, effectively integrating established ontology matching techniques with LLM-based reasoning.

  • •

    We evaluate AgentMap on four HOM datasets, demonstrating consistent improvements over alternative LLM-based strategies.我们进一步将 AgentMap 与传统的仅等价和仅包含设置下的经典和最新 OM 系统进行比较，在这些设置中它也实现了卓越的性能。We evaluate AgentMap on four HOM datasets, demonstrating consistent improvements over alternative LLM-based strategies. We further compare AgentMap with both classic and recent OM systems under the conventional equivalence-only and subsumption-only settings, where it also achieves superior performance.

5.1 等价本体匹配Equivalence Ontology Matching

等价 OM 已经从基于规则的系统发展到神经表示学习，以及最近的基于 LLM 的推理。早期的系统，例如 LogMapEquivalence OM has evolved from rule-based systems to neural representation learning and, more recently, LLM-based reasoning. 随着深度学习的发展，OM越来越依赖于学习到的语义表示With the development of deep learning, OM has increasingly relied on learned semantic representations[8]。早期的神经方法采用卷积神经网络 (CNN) 从文本描述中编码本体概念. 尽管这些方法在架构上有很大不同，但它们都是为等效 OM 设计的，其目标是跨异构本体识别引用相同现实世界实体的概念。Although these methods differ substantially in architecture, they are all designed for equivalence OM, where the objective is to identify concepts referring to the same real-world entity across heterogeneous ontologies.

5.2 包含本体匹配Subsumption Ontology Matching

与等价 OM 相比，包含 OM 受到的关注要少得多。现有的研究通常将任务表述为候选排名问题Compared with equivalence OM, subsumption OM has received considerably less attention. Existing studies commonly formulate the task as a candidate ranking problem[10]，其中目标是从预定义的候选列表中识别正确的子使用者。, where the objective is to identify the correct subsumer from a predefined candidate list.

BERTSub [3]是专门为归并 OM 设计的少数方法之一。它使用上下文语言模型在现有基准设置下对候选子消费者进行排名。然而，BERTSub 假设基准支持的包含者已经包含在候选列表中，因此评估候选者排名，而不是本体范围内的包含发现。is one of the few approaches specifically designed for subsumption OM. It uses contextual language models to rank candidate subsumers under existing benchmark settings. However, BERTSub assumes that the benchmark-supported subsumer is already contained in the candidate list and therefore evaluates candidate ranking rather than ontology-wide subsumption discovery.

OnT等本体表示学习方法Ontology representation learning methods such as OnT[26]和HiTand HiT[11]也被用作相关排名设置中的概念编码器。然而，它们并不是专门为包容 OM 而设计的。在这项工作中，我们仅将它们作为基于编码器的基线，以检查本体感知表示在我们的评估协议下的执行情况。have also been used as concept encoders in related ranking settings. However, they are not designed specifically for subsumption OM. In this work, we include them only as encoder-based baselines to examine how ontology-aware representations perform under our evaluation protocol.

HOM 需要搜索目标本体来共同识别目标概念并确定关系是否存在HOM instead requires searching the target ontology to jointly identify the target concept and determine whether the relation is等价性equivalence or 包容subsumption——据我们所知，现有的 OM 框架不支持这种联合表述。— a joint formulation that, to the best of our knowledge, no existing OM framework supports.

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了混合本体匹配（HOM），将等价性和包含发现重新定义为联合评估的单个任务，反映了这样一个事实：在实践中，源概念是否具有精确匹配或仅具有更广泛的匹配是事先未知的。 AgentMap 通过将联合决策分解为分阶段的、相互依赖的代理推理步骤而不是单个 LLM 判断来解决 HOM。我们的消融和跨主干实验表明，这种分阶段分解，而不是候选覆盖范围或主干强度，推动了 AgentMap 的收益，这表明迭代、结构感知推理是层次结构上 LLM 代理的一般原则。 AgentMap 还在包容匹配方面设定了新的技术水平，尽管其绝对准确度在四个基准测试中的三个上仍然低于 0.5，这表明该子任务仍然比等价匹配困难得多。未来的工作包括通过更有针对性的分层搜索来缩小这一差距，并将 HOM 扩展到更丰富的语义关系。We introduced Hybrid Ontology Matching (HOM), reframing equivalence and subsumption discovery as a single task evaluated jointly, reflecting the fact that, in practice, whether a source concept has an exact match or only a broader one is not known in advance. AgentMap addresses HOM by decomposing this joint decision into staged, interdependent agent reasoning steps rather than a single LLM judgment. Our ablation and cross-backbone experiments show that this staged decomposition, rather than candidate coverage or backbone strength, drives AgentMap’s gains, suggesting iterative, structure-aware reasoning as a general principle for LLM agents over hierarchical structures. AgentMap also sets a new state of the art on subsumption matching, though its absolute accuracy remains below 0.5 on three of four benchmarks, showing this sub-task is still considerably harder than equivalence matching. Future work includes closing this gap through more targeted hierarchical search and extending HOM to richer semantic relations.
