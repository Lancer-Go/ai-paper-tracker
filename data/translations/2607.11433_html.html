摘要Abstract

全模态寻证 QA 要求代理回答其证据稀疏分布在视频、音频、图像、网页和计算结果中的问题。现有的代理多模态系统通常会在草稿本、工具轨迹或自由形式的历史记录中留下证据，使得很难追踪哪些内容已经落地、哪些内容仍然缺失，以及何时证据足以回答问题。 我们提出了 Omni-Decision，这是一种无需训练的证据状态系统，可将全模式 QA 转变为查询范围的证据闭合过程。对于每个查询，Omni-Decision 都会维护一个结构化证据状态，其中包含已确认的证据、未解决的冲突、事实和计算依赖性以及开放的证据需求。共享状态视图条件规划、证据获取、验证、修复和最终确定。来自媒体、网络、计算和验证模块的异构观察通过确定性状态更新进行标准化、判断和提交。这种设计能够实现有针对性的证据获取，保留稀疏的跨模式线索，并提供对修复和停止的可检查控制。 Omni-Decision 在 OmniGAIA 上实现了 45.6% 的准确率，在 WorldSense 上实现了 58.3% 的准确率，分别比基准提高了 27.3 个百分点和 30.2 个百分点。无状态消融和轨迹审计进一步支持显式证据状态控制在多步骤全模式证据寻求中的作用。Omni-modal evidence-seeking QA requires agents to answer questions whose evidence is sparsely distributed across videos, audio, images, web pages, and computation results. Existing agentic multimodal systems often leave evidence in scratchpads, tool trajectories, or free-form histories, making it difficult to track what has been grounded, what remains missing, and when the evidence is sufficient to answer. We propose Omni-Decision, a training-free evidence-state system that turns omni-modal QA into a query-scoped evidence-closure process. For each query, Omni-Decision maintains a structured evidence state containing confirmed evidence, unresolved conflicts, fact and computation dependencies, and open evidence needs. A shared state view conditions planning, evidence acquisition, validation, repair, and finalization. Heterogeneous observations from media, web, computation, and verification modules are normalized, judged, and committed through deterministic state updates. This design enables targeted evidence acquisition, preserves sparse cross-modal cues, and provides inspectable control over repair and stopping. Omni-Decision achieves 45.6% accuracy on OmniGAIA and 58.3% on WorldSense, improving over the baselines by +27.3 and +30.2 percentage points, respectively. No-state ablations and trajectory audits further support the role of explicit evidence-state control in multi-step omni-modal evidence seeking.

1 简介Introduction

全模态问答正在超越封闭形式的感知理解，转向寻求证据的 QAOmni-modal question answering is moving beyond closed-form perceptual understanding toward evidence-seeking QA(Fu et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026)。这种设置更接近于实际的代理问题解决：用户提出基于异构媒体的问题，答案可能需要系统识别相关的视觉或听觉证据，从外部源完成缺失的属性，检查模态之间的一致性，有时在响应之前计算派生值. This setting is closer to practical agentic problem solving: a user asks a question grounded in heterogeneous media, and the answer may require the system to identify relevant visual or acoustic evidence, complete missing attributes from external sources, check consistency across modalities, and sometimes compute a derived value before responding(Nakano et al., 2022; Schick et al., 2023; Yu et al., 2026).

由于三个原因，此设置很困难。首先，证据稀疏且分布广泛This setting is difficult for three reasons. First, evidence is sparse and distributed(Zhong et al., 2022; Ranasinghe et al., 2025; Ren et al., 2025; Tang et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2026)。相关线索可以出现在短视频片段、字幕片段、音频事件、图像区域、网页或计算结果中。其次，证据链是部分可观察的。系统通常事先不知道需要哪个实体属性、关系或中间值，直到早期证据得到证实。第三，回答准备本身就是一个控制问题。系统不仅必须决定下一步要获取什么，还要决定候选答案是否受支持、冲突是否应该触发修复以及剩余的差距是否在可用操作下无法填补. A relevant clue may appear in a short video segment, a subtitle span, an audio event, an image region, a web page, or a computation result. Second, the evidence chain is partially observable. The system usually does not know in advance which entity attribute, relation, or intermediate value will be needed until earlier evidence has been grounded. Third, answer readiness is itself a control problem. A system must decide not only what to acquire next, but also whether a candidate answer is supported, whether a conflict should trigger repair, and whether the remaining gap is unfillable under the available actions(Shinn et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023; Han et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2026a; Zhang et al., 2026).

这些属性使全模式寻证 QA 成为一个状态维护问题，而不仅仅是一个上下文扩展问题These properties make omni-modal evidence-seeking QA a state-maintenance problem rather than merely a context-scaling problem(Wu et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2025; He et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2026b)。更长的上下文窗口或更多的帧可以暴露更多的原始观察结果，但它本身并不能指定哪个实体已被接地，哪个属性槽保持开放，哪个关系已被验证，或者证据链是否已准备好最终确定。. A longer context window or a larger number of frames can expose more raw observations, but it does not by itself specify which entity has been grounded, which attribute slot remains open, which relation has been verified, or whether the evidence chain is ready for finalization.

这种观点也符合人类通常解决多步骤证据任务的方式：我们不是简单地重放每个观察结果，而是保持与任务相关的工作状态，包括已知的内容、缺失的内容以及接下来应该检查的内容This view also aligns with how humans often solve multi-step evidence tasks: we do not simply replay every observation, but maintain a task-relevant working state of what is known, what is missing, and what should be checked next(Miller and Cohen, 2001; Baddeley, 2020)。我们仅将这种认知控制类比用作动机，而不是生物学主张。 Omni-Decision 遵循相同的工程原理，通过使查询范围的证据陈述通过证据获取、验证、修复、最终确定和不充分停止来读取的对象。. We use this cognitive-control analogy only as motivation, not as a biological claim. Omni-Decision follows the same engineering principle by making the query-scoped evidence state the object read by evidence acquisition, verification, repair, finalization, and insufficient stopping.

现有的基准以不同的形式实例化了这种更广泛的设置Existing benchmarks instantiate this broader setting in different forms(Fu et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026; Yu et al., 2026)。 OmniGAIA 强调开放世界、跨媒体、网络事实、浏览和计算的工具增强证据收集。 WorldSense强调多选读出格式下的独立音视频证据集成。它们的输出格式不同，但都要求系统围绕查询组织证据，而不是执行单个前向读出。. OmniGAIA stresses open-world, tool-augmented evidence collection across media, web facts, browsing, and computation. WorldSense stresses self-contained audio-video evidence integration under a multiple-choice read-out format. Their output formats differ, but both require the system to organize evidence around a query rather than perform a single forward read-out.

5 讨论和限制Discussion and limitations

Omni-Decision 应该被理解为一个系统级控制接口，而不是一个固定的多角色实现。相同的证据状态抽象可以在学习策略、多代理系统或更轻的整体系统中使用Omni-Decision should be read as a system-level control interface rather than a fixed multi-role implementation. The same evidence-state abstraction could be used inside a learned policy, a multi-agent system, or a lighter monolithic system(Wu et al., 2023; Kumar et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025a; LangChain, 2026b)。这很重要，因为贡献不是角色数量或特定工具库存，而是读/写契约：系统模块使用相同的状态视图，并且只有规范化事件被提交回. This matters because the contribution is not the number of roles or the particular tool inventory, but the read/write contract: system modules consume the same state view, and only normalized events are committed back toStS_{t}.

本评估也反映了全模式寻证 QA 社区基准的现阶段。 OmniGAIA 提供了一个重要的开放世界设置，具有基于媒体的证据、网络检索、浏览和计算，而 WorldSense 提供了一个互补的独立音频视频设置，具有多项选择读出功能The present evaluation also reflects the current stage of community benchmarks for omni-modal evidence-seeking QA. OmniGAIA provides an important open-world setting with media-grounded evidence, web retrieval, browsing, and computation, while WorldSense provides a complementary self-contained audio-video setting with multiple-choice read-out(Li et al., 2026; Hong et al., 2025)。然而，真实的用户场景比单独的任何一个基准都更广泛：许多任务混合了私人文档、个人媒体、动态网页、长交互历史记录、UI 操作和不断变化的外部状态。现有的评估还提供了对流程级行为的有限覆盖，例如代理是否提出正确的后续证据问题、将不确定性定位到正确的缺失证据槽、适当验证基于工具的声明，或者在证据不可用时因正确的原因停止. However, real user scenarios are broader than either benchmark alone: many tasks mix private documents, personal media, dynamic web pages, long interaction histories, UI operations, and changing external states. Existing evaluations also provide limited coverage of process-level behavior, such as whether an agent asks the right follow-up evidence question, localizes uncertainty to the right missing evidence slot, verifies tool-grounded claims appropriately, or stops for the right reason when evidence is unavailable(Shinn et al., 2023; Zhu et al., 2025; Ma et al., 2026)。因此，我们的实验应该被解释为在代表性当前设置下查询范围证据状态控制价值的证据，而不是作为所有现实世界部署中全模式代理的完整表征。未来的基准可以进一步扩展到最终答案准确性之外，对证据覆盖范围、不确定性定位、基于工具的验证和弃权行为进行更丰富的评估。. Therefore, our experiments should be interpreted as evidence for the value of query-scoped evidence-state control under representative current settings, rather than as a complete characterization of omni-modal agents in all real-world deployments. Future benchmarks could further expand beyond final-answer accuracy toward richer evaluation of evidence coverage, uncertainty localization, tool-grounded verification, and abstention behavior.

6 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced全方位决策Omni-Decision，一种用于全模式寻证 QA 的免训练证据状态系统。核心主张是，长视野多模式控制不应留给隐式对话历史：证据获取、验证、修复、最终确定和不充分停止应消耗相同的查询范围证据状态。, a training-free evidence-state system for omni-modal evidence-seeking QA. The central claim is that long-horizon multimodal control should not be left to implicit dialogue history: evidence acquisition, verification, repair, finalization, and insufficient stopping should consume the same query-scoped evidence state.

开放世界 OmniGAIA 的实验支持了这一观点，并表明同一系统可以转移到 WorldSense 的独立 MCQ 设置。轨迹审计进一步阐明了该方法在哪些方面有帮助，在哪些方面仍然受到限制：许多不正确的运行仍然使证据链的一部分向前推进，但后来被未封闭的感知、检索、计算或准备槽所阻碍。因此，查询范围的证据状态作为证据组织的可控和可检查的骨干是最有用的。它改进了系统决定缺少什么以及何时继续的方式，但它并不能取代低水平的感知、校准的不确定性或预算意识的行动选择。The experiments support this view on open-world OmniGAIA and show that the same system can transfer to WorldSense’s self-contained MCQ setting. The trajectory audits further clarify where the approach helps and where it remains limited: many incorrect runs still move part of the evidence chain forward, but later become blocked by unclosed perception, retrieval, computation, or readiness slots. Thus, query-scoped evidence state is most useful as a controllable and inspectable backbone for evidence organization. It improves how the system decides what is missing and when to continue, but it does not replace low-level perception, calibrated uncertainty, or budget-aware action selection.
