摘要Abstract

来自人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）已经成为一种强大但样本效率低下的方法，用于根据人类偏好学习奖励模型，使主动学习成为合成信息偏好查询的关键组成部分。然而，主动学习所需的有效不确定性量化仍然是大型神经网络奖励模型的关键挑战。 在本文中，我们介绍了 PreferenceEKF，这是一种样本有效的方法，通过将主动偏好学习构建为顺序贝叶斯过滤问题来跟踪奖励模型的不确定性。 我们的方法不是依赖于整个神经网络参数空间上计算上令人望而却步的后验推理，而是通过低维参数子空间内的扩展卡尔曼滤波器执行顺序推理，随着新的偏好查询到达而不断更新奖励模型后验。我们的方法能够对神经网络参数进行可扩展的采样，从而有效地计算主动奖励学习的获取函数。 D4RL 和 V-D4RL 基准测试表明，与其他贝叶斯深度学习方法相比，我们的方法实现了更好的样本效率、运行时间、可扩展性和校准，并且学习的奖励模型带来了有竞争力的离线强化学习策略性能。 这凸显了可扩展贝叶斯方法在 RLHF 中基于偏好的奖励建模的潜力。Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) has emerged as a powerful yet sample-inefficient approach for learning reward models from human preferences, making active learning a critical component in synthesizing informative preference queries. However, effective uncertainty quantification required for active learning remains a key challenge for large neural network reward models. In this paper, we introduce PreferenceEKF, a sample-efficient approach that tracks reward model uncertainty by framing active preference learning as a sequential Bayesian filtering problem. Instead of relying on computationally prohibitive posterior inference over the full neural network parameter space, our method performs sequential inference via an extended Kalman filter within a low-dimensional parameter subspace, continuously updating the reward model posterior as new preference queries arrive. Our approach enables scalable sampling of neural network parameters to efficiently compute acquisition functions for active reward learning. Experiments on the D4RL and V-D4RL benchmarks demonstrate that our approach achieves better sample efficiency, runtime, scalability, and calibration compared to other Bayesian deep learning approaches, and the learned reward models lead to competitive offline reinforcement learning policy performance. 1 简介Introduction

近年来，基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）已成为使决策主体与人类意图保持一致的主导技术In recent years, reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) has become the dominant technique for aligning decision-making agents with human intentions(christiano2017deep; ouyang2022training)。 提供偏好反馈的便利性一直是其作为奖励建模的反馈类型受欢迎的关键因素，但由于每个反馈最多提供一位信息，因此它们也因样本效率差而闻名；向人类提出数千个比较问题来学习奖励模型 (RM) 通常是不可扩展的。. The ease of providing preference feedback has been a crucial factor in its popularity as a feedback type for reward modeling, but since each feedback provides at most one bit of information, they are also known for their poor sample efficiency; asking a human thousands of comparison questions to learn a reward model (RM) is often not scalable.

RLHF 的核心问题是主动学习，我们希望智能体能够明智地处理其提出的查询，以尽可能有效地了解人类的偏好A core problem of RLHF is active learning, where we want an agent to be judicious about the queries it asks to learn about a human’s preferences as efficiently as possible(sadigh2017active; casper2023open; baraka2025humaninteractive)。许多主动学习方法需要对不确定性进行概率建模来计算数据采集函数，从而使适当的不确定性表示成为活跃的研究领域. Many active learning approaches require probabilistic modeling of uncertainty for computing data acquisition functions, making proper uncertainty representation an active area of research(ovadia2019can; tran2020methods; papamarkou2024position)。虽然贝叶斯方法原理良好，但它们很难扩展到大型神经网络 (NN). While Bayesian methods are well-principled, they are hard to scale to large neural networks (NN)(izmailov2021what)。另一方面，集成方法的简单性. On the other hand, the simplicity of ensemble methods(dietterich2000ensemble; lakshminarayanan2016simple)和辍学and dropout(srivastava2014dropout; gal2016dropout)使它们成为主动学习的热门选择。然而，训练多个模型可能需要大量计算，特别是对于大型神经网络奖励模型。 尽管 Dropout 解决了这个问题，但其有效性一直是争论的话题has made them popular choices for active learning. However, training multiple models can be computationally intensive, especially for large NN reward models. Although dropout gets around this issue, its effectiveness has been a subject of debate(fort2020deep; osband2022neural; hron2018variational).

由于近似推理的最新进展，贝叶斯深度学习变得越来越可扩展Due to recent advancements in approximate inference, Bayesian deep learning has become increasingly scalable(daxberger2024laplace; shen2024variational)。在这项工作中，我们开发了一种称为 PreferenceEKF 的方法，该方法能够有效训练贝叶斯神经网络，以表示主动基于偏好的奖励学习中的奖励模型。 具体来说，通过在构建的神经网络子空间中执行贝叶斯过滤，我们以计算和内存高效的方式维持模型的不确定性。子空间的降维使得能够应用扩展卡尔曼滤波器（EKF）（一种经典的推理方法）来训练神经网络。这允许从模型后验中采样任意数量的奖励模型，并使用样本来计算常见的基于不确定性的获取函数，例如预期信息增益和不一致. In this work, we develop a method called PreferenceEKF that enables efficient training of Bayesian neural networks for representing reward models in active preference-based reward learning. Specifically, by performing Bayesian filtering in a constructed neural network subspace, we maintain model uncertainty in a compute- and memory-efficient manner. The reduced dimensionality of the subspace enables application of the extended Kalman filter (EKF), a classic inference method, for training neural networks. This allows sampling of an arbitrary number of reward models from the model posterior, and usage of the samples for computing common uncertainty-based acquisition functions such as expected information gain and disagreement(hennig2012entropy; hernandez-lobato2014predictive; biyik2022learning).

据我们所知，我们是第一个利用子空间过滤从偏好反馈中训练神经网络奖励模型的人。我们将我们的方法 PreferenceEKF 与四种广泛使用的贝叶斯深度学习方法进行比较，这些方法用于连续控制任务中基于偏好的主动奖励学习。我们进一步测试学习到的奖励模型对于离线 RL 任务中的策略优化是否有用To the best of our knowledge, we are the first to leverage subspace filtering to train neural network reward models from preference feedback. We compare our method, PreferenceEKF, to four widely used Bayesian deep learning methods for active preference-based reward learning in continuous control tasks. We further test whether the learned reward models are useful for policy optimization in offline RL tasks(levine2020offline)。我们在 D4RL 中进行实验. We conduct our experiments in the D4RL(fu2020d4rl)和 V-D4RLand the V-D4RL(lu2023challenges)基准。我们的研究结果如下：benchmarks. Our findings are as follows:

  • •

    在偏好建模任务中的样本效率和校准方面，PreferenceEKF 的表现与所有贝叶斯深度学习基线相当或更好。PreferenceEKF performs on par with or better than all Bayesian deep learning baselines in terms of sample efficiency and calibration in preference modeling tasks.

  • •

    与所有其他方法相比，PreferenceEKF 的运行时间不仅更快，而且在模型大小和后验样本数量方面具有更好的扩展性。PreferenceEKF’s runtime is not only much faster, but also scales better with both model size and number of posterior samples compared to all other methods.

  • •

2 相关工作Related Work

根据人类偏好进行强化学习。Reinforcement learning from human preferences.虽然奖励学习的早期工作侧重于从专家演示中学习While early works in reward learning focused on learning from expert demonstrations(abbeel2004apprenticeship; finn2016guided; ho2016generative)，最近的大部分兴趣都集中在成对比较的奖励学习上，其中要求人类注释者比较两个潜在的结果，例如标签、响应或轨迹, much of the recent interest has focused on reward learning from pairwise comparisons where human annotators are asked to compare two potential outcomes, e.g., labels, responses, or trajectories(wirth2017survey; christiano2017deep; brown2019extrapolating)。尽管注释者提供偏好反馈比演示要容易得多，但二进制偏好查询中包含的最少量信息需要收集大量反馈数据。. Although preference feedback is much easier for annotators to provide than demonstrations, the minimal amount of information contained within a binary preference query necessitates collection of large amounts of feedback data.

主动学习是一种广泛使用的方法，可以最大限度地减少收集人类反馈的耗时过程。它本质上是一个序列问题，因为它根据模型的当前状态（例如参数后验不确定性）迭代收集最有用的数据样本。Active learning is a widely used approach for minimizing the time-consuming process of collecting human feedback. It is a sequential problem in nature, as it iteratively collects the most useful data sample based on the model’s current state, such as parameter posterior uncertainty.(sadigh2017active; settles2009active)。 虽然贝叶斯方法已成功应用于使用低维线性和高斯过程奖励模型获得主动奖励学习的后验. While Bayesian methods have been successfully applied to obtain posteriors for active reward learning using lower-dimensional linear and Gaussian process reward models(biyik2022learning; biyik2024active)，它们尚未广泛应用于神经奖励模型，因为获取函数通常需要从模型参数的高维分布中进行采样。相反，集成和 dropout 一直是基于神经网络的主动奖励学习的关键推动者, they have not been widely adopted for neural reward models, since acquisition functions typically require sampling from the high-dimensional distribution of model parameters. Instead, ensembles and dropout have been the key enabler of neural network-based active reward learning(lee2021pebble; christiano2017deep)。我们的工作重点是用于主动奖励学习的高效且高性能的后推理，而无需对多个独立模型进行昂贵的训练。. Our work focuses on efficient yet performant posterior inference for active reward learning, without expensive training of multiple independent models.

神经网络的不确定性量化。Uncertainty quantification for neural networks.已成功用于神经网络不确定性量化的经典贝叶斯方法包括拉普拉斯近似Classic Bayesian methods that have been successfully used for neural network uncertainty quantification include Laplace approximation(daxberger2024laplace), 哈密顿蒙特卡罗, Hamiltonian Monte Carlo(neal2011mcmc)和变分推理, and variational inference(blei2017variational)。虽然并没有严格遵循贝叶斯原理，但集成的简单性. While not strictly motivated by Bayesian principles, the simplicity of ensembling(dietterich2000ensemble; lakshminarayanan2016simple)和辍学and dropout(srivastava2014dropout; gal2016dropout)使它们在不确定性量化 (UQ) 中广受欢迎。尽管 dropout 方法避免了集成方法训练多个独立模型的成本，但它已被证明会导致后验近似质量较差has made them popular for uncertainty quantification (UQ). Although the dropout method gets around ensemble method’s cost of training multiple independent models, it has been shown to lead to poor posterior approximation quality(hron2018variational; osband2022neural).

贝叶斯过滤方法专注于从噪声观测中推断隐藏状态，为顺序学习提供了原则性方法，并已广泛应用于机器人和信号处理中Bayesian filtering methods, which focus on inferring hidden states from noisy observations, provide a principled approach to sequential learning, and have been widely used in robotics and signal processing(thrun2005probabilistic; sarkka2023bayesian)。 贝叶斯过滤在训练神经网络中的应用. Application of Bayesian filtering for training neural networks(singhal1988training; defreitas2000hierarchical)最近才通过子空间方法应用于深度网络has only recently been applied to deep networks via subspace methods byduran-martin2022efficient.

一个单独的工作重点是利用非参数统计技术（例如引导程序）来执行 UQ，而不是从后验推理中得出认知不确定性Instead of deriving epistemic uncertainty from posterior inference, a separate line of work has focused on leveraging nonparametric statistics techniques such as the bootstrap to perform UQ(efron1992bootstrap)，并成功应用该技术进行深度强化学习的探索, and has successfully applied this technique for exploration in deep reinforcement learning(osband2018randomized; osband2016deep)。同一组作者还利用了昆士兰大学的联合预测，并将该想法应用于微调大型语言模型. The same group of authors has also leveraged joint predictions for UQ, and has applied the idea to finetuning large language models(osband2023epistemic; osband2023finetuning)。我们的工作利用贝叶斯过滤在主动奖励学习设置中训练神经网络奖励模型，其中我们主要关注参数不确定性而不是联合预测不确定性。. Our work leverages Bayesian filtering to train neural network reward models in active reward learning settings, where we focus primarily on parameter uncertainty instead of joint predictive uncertainty.

神经网络的子空间方法。Subspace methods for neural networks.虽然存在大量关于减小神经网络大小以通过架构搜索进行有效训练和服务的文献While there exists a vast literature on decreasing neural network size for efficient training and serving via architecture search(elsken2019neural), 量化, quantization(gholami2021survey)和修剪, and pruning(frankle2022lottery)，我们只关注在简化模型中实现易于处理的推理的工作。具体来说，越来越多的证据表明，神经网络解决任务所需的参数数量通常远小于网络的总参数数量；这些子参数所跨越的子空间中的优化和推理不仅提供了计算效率，而且还提供了应用贝叶斯方法进行神经网络训练的易处理性, we focus only on works that enable tractable inference in the reduced model. Specifically, there is growing evidence that the number of parameters required for a neural network to solve a task is often much smaller than the network’s total parameter count; optimization and inference in the subspace spanned by these sub-parameters offer not only computational efficiency, but also tractability of applying Bayesian methods for neural network training(fort2020deep; larsen2022how)。这些参数要么作为神经网络参数的子集，要么在其低维子空间内。. These parameters are found either as a subset of neural network parameters, or within a lower-dimensional subspace thereof.

关注参数子集的方法通常将贝叶斯方法（例如贝叶斯线性回归或变分推理）应用于神经网络的最后一层，并将点估计方法（例如随机梯度下降（SGD））应用于中间层Methods focusing on parameter subsets typically apply Bayesian methods such as Bayesian linear regression or variational inference to the last layer of the neural network, and point estimation methods like stochastic gradient descent (SGD) to the intermediate layers(snoek2015scalable; harrison2023variational; brunzema2024bayesian)。另一方面，子空间方法通常通过全网络的 SGD 迭代的随机投影或奇异值分解来构造低维子空间；任何推理或优化技术，例如切片采样. On the other hand, subspace methods typically construct the low-dimensional subspaces via either random projection or singular value decomposition of SGD iterates of the full network; any inference or optimization technique such as sliced sampling(izmailov2020subspace)或新元or SGDli2018measuring然后可以以易于处理的方式应用于子空间。can then be applied in the subspace in a tractable manner.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们成功地采用扩展卡尔曼滤波器在基于主动偏好的奖励建模设置中训练神经网络。我们展示了维持子空间分布相对于神经网络参数的几个优点In this work, we successfully adopted extended Kalman filters to train neural networks in an active preference-based reward modeling setting. We showed several advantages of maintaining a subspace distribution over neural network parametersp⁡(𝜽∣𝒟)p(\bm{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D})，与其他四种广泛使用的用于主动奖励学习的贝叶斯深度学习方法相比。 我们的方法带来了样本效率更高的主动奖励学习、类似性能的强化学习策略优化、模型大小和模型样本数量方面更好的运行时扩展，以及通过更高质量的不确定性表示进行更好的校准。, in comparison to four other widely used Bayesian deep learning methods for active reward learning. Our approach led to more sample-efficient active reward learning, similarly performant RL policy optimization, better runtime scaling with respect to model size and model sample count, and better calibration through higher-quality uncertainty representation.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and future work.虽然我们发现子空间方法是缩放神经网络训练的贝叶斯过滤方法的有效工具，但尚不清楚这种方法是否能有效地将贝叶斯方法应用于基础模型规模奖励模型While we found subspace methods to be an effective tool for scaling Bayesian filtering methods for neural network training, it is unclear whether this approach will be effective for applying Bayesian methods to foundation model-scale reward models(mahan2024generative; zhang2024generative)。由于扩展卡尔曼滤波器保持高斯分布的单峰性，可能需要研究替代方法来近似多模态后验，例如，从具有不同偏好的注释器中学习奖励函数. 更广泛的影响Broader Impacts

我们的工作提出了一种基于偏好的奖励建模中的主动学习算法，提高了需要主观人类评估的应用中神经网络训练的效率和准确性，例如自然语言处理、个性化推荐和人机交互。通过围绕不确定或高影响力的偏好优化数据收集，我们的方法可以降低标签成本并提高模型与人类意图的一致性。然而，在缩减子空间中工作并使用扩展卡尔曼滤波器进行推理可能会在偏好建模中引入次优性，例如偏差放大或忽视少数偏好。为了减轻这些风险，未来的研究应该调查 PreferenceEKF 的稳健性以及子空间缩减导致的潜在信息丢失。Our work presents an algorithm for active learning in preference-based reward modeling, enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of neural network training in applications requiring subjective human evaluations, such as natural language processing, personalized recommendations, and human-robot interaction. By optimizing data collection around uncertain or high-impact preferences, our approach can reduce labeling costs and improve model alignment with human intentions. However, it is possible that working in the reduced subspace and performing inference with the extended Kalman filter may introduce suboptimalities in preference modeling such as bias amplification or neglect of minority preferences. To mitigate these risks, future research should investigate the robustness of PreferenceEKF and potential information loss caused by subspace reduction.

A.3.2 实施细节Implementation Details

离线强化学习算法利用奖励信息进行策略优化的程度，即奖励诱导的策略性能是否是评估学习奖励模型的良好指标，在很大程度上取决于轨迹数据集：当在仅由专家演示组成的数据集上运行时，离线强化学习算法将在很大程度上忽略奖励信息并采用类似行为克隆的学习策略。另一方面，通常很难从由纯随机行为组成的数据集中训练策略The extent to which offline RL algorithms leverage reward information for policy optimization, i.e., whether reward-induced policy performance is a good metric for assessing learned reward models, is heavily dependent on the trajectory dataset: when run on datasets consisting solely of expert demonstrations, offline RL algorithms will largely ignore reward information and adopt a behavioral cloning-like learning strategy. On the other hand, it is generally difficult to train a policy from a dataset consisting of purely random behavior(kumar2021should).
