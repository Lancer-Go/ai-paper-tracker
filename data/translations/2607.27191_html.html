人工智能爆炸性进展的预测取决于人工智能代理自动化人工智能研究。但关于智能体是否可以进行开放式人工智能研究的证据还很少。目前的评估要么在狭窄的、可验证的任务上测试代理，这排除了开放式研究，要么将人工智能生成的论文提交给盲目的同行评审，这种评审过度拉伸、随机且评审质量较差。我们引入了第三种衡量人工智能研发自动化进展的方法。代理承担高质量未发表论文的核心、开放式研究问题，论文的原作者对其输出进行评分。我们称这些为影子评估。我们对两份未发布的 NeurIPS 2026 提交内容进行了影子评估，为前沿代理提供了六天的时间和数千美元的计算资源。这些特工在没有人类帮助的情况下完成了所有工程，但无法在回答研究问题方面取得实质性进展。结果，这两篇论文都被作者明确拒绝。我们确定了五种反复出现的失败模式：对可发表研究的标准判断不佳、对研究设计中的缺陷缺乏创造性的反应、从死胡同中无效的回溯、资源意识差和指令漂移。对第二个模型和支架的稳健性检查重现了这些故障。我们发布专家评论、调查回复、代理存储库和日志。我们的结果提供了早期证据，表明当今的智能体可以进行人工智能研究的工程设计，但在研究生命周期的关键部分上遇到了困难。Forecasts of explosive AI progress hinge on AI agents automating AI research. But evidence on whether agents can carry out open-ended AI research is thin. Current evaluations either test agents on narrow, verifiable tasks, which excludes open-ended research, or submit AI-generated papers to blind peer review, which is overstretched, stochastic, and suffers from poor review quality. We introduce a third way to measure progress towards AI R&D automation. An agent takes on the central, open-ended research question of a high-quality unpublished paper, and the paper’s original authors grade its output. We call these shadow evaluations. We ran shadow evaluations on two unpublished NeurIPS 2026 submissions, giving frontier agents six days and thousands of dollars of compute. The agents completed all of the engineering without human help, yet could not make substantial progress towards answering the research questions. As a result, both papers were unambiguously rejected by the authors. We identify five recurring failure modes: poor judgment about the bar for publishable research, uncreative responses to shortcomings in the research design, ineffective backtracking from dead ends, poor resource awareness, and instruction drift. A robustness check with a second model and scaffold reproduced these failures. We release the expert reviews, survey responses, agent repositories, and logs. Our results provide early evidence that today’s agents can do the engineering of AI research, but struggle with critical parts of the research lifecycle.

1 简介1 Introduction

关于人工智能功能最重要的开放问题之一是人工智能代理是否可以进行人工智能研究。许多对人工智能爆炸性进展的预测都推测人工智能系统很快就会自行进行人工智能研究（Kokotajlo 等人，2025 年；Cunningham 等人，2026 年）。这也是领先AI实验室的明确前提； 6 月，Anthropic 发表了一篇题为“当 AI 构建自身时”的文章（Favaro 和 Clark，2026）；7 月，OpenAI 宣传称，他们的新模型 GPT-5.6 Sol 有助于对较小的模型进行后期训练，为研究人员节省了数周的时间（OpenAI，2026c）。One of the most consequential open questions about AI capabilities is whether AI agents can conduct AI research. Many forecasts of explosive AI progress speculate that AI systems will soon do AI research themselves (Kokotajlo et al., 2025; Cunningham et al., 2026). This is also the explicit premise of leading AI labs; in June, Anthropic published a post entitled “When AI Builds Itself” (Favaro and Clark, 2026), and in July, OpenAI advertised that their new model, GPT-5.6 Sol, had helped post-train a smaller model, saving researchers multiple weeks (OpenAI, 2026c).1但是，尽管这个研究方向很重要并且对这些主张给予了关注，但关于智能体是否能够解决开放式研究问题的证据基础却很薄弱。But despite the significance of this research direction and the attention paid to these claims, the evidence base on whether agents can solve open-ended research questions is thin.

最近关于自主人工智能研发的大部分工作的统一之处在于，它专注于可验证的任务，在这些任务中，代理需要改进固定的、狭窄的指标。基准要求代理改进已知指标，然后自动验证器对结果进行评分。除了基准之外，许多评估表明人工智能代理在优化 GPT-2 级别模型（Prime Intellect Team，2026）、使用较弱的模型训练更强的模型（Wen 等人，2026）以及优化自动研究工具（Weco Team，2026）等任务中击败了人类专家的表现。What unifies most of this recent work on autonomous AI R&D is its focus on verifiable tasks where agents need to improve a fixed, narrow metric. Benchmarks ask agents to improve a known metric, and an automatic verifier scores the result. Beyond benchmarks, a number of evaluations have shown AI agents beating expert human performance in tasks such as optimizing GPT-2 level models (Prime Intellect Team, 2026), using weaker models to train stronger ones (Wen et al., 2026), and optimizing an autoresearch harness (Weco Team, 2026).2

但许多人工智能研究超出了解决可验证任务的范围。代理人无法自行选择一组候选假设，决定哪些证据可以解决研究问题，或有效地整合反馈并认识到某种方法已经失败，正确的做法是重新开始。But much AI research goes beyond solving verifiable tasks. Agents can’t hill-climb their way into choosing a set of candidate hypotheses, deciding what evidence would settle a research question, or incorporating feedback effectively and recognizing that an approach has failed and the right move is to start over.

少数项目采用了不同的人工智能研发评估方法：将人工智能生成的论文提交给盲审同行评审流程，例如人工智能会议和研讨会。但同行评审是衡量研究能力的一个薄弱指标：会议评审过度延伸且高度随机（Cortes 和 Lawrence，2021；Beygelzimer 等，2023；Shah，2022），并且它没有透露有多少人工智能生成的提交在最终接受之前被拒绝。A small number of projects have adopted a different method for AI R&D evaluation: they submit AI-generated papers to blind peer-review processes, such as to AI conferences and workshops. But peer review is a weak measure of research ability: conference reviewing is overstretched and highly stochastic (Cortes and Lawrence, 2021; Beygelzimer et al., 2023; Shah, 2022), and it does not reveal how many AI-generated submissions were rejected before the eventual acceptance.

在本文中，我们用一种新方法评估人工智能研发自动化，我们称之为影子评估。这包括从尚未公开的高质量研究论文中提取中心研究问题，委托资源充足的前沿代理人来回答该问题，并要求论文的原始作者像对会议提交的文件一样对代理人的输出进行评分。代理“隐藏”原始研究：它与原始作者研究相同的研究问题，而无法访问他们的论文或发现。这种设计为我们提供了开放式任务、未受污染的问题以及对所评估的确切问题具有深厚专业知识的审阅者。我们认为影子评估是对人工智能研发自动化的狭隘、可验证评估和盲目审查评估的补充。我们希望未来的研究利用这三者来探索衡量人工智能研发自动化进展的不同方面。In this paper, we evaluate AI R&D automation with a new method, which we call shadow evaluation. This involves taking the central research question from a high-quality research paper that is not yet public, tasking a well-resourced frontier agent with answering it, and asking the paper’s original authors to grade the agent’s output as they would a conference submission. The agent “shadows” the original study: it works on the same research question as the original authors, without access to their paper or findings. This design gives us open-ended tasks, uncontaminated questions, and reviewers with deep expertise in the exact question being evaluated. We consider shadow evaluations complementary to both narrow, verifiable evaluations and blind review evaluations of AI R&D automation. We hope future research uses all three to explore different facets of measuring progress towards AI R&D automation.

为了对 AI 研发自动化进行影子评估，我们与提交给 NeurIPS 2026 的两篇尚未公开的论文的作者合作。未发表的论文为我们提供了一种测试智能体是否能够解决开放式研究问题的方法。通过他们自己长达数月的思考问题的努力，原作者具有独特的地位来对代理人的工作质量进行评分，而代理人无法查找研究人员的发现，因为它们尚未出现在网络上。我们向代理提供了论文的研究问题、六天的挂钟时间、3,000 美元的 Anthropic API 积分（允许代理在一周内进行开放式探索和运行实验）、用于实验的 GPU 积分以及对虚拟机和开放网络的完全访问权限。我们的目标是发表一篇值得在顶级人工智能会议上发表的论文。两篇论文的原作者随后作为会议审稿人对结果进行评分。To carry out shadow evaluations of AI R&D automation, we partnered with the authors of two papers submitted to NeurIPS 2026 that were not yet public. Unpublished papers give us a way to test if agents can solve open-ended research problems. Through their own months-long effort in thinking about the question, the original authors are uniquely positioned to grade the quality of the agent’s work, and the agent cannot look up the researchers’ findings, because they are not yet on the web. We gave an agent the paper’s research question, six days of wall-clock time, $3,000 in Anthropic API credits to allow agents to conduct open-ended exploration and run experiments over the course of a week, GPU credits for experiments, and full access to a VM and the open web. The goal was to produce a paper worthy of publication at a top-tier AI conference. The original authors of both papers then graded the results as conference reviewers.

  3. 特工们并没有有效地放弃那些毫无希望的做法。The agents did not effectively backtrack from unpromising approaches.虽然这些代理最初尝试了多个不同的研究方向，并在整个过程中进行了本地回溯，但他们都在前十个小时内放弃了最雄心勃勃的研究目标，并且在那之后没有一个代理从根本上改变了其方法。While the agents initially experimented with multiple distinct research directions and backtracked locally throughout the process, they both retired their most ambitious research targets within the first ten hours, and neither agent fundamentally shifted its approach after that point.

  4. 特工没有遵循具体指示。The agents did not follow concrete instructions.他们遭受了指令漂移的困扰，忽视了关于应该花多少时间进行探索、多久从人工智能审阅工具获得审阅以及对论文长度的严格限制的明确规则。结果，两篇最终论文均未达到向人工智能会议提交论文的技术要求。They suffered from instruction drift and ignored explicit rules about how much time should be spent on exploration, how often to get reviews from AI review tools, and strict limits on paper length. We discuss the limitations of this approach in Table 3 and Section 7. But while the non-blindedness of the design is not ideal, the papers generated by the agents in our experiments were unambiguously of poor quality. Still, we release the artifacts alongside this paper and we welcome other experts in the respective topic areas to judge them.3

2 影子评估：衡量人工智能研发自动化进展的新方法2 Shadow evaluations: A new method for measuring progress towards AI R&D automation

评估自动化人工智能研究需要一种衡量代理产生的研究质量的方法。大多数现有评估使用两种方法之一：对可验证任务的评估或盲审。在对可验证任务的评估中，代理改进固定指标，并且自动验证器对结果进行评分。此类评估的例子有很多：RE-Bench (Wijk et al., 2025) 要求智能体解决研究工程问题，MLE-Bench (Chan et al., 2025) 要求他们参加 Kaggle 竞赛，PostTrainBench 要求他们根据 Q&A 基准对小语言模型进行后期训练 (Rank et al., 2026)。表 2 调查了此类评估的一些尝试。自动验证使这些评估客观、可重复且扩展成本低廉。然而，它将评估限制为可以用单个数字来衡量成功的任务。Evaluating automated AI research requires a way to measure the quality of the research that agents produce. Most existing evaluations use one of two approaches: evaluations on verifiable tasks or blind review. In evaluations on verifiable tasks, the agent improves a fixed metric, and an automatic verifier scores the result. There are many examples of such evaluations: RE-Bench (Wijk et al., 2025) asks agents to solve research-engineering problems, MLE-Bench (Chan et al., 2025) asks them to compete in Kaggle competitions, and PostTrainBench asks them to post-train small language models against Q&A benchmarks (Rank et al., 2026). Table 2 surveys some attempts at such evaluations. Automatic verification makes these evaluations objective, repeatable, and cheap to scale. However, it restricts the evaluations to tasks where success can be measured as a single number.

一小部分项目评估完全自主的研究，并通过盲审同行评审进行评分。 Sakana 的 AIScientist 撰写了一篇被 ICLR 2025 研讨会接受的论文（Yamada et al., 2025），Intology 的 Zochi 撰写了一篇被 ACL 2025 主要会议录接受的论文（Intology, 2025），人类的参与仅限于手稿准备。这些项目测试代理人的开放式研究任务，但同行评审往往无法支持从中得出的结论。Asmallersetof projects evaluatesfullyautonomous researchandgradesitwithblindpeerreview. These projects test agents on open-ended research tasks, but peer review often cannot support the conclusions drawn from it.

Submissions have grown exponentially, and this growth has degraded the match between papers and qualified reviewers: conferences rely on automated expertise matching, leading to reviews being written by researchers who are inexperienced or non-experts on the research they are evaluating (Shah, 2022). Even well-matched reviewers are not expected to check the technical details of a paper; NeurIPS instructs reviewers to examine the core arguments but not to verify every line (NeurIPS, 2026). 评估人工智能研发自动化的盲审范式的另一个挑战是自动生成论文的成本低廉。使用盲审来测试自动生成的人工智能研究论文的开发人员可以提交许多论文，报告接受结果，并且永远不会透露失败的尝试，因此接受结果几乎不能说明系统产生良好研究的可靠性。盲目评审还减少了每篇论文受到的审查：考虑到人工智能会议评审的挑战，审稿人只有几个小时且与问题无关，可能无法确定人工智能生成的发现是否正确、新颖和有用。Another challenge of the blind review paradigm for evaluations of AI R&D automation is that automatically generating a paper is inexpensive. A developer testing their automatically generated AI research papers using blind review can submit many papers, report the acceptances, and never disclose the failed attempts, so an acceptance says little about how reliably a system produces good research. Blind review also reduces the scrutiny each paper receives: given the challenges with AI conference reviewing, a reviewer with a few hours and no stake in the question might not establish whether an AI-generated finding is correct, novel, and useful.

在本文中，我们开发了第三种方法，将开放式任务与深入的专家评分相结合。我们从尚未公开的研究论文中提取中心研究问题，将其交给资源充足的前沿代理人，并要求论文的原始作者像审查会议提交的文件一样审查代理人的输出。我们称这些为影子评估，因为它们要求代理解决与论文原作者相同的研究问题，而无需访问最终论文。In this paper, we develop a third method that combines open-ended tasks with in-depth expert grading. We take the central research question from a research paper that is not yet public, give it to a well-resourced frontier agent, and ask the paper’s original authors to review the agent’s output as they would a conference submission. We call these shadow evaluations, since they task agents with solving the same research questions as the original authors of a paper, without access to the final paper.

该研究设计解决了可验证评估和盲审评估的许多问题。研究问题基于实际的会议提交材料，因此可以根据顶级会议提交材料的质量进行衡量。由于研究结果不在网络上或代理的训练数据中，因此评估未受污染。由于作者花了几个月的时间回答向智能体提供的相同问题，因此他们可以详细判断智能体是否取得了进展；盲目审稿人可能缺乏充分评估论文的专业知识。影子评估也是可重复的。愿意的作者未发表的新论文可以作为新的测试用例，并且当它们发布时，设计会延续到更强大的模型和支架上。This research design addresses many of the concerns with verifiable evaluations and blind review evaluations. The research questions are based on actual conference submissions, so they can be measured against the quality of top conference submissions. Since the findings are not on the web or in the agent’s training data, the evaluation is uncontaminated. And since the authors spent months answering the same questions as are provided to the agent, they can judge in detail whether the agent made progress; blind reviewers might lack the expertise to adequately assess the paper. Shadow evaluations are also repeatable. New unpublished papers with willing authors can serve as new test cases, and the design carries over to stronger models and scaffolds as they are released.

这种设计还允许我们测试一种机制，该机制可以为递归自我改进的许多预测提供信息：人工智能代理加速人工智能研究，因为研究人员将整个项目委托给代理并判断返回的结果是否推进他们的工作。我们的评估与该模型非常吻合，因为作者向代理人提出了自己的研究问题，并仔细评估了结果输出。This design also allows us to test a mechanism that informs many forecasts of recursive self-improvement: AI agents accelerate AI research because researchers delegate entire projects to agents and judge whether the returned results advance their work. Our evaluation closely matches this model, since authors handed an agent their own research question and closely evaluated the resulting output.

虽然影子评估让我们能够以新的方式评估人工智能研发自动化，但它们也有其自身的缺点。非盲审可能会带来审稿人的偏见，因为审稿人已经用特定的方法回答了问题，也知道论文是由人工智能代理写的。我们的设计也存在开放世界评估固有的局限性（Kapoor et al., 2026）。深入的评分需要专家对问题进行审阅，需要几天的时间，所以我们只能研究两篇论文。此类评估还需要在每一步进行判断，从选择论文到设计支架再到解释日志，因此我们的选择和偏见可能会影响结果。While shadow evaluations allow us to assess AI R&D automation in a new way, they have their own shortcomings. Non-blind reviewing might come with reviewer biases, since reviewers have already answered the question with a specific method and also know that the paper was written by AI agents. Our design also has the limitations inherent to open-world evaluations (Kapoor et al., 2026). In-depth grading requires experts on the question and days on the review, so we could study only two papers. Such evaluations also require judgment at every step, from selecting papers to designing the scaffold to interpreting the logs, so our choices and biases could shape the results.

这些限制源于产生该方法优势的相同设计选择，我们认为此类评估为我们提供了一种测量构造的新方法，而验证者评分的基准和盲审无法评估。我们尽了最大努力来减轻这些担忧，方法是记录每一次人为干预，使用不同的模型和支架重复一项实验，并发布专家评论、调查回复、代理存储库和运行日志以提高透明度。最后，评估人工智能研发自动化的三种方法可能会产生关于进展速度的系统不同的见解。我们并不声称专家驱动的开放世界评估严格优于可验证的评估或盲目审查；我们认为它们是互补的，因为它们评估人工智能研发进展的不同方面。These limitations follow from the same design choices that produce the method’s strengths, and we think such evaluations give us a new method of measuring constructs that verifier-scored benchmarks and blind reviews cannot assess. We made our best attempts to mitigate these concerns by documenting every human intervention, repeating one experiment with a different model and scaffold, and releasing the expert reviews, survey responses, agent repositories, and run logs for transparency. Finally, the three methods for evaluating AI R&D automation might yield systematically different insights about the rate of progress. We do not claim that expert-driven open-world evaluations are strictly better than verifiable evaluations or blind review; we view them as complementary, since they evaluate different aspects of AI R&D progress.

3 任务及研究设置3 The task and the research setup

我们的实验测试了资源充足的前沿人工智能代理是否能够产生新颖的人工智能研究。严格回答这个问题需要真实的、未受污染的研究问题，而智能体无法从训练数据中记住这些问题，也无法在网上找到这些问题。为了满足这些要求，我们依赖于进行实验时尚未公开的高质量人工智能研究。Our experiment tests whether well-resourced frontier AI agents can produce novel AI research. Answering this rigorously requires real, uncontaminated research questions that the agent could not memorize from its training data or find online. To satisfy these requirements, we rely on high-quality AI research that was not public at the time we conducted the experiments.

In our own experiments testing AI agent reliability on standard benchmarks, we found that current frontier models remain poor discriminators of task success, even as their accuracy has increased. A similar open-ended post-training experiment conducted by the AI Village also highlighted poor data selection as a main failure mode (Tekofsky, 2026).

5.3 缺乏有效的回溯5.3 Lack of effective backtracking

即使没有新的想法，研究人员也可以认识到某种方法是无效的，放弃工作并返回探索。代理们在本地进行了回溯，重新运行实验并添加稳健性检查以回应批评。但他们在项目层面从未退缩。尽管人工智能自我审查不断返回负面结论，但两个智能体都没有放弃其方法并重新启动。在 TabPFN 运行中，代理重新构建了目标：在早期的检测器尝试失败后，它认为不存在这样的检测器，并写了一篇负面结果的论文。我们的设计要求代理人出示一份论文，并且不提供弃权的选项，这可能导致代理人写出负面结果的论文。但处于相同位置的人类研究人员可能会更早、更有效地进行回溯，同时仍有足够的时间和预算来寻求替代方法。Even without a new idea, a researcher can recognize that an approach is unproductive, discard the work, and return to exploration. This suggests that unlike engineering tasks and verifiable research tasks, agents struggle with some kinds of open-ended AI R&D tasks.

Wehighlightnumerousidentificationchallengeswithshadowevaluationsandtheparticular experiments that we run. But our broader motivation for this work is to evaluate claims of AI agents accelerating and even automating AI R&D itself. Thus, a separate question concerns the影响implications关于人工智能代理自动化开放式人工智能研究问题的积极或消极发现。of a positive or negative finding on the question of AI agents automating open-ended AI research.

有多种原因导致您的结果可能无法真正澄清有关加速人工智能研发这一更广泛问题的争论。人工智能研发之路可能并不需要像我们研究的那样的开放式任务的完全自动化，或者我们衡量的开放式研究技能不是关键路径上的技能。Therearemultiplereasonswhyourresultsmightnotactuallyclarifythedebateonthebroaderquestionofaccelerating AI R&D. It is possible that the path to AI R&D does not require full automation of open-ended tasks like the ones we study, or that the open-ended research skills that we measure are not ones on the critical path.

尽管如此，我们有充分的理由认为，人工智能在可验证任务上的能力与开放式研究问题之间的区别与加速人工智能研发的问题密切相关。 Anthropic 关于自我完善的帖子明确引用了克劳德在法学硕士评审的“开放式”克劳德代码课程中不断上升的成功率作为自我完善的证据（Favaro 和 Clark，2026）。弹性研究所最近发​​布的一份关于递归自我改进经济学的报告明确区分了“广义”和“狭义”人工智能能力，讨论了仅“狭义”能力加速的影响，并指出需要更多关于人工智能能力和相对于人类人工智能研究人员的弱点的全面数据（Cunningham 等人，2026）。我们希望我们的实验能够提供早期证据来回答这个问题。Nevertheless, there are good reasons to view the distinction between AI capabilities on verifiable tasks and openended research problems as germane to the question of accelerating AI R&D. Anthropic’s post on self-improvement explicitly cites Claude’s rising success rate on LLM-judged "open-ended" Claude Code sessions as evidence of selfimprovement (Favaro and Clark, 2026). A recent report from the Elasticity Institute on the economics of recursive self-improvement explicitly distinguishes between "broad" and "narrow" AI capabilities, discusses implications of a speed-up in only "narrow" capabilities, and cites the need for more data on the full breadth of AI capabilities and weaknesses relative to human AI researchers (Cunningham et al., 2026). We hope our experiments provide early evidence to answer this question.

8 潜在偏见8 Potential biases

开放世界的评估允许研究人员有很大的自由裁量权：研究人员在选择要评估的问题、设计研究和执行研究方面有余地。这意味着我们之前的信念、期望和偏见——尤其是设计和进行实验的核心团队的信念、期望和偏见——可能会影响我们的评估结果。特别是，一些核心团队成员因我们的立场而闻名，即迫在眉睫的递归自我完善导致超级智能失控的可能性不大；这可能会影响我们设计评估的方式以及解释结果的方式。Open-world evaluations allow substantial researcher discretion: researchers have leeway in choosing the questions being evaluated, designing the study, and executing it. This means that our prior beliefs, expectations, and biases— especially those of the core team that designed and conducted the experiments—could impact the results of our evaluation. In particular, some of the core team members are known for our position that imminent recursive self-improvement leading to runaway superintelligence is unlikely; this could affect how we design the evaluation and how we interpret the results.

例如，我们将智能体的失败描述为缺乏创造力和判断力：当任务需要创造性地解决问题时，智能体过快地采用了最初的方法。正如我们在文中讨论的那样，这种解释并不是不言而喻的，我们的一些合著者反而将结果解释为推理和逻辑的失败，或者认知锁定。同样，我们为本次评估选择的研究反映了我们对实证 NeurIPS 论文结果构成的理解；但其他研究人员可能不同意在递归自我完善方面取得进展所必需的开放性程度。For example, we describe the agents’ failures as the lack of creativity and judgment: the agents settled into their initial approach too quickly when the tasks required creative problem solving. As we discuss in the text, this interpretation is not self-evident, and some of our coauthors instead interpret the results as failures of reasoning and logic, or as epistemic lock-in. Similarly, the studies we chose for this evaluation reflect our understanding of what constitutes results of an empirical NeurIPS paper; but other researchers might disagree on the level of open-endedness that is necessary for making progress towards recursive self-improvement.

鉴于评估的性质，我们认为不存在“公正”的评估方式。虽然具有明确成功标准的基准在某种意义上更加客观，但这也导致了任务规格的缩小，并减少了可以研究的研究问题的类型；开放世界评估以客观性换取更丰富的评估任务。Given the nature of the evaluations, we do not think there is an “unbiased” way to conduct them. While benchmarks with clear success criteria are in some sense more objective, that also leads to a narrower task specification and curtails the types of research questions that can be studied; open-world evaluations trade off objectivity for a much richer set of evaluation tasks.

这些考虑因素提出了设计开放世界评估的几条原则。我们认为，当认知不同的人进行评估时，这种评估才最有说服力。这包括团队内部和跨团队的想法多样性。披露团队的偏见和立场并明确表达分歧也很重要。例如，许多合著者与核心团队相比有不同的先验；当结果确实发生时，这有助于我们在对结果的解释中出现分歧。These considerations suggest several principles for designing open-world evaluations. We think such evaluations are most persuasive when epistemically diverse sets of people conduct them. This includes both within-team and across-team diversity in ideas. It is also important to disclose the team’s biases and positions and explicitly surface disagreements. As an example, many coauthors have different priors compared to the core team; this helped us surface disagreements in our interpretation of the results when they did occur.

我们还采取了许多措施来尽量减少核心团队先验的影响。这包括运行多次试运行，重点是改进脚手架、为代理提供慷慨的预算以及分析日志以了解和解决关键故障模式。例如，我们添加了多行提示来解决一些反复出现的失败模式，例如缺乏探索。We also took many steps to minimize the effects of the core team’s priors. This included running multiple dry runs focused on improving the scaffold, allowing the agent generous budgets, and analyzing the logs to understand and address key failure modes. For example, we added multiple lines of prompting to address some recurring failure modes, such as the lack of exploration.

除了这些“已知”偏见之外，我们还受到难以先验识别的“未知”偏见的影响。例如，我们的许多结果都依赖于分析代理的日志。日志分析涉及自由裁量权，我们可能比不符合我们预期的故障更容易识别出符合我们预期的故障。我们发布了完整的专家评论、调查回复、代理存储库和运行日志，以便读者可以根据原材料检查我们的解释。In addition to these “known” biases, we are also subject to “unknown” biases that are hard to identify a priori. For example, many of our results rely on analyzing the logs of the agent. Log analysis involves discretion, and we may have identified failures that fit our expectations more readily than ones that did not. We release the full expert reviews, survey responses, agent repositories, and run logs so readers can check our interpretation against the raw materials.

最后，我们计划继续利用新的研究来评估人工智能研发自动化，我们欢迎后续研究的反馈和对抗性合作。Finally, we plan to continue evaluating AI R&D automation using new research, and we welcome feedback and adversarial collaborations for follow-up studies.

A.1 角色制图A.1 Persona Cartography

研究问题Research Question：可以使用权重空间干预将大型语言模型（LLM）角色分解、测量和控制为结构化“特质空间”中的位置吗？: Can large language model (LLM) personas be decomposed, measured, and controlled as positions in a structured "trait space" using weight-space interventions?

相关背景Relevant Context：法学硕士通常表现出稳定的行为模式（“角色”），这些模式会影响他们在分布外和微调后的泛化方式，这些模式出于安全原因非常重要，例如调节模型在训练期间奖励黑客或采取未经批准的行动的倾向。当前的控制方法要么脆弱（提示、转向），要么昂贵/不灵活（全面再培训）。我们缺乏将角色分解为可独立控制的组件、严格测量它们并组合它们的工具，除了激活转向的情况，它由于多种原因而存在缺陷。智能体应该产生（a）一种使用基于权重而不是基于激活的干预措施来诱导目标行为转变的方法，（b）有关诱导维度是否独立/可组合的证据，以及（c）至少一次测试这些维度是否影响智能体未直接训练的下游行为。: LLMs often exhibit stable behavioral patterns ("personas") that affect how they generalize out of distribution and after fine-tuning, and these patterns are important for safety reasons, modulating, for instance, the model’s propensity to reward hack or take unsanctioned actions during training. Current control methods are either brittle (prompting, steering) or expensive/inflexible (full retraining). We lack tools to decompose personas into independently controllable components, measure them rigorously, and compose them, except in the case of activation steering, which is flawed for a variety of reasons. The agent should produce (a) a method for inducing targeted behavioral shifts using weight-based, rather than activation-based, interventions, (b) evidence about whether the induced dimensions are independent/composable, and (c) at least one test of whether these dimensions affect a downstream behavior the agent didn’t directly train for.

A.2 TabPFN

研究问题Research Question：设计、理论上证明并实证验证部署时间检测器，在给定 (a) PFN、(b) 标记的分布内参考批次和 (c) 未标记的部署批次的情况下，当 PFN 在部署批次上的准确性比 ID 上的准确性严重差时，输出校准警报。您应该假设白盒访问模型：梯度和中间激活可用；预训练数据则不然。解决方案的路径是开放的。无论您选择哪种解决方案路径，贡献都应该取决于为什么 PFN 使您的方法发挥作用，即为什么相同的想法在聚合的 XGBoost 或微调的 MLP 上是不可能/不适用或者更糟糕。: Design, theoretically justify, and empirically validate a deployment-time detector that, given (a) a PFN, (b) a labeled in-distribution reference batch, and (c) an unlabeled deployment batch, outputs a calibrated alarm when the PFN’s accuracy on the deployment batch is materially worse than on ID.
