1 简介Introduction

以视觉为中心的自动驾驶因其经济优势和可扩展性而成为一个有前途的方向Vision-centric autonomous driving is a promising direction due to its economic advantages and scalability[37, 21, 67, 60, 19, 27]。 传统方法通常遵循模块化的感知流程. Conventional methods typically follow a modular pipeline of perception[20, 41, 22, 23, 86], 预测, prediction[79, 66, 87, 82]，以及规划, and planning[19, 27, 80, 2, 81]，它严重依赖昂贵的 3D 注释，因此在实际应用中面临限制。 最近，视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型的出现，利用大型语言模型中丰富的世界知识将视觉输入映射到驾驶动作, which heavily relies on expensive 3D annotations and thus faces limitations in real-world applications. Recently, vision-language-action (VLA) models have emerged to leverage rich world knowledge from large language models to map visual inputs to driving actions[62, 25, 72, 13]，展示了跨驾驶场景的卓越泛化能力。, demonstrating remarkable generalization across driving scenarios.

尽管在驾驶场景中具有良好的泛化能力，但大多数现有的 VLA 模型仅使用场景描述或决策推理的语言，缺乏灵活的指令跟踪能力Despite their good generalization across driving scenarios, most existing VLA models only use languages for scene descriptions or decision reasoning and lack flexible instruction-following capabilities[84, 13, 85, 75, 35]。 它们要么接受过模仿平均专家策略的训练，要么仅限于一组封闭的简单导航命令，例如“左转”或“直行”，无法概括为开放式且灵活的自然语言指令。 相比之下，通用驾驶代理不仅应该自主导航，还应该理解和执行各种用户指定的自然语言指令。 例如，赶时间的用户可能会指示车辆“超越前车以赶下一个绿灯”，而不是遵循从训练数据中学到的保守策略。. They are either trained to imitate an averaged expert policy, or are confined to a closed set of simple navigational commands like “turn left” or “go straight”, failing to generalize to open-ended and flexible natural language instructions. In contrast, a general driving agent should not only navigate autonomously but also comprehend and execute diverse, user-specified natural language instructions. For instance, a user in a hurry might instruct the vehicle to “overtake the front car to catch the next green light” rather than adhere to the conservative policy learned from the training data.

为了促进从模仿驾驶到教学驾驶的转变，我们构建了一个大规模的驾驶数据集，To facilitate the shift from imitation driving to instructional driving, we construct a large-scale driving dataset,指导场景InstructScene，大约有 100,000 个带指令注释的场景以及基于 NAVSIM 构建的相应轨迹, with around 100,000 instruction-annotated scenes and the corresponding trajectories built on NAVSIM[5]。 虽然直接的方法是在包含丰富指令的驾驶数据集上训练 VLA 模型，但我们发现它很难生成可行的轨迹并准确地遵循指令。 我们认为这是由于高维视觉指令输入和低维动作预测之间存在显着的信息差异，使得模型很难在复杂和动态的环境中学习从高级指令到低级动作的可概括映射。. While a direct way is to train a VLA model on our driving dataset containing rich instructions, we find that it struggles to generate feasible trajectories and follow instructions accurately. We think this is due to the significant information disparity between the high-dimensional visual-instruction inputs and the low-dimensional action prediction, making it difficult for the model to learn a generalizable mapping from high-level instructions to low-level actions in complex and dynamic environments.

2.2 自动驾驶的世界模型World Models for Autonomous Driving

世界模型通常被定义为根据过去的观察和当前行动预测未来状态的生成模型World models are typically defined as generative models that predict future states conditioned on past observations and current actions[16]。 在自动驾驶中，世界模型的应用可以分为三种主要方法：基于图像的方法、基于占用的方法和基于VLA的方法。基于图像的方法利用强大的生成架构来合成高保真驾驶视频，主要用于数据生成和场景模拟. In autonomous driving, applications of world models can be categorized into three main approaches: image-based, occupancy-based, and VLA-based methods. Image-based methods leverage powerful generative architectures to synthesize high-fidelity driving videos, primarily for data generation and scene simulation[18, 54, 64, 78, 14, 65]。 基于占用的方法对 3D 占用空间中的场景演化进行建模，以增强场景理解. Occupancy-based methods model scene evolution in 3D occupancy space to enhance scene understanding[66, 47, 87]和规划and planning[79, 66, 74, 31]，但它们对密集 3D 标签的依赖限制了可扩展性。 最近，基于 VLA 的方法随 Doe-1 一起出现, but their reliance on dense 3D labels limits scalability. 2.3 统一的视觉理解和生成Unified Visual Understanding and Generation

统一的视觉理解和生成方法可以分为三个主要流程：量化自回归（AR）、外部扩散和集成变压器。 量化 AR 模型将图像量化为离散标记Unified visual understanding and generation methods can be categorized into three main pipelines: quantized autoregressive (AR), external diffusion, and integrated transformers. Quantized AR models quantize images into discrete tokens[30, 77]，在本机自回归框架内启用生成, enabling generation within the native autoregressive framework[69, 3, 42, 51, 70, 71, 59, 63]。 虽然这种设计很简单，但其视觉质量通常落后于基于扩散的方法。 外部扩散器方法将 VLM 与外部扩散模型配对. While this design is straightforward, its visual quality typically lags behind that of diffusion-based methods. The External Diffuser approach pairs a VLM with an external diffusion model[9, 15, 58, 61]。 VLM 通过生成一些调节扩散生成器的潜在标记来提供高级理解。 然而，理解和生成之间的狭窄界面可能会限制信息流动. The VLM provides a high-level understanding by generating a few latent tokens that condition the diffusion generator. However, this narrow interface between understanding and generation can restrict information flow[6]。 集成变压器模型将自回归和扩散机制合并到单个变压器中. Integrated transformer models merge autoregressive and diffusion mechanisms into a single transformer[44, 56, 83, 6, 38]，实现强大的理解和生成能力的深度融合。 在本文中，我们采用集成变压器来实现基于指令的联合视觉生成和动作规划。, enabling a deep integration of powerful understanding and generation capabilities. In this paper, we adopt the integrated transformer to achieve instruction-based joint visual generation and action planning.

3.3 联合自回归-扩散架构Joint Autoregressive-Diffusion Architecture

统一的生成和规划要求我们的模型不仅具有显着的视觉文本理解、视觉生成和动作规划能力，而且还要集成它们来解决复杂的驾驶场景。 目前的研究主要采用三种方法来弥合视觉文本理解（主要使用自回归 VLM）和图像生成（通常采用扩散模型）之间的差距。 然而，大多数方法都达不到我们的要求。 具有离散视觉分词器的自回归视觉生成模型很难在图像质量上与扩散模型相匹配，并且由于其顺序生成管道而还存在高延迟。 LLM 与外部扩散器相结合产生了有竞争力的结果，但受到从 LLM 传递到生成模块的潜在令牌数量有限导致的信息瓶颈的限制。 为了解决这些问题，我们采用集成变压器架构Unified generation and planning requires our model to not only possess significant visual-text understanding, visual generation, and action planning capabilities, but also integrates them to solve complex driving scenarios. Current research mainly follows three approaches to bridge the gap between visual-text-understanding, which primarily uses auto-regressive VLM, and image generation, which often adopts diffusion models. However, most methods fall short of our requirements. Autoregressive visual generation models with discrete visual tokenizers struggle to match diffusion models in image quality and also suffer from high latency due to their sequential generation pipeline. LLMs combined with external diffusers yield competitive results, but are constrained by an information bottleneck caused by the limited number of latent tokens passed from LLMs to generation modules. To address these, we adopt the Integrated Transformer architecture[6]，它将自回归VLM和扩散变压器融合到一个模型中，使生成模块能够与理解模块交互，而不会丢失信息，从而实现统一的理解和生成能力。, which fuses auto-regressive VLM and diffusion transformer into a single model, enabling the generation module to interact with the understanding module without information loss and resulting in unified understanding and generation capabilities.

我们的集成模型采用统一的范式来预测图像和动作。它首先对多模态输入进行编码，包括文本、图像和动作，并将它们与目标图像或动作的噪声连接起来，形成统一的序列。 然后，该模型将序列作为一个整体进行处理，计算跨模态的因果注意力，以确保文本、图像和潜在动作之间的完整信息流。 最后，去噪后的潜在信息被各自的解码器解码为图像或动作。Our integrated model employs a unified paradigm to predict images and actions. The model then processes the sequence as a whole, calculating causal attention across modalities to ensure full information flow among text, image, and action latents. Finally, the denoised latents are decoded by their respective decoders into images or actions.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们旨在解决当前驾驶模型无法遵循多样化驾驶指令的问题。 我们已经介绍了In this paper, we have aimed to address current driving models’ inability to follow diverse driving instructions. We have introduced织女星Vega，一个统一的视觉-语言-世界-动作模型，通过利用未来的视觉生成作为密集的监督信号来弥合这一差距。 我们建立了一个带有指令注释的大规模驾驶数据集，以实现教学驾驶的训练。 通过联合生成符合指令的未来图像和规划动作，该模型可以学习指令、动作和视觉结果之间的因果关系。 我们的模型建立在集成变压器架构和指令注释数据集的基础上，实现了 SOTA 规划性能，同时在视觉生成和行动规划方面展示了强大的指令跟踪能力。, a unified vision-language-world-action model that bridges this gap by leveraging future visual generation as a dense supervision signal. We have built a large-scale driving dataset with instruction annotations to enable the training for instructional driving. By jointly generating instruction-compliant future images and planning actions, the model learns the causal relationships among instructions, actions, and the visual outcomes. Built upon an integrated transformer architecture and an instruction-annotated dataset, our model achieves SOTA planning performance while demonstrating strong instruction-following capabilities in both visual generation and action planning.
