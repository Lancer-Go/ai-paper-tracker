当公平指标不一致时：评估机器学习中人口统计公平性评估的可靠性When Fairness Metrics Disagree: Evaluating the Reliability of Demographic Fairness Assessment in Machine Learning

哈立德·阿德南·阿尔赛义德Khalid Adnan Alsayed英国米德尔斯堡计算、工程与数字技术学院。School of Computing, Engineering & Digital Technologies, Middlesbrough, Uk. F5044605@live.tees.ac.uk s.khalid.adnan@gmail.com

摘要Abstract– 机器学习系统的公平性评估已成为高风险应用中的一个核心问题，包括生物特征识别、医疗保健决策和自动风险评估。现有方法通常依赖于少量的公平性指标来评估跨组分区的模型行为，隐含地假设这些指标提供一致且可靠的结论。然而，不同的公平性指标捕获模型性能的不同统计属性，因此在应用于同一系统时可能会产生相互矛盾的评估。–The evaluation of fairness in machine learning systems has become a central concern in highstakes applications, including biometric recognition, healthcare decision-making, and automated risk assessment. Existing approaches typically rely on a small number of fairness metrics to assess model behaviour across group partitions, implicitly assuming that these metrics provide consistent and reliable conclusions. However, different fairness metrics capture distinct statistical properties of model performance and may therefore produce conflicting assessments when applied to the same system.

在这项工作中，我们通过对机器学习模型中的人口统计偏差进行系统的多指标分析来调查公平性评估的一致性。使用人脸识别作为受控实验设置，我们在一系列常用的公平性指标（包括错误率差异和基于性能的指标）下评估跨多个组分区的模型性能。我们的结果表明，根据指标的选择，公平性评估可能会有很大差异，从而导致关于模型偏差的矛盾结论。In this work, we investigate the consistency of fairness evaluation by conducting a systematic multimetric analysis of demographic bias in machine learning models. Using face recognition as a controlled experimental setting, we evaluate model performance across multiple group partitions under a range of commonly used fairness metrics, including error-rate disparities and performance-based measures. Our results demonstrate that fairness assessments can vary significantly depending on the choice of metrics, leading to contradictory conclusions regarding model bias.

为了量化这种现象，我们引入了公平分歧指数（FDI），该指标旨在捕捉公平指标之间的不一致程度。我们进一步表明，阈值和模型配置方面的分歧仍然很大。这些发现凸显了当前公平性评估实践的一个关键局限性，并表明单一指标报告不足以进行可靠的偏见评估。To quantify this phenomenon, we introduce the Fairness Disagreement Index (FDI), a measure designed to capture the degree of inconsistency across fairness metrics. We further show that disagreement remains high across thresholds and model configurations. These findings highlight a critical limitation in current fairness evaluation practices and suggest that single-metric reporting is insufficient for reliable bias assessment.

一、简介1. Introduction

机器学习的最新进展导致人工智能系统在生物识别、医疗保健和刑事司法等高风险领域得到广泛部署。随着这些系统越来越多地影响关键决策，对公平性和人口统计偏见的担忧已成为研究的中心焦点[1]、[2]、[11]，之前的工作强调了人口统计属性之间的差异。大量研究表明，机器学习模型可以在不同群体划分的性能上表现出系统性差异，特别是在人脸识别系统中，不同种族、年龄和性别之间的错误率差异已被广泛报道[1]、[6]、[12]。Recent advances in machine learning have led to widespread deployment of AI systems in highstakes domains, including biometric identification, healthcare, and criminal justice. As these systems increasingly influence critical decisions, concerns regarding fairness and demographic bias have become a central focus of research [1], [2], [11], with prior work highlighting disparities across demographic attributes. Numerous studies have demonstrated that machine learning models can exhibit systematic disparities in performance across group partitions, particularly in face recognition systems, where differences in error rates across race, age, and gender have been widely reported [1], [6], [12].

为了解决这些问题，人们提出了各种公平性指标来评估跨群体的模型行为。其中包括基于误差的衡量标准，例如假阳性率 (FPR) 和假阴性率 (FNR) 差异，以及更广泛的统计标准，例如人口统计均等、均等赔率和预测均等 [2]、[4]、[13]。虽然这些指标提供了不同的视角To address these concerns, a variety of fairness metrics have been proposed to evaluate model behaviour across groups. These include error-based measures such as false positive rate (FPR) and false negative rate (FNR) disparities, as well as broader statistical criteria such as demographic parity, equalized odds, and predictive parity [2], [4], [13]. While these metrics provide different perspectives

尽管如此，当前的评估实践依赖于公平性指标的有限子集，隐含地假设这些指标提供了一致且可靠的模型偏差评估。在人脸识别的背景下，最近的基准测试工作强调了分组评估的重要性和总体绩效指标的局限性[6]。此外，先前的研究表明，绩效差异可能会因群体的定义和评估方式而异 [1]。Despite this, current evaluation practices rely on a limited subset of fairness metrics, implicitly assuming these measures provide consistent and reliable assessments of model bias. In the context of face recognition, recent benchmarking efforts have highlighted the importance of subgroup evaluation and the limitations of aggregate performance metrics [6]. Furthermore, prior work has shown that performance disparities can vary depending on how groups are defined and evaluated [1].

然而，一个重要的问题在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索：在评估同一模型时，不同的公平性指标是否一致？However, an important question remains largely underexplored: Do different fairness metrics agree when evaluating the same model?

这个问题至关重要，因为相互冲突的结果可能会导致关于系统公平性的不一致或误导性结论。虽然先前的工作已经承认公平定义之间的理论权衡[7]，但对这种不一致在实际评估环境中表现出来的程度的实证研究有限。This question is critical, as conflicting outcomes can lead to inconsistent or misleading conclusions about system fairness. While prior work has acknowledged theoretical trade-offs between fairness definitions [7], there has been limited empirical investigation into the extent to which such inconsistencies manifest in practical evaluation settings.

在本文中，我们通过对机器学习系统中的度量分歧进行系统研究来解决这一差距。使用人脸识别作为受控实验域，我们在一组不同的常用公平性指标下评估跨多个组分区的模型性能。我们证明，根据所选指标的不同，公平性评估可能会有很大差异，并且在不同的分组策略和不同的决策阈值下，这些差异会进一步加剧[15]。In this paper, we address this gap by conducting a systematic study of metric disagreement in machine learning systems. Using face recognition as a controlled experimental domain, we evaluate model performance across multiple group partitions under a diverse set of commonly used fairness metrics. We demonstrate that fairness assessments can vary significantly depending on the chosen metric, and that these discrepancies are further exacerbated under different grouping strategies and varying decision thresholds [15].

为了捕捉这一现象，我们引入了公平分歧指数（FDI），这是一种量化指标，旨在评估公平指标之间的不一致程度。我们的研究结果表明，在当前实践中，公平性评估本质上是不稳定的，这引发了有关单一指标报告的可靠性和机器学习系统中公平性声明的解释的重要问题。To capture this phenomenon, we introduce the Fairness Disagreement Index (FDI), a quantitative measure designed to assess the degree of inconsistency across fairness metrics. Our findings reveal that fairness evaluation is inherently unstable under current practices, raising important questions about the reliability of single-metric reporting and the interpretation of fairness claims in machine learning systems.

2. 方法论2. Methodology

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个用于评估机器学习系统中公平性评估一致性的框架。与依赖单一公平性指标的传统方法不同，所提出的框架同时考虑多个指标并量化它们之间的一致性程度。In this work, we propose a framework for evaluating the consistency of fairness assessments in machine learning systems. Unlike conventional approaches that rely on single fairness metrics, the proposed framework considers multiple metrics simultaneously and quantifies the degree of agreement between them.

总体评估流程由四个主要阶段组成。首先，将经过训练的模型应用于包含面部图像和相关身份标签的数据集以生成预测分数。然后使用这些分数来计算预定义组分区的性能结果。其次，应用一组不同的公平性指标来量化这些群体之间模型行为的差异。第三，对这些指标的输出进行联合分析，以评估它们产生一致或冲突评估的程度。最后，计算拟议的公平分歧指数（FDI），以提供跨指标不一致的定量衡量标准。The overall evaluation pipeline consists of four main stages. First, a trained model is applied to a dataset containing facial images and associated identity labels to generate prediction scores. These scores are then used to compute performance outcomes across predefined group partitions. Second, a diverse set of fairness metrics are applied to quantify disparities in model behaviour across these groups. Third, the outputs of these metrics are analysed jointly to assess the extent to which they produce consistent or conflicting evaluations. Finally, the proposed Fairness Disagreement Index (FDI) is computed to provide a quantitative measure of inconsistency across metrics.

为了进一步研究公平性评估的稳健性，该框架包含两个附加组件。首先，进行阈值敏感性分析，以检查公平性评估在不同决策阈值之间的差异。其次，进行基于群体的分析，以评估替代群体定义的公平性，从而能够对偏见进行更细粒度的评估。To further investigate the robustness of fairness evaluation, the framework incorporates two additional components. First, a threshold sensitivity analysis is performed to examine how fairness assessments vary across different decision thresholds. Second, a group-based analysis is conducted to evaluate fairness across alternative group definitions, enabling a more fine-grained assessment of bias.

3.6 公平性指标3.6 Fairness Metrics

我们使用一组跨三个类别的常用指标来评估公平性：We evaluate fairness using a set of commonly used metrics across three categories:

  • 基于误差的指标：FPR 差异和 FNR 差异。Error-based metrics: FPR disparity and FNR disparity.

  • 基于性能的指标：准确度差异和最差组准确度。Performance-based metrics: Accuracy disparity and worst-group accuracy.

  • 基于分布的指标：使用 Wasserstein 距离对分布散度进行评分。Distribution-based metrics: Score distribution divergence using Wasserstein distance.

这些指标捕获模型行为的不同方面，并广泛用于公平性评估研究 [2]、[3]。These metrics capture different aspects of model behaviour and are widely used in fairness evaluation studies [2], [3].

虽然可以纳入其他公平性定义，例如人口均等、均等赔率和预测均等，但所选指标提供了一个代表集，捕获基于错误和基于绩效的差异。这项工作的重点是度量分歧，而不是详尽覆盖所有可能的公平性定义。While additional fairness definitions such as demographic parity, equalized odds, and predictive parity could be incorporated, the selected metrics provide a representative set capturing both error-based and performance-based disparities. The focus of this work is on metric disagreement rather than exhaustive coverage of all possible fairness definitions.

5. 结论5. Conclusion

在本文中，我们通过分析多个公平性指标的一致性来研究机器学习系统中公平性评估的可靠性。虽然现有方法通常依赖于一组有限的指标，但我们的结果表明，当应用于相同的模型和数据集时，不同的公平性度量可能会产生相互矛盾的评估。In this paper, we investigated the reliability of fairness evaluation in machine learning systems by analysing the consistency of multiple fairness metrics. While existing approaches typically rely on a limited set of metrics, our results demonstrate that different fairness measures can produce conflicting assessments when applied to the same model and dataset.

通过系统的实验分析，我们表明公平性结论不仅因指标而异，而且因决策阈值和群体定义而异。基于群体的评估揭示了更明显的差异，阈值依赖分析凸显了不同运营条件下公平评估的不稳定性。Through a systematic experimental analysis, we showed that fairness conclusions vary not only across metrics, but also across decision thresholds and group definitions. Group-based evaluation revealed more pronounced disparities, and threshold-dependent analysis highlighted the instability of fairness assessments under different operating conditions.

为了解决这个问题，我们引入了公平分歧指数（FDI），这是一种量化指标，旨在捕捉公平指标之间的不一致程度。结果表明，分歧是不可忽视的，并且在许多情况下是巨大的，这表明不能使用单一指标来可靠地描述公平性评估。To address this issue, we introduced the Fairness Disagreement Index (FDI), a quantitative measure designed to capture the degree of inconsistency across fairness metrics. The results showed that disagreement is non-negligible and, in many cases, substantial, indicating that fairness evaluation cannot be reliably characterised using a single metric.

这些发现表明，公平性应被视为机器学习系统的多维属性，而不是单值度量。拟议的框架提供了一种结构化方法，用于识别公平性评估中的不一致之处，并为迈向更稳健和透明的评估实践迈出了一步。These findings suggest that fairness should be treated as a multi-dimensional property of machine learning systems, rather than a single-value measure. The proposed framework provides a structured approach for identifying inconsistencies in fairness evaluation and offers a step towards more robust and transparent assessment practices.

6. 局限性和未来的工作6. Limitations and Future Work

尽管做出了这些贡献，但仍然存在一些局限性。Despite these contributions, several limitations remain.

首先，这项工作中的实验是在面部识别领域进行的，虽然代表了高风险应用程序，但可能无法完全捕获其他机器学习领域中公平性指标的行为。未来的工作应该将提议的框架扩展到其他任务，例如医疗预测和决策支持系统。First, the experiments in this work are conducted within the domain of facial recognition, which, while representative of high-stakes applications, may not fully capture the behaviour of fairness metrics in other machine learning domains. Future work should extend the proposed framework to additional tasks such as healthcare prediction and decision-support systems.

其次，分析重点关注一组特定的常用公平性指标。虽然这些指标被广泛采用，但其他公平性定义和评估标准可能会表现出不同的分歧模式。扩展度量集可以提供对公平不一致的更全面的理解。Second, the analysis focuses on a specific set of commonly used fairness metrics. While these metrics are widely adopted, other fairness definitions and evaluation criteria may exhibit different disagreement patterns. Expanding the metric set could provide a more comprehensive understanding of fairness inconsistency.

13

第三，拟议的外国直接投资汇总了各种指标的分歧，但没有明确考虑各个公平指标的相对重要性或可解释性。未来的工作可以探索索引的加权或上下文感知扩展。Third, the proposed FDI aggregates disagreement across metrics but does not explicitly account for the relative importance or interpretability of individual fairness measures. Future work could explore weighted or context-aware extensions of the index.

进一步的工作应结合统计验证技术，例如自举重采样或置信区间估计，以评估在阈值和模型之间观察到的公平性分歧差异的显着性。Further work should incorporate statistical validation techniques, such as bootstrap resampling or confidence interval estimation, to assess the significance of observed differences in fairness disagreement across thresholds and models.

最后，虽然阈值敏感性分析强调了公平性结论的不稳定性，但仍需要进一步研究来开发原则性方法来选择平衡性能和公平性考虑的操作阈值。Finally, while threshold sensitivity analysis highlights instability in fairness conclusions, further investigation is needed to develop principled methods for selecting operating thresholds that balance performance and fairness considerations.
