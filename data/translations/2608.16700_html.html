1. 简介Introduction

随着社会的进步，可用数据量显着增加。这种增长使我们能够访问众多个人在线共享的广泛数据，丰富了我们的生活。然而，这也给用户的隐私带来了风险。为此，出台了CCPA（加州消费者隐私法）、PIPEDA（个人信息保护和电子文档法）和GDPR（通用数据保护条例）等立法措施，通过删除数据的权利来建立保护用户隐私的规则。从机器学习模型中删除数据的任务称为机器取消学习With the progress of society, there has been a significant increase in the amount of available data. This growth has enriched our lives by allowing us to access a wide range of data shared by numerous individuals online. However, it also presents risks to the privacy of users. In response to this, legislative measures like CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act), and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) have been introduced to establish rules that protect users’ privacy by the right to delete their data. The task of erasing data from a machine learning model is named machine unlearning(4; 1; 22).

一种简单的方法是在剩余数据上重新训练模型，但这很昂贵，因为模型通常是在非常大的数据集上训练的。近年来，提出高效且有效的机器去学习方法引起了研究人员的关注。A naive approach is to retrain the model on the remaining data, but this is expensive as models are often trained on very large datasets. Proposing efficient and effective machine unlearning methods has attracted the attention of researchers in recent years.

当前的方法侧重于设计复杂的遗忘功能Current approaches focus on designing a complex unlearning functionUU使得未学习的模型的表现接近重新训练的模型。 例如，一些方法设计so that the unlearned model performs close to the retrained model. For example, some methods designUU作为对全部或部分数据的重新训练，这是非常耗时的，并且每次都需要针对取消学习的请求进行重新训练。一些方法需要计算 Hessian 矩阵来设计as a retraining on all or part of the data, which is very time-consuming and requires retraining every time on an unlearning request. Some methods necessitate the calculation of the Hessian matrix for the designedUU，这进一步增加了计算成本。因此，即使对于相对简单的模型，遗忘效率仍然受到设计的复杂性的瓶颈。, which further increases the computational cost. Consequently, even for relatively simple models, the unlearning efficiency remains bottlenecked by the complexity of the designedUU，尤其是在浏览海量数据集时。, especially when navigating massive datasets.

遗忘函数的目标The goal of an unlearning functionUU就是生成一个模型，该模型已经忘记了用户数据，并且性能接近于对剩余数据进行重新训练的模型。is to produce a model that has unlearned the user data and performs close to the model retrained on the remaining data.为什么我们不学会直接忘却呢？Why don’t we learn to unlearn directly?

受到学习优化 (L2O) 的启发Inspired by Learning to Optimize (L2O)(6; 27)，像Adam这样的传统优化算法是由人类专家通过理论推导手动设计的。相比之下，L2O 方法旨在让机器自动学习如何优化。具体来说，L2O模型将优化迭代的当前状态作为输入（例如当前解及其梯度），并学习预测下一次迭代的更新量。, traditional optimization algorithms like Adam are manually designed by human experts through theoretical derivation. In contrast, the L2O approach aims to let machines automatically learn how to optimize. Specifically, the L2O model takes the current state of the optimization iteration as input (such as the current solution and its gradient) and learns to predict the update amount for the next iteration.而不是设计一个复杂且非常耗时的Instead of designing a complex and highly time-consumingUU，我们使用重新训练的模型作为基本事实来学习, we use the retrained model as ground truth to learn aUU其结构简单但足以忘记whose structure is simple but enough to unlearnDfD_{f}有效地effectively。这样，我们就能更有效地达到忘却学习的目的。 尽管许多方法声称学习是为了忘记. 8.3. 细分机器取消学习任务Subdivide the machine unlearning task

当前的机器遗忘方法通常将遗忘任务视为一个整体问题，忽略了模型复杂性和数据规模固有的多样性。我们主张采取更细致、针对特定任务的方法。由于遗忘任务是由模型和训练数据共同定义的，因此我们提出了一种分类法，根据模型大小和数据集大小将机器遗忘分为四种不同的场景：Current machine unlearning methods often treat forgetting tasks as a monolithic problem, neglecting the inherent diversity in model complexity and data scale. We argue for a more nuanced, task-specific approach. Since a forgetting task is defined by both the model and the training data, we propose a taxonomy that categorizes machine unlearning into four distinct scenarios based on model size and dataset size:

1）小数据集上的简单模型。这代表了最直接的场景，从头开始重新训练在计算上仍然可行，并且通常是首选解决方案。1) Simple models on small datasets. This represents the most straightforward scenario, where retraining from scratch remains computationally feasible and is often the preferred solution.

2）小数据集上的复杂模型。尽管训练数据有限，但模型复杂性的增加显着提高了重新训练的成本，使其成为不切实际的选择。2) Complex models on small datasets. Although the training data is limited, the increased model complexity significantly raises the cost of retraining, making it an impractical choice.

3）大型数据集上的简单模型。这是我们工作的重点场景。由于模型本身是轻量级的，因此应用为复杂架构设计的复杂的遗忘技术将不必要地占用资源——本质上是矫枉过正。相反，一种轻量级的方法，比如我们提出的方法，足以实现有效的遗忘。3) Simple models on large datasets. This is the focal scenario of our work. Because the model itself is lightweight, applying sophisticated unlearning techniques designed for complex architectures would be unnecessarily resource-intensive—essentially overkill. Instead, a lightweight method, such as the one we propose, is sufficient to achieve effective forgetting.

4）大型数据集上的复杂模型（例如大型语言模型）。这是最具挑战性的场景，需要先进且可扩展的忘却策略。4) Complex models on large datasets (e.g., large language models). This constitutes the most challenging scenario, demanding advanced and scalable unlearning strategies.

这种分类法的关键优势在于其战略灵活性：通过将遗忘方法与任务的具体复杂性相匹配，我们可以实现高效的数据删除，而不会产生过高的计算成本，确保我们既不会“用大锤砸坚果”，也不会为苛刻的场景部署不充分的解决方案。The key advantage of this taxonomy lies in its strategic flexibility: by matching the unlearning method to the specific complexity of the task, we can achieve efficient data removal without incurring prohibitive computational costs, ensuring we neither "use a sledgehammer to crack a nut" nor deploy inadequate solutions for demanding scenarios.

9. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，据我们所知，我们提出了 Learning-to-UnLearn (L2UL)，这是第一个通过学习忘却行为而不是手动设计复杂函数来解决机器忘却问题的框架。通过优先考虑面向分布的视角，L2UL 实现了显着简化且强大的遗忘能力。针对模型简单但数据训练数据集庞大的场景，我们利用逻辑回归和多层感知器来演示 L2UL 以超快的速度处理海量数据集且不损失准确性的能力。理论和实证结果都证明L2UL在效率上显着优于现有方法。此外，对污染模型的测试和敏感性分析证实了 L2UL 在各种配置中的有效性和稳定性。最后，ResNet 上的成功验证强调了 L2UL 到更复杂模型的可扩展性，为现代隐私要求提供了强大而高效的解决方案。In this paper, we propose Learning-to-UnLearn (L2UL), to the best of our knowledge, the first framework to address machine unlearning by learning the unlearning behaviors rather than manually designing complex functions. By prioritizing a distribution-oriented perspective, L2UL achieves a remarkably streamlined yet potent unlearning capability. Focusing on scenarios where the model is simple but the data training dataset is large, we utilized Logistic Regression and Multilayer Perceptrons to demonstrate the ability of L2UL to handle massive datasets with exceptional speed and no loss in accuracy. Both theoretical and empirical results prove that L2UL significantly outperforms existing methods in efficiency. Furthermore, tests on contaminated models and sensitivity analyses confirm the effectiveness and stability of L2UL across various configurations. Finally, successful validation on ResNet underscores the scalability of L2UL to more complex models, offering a robust and efficient solution for modern privacy requirements.
