摘要Abstract

机械连杆设计涉及组合拓扑选择和连续参数拟合。我们证明语言模型可以通过符号表示系统地改进链接设计。语言模型代理探索离散拓扑，而数值优化器则拟合连续参数。符号提升算子将模拟器轨迹转换为定性描述符、运动标签、时间谓词和结构诊断，以便模型在迭代设计周期中进行解释。在六个与工程相关的运动目标和三个开源模型（Llama 3.3 70B、Qwen3 4B、Qwen3 MoE 30B-A3B）中，模块化架构比整体基线减少了高达 68% 的几何误差，并将结构有效性提高了高达 134%。至关重要的是，78.6% 的迭代细化轨迹显示出可测量的改进，系统正确诊断过度约束 (56.3%) 和欠约束 (35.6%) 故障模式并提出接地修正。所有三个系列的模型无需微调即可获得可解释的机械推理策略，这表明有原则的符号抽象连接了生成式人工智能和工程设计所需的数值精度。Designing mechanical linkages involves combinatorial topology selection and continuous parameter fitting. We show that language models can systematically improve linkage designs through symbolic representations. Language model agents explore discrete topologies while numerical optimisers fit continuous parameters. A symbolic lifting operator translates simulator trajectories into qualitative descriptors, motion labels, temporal predicates, and structural diagnostics that models interpret across iterative design cycles. Across six engineering-relevant motion targets and three open-source models (Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen3 4B, Qwen3 MoE 30B-A3B), the modular architecture reduces geometric error by up to 68% and improves structural validity by up to 134% over monolithic baselines. Critically, 78.6% of iterative refinement trajectories show measurable improvement, with the system correctly diagnosing overconstraint (56.3%) and underconstraint (35.6%) failure modes and proposing grounded corrections. Models across all three families acquire interpretable mechanical reasoning strategies without fine-tuning, demonstrating that principled symbolic abstraction bridges generative AI and the numerical precision required for engineering design.

1 简介1 Introduction

机械连杆是通过关节连接的刚体系统，可将输入运动转换为所需的输出路径，在工程系统中无处不在：从汽车悬架和手术机器人到可部署的航空航天结构 [1, 2]。设计连接来追踪规定的轨迹是一个长期存在的挑战，涉及两个必须共同解决的紧密耦合的子问题[8]。第一个是Mechanical linkages are systems of rigid bodies connected by joints that convert input motion into a desired output path, and are ubiquitous in engineering systems: from automotive suspensions and surgical robots to deployable aerospace structures [1, 2]. Designing linkages to trace prescribed trajectories is a long-standing challenge that involves two tightly coupled subproblems that must be solved jointly [8]. The first is a组合的combinatorial决策：选择要使用多少个连杆和接头以及如何连接它们（decision: choosing how many links and joints to use and how to connect them (the拓扑结构topology）。第二个是一个). The second is a连续的continuous拟合任务：选择连杆长度、关节偏移和曲柄角度，使末端执行器路径与目标曲线匹配。传统方法通过数学优化来解决这两个子问题[3]，但是可能的拓扑的组合爆炸使得穷举搜索变得不切实际，并且基于梯度的拟合可能会陷入局部最小值。fitting task: selecting link lengths, joint offsets, and crank angles so that the end-effector path matches a target curve. Conventional methods address both subproblems with mathematical optimisation [3], but the combinatorial explosion of possible topologies makes exhaustive search impractical, and gradient-based fitting can stall in local minima.

我们的方法弥补了机制综合领域的两个空白。一方面，经典数学优化 [1,2,3] 和最近的 ML 驱动方法 [22,17,24,26] 执行有效的连续拟合，但缺乏基于类比的提议机制来导航组合拓扑空间。另一方面，神经符号方法[16, 11]已经证明了神经推理和符号推理相结合的价值，但尚未应用于经过验证的物理约束的工程综合。Our approach bridges two gaps in the mechanism synthesis landscape. On one hand, classical mathematical optimisation [1, 2, 3] and recent ML-driven approaches [22, 17, 24, 26] perform effective continuous fitting but lack analogy-based proposal mechanisms for navigating combinatorial topology spaces. On the other hand, neuro-symbolic methods [16, 11] have demonstrated the value of combining neural and symbolic reasoning, but have not been applied to engineering synthesis with verified physical constraints.

我们的工作还涉及机器人技术中的基础语言模型系统。 SayCan [14] 通过技能可供性来建立 LLM 处方，但基于行动选择而不是设计综合。 HuggingGPT [19] 通过控制器模式编排专业模型，这是我们协调器的架构先例，但目​​标是开放式任务而不是受限的工程问题。我们框架的独特贡献是将符号提升与闭环中经过验证的运动学模拟相结合，确保每次设计迭代都是可物理验证的。Our work also relates to grounded language model systems in robotics. SayCan [14] grounds LLM prescriptions through skill affordances, but operates on action selection rather than design synthesis. HuggingGPT [19] orchestrates specialist models via a controller pattern, an architectural precedent for our coordinator, but targets open-ended tasks rather than constrained engineering problems. The distinctive contribution of our framework is the combination of symbolic lifting with validated kinematic simulation in a closed loop, ensuring that every design iteration is physically verifiable.

3.5 局限性和未来方向3.5 Limitations and future directions

应该注意一些限制。首先，我们的评估仅限于平面连杆；扩展到空间（3D）机制将需要更丰富的符号词汇和更复杂的运动解算器。其次，虽然我们通过符号反馈展示了反思性推理，但我们没有对机制设计语料库上的语言模型进行微调；这样做可以进一步提高性能，特别是对于较小的模型。Some limitations should be noted. First, our evaluation is restricted to planar linkages; extending to spatial (3D) mechanisms will require richer symbolic vocabularies and more complex kinematic solvers. Second, while we demonstrated reflective reasoning through symbolic feedback, we did not fine-tune the language models on mechanism design corpora; doing so could further improve performance, especially for smaller models.

正如为了重现性所建议的那样，对开源模型的依赖 [20, 28]，意味着我们的研究结果是完全可重现的。The reliance on open-source models, as recommended for reproducibility [20, 28], means our findings are fully reproducible.

未来的工作应探索：（i）扩展到空间机制和多体动力学； (ii) 与物理信息神经网络集成以进行替代模拟[10]； (iii) 人机交互工作流程，其中符号表示充当人工智能代理和工程师之间的可解释通信层； (iv) 对框架的可解释性属性进行系统评估，使用符号推理轨迹作为设计原理，可以根据工程标准对其进行正式审核。Future work should explore: (i) extension to spatial mechanisms and multi-body dynamics; (ii) integration with physics-informed neural networks for surrogate simulation [10]; (iii) human-in-theloop workflows where the symbolic representations serve as an interpretable communication layer between AI agents and engineers; and (iv) systematic evaluation of the framework’s explainability properties, using the symbolic reasoning traces as design rationales that can be formally audited against engineering standards.

4.1 问题表述4.1 Problem formulation

设计任务是交互式和语言驱动的：给定一个用自然语言表达的运动目标，基于语言模型的代理团队必须提出、评估和迭代地完善平面连接，以便其末端执行器遵循所需的路径。该问题分解为离散组合子问题（选择拓扑：使用哪些连杆和关节，以及如何连接它们）和连续子问题（拟合连杆长度、关节偏移和曲柄角度，以最小化实现轨迹和目标轨迹之间的距离）。The design task is interactive and language-driven: given a motion goal expressed in natural language, a team of language-model-based agents must propose, evaluate, and iteratively refine a planar linkage so that its end-effector follows the desired path. The problem decomposes into a discrete combinatorial subproblem (selecting a topology: which links and joints to use, and how to connect them) and a continuous subproblem (fitting link lengths, joint offsets, and crank angles to minimise the distance between the realised and target trajectories).
