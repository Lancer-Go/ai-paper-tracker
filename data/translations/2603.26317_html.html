摘要Abstract

In the era of foundation-model, fine-tuning with Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) is prevalent, making LoRA merging a promising target. Existing approaches can work in homogeneous settings where all target tasks are classification but often fail when tasks span classification and regression. 1 简介Introduction

现代深度学习模型，包括视觉转换器Modern deep learning models, including vision transformers[16]和 大型基础模型，例如法学硕士and large foundation models such as LLMs[18]和 VLMand VLMs[47, 46]，取得强劲业绩并展示广泛 泛化到未见过的任务。 尽管规模很大，但当目标达到时，这些模型仍然需要针对特定任务进行微调 分布或目标偏离预训练期间涵盖的内容。 然而，传统的完全微调需要大量计算和内存，限制了其 在许多现实环境中的实用性。, achieve strong performance and exhibit broad generalization to unseen tasks. Despite their scale, these models still require task-specific fine-tuning when the target distribution or objective departs from what was covered during pretraining. Conventional full fine-tuning, however, is computationally and memory intensive, limiting its practicality in many real-world settings.

这一挑战推动了参数高效微调（PEFT）的发展 方法This challenge has motivated the development of parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods[27, 54]。 其中，低秩适应（LoRA）. Among them, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)[28]已成为广泛采用的策略 用于适应大型模型，同时保持基本重量冻结。 LoRA 有利于训练和部署，因为它大大减少了 微调过程中可训练参数的数量并产生紧凑的适配器模块 易于通过开源模型中心分发和共享。has become a widely adopted strategy for adapting large models while keeping base weights frozen. LoRA is advantageous for training and deployment, as it substantially reduces the number of trainable parameters during fine-tuning and produces compact adapter modules that are easy to distribute and share through open-source model hubs.

除了单任务适应之外，现代系统还有望处理多个下游任务。传统的解决方案是多任务学习Beyond single-task adaptation, modern systems are expected to handle multiple downstream tasks. The traditional solution is multi-task learning[99, 45, 44, 33, 35, 34, 32, 31]，这需要统一的训练管道和具有对齐的每个任务标签的联合数据集。这在管理和计算方面成本高昂，并且当标签是专有的或受许可证限制时通常不可行。它还使得利用分散在开放模型中心的大量任务专用模型变得困难，因为如果不进行集中再训练，这些检查点就无法直接重用。这些约束激发了模型合并，其中独立微调的检查点被组合而无需重新访问标记数据。在基础模型时代，模型合并对于 LoRA 适配器尤其有效。人们可以合并或组合经过不同任务训练的适配器，从模型中心独立检索，以合成一个继承其优点的模型，同时保留轻量级存储和易于分发的优点。, which requires a unified training pipeline and a joint dataset with aligned per-task labels. This is costly in curation and computation, and often infeasible when labels are proprietary or restricted by license. It also makes it difficult to leverage numerous task-specialized models that are scattered across open model hubs, because those checkpoints cannot be reused directly without centralized retraining. These constraints motivate model merging, where independently finetuned checkpoints are combined without revisiting labeled data. In the foundation-model era, model merging is especially effective with LoRA adapters. One can merge or compose adapters trained on diverse tasks, retrieved independently from model hubs, to synthesize a single model that inherits their strengths while preserving the benefits of lightweight storage and easy distribution.

传统的模型合并主要集中于完整模型权重Conventional model merging has largely focused on full model weights[30, 36, 90, 95, 98]。任务算术. Task Arithmetic[30]将任务向量构造为微调检查点和预训练检查点之间的差异，然后将它们与在小型验证分割上选择的比例相加。相比之下，LoRA 感知合并constructs task vectors as differences between fine-tuned and pretrained checkpoints, then adds them with a scale chosen on a small validation split. In contrast, LoRA-aware merging[71, 106]直接在低阶适配器上运行，而不是在完整权重上运行。在低秩空间中工作可以提高内存和计算效率，并且在 LoRA 设置中表现明显更好，在 LoRA 设置中，传统的全权重合并可能会由于适配器之间严重的子空间不对齐而降级operates directly on the low-rank adapters rather than the full weights. Working in the low-rank space is memory and compute efficient and it performs markedly better in LoRA settings, where conventional full-weight mergers can degrade due to strong subspace misalignment between adapters[71]。鉴于 LoRA 微调现已成为 LLM 和 VLM 的标准，以 LoRA 为目标的合并是一个有前途的方向。. Given that LoRA fine-tuning is now standard for LLMs and VLMs, LoRA-targeted merging is a promising direction.

LoRA 合并的另一个优点是，它可以使用梯度信号来设置跨检查点的合并权重，这在全权重操作时在 LLM 或 VLM 规模上是令人望而却步的。阿达合并Another advantage of LoRA merging is that it can use gradient signals to set merge weights across checkpoints, which is prohibitive at the scale of LLMs or VLMs when operating on full weights. AdaMerging[95]是一种基于梯度的代表性方法，通过最小化输出熵作为替代目标来估计没有标签的合并权重。该替代品是测试时间适应的标准is a representative gradient-based method that estimates merge weights without labels by minimizing output entropy as a surrogate objective. This surrogate is standard in test-time adaptation[79]并已被 AdaMerging 及后续工作采用and has been adopted by AdaMerging and subsequent work[73, 84, 6]，在分类上产生了巨大的收益。然而，基于熵的方法在更广泛的任务和领域中面临着根本性的限制。首先，熵不适用于深度估计或表面法线预测等回归问题。其次，对于 LLM 和 VLM，必须在每个令牌预测时计算熵，因此成本随着生成令牌的数量而增长，并很快成为瓶颈, yielding strong gains on classification. However, entropy-based approaches face fundamental limitations in broader tasks and domains. First, entropy does not apply to regression problems such as depth estimation or surface normal prediction. Second, for LLMs and VLMs, entropy must be computed at each token prediction, so cost grows with the number of generated tokens and quickly becomes a bottleneck for基于熵的entropy-based合并。merging.

5 结论Conclusion

我们研究了 LoRA 合并，其中组合多个适配器来构建单个模型。 LoRA微调时，下投影因子We studied LoRA merging, where multiple adapters are combined to build a single model. We define the proportion of the activation that is suppressed by this projection as the null-space ratio. Empirically, this ratio decreases as task performance improves, providing a simple label-free signal for learning merge weights. Building on this insight, we introduce Null-Space Compression (NSC) Merging, a computationally efficient approach for combining multiple LoRA adapters. Across twenty heterogeneous vision tasks, six NLI tasks, and six vision–language tasks, NSC achieves balanced, state-of-the-art performance.
