1 简介Introduction

工业4.0通过整合人工智能（AI）、机器人和信息技术等先进技术，提高生产效率和质量Industry 4.0 has enhanced production efficiency and quality by integrating advanced technologies, including artificial intelligence (AI), robotics, and information technology[huang2022industry]。然而，工业 5.0 代表了一种范式转变，它超越了自动化和数字化，强调以人为本的价值观，包括工人福祉、创造力和认知参与度。这一愿景的一个关键支柱是人机协作（HRC），即人类和智能机器人协同工作，发挥互补优势。然而，在现实世界的制造环境中，人类工人经常会感到疲劳，这会对人类绩效产生不利影响. Industry 5.0, however, represents a paradigm shift that goes beyond automation and digitization to emphasize human-centric values, including worker well-being, creativity, and cognitive engagement. A key pillar of this vision is human-robot collaboration (HRC), where humans and intelligent robots work in synergy, leveraging their complementary strengths. However, in real-world manufacturing environments, human workers often experience fatigue, which can adversely affect human performance[li2025q]。因此，应将人体工程学考虑纳入HRC流程中，以确保可持续生产。. Therefore, ergonomic considerations should be integrated into the HRC process to ensure sustainable production.

任务规划和分配 (TPA) 是指确定何时执行特定任务并将其分配给合适的代理（无论是人类、机器人还是机器）以满足特定制造要求的过程Task planning and allocation (TPA) refers to the process of determining when to perform specific tasks and allocating them to suitable agents—whether humans, robots, or machines—to fulfill specific manufacturing requirements[cheng2019task, lee2022task, zhao2025safety]。它在提高先进制造系统的有效性方面发挥着至关重要的作用。在 HRC 的背景下，这一挑战变得更加复杂，演变成人机 TPA (HRTPA) 问题，其中人类工人和机器人代理必须动态协调才能实现最佳系统性能。. It plays a crucial role in enhancing the effectiveness of advanced manufacturing systems. Within the context of HRC, this challenge becomes more complex, evolving into the human-robot TPA (HRTPA) problem, where both human workers and robotic agents must be dynamically coordinated to achieve optimal system performance.

2.5 研究差距Research gaps

综上所述，上述文献综述主要关注动态生产环境、人体工程学、安全强化学习和在线参数估计中的 HRTPA。然而，目前的研究存在以下局限性：In summary, the above literature review focuses on HRTPA in dynamic production environments, ergonomics, safe RL, and online parameter estimation. However, current research has the following limitations:

(1) 当前的 HRTPA 研究努力解决不准确或未知的疲劳模型参数，通常假设这些系数是预先定义的并且是先验已知的，同时忽略了人类疲劳敏感性的固有日常变化和疲劳累积模式的个体差异。(1) Current HRTPA research struggles to address inaccurate or unknown fatigue model parameters, typically assuming these coefficients are predefined and known a priori, while overlooking the inherent daily variability in human fatigue sensitivity and individual differences in fatigue accumulation patterns.

(2)尽管基于强化学习的方法在解决HRTPA问题方面取得了进展，但它们在解决约束问题时经常忽略疲劳因素或面临约束违规，导致生产中人类工作量过大。(2) Despite the progress of RL-based approaches for the HRTPA problem, they often neglect fatigue factors or face constraint violations when solving constrained problems, leading to excessive workload for humans in production.

(3) 虽然安全强化学习为约束感知决策提供了原则框架，但之前的工作很少应用于 HRTPA，也很少考虑明确的人体工程学约束或将疲劳动力学纳入安全机制。(3) While safe RL provides a principled framework for constraint-aware decision-making, prior works are rarely applied to HRTPA and seldom consider explicit ergonomic constraints or incorporate fatigue dynamics into the safety mechanism.

6 讨论Discussion

这项工作提出了带有约束决斗双深度 Q 学习 (PF-CD3Q) 算法的粒子滤波器作为 HRTPA 问题的一种新颖解决方案。实验结果突出了与现有方法相比的几个重要优势。一、基于PF的在线过滤器This work presents the particle filter with constrained dueling double deep Q-learning (PF-CD3Q) algorithm as a novel solution to the HRTPA problem. The experimental results highlight several important advantages over existing methods.
