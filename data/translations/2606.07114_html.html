DIFFRACT：通过可微分编程实现无线网络的神经化效用最大化DIFFRACT: Neuralized Utility Maximization for Wireless Networks by Differentiable Programming

Chee Wei Tan1, Siya Chen2
摘要Abstract

下一代无线网络，包括卫星到 Open RAN 系统，需要灵活的智能资源管理，能够在随机服务质量约束下处理动态多用户干扰。本文介绍了 DIFFRACT，这是一种神经化效用最大化框架，它利用可微分编程将深度学习与无线网络优化相结合。我们方法的核心是利用标准干扰函数的数学结构，这是无线功率控制的基础。通过为这些函数开发对偶理论，我们通过算法展开将迭代干扰管理算法映射到可微神经网络架构中。这使得能够在网络边缘进行分布式、基于梯度的端到端学习，支持实时适应陆地和非陆地环境中的干扰。 DIFFRACT 通过对复杂的通道动态进行建模并利用可微分模型的表达能力，实现可扩展且稳健的效用最大化。实验结果证实了该框架的理论可靠性和对于下一代无线系统的实际有效性。Next-generation wireless networks, including satellite-to-Open RAN systems, demand agile and intelligent resource management capable of handling dynamic multi-user interference under stochastic quality of service constraints. This paper introduces DIFFRACT, a neuralized utility maximization framework that leverages differentiable programming to integrate deep learning with optimization in wireless networks. Central to our approach is the exploitation of the mathematical structure of standard interference functions, which are foundational in wireless power control. By developing a duality theory for these functions, we map iterative interference management algorithms into differentiable neural network architectures via algorithm unrolling. This enables distributed, end-to-end gradient-based learning at the network edge, supporting real-time adaptation to interference in both terrestrial and non-terrestrial environments. DIFFRACT allows for scalable and robust utility maximization by modeling complex channel dynamics and leveraging the expressiveness of differentiable models. Experimental results confirm the framework’s theoretical soundness and practical effectiveness for next-generation wireless systems.

IV-B 通过算法展开进行可微分编程Differentiable Programming by Algorithm Unrolling

In essence, algorithm unrolling maps each iteration to a network layer, enabling a trainable deep neural network that embeds optimization logic within a differentiable programming framework, enhancing both performance and interpretability[7].

VI 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 DIFFRACT，这是一种用于无线网络效用最大化的通用可微分编程框架，在不可预测的干扰下管理资源特别有效，这在卫星地面系统中经常遇到。我们的工作通过建立标准干扰函数的可微性和单调算子表征揭示了新的对偶结果。这提供了非线性 Perron-Frobenius 算子视角，可以通过高效的自动微分顺利集成到可微分编程和现代深度学习框架中。 DIFFRACT 对复杂的干扰模式进行建模，例如大气衰落中的中断，这对于空对地通信至关重要。This paper presents DIFFRACT, a general differentiable programming framework for utility maximization in wireless networks, particularly effective in managing resources under unpredictable interference, as commonly encountered in satellite-terrestrial systems. Our work reveals new duality results by establishing the differentiability and monotone operator characterization of standard interference functions. This provides a nonlinear Perron-Frobenius operator perspective that enables smooth integration into differentiable programming and modern deep learning frameworks with efficient automatic differentiation. DIFFRACT models complex interference patterns–such as outage in atmospheric fading, which is critical for space-to-ground communication–within an端到端可训练的 AI 原生堆栈end-to-end trainable, AI-native stack通过将具有隐式干扰约束的效用最大化算法展开为可微的原始对偶更新。实验表明，DIFFRACT 为无线网络优化中的 AI 原生资源控制提供了理论上良好的性能和可扩展性。未来的工作将利用定理中的单调算子特征by unrolling a utility maximization algorithm with implicit interference constraints into differentiable primal-dual updates.
