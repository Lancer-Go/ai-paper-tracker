Em-Garde：用于主动流媒体视频理解的提议匹配框架Em-Garde: A Propose-Match Framework for Proactive Streaming Video Understanding

Yikai Zheng    Xin Ding    Yifan Yang    Shiqi Jiang    Hao Wu    Qianxi Zhang    Weijun Wang    Ting Cao    Yunxin Liu
摘要Abstract

流媒体视频理解的最新进展启用了一种新的交互范例，其中模型主动响应用户查询。当前的主动式 VideoLLM 依赖于每帧触发决策，这面临着效率与准确性的困境。我们建议Recent advances in Streaming Video Understanding has enabled a new interaction paradigm where models respond proactively to user queries. Current proactive VideoLLMs rely on per-frame triggering decision making, which suffers from an efficiency-accuracy dilemma. We propose埃姆加德Em-Garde，一个新颖的框架，将语义理解与流感知分离。在查询时，指令引导提案解析器将用户查询转换为结构化的、基于感知的视觉提案；在流式传输期间，轻量级提案匹配模块执行高效的基于嵌入的匹配以触发响应。 StreamingBench 和 OVO-Bench 上的实验表明，在主动响应准确性和效率方面比先前模型有了持续改进，验证了在严格的计算约束下主动视频理解的有效解决方案。代码可在, a novel framework that decouples semantic understanding from streaming perception. At query time, the Instruction-Guided Proposal Parser transforms user queries into structured, perceptually grounded visual proposals; during streaming, a Lightweight Proposal Matching Module performs efficient embedding-based matching to trigger responses. Experiments on StreamingBench and OVO-Bench demonstrate consistent improvements over prior models in proactive response accuracy and efficiency, validating an effective solution for proactive video understanding under strict computational constraints. 1 简介Introduction

多模式法学硕士（MLLM）的最新进展The recent advances in Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs)(Bai et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025a; Li et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2024d; Chen et al., 2024b; Shen et al., 2024; Yao et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024a)，在流媒体视频理解方面取得了重大成功。 Gemini Live 等工业模型, have brought about significant success in streaming video understanding. Industrial models like Gemini Live(Team et al., 2024)能够理解流媒体视频内容并实时响应用户查询，带来前所未有的交互体验。学术努力进一步推动能够主动回答用户查询的模型are able to comprehend streaming video content and respond to user queries in real time, bringing an unprecedented interactive experience. Academic efforts push even further towards models that can answer a user query proactively(Chen et al., 2024a; Wu et al., 2024; Ding et al., 2025b; Wang et al., 2025a; Qian et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025)：这些模型不是在查询时被动地打开并应答，而是持续监视视频流，并自动决定何时响应查询（例如，当水沸腾时提醒用户）。这种新的互动模式开辟了从体育评论到家庭援助的大量新应用: instead of passively switching on and answering at query time, these models keep monitoring the video stream, and automatically decide when to respond to the query (e.g. remind the user when water boils). This new interactive paradigm opens up vast new applications from sports commentary to household assistance(Deliège et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025; Merrill et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2025b; Wen et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025a)，同时也给社区带来了根本性的新挑战。, yet also posing a fundamental new challenge to the community.

主动流媒体视频理解的独特挑战在于需要决定是否在每个时间步实时响应The unique challenge of proactive streaming video understanding lies in the need to decide whether to respond in real time at every timestep(Wang et al., 2024b; Chen et al., 2024a; Qian et al., 2025)。每当新帧到达时，系统必须执行多个感知和语义推理过程来确定是否应该触发响应，包括视觉感知（例如对象和动作识别）、事件转换检测和查询感知相关性评估。然而，处理速度必须与视频帧速率（至少每秒 5-10 帧 (fps)）相匹配，这使得可用的计算预算受到严重限制. Whenever a new frame arrives, the system must perform multiple perceptual and semantic reasoning processes to determine whether a response should be triggered, including visual perception (e.g. object and action recognition), event transition detection, and query-aware relevance assessment. However, the processing speed must match the video frame rate (at least 5-10 frames per second (fps)), making the available computational budget severely limited(Di et al., 2025; Yao et al., 2025; Qian et al., 2024; Ding et al., 2025a)。这在需求之间产生了内在的紧张关系. This creates an inherent tension between the need for复杂的视觉语义理解和快速每帧计算的要求complicated visual-semantic understanding and the requirement for fast per-frame computation.

现有的方法大多将问题表述为Existing approaches mostly formulate the problem as a每帧决策问题per-frame decision making problem。具体来说，他们利用 MLLM 或轻量级门在每个时间步输出响应/沉默决策。为了跟上流速度，他们缩小了模型大小. Specifically, they utilize an MLLM or lightweight gate to output a response/silence decision at every timestep. To keep up with the streaming speed, they reduce the model size(Ding et al., 2025b; Wang et al., 2025a; Qian et al., 2025)或提高信息压缩率or increase information compression rate(Zhang et al., 2024a; Qian et al., 2024; Zeng et al., 2025; Yao et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025c; Ning et al., 2025)执行快速的逐帧理解。然而，这自然限制了视觉感知的粒度和模型的决策质量，使得丰富的视觉理解需求与严格的实时约束之间的紧张关系从根本上得不到解决。to perform fast framewise understanding. However, this naturally limits the granularity of visual perception and the decision-making quality of the model, making the tension between the need for rich visual understanding and the strict real-time constraint fundamentally unsolved.

为了解决效率与准确性的困境，我们试图摆脱这种范式。我们注意到，复杂的决策问题可以分为两个阶段：在查询时，模型已经可以决定哪种事件值得响应，并想象指向它们的视觉证据。然后，只需要简单的视觉感知来匹配证据To solve the efficiency-accuracy dilemma, we try to break away from this paradigm. We note that the complicated decision-making problem can break down into two stages: Upon the query, the model can already decide which kind of events are worth responding to and imagine the visual evidence that points to them. Then, only simple visual perception is needed to match the evidence(Lee et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025)。基于这一见解，我们提出了. Based on this insight, we propose a每个查询的解析范例per-query parsing paradigm (Figure 1）将昂贵的语义解释完全转移到流循环之外。具体来说，系统在查询时将用户查询解析为基于感知的建议，从而使后续的流处理能够简化为高效的帧级视觉匹配。例如，如果用户请求在“水沸腾”时收到通知，则系统不需要在每一帧重复解释该请求。相反，它在查询时将指令解析为具体的视觉建议，例如“剧烈冒泡”或“持续蒸汽排放”，然后不断执行轻量级帧级匹配来检测这些线索。) that shifts expensive semantic interpretation entirely outside the streaming loop. Specifically, the system parses the user query into perceptually grounded proposals at query time, enabling subsequent streaming processing to be reduced to efficient frame-level visual matching. For example, if the user requests to be notified when “the water boils”, the system does not need to repeatedly interpret this request at every frame. Instead, it parses the instruction at query time into concrete visual proposals, such as “vigorous bubbling” or “sustained steam emission”, and then continuously performs lightweight frame-level matching to detect these cues.

这种分离带来了两个关键的技术挑战。首先，没有通用的解析方案可以泛化不同的和开放式的用户查询。系统必须处理不同的查询结构、多种可能的事件类型和不同的抽象级别，并生成轻量级感知模型可以可靠使用的结构化视觉建议。其次，考虑到多个细粒度的视觉建议，轻量级模型将它们快速可靠地与流视频中的实际视觉证据相匹配并非易事。This separation introduces two key technical challenges. Firstly, there is no universal parsing scheme that can generalize across diverse and open-ended user queries. The system must handle varied query structures, multiple possible event types, and different levels of abstraction, and produce structured visual proposals that a lightweight perception model can reliably use. Secondly, given multiple fine-grained visual proposals, it is non-trivial for a lightweight model to quickly and reliably match them to actual visual evidence in streaming video.

通过解决这些挑战，我们实现了一种主动理解流媒体视频的新颖框架，称为By addressing these challenges, we realize a novel framework for proactive streaming video understanding, called埃姆加德Em-Garde，它将语义推理与感知分开，并在严格的计算约束下实现实时主动响应。它集成了两项关键技术。, which separates semantic reasoning from perception and enables real-time proactive response under strict computational constraints. It integrates two key techniques.

对于语义解析，我们引入了指令引导提案解析器（IGPP），它利用 MLLM 的推理能力将高级指令解析为结构化视觉提案。每个提案都包含平行的视觉线索，描述不同的值得注意的事件或单个事件的详细方面。 IGPP 结合了一个短视频上下文来随着时间的推移调整建议，使解析过程能够随着正在进行的视频而发展，同时仍然尊重查询指令。For semantic parsing, we introduce the Instruction-Guided Proposal Parser (IGPP), which leverages the reasoning ability of the MLLM to parse high-level instructions into structured visual proposals. Each proposal consists of parallel visual cues describing either distinct noteworthy events or detailed aspects of a single event. IGPP incorporates a short video context to adapt proposals over time, enabling the parsing process to evolve with the ongoing video while still respecting the query instruction.

对于流感知，我们提出了轻量级提案匹配模块（LPMM）。 LPMM 使用滑动窗口对最近的视频上下文进行编码，并使用轻量级嵌入模型嵌入视频片段和 IGPP 提案For streaming perception, we propose the Lightweight Proposal Matching Module (LPMM). LPMM encodes recent video context with a sliding window and embeds both the video segment and the IGPP proposals using a lightweight embedding model(Zhai et al., 2023; Meng et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2024c; Jian et al., 2025)。在嵌入空间中比较嵌入以产生相似性分数的时间序列。相似度激增表明存在潜在匹配，从而触发 MLLM 响应器生成响应并验证其适当性。这种设计将昂贵的推理与高频感知分离，从而实现高效、帧速率匹配的主动响应。. The embeddings are compared in the embedding space to produce a temporal sequence of similarity scores. A surge in similarity indicates a potential matching, triggering the MLLM responder to generate a response and verify its appropriateness. This design decouples expensive reasoning from high-frequency perception, enabling efficient, frame-rate-matched proactive responses.
