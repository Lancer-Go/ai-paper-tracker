3.1 混合内存Hybrid Memory

记忆是指模型保留输入或生成内容中的信息的能力，确保整个生成过程的一致性。静态记忆可确保固定元素（例如建筑物、道路）的一致性，通常通过评估相机返回到先前姿势时场景是否看起来相同来进行评估Memory refers to the model’s ability to retain information from inputs or generated content, ensuring consistency throughout the generation process. Static memory ensures the consistency of immobile elements (e.g., buildings, roads), and is typically evaluated by assessing whether a scene looks identical when the camera returns to a previous pose[15]。然而，混合记忆需要更复杂的认知飞跃。它要求模型在跟踪动态主体（例如行人、走狗）的同时锚定静态背景。如图所示. Hybrid memory, however, demands a far more sophisticated cognitive leap. It requires the model to simultaneously anchor the static background while tracking the dynamic subjects (e.g., pedestrians, running dogs). As illustrated in Fig.2，当主体退出并重新进入画面时，混合记忆决定它不仅必须保留其原始的视觉身份，而且还必须以一致的运动状态重新出现在合理的位置。, when a subject exits and re-enters the frame, hybrid memory dictates that it must not only retain its original visual identity but also reappear at a plausible location with a consistent motion state.

6 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了新的范式In this paper, we introduce the novel paradigm of混合内存Hybrid Memory，挑战模型同时保持静态背景一致性和动态主题一致性，特别是在复杂的退出和重新进入事件期间。为了系统地促进该领域的研究，我们构建, challenging models to simultaneously maintain static background consistency and dynamic subject coherence, particularly during complex exit-and-re-entry events. To systematically facilitate research in this field, we constructHM世界HM-World，第一个专用于混合内存的大规模视频数据集，具有高度多样化的场景和复杂的动态过程。为了应对混合内存的挑战，我们建议, the first large-scale video dataset dedicated to hybrid memory, featuring highly diverse scenarios and complex dynamic processes. To tackle the challenge of hybrid memory, we propose海德拉HyDRA，一种先进的内存架构，专门设计用于有效提取和检索运动和外观线索，以实现一致的生成。大量实验表明 HyDRA 的性能明显优于现有方法。我们希望混合内存范式与 HM-World 数据集和 HyDRA 框架一起，能够激发新的研究，并为推进视频世界模型提供坚实的基础。, an advanced memory architecture specifically designed to effectively extract and retrieve motion and appearance cues for consistent generation. Extensive experiments demonstrate that HyDRA significantly outperforms existing methods. We hope that the hybrid memory paradigm, alongside the HM-World dataset and the HyDRA framework, will inspire new research and provide a solid foundation for advancing video world models.

局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work。尽管取得了有希望的结果，但我们的工作仍然存在一定的局限性。具体来说，在涉及三个或更多主体或严重遮挡的高度复杂场景中，HyDRA 在保持一致生成方面的性能往往会下降。在未来的工作中，我们计划探索更先进、更强大的记忆机制来处理复杂的多主体动态，并将我们的方法扩展到不受约束的现实世界环境。. Despite the promising results, our work still presents certain limitations. Specifically, HyDRA’s performance in maintaining consistent generation tends to degrade in highly complex scenes involving three or more subjects or severe occlusions. In future work, we plan to explore more advanced and robust memory mechanisms to handle intricate multi-subject dynamics and scale our approach to unconstrained real-world environments.
