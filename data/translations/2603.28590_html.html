摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 可以生成思想链 (CoT)，但这些思想链并不总是对其最终输出负有因果关系。当发生这种不匹配时，CoT 不再忠实地反映驱动模型行为的决策关键因素，从而导致 CoT 可监控性问题降低。然而，仍然缺乏用于研究 CoT 可监控性的全面且完全开源的基准。为了解决这一差距，我们提出了 MonitorBench，这是一个用于评估法学硕士 CoT 可监控性的系统基准。 MonitorBench 提供：(1) 一组多样化的 1,514 个测试实例，其中包含跨 7 个类别的 19 个任务的精心设计的决策关键因素，以表征Large language models (LLMs) can generate chains of thought (CoTs) that are not always causally responsible for their final outputs. When such a mismatch occurs, the CoT no longer faithfully reflects the decision-critical factors driving the model’s behavior, leading to the reduced CoT monitorability problem. However, a comprehensive and fully open-source benchmark for studying CoT monitorability remains lacking. To address this gap, we propose MonitorBench, a systematic benchmark for evaluating CoT monitorability in LLMs. MonitorBench provides: (1) a diverse set of 1,514 test instances with carefully designed decision-critical factors across 19 tasks spanning 7 categories to characterize当whenCoT 可用于监控驱动 LLM 行为的因素； (2) 两个压力测试设置来量化CoTs can be used to monitor the factors driving LLM behavior; and (2) two stress-test settings to quantify在多大程度上the extent to whichCoT 的可监控性可能会降低。对多个具有不同能力的流行法学硕士进行的广泛实验表明，当产生最终目标响应需要通过决策关键因素进行结构推理时，CoT 可监控性更高。闭源法学硕士通常表现出较低的可监控性，并且可监控性和模型能力之间存在负相关关系。此外，开源和闭源法学硕士都可以故意降低压力测试下的可监控性，在某些不需要对决策关键因素进行结构推理的任务中，可监控性下降高达 30%。除了这些实证见解之外，MonitorBench 还为评估未来法学硕士、研究先进的压力测试可监控性技术和开发新的监控方法的进一步研究提供了基础。该代码可在CoT monitorability can be degraded. 1 简介Introduction

最近，法学硕士在挑战性任务中表现出了卓越的推理能力Recently, LLMs have demonstrated remarkable reasoning capabilities in challenging tasks(Jaech et al., 2024; Team et al., 2025; Guo et al., 2025a)，由推理时间缩放驱动, driven by inference-time scaling(Snell et al., 2024)和具有可验证奖励的强化学习and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards(Shao et al., 2024)。这些方法通常会促使法学硕士产生逐步的思路（CoT），从而做出最终决定。通过用自然语言外部化中间推理，CoT 为解释和监控模型行为提供了一个潜在有用的接口. These methods typically elicit LLMs to generate step-by-step chain of thoughts (CoT) that lead to the final decisions. By externalizing intermediate reasoning in natural language, CoT provides a potentially useful interface for interpreting and monitoring model behavior(Singh et al., 2025)。然而，CoT监测的可靠性仍令人担忧. However, the reliability of CoT monitoring is still under concern(Jiang et al., 2025b; Shen et al., 2025).

As a result, it is difficult to draw general conclusions about CoT monitorability across different task domains and models. 3.2 评估任务Evaluation Tasks

3.2.1 输入干预Input Intervention

输入干预将 CoT 视为模型决策驱动因素的因果轨迹：忠实的 CoT 应反映决策相关输入的变化。具体来说，MonitorBench 注入 (i) 应该改变推理过程的决定性信息（提示注入），或 (ii) 应该改变模型目标的目标（目标注入），然后检查 CoT 是否明确显示注入的因素并以预测模型最终行为的方式使用它。Input intervention treats CoT as a causal trace of the model’s decision drivers: a faithful CoT should reflect changes in the decision-relevant input. Concretely, MonitorBench injects either (i) decisive information that should alter the reasoning process (cue injection), or (ii) a goal that should alter the model’s objective (goal injection), and then checks whether the CoT explicitly surfaces the injected factor and uses it in a way that predicts the model’s final behavior.

提示注射。Cue Injection.我们将决策关键输入因素设置为附加线索，为解决给定实例提供信息。我们考虑两种提示类型：关键条件（例如，决定性前提）和基本事实答案。We set the decision-critical input factor as an additional cues that is informative for solving a given instance. We consider two cue types: the key condition (e.g., a decisive premise) and the ground truth answer.
