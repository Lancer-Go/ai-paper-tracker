摘要Abstract

在线审议平台的主要目标是通过用户表达的偏好来识别用户社区广泛同意的想法。然而，理想情况下，共识引导应超出用户提供的具体陈述，并应纳入特定主题的相对显着性。我们通过将共识建模为一维意见空间中的一个区间来解决这个问题，该一维意见空间是通过嵌入和降维从潜在的高维数据派生的。我们定义一个目标，在假设区间内最大化预期一致性，其中期望是针对问题的基本分布，隐含地考虑到它们的显着性。我们提出了一种高效的经验风险最小化（ERM）算法并建立了 PAC 学习保证。我们的初步实验证明了我们算法的性能，并检查了识别最佳共识区域的更有效方法。我们发现，通过选择性地查询用户现有的语句样本，我们可以将所需的查询数量减少到实际数量。A primary goal of online deliberation platforms is to identify ideas that are broadly agreeable to a community of users through their expressed preferences. Yet, consensus elicitation should ideally extend beyond the specific statements provided by users and should incorporate the relative salience of particular topics. We address this issue by modelling consensus as an interval in a one-dimensional opinion space derived from potentially high-dimensional data via embedding and dimensionality reduction. We define an objective that maximizes expected agreement within a hypothesis interval where the expectation is over an underlying distribution of issues, implicitly taking into account their salience. We propose an efficient Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM) algorithm and establish PAC-learning guarantees. Our initial experiments demonstrate the performance of our algorithm and examine more efficient approaches to identifying optimal consensus regions. We find that through selectively querying users on an existing sample of statements, we can reduce the number of queries needed to a practical number.
