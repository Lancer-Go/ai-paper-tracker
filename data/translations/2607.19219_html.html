超越分数预测：基于 LLM 的论文评分和通过强化学习与 Rubric 奖励生成反馈Beyond Score Prediction: LLM-Based Essay Scoring and Feedback Generation via Reinforcement Learning with Rubric Rewards

Xuefeng Jin, Jiashuo Zhang, Teng Cao, Bin Yang Contact: myjinxf@163.com
摘要Abstract

大语言模型（LLM）已广泛应用于自动论文评分（AES）和自动反馈生成（AFG）。然而，现有的研究主要依赖于即时工程或监督微调，而对强化学习（RL）训练后和反馈质量自动评估的系统研究仍然有限。我们提出了 RLAES，这是一个统一的法学硕士框架，通过 RL 联合优化论文评分和反馈生成。为了使反馈质量可测量、可解释并可用于培训，我们引入了基于 Rubric 的反馈评估 (RFE)，这是一个基于论文的反馈评估框架，由 166 个细粒度的二元评估项目和一个法学硕士作为评委。在 RFE 的基础上，我们提出了自适应门控反馈优化 (AGFO)，它在 RL 期间按需激活基于规则的反馈奖励，减少评估开销，同时提高反馈质量。我们还提出相邻对比推理（ACR），通过明确对比相邻分数水平来改进序数分数校准。实验结果表明，RFE 框架捕获了论文反馈的一致性，表现出强大的成对判别能力，并且与专家偏好紧密一致。在 ASAP 基准上，RLAES-AGFO 在基于 LLM 的方法中实现了最佳评分性能（QWK = 0.803），同时保持了与 GPT-5.5 相当的反馈质量，并避免了在仅评分 RL 下观察到的反馈退化。代码和数据集可公开获取Large language models (LLMs) have been widely applied to automated essay scoring (AES) and automated feedback generation (AFG). However, existing studies rely primarily on prompt engineering or supervised fine-tuning, while systematic research on reinforcement learning (RL) post-training and automated evaluation of feedback quality remains limited. We propose RLAES, a unified LLM framework that jointly optimizes essay scoring and feedback generation through RL. To make feedback quality measurable, interpretable, and usable for training, we introduce Rubric-based Feedback Evaluation (RFE), an essay-grounded feedback evaluation framework comprising 166 fine-grained binary rubric items and an LLM-as-judge. Building on RFE, we propose Adaptive Gated Feedback Optimization (AGFO), which activates rubric-based feedback rewards on demand during RL, reducing evaluation overhead while improving feedback quality. We also propose Adjacent Contrastive Reasoning (ACR) to improve ordinal score calibration by explicitly contrasting adjacent score levels. Experimental results show that the RFE framework captures essay-feedback consistency, exhibits strong pairwise discriminative power, and closely aligns with expert preferences. On the ASAP benchmark, RLAES-AGFO achieves the best scoring performance among LLM-based methods (QWK = 0.803), while maintaining feedback quality comparable to GPT-5.5 and avoiding the feedback degradation observed under score-only RL. Code and datasets are publicly available athttps://github.com/hellomuyi/RLAES.

1 简介Introduction

自动作文评分（AES）和自动反馈生成（AFG）是人工智能在教育中的核心应用Automated Essay Scoring (AES) and Automated Feedback Generation (AFG) are core applications of artificial intelligence in education(Abudalfa and Barrot 2026)。 这些系统使用自然语言处理、机器学习和大型语言模型 (LLM) 自动对学生论文进行评分并生成建设性反馈。 AES 和 AFG 一起可以减少教育工作者的评分工作量，同时为学生提供及时、个性化的诊断反馈，帮助他们发现弱点并随着时间的推移提高写作水平. These systems use natural language processing, machine learning, and large language models (LLMs) to automatically score student essays and generate constructive feedback. Together, AES and AFG can reduce educators’ grading workload while providing students with timely, personalized diagnostic feedback that helps them identify weaknesses and improve their writing over time(Li and Collins 2026).

AES 的研究可以追溯到 20 世纪 60 年代Research on AES dates back to the 1960s(Sun et al. 2025)。传统的 AES 方法是从依赖手工特征的回归模型发展而来的. Traditional AES approaches have evolved from regression models relying on hand-crafted features(Choi et al. 2026)深度学习范式将 AES 表述为具有自动表示学习的数值分类或回归。这种演变是从卷积神经网络 (CNN) 和循环神经网络 (RNN) 发展而来的to deep learning paradigms that formulate AES as numerical classification or regression with automatic representation learning. This evolution has progressed from convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs)(Taghipour and Ng 2016)到更先进的基于 Transformer 的预训练语言模型，最著名的是 BERT 系列to more advanced Transformer-based pretrained language models, most notably the BERT family(Li and Pan 2025b; Xie et al. 2022)。 最近基于 LLM 的 AES 方法可大致分为快速工程和微调。即时工程研究已广泛探索零样本. Recent LLM-based approaches to AES can be broadly divided into prompt engineering and fine-tuning. Prompt engineering studies have extensively explored zero-shot(Seßler et al. 2025; Su et al. 2025)和少样本学习and few-shot learning(Huang et al. 2026; Mansour et al. 2024)，通过评分细则增强提示, prompts augmented with scoring rubrics(Mansour et al. 2024)，链式思维（CoT）推理, chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning(Xiao et al. 2025), 两两比较, pairwise comparisons(Choi et al. 2026; Cai et al. 2025; Shibata and Miyamura 2025)和多代理框架, and multi-agent frameworks(Wang et al. 2026)。微调研究主要集中在监督微调（SFT）. Fine-tuning studies focus mainly on supervised fine-tuning (SFT)(Xiao et al. 2025; Ormerod and Kwako 2024; Johnsi and Kumar 2025)。这些研究表明，经过微调的法学硕士，甚至是规模较小的开源法学硕士，其表现远远优于配备了一系列增强提示策略的大型专有法学硕士。. These studies have shown that fine-tuned LLMs, even smaller open-source ones, substantially outperform their extremely large proprietary counterparts equipped with a range of enhanced prompting strategies.

除了分数预测之外，尽管形成性反馈在教学上很重要，但对 AFG 的关注较少，包括反馈的评估和优化Beyond score prediction, less attention has been paid to AFG, including the evaluation and optimization of feedback, despite the pedagogical importance of formative feedback(Liu et al. 2024)。 在 AES 上具有竞争力的小型模型通常使用基于 BERT 的纯编码器架构，如果没有额外的解码器就无法生成反馈。 现有的基于LLM的反馈生成方法要么直接提示LLM. Small models that perform competitively on AES typically use BERT-based encoder-only architectures and cannot generate feedback without an additional decoder. Existing LLM-based feedback generation methods either directly prompt an LLM(Sasaki et al. 2026; Xiao et al. 2024; Ormerod and Kwako 2024)或对强推理模型生成的反馈执行 SFTor perform SFT on feedback generated by strong reasoning models(Xiao et al. 2025; Li and Pan 2025a)。 对于反馈评估，仍然缺乏广泛接受的自动化评估框架。传统的反馈评估依赖于带注释的反馈数据集. For feedback evaluation, a widely accepted framework for automated evaluation is still lacking. Traditional feedback evaluation relies on annotated feedback datasets(Sasaki et al. 2026; Liu et al. 2024)。然而，此类注释通常仅涵盖有限的类别，因此不适合评估法学硕士生成的丰富反馈。 LLM 生成的反馈通常是手动评估的. However, such annotations typically cover only a limited set of categories and are therefore ill-suited to evaluating the rich feedback generated by LLMs. LLM-generated feedback is generally evaluated manually(Xiao et al. 2025; Ormerod and Kwako 2024)，但人工评估成本高昂，难以扩展，并且难以转化为优化反馈质量的奖励信号。, but human evaluation is costly, difficult to scale, and difficult to convert into a reward signal for optimizing feedback quality.Li and Pan (2025a)提出了一种自动评估方法，通过计算生成的反馈和与黄金分数相关的评分标准描述之间的语义相似性来评估反馈质量。propose an automated evaluation method that assesses feedback quality by computing the semantic similarity between the generated feedback and the scoring rubric descriptions associated with the gold score.

尽管法学硕士为 AES 和 AFG 创造了新的技术可能性，但仍然存在两个挑战。 一方面，在基于 LLM 的 AES 和 AFG 中，强化学习 (RL) 训练后仍未得到充分探索。当前的法学硕士方法主要侧重于即时工程和 SFT。 即时工程很灵活，但对即时设计很敏感，而SFT则直接模仿黄金分数。强化学习可能更适合评分推理以及与评分标准的一致性，但其对评分准确性和反馈质量的影响尚未得到系统研究。Although LLMs have created new technical possibilities for AES and AFG, two challenges remain. On the one hand, reinforcement learning (RL) post-training remains underexplored in LLM-based AES and AFG. Current LLM approaches focus primarily on prompt engineering and SFT. Prompt engineering is flexible but sensitive to prompt design, whereas SFT directly imitates gold scores. RL may be better suited to score reasoning and alignment with scoring rubrics, but its effects on both scoring accuracy and feedback quality have not been systematically studied.

另一方面，AFG 缺乏有效且可扩展的反馈评估和优化方法。 LLM 生成的反馈通常丰富且结构复杂，导致现有工作严重依赖人工评估On the other hand, AFG lacks effective and scalable methods for feedback evaluation and optimization. LLM-generated feedback is often rich and structurally complex, leading existing work to rely heavily on human assessment(Xiao et al. 2025; Ormerod and Kwako 2024)。 BERTScore 等自动化替代方案可衡量生成的反馈和与黄金分数相关的评分标准描述之间的语义相似性. Automated alternatives such as BERTScore measure the semantic similarity between generated feedback and scoring rubric descriptions associated with the gold score(Li and Pan 2025a)。 然而，一般的、高水平的评分标准和针对论文的反馈之间的差距限制了这种评估策略的有效性。 缺乏有效的反馈评估方法直接限制了AFG的优化。. However, the gap between general, high-level scoring rubrics and essay-specific feedback limits the effectiveness of this evaluation strategy. The lack of an effective feedback evaluation method directly limits AFG optimization.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 RLAES，这是一个针对 AES 和 AFG 的基于 LLM 的统一 RL 后训练框架。 首先，为了填补有效反馈评估的空白，我们引入了基于Rubric的反馈评估（RFE），这是一个特定提示的、以论文为基础的框架，由166个二元评估项目和一个法学硕士作为评委组成。 RFE 提供了反馈质量的细粒度自动化测量，可以作为 RL 奖励。 在 RFE 的基础上，我们提出了自适应门控反馈优化（AGFO），它可以在 RL 期间按需激活基于规则的反馈奖励。 AGFO 减少了 LLM 作为评委的开销，同时联合优化评分准确性和反馈质量，从而防止仅评分 RL 导致的反馈退化。 由此产生的 RLAES-AGFO 模型在基于 LLM 的 AES 方法中实现了最佳性能。此外，我们提出了相邻对比推理（ACR），这是一种明确指导模型对比相邻分数水平的通用策略。 ACR 提高了对细粒度质量差异的敏感性，并减少了序数分类中相邻的混乱。 我们的主要贡献总结如下：To address these challenges, we propose RLAES, a unified LLM-based RL post-training framework for AES and AFG. 2.2 强化学习训练后RL Post-Training

可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 依赖于明确的、可验证的二进制规则作为奖励信号，例如输出的正确性。 DeepSeek-R1Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) relies on explicit, verifiable binary rules as reward signals, such as output correctness. 3.3 相邻对比推理（ACR）Adjacent Contrastive Reasoning (ACR)

自动论文评分可以表述为序数分类问题：分数水平是有序标签而不是独立类别。 主要困难在于确定一篇文章是否属于给定的分数级别或相邻的较低或较高级别。 标准的提示方法要求法学硕士进行整体评估和评分，但没有明确要求在分数边界进行比较。 这种遗漏可能会使模型更容易预测与黄金分数相邻的分数水平，我们称之为这种现象Automated essay scoring can be formulated as an ordinal classification problem: score levels are ordered labels rather than independent classes. The main difficulty is determining whether an essay belongs to a given score level or an adjacent lower or higher level. Standard prompting methods ask an LLM to produce a holistic evaluation and score but do not explicitly require comparisons at score boundaries. This omission may make the model more prone to predicting a score level adjacent to the gold score—a phenomenon we call相邻混乱adjacent confusion.

为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了相邻对比推理（ACR），其灵感来自于人类判断中的锚定和调整启发式To alleviate this issue, we propose Adjacent Contrastive Reasoning (ACR), inspired by the anchoring-and-adjustment heuristic in human judgment(Tversky and Kahneman 1974)。如图所示. As illustrated in Figure2，ACR 将预测分数周围相邻的较低和较高分数视为局部硬负标签，并要求模型在生成最终分数之前明确回答两个对比问题：为什么论文不应获得相邻的较低/较高分数？这种局部反​​事实比较鼓励模型识别区分相邻分数水平的证据，而不是仅产生通用的整体评估。 ACR 通过三种方式提高得分。首先，它将评分从直接分数映射转变为局部边界辨别，从而将模型的注意力集中在相邻分数之间的细粒度差异上。 其次，将相邻级别视为硬阴性会将注意力引向更具辨别力的文本证据。 第三，考虑两个相邻方向提供了双向校准信号，有助于减少序数分数空间中的系统高估或低估。, ACR treats the adjacent lower and higher scores around a predicted score as local hard-negative labels and requires the model to explicitly answer two contrastive questions before producing its final score: Why should the essay not receive the adjacent lower/higher score? This local counterfactual comparison encourages the model to identify evidence that distinguishes adjacent score levels instead of producing only a generic holistic assessment. ACR improves scoring in three ways. First, it transforms scoring from direct score mapping into local boundary discrimination, thereby focusing the model’s attention on fine-grained differences between adjacent scores. Second, treating adjacent levels as hard negatives directs attention toward more discriminative textual evidence. Third, considering both adjacent directions provides a bidirectional calibration signal, helping reduce systematic overestimation or underestimation in the ordinal score space.

ACR 不同于现有的少样本提示和成对比较方法，后者引入外部参考文章并通过比较目标文章与参考文章的相对质量来辅助评分。 相比之下，ACR 不需要外部参考样本，仅添加与相邻分数水平的局部比较。 我们还评估了 ACR-Lower，这是一种片面的变体，只保留了为什么论文不应获得相邻的较低分数的问题。 作为一种通用策略，ACR 可以扩展到其他序数分类问题。ACR differs from existing few-shot prompting and pairwise-comparison methods, which introduce external reference essays and assist scoring by comparing the relative quality of a target essay against the reference. In contrast, ACR requires no external reference samples and adds only local comparisons with adjacent score levels. We also evaluate ACR-Lower, a one-sided variant that retains only the question of why the essay should not receive the adjacent lower score. As a general strategy, ACR may be extended to other ordinal classification problems.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 RLAES-AGFO，这是一个用于联合优化论文评分和反馈生成的 RL 框架。我们引入 RFE 进行细粒度反馈评估，产生可用作 RL 奖励的分数，并证明其排名与专家偏好紧密一致。 AGFO 以 RFE 为基础，在 RL 期间自适应地激活反馈奖励，相对于完全反馈优化减少了训练时间，同时避免了仅评分 RL 的反馈退化。 我们还引入了 ACR 来减轻 AES 中的相邻混乱。 实验表明，RLAES-AGFO 在基于 LLM 的方法中实现了最高的 QWK，RFE 分数与 GPT-5.5 相当，而 ACR 提高了两个评估的基础模型的评分性能。We present RLAES-AGFO, an RL framework for jointly optimizing essay scoring and feedback generation. We introduce RFE for fine-grained feedback evaluation, yielding scores usable as RL rewards, and demonstrate that its rankings closely align with expert preferences. Building on RFE, AGFO adaptively activates feedback rewards during RL, reducing training time relative to full feedback optimization while avoiding the feedback degradation of score-only RL. We also introduce ACR to mitigate adjacent confusion in AES. Experiments show that RLAES-AGFO achieves the highest QWK among the LLM-based methods and an RFE score comparable to that of GPT-5.5, while ACR improves scoring performance across both evaluated base models.

RLAES 仍然因其对 LLM 法官的依赖而受到限制，法官的不同偏好可能会导致奖励不匹配，特别是对于复杂的评分标准。评估也仅限于 ASAP 的针对提示的英语论文。未来的工作应该研究跨提示、跨域和多语言设置，以及联合评分和反馈优化的替代策略。RLAES remains limited by its dependence on LLM judges, whose differing preferences can cause reward mismatch, particularly for complex scoring rubrics. Evaluation is also restricted to prompt-specific English essays from ASAP. Future work should examine cross-prompt, cross-domain, and multilingual settings, as well as alternative strategies for joint scoring and feedback optimization.
