摘要Abstract

最近的 FPGA 通过引入张量块并支持 BRAM 内计算，提高了深度学习 (DL) 推理效率。基于 ReRAM 的模拟内存计算 (IMC) 内核通过直接在 ReRAM 交叉开关内执行向量矩阵乘法 (VMM)，提供比传统数字计算高出一个数量级的计算密度和能源效率。先前的工作已将此类 IMC 模块集成到 FPGA 中以进行深度学习推理。然而，传统的 IMC 设计仅支持静态权重 VMM，而非线性和动态矩阵-矩阵乘法 (DIMM) 仍由 FPGA 架构处理。因此，IMC 的优势在很大程度上仅限于静态权重深度学习模型，而基于 Transformer 的模型需要频繁的非线性和 DIMM 操作，只能获得有限的优势。此外，IMC 块内的 ADC 消耗超过 70% 的面积和功耗，进一步限制了系统效率和可扩展性。为了解决这些问题，我们提出了一种新颖的 FPGA 架构，它将无 ADC 的 IMC 替代方案集成到 FPGA 中，用模拟内容可寻址存储器 (ACAM) 取代传统的 ADC，ACAM 在 IMC 块内本机执行非线性操作。为了充分利用这个新模块，我们进行了 FPGA 感知设计空间探索，确定最佳交叉开关尺寸，同时平衡 FPGA 面积、灵活性和深度学习性能。我们进一步部署了一个高效的映射，使用 ACAM 高效地执行 DIMM 操作，将架构适用性扩展到注意力计算。在基于 CNN 和 Transformer 的基准测试中，我们提出的 FPGA 架构达到了Recent FPGAs have improved deep learning (DL) inference efficiency by introducing tensor blocks and enabling in-BRAM computation. ReRAM-based analog in-memory computing (IMC) cores offer an order of magnitude higher compute density and energy efficiency than conventional digital computation by performing vector-matrix multiplication (VMM) directly within the ReRAM crossbar. Prior work has integrated such IMC blocks into FPGAs for DL inference. However, conventional IMC designs support only static-weight VMM, while nonlinear and dynamic matrix-matrix multiplications (DIMM) are still handled by the FPGA fabric. As a result, the benefits of IMC are largely limited to static-weight DL models, whereas Transformer-based models, which require frequent nonlinear and DIMM operations, achieve only limited benefit. In addition, ADCs within the IMC block consume more than 70% of area and power, further limiting system efficiency and scalability. To address these issues, we propose a novel FPGA architecture that integrates an ADC-free IMC alternative into FPGAs, replacing the conventional ADC with analog content-addressable memories (ACAMs) that natively perform nonlinear operations inside the IMC block. To fully utilize this new block, we conduct an FPGA-aware design-space exploration that determines the optimal crossbar sizes while balancing FPGA area, flexibility, and DL performance. We further deploy an efficient mapping that uses ACAMs to efficiently perform DIMM operations, extending architectural applicability to Attention computation. Across CNN and Transformer-based benchmarks, our proposed FPGA architecture achieves up to40×40\times和and1.9×1.9\times更高的能源效率，以及higher energy efficiency, and4.1×4.1\times和and2.5×2.5\times面积效率。总体而言，所提出的架构显着提高了 FPGA DL 推理效率，并在跨长输入序列的基于 Transformer 的工作负载上显示出强大的效率增益，从而推进了领域专用 FPGA 设计。area efficiency. Overall, the proposed architecture significantly improves the FPGA DL inference efficiency and shows robust efficiency gain on Transformer-based workloads across long input sequences, advancing domain-specialized FPGA design.

IV 拟议的系统Proposed System

最近的 IMC 研究提出了一种无 ADC 的 IMC 模块，Recent IMC research has proposed an ADC-free IMC block,NL-DPE，具有原生非线性计算能力, with native nonlinear computation capability[23, 22, 21]与普通基于 ADC 的 IMC 模块相比，显示出极高的效率。 我们将此块作为一流的硬块集成到 FPGA 架构中，为 CNN 和 Transformer 工作负载解锁其非线性功能。 ReRAM 和 FPGA 的 CMOS 逻辑在不同层中制造，互不干扰：ReRAM 单元是晶体管上方金属互连中的后端 (BEOL) 沉积物，与前端晶体管节点解耦。因此，FPGA 的可编程结构可以保持在领先的 CMOS 节点，而 ReRAM 单元则占据上面的金属层，不会造成逻辑密度损失。and shows great efficiency compared to normal ADC-based IMC blocks. We integrate this block into the FPGA fabric as a first-class hard block, unlocking its nonlinear functionality for CNN and Transformer workloads. ReRAM and the FPGA’s CMOS logic are fabricated in different layers and do not interfere: the ReRAM cells are a back-end-of-line (BEOL) deposit in the metal interconnect above the transistors, decoupled from the front-end transistor node. The FPGA’s programmable fabric can therefore remain at the leading-edge CMOS node while the ReRAM cells occupy the metal layers above, incurring no logic-density penalty.

IV-B 噪声和精度建模Noise & Precision Modeling

与所有模拟内存计算一样，ReRAM 交叉开关和 ACAM 都会受到设备噪声的影响，主要来自不精确的电导编程和读取时电导漂移。为了补偿，我们采用噪声感知微调（NAF）方法Like all analog in-memory computing, both the ReRAM crossbar and the ACAM are subject to device noise, primarily from imprecise conductance programming and read-time conductance drifts. To compensate, we adopt the noise-aware finetuning (NAF) methodology of[23, 21]，它针对硬件校准的噪声模型联合优化了交叉开关权重和 ACAM 阈值。 NAF 在生成比特流之前完全在软件中执行，部署后无需针对每个设备进行校准。在 INT8 精度下，它在 CNN 和 Transformer 工作负载上将精度恢复到 FP32 基线的百分之几以内，并且 BERT-Tiny 基本上没有损失, which jointly optimizes the crossbar weights and ACAM thresholds against a hardware-calibrated noise model. NAF is performed entirely in software prior to bitstream generation and requires no per-device calibration after deployment. At INT8 precision, it restores accuracy to within a few percent of the FP32 baseline across both CNN and Transformer workloads, with essentially no loss on BERT-Tiny[23].

七VII结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出In this work, we present尼法NIFA，一种异构 FPGA 架构，将无 ADC、基于 ACAM 的模拟 IMC 块集成为一流的硬块，从而在 IMC 内核内实现本机非线性计算。系统性两轮 FPGA 感知 DSE 共同优化了 FPGA 面积、DL 吞吐量和架构灵活性之间的权衡，高效映射将 IMC 模块的适用性扩展到 Transformer Attention 操作中的动态输入矩阵乘法。在 CNN 和 BERT-Tiny 基准测试中，所提出的架构比基于最先进的模拟 IMC 的 FPGA 架构具有显着的能源和性能效率。 最重要的是，随着输入序列长度的增长，这一优势仍然存在, a heterogeneous FPGA architecture that integrates ADC-free, ACAM-based analog IMC blocks as first-class hard blocks, enabling native nonlinear computation inside the IMC core. A systematic two-round FPGA-aware DSE jointly optimizes the trade-off between FPGA area, DL throughput, and architectural flexibility, and an efficient mapping extends the IMC block’s applicability to dynamic-input matrix multiplications in Transformer Attention operation. Across CNN and BERT-Tiny benchmarks, the proposed architecture demonstrates significant energy and performance efficiency over state-of-the-art analog IMC based FPGA architectures.
