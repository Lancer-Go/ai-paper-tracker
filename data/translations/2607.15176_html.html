多模态大语言模型的基准测试Benchmarking Multimodal Large Language Models for
科学可视化素养Scientific Visualization Literacy

Patrick Phuoc Do e-mail: mdo23@nd.edu    Chau M. Ta e-mail: cta@nd.edu    Chaoli Wang e-mail: chaoli.wang@nd.edu University of Notre Dame
摘要Abstract

多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 越来越多地用于解释可视化，但当前的评估仍然主要以图表为中心，并提供有限的科学可视化 (SciVis) 理解证据。我们在科学可视化素养评估测试中对 6 个 MLLM 进行了基准测试，这是一种标准化的 SciVis 素养评估，包含基于 18 种科学可视化和插图的 49 个项目，涵盖 8 种技术和 11 种任务类型。我们在封闭世界协议下评估了三个闭源模型和三个开源模型，并使用 485 名人类参与者的数据比较了它们的性能。结果表明，当前的 MLLM 并未表现出统一的科学素养。 Gemini 是整体上最强的模型，超过了评估子集的人类平均值，而开源模型仍低于人类基线。不同技术和任务的性能极不平衡：模型在科学插图、搜索和空间理解方面表现最佳，但在基于纹理和基于集成的可视化以及定量估计方面表现不佳。误差分析揭示了细粒度定量估计、流向解释和接地编码解释中反复出现的失败。这些发现将 SciVis 素养定位为评估多模式人工智能系统的必要基准维度。Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) are increasingly used to interpret visualizations, yet current evaluations remain largely chart-centric and provide limited evidence of understanding of scientific visualization (SciVis). We benchmark six MLLMs on the scientific visualization literacy assessment test, a standardized SciVis literacy assessment comprising 49 items based on 18 scientific visualizations and illustrations, spanning 8 techniques and 11 task types. We evaluate three closed-source and three open-source models under a closed-world protocol and compare their performance using data from 485 human participants. Results show that current MLLMs do not exhibit uniform SciVis literacy. Gemini is the strongest model overall, exceeding the human mean across the evaluated subsets, whereas the open-source models remain below the human baseline. Performance is highly uneven across techniques and tasks: models perform best on scientific illustration, search, and spatial understanding, but struggle on texture-based and integration-based visualizations and on quantitative estimation. Error analysis reveals recurring failures in fine-grained quantitative estimation, flow-direction interpretation, and grounded encoding interpretation. These findings position SciVis literacy as a necessary benchmark dimension for evaluating multimodal AI systems.\热\hot我们的代码和模型输出可在以下位置公开获取：Our code and model outputs are publicly available athttps://github.com/patdmp/mllm-scivis-lit-benchmark.

简介 科学可视化 (SciVis) 长期以来一直是探索、分析和交流复杂科学数据的重要媒介，包括模拟输出、医学成像、气候数据和其他基于空间的科学现象Introduction Scientific visualization (SciVis) has long served as an important medium for exploring, analyzing, and communicating complex scientific data, including simulation output, medical imaging, climate data, and other spatially grounded scientific phenomena[25, 19]。与信息可视化 (InfoVis) 相比，SciVis 通常表示具有内在空间或物理结构的数据。这种区别与基于物理的数据和给定空间化的概念密切相关，这使 SciVis 与 InfoVis 有很大区别. In contrast to information visualization (InfoVis), SciVis typically represents data with intrinsic spatial or physical structure. This distinction is closely tied to the notions of physically based data and given spatialization, which differentiate much of SciVis from InfoVis[31]。此外，在解释 SciVis 时，观看者需要推理视觉表示中编码的空间结构、多元关系以及随时间变化或具有物理意义的模式. In addition, when interpreting SciVis, viewers need to reason about spatial structure, multivariate relationships, and time-varying or physically meaningful patterns encoded in the visual representation[19, 31].

与此同时，多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 处理图像和文本以及支持可视化解释、科学交流和可视化分析工作流程的能力迅速提高Meanwhile, multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have rapidly advanced in their ability to process images alongside text and to support visualization interpretation, scientific communication, and visual analytics workflows[1, 2, 34]。然而，现有证据表明它们在可视化任务上的表现仍然参差不齐，目前的评估主要集中在 InfoVis 而不是 SciVis. However, existing evidence suggests that their performance on visualization tasks remains uneven, and current evaluations have focused primarily on InfoVis rather than SciVis[4, 16]。因此，当前的 MLLM 是否拥有. As a result, whether current MLLMs possess科学素养SciVis literacy仍然是一个悬而未决的问题remains an open question[32].

为了弥补这一差距，我们使用科学可视化素养评估测试 (SVLAT) 对 MLLM 进行基准测试To address this gap, we benchmark MLLMs using the scientific visualization literacy assessment test (SVLAT)[11]是最近推出的一种工具，专门用于衡量非专家阅读、理解和解释科学可视化和插图的程度。由于 SVLAT 在封闭世界设计下涵盖了多种 SciVis 技术和任务，因此它为评估 MLLM 中的 SciVis 理解提供了合适的基准。, a recently introduced instrument designed specifically to measure how well non-experts read, understand, and interpret scientific visualizations and illustrations. Because SVLAT spans multiple SciVis techniques and tasks under a closed-world design, it provides a suitable benchmark for evaluating SciVis understanding in MLLMs.

使用 SVLAT，我们的目标是检查当前的 MLLM 是否表现出可衡量的 SciVis 素养，如果是的话，他们在技术和任务类别方面的优势和劣势在哪里。更广泛地说，我们将 SciVis 素养定位为多模式人工智能系统缺失的基准维度。通过这样做，我们的目标是为未来的可信模型评估、模型辅助科学传播以及围绕科学视觉内容的人机交互等工作提供更清晰的评估目标。Using SVLAT, our goal is to examine whether current MLLMs demonstrate measurable SciVis literacy and, if so, where their strengths and weaknesses lie across techniques and task categories. More broadly, we position SciVis literacy as a missing benchmark dimension for multimodal AI systems. By doing so, we aim to contribute a clearer evaluation target for future work on trustworthy model assessment, model-assisted scientific communication, and human–AI interaction around scientific visual content.

1 相关工作Related Work

可视化素养评估。Visualization literacy assessment.可视化素养是指观看者阅读、理解和解释数据可视化的能力Visualization literacy refers to a viewer’s ability to read, understand, and interpret data visualizations[6, 23, 5]。男孩等人。. Building on this psychometric perspective, Lee et al.[21]开发了 VLAT，这是一种针对非专家用户的标准化评估，可衡量 12 种可视化类型和 8 种可视化任务的读写能力。卡尔维developed VLAT, a standardized assessment for non-expert users that measures literacy across 12 visualization types and 8 visualization tasks. CALVI[14]通过关注对误导性或错误的可视化的批判性思考，将可视化素养的概念扩展到常规阅读之外。为了提高实用性并减少管理时间，后来的工作引入了更短、更专业的工具。例如，迷你VLATextended the notion of visualization literacy beyond routine reading by focusing on critical thinking about misleading or erroneous visualizations. To improve practicality and reduce administrative time, later work introduced shorter, more specialized instruments. For instance, Mini-VLAT[28]将 VLAT 压缩为简短但有效的简短测试，同时保留其大部分测量实用性。 VLAT 和 CALVI 的自适应版本condensed VLAT into a brief but effective short-form test while preserving much of its measurement utility. Adaptive versions of VLAT and CALVI[9]减少测试负担，同时保持可靠性和有效性。这些研究表明，可视化素养是 InfoVis 内评估的一个成熟主题。reduced testing burden while retaining reliability and validity. These studies show that visualization literacy is a mature topic for assessment within InfoVis.

科学素养。SciVis literacy.与 InfoVis 相比，SciVis 引入了额外的解释要求。科学可视化通常使用固有的空间和物理结构对数据进行编码，要求用户推理几何、深度、拓扑、运动以及标量、矢量或张量场之间的关系。先前有关 SciVis 任务的工作强调了针对科学数据分析（特别是体数据和空间推理）量身定制的任务分类的重要性Compared with InfoVis, SciVis introduces additional interpretive demands. Scientific visualizations often encode data with inherent spatial and physical structure, requiring users to reason about geometry, depth, topology, motion, and relationships among scalar, vector, or tensor fields. Prior work on SciVis tasks has emphasized the importance of task taxonomies tailored to scientific data analysis, especially for volume data and spatial reasoning[20]。这些特征使得 SciVis 素养与传统图表背景下的 InfoVis 素养有着质的不同。尽管 SciVis 在科学分析和交流中非常重要，但直到最近，该领域的标准化评估仍然基本上不存在。超快速拉特. These characteristics make SciVis literacy qualitatively different from InfoVis literacy in the context of conventional charts and graphs. Despite the importance of SciVis in scientific analysis and communication, standardized assessment in this area has remained largely absent until recently. SVLAT[11]是最近提出的一种基于心理测量的工具，用于测量 SciVis 素养。 SVLAT 围绕 8 种规范的 SciVis 技术和 11 项解释任务而设计，使读写能力能够以更好地反映科学视觉表示需求的方式进行测量。由于 SVLAT 是针对科学视觉材料进行标准化、验证和明确构建的，因此它为在 SciVis 素养上对 MLLM 进行基准测试提供了适当的基础，而不是依赖于图表导向的代理。is a recently proposed psychometrically grounded instrument for measuring SciVis literacy. SVLAT is designed around 8 canonical SciVis techniques and 11 interpretation tasks, enabling literacy to be measured in a way that better reflects the demands of scientific visual representations. Because SVLAT is standardized, validated, and explicitly constructed for scientific visual material, it provides an appropriate foundation for benchmarking MLLMs on SciVis literacy rather than relying on chart-oriented proxies.

MLLM 和可视化理解。MLLMs and visualization understanding.致力于对最初通过图表和图形问答的基准数据集开发的可视化数据进行机器理解。早期数据集，例如FigureQAWork on machine understanding of visualized data initially developed through benchmark datasets for chart and figure question answering. Early datasets such as FigureQA[18]和DVQAand DVQA[17]分别专注于综合科学风格的图形和条形图，建立受控测试平台，对绘制的数据进行视觉推理。绘图QAfocused on synthetic scientific-style figures and bar charts, respectively, establishing controlled testbeds for visual reasoning over plotted data. PlotQA[26]通过引入开放词汇和数字答案，更接近现实的科学情节，而 ChartQAmoved closer to realistic scientific plots by introducing open-vocabulary and numerical answers, while ChartQA[24]将任务扩展到需要视觉和逻辑推理的图表。最近的基准进一步强调了当前 MLLM 的局限性。字符Xivexpanded the task to charts requiring both visual and logical reasoning. More recent benchmarks have further stressed the limitations of current MLLMs. CharXiv[33]评估了从科学论文中收集的真实图表，并表明先前的基准测试可能高估了模型的能力，而 MultiChartQAevaluated realistic charts collected from scientific papers and showed that prior benchmarks can overestimate model capability, and MultiChartQA[35]研究了多图表推理，其中模型必须整合多个相关可视化的信息。除了仅图表设置之外，SPIQAstudied multi-chart reasoning, where models must integrate information across several related visualizations. Beyond chart-only settings, SPIQA[29]评估了对科学论文中嵌入的图形和表格的多模态问答，将问题扩展到基于文档的科学图形理解。evaluated multimodal question answering over figures and tables embedded in scientific papers, extending the problem toward document-grounded scientific figure understanding.

在可视化社区中，最近的工作已经开始通过可视化素养本身的视角来研究 MLLM。本德克和斯塔斯科Within the visualization community, recent work has begun to study MLLMs through the lens of visualization literacy itself. Bendeck and Stasko[4]在一系列与可视化读写相关的任务上评估了 GPT-4，发现了优点和缺点的混合模式：该模型可以识别更高级别的趋势和极值，但它在精确的值检索、颜色辨别和一致性方面遇到了困难。洪等人。evaluated GPT-4 on a suite of visualization-literacy-related tasks and found a mixed pattern of strengths and weaknesses: the model could identify higher-level trends and extreme values, but it struggled with precise value retrieval, color discrimination, and consistency. Hong et al.[16]然后，通过对修改后的 VLAT 式可视化进行测试，检验法学硕士是否真正具备可视化素养，该可视化旨在探讨泛化和对视觉证据而不是先验知识的依赖。他们的研究结果表明，在良性视觉修改下，模型性能可能会大幅下降，引发人们对鲁棒性和真正视觉基础的担忧。达斯等人。then examined whether LLMs truly possess visualization literacy by testing them on modified VLAT-style visualizations designed to probe generalization and reliance on visual evidence rather than prior knowledge. Their findings suggest that model performance can degrade substantially under benign visual modifications, raising concerns about robustness and genuine visual grounding. Das et al.[10]进一步表明，结构化提示可以显着提高 VLAT 类型任务的表现，强调测量的能力不仅取决于模型本身，还取决于评估协议。 Varona 等人补充了这些研究。further showed that structured prompting can substantially improve performance on VLAT-style tasks, highlighting that measured capability depends not only on the model itself but also on the evaluation protocol. Complementing these studies, Varona et al.[13]通过对错误响应进行定性编码，分析了 MLLM 中的可视化素养障碍，提供了故障模式分类，有助于解释为什么即使基准准确性看起来具有竞争力，模型也会陷入困境。董和克里森analyzed visualization-literacy barriers in MLLMs by qualitatively coding erroneous responses, providing a taxonomy of failure modes that helps explain why models struggle even when benchmark accuracy appears competitive. Dong and Crisan[12]进一步探讨了用于图表理解的视觉语言模型的内部推理行为，认为仅答案正确性不足以表征模型中的可视化素养。further probed the internal reasoning behavior of vision-language models for chart understanding, arguing that answer correctness alone is insufficient for characterizing visualization literacy in models.

大多数现有数据集、评估和提示研究都侧重于 InfoVis 风格的图表或类似图表的图形，而不是 SciVis 本地表示。总之，这些研究表明，MLLM 对可视化任务的评估仍然主要以图表为中心，而 SciVis 原生评估相对而言尚未得到充分探索。Most existing datasets, evaluations, and prompting studies focus on InfoVis-style charts or chart-like figures rather than SciVis-native representations. Together, these studies show that evaluation of MLLMs on visualization tasks remains largely chart-centric, leaving SciVis-native assessment comparatively underexplored.

2 方法论Methodology

评估仪器。Assessment instrument.我们使用 SVLAT 评估 MLLMWe evaluate MLLMs using SVLAT[11]，SciVis 素养的标准化评估。 SVLAT 包含基于 18 种科学可视化和插图的 49 个项目，包括静态图像和动画，涵盖 8 种 SciVis 技术和 11 种任务类型。它遵循封闭世界设计，其中每个项目仅使用提供的可视化和标题来回答，使其适合评估 MLLM 中的 SciVis 素养，同时限制对外部知识的依赖。, a standardized assessment of SciVis literacy. SVLAT consists of 49 items based on 18 scientific visualizations and illustrations, including static images and animations, spanning 8 SciVis techniques and 11 task types. It follows a closed-world design in which each item is intended to be answerable using only the provided visualization and caption, making it suitable for assessing SciVis literacy in MLLMs while limiting reliance on external knowledge.

提示设计。Prompt design.我们对所有模型使用单一指令提示来标准化评估设置。该提示将模型定义为 SciVis 专家，并明确限制其仅使用提供的可视化和标题。它还要求模型返回一个结构化 JSON 对象，其中包含所选选项、完整答案文本和简要理由。为了阻止不受支持的猜测，提示包括We use a single instruction prompt for all models to standardize the evaluation setting. It also requires the model to return a structured JSON object containing the selected option, the full answer text, and a brief rationale. 评估协议。Evaluation protocol.我们每次在 SVLAT 上针对一个问题评估每个模型。对于每个项目，我们使用不同的随机种子运行模型 10 次，并计算运行期间的平均得分。这种重复运行的协议有助于减少残留采样变异性或后端不确定性的影响。 除了选定的答案之外，我们还记录模型对每个答案的基本原理。这些解释用于检查模型的推理是否基于所提供的可视化和标题，而不是基于不受支持的先验知识或推测。尽管基本原理不直接用于对准确性进行评分，但它提供了关于模型如何得出答案以及错误响应是否源于对视觉证据的误解、过度自信的猜测或对外部知识的依赖的定性证据。 我们还通过 SciVis 技术和任务类别来分析性能，以确定系统的优势和劣势。We evaluate each model on SVLAT one question at a time. For each item, we run the model 10 times using different random seeds and compute the average score across runs. This repeated-run protocol helps reduce the impact of residual sampling variability or backend nondeterminism. In addition to the selected answer, we record the model’s rationale for each response. These explanations are used to examine whether the model’s reasoning is grounded in the provided visualization and caption rather than unsupported prior knowledge or speculation. Although the rationale is not used directly for scoring accuracy, it provides qualitative evidence about how the model arrives at its answers and whether incorrect responses stem from misinterpretation of visual evidence, overconfident guessing, or reliance on external knowledge. 3.4 误差分析Error Analysis

\热\hot

前面的结果表明，SVLAT 上的 MLLM 性能在不同技术、任务和模型系列之间存在很大差异。为了更好地理解这些差异的根源，我们定性地检查模型的基本原理，以识别模型如何解释、误解或未能根据视觉证据的重复模式。The preceding results show that MLLM performance on SVLAT varies substantially across techniques, tasks, and model families. 4 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and Future Work

我们的结果表明，当前的 MLLM 尚未表现出统一的科学素养。不同技术和任务的性能差异很大。在各种技术中，模型在以下方面表现最佳Our results show that current MLLMs do not yet exhibit uniform SciVis literacy. Performance varies substantially across techniques and tasks. Across techniques, the models perform best on科学插图Scientific Illustration，而双子座也表现强劲, while Gemini also performs strongly on表面渲染Surface Rendering, 体绘制Volume Rendering, 和, and混合渲染Mixed Rendering。在各个任务中，他们最强的结果是. Across tasks, their strongest results are in搜索Search和and空间理解Spatial Understanding，特别是在, particularly in存在/缺席Presence/Absence, 计数Counting, 和, and交叉口Intersection。然而，开源模型总体上仍然不够稳健，并且表现出更狭窄、更不平衡的优势。定性分析表明，它们反映了细粒度定量估计、基于纹理的可视化中的流向解释以及高级 SciVis 解释期间不受支持的编码推断中反复出现的弱点。这些模式表明，当前的 MLLM 通常可以识别任务的一般结构，但当成功取决于精确测量、解码密集动态场中的局部运动或在可视化和标题指定的实际编码中建立更高级别的判断时，仍然会遇到困难。. However, the open-source models remain less robust overall and show narrower, more uneven strengths. The qualitative analysis shows that they reflect recurring weaknesses in fine-grained quantitative estimation, flow-direction interpretation in texture-based visualizations, and unsupported encoding inference during higher-level SciVis interpretation. These patterns suggest that current MLLMs can often identify the general structure of a task, but still struggle when success depends on precise measurement, decoding local motion in dense dynamic fields, or grounding higher-level judgments in the actual encoding specified by the visualization and caption.

这项研究有几个局限性。This study has several limitations.\热\hotGPT 和 Claude 通过帧提取对动画项目进行评估，因为他们的 API 在评估过程中不支持直接视频输入。GPT and Claude were evaluated on animation items via frame extraction, as their APIs did not support direct video input during the evaluation.\热\hot此外，评估基于单一基准SVLAT和单一标准化提示格式，开源比较仅限于轻量级模型。In addition, the evaluation is based on a single benchmark, SVLAT, and a single standardized prompt format, and the open-source comparison is limited to lightweight models.\热\hot此外，某些任务仅由一个或两个项目表示，这限制了每个任务估计的可靠性，并且可能无法完全捕获这些任务中的难度范围。未来的工作应该将此基准扩展到更大、更多样化的模型、更丰富的提示和工具使用设置以及其他 SciVis 原生评估工具。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，当前的 MLLM 具有选择性能力，而不是广泛的科学素养。因此，科学可视化能力是一个必要的基准维度，因为科学可视化需要空间、时间和基于编码的解释形式，而一般的多模式评估无法捕获这些解释。Additionally, some tasks are represented by only one or two items, which limits the reliability of per-task estimates and may not fully capture the range of difficulty within those tasks. Future work should extend this benchmark to larger and more diverse models, richer prompting and tool-use settings, and additional SciVis-native evaluation instruments. Overall, our findings show that current MLLMs are selectively capable rather than broadly SciVis literate. SciVis literacy is, therefore, a necessary benchmark dimension because scientific visualizations require forms of spatial, temporal, and encoding-grounded interpretation that are not captured by general multimodal evaluations.
