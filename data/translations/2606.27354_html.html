6 结论和局限性Conclusion and Limitations

我们引入了误差条件神经求解器（ENS），这是一种循环框架，它将偏微分方程残差场视为直接输入而不是外部优化目标，并学习残差场的非线性校正策略。与对残差应用一阶或二阶数值方案的现有混合方法相比，ENS 在不同 PDE 系列的大多数设置中获得了最高的预测精度。我们进一步表明，最小化 PDE 残差是病态系统中重建精度的不可靠代理，这有助于解释现有混合方法中低残差和次优重建之间的差距。 ENS 的优势在分布转移任务上很明显，包括改变系数和跨方程转移，这表明了一条通向通用方法的道路We introduced Error-Conditioned Neural Solvers (ENS), a recurrent framework that treats the PDE residual field as a direct input rather than an external optimization objective, and learns a nonlinear correction policy over the residual field. Compared to existing hybrid methods, which apply first- or second-order numerical schemes to the residual, ENS attains the highest prediction accuracy in the large majority of settings across diverse PDE families. We further show that minimizing the PDE residual is an unreliable proxy for reconstruction accuracy in ill-conditioned systems, which helps explain the gap between low residuals and suboptimal reconstructions in existing hybrid methods. ENS’s advantage is pronounced on distribution-shift tasks, including changed coefficients and cross-equation transfer, suggesting a path toward a general神经解算器neural solver对于看不见的系统。for unseen systems.

我们的实验仅限于相对简单的二维系统，并假设推论时控制方程已知。由于 ENS 将残差读取为输入而不是将其最小化，因此我们假设它更能容忍来自离散化误差或部分已知方程的不完美残差，这会误导基于优化的校正器。将 ENS 扩展到三维问题以及具有真实、嘈杂观测的环境是未来工作的一个令人兴奋的方向。Our experiments are limited to relatively simple 2D systems and assume the governing equations are known at inference. Because ENS reads the residual as an input rather than minimizing it, we hypothesize that it is more forgiving of imperfect residuals from discretization error or partially known equations, which would mislead an optimization-based corrector. Extending ENS to three-dimensional problems and to settings with real, noisy observations is an exciting direction for future work.

经典数值求解器。Classical numerical solvers.

求解偏微分方程的主要经典方法——有限元法、有限差分法和谱法——依赖于将控制方程离散化为大型线性或非线性系统The dominant classical approaches to solving PDEs — finite element methods, finite difference methods, and spectral methods — rely on discretizing the governing equations into large linear or nonlinear systems(Aliabadi, 2020; Ŝolín, 2005)。尽管这些方法具有强大的收敛保证，但它们的成本随着分辨率和维数以及迭代求解器（例如多重网格和 Krylov 方法）的变化而不利. ENS is motivated precisely by this bottleneck: it amortizes the correction process across a family of PDE instances, replacing expensive per-instance numerical optimization with a single learned recurrent policy.
