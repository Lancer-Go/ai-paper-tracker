However, existing reinforcement learning post-training paradigms, such as Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), typically assign the same set-level scalar reward to every candidate in the set. This leads to noisy training signals where poor candidates free-ride on the high reward produced by a single strong peer, resulting in suboptimal exploration. To address this, we propose沙普Shap莱-ley-E增强型 GRPO (ShapE-GRPO)。通过利用集合级效用的排列不变性， 我们从合作博弈论中推导出 Shapley 增强公式 将设定级别的奖励分解为细粒度的、特定于候选人的信号。我们表明，我们的公式保留了 Shapley 值的基本公理，同时保持多项式时间复杂度的计算效率。根据经验，ShapE-GRPO 在不同的数据集上始终优于标准 GRPO，并且在训练期间加速了收敛。nhanced GRPO (ShapE-GRPO). By leveraging the permutation-invariant nature of set-level utility, we derive a Shapley-enhanced formulation from cooperative game theory to decompose set-level rewards into granular, candidate-specific signals. We show that our formulation preserves the fundamental axioms of the Shapley value while remaining computationally efficient with polynomial-time complexity. Empirically, ShapE-GRPO consistently outperforms standard GRPO across diverse datasets with accelerated convergence during training.

In such settings, the objective of generation is no longer to maximize the reward of each individual candidate independently, but rather to maximize the usefulness of the entire set of candidates presented to the user. As a result, the probability that at least one candidate matches the user’s interests increases. In applications such as shopping recommendations, this directly translates into higher click-through rates and improved downstream value.

1.1 我们的贡献Our Contributions

Shapley 增强型多候选生成奖励分配。Shapley-Enhanced reward allocation for multi-candidate generation.

我们提出了 ShapE-GRPO，这是一种 Shapley 增强的奖励分配框架，具有适当的粒度，可为多候选生成设置中的 RL 后期训练提供有效的候选级奖励信号。它填补了 LLM 培训后如何分配设定级别奖励的空白，并且潜在的候选人级别 Shapley 值满足合作博弈论的标准公理。We propose ShapE-GRPO, a Shapley-enhanced reward allocation framework with an appropriate granularity that provides effective candidate-level reward signals for RL post-training in multi-candidate generation settings. It fills the gap in how rewards at the set level can be assigned during LLM post-training, and the underlying candidate-level Shapley values satisfy the standard axioms from cooperative game theory.

公正、信息丰富且可有效计算的候选人级别奖励。Unbiased, informative and efficiently computable candidate-level rewards.

我们从理论上证明，在温和的假设下，所提出的候选级沙普利奖励是集合级奖励的无偏估计，并提供有意义的梯度，指导对表现更好的候选者进行培训。我们进一步证明，在 LLM 作为推荐器的设置中，可以在多项式时间内计算 Shapley 值，确保步数方面的更快收敛不会以增加每步训练时间为代价。We theoretically show that under mild assumptions, the proposed candidate-level Shapley rewards are unbiased estimators of the set-level reward and provide meaningful gradients that guide training toward higher-performing candidates. We further prove that in the LLM-as-a-recommender setting, the Shapley values can be computed in polynomial time, ensuring that faster convergence in terms of the number of steps does not come at the cost of increased per-step training time.

改进的性能和新的推荐基准。Improved performance and a new recommendation benchmark.

我们在多个 LLM 生成任务（包括摘要和代码生成）上评估了 ShapE-GRPO，并表明与标准 GRPO 基线相比，它持续提高了集合级性能。此外，我们还发布了专门针对 LLM 作为推荐者场景的 Netflix 电影推荐数据集，支持未来对多候选推荐和语言模型集级优化的研究。We evaluate ShapE-GRPO on multiple LLM generation tasks, including summarization and code generation, and show that it consistently improves set-level performance compared with the standard GRPO baseline. Furthermore, we release a curated Netflix movie recommendation dataset tailored to the LLM-as-a-recommender scenario, enabling future research on multi-candidate recommendation and set-level optimization for language models.

6 结论与讨论Conclusion and Discussion

在本文中，我们提出了 ShapE-GRPO，一种 Shapley 增强型 法学硕士作为推荐人设置的政策优化框架。我们从理论和经验上都表明，ShapE-GRPO 在不同的模型架构和任务中持续改进了标准 GRPO 算法的性能。 我们的主要见解是，LLM 作为推荐者问题中的奖励信号是在设定级别自然生成的。 根据每个候选人的边际分配奖励 贡献，ShapE-GRPO提供了一种排列不变、有效且高效的奖励分配方案。所提出的 Shapley 增强优势保留了 Shapley 值的原始动机，同时在候选级别设置中保持计算上的易处理性。 总之，这些结果表明，候选人级别的奖励分配为法学硕士作为推荐者场景中的强化学习提供了原则性且实用的解决方案。In this paper, we propose ShapE-GRPO, a Shapley-enhanced policy optimization framework for the LLM-as-a-recommender setting. We show both theoretically and empirically that ShapE-GRPO consistently improves the performance of the standard GRPO algorithm across different model architectures and tasks. Our key insight is that reward signals in LLM-as-a-recommender problems are naturally generated at the set level. By allocating rewards according to each candidate’s marginal contribution, ShapE-GRPO provides a permutation-invariant, effective and efficient reward allocation scheme. The proposed Shapley-enhanced advantage preserves the original motivation of Shapley value while remaining computationally tractable in the candidate-level setting. Together, these results demonstrate that candidate-level reward allocation provides a principled and practical solution for reinforcement learning in LLM-as-a-recommender scenarios.

RLHF 和 LLM 对齐RLHF and LLM Alignment

来自人类反馈的强化学习已成为使大型语言模型与人类偏好保持一致的主导范例。早期工作表明，通过人类比较训练的奖励模型可以指导复杂任务中的强化学习策略Reinforcement learning from human feedback has become the dominant paradigm for aligning large language models with human preferences. Early work demonstrated that reward models trained from human comparisons can guide reinforcement learning policies in complex tasks(Christiano et al., 2017)，一个后来适应于 InstructGPT 等系统中的大型语言模型的框架, a framework that was later adapted to large language models in systems such as InstructGPT(Ouyang et al., 2022)。从那时起，越来越多的工作探索了偏好优化和对齐的替代算法和公式，包括基于偏好的强化学习方法、直接优化目标和改进的训练管道. Since then, a growing body of work has explored alternative algorithms and formulations for preference optimization and alignment, including preference-based reinforcement learning methods, direct optimization objectives, and improved training pipelines(Stiennon et al., 2020; Bai et al., 2022; Rafailov et al., 2023; Yuan et al., 2023; Shao et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025)。多项研究进一步研究了基于 RLHF 的训练的理论基础、稳定性特性和局限性. Several studies have further investigated the theoretical foundations, stability properties, and limitations of RLHF-based training(Gao et al., 2023; Casper et al., 2023)，而其他人提出了可扩展的替代方案，绕过显式强化学习，但仍然优化偏好信号, while others propose scalable alternatives that bypass explicit RL but still optimize preference signals(Ethayarajh et al., 2024)。尽管算法和优化策略存在差异，但这些方法都有一个共同的结构假设：为每个生成的响应分配一个奖励。在典型的 RLHF 管道中，模型会针对提示生成一个或多个候选建议，奖励模型会评估整个响应，之后 PPO 或相关变体等策略优化算法会更新模型以最大化该单一稀疏奖励。虽然这种公式已被证明对于对齐单个响应是有效的，但它并不能自然地捕获模型生成候选推荐集并且效用取决于整个集合的覆盖范围或多样性的场景。这种设置自然出现在许多应用程序中，用户从多个候选者中选择一个响应。与之前专注于序列级奖励的工作相比，我们的工作明确地对候选级奖励信号进行建模，并研究如何使用 Shapley 增强框架在多候选生成中在候选者之间分配奖励。. Despite differences in algorithms and optimization strategies, these approaches share a common structural assumption: a single reward is assigned to each generated response. In typical RLHF pipelines, a model produces one or several candidate recommendations for a prompt, and a reward model evaluates the whole response, after which policy optimization algorithms such as PPO or related variants update the model to maximize this single sparse reward. While this formulation has proven effective for aligning single responses, it does not naturally capture scenarios where models generate sets of candidate recommendations and the utility depends on the coverage or diversity of the entire set. Such settings arise naturally in many applications, where users select one response from multiple candidates. In contrast to prior work that focuses on sequence-level rewards, our work explicitly models candidate-level reward signals and studies how to allocate reward among candidates in multi-candidate generation using a Shapley-enhanced framework.
