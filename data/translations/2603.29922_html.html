摘要Abstract

目的：Purpose:

研究综合生成的分形数据是否可用于训练动态 MRI 重建的深度学习 (DL) 模型，从而避免与心脏 MR 训练数据集相关的隐私、许可和可用性限制。To investigate whether synthetically generated fractal data can be used to train deep learning (DL) models for dynamic MRI reconstruction, thereby avoiding the privacy, licensing, and availability limitations associated with cardiac MR training datasets.

方法：Methods:

使用四元数 Julia 分形生成训练数据集以生成 2D+时间图像。模拟多线圈 MRI 采集，生成配对的完全采样和径向欠采样 k 空间数据。使用这些分形数据 (F-DL) 训练 3D UNet 深度伪影抑制模型，并与根据心脏 MRI 数据训练的相同模型 (CMR-DL) 进行比较。两种模型均通过前瞻性采集的 10 名患者的径向实时心脏 MRI 进行评估。将重建与压缩感知（CS）和低秩深度图像先验（LR-DIP）进行比较。所有重建均根据图像质量进行排名，同时将心室容积和射血分数与参考屏气电影 MRI 进行比较。A training dataset was generated using quaternion Julia fractals to produce 2D+time images. Multi-coil MRI acquisition was simulated to generate paired fully sampled and radially undersampled k-space data. A 3D UNet deep artefact suppression model was trained using these fractal data (F-DL) and compared with an identical model trained on cardiac MRI data (CMR-DL). Both models were evaluated on prospectively acquired radial real-time cardiac MRI from 10 patients. Reconstructions were compared against compressed sensing(CS) and lowrank deep image prior (LR-DIP). All reconstrctuions were ranked for image quality, while ventricular volumes and ejection fraction were compared with reference breath-hold cine MRI.

结果：Results:

同时均优于 CS 和 LR-DIP (p<0.001)。 F-DL 得出的心室容积和功能与 CMR-DL 相似，与参考电影成像相比，没有显示出显着的偏差和可接受的一致性限制。然而，LR-DIP 存在显着偏差 (p=0.016) 且一致性范围更广。while both outperformed CS and LR-DIP (p<0.001). Ventricular volumes and function derived from F-DL were similar to CMR-DL, showing no significant bias and accptable limits of agreement compared to reference cine imaging. However, LR-DIP had a signifcant bias (p=0.016) and wider lmits of agreement.

结论：Conclusion:

使用合成分形数据训练的深度学习模型可以重建实时心脏 MRI，其图像质量和临床测量结果与使用真实心脏 MRI 数据训练的模型相当。分形训练数据为临床数据集提供了一种开放、可扩展的替代方案，并且可以为动态 MRI 开发更通用的深度学习重建模型。DL models trained using synthetic fractal data can reconstruct real-time cardiac MRI with image quality and clinical measurements comparable to models trained on true cardiac MRI data. Fractal training data provide an open, scalable alternative to clinical datasets and may enable development of more generalisable DL reconstruction models for dynamic MRI.

简介Introduction

实时 MRI 能够在没有生理门控的情况下评估动态变化，但需要大量数据欠采样以确保足够的时空分辨率。压缩感知（CS）广泛用于从欠采样实时数据重建无伪影图像[1]，但受到耗时的迭代重建的限制。最近，与 CS 相比，深度学习 (DL) 重建方法已表现出更快的推理速度和卓越的图像质量 [2]。Real-time MRI enables assessment of dynamic changes without physiological gating but requires significant data undersampling to ensure su"icient spatiotemporal resolution. Compressed Sensing (CS) is widely used to reconstruct artefact-free images from undersampled real-time data[1], but is limited by time-consuming iterative reconstructions. More recently, Deep Learning (DL) reconstruction methods have demonstrated faster inference and superior image quality compared to CS [2].

快速重建在心脏应用中尤其重要，操作员通常需要在计划后续采集之前查看最新的扫描。用于快速重建实时 MRI 数据的深度学习方法之一是深度伪影抑制，这是一种“后处理”方法，与非笛卡尔轨迹结合时显示出强大的性能。然而，这种方法受到对大型特定应用 MRI 训练数据集的需求的限制，而这些数据集很难获得。即使本地有大型数据集，数据保护法规也常常限制数据共享。我们之前已经表明，开源视频可以用于训练基于 DL 的 MRI 重建 [3]，但有几个问题限制了它们的使用，包括：（i）对高度外分布内容（例如动物、车辆或动物）训练的 DL 重建的临床怀疑。风景）；以及 (ii) 对视频数据集的许可限制，这可能会使商业部署复杂化。Fast reconstruction is particularly important in cardiac applications, where operators often need to review the latest scan prior to planning subsequent acquisitions. One DL approach for rapid reconstruction of real-time MRI data is deep artefact suppression, a ‘post-processing’ method that has shown strong performance when combined with non-Cartesian trajectories. However, this approach is limited by the need for large application-specific MRI training datasets, which can be di"icult to obtain. Even where large datasets are available locally, data protection regulations often limit data-sharing. We have previously shown that open-source videos can be used to train DL-based MRI reconstructions [3], but several issues restrict their use, including: (i) Clinical scepticism towards DL reconstructions trained on highly out-of-distribution content (e.g. animals, vehicles, or landscapes); and (ii) Licensing restrictions on video datasets, which may complicate commercial deployment.

这激发了对结构丰富但不受隐私或许可限制的替代性合成训练数据的探索。因此，我们提出了替代的训练数据源；随时间变化的 Julia 集合分形。这些数据结构复杂但抽象，使它们看起来不像自然视频那么不协调，同时保留了高空间和时间复杂性并保持完全开源。This motivates the exploration of alternative, synthetic training data that are structurally rich yet unconstrained by privacy or licensing limitations. Consequently, we propose an alternate source of training data; time-varying Julia set fractals. These data are structurally complex but abstract, making them seem less incongruous than natural videos, while retaining high spatial and temporal complexity and remaining fully open-source.

这项概念验证研究的目的是： (i) 证明生成合成分形数据集的可行性； (ii) 使用这些数据训练用于动态 MRI 重建的深度伪影抑制模型； (iii) 评估前瞻性获取的径向实时心脏 MRI 数据的方法； (iv) 与其他重建方法进行性能比较，包括在心脏 MRI 数据、CS 和深度图像先验 (DIP) 上训练的深度伪影抑制。The aims of this proof-of-concept study are: (i) To demonstrate the feasibility of generating a synthetic fractal dataset; (ii) To use this data to train a deep artefact suppression model for dynamic MRI reconstruction; (iii) To evaluate the approach on prospectively acquired radial realtime cardiac MRI data; and (iv) To compare performance against other reconstruction methods, including deep artefact suppression trained on cardiac MRI data, CS and Deep Image Prior (DIP).

- 转换为复值动态图像- Conversion to Complex Valued Dynamic Images

从每个 4D 分形中，我们提取 z 轴的中心切片来表示 2D+时间数据，并在 0 和 1 之间归一化。为了模拟复值图像数据，生成了两个具有不同频率（一个固定为 0.25Hz，另一个在 0.25Hz 和 1Hz 之间随机采样）和随机相移（0 到 2𝜋 弧度之间）的独立正弦波。然后将归一化像素强度映射到这两个正弦曲线的 x 轴，相应的 y 值定义每个像素的实部和虚部。然后对每个通道进行高斯模糊（σ 在 0.2 和 0.4 之间）以抑制更精细的分形细节，并随后应用高斯平滑（σ = 0.1 和锐化强度 α = 50）的不锐化掩模以避免边缘过于平滑。From each 4D fractal, we extracted the central slice in the z-axis to represent our 2D+time data and normalised between 0 and 1. To simulate complex-valued image data, two independent sine waves with di"erent frequencies (one fixed to 0.25Hz and the other randomly sampled between 0.25Hz and 1Hz) and random phase shifts (between 0 and 2𝜋 radians) were generated. Normalised pixel intensities were then mapped to the x-axis of these two sinusoids, with the corresponding y-values defining the real and imaginary components of each pixel. Each channel was then Gaussian blurred (σ between 0.2 and 0.4) to suppress finer fractal detail and subsequently applied unsharp masking with Gaussian smoothing (σ = 0.1 and sharpening strength α = 50) to avoid overly smooth edges.

多线圈MR数据模拟Simulation of multi-coil MR data

我们按照之前应用于自然视频数据[3]的方法来获取多线圈数据，包括以下步骤：We follow the method previously applied to natural video data[3] to obtain multi-coil data consisting of the following steps:

  • 1）将随机缩放和旋转的椭圆掩模叠加在图像上以模拟身体的形状。1) A randomly scaled and rotated elliptical mask was superimposed on the image to simulate the shape of a body.

  • 2) 通过随机生成 6x6 矩阵、将其上采样到图像大小并添加到图像相位来模拟背景相位。2) Background phase was simulated by randomly generating a 6x6 matrix, upsampling it to the image size and added to the image phase.

  • 3) 使用具有随机中心位置、x 和 y 轴不同标准差、随机相位偏移和随机强度的 2D 高斯函数创建合成线圈灵敏度图。3) Synthetic coil sensitivity maps were created using 2D Gaussian functions with random centre positions, varying standard deviations in the x and y axes, random phase o"set and random intensity.

  • 4) 通过将复值图像与合成线圈图相乘并向每个线圈和时间帧添加随机背景噪声来生成多线圈数据。4) Multi-coil data was generated by multiplying the complex value images with the synthetic coil maps and adding random background noise to each coil and time frame.

  • 5）通过对图像应用快速傅里叶变换（FFT）获得完全采样的笛卡尔多线圈k空间数据。5) Fully-sampled Cartesian multi-coil k-space data was obtained by applying the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to the images.

使用上述步骤，生成了 692 个配对分形图像集的数据集，以匹配可用心脏 MRI 训练数据集的大小 [3]。Using the above steps, a dataset of 692 paired fractal image sets was generated to match the size of the available cardiac MRI training dataset[3].

心脏 MRI 训练数据Cardiac MRI Training Data

我们使用了先前描述的 [3] 数据集，其中包括在七个心脏方向获得的 692 个心电图触发的笛卡尔平衡稳态自由进动 (bSSFP) CINE 多线圈原始采集数据（请参阅支持信息 S2）。原始数据通过 2 倍欠采样获取，具有 44 条参考线，允许使用 GRAPPA 重建完全采样的多线圈笛卡尔 k 空间数据。We used a previously described[3] dataset that included 692 electrocardiogram-triggered, Cartesian balanced steady state free precession (bSSFP) CINE multi-coil raw acquisitions obtained in seven cardiac orientations (See Supporting Information S2). The raw data were acquired with 2×undersampling, with 44 reference lines, allowing fully-sampled multi-coil Cartesian k-space data to be reconstructed using GRAPPA.

训练深度伪影抑制模型Training the Deep Artefact Suppression Model

深度伪影抑制模型分别根据分形数据和心脏 MRI 数据进行训练。该架构基于先前描述的[3] 3D UNet [4]，它采用多线圈、复杂、2D+时间数据作为输入，并输出单通道、幅度、2D+时间数据（支持信息图S3中的完整架构细节）。Deep artefact suppression models were separately trained on fractal data and cardiac MRI data. The architecture was based on a previously described[3] 3D UNet[4] that takes multi-coil, complex, 2D+time data as input and outputs single-channel, magnitude, 2D+time data (Full architecture details in Supporting Information figure S3).

心脏 MRI 和分形数据都以类似的方式进行处理，以创建反映预期数据的配对训练数据。使用奇异值分解将完全采样的笛卡尔多线圈 k 空间数据压缩为 10 个线圈，并在每个时间帧重新采样到 13 个排序的黄金角度径向辐条。然后，对这种径向欠采样的多线圈 k 空间数据进行非均匀快速傅里叶变换 (NUFFT)，以生成多线圈、复杂的 2D+时间图像数据，并对其进行归一化，以便线圈组合幅度图像数据在 [0, 1] 范围内。该数据被输入 3D UNet，地面真实目标是归一化线圈组合（平方和根）、2D+时间、从完全采样的 k 空间数据导出的幅度数据。Both the cardiac MRI and fractal data were processed in a similar fashion to create paired training data that mirrored the prospective data. The fully-sampled Cartesian multi-coil k-space data was compressed to 10 coils using singular value decomposition and resampled onto 13 sorted golden angle radial spokes per time frame. This radially undersampled multi-coil k-space data was then non-uniformly fast Fourier transformed (NUFFT) to produce multi-coil, complex 2D+time image data, that was normalised so that the coil combined magnitude image data was in the range [0, 1]. This data was input into the 3D UNet with the ground truth target being the normalised coil combined (root-sum-of-squares), 2D+time, magnitude data derived from the fully-sampled k- space data.

单独的 3D UNet 模型使用结构相似性指数测量 (SSIM)[5] 损失和 Adam[6] 优化器，使用 519/69/104 的训练/验证/测试分割进行 300 个时期的训练，学习率设置为 10[#&] 。训练是在单个 NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 上进行的，训练代码和相应的预训练网络可从以下网址获取：https://github.com/mrphys/Image_Reconstruction_Fractal。The separate 3D UNet models were trained with a training/validation/test split of 519/69/104 for 300 epochs with the Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM)[5] loss and Adam[6] optimizer with learning rate set to 10[#&] . Training was performed on a single NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 and the training code and corresponding pre-trained networks are available at: https://github.com/mrphys/Image_Reconstruction_Fractal.

对前瞻性实时数据的四种重建（F-DL、CMR-DL、LR-DIP 和 CS）在重建时间和图像质量方面进行了比较。还将它们与参考标准屏气 CINE 成像进行比较，以量化心室体积和功能。The four reconstructions (F-DL, CMR-DL, LR-DIP and CS) of the prospective real-time data were compared in terms of reconstruction time and image quality. 定性图像评估：所有四种重建方法的心室中层切片均由两名 CMR 专家（MQ、RV）进行评估。所有重建均以 4×1 并排布局排列的单个 CINE 剪辑呈现，顺序随机排列，对评分者不知情。评分者根据主观图像质量对图像进行排名，从最好 (1) 到最差 (4)，并有可能得分相同。Qualitative image evaluation: The mid-ventricular slice from all four reconstruction methods was assessed by two CMR specialists (MQ, RV). All reconstructions were presented in a single CINE clip arranged in a 4×1 side-by-side layout, with the order randomly shu"led, blinded to the scorers. The scorers ranked the images based on subjective image quality, from best (1) to worst (4) with possibility of tied scores.

定量图像评分评估：使用对比度与噪声比（CNR）、边缘锐度（ES）和ES的时间标准差（STD）来比较实时数据的重建。 CNR计算为左心室(LV)血池和心肌之间平均像素强度的差异除以肺中随时间变化的像素强度的标准偏差。 ES 是通过参数模型跨左心室心肌和左心室血池之间的边界测量的[8]，时间 STD ES 是 ES 随时间变化的标准差，值越低表明清晰度越一致。对于每个患者，所有测量在跨越左心室血池和心肌之间边界的不同位置重复三次。Quantitative image scoring evaluation: Reconstructions of real-time data were compared using Contrast-to-Noise Ratio (CNR), edge sharpness (ES) and the temporal standard deviation (STD) of ES. The CNR was calculated as the di"erence in mean pixel intensity between the Left Ventricular (LV) blood-pool and myocardium, divided by the standard deviation of pixel intensities in the lung across time. The ES was measured across the boundary between LV myocardium and LV blood-pool via parametric modelling[8], and the temporal STD ES was the standard deviation of ES across time, with a lower value indicating more consistent sharpness. All measurements were repeated three times at di"erent positions across the boundary between LV blood-pool and myocardium for each patient.

讨论Discussion

使用综合生成的分形数据进行训练。此外，这些重建产生的图像质量和临床测量结果与根据实际 CMR 数据训练的 DL 模型相当。trained using synthetically generated fractal data. Furthermore, these reconstructions yielded comparable image quality and clinical measures to DL-models trained on actual CMR data.

尽管开源 CMR 原始数据存储库变得越来越可用（例如 CMRXrecon[11]），但我们相信我们的分形方法具有一些关键优势。首先，我们可以生成几乎无限量的合成 k 空间数据，这对于更多人来说可能很重要Although open-source CMR raw data repositories are becoming increasingly available (e.g. CMRXrecon[11]), we believe our fractal method has some key advantages. Firstly, we can generate almost unlimited amounts of synthetic k-space data, which may be important for more

“数据饥饿”模型（例如 Transformer）。其次，分形不限于单一解剖结构，可用于一系列不同的实时应用（例如语音或肠道成像）。最后，我们的分形本身是 4D 的，可用于训练缺乏高质量训练数据的 3D+t 数据模型（例如 3D CINE 或时间分辨血管造影）。此外，与自然视频不同，分形可能引起较少的临床怀疑论，因为它们是抽象的、通过数学生成的，并且没有不协调的现实世界内容（例如，移动的车辆）。‘data hungry’ models (e.g. transformers). Secondly, fractals are not limited to a single anatomy and could be used for a range of di"erent real-time applications (e.g. speech or bowel imaging). Finally, our fractals are natively 4D and could be used to train models for 3D+t data (e.g. 3D CINE or time resolved angiography) where there is a paucity of high-quality training data. Furthermore, unlike natural videos, fractals may elicit less clinical scepticism because they are abstract and mathematically generated and free from incongruous real-world content (e.g., moving vehicles).

在我们的研究中，使用 F-DL 和 CMR-DL 重建的前瞻性实时 MRI 数据表现出相当的图像质量。这与我们在自然视频上的工作一致[3]，并且很可能是由于深度伪影抑制主要是“学习”在心脏 MRI 和分形数据中相似的局部图像特征。我们还表明，从实时数据的 F-DL 和 CMR-DL 重建得出的 LV 和 RV 体积和功能与参考标准屏气 CINE 成像具有相当的一致性。这一点至关重要，因为临床相关性的证明是未来更广泛使用的先决条件。In our study, prospective real-time MRI data reconstructed using F-DL and CMR-DL demonstrated comparable image quality. This is consistent with our work on natural videos[3], and is most likely due to deep artefact suppression primarily ‘learning’ local image features that are similar in both cardiac MRI and fractal data. We also showed that LV and RV volumes and function derived from F-DL and CMR-DL reconstructions of real-time data had comparable agreement with reference-standard breath-hold CINE imaging. This is vital as demonstration of clinical relevance is a pre-requisite of future wider use.

我们还将监督式深度学习方法与包括 LR-DIP 和传统 CS 在内的竞争方法进行了比较。 LR-DIP 引起了人们极大的兴趣，因为它无需训练数据就能获得 DL 的一些好处。然而，在我们的研究中，两种监督方法（F-DL 和 CMR-DL）均略优于 LR-DIP 和 CS。更重要的是，CS 和 LR-DIP 的重建时间都明显长于深度伪影抑制，LR-DIP 需要数小时才能重建 SAX 堆栈。这极大地阻碍了临床使用，并强化了监督式深度学习的应用，从而强化了合成训练数据的使用。应该指出的是，较新的迭代自监督方法确实具有较短的重建时间[12]，但它们也可能受益于合成数据的预训练。We also compared supervised DL methods to competing approaches including LR-DIP and conventional CS. LR-DIP is of great interest as it promises some of the benefits of DL without the need for training data. However, in our study, both supervised methods (F-DL and CMR-DL) slightly outperformed both LR-DIP and CS. More importantly, the reconstruction times for CS and LR-DIP were both significantly longer than deep artefact suppression, with LR-DIP taking hours to reconstruct a SAX stack. This substantially hampers clinical use and strengthens the case for supervised DL, and consequently for the use of synthetic training data. It should be noted that newer iterative self-supervised methods do have lower reconstruction times[12], but they could also potentially benefit from pretraining on synthetic data.

测试了采样模式和一种深度学习方法，还需要进一步的工作来证明分形生成的训练数据可以跨不同的轨迹和重建架构进行推广。然而，我们之前对自然视频的研究确实表明合成数据具有高度通用性。其次，其他研究已经证明了从仅幅度图像生成合成相位的不同方法[13]、[14]。改进框架的这个组成部分可能有利于一些应用和需要调查。最后，患者队列较小，需要更大、更多样化的人群来进行全面的临床验证。sampling pattern and one DL method were tested, and further work is required to demonstrate that fractal-generated training data generalises across di"erent trajectories and reconstruction architectures. However, our previous work on natural videos does suggest that synthetic data is highly generalisable. Secondly, other studies have demonstrated di"erent methods for generating synthetic phase from magnitude-only images[13], [14]. Improving this component of the framework may benefit some applications and needs investigation. Finally, the patient cohort was small, and larger more diverse population are required for comprehensive clinical validation.

结论Conclusion

我们开发并演示了使用自行生成的分形数据来训练用于动态 MRI 重建的深度学习模型。我们相信这种方法在未来训练高度通用的动态 MRI 重建模型方面具有强大的潜力。We developed and demonstrated using self-generated fractal data to train DL models for dynamic MRI reconstruction. We belive this method has strong potential for training highly generalisable dynamic MRI reconstruction models in the future.
