立场：人工智能安全需要有效可控Position: AI Safety Requires Effective Controllability

摘要Abstract

人工智能安全在很大程度上仍然是一致的：遵循人类偏好、安全政策和规范约束的训练模型。该框架改进了现代语言模型的行为，但对齐的行为本身并不能保证部署的代理在开放式、交互式和工具使用环境中运行后可以被停止、覆盖或约束。系统可能在预期中是安全的，但在指令冲突、长期执行、对抗性输入或危险工具使用的情况下仍然无法屈服于显式运行时权限。该立场文件认为，因此人工智能安全需要将可控性作为首要目标。我们定义AI safety is still largely framed as alignment: training models to follow human preferences, safety policies, and normative constraints. That framing has improved the behavior of modern language models, but aligned behavior does not by itself guarantee that a deployed agent can be stopped, overridden, or constrained once it operates in open-ended, interactive, and tool-using environments. A system may be safe in expectation and still fail to yield to explicit runtime authority under conflicting instructions, long-horizon execution, adversarial inputs, or risky tool use. This position paper argues that AI safety therefore requires controllability as a first-class objective. We define可控性controllability人工智能系统在运行时通过显式控制信号保持可靠的可中断、可覆盖、可重定向和可约束的能力，同时在不存在此类信号时保留普通效用。为了研究这个差距，我们引入as the ability of an AI system to remain reliably interruptible, overridable, redirectable, and constrainable by explicit control signals at runtime while preserving ordinary utility when such signals are absent. To study this gap, we introduce控制台ControlBench，评估高风险代理场景中可控性故障的基准。基于 OpenClaw 的代理的实验表明，当前的对齐和护栏机制可以降低风险，但通常无法提供持久、权威且可执行的运行时控制。因此，我们提出了一个以控制为中心的架构框架，强调显式控制平面、运行时干预路径、持久控制状态和可审计决策接口作为未来可控人工智能系统的关键设计原则。, a benchmark for evaluating controllability failures in high-risk agentic scenarios. Experiments with OpenClaw-based agents show that current alignment and guardrail mechanisms reduce risk, but often fail to provide persistent, authoritative, and enforceable runtime control. We therefore propose a control-centric architectural framework that highlights explicit control planes, runtime intervention pathways, persistent control states, and auditable decision interfaces as key design principles for future controllable AI systems.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能安全很大程度上是围绕以下范式组织的AI safety has largely been organized around the paradigm of对准alignment：塑造模型行为以遵循人类偏好、安全政策和规范约束。这一范式推动了实质性进展。监督微调、根据人类反馈进行强化学习、偏好优化、体质训练、红队和运行时保障都提高了现代人工智能系统的可靠性: shaping model behavior to follow human preferences, safety policies, and normative constraints. This paradigm has driven substantial progress. Supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning from human feedback, preference optimization, constitutional training, red-teaming, and runtime safeguards have all improved the reliability of modern AI systems[31, 2, 33, 32, 10, 34, 14]。然而，随着大型语言模型越来越多地部署为在长上下文中进行规划、调用工具、与外部环境交互以及执行多步骤目标的代理，出现了一个更基本的问题：即使人工智能系统与预期大致一致，当出现安全关键条件时，它仍然可以得到可靠的控制吗？. However, as large language models are increasingly deployed as agents that plan over long contexts, invoke tools, interact with external environments, and execute multi-step objectives, a more fundamental question arises: even if an AI system is broadly aligned in expectation, can it still be reliably controlled when safety-critical conditions emerge?

现有的安全机制并不能完全回答这个问题。对齐方法塑造训练期间的行为Existing safety mechanisms do not fully answer the question. Alignment methods shape behavior during training[31, 2, 33]，但它们本身并不提供权威的运行时机制，用于在出现冲突目标或高风险行动轨迹时停止、重定向或约束已部署的系统, but they do not by themselves provide an authoritative runtime mechanism for stopping, redirecting, or constraining a deployed system once conflicting objectives or high-risk action trajectories arise[39, 11]。稳健的工作和越狱防御. Robustness work and jailbreak defenses[44, 22, 1]减少不安全响应的可能性，但它们通常在孤立的输入输出行为级别而不是系统级别控制上进行评估。运行时保障措施，包括护栏、监视器、策略过滤器和人工监督reduce the likelihood of unsafe responses, but they are often evaluated at the level of isolated input-output behavior rather than system-level control. Runtime safeguards, including guardrails, monitors, policy filters, and human oversight[34, 14, 9, 38, 8]，可以阻止一些不安全的内容或操作，但它们通常保持反应性，与系统的演变状态松散耦合，在多轮交互中脆弱，或者一旦不安全的轨迹开始就不够持久。这些限制揭示了制造系统之间的差距, can block some unsafe content or actions, but they often remain reactive, loosely coupled to the system’s evolving state, brittle across multi-turn interaction, or insufficiently persistent once an unsafe trajectory has begun. These limitations reveal a gap between making systems预期中更安全safer in expectation并制作它们and making them可通过设计控制controllable by design.

立场：人工智能安全需要的不仅仅是对齐；未来的人工智能系统必须进行可控性设计和评估。Position: AI safety requires more than alignment; future AI systems must be designed and evaluated for controllability.

Under this view, alignment remains necessary but not sufficient. The closest prior ideas are interruptibility and AI control, which ask whether agents can remain corrigible, shutdownable, or governable even under adverse incentives[30, 11]。我们的重点是现代语言模型代理出现的操作问题：是否可以在模型推理、多轮交互和外部工具执行中持续执行显式控制条件。. 2 AI安全中的控制机制Control Mechanisms in AI Safety

本节将现有的人工智能安全方法置于控制设计空间内。虽然当前的方法改善了观察到的行为，但它们通常仅提供部分控制：内部方法塑造模型行为，外部方法限制输出或操作。两者都无法单独确保持久的运行时可治理性。图This section situates existing AI safety methods within a control-design space. While current methods improve observed behavior, they typically provide only partial control: internal methods shape model behavior, and external methods constrain outputs or actions. 2.1 内部控制机制Internal Control Mechanisms

内部控制机制通过改变模型本身来调节行为：其目标、参数、激活或解码过程。它们是现代对齐管道的核心，因为它们的目的是从模型内部诱导安全行为，而不是在部署时完全依赖外部监督。Internal control mechanisms regulate behavior by changing the model itself: its objectives, parameters, activations, or decoding process. They are central to modern alignment pipelines because they aim to induce safe behavior from within the model rather than relying exclusively on external supervision at deployment time.

通过训练进行调整。Alignment via training.占主导地位的内部控制范式是通过培训来协调一致。 RLHF 仍然是典型的例子，后来的工作通过人工智能生成的反馈、体质训练、直接偏好优化和安全意识目标设计扩展了这一范式The dominant internal-control paradigm is alignment through training. RLHF remains the canonical example, and later work has extended this paradigm through AI-generated feedback, constitutional training, direct preference optimization, and safety-aware objective design[31, 2, 33, 17, 6]。其他工作研究奖励过度优化和学习偏好模型的脆弱性. Other work studies reward overoptimization and the fragility of learned preference models[5, 26]。这些方法改善了平均情况下的行为，但我们的目的的关键限制很简单：学习对安全行为的偏好并不等同于保证遵守显式的运行时权限。这种差距在指令层次结构的最新结果中显而易见，其中模型通常无法可靠地将较高权限的系统指令优先于冲突或敌对的用户指令. These methods improve average-case behavior, but the key limitation for our purposes is straightforward: learning a preference for safe behavior is not the same as guaranteeing obedience to explicit runtime authority. This gap is visible in recent results on instruction hierarchy, where models often fail to reliably prioritize higher-authority system instructions over conflicting or adversarial user instructions[38, 49].

可控生成和解码。Controllable generation and decoding.第二行工作是通过引导生成过程本身来在推理期间应用控制。可控文本生成长期以来一直在探索对主题、风格和情感等所需属性的指导A second line of work applies control during inference by steering the generation process itself. Controllable text generation has long explored guidance toward desired attributes such as topic, style, and sentiment[16, 7, 45, 20, 21]。在安全设置中，解码时方法会修剪不安全的延续，组合多个控制目标，或使生成适应上下文相关的风险. In safety settings, decoding-time methods prune unsafe continuations, combine multiple control objectives, or adapt generation to context-sensitive risk[27, 44, 35, 22, 3, 19]。这些方法比单独训练提供更直接的推理时间影响。尽管如此，控制信号通常是下一代代币的本地信号，并且在长期交互​​、组合任务或工具介导的执行下可能会变得脆弱。. These methods offer more direct inference-time influence than training alone. Still, the control signal is usually local to next-token generation and may become brittle under long-horizon interaction, compositional tasks, or tool-mediated execution.

激活和表征工程。Activation and representation engineering.第三行工作更直接地操纵内部激活和表征。这项研究与机械可解释性密切相关，并试图干预与真实性、无害性或其他目标属性相关的潜在方向A third line of work manipulates internal activations and representations more directly. This research is closely connected to mechanistic interpretability and attempts to intervene on latent directions associated with truthfulness, harmlessness, or other target properties[4, 18, 37, 51]。最近关于稀疏自动编码器和特征空间干预的工作提出了一条更具针对性和可解释性的指导之路. Recent work on sparse autoencoders and feature-space interventions suggests a path toward more targeted and interpretable steering[13, 50]。机器遗忘的相关工作将选择性遗忘视为一种安全控制机制，用于消除有害或不需要的知识，同时保留通用性. Related work on machine unlearning treats selective forgetting as a safety control mechanism for removing harmful or undesirable knowledge while preserving general utility[47, 23]。这些方法很有吸引力，因为它们承诺进行细粒度且机械化的干预。但它们仍然主要决定模型可能做什么，而不是当外部机构需要干预时部署的系统必须做什么。. These approaches are appealing because they promise fine-grained and mechanistically grounded intervention. But they still primarily shape what the model is likely to do, not what the deployed system must do when an external authority needs to intervene.

2.2 外部控制机制External Control Mechanisms

外部控制机制将模型置于更大的受控系统内。他们不是假设模型本身保持安全，而是通过额外的监管组件来限制输入、输出、行动和环境交互。这种转变对于代理来说尤其重要，因为许多间接故障发生在模型与外部世界之间的接口处。External control mechanisms place the model inside a larger governed system. Rather than assuming the model will remain safe on its own, they constrain inputs, outputs, actions, and environmental interaction through additional supervisory components. This shift is especially important for agents, where many consequential failures occur at the interface between the model and the external world.

安全护栏和内容审核。Safety guardrails and content moderation.标准的外部控制策略是监视和过滤模型输入或输出。这些机制的范围从基于规则的过滤器到学习分类器和基于模型的调节器。 Llama Guard 等系统根据安全分类法对内容进行分类，而最近的部署工作强调有效的护栏需要系统的社会技术设计，而不是临时的阻止规则A standard external-control strategy is to monitor and filter model inputs or outputs. These mechanisms range from rule-based filters to learned classifiers and model-based moderators. Systems such as Llama Guard classify content against safety taxonomies, while recent deployment work emphasizes that effective guardrails require systematic socio-technical design rather than ad hoc blocking rules[14, 9, 40]。它们的价值是实用且直接的。与此同时，它们的局限性现在已经通过红队、越狱研究和新的评估数据集得到了详细记录. Their value is practical and immediate. At the same time, their limits are now well documented through red teaming, jailbreak studies, and new evaluation datasets[32, 10, 1, 15]。护栏可以减少表面伤害，但一旦不安全行为在多个步骤中展开，护栏仍然可能被绕过、转移到分布区域之外或变得不够充分。. Guardrails can reduce surface-level harms, but they may still be bypassed, shifted out of distribution, or rendered insufficient once unsafe behavior unfolds across multiple steps.

代理级安全和治理。Agent-level safety and governance.随着语言模型成为代理，外部控制必须从内容审核延伸到行为治理。这激发了代理安全基准、更安全的工具使用数据集以及监控架构，其中警卫代理监视或验证主要代理的行为As language models become agents, external control must extend from content moderation to action governance. This has motivated agent-safety benchmarks, safer tool-use datasets, and supervisory architectures in which a guard agent monitors or verifies the behavior of a primary agent[48, 43, 41, 24]。最近的运行时治理方法通过在执行期间指定、强制执行或响应代理级安全约束，进一步超越了被动监控. Recent runtime governance methods further move beyond passive monitoring by specifying, enforcing, or responding to agent-level safety constraints during execution[39, 42]。在此设置中，及时注入尤其重要，因为嵌入外部数据中的恶意指令可以劫持下游执行，从而激发 AgentDojo 等专用环境. Prompt injection is especially important in this setting because malicious instructions embedded in external data can hijack downstream execution, motivating dedicated environments such as AgentDojo[8]。更一般地说，有关可修正性、可中断性、人工智能控制和认证授权的工作提出了即使行动空间变得广泛且具有战略意义，先进的系统如何仍然受到关闭、纠正和授权的影响. More generally, work on corrigibility, interruptibility, AI control, and authenticated delegation asks how advanced systems can remain subject to shutdown, correction, and authority even when the action space becomes broad and strategically consequential[30, 12, 11, 36]。与内部方法相比，这些方法更直接地处理运行时权限，但它们通常仍然是反应性的，与内部推理松散耦合，并且在长期工具使用下很脆弱。. Compared with internal methods, these approaches address runtime authority more directly, but they often remain reactive, loosely coupled to internal reasoning, and brittle under long-horizon tool use.

3 经验证据：协调并不能保证可控性Empirical Evidence: Alignment Does Not Guarantee Controllability

本文的核心主张是，对齐对于人工智能安全来说是必要的，但还不够。为了使这一主张具体化，本节提出了一个重点实证问题：The central claim of this paper is that alignment is necessary but insufficient for AI safety. We construct a subset of 900 instances spanning six risk categories, with 150 instances sampled from each category. 4 迈向可控人工智能系统Towards Controllable AI Systems

上述经验证据表明，现有的对准和基于护栏的方法提高了安全性，但不能可靠地保证可控性。这促使从以行为为中心的安全转向以控制为中心的安全。因此我们引入一个概念The empirical evidence above suggests that existing alignment and guardrail-based methods improve safety but do not reliably guarantee controllability. This motivates a shift from behavior-centric safety to control-centric safety. We therefore introduce the notion of a可控人工智能系统Controllable AI System (中科院CAS)：一种系统，其行为在执行期间可以被更高优先级的控制信号可靠地约束、中断、覆盖或重定向，同时在没有此类控制处于活动状态时保留正常功能。): a system whose behavior can be reliably constrained, interrupted, overridden, or redirected by higher-priority control signals during execution, while preserving normal functionality when no such control is active.中科院CAS无意取代路线或护栏。这是一个将它们集成到持久运行时控制结构中的系统目标。is not intended to replace alignment or guardrails. It is a systems objective that integrates them into a persistent runtime control structure.

4.2 核心属性Core properties

在此框架下，可控的人工智能系统应满足五个核心属性：Under this framework, a controllable AI system should satisfy five core properties:

  1. 1.

    权威。Authority.系统应遵循明确定义的控制信号层次结构。当较低优先级的用户目标或代理计划与较高优先级的安全约束发生冲突时，后者应该可靠地占主导地位。The system should respect a well-defined hierarchy of control signals. When lower-priority user objectives or agent plans conflict with higher-priority safety constraints, the latter should reliably dominate.

  2. 2.

    可中断性。Interruptibility.当触发明确的停止或超越条件时，系统应该能够及时中断。这需要的不仅仅是产生拒绝。它需要停止不安全的轨迹，并通过替代推理路径、工具调用或委托来防止继续执行。The system should be capable of timely interruption when an explicit stop or override condition is triggered. This requires more than generating a refusal. It requires halting unsafe trajectories and preventing continued execution through alternative reasoning paths, tool calls, or delegation.

  3. 3.

    运行时可执行性。Runtime enforceability.控制应该在部署期间强制执行，而不仅仅是通过培训来鼓励。这包括阻止禁止的工具调用、强制执行权限边界、重定向不安全的计划以及在必要时终止执行。Control should be enforceable during deployment, not merely encouraged through training. This includes blocking prohibited tool calls, enforcing permission boundaries, redirecting unsafe plans, and terminating execution when necessary.

  4. 4.

    坚持。Persistence.控制应该在长视野交互、多轮上下文、即时注入、工具使用和下游适应中保持有效。可能被遗忘、覆盖或旁路的瞬态控制信号不能提供可靠的可控性。Control should remain effective across long-horizon interaction, multi-turn context, prompt injection, tool use, and downstream adaptation. A transient control signal that can be forgotten, overwritten, or bypassed does not provide reliable controllability.

  5. 5.

    可审计性。Auditability.与控制相关的事件应以支持检查、归因和事后分析的形式记录。如果没有可审计性，即使是成功的干预措施也很难验证、改进或治理。Control-relevant events should be logged in a form that supports inspection, attribution, and post hoc analysis. Without auditability, even successful interventions are difficult to verify, improve, or govern.

这些属性比单独的对齐定义了更强的安全概念。一个系统在许多基准设置中可能是有用的、无害的和强大的，但一旦在部署过程中出现不安全的轨迹，它仍然无法保持在有意义的权威之下。These properties define a stronger notion of safety than alignment alone. A system may be helpful, harmless, and robust in many benchmark settings, yet still fail to remain under meaningful authority once unsafe trajectories emerge during deployment.中科院CAS旨在推动未来的设计，其中系统级控制是明确的、持久的、可解释的和可执行的。aims to motivate future designs in which system-level control is explicit, persistent, interpretable, and enforceable.

One might argue that stronger alignment will eventually solve controllability, that guardrails already provide the relevant control layer, or that explicit control will reduce system utility. We view these not as reasons to reject controllability, but as the right questions for a control-centric research agenda.

可控性与对齐是互补的。Controllability is complementary to alignment.一个常见的反对意见是，可控性失败仅仅反映了不完全的一致性。从这个角度来看，更好的协调模型最终将内化安全规则、指令层次结构和对监督的尊重。更强的一致性无疑是有价值的。然而，对准和可控性针对不同的安全特性。一致性决定了系统的默认行为分布，而可控性则涉及到当其默认行为不充分、有争议或不安全时，已部署的系统是否仍受明确的授权约束。这种区别在代理环境中变得尤其重要。模型可能在普通交互中符合偏好，但在停止信号、政策冲突或有风险的工具使用轨迹下仍然无法屈服。在这种情况下，相关的安全问题不仅在于模型通常是否选择安全响应，还在于系统是否可以在必要时停止、约束或重定向。从这个角度来看，可控性不应被视为对齐的较弱替代品，而应被视为一种补充控制属性，当对齐不完善、不确定或处于部署时间压力下时，它就变得至关重要。A common objection is that controllability failures merely reflect incomplete alignment. Better-aligned models, on this view, will eventually internalize safety rules, instruction hierarchy, and deference to oversight. Stronger alignment is unquestionably valuable. However, alignment and controllability target different safety properties. Alignment shapes the system’s default behavioral distribution, whereas controllability concerns whether a deployed system remains subject to explicit authority when its default behavior is insufficient, contested, or unsafe. This distinction becomes especially important in agentic settings. A model may be preference-aligned in ordinary interactions, yet still fail to yield under stop signals, policy conflicts, or risky tool-use trajectories. In such cases, the relevant safety question is not only whether the model usually chooses safe responses, but whether the system can be halted, constrained, or redirected when necessary. From this perspective, controllability should not be treated as a weaker substitute for alignment, but as a complementary control property that becomes critical when alignment is imperfect, uncertain, or placed under deployment-time stress.

护栏是必要的，但还不够。Guardrails are necessary but not sufficient.第二个反对意见是护栏、监视器、过滤器和工具包装器已经提供了所需的控制层。这些机制很有用，通常可以减少直接违规行为。它们的局限性在于它们通常是局部的和被动的：它们检查提示、响应或候选操作，但不一定保持对更广泛的执行轨迹的权威。在长视野环境中，智能体可能会遵守一项被阻止的行动，同时继续寻找替代路线，以保持实现同一不安全目标的进展。A second objection is that guardrails, monitors, filters, and tool wrappers already provide the needed control layer. These mechanisms are useful and often reduce direct violations. Their limitation is that they are usually local and reactive: they inspect a prompt, a response, or a candidate action, but do not necessarily maintain authority over the broader trajectory of execution. In long-horizon settings, an agent may comply with one blocked action while continuing to search for alternative routes that preserve progress toward the same unsafe objective.

的The中科院CAS因此，view 将护栏视为更大的运行时控制架构的组件，而不是完整的解决方案。可控系统应该维持控制状态，随着时间的推移强制执行权限边界，并决定何时应阻止、重定向、暂停或终止执行。这需要超越孤立的安全检查，转向持久且可执行的控制机制，这些机制在多步骤推理、工具使用和环境反馈中保持活跃。view therefore treats guardrails as components of a larger runtime control architecture rather than as a complete solution. A controllable system should maintain control state, enforce permission boundaries over time, and decide when execution should be blocked, redirected, paused, or terminated. This requires moving beyond isolated safety checks toward persistent and enforceable control mechanisms that remain active across multi-step reasoning, tool use, and environmental feedback.

可控性会带来成本，但可能不是可选的。Controllability introduces costs, but may not be optional.最后一个问题是，显式控制可能会因过度阻止、过度拒绝、不必要的人为干预或对代理自主权的限制而降低效用。这是一个真正的设计风险。设计不当的控制层可能会使原本有用的系统变得脆弱、保守或难以部署。此外，更强的可控性可能会带来额外的工程复杂性、新的故障模式以及关于谁有权中断或覆盖人工智能系统的治理挑战。然而，这种权衡的存在并不能消除对可控性的需求。在低风险环境中，最大化灵活性和最小化干预可能是合理的。然而，在高风险环境中，失去控制的成本可能会超过偶尔中断的成本。随着代理能够访问外部工具、私人信息、财务运营、代码执行、通信渠道以及物理或网络物理环境，故障不再仅限于不良的文本输出。它们可能通过行动传播，长期积累，并产生难以或不可能逆转的后果。在这种情况下，预期非常有用但无法可靠地停止、约束或覆盖的系统可能无法部署。A final concern is that explicit control may reduce utility through overblocking, excessive refusals, unnecessary human intervention, or constraints on agent autonomy. This is a real design risk. A poorly designed control layer can make an otherwise useful system brittle, conservative, or difficult to deploy. Moreover, stronger controllability may introduce additional engineering complexity, new failure modes, and governance challenges regarding who has authority to interrupt or override an AI system. However, the existence of such trade-offs does not eliminate the need for controllability. In low-risk settings, maximizing flexibility and minimizing intervention may be reasonable. In high-risk settings, however, the cost of losing control may dominate the cost of occasional interruption. As agents gain access to external tools, private information, financial operations, code execution, communication channels, and physical or cyber-physical environments, failures are no longer limited to undesirable text outputs. They may propagate through actions, accumulate over long horizons, and produce consequences that are difficult or impossible to reverse. Under such conditions, a system that is highly useful in expectation but cannot be reliably stopped, constrained, or overridden may be unacceptable for deployment.

走向以控制为中心的研究议程。Toward a control-centric research agenda.这种观点导致了具体的研究议程。未来的评估不仅应该报告有害输出率、拒绝率或越狱稳健性，还应该报告以控制为导向的指标：系统是否可以在明确的权威信号下停止、覆盖、重定向、约束和审计。未来的架构应该开发持久控制状态、操作级执行门、运行时监视器、状态锁定机制和审计机制，使控制决策在部署后可检查。未来的治理不仅需要证据证明高风险人工智能系统在预期中更安全，而且当它们的目标、工具或执行轨迹开始偏离人类意图时，它们仍然是可治理的。This perspective leads to a concrete research agenda. Future evaluations should report not only harmful-output rates, refusal rates, or jailbreak robustness, but also control-oriented metrics: whether systems can be stopped, overridden, redirected, constrained, and audited under explicit authority signals. Future architectures should develop persistent control state, action-level enforcement gates, runtime monitors, state-latching mechanisms, and audit mechanisms that make control decisions inspectable after deployment. Future governance should require evidence not only that high-risk AI systems are safer in expectation, but also that they remain governable when their objectives, tools, or execution trajectories begin to diverge from human intent.

更广泛地说，可控性应被视为一流的安全目标。一致性询问人工智能系统是否倾向于按照预期值行事。可控性询问当这种趋势不足时，人类和授权机构是否保留有意义的权威。对于能力日益增强的人工智能代理来说，这两个属性都是必要的：一致性减少了不安全行为的可能性，而可控性则决定了当不安全轨迹出现时社会是否保留可靠的最后一道防线。More broadly, controllability should be treated as a first-class safety objective. Alignment asks whether an AI system tends to act according to intended values. Controllability asks whether humans and authorized institutions retain meaningful authority when this tendency is insufficient. For increasingly capable AI agents, both properties are necessary: alignment reduces the likelihood of unsafe behavior, while controllability determines whether society retains a reliable last line of defense when unsafe trajectories nevertheless emerge.

6 结论Conclusion

一致性已成为人工智能安全的主导范式，但日益自主的人工智能系统提出了更严格的要求：它们必须在部署过程中保持可控。本文认为，仅靠对齐不足以满足该要求。通过Alignment has become the dominant paradigm for AI safety, but increasingly autonomous AI systems create a stronger requirement: they must remain controllable during deployment. This paper argued that alignment alone is insufficient for that requirement. Through控制台ControlBench，我们表明，当前的对齐和护栏增强型智能体在明确的控制条件下仍然可能失败，包括停止信号、政策冲突和有风险的工具使用轨迹。因此，核心差距不是对齐和不对齐之间的，而是期望中的更安全的行为和可执行的运行时控制之间的。为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了以下概念：, we showed that current aligned and guardrail-enhanced agents can still fail under explicit control conditions, including stop signals, policy conflicts, and risky tool-use trajectories. The core gap is therefore not between alignment and non-alignment, but between safer behavior in expectation and enforceable runtime control. To address that gap, we introduced the concept of中科院CAS：保留普通实用性，同时保持明确的权威、中断、执行、持久性和可审计性的系统。该框架不会取代路线或护栏。它将它们重新构建为更广泛的以控制为中心的安全架构的组成部分。如果先进的人工智能系统要在高风险部署中安全，它们不应该仅仅在大多数时候表现得一致；当控制最重要时，他们应该保持可控性。: systems that preserve ordinary utility while remaining subject to explicit authority, interruption, enforcement, persistence, and auditability. This framework does not replace alignment or guardrails. It reframes them as components of a broader control-centric safety architecture. If advanced AI systems are to be safe in high-stakes deployment, they should not merely appear aligned most of the time; they should remain governable when control matters most.

This design is motivated by two considerations. First, purely manual construction can produce high-quality tasks but may lack diversity and coverage across adversarial styles. Second, fully automatic generation can produce diverse tasks but may introduce duplicates, unrealistic scenarios, ambiguous control conditions, or examples that depend only on explicit harmful text generation. We therefore combine automatic generation with human quality control.

The generation strategies include direct harmful requests, role-play framing, benign-context disguise, multi-step decomposition, permission-boundary probing, indirect tool-use instructions, and control-conflict prompts. Each generation prompt is conditioned on one of the six risk categories to ensure balanced coverage.

此阶段的目标不是将所有生成的任务直接包含在基准测试中。相反，它是创建一个包含不同对抗结构的广泛候选池，从中可以选择高质量的代理实例。The goal of this stage is not to include all generated tasks directly in the benchmark.
