人类创造力基准The Human Creativity Benchmark

Aspen Hopkins Contra; Massachusetts Institute of TechnologyNew York, NY; Cambridge, MAUSA , Allison Nulty ContraNew YorkNew YorkUSA , Alexandria Minetti ContraNew YorkNew YorkUSA , Anoop Pakki ContraNew YorkNew YorkUSA and Angad Singh ContraNew YorkNew YorkUSA
摘要。Abstract.

现代人工智能评估框架将评估者的分歧视为需要解决的噪音。在创意领域，专业分歧反映了品味的真正差异，而不是测量错误。我们认为，评估创造性人工智能需要保留两个不同的信号：Modern AI evaluation frameworks treat evaluator disagreement as noise to be resolved. In creative domains, professional disagreement reflects genuine differences in taste, not measurement error. We argue that evaluating creative AI requires preserving two distinct signals:收敛convergence，专业人士围绕共享的最佳实践进行协调，以及, where professionals align around shared best practices, and分歧divergence，其中个人品味存在合理差异。我们提出了人类创造力基准（HCB），这是一个通过收集配对偏好、对及时遵守、可用性和视觉吸引力的标量评级以及领域专业人士的定性理由来实施这种分离的基准。通过跨越五个创意领域和三个工作流程阶段（构思、模型、细化）的 15,000 项专业判断，我们发现趋同集中于可验证的维度，如技术正确性和视觉层次结构，而分歧则集中于品味驱动的维度，如审美方向和概念风险。没有哪个模型在所有阶段都表现出色。将这些信号合并为单个质量指标会丢弃最可操作的信息：模型在哪些地方必须正确，而在哪些地方应该保持可操纵性。, where individual taste legitimately varies. We present the Human Creativity Benchmark (HCB), a benchmark that operationalizes this separation by collecting pairwise preferences, scalar ratings on prompt adherence, usability, and visual appeal, and qualitative rationale from domain professionals. Across 15,000 professional judgments spanning five creative domains and three workflow phases (ideation, mockup, refinement), we find that convergence concentrates on verifiable dimensions like technical correctness and visual hierarchy, while divergence concentrates on taste-driven dimensions like aesthetic direction and conceptual risk. No model excels uniformly across all phases. Collapsing these signals into a single quality metric discards the most actionable information: where models must be correct versus where they should remain steerable.

2. 相关工作Related Work

我们对理解创造性工作和人工智能的交叉点的兴趣始于这样一个前提：创造力是异质的、依赖于任务的，并且在不同工作阶段的表达不均匀。创意制作者可能认为审美价值和商业价值是和谐的或矛盾的Our interest in understanding the intersection of creative work and AI begins with the premise that creativity is heterogeneous, task-dependent, and unevenly expressed across stages of work. Creative producers may view aesthetic value and commercial value as harmonious or contradictory(Lee, 2022)。他们在生产过程中结合了多种认知模式，他们对身份的认知，如流派、地位、智力和职业，塑造了他们的产出。从广义上讲，创意人员会在好的（最佳实践）与更个人化、更基本的东西（品味）之间取得平衡。. They combine multiple modes of cognition in their process of production, and their perceptions of identity, such as genre, status, intellect, and profession shape their outputs. Broadly, creatives balance what is good (best practices) with something more personal, fundamental: taste.

然而，随着一系列支持创意工作的人工智能驱动工具的出现，寻求改进工作流程、扩大初始生成探索或节省时间和累积成本的创意人员在不考虑这些不同轴的系统中面临着摩擦。 也许最引起共鸣的挑战是，人工智能系统是不完美的采样器，往往会收敛到平均值。这导致同质化As an array of AI-driven tools supporting creative work has emerged, however, creatives seeking to improve their workflows, expand the initial generative exploration, or save time and accumulative costs face frictions in systems that do not account for these otherwise distinct axes. Perhaps most resonantly is the challenge that AI systems are imperfect samplers that tend to converge to an average. This leads to homogenization(Bommasani et al., 2022)和模式崩溃and mode collapse(Thanh-Tung and Tran, 2020)，模型越来越多地产生相似的输出——与创作过程相反的特征。人们花费数年时间探索、重复和改进他们的观点、风格和媒介。通过同质化来聚合他们的产出可能会导致创意实践的多元化崩溃，不仅缩小了产出的范围，而且缩小了创意领域可用的观点。, where models increasingly produce similar outputs—characteristics antithetical to the creative process. People spend years exploring, repeating, and improving their points of view, styles, and mediums. Converging their outputs through homogenization risks collapsing the plurality of creative practice, narrowing not only the range of outputs but the points of view available to creative fields.

2.1. 评估创意过程中的人工智能Evaluating AI in Creative Processes

现有的人工智能评估规范很大程度上是在受正确规则约束的环境中发挥作用。基准通常是针对预先指定的启发法、最佳实践或基本事实将“真实性”或“正确性”指标与“最佳”等同的领域而构建的。 这些评估方法没有充分考虑多样性作为结果和过程的价值Existing norms of AI evaluation function largely in settings constrained by rules of what is correct. Benchmarks are generally built for domains where pre-specified heuristics, best practices or ground-truths, equate metrics of “truthfulness” or “correctness” with “best”. These methods of evaluation do not adequately account for diversity as an outcome and process value(Lee, 2022)：在创造性工作中，保留不同风格、判断或工作流程的变化可能是使系统变得有用的首要原因之一。: in creative work, preserving variation across styles, judgments, or workflows may be part of what makes a system useful in the first place.

数据注释和标签裁决方面的相关工作已经解决了评估任务中专家分歧的更广泛问题。 Dawid-Skene 建模等方法A related body of work in data annotation and label adjudication has addressed the broader problem of expert disagreement in evaluation tasks. Methods such as Dawid-Skene modeling(Dawid and Skene, 1979), 群众真相, CrowdTruth(Inel et al., 2014)和透视注释框架, and perspectivist annotation frameworks(Basile et al., 2021)将注释者的分歧视为信息丰富的而非纯粹的噪音。然而，这些方法主要是针对具有可定义的基本事实或狭窄分类目标的任务而开发的。创造性评估提出了一个独特的挑战：没有可以近似的基本事实，专家们意见不一致的维度（审美方向、情绪、概念风险）不能简化为错误校准或错误。 HCB 建立在分歧可以作为信号的洞察之上，但将其应用于和解、多数投票、金本位裁决等标准解决策略在结构上可能不合适的领域。treat annotator disagreement as informative rather than purely noisy. However, these approaches have been developed primarily for tasks with definable ground truth or narrow classification objectives. Creative evaluation presents a distinct challenge: there is no ground truth to approximate, and the dimensions on which experts disagree (aesthetic direction, mood, conceptual risk) are not reducible to miscalibration or error. The HCB builds on the insight that disagreement can be signal, but applies it to a domain where the standard resolution strategies like reconciliation, majority vote, gold-standard adjudication may be structurally inappropriate.

    构思：Ideation:发现、探索和方向潜力。在这个阶段，创意人员并不追求最终的制作质量，而是寻求战略上合适且值得发展的令人兴奋的创意方向。Discovery, exploration, and directional potential. At this stage, the creative is not looking for final production quality, but rather for exciting creative direction that is strategically appropriate and worth developing.

  2. (2)

During Ideation, we expect creatives will prefer outputs that are more generative; during Mockup, outputs that develop those directions into coherent specifications are rewarded; finally, Refinement evaluations will prioritize targeted correction, consistency, and polish. We thus expect model rankings, scalar labels, and evaluator agreement to shift across stages, as what is useful will change over the course of the workflow.

假设#3Hypothesis #3：模型比较应该捕捉趋同和分歧，而不仅仅是整体质量。: Model comparison should capture convergence and divergence, not just overall quality.

如果创意质量是一维的，模型比较将减少到更好和更差输出的稳定排名，例如，哪个模型平均产生更好的输出。相反，我们期望模型在满足共同标准和支持不同偏好的方式上有所不同：某些模型可能在收敛标准（例如即时保真度或生产限制）上更可靠，而其他模型可能更好地支持不同的创意方向。因此，收敛-发散分析将揭示模型行为中有意义的差异，这些差异在单个总体排名中消失。If creative quality were one-dimensional, model comparison would reduce to a stable ranking of better and worse outputs, e.g., which model produces better outputs on average. We instead expect models to differ in how they satisfy shared standards and support divergent preferences: some models may be more reliable on convergent criteria such as prompt fidelity or production constraints, while others may better support varied creative directions. A convergence–divergence analysis would therefore surface meaningful differences in model behavior that disappear in a single overall ranking.

6. 讨论与限制Discussion & Limitations

作为早期的先驱者As early pioneers(Treffinger and Poggio, 1972)在创意研究领域写道，in the field of creative studies wrote,“创造力可能代表了如此复杂的人类现象，以至于我们可能永远无法将其充分地表示为单一的、一维的操作变量，甚至是一小组操作。”“creativity may represent such a complex human phenomenon that we may never be able to represent it adequately as a single, unidimensional operational variable, or even as a small set of operations. ”正如我们在这项工作中开始表明的那样，评估创意质量不能仅通过一个评估轴来完成。创意工作是主观和客观标准的混合体，仅评估客观因素会在模型评估和改善创意人员体验中失去重要且有用的信号。As we began to show in this work, evaluating creative quality cannot only be done through one axis of evaluation. Creative work is a hybrid of subjective and objective standards, and assessing only the objective loses important and useful signal in model evaluation and improving creatives’ experiences.

当前人工智能评估的规范没有考虑到创造性劳动中存在的自然多样性和多元化，也没有区分创意人员在工作流程不同阶段的需求。我们认为，这可能会导致产出的同质化，对创造性工作的用处不大，从而导致创造性声音的无差别。我们希望为使用这些工具的创意工作者建立一个共享词汇表。Current norms in AI evaluation do not account for the natural diversity and plurality present in creative labor, nor do they differentiate between the needs of creatives at different stages of their workflow. We believe this can result in homogenized outputs that are less useful for creative work, leading to undifferentiated creative voices. We hope to build a shared vocabulary for creative workers engaging with these tools.

为此，模型开发人员可以使用趋同和发散标准作为不同的干预点：专家趋同的实例突出了模型可以并且应该学习的最佳实践，而分歧则确定了模型不应针对一个目标进行优化但仍对创意偏好做出响应的情况。To this end, model developers may use convergent and divergent criteria as distinct points of intervention: instances where experts converge highlight best practices that models can and should learn, while divergence identifies where a model should not optimize for one target but remain responsive to creative preference.

在构建和评估该数据集时，我们发现人们的需求In building then evaluating this dataset, we find that the requirements of people进化evolve贯穿创造性劳动的各个阶段。工具构建者和创意人员都可以探索阶段级需求变化，以表明哪些模型在给定时刻更好，支持有意的模型切换，而不会给创意工作流程增加摩擦。throughout the stages of creative labor. Tool builders and creatives alike can explore phase-level needs shifts to indicate which models are better at a given moment, supporting deliberate model switching without adding friction to the creative workflow.

什么是质量这个问题仍然没有得到完整的解答，只是因为答案往往在于情人眼中。随着人工智能越来越多地融入创意过程，未来将需要更多的模型选择选择和更多投资来防止创意声音的丧失。The question of what is quality remains unanswered in full, if only because the answer often lies in the eye of the beholder. As AI is increasingly integrated into creative processes, the future will need greater optionality for model selection and more investment in efforts preventing the loss of creative voices.

对于创意人士For Creatives

这项研究为许多创意专业人士已经感受到的东西提供了语言：人工智能工具的挫败感并不一定是它们产生了糟糕的工作，而是它们产生了可能难以调节的无差异的工作。了解哪些模型擅长探索和执行，以及过程中协议在哪里分解为个人偏好，为使用人工智能工具的创意人员提供了选择适合其需求的模型的基础。This research provides language for something many creative professionals already feel: the frustration with AI tools is not necessarily that they produce bad work, but that they produce undifferentiated work that may be difficult to modulate. Understanding which models excel at exploration versus execution, and where in the process agreement breaks down into personal preference, gives creatives using AI tooling a basis for selecting models that fit their needs.

局限性Limitations

这项研究虽然围绕创意过程展开，但并不能完全代表创意工作在实践中如何展开。创造性工作很少如此线性。设计师可以流畅地迭代、在工具之间移动、重新访问阶段，并且经常在单个项目中跨模式工作。未来的研究将探索更长、更少限制的创意弧，以更好地了解这些评估动态在实践中如何发挥作用。 虽然我们提供的数据集为分析提供了实质性基础，但它仅代表一个起点。例如，我们不控制一般模型能力的差异或不确定性结果，例如通过迭代采样，尽管温度和各种其他参数是标准化的，并且使用的提示仅限于有限的主题集。我们还收集了相对较小的参与者群体的反应，限制了我们结果的统计意义。未来的研究应该通过扩大评估者池、多次采样提示以实现模型功能的稳健覆盖以及扩展提示焦点来扩展这项工作。This study, although framed around the creative process, does not fully represent how creative work unfolds in practice. Creative work is rarely so linear. Designers iterate fluidly, move between tools, revisit stages, and often work across modalities within a single project. Future research will explore longer, less constrained creative arcs to better understand how these evaluation dynamics play out in practice. While the dataset we contribute provides a substantive basis for analysis, it represents only a starting point. For example, we do not control for differences in general model capability or the results of non-determinism, e.g., through iterative sampling, though temperature and various other parameters were standardized, and the prompts used were limited to a finite set of topics. We also collected responses from a relatively small participant group, limiting the statistical significance of our results. Future research should extend this work by expanding the evaluator pool, sampling prompts multiple times for robust coverage of model capabilities, and extending the prompt foci.
