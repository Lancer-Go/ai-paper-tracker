摘要Abstract

模型反转攻击（MIA）旨在从人脸识别模型中重建目标身份的代表性训练样本，从而暴露关键的安全漏洞。现有的方法通常依赖于间接引导或高度随机的引导，使得难以稳定地优化目标面部图像的生成轨迹。在本文中，我们提出了转向流模型反演（SFMI），这是一种新颖的两阶段白盒模型反演方法，它将反演重新表述为轨迹转向任务。 具体来说，步骤一，学习通用流匹配先验，预训练通用无条件流匹配模型，以将人脸流形编码为稳健的先验。第二步，使用渐进式指导调度器 (PGS) 进行攻击，在采样期间注入时间相关的特定目标梯度。通过反向传播目标模型以获得中间生成状态的梯度，PGS 逐步将自适应引导信号注入矢量场。这个过程有效地将当前的生成流从随机噪声引导到目标类的高密度区域。 在使用 CelebA 数据集的身份不相交交叉评估设置下，SFMI 在 ArcFace 目标上实现了 0.9248 的 ACC、22.61 的 FID 和 0.3874 的 LPIPS。 对多个目标模型的大量实验表明，SFMI 在评估的白盒​​协议下，在攻击成功和视觉保真度方面实现了具有竞争力的最先进性能。Model Inversion Attacks (MIAs) aim to reconstruct representative training samples of target identities from face recognition models, exposing critical security vulnerabilities. Existing methods typically rely on indirect guidance or highly stochastic guidance, making it difficult to stably optimize generation trajectories toward target facial images. In this paper, we propose Steering Flow Model Inversion (SFMI), a novel two-stage white-box model inversion method that reformulates inversion as a trajectory-steering task. Specifically, Step I, Learning a Generic Flow Matching Prior, pre-trains a generic unconditional Flow Matching model to encode the manifold of human faces as a robust prior. Step II, Attacking with Progressive Guidance Scheduler (PGS), injects time-dependent target-specific gradients during sampling. By backpropagating through the target model to obtain gradients from intermediate generated states, PGS progressively injects adaptive guidance signals into the vector field. This process effectively steers the current generative flow from random noise toward the high-density regions of the target class. Under an identity-disjoint cross-evaluation setting using the CelebA dataset, SFMI achieves an ACC of 0.9248, an FID of 22.61, and an LPIPS of 0.3874 on the ArcFace target. Extensive experiments on multiple target models demonstrate that SFMI achieves competitive state-of-the-art performance in attack success and visual fidelity under the evaluated white-box protocol.

I 简介Introduction

深度神经网络 (DNN) 已成为现代生物识别身份验证的支柱，在人脸识别 (FR) 等任务中实现超人的准确度Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) have emerged as the backbone of modern biometric authentication, achieving superhuman accuracy in tasks such as Face Recognition (FR)[46]。这些模型的功效取决于它们从大规模数据集中提取高级语义特征的能力。然而，这种对广泛训练数据的依赖导致了实用性和隐私之间的根本紧张。理论和经验证据表明，DNN 倾向于无意中“记住”特定的训练样本，而不仅仅是学习通用模式。这种现象，加上关键基础设施中普遍部署的 FR 系统，带来了严重的隐私风险。鉴于 GDPR 等严格法规，审计敏感生物识别信息的潜在泄漏已成为一项至关重要的研究当务之急。. The efficacy of these models is predicated on their capacity to extract high-level semantic features from massive-scale datasets. However, this reliance on extensive training data engenders a fundamental tension between utility and privacy. Theoretical and empirical evidence suggests that DNNs tend to unintentionally “memorize” specific training samples rather than merely learning generalized patterns. This phenomenon, coupled with the pervasive deployment of FR systems in critical infrastructure, poses severe privacy risks. In light of stringent regulations such as the GDPR, auditing the potential leakage of sensitive biometric information has become a paramount research imperative.

在新兴的隐私威胁中，模型反转攻击 (MIA) 表明 DNN（尤其是人脸识别模型）本质上很容易遭受身份泄露。在 MIA 中，对手通过利用训练模型中编码的信息来重建目标身份的代表性面部图像。一般来说，MIA 根据对手的知识分为黑盒和白盒设置。虽然黑盒设置限制对模型查询的访问，但白盒设置假设可以完全访问模型的参数和架构。在本文中，我们主要关注白盒场景，如图 1 所示。Among the emerging privacy threats, Model Inversion Attacks (MIAs) reveal that DNNs—especially face recognition models—are inherently vulnerable to identity leakage. In an MIA, the adversary reconstructs a representative facial image of a target identity by exploiting the information encoded in a trained model. Generally, MIAs are categorized into black-box and white-box settings based on the adversary’s knowledge. While the black-box setting restricts access to model queries, the white-box setting assumes full access to the model’s parameters and architecture. In this paper, we focus on the white-box scenario, as illustrated in Fig.1。在这里，对手利用对模型参数的完全访问来反向传播特定标签（例如“ID：999”）的梯度。这种反馈迭代地引导随机噪声演变成语义上与目标身份一致的面部图像。 在物联网部署中，当对手通过固件检查或暴露的本地存储从物理受损的智能相机、访问控制终端或移动设备中提取设备上的 FR 模型时，就会出现此类访问。然后可以使用外部计算资源对提取的模型进行离线分析，因此反演不需要在资源受限的设备本身上运行。因此，我们将白盒反演视为一种现实的高访问率、最坏情况的隐私审计，而不是一种通用的对抗能力。. Here, the adversary leverages full access to the model parameters to backpropagate gradients for a specific label (e.g., “ID: 999”). This feedback iteratively guides random noise to evolve into a facial image that semantically aligns with the target identity. In IoT deployments, such access can arise when an adversary extracts an on-device FR model from a physically compromised smart camera, access-control terminal, or mobile device through firmware inspection or exposed local storage. The extracted model can then be analyzed offline using external computing resources, so inversion need not run on the resource-constrained device itself. We therefore treat white-box inversion as a realistic high-access, worst-case privacy audit rather than a universal adversarial capability.

尽管有明确定义的威胁模型，但由于搜索空间的高维性，恢复高保真图像仍然是一项艰巨的挑战。早期的方法将反演视为像素空间中的直接优化问题，旨在最大化目标类别的可能性。然而，像素值的优化景观是高度非凸的，使得这些方法容易陷入局部最小值。因此，重建的图像通常会出现明显的视觉伪影，并且无法保持语义一致性。为了缓解这一问题，后续工作利用了生成对抗网络（GAN）Despite the well-defined threat model, recovering high-fidelity images remains a formidable challenge due to the high dimensionality of the search space. Early approaches framed inversion as a direct optimization problem in the pixel space, aiming to maximize the likelihood of the target class. However, the optimization landscape of pixel values is highly non-convex, rendering these methods prone to entrapment in local minima. Consequently, the reconstructed images often suffer from pronounced visual artifacts and fail to preserve semantic coherence. To mitigate this, subsequent works leveraged Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)[10, 11]作为先验知识，优化生成器流形内的潜在代码。虽然 GAN 先验提高了视觉可信度，但它们引入了结构不匹配：从潜在空间到图像空间的映射本质上是非线性和不平滑的。这会导致梯度反向传播不稳定，阻碍优化有效地遍历流形以获得目标身份的精确特征。as prior knowledge, optimizing a latent code within the generator’s manifold. While GAN priors improve visual plausibility, they introduce a structural mismatch: the mapping from the latent space to the image space is inherently non-linear and non-smooth. This results in unstable gradient backpropagation, impeding the optimization from effectively traversing the manifold toward the precise features of the target identity.

近年来，扩散模型（DMs）In recent years, Diffusion Models (DMs)[23, 32, 38]及其连续时间概括，特别是流量匹配 (FM)and their continuous-time generalizations, specifically Flow Matching (FM)[26]，已经取代 GAN 成为生成建模领域最先进的技术。虽然这些模型在对复杂数据分布进行建模方面提供了卓越的功能，但使其适应对抗性反演仍然很重要。大多数基于扩散的反演方法依赖于基于随机微分方程 (SDE) 的采样。然而，沿 SDE 轨迹注入的随机噪声可能会误导甚至干扰目标类别的采样过程。至关重要的是，这些方法缺乏基于数学的机制来根据目标模型的响应有效地纠正生成路径。因此，他们很难明确地将生成流引导至目标分类器定义的特定身份区域。, have superseded GANs as the state-of-the-art in generative modeling. While these models offer superior capabilities in modeling complex data distributions, adapting them for adversarial inversion remains non-trivial. Most diffusion-based inversion methods rely on Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE)-based sampling. However, the stochastic noise injected along the SDE trajectory can mislead, or even interfere with, the sampling process toward the target class. Crucially, these methods lack a mathematically grounded mechanism to effectively rectify the generation path based on the target model’s response. Consequently, they struggle to explicitly navigate the generative flow toward the specific identity regions defined by the target classifier.

在本文中，我们通过提出转向流模型反演（SFMI）来弥补这些差距，这是一种基于流匹配的白盒模型反演方法，将反演重新表述为轨迹转向任务。与依赖复杂随机微分方程的扩散模型不同，流量匹配通过常微分方程 (ODE) 对数据生成进行建模，提供相对平滑且稳定的速度场。我们利用这个属性，不将反演过程视为静态优化，而是将其视为引导从简单先验分布到目标类流形的流程。具体来说，SFMI 分两个阶段运行：第一步，学习通用流匹配先验，预训练无条件流匹配模型以封装稳健的人脸先验；第二步，使用渐进式指导调度器（PGS）进行攻击，它在集成过程中注入特定于目标的梯度。通过在中间积分步骤中反向传播目标模型，PGS 将自适应梯度注入速度场，有效地将流“引导”向目标身份的高密度区域。In this paper, we bridge these gaps by proposing Steering Flow Model Inversion (SFMI), a Flow Matching-based white-box model inversion method that reformulates inversion as a trajectory-steering task. Unlike diffusion models that rely on complex stochastic differential equations, Flow Matching models data generation through an Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE), providing relatively smooth and stable velocity fields. We leverage this property to treat the inversion process not as a static optimization, but as guiding a flow from a simple prior distribution to the target class manifold. Specifically, SFMI operates in two stages: Step I, Learning a Generic Flow Matching Prior, which pre-trains an unconditional Flow Matching model to encapsulate a robust human face prior; and Step II, Attacking with Progressive Guidance Scheduler (PGS), which injects target-specific gradients during integration. By backpropagating through the target model at intermediate integration steps, PGS injects adaptive gradients into the velocity field, effectively “steering” the flow toward the high-density regions of the target identity.

我们在身份不相交协议下跨不同人脸识别目标评估 SFMI，包括基于 ResNet 的主干、常用的人脸识别模型（例如 ArcFace 和 CosFace）、基于现代 Transformer 的 ViT 模型以及在物联网和边缘人脸识别场景中广泛采用的 MobileFaceNet。 SFMI 的平均攻击准确度超过 90%，具有良好的感知相似性，在评估的白盒​​协议下相对于最先进的方法表现出强大且有竞争力的性能。We evaluate SFMI under an identity-disjoint protocol across diverse face recognition targets, including ResNet-based backbones, commonly used face recognition models such as ArcFace and CosFace, the modern transformer-based ViT model, and MobileFaceNet, which is widely adopted in IoT and edge-face-recognition scenarios. SFMI achieves an average attack accuracy above 90% with favorable perceptual similarity, demonstrating strong and competitive performance relative to state-of-the-art methods under the evaluated white-box protocol.

Ⅱ-BII-B 模型反转攻击Model Inversion Attacks

模型反转攻击 (MIA) 旨在重建敏感训练数据或从经过训练的机器学习模型推断隐私属性，从而构成严重的隐私威胁Model Inversion Attacks (MIAs) pose a severe privacy threat by aiming to reconstruct sensitive training data or infer private attributes from trained machine learning models[8, 51]。该概念最初是通过利用简单分类器的预测置信信息来恢复可识别图像来形式化的. The concept was initially formalized by exploiting prediction confidence information from simple classifiers to recover recognizable images[9]。随后的研究迅速将 MIA 扩展到严格的白盒设置中. Subsequent research rapidly expanded MIAs into rigorous white-box settings[30, 37]，将攻击表述为连续像素空间中的显式优化问题。通过利用梯度下降, formulating the attack as an explicit optimization problem in continuous pixel space. By utilizing gradient descent[53]，攻击者试图将随机噪声合成到输入中，以最大化目标类别的概率。然而，由于这种优化直接在极高维和高度非凸的自然图像像素空间中进行，传统方法很容易陷入较差的局部最优，常常导致失败模式，例如由抽象高频噪声主导的语义上无意义的重建。, attackers attempted to synthesize random noise into inputs that maximize the target class probability. However, because this optimization is performed directly in the extremely high-dimensional and highly non-convex natural image pixel space, traditional methods are prone to being trapped in poor local optima, often resulting in failure modes such as semantically meaningless reconstructions dominated by abstract high-frequency noise.

物联网背景下的相关隐私和人脸识别研究调查了不同但互补的方向，包括雾计算下的安全人脸识别Related privacy and face-recognition studies in IoT contexts have investigated distinct but complementary directions, including secure face identification under fog computing[16]，物联网人脸识别的稀疏低秩表示, sparse low-rank representation for IoT face recognition[50]，以及垂直联合预测中的实用特征推理攻击, and practical feature inference attacks in vertical federated prediction[49]，共同强调物联网场景中与隐私相关的风险。, collectively highlighting privacy-related risks in IoT scenarios.

为了减轻直接像素优化的数学不适定性，随着辅助生成先验的引入，发生了深刻的范式转变。张等人。To mitigate the mathematical ill-posedness of direct pixel optimization, a profound paradigm shift occurred with the introduction of auxiliary generative priors. Zhang et al.[56]首创了生成模型反转（GMI）攻击，该攻击利用预先训练的生成对抗网络（GAN）将对抗搜索空间严格限制在自然图像流形内。在这一突破之后，生成式 MIA 大量出现，以提高重建保真度和攻击可转移性。显着的进步包括私有数据分布建模 (KEDMI)pioneered the Generative Model Inversion (GMI) attack, which leverages a pre-trained Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) to constrain the adversarial search space strictly to a natural image manifold. Following this breakthrough, a proliferation of generative MIAs emerged to enhance reconstruction fidelity and attack transferability. Notable advancements include modeling private data distributions (KEDMI)[2]，通过即插即用攻击（PPA）将目标模型与先验模型分离, decoupling target models from priors via Plug & Play Attacks (PPA)[39]，并利用伪标签引导的条件 GAN (PLG) 来解开搜索空间, and utilizing pseudo-label-guided conditional GANs (PLG) to disentangle search spaces[54]。最近，研究人员专注于通过利用中间 GAN 特征 (IFGMI) 来剖析现有架构本身. 尽管视觉可信度有所提高，但现有的生成式 MIA 在重建特定身份目标时仍然存在基本限制。一方面，基于 GAN 的方法依赖于潜在空间优化，其中潜在到图像的映射是高度非线性的，并且通常是不平滑的，导致不稳定的优化动态和频繁收敛到次优解决方案。另一方面，基于扩散的方法通常采用基于随机微分方程（SDE）的采样，其注入的随机扰动可以持续使生成轨迹偏离目标类信号。尽管DiffMI引入了针对特定目标的条件扩散微调，但其基于DDPM的采样仍然面临潜在的轨迹偏差，并且需要多步级联反向传播，这可能会在反演过程中误导生成先验。 FGMIA 通过使用恢复的特征编码来减少迭代优化，但它不使用目标模型反馈来逐步纠正生成轨迹。因此，这两种范式都难以一致地恢复精确的、特定于身份的目标人脸图像，特别是针对现代基于边缘的 FR 模型（例如，第 2 节中的 CosFace 和 ArcFace）。Despite improving visual plausibility, existing generative MIAs still suffer from fundamental limitations when reconstructing identity-specific targets. On the one hand, GAN-based approaches rely on latent-space optimization, where the latent-to-image mapping is highly non-linear and often non-smooth, leading to unstable optimization dynamics and frequent convergence to suboptimal solutions. On the other hand, diffusion-based approaches typically adopt Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE)-based sampling, whose injected stochastic perturbations can continuously deflect the generation trajectory away from the target class signal. Although DiffMI introduces target-specific conditional diffusion fine-tuning, its DDPM-based sampling still faces potential trajectory deviation and requires multi-step cascaded backpropagation, which can mislead the generative prior during inversion. FGMIA reduces iterative optimization by using recovered feature encodings, but it does not use target-model feedback to progressively correct the generation trajectory. As a result, both paradigms struggle to consistently recover precise, identity-specific target face images, especially against modern margin-based FR models (e.g., CosFace and ArcFace in Sec.II-A).

这种基本的优化不稳定性直接激发了我们提出的转向流模型反演（SFMI）。 SFMI 使用流匹配构建确定性 ODE 轨迹，并在采样期间逐步注入目标模型反馈。通过将生成先验重新表述为具有平滑轨迹的确定性 ODE 并引入渐进引导机制，SFMI 提供了轨迹引导主干。这种机制有效地弥合了语义差距，使特定目标的对抗梯度能够稳定、精确地将生成轨迹引导向目标身份，从而克服高度非凸 GAN 和随机扩散模型的局限性。This fundamental optimization instability directly motivates our proposed Steering Flow Model Inversion (SFMI). SFMI uses Flow Matching to construct a deterministic ODE trajectory and progressively injects target-model feedback during sampling. By reformulating the generative prior as a deterministic ODE with smooth trajectories and introducing a progressive guidance mechanism, SFMI provides a trajectory-steering backbone. This mechanism effectively bridges the semantic gap, enabling target-specific adversarial gradients to stably and precisely steer the generative trajectory toward the target identity and thereby overcome the limitations of both highly non-convex GANs and stochastic diffusion models.

V 道德考虑Ethical Considerations

这项工作研究了模型反转攻击，以在白盒、强访问威胁模型下审计人脸识别系统中的隐私泄露。所有实验均在公开的研究数据集上进行，包括 CelebA 和 FFHQ，这些数据集仅根据其数据集条款用于学术评估，而不重新分配原始数据。本文中显示的定性重建是为了证明潜在的隐私泄露风险，而不是为了识别、分析或针对任何个人。我们将出版物视为对此隐私风险的有限学术披露，承认身份重建攻击的双重用途性质，并将 SFMI 视为研究人员、开发人员和系统所有者的隐私审计工具，以激发更强大的保护隐私的人脸识别防御，而不是作为滥用的实用指南。This work studies model inversion attacks to audit privacy leakage in face recognition systems under a white-box, strong-access threat model. All experiments are conducted on publicly available research datasets, including CelebA and FFHQ, which are used only for academic evaluation in accordance with their dataset terms and without redistributing the original data. The qualitative reconstructions shown in the paper are included to demonstrate potential privacy leakage risks rather than to identify, profile, or target any individual. We treat publication as bounded academic disclosure of this privacy risk, acknowledge the dual-use nature of identity-reconstruction attacks, and frame SFMI as a privacy-auditing tool for researchers, developers, and system owners to motivate stronger privacy-preserving face recognition defenses, rather than as a practical guide for misuse.

VI 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了转向流模型反演（SFMI），这是一种将白盒模型反演攻击重新表述为确定性轨迹转向问题的方法。通过利用流匹配的连续 ODE 公式，SFMI 有助于减轻高度非凸 GAN 潜在空间中的优化不稳定性以及标准扩散模型典型的随机破坏。 此外，我们设计了一种渐进式梯度引导机制，可以在集成步骤中动态调节对抗力，确保与生成流在时间和空间上精确对齐。 大量实验表明，相对于最先进的方法，SFMI 提供了强大且有竞争力的性能，特别是在攻击准确性和视觉保真度方面，同时保持了有竞争力的分发级保真度。 SFMI还对CosFace和ArcFace等鲁棒的基于边缘的人脸识别架构表现出强大的攻击能力，并保持跨分布泛化。In this paper, we introduced Steering Flow Model Inversion (SFMI), a method that reformulates white-box model inversion attacks as a deterministic trajectory-steering problem. By leveraging the continuous ODE formulation of Flow Matching, SFMI helps mitigate the optimization instability in highly non-convex GAN latent spaces and the stochastic disruption typical of standard diffusion models. Furthermore, we designed a progressive gradient guidance mechanism that dynamically modulates adversarial force across integration steps, ensuring precise temporal and spatial alignment with the generative flow. Extensive experiments demonstrate that SFMI delivers strong and competitive performance relative to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in attack accuracy and visual fidelity, while maintaining competitive distribution-level fidelity. SFMI also exhibits strong attack capability against robust margin-based face recognition architectures such as CosFace and ArcFace and maintains cross-distribution generalization.

SFMI 仍然有局限性。首先，它涉及相对较多的超参数，调整可能很困难，有效的设置取决于对制导动力学的清晰理解。其次，SFMI是一种白盒方法，需要完全访问包括梯度在内的目标模型，这对应于实际场景中访问权限较高的设置。 第三，SFMI依赖于对相关公共辅助人脸数据的访问，在公共数据和私有数据之间较大的域转移下其性能可能会下降。 第四，SFMI无意直接攻击物联网设备；相反，必须首先从设备中提取部署的目标模型，然后使用外部计算资源进行攻击。 第五，跨模型 ACC 评估和 LPIPS 提供了身份恢复和感知相似性的补充证据。尽管如此，ACC 仍然是一个跨模型识别代理，令人满意的人脸识别评估者并不一定意味着与人类身份感知一致。 总体而言，SFMI 为审计深度生物识别系统中的隐私泄露提供了严格且有效的工具。在未来的工作中，我们计划研究保护隐私的人脸识别模型、较弱的访问和黑盒模型反转场景、减少对许多超参数依赖的更鲁棒的攻击方法、针对模型反转攻击的更强的防御范式以及更好地符合人类身份判断的模型反转方法。SFMI still has limitations. First, it involves relatively many hyperparameters and tuning can be difficult, and effective settings depend on a clear understanding of guidance dynamics. Second, SFMI is a white-box method that requires full access to the target model including gradients, which corresponds to a relatively high-access setting in practical scenarios. Third, SFMI depends on access to relevant public auxiliary face data, and its performance may degrade under larger domain shifts between the public and private data. Fourth, SFMI is not intended to attack directly on IoT devices; instead, the deployed target model must first be extracted from the device and then attacked using external computing resources. Fifth, cross-model ACC evaluation and LPIPS provide complementary evidence of identity recovery and perceptual similarity. Nevertheless, ACC remains a cross-model recognition proxy, and satisfying face recognition evaluators does not necessarily imply alignment with human identity perception. Overall, SFMI provides a rigorous and effective tool for auditing privacy leakage in deep biometric systems. In future work, we plan to investigate privacy-preserving face recognition models, weaker-access and black-box model inversion scenarios, more robust attack methods that reduce reliance on many hyperparameters, stronger defensive paradigms against model inversion attacks, and model inversion methods that better align with human identity judgments.
