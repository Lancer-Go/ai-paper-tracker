摘要。Abstract.

尽管用于企业自动化和决策的人工智能代理取得了快速进展，但其在现实世界中的部署和进一步的性能提升仍然受到有限的数据质量和数量、复杂的现实世界推理需求、自我对弈困难以及缺乏可靠反馈信号的限制。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一个轻量级的、与模型无关的框架，用于通过离线强化学习（RL）来改进基于 LLM 的企业代理。所提出的通过DT-MDP（DT-MDP-CE）框架进行上下文工程包括三个关键组件：（1）数字孪生马尔可夫决策过程（DT-MDP），它将代理的推理行为抽象为有限MDP； (2) 强大的对比逆强化学习，配备 DT-MDP，可以有效地估计有充分依据的奖励函数，并从混合质量的离线轨迹中引入策略； （3）强化学习引导的上下文工程，使用从（1）和（2）的集成过程中获得的策略来改善智能体的决策行为。作为案例研究，我们将该框架应用于面向企业的 IT 自动化领域的代表性任务。大量的实验结果表明，在各种评估设置中，基准代理均取得了一致且显着的改进，这表明该框架可以推广到企业环境中具有相似特征的其他代理。Despite rapid progress in AI agents for enterprise automation and decision-making, their real-world deployment and further performance gains remain constrained by limited data quality and quantity, complex real-world reasoning demands, difficulties with self-play, and the lack of reliable feedback signals. To address these challenges, we propose a lightweight, model-agnostic framework for improving LLM-based enterprise agents via offline reinforcement learning (RL). The proposed Context Engineering via DT-MDP (DT-MDP-CE) framework comprises three key components: (1) A Digital-Twin Markov Decision Process (DT-MDP), which abstracts the agent’s reasoning behavior as a finite MDP; (2) A robust contrastive inverse RL, which, armed with the DT-MDP, to efficiently estimate a well-founded reward function and induces policies from mixed-quality offline trajectories; and (3) RL-guided context engineering, which uses the policy obtained from the integrated process of (1) and (2), to improve the agent’s decision-making behavior. As a case study, we apply the framework to a representative task in the enterprise-oriented domain of IT automation. Extensive experimental results demonstrate consistent and significant improvements over baseline agents across a wide range of evaluation settings, suggesting that the framework can generalize to other agents sharing similar characteristics in enterprise environments.

2.1. 基于LLM的AI代理LLM-based AI Agent

我们的目标是开发人工智能代理，以代表或协助企业环境中的各种角色执行任务，例如信息技术运营 (ITOps) 中的站点可靠性工程师 (SRE)。这些任务通常涉及多轮推理，其中人类操作员在与环境交互时做出顺序决策，例如通过收集信息和执行操作。在 SRE 设置中，此类任务包括诊断系统故障、识别事件的根本原因以及实施补救策略。因此，为此设置设计的基于 LLM 的代理会执行多步骤推理过程，根据中间观察结果生成一系列决策。We aim to develop AI agents that perform tasks on behalf of, or in assistance to, various personas in enterprise settings, such as Site Reliability Engineers (SRE) in Information Technology Operations (ITOps). These tasks typically involve multi-turn reasoning, where human operators make sequential decisions while interacting with the environment, for example, by collecting information and executing actions. 5. 相关作品Related Works

LLM 的奖励设计：Reward Design for LLMs:奖励设计决定了法学硕士在强化学习期间获得什么样的推理行为。现有的方法包括可验证的奖励（例如，正确性检查）、生成或学习的奖励、密集的过程级奖励和无监督的内在信号Reward design determines what reasoning behaviors an LLM acquires during RL. Existing approaches include verifiable rewards (e.g., correctness checks), generative or learned rewards, dense process-level rewards, and unsupervised intrinsic signals(Zhang et al., 2025a)。可验证的奖励是可靠的，但通常很少，而密集或学习的奖励需要昂贵的注释或风险偏差。相比之下，我们的框架通过对比逆强化学习直接从混合质量轨迹估计奖励，减少了对手工制作或密集注释奖励信号的依赖。. Verifiable rewards are reliable but generally sparse, while dense or learned rewards require costly annotations or risk bias. In contrast, our framework estimates rewards directly from mixed-quality trajectories via contrastive inverse RL, reducing reliance on handcrafted or densely annotated reward signals.

情境工程：Context Engineering:情境工程研究如何选择、构建和更新提供给法学硕士的信息，包括检索的知识、记忆和工具输出Context engineering studies how to select, structure, and update the information provided to LLMs, including retrieved knowledge, memory, and tool outputs(Mei et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024; Jeong et al., 2024)。最近的方法将上下文构建视为顺序决策问题，使用 RL 或 bandit 算法来选择相关文档、工具或提示标记. Recent methods treat context construction as a sequential decision problem, using RL or bandit algorithms to choose relevant documents, tools, or prompt tokens(Zhang et al., 2025b; Deng et al., 2022; Luo et al., 2025)。与这些直接在高维文本空间中应用 RL 的方法不同，我们的方法首先构建代理推理过程的有限 DT-MDP 抽象，从而在应用生成的策略来指导上下文工程之前实现样本高效的离线学习。. Unlike these approaches, which apply RL directly in high-dimensional text spaces, our method first builds a finite DT-MDP abstraction of the agent’s reasoning process, enabling sample-efficient offline learning before applying the resulting policy to guide context engineering.

针对 LLM 代理人的基于模型的干预：Model-based Interventions for LLM Agents:大多数 LLM 代理都是无模型的，依赖于上下文推理，没有环境动态的明确表示Most LLM agents are model-free, relying on contextual reasoning without explicit representations of environment dynamics(Yao et al., 2022; Schick et al., 2023; Trivedi et al., 2023)。基于模型的方法而是结合世界模型或模拟器来指导规划和评估. Model-based approaches instead incorporate world models or simulators to guide planning and evaluation(M. Moerland et al., 2023; Ferrag et al., 2025)。我们的 DT-MDP 抽象充当从轨迹学习的轻量级行为模型。它不是对整个环境进行建模，而是捕获有限 MDP 中的关键推理动态，从而在数据受限的企业环境中实现实用的离线策略学习。. Our DT-MDP abstraction serves as a lightweight behavioral model learned from trajectories. Rather than modeling the full environment, it captures key reasoning dynamics in a finite MDP, enabling practical offline policy learning in data-constrained enterprise settings.

有监督和 RL 微调：Supervised and RL Fine-tuning:有监督微调（SFT）和基于强化学习的微调是法学硕士适应专业领域的主要方法Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and RL-based fine-tuning are the dominant approaches for adapting LLMs to specialized domains(Dong et al., 2024; Shao et al., 2024b)。 SFT总体上稳定有效，但需要大量高质量的标签数据，这些数据在企业环境中成本高昂且难以获取. SFT is generally stable and effective, but it requires large volumes of high-quality labeled data, which are costly and difficult to obtain in enterprise settings(Shen, 2024)。另一方面，基于强化学习的微调依赖于可靠的奖励信号，通常需要在线交互或自我对弈来收集训练数据. RL-based fine-tuning, on the other hand, relies on reliable reward signals and often requires online interaction or self-play to collect training data(Zha et al., 2025)，这在企业环境中可能不切实际、不安全或受到限制。这些限制使得大规模微调难以在此类领域中部署。我们的方法避免了广泛的微调，而是通过高效的离线逆强化学习和策略引导的上下文工程来改善代理行为，只需要有限的轨迹数据，不需要在线交互。, which may be impractical, unsafe, or restricted in enterprise environments. These limitations make large-scale fine-tuning difficult to deploy in such domains. Our approach avoids extensive fine-tuning and instead improves agent behavior through efficient offline inverse RL and policy-guided context engineering, requiring only limited trajectory data and no online interaction.

6. 讨论与结论Discussions & Conclusions

在本文中，我们提出了一个基于 DT-MDP 公式来改进基于 LLM 的代理的框架，用于面向企业的任务。通过 IT 自动化领域的广泛实证评估，我们表明，以 DT-MDP 策略为指导的基于 RL 的上下文工程能够持续提高代理性能。该框架是通用的且与代理无关，因为它基于抽象的状态-动作表示而不是特定于任务的逻辑进行操作。这种设计表明它适用于更广泛的企业场景和具有类似特征的其他设置。更广泛地说，DT-MDP-CE 可以被视为一种用于推理 LLM 的基于模型的上下文工程。通过上下文工程整合结构化模型，该方法提高了 LLM 代理的可靠性、可解释性和一致性，为复杂的现实环境中的更可控和更值得信赖的系统提供了一条途径。这种语言推理和结构化建模的集成形成了一种平衡灵活性和精度的混合架构，并为构建不仅有能力，而且在实践中可控、可解释和可靠的 LLM 代理指明了一个有前途的方向。In this paper, we proposed a framework for improving LLM-based agents for enterprise-oriented tasks based on the DT-MDP formulation. Through extensive empirical evaluation in the domain of IT automation, we showed that RL-based context engineering guided by DT-MDP policies consistently improves agent performance. The framework is general and agent-agnostic, as it operates on abstract state-action representations rather than task-specific logic. This design suggests its applicability to a broader range of enterprise scenarios and other settings with similar characteristics. More broadly, DT-MDP-CE can be viewed as a form of model-based context engineering for reasoning LLMs. By incorporating structured models through context engineering, the approach improves the reliability, interpretability, and alignment of LLM agents, offering a pathway toward more controllable and trustworthy systems in complex real-world settings. This integration of linguistic reasoning and structured modeling forms a hybrid architecture that balances flexibility with precision, and points to a promising direction for building LLM agents that are not only capable, but also controllable, explainable, and dependable in practice.

对于未来的工作，我们计划将 DT-MDP-CE 应用于更广泛的 SRE 场景以及更多样化的企业应用程序。我们在企业环境中确定的四个特征（例如，有限的数据、复杂的推理需求、自我博弈的难度和缺乏反馈）也出现在其他领域，例如医疗保健，DT-MDP-CE 可能会提供类似的好处。此外，我们计划将DT-MDP框架扩展到微调。特别是，我们的目标是利用 DT-MDP 公式产生的长期累积奖励估计来指导监督微调并进一步提高代理性能。For future work, we plan to apply DT-MDP-CE across a broader range of SRE scenarios, as well as more diverse enterprise applications. The four characteristics we identified in enterprise settings (e.g., limited data, complex reasoning demands, difficulty of self-play, and scarce feedback) also arise in other domains, such as healthcare, where DT-MDP-CE may provide similar benefits. In addition, we plan to extend the DT-MDP framework to fine-tuning. In particular, we aim to leverage the long-term cumulative reward estimates produced by the DT-MDP formulation to guide supervised fine-tuning and further improve agent performance.

LLM 的奖励设计。Reward Design for LLMs.奖励设计是法学硕士强化学习的基础，因为它决定了法学硕士将学习什么样的推理行为。一般来说，奖励可以分为可验证的、生成的、密集的和无监督的Reward design is the foundation of RL for LLMs, as it dictates what kind of reasoning behaviors an LLM will learn. Rewards in general can be categorized into verifiable, generative, dense, and unsupervised(Zhang et al., 2025a)。具体来说，可验证的奖励（例如正确性检查或单元测试结果）是最具可扩展性和可靠性的，使其成为具有客观可衡量结果的任务的理想选择，但它们通常稀疏且延迟，提供的学习信号有限。当明确的正确性不可用时，生成或学习奖励依赖于基于模型或人工智能的反馈，提供了更大的灵活性，但存在风险偏差和奖励利用。密集且面向过程的奖励提供有关中间推理步骤的反馈，帮助模型学习如何推理，而不仅仅是优化最终答案。当外部监督有限时，源自新颖性、不确定性或自我一致性等内在信号的无监督奖励是有价值的。有效的奖励塑造混合了多种奖励信号以平衡准确性、连贯性和多样性，对于稳定训练和增强推理任务的泛化至关重要。与之前依赖于手动指定信号、可验证检查或学习奖励模型的奖励设计方法不同，我们的方法通过对比逆强化学习从轨迹排名中自动获得奖励。这允许从稀疏、嘈杂和混合质量的企业轨迹中进行奖励估计，而不需要步骤级别的监督或手工设计的奖励。. Specifically, verifiable rewards, such as correctness checks or unit test outcomes, are the most scalable and reliable, making them ideal for tasks with objectively measurable outcomes, while they are often sparse and delayed, providing limited learning signals. Generative or learned rewards, which rely on model-based or AI feedback when explicit correctness is unavailable, offer greater flexibility yet risk bias and reward exploitation. Dense and process-oriented rewards deliver feedback on intermediate reasoning steps, helping models learn how to reason rather than only optimizing final answers. Unsupervised rewards, derived from intrinsic signals such as novelty, uncertainty, or self-consistency, are valuable when external supervision is limited. Effective reward shaping, which blends multiple reward signals to balance accuracy, coherence, and diversity, is essential for stabilizing training and enhancing generalization across reasoning tasks. Unlike prior reward design approaches that rely on manually specified signals, verifiable checks, or learned reward models, our method derives rewards automatically from trajectory rankings via contrastive inverse RL. This allows reward estimation from sparse, noisy, and mixed-quality enterprise trajectories without requiring step-level supervision or handcrafted rewards.

情境工程。Context Engineering.上下文工程包含用于选择、构建和管理提供给大型语言模型的信息的方法，塑造它们在不同任务中的推理和执行方式。上下文工程不是将上下文视为单个静态提示，而是将其视为动态组装过程，将检索到的知识、记忆、工具、中间输出和特定于任务的信号集成到连贯的输入中Context engineering encompasses the methods used to select, structure, and manage the information provided to large language models, shaping how they reason and perform across diverse tasks. Rather than viewing context as a single, static prompt, context engineering treats it as a dynamic assembly process that integrates retrieved knowledge, memory, tools, intermediate outputs, and task-specific signals into a coherent input(Mei et al., 2025)。这个视角将上下文作为优化目标，在上下文长度、延迟和可靠性等实际约束下确定要包含哪些信息、如何格式化它以及何时更新或丢弃它。通过将上下文视为系统设计的核心组成部分，上下文工程旨在通过优化模型看到的内容、看到的方式以及看到的时间来提高基础性、适应性和推理能力。. This perspective frames context as an optimization target, determining what information to include, how to format it, and when to update or discard it, under practical constraints such as context length, latency, and reliability. By treating context as a core component of system design, context engineering aims to improve grounding, adaptability, and reasoning by optimizing what the model sees, how it sees it, and when it sees it.

LLM 代理人基于模型的干预。Model-based Interventions for LLM Agents.LLM 代理已成为强大的通用推理系统，能够执行对话、规划、编码和多步骤问题解决等多种任务LLM agents have emerged as powerful general-purpose reasoning systems capable of performing diverse tasks such as dialogue, planning, coding, and multi-step problem solving(Yao et al., 2022; Schick et al., 2023; Trivedi et al., 2023)。他们将自然语言理解与程序推理相结合的能力为自主代理开辟了新的途径，可以通过语言指令进行计划和行动。尽管取得了这些进步，当前的 LLM 智能体仍然主要是无模型的：它们依赖于模式识别和上下文推理，而没有明确了解环境的动态或其行为背后的因果结构。这种反应性限制了他们的远见、一致性和安全决策的能力，特别是在动态或高风险的环境中. Their ability to integrate natural language understanding with procedural reasoning has opened new avenues for autonomous agents that can plan and act through linguistic instructions. Despite these advances, current LLM agents remain predominantly model-free: they rely on pattern recognition and contextual inference without an explicit understanding of the environment’s dynamics or the causal structure underlying their actions. This reactive nature limits their capacity for foresight, consistency, and safe decision-making, particularly in dynamic or high-stakes settings(Huang et al., 2024).

相比之下，基于模型的推理是强化学习和控制理论等领域长期确立的原则，强调在行动之前使用显式世界或行为模型来模拟、预测和评估结果In contrast, model-based reasoning, a principle long established in fields such as reinforcement learning and control theory, emphasizes the use of explicit world or behavioral models to simulate, predict, and evaluate outcomes before acting(M. Moerland et al., 2023)。将此范式应用于 LLM 代理引入了基于模型的干预的概念：可以指导、监控或修改代理的推理过程的外部或嵌入式模型。这些干预措施可以采取学习世界模型、形式逻辑约束、模拟器或元预测组件的形式，用于评估代理计划的合理性、安全性或效率。通过整合此类结构化模型，LLM 代理能够推理“假设”场景、预测其行动结果，并主动而非被动地调整行为. Applying this paradigm to LLM agents introduces the concept of model-based interventions: external or embedded models that can guide, monitor, or modify an agent’s reasoning process. These interventions may take the form of learned world models, formal logic constraints, simulators, or meta-predictive components that evaluate the plausibility, safety, or efficiency of an agent’s plans.
