证据门控任务和运动规划Evidence-Gated Task and Motion Planning
与视觉语言模型with Vision-Language Models

Tsunehiko Tanaka    Matthew Stephenson Affiliation: Waseda University, Flinders University    Alistair Macvicar Affiliation: Waseda University, Flinders University    Edgar Simo-Serra
摘要Abstract

根据自然语言指令执行长视野操作任务的机器人必须推理语义任务结构和几何可行性。然而，在部分可观察性下，目标相关对象的可用性可能是不确定的。在这种情况下，将视觉语言模型 (VLM) 与任务和运动规划 (TAMP) 相结合的方法可能会生成依赖于 VLM 的先验知识而没有观察支持的子目标，从而导致执行失败或意外结果。我们提出了证据获取和可行性门控（EAFG），这是一个通过 VLM 生成的探索性子目标和基于 TAMP 的执行来获取视觉证据的框架。然后，EAFG 应用可行性门来决定是否继续进行任务规划、获取进一步的证据或停止。我们的实验表明，在对象使用不明确的烹饪任务中，EAFG 通过在计划之前发现与任务相关的对象来提高食谱完成度。对于需要不存在对象的指令，EAFG 会促进适当的停止决策并减少重复尝试操纵该对象。Robots executing long-horizon manipulation tasks from natural-language instructions must reason about both semantic task structure and geometric feasibility. However, under partial observability, the availability of goal-relevant objects may be uncertain. In such cases, approaches that combine Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with Task and Motion Planning (TAMP) may generate subgoals that rely on the VLM’s prior knowledge without observational support, leading to execution failures or unintended outcomes. We propose Evidence Acquisition and Feasibility Gating (EAFG), a framework that acquires visual evidence through VLM-generated exploratory subgoals and TAMP-based execution. EAFG then applies a feasibility gate to decide whether to proceed with task planning, acquire further evidence, or halt. Our experiments show that, in cooking tasks with ambiguous object use, EAFG improves recipe completion by discovering task-relevant objects before planning. For instructions requiring an absent object, EAFG promotes appropriate halt decisions and reduces repeated attempts to manipulate that object.

1 简介Introduction

在日常环境中运行的机器人必须能够根据自然语言指令执行复杂的、长期的操作任务。例如，诸如“做汤”之类的指令必须扩展为涉及多个对象的一系列操作动作，包括可用的原料和加热设备。此类任务要求机器人解释语言指令的语义，同时满足几何和运动学约束，例如抓取可行性、放置稳定性和无碰撞可达性。因此，任务和运动规划（TAMP）已被广泛用作长视野操纵规划的核心技术，因为它可以生成考虑问题的几何和运动学方面的动作序列Robots operating in everyday environments must be able to execute complex, long-horizon manipulation tasks from natural-language instructions. For example, an instruction such as “make soup” must be expanded into a sequence of manipulation actions involving multiple objects, including available ingredients and heating appliances. Such tasks require the robot to interpret the semantic meaning of the language instruction while satisfying geometric and kinematic constraints, such as grasp feasibility, placement stability, and collision-free reachability. Task and Motion Planning (TAMP) has therefore been widely used as a core technique for long-horizon manipulation planning, as it can generate action sequences that account for the geometric and kinematic aspects of the problem[18, 5]。另一方面，视觉语言模型（VLM）可以整合视觉和语言信息，从自然语言目标推断常识程序和语义上有意义的中间目标。因此，分层集成（其中 VLM 生成高级子目标，TAMP 将每个子目标细化为几何上可行的动作序列）是自然语言长视野机器人操作的一种有前途的方法. On the other hand, Vision-Language Models (VLMs) can integrate visual and linguistic information to infer commonsense procedures and semantically meaningful intermediate goals from natural-language objectives. Consequently, a hierarchical integration in which a VLM generates high-level subgoals and TAMP refines each subgoal into a geometrically feasible action sequence is a promising approach to natural-language long-horizon robot manipulation[21, 12]。然而，此类系统的成功取决于用于形成子目标的信息是否实际上得到环境观察的支持。. However, the success of such systems depends on whether the information used to form subgoals is actually supported by observations of the environment.

当最初的观察只是部分的时，就会出现一个关键的限制A key limitation arises when the initial observation is only partial[6, 16]。与目标相关的对象可能隐藏在封闭的存储空间内，或者它们可能根本不存在于环境中。在这种情况下，VLM 可以依赖先验知识或常识性假设来推断这些对象可能位于何处并生成合理的配方步骤。 TAMP 本身并不提供在规划之前主动验证未观察对象的机制。因此，结合 VLM 和 TAMP 的现有方法通常假设完全可观察的面向对象状态. Goal-relevant objects may be hidden inside closed storage spaces, or they may not exist in the environment at all. In such cases, a VLM may rely on prior knowledge or commonsense assumptions to infer where those objects are likely to be and to generate plausible recipe steps. TAMP does not by itself provide a mechanism for actively verifying unobserved objects before planning. As a result, existing methods that combine VLMs and TAMP typically assume a fully observable object-oriented state[21, 12]。在部分可观察性下生成的计划可能会忽略未观察到的对象或尝试使用不存在的对象。此类计划可能效率低下，并导致无法满足指示的意外结果。因此，长期操纵计划需要一个明确的机制，用于在任务计划之前确定可用的观察结果是否为实现目标提供了足够的证据。. Plans generated under partial observability may ignore unobserved objects or attempt to use objects that are not present. Such plans can be inefficient and lead to unintended outcomes that fail to satisfy the instruction. Long-horizon manipulation planning thus requires an explicit mechanism for determining, before task planning, whether the available observations provide sufficient evidence for achieving the goal.

在这项工作中，我们提出了证据获取和可行性门控（EAFG），这是一个主动收集信息并根据所获取的证据确定可行性的框架。 EAFG 首先生成子目标来解决缺失或不确定的信息，例如打开封闭存储空间的门。每个子目标都由TAMP解决，由机器人执行，并用于收集观察图像。然后，VLM 评估所获得的观测结果是否为实现目标的规划提供了足够的信息。如果收集到足够的证据，系统会根据该信息生成任务计划。这使得规划能够以经过验证的观察结果以及 VLM 的先验知识为基础。如果 VLM 确定实现目标所需的证据不充分，EAFG 将停止执行。通过这种方式，EAFG 减少了涉及不存在对象的重复计划或执行尝试，并防止出现意外结果。为了评估这些属性，我们构建了三个烹饪场景：具有明确指定对象的目标、未指定的目标以及涉及缺失对象的目标。我们的结果表明，EAFG 在使用环境中实际存在的对象完成未指定的目标以及通过在证据获取后停止来处理不可行的丢失对象任务方面优于 VLM-TAMP 基线。In this work, we propose Evidence Acquisition and Feasibility Gating (EAFG), a framework that actively gathers information and determines feasibility based on the acquired evidence. EAFG first generates subgoals for resolving missing or uncertain information, such as opening the doors of closed storage spaces. Each subgoal is solved by TAMP, executed by the robot, and used to collect observation images. The VLM then evaluates whether the acquired observations provide sufficient information for planning toward the goal. If sufficient evidence has been collected, the system generates a task plan conditioned on this information. This enables planning to be grounded in verified observations as well as in the VLM’s prior knowledge. If the VLM determines that the evidence required for goal achievement is insufficient, EAFG halts execution. In this way, EAFG reduces repeated planning or execution attempts involving nonexistent objects and prevents unintended outcomes. To evaluate these properties, we construct three cooking scenarios: goals with explicitly specified objects, underspecified goals, and goals involving missing objects. Our results show that EAFG outperforms a VLM-TAMP baseline in completing underspecified goals using objects actually present in the environment and in handling infeasible missing-object tasks by halting after evidence acquisition.

这项工作的贡献有三个：The contributions of this work are threefold:

  • •

    我们引入了一种预先规划的证据获取机制，该机制促使 VLM 规划信息收集子目标、揭示隐藏区域并验证与目标相关的对象。We introduce a pre-planning evidence-acquisition mechanism that prompts a VLM to plan information-gathering subgoals, revealing hidden regions and verifying goal-relevant objects.

  • •

    我们提出了一种可行性门控机制，用于评估所获得的证据是否支持可行的任务规划，要求额外的观察或在目标要求仍然不受支持时停止。We propose a feasibility-gating mechanism that evaluates whether acquired evidence supports feasible task planning, requesting additional observations or halting when goal requirements remain unsupported.

  • •

    我们设计了三个涵盖明确目标、未指定目标和缺失目标目标的烹饪场景，并凭经验表明 EAFG 改进了接地完成和不可行性检测。We design three cooking scenarios spanning explicit, underspecified, and missing-object goals, and empirically show that EAFG improves grounded completion and infeasibility detection.

In contrast, our method enables the robot to act autonomously to gather evidence and, based on that evidence, decide whether to plan, acquire additional evidence, or halt.

总体而言，先前的工作通过 TAMP 提供几何可行性，通过基础模型进行语义任务分解，或通过部分可观测性规划进行信息收集。我们的贡献是将证据获取置于任务计划之前，并将获得的观察结果用作接地计划或停止的明确可行性门。Overall, prior work provides geometric feasibility through TAMP, semantic task decomposition through foundation models, or information gathering through partial-observability planning. Our contribution is to place evidence acquisition before task planning and to use acquired observations as an explicit feasibility gate for grounded planning or halting.

3 证据获取和可行性门控Evidence Acquisition and Feasibility Gating

我们提出证据获取和可行性门控（EAFG），它使用 VLM 主动获取观察证据，以在任务规划之前验证与目标相关的对象和环境条件。 EAFG 生成证据获取子目标来解决缺失或不确定的信息，并使用获取的证据来确定是否应该继续进行目标规划。如果在探索目标相关区域后无法获得所需的证据，EAFG 将停止执行，以避免根据不受支持的假设生成计划。We propose Evidence Acquisition and Feasibility Gating (EAFG), which uses a VLM to actively acquire observational evidence for verifying goal-relevant objects and environmental conditions before task planning. EAFG generates evidence-acquisition subgoals that resolve missing or uncertain information, and uses the acquired evidence to determine whether planning for the goal should proceed. If the required evidence cannot be obtained after exploring the goal-relevant regions, EAFG halts execution to avoid generating a plan based on unsupported assumptions.

5 局限性Limitations

EAFG 依靠 TAMP 模块将每个证据获取子目标实例化为几何和运动学上可行的低级动作序列。然而，EAFG 没有明确处理证据采集期间发生的操作失败的恢复。因此，当 TAMP 失败时，系统可能无法获取环境中与任务相关的证据，导致可行性门控根据不充分的信息评估可行性。尽管存在这一限制，EAFG 仍与将 VLM 与结构化规划器相结合的多种方法兼容，并且随着底层规划和反馈机制的进步，其稳健性预计将随着时间的推移而提高。EAFG relies on the TAMP module to instantiate each evidence-acquisition subgoal as a geometrically and kinematically feasible low-level action sequence. However, EAFG does not explicitly handle recovery from manipulation failures that occur during Evidence Acquisition. Therefore, when TAMP fails, the system may be unable to acquire that task-relevant evidence in the environment, causing Feasibility Gating to assess feasibility based on insufficient information. Despite this limitation, EAFG is compatible with a wide range of methods that combine VLMs with structured planners, and its robustness is expected to improve over time as the underlying planning and feedback mechanisms advance.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 EAFG，这是一个在任务规划之前主动获取视觉证据的框架，并使用可行性门来决定是否计划、进一步探索或停止。实验表明，EAFG 通过在规划之前发现与任务相关的隐藏对象，在部分可观察性下提高了基础任务的完成度。 EAFG 还提倡在无法确认所需对象时适当停止，从而减少对不存在对象的不必要的执行尝试。We proposed EAFG, a framework that actively acquires visual evidence before task planning and uses a feasibility gate to decide whether to plan, explore further, or halt. Experiments show that EAFG improves grounded task completion under partial observability by discovering task-relevant hidden objects before planning. EAFG also promotes appropriate halting when required objects cannot be confirmed, reducing unnecessary execution attempts toward absent objects.
