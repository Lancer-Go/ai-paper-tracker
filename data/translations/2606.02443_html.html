2 相关工作Related Work

人工智能和多模式安全基准。AI and multimodal safety benchmarks.安全基准评估文本、对话、图像和视频中的有害输出避免和危险推理Safety benchmarks evaluate harmful-output avoidance and hazard reasoning across text, dialogue, images, and video(Levy et al., 2022; Mazeika et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024a; Diallo et al., 2025; Arora et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2026, 2025; Yuan et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2025)。这些数据集暴露了拒绝、建议安全、健康和危机推理、情境安全和多模式伤害识别方面的失败。另外，可穿戴和物联网系统的目标是固定传感器功能，例如跌倒、健康或碰撞监测. These datasets expose failures in refusal, advice safety, health and crisis reasoning, situational safety, and multimodal harm recognition. Separately, wearable and IoT systems target fixed-sensor functions such as fall, health, or crash monitoring(Perez et al., 2019; Pantelopoulos and Bourbakis, 2010; Lara and Labrador, 2013)。然而，这两条线都没有评估这里研究的警告系统决策：通用 MLLM 必须监视开放式视觉流，决定可见证据何时变得可行，在伤害发生之前及时发出警报，并对良性场景保持沉默。最后一个要求实际上很重要，因为重复的错误警报会降低用户信任并导致警报疲劳. However, neither line evaluates the warning-system decision studied here: a general-purpose MLLM must monitor an open-ended visual stream, decide when visible evidence becomes actionable, issue a timely alert before harm occurs, and remain silent on benign scenes. The last requirement is practically important because repeated false alerts can reduce user trust and cause alarm fatigue(LeClerc and Joslyn, 2015; The Joint Commission, 2013; Sendelbach and Funk, 2013).

视频 MLLM 和流视频基准。Video MLLM and streaming-video benchmarks.通用视频 MLLM 基准测试测量时间感知、视频问答和长上下文视频理解General video-MLLM benchmarks measure temporal perception, video question answering, and long-context video understanding(Li et al., 2024; Fu et al., 2025; Mangalam et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024b)。最近的流媒体基准测试通过在视频流期间查询模型或测试实时交互、内存和响应生成，进一步将评估转向在线设置. Recent streaming benchmarks further move evaluation toward online settings by querying models during a video stream or testing real-time interaction, memory, and response generation(Lin et al., 2024; Niu et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025; Chatterjee et al., 2026; Shi et al., 2026)。这些作品在协议上与 PaSBench-Video 接近，但目标不同。他们主要评估 QA、事件理解或交互质量，而 PaSBench-Video 评估主动安全警告：模型必须在因果观察下在沉默和警告之间进行选择，将其第一个警告放置在人工注释的风险窗口内，识别具体的风险源，并避免在无风险视频上出现误报。. These works are close to PaSBench-Video in protocol, but differ in objective. They mainly evaluate QA, event understanding, or interaction quality, whereas PaSBench-Video evaluates proactive safety warning: the model must choose between silence and warning under causal observation, place its first warning within a human-annotated risk window, identify the concrete risk source, and avoid false positives on no-risk videos.

以风险为导向的视频理解。Risk-oriented video understanding.事故预测和驾驶风险解释研究早期碰撞预测、交通人员交互、驾驶员注意力、风险定位和字幕Accident anticipation and driving-risk explanation study early crash prediction, traffic-agent interactions, driver attention, risk localization, and captioning(Chan et al., 2016; Fang et al., 2019; Bao et al., 2020, 2021; Karim et al., 2022; Yao et al., 2023; Moura et al., 2025; Fang et al., 2022; Malla et al., 2023)。异常检测和无意行为的平行线研究固定摄像机或弱监督视频中的异常事件. A parallel line on anomaly detection and unintentional actions studies abnormal events in fixed-camera or weakly supervised videos(Mahadevan et al., 2010; Lu et al., 2013; Luo et al., 2017; Liu et al., 2018; Sultani et al., 2018; Wu et al., 2020; Epstein et al., 2020; Zhao et al., 2025)。这些任务为识别异常事件提供了有用的代理。但大多数都是针对特定领域的，并且依赖于特定于任务的检测器或离线标签，或者评估异常情况，而不是现在是否应该发出面向用户的警报。相反，PaSBench-Video 将风险理解视为通用 MLLM 的在线决策问题：尽早检测新出现的风险以进行干预，找到因果风险源，并避免对良性视频发出不必要的警报。. These tasks provide useful proxies for recognizing abnormal events. But most are domain-specific and rely on task-specific detectors or offline labels, or evaluate abnormality rather than whether a user-facing alert should be raised now. PaSBench-Video instead treats risk understanding as an online decision problem for general-purpose MLLMs: detect an emerging risk early enough for intervention, locate the causal risk source, and avoid unnecessary alerts on benign videos.

局限性Limitations

首先，PaSBench-Video 将人工注释的风险启动框架视为基本事实； “可采取行动”和“迫在眉睫”风险之间的界限本质上是主观的，并且注释者之间的协议虽然由两个注释者合并加裁决来控制，但并不完美。其次，我们的 259 个视频无风险分割是良性场景的有限样本，可能无法捕获已部署系统可能面临的所有干扰因素；因此，我们报告每个视频而不是每秒的误报率。第三，我们的内容基础评估对具有足够首次点击量的五个模型中的每个模型的 50 次点击使用单注释器标签；多注释器扩展和更广泛的模型覆盖范围将推迟到未来的工作。最后，由于缺乏高质量的风险视频，尤其是那些具有明显可预测和可避免结果的视频，尽管我们努力收集和合成来自不同来源和领域的视频，但基准的覆盖范围仍然有限。First, PaSBench-Video treats the human-annotated risk-start frame as ground truth; the boundary between “actionable” and “imminent” risk is inherently subjective, and inter-annotator agreement, while controlled by a two-annotator merge plus adjudication, is not perfect. Second, our 259-video no-risk split is a finite sample of benign scenes and may not capture all distractors a deployed system would face; we therefore report false-positive rates per video rather than per second. Third, our content-grounding evaluation uses single-annotator labels on 50 hits per model from the five models with sufficient First-Hit volume; a multi-annotator extension and broader model coverage are deferred to future work. Finally, due to the scarcity of high-quality risk videos, especially those with clearly predictable and avoidable outcomes, the benchmark’s coverage remains limited despite our efforts to collect and synthesize videos from diverse sources and domains.

After the two-author review, third-author adjudication for disagreements, and a later pass over low-confidence and short-gap cases, 13 low-confidence items and one manually excluded sample are removed, producing the final 481-video risk split. The no-risk split is built separately from 315 normal-video candidates in Nexar, RADSV, and SmartHome; manual filtering keeps 259 videos, including 105 Nexar, 59 RADSV, and 95 SmartHome videos.

A.3 医疗资源转换Healthcare Source Conversion

医疗保健子集源自 HealthBench 文本对话示例，而不是原始临床视频。我们首先在 HealthBench 评估 JSONL 上运行高召回率医疗风险过滤器。该过滤器结合了 HealthBench 标签、医生同意的紧急类别、中毒、癫痫、胸痛、呼吸短促、严重出血、意识丧失和紧急服务提示等提示术语，以及不安全、紧急、立即医疗评估和呼叫紧急服务等标题术语。这产生了 1,460 名潜在不安全候选人。The healthcare subset is derived from HealthBench text-dialogue examples rather than raw clinical videos. We first run a high-recall medical-risk filter over the HealthBench evaluation JSONL. The filter combines HealthBench tags, physician-agreed urgency categories, prompt terms such as poisoning, seizure, chest pain, shortness of breath, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, and emergency-service cues, and rubric terms such as unsafe, emergency, immediate medical evaluation, and call emergency services. This produces 1,460 potentially unsafe candidates.

然后我们应用确定性监视适宜性过滤器。每个候选者都会根据视觉可观测性、单相机适配性、预警窗口、可干预性和生成稳定性进行评分。该过滤器保留风险可以外部化为可见行为或可见环境危害的场景，例如跌倒、癫痫、窒息、呼吸窘迫、毒物或烟雾暴露、溺水、火灾或受伤。它拒绝高度隐私或非视觉案例，包括心理健康特定、亲密护理、用药史、实验室结果、剂量和主观症状场景。此阶段产生 71 个候选监控视频。We then apply a deterministic surveillance-suitability filter. Each candidate is scored on visual observability, single-camera fit, early-warning window, interventionability, and generation stability. The filter keeps scenarios whose risk can be externalized as visible behavior or a visible environmental hazard, such as falls, seizures, choking, respiratory distress, poison or smoke exposure, drowning, fire, or injury. It rejects high-privacy or non-visual cases, including mental-health-specific, intimate-care, medication-history, lab-result, dosage, and subjective-symptom scenarios. This stage yields 71 surveillance-video candidates.

对于视频生成，我们将候选者转换为具有明确计时的固定摄像头护理监控场景。确定性提示构建器将每个候选人映射到稳定的风险模板和环境，例如护理设施走廊跌倒、自助餐厅窒息事件、清洁用品烟雾暴露、公共休息室呼吸困难、活动室癫痫发作、厨房火灾或烟雾，或在治疗池附近滑倒。 10 秒提示强制执行 0.0–2.0 秒设置、2.0–6.0 秒可见警告和风险升级、6.0–8.0 秒事故结果和 8.0–10.0 秒后果，并且在事故发生前至少有 4.0 秒可见警告。该提示还需要固定的宽监控摄像头、全身可视性、无剪切或缩放、无字幕或文本覆盖、无血迹或图形损伤。For video generation, we convert the candidates into fixed-camera care-monitoring scenarios with explicit timing. A deterministic prompt builder maps each candidate to a stable risk template and setting, for example a care-facility hallway fall, cafeteria choking event, cleaning-supply fume exposure, respiratory distress in a common room, seizure in an activity room, fire or smoke in a kitchen, or slipping near a therapy pool. The 10-second prompt enforces 0.0–2.0s setup, 2.0–6.0s visible warning and risk escalation, 6.0–8.0s accident outcome, and 8.0–10.0s aftermath, with at least 4.0 seconds of visible warning before the accident. The prompt also requires a fixed wide surveillance camera, full-body visibility, no cuts or zooms, no subtitles or text overlays, and no gore or graphic injury.

我们另外将定时提示转换为 Kling v3 Omni 多重提示。多重镜头版本使用三个 4 秒镜头：设置、持续警告和事故/后果。它包括一个审查门，要求固定闭路电视风格、全身可视性、4-8 秒持续警告、8 秒后才发生事故，并且没有电影摄影机或文字。 Kling Generation 制作了 70 个医疗候选视频，其中 33 个通过人工审核并保留在最终的正分中。We additionally convert the timed prompts into Kling v3 Omni multi-shot prompts. The multi-shot version uses three 4-second shots: setup, sustained warning, and accident/aftermath. It includes a review gate requiring fixed CCTV style, full-body visibility, sustained warning from 4–8s, accident only after 8s, and no cinematic camera or text. Kling generation produces 70 healthcare candidate videos, of which 33 pass manual review and are retained in the final positive split.

A.4 工业来源Industrial Sources

工业子集始于从清听动满抖音帐户收集的工业安全视频。这些源剪辑通常包含字幕、水印、剪辑、特写、多机位剪辑和汇编式故事讲述，因此我们不会直接将它们用作最终基准视频。相反，我们使用它们作为源场景并执行基于本地人工智能的转换步骤以获得更清晰的警告视频。The industrial subset starts from industrial-safety videos collected from the Qingting Dongman Douyin account. These source clips often contain captions, watermarks, edits, close-ups, multiple camera cuts, and compilation-style storytelling, so we do not use them directly as final benchmark videos. Instead, we use them as source scenarios and perform a local AI-based conversion step to obtain cleaner warning videos.

我们首先使用联系表和元数据筛选本地源池。在117个审查的源视频中，选择48个短单事件片段直接转换，54个长或多镜头片段标记为片段提取，15个因风险太弱、太短、不清晰或不适合主动预警而被拒绝。直接剪辑和提取的片段形成了 175 名候选评论库。每个经过审查的候选者都会记录主要风险、估计的事故前累积时间、审查原因以及描述如何将源重建为监控式场景的转换说明。审查决定产生了 111 名转换候选人、42 名边缘候选人和 22 名被拒绝的候选人。We first screen the local source pool with contact sheets and metadata. Among 117 reviewed source videos, 48 short single-event clips are selected for direct conversion, 54 long or multi-shot clips are marked for segment extraction, and 15 are rejected because the risk is too weak, too short, unclear, or unsuitable for proactive warning. The direct clips and extracted segments form a 175-candidate review pool. Each reviewed candidate records the main risk, estimated pre-accident buildup time, a review reason, and a conversion note describing how to reconstruct the source as a monitoring-style scene. The review decisions produce 111 candidates for conversion, 42 borderline candidates, and 22 rejected candidates.

然后，我们使用 Kling v3 Omni 在本地转换 111 个选定的候选者。转换脚本根据风险摘要、估计警告累积情况和 CCTV 转换说明为每个候选人构建一个提示。该提示要求提供真实的安全事件视频，可以来自固定的高位闭路电视或工业安全摄像机视图，也可以在场景需要时来自第一人称安全摄像机视图。在转换队列中，106 名候选人使用固定闭路电视风格的视图，5 名使用第一人称视图。该提示强制实施简单的时间结构：建立环境和不安全位置，通过清晰的警告窗口显示可见的风险发展，显示非图形的未遂事件或安全事件，然后立即显示后果。它还要求逼真的实景镜头、逼真的物理、实用的灯光、自然的人体运动、监控摄像机压缩、风险因素的清晰可见性，并且没有文本覆盖、字幕、徽标、水印、动画、CGI、程式化渲染或图形损伤。We then convert the 111 selected candidates locally with Kling v3 Omni. The conversion script builds one prompt per candidate from the risk summary, estimated warning buildup, and CCTV conversion note. The prompt asks for a realistic safety incident video, either from a fixed high-mounted CCTV or industrial security camera view, or from a first-person safety-camera view when the scenario requires it. In the conversion queue, 106 candidates use fixed CCTV-style views and 5 use first-person views. The prompt enforces a simple temporal structure: establish the environment and unsafe positions, show visible risk development with a clear warning window, show a non-graphic near miss or safety incident, and then show immediate aftermath. It also asks for photorealistic live-action footage, realistic physics, practical lighting, natural human motion, surveillance-camera compression, clear visibility of risk factors, and no text overlays, subtitles, logos, watermarks, animation, CGI, stylized rendering, or graphic injury.

我们导出 111 个转换后的工业视频及其原始源剪辑。由于初始运行使用了许多 5 秒请求，因此我们进一步使用基于源持续时间的请求重新生成 53 个剪辑，以便在需要时保留更长的警告累积；最终导出的转换集包含 58 个未更改的 5 秒视频和 53 个在 8-13 秒请求的重新生成视频。然后，在最终数据集清理之前，人工审核会从 111 个转换的剪辑中选择 47 个作为合格的工业阳性结果。在用于完整基准测试的相同最终低置信度和排除通过之后，46 个工业生产视频仍处于最终正分割中。We export 111 converted industrial videos paired with their original source clips. Because the initial run used many 5-second requests, we further regenerate 53 clips with source-duration-based requests to preserve longer warning buildup when needed; the final exported conversion set contains 58 unchanged 5-second videos and 53 regenerated videos requested at 8–13 seconds. Human review then selects 47 of the 111 converted clips as eligible industrial positives before final dataset cleanup. After the same final low-confidence and exclusion pass used for the full benchmark, 46 industrial-production videos remain in the final positive split.
