摘要Abstract

LLM 代理需要完成跨软件工具、业务服务和本地工作空间的端到端工作单元。 然而，许多代理基准测试会在发布时冻结策划的任务集，并主要对最终响应进行评分，这使得根据不断变化的工作流需求评估代理或验证任务是否已执行变得困难。 我们推出了 Claw-Eval-Live，这是一个工作流代理的实时基准测试，它将可刷新的信号层（跨版本更新的公共工作流需求信号）与可重现的带时间戳的发布快照分开。 在构建方面，每个版本都是根据公共工作流程需求信号构建的，并使用当前版本中使用的 ClawHub Top-500 技能，并具体化为具有固定固定装置、服务、工作空间和分级机的受控任务。 在评分方面，Claw-Eval-Live 记录执行跟踪、审计日志、服务状态和运行后工作区工件，在证据充足时使用确定性检查，并仅针对语义维度进行结构化 LLM 判断。 该版本包含 105 项任务，涵盖受控业务服务和本地工作区修复，并在共享公共通行证规则下评估 13 个前沿模型。 对这些公共模型的实验表明，可靠的工作流自动化还远未得到解决，领先的模型仅通过了 66.7% 的任务，没有一个模型达到 70%。 他们表明，故障是由任务系列和执行面构成的，人力资源、管理和多系统业务工作流程是持续的瓶颈，本地工作区修复相对容易但不饱和。 最后，仅靠排行榜排名是不够的，因为具有相似通过率的模型在总体完成度上可能存在差异，并且任务级别歧视集中在评估任务的中间地带。 Claw-Eval-Live 建议工作流代理评估应基于新的外部需求和可验证的代理行为两次。LLM agents are expected to complete end-to-end units of work across software tools, business services, and local workspaces. Yet many agent benchmarks freeze a curated task set at release time and grade mainly the final response, making it difficult to evaluate agents against evolving workflow demand or verify whether a task was executed. We introduce Claw-Eval-Live, a live benchmark for workflow agents that separates a refreshable signal layer, updated across releases from public workflow-demand signals, from a reproducible, time-stamped release snapshot. On the construction side, each release is constructed from public workflow-demand signals, with ClawHub Top-500 skills used in the current release, and materialized as controlled tasks with fixed fixtures, services, workspaces, and graders. On the grading side, Claw-Eval-Live records execution traces, audit logs, service state, and post-run workspace artifacts, using deterministic checks when evidence is sufficient and structured LLM judging only for semantic dimensions. The release contains 105 tasks spanning controlled business services and local workspace repair, and evaluates 13 frontier models under a shared public pass rule. Experiments on these public models reveal that reliable workflow automation remains far from solved, with the leading model passing only 66.7% of tasks and no model reaching 70%. They show that failures are structured by task family and execution surface, with HR, management, and multi-system business workflows as persistent bottlenecks and local workspace repair comparatively easier but unsaturated. Finally, leaderboard rank alone is insufficient because models with similar pass rates can diverge in overall completion, and task-level discrimination concentrates in a middle band of evaluation tasks. Claw-Eval-Live suggests that workflow-agent evaluation should be grounded twice, in fresh external demand and in verifiable agent action.

1 简介Introduction

随着大型语言模型（LLM）智能体从单轮问答转向多步执行，评估的目标也在发生变化。 之前的工作已将法学硕士与工具使用、规划和代理支架联系起来As Large Language Model (LLM) agents move from single-turn question answering to multi-step execution, the target of evaluation is changing as well. Prior work has connected LLMs to tool use, planning, and agentic scaffolds[48, 36, 37, 32, 42, 12, 39]。 现代代理工具，例如 Codex. Modern agent harnesses such as Codex[28], 克劳德·代码, Claude Code[1], 开爪, OpenClaw[29]、爱马仕代理, and Hermes Agent[27]装备模型来调用工具、导航工作区、查询服务以及跨应用程序协调操作。 核心问题日益成为代理是否能够完成端到端的工作单元，而不仅仅是产生合理的响应。 这种转变反映在网络、桌面、移动、软件和专业代理基准测试中equip models to call tools, navigate workspaces, query services, and coordinate actions across applications. The central question is increasingly whether an agent can complete an end-to-end unit of work rather than merely produce a plausible response. This shift is reflected in web, desktop, mobile, software, and professional-agent benchmarks[54, 43, 34, 15, 45, 49]。 现在，典型的部署要求代理协调事务、准备来自多个系统的会议摘要、诊断损坏的工作区、协调审批链或修复本地开发堆栈。 这些是工作流程而不是孤立的提示。 它们需要跨系统检索、状态更改写入、工件级编辑以及代理实际操作的可审计证据。. Typical deployments now ask agents to reconcile transactions, prepare a meeting brief from multiple systems, diagnose a broken workspace, coordinate an approval chain, or repair a local development stack. These are workflows rather than isolated prompts. They require cross-system retrieval, state-changing writes, artifact-level edits, and auditable evidence of what the agent actually did.

这给基准设计带来了问题。 许多公共代理基准测试仍然是静态版本。 多域套件、Web 和桌面基准测试以及软件代理任务集均在发布时冻结特定的任务组合This creates a problem for benchmark design. Many public agent benchmarks are still static releases. Multi-domain suites, web and desktop benchmarks, and software-agent task sets each freeze a particular task mixture at publication time[23, 26, 54, 8, 43, 15, 25]。 即使这些基准是现实的并且技术上强大的，固定的混合物也可能会变得陈旧。 这很重要，因为现实世界的工作流程组合不断变化。 工具堆栈不断发展，企业瓶颈发生变化，某些类别的自动化变得更加重要，而其他类别则变得不那么重要。 因此，基准测试可以保持可重复性，同时慢慢偏离用户当前关心的工作流程。 对于工作流代理来说，这种偏差通​​常不是来自任何单个任务，而是来自任务组合本身。 图. Even when these benchmarks are realistic and technically strong, that fixed mixture can become stale. That matters because the real-world workflow mix keeps changing. Tool stacks evolve, enterprise bottlenecks shift, and some categories of automation become far more important while others become less central. A benchmark can therefore remain reproducible while slowly drifting away from the workflows that users currently care about. For workflow agents, this drift often comes less from any single task than from the task mix itself. 工作流基准测试还面临第二个问题。 完善的备忘录并不能证明代理查询了正确的记录、比较了正确的实体、更新了正确的对象或修复了任务实际依赖的损坏的工件。 这种故障模式激发了轨迹意识和风险意识评估Workflow benchmarks face a second problem as well. A polished memo is not evidence that the agent queried the correct records, compared the right entities, updated the right object, or repaired the broken artifact that the task actually depended on. This failure mode motivates trajectory-aware and risk-aware evaluation[49, 35, 51, 53]。 在工作流程设置中，“听起来正确”和“完成工作”之间的区别并不是表面上的。 代理可能会生成流畅的报告，同时默默地错过所需的写入、编辑错误的文件或将建议建立在不正确的记录中。 这对于面向部署的工作流程尤其重要，这些工作流程通常将服务工作与本地工作区工作结合起来。 在服务支持的任务中，代理必须查询记录、交叉引用系统并提交状态更改操作，例如草稿、日历更新或任务创建。 在工作区任务中，代理必须检查日志、编辑文件、运行命令并证明修复确实有效。 许多高价值工作流程跨越了这些界限。 现有的基准测试通常只展示一侧：Web 和设备基准测试强调交互环境，而软件和命令行基准测试则强调工作空间执行. In workflow settings, the difference between “sounds right” and “did the work” is not cosmetic. An agent may produce a fluent report while silently missing a required write, editing the wrong file, or grounding a recommendation in incorrect records. This matters especially for deployment-facing workflows, which commonly combine service work with local workspace work. In service-backed tasks, the agent must query records, cross-reference systems, and commit state-changing actions such as drafts, calendar updates, or task creation. In workspace tasks, the agent must inspect logs, edit files, run commands, and prove that a repair actually worked. Many high-value workflows cross these boundaries. Existing benchmarks usually make one side visible: web and device benchmarks emphasize interaction environments, while software and command-line benchmarks emphasize workspace execution[50, 30, 52, 43, 34, 15, 22, 25]。 仅对最终响应进行评分或仅涵盖其中一项设置的基准无法可靠地判断代理是否确实检索了正确的信息、更改了正确的状态并修复了正确的工件。 对于面向部署的工作流程，缺少的要求是它们的组合。. A benchmark that only scores final responses, or that covers only one of these settings, cannot reliably tell whether the agent actually retrieved the right information, changed the right state, and repaired the right artifact. For deployment-facing workflows, the missing requirement is their combination.

Claw-Eval-Live 的构建理念是工作流代理评估应该校准两次。 首先，发布的任务分布应该接近不断发展的现实世界工作流程，而不是一次性的作者快照。 其次，每项任务的分数应基于可观察的执行证据，而不是仅基于最终文本的合理性。 为此，Claw-Eval-Live 将每个公开版本视为一个带时间戳的基准快照，该快照是根据公共工作流信号上的可重新运行的信号到任务管道构建的，然后使用特定于任务的、基于操作的评分器来评估受控服务和沙盒工作区中的代理。Claw-Eval-Live is built around the idea that workflow-agent evaluation should be calibrated twice. First, the released task distribution should stay close to evolving real-world workflows rather than to a one-time author snapshot. Second, the score of each task should be anchored in observable execution evidence rather than in final-text plausibility alone. To that end, Claw-Eval-Live treats each public release as a time-stamped benchmark snapshot built from a rerunnable signal-to-task pipeline over public workflow signals, then evaluates agents in controlled services and sandboxed workspaces with task-specific, action-grounded graders.

3.1 从公共工作流程信号开始Starting from public workflow signals

Claw-Eval-Live 的一个核心设计选择是任务分配不是由基准测试作者单独指定的。 相反，我们使用公共上游代理来满足当代工作流程需求：ClawHub Top-500 流行技能，按发布构建时的下载量和受欢迎程度进行排名。 这些信号不被视为部署频率、经济价值或任务难度的真实测量结果。 相反，它们充当外部可检查的先验，其中工作流类型当前对用户来说很重要。 这种区别对于基准有效性很重要：信号指导任务族的混合，而发布的任务本身仍然在受控评估条件下实施、筛选和分级。A central design choice in Claw-Eval-Live is that the task distribution is not specified solely by the benchmark authors. Instead, we use a public upstream proxy for contemporary workflow demand: the ClawHub Top-500 popular skills, ranked by downloads and popularity at the time of release construction. These signals are not treated as ground-truth measurements of deployment frequency, economic value, or task difficulty. Rather, they serve as an externally inspectable prior over which workflow types are currently salient to users. This distinction matters for benchmark validity: the signals guide the mixture of task families, while the released tasks themselves are still implemented, screened, and graded under controlled evaluation conditions.

这种分离为 Claw-Eval-Live 提供了带有时间戳的发布设计，而不会牺牲再现性。 版本发布后，其任务、固定装置和评分器保持固定，以进行稳定的模型比较。 不同版本之间的变化是用于构建下一个任务混合的上游信号快照。 这种设计使基准测试可以通过未来的更新来吸收工作流生态系统中的变化，而不是通过修改已经发布的评估集。This separation gives Claw-Eval-Live a time-stamped release design without sacrificing reproducibility. Once a release is published, its tasks, fixtures, and graders remain fixed for stable model comparison. What changes across releases is the upstream signal snapshot used to construct the next task mixture. This design lets the benchmark absorb shifts in the workflow ecosystem through future refreshes, rather than by modifying an already published evaluation set.

模式2：使用更小的语义组件进行操作验证。Pattern 2: operation verification with a smaller semantic component.

对于起草、会议安排或票证分类等任务，确定性验证更重要，因为通常可以直接从审核日志中检查正确性。 当演示质量仍然相关时，评判仅用于语气、组织或摘要质量等方面。For tasks such as drafting, meeting scheduling, or ticket triage, deterministic verification carries more weight because correctness can often be checked directly from audit logs. 模式 3：工作区任务的脚本优先验证。Pattern 3: script-first verification for workspace tasks.

当需要判断时，我们使用GPT-5.4作为判断模型。 每个法官调用都会收到任务提示、代理跟踪、可观察操作的结构化摘要以及特定于任务的标题。 因此，法官不需要对隐藏的证据产生幻觉；它被要求在显式跟踪和检查的基础上对语义维度进行评分。 由于GPT-5.4也是评估模型之一，因此这种设计会引入判断模型偏差。 我们通过将法学硕士的判断限制在确定性检查无法覆盖的语义维度、将每个法官的输入基于痕迹和规则以及报告每个任务使用的确定性证据来源来减轻这种风险。 我们不将法官视为独立的人类审判者。When a judge is required, we use GPT-5.4 as the judge model. Each judge call receives the task prompt, the agent trace, structured summaries of observable actions, and the task-specific rubric. The judge is therefore not asked to hallucinate hidden evidence; it is asked to score semantic dimensions on top of explicit traces and checks. Because GPT-5.4 is also one of the evaluated models, this design can introduce judge-model bias. We mitigate this risk by limiting LLM judging to semantic dimensions that deterministic checks cannot cover, grounding every judge input in traces and rubrics, and reporting the deterministic evidence sources used by each task. We do not treat the judge as an independent human adjudicator.

The asymmetry is stark. All models score at least 72.2% pass rate on the workspace slice, and several models are at or near 100%. But no model is above 59.8% on service-backed workflows. Claude Opus 4.6 leads that slice at 59.8%, with GPT-5.4 at 56.3% and GLM-5 at 55.2%.

这很重要，因为它改变了排行榜的解释方式。 目前的代理通常已经能够在固定限制下进行局部诊断和修复。 更困难的瓶颈是跨多个服务的协调、证据敏感的业务执行。 换句话说，主要的差距不是“模型是否可以使用终端？”但是“它能否在不丢失状态、丢失证据或无法执行所需写入的情况下完成多系统工作流程？”This matters because it changes how the leaderboard should be interpreted. Current agents are often already competent at local diagnosis and repair under fixed constraints. The harder bottleneck is coordinated, evidence-sensitive business execution across multiple services. In other words, the main gap is not “can the model use a terminal at all?” but “can it complete a multi-system workflow without losing state, missing evidence, or failing to execute required writes?”

5.4 排名差异以及 Claw-Eval-Live 真正衡量的是什么Ranking divergence and what Claw-Eval-Live is really measuring

Claw-Eval-Live 得出的排名不仅仅反映了一般的聊天或写作能力。 这是预期的。 该基准测试不仅奖励最终答案的可读性，还奖励最终答案的可读性。它还奖励跨系统证据收集、正确的记录链接、操作完成和运行后状态完整性。 因此，如果模型未能调用所需的工具、错过了关键证据或将工作区置于错误的状态，那么编写流畅摘要的模型仍然可能得分很低。The ranking induced by Claw-Eval-Live does not simply mirror general chat or writing ability. That is expected. The benchmark does not reward only the readability of the final answer; it also rewards cross-system evidence collection, correct record linkage, action completion, and post-run state integrity. A model that writes fluent summaries can therefore still score poorly if it failed to call the required tools, missed crucial evidence, or left the workspace in the wrong state.

这是“可以说”与“可以做”的核心区别。 例如，入职式人力资源任务通常会引发精美但笼统的撰写。 然而，在基于行动的评分下，如果这些输出忽略了员工特定的详细信息、错过了所需的工具调用或不满足任务的证据检查，那么这些输出就会失败。 同样的模式也出现在管理和工作流程系列中，其中最终文本可能看起来似乎合理，但潜在的证据线索仍然不完整。 因此，Claw-Eval-Live 不仅衡量语言的合理性，还衡量可追踪证据下的执行结束。This is the core “can say” versus “can do” distinction. For example, onboarding-style HR tasks often elicit polished but generic writeups. However, under action-grounded grading, these outputs fail if they omit employee-specific details, miss required tool calls, or do not satisfy the task’s evidence checks. The same pattern appears in management and workflow families, where the final text may look plausible while the underlying evidence trail remains incomplete. Claw-Eval-Live therefore measures not just linguistic plausibility, but executional closure under traceable evidence.

6 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 Claw-Eval-Live，这是一个围绕两个设计目标构建的实时代理基准测试：工作流程校准和证据对齐。 工作流程校准使发布的任务组合接近用户当前想要自动化的工作流程。 证据对齐根据智能体可观察到的行为进行评分，而不仅仅是最终文本的合理性。 当前的快照显示了为什么这很重要以及当前代理的不足之处。 最好的模型仅通过了 66.7% 的任务，本地工作区修复比 API 支持的业务工作流程要容易得多，而且人力资源、管理和多系统协调仍远未解决。 因此，每个版本最好被视为带有时间戳的公共快照，而不是工作流代理能力的永久定义。 在各个版本中，信号层可以通过新的公共工作流程信号进行刷新，从而使基准测试能够跟踪不断变化的自动化需求，而不会使过去的评估变得不稳定。 基准测试的长期价值在于让每个版本都明确说明其构建原因、衡量的好点以及仍然遗漏的内容。We introduced Claw-Eval-Live, a live agent benchmark built around two design goals: workflow calibration and evidence alignment. Workflow calibration keeps the released task mix close to workflows users currently want to automate. Evidence alignment grounds scores in what the agent observably did rather than in final-text plausibility alone. The current snapshot shows both why this matters and where current agents fall short. The best model passes only 66.7% of tasks, local workspace repair is materially easier than API-backed business workflows, and HR, management, and multi-system coordination remain far from solved. Each release is therefore best read as a time-stamped public snapshot rather than a permanent definition of workflow-agent ability. Across releases, the signal layer can be refreshed from new public workflow signals, allowing the benchmark to track changing automation demand without making past evaluations unstable. The long-term value of the benchmark is to make every release explicit about why it was constructed, what it measures well, and what it still leaves out.
