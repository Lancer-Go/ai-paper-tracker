10 结论Conclusion

代理技能风险在整个生命周期中是渐进的，并且具有否认的效果 可以重新进入曲面形状、刀具或车削。 ClawSentry 通过与框架无关的方法来应对这一问题 网关结合使用前包审查、效果标准化 L1-L3 中介、 反绕过记忆和行动后证据，同时保持审阅者只读 和策略权威网关拥有。在五个工作代理中，它限制了 技能安全 ASR 为 9.09–15.03%（33.5–49.7% 原始），清洁技能 TSR 为 98.7%； 只有 13.8% 的 L1 事件需要模型审查。其 6.7 点的 TSR 成本位于 执法边界，而进一步的收获在于证据携带 分销渠道和基于 AHP 的多代理委托。Agentic skill risk is progressive across the lifecycle, and a denied effect can re-enter across surface form, tool, or turn. ClawSentry counters this with a framework-agnostic gateway combining pre-use package review, effect-normalized L1–L3 mediation, anti-bypass memory, and post-action evidence, while keeping reviewers read-only and policy authority gateway-owned. Across five Work Agents, it confines SkillsSafety ASR to 9.09–15.03% (33.5–49.7% raw) at 98.7% clean-skill TSR; only 13.8% of L1 events require model review. Its 6.7-point TSR cost locates the enforcement boundary, while further gains lie in evidence-carrying distribution channels and AHP-based multi-agent delegation.
