摘要Abstract

人类世的一个决定性问题是将物理地球和人类社会建模为一个耦合系统，但没有任何学习的表征能够跨越其观察范围。我们认为障碍是几何上的：物理地球被测量为忽略政治边界的连续场，而社会则以行政单位来报告。地球系统基础模型服务于第一几何；将其耦合到第二个需要跨边界有损平均。我们介绍A defining problem of the Anthropocene is to model the physical Earth and human societies as one coupled system, yet no learned representation spans their observational breadth. We argue the obstacle is geometric: the physical Earth is measured as continuous fields that ignore political borders, whereas societies are reported for administrative units. Earth-system foundation models serve the first geometry; coupling it to the second has required lossy averaging over borders. We introduce特拉诺瓦TerraNova，一个基础模型，根据 1,024 个自然几何和社会记录进行训练：512 个网格地球系统字段和 512 个国家指标。专用编码器代表位置、国家/地区、时间和任务，跨模式转换器将它们融合成共享的时空状态，超网络生成每个查询的解码器，其证据头返回预测分布。两个对比目标将表示结合在一起：每个国家与其领土内的坐标之间的人口加权对齐，以及携带图像衍生语义的预训练地理空间嵌入。通过该解码器读出，该表示与专用地理空间编码器具有竞争力，同时跨越它们不表示的轴（时间、海洋和不确定性）并支持国家级功能。冻结的主干网从稀疏的观察中重建密集的场，并在几分钟内适应消费者硬件上看不见的变量。, a foundation model trained on 1,024 physical and societal records in their native geometries: 512 gridded Earth-system fields and 512 national indicators. Dedicated encoders represent location, country, time and task, cross-modal transformers fuse them into a shared spatiotemporal state, and a hypernetwork generates a per-query decoder whose evidential head returns a predictive distribution. Two contrastive objectives couple the representation: a population-weighted alignment between each country and coordinates in its territory, and one to pretrained geospatial embeddings carrying image-derived semantics. Read out through that decoder, the representation is competitive with purpose-built geospatial encoders while spanning axes they do not represent (time, oceans and uncertainty) and supporting country-level capabilities. The frozen backbone reconstructs dense fields from sparse observations and adapts to unseen variables in minutes on consumer hardware.

1 简介Introduction

人类世的一个关键科学问题A key scientific problem of the Anthropocene(Crutzen, 2002)共同模拟社会和地球系统作为它们形成的相互关联的整体：温室气体排放改变大气成分；土地改造改变了水文、反照率和碳储存；基础设施集中暴露；机构塑造脆弱性；气候影响影响经济发展、移民和政策is to jointly model societies and the Earth system as the interconnected whole they form: greenhouse-gas emissions alter atmospheric composition; land transformation changes hydrology, albedo and carbon storage; infrastructure concentrates exposure; institutions shape vulnerability; and climate impacts affect economic development, migration and policy(Steffen et al., 2015; Richardson et al., 2023)。这些联系在观察中已经显而易见：气候变化改变了各国经济增长和不平等的分布. These connections are already visible in observations: climate change has altered the distribution of economic growth and inequality across countries(Diffenbaugh and Burke, 2019)，其影响已经在人类发展成果中显现出来, its effects are already visible in human development outcomes(Tavoni et al., 2025)，对其经济成本的估计很大且分散, and estimates of its economic cost are large and widely dispersed(Moore et al., 2024)。行星变化和政策相关研究需要跨越物理、生态、经济和制度系统的数据表示，而不是将其记录视为不相关的数据集. Planetary change and policy-relevant research requires data representations that span physical, ecological, economic and institutional systems, rather than treating their records as unrelated datasets(Ou et al., 2026).

现有的模型系列仅涵盖该系统的一部分。天气和气候模型通过物理定律的数值近似来解决物理动力学问题，并通过协调的多模型协议进行比较Existing model families cover only a part of that system. Weather and climate models resolve physical dynamics through numerical approximations to physical law, and are compared through coordinated multi-model protocols(Eyring et al., 2016);经济和综合评估模型通过区域总量、情景和损害关系代表福利和政策; economic and integrated-assessment models represent welfare and policy through regional aggregates, scenarios and damage relationships(Nordhaus, 2017; Riahi et al., 2017)。地球系统基础模型提高了全球预测的地球物理预测的准确性和速度. Earth-system foundation models have improved the accuracy and speed of geophysical prediction, from global forecasting(Lam et al., 2023; Bi et al., 2023)多变量和多领域适应to multi-variable and multi-domain adaptation(Nguyen et al., 2023; Bodnar et al., 2025)，但它们仍然局限于物理几何形状。没有任何一种学习表征可以跨越物理地球和其中的社会，而且据我们所知，没有一种表征能够在全球范围内同时学习这两种表征。, but they remain confined to the physical geometry. No single learned representation spans the physical Earth and the societies embedded in it, and to our knowledge none learns from both simultaneously at a global scale.

物理地球被测量为The physical Earth is measured as a领域field：气候、植被或土壤湿度随空间和时间不断变化。社会被观察到: climate, vegetation, or soil moisture vary continuously over space and time. Societies are observed within边界boundaries：每年都会报告政治边界而非实际边界的国家和其他单位的治理、健康和发展情况。意大利不是一个坐标，阿尔卑斯山的网格单元也不是一个经济体，但当地的环境条件和国家机构以不同的尺度描述了同一个地方。标准处理通过对国家/地区的字段进行平均或栅格化行政统计数据来解决不匹配问题，但这两种转换都是有损的：聚合隐藏了国家内部的梯度，而栅格化则意味着从未观察到的精度。因此，我们将环境-社会耦合模型制定为: their governance, health and development are reported yearly for countries and other units whose borders are political rather than physical. Italy is not a coordinate, and a grid cell in the Alps is not an economy, yet local environmental conditions and national institutions describe the same places at different scales. Standard processing resolves the mismatch by averaging fields over countries or rasterising administrative statistics, but both transformations are lossy: aggregation hides within-country gradients, whereas rasterisation implies precision that was never observed. We therefore formulate coupled environmental–societal modelling as a多几何表示学习multi-geometry representation-learning问题（图problem (Figure1).

在这里我们介绍Here we present特拉诺瓦TerraNova，一个基础模型，针对与人类世相关的 1,024 个变量进行训练，从物理地球系统场到国家社会经济和制度指标，每个变量都有其原生几何形状。专门构建的编码器表示位置、国家/地区、时间和任务，跨模式转换器将它们融合成共享的时空状态，超网络生成每个查询的解码器，其证据头返回具有任意和认知不确定性代理的预测分布。两个对比目标将几何形状和模式结合起来：一个人口加权、时间感知的目标将每个国家与其领土采样的坐标对齐，建立场边界对应关系，而第二个目标将 TerraNova 的位置表示与预训练的地理空间嵌入对齐，导入从视觉信息中学习的地理语义。从概念上讲，TerraNova 将物理场、行政记录和地理空间图像视为对一个潜在现实的不同观察。它学习的共享统计结构支持迁移、空间推理和假设生成，而不是因果或场景分析。我们做出以下贡献：, a foundation model trained on 1,024 variables relevant to the Anthropocene, from physical Earth-system fields to national socioeconomic and institutional indicators, each in its native geometry. Purpose-built encoders represent location, country, time and task, cross-modal transformers fuse them into a shared spatiotemporal state, and a hypernetwork generates a per-query decoder whose evidential head returns a predictive distribution with aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty proxies. Two contrastive objectives couple the geometries and modalities: a population-weighted, time-aware objective aligns each country with coordinates sampled from its territory, establishing the field–boundary correspondence, while a second aligns TerraNova’s location representations with pretrained geospatial embeddings, importing geographic semantics learned from visual information. Conceptually, TerraNova treats physical fields, administrative records and geospatial imagery as different observations of one latent reality. The shared statistical structure it learns supports transfer, spatial inference and hypothesis generation rather than causal or scenario analysis. We make the following contributions:

    据我们所知，TerraNova 是第一个在人类世观测范围内学习一种表示的模型，它在单个主干内的两个几何形状的 1,024 个变量上进行了训练。To our knowledge, TerraNova is the first model to learn one representation across the observational breadth of the Anthropocene, trained on 1,024 variables in two geometries within a single backbone.

  • •

    这种广度是通过几何形状间的耦合而成为可能的。国家/地区对比目标将行政单位与其领土连接起来，而与地理空间嵌入的对齐则锚定了图像衍生语义中的表示。这种耦合支持两种几何体都无法单独表达的功能，从国家/地区检索到国家/地区到网格的降尺度。That breadth is made possible by inter-geometry coupling. A country–location contrastive objective connects administrative units to their territories, while alignment to geospatial embeddings anchors the representation in image-derived semantics. The coupling supports capabilities neither geometry expresses alone, from country–territory retrieval to national-to-gridded downscaling.

  • •

    通过其任务条件解码器读出，共享表示导致大多数静态目标和标签预算上的专门构建的地理空间嵌入，并扩展到它们不表示的轴：不确定性、时间和海洋。Read out through its task-conditioned decoder, the shared representation leads purpose-built geospatial embeddings on most static targets and label budgets, and extends to axes they do not represent: uncertainty, time and the oceans.

  • •

    每个查询都会返回一个预测分布，一个超网络生成的证据头在每个预测旁边提供任意和认知不确定性代理。通过冻结主干上的轻量级适配器，可以以低廉的成本实现对新变量的适应。Every query returns a predictive distribution, a hypernetwork-generated evidential head supplying aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty proxies alongside each prediction. Adaptation to new variables can be reached cheaply through lightweight adapters on a frozen backbone.

预测的不确定性。Predictive uncertainty.

7 讨论Discussion

在两种几何结构中的 1,024 个变量上训练一个模型可以提供网格地球物理模型和国家级面板本身都不具备的表示属性。Training one model on 1,024 variables in two geometries gives the representation properties that neither a gridded geophysical model nor a country-level panel has on its own.

稀疏重建结果是其中最清晰的。一个在整个地球上看到了一千个变量的模型The sparse-reconstruction result is the sharpest of them. A model that has seen a thousand variables over the whole planet知道knows在看到新场的单个测量之前，一个合理的场是什么样子的，因此少量的观察就足以将该场放置在模型已经拥有的结构中。同样的先验使潜在空间变得可读，而不是查找表：地理、生物群落结构和连贯的海洋盆地在位置嵌入中清晰可见，一旦从预测中删除发展指标本身，模型对每个国家的预测就会出现发展梯度。字段、记录和图像衍生的嵌入承认兼容的表示，尽管这里的兼容性是通过显式对齐构建的，而不是仅从数据中产生。这种耦合购买了两种几何都无法单独表达的功能，而跨几何检索和国家到网格缩小尺度是在两个方向上读取的耦合：检索命名领土所属的国家，缩小尺度将国家数量写回到网格上，两者都受到相同的人口加权对齐的支持。这种消融表明，两个对齐目标在做不同的工作，一个构建公共空间，另一个丰富它。what a plausible field looks like before it sees a single measurement of a new one, so a handful of observations is enough to place that field in a structure the model already holds. The same prior is what makes the latent space readable rather than a lookup table: geography, biome structure and coherent ocean basins are legible in the location embedding, and a development gradient emerges from what the model predicts about each country once the development indicators themselves are removed from the projection. Fields, records and image-derived embeddings admit a compatible representation, although here that compatibility is built by explicit alignment rather than emerging from data alone. The coupling buys capabilities that neither geometry expresses alone, and cross-geometry retrieval and national-to-gridded downscaling are one coupling read in two directions: retrieval names the country a territory belongs to, downscaling writes a national quantity back onto the grid, both supported by the same population-weighted alignment. The ablation shows that the two alignment objectives do separable work, one building the common space and the other enriching it.

不确定性是一流的输出。宽度响应证据，随着测量网格变薄以及查询超出记录涵盖的年份而变宽，因此报告不确定位置的模型会标记自己的推断，而不是默默地失败。廉价的适应来自于产生基准裕度的相同架构选择：因为解码器是根据任务和时空状态的每个查询生成的，所以在融合内部达到一个新变量，而无需触及任何预训练任务。Uncertainty is a first-class output. The width responds to evidence, widening as a measurement lattice thins and as a query moves beyond the years a record covers, so a model that reports where it is uncertain flags its own extrapolation instead of failing silently. Cheap adaptation follows from the same architectural choice that produces the benchmark margin: because the decoder is generated per query from the task and the spatiotemporal state, a new variable is reached inside that fusion without touching any pretrained task.

局限性。Limitations.

与专用地理空间编码器的比较在容量上不匹配（§The comparison against specialised geospatial encoders is not capacity-matched (§6.1）。领先优势是由任务条件解码器及其适应的融合提供的，而不是通过冻结嵌入提供的，并且在最小的标签预算下，最强的基线仍然领先。缩小规模是耦合表示的一种能力，而不是经过验证的产品，并且如果国家内部的数量几乎没有变化，它就无法恢复任何内容（§). The lead is delivered by the task-conditioned decoder and the fusion it adapts rather than by the frozen embedding, and at the smallest label budgets the strongest baseline is still ahead. Downscaling is a capability of the coupled representation rather than a validated product, and it recovers nothing where a quantity barely varies within countries (§6.5）。社会经济和制度输入是观察性的，并且带有学习表征可以传播的历史和测量偏差；对观察不足的国家的预测具有最大的间隔，应在阅读时附上它们。 TerraNova 是一个表示层，而不是气候损害的因果模型、综合评估模型或基于过程的模拟的替代品。它无法识别因果效应，模拟气候社会反馈或项目政策情景，并且解读其预测，就好像它确实是一种滥用一样。它提供的是一个联合观测特征层，其中可以在一个潜在空间中研究传输、不确定性空间推断、跨域预测和假设生成，以及最终回答气候社会问题的因果和机械工具。). The socioeconomic and institutional inputs are observational and carry historical and measurement bias that a learned representation can propagate; predictions for under-observed countries carry the widest intervals and should be read with them attached. TerraNova is a representation layer, not a causal model of climate damages, an integrated-assessment model, or a replacement for process-based simulation. It does not identify causal effects, simulate climate-society feedbacks or project policy scenarios, and reading its predictions as if it did would be a misuse. What it provides is a joint observational feature layer in which transfer, spatial inference with uncertainty, cross-domain prediction and hypothesis generation can be studied in one latent space, alongside the causal and mechanistic tools that ultimately answer climate-society questions.

展望。Outlook.

该模型将时间视为额外的输入变量，这限制了其学习条件动态的能力，例如不同辐射强迫假设下经济的演化。将 TerraNova 提升为更适合这些动态的表示可以实现高分辨率、不确定性感知的场景生成。除了这里使用的三种模式之外，法学硕士衍生的地点、文化或事件的嵌入可能携带无法直接量化的信息，尽管在这方面应该极其谨慎地考虑偏见、透明度和全球南方的代表性。最后，TerraNova 隐含地假设各国在某种程度上是独立的，因为我们没有明确地模拟双边贸易或移民网络；移民和贸易网络上的图神经网络，可预测次国家范围内的区域经济成果The model treats time as an additional input variable, which limits its ability to learn conditioned dynamics, such as the evolution of an economy under different radiative-forcing assumptions. Lifting TerraNova into a representation better suited to those dynamics could enable high-resolution, uncertainty-aware scenario generation. Beyond the three modalities used here, LLM-derived embeddings of locations, cultures or events could carry information that is not directly quantifiable, although this should be done with the utmost care over bias, transparency, and representation of the Global South. Finally, TerraNova implicitly assumes that countries are somewhat independent, since we do not explicitly model bilateral trade or migration networks; graph neural networks over migration and trade networks, which predict regional economic outcomes at the sub-national scale(Xu et al., 2020)，可以将其扩展到国家之间的级联气候和经济影响。这些扩展与本文阐述的基础相同：使人类世的耦合数据流在一个模型内可学习、可转移和可查询，并且足够便宜，足以将其置于远离训练它的群体的范围内。这就是综合的、跨学科的建模，被称为气候研究人工智能的中心目标, could extend it to cascading climate and economic impacts between countries. These extensions sit on the same foundation this paper sets out: making the coupled data streams of the Anthropocene learnable, transferable and queryable within one model, cheaply enough to put it within reach of groups far from the one that trained it. That is the integrated, cross-disciplinary modelling named a central goal for artificial intelligence in climate research(Ou et al., 2026)：不是单独模型的耦合，而是一种共享表示，其中地方环境结构和国家结果直接相互告知。: not a coupling of separate models, but a shared representation in which local environmental structure and national outcomes inform one another directly.

TerraNova 降低了使用行星尺度模型的成本。新变量在冻结的主干上安装了紧凑的适配器，因此具有区域调查或定制指示器的小组可以在普通硬件上专门表示，而不是依赖超级计算机。这在数据匮乏的环境中最为重要，因为此类模型最难从头开始训练。校准的不确定性是这个故事的另一半：报告不确定性的模型比裸点预测器更难盲目信任。这一论点的局限性在于，廉价的适应分配了行星表征的产品，同时留下了生产产品的手段、语料库、计算和组装两者的劳动力，集中在它们已经存在的地方TerraNova lowers the cost of using a planetary-scale model. A new variable is fitted with a compact adapter on a frozen backbone, so a group with a regional survey or a bespoke indicator can specialise the representation on ordinary hardware instead of depending on a supercomputer. This matters most in the data-poor settings where such models are hardest to train from scratch. Calibrated uncertainties are the other half of that story: a model that reports where it is uncertain is harder to trust blindly than a bare point predictor. The limit of this argument is that cheap adaptation distributes the product of a planetary representation while leaving the means of producing one, the corpus, the compute and the labour that assembles both, concentrated where they already wereMohamed et al. (2020); Crawford (2021).

该模型特有的风险源于其广度。由于 TerraNova 学习的任务范围如此广泛，因此它的表征远远超出了物理领域，涉及到人类发展、治理和制度，并且它可以学习环境条件和国家成果之间的关联，而世界上这些成果的当前分布是由历史造成的：殖民、帝国主义、采掘性经济实践、战争、占领、贸易结构和政策决策。该模型没有明确观察任何历史，也没有直接表示它。因此，它的表示恢复了真正的规律性，其原因超出了其输入，而发展轴是对那个混乱世界的统计描述，而不是对它的解释。阅读博学的环境与发展关联，就好像地理产生的发展会重塑制度和历史进程一样The risks specific to this model follow from its breadth. Because TerraNova learns across such a wide variety of tasks, its representation reaches well beyond physical fields into human development, governance and institutions, and it may learn associations between environmental conditions and national outcomes in a world whose present distribution of those outcomes was produced by history: colonisation, imperialism, extractive economic practices, wars, occupations, trade structure and policy decisions. The model observes none of that history explicitly and does not represent it directly. Its representation therefore recovers real regularities whose causes lie outside its inputs, and the development axis is a statistical description of that confounded world rather than an explanation of it. Reading a learned environment-development association as though geography produced development would recast institutional and historical processesAcemoglu et al. (2001)作为自然决定论：地点之间关系的历史将作为地点本身的属性出现，而产生它的关系在结果中不再可见as natural determinism: a history of relations between places would appear instead as a property of the places themselves, with the relations that produced it no longer visible in the resultWinner (1980)。我们将轴报告为对代表性的洞察，而不是对国家差异原因的发现。. We report the axis as an insight about the representation, not as a finding about why nations differ.

该语料库有自己的观点。国家指标是构建的而不是观察的，是统计能力本身分布不均的产物，而治理和机构质量是其中最难确定的。在对其进行评分之前，必须先定义该结构，该定义包含机构应是什么样子的规范模型，而得出的指数主要由全球北方的机构汇总。正如我们按任务标准化所做的那样，使这些异构记录具有可比性，既方便培训，又是一种实质性操作：不同机构、不同目的、在不同条件下生产的数量被放在一个尺度上，并可用于单一目标The corpus carries a viewpoint of its own. National indicators are constructed rather than observed, the product of statistical capacity that is itself unequally distributed, and governance and institutional quality are the hardest of them to pin down. The construct has to be defined before it can be scored, that definition carries a normative model of what institutions ought to look like, and the resulting indices are assembled largely by institutions of the Global North. Rendering such heterogeneous records commensurable, as our per-task standardisation does, is a convenience for training and also a substantive operation: quantities produced under different conditions, by different institutions, for different purposes, are placed on one scale and made available to a single objectiveEspeland and Stevens (1998)。由于历史原因，覆盖范围是不平等的，因此模型的无知性不是随机分布的：在最有可能寻求其预测的地方，它的无知性最大。这种谨慎态度贯穿于模型涵盖的每一个社会变量。. Coverage is unequal for historical reasons, so the model’s ignorance is not randomly distributed: it is greatest where its predictions are most likely to be sought. This caution extends across every societal variable the model spans.

同样的谨慎也适用于模型的输出。缩小比例的字段是具有测量视觉权威的预测，手稿中的警告不会随光栅一起传播。只有在国家内部数量完全不同的情况下，缩减规模才能恢复结构，否则就会悄然失败，为无法解决的目标绘制一张看似合理的地图。在观察最稀疏的地方，预测受到的约束最小，这也是预测最有可能被视为唯一可用估计的地方。这种表面之所以有吸引力，正是因为它们使人们能够以从未测量过的分辨率进行管理The same caution should apply to the model’s outputs. A downscaled field is a prediction carrying the visual authority of a measurement, and a caveat in a manuscript does not travel with a raster. Downscaling recovers structure only to the extent that a quantity varies within countries at all, and it fails quietly otherwise, painting a plausible map for a target it cannot resolve. Predictions are least constrained where observations are sparsest, which is also where they are most likely to be taken as the only estimate available. Such surfaces are attractive precisely because they render populations legible to administration at a resolution at which they were never measuredScott (1998)，并且易读性本身并不是目的，而是分配的输入：气候融资和损失和损害资格、人道主义目标、地方资源分配。这种不对称性就是为什么每个单元的不确定性是这里预测的一部分而不是诊断的一部分，也是为什么该模型的网格化社会经济输出不应直接用于关于它们所描述的地方的分配、目标或资格决策。如果考虑此类用途，我们会建议领域专业知识并与政策制定者和受影响社区进行共同设计，同时指出参与不是设计修复，如果是短期或榨取性的，就会成为参与清洗, and that legibility is not an end in itself but an input to distribution: climate finance and loss-and-damage eligibility, humanitarian targeting, subnational resource allocation. That asymmetry is why per-cell uncertainty is part of the prediction here rather than a diagnostic beside it, and why gridded socioeconomic outputs from this model should not be used directly for allocation, targeting or eligibility decisions about the places they describe. Where such uses are contemplated we advise domain expertise and co-design with policy-makers and affected communities, while noting that participation is not a design fix and becomes participation-washing where it is short-term or extractiveSloane et al. (2022)。廉价的适应也可能存在问题。骨干网接受任何新任务，因此让公共卫生组织附加区域指标的机制也允许任何人附加我们既没有预期也没有评估的目标，继承表示的偏见而不继承其评估。接受后，我们将在许可下发布模型权重和配置、评估代码和教程。. Cheap adaptation can also be problematic. The backbone accepts any new task, so the mechanism that lets, for instance, a public-health group attach a regional indicator also lets anyone attach a target we neither anticipated nor evaluated, inheriting the representation’s biases without inheriting its evaluation. Upon acceptance, we will release the model weights and configuration, evaluation code and tutorials under permissive licenses.
