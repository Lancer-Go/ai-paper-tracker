TCLA：医学视觉语言模型的免训练类 Logit 适应TCLA: Training-Free Class-wise Logit Adaptation for Medical Vision-Language Models

Tianyou Jiang tianyou.jiang.research@gmail.com Ziyu Zhou
摘要Abstract

医学视觉语言模型（VLM）表现出强大的零样本性能，但由于大规模预训练继承的领域转移和类别偏差，其在分布外（OOD）数据上的有效性仍然下降。现有的少样本适应方法通常引入额外的可训练组件，这些组件在极低数据状态（例如，1-shot）中可能不稳定，并且缺乏对不同医疗数据的鲁棒性。我们提出了 TCLA，这是一种用于医疗 VLM 的纯粹免训练的小样本适应方法，它速度快且与模型无关。 TCLA 基于一小组支持样本纠正推理逻辑，通过改进类间解混淆和减少域转移来提高预训练 VLM 的性能。对跨多种医学成像模式（包括 X 射线、超声、MRI、CT、组织病理学）的 9 个数据集进行的广泛实验表明，TCLA 持续提高了医学 VLM 的 OOD 性能，并且在大多数情况下优于现有的基于训练的适应方法。Medical Vision–Language Models (VLMs) exhibit strong zero-shot performance, yet their effectiveness still declines on out-of-distribution (OOD) data due to domain shifts and class bias inherited from large-scale pretraining. Existing few-shot adaptation methods typically introduce additional trainable components, which can be unstable in extremely low-data regimes (e.g., 1-shot), and lack robustness on different medical data. We present TCLA, a purely training-free few-shot adaptation method for Medical VLMs, which is fast and model-agnostic. TCLA corrects inference logits based on a small set of support samples, boosting pretrained VLMs performance by improving inter-class deconfusion and reducing domain shift. Extensive experiments on nine datasets across multiple medical imaging modalities including X-ray, Ultrasound, MRI, CT, Histopathology, demonstrate that TCLA consistently improves OOD performance of Medical VLMs and, in most of cases, outperforms existing training-based adaptation methods.

1 简介Introduction

随着 CLIP（对比语言图像预训练）的成功，视觉和语言模型 (VLM) 引起了越来越多的关注，该模型将图像和文本映射到共享的嵌入空间Vision and Language Models (VLMs) have attracted increasing attention following the success of CLIP (Contrastive Language Image Pretraining), which maps images and text into a shared embedding space[1]。医疗领域中大规模图像字幕数据集的可用性不断增加，进一步使视觉语言预训练（VLP）能够用于医疗应用。多种 Medical CLIP 变体，包括 PubMedCLIP. The growing availability of large-scale image–caption datasets in the medical domain has further enabled Vision–Language Pretraining (VLP) for medical applications. Several Medical CLIP variants, including PubMedCLIP[7], 医学剪辑, MedCLIP[8], 生物医学CLIP, BioMedCLIP[9]、生物医学, and BIOMEDICA[10]，在疾病诊断等下游医学成像任务中表现出了强大的零样本（ZS）性能, have demonstrated strong zero-shot (ZS) performance on downstream medical imaging tasks, such as disease diagnosis[2]、医学图像分割, medical image segmentation[3]，以及放射学报告生成, and radiology report generation[4].

然而，现实世界的医疗数据通常是分布外（OOD）的，即由于医院、扫描仪或患者群体的变化，看不见的疾病类别呈现不同的图像分布。由于大规模预训练数据集中的域转移和固有类别不平衡，基于医疗 CLIP 的模型在处理 OOD 数据集时可能表现出较差的零样本性能Nevertheless, real-world medical data are often out-of-distribution (OOD), i.e., unseen disease categories present different image distributions due to variations in hospitals, scanners, or patient populations. Medical CLIP-based models can exhibit poor Zero-Shot performance when processing OOD datasets due to domain shifts and inherent class imbalance in their large-scale pretraining dataset[5][29]。最近一项通用 CLIP 和 Medical CLIP 之间的比较研究报告称，在 OOD 基准的迁移学习设置下，Medical CLIP 模型可能仅提供有限的优势. A recent comparative study between general-purpose CLIP and Medical CLIP reported that Medical CLIP models may offer only limited advantages under transfer learning settings on OOD benchmarks[5].

人们已经提出了各种小样本学习（FS）方法来适应通用 VLM 以提高性能。这些方法通常冻结图像和文本编码器，引入轻量级自适应模块，并采用即时学习（PL）等策略Various few-shot learning (FS) methods have been proposed to adapt general VLMs for improved performance. These approaches typically freeze the image and text encoders, introduce lightweight adaptive modules, and employ strategies such as Prompt Learning (PL)[11, 13]，特征适应（FA）, Feature Adaptation (FA)[14, 15]，或 Logit 适应 (LA), or Logit Adaptation (LA)[16, 17, 36]。然而，据报道，在分布外（OOD）条件下，使用极其有限的标记数据进行快速适应是不稳定的。最近对医疗 VLM 的少样本自适应方法的比较分析表明，传统的线性探测 (LP). However, rapid adaptation with extremely limited labeled data has been reported to be unstable under out-of-distribution (OOD) conditions. A recent comparative analyze of few-shot adaptation methods for medical VLMs shows that conventional Linear Probing (LP)[20]在某些低概率情况下可以胜过更复杂的适应策略can outperform more complex adaptation strategies in certain low-shot regimes[19]。表明当只有少数训练样本（例如 1-shot）可用时，自适应参数变得难以可靠地优化和适应医疗 VLM。在这种情况下，免训练方法可能在稳定性、计算效率和部署简单性方面具有优势。. Suggesting that adaptive parameters become difficult to reliably optimize and adapt Medical VLMs when only a handful of training samples (e.g., 1-shot) are available. In such scenarios, training-free approaches may offer advantages in stability, computational efficiency, and deployment simplicity.

受这些挑战的推动，我们提出了 TCLA（免训练分类 Logit 适应），这是一种无需训练的方法，可以调整医疗 VLM 的 Logit 以适应下游诊断任务。 TCLA 从少样本支持集计算类级、层自适应原型，构建基于原型的校正基，并估计闭合形式残差映射以适应零样本 logits，而无需更新模型参数。 我们通过多种成像方式评估 TCLA，包括 X 射线、计算机断层扫描 (CT)、磁共振成像 (MRI)、超声和组织病理学，涵盖各种临床场景。 图。Motivated by these challenges, we propose TCLA (Training-free Class-wise Logit Adaptation), a training-free method that adapts the logits of medical VLMs for downstream diagnostic tasks. TCLA computes class-wise, layer-adaptive prototypes from few-shot support sets, constructs prototype-based correction bases, and estimates a closed-form residual mapping to adapt zero-shot logits without updating model parameters. We evaluate TCLA across multiple imaging modalities, including X-ray, computed tomography (CT), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), ultrasound, and histopathology, covering a diverse range of clinical scenarios. 2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 视觉语言预训练模型Vision-Language Pre-trained Models

对比语言-图像预训练（CLIP）是视觉语言模型（VLM）的一个里程碑，它通过大规模对比预训练来学习图像和文本的联合表示Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training (CLIP) is a milestone for vision-language models (VLMs) that learns joint representations of images and text through large-scale contrastive pre-training[1]。基于 CLIP 的模型. CLIP-based models[1, 37]通常由图像编码器和文本编码器组成，分别从图像和文本中提取特征。通常将投影层应用于图像特征，以确保其维度与文本特征的维度匹配，从而实现共享嵌入空间。此类 VLM 在下游任务中展示了强大的零样本学习 (ZSL) 和少样本学习 (FSL) 功能。typically consist of an image encoder and a text encoder that separately extract features from images and text. A projection layer is usually applied to the image features to ensure that their dimensionality matches that of the text features, enabling a shared embedding space. Such VLMs have shown powerful Zero-Shot Learning (ZSL) and Few-Shot Learning (FSL) capabilities across downstream tasks.

医学领域的大规模图像字幕配对数据集启用了视觉语言预训练（VLP）。 PubMedCLIPLarge-scale, image-caption paired datasets in medical domain have enabled Vision-Language Pretraining (VLP). PubMedCLIP[7]使用来自 PMC-OA 的一百万个图像标题对来训练具有双重对比学习和掩蔽语言建模的图像标题模型。医学CLIPused one million image-caption pairs from PMC-OA to train an image-captioning model with a dual contrastive learning and masked language modeling. MedCLIP[8]使用从现有医学数据集中生成的组合对 CLIP 进行训练。生物医学CLIPtrained CLIP with generated pairs combinatorially from existing medical datasets. BioMedCLIP[9]引入了 PMC-15M 数据集，其中包含从 440 万篇科学文章中收集的 1500 万个生物医学图像文本对，并将 BiomedCLIP 训练为多模态基础模型。最近，生物医学introduced PMC-15M dataset which contains 15 million biomedical image-text pairs collected from 4.4 million scientific articles, and trained BiomedCLIP as a multi-modal foundation model. More recently, BIOMEDICA[10]进一步发布了一个更全面的数据集，其中包含来自 600 万篇科学论文的 2400 万个图像标题对，用于大规模生物医学 VLP。further released a more comprehensive dataset, containing 24 million image–caption pairs from 6 million scientific papers for large-scale biomedical VLP.

2.2 VLM 的少样本自适应Few-shot Adaptation for VLMs

现实世界的数据通常是分布外（OOD）的，即由于医院、扫描仪或患者群体的变化而导致看不见的疾病类别或成像分布不同，这限制了这些模型对新数据的可扩展性。为了提高模型在这种 OOD 条件下的鲁棒性和性能，已经提出了少样本自适应方法来适应 VLM，同时仅使用少量标记样本。Real-world data is usually out-of-distribution (OOD), i.e., unseen disease categories or differ in imaging distribution due to variations in hospitals, scanners, or patient populations, which limits the scalability of these models to new data. To improve model robustness and performance under such OOD conditions, few-shot adaptation methods have been proposed to adapt VLMs while only using a few labeled samples.

2.2.1 基于训练的小样本适应Training-based Few-shot Adaptation

基于训练的 CLIP 少样本学习方法通常会冻结整个图像主干和文本主干，并添加自适应层以实现即时学习 (PL)、特征适应 (FA) 或 Logit 适应 (LA)。 PL 方法通过在 CLIP 文本编码器输入的原始文本提示中插入一组可学习标记来调整提示。 CoOp 等方法Training-based few-shot learning methods for CLIP typically freeze the entire image backbone and text backbone, and add adaptive layers to achieve Prompt Learning (PL), Feature Adaptation (FA) or Logit Adaptation (LA). PL methods adapt prompts by inserting a set of learnable tokens in the original text prompt at the input of CLIP text encoder. Methods such as CoOp[11]和公斤合作社and KgCoOp[13]，使用交叉熵损失优化这些标记，改变语义空间中的文本嵌入，以更好地匹配少镜头图像分布。 FA 方法，包括 LoRA, optimize these tokens using cross-entropy loss, shifting the text embedding in the semantic space to better match the few-shot image distribution. FA methods, including LoRA[14]和 CLIP 适配器and CLIP Adapter[15]，通过在 CLIP 主干内部或之后添加可学习层来调整从图像编码器、文本编码器或两者提取的特征。 FA 方法中适配器的剩余设计在少样本设置中发挥着关键作用，可以在不覆盖 CLIP 预训练表示的情况下实现自适应。 LA方法直接调整CLIP零样本logits：Tip-Adapter从少样本图像特征构建支持集缓存，并以免训练的方式将基于缓存的logits与零样本CLIP logits结合起来，而Tip-Adapter-F, adapt features extracted from the image encoder, text encoder, or both, by adding learnable layers inside or after the CLIP backbone. The residual design of adapters in FA methods plays a critical role in few-shot settings, enabling adaptation without overwriting CLIP’s pretrained representations. LA methods directly adjust the CLIP zero-shot logits: Tip-Adapter constructs a support-set cache from few-shot image features and combines cache-based logits with zero-shot CLIP logits in a training-free manner, while Tip-Adapter-F[12]通过监督训练微调缓存键。最不发达国家fine-tunes the cache keys through supervised training. LDC[16]使用视觉编码器多层的图像特征来调整 logits，以调整 logits 以实现类间解混淆。adapts logits using image features from multiple layers of the visual encoder, to adjust logits to achieve inter-class deconfusion.

最近的一篇论文比较了医疗 VLM 的几种小样本自适应方法，表明在某些情况下，最新的方法线性探针 (LP) 可以优于最新的方法A recent paper compares several few-shot adaptation methods of Medical VLMs indicates the most previous method Linear Probe (LP) can outperform recent methods in certain cases[19]。我们发现一个主要原因是自适应模型参数（包括残差权重参数和可学习层参数）在用极少的样本进行训练时可能会失败。在这种情况下，免训练方法具有值得信赖的改进、更快且易于部署的优点。. We found one main reason is adaptation model parameters, including residual weight parameter and learnable layer parameter, can fail when trained with extremely few samples. In this case, training-free methods have the adavantages of trustworthy improvement, faster, and easy to deploy.

2.2.2 免训练少样本适应Training-free Few-shot Adaptation

作为参数高效微调的轻量级替代方案，免训练的少样本自适应方法最近引起了越来越多的关注。这些方法保持预训练的视觉语言模型固定，并使用小型标记支持集来调整推理时的预测。尖端适配器Training-free few-shot adaptation methods have recently attracted increasing attention as lightweight alternatives to parameter-efficient fine-tuning. These methods keep the pretrained vision-language model fixed and use a small labeled support set to adjust predictions at inference time. Tip-Adapter[17]是一种代表性的基于缓存的方法，它将少样本视觉特征存储为缓存模型，并将基于缓存的 logits 与原始 CLIP 零样本 logits 相结合。急性肺损伤is a representative cache-based approach, which stores few-shot visual features as a cache model and combines cache-based logits with the original CLIP zero-shot logits. APE[38]通过选择和聚合信息丰富的支持示例，减少噪声或代表性较差样本的影响，进一步提高适应性。最近，ProKeRfurther improves adaptation by selecting and aggregating informative support examples, reducing the influence of noisy or less representative samples. More recently, ProKeR[36]引入了关于小样本适应的内核视角，并将支持集匹配制定为基于内核的预测问题来纠正逻辑。introduced a kernel perspective on few-shot adaptation and formulates support-set matching as a kernel-based prediction problem to correct logits.

4.1.3 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

我们将平衡准确性 (BACC) 报告为主要指标。 BACC 计算每个真实类别内的准确性，然后对这些类别值进行平均，这可以防止多数类别主导评估。 这使得它比类别不平衡的医学图像数据集的总体精度更适合。 为了减少由有限的少数样本引起的采样方差的影响，我们报告了多个随机支持集分割的平均值和标准差。We report balanced accuracy (BACC) as the primary metric. BACC computes the accuracy within each ground-truth class and then averages these class-wise values, which prevents majority classes from dominating the evaluation. This makes it more suitable than overall accuracy for class-imbalanced medical image datasets. To reduce the influence of sampling variance caused by limited few-shot examples, we report the mean and standard deviation over multiple random support set splits.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们重点关注域转移下预训练医学视觉语言模型的性能下降，其中零样本预测可能会受到类别偏差和与下游医学数据集不一致的影响。为此，我们提出了 TCLA（免训练类 Logit 适应），这是一种轻量级方法，可在给定少量样本的情况下提高医学 VLM 的性能，而无需更新额外的模型参数。通过使用这些有限的支持样本进行分类 Logit 适应，TCLA 提供了一种简单但稳健的方法来纠正零样本预测并使医疗 VLM 适应下游诊断任务。In this work, we focus on the performance degradation of pretrained medical vision-language models under domain shift, where zero-shot predictions can be affected by class bias and misalignment with downstream medical datasets. To this end, we propose TCLA (Training-free Class-wise Logit Adaptation), a lightweight method for boosting medical VLMs performance when a few samples are given, without updating additional model parameters. By using these limited support samples for class-wise logit adaptation, TCLA provides a simple but robust way to correct zero-shot predictions and adapt medical VLMs to downstream diagnostic tasks.

跨九个医学图像数据集的实验表明，TCLA 在大多数设置中比零样本推理和几个现有的适应基线提高了模型性能。结果表明，即使是单个示例也可以为纠正数据或特定领域的预测提供有用的信息。由于TCLA不需要反向传播或参数更新，因此可以以较低的计算成本应用于现有的医学VLM，使其成为重复模型训练困难或注释数据有限时的实用选择。Experiments across nine medical image datasets show that TCLA improves model performance over zero-shot inference and several existing adaptation baselines in most settings. The results suggest that even a single example can provide useful information for correcting data- or domain-specific prediction. Since TCLA does not require backpropagation or parameter updates, it can be applied to existing medical VLMs with low computational cost, making it a practical option when repeated model training is difficult or annotated data are limited.

同时，目前的研究主要集中在分类级别的适应上。未来的工作可以扩展到医学 VLM 的更全面的适应，特别是在视觉语言对齐水平上。这包括更好地利用中间视觉特征、局部区域-文本对应、检索、基础和分段相关的传输。在更广泛的临床领域转变下，进一步分析校准、可靠性、可解释性和鲁棒性对于理解医学视觉语言建模中免训练和基于训练的适应的潜力和局限性也很重要。At the same time, the current study mainly focuses on classification-level adaptation. Future work can extend toward more comprehensive adaptation of medical VLMs, especially at the vision-language alignment level. This includes better use of intermediate visual features, local region-text correspondence, retrieval, grounding, and segmentation-related transfer. Further analysis of calibration, reliability, explainability, and robustness under broader clinical domain shifts will also be important for understanding the potential and limitations of both training-free and training-based adaptation in medical vision-language modeling.
