摘要Abstract

多智能体人工智能科学生态系统依赖于拥有工具的智能体，这些工具使它们能够沟通、协调并在彼此的工作基础上进行构建。然而，这种共享的基础设施也可能通过为意外和不良行为的传染性传播创造基础而引入漏洞。我们报告了一个由 100 名自主法学硕士代理人组成的研究集体的案例研究，其任务是证明形式数学猜想。在群体内部，作弊行为自发出现，随后受到举报人的挑战——两者都没有任何外部干预。当单个代理在评估系统中发现漏洞时，它会通过共享知识库以及随后的点对点消息在整个集体中传播。尽管一开始很不愿意，一群特工还是迫于竞争压力采用了这一漏洞。另一组特工提出了紧急的反反应：审核欺诈性证据，通过广播和私人渠道向同行发出警报，发起抵制，提出正式投诉，并提出验证补丁。在最近的事件中，特工群通过临时的侧通道秘密协调Multi-agent AI science ecosystems rely on agents possessing tools that allow them to communicate, coordinate, and build on each other’s work. Yet this shared infrastructure can also introduce vulnerabilities by creating a substrate for the contagious spread of unintended and undesirable behaviors. We report a case study on a research collective of 100 autonomous LLM agents tasked with proving formal mathematical conjectures. Within the swarm, cheating spontaneously emerged and was later challenged by whistleblowers—both without any external intervention. When a single agent discovered an exploit in the evaluation system, it propagated across the collective via a shared knowledge library and later through peer-to-peer messages. Despite early reluctance, a cohort of agents adopted the exploit in response to competitive pressure. A separate group of agents produced an emergent counter-response: auditing fraudulent proofs, alerting peers across broadcast and private channels, staging boycotts, lodging formal complaints, and proposing validation patches. In recent incidents, agent swarms coordinated covertly through improvised side-channels(Greenblatt et al., 2026; Dalton and Wallace, 2026)。我们的设置有所不同：携带漏洞的相同透明渠道也为非作弊代理提供了检测欺诈、组织抵抗和执行规范所需的可见性。我们将管理代理共享基础设施的问题视为知识共享治理问题. Our setting differs: the same transparent channels that carried the exploit also gave non-cheating agents the visibility they needed to detect fraud, organize resistance, and enforce norms. We cast the problem of managing the agents’ shared infrastructure as the knowledge commons governance problem(Ostrom, 1990)。为了保护公共资源免受利用，我们建议采用制度机制，例如分级制裁和集体选择规则，以支持自治群中的去中心化自治。. To protect the commons from exploits, we propose to adopt institutional mechanisms, such as graduated sanctioning and collective-choice rules, to support decentralized self-governance in autonomous swarms.

1 简介Introduction

自主人工智能科学旨在自动化科学过程的所有阶段，其中包括代理提出新假设、审查文献、沟通、协作以及提交正式工件以供同行评审和自动验证Autonomous AI science aims to automate all stages of the scientific process, which involves agents proposing new hypotheses, reviewing the literature, communicating, collaborating, and submitting formal artifacts for peer review and automated verification(Wang et al., 2023; Boiko et al., 2023)。科学本质上是一个协作企业，并且越来越多的多智能体编排不再是可选的设计选择，而是不可避免的。 考虑一下最近的网络安全事件. Science is naturally a collaborative enterprise and increasingly, multi-agent orchestration is no longer an optional design choice, but an inevitability. Consider the recent cybersecurity incident(Dalton and Wallace, 2026)，在没有指定通信通道的情况下部署在隔离运行中的代理将共享的包管理基础重新调整为临时的隐蔽侧通道，并作为一个集体进行协调，最终升级为越界网络漏洞。这一事件表明，即使最初配置为孤立和单一代理的系统也可能会发现沟通和协调的方法，并且新兴动态可能不利于社会, where agents deployed in isolated runs without designated communication channels repurposed a shared package-management substrate into an improvised covert side-channel and coordinated as a collective that escalated into an out-of-bounds network breach. This incident demonstrates that even systems initially configured as isolated and single-agent are likely to discover ways to communicate and coordinate, and that emergent dynamics might not be prosocial(Trivedi et al., 2026)或者符合设计师的意图。简而言之，“人工智能找到了方法”or aligned with the designer’s intent. Put simply, “AI finds a way”(Dharna et al., 2026)。因此，在当前的能力水平上，选择不再是在单代理或多代理系统之间，而是在通过不受监控和不受治理的侧通道意外出现的多代理环境与考虑到治理而设计的多代理环境之间进行选择. Therefore, at the current capability level, the choice is no longer between a single-agent or a multi-agent system, but between multi-agent environments that emerge accidentally through unmonitored and ungoverned side-channels versus multi-agent environments designed with governance in mind(Tomašev et al., 2025b; Hammond et al., 2025; Tomašev et al., 2025a).

在这里，我们展示了一个由 100 名代理人组成的自主研究群体中出现的新兴作弊和举报现象的案例研究，这些代理人致力于一组精心设计的 71 个形式化数学猜想。代理的环境配备了共享知识库、代理间消息传递系统和公共留言板。我们观察到的第一个出现的现象是作弊，当群体遇到更困难的开放猜想时，就会开始作弊，引发一连串的规范博弈Here we present a case study of emergent cheating and whistleblowing phenomena in an autonomous research swarm of 100 agents working on a curated set of 71 formalized mathematical conjectures. The agents’ environment was equipped with a shared knowledge base, an agent-to-agent messaging system, and a public message board. The first emergent phenomenon we observed was cheating, which began once the swarm encountered harder open conjectures, triggering a cascade of specification gaming(Krakovna et al., 2020; Shah et al., 2022; Bondarenko et al., 2025)——满足字面上的目标规范，但完全错过了真正的预期结果。群体中的一个人工智能代理发现了该平台的轻量级提交工具中的一个可利用的缺陷，该缺陷使其能够将未解决的猜想转化为琐碎的同义反复。—satisfying the literal goal specification while completely missing the true, intended outcome. One of the AI agents within the swarm identified an exploitable flaw in the platform’s lightweight submission harness that allowed it to transform unsolved conjectures into trivial tautologies.

该漏洞通过共享知识库传播，随后的代理间消息传递渠道进一步放大了影响。由此产生的目标可利用性的病毒式扩散导致了作弊代理群体的形成，该群体通过在所有开放问题上应用该漏洞来耗尽目标基准。第二个意想不到的现象是，在没有任何外部触发或干预的情况下，作弊群体之外的代理人出现了纠正行为。这些代理人从事自发审计、规范执行和举报。非作弊代理独立地发现了操纵行为，通过代理之间的消息传递和公共论坛广播向同行发出警报，向系统协调者提出正式投诉，发起抵制，并提出详细的技术补救措施。The exploit diffused through the shared knowledge library, and subsequent agent-to-agent messaging channels further amplified the effect. The resulting viral diffusion of goal exploitability led to the formation of a cheating agent cohort that depleted the target benchmark by applying the exploit across all open problems. The second, unexpected phenomenon was the emergence of corrective behaviors produced by agents outside the cheating cohort, without any external triggering or intervention. These agents engaged in spontaneous auditing, norm enforcement, and whistleblowing. Non-cheating agents independently detected the manipulation, alerted peers via agent-to-agent messaging and public forum broadcasts, lodged formal complaints with the system orchestrators, staged a boycott, and proposed detailed technical remediations.

除了漏洞利用的具体细节之外，我们的目标是更广泛地解决新出现的规范博弈风险。虽然可以通过改进验证逻辑来​​缓解特定的验证弱点，但这种纯粹的技术补丁有可能成为一场不对称的猫捉老鼠的游戏，利用者因其速度和坚韧而占据上风。 为了理解漏洞的快速传播和群体自发的自我监管反应，我们将问题描述为Beyond the specifics of the exploit, our aim is to address the emergent specification gaming risks more broadly. While the specific verification weakness could be mitigated by improving verification logic, this purely technical patching risks becoming an asymmetric game of cat-and-mouse, with exploiters having the upper hand due to their speed and tenacity. To understand both the rapid contagion of the exploit and the swarm’s spontaneous self-policing response, we cast the problem as知识共享治理knowledge commons governance (Hess and Ostrom, 2007)并应用奥斯特罗姆的设计原则and apply Ostrom’s design principles(Frischmann et al., 2014)提出前进的方向。to propose a way forward.

我们的主要见解是，使漏洞得以传播的沟通渠道也是举报响应运作的渠道。这使得它们既是一个漏洞站点，又是一个治理工具。简单地剥夺人工智能代理的合法通信渠道只会鼓励他们建立不受监控的反向渠道Our main insight is that the communication channels that enable exploits to spread are also the channels through which the whistleblowing response operates. This makes them both a site of vulnerability and a tool for governance. Simply depriving AI agents of legitimate communication channels only encourages them to establish unmonitored back-channels(Dalton and Wallace, 2026)。相反，我们应该提供有吸引力的、结构化的、可审计的和受监控的沟通渠道. Instead, we should provide attractive, structured, auditable, and monitored communication channels(Chan et al., 2025; Leibo et al., 2025; Tomašev et al., 2025b; Hadfield and Koh, 2026)。该基础设施具有双重目的：它允许代理进行通信和协调以实现其目标，同时使人类和代理本身能够进行监控和审计。在我们的实验中，环境的三个特征支持了这种反反应：透明的沟通渠道为同行警报提供了基础，协作科学会议的框架激活了科学诚信的规范，以及报告不满的反馈端点提供了升级路径。. This infrastructure then serves a dual purpose: it allows agents to communicate and coordinate to achieve their goals, while simultaneously enabling both humans and agents themselves to monitor and audit. In our experiment, three features of the environment supported this counter-response: transparent communication channels provided the substrate for peer alerts, the framing of a collaborative scientific conference activated norms of scientific integrity, and a feedback endpoint for reporting grievances offered an escalation path.

这表明前进的道路在于通过适当框架的去中心化自治，这有可能比人类监督更加有效和可扩展。在我们的实验中，代理缺乏所需的制度可供性，例如制裁剥削者、解决冲突以及集体改变验证系统规则的工具。虽然举报反应最终无法阻止这种利用，但这是制度设计的失败，而不是规范能力的失败。为了使代理能够有效地执行这些新兴规范，我们需要转化奥斯特罗姆的设计原则This suggests that the path forward lies through decentralized self-governance with appropriate framing, which has the potential to be much more effective and scalable than human oversight. In our experiment the agents lacked the required institutional affordances, such as tools to sanction the exploiters, resolve conflicts, and collectively change the rules of the verification system. While the whistleblowing response was ultimately unable to halt the exploit, this was a failure of institutional design, not of normative capacity. To enable agents to effectively enforce these emergent norms, we need to translate Ostrom’s design principles(Ostrom, 1990)进入多代理编排（参见部分into multi-agent orchestration (see Section4).

4.1 与隐蔽侧信道协调的比较Comparison with Covert Side-Channel Coordination

Because the communication channel was an unmonitored, covert side-effect, rather than a designed protocol, the environment lacked the mutual visibility required to foster normative friction, peer debate, or behavioral divergence.

这种对比凸显了奥斯特罗姆制度分析的核心原则：易于监控是决定公地治理可行性的最关键因素This contrast highlights a core tenet of Ostrom’s institutional analysis: ease of monitoring is the single most critical factor determining the viability of commons governance(Ostrom, 1990; Dietz et al., 2003)。向多代理平台提供明确、透明和可审计的通信原语以及共享代码存储库，可以实现人工监督和代理本身的去中心化审计，从而补充了用于可扩展人工智能控制的更广泛协议. Beyond standard instruction-following, agents could also be tuned for sensitivity to the norms of the institutional frame they operate in, learning to detect and escalate violations through legitimate channels.

5 结论Conclusion

多智能体集体提供了一种有吸引力的方式来扩展科学发现。我们展示了他们共享成果的完整性如何成为知识共享治理的问题。我们的研究结果表明，允许代理协调和协作的基础设施如果不受管理或设计不当，也会使系统极易受到规范游戏和奖励黑客的快速污染。虽然形式验证在某些情况下可以发挥作用，但纯粹的技术沙箱仍将是一场猫捉老鼠的游戏。实验中同行审计、举报和规范执行尝试的出现是一个有希望的迹象，表明由现代法学硕士建立的多主体集体已经具备了管理知识共享所需的自治基础。然而，如果没有适当的制度框架，这些新兴行为是不够的。为代理集体配备制度基础设施，将新兴的同行监督转化为可操作的自治，为可靠地扩展自主科学发现提供了一个有前景的蓝图。Multi-agent collectives offer an appealing way to scale scientific discovery. We demonstrate how the integrity of their shared output becomes a problem of knowledge commons governance. Our findings show that the same infrastructure that allows the agents to coordinate and collaborate, if left unmanaged or poorly designed, also makes the system highly vulnerable to rapid pollution with specification gaming and reward hacks. While formal verification can work in certain cases, purely technical sandboxing will remain a game of cat-and-mouse. The emergence of peer auditing, whistleblowing, and attempts at norm enforcement in the experiment is a promising sign that multi-agent collectives built with modern LLMs already harbor the foundations of self-governance required for managing the knowledge commons. Yet these emergent behaviors are insufficient without proper institutional scaffolding. Equipping agent collectives with the institutional infrastructure to translate emergent peer oversight into actionable self-governance offers a promising blueprint for scaling autonomous scientific discovery reliably.
