评估跨领域映射对人类和法学硕士创造力的影响Evaluating the Impact of Cross-domain Mappings on Human and LLM Creativity

Qiawen Ella Liu1, Marina Dubova2∗, Henry Conklin1∗, Takumi Harada1,3, & Thomas L. Griffiths1
1Princeton University
2Santa Fe Institute
3Toyota Motor North America, Inc., Plano, Texas
∗Equal contribution
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 是否具有与人类相同的创造力，相同的干预措施能否提高两者的创造力？我们评估了一种很有前途但基本上未经测试的创造力干预措施：迫使创作者从随机的远程源域中进行类比（“跨域映射”）。人类参与者和法学硕士在两个提示下为十种日常产品（例如背包、电视）生成了新颖的特征：（i）跨域映射，这需要从随机分配的来源（例如章鱼、仙人掌、GPS）转换属性，以及（ii）用户需求，这需要针对未满足的用户需求提出创新。我们表明，人类可靠地受益于随机分配的跨域映射，而法学硕士平均比人类产生更多原创想法，并且没有显示出跨域映射的统计显着效果。然而，在这两个系统中，当灵感源在语义上距离目标更远时，跨域映射的影响就会增加。我们的结果强调了远程关联在创意构思中的作用，以及人类和法学硕士对相同创造力干预的反应的系统差异。Are large language models (LLMs) creative in the same way humans are, and can the same interventions increase creativity in both? We evaluate a promising but largely untested intervention for creativity: forcing creators to draw an analogy from a random, remote source domain (“cross-domain mapping”). Human participants and LLMs generated novel features for ten daily products (e.g., backpack, TV) under two prompts: (i) cross-domain mapping, which required translating a property from a randomly assigned source (e.g., octopus, cactus, GPS), and (ii) user-need, which required proposing innovations targeting unmet user needs. We show that humans reliably benefit from randomly assigned cross-domain mappings, while LLMs, on average, generate more original ideas than humans and do not show a statistically significant effect of cross-domain mappings. However, in both systems, the impact of cross-domain mapping increases when the inspiration source becomes more semantically distant from the target. Our results highlight both the role of remote association in creative ideation and systematic differences in how humans and LLMs respond to the same intervention for creativity.

简介Introduction

1941 年，瑞士工程师乔治·德·梅斯特拉尔 (George de Mestral) 徒步旅行回来后，发现他的狗的皮毛上顽固地粘着毛刺。用显微镜仔细检查这些毛刺后，他发现许多小钩子挂在环上——这种结构激发了他发明尼龙搭扣的灵感，这种紧固件现在可以固定从幼儿鞋到宇航服的所有物品。Coming back from a hike in 1941, Swiss engineer George de Mestral noticed burrs stuck stubbornly to his dog’s fur. Upon close examination of these burrs with a microscope, he found numerous tiny hooks catching on loops – a structure that inspired his invention of Velcro, the fastener now securing everything from toddlers’ shoes to spacesuits[31]。类似的跨域传输贯穿整个发明史：早期飞机的灵感来自于鸟翅膀的结构. Similar cross-domain transfers appear throughout the history of invention: Early aircraft were inspired by the structure of bird wings[21]，新生儿培养箱的灵感来自于家禽育雏器的温度调节技术, and neonatal incubators was inspired by the temperature-regulation techniques from poultry brooders[5]。在每种情况下，发明都不是凭空而来，而是来自跨领域映射现有概念。. In each case, invention comes not from nothing but from mapping existing concepts across domains.

从偏远地区汲取灵感可能看起来更像是一种机会游戏，而不是一种激发创造力的系统方法，这取决于特定地点与特定狗的特定徒步旅行的偶然组合。在一般情况下，是什么使跨域映射成功？它能否被设计为对创造力的可靠干预？答案可能很大程度上取决于我们想要谁发挥创造力。众所周知，人类很容易陷入固定状态：一旦以熟悉的方式构建问题，人们就会倾向于在单个领域内重用已知的解决方案，而不是跨领域寻找遥远的替代方案Drawing inspiration from remote domains may appear more like a game of chance than a systematic method for inducing creativity, depending on the serendipitous combination of a specific hike with a specific dog at a specific place. In the general case, what makes a cross-domain mapping successful and can it be engineered as a reliable intervention for creativity? The answer may depend critically on who we want to be creative. Humans are notoriously prone to fixation: once a problem is framed in a familiar way, people tend to reuse known solutions within a single domain rather than scanning for distant alternatives across domains[1, 16, 11]。即使人们试图通过积极考虑替代领域来克服固着，他们仍然会受到知识碎片化的限制. Even when people try to overcome fixation by actively considering alternative domains, they can still be constrained by knowledge fragmentation[29]，其特点是经典的“未知的未知”问题, characterized by the classic ‘unknown unknown’ problem[9]。 相比之下，大型语言模型（LLM）是在大规模的跨学科语料库上进行训练的，这可能使它们能够在没有相同认知瓶颈的情况下生成远程关联。虽然人类创造力研究的一个中心主题是开发可扩展的方法，帮助创新者克服固着和未知的未知，但为人类设计的干预措施是否对具有根本不同的存储、检索和重组知识能力的系统有效，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。. Large language models (LLMs), by contrast, are trained on massive, cross-disciplinary corpora that might allow them to generate remote associations without the same cognitive bottlenecks. While a central theme of human creativity research is developing scalable methods that help innovators overcome both fixation and unknown unknowns, it remains an open question whether interventions designed for humans will prove effective for systems with fundamentally different capacity for storing, retrieving, and recombining knowledge.

在这里，我们测试跨域映射​​作为人类和法学硕士产生想法的实际干预措施。我们通过明确地从遥远的源领域汲取灵感或根据现有用户需求提出渐进式改进来促使人类和法学硕士产生新颖的产品创意。然后，人类评估者从四个维度评估这些想法：原创性（我们的焦点维度）、可行性、实用性和整体投资价值。我们询问跨域映射是否可靠地提高了想法的原创性，其效果在人类和法学硕士之间是否有所不同，以及什么类型的跨域映射在两个系统中都是生成的。Here, we test cross-domain mapping as a practical intervention for idea generation by both humans and LLMs. We prompt humans and LLMs to generate novel product ideas either by explicitly drawing inspiration from a distant source domain or by proposing incremental improvements based on existing user needs. Human raters then assess the ideas along four dimensions: originality (our focal dimension), feasibility, usefulness, and overall investment worthiness. We ask whether cross-domain mapping reliably boosts originality of ideas, whether its effects differ between humans and LLMs, and what kinds of cross-domain mappings are generative in both systems.

我们表明，跨领域映射显着提高了人类而不是法学硕士的想法的原创性，但法学硕士平均比人类产生更多的原创想法。我们还表明，随机的跨域映射并不能保证激发创意；相反，当灵感来自更遥远的领域时，想法就会被认为更具原创性。总的来说，我们的结果表明，增强人类创造力的机制可能在认知架构与我们截然不同的系统中发挥不同的（在我们的例子中，减弱的）作用。We show that cross-domain mapping significantly boosts the originality of ideas for humans and not LLMs, but LLMs produce more original ideas than humans on average. We also show that a random cross-domain mapping is not guaranteed to inspire creative ideas; rather, ideas are seen as more original when the inspiration is drawn from a more distant domain. Overall, our results suggests that mechanisms that enhance human creativity may play a different (in our case, diminished) role in systems whose cognitive architectures differ radically from our own.

背景Background

创造力是连接远程关联的能力Creativity as the ability to bridge remote associations

创造力研究人员很早就认识到，创造性的想法通常是通过将概念联系起来而产生的。Creativity researchers have long recognized that creative ideas often arise by linking concepts that are弱关联weakly associated，在语义空间中遍历更长的距离或重新组合来自不同语义域的元素, traversing longer distances in semantic space or recombining elements drawn from distinct semantic domains[24, 19, 18, 34]。在这些关于创造力的联想描述中，新颖想法的产生取决于语义记忆的组织方式。语义检索并不统一，而是组织成. In these associative accounts of creativity, generating novel ideas depends on how semantic memory is organized. Semantic retrieval is not uniform, but organized into关联层次结构associative hierarchies，这样一来，具有陡峭等级制度的个人会优先考虑占主导地位的传统同事，而具有较平坦等级制度的人更容易与遥远的同事建立联系, such that individuals with steep hierarchies prioritize dominant, conventional associates, whereas those with flatter hierarchies more readily connect distant associates[24]。与这一观点一致的是，高度创造力的个体倾向于更广泛地导航语义空间，检索更远的同事. Consistent with this view, highly creative individuals tend to navigate semantic space more expansively, retrieving more distant associates[2, 12, 17]，跨语义子类别灵活转换, shifting flexibly across semantic subcategories[36]，并在有或没有明确目标的情况下产生更大的联想飞跃（, and producing larger associative leaps both with and without explicit goals ([3, see]).

尽管人们普遍认为创造力可以从桥接远程关联中受益，但系统地诱导这种联系的干预措施仍然相对较少Despite broad agreement that creativity benefits from bridging remote associations, interventions that systematically induce such connections remain relatively rare[22, 35]。虽然某些干预措施可以有效地在某些情况下引发创意. While some interventions are effective at inducing creative ideas in certain contexts[35, e.g.,]，这些通常集中于一些目标和灵感，很少覆盖潜在映射的巨大组合空间。 我们使用跨域映射作为系统干预来“扁平化”关联层次结构，迫使创建者跨越目标域中常见的语义邻域。 由于潜在有用灵感的空间是巨大的，我们通过随机选择源域对来创建一组广泛的跨域映射。在此过程中，我们希望有意重现在发现史上如此重要的机缘巧合, these usually focus on a few targets and inspirations, offering little coverage of the vast combinatorial space of potential mappings. We use cross-domain mapping as a systematic intervention to “flatten” the associative hierarchy, forcing creators to leap beyond a target domain’s well-trodden semantic neighborhood. Since the space of potentially useful inspirations is vast, we create a broad set of cross-domain mappings by randomly selecting pairs of source domains. In doing so we hope to intentionally recreate the kind of serendipity that has been shown to be so important in the history of discovery[30, 15].

类比为跨域转移关系结构的机制Analogy as the mechanism for transferring relational structure across domains结构映射理论Structure-Mapping Theory[10]形式化了跨领域灵感背后的认知机制。类比可以通过调整源和目标之间的关系结构来支持系统推理——导入连贯的关系系统，使目标问题以前被忽视的方面变得可见formalises the cognitive mechanism underlying cross-domain inspiration. An analogy can support systematic inference, by aligning relational structure between a source and a target – importing a coherent system of relations that makes previously overlooked aspects of the target problem visible[10, 14]。特别是，与源结构对齐和投射可以突出目标中隐含的限制（约束），并提出新的干预可能性（可供性）。例如，通过映射. In particular, aligning with and projecting from the source structure can highlight implicit limitations in the target (constraints) as well as suggest new possibilities for intervention (affordances). For example, by mapping the循环系统circulatory system到onto城市交通流量urban traffic flow，规划者可能会注意到需要单向“阀门”来防止回流，或者看到有机会建造许多小型社区路线，将交通输送到小巷深处，就像毛细血管将血液输送到组织中一样。在当前的研究中，我们设计了跨域映射方法，以在人类和法学硕士中明确实例化这种类比结构映射过程（见下文，, a planner might notice the need for one-way “valves” to prevent backflow, or see an opportunity to build lots of small neighborhood routes that deliver traffic deep into side streets, the way capillaries carry blood into tissue. In the current study, we design our cross-domain mapping approach to explicitly instantiate such an analogical structure-mapping process in humans and LLMs (see below,Eliciting Novel Ideas from Humans and LLMs).

法学硕士作为创造性系统，可以大规模产生跨领域关联LLMs as creative systems that can generate cross-domain associations at scale在创意写作、设计和假设生成任务中，法学硕士可靠地产生大量新颖的想法，并且经常模仿或超越人类参与者Across creative writing, design, and hypothesis generation tasks, LLMs reliably produce large numbers of novel ideas and often emulate or outperform human participants[28, 7, 4, 20]。尽管最具创造力的人类在其产生的想法的多样性方面仍然优于当前的模型. While the most creative humans still outperform current models in the diversity of the ideas they produce[33, 26, 20]尽管如此，有证据表明当代法学硕士具有强大的创造力。, evidence nevertheless suggests contemporary LLMs have substantial creative capacity.

经过大规模跨学科语料库的培训，法学硕士可以检测看似不相关领域的结构相似性，并大规模生成跨领域映射——这可能会提高他们的创造力。最近的证据表明，法学硕士可以进行与人类表现相媲美的类比推理Trained on massive, cross-disciplinary corpora, LLMs can detect structural parallels across seemingly unrelated fields and generate cross-domain mappings at scale – something that may drive their creative ability. Recent evidence shows that LLMs can perform analogical reasoning that rivals human performance[8, 32]并灵活地重新组合知识以生成新颖的解决方案and flexibly recombine knowledge to generate novel solutions[25, 13]，也许反映了这样一个事实，即它们较少受到通常限制人类类比推理的认知瓶颈的影响（例如，有限的知识获取、陡峭的联想层次结构和固定）。因此，很自然地会问跨域映射是否可以使人类和法学硕士在相同程度上受益。, perhaps reflecting the fact that they are less affected by the cognitive bottlenecks that often constrain human analogical reasoning (e.g., limited knowledge access, steep associative hierarchies, and fixation). It is thus natural to ask whether cross-domain mapping might benefit humans and LLMs to the same degree.

讨论Discussion

创造力常常与突然和偶然的跨领域灵感联系在一起。推动这项工作的核心问题是这种跨领域映射是否可以大规模诱导，以及跨领域映射对人类和法学硕士的创造性见解是否有不同的影响。我们发现（1）法学硕士产生的想法平均被认为更具原创性，无论是否明确提示跨领域映射； （2）平均而言，跨领域映射提示增加了人类想法的原创性，但没有增加法学硕士想法； （3）在这两个系统中，目标域和灵感域之间的语义距离越大，则预示着更高的原创性。Creativity is often associated with sudden and serendipitous cross-domain inspirations. The central question motivating this work was whether such cross-domain mappings can be induced at scale, and whether cross-domain mappings affect creative insights differently for humans and LLMs. We found (1) LLMs generated ideas that were perceived as more original on average, regardless of whether cross-domain mappings were explicitly prompted; (2) on average, cross-domain mapping prompts increased the perceived originality of human ideas but not LLM ideas; and (3) across both systems, greater semantic distance between target and inspiration domains predicted higher perceived originality.

语义距离作为原创性的驱动力Semantic distance as a driver of originality

我们发现跨领域映射干预增加了人们的原创性，但对法学硕士却没有。一种解释是，如果没有明确邀请跨领域进行映射，人类参与者可能会继续关注产品已经提供的渐进式改进。We found a cross-domain mapping intervention increased originality for people but not for LLMs. One interpretation is that without an explicit invitation to map across domains, human participants may remain anchored to incremental improvements that the product already affords.

我们的结果还表明，目标和灵感之间的语义距离对于人类和法学硕士都很重要。在法学硕士产生的想法中，随着目标域和灵感域之间语义距离的增加，原创性也随之增加。因此，我们将人类与法学硕士对跨领域干预的不对称性解释为更多的是对提示的敏感性差异，而不是跨领域映射在一般情况下是否可以生成的指示。一种可能性是，即使在用户需求框架下，法学硕士也已经利用了广泛的关联，在给出明确的跨域映射时，留下的改进空间较小。另一个相关的可能性是，法学硕士的提示效果取决于距离——只有当目标-源配对非常遥远时（例如，汽车和章鱼、背包和仙人掌），明确的映射提示可能才重要。测试跨领域映射成为法学硕士有效干预的条件需要进行实验，系统地操纵广泛概念空间中的语义距离，并评估此类提示是否以及何时相对于另一个提示框架（例如用户需求）增加原创性。Our results also show that semantic distance between targets and inspirations matters for both humans and LLMs. Within LLM-generated ideas, originality increased as the semantic distance between the target and inspiration domains grew. Thus, we interpret the human–LLM asymmetry to cross-domain intervention as more of a difference in sensitivity to prompting than an indication of whether cross-domain mappings can be generative in the general case. One possibility is that LLMs already draw on broad associations even under a user-need framing, leaving less room for improvement when given an explicit cross-domain mapping. Another related possibility is that prompting effects for LLMs depend on distance – explicit mapping prompts may matter only when the target–source pairing is highly remote (e.g., car and octopus, backpack and cactus). Testing the conditions where cross-domain mapping becomes an effective intervention for LLMs would require experiments that systematically manipulate semantic distance across a wide range of conceptual space and assess whether and when such prompts increase originality relative to another prompt framing such as user need.

原创性、可行性、实用性Originality, feasibility, and usefulness

独创性和可行性之间存在很强的负相关关系，而独创性和实用性之间几乎没有关系。从表面上看，这可能表明将想法推向高度原创性必然会产生不切实际且效用不明确的想法。然而，对可行性和有用性的判断必须相对于当前可想象的问题/解决方案空间来进行There was a strong negative relationship between originality and feasibility, and almost no relationship between originality and usefulness. At face value, this might suggest that pushing ideas toward high originality necessarily produces impractical ideas with unclear utility. However, judgments of feasibility and usefulness are necessarily made relative to a currently imaginable problem/solution space[27]。位于该空间之外的想法可能看起来既不可能又毫无意义，不一定是因为它们违反了物理或市场原理，而是因为实现它们的机制尚无法表征。许多具有历史意义的变革性思想最初都遇到了这个问题。例如，早期对人工智能生成艺术的怀疑常常基于这样的假设：创造性机器需要像人类一样的机器人手臂来操纵画笔. Ideas that sit outside that space may look both impossible and pointless, not necessarily because they violate principles of physics or markets, but because the mechanisms by which they could be realized are not yet representable. Many historically transformative ideas initially suffered from this problem. For example, early skepticism about AI-generated arts often rested on the assumption that a creative machine would entail a human-like robot arms manipulating brushes[23]，而不是对潜在表示进行操作的统计模型——这种实施路径不仅不可能，而且有效, rather than statistical models operating over latent representations—an implementation path that was not merely unlikely, but effectively难以想象的unthinkable当时。一旦出现可行的路径，对可行性的判断就会迅速转变；在采用之前，其有用性必然是推测性的。at the time. Once a workable path appears, judgments of feasibility can shift rapidly; and until adoption, usefulness is necessarily speculative.

局限性和未来方向Limitations and future directions

首先，我们当前的分析侧重于定量结果（例如原创性评级），但人类和法学硕士在绘制的跨领域映射方面也可能存在质的差异。初步定性检查表明，人类和法学硕士经常利用同一源域的不同属性。人类的想法似乎经常优先考虑显着的知觉或感觉运动特征。例如，受到泡菜的启发，许多人类参与者提出了一辆带有“带有凹槽和凹凸的泡菜绿色油漆”的汽车，或者受到仙人掌的启发，一种带有刺纹理的背包，以防止盗窃。相比之下，法学硕士跨领域映射更经常地协调和投射跨领域的机制或功能：同样的例子，以泡菜为灵感的汽车会产生自我修复、防腐机制（密封划痕的微型盐水胶囊），而仙人掌则成为一个集水系统（微型脊柱冷凝翅片将水分引导到可饮用的水库中）。法学硕士不太可能不知道泡菜通常是绿色的，有凹痕，而仙人掌是尖刺的，但它们与人类的不同之处在于类比转移过程中被视为生成的东西。我们的下一步是系统地编码想法内容，例如感知与功能/机械特征，并测试法学硕士和人类倾向于绘制什么样的映射。First, our current analysis focused on quantitative outcomes (e.g., originality rating), but humans and LLMs may also differ qualitatively in the cross-domain mappings they draw. Preliminary qualitative inspection suggests that humans and LLMs often draw on different properties of the same source domain. Human ideas seem to frequently prioritize salient perceptual or sensorimotor features. For example, inspired by pickles, many human participants proposed a car with “pickle-green paint with grooves and bumps,” or inspired by cacti, a backpack with thorny textures to deter theft. In contrast, LLM cross-domain mappings more often align and project mechanisms or functions across domains: the same example of a pickle-inspired car leads to a self-healing, anti-corrosion mechanism (micro-brine capsules that seal scratches), while the cactus becomes a water-harvesting system (micro-spine condensation fins channeling moisture into a drinkable reservoir). It’s unlikely that LLMs don’t know pickles are typically green and dimpled while cacti are spiky, but they differ from humans in what is treated as generative during analogical transfer. Our next step is to systematically code idea content—e.g., perceptual vs. functional/mechanistic features, and test what kind of mappings LLMs and humans tend to draw.

此外，我们的设计捕获了一次性创意的产生，而现实世界的创造力本质上是迭代的：创意经过批评、组合、修改，并逐渐变得可行。因此，未来的工作应该研究迭代工作流程，它可以实例化人类在创造性实践中自然使用的明确机制，例如，可以在模型（自我批评和细化循环）、多主体或文化进化范式或人类与人工智能合作中实施的反思和对抗性批评。In addition, our design captures one-shot idea generation, whereas real-world creativity is inherently iterative: ideas are criticized, combined, revised, and gradually made workable. Future work should therefore study iterative workflows which could instantiate explicit mechanisms that humans naturally use in creative practice—e.g., reflection and adversarial critique that could be implemented either within models (self-critique and refinement loops), in multi-agent or cultural-evolution paradigms, or in human–AI collaborations.
