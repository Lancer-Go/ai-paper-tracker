1 简介Introduction

Unlike traditional task-oriented (TOD) dialogue systems that utilize modular, hard-coded pipelines, these agents integrate reasoning, planning, and tool use to interact with real-world environments. They leverage the massive context windows of frontier models to reason and act through emergent paradigms such as ReAct[20]和思想链 (CoT)and Chain-of-Thought (CoT)[18]。 然而，随着任务范围的扩展和上下文密度的增加，出现了系统可靠性差距：由于上下文衰减，早期自主代理在 65-70% 的现实世界知识工作任务中失败.
