对学生绘制的科学模型进行置信感知自动评估Confidence-Aware Automated Assessment of Student-Drawn Scientific Models

Luyang Fang    Yingchuan Zhang    Jongchan Park    Zhaoji Wang    Ping Ma    Xiaoming Zhai Corresponding author.
摘要Abstract

学生生成的绘图广泛用于科学教育中，以评估学习者对符合下一代科学标准 (NGSS) 的基于建模的任务的概念理解。然而，对此类绘图进行评分需要专家的人类判断来解释复杂的视觉表示，使得大规模评估在课堂环境中实施和维持成本高昂。 在这项工作中，我们研究使用基于视觉的模型对学生生成的科学绘图进行自动评分。我们评估具有参数高效适应的视觉变换器（ViT），并提出了一个置信感知评分框架，该框架从测试时间预测分布中得出响应级别置信度。这种置信信号通过自动对高置信度响应进行评分，同时推迟不确定的情况进行人工审查，从而实现选择性自动化。 对六个符合 NGSS 的中学评估项目的实验表明，所提出的方法提高了评分可靠性，同时支持自动覆盖范围和评分风险之间的实际权衡，凸显了可信教育评估的置信意识方法的价值。Student-generated drawings are widely used in science education to assess learners’ conceptual understanding in modeling-based tasks aligned with the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS). However, scoring such drawings requires expert human judgment to interpret complex visual representations, making large-scale assessment costly to implement and sustain in classroom settings. In this work, we study automated scoring of student-generated scientific drawings using a vision-based model. We evaluate a Vision Transformer (ViT) with parameter-efficient adaptation and propose a confidence-aware scoring framework that derives response-level confidence from test-time predictive distributions. This confidence signal enables selective automation by scoring high-confidence responses automatically while deferring uncertain cases for human review. Experiments on six NGSS-aligned middle school assessment items show that the proposed approach improves scoring reliability while supporting a practical trade-off between automated coverage and scoring risk, highlighting the value of confidence-aware methods for trustworthy educational assessment.

1 简介Introduction

在科学教育中，评估科学建模实践涉及通过结合绘图和书面解释来引出和解释学生理解的证据In science education, assessing scientific modeling practice involves eliciting and interpreting evidence of students’ understanding through a combination of drawings and written explanations[12]。由于绘图可以将结构、关系和机制具体化，而这些结构、关系和机制可能仍然隐含在纯文本响应中，因此建模评估通常依赖于对学生生成的绘图进行基于标题的评估以及书面解释. Since drawings can externalize structures, relationships, and mechanisms that may remain implicit in text-only responses, modeling assessments often rely on rubric-based evaluation of student-generated drawings alongside written explanations[16]。然而，一致的基于评分标准的绘图需要仔细的人为判断并持续关注评分质量；在日常课堂环境中，一致且可靠的评分所需的时间、专业知识和注意力通常是有限的. However, consistent rubric-based scoring of drawings requires careful human judgment and sustained attention to scoring quality; in everyday classroom contexts, the time, expertise, and attention required for consistent and reliable scoring are often limited[12]。从规模上看，对人工评分的依赖成为主要障碍，因为对学生的绘画进行评分是劳动密集型且成本高昂的. At scale, reliance on human scoring becomes a major hurdle because scoring students’ drawings is labor-intensive and costly[13]。这些限制催生了自动评分方法，旨在更有效地支持学生绘图的标准对齐解释. These constraints have motivated automated scoring approaches that aim to support rubric-aligned interpretation of student drawings more efficiently[11].

先前的研究已经证明了对学生生成的视觉伪影进行计算分析的可行性。早期的工作重点是从图纸中提取可量化的视觉特征，并将它们与符合标准的评分标准联系起来，以支持自动评估Prior research has demonstrated the feasibility of computational analysis of student-generated visual artifacts. Early work focused on extracting quantifiable visual features from drawings and linking them to rubric-aligned scoring criteria to support automated assessment[13, 15]。最近，人工智能的进步使得现代模型成为可能，包括 Vision Transformer (ViT) 架构. More recently, advances in artificial intelligence have enabled modern models, including Vision Transformer (ViT) architectures[6]，直接从学生的回答中学习与评分相关的表示, to directly learn scoring-relevant representations from student responses[11, 14, 3, 10].

尽管取得了这些进步，现有的自动评分方法通常只产生单个分数或熟练程度标签，为如何在教学决策中使用此类输出提供有限的支持。在形成性评估中，教师不仅必须判断预测分数，还要判断其可靠性，特别是对于视觉上复杂且模糊的学生绘画。由于缺乏明确的置信度信息，因此很难确定何时可以信任自动评分以及何时需要进行手动审核，从而限制了自动评估系统的实际可用性Despite these advances, existing automated scoring approaches typically produce only a single score or proficiency label, offering limited support for how such outputs should be used in instructional decision-making. In formative assessment contexts, teachers must judge not only the predicted score but also its reliability, particularly for visually complex and ambiguous student drawings. The absence of explicit confidence information makes it difficult to determine when automated scores can be trusted and when manual review is warranted, limiting the practical usability of automated assessment systems[2, 5].

为了解决这些差距，我们提出了一个有信心的To address these gaps, we propose a confidence-aware基于视觉的vision-based对学生生成的科学绘图进行自动评分的方法。建立在置信引导推理研究的基础上approach for automated scoring of student-generated scientific drawings. Building on the confidence-guided inference research[1, 4, 17]，该方法根据基于 ViT 的模型在语义保留测试时间扰动下的预测稳定性得出响应级别置信度得分。我们进一步融入, the approach derives a response-level confidence score from the stability of a ViT-based model’s predictions under semantic-preserving test-time perturbations. We further incorporate选择性信任的测试时间选择test-time selection with selective trust在形成最终评分决策时强调可靠的扰动预测。由此产生的置信度分数可以实现选择性自动评分：系统可以在强大的预测支持下对响应进行自动评分，同时推迟低置信度案例进行人工审核，帮助教师在实践中对响应进行分类并校准对自动评分的依赖。to emphasize reliable perturbed predictions when forming the final scoring decision. The resulting confidence score enables selective automated scoring: the system can auto-score responses with strong predictive support while deferring low-confidence cases for human review, helping teachers triage responses and calibrate reliance on automated scores in practice.

我们的研究评估了学生生成的绘图的拟议框架，这些绘图来自六个符合 NGSS 的中学科学建模评估项目，具有基于标准的熟练程度。根据经验，该方法提高了与专家评分的一致性，并提供直观的置信度分数，指示自动预测何时可靠，从而支持在课堂评估中何时需要人工审核的明智决策。Our study evaluates the proposed framework on student-generated drawings from six NGSS-aligned middle school science modeling assessment items with rubric-based proficiency levels. Empirically, the approach improves agreement with expert scoring and provides an intuitive confidence score indicating when automated predictions are reliable, supporting informed decisions about when human review is needed in classroom assessment.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们研究了使用基于视觉的方法对学生生成的科学绘图进行置信感知的自动评分。通过从测试时间预测分布中得出响应水平置信度并选择性地聚合可靠的预测，所提出的框架可以实现平衡效率和可靠性的自动评分。对六项 NGSS 一致的科学建模任务进行的实验表明，置信感知方法可以提高与人工评分的一致性，同时提供直观的信号来决定何时可以信任自动评分。除了性能提升之外，这项工作还强调了将置信度估计集成到自动评估系统中以支持课堂环境中负责任的部署的重要性。同时，本研究的结果应根据数据集背景进行解释。这些数据是从美国一个地区的中学科学课堂收集的，学生的代表性实践可能反映了当地的课程和课堂环境。此外，由于使用专家提供的分数作为参考，因此人类评分中的任何系统倾向也可能反映在模型性能中。考虑到这些注意事项，未来的工作将在更多样化的学生群体和评估环境中检验所提出的方法，并探索多模式学生反应的扩展以及与教学使用相一致的更细粒度的反馈。In this work, we studied confidence-aware automated scoring of student-generated scientific drawings using a vision-based approach. By deriving response-level confidence from test-time predictive distributions and selectively aggregating reliable predictions, the proposed framework enables automated scoring that balances efficiency and reliability. Experiments on six NGSS-aligned science modeling tasks demonstrate that confidence-aware methods improve agreement with human scoring while providing an intuitive signal for deciding when automated scores can be trusted. Beyond performance gains, this work highlights the importance of integrating confidence estimation into automated assessment systems to support responsible deployment in classroom settings. At the same time, findings in this study should be interpreted in light of the dataset context. The data were collected from middle school science classrooms in one region of the United States, and students’ representational practices may reflect local curricular and classroom contexts. In addition, because expert-provided scores were used as the reference, any systematic tendencies in human scoring may also be reflected in model performance. With these caveats, future work will examine the proposed approach across more diverse student populations and assessment contexts, as well as explore extensions to multimodal student responses and more fine-grained feedback aligned with instructional use.
