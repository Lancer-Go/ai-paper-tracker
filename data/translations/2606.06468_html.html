2.1 蓝图生成Blueprint generation

蓝图生成阶段收到正式声明 目标定理并将依赖图作为单个 Lean 文件发出。 每个节点都是一个正式陈述的定义或引理。每个引理节点也 声明其证明允许依赖哪些其他节点；这些 声明的依赖关系成为图的边缘，被记录并 由 LeanArchitect Lean 包验证The blueprint generation stage receives the formal statement of the target theorem and emits a dependency graph as a single Lean file. Each node is a formally stated definition or lemma. Each lemma node also declares which other nodes its proof is allowed to rely on; these declared dependencies become the edges of the graph, recorded and validated by the LeanArchitect Lean package(Zhu et al., 2026)。目标定理是唯一汇 图表并保留原始正式声明的签名。引理体尚未得到证明 阶段。该模型针对精益编译器进行迭代，以便 发出的文件解析，每个节点都是类型良好的，并且图形是 格式良好（非循环，每个节点均可从目标到达）。. The model iterates against the Lean compiler so that the emitted file parses, every node is well-typed, and the graph is well-formed (acyclic, with every node reachable from the target).

By design, this natural-language proof contains only the informal mathematical argument and is not Lean-aware, so that any strong informal prover can be used to generate it. The blueprint generator consumes that proof as a structural guide for the dependency graph. We find that the resulting blueprint reflects the strategy in the natural-language proof closely: some strategies are easier to realize formally than others that are easier to state informally.

2.2 定理证明Theorem proving

蓝图中的每个引理都被发送给精益定理证明者。 证明者只能看到它正在证明的引理以及定义和 它声明为依赖项的引理，而不是图表的其余部分；的 声明的依赖关系被呈现为可用事实，其 证明者可以按名称调用签名。引理被证明在 平行。证明者可以访问 Lean 编译器和 Mathlib 检索工具，并且可以迭代地调用它们，直到它关闭 目标或耗尽其每个引理预算。当证明者放弃时 在引理上，它返回一个结构化诊断，记录它的内容 尝试过的以及它认为存在差距的地方；当它产生一个 编译器证实的反例，它可以注册一个证明 否定的陈述代替原来的陈述。这些每引理信号 是蓝图细化的输入。Each lemma in the blueprint is dispatched to a Lean theorem prover. The prover sees only the lemma it is proving and the definitions and lemmas it declared as dependencies, not the rest of the graph; the declared dependencies are presented as available facts whose signatures the prover may invoke by name. Lemmas are proved in parallel. The prover has access to the Lean compiler and a Mathlib retrieval tool, and may call them iteratively until it either closes the goal or exhausts its per-lemma budget. When the prover gives up on a lemma, it returns a structured diagnosis recording what it attempted and where it believes the gap lies; when it produces a compiler-corroborated counterexample, it can register a proof of the negated statement in lieu of the original. These per-lemma signals are the input to blueprint refinement.

2.3 蓝图细化Blueprint refinement

如果在证明通过后有任何引理未得到证明，则蓝图 细化阶段重写围绕故障的图表。一个细化 模型读取每个引理痕迹，将每个引理标记为已证明或 未经证实（携带证明者的诊断和任何正式的反驳 未经证明的引理），并发出修改后的图表。典型的改进 包括将硬引理分解为中间辅助引理， 重新连接依赖关系，以便引理能够访问它需要的结果，以及 修复或删除证明者争论的引理的陈述 假的。先前通过证明的引理与它们一起保留 签名完好无损，因此已经花费在其上的证明预算不是 被丢弃。精炼后的图被交给另一个验证过程，并且 循环继续，直到每个引理都被证明或迭代 预算达到。If any lemma is unproved after a proving pass, the blueprint refinement stage rewrites the graph around the failures. A refinement model reads the per-lemma traces, marks each lemma as proved or unproved (carrying the prover’s diagnosis and any formal disproof for unproved lemmas), and emits a revised graph. Typical refinements include decomposing a hard lemma into intermediate helper lemmas, rewiring dependencies so a lemma has access to results it needs, and repairing or dropping the statement of a lemma the prover argued was false. Lemmas that the prior pass proved are preserved with their signatures intact, so the proving budget already spent on them is not discarded. The refined graph is handed to another proving pass, and the loop continues until every lemma is proved or the iteration budget is reached.
