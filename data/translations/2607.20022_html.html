摘要Abstract

形式的差异约束Difference constraints of the formx − y ≤ d由于它们与最短路径的联系，因此得到了很好的研究，具有满足和暗示的有效算法。然而，有限域传播算法通常不使用这些算法，并将每个差异约束视为单独的传播器。传播确实保证了求解的完整性，但可能会不必要地缓慢。在本文中，我们描述了如何为同时处理所有差异约束的（边界一致）全局传播器。一段时间以来，SAT 模理论求解器已经包含了差分约束的理论求解器。虽然差分约束的理论求解器给出了全局差分约束传播器的基础，但我们展示了对传播器的要求有何不同。至关重要的是，我们展示了如何通过全局差异约束传播器来解释传播，以便在惰性子句生成求解器中使用它。我们的实验表明，全局处理差异约束可以大大改进标准传播方法。are well studied, with efficient algorithms for satisfaction and implication, because of their connection to shortest paths. Finite domain propagation algorithms, however, typically do not make use of these algorithms, and treat each difference constraint as a separate propagator. Propagation does guarantee completeness of solving, but can be needlessly slow. In this paper we describe how to build a (bounds consistent) global propagator for difference constraints that treats them all simultaneously. SAT modulo theory solvers have included theory solvers for difference constraints for some time. While a theory solver for difference constraints gives the basis of a global difference constraint propagator, we show how the requirements on the propagator are quite different. Crucially, we show how to explain propagations by a global difference constraint propagator, in order to use it within a lazy clause generation solver. We give experiments showing that treating difference constraints globally can substantially improve on the standard propagation approach.

8 结论8 Conclusion

差异约束广泛出现在约束编程模型中，并且作为单独传播器的默认表示具有一些已知的不良行为。在本文中，我们探讨了差异约束的全局处理如何改善传播。我们探索了许多不同的设计选择。总体而言，我们发现以最低优先级的边界应用全局传播器、以最高优先级的布尔值应用全局传播器、不运行 IncImp 算法以及应用初始简化会带来总体最佳结果。Difference constraints appear widely in constraint programming models, and the default representation as individual propagators has some known bad behavior. In this paper we explore how a global treatment of difference constraints can improve propagation. We explore a number of different design choices. Overall we find that applying the global propagator at lowest priority for bounds, highest priority for booleans, not running the IncImp algorithm, and applying initial simplification leads to the best results overall.

我们注意到，虽然 SMT [4, 5] 求解器在全局范围内处理差异约束，但由于边界传播对于 FD 的相对重要性，简单地导入其方法对于有限域传播求解来说是不切实际的。对于有边界约束的问题，4.1 节中概述的方法比 gp 慢一个数量级。We note that while SMT [4, 5] solvers treat difference constraints globally, the naive importing of their methodology is impractical for finite domain propagation solving because of the relative importance of bounds propagation for FD. For problems with bounds constraints the approach outlined in Section 4.1 is an order of magnitude slower than gp.

全局差异求解器通过检测全局变量以利用已知差异约束和/或学习传播的新差异逻辑结果，开启了改进 CP 求解器行为的可能性，如 Tempo 的析取传播器。这仍然是未来的重要工作。The global difference solver opens the possibility of improving the behaviour of CP solvers by instrumenting globals to take advantage of known difference constraints and/or learn new difference logic consequences of propagation, as in the disjunctive propagator for Tempo. This remains as important future work.
