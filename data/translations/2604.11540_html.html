一、简介1. Introduction

晶体材料的加速发现对于能量转换系统、电子元件和航空航天结构至关重要。[1-3]传统上，材料开发高度依赖于迭代试错实验，导致周期跨越10-20年。[4-8]尽管高通量筛选和机器学习的进步已经成功建立了庞大的数据库和预测模型，但发现过程仍然受到科学工作流程碎片化的严重瓶颈。[9-13]具体来说，探索材料的结构-性质-合成关系需要人类专家手动将理论原理与专门的计算工具联系起来。[14-16]然而，这种手动方法本质上是不可扩展的并且是劳动密集型的，严重限制了对广阔化学空间的探索。 因此，需要能够将特定领域推理与工具编排相结合的自主系统，以加快从概念化到验证的时间线。The accelerated discovery of crystal materials is critical for energy conversion systems, electronic components, and aerospace structures.[1-3] Traditionally, material development has highly depended on iterative trial-and-error experimentation, resulting in cycles spanning 10-20 years.[4-8] Although advances in high-throughput screening and machine learning have successfully established vast databases and predictive models, the discovery process remains critically bottlenecked by the fragmentation of scientific workflows.[9-13] Specifically, the exploration of structure-property-synthesis relationships of material requires human experts to manually connect theoretical principles with specialized computational tools.[14-16] However, this manual method is inherently non-scalable and labor-intensive, severely limiting the exploration of vast chemical spaces. Therefore, autonomous systems capable of coupling domain-specific reasoning with tool orchestration are needed to accelerate the timeline from conceptualization to validation.

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在整合知识、执行逻辑推理、处理信息和协调跨学科任务方面表现出了巨大的潜力。[17-19] 最近的高级研究，例如 ChemCrow，已经显示出工具增强化学发现的良好能力。[20,21] 然而，将通用 LLM 应用于材料科学面临着独特的挑战。[22,23] 通用 LLM 本质上缺乏严格的 3D 几何约束和量子力学效应的基础。 （例如，晶格对称性、能带结构），[24-26]同时还努力解决所需的严格句法精度Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated considerable potential for integrating knowledge, performing logical reasoning, processing information, and coordinating tasks across disciplines.[17-19] Recent advanced research, such as ChemCrow, has shown promising capabilities for tool-augmented chemical discovery.[20,21] However, applying general LLMs to materials science presents unique challenges.[22,23] General LLMs inherently lack grounding in rigorous 3D geometric constraints and quantum mechanical effects (e.g., lattice symmetries, band structures),[24-26] while also struggling with the rigid syntactic precision required for

迭代、多步骤工具编排。[25,27-29]为了缩小这些推理差距，最近的趋势有利于扩展到大规模架构（例如，DeepSeek-R1 中的 600B+ 参数）。 然而，这种大规模模型涉及大量的培训开销和部署成本，限制了少数资金充足的机构的访问，并阻碍了实时、交互式工作流程。[28,30,31]更根本的是，这些双重任务所需的不同熵动力学产生了关键的优化冲突。 具体来说，可靠的晶体学分析需要最小化熵以确保确定性准确性，而强大的工具编排需要灵活的高熵策略来实现自适应规划和多样化的推理探索，这对于导航复杂的决策空间至关重要。[32-34]因此，强制统一的轻量级模型来满足这些相互冲突的目标通常会导致“熵崩溃”，其中模型要么失去处理复杂工作流程的灵活性，要么产生确定性事实的幻觉。[35]iterative, multi-step tool orchestration.[25,27-29] To mitigate these reasoning gaps, recent trends have favored scaling up to massive architectures (e.g., 600B+ parameters in DeepSeek-R1). Yet, such large-scale models involve substantial training overhead and deployment costs, restricting their accessibility to a few well-funded institutions and hindering real-time, interactive workflows.[28,30,31] More fundamentally, a critical optimization conflict arises from the distinct entropy dynamics required for these dual tasks. Specifically, reliable crystallographic analysis requires minimizing entropy to ensure deterministic accuracy, whereas robust tool orchestration requires a flexible, high-entropy policy to enable the adaptive planning and diverse reasoning exploration essential for navigating complex decision spaces.[32-34] Consequently, forcing a unified lightweight model to satisfy these conflicting objectives often leads to “entropy collapse”, where the model either loses the flexibility to handle complex workflows or hallucinates deterministic facts.[35]

科学模型训练的一个基本挑战是A fundamental challenge in the training of scientific models is presented by the

材料设计中解决方案轨迹的非唯一性，其中严格的基于结果的监督常常被证明是稀疏的或具有误导性的。 为了解决这个问题，科学工具的确定性本质被用作内在验证器，其中有效执行充当遵守物理约束的代理。[50,51]因此，我们不再仅仅依赖于最终答案匹配，而是重新制定了优化目标，以优先考虑流程保真度和可执行性。 通过这种策略，建立了一个自我监督的框架来减轻“奖励黑客”，确保准确性来自严格的工具执行而不是答案记忆。 [52]因此，策略优化利用复合奖励函数，将语法有效性、格式合规性和工作流程效率视为目标结果。non-uniqueness of solution trajectories in materials design, where rigid outcome-based supervision often proves sparse or misleading. The output of this node is structured into two distinct components: a scientific interpretation of the current experimental results and a strategic intent for the subsequent action.
