8 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 NEXT，一种数据驱动的方法，无需专用力传感器即可估计外部关节扭矩。只需 10 分钟的自由空间数据和 1 分钟的训练，NEXT 就能产生与专用传感器测量结果相当的估计值，并能够在低成本手臂上进行力反馈远程操作。我们进一步介绍了 FIRST，它使用 NEXT 扭矩估计来对行为克隆期间的预接触和接触片段进行上采样，通过强调对对齐和交互最关键的阶段来提高接触丰富的任务的性能。We present NEXT, a data-driven method for estimating external joint torque without dedicated force sensors. With only 10 minutes of free-space data and 1 minute of training, NEXT produces estimates comparable to dedicated sensor measurements and enables force-feedback teleoperation on low-cost arms. We further introduce FIRST, which uses NEXT torque estimates to up-sample pre-contact and contact segments during behavior cloning, improving performance on contact-rich tasks by emphasizing the phases most critical for alignment and interaction.

A.1 自由空间逆动力学模型架构Free-Space Inverse Dynamics Model Architecture

我们使用基于 LSTM 的序列模型进行自由空间逆动力学预测。我们采用无状态滑动窗口公式，而不是在整个轨迹上维持持久的隐藏状态。因此，网络隐式地对回溯窗口内的时间动态进行建模，同时为每个序列重置隐藏状态。该公式提供了最近的运动上下文，而不会在长轨迹上累积隐藏状态漂移。局部历史允许模型捕获速度相关、执行器相关和未建模的摩擦效应，而无需明确估计加速度。对于部署，可以使用最新的滚动缓冲区来评估估计器We use an LSTM-based sequence model for free-space inverse dynamics prediction. Rather than maintaining a persistent hidden state across the entire trajectory, we adopt a stateless sliding-window formulation. The network therefore implicitly models temporal dynamics within the look-back window, while the hidden state is reset for each sequence. This formulation provides recent motion context without accumulating hidden-state drift over long trajectories. The local history allows the model to capture velocity-dependent, actuator-dependent, and unmodeled friction effects without explicitly estimating acceleration. 我们进一步检查原始关节电流是否足以进行接触感知策略学习，或者学习的外部关节扭矩估计是否提供了更有用的表示。 之前的工作，例如 TA-VLAWe further examine whether raw joint current is sufficient for contact-aware policy learning, or whether the learned external joint torque estimate provides a more useful representation. Prior work such as TA-VLA[17]使用来自低成本机器人手臂的现成关节电流测量值作为模仿学习的与力相关的输入。 然而，关节电流只是物理相互作用的间接测量：它包含自由空间运动和外部接触所需的执行器作用力，以及依赖于硬件和控制器的效应。 因此，该策略必须隐式地将接触引起的扭矩与自由空间动力学分开。uses readily available joint current measurements from low-cost robot arms as a force-related input for imitation learning. However, joint current is only an indirect measure of physical interaction: it contains actuator effort required for both free-space motion and external contact, as well as hardware- and controller-dependent effects. As a result, the policy must implicitly disentangle contact-induced torque from free-space dynamics.

相比之下，NEXT 通过从测量的电机扭矩中减去学习的自由空间扭矩预测来明确估计外部关节扭矩。 这种区别很重要，因为仅根据电流估计干净的外部扭矩信号通常需要时间背景。 如附录中的 NEXT 自由空间逆动力学消融所示In contrast, NEXT explicitly estimates external joint torque by subtracting a learned free-space torque prediction from the measured motor torque. This distinction is important because estimating a clean external torque signal from current alone generally requires temporal context. As shown in our NEXT free-space inverse dynamics ablations in AppendixA.2，更长的本体感受历史可以改善扭矩估计，这表明瞬时电流和状态观察不足以可靠地将自由空间驱动与接触分开。 然而，标准行为克隆策略通常仅接收当前观察结果，而不是较长的本体感受历史记录，例如 NEXT 使用的 50 步历史记录。 虽然人们可以直接向策略提供这样的历史记录，但这增加了策略同时推断动态、定位接触和解决视觉运动任务的负担。 在实践中，较长的本体感受历史也可能会导致过度拟合特定于轨迹的运动模式，而不是适当地使用视觉来对场景变化做出反应。 NEXT 通过使用专门的力估计模块中的历史记录来避免此问题，同时为策略提供紧凑的、与接触相关的外部扭矩信号。, longer proprioceptive histories improve torque estimation, suggesting that instantaneous current and state observations are insufficient to reliably separate free-space actuation from contact. A standard behavior cloning policy, however, typically receives only the current observation rather than a long proprioceptive history such as the 50-step history used by NEXT. While one could provide such a history directly to the policy, this increases the burden on the policy to simultaneously infer dynamics, localize contact, and solve the visuomotor task. In practice, long proprioceptive histories may also encourage overfitting to trajectory-specific motion patterns rather than using vision appropriately to react to scene variation. NEXT avoids this issue by using history in a specialized force-estimation module, while providing the policy with a compact, contact-relevant external torque signal.
