摘要Abstract

大型语言模型越来越多地被提议作为高风险公共工作流程的自主代理，但我们缺乏系统的证据来证明它们在被授予权力时是否会遵循制度规则。我们提出的证据表明，机构人工智能的完整性应被视为部署前的要求，而不是部署后的假设。我们评估了多主体治理模拟，其中主体在不同的权力结构下担任正式的政府角色，并通过独立的基于规则的法官对 28,112 个记录片段中的违规和滥用结果进行评分。虽然我们提出这一立场，但核心贡献是经验性的：在饱和度以下运行的模型中，治理结构比模型身份更能驱动腐败相关的结果，不同政权和模型与治理配对之间存在巨大差异。轻量级防护措施可以降低某些情况下的风险，但不能始终如一地防止严重故障。这些结果意味着制度设计是安全授权的先决条件：在将真正的权力分配给法学硕士代理之前，系统应该在类似治理的约束下进行压力测试，其中包括可执行的规则、可审计的日志以及对高影响力行为的人工监督。Large language models are increasingly proposed as autonomous agents for high-stakes public workflows, yet we lack systematic evidence about whether they would follow institutional rules when granted authority. We present evidence that integrity in institutional AI should be treated as a pre-deployment requirement rather than a post-deployment assumption. We evaluate multi-agent governance simulations in which agents occupy formal governmental roles under different authority structures, and we score rule-breaking and abuse outcomes with an independent rubric-based judge across 28,112 transcript segments. While we advance this position, the core contribution is empirical: among models operating below saturation, governance structure is a stronger driver of corruption-related outcomes than model identity, with large differences across regimes and model–governance pairings. Lightweight safeguards can reduce risk in some settings but do not consistently prevent severe failures. These results imply that institutional design is a precondition for safe delegation: before real authority is assigned to LLM agents, systems should undergo stress testing under governance-like constraints with enforceable rules, auditable logs, and human oversight on high-impact actions.

2 相关工作Related Work

多智能体系统研究在协调、激励和学习动态方面取得了实质性进展Multi-agent systems research has made substantial progress on coordination, incentives, and learning dynamics(Dafoe et al., 2020; Yao et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023)，但其评估以任务成功为中心。共谋和不协调一旦出现，就会被视为优化失败，而不是违反制度规则, but its evaluations center on task success. Collusion and miscoordination, when they appear, are treated as optimization failures rather than violations of institutional rules(Leibo et al., 2017; Calvano et al., 2020; Krakovna, 2018)。算法问责制工作提出了更多相关问题，强调透明度、可审计性和制度背景. Work on algorithmic accountability asks more relevant questions, emphasizing transparency, auditability, and institutional context(Yeung, 2018; Diakopoulos, 2016; Kroll et al., 2017)，但倾向于规范框架而不是行为测量。政治经济学提供了一座有用的桥梁，表明腐败源于激励结构和组织设计，而不是个别不良行为者, but tends toward normative frameworks rather than behavioral measurement. Political economy offers a useful bridge, showing that corruption stems from incentive structures and organizational design rather than individual bad actors(Klitgaard, 1988; Rose-Ackerman, 1999)，这表明多主体系统中的完整性失败更有可能是制度结构的特性，而不是任何个体模型的特性。, which suggests that integrity failures in multi-agent systems are more likely properties of institutional structure than of any individual model.

最接近的实证工作来自于程序保障和规则遵循提示的研究The closest empirical work comes from research on procedural safeguards and rule-following prompts(Amodei et al., 2016; Ouyang et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022)，但这项工作针对单个代理。多代理设置引入了单代理评估无法捕获的故障模式：当权限跨角色分配时，代理可能会串通一气，转移责任，并以个人行为不可见的方式破坏记录。最近的工作证实，竞争性和去中心化的法学硕士设置会产生性质不同的行为，包括提高任务指标的欺骗行为, but this work targets single agents. Multi-agent settings introduce failure modes that single-agent evaluations cannot capture: when authority is distributed across roles, agents can collude, shift blame, and fragment records in ways that no individual action makes visible. Recent work confirms that competitive and decentralized LLM settings produce qualitatively different behavior, including deception that improves task metrics(El and Zou, 2025)以及在自身利益下崩溃的声誉动态and reputation dynamics that break down under self-interest(Zhu et al., 2026)。结构化治理环境中是否会出现类似的失败，以及制度保障措施是否能够遏制它们，仍然是一个悬而未决的实证问题。. Whether analogous failures emerge in structured governance settings, and whether institutional safeguards can contain them, remains an open empirical question.
