摘要Abstract

本技术说明在统一的比较框架下重新审视 RaBitQ 和 TurboQuant 之间的关系。我们使用可重复、透明和对称的设置，从方法论、理论保证和实证性能方面比较这两种方法。我们的结果表明，尽管 TurboQuant 声称具有优势，但在直接可比较的设置中，TurboQuant 并没有提供比 RaBitQ 一致的改进；在许多测试的配置中，它的性能比 RaBitQ 差。我们进一步发现 TurboQuant 论文中报告的一些运行时和召回结果无法在指定配置下从已发布的实现中重现。总体而言，本说明阐明了两条工作线之间的共享结构和真正差异，同时记录了 TurboQuant 论文报告的实验结果中的再现性问题。This technical note revisits the relationship between RaBitQ and TurboQuant under a unified comparison framework. We compare the two methods in terms of methodology, theoretical guarantees, and empirical performance, using a reproducible, transparent, and symmetric setup. Our results show that, despite the claimed advantage of TurboQuant, TurboQuant does not provide a consistent improvement over RaBitQ in directly comparable settings; in many tested configurations, it performs worse than RaBitQ. We further find that several reported runtime and recall results in the TurboQuant paper could not be reproduced from the released implementation under the stated configuration. Overall, this note clarifies the shared structure and genuine differences between the two lines of work, while documenting reproducibility issues in the experimental results reported by the TurboQuant paper.

1 简介Introduction

高维欧几里德空间中的矢量量化已成为现代人工智能系统中的一个基本问题，包括矢量数据库和大语言模型（LLM）服务。 在这些设置中，量化的目标是（1）通过压缩高维向量数据来减少内存使用； (2)减少与向量相关的计算成本； (3)保存下游任务所需的几何量，特别是内积。Vector quantization in high-dimensional Euclidean spaces has become a fundamental problem in modern AI systems, including vector databases and large language model (LLM) serving. In these settings, the goal of quantization is to (1) reduce memory usage by compressing high-dimensional vector data; (2) reduce computational costs related to the vectors; and (3) preserve the geometric quantities needed by downstream tasks, especially inner products.

最近，TurboQuant 于 2025 年 4 月首次发布在 arXiv 上，后来在 ICLR 2026 上被接受Recently, TurboQuant, first posted on arXiv in April 2025 and later accepted at ICLR 2026111https://openreview.net/forum?id=tO3ASKZlok，通过诸如“至少 6 倍内存减少和高达 8 倍加速，且全部精度损失为零”等主张引起了公众的广泛关注, has drawn substantial public attention through claims such as “at least 6x memory reduction and up to 8x speedup, all with zero accuracy loss”(Zandieh et al., 2025a; Zandieh and Mirrokni, 2026)。然而，这些面向公众的主张主要是针对未压缩的基线制定的，因此，它们本身并不能解释相对于先前的量化方法应该如何理解 TurboQuant。. However, these public-facing claims are framed primarily against an uncompressed baseline, and thus, do not by themselves explain how TurboQuant should be understood relative to prior quantization methods.

最直接相关的现有方法之一是 RaBitQ。最初的1位RaBitQ于2023年10月提交给SIGMOD 2024，被接受，并于2024年5月发布在arXiv上One of the most directly relevant prior methods is RaBitQ. The original 1-bit RaBitQ was submitted to SIGMOD 2024 in October 2023, accepted, and posted on arXiv in May 2024(Gao and Long, 2024);其多位扩展于 2024 年 9 月发布在 arXiv 上，后来在 SIGMOD 2025 上被接受; its multi-bit extension was posted on arXiv in September 2024 and later accepted at SIGMOD 2025(Gao et al., 2025b)。 TurboQuant和RaBitQ密切相关：两条线在应用场景上重叠，​​共享重要的方法级结构，并强调密切相关的理论保证。然而，TurboQuant 论文并没有从三个方面对这种关系提供准确和平衡的解释。在方法层面，RaBitQ 没有以足够的精度进行描述或比较。在理论层面，RaBitQ 理论保证的一些特征实际上是不正确的且不受支持。在实验层面，RaBitQ 基线所使用的设置尚未完全公开；随后的电子邮件通信显示，基线是在对 RaBitQ 非常不利的条件下运行的。. TurboQuant and RaBitQ are closely related: the two lines overlap in application scenarios, share important method-level structure, and emphasize closely related theoretical guarantees. Yet the TurboQuant paper does not provide an accurate and balanced account of that relationship in three respects. At the method level, RaBitQ is not described or compared with sufficient accuracy. At the theory level, some characterizations of RaBitQ’s theoretical guarantees are factually incorrect and unsupported. At the experimental level, the setup used for the RaBitQ baseline is not fully disclosed; subsequent email correspondence reveals that the baseline was run under conditions highly unfavorable to RaBitQ.

本技术说明就是为了解决这一差距而编写的。我们的目的是将 RaBitQ 和 TurboQuant 放在一个比较框架内，并准确地描述哪些是共享的，哪些是真正不同的，以及哪些关于理论和实验的结论在可重复、透明和对称的标准下是合理的。该报告同时有两个目的：它提供了可引用的技术比较，并为 RaBitQ 和 TurboQuant 实际上如何相互比较这一更广泛的公众问题提供了明确的答案。 我们的实验比较进一步表明，TurboQuant 论文中的经验主张很难与使用已发布的工件进行的可重复评估相一致。在直接可比较的内积估计和最近邻搜索设置中，TurboQuant 并没有始终优于 RaBitQ；在许多测试的配置中，它的性能更差。对于量化时间，我们发现报告的 TurboQuant 结果无法在规定的硬件配置下从已发布的实现中重现，我们测量的运行时间与报告的数字有很大差异。我们还观察到报告和复制的 RaBitQ 召回率和运行时结果之间存在不一致。这些差异表明 TurboQuant 论文中报告的实验结果无法从所声明的实验配置下发布的工件中重现。此外，RaBitQ 基线未公开使用非对称硬件和并行设置，进一步削弱了所报告的比较，作为与 RaBitQ 相比具有一致经验优势的证据。This technical note is written to address this gap. Our purpose is to place RaBitQ and TurboQuant within a single comparison framework and to characterize precisely what is shared, what is genuinely different, and what conclusions about theory and experiments are justified under a reproducible, transparent and symmetric standard. The report serves two purposes simultaneously: it provides a citable technical comparison and it offers a clear answer to the broader public question of how RaBitQ and TurboQuant are actually compared to each other. Our experimental comparison further shows that the empirical claims in the TurboQuant paper are difficult to reconcile with reproducible evaluations using the released artifacts. In directly comparable inner-product estimation and nearest-neighbor search settings, TurboQuant does not consistently improve over RaBitQ; in many tested configurations, it performs worse. For quantization time, we find that the reported TurboQuant results cannot be reproduced from the released implementation under the stated hardware configuration, with our measured running times differing substantially from the reported numbers. We also observe inconsistencies between the reported and reproduced RaBitQ recall and runtime results. These discrepancies indicate that the reported experimental results in the TurboQuant paper are not reproducible from the released artifacts under the stated experimental configuration. In addition, the undisclosed use of asymmetric hardware and parallelism settings for the RaBitQ baseline further weakens the reported comparison as evidence of a consistent empirical advantage over RaBitQ.

2.2.1 RaBitQ 和 TurboQuant 的预处理。Preprocessing of Both RaBitQ and TurboQuant.

RaBitQ 和 TurboQuant 都分别对向量的范数和方向进行编码。实际上，一旦存储了范数，每种方法的核心量化过程都集中在量化归一化向量上。Both RaBitQ and TurboQuant encode the norm and direction of a vector separately. In practice, once the norm is stored, the core quantization procedure of each method focuses on quantizing the normalized vector.

RaBitQ 和 TurboQuant 都应用随机旋转，这是 Johnson–Lindenstrauss 变换的一种形式Both RaBitQ and TurboQuant apply a random rotation, which is a form of Johnson–Lindenstrauss Transformation(Johnson and Lindenstrauss, 1984)，作为所有向量的第一步。两种方法都利用随机旋转后向量的分布信息来设计其量化算法。具体来说，这两种方法都采样并存储随机旋转矩阵， 并对所有向量应用相同的随机旋转。在下面的部分中，如果没有进一步说明，我们假设所有向量都已通过该随机矩阵旋转。, as the first step for all vectors. Both methods use the distributional information of vectors after random rotation to design their quantization algorithms. Specifically, both methods sample and store a random rotation matrix, and apply the same random rotation to all vectors. In the following sections, without further specification, we assume that all vectors have been rotated by this random matrix.
