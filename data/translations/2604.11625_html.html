摘要。Abstract.

神经算子已经成为偏微分方程 (PDE) 求解器的强大替代者，但它们通常被训练为单个 PDE 的整体模型，需要能源密集型 GPU 硬件，并且当新物理出现时必须从头开始重新训练。 我们介绍Neural operators have emerged as powerful surrogates for partial differential equation (PDE) solvers, yet they are typically trained as monolithic models for individual PDEs, require energy-intensive GPU hardware, and must be retrained from scratch when new physics emerge. We introduce the尖峰组合神经算子Spiking Compositional Neural Operator(SCNO)，一种模块化架构，结合了尖峰和传统组件，解决了所有三个限制。 SCNO 维护一个小型尖峰神经算子块库，每个块都在单个基本微分算子（对流、扩散、反应）上进行训练，并通过轻量级输入条件聚合器组合它们，以解决块训练期间未见的耦合偏微分方程。 一个小型校正网络学习交叉耦合残差，同时保持所有块和聚合器冻结，通过构造保留零遗忘的模块化扩展。 我们在八个 PDE 系列上评估 SCNO，包括五个耦合系统和一个与核相关的 1 组中子扩散方程。 经过校正的 SCNO 达到最低相对值(SCNO), a modular architecture combining spiking and conventional components that addresses all three limitations. SCNO maintains a library of small spiking neural operator blocks, each trained on a single elementary differential operator (convection, diffusion, reaction), and composes them through a lightweight input-conditioned aggregator to solve coupled PDEs not seen during block training. A small correction network learns cross-coupling residuals while keeping all blocks and the aggregator frozen, preserving zero-forgetting modular expansion by construction. We evaluate SCNO on eight PDE families including five coupled systems and a nuclear-relevant 1-group neutron diffusion equation. SCNO with correction achieves the lowest relativeL2L^{2}五个耦合偏微分方程中的四个的误差，优于整体尖峰 DeepONet（高达 62%，平均超过 3 个种子）和标准 ANN DeepONet（高达 65%），同时仅需要 95K 个可训练参数，而整体基线需要 462K 个参数。 据我们所知，这是第一个组合尖峰神经算子，也是第一个具有内置无遗忘扩展的模块化神经形态偏微分方程求解的概念验证。error on four of five coupled PDEs, outperforming both a monolithic spiking DeepONet (by up to 62%, mean over 3 seeds) and a standard ANN DeepONet (by up to 65%), while requiring only 95K trainable parameters versus 462K for the monolithic baseline. To our knowledge, this is the first compositional spiking neural operator and the first proof-of-concept for modular neuromorphic PDE solving with built-in forgetting-free expansion.

1. 简介Introduction

偏微分方程 (PDE) 几乎控制着工程领域的所有物理系统，从流体动力学和传热到核反应堆中的中子输运。 神经算子Partial differential equations (PDEs) govern virtually all physical systems of engineering interest, from fluid dynamics and heat transfer to neutron transport in nuclear reactors. Neural operators(lu2021deeponet, ; li2021fno, )已经证明，函数空间之间的映射可以直接从数据中学习，从而实现比经典求解器快几个数量级的实时偏微分方程代理。 然而，当前的神经算子面临三个相互关联的限制，阻碍了它们在资源受限的边缘硬件（例如核设施的神经形态处理器）上的部署。have demonstrated that mappings between function spaces can be learned directly from data, enabling real-time PDE surrogates that are orders of magnitude faster than classical solvers. However, current neural operators suffer from three interrelated limitations that hinder their deployment on resource-constrained edge hardware such as neuromorphic processors at nuclear facilities.

第一First，神经算子是单一的：必须为每个偏微分方程训练一个单独的模型，没有机制可以重用相关物理知识。 当核反应堆在运行状态（新鲜燃料、循环中期、循环结束）之间转换时，控制方程会随着裂变产物浓度的变化和材料性能的下降而变化，需要操作员从头开始重新培训。, neural operators are monolithic: a separate model must be trained for each PDE, with no mechanism to reuse knowledge across related physics. When a nuclear reactor transitions between operational regimes (fresh fuel, mid-cycle, end-of-cycle), the governing equations change as fission product concentrations evolve and material properties degrade, requiring the operator to be retrained from scratch.第二Second，神经算子是能源密集型的：基于傅里叶神经算子（FNO）的标准架构, neural operators are energy-intensive: standard architectures based on Fourier neural operators (FNO)(li2021fno, )或深网or DeepONet(lu2021deeponet, )需要 GPU 级硬件进行训练和推理，无法部署在远程核站点的低功耗边缘设备上。require GPU-class hardware for both training and inference, precluding deployment on low-power edge devices at remote nuclear sites.第三Third，神经算子表现出灾难性遗忘：当对多个偏微分方程系列进行连续训练时，早期任务的性能严重下降，这是必须跟踪不断发展的物理过程的反应堆数字孪生的安全关键故障模式。, neural operators exhibit catastrophic forgetting: when sequentially trained on multiple PDE families, performance on earlier tasks degrades severely, a safety-critical failure mode for reactor digital twins that must track evolving physics.

最近的工作已经开始从互补的方向解决这些挑战。 组合神经算子，例如 CompNORecent work has begun addressing these challenges from complementary directions. Spiking neural operators including SPINONet(garg2026spinonet, )以及 Kahana 等人的尖峰 DeepONet。and the spiking DeepONet of Kahana et al.(kahana2022spiking, )将节能的事件驱动计算引入偏微分方程求解，但它们是整体的，必须针对每个新系统进行重新训练。 塞尔曼和艾蒙bring energy-efficient event-driven computation to PDE solving, but are monolithic and must be retrained for each new system. Theilman and Aimone(theilman2025neurofem, )在英特尔的 Loihi 2 神经拟态平台上演示了直接基于 FEM 的 PDE 求解，但仅限于静态（稳态）问题，没有学习或组合能力。demonstrated direct FEM-based PDE solving on Intel’s Loihi 2 neuromorphic platform, but were limited to static (steady-state) problems with no learning or compositional capability.现有的方法还没有将组合算子学习与尖峰神经网络结合起来。No existing method combines compositional operator learning with spiking neural networks.

6. 讨论Discussion

我们的结果揭示了一个清晰的模式：当耦合偏微分方程的算子为Our results reveal a clear pattern: composition excels when the coupled PDE’s operators are弱相互作用weakly interacting。 在反应扩散中，扩散使空间梯度变得平滑，而反应则在局部起作用——这些效应大致是可分离的。 聚合器很容易学会将它们组合起来，并且冻结的 SCNO (2.1%) 优于整体训练 (5.5%)。 相比之下，对流扩散涉及紧密耦合：扩散平滑了对流传输的信号，创建了两个模块都无法单独学习的特征。 对流模块较高的基本误差（反应为 8.6% 对比 5.6%）加剧了这一点：校正缩小了差距（整体为 14.1% 对比 10.7%），但并未缩小差距，这表明采用基于物理的模块训练. In reaction-diffusion, diffusion smooths spatial gradients while reaction acts locally—these effects are approximately separable. The aggregator easily learns to combine them, and frozen SCNO (2.1%) outperforms monolithic training (5.5%). In contrast, convection-diffusion involves tight coupling: diffusion smooths the signal that convection transports, creating features that neither block learned in isolation. The convection block’s higher base error (8.6% vs. 5.6% for reaction) compounds this: correction reduces the gap (14.1% vs. 10.7% monolithic) but does not close it, suggesting physics-informed block training(garg2026spinonet, )可能需要紧密的传输耦合。 这种弱耦合和强耦合体系的分解为实践者提供了指导：对于弱耦合物理，冻结成分就足够了；对于强耦合物理，添加校正网络。may be needed for tight transport coupling. This decomposition into weak- and strong-coupling regimes provides practitioners with guidance: for weakly-coupled physics, frozen composition suffices; for strongly-coupled physics, add a correction network.

7. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 SCNO，第一个用于 PDE 求解的组合尖峰神经算子。 通过维护冻结尖峰模块库并通过带有可选校正网络的聚合器将它们组合起来，SCNO 同时实现了三个属性：(1) 在耦合偏微分方程上具有竞争性的准确性，在五个系统中的四个上优于整体基线； (2) 通过构建实现零遗忘，从而能够增量扩展新的物理； (3) 与神经形态边缘硬件兼容的基于尖峰的计算。 在核中子扩散方面，经过校正的 SCNO 实现了 4.6% 的误差，比整体 SNN 基线低 58%，这表明可以通过组合通用算子块来解决与反应堆相关的物理问题。We presented SCNO, the first compositional spiking neural operator for PDE solving. By maintaining a library of frozen spiking blocks and composing them through an aggregator with an optional correction network, SCNO achieves three properties simultaneously: (1) competitive accuracy on coupled PDEs, outperforming monolithic baselines on four of five systems; (2) zero forgetting by construction, enabling incremental expansion to new physics; and (3) spike-based computation compatible with neuromorphic edge hardware. On nuclear neutron diffusion, SCNO with correction achieves 4.6% error, 58% lower than the monolithic SNN baseline, demonstrating that reactor-relevant physics can be solved by composing generic operator blocks.

这些结果指向更广阔的视野These results point toward a broader vision of科学计算的尖峰基础模型spiking foundation models for scientific computing：可重用、节能的神经算子块库，可以在神经形态硬件上进行组合、扩展和部署，以在边缘进行实时多物理场模拟。: libraries of reusable, energy-efficient neural operator blocks that can be composed, extended, and deployed on neuromorphic hardware for real-time multi-physics simulation at the edge.
