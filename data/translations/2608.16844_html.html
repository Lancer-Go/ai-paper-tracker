1 简介Introduction

变形金刚的崛起The rise of Transformers(77)，纯粹基于注意力的架构，彻底改变了机器学习和人工智能的用例，从特定于任务的模型转向通用系统, pure attention-based architectures, has revolutionized the use cases of machine learning and AI, moving from task-specific models to general-purpose systems(16; 37)。 Softmax Attention是Transformers的骨干，也是主要负责序列混合的组件，充当无损内存模块，缓存序列中的每一个token，使其内存大小随着序列长度线性增长. Softmax attention, the backbone of Transformers and the component mainly responsible for sequence mixing, acts as a lossless memory module that caches every single token in the sequence, so that its memory size grows linearly with sequence length(61; 14; 10)。虽然这使得注意力非常适合检索，但不断增长的记忆会带来与序列长度相关的二次计算成本，从而限制了模型的长上下文处理。. While this makes attention well suited to retrieval, the growing memory comes with a quadratic computational cost with respect to the sequence length, limiting the model’s long-context handling.

为了克服 Transformer 的二次成本及其在需要顺序推理的任务上的有限表达能力，现代循环神经网络近年来重新流行起来To overcome the quadratic cost of Transformers, as well as their limited expressivity on tasks that require sequential reasoning, modern recurrent neural networks have re-gained popularity in recent years(38; 35; 73; 72; 12)。与内存不断增长的 Transformer 相比，现代 RNN 采用固定大小的内存（也称为隐藏状态）来压缩上下文，以便每个标记的解码成本是恒定的，并且总体计算成本与序列长度成线性或次二次方。然而，这种效率是有代价的：将无界上下文压缩为固定大小的状态限制了 RNN 的长度外推能力（它们泛化到超出训练中看到的序列长度的能力），从而限制了它们理解长上下文的潜力. In contrast to Transformers with their growing memory, modern RNNs employ a fixed-size memory (a.k.a. a hidden state) to compress the context, so that the decoding cost per token is constant and the overall computational cost is linear or sub-quadratic in the sequence length. This efficiency, however, comes at a price: compressing an unbounded context into a fixed-size state limits the length extrapolation ability of RNNs—their ability to generalize to sequence lengths beyond those seen in training—and thus their potential for long-context understanding(3; 41; 31; 75).

大量工作试图通过（元学习）内存初始化来缩小这一差距A substantial body of work has sought to close this gap through (meta-learning) memory initialization(72; 12)，更稳健的更新规则, more robust update rules(65; 79; 8)，以及更强大的内存架构, and more powerful memory architectures(72; 12; 86)。尽管取得了这些进步，循环模型仍然难以充分利用其记忆容量，我们认为一个关键原因是. Despite these advances, recurrent models still struggle to make full use of their memory capacity, and we argue that a key reason is当when，而不仅仅是信息如何写入。因为内存是在线写入的，所以早期令牌在状态几乎为空时到达：它们几乎没有面临容量竞争，并且几乎没有压缩压力，因此它们可以自由地占据内存自由度的不成比例的份额。相比之下，后来的令牌必须被压缩到剩余的容量中，并且写入它们会越来越多地覆盖或干扰已经存储的内容。结果是内存偏向初始标记的不平衡, not just how, information is written. Because the memory is written online, early tokens arrive when the state is nearly empty: they face little competition for capacity and are under almost no pressure to compress, and so they are free to occupy a disproportionate share of the memory’s degrees of freedom. Later tokens, by contrast, must be squeezed into whatever capacity remains, and writing them increasingly overwrites or interferes with what is already stored. The result is an imbalance in which the memory is biased toward initial tokens(4; 9)并且努力融入后来的上下文——这正是长上下文任务最强调的机制。and struggles to incorporate later context—precisely the regime that long-context tasks stress most.

The design directly targets the imbalance above. Since the problem originates with early tokens being under-compressed, we deliberately impose a capacity bottleneck early in the sequence, forcing the model to summarize the initial context rather than memorize it; and since later tokens suffer from interference, we progressively unlock fresh, previously unused capacity as the context grows, giving new information room to be written without overwriting what came before. In this way, the two mechanisms—an early bottleneck and fresh capacity over time—address the two failure modes of static memory in tandem. 7 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced增量记忆激活incremental memory activation，长上下文序列建模的简单范例，其中, a simple paradigm for long-context sequence modeling in which the有效的effective能力在环境中逐渐扩展，而不是从一开始就静态地暴露。 我们实例化了这个想法capacity is progressively expanded over the context rather than exposed statically from the start. We instantiated this idea in变形杆菌Proteus，一种轻量级的分块门控机制，可限制对内存的活动子集的读取和写入，并随着时间的推移解锁新容量，并且通过嵌套学习视图，将相同的原理应用于模型的参数。 跨越语言建模和常识推理的四种架构（SWLA、Comba、Titans 和 Hope-Attention），以及大海捞针、检索和 LongBench 等我们详细评估的记忆主干，, a lightweight, block-wise gating mechanism that restricts both reads and writes to an active subset of the memory and unlocks fresh capacity over time, and, through the Nested Learning view, applies the same principle to a model’s parameters. Across four architectures (SWLA, Comba, Titans, and Hope-Attention) on language modeling and commonsense reasoning, and on needle-in-a-haystack, retrieval, and LongBench for the memory backbones we evaluate at length,变形杆菌Proteus持续提高长上下文鲁棒性和长度外推，最大增益出现在最长上下文长度处。 单一的、与架构无关的机制有助于跨越四种不同的内存设计，这表明其好处源于如何在上下文中分配容量，而不是来自任何一种架构：将容量视为静态是次优的，而对其进行调度是一种实用且广泛适用的长上下文计算工具。consistently improves long-context robustness and length extrapolation, with the largest gains appearing at the longest context lengths. That a single, architecture-agnostic mechanism helps across four distinct memory designs suggests the benefit stems from how capacity is allocated over the context, not from any one architecture: treating capacity as static is suboptimal, and scheduling it is a practical and broadly applicable tool for long-context computation.
