摘要：Abstract:

自动驾驶技术有潜力减少每年因人为失误造成的大量道路交通事故，但它也带来了新类型的风险，需要从技术、道德和法规方面进行评估。根据美国国家公路交通安全管理局 (NHTSA) 的公开碰撞数据、加州机动车辆管理局 (DMV) 的脱离报告、麻省理工学院道德机器数据集以及五个司法管辖区的比较监管分析，我们发现技术故障模式的主要类型是感知和分类错误。这些在已报告的事故中占比较大，可以得出这样的结论：自动驾驶汽车决策存在不同的伦理框架，不同领域的法规不一致增加了广泛应用的不确定性。一般来说，技术、伦理和监管问题密切相关，需要共同解决。因此，本文建议采用一种更具适应性和合作性的治理方法，将工程标准、伦理讨论和机构监督相结合。Autonomous driving technology has the potential to reduce the large number of road traffic accidents caused by human error each year, but it also brings new types of risks that need to be evaluated from the aspects of technology, ethics and regulations. Based on public crash data from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), disengagement reports from the California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV), the MIT Moral Machines dataset, and a comparative regulatory analysis of five jurisdictions, we have found that the main types of technical failure modes are perception and classification errors. These account for a relatively large proportion of the reported accidents, and it can be concluded that there are different ethical frameworks for autonomous vehicle decision-making, and inconsistent regulations in different areas increase the uncertainty of widespread application. Generally speaking, the problems of technology, ethics and regulation are closely related and need to be solved together. Therefore, this paper recommends a more adaptive and cooperative governance approach that combines engineering standards, ethical discussion, and institutional supervision.

一、简介1. Introduction

自动驾驶技术的发展是现代交通生命周期中最重要的技术变革之一。过去 10 年里，Waymo、Cruise 和特斯拉等公司以及传统汽车制造商花费了数百亿美元试图制造能够自行应对复杂路况的车辆。截至 2025 年，全球自动驾驶汽车市场估计将达到 540 亿美元，预计到 2030 年估值将超过 3000 亿美元（Statista，2024）。然而，在技术、道德和监管这三个关键领域存在突出风险，需要认真评估，直到我们确信自动驾驶汽车的广泛引入或使用。Autonomous driving technology development is one of the most important tech evolutions in our modern transportation lifetime. Over the past 10 years, companies including Waymo, Cruise and Tesla, along with legacy automotive manufacturers have spent tens of billions attempting to build what are essentially vehicles that can handle complex road conditions on their own. The global market for autonomous vehicles has been estimated to be in the range of $54 billion as of 2025, with forecasts suggesting a valuation exceeding $300 billion by 2030 (Statista, 2024) Nevertheless, there are outstanding risks in three key areas: technical, ethical, as well as regulatory that need to be seriously evaluated until we can be assured of the widespread introduction or use of autonomous vehicles.

因此，这种评估的动机既紧迫又全面。具体而言，自动驾驶的前景植根于安全性：据估计，美国 94% 的严重机动车事故是由人为错误造成的As a result, the motivation for such assessment is both urgent and comprehensive. To be specific, the promise of autonomous driving is anchored in safety: human error is responsible for an estimated 94 percent of serious motor vehicle crashes in the United

各州（NHTSA，2023）。据世界卫生组织估计，全世界每年约有 135 万人因道路交通伤害死亡，还有数千万人遭受非致命伤害，往往导致长期残疾（世界卫生组织，2023）。自动驾驶汽车的支持者指出，算法系统比人类驾驶员更不易犯错，并相信它们可以大幅减少这些数字；每年挽救数十万人的生命。这种功利主义的计算，更少的死亡、更少的伤害、更少的经济损失，是这项技术的道德和经济基础。States (NHTSA, 2023). Worldwide, the World Health Organization estimates approximately 1.35 million human deaths each year due to road traffic injuries with tens of millions more suffering non-fatal injuries that often result in long-term disability (World Health Organization, 2023). Supporters of self-driving vehicles point to how algorithmic systems are less fallible than human drivers, and believe that they could reduce these numbers dramatically; saving hundreds of thousands of lives every year. This utilitarian calculus, fewer deaths, fewer injuries, fewer economic losses, is the moral and economic basis for this technology.

然而，自动驾驶汽车的部署不仅仅消除了风险，相反，它还重新分配和改变了必要风险的表达方式。自动驾驶系统的新故障模式，例如传感器退化、软件错误、对抗性漏洞、算法偏差和与人类道路使用者的不可预测的交互，带来的风险类别与人类驾驶员熟悉的风险有本质上的不同。如果技术要兑现其安全承诺，而不是成为一种新颖的、潜在的系统性危害源，那么就必须量化风险，并尽快通过监管或实践来减轻新出现的风险。However, the deployment of AVs does not just remove risk, instead, it reallocates and transforms how that necessary risk is expressed. New failure modes from self-driving systems, such as sensor degradation, software errors, adversarial vulnerabilities, algorithmic bias and unpredictable interactions with human road users pose classes of risk that are qualitatively different from the familiar ones from human drivers. If technology is to redeem its safety promise and not become a novel, potentially systemic source of harm, then risks must be quantified, and the emergent risks mitigated by regulation or practice as soon as possible.

最紧迫的是围绕技术故障的问题。自动驾驶汽车依赖于一组传感器，包括摄像头、LiDAR（光检测和测距）、雷达和超声波。每一种都有其自身的局限性。 2018 年 10 月，一辆 Uber ATG 测试车在亚利桑那州坦佩发生致命事故，引发了一系列感知失败的悲剧（美国国家运输安全委员会，2019 年）。除了感知之外，深度神经网络对对抗性扰动的敏感性还增加了当前架构尚未面临的网络安全问题（Goodfellow 等人，2015）。The most pressing are issues surrounding technical failure. Autonomous vehicles rely on a set of sensors, consisting of cameras, LiDAR (light detection and ranging), radar and ultrasound. Each one has its own limitations. A tragedy of cascading perception failures was enacted in the October 2018 fatal crash of an Uber ATG test vehicle in Tempe, Arizona (National Transportation Safety Board 2019). In addition to perception, the sensitivity of deep neural networks to adversarial perturbations adds a cybersecurity aspect that has yet been confronted by current architectures (Goodfellow et al., 2015).

自动驾驶引发了一些完全独特且道德的自然问题。系统如何分配责任的算法很像古代道德哲学问题的计算版本：一辆电车正驶向轨道上的五个人，但它可以转移到另一条只有一个人的轨道上。麻省理工学院道德机器实验对来自 233 个国家的数百万参与者进行了调查，结果表明，不同文化对此类场景的道德偏好差异很大，并表明单一的通用道德框架不太可能被压缩到 AV 决策系统中（Awad 等人，2018）。历史注释：研究伦理的原因之一是，伦理问题不仅限于交通事故场景，还包括算法偏差，包括某些研究中指出的基于性别或种族的行人检测的显着差异（Wilson 等人，2019），以及监控、数据隐私、自动驾驶汽车技术取代专业驾驶员以及获得自动驾驶移动服务的不平等等问题。Autonomous driving leads to questions of nature both utterly unique and moral. 鉴于当前技术、伦理和监管问题相互交织的情况，本文旨在实现三个目标。首先，通过有组织的研究，识别自动驾驶系统的主要技术故障原因，并在此基础上通过实际碰撞数据和脱离报告找出当前感知和决策路径的缺陷。其次，它考察了自动驾驶汽车运行过程中出现的道德问题，并探讨了在实际实践中为解决这些问题而提出的道德体系及其文化背景。第三，比较了美国、欧盟国家、中国和日本等国家的治理体系，指出了一些比较显着的细小差异，以及建立更加一致的治理体系的可能途径。Given the current situation of intertwined technical, ethical and regulatory problems, this paper aims to achieve three goals. First, by way of an organized study, the main technical fault reasons in autonomous driving systems will be identified, and based on this, deficiencies in the current perception and decision pathways will be identified through actual crash data and disengagement reports. Second, it examines the moral problems that arise during the operation of autonomous vehicles and explores ethical systems that have been proposed to address these issues in actual practice, as well as their cultural backgrounds. Third, it compares the governance systems of the United States, countries in the European Union, China and Japan, etc., and points out some small differences that are quite significant, as well as possible ways to build more consistent governance systems.

包罗万象的方法是必要的，因为技术、伦理和法律方面需要解决的问题不会单独解决。在不完善的法规下驾驶技术安全的车辆对社会来说仍然是危险的，尽管一些支持严格法规的人相信技术会及时解决所有问题，但仍然存在某些基本的技术限制，仅靠创新无法解决。本文旨在通过整合所有这些领域的结果来提供对自动驾驶风险的更全面的看法，而不是需要结合起来才能充分理解这项技术的各个孤立的观点。An all-encompassing method is necessary because the problems that need to be solved in terms of technology, ethics and laws will not be resolved individually. Operating a technically safe vehicle under faulty regulations is still dangerous for society, and although there is a belief among some people who support strict regulations that technology will solve all problems in time, there are still certain fundamental technical limitations that innovation alone cannot address. This paper aims to offer a more all-round view of autonomous driving risk by integrating the results from all these areas, rather than the individual siloed views that will need to be combined in order to fully understand this technology.

随着自动驾驶技术的快速发展，工程、计算机科学、哲学、法律和公共政策的许多领域都受到影响，并进行了大量的研究。本节在之前的研究中介绍了为当前风险评估提供知识基础的四个领域。With the rapid development of autonomous driving technology, many areas in engineering, computer science, philosophy, law and public policy have been affected and numerous studies have been conducted. 2.1 自动驾驶汽车安全研究基础2.1 Foundations of Autonomous Vehicle Safety Research

对自动驾驶汽车安全性的首次研究为我们理解该问题奠定了基础，并确立了一定水平的系统可靠性的要求。关于机器人汽车发展的最早的技术介绍之一，描述了当时的传感器架构、概率推理方法和机器学习技术，也出现在简短的内容中The first study of autonomous vehicle safety has laid the foundation for our understanding of the problem and established the requirements for a certain level of system reliability. One of the first technical introductions to the development of robotic cars, which described the architecture of sensors, probabilistic reasoning methods and machine learning techniques at that time, also appeared in a short

Thrun (2010) 根据 DARPA Grand Challenge 和 Urban Grand Challenge 参赛者的经验提供了教程。他的工作表明，自动驾驶问题实际上是一个不确定性鲁棒感知和规划问题，至今仍然是技术研究的焦点之一。tutorial provided by Thrun (2010) based on the experience of competitors in the DARPA Grand Challenge and Urban Grand Challenge. His work indicated that the autonomous driving problem is in fact a problem of uncertainty-robust perception and planning, which remains one of the focuses of technical research to this day.

Kalra 和 Paddock（2016）是兰德公司报告中最先指出这一问题的人之一：从统计角度来看，不可能证明自动驾驶汽车在公共道路上是安全的。预计自动驾驶车队需要行驶数千万甚至数亿至数千亿英里，才能以统计确定性与人类驾驶员进行比较。因此，为了满足安全案例结构中基于仿真的测试和形式化验证的需求，出现了新的验证手段。 Kalra 和 Paddock 的研究是迄今为止 AV 安全领域经常被引用的著作之一，并且它继续为部署的最低可接受证据标准提供参考。Kalra and Paddock (2016) were one of the first to point out this problem in the RAND Corporation's report: statistically speaking, it would be impossible to demonstrate that autonomous vehicles are safe on public roads. It is expected that a fleet of autonomous vehicles will need to drive tens or even hundreds of millions to hundreds of billions of miles before it can be shown with statistical certainty compared with human drivers. Therefore, in order to meet the demands of simulation-based testing and formal verification in the structure of safety cases, new validation means have appeared. The Kalra and Paddock study is one of the frequently cited works in the field of AV safety to this day, and it continues to provide a reference for the minimum acceptable evidentiary standard for deployment.

Koopman和Wagner（2017）以此为基础，解决基于机器学习的大规模先进系统的安全验证问题。他们认为当前的汽车安全标准（例如 ISO 26262）基于传统汽车的确定性行为，因此不适合由具有数十亿参数的深度神经网络做出决策的系统。他们提出的框架包括 ODD 规范、边缘情况识别方法和持续的部署后监控；迄今为止，这些想法已被领先组织和监管机构采用。Koopman and Wagner (2017) have taken the above as a foundation to address the problem of safety verification for large-scale advanced systems based on machine learning. They thought that the current automotive safety standards, such as ISO 26262, were based on the deterministic behaviour of traditional cars and thus not suitable for systems where decisions are made by deep neural networks with billions of parameters. The framework they proposed includes ODD specification, edge-case identification methodology and continuous post-deployment monitoring; these ideas have been employed by the leading organizations and regulatory authorities to this day.

同样在2017年，Shalev-Shwartz、Shammah和Shashua提出了名为责任敏感安全（RSS）的官方数学安全模型，并证明在上述假设下，自动驾驶车辆的规划决策永远不会导致危险情况。Also in 2017, Shalev-Shwartz, Shammah and Shashua put forward an official mathematical safety model called Responsibility-Sensitive Safety (RSS), and it has been demonstrated that under the above assumptions, the planning decisions of an autonomous vehicle will never lead to a dangerous situation.

RSS是一种给人类合理的安全保证的方式，否则它只是一个机器学习系统的统计报表。尽管众所周知，但批评者指出，该模型中对其他道路使用者行为的假设可能过于简单，无法充分考虑混合交通条件的所有情况（Pek、Manzinger、Koschi 和 Althoff，2020）。RSS is a way to give humans a reasonable assurance of safety, otherwise it would only be a statistical statement from a machine learning system. Although it is well-known, critics have pointed out that the assumptions made about the behaviour of other road users in this model may be too simple and cannot fully consider all circumstances of mixed-traffic conditions (Pek, Manzinger, Koschi, & Althoff, 2020).

2.2 技术故障模式和感知漏洞2.2 Technical Failure Modes and Perception Vulnerabilities

人们对自动驾驶汽车的特定技术故障模式进行了大量的研究。 Goodfellow、Shlens 和 Szegedy（2015）最先引入了对抗性示例的概念，这些示例是对输入数据的少量添加，可能导致神经网络高置信度地犯错误。研究表明，即使图像中的微小变化或微小干扰也可以被良好的人工智能分类器识别。因此，我们不确定用于自动驾驶的基于视觉的感知系统在安全性方面是否强大。A considerable amount of research has been carried out on the particular technical failure modes of AV. Goodfellow, Shlens and Szegedy (2015) were the first to introduce the idea of adversarial examples, which are small additions to the input data that can cause neural networks to make mistakes with high confidence. Research has shown that even a small change or a small disturbance in an image can be recognised by a good AI classifier. Therefore, we are not sure if the vision-based perception system for autonomous driving will be robust in terms of safety.

Eykholt等人（2018）证明上述问题在现实世界中确实存在；也就是说，路标的微小添加很容易使深度学习模型在实际操作中将停车标志识别为限速标志。从本文可以看出，自动驾驶车辆的感知管道很容易受到恶意攻击和其他自然视觉噪声的破坏。Eykholt and others (2018) have shown that the above problems actually exist in the real world; that is, a small addition to the road sign is prone to making the deep learning model identify a stop sign as a speed limit sign during actual operation. It can be seen from this paper that the perception pipeline of autonomous vehicles is vulnerable to damage by malicious attacks and other natural visual noise.

对传感器问题的研究最近才开始。 Petit、Stottelaar、Feiri 和 Kargl（2015）发现低成本激光设备可用于欺骗 LiDAR 系统并向车辆的环境模型添加虚假对象。 Cao等人（2019）开发了一些高科技激光雷达欺骗攻击，可以欺骗多传感器融合系统，并且事实证明，即使使用多种类型的传感器，也无法完全防止对抗性威胁。Research on the problems of sensors has only recently begun. Petit, Stottelaar, Feiri and Kargl (2015) have found that low-cost laser equipment can be used to spoof LiDAR systems and add false objects to the environmental model of a vehicle. Cao and others (2019) have developed some high-tech LiDAR spoofing attacks that can deceive multi-sensor fusion systems, and it has been shown that even with the use of multiple types of sensors, adversarial threats cannot be fully prevented.

Amodei 等人（2016）研究了分布转移问题，即部署环境和训练数据之间的差异；他们还提出了人工智能安全的五个必要方向：预防不良后果、避免奖励黑客、可扩展的监督、安全探索和对分配转移的鲁棒性。该模型的结构适用于自动驾驶汽车，但由于训练数据大多是在良好的天气条件下，因此在恶劣天气和弱光情况下可能表现不佳。Amodei and others (2016) have investigated the problem of distributional shift, which is the difference between the deployment environment and the training data; they have also put forward the five required directions for AI safety: prevention of adverse consequences, avoidance of reward hacking, scalable oversight, safe exploration and robustness to distributional shift. The structure of this model is applicable to self-driving cars, but since the training data are mostly in good weather conditions, they may perform poorly in adverse weather and low-light situations.

在 NTSB 2019 年对 2018 年 Uber ATG 事故的调查中，一些有关这一技术故障链原因的详细信息被公开。报道称，虽然感知系统在碰撞前约六秒检测到行人的存在，但它不断改变物体的分类为“车辆”、“自行车”和“未知”，因此预测模块和规划模块都无法生成合理的避让计划。报道称，Uber禁用了沃尔沃原厂安装的紧急制动系统，而且安全文化也存在问题。In the 2019 investigation by the NTSB into the 2018 Uber ATG accident, some detailed information about the cause of this technical failure chain was released to the public. According to the report, although the perception system detected the presence of a pedestrian about six seconds before a collision, it kept changing the classification of the object among "vehicle", "bicycle" and "unknown", and thus neither the prediction module nor the planning module was able to generate a reasonable avoidance plan. According to the report, Uber has disabled the Volvo's factory-installed emergency braking system and also has a problem with the safety culture.

2.3 算法决策的道德框架2.3 Ethical Frameworks for Algorithmic Decision-Making

自动驾驶汽车存在诸多伦理问题，备受学术界和社会关注。 Bonnefon、Shariff 和 Rahwan（2016）是最早对 2010 年代初期公众对自动驾驶汽车的态度进行实证研究的人之一。鉴于他们的结果所面临的社会困境，尽管大多数人愿意接受功利的自动驾驶汽车的想法，这种汽车会牺牲乘客的便利来拯救更多的行人，但用户却非常不愿意乘坐自我保护的车辆。因此，对于生产者和政府来说，都存在一个根本性的问题。There are many ethical problems in self-driving cars that have drawn much attention from academia and society. Bonnefon, Shariff and Rahwan (2016) were among the first to conduct empirical research on public attitudes towards self-driving cars in the early 2010s. In light of the social dilemma in their results, although most people were willing to accept the idea of utilitarian self-driving cars that would sacrifice the convenience of passengers to save more pedestrians, users were found to be highly reluctant to ride in self-protecting vehicles. Therefore, there is a fundamental problem for both the producer and the government.

麻省理工学院的道德机器实验（Awad 等，2018）将这个问题提升到了一个不同的水平，并从世界各地的人们那里收集了大约 4000 万个答案；它已发表在《自然》杂志上。结果表明，人们普遍存在为了动物的生命而牺牲人类生命或为了少数人的利益而牺牲人类生命的道德倾向，并且基于年龄和社会地位对某些群体也存在显着的跨文化差异和偏见。根据该数据，已The MIT Moral Machine experiment (Awad et al., 2018) took this issue to a different level and collected roughly 40 million answers from people around the world; it has since been published in Nature. The results show that there is a general moral tendency to sacrifice human life for the lives of animals or the many for the sake of the few, and there are also significant cross-cultural differences and prejudices against certain groups based on age and social status. Based on this data, it has been

得出的结论是，没有一个可以被普遍接受的 AV 道德体系；因此，世界范围内很难形成统一的标准。concluded that there is no single ethical system for AV that can be universally accepted; thus, it is difficult to form a uniform standard for the world.

Nyholm和Smids（2016）在研究中指出，电车问题的框架不适合实际驾驶的情况；例如，结果缺乏确定性，这是一个随着时间的推移而持续存在的问题，而不是突然的二选一情况，并且它通常属于概率风险管理而不是确定性损害划分。有人提出，为了实现自动驾驶汽车的伦理设计，相应的风险应该公平地分配给所有道路使用者，并且不应该有特定的规则来规定二元牺牲场景。Nyholm and Smids (2016) have pointed out in their studies that the framing of the trolley problem is not suitable for the circumstances of actual driving; for example, there is a lack of certainty in the results, it is a continuous problem over time rather than a sudden two-choice situation, and it generally falls under probabilistic risk management rather than deterministic division of harm. It has been proposed that to achieve the ethical design of self-driving cars, the corresponding risks should be distributed among all road users equitably, and there should be no specific rules dictating a binary sacrifice scenario.

Lin（2016）提出自动驾驶伦理不应仅解决严重交通事故的观点。许多技术设计决策，例如如何紧急制动、与后面的车辆保持多远的距离以及如何绕过骑车人，实际上都是关于不同道路使用者愿意承担的风险程度的道德选择。日常生活中影响所有人风险分配的设计决策比偶尔出现的困境更为严重。Lin (2016) put forward the idea that AV ethics should not only address serious traffic accidents. Many technical design decisions, such as how to urgently brake, how far back to keep from a following vehicle, and how to navigate around cyclists, are in fact moral choices about the degree of risk that different road users are willing to take. The design decisions in daily life that affect the distribution of risk for all people are more serious than occasional dilemma situations.

2017年，德国自动和互联驾驶道德委员会发布了第一套政府道德准则；它列出了20条基本原则，例如事故算法不应基于人的个人属性、人的生命必须优先于财产损失、批准产生风险的技术的一方应比仅使用该技术的人承担更多的责任等。这就是欧洲制定法规的原因。In 2017, the German Ethics Commission on Automated and Connected Driving released its first set of governmental ethical guidelines; it listed 20 basic principles, such as that the accident algorithm should not be based on people's personal attributes, human life must be given priority over property damage, and the party that approves risk-generating technology should bear more responsibility than the person only using it. These are the reasons why Europe has built its regulations.

Fagnant和Kockelman（2015）最先对自动驾驶进行了广泛研究，发现在美国全面实施自动驾驶汽车每年将节省约1.3万亿美元的事故、生产和能源成本等造成的经济损失。不过，他们也指出了该政策的一些问题，如责任划分、网络安全、数据隐私等。Fagnant and Kockelman (2015) were the first to study AV widely and found that fullscale implementation of self-driving cars in the United States would save around $1.3 trillion each year in economic losses from accidents, production and energy costs, etc. However, they have also pointed out some problems with the policy, such as liability division, cybersecurity, data privacy, etc.

Glancy（2015）认为联邦政府应该制定车辆安全标准，但各省市也负责处理登记、许可、交通法规等；不然的话，如果各地都有自己的规矩的话，情况就会变得更加混乱。需要指出的是，欧盟人工智能法案（2024）认为自动驾驶汽车属于高风险人工智能系统，将受到严格的合格评定、人类监督等（欧洲议会2024）。 Stilgoe（2018）认为，以负责任的方式，自动驾驶技术的发展应该解决这一过程中人们的生命风险和社会价值观。Glancy (2015) believes that the federal government should set up vehicle safety standards, but provinces and municipalities are also responsible for dealing with registration, licensing, traffic laws, etc.; otherwise, if each place has its own rules, the situation will become even more chaotic. It should be pointed out that the EU AI Act (2024) considers autonomous vehicles to be high-risk AI systems and will be subject to strict conformity assessment, human supervision, etc. (European Parliament 2024). Stilgoe (2018) believes that, in a responsible manner, the development of autonomous driving technology should address both the risks for people's lives and the values of society in the process.

Marchant和Lindor（2012）曾预测自动驾驶汽车会出现侵权责任问题，并预计现有的涵盖设计缺陷和未能警告的产品责任体系需要进行改革。 Geistfeld（2017）提出了一个基于过失标准的体系，即Marchant and Lindor (2012) had predicted that there would be problems with tort liability and expected that the existing product liability system, covering design defects and failure to warn, would need to be reformed for autonomous vehicles. Geistfeld (2017) proposed a system based on the negligence standard, that is to say,

应该使用功能正常的自主系统的合理操作，而不是传统的人类驾驶员合理人标准。instead of the traditional reasonable-person standard for human drivers, the reasonable operation of a properly functioning autonomous system should be used.

简而言之，这篇文献综述表明，技术的发展已经超过了伦理协议、法规和经验安全数据的进步，这就是现在进行这种综合分析的原因。In short, this literature review shows that the development of technology has exceeded the progress of ethical agreement, regulations and empirical safety data, which is the reason for conducting this comprehensive analysis now.

3.2 说明性假设示例：孤立风险评估与整体风险评估3.2 Illustrative Hypothetical Example: Siloed versus Holistic Risk Assessment

为了说明综合风险评估方法的价值，这里有一个简化的自动驾驶场景。一辆自动驾驶汽车在城市环境中夜间行驶并接近人行横道。感知系统检测到车辆路径附近的物体，但低照明和部分遮挡会降低分类置信度。因此，系统无法足够确定地确定该物体是行人、静止物体还是未知障碍物。对场景的延迟解释导致反应迟缓和险些发生碰撞事件。To illustrate the value of an integrated risk assessment approach, here is a simplified autonomous driving scenario. An autonomous vehicle is operating at night in an urban environment and approaches a pedestrian crosswalk. The perception system detects an object near the vehicle’s path, but low illumination and partial occlusion reduce classification confidence. As a result, the system is unable to determine with sufficient certainty whether the object is a pedestrian, a stationary object, or an unknown obstacle. The delayed interpretation of the scene leads to a late response and a near-collision event.

在这种情况下，孤立评估将主要关注立即的技术故障。该事件可能归因于弱光感知的限制、训练数据不足、传感器融合不足或分类阈值不适当。虽然这些技术改进很重要，但它们只解决了问题的一个方面。道德评估将强调保护弱势道路使用者，特别是行人的必要性。然而，如果技术系统无法首先可靠地识别弱势个体，道德目标就无法有效实现。同样，监管评估可能要求事件报告或对夜间操作施加限制，但如果不清楚了解根本的技术原因，这些措施可能还不够。In this situation, a siloed assessment would primarily focus on the immediate technical failure. The incident may be attributed to limitations in low-light perception, insufficient training data, inadequate sensor fusion, or inappropriate classification thresholds. While these technical improvements are important, they address only one dimension of the problem. An ethical assessment would emphasize the need to protect vulnerable road users, particularly pedestrians. However, ethical objectives cannot be effectively implemented if the technical system is unable to reliably identify vulnerable individuals in the first place. Similarly, a regulatory assessment might require incident reporting or impose restrictions on night-time operation, yet such measures may be insufficient without a clear understanding of the underlying technical causes.

相比之下，整体评估考虑技术、道德和监管因素之间的相互作用。从技术角度来看，该事件凸显了在分类置信度较低时改进低光感知、不确定性感知决策以及故障安全制动策略的必要性。从道德角度来看，即使在不确定的情况下，当可能存在弱势道路使用者时，系统也应采取预防行为。从监管角度来看，该事件表明需要明确定义的操作设计领域限制、安全关键的未遂事件的报告要求、感知相关软件更新的验证程序以及开发商、运营商和车主之间透明的责任分配。In contrast, a holistic assessment considers the interactions among technical, ethical, and regulatory factors. From a technical perspective, the incident highlights the need for improved low-light perception, uncertainty-aware decision-making, and fail-safe braking strategies when classification confidence is low. From an ethical perspective, the system should adopt precautionary behaviour when vulnerable road users may be present, even under conditions of uncertainty. From a regulatory perspective, the event suggests the need for clearly defined operational design domain limits, reporting requirements for safety-critical near-miss events, validation procedures for perception-related software updates, and transparent liability allocation among developers, operators, and vehicle owners.

这个例子说明了为什么不应仅通过技术、道德或监管的角度来孤立地评估自动驾驶风险。如果测试、报告或问责机制不充分，同一事件可能同时代表感知失败、未能充分保护弱势道路使用者以及治理失败。通过整合这些观点，整体评估不仅可以确定风险事件的直接原因，还可以确定减少未来部署中类似事件的可能性和严重性所需的更广泛的保障措施。This example demonstrates why autonomous driving risks should not be evaluated solely through technical, ethical, or regulatory lenses in isolation. The same event can simultaneously represent a perception failure, a failure to adequately protect vulnerable road users, and a governance failure if testing, reporting, or accountability mechanisms are inadequate. By integrating these perspectives, a holistic assessment can identify not only the immediate causes of a risk event, but also the broader safeguards required to reduce the likelihood and severity of similar incidents in future deployments.

4.3 道德框架权衡4.3 Ethical Framework Trade-offs

自动驾驶决策的道德框架之间存在基本的权衡，根据我们的系统比较，没有一个框架在所有评估标准方面都明显优于其他框架。There are basic trade-offs between the ethical frameworks for autonomous driving decision-making, and no single framework clearly outperforms the others in terms of all evaluation criteria, according to our methodical comparison.

义务论（基于规则）方法Deontological (rule-based) approaches提供高内部一致性和计算易处理性。例如，诸如“绝不针对特定个人”之类的绝对禁令可以很容易地转化为确定性的决策逻辑。也就是说，在某些情况下，严格遵守规则可能会导致违反直觉的结果，因为轻微的规则违规实际上可能会防止重大的总体伤害。这导致公众对研究中这种方法的接受度较低（Lin，2016）。offer high internal consistency and computational tractability. For instance, absolute prohibitions such as “never target a specific individual” can be easily translated into deterministic decision-making logic. That said, in certain situations, strict adherence to rules can lead to counterintuitive outcomes, as minor rule violations might actually prevent significant overall harm. This has resulted in lower public acceptance of this approach in research studies (Lin, 2016).

功利主义方法Utilitarian approaches拥有更广泛的民众支持。根据道德机器数据集，总损害最小化反映了全世界普遍存在的道德直觉（Awad 等人，2018）。然而，功利主义计算存在显着的文化差异，因为不同地方的人数、年龄和社会地位的权重不同，这使得制造商很难在全球范围内使用相同的算法。have greater popular support. According to the Moral Machine dataset, aggregate damage minimization mirrors prevailing lay moral intuitions worldwide (Awad et al., 2018). However, there exists significant cultural variety in utilitarian calculus because the number of people, age, and social status are all weighted differently in different places, which makes it difficult for manufacturers to use the same algorithms globally.

基于公平的罗尔斯方法Fairness-based Rawlsian approaches优先保护最脆弱的道路使用者符合公平问题，但在计算上很困难，因为实时脆弱性评估需要超出当前感知能力的上下文推断。that prioritize the protection of the most vulnerable road users are aligned with equity concerns, but are computationally intractable, as real-time vulnerability assessment requires contextual inference beyond the capabilities of current perception.

操作数据表明，驾驶员必须在两种不利结果之间做出选择的“电车问题”情况比常规操作故障（例如传感器遮挡、绘图错误和预测错误）少见。根据工程分析，减速、路线修改或受控停止可以解决 99.5% 以上的安全关键场景，而无需强制进行伤害分配判断（Nyholm & Smids，2016）。这一结果具有实际影响，因为它意味着电车问题情景与其操作意义相比受到了不成比例的公众和学术关注，并且工程资源最好用于消除常规感知和预测失败，而这些失败是现实世界自动驾驶安全事件的绝大多数。Operational data indicate the “trolley problem” cases where the driver has to choose between two unfavourable outcomes are less common than regular operational failures, such as sensor occlusion, mapping errors, and prediction errors. Based on engineering analyses, slowdown, course modification, or controlled stopping can resolve more than 99.5% of safety-critical scenarios without necessitating forced harm-allocation judgments (Nyholm & Smids, 2016). This outcome has practical ramifications, because it implies that the trolley-problem scenarios receive a disproportionate amount of public and scholarly attention compared to their operational significance, and that engineering resources are best used to eliminate the routine perception and prediction failures that account for the great majority of real-world AV safety events.

普通的工程决策，例如速度设置、跟车距离安排、与行人的交互协议以及变道程度，对自动驾驶系统的风险特征的影响比这两个罕见的挑战更为紧迫。正如 Lin（2016）所表达的，这些“微观道德”决策通常不受公众监督，但却对安全结果产生不成比例的影响。为了最大限度地提高交通吞吐量而设计的保持更紧密跟随距离的车辆隐含地接受了更高的追尾碰撞风险。在任何潜在交叉点都非常保守地让行人的车辆可能会更安全，但代价是交通吞吐量和用户的挫败感，从而威胁到采用。Ordinary engineering decisions, such as speed setting, following distance arrangement, interaction protocols with pedestrians, and the degree of lane changing, have a far more pressing impact on the risk characteristics of autonomous driving systems than those two rare challenges. These “micro-ethical” decisions, as Lin (2016) expressed, are often invisible to public scrutiny but have a disproportionate impact on safety outcomes. A vehicle designed to maintain tighter following distances in the interest of maximizing traffic throughput implicitly accepts a higher risk of rear-end collisions. A vehicle designed to be very conservative in yielding to pedestrians at any potential crossing point may be safer, but at the expense of traffic throughput and user frustration that threatens adoption.

对以上内容进行了一些分析。首先，责任问题是造成政策差异的原因。英国模式拥有最好的架构；它建立了一种新型的法律实体，即“授权自动驾驶实体”，以承担责任并解决其他国家制造商、运营商和用户之间谁负责的模糊性。美国模式仍然最为分散，责任结果取决于事故情况、产品技术水平以及现有的产品责任规则。Some analysis has been conducted on the above. Firstly, the problem of liability is the cause of the differences in policies. The UK model has the best architecture; it established a new type of legal entity, the "Authorised Self-driving Entity", to take on liability and resolve the ambiguity of who is responsible among manufacturers, operators and users in other countries. 5. 讨论：跨域风险交互5. Discussion: Cross-Domain Risk Interactions

分析的主要结论是，技术风险、道德风险和政策风险这三类风险并不是孤立的，而是共同作用的。孤立的评估方法可能不尽如人意还有其他原因。The main conclusion of the analysis is that the three types of risks, technical, ethical and policy, are not isolated but rather work together. There are other reasons that a siloed assessment method may be less than desirable.

道德实现的技术限制Technical Limitations in Ethical Realisation。技术认知的局限性抑制了伦理学的应用。由于感知系统的错误，功利主义的伤害最小化算法将无法在弱光条件下区分 50 米外的儿童和交通锥。根据脱离数据分析，感知错误和分类错误仍然是领先系统的主要故障类型；因此，在边缘情况下进行高级伦理推理所需的技术能力仍然需要很多年的时间。遵循 Nyholm 和 Smids (2016) 的想法，工程工作是纠正习惯性的感知错误，并且仅在非常罕见的困境情况下改变行为。. The limitation of technical perception is an inhibition of ethics' application. A utilitarian harm-minimisation algorithm will not be able to distinguish between a child and a traffic cone at a distance of 50 meters in low light because of errors in the perception system. According to the analysis of disengagement data, both perception and classification errors are still the main types of failures in the leading systems; therefore, the necessary technical ability for advanced ethical reasoning in edge cases is still many years away. Following Nyholm and Smids' (2016) idea, engineering effort is to correct habitual perception errors and only change behaviour in very rare cases of dilemma.

规则越多风险越大。由于国际上没有统一的规则，一些企业选择将生产基地设立在监管宽松、成本较低的地方；其结果是，在法规和道德方面，“竞相逐底”的趋势将会加剧。欧盟对3级最高时速60公里/小时的要求相对严格，而美国在州一级有不同的规定。公司可以选择在门槛较低的地区进行制造，在那里获得运营经验，然后利用这些经验在世界各地开发各种商品；因此，该生产管辖范围内较宽松的监管已经隐含地设定了国际标准。More rules are riskier. Because there are no uniform international rules, some companies have chosen to set up their production bases in places with lax regulation and lower costs; as a result, there will be an increasing trend of a "race to the bottom" in terms of regulations and ethics. The EU is relatively strict about the maximum speed of 60km/h for Level 3, and the US has different rules at the state level. Companies may choose to manufacture in an area with a low barrier, gain operating experience there, and then adapt this experience to develop various goods around the world; thus, the looser regulation in that production jurisdiction has implicitly set an international standard.

投资修正作为市场信号。Correction of Investment as a Market Signal.2021年以来，出现了投资回调，市场也发现了其问题；在融资方面，已从 284 亿美元的高位下降至 2024 年约 10-130 亿美元，并且随着时间的推移下降了 57% 以上（PitchBook，2024）。尽管技术长尾、监管模糊和自动驾驶解决方案责任悬而未决的问题仍然难以解决，但该行业一直由资本雄厚、拥有部署记录的公司（Waymo）或政府的战略支持（百度阿波罗）引领。一些资金雄厚的竞争对手已经退出该行业（Argo AI于2022年关闭，Cruise于2023年停止运营），同时也出现了技术和监管问题。Since 2021, there has been an investment correction and the market has identified its problems; in terms of funding, it has dropped from a high of US$28.4 billion to about US$10-13 billion in 2024 and has declined by more than 57% over time (PitchBook, 2024). Although the problem of technical long-tail, regulatory ambiguity and unanswered questions about the liability of self-driving solutions are still difficult to resolve, the industry has been led by well-capitalised companies with a track record of deployment (Waymo) or by strategic support from the government (Baidu Apollo). Some well-funded competitors have exited the industry (Argo AI closed in 2022, Cruise stopped operating in 2023), and at the same time, there have been technical and regulatory problems.

六、结束语6. Concluding Remarks

本文从三个相互关联的领域对自动驾驶相关风险进行了分析审查：政策框架、道德困境和技术故障。该分析得出了几个重要的发现。An analytical review of the risk associated with autonomous driving has been offered in this paper in three interrelated domains: policy frameworks, ethical quandaries, and technical failures. Several important findings emerge from this analysis.

从技术角度来看，感知和分类错误仍然是已部署的自动驾驶车辆系统的主要故障模式。领先的平台已显示出稳步改进，可靠性每年提高约 19-25%。 Waymo 公布的数据还表明，其财产损失和伤害相关的碰撞率远低于人类驾驶基准。然而，与对致命碰撞安全性做出强有力的统计声明所需的数十亿英里相比，自动驾驶汽车的实际行驶里程仍然要少得多。因此，现在就断言自动驾驶汽车在致命事故中比人类驾驶员更安全还为时过早From a technical perspective, perception and classification errors remain the main failure modes in deployed autonomous vehicle systems. Leading platforms have shown steady improvement, with reliability increasing by about 19–25% per year. Waymo’s published data also suggests that its property-damage and injury-related crash rates are much lower than human driving benchmarks. However, autonomous vehicles still have far fewer real-world miles compared with the billions of miles needed to make strong statistical claims about fatal-crash safety. Therefore, it is still too early to conclusively say that AVs are safer than human drivers in fatal-crash

场景（Kalra 和 Paddock，2016）。 “长尾”问题仍然是一个重大的技术挑战，因为罕见和不寻常的驾驶情况使得进一步提高可靠性变得越来越困难。scenarios (Kalra & Paddock, 2016). The “long-tail” problem also remains a major technical challenge, because rare and unusual driving situations make further reliability improvements increasingly difficult.

伦理分析表明，自动驾驶汽车的决策不能以一种普遍接受的道德框架为指导。道德机器实验揭示了人们如何判断道德驾驶决策的明显文化差异，这使得设计一种在世界各地同样有效的道德算法变得困难。然而，由于与日常技术故障相比，现实生活中的道德困境场景极为罕见，因此开发人员应该更多地关注减少常见的感知和预测错误，而不是过分强调罕见的基于牺牲的场景。The ethical analysis shows that autonomous vehicle decision-making cannot be guided by one universally accepted moral framework. The Moral Machine experiment revealed clear cultural differences in how people judge ethical driving decisions, which makes it difficult to design one ethical algorithm that works equally well across the world. However, since real-life moral dilemma scenarios are extremely rare compared with everyday technical failures, developers should focus more on reducing common perception and prediction errors rather than overemphasizing rare sacrifice-based scenarios.

政策分析显示，各地规定较多，规定不一；它们在责任、测试、道德等方面有不同的标准，使得部署具有不确定性并且容易逃避监管。英国的Approved SelfDriving Entity模型是解决责任问题的一个很好的架构思想。Analysis of the policy shows that there are many different regulations in various places; they have different standards for responsibility, tests, ethics, etc., making deployment uncertain and susceptible to regulatory evasion. The UK's Approved SelfDriving Entity model is a good architectural idea to solve the problem of liability.

局限性。Limitations.有几个限制值得承认。本研究采用二手数据分析代替一手实验研究；作者没有首先进行原始传感器测试、模拟建模或人体实验。其次，为本项目分析的公开数据集（例如 NHTSA SGO、CA DMV 报告）具有众所周知的局限性，包括制造商之间的报告定义不一致、轻微事件可能漏报以及缺乏标准化严重性编码。第三，伦理分析主要基于西方哲学框架和英国学术，存在边缘化非西方民族传统的风险，而这些传统在与中国或日本相关的全球部署环境中可能高度相关。第四，技术的快速进步意味着一些评估可能会随着其他设计的创建而失效。最后，由于本文的跨领域性质，这种分析必然是广泛的，并导致在任何单个领域内缺乏深度。Several limitations warrant acknowledgment. This study uses secondary data analysis instead of primary experimental research; the authors did not do original sensor testing, simulation modeling or human-subjects experiments first. Second, the publicly available datasets analyzed for this project such as NHTSA SGO, CA DMV reports, have well-known limitations including inconsistent reporting definitions across manufacturers and potential underreporting of minor incidents, and the absence of standardized severity coding. Third, the ethical analysis is primarily situated within Western philosophical frameworks and English scholarship at risk of marginalizing non-Western ethnic traditions that can be highly pertinent in global deployment contexts associated with China or Japan. Fourth, the quick advance of technology implies a few evaluations may lapse as other designs are created. Lastly, this analysis was necessarily broad due to the cross-domain nature of this paper and has resulted in a lack of depth within any individual domain.

未来的研究应该优先考虑几个关键方向。这使得有必要对跨区域地理和作战领域的商业部署自动驾驶汽车的安全性能进行纵向实证研究，以建立足以超越风险建模的态势感知水平。与航空和医药监管一样，国际公认的安全基准的出现有助于验证（和监管一致性），这将促进进展。为了实现这一目标，必须通过整合道德心理学、文化人类学和参与式设计的多学科研究来开发基于真正的利益相关者价值观多样性的道德框架。 AV 风险：V2X 通信拓扑极大地增加了攻击面，因此 AV 风险的网络安全方面需要更多的关注。最后但并非最不重要的一点是，研究适应性监管模型以控制风险和能力始终在变化而非静态的技术非常重要。Future research should prioritize several critical directions. This makes longitudinal empirical studies that follow safety performance of commercially deployed AVs across regional geographies and operational domains necessary to establish a level of situational awareness sufficiently beyond risk modeling. As they are for aviation and pharmaceutical regulation, the emergence of internationally recognized safety benchmarks to assist with validation (and regulatory consistency) would facilitate progress. To achieve this, ethical frameworks based on genuine stakeholder values variety must be developed through multidisciplinary research that integrates moral psychology, cultural anthropology, and participatory design. AV risk: V2X communication topologies increase the attack surface massively, therefore the cybersecurity aspects of AV risk need significantly more attention. Last but not least, it is important to research adaptive models of regulation to control a technology whose risks and capabilities are always evolving, not static.

简而言之，自动驾驶是一项强大的技术，它将改变人们用汽车出行的方式；为了实现这一理想，需要建立负责任的、综合的、全面的风险治理，并且不应孤立地对待技术问题，而应将其作为社会技术系统的一部分来共同解决。In short, autonomous driving is a powerful technology that will change the way people travel in their cars; to realize this ideal responsible, integrated and comprehensive risk governance needs to be established, and the technical problems should not be treated in isolation but rather addressed together as part of a sociotechnical system.
