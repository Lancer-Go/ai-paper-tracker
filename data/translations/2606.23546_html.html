摘要Abstract

基于 Transformer 的模型支撑着现代自然语言处理，但会带来快速增长的计算和能源成本。随着模型大小和并行性的训练规模不断扩大，准确预测能耗对于可持续且具有成本意识的系统设计至关重要。我们提出了一个框架，用于对多个 GPU 上的 Transformer 训练的能耗进行建模。使用受控架构 通过扫描 BERT 模型，我们将测量到的能量与计算、内存流量和硬件效率的轻量级代理联系起来。受屋顶线模型的启发，我们的方法结合了基于加速的硬件效率因素，可以捕获张量并行性和完全分片数据并行性的影响。我们推导出一个标度律模型，可以准确预测跨异构配置的训练能量。Transformer-based models underpin modern natural language processing but incur rapidly growing computational and energy costs. As training scales in both model size and parallelism, accurately predicting energy consumption has become critical for sustainable and cost-aware system design. We present a framework for modeling the energy consumption of Transformer training on multiple GPUs. Using controlled architectural sweeps of BERT models, we relate measured energy to lightweight proxies for compute, memory traffic, and hardware efficiency. Inspired by roofline models, our approach incorporates a speedup-based hardware-efficiency factor that captures the effects of tensor parallelism and fully sharded data parallelism. We derive a scaling law model that accurately predicts training energy across heterogeneous configurations.

7 讨论Discussion

通过将训练能量分解为计算、内存流量和 执行效率，我们解释了为什么能量扩展不同于仅 FLOPs 直觉并确定影响最大的系统级因素 培训效率。我们的研究结果表明，能源效率低下不仅源于 计算强度，但也来自内存压力和并行化开销， 激发系统感知的能源模型，而不是纯粹以模型为中心的模型。By decomposing training energy into contributions from compute, memory traffic, and execution efficiency, we explain why energy scaling departs from FLOPs-only intuition and identify the system-level factors that most strongly influence training efficiency. Our results show that energy inefficiency arises not only from compute intensity, but also from memory pressure and parallelization overheads, motivating system-aware energy models rather than purely model-centric ones.

超越 FLOP 和基于运行时的扩展。Beyond FLOPs and runtime-based scaling.

在所有实验中，硬件效率是训练能量变化的主要驱动因素。虽然总计算量仍然是工作负载的必要描述 一旦考虑到利用率，它对能源的影响是强烈的次线性的。更快的配置通常以更高的瞬时功率运行，抵消了运行时间的减少，并表明浮点运算和运行时间本身都不足以代表能源成本。Across all experiments, hardware efficiency is the dominant driver of training energy variation. While total compute remains a necessary descriptor of workload size, its effect on energy is strongly sub-linear once utilization is accounted for. Faster configurations often operate at higher instantaneous power, offsetting runtime reductions and showing that neither FLOPs nor runtime alone is a sufficient proxy for energy cost.

并行性和收益递减。Parallelism and diminishing returns.

并行性引入了解决时间和能量之间的基本权衡 效率。尽管张量并行性和完全分片数据并行性降低了 运行时，它们还会产生协调、通信和同步开销 除非并行效率保持很高，否则这会增加总能量。作为一个 结果，扩展至更大的设备数量可能会因能源消耗而适得其反 如果所选策略与模型结构、硬件不匹配， 或互连制度。Parallelism introduces a fundamental trade-off between time-to-solution and energy efficiency. Although tensor parallelism and fully sharded data parallelism reduce runtime, they also incur coordination, communication, and synchronization overheads that can increase total energy unless parallel efficiency remains high. As a result, scaling to larger device counts can be counterproductive from an energy perspective if the chosen strategy is poorly matched to model structure, hardware, or interconnect regime.

架构扩展和策略选择。Architectural scaling and strategy choice.完全分片的数据并行性始终能够实现比 张量并行，特别是对于更大和更深的模型。这表明 分片参数和优化器状态通常比 跨设备划分计算。这些差异随着模型的增加而放大 规模，表明架构扩展和并行化策略必须是 共同设计用于节能培训。Fully sharded data parallelism consistently achieves lower training energy than tensor parallelism, particularly for larger and deeper models. This suggests that sharding parameters and optimizer state is often more energy efficient than partitioning computation across devices. These differences amplify with model scale, indicating that architectural scaling and parallelization strategy must be co-designed for energy-efficient training.

局限性。Limitations.我们的实证评估仅限于 BERT 式 Transformer 微调 受控的建筑扫描。虽然建模结构与 BERT 无关 具体来说，我们不会在仅解码器预训练中验证它， 专家混合模型、长上下文注意力变体或管道并行 执行；因此，较大公共模型的结果呈现为趋势水平 一致性检查而不是直接验证。Our empirical evaluation is limited to BERT-style Transformer fine-tuning under controlled architectural sweeps. While the modeling structure is not tied to BERT specifically, we do not validate it on decoder-only pretraining, mixture-of-experts models, long-context attention variants, or pipeline-parallel execution; results on larger public models are therefore presented as trend-level consistency checks rather than direct validation.

该模型还依赖于粗粒度代理来满足计算和内存需求， 选择最小的足够统计数据而不是内核级归因。 因此，拟合系数和策略特定常数应为 解释为反映沟通、同步和 执行开销。最后，加速能量联动假设运行在 固定的硬件类别和操作机制，其中平均设备功率变化更大 比执行时间慢；它不考虑具体排放量， 数据中心级影响或异构电源管理行为。The model also relies on coarse-grained proxies for compute and memory demand, chosen as minimal sufficient statistics rather than kernel-level attributions. Accordingly, fitted coefficients and strategy-specific constants should be interpreted as aggregate effects reflecting communication, synchronization, and execution overheads. Finally, the speedup energy linkage assumes operation within a fixed hardware class and operating regime, where average device power varies more slowly than execution time; it does not account for embodied emissions, data-center level effects, or heterogeneous power-management behavior.

更广泛的影响和前景。Broader implications and outlook.我们的研究结果表明，减少大规模的环境足迹 培训需要从以模型为中心的指标转向系统感知的指标 效率考虑。报告执行时间和并行效率 与 FLOP 和参数计数一起可以进行更有意义的比较 跨型号和硬件平台。未来的工作可以将该框架扩展到 多节点和异构设置，结合更细粒度的通信和 注意力内核模型，并集成碳强度感知调度以实现更多 全面的能源感知系统设计。Our findings suggest that reducing the environmental footprint of large-scale training requires a shift from model-centric metrics toward system-aware efficiency considerations. Reporting execution time and parallel efficiency alongside FLOPs and parameter counts would enable more meaningful comparisons across models and hardware platforms. Future work could extend this framework to multi-node and heterogeneous settings, incorporate finer-grained communication and attention-kernel models, and integrate carbon-intensity aware scheduling for more comprehensive energy-aware system design.

8 结论Conclusion

这项工作表明 Transformer 训练的能源成本不仅取决于 总计算量，还取决于硬件效率和并行化策略。同时 分布式训练减少了解决问题的时间，当并行扩展不完善或协调开销占主导地位时，它可以增加总能量。通过建模 训练能量作为计算、内存流量和加速派生函数的函数 硬件效率方面，我们超越了仅 FLOPs 的缩放法则，并提供了 可解释的能源预测框架。 我们的结果进一步表明，执行时间缩放可以作为有用的代理 在固定硬件类别和操作机制内提高能源效率，使 即使直接功率测量不可用，也可以进行样本外能量估计。 总体而言，该框架建议提高并行效率和利用率 与减少名义计算一样重要，为 分布式变压器培训系统的能源感知设计和评估。This work shows that the energy cost of Transformer training is governed not only by total compute, but also by hardware efficiency and parallelization strategy. While distributed training reduces time-to-solution, it can increase total energy when parallel scaling is imperfect or coordination overheads dominate. By modeling training energy as a function of compute, memory traffic, and a speedup-derived hardware-efficiency term, we move beyond FLOPs-only scaling laws and provide an interpretable framework for energy prediction. Our results further show that execution-time scaling can serve as a useful proxy for energy efficiency within a fixed hardware class and operating regime, enabling out-of-sample energy estimation even when direct power measurements are unavailable. Overall, the framework suggests that improving parallel efficiency and utilization can be as important as reducing nominal compute, offering practical guidance for the energy-aware design and evaluation of distributed Transformer training systems.
