摘要Abstract

Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and genetic algorithms (GAs) are jointly employed to generate pixelated Doherty combiner networks that integrate load modulation, impedance matching, power combining, and phase compensation into a single structure. As a proof of concept, we design and fabricate a GaN HEMT Doherty PA with a pixelated output combiner. The prototype achieves a measured peak drain efficiency of 51%–63% and a 6-dB back‑off efficiency of 48%–54% over 1.9–2.5 GHz. Within the same frequency range, the measured output power is 44±\pm0.3 dBm。此外，应用数字预失真 (DPD) 后，原型电路的邻道泄漏比 (ACLR) 优于 -53.2 dBc。0.3 dBm. Furthermore, with digital predistortion (DPD) applied, the prototype circuit demonstrates an adjacent channel leakage ratio (ACLR) better than -53.2 dBc.

I 简介Introduction

随着数据吞吐量持续大幅增加以及更复杂的调制方案的采用，功率放大器 (PA) 必须在输出功率水平显着降低的情况下保持高效率，同时还要在更宽的频率范围内运行。 Doherty PA 仍然是部署最广泛的架构之一，因为它通过简单的电路实现提供了显着的回退效率增强。与最近提出的负载调制 PA 架构相比，这一优势尤其显着As data throughput continues to increase dramatically and more complex modulation schemes are adopted, power amplifiers (PAs) must maintain high efficiency at significantly reduced output power levels while also operating over wider frequency ranges. The Doherty PA remains one of the most widely deployed architectures because it provides substantial back-off efficiency enhancement with a simple circuit implementation. This advantage is especially notable when compared with more recently proposed load‑modulated PA architectures[9, 7, 6, 5, 15]。然而，设计Doherty组合器非常具有挑战性，因为主放大器和辅助放大器直接通过该网络交互，并且这种强交互也限制了可实现的带宽。. However, designing the Doherty combiner is highly challenging because the main and auxiliary amplifiers interact directly through this network, and this strong interaction also limits the achievable bandwidth.

最近，基于像素化电路布局和深度学习的逆向设计技术已被引入射频电路设计Recently, inverse design techniques based on pixelated circuit layouts and deep learning have been introduced for RF circuit design[4]。这些方法探索任意电磁 (EM) 布局，并可以发现更能够定位全局最优设计的非直观解决方案。因此，它们已成功应用于不同频段和工艺的各种 PA 实施. These methods explore arbitrary electromagnetic (EM) layouts and can uncover non‑intuitive solutions that are more capable of locating globally optimal designs. As a result, they have been successfully applied to a variety of PA implementations across different frequency bands and processes[4, 13, 2]。在. In[14]，我们将这种方法扩展到 Doherty PA，将 Doherty 组合器视为有损且互易的双端口网络，如图 1(a) 所示。我们首先将黑盒 Doherty 设计方法与深度学习相结合，直接从负载牵引数据合成像素化三端口 Doherty 组合器。黑盒 Doherty 方法具有优势，因为它在使用对称晶体管时提供了扩展的动态范围。然而，如图 1 所示，使用这种方法合成的组合器 B 的带宽性能下降。为了解决这个限制，我们提出了一种双态阻抗综合 Doherty 设计方法, we extended this approach to the Doherty PA by treating the Doherty combiner as a lossy and reciprocal two‑port network, as illustrated in Fig. 1(a). We first combined the black‑box Doherty design method with deep learning to synthesize a pixelated three‑port Doherty combiner directly from load‑pull data. The black‑box Doherty method is advantageous because it provides an extended dynamic range when symmetric transistors are used. However, as shown in Fig. 1, the Combiner B synthesized using this approach exhibits degraded bandwidth performance. To address this limitation, we proposed a dual‑state impedance synthesis Doherty design methodology in[12]实现带宽略有改善的传统 Doherty PA（组合器 A）。然而，传统 Doherty PA 的带宽仍然有限。倒置 Doherty PA，最初提出于to realize the conventional Doherty PA (Combiner A) with slightly improved bandwidth. Nevertheless, the bandwidth of the conventional Doherty PA remains limited. The inverted Doherty PA, originally proposed in[1, 3]与组合器 C 相对应，可提供更大的带宽潜力。and corresponding to Combiner C, offers significantly greater bandwidth potential.

在本文中，我们提出了第一个由深度学习驱动的倒置 Doherty PA 设计。我们利用双态阻抗合成方法并训练深度卷积神经网络 (CNN) 作为替代模型来取代全波电磁仿真，从而使我们能够快速捕获像素化电磁结构和 S 参数之间的非线性关系。结合遗传算法 (GA)，我们成功设计并验证了 GaN HEMT Doherty PA 原型，该原型使用紧凑的像素化组合器，并在较宽的带宽内实现了出色的测量性能。In this paper, we propose the first inverted Doherty PA design driven by deep learning. We leverage the dual‑state impedance synthesis approach and train deep convolutional neural networks (CNN) as a surrogate model to replace full‑wave EM simulation, allowing us to rapidly capture the nonlinear relationships between pixelated EM structures and S‑parameters. Together with a genetic algorithm (GA), we have successfully designed and validated a GaN HEMT Doherty PA prototype that use compact pixelated combiners and achieve excellent measured performance over a wide bandwidth.

To train the deep CNN, we generated a dataset of 5000 layouts together with their EM‑simulated S‑parameters. Figure 2 illustrates the deep CNN structure with residual connections. The model takes the binary layout matrix as input and predicts the corresponding S‑parameters. The network contains twelve convolutional layers followed by five fully connected (FC) layers. Each convolution stage uses batch normalization and a leaky‑ReLU activation. After feature extraction, the output feature maps are flattened before the FC layers. Dropout regularization is applied during training to reduce overfitting.

IV 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了一种深度学习驱动的方法，用于设计具有像素化输出组合器的倒置 Doherty PA。通过将深度 CNN 代理模型与遗传算法相结合，所提出的方法能够实现紧凑、宽带 Doherty 输出网络的高效逆合成。一个This paper introduces a deep learning–driven methodology for designing an inverted Doherty PA with a pixelated output combiner. By combining a deep CNN surrogate model with a genetic algorithm, the proposed approach enables efficient inverse synthesis of a compact, wideband Doherty output network. A1.9​–​2.5​GHz1.9–2.5~\mathrm{GHz}原型验证了该方法，实现了prototype validates the method, achieving51%−63%51\%-63\%峰值效率，peak efficiency,48%−54%48\%-54\%回退效率和饱和功率back‑off efficiency, and a saturated power of44±0.3​dBm44\pm 0.3~\mathrm{dBm}。这些结果证明了基于深度学习的逆合成与像素化 EM 结构在推进宽带 Doherty PA 设计方面的巨大潜力。. These results demonstrate the strong potential of deep learning–based inverse synthesis with pixelated EM sturctuers in advancing wideband Doherty PA design.
