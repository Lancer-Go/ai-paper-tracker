DART-VLN：离散视觉语言导航的测试时内存衰减和反循环正则化DART-VLN: Test-Time Memory Decay and Anti-Loop Regularization for Discrete Vision-Language Navigation††谢谢：thanks:* 通讯作者。* Corresponding author.
代码将发布于Code will be released athttps://github.com/Japluto/DART-VLN.

Shaoheng Zhang1, Zhichen Li2, and Jie Mei1,∗
摘要Abstract

基于记忆的离散视觉语言导航（VLN）代理必须在部分可观察性下行动，但即使是强大的冻结骨干在测试时仍然容易受到攻击。两种常见的故障模式是内存读出时的陈旧历史证据和动作选择期间低效的局部回溯。我们提出了 DART-VLN，一种用于离散 VLN 的免训练测试时间控制框架。 DART-VLN 结合了测试时内存衰减（一种读取端内存重新加权规则，可抑制陈旧和冗余证据而不重写存储内容）和反循环正则化（一种轻量级下一跳惩罚，可阻止在操作选择期间立即逆转）。该框架没有引入新的可学习参数，并且保持学习的主干不变。 R2R 和 REVERIE 上的实验显示出一致的模式：仅衰减提供稳定的读取侧增益，而衰减+反循环实现最佳的整体质量效率权衡，产生更短的轨迹、更低的运行时间并提高关键设置中的导航性能。行为分析进一步证实，反循环正则化减少了局部回溯并提高了冻结主干网下的路径效率。总体而言，结果表明，适度的测试时间控制可以使基于内存的离散 VLN 更加可靠和高效，而无需重新训练。Memory-based discrete vision-language navigation (VLN) agents must act under partial observability, yet even strong frozen backbones remain vulnerable at test time. Two common failure modes are stale historical evidence at memory readout and inefficient local backtracking during action selection. We present DART-VLN, a training-free test-time control framework for discrete VLN. DART-VLN combines Test-Time Memory Decay, a read-side memory reweighting rule that suppresses stale and redundant evidence without rewriting stored content, with Anti-Loop Regularization, a lightweight next-hop penalty that discourages immediate reversals during action selection. The framework introduces no new learnable parameters and leaves the learned backbone unchanged. Experiments on R2R and REVERIE show a consistent pattern: decay-only provides stable read-side gains, while decay+anti-loop achieves the best overall quality-efficiency trade-off, yielding shorter trajectories, lower runtime, and improved navigation performance in key settings. Behavioral analysis further confirms that anti-loop regularization reduces local backtracking and improves path efficiency under frozen backbones. Overall, the results show that modest test-time control can make memory-based discrete VLN more reliable and efficient without retraining.

I 简介Introduction

语言引导的具体导航需要在部分可观察性下进行可靠的顺序决策。视觉语言导航（VLN）通过要求实体代理使用语言和视觉观察在环境中移动，为该问题提供了一个标准测试平台Language-guided embodied navigation requires reliable sequential decision-making under partial observability. Vision-language navigation (VLN) provides a standard testbed for this problem by asking an embodied agent to move through an environment using language and visual observations[2, 22]。在现有的公式中，离散 VLN 特别有吸引力，因为它在显式视点图上运行并支持可控的逐步决策. Among existing formulations, discrete VLN is especially attractive because it operates over explicit viewpoint graphs and supports controllable step-wise decision-making[19, 4, 1].

VLN 的最新进展来自于更强大的预训练导航器、显式记忆或地图表示、更广泛的培训资源以及更复杂的恢复或规划机制Recent progress in VLN has come from stronger pretrained navigators, explicit memory or map representations, broader training resources, and more sophisticated recovery or planning mechanisms[10, 3, 19, 6, 5]。总之，这些进步极大地提高了长期推理和基准性能。然而，在推理时仍然存在实际差距：即使是强大的基于内存的离散 VLN 主干在冻结参数下仍然可能表现不可靠。在许多情况下，进一步的收益依赖于再培训、架构重新设计或更重的规划模块，当目标是以最少的干预来增强已经具有竞争力的导航器时，这些模块的吸引力就不那么大了。. Together, these advances have substantially improved long-horizon reasoning and benchmark performance. Yet a practical gap remains at inference time: even strong memory-based discrete VLN backbones can still behave unreliably under frozen parameters. In many cases, further gains rely on retraining, architectural redesign, or heavier planning modules, which are less appealing when the goal is to strengthen an already competitive navigator with minimal intervention.

在这种情况下，两种重复出现的故障模式变得尤为重要。第一个出现在内存读出时。显式导航记忆通过存储先前访问过的视点、视觉特征或地图级上下文，帮助智能体推理更长的轨迹In this setting, two recurring failure modes become particularly important. The first appears at memory readout. Explicit navigation memory helps agents reason over longer trajectories by storing previously visited viewpoints, visual features, or map-level context[19, 3, 4, 1, 7, 21, 12]。然而，随着导航的进行，过时的或反复观察的证据在其有用性消失后可能仍然活跃，从而使得记忆聚合在决策时变得更加嘈杂。第二个出现在操作选择时。即使有强大的冻结主干，智能体仍然表现出低效的局部行为，例如立即逆转和短循环. Yet as navigation proceeds, stale or repeatedly observed evidence may remain active after its usefulness has faded, making memory aggregation noisier at decision time. The second appears at action selection. Even with a strong frozen backbone, agents still exhibit inefficient local behaviors such as immediate reversals and short loops[14, 18, 20]。这些行为并不总是会破坏最终结果，但它们会延长轨迹、浪费步骤并增加运行时间。. These behaviors do not always destroy the final outcome, but they lengthen trajectories, waste steps, and increase runtime.

这就引发了一个简单的问题：在不重新训练模型或重新设计导航堆栈的情况下，仅从轻量级测试时间控制中可以获得多少收益？我们用 DART-VLN 来回答这个问题，DART-VLN 是一种基于内存的离散 VLN 的免训练测试时间控制框架。总结如下图所示。This motivates a simple question: how much can be gained from lightweight test-time control alone, without retraining the model or redesigning the navigation stack? We answer this question with DART-VLN, a training-free test-time control framework for memory-based discrete VLN. II 相关工作Related Work

基于内存和映射的离散 VLN：Memory and map-based discrete VLN:显式记忆和图表示已成为稳定离散 VLN 中长视野推理的常用方法。最近的方法存储访问过的视点，聚合图形级上下文，或构建更丰富的拓扑或类似地图的状态表示以进行导航Explicit memory and map representations have become a common way to stabilize long-horizon reasoning in discrete VLN. Recent methods store visited viewpoints, aggregate graph-level context, or build richer topological or map-like state representations for navigation[19, 4, 1, 7, 21]。尽管这些方法在形式上有所不同，但它们有一个共同的目标：通过信息更丰富的外部状态来加强空间基础和历史聚合。相比之下，DART-VLN 没有引入新的内存结构、映射编码器或融合架构。相反，它在现有的显式内存管道之上运行，并专注于改进在冻结参数下的测试时使用历史证据的方式。因此，区别不在于内存是否重要，而在于改进是来自于更丰富的内存构造，还是来自于推理时对内存使用的更严格控制。. Although these approaches differ in form, they share a common goal: strengthening spatial grounding and history aggregation through a more informative external state. By contrast, DART-VLN does not introduce a new memory structure, map encoder, or fusion architecture. Instead, it operates on top of an existing explicit-memory pipeline and focuses on improving how historical evidence is used at test time under frozen parameters. The distinction is therefore not whether memory matters, but whether improvement comes from richer memory construction or from tighter control over memory usage at inference time.

回溯、倒带和本地恢复：Backtracking, rewind, and local recovery:第二行工作针对局部导航故障，例如错误转弯、回溯和短环路A second line of work targets local navigation failures such as wrong turns, backtracking, and short loops[14, 18, 20]。后悔意识和有远见的导航方法表明，局部恢复决策可以对轨迹效率产生重大影响，而更多以规划为导向的方法通过更丰富的控制机制进一步加强纠正行为。这些方法强调了局部轨迹管理的重要性，但它们通常与学习进度估计、明确的校正策略或更强大的规划模块相结合。我们的反循环模块具有相同的行为动机，但它的范围故意缩小：它在推理时充当图局部动作分数的轻量级下一跳正则化器，而不是充当规划器或学习的自我校正策略。. Regret-aware and foresighted navigation methods show that local recovery decisions can materially affect trajectory efficiency, while more planning-oriented approaches further strengthen correction behavior through richer control mechanisms. These methods highlight the importance of local trajectory management, but they are often coupled with learned progress estimation, explicit correction policies, or stronger planning modules. Our anti-loop module shares the same behavioral motivation, yet it is deliberately narrower in scope: it acts as a lightweight next-hop regularizer on graph-local action scores at inference time, rather than as a planner or learned self-correction policy.

推理时间控制与重训练改进：Inference-time control versus retraining-heavy improvement:VLN 的许多强大收益来自架构扩展、更丰富的场景建模或更重的规划模块Many strong gains in VLN come from architectural extension, richer scene modeling, or heavier planning modules[8, 6, 5]。这些方向是有效的，但当实际目标是在不重新开放训练管道的情况下改进已经具有竞争力的冻结导航器时，它们就不太合适了。最近的工作也开始探索 VLN 中的免培训或测试时干预，这反映出人们对在不进行新培训周期的情况下改进已部署导航器的兴趣日益浓厚. These directions are effective, but they are less suitable when the practical goal is to improve an already competitive frozen navigator without reopening the training pipeline. More recent work has also begun exploring training-free or test-time intervention in VLN, reflecting growing interest in improving deployed navigators without a new training cycle[17, 11]。 DART-VLN 处于这种低入侵机制中：它不引入新的可学习参数，保持学习的主干不变，并将干预限制在读取端内存控制和操作端局部正则化。从这个意义上说，我们的目标不是重新设计导航器本身，而是以轻量级和可解释的方式改进其测试时行为。. DART-VLN is positioned in this low-intrusion regime: it introduces no new learnable parameters, leaves the learned backbone unchanged, and confines intervention to read-side memory control and action-side local regularization. In this sense, our goal is not to redesign the navigator itself, but to improve its test-time behavior in a lightweight and interpretable manner.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 测试时记忆衰退Test-Time Memory Decay

Ⅲ-BIII-B1 动机Motivation

在基于记忆的导航中，并非所有历史证据在整个推理过程中都同样有用In memory-based navigation, not all historical evidence remains equally useful throughout inference[8, 7, 21]。随着轨迹的增长，一些内存槽可能会编码过时、过度重复或与当前决策相关性较弱的信息。如果这些证据继续以与新观察结果相同的强度做出贡献，那么记忆聚合在测试时可能会变得更加嘈杂且选择性较差。. As the trajectory grows, some memory slots may encode information that is outdated, overly repeated, or only weakly relevant to the current decision. If such evidence continues to contribute with the same strength as fresher observations, memory aggregation can become noisier and less selective at test time.

为了解决这个问题，我们采用保守的读端策略。我们不重写存储的内存内容，而是仅调整每个插槽在读出时的贡献程度。这保留了与基线内存结构的兼容性，同时允许在推理过程中抑制陈旧和冗余的证据。To address this issue, we adopt a conservative read-side strategy. Rather than rewriting stored memory content, we adjust only how strongly each slot contributes at readout. This preserves compatibility with the baseline memory structure while allowing stale and redundant evidence to be suppressed during inference.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 导航决策的反环正则化Anti-Loop Regularization for Navigation Decision-Making

Ⅲ-CIII-C1 动机Motivation

立即回溯是体现导航中常见的局部故障模式Immediate backtracking is a common local failure mode in embodied navigation[14, 18, 20]。即使智能体最终到达合理的目的地，重复的反转和相关的短循环也会增加路径长度、浪费步骤并降低轨迹效率。因此，这些行为很重要，不仅因为它们效率低下，还因为它们使代理面临额外的决策点，这些决策点可能会在测试时累积更多错误。. Repeated reversals and related short loops can inflate path length, waste steps, and degrade trajectory efficiency even when the agent eventually reaches a reasonable destination. These behaviors therefore matter not only because they are inefficient, but also because they expose the agent to additional decision points that may accumulate further error at test time.

这使得局部环路抑制成为轻量级推理时间控制的合适目标。在我们的设计中，反循环正则化有意保守：其目的是改善冻结骨干网下的本地路径行为，而不是充当规划器或保证所有端点指标的单调增益。This makes local loop suppression a suitable target for lightweight inference-time control. In our design, anti-loop regularization is intentionally conservative: its purpose is to improve local path behavior under frozen backbones, rather than to serve as a planner or to guarantee monotonic gains on all endpoint metrics.

Ⅲ-CIII-C3 推理范围Inference Scope

反环路模块仅在测试时应用，旨在用于确定性的、基于 argmax 的导航推理。在完整的 DART-VLN 配置中，它与读取端内存衰减相结合，并在操作选择之前应用于候选操作分数。该模块不会修改停止头、引入单独的规划组件或更改学习的主干。它的范围被故意缩小：它充当图局部动作正则化器，用于改善冻结推理下的效率侧行为。The anti-loop module is applied only at test time and is intended for deterministic, argmax-based navigation inference. In the full DART-VLN configuration, it is combined with read-side memory decay and applied to candidate action scores before action selection. The module does not modify the stop head, introduce a separate planning component, or alter the learned backbone. Its scope is deliberately narrow: it acts as a graph-local action regularizer for improving efficiency-side behavior under frozen inference.

Ⅲ-DIII-D 设计原则：保守的测试时间控制Design Principle: Conservative Test-Time Control

DART-VLN 遵循一个简单的原则：在冻结的骨干网下，适度的测试时间干预通常比积极的重写或纠正更可靠。因此，实用的主线将读取侧内存衰减与局部反循环正则化相结合，而不是依赖于导航过程的繁重操作。DART-VLN follows a simple principle: under frozen backbones, modest test-time interventions are often more dependable than aggressive rewriting or correction. Accordingly, the practical mainline combines read-side memory decay with local anti-loop regularization, rather than relying on heavier manipulation of the navigation process.

从实际推理的角度来看，这个结果很重要。浪费性的逆转会消耗额外的动作，增加运行时间，并使代理接触更多的决策点，而不必添加有用的证据。因此，减少这种振荡转变不仅可以提高效率，还可以提高执行路径的可解释性。从这个意义上说，反循环正则化的价值最好理解为保守的本地控制器，它使冻结主干推理更干净、更高效，而不是作为每个端点增益的独立解释。This result matters from a practical inference perspective. Wasteful reversals consume extra actions, increase runtime, and expose the agent to more decision points without necessarily adding useful evidence. Reducing such oscillatory transitions therefore improves not only efficiency, but also the interpretability of the executed path. In this sense, the value of anti-loop regularization is best understood as a conservative local controller that makes frozen-backbone inference cleaner and more efficient, rather than as a standalone explanation for every endpoint gain.

V 结论Conclusion

本文研究了如何通过轻量级测试时间控制来增强冻结骨干网下基于记忆的离散 VLN。 DART-VLN 将读取端内存衰减与操作端反循环正则化相结合，改进了实际质量效率权衡，而无需重新训练或架构重新设计。在 R2R 和 REVERIE 中，该方法显示出一致的模式：This paper studied how to strengthen memory-based discrete VLN under frozen backbones through lightweight test-time control. DART-VLN combines read-side memory decay with action-side anti-loop regularization, improving the practical quality-efficiency trade-off without retraining or architectural redesign. Across R2R and REVERIE, the method shows a consistent pattern:仅衰变decay-only提供稳定的读端增益，而最终provides a stable read-side gain, while the final衰减+抗环路decay+anti-loop配置可实现导航质量、轨迹效率和运行时间的最佳总体平衡。行为分析进一步表明，反循环正则化最明显的直接效果是在冻结推理下减少局部回溯和更清晰的路径执行。configuration yields the best overall balance of navigation quality, trajectory efficiency, and runtime. Behavioral analysis further shows that the clearest direct effect of anti-loop regularization is reduced local backtracking and cleaner path execution under frozen inference.

这些发现应在范围内进行解释。当前的研究以具有 GridMM 式主干的离散 VLN 基准为中心，反循环正则化的最直接证据在于局部行为和效率，而不是每个端点增益的完整因果解释。即使有这些限制，实际的信息仍然很明确：当强大的基于内存的导航器已经可用时，适度且可解释的测试时间控制仍然可以改善推理行为，而无需重新开始训练。将这种视角扩展到其他骨干网和更广泛的具体导航设置是未来工作的自然方向。These findings should be interpreted within scope. The current study is centered on discrete VLN benchmarks with a GridMM-style backbone, and the most direct evidence for anti-loop regularization lies in local behavior and efficiency rather than in a complete causal explanation of every endpoint gain. Even with these limits, the practical message is clear: when a strong memory-based navigator is already available, modest and interpretable test-time control can still improve inference behavior without reopening training. Extending this perspective to other backbones and broader embodied navigation settings is a natural direction for future work.
