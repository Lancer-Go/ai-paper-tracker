摘要Abstract

本文提出了一种可维护的混合生成架构的设计和评估，用于从旋律自动生成音乐和声。所提出的系统将重叠旋律上下文中受量子启发的候选探索与基于明确规则的优化相结合，以平衡生成灵活性和结构控制。该架构使用明确且可重复的指标进行评估，涵盖结构一致性、功能一致性、和谐相似性和鲁棒性。结果表明，所提出的方法产生的和声可以保留音调结构和韵律行为，同时允许多个有效的和声实现。此外，优化层提高了结构的连贯性、稳定性和可预测性，而无需训练语料库。该研究表明，可以在信息系统开发的背景下系统地设计和评估透明且可控的混合生成系统。This paper presents the design and evaluation of a maintainable hybrid generative architecture for automated music harmony generation from melody. The proposed system combines quantum-inspired candidate exploration over overlapping melodic contexts with explicit rule-based optimization to balance generative flexibility and structural control. The architecture is evaluated using explicit and reproducible metrics covering structural coherence, functional agreement, harmonic similarity, and robustness. The results show that the proposed approach produces harmonizations that preserve tonal structure and cadential behavior while allowing multiple valid harmonic realizations. Furthermore, the optimization layer improves structural coherence, stability, and predictability without requiring a training corpus. The study demonstrates that transparent and controllable hybrid generative systems can be systematically designed and evaluated within the context of Information Systems Development.

一、简介1. Introduction

从旋律输入生成和声结构的问题已经在多个领域进行了研究，包括音乐理论、认知科学、人工智能和计算建模。从基于数学关系[1]和毕达哥拉斯和谐概念[2]的早期和谐理论表述，到现代计算方法，和谐组织已被理解为受形式规则和感知约束支配的结构化系统。The problem of generating harmonic structures from melodic input has been studied across multiple domains, including music theory, cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and computational modeling. From early theoretical formulations of harmony based on mathematical relationships[1] and Pythagorean concepts of consonance [2], to modern computational approaches, harmonic organization has been understood as a structured system governed by both formal rules and perceptual constraints.

在传统音乐理论中，和声是根据和弦之间的功能关系来描述的，例如主音、次属音和主音[3]、[4]。这些功能关系与人类对音调稳定性和期望的感知密切相关[5]、[6]、[7]。从认知角度来看，调和处理可以解释为在约束下的决策过程，其中可能存在多种有效的替代方案[8]、[9]。In traditional music theory, harmony is described in terms of functional relationships between chords, such as tonic, subdominant, and dominant roles [3], [4]. These functional relationships are closely tied to human perception of tonal stability and expectation[5], [6], [7]. From a cognitive perspective, harmonic processing can be interpreted as a decision-making process under constraints, where multiple valid alternatives may exist [8], [9].

与此同时，人工智能领域开发了自动和声和音乐生成的方法。早期的方法包括概率模型和基于规则的系统[10]、[11]，而最近的工作重点是机器学习和深度神经网络[12]、[13]。这些方法通常旨在从大型数据集中学习统计模式并生成类似于现有音乐风格的输出。In parallel, the field of artificial intelligence has developed methods for automatic harmonization and music generation. Early approaches include probabilistic models and rule-based systems [10], [11], while more recent work focuses on machine learning and deep neural networks [12], [13]. These approaches often aim to learn statistical patterns from large datasets and generate outputs that resemble existing musical styles.

然而，纯粹的数据驱动方法在可解释性、可控性和可重复性方面存在局限性。生成的输出可能难以分析，并且内部决策过程通常不透明。从信息系统开发（ISD）的角度来看，这带来了挑战，其中透明度、可维护性和明确的评估至关重要[14]、[15]、[16]。However, purely data-driven approaches have limitations in terms of interpretability, controllability, and reproducibility. The generated outputs may be difficult to analyze, and the internal decision processes are often opaque. This creates challenges from an Information Systems Development (ISD) perspective, where transparency, maintainability, and explicit evaluation are essential[14], [15], [16].

最近的工作还探索了使用形式模型和可视化技术来更好地理解调和结构并减少认知负荷[17]，Recent work has also explored the use of formal models and visualization techniques to better understand harmonic structure and reduce cognitive load [17],

The paper demonstrates that generative systems can be systematically evaluated using explicit and reproducible metrics, including structural properties, functional agreement, and harmonic similarity.

建筑意义Architectural Implications

生成式探索和基于规则的细化之间的分离代表了一种关键的架构模式。它提高了系统的可维护性，因为每个组件都可以独立修改，并且提高了透明度，因为决策过程可以通过明确的规则进行部分解释。This separation between generative exploration and rule-based refinement represents a key architectural pattern. It improves system maintainability, as each component can be modified independently, and enhances transparency, since the decision process is partially interpretable through explicit rules.
