PAMF：不完整时间序列数据的先验多模态融合PAMF: Prior-Aware Multimodal Fusion for Incomplete Time Series Data

Ziwen Kan    Wugeng Zheng    Tianlong Chen2    Song Wang1
1Department of Computer Science, University of Central Florida
2Department of Computer Science, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
摘要Abstract

在医疗保健领域，多模式时间序列任务通常在实践中基于不完整的观察进行操作，例如，由于电极分离而导致心电图片段丢失或在夜间监测期间整个呼吸通道不可用。 这种缺失通常以两种结构上不同的模式出现：模态内缺失（在其他方式观察到的模态中不存在值）和模态级别缺失（整个模态不可用）。 现有方法通常通过掩码或缺失嵌入隐式表示未观察到的数据，而不学习特定于实例的缺失信息，并且大多数方法仅针对一种缺失模式而设计。 一种自然的方法是明确估计缺失的数据；然而，现有的插补方法尽管其结构先验不同，但对缺失的处理是统一的，并且插补过程通常与下游任务隔离，从而阻止下游任务引导插补获得更多信息的表示。 为了解决这些限制，我们提出了 PAMF，一种多模态时间序列框架，它明确处理不同的缺失模式，同时通过先验感知流匹配和权重共享将插补与下游预测耦合。 具体来说，该方法使用特定于类型的先验来初始化流匹配源状态，以区分两种缺失的类型。 它通过架构上匹配的编码器和权重共享进一步连接插补和分类，将与任务相关的表示转移到插补过程中。 对多个多模式医疗保健时间序列基准的实验表明，与现有基线相比，所提出的方法在不同数据集和缺失设置中实现了最强的整体下游性能。In healthcare, multimodal time series tasks often operate on incomplete observations in practice, for example when ECG segments are lost because electrodes detach or an entire respiratory channel is unavailable during overnight monitoring. Such missingness typically appears in two structurally distinct patterns: within-modality missing, where values are absent within an otherwise observed modality, and modality-level missing, where an entire modality is unavailable. Existing methods typically represent unobserved data implicitly through masks or missing embeddings, without learning instance-specific missing information, and most are designed for only one missingness pattern. A natural approach is to explicitly estimate the missing data; however, existing imputation methods treat missingness uniformly despite their different structural priors, and the imputation process is often isolated from downstream tasks, preventing downstream tasks from guiding imputation toward more informative representations. To address these limitations, we present PAMF, a multimodal time-series framework that explicitly handles different missingness patterns while coupling imputation with downstream prediction through prior-aware flow matching and weight sharing. Specifically, the method initializes the flow-matching source state with type-specific priors to distinguish two missing types. It further connects imputation and classification through architecturally matched encoders with weight sharing, transferring task-relevant representations into the imputation process. Experiments on multiple multimodal healthcare time-series benchmarks show that the proposed method achieves the strongest overall downstream performance across diverse datasets and missing settings compared with existing baselines.

5 结论Conclusion

缺失数据仍然是多模态时间序列分析的主要障碍，因为模态内缺失和模态级缺失都会严重降低下游性能。 PAMF 通过流匹配框架中的先验感知初始化以及通过将插补与分类连接起来的插补数据微调的权重共享来解决这一差距。对四个数据集的实验表明，这种组合针对缺失感知基线和直接插补管道提供了最强的整体下游性能，支持区分缺失结构以及将信号恢复与下游学习耦合的价值。Missing data remains a major obstacle in multimodal time series analysis, because both within-modality missing and modality-level missing can severely degrade downstream performance. PAMF addresses this gap through prior-aware initialization in a flow-matching framework and through weight sharing with imputed-data fine-tuning that connects imputation to classification. Experiments on four datasets show that this combination delivers the strongest overall downstream performance against missing-aware baselines and direct imputation pipelines, supporting the value of distinguishing missingness structure and coupling signal recovery with downstream learning.

Its role in our benchmark is to provide a principled diffusion-based reconstruction baseline that fills in missing values before the downstream classifier is applied. 差距总结。Summary of the gap.

总的来说，我们研究中的基线分为两个不完整的家族。 缺失感知融合模型可以在输入缺失的情况下进行分类，但通常不会 重建丢失的信号，而显式输入器可以重建 信号，但不是为了处理多模式模态级别的缺席而构建的 特定类型的先验或下游耦合训练。这正是差距 这激发了主论文中的 PAMF。因此，基准不仅仅是 比较强分类器与弱分类器；它正在比较三种不同的 对缺失数据的反应：容忍它，一般地估算它，或者 使用与缺失类型匹配的先验显式重建它，同时 仍在针对下游任务进行优化。Taken together, the baselines in our study split into two incomplete families. Missing-aware fusion models can classify with absent inputs but usually do not reconstruct the missing signal, whereas explicit imputers can reconstruct signals but are not built to handle multimodal modality-level absence with type-specific priors or downstream-coupled training. This is precisely the gap that motivates PAMF in the main paper. The benchmark is therefore not just comparing stronger versus weaker classifiers; it is comparing three different responses to missing data: tolerating it, generically imputing it, or explicitly reconstructing it with priors matched to the missingness type while still optimizing for the downstream task.
