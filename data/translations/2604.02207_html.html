摘要Abstract

背景：Background:放射学报告的准确翻译不仅对于多语言研究和临床交流很重要，而且对于放射学教育和学员学习也很重要。最近的大型语言模型 (LLM) 在医学翻译中表现出了强大的性能，但基于 LLM 的教育相关翻译质量评估的有效性仍不清楚。Accurate translation of radiology reports is important not only for multilingual research and clinical communication but also for radiology education and trainee learning. Recent large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated strong performance in medical translation, but the validity of LLM-based evaluation for educationally relevant translation quality remains unclear.

目标：Objective:使用盲法放射科医生评估来评估法学硕士生成的胸部 CT 报告日语翻译的教育适用性，并将专家评级与法学硕士作为法官的评估进行比较。To evaluate the educational suitability of LLM-generated Japanese translations of chest CT reports using blinded radiologist assessment and to compare expert ratings with LLM-as-a-judge evaluations.

方法：Methods:我们使用了来自 CT-RATE-JPN 验证集的 150 份胸部 CT 报告。对于每份原始英文报告，我们将人工编辑的日语翻译与 DeepSeek-V3.2 生成的法学硕士生成的翻译进行了比较。一名经过委员会认证的放射科医生（放射科医生 1）和一名放射科医生住院医生（放射科医生 2）根据四个标准独立进行了盲法成对评估：术语准确性、可读性和流畅性、整体报告质量以及放射科医生风格的真实性（翻译看起来更像是放射科医生撰写的报告）。与此同时，三位法学硕士评委 DeepSeek-V3.2、Mistral Large 3 和 GPT-5 使用相同的标准评估相同的翻译对。使用二次加权卡帕 (QWK) 和百分比一致性来评估放射科医生之间以及放射科医生和法学硕士法官之间的一致性。We used 150 chest CT reports from the validation set of CT-RATE-JPN. For each original English report, we compared a human-edited Japanese translation with an LLM-generated translation produced by DeepSeek-V3.2. A board-certified radiologist (radiologist 1) and a radiology resident (radiologist 2) independently performed blinded pairwise evaluations across four criteria: terminology accuracy, readability and fluency, overall report quality, and radiologist-style authenticity (which translation appeared more like a report written by a radiologist). In parallel, three LLM judges, DeepSeek-V3.2, Mistral Large 3, and GPT-5, evaluated the same translation pairs using identical criteria. Agreement between radiologists and between radiologists and LLM judges was assessed using quadratic weighted kappa (QWK) and percentage agreement.

Agreement between the two radiologists remained poor (QWK = 0.01 to 0.06). The radiologist 1 found the two translations nearly equivalent in terminology (59% tie) but favored the LLM translation for readability (51%) and overall quality (51%). The radiologist 2 found translations equivalent in readability (75% tie) but favored the human-edited translation for overall quality (40% vs 21%). 结论：Conclusions:法学硕士生成的胸部 CT 报告翻译被法学硕士法官和一名人类评估员评为高度自然和流畅，但这两位放射科医生的评估存在很大差异。法学硕士作为法官的评估显示出对法学硕士翻译的强烈定向偏好，并且与放射科医生的一致性可以忽略不计。对于放射学翻译报告的教育部署，仅基于法学硕士的自动化评估是不够的；放射科医生的专家评审仍然很重要。LLM-generated translations of chest CT reports were rated highly natural and fluent by both LLM judges and one human evaluator, but the two radiologists differed substantially in their assessments. LLM-as-a-judge evaluations showed a strong directional preference for the LLM translation and negligible agreement with radiologists. For educational deployment of translated radiology reports, automated LLM-based evaluation alone is insufficient; expert radiologist review remains important.

简介Introduction

医疗文件的准确翻译是现代全球化医疗保健的基石，不仅促进跨境临床交流，而且促进医学教育和患者赋权[1-3]。在数据驱动的医学时代，高质量翻译数据集的可用性变得更加重要。大规模英语医学数据集越来越多地被用来通过机器翻译开发其他语言的人工智能模型，迫切需要保持临床细微差别和技术精度的翻译方法[4-7]。Accurate translation of medical documentation is a cornerstone of modern globalized healthcare, facilitating not only cross-border clinical communication but also medical education and patient empowerment [1–3]. In the era of data-driven medicine, the availability of high-quality translated datasets has become even more critical. Large-scale English-language medical datasets are increasingly being leveraged to develop AI models in other languages through machine translation, creating a pressing need for translation methods that maintain clinical nuance and technical precision [4–7].

大型语言模型（LLM）最近已成为医学自然语言处理领域的变革性工具[8-10]。最近的研究表明，最先进的法学硕士可以在一般医学环境中实现与专业翻译人员相当的翻译质量，包括面向患者的教学材料和出院文件的简化[11,12]。然而，法学硕士在放射学等高度专业化领域的应用仍然相对有限[13]。放射学报告包含密集的技术术语、尽管不断努力标准化但仍存在大量风格差异以及高度重要的临床信息，即使是细微的措辞差异也可能会影响解释[14,15]。据我们所知，专门评估胸部 CT 报告英日翻译的研究仍然很少，需要对最新模型进行更严格的评估。Large Language Models (LLMs) have recently emerged as transformative tools in the field of medical natural language processing [8–10]. Recent studies suggest that state-of-the-art LLMs can achieve translation quality comparable to professional translators in general medical contexts, including patient-facing instructional materials and the simplification of discharge documentation [11,12]. However, the application of LLMs to highly specialized fields like radiology remains relatively limited [13]. Radiology reports contain dense technical terminology, substantial stylistic variation despite ongoing efforts toward standardization, and highly consequential clinical information for which even subtle wording differences may affect interpretation [14,15]. To our knowledge, research specifically evaluating the English-to-Japanese translation of chest CT reports remains scarce, warranting a more rigorous assessment of the latest models.

推进医学翻译的一个重要瓶颈是质量评估的挑战。传统的自动化指标，例如双语评估研究 (BLEU) [16] 和面向回忆的 Gisting 评估研究 (ROUGE) [17]，主要依赖于词汇或 n 元语法重叠，通常无法捕获语义等价性、事实一致性或临床准确性 [18]。在医学文本评估中，质量评估本质上是具有挑战性的，因为语义等价且A significant bottleneck in advancing medical translation is the challenge of quality evaluation. Traditional automated metrics, such as Bilingual Evaluation Understudy (BLEU) [16] and Recall-Oriented Understudy for Gisting Evaluation (ROUGE) [17], rely primarily on lexical or n-gram overlap and often fail to capture semantic equivalence, factual consistency, or clinical accuracy [18]. In medical text evaluation, quality assessment is inherently challenging because semantically equivalent and

临床上可接受的输出在措辞上可能有很大差异，使得基于词汇重叠的评估本质上受到限制[19]，并且评估翻译是否保留与放射科医生一致的措辞和报告风格可能更加困难。因此，“法学硕士作为法官”框架（其中法学硕士评估另一个模型的输出）作为劳动密集型人工审查的可扩展替代方案而受到关注[20,21]。然而，它在高风险放射学翻译中的有效性，特别是它与人类专家细致入微的判断的一致性，仍然没有得到充分验证。clinically acceptable outputs may differ considerably in wording, making lexicaloverlap-based evaluation inherently limited [19], and evaluating whether a translation preserves radiologist-aligned phrasing and reporting style may be even more difficult. Consequently, the “LLM-as-a-judge” framework, in which an LLM evaluates the output of another model, has gained traction as a scalable alternative to labor-intensive human review [20,21]. However, its validity in high-stakes radiology translation, particularly its alignment with the nuanced judgments of human specialists, remains insufficiently validated.

在这项研究中，我们评估了 DeepSeek-V3.2（一种最先进的开放权重法学硕士 [22]）在将胸部 CT 报告从英语翻译为日语方面的性能。通过将模型生成的翻译与 CTRATE-JPN 数据集 [23] 中的人工编辑版本进行比较，我们的目标是评估最新法学硕士在医学教育和研究中的临床效用。此外，我们批判性地研究法学硕士作为法官的框架是否为专家评估提供了可靠的替代方案，或者它是否引入了与委员会认证的放射科医生和住院医师的专业标准不同的系统性偏差。In this study, we evaluate the performance of DeepSeek-V3.2, a state-of-the-art open-weight LLM [22], in translating chest CT reports from English to Japanese. By comparing model-generated translations with human-edited versions from the CTRATE-JPN dataset [23], we aim to assess the clinical utility of the latest LLMs for medical education and research. Furthermore, we critically examine whether the LLM-as-a-judge framework provides a reliable surrogate for expert evaluation, or whether it introduces systematic biases that diverge from the professional standards of a board-certified radiologist and resident.

方法Methods

道德考虑Ethical Considerations

鉴于这项研究使用了带有去识别化患者信息的公开数据集，并且我们的研究重点是现有数据集的翻译和语言分析，而不访问任何其他患者数据，因此这项研究不需要机构审查委员会的批准。Given that this study utilized a publicly available dataset with deidentified patient information, and that our research focused on the translation and linguistic analysis of the existing dataset without accessing any additional patient data, institutional review board approval was not required for this research.

数据集Dataset

我们使用了来自 CT-RATE-JPN [23] 验证集的 150 份胸部 CT 报告，该报告源自 CT-RATE [24]，这是一个公开数据集，包含胸部 CT 成像研究和相应的自由文本放射学报告。每份报告都有两种日语翻译：We used 150 chest CT reports from the validation set of CT-RATE-JPN [23], which is derived from CT-RATE [24], a publicly available dataset comprising chest CT imaging studies with corresponding free-text radiology reports. For each report, two Japanese translations were available:

  • 人工编辑翻译：通过多阶段质量控制流程生成的翻译。最初的机器翻译由 GPT-4o mini 生成，然后由放射科住院医师审核和纠正，最后由委员会认证的放射科医生完善。这个多阶段过程充当 CT-RATE-JPN 中的真实日语翻译。Human-edited translation: a translation produced through a multi-stage quality control pipeline. An initial machine translation was generated by GPT-4o mini, then reviewed and corrected by a radiology resident, and finally refined by a board-certified radiologist. This multi-stage process serves as the ground-truth Japanese translation in CT-RATE-JPN.

  • LLM生成的翻译：由DeepSeek-V3.2生成，这是一个具有强大的多语言和医学领域性能的大型语言模型。LLM-generated translation: produced by DeepSeek-V3.2, a large language model with strong multilingual and medical domain performance.

每个评估实例均由原始英文报告、人工编辑的日语翻译和法学硕士生成的日语翻译组成。两个日语翻译的呈现顺序（左/右或 A/B）是随机和平衡的。Each evaluation instance consisted of the original English report, the human-edited Japanese translation, and the LLM-generated Japanese translation. The presentation order of the two Japanese translations (left/right or A/B) was randomized and counterbalanced.

翻译生成Translation Generation

LLM 生成的翻译是使用 DeepSeek-V3.2 [22] 生成的。 DeepSeekV3.2 因其在多语言医疗任务上的强大性能以及允许未来数据集共享的许可再分发许可证 (Apache-2.0) 而被选中。翻译是在温度 0 时使用标准化提示生成的，指示模型从英语源生成惯用的日语放射学报告。补充材料 1 中提供了完整的提示。LLM-generated translations were produced using DeepSeek-V3.2 [22]. DeepSeekV3.2 was selected for its strong performance on multilingual medical tasks and its permissive redistribution license (Apache-2.0), which enables future dataset sharing. Translations were generated at temperature 0 using a standardized prompt instructing the model to produce an idiomatic Japanese radiology report from the English source. The full prompt is provided in Supplementary Material 1.

翻译对的语言分析Linguistic Analysis of Translation Pairs

为了量化两个翻译集之间的文本级别差异，我们对所有 150 个报告对进行了一系列配对分析。删除空格和换行符后计算字符数。通过对日语句尾标点符号和换行符进行分割来进行句子分割，保留两个以上字符的片段。对于形态分析，我们使用 Janome tokenizer [25] 对每个报告进行标记，并计算类型标记比 (TTR)，即唯一标记类型与总标记的比率，作为词汇多样性的度量，仅使用所有词性和实词（名词、动词、形容词和副词）。在可用的情况下使用引理级基本形式。使用 Wilcoxon 符号秩检验和配对 t 检验对配对人工编辑翻译和 DeepSeek 翻译之间进行统计比较；报告双尾 p 值。To quantify text-level differences between the two translation sets, we performed a series of paired analyses on all 150 report pairs. Character count was computed after removing whitespace and newlines. Sentence segmentation was performed by splitting on Japanese sentence-ending punctuation and newlines, retaining segments of more than two characters. For morphological analysis, we used the Janome tokenizer [25] to tokenize each report and computed type-token ratio (TTR), the ratio of unique token types to total tokens, as a measure of lexical diversity, using both all parts of speech and content words only (nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs). Lemma-level base forms were used where available. Statistical comparisons between paired human-edited and DeepSeek translations were performed using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test and paired t-test; two-tailed p- values are reported.

放射科医生盲法评估Blinded Radiologist Evaluation

翻译对由两名放射科医生独立评估：一名委员会认证的放射科医生（放射科医生 1）和一名放射科住院医师（研究生 5 年，放射科医生 2）。两位评估者都不知道每个翻译是人工编辑的还是法学硕士生成的。The translation pairs were independently evaluated by two radiologists: one boardcertified radiologist (radiologist 1) and one radiology resident (postgraduate year 5, radiologist 2). Both evaluators were blinded to whether each translation was human-edited or LLM-generated.

评估程序Evaluation Procedure

对于 150 个报告对中的每一个，评估人员都显示：For each of the 150 report pairs, evaluators were shown:

  1. 英文原件胸部CT报告The original English chest CT report

  2. 日语翻译AJapanese Translation A

  3. 日语翻译BJapanese Translation B

人工编辑和法学硕士生成的翻译对位置 A 和 B 的分配是随机的。评估者独立选择他们喜欢的翻译或指示每个标准的等效内容。The assignment of human-edited and LLM-generated translations to positions A and B was randomized. Evaluators selected their preferred translation or indicated equivalence for each criterion independently.

评价标准Evaluation Criteria

评估人员评估了四个标准：Evaluators assessed four criteria:

  1. 术语准确性：医学和解剖术语的准确性Terminology accuracy: accuracy of medical and anatomical terminology

  2. 可读性和流畅性：译文的自然性和连贯性Readability and fluency: naturalness and coherence of the translated text

  3. 总体临床适用性：用作临床放射学报告的总体适用性Overall clinical suitability: overall appropriateness for use as a clinical radiology report

  4. 放射科医生风格的真实性：翻译是否更接近放射科医生撰写的报告的风格和表达Radiologist-style authenticity: whether the translation more closely resembled the style and expression of a report written by a radiologist

对于标准 1-3，评估者选择三个选项之一：翻译 A 更好、翻译 B 更好或等效。对于标准 4，评估者选择翻译 A、翻译 B 或同等版本，因为该标准旨在捕捉风格印象而不是客观优势。事后对回复进行解码，以确定首选人工编辑的翻译还是法学硕士生成的翻译。For criteria 1–3, evaluators chose one of three options: Translation A is better, Translation B is better, or Equivalent. For criterion 4, evaluators chose Translation A, Translation B, or Equivalent, as this criterion was intended to capture stylistic impression rather than objective superiority. Responses were decoded post hoc to determine whether the human-edited or LLM-generated translation was preferred.

法学硕士法官评估LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation

三名法学硕士评委使用相同的提示结构评估了相同的 150 个翻译对：Three LLM judges evaluated the same 150 translation pairs using an identical prompt structure:

  • DeepSeek-V3.2

  • 米斯特拉尔大号 3Mistral Large 3

  • GPT-5

这些模型的选择代表了不同的评估视角：DeepSeek-V3.2被纳入作为自我评判，因为它也被用作翻译模型，GPT-5被纳入作为高端商业模型[26]，Mistral Large 3被纳入作为相对中性的开放权重模型[27]。每位评委收到英文报告原件和按随机顺序排列的两份日文译文，翻译来源被隐藏。法官被指示评估相同的四个标准，并提供结构化的 JSON 响应，指示每个标准（A / B / TIE）首选哪种翻译，以及简短的理由。所有评委评估的温度均设为 0。补充材料中提供了完整的法官提示。These models were selected to represent different evaluation perspectives: DeepSeek-V3.2 was included as a self-judge because it was also used as the translation model, GPT-5 was included as a high-end commercial model [26], and Mistral Large 3 was included as a comparatively neutral open-weight model [27]. Each judge received the original English report and the two Japanese translations in randomized order, with the translation source hidden. The judge was instructed to evaluate the same four criteria and provide a structured JSON response indicating which translation was preferred for each criterion (A / B / TIE), along with a brief justification. Temperature was set to 0 for all judge evaluations. The full judge prompt is provided in the Supplementary Material.

统计分析Statistical Analysis

翻译对的语言比较Linguistic Comparison of Translation Pairs

人工编辑翻译的总字符数明显长于 DeepSeek 生成的翻译（中位数 517.5 个字符与 481.5 个字符；Wilcoxon 符号秩检验，W = 729.5，p < .001），而 DeepSeek 翻译的平均长度约为人工编辑翻译长度的 94%（平均比率 0.940）。尽管整体简洁，但 DeepSeek 翻译中每个报告包含的句子明显更多（平均 20.3 与 19.6；p < .001），而单个句子则明显较短（平均句子长度为 24.7 与 27.3 个字符；p < .001）。这种模式表明，与人工编辑的版本相比，DeepSeek 生成的翻译将内容分割成更多数量的较短句子。当对所有词性进行计算时，通过 TTR 测量的词汇多样性在两个翻译集之间存在显着差异：DeepSeek 翻译显示出更高的 TTR（平均值 0.402 与 0.381；p < .001），表明相对于总标记数而言，词汇多样性更大。这一看似违反直觉的发现部分归因于 DeepSeek 翻译的整体长度较短，因为 TTR 往往会随着文本较短而增加。当 TTR 仅限于实词（名词、动词、形容词和副词）时，没有观察到显着差异（平均值 0.550 与 0.549；p = .665），这表明这两种翻译方法采用了相当广度的临床有意义的词汇。Human-edited translations were significantly longer than DeepSeek-generated translations in total character count (median 517.5 vs. 481.5 characters; Wilcoxon signed-rank test, W = 729.5, p < .001), with DeepSeek translations averaging approximately 94% of the length of their human-edited counterparts (mean ratio 0.940). Despite this overall brevity, DeepSeek translations contained significantly more sentences per report (mean 20.3 vs. 19.6; p < .001), while individual sentences were significantly shorter (mean sentence length 24.7 vs. 27.3 characters; p < .001). This pattern indicates that DeepSeek-generated translations segmented content into a greater number of shorter sentences compared with the human-edited versions. Lexical diversity, measured by TTR, differed significantly between the two translation sets when computed across all parts of speech: DeepSeek translations showed higher TTR (mean 0.402 vs. 0.381; p < .001), indicating greater lexical variety relative to total token count. This seemingly counterintuitive finding is partly attributable to the shorter overall length of DeepSeek translations, as TTR tends to increase with shorter texts. When TTR was restricted to content words only (nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs), no significant difference was observed (mean 0.550 vs. 0.549; p = .665), suggesting that the two translation methods employed a comparable breadth of clinically meaningful vocabulary.

讨论Discussion

主要成果Principal Results

这项研究发现，法学硕士生成的胸部 CT 报告的日语翻译收到了不同的评估，具体取决于评估者。放射科医生 1 在可读性和整体质量方面对 LLM 翻译给予了好评，高于放射科医生 2。相比之下，放射科医生 2 发现翻译通常是等效的，并且整体上对人工编辑版本略有偏好。至关重要的是，所有三位法学硕士评委都对法学硕士翻译表现出了强烈和系统的偏好，远远超过了两个人工评估中更有利的一个，并且与任何一个人工评估者的一致性都可以忽略不计。This study found that LLM-generated Japanese translations of chest CT reports received divergent assessments depending on the evaluator. The radiologist 1 rated the LLM translation favorably for readability and overall quality, more so than the radiologist 2. The radiologist 2, by contrast, found translations frequently equivalent and showed a slight preference for the human-edited version overall. Crucially, all three LLM judges showed strong and systematic preference for the LLM translation, far exceeding even the more favorable of the two human assessments, and showed negligible agreement with either human rater.

两位放射科医生之间的评估者间一致性极低（所有标准的 QWK = 0.01-0.06），这凸显出即使是放射科医生专家在评估翻译质量时也不会采用相同的标准。重要的是，这种低一致性应该在上下文中解释，而不是简单地视为注释失败。在事后汇报中，两位放射科医生都报告说，在许多情况下，两种翻译的整体质量几乎没有区别，最终的偏好往往取决于细微的措辞差异或细微的差异。The inter-rater agreement between the two radiologists was strikingly low (QWK = 0.01–0.06 for all criteria), underscoring that even expert radiologists do not apply identical standards when evaluating translation quality. Importantly, this low agreement should be interpreted in context rather than viewed simply as annotation failure. In post hoc debriefing, both radiologists reported that, in many cases, the two translations were nearly indistinguishable in overall quality, and that final preferences were often determined by subtle wording differences or minor

细微差别。在这种情况下，成对偏好判断会出现一定程度的变化。然而，这种程度的变异性具有重要的方法论意义：依靠单个人类评估者来验证法学硕士翻译质量的研究可能会产生特定于评估者偏好的结论，而不是反映共同的临床标准。nuances. Under such conditions, some degree of variability in pairwise preference judgments is expected. Nevertheless, this level of variability has important methodological implications: studies relying on a single human rater to validate LLM translation quality may produce conclusions that are specific to that rater's preferences rather than reflecting a shared clinical standard.

法学硕士翻译质量及其对教育和人工智能发展的影响LLM Translation Quality and Its Implications for Education and AI Development

语言分析显示，与人工编辑的翻译相比，法学硕士生成的翻译系统地更短，包含更多句子，并且单个句子更短。尽管如此，两种方法之间的内容词词汇多样性具有可比性，这表明核心临床词汇具有同等的代表性。这些结构差异可能是两位放射科医生报告的不同风格印象的基础。从教育的角度来看，这项研究与翻译医学材料在培训和跨语言学习环境中的更广泛使用直接相关[1-3]。对于需要精确临床术语和忠实表达诊断不确定性的教育环境来说，这是放射学报告的基本特征[28,29]，简洁和自然的措辞虽然是可取的，但不应被视为医学文本翻译质量的最重要指标。除了医学教育之外，机器翻译的放射学报告的质量对人工智能模型的开发也具有重要影响。大规模多语言数据集越来越多地用于训练和评估非英语临床环境的人工智能模型[4-7]。 LLM 翻译保留临床细微差别、术语惯例和不确定性表达的保真度直接决定了这些数据集提供的训练信号的质量。我们的研究结果表明，LLM 生成的翻译基本上足以丰富大量数据集，例如预训练或语言模型微调，其中优先考虑广度和流畅性。然而，对于旨在支持高风险下游任务（例如自动放射学报告生成）的数据集[30-32]，仍然有必要对至少一个代表性子集进行专家审查，以确保忠实保留微妙的临床惯例。The linguistic analysis revealed that LLM-generated translations were systematically shorter, contained more sentences, and had shorter individual sentences compared with human-edited counterparts. Despite this, content-word lexical diversity was comparable between the two methods, suggesting that the core clinical vocabulary was equivalently represented. These structural differences likely underlie the divergent stylistic impressions reported by the two radiologists. From an educational standpoint, this study has direct relevance to the broader use of translated medical materials in training and cross-lingual learning contexts [1–3]. For educational contexts requiring precise clinical terminology and faithful representation of diagnostic uncertainty, an essential feature of radiology reporting [28,29], conciseness and natural phrasing, although desirable, should not be assumed to be the most important indicators of translation quality in medical texts. Beyond medical education, the quality of machine-translated radiology reports has important implications for AI model development. Large-scale multilingual datasets are increasingly used to train and evaluate AI models for non-English clinical environments [4–7]. The fidelity with which LLM translation preserves clinical nuance, terminology conventions, and uncertainty expressions directly determines the quality of the training signal these datasets provide. Our findings suggest that LLM-generated translations are broadly adequate for high-volume dataset enrichment, such as pre-training or language model fine-tuning, where breadth and fluency are prioritized. However, for datasets intended to support high-stakes downstream tasks such as automated radiology report generation [30–32], expert review of at least a representative subset remains necessary to ensure that subtle clinical conventions are faithfully preserved.

为什么法学硕士法官与放射科医生存在分歧Why LLM Judges Diverge from Radiologists

相比之下，拥有 LLM 学位的法官表现出更强、更有方向性的偏好模式，在所有标准中，79-95% 的案件都倾向于 LLM 翻译。这种差异表明法学硕士法官可能并没有单独检测出临床上有意义的优势，但也可能反映了模型特定的文体偏好或与特定词汇和句法模式的一致性，这与之前关于法学硕士法官背景中自我偏好偏差的报告一致[33]。换句话说，当候选翻译的质量大致相似时，LLM-asa-judge 可能会将微小的文本差异放大为不成比例的自信偏好。By contrast, the LLM-based judges showed a much stronger and more directional preference pattern, favoring the LLM translation in 79–95% of cases across all criteria. This discrepancy suggests that the LLM judges may not have been detecting clinically meaningful superiority alone, but may also have reflected model-specific stylistic preferences or alignment with particular lexical and syntactic patterns, consistent with prior reports of self-preference bias in LLM-as-a-judge settings [33]. In other words, when candidate translations are broadly similar in quality, LLM-asa-judge may amplify small textual differences into disproportionately confident preferences.

这种系统性偏差可能有多种机制造成。先前的工作表明，法学硕士作为法官系统容易受到多种形式的偏见的影响[34]，这增加了我们的三名法学硕士法官受到类似评估影响的可能性Several mechanisms may underlie this systematic bias. Prior work has shown that LLM-as-a-judge systems are susceptible to multiple forms of bias [34], raising the possibility that our three LLM judges were influenced by similar evaluation

倾向。因此，他们可能会奖励看起来优美、流畅和词汇精确的文本，即使这些特征不一定符合更大的临床适用性。一个相关的解释是流畅性可能隐含地与整体翻译质量混为一谈[35]。由于当代法学硕士在一定程度上是通过人类偏好信号进行优化的，这些信号通常会奖励自然性和可读性[36]，因此他们可能会系统地为表面上听起来更流畅的输出分配更高的分数，从而为法学硕士生成的翻译创造结构优势[37]。tendencies. As a result, they may have rewarded text that appeared polished, smooth, and lexically precise, even when those characteristics did not necessarily correspond to greater clinical appropriateness. A related explanation is that fluency may be implicitly conflated with overall translation quality [35]. Because contemporary LLMs are optimized in part through human preference signals that often reward naturalness and readability [36], they may systematically assign higher scores to outputs that sound more fluent on the surface, creating a structural advantage for LLM-generated translations [37].

另一个可能的因素是对临床记录的敏感性有限。日本放射学报告在措辞、不确定性标记、解剖描述和整体句子风格方面遵循高度传统的模式。即使翻译在语法上正确且语义上合理，与这些约定的微小偏差也可能使其难以被放射科医生接受。法学硕士法官可能没有充分了解放射学特定的报告规范，无法识别并适当权衡此类区别。Another likely factor is limited sensitivity to clinical register. Japanese radiology reports follow highly conventionalized patterns in wording, uncertainty marking, anatomic description, and overall sentence style. Even when a translation is grammatically correct and semantically plausible, small deviations from these conventions may render it less acceptable to radiologists. LLM judges may not be sufficiently grounded in radiology-specific reporting norms to recognize and appropriately weight such distinctions.

这种解释得到了放射科医生风格判断中明显差异的进一步支持。所有三位法学硕士法官几乎一致认为法学硕士翻译更具放射科医生写作特征（> 93% 的案例）。然而，两位放射科医生不仅不同意 LLM 法官的意见，而且彼此之间也存在分歧：放射科医生 2 对人工编辑的翻译表现出高于机会的偏好 (p = .002)，而放射科医生 1 则表现出相反的偏好 (p = .002)。这种模式表明，用于评估放射科医生风格真实性的标准在不同评估者（无论是人类还是法学硕士）之间存在很大差异，并且仅表面流畅性可能无法捕捉放射科医生专家认为的专业真实写作。This interpretation is further supported by the marked discrepancy in judgments of radiologist-like style. All three LLM judges almost uniformly rated the LLM translation as more characteristic of radiologist writing (>93% of cases). The two radiologists, however, not only disagreed with the LLM judges but also with each other: radiologist 2 showed an above-chance preference for the human-edited translation (p = .002), while radiologist 1 showed the opposite preference (p = .002). This pattern suggests that the criteria used to assess radiologist-style authenticity vary substantially across evaluators—whether human or LLM—and that surface fluency alone may not capture what expert radiologists consider professionally authentic writing.

实用推荐Practical Recommendation

这些发现指出了部署法学硕士翻译的放射学报告的分层方法。对于优先考虑广度和可读性的应用，例如用于语言学习的平行语料库、多语言教学集合或用于人工智能开发的大规模预训练数据集，可以放心使用LLM生成的翻译。对于风险较高的应用，包括模型报告、教学文件、标准化评估材料或临床人工智能系统的培训数据，其中忠实表示不确定性至关重要，放射科医生专家评审仍然是重要的质量关卡。These findings point toward a tiered approach for deploying LLM-translated radiology reports. For applications where breadth and readability are prioritized, such as parallel corpora for language learning, multilingual teaching collections, or large-scale pre-training datasets for AI development, LLM-generated translations may be used with confidence. For higher-stakes applications, including model reports, teaching files, standardized assessment materials, or training data for clinical AI systems in which faithful representation of uncertainty is critical, expert radiologist review remains an important quality gate.

至关重要的是，这些发现表明，法学硕士作为法官不应成为教育内容或人工智能培训数据的唯一质量关卡。鉴于与放射科医生评估的一致性接近于零，并且系统倾向于支持法学硕士生成的输出，自动判断似乎大大高估了教育和临床适用性。因此，更实用的方法是混合工作流程：法学硕士翻译和自动评估以进行初始扩展和筛选，然后对高风险应用程序的案例进行有针对性的专家审查。该工作流程平衡了可扩展性与放射学特定用例所需的临床严谨性。Critically, these findings indicate that LLM-as-a-judge should not serve as the sole quality gate for either educational content or AI training data. Given the near-zero agreement with radiologist assessments and the systematic tendency to favor LLMgenerated output, automated judging appears to substantially overestimate educational and clinical suitability. A more practical approach is therefore a hybrid workflow: LLM translation and automated evaluation for initial scaling and screening, followed by targeted expert review of cases intended for high-stakes applications. This workflow balances scalability with the clinical rigor that radiology-specific use cases demand.

局限性Limitations

应该注意几个限制。首先，我们的数据集仅包含胸部 CT 报告，研究结果可能无法推广到其他模式或报告样式。其次，仅评估日语翻译；对于其他目标语言，结果可能有所不同。第三，我们只有两名人类评估者，限制了评估者间分析的统计能力，并排除了对分歧的裁决。第四，法学硕士法官分析基于单一提示框架，不包括对替代法官提示、标题结构或提示优化策略的系统探索。第五，我们没有评估下游教育成果——从法学硕士翻译报告和人工编辑报告中学习的学员在知识获取或术语熟练程度方面是否存在差异。此类结果研究对于确定法学硕士翻译报告的最终教育效用是必要的。Several limitations should be noted. First, our dataset comprised only chest CT reports, and findings may not generalize to other modalities or report styles. Second, only Japanese translations were evaluated; results may differ for other target languages. Third, we had only two human raters, limiting statistical power for inter-rater analyses and precluding adjudication of disagreements. Fourth, the LLMas-a-judge analysis was based on a single prompt framework and did not include systematic exploration of alternative judge prompts, rubric structures, or prompt optimization strategies. Fifth, we did not assess downstream educational outcomes—whether trainees who learn from LLM-translated versus human-edited reports show differences in knowledge acquisition or terminology proficiency. Such outcomes studies would be necessary to establish the ultimate educational utility of LLM-translated reports.

未来的方向Future Directions

未来的工作应该调查：（1）翻译质量对受控教育环境中学员学习成果的影响； (2) 基于放射学术语标准的法学硕士评判提示和细则设计的系统探索和优化； (3) 扩展到其他语言、模式和报告类型； (4) 开发与放射科医生一致的医学翻译质量自动评估指标。Future work should investigate: (1) the impact of translation quality on trainee learning outcomes in controlled educational settings; (2) systematic exploration and optimization of LLM-as-a-judge prompts and rubric designs grounded in radiology terminology standards; (3) extension to other languages, modalities, and report types; and (4) the development of radiologist-aligned automated evaluation metrics for medical translation quality.

结论Conclusions

法学硕士生成的胸部 CT 报告的日语翻译收到了两位放射科医生的不同评估，所有标准的评估者间一致性较差。然而，两位人类评估员与三位法学硕士评委明显不同，他们对法学硕士翻译表现出系统性和极端的偏好，远远超出了放射科医生的评估。法学硕士作为法官的评估显示，QWK 与放射科医生的判断的一致性几乎为零，并且在超过 93% 的案例中，法学硕士生成的翻译被评为更像放射科医生。这些发现表明，法学硕士生成的放射学报告翻译可能有望提供教育支持，但法学硕士作为法官的评估不足以保证该领域的质量。在翻译放射学报告用于教育用途时，专家放射科医生审查仍然是质量控制的重要组成部分。LLM-generated Japanese translations of chest CT reports received divergent assessments from the two radiologists, with poor inter-rater agreement across all criteria. However, both human evaluators differed markedly from the three LLM judges, who showed systematic and extreme preference for the LLM translation far beyond either radiologist's assessment. LLM-as-a-judge evaluations showed nearzero QWK agreement with radiologist judgments and rated the LLM-generated translation as more radiologist-like in >93% of cases. These findings indicate that LLM-generated radiology report translations may hold promise for educational support, but that LLM-as-a-judge evaluation is insufficient for quality assurance in this domain. Expert radiologist review remains an important component of quality control when translating radiology reports for educational use.
