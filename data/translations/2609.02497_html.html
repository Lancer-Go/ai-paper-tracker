RINSE：稳健的目标时间正态性估计RINSE: Robust Target-Time Normality Estimation
用于零样本图异常检测for Zero-Shot Graph Anomaly Detection

Taufikur Rahman Fuad    Md Abrar Jahin    Amir Hussain
摘要Abstract

零样本图异常检测旨在将在源图上训练的检测器部署到看不见的、未标记的目标，但域转移可能会使源衍生的正态性概念变得不可靠。我们引入了 RINSE（鲁棒迭代正态性自估计），这是一种无梯度目标时间框架，可保持源训练检测器固定，同时从目标图中顺序估计目标正态性、表示校准和证据可靠性。其核心思想是识别低残差目标节点的可靠子集，使用它们构建修剪后的目标感知正态性模型，并通过可靠性门控等级融合和编码器集成来组合互补的异常证据。在八个看不见的目标图中，RINSE 在两个单独的预处理协议下的评估方法中实现了最高的平均 AUPRC，而块消融和敏感性分析支持组合设计。这些结果支持鲁棒的目标时间估计作为通用图异常检测的实用方法，无需目标标签、梯度或每个目标调整。Zero-shot graph anomaly detection seeks to deploy a detector trained on source graphs to unseen, unlabeled targets, yet domain shift can make source-derived notions of normality unreliable. We introduce RINSE (Robust Iterative Normality Self-Estimation), a gradient-free target-time framework that keeps the source-trained detector fixed while sequentially estimating target normality, representation calibration, and evidence reliability from the target graph. Its core idea is to identify a reliable subset of low-residual target nodes, use them to construct a trimmed target-aware normality model, and combine complementary anomaly evidence through reliability-gated rank fusion and encoder ensembling. Across eight unseen target graphs, RINSE achieves the highest average AUPRC among the evaluated methods under two separate preprocessing protocols, while block ablations and sensitivity analyses support the combined design. These results support robust target-time estimation as a practical approach to generalist graph anomaly detection without target labels, gradients, or per-target tuning.

1 简介Introduction

图异常检测（GAD）识别图中属性或连接性偏离正态性的节点。它支持金融和电子商务网络中的欺诈检测、社交平台上的垃圾邮件和机器人检测以及评论操纵检测Graph anomaly detection (GAD) identifies nodes in graphs whose attributes or connectivity deviate from normality. It supports fraud detection in financial and e-commerce networks, spam and bot detection on social platforms, and review manipulation detection(Ding et al. 2019; Tang et al. 2022; Gao et al. 2023)。由于异常现象不太常见，而且节点级标签成本高昂，因此该范式通常是无监督的，在每个评分图上训练一个检测器. Because anomalies are less frequent and node-level labels are costly, the paradigm is often unsupervised, with a detector trained on each scored graph(Qiao et al. 2025b)。重建方法标记图自动编码器重建不良的节点. Reconstruction methods flag nodes reconstructed poorly by graph autoencoders(Ding et al. 2019; He et al. 2024);对比方法检测与采样上下文视图不一致的节点; contrastive methods detect nodes inconsistent with sampled contextual views(Liu et al. 2022; Zheng et al. 2023);和亲和方法得分与当地社区的分歧; and affinity methods score disagreement with local neighborhoods(Qiao and Pang 2023; Zheng et al. 2025).

每图训练会产生结构成本。每个图都需要单独的训练运行，但如果没有标签就无法验证超参数和停止标准；无标签模型选择仍然是一个悬而未决的问题Per-graph training incur structural costs. Each graph requires a separate training run, yet hyperparameters and stopping criteria cannot be validated without labels; label-free model selection remains an open problem(Zhao et al. 2021; Goswami et al. 2023; Ma et al. 2023)。无监督目标还针对受污染的数据进行了优化，使自动编码器能够吸收他们旨在检测的异常情况. Unsupervised objectives are also optimized on contaminated data, allowing autoencoders to absorb the anomalies they aim to detect(He et al. 2024; Kim et al. 2024)。通才 GAD 通过在标记的源图上训练一个模型并将其应用于看不见的目标来降低这些成本. Generalist GAD reduces these costs by training one model on labeled source graphs and applying it to unseen targets(Liu et al. 2024; Pan et al. 2025)。在零样本设置中，模型对未见过的目标图中的节点进行排名，无需标签、微调或特定于目标的配置. In the zero-shot setting, the model ranks nodes in unseen target graphs without labels, fine-tuning, or target-specific configuration(Niu et al. 2025; Qiao et al. 2025a; Zhang et al. 2025a; Zheng et al. 2026)。这就是我们研究的环境。. This is the setting we study.

零样本通才在源训练期间将异常标准编码为邻域提示、与图无关的原型或正常模式字典，然后将它们应用于未标记的目标Zero-shot generalists encode anomaly criteria during source training as neighborhood prompts, graph-agnostic prototypes, or normal-pattern dictionaries, then apply them to unlabeled targets(Niu et al. 2025; Qiao et al. 2025a; Zheng et al. 2026)。 ARC 在推理时使用标记的正常上下文. RINSE jointly estimates a trimmed target-normality dictionary, embedding calibration, and evidence reliability around a frozen detector.

测试时的适应提供了一条相关的途径。现有方法使用未标记的测试数据来优化熵、自监督目标、图转换或图级对齐器Test-time adaptation offers a related route. Existing approaches optimize entropy, self-supervised objectives, graph transformations, or graph-level aligners using unlabeled test data(Wang et al. 2021; Sun et al. 2020; Jin et al. 2023; Pan et al. 2026)。相反，RINSE 遵循无梯度路线：它从低残差节点估计目标正态性，并从后续校准和字典更新中排除高残差候选节点。这将零样本 GAD 与污染目标数据下的稳健估计联系起来. 6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 RINSE，一种用于零样本图异常检测的稳健、无梯度目标时间框架。围绕冷冻源训练的评分器，RINSE 通过修剪迭代来估计目标正态性，校准嵌入统计数据，通过无标签可靠性对补充证据进行加权，并对独立训练的编码器进行排名平均。在八个看不见的目标图中，RINSE 在两种预处理协议下的评估方法中实现了最高的平均精度，而消融和敏感性分析支持其各个组件。本研究重点关注归因节点级图和截断注意力评分器；将框架扩展到更大的图、替代的冻结检测器和其他异常设置是一个自然的方向。总体而言，结果支持稳健的目标时间估计作为通用图异常检测的实用方法。We presented RINSE, a robust, gradient-free target-time framework for zero-shot graph anomaly detection. Around a frozen source-trained scorer, RINSE estimates target normality through trimmed iteration, calibrates embedding statistics, weights complementary evidence by label-free reliability, and rank-averages independently trained encoders. Across eight unseen target graphs, RINSE achieves the highest mean average precision among the evaluated methods under both preprocessing protocols, while the ablation and sensitivity analyses support its individual components. The present study focuses on attributed node-level graphs and a truncated-attention scorer; extending the framework to larger graphs, alternative frozen detectors, and other anomaly settings is a natural direction. Overall, the results support robust target-time estimation as a practical approach to generalist graph anomaly detection.

RINSE 在已建立的基准数据集上进行评估，并且不收集新的人类受试者数据。它可以支持对潜在欺诈、垃圾邮件相关或操纵活动的审查，而无需目标域标签。然而，异常排名可能包含误报，并且可能对用户或群体产生不均匀的影响。因此，它们应该协助人工审查，而不是触发自动惩罚性决策。实际部署应包括特定领域的验证、适当的阈值选择、隐私保护和相关的子组审核。 RINSE 减少了对标记目标数据的依赖，但不提供因果保证。其使用应遵循适用的法律、平台政策和数据治理要求。RINSE is evaluated on established benchmark datasets and does not collect new human-subject data. It may support the review of potentially fraudulent, spam-related, or manipulative activity without requiring target-domain labels. However, anomaly rankings can contain false positives and may affect users or groups unevenly. They should therefore assist human review rather than trigger automatic punitive decisions. Practical deployment should include domain-specific validation, appropriate threshold selection, privacy safeguards, and subgroup auditing where relevant. RINSE reduces dependence on labeled target data but provides no causal guarantees. Its use should follow applicable laws, platform policies, and data-governance requirements.
