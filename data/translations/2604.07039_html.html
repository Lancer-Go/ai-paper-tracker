具有体现能力模块with Embodied Capability Modules

Xue Qin qinxue@me.com Simin Luan luansiminiot@gmail.com John See J.See@hw.ac.uk Cong Yang cong.yang@suda.edu.cn Zhijun Li lizhijunos@hit.edu.cn
摘要Abstract

机器人系统缺乏组织智能的原则性抽象， 能力，并以统一的方式执行。 现有方法要么在整体架构中结合技能 或将功能分解为松散协调的模块或多个代理， 通常没有一致的身份和控制权模型。 在本文中，我们认为机器人不应该被构建为代理的集合， 但作为一个持久的智能主体，其能力得到了扩展 通过可安装的软件包。 我们将这种观点形式化为单代理机器人架构， 我们称之为 EROS（代理执行运行时操作系统）， 其中每个机器人对应一个持久代理， 和能力是通过体现能力模块（ECM）提供的。 每个 ECM 都封装了可执行的技能、模型和工具， 同时执行执行约束和安全保证 由策略分离的运行时。 这种分离实现了模块化可扩展性、可组合功能执行、 和一致的系统级安全性。 我们实现参考原型并评估架构 使用 Franka Panda 7-DOF 机械臂进行 PyBullet 物理模拟 涵盖动态重新规划、故障恢复、 政策执行、三项任务的已发布基线比较、 跨任务通用性、运行时 ECM 热插拔、 部件烧蚀和失效边界分析。 每个条件超过 100 次随机试验的实验结果表明 EROS 在所有三项任务中均取得了 100% 的任务成功 与已发布的基线（BehaviorTree.CPP 式执行和 ProgPrompt 式执行） 为 92–93%，平坦管道为 67–73%）， 基于规则的策略层阻止所有无效操作，零错误接受 （构造的确定性结果）， 运行时的好处可以推广到不同的任务，无需针对特定任务进行调整， 并且 ECM 可以在运行时加载，并且交换后任务 100% 成功。Robotic systems lack a principled abstraction for organizing intelligence, capabilities, and execution in a unified manner. Existing approaches either couple skills within monolithic architectures or decompose functionality into loosely coordinated modules or multiple agents, often without a coherent model of identity and control authority. In this paper, we argue that a robot should not be constructed as a collection of agents, but as a single persistent intelligent subject whose capabilities are extended through installable packages. We formalize this view as a single-agent robotic architecture, which we refer to as AEROS (Agent Execution Runtime Operating System), in which each robot corresponds to one persistent agent, and capabilities are provided through Embodied Capability Modules (ECMs). Each ECM encapsulates executable skills, models, and tools, while execution constraints and safety guarantees are enforced by a policy-separated runtime. This separation enables modular extensibility, composable capability execution, and consistent system-level safety. We implement a reference prototype and evaluate the architecture in PyBullet physics simulation with a Franka Panda 7-DOF manipulator across eight experiments covering dynamic re-planning, failure recovery, policy enforcement, published-baseline comparison on three tasks, cross-task generality, runtime ECM hot-swapping, component ablation, and failure boundary analysis. Experimental results over 100 randomized trials per condition demonstrate that AEROS achieves 100% task success across all three tasks versus published baselines (BehaviorTree.CPP-style execution and ProgPrompt-style execution at 92–93%, flat pipeline at 67–73%), the rule-based policy layer blocks all invalid actions with zero false acceptances (a deterministic result by construction), the runtime benefits generalize across diverse tasks without task-specific tuning, and ECMs can be loaded at runtime with 100% post-swap task success.

1 简介Introduction

机器人系统正在经历从特定任务自动化的根本转变 到通用智能系统。 然而，尽管大型模型和体现智能取得了快速进展Robotic systems are undergoing a fundamental transition from task-specific automation to general-purpose intelligent systems. However, despite rapid progress in large models and embodied intelligenceAhn et al. (2022); Brohan et al. (2023a); Driess et al. (2023), 仍然缺乏组织情报的原则性系统架构， 能力，并以统一的方式执行。, there remains a lack of a principled system architecture for organizing intelligence, capabilities, and execution in a unified manner.

现有的机器人系统通常采用两种方法之一。 第一个依赖于整体架构，其中技能和控制逻辑紧密耦合， 使系统难以扩展或重用。 第二个将功能分解为松散耦合的模块或多个代理， 但往往缺乏统一的身份、记忆和控制权限模型， 导致系统行为分散和安全挑战。Existing robotic systems typically adopt one of two approaches. The first relies on monolithic architectures in which skills and control logic are tightly coupled, making systems difficult to extend or reuse. The second decomposes functionality into loosely coupled modules or multiple agents, but often lacks a unified model of identity, memory, and control authority, leading to fragmented system behavior and safety challenges.

在这项工作中，我们认为机器人不应该被构建为代理的集合， 而是作为一个持久的智能主体，其功能通过可安装的软件包进行扩展。 我们将这种观点形式化为In this work, we argue that a robot should not be constructed as a collection of agents, but as a single persistent intelligent subject whose capabilities are extended through installable packages. Unlike prior approaches, ECMs do not encapsulate independent agents, but instead provide capabilities that are invoked by the single persistent agent.

为了确保安全和可组合的执行，我们进一步提出了To ensure safe and composable execution, we further propose a策略分离的运行时policy-separated runtime, 其中执行约束、权限和安全策略是独立执行的 来自能力定义。 这种分离使得相同的 ECM 能够在不同的安全和资源限制下运行， 同时保留系统级保证。, in which execution constraints, permissions, and safety policies are enforced independently from capability definitions. 5.3 政策沙箱Policy Sandbox

实施的核心目标是强制执行 策略逻辑分离原则。 为此，我们在运行时内实现了一个策略沙箱， 它在执行请求的操作之前评估是否允许这些操作。A central objective of the implementation is to enforce the Policy-Logic Separation Principle. To this end, we implement a policy sandbox inside the runtime, which evaluates whether requested actions are permitted before they are executed.

每个 ECM 都与声明的权限配置文件相关联， 包括访问传感器、执行器、通信通道和计算资源。 这些声明本身并不授予权力； 相反，它们由运行时解释， 它强制执行活动策略配置。 因此，相同的 ECM 可以部署在不同的运行时策略下 无需修改其内部逻辑。Each ECM is associated with a declared permission profile, including access to sensors, actuators, communication channels, and computational resources. These declarations do not grant authority by themselves; instead, they are interpreted by the runtime, which enforces the active policy configuration. As a result, the same ECM can be deployed under different runtime policies without modification to its internal logic.

政策沙箱在两个层面上运行。The policy sandbox operates at two levels.

在访问控制级别，它规定给定功能可以使用哪些资源。 例如，可以允许 ECM 读取对象状态观察结果 但拒绝直接访问运动执行接口。 同样，访问网络服务、日志记录通道或可选加速器 可以通过运行时策略有选择地启用或禁用。At the access-control level, it regulates which resources may be used by a given capability. For example, an ECM may be permitted to read object state observations but denied direct access to motion execution interfaces. Similarly, access to networked services, logging channels, or optional accelerators can be selectively enabled or disabled through runtime policy.

在执行控制层面，它限制了行动的执行方式。 在将操作转发到 ROS 2 或 PyBullet 之前， 运行时根据全局安全和资源规则检查它， 例如执行器范围、运动限制或执行配额。 违反政策的行为将被拒绝或减弱， 并且运行时将该事件记录为策略违规。At the execution-control level, it constrains how actions are carried out. Before an action is forwarded to ROS 2 or PyBullet, the runtime checks it against global safety and resource rules, such as actuator scope, motion limits, or execution quotas. Actions that violate policy are rejected or attenuated, and the runtime records the event as a policy violation.

为了支持分析和调试， 沙箱包括运行时监控和审计日志记录。 每个被阻止的操作、策略拒绝和功能激活事件 与其关联的 ECM 和执行上下文一起记录。 这种监测机制对于实证评估至关重要 在部分To support analysis and debugging, the sandbox includes runtime monitoring and audit logging. Every blocked action, policy rejection, and capability activation event is recorded together with its associated ECM and execution context. This monitoring mechanism is essential both for the empirical evaluation in Section6并证明政策执行仍然是技能逻辑之外的。and for demonstrating that policy enforcement remains external to skill logic.

Designing and validating such fault tolerance mechanisms is an important direction for future work.

7.3 通用性和平台独立性Generality and Platform Independence

尽管参考实现是基于 ROS 2 和 PyBullet 构建的， 所提出的架构不依赖于特定的中间件或模拟框架。 代理逻辑、功能包和运行时执行之间的分离 允许系统映射到不同的执行后端。Although the reference implementation is built on ROS 2 and PyBullet, the proposed architecture is not tied to a specific middleware or simulation framework. The separation between agent logic, capability packages, and runtime enforcement allows the system to be mapped onto different execution backends.

特别是，我们架构中的持久代理代表了一个逻辑 控制实体而不是具体的实现。 它可以使用各种代理系统进行实例化， 包括基于LLM的规划代理或传统的基于规则的控制器， 使框架与模型无关并且与实现无关。 同样，ECM接口和运行时可以适应不同的 硬件平台或实时控制系统。 这表明该架构定义了候选计算模型 对于具体化智能系统， 而不是特定于特定软件堆栈的框架。 跨不同平台和任务域验证这种通用性 仍是今后工作的重要方向。In particular, the persistent agent in our architecture represents a logical control entity rather than a specific implementation. It can be instantiated using a wide range of agentic systems, including LLM-based planning agents or conventional rule-based controllers, making the framework both model-agnostic and implementation-agnostic. Similarly, the ECM interface and runtime can be adapted to different hardware platforms or real-time control systems. This suggests that the architecture defines a candidate computational model for embodied intelligent systems, rather than a framework specific to a particular software stack. Validating this generality across diverse platforms and task domains remains an important direction for future work.

7.4 单代理范围和多机器人扩展Single-Agent Scope and Multi-Robot Extension

这项工作侧重于单机器人设置， 其中一个持久代理管理整个系统。 我们承认单个机器人内的多代理分解 提供真正的优势： 跨子系统的并发推理可以减少延迟， 专业代理可独立开发和测试， 代理边界提供了自然的故障隔离单元。 这些属性在大型系统中特别有价值 具有异构硬件和时间关键的子系统。This work focuses on the single-robot setting, where one persistent agent governs the entire system. We acknowledge that multi-agent decomposition within a single robot offers genuine advantages: concurrent reasoning across subsystems can reduce latency, specialized agents can be independently developed and tested, and agent boundaries provide natural fault-isolation units. These properties are particularly valuable in large-scale systems with heterogeneous hardware and time-critical subsystems.

然而，我们认为对于广泛的操纵和服务任务， 这些好处被成本所抵消 保持多个控制机构的一致性， 解决共享状态中的冲突， 并调试代理间交互产生的紧急行为。 单代理模型用并行性换取一致性： 它提供了身份、记忆和决策权的单一位置 这简化了对系统行为和安全性的推理。However, we argue that for a broad class of manipulation and service tasks, these benefits are outweighed by the costs of maintaining consistency across multiple control authorities, resolving conflicts in shared state, and debugging emergent behavior arising from inter-agent interactions. The single-agent model trades parallelism for coherence: it provides a single locus of identity, memory, and decision authority that simplifies reasoning about system behavior and safety.

重要的是，这并不排除多机器人场景。 相反，我们设想一个系统，其中每个机器人对应一个持久代理， 多机器人协调是由更高级别的代理之间的交互产生的。 这种区别避免了在单个机器人中引入多个代理， 同时仍然允许构建分布式系统 作为单代理机器人的集合。Importantly, this does not preclude multi-robot scenarios. Instead, we envision a system in which each robot corresponds to one persistent agent, and multi-robot coordination emerges from interactions between agents at a higher level. This distinction avoids introducing multiple agents within a single robot, while still allowing distributed systems to be constructed as collections of single-agent robots.

7.5 未来的方向Future Directions

主要方向包括： (1) 具有物理策略验证和模拟到真实传输的真实机器人部署； (2) 基于学习的能力获取，从数据中学习技能 并封装为 ECM； (3) 具有 ECM 版本控制、共享和信任的大规模能力生态系统； (4) 标准化架构基准，实现受控 架构与架构的比较。Key directions include: (1) real-robot deployment with physical policy validation and sim-to-real transfer; (2) learning-based capability acquisition, where skills are learned from data and packaged as ECMs; (3) large-scale capability ecosystems with ECM versioning, sharing, and trust; and (4) standardized architectural benchmarks enabling controlled architecture-vs-architecture comparisons.
