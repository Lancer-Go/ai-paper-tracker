Realizing this potential, however, depends on effective whole-body control that requires the coordinated regulation of numerous degrees of freedom to achieve natural, responsive and robust behaviors across multiple tasks and environmental contexts.

We instantiate motion tracking with Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) [17], given its mature ecosystem in humanoid control, while noting that alternative algorithms have also shown promising results [18, 19]. Existing studies often equate training data scaling with increasing the number of reference motions. However, under the PPO framework, effective training data are the on-policy rollouts collected through environment interaction. Therefore, data quantity is more directly governed by factors such as the number of parallel environments and the rollout horizon, whereas increasing the number of reference motions primarily shapes the behavioral distribution and enriches its diversity when properly curated. We thus posit that data scaling for BFM pretraining emerges from the synergy between quantity and diversity: quantity is obtained by scaling on-policy data collection, while diversity is achieved by expanding the reference motion corpus.

最后，我们强调，富有表现力且可扩展的模型架构对于扩展 BFM 至关重要。虽然现有的工作主要采用 MLP 作为主干，并且最近的研究表明扩展 MLP 可以提高性能 [11]，但我们研究更具表现力的架构是否表现出更强的扩展行为。除了架构设计之外，我们还进一步研究了行为意图的结构化潜在表示是否可以自然地出现，而不依赖于可能编码人类先验的辅助目标。这种表示可以促进 BFM 学习的行为知识的灵活利用和高效转移，从而支持多样化的下游应用。Finally, we emphasize that an expressive and scalable model architecture is essential for scaling BFMs. While existing works primarily adopt MLPs as the backbone, and recent studies have shown that scaling MLPs can improve performance [11], we investigate whether more expressive architectures exhibit stronger scaling behavior. Beyond architectural design, we further examine whether structured latent representations of behavioral intentions can naturally emerge without relying on auxiliary objectives that may encode human priors. Such representations could facilitate the flexible utilization and efficient transfer of behavioral knowledge learned by BFMs, thereby supporting diverse downstream applications.

Through comprehensive experiments in both simulation and real-world deployment, we validate our findings and demonstrate that our approach significantly improves control fidelity, execution robustness, and generalization capability, reducing Mean Per-Keypoint Position Error (MPKPE) on the test set by over 10% in local mode and 82% in global mode compared with existing humanoid controllers. BFM4Humanoid [6] pretrains BFMs within the DAgger framework [37], where actions are labeled by an expert policy trained via motion tracking. It further introduces an explicit control interface, enabling the pretrained model to directly respond to diverse behavior specifications at deployment. With the promptable latent space learned through pretraining, it enables zero-shot generalization and few-shot adaptation through sampling-based inference at test time. More recently, SONIC [11] explores the direct usage of motion tracking for BFM pretraining. By learning discrete behavioral representations and enforcing alignment across multiple command forms, it enables versatile control through the resulting shared latent space.

随着 BFM 继续在不同的人形控制设置中展现出强大的性能，扩展自然成为进一步提高其能力的关键方向。然而，它们的具体缩放行为尚未被系统地识别。虽然 SONIC 通过扩展运动跟踪框架内的参考运动数量、模型容量和 GPU 小时数提供了初步证据，但它未能阐明控制有效扩展的关键因素之间的耦合，包括学习范式、训练数据和模型架构。在这项工作中，我们重新审视了运动跟踪的设计，阐明了基于 PPO 的预训练中训练数据的概念，并引入了一种更具表现力的架构，称为 Humanoid Transformer。这些探索旨在提供对 BFM 缩放更原则性的理解，并为通用人形控制奠定更坚实的基础。As BFMs continue to demonstrate strong performance across diverse humanoid control settings, scaling has naturally emerged as a key direction for further improving their capabilities. In this work, we revisit the design of motion tracking, clarify the notion of training data in PPO-based pretraining, and introduce a more expressive architecture termed the Humanoid Transformer. These explorations aim to offer a more principled understanding of BFM scaling and lay a stronger foundation for general humanoid control.

We further include an ablation baseline trained using the same pipeline but with the reward design of BeyondMimic [9], allowing us to evaluate the contribution of the proposed reward formulation. As shown in table 3, the proposed BFM consistently achieves higher success rates and lower tracking errors across both local and global control modes, outperforming all competing methods by a clear margin. Notably, the proposed reward formulation consistently yields lower global tracking errors than the ablation baseline, suggesting that tracking integrated whole-body trajectories in the global frame may provide more coherent behavioral guidance without compromising the coordination between root motion and whole-body pose evolution. 5 讨论5 Discussion

在本文中，我们通过重新审视三个基本方面：学习范式、行为数据和模型架构，对人形机器人行为基础模型的扩展行为进行了系统研究。通过广泛的实证研究，我们得出了一种有效的扩展方法，可以在控制保真度、执行鲁棒性和泛化能力方面产生显着的改进。然而，拟议的框架有其局限性。尽管我们策划了具有多种控制模式的统一控制接口，但仍不清楚该接口是否是最合适的抽象以及这些模式应如何与未来的高层策略集成。因此，BFM 的设计应随着高层政策学习的进步而不断发展。此外，当前 BFM 预训练的基础设施仍然相对初级且受到限制，限制了其在更广泛环境中的可扩展性。我们希望这项工作为 BFM 的系统开发奠定基础，最终有助于实现通用类人智能。In this paper, we present a systematic investigation of the scaling behavior of Behavior Foundation Models for humanoid robots by revisiting three fundamental aspects: the learning paradigm, behavioral data, and model architecture. Through extensive empirical studies, we derive an effective scaling recipe that yields substantial improvements in control fidelity, execution robustness, and generalization capability. Nevertheless, the proposed framework has its limitations. Although we curate a unified control interface with diverse control modes, it remains unclear whether this interface is the most appropriate abstraction and how these modes should be integrated with future high-level policies. Consequently, the design of BFMs should continue to evolve alongside advances in high-level policy learning. Moreover, the current infrastructure for BFM pretraining remains relatively preliminary and constrained, limiting its scalability to broader settings. We hope that this work provides a foundation for systematic development of BFMs, ultimately contributing to the realization of general-purpose humanoid intelligence.

B.6 使用模型架构进行扩展B.6 Scaling with Model Architectures

接下来我们探讨模型架构对 BFM 扩展行为的影响。具体来说，我们检查所提出的 Humanoid Transformer 是否优于 MLP 架构，以及进一步增加其容量是否会带来额外的性能提升。在本文中，参与者和评论家都使用相同的主干架构和模型能力进行实例化。它们仅在输入和输出维度上有所不同，反映了不对称的观察和预测目标。We next explore the effect of model architectures on the scaling behavior of BFMs. Specifically, we examine whether the proposed Humanoid Transformer outperforms the MLP architecture and whether further increasing its capacity leads to additional performance gains. Throughout this paper, the actor and critic are instantiated with identical backbone architectures and model capacities. They differ only in their input and output dimensions, reflecting the asymmetric observations and prediction objectives.

所有算法和环境配置在实验中保持固定，包括表 7 中列出的 PPO 超参数、64 个 GPU 的策略内数据收集配置（滚动范围为 64）以及参考运动数据集的 L 分区。我们仍然报告四种代表性控制模式下的两个测试集的结果：根和手模式（图24和25）、根和末端执行器模式（图26和27）、根和上半身模式（图28和29）和全身模式（图8和9）。我们评估每个实验 22,200 个训练周期后获得的检查点。All algorithmic and environmental configurations and kept fixed across experiments, including the PPO hyperparameters listed in table 7, the on-policy data collection configuration of 64 GPUs with a rollout horizon of 64, and the L partition of the reference motion dataset. We still report results of two test sets under four representative control modes: root-and-hand mode (figure 24 and 25), root-and-endeffector mode (figure 26 and 27), root-and-upper-body mode (figure 28 and 29), and whole-body mode (figure 8 and 9). We evaluate the checkpoint obtained after 22,200 training epochs for each experiment.

对于 MLP 主干，我们根据之前的工作采用了两种代表性配置。小配置包含 3.05M 参数，隐藏维度为 [1024, 768, 512]，而大配置包含 11.86M 参数，隐藏维度为 [2048, 2048, 1024, 1024, 512, 512]。两种 MLP 变体都采用 ELU 激活。没有对观察结果进行归一化。For the MLP backbone, we adopt two representative configurations following prior work. The small configuration contains 3.05M parameters, with hidden dimensions of [1024, 768, 512], while the large configuration contains 11.86M parameters, with hidden dimensions of [2048, 2048, 1024, 1024, 512, 512]. Both MLP variants employ ELU activations. No normalization is applied to the observations.

对于所提出的 Humanoid Transformer，我们通过改变四个关键组件来评估四种模型配置：嵌入维度、前馈维度、注意力头数量和网络深度。 S模型包含0.41M参数，嵌入维度为128，2个注意力头，前馈维度为128，以及2个Transformer层。 M模型包含300M个参数，嵌入维度为256，4个注意力头，前馈维度为256，4层。 L模型包含4.44M参数，与M模型具有相同的宽度，但将深度增加到6层，从而隔离了缩放网络深度的影响。最后，XL模型包含9.91M参数，嵌入维度为384，6个注意力头，前馈维度为384，6层，代表网络宽度和深度的联合缩放。For the proposed Humanoid Transformer, we evaluate four model configurations by varying four critical components: the embedding dimension, feed-forward dimension, number of attention heads, and network depth. The S model contains 0.41M parameters, with an embedding dimension of 128, 2 attention heads, a feed-forward dimension of 128, and 2 Transformer layers. The M model contains 3.00M parameters, with an embedding dimension of 256, 4 attention heads, a feed-forward dimension of 256, and 4 layers. The L model contains 4.44M parameters and shares the same width as the M model but increases the depth to 6 layers, thereby isolating the effect of scaling network depth. Finally, the XL model contains 9.91M parameters, with an embedding dimension of 384, 6 attention heads, a feed-forward dimension of 384, and 6 layers, representing joint scaling of both network width and depth.

值得注意的是，与针对大型语言模型进行的研究相比，我们的扩展研究仍然相对有限。这种限制源于两个主要因素。首先，人形机器人控制仍然缺乏支持真正大规模预训练所需的成熟基础设施。由于多 GPU 通信、模拟器不稳定以及针对人形基础模型定制的高效训练系统的探索有限，分布式训练仍然脆弱，更不用说社区尚未就适合人形机器人的基础模型范式达成共识。其次，实际部署施加了严格的计算限制。为了维持高于 50 Hz 的推理频率，同时为未来与高级模型的集成保留计算预算，行为基础模型的参数计数在当前硬件限制下无法无限增加。尽管如此，随着机载计算能力的不断提高，建立可扩展的训练管道对于未来的人形基础模型具有重要价值。我们正在积极努力解决当前硬件和软件系统的限制。尽管如此，我们相信，实质性进展最终需要更广泛的社会的协调努力。为了促进这一进展，我们将开源所有资源，希望为社区共同构建提供可扩展的基础。It is worth noting that our scaling study remains relatively limited compared with those conducted for Large Language Models. This limitation stems from two primary factors. First, humanoid robot control still lacks the mature infrastructure required to support truly large-scale pretraining. Distributed training remains fragile due to multi-GPU communication, simulator instability, and the limited exploration of efficient training systems tailored to humanoid foundation models, not to mention the community has yet to reach a consensus on the appropriate foundation model paradigm for humanoid robots. Second, practical deployment imposes strict computational constraints. To sustain an inference frequency above 50 Hz while reserving computational budget for future integration with high-level models, the parameter count of Behavior Foundation Models cannot be increased indefinitely under current hardware constraints. Nevertheless, as onboard computing capabilities continue to improve, establishing a scalable training pipeline is of great value for future humanoid foundation models. We are actively working to address these limitations with the constraints of current hardware and software systems. Nevertheless, we believe that substantial progress will ultimately require coordinated efforts from the broader community. To facilitate such progress, we will open-source all the resources, with the hope of providing a scalable foundation upon which the community can collectively build.
