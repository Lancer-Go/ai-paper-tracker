DeepBD：用于遗传性出生缺陷的变异优先级排序和诊断的基础代理工作流程DeepBD: A Grounded Agentic Workflow for Variant Prioritization and Diagnosis of Genetic Birth Defects

摘要Abstract

出生缺陷是胎儿流产、新生儿发病和长期残疾的主要原因。在疑似遗传病因的子集中，外显子组和基因组测序已将许多病例从变异检测转移到测序后解释：临床医生必须根据不完整的胎儿或婴儿表型以及来自群体遗传学、变异效应预测、基因疾病有效性、表型本体、细胞和通路背景、蛋白质结构和临床文献的异质证据对患者特异性候选变异进行排序。我们推出了 DeepBD，这是一种用于遗传性出生缺陷的变异优先级排序和诊断解释的基础代理工作流程。 DeepBD 将工作流程组织成法学硕士辅助的案例结构、预训练的证据引擎、专业证据模块和基础诊断审查层。证据引擎从结构化规则证据、序列和变异效应表示以及表型调节的生物背景中学习患者特定的变异评分，而专家模块和代理层则提供基于工具的细化、候选池审查和来自排名候选的面向诊断的综合。 DeepBD 使用包含 18,622 例的内部胎儿和婴儿队列开发，实现了 Recall@1/3/5/10 在内部保留的已解决案例基准上为 0.658/0.882/0.912/0.929，优于独立的 Exomiser、DeepRare 并提示 LLM 重新排名基线，在 Exomiser 派生的前 20 个候选变体上进行评估。消融和重叠分析表明，规则证据、机制背景和专家细化提供了互补信号。这些结果支持一种扎根的代理方法，其中数据驱动的证据整合、基于工具的细化和法学硕士辅助诊断审查在遗传性出生缺陷解释和诊断中被赋予不同的作用，并进行定量评估 专注于回顾性变量优先级性能。Birth defects are a major cause of fetal loss, neonatal morbidity and long-term disability. In the subset with suspected genetic etiologies, exome and genome sequencing have moved many cases from variant detection to post-sequencing interpretation: clinicians must rank patient-specific candidate variants under incomplete fetal or infant phenotypes and heterogeneous evidence from population genetics, variant-effect prediction, gene-disease validity, phenotype ontologies, cellular and pathway context, protein structure and clinical literature. We present DeepBD, a grounded agentic workflow for variant prioritization and diagnostic interpretation of genetic birth defects. DeepBD organizes the workflow into LLM-assisted case structuring, a pretrained evidence engine, specialist evidence modules and a grounded diagnostic review layer. The evidence engine learns patient-specific variant scores from structured rule evidence, sequence and variant-effect representations and phenotype-conditioned biological context, whereas specialist modules and the agentic layer provide tool-based refinement, candidate-pool review and diagnosis-oriented synthesis from ranked candidates. Developed using an in-house fetal and infant cohort comprising 18,622 cases, DeepBD achieved Recall@1/3/5/10 of 0.658/0.882/0.912/0.929 on an internal held-out solved-case benchmark, outperforming standalone Exomiser, DeepRare and prompted LLM reranking baselines evaluated on Exomiser-derived top-20 candidate variants. Ablation and overlap analyses indicate that rule evidence, mechanism context and specialist refinement contribute complementary signal. These results support a grounded agentic approach in which data-driven evidence integration, tool-based refinement and LLM-assisted diagnostic review are assigned distinct roles in genetic birth defect interpretation and diagnosis, with quantitative evaluation focused on retrospective variant-prioritization performance.

1 简介Introduction

出生缺陷，也称为先天性异常、先天性疾病或先天性畸形，包括子宫内生命期间出现的结构或功能异常，可在产前、出生时或婴儿期发现Birth defects, also referred to as congenital anomalies, congenital disorders or congenital malformations, comprise structural or functional abnormalities that arise during intrauterine life and may be recognized prenatally, at birth or during infancy[54, 6, 11]。他们代表 巨大的全球健康负担，影响了大约 6% 的婴儿 在世界范围内，对新生儿和 5 岁以下儿童死亡率造成很大影响. They represent a substantial global health burden, affecting an estimated 6% of babies worldwide and contributing considerably to neonatal and under-5 mortality[54, 6]。出生缺陷的原因多种多样，包括遗传、环境、感染和多因素因素。在这篇手稿中，我们使用遗传性出生缺陷来表示出生缺陷或疑似染色体或单基因病因的早发先天性表现。对于这一临床上可行的子集，分子诊断可以为胎儿预后、围产期管理、新生儿护理、复发风险咨询和生殖决策提供信息. Birth defects have heterogeneous causes, including genetic, environmental, infectious and multifactorial factors. In this manuscript, we use genetic birth defects to denote birth defects or early-onset congenital presentations with suspected chromosomal or single-gene etiologies. For this clinically actionable subset, a molecular diagnosis can inform fetal prognosis, perinatal management, neonatal care, recurrence-risk counseling and reproductive decisions[33, 34, 52]。大型发育障碍研究还表明，致病性新发和罕见编码变异对严重的早发性发育表型做出了重大贡献，其中许多与出生缺陷的临床空间重叠. Large developmental-disorder studies have also shown that pathogenic de novo and rare coding variants make a substantial contribution to severe early-onset developmental phenotypes, many of which overlap the clinical space of birth defects[9, 29].

基因组测试已经改变了诊断最困难的部分。染色体微阵列、外显子组测序和基因组测序可以在胎儿结构异常或婴儿早期表现后识别候选分子原因，多项研究已经确立了测序在这些情况下的临床价值Genomic testing has changed where the hardest part of diagnosis lies. Chromosomal microarray, exome sequencing and genome sequencing can identify candidate molecular causes after fetal structural anomalies or early infant presentations, and multiple studies have established the clinical value of sequencing in these settings[27, 37, 13, 30, 53]。测试后的工作流程仍然很困难。一个典型的病例可能包含过滤前的数千个变异、注释后的几个看似合理的基因以及至少一个意义不确定的变异。产前和儿科测序研究反复强调，产量不仅取决于测序技术，还取决于适应症、表型质量、家族结构、变异解释和再分析能力. The post-test workflow remains difficult. A typical case may contain thousands of variants before filtering, several plausible genes after annotation and at least one variant of uncertain significance. Prenatal and pediatric sequencing studies have repeatedly emphasized that yield depends not only on sequencing technology, but also on indication, phenotype quality, family structure, variant interpretation and reanalysis capacity[34, 52, 53]。结果是测序后的瓶颈：系统必须确定哪种变异与当前胎儿或婴儿有因果关系，哪些证据是可靠的，以及如何将排序后的候选变异转化为有临床意义的诊断假设。. The result is a post-sequencing bottleneck: the system must determine which variant is causal for the current fetus or infant, which evidence is reliable and how a ranked candidate should be converted into a clinically meaningful diagnostic hypothesis.

遗传性出生缺陷是这一瓶颈的集中体现。胎儿表型通常通过超声、磁共振成像或其他间接测量来观察；表型概况可能不完整，因为许多发育特征尚不可见；并且其他特征可能仅在出生后或稍后的临床随访后出现。人类表型本体 (HPO) 术语为表型异常提供了可计算的语言，表型驱动的系统已成为基因组诊断的核心Genetic birth defects impose a concentrated version of this bottleneck. Fetal phenotypes are often observed through ultrasound, magnetic resonance imaging or other indirect measurements; the phenotype profile may be incomplete because many developmental features are not yet visible; and additional features may appear only after birth or later clinical follow-up. Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO) terms provide a computable language for phenotypic abnormalities, and phenotype-driven systems have become central to genomic diagnostics[39, 22]。然而，对遗传性出生缺陷的解释还需要通过细胞、解剖学和通路背景将早期器官系统表型与候选基因联系起来。这就是为什么表型资源、基因型-表型知识图和病例报告搜索系统是工作流程的有用组成部分. However, interpretation of genetic birth defects also requires connecting early organ-system phenotypes to candidate genes through cellular, anatomical and pathway contexts. This is why phenotype resources, genotype-phenotype knowledge graphs and case-report search systems are useful components of the workflow[42, 14]。一个强大的系统必须以可评分、审计和审查的形式保存患者表型、变异证据和生物学背景。. A robust system must preserve the patient phenotype, variant evidence and biological context in a form that can be scored, audited and reviewed.

计算变量优先级工具已经解决了这个问题的重要部分。 Phen-Gen、Exomiser、Xrare 和 LIRICAL 将表型信息与遗传证据相结合，以优先考虑基因、变异或候选疾病Computational variant-prioritization tools have addressed important parts of this problem. Phen-Gen, Exomiser, Xrare and LIRICAL integrate phenotype information with genetic evidence to prioritize genes, variants or candidate diseases[18, 44, 25, 40]。 SIFT、PolyPhen-2、MutationTaster、CADD、REVEL、PrimateAI、SpliceAI 和 AlphaMissense 等变异效应预测因子和注释资源提供了有关分子后果的补充证据. Variant-effect predictors and annotation resources such as SIFT, PolyPhen-2, MutationTaster, CADD, REVEL, PrimateAI, SpliceAI and AlphaMissense provide complementary evidence about molecular consequence[35, 1, 21, 41, 16, 47, 17, 7]。临床数据库和管理框架（包括 ClinVar、ClinGen、HGMD、OMIM 和 ACMG/AMP 指南）为变异解释提供可审核的人类证据和规则. Clinical databases and curation frameworks including ClinVar, ClinGen, HGMD, OMIM and ACMG/AMP guidelines provide auditable human evidence and rules for variant interpretation[38, 46, 23, 45, 4]。这些系统和资源使基因组解释更加系统化。剩下的差距是如何组织出生缺陷环境的异质信号：独立的优先顺序、分子预测因子和临床数据库都提供了有用的证据，但它们的输出仍然必须针对患者特定的胎儿或婴儿表型进行加权，在不完整或冲突时进行协调，并转化为可审查的诊断假设。. These systems and resources have made genomic interpretation more systematic. The remaining gap is how to organize heterogeneous signals for the birth-defect setting: standalone prioritizers, molecular predictors and clinical databases each provide useful evidence, but their outputs must still be weighted for a patient-specific fetal or infant phenotype, reconciled when incomplete or conflicting and translated into a reviewable diagnostic hypothesis.

基于学习的优先级方法使该问题的数据要求明确。 Xrare 联合对表型和遗传证据进行建模，DeepPVP 使用深度学习进行基于表型的因果变异优先级排序，MAVERICK 将深度结构化学习应用于孟德尔变异优先级排序，AI-MARRVEL 使用知识驱动的随机森林系统，对诊断病例的数百万个变异进行训练Learning-based prioritization methods make the data requirement of this problem explicit. Xrare jointly models phenotype and genetic evidence, DeepPVP uses deep learning for phenotype-based causal-variant prioritization, MAVERICK applies deep structured learning to Mendelian variant prioritization and AI-MARRVEL uses a knowledge-driven random-forest system trained on millions of variants from diagnosed cases[25, 5, 8, 28]。 SHEPHERD 进一步表明，知识引导的学习可以支持表型驱动的罕见疾病诊断，同时还说明了当直接标记的患者数据稀缺时如何使用模拟和小样本策略. SHEPHERD further shows that knowledge-guided learning can support phenotype-driven rare-disease diagnosis, while also illustrating how simulation and few-shot strategies are used when directly labeled patient data are scarce[3]。这些研究反对简单的规则与学习二分法。更相关的问题是，学习在哪里得到真实案例分布的支持，以及外部证据在哪里应该保持模块化。对于遗传性出生缺陷，公共资源包含丰富的疾病、变异和本体知识，产前测序研究提供重要的队列和标准. These studies argue against a simple rule-versus-learning dichotomy. The more relevant question is where learning is supported by real case distributions and where external evidence should remain modular. For genetic birth defects, public resources contain rich disease, variant and ontology knowledge, and prenatal sequencing studies provide important cohorts and standards[27, 13, 30, 53]。然而，出生缺陷优先排序引擎所需的特定训练基础——胎儿或早期婴儿表型、来自同一患者的测序衍生的竞争性变异、临床策划的因果标签和现实的测序后噪音——很难大规模获得。. Yet the specific training substrate needed for a birth-defect prioritization engine–fetal or early-infant phenotypes, sequencing-derived competing variants from the same patient, clinically curated causal labels and realistic post-sequencing noise–is difficult to obtain at scale.

基于法学硕士和代理的医疗人工智能系统最近扩大了设计空间。 Med-PaLM 和 AMIE 表明大型语言模型可以编码临床知识、回答医学问题并支持结构化评估下的诊断对话LLM-based and agentic medical AI systems have recently expanded the design space. Med-PaLM and AMIE showed that large language models can encode clinical knowledge, answer medical questions and support diagnostic dialogue under structured evaluation[43, 49]。 DeepRare 展示了具有可追溯推理的代理罕见疾病系统，Hygieia 和 Berrylyzer 探索了罕见疾病和产前基因诊断的相关代理设计. DeepRare demonstrated an agentic rare-disease system with traceable reasoning, and Hygieia and Berrylyzer explored related agentic designs for rare-disease and prenatal genetic diagnosis[55, 26, 31]。这些研究表明了语言模型对于临床语言规范化、检索、工具协调、反思和人类可读综合的价值。遗传性出生缺陷的解释给此类系统带来了不同的负担。核心决策通常不是开放式的诊断头脑风暴，而是在不完整发育表型下对患者特异性候选变异进行排名比较。通用代理循环可以检索和总结证据，但变量级因果排序需要一个稳定的证据基础，可以从之前的胎儿和婴儿案例中学习，纳入明确的临床规则并将外部工具视为校准证据而不是不受约束的推理步骤。这激发了扎根的代理设计，其中证据计算、专业工具的使用和基于法学硕士的审查被分配了不同的角色。. These studies show the value of language models for clinical-language normalization, retrieval, tool coordination, reflection and human-readable synthesis. Genetic birth defect interpretation places a different load on such systems. The central decision is usually not open-ended diagnostic brainstorming, but a ranked comparison among patient-specific candidate variants under incomplete developmental phenotypes. General-purpose agent loops can retrieve and summarize evidence, but variant-level causal ranking requires a stable evidence substrate that can learn from prior fetal and infant cases, incorporate explicit clinical rules and treat external tools as calibrated evidence rather than unconstrained reasoning steps. This motivates a grounded agentic design in which evidence computation, specialist tool use and LLM-based review are assigned distinct roles.

DeepBD 是围绕这一证据分配原则设计的。该系统首先根据内部胎儿和婴儿变异数据初始化预训练的证据引擎，然后使用策划的已解决案例对其进行优化，以实现因果变异优先级排序。对于每个患者和每个候选变异，证据引擎根据测序衍生的变异特征、明确的临床规则和表型条件的生物学背景构建结构化基质。然后，专业模块在该基底周围添加选定的外部证据信号。扎根的诊断代理可协助构建案例、组织保留来源的证据、协调细化模块并支持排名候选的 top-k 审查和面向诊断的综合。该设计使用代理功能进行管理、路由、审查和证据合成，同时将变量级别评分放置在可训练的模型中，该模型可以进行基准测试和消融。DeepBD was designed around this evidence-allocation principle. The system first initializes a pretrained evidence engine on in-house fetal and infant variant data and then optimizes it for causal variant prioritization using curated solved cases. For each patient and each candidate variant, the evidence engine constructs a structured substrate from sequencing-derived variant features, explicit clinical rules and phenotype-conditioned biological context. Specialist modules then add selected external evidence signals around this substrate. A grounded diagnostic agent assists case structuring, organizes provenance-preserving evidence, coordinates refinement modules and supports top-k review and diagnosis-oriented synthesis from ranked candidates. This design uses agentic capabilities for curation, routing, review and evidence synthesis while placing variant-level scoring in a trainable model that can be benchmarked and ablated.

在这里，我们描述了 DeepBD 工作流程，并在内部胎儿和婴儿队列中利用测序和表型数据对其进行了评估。我们将遗传出生缺陷作为胎儿到早期婴儿的诊断连续体来关注，因为产前发现和产后早期表型通常属于同一分子诊断途径。在内部已解决案例基准中，我们表明 DeepBD 比已建立的基于工具、LLM 和代理重排基线改进了因果变量优先级，恢复了案例的补充子集，并从多个证据级别得出其性能。这些结果支持对遗传性出生缺陷进行测序后解释和诊断的基于证据的代理方法。Here we describe the DeepBD workflow and evaluate it in an in-house fetal and infant cohort with sequencing and phenotype data. We focus on genetic birth defects as a fetal-to-early-infant diagnostic continuum, because prenatal findings and early postnatal phenotypes often belong to the same molecular diagnostic pathway. In an internal solved-case benchmark, we show that DeepBD improves causal variant prioritization over established tool-based, LLM and agentic reranking baselines, recovers a complementary subset of cases and derives its performance from multiple evidence levels. The results support an evidence-grounded, agentic approach for post-sequencing interpretation and diagnosis of genetic birth defects.

2 结果Results

2.1 接地代理工作流程Grounded agentic workflow

DeepBD 将患者表型概况和测序衍生的候选变异表作为输入。表型输入可以是结构化的 HPO 标识符或自由文本临床描述，它们映射到 HPO 概念以进行下游计算DeepBD takes as input a patient phenotype profile and a sequencing-derived candidate-variant table. The phenotype input can be structured HPO identifiers or free-text clinical descriptions that are mapped to HPO concepts for downstream computation[39, 22]。在产前病例中，表型可能包括超声或影像学表现，例如颈项透明层增加、先天性心脏缺陷、骨骼发育不良、肾脏异常或胎儿生长受限。对于婴儿病例，可能包括产后检查、生长、神经发育或实验室检查结果。基因组输入是 VCF 或 TSV 衍生表，其中包含基因组坐标、参考和替代等位基因、受影响的基因、结果注释、基因型、转录本或 HGVS 描述和测序质量字段。 DeepBD 一次处理一个患者病例，对保留该病例中的候选变异进行评分，并返回一个包含证据字段的排名表以供审查。. In a prenatal case, the phenotype may consist of ultrasound or imaging findings such as increased nuchal translucency, congenital heart defect, skeletal dysplasia, renal anomaly or fetal growth restriction. In an infant case, it may include postnatal examination, growth, neurodevelopmental or laboratory findings. The genomic input is a VCF- or TSV-derived table containing genomic coordinates, reference and alternate alleles, affected gene, consequence annotation, genotype, transcript or HGVS description and sequencing quality fields. DeepBD processes one patient case at a time, scores retained candidate variants within that case and returns a ranked table with evidence fields for review.

工作流程是围绕证据分配原则设计的（图 1）。The workflow was designed around an evidence-allocation principle (Fig.1一个）。遗传出生缺陷解释包含具有不同计算特征的任务。表型标准化、候选人库审查和证据合成需要大量语言，并受益于法学硕士辅助的结构化。变体排名是一个高维、案例条件比较问题，受益于可训练的证据引擎。 SHEPHERD、Exomiser 和结构建模等外部算法是有用的专业证据来源，但它们的输出需要根据患者的情况进行解释。因此，DeepBD 将工作流程组织为四个相互作用的层：LLM 辅助的案例结构、预训练的证据引擎、专业证据模块和基础诊断代理。a). Genetic birth defect interpretation contains tasks with different computational character. Phenotype normalization, candidate-pool review and evidence synthesis are language-heavy and benefit from LLM-assisted structuring. Variant ranking is a high-dimensional, case-conditioned comparison problem and benefits from a trainable evidence engine. External algorithms such as SHEPHERD, Exomiser and structure modeling are useful specialist evidence sources, but their outputs need to be interpreted in the context of the patient. DeepBD therefore organizes the workflow into four interacting layers: LLM-assisted case structuring, a pretrained evidence engine, specialist evidence modules and a grounded diagnostic agent.

病例构建层将异构的临床基因组输入转换为可计算的工作空间。临床描述被映射到 HPO 术语，同时保留原始文本，因为胎儿和婴儿表型可能不完整、来自成像或不同地点的措辞不同。测序结果被归一化为每个变异记录，包括基因组坐标、基因分配、转录水平结果、接合性和质量字段。这一层故意很轻：它准备结构化输入并保留来源，但它不能确定因果变量。The case-structuring layer converts heterogeneous clinical-genomic input into a computable workspace. Clinical descriptions are mapped to HPO terms while preserving the original text, because fetal and infant phenotypes can be incomplete, imaging-derived or phrased differently across sites. Sequencing results are normalized into per-variant records with genomic coordinates, gene assignment, transcript-level consequence, zygosity and quality fields. This layer is deliberately light: it prepares structured inputs and preserves provenance, but it does not determine the causal variant.

预训练的证据引擎是核心排名组件。对于每个候选变体，它构建三个核心证据表示。首先，规则证据编码临床可解释的信号，例如等位基因频率、后果、剂量敏感性、基因疾病有效性和策划的变异证据The pretrained evidence engine is the central ranking component. For each candidate variant, it builds three core evidence representations. First, rule evidence encodes clinically interpretable signals such as allele frequency, consequence, dosage sensitivity, gene-disease validity and curated variant evidence[20, 46, 23, 45, 38]。其次，变异内在证据代表来自基因组基础模型序列表示和变异效应预测因子的序列背景和分子效应信息. Second, variant-intrinsic evidence represents sequence context and molecular-effect information from genomic foundation-model sequence representations and variant-effect predictors[36, 16, 17, 7]。第三，表型调节的生物背景通过细胞、解剖和通路背景将候选基因与患者的 HPO 图谱联系起来，然后通过知识引导的注意力计算图形衍生的表示. Third, phenotype-conditioned biological context links the candidate gene to the patient’s HPO profile through cellular, anatomical and pathway context, then computes a graph-derived representation through knowledge-guided attention[51, 15, 32, 10]。这些证据流被融合成患者特定的变异评分。. These evidence streams are fused into a patient-specific variant score.

这种分工是由数据驱动的。证据引擎被分配了重复的、可学习的解释部分：如何在许多胎儿和婴儿病例中对规则证据、变异内在特征和表型条件的生物学背景进行加权。专家模块分配的证据是有用的，但可用性不均匀、计算量较大或可能随着外部资源的改进而改变。诊断代理被分配面向语言和面向审查的任务：管理、约束重排序、出处审计和综合。从这个意义上说，代理系统不仅仅是工具的集合；它是一个工作流程，它将可以从内部案例分布中学到的内容与应该保持可调用、可刷新和可检查的内容分开。This division of labor is data-enabled. The evidence engine is assigned the repeated, learnable part of interpretation: how rule evidence, variant-intrinsic features and phenotype-conditioned biological context should be weighted across many fetal and infant cases. The specialist modules are assigned evidence that is useful but unevenly available, computationally heavier or likely to change as external resources improve. The diagnostic agent is assigned language-facing and review-facing tasks: curation, constrained reranking, provenance audit and synthesis. In this sense, the agentic system is not only a collection of tools; it is a workflow that separates what can be learned from the in-house case distribution from what should remain callable, refreshable and inspectable.

专业证据模块围绕证据引擎运行。它们为值得更深入审查的候选者提供了额外的视图：来自 SHEPHERD 的表型驱动分数、用于候选池校准和 LLM 辅助重新排名的独立 Exomiser 跟踪，以及当蛋白质建模有意义时来自 AlphaFold DB 和 Rosetta/PyRosetta 式建模的结构通知细化Specialist evidence modules operate around the evidence engine. They provide additional views for candidates that warrant deeper review: phenotype-driven scores from SHEPHERD, an independent Exomiser track for candidate-pool calibration and LLM-assisted reranking, and structure-informed refinement from AlphaFold DB and Rosetta/PyRosetta-style modeling when protein modeling is meaningful[44, 3, 19, 50, 12, 2]。该层非常有用，因为某些证据源过于专业化、计算成本高昂或特定于变体类，无法被视为每个候选者的通用输入。 DeepBD 将它们视为可调用的证据模块，其输出可以完善排名并支持诊断审查。. This layer is useful because some evidence sources are too specialized, computationally expensive or variant-class-specific to be treated as universal inputs for every candidate. DeepBD treats them as callable evidence modules whose outputs can refine ranking and support diagnostic review.

接地诊断剂位于该证据管道的末端。其输入是一个排名证据工作区，其中包含候选变体、分数、特定源证据字段、图形跟踪和模块输出。当前的实现支持工作区中受限的 top-k 重新排名、反思式审查、证据审计和案例级证据合成。因果分数仍然锚定在证据引擎和专家模块输出中。The grounded diagnostic agent sits at the end of this evidence pipeline. Its input is a ranked evidence workspace containing candidate variants, scores, source-specific evidence fields, graph traces and module outputs. The current implementation supports constrained top-k reranking, reflection-style review, evidence audit and case-level evidence synthesis within the workspace. The causal score remains anchored in the evidence engine and specialist-module outputs.

2.2 胎儿-婴儿队列Fetal-infant cohort

This scope is deliberate. Prenatal genetic diagnosis and infant genetic diagnosis are often presented as separate workflows, but genetic birth defects frequently move across this boundary as the same fetus is imaged, sequenced, delivered, examined and reinterpreted over time[52, 53]。因此，与纯粹的成人罕见疾病基准或仅产前演示相比，胎儿到婴儿队列为该疾病空间提供了更合适的基础。. A fetal-to-infant cohort therefore provides a more appropriate substrate for this disease space than a purely adult rare-disease benchmark or a prenatal-only demonstration.

这种配对的表型测序资源也是该方法的一部分，而不仅仅是评估集。公共遗传疾病资源包含有价值的精选基因、变异、本体术语和病例报告，但它们通常分散在疾病数据库、变异档案、表型本体、文献和特定工具注释中。他们很少大规模地提供训练出生缺陷优先排序模型所需的确切组合：胎儿或早期婴儿表型、测序衍生的候选变异、临床策划的因果标签和来自同一患者的现实非因果竞争变异。内部队列允许 DeepBD 在对已解决案例进行监督优化之前了解该临床环境中遇到的候选变体的分布。这是将稳定、重复的证据集成分配给预先训练的证据引擎，同时将快速变化或高度专业化的证据留给模块化检索和审查的经验基础。This paired phenotype-sequencing resource is also part of the method rather than only an evaluation set. Public genetic-disease resources contain valuable curated genes, variants, ontology terms and case reports, but they are usually fragmented across disease databases, variant archives, phenotype ontologies, literature and tool-specific annotations. They rarely provide, at scale, the exact combination needed to train a birth-defect prioritization model: fetal or early-infant phenotypes, sequencing-derived candidate variants, clinically curated causal labels and realistic non-causal competing variants from the same patient. The in-house cohort allowed DeepBD to learn the distribution of candidate variants encountered in this clinical setting before supervised optimization on solved cases. This is the empirical basis for assigning stable, repeated evidence integration to the pretrained evidence engine while leaving rapidly changing or highly specialized evidence to modular retrieval and review.

这一点也澄清了为什么队列是贡献而不是后台记账。孟德尔和罕见疾病诊断中基于学习的优先顺序受益于真实诊断病例、策划的致病变异、模拟患者或广泛的临床基因组存储库This point also clarifies why the cohort is a contribution rather than background bookkeeping. Learning-based prioritizers in Mendelian and rare-disease diagnosis have benefited from real diagnosed cases, curated pathogenic variants, simulated patients or broad clinical-genomic repositories[5, 8, 28, 3]。这些资源很有价值，但它们并没有完全捕获胎儿到婴儿的出生缺陷环境，其中表型通常是成像衍生的、发育不完整的，并且与时间敏感的生殖或新生儿决策相关。内部队列使 DeepBD 能够访问临床医生在这种情况下测序后实际面临的竞争变异分布，这正是训练排序基质所需的信息，而不是仅仅依赖于固定规则或开放式代理推理。. Those resources are valuable, but they do not fully capture the fetal-to-infant birth-defect setting in which phenotypes are often imaging-derived, developmentally incomplete and linked to time-sensitive reproductive or neonatal decisions. The in-house cohort gives DeepBD access to the competing-variant distribution that clinicians actually face after sequencing in this setting, which is precisely the information needed to train a ranking substrate rather than relying only on fixed rules or open-ended agentic reasoning.

用于排名评估的已解决案例基准包含临床策划的阳性变异。保留的排名划分是在先证者层面进行抽样的，包含 549 个先证者病例，其中有 1,307 个精心策划的阳性变异。保留的候选变体涵盖 22 个类别，并包括广泛的预测分子后果（图 1）。The solved-case benchmark used for ranking evaluation contained clinically curated positive variants. The held-out ranking split was sampled at the proband level and contained 549 proband cases with 1,307 curated positive variants. Retained candidate variants spanned 22 categories and included a broad range of predicted molecular consequences (Fig.1d).这种分布促进了多层次的证据整合。一些阳性变异得到已建立的临床数据库或明确的功能丧失规则的支持；另一些则需要来自表型匹配、细胞或通路背景、基于序列的效应预测、外部优先排序工具或蛋白质结构分析的较弱但补充的证据。内部数据集不可公开发布，因为它包含敏感的胎儿、婴儿、基因组和医院衍生的临床信息，但其规模允许证据模型预训练、监督排名开发和消除遗传性出生缺陷难以获得的数据体系。d). The distribution motivates multi-level evidence integration. Some positive variants are supported by established clinical databases or clear loss-of-function rules; others require weaker but complementary evidence from phenotype match, cellular or pathway context, sequence-based effect prediction, external prioritization tools or protein-structure analysis. The in-house dataset is not publicly releasable because it contains sensitive fetal, infant, genomic and hospital-derived clinical information, but its scale allowed evidence-model pretraining, supervised ranking development and ablation in a data regime that is difficult to obtain for genetic birth defects.

该群体还塑造了工作流程的预期用途。在仅限产前的工作流程中，表型信息通常以影像描述符为主，并且可能会丢失产后表现。在一般罕见病工作流程中，表型可能包括多年的纵向体征、实验室结果和专家注释。这里使用的胎儿和婴儿病例介于这两个极端之间。它们包括出生前可见的结构发现、分娩后出现的早期新生儿体征以及在完全了解表型之前必须解释的分子结果。因此，DeepBD 将表型概况视为患者特定的背景，而不是完整的疾病描述。The cohort also shaped the intended use of the workflow. In a prenatal-only workflow, phenotype information is often dominated by imaging descriptors and may be missing postnatal manifestations. In a general rare-disease workflow, the phenotype may include years of longitudinal signs, laboratory results and specialist notes. The fetal and infant cases used here lie between these extremes. They include structural findings that are visible before birth, early neonatal signs that emerge after delivery and molecular results that must be interpreted before the phenotype is fully known. DeepBD therefore treats the phenotype profile as a patient-specific context rather than as a complete disease description.

2.3 变体级别重新排名基准Variant-level reranking benchmark

该评估的设计类似于测序后面临的实际问题：在胎儿或婴儿病例的保留候选变异中，系统对精选的阳性变异进行多高的排名？因此，我们使用病例级 Recall@K 而不是全局二元致病性分类。 Recall@K 衡量精心策划的阳性变异是否出现在用于人工审查的前 K 个候选者中，并且具有临床意义，因为遗传学家通常会在返回更广泛的筛选之前检查少量优先候选者。该变异水平终点适用于测序后解释，其中可操作单元是特定变异、遗传模式和证据包，而不仅仅是基因名称。The evaluation was designed to resemble the practical question faced after sequencing: among retained candidate variants for a fetal or infant case, how highly does the system rank the curated positive variant or variants? We therefore used case-level Recall@K rather than global binary pathogenicity classification. Recall@K measures whether a curated positive variant appears within the first K candidates for manual review, and is clinically meaningful because a geneticist usually examines a small number of prioritized candidates before returning to broader filtering. This variant-level endpoint is appropriate for post-sequencing interpretation, where the actionable unit is a specific variant, inheritance pattern and evidence package rather than a gene name alone.

我们将 DeepBD 与三个基线组进行了比较。第一组由独立的 Exomiser 组成，这是一种已建立的表型驱动变异和基因优先排序工具We compared DeepBD with three baseline groups. The first group consisted of standalone Exomiser, an established phenotype-driven variant and gene prioritization tool[44]。第二组由一般性和推理性法学硕士组成。由于通用 LLM 无法合理地处理完整的 VCF 规模候选空间，因此它们被评估为对每种情况下 Exomiser 派生的前 20 个候选变体进行重新排序；他们收到了结构化变异和表型信息，但没有 Exomiser 排名顺序。第三组包括 DeepRare，一种罕见疾病代理诊断系统. The second group consisted of prompted general and reasoning LLMs. Because general-purpose LLMs cannot reasonably process full VCF-scale candidate spaces, they were evaluated as rerankers over the Exomiser-derived top-20 candidate variants for each case; they received structured variant and phenotype information without the Exomiser rank order. The third group consisted of DeepRare, an agentic rare-disease diagnostic system[55]。我们在此配置下报告 DeepRare，因为它与其本机工具使用设置相匹配；然而，我们将其视为重新排名 基线，而不是 DeepRare 端到端诊断工作流程的完整再现。因此，LLM 和代理基线是在易于处理的候选重新排名设置中进行测试的，而不是作为完整的 VCF 到诊断管道。. We report DeepRare under this configuration because it matches its native tool-use setting; however, we treat this as a reranking baseline rather than a full reproduction of DeepRare’s end-to-end diagnostic workflow. Thus, LLM and agentic baselines were tested in a tractable candidate-reranking setting rather than as full VCF-to-diagnosis pipelines.

此设置还将排名性能与诊断解释区分开来。 DeepBD 的主要定量终点是变体优先级。包含诊断剂是因为解释和诊断需要保留来源的合成，但其叙述输出不被视为正确性的独立证明。因此，比较在候选者预先确定优先级后分离出证据权重，并单独评估诊断综合。This setting also distinguishes ranking performance from diagnostic explanation. DeepBD’s primary quantitative endpoint is variant prioritization. The diagnostic agent is included because interpretation and diagnosis require provenance-preserving synthesis, but its narrative output is not treated as independent proof of correctness. The comparison therefore isolates evidence weighting after candidate preprioritization, with diagnostic synthesis evaluated separately.

This complementarity is important because it shows that DeepBD is not merely reproducing the same candidates prioritized by existing systems. Rather, the workflow changes the relative ordering of variants in a way that produces additional top-ranked recoveries after conventional candidate preprioritization.

2.6 以诊断为导向的审查Diagnosis-oriented review

DeepBD 的诊断剂有意以证据为基础。它接收排名的候选人及其证据跟踪，将它们组织到可审查的工作区中并支持面向诊断的综合。在当前的实现中，该层包括受约束的 LLM 辅助的 top-k 重新排名和对结构化候选人记录的反思式审查，以及用于组织候选人机制的伴随证据分析提示。对于每个候选者，审查层可以公开群体频率信号、结果注释、变异效应预测、基因疾病有效性、ClinVar 或 HGMD 证据、表型相关图路径、高度关注的细胞或通路背景、外部工具信号和结构信息证据（如果可用）。这种结构旨在保留来源，以便临床医生可以检查候选诊断的来源。DeepBD’s diagnostic agent is intentionally grounded in the evidence substrate. It receives ranked candidates and their evidence traces, organizes them into a reviewable workspace and supports diagnosis-oriented synthesis. In the current implementation, this layer includes constrained LLM-assisted top-k reranking and reflection-style review over structured candidate records, with companion evidence-analysis prompts for organizing candidate mechanisms. For each candidate, the review layer can expose population-frequency signal, consequence annotation, variant-effect predictions, gene-disease validity, ClinVar or HGMD evidence, phenotype-linked graph paths, high-attention cellular or pathway context, external tool signals and structure-informed evidence when available. This structure is designed to preserve provenance so that a clinician can inspect the sources underlying a candidate diagnosis.

此设计不同于法学硕士优先的诊断工作流程。在 LLM-first 系统中，代理通常在一个循环中执行检索、推理和最终诊断。当任务是广泛的鉴别诊断时，特别是当输入是自由文本临床叙述并且所需的输出是具有支持理由的疾病列表时，此类系统可能非常强大This design differs from an LLM-first diagnostic workflow. In an LLM-first system, the agent often performs retrieval, reasoning and final diagnosis in one loop. Such systems can be powerful when the task is broad differential diagnosis, especially when the input is free-text clinical narrative and the desired output is a list of diseases with supporting rationale[55, 26, 31]。对于遗传性出生缺陷的测序后解释，关键决策是对患者特异性变异进行排名比较。 DeepBD 将代理层置于证据计算周围，可以提高可用性、证据审核和面向诊断的综合，同时因果排名仍保持基准。. For post-sequencing interpretation of genetic birth defects, the key decision is a ranked comparison among patient-specific variants. DeepBD places the agentic layer around evidence computation, where it can improve usability, evidence audit and diagnosis-oriented synthesis while the causal ranking remains benchmarkable.

排名靠前的变异并不自动成为诊断；它必须与遗传、表型匹配、分子结果和疾病有效性相关。因此，DeepBD 的输出被构建为排名证据表加上面向诊断的假设，而不是黑盒疾病标签。这对于胎儿和婴儿病例很重要，因为相同的分子结果可能导致预后讨论、验证性检测、父母检测、产后随访或以后的重新分析。在本研究中，主要的定量主张是变体优先级排序；诊断报告的质量、真实性和临床实用性需要单独的专家评估。A top-ranked variant is not automatically a diagnosis; it must be connected to inheritance, phenotype fit, molecular consequence and disease validity. DeepBD’s output is therefore framed as a ranked evidence table plus a diagnosis-oriented hypothesis rather than as a black-box disease label. This is important for fetal and infant cases, where the same molecular result may lead to prognosis discussion, confirmatory testing, parental testing, postnatal follow-up or later reanalysis. In this study, the primary quantitative claim is variant prioritization; diagnostic report quality, factuality and clinical usefulness require separate expert evaluation.

3 讨论Discussion

DeepBD 通过结合预先训练的证据引擎、专业证据模块和接地代理审查层来解决遗传性出生缺陷的测序后解释问题。关键的设计选择是证据分配。核心变异优先级排序是由可训练模型执行的，该模型集成了明确的临床规则、序列和变异效应信息以及表型条件的生物学背景。然后，专家模块对选定的候选者进行细化或情境化，代理组件围绕生成的证据表进行操作，以构建案例、协调证据使用、组织出处并支持诊断综合。这种架构与高风险的基因组工作流程非常匹配，其中可以通过排名准确性来衡量性能，并且解释必须保持可追溯至证据来源。DeepBD addresses post-sequencing interpretation of genetic birth defects by combining a pretrained evidence engine, specialist evidence modules and a grounded agentic review layer. The key design choice is evidence allocation. Core variant prioritization is performed by a trainable model that integrates explicit clinical rules, sequence and variant-effect information and phenotype-conditioned biological context. Specialist modules then refine or contextualize selected candidates, and agentic components operate around the resulting evidence table to structure cases, coordinate evidence use, organize provenance and support diagnostic synthesis. This architecture is well matched to a high-stakes genomic workflow in which performance can be measured by ranking accuracy and explanations must remain traceable to evidence sources.

这项工作将表型驱动的变异优先级扩展到出生缺陷背景感知的方向。 Phen-Gen、Exomiser、Xrare 和 LIRICAL 等已建立的系统表明表型和基因型信息应一起解释The work extends phenotype-driven variant prioritization in a birth-defect-context-aware direction. Established systems such as Phen-Gen, Exomiser, Xrare and LIRICAL showed that phenotype and genotype information should be interpreted together[18, 44, 25, 40]。 DeepBD 保留了这一原则，但在更广泛的患者特定背景下代表了候选变体：表型概况决定了每个候选基因周围的生物背景，并且在通过专业模块对选定的候选基因进行细化之前，这种表示与变体内在和基于规则的证据融合。由此产生的模型并不声称可以模拟人类发展。它的更狭义和更可靠的作用是计算表型条件的生物学背景以解释出生缺陷。. DeepBD retains this principle but represents the candidate variant in a broader patient-specific context: the phenotype profile conditions biological context around each candidate gene, and this representation is fused with variant-intrinsic and rule-based evidence before selected candidates are refined by specialist modules. The resulting model does not claim to simulate human development. Its narrower and more defensible role is to compute phenotype-conditioned biological context for birth defect interpretation.

这项工作还重新定义了代理人工智能在基因组诊断中的应用方式。法学硕士和代理人很有吸引力，因为他们可以解析临床语言，检索不断发展的外部知识，协调工具并生成人类可读的诊断叙述The work also reframes how agentic AI can be used in genomic diagnosis. LLMs and agents are attractive because they can parse clinical language, retrieve evolving external knowledge, coordinate tools and generate human-readable diagnostic narratives[43, 49, 55, 26, 31]。在变体解释中，自由形式推理是因果排序的脆弱基础，除非受到结构化证据的约束。因此，DeepBD 使用扎根的代理设计：代理层接收排序的候选者、证据向量和特定源的痕迹，其作用是综合和交流，而不是发明潜在的因果分数。这并不会降低代理商的价值；它使他们处于优势最有用且风险更容易控制的阶段。. In variant interpretation, free-form reasoning is a fragile foundation for causal ranking unless it is constrained by structured evidence. DeepBD therefore uses a grounded agentic design: the agentic layer receives ranked candidates, evidence vectors and source-specific traces, and its role is to synthesize and communicate rather than to invent the underlying causal score. This does not diminish the value of agents; it places them at the stage where their strengths are most useful and their risks are easier to control.

胎儿和婴儿队列是一个实用的优势。遗传性出生缺陷占据一个连续体，从产前成像开始，并可能持续到新生儿或婴儿随访。纯粹的产前框架会低估产后表型进化的作用，而广泛的罕见疾病框架会掩盖胎儿成像、发育不完整和出生缺陷类别的具体挑战。尽管隐私和数据使用限制限制了公开发布，但 18,622 例内部队列为模型开发提供了丰富的疾病背景基础。因此，目前的结果支持了以出生缺陷为中心的基准的价值，同时也强调了跨机构进行外部验证的必要性。The fetal and infant cohort is a practical strength. Genetic birth defects occupy a continuum that begins with prenatal imaging and may continue through neonatal or infant follow-up. A purely prenatal framing would understate the role of postnatal phenotype evolution, whereas a broad rare-disease framing would obscure the specific challenges of fetal imaging, developmental incompleteness and birth-defect categories. The 18,622-case in-house cohort provides a disease-context-rich substrate for model development, although privacy and data-use constraints limit public release. The present results therefore support the value of a birth-defect-focused benchmark, while also highlighting the need for external validation across institutions.

与单个端到端诊断聊天机器人相比，分层架构还提供了更清晰的验证路径。每个证据流都可以由领域专家检查，在消融中删除，在外部知识发生变化时刷新，并链接回使用它的排名候选者。这对于出生缺陷的解释很有用，因为证据会随着时间的推移而变化。产前影像可以在额外扫描后重新解释，新生儿检查结果可以添加新的 HPO 术语，父母检测可以澄清遗传，新发表的基因与疾病关系可以改变候选者的合理性。一个将表型标准化、变异排序、生物背景建模、基于工具的细化和诊断审查分开的系统可以比一次性预测管道更自然地支持这种迭代工作流程。The layered architecture also provides a clearer path for validation than a single end-to-end diagnostic chatbot. Each evidence stream can be inspected by a domain expert, removed in ablation, refreshed when external knowledge changes and linked back to the ranked candidate that used it. This is useful in birth defect interpretation because evidence changes over time. Prenatal imaging can be reinterpreted after additional scans, neonatal findings can add new HPO terms, parental testing can clarify inheritance and newly published gene-disease relationships can change the plausibility of a candidate. A system that separates phenotype normalization, variant ranking, biological-context modeling, tool-based refinement and diagnostic review can support this iterative workflow more naturally than a one-shot prediction pipeline.

因此，此类系统的临床作用应理解为优先级划分和证据组织。高 DeepBD 排名可以通过提出合理的因果变异并显示哪些证据流支持该排名来加速专家审查。低排名不应排除临床判断，特别是对于当前资源中代表性不足的变异类别、遗传模式或基因。这种划分对于负责任的部署非常重要：模型提供了可衡量的排名功能，而审查层提供了一个结构化的场所，可以在最终确定任何诊断之前检查支持证据、冲突证据和缺失证据。The clinical role of such a system should therefore be understood as prioritization and evidence organization. A high DeepBD rank can accelerate expert review by bringing plausible causal variants forward and by showing which evidence streams support that rank. A low rank should not exclude clinical judgment, especially for variant classes, inheritance patterns or genes that are under-represented in current resources. This division is important for responsible deployment: the model provides a measurable ranking function, and the review layer provides a structured place to examine supporting evidence, conflicting evidence and missing evidence before any diagnosis is finalized.

仍然存在一些限制。该队列是单一机构或协作衍生的，需要跨测序管道、医院和血统团体进行外部验证。当前的基准侧重于已解决或策划案例中的变体优先级；诊断报告质量、临床可操作性和预期工作流程影响需要额外的专家审查。 LLM 和代理基线被评估为 Exomiser 衍生的前 20 名候选者的重新排名，这在临床上易于处理，但与完整的 VCF 规模发现或广泛的疾病水平鉴别诊断不同。基于结构的精化对于某些错义或高优先级的不确定变体很有用，但不能在所有变体类别中统一应用。在当前版本中，代理诊断层有意为轻量级，并且随着其成熟，应与排名基底分开评估。未来的工作将把评估范围扩大到外部队列，进行前瞻性临床验证，并测试保留来源的证据表是否可以改善产前和新生儿遗传学的多学科审查。Several limitations remain. The cohort is single-institution or collaboration-derived and requires external validation across sequencing pipelines, hospitals and ancestry groups. The current benchmark focuses on variant prioritization in solved or curated cases; diagnostic-report quality, clinical actionability and prospective workflow impact require additional expert review. The LLM and agentic baselines were evaluated as rerankers over Exomiser-derived top-20 candidates, which is clinically tractable but distinct from full VCF-scale discovery or broad disease-level differential diagnosis. Structure-informed refinement is useful for certain missense or high-priority uncertain variants but cannot be applied uniformly across all variant classes. The agentic diagnostic layer is intentionally lightweight in the present version and should be evaluated separately from the ranking substrate as it matures. Future work will extend evaluation to external cohorts, perform prospective clinical validation and test whether provenance-preserving evidence tables improve multidisciplinary review in prenatal and neonatal genetics.

更广泛地说，DeepBD 提出了将人工智能应用于基因组医学的实用途径。该系统没有将诊断视为无约束的语言生成问题，而是将诊断组织为证据到诊断的工作流程。证据引擎产生定量的、可测试的变体排序；图和结构模块提供生物背景；规则证据将预测锚定于临床资源；专业工具提供额外的校准或改进；代理层将这些输出转换为可以审查和更新的格式。这种分层设计尤其适用于遗传性出生缺陷，其中诊断途径对时间敏感，表型信息不完整，并且证据经常随着病例的发展而变化。More broadly, DeepBD suggests a practical path for applying AI to genomic medicine. Rather than treating diagnosis as an unconstrained language-generation problem, the system organizes diagnosis as an evidence-to-diagnosis workflow. The evidence engine produces a quantitative, testable ordering of variants; graph and structure modules provide biological context; rule evidence anchors predictions to clinical resources; specialist tools provide additional calibration or refinement; and the agentic layer turns these outputs into a format that can be reviewed and updated. This layered design is especially relevant for genetic birth defects, where the diagnostic pathway is time-sensitive, phenotype information is incomplete and evidence often changes as the case evolves.
