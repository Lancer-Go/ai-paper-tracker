摘要ABSTRACT

背景。Background.环境数字抄写员的广泛部署正在推动临床医生与患者对话的大规模捕获。依赖人类编码员注释临床通信数据的传统方法很难满足效率、一致性和可扩展性的要求，这使得人工智能驱动的通信编码系统变得越来越重要。然而，评估此类系统取决于现实世界的临床对话和人类编码标签，这两者都很难大规模获得。The widespread deployment of ambient digital scribes is driving the large-scale capture of clinician–patient dialogues. Traditional approaches that rely on human coders to annotate clinical communication data struggle to meet the demands of efficiency, consistency, and scalability, making AI-driven communication coding systems increasingly important. However, evaluating such systems depends on real-world clinical dialogues and human-coded labels, both of which are difficult to obtain at scale.

方法。Methods.我们开发了SIMAX（We developed SIMAX (S可校准和calable andI可解释的框架nterpretable Framework forM多保真度和ulti-Fidelity andA带注释的临床医生与患者对话nnotated Clinician–Patient DialogueS模拟），一个用于生成带有参考行为注释的大规模受控临床对话数据的框架。 SIMAX 根据预定义的临床场景、角色和语音条件以及目标沟通行为生成临床医生与患者的对话。通信行为使用两个互补的码本进行控制：用于总体通信质量的全局码本和用于特定且可计数的通信行为的 WISER 码本。我们评估了 SIMAX 的内在数据质量和下游实用性，包括自动和人工质量评估以及通信编码系统的示例评估。imulation), a framework for generating large-scale, controlled clinical dialogue data with reference behavioral annotations. SIMAX generates clinician–patient dialogues based on predefined clinical scenarios, personas and voice conditions, and target communication behaviors. Communication behaviors are controlled using two complementary codebooks: the Global Codebook for overall communication quality and the WISER Codebook for specific and countable communication behaviors. We evaluated SIMAX across intrinsic data quality and downstream utility, including automated and human quality assessments and an example evaluation of a communication coding system.

结果。Results.SIMAX 生成了 3,388 个模拟对话，涵盖三个临床专业、多个就诊阶段、角色特征和口音条件。自动评估显示平均 UTMOS 和 WV-MOS 分数分别为 3.03 和 2.61，表明语音自然度合理； WER和CER分别为0.07和0.05，表明转录保真度较高； CLAP余弦相似度为0.41，表明文本-音频语义正相关。人类评估显示，中位 MOS 为 4.67，表明良好的清晰度、自然度和说话者区分度，中位临床真实感得分为 3.00。下游评估表明，SIMAX生成的数据可以初步评估通信编码系统如何响应不同的行为目标，并识别某些维度的敏感性不足。SIMAX generated 3,388 simulated dialogues covering three clinical specialties, multiple visit stages, persona characteristics, and accent conditions. Automated assessment showed mean UTMOS and WV-MOS scores of 3.03 and 2.61, suggesting reasonable speech naturalness; WER and CER were 0.07 and 0.05, indicating high transcription fidelity; and CLAP cosine similarity was 0.41, indicating positive text–audio semantic correspondence. Human evaluation showed a median MOS of 4.67, suggesting good clarity, naturalness, and speaker differentiation, and a median clinical realism score of 3.00. Downstream evaluation suggests that SIMAX generated data can preliminarily assess how the communication coding system responds to different behavioral targets and identifies insufficient sensitivity in certain dimensions.

结论。Conclusions.SIMAX 生成受控且可重复的模拟临床医患对话，为通信编码系统的开发、验证和完善提供数据基础。SIMAX generates controlled and reproducible simulated clinicianpatient dialogues, providing a data foundation for the development, validation, and refinement of communication coding systems.

简介Introduction

环境人工智能 (AI) 系统，特别是自动数字抄写员 (ADS)，正越来越多地被引入常规临床实践中Ambient artificial intelligence (AI) systems, particularly automated digital scribes (ADS), are increasingly being introduced into routine clinical practice1,2。这些系统的主要目的是减少文档负担，提高工作流程效率，并让临床医生更直接地关注患者. These systems are primarily designed to reduce documentation burden, improve workflow efficiency, and allow clinicians to focus more directly on patients3。同时，ADS 的广泛部署意味着可以捕获和转录大量临床医生与患者的对话，从而生成前所未有规模的临床通信数据. At the same time, the widespread deployment of ADS means that large volumes of clinician–patient dialogues can be captured and transcribed, generating clinical communication data at an unprecedented scale4,5。随着这些数据的不断增长，依赖人类编码员逐条记录地注释通信行为的传统方法将难以满足效率、一致性和可扩展性的需求. As these data continue to grow, traditional approaches that rely on human coders to annotate communication behaviors record by record will struggle to meet the demands of efficiency, consistency, and scalability6。因此，能够自动分析临床通信数据的人工智能驱动的通信编码系统变得越来越重要. Therefore, AI-driven communication coding systems capable of automatically analyzing clinical communication data are becoming increasingly important7,8 .

然而，对人工智能驱动的通信编码系统的严格评估仍然具有挑战性。通常通过将其输出与人类编码标签进行比较来评估这些系统，以确定它们是否符合既定的编码标准。一项关键挑战是真实临床医患对话的收集、共享和重用受到隐私和监管要求的限制However, rigorous evaluation of AI-driven communication coding systems remains challenging. These systems are typically assessed by comparing their outputs against human-coded labels to determine whether they align with established coding standards. One key challenge is that the collection, sharing, and reuse of real clinician–patient dialogues are constrained by privacy and regulatory requirements9，使得现有数据不足以涵盖不同的临床环境、患者群体和边缘案例通信场景。另一个关键挑战是很难获得高质量的人类标签。人工注释依赖于训练有素的编码人员，使得该过程耗时且难以扩展, making existing data insufficient to cover diverse clinical settings, patient populations, and edge-case communication scenarios. Another key challenge is that high-quality human labels are difficult to obtain. Human annotation depends on trained coders, making the process timeconsuming and difficult to scale10。这些障碍共同造成了一个严重的差距：具有可靠行为标签的大规模、符合隐私要求的临床医患对话数据集仍然稀缺，限制了通信编码系统的系统评估。. Together, these barriers create a critical gap: large-scale, privacy-compliant clinician–patient dialogue datasets with reliable behavioral labels remain scarce, limiting the systematic evaluation of communication coding systems.

综合临床医生与患者对话为构建评估数据提供了可行的补充方法Synthetic clinician–patient dialogues offer a feasible complementary approach for constructing evaluation data11,12。然而，现有的研究很大程度上优先考虑内容真实性，重点关注生成的对话是否与基础临床记录一致，而较少强调交互行为的可控性. However, existing studies have largely prioritized content realism, focusing on whether generated dialogues are consistent with underlying clinical records, while placing less emphasis on the controllability of interactional behaviors13。对于评估通信编码系统，仅仅内容真实的对话是不够的；理想的评估数据应包括明确的行为目标以及可靠的参考标签. For evaluating communication coding systems, dialogues that are merely realistic in content are not enough; ideal evaluation data should include explicit behavioral targets as well as reliable reference labels8。因此，现有的综合对话资源在支持通信编码系统的受控和可重复评估的能力方面仍然有限。. Therefore, existing synthetic dialogue resources remain limited in their ability to support controlled and reproducible evaluation of communication coding systems.

实验配置和通信目标规范Experimental Configurations and Communication Target Specification

对于每个模拟对话，SIMAX 中的实验配置定义了三个可控组件：临床场景、角色和语音条件以及目标通信行为。临床场景指定对话发生的就诊背景，包括临床专业、就诊阶段和背景背景（例如，计划生育讨论、游乐场滑梯损伤或骨关节炎疼痛管理）。角色和声音条件指定临床医生和患者的个人和声音特征，包括年龄、姓名、性别、声音来源和口音条件。目标沟通行为指定了对话中要表达的交互行为的预期级别。For each simulated dialogue, the experimental configuration in SIMAX defines three controllable components: the clinical scenario, persona and voice conditions, and target communication behaviors. The clinical scenario specifies the visit context in which the dialogue takes place, including the clinical specialty, visit stage, and contextual background (e.g., birth planning discussion, playground slide injury, or osteoarthritis pain management). Persona and voice conditions specify the individual and vocal characteristics of the clinician and patient, including age, name, gender, voice source, and accent condition. Target communication behaviors specify the intended level of interactional behaviors to be expressed in the dialogue.

为了使这些目标通信行为可解释和可再现，SIMAX 使用两个互补的结构化通信码本将它们转化为具体的行为标准。我们选择 Global Codebook 和 WISER Codebook 是因为它们捕获了临床通信编码的两个重要级别：前者侧重于整体通信质量和关系交互，而后者侧重于更具体和可计算的临床医生通信行为。具体来说，全球密码本捕捉了注意力（例如，始终如一地回应患者的叙述）、关心（例如，解决患者的担忧）、流程（例如，自然地引导咨询）、温暖（例如，表达支持和友善）和尊重（例如，以不评判的方式回应患者），每个维度都定义为 1-5 级的序数目标To make these target communication behaviors interpretable and reproducible, SIMAX uses two complementary structured communication codebooks to translate them into concrete behavioral standards. We selected the Global Codebook and the WISER Codebook because they capture two important levels of clinical communication coding: the former focuses on overall communication quality and relational interaction, whereas the latter focuses on more specific and countable clinician communication behaviors. Specifically, the Global Codebook captures attentiveness (e.g., consistently responding to the patient’s narrative), concern (e.g., addressing patient worries), flow (e.g., naturally guiding the consultation), warmth (e.g., expressing support and friendliness), and respect (e.g., responding to the patient in a nonjudgmental manner), with each dimension defined as an ordinal target on a 1–5 scale14,15。 WISER 密码本侧重于共情反应（例如，明确承认患者情绪）、开放式问题（例如，邀请患者详细阐述）和反思性陈述（例如，总结患者表达的担忧），这些被定义为目标计数范围. The WISER Codebook focuses on empathic responses (e.g., explicitly acknowledging patient emotions), open-ended questions (e.g., inviting the patient to elaborate), and reflective statements (e.g., summarizing the patient’s expressed concerns), which are defined as target count ranges10。通过这种设计，每个模拟对话都有明确定义的临床背景、角色和声音设置以及预定义的沟通行为目标。. Through this design, each simulated dialogue has a clearly defined clinical context, persona and voice setting, and predefined communication behavior targets.

Intrinsic data quality assessment examined whether the generated data had high-quality audio and reasonable clinical realism. Downstream utility assessment examined whether the simulated dialogues could be used to test how a communication coding system responds to controlled variation in communication behaviors.

讨论Discussion

在这项研究中，我们开发了 SIMAX 来为通信编码系统提供受控且可重复的临床对话数据。通过产生大In this study, we developed SIMAX to provide controlled and reproducible clinical dialogue data for communication coding systems. By generating large-

SIMAX 通过预定义的沟通行为目标和参考行为注释来模拟临床医生与患者的对话，使编码系统能够在已知的行为条件下进行系统评估，而不是仅仅依赖于有限的现实世界样本。该数据基础支持更清晰地评估编码系统如何响应通信行为类型和强度的变化，从而促进通信编码系统的开发、验证和完善。除了这项研究之外，SIMAX 还旨在作为一种可重复使用的资源：其他用户可以将其应用于自己的临床场景和行为目标，以对通信编码系统进行基准测试，而无需受保护的现实世界对话。scale simulated clinician–patient dialogues with predefined communication behavior targets and reference behavioral annotations, SIMAX enables coding systems to be systematically evaluated under known behavioral conditions, rather than relying solely on limited real-world samples. This data foundation supports clearer assessment of how coding systems respond to variations in communication behavior type and intensity, thereby facilitating the development, validation, and refinement of communication coding systems. Beyond this study, SIMAX is intended as a reusable resource: other users can apply it to their own clinical scenarios and behavioral targets to benchmark communication coding systems without requiring protected real-world dialogues.

我们从两个维度评估了 SIMAX：内在数据质量和通信编码系统的下游效用。对于内在数据质量，SIMAX 生成的记录在音频质量和临床真实性方面显示出可接受的可用性。自动音频评估指标支持语音自然度、转录保真度以及文本和音频之间的语义一致性，而人工评估则进一步评估清晰度、自然度和说话者差异化。临床真实性评级表明，模拟对话反映了评估场景中合理的门诊互动。We evaluated SIMAX across two dimensions: intrinsic data quality and downstream utility for communication coding systems. For intrinsic data quality, SIMAX generated records showed acceptable usability in terms of audio quality and clinical realism. Automated audio assessment metrics supported speech naturalness, transcription fidelity, and semantic consistency between text and audio, while human evaluation further assessed clarity, naturalness, and speaker differentiation. Clinical realism ratings suggested that the simulated dialogues reflected plausible outpatient interactions in the evaluated scenarios.

为了评估 SIMAX 的下游效用，我们使用 SIMAX 生成的对话的音频转录文本作为测试输入来评估 MOSAIC，并将 MOSAIC 输出与预定义的行为目标进行比较。结果表明，不同类型的行为目标的 MOSAIC 反应并不完全一致。在Global Codebook批次中，MOSAIC分数随着预定义目标分数的增加并没有表现出明显的单调增加趋势；相比之下，在 WISER Codebook 批次中，MOSAIC 检测到的行为计数通常随着预定义计数范围的增加而增加。这些发现表明，SIMAX生成的数据可用于初步检查通信编码系统在不同码本类型和通信行为维度上的响应模式，同时还可以识别某些维度上灵敏度不足的情况。To assess the downstream utility of SIMAX, we used the audio-derived transcripts of SIMAX generated dialogues as test inputs to evaluate MOSAIC and compared the MOSAIC outputs against the predefined behavioral targets. The results showed that MOSAIC responses were not fully consistent across different types of behavioral targets. In the Global Codebook batch, MOSAIC scores did not show a clear monotonic increasing trend as the predefined target scores increased; by contrast, in the WISER Codebook batch, the behavior counts detected by MOSAIC generally increased as the predefined count ranges increased. These findings suggest that SIMAX generated data can be used to preliminarily examine the response patterns of communication coding systems across different codebook types and communication behavior dimensions, while also identifying insufficient sensitivity in certain dimensions.

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，SIMAX 生成综合临床对话，因此应被视为受控评估资源，而不是使用真实临床对话进行外部验证的直接替代品This study has several limitations. First, SIMAX generates synthetic clinical dialogues and should therefore be viewed as a controlled evaluation resource rather than a direct substitute for external validation using real clinical dialogues22。尽管模拟对话是根据临床场景构建的，但它们可能无法完全捕捉真实临床的复杂性. Although the simulated dialogues are constructed based on clinical scenarios, they may not fully capture the complexity of real clinical

互动interactions23。此外，SIMAX 生成对话的平均持续时间约为 5 分钟，这比真实门诊环境中完整的临床交流（通常为 15-20 分钟左右）要短。其次，当前框架中的行为控制主要通过通信密码本实现，这使得通信行为的操纵具有可重复性，但针对不同类型的行为目标，控制的有效性存在差异。特别是，在全局密码本批次中，MOSAIC 输出在预定义的目标水平上没有表现出明显的单调增加趋势。第三，虽然SIMAX支持环境音频，但当前版本并未完全模拟语音重叠、临床医生或患者中断以及现实世界对话中常见的其他现象。第四，本研究中的下游评估使用了音频转录本；因此，生成音频的质量可能会影响自动转录和后续通信编码的稳定性，但目前的研究尚未系统地评估这种关系。最后，本研究没有直接将 SIMAX 生成的对话与人类编码的真实临床对话进行比较。. In addition, the average duration of SIMAX generated dialogues is approximately 5 minutes, which is shorter than the complete clinical exchanges in real outpatient settings, which are typically around 15-20 minutes. Second, behavioral control in the current framework is primarily implemented through communication codebooks, which makes the manipulation of communication behaviors reproducible, but the effectiveness of control varies across different types of behavioral targets. In particular, in the Global Codebook batch, MOSAIC outputs did not show a clear monotonic increasing trend across the predefined target levels. Third, although SIMAX supports environmental audio, the current version does not fully model speech overlap, clinician or patient interruptions, and other phenomena commonly observed in real-world dialogues. Fourth, the downstream evaluation in this study used audio-derived transcripts; therefore, the quality of generated audio may affect automatic transcription and the stability of subsequent communication coding, but this relationship was not systematically evaluated in the current study. Finally, this study did not directly compare SIMAX generated dialogues with human-coded real-world clinical dialogues.

未来的工作应该提高 SIMAX 的临床真实性和普遍性。一个重要的方向是在伦理和法律框架允许的情况下，将模拟对话与去识别化的真实临床记录结合起来，在同一通信编码系统下量化它们之间的差异，并利用这些差异来校准对话流程和行为控制策略。 SIMAX 还应该扩展到更多的临床专业和护理机构，尤其是越来越多地采用 ADS 的初级护理和远程医疗机构Future work should improve the clinical realism and generalizability of SIMAX. One important direction is to align simulated dialogues with deidentified real clinical recordings, where permitted by ethical and legal frameworks, to quantify differences between them under the same communication coding system and use these differences to calibrate dialogue flow and behavior control strategies. SIMAX should also be extended to more clinical specialties and care settings, especially primary care and telehealth settings where ADS are increasingly being adopted5。此外，多语言扩展对于支持在更广泛的患者群体和临床环境中评估通信编码系统非常重要. In addition, multilingual expansion will be important to support the evaluation of communication coding systems across broader patient populations and clinical environments24,25,26 .
