重新思考本地计算机使用代理中的推理时间缩放：故障模式和计算权衡Rethinking Inference-Time Scaling in Local Computer-Use Agents: Failure Modes and Compute Tradeoffs

Woongkyu Lee Jungwook Choi Hanyang University {lwghanyang, choij}@hanyang.ac.kr Corresponding author.
摘要Abstract

在本地部署自主计算机使用代理 (CUA) 对于隐私、成本效率和实际可用性越来越重要，但在严格的硬件限制下提高其性能仍然具有挑战性。虽然最近的研究表明，推理时间扩展可以通过执行期间的额外计算来改进前沿计算机使用代理，但其对于资源受限的本地模型的有效性仍然知之甚少。我们对本地 CUA 中跨上下文、时间、结构和并行维度的推理时间缩放进行了系统的实证研究。我们在 OSWorld 基准测试中评估了 Qwen3-VL-8B/30B-A3B、UI-TARS-1.5-7B 和 OpenCUA-7B。我们的结果表明，在改变故障模式时，额外的计算通常会产生收益递减。上下文缩放提供了提高轨迹稳定性和任务准确性的历史基础，但随着代币成本的增加以及失败从重复或停滞的轨迹转向过早的错误成功，其收益会饱和。时间缩放类似地减少了最大步停顿，但并没有显着提高任务成功率，这表明更长的视野通常会扩展错误的轨迹，而不是纠正它们。我们进一步发现，结构分解可能会在本地两阶段代理中引入规划和格式化开销，而并行扩展则以大量计算成本部分缓解了这些故障。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，高效的本地 CUA 需要选择性的计算分配、故障感知控制机制以及围绕本地模型的功能和限制而设计的代理框架。Deploying autonomous computer-use agents (CUAs) locally is increasingly important for privacy, cost efficiency, and practical usability, yet improving their performance under strict hardware constraints remains challenging. While recent studies show that inference-time scaling can improve frontier computer-use agents through additional computation during execution, its effectiveness for resource-constrained local models remains poorly understood. We present a systematic empirical study of inference-time scaling in local CUAs across contextual, temporal, structural, and parallel dimensions. We evaluate Qwen3-VL-8B/30B-A3B, UI-TARS-1.5-7B, and OpenCUA-7B on the OSWorld benchmark. Our results show that additional computation often yields diminishing returns while changing failure modes. Contextual scaling provides historical grounding that improves trajectory stability and task accuracy, but its gains saturate as token cost increases and failures shift from repetitive or stalled trajectories toward premature false successes. Temporal scaling similarly reduces max-step stalls, yet does not substantially improve task success, indicating that longer horizons often extend erroneous trajectories rather than correct them. We further find that structural decomposition can introduce planning and formatting overhead in local two-stage agents, while parallel scaling partially mitigates these failures at a substantial computational cost. Overall, our findings suggest that efficient local CUAs require selective compute allocation, failure-aware control mechanisms, and agentic frameworks designed around the capabilities and limitations of local models.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的出现从根本上改变了数字交互的范式，从简单的文本生成转向能够导航复杂图形用户界面 (GUI) 的自主计算机使用代理 (CUA)。 与传统的特定任务机器人不同，这些代理利用多模式推理来感知屏幕状态和交互历史记录，执行 GUI 操作（例如单击、键入和滚动）来完成高级用户指令。 现实评估基准的制定推动了这一进展The emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) has fundamentally shifted the paradigm of digital interaction, moving beyond simple text generation toward autonomous Computer-use Agents (CUAs) capable of navigating complex Graphical User Interfaces (GUIs). Unlike traditional task-specific bots, these agents leverage multimodal reasoning to perceive screen states and interaction history, execute GUI actions (e.g., click, type, and scroll) to fulfill high-level user instructions. This progress has been fueled by both the development of realistic evaluation benchmarks(Xie et al., 2024; Deng et al., 2023; Bonatti et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2024)以及专门的多式联运模型的出现and the emergence of specialized multimodal models(Qin et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025; Xue et al., 2026; Lin et al., 2024)专为 GUI 交互和动作基础而设计。总之，这些进步使得在构建能够在复杂的现实数字环境中运行的代理方面取得了快速进展。designed for GUI interaction and action grounding. Together, these advances have enabled rapid progress in building agents capable of operating in complex, real-world digital environments.

最近的进步引入了高性能的代理架构。以特工S系列为例Recent advancements have introduced highly capable agentic architectures. For instance, the Agent S series(Agashe et al., 2024; 2025; Gonzalez-Pumariega et al., 2026)证明了分层规划和子目标管理的有效性，而 JEDIdemonstrated the effectiveness of hierarchical planning and sub-goal management, while JEDI(Xie et al., 2025)和GTA-1and GTA-1(Yang et al., 2025)采用解耦的规划-基础框架，其中高级计划由推理模型生成并由专门的基础模型执行。为了进一步提高性能，最近的工作探索了推理时间缩放，它在执行期间分配额外的计算以提高代理的准确性adopt decoupled planning–grounding frameworks, where high-level plans are generated by a reasoning model and executed by a specialized grounding model. To further improve performance, recent work explores inference-time scaling, which allocates additional computation during execution to enhance agent accuracy(Gonzalez-Pumariega et al., 2026; Yang et al., 2025)。在计算机使用的代理中，这是通过轨迹级缩放和并行计划生成等技术实现的，从而在任务成功方面取得显着进展。. In computer-use agents, this is realized through techniques such as trajectory-level scaling and parallel plan generation, leading to notable gains in task success.

然而，模型能力和运营效率之间仍然存在根本性的紧张关系。虽然最先进的代理使用专有模型实现了强大的性能However, a fundamental tension remains between model capability and operational efficiency. While state-of-the-art agents achieve strong performance using proprietary models(Singh et al., 2025; Anthropic, 2025)，与人类效率相比，它们在步数计数和令牌消耗方面产生了巨大的成本, they incur substantial costs in step counts and token consumption compared to human efficiency(Abhyankar et al., 2025)。因此，基于小型开源模型的本地 CUA 对于实际和安全的部署非常重要。然而，尽管推理时间缩放. Local CUAs based on small open-source models are therefore important for practical and secure deployment. Yet, although inference-time scaling(Yang et al., 2025; Gonzalez-Pumariega et al., 2026)虽然改进了大型专有代理，但目前尚不清楚增加的计算对于资源受限的本地模型是否值得，或者哪些故障模式限制了进一步的收益。这激发了对局部环境中推理时间缩放的边际效用和失败行为的系统研究。improves large proprietary agents, it remains unclear whether the added computation is worthwhile for resource-constrained local models, or which failure patterns limit further gains. This motivates a systematic study of the marginal utility and failure behavior of inference-time scaling in local settings.

Our empirical analysis reveals that the scaling of local CUAs is governed by non-linear dynamics, where incremental resource allocation often leads to diminishing returns. Specifically, while minimal history is a prerequisite for stability, excessive contextual scaling and structural decomposition trigger saturation effects and reasoning bottlenecks that escalate costs without accuracy gains. These observations suggest that inference-time scaling in local CUAs changes failure modes more than it increases task success, requiring compute to be allocated selectively around the limitations of local models.

自主计算机使用代理Autonomous Computer-Use Agents综合基准的引入加速了能够导航图形用户界面（GUI）的自主代理的开发The development of autonomous agents capable of navigating Graphical User Interfaces (GUIs) has been accelerated by the introduction of comprehensive benchmarks(Xie et al., 2024; Bonatti et al., 2024; Deng et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024)。现有的方法可以大致分为两个方向。 第一个方向利用单模型架构. Existing approaches can be broadly categorized into two directions. The first direction leverages single-model architectures(Anthropic, 2025; Bai et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025; Qin et al., 2025)，其中多模态模型以端到端的方式直接将视觉观察和任务指令映射到可执行操作。第二个方向采用代理框架，将决策过程分解为多个阶段，例如规划和落地。 Agent S 等框架, where a multimodal model directly maps visual observations and task instructions to executable actions in an end-to-end manner. The second direction adopts agentic frameworks that decompose the decision-making process into multiple stages, such as planning and grounding. Frameworks such as Agent S(Agashe et al., 2024; 2025; Gonzalez-Pumariega et al., 2026)和GTA-1and GTA-1(Yang et al., 2025)已经证明，结构化分解可以通过分层规划和协调来提高任务成功率。然而，尽管它们很有效，但这些智能体严重依赖基于 API 的专有模型进行规划，从而激发了在严格的硬件和推理时间限制下基于开源模型的本地 CUA 的研究。have demonstrated that structured decomposition can improve task success through hierarchical planning and coordination. However, despite their effectiveness, these agents rely heavily on proprietary API-based models for planning, motivating the study of local CUAs based on open-source models under strict hardware and inference-time constraints.

推理时间缩放定律Inference-time Scaling Laws推理时间扩展，定义为通过在执行期间分配额外的计算资源来提高性能，随着大规模推理模型的成功而受到重视Inference-time scaling, defined as improving performance by allocating additional computational resources during execution, has gained prominence following the success of large-scale reasoning models(Snell et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023)。最近的工作已经开始在计算机使用代理的背景下探索这种范例。例如，Agent S3 通过多次部署和行为选择引入了轨迹级缩放. Recent work has begun to explore this paradigm in the context of computer-use agents. For instance, Agent S3 introduces trajectory-level scaling via multiple rollouts and behavior selection(Gonzalez-Pumariega et al., 2026)，而 GTA-1 通过多个候选计划采用并行测试时间扩展, while GTA-1 employs parallel test-time scaling through multiple candidate plans(Yang et al., 2025)。尽管取得了这些进展，多模式 GUI 环境中推理时间参数与任务成功之间的经验关系仍未得到充分探索，我们通过系统分析缩放维度之间的相互作用来解决这一问题。. Despite these advances, the empirical relationship between inference-time parameters and task success in multimodal GUI environments remains under-explored, which we address by systematically analyzing the interplay between the scaling dimensions.

Recent work emphasizes efficiency in evaluation, with OSWorld-Human introducing step-based human baselines and revealing significant efficiency gaps(Abhyankar et al., 2025)。为了减轻长上下文成本，先前的方法探索压缩技术，包括键值缓存压缩和令牌修剪. 5 讨论Discussion

5.1 资源分配策略和实用指南Resource Allocation Strategy and Practical Guidelines

基于观察到的准确性-成本权衡和故障模式模式，我们建议本地 CUA 中的计算应该有选择地分配，而不是统一缩放。Based on the observed accuracy–cost tradeoffs and failure-mode patterns, we propose that compute in local CUAs should be allocated selectively rather than scaled uniformly.

情境尺度和历史基础Contextual Scaling and Historical Grounding上下文扩展提供了最强的初始优势。搬家自Contextual scaling offers the strongest initial benefit. Moving fromH=0H=0 to H=1H=1减少重复循环和最大步停顿，同时reduces repetitive loops and max-step stalls, whileH=4H=4进一步提高了步进效率。然而，更长的历史越来越多地将失败转向过早的错误成功，这表明适度的历史比无限制的背景扩展更可取。further improves step efficiency. However, longer histories increasingly shift failures toward premature false successes, suggesting that moderate history is preferable to unbounded context expansion.

时间缩放的局限性Limitations of Temporal Scaling时间缩放提供的收益有限。增加Temporal scaling provides limited gains. IncreasingSS减少最大步数停顿，但不会显着提高任务成功率；相反，失败常常会导致过早的错误成功。因此，reduces max-step stalls but does not substantially improve task success; instead, failures often shift toward premature false successes. Thus,SS应被视为与模型推理能力一致的有限预算。should be treated as a bounded budget aligned with the model’s reasoning capacity.

并行扩展的策略性使用Strategic Use of Parallel Scaling并行扩展可以通过增加计划多样性和减少格式失败来部分恢复两阶段性能。然而，它的收益相对于代币成本仍然是次线性的，因此应该有选择地使用它，而不是默认使用。Parallel scaling can partially recover two-stage performance by increasing plan diversity and reducing format-failures. However, its gains remain sub-linear relative to the token cost, so it should be used selectively rather than by default.

总的来说，我们建议优先考虑结构简单性，校准时间预算，并使用适度的上下文历史来平衡效率和轨迹稳定性。Overall, we recommend prioritizing structural simplicity, calibrating temporal budgets, and using moderate contextual history to balance efficiency and trajectory stability.

5.2 迈向高效的本地计算机使用代理Toward Efficient Local Computer-Use Agents

我们的研究结果表明，改进本地 CUA 需要有针对性的优化，而不是无约束的推理时间缩放。由于额外的计算通常会改变故障模式而不是解决它们，因此未来的工作应重点关注上下文质量、故障感知控制和模型感知代理设计。Our findings suggest that improving local CUAs requires targeted optimization rather than unconstrained inference-time scaling. Since additional compute often shifts failure modes instead of resolving them, future work should focus on context quality, failure-aware control, and model-aware agent design.

高效的上下文管理Efficient Context Management上下文缩放通过提供历史基础来提高准确性，但它也会增加代币成本，并且当历史变得太长时，它的好处就会饱和。这表明本地 CUA 需要机制来保留与任务相关的历史记录，同时过滤掉不太有用的过去观察结果。本地代理不应简单地扩展上下文窗口，而应支持选择性记忆保留和紧凑的历史表示，如 PAL-UI 中所探讨的那样Contextual scaling improves accuracy by providing historical grounding, but it also increases token cost and its benefit saturates when histories become too long. This suggests that local CUAs need mechanisms to retain task-relevant history while filtering out less useful past observations. Rather than simply expanding the context window, local agents should favor selective memory retention and compact history representations, as explored in PAL-UI(Liu et al., 2025).

故障感知代理控制Failure-Aware Agent Control我们的失败分析表明，本地代理经常遭受重复循环、进度停滞和过早错误成功的困扰。这些模式表明，仅采取额外步骤是不够的，除非智能体能够检测到它们何时不再取得有意义的进展或何时错误终止。事件驱动级联Our failure analysis shows that local agents often suffer from repetitive loops, stalled progress, and premature false successes. These patterns suggest that additional steps alone are insufficient unless agents can detect when they are no longer making meaningful progress or when they have terminated incorrectly. Event-driven cascades(Wei et al., 2026)当检测到进度停滞或语义漂移时，通过升级到更强大的模型来解决类似的风险。然而，完全本地的 CUA 需要大型模型恢复的轻量级替代方案，包括循环检测、轨迹修正和任务完成验证。address similar risks by escalating to stronger models when progress stalls or semantic drift is detected. However, fully local CUAs require lightweight alternatives to large-model recovery, including loop detection, trajectory revision, and task-completion verification.

本地 CUA 的代理框架Agentic Frameworks for Local CUAs我们的结果还表明，为更强的模型设计的代理框架可能不会直接转移到本地模型。当局部模型无法可靠地遵循指令时，复杂的分解可能会引入额外的故障。因此，本地多智能体系统应该将结构与模型能力相匹配，或者通过有针对性的训练或微调来使每个模型适应其指定的角色。Our results also indicate that agentic frameworks designed for stronger models may not transfer directly to local models. Complex decomposition can introduce additional failures when local models cannot reliably follow the instructions. Therefore, local multi-agent systems should match the structure to model capability, or adapt each model to its assigned role through targeted training or fine-tuning.

6 结论Conclusion

我们系统地研究了本地 CUA 中跨上下文、时间、结构和并行维度的推理时间缩放。我们的结果表明，额外的计算并不一定会转化为更高的任务成功率。上下文和时间缩放可以通过减少重复循环和最大步停顿来提高稳定性，但随着失败越来越多地转向过早的错误成功，它们的增益会饱和。结构分解可以进一步在本地两阶段代理中引入规划和格式解析开销，而并行扩展则以大量计算成本部分缓解这些问题。这些发现表明，本地 CUA 的未来进展将更多地依赖于设计根据本地模型的功能和限制分配计算的代理，而不是增加推理时间计算。We systematically studied inference-time scaling in local CUAs across contextual, temporal, structural, and parallel dimensions. Our results show that additional computation does not necessarily translate into higher task success. Contextual and temporal scaling can improve stability by reducing repetitive loops and max-step stalls, but their gains saturate as failures increasingly shift toward premature false successes. Structural decomposition can further introduce planning and format-parsing overhead in local two-stage agents, while parallel scaling partially mitigates these issues at a substantial computational cost. These findings suggest that future progress in local CUAs will depend less on increasing inference-time compute than on designing agents that allocate compute according to the capabilities and limitations of local models.
