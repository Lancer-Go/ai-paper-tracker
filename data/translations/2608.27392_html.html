3 简介Introduction

远程光电体积描记法 (rPPG) 通过摄像机记录的皮肤反射率的细微变化来估计心血管搏动，并为远程监控、家庭监控、人机交互和相关应用提供非接触式生理传感Remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) estimates cardiovascular pulsatility from subtle camera-recorded variations in skin reflectance and enables contactless physiological sensing for telemonitoring, home monitoring, human–computer interaction, and related applications[2]。相比之下，接触式光电体积描记法 (PPG) 通过与组织的直接光学相互作用来测量外周血容量的心脏同步变化. Contact photoplethysmography (PPG), by contrast, measures cardiac-synchronous changes in peripheral blood volume through direct optical interaction with tissue[1]。因此，相机衍生的 rPPG 并不是接触式 PPG 的直接替代品：组织光学、照明、相机响应、空间采样、压缩、面部区域选择和信号重建在估计终点之前改变生理观察结果. Camera-derived rPPG is therefore not a direct substitute for contact PPG: tissue optics, illumination, camera response, spatial sampling, compression, facial-region selection, and signal reconstruction transform the physiological observation before an endpoint is estimated[3, 2, 4].

因此，大多数 rPPG 验证强调终点性能，特别是心率 (HR) 平均绝对误差 (MAE)、均方根误差 (RMSE)、百分比误差和相关性Most rPPG validation consequently emphasizes endpoint performance, particularly heart-rate (HR) mean absolute error (MAE), root-mean-square error (RMSE), percentage error, and correlation[11, 14, 5]。这些指标是必要的，但不能建立信号等效性。传感转换可以保留一种生理或信号特性，同时降低另一种特性。此外，人口水平的相似性并不意味着记录身份的保留。对于物业经营者. These metrics are necessary but do not establish signal equivalence. A sensing transformation may preserve one physiological or signal property while degrading another. 7.1 跨观察路径的特定财产保护Property-specific preservation across an observation pathway

信号处理的核心发现是 PPG 到 rPPG 的转换不具有单一级别的可恢复性。在相同的基于 CHROM 的固定观测路径下，终点 HR、常规时间和光谱结构以及最大李雅普诺夫动力学表现出不同程度的记录特定对应关系。时间自相关保留了适度的配对优势，而频谱和复发率测量显示与混洗控制几乎没有分离，并且记录特定The central signal-processing finding is that PPG-to-rPPG conversion does not have a single level of recoverability. Under the same fixed CHROM-based observation pathway, endpoint HR, conventional temporal and spectral structure, and maximal-Lyapunov dynamics exhibited different degrees of recording-specific correspondence. Temporal autocorrelation retained a modest matched-pair advantage, whereas spectral and recurrence-rate measures showed little separation from shuffled controls, and recording-specificλmax\lambda_{\max}无法检测到对应关系。因此，可恢复性应分配给定义的观察路径下的定义的生理特性，而不是全局分配给 rPPG 模式。同样，相关对象是已定义属性的可恢复性correspondence was not detectable. Recoverability should therefore be assigned to a defined physiological property under a defined observation pathway rather than globally to the rPPG modality. Equivalently, the relevant object is the recoverability of a defined propertyϕj\phi_{j}观察变换下under an observation transformationℋθ\mathcal{H}_{\theta}，而不是派生模态作为一个整体的可恢复性。, rather than recoverability of the derived modality as a whole.

这些发现完善了我们早期在肤色稳健拓扑信号处理方面的工作These findings refine our earlier work on skin-tone-robust topological signal processing[18]。跨群体或观察组的拓扑描述符的鲁棒性本身并不能证明通过跨异构传感路径的记录来保留相应的属性。. Robustness of a topological descriptor across population or observation groups does not by itself establish that the corresponding property is preserved recording by recording across heterogeneous sensing pathways.

7.4 范围以及与先前表示框架的关系Scope and relation to prior representation frameworks

较弱的 CHROM 端点基准测试并未建立基于摄像头的生理传感的内在限制。 CHROM 被用作已建立的、无监督的观察途径，其在 MMPD 上的基准行为可以重现，而无需根据下游结果选择 rPPG 方法。因此，结论是特定于操作员的：它们描述了在基于 CHROM 的转换下哪些测试属性仍然可以访问，而不是每种可能的相机 rPPG 方法基本上无法恢复的属性。The weak CHROM endpoint benchmark does not establish an intrinsic limit of camera-based physiological sensing. CHROM was used as an established, unsupervised observation pathway whose benchmark behavior on MMPD could be reproduced without selecting an rPPG method according to downstream results. The conclusions are therefore operator-specific: they characterize which tested properties remain accessible under this CHROM-based transformation, not what is fundamentally unrecoverable by every possible camera-rPPG method.

目前的研究结果还提供了第 1 节中讨论的先前 CFD 和 CST 工作中引入的表征问题的观察级扩展。The present findings also provide an observation-level extension of the representation question introduced in the prior CFD and CST work discussed in Section3。 CFD 激发了更广泛的前提，即可用于推理的生理信息可以依赖于表示，而 CST 则强调接触 PPG 中的时间和动态组织。本研究提出了下一个观察级问题：当传感路径从接触式 PPG 变为相机衍生的 rPPG 时，哪些测试属性保留了记录特定的对应关系？这种关系是概念性的而非假设性的；当前的框架和实证结论独立于 CFD 或 CST. CFD motivates the broader premise that physiological information available for inference can depend on representation, while CST emphasizes temporal and dynamical organization in contact PPG. The present study asks the next observation-level question: when the sensing pathway changes from contact PPG to camera-derived rPPG, which tested properties retain recording-specific correspondence? This relationship is conceptual rather than assumptive; the current framework and empirical conclusions stand independently of CFD or CST[16, 17].

总而言之，结果表明端点差异、传统结构对应、最大李雅普诺夫对应、测量的 RGB SNR 和采集条件影响不会崩溃到单个可恢复性轴。因此，基于相机的生理验证应指定声称要恢复的属性，在适当的时候测试该属性的记录特定对应关系，并评估其在相关观察条件下的稳定性。Taken together, the results show that endpoint discrepancy, conventional structural correspondence, maximal-Lyapunov correspondence, measured RGB SNR, and acquisition-condition effects do not collapse to a single recoverability axis. Camera-derived physiological validation should therefore specify the property claimed to be recovered, test recording-specific correspondence for that property when appropriate, and evaluate its stability across relevant observation conditions.

8 局限性Limitations

这些发现的范围受到一些限制。首先，所有分析均使用 MMPD。尽管数据集包含跨越多个采集条件的数百个记录，但独立参与者的数量要少得多，特别是在一些菲茨帕特里克阶层中。主题感知混合效应模型、分组交叉验证和主题级引导重采样可解决重复测量问题，但不能替代独立的外部验证。因此，菲茨帕特里克运动发现应被视为值得复制的证据，而不是亚组特定运动效应的明确表征。Several limitations define the scope of these findings. First, all analyses used MMPD. Although the dataset contains hundreds of recordings spanning multiple acquisition conditions, the number of independent participants is much smaller, particularly within some Fitzpatrick strata. Subject-aware mixed-effects models, grouped cross-validation, and subject-level bootstrap resampling address repeated measurements but do not substitute for independent external validation. The Fitzpatrick-by-motion finding should therefore be treated as replication-worthy evidence rather than a definitive characterization of subgroup-specific motion effects.

9 结论Conclusion

这项研究支持一个特定于属性的框架，用于评估跨异质观察途径的生理测量。在评估的接触式 PPG 到相机 rPPG 转换下，群体水平的合理性并不能保证记录特定的保存，并且观察相关的退化在属性之间既不均匀，也不能通过简单的信号质量描述符完全捕获。这些发现表征了评估的观察路径，而不是相机传感的基本限制。This study supports a property-specific framework for evaluating physiological measurements across heterogeneous observation pathways. Under the evaluated contact-PPG-to-camera-rPPG transformation, population-level plausibility did not guarantee recording-specific preservation, and observation-associated degradation was neither uniform across properties nor completely captured by simple signal-quality descriptors. These findings characterize the evaluated observation pathway rather than a fundamental limit of camera sensing.

更广泛地说，跨模式生理验证应识别声称已恢复的特性，并在相关观察条件下建立其记录水平的保存和稳定性，而不是仅从终点性能推断信号等效性。More broadly, cross-modal physiological validation should identify the property claimed to be recovered and establish its recording-level preservation and stability under relevant observation conditions rather than infer signal equivalence from endpoint performance alone.
