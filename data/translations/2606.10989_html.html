摘要Abstract

大型语言模型取消学习的目的是抑制指定的不需要的知识，同时保留良性的能力。 许多遗忘目标侧重于抑制不需要的答案，而最近的目标引导变体指定了替换行为，但仍然使更新局部性在很大程度上不受约束。 本文介绍Large language model unlearning aims to suppress designated undesirable knowledge while preserving benign capabilities. Many unlearning objectives focus on suppressing undesired answers, while recent target-guided variants specify replacement behavior but still leave update locality largely unconstrained. This paper introduces零空间约束响应指定忘却Null-Space Constrained Response-Specified Unlearning(NSRU)，一个用于受控 LLM 忘却的投影约束低秩框架。 NSRU 使用显式结构化的安全目标响应来指定每个忘记查询的所需行为，同时抑制原始不需要的内容。 为了局部化适应，NSRU 根据良性隐藏表示估计每个模块保留子空间，并使用正交投影低秩参数化将 LoRA 更新限制在保留子空间的零空间。 由此产生的目标在这种约束参数化下联合优化安全目标学习、不良响应抑制和保留保留。 我们提供了本地一阶分析，表明预计的更新减少了保留侧扰动，同时保留了用于塑造忘记查询行为的可编辑方向。 TOFU 上的实验表明，NSRU 有效抑制了可提取的遗忘集知识，同时提高了保留 QA 性能、模型实用性和代表性基线上的安全目标对齐。 在 WMDP 上，NSRU 将危险域精度保持在随机选择区域附近，同时保留广泛且与域相邻的 MMLU 实用性。 消融研究支持安全目标监督、非期望响应抑制、保留损失和零空间预测更新的互补作用，而敏感性和鲁棒性分析表明在测试的超参数和提示变化中行为稳定。(NSRU), a projection-constrained low-rank framework for controlled LLM unlearning. NSRU uses an explicitly structured safe target response to specify the desired behavior for each forget query, while suppressing the original undesired content. To localize adaptation, NSRU estimates per-module retain subspaces from benign hidden representations and uses an orthogonal-projected low-rank parameterization to confine LoRA updates to the null space of the retain subspace. The resulting objective jointly optimizes safe-target learning, undesired-response suppression, and retention preservation under this constrained parameterization. We provide a local first-order analysis showing that the projected update reduces retain-side perturbations while preserving editable directions for shaping forget-query behavior. Experiments on TOFU show that NSRU effectively suppresses extractable forget-set knowledge while improving retain QA performance, model utility, and safe-target alignment over representative baselines. On WMDP, NSRU keeps hazardous-domain accuracy near the random-choice region while preserving broad and domain-adjacent MMLU utility. Ablation studies support the complementary roles of safe-target supervision, undesired-response suppression, retention loss, and null-space projected updates, while sensitivity and robustness analyses indicate stable behavior across the tested hyperparameter and prompt variations.

I 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地部署在必须保留广泛实用性的环境中，同时避免复制受限制或不需要的知识。 他们的训练数据可能包含敏感的个人详细信息、危险指令、受版权保护的内容或部署的模型不应泄露的其他信息Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed in settings where they must preserve broad utility while avoiding the reproduction of restricted or undesirable knowledge. Their training data may contain sensitive personal details, hazardous instructions, copyrighted content, or other information that a deployed model should not reveal[3, 48, 26]。 LLM 忘却通过减少指定遗忘数据的影响来解决这个问题，同时保留模型对良性和不相关输入的行为. LLM unlearning addresses this problem by reducing the influence of designated forget data while preserving the model’s behavior on benign and unrelated inputs[48, 26, 52, 23]。 最近的基准测试和审计进一步表明，可靠的 LLM 忘却的评估应该超越名义上的遗忘设置性能，因为残余知识仍然可以通过基准扰动、下游适应或内部表征来恢复. Recent benchmarks and audits further show that reliable LLM unlearning should be evaluated beyond nominal forget-set performance, because residual knowledge can remain recoverable through benchmark perturbations, downstream adaptation, or internal representations[36, 11, 38, 40, 16].

一大类 LLM 忘却方法专注于抑制不需要的答案。 给定忘记查询，这些方法通过梯度上升、基于偏好的目标或相关的微调方案降低原始响应的可能性A large class of LLM unlearning methods focuses on suppressing undesired answers. Given a forget query, these methods lower the likelihood of the original response through gradient ascent, preference-based objectives, or related fine-tuning schemes[48, 26, 52]。 最近的目标引导方法进一步指定了遗忘后的替代反应. Recent target-guided methods further specify replacement responses after unlearning[22, 28]。 这些工作解决了遗忘的响应方面，但受控遗忘也取决于更新局部性：因为遗忘和保留行为共享模型表示和参数，改变前者可能会无意中干扰后者。 这导致不受控制的行为的两个来源。. These works address the response side of unlearning, but controlled unlearning also depends on update locality: because forget and retain behaviors share model representations and parameters, changing the former can inadvertently perturb the latter. This leads to two sources of uncontrolled behavior.

第一个挑战是响应控制：The first challenge is response control:抑制不需要的答案并不能指定在忘记之后应该用什么安全和连贯的反应来取代它。 在对不需要的响应进行惩罚后，模型仍然可能会产生部分披露、不稳定的拒绝、损坏的响应或不相关的文本。 响应指定的遗忘在其成功标准上也不同于普通的安全对齐：模型不仅应该产生安全的响应，而且还应该降低原始不需要的内容的可恢复性。 因此，特定反应的忘却必须将安全目标学习与明确的抑制结合起来。suppressing an undesired answer does not specify what safe and coherent response should replace it after unlearning. After the undesired response is penalized, the model may still produce a partial disclosure, an unstable refusal, a corrupted response, or unrelated text. Response-specified unlearning also differs from ordinary safety alignment in its success criterion: the model should not only produce a safe response, but also reduce recoverability of the original undesired content. Thus, response-specified unlearning must couple safe-target learning with explicit suppression ofy−y^{-} [22, 28].

第二个挑战是更新局部性：The second challenge is update locality:忘记和保留行为与模型表示纠缠在一起，因此抑制忘记响应的更新也可能会扰乱良性功能所需的方向。 保留损失和正则化器通过平衡目标来减轻这种影响forget and retain behaviors are entangled in model representations, so an update that suppresses the forget response may also perturb directions needed for benign capabilities. Retention losses and regularizers mitigate this effect by balancing objectives[3, 48, 26]，但它们并不能确定模型允许改变的方向。 LoRA 减少了可训练参数的数量，但参数效率本身并不能确保学习到的低秩方向避免支持保留行为的表示方向, but they do not determine the directions in which the model is allowed to change. LoRA reduces the number of trainable parameters, yet parameter efficiency alone does not ensure that the learned low-rank directions avoid representation directions that support retained behavior[19, 2, 25, 44].

这两个挑战共同提出了一种针对特定反应的遗忘的有限适应观点。 在行为层面，模型需要指定的替换响应和针对恢复不需要的响应的明确惩罚。 在更新级别，应保护与保留行为密切相关的方向，而适应应通过剩余的可编辑方向进行操作。 受此观点的启发，我们建议Together, these two challenges suggest a constrained adaptation view of response-specified unlearning. At the behavioral level, the model needs a specified replacement response and an explicit penalty against recovering the undesired response. At the update level, directions strongly associated with retained behavior should be protected, while adaptation should act through the remaining editable directions. Motivated by this view, we propose零空间约束响应指定忘却Null-Space Constrained Response-Specified Unlearning(NSRU)，一个用于受控 LLM 忘却的低阶适应框架。(NSRU), a low-rank adaptation framework for controlled LLM unlearning.据我们所知，NSRU 是第一个 LLM 遗忘框架，它将指定响应的遗忘作为单一约束适应公式，从而统一安全目标重定向、显式不良响应抑制和保留子空间投影 LoRA 更新。To the best of our knowledge, NSRU is the first LLM unlearning framework to cast response-specified unlearning as a single constrained adaptation formulation, thereby unifying safe-target redirection, explicit undesired-response suppression, and retain-subspace projected LoRA updates.如图所示As illustrated in Fig.1，NSRU 将忘记查询重定向到指定的安全目标，同时将 LoRA 适应限制到与估计保留子空间正交的方向。, NSRU redirects forget queries toward specified safe targets while constraining LoRA adaptation to directions orthogonal to the estimated retain subspace.

NSRU 通过三个步骤实例化这种耦合。 首先，对于每个忘记查询，它使用原始的不需要的响应NSRU instantiates this coupling in three steps. First, for each forget query, it uses both the original undesired responsey−y^{-}和安全的目标反应and a safe target responsey+y^{+}。 其次，它根据选定的可训练模块的良性隐藏表示估计经验保留子空间，并将其正交补集视为可编辑空间。 第三，它训练预计的 LoRA 适配器，其共同目标是促进. 我们的本地一阶分析解释了为什么 NSRU 可以减少保留侧干扰而不关闭安全重定向方向。 对于表示接近估计保留子空间的保留输入，投影更新仅具有很小的一阶效应；对于忘记具有非零零空间能量的输入，相同的约束仍然保留局部有用的行为塑造方向。 TOFU 上的实验表明，NSRU 实现了有效的遗忘集抑制，同时提高了保留 QA 性能、模型实用性和相对于代表性基线的安全目标对齐。 在 WMDP 上，NSRU 将危险域精度保持在随机选择区域附近，同时保留更强大的广泛和域相邻的 MMLU 效用，从而产生更实用的危险抑制-效用权衡。 消融进一步表明，这种行为取决于安全目标监督、不良响应抑制、保留正则化和零空间投影的组合。Our local first-order analysis explains why NSRU can reduce retain-side interference without closing off safe-redirection directions. For retain inputs whose representations lie close to the estimated retain subspace, the projected update has only a small first-order effect; for forget inputs with nonzero null-space energy, the same constraint still preserves locally useful behavior-shaping directions. Experiments on TOFU show that NSRU achieves effective forget-set suppression while improving retain QA performance, model utility, and safe-target alignment over representative baselines. On WMDP, NSRU keeps hazardous-domain accuracy near the random-choice region while preserving stronger broad and domain-adjacent MMLU utility, yielding a more practical hazardous-suppression–utility trade-off. Ablations further show that this behavior depends on the combination of safe-target supervision, undesired-response suppression, retention regularization, and null-space projection.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于优化的 LLM 忘却Optimization-Based LLM Unlearning

近似忘却已成为从法学硕士中删除不需要的知识而无需从头开始重新培训的标准途径。 早期研究表明，有针对性的事后微调可以减少记忆内容，同时尝试保留一般能力Approximate unlearning has become a standard route for removing undesirable knowledge from LLMs without retraining from scratch. Early studies showed that targeted post hoc fine-tuning can reduce memorized content while attempting to preserve general capabilities[13, 47]，随后的工作研究了梯度上升、梯度差以及预训练的 LLM 失学习的相关目标, and subsequent work studied gradient ascent, gradient difference, and related objectives for pre-trained LLM unlearning[46]。 TOFU 引入了受控的虚构学习基准，并表明许多基于优化的基准无法匹配再训练行为. TOFU introduced a controlled fictitious-unlearning benchmark and showed that many optimization-based baselines fail to match retraining behavior[26]，而最近的基准研究强调多维度评估、度量稳健性以及过度解释仅基准成功的风险, while recent benchmark studies emphasized multi-dimensional evaluation, metric robustness, and the risk of over-interpreting benchmark-only success[36, 11, 38]。 负偏好优化 (NPO) 将遗忘重新表述为偏好型目标. Negative Preference Optimization (NPO) reformulated unlearning as a preference-style objective[52]，后续工作提高稳定性、有效性和响应质量, with follow-up work improving stability, effectiveness, and response quality[14, 45]。 这些方法为LLM忘学习奠定了优化基础。 它们的主要设计变量是用于遗忘和保留样本的损失，而遗忘后的替换行为和参数更新的几何形状通常通过辅助目标或正则化来间接处理。. These methods establish the optimization basis for LLM unlearning. Their main design variable is the loss applied to forget and retain samples, whereas the replacement behavior after forgetting and the geometry of the parameter update are usually handled indirectly through auxiliary objectives or regularization.

Ⅱ-BII-B 特定反应遗忘Response-Specified Unlearning

密切相关的工作不仅研究模型是否忘记，还研究模型忘记后的行为方式。 该方向与语言模型对齐的偏好优化相关，其中直接偏好优化（DPO）表明无需显式强化学习即可实现基于偏好的对齐A closely related line of work studies not only whether a model forgets, but also how it behaves after forgetting. This direction is connected to preference optimization for language model alignment, where Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) showed that preference-based alignment can be achieved without explicit reinforcement learning[33]，而 NPO 将这种观点改编为通过负面偏好监督来遗忘, and NPO adapted this view to unlearning through negative preference supervision[52]。 最近的工作进一步朝着明确的遗忘后行为控制方向发展：AltPO 认为，仅依赖负面反馈可能会导致不一致或低质量的反应. Recent work has moved further toward explicit post-unlearning behavior control: AltPO argued that relying only on negative feedback can lead to inconsistent or low-quality responses[28]，而 TRU 引入了结构化目标响应，以促进一致的拒绝并更好地控制范围内与范围外的查询, while TRU introduced structured target responses to promote coherent refusals and better control over in-scope versus out-of-scope queries[22]。 R-TOFU 进一步表明，在推理密集型模型中，答案级别的遗忘可能会错过中间痕迹中的残留信息，从而激发学习后规范，而不仅仅是简单地降低原始答案的可能性. R-TOFU further showed that, in reasoning-intensive models, answer-level forgetting can miss residual information in intermediate traces, motivating post-unlearning specifications that go beyond simply lowering the likelihood of the original answer[49]。 这一系列工作会在忘却之后激发明确的目标反应。 然而，响应规范主要是一个客观层面的约束：它规定了在忘记学习后应该首选什么输出，同时将模型参数如何以及在哪里可以改变到底层的适应机制。. This line of work motivates explicit target responses after unlearning. However, response specification is primarily an objective-level constraint: it states what output should be preferred after unlearning, while leaving how and where the model parameters may change to the underlying adaptation mechanism.

Ⅱ-CII-C 本地化更新和投影约束适应Localized Updates and Projection-Constrained Adaptation

另一项工作是通过对隐藏表示或局部参数的结构化干预来控制遗忘。 WMDP 引入了 RMU，一种表示引导方法，用于抑制危险知识，同时保留一般功能Another line of work controls forgetting through structured interventions in hidden representations or localized parameters. WMDP introduced RMU, a representation-steering method for suppressing hazardous knowledge while preserving general capabilities[21]。 后续工作表明，表示转向强烈依赖于层选择和转向强度. Follow-up work showed that representation steering depends strongly on layer selection and steering strength[8]，以及最近的激活和方向引导方法进一步提高了可控性并减少了附带遗忘, and recent activation- and direction-guided methods further improve controllability and reduce collateral forgetting[34, 43]。 局部模型修改提供了相关的参数空间视角。 LoRA通过低秩更新降低适应成本. Localized model modification provides a related parameter-space perspective. LoRA reduces adaptation cost through low-rank updates[19]，后来的分析表明，低秩适应往往比完全微调学习得更少，忘记得更少, and later analysis showed that low-rank adaptation tends to learn less and forget less than full fine-tuning[2]。 最近的参数有效的忘却方法探索了 LoRA 风格或基于适配器的更新机制，用于可扩展的知识删除. Recent parameter-efficient unlearning methods have explored LoRA-style or adapter-based update mechanisms for scalable knowledge removal[4, 10, 24, 1]。 模型编辑方法还通过知识神经元、本地化一级更新、学习编辑转换和多事实编辑来强调局部性. Model editing methods also emphasize locality through knowledge neurons, localized rank-one updates, learned edit transformations, and multi-fact editing[7, 29, 32, 30]。 这些方法共同表明，内部表示和局部参数更新对于减少附带变化是有用的，但局部性本身并不能指定抑制不良响应的耦合后遗忘行为. Together, these methods show that internal representations and localized parameter updates are useful handles for reducing collateral changes, but locality alone does not specify the coupled post-unlearning behavior of suppressing an undesired responsey−y^{-}并将查询重定向到安全目标and redirecting the query toward a safe targety+y^{+}.

VI 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了This paper introduced零空间约束响应指定忘却Null-Space Constrained Response-Specified Unlearning(NSRU)，一个用于受控 LLM 忘却的投影约束低秩框架。 NSRU 并没有将遗忘视为纯粹的答案抑制任务，而是通过安全目标响应明确定义遗忘后行为，同时积极惩罚原始的不需要的输出。 通过将可训练的 LoRA 更新投影到凭经验估计的保留子空间的零空间上，NSRU 成功实现了行为功能规范（“什么”）和参数几何限制（“哪里”）之间的彻底解耦。(NSRU), a projection-constrained low-rank framework for controlled LLM unlearning. Rather than treating unlearning as purely an answer-suppression task, NSRU explicitly defines the post-unlearning behavior via a safe target response while actively penalizing the original undesired output. By projecting trainable LoRA updates onto the null space of an empirically estimated retain subspace, NSRU successfully achieves a clean decoupling between behavioral functional specification (the ”what”) and parametric geometric restriction (the ”where”).
