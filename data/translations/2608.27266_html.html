天真的提示优化：Naive Prompt Optimization:
重新思考复杂即时搜索的必要性Rethinking the Need for Complex Prompt Search

Yuan Chang    Xiaoqi Chen Affiliation: Purdue University
摘要Abstract

有效改进跨不同任务的自主代理对于加速代理人工智能中的递归自我改进（RSI）至关重要，即时优化正在成为一种有前途的方法，能够提供与微调模型权重所实现的性能增益相当的性能增益，同时降低优化和服务中的计算成本。 然而，最近的发展越来越有利于不必要的复杂提示优化器。 我们引入了朴素提示优化（NPO），这是一种轻量级的单谱系方法，可以使用带有推出反馈的教师模型迭代地修改提示。 NPO 通过较少的部署实现了与 GEPA 相当或更好的性能，并且其优势随着更强的教师模型而增加，这表明更强的教师推理可以部分替代优化器端的搜索复杂性。在互动游戏中，NPO 与 GEPA 仍然具有广泛的竞争力，而 GRPO 在一些不太适合立即优化的任务上表现更好。 我们还表明，当逐字应用于其他学生模型时，NPO 优化的提示会带来类似的性能改进，尤其是同一系列中的模型。总的来说，我们的初步结果表明，简单的线性提示优化可以与更复杂的搜索程序相媲美。Efficiently improving autonomous agents across diverse tasks is central to accelerating recursive self-improvement (RSI) in agentic AI, with prompt optimization emerging as a promising approach capable of delivering performance gains comparable to those achieved by fine-tuning model weights, while reducing computational costs in both optimization and serving. However, recent developments increasingly favor unnecessarily complex prompt optimizers. We introduce Naive Prompt Optimization (NPO), a lightweight single-lineage method that iteratively revises prompts using a teacher model with rollout feedback. NPO achieves comparable or better performance than GEPA with fewer rollouts, and its advantage increases with stronger teacher models, suggesting that stronger teacher reasoning can partially substitute for optimizer-side search complexity. In interactive games, NPO remains broadly competitive with GEPA, while GRPO performs better on some tasks less amenable to prompt optimization. We also show that NPO-optimized prompts elicit similar performance improvements when applied verbatim to other student models, especially across models within the same family. Overall, our preliminary results show that simple, linear prompt optimization can rival substantially more sophisticated and complex search procedures.

3.4 优化提示会泄露评估答案吗？Do Optimized Prompts Leak Evaluation Answers?

NPO，特别是与 GPT-5.5 等更强​​大的教师配合使用时，通常会产生比 GEPA 更长的提示。由于教师使用培训推出的反馈来修改提示，因此优化的提示可以自然地包含特定于培训示例的信息。这种合并不一定有问题：就像少样本提示一样，提示优化旨在从训练示例中提取有用的信息并将其编码到结果提示中。 然而，如果保留验证集中的黄金答案泄漏到教师模型的训练数据中，编码到教师的参数知识中，并随后纳入优化的提示中，那么测量到的收益可能反映的是这种污染，而不是提高的任务解决能力。NPO, particularly when paired with stronger teachers such as GPT-5.5, often produces substantially longer prompts than GEPA. Because the teacher revises prompts using feedback from training rollouts, optimized prompts may naturally incorporate information specific to the training examples. Such incorporation is not necessarily problematic: like few-shot prompting, prompt optimization is intended to extract useful information from training examples and encode it in the resulting prompt. However, if gold answers from the held-out validation set had leaked into the teacher model’s training data, become encoded in the teacher’s parametric knowledge, and subsequently been incorporated into an optimized prompt, the measured gains could reflect such contamination rather than improved task-solving ability.

为了评估这种可能性，我们将优化期间生成的每个提示版本与训练集和验证集的黄金答案进行比较，从而使我们能够检查答案重叠是否在连续修订中累积。如图To assess this possibility, we compare every prompt version generated during optimization against gold answers from both the training and validation sets, allowing us to examine whether answer overlap accumulates over successive revisions. As shown in Figure7，NPO 和 GEPA 都会产生与训练集答案的重叠自然增长的提示，而与验证答案的重叠仍然可以忽略不计。手动检查进一步表明，少量非零验证重叠源自共享相同答案的不同训练和验证问题，而不是直接接触验证示例。这些结果表明，观察到的性能提升不太可能用评估答案泄漏来解释。, both NPO and GEPA yield prompts whose overlap with training set answers grows naturally, whereas overlap with validation answers remains negligible. Manual inspection further shows that the small amount of nonzero validation overlap arises from distinct training and validation questions that share the same answer, rather than from direct exposure to validation examples. These results suggest that the observed performance gains are unlikely to be explained by evaluation-answer leakage.

4 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

我们引入 NPO 作为一个简单的迭代基线，用于测试是否需要复杂的提示搜索。在 IFBench 和 HotpotQA 上，NPO 仍然与 GEPA 大致相当，并且从更强大的教师和丰富的轨迹级反馈中受益更多，这表明这些因素可以部分替代优化器端的搜索复杂性。优化的提示也可以在更大的跨家庭学生模型中有意义地转移，而无需重新优化，但模型、任务和优化方法组合之间存在一些变化。 在 22 款 TextArena 游戏中，NPO 和 GEPA 再次显示出大致可比的性能，而 GRPO 在一些即时优化效率较低的游戏中提供了互补的收益。总体而言，结果将简单的迭代提示优化建立为强大的基线，并表明增加的提示搜索或参数优化复杂性在产生明显的任务相关收益时是最有价值的。We introduce NPO as a simple iterative baseline for testing whether sophisticated prompt search is necessary. On IFBench and HotpotQA, NPO remains broadly comparable to GEPA and benefits more from stronger teachers and rich trajectory-level feedback, suggesting that these factors can partially substitute for optimizer-side search complexity. Optimized prompts also transfer meaningfully across larger and cross-family student models without re-optimization, with some variation across model, task, and optimization-method combinations. Across 22 TextArena games, NPO and GEPA again show broadly comparable performance, while GRPO provides complementary gains on several games where prompt optimization is less effective. Overall, the results establish simple iterative prompt optimization as a strong baseline and suggest that added prompt-search or parameter-optimization complexity is most valuable when it yields clear task-dependent benefits.

负责任使用声明。Responsible-Use Statement.

我们的结果表明，强大的教师模型可能能够通过很少的部署来提高学生在任意任务上的表现，从而降低针对恶意任务快速优化小型本地部署模型的成本；这种效率可能会被滥用来快速开发或增强恶意代理。一个关键的缓解措施是保留教师模型的安全护栏：教师不仅应该拒绝自己执行有害的任务，而且还应该避免生成有助于另一个模型执行这些任务的优化提示。 由于现代多层人工智能防护措施通常包括专用的内容和意图过滤组件，因此无论教师被要求直接执行有害任务还是为该任务优化另一个模型，我们预计它们都会表现出类似的拒绝；然而，这种行为应该在部署中明确验证和监控。 NPO 还要求与教师模型共享学生的部署跟踪，该模型可能托管在不受信任的第三方基础设施上，特别是当使用封闭模型作为教师时。 因此，涉及敏感数据的任务需要屏蔽和清理。Our results suggest a strong teacher model might be able to improve student performance for arbitrary tasks with very few rollouts, lowering the cost of rapidly optimizing small, locally deployed models for malicious tasks; this efficiency could be misused to quickly develop or strengthen malicious agents. A key mitigation is to preserve the teacher model’s safety guardrails: the teacher should not only refuse to perform harmful tasks itself, but also avoid generating optimized prompts that would help another model perform them. Because modern multi-layered AI safeguards commonly include dedicated content- and intent-filtering components, we expect them to exhibit similar refusal whether the teacher is asked to perform a harmful task directly or to optimize another model for that task; nevertheless, this behavior should be explicitly verified and monitored in deployment. NPO also requires sharing the student’s rollout traces with the teacher model, which may be hosted on untrusted third-party infrastructure, particularly when a closed model is used as the teacher. Thus, masking and sanitization are required for tasks involving sensitive data.

局限性。Limitations.

我们的初步研究仅使用了一组有限的任务，NPO 的优势是否扩展到更复杂、更长期的代理环境尚不确定。作为学生，我们也不会评估像 GPT-5.5 这样的前沿规模封闭模型，主要是因为时间性能不一致以及缺乏令牌级 logits 和通过 API 公开的足够的细粒度解码控制。 此外，我们观察到强化学习训练在某些任务上仍然不稳定，并且不同的任务可能有利于不同的强化学习算法或奖励设计。长视野环境进一步增加了 RL 的不稳定性，以及为 NPO 教师提供足够大的展示历史滑动窗口所需的环境；因此，我们的结果尚未确定即时优化和强化学习在长期设置中的相对有效性。Our preliminary study used only a limited set of tasks, leaving open whether NPO’s advantage extends to more complex, long-horizon agent environments. We also do not evaluate frontier-scale closed models like GPT-5.5 as students, primarily due to temporal performance inconsistencies and the lack of token-level logits and sufficient fine-grained decoding control exposed via APIs. In addition, we observe that RL training remains unstable on some tasks and that different tasks may favor different RL algorithms or reward designs. Long-horizon environments further increase both RL instability and the context required to provide NPO teachers with sufficiently large sliding windows over rollout histories; therefore, our results do not yet establish the relative effectiveness of prompt optimization and RL in longer-horizon settings.

未来的方向。Future Directions.

基于这些初步发现，我们确定了未来工作的两个潜在方向。首先，对于一般的提示调整技术，可能值得研究针对多个不同任务独立优化的提示是否可以合并为紧凑的多任务提示，同时保留其特定于任务的增益，然后在合并的多任务提示上进行几轮额外的 NPO。 其次，我们建议使用较小的教师和学生模型系统地搜索一组紧凑的代表性Building on these preliminary findings, we identify two potential directions for future work. First, for prompt tuning techniques in general, it may be worthwhile to investigate whether prompts optimized independently for multiple diverse tasks can be consolidated into a compact multi-task prompt while preserving their task-specific gains, followed by few additional rounds of NPO on the consolidated multi-task prompt. Second, we propose systematically searching, using smaller teacher and student models, for a compact set of representative本征-eigen-使得在这个小基础上优化的提示可以被提炼成通用提示，从而在更广泛的任务空间中产生实质性的改进，同时仍然可以转移到更大的学生模型。例如，在由棋盘游戏、纸牌游戏和知识检索任务组成的基础上优化提示可能会产生一个提示，该提示可以在不同的棋盘游戏、纸牌游戏和知识检索任务中产生普遍的改进。tasks such that prompts optimized on this small basis can be distilled into a general-purpose prompt that yields substantial improvements across the broader task space, while remaining transferable to larger student models. For example, optimizing prompts on a basis consisting of a board game, a card game, and a knowledge-retrieval task might yield a prompt that produces generalizable improvements across diverse board games, card games, and knowledge-retrieval tasks.
