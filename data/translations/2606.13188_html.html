用于直接心脏网格重建的变压器引导图注意力：结构数字孪生框架Transformer-Guided Graph Attention for Direct Cardiac Mesh Reconstruction: A Structural Digital Twin Framework

摘要。Abstract.

构建患者特异性心脏模型是精准心脏病学的核心，但将这些模型投入实际临床使用却总是遇到同样的问题：网格生成缓慢、混乱且令人沮丧。标准工作流程（分割图像、运行 Marching Cubes，然后花费数小时手动清理结果）非常耗时，而且操作员之间不一致，并且需要大多数临床团队根本不具备的专业知识水平。Building patient-specific cardiac models sits at the heart of precision cardiology, yet getting those models into actual clinical use keeps running into the same wall: mesh generation is slow, messy, and frustrating. The standard workflow—segmenting the image, running Marching Cubes, and then spending hours manually cleaning up the result—is time-consuming, inconsistent across operators, and demands a level of specialist knowledge that most clinical teams simply do not have available.

在本文中，我们采取了一种根本不同的方法。我们没有将分割和网格生成视为两个独立的问题，而是训练一个端到端网络，该网络直接从原始 3D 医学图像转换为平滑的、可用于模拟的心脏表面网格。我们框架的核心是 3D Swin Transformer 编码器-解码器，它可以从 CT 或 MRI 体积中提取丰富的体积特征，并与图形注意力网络 (GAT) 头配对，该头采用模板网格并完全自动迭代地使其变形，直到它适合患者的心脏边界。In this paper, we take a fundamentally different approach. Instead of treating segmentation and mesh generation as two separate problems, we train a single end-to-end network that goes directly from a raw 3D medical image to a smooth, simulation-ready cardiac surface mesh. The core of our framework is a 3D Swin Transformer encoder–decoder that pulls rich volumetric features out of CT or MRI volumes, paired with a Graph Attention Network (GAT) head that takes a template mesh and deforms it—iteratively, and entirely automatically—until it fits the patient’s cardiac boundary.

我们使用 CT 和 MRI 数据在 MM-WHS 2017 基准测试中测试了该框架。体积分割分数具有竞争力（CT 上的 Dice 为 0.84，MRI 上的 Dice 为 0.83），但我们真正关心的数字孪生结构是网格质量，在这方面，结果很强劲：平均倒角距离为 1.8 毫米，第 95 百分位表面距离完全低于 5 毫米。至关重要的是，每一个网格都来自一次前向传递，没有行进立方体，没有平滑过滤器，也没有任何手动清理。We tested the framework on the MM-WHS 2017 benchmark using both CT and MRI data. Volumetric segmentation scores came out competitive (Dice of 0.84 on CT, 0.83 on MRI), but what we really care about for digital twin construction is mesh quality—and on that front, results were strong: mean Chamfer distance of 1.8 mm, with 95th-percentile surface distance comfortably below 5 mm. Crucially, every one of those meshes came out of a single forward pass, with no Marching Cubes, no smoothing filters, and no manual cleanup whatsoever.

我们在整个工作中的核心论点是，对于心脏数字孪生管道来说，几何保真度和拓扑正确性比像素级 Dice 分数更重要。通过消除后处理瓶颈，这种直接网格化方法使得构建个性化心脏模拟模型并将其实际投入临床使用变得更加容易。Our core argument throughout this work is that for cardiac digital twin pipelines, geometric fidelity and topological correctness matter a great deal more than pixel-level Dice scores. By removing the post-processing bottleneck, this direct-to-mesh approach makes it substantially easier to build personalized cardiac simulation models and actually get them into clinical use.

1. 简介Introduction

1.1. 动机：从细分到数字孪生Motivation: From Segmentation to Digital Twins

心血管疾病仍然是世界各地死亡和残疾的主要原因之一，对能够支持患者个体临床决策的计算工具的需求日益增长。根据患者自身的成像数据构建的患者特定模型，为更精确的诊断、更好的风险分层和真正针对您面前的个人量身定制的治疗计划提供了一条真正的途径。在这个更广阔的前景中，数字孪生作为一种潜在的变革性想法引起了相当大的关注：数字孪生是患者生理机能的不断更新的虚拟复制品，可以被查询、询问并用于运行模拟Cardiovascular disease remains one of the leading causes of death and disability around the world, and there is a growing need for computational tools that can support clinical decision-making at the individual patient level. Patient-specific models—built from a person’s own imaging data—offer a real path toward more precise diagnosis, better risk stratification, and treatment planning that is actually tailored to the individual in front of you. Within this broader landscape, digital twins have attracted considerable attention as a potentially transformative idea: a digital twin is a continuously updated virtual replica of a patient’s physiology, one that can be queried, interrogated, and used to run simulations(corralacero2020, ; sel2024, ; cardiovascularframework2025, ).

这个愿景令人信服。通过将解剖结构与电生理学和血流动力学模拟相结合，心脏数字孪生可以为临床医生提供有关特定患者心脏功能的真实预测图，以及它对不同干预措施的反应。但实现这一愿景取决于解决一个往往被低估的基本问题：我们所说的The vision is compelling. By combining anatomical structure with electrophysiological and hemodynamic simulation, a cardiac digital twin could give clinicians a genuinely predictive picture of how a specific patient’s heart is functioning—and how it might respond to different interventions. But realizing that vision depends on solving a foundational problem that tends to get underappreciated: what we call结构孪生structural twinning。这是将原始医学图像（CT 或 MRI）转换为准确、高保真 3D 模型的过程，这些模型实际上可以输入到模拟软件中，而无需先进行数天的手动工作。. This is the process of converting raw medical images—CT or MRI—into accurate, high-fidelity 3D models that can actually be fed into simulation software without requiring days of manual work first.

1.2. 当前管道的问题The Problem with Current Pipelines

现代分割网络（无论是基于 U-Net、nnU-Net 还是 Transformer 架构构建）在体素级精度方面都非常出色，在标准基准测试中通常能达到 0.90 以上的 Dice 分数。但分割掩模并不是模拟求解器所需要的。在网格实际用于有限元或流体动力学计算之前，它必须经过后处理管道，该管道通常涉及三个不同的步骤。Modern segmentation networks—whether built on U-Net, nnU-Net, or transformer architectures—have gotten remarkably good at voxel-level accuracy, routinely hitting Dice scores above 0.90 on standard benchmarks. But a segmentation mask is not what a simulation solver needs. Before a mesh can actually be used for finite element or fluid dynamics computations, it has to go through a post-processing pipeline that typically involves three distinct steps.

第一个是行进立方体The first is Marching Cubes(lorensen1987, )，它将二元或多标签分割掩模转换为三角形表面网格。众所周知，该步骤对体素级噪声非常敏感，并且会沿着轴对齐表面可靠地产生阶梯伪影。第二个是平滑：应用拉普拉斯或类似的滤波器来减少体素对齐几何体引入的块效应，尽管这是以模糊解剖学上有意义的细节为代价的。第三种是手动细化，领域专家检查网格并修复先前步骤引入的任何拓扑问题——翻转的面、孔、断开的组件、在解剖学上不合理的形状。至关重要的是，该流程基于一个经常错误的假设：高 Dice 分数自动意味着高质量的网格。, which converts a binary or multi-label segmentation mask into a triangle surface mesh. This step is notoriously sensitive to voxel-level noise and reliably produces staircase artifacts along axis-aligned surfaces. The second is smoothing: Laplacian or similar filters are applied to reduce the blockiness introduced by voxel-aligned geometry, though this comes at the cost of blurring anatomically meaningful detail. The third is manual refinement, where a domain expert inspects the mesh and fixes whatever topological problems the earlier steps introduced—flipped faces, holes, disconnected components, shapes that are anatomically implausible. Crucially, this pipeline rests on an assumption that is frequently wrong: that a high Dice score automatically means a good-quality mesh.

1.3. 我们的贡献：直接网格重建Our Contribution: Direct Mesh Reconstruction

我们建议完全放弃这种两阶段范式。我们不是首先生成分割，然后将其转换为网格，而是训练网络直接从原始体积图像预测平滑、可用于模拟的表面网格。分割和网格重建作为单个端到端框架内的统一任务进行处理。We propose stepping away from this two-stage paradigm entirely. Rather than first producing a segmentation and then converting it to a mesh, we train a network to directly predict smooth, simulation-ready surface meshes from raw volumetric images. Segmentation and mesh reconstruction are handled as a unified task within a single end-to-end framework.

中心思想是，将基于变压器的体积特征提取器与基于图形的网格变形模块相结合，可以在不依赖行进立方体的情况下预测表面几何形状。具体来说：The central idea is that combining a transformer-based volumetric feature extractor with a graph-based mesh deformation module makes it possible to predict surface geometry without any reliance on Marching Cubes. Concretely:

  • •

    3D Swin Transformer 编码器-解码器处理体积 CT 或 MRI 输入，并生成丰富的多尺度上下文特征图，捕获局部细节和全局解剖上下文。A 3D Swin Transformer encoder–decoder processes the volumetric CT or MRI input and produces rich, multi-scale contextual feature maps that capture both local detail and global anatomical context.

  • •

    然后，图注意力网络（GAT）头对模板网格进行操作，使用这些特征迭代地使其变形，直到它与患者的心脏边界相匹配。A Graph Attention Network (GAT) head then operates on a template mesh, using those features to iteratively deform it until it matches the patient’s cardiac boundary.

  • •

    该模型在体素分割目标和几何网格质量目标上联合训练，因此输出同时具有解剖学精确性和模拟兼容性。The model is trained jointly on voxel-wise segmentation objectives and geometric mesh quality objectives, so the output is simultaneously anatomically accurate and simulation-compatible.

其结果是一个干净、拓扑健全的网格，几乎不需要后处理，这使得它比传统管道中的任何东西都更容易用于临床。The result is a clean, topologically sound mesh that requires little to no post-processing—making it far more accessible for clinical use than anything coming out of a traditional pipeline.

  1. (1)

    新颖的直接网格架构。Novel direct-to-mesh architecture.我们提出了一个端到端框架，将 3D Swin Transformers 与图注意力网络相结合，直接输出平滑的心脏表面网格，而不依赖于 Marching Cubes。We propose an end-to-end framework that combines 3D Swin Transformers with Graph Attention Networks to directly output smooth cardiac surface meshes, without any dependence on Marching Cubes.

  2. (2)

    综合多模式评价。Comprehensive multi-modal evaluation.我们使用 CT 和 MRI 在 MM-WHS 2017 上进行验证，报告了强大的网格质量指标（平均倒角距离 1.8 毫米，第 95 个百分点的表面距离低于 5 毫米），与传统的分割到网格管道相比具有优势。We validate on MM-WHS 2017 using both CT and MRI, reporting strong mesh quality metrics (mean Chamfer distance 1.8 mm, 95th-percentile surface distance below 5 mm) that compare favorably to conventional segmentation-to-mesh pipelines.

3.3. 网络架构Network Architecture

所提出的框架有两个主要组成部分（如图所示）The proposed framework has two main components (shown in Figure2）。首先，3D Swin Transformer 编码器-解码器可生成体积分割图和深度特征表示。其次，图注意力网络（GAT）网格变形头使用这些特征直接将模板网格变形为患者心脏的形状。这两个组件都使用结合了体素目标和几何目标的损失函数进行联合训练。). First, a 3D Swin Transformer encoder–decoder that produces volumetric segmentation maps and deep feature representations. Second, a Graph Attention Network (GAT) mesh deformation head that uses those features to directly deform a template mesh into the shape of the patient’s heart. Both components are trained jointly using a loss function that combines voxel-wise and geometric objectives.

3.3.1. 3D Swin Transformer 编码器-解码器3D Swin Transformer Encoder–Decoder

分割主干是 3D Swin Transformer。输入量被分为不重叠的块，这些块通过基于窗口的自注意力阶段的层次结构进行处理。编码器有四个阶段，每个阶段以逐渐降低的空间分辨率和相应更大的特征维度运行，从而允许模型构建跨越越来越大的空间上下文的表示。The segmentation backbone is a 3D Swin Transformer. The input volume is divided into non-overlapping patches, which are processed through a hierarchy of window-based self-attention stages. The encoder has four stages, each operating at progressively lower spatial resolution with a correspondingly larger feature dimension, allowing the model to build representations that span increasingly large spatial contexts.

关键的创新是转移窗口注意力机制。通过在基于标准窗口的注意力和在移动窗口上计算的注意力之间进行交替，该架构实现了跨窗口信息交换，而不会产生全局自注意力的二次成本，使其可以实际应用于高分辨率 3D 医学图像。The key innovation is the shifted window attention mechanism. By alternating between standard window-based attention and attention computed over shifted windows, the architecture achieves cross-window information exchange without incurring the quadratic cost of global self-attention—making it practical to apply to high-resolution 3D medical images.

5.1. 分割与网格质量：结果意味着什么Segmentation vs. Mesh Quality: What the Results Mean

我们的整体分割精度（CT 上的 Dice 为 0.84，MRI 上的 Dice 为 0.83）非常可靠，但与以分割为中心的方法报告的最高分数不太相符。这个间隙是设计使然的。我们的框架针对网格质量而不是体素级重叠进行了优化。通过大幅平滑其预测来实现 Dice 为 0.90 的模型可能在分割排行榜上看起来令人印象深刻，但可能会生成完全无法用于有限元模拟的网格。Our overall segmentation accuracy—Dice of 0.84 on CT and 0.83 on MRI—is solid, but does not quite match the top scores reported by segmentation-focused methods. This gap is by design. Our framework is optimized for mesh quality rather than voxel-level overlap. A model that achieves Dice of 0.90 by heavily smoothing its predictions may look impressive on a segmentation leaderboard but could produce meshes that are completely unusable for finite element simulation.

这里真正新颖的贡献是，该框架直接输出平均倒角距离为 1.8 毫米、第 95 百分位数表面到表面误差低于 5 毫米的可仿真网格。与传统的分割流程相比，这代表了一个本质上不同的优化目标——它实际上符合心脏数字双胞胎构建的要求，而不是符合为不同目的而设计的基准惯例。The genuinely novel contribution here is that the framework directly outputs simulation-ready meshes with a mean Chamfer distance of 1.8 mm and a 95th-percentile surface-to-surface error below 5 mm. This represents a qualitatively different optimization target compared to traditional segmentation pipelines—one that is actually aligned with the requirements of cardiac digital twin construction, rather than with benchmark conventions that were designed for a different purpose.

5.2. 架构论证Architectural Justification

选择 Swin Transformer 编码器而不是基于 CNN 的编码器（U-Net、3D-UNet）是因为它具有分层、基于窗口的注意力，能够捕获具有线性复杂性的远程空间依赖性，这对于跨尺度描绘大型心脏结构至关重要。选择 GAT 优于隐式函数方法（DeepSDF、Voxel2Mesh），因为它直接预测每个顶点的位移，无需昂贵的查询点评估；也优于标准 GCN，因为它学习的注意力机制适应不规则的网格拓扑和解剖变化。该组合可以实现分段精度和几何网格质量的端到端联合优化，这是顺序管道无法实现的。The Swin Transformer encoder was selected over CNN-based encoders (U-Net, 3D-UNet) for its hierarchical, window-based attention, which captures long-range spatial dependencies with linear complexity—essential for delineating large cardiac structures across scales. GAT was chosen over implicit function methods (DeepSDF, Voxel2Mesh) because it directly predicts per-vertex displacements without expensive query-point evaluations, and over standard GCN because its learned attention mechanism adapts to irregular mesh topology and anatomical variation. The combination enables end-to-end joint optimization for both segmentation accuracy and geometric mesh quality, which sequential pipelines cannot achieve.

5.3. 临床意义Clinical Implications

平滑、拓扑一致的网格是心脏模拟中使用的有限元和有限体积求解器的硬性要求。通过消除手动网格细化，我们的直接网格方法消除了主要瓶颈，并使患者特定的模拟无需专门的网格工程专业知识即可进行。该框架是模块化的，无需修改即可插入现有的电生理和血液动力学模拟环境。Smooth, topologically consistent meshes are a hard requirement for finite element and finite volume solvers used in cardiac simulation. By eliminating manual mesh refinement, our direct-to-mesh approach removes a major bottleneck and makes patient-specific simulation accessible without specialized mesh engineering expertise. The framework is modular and can be plugged into existing electrophysiological and hemodynamic simulation environments without modification.

5.4. 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

当前的框架重建了单个集成的全心脏网格；未来的工作应该将其扩展到心房、心室和大血管的独立、耦合的网状组件。该方法在静态单帧几何结构上运行——对整个周期的心脏运动进行建模是实现全数字孪生保真度的必要下一步。The current framework reconstructs a single integrated whole-heart mesh; future work should extend this to separate, coupled mesh components for the atria, ventricles, and great vessels. The method operates on static single-frame geometry—modeling cardiac motion across the cycle is a necessary next step for full digital twin fidelity.

目前评估仅限于结构正常的心脏。对 10 例病理病例（扩张型心肌病、心房颤动重构）进行的初步测试显示，10 例中的​​ 9 例模板定位成功，但 1 例严重左心室扩张失败，这表明病理解剖需要特定于解剖结构的模板或学习的初始化网络。在临床部署之前，对不同群体的全面验证仍然至关重要。实时电生理学或血液动力学模拟环境中的端到端验证正在进行中。Evaluation is currently limited to structurally normal hearts. Pilot testing on 10 pathological cases (dilated cardiomyopathy, atrial fibrillation remodeling) showed successful template positioning in 9 of 10, but one severe case with extreme LA dilation failed, suggesting that pathological anatomies will require anatomy-specific templates or a learned initialization network. Full validation on diverse cohorts remains critical before clinical deployment. End-to-end validation within a live electrophysiological or hemodynamic simulation environment is in progress.
