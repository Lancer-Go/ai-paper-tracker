1 简介Introduction

长视频理解已成为现代视觉语言模型（VLM）的重要能力，支持视频问答和视频检索等应用。 然而，直接处理整个长视频在计算上仍然令人望而却步，因为视频可能包含数万帧，远远超过了 VLM 的上下文窗口。 实际上，只能提供一小部分帧作为稀疏视觉证据。因此，在有限的框架预算下选择信息证据对于推理效率和答案准确性至关重要。Long-video understanding has become an important capability of modern vision–language models (VLMs), supporting applications such as video question answering and video retrieval. However, directly processing an entire long video remains computationally prohibitive, as a video may contain tens of thousands of frames, far exceeding the context window of VLMs. In practice, only a small subset of frames can be provided as sparse visual evidence. Consequently, selecting informative evidence under a limited frame budget is critical to both inference efficiency and answer accuracy.

7 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 EcoFrame，这是一种用于低开销查询自适应视觉证据调度的免训练框架。 通过使用输出熵来调整帧预算和帧级注意力来指导候选扩展，​​EcoFrame 实现了强大的准确性与效率权衡，而无需昂贵的基于代理的推理。 我们的结果证明了重用目标 VLM 生成的内部信号对于轻量级、自适应证据调度的价值。We introduce EcoFrame, a training-free framework for low-overhead query-adaptive visual evidence scheduling. By using output entropy to adapt the frame budget and frame-level attention to guide candidate expansion, EcoFrame achieves a strong accuracy–efficiency trade-off without expensive agent-based reasoning. Our results demonstrate the value of reusing internal signals produced by the target VLM for lightweight, adaptive evidence scheduling.

场景相关的计算配置。Scenario-Dependent Compute Configuration.

大海捞针的证据。Needle-in-a-Haystack Evidence.

当极短的证据片段仅占长视频的一小部分时，它们可能仍然具有挑战性。 注意力引导的候选提案通过在注意力分布分散时保留全局覆盖来缓解这个问题，但在有限的候选框架下仍然可能会错过极其简短的事件。未来的工作可以结合互补的时间线索，例如事件边界或运动显着性，以实现更精确的证据搜索。Extremely short evidence segments may remain challenging when they occupy only a very small fraction of a long video. Attention-guided candidate proposal mitigates this issue by preserving global coverage when the attention distribution is diffuse, but extremely brief events may still be missed with limited candidate frames. Future work could incorporate complementary temporal cues, such as event boundaries or motion saliency, to enable more precise evidence search.
