摘要Abstract

解释在时间序列数据上运行的深度学习模型对于需要对模型行为进行透明且可解释的洞察的各种应用程序至关重要。现有的解释方法通常分为两类：基于归因的解释，它确定对预测最有影响的时间区域；反事实解释，它揭示应如何修改输入以改变模型的决策。 尽管见解很有价值，但这两个领域在很大程度上是独立研究的。这种脱节使得归因方法缺乏因果验证，而反事实方法则严重不稳定，产生类似对抗性的噪音，而不是有意义的解释。 在这项工作中，我们从信息论的角度重新审视时间序列的可解释性，并表明现有的解释器容易受到琐碎解决方案和分布变化的影响。 为了解决这些限制，我们提出了一个用于可解释时间序列学习的统一目标函数，该函数在单个框架内连接归因和反事实推理。 基于信息瓶颈原则，我们的表述明确防止了琐碎的解释和分布外的反事实。 基于这个目标函数，我们引入了 TimeX++，一种新颖的解释框架，它学习参数转换网络来构建解释嵌入实例，其中保留的信息产生归因解释，受控信息删除产生稳定的反事实解释。 我们根据最先进的基准在合成基准和现实基准上评估 TimeX++。广泛的定量和定性结果表明，TimeX++ 始终优于竞争方法，产生忠实的归因和稳定的反事实解释。Explaining deep learning models operating on time series data is crucial in various applications that require transparent and interpretable insights into model behavior. Existing explanation methods generally fall into two categories: attribution-based explanations, which identify the temporal regions most responsible for a prediction, and counterfactual explanations, which reveal how an input should be modified to alter the model’s decision. Despite valuable insights, these two fields are largely studied independently. This disconnect leaves attribution methods lacking causal validation, while counterfactual methods suffer from severe instability, producing adversarial-like noise instead of meaningful explanations. In this work, we revisit time-series explainability from an information-theoretic perspective and show that existing explainers are vulnerable to trivial solutions and distributional shifts. To address these limitations, we propose a unified objective function for explainable time series learning that bridges attribution and counterfactual reasoning within a single framework. Building upon the Information Bottleneck principle, our formulation explicitly prevents trivial explanations and out-of-distribution counterfactuals. Based on this objective function, we introduce TimeX++, a novel explanation framework that learns a parametric transformation network to construct explanation-embedded instances, where preserved information yields attribution explanations and controlled information removal produces stable counterfactual explanations. We evaluate TimeX++ on synthetic and real-world benchmarks against state-of-the-art baselines. Extensive quantitative and qualitative results show that TimeX++ consistently outperforms competing methods, yielding faithful attributions and stable counterfactual explanations.

七VII结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们从理论上研究了时间序列解释的信息论指导目标，以确保紧凑性和信息性。 我们提出了一种基于 IB 原理的新颖方法 TimeX++，它允许可追溯性计算产生忠实的归因解释和稳健的反事实解释。 对合成数据集和真实世界数据集的综合研究已经证实 TimeX++ 在性能和效率方面超越了现有方法。 其有效性表明TimeX++能够准确反映预训练时间序列分类器的复杂行为。 然而，它可能会在学习目标中涉及一些超参数来控制解释的量词，特别是在处理不同的数据集时，这可能是关键的限制。In this work, we theoretically investigate an information-theoretic guided objective for time series explanations that ensures compactness and informativeness. We propose a novel approach, TimeX++, based on the IB principle, which allows a traceability computation to produce faithful attribution explanations and robust counterfactual explanations. Comprehensive studies on synthetic and real-world datasets have confirmed that TimeX++ surpasses existing approaches in performance and efficiency. Its effectiveness shows that TimeX++’s capability to reflect complex behaviors of pre-trained time series classifiers accurately. However, it may involve some hyperparameters in the learning objective to control the quantifiers of the explanation, especially when dealing with different datasets, which might be the key limitation.
