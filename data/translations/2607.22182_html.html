从孤立的任务到结构化的功能：大型语言模型的多层分类法From Isolated Tasks to Structured Capabilities: A Multilayer Taxonomy for Large Language Models

Fudan University
fangshixin@fudan.edu.cn; 25213050400@m.fudan.edu.cn

摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 评估涵盖不同的任务和基准，但证据仍然围绕任务而不是它们探索的能力进行组织。这种碎片化限制了交叉研究比较，模糊了能力任务的招募，并使覆盖范围的差距难以识别。Large language model (LLM) evaluation spans diverse tasks and benchmarks, yet evidence remains organized around tasks rather than the capabilities they probe. This fragmentation limits cross-study comparison, obscures capabilities tasks recruit, and makes coverage gaps difficult to identify.

我们引入了跨原始层、构造层和集成层的 14 个功能域和 91 个子技能的多层分类法。人类认知科学指导能力定义和组织，而不是法学硕士架构。层分配利用了发展优先级和假设的功能支持，而人类起源的构造则适应可观察的模型行为。We introduce a multi-layer taxonomy of 14 capability domains and 91 subskills across Primitive, Constructed, and Integrative layers. Human cognitive science guides capability definition and organization, not LLM architecture. Layer assignments draw on developmental precedence and hypothesized functional support, while human-origin constructs are adapted to observable model behavior.

1 简介Introduction

人类认知通常被描述为一个随着时间的推移而发展的相互作用的基础和高阶过程的系统（Diamond，2013）。然而，当代大型语言模型（LLM）通常表现出截然不同的能力概况。Human cognition is often described as a system of interacting foundational and higher-order processes that develop over time (Diamond, 2013). Contemporary large language models (LLMs), however, often show a very different capability profile.

最先进的法学硕士现在在困难知识和多步骤推理评估方面取得了出色的表现（OpenAI，2025；Wei 等人，2022）。与此同时，它们在基本的规则管理操作上仍然很脆弱，包括计数和简单的符号转换（McCoy 等人，2024）。即使在明显的成功之后，这种脆弱性也可能持续存在：当任务前提或规则被反事实地修改时，性能可能会下降（Wu et al., 2024）。我们指的是这种模式能力倒置：模型在通常被认为是认知高级的任务上可能看起来很强大，但在理解相关规则后人类经常一致执行的基本操作上仍然不可靠。对于 LLM 评估，能力反演将明显的异常转化为测量问题，因为基准测试的成功本身并不能表明底层能力是否稳定、可转移或组合支持。State-of-the-art LLMs now achieve strong performance on difficult knowledge and multi-step reasoning evaluations (OpenAI, 2025; Wei et al., 2022). At the same time, they can remain brittle on elementary rule-governed operations, including counting and simple symbolic transformations (McCoy et al., 2024). This fragility can persist even after apparent success: performance may degrade when task premises or rules are counterfactually modified (Wu et al., 2024). We refer to this pattern ability inversion: a model may look strong on tasks usually regarded as cognitively advanced while remaining unreliable on basic operations that humans often perform consistently once the relevant rules are understood. For LLM evaluation, ability inversion turns an apparent anomaly into a measurement problem, because benchmark success alone does not show whether the underlying capability is stable, transferable, or compositionally supported.

这一衡量问题反映了法学硕士能力如何表示、研究和评估的结构性限制。大多数基准测试都是以任务为中心的：它们衡量特定数据集、任务或场景的性能，而不指定正在探测哪些功能、任务招募哪些附加功能，或者失败对模型更广泛的功能概况意味着什么。因此，基准分数通常是描述性的，而不是诊断性的。例如，如果模型未能完成推理任务，则失败可能反映了推理本身、记忆或注意力的限制，或者协调这些能力的失败。现有的评估更多地告诉我们模型的得分，而不是它成功或失败的原因。This measurement problem reflects a structural limitation in how LLM capabilities are represented, studied, and evaluated. Most benchmarks are task-centric: they measure performance on particular datasets, tasks, or scenarios without specifying which capability is being probed, which additional capabilities the task recruits, or what a failure implies about the model’s broader capability profile. As a result, benchmark scores are often descriptive but not diagnostic. If a model fails a reasoning task, for example, the failure may reflect reasoning itself, limitations in memory or attention, or a breakdown in coordinating these capabilities. Existing evaluations more often tell us what a model scored than why it succeeded or failed.

以任务为中心的评估的一个相关后果是研究注意力在认知能力上的分布不均匀。对来自领先的 NLP 和 ML 会议的 445 个 LLM 基准的系统回顾发现，仅推理就占所有评估现象的 18.5%，覆盖范围集中在少数领域（Bean 等人，2025）。使用布鲁姆分类法，补充分析发现现有数据集围绕较低级别的记忆和理解而聚集，而元认知和创造性推理的代表性仍然不足（Zhang 等人，2025）。在当前的法学硕士评估中，心理和以人为中心的结构仍然分散且标准化程度较低（Chang et al., 2024；Ye et al., 2025）。这就提出了一个实证问题，即研究注意力如何分布在更广泛的认知能力空间，包括社会、文化或道德领域。这种集中还可能决定随后的评估和开发优先事项，从而使较少研究的能力相对被低估且难以诊断。A related consequence of task-centric evaluation is the uneven distribution of research attention across cognitive capabilities. A systematic review of 445 LLM benchmarks from leading NLP and ML conferences finds that reasoning alone accounts for 18.5% of all evaluated phenomena, with coverage concentrated in a handful of domains (Bean et al., 2025). Using Bloom’s Taxonomy, a complementary analysis finds that existing datasets cluster around lower-level remembering and understanding while metacognitive and creative reasoning stay underrepresented (Zhang et al., 2025). Psychological and human-centered constructs remain fragmented and less standardized in current LLM evaluation (Chang et al., 2024; Ye et al., 2025). This raises the empirical question of how research attention is distributed across the broader cognitive capability space, including for example, social, cultural, or moral domains. Such concentration may also shape subsequent evaluation and development priorities, leaving less frequently studied capabilities comparatively undermeasured and difficult to diagnose.

现有方法部分解决了这些诊断和覆盖问题。最近的基准框架扩大了法学硕士评估的广度和现实性，包括专家级知识评估、编码、多模态推理和面向主体的评估，如 Humanity’s Last Exam (Phan et al., 2025)、LiveCodeBench (Jain et al., 2025) 和 BrowseComp (Wei et al., 2025)。然而，许多评估仍然围绕任务或部署场景进行组织，而不是围绕评估目标功能的结构化描述。数据驱动的方法可以揭示任务和技能之间的经验相似性或转移关系（Chen 等人，2023），但这些关系本身并不能提供能力空间的广泛认知组织。 CogBench (Coda-Forno et al., 2024)、CogLM (Wang et al., 2024)、CognitivEval (de Langis et al., 2025) 和 NeuroCognition (Haznitama et al., 2026) 等认知启发的评估显示了以心理为基础的构建对于解释超越总分的 LLM 行为的价值。然而，它们的主要分析单位仍然是测量的构造或任务，而不是跨能力域的系统关系。最近的框架，包括 CDT（Mo 等人，2025）和 Burnell 等人的认知框架。 （2026），在更结构化的层面上组织能力。伯内尔等人。 （2026）明确区分基本能力和复合能力，并承认认知能力相互作用并相互构建，但它们的框架和 CDT 都没有共同使用发展优先级和假设的功能支持来定义多层分类法。 HELM 等整体框架通过共同考虑场景和多种需求，将评估范围扩大到准确性之外，但其组织单位仍然是场景和指标，而不是认知能力关系（Liang 等，2023）。总而言之，这些进步留下了一个未解决的核心代表性问题：如何将碎片化的任务级证据组织到一个共同的能力空间中，以支持系统比较、覆盖分析和诊断假设生成。Existing approaches partially address these diagnostic and coverage problems. Recent benchmark frameworks have expanded the breadth and realism of LLM evaluation, including expert-level knowledge assessment, coding, multimodal reasoning, and agent-oriented evaluation, as in Humanity’s Last Exam (Phan et al., 2025), LiveCodeBench (Jain et al., 2025), and BrowseComp (Wei et al., 2025). However, many remain organized around tasks or deployment scenarios rather than around a structured account of the capabilities those evaluations target. Data-driven approaches can reveal empirical similarity or transfer relations among tasks and skills (Chen et al., 2023), but these relations do not by themselves provide a broad cognitive organization of the capability space. Cognitive-inspired evaluations such as CogBench (Coda-Forno et al., 2024), CogLM (Wang et al., 2024), CognitivEval (de Langis et al., 2025), and NeuroCognition (Haznitrama et al., 2026) show the value of psychologically grounded constructs for interpreting LLM behavior beyond aggregate scores. Their primary unit of analysis, however, remains the measured construct or task rather than systematic relations across capability domains. More recent frameworks, including CDT (Mo et al., 2025) and the cognitive framework of Burnell et al. (2026), organize capabilities at a more structured level. Burnell et al. (2026) explicitly distinguish basic from composite faculties and acknowledge that cognitive faculties interact and build on one another, but neither their framework nor CDT jointly uses developmental precedence and hypothesized functional support to define a multi-layer taxonomy. Holistic frameworks such as HELM broaden evaluation beyond accuracy by jointly considering scenarios and multiple desiderata, but their organizing units remain scenarios and metrics rather than cognitive capability relations (Liang et al., 2023). Taken together, these advances leave a central representational problem unresolved: how to organize fragmented task-level evidence into a common capability space that supports systematic comparison, coverage analysis, and diagnostic hypothesis generation.

人类认知为解决这一差距提供了原则性参考。法学硕士可能会在人类认为简单的任务上失败，而在对大多数人来说困难的任务上取得成功。这种不对称性表明，人类认知可能会产生关于法学硕士能力差距的有用的结构假设。发展、神经认知和认知传统的融合将认知描述为涉及异质发展轨迹和协调功能支持的相互作用过程（Diamond，2013；Gogtay 等，2004；Miller & Cohen，2001；Piaget，1952；Vygotsky，1978）。因此，人类认知研究为分类法构建提供了三种资源：广泛的候选能力空间、组织能力之间关系的证据以及可适应法学硕士研究可观察目标的既定结构。Human cognition provides a principled reference for addressing this gap. LLMs may fail on tasks that humans find elementary while succeeding on tasks that are difficult for most people. The asymmetry suggests that human cognition may generate useful structural hypotheses about capability gaps of LLMs. Converging developmental, neurocognitive, and cognitive traditions portray cognition as involving interacting processes with heterogeneous developmental trajectories and coordinated functional support (Diamond, 2013; Gogtay et al., 2004; Miller & Cohen, 2001; Piaget, 1952; Vygotsky, 1978). Human cognitive research therefore offers three resources for taxonomy construction: a broad candidate capability space, evidence for organizing relationships among capabilities, and established constructs that can be adapted into observable targets for LLM research.

这些资源导致了研究法学硕士的三个设计决策：包括哪些功能、如何组织它们以及如何使它们可用于法学硕士研究。它们激发了三个原则。首先，人类认知科学为识别现有任务和基准类别之外的能力提供了广阔的参考空间。其次，能力应该根据发展优先顺序和假设的功能支持来组织，而不是表示为独立技能的简单列表。第三，人类认知结构必须适应可观察和可操作的有用目标，以用于研究和评估法学硕士。这些原则并不意味着法学硕士重现人类认知架构或遵循人类发展轨迹。相反，他们定义了一个结构化的能力空间，可以组织现有的研究，揭示覆盖和忽视的模式，并生成有关能力之间关系的可检验的假设。These resources lead to three design decisions for studying LLMs: which capabilities to include, how to organize them, and how to make them operational for LLM research. They motivate three principles. First, human cognitive science provides a broad reference space for identifying capabilities beyond existing task and benchmark categories. Second, capabilities should be organized according to developmental precedence and hypothesized functional support rather than represented as a flat list of independent skills. Third, human cognitive constructs must be adapted into observable and operationally useful targets for studying and evaluating LLMs. These principles do not imply that LLMs reproduce human cognitive architectures or follow human developmental trajectories. Instead, they define a structured capability space that can organize existing research, reveal patterns of coverage and neglect, and generate testable hypotheses about relationships among capabilities.

基于这些原则，我们提出了一种多层分类法，将 LLM 认知能力表示为一个结构化空间，包含跨三层的 14 个领域：原始 (L1)、建构 (L2) 和综合 (L3) 认知能力（图 1）。分类法表示跨层的候选垂直关系和层内的横向关系。它不是仅根据应用领域、任务、基准或输出相似性来组织法学硕士研究，而是允许根据研究工件明确研究的功能以及这些功能可操作的子结构来映射研究工件。这种结构化表示支持研究和基准映射，揭示能力覆盖范围的差距和不平衡，并为有针对性的后续评估生成可测试的假设。Building on these principles, we propose a multilayer taxonomy that represents LLM cognitive capabilities as a structured space comprising 14 domains across three layers: Primitive (L1), Constructed (L2), and Integrative (L3) Cognitive Capabilities (Figure 1). The taxonomy represents candidate vertical relations across layers and lateral relations within layers. Rather than organizing LLM research solely by application domains, tasks, benchmarks, or output similarity, it allows research artifacts to be mapped according to the capabilities they explicitly study and the subconstructs through which those capabilities are operationalized. This structured representation supports research and benchmark mapping, reveals gaps and imbalances in capability coverage, and generates testable hypotheses for targeted follow-up evaluation.

本文做出了三点贡献。首先，它引入了一种多层分类法，将 14 个认知能力领域和 91 种子技能组织成原始层、建构层和综合层，并根据人类认知中的发展优先级和假设的功能支持来进行层分配。其次，它将分类法作为注释密码本和可扩展的映射程序来操作，将作为主要目标研究的能力与仅间接或上下文出现的能力区分开来。第三，它映射了 15,934 篇以法学硕士为重点的论文，揭示了系统的不对称性，其中能力领域受到关注，哪些子技能在其中占主导地位，以及哪些能力被一起研究。这些贡献为比较评估证据、识别能力景观中的盲点以及生成可测试的诊断假设提供了共同基础。This paper makes three contributions. First, it introduces a multi-layer taxonomy that organizes 14 cognitive capability domains and 91 subskills into Primitive, Constructed, and Integrative layers, with layer assignments informed by developmental precedence and hypothesized functional support in human cognition. Second, it operationalizes the taxonomy as an annotation codebook and scalable mapping procedure that distinguishes capabilities studied as primary targets from those appearing only indirectly or contextually. Third, it maps 15,934 LLM-focused papers, revealing systematic asymmetries in which capability domains receive attention, which subskills dominate within them, and which capabilities are studied together. These contributions provide a common basis for comparing evaluation evidence, identifying blind spots in the capability landscape, and generating testable diagnostic hypotheses.

2 分类设计原则与构建Taxonomy Design Principles and Construction

本节描述了所提出的分类法的设计原则和构建过程。它围绕三个问题组织：什么样的能力空间应该作为法学硕士研究的参考？该空间内的能力应如何相互组织？源自人类认知的构造应该如何重新表述为法学硕士研究的可观察且可操作有用的目标？这些问题激发了三个设计原则：人类认知作为一般认知能力的参考空间、多层组织以及法学硕士特定的适应和操作化。This section describes the design principles and construction process of the proposed taxonomy. It is organized around three questions: What capability space should serve as a reference for LLM research? How should capabilities within that space be organized in relation to one another? And how should constructs derived from human cognition be reformulated as observable and operationally useful targets for LLM research? These questions motivate three design principles: human cognition as a reference space for general cognitive capability, multi-layer organization, and LLM-specific adaptation and operationalization.

2.1 范围和设计目标Scope and Design Objective

分类法是一个结构化的能力空间，用于描述、组织和关联法学硕士的认知能力。它不是LLM内部架构的模型，也不是基准套件。相反，它提供了一个表示层，可以支持文献综合、评估设计、能力诊断和面向发展的分析，而无需由法学硕士的任何单个应用程序定义。The taxonomy is a structured capability space for describing, organizing, and relating cognitive capabilities in LLMs. It is not a model of LLM internal architecture and not a benchmark suite. Instead, it provides a representational layer that can support literature synthesis, evaluation design, capability diagnosis, and development-oriented analysis without being defined by any single application for LLMs.

人类认知研究为我们的分类学构建提供了三种资源：用于区分能力的完善词汇、对其功能关系的经验和理论解释，以及可以为构造操作化和任务设计提供信息的既定测量传统。因此，分类法代表了可观察的能力构造和它们之间的候选功能关系。Research on human cognition provides three resources for our taxonomy construction: a well-developed vocabulary for distinguishing capabilities, empirical and theoretical accounts of their functional relationships, and established measurement traditions that can inform construct operationalization and task design. The taxonomy therefore represents observable capability constructs and candidate functional relationships among them.

2.2 设计原则Design Principles

这三个原则对应于三个不同的设计决策：如何定义初始能力空间，如何在该空间内组织能力，以及如何将人类认知结构转化为法学硕士兼容的概念和操作分类法。The three principles correspond to three distinct design decisions: how to define the initial capability space, how to organize capabilities within that space, and how to translate human cognitive constructs into an LLM-compatible conceptual and operational taxonomy.

2.2.1 原则 1：人类认知作为一般认知能力的参考空间Principle 1: Human Cognition as a Reference Space for General Cognitive Capability

人类认知科学为识别与一般认知能力相关的能力提供了成熟且广泛的参考空间。因此，我们从认知心理学、认知科学、认知神经科学和邻近领域重复出现的构造中得出了最初的候选空间。Human cognitive science provides a mature and broad reference space for identifying the capabilities relevant to general cognitive competence. We therefore derived the initial candidate space from constructs that recur across cognitive psychology, cognitive science, cognitive neuroscience, and adjacent fields.

我们的起始来源包括广泛使用的综合教科书和手册，例如Our starting sources included widely used integrative textbooks and handbooks, such as认知心理学：学生手册Cognitive Psychology: A Student’s Handbook（艾森克和基恩，2020），(Eysenck & Keane, 2020),认知：探索心灵的科学Cognition: Exploring the Science of the Mind（赖斯伯格，2013），以及(Reisberg, 2013), and认知神经科学Cognitive Neuroscience（巴尼奇和康普顿，2023）。在这些来源中，重复出现一组相对稳定的结构，包括感知、注意力、记忆、语言、推理和问题解决、判断和决策以及认知控制。(Banich & Compton, 2023). Across these sources, a relatively stable set of constructs recurs, including perception, attention, memory, language, reasoning and problem solving, judgment and decision-making, and cognitive control.

随着法学硕士被部署在代理、教育、咨询、人际和具有社会影响力的环境中，这个参考空间涵盖了变得越来越重要的能力。因此，旨在描述具有广泛能力的系统的分类法必须超越语言能力、事实知识和抽象推理。更广泛的文献还强调情感、创造力、元认知、计划、社会认知以及认知的文化和发展背景。This reference space covers capabilities that become increasingly relevant as LLMs are deployed in agentic, educational, advisory, interpersonal, and socially consequential settings. A taxonomy intended to characterize broadly capable systems must therefore extend beyond language proficiency, factual knowledge, and abstract reasoning. Broader literatures additionally foreground emotion, creativity, metacognition, planning, social cognition, and the cultural and developmental contexts of cognition.

2.2.2 原则 2：多层组织Principle 2: Multi-Layer Organization

第二个原则涉及如何组织能力之间的关系。层分配利用两种互补形式的证据：人类发展优先级和功能支持。发展优先性涉及一种能力是相对较早出现还是通过协调较早建立的能力来发展，而功能支持涉及一种能力是否提供另一种能力构建的表示或操作。这些标准被用作趋同的组织证据，而不是声称法学硕士再现了人类发展轨迹或认知架构。The second principle concerns how capabilities are organized in relation to one another. Layer assignment draws on two complementary forms of evidence: human developmental precedence and functional support. Developmental precedence concerns whether a capability emerges relatively early or develops through the coordination of capacities established earlier, whereas functional support concerns whether one capability provides representations or operations on which another capability builds. These criteria are used as convergent organizational evidence rather than as claims that LLMs reproduce human developmental trajectories or cognitive architectures.

发展优先性为区分相对基础的能力和涉及更大协调和整合的能力提供了证据。广义的认知发展理论将发展描述为通过协调先前水平建立的技能来逐步构建日益复杂的技能（Fischer，1980）。例如，注意力和记忆的早期表现可以通过视觉注意和识别记忆范式在婴儿期进行测量（Reynolds＆Richards，2019），而规划和决策的规划部分遵循更持久的轨迹，在困难的战略规划问题上的表现在青春期后期和成年早期持续改善（Albert＆Steinberg，2011）。然而，当代的观点强调，发展轨迹是重叠的、领域敏感的和非线性的，而不是构成能力获取的单一不变序列（Gauvain，2022；Houdé＆Borst，2022）。因此，发展优先级被视为层分配的收敛证据，而不是声称所有能力或法学硕士都通过固定的人类阶段发展。Developmental precedence provides evidence for distinguishing relatively foundational capabilities from those involving greater coordination and integration. Broad theories of cognitive development characterize development as the progressive construction of increasingly complex skills through the coordination of skills established at preceding levels (Fischer, 1980). For example, early manifestations of attention and memory are measurable in infancy through visual-attention and recognition-memory paradigms (Reynolds & Richards, 2019), whereas the planning component of planning and decision-making follows a more protracted trajectory, with performance on difficult strategic-planning problems continuing to improve through late adolescence and into early adulthood (Albert & Steinberg, 2011). Contemporary accounts nevertheless emphasize that developmental trajectories are overlapping, domain-sensitive, and nonlinear rather than constituting a single invariant sequence of capability acquisition (Gauvain, 2022; Houdé & Borst, 2022). Developmental precedence is therefore treated as convergent evidence for layer assignment, not as a claim that all capabilities or LLMs develop through fixed human stages.

功能支持为层分配提供了补充标准。当 B 的表现通常会招募与 A 相关的表征或操作时，能力 A 被视为支持 B。任务共现本身是不够的。例如，Functional support provides a complementary criterion for layer assignment. Capability A is treated as supporting B when performance on B typically recruits representations or operations associated with A. Task co-occurrence alone is insufficient. For example,语言语义能力Language-Semantic Competence建立在builds on内存Memory当话语层面的意义需要保留先前引入的信息并将其与随后的语言输入整合时（Baddeley，2003；Just＆Carpenter，1992）。when discourse-level meaning requires information introduced earlier to be maintained and integrated with subsequent linguistic input (Baddeley, 2003; Just & Carpenter, 1992).道德推理Moral Reasoning同样建立在similarly builds on心智理论Theory of Mind当评估一项行动时，需要整合代理人的信念和意图，而不是单独判断结果（Fadda et al., 2016; Moran et al., 2011）。更一般地说，对执行和层次控制的研究支持相对基本的操作和协调它们的更复杂的能力之间的区别（Badre & Nee，2018；Diamond，2013）。这些关系并不意味着 A 对于 B 来说是充分的，B 的每一种表现形式都同样依赖于 A，或者 LLM 实施相应的人类机制。when evaluating an action requires integrating an agent’s beliefs and intentions rather than judging outcomes alone (Fadda et al., 2016; Moran et al., 2011). More generally, research on executive and hierarchical control supports the distinction between relatively basic operations and more complex capacities that coordinate them (Badre & Nee, 2018; Diamond, 2013). These relations do not imply that A is sufficient for B, that every manifestation of B depends equally on A, or that LLMs implement the corresponding human mechanisms.

因此，由此产生的层次反映了发展的出现和功能组织。原始认知能力提供相对较早出现且广泛可重用的表示和操作。建构认知能力将这些基础组织成连贯的语言、推理、情感和心理状态能力。综合认知能力协调跨环境、目标和扩展行动过程的多种原始和构建能力。The resulting layers therefore reflect both developmental emergence and functional organization. Primitive Cognitive Capabilities provide relatively early-emerging and broadly reusable representations and operations. Constructed Cognitive Capabilities organize these foundations into coherent linguistic, inferential, affective, and mental-state capacities. Integrative Cognitive Capabilities coordinate multiple primitive and constructed capabilities across contexts, goals, and extended courses of action.

2.2.3 原则 3：LLM 特定的适应和实施Principle 3: LLM-Specific Adaptation and Operationalization

第三个原则涉及如何将人类认知中的构造应用于法学硕士研究。根据候选结构与可观察模型行为、能力分析和部署环境的相关性，对候选结构进行过滤、重新表述、整合或扩展。The third principle concerns how constructs drawn from human cognition are adapted for LLM research. Candidate constructs were filtered, reformulated, consolidated, or extended according to their relevance to observable model behavior, capability analysis, and deployment contexts.

没有有意义的行为类似物的结构被排除在外。例如，精细运动控制是人类功能的核心，但不属于该分类的范围。仅当可以通过可观察的行为、显式模型输出或系统级功能类似物来表达时，才会保留涉及主观经验、动机或内部状态的构造；他们的纳入并不意味着法学硕士拥有相应的人类经验或机制。据此，Constructs without meaningful behavioral analogues were excluded. Fine motor control, for example, is central to human functioning but falls outside the scope of this taxonomy. Constructs referring to subjective experience, motivation, or internal states are retained only insofar as they can be expressed through observable behavior, explicit model outputs, or system-level functional analogues; their inclusion does not imply that LLMs possess the corresponding human experiences or mechanisms. Accordingly,感知Perception指的是可观察的输入处理功能，而不是生物感觉传导或主观经验。当相关的人类结构发挥重叠的功能或分析作用时，它们就会得到巩固。语言分为refers to observable input-processing functions rather than biological sensory transduction or subjective experience. 法学硕士特定的适应还促使纳入一些能力，这些能力并不总是被视为认知中的独立领域，但在具有社会影响的部署中日益重要。例如，LLM-specific adaptation also motivated the inclusion of capabilities that are not always treated as independent domains in cognition but are increasingly important in socially consequential deployment. 最后，每个领域都在可以链接到可观察的模型行为、任务、基准和研究工件的级别上进行定义。在本文中，如果注释者和研究人员能够识别该领域，将其与相邻领域区分开来，并将其一致地应用于文献映射、基准分析、能力诊断和模型开发研究中，则该领域是可操作的。Finally, each domain is defined at a level that can be linked to observable model behavior, tasks, benchmarks, and research artifacts. In this paper, a domain is operationalizable if annotators and researchers can identify it, distinguish it from adjacent domains, and apply it consistently in literature mapping, benchmark analysis, capability diagnosis, and model-development research.

2.3 施工流程Construction Process

我们通过四个迭代阶段开发了分类法。We developed the taxonomy through four iterative phases.

第一阶段：候选人识别。Phase 1: Candidate identification.我们从认知心理学、认知科学、认知神经科学和相关领域的主要教科书和手册中反复出现的结构中确定了初步的能力库。We identified an initial pool of capabilities from recurring constructs in major textbooks and handbooks across cognitive psychology, cognitive science, cognitive neuroscience, and related fields.

第 2 阶段：构建细化。Phase 2: Construct elaboration.候选结构被临时分组以指导特定领域的搜索。十名研究助理在 Web of Science 中搜索了过去 50 年发表的具有影响力且被广泛引用的理论和评估文献。综合所得证据以完善构造定义、领域边界和候选子技能。Candidate constructs were provisionally grouped to guide domain-specific searches. Ten research assistants searched Web of Science for seminal and widely cited theoretical and assessment literature published over the preceding 50 years. The resulting evidence was synthesized to refine construct definitions, domain boundaries, and candidate subskills.

第 3 阶段：领域最终确定和分层组织。Phase 3: Domain finalization and hierarchical organization.在此综合的基础上，根据概念的独特性、与法学硕士研究和部署的相关性以及功能作用，保留、合并或排除结构。由此产生的分类包含 14 个领域的 91 个规范子技能。使用发展优先级和功能支持将领域分配到三层，当两个标准出现分歧时，功能支持会得到更大的权重。Based on this synthesis, constructs were retained, consolidated, or excluded according to conceptual distinctiveness, relevance to LLM research and deployment, and functional role. The resulting taxonomy comprised 91 canonical subskills across 14 domains. Domains were assigned to three layers using developmental precedence and functional support, with greater weight given to functional support when the two criteria diverged.

第 4 阶段：LLM 特定的实施。Phase 4: LLM-specific operationalization.领域和子技能定义适应可观察的模型行为、显式输出和系统级功能类似物。这一阶段产生了文献映射分析中使用的最终分类和注释密码本，包括纳入标准、证据强度规则以及注意力、内存、缓存、路由和奖励塑造等工程术语的负映射规则。Domain and subskill definitions were adapted to observable model behavior, explicit outputs, and system-level functional analogues. This phase produced the final taxonomy and annotation codebook used in the literature-mapping analysis, including inclusion criteria, evidence-strength rules, and negative mapping rules for engineering terms such as attention, memory, cache, routing, and reward shaping.

虽然按顺序呈现，但该过程是迭代的，后来的综合和操作化通知了对域边界、子技能定义和层分配的修订。该过程建立了分类法的概念和操作基础，但并未独立验证其提出的依赖关系。Although presented sequentially, the process was iterative, with later synthesis and operationalization informing revisions to domain boundaries, subskill definitions, and layer assignments. The process establishes the taxonomy’s conceptual and operational basis but does not independently validate its proposed dependency relations.

我们构建了一个分类注释代理，为法学硕士研究论文分配认知能力标签。对于每篇论文，代理都会发出两类标签：(i) 能力标签，取自跨三层分类（原始、构造、综合）组织的 14 个概念域；(ii) 子技能标签，取自嵌套在其父域下的 91 个规范子技能。We build a taxonomy annotation agent that assigns cognitive capability labels to LLM research papers. For each paper the agent emits two categories of labels: (i) capability labels drawn from the 14 conceptual domains organized across the three layers of the taxonomy (Primitive, Constructed, Integrative), and (ii) subskill labels drawn from the 91 canonical subskills nested under their parent domains.

对于每篇论文，智能体都会从 14 个能力域中分配一个或多个标签，并为每个分配的域生成一个 {strong、weak} 的证据强度标签，以及以分配的父域为条件的一组认可的子技能。当论文明确命名或操作该能力作为研究的中心对象，并通过专门的任务、基准、诊断实验、机制分析或主要结论来支持该主张时，就会发出强有力的证据标签。当能力间接出现时，例如作为支持结构、任务上下文、下游行为或启用机制，就会发出弱证据标签。领域并不被视为相互排斥：一篇论文在实质性地操作重叠结构时可能会收到多个领域和子技能标签。当有文本支持时，认知标签之间的概念重叠是允许的，但仅与工程术语的词汇重叠不足以许可认知能力标签。For each paper, the agent assigns one or more labels from the 14 capability domains and produces an evidence-strength label in {strong, weak} for each assigned domain, together with the set of endorsed subskills conditional on an assigned parent domain. A strong-evidence label is emitted when the paper explicitly names or operationalizes the capability as a central object of study and supports the claim through a dedicated task, benchmark, diagnostic experiment, mechanistic analysis, or primary conclusion. A weak-evidence label is emitted when the capability appears indirectly, e.g., as a supporting construct, task context, downstream behavior, or enabling mechanism. Domains are not treated as mutually exclusive: a paper may receive multiple domain and subskill labels when it substantively operationalizes overlapping constructs. Conceptual overlap among cognitive labels is permitted when supported by the text, but lexical overlap with engineering terminology alone is insufficient to license a cognitive capability label.

为了保持整个语料库中标签空间的一致性，子技能输出仅限于 91 个子技能的规范清单。在聚合期间，大小写和标点符号变体被标准化为规范标签，不匹配的标签被排除在子技能级别分析之外，并且每个子技能必须附加到其指定的父能力。语言语义能力和语言语用能力被保留为不同的域，以便保留标签空间中的细粒度分离，而不是在注释时折叠。To keep the label space consistent across the corpus, subskill outputs are restricted to the canonical inventory of 91 subskills. During aggregation, capitalization and punctuation variants are normalized to canonical labels, unmatched labels are excluded from subskill-level analyses, and every subskill must attach to its designated parent capability. Language-Semantic Competence and Language-Pragmatic Competence are maintained as distinct domains, so that fine-grained separations in the label space are preserved rather than collapsed at annotation time.

4.1.3 注释管道Annotation Pipeline

注释代理被实现为多级管道，它将论文作为输入并生成第 4.1.2 节中定义的两个标签类别。该管道具有三个组件——纸张处理、结构化输出和多模型集成——并根据领域专家参考集进行评估。The annotation agent is implemented as a multi-stage pipeline that takes a paper as input and produces the two label categories defined in Section 4.1.2. The pipeline has three components — paper processing, structured output, and multi-model integration — and is evaluated against a domain-expert reference set.

4.1.4 共识与仲裁Consensus and Arbitration

每篇论文的三个注释首先进行结构验证。能力标签必须出现在正确的分类层中，缺失的标签不能与同一层中的实质性标签同时出现，并且每个子技能必须与其指定的父能力相对应。最终的相关性判断决定了论文是否进入分析语料库。The three annotations for each paper were first subjected to structural validation. Capability labels were required to appear in the correct taxonomy layer, an absent label could not co-occur with a substantive label within the same layer, and each subskill had to correspond to its designated parent capability. The final relevance judgment determined whether the paper entered the analytic corpus.

然后，我们计算了三个模型的非缺席能力标签集的层级一致性，将一致缺席能力视为一致性。当论文的平均层级一致性至少为 0.60 并且没有层包含未解决的平局时，通过三分之二的多数投票来解决该论文。功能标签需要至少两个模型的支持。子技能是从认可相应父能力的模型中汇集出来的，并且证据强度评级中的联系被保守地解决，有利于弱证据。We then computed layer-level agreement across the three models’ sets of non-absent capability labels, treating unanimous absence of capabilities as agreement. A paper was resolved by two-of-three majority voting when its mean layer-level agreement was at least 0.60 and no layer contained an unresolved tie. A capability label required support from at least two models. Subskills were pooled from models that endorsed the corresponding parent capability, and ties in evidence-strength ratings were resolved conservatively in favor of weak evidence.

The analysis provides an initial demonstration of the taxonomy as a framework for research mapping and motivates its broader use in benchmark design, capability diagnosis, and model-development research.

5.1 从任务类别到结构化能力空间From Task Categories to a Structured Capability Space

该分类法将分析单位从评估工件转变为它们想要探测的功能。任务、基准、数据集和部署场景提供了重要的证据，但它们本身并没有提供正在评估的构造的通用表示。一项任务可能需要多种能力，而相同的能力可以在不同的任务和模式中表达。因此，功能级别注释可以在原本不相关的基准系列和应用程序域之间进行比较。The taxonomy shifts the unit of analysis from evaluation artifacts to the capabilities they are intended to probe. Tasks, benchmarks, datasets, and deployment scenarios provide important evidence, but they do not by themselves offer a common representation of the constructs being evaluated. A single task may recruit multiple capabilities, while the same capability may be expressed across different tasks and modalities. Capability-level annotation therefore enables comparison across otherwise disconnected benchmark families and application domains.

分类法以两种方式扩展了这种方法。首先，它的 91 个子技能密码本将广泛的领域覆盖范围与每个领域内检查的具体能力形式区分开来。正如映射结果所示，即使大多数研究集中于单一子技能，某个领域也能得到很好的体现。其次，三层组织对假设的支持关系进行编码，而不是将所有能力视为平面列表中的独立条目。这种结构使研究人员能够将评估所针对的能力与也可能影响绩效的候选人支持能力区分开来。The taxonomy extends this approach in two ways. First, its 91-subskill codebook distinguishes broad domain coverage from the specific forms of capability examined within each domain. As the mapping results show, a domain can appear well represented even when most studies concentrate on a single subskill. Second, the three-layer organization encodes hypothesized support relations rather than treating all capabilities as independent entries in a flat list. This structure allows researchers to distinguish the capability targeted by an evaluation from candidate supporting capabilities that may also affect performance.

因此，分类法提供了一个通用的分析层，用于整合异构评估证据、审计领域和子技能级别的覆盖范围，以及制定有关能力之间关系的可测试假设。The taxonomy thus provides a common analytical layer for integrating heterogeneous evaluation evidence, auditing coverage at both domain and subskill levels, and formulating testable hypotheses about relations among capabilities.

5.2 对评估设计和能力诊断的启示Implications for Evaluation Design and Capability Diagnosis

该分类法支持三个阶段的评估：构造规范、基准组成和性能解释。The taxonomy supports evaluation at three stages: construct specification, benchmark composition, and performance interpretation.

在规范阶段，评估除了任务格式和应用程序设置之外，还应确定其目标的能力域和子技能。类似的任务可能会探测不同的能力，而相同的能力可能会在不同的任务和模式中表达。因此，明确的能力标签使评估目标更容易比较。At the specification stage, evaluations should identify the capability domains and subskills they target, in addition to their task formats and application settings. Similar tasks may probe different capabilities, while the same capability may be expressed across different tasks and modalities. Explicit capability labels therefore make evaluation objectives easier to compare.

在组合阶段，基准套件可以作为能力组合进行审核。开发人员可以评估哪些领域和子技能被覆盖，哪些覆盖范围缺失，以及多个基准测试是否重复测量类似的能力概况。目标不是所有领域的统一覆盖，而是明确且适合评估预期用途的覆盖。At the composition stage, benchmark suites can be audited as capability portfolios. Developers can assess which domains and subskills are covered, where coverage is missing, and whether multiple benchmarks repeatedly measure similar capability profiles. The goal is not uniform coverage across all domains, but coverage that is explicit and appropriate for the evaluation’s intended use.

在判读阶段，多层结构支持更有针对性的诊断。综合能力表现不佳可能反映出目标本身或候选支持能力的局限性。后续探测可以区分这些解释并识别潜在的下层瓶颈。这些分析产生结构化的诊断假设；它们本身并不建立因果依赖性。At the interpretation stage, the multi-layer structure supports more targeted diagnosis. Poor performance on an Integrative capability may reflect limitations in the target itself or in candidate supporting capabilities. Follow-up probes can distinguish between these explanations and identify potential lower-layer bottlenecks. These analyses generate structured diagnostic hypotheses; they do not by themselves establish causal dependencies.

因此，该分类法通过将异构评估任务置于通用能力表示中来补充现有基准。它阐明了每个基准测试的目标、哪些支持功能可能会影响性能以及功能空间的哪些区域仍未测量。这种表示可以改善对单个基准测试结果和聚合模型配置文件的解释。The taxonomy thus complements existing benchmarks by placing heterogeneous evaluation tasks within a common capability representation. It clarifies what each benchmark targets, which supporting capabilities may affect performance, and which regions of the capability space remain unmeasured. This representation can improve the interpretation of both individual benchmark results and aggregate model profiles.

5.3 对培训和转移研究的影响Implications for Training and Transfer Research

该分类法定义了针对能力的培训的假设空间。研究人员可以将综合能力的直接培训与首先针对候选人支持能力的分阶段培训进行比较。这些实验可以测试分层引导测序是否提高了样本效率、稳健性或泛化能力。The taxonomy defines a hypothesis space for capability-targeted training. Researchers can compare direct training on an Integrative capability with staged training that first targets candidate supporting capabilities. These experiments can test whether layer-guided sequencing improves sample efficiency, robustness, or generalization.

91 个子技能密码本还为数据选择和传输分析提供了结构化基础。训练示例可以通过它们所涉及的功能进行注释，从而可以对共享目标功能、候选支持重叠或具有不同功能概况的任务进行比较。这种设计有助于分离特定于任务的适应、能力内泛化和跨能力转移。The 91-subskill codebook also provides a structured basis for data selection and transfer analysis. Training examples can be annotated by the capabilities they engage, enabling comparisons across tasks that share a target capability, overlap in candidate supports, or have distinct capability profiles. This design helps separate task-specific adaptation, within-capability generalization, and cross-capability transfer.

候选人支持关系需要因果检验。受控干预、消融和匹配任务比较可以测试提高较低层能力是否可靠地有利于较高层目标，以及收益是否来自能力转移而不是共享表面特征、基准格式或训练数据。文献映射可以优先考虑待研究的领域和能力组合，但它无法确定训练顺序或转移方向。未来的工作应该测试层引导的干预措施是否可以减少能力反转，改善转移，并将特定目标的失败与较低层的瓶颈区分开来。Candidate support relations require causal testing. Controlled interventions, ablations, and matched-task comparisons can test whether improving a lower-layer capability reliably benefits a higher-layer target and whether gains arise from capability transfer rather than shared surface features, benchmark formats, or training data. The literature mapping can prioritize understudied domains and capability combinations, but it cannot determine training order or transfer direction. Future work should test whether layer-guided interventions reduce ability inversion, improve transfer, and distinguish target-specific failures from lower-layer bottlenecks.

5.4 限制和边界条件Limitations and Boundary Conditions

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，分类法并不是法学硕士架构的蓝图。因此，所提出的领域、层次和能力关系应被视为概念和诊断假设，而不是法学硕士的既定属性。This study has several limitations. First, the taxonomy is not a blueprint for LLM architecture. The proposed domains, layers, and capability relations should therefore be viewed as conceptual and diagnostic hypotheses, not as established properties of LLMs.

其次，文献映射在论文层面进行，依赖于构造注释而不是直接的模型评估。尽管注释管道包括多模型注释、仲裁和专家评审，但概念上相邻的领域仍然存在一些模糊性。此外，由于分类法允许非排他性标签，因此共现模式应解释为研究组织的描述，而不是因果或功能依赖性的证据。Second, the literature mapping operates at the paper level and relies on construct annotation rather than direct model evaluation. Although the annotation pipeline included multi-model annotation, arbitration, and expert review, some ambiguity remains for conceptually adjacent domains. Moreover, because the taxonomy permits non-exclusive labels, co-occurrence patterns should be interpreted as descriptions of research organization rather than evidence of causal or functional dependency.

第三，该语料库包括 2023 年至 2025 年间来自 ACL、AAAI、ICML 和 NeurIPS 的以法学硕士为重点的论文。因此，报告的模式描述了选定地点和时期内的研究关注度，而不是整个法学硕士能力文献。Third, the corpus includes LLM-focused papers from ACL, AAAI, ICML, and NeurIPS between 2023 and 2025. The reported patterns therefore characterize research attention within the selected venues and period rather than the entire LLM capability literature.

5.5 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了一种多层分类法，将 LLM 认知能力分为 14 个领域、91 个子技能和三个层次。将分类法​​应用于最近的法学硕士研究表明，以能力为中心的表示如何揭示覆盖范围和跨能力组织的系统模式，而这些模式仍然被基于任务和基准的分类所掩盖。该分类法不是作为法学硕士认知的新基准或机械模型，而是为能力规范、覆盖分析、诊断假设生成以及未来评估、培训和转移的实证研究提供了一个通用的表征框架。This paper introduced a multi-layer taxonomy that organizes LLM cognitive capabilities into 14 domains, 91 subskills, and three hierarchical layers. Applying the taxonomy to recent LLM research demonstrated how a capability-centered representation can reveal systematic patterns in coverage and cross-capability organization that remain obscured by task- and benchmark-based classifications. Rather than serving as a new benchmark or a mechanistic model of LLM cognition, the taxonomy provides a common representational framework for capability specification, coverage analysis, diagnostic hypothesis generation, and future empirical research on evaluation, training, and transfer.
