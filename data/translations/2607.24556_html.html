1 简介Introduction

协作机器学习已成为一种关键范例，使资源受限的设备和数据提供商能够联合训练深度模型，而无需集中敏感数据。虽然联邦学习（FL）已成为一种广泛采用的隐私保护训练机制，但分割学习（SL）作为替代方案越来越受到关注。在标准 SL 框架中，客户端计算一系列早期神经层，将生成的中间表示 (IR) 传输到服务器，并在反向传播期间接收梯度。这种架构使大多数计算能够卸载到功能强大的服务器，同时将原始数据完全保留在客户端，这使得 SL 对于医疗保健、移动传感和大规模物联网系统等隐私敏感领域具有吸引力。Collaborative machine learning has emerged as a critical paradigm for enabling resource-constrained devices and data providers to jointly train deep models without centralising sensitive data. While Federated Learning (FL) has become a widely adopted privacy-preserving training mechanism, split learning (SL) is increasingly gaining attention as an alternative. In the standard SL frameworks, a client computes a sequence of early neural layers, transmits the resulting intermediate representation (IR) to the server, and receives gradients during backpropagation. This architecture enables most computation to be offloaded to a powerful server while keeping raw data entirely on the client side, making SL attractive for privacy-sensitive domains such as healthcare, mobile sensing, and large-scale IoT systems.

与 FL 不同，FL 在隐私方面进行了大量研究Unlike FL, which has undergone considerable research in privacy aspects[1]，SL仍处于初级阶段。因此，除了通过数据局部性和梯度粉碎实现隐私之外，SL 在设计上还提供了有限的隐私保证。最近的研究表明，在分裂点交换的粉碎激活仍然可能通过重建和推理攻击泄露大量私人信息, SL is still in its initial stages. Thus, apart from privacy via data locality and gradient smashing, SL provides a limited privacy guarantee by design. Recent studies have demonstrated that smashed activations exchanged at the split point may still leak substantial private information through reconstruction and inference attacks[20]。因此，SL 的隐私从根本上取决于. Consequently, the privacy of SL fundamentally depends on哪里where模型已分区。the model is partitioned.

然而，选择分割层仍然是一个悬而未决且尚未充分探索的问题。大多数现有系统应用架构启发法（例如，避免早期层）或基于通信的约束。虽然这些方法可以减少计算或带宽成本，但它们没有提供与架构无关的机制来量化给定分割点的隐私泄漏。此外，使用重建攻击评估候选分割在部署过程中计算成本昂贵且不切实际。因此，当前的 SL 部署缺乏在泄漏发生之前识别隐私敏感层的系统方法。However, selecting the split layer remains an open and underexplored problem. Most existing systems apply architectural heuristics (e.g., avoiding early layers) or communication-based constraints. While these approaches may reduce computation or bandwidth costs, they do not offer an architecture-agnostic mechanism for quantifying privacy leakage at a given split point. Moreover, evaluating candidate splits using reconstruction attacks is computationally expensive and impractical during deployment. As a result, current SL deployments lack a systematic method of identifying privacy-sensitive layers before leakage occurs.

受这些发现的启发，本研究引入了 SL 中隐私泄露的拓扑视角。该研究最初观察到中间神经激活形成几何流形，其结构随着网络深度的变化而演变。早期的层倾向于产生与输入域紧密相关的低维表示，而更深的层则编码越来越抽象和压缩的特征。这一观察结果引发了这样的假设：IR 的几何和拓扑结构表明了其攻击敏感性。Motivated by these findings, this study introduces a topological perspective on privacy leakage in SL. The study initially observes that intermediate neural activations form geometric manifolds whose structure evolves across network depth. Early layers tend to produce low-dimensional representations closely tied to the input domain, while deeper layers encode increasingly abstract and compressed features. This observation motivates the hypothesis that the geometric and topological structure of IR is indicative of its attack susceptibility.

为了量化这种结构，我们利用拓扑数据中的工具 分析（TDA），特别是持久同源性和贝蒂数， 来表征粉碎激活的几何形状。我们专注于 第一个 Betti 数 (Betti-1) 作为表示描述符 复杂度并得出分层隐私指标，称为 BettiLeak 分数，用于仅基于对候选分割层进行排名 关于激活结构。To quantify this structure, we leverage tools from topological data analysis (TDA), specifically persistent homology and Betti numbers, to characterise the geometry of smashed activations. We focus on the first Betti number (Betti-1) as a descriptor of representation complexity and derive a layer-wise privacy indicator, referred to as the BettiLeak score, for ranking candidate split layers based solely on activation structure.

我们的主要见解是，激活表现出更高拓扑复杂性的层往往会保留更多有关原始输入的可重构信息，使它们更容易受到隐私攻击。这种关系使得拓扑复杂性可以作为 SL 中隐私风险的实用指标。Our key insight is that layers whose activations exhibit higher topological complexity tend to retain more reconstructible information about the original input, making them more vulnerable to privacy attacks. This relationship allows topological complexity to serve as a practical indicator of privacy risk in SL.

我们跨多个架构、数据集和威胁模型对我们的方法进行实证评估。结果表明，拓扑复杂性与隐私泄漏密切相关，并且与启发式基线相比，Betti 引导的分割选择显着降低了重建风险，同时保留了模型的实用性。除了其实证贡献之外，这项工作还在协作学习中的拓扑分析和隐私之间建立了一座概念桥梁。 BettiSplit 没有提出新的攻击或依赖启发式方法，而是将持久同源性定位为隐私意识 SL 的诊断和决策工具。We empirically evaluate our approach across multiple architectures, datasets, and threat models. The results demonstrate that topological complexity correlates strongly with privacy leakage and that Betti-guided split selection significantly reduces reconstruction risk compared to heuristic baselines while preserving model utility. Beyond its empirical contributions, this work establishes a conceptual bridge between topological analysis and privacy in collaborative learning. Rather than proposing new attacks or relying on heuristics, BettiSplit positions persistent homology as a diagnostic and decision-making tool for privacy-aware SL.

2.3 分割点选择策略Split Point Selection Strategies

分层选择直接影响分裂和分裂联邦学习系统中的计算分布、通信开销、收敛行为、能耗和隐私泄露。因此，人们投入了大量精力来了解拆分放置如何影响系统性能以及如何在异构和资源受限的环境下对其进行优化。Split-layer selection directly affects computation distribution, communication overhead, convergence behaviour, energy consumption, and privacy leakage in split and split-federated learning systems. Consequently, substantial effort has been put into understanding how split placement affects system performance and how it can be optimised under heterogeneous and resource-constrained environments.

文章Article[3]强调指出，剪切层选择的影响在整个 SFL 中各不相同，从对训练行为的影响可以忽略不计，到非独立同分布数据分布下的强烈敏感性。要显式优化拆分布局，highlights that the impact of cut layer selection varies across SFL, ranging from negligible effects on training behaviour to strong sensitivity under non-IID data distributions. To explicitly optimise split placement,[17]将剪切层选择公式化为边缘辅助 SFL 中的最小-最大延迟优化问题。通过对分割深度、计算成本和通信开销之间的关系进行建模，问题被放松为易于处理的连续形式，并通过交替优化来解决。formulates cut layer selection as a min–max latency optimisation problem in edge-assisted SFL. By modelling the relationship between split depth, computation cost, and communication overhead, the problem is relaxed into a tractable continuous form and solved via alternating optimisation.

BettiSafe 通过选择分裂层来降低隐私风险，这些分裂层的破坏激活表现出降低的拓扑复杂性，而不需要在分裂选择期间执行重建攻击。这种设计时选择限制了对隐私敏感的表示的暴露。BettiSafe mitigates privacy risk by selecting split layers whose smashed activations exhibit reduced topological complexity, without requiring execution of reconstruction attacks during split selection. By discouraging the formation of loop-level features associated with input-specific structure, this mechanism provides an auxiliary defence that complements topology-guided split selection.

6.3 层相关的隐私转换Layer-Dependent Privacy Transitions

除了拓扑复杂性和隐私泄漏之间的总体关系之外，结果还揭示了深层网络中存在不同的隐私过渡区域。如节所示Beyond the overall relationship between topological complexity and privacy leakage, the results reveal the presence of distinct privacy transition regions within deep networks. As shown in Section5.2，分割位置的微小变化可能会导致反转泄漏的不成比例的变化，从而在隐私保护层和隐私敏感层之间产生急剧的过渡。, small changes in split location can lead to disproportionate changes in inversion leakage, producing sharp transitions between privacy-preserving and privacy-sensitive layers.

仅使用架构深度很难解释这些转变。在 FashionMNIST 和 CIFAR-10 中，相邻层尽管仅被少量网络操作分开，但通常表现出明显不同的重建行为。这一观察结果表明，隐私泄漏是由表示结构的变化而不是深度本身决定的。These transitions are difficult to explain using architectural depth alone. Across both FashionMNIST and CIFAR-10, neighbouring layers often exhibit markedly different reconstruction behaviour despite being separated by only a small number of network operations. This observation suggests that privacy leakage is governed by changes in representation structure rather than by depth itself.

拓扑视角为这种行为提供了自然的解释。随着表示通过连续的网络层演变，激活空间的几何形状经历相对稳定的时期，然后是快速重组的阶段。观察到的隐私转变经常与这些结构变化相一致，这表明当表示保留足够的几何组织以支持重建时，就会出现反转漏洞。A topological perspective provides a natural explanation for this behaviour. As representations evolve through successive network layers, the geometry of the activation space undergoes periods of relative stability followed by phases of rapid reorganisation. The observed privacy transitions frequently coincide with these structural changes, indicating that inversion vulnerability emerges when representations preserve sufficient geometric organisation to support reconstruction.

这种解释也解释了基于深度的分割选择启发式的局限性。由于隐私敏感区域不会出现在固定深度，并且随着架构和数据集的不同而变化，因此仅根据网络位置选择分割层可能会忽略关键的表示转换。相比之下，拓扑感知分析直接识别表示结构的变化，使其更好地符合隐私泄露的经验行为。This interpretation also explains the limitations of depth-based split selection heuristics. Since privacy-sensitive regions do not occur at fixed depths and vary across architectures and datasets, selecting split layers solely according to network position may overlook critical representational transitions. In contrast, topology-aware analysis identifies changes in representation structure directly, making it better aligned with the empirical behaviour of privacy leakage.

总而言之，这些发现表明分裂学习中的隐私转变从根本上来说是表征驱动的现象。隐私风险并不是随着深度的增加而逐渐出现，而是在表示空间的底层几何结构经历实质性结构重组的层中变化最为显着。Taken together, these findings suggest that privacy transitions in split learning are fundamentally representation-driven phenomena. Rather than emerging gradually with increasing depth, privacy risk changes most significantly at layers where the underlying geometry of the representation space undergoes substantial structural reorganisation.

7.1 拓扑隐私分析的范围和局限性Scope and Limitations of Topological Privacy Analysis

实证结果表明，拓扑引导的隐私分析的实用性在很大程度上取决于所考虑的攻击模型。虽然拓扑提供了隐私风险的有用表示级视图，但其有效性因泄漏机制和部署设置而异。然而，拓扑的预测效用在不同的攻击模型中并不统一。如节所示The empirical findings indicate that the utility of topology-guided privacy analysis depends strongly on the attack model being considered. While topology provides a useful representation-level view of privacy risk, its effectiveness varies across leakage mechanisms and deployment settings. However, the predictive utility of topology is not uniform across attack models. As shown in Section5.5，基于梯度的攻击（例如 DLG）对逐层拓扑复杂性表现出有限的敏感性。这一观察结果表明，梯度泄漏是由优化动力学和梯度结构控制的，而不是由粉碎激活的几何组织控制的。, gradient-based attacks such as DLG exhibit limited sensitivity to layer-wise topological complexity. This observation suggests that gradient leakage is governed by optimisation dynamics and gradient structure rather than the geometric organisation of smashed activations.

结果进一步表明隐私行为仍然依赖于数据集。虽然 FashionMNIST 和 CIFAR-10 之间的网络深度和泄漏之间的关系存在很大差异，但拓扑引导指标在这两种设置中仍然提供了丰富的信息。因此，Betti 复杂性似乎捕获了泛化到特定数据集之外的表示级属性，同时对底层隐私制度保持敏感。The results further demonstrate that privacy behaviour remains dataset-dependent. While the relationship between network depth and leakage differs substantially between FashionMNIST and CIFAR-10, topology-guided indicators remain informative across both settings. Consequently, Betti complexity appears to capture representation-level properties that generalise beyond specific datasets while remaining sensitive to the underlying privacy regime.

这些观察结果阐明了拓扑引导的隐私分析的范围。基于 Betti 的描述符不是充当所有攻击类别的通用预测器，而是最有效地识别与中间表示的几何结构相关的隐私风险。These observations clarify the scope of topology-guided privacy analysis. Rather than serving as a universal predictor of all attack classes, Betti-based descriptors are most effective for identifying privacy risks associated with the geometric structure of intermediate representations.

这些发现不仅将拓扑定位为隐私分析的诊断信号，而且还作为隐私感知表示学习的实用设计参数。更广泛地说，他们强调了拓扑引导目标作为特定攻击防御的替代方案的潜力，而特定攻击防御通常需要对学习管道进行大量修改。These findings position topology not only as a diagnostic signal for privacy analysis but also as a practical design parameter for privacy-aware representation learning. 7.4 局限性和未来范围Limitations and Future Scope

虽然所提出的拓扑引导框架表现出很强的实证有效性，但一些局限性限制了当前研究的范围并激励了未来的研究。While the proposed topology-guided framework demonstrates strong empirical effectiveness, several limitations constrain the scope of the present study and motivate future research.

首先，分析主要集中于特征空间重建攻击，其拓扑复杂性表现出很强的预测能力。基于梯度的攻击（例如 DLG）与表示拓扑的一致性明显较弱，这表明拓扑对于与表示几何相关的隐私威胁（而不是优化动态）提供的信息最多。将框架扩展到其他攻击系列仍然是未来工作的重要方向。First, the analysis focuses primarily on feature-space reconstruction attacks, for which topological complexity exhibits strong predictive power. Gradient-based attacks such as DLG show substantially weaker alignment with representation topology, indicating that topology is most informative for privacy threats linked to representation geometry rather than optimisation dynamics. Extending the framework to additional attack families remains an important direction for future work.

其次，尽管考虑了多种架构和数据集，但实验仅限于基于图像的学习任务。更高分辨率图像、医学成像、图形数据、自然语言处理和表格学习的推广仍有待研究。Second, although multiple architectures and datasets are considered, the experiments are restricted to image-based learning tasks. Generalisation to higher-resolution imagery, medical imaging, graph data, natural language processing, and tabular learning remains to be investigated.

第三，当前的框架完全依赖一维 Betti 数来提高效率和稳定性。虽然足以捕获观察到的隐私转变，但更丰富的描述符（例如持久性熵、持久性景观或高阶同源特征）可以提供额外的洞察力。Third, the current framework relies exclusively on one-dimensional Betti numbers for efficiency and stability. While sufficient to capture the observed privacy transitions, richer descriptors such as persistence entropy, persistence landscapes, or higher-order homological features may provide additional insight.

从计算的角度来看，持久同源性是在适度的校准批次上计算的，以保持易处理性。开发可扩展的近似、流式公式或在线拓扑估计器将提高大规模实时协作学习环境的适用性。From a computational perspective, persistent homology is computed on moderate calibration batches to maintain tractability. Developing scalable approximations, streaming formulations, or online topological estimators would improve applicability to large-scale and real-time collaborative learning environments.

最后，所提出的框架本质上是有意实证和诊断的。基于 Betti 的分析提供了与攻击无关的隐私风险指标，但并不构成正式的隐私保证。在表示拓扑、可重构性和信息泄漏之间建立理论联系仍然是一个开放且有前途的研究方向。Finally, the proposed framework is intentionally empirical and diagnostic in nature. Betti-based analysis provides an attack-agnostic indicator of privacy risk, but does not constitute a formal privacy guarantee. Establishing theoretical links between representation topology, reconstructability, and information leakage remains an open and promising research direction.

总的来说，这些限制并没有削弱这项工作的核心贡献。相反，他们强调了将拓扑引导的隐私分析扩展到更全面、可扩展且有理论基础的分裂学习系统的机会。Overall, these limitations do not diminish the core contributions of this work.
