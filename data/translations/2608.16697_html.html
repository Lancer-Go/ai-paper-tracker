摘要Abstract

视觉-语言-动作模型 (VLA) 将视觉感知、语言指令和动作生成集成到跨异构架构的端到端策略中。然而，使 VLA 能够在没有外部监督的情况下自我评估其动作生成的可靠性仍然是一个重大挑战。现有方法要么依赖专家注释，要么仅根据输出统计数据估计不确定性，很大程度上忽略了内部信号。在这项工作中，我们观察到内部视觉模态熵在跨异构 VLA 的成功任务和失败任务之间表现出一致的区别。尽管 VLA 的架构在动作生成方面有所不同，但我们表明它们共享在视觉感知、语言指令和状态输入下演化的共同潜在动作生成抽象，我们将其表述为条件生成马尔可夫链。基于这个公式，我们建议Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) integrate visual perception, language instruction, and action generation into end-to-end policies across heterogeneous architectures. In this work, we observe that internal visual modality entropy exhibits consistent distinctions between successful and failed tasks across heterogeneous VLAs. 1 简介Introduction

视觉-语言-动作模型 (VLA)Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs)[37, 23, 22]通过使预训练的视觉语言模型适应具体控制，将视觉观察和语言指令转化为可执行的机器人动作，如图所示translate visual observations and language instructions into executable robot actions by adapting pretrained Vision-Language Models to embodied control as illustrated in Figure1。 然而，它们的行动生成机制是不同的。 在这项工作中，我们区分了两个广泛的动作生成家族：潜在读出 VLA 和潜在细化 VLA。 前者演化潜在动作表示，并通过读出头将最终潜在状态映射到可执行动作； OpenVLA 使用自回归离散读数. Their action-generation mechanisms, however, are heterogeneous. In this work, we distinguish two broad action-generation families: Latent-Readout VLAs and Latent-Refinement VLAs. The former evolves latent action representations and maps the final latent state to executable actions through a readout head; OpenVLA uses autoregressive discrete readout[23]，而OpenVLA-OFT使用连续读出, while OpenVLA-OFT uses continuous readout[22]。 后者维持不断发展的行动或行动轨迹表示，并在学习的流动或扩散式动态下迭代地完善它，如 PI 家族策略. The latter maintains an evolving action or action-trajectory representation and refines it iteratively under learned flow- or diffusion-style dynamics, as in PI-family policies[35]。 随着 VLA 泛化到机器人任务中. As VLAs generalize across robotic tasks[30]，他们还需要对其生成的操作何时可能失败进行可靠的估计, they also require reliable estimates of when their generated actions are likely to fail[43].

可靠性工作的第一线依靠The first line of reliability work relies on外部监督External Supervision。 早期研究引入了产出水平的不确定性和质量指标，通常通过专家注释进行校准. Early studies introduced output-level uncertainty and quality metrics, often calibrated by expert annotations[40]。 后来的工作超越了被动评估，使用辅助模型来检测、解释、验证和从故障中恢复. Later work moved beyond passive evaluation by using auxiliary models to detect, explain, verify, and recover from failures[12, 36, 29, 26, 10, 31, 7]。 这些方法可以提高VLA的可靠性，但它们的评估信号是在策略之外提供的。 这可能会增加部署成本，引入对评估器或注释转移的敏感性，并限制对产生失败操作的内部状态转换的访问. These methods can improve VLA reliability, but their evaluation signal is supplied outside the policy. This can increase deployment cost, introduce sensitivity to evaluator or annotation shift, and give limited access to the internal state transitions that produced a failed action[6].

二线研究A second line studies内部自我评价Internal Self-evaluation，其中可靠性是根据 VLA 自身的信号推断出来的。 现有的内部方法可以分为黑盒方法和白盒方法。 黑盒方法依靠输出统计数据（例如多样本分歧或自我不确定性分数）来选择无需外部监督的操作, where reliability is inferred from the VLA’s own signals. Existing internal methods can be grouped into black-box and white-box approaches. Black-box methods rely on output statistics, such as multi-sample disagreement or self-uncertainty scores, to select actions without external supervision[2, 21, 42]。 然而，他们可能过于自信并提供有限的解释性. However, they can be over-confident and provide limited interpretability[2]。 白盒自我评估在大型语言模型 (LLM) 中得到了更系统的研究，其中内部信号（如 softmax 置信度、温度缩放或激活模式）用于在没有外部监督的情况下估计发电可靠性. White-box self-evaluation has been studied more systematically in Large Language Models (LLMs), where internal signals such as softmax confidence, temperature scaling, or activation patterns are used to estimate generation reliability without external supervision[2, 38, 17, 32]。 对于 VLA，相关证据仍然有限且支离破碎。 现有研究表明，内部信号可以反映路径偏差、执行范围、路径专业化或潜在行动瓶颈. For VLAs, related evidence remains limited and fragmented. Existing studies show that internal signals can reflect path deviation, execution horizon, pathway specialization, or latent action bottlenecks[18, 41, 15, 16, 5, 27]。 仍然缺少的是一种将此类内部信号转换为自我评估指标的统一方法，这些指标在异构 VLA 操作生成架构中仍然有意义。 这种差距引发了以下问题：. What is still missing is a unified way to convert such internal signals into self-evaluation metrics that remain meaningful across heterogeneous VLA action-generation architectures. This gap motivates the following question:

VLA内部信号能否在异构动作生成机制的统一考虑下，在不依赖外部监督的情况下，转化为动作生成可靠性的自评估指标？Can VLA internal signals be transformed into self-evaluation metrics for action-generation reliability under a unified account of heterogeneous action-generation mechanisms, without relying on external supervision?

视觉-语言-行动模型。Vision-Language-Action Models.

VLA 将视觉观察和语言指令转化为输出动作，以大规模机器人学习和多模态建模的进展为基础。 RT-1 等早期的通才策略展示了来自现实世界机器人轨迹的可扩展的基于变压器的控制，而 VIMA 研究了以多模式任务规范为条件的机器人操作VLAs translate visual observations and language instructions into output actions, building on progress in large-scale robot learning and multimodal modeling. Early generalist policies such as RT-1 demonstrate scalable transformer-based control from real-world robot trajectories, while VIMA studies robot manipulation conditioned on multimodal task specifications[4, 19]。 PaLM-E 将视觉观察纳入预训练的大型语言模型 (LLM)，而 RT-2 通过动作标记预测将网络规模的视觉语言预训练与动作生成连接起来. PaLM-E incorporates visual observations into a pretrained Large Language Model (LLM), and RT-2 connects web-scale vision-language pretraining with action generation through action-token prediction[11, 44]。大规模数据集和开放策略进一步支持跨任务和机器人实施例的泛化，包括 Open X-Embodiment、RoboCat 和 Octo. Large-scale datasets and open policies further support generalization across tasks and robot embodiments, including Open X-Embodiment, RoboCat, and Octo[9, 3, 14]。除了这些政策规模的发展之外，连续的生成策略（例如扩散策略）和高效的动作表示（例如 FAST）扩展了动作生成的可用设计. Beyond these policy-scale developments, continuous generative policies such as Diffusion Policy and efficient action representations such as FAST expand the available designs for action generation[8, 34]。这些研究共同建立了一系列广泛的政策，这些政策共享视觉观察和语言指令作为条件，但在如何产生行动方面有所不同。. Together, these studies establish a broad family of policies that share visual observations and language instructions as conditions, but differ in how actions are generated.

附录HAppendix H数据集源注意事项Dataset-Source Considerations

一个潜在的担忧是，可靠性评分可能会将标准 LIBERO 情节与 LIBERO-PRO 情节分开，而不是估计情节级别的动作可靠性。 LIBERO-Reflect 旨在减少构建和评估级别的这种一阶源捷径。首先，两个名义池通过套件组织、任务计数、初始化数量、模拟器、策略接口和推出协议进行匹配。因此，评估代码、控制范围、观察记录和度量计算中的差异不能作为评分函数的线索。其次，基准测试不会继承数据集成员身份的标签。名义来源仅用于组建混合难度评估池；所有报告的指标都是使用每次部署的已实现模拟器成功标志来计算的。因此，标准 LIBERO 端的失败和 LIBERO-PRO 端的成功将被保留并根据其实际结果进行评估。A potential concern is that a reliability score may separate standard LIBERO episodes from LIBERO-PRO episodes rather than estimate episode-level action reliability. LIBERO-Reflect is designed to reduce this first-order source shortcut at both the construction and evaluation levels. First, the two nominal pools are matched by suite organization, task count, number of initializations, simulator, policy interface, and rollout protocol. Thus, differences in evaluation code, control horizon, observation logging, and metric computation are not available as cues to the scoring function. Second, the benchmark does not inherit labels from dataset membership. The nominal source is used only to assemble a mixed-difficulty evaluation pool; all reported metrics are computed using the realized simulator success flag of each rollout. As a result, failures from the standard LIBERO side and successes from the LIBERO-PRO side are retained and evaluated according to their actual outcomes.

这种区别对于解释 MAE 很重要。源级快捷方式将为所有标准 LIBERO 剧集分配几乎统一的可靠性，并为所有 LIBERO-PRO 剧集分配统一的低可靠性。每当名义源和实现的结果不一致时，这样的规则就会受到惩罚，并且它不会捕获具有相同数据集来源但不同执行结果的事件之间的源内变化。相比之下，MAE 是根据在当前条件环境下生成动作的相同前向传递过程中策略的内部注意力动态来计算的。因此，分数无法访问分割标识符或名义来源标签；它只能利用潜在动作状态在当前情节条件下如何路由信息。This distinction is important for interpreting MAE. A source-level shortcut would assign nearly uniform reliability to all standard LIBERO episodes and uniformly low reliability to all LIBERO-PRO episodes. Such a rule is penalized whenever nominal source and realized outcome disagree, and it does not capture within-source variation among episodes with the same dataset origin but different execution outcomes. In contrast, MAE is computed from the policy’s internal attention dynamics during the same forward passes that generate actions under the current conditioning context. The score therefore has no access to split identifiers or nominal source labels; it can only exploit how the latent action state routes information under the current episode condition.

我们使用 LIBERO-PRO 作为具有挑战性的部署的受控源，以增加可靠性排名所需的故障密度，而实际模拟器的成功仍然是评估标签。由此产生的基准是匹配的混合难度池下的情节级可靠性测试。两个名义池的诊断成功率报告了剩余的源级别差异，并且所有指标都被解释为对已实现执行的可靠性排名，而不是源不变分类。We use LIBERO-PRO as a controlled source of challenging rollouts to increase the density of failures needed for reliability ranking, while actual simulator success remains the evaluation label. The resulting benchmark is an episode-level reliability test under a matched mixed-difficulty pool. Diagnostic success rates for both nominal pools report the remaining source-level differences, and all metrics are interpreted as reliability ranking over realized executions rather than as source-invariant classification.
