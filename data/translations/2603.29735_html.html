摘要Abstract

人工系统中智能的进化为识别通用计算原理提供了独特的机会。在这里，我们展示了大型语言模型自发地发展出协同核心，其中信息集成超过了与人脑非常相似的各个部分。使用跨多个架构的集成信息分解，我们发现中间层表现出协同处理，而早期层和晚期层依赖于冗余。这种组织是动态的，随着任务难度的增加而出现物理阶段的转变。至关重要的是，消除协同成分会导致灾难性的性能损失，这证实了它们作为抽象推理的物理实体以及连接人工智能和生物智能的角色。The evolution of intelligence in artificial systems provides a unique opportunity to identify universal computational principles. Here we show that large language models spontaneously develop synergistic cores where information integration exceeds individual parts remarkably similar to the human brain. Using Integrated Information Decomposition across multiple architectures we find that middle layers exhibit synergistic processing while early and late layers rely on redundancy. This organization is dynamic and emerges as a physical phase transition as task difficulty increases. Crucially ablating synergistic components causes catastrophic performance loss confirming their role as the physical entity of abstract reasoning and bridging artificial and biological intelligence.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）近年来发展迅速Large Language Models (LLMs) have developed rapidly in recent years(Comanici et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2025a)，尤其是他们非凡的零样本泛化和抽象推理能力, especially in their extraordinary ability for zero shot generalization and abstract reasoning(Brown et al., 2020)。然而，一个主要的争论仍然存在：这种强大的抽象能力从何而来？. However, a major debate remains: where does this powerful abstract capability come from(Schaeffer et al., 2023)？这种智能产生的过程是否与人类成长过程中产生智慧的过程类似？了解这样的智能系统如何整合来自多个维度的信息以产生超越单纯记忆的抽象推理对于人工智能和神经科学的进步至关重要。? Is the process of this intelligence emerging similar to how wisdom is created during human growth? Understanding how such an intelligent system integrates information from multiple dimensions to produce abstract reasoning that goes beyond mere memory is vital for the progress of both artificial intelligence and neuroscience.

大量研究探索了LLM与人脑的结构相似性A large number of studies have explored the structural similarities between LLMs and the human brain(Li et al., 2025a, b)。对大型模型机制的研究甚至帮助神经科学加深了对人类的理解. Investigations into the mechanisms of large models have even helped neuroscience deepen the understanding of humans(Li et al., 2025b; Yang et al., 2025b; Tian et al., 2026)，其他研究表明当前模型包含显着的冗余, other research suggests that current models contain significant redundancy(Gao et al., 2025)。这些研究挑战了主流深度学习理论，认为简单地增加模型参数规模并不能创造更复杂的高级特征组合或多步推理能力. These studies challenge mainstream deep learning theories, arguing that simply increasing model parameter scale does not create more complex abilities for high level feature combination or multi step reasoning(Csordás et al., 2025)。此外，对当今人工智能中最大问题（例如终身学习和遗忘问题）的研究表明，法学硕士中智能的出现类似于顺行性遗忘症. Furthermore, work on the biggest problems in AI today, such as lifelong learning and the issue of forgetting, suggests that the emergence of intelligence in LLMs resembles anterograde amnesia(Scoville and Milner, 1957)。这是一种人类神经系统疾病，患病后大脑无法再形成新的长期记忆，而现有的记忆和信息处理技能不受影响。这些相互矛盾的发现引出了一个问题：法学硕士的抽象能力和人类智力的产生方式之间是否存在共同点？这种抽象能力以什么形式存在并在模型中发挥作用？. This is a human neurological condition where the brain can no longer form new long term memories after an illness, while existing memories and information processing skills remain unaffected. These conflicting findings lead to a question: is there a common ground between the abstract abilities of LLMs and the way human intelligence is produced? In what form does this abstract capability exist and function inside the model?

在这项工作中，受到香农信息论的启发In this work, inspired by Shannon information theory(Shannon, 1948)，我们将大型模型视为多节点信息处理系统。通过使用信息重组分析, we view large models as a multi node information processing system. By using information reorganization analysis(Mediano et al., 2025)，我们发现了法学硕士内部存在跨不同模型系列和规模的信息处理逻辑，类似于人脑。在模型的前层和后层中，每个变换层都用于简单的信息接收和无损传输。这些层显示出明显的神经可塑性, we discovered an information processing logic inside LLMs that exists across different model series and scales, similar to the human brain. In the early and late layers of the model, each transformer layer functions for simple information reception and lossless transmission. These layers show clear neural plasticity(Johnston, 2009)，这意味着这些层中某些神经元的死亡不会影响模型的性能。相比之下，中间层的神经元表现出很强的信息合成能力。他们可以计算并组合传递给他们的主要特征，以获得以前不存在的新的高级重组特征。这是模型抽象能力的物理实体。该区域的神经元表现出高度的独特性，部分神经元的丢失会导致模型性能崩溃。这一发现表明法学硕士和人脑的抽象能力相似。某种程度上，证明了这种情报的天然正确性。它不是像记忆阅读器那样处理信息的非生命系统，而是像大脑一样的智能。, meaning that the death of some neurons in these layers does not affect the performance of the model. In contrast, neurons in the middle layers show a strong ability for information composition. They can calculate and combine the primary features passed to them to obtain new high level reorganized features that did not exist before. This is the physical entity of the abstract capability of the model. Neurons in this area show a high level of uniqueness, and the loss of some neurons will cause the model performance to collapse. This finding suggests a similarity in abstract capability between LLMs and the human brain. To some extent, it proves the natural correctness of this intelligence. It is not a non living system for processing information like a memory reader, but a brain like intelligence.

我们专注于最先进的开源模型We focus on the most advanced open source models(Yang et al., 2025a; Liu et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024; Team et al., 2025)，特别是 Qwen3 和 Gemma3 系列。根据之前的研究, specifically the Qwen3 and Gemma3 series. Based on previous research(Yang et al., 2025b; Csordás et al., 2025)，我们主要看涉及数学计算和抽象推理的任务。这些模型在面对不同类型的任务时表现出相同的信息处理逻辑。我们的贡献包括以下部分：, we mainly look at tasks involving mathematical calculation and abstract reasoning. The models show the same information processing logic when facing different types of tasks. Our contributions consist of the following parts:

  • •

    我们使用信息重组分析来定位模型内部两个不同的模块：内存存储和传输层以及组合推理和抽象层。它们分别位于早期、晚期和中期，两者之间有明显的界限。We used information reorganization analysis to locate two distinct modules inside the model: the memory storage and transmission layers and the layers for combined reasoning and abstraction. These are located in the early or late stages and the middle stages respectively, with a clear boundary between the two.

  • •

    我们分析了内部残余流并测量了各层之间的变化趋势。从能量的角度来看，我们认为记忆层通过增强输入特征来增强记忆，而抽象层则通过擦除和写入从前一层传递的特征来组合特征以创建新的高级抽象含义。We analyzed the internal residual flow and measured the trends of change between layers. From an energy perspective, we argue that memory layers strengthen memory by enhancing input features, while abstraction layers combine features to create new high level abstract meanings by erasing and writing over the features passed from the former layers.

  • •

    我们在网络层面进行了进一步分析，发现其特征与人脑中的网络高度相似。存储层包含多个节点用于信息存储，以确保无损传输。抽象层负责将信息收集在一起，以确保可以组合所有主要功能。消融实验也证明这两个部分的重要性并不相同。We performed further analysis at the network level and found that the characteristics are highly similar to the networks in the human brain. The memory layers contain multiple nodes for information storage to ensure lossless transmission. The abstraction layers are responsible for gathering information together to ensure all primary features can be combined. Experiments with ablation also prove that the importance of these two parts is not the same.

  • •

    最后，我们测试了这种处理逻辑在各种任务和难度级别上的通用性。我们证明它不是专门用于某些任务或模型的属性。有趣的是，我们还发现，对于低难度的抽象推理任务，模型不会自发地进化出专门的内存和抽象层以节省能量。随着难度的增加，模型主动输入能量导致熵减少，导致两个模块之间的边界出现。这种节省能量的行为与人脑在处理不同任务时不同区域的葡萄糖消耗高度相似。Finally, we tested the generalization of this processing logic across various tasks and difficulty levels. We proved that it is not a property specialized for certain tasks or models. Interestingly, we also found that for low difficulty abstract reasoning tasks, the model does not spontaneously evolve specialized memory and abstraction layers in order to save energy. As the difficulty increases, the model actively inputs energy to cause entropy reduction, leading to the appearance of the boundary between the two modules. This behavior for saving energy is highly similar to the glucose consumption in different regions of the human brain when handling different tasks.

总之，这些发现支持了当前的法学硕士与人脑共享相似的信息处理方法的观点。他们可以自发地调整其内部结构，以执行最具成本效益的任务操作。创建复杂特征的能力来自于抽象层对主要特征的组合。同时，存储层中许多参数的冗余对于确保泛化性和鲁棒性至关重要。这些见解将进一步推动人工智能和神经科学的发展。In summary, these findings support the idea that current LLMs share similar information processing methods with the human brain. They can spontaneously adjust their internal structures to perform the most cost effective operations for a task. The ability to create complex features comes from the combination of primary features by the abstraction layer. At the same time, the redundancy of many parameters in the memory layer is essential to ensure generalization and robustness. These insights will further advance the development of artificial intelligence and neuroscience.

2 相关工作Related Work

对法学硕士内部特征处理机制和泛化能力的研究有着丰富的历史。这些研究发现，神经元自发地组织成专门的单元，这些单元一起工作来执行任务Research on the internal feature processing mechanisms and generalization capabilities of LLMs has a rich history. These studies have found that neurons spontaneously organize into specialized units that work together to execute tasks(Liu et al., 2022; Webb et al., 2023, 2024; Deletang et al., 2024)。一个典型的例子是早期对 Transformer 架构的涌现能力的研究. A classic example is the early research on the emergent abilities of the transformer architecture(Yang et al., 2025b)。模块化算术任务中的 grokking 现象通常被认为是研究大型模型中涌现的果蝇实验. The phenomenon of grokking in modular arithmetic tasks is often considered the Drosophila experiment for studying emergence in large models(Wang et al., 2024)。随后的工作发现，在这些任务期间，模型内会形成用于记忆和抽象的不同逻辑电路. Subsequent work discovered that distinct logic circuits for memory and abstraction develop within the model during these tasks(Varma et al., 2024; Arora et al., 2026)。这表明，无论模型规模如何，突发现象都对应着内部结构重组和专业化。. This indicates that, regardless of model scale, the phenomena of emergency corresponds to internal structural reorganization and specialization.

最近对大型模型的深度和宽度的研究表明，当前的架构包含显着的冗余Recent studies on the depth and width of large models suggest that current architectures contain significant redundancy(Csordás et al., 2025; Behrouz et al., 2025)。较深层的模型通常被视为浅层模型的扩展，不一定提供新的推理能力，导致参数效率较低。相反，一些研究在训练过程中使用大量正则化来去除大多数神经元。在生成的稀疏模型中，发现了用于语法、编程和数学任务的特定电路. Deeper models are often seen as extensions of shallow ones and do not necessarily provide new reasoning capabilities, leading to low parameter efficiency. Conversely, some research has used heavy regularization to remove most neurons during training. In the resulting sparse models, specific circuits for syntax, programming, and mathematical tasks were discovered(Gao et al., 2025)。这也表明参数冗余是保证LLM鲁棒性和泛化性能的关键因素。. This also suggests that parameter redundancy is a key factor in ensuring the robustness and generalization performance of LLMs.

其他工作从神经科学中寻求灵感，特别是不同大脑区域的特性。研究人员在法学硕士中观察到类似于脑电波跨区域通信的现象Other work has sought inspiration from neuroscience, specifically the properties of different brain regions. Researchers have observed phenomena in LLMs similar to the cross region communication of brain waves(Behrouz et al., 2025)。与灵长类脑干的比较揭示了图式神经关联，其中单个神经元可以包含多个语义. Comparisons with the primate brainstem revealed schema neural associations where a single neuron can contain multiple semantics(Marks et al., 2025; Lieberum et al., 2024; Rajamanoharan et al., 2024; Minegishi et al., 2025)。这些神经元自发地切换状态以实现特定任务的功能，例如抽象、归纳和检索。这种神经可重用性确保了效率和鲁棒性. These neurons spontaneously switch states for task specific functions like abstraction, induction, and retrieval. Such neural reusability ensures efficiency and robustness(Yang et al., 2025b)。这些研究证明法学硕士内的计算单元分布并不均匀，但表现出明显的差异。然而，很少有研究调查抽象能力在模型中所处的具体几何位置。. These studies prove that computational units within LLMs are not uniformly distributed but show clear differentiation. However, very few studies have investigated the specific geometric location where abstract capability resides within the model.

多项研究证明了层干预的有效性，例如层跳跃、折叠和合并Multiple studies have demonstrated the effectiveness of layer interventions, such as layer skipping, folding, and merging(Gromov et al., 2025; Sun et al., 2025; Lad et al., 2024)。这些发现表明，模型对于大多数任务的此类干预都是稳健的；即使删除整个层通常对性能的影响也很小。仅在需要多步计算的数学任务中观察到灵敏度，例如 GSM8K 和 MATH. These findings show that models are robust to such interventions across most tasks; even removing an entire layer often has little impact on performance. Sensitivity is only observed in mathematical tasks that require multi step calculations, such as GSM8K and MATH(Cobbe et al., 2021; Hendrycks et al., 2021)。这种敏感性归因于这些任务需要结合多个子问题的结果。这解释了为什么我们的研究主要集中在数学和推理任务上。. This sensitivity is attributed to the need for these tasks to combine the results of multiple sub problems. This explains why our research primarily focuses on mathematical and reasoning tasks.
