摘要Abstract

However, current phenome-wide association studies rely on pre-defined, single-variable phenotypes or expert-crafted features, which limits their ability to capture clinically meaningful non-linear effects and cross-phenotype interactions. 1 简介Introduction

确定心脏成像表型与临床疾病之间的牢固关联对于了解疾病机制和建立可靠的风险分层模型至关重要Identifying robust associations between cardiac imaging phenotypes and clinical diseases is central to understanding disease mechanisms and building reliable risk stratification models[16]。现代心血管成像队列，例如大规模 CMR 和超声心动图生物库，提供了丰富的定量表型，描述心室大小和功能、心室质量和应变、主动脉几何形状. Modern cardiovascular imaging cohorts, such as large-scale CMR and echocardiography biobanks, provide rich quantitative phenotypes that describe chamber size and function, ventricular mass and strain, aortic geometry[14]。这些成像衍生的特征作为将遗传和环境因素与临床结果联系起来的中间表型，并且越来越多地用于机制研究、生物标志物发现和治疗反应评估. These imaging-derived traits serve as intermediate phenotypes linking genetic and environmental factors to clinical outcomes, and are increasingly used in mechanistic studies, biomarker discovery, and treatment response assessment[7].

具有标准化 CMR 和超声心动图协议的基于人群的队列的出现，加上自动图像分析流程，产生了前所未有的大规模成像数据集The emergence of population-based cohorts with standardized CMR and echocardiography protocols, coupled with automated image analysis pipelines, has produced large imaging datasets at unprecedented scale[4]。这使得心脏成像现象能够系统地映射到不同的临床状况和风险因素. While this enables systematic mapping of the cardiac imaging phenome to diverse clinical conditions and risk factors[15]，它还带来了巨大的分析挑战。在实践中，成像衍生性状的数量庞大、冗余和复杂的依赖结构造成了巨大的分析瓶颈：决定测试哪些表型组合、如何捕获成像性状和临床输出之间潜在的非线性关系以及如何确保所得关联是稳健的且临床上可解释的，这些都是非常重要的。, it also introduces substantial analytic challenges. In practice, the sheer number, redundancy, and complex dependency structure of imaging-derived traits create a substantial analytic bottleneck: it is non-trivial to decide which combinations of phenotypes to test, how to capture potentially non-linear relationships between imaging traits and clinical outputs, and how to ensure that resulting associations are robust and clinically interpretable[12].

之前的工作主要采用 PheWAS 风格的管道Previous work has largely adopted PheWAS-style pipelines[5]，其中领域专家预先定义了一组固定的单变量成像表型，并在仔细指定的统计模型下评估它们与一系列疾病的关联, in which domain experts predefine a fixed set of single-variable imaging phenotypes and evaluate their associations with a range of diseases under carefully specified statistical models[3, 2]或机器学习算法or machine learning algorithms[8, 13]。后续工作根据先前的临床知识构建综合指数，例如，通过组合相关的体积测量或将区域特征总结为全局分数. Subsequent work constructs composite indices based on prior clinical knowledge, for example, by combining related volumetric measures or summarizing regional features into global scores[18, 9]。虽然这些方法是透明的并且有临床依据，但如果没有大量的手动工作，它们就无法很好地扩展，并且受到专家定义的特征和线性建模假设的限制。. While these approaches are transparent and clinically grounded, they do not scale well without substantial manual effort, and are constrained by expert-defined features and linear modeling assumptions.

最近的研究探索了自动化特征构建和数据驱动的发现管道，包括符号组合和代理辅助数据科学系统More recent studies have explored automated feature construction and data-driven discovery pipelines, including symbolic composition and agent-assisted data science systems[1, 17, 6]。尽管此类方法扩大了候选特征空间，但它们通常是为一般预测建模而开发的，而不是为具有明确的混杂因素控制和数值保障的大规模表型发现而开发。更大的灵活性可以增强表现力，但在应用于相关成像特征时也可能会带来不稳定。. Although such methods broaden the candidate feature space, they are typically developed for general predictive modelling rather than large-scale phenotype discovery with explicit confounder control and numerical safeguards. Greater flexibility can enhance expressiveness, but it may also introduce instability when applied to correlated imaging traits.

专家驱动和自动化方法在高维、人口规模成像数据方面都面临两个主要限制。首先，预定义的特征集限制了捕获具有临床意义的非线性效应和跨表型相互作用的能力。其次，通过自适应组合扩展特征空间会增加多重测试负担，并增加在队列异质性和残余混杂下出现不稳定结果的脆弱性。Both expert-driven and automated approaches face two major limitations in high-dimensional, population-scale imaging data. First, predefined feature sets restrict the ability to capture clinically meaningful non-linear effects and interactions across phenotypes. Second, expanding the feature space through adaptive composition inflates the multiple-testing burden and increases vulnerability to unstable findings under cohort heterogeneity and residual confounding.

The main contributions are: (i)代理迭代表型发现。Agentic iterative phenotype discovery.我们将表型发现制定为由专门代理协调的迭代分析-建议-验证循环，从而能够在受限的操作符集中自动构建复合表型。 (二)We formulate phenotype discovery as an iterative analyse-propose-verify loop coordinated by specialised agents, enabling automatic construction of composite phenotypes within a constrained operator set. (ii)统计信息的组成和验证。Statistics-informed composition and validation.我们首先总结数据中的关键统计结构（例如分布、冗余和相关模式），然后使用交叉验证的指标进行原则性的筛选和验证。仅当改进在折叠和模型之间一致并得到统计测试的支持时才会保留候选复合材料，从而减轻选择引起的过度拟合。 (三)We first summarizes key statistical structure in the data (e.g., distributions, redundancy, and correlation patterns), and then performs principled screening and verification using cross-validated metrics. (iii)可解释和可重现的输出。Interpretable and reproducible outputs.我们将每个发现的复合表型表示为紧凑的封闭式公式，具有清晰的构建轨迹和交叉验证的证据摘要，从而实现透明的审计并促进可重复的下游关联分析和外部复制。We represent each discovered composite phenotype as a compact, closed-form formula with a clear construction trace and cross-validated evidence summaries, enabling transparent auditing and facilitating reproducible downstream association analyses and external replication.

4 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一个代理的、迭代的表型组成框架，该框架发现了临床上可解释的复合心脏成像表型，并增强了超出专家定义的单一特征的表型与疾病关联信号。通过将分析师、提议者和验证者代理与基于统计的稳健性检查相结合，我们的方法产生了紧凑的、可审计的表型库，这些表型库与潜在的病理生理学更好地保持一致。未来的工作将将该框架扩展到多模式数据，以评估复合表型如何进一步支持临床决策和试验设计。We have presented an agentic, iterative phenotype‐composition framework that discovers clinically interpretable composite cardiac imaging phenotypes and strengthens phenotype–disease association signals beyond expert-defined single traits. By combining analyst, proposer, and verifier agents with statistically grounded robustness checks, our approach yields compact, auditable phenotype libraries that are better aligned with underlying pathophysiology. Future work will extend the framework to multi-modal data to evaluate how composite phenotypes can further support clinical decision-making and trial design.
