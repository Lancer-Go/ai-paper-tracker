摘要Abstract

强大的实体导航依赖于互补的感官线索。然而，在实践中通常很难获得高质量且一致的多模态数据。训练单一模型也具有挑战性，因为丰富的多模式输入会产生复杂的表示并大大扩大政策空间。轻量级模态专业代理之间的跨模态协作提供了可扩展的范例。它支持灵活部署和并行执行，同时保留每种模式的优势。在本文中，我们建议Robust embodied navigation relies on complementary sensory cues. However, high-quality and well-aligned multi-modal data is often difficult to obtain in practice. Training a monolithic model is also challenging as rich multi-modal inputs induce complex representations and substantially enlarge the policy space. Cross-modal collaboration among lightweight modality-specialized agents offers a scalable paradigm. It enables flexible deployment and parallel execution, while preserving the strength of each modality. In this paper, we propose克罗纳CRONA，一个多智能体强化学习（MARL）框架, a Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) framework for克罗Cro短纤莫代尔ss-ModalNa航行。 CRONA 通过利用与控制相关的辅助信念和具有全局状态的集中式多模式批评家来改善协作。视声导航任务的实验表明，多智能体方法比单智能体基线显着提高了性能和效率。我们发现，有限模式的同质协作足以在显着线索下进行短程导航；具有互补模式的代理之间的异构协作通常是高效且有效的；大型复杂环境中的导航需要更丰富的多模态感知和更高的模型容量。vigation. CRONA improves collaboration by leveraging control-relevant auxiliary beliefs and a centralized multi-modal critic with global state. Experiments on visual-acoustic navigation tasks show that multi-agent methods significantly improve performance and efficiency over single-agent baselines. We find that homogeneous collaboration with limited modalities is sufficient for short-range navigation under salient cues; heterogeneous collaboration among agents with complementary modalities is generally efficient and effective; and navigation in large, complex environments requires both richer multi-modal perception and increased model capacity.

1 简介Introduction

在实体导航中，智能体通过不同的感官输入来感知环境，例如 RGB-D 图像、音频、雷达、激光雷达和语言指令In embodied navigation, agents perceive the environment through diverse sensory inputs, e.g., RGB-D images, audio, radar, LiDAR, and language instructionsKolve et al. (2017); Manolis Savva* et al. (2019); Szot et al. (2021); Anderson et al. (2018); Duan et al. (2022)。 这些输入提供了跨模态的丰富几何、语义和声音提示，使代理能够定位目标对象并在复杂环境中导航，例如自动驾驶、机器人系统和人机交互. These inputs provide rich geometric, semantic, and acoustic cues across modalities, enabling agents to locate target objects and navigate in complex environments, such as autonomous driving, robotic systems, and human-computer interactionChen et al. (2017); Das et al. (2017); Ku et al. (2018); Qi et al. (2018); Shridhar et al. (2022); Brohan et al. (2022); Driess et al. (2023).

Although many methods align different modalities within the model, they differ substantially in dimensionality, noise levels, and temporal structureChen et al. (2020a); Shridhar et al. (2020b, a)。这种不匹配通常会导致不平衡的联合优化，其中主导模态驱动大多数梯度更新，而较弱或噪声较大的模态未得到充分利用. Rich-modal models also tend to rely on large architectures to align diverse signals, making them hard to deploy and costly at test timeKim et al. (2021); Yao et al. (2024); Li et al. (2023).

许多研究利用多智能体协作来提高导航效率和鲁棒性Many studies leverage multi-agent collaboration to improve navigation efficiency and robustnessSimmons et al. (2000); Parker (2002); Burgard et al. (2005); Gu et al. Specifically, it remains unclear which modalities improve collaborative capability, which team configurations support both effective and efficient navigation, and what cooperative behaviors emerge among agents.

最近的研究探索了导航的多智能体协作。然而，协作导航仍然具有挑战性，因为代理需要在执行过程中实时协调。 早期的方法依赖于集中式规划，其中中央控制器协调所有代理Recent studies have explored multi-agent collaboration for navigation. However, collaborative navigation remains challenging because agents need to coordinate in real time during execution. Early methods rely on centralized planning, where a central controller coordinates all agentsBruce and Veloso (2006); van Den Berg et al. (2009); Janssen et al. (2016)。 此类设计的可扩展性和鲁棒性有限. Such designs suffer from limited scalability and robustnessVelagapudi et al. (2010); Luna and Bekris (2011); Iqbal and Sha (2019)。 因此，最近的方法转向分散式协作，其中代理根据本地观察采取行动，沟通有限甚至没有沟通. Recent approaches therefore shift toward decentralized collaboration, where agents take action based on their local observations with limited or even without communicationHuang et al. (2021); Qin et al. (2021); Azzam et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2024); Xue and Chen (2023); Wang et al. (2026)。然而，大多数分散式导航研究仍然假设同质代理或具有相似感官输入的代理. However, most decentralized navigation studies still assume homogeneous agents or agents with similar sensory inputsZhang et al. (2025); Xiao et al. (2020, 2022)。 具有异构模式的代理如何有效协作在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索. How agents with heterogeneous modalities collaborate effectively remains largely underexploredHao et al. (2025); Liu et al. (2025); Hu et al. (2023).

合作 MARL 研究多个智能体如何在共同目标下学习协调Cooperative MARL studies how multiple agents learn to coordinate under a shared objectiveZhang et al. (2021); Albrecht et al. (2024); Yuan et al. (2023)。一种简单且可扩展的方法是独立学习，其中代理被单独训练. A simple and scalable approach is independent learning, where agents are separately trainedTan and others (1993); Peshkin et al. (2001)。 但由于所有智能体同时更新其策略，每个智能体都面临着非平稳的学习环境，这通常会导致不稳定和收敛问题. But as all agents update their policies concurrently, each agent faces a non-stationary learning environment, which often leads to instability and convergence issuesClaus and Boutilier (1998); Tuyls et al. (2003); Wunder et al. (2010)。分散执行的集中训练（CTDE）通过在训练期间利用集中信息来缓解这个问题. Centralized training with decentralized execution (CTDE) mitigates this issue by exploiting centralized information during trainingAmato (2024)。例如，集中批评家可以根据联合历史和全球状态估计联合价值. For example, a centralized critic can estimate joint values from joint histories and global statesLowe et al. (2017); Yu et al. (2022); Foerster et al. (2018); Lyu et al. (2021, 2023)。由于批评者在执行时被丢弃，每个代理仍然以分散的方式执行。 CRONA 遵循这一范式，并将任务进度纳入多模态集中批评家中，以进行联合价值估计。. Since the critic is discarded at execution time, each agent remains execute in a decentralized manner. CRONA follows this paradigm and incorporates task progress into a multi-modal centralized critic for joint value estimation.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 CRONA，一个用于跨模式导航的去中心化 MARL 框架。通过将互补的感觉模式分配给不同的代理，CRONA 减轻了每个代理内学习密集多模态表示的负担，同时在测试时保留完全分散的执行。实验表明，具有有限模式的同质协作可能足以满足短距离导航，而具有互补模式的异构协作通常表现更好。在更复杂的场景中，更丰富的多模态输入和足够的模型容量对于导航也很重要。总体而言，跨模式协作是多模式协作的强大且高效的替代方案，特别是当目标表现出清晰的、特定于模式的线索时。We propose CRONA, a decentralized MARL framework for cross-modal navigation. By assigning complementary sensory modalities to different agents, CRONA reduces the burden of learning dense multi-modal representations within each agent, while retaining fully decentralized execution at test time. Experiments show that homogeneous collaboration with limited modalities may suffice for short-range navigation, while heterogeneous collaboration with complementary modalities generally performs better. In more complex scenes, richer multi-modal inputs and sufficient model capacity are also important for navigation. Overall, cross-modal collaboration is a robust and efficient alternative to multi-modal collaboration, especially when targets exhibit clean, modality-specific cues.

局限性Limitations这项工作有一些局限性，为未来的探索指明了方向。首先，我们关注两种常见的模式，视觉和音频，并将 CRONA 扩展到其他感官输入，例如点云、激光雷达或触觉信号，需要进一步研究。其次，我们使用位置和类别信念作为辅助信念学习的概念证明。为更广泛的模式和任务结构开发与控制相关的信念表示是一个重要方向。最后，由于环境配置的限制，我们当前的任务是在 2D 导航设置中实例化的。将跨模式协作扩展到完整的 3D 体现环境将进一步测试其通用性和实际适用性。This work has several limitations that suggest directions for future exploration. First, we focus on two common modalities, vision and audio, and extending CRONA to other sensory inputs, such as point clouds, LiDAR, or tactile signals, requires further study. Second, we use location and category beliefs as a proof of concept for auxiliary belief learning. Developing control-relevant belief representations for broader modalities and task structures is an important direction. Finally, due to constraints in the environment configuration, our current tasks are instantiated in 2D navigation settings. Extending cross-modal collaboration to full 3D embodied environments would further test its generality and practical applicability.
