摘要Abstract

将生成人工智能（GenAI）工具集成到高等教育评估中提出了关于学生如何理解、解释和回应人工智能介导的评估的重要问题。随着教师越来越多地探索用于提供反馈的 AI 工具，之前的研究已经检验了 GenAI 生成的反馈是否可以提高写作成绩以及学生如何看待其有用性；然而，当学生明确得知反馈和分数是由人工智能系统而不是人类教师产生时，他们如何解释这种评估，人们知之甚少。本研究报告了在沙特公立大学计算机系学生的本科技术传播课程中进行的定性教学调查的结果。十三名男性本科计算机生完成了课堂手写写作任务； ChatGPT 使用与任务目标一致的基于标题的提示对扫描的提交内容进行评估。然后明确告知学生 ChatGPT 已生成分数和反馈，并邀请他们以书面形式反思评估。对这些反思的归纳主题分析确定了四个主题：反馈的感知有用性；意识到人工智能的背景和教学局限性；有条件的信任，区分反馈效用和评价权威；以及对人类教师的制度和教学作用的反思。参与者接受 GenAI 反馈对于表面级别的修订有用，但始终将人类讲师定位为评分决策的适当权威。该研究将此视为反馈效用和评估权威之间的区别，学生将这两种判断视为分析上独立的，而不是单一认可量表的两端。在本研究的有限背景和样本内，研究结果表明，GenAI 参与评估的透明度可能会将学生的反思从狭隘地关注反馈质量转向更广泛地考虑权威、信任和评估合法性。该论文提出了一个三原则框架（透明度、人工调解、反思实践），用于将道德 GenAI 整合到写作评估中。The integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) tools into higher education assessment raises important questions about how students understand, interpret, and respond to AI-mediated evaluation. As instructors increasingly explore AI tools for providing feedback, prior research has examined whether GenAI-generated feedback improves writing performance and how students perceive its usefulness; comparatively little is known, however, about how students interpret such evaluation when they are explicitly informed that an AI system, rather than a human instructor, produced the feedback and the score. This study reports findings from a qualitative pedagogical inquiry conducted in an undergraduate technical communication course for computing students at a Saudi public university. Thirteen male undergraduate computing students completed an in-class handwritten writing task; the scanned submissions were evaluated by ChatGPT using a rubric-based prompt aligned with the task objectives. Students were then explicitly informed that ChatGPT had generated the score and feedback and were invited to reflect on the evaluation in writing. Inductive thematic analysis of these reflections identified four themes: perceived usefulness of feedback; awareness of AI's contextual and pedagogical limitations; conditional trust, distinguishing feedback utility from evaluative authority; and reflection on the institutional and pedagogical role of the human instructor. Participants accepted GenAI feedback as useful for surface-level revision but consistently positioned the human instructor as the appropriate authority over grading decisions. The study identifies this as a distinction between feedback utility and evaluative authority, two judgments that students treat as analytically separate rather than as opposite ends of a single approval scale. Within the bounded context and sample of this study, the findings suggest that transparency about GenAI involvement in assessment may shift student reflection from a narrow focus on feedback quality toward broader considerations of authority, trust, and assessment legitimacy. The paper proposes a three-principle framework (transparency, human mediation, reflective practice) for ethical GenAI integration in writing assessment.

一、简介1. Introduction

写作仍然是高等教育的一个核心问题。能够清晰表达自己想法的学生在课程作业、专业沟通和就业成果方面具有优势（Hyland，2019；AlGhamdi，2019）。对于计算机专业的学生来说，书面交流尤其重要：毕业生需要向非专业受众解释技术概念，清楚地记录系统，并证明设计选择的合理性（ABET，2025）。然而，近年来，对学生写作的评估变得更具挑战性。虽然对学生提交的作品的真实性的担忧并不新鲜，但自 2022 年底以来生成式人工智能工具的快速普及给这个问题带来了一个新的维度：学生现在可以在几秒钟内写出流畅的草稿，这使得教师评估原创性、努力程度以及学生作品随时间的发展情况的工作变得更加复杂。Writing remains a central concern in higher education. Students who can articulate their ideas clearly are advantaged in coursework, professional communication, and employment outcomes (Hyland, 2019; AlGhamdi, 2019). For computing students in particular, written communication is important: graduates are expected to explain technical concepts to non-specialist audiences, document systems clearly, and justify design choices (ABET, 2025). The evaluation of student writing has, however, become more challenging in recent years. While concerns about the authenticity of student-submitted writing are not new, the rapid diffusion of generative AI tools since late 2022 has introduced a new dimension to the problem: students can now produce fluent drafts in seconds, complicating instructors' efforts to assess originality, effort, and how student work develops over time.

实证研究表明，此类工具现在被跨学科的大学生广泛采用，通常作为写作助手，而不仅仅是参考辅助工具（Balraj，2025；Zhang 等，2024）。这给教师提出了实质性挑战：评估作者身份和发展过程可能不确定的写作（Uddin et al., 2024; Delikoura et al., 2025）。鉴于学生可以使用这些工具，禁止性反应的实际价值有限；一个更有成效的问题涉及教师如何以支持而不是取代学生学习的方式与 GenAI 互动。Empirical research indicates that such tools are now widely adopted by university students across disciplines, often as writing assistants rather than as reference aids alone (Balraj, 2025; Zhang et al., 2024). This raises a substantive challenge for instructors: assessing writing whose authorship and developmental process may be uncertain (Uddin et al., 2024; Delikoura et al., 2025). Prohibitive responses are of limited practical value given the accessibility of these tools to students; a more productive question concerns how instructors can engage with GenAI in ways that support, rather than displace, student learning.

针对这一挑战的一种务实应对措施是让 GenAI 参与提供反馈。在每周都有写作任务的大型招生课程中，教师通常会面临他们可以提供的个性化反馈的深度和频率的实际限制。最近的研究表明，如果经过深思熟虑的部署，GenAI 反馈可以提高写作质量，提高学生的参与度，并减少教师的工作量（Kinder 等人，2025 年；Çağlar-Özhan 等人，2025 年）。然而，同一份工作揭示了学生的不同反应，包括对准确性的怀疑、对一般反馈的担忧以及对人类监督的持续偏好（Aljasser，2025；Le et al.，2025）。该领域的大多数现有研究侧重于书写表现结果（例如，Kinder 等人，2025；Çağlar-Özhan 等人，2025）或 AI 反馈的感知有用性（例如，Aljasser，2025；Le 等人，2025），相对较少关注一旦涉及评估机构和评分，学生如何解释和理解 GenAI 评估。One pragmatic response to this challenge is to involve GenAI in the provision of feedback. In large enrolment courses with weekly writing tasks, instructors often face practical limits on the depth and frequency of individualised feedback they can provide. Recent studies indicate that GenAI feedback may improve writing quality, increase student engagement, and reduce instructor workload when deployed thoughtfully (Kinder et al., 2025; Çağlar-Özhan et al., 2025). The same body of work, however, reveals mixed student responses, including scepticism about accuracy, concerns about generic feedback, and a persistent preference for human oversight (Aljasser, 2025; Le et al., 2025). Most existing studies in this area focus on writing performance outcomes (e.g., Kinder et al., 2025; Çağlar-Özhan et al., 2025) or on the perceived usefulness of AI feedback (e.g., Aljasser, 2025; Le et al., 2025), with comparatively less attention to how students interpret and make sense of GenAI evaluation once assessment authority and grading are involved.

一个不太受关注的问题是透明度，特别是学生是否知道 GenAI 做出了他们的评估。对算法厌恶和信任的研究表明，对反馈质量和公平性的看法不仅取决于内容，还取决于对其来源的信念（Dietvorst 等人，2015 年；Burton 等人，2020 年）。对算法欣赏和厌恶的研究表明，对来源（算法与人类）的看法会影响跨领域的建议和决策的接受度（Logg 等人，2019 年；Castelo 等人，2019 年）。这些来源效应是否以及如何在教育环境中表现出来，特别是在写作评估中，仍然不太确定。One issue that has received less attention is transparency, specifically, whether students know that GenAI produced their evaluation. Research on algorithm aversion and trust suggests that perceptions of feedback quality and fairness are shaped not only by content but also by beliefs about its source (Dietvorst et al., 2015; Burton et al., 2020). Research on algorithm appreciation and aversion suggests that perceptions of source (algorithmic vs. human) influence acceptance of advice and decisions across domains (Logg et al., 2019; Castelo et al., 2019). Whether and how these source effects manifest in educational settings, particularly in writing assessment, remains less well established.

本研究旨在研究学生在知道人工智能评估了他们的工作后如何反应：他们如何评估反馈，他们是否以及如何信任它，以及他们如何评估其公平性。该研究被定位为同一技术传播课程中进行的教学探究计划的一部分。作者之前的一项研究是在盲态条件下进行的，学生们不知道 ChatGPT 生成了反馈，因此他们的反思不会受到来源知识的影响（AlGhamdi，2024）。当时，学生接触 ChatGPT 的机会相对有限，很少有学生能够接触到 ChatGPT。The present study was designed to examine how students respond when they know that AI has evaluated their work: how they appraise the feedback, whether and how they trust it, and how they assess its fairness. The study is positioned as part of a programme of pedagogical inquiry conducted within the same technical communication course. A prior study by the author was conducted under blinded conditions, in which students were unaware that ChatGPT had generated the feedback, so that their reflections were not shaped by knowledge of the source (AlGhamdi, 2024). At that time, student access to ChatGPT was relatively limited and few students were

因此，本研究采用透明的、以反思为导向的教学设计，明确告知学生 ChatGPT 评估了他们的手写写作。评估之后，学生完成一项简短的课堂反思任务，检查他们对反馈和分数的同意程度、评估揭示的有关他们写作的内容，以及他们对使用 ChatGPT 进行写作评估的适当性的看法。通过关注学生的想法而不是 GenAI 反馈的准确性，本研究解决了有关信任、公平和学习的问题，这些问题是高等教育中负责任的 GenAI 整合的核心（Yusuf 等人，2024 年；Donadel 和 Zuin，2026 年）。在此过程中，该研究提出了现有文献中尚未明确指出的概念区别：学生接受人工智能作为反馈机制（反馈效用）和接受人工智能作为具有评分权威的评估者（评估​​权威）。这种区别有助于协调算法信任文献中相互竞争的发现，并为设计人工智能介导的评估实践提供更精确的词汇。Accordingly, the present study adopts a transparent, reflection-oriented pedagogical design in which students are explicitly informed that ChatGPT evaluated their handwritten writing. Following the evaluation, students complete a short, in-class reflective task examining their agreement with the feedback and score, what the evaluation revealed about their writing, and their views on the appropriateness of using ChatGPT for writing assessment. By focusing on what students think rather than on the accuracy of GenAI feedback, this study addresses questions about trust, fairness, and learning that are central to responsible GenAI integration in higher education (Yusuf et al., 2024; Donadel and Zuin, 2026). In doing so, the study contributes a conceptual distinction that has not been clearly named in the existing literature: between students' acceptance of AI as a feedback mechanism (feedback utility) and their acceptance of AI as an evaluator with grading authority (evaluative authority). This distinction helps reconcile competing findings in the algorithm-trust literature and offers a more precise vocabulary for designing AI-mediated assessment practices.

2.文献综述2. Literature Review

GenAI 工具在高等教育中的出现引发了大量实证研究，探讨这些工具在写作教学中的作用以及学生如何对人工智能生成的反馈做出反应。这篇综述将本研究置于该文献的几个重叠部分中：人工智能反馈技术充分性的研究；关于学生信任和算法厌恶或欣赏的研究；关于反馈素养和评估的关系性质的研究；关于人工智能的反思性和元认知参与的研究；伦理整合研究；以及非西方高等教育环境中人工智能采用的研究。在这些方面，一个反复出现的观察结果是，反馈的来源决定了学生如何解释和采取行动，但相对较少的研究探讨了当学生被明确告知人工智能系统（而不是人类教师）生成了他们的评估时会发生什么。The emergence of GenAI tools in higher education has prompted a substantial body of empirical research examining how such tools function in writing pedagogy and how students respond to AIgenerated feedback. This review situates the present study within several overlapping strands of that literature: research on the technical adequacy of AI feedback; research on student trust and algorithm aversion or appreciation; research on feedback literacy and the relational nature of assessment; research on reflective and metacognitive engagement with AI; research on ethical integration; and research on AI adoption in non-Western higher education contexts. Across these strands, a recurring observation is that the source of feedback shapes how students interpret and act on it, yet relatively few studies have examined what happens when students are explicitly told that an AI system, rather than a human instructor, has generated their evaluation.

The question has shifted: not whether AI can produce useful feedback, but how students engage with it once they know it is AI. For the present study, this shift is important because participants were explicitly told that ChatGPT had evaluated their work, allowing the analysis to focus on the interpretive layer that sits above the technical content of the feedback itself.

总而言之，这些工作表明，学生可能会同时欣赏人工智能的某些功能（高效、一致、表面反馈），并抵制人工智能的其他功能（涉及主观判断的高风险评估）。本研究直接检验了这种区别：分析关注的是学生如何在同一评估事件中区分人工智能的功能，而不是询问学生是否接受或拒绝整个人工智能评估。Taken together, this body of work suggests that students may simultaneously appreciate AI for some functions (efficient, consistent, surface-level feedback) and resist it for others (high-stakes evaluation involving subjective judgement). The present study examines this distinction directly: rather than asking whether students accept or reject AI evaluation as a whole, the analysis attends to how students separate functions of AI within the same evaluation event.

2.3.反馈素养和评估的相关性2.3. Feedback literacy and the relational nature of assessment

第三个研究领域在 GenAI 文献中不太突出，但却是本研究框架的核心，涉及反馈素养和教育评估的关系特征。 Carless 和 Boud (2018) 将学生反馈素养定义为理解反馈并利用反馈来增强学习所需的理解、能力和倾向，围绕四个相互关联的特征组织：欣赏反馈、做出判断、管理情感和采取行动。在一篇相关的早期论文中，Boud 和 Molloy（2013）对比了两种反馈模型：一种源自控制论和生理隐喻的传输模型，其中教师充当反馈的驱动者；另一种是可持续评估模型，其中学习者在生成、征求和使用反馈以促进自身发展方面发挥积极作用。A third strand of research, less prominent in the GenAI literature but central to the framing of the present study, concerns feedback literacy and the relational character of educational assessment. Carless and Boud (2018) defined student feedback literacy as the understandings, capacities, and dispositions needed to make sense of feedback and use it to enhance learning, organised around four inter-related features: appreciating feedback, making judgements, managing affect, and taking action. In a related earlier paper, Boud and Molloy (2013) contrasted two models of feedback: a transmission model derived from cybernetic and physiological metaphors, in which teachers act as the drivers of feedback, and a sustainable assessment model, in which learners take an active role in generating, soliciting, and using feedback for their own development.

2.7.总结与差距2.7. Summary and gap

综上所述，上述文献指出了四个主要观察结果。首先，人工智能反馈已被证明在许多写作任务的清晰度和教学实用性方面与教师反馈相匹配。其次，学生有选择地参与此类反馈，对准确性、公平性和评估权威表达微妙且有时相互矛盾的保留意见，尤其是在成绩受到威胁时。第三，反馈不是作为离散的信息传递来接收的，而是在对谁在评估、他们可以了解学习者以及评估后可以进行哪些对话的关系理解中进行解释。第四，人工智能参与评估的透明度似乎影响了学生的看法，但披露的具体机制和后果，特别是在非西方高等教育背景下，仍然没有得到充分审查。Taken together, the literature reviewed above points to four principal observations. First, AI feedback has been shown to match instructor feedback in clarity and instructional utility for many writing tasks. Second, students engage selectively with such feedback, expressing nuanced and sometimes contradictory reservations regarding accuracy, fairness, and evaluative authority, particularly when grades are at stake. Third, feedback is not received as a discrete information transfer but is interpreted within a relational understanding of who is evaluating, what they can know about the learner, and what dialogue is possible after the evaluation. Fourth, transparency about AI involvement in assessment appears to shape student perceptions, but the specific mechanisms and consequences of disclosure, particularly in non-Western higher education contexts, remain under-examined.

本研究通过透明的评估后反思设计来研究沙特计算机专业的学生在被明确告知人工智能系统生成了反馈和分数时如何推理人工智能介导的写作评估，从而解决了这一差距。该分析不仅关注学生是否接受或拒绝人工智能反馈，还关注学生自己在评估过程中对人工智能不同功能的区分。The present study addresses this gap by examining, in a transparent post-assessment reflective design, how Saudi computing students reason about AI-mediated writing evaluation when they have been explicitly informed that an AI system generated their feedback and score. The analysis attends not only to whether students accept or reject AI feedback, but to the distinctions students themselves draw between different functions of AI within the assessment process.

3. 方法论3. Methodology

3.1.研究设计3.1. Research Design

这项研究源于一个从业者和研究者的问题：当教师公开使用人工智能评估他们的工作时，学生会如何反应？该研究采用了定性的、嵌入课程的教学探究方法，对学生反思性写作进行归纳主题分析。背景是作者教授的本科生技术交流课程，其目的是检查学生在明确得知 ChatGPT（而不是教师）评估了他们的写作时的反应。与作者早期的双盲研究（AlGhamdi，2024）不同，在该研究中，学生不知道 ChatGPT 生成了反馈，这项研究通过将 GenAI 评估定位为明确的教学干预而不是隐藏的评估机制，从而突出了透明度。This study emerged from a practitioner-researcher question: how do students respond when the instructor is transparent about using AI to evaluate their work? The study adopted a qualitative, course-embedded pedagogical inquiry approach, using inductive thematic analysis of student reflective writing. The setting was an undergraduate Technical Communication course taught by the author, in which the aim was to examine how students respond when explicitly informed that ChatGPT, rather than the instructor, has evaluated their writing. Unlike the author’s earlier blinded study (AlGhamdi, 2024), in which students were unaware that ChatGPT had generated the feedback, this study foregrounds transparency by positioning GenAI evaluation as an explicit instructional intervention rather than a concealed assessment mechanism.

该研究的框架是一项嵌入课程的教学探究，旨在应对当代挑战，即学生广泛接触 GenAI 改变了高等教育中的写作实践、作者身份和评估真实性（Zhang 等人，2024）。当前的研究并没有试图禁止 GenAI 的使用，而是研究了明确的 AI 介导的评估如何重塑学生的元认知意识、信任、怀疑、道德推理和反馈参与。The study is framed as a course-embedded pedagogical inquiry, responding to the contemporary challenge that widespread student access to GenAI has altered writing practices, authorship, and assessment authenticity in higher education (Zhang et al., 2024). Rather than attempting to prohibit GenAI use, the current study examines how explicit AI-mediated evaluation may reshape students’ metacognitive awareness, trust, skepticism, ethical reasoning, and engagement with feedback.

这项研究是在沙特一所公立大学为计算机学院本科生提供的技术传播课程中进行的。该课程强调实用的书面沟通技巧，包括清晰的表达、逻辑组织和受众意识。The study was conducted at a Saudi public university, within a Technical Communication course offered to undergraduate students at a computing faculty. The course emphasises practical written communication skills, including clarity of expression, logical organisation, and audience awareness.

研究样本由 19 名男学生组成，他们在研究学期内选修了该课程的一个部分。所有参与者都是大学二年级学生，在加入计算机系之前完成了预科课程。作为日常课程的一部分，学生完成每周的写作任务，然后进行结构化反馈和评分。本研究中分析的反思部分嵌入到常规评估活动中，而不是作为单独的研究工具进行管理。 19名注册学生中，有13人完成了反思任务并进入最终分析。因此，分析样本代表了在人工智能生成的评估后提交完整反思性回答的学生。The study sample consisted of 19 male students enrolled in a single section of the course during the study semester. 3.4.数据分析3.4. Data Analysis

为了分析学生的反思，根据 Braun 和 Clarke（2006；2021）的指导方针进行了归纳主题分析。该分析是数据驱动的，允许主题从学生自己的话语中出现，而不是从预定的理论类别中出现。该过程涉及：（a）反复熟悉数据集； (b) 与 ChatGPT 反馈的感知、信任和怀疑、学习意识、情绪反应、道德关注以及与人类评估的比较相关的有意义单元的开放语义编码； (c) 通过不断比较，将代码迭代分组和细化为连贯的主题。最终的主题得到了明确的定义和分析性的描述，同时关注它们与人工智能介导的写作评估的教学相关性。保留了具有代表性的学生引文以支持分析主张，并通过将分析集中于学生的自我表达经验而不是对写作质量的评价判断来保持反思性。To analyze students' reflections, an inductive thematic analysis was conducted following Braun and Clarke's (2006; 2021) guidelines. The analysis was data-driven, allowing themes to emerge from students' own words rather than from predetermined theoretical categories. The process involved: (a) repeated familiarisation with the dataset; (b) open semantic coding of meaningful units related to perceptions of ChatGPT feedback, trust and skepticism, learning awareness, emotional responses, ethical concerns, and comparisons with human evaluation; and (c) iterative grouping and refinement of codes into coherent themes through constant comparison. Final themes were clearly defined and analytically described with attention to their pedagogical relevance to AImediated writing assessment. Representative student quotations were retained to ground analytic claims, and reflexivity was maintained by centering analysis on students' self-articulated experiences rather than on evaluative judgments of writing quality.

主题分析分两个互补的阶段进行。在第一阶段，作者使用 NVivo 进行了初步编码，从转录的反射中进行归纳，以识别开放代码，将它们分组为临时主题，并通过迭代数据来完善主题结构。第 4 节中报告的四个主题是从这项初步分析中得出的。在第二阶段，使用 Claude Code（Anthropic，模型 Opus 4.7）对扫描的手写反思进行独立重新分析，作为验证性分析检查，而无需事先了解作者的主题结构。这种首先是人工编码，其次是人工智能辅助验证的排序是故意采用的，以确保作者的分析判断不会被人工智能生成的框架所锚定（AlGhamdi，2026）。The thematic analysis was conducted in two complementary stages. In the first stage, the author conducted the primary coding using NVivo, working inductively from the transcribed reflections to identify open codes, group these into provisional themes, and refine the theme structure through iterative engagement with the data. The four themes reported in Section 4 emerged from this primary analysis. In the second stage, the scanned handwritten reflections were independently reanalysed using Claude Code (Anthropic, model Opus 4.7) as a confirmatory analytic check, conducted with no prior access to the author's thematic structure. This sequencing, with humanled coding first and AI-assisted verification second, was adopted deliberately to ensure that the author's analytic judgement was not anchored by AI-generated framings (AlGhamdi, 2026).

验证性分析与主题 1 和 2 的主要分析相一致，并促使对主题 3 和 4 所用的框架进行细化，以更密切地反映参与者自己的表达：主题 3 得到了强化，以强调反馈效用和评估权威之间的区别，而主题 4 则从关注透明度和公平性重新构建为关注参与者对人类教师的制度和教学角色的推理。这些改进是在重新检查数据的基础上采用的，而不是在不加批判地接受第二位分析师的解释的基础上进行的。人工智能辅助分析的使用被认为存在局限性，包括大型语言模型之间可能存在共同的解释偏差；未来的复制研究将受益于人类分析师的独立第二次编码。The confirmatory analysis converged with the primary analysis on Themes 1 and 2 and prompted refinement of the framings used for Themes 3 and 4 to more closely reflect participants' own articulations: Theme 3 was sharpened to highlight the distinction between feedback utility and evaluative authority, and Theme 4 was reframed from a focus on transparency and fairness to a focus on participants' reasoning about the institutional and pedagogical role of the human instructor. These refinements were adopted on the basis of re-examining the data, not on the basis of accepting the second analyst's interpretations uncritically. The use of AI-assisted analysis is acknowledged as having limitations, including the possibility of shared interpretive biases between large language models; future replication studies would benefit from independent second coding by a human analyst.

3.5.道德考虑3.5. Ethical Considerations

几个具体的程序值得在这里进行明确的讨论。首先，在数据处理方面，扫描学生手写提交的材料，并通过 ChatGPT 处理生成的图像进行评估；所有提交的内容在处理前均经过匿名处理，删除或编辑了学生姓名和任何身份信息，并且在本研究分析的数据集中没有保留任何个人身份元数据。其次，关于学生自主性，人工智能介导的评估是作为课程层面的教学活动实施的，而不是作为强加于个别学生的研究特定程序；不愿意将自己的作品用于研究部分的学生可以自由拒绝，并且将他们的反思用于研究目的与他们参与常规课程活动不同。没有学生拒绝参与研究，也没有标识符与分析数据集相关联。第三，关于讲师-研究员的双重角色，这一职位引入了众所周知的反应偏差和权力失衡的风险；该研究通过以下方式减轻了这些风险：(a) 将反思提示设计为开放式探究，明确欢迎批评性和不同的观点；(b) 将反思嵌入日常课程作业中，而不是将其作为研究工具呈现；(c) 在分析之前匿名回答；(d) 将分析注意力集中在学生自我表达的经历上，而不是对写作质量的评估判断上。作者承认在这种情况下无法完全消除残余偏见，并在第 6 节中指出这是一个限制。Several specific procedures warrant explicit discussion here. First, regarding data handling, handwritten student submissions were scanned and the resulting images were processed through ChatGPT for evaluation; all submissions were anonymised before processing, with student names and any identifying information removed or redacted, and no personally identifying metadata was retained in the dataset analysed for this study. Second, regarding student autonomy, the AImediated evaluation was implemented as a course-level pedagogical activity, not as a researchspecific procedure imposed on individual students; students who preferred not to have their work used for the research component were free to decline, and the use of their reflections for research purposes was distinct from their participation in the routine course activity. No student declined to participate in research, and no identifiers were linked to the analytic dataset. Third, regarding the dual role of instructor-researcher, this position introduces well-recognised risks of response bias and power imbalance; the study mitigated these risks through (a) framing the reflective prompt as open-ended inquiry that explicitly welcomed critical and divergent views, (b) embedding the reflection within routine coursework rather than presenting it as a research instrument, (c) anonymising responses prior to analysis, and (d) centring analytic attention on students’ selfarticulated experiences rather than evaluative judgements of writing quality. The author acknowledges that residual bias cannot be entirely eliminated under such conditions, and notes this as a limitation in Section 6.

虽然讲师兼研究员的职位存在上述风险，但它也允许根据背景深入了解学生如何参与本课程的反馈。在分析过程中，作者注重理解学生陈述的含义，而不是评价其正确性；怀疑和分歧被视为具有分析意义的数据，而不是需要解决的不一致之处。While the instructor-researcher position carries the risks noted above, it also permitted contextually grounded insight into how students engaged with feedback in this course. During analysis, the author focused on understanding the meaning of students' statements rather than evaluating their correctness; doubts and disagreements were treated as analytically meaningful data rather than as inconsistencies to be resolved.

4. 调查结果4. Findings

本节报告了学生对 ChatGPT 生成的每周写作任务评估的书面反思进行归纳主题分析的结果。该分析研究了当学生明确得知 ChatGPT 生成了反馈和分数时，他们如何看待评估。整个数据集中一致出现了四个主题：（1）反馈的感知有用性； (2) 了解人工智能的背景和教学局限性； （3）有条件的信任，区分反馈效用和评价权威； (4) 反思人类导师的制度和教学作用。表 1 总结了这些主题在 13 名参与者中的分布。为了分析清晰起见，这些主题是分开呈现的，尽管在学生的反思中，这些主题经常交织在一个单一的答案中。This section reports the findings from inductive thematic analysis of students’ written reflections on ChatGPT-generated evaluations of their weekly writing tasks. The analysis examined how students perceived the evaluation when they were explicitly informed that ChatGPT had generated the feedback and the score. Four themes emerged consistently across the dataset: (1) perceived usefulness of feedback; (2) awareness of AI’s contextual and pedagogical limitations; (3) conditional trust, distinguishing feedback utility from evaluative authority; and (4) reflection on the institutional and pedagogical role of the human instructor. 4.1.反馈的感知有用性4.1. Perceived usefulness of feedback

所有 13 名参与者都将 ChatGPT 生成的反馈描述为有用，特别是重视其对具体问题的识别，例如语法错误或杂乱的段落，而不是诸如“需要改进”之类模糊的评价性评论。一些学生将反馈描述为“清晰”、“直接”、“组织良好”和“客观”。一位参与者写道：“这些评论很清晰，帮助我理解了自己的错误，特别是在语法和句子结构方面。”另一位表示：“[ChatGPT]直接显示了我的错误，并给了我具体的改进点。这也让我更加意识到如何以更清晰、更有条理的方式写作。”第三位参与者将反馈描述为“非常有帮助”，因为“它给了我明确的具体反馈点，特别是关于语法和拼写的建议。”考虑到其他研究中学生对人工智能的反应存在争议（参见第 5.1 节），这种对反馈有用性的普遍认可本身就是一个值得注意的发现。All thirteen participants described the ChatGPT-generated feedback as useful, particularly valuing its identification of specific issues, for example, a grammar error or a disorganised paragraph, rather than vague evaluative comments such as “needs improvement.” Several students described the feedback as “clear,” “direct,” “well-organized,” and “objective.” One participant wrote: “The comments were clear and helped me understand my mistakes, especially in grammar and sentence structure.” Another stated: “[ChatGPT] showed my mistakes directly and gave me specific points to improve. It also made me more aware of how to write in a clearer and more organized way.” A third participant described the feedback as “very helpful” because “it gave me clear specific feedback points, especially the suggestion to work on grammar and spelling.” This universal endorsement of feedback usefulness is itself a noteworthy finding, given the contested nature of student responses to AI in other studies (see Section 5.1).

一些参与者明确指出了他们从反馈中学到了什么。一位参与者指出，评估“帮助我建立了对写作的信心，同时也提供了进一步改进写作的建议。”另一位表示，反馈“清楚地解释了我避免了普通学生会犯的错误，同时指出了我写作中看到的独特特征”，并补充说“分数与解释完美匹配”。这些回复表明参与者认识到 ChatGPT 反馈的技术价值，特别是对于表面级书写功能。反馈的一致性和系统性被强调为优势，特别是与大课堂中经常遇到的简短或延迟反馈相比。Several participants explicitly identified what they had learned from the feedback. One participant noted that the evaluation “helped me build confidence in my writing while also providing advice to improve the writing even more.” Another stated that the feedback “explained clearly what mistakes I avoided that the average student would make, while pointing out the unique traits seen from my writing,” adding that “the scores matched the explanation perfectly.” These responses indicate that participants recognised the technical value of ChatGPT feedback, particularly for surface-level writing features. The consistency and systematic nature of the feedback were highlighted as strengths, especially when compared with the brief or delayed feedback often encountered in large classes.

4.2.对人工智能局限性的认识4.2. Awareness of AI limitations

虽然参与者重视反馈，但大多数人也阐明了人工智能评估的具体局限性。这些限制分为两类：技术限制和背景限制。在技​​术层面，一些参与者担心 ChatGPT 可能会误解手写内容、书写意图或上下文含义，特别是因为书写内容是手写的，然后进行扫描。一位参与者观察到：“我确实注意到它把我的姓氏拼错了，它认为我拼写错误，但其实我没有。我认为这是因为我的笔迹，所以它一定是读错了。”这位参与者补充道：“我也不会太认真地对待它的分数，因为正如我提到的，它可能会误读单词，并可能做出错误的假设。”另一位更直言不讳地说：“人工智能可能会犯错误，从而影响学生的成绩。”While participants valued the feedback, the majority also articulated specific limitations of the AI evaluation. These limitations fell into two categories: technical and contextual. At the technical level, several participants raised concerns that ChatGPT could misinterpret handwriting, writing intent, or contextual meaning, especially because the writing was handwritten and later scanned. One participant observed: “I did notice that it spelt my last name wrong and it thinks I made mistakes in my spelling when I didn’t. I think this is because of my handwriting so it must have misread it.” This participant added: “I would also take its score less seriously because, as I mentioned, it can misread words and possibly make false assumptions.” Another stated more bluntly: “AI can make mistakes that will affect the student’s grades.”

在背景层面，参与者发现了超出技术错误的限制。一位参与者指出人工智能的评估与机构评级系统之间存在不一致：“人工智能评级有一个问题，那就是人工智能不知道[教师的]评级系统。”另一位人士对人工智能一贯的积极性提出了担忧：“我对使用人工智能提供反馈的看法是，人工智能总是试图保持积极的态度，但这并不意味着它无法使用。我认为我们可以将其用作初始反馈。”这些限制涉及人工智能解释上下文、意图、努力和制度规范的能力，而不是其生成流畅文本的能力。At the contextual level, participants identified limitations that went beyond technical error. One participant pointed to a misalignment between the AI's evaluation and the institutional rating system: “The AI rating had one problem, which is the AI doesn't know [the instructor's] rating system.” Another raised a concern about AI's consistent positivity: “My opinion about using AI to give feedback is [that] AI always tries to be positive, but that doesn't mean it's unable to use. I think we can use it as initial feedback.” These limitations concern AI's capacity to interpret context, intent, effort, and institutional norms rather than its capacity to produce fluent text.

这些回应表明，至少本研究的一些参与者并未将 ChatGPT 评估视为中立或绝对正确。相反，他们积极质疑其准确性和可靠性，特别是在上下文理解和意义解释方面。These responses suggest that at least some participants in this study did not treat ChatGPT evaluation as neutral or infallible. Instead, they actively questioned its accuracy and reliability, especially regarding contextual understanding and the interpretation of meaning.

4.3.有条件的信任：区分反馈效用和评估权威4.3. Conditional Trust: Distinguishing Feedback Utility from Evaluative Authority

数据集中最具分析性的独特模式是参与者可以同时肯定 ChatGPT 反馈的有用性并拒绝 ChatGPT 作为合适的最终评估者。参与者在“这个反馈很有用”和“这个系统应该决定我的成绩”之间做出了一致的区分。这些被视为分析上独立的判断，而不是单一批准量表的两端。该模式在整个数据集中以多种形式表达出来。一位参与者简洁地捕捉到了其中的区别：“在我看来，对 ChatGPT 反馈的看法，我同意它，但有一个条件，即医生检查反馈以编辑任何错误。”另一位则通过参考教师对学生的卓越知识制定了相同的立场：“教师更了解学生，并且更了解给学生评分和支持学生学习的好方法。”。The most analytically distinctive pattern in the dataset was that participants could simultaneously affirm the usefulness of ChatGPT’s feedback and reject ChatGPT as an appropriate final evaluator. Participants drew a consistent distinction between “this feedback is useful” and “this system should decide my grade.” These were treated as analytically separate judgements rather than two ends of a single approval scale. The pattern was articulated in many forms across the dataset. One participant captured the distinction concisely: “In my opinion on ChatGPT feedback, I agree with it, but with one condition, that the doctor checks the feedback to edit any mistakes.” Another formulated the same position by reference to the instructor’s superior knowledge of students: “The teacher knows much better about students and knows better [about] good ways to grade students and support students’ learning.”.

一些参与者明确认可人工智能的一种角色，同时拒绝人工智能的另一种角色。其中一位写道：“医生只能使用人工智能来[阅读]考试反馈，但如果他们将其用于[评分]学生，就会出现问题。”另一个人区分了“初始反馈”和最终评分，接受前者并质疑后者。一位特别善于表达的参与者将拒绝人工智能作为评分者的理由建立在对话推理的基础上：“ChatGPT 不像人。你与他们交谈并解释考虑的借口，或者在失败时再给一次机会。人们谈论并讨论问题Some participants explicitly endorsed AI for one role while rejecting it for another. One wrote: “Doctors can use AI for [reading] feedback for exams only, but if they use it for [grading] students, problems will happen.” Another distinguished between “initial feedback” and final grading, accepting the former and questioning the latter. A particularly articulate participant grounded the rejection of AI-as-grader in dialogical reasoning: “ChatGPT is not like people. You talk to them and explain excuses to consider or give another chance if you fail. People talk and discuss problems

并了解彼此的问题。 ……我喜欢普通老师对我的作业进行评分。”值得注意的是，即使在对 ChatGPT 最热衷的参与者中也观察到了相同的条件信任模式：一位将 ChatGPT 重新定义为“你自己的老师”的参与者仍然将这种认可视为与学习支持而不是对权威进行评分有关，而另一位最初“持怀疑态度”的人建议“更多的教师至少应该尝试使用人工智能，尤其是反馈”，明确将认可限制为反馈而不是评估。and understand each other’s problems. … I like normal teacher [to] grade my work.” Notably, the same conditional-trust pattern was observed even among participants most enthusiastic about ChatGPT: one who reframed ChatGPT as “your own teacher” nevertheless framed the endorsement as relating to learning support rather than grading authority, and another, initially “skeptical,” recommended that “more teachers should at least try using AI, especially for feedback”, explicitly limiting the endorsement to feedback rather than evaluation.

4.4.对人类教师的制度和教学作用的反思4.4. Reflection on the Institutional and Pedagogical Role of the Human Instructor

一些参与者不仅对反馈本身进行评估，还阐明了人类讲师在评估中很重要的原因。这些反思超越了人工智能的技术限制，解决了参与者认为只有人类教师才能实现的教学和机构功能。数据集中出现了四个相互关联的论点。首先，参与者援引了教师对个别学生的了解。一位参与者写道：“老师更了解学生，知道给学生评分和支持学生学习的好方法。”另一位参与者在反思结束时部分用阿拉伯语撰写，阐述了 ChatGPT 无法复制的教学某些方面，例如与学生单独互动以及以适合个人的方式解释概念。Several participants moved beyond an appraisal of the feedback itself to articulate reasons why the human instructor matters in assessment. These reflections went beyond technical limitations of AI to address the pedagogical and institutional functions that, in participants’ view, only a human teacher can fulfil. Four interrelated arguments appeared across the dataset. First, participants invoked the teacher’s knowledge of individual students. One participant wrote: “Teacher knows much better about students and knows good ways to grade students and support students’ learning.” Another participant, writing partly in Arabic at the end of the reflection, elaborated that there are aspects of teaching that ChatGPT cannot replicate, such as engaging with students individually and explaining concepts in personally appropriate ways.

其次，参与者强调了人类评估的对话性质：“ChatGPT 不像人。你与他们交谈并解释考虑的借口，或者在失败时再给一次机会。”这一论点将评分视为一种需要解释和对话可能性的关系实践，而不是一种单向判断。第三，当人工智能接管评估时，参与者对大学本身的作用提出了机构方面的担忧。一位与会者尖锐地阐述了这一点：“如果 ChatGPT 检查考试，我们为什么要上大学？为什么不向 ChatGPT 学习并在 ChatGPT 中测试自己？”这个问题主要不是关于反馈质量；而是关于反馈质量。当评估委托给人工智能时，它关系到正规高等教育的合法性和目的。第四，参与者提出了与公平相关的担忧，特别是与人工智能错误的后果相关的问题：“人工智能可能会犯一些错误，从而影响学生的成绩。”这些担忧使讲师成为防止人工智能错误传播到后续评估决策的必要保障。这些论点共同构成了教师权威的教学案例，它不同于简单地反对技术，而且比简单的反对技术更强大。参与者并没有拒绝人工智能；而是他们阐述了为什么在高等教育评估中应保留人类评估权威的积极原因。Second, participants emphasised the dialogical nature of human assessment: “ChatGPT is not like people. You talk to them and explain excuses to consider or give another chance if you fail.” This argument framed grading not as a one-way judgement but as a relational practice that requires interpretation and the possibility of dialogue. Third, participants raised institutional concerns about the role of the university itself when AI takes over evaluation. One participant articulated this in pointed terms: “If ChatGPT [is] checking the exams, why are we going to university? Why not learn from ChatGPT and test ourselves in ChatGPT?” This question is not primarily about feedback quality; it concerns the legitimacy and purpose of formal higher education when assessment is delegated to AI. Fourth, participants raised fairness-related concerns specifically connected to the consequences of AI error: “AI can make mistakes that will affect the student’s grades.” Such concerns positioned the instructor as a necessary safeguard against the propagation of AI errors into consequential evaluative decisions. Together, these arguments construct a pedagogical case for instructor authority that is distinct from, and stronger than, simple opposition to technology. Participants were not rejecting AI; they were articulating positive reasons why human evaluative authority should be preserved in higher education assessment.

5. 讨论5. Discussion

整个数据集中的一个中心观察结果是，当学生意识到 ChatGPT 评估了他们的工作时，他们的反思超出了对反馈本身的评估，延伸到了有关评估过程的更广泛问题。参与者提出了有关公平性、机器生成成绩的适当性以及教师在人工智能介导的评估中的作用的问题。这些担忧超出了反馈内容是否准确的范围。A central observation across the dataset was that when students were aware ChatGPT had evaluated their work, their reflections extended beyond appraisal of the feedback itself to broader questions about the assessment process. Participants raised questions about fairness, the appropriateness of machine-generated grades, and the instructor's role in AI-mediated assessment. These concerns extended beyond whether the feedback content was accurate.

接下来的小节详细讨论了四种相互关联的模式，并将这些发现置于人工智能介导的反馈、透明度和写作教学法的现有研究中。本节末尾给出了综合这些模式的概念模型（图 6）。The subsections that follow discuss the four interrelated patterns in detail, situating the findings within existing research on AI-mediated feedback, transparency, and writing pedagogy. 5.1.区分反馈效用和评估权威5.1. Distinguishing Feedback Utility from Evaluative Authority

在这个样本中观察到的一个显着模式是学生对人工智能生成的反馈的同意与他们接受人工智能作为权威评估者之间的区别。虽然许多学生承认 ChatGPT 提供的反馈的清晰度、结构和技术实用性，但这种认可并没有转化为对基于人工智能的评分的无条件信任。One prominent pattern observed in this sample was the distinction between students’ agreement with AI-generated feedback and their acceptance of AI as an authoritative evaluator. While many students acknowledged the clarity, structure, and technical usefulness of the feedback provided by ChatGPT, this recognition did not translate into unconditional trust in AI-based grading.

学生的反映表明，ChatGPT 反馈的价值主要在于其识别写作中表面问题的能力，例如语法、句子结构、组织和简洁性。这些看法与之前的研究一致，表明 GenAI 可以针对低阶写作问题提供与人类评估者相当的反馈（Çağlar-Özhan 等人，2025 年；Kinder 等人，2025 年）。在这项研究中，人工智能评估的透明度似乎激发了更批判的立场，促使学生将反馈效用与评估合法性分开。Students’ reflections indicate that ChatGPT feedback was primarily valued for its ability to identify surface-level issues in writing, such as grammar, sentence construction, organization, and conciseness. These perceptions align with previous research showing that GenAI can provide feedback comparable to human evaluators for lower-order writing concerns (Çağlar-Özhan et al., 2025; Kinder et al., 2025). In this study, transparency in AI evaluation appeared to activate a more critical stance, prompting students to separate feedback utility from assessment legitimacy.

参与者并没有接受人工智能作为中立或高级的评估者，而是一致强调，评分具有制度和道德的重要性，而这应该仍然是人类教师的责任。这一立场与有据可查的算法厌恶现象相一致：即使算法优于人类，个人也倾向于拒绝算法决策，特别是在人类判断被认为至关重要的领域，例如教育（Dietvorst et al., 2015）。随着决策风险的增加，算法厌恶感也会加剧（Burton et al., 2020），这与本研究中参与者在评分出现问题时表达的对教师监督的强烈偏好一致。参与者的怀疑似乎并非源于对技术本身的拒绝，而是源于对人工智能的背景局限性及其缺乏教学责任的认识。Rather than accepting AI as a neutral or superior assessor, participants consistently emphasised that grading carries institutional and ethical weight that should remain the responsibility of a human instructor. This position aligns with the well-documented phenomenon of algorithm aversion: the tendency of individuals to reject algorithmic decisions even when algorithms outperform humans, particularly in domains where human judgement is perceived as essential, such as education (Dietvorst et al., 2015). Algorithm aversion has been shown to intensify as the stakes of the decision increase (Burton et al., 2020), consistent with the strong preference for instructor oversight expressed by participants in the present study when grading was at issue. Participants’ scepticism did not appear to stem from a rejection of technology itself, but rather from awareness of AI’s contextual limitations and its lack of pedagogical accountability.

该样本中观察到的模式也有助于协调更广泛文献中相互竞争的发现。而 Dietvorst 等人。 （2015）和伯顿等人。 (2020) 记录了风险决策中的算法厌恶，Logg、Minson 和 Moore (2019) 报告了数值估计任务中的算法欣赏，其中外行人更重视算法建议而不是人类建议。 Castelo、Bos 和 Lehmann（2019）证明，这种厌恶是与任务相关的，当任务被认为需要人类独特的能力（例如主观判断）时，这种厌恶就会加剧。本研究的参与者既没有表现出普遍的厌恶，也没有表现出普遍的赞赏；相反，他们在同一评估事件中区分人工智能的功能，接受人工智能的表面反馈，同时保留人类的评估权威。这种特定于任务的信任模式给算法厌恶和算法欣赏账户增加了细微差别，这表明学生并不是对人工智能做出单一的接受或拒绝决定，而是对同一系统的不同功能做出单独的判断。The pattern observed in this sample also helps to reconcile competing findings in the broader literature. While Dietvorst et al. (2015) and Burton et al. (2020) document algorithm aversion in stakes-bearing decisions, Logg, Minson, and Moore (2019) reported algorithm appreciation in numeric estimation tasks where lay people weighted algorithmic advice more heavily than human advice. Castelo, Bos, and Lehmann (2019) demonstrated that this aversion is task-dependent, intensifying when tasks are perceived as requiring uniquely human capacities such as subjective judgement. Participants in the present study expressed neither generalised aversion nor generalised appreciation; instead, they distinguished between AI functions within the same evaluation event, accepting AI for surface-level feedback while preserving human authority for evaluation. This task-specific pattern of trust adds nuance to both algorithm-aversion and algorithm-appreciation accounts, suggesting that students are not making a single accept-or-reject decision about AI but are instead making separate judgements about distinct functions of the same system.

同一课程的一项早期研究在盲态条件下对不同群体进行，发现学生倾向于关注反馈内容，而不质疑其来源或权威（AlGhamdi，2024）。相比之下，目前知道 ChatGPT 参与其中的人群明确阐明了人工智能角色的界限。虽然这些研究无法直接进行比较（不同的学生、不同的时间段、更广泛的文化中对人工智能的熟悉程度不同），但这种对比是有启发性的：透明度可能有助于将反馈从理所当然的教学行为转变为反思的对象。然而，这种解释仍然是暂时的，需要通过受试者内或受控实验设计进行验证。An earlier study in the same course, conducted under blinded conditions with a different cohort, found that students tended to focus on feedback content without questioning its source or authority (AlGhamdi, 2024). In contrast, the current cohort, who knew ChatGPT was involved, explicitly articulated boundaries around AI's role. While these studies cannot be directly compared (different students, different time periods, different levels of AI familiarity in the broader culture), the contrast is suggestive: transparency may have helped transform feedback from a taken-for-granted instructional act into an object of reflection. However, this interpretation remains tentative and requires verification through within-subject or controlled experimental designs.

5.2.有条件的信任和导师监督5.2. Conditional Trust and Instructor Oversight

在这项研究的参与者中，对 ChatGPT 生成的反馈的信任似乎是有条件的。虽然学生们承认 ChatGPT 反馈的效率和清晰度，但这种信任始终取决于讲师的存在、验证和监督。学生们并没有将人工智能视为一个独立的评估者，而是将其定位为一种补充工具，其输出需要人类解释和情境化。Among participants in this study, trust in ChatGPT-generated feedback appeared to be conditional. While students acknowledged the efficiency and clarity of ChatGPT’s feedback, this trust was consistently framed as contingent upon instructor presence, validation, and oversight. Rather than viewing AI as an independent evaluator, students positioned it as a supplementary tool whose outputs require human interpretation and contextualization.

研究中报告了类似的模式，表明学生有选择地接受人工智能建议，接受一些建议，质疑或忽略其他建议（Chen 等人，2025 年；Yu 等人，2025 年）。在本研究中，意识到人工智能系统是评估者似乎强化了这种选择性参与：参与者不会仅仅因为反馈是通过算法生成的而不加批判地接受反馈。因此，透明度可能鼓励参与者积极询问反馈过程，使他们明确表达对问责制、公平性和教学责任的期望。Comparable patterns have been reported in studies showing that students engage selectively with AI suggestions, accepting some and disputing or ignoring others (Chen et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025). In the present study, awareness that an AI system was the evaluator appeared to intensify this selective engagement: participants did not accept feedback uncritically simply because it had been algorithmically generated. Transparency may therefore have encouraged participants to actively interrogate the feedback process, leading them to articulate expectations regarding accountability, fairness, and pedagogical responsibility.

这种选择性、有条件的信任模式扩展了先前关于反馈关系特征的研究。 Boud 和 Molloy (2013) 认为，反馈不应被概念化为离散的信息传递，而应被视为一种可持续的实践，其中学习者在关系环境中积极参与反馈。 Carless 和 Boud (2018) 类似地将学生的反馈素养定义为一种能力，包括对反馈做出判断，而不是被动地接受反馈。 Henderson、Ryan 和 Phillips（2019）进一步强调，反馈是社会构建的、情境化的，而不是离散的信息传递。本研究的参与者恰恰表现出了这种主动判断的能力，但将其扩展到了一个较少受到实证关注的方向：他们不仅区分要接受哪些反馈，而且区分哪个来源有权评估。这一扩展表明，在人工智能介导的环境中，反馈素养包括一个额外的组成部分，即推理评估权威本身的能力，与推理反馈质量的能力分开。This pattern of selective, conditional trust extends prior work on the relational character of feedback. Boud and Molloy (2013) argued that feedback should be conceptualised not as a discrete information transfer but as a sustainable practice in which learners actively engage with feedback within a relational context. Carless and Boud (2018) similarly framed student feedback literacy as a capacity that includes making judgements about feedback rather than receiving it passively. Henderson, Ryan, and Phillips (2019) further emphasised that feedback is socially constructed and contextually situated rather than a discrete transfer of information. Participants in the present study exhibited precisely this capacity for active judgement, but extended it in a direction that has received less empirical attention: they distinguished not only what feedback to accept but also which source has the authority to evaluate. This extension suggests that in AI-mediated contexts, feedback literacy includes an additional component, the capacity to reason about evaluative authority itself, separate from the capacity to reason about feedback quality.

参与者对讲师监督的重视也反映了文献中记录的更广泛的道德问题，特别是关于将高风险学术决策委托给自动化系统的风险（Uddin 等人，2024 年；Delikoura 等人，2025 年）。学生们担心人工智能系统可能会误解背景、忽视意图或不充分考虑个人的努力。他们认为这些限制需要人类的判断。这些担忧似乎并不反映对创新的抵制，而是似乎代表了对平衡整合的呼吁，其中 GenAI 增强而不是取代了教师的专业知识。Participants' emphasis on instructor oversight also reflects broader ethical concerns documented in the literature, especially regarding the risks of delegating high-stakes academic decisions to automated systems (Uddin et al., 2024; Delikoura et al., 2025). Students expressed concern that AI systems may misinterpret context, overlook intent, or inadequately account for individual effort. These were limitations that they believed necessitated human judgment. These concerns did not appear to reflect resistance to innovation but rather seemed to represent calls for balanced integration in which GenAI augments, rather than replaces, instructor expertise.

在沙特计算机课程中观察到的这种模式与更广泛的沙特背景调查结果一致，即学生接受人工智能反馈并持续偏爱人类监督（Aljasser，2025；Alsofyani 和 Barzanji，2025；Sobaih、Elshaer 和 Hasanein，2024），并通过确定反馈效用和参与者得出的评估权威之间的具体区别来扩展这些发现。This pattern, observed in a Saudi computing course, is consistent with broader Saudi-context findings of student acceptance of AI feedback alongside continued preference for human oversight (Aljasser, 2025; Alsofyani and Barzanji, 2025; Sobaih, Elshaer, and Hasanein, 2024), and extends those findings by identifying the specific distinction between feedback utility and evaluative authority that participants drew.

5.3.透明度的教学效果5.3. Pedagogical Effects of Transparency

明确告知学生 ChatGPT 已经评估了他们的写作，这似乎不仅仅是一种程序性披露；它本身就是一种教学干预。一旦学生意识到人工智能系统生成了评估，他们的反思就不再局限于反馈内容，而是更广泛地审视谁有权评判他们的工作、对评估的信任需要什么，以及人类教师在人工智能介导的评估中应该扮演什么角色。通过明确披露 ChatGPT 生成评估，评估过程从常规教学实践重新构建为反思和探究的对象。Explicitly informing students that ChatGPT had evaluated their writing appeared to function as more than a procedural disclosure; it operated as an instructional intervention in its own right. Once students were aware that an AI system had generated the evaluation, their reflections moved beyond engagement with the feedback content to a broader examination of who holds authority to judge their work, what trust in evaluation requires, and what role human instructors should play in AI-mediated assessment. By explicitly disclosing that ChatGPT generated the evaluation, the assessment process was reframed from a routine instructional practice into an object of reflection and inquiry.

这种模式与研究一致，研究表明 GenAI 参与的透明度可以通过使原本看不见的系统接受审查来激活学习者的元认知和道德意识（Zhang 等人，2024 年；Delikoura 等人，2025 年）。在本研究中，透明度似乎促使学生质疑有关客观性、准确性和公平性的假设，特别是与人工智能解释背景、意图和努力的能力相关的假设。This pattern is consistent with research suggesting that transparency about GenAI involvement can activate learners’ metacognitive and ethical awareness by making otherwise invisible systems open to scrutiny (Zhang et al., 2024; Delikoura et al., 2025). In the present study, transparency appeared to prompt students to question assumptions about objectivity, accuracy, and fairness, particularly in relation to AI’s capacity to interpret context, intent, and effort.

值得注意的是，透明度并没有导致脱离或抵抗。相反，学生们采取了反思的立场，在同意反馈的同时评估其局限性。学生们表达了对过度依赖人工智能、误解风险以及人类判断的必要性的担忧，展示了超越直接绩效结果的道德推理水平。这一发现与强调反思性实践在培养反馈素养中的作用（Carless 和 Boud，2018）以及在教育中负责任地使用 GenAI 的作用（Uddin 等人，2024；Delikoura 等人，2025）的文献产生了共鸣。Notably, transparency did not lead to disengagement or resistance. Instead, students adopted a reflective stance in which agreement with feedback was accompanied by evaluation of its limitations. Students articulated concerns about over-reliance on AI, the risk of misinterpretation, and the necessity of human judgement, demonstrating a level of ethical reasoning that extended beyond immediate performance outcomes. This finding resonates with literature emphasising the role of reflective practices in fostering feedback literacy (Carless and Boud, 2018) and responsible GenAI use in education (Uddin et al., 2024; Delikoura et al., 2025).

对于从业者来说，这一发现具有直接的意义：人工智能参与的透明度可能不仅仅是一项道德义务。它本身可能是一种教学策略，它促使学生批判性地参与反馈，而不是被动地接受反馈。因此，考虑人工智能辅助反馈的教师可能不会将披露视为免责声明，而是将其视为教学提示。For practitioners, this finding has an immediate implication: transparency about AI involvement may be more than an ethical obligation. It may be a pedagogical strategy in its own right, one that prompts students to engage critically with feedback rather than accepting it passively. Instructors considering AI-assisted feedback might therefore frame disclosure not as a disclaimer but as an instructional prompt.

5.4.评估后反思和系统级意识5.4. Post-Assessment Reflection and System-Level Awareness

大多数关于 GenAI 和写作的研究都集中在起草或修订过程中的反思。这项研究要求学生在收到分数后进行反思，这是一个经过深思熟虑的选择。事实证明，这种时间定位是重要的。通过邀请学生在收到 GenAI 分数和反馈后进行反思，该任务将学生的注意力从文本改进转向对评估系统本身进行更广泛的检查。Most research on GenAI and writing focuses on reflection during drafting or revision. This study instead asked students to reflect after receiving their scores, a deliberate choice. This temporal positioning proved consequential. By inviting students to reflect after receiving a GenAI score and feedback, the task shifted students’ attention beyond textual improvement toward a broader examination of the assessment system itself.

学生的思考从“我怎样才能改进这句话？”的问题转移过来。更系统的考虑，例如“谁应该评估我的工作？”以及“人工智能在评分中应该扮演什么角色？”，这表明反思的时间定位是在评估之后而不是评估期间，将学生推理的焦点转移到有关评估系统的更广泛的问题上。Students' reflections moved from questions of 'How can I improve this sentence?' to more systemic considerations such as 'Who should evaluate my work?' and 'What role should AI play in grading?', suggesting that the temporal positioning of reflection, after rather than during evaluation, shifted the focus of student reasoning toward broader questions about the assessment system.

这种评估后的反思似乎并没有减少学生对写作质量的参与。相反，它将写作重新定义为更大的评估生态系统的一部分，在这个生态系统中，工具、标准和人类判断相互作用。学生对公平、误解和问责制的担忧表明，人们逐渐认识到评估是一种社会情境实践，而不是纯粹的技术程序（Henderson 等，2019）。这种认识与文献中的批评产生了共鸣，这些批评警告不要在没有批判性参与的情况下将自动评估标准化（Delikoura 等人，2025）。This post-assessment reflection did not appear to diminish students’ engagement with writing quality. Instead, it reframed writing as part of a larger evaluative ecosystem in which tools, criteria, and human judgment interact. Students' concerns about fairness, misinterpretation, and accountability indicate an emerging awareness of assessment as a socially situated practice rather than a purely technical procedure (Henderson et al., 2019). This awareness resonates with critiques in the literature warning against the normalization of automated evaluation without critical engagement (Delikoura et al., 2025).

本研究中使用的评估后反思任务成本低廉且易于复制。要求学生简要写下他们对人工智能反馈的同意以及他们作为评估者对人工智能的看法，不需要额外的技术，并且可以嵌入到任何使用人工智能反馈工具的课程中。反思性格式似乎呈现了教师无法看到的推理，这表明它值得在人工智能辅助评估环境中得到更广泛的采用The post-assessment reflective task used in this study is inexpensive and easily replicated. Asking students to write briefly about their agreement with AI feedback and their views on AI as an evaluator requires no additional technology and can be embedded in any course that uses AI feedback tools. The reflective format appeared to surface reasoning that would not otherwise be visible to instructors, suggesting it merits wider adoption in AI-assisted assessment contexts

研究结果初步表明，评估后反思可能会减轻与 GenAI 过度依赖相关的一些风险，尽管这一假设需要实证检验。反思似乎并没有将 GenAI 的反馈视为无可置疑的权威，而是鼓励学生将人工智能输出置于情境中，认识到其局限性，并重申教师判断的作用。在此过程中，反思不仅可以帮助学生了解反馈如何运作，还可以帮助学生了解评估性决策是如何做出和论证的。The findings tentatively suggest that post-assessment reflection could potentially mitigate some risks associated with GenAI over-reliance, though this hypothesis requires empirical testing. Rather than positioning GenAI feedback as an unquestioned authority, reflection appeared to encourage students to contextualize AI outputs, recognize their limitations, and reaffirm the role of instructor judgment. In doing so, reflection may help students understand not only how feedback operates but also how evaluative decisions are made and justified.

6. 局限性和未来的工作6. Limitations and Future Work

这项研究有几个重要的局限性，在解释研究结果时应考虑这些局限性，并限制了结论的普遍性。This study has several important limitations that should be considered when interpreting the findings and that constrain the generalizability of the conclusions.

首先，分析样本由 19 名注册学生中完成反思性回答的 13 名学生组成，代表课程人群中一小部分可能是自我选择的子集。该样本属于通常认为足以进行具有同质、有界样本的反射性主题分析的范围（Guest et al., 2006; Braun & Clarke, 2021; Malterud et al., 2016），并且主题饱和在数据集中显得很明显，所有参与者都阐明主题 1 和四个主题中的三个在超过一半的数据集中重复出现。然而，同质样本中的饱和度并不能证明相同的主题会出现在具有不同特征的样本中。First, the analytic sample consisted of 13 students who completed reflective responses out of 19 enrolled, representing a small and potentially self-selected subset of the course population. This sample falls within the range commonly considered adequate for reflexive thematic analysis with a homogeneous, bounded sample (Guest et al., 2006; Braun & Clarke, 2021; Malterud et al., 2016), and thematic saturation appeared evident within the dataset, with all participants articulating Theme 1 and three of four themes recurring across more than half of the dataset. Nevertheless, saturation within a homogeneous sample does not establish that the same themes would emerge in samples with different characteristics.

该样本在重要方面在人口统计上也具有同质性。所有参与者都是沙特一所公立大学的男性本科计算机学生，在一个学期内，在一个课程部分，由一名教师授课。这种同质性限制了研究结果向其他人群的转移，包括女性、非计算学科的学生、其他机构的学生以及来自不同文化背景的学生。The sample is also demographically homogeneous in important respects. All participants were male undergraduate computing students at a single Saudi public university, in a single course section, taught by a single instructor, during a single semester. This homogeneity restricts the transferability of findings to other populations, including women, students in non-computing disciplines, students at other institutions, and students from different cultural contexts.

由于研究人员既担任课程讲师又担任首席研究员，因此学生可能会提供他们认为预期或有利的回答，尽管他们保证Because the researcher served as both the course instructor and principal investigator, students may have provided responses they perceived as expected or favorable, despite assurances that

反思不会影响成绩。第 3.5 节描述了为解决此风险而采取的缓解程序。尽管如此，不能完全排除反应偏差的可能性。reflections would not affect grades. The mitigation procedures adopted to address this risk are described in Section 3.5. Nevertheless, the possibility of response bias cannot be entirely ruled out.

这项透明研究与早期盲法研究（AlGhamdi，2024）之间的比较受到几个混杂因素的限制。这两项研究涉及不同的学生群体，在不同的学年进行，并且针对 GenAI 的文化和机构熟悉程度不同。在早期的研究中，ChatGPT 相对较新，学生接触的机会有限；截至本研究开展时，GenAI 工具已在高等教育中标准化。这些差异使得我们无法将透明度的影响与群体效应、时间效应或更广泛的学生对人工智能态度的转变分开。The comparison between this transparent study and the earlier blinded study (AlGhamdi, 2024) is limited by several confounding factors. The two studies involved different student cohorts, were conducted in different academic years, and occurred against different levels of cultural and institutional familiarity with GenAI. In the earlier study, ChatGPT was relatively new, and student exposure was limited; by the time of the current study, GenAI tools had become normalized in higher education. These differences make it impossible to isolate the effect of transparency from cohort effects, temporal effects, or broader shifts in student attitudes toward AI.

图 6 中呈现的模型是作为观察到的模式的理论综合而不是作为经过测试的因果路径提供。对所提出阶段的实证验证，特别是从教师权威到人工智能集成的反馈循环，需要通过纵向和比较设计进行进一步研究。The model presented in Figure 6 is offered as a theoretical synthesis of observed patterns rather than as a tested causal pathway. Empirical validation of the proposed stages, particularly the feedback loop from instructor authority back to AI integration, requires further research with longitudinal and comparative designs.

未来的研究应该通过采用更大、更多样化的样本、将混合方法或实验设计与对照组相结合、纵向跟踪感知以及跨多个学科、机构和文化背景检查人工智能介导的评估来解决这些局限性。由研究人员独立于教学环境进行的研究也将增强研究结果的可信度。在这些方向中，对于测试本研究中提出的概念模型最重要的是受试者内或实验设计，比较同一队列中盲法和透明评估条件下学生的反映。Future research should address these limitations by employing larger and more diverse samples, incorporating mixed-method or experimental designs with comparison groups, tracking perceptions longitudinally, and examining AI-mediated assessment across multiple disciplines, institutions, and cultural contexts. Studies conducted by researchers independent of the instructional context would also strengthen the credibility of findings. Of these directions, the most consequential for testing the conceptual model proposed in this study would be within-subject or experimental designs comparing student reflection under blinded and transparent evaluation conditions in the same cohort.

七、结论7. Conclusion

这项研究考察了学生在被明确告知人工智能系统已经评估了他们的写作时的反应。在本研究的有限背景和样本中，参与者的反思表明，人工智能参与的披露促进了对评估的批判性参与，而不是被动接受，注意力超出了文本本身，延伸到了权威、信任和人类指导者的角色问题。目前的设计无法证明透明度导致了这种参与；然而，参与者的想法是一致的。在整个数据集中，ChatGPT 生成的反馈因其清晰性和实用性而受到认可，特别是在识别表面水平的写作问题方面。然而，这种感知到的效用并没有转化为人们接受 GenAI 作为合法或自主的评估者。相反，参与者提出了一种有条件的信任形式，其中人工智能被视为一种支持性反馈机制，但不能替代人类监督。教师的判断似乎仍然是参与者对评估的公平性、责任感和信任感的核心。这项研究的核心概念贡献是反馈效用和评估权威之间的区别：人工智能在评估中的两个功能，即使在同一评估事件中，学生似乎也会单独评估。This study examined how students respond when they are explicitly informed that an AI system has evaluated their writing. Within the bounded context and sample of this study, participants’ reflections suggest that disclosure of AI involvement prompted critical engagement with the evaluation rather than passive acceptance, with attention extending beyond the text itself to questions of authority, trust, and the role of human instructors. The present design cannot demonstrate that transparency caused this engagement; the pattern, however, was consistent across participant reflections. Across the dataset, ChatGPT-generated feedback was recognised for its clarity and usefulness, particularly in identifying surface-level writing issues. This perceived utility, however, did not translate into acceptance of GenAI as a legitimate or autonomous evaluator. Participants instead articulated a form of conditional trust in which AI was valued as a supportive feedback mechanism but not as a replacement for human oversight. Instructor judgement appeared to remain central to participants’ perceptions of fairness, accountability, and trust in assessment. The central conceptual contribution of this study is the distinction between feedback utility and evaluative authority: two functions of AI in assessment that students appear to evaluate separately even within the same evaluation event.

回到研究问题，该研究在样本和设计的背景下得出以下答案。关于 RQ1（学生在知道 ChatGPT 产生的反馈后如何评估 ChatGPT 生成的反馈），参与者认为反馈清晰、结构化，并且对于表面级别的修改有用，特别是对于语法、句子结构和组织，同时质疑人工智能系统作为他们工作的最终评估者的适当性。关于 RQ2（学生如何推理 ChatGPT 作为评估者的角色），参与者在反馈效用和评估权威之间做出了一致的区分：人工智能被认为是一种有用的反馈机制，但被拒绝作为自主评分者，参与者强调在做出任何评分决定之前，教师对人工智能输出进行审查的必要性。关于 RQ3Returning to the research questions, the study yields the following answers within the context of the sample and design. With respect to RQ1 (how students evaluate ChatGPT-generated feedback once they know ChatGPT produced it), participants regarded the feedback as clear, structured, and useful for surface-level revision, particularly for grammar, sentence structure, and organisation, while questioning the appropriateness of an AI system as the final evaluator of their work. With respect to RQ2 (how students reason about ChatGPT's role as an evaluator), participants drew a consistent distinction between feedback utility and evaluative authority: AI was accepted as a useful feedback mechanism but rejected as an autonomous grader, with participants emphasising the necessity of instructor review of AI outputs before any grading decision. With respect to RQ3

（学生反思中出现的更广泛的教学和制度问题），参与者对人工智能潜在错误的公平性、人工智能无法复制的人类教学的对话和个人关注功能以及将评估委托给人工智能时高等教育的制度目的提出了担忧。(the broader pedagogical and institutional concerns that emerge in students' reflections), participants raised concerns about fairness in light of potential AI errors, about the dialogical and individually attentive functions of human teaching that AI cannot replicate, and about the institutional purpose of higher education when assessment is delegated to AI.

通过在评估后进行反思，该研究揭示了学生的注意力从个人文本修正转向有关评估实践的更广泛的系统问题。这种模式指出了反思活动在帮助学生批判性地参与自动化评估过程方面的潜在作用，这种可能性值得进一步研究。在这种情况下，透明度不仅是一种道德考虑，而且也是支持批判性参与的潜在教学策略。By situating reflection after assessment, the study revealed a shift in students' focus from individual textual correction to broader systemic questions about evaluation practices. This pattern points to the potential role of reflective activities in helping students critically engage with automated assessment processes, a possibility that warrants further investigation. In this context, transparency served not only as an ethical consideration but also as a potential instructional strategy that supported critical engagement.

虽然这项研究是在特定的课程背景下进行的，但其研究结果为教育工作者进行人工智能介导的写作评估提供了潜在的可转移的见解。随着 GenAI 在学术环境中变得普遍，开放和深思熟虑的整合更有可能产生富有成效的反应，而不是禁止。为了支持这一目标，图 7 提出了一个将 GenAI 集成到写作评估中的拟议框架。该框架围绕三个指导原则组织：透明度、人工调解和反思实践，对教师、评估设计和学生具有特定的影响。While this study is situated within a specific course context, its findings offer potentially transferable insights for educators navigating AI-mediated writing assessment. As GenAI becomes common in academic settings, productive responses are more likely to emerge from open and thoughtful integration than from prohibition. 对于教师在评估中使用 GenAI 的影响是暂时的，但具有启发性。披露人工智能的参与，而不是破坏评估过程，可能会为对权威、信任以及自动评估合适的条件进行富有成效的反思创造机会。然而，这些观察是从一门课程、一个学期和十三名参与者中得出的。在得出更有力的结论之前，有必要进行跨其他学科、机构和文化环境以及不同 GenAI 平台的研究。随着 GenAI 能力和学生熟悉程度的不断发展，人工智能介导的评估提出的实践和伦理问题将需要持续的经验和教学关注。The implications for instructors using GenAI in assessment are tentative but suggestive. Disclosure about AI involvement, rather than undermining the assessment process, may create opportunities for productive reflection on authority, trust, and the conditions under which automated evaluation is appropriate. These observations, however, are drawn from a single course, a single semester, and thirteen participants. Studies across other disciplines, institutions, and cultural settings, and across different GenAI platforms, would be necessary before stronger conclusions could be advanced. As GenAI capabilities and student familiarity continue to evolve, the practical and ethical questions raised by AI-mediated assessment will require sustained empirical and pedagogical attention.
