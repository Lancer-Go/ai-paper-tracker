简介Introduction

制造系统越来越依赖密集、异构的传感器和自动化生产线，这些传感器和自动化生产线必须在严格的质量和产量限制下保持连续运行。实时检测异常并准确识别其根本原因对于防止代价高昂的报废、停机和潜在缺陷至关重要。然而，传统的分析流程将异常检测、因果发现和决策视为孤立的阶段。传感器为黑盒模型提供数据，专家解释警报，并且以临时方式进行干预，从而导致诊断延迟、可追溯性有限并降低操作员信任度。由于多模式和不完整的生产数据，可靠的根本原因分析 (RCA) 仍然很困难Manufacturing systems increasingly rely on dense, heterogeneous sensors and automated production lines that must maintain continuous operation under strict quality and yield constraints. Real-time detection of anomalies and accurate identification of their root causes are essential to prevent costly scrap, downtime, and latent defects. However, traditional analytic pipelines treat anomaly detection, causal discovery, and decision-making as isolated stages. Sensors feed black-box models, experts interpret alerts, and interventions occur in an ad hoc manner, leading to delayed diagnosis, limited traceability, and reduced operator trust. Reliable root cause analysis (RCA) remains difficult due to multimodal and incomplete production data(Kuo et al. 2021; Pietsch et al. 2024)，部分已知且依赖于上下文的因果关系, partially known and context-dependent causal relationships(Ko et al. 2023; Pearl 2019)，以及对可解释、可审计、操作员在环系统的需求，以确保安全和问责制, and the need for explainable, auditable, operator-in-the-loop systems that ensure safety and accountability(Sofianidis et al. 2021; Trivedi et al. 2024)。大规模部署需要模块化、可互操作和可组合的架构来支持工业管道中的稳健集成. Large-scale deployment requires modular, interoperable, and composable architectures to support robust integration in industrial pipelines(Ribeiro and Gomes 2021).

Structure-learning algorithms characterize conditional independence and score-based approaches to infer causal graphs from observational data(Spirtes et al. 2000; Chickering 2002)。多代理系统和面向服务的代理框架展示了分布式推理和标准化通信协议如何在工业环境中实现模块化、可扩展的自动化. Multi-agent systems and service-oriented agent frameworks have demonstrated how distributed reasoning and standardized communication protocols enable modular, scalable automation in industrial settings(Bellifemine et al. 2000; Wooldridge 2009)。最近，大型语言模型已经实现了将推理和工具使用相结合的实用方法，将基于模型的规划与外部工具集成以扩展其功能. Recently, large language models have enabled practical methods that combine reasoning and tool use, integrating model-based planning with external tools to extend their capabilities(Yao et al. 2022; Schick et al. 2023)。这些发展促进了原则性因果推理、神经符号领域先验和代理编排的综合，以产生可解释的、可操作的 RCA 系统。尽管取得了令人鼓舞的进步，但当前的工业副驾驶员和诊断平台缺乏统一的、基于标准的多代理框架，该框架（i）集成预处理和多模式数据，（ii）用领域规则限制因果分析，（iii）产生透明且可解释的结果，（iv）支持操作员驱动的运行时选择、审查和重新规划，以及（v）能够轻松扩展到新的用例和制造任务。. These developments motivate the synthesis of principled causal inference, neurosymbolic domain priors, and agentic orchestration to produce interpretable, operational RCA systems. Despite promising advances, current industrial copilots and diagnostic platforms lack a unified, standards-based, multi-agent framework that (i) integrates preprocessing and multi-modal data, (ii) constrains causal analysis with domain rules, (iii) produces transparent and explainable results, (iv) supports operator-driven selection, review, and replanning at runtime, and (v) enables easy extensibility to new use cases and manufacturing tasks.

The LangGraph-based Workflow Engine is positioned between the UI and Agent layers.

代理层：Agent Layer:

包含两类代理：通用代理（可跨域和应用程序重用）和专用代理（针对特定因果诊断任务量身定制）。所有代理均实现为 FastAPI 微服务，由代理卡进行描述，并通过 A2A 通信与基于注册表的编排进行协调。Comprises two categories of agents: common agents, which are reusable across domains and applications, and specialized agents, which are tailored for specific causal diagnostic tasks. All agents are implemented as FastAPI microservices, described by agent cards, and coordinated through A2A communication with registry-based orchestration.

实用层Utility Layer

提供支持代理操作的 MCP 工具、服务和资源。服务集成了领域信息服务，例如流程本体、LLM、知识图、策划的技术手册和规则库。资源是工件，包括代理卡、预处理文档、存储的因果图、RCA 结果和预先计算的因果知识。Provides MCP tools, services, and resources that underpin agent operations. Services integrate domain-informed services such as process ontologies, LLMs, knowledge graphs, curated technical manuals, and rule bases. Resources are artifacts, including agent cards, preprocessed documents, stored causal graphs, RCA results, and precomputed causal knowledge.
