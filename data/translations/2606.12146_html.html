摘要Abstract

旋转位置嵌入（RoPE）在 Transformer 模型中被广泛采用，但其向高维领域的扩展缺乏统一的理论表述。大多数现有方法要么沿着每个轴独立地应用旋转，要么根据经验混合频率，这限制了跨维度相互作用并产生依赖于方向的表示。为了解决这些限制，我们建议Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) is widely adopted in Transformer models, yet its extension to high-dimensional domains lacks a unified theoretical formulation. Most existing approaches either apply rotations independently along each axis or empirically mix frequencies, which limits cross-dimensional interactions and yields direction-dependent representations. To address these limitations, we proposenD-RoPE，RoPE 到任意维度的无分解推广。从连续希尔伯特空间中的平移不变公式，我们推导出各向同性的谱条件，需要将位置和频率视为耦合, a decomposition-free generalization of RoPE to arbitrary dimensions. We instantiate this formulation with a multi-scale regular-simplex wave-vector design, which provides non-degenerate spatial coverage and a symmetric, directionally balanced second-order response. Experiments across images, videos, and point clouds demonstrate consistent performance gains and improved generalization in high-dimensional settings.

1 简介Introduction

理解空间关系在构建世界的内部模型方面发挥着至关重要的作用，使智能系统能够在复杂的高维认知地图中组织知识Understanding spatial relations plays a crucial role in constructing an internal model of the world, enabling intelligent systems to organize knowledge within complex, high-dimensional cognitive maps(Tolman, 1948; Gornet and Thomson, 2024)。从建模的角度来看，空间关系可以自然地表述为学习位置之间的成对相互作用. From a modeling perspective, spatial relations can be naturally formulated as learning pairwise interactions between positions(Gao et al., 2019)，这与自注意力机制很好地吻合。因此，建立在自注意力基础上的 Transformer 架构表现出了捕捉高维空间中复杂关系结构的强大能力，这一特性被认为是其在广泛任务中取得成功的基础, which aligns well with the self-attention mechanism. Accordingly, the Transformer architecture, built upon self-attention, has demonstrated a strong ability to capture complex relational structures in high-dimensional spaces, a property believed to underlie its success across a wide range of tasks(Reinauer et al., 2021).

然而，这种关系建模能力本身并不编码位置，因为自注意力是排列不变的，并且除非明确提供位置信息，否则无法区分不同的空间排列。因此，大量研究集中在增强位置嵌入，以使 Transformers 更好地捕获这些信息However, this relational modeling ability does not by itself encode position, since self-attention is permutation-invariant and cannot distinguish between different spatial arrangements unless positional information is explicitly provided. As a result, substantial research has focused on enhancing position embedding to enable Transformers to better capture this information(Li et al., 2021; Raffel et al., 2020; Press et al., 2021)。在这些进步中，RoPE. Among these advancements, RoPE(Su et al., 2024)已经获得了极大的关注。由于其在长上下文建模方面的强大性能，RoPE已被多种大型语言模型采用，包括LLaMA系列has gained significant attention. Owing to its strong performance in long-context modeling, RoPE has been adopted in several large language models, including LLaMA series(Touvron et al., 2023; Grattafiori et al., 2024)和Qwen系列and Qwen series(Wang et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2025)。然而，RoPE 最初是为语言任务而设计的，其向高维空间的扩展面临着固有的局限性。特别是，相对位置在高维域中变得越来越复杂：例如，在 2D 图像中，位置编码必须联合表示水平和垂直偏移，而不是单个连续距离. Nevertheless, RoPE was originally designed for language tasks, and its extension to higher-dimensional spaces faces inherent limitations. In particular, relative positions become increasingly complex in higher-dimensional domains: for example, in 2D images, a positional encoding must jointly represent horizontal and vertical offsets rather than a single sequential distance(Shaw et al., 2018)。虽然多项努力已使 RoPE 适应特定的高维设置——涵盖图像、视频、空间表示、3D 几何和多模态输入. While several efforts have adapted RoPE to specific high-dimensional settings—spanning images, videos, spatial representations, 3D geometry, and multi-modal inputs(Heo et al., 2024; Wei et al., 2025; Mai et al., 2020; Ji et al., 2025; Lu et al., 2024)- 仍然缺乏将 RoPE 扩展到轴向结构之外的统一框架。—a unified framework for extending RoPE beyond axis-wise constructions is still lacking.

当将位置编码扩展到一维之外时，常见的方法是将位置分解为独立的轴并分别对每个维度进行编码。然而，这种轴向公式隐含地假设多维位移可以在不考虑几何耦合的情况下进行分解。考虑对角线移位：将其建模为独立的水平和垂直旋转人为地分割单个相干位移，破坏跨维度相互作用并导致注意力计算中相对相位不一致。这揭示了一个更深层次的原则：位置不应该被拆解，而应该被编码为统一的向量（见图 1）。When extending positional encoding beyond 1D, a common approach is to decompose positions into independent axes and encode each dimension separately.
