摘要Abstract

我们推出了 VLA Foundry，这是一个开源框架，它将 LLM、VLM 和 VLA 培训统一在一个代码库中。大多数开源 VLA 工作专注于动作训练阶段，通常将不兼容的预训练管道拼接在一起。 VLA Foundry 相反提供了一个具有端到端控制的共享训练堆栈，从语言预训练到动作专家微调。 VLA Foundry 支持从头开始训练和 Hugging Face 的预训练骨干网。为了展示我们框架的实用性，我们训练并发布了两种类型的模型：第一个模型通过我们的法学硕士从头开始完全训练We present VLA Foundry, an open-source framework that unifies LLM, VLM, and VLA training in a single codebase. Most open-source VLA efforts specialize on the action training stage, often stitching together incompatible pretraining pipelines. VLA Foundry instead provides a shared training stack with end-to-end control, from language pretraining to action-expert fine-tuning. VLA Foundry supports both from-scratch training and pretrained backbones from Hugging Face. To demonstrate the utility of our framework, we train and release two types of models: the first trained fully from scratch through our LLM→\rightarrow视觉语言管理VLM→\rightarrowVLA 管道和第二条管道基于预训练的 Qwen3-VLVLA pipeline and the second built on the pretrained Qwen3-VL[bai2025qwen3]骨干。我们在 LBM Eval 上评估两种模型的闭环策略性能backbone. We evaluate closed-loop policy performance of both models on LBM Eval[lbm_eval2025]，一个开放数据、开源模拟器。我们还为模拟器和 STEP 做出了可用性改进, an open-data, open-source simulator. We also contribute usability improvements to the simulator and the STEP[snyder2025step]更方便公众使用的分析工具。 在名义评估环境中，我们完全开放的从头开始的模型与我们之前的闭源工作相当analysis tools for easier public use. 1 简介Introduction

机器人基础模型正在快速发展，有许多系统Robotics foundation models are advancing at a rapid pace, with many systems[black2025pi05, intelligence2025pi06star, shukor2025smolvla, molmoact2025, dreamzero_ye2026worldactionmodelszeroshot, lin2026holobrain]展示了几年前似乎遥不可及的能力。随着前沿发展速度加快，支持严格研究所需的工具也必须跟上步伐。 许多高影响力的问题——关于数据扩展、主干预训练以及机器人和非机器人数据之间的相互作用——需要规模（计算、数据等），以及允许用户完全控制模型和训练管道的不同部分的模块化算法基础设施。 然而，大多数现有的代码库要么没有经过大规模的广泛测试demonstrating capabilities that would have seemed out of reach just a few years ago. As the frontier moves faster, the tooling required to support rigorous research must keep pace. Many high-impact questions – about data scaling, backbone pretraining, and the interplay between robotics and non-robotics data – require both scale (compute, data, etc.), as well as modular algorithmic infrastructure that allow users full control over different parts of the model and training pipeline. However, most existing codebases have either not been extensively tested at scale[galahad2025vlascratch]，或者主要专注于模型发布, or are largely focused on model releases[molmoact2025, physicalintelligence2025openpi, gr00tn1_2025]因此与特定的算法决策紧密耦合，限制了研究的灵活性。and therefore tightly coupled to specific algorithmic decisions, limiting research flexibility.

与此同时，数据稀缺仍然是机器人技术的基本瓶颈。相对于用于语言和视觉模型的数据，机器人交互数据受到严重限制，特别是在多样性和每个令牌的信号密度方面At the same time, data scarcity remains a fundamental bottleneck in robotics. Robot interaction data is severely constrained relative to data used for language and vision models, especially in diversity and in signal density per token[bandaru2025foundation]。随着机器人政策的不断扩大，非机器人数据的相对重要性只会增长. As robot policies continue to scale, the relative importance of non-robotics data only grows[lin2026systematic]。尽管存在这种数据差异，但大多数开源 VLA 框架都狭隘地关注动作训练阶段，将上游数据配方视为固定或超出范围。这种分离是有问题的：LLM 和 VLM 预训练期间做出的数据决策对下游机器人性能有直接影响。探索设计空间需要一个框架，将整个流程（从预训练到策略学习）视为单个可控系统。. Despite this data disparity, most open-source VLA frameworks focus narrowly on the action training stage, treating the upstream data recipe as fixed or out-of-scope. Such separation is problematic: data decisions made during LLM and VLM pretraining have direct consequences for downstream robotics performance. Exploring the design space requires a framework that treats the entire pipeline, from pretraining to policy learning, as a single, controllable system.

VLA Foundry is a unified, open-source framework with a shared data-loading and training stack that spans LLM, VLM, and VLA training in a single codebase, giving practitioners control over the entire pipeline – from backbone pretraining to action-expert fine-tuning. Because every stage shares the same infrastructure, researchers can co-train across modalities, mix datasets, and prototype new architectures without stitching together disparate tools. The framework natively supports pretrained backbones from Hugging Face, and its modular, configuration-driven design lets users swap architectures, data pipelines, and training recipes through simple command-line or YAML changes.

2.1 LLM/VLM 培训框架LLM/VLM Training Frameworks

大型语言模型 (LLM) 构成了现代多模态系统的基础，提供可扩展的序列建模功能、强大的语言表示和紧急推理能力。早期的工作确立了基于 Transformer 的语言模型扩展的有效性，而后续的工作主要集中在提高培训效率、透明度和可重复性上。威震天-LM 等项目Large Language Models (LLMs) form the foundation of modern multimodal systems, providing scalable sequence modeling capabilities, strong linguistic representations, and emergent reasoning abilities. Early work established the effectiveness of the scaling of transformer-based language models while subsequent efforts have largely focused on improving training efficiency, transparency, and reproducibility. Projects such as Megatron-LM[megatron-lm], 深速, DeepSpeed[rasley2020deepspeed]、GPT Neox, and GPT Neox[gpt-neox-library]引入了分布式训练策略，可以扩展到数千亿个参数。更新且易于访问的开放培训计划，包括 OpenLMintroduced distributed training strategies that enabled scaling to hundreds of billions of parameters. More recent and accessible open training initiatives, including OpenLM[open_lm], 奥尔莫, Olmo[olmo20242olmo2furious], 法学硕士360, LLM360[liu2023llm360]，及其后续型号K2, and its follow-up K2 model[liu2025k2]通过发布训练数据、代码、中间检查点和日志来强调全堆栈透明度。 FastLLM 等补充工作emphasize full stack transparency by releasing training data, code, intermediate checkpoints, and logs. Complementary efforts such as FastLLM[fastllm2024]提供在更有限的计算预算下训练竞争模型的实用方法，而 nanoGPT 等教育存储库provide practical recipes for training competitive models under more constrained compute budgets, while educational repositories such as nanoGPT[karpathy2022nanogpt]和从零开始的法学硕士and LLMs from scratch[build-llms-from-scratch-book]进一步降低了复制端到端 LLM 培训渠道的障碍。 DCLM等数据集框架have further lowered the barrier to reproducing end-end-end LLM training pipelines. Dataset frameworks such as DCLM[li2024datacomp]和FineWeband FineWeb[penedo2024fineweb]提供高质量的语言数据集。这些工作共同凸显了向可复制和模块化法学硕士培训框架的转变。provide high quality language datasets. Together, these works highlight a shift towards reproducible and modular LLM training frameworks.

视觉语言模型（VLM）框架还必须解决跨模式表示学习和异构数据集成问题。 VLM 中常见且突出的设计模式是将预训练的视觉编码器与语言模型主干结合起来，中间模块负责对齐视觉和文本表示。 OpenFlamingo 等框架Vision-language model (VLM) frameworks must additionally address cross-modal representation learning and heterogeneous data integration. A common and prominent design pattern in VLMs is to couple a pretrained vision encoder with language model backbone, with intermediate modules responsible for aligning visual and text representations. Frameworks such as OpenFlamingo[awadalla2023openflamingo]通过提供用于交错图像-文本序列构建、多模式批处理和各种架构选择的基础设施来实施这种方法，从而能够在网络规模数据上训练自回归 VLM。同样，LLaVAoperationalize this approach by providing infrastructure for interleaved image-text sequence construction, multimodal batching, and various architecture choices that enable the training of autoregressive VLMs on web-scale data. Similarly, LLaVA[liu2023visual]为多模式指令调整提供简化的管道，包括数据格式化、视觉特征提取和监督微调阶段。offers a streamlined pipeline for multimodal instruction tuning, including data formatting, visual feature extraction, and supervised fine-tuning stages.

其他框架强调模块化和可组合性作为一流的设计原则。 BLIP-2Other frameworks emphasize modularity and composability as first-class design principles. BLIP-2[li2023blip2]引入了模块化桥接组件（Q-former），可将视觉和语言主干解耦，从而允许每个主干独立重用。棱柱形 VLMintroduces a modular bridging component (Q-former) that decouples vision and language backbones, allowing each to be reused independently. Prismatic VLMs[karamcheti2024prismatic]通过围绕可互换组件明确构建框架来扩展这一想法，从而实现对视觉编码器、语言模型和训练混合的受控实验。实习生VLextend this idea by explicitly structuring the framework around interchangeable components, enabling controlled experimentation over vision encoders, language models, and training mixtures. InternVL[chen2024internvl]进一步展示了如何扩展此类模块化设计，将大型视觉编码器和分阶段对齐策略合并到统一的管道中。奎文further demonstrates how such modular designs can be scaled, incorporating large vision encoders and staged alignment strategies within a unified pipeline. Qwen[Qwen-VL, bai2025qwen3]在开源代码库中提供最先进的 VLM 功能。补充主要关注模型架构、数据集框架（例如 DataComp）的这一工作线offers state-of-the-art VLM capabilities in an open-source codebase. Complementing this line of work that primarily focuses on model architecture, dataset frameworks such as DataComp[gadre2023datacomp]为构建和评估大规模图像文本数据集提供标准化管道，解决可重复多模态训练中的关键瓶颈。provide standardized pipelines for construction and evaluating large-scale image-text datasets, addressing a critical bottleneck in reproducible multimodal training.

在 LLM 和 VLM 设置中，这些框架公开了设计的几个关键维度，包括数据管道（例如预标记化与动态处理）、模型组成（例如整体架构与模块化架构）和训练编排（例如分发执行、分阶段与联合优化）。因此，现有系统涵盖从高度优化的分布式训练后端到更加模块化、面向研究的框架，以促进模型架构和数据混合的实验。Across LLM and VLM settings, these frameworks expose several key dimensions of design including data pipelines (e.g. pre-tokenized vs. dynamic processing), model composition (e.g. monolithic vs. modular architectures), and training orchestration (e.g. distribution execution, staged vs joint optimization). As a result, existing systems span a spectrum from highly optimized distributed training backends to more modular, research oriented frameworks that facilitate experimentation with model architecture and data mixtures.

5 结论Conclusions

局限性Limitations

这个初始版本反映了范围上的深思熟虑的选择，而不是框架限制。我们报告的评估仅限于一小部分实施例上的闭环 LBM 模拟，并且我们尚未包含真实的硬件数据； VLA Foundry 的共享评估和数据加载器抽象经过精心设计，可以在不触及核心训练堆栈的情况下添加额外的模拟套件（例如 LIBERO、SimplerEnv、RoboCasa）、新实施例和机器人评估。本报告中的所有实验均使用流量匹配动作头；虽然代码库中已经实现了扩散策略等附加头，但动作头是一个模块化组件，集成更多变体（例如自回归离散动作标记化）只需要一个新的头模块，而不需要更改训练循环或数据管道。最后，尽管 VLA Foundry 公开了具有概率多模态混合的完整 LLM-VLM-VLA 管道，但我们尚未描述跨阶段的最佳数据配方，也没有解决具体代理的安全性、对齐或故障模式检测问题。我们将这些视为 VLA Foundry 完全有能力实现的开放研究方向，并邀请社区在此基础上进行探索。This initial release reflects deliberate choices in scope rather than framework constraints. Our reported evaluation is restricted to closed-loop LBM simulation on a narrow slice of embodiments, and we do not yet include real-hardware numbers; VLA Foundry’s shared evaluation and dataloader abstractions are designed so that additional simulation suites (e.g., LIBERO, SimplerEnv, RoboCasa), new embodiments, and on-robot evaluation can be added without touching the core training stack. All experiments in this report use a flow-matching action head; while additional heads such as a diffusion policy are already implemented in the codebase, the action head is a modular component and integrating further variants – for example, autoregressive discrete action tokenizations – requires only a new head module rather than changes to the training loop or data pipeline. Finally, although VLA Foundry exposes the full LLM–VLM–VLA pipeline with probabilistic multi-modal mixing, we do not yet characterize optimal data recipes across stages, nor do we address safety, alignment, or failure-mode detection for embodied agents. We view these as open research directions that VLA Foundry is well-positioned to enable, and we invite the community to build on it to explore them.

6.1.1 模型训练循环Model Training Loop

VLA Foundry 中的训练是在单个训练循环中完成的，该循环在管道中的每个模型阶段共享：LLM 预训练、VLM 预训练和 VLA 微调。 VLA Foundry 采用以数据为中心的方法。它将训练预算表示为多个样本而不是多个步骤，因此不同批量大小或 GPU 数量的运行保持直接可比较。Training in VLA Foundry is done in a single training loop that is shared across every model stage in the pipeline: LLM pretraining, VLM pretraining, and VLA fine-tuning. VLA Foundry takes a data-centered approach. It expresses training budgets as a number of samples instead of a number of steps, so that runs at different batch sizes or GPU counts remain directly comparable.

训练循环故意与模型无关。将批次解包为输入和目标，屏蔽损失，并将模型输出减少为标量，这些操作附加到每个模型类，而不是烘焙到循环中，因此相同的训练路径驱动LLM从原始文本学习，VLM从图像标题对学习，以及VLA学习去噪动作。该循环与用户期望的分布式训练原语完美地组合在一起：带有可选 CPU 卸载的 FSDP、混合精度执行、梯度累积、梯度检查点、The training loop is deliberately model-agnostic. Unpacking a batch into inputs and targets, masking the loss, and reducing model outputs to a scalar is attached to each model class rather than baked into the loop, so the same training pathway drives an LLM learning from raw text, a VLM learning from image–caption pairs, and a VLA learning to denoise actions. The loop composes cleanly with the distributed training primitives users expect: FSDP with optional CPU offloading, mixed-precision execution, gradient accumulation, gradient checkpointing,火炬编译torch.compile，以及权重的指数移动平均值。, and exponential moving average of weights.
