反射：为反应关键操作启用快速和预测性的视觉-语言-动作模型Reflex: Enabling Fast and Predictive Vision-Language-Action Models for Reaction-Critical Manipulation

Yuxuan Chen Affiliation:  Shanghai Jiao Tong University chen_yuxuan@sjtu.edu.cn    Wanruo Zhang Affiliation:  Shanghai Jiao Tong University chen_yuxuan@sjtu.edu.cn       Xiao Li Affiliation:  Shanghai Jiao Tong University chen_yuxuan@sjtu.edu.cn
摘要Abstract

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型最近在机器人操作方面取得了可喜的性能。然而，现有的基准主要评估静态操作任务的泛化能力，而很大程度上忽略了动态交互场景。为了解决这一差距，我们提出Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have recently achieved promising performance in robotic manipulation. However, existing benchmarks mainly evaluate generalization on static manipulation tasks and largely overlook dynamic interaction scenarios. To address this gap, we present反射凳ReflexBench，反应关键操作的基准。 ReflexBench 包含六个动态任务，并引入了一个评估框架，该框架将模拟器步进与机器人控制解耦，同时支持同步和异步推理下的可配置延迟。基于 ReflexBench，我们建议, a benchmark for reaction-critical manipulation. ReflexBench contains six dynamic tasks and introduces an evaluation framework that decouples simulator stepping from robot control while supporting configurable latency under synchronous and asynchronous inference. Building upon ReflexBench, we propose反射VLAReflexVLA，一种高效的 VLA 模型，专为反应关键操作而设计，无需大规模机器人数据预训练。 ReflexVLA 通过视觉主干内的潜在未来预测和多帧时间融合增强时间推理，同时通过批量视觉编码和 CUDA 图形重播减少部署延迟。实验表明，ReflexVLA 不断提高动态操纵性能，同时在标准静态操纵基准上保持有竞争力的准确性，而真实世界的实验进一步证明了其在实际部署条件下的有效性。项目网站：, an efficient VLA model designed for reaction-critical manipulation without large-scale robot-data pretraining. ReflexVLA enhances temporal reasoning through latent future prediction and multi-frame temporal fusion within the vision backbone, while reducing deployment latency through batched visual encoding and CUDA Graph replay. Experiments show that ReflexVLA consistently improves dynamic manipulation performance while maintaining competitive accuracy on standard static manipulation benchmarks, and real-world experiments further demonstrate its effectiveness under practical deployment conditions. I 简介Introduction

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型的最新进展通过在单一框架内统一感知、语言理解和动作生成，显着扩展了机器人操作的能力。基于大规模视觉语言模型的成功，越来越多的工作表明，机器人策略可以从不同的多模态数据中学习，并通过自然语言指令在任务中推广。代表性方法Recent advances in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have significantly expanded the capabilities of robotic manipulation by unifying perception, language understanding, and action generation within a single framework. Building upon the success of large-scale vision-language models, a growing body of work demonstrates that robot policies can be learned from diverse multimodal data and generalized across tasks through natural language instructions. Representative approaches[4] [7] [1]表明扩展模型容量和训练数据可以显着改善任务多样性、指令遵循和跨领域泛化。这些发展使 VLA 模型成为通用机器人控制的一个有希望的基础，并激发了对能力越来越强的实体代理的进一步探索。show that scaling model capacity and training data leads to substantial improvements in task diversity, instruction following, and cross-domain generalization. These developments establish VLA models as a promising foundation for general-purpose robot control and motivate further exploration toward increasingly capable embodied agents.

尽管取得了这些进步，当前的 VLA 模型在需要快速反应和对未来状态进行推理的操作场景中仍然面临挑战。许多现有方法主要根据当前观察来调节操作，它们的推理延迟会导致感知和执行之间的延迟。这种延迟在动态环境中变得尤其重要，在动态环境中，机器人必须对移动物体做出反应，在精确的时间拦截目标，或者操纵其轨迹影响任务成功的物体。Despite these advances, current VLA models remain challenged in manipulation scenarios that require both rapid reaction and reasoning about future states. Many existing approaches primarily condition actions on the current observation, and their inference latency introduces a delay between perception and execution. This delay becomes particularly consequential in dynamic environments where robots must respond to moving objects, intercept targets at precise times, or manipulate objects whose trajectories affect task success.

为了解决这些局限性，最近的研究探索了几个互补的方向。其中一项工作引入了专注于动态操作场景的专门基准测试To address these limitations, recent research explores several complementary directions. One line of work introduces specialized benchmarks that focus on dynamic manipulation scenarios[18] [5] [22]。与此同时，越来越多的研究调查如何为 VLA 模型配备更适合未来预测的功能. In parallel, a growing number of studies investigate how to equip VLA models with capabilities that are better suited for future prediction[18] [10]。同时，我们致力于通过架构优化、模型压缩和系统级加速技术来提高VLA推理的效率，以减少延迟并实现在实时机器人应用中的部署. Meanwhile, considerable effort is devoted to improving the efficiency of VLA inference through architectural optimization, model compression, and system-level acceleration techniques, with the goal of reducing latency and enabling deployment in real-time robotic applications[13] [11]。然而，这些研究方向很大程度上是孤立探索的。现有的动态操作基准主要用作评估平台，没有提供用于研究预期和效率的统一框架，而增强预测推理的方法通常会引入额外的计算开销。相反，优先考虑推理加速的方法通常只关注效率，而没有明确满足未来感知决策的需求。因此，在当前的 VLA 研究中，同时实现强预测能力和快速推理的挑战仍然没有得到充分探索。. However, these research directions are largely explored in isolation. Existing dynamic manipulation benchmarks primarily serve as evaluation platforms without providing a unified framework for studying both anticipation and efficiency, while methods that enhance predictive reasoning often introduce additional computational overhead. Conversely, approaches that prioritize inference acceleration generally focus on efficiency alone and do not explicitly address the need for future-aware decision making. As a result, the challenge of simultaneously achieving strong anticipatory capability and fast inference remains insufficiently explored in current VLA research.

在本文中，我们首先提出In this paper, we first present反射凳ReflexBench，旨在评估需要高效执行和预测未来环境动态的操作任务中的 VLA 策略的基准。 ReflexBench 包含六个基于模拟的任务，捕捉反应关键操作中的代表性挑战。为了更好地反映实际部署条件，该基准明确纳入了延迟效应，并使评估过程尽可能与现实世界的执行延迟保持一致。在 ReflexBench 的基础上，我们进一步研究了使 VLA 策略成功完成此类任务的关键因素，并提出了建议, a benchmark designed to evaluate VLA policies in manipulation tasks that require both efficient execution and anticipation of future environmental dynamics. ReflexBench consists of six simulation-based tasks that capture representative challenges in reaction-critical manipulation. To better reflect practical deployment conditions, the benchmark explicitly incorporates latency effects and aligns the evaluation process as closely as possible with real-world execution delays. Building upon ReflexBench, we further investigate the key factors that enable VLA policies to succeed in such tasks and propose反射VLAReflexVLA，专为快速和预测性机器人控制而设计的 VLA。 ReflexVLA 基于三个核心组件。首先，它引入了对未来视觉观察的潜在预测，这鼓励政策对近期的环境状态进行推理。其次，它利用多个历史观察结果作为输入，为决策提供更丰富的时间背景。第三，它结合了推理延迟优化，以减少感知和动作执行之间的延迟。大量的实验表明，这些设计选择有助于提高性能，并且它们的组合可以为既需要预期又需要效率的动态操作场景提供更有效的解决方案。为了总结我们的贡献，我们, a VLA designed for fast and predictive robot control. ReflexVLA is based on three core components. First, it introduces latent prediction of future visual observations, which encourages the policy to reason about near-future environmental states. Second, it leverages multiple historical observations as input, providing richer temporal context for decision making. Third, it incorporates inference latency optimization to reduce the delay between perception and action execution. Extensive experiments demonstrate that these design choices contribute to improved performance, and their combination leads to a more effective solution for dynamic manipulation scenarios that demand both anticipation and efficiency. Ⅲ-BIII-B 数据收集Data Collection

收集动态操作任务的演示比传统的静态操作基准更具挑战性。在静态环境中，通常可以通过简单的脚本化策略或远程操作来生成专家轨迹。相比之下，ReflexBench 中的任务涉及移动对象和依赖时间的交互，其中成功执行需要不断适应环境动态。Collecting demonstrations for dynamic manipulation tasks is substantially more challenging than for conventional static manipulation benchmarks. In static environments, expert trajectories can often be generated through straightforward scripted policies or teleoperation. In contrast, the tasks in ReflexBench involve moving objects and time-dependent interactions, where successful execution requires continuous adaptation to environmental dynamics.

我们采用基于规划的数据收集管道，将每个任务分解为具有不同子目标的连续阶段。例如，在旋转钉插入中，机器人执行预对准、目标跟踪和定时插入。为了处理动态对象，规划器根据实时位置和速度预测未来轨迹，从而可以针对预期的未来状态规划行动。当单独的规划无法处理复杂的交互动态时，我们训练特定于任务的强化学习策略来生成演示轨迹。We employ a planning-based data collection pipeline that decomposes each task into sequential phases with distinct subgoals. For example, in Rotating Peg Insertion, the robot performs pre-alignment, target tracking, and timed insertion. To handle dynamic objects, the planner predicts future trajectories from real-time position and velocity, allowing actions to be planned toward anticipated future states. When planning alone cannot handle complex interaction dynamics, we train a task-specific reinforcement learning policy to generate demonstration trajectories.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 延迟感知评估Latency-Aware Evaluation

ReflexBench 的一个关键特性是其延迟感知评估协议。与在策略推理期间暂停环境的现有模拟基准不同，ReflexBench 将模拟与机器人控制分离，允许环境动态独立于策略计算而发展，并实现更现实的评估。A key feature of ReflexBench is its latency-aware evaluation protocol. Unlike existing simulation benchmarks that pause the environment during policy inference, ReflexBench decouples simulation from robot control, allowing environmental dynamics to evolve independently of policy computation and enabling more realistic evaluation.

ReflexBench 显式地对两种常见部署范例下的推理延迟进行建模：同步推理和异步推理。同步推理会阻止执行，直到策略推理完成，而异步推理则将操作执行与策略计算重叠，从而以稍微陈旧的操作为代价来提高响应能力。ReflexBench explicitly models inference latency under two common deployment paradigms: synchronous and asynchronous inference. Synchronous inference blocks execution until policy inference completes, whereas asynchronous inference overlaps action execution with policy computation, improving responsiveness at the cost of slightly stale actions.

为了忠实地再现模拟中的延迟效应，我们引入了延迟阻塞机制。图。To faithfully reproduce latency effects in simulation, we introduce a latency blocking mechanism. Under synchronous inference, the simulator pauses to collect the current observation and query the policy. After inference, the simulator resumes, the robot remains idle for the specified latency, and then executes the predicted action chunk, faithfully mimicking inference-blocking deployment. Under asynchronous inference, the policy infers a new action chunk from the current observation while the robot executes the action chunk generated in the previous cycle. The newly inferred action becomes available in the next control period.

IV-A 潜在的未来预测Latent Future Prediction

IV-C 推理延迟优化Inference Latency Optimization

推理延迟是动态操作中的一个关键因素，如果操作生成得太慢，即使是强大的策略也可能会失败。为了提高现实世界的响应能力，ReflexVLA 通过优化 VLA 系统中两个常见的开销来源（视觉处理和 GPU 执行）来减少端到端推理延迟。Inference latency is a critical factor in dynamic manipulation, where even strong policies can fail if actions are generated too slowly. To improve real-world responsiveness, ReflexVLA reduces end-to-end inference latency by optimizing two common sources of overhead in VLA systems: visual processing and GPU execution.

第一个优化针对视觉编码。由于 ReflexVLA 消耗多个摄像机视图和历史观察结果，因此简单的实现将通过视觉主干独立处理每个图像，从而导致The first optimization targets visual encoding. Since ReflexVLA consumes multiple camera views and historical observations, a naive implementation would independently process each image through the vision backbone, resulting inV×TV\times T单独向前传球。这种重复执行会引入不必要的框架开销，并导致 GPU 利用率不理想。相反，ReflexVLA 将所有视图帧图像批处理到单个视觉编码器调用中。separate forward passes. Such repeated execution introduces unnecessary framework overhead and leads to suboptimal GPU utilization. Instead, ReflexVLA batches all view-frame images into a single visual encoder invocation.

The system-level optimizations substantially improve execution efficiency and make ReflexVLA more suitable for latency-sensitive robotic manipulation tasks.

维-EV-E 局限性Limitations

我们的工作有一些局限性，需要未来的调查。首先，虽然 ReflexVLA 引入了潜在的未来预测和多帧时间融合，但这些组件仅在微调期间合并，而不是通过大规模预训练，这可能会限制它们的全部潜力。其次，我们的研究重点是两种广泛采用的推理范式：朴素同步推理和异步推理。更先进的推理机制，例如RTCOur work has several limitations that warrant future investigation. First, although ReflexVLA introduces latent future prediction and multi-frame temporal fusion, these components are incorporated only during fine-tuning rather than through large-scale pretraining, which may limit their full potential. Second, our study focuses on two widely adopted inference paradigms, naive synchronous and asynchronous inference. More advanced inference mechanisms, such as RTC[3]，在这项工作中没有进行探讨，并且可能会在延迟敏感的操作场景中提供额外的改进。, are not explored in this work and may provide additional improvements in latency-sensitive manipulation scenarios.

VI 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 ReflexBench，它是延迟感知动态操作的基准。 ReflexBench 包含六个具有代表性的动态任务，并引入了延迟感知评估框架，该框架将模拟器步进与机器人控制分离。我们还推出了 ReflexVLA，这是一种用于动态机器人操作的延迟感知视觉语言动作框架。通过集成潜在未来预测、多帧时间融合和推理优化，ReflexVLA 使机器人能够更好地预测未来状态，同时实现高效的实时执行。 ReflexBench 上的实验表明，ReflexVLA 在保持轻量级 1B 参数模型的效率的同时，在现有 VLA 基线中实现了具有竞争力的性能。此外，ReflexVLA 在标准静态操作基准 LIBERO 上仍然具有竞争力。对三个反应关键操作任务的真实实验进一步验证了我们方法的有效性和实用性。In this work, we present ReflexBench, a benchmark for latency-aware dynamic manipulation. ReflexBench contains six representative dynamic tasks and introduces a latency-aware evaluation framework that decouples simulator stepping from robot control. We also present ReflexVLA, a latency-aware vision-language-action framework for dynamic robotic manipulation. By integrating latent future prediction, multi-frame temporal fusion, and inference optimization, ReflexVLA enables robots to better anticipate future states while achieving efficient real-time execution. Experiments on ReflexBench demonstrate that ReflexVLA achieves competitive performance among existing VLA baselines while maintaining the efficiency of a lightweight 1B parameter model. Furthermore, ReflexVLA remains competitive on the standard static manipulation benchmark LIBERO. Real-world experiments on three reaction-critical manipulation tasks further validate the effectiveness and practicality of our approach.
