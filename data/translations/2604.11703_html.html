摘要Abstract

无家可归者 (PEH) 在获取及时、准确的社区服务信息方面面临巨大障碍。 DreamKG 通过知识图增强对话系统解决了这个问题，该系统将响应基于有关费城组织、服务、位置和时间的经过验证的最新数据。与容易产生幻觉的标准大语言模型 (LLM) 不同，DreamKG 将 Neo4j 知识图与结构化查询理解相结合，以可靠地处理位置感知和时间敏感的查询。该系统对基于距离的建议进行空间推理，并对营业时间进行时间过滤。初步评估显示，在相关查询方面，Google Search AI 的优越性为 59%，在不相关查询方面的拒绝率为 84%。该演示强调了混合架构的潜力，该架构将法学硕士的灵活性与知识图谱的可靠性相结合，以有效提高弱势群体的服务可及性。People experiencing homelessness (PEH) face substantial barriers to accessing timely, accurate information about community services. DreamKG addresses this through a knowledge graph-augmented conversational system that grounds responses in verified, up-to-date data about Philadelphia organizations, services, locations, and hours. Unlike standard large language models (LLMs) prone to hallucinations, DreamKG combines Neo4j knowledge graphs with structured query understanding to handle location-aware and time-sensitive queries reliably. The system performs spatial reasoning for distance-based recommendations and temporal filtering for operating hours. Preliminary evaluation shows 59% superiority over Google Search AI on relevant queries and 84% rejection of irrelevant queries. This demonstration highlights the potential of hybrid architectures that combines LLM flexibility with knowledge graph reliability to improve service accessibility for vulnerable populations effectively.

一、简介I. INTRODUCTION

及时、准确地获取有关基本社区服务（包括庇护所、食品计划、医疗保健和社会服务）的信息对于 PEH 至关重要 [1, 2]。然而，信息差距仍然存在，不稳定的住房、有限的数字访问以及可用资源的短暂性进一步加剧了这种差距 [3, 4]。Access to timely, accurate information about essential community services, including shelters, food programs, healthcare, and social services is critical for PEH [1, 2]. However, significant information gaps persist, further compounded by unstable housing, limited digital access, and the transient nature of available resources [3, 4].

现有的解决方案无法满足这些需求。像 ChatGPT 这样的通用法学硕士，虽然对话流畅，但会出现幻觉，反应自信但不正确，临床评估中的错误率超过 50% [5-7]。对于需要有关服务地点、营业时间和资格的可靠信息的弱势群体来说，此类不准确可能会造成严重后果。当前用于无家可归者服务的聊天机器人系统很少集成结构化知识或提供位置感知、时间敏感的建议 [2,8,9]。虽然检索增强生成（RAG）和知识图（KG）方法提高了事实准确性[10-13]，但很少有系统结合这些技术来满足 PEH 的特定时空信息需求。Existing solutions fall short in addressing these needs. General-purpose LLMs like ChatGPT, while conversationally fluent, suffer from hallucinations, confident but incorrect responses, with error rates exceeding 50% in clinical evaluations [5–7]. For vulnerable populations requiring reliable information about service locations, operating hours, and eligibility, such inaccuracies can have serious consequences. Current chatbot systems for homeless services rarely integrate structured knowledge or provide location-aware, time-sensitive recommendations [2, 8, 9]. While retrievalaugmented generation (RAG) and knowledge graph (KG) approaches improve factual accuracy [10–13], few systems combine these techniques to address the specific spatial-temporal information needs of PEH.

DreamKG 通过 KG 增强对话系统解决了这一差距，该系统旨在支持费城的社区服务导航。该系统集成了基于 Neo4j 的 KG（对本地组织的经过验证的信息进行编码）与基于 LLM 的查询理解，以提供可靠、可解释、位置感知的服务DreamKG addresses this gap through a KG-augmented conversational system designed to support community service navigation in Philadelphia. 二.演示说明II. DEMO DESCRIPTION

A. 系统总体架构A. System Overall Architecture

DreamKG 采用混合架构（图 1），将基于 LLM 的自然语言理解与 Neo4j KG 检索相结合。用户查询通过六个阶段进行处理：（1）关键词提取以识别服务类型、空间线索、时间约束和对话上下文； (2) 标准化和预处理，包括地理编码位置和标准化文本； (3) Cypher 查询生成，它构造模式约束的数据库查询，并对无法映射到模式的输入进行回退处理； (4) 通过对 KG 执行查询来检索信息； (5) 响应格式化，其中结果根据查询类型进行结构化； (6) 输出渲染，呈现交互式可视化以及详细的服务信息。DreamKG employs a hybrid architecture (Fig. 1) that combines LLM-based natural language understanding with Neo4j KG retrieval. User queries are processed through six stages: (1) keyword extraction to identify service types, spatial cues, temporal constraints, and conversational context; (2) normalization and preprocessing, including geocoding locations and standardizing text; (3) Cypher query generation, which constructs schema-constrained database queries with fallback handling for inputs that cannot be mapped to the schema; (4) information retrieval through execution of queries against the KG; (5) response formatting, where results are structured based on query type; and (6) output rendering, which presents interactive visualizations alongside detailed service information.

B. 数据源和本体B. Data Source and Ontology

DreamKG 集成了来自权威来源的异构数据，包括 FindHelp、OpenDataPhilly、无家可归者服务办公室 (OHS)、行为健康和智力障碍服务部 (DBHIDS)、费城免费图书馆 (FLP) 和社会保障管理局 (SSA)。 Neo4j KG 存储组织节点（例如，名称、地址、电话、服务）、位置节点（例如，坐标、邮政编码、社区）、服务节点（例如，类型、成本、资格）和时间节点（例如，每天的运行时间）。涵盖心理健康服务、社会保障办公室、图书馆、庇护所和食品服务的五个领域本体为查询解释提供了结构化框架。DreamKG integrates heterogeneous data from authoritative sources including FindHelp, OpenDataPhilly, the Office of Homeless Services (OHS), Department of Behavioral Health and Intellectual disAbility Services (DBHIDS), the Free Library of Philadelphia (FLP), and Social Security Administration (SSA). The Neo4j KG stores organization nodes (e.g., name, address, phone, services), location nodes (e.g., coordinates, ZIP codes, neighborhoods), service nodes (e.g., type, cost, eligibility), and temporal nodes (e.g., operating hours by day). A five-domain ontology, covering mental health services, social security offices, libraries, shelters, and food services, provides a structured framework for query interpretation.

A spatial intelligence module geocodes inputs and ranks results by distance. 三．评价III. EVALUATION

我们使用由 GPT4.1-mini 在 300 个基准查询（包括 200 个域相关查询和 100 个范围外查询）中判断的结构化盲成对比较来评估 DreamKG 与 Google AI 模式。We evaluated DreamKG against Google AI Mode using a structured, blind pairwise comparison judged by GPT4.1-mini across 300 benchmark queries, including 200 domain-relevant and 100 out-of-scope queries.

基准设计。Benchmark Design.综合生成了 200 个相关查询，以反映五个服务类别中现实的以帮助者为导向的搜索行为：食品银行、心理健康服务、庇护所、公共图书馆和社会保障办公室。查询是在全因子设计下构建的，具有两种类型的时间表达式（隐式或显式时钟时间）、四种位置规范（邮政编码、社区名称、街道地址和模糊的环境线索）和五种语言风格（疑问式、陈述式、搜索式、因果/反射式和社区导向）。相关性被定义为与费城无家可归者服务导航的一致性。这 100 个不相关的查询由通用问题组成（The 200 relevant queries were synthetically generated to reflect realistic helper-oriented search behavior across five service categories: food banks, mental health services, shelters, public libraries, and Social Security offices. Queries were constructed under a full factorial design, varying with two types of temporal expressions (implicit or explicit clock times), four location specifications (ZIP code, neighborhood name, street address, and ambiguous environmental cues), and five language styles (interrogative, declarative, search-style, causal/reflective, and community-oriented). Relevancy was defined as alignment with Philadelphia-based homeless service navigation. The 100 irrelevant queries consisted of general-purpose questions (例如e.g.、烹饪、技术支持、旅行）与社会服务无关。, cooking, technology support, travel) unrelated to social services.

基线。Baseline.Google AI 模式生成的摘要响应作为基准。 DreamKG 的回答将简洁的直接答案与更长、更详细的上下文解释结合在一起，作为单个输出一起评估。Google AI Mode's generated summary response served as the baseline. DreamKG responses combined a concise direct answer with a longer, more detailed contextual explanation, evaluated together as a single output.

法官设置。Judge Setup.对于每个相关查询，法官都会收到匿名 A/B 回复，并选择能够更好地支持现实世界帮助者寻求清晰、可操作且高可信度服务信息的回复。评估标准的优先顺序如下：（1）位置特异性（靠近所请求的邮政编码）； (2) 服务相关性（For each relevant query, the judge received anonymized A/B responses and selected the response that better supports a real-world helper seeking clear, actionable, and high-confidence service information. Evaluation criteria were prioritized as follows: (1) location specificity (proximity to the requested ZIP code); (2) service relevance (例如e.g.，特定领域的提供者而不是通用资源）； (3) 运营详情（地址、电话、营业时间和服务说明）； （4）结构清晰（信息组织和可读性）； (5) 可操作性（直接联系或访问提供商的能力）； (6)信噪比(重点、简洁)； (7) 整体准确性和合理性（真实数据）。, domain-specific providers rather than generic resources); (3) operational detail (availability of address, phone, hours, and service descriptions); (4) structural clarity (information organization and readability); (5) actionability (ability to directly contact or visit a provider); (6) signal-to-noise ratio (focus and conciseness); and (7) overall accuracy and plausibility (realistic data).

In contrast, for "19124 是否有为我今天早些时候想要帮助的人提供无需预约的心理健康危机中心？Is there a walk-in mental health crisis center in 19124 for someone I'm trying to help earlier today?”，谷歌 AI 模式是首选，因为它明确确认了无需预约的可用性和当天的工作时间。此示例凸显了 DreamKG 的一个关键限制：它对预定运行时间的依赖限制了其捕获计划外实时变化（例如临时关闭或中断）的能力。", Google AI Mode was preferred because it explicitly confirmed walk-in availability and same-day hours. 四．新奇IV. NOVELTY

DreamKG 推出了 PEH 社区服务导航的三项关键创新：(1)DreamKG introduces three key innovations for community services navigation among PEH: (1)组合查询构造compositional query construction，它将服务类型、空间邻近性和时间约束联合建模为多轮交互，可以通过会话参考解析分解复杂的查询； (2), which jointly models service type, spatial proximity, and temporal constraints into multi-turn interactions, which can decompose complex queries with conversational reference resolution; (2)约束KG检索constrained KG retrieval结合了地理编码、基于坐标的图形遍历和时间窗口过滤，以及跨异构数据源的回退处理； (3)that combines geocoding, coordinate-based graph traversal, and time-window filtering, with fallback handling across heterogeneous data sources; (3)社区参与的数据管理，community-engaged data curation,由模块化系统架构支持，允许迭代集成社区合作伙伴提供的异构服务数据。supported by a modular system architecture that allows the iterative integration of heterogeneous service data provided by community partners.

五、结论V. CONCLUSION

DreamKG 展示了 KG 增强型对话式 AI 的潜力，可以可靠地支持需要准确、位置感知和时间敏感信息的弱势群体。在使用独立法学硕士法官进行的初步评估中，DreamKG 在六个标准上优于 Google Search AI：位置特异性、服务相关性、操作细节、结构清晰度、可操作性和信噪比，在相关查询方面显示出 59% 的优势，在不相关查询方面显示出 84% 的适当拒绝率。该系统通过社区咨询委员会 (CAB) 审查进行了进一步评估，与既定的最佳实践保持一致 [14-18]，并确保对现实世界服务导航挑战的响应，同时减轻误传或伤害的风险。这项工作强调了结构化知识表示如何实现精确的时空推理、会话记忆如何支持多轮对话，以及透明的查询构建如何促进面临系统性障碍的人群的信任。未来的工作将涉及不同城市环境的多地点评估，以评估该系统对无家可归者的服务获取、护理协调以及健康和社会结果的影响。DreamKG demonstrates the potential of KG-augmented conversational AI to reliably support vulnerable populations requiring accurate, location-aware, and time-sensitive information. In preliminary evaluation using an independent LLM-based judge, DreamKG outperformed Google Search AI across six criteria: location specificity, service relevance, operational detail, structural clarity, actionability, and signal-to-noise ratio, showing 59% superiority on relevant queries and 84% appropriate rejection of irrelevant queries. The system was further evaluated through Community Advisory Board (CAB) review, aligning with established best practices [14–18] and ensuring responsiveness to real-world service navigation challenges while mitigating risks of misrepresentation or harm. The work highlights how structured knowledge representations enable precise spatial-temporal reasoning, how conversational memory supports multi-turn dialogues, and how transparent query construction fosters trust with populations facing systemic barriers. Future work will involve multi-site evaluations across diverse urban settings to assess the system’s impact on service access, care coordination, and health and social outcomes among people experiencing homelessness.
