使用 XAI 分析欧洲电力市场的驱动因素和相互依赖性Analysing drivers and interdependencies in European electricity markets using XAI

Antoine Pesenti Aidan O’Sullivan UCL Energy Institute, University College London, UK
（2026 年 6 月）(June 2026)
摘要Abstract

电力市场本质上是复杂的系统，其特点是强非线性、高维相互作用以及区域间日益增强的相互依赖性。虽然深度神经网络 (DNN) 已展现出强大的电价预测能力，但其缺乏可解释性限制了其在理解价格形成的潜在驱动因素方面的有用性。Electricity markets are inherently complex systems characterised by strong nonlinearities, high-dimensional interactions, and increasing interdependence across regions. While deep neural networks (DNNs) have demonstrated strong predictive capabilities for electricity prices, their lack of interpretability limits their usefulness for understanding the underlying drivers of price formation.

本文通过将 DNN 模型与可解释的人工智能 (XAI) 技术相结合来分析 39 个欧洲竞标区的电价决定因素，从而解决了这一差距。我们采用 SHAP（SHapley Additive exPlanations）来量化特征贡献，并应用和扩展 SSHAP（一种聚合框架，以提高高维设置中的可解释性）。This paper addresses this gap by combining DNN models with explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) techniques to analyse the determinants of electricity prices across 39 European bidding zones. We employ SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to quantify feature contributions and apply and extend SSHAP, an aggregation framework to improve interpretability in high-dimensional settings.

分析指出，可再生能源，特别是太阳能，尽管在总发电量中所占份额较低，但在价格形成中发挥着不成比例的重要作用。天然气价格仍然是整个电力市场的主导且持续的驱动因素，而互联互通显着影响价格动态，凸显了欧洲电力系统的强烈相互依赖性。此外，还构建了一个综合的欧盟范围电力市场，以探索具有单一价格的完全一体化市场的反事实场景。The analysis identifies that renewable energy sources, particularly solar, play a disproportionately important role in price formation despite their lower share in total power generation. Gas prices remain a dominant and consistent driver across electricity markets, while interconnections significantly shape price dynamics, highlighting the strong interdependence of European electricity systems. In addition, a synthetic EU-wide electricity market is constructed to explore the counterfactual scenario of a fully integrated market with a single price.

2 文献综述Literature review

电价预测 (EPF) 是能源领域的一个重要研究领域，受到电力市场固有的复杂性、波动性和放松管制性质的推动Electricity Price Forecasting (EPF) is a critical research area within the energy sector, driven by the inherent complexity, volatility, and deregulated nature of electricity markets[27, 15]。传统的计量经济模型，通常称为白盒模型，已被广泛用于通过利用供应、需求、燃料价格和天气条件等基本市场变量来预测和解释电价. Traditional econometric models, often referred to as white-box models, have been extensively used to predict and explain electricity prices by leveraging fundamental market variables such as supply, demand, fuel prices, and weather conditions[9, 8].

大量文献记录了可再生能源发电在多个市场（包括德国）的价格抑制效应A substantial body of literature documents the price-suppressing effect of renewable generation across multiple markets, including Germany[41], 澳大利亚, Australia[10], 美国, the United States[33], 土耳其, Turkey[1], 新西兰, New Zealand[39]和加拿大and Canada[35]。白盒模型的优点是可以从模型中提供直接解释，无需中介方法。然而，虽然这些模型提供了直接的可解释性，但它们往往难以捕捉电力市场的非线性和高维相互作用特征. White-box models have the advantage of providing direct explanations from their models, without intermediary methods. However while these models offer direct interpretability, they often struggle to capture the nonlinearities and high-dimensional interactions characteristic of electricity markets[2, 40, 23].

机器学习的出现为解决传统预测方法的局限性提供了新途径。尤其是 DNN，在对高维数据中复杂的依赖关系和交互进行建模方面表现出了良好的前景，与计量经济学方法和更简单的机器学习模型相比，它具有更高的预测准确性The advent of machine learning has provided new avenues to address the limitations of traditional forecasting approaches. 为了弥补这种可解释性差距，XAI 技术近年来得到了重视。 XAI旨在为人工智能模型决策和预测提供可理解和可解释的解释，从而提高透明度、可信度和问责制To bridge this interpretability gap, XAI techniques have gained prominence in recent years. 4.1 型号Models

该模型以负荷、发电结构和燃气价格作为输入，输出市场电价。该模型没有时间维度，输入和输出均指同一小时周期，并且不存在滞后数据。事实上，日前电价是在实际负荷和发电组合之前设定的，因此显然不是一个预测模型。这些模型的建立只是为了帮助理解电力市场的机制。The models take load, power generation mix and gas price as input and outputs the market electricity price. The models have no time dimension, the input and output refer to the same one hour period and there is no lagged data. In fact, the day-ahead electricity price is set ahead of the actual load and power generation mix, so it is clearly not a forecasting model. The models have been built only to help to understand the mechanisms of electricity markets.

主要模型是一个简单的多层感知器 (MLP)，具有两个隐藏层和 Adam 优化器The main model is a straightforward multi-layer perceptron (MLP) with two hidden layers with the Adam optimiser[20]和 MAE（平均绝对误差）损失函数。该模型第一个隐藏层有 128 个神经元，第二个隐藏层有 32 个神经元，其激活函数为 ReLu，学习率为 0.0001，dropout 率为 0.175，并且存在 lambda 等于 0.0002 的 L1 正则化。基线模型是线性模型 (LR)、随机森林 (RF) 和梯度增强回归器 (GBR)，均来自 scikit-learn 库and the MAE (Mean Absolute Error) loss function. 4.2 XAI方法XAI methods

由于这些 DNN 模型使用平滑的表格数据作为输入，所有主要标准特征重要性可解释方法都给出了非常相似的结果，因此我们在本文中使用 SHAP 方法，因为它是最常见的并且具有附加属性Because these DNN models use smooth tabular data as input, all the main standard Feature Importance explainable methods gave very similar results, hence we use the SHAP method in the paper as it is the most common and it has additional properties[24]。作为标准做法，DNN 模型根据输入和输出的标准化数据进行训练。然而，在应用可解释的方法时，我们使用非规范化输出来量化特征的绝对重要性而不是相对值。因此，SHAP 值与电价具有相同的单位，即欧元每兆瓦时，这很有用。在确定 Shapley 值时，我们使用条件期望而不是边际期望. The DNN models are trained on normalised data for inputs and outputs as standard practice. However, when applying the explainable methods, we use denormalised outputs to quantify the absolute importance of the features rather than relative values. So the SHAP values have the same unit as the electricity prices, i.e. Euros per MWh, which is useful. We use conditional expectation rather than marginal expectation when determining the Shapley values[7, 6]。为了使用 DNN 模型估计条件 Shapley 值，我们使用由. To estimate the conditional Shapley values with the DNN models, we use the Monte-Carlo sampling method developed by[36]并在 GitHub SHAP 库中实现and implemented in the GitHub SHAP library444https://github.com/shap/shap。对于集成树模型（LR 和 GBR），我们使用 Tree SHAP. For the ensemble tree models (LR and GBR), we use Tree SHAP[25]，这是一种计算 SHA 值的快速且精确的算法。, which is a fast and exact algorithm to compute SHAP values.

SHAP 值是局部解释，其中每个值表示特定特征对于特定预测的重要性。为了获得每个特征的全局解释，我们取整个数据集中 SHAP 值绝对值的平均值。与许多机器学习模型一样，特征的总数非常大，因此我们将整个特征集划分为少量子集，以成为有意义的类别。对子集中的平均绝对 SHAP 值进行求和的缺点是，可能会夸大某些特征相互抵消的子集的重要性，当存在如此多的特征并且许多特征相互依赖时，这种情况很可能发生。因此，我们将使用 Super-SHAP (SSHAP) 值作为特征分区或超级特征，如SHAP values are local explanations where each value indicates the importance of a specific feature for a specific prediction. In order to get global explanations per feature, we take the mean across the dataset of the absolute values of the SHAP values. As often with many machine learning models, the overall number of features is very large, so we partition the overall set of features in a small number of subsets to become meaningful categories. The drawback of summing the mean absolute SHAP values across the subsets is to potentially overstate the importance of certain subsets where features offset each others, which is very likely when there are so many features and many have dependencies with each others. Hence we will use Super-SHAP (SSHAP) values for feature partitions or super-features as introduced in[31]允许在考虑全局解释（使用绝对值的平均值）之前在子集中完成净额计算。 Shapley 和 SHAP 值的一个显着属性是效率属性that allow the nettings to be done within the subsets before considering the global explanations (with the mean of the absolute values). A notable property of Shapley and SHAP values is the efficiency property[34]这是在 SSHAP 值中维护的。and this is maintained in SSHAP values.

通过 SSHAP，我们将通过两个不同的角度分析电力市场：发电结构和互联邻居。在第一种情况下，我们将 364 个特征集划分为 9 个超级特征：第 7 节中的 7 个发电混合类别With SSHAPs, we will analyse the electricity markets through two different angles: the power generation mix and the interconnected neighbours. In the first case, we partition the set of 364 features in 9 super-features: the 7 power generation mix categories as in Section3.2、负荷和天然气价格。例如，超级特征风电包含了39个竞标区的陆上和海上风电的所有特征。我们将为 SHAP 汇总表使用相同的分区, load and gas price. For example, the super-feature wind contains all the features onshore and offshore wind for the 39 bidding zones. We will use the same partition for the SHAP summary table2。在第二种情况下，我们将 364 个特征集划分为 40 个超级特征，每个超级特征对应 39 个竞价区域和 Gas 价格。例如，超级特征法国（FR）包含法国负荷特征和所有法国发电组合特征。. In the second case, we partition the set of 364 features in 40 super-features, one for each of the 39 bidding zone, and gas price. For example, the super-feature France (FR) contains the French load feature and all the French power generation mix features.

最后，由于EU模型是39个模型的线性组合，并且SHAP和SSHAP是线性函数，因此EU模型的SHAP和SSHAP值只是39个模型SHAP和SSHAP值的加权平均值。欧盟模型SHAP和SSHAP值对39个模型进行了有价值的总结。Finally as the EU model is a linear combination of 39 models and SHAP and SSHAP are linear functions, SHAP and SSHAP values of the EU model are simply the weighted average of the 39 model SHAP and SSHAP values. And the EU model SHAP and SSHAP values give a valuable summary of the 39 models.

原因之一很简单，一个特征最重要的特征不一定是它的平均值；而是它的平均值。例如，如果某个功能是恒定的，那么根据定义，即使它具有很高的价值，它对价格的影响也为零。这就是核电对价格影响最小的原因，它的发电量作为基本负荷电力相当稳定。相反，从标准差来看，太阳能在 39 个投标区的相对标准差中的加权平均值最高，为 25%。此外，39个招标区的太阳能总发电量，即欧盟太阳能发电量，其标准偏差甚至更高，达到33%。这是因为与其他来源相比，太阳能发电在 39 个投标区之间的相关性更高；特别是，大风天气更多是一种局部现象，而晴天则更多是区域性的，或者在较大区域内通常更加均匀。One reason for this is simply that the most important trait of a feature is not necessarily its mean; for example if a feature was constant, then its impact on price would be nil by definition, even if it had high values. 7 结论Conclusions

本文使用 DNN 模型与 XAI 技术相结合，对欧洲 39 个竞标区电价的驱动因素和相互依赖性进行了分析。通过利用 SHAP 和新推出的 SSHAP 框架，我们超越了预测性能，为高度互联的市场中影响电价形成的机制提供了可解释的见解。This paper provides an analysis of the drivers and interdependencies of European electricity prices across 39 bidding zones, using DNN models combined with XAI techniques. By leveraging SHAP and the newly introduced SSHAP framework, we move beyond predictive performance to deliver interpretable insights into the mechanisms shaping electricity price formation in highly interconnected markets.

分析得出了几个关键结果。首先，虽然传统上将负载视为电价的主要驱动因素，但一旦使用 SSHAP 方法来减少噪声特征的影响，其解释力就比预期更加有限。相比之下，可再生能源，特别是太阳能，在价格形成中发挥着不成比例的重要作用。尽管太阳能在总发电量中所占份额较小，但在 SSHAP 分析中却成为最具影响力的发电来源，反映出其高度可变性和对价格的强烈定向影响。其次，天然气价格仍然是整个电力市场的主导且高度一致的驱动因素，这证实了燃气发电在优先顺序机制中的持续重要性。然而，其影响因每个投标区的发电组合而异，对于以水电等非天然气基本负荷来源为主的系统影响较小。第三，分析强调了互连的关键作用。电价不仅仅由国内条件决定：邻近电力市场的影响力很大，往​​往超过当地变量的影响力。只有少数招标区的国内制度是价格的主要决定因素。这一发现强调了欧洲电力系统内部的高度相互依赖以及跨境流动在价格形成中的重要性。Several key results emerge from the analysis. First, while load is traditionally viewed as a primary driver of electricity prices, its explanatory power is more limited than expected once the SSHAP method is used to reduce the impact of noisy features. In contrast, renewable energy sources, particularly solar, play a disproportionately large role in price formation. Despite representing a smaller share of total power generation, solar energy emerges as the most influential generation source in the SSHAP analysis, reflecting its high variability and strong directional impact on prices. Second, gas price remains a dominant and highly consistent driver across electricity markets, confirming the continued importance of gas-fired generation in the merit-order mechanism. However, its influence varies significantly depending on the power generation mix of each bidding zone, with reduced impact in systems dominated by non-gas base load sources such as hydropower. Third, the analysis highlights the critical role of interconnections. Electricity prices are not solely determined by domestic conditions: neighbouring electricity markets exert substantial influence, often exceeding that of local variables. In only a minority of bidding zones is the domestic system the primary determinant of prices. This finding underscores the high degree of interdependence within the European electricity system and the importance of cross-border flows in price formation.

未来的工作可以通过明确地建模传输约束和流量来完善互连的表示，而不是仅仅依赖于邻近的特征。这将有助于更好地捕捉拥堵效应和市场一体化的物理限制。未来的工作还可以区分对不同价格范围，特别是非常高的价格的解释。正如蜂群图所强调的那样，太阳能和风能的分布具有长期的负尾，当它们的世代出现时，会压低价格。 非常高，但当他们的世代非常低或为零时，他们不会将价格推高太多。因此，它们并不能很好地解释极高的价格。树集成基线模型（RF 和 GBR）的 SSHAP 值明显较低，证实了这一点 使用 RMSE（均方根误差）作为损失函数而不是 MAE。Future work could refine the representation of interconnections by explicitly modelling transmission constraints and flows, rather than relying solely on neighbouring features. This would help to better capture congestion effects and the physical limits of market integration. Future work could also differentiate explanations for different price ranges and for very high prices in particular. As highlighted with the beeswarm plots, solar and wind distributions have long negative tails to push down prices when their generations are very high, but they do not push much the prices up when their generations are very low or nil. Hence they are not good explanations for extreme high prices. This is confirmed by their SSHAP values being significantly lower with tree ensemble baseline models (RF and GBR) that use RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error) as loss function rather than MAE.

最后，从政策角度来看，该分析提供的见解可用于评估不同市场设计选择的影响，例如增加互连能力或替代定价机制。探索反事实情景——例如具有单一清算价格的完全一体化的欧洲电力市场——仍然是未来研究的一个特别有前途的方向。Finally, from a policy perspective, the insights provided by this analysis could be used to assess the impact of different market design choices, such as increased interconnection capacity or alternative pricing mechanisms. Exploring counterfactual scenarios—such as a fully integrated European electricity market with a single clearing price—remains a particularly promising direction for future research.
