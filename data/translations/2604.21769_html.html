谁来定义“最好”？迈向交互式、用户定义的 LLM 排行榜评估Who Defines ”Best”? Towards Interactive, User-Defined Evaluation of LLM Leaderboards

Minji Jung Yonsei UniversitySeoulSouth Korea minji2744@yonsei.ac.kr , Minjae Lee Yonsei UniversitySeoulSouth Korea minjaelee@yonsei.ac.kr , Yejin Kim Yonsei UniversitySeoulSouth Korea gina261@yonsei.ac.kr , Sarang Choi Yonsei UniversitySeoulSouth Korea sarangchoi@yonsei.ac.kr and Minsuk Kahng Yonsei UniversitySeoulSouth Korea minsuk@yonsei.ac.kr

摘要。Abstract.

LLM 排行榜广泛用于比较模型和指导部署决策。然而，排行榜排名是由基准设计者设定的评估优先级决定的，而不是由实际用户和组织的不同目标和限制决定的。单个总分通常会掩盖模型在不同提示类型和组合中的行为方式。在这项工作中，我们对 LMArena（以前称为 Chatbot Arena）基准测试中使用的数据集进行了深入分析，并通过设计交互式可视化界面作为设计探针来研究这一评估挑战。我们的分析表明，数据集严重偏向某些主题，模型排名因提示片段而异，并且基于偏好的判断的使用方式模糊了其预期范围。在此分析的基础上，我们引入了一个可视化界面，允许用户通过选择和加权提示切片来定义自己的评估优先级，并探索排名如何相应变化。一项定性研究表明，这种交互式方法提高了透明度并支持更多针对具体情况的模型评估，指出了设计和使用 LLM 排行榜的替代方法。LLM leaderboards are widely used to compare models and guide deployment decisions. However, leaderboard rankings are shaped by evaluation priorities set by benchmark designers, rather than by the diverse goals and constraints of actual users and organizations. A single aggregate score often obscures how models behave across different prompt types and compositions. In this work, we conduct an in-depth analysis of the dataset used in the LMArena (formerly Chatbot Arena) benchmark and investigate this evaluation challenge by designing an interactive visualization interface as a design probe. Our analysis reveals that the dataset is heavily skewed toward certain topics, that model rankings vary across prompt slices, and that preference-based judgments are used in ways that blur their intended scope. Building on this analysis, we introduce a visualization interface that allows users to define their own evaluation priorities by selecting and weighting prompt slices and to explore how rankings change accordingly. A qualitative study suggests that this interactive approach improves transparency and supports more context-specific model evaluation, pointing toward alternative ways to design and use LLM leaderboards.

1. 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地通过基准数据集和排行榜进行评估、比较和选择。这些排行榜在塑造研究叙述、部署决策和公众对模型质量的看法方面发挥着核心作用。然而，尽管有影响力，大多数评估框架都是由一小群基准创建者设计和确定的，而他们的结果却被更广泛和更多样化的受众所消费。Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly evaluated, compared, and selected through benchmark datasets and leaderboards. These leaderboards play a central role in shaping research narratives, deployment decisions, and public perceptions of model quality. Yet, despite their influence, most evaluation frameworks are designed and fixed by a small group of benchmark creators, while their results are consumed by a much broader and more diverse audience.

这种不对称性引发了一个基本问题：评估结果很大程度上取决于其他人做出的数据组成和聚合选择，然后由目标、上下文和用例可能存在很大差异的用户继承。排行榜排名隐式反映了包含哪些提示、它们出现的频率以及结果如何汇总为单个分数。全球综合排名掩盖了主题、领域或任务类型之间的巨大差异。对于许多现实场景，特定数据子集的性能可能比整体平均值更重要，但这种区别很少可见或可操作。This asymmetry raises a fundamental concern: evaluation outcomes are largely determined by data composition and aggregation choices made by others, and then inherited by users whose goals, contexts, and use cases may differ substantially. Leaderboard rankings implicitly reflect which prompts are included, how frequently they appear, and how results are aggregated into a single score. Global aggregate rankings obscure substantial variation across topics, domains, or task types. For many real-world scenarios, performance on particular data subsets may matter far more than overall averages, yet such distinctions are rarely visible or actionable.

针对这一限制的一种常见反应是分解评估结果One common response to this limitation is to disaggregate evaluation results(Barocas et al., 2021; Madaio et al., 2022)例如，通过报告预定义切片的性能或使用自动化方法来识别数据中有意义的子组。虽然这些方法很有价值，但通常假设切片的结构和相对重要性是预先固定的。然而，在实践中，用户经常需要探索数据的不同部分如何对整体结果做出贡献，并根据其上下文自行决定哪些子集值得更加重视。, for example by reporting performance across predefined slices or by using automated methods to identify meaningful subgroups in the data. While valuable, these approaches typically assume that the structure and relative importance of slices are fixed in advance. In practice, however, users often need to explore how different parts of the data contribute to overall results, and to decide for themselves which subsets deserve greater emphasis based on their context.

在本文中，我们认为法学硕士评估应该支持这种交互式探索和重新加权过程。我们建议将排行榜评估视为一种意义建构活动，而不是要求用户采用固定的全球排名或一组固定的分类视图，用户可以在其中检查数据切片，调整其相对重要性，并观察排名如何变化。In this paper, we argue that LLM evaluation should support this interactive exploration and reweighting process. Rather than asking users to take a fixed global ranking, or a fixed set of disaggregated views, we propose treating leaderboard evaluation as a sensemaking activity, where users can examine data slices, adjust their relative importance, and observe how rankings change as a result.

为了支持这一论点，我们对流行的 LMArena（以前称为 Chatbot Arena）基准测试中使用的数据集进行了深入分析To ground this argument, we conduct an in-depth analysis of the dataset used in the popular LMArena (formerly Chatbot Arena) benchmark(Chiang et al., 2024)。它揭示了即时覆盖范围的巨大偏差以及不同数据切片的模型排名的显着差异。这些发现表明，单个聚合排名如何反映数据集的主要组成，同时隐藏当注意力转移到特定子集时出现的权衡。. It reveals substantial skews in prompt coverage and pronounced variability in model rankings across different data slices. These findings demonstrate how a single aggregate ranking can reflect the dominant composition of the dataset, while concealing trade-offs that emerge when attention shifts to specific subsets.

在此分析的基础上，我们设计了一个交互式可视化工具作为设计探针，以探索用户如何参与分类排行榜结果。该界面公开了评估数据集的组成，并允许用户定义重要的提示片段并调整其相对重点，从而可以观察当用户将注意力转移到不同数据子集时模型排名如何变化。可视化作为我们分析的具体实例，以检查用户交互如何支持对排行榜结果进行更多上下文解释。Building on this analysis, we design an interactive visualization tool as a design probe to explore how users might engage with disaggregated leaderboard results. The interface exposes the composition of the evaluation dataset and allows users to define important prompt slices and adjust their relative emphasis, making it possible to observe how model rankings change as users shift their attention across different subsets of data. The visualization serves as a concrete instantiation of our analysis to examine how user interaction can support more contextual interpretation of leaderboard outcomes.

我们进行了一项小型定性研究 (N=10)，以了解从业者如何在模型选择过程中使用此设计。参与者使用我们的系统检查不同的提示类型，并探索强调不同的部分如何影响他们的需求和限制下的排名。这些观察结果表明，交互式、基于切片的视图可以帮助用户超越全球排名并更批判性地解释排行榜结果。We conducted a small qualitative study (N=10) to understand how practitioners would use this design in their model selection process. Participants used our system to inspect different prompt types and explore how emphasizing different slices affected rankings under their needs and constraints. These observations suggest that interactive, slice-based views can help users move beyond global rankings and interpret leaderboard results more critically.

  • •

2. 相关工作Related Work

2.1. LLM评估中的基准测试实践Benchmarking Practices in LLM Evaluation

虽然基准测试已成为人工智能评估事实上的标准，但其主导地位带来了重大的实际限制。当前的基准无法代表现实世界使用的全部范围；它们充当有限样本而不是一般智力的衡量标准While benchmarking has become the de facto standard for AI evaluation, its dominance has introduced significant practical limitations. Current benchmarks cannot represent the full scope of real-world use; they act as finite samples rather than measures of general intelligence(Raji et al., 2021)。过分强调排行榜竞争改变了评价 进入一些人所说的“人工智能作为一项运动”，超越基准分数成为目标. The overemphasis on leaderboard competition has transformed evaluation into what some call “AI as a sport,” where beating benchmark scores becomes the goal(Orr and Kang, 2024)。研究工作往往围绕少量重复使用和回收的数据集进行循环，鼓励对特定基准进行优化，但牺牲了更广泛的适用性. Research efforts tend to cycle around a small number of datasets that are repeatedly reused and recycled, encouraging optimization for specific benchmarks at the expense of broader applicability(Koch et al., 2021)。这些实践假设基准测试集代表现实世界，但是当数据集反映有偏见或 不完整的表述，评估本身就变得系统化 有偏见的. These practices assume that benchmark test sets are representative of the real world, but when datasets reflect biased or incomplete representations, the evaluations themselves become systematically biased(Buolamwini and Gebru, 2018).

为了评估语言模型的不同能力，该领域越来越多地采用大规模、多任务基准测试To assess diverse capabilities of language models, the field has increasingly adopted large-scale, multi-task benchmarks(Hendrycks et al., 2021; Srivastava et al., 2023; Chung et al., 2024; Suzgun et al., 2023)。与此同时，评估实践正在转向整体方法，旨在捕获更广泛的指标和模型行为. In parallel, evaluation practices are shifting toward holistic approaches that aim to capture a broader range of metrics and model behaviors(Shevlane et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2022)。例如，赫尔姆. For example, HELM(Liang et al., 2022)评估模型在准确性之外的性能，结合校准、鲁棒性、公平性、毒性和效率等指标。然而，随着基准扩展到涵盖更多任务和维度，它们的总体排名变得越来越不稳定，并且对看似微小的变化变得越来越敏感assesses model performance beyond accuracy, incorporating measures such as calibration, robustness, fairness, toxicity, and efficiency. However, as benchmarks expand to encompass more tasks and dimensions, their aggregate rankings become increasingly unstable and sensitive to seemingly minor changes(Zhang and Hardt, 2024).

2.2. 基于偏好的评估和排行榜Preference-based Evaluation and Leaderboards

法学硕士的评估已从基于参考的指标转向基于偏好的判断，反映了语言生成任务的开放性和主观性。 LMSYS 小组通过使用成对偏好比较推广了这种方法，最初采用强大的法学硕士作为评委，为模型质量提供可扩展的代理The evaluation of LLMs has shifted from reference-based metrics toward preference-based judgments, reflecting the open-ended and subjective nature of language generation tasks. The LMSYS group popularized this approach by using pairwise preference comparisons, initially employing strong LLMs as judges to provide a scalable proxy for model quality(Team, 2023; Zheng et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024b; Dubois et al., 2024)。 在此工作的基础上，同一小组推出了 Chatbot Arena. Building on this work, the same group introduced Chatbot Arena(Chiang et al., 2024)，后来更名为 LMArena，, later rebranded as LMArena,111请注意，该平台于 2026 年 1 月下旬再次从 LMArena 更名为 Arena。在本文中，我们使用 LMArena 来反映进行大部分研究时的名称。Note that the platform was again rebranded from LMArena to Arena in late January 2026. In this paper, we use LMArena to reflect the name when most of this research was conducted.用户并排比较两个模型的响应并投票选出他们喜欢的响应。这些众包判断通过基于 Elo 的评级系统进行汇总，生成一个不断更新的排行榜，旨在反映现实世界的人类效用。where users compare two model responses side-by-side and vote for their preferred response. These crowdsourced judgments are aggregated using an Elo-based rating system to produce a continuously updated leaderboard intended to reflect real-world human utility.

最近的研究揭示了 LMArena 式平台和基于偏好的评估的根本局限性。 Elo 等聚合机制掩盖了不同任务之间的性能异质性Recent studies have revealed fundamental limitations in LMArena-style platforms and preference-based evaluations. Aggregation mechanisms such as Elo obscure performance heterogeneity across different tasks(Rofin et al., 2023; Lanctot et al., 2023; Boubdir et al., 2024; Singh et al., 2025)，对于非常简单或高度客观的查询，评估不成比例地发生，这意味着关系更多地由内在查询属性驱动，而不是由模型功能驱动, and evaluations occur disproportionately for queries that are very easy or highly objective, meaning ties are driven more by intrinsic query properties than by model capability(Tang et al., 2025)。该系统也容易受到对抗性操纵. The systems are also vulnerable to adversarial manipulation(Min et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025; Frick et al., 2025)。 也许最关键的是，基于偏好的评估倾向于优先考虑感知的帮助，同时弱约束其他重要维度，如事实正确性、诚实性和安全性. Perhaps most critically, the preference-based evaluation tends to prioritize perceived helpfulness, while weakly constraining other important dimensions, like factual correctness, honesty, and safety(Feuer et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024; Wu and Aji, 2025)。 这些局限性并非源于任何特定的评估方法，而是源于对汇总偏好信号的结构性依赖，这促使我们关注分类和透明的评估方法。. These limitations stem not from any specific evaluation method, but from the structural reliance on aggregated preference signals, motivating our focus on disaggregated and transparent evaluation approaches.

2.3. 子组分析和交互式切片Subgroup Analysis and Interactive Slicing

全局聚合指标通常会掩盖不同领域、人口群体和用户意图之间的性能异质性。 未能分解绩效数据会抹去少数群体的经历并加剧系统性的社会不平等Global aggregate metrics often obscure performance heterogeneity across diverse domains, demographic groups, and user intents. Failing to disaggregate performance data erases minority group experiences and reinforces systemic social inequities(Buolamwini and Gebru, 2018)，这在医疗保健等高风险领域尤其重要, which can be particularly consequential in high-stakes domains like healthcare(Obermeyer et al., 2019; Seyyed-Kalantari et al., 2021)。 总体指标在结构上无法确保人工智能系统的公平性和责任感，因此迫切需要分类评估. Aggregate metrics are structurally incapable of ensuring fairness and responsibility in AI systems, creating a critical need for disaggregated evaluation(Herlihy et al., 2024; Diaz and Madaio, 2024; Khodak et al., 2024; Pfohl et al., 2025)。 行为测试方法同样强调 系统化、细粒度的探测对于暴露局部故障的重要性 模式. Behavioral testing methodologies similarly emphasize the importance of systematic, fine-grained probing to expose localized failure modes(Ribeiro et al., 2020; Wu et al., 2019)。 这些方法可以确保人工智能的好处在一系列利益相关者和用例之间公平分配. These approaches can ensure that the benefits of AI are distributed equitably across a range of stakeholders and use cases(Barocas et al., 2021; Madaio et al., 2022).

人机交互 (HCI) 社区的一个重要见解是，分类评估必须 具有互动性。 交互式工具不是向用户提供一组固定的切片，而是允许用户定义、 探索并比较对其特定上下文重要的切片A key insight from the human-computer interaction (HCI) community is that disaggregated evaluation must be interactive. Rather than presenting users with a fixed set of slices, interactive tools allow users to define, explore, and compare slices that matter for their specific context(Cabrera et al., 2023; Sivaraman et al., 2025; Kahng et al., 2025)。 这些系统利用可视化分析 帮助用户通过迭代理解复杂模式的技术， 人类引导的探索，强调用户代理. These systems leverage visual analytics techniques that help users make sense of complex patterns through iterative, human-guided exploration, emphasizing user agency(Cabrera et al., 2019; Zhang et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2024)。 这种交互式理念不仅限于模型评估，还延伸到了文档记录。 交互式模型卡表明静态文档对于不同的利益相关者来说是不够的，提倡使用允许用户交互式探测模型边界的工具. This interactive philosophy extends beyond model evaluation to documentation. The Interactive Model Cards suggest that static documentation is insufficient for diverse stakeholders, advocating for tools that allow users to interactively probe model boundaries(Crisan et al., 2022)。我们的工作建立在这种交互式切片方法的基础上 排行榜 评估，这一设置在之前的工作中很少受到关注。虽然现有 工具专注于分析单个模型或成对比较，我们使用户能够交互式地定义评估优先级，同时分析许多模型的相对地位。. Our work builds on this interactive slicing approach to leaderboard evaluation, a setting that has received little attention in prior work. While existing tools focus on analyzing individual models or pairwise comparisons, we enable users to interactively define evaluation priorities that simultaneously analyze the relative standing of many models.

4. 交互式可视化界面Interactive Visualization Interface

基于我们的分析，我们引入了一个交互式可视化界面作为设计探针，以探索用户如何 以反映自己的目标和背景的方式积极塑造评估。Building on our analysis, we introduce an interactive visualization interface as a design probe to explore how users might actively shape evaluation in ways that reflect their own goals and contexts.

4.1. 设计目标Design Goals

我们的目标不是解决分析中揭示的所有限制，而是通过一个界面探索核心思想，该界面指向参与排行榜评估的不同方式。该设计以三个关键目标为指导：Our goal is not to address every limitation revealed in our analysis, but to explore a core idea through an interface that points toward a different way of engaging with leaderboard evaluation. The design is guided by three key goals:

  1. (1)

    首先，我们的目标是将评估构成的控制权从基准设计者（例如 LMArena 中的人群用户）转移到开发基于 LLM 的应用程序的利益相关者。用户应该能够根据自己的优先级选择和加权提示类别来构建自己的评估数据集，而不是依赖于基准测试的原始数据构成确定的固定总体指标。First, we aim to shift control over evaluation composition from benchmark designers, such as crowd users in LMArena, to stakeholders who develop LLM-based applications. Instead of relying on a fixed overall metric determined by the benchmark’s original data composition, users should be able to construct their own evaluation dataset by selecting and weighting prompt categories according to their priorities.

  2. (2)

    其次，我们认识到用户无法预先完全指定理想的评估设置。因此，界面支持增量意义建构，允许用户通过交互迭代探索、细化和调整他们的评价选择。Second, we recognize that users cannot fully specify an ideal evaluation setup upfront. Therefore, the interface supports incremental sensemaking, allowing users to iteratively explore, refine, and adjust their evaluation choices through interaction.

  3. (3)

    第三，该界面不将评估限制于抽象类别和聚合模型分数，而是强调对具体提示示例以及类别和模型之间的交叉模式的访问。这使用户能够更好地理解为什么模型在各个切片上表现不同，而不仅仅是它们的排名。Third, rather than limiting evaluation to abstract categories and aggregate model scores, the interface emphasizes access to concrete prompt examples and to the intersection patterns between categories and models. This allows users to better understand why models perform differently across slices, not just how they rank.

6. 讨论Discussion

我们讨论了我们的研究结果对评估实践和支持更负责任地使用排行榜的交互式工具设计的影响。We discuss the implications of our findings for evaluation practice and for the design of interactive tools that support more responsible use of leaderboards.

6.1. 排行榜作为一种意义建构过程Leaderboards as a Sensemaking Process

我们的数据集分析表明，LMArena 中的模型排名在不同提示类别之间存在很大差异。特别是，我们观察到数据处理和数学等类别的排名存在明显差异，以及政治争议提示的不稳定。这些结果表明，排行榜排名并不能稳定地反映模型质量的基本统一概念，而是高度取决于所强调的数据子集。Our dataset analysis shows that model rankings in LMArena vary substantially across prompt categories. In particular, we observed sharp ranking divergences in categories such as data processing and mathematics, as well as instability in politically contested prompts. These results indicate that leaderboard rankings are not stable reflections of an underlying, unified notion of model quality, but are instead highly contingent on which subsets of data are emphasized.

在这方面，排行榜评估最好被理解为一个意义建构过程，而不是一个明确的比较。评估的作用从确定单个“最佳”模型转变为了解有关数据组成和任务重要性的不同假设如何导致不同的结论。我们的交互式界面通过使这些假设明确且可操作来探索这个方向，允许用户探索随着注意力跨切片转移而排名如何变化。In this regard, leaderboard evaluation is better understood as a sensemaking process rather than a definitive comparison. The role of evaluation shifts from identifying a single “best” model to understanding how different assumptions about data composition and task importance lead to different conclusions. Our interactive interface explores this direction by making these assumptions explicit and manipulable, allowing users to explore how rankings change as attention shifts across slices.

6.2. 对负责任的人工智能评估的影响Implications for Responsible AI Evaluation

我们的分析表明，基于偏好的评估在不同的任务类型中表现不同。即使答案是确定性的，评估者之间的风格偏好也会有所不同，并且在价值依赖领域中，对于多元化或采取立场的回应是否更可取，也没有一致的共识。这些发现凸显了聚合偏好信号的结构局限性。Our analyses show that preference-based evaluation behaves differently across task types. Even when answers are deterministic, stylistic preferences vary across evaluators, and in value-dependent domains, there is no consistent agreement on whether pluralistic or position-taking responses are preferable. These findings highlight a structural limitation of aggregated preference signals.

这种合并产生了重要的后果。当排名是根据不同评估者的汇总偏好得出时，它们可能会系统地反映更活跃或占主导地位的群体的价值观，特别是在政治敏感的背景下。我们的结果表明，这种影响并非异常，而是评估范式所固有的。这意味着每种评估方法的范围和局限性都必须显而易见。分类和交互式界面（例如本工作中探讨的界面）可以帮助揭示哪些地方总体排名稳定，哪些地方依赖于评估者不同的判断。This conflation has important consequences. When rankings are derived from pooled preferences across diverse evaluators, they may systematically reflect the values of more active or dominant groups, particularly in politically sensitive contexts. Our results suggest that such effects are not anomalies, but inherent to the evaluation paradigm. The implication is that each evaluation approach’s scope and limitations must be made visible. Disaggregated and interactive interfaces, such as those explored in this work, can help surface where aggregate rankings are stable and where they rely on judgments that vary across evaluators.

6.3. 交互式评估的局限性和风险Limits and Risks of Interactive Evaluation

虽然交互式切片解决了总体排名的一些不透明问题，但我们的研究结果也警告不要将用户定义的评估视为本质上更可靠或公平。激发互动探索的相同分析也表明，判断可能不稳定，特别是当反应相似或评估者的价值观存在分歧时。允许用户定义切片和权重会使这些不稳定性变得明显，但并不能解决它们。While interactive slicing addresses some of the opacity of aggregate rankings, our findings also caution against treating user-defined evaluation as inherently more reliable or fair. The same analyses that motivate interactive exploration also show that judgments can be unstable, particularly when responses are similar or when evaluators’ values diverge. Allowing users to define slices and weights makes these instabilities visible, but it does not resolve them.

此外，将控制权转移给用户也会带来风险。用户可以定义反映狭窄目标、不完整理解或组织偏见的评估配置。正如我们的用户研究表明的那样，该界面支持反思和探索，但不能保证共识或正确性。这些局限性强化了我们分析的核心教训：评估结果是偶然的且具有价值的。因此，交互式工具应被视为呈现和协商这些突发事件的机制，而不是产生单一的权威排名。Moreover, shifting control to users introduces its own risks. Users may define evaluation configurations that reflect narrow objectives, incomplete understanding, or organizational biases. As our user study suggests, the interface supports reflection and exploration, but it does not guarantee consensus or correctness. These limitations reinforce the central lesson of our analysis: evaluation outcomes are contingent and value-laden. Interactive tools should therefore be seen as mechanisms for surfacing and negotiating these contingencies, not for producing a single authoritative ranking.

6.4. 超越胜率：支持多种指标Beyond Win Rates: Supporting Multiple Metrics

尽管我们发现了基于偏好的评估的局限性，但我们的界面目前使用获胜率作为其唯一的绩效指标。虽然跨切片的交互式分解可以帮助确定这些限制最明显的地方，但不同的切片可能需要根本不同的评估标准，而仅凭偏好判断无法捕获这些标准。理想情况下，用户不仅能够选择要强调的提示切片，还能够选择对每个切片应用哪些指标。例如，对于具有确定性答案（例如数学）的提示，用户可以选择使用基于正确性的指标。我们的界面使用胜率，因为 LMArena 数据集仅提供成对偏好判断，但切片和加权范式可以扩展以支持多个指标。未来的工作可以探索用户如何跨切片交互地选择和组合不同的指标，以便指标的选择更好地反映对每种任务类型重要的内容。Although we identified limitations of preference-based evaluation, our interface currently uses win rates as its sole performance metric. While interactive disaggregation across slices can help identify where these limitations are most pronounced, different slices may call for fundamentally different evaluation criteria that preference judgments alone cannot capture. Ideally, users would be able to choose not only which prompt slices to emphasize, but also which metrics to apply to each slice. For instance, for prompts with deterministic answers such as mathematics, users may choose to use correctness-based metrics. Our interface uses win rates because the LMArena dataset provides only pairwise preference judgments, but the slicing and weighting paradigm can be extended to support multiple metrics. Future work can explore how users might interactively select and compose different metrics across slices, so that the choice of metric better reflects what matters for each task type.

7. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们分析了 LLM 排行榜排名如何由数据集组成决定，并使用此分析来激发交互式、用户定义的评估方法。我们对 LMArena 数据集的分析表明，不同提示类别的模型排名存在很大差异，并且基于偏好的判断通常应用于不能可靠反映正确性或价值差异的问题。 为了解决这个问题，我们引入了一个交互式可视化界面，允许用户选择、加权和检查提示切片并观察排名如何变化。一项定性用户研究表明，交互式界面可以帮助用户主动构建评估配置和 根据他们的用例来解释他们的决定。In this paper, we analyzed how LLM leaderboard rankings are shaped by dataset composition and used this analysis to motivate an interactive, user-defined evaluation approach. Our analysis of the LMArena dataset showed that model rankings differ substantially across prompt categories, and that preference-based judgments are often applied to problems where they do not reliably reflect correctness or value differences. To address this, we introduced an interactive visualization interface that allows users to select, weight, and inspect prompt slices and observe how rankings change. A qualitative user study suggests that interactive interfaces can help users actively construct evaluation configurations and reason about their decisions based on their use cases.

这项工作为未来的研究留下了几个方向。首先，除了当前的三级主题层次结构之外，未来的系统可以支持跨维度的更丰富的交叉，例如语言、类别组合和完全用户定义的切片。 对于许多可能的切片，交互功能（例如排序或过滤以及突出显示具有高排名差异的切片）可以帮助用户导航空间，尤其是那些还不知道从哪里开始的用户。 其次，我们的研究结果表明，具有确定性答案的任务和由主观或基于价值的判断驱动的任务不应依赖相同的基于偏好的信号；未来的工作可以 支持多种类型的指标，并允许用户选择应用于每个切片的指标，如第 1 节中所述This work leaves several directions for future research. First, beyond the current three-level topic hierarchy, future systems could support richer intersections across dimensions such as language, category combinations, and fully user-defined slices. With many possible slices, interactive features, such as sorting or filtering, and highlighting slices with high ranking divergence, could help users navigate the space, especially those who do not yet know where to begin. Second, our findings suggest that tasks with deterministic answers and tasks driven by subjective or value-based judgments should not rely on the same preference-based signal; future work could support multiple types of metrics and allow users to select which metrics to apply to each slice, as discussed in Section6。 第三，虽然我们专注于LMArena， 扩展此分析 到其他排行榜将获得更广泛的见解。 此外，由于排行榜数据不断发展，支持主题和排名的增量更新可能是一个重要的实用方向。 最后，部署更成熟的界面版本并进行更大规模的纵向用户研究将有助于评估交互式、用户定义的评估如何影响模型选择和负责任的部署实践。. Third, while we focused on LMArena, extending this analysis to other leaderboards would enable broader insights. Furthermore, because leaderboard data evolve continuously, supporting incremental updates to topics and rankings could be an important practical direction. Finally, deploying a more mature version of the interface and conducting larger, longitudinal user studies would help assess how interactive, user-defined evaluation affects model selection and responsible deployment practices.
