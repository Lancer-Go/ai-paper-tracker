重新思考高维度基于分数的数据同化的前向过程Rethinking Forward Processes for Score-Based Data Assimilation in High Dimensions

尹恩菲Eunbi Yoon1, 金东韩, Donghan Kim2、金大旭, and Dae Wook Kim1

1韩国科学技术院脑与认知科学系，大田韩国Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences, KAIST, Daejeon, South Korea

2韩国科学技术院数学科学系，大田韩国Department of Mathematical Sciences, KAIST, Daejeon, South Korea

 
摘要Abstract

数据同化是通过整合模型预测和噪声观测来估计动力系统随时间演化的状态的过程。 它通常被表述为贝叶斯过滤，但经典过滤器经常在高维精度或计算可行性方面遇到困难。 最近，基于分数的生成模型已成为高维数据同化的可扩展方法，能够对复杂分布进行精确建模和采样。 然而，现有的基于分数的过滤器通常指定独立于数据同化的前向过程。 因此，测量更新步骤取决于似然分数的启发式近似，这会随着时间的推移累积误差并降低性能。 在这里，我们提出了一种测量感知的基于分数的滤波器（MASF），它直接从测量方程定义测量感知的前向过程。 这种结构使得似然得分在分析上易于处理：对于线性测量，我们得出精确的似然得分并将其与学习的先验得分相结合以获得后验得分。 涵盖一系列设置（包括高维数据集）的数值实验表明，与现有基于分数的滤波器相比，准确性和稳定性有所提高。Data assimilation is the process of estimating the time-evolving state of a dynamical system by integrating model predictions and noisy observations. It is commonly formulated as Bayesian filtering, but classical filters often struggle with accuracy or computational feasibility in high dimensions. Recently, score-based generative models have emerged as a scalable approach for high-dimensional data assimilation, enabling accurate modeling and sampling of complex distributions. However, existing score-based filters often specify the forward process independently of the data assimilation. As a result, the measurement-update step depends on heuristic approximations of the likelihood score, which can accumulate errors and degrade performance over time. Here, we propose a measurement-aware score-based filter (MASF) that defines a measurement-aware forward process directly from the measurement equation. This construction makes the likelihood score analytically tractable: for linear measurements, we derive the exact likelihood score and combine it with a learned prior score to obtain the posterior score. Numerical experiments covering a range of settings, including high-dimensional datasets, demonstrate improved accuracy and stability over existing score-based filters.

1 简介Introduction

数据同化通过将动态模型预测与随时间变化的测量信息相结合来估计部分和噪声观测的潜在状态Data assimilation estimates latent states from partial and noisy observations by combining dynamical model predictions with measurement information over timeEvensen (2009a); Reich and Cotter (2015)。 它通常被表述为贝叶斯过滤，在时间更新和测量更新之间交替. It is often formulated as Bayesian filtering, which alternates between a time update and a measurement updateSärkkä (2013)。 在时间更新中，当前状态在状态方程下传播以产生预测. In the time update, the current state is propagated under the state equation to produce a predictionAsch et al. (2016)。然后，通过测量方程使用观测值在测量更新中对此进行校正。 此类过滤问题广泛出现在必须从不完整信息推断时间演化动态的领域，例如地球物理预测和生物过程. This is then corrected in the measurement update using observations via the measurement equation. Such filtering problems arise broadly in domains where time-evolving dynamics must be inferred from incomplete information, e.g., geophysical forecasting, and biological processesChipilski et al. (2020); Aksoy et al. (2009); Cogan et al. (2021)。 尽管最佳贝叶斯滤波器已明确定义，但在高维非线性设置中精确计算很少可行，因为两种更新都涉及通常不允许闭合形式表达式的积分. Although the optimal Bayesian filters are well defined, exact computation is rarely feasible in high-dimensional nonlinear settings, since both updates involve integrals that typically admit no closed-form expressionsSärkkä (2013); Doucet et al. (2001).

在实践中，两个广泛使用的贝叶斯滤波器系列是卡尔曼滤波器和粒子滤波器In practice, two widely used families of Bayesian filters are Kalman filters and particle filtersKalman (1960); Evensen (2009b)。 卡尔曼滤波器，例如集成卡尔曼滤波器（EnKF），通过高斯矩近似后验并递归更新它们. Kalman filters, such as the ensemble Kalman filter (EnKF), approximate the posterior through Gaussian moments and update them recursivelyKalman (1960); Whitaker and Hamill (2002)。 它们的计算效率很高，但当后验非高斯或状态和测量方程高度非线性时，它们的精度可能会降低. They are computationally efficient, but their accuracy can degrade when the posterior is non-Gaussian or when the state and measurement equations are highly nonlinearAsch et al. (2016); Houtekamer and Mitchell (1998)。 粒子滤波器，例如辅助粒子滤波器，用加权样本表示后验，可以更忠实地捕获非高斯结构. Particle filters, such as the auxiliary particle filter, represent the posterior with weighted samples and can capture non-Gaussian structure more faithfullyGordon et al. (1993); Andrieu et al. (2010);然而，它们在高维度上经常遭受严重的重量退化; however, they often suffer from severe weight degeneracy in high dimensionsArulampalam et al. (2002); Snyder et al. (2008).

最近，人们对基于分数的生成模型作为表示复杂高维分布的工具越来越感兴趣Recently, there has been growing interest in score-based generative models as a tool for representing complex high-dimensional distributionsSong and Ermon (2019); Song et al. (2021); Dhariwal and Nichol (2021)。 这些模型学习得分函数、对数密度的梯度，并通过运行逆时过程来实现采样. These models learn the score function, the gradient of the log-density, and enable sampling by running a reverse-time processHyvärinen (2005); Vincent (2011)。 受此启发，最近在数据同化方面的工作训练得分模型来估计先验得分，并通过似然得分包括采样时的测量值. Motivated by this, recent work in data assimilation trains score models to estimate the prior score and include measurements at sampling time through a likelihood scoreRozet and Louppe (2023); Bao et al. (2024a, b); Ding et al. (2025).

一种代表性的方法是基于分数的滤波器（SF），它应用标准的前向过程来生成扰动状态并训练分数模型来估计相应的先验分数A representative approach is the score-based filter (SF), which applies a standard forward process to generate perturbed states and trains a score model to estimate the corresponding prior scoreBao et al. (2024a)。 在下文中，我们使用基于分数的过滤来广泛地指代这一系列方法； SF具体表示算法为. In what follows, we use score-based filtering to refer broadly to this family of methods; SF specifically denotes the algorithm ofBao et al. (2024a)。 在测量更新步骤中，SF 通过近似相对于扰动状态的似然得分来合并测量，并使用学习的先验得分和近似似然得分的总和来指导逆时采样。 然而，这种似然得分近似在理论上是不合理的，并且可能导致在连续更新过程中累积的错误。. At the measurement-update step, SF incorporates measurements by approximating the likelihood score with respect to the perturbed state, and guides reverse-time sampling using the sum of the learned prior score and the approximated likelihood score. However, this likelihood score approximation is not theoretically justified and can lead to errors that accumulate over sequential updates.

基于分数的顺序朗之万采样（SSLS）改为采用分数匹配与退火朗之万蒙特卡罗Score-based Sequential Langevin Sampling (SSLS) instead adopts score matching with Annealing Langevin Monte CarloSong and Ermon (2019)，其中采样只需要目标分布的分数, where sampling only requires the score of the target distributionDing et al. (2025)。 在此框架中，可以从测量方程导出精确的似然得分，从而避免需要近似扰动的似然得分。 然而，基于朗之万的采样通常依赖于对噪声水平的退火，这会大大增加采样步骤的数量并使推理计算成本高昂. In this framework, the exact likelihood score can be derived from the measurement equation, avoiding the need to approximate a perturbed likelihood score. Nevertheless, Langevin-based sampling typically relies on annealing over noise levels, which can substantially increase the number of sampling steps and make inference computationally expensiveSong et al. (2021).

一个关键的挑战是保留基于分数的过滤器的优势，同时消除现有方法的主要瓶颈。 这是数据同化中的一个问题，其中测量更新是随着时间的推移顺序执行的；因此，与条件生成不同，即使很小的近似误差也会随着时间的推移而累积并降低性能。 主要障碍是采样是在前向过程产生的扰动状态上演化的，而调节则需要评估这些相同扰动状态下的似然得分。 当独立于测量方程选择前向过程时，扰动状态引起的似然通常很棘手，迫使每次更新时都进行似然分数近似。A key challenge is to retain the advantages of score-based filters while removing the main bottleneck of existing approaches. This is a problem in data assimilation where the measurement updates are performed sequentially over time; as a result, even small approximation errors can accumulate and degrade performance over time, unlike in conditional generation. The main obstacle is that sampling evolves on perturbed states produced by a forward process, whereas conditioning requires evaluating the likelihood score at those same perturbed states. When the forward process is chosen independently of the measurement equation, the likelihood induced on perturbed states is generally intractable, forcing a likelihood score approximation at every update.

为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一种基于测量感知分数的滤波器（MASF），它直接从测量方程定义了一个测量感知前向过程。 该方法确保沿着扰动轨迹的似然得分在分析上仍然可行，如图 2 所示。To address this problem, we propose a Measurement-Aware Score-based Filter (MASF) that defines a measurement-aware forward process directly from the measurement equation. In the linear measurement case, we construct the forward process by interpolating between the identity and the measurement operator, progressively mapping from state space to the measurement space over time; see Fig.2为例。 这产生了沿扰动轨迹的似然得分的闭合形式表达式，我们将其与学习的先验得分相结合以获得后验得分并导出逆时采样，而无需临时近似和退火。 Lorenz-63、Lorenz-96 和 Kolmogorov 流的实验表明，所提出的方法在广泛的设置（包括高维数据集）上持续提高了现有基于分数的滤波器的准确性和稳定性，在理论上和实验上都支持该方法。for an illustration. Experiments on Lorenz–63, Lorenz–96, and Kolmogorov flow demonstrate that the proposed method consistently improves accuracy and stability over existing score-based filters over a broad range of settings, including high-dimensional datasets, supporting the approach both theoretically and experimentally.

6 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Works

MASF 有一些局限性，为未来的工作指明了方向。 首先，当前的公式假设状态空间和测量空间具有相同的维度，尽管在现实环境中经常发生维度不匹配。 解决这个问题的一个自然扩展是结合表示学习，从而在共享潜在空间中进行过滤MASF has several limitations that suggest directions for future work. First, the current formulation assumes the same dimensions for the state and measurement spaces, although dimensional mismatch often occurs in real-world settings. A natural extension to address this is to combine representation learning, enabling filtering in a shared latent spaceAmendola et al. (2020); Fan et al. (2025); Pasmans et al. (2025)。 其次，我们的矩匹配 SDE 的适定性依赖于具有非负谱的测量算子。 当违反此假设时，用于定义前向过程的插值可能无效。因此，可能需要替代的插值方案，例如扩展到复数域. Second, the well-posedness of our moment-matching SDE relies on the measurement operator having a nonnegative spectrum. When this assumption is violated, the interpolation used to define the forward process may be invalid. Thus, alternative interpolation schemes may be required, such as extensions to the complex domainHigham (1986); Gawlik and Leok (2018)。 第三，将矩匹配 SDE 的想法扩展到非线性测量并不简单. Third, extending the idea for moment-matching SDE to nonlinear measurements is not straightforwardSolin and Särkkä (2019)。 在线性情况下，闭式矩产生平均 ODE 和协方差的 Lyapunov 方程，它们共同指定一致的前向过程。 对于非线性测量，力矩约束通常不会唯一地确定全局一致的漂移，并且随着时间的推移与规定力矩匹配的单个 SDE 可能不存在. In the linear case, closed-form moments yield a mean ODE and a Lyapunov equation for the covariance, which together specify a consistent forward process. For nonlinear measurements, moment constraints generally do not uniquely determine a globally consistent drift, and a single SDE that matches prescribed moments over time may not existFaedo et al. (2021); Varona et al. (2019)。 第四，当前基于分数的滤波器的实现可能在计算上要求很高，因为随着状态分布的发展，先前的分数可能需要在每个测量步骤中重新训练。 下一步是设计共享参数模型. Fourth, current implementations of score-based filters can be computationally demanding because the prior score may need to be retrained at each measurement step as the state distribution evolves. Designing shared-parameter models is a next stepBecker et al. (2019)。 最后，MASF 可以被视为一种新的条件生成框架，它将分析似然得分与学习到的先验得分相结合。 这种观点表明对条件推理问题具有更广泛的适用性。. Finally, MASF can be viewed as a new conditional generation framework that combines an analytic likelihood score with a learned prior score. This perspective suggests broader applicability to conditional inference problems.

7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一种新的基于分数的滤波器，它将测量方程明确地合并到前向过程中。 这种构造产生沿扰动轨迹的精确似然性，从而能够根据学习的先验分数和分析计算的似然性分数构造后验分数。 因此，所提出的方法执行顺序测量更新，而无需临时似然分数近似。 我们表明，这种理论上有充分依据的方法在具有稀疏测量的混乱和高维基准中优于基线。我们的结果证明了在具有挑战性的现实世界系统中进行稳健的状态跟踪和重建的能力。We proposed a new score-based filter that explicitly merges the measurement equation into the forward process. This construction yields an exact likelihood along the perturbed trajectory, enabling construction of the posterior score from a learned prior score and an analytically computed likelihood score. As a result, the proposed method performs sequential measurement updates without ad hoc likelihood-score approximations. We showed that this theoretically well-grounded approach outperformed baselines, across chaotic and high-dimensional benchmarks with sparse measurements. Our results demonstrate the capability for robust state tracking and reconstruction in challenging real-world systems.
