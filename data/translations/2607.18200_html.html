学习视觉导航的自适应安全裕度Learning Adaptive Safety Margins for Visual Navigation

Junyi Hu1,†, Shuaihang Yuan1,†,‡, Geeta Chandra Raju Bethala1, Anthony Tzes1 and Yi Fang1,∗ 1New York University Abu Dhabi, UAE. †Equal contribution. ‡Project lead. ∗Corresponding author: yfang@nyu.eduProject page: junyi2005.github.io/safety-critic
摘要Abstract

杂乱的室内空间中的机器人经常失败，不是因为它们无法生成无碰撞路径，而是因为固定的安全裕度校准错误：保守的裕度会导致绕道和超时，而宽松的裕度会在感知偏差下导致近边界捷径。 基于扩散的规划器从以自我为中心的 RGB-D 中提出了多种候选轨迹，但可靠的选择仍然是瓶颈。 我们提出了一个上下文条件安全批评器，它学习对扩散建议进行排名的自适应间隙偏好，分解为三个互补项：（i）具有间隙预算惩罚和控制障碍函数残差的安全项，用于航路点和过渡安全，（ii）将平滑度惩罚与安全门绕道比惩罚相结合的效率项，避免绕道而不激励危险的捷径，以及（iii）距离约束匹配项，将学习的预算锚定为实现了 ESDF 许可，以防止利润崩溃。 我们在模拟中使用专有的 ESDF 几何来训练批评家，并通过两阶段的师生程序将其提炼为仅感知的选择器。 在 HM3D 和 MP3D 中的 PointGoal 导航（包括跨数据集传输）上，我们的方法在强扩散、优化和 RL 基线中实现了最高的成功率 (SR) 和按路径长度加权的成功率 (SPL)。 它经过纯粹的模拟训练，可以转移到 Unitree G1 人形机器人上，并在杂乱的室内场景中导航，无需针对特定任务进行调整。Robots in cluttered indoor spaces often fail not because they cannot generate collision-free paths, but because a fixed safety margin is mis-calibrated: conservative margins cause detours and timeouts, while permissive margins lead to near-boundary shortcuts under perception bias. Diffusion-based planners propose diverse trajectory candidates from egocentric RGB-D, yet reliable selection remains the bottleneck. We propose a context-conditioned safety critic that learns an adaptive clearance preference for ranking diffusion proposals, decomposed into three complementary terms: (i) a safety term with a clearance-budget penalty and a control-barrier-function residual for waypoint- and transition-wise safety, (ii) an efficiency term combining a smoothness penalty with a safety-gated detour-ratio penalty that avoids detours without incentivizing risky shortcuts, and (iii) a distance-constraint matching term that anchors the learned budget to realized ESDF clearances to prevent margin collapse. We train the critic with privileged ESDF geometry in simulation and distill it into a perception-only selector via a two-stage teacher–student procedure. On PointGoal navigation in HM3D and MP3D, including cross-dataset transfer, our method achieves the highest success rate (SR) and success weighted by path length (SPL) among strong diffusion, optimization, and RL baselines. Trained purely in simulation, it transfers to a Unitree G1 humanoid and navigates cluttered indoor scenes without task-specific tuning.

I 简介Introduction

移动机器人越来越多地部署在以人为中心的室内环境中，例如家庭、医院和实验室，其中的成功不仅取决于几何可行性，还取决于在执行轨迹时保持足够的间隙。 至关重要的是，所需的间隙取决于环境：局部杂乱、机动要求（例如，狭窄通道中的急转弯）和瞬时感知可靠性可以使相同的测量距离在一种情况下安全，而在另一种情况下则存在风险。这在基于视觉的导航中被放大，其中深度和自我运动估计随着视点、运动和传感伪影的变化而变化，因此最小测量间隙是可执行性的嘈杂代理。 因此，具有全局固定间隙阈值的硬选择规则在两种故障模式之间振荡：开放区域中的过度保守行为（绕道和超时）和密集杂乱中的脆弱行为（感知偏差下的危险捷径）。Mobile robots are increasingly deployed in human-centric indoor environments such as homes, hospitals, and labs, where success depends not only on geometric feasibility but on maintaining sufficient clearance while executing a trajectory. Crucially, the required clearance is context dependent: local clutter, maneuvering demands (e.g., sharp turns in narrow passages), and momentary perception reliability can make the same measured distance safe in one situation and risky in another. This is amplified in vision-based navigation, where depth and egomotion estimates vary with viewpoint, motion, and sensing artifacts, so minimum measured clearance is a noisy proxy for executability. As a result, hard selection rules with a globally fixed clearance threshold oscillate between two failure modes: overly conservative behavior in open areas (detours and timeouts) and brittle behavior in dense clutter (risky shortcuts under perception bias).

基于扩散的政策对基于观察的多模态轨迹分布进行建模，从而实现多样化、可行的建议和实用的生成-选择范式Diffusion-based policies model multi-modal trajectory distributions conditioned on observations, enabling diverse, feasible proposals and a practical generate–select paradigm[4]。 然而，选择器通常是瓶颈：许多扩散导航管道仍然使用具有手动调整的全局固定安全裕度的分析批评器对候选者进行排名. However, the selector is often the bottleneck: many diffusion navigation pipelines still rank candidates using analytic critics with hand-tuned, globally fixed safety margins[1, 7]，它不能同时足够保守以应对严重的混乱，同时又足够宽松以避免在开放空间中走弯路。 这会激励选择者学习“安全距离”的概念，并根据当前场景调整其安全效率权衡。, which cannot be simultaneously conservative enough for tight clutter and permissive enough to avoid detours in open space. This motivates a selector whose notion of “safe distance” is learned and adapts its safety–efficiency trade-off to the current scene.

在这项工作中，我们提出了一种基于扩散的视觉导航的上下文条件安全批评器，它用可学习的间隙偏好取代了固定边缘排名，该偏好由三个互补术语组成： (一)In this work, we propose a context-conditioned safety critic for diffusion-based visual navigation that replaces fixed-margin ranking with a learnable clearance preference, structured into three complementary terms: (i)安全Safety：惩罚清理预算违规行为，并纳入控制障碍功能残差，以实现过渡时期的正规化； (二): penalizes clearance-budget violations and incorporates a control-barrier-function residual for transition-wise regularization; (ii)效率Efficiency：将平滑度惩罚与安全门控绕行率惩罚相结合，主要在有间隙时阻止绕行；和 (三): combines a smoothness penalty with a safety-gated detour-ratio penalty that discourages detours primarily when clearance is available; and (iii)平衡Balance：距离约束匹配项，将学习的预算与实现的间隙对齐，防止退化校准。 我们在匹配的起始目标条件下使用 ESDF 派生的间隙监督和严格的非专家轨迹来训练教师评论家，然后将其提炼为学生选择器，该学生选择器可以根据以自我为中心的 RGB-D 观察来预测评论家分数，从而无需特权几何即可进行部署。: a distance-constraint matching term that aligns the learned budget with realized clearances, preventing degenerate calibration. We train a teacher critic using ESDF-derived clearance supervision and hard non-expert trajectories under matched start–goal conditions, then distill it into a student selector that predicts critic scores from egocentric RGB-D observations, enabling deployment without privileged geometry.

我们在 HM3D 和 MP3D 上、域内和跨数据集传输下评估 PointGoal 导航，我们的方法在强扩散、基于优化和强化学习基线中实现了最高的 SR 和 SPL。我们进一步在杂乱的室内场景中在 Unitree G1 人形机器人上演示了模拟到真实的传输，无需针对特定任务进行调整，并且我们的消融隔离了每个关键组件的贡献。We evaluate PointGoal navigation on HM3D and MP3D, in-domain and under cross-dataset transfer, where our method achieves the highest SR and SPL among strong diffusion, optimization-based, and reinforcement-learning baselines. We further demonstrate sim-to-real transfer on a Unitree G1 humanoid in cluttered indoor scenes without task-specific tuning, and our ablations isolate the contribution of each critic component.

II 相关工作Related Work

端到端视觉导航通过将自我中心观察映射到低级动作或短视野运动片段来闭合感知-行动循环，从而减少感知、映射和规划之间的延迟和错误传播。 Habitat 等大型模拟器提供标准化的具体任务，其中深度 RL 代理（例如 DD-PPO）设置强大的 PointGoal 基线End-to-end visual navigation closes the perception–action loop by mapping egocentric observations to low-level actions or short-horizon motion segments, reducing latency and error propagation across perception, mapping, and planning. Large-scale simulators such as Habitat provide standardized embodied tasks, where deep RL agents (e.g., DD-PPO) set strong PointGoal baselines[11]，以及主动神经 SLAM 等混合系统通过学习映射和自上而下地图上的分层策略添加显式空间记忆, and hybrid systems such as Active Neural SLAM add explicit spatial memory via learned mapping and hierarchical policies over a top-down map[3].

最近的生成决策对动作或轨迹序列的多模态分布进行建模，提供了一种在条件条件下对不同候选者进行采样的原则性方法Recent generative decision-making models multi-modal distributions over action or trajectory sequences, providing a principled way to sample diverse candidates under conditioning[4, 6]。 除了学习的生成器之外，iPlanner 将感知与可微分成本图和双层优化结合起来，通过单个深度测量生成无碰撞路径. Beyond learned generators, iPlanner couples perception with a differentiable cost map and bi-level optimization to produce collision-free paths from a single depth measurement[12]，VLFM 使用预训练的视觉语言模型对候选边界进行评分，以实现零样本导航, and VLFM scores candidate frontiers with a pre-trained vision-language model for zero-shot navigation[13]。 在基于模型的方面，几何驱动的局部规划在与 Voxblox 等高质量距离场配合使用时仍然具有竞争力. On the model-based side, geometry-driven local planning remains competitive when paired with high-quality distance fields such as Voxblox[8]，而 EGO-Planner 执行基于梯度的实时重新规划，无需显式 ESDF 构造, while EGO-Planner performs real-time gradient-based replanning without explicit ESDF construction[14]。 尽管存在差异，这些系统最终依赖于手工制定的评分规则（通常是固定的间隙余量）在候选人之间进行仲裁，这可能会在感知偏差或混乱中过于保守的弯路下产生不安全的捷径。. Despite their differences, these systems ultimately depend on a hand-crafted scoring rule—often a fixed clearance margin—to arbitrate among candidates, which can yield unsafe shortcuts under perception bias or overly conservative detours in clutter.

V 结论Conclusion

我们引入了一个生成-选择框架，用一个可学习的、基于上下文的安全评论家取代了固定的边际，融合了安全性和效率的平衡。在模拟和现实世界测试中，它实现了最高的 SR 和 SPL，并且在杂乱的室内场景中从纯模拟转变为真实的人形机器人，而无需针对特定任务进行调整。消融证实每个关键术语都有助于实现有利的安全效率权衡，并在固定阈值基线上获得一致的收益。We introduced a generate–select framework that replaces fixed margins with a learnable, context-conditioned safety critic fusing safety and efficiency in balance. Across simulation and real-world tests it achieves the highest SR and SPL, and transfers from pure simulation to a real humanoid in cluttered indoor scenes without task-specific tuning. Ablations confirm each critic term contributes to a favorable safety–efficiency trade-off and consistent gains over fixed-threshold baselines.
