什么时候重要？诊断和改善视觉运动模仿策略中的条件视觉接地What Matters, When? Diagnosing and Improving Conditional Visual Grounding in Visuomotor Imitation Policies

摘要Abstract

视觉运动模仿策略可以在精心设计的环境中实现高性能，但当视觉相似的物体与任务相关实体竞争时就会失败。我们将这种行为视为一个问题来研究Visuomotor imitation policies can achieve high performance in curated environments yet fail when visually similar objects compete with task-relevant entities. We study this behavior as a problem of有条件视觉接地conditional visual grounding：哪个视觉实体重要取决于当前的操作阶段，并且在更复杂的任务中，取决于推断的任务状态。使用 Transformers 的动作分块 (ACT)，我们系统地改变对象和容器竞争对手，并定位拾取和放置失败的位置。由此产生的错误是特定于提示和阶段的，并且伴随着学习的视觉表征的相应变化。在这一诊断的指导下，我们评估了干扰物增强、阶段依赖性注意力正则化和基于外观的视觉提示作为补充干预措施，以加强与任务相关的基础，同时保留控制所需的空间信息。这些干预措施大大提高了模拟和物理 UR3e 的鲁棒性。我们进一步在状态条件仪器处理任务的预训练视觉语言动作策略中检查相同的诊断干预原则，其中正确的目的地取决于观察到的仪器状态。总之，这些结果支持条件视觉基础作为诊断和提高不同视觉运动政策学习制度稳健性的有用框架。 8页纸！: which visual entity matters depends on the current manipulation phase and, in more complex tasks, on the inferred task state. Using Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT), we systematically vary object and receptacle competitors and localize failures to picking and placement. The resulting errors are cue- and phase-specific and are accompanied by corresponding changes in learned visual representations. Guided by this diagnosis, we evaluate distractor augmentation, phase-dependent attention regularization, and appearance-based visual prompting as complementary interventions for strengthening task-relevant grounding while preserving spatial information required for control. These interventions substantially improve robustness in simulation and on a physical UR3e. We further examine the same diagnosis-intervention principle in a pretrained vision-language-action policy on a state-conditioned instrument-handling task, where the correct destination depends on the observed instrument state. Together, the results support conditional visual grounding as a useful framework for diagnosing and improving robustness across distinct visuomotor policy-learning regimes. 8 Page paper!

I 简介Introduction

基于视觉的模仿策略通常在精心设计的视觉条件下进行训练和评估，而部署场景可能包含视觉上相似的物体、容器和不相关的杂乱Vision-based imitation policies are commonly trained and evaluated under curated visual conditions, while deployment scenes can contain visually similar objects, receptacles, and irrelevant clutter[1, 2]。添加此类竞争对手既不会改变操纵目标，也不会改变潜在的机器人动态，但会大大降低政策的成功率. Adding such competitors changes neither the manipulation objective nor the underlying robot dynamics, yet can substantially reduce policy success[3, 2]。然而，仅凭总体成功无法区分失去执行操纵能力的政策和保留运动行为但将其应用于错误实体的政策。. Aggregate success alone, however, cannot distinguish between a policy that has lost the ability to execute the manipulation and one that retains the motor behavior but applies it to the wrong entity.

For Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT)[4]，我们检查相位条件相关性：被操纵的物体在目标获取和抓取过程中至关重要，而容器在放置过程中变得至关重要。在我们的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）案例研究中，相关性还受到状态条件的影响：观察到的医疗仪器状态决定了哪个容器是正确的目的地。, we examine phase-conditioned relevance: the manipulated object is critical during target acquisition and grasping, whereas the receptacle becomes critical during placement. In our vision-language-action (VLA) case study, relevance is additionally state-conditioned: the observed state of a medical instrument determines which receptacle is the correct destination.

我们询问视觉失败是否可以定位于基础变得不正确的提示、参照物和执行阶段，以及该诊断是否表明有效的干预措施。受控 ACT 实验首先暴露了不同的物体和容器选择瓶颈。然后，我们评估轻量级以对象为中心的干预措施，并在模拟和物理 UR3e 上验证其效果。最后，我们检查相同的诊断干预原则在状态条件路由任务的预训练 VLA 策略中是否仍然有用。We ask whether visual failures can be localized to the cue, referent, and execution stage at which grounding becomes incorrect, and whether this diagnosis suggests effective interventions. Controlled ACT experiments first expose distinct object- and receptacle-selection bottlenecks. We then evaluate lightweight object-centric interventions and validate their effect in simulation and on a physical UR3e. Finally, we examine whether the same diagnosis-intervention principle remains useful in a pretrained VLA policy on a state-conditioned routing task.

II 相关工作Related Work

视觉运动模仿政策容易受到虚假观察-行动相关性的影响Visuomotor imitation policies are vulnerable to spurious observation-action correlations[5]，最近的工作系统地量化了物体外观、场景配置和视觉干扰因素变化下的泛化失败, and recent work has systematically quantified generalization failures under changes in object appearance, scene configuration, and visual distractors[1]。特别是，Causal-ACT 表明 ACT 可以利用不相关的干扰相关性. In particular, Causal-ACT shows that ACT can exploit irrelevant distractor correlations[3]，而 ImitDiff 和 DRAIL 分别通过语义指导和任务相关的区域感知表示来提高鲁棒性, while ImitDiff and DRAIL improve robustness through semantic guidance and task-relevant region-aware representations, respectively[2, 6].

补充方法通过接地掩模和视觉提示提供明确的空间或视觉指导Complementary approaches provide explicit spatial or visual guidance through grounding masks and visual prompts[7, 8]。视觉鲁棒性在预训练的视觉-语言-动作策略中同样未得到解决：BYOVLA 表现出对与任务无关的视觉内容的敏感性，并通过运行时观察干预来缓解它. 标准 ACT 的解码器状态在放置过程中在干扰因素下发生显着变化，并且仅保留其干净拾取/放置分离的 13.1%。两种以对象为中心的变体都强烈抑制这种转变并保留相位结构。仅增强模型为干扰器不变性提供了一个关键的反例，作为对鲁棒性的充分解释：它比 ACT-Modified 更具不变性，但保留了较弱的容器位置几何形状，并且仅达到 66.3% 的条件放置，而 ACT-Modified 为 100.0%。在这三个策略中，目标几何保留比单独的干净干扰不变性更紧密地遵循放置性能。因此，鲁棒行为与抑制干扰引起的变化有关，同时保留当前相关参照物所需的空间结构。Standard ACT’s decoder state shifts substantially under distractors during placement and retains only 13.1% of its clean pick/place separation. The augmentation-only model provides a critical counterexample to distractor invariance as a sufficient explanation of robustness: it is more invariant than ACT-Modified, yet retains weaker container-position geometry and reaches only 66.3% conditional placement compared with 100.0% for ACT-Modified. Robust behavior is therefore associated with suppressing nuisance-induced variation while preserving the spatial structure required by the currently relevant referent.

V 讨论与展望Discussion and Outlook

ACT 和 VLA 研究以不同的方式实例化条件视觉基础。在ACT中，操作阶段决定物体或容器是否相关；在VLA任务中，观察到的仪器状态决定了相关的目的地。在这两种设置中，有效的运动行为可能与上下文相关参照物的错误选择共存。因此，贡献不是两种架构都同样失败，而是分析The ACT and VLA studies instantiate conditional visual grounding differently. In ACT, manipulation phase determines whether the object or receptacle is relevant; in the VLA task, the observed instrument state determines the relevant destination. Across both settings, competent motor behavior can coexist with incorrect selection of a context-dependent referent. The contribution is therefore not that both architectures fail identically, but that analyzing什么what必须接地must be grounded当when为定位视觉故障和设计有针对性的干预措施提供了一个通用框架。 ACT 表示分析进一步表明，鲁棒性不仅需要抑制干扰物引起的变化，还需要保留控制所需的与任务相关的几何形状。provides a common framework for localizing visual failures and designing targeted interventions. The ACT representation analysis further shows that robustness requires not only suppressing distractor-induced variation but also preserving the task-relevant geometry required for control.

这些结论受到一些限制。观察到的 ACT 颜色形状层次结构特定于评估的资产，需要使用完全平衡的视觉因素进行验证。即时承载 ACT 变体接收额外的目标信息，尽管仅增强的结果表明在没有这种优势的情况下具有显着的鲁棒性增益。注意力和表征分析保持相关性，硬件评估每个单元仅包含 20 个试验，探索性 VLA 研究每个子目标仅包含 5 个具有非等效指导条件的试验。 VLA 任务还使用可见的污染替代品和固定的路由规则，而不是一般的污染理解。Several limitations bound these conclusions. The observed ACT color-shape hierarchy is specific to the evaluated assets and requires validation with fully counterbalanced visual factors. Prompt-bearing ACT variants receive additional target information, although augmentation-only results demonstrate substantial robustness gains without this advantage. Attention and representation analyses remain correlational, the hardware evaluation contains only 20 trials per cell, and the exploratory VLA study contains only five trials per subgoal with non-equivalent guidance conditions. The VLA task additionally uses a visible contamination surrogate and fixed routing rule rather than general contamination understanding.
