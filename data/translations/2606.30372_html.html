1 简介Introduction

A typical between-subjects study requires recruiting dozens to hundreds of participants per condition to detect meaningful effect sizes with adequate statistical power1。招募成本高昂、耗时，并且受到参与者可用性、自我选择偏差和人口统计偏差的限制. Recruitment is expensive, time-consuming, and bounded by participant availability, self-selection bias, and demographic skew1。动力不足的研究会产生嘈杂的估计，而足够样本量的成本限制了实验研究项目的范围和雄心。. Underpowered studies produce noisy estimates, and the cost of adequate sample sizes limits the scope and ambition of experimental research programs.

This question is not merely speculative. Researchers across disciplines have begun using LLMs to simulate survey respondents, annotate behavioural data, and generate synthetic experimental outcomes across psychology, political science, marketing, and health research.

8.2 范围和边界Scope and Boundaries

该框架适用于特定的、明确的研究类别。当以下所有条件成立时，功能风险等价成立：The framework applies to a specific, well-defined class of studies. Functional risk equivalence holds when all of the following hold:

  1. C1.

    定量连续响应。Quantitative continuous response.因变量以区间或比率量表（比例、视为连续的李克特量表、反应时间、测试分数、金钱金额）来测量。该框架不适用于纯粹分类或开放式文本响应。The dependent variable is measured on an interval or ratio scale (proportions, Likert scales treated as continuous, reaction times, test scores, monetary amounts). The framework does not apply to purely categorical or open-ended text responses.

  2. C2.

    离散、有限的条件。Discrete, finite conditions.实验设计具有有限数量的条件和已知的分配。连续治疗需要离散化。The experimental design has a finite number of conditions with known allocations. Continuous treatments require discretization.

  3. C3.

    代表性的训练数据。Representative training data.LLM 的训练数据大约是独立同分布的。来自目标人群。这是最严格的要求，也是实践中最容易违反的要求。网络抓取的培训语料库过多地代表了某些人口统计数据，而低估了其他人口统计数据。The LLM’s training data are approximately i.i.d. from the target population. 8.3 局限性和未解决的问题Limitations and Open Problems

几个重要的限制值得关注。Several important limitations merit attention.

训练数据的代表性。Training data representativeness.LLM 训练数据独立同分布的假设从目标人群来看，这是一种理想化。真正的训练语料库是具有复杂选择偏差的便利样本The assumption that LLM training data are i.i.d. from the target population is an idealization. Real training corpora are convenience samples with complex selection biases1。当训练分布以已知方式与目标不同时，未来的工作应该为退化制定正式的界限，从而扩展第 5 节的跨域分析. Future work should develop formal bounds for the degradation when the training distribution differs from the target in known ways, extending the cross-domain analysis of Section5到分布偏移是估计的而不是假设已知的设置。to settings where the distribution shift is estimated rather than assumed known.

有限条件假设。Finite-condition assumption.该框架需要离散条件。扩展到连续治疗（例如，连续剂量的剂量反应曲线）需要非参数回归理论，并且是概括的自然方向。The framework requires discrete conditions. Extending to continuous treatments (e.g., dose-response curves with continuous dosage) requires nonparametric regression theory and is a natural direction for generalization.

时间稳定性。Temporal stability.人口及其行为模式会随着时间的推移而发生变化。随着人口的发展，受过数据快照训练的法学硕士可能会出现错误校准。自适应重新校准程序（即定期对新鲜人类样本对法学硕士进行重新基准测试）将解决这一问题。Human populations and their behavioural patterns drift over time. An LLM trained on a snapshot of data may become miscalibrated as the population evolves. Adaptive recalibration procedures, where the LLM is periodically re-benchmarked against fresh human samples, would address this concern.

构建效度。Construct validity.群体水平效应大小的一致性并不能保证结构的有效性——法学硕士可能会因为错误的原因产生正确的数字。例如，法学硕士可能会学习在不建模潜在态度的情况下重现调查响应模式，产生有效的人口水平估计，但在个人水平预测任务上失败。该框架保证条件手段的平方损失风险有效性，而不是心理有效性。Agreement of population-level effect sizes does not guarantee construct validity — the LLM may produce the right numbers for the wrong reasons. For example, an LLM might learn to reproduce survey response patterns without modelling the underlying attitudes, producing valid population-level estimates while failing on individual-level prediction tasks. The framework guarantees squared-loss risk validity for conditional means, not psychological validity.

响应分布超出平均值。Response distributions beyond the mean.该框架侧重于条件均值，它是大多数受试者间设计中平方误差损失和兴趣量的自然目标。扩展到其他函数（分位数、方差、分布形状）需要不同的损失函数和估计器类别。The framework focuses on the conditional mean, which is the natural target for squared error loss and the quantity of interest in most between-subjects designs. Extending to other functionals (quantiles, variances, distributional shape) requires different loss functions and estimator classes.

8.4 与现有工作的关系Relationship to Existing Work

使用语言模型作为实验对象已经在心理学、政治学和市场营销领域进行了实证探索。这些研究通常根据具体情况将法学硕士产生的效应大小与人类基线进行比较，报告相关性和平均差异。虽然信息丰富，但这些比较并不能提供正式的保证，并且一项研究中的有利结果不会在没有额外验证的情况下转移到另一项研究中。The use of language models as experimental subjects has been explored empirically across psychology, political science, and marketing. These studies typically compare LLM-generated effect sizes to human baselines on a case-by-case basis, reporting correlations and mean differences. While informative, these comparisons do not provide formal guarantees, and a favourable result in one study does not transfer to another without additional validation.

我们的贡献是用有原则的数学基础取代这种经验拼凑。通过将问题简化为平方损失下的条件均值估计，我们将 LLM 人类风险等价与经典统计理论（MLE 一致性、强定律、集中不等式）联系起来，并提供了第一个正式证明，证明该方法在最小假设下渐近有效。Our contribution is to replace this empirical patchwork with a principled mathematical foundation. By reducing the problem to conditional mean estimation under squared loss, we connect LLM–human risk equivalence to classical statistical theory (MLE consistency, the Strong Law, concentration inequalities) and provide the first formal proof that the approach is asymptotically valid under minimal assumptions.

我们提出的校准协议与心理测量学中的测量不变性测试和机器学习中的领域适应相关。关键区别在于，我们提供决策规则（可行性检查和 TOST），直接确定 LLM 估计器和人类数据估计器在特定应用中是否实现相同的风险，而不是要求从业者解释连续拟合指数。The calibration protocol we present is related to measurement invariance testing in psychometrics and to domain adaptation in machine learning. The key difference is that we provide decision rules (the feasibility check and TOST) that directly determine whether an LLM estimator and human-data estimator achieve the same risk in a specific application, rather than requiring the practitioner to interpret continuous fit indices.

10 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一个严格的数学框架，建立预训练的大型语言模型在平方损失下诱导条件期望的风险等价估计，在条件均值依赖推理问题类中建立受限的功能风险等价。该框架的特点是严格证明来自基本原则，具有最少的假设、关于法学硕士的零架构假设以及从业者的明确范围边界。We have presented a rigorous mathematical framework establishing that pretrained large language models induce risk-equivalent estimators of conditional expectations under squared loss, establishing restricted functional risk equivalence within the class of conditional-mean-dependent inference problems. The framework is distinguished by rigorous proofs from first principles with minimal assumptions, zero architectural hypotheses about the LLM, and explicit scope boundaries for practitioners.

核心定理——偏差-方差分解、不可约误差最小化、效应大小一致性、功率放大和跨域转移——与有限样本浓度界限一起提供了完整的公理基础。有限样本分析将渐近保证转化为操作样本量要求。校准协议及其可行性检查和 TOST 等效性验证可实现具有明确决策规则的操作部署。The core theorems—bias-variance decomposition, irreducible error minimality, effect size consistency, power amplification, and cross-domain transfer—together with finite-sample concentration bounds provide a complete axiomatic foundation. The finite-sample analysis translates asymptotic guarantees into operational sample size requirements. The calibration protocol, with its feasibility check and TOST equivalence validation, enables operational deployment with explicit decision rules.

该框架声称有一个狭隘的、可证明的陈述：为了在离散条件下的定量实验中估计总体均值，经过良好校准的法学硕士会引入一个估计器，其预测风险收敛于平方损失下的贝叶斯风险，从而在条件均值依赖推理问题类中实现受限功能风险等价。The framework claims a narrow, provable statement: for estimating population means in quantitative experiments with discrete conditions, a well-calibrated LLM induces an estimator whose prediction risk converges to the Bayes risk under squared loss, achieving restricted functional risk equivalence within the class of conditional-mean-dependent inference problems.

这是一个温和的主张，受到明确的范围边界的仔细限制。它并不断言法学硕士可以在所有研究环境中取代人类，法学硕士生成的数据与分布中的人类数据没有区别，或者法学硕士理解他们测量的结构。它仅断言，在指定条件下，受限函数风险等价适用于平方损失、条件均值依赖推理。我们相信这种精确度对于负责任的部署以及为法学硕士辅助的定量研究建立数学上可靠的基础至关重要。This is a modest claim, carefully circumscribed by explicit scope boundaries. It does not assert that LLMs can replace humans in all research contexts, that LLM-generated data are indistinguishable from human data in distribution, or that LLMs understand the constructs they measure. It asserts only that, under the specified conditions, restricted functional risk equivalence holds for squared-loss, conditional-mean-dependent inference. We believe this level of precision is essential for responsible deployment and for building a mathematically defensible foundation for LLM-assisted quantitative research.

在实际应用中，这意味着在满足条件和校准良好的模型的情况下，大型语言模型可以用于许多原本依赖人体实验的预测和决策任务，以较低的成本逼近近乎最优的统计推断。In practical applications, this means that under satisfied conditions and well-calibrated models, large language models can be used in many prediction and decision-making tasks that originally relied on human experiments, approximating near-optimal statistical inference at lower cost.
