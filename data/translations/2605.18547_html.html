1 简介Introduction

随着智能眼镜、车载摄像头、实体代理等视觉传感平台的发展，机器可以持续观察现实世界交互中的面部表情、身体动作和交互行为With the development of visual sensing platforms such as smart glasses, in-vehicle cameras, and embodied agents, machines can continuously observe facial expressions, body movements, and interactive behaviors in real-world interactions[35, 7]。 然而，有效的人机交互不仅需要理解可观察的行为，还需要推断人类的情感状态以支持上下文适当的响应和决策. However, effective human-machine interaction requires not only understanding observable behaviors, but also inferring human affective states to support contextually appropriate responses and decisions[3, 31]。 因此，对话中的情绪识别（ERC）应运而生，旨在识别多轮对话中每个说话者的情绪状态. Consequently, Emotion Recognition in Conversation (ERC) has emerged to address this need, aiming to identify the emotional state of each speaker at each utterance in multi-turn dialogues[27, 4].

早期的 ERC 方法依赖文本信号来捕获上下文语义和情感表达Early ERC methods rely on textual signals to capture contextual semantics and emotional expressions[10, 16]。 虽然这些方法很简单，但通常直接从关键词汇线索推断情绪状态. While these approaches are straightforward, often inferring emotional states directly from key lexical cues[26, 22]，他们本质上忽视了重要的非语言信息。面部表情、声调（语调）和身体姿势等关键情绪指标完全被忽视。 这种限制在说话者故意掩盖真实意图或使用讽刺的复杂场景中变得尤为明显, they inherently neglect vital non-verbal information. Crucial emotional indicators such as facial expressions, vocal prosody (tone of voice), and body gestures are completely overlooked. This limitation becomes particularly evident in complex scenarios where a speaker deliberately masks their true intentions or uses sarcasm[5, 28]，导致纯文本方法无法准确提取潜在的情感状态。, causing text-only methods to fail in accurately extracting the underlying affective state.

现有的研究已经开始直接分析视频以进行对话中的情绪识别（ERC），特别是随着视觉语言模型（VLM）的出现Existing research has begun to directly analyze videos for Emotion Recognition in Conversation (ERC), especially with the emergence of Vision-Language Models (VLMs)[35, 7, 3]。 这些模型打破了传统方法的局限性，传统方法要么只关注局部面部表情，要么会导致信息丢失。. These models break the limitations of traditional approaches that either suffer from information loss by narrowly focusing solely on localized facial expressions[6, 34]或者在使用全局视频表示时与背景噪声和非目标说话者干扰作斗争or struggle with background noise and non-target speaker interference when using global video representations[12, 8, 31]。 然而，VLM 本质上并不是为 ERC 量身定制的。 他们不会自动关注当前说话者的特定情绪区域. However, VLMs are not inherently tailored for ERC. They do not automatically fixate on the current speaker’s emotion-specific regions[3, 35, 7]。 如图所示. This brings us to our first core challenge:我们如何使用免调音方法释放 VLM 潜在的 ERC 推理能力，使它们能够高效、准确地关注主动发言者的情感视觉线索？How can we unlock the latent ERC reasoning capabilities of VLMs using a tuning-free approach, enabling them to efficiently and precisely attend to the active speaker’s emotional visual cues?

2 相关工作Related Work

ERC 通过上下文建模和多模态学习得到了广泛的研究。 早期的方法主要集中在文本上下文建模，包括顺序话语建模、说话人状态跟踪、基于图的说话人关系建模和预训练语言表示ERC has been widely studied through contextual modeling and multimodal learning. Early methods mainly focus on textual context modeling, including sequential utterance modeling, speaker-state tracking, graph-based speaker relation modeling, and pretrained language representations[26, 22, 10, 14, 19, 29, 17, 16]。 后来的多模态方法通过动态融合、自蒸馏、超图建模、自适应图学习和证据原因推理引入声学和视觉信号. Later multimodal methods introduce acoustic and visual signals through dynamic fusion, self-distillation, hypergraph modeling, adaptive graph learning, and evidence-cause reasoning[12, 8, 20, 37, 33, 1, 38].

最近基于大模型的方法进一步探索了 ERC 的法学硕士。 DialogueLLM 使法学硕士适应对话式情感推理Recent large-model-based methods further explore LLMs for ERC. DialogueLLM adapts LLMs for conversational emotion reasoning[39]，LaERC-S 为零样本 ERC 引入了与说话者相关的提示, LaERC-S introduces speaker-related cues for zero-shot ERC[9]，SpeechCueLLM 将声音提示转换为文本提示，以进行语音感知推理, and SpeechCueLLM converts acoustic cues into textual prompts for speech-aware reasoning[36]。 这些方法展示了 ERC 大型模型的潜力，但它们主要依赖于以语言为中心或语音感知的推理。. These methods demonstrate the potential of large models for ERC, but they mainly rely on language-centered or speech-aware reasoning.

对于视觉建模，现有方法通常依赖于以面部为中心的流程，例如主动说话者定位、面部裁剪、动作单元提取和面部情感编码For visual modeling, existing methods usually rely on face-centric pipelines, such as active speaker localization, face cropping, action unit extraction, and facial affective encoding[31, 6, 34, 2, 30, 32, 40]，或使用预训练的视觉/视频编码器提取全局视频特征, or extract global video features with pretrained visual/video encoders[12, 8, 20, 1, 38]。 以人脸为中心的方法提供细粒度的局部情感线索，而全局视频特征则保留更广泛的场景信息。 最近的视觉语言模型，例如 Qwen2-VL 和 InternVL，提供了更强的全框架理解、视觉语言对齐和指令跟踪能力. Face-centric methods provide fine-grained local affective cues, while global video features preserve broader scene information. Recent vision-language models, such as Qwen2-VL and InternVL, provide stronger full-frame understanding, visual-language alignment, and instruction-following abilities[35, 7]，使得它们在 ERC 中的视觉情感建模方面很有前景。, making them promising for visual affective modeling in ERC.

3.2 可靠性引导的情感互补Reliability-Guided Affective Complementation

之后AfterSCAG，模型获得以说话人为中心的视觉情感特征。 然而，视觉线索在对话环境中可能仍然不明确或不可靠。 根据语言内容、韵律和对话历史，相同的视觉表达可能传达不同的情感；同时，遮挡、运动模糊或微妙的运动可能会进一步削弱纯粹的视觉推理。 因此，视觉情感特征仍然需要用文本和听觉背景来解释。 为了应对这一挑战，我们建议, the model obtains speaker-centered visual affective features. 5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们从以目标说话人为中心的角度重新审视视频在对话中的情感识别中的作用。我们表明，通过将视觉情感线索置于目标说话者身上并用情感语义信息引导冻结的 VLM，可以有效改善视频的有限且不稳定的贡献。为此，我们建议In this work, we revisit the role of video in Emotion Recognition in Conversation from a target-speaker-centered perspective. We show that the limited and unstable contribution of video can be effectively improved by grounding visual affective cues on the target speaker and guiding frozen VLMs with affective semantic information. To this end, we propose维萨夫VISAFF，一个两阶段框架，首先使用冻结的 VLM 提取以说话者为中心的视觉情感表示，然后在视觉可靠性的控制下用文本和听觉情感参考补充不确定的视觉线索。 MELD 和 IEMOCAP 的实验表明, a two-stage framework that first extracts speaker-centered visual affective representations with a frozen VLM, and then complements uncertain visual cues with textual and acoustic affective references under the control of visual reliability. Experiments on MELD and IEMOCAP demonstrate that维萨夫VISAFF无需 ERC 特定的 VLM 微调即可实现强大的性能。结果验证了从目标说话者视觉信息开始进行会话情感识别的有效性，并表明冻结的 VLM 在正确引导下可以提供有用的视觉情感表示。进一步的分析证实，情感语义引导大大提高了视觉表征的辨别能力，而当视觉线索模糊或不可靠时，可靠性引导的互补带来了一致的增益。总的来说，这项工作为 ERC 提供了一个面向视觉的视角，并强调了使用冻结 VLM 作为推理时视觉情感特征提取器的潜力。我们希望这项研究能够鼓励未来的研究进一步探索以说话者为中心的视觉推理、可靠性感知的多模态互补，以及在会话情感理解中更有效地利用视觉线索。achieves strong performance without ERC-specific VLM fine-tuning. The results validate the effectiveness of starting from target-speaker visual information for conversational emotion recognition, and show that frozen VLMs can provide useful visual affective representations when properly guided. Further analyses confirm that affective semantic guidance substantially improves the discriminative ability of visual representations, while reliability-guided complementation brings consistent gains when visual cues are ambiguous or unreliable. Overall, this work provides a visual-oriented perspective for ERC and highlights the potential of using frozen VLMs as inference-time visual affective feature extractors. We hope this study can encourage future research to further explore speaker-centered visual reasoning, reliability-aware multimodal complementation, and more effective use of visual cues in conversational emotion understanding.
