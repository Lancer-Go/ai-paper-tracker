摘要Abstract

使用强化学习训练的推理语言模型通常在固定的令牌预算下运行，而不是在明确的自适应预算下运行，这可能导致对简单问题的过度计算和对困难问题的计算不足。我们研究模型是否可以通过选择以下三种模式之一作为其响应的第一个标记来学习分配自己的推理工作：NoThink（尽快回答）、Short（简短推理）或 Long（扩展推理）。该选择是在组相对策略优化 (GRPO) 中学习的，无需单独的路由器，通过形状奖励使每种模式在不同的响应长度下都值得，并加上每个模式的硬令牌上限以保持模式的不同。在 MATH 训练的 1.5B 蒸馏模型上，三种模式出现而没有崩溃为单一选择，并且简短模式最终比长模式更准确，这表明路由器按难度而不是随机对问题进行排序。对三个种子进行平均，得到的策略在 MATH500 上保持接近基本模型的准确性（0Reasoning language models trained with reinforcement learning typically operate under a fixed token budget rather than an explicitly adaptive one, which can lead to over-computation on easy problems and insufficient computation on difficult ones. We study whether a model can learn to allocate its own reasoning effort by choosing, as the first token of its response, one of three modes: NoThink (answer as quickly as possible), Short (brief reasoning), or Long (extended reasoning). The choice is learned inside Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with no separate router, through a shaped reward that makes each mode worthwhile at a different response length, together with hard per-mode token caps that keep the modes distinct. On a 1.5B distilled model trained on MATH, the three modes emerge without collapsing to a single choice, and the brief modes end up more accurate than Long, which shows that the router sorts problems by difficulty rather than at random. 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在复杂推理任务上取得了显着的性能，这主要是由思想链 (CoT) 提示驱动的 (Wei et al. 2022)。通过将问题分解为中间步骤，DeepSeek-R1（Guo et al. 2025）等模型在数学和编码基准上达到了最先进的结果。然而，这种能力需要付出巨大的计算成本：扩展的推理链会消耗大量的令牌，从而增加推理延迟和训练成本。较小模型的负担最为严重，这些模型通常表现出“冗长补偿”，生成不必要的冗长解释以补偿有限的容量（Chen 等人，2024 年；Zhang、Das 和Zhang，2024 年）。根据经验，错误答案往往明显长于正确答案，这表明当前模型经常低效地花费推理令牌（Agentica Team 2025）。Large language models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable performance on complex reasoning tasks, driven largely by Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting (Wei et al. 2022). By breaking problems into intermediate steps, models such as DeepSeek-R1 (Guo et al. 2025) reach state-of-the-art results across mathematical and coding benchmarks. This capability, however, comes at a substantial computational cost: extended reasoning chains consume large numbers of tokens, increasing both inference latency and training cost. The burden falls hardest on smaller models, which often exhibit “verbosity compensation,” generating unnecessarily long explanations to compensate for limited capacity (Chen et al. 2024; Zhang, Das, and Zhang 2024). Empirically, incorrect answers tend to be markedly longer than correct ones, indicating that current models frequently spend reasoning tokens inefficiently (Agentica Team 2025).

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为微调推理模型的主导范例（Shao et al. 2024）。 PPO (Schulman et al. 2017) 和 GRPO (Shao et al. 2024) 等标准算法在所有训练样本中统一应用单个最大响应长度。这导致了两个相互竞争的低效率问题：在训练早期，模型浪费计算生成长且不正确的跟踪；后来，同样的固定上限可以防止解决真正困难问题所需的延长链条。最近的分析进一步表明Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has become the dominant paradigm for fine-tuning reasoning models (Shao et al. 2024). Standard algorithms such as PPO (Schulman et al. 2017) and GRPO (Shao et al. 2024) operate with a single maximum response length applied uniformly across all training samples. This couples two competing inefficiencies: early in training, the model wastes compute generating long, incorrect traces; later, the same fixed ceiling can prevent the extended chains needed to solve genuinely hard problems. 对长度问题的原则性回应是时间折扣：通过每个代币因子缩放奖励会直接惩罚长时间的推出，而基于折扣的 GRPO 变体已被证明可以在保持准确性的同时缩短推理时间（Parthasarathi 等人，2025 年；Ayoub 等人，2025 年；Zhang 和 Zuo，2025 年；Yang 等人，2026 年）。然而，折扣是非自适应的：无论提示的难度或其解决方案实际需要的响应长度如何，它都会施加固定的长度惩罚。即使最佳推理预算不同，简单问题和困难问题也必须同样简短。自然的下一步是让模型根据提示决定问题需要多少推理，仅在合理的情况下施加简洁性压力。A principled response to the length problem is temporal discounting: scaling reward by a per-token factor penalizes long rollouts directly, and discount-based variants of GRPO have been shown to shorten reasoning while preserving accuracy (Parthasarathi et al. 2025; Ayoub et al. 2025; Zhang and Zuo 2025; Yang et al. 2026). Discounting, however, is non-adaptive: it imposes a fixed length penalty regardless of a prompt’s difficulty or the response length its solution actually requires. An easy question and a hard question are pressured to be equally brief, even though the optimal reasoning budget differs. The natural next step is to let the model decide, per prompt, how much reasoning a problem warrants, applying brevity pressure only where it is justified.

我们直接研究这个问题。我们要求 1.5B 精炼推理模型发出三个离散路由决策（NoThink、Short 或 Long）之一作为其响应的第一个标记，然后进行相应的推理。路由令牌是主要策略的一部分，并通过标准 GRPO 目标接收梯度，没有单独的路由器网络。核心困难是路由崩溃：因为路由决策是单个自我强化的离散令牌，即使奖励边缘很小的模式也会被更多地采样，强化更多，并迅速将替代方案排除在策略之外。We study this question directly. We ask a 1.5B distilled reasoning model toemit, as the veryfirst token of itsresponse, one of three discrete routing decisions—NoThink, Short, or Long—and then to reason accordingly. The routing token is part of the main policy and receives gradient through the standard GRPO objective, there is no separate router network. The central difficulty is routing collapse: because the routing decision is a single self-reinforcing discrete token, a mode with even a small reward edge is sampled more, reinforced more, and quickly drives the alternatives out of the policy.

我们的方法通过两种机制的共同作用来克服崩溃。首先，每个模式的硬令牌上限使模式在结构上有所不同，因此路由标签无法与行为分离。其次，一个简单的平衡项直接增加了优势，类似于在 Mixture-ofExperts 路由中用于阻止专家崩溃的负载平衡惩罚（Fedus、Zoph 和 Shazeer 2022），可以防止策略陷入单一模式。强制推出预热可以稳定早期动态。Our method overcomes collapse with two mechanisms working together. First, hard per-mode token caps make the modes distinct by construction, so the routing label cannot decouple from behavior. Second, a simple balance term added directly to the advantage, similar to the load-balancing penalties used to discourage expert collapse in Mixture-ofExperts routing (Fedus, Zoph, and Shazeer 2022), keeps the policy from settling on a single mode. A forced-rollout warmup stabilizes the early dynamics.

我们的贡献是双重的。首先，我们展示了 1.5B 推理模型可以通过难度来学习路由问题，在三种推理模式中选择作为其第一个生成的令牌，在 GRPO 内端到端，没有单独的路由器，也没有监督预热。其次，我们表明，这种路由大幅削减了推理令牌预算，同时将策略置于或高于单模式基线追踪的准确性长度帕累托前沿——无论是数学上的分布还是保留的基准。作为分析的一部分，倒计时任务的负面结果解释了难度路由何时是可学习的，何时是不可学习的。Our contributions are twofold. First, we show that a 1.5B reasoning model can be taught to route problems by difficulty, choosing among three reasoning modes as its first generated token, end-to-end inside GRPO, with no separate router and no supervised warm-up. Second, we show that this routing cuts the reasoning-token budget substantially while placing the policy on or above the accuracy–length Pareto frontier traced by the single-mode baselines — both in-distribution on MATH and on held-out benchmarks. As part of the analysis, a negative result on the Countdown task explains when difficulty routing is and is not learnable.

自适应推理模式选择Adaptive Reasoning-Mode Selection

相关工作通过根据问题难度跳过或缩写推理来缩短推理，方法的不同之处在于需要多少训练机器。 AdaptThink (Zhang et al. 2025) 和 AutoThink (Tu et al. 2025) 停留在纯 RL 范围内——前者通过冷启动重要性采样的约束目标在完全推理和无推理模式之间进行选择，后者使用多阶段课程——每个课程将 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B 上的响应长度大致减半，同时保持或提高准确性。 ARM (Wu et al. 2025) 和 Thinkless (Fang, Ma, and Wang 2025) 相反，在 RL 之前预先安装带有监督阶段的模式：ARM 对以四种格式（直接答案、短链和长链思想以及代码）注释的数据进行监督微调，而 Thinkless 将推理专家和指令专家提炼为两个控制令牌，然后以解耦目标运行 RL，因此单独的控制令牌是没有被响应令牌淹没。A related line of work shortens reasoning by skipping or abbreviating it according to problem difficulty, and the methods differ in how much training machinery they require. AdaptThink (Zhang et al. 2025) and AutoThink (Tu et al. 2025) stay within pure RL—the former choosing between a fullreasoning and a no-reasoning mode via a constrained objective with importance sampling for cold start, the latter using a multi-stage curriculum—each roughly halving response length on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B while preserving or improving accuracy. ARM (Wu et al. 2025) and Thinkless (Fang, Ma, and Wang 2025) instead pre-install the modes with a supervised stage before RL: ARM supervised-finetunes on data annotated in four formats (direct answer, short and long chain-of-thought, and code), and Thinkless distills a reasoning expert and an instruction expert into two control tokens, then runs RL with a decoupled objective so the lone control token is not swamped by the response tokens.

所有这些方法共有的一个困难是路由崩溃：因为模式决策是一个单一的离散的、自我强化的选择，即使具有很小的奖励优势的模式也会被更多地采样、强化，并将替代方案排除在策略之外。全单模式状态在策略梯度下是稳定的，而混合分裂则不然，这种效果在具有低输出熵和强先验推理的小型蒸馏模型上更强。上述方法通过重要性采样 (AdaptThink)、课程分级 (AutoThink) 或预安装模式的监督阶段（ARM、Thinkless）来稳定选择。我们的模型是其中最精简的——没有监督或蒸馏阶段，通过 RL 端到端学习三种模式——而是依靠强制预热、每个模式的硬上限以及对优势的平衡惩罚来保持所有模式的活力。A difficulty shared by all of these methods is routing collapse: because the mode decision is a single discrete, selfreinforcing choice, a mode with even a small reward advantage is sampled more, reinforced more, and drives the alternatives out of the policy. The all-one-mode states are stable under policy gradient while the mixed split is not, an effect that is stronger on a small distilled model with low output entropy and a strong prior to reason at length. The methods above stabilize the choice with importance sampling (AdaptThink), curriculum staging (AutoThink), or a supervised stage that pre-installs the modes (ARM, Thinkless). Ours is the leanest of these—no supervised or distillation stage, three modes learned end-to-end with RL—relying instead on a forced warmup, hard per-mode caps, and a balance penalty on the advantage to keep all modes alive.

方法Method

折扣对每个提示都施加相同的惩罚。我们的目标是让模型根据提示分配推理工作：当问题很琐碎时跳过推理，当问题很简单时进行简短推理，当问题困难时进行详细推理。我们通过让模型发出离散路由令牌作为其响应的第一个词并根据该选择调节奖励和硬令牌预算来实现这一点。我们首先在倒计时任务中使用两种模式（短/长）路由器探索了这个想法；它崩溃为单一模式，因为倒计时的统一难度使路由器没有可供学习的准确性差距（请参阅补充附录）。这一负面结果激发了以下设计：每个模式的硬代币上限使模式在行为上截然不同，以及对 MATH 的评估，其广泛的难度为路由提供了一些需要学习的东西。Discounting applies the same penalty to every prompt. Our goal is to let the model allocate reasoning effort per prompt: skip reasoning when a problem is trivial, reason briefly when it is easy, and reason at length when it is hard. We realize this by having the model emit a discrete routing token as the first word of its response and conditioning both reward and a hard token budget on that choice. We first explored this idea with a two-mode (Short/Long) router on the Countdown task; it collapsed to a single mode because Countdown’s uniform difficulty gives the router no accuracy gap to learn from (see the supplementary appendix). That negative result motivates the design below: hard per-mode token caps that make the modes behaviorally distinct, and evaluation on MATH, whose wide spread of difficulty gives routing something to learn.

通过实验，我们想回答三个问题：（1）稳定性：GRPO 中可以端到端地学习三种推理模式而不崩溃为单一模式吗？ （2）意义：学习到的路由是否按难度对问题进行排序，而不是任意划分？ (3) 效率：与固定推理预算相比，路由提供的准确性和令牌成本是多少，并且它是否超出了训练分布？下面的三个小节依次解决这些问题。Through the experiments we would like to answer three questions: (1) Stability:can three reasoning modes be learnedendto-end inside GRPO without collapsing to a single mode? 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

我们提出了 GRPO 的三模式自路由，其中推理模型通过发出单个路由令牌来选择是快速回答、简要推理还是详细推理。在小型精炼模型上，仅靠奖励无法强制执行此选择：没有什么可以阻止策略发出简短模式令牌，然后进行详细推理，因此标签与行为脱钩，一种模式接管，这正是两种模式倒计时先驱的失败（在补充附录中）。每个模式的硬上限将标签锁定为行为并产生真正的准确度差距We presented three-mode self-routing for GRPO, in which a reasoning model chooses whether to answer quickly, reason briefly, or reason at length by emitting a single routing token. On a small distilled model the reward alone cannot enforce this choice: nothing prevents the policy from emitting a brief-mode token and then reasoning at length, so the label decouples from behavior and one mode takes over, exactly the failure of the two-mode Countdown precursor (in the supplementary appendix). 由此产生的策略按难度处理问题，将验证精度保持在基本模型附近，同时在每个基准上大幅缩短平均响应长度。路由最好被理解为一种令牌效率机制，而不是一种准确性机制：在长度/准确性平面中，它不仅以准确性换取令牌，而且将整个权衡向外推。在三个数据集中的两个 — MATH-500（图 6）和 GSM8K（图 7） — 自由路由明显位于单模基线追踪的 Pareto 前沿之上，与任何固定模式相比，每个令牌的准确性更高。第三个是 AIME（图 7），其问题确实需要扩展推理，路由器将几乎所有内容发送给 Long，并且策略落在帕累托前沿。The resulting policy routes problems by difficulty, holding validation accuracy near the base model while cutting mean response lengthsubstantially on every benchmark. Routing is best understood as a token-efficiency mechanism, not an accuracy mechanism: in the length/accuracy plane it does not only trade accuracy for tokens but pushes the whole trade-off outward. On two of the three datasets — MATH-500 (Figure 6) and GSM8K (Figure 7) — free routing sits clearly above the Pareto front traced by the single-mode baselines, delivering more accuracy per token than any fixed mode. On the third, AIME (Figure 7), whose problems genuinely require extended reasoning, the router sends nearly everything to Long and the policy lands on the Pareto front.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and future work.结果涵盖了一种数学训练分布上的一个 1.5B 模型的三个种子。模式上限是特定于任务的，并且必须与目标任务的长度分布相匹配，并且无上限验证使得简短模式的准确性相对于训练目标来说稍微乐观。因此，下一步最重要的是广度：在更广泛的数学基准上建立机制，然后超越数学，无论问题在需要多少推理方面有所不同，例如代码生成、代理和工具使用设置等。The results cover three seeds of one 1.5B model on one mathematical training distribution. The mode caps are task-specific and must be matched to the target task’s length distribution, and uncapped validation makes brief-mode accuracy marginally optimistic relative to the trained objective. The most important next step is therefore breadth: establishing the mechanism on a wider range of mathematical benchmarks and then beyond mathematics, wherever problems differ in how much reasoning they require such as code generation, agentic and tool-use settings, etc.
