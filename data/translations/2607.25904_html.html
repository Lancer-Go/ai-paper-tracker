简介Introduction

图形用户界面（GUI）任务评估旨在确定GUI代理是否成功完成用户指令。最近，自动化 GUI 任务评估引起了越来越多的关注，因为可靠的自动化评估对于测试和训练都很重要。对于测试，自动评估可以扩展测试时间Graphical user interface (GUI) task evaluation aims to determine whether a GUI agent has successfully completed a user instruction. Recently, automated GUI task evaluation has attracted growing attention because reliable automated evaluation is important for both testing and training. For testing, automated evaluation enables test-time scaling(Lai et al. 2025; Gonzalez-Pumariega et al. 2025)。对于训练，评估信号可以直接作为训练后的奖励. For training, evaluation signals can serve directly as rewards for post-training(Xu et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2026b; Jiang et al. 2026).

现有方法为 GUI 任务的自动评估提供了坚实的基础。 现有 GUI 基准测试中使用的基于脚本的评估器可以检查文件、系统配置、应用程序状态和环境变量，以确定任务是否成功Existing methods provide a strong foundation for automated evaluation of GUI tasks. Script-based evaluators used in existing GUI benchmarks can inspect files, system configurations, application states, and environment variables to determine task success(Zhou et al. 2024; Bonatti et al. 2024; Sun et al. 2025; Xie et al. 2025; Zheng et al. 2025; Shi et al. 2017)。与此同时，视觉语言模型（VLM）的最新进展使得更通用的评估方法成为可能，可以根据执行屏幕截图来判断任务完成情况 轨迹. At the same time, recent progress in vision-language models (VLMs) has enabled more general evaluation methods that judge task completion from screenshots of execution trajectories(Hong et al. 2024; Lu et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2024; Wu et al. 2025; Qin et al. 2025; Hu et al. 2025)。这些基于 VLM 的评估器减少了对每个任务手动注释脚本的需求，并使可扩展的评估变得实用。 然而，任务完成情况也可能反映在环境状态证据中，例如系统配置、文件数据和应用程序设置，而不仅仅是屏幕截图显示的内容. These VLM-based evaluators reduce the need for per-task manually annotated scripts and make scalable evaluation practical. However, task completion may also be reflected in environment-state evidence, such as system configurations, file data, and application settings, beyond what screenshots reveal(Zheng et al. 2026; Wang et al. 2025c; Ye et al. 2026; Cheng et al. 2024; Gou et al. 2025; Liu et al. 2026b)。如图。. 这一观察促使我们将 GUI 任务评估制定为评估者与环境之间的交互过程，其中评估者在做出最终判断之前迭代地收集证据。为此，我们必须解决 GUI 任务完成交互式评估的核心挑战。 所需的证据通常分布在异构源中，包括系统配置、文件数据和应用程序设置，其位置和相关性无法提前确定。不同的任务可能需要不同的验证策略，当接口发生变化、应用程序行为异常或相关证据仅部分可见时，固定的检查程序可能会失败。因此，可靠的GUI评估不仅需要确定应验证哪些条件，还需要动态确定如何从环境中获取相应的证据并进行验证。This observation motivates us to formulate GUI task evaluation as an interaction process between the evaluator and the environment, in which the evaluator iteratively collects evidence before reaching a final judgment. In doing so, we must address a central challenge for interactive evaluation of GUI task completion. The required evidence is often distributed across heterogeneous sources, including system configurations, file data, and application settings, whose locations and relevance cannot be determined in advance. Different tasks may require different verification strategies, and a fixed checking procedure may fail when interfaces change, applications behave unexpectedly, or relevant evidence is only partially observable. As a result, reliable GUI evaluation requires not only identifying what conditions should be verified, but also dynamically determining how the corresponding evidence should be obtained from the environment and validated.

在本文中，我们提出了一种交互式奖励代理（IRA），它构建在提议然后验证框架上，并配备了与环境交互的工具。给定任务指令和执行后 GUI 环境，IRA 首先生成明确的任务完成条件，然后通过调用这些工具来验证它们。具体来说，IRA使用VLM来提出特定于任务的完成条件，而不是直接判断任务成功。然后，它通过系统工具、应用程序工具和 GUI 工具从实际环境中收集证据来验证这些条件。该框架保留了基于 VLM 的评估器在不同任务中的可扩展性，同时支持与基于脚本的评估器使用的类似的基于证据的检查In this paper, we propose an interactive reward agent (IRA) built on a propose-then-verify framework and equipped with tools that interact with the environment. Given a task instruction and the post-execution GUI environment, IRA first generates explicit task completion conditions and then verifies them by invoking these tools. Concretely, IRA uses a VLM to propose task-specific completion conditions, rather than directly judging task success. It then verifies these conditions by collecting evidence from the actual environment through system tools, application tools, and GUI tools. This framework retains the scalability of VLM-based evaluators across diverse tasks while enabling evidence-grounded checks similar to those used by script-based evaluators(Wei et al. 2026; Chen et al. 2026).

GUI 代理。GUI Agents.GUI 代理是从基于结构化 DOM 或 HTML 观察的 Web 代理发展而来的GUI agents have progressed from web agents based on structured DOM or HTML observations(Shi et al. 2017; Deng et al. 2023)到跨 Web、移动和桌面环境运行的基于 VLM 的系统。最近的工作使用屏幕截图或解析的界面元素改进了视觉基础to VLM-based systems that operate across web, mobile, and desktop environments. Recent work has improved visual grounding using screenshots or parsed interface elements(Hong et al. 2024; Cheng et al. 2024; Lu et al. 2024; Wu et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2026)，将GUI代理扩展到端到端计算机使用, extended GUI agents to end-to-end computer use(Qin et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2026c; Agashe et al. 2025; Lai et al. 2025)，并通过强化学习、验证者反馈、自适应数据管理和特定于任务的知识来增强长期执行力, and enhanced long-horizon execution through reinforcement learning, verifier feedback, adaptive data curation, and task-specific knowledge(Xu et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2025a; Ding et al. 2023; Zhang et al. 2025b; Wang et al. 2025b; Liu et al. 2026a; Xie et al. 2026)。随着 GUI 代理的能力越来越强，可靠地评估它们是否确实满足了用户指令变得相应重要。然而，最近的许多训练和评估流程主要从屏幕截图轨迹推断任务成功，这仅提供了环境的部分视图. As GUI agents become increasingly capable, reliably evaluating whether they have actually satisfied a user instruction becomes correspondingly important. However, many recent training and evaluation pipelines infer task success primarily from screenshot trajectories, which provide only a partial view of the environment(Bai et al. 2024; Wang et al. 2025a; Qi et al. 2025)。他们可能会错过对文件、配置或后端状态的更改，并且显示的界面可能无法反映当前的系统状态。 因此，视觉上合理的轨迹或最终屏幕截图不一定能确认任务完成。 IRA 通过从指令中导出完成条件并根据最终环境状态主动验证它们来解决此限制。. They may miss changes to files, configurations, or backend states, and the displayed interface may not reflect the current system state. Consequently, a visually plausible trajectory or a final screenshot does not necessarily confirm task completion. IRA addresses this limitation by deriving completion conditions from the instruction and actively verifying them against the final environment state.

Cross-platform suites further evaluate capabilities ranging from interface understanding and grounding to task automation and collaboration(Wang et al. 2025c)。尽管它们多种多样，但这些基准测试主要询问 GUI 代理是否可以成功执行任务。 GUI-RewardBench 研究了一个不同的问题。它询问评估者是否可以正确确定任务完成情况。这将评估目标从动作生成转变为完成验证，要求评估者对屏幕截图、文件、配置、生成的工件和应用程序状态等异构证据进行推理。因此，GUI-RewardBench 通过直接评估奖励评估器的可靠性来补充现有的代理能力基准。. Despite their diversity, these benchmarks primarily ask whether a GUI agent can execute a task successfully. GUI-RewardBench studies a different problem. It asks whether an evaluator can correctly determine task completion. This shifts the evaluation target from action generation to completion verification, requiring evaluators to reason over heterogeneous evidence such as screenshots, files, configurations, generated artifacts, and application states. GUI-RewardBench therefore complements existing agent-capability benchmarks by directly evaluating the reliability of reward evaluators.

GUI 任务评估。GUI Task Evaluation.现有的GUI任务评估方法大致可以分为基于脚本的、基于VLM的和基于代理的。基于脚本的评估器直接检查系统或应用程序状态，并可以提供精确的特定于任务的检查，但它们需要大量的手工劳动来注释每个任务Existing GUI task evaluation methods can be broadly categorized as script-based, VLM-based, and agent-based. Script-based evaluators directly inspect system or application states and can provide precise task-specific checks, but they require substantial manual labor for annotating each task(Zhou et al. 2024; Sun et al. 2025; Xie et al. 2025; Zheng et al. 2025; Shi et al. 2017)。基于 VLM 的评估器更容易跨任务扩展，但通常根据屏幕截图或轨迹判断完成情况，因此仅限于可见证据. VLM-based evaluators are easier to scale across tasks, but typically judge completion from screenshots or trajectories and are therefore limited to visible evidence(Wang et al. 2025a; Bai et al. 2024; Yang et al. 2025)。基于代理的评估器依赖于语言模型和程序规则的推理能力. Agent-based evaluators rely on the reasoning abilities of language models and procedural rubrics(Zhuge et al. 2024; Wadhwa et al. 2025)，但一般假设所要判断的证据已经提供。 GUI 任务评估提出了不同的挑战。决定性证据可能不包含在给定的轨迹中，而是可以分布在屏幕截图、应用程序状态、文件、配置值和命令输出中, but generally assume that the evidence to be judged has already been provided. GUI task evaluation presents a different challenge. The decisive evidence may not be contained in the given trajectory and can instead be distributed across screenshots, application states, files, configuration values, and command outputs(Wang et al. 2025c; Zheng et al. 2026; Ye et al. 2026; Liu et al. 2026b)。因此，IRA 将证据获取本身视为评估的一部分。它首先导出完成条件，然后确定哪些环境状态和工具是相关的，并主动收集证据来验证每个条件。这将 IRA 与之前基于代理的法官区分开来，后者根据固定观察进行推理，并根据从实际执行后环境中收集的证据来做出最终判决。. IRA therefore treats evidence acquisition itself as part of evaluation. It first derives completion conditions, then determines which environment states and tools are relevant, and actively gathers evidence to verify each condition. This distinguishes IRA from prior agent-based judges that reason over fixed observations and grounds IRA’s final judgment in evidence collected from the actual post-execution environment.

评估协议Evaluation Protocol

所有评估方法都接收相同的任务指令，并评估由相同评估管道产生的环境状态：环境初始化、轨迹回放、评估器检查和特定于任务的脚本验证。每种方法都会输出一个标量奖励，然后使用相同的阈值将其转换为二进制预测。大于 0.8 的奖励被归类为成功。在计算精度、召回率和 F1 时，成功被视为正类。尽管选择的 GUI-RewardBench 轨迹相对重播稳定，但评估仍然在实时桌面环境中进行。 UI 漂移、异步应用程序行为、网络变化、重放缺陷和评估器交互可能会导致重放状态与原始记录的状态不同。因此，在每次实时评估运行后，我们执行特定于任务的评估脚本，并使用其输出作为计算 TP、FP、TN 和 FN 的真实标签。该协议将每个评估器与其检查的实际环境状态进行比较，同时保持任务指令、记录轨迹、重放过程和跨方法固定的验证脚本。All evaluation methods receive the same task instruction and evaluate the environment state produced by the same evaluation pipeline: environment initialization, trajectory replay, evaluator inspection, and task-specific script verification. Each method outputs a scalar reward, which is then converted to a binary prediction using the same threshold. Rewards greater than 0.8 are classified as success. Success is treated as the positive class when computing precision, recall, and F1. Although GUI-RewardBench trajectories are selected to be relatively replay-stable, evaluation is still conducted in a live desktop environment. UI drift, asynchronous application behavior, network variability, replay imperfections, and evaluator interactions may cause the replayed state to differ from the originally recorded state. Therefore, after each live evaluation run, we execute the task-specific evaluation script and use its output as the ground-truth label for computing TP, FP, TN, and FN. This protocol compares each evaluator against the actual environment state it inspected, while keeping the task instruction, recorded trajectory, replay procedure, and verification script fixed across methods.

A.2 系统提示结构System Prompt Structure

IRA 系统提示强制执行推理-行动-观察循环，并包含五个关键组件：The IRA system prompt enforces a reasoning-action-observation loop and contains five key components:

工作流程结构。Workflow Structure.代理在 ReAct 风格的循环中运行：The agent operates in a ReAct-style loop:

  • •

    思想Thought— Agent以自然语言输出推理：验证什么条件，需要什么证据，使用什么工具— The agent outputs reasoning in natural language: what condition to verify, what evidence is needed, which tool to use

  • •

    行动Action— 代理通过函数调用 API 只调用一个工具— The agent calls exactly one tool via function calling API

  • •

    观察Observation— 系统执行工具并返回结果— The system executes the tool and returns the result

这种模式将持续下去，直到所有条件都得到验证或代理输出最终答案。This pattern continues until all conditions are verified or the agent outputs a final answer.

评估协议。Evaluation Protocol.该提示强制执行五步评估过程：The prompt enforces a five-step evaluation process:

  • •

    初步分析Initial Analysis— 了解任务要求，比较初始和最终屏幕截图— Understand task requirements, compare initial and final screenshots

  • •

    验证策略Verification Strategy— 为每个条件选择最强的验证路径— Choose the strongest verification path for each condition

  • •

    定义完成条件Define Completion Conditions— 将任务分解为原子的、必要的完成条件— Decompose task into atomic, necessary completion conditions

  • •

    验证每个条件Verify Each Condition— 使用工具收集证据，将每个条件标记为满意/不满意— Use tools to gather evidence, mark each condition as satisfied/unsatisfied

  • •

    校准判决Calibrate Verdict— 将条件结果汇总为最终奖励— Aggregate condition results into final reward

核心原则。Core Principles.指导评估的三个原则：Three principles guide evaluation:

  • •

    仅最终状态Final-State Only— 仅根据最终环境状态进行判断；中间进度被忽略— Judge based solely on final environment state; intermediate progress is ignored

  • •

    以任务为基础的条件Task-Grounded Conditions——每一个条件都必须是完成任务所必需的；不要引入超出任务说明的要求— Every condition must be strictly necessary for task completion; do not introduce requirements beyond task instruction

  • •

    保守评价Conservative Evaluation— 不明确或无法验证的条件被视为不满足— Ambiguous or unverifiable conditions are treated as NOT satisfied

证据选择政策。Evidence Selection Policy.提示指定严格的证据层次结构：The prompt specifies a strict evidence hierarchy:

  • •

    对于可见状态 → 使用附加的屏幕截图For visible states → use attached screenshots

  • •

    对于应用程序状态 → 使用专用工具（例如，For application states → use specialized tools (e.g.,获取书签get_bookmarks, 获取vscode设置get_vscode_settings)

  • •

    对于隐藏状态 → 使用 CLI/文件工具（例如，For hidden states → use CLI/file tools (e.g.,执行虚拟机命令execute_vm_command, 获取虚拟机文件get_vm_file)

  • •

    用于 GUI 检查 → 使用For GUI inspection → use电脑computer作为最后手段的工具tool as a last resort

这种层次结构鼓励代理人首先使用更可靠的证据来源。This hierarchy encourages the agent to use more reliable evidence sources first.

严格禁止。Strict Prohibitions.提示明确禁止常见故障模式：The prompt explicitly forbids common failure modes:

  • •

    收到结果后不要重复工具调用Do NOT repeat tool calls after receiving results

  • •

    不要在可观察到的证据之外进行推测Do NOT speculate beyond observable evidence

  • •

    不要跳过在行动之前先写想法Do NOT skip writing Thought before Action

  • •

    每回合不要调用多个工具Do NOT make multiple tool calls per turn

  • •

    未经明确验证，请勿假设成功Do NOT assume success without explicit verification

  • •

    不要添加超出任务要求的条件Do NOT add conditions beyond task requirements
