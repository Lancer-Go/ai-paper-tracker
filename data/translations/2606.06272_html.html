您的 GFlowNet 秘密学习最佳传输计划Your GFlowNet Secretly Learns an Optimal Transport Plan

Ian Maksimov    Nikita Morozov    Denis Belomestny    Sergey Samsonov
摘要Abstract

生成流网络 (GFlowNets) 是一个通过有向图中的随机轨迹对结构化对象进行采样的框架。在这项工作中，我们在非无环 GFlowNet 和最优传输 (OT) 之间建立了理论联系。我们证明，固定最小流量 GFlowNet 中的初始流量分布可将其目标降低为具有图诱导的最短路径成本的 Kantorovich OT 问题。因此，在最优情况下，学习到的 GFlowNet 策略会编码从源分布到目标分布的最优传输计划：我们表明，来自最小流量 GFlowNet 的采样轨迹恢复了相应的最优耦合。我们的公式能够通过边缘流和神经参数化将 GFlowNet 学习框架应用于大图上的 OT 问题。实验证实了与精确 OT 求解器的一致性，并证明 GFlowNet 可以学习高质量的传输计划。Generative Flow Networks (GFlowNets) are a framework for sampling structured objects via stochastic trajectories in a directed graph. In this work, we establish a theoretical connection between non-acyclic GFlowNets and optimal transport (OT). We show that fixing the initial flow distribution in a minimum-flow GFlowNet reduces its objective to a Kantorovich OT problem with graph-induced shortest path costs. At the optimum, the learned GFlowNet policy therefore encodes an optimal transport plan from the source distribution to the target distribution: we show that sampling trajectories from the minimum-flow GFlowNet recovers the corresponding optimal coupling. Our formulation enables applying the GFlowNet learning framework to OT problems on large graphs via edge flows and neural parameterization. Experiments confirm agreement with exact OT solvers and demonstrate that GFlowNets can learn high-quality transport plans.

5 结论Conclusion

我们通过建立与图上最优传输的等价关系来扩展非无环 GFlowNet 的理论框架。与仅学习耦合的标准 OT 方法不同，我们的框架学习一种随机策略，通过可行的局部移动以接近最优的预期路径成本来传输样本，从而解决传输发生的位置和方式。 我们进一步对 GFlowNets 的最短路径公式进行了新颖的解释We extended the theoretical framework of non-acyclic GFlowNets by establishing an equivalence with optimal transport on graphs. Unlike standard OT approaches that only learn a coupling, our framework learns a stochastic policy that transports samples through feasible local moves with near-optimal expected path cost, thereby addressing both where and how transport occurs. We further provided a novel interpretation of the shortest-path formulation of GFlowNets frommorozov2026learningshortestpathsgenerative。定理中建立的等价性. The equivalence established in Theorem3.2开辟了几个有前途的方向，包括后向策略优化算法opens several promising directions, including backward policy optimization algorithms(jang2024pessimistic; gritsaev2024optimizing)以及由 GFlowNets 与熵正则化 RL 连接激发的强化学习技术and reinforcement learning techniques motivated by the connection of GFlowNets to entropy-regularized RL(tiapkin2024generative; deleu2024discrete; mohammadpour2024maximum; lau2024qgfn; morozov2024improving; he2025random)。未来的工作还可以解决我们的实验评估所强调的实际挑战。在提出一种计算高效的训练方法时，非循环 GFlowNet 包括正则化系数. Future work could also address practical challenges highlighted by our experimental evaluation. While presenting a computationally efficient training method, non-acyclic GFlowNets include the regularization coefficientλ\lambda必须仔细调整以平衡 OT 最优性和采样质量。此外，现有的 GFlowNet 方法可以潜在地应用于学习非标准化概率分布之间的传输图。that must be carefully tuned to balance OT optimality and sampling quality. In addition, existing GFlowNet methodology could be potentially applied to learn transport maps between unnormalized probability distributions.
