1 简介Introduction

在无信号交叉口协调多辆自动驾驶车辆 (AV) 是智能交通的核心挑战：车辆必须共同导航复杂、动态的场景，同时保证无碰撞运行并保持吞吐量Coordinating multiple autonomous vehicles (AVs) at unsignalized intersections is a central challenge for intelligent transportation: vehicles must jointly navigate complex, dynamic scenarios while guaranteeing collision-free operation and maintaining throughput[Wang et al.(2024)]。这种多智能体协调问题的难度推动了多智能体深度强化学习（MADRL）的广泛研究. The difficulty of this multi-agent coordination problem has driven extensive research in multi-agent deep reinforcement learning (MADRL)[Chen et al.(2024)]，跨越价值分解方法, spanning value-decomposition methods[Huang et al.(2023)]，基于图形的表示, graph-based representations[Cai et al.(2022)]、政策优化, policy optimization[Peng et al.(2023), Xu et al.(2022)]和分层框架, and hierarchical frameworks[Al-Sharman et al.(2022), Liu et al.(2025), Zhao et al.(2024)].

尽管取得了显着的进展，但现有方法仍存在一些局限性。一、展品众多Despite notable progress, existing approaches share several limitations. First, many exhibit有限的概括limited generalization，因为它们是在简化的布局上进行训练和评估的，而这些布局并不能反映现实世界中交叉路口的多样性。其次，最先进的方法通常取决于, as they are trained and evaluated on simplified layouts that do not reflect the real-world diversity of intersections. Second, state-of-the-art methods often depend on特权信息privileged information，例如未来轨迹、博弈论先验、基于规则的安全层或专家演示，这些在实际部署中可能不可用。三、大部分方法采用, such as future trajectories, game-theoretic priors, rule-based safety layers, or expert demonstrations, that may be unavailable in practical deployments. Third, most methods employ固定、离散的动作空间fixed, discrete action spaces由一小组预定义的动作组成，这些动作与代理的数量组合扩展，并且当车辆功能发生变化时需要重新设计。consisting of small sets of predefined maneuvers, which scale combinatorially with the number of agents and require redesign when vehicle capabilities change.

模拟保真度。Simulation fidelity.

This choice is intentional: it isolates the multi-agent coordination mechanism from confounding factors, providing a controlled testbed for validating the原型计划proto-plan架构。然而，需要在更高保真度环境（例如 SUMO 或 CARLA）中进行验证，以确认当存在更丰富的动态和传感器噪声时，协调优势会转移。architecture. However, validation in higher-fidelity environments (e.g., SUMO or CARLA) is needed to confirm that coordination benefits transfer when richer dynamics and sensor noise are present.
