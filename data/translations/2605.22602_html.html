摘要Abstract

However, due to reliance on simple prompting strategies and insufficient ToM knowledge, existing LLMs often fail to capture the intrinsic dependencies among mental states, leading to fragmented representations and unstable reasoning. To address these challenges, we introduce the基于 ToM 的说服性对话 (ToM-PD)ToM-based Persuasive Dialogue (ToM-PD)任务，扎根于task, grounded in the信念-愿望-意图Belief–Desire–Intention（BDI）框架，它明确地模拟了多轮对话中心理状态之间的顺序依赖关系。为了促进这项任务的研究，我们构建了一个大规模的注释数据集，(BDI) framework, which explicitly models the sequential dependencies among mental states in multi-turn dialogues. To facilitate research on this task, we construct a large-scale annotated dataset,基于 ToM 的广泛说服性对话 (ToM-BPD)ToM-based Broad Persuasive Dialogues (ToM-BPD)，捕捉细粒度的心理状态和相应的说服策略。我们进一步建议, capturing fine-grained mental states and corresponding persuasive strategies. I 简介Introduction

说服是人类社会互动的基本组成部分Persuasion is a fundamental component of human social interaction[29, 30]。随着法学硕士的快速发展，基于法学硕士的说服性对话系统在情感支持等不同领域获得了越来越大的影响力. With the rapid advancement of LLMs, LLM-based persuasive dialogue systems have gained increasing influence across diverse domains, such as emotional support[23, 48]、慈善捐赠, charitable donation[27, 39]，以及谈判, and negotiation[22]。这些系统不仅需要生成流畅连贯的响应，还需要准确推断并有策略地影响用户的潜在心理状态. These systems not only need to generate fluent and coherent responses but also accurately infer and strategically influence users’ latent mental states[15].

心智理论，即推断他人信念、欲望和意图的能力，被广泛认为是社会推理和战略互动的核心认知机制Theory of Mind, the ability to infer others’ beliefs, desires, and intentions, is widely recognized as a core cognitive mechanism underlying social reasoning and strategic interaction[31, 1]。受这一见解的推动，最近的研究探索了将 ToM 建模集成到说服性对话系统中。例如，之前的工作将说服任务分解为多个协作代理，并使用专用组件来识别用户的情绪状态. Motivated by this insight, recent studies have explored the integration of ToM modeling into persuasive dialogue systems. For instance, prior work decomposes persuasion tasks into multiple collaborating agents, with dedicated components for identifying users’ emotional states[7]。其他方法模拟潜在的用户反驳和态度以提高说服效果. Other approaches simulate potential user rebuttals and attitudes to improve persuasive effectiveness[15]。此外，还利用思维链推理来模拟用户的心理状态，增强系统响应的同理心. In addition, chain-of-thought reasoning has been leveraged to model users’ mental states and enhance the empathy of system responses[48, 3]。尽管在基准任务上有效，但这些方法通常将不同的心理状态视为自变量，忽略它们的内在依赖性，这可能导致复杂对话中的不一致或不稳定的推论。. Although effective on benchmark tasks, these approaches often treat different mental states as independent variables, neglecting their intrinsic dependencies, which can lead to inconsistent or unstable inferences in complex dialogues.

一些基准还评估了法学硕士在有说服力的背景下的 ToM 推理能力。谈判模式Several benchmarks have also evaluated LLMs’ ToM reasoning capabilities in persuasive contexts. NegotiationToM[2]专注于谈判场景，ToMATOfocuses on negotiation scenarios, ToMATO[37]针对捐赠请求，以及 BigToMtargets donation requests, and BigToM[12]评估叙事上下文中的动作预测。有说服力的汤姆evaluates action prediction in narrative contexts. PersuasiveToM[46]进一步检查法学硕士根据用户的心理状态推理和预测说服策略的能力。然而，实证结果一致表明，法学硕士缺乏足够的先验知识来进行可靠的心理状态推理，限制了其推理的稳定性和鲁棒性。further examines LLMs’ ability to reason about and predict persuasive strategies based on users’ mental states. However, empirical results consistently indicate that LLMs lack sufficient prior knowledge for reliable mental-state inference, limiting the stability and robustness of their reasoning.

综上所述，将ToM应用于说服性对话系统并提高其性能仍然面临两大挑战：（1）如何系统地建模和推断用户的ToM状态及其在说服性对话中的内在依赖关系；（2）如何为LLM提供足够的知识以增强其ToM推理能力。In summary, applying ToM to persuasive dialogue systems and improving their performance still faces two major challenges: (1) how to systematically model and infer users’ ToM states and their intrinsic dependencies in persuasive dialogues, and (2) how to equip LLMs with sufficient knowledge to enhance their ToM reasoning capabilities.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于法学硕士的说服性对话代理LLM-based Persuasive Dialogue Agents

有说服力的对话旨在通过有针对性的沟通策略影响个人的信念、态度或行为Persuasive dialogue aims to influence individuals’ beliefs, attitudes, or behaviors through targeted communication strategies[35]。早期的研究主要集中在特定领域的场景，例如情感支持. Early studies mainly focused on domain-specific scenarios such as emotional support[23]、政策劝说, policy persuasion[5]，以及慈善筹款, and charitable fundraising[40]。随着大型语言模型的兴起，最近的工作将说服性对话扩展到更加多样化和复杂的环境，包括多轮推荐. With the rise of large language models, recent work has extended persuasive dialogue to more diverse and complex settings, including multi-turn recommendation[20], 对抗性提示, adversarial prompting[47]，以及误导或操纵行为, and misleading or manipulative behaviors[42, 11].

从技术角度来看，现有方法可大致分为三类。From a technical perspective, existing approaches can be broadly categorized into three lines.基于策略的方法Strategy-based methods使用预定义或学习的对话策略指导生成，提高可控性和可解释性guide generation with predefined or learned dialogue strategies, improving controllability and interpretability[40, 23, 6, 48, 3]. 知识增强方法Knowledge-enhanced methods结合外部知识或结构化记忆来提高情境理解和响应质量incorporate external knowledge or structured memory to improve contextual understanding and response quality[33, 10, 18, 24]. 多代理系统Multi-agent systems将对话分解为多个协调代理，从而实现更复杂的推理和协作决策decompose dialogue into multiple coordinated agents, enabling more complex reasoning and collaborative decision-making[7, 15, 36].

然而，大多数现有方法缺乏对用户心理状态的明确建模，限制了它们在说服性互动中的可解释性和长期有效性。相比之下，我们的框架以逐步的方式明确地模拟这些心理状态之间的依赖性，从而实现更稳健和可解释的推理来指导有说服力的策略选择。However, most existing approaches lack explicit modeling of users’ mental states, limiting their interpretability and long-term effectiveness in persuasive interactions. In contrast, our framework explicitly models the dependencies among these mental states in a stepwise manner, enabling more robust and interpretable inference to guide persuasive strategy selection.

Ⅱ-BII-B 人工智能中的心智理论Theory of Mind in AI

心理理论是指推断自己和他人心理状态的能力。在大语言模型时代，人们提出了各种基准来评估跨不同场景的ToM能力，包括具体环境、谈判设置、叙述理解和说服性交互Theory of Mind refers to the ability to infer one’s own and others’ mental states. In the era of large language models, a variety of benchmarks have been proposed to evaluate ToM capabilities across diverse scenarios, including embodied environments, negotiation settings, narrative understanding, and persuasive interactions[16, 2, 37, 12, 46]。现有证据表明，当前的法学硕士在心理状态推理方面仍然表现出有限的稳定性和可靠性，部分原因是这些模型中编码的先验 ToM 知识不足。. Existing evidence suggests that current LLMs still exhibit limited stability and reliability in mental-state inference, partly due to insufficient prior ToM knowledge encoded in these models.

为了增强法学硕士的 ToM 推理，现有的方法可以大致分为三类。To enhance ToM reasoning in LLMs, existing approaches can be broadly categorized into three lines.基于提示的方法Prompting-based methods通过推理时推理支架（例如观点采择提示、角色调节和假设驱动推理）改进 ToMimprove ToM through inference-time reasoning scaffolds, such as perspective-taking prompts, role conditioning, and hypothesis-driven reasoning[41, 38, 17, 8]. 结构化和神经符号方法Structured and neuro-symbolic approaches通过集成符号表示或概率推理框架来显式地建模信念和目标推理explicitly model belief and goal inference by integrating symbolic representations or probabilistic reasoning frameworks[34, 45]. 多智能体认知框架Multi-agent cognitive frameworks将 ToM 推理分解为多个交互组件来模拟社会认知过程decompose ToM reasoning into multiple interacting components to simulate social cognition processes[49].

尽管取得了这些进步，大多数现有方法主要侧重于对抽象或静态状态的推理，并且不足以对现实社会交互场景中用户的动态心理状态进行细粒度建模。我们的方法通过将 BDI 状态的逐步向后推理与知识增强推理相结合来解决这些限制，从而在多轮说服性对话中实现更准确、一致和可解释的 ToM 推理。Despite these advances, most existing methods primarily focus on reasoning over abstract or static states, and remain insufficient for fine-grained modeling of users’ dynamic mental states in real-world social interaction scenarios. Our approach addresses these limitations by combining stepwise backward inference over BDI states with knowledge-enhanced reasoning, enabling more accurate, consistent, and interpretable ToM inference in multi-turn persuasive dialogues.
