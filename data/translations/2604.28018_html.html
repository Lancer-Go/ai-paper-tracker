摘要Abstract

设计结构矩阵（DSM）模块化，将系统元素划分为 内聚模块，是工程中的基本组合挑战 设计。传统方法将模块化视为纯图 优化，无需访问嵌入的工程上下文 系统。以之前基于 LLM 的组合优化工作为基础 对于DSM测序，本文将方法扩展到模块化 涵盖五个案例和三个骨干法学硕士。我们的方法实现了 30 次迭代内接近参考质量，无需专门 优化代码。与直觉相反，领域知识有益于 排序，始终会损害更复杂的 DSM 的性能。我们 将此归因于法学硕士的功能之间的语义不一致 先验和纯结构优化目标，并提出Design Structure Matrix (DSM) modularization, the task of partitioning system elements into cohesive modules, is a fundamental combinatorial challenge in engineering design. Traditional methods treat modularization as a pure graph optimization, without access to the engineering context embedded in the system. Building on prior work on LLM-based combinatorial optimization for DSM sequencing, this paper extends the method to modularization across five cases and three backbone LLMs. Our method achieves near-reference quality within 30 iterations without requiring specialized optimization code. Counterintuitively, domain knowledge, beneficial in sequencing, consistently impairs performance on more complex DSMs. We attribute this to semantic misalignment between the LLM’s functional priors and the purely structural optimization objective, and propose the语义对齐假设semantic-alignment hypothesis作为可测试的条件 法学硕士的知识有效性。消融研究确定了最 有效的输入表示、目标表述和解决方案池 为实际部署而设计。这些发现提供了实际指导 用于在工程设计优化中部署法学硕士。as a testable condition governing knowledge effectiveness with LLMs. Ablation studies identify the most effective input representation, objective formulation, and solution pool design for practical deployment. These findings offer practical guidance for deploying LLMs in engineering design optimization.

1 简介Introduction

日益复杂的工程系统需要结构化方法 组织和管理其成员之间的相互依赖性 元素。设计结构矩阵（DSM）已成为广泛采用的 为此目的，提供了一个紧凑的矩阵表示 系统内的成对依赖关系Engineering systems of growing complexity require structured methods for organizing and managing interdependencies among their constituent elements. The Design Structure Matrix (DSM) has become a widely adopted tool for this purpose, providing a compact matrix representation of pairwise dependencies within a system[10, 25]。 DSM方法已应用于产品架构、流程规划、 项目管理和组织设计. DSM methods have been applied to product architecture, process planning, project management, and organizational design[15, 17, 21, 23]。许多方法论文献 围绕核心分析任务开发：测序、 分区、模块化. Many methodological literature has developed around the core analytical tasks: sequencing, partitioning, and modularization[8].

这些任务中，DSM模块化（也称为集群） 对于系统设计尤其重要。寻求模块化 将 DSM 的元素划分为内聚的模块，从而最大限度地减少 跨模块边界耦合并最大化内部交互密度 模块。良好的模块化架构可实现并行开发、支持 重用和升级，减少设计变更的传播Among these tasks, DSM modularization (also referred to as clustering) is particularly consequential for systems design. Modularization seeks to partition the elements of a DSM into cohesive modules that minimize coupling across module boundaries and maximize interaction density within modules. Good modular architectures enable parallel development, support reuse and upgrade, and reduce the propagation of design changes[11, 19]。 Thebeau 正式化了这一点 作为通过 TotalCost 指标的优化问题，该指标已 被许多研究采用. Thebeau formalized this as an optimization problem via the TotalCost metric, which has been adopted in many studies[26]。潜在的组合问题是 NP-hard：可行分区的数量随着贝尔数的增加而增加. 现有的优化算法，包括模拟退火、遗传 算法和改进的聚类启发法找到接近最优的结果 有效分区Existing optimization algorithms, including Simulated Annealing, Genetic Algorithms, and improved clustering heuristics, find near-optimal partitions effectively[4, 26, 28]。然而，这些方法 将模块化视为纯粹的图优化：它们最小化 目标而不考虑其工程意义 元素。因此，他们无法将设计师的知识融入其中 功能关系、系统架构意图或物理关系 限制。大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展提供了 本质上不同的计算范式。通过上下文 学习和复杂推理. However, these methods treat modularization as a pure graph optimization: they minimize the objectives without reasoning about the engineering meaning of the elements. As a result, they cannot incorporate designer knowledge about functional relationships, system architecture intent, or physical constraints. Recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) offer a qualitatively different computational paradigm. Through in-context learning and complex reasoning[5], 法学硕士可以同时处理 元素的结构信息和自然语言描述， 原则上将优化能力与工程推理相结合。, LLMs can process both structural information and natural language descriptions of the elements, in principle combining optimization power with engineering reasoning.

2.2 组合优化法学硕士LLMs for Combinatorial Optimization

组合优化在运筹学领域有着悠久的历史 计算机科学，范围从精确方法（例如分支定界法， 动态规划）到元启发法（例如模拟退火， 进化算法）。最近的工作探索了一种新的范式：使用 法学硕士作为优化器。杨等人。Combinatorial optimization has a long history in operations research and computer science, ranging from exact methods (e.g., branch-and-bound, dynamic programming) to metaheuristics (e.g., simulated annealing, evolutionary algorithms). Recent work has explored a new paradigm: using LLMs as optimizers. Yang et al.[27]引入了 OPRO，证明了 法学硕士可以通过迭代细化候选人来优化目标函数 用自然语言描述的解决方案。给出包含以下内容的提示 之前评估的解决方案按质量排名，法学硕士生成 通过从示例中推断模式来改进候选者。这个 方法不需要梯度计算或显式求解器代码， 相反，依赖于法学硕士的模式完成能力。趣搜introduced OPRO, demonstrating that LLMs can optimize objective functions by iteratively refining candidate solutions described in natural language. Given a prompt containing previously evaluated solutions ranked by quality, the LLM generates improved candidates by extrapolating patterns from the examples. This approach requires no gradient computation or explicit solver code, relying instead on the LLM’s pattern-completion capabilities. FunSearch[22]将这个想法扩展到数学发现， 使用 LLM 在程序空间中搜索可改进的功能 困难组合问题的已知解决方案。他们的结果表明 LLM 引导的搜索可以发现最先进的问题解决方案 包括瓶盖结构和箱体包装。这些方法都有一个共同点 共同结构：法学硕士生成候选解决方案，这些解决方案是 由外部评分员进行评估，最佳解决方案反馈为 下一代回合的上下文示例。extended this idea to mathematical discovery, using LLMs to search over program space for functions that improve on known solutions to hard combinatorial problems. Their results demonstrated that LLM-guided search can discover state-of-the-art solutions on problems including cap set construction and bin packing. These approaches share a common structure: an LLM generates candidate solutions, which are evaluated by an external scorer, with the best solutions fed back as in-context examples for the next generation round.

在 DSM 领域，最近的两项工作将 LLM 应用于 DSM 任务。 许In the DSM domain, two recent lines of work apply LLMs to DSM tasks. Koh[13]推出 Auto-DSM，证明法学硕士可以 从自然语言项目描述生成 DSM 结构， 生成任务而不是优化。后续研究introduced Auto-DSM, demonstrating that LLMs can generate DSM structures from natural language project descriptions, a generation task rather than optimization. 6 结束语Concluding Remarks

本文对基于法学硕士的系统研究 跨五个 DSM 模块化的组合优化 工程案例和三个骨干法学硕士。 LLM-CO 实现接近参考 30 次迭代内的质量，无需求解器代码或梯度计算， Claude-Sonnet-4.6 展示了最一致的性能 在所有情况下。核心发现是This paper presents a systematic investigation of LLM-based combinatorial optimization for DSM modularization across five engineering cases and three backbone LLMs. LLM-CO achieves near-reference quality within 30 iterations without solver code or gradient computation, with Claude-Sonnet-4.6 demonstrating the most consistent performance across all cases. The central finding is a知识悖论knowledge paradox： 领域知识，有利于法学硕士的 DSM 测序，系统地 损害更复杂的 DSM 上的模块化性能。我们归因 这是因为法学硕士的功能先验和 纯粹的结构优化目标。跨模型，我们发现 这种悖论在能力更强的模型中最为明显 可靠的结构优化，而能力较差的模型则显示出 领域知识带来的早期优势随着解决方案的出现而减弱 池成熟。: domain knowledge, beneficial in DSM sequencing with LLMs, systematically impairs modularization performance on more complex DSMs. We attribute this to semantic misalignment between the LLM’s functional priors and the purely structural optimization objective. Across models, we find that this paradox is most clearly observable in stronger models capable of reliable structural optimization, while less capable models show an early advantage from domain knowledge that diminishes as the solution pool matures.

这项研究有几个局限性。基准案例涵盖 DSM 最多 19 个元素，对更大实例的可扩展性仍然是开放的 问题。对于较大的情况，SA 参考并非最佳选择， 以及除了三个测试之外的其他模型系列可能会展示 不同的知识敏感性模式。不同的 DSM 类型可能会表现出 语义对齐的不同模式，知识效应可能 相应地有所不同。例如，索萨等人。This study has several limitations. The benchmark cases cover DSMs with at most 19 elements, and scalability to larger instances remains an open question. The SA reference is not provably optimal for the larger cases, and additional model families beyond the three tested may exhibit different knowledge sensitivity patterns. Different DSM types may exhibit different patterns of semantic alignment, and the knowledge effect may vary accordingly. For instance, Sosa et al.[24]凭经验证明 组件架构和组织之间的不一致 结构可能很大且难以检测，这表明 有关物理接口的领域知识可能更加隐性和困难 法学硕士可以有效地利用有关活动或活动的知识 参数。跨 DSM 类型系统地调查这一点需要 比本研究提供的实证覆盖范围更广。最后，这个 研究使用 TotalCost 和 Clustering 评估模块化质量 效率，这是工程设计界常用的； 未来的工作应该检查研究结果是否可以推广到替代方案 来自网络科学界的指标，例如模块化指标 描述于demonstrated empirically that misalignment between component architecture and organizational structure can be substantial and difficult to detect, suggesting that domain knowledge about physical interfaces may be more tacit and harder for LLMs to leverage effectively than knowledge about activities or parameters.
