用于车辆事故伤害严重程度预测的新型动态批量敏感 Adam 优化器。Novel Dynamic Batch-Sensitive Adam Optimiser for Vehicular Accident Injury Severity Prediction.

摘要ABSTRACT

优化器的选择在深度学习中很重要，因为它强烈影响模型效率和收敛速度。然而，许多常用的优化器在应用于不平衡和顺序数据集时遇到困难，限制了它们捕获少数类模式的能力。在本研究中，我们提出了动态批量敏感 Adam (DBS-Adam)，这是一种优化器，它使用从梯度范数和批量损失的指数移动平均值导出的批量难度分数来动态缩放学习率。 DBS-Adam 通过增加困难批次的更新并减少较容易批次的更新来提高训练稳定性并加速收敛。我们通过将 DBS-Adam 与用于事故伤害严重程度预测的双向 LSTM 网络集成来对其进行评估，并通过 SMOTE-ENN 重采样和焦点损失解决类别不平衡问题。四种实验配置比较基线 Bi-LSTM 模型和替代架构，以评估优化器的影响。通过五个随机种子与最先进的优化器（AMSGrad、AdamW、AdaBound）进行严格比较，证明了 DBS-Adam 的竞争性能，具有统计上显着的精度改进（𝑝= 0.020）。结果表明，DBS-Adam 的性能优于标准优化方法，测试准确率达到 95.22%，精确度达到 96.11%，召回率达到 95.28%，F1 分数达到 95.39%，测试损失达到 0.0086。所提出的框架能够对有针对性的应急响应和道路安全干预措施进行有效的实时事故严重程度分类，展示了 DBS-Adam 从不平衡序列数据中学习的价值。The choice of optimiser is important in deep learning, as it strongly influences model efficiency and speed of convergence. However, many commonly used optimisers encounter difficulties when applied to imbalanced and sequential datasets, limiting their ability to capture patterns of minority classes. In this study, we propose Dynamic Batch-Sensitive Adam (DBS-Adam), an optimiser that dynamically scales the learning rate using a batch difficulty score derived from exponential moving averages of gradient norms and batch loss. DBS-Adam improves training stability and accelerates convergence by increasing updates for difficult batches and reducing them for easier ones. We evaluate DBS-Adam by integrating it with Bi-Directional LSTM networks for accident injury severity prediction, addressing class imbalance through SMOTE-ENN resampling and Focal Loss. Four experimental configurations compare baseline Bi-LSTM models and alternative architectures to assess optimiser impact. Rigorous comparison against state-of-the-art optimisers (AMSGrad, AdamW, AdaBound) across five random seeds demonstrated DBS-Adam's competitive performance with statistically significant precision improvements (𝑝= 0.020). Results indicate that DBS-Adam outperforms standard optimisation approaches, achieving 95.22% test accuracy, 96.11% precision, 95.28% recall, 95.39% F1-score, and a test loss of 0.0086. The proposed framework enables effective real-time accident severity classification for targeted emergency response and road safety interventions, demonstrating the value of DBS-Adam for learning from imbalanced sequential data.

一、简介1. Introduction

深度学习模型的性能很大程度上受到训练中采用的优化技术的影响。 随机梯度下降 (SGD)、Adam 和 RMSProp 是常见优化器的示例，当用于噪声、不平衡或顺序数据集时，这些优化器可能会遇到收敛缓慢、不稳定和效率下降的问题 [1]、[2]、[3]、[4]。 依赖安全的应用程序受这些缺陷的影响最大。 由于道路事故记录结合了时间结构、测量噪声和明显的类别不平衡，因此它们是这些困难的典型例子，这使得训练值得信赖的模型更具挑战性。The performance of deep learning models is heavily influenced by the optimisation technique employed in training. Stochastic gradient descent (SGD), Adam, and RMSProp are examples of common optimisers that may experience slow convergence, instability, and decreased efficacy when used on noisy, unbalanced, or sequential datasets [1], [2], [3], [4]. Applications that depend on safety are most affected by these flaws. Because they combine temporal structure, measurement noise, and a noticeable class imbalance, road accident records are a prime example of these difficulties, making it more challenging to train trustworthy models.

道路交通事故 (RTA) 是全球严重的健康负担：世界卫生组织估计 2021 年约有 130 万人死亡（约每 10 万人中有 15 人死亡），其中低收入和中等收入国家受到的影响最大。使用机器学习 (ML) 和深度学习 (DL) 方法准确预测 RTA 伤害严重程度可以改善应急响应、降低死亡率并实现更有效的医疗资源分配。这种预测系统可以为分诊决策提供信息，确定救护车调度的优先顺序，并指导事故现场和接收医院的短期资源规划[5]、[6]、[7]。最近的工作表明了神经网络在事故预测中的有用性。 [8] 报道称，基于 LSTM 的 RNN 在马来西亚事故数据上的准确率达到了 71.77%，优于多层感知器 (65.48%) 和贝叶斯逻辑回归 (58.30%)。 [9]应用带有 SMOTE 的 LSTM 进行实时碰撞风险预测，其灵敏度达到 60.67%，而条件逻辑模型的灵敏度为 56.72%。这些研究证实了 ANN 和 LSTM 方法在事故分析方面的潜力，但也强调了类别不平衡和对传统优化器的依赖所带来的持续困难，指出需要改进优化策略，例如本研究中提出的 DBS-Adam。Road traffic accidents (RTAs) represent a critical global health burden: the World Health Organisation estimates about 1.3 million deaths in 2021 (≈15 deaths per 100,000 population), with the greatest impact felt in low- and middleincome countries. Accurate prediction of injury severity from RTAs using machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) methods can improve emergency response, reduce mortality, and enable more efficient allocation of medical resources. Such predictive systems can inform triage decisions, prioritise ambulance dispatch, and guide short-term resource planning at crash sites and receiving hospitals [5], [6], [7]. Recent work has shown the usefulness of neural networks for accident prediction. [8] reported that an LSTM-based RNN achieved 71.77% accuracy, outperforming a multilayer perceptron (65.48%) and Bayesian logistic regression (58.30%) on Malaysian accident data. [9] applied an LSTM with SMOTE for real-time crash risk prediction, reaching a sensitivity of 60.67% compared with 56.72% for a conditional logistic model. These studies confirm the potential of ANN and LSTM methods for accident analysis but also highlight continuing difficulties with class imbalance and reliance on conventional optimisers, pointing to the need for improved optimisation strategies such as the DBS-Adam proposed in this study.

为了解决不平衡序列域中的一些优化挑战，本研究引入了动态批量敏感 Adam (DBS-Adam)，这是一种新颖的优化器，它使用从梯度范数和批量损失的指数移动平均值导出的难度分数来调整批量级别的全局学习率。 DBS-Adam 旨在稳定训练并加速在不平衡交通事故数据上训练的深度模型的收敛。我们通过将优化器与用于伤害严重程度预测的双向长短期记忆 (Bi-LSTM) 网络集成来评估优化器，并证明其在克服该领域关键优化问题方面的有效性。To address some of these optimisation challenges in imbalanced sequential domains, this study introduces Dynamic Batch-Sensitive Adam (DBS-Adam), a novel optimiser that adapts the global learning rate at the batch level using a difficulty score derived from exponential moving averages of gradient norms and batch loss. DBS-Adam is designed to stabilise training and accelerate convergence for deep models trained on imbalanced traffic-accident data. We evaluate the optimiser by integrating it with a Bi-Directional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) network for injuryseverity prediction and demonstrate its effectiveness in overcoming key optimisation issues in this domain.

1.1.传统统计方法与机器学习方法1.1. Traditional Statistical Methods vs. Machine Learning Approaches

传统的统计方法，例如逻辑回归和广义线性模型，长期以来一直被用来分析碰撞伤害的严重程度。采用逻辑回归的研究报告准确度高达 87% [10]，而广义线性模型的确定系数约为 0.62 [11]。 ARIMA 等时间序列技术也得到了应用，在预测马来西亚数据集中的事故频率方面，其性能优于泊松模型和负二项式模型 [12]。然而，这些方法依赖于强大的分布假设，并且通常难以捕获异构崩溃环境中的复杂交互。Traditional statistical approaches, such as logistic regression and generalised linear models, have long been used to analyse crash injury severity. Studies employing logistic regression report accuracies of up to 87% [10], while generalised linear models achieve coefficients of determination of approximately 0.62 [11]. Time-series techniques such as ARIMA have also been applied, outperforming Poisson and negative binomial models in predicting accident frequency in Malaysian datasets [12]. These approaches, however, rely on strong distributional assumptions and often struggle to capture complex interactions in heterogeneous crash environments.

相比之下，机器学习方法已经证明了预测能力的提高，特别是在建模非线性关系和高维特征空间时。随机森林、XGBoost、支持向量机、多层感知器和朴素贝叶斯分类器等技术在各种事故严重性研究中显示出准确性和灵敏度的显着提高 [13]、[14]、[15]、[16]、[17]。基于神经网络的方法（包括 LSTM 和 Bi-LSTM 架构）通过对时间依赖性和上下文模式进行建模，进一步提高了顺序碰撞数据的性能。这些方法的越来越多的采用反映了与传统统计模型相比，它们的灵活性和更强的实证性能。In contrast, machine learning methods have demonstrated improved predictive capability, particularly when modelling non-linear relationships and high-dimensional feature spaces. Techniques such as Random Forests, XGBoost, Support Vector Machines, multilayer perceptrons and naïve Bayes classifiers have shown substantial gains in accuracy and sensitivity across various accident-severity studies [13], [14], [15], [16], [17]. Neural network-based approaches, including LSTM and Bi-LSTM architectures, have further improved performance in sequential crash data by modelling temporal dependencies and contextual patterns. The increasing adoption of these methods reflects their flexibility and stronger empirical performance compared with traditional statistical models.

1.2.人工神经网络架构1.2. Artificial Neural Network Architectures

用于事故预测的最常用的 ANN 架构包括前馈网络（单层和多层感知器）、循环神经网络（RNN）和卷积神经网络（CNN），所有这些都在事故相关预测中表现出高精度[18]、[19]。使用具有碰撞相关参数的多层感知器的模型报告的准确度在 73.5% 至 74.6% 之间 [20]、[21]，而模糊 c 均值等聚类技术进一步提高了伤害严重程度预测的性能[20]、[22] [1]、[20]、[22]。最近的应用凸显了 ANN 的多功能性：使用 ANN 突出显示了土耳其高速公路上的道路几何形状； [23] 采用 ANN-LSTM 混合模型进行时间序列预测； [24]报告严重性建模中的 R ≈ 0.87，马来西亚 RNN 模型的验证准确率达到 71.8% [8]。向机器学习方法的转变解决了传统统计模型的局限性，传统统计模型通常带有可能导致结果不准确的固有假设[25]。预测准确性的提高有助于缩短医疗响应时间、加强交通管理，并与联合国 2030 年可持续发展目标保持一致，减少道路事故死亡人数[26]。The most commonly used ANN architectures for accident prediction include feedforward networks (single-layer and multilayer perceptrons), recurrent neural networks (RNNs), and convolutional neural networks (CNNs), all demonstrating high accuracy in accident-related predictions[18], [19]. Models using multilayer perceptrons with crashrelated parameters report accuracies between 73.5% to 74.6% [20], [21], and clustering techniques such as fuzzy c-means further enhance performance in injury severity prediction[20], [22] [1],[20], [22]. More recent applications highlight ANN's versatility: highlighted roadway geometry on Turkish highways using ANN; [23] employed an ANN–LSTM hybrid for time-series forecasting; [24] reported R ≈ 0.87 in severity modelling, and a Malaysian RNN model reached 71.8% validation accuracy [8]. The shift toward machine learning approaches addresses limitations of traditional statistical models, which often carry inherent assumptions that may lead to inaccurate results[25]. Improved prediction accuracy supports faster medical response times, enhanced traffic management, and alignment with UN 2030 Sustainable Development Goals for reducing road accident fatalities[26].

1.3.神经网络训练的优化算法1.3. Optimisation Algorithms for Neural Network Training

在神经网络训练中，最常用的优化算法是一阶方法，例如随机梯度下降（SGD）和自适应变体，包括 RMSProp、Adam 和 Adagrad。这些算法已广泛应用于图像分类、自然语言处理和时间序列预测等任务中[3]、[4]。自适应方法通常表现出更好的收敛性和稳定性。 Adam 和 RMSProp 尤其在训练和评估阶段都表现良好 [3]。比较研究表明，虽然 Adam 通常收敛速度更快 [27]，但具有动量的 SGD 可以在某些数据集上实现更高的性能。混合规范 Adam (HN_Adam) 等修改方法已显示出比标准优化器 [28] 更高的收敛性和准确性，而混合方法（例如混合了 Adam 和 SGD 的自适应元优化器 (ATMO)）在分类任务中取得了比使用单个优化器更好的结果 [29]。然而，在顺序学习任务中，优化仍然具有挑战性，特别是在类别不平衡的情况下。In neural network training, the most commonly used optimisation algorithms are first-order methods such as Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) and adaptive variants including RMSProp, Adam, and Adagrad. These algorithms have been extensively applied across tasks in image classification, natural language processing, and time series forecasting [3], [4]. Adaptive methods often demonstrate better convergence and stability. Adam and RMSProp, in particular, perform well in both training and evaluation phases [3]. Comparative studies report that while Adam is typically faster to converge [27], SGD with momentum can achieve higher performance on some datasets. Modified approaches such as Hybrid Norm Adam (HN_Adam) have shown improved convergence and accuracy over standard optimisers [28], and hybrid methods like Adaptive Meta Optimisers (ATMO), which blend Adam and SGD, have achieved superior results in classification tasks than using a single optimiser [29]. However, optimisation remains challenging in sequential learning tasks, particularly under class imbalance.

最近在优化和特征选择方面的工作进一步证明了混合和元启发式方法在提高学习稳定性和参数效率方面的价值。 [30]采用后向消除和网格搜索优化来增强基于随机森林的 DDoS 分类，表明系统的超参数选择可以显着提高检测性能。在相关研究中，[31]引入了一种将二元粒子群优化与黑寡妇算法相结合的混合优化策略，用于特征选择和超参数调整，从而大幅提高了空气质量预测精度。这些发现强调了集成特征选择和优化框架在复杂、高维数据与敏感超参数交互的领域中具有更广泛的相关性，这个问题同样影响着事故严重程度预测的深度学习模型。Recent work in optimisation and feature selection further demonstrates the value of hybrid and meta-heuristic approaches for improving learning stability and parameter efficiency. [30] employed backward elimination with gridsearch optimisation to enhance Random Forest-based DDoS classification, showing that systematic hyperparameter selection can markedly improve detection performance. In a related study, [31] introduced a hybrid optimisation strategy combining Binary Particle Swarm Optimisation with the Black Widow Algorithm for feature selection and hyperparameter tuning, achieving substantial gains in air-quality prediction accuracy. These findings highlight the broader relevance of integrated feature-selection and optimisation frameworks in domains where complex, high-dimensional data interact with sensitive hyperparameters, an issue that equally affects deep learning models for accident-severity prediction.

此外，一项关于孟加拉文献作者身份检测的研究使用了使用 Adam 优化器以 0.0001 的学习率优化的 Bi-LSTM 模型，取得了 92% 的最高测试精度，超过了 85% 的 RMSprop[39]。对于电价预测，与传统方法相比，Adam 优化的 LSTM 神经网络表现出更高的预测精度 [40]。这些研究共同强调了 Adam 优化器在增强跨不同领域的 Bi-LSTM 模型性能方面的有效性，包括故障诊断、语言翻译、作者归属和价格预测。Also, a research study on Bangla literature authorship detection using a Bi-LSTM model optimised with Adam optimiser at a learning rate of 0.0001 achieved the highest testing accuracy of 92% over RMSprop of 85%[39]. For electricity price forecasting, Adam-optimised LSTM neural networks demonstrated improved prediction accuracy compared to traditional methods [40]. These studies collectively highlight the effectiveness of Adam optimiser in enhancing Bi-LSTM model performance across diverse domains, including fault diagnosis, language translation, authorship attribution, and price forecasting.

尽管 Adam 很受欢迎，但它也有明显的缺点，包括某些情况下泛化性差、对超参数敏感以及收敛问题，特别是在深度网络中 [1]。为了解决 Adam 的缺点，人们提出了几种变体：AMSGrad 通过确保学习率不会突然增加来对 Adam 进行修正，从而防止出现收敛问题 [1]。此外，AdaBound 应用动态学习率界限来提高泛化能力。虽然它可以稳定训练，但需要仔细调整[41]、[42]。 Adam 前瞻（AWL）算法通过结合前瞻机制增强了传统的 Adam 优化器，从而提高了各种模型（包括逻辑回归和神经网络）的收敛特性和性能[43]。然而，它仍然在收敛速度或过度拟合方面遇到困难。Despite its popularity, Adam has notable drawbacks, including poor generalisation in some cases, sensitivity to hyperparameters, and issues with convergence, particularly in deep networks [1]. To address Adam's shortcomings, several variants have been proposed: AMSGrad introduces a correction to Adam by ensuring the learning rate does not increase abruptly, preventing convergence issues [1]. Additionally, AdaBound applies dynamic learning rate bounds to improve generalisation. While it stabilises training, it requires careful tuning [41], [42]. 2.1.提议的动态批量敏感 Adam (DBS-Adam) 优化器2.1. Proposed Dynamic Batch-Sensitive Adam (DBS-Adam) Optimiser

所提出的动态批量敏感 Adam (DBS-Adam) 优化器引入了自适应学习率机制，可响应训练期间的批量难度。与仅在参数级别调整学习率的标准 Adam 不同，DBS-Adam 使用从归一化梯度范数和批量损失导出的难度分数来调节全局学习率。这种方法使优化器能够将更大的更新分配给具有挑战性的批次，这些批次通常富含少数类样本，同时减少更容易的批次的更新，从而稳定收敛并增强少数类学习。The proposed Dynamic Batch-Sensitive Adam (DBS-Adam) optimiser introduces an adaptive learning-rate mechanism that responds to batch difficulty during training. Unlike standard Adam, which adjusts learning rates only at the parameter level, DBS-Adam modulates the global learning rate using a difficulty score derived from the normalised gradient norm and batch loss. This approach enables the optimiser to allocate larger updates to challenging batches, which are often enriched with minority-class samples, while reducing updates for easier batches, thereby stabilising convergence and enhancing minority-class learning.

DBS-Adam 的公式直接与 Adam 的矩估计框架集成，确保计算兼容性，同时引入捕获批次级复杂性的原则方法。The formulation of DBS-Adam integrates directly with Adam’s moment-estimation framework, ensuring computational compatibility whilst introducing a principled method for capturing batch-level complexity.

本研究使用梯度范数和批次损失作为训练期间批次难度的补充指标。梯度范数反映了模型参数必须改变多大程度才能减少给定批次的误差。当模型遇到难以学习的模式、冲突的特征或代表性不足的类时，通常会出现大梯度范数，这表明批次施加了强大的优化压力。先前的研究表明，梯度大小是学习难度和优化不稳定性的可靠信号，特别是在深度和不平衡模型中[32]、[33]、[35]。The gradient norm and batch loss are used in this study as complementary indicators of batch difficulty during training. The gradient norm reflects how strongly the model parameters must change to reduce error for a given batch. Large gradient norms typically arise when the model encounters hard-to-learn patterns, conflicting features, or underrepresented classes, indicating that the batch exerts strong optimisation pressure. Prior studies have shown that gradient magnitude is a reliable signal of learning difficulty and optimisation instability, particularly in deep and imbalanced models [32], [33], [35].

相比之下，批次损失衡量的是模型当前对批次内样本的预测有多差。高损失值表明该批次包含错误分类或不明确的样本，而低损失值表明该批次已经被很好地学习。基于损失的难度测量已广泛应用于课程学习和困难示例挖掘中，以优先考虑信息样本并改善少数群体的学习[34]，[48]。The batch loss, in contrast, measures how poorly the model currently predicts the samples within a batch. High loss values indicate that the batch contains misclassified or ambiguous samples, while low loss values suggest that the batch is already well learned. Loss-based difficulty measures have been widely adopted in curriculum learning and hardexample mining to prioritise informative samples and improve minority-class learning [34], [48].

与单独使用任一信号相比，梯度范数和批次损失一起使用可以提供更稳健的批次难度估计。批次损失捕获预测误差，而梯度范数捕获优化压力。它们的组合使优化器能够识别预测不佳且难以优化的批次，这在不平衡数据集中很常见。这一原则在之前的工作中得到了支持，表明结合基于梯度和基于损失的信号可以带来更稳定和有效的学习动态[32]、[33]。在 DBS-Adam 中，这种组合的难度信号用于调整批次级别的学习率，从而为困难的批次提供更大的更新，为更容易的批次提供更小的更新。Used together, gradient norm and batch loss provide a more robust estimate of batch difficulty than either signal alone. While batch loss captures predictive error, gradient norm captures optimisation stress. Their combination allows the optimiser to identify batches that are both poorly predicted and difficult to optimise, which are common in imbalanced datasets. This principle has been supported in prior work showing that combining gradient- and loss-based signals leads to more stable and effective learning dynamics [32], [33]. In DBS-Adam, this combined difficulty signal is used to adapt the learning rate at the batch level, enabling larger updates for difficult batches and smaller updates for easier ones.

尽管 Bi-LSTM 与 Adam 相结合通常表现良好，但其行为在不平衡的序列数据集上可能会不一致。 Adam 的每参数更新规则没有考虑批次组成的变化，当少数类样本很少出现或集中批次出现时，会导致不稳定的梯度动态 [4]、[36]、[49]。这些波动削弱了罕见但严重伤害类别的表现[32]，[35]。 DBS-Adam 通过将批次难度纳入优化过程、跨异构批次产生更稳定的更新以及增强少数类别体系中的学习来解决这一限制。尽管计算密集且成本高昂，但我们的目标是使模型能够更有效地响应具有挑战性的数据，并防止训练期间过度拟合或停滞。下面小节详细介绍了 DBS-Adam 的数学公式和更新规则。Although Bi-LSTM combined with Adam generally performs well, its behaviour can be inconsistent on imbalanced sequential datasets. These fluctuations weaken performance on rare but critical injury classes [32], [35]. DBS-Adam addresses this limitation by incorporating batch difficulty into the optimisation process, producing more stable updates across heterogeneous batches and enhancing learning in minority-class regimes. 2.2. DBS-Adam 在现有优化器中的定位2.2. Positioning of DBS-Adam within Existing Optimisers

SGD、RMSProp 和 Adam 等一阶优化算法仍然广泛用于训练神经网络。 SGD 简单且有效，但对学习率选择敏感，并且通常在噪声或不平衡数据集上收敛缓慢。 RMSProp 和 Adam 引入了自适应步长，提高了跨异构梯度的收敛稳定性，尽管 Adam 在高度可变的梯度幅度下可能表现出次优的泛化和不稳定的行为 [1]、[3]。 AMSGrad、AdamW 和 AdaBound 等增强型变体旨在通过修改矩估计或限制学习率动态来解决这些问题，但它们仍然依赖于每个参数的自适应，而不考虑批量级别的难度。所提出的 DBS-Adam 优化器通过引入一种动态机制来补充这些方法，该机制根据批次难度缩放全局学习率，从而在不平衡的顺序数据集上实现更一致的优化行为。这将 DBS-Adam 定位为批量敏感的改进，而不是现有自适应优化器的替代品。First-order optimisation algorithms such as SGD, RMSProp and Adam remain widely used for training neural networks. SGD is simple and effective, but is sensitive to learning-rate choices and often converges slowly on noisy or imbalanced datasets. RMSProp and Adam introduce adaptive step sizes, improving convergence stability across heterogeneous gradients, although Adam may exhibit suboptimal generalisation and erratic behaviour under highly variable gradient magnitudes [1], [3]. Enhanced variants such as AMSGrad, AdamW and AdaBound aim to address these issues by modifying moment estimates or constraining learning-rate dynamics, yet they continue to rely on per-parameter adaptation without considering batch-level difficulty. The proposed DBS-Adam optimiser complements these methods by introducing a dynamic mechanism that scales the global learning rate according to batch difficulty, enabling more consistent optimisation behaviour on imbalanced sequential datasets. This positions DBS-Adam as a batch-sensitive refinement rather than a replacement for existing adaptive optimisers.

Although the improvements over AMSGrad and AdamW do not reach statistical significance at the p < 0.05 threshold, DBS-Adam’s consistent advantage across all metrics, combined with its statistically significant precision gain over AdaBound, establishes it as a strong optimisation strategy for accident-severity classification. 4. 讨论4. Discussion

研究结果表明，所提出的 Bi-LSTM 框架经过动态 BatchSensitive Adam (DBS-Adam) 优化器的增强，可以有效地将道路交通事故伤害严重程度分为三类：轻微、严重和致命。在四种实验配置中，该模型始终优于标准 LSTM 和 Bi-LSTM 基线，特别是在处理类别不平衡和实现卓越收敛方面。The study results demonstrate that the proposed Bi-LSTM framework, enhanced with the Dynamic BatchSensitive Adam (DBS-Adam) optimiser, effectively classifies road traffic accident injury severity into three categories: slight, severe, and fatal. Across the four experimental configurations, the model consistently outperformed standard LSTM and Bi-LSTM baselines, particularly in handling class imbalance and achieving superior convergence.

4 类和 3 类配方之间的系统比较证明了标签质量的至关重要性。删除“未知”类别后，准确率提高了 17.55 个百分点，从 77.39% 提高到 94.94%，测试损失降低了 93.6%。这验证了较小数据集上的明确标签优于具有模糊目标信息的较大数据集[54]，[55]。The systematic comparison between 4-class and 3-class formulations demonstrated the critical importance of label quality. Removing the Unknown category resulted in a 17.55 percentage-point accuracy improvement from 77.39% to 94.94% and a 93.6% reduction in test loss. This validates that unambiguous labels on smaller datasets outperform larger datasets with ambiguous target information [54], [55].

However, the integration of DBS-Adam, focal loss and SMOTE-ENN extends previous work by improving performance on minority classes, which are often overlooked in accident severity studies. This improvement is particularly relevant in real-world applications, where accurate identification of severe and fatal cases is critical for emergency response.

5. 结论5. Conclusion

本研究开发了用于车辆事故伤害严重程度预测的双向 LSTM 框架，并通过焦点损失、SMOTE-ENN 和动态批量敏感 Adam (DBS-Adam) 优化器进行了增强。实验证实标签质量强烈影响模型的可靠性。删除不明确的未知类别将准确性从 77.39% 提高到 94.94%，这表明，与报告不一致的较大数据集相比，精炼标签对预测性能的贡献更大。最终模型的平均准确度为 95.22%，精确度为 96.11%，召回率为 95.28%，优于基线 LSTM 模型，并在所有严重级别上保持平衡的结果。This study developed a Bi-Directional LSTM framework for vehicular accident injury-severity prediction, enhanced with Focal Loss, SMOTE-ENN and a Dynamic Batch-Sensitive Adam (DBS-Adam) optimiser. The experiments confirmed that label quality strongly affects model reliability. Removing the ambiguous Unknown category improved accuracy from 77.39% to 94.94%, demonstrating that refined labels contribute more to predictive performance than larger datasets with inconsistent reporting. The final model achieved a mean accuracy of 95.22%, with 96.11% precision and 95.28% recall, outperforming baseline LSTM models and maintaining balanced results across all severity classes.

与 AMSGrad、AdamW 和 AdaBound 的比较分析表明，DBS-Adam 对于不平衡的序列数据提供了一致的增益。与 AdaBound 相比，它在统计上取得了显着的精度改进（p = 0.020，Cohen d = 1.35），并且比 AMSGrad 和 AdamW 取得了适度但稳定的改进。其批量难度调制支持稳定收敛并增强少数类学习，特别是对于致命伤害。Comparative analysis with AMSGrad, AdamW and AdaBound showed that DBS-Adam provides consistent gains for imbalanced sequential data. It achieved a statistically significant precision improvement over AdaBound (p = 0.020, Cohen’s d = 1.35) and modest but steady improvements over AMSGrad and AdamW. Its batch-difficulty modulation supported stable convergence and enhanced minority-class learning, particularly for Fatal injuries.

该研究的结论应根据数据集的限制来考虑，包括覆盖范围有限和标签细化后尺寸减小。尽管可以通过分层抽样和重复运行来缓解，但更广泛的数据集将增强普遍性。未来的研究可能会扩展难度制定、探索多任务设置并评估紧急响应系统内的操作部署。The study’s conclusions should be considered in light of dataset limitations, including restricted coverage and reduced size after label refinement. Although mitigated through stratified sampling and repeated runs, broader datasets would strengthen generalizability. Future research may extend the difficulty formulation, explore multi-task settings and evaluate operational deployment within emergency-response systems.
