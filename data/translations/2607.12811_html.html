摘要Abstract

尽管拓扑映射和导航已被广泛研究，但环路闭合在纯拓扑表示中的具体作用和下游效应受到的关注相对较少。重要的是，拓扑图上的环路闭合与全局参考轨迹和度量图上的环路闭合不同。基于最近基于像素级、相对 3D 几何的更密集拓扑，我们提出Although topological mapping and navigation have been studied extensively, the specific role and downstream effect of loop closures in purely topological representations has received relatively little attention. Importantly, loop closure over topological maps is distinct from loop closure over globally referenced trajectories and metric maps. Building on recent denser topologies grounded in pixel-level, relative 3D geometry, we propose像素循环PixelLoop它直接在像素空间中引入了循环闭包。与 SLAM 中的稀疏图像级边缘或姿态图校正不同，我们的像素级闭包充当密集拓扑捷径，改变规划连接和成本传播，而不仅仅是对齐坐标。这种密集的连接性可实现稳定的任意点到任意点的导航，并生成与几何最短路径精确对齐的成本图。特别是，我们展示了将闭环应用于细粒度像素拓扑而不是图像级拓扑的明显优势。在广泛的模拟实验中，与图像相关基线相比，PixelLoop 在成功率和 SPL 方面实现了超过 35% 的绝对改进，其中在需要快捷方式利用的场景中收益最大。通过现实世界的移动机器人部署进一步验证了结果，表明密集​​的像素级闭环为拓扑视觉导航提供了实用且强大的基础。which introduces loop closures directly in pixel space. Unlike sparse image-level edges or pose-graph corrections in SLAM, our pixel-level closures act as dense topological shortcuts that alter planning connectivity and cost propagation rather than merely aligning coordinates. This dense connectivity enables stable any-point-to-any-point navigation and produces costmaps that align accurately with geometric shortest paths. In particular, we showcase the distinct advantage of applying loop closures to fine-grained pixel topologies rather than image-level topologies. Across extensive simulated experiments, PixelLoop achieves over 35% absolute improvement in both Success Rate and SPL compared to image-relative baselines, with the largest gains in scenarios requiring shortcut exploitation. Results are further validated through real-world mobile robot deployments, demonstrating that dense pixel-level loop closures provide a practical and robust foundation for topological visual navigation.

V 结论CONCLUSION

在这项工作中，我们介绍了In this work, we introduced像素循环PixelLoop，证明稳健拓扑导航的关键不仅在于检测闭环，还在于以密集的几何保真度执行它们。通过直接在像素空间中操作，我们的框架克服了严重限制离散图像相关规划器的信息瓶颈。我们表明，通过零成本像素对应缝合不相交的轨迹从根本上改变了底层的规划几何形状，使我们能够生成连续的、细粒度的成本图。, demonstrating that the key to robust topological navigation lies not merely in detecting loop closures, but in executing them with dense geometric fidelity. By operating directly in pixel space, our framework overcomes the information bottlenecks that severely limit discrete image-relative planners. We showed that stitching disjoint trajectories via zero-cost pixel correspondences fundamentally alters the underlying planning geometry, allowing us to generate continuous, fine-grained costmaps.

我们的实证评估证实，这种结构转变使局部神经控制器能够可靠地发现和利用新颖的物理捷径。通过在任意开始到目标导航中实现最先进的性能，而无需全局度量 SLAM 的开销，我们验证了密集拓扑缩放的优越性。至关重要的是，现实世界中移动机器人的成功部署凸显了我们方法的稳健性和实际可行性。Our empirical evaluations confirm that this structural shift empowers a local neural controller to reliably discover and exploit novel physical shortcuts. By achieving state-of-the-art performance in arbitrary start-to-goal navigation without the overhead of global metric SLAM, we validate the superiority of dense topological scaling. Crucially, successful real-world mobile robot deployments highlight the robustness and practical viability of our approach.
