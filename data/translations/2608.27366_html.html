Stageboost：基于反事实估计推荐信号Stageboost: Recommending Signals based on Counterfactual Estimations

会议：Conference:第 20 届 ACM 推荐系统会议、研讨会； 2026年9月28日-10月2日；美国明尼阿波利斯20th ACM Conference on Recommender Systems, Workshops; September 28-October 2, 2026; Minneapolis, USA
Darpan Singhal1, Matan Mandelbrod2, Tal Franji2, Manasa Kolla1, Vipul Gaba1, Yuri Brovman3 email: dsinghal, mmandelbrod, tfranji, manak, vgaba, ybrovman@ebay.com Affiliation: 1eBay, 1India, 2eBay, 2Israel, 3eBay, 3USA

摘要。Abstract.

信号是 eBay 查看项目 (VI) 页面上显示的简短文本或视觉片段，为用户提供有关所查看项目的附加上下文信息。显示这些信号的目的是促进智能购买并激励参与。在本文中，我们提出了一个基于 2 阶段 xgboost 的模型，该模型可以用信号最佳地填充 VI 页面。这种方法显示，总体 GMB（商品购买总额）增长了 0.08%，零部件 GMB 增长了 0.58%，这主要是由于在线实验中高平均价格商品的转化率增加。Signals are short textual or visual snippets displayed on the eBay View-Item (VI) page, providing additional, contextual information for users about the viewed item. The aim of displaying these signals is to facilitate intelligent purchase and to incentivize engagement. In this paper, we present a 2 stage xgboost based model that optimally populates the VI page with signals. This approach has shown a 0.08% lift in overall GMB (Gross Merchandise Bought) and 0.58% increase in Parts and Accessories GMB, primarily due to increase in conversion of high average price items in online experimentation.

1. 简介Introduction

查看商品 (VI) 页面是 eBay 客户旅程中最关键的接触点之一，每天都会收到数亿次浏览。该页面作为买家的主要信息中心，显示全面的商品详细信息，包括图像、价格、描述、卖家信息和运输选项。除了这些核心元素之外，VI 页面还具有简短的上下文文本提示（称为信号），这些提示战略性地放置在预定义的位置上，以指导购买决策并鼓励用户参与。The View Item (VI) page is one of the most critical touch points in eBay’s customer journey, receiving hundreds of millions of daily views. This page serves as the primary information hub for buyers, displaying comprehensive item details including images, price, descriptions, seller information, and shipping options. In addition to these core elements, the VI page features brief, contextual text cues known as signals that are strategically positioned across predefined placements to guide purchase decisions and encourage user engagement.

每个展示位置都按主题组织，并显示一组与其目的相符的精选候选信号。例如，移动设备图像面板上显着位置的“紧急”位置会显示突出时间敏感或与稀缺性相关细节的信号，例如“最后一件可用”或“过去 24 小时内售出 5 件”。同样，对话式展示位置也包含与商品优势和可信度相关的信号，例如“免费送货”或“接受 30 天退货”。Each placement is thematically organized and displays a curated set of candidate signals aligned with its purpose. For instance, the Urgency placement which is prominently positioned over the image panel on mobile devices shows signals that highlight time-sensitive or scarcity-related details, such as “Last one available” or “5 sold in the last 24 hours”. Similarly, the Conversational placement features signals related to item benefits and trustworthiness, such as "Free shipping" or "30-day returns accepted".Figure 1通过显示信号的示例（用橙色虚线标记）说明了 VI 移动体验的放置方案。illustrates the placement scheme on the VI mobile experience with examples of displayed signals (marked with orange dashed lines).

在我们之前的工作中，我们开发了一个转换似然估计器（CLE）In our prior work, we developed a Conversion Likelihood Estimator (CLE)(Mandelbrod et al., 2025)跨两个主要放置的最佳信号分配模型，即紧急放置（最多显示 1 个信号）和对话放置（同时显示最多 2 个信号）。 CLE 模型在两个布局中输出总共 3 个信号的分配元组，并在最新的 A/B 测试中产生了显着的 GMB 提升。然而，这种方法有一个关键的限制，它没有结合用户偏好或详细的物品特征，导致只要一组特定的信号符合条件，就会产生确定性的信号分配。model for optimal signal assignment across two primary placements, the Urgency placement (displaying up to 1 signal) and the Conversational placement (displaying up to 2 signals simultaneously). The CLE model outputs an assignment tuple of 3 signals total across both placements and has generated significant GMB uplift in the latest A/B test. However, this approach has a critical limitation, it does not incorporate user preferences or detailed item characteristics, resulting in deterministic signal assignments whenever a specific set of signals qualify.

为了解决这一限制，我们引入了 Stageboost，这是一种基于 XGBoost 的新型两级方法，除了显式建模转换提升之外，还可以实现个性化信号分配。在第一阶段 (M1)，模型学习使用商品属性（价格、类别、卖家信誉）和用户特征（平台、垂直行业、买家细分）等上下文特征来预测给定商品-用户对的基线转换概率。在第二阶段 (M2)，模型学习估计由于显示每个特定信号而导致的转换概率的增量提升，从而有效地学习哪个信号在基线预测之上提供最大边际值。To address this limitation, we introduce Stageboost, a novel two-stage XGBoost-based approach that enables personalized signal assignment in addition to explicitly modeling conversion uplift. In the first stage (M1), the model learns to predict the baseline probability of conversion for a given item-user pair using contextual features such as item attributes (price, category, seller reputation) and user characteristics (platform, vertical, buyer segment). In the second stage (M2), the model learns to estimate the incremental uplift in conversion probability attributable to showing each specific signal, effectively learning which signal provides maximum marginal value on top of the baseline prediction.

1.1. 相关工作Related Work

在我们之前的工作中，我们开发了转换似然估计器 (CLE)In our prior work, we developed the Conversion Likelihood Estimator (CLE)(Mandelbrod et al., 2025)作为第一个用于 VI 信号分配的机器学习方法。 CLE 方法直接计算每个资格组内的信号转换率，并根据这些经验率对信号进行排名，前提是它们的差异（代表提升）具有统计显着性。例如：基于受控随机信号分配，如果我们发现当“购买 n 次”和“在 n 辆购物车”符合条件时，并且“购买 n 次”的转化率较高，则这意味着对于 <“购买 n 次”和“在 n 辆购物车”的资格集，CLE 将始终推荐“购买 n 次”。as the first machine learning approach for VI signals assignment. The CLE approach directly calculates signal conversion rates within each qualification set and ranks signals based on these empirical rates, provided their differences (representing uplift) are statistically significant. For example: Based on controlled random signal assignments, if we see that when "Purchased n times" and "In n carts" qualifies and we have a higher conversion for "Purchased n times", then this means for qualification set of <"Purchased n times" and "In n carts">, CLE will always recommend "Purchased n times".

虽然 CLE 取得了令人印象深刻的成果（GMB 增长了 0.28%），但它有根本性的局限性。首先，它不提供个性化：无论项目特征、用户偏好或上下文因素如何，相同的资格集始终接收相同的信号分配。其次，它存在稀有资格元组的数据稀疏性，因为每个元组都需要足够的历史观察来建立统计上显着的转化率差异。这些限制促使 Stageboost 的开发，它明确地模拟了项目/用户特征和信号有效性之间的关系。While the CLE achieved impressive results (0.28% increase in GMB), it has fundamental limitations. First, it provides no personalization: identical qualification sets always receive identical signal assignments regardless of item characteristics, user preferences, or contextual factors. Second, it suffers from data sparsity for rare qualification tuples, as each tuple requires sufficient historical observations to establish statistically significant conversion rate differences. These limitations motivated the development of Stageboost, which explicitly models the relationship between item/user features and signal effectiveness.

3. 方法论Methodology

3.1. 概述Overview

Stageboost 是一种两阶段梯度增强方法，旨在通过显式建模基本转换概率和信号特定提升来个性化 VI 信号分配。该模型由在单个 XGBoost 整体中训练的两个不同阶段组成。 M1（基线转换模型）学习在缺乏信号特定信息的情况下预测给定项目-用户对的基线转换概率。 M2 学会估计由于显示每个特定信号而导致的转换概率的增量提升。Stageboost is a two-stage gradient boosting approach designed to personalize VI signal assignment by explicitly modeling both base conversion probability and signal-specific uplift. The model consists of two distinct stages trained within a single XGBoost ensemble. M1 (Baseline Conversion Model) learns to predict the baseline probability of conversion for a given item-user pair in the absence of signal-specific information. M2 learns to estimate the incremental uplift in conversion probability attributable to displaying each specific signal.

3.2. 数据准备Data Preparation

Stageboost 的训练数据是通过在 eBay 的生产 VI 页面上进行的随机对照试验 (RCT) 收集的。与可能受到选择偏差和混杂影响的纯粹观察数据收集不同，随机对照试验通过将 VI 印象随机分配给治疗组，其中根据不同的分配策略显示信号，从而提供信号有效性的无混杂实验证据。随机化发生在资格集中，例如，如果信号“在 x 辆购物车中”、“购买 y 次”、“查看 z 次”符合给定的印象，则治疗组随机选择要以统一概率显示的信号。这确保了无论项目/用户特征如何，每个合格信号都有机会被分配，从而消除选择偏差并在所有合格信号之间提供平衡的训练数据。我们无法使用 CLE 生产数据来训练 stageboost，因为它会引入多种偏差，从而阻止模型学习有效的个性化。对于给定的资格集Training data for Stageboost is collected through Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs) conducted on eBay’s production VI page. Unlike purely observational data collection, which can suffer from selection bias and confounding, RCTs provide unconfounded experimental evidence of signal effectiveness by randomly assigning VI impressions to treatment groups where signals are displayed according to different assignment strategies. Randomization occurs within qualification sets, for example if signals "In x Carts", "Purchased y times", "Viewed z times" qualify for a given impression, the treatment group randomly selects which signal(s) to show with uniform probability. This ensures that every qualified signal has a chance of being assigned regardless of item/user features, eliminating selection bias and providing balanced training data across all qualified signals. 3.3. 培训Training

Stageboost 采用经过专门技术训练的单个 XGBoost 分类器来平衡基本转换预测 (M1) 和信号特定提升估计 (M2) 的双重目标。训练过程利用差异特征加权和策略特征子采样，以确保模型在统一的梯度提升框架内有效地学习这两个阶段。Stageboost employs a single XGBoost classifier trained with specialized techniques to balance the dual objectives of base conversion prediction (M1) and signal-specific uplift estimation (M2). The training process leverages differential feature weighting and strategic feature subsampling to ensure the model learns both stages effectively within a unified gradient boosting framework.

我们没有为 M1 和 M2 训练单独的模型，而是在完整的特征集（包括项目/用户特征和信号分配指标）上训练单个 XGBoost 分类器。关键的创新是使用特征重要性权重来控制哪些特征在不同的提升阶段主导学习。具体来说，我们构建了一个特征权重向量，其中信号分配指示符特征（例如，显示信号的二进制指示符）接收显着更高的重要性权重，而所有其他特征（项目特征、用户上下文、参与度指标）接收大幅减少的权重。信号指标和上下文特征之间的这种极端差异权重确保早期的提升回合主要从项目/用户特征（M1 行为）中学习基础转换模式，而后期的回合几乎完全专注于信号特定的提升模式（M2 行为）。 XGBoost 的特征重要性加权机制在计算分割增益时应用这些权重，一旦建立 M1 模式，就可以有效地使上下文特征对分割的吸引力低于信号指标。此外，我们应用由 delta 参数控制的每个节点的特征子采样，它将 XGBoost 的每节点列采样参数设置为 min(1.0, (K + D) / M)，其中 K 是信号指示器的数量，M 是特征的总数。较小的 D (delta) 值会导致激进的特征二次采样，并控制 M1 中出现在分割中的非信号特征的数量。Rather than training separate models for M1 and M2, we train a single XGBoost classifier on the full feature set including both item/user features and signal assignment indicators. The key innovation is the use of feature importance weights to control which features dominate learning at different stages of boosting. Specifically, we construct a feature weight vector where signal assignment indicator features (e.g., binary indicators for which signal was shown) receive significantly higher importance weights, while all other features (item characteristics, user context, engagement metrics) receive substantially reduced weights. This extreme differential weighting between signal indicators and context features—ensures that early boosting rounds primarily learn base conversion patterns from item/user features (M1 behavior), while later rounds focus almost exclusively on signal-specific uplift patterns (M2 behavior). XGBoost’s feature importance weighting mechanism applies these weights when computing split gain, effectively making context features orders of magnitude less attractive for splitting than signal indicators once M1 patterns are established. Additionally, we apply feature subsampling per node controlled by a delta parameter, which sets XGBoost’s per-node column sampling parameter to min(1.0, (K + D) / M) where K is the number of signal indicators and M is the total number of features. Smaller D (delta) values result in aggressive feature subsampling and controls the number of non signal features from M1 that will appear in a split.

为了确保 M1 的基本转换预测保持稳定和高质量，我们采用由配置标志控制的顺序训练。在第一阶段，我们仅使用项目和用户特征（不包括显示的信号指标）来训练 M1。在第二阶段，我们通过使用预训练的 M1 模型初始化 M2 并拟合包括原始上下文特征和信号分配指标的扩展特征集，继续从 M1 的检查点进行训练。典型的 M2 超参数与 M1 不同：较低的学习率用于更仔细的细化，增加最大深度以捕获信号上下文交互，以及上述的特征加权和子采样方案。To ensure M1’s base conversion predictions remain stable and high-quality, we employ sequential training controlled by a configuration flag. In the first phase, we train M1 using only item and user features (excluding shown signal indicators). In the second phase, we continue training from M1’s checkpoint by initializing M2 with the pretrained M1 model and fitting on the expanded feature set that includes both the original context features and signal assignment indicators. Typical M2 hyperparameters differ from M1: a lower learning rate for more careful refinement, increased maximum depth to capture signal-context interactions, and the feature weighting and subsampling schemes described above.

3.4. 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

在进行昂贵的在线实验之前，离线评估指标对于比较模型变体和调整超参数至关重要。然而，AUC、精度和 F1 分数等标准分类指标被证明不足以满足信号分配任务的需要。标准指标可能会产生误导，模型可能会实现良好的分类准确性，同时通过始终将较低转换信号排在较高转换信号之上而产生负面提升。由于信号具有几乎相同的转换率，因此传统指标无法区分做出良好信号分配决策的模型与系统地选择次优信号的模型。Offline evaluation metrics are essential for comparing model variants and tuning hyperparameters prior to costly online experimentation. However, standard classification metrics like AUC, precision, and F1-score prove inadequate for the signal assignment task. Standard metrics can be misleading, a model may achieve good classification accuracy while producing negative uplift by consistently ranking lower-converting signals above higher-converting alternatives. Since signals have nearly identical conversion rates, traditional metrics cannot distinguish between a model that makes good signal assignment decisions versus one that systematically chooses suboptimal signals.

4. 实证结果Empirical Results

我们评估了 StageBoost 框架的两个变体，它们所使用的预测器数量不同。 Lean StageBoost 变体遵循与 StageBoost 相同的架构设计，但使用减少的功能集来满足生产延迟预算。相比之下，完整的 StageBoost 模型包含了更大的功能集，并实现了更高的估计提升，尽管代价是增加了推理延迟。考虑到严格的服务延迟要求，选择Lean StageBoost进行在线部署。We evaluate two variants of the StageBoost framework that differ in the number of predictors used. The Lean StageBoost variant follows the same architectural design as StageBoost but uses a reduced feature set to satisfy the production latency budget. In contrast, the full StageBoost model incorporates a larger feature set and achieves higher estimated uplift, albeit at the cost of increased inference latency. Given the strict serving latency requirements, Lean StageBoost was selected for online deployment.

为了验证线下收益，我们使用 Lean StageBoost 进行了大规模的在线 A/B 实验，同时涵盖了紧急和对话展示位置。每个位置都是独立处理的，对于精益 stageboost 变体，根据合格候选集中的转换概率选择前 3 个信号（1 个紧急信号和 2 个对话信号）。在整个市场层面，该实验导致商品购买总额 (GMB)** 增加了 **0.08%，尽管这在统计上并不显着（基于 p 值）。在这项工作的主要焦点类别之一中观察到了更明显的改进。在此类别中，所提出的方法在促进高价值购买方面特别有效，导致 GMB 增长 0.58%，平均售价 (ASP) 增长 0.41%，这表明优化的投放策略帮助用户发现和购买更高价值的商品。To validate the offline gains, we conducted a large scale online A/B experiment using Lean StageBoost, covering both Urgency and Conversational placements simultaneously. Each placement was treated independently and for the lean stageboost variant, the top 3 signals (1 urgency and 2 conversational) were selected based on conversion probability from the qualified candidate set. At the overall marketplace level, the experiment resulted in a **0.08% increase in Gross Merchandise Bought (GMB)** , although it was not statistically significant (based on p-value). More pronounced improvements were observed in one of the primary focus categories for this work. In this category, the proposed approach was particularly effective at promoting higher-value purchases, resulting in a 0.58% increase in GMB and 0.41% increase in Average Selling Price (ASP), indicating that the optimized placement strategy helped users discover and purchase higher-value items.

基于这些积极的在线结果，拟议的排名系统已投入生产。为了确保仅在存在有意义的选择时才显示信号，仅当合格信号的数量超过可用放置位置的数量时才会调用模型。Based on these positive online results, the proposed ranking system has been launched in production. To ensure that signals are displayed only when meaningful choices exist, the model is invoked only when the number of eligible signals exceeds the number of available placement positions.

5. 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

在本文中，我们介绍了 Stageboost，这是一种基于 XGBoost 的新型两阶段方法，用于在 eBay 的查看项目页面上进行个性化信号分配。与之前提供确定性分配的 CLE 模型不同，Stageboost 通过具有差异特征权重的统一梯度提升框架显式建模基本转换概率和信号特定提升。这使得个性化信号推荐能够适应项目特征和用户上下文。In this paper, we introduced Stageboost, a novel two-stage XGBoost-based approach for personalized signal assignment on eBay’s View-Item page. Unlike the previous CLE model which provided deterministic assignments, Stageboost explicitly models both base conversion probability and signal-specific uplift through a unified gradient boosting framework with differential feature weighting. This enables personalized signal recommendations that adapt to item characteristics and user context.

我们的方法解决了该问题固有的几个关键技术挑战：极小的隆升幅度（0.0001）、稀有资格集中的数据严重稀疏以及信号之间的高特征相关性。通过随机对照试验而不是观察方法收集训练数据，我们确保通过随机探索对所有合格信号进行无偏差的训练信号覆盖。事实证明，基于反事实转化率估计的专门离线评估指标对于模型选择至关重要，而传统的分类指标无法区分具有显着不同业务影响的模型。考虑到严格的生产延迟限制和基于在线实验的观察，我们在生产中采用了 Lean Stageboost 模型。我们承认，针对参与度进行优化的个性化紧迫性和稀缺性信号可能会影响用户的自主权。为了解决这个问题，我们监控信号有效性、买家细分层面的结果和购买后指标，作为我们持续生产评估的一部分。Our approach addresses several critical technical challenges inherent to this problem: extremely small uplift magnitudes (0.0001), severe data sparsity in rare qualification sets, and high feature correlation among signals. By collecting training data through RCTs rather than observational methods, we ensure unbiased training signal coverage across all qualified signals through randomized exploration. The specialized offline evaluation metric based on counterfactual conversion rate estimation proved essential for model selection, where traditional classification metrics failed to distinguish between models with significantly different business impact. Given strict production latency constraints and observations based on online experimentation, we have Lean Stageboost model in production. We acknowledge that personalized urgency and scarcity signals optimized for engagement may influence user autonomy. To address this, we monitor signal validity, buyer-segment-level outcomes, and post-purchase indicators as part of our ongoing production evaluation.

未来的工作包括探索在线适应的强化学习方法、整合额外的用户功能以及将框架扩展到 VI 页面上的其他位置。Future work includes exploring reinforcement learning approaches for online adaptation, incorporating additional user features, and extending the framework to additional placements on the VI page.
