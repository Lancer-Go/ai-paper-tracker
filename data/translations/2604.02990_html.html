联邦调查问卷FedSQ:

摘要Abstract

联邦学习 (FL) 可以在不共享原始数据的情况下实现跨组织的协作训练，但它受到统计异质性（非独立同分布客户数据）和客户漂移下朴素权重平均的不稳定性的阻碍。在许多跨孤岛部署中，FL 从强大的预训练主干（例如 ImageNet-1K）热启动，然后适应本地域。最近有证据表明类似 ReLU 的门控机制（结构知识）比其余参数值（定量知识）更早稳定，我们提出Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative training across organizations without sharing raw data, but it is hindered by statistical heterogeneity (non-i.i.d. client data) and by instability of naive weight averaging under client drift. In many cross-silo deployments, FL is warm-started from a strong pretrained backbone (e.g., ImageNet-1K) and then adapted to local domains. Motivated by recent evidence that ReLU-like gating regimes (structural knowledge) stabilize earlier than the remaining parameter values (quantitative knowledge), we propose联邦调查问卷FedSQ（联合结构定量学习），一种基于深度网络的 DualCopy、分段线性视图的转移初始化神经联合过程。(Federated Structural-Quantitative learning), a transfer-initialized neural federated procedure based on a DualCopy, piecewise-linear view of deep networks.联邦调查问卷FedSQ冻结预训练模型的结构副本，以在联合微调期间引入固定的二进制门控掩模，而仅对定量副本进行本地优化并跨轮聚合。修复门控可以减少对区域内仿射细化的学习，从而稳定异构分区下的聚合。 i.i.d. 下两个卷积神经网络主干的实验和狄利克雷分裂表明freezes a structural copy of the pretrained model to induce fixed binary gating masks during federated fine-tuning, while only a quantitative copy is optimized locally and aggregated across rounds. Fixing the gating reduces learning to within-regime affine refinements, which stabilizes aggregation under heterogeneous partitions. Experiments on two convolutional neural network backbones under i.i.d. and Dirichlet splits show that联邦调查问卷FedSQ提高稳健性，并可以减少相对于标准基线的最佳验证性能轮次，同时保持传输设置的准确性。improves robustness and can reduce rounds-to-best validation performance relative to standard baselines while preserving accuracy in the transfer setting.

In many real-world scenarios, collecting such data in a centralized repository is impractical or undesirable due to privacy, legal, or operational constraints. V 结论和未来工作Conclusion and future work

我们介绍了We introduced联邦调查问卷FedSQ，由之间的解耦驱动的联邦训练程序, a federated training procedure motivated by the decoupling betweenSK和andQK。在. In联邦调查问卷FedSQ，一个冻结的结构组件会产生二元门控掩模，并且联邦阶段专注于仅聚合定量副本，在 DualCopy 分段线性视图下有效地学习区域内仿射细化。跨越两个, a frozen structural component induces binary gating masks, and the federated stage focuses on aggregating only the quantitative copy, effectively learning within-regime affine refinements under a DualCopy piecewise-linear view. Across two美国有线电视新闻网CNN架构和异构客户端分区，我们的结果表明，这种设计可以提高非独立同分布下的收敛行为和鲁棒性。分割，并且与标准基线相比可以达到有竞争力的（在某些情况下更高的）验证准确性。architectures and heterogeneous client partitions, our results show that this design can improve convergence behavior and robustness under non-i.i.d. splits, and can reach competitive (and in some cases superior) validation accuracy compared to standard baselines.

除了经验收益之外，Beyond the empirical gains,联邦调查问卷FedSQ提供了一个实用的方法来明确地利用门控provides a practical handle to exploit gating explicitly inFL，与以优化器为中心或以个性化为中心的方法相比，这种方法受到的关注相对较少。这开启了几条研究方向。首先，一旦结构组件固定，定量更新可以在不牺牲性能的情况下允许额外的压缩，例如通过低秩或稀疏更新，从而降低通信成本。其次，解耦提出了自适应系统策略：更少的本地 epoch、每轮参与的客户端更少，或者在结构组件稳定后降低通信频率，以受控方式用计算换取通信。第三，虽然本文重点关注转移初始化机制，但下一步重要的是研究算法中的替代初始化路径, which has received comparatively less attention than optimizer-centric or personalization-centric approaches. This opens several lines of research. First, once the structural component is fixed, the quantitative updates may admit additional compression without sacrificing performance, for instance via low-rank or sparsified updates, reducing communication costs. Second, the decoupling suggests adaptive system policies: fewer local epochs, fewer participating clients per round, or reduced communication frequency after the structural component has stabilized, trading compute for communication in a controlled manner. Third, while this paper focuses on a transfer-initialized regime, an important next step is to study an alternative initialization pathway in Algorithm1：学习结构知识: learning structural knowledge从头开始from scratch通过对校准集进行简短的集中训练阶段，一旦成熟就将其冻结，然后继续进行联合via a short centralized training phase on a calibration set, freezing it once mature, and then proceeding with federatedQK- 仅限细化。最后，-only refinement. Finally,联邦调查问卷FedSQ看起来与异步或部分可靠的联合设置自然兼容，在这些设置中，客户端可能会间歇性地加入，这导致了一项有前途的工作，其中提前修复门控结构可以减少由于漂移而导致的不稳定性，使后期聚合更容易稳定。appears naturally compatible with asynchronous or partially reliable federated settings, where clients may join intermittently, which lead to a promising line of work, where fixing the gating structure in advance can reduce instability due to drift, making late-stage aggregation easier to stabilize.
