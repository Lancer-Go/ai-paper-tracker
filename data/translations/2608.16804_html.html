Translation between sign languages and spoken languages is crucial for facilitating communication between sign language users and those who do not use sign language. However, Sign Language Recognition (SLR) is not as extensively researched as other types of action recognition, and SLR studies tend to focus on only a few of the 135 sign languages used worldwide due to the lack of high-resource datasets for most of these sign languages. It is common knowledge that low-resource datasets are prone to overfitting in classification or recognition tasks. Our solution to the low-resource dataset problem for SLR is cross-sign language transfer learning, where we transfer knowledge from higher-resource sign language datasets. This method is domain-specific and differs from state-of-the-art SLR methods that use non-sign language-specific datasets, such as ImageNet. By applying sign language to sign language transfer learning, we can improve SLR by learning high-level, specific visual features that cannot be provided by general large-scale non-sign language datasets, which are limited to low-level, superficial features.

2.2 领域适应的相关工作Related Works on Domain Adaptation

领域适应是迁移学习的一种，旨在泛化在一个领域训练的模型，使其在另一个领域表现良好。这是必要的，因为由于域转移，在一个域中训练的模型可能无法在另一域中很好地工作。当源域和目标域具有不同的概率分布时，即使它们共享相同的任务，也会发生域转移。为了解决这个问题，域适应调整目标数据的分布以匹配源数据的分布。Domain Adaptation is a type of Transfer Learning that aims to generalize a model trained in one domain to perform well in another domain. This is necessary because models trained in one domain may not work well in another domain due to domain shift. Domain shift occurs when the source and target domains have different probability distributions, even though they share the same task. To address this issue, domain adaptation adjusts the target data’s distribution to match that of the source data.

虽然基于神经网络的迁移学习和领域适应都可以帮助网络学习低资源环境的判别性特征，但它们的执行情况有所不同。基于神经网络的迁移学习在目标任务的网络中重用源任务的特征和权重。另一方面，域适应尝试通过重新加权源特征来减少两个不同域之间的转移。While both neural network-based transfer learning and domain adaptation can help networks learn discriminative features for low-resource settings, their execution differs. Neural network-based transfer learning reuses the source task’s features and weights in the target task’s network. Domain Adaptation, on the other hand, attempts to reduce shift between two different domains by re-weighting source features.

与视频分类任务类似，手语分类可能会遇到空间和时间方面的域差异。因此，需要领域适应方法，特别是视频识别和分类。目前，将领域适应应用于视频分类的研究很少Similar to video classification tasks, Sign Language classification can suffer from domain discrepancy in both spatial and temporal aspects. Hence, there is a need for domain adaptation approaches, specifically for video recognition and classification. Currently, there are only a few studies that apply Domain Adaptation to video classification26; 30; 10; 20。仅时间注意力对抗适应网络（TA3N）. 5 结论Conclusion

我们对不同手语之间的迁移学习方法的研究为如何通过以多时间尺度的方式将源手语的时空特征与目标手语的时空特征对齐来改进资源匮乏条件下的分类和识别提供了见解。据我们所知，我们是第一个在RGB和光流模式下研究LSA（阿根廷手语）到ASL（美国手语）和CSL（中国手语）到ASL之间的知识适应性的人。我们也是第一个在手语之间进行几次领域适应的人。在 SLR 中应用少样本学习对于推进该领域并将其置于其他领域（例如计算语言学、自然语言处理和其他动作识别领域）的水平非常重要，在这些领域中，许多研究都是针对少样本学习的应用进行的。Our research on transfer learning approaches between different sign languages provided insights on how to improve classification and recognition in low-resource conditions by aligning the source sign language’s spatiotemporal features with the target sign language’s in a multiple-timescale fashion. From our best knowledge, we are the first to study the knowledge adaptability between LSA (Argentine Sign Language) to ASL (American Sign Language) and CSL (Chinese Sign Language) to ASL in both RGB and optical flow modes. We are also the first to conduct a few-shot domain adaptation between sign languages. Applying few-shot learning in SLR is important to advance the field and place it at the levels of other fields such as computational linguistics, natural language processing, and other action recognition areas where many studies are being conducted on the application of few-shot learning.

我们的研究得出的经验证据表明，LSA 和 ASL、CSL 和 ASL 之间的多尺度时域适应的表现明显优于预训练 ASL 和 CSL 和 LSA。预训练方法产生的负迁移多于正迁移。我们还能够证明我们的假设，即对齐短期多尺度 TRN（如 3、5 或 7）比对齐长期多尺度 TRN（如 10 或 15）更有利于这些手语之间的域适应。该假设适用于我们所有的实验设置，例如全尺度和少样本域适应，以及 RGB 和光流输入模式（参见第 1 节）The empirical evidence resulting from our study showed that multi-scale temporal Domain Adaptation between LSA and ASL, and CSL and ASL performed significantly better than pre-training ASL with CSL and LSA. The pre-training approach yielded more negative transfers than positive ones. We were also able to prove our hypothesis that aligning shorter-term multiscale TRNs such as 3, 5, or 7 would be more beneficial for the domain adaptation between these sign languages than aligning longer-term multi-scale TRNs such as 10 or 15. This hypothesis holds for all of our experiment settings, such as full-scale and few-shot domain adaptation, and RGB and optical flow input modes (see Section4.2）。这对于 SLR 和动作识别领域来说是一个好消息，因为训练长期的多尺度 TRN 会消耗更多的 GPU 资源和时间来完成。此外，我们认为，只有在源域和目标域的相似性多于差异性的情况下，长期多尺度 TRN 才会优于短期多尺度 TRN。). This is a piece of great news for the fields of SLR and action recognition in general as training longer-term multi-scale TRNs consume more GPU resources and time to complete. Moreover, we believe that longer-term multi-scale TRNs will only outperform shorter-term ones in cases where the source and target domains bear more similarities than differences.

我们的研究还证明了我们的主要假设，即将一种手语领域适应另一种手语领域（即使它们不来自同一手语族）会比根本不进行任何领域适应或仅使用另一种手语或通用大型数据集（例如 ImageNet）对目标领域进行预训练更好的 SLR 模型。Our study also proved our main hypothesis that adapting the domain of one sign language to another - even if they are not from the same sign language family would make a better SLR model than not doing any domain adaptation at all or only pre-training the target domain with another sign language or with a generic large-scale dataset such as ImageNet.

尽管我们的实验表明 RGB 的表现优于光流（参见第Although our experiment showed that RGB performed better than optical flow (see Section4.2），我们相信我们的研究为光流在单反领域的应用开启了更多的可能性。未来，我们计划进行研究，旨在发现此类运动表示的更多潜力。), we believe that our study opened more possibilities for the application of optical flow in the field of SLR. In the future, we plan to conduct studies that aim to discover more potential in this type of motion representation.

最后，我们预计我们的研究将成为将手语手势实时翻译成单词或短语或转录手语视频的宝贵资源。然而，为了有效地对手语进行建模，由于口语和手语之间的语法结构的差异，必须利用语言建模技术。尽管如此，这些语法差异可以通过领域适应来解决。一个例子是将具有口语转录和注释级别注释的句子级手语数据集调整为仅具有注释注释的数据集，以自动将注释级别注释转换为转录。在数据集中找到相似的注释注释是一项重大挑战。或者，可以在未来的工作中探索将带有转录的非手语视频改编为带有注释级注释的句子级手语视频。Lastly, we envision that our study will be a valuable resource in the real-time translation of sign gestures into words or phrases, or in transcribing videos that feature sign languages. However, to effectively model sign languages, a language modeling technique must be utilized due to differences in grammar structure between spoken languages and sign languages. Nonetheless, these grammar differences can be addressed through domain adaptation. One example is adapting from a sentence-level sign language dataset with spoken language transcription and gloss-level annotation to a dataset with only gloss annotation, to automatically convert gloss-level annotations into transcriptions. Finding similar gloss annotations across datasets is a significant challenge. Alternatively, adapting a non-sign language video with transcriptions to sentence-level sign language videos with gloss-level annotations may be explored in future work.
