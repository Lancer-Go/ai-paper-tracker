规模可以使我们免于大型语言模型的可塑性损失吗？Can Scale Save Us From Plasticity Loss in Large Language Models?

J. Fernando Hernandez-Garcia*, Tomas Figliolia*, Beren Millidge
Zyphra
San Francisco, CA
摘要Abstract

可塑性的丧失——网络在学习旧信息后学习新信息的能力——是创建能够持续学习的人工神经网络的基本挑战。尽管这种现象几十年前就已为人所知，但它主要是在较旧的、相对较小的体系结构中进行研究，很少在自然语言领域中进行研究。为了确定可塑性损失是否仍然是现代基于 Transformer 的 LLM 范式中的一个问题，我们研究了在多语言持续学习问题上训练的 GPT 式 Transformer 模型中的可塑性损失。与之前的工作一致，我们发现了从 5M 到 314M 非嵌入参数范围内的模型可塑性损失的证据，这是通过越南探测任务的恶化来衡量的。我们进一步发现，可塑性损失的发生遵循可预测的缩放定律，随着模型尺寸的增加呈次线性增长。 这些结果表明，较大的模型可能会延迟塑性损失的可测量影响，但仅增加参数数量可能不足以完全防止它。我们还发现了固定多语言训练下可塑性损失的证据，挑战了这种现象是任务突然变化的持续学习所独有的观点。 总的来说，我们的结果表明，即使是在自然语言上训练的大型 Transformer 语言模型，在连续和静态环境中经过足够长的训练后，最终也会失去有效适应新数据的能力。The loss of plasticity – the ability of a network to learn new information after having already learned older information – is a fundamental challenge in creating artificial neural networks capable of continual learning. Although this phenomenon has been known for decades, it has mostly been studied in older, relatively small architectures and rarely in natural-language domains. To determine whether loss of plasticity remains a problem in the modern transformer-based LLM paradigm, we study plasticity loss in GPT-style Transformer models trained on a multilingual continual learning problem. Consistent with prior work, we find evidence of plasticity loss across models ranging from 5M to 314M non-embedding parameters, as measured by deterioration on a held-out Vietnamese probing task. We further find that the onset of plasticity loss follows a predictable scaling law, growing sublinearly with model size. These results suggest that larger models may delay the measurable effects of plasticity loss, but that increasing parameter count alone is likely to be insufficient to completely prevent it. We also find evidence of plasticity loss under stationary multilingual training, challenging the view that the phenomenon is exclusive to continual learning with abrupt task changes. Overall, our results suggest that even large Transformer language models trained on natural-language will eventually lose the ability to efficiently adapt to new data after sufficiently long training, in both continual and stationary settings.

I 简介Introduction

长时间接受不断变化的数据训练的神经网络可能会逐渐失去从新观察中学习的能力，这种现象称为Neural networks trained for extended periods on changing data can gradually lose their ability to learn from new observations—a phenomenon known as失去可塑性loss of plasticity (Ash and Adams, 2020; Lyle et al., 2022; Nikishin et al., 2022; Dohare et al., 2024)。在这项工作中，我们将可塑性损失视为模型在固定训练预算下改进目标分布的能力的下降。这种现象对开发能够持续学习的系统来说是一个根本性的挑战，因为这样的系统必须能够在不失去适应能力的情况下纳入新信息。重要的是，可塑性丧失与灾难性遗忘不同，可塑性丧失是指网络变得无法学习新信息，而不是忘记旧信息。. In this work, we operationalize plasticity loss as a degradation in a model’s ability to improve on a target distribution under a fixed training budget. This phenomenon represents a fundamental challenge for developing systems capable of continual learning, since such systems must be capable of incorporating new information without losing the ability to adapt. Importantly, loss of plasticity differs from catastrophic forgetting in that plasticity loss is about the network becoming incapable of learning new information, rather than forgetting old information.

持续学习是语言模型的一个基本前沿，其有用性取决于它们适应新事实、领域、语言、代码库和用户需求的能力。例如，持续学习可以帮助减少知识中断，并允许编码代理适应新的存储库，而无需完全依赖通过长提示进行的上下文学习。由于这一前景，理解和防止塑性损失已成为一个日益活跃的研究领域Continual learning is a fundamental frontier for language models, whose usefulness depends on their ability to adapt to new facts, domains, languages, codebases, and user needs. For example, continual learning could help mitigate knowledge cutoffs and allow coding agents to adapt to new repositories without relying entirely on in-context learning via long prompts. Because of this promise, understanding and preventing plasticity loss has become an increasingly active area of research(Kumar et al., 2024; Lyle et al., 2024b; Chung et al., 2024; Elsayed et al., 2024; Elsayed and Mahmood, 2024; Lewandowski et al., 2024; Lyle et al., 2024a; Liu and Mou, 2026; Lillo and Cheney, 2026).

虽然可塑性损失的研究已经扩展到更广泛的领域和架构，但对于大型 Transformer 模型的自然语言环境中的这一现象的研究仍然很少，尤其是大规模的。之前涉及 Transformer 架构的工作要么集中在视觉领域While research on plasticity loss has expanded to include a wider range of domains and architectures, there are still few studies of the phenomenon in natural-language settings with large Transformer models, especially at scale. Previous work involving Transformer architectures has either focused on vision domains(Lyle et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2024; Hernandez-Garcia et al., 2025; Lewandowski et al., 2025)或使用合成文本数据or used synthetic text data(Farias and Jozefiak, 2025)，通常网络包含的参数少于 2000 万个。在具有大型 Transformer 模型的自然语言环境中，现有评估考虑的任务相对较少，因此很难表征可塑性损失的发生和进展, often with networks containing fewer than 20 million parameters. In natural-language settings with large Transformer models, existing evaluations have considered relatively few tasks, making it difficult to characterize the onset and progression of plasticity loss(Cho et al., 2026)。因此，目前尚不清楚在现实文本流上训练的 GPT 式模型是否表现出可塑性损失、这种效果如何随模型规模变化，以及这是否是现实应用中的实际问题。. Thus, it remains unclear whether GPT-style models trained on realistic text streams exhibit plasticity loss, how this effect changes with model scale, and whether it is a practical problem in real-world applications.

在本文中，我们研究了在自然语言数据上训练的 GPT 式解码器 Transformer 模型中的可塑性损失。与原来的 Transformer 架构不同In this paper, we investigate plasticity loss in GPT-style decoder-only Transformer models trained on natural-language data. Unlike the original Transformer architecture ofVaswani et al. (2017)，它使用后层标准化，我们的模型使用现代语言模型中常见的前层标准化架构, which used post-layer normalization, our models use the pre-layer-normalization architecture common in modern language models(Radford et al., 2019; Brown et al., 2020; DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025)。为了衡量可塑性的损失，我们引入了一种多语言下一个标记预测问题，其中针对固定数量的标记一次呈现一种语言，并且该过程循环通过一系列语言，以便可以无限期地延长训练。然后，我们定期评估每个模型适应未包含在连续预训练序列中的越南探测任务的效率。. To measure loss of plasticity, we introduce a multilingual next-token prediction problem in which languages are presented one at a time for a fixed number of tokens, and the process cycles through a sequence of languages so that training can be extended indefinitely. We then periodically evaluate how efficiently each model adapts to a held-out Vietnamese probing task that is not included in the continual pretraining sequence.

我们的实验显示了从 5M 到 314M 非嵌入参数的模型中可塑性损失的证据，较小的模型更早地表现出可测量的恶化。我们发现，可塑性损失的发生遵循可预测的次线性幂律随模型大小的变化，这表明仅增加参数数量作为缓解策略的回报会递减。我们还发现了固定多语言训练下可塑性损失的证据，表明任务突然变化（这在可塑性损失文献中很常见，但在实践中并不常见）并不是这种现象出现所必需的。最后，我们测量了可塑性损失的几个相关因素，包括参数增长、休眠单元以及崩溃或懒惰的注意力头，这为设计在长期训练范围内保持可塑性的方法提供了线索。然而，我们还没有找到一个“确凿的证据”来诊断和抵消法学硕士的可塑性损失。Our experiments show evidence of plasticity loss in models ranging from 5M to 314M non-embedding parameters, with smaller models exhibiting measurable deterioration earlier. We find that the onset of plasticity loss follows a predictable sublinear, power-law scaling with model size, suggesting diminishing returns from increasing parameter count alone as a mitigation strategy. We also find evidence of plasticity loss under stationary multilingual training, indicating that abrupt task changes, which are common in the plasticity loss literature but uncommon in practice, are not necessary for the phenomenon to emerge. Finally, we measure several correlates of plasticity loss, including parameter growth, dormant units, and collapsed or lazy attention heads, which provide clues for designing methods that maintain plasticity over long training horizons. However, we do not yet manage to find a ’smoking gun’ to diagnose and counteract plasticity loss in LLMs.

II 背景Background

人工神经网络在训练后会失去学习能力的发现可以追溯到二十多年前The finding that artificial neural networks can lose their ability to learn after training dates back more than two decades(Smith et al., 2000; Ellis and Lambon Ralph, 2000)。最近的研究表明，这种现象不仅是早期神经网络的病态，而且也出现在经过长时间训练的现代深度学习系统中. Recent work has shown that this phenomenon is not merely a pathology of early neural networks, but also arises in modern deep learning systems trained for extended periods(Lyle et al., 2022; Nikishin et al., 2022; Dohare et al., 2023; Abbas et al., 2023)。这些观察结果推动了越来越多的工作，旨在描述可塑性损失何时发生、它在架构和领域中出现的广泛程度以及如何预防它。. These observations have motivated a growing body of work aimed at characterizing when plasticity loss occurs, how broadly it appears across architectures and domains, and how it might be prevented.

在前馈网络中观察到可塑性损失Loss of plasticity has been observed in feedforward networks(Dohare et al., 2024; Lyle et al., 2023; Kumar et al., 2024; Elsayed and Mahmood, 2024)、卷积网络, convolutional networks(Abbas et al., 2023; Nikishin et al., 2022; Lee et al., 2023)、残差网络, residual networks(Dohare et al., 2024; Lyle et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2024)和 Transformer 架构, and Transformer architectures(Lyle et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2024; Hernandez-Garcia et al., 2025; Farias and Jozefiak, 2025; Cho et al., 2026; Liu and Mou, 2026)。此外，在监督学习、强化学习和自监督学习等多种学习问题中也观察到了这种现象. In addition, the phenomenon has been observed across multiple learning problems, such as supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and self-supervised learning(Springer et al., 2025)——尽管作者将这种现象命名为过度训练。由此，可塑性损失的普遍性逐渐被社会所认识。—though the authors named the phenomenon over-training. IV-A 多语言持续学习问题Multilingual Continual Learning Problem

我们在设计适合研究大型语言模型可塑性损失的持续学习问题时寻找了三个必要条件：We looked for three desiderata in designing a continual learning problem suitable for studying plasticity loss with large language models:

  1. 1.

    数据应该是自然语言的，克服了以前使用合成数据集的研究的缺点。The data should be natural-language, overcoming the shortcomings of previous studies using synthetic datasets.

  2. 2.

    任务的顺序应该可以无限期地延长，以使训练长度能够观察到可测量的可塑性损失的影响。The sequence of tasks should be extendable indefinitely to enable the training length required to observe measurable effects of plasticity loss.

  3. 3.

    这些任务应该是自然的并且具有足够的挑战性，以便规模化能够带来有意义的绩效改进。The tasks should be naturalistic and challenging enough for scale to result in meaningful improvements in performance.

VI 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们研究了在自然语言数据上训练的 GPT 式 Transformer 模型中的可塑性损失。尽管之前对可塑性损失的研究主要集中在较小的模型、综合问题或非语言领域，但我们的结果表明，即使在真实的大规模自然语言数据集上进行训练，具有超过 1 亿个非嵌入参数的 GPT 式模型也可能失去有效适应新数据的能力。In this paper, we studied loss of plasticity in GPT-style Transformer models trained on natural-language data. Whereas previous studies of plasticity loss have largely focused on smaller models, synthetic problems, or non-language domains, our results show that GPT-style models with more than 100 million non-embedding parameters can lose the ability to efficiently adapt to new data even when trained on real, large-scale natural-language datasets.

除了在这种情况下证明可塑性损失之外，我们还第一个拟合了一个模型，该模型预测可塑性损失的发生作为模型大小的函数。为了使这种比较更加可控，我们在模型尺寸上保持长宽比和注意力头尺寸固定，从而减少超出比例的架构变化。由此产生的模型是参数数量和标记数量之间的经典幂律“缩放律”，直到可塑性损失开始。我们的定律预测，可塑性损失与非嵌入参数的数量呈次线性关系，这意味着仅增加模型大小作为防止可塑性损失的策略所带来的收益递减。这一预测回答了本文标题中提出的问题：仅靠规模无法使我们免于可塑性损失。Beyond demonstrating plasticity loss in this setting, we are the first to fit a model predicting the onset of plasticity loss as a function of model size. To make this comparison more controlled, we kept the aspect ratio and attention-head dimension fixed across model sizes, reducing architectural variation beyond scale. The resulting model is a classical power-law ‘scaling-law’ between the number of parameters and the number of tokens until the onset of plasticity loss. Our law predicts that plasticity loss scales sublinearly with the number of non-embedding parameters, implying diminishing returns from increasing model size alone as a strategy for preventing plasticity loss. This prediction speaks to the question posed in the title of this paper: scale alone cannot save us from plasticity loss.

鉴于我们还观察到固定多语言训练下可塑性损失的证据，我们的结果更加引人注目。这意味着可塑性损失不仅仅是由我们最初的、有点人为的非平稳任务（我们在语言中循环）引起的。相反，可塑性损失似乎也会影响在固定数据集上进行长期定期预训练的模型。总的来说，我们可以初步得出结论，缩放会延迟可塑性损失，通常对于大量令牌来说，但不能完全击败它，其次，我们应该预期可塑性损失最终会影响使用标准方法训练的标准语言模型。这意味着可塑性的损失不是小型网络、过时的架构或不寻常的训练设置的产物，而是神经网络训练的基本且极其普遍的属性。Our result is even more striking given that we also observed evidence of plasticity loss under stationary multilingual training. This means that plasticity loss is not simply caused by our original, somewhat artificially nonstationary task where we cycle through languages. Rather, plasticity loss also seems to affect models which have undergone regular pretraining for a long period on a stationary dataset. Overall, then, we can tentatively conclude that scaling will delay plasticity loss, often for a substantial number of tokens, but cannot fully defeat it, and secondly that we should expect plasticity loss to eventually affect standard language models which have been trained using standard methods. This implies that loss of plasticity is not an artifact of small networks, outdated architectures, or unusual training setups, but rather a fundamental and extremely general property of neural network training.

最后，我们测量了网络内部可塑性损失的几个相关性。当模型失去可塑性时，它们往往会积累低活性的 MLP 单元、懒惰的注意力头、崩溃的注意力头和更大的参数量值。这些效应在所有模型中的表现并不统一，并且没有一个单独的效应能够完全解释所观察到的探测性能恶化。尽管如此，它们提供了有用的诊断并提出了可能的缓解方向，从对参数增长的更强约束到跟踪单元活动或重新初始化未充分使用的组件的方法。我们希望这些结果有助于指导学习系统的设计，使其在固定和持续学习环境中的长期训练范围内保持可塑性。Lastly, we measured several correlates of plasticity loss inside the network. As models lost plasticity, they tended to accumulate low-activity MLP units, lazy attention heads, collapsed attention heads, and larger parameter magnitudes. These effects did not appear uniformly across all models, and none of them alone fully explains the observed deterioration in probing performance. Nevertheless, they provide useful diagnostics and suggest possible directions for mitigation, ranging from stronger constraints on parameter growth to methods that track unit activity or reinitialize underused components. We hope these results help guide the design of learning systems that maintain plasticity over long training horizons in both stationary and continual learning settings.
