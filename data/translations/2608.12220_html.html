1 简介Introduction

近年来，视觉语言模型（VLM）在基本视觉任务中取得了显着的成功In recent years, Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have achieved remarkable success in fundamental visual tasks[3, 23, 10]。 尽管取得了这些进展，但在许多需要高级空间推理的关键下游应用中，例如机器人导航. Despite this progress, in many critical downstream applications that require advanced spatial reasoning, such as robot navigation[29, 51]、机器人操控, robot manipulation[13, 21, 56]、自动驾驶, autonomous driving[43, 45]和虚拟现实系统, and virtual reality systems[50, 6]，当前模型仍然存在显着的性能差距。 增强 VLM 的空间推理能力是现实世界人工智能应用的关键前沿, current models still suffer from significant performance gaps. Enhancing the spatial reasoning capabilities of VLMs represents a critical frontier for real-world artificial intelligence applications[55].

为了增强 VLM 的空间推理能力，基于监督微调 (SFT) 的早期数据驱动方法通常依赖于具有辅助空间表示的大规模合成数据集的后训练To enhance the spatial reasoning capabilities of VLMs, early data-driven approaches based on Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) typically rely on post-training on massive synthetic datasets with auxiliary spatial representations[7, 8, 5, 37]。然而，这些工作是高度数据密集型的，需要大量的数据管理工作。此外，通过 SFT 进行训练往往会导致严格的死记硬背，最终限制了模型的泛化能力. However, these works are highly data-intensive, requiring substantial effort for data curation. Moreover, training via SFT tends to induce strict rote memorization, which ultimately restricts the models’ generalization capabilities[9].

为了开发强大且可泛化的空间推理能力，可验证奖励强化学习（RLVR）的最新进展被引入空间推理领域To develop robust and generalizable spatial reasoning capabilities, recent advances in Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) are introduced to the spatial reasoning domain[36, 26, 39, 47, 53]。这些作品利用思想链 (CoT) 提示和可验证的结果奖励来提高推理性能。 然而，完全依赖稀疏的结果奖励会阻碍细粒度的优势估计，导致中间推理步骤的信用分配不准确。 与此同时，另一项研究提出了结构化 CoT 模板，旨在改善类人认知过程中的感知. These works leverage Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting alongside verifiable outcome rewards to improve reasoning performance. However, relying exclusively on sparse outcome rewards hinders fine-grained advantage estimation, leading to inaccurate credit assignment for intermediate reasoning steps. Concurrently, another line of research proposes structured CoT templates, aiming to improve perception in human-like cognitive process[34]并增强推理轨迹的可解释性and enhance the interpretability of the reasoning trajectories[4, 20]。 然而，这些研究严重忽视了模板中与深度相关的信息，从根本上限制了对 3D 空间的理解。 此外，他们还遭受不良信用分配的困扰，未能奖励结构化信息中的特定推理行为。. Nevertheless, these studies critically overlook depth-related information within their templates, fundamentally restricting the comprehension of 3D spaces. Furthermore, they also suffer from poor credit assignment, failing to reward specific reasoning behaviors in the structured information.

为了解决这些关键差距，我们提出了一个结构化推理 CoT 框架，该框架显式地模拟 3D 空间感知，确保稳健的环境理解。如图所示To address these critical gaps, we propose a structured reasoning CoT framework that explicitly models 3D spatial perception, ensuring robust environmental understanding. More importantly, by partitioning the CoT into independently extractable perception and analysis modules, it unlocks the potential for fine-grained, targeted verification and accurate credit assignment for each reasoning step during RL training.

在结构化 CoT 的基础上，我们引入了一种新颖的强化学习算法，该算法将多目标过程奖励与先进的优势估计方法相结合。 具体来说，我们为物体的边界框（bbox）和深度感知设计了专用的奖励函数，提供精确的监督信号来优化模型的3D空间理解能力。 此外，我们提出了推理一致性奖励，用于评估推理轨迹与最终答案之间的一致性，有效指导模型产生连贯的推论。 至关重要的是，所提出的优势估计算法集成了这些过程奖励，以促进 CoT 跟踪的不同部分之间的细粒度信用分配。 通过明确地将每个推理模块的贡献归因于最终结果，我们的强化学习算法从根本上促进了 VLM 中的高级空间推理。Building on the structured CoT, we introduce a novel reinforcement learning algorithm that integrates multi-objective process rewards coupled with an advanced advantage estimation method. Specifically, we design dedicated reward functions for object’s bounding box(bbox) and depth perception, providing precise supervisory signals to optimize the model’s 3D spatial understanding capabilities. Furthermore, we propose a reasoning consistency reward that evaluates the alignment between the reasoning trajectory and the final answer, effectively guiding the model to generate coherent deductions. Crucially, the proposed advantage estimation algorithm integrates these process rewards to facilitate fine-grained credit assignment across different segments of the CoT trace. By explicitly attributing the contribution of each reasoning module to the final outcome, our RL algorithm fundamentally fosters advanced spatial reasoning in VLMs.

4 相关工作Related Work

4.1 通过监督微调进行空间推理Spatial Reasoning via Supervised Fine-Tuning

尽管 VLM 在一般视觉语言任务中取得了成功Despite the success of VLMs in general vision-language tasks[10, 3, 12, 32, 28]，强大的空间推理仍然是一个关键瓶颈。现有的 SFT 方法可以通过对合成数据集进行数据密集型后训练来解决这个问题, robust spatial reasoning remains a critical bottleneck. Existing SFT methods address this either via data-intensive post-training on synthetic datasets[7, 8, 5]或者通过集成专门的几何编码器or by integrating specialized geometric encoders[11, 37, 30, 17, 19]。然而，这些方法会产生高数据/计算开销或限制架构的多功能性。至关重要的是，基于 SFT 的模型倾向于记住训练模板，而不是真正概括空间原理。. However, these approaches incur high data/computational overhead or limit architectural versatility. Crucially, SFT-based models tend to memorize training templates rather than genuinely generalizing spatial principles.

4.2 通过可验证的强化学习进行空间推理Spatial Reasoning via Verifiable Reinforcement Learning

为了提高泛化能力，最近的工作将具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）应用于多模态领域To improve generalization, recent works adapt Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) to multimodal domains[16, 38, 49, 22, 33, 52, 41]，包括空间推理任务和结构化CoT设计, including spatial reasoning tasks and structured CoT design[36, 26, 39, 47, 34, 4, 20]。尽管如此，当前的方法面临两个关键限制：（1）它们的结构化 CoT 忽略了 3D 感知的关键深度相关信息，（2）它们对稀疏结果奖励的依赖阻碍了中间步骤的准确信用分配。为了解决这些问题，我们提出了一种深度感知的结构化 CoT 来捕获 3D 线索，再加上多目标过程奖励和细粒度优势估计算法，以确保精确的信用分配。. Nonetheless, current approaches face two key limitations: (1) their structured CoTs overlook critical depth-related information for 3D perception, and (2) their reliance on sparse outcome rewards hinders accurate credit assignment for intermediate steps. To resolve these, we propose a depth-aware structured CoT to capture 3D cues, coupled with multi-objective process rewards and a fine-grained advantage estimation algorithm to ensure precise credit assignment.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 SCOUT，这是一种新颖的模型，旨在解决 VLM 在 3D 空间推理能力方面的关键瓶颈。通过将深度感知的结构化 CoT 与定制的过程监督强化学习算法相结合，SCOUT 有效解决了奖励稀疏和学分分配不准确的持续挑战。此外，我们还介绍了 SCOUT-24k，这是一个涵盖基本空间关系到复杂透视变换的综合数据集。广泛的评估表明，SCOUT-7B 模型从通用空间基准到复杂的空间推理任务都实现了最先进的性能，同时在多图像和视频场景中表现出强大的域外泛化能力。这项研究建立了一种在 VLM 中培养空间推理的新方法，为下一代空间感知人工智能系统铺平了一条非常有前途的道路。In this work, we present SCOUT, a novel model designed to address the critical bottleneck of VLMs in 3D spatial reasoning capabilities. By coupling a depth-aware structured CoT with a tailored process-supervised reinforcement learning algorithm, SCOUT effectively resolves the persistent challenges of sparse rewards and inaccurate credit assignment. Additionally, we introduce SCOUT-24k, a comprehensive dataset spanning from basic spatial relations to complex perspective transformations. Extensive evaluations demonstrate that the SCOUT-7B model achieves state-of-the-art performance from general spatial benchmarks to complex spatial reasoning tasks, while exhibiting robust out-of-domain generalization across multi-image and video scenarios. This research establishes a new approach for cultivating spatial reasoning in VLMs, paving a highly promising pathway toward the next generation of spatially aware AI systems.

  2. 2.

    相对距离预测Relative Distance Prediction：此任务涉及一个目标对象和两个候选对象。我们设置 0.03 米的距离阈值。低于该阈值的变化被归类为等距场景，而超过该阈值的差异则明确地将严格更接近的对象识别为地面实况。根据它们的 3D 空间坐标，我们计算从目标到每个候选者的欧几里德距离，以确定最近的一个。: This task involves a target object alongside two candidate objects. Variations below this threshold are classified as equidistant scenarios, whereas differences exceeding it definitively identify the strictly closer object as the ground truth. Based on their 3D spatial coordinates, we compute the Euclidean distance from the target to each candidate to determine the nearest one.

  3. 3.

    视角转换推理Perspective Transformation Reasoning：此任务将虚拟相机放置在指定方向的参考对象上。这些问题按照 3D 空间偏移进行系统分类：显着的水平位移和可忽略的深度差异会产生横向查询（例如，左/右）；主要的深度差异导致纵向查询（例如，前/后）；并且两个轴上的大量偏移会产生复合方向查询（例如，左前或右后）。我们根据 x 轴和 z 轴计算相对坐标，然后应用透视变换从相机的角度构建推理过程。: This task places a virtual camera at a reference object with a designated orientation. We compute the relative coordinates based on the x-axis and z-axis, then apply a perspective transformation to construct the reasoning process from the camera’s point of view.

  4. 4.

    以对象为中心的空间推理Object-Centric Spatial Reasoning：与第一个任务类似，数据是从 STVQA 答案注释中提取的。处理管道的初始阶段用于提取场景信息和字幕，有效地绕过问答生成步骤。: Similar to the first task, the data are extracted from the STVQA answer annotations. 虽然所提出的方法显示出有希望的结果，但它仍然受到一些限制。首先，由于计算资源的限制，我们的实验目前仅限于 3B 和 7B 参数尺度的模型。其次，数据集依赖于边界框和对象标签的注释，缺乏更广泛的模式，例如多图像上下文或视频数据。最后，所提出的强化学习方法需要严格结构化的思想链格式来提取感知结果，这限制了灵活性，并且对于其他视觉推理任务来说可能不是最佳的。While the proposed approach demonstrates promising results, it remains subject to several limitations. First, due to computational resource constraints, our experiments are currently restricted to models at the 3B and 7B parameter scales. Second, the datasets rely on annotations of bounding boxes and object labels, lacking broader modalities such as multi-image contexts or video data. Finally, the proposed reinforcement learning method requires a strictly structured chain-of-thought format to extract perception results, which restricts flexibility and may be sub-optimal for other visual reasoning tasks.

为了解决这些限制，未来的工作可以集中在几个关键方向上。首先，将框架扩展到更大的基础模型（例如 14B、32B 等）将有助于研究增加的模型容量如何影响空间推理和强化学习优化。其次，集成多图像和视频模态将把我们的研究从静态二维理解扩展到动态时空推理，并合并 VLM 生成的位置和标题将减少对密集边界框和标签注释的依赖。最后，放松对结构化 CoT 格式的严格依赖可能会实现更灵活、自由形式的推理路径，从而泛化到更广泛的复杂视觉推理任务。To address these limitations, future work can focus on several key directions. First, scaling the framework to larger foundation models (e.g., 14B, 32B, and beyond) will facilitate the investigation of how increased model capacity affects spatial reasoning and RL optimization. Second, integrate multi-image and video modalities will extend our research from static 2D understanding to dynamic, spatiotemporal reasoning and incorporate locations and captions generated by VLMs will reduce the reliance on dense bounding box and label annotations. Finally, relaxing the strict dependency on a structured CoT format may enable more flexible, free-form reasoning pathways that generalize across a broader spectrum of complex visual reasoning tasks.
