I 介绍Introduction

复杂环境中的具身导航包含两项主要任务：视觉语言导航（VLN），智能体遵循自然语言指令进行导航Embodied navigation in complex environments encompasses two major tasks: Vision-Language Navigation (VLN), where agents follow natural language instructions to navigate[1, 2]，以及对象目标导航（OGN），其中代理搜索指定的目标, and Object-Goal Navigation (OGN), where agents search for specified targets[3]。尽管取得了重大进展，当前的深度学习代理仍然缺乏实际部署所需的可信度。 最先进的 VLN 代理已被证明在轻微的语言扰动下会失败. Despite significant progress, current deep learning agents still lack the level of trustworthiness required for real-world deployment. State-of-the-art VLN agents have been shown to fail under minor linguistic perturbations[4, 5]，而领先的 OGN 代理在小域变化（例如低光照、运动模糊）下会急剧退化, while leading OGN agents degrade sharply under small domain shifts (e.g., low lighting, motion blur)[6]，导致不可靠的行为。 然而，这些漏洞在很大程度上被现有基准测试所忽视，现有基准测试通常报告干净、理想化输入条件下的性能。当前的基准还忽略了深度传感器损坏，并且缺乏系统评估鲁棒性缓解策略的统一框架。, resulting in unreliable behaviors. However, these vulnerabilities are largely overlooked by existing benchmarks, which typically report performance under clean, idealized input conditions. Current benchmarks also neglect depth-sensor corruptions and lack a unified framework for systematically evaluating robustness mitigation strategies.

However, the fidelity of this modality is often taken for granted. To stress-test this overlooked yet critical sensor input, we introduce four types of depth corruptions that simulate common failure modes in indoor depth cameras. Such corruptions are essential for robustness evaluation, as errors in the depth map can lead to incorrect distance estimation and flawed planning.

Ⅲ-DIII-D 缓解策略Mitigation Strategy

为了解决我们的基准测试发现的漏洞，我们研究了四种增强代理在 R2R 数据集子集上的鲁棒性的策略。 这些互补机制为开发更值得信赖和更有弹性的实体导航系统提供了建设性途径。To address the vulnerabilities identified by our benchmark, we investigate four strategies for enhancing agent robustness on a subset of R2R dataset. 腐败感知数据增强Corruption-Aware Data Augmentation在训练期间引入 RGB 和深度损坏以及干净的帧。 这可以应用于每帧（瞬态），其中对每个单独的帧随机采样损坏，也可以应用于每集（持久），其中选择单一类型的损坏并将其一致地应用于整个剧集中的所有帧。此外，分布式变体根据先前的评估对损坏类型的采样进行加权，为表现较差的损坏类型分配更高的概率，以优先考虑鲁棒性增益。introduces RGB and depth corruption alongside clean frames during training. This can be applied either per-frame (transient), where corruption is randomly sampled for each individual frame, or per-episode (persistent), where a single type of corruption is selected and applied consistently across all frames within an entire episode. Additionally, a distributed variant weights the sampling of corruption types based on prior evaluation, assigning higher probabilities to those exhibiting poorer performance to prioritize robustness gains.
师生蒸馏Teacher-Student Distillation由教师模型（经过数据增强策略训练）组成，指导学生模型处理损坏的输入consists of a teacher model (trained in data augmentation strategies) that guides the student model to process corrupted inputs[35]。通过统一逐步行动空间并优化复合目标函数（模仿学习、策略-KL 散度和特征-MSE），该方法将教师稳健的决策逻辑转移到学生模型中。 TS 方法通过内化教师的稳健推理来训练学生模型的弹性。. By unifying their stepwise action spaces and optimizing a composite objective function (imitation learning, policy-KL divergence, and feature-MSE), this method transfers the teacher’s robust decision making logic to the student model. TS method trains the student model to be resilient by internalizing the teacher’s robust reasoning.
适配器Adapters称为参数高效适配器，添加到深度和 RGB 路径中，仅占权重的 1-3%known as parameter-efficient adapters which are added to the depth and RGB pathways, with just 1-3% of the weights[36]。每个适配器在感知路径中都有一个残余瓶颈，在主干保持冻结状态时学习校正增量。为了稳定全景表示，对每个视图进行使用可靠性权重的每个视图嵌入的融合，该融合根据相对于全景平均值的特征大小估计可靠性分数，通过上限衰减来降低离群值的权重，然后计算跨视图的归一化加权平均值。这种配对减少了噪声或缺失感知值的影响，并产生更稳定的全景图，而无需重新训练整个编码器。. Each adapter has a residual bottleneck in the perceptual pathway that learns corrective deltas while the backbone remains frozen. To stabilize the panoramic representation, a fusion of per-view embeddings using reliability weights is done for each view, which estimates a reliability score from the feature magnitude relative to the panorama average, down-weights outliers with a capped decay, and then computes a normalized weighted average across views. This pairing reduces the impact of noisy or missing perception values and produces a more stable panorama without retraining the full encoder.

We also explore prompt engineering on OpenAI o3 as an alternative approach. It runs once per episode to strip unsafe text and paraphrase inputs without altering the core intent, reducing instruction-induced failures with negligible latency and memory overhead.

This gap potentially stems from ETPNav’s early-fusion design, which feeds raw depth directly into its transformer stack, so Gaussian noise, quantization, or multipath corruptions contaminate every token the planner processes. This late-fusion with noise filtering outperforms raw early fusion. On the R2R dataset, ETPNav exhibits a larger performance drop, which may be because depth failures are no longer compensated by the fine-grained RxR instructions, as the simpler R2R directions provide weaker guidance and thus amplify the impact of corrupted depth.

V 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 NavTrust，这是第一个用于评估跨感知和语言模式的嵌入式导航系统可信度的统一基准，涵盖 VLN 和 OGN 代理。 通过受控的 RGB 深度和指令损坏，NavTrust 揭示了最先进代理的性能漏洞。 通过提供广泛的比较研究，我们使社区不仅能够关注名义条件下的最佳性能，而且能够关注腐败条件下稳健、可靠和值得信赖的行为。 在未来的工作中，我们将通过自适应对抗策略扩展 NavTrust，以解决全面的具体导航挑战。 这些扩展将进一步促进代理的开发，这些代理不仅在名义情况下具有高性能，而且在现实环境中也安全可靠。We introduced NavTrust, the first unified benchmark for evaluating the trustworthiness of embodied navigation systems across both perception and language modalities, which covers VLN and OGN agents. Through controlled RGB-Depth and instruction corruptions, NavTrust reveals performance vulnerabilities across state-of-the-art agents. By providing an extensive comparative study, we enable the community to focus on not just peak performance under nominal conditions but also robust, reliable, and trustworthy behavior under corruptions. In future work, we will expand NavTrust with adaptive adversarial strategies to address the full stack of embodied navigation challenges. These extensions will further facilitate the development of agents that are not only high-performing in nominal situations but also safe and reliable in real-world environments.
