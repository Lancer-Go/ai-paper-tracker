将秘密与占位符分离：用于三类凭证泄漏检测的混合 CNN-CodeBERT 框架Separating Secrets from Placeholders: A Hybrid CNN-CodeBERT Framework for Three-Class Credential Leakage Detection

Maksuda Bilkis Baby Information Systems, University of Maryland, Baltimore County, USA Khusika Shah Computer Science and Electrical Engineering, University of Maryland, Baltimore County, USA Naiyue Liang Information Systems, University of Maryland, Baltimore County, USA Lei Zhang Information Systems, University of Maryland, Baltimore County, USA
摘要Abstract

公共源代码存储库中的凭证泄露构成了严重的安全威胁，仅 2024 年就有超过 2380 万个秘密被泄露。现有的检测工具误报率很高，因为严格的模式匹配和二元分类方案无法区分真实凭证与占位符或弱凭证。我们提出了一个三类分类框架，利用基于 CodeBERT 的语义理解与字符级模式识别相结合，将占位符或弱凭证显式建模为不同的类。我们在新构建的数据集上评估了我们的方法，该数据集包含 10 种编程语言的 9,426 个样本。我们的模型实现了 0.86 的马修斯相关系数和 0.90 的宏观 F1 分数，实现了真实凭证泄露的 93% 召回率和 89% 准确率，同时将高严重性警报减少了 33.0%（从 373 到 250），而无需牺牲安全覆盖范围。与之前的字符级方法相比，我们的方法将占位符或弱凭据检测的 F1 分数从 54% 提高到 81%，同时保持强大的跨语言泛化性，10 种语言中的 9 种在保留一种语言评估下实现了 0.80 以上的 F1。Credential leakage in public source code repositories poses a critical security threat, with over 23.8 million secrets exposed in 2024 alone. Existing detection tools suffer from high false-positive rates because rigid pattern matching and binary classification schemes fail to distinguish genuine credentials from placeholder or weak credentials. We propose a three-class classification framework that explicitly models placeholder or weak credentials as a distinct class, leveraging CodeBERT-based semantic understanding combined with character-level pattern recognition. We evaluate our approach on a newly constructed dataset of 9,426 samples spanning 10 programming languages. Our model achieves a Matthews Correlation Coefficient of 0.86 and a macro F1-score of 0.90, achieving 93% recall and 89% precision for genuine credential leaks while reducing high-severity alerts by 33.0% (from 373 to 250) without sacrificing security coverage. Compared to prior character-level approaches, our method improves placeholder or weak credential detection from 54% to 81% F1-score while maintaining strong cross-language generalization, with 9 of 10 languages achieving F1 above 0.80 under leave-one-language-out evaluation.

Ⅱ-AII-A 实证研究与测量Empirical Studies and Measurement

大规模研究表明，硬编码凭证经常出现在公共存储库中，并且通常保持有效时间足以构成严重风险。梅利等人。证明暴露的秘密会随着时间的推移在 GitHub 存储库中持续存在，从而创造了妥协的机会Large-scale studies show that hardcoded credentials frequently appear in public repositories and often remain valid long enough to pose serious risks. Meli et al. demonstrated that exposed secrets persist over time in GitHub repositories, creating opportunities for compromise[23]以及相关工作证实，安全敏感的硬编码值在实践中仍然普遍存在, and related work confirms that security-sensitive hardcoded values remain prevalent in practice[27]。尽管这些研究确定了秘密泄露的严重性，但它们侧重于生态系统级别的测量，并且不提供用于培训或评估的标记数据集。. Although these studies establish the severity of secret leakage, they focus on ecosystem-level measurement and do not provide labeled datasets for training or evaluation.

后续研究表明，泄露的凭证通过分叉和依赖项传播，而攻击者则主动监控存储库是否有新的泄露Subsequent research shows that leaked credentials propagate through forks and dependencies, while attackers actively monitor repositories for new leaks[39, 38, 34]。泄漏从源代码延伸到容器镜像和移动应用程序. Despite broadening the understanding of credential exposure, these works largely adopt a binary notion of leakage and do not address ambiguity from placeholders.

本文介绍了 CLD 的三类公式： 明确区分真正的凭证泄漏、占位符、 和良性代码。对 9,426 个手动注释的片段进行评估 跨 10 种编程语言展现出强大的性能 （Macro-F1：0.90，MCC：0.86，真正泄漏召回率：0.93） 跨语言泛化（LOLO平均F1：0.83），减少 高严重性警报增加 33.0%，无需 牺牲安全覆盖范围。与之前的字符级方法相比，占位符检测的 F1 分数从 54% 提高到 81%。消融研究证实语义代码理解是关键 组件，具有提供补充的字符级功能 覆盖率和语言适配器，改进了 10 种编程语言中的 8 种。This paper introduces a three-class formulation for CLD that explicitly separates genuine credential leaks, placeholders, and benign code. Evaluation on 9,426 manually annotated snippets across 10 programming languages demonstrates strong performance (Macro-F1: 0.90, MCC: 0.86, Genuine Leak recall: 0.93) with robust cross-language generalization (LOLO average F1: 0.83), reducing high-severity alerts by 33.0% without sacrificing security coverage. 未来的工作包括但不限于：（i）合并存储库级上下文（例如，依赖结构和版本历史）以改善歧义，（ii）将检测范围从源代码扩展到凭证经常出现的非代码工件（例如配置文件和文档），以及（iii）研究基于LLM的增强以更好地处理硬边界情况，例如，通过利用LLM辅助注释进行主动学习和有针对性地合成不明确的占位符。此外，我们计划进行用户研究，以评估多类别分类是否可以减少实践中的警报疲劳，并探索适合持续集成的轻量级部署变体。Future work includes, but is not limited to: (i) incorporating repository-level context (e.g., dependency structure and version history) to improve disambiguation, (ii) extending detection beyond source code to non-code artifacts where credentials commonly appear (e.g., configuration files and documentation), and (iii) investigating LLM-based augmentation to better handle hard borderline cases, e.g., via active learning with LLM-assisted annotation and targeted synthesis of ambiguous placeholders. In addition, we plan to conduct user studies to assess whether multi-class triage reduces alert fatigue in practice, and to explore lightweight deployment variants suitable for continuous integration.
