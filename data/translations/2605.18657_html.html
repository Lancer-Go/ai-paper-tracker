摘要Abstract

时间序列基础模型 (TSFM) 在通用预测任务中取得了显着的成功；然而，它们对专门分类问题的适应仍然受到标准注意力的计算瓶颈和经典统计知识的系统性遗漏的限制。本技术报告介绍了 KairosHope，这是一种下一代 TSFM，旨在协调分类任务中的大规模泛化与分析精度。该提案的核心是 HOPE Block，这是一种用双记忆系统取代二次注意力的架构：用于动态短期保留的泰坦模块和用于抽象长期历史背景的连续记忆系统（CMS）。Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) have demonstrated notable success in general-purpose forecasting tasks; however, their adaptation to specialized classification problems remains constrained by the computational bottleneck of standard attention and the systematic omission of classical statistical knowledge. This technical report introduces KairosHope, a next-generation TSFM designed to reconcile massive generalization with analytical precision in classification tasks. The core of the proposal is the HOPE Block, an architecture that replaces quadratic attention with a dual-memory system: Titans modules for dynamic short-term retention and a Continuum Memory System (CMS) for the abstraction of long-term historical context.

为了丰富归纳偏差，引入了混合决策头，它将深层潜在表示与通过提取的确定性统计特征融合在一起To enrich the inductive bias, a Hybrid Decision Head is introduced, which fuses deep latent representations with deterministic statistical features extracted viats特征tsfeatures [11]。 KairosHope 在大量 Monash 档案上进行自我监督预训练，结合了掩模时间序列建模 (MTSM) 和对比学习 (InfoNCE)。其随后对 UCR 基准数据集的适应是通过严格的线性探测和完全微调 (LP-FT) 协议进行的，以防止灾难性遗忘。经验结果表明，在具有严格时间因果关系的领域（例如 HAR 或传感器数据）具有卓越的性能。因此，KairosHope 建立了一个强大而高效的框架，使基础模型适应时间序列分析。. KairosHope undergoes self-supervised pre-training on the massive Monash archive, combining Masked Time Series Modeling (MTSM) and contrastive learning (InfoNCE). Its subsequent adaptation to the UCR benchmark datasets is conducted through a rigorous Linear Probing and Full Fine-Tuning (LP-FT) protocol to prevent catastrophic forgetting. Empirical results demonstrate superior performance in domains characterized by strict temporal causality—such as HAR or Sensor data. Consequently, KairosHope establishes a robust and efficient framework for the adaptation of foundation models to time series analysis.

1 简介Introduction

时间序列分析和分类是跨多个学科的核心挑战，包括医学生命体征监测Time series analysis and classification represent a central challenge across multiple disciplines, ranging from medical vital sign monitoring[13]物联网 (IoT) 中的异常检测to anomaly detection in the Internet of Things (IoT)[6]和财务分析and financial analysis[1]。与自然语言处理（NLP）或计算机视觉不同，现实世界的时间序列表现出高度异质性，其特点是可变长度序列、非平稳分布以及局部动态（短期波动）和全局依赖性（宏观季节性）之间的复杂相互作用。. Unlike natural language processing (NLP) or computer vision, real-world time series exhibit a highly heterogeneous nature, characterized by variable-length sequences, non-stationary distributions, and complex interactions between local dynamics (short-term volatility) and global dependencies (macro-seasonality).

从历史上看，时间序列分类主要是基于弹性距离的方法，例如动态时间规整（DTW）Historically, time series classification was dominated by approaches based on elastic distances, such as Dynamic Time Warping (DTW)[4]，以及基于字典和 shapelet 的算法, as well as dictionary and shapelet-based algorithms[16]。深度学习的出现从根本上改变了这个领域。全卷积网络 (FCN) 等架构. The advent of deep learning radically transformed the field. Architectures such as Fully Convolutional Networks (FCN)[22]最突出的是 InceptionTimeand, most prominently, InceptionTime[12]，通过展示无需手动特征工程即可提取不变局部模式的卓越能力，建立了新的性能标准。然而，这些传统的深度学习模型在孤立的范式下运行：它们必须针对每个特定数据集从头开始初始化和训练，这使得它们无法跨领域传输结构知识。, established new performance standards by demonstrating a superior capacity to extract invariant local patterns without the need for manual feature engineering. However, these traditional deep learning models operate under an isolated paradigm: they must be initialized and trained from scratch for each specific dataset, rendering them incapable of transferring structural knowledge across domains.

最近，大型语言模型（LLM）的成功促进了时间序列基础模型（TSFM）的发展。 PatchTST 等开创性提案Recently, the success of large language models (LLMs) has catalyzed the development of Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs). Seminal proposals such as PatchTST[18]证明了将系列分割成补丁以保留局部语义并降低计算复杂性的功效。同时，TimesFM等行业模型demonstrated the efficacy of segmenting series into patches to preserve local semantics and reduce computational complexity. Concurrently, industry models such as TimesFM[7]和莫伊莱and MOIRAI[25]对海量数据收集进行大规模预训练，以实现广义预测能力。去年，研究大力扩展到一般潜在理解和多领域分类，突出了 UDE 等架构scaled pre-training on massive data collections to achieve generalized forecasting capabilities. Throughout the last year, research has vigorously expanded toward general latent understanding and multi-domain classification, highlighting architectures such as UDE[23]，它集成了 Koopman 算子和通用延迟投影，或者像 UniShape 这样的提案, which integrate Koopman operators and universal delay projections, or proposals like UniShape[17]，它试图将几何可解释性（shapelet）纳入大规模架构中。, which attempt to incorporate geometric interpretability (shapelets) into massive architectures.

尽管发展迅速，当前一代 TSFM 对专门分类任务的适应仍然存在严重的局限性。标准注意力机制存在二次记忆瓶颈，限制了深层历史背景，并且绝大多数纯神经架构完全抛弃了经典时间序列理论中固有的有价值的统计知识。Despite this rapid evolution, the adaptation of the current generation of TSFMs to specialized classification tasks continues to present critical limitations. The standard attention mechanism suffers from a quadratic memory bottleneck that limits deep historical context, and the vast majority of purely neural architectures completely discard the valuable statistical knowledge inherent in classical time series theory.

为了解决这一差距，本文引入了 KairosHope，这是一种下一代时间序列基础模型，专门设计用于协调大规模泛化与专业分类中的分析精度。 KairosHope 提供了三个基本贡献。首先，希望块To address this gap, this paper introduces KairosHope, a next-generation Time Series Foundation Model designed specifically to reconcile massive generalization with analytical precision in specialized classification. KairosHope provides three fundamental contributions. First, the HOPE Block[2]用双内存架构取代了传统的二次注意力，能够以线性计算成本对长期依赖关系进行建模。其次，基于庞大且异构的莫纳什存储库的双目标自监督预训练体系replaces traditional quadratic attention with a dual-memory architecture capable of modeling long-term dependencies with linear computational cost. Second, a dual-objective self-supervised pre-training regime based on the vast and heterogeneous Monash repository[10]迫使模型学习稳健且不变的表示。最后，混合决策头与 LP-FT 协议相结合，将深度嵌入与确定性提取的统计特征融合在一起，确保有效适应在 UCR 基准上评估的分类域forces the model to learn robust and invariant representations. Finally, a Hybrid Decision Head, combined with the LP-FT protocol, fuses deep embeddings with deterministically extracted statistical features, ensuring efficient adaptation to classification domains evaluated on the UCR benchmark[8].

在本文档中，详细介绍了底层架构、自监督训练策略以及 UCR 基准数据集的实证结果，展示了 KairosHope 如何在适应时间序列分类的基础模型方面建立了新的范式。Throughout this document, the underlying architecture, the self-supervised training strategy, and the empirical results on the UCR benchmark datasets are detailed, demonstrating how KairosHope establishes a new paradigm in the adaptation of foundation models for time series classification.

2 我们的建议Our proposal

本节介绍了所提出的时间序列分类基础模型：KairosHope。 KairosHope 的架构旨在解决 TSFM 的固有挑战，即可变序列长度、非平稳性以及对局部语义和长期历史依赖性进行建模的必要性。以下小节详细介绍了所采用的架构和训练策略。This section introduces the proposed foundation model for time series classification: KairosHope. The architecture of KairosHope is designed to address the inherent challenges of TSFM, namely variable sequence lengths, non-stationarity, and the necessity of modeling both local semantics and long-term historical dependencies. 2.3 培训策略Training Strategy

KairosHope 的训练由两阶段范式控制，旨在将通用架构转变为特定领域的高精度预测器。这一过程使模型能够首先在自我监督的环境中学习时间序列的“普遍规律”，然后实现有效的专业化。The training of KairosHope is governed by a two-stage paradigm designed to transform a general-purpose architecture into a high-precision predictor for specific domains. This progression enables the model to first learn the “universal laws” of time series in a self-supervised setting, and subsequently achieve efficient specialization.

2.3.2 微调 KairosHopeFine-tuning KairosHope

一旦 HOPE 编码器对时间动态有了普遍的理解，就会利用线性探测 - 完全微调 (LP-FT) 策略对目标数据集进行微调Once the HOPE encoder has developed a universal understanding of temporal dynamics, fine-tuning is conducted on the target dataset utilizing the Linear Probing - Full Fine-tuning (LP-FT) strategy[15]。这种方法对于确保在整个微调过程中保留广义表示至关重要。. This approach is vital for ensuring the retention of generalized representations throughout the fine-tuning process.

  1. 1.

    线性探测 (LP)。Linear Probing (LP).最初，编码器权重被冻结，并且仅训练混合决策头。在此阶段，模型学习将丰富的预训练表示映射到目标域的特定类或值。保持编码器完好无损可以防止初始梯度噪声降低 Titans 和 CMS 模块中学习到的内存结构。Initially, the encoder weights are frozen, and only the hybrid decision head is trained. During this phase, the model learns to map rich, pre-trained representations to the specific classes or values of the target domain. Maintaining the encoder intact prevents initial gradient noise from degrading the memory structures learned within the Titans and CMS modules.

  2. 2.

    全面微调 (FT)。Full Fine-tuning (FT).在决策头初始收敛后，所有模型参数都被解锁以进行综合训练。在此阶段，采用显着较低的学习率。这有助于对编码器权重进行细粒度调整，使系统内存专门针对目标数据集的细微差别和特殊性，同时保留底层的通才知识库。Upon initial convergence of the decision head, all model parameters are unlocked for comprehensive training. During this stage, a significantly lower learning rate is employed. This facilitates fine-grained adjustments to the encoder weights, specializing the system’s memory toward the nuances and particularities of the target dataset while preserving the underlying generalist knowledge base.

这种双重 LP-FT 策略确保 KairosHope 继承预训练阶段的稳定性，同时在特定目标任务中实现最先进的精度。This dual LP-FT strategy ensures that KairosHope inherits the stability of the pre-training phase while achieving state-of-the-art precision in the specific target task.

4 讨论Discussion

对预训练和微调 (LP-FT) 阶段之后从 UCR 基准获得的实证结果进行分析，为模型的归纳偏差提供了重要的见解。一般来说，Titans 和 CMS 模块在主干网中的集成在传感器、运动和心电图数据集中表现出显着的优势，其中时间因果关系和长期历史依赖性是成功分类的决定因素。混合决策头成功地利用了 | 的统计先验。The analysis of the empirical results obtained from the UCR benchmark following the pre-training and fine-tuning (LP-FT) phases provides critical insights into the inductive biases of the model. Generally, the integration of the Titans and CMS modules within the backbone demonstrates a notable superiority in Sensor, Motion, and ECG datasets, where temporal causality and long-term historical dependencies are the determining factors for successful classification. The hybrid decision head successfully capitalizes on the statistical priors of |ts特征tsfeatures在这些领域内。within these domains.

然而，按数据集类型分组的分析揭示了分类为 IMAGE 的数据集系列的不同行为和改进余地。在这些问题中，一维时间序列并不代表随时间变化的动态现象；相反，它们是从图像中展开或提取二维对象的空间轮廓的结果。However, the analysis grouped by dataset typology reveals disparate behavior and a margin for improvement in the family of datasets categorized as IMAGE. In these problems, the one-dimensional time series do not represent a dynamic phenomenon over time; rather, they are the result of unwinding or extracting the spatial contour of two-dimensional objects from images.

为了提高该特定类别中模型的泛化能力和性能，设计并实施了数据增强策略。受空间形状处理文献的启发，按照 中描述的轮廓提取方法，通过从具有形态变化的图像生成合成时间序列来丰富训练集。To improve the generalization capability and performance of the model in this specific category, a data augmentation strategy was designed and implemented. Inspired by spatial shape processing literature, the training set was enriched by generating synthetic time series from images with morphological variations, following the contour extraction methodology described in[5]。这种干预的目的是为微调阶段提供大量数据，以根据空间轮廓固有的旋转、平移和尺度变化来校准网络。. The objective of this intervention was to provide the fine-tuning phase with a significantly larger volume of data to calibrate the network against rotations, translations, and scale variations intrinsic to spatial contours.

尽管采用了这种强大的增强策略，但实证结果并未显示 IMAGE 类型数据集的准确性或 F1 分数指标有显着改善，相对于没有增强的基线，性能仍然停滞不前。这一负面结果信息量很大，并提出了关于该特定利基方法的结构局限性的两个主要假设：Despite this robust augmentation strategy, empirical results did not show a significant improvement in Accuracy or F1-Score metrics for IMAGE-type datasets, with performance remaining stagnant relative to the baseline without augmentation. 5 结论Conclusions

在本技术报告中，介绍了 KairosHope，这是一种基础模型，旨在通过双内存架构和两阶段训练机制来转换时间序列分类。获得的结果验证了中心假设，即长期（CMS）和短期（Titans）记忆机制的整合能够捕获传统模型经常忽略的复杂时间动态。In this technical report, KairosHope has been presented, a foundation model designed to transform time series classification through a dual-memory architecture and a two-phase training regime. The results obtained validate the central hypothesis that the integration of long-term (CMS) and short-term (Titans) memory mechanisms enables the capture of complex temporal dynamics that conventional models frequently overlook.

从这一发展中得出的主要结论总结如下：The primary conclusions derived from this development are summarized as follows:

  • •

    HOPE 架构的功效：HOPE 块已被证明是二次注意力的有效替代方案，促进线性处理，而不会牺牲对广泛历史依赖性进行建模的能力。Efficacy of the HOPE Architecture: The HOPE block has proven to be an efficient alternative to quadratic attention, facilitating linear processing that does not sacrifice the ability to model extensive historical dependencies.

  • •

    混合表示的价值：深度神经嵌入与确定性统计特征的融合（Value of the Hybrid Representation: The fusion of deep neural embeddings with deterministic statistical features (ts特征tsfeatures）提供了稳健性的重要锚定，当微调数据有限时，这特别有用。) provides an essential anchor of robustness, which is particularly beneficial when fine-tuning data is limited.

  • •

    LP-FT 协议的鲁棒性：线性探测策略和完全微调被证实是一种从基础模型进行知识转移的优越方法，可以实现精确的专业化，而不会降低预训练期间学到的通用表示。Robustness of the LP-FT Protocol: The Linear Probing strategy followed by Full Fine-Tuning is confirmed as a superior methodology for knowledge transfer from foundation models, achieving precise specialization without degrading the universal representations learned during pre-training.

  • •

    时间偏差的局限性：对图像类型数据集的实验和合成数据增强技术的利用表明，空间轮廓的几何性质需要与纯粹因果时间偏差不同的归纳偏差。Limitations of Temporal Biases: Experimentation with IMAGE-type datasets and the utilization of synthetic data augmentation techniques reveal that the geometric nature of spatial contours requires inductive biases distinct from purely causal-temporal ones.

6 未来的工作Future Work

基于这些发现，KairosHope 演变的未来研究方向将集中在以下领域：Building upon these findings, future lines of research for the evolution of KairosHope will focus on the following areas:

  1. 1.

    几何不变性：为轮廓型数据开发特定的特征提取模块，可以将其集成到混合头中，而不会影响动态系列的性能。Geometric Invariance: Developing specific feature extraction modules for contour-type data that can be integrated into the hybrid head without compromising performance on dynamic series.

  2. 2.

    预训练可扩展性：将预训练语料库扩展到更大规模和多模态领域，以观察零样本分类能力的出现点。Pre-training Scalability: Expanding the pre-training corpus toward even more massive and multimodal domains to observe the emergence point of zero-shot classification capabilities.

  3. 3.

    CMS 优化：研究更积极的内存压缩技术，以实现在消费级硬件上处理超长序列（包含数百万个时间步）。CMS Optimization: Investigating more aggressive memory compression techniques to enable the processing of ultra-long sequences (comprising millions of time steps) on consumer-grade hardware.

  4. 4.

    多元扩展：扩展 KairosHope 架构以处理多元时间序列分类。这涉及研究通道间相关机制和跨维度注意力，以捕获多个变量之间的复杂依赖关系，而不影响 HOPE 块的计算效率。Multivariate Extension: Extending the KairosHope architecture to handle multivariate time series classification. This involves researching inter-channel correlation mechanisms and cross-dimensional attention to capture the complex dependencies between multiple variables without compromising the computational efficiency of the HOPE block.
