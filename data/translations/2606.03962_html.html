1 简介Introduction

经典强化学习 (RL) 理论基于这样的基本假设：偏好可以通过标量奖励函数的预期总和来捕获。该假设的一个众所周知的结果是存在最优确定性策略Classical reinforcement learning (RL) theory rests on the foundational assumption that preferences can be captured by the expected sum of a scalar reward function. A well-known consequence of this assumption is the existence of an optimal deterministic policy[puterman94mdp]，因此，大多数标准强化学习方法都旨在收敛到这样的解决方案。然而，随着强化学习越来越多地应用于开放领域，单一行为的收敛已成为一个重大限制。在大型语言模型 (LLM) 的微调等设置中，通常必须保持预训练策略的多样性，以保持其对创造性任务和思维链推理的有用性, and, accordingly, most standard RL methods are designed to converge to such a solution. However, as RL is increasingly applied to open-ended domains, convergence to a single behaviour has become a significant limitation. In settings such as the fine-tuning of large language models (LLMs), it is often essential to maintain the diversity of the pre-trained policy in order to preserve its usefulness for creative tasks and chain-of-thought reasoning[kirk2023understanding, chung2025modifying, ismayilzada2025creative, cui2025entropy, west2025base, zhao2025echo, shypula2025evaluating, yang2025alignment, yun2025price]。除了语言之外，推理时间探索的能力对于科学发现至关重要，智能体必须在广阔的搜索空间中导航以识别罕见的高价值解决方案. Beyond language, the capacity for inference-time exploration is vital for scientific discovery, where agents must navigate vast search spaces to identify rare, high-value solutions[romera-paredes2024, Hubert2025, aygun2025aihelpscientistswrite]。此外，当奖励模型本身只是某些真实潜在偏好的不完美代理时，收敛到确定性最优策略是有害的，并且过度优化会导致发电质量恶化. Furthermore, convergence to a deterministic optimal policy is detrimental when the reward model itself is only an imperfect proxy for some true underlying preference, and over-optimization results in the deterioration of generation quality[gao2022rm, coste2023reward, moskovitz2023confronting, rafailov2024scaling, lambert2026overoptimization].

7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一种新颖的通用强化学习框架，该框架将优化统一起来We presented a novel, generalized reinforcement learning framework that unifies optimization for动作组sets of actions结束over奖励函数的分布distributions of reward functions。通过将目标从最大化预期标量回报转移，我们获得了政策梯度目标，其回报最大化解决方案是精心设计的. By shifting the objective away from maximizing expected scalar returns, we obtain policy gradient objectives whose reward-maximizing solutions are by design分布distributions，从而解决奖励最大化和政策熵之间的紧张关系。这解决了标准策略梯度的各种限制， 例如奖励函数错误下的模式崩溃和脆性。我们对该目标的行为、优化效率和最优解决方案提供了严格的理论分析，并且还提供了小规模和大规模的经验证据，说明 ROSA 如何应用于各种示例设置。最终，这项工作为训练随机策略提供了原则基础，这些策略与它们所处的环境一样多样化和适应性强。, thereby resolving the tension between reward maximization and policy entropy. This addresses a variety of limitations with standard policy gradients, such as mode collapse and brittleness under reward function errors. We provide rigorous theoretical analysis for the behaviour, optimization efficiency, and optimal solution of this objective, and also provide empirical evidence at small and large scales for how ROSA can be applied to a wide variety of example settings. Ultimately, this work provides a principled foundation for training stochastic policies that are as diverse and adaptable as the environments they inhabit.
