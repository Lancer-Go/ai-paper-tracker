摘要Abstract

神经架构搜索（NAS）旨在自动化神经网络架构设计，减少对人类专业知识的依赖。在各种 NAS 方法中，可微分 NAS 因其与传统 NAS 方法相比的效率和准确性而受到关注。由于可微 NAS 将架构搜索空间放宽到连续域，因此可以将连续优化的原理应用于 NAS。在本文中，我们提出了基于线性规划的 NAS（LP-NAS），这是一种基于数学规划的可微 NAS 框架，适用于广泛的连续搜索空间。 LP-NAS 使用验证损失梯度和训练损失 Hessian 制定线性程序 (LP) 来计算架构更新方向，从而在保持模型参数最优性的同时提高泛化能力。通过遵循 LP 导出的下降方向，LP-NAS 可以有效地导航架构搜索空间，从而实现更快、更有效的架构优化。我们引入了 LP-NAS 的两种计算高效的变体，即 S-LP-NAS 和 R-LP-NAS。将 LP-NAS 应用到可微架构搜索 (DARTS) 搜索空间会产生两种算法变体：S-LP-DARTS 和 R-LP-DARTS。与标准 DARTS 算法相比，这两种变体在早期搜索迭代期间都实现了更快的收敛和显着更高的验证性能。 CIFAR-10 和 CIFAR-100 上的大量实验表明，LP-DARTS 在架构搜索和评估阶段都优于标准 DARTS。此外，我们还在 CIFAR-10 数据集上将我们的方法与几种 DARTS 变体（P-DARTS、PC-DARTS 和 STO-DARTS）进行比较，并证明其有效性。此外，我们通过在 ImageNet 数据集上进行实验来验证所发现架构的可转移性。Neural Architecture Search (NAS) aims to automate neural network architecture design, reducing reliance on human expertise. Among the various NAS methods, differentiable NAS has gained prominence due to its efficiency and accuracy compared to conventional NAS approaches. Since differentiable NAS relaxes the architecture search space into a continuous domain, it is possible to apply principles from continuous optimization to NAS. In this paper, we propose Linear Programming-based NAS (LP-NAS), a mathematical programming-based framework for differentiable NAS that is applicable to a wide range of continuous search spaces. LP-NAS formulates a linear program (LP) using the validation-loss gradient and the training-loss Hessian to compute an architecture update direction that improves generalization while preserving the optimality of the model parameters. By following this LP-derived descent direction, LP-NAS efficiently navigates the architecture search space, leading to faster and more effective architecture optimization. We introduce two computationally efficient variants of LP-NAS, namely S-LP-NAS and R-LP-NAS. Applying LP-NAS to the Differentiable Architecture Search (DARTS) search space results in two algorithmic variants, S-LP-DARTS and R-LP-DARTS. Both variants achieve faster convergence and significantly higher validation performance during the early search iterations than the standard DARTS algorithm. Extensive experiments on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 show that LP-DARTS outperforms standard DARTS in both the architecture search and evaluation phases. Additionally, we compare our approach with several DARTS variants (P-DARTS, PC-DARTS, and STO-DARTS) on the CIFAR-10 dataset and demonstrate its effectiveness. Furthermore, we validate the transferability of the discovered architectures through experiments on the ImageNet dataset.

This emerging area of research, referred to as Neural Architecture Search (NAS), a sub-field of HPO and Automated Machine Learning (AutoML), aims to facilitate the automated development of neural network architectures for specific requirements.

5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 LP-NAS，一种在双层优化框架内进行架构搜索的方法。该方法要求在每次迭代中求解LP以获得验证损失的下降方向，同时确保训练损失保持最佳。应用于 DSS 中的 CNN 后，LP-NAS 超越了 DARTS 的性能，发现了可带来卓越结果的单元架构。在 CIFAR-10 数据集上的时间受限实验设置下，它的性能也优于多个 DARTS 变体，同时在架构向 ImageNet 数据集的可迁移性方面保持竞争力。In this paper, we have introduced LP-NAS, an approach for architecture search within the bilevel optimization framework. The approach requires an LP to be solved in each iteration to obtain a descent direction for validation loss while ensuring that the training loss remains optimal. Applied to CNNs in DSS, LP-NAS surpasses the performance of DARTS, discovering cell architectures that lead to superior results. It also outperforms several DARTS variants under time-constrained experimental settings on the CIFAR-10 dataset while remaining competitive in architecture transferability to the ImageNet dataset.

LP-NAS的一个关键挑战在于Hessian矩阵的计算和LP的求解。为了解决与这些挑战相关的计算问题，我们使用简化的 Hessian 矩阵，使用 L-BFGS 对其进行近似，并制定简化的 LP。与标准 DARTS 算法相比，使用来自较低级别的有限二阶信息仍然是有利的，因为该方法在我们所有的实验中快速导致更好的损失和准确性。所提出的基于 LP 的方法可以与任何具有连续架构和模型参数的架构搜索问题集成。未来可以进行有趣的研究，适当选择模型参数来制定简化的 Hessian 矩阵，以便提取最大量的二阶信息。此外，可以制定具有特殊结构（例如块对角线、块角、块三角形、阶梯和有边角）的近似 Hessian 矩阵，以便更快地求解 LP。结果很有希望，我们相信它有潜力开辟一条新的研究路线，通过利用二阶信息使用双层方法解决架构搜索问题。A key challenge in LP-NAS lies in the calculation of the Hessian matrix and solving the LP. To cater to the computational issues associated with these challenges we use a reduced Hessian, approximate it using L-BFGS, and formulate a reduced LP. The use of limited second-order information from the lower-level is still advantageous as the method quickly leads to better losses and accuracies in all our experiments as compared to the standard DARTS algorithm. The proposed LP based approach can be integrated with any architecture search problem with continuous architecture and model parameters. Interesting future research can be performed on appropriate choice of model parameters for the formulation of the reduced Hessian such that maximum amount of second-order information is extracted. Additionally, approximate Hessians with special structures, such as, block diagonal, block angular, block triangular, staircase, and bordered angular, can be formulated for faster solution of the LP. The results are promising and we believe it has the potential to open a new line of research on solving architecture search problems using bilevel methods by utilizing second-order information.
