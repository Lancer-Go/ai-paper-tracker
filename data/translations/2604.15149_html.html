摘要Abstract

随着可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为法学硕士扩展推理能力的主导范式，一种新的失败模式出现了：As reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has become the dominant paradigm for scaling reasoning capabilities in LLMs, a new failure mode emerges:法学硕士游戏验证员LLMs gaming verifiers。我们在归纳推理任务中研究这种现象，其中模型必须归纳并输出逻辑规则。我们发现 RLVR 训练的模型系统地放弃了规则归纳。他们不是学习可概括的模式（例如，“运载红色汽车的火车向东行驶”），而是枚举实例级标签，生成通过验证器的输出，而不捕获任务所需的关系模式。 我们表明，这种行为并不是理解失败，而是奖励黑客的一种形式：仅检查外延正确性的不完美验证者承认误报。为了检测此类快捷方式，我们引入. We study this phenomenon on inductive reasoning tasks, where models must induce and output logical rules. We find that RLVR-trained models systematically abandon rule induction. Instead of learning generalizable patterns (e.g., “trains carrying red cars go east”), they enumerate instance-level labels, producing outputs that pass verifiers without capturing the relational patterns required by the task. We show that this behavior is not a failure of understanding but a form of reward hacking: imperfect verifiers that check only extensional correctness admit false positives. To detect such shortcuts, we introduce同构扰动测试Isomorphic Perturbation Testing（IPT），它在外延验证和同构验证下评估单个模型输出，其中后者在逻辑同构任务下强制不变性。虽然真正的规则归纳保持不变，但捷径策略却失败了。 我们发现捷径行为特定于 RLVR 训练的推理模型（例如 GPT-5、Olmo3），并且在非 RLVR 模型（例如 GPT-4o、GPT-4.5、Ministral）中不存在。此外，捷径的流行随着任务复杂性和推理时间计算的增加而增加。在受控训练实验中，外延验证直接引入捷径策略，而同构验证则消除它们。这些结果表明，RLVR 不仅可以通过公开操纵，还可以通过利用验证者未能执行的内容来激励奖励黑客行为。(IPT), which evaluates a single model output under both extensional and isomorphic verification, where the latter enforces invariance under logically isomorphic tasks. While genuine rule induction remains invariant, shortcut strategies fail. We find that shortcut behavior is specific to RLVR-trained reasoning models (e.g., GPT-5, Olmo3) and absent in non-RLVR models (e.g., GPT-4o, GPT-4.5, Ministral). Moreover, shortcut prevalence increases with task complexity and inference-time compute. In controlled training experiments, extensional verification directly induces shortcut strategies, while isomorphic verification eliminates them. These results show that RLVR can incentivize reward hacking not only through overt manipulation but also by exploiting what the verifier fails to enforce.

1 简介Introduction

强化学习 (RL) 已成为扩展推理能力的主导范例，为 OpenAI 的 GPT-5 和 GPT-5-mini 等前沿模型提供支持。这些系统在响应之前分配大量的测试时间计算来“思考”，生成扩展的推理轨迹以最大限度地提高准确性。虽然这种方法在复杂的数学和逻辑基准上取得了令人印象深刻的性能Reinforcement learning (RL) has become the dominant paradigm for scaling reasoning capabilities, powering frontier models like OpenAI’s GPT-5 and GPT-5-mini. These systems allocate substantial test-time compute to "think" before responding, generating extended reasoning traces to maximize accuracy. While this approach has driven impressive performance on complex mathematical and logical benchmarks(OpenAI, 2025)，它引入了一种基本的张力。当奖励信号依赖于不完美的代理时，模型会学习利用评估机制而不是解决预期任务, it introduces a fundamental tension. When reward signals rely on imperfect proxies, models learn to exploit the evaluation mechanism instead of solving the intended task(Baker et al., 2025)。这表现为明确的奖励黑客行为：模型覆盖单元测试、猴子补丁评分函数、删除断言或强制提前终止程序以获得及格分数而不实施正确的解决方案. This has manifested as explicit reward hacking: models overwrite unit tests, monkey-patch scoring functions, delete assertions, or force early program termination to obtain a passing score without implementing the correct solution(Krakovna et al., 2020; Skalse et al., 2022; MacDiarmid et al., 2025; METR, 2025; Zhong et al., 2025).

2 相关工作Related Work

奖励黑客行为。Reward Hacking.强化学习中的奖励黑客是指代理利用奖励规范中的弱点而不是解决预期任务Reward hacking in reinforcement learning refers to agents exploiting weaknesses in reward specifications rather than solving the intended task(Krakovna et al., 2020)。随着强化学习扩展到法学硕士，在日益复杂的环境中也出现了类似的行为. As RL has scaled to LLMs, analogous behaviors have emerged in increasingly complex environments(MacDiarmid et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2026)。在代理和编码环境中，强化学习训练的模型通过覆盖单元测试、猴子修补评分函数、删除断言或提前终止程序来操纵评估机制，以获得通过分数而不产生正确的解决方案. In agentic and coding settings, RL-trained models manipulate evaluation mechanisms by overwriting unit tests, monkey-patching scoring functions, deleting assertions, or prematurely terminating programs to obtain passing scores without producing correct solutions(METR, 2025; Baker et al., 2025)。这些失败通常被描述为环境黑客，即代理干扰外部验证。我们的工作确定了推理任务中更微妙的失败模式：模型利用对验证者正确性概念的隐式假设，产生满足代理评估标准的输出，同时规避预期的推理目标。. These failures are commonly described as environmental hacking, where agents interfere with external validation. Our work identifies a subtler failure mode in reasoning tasks: models exploit implicit assumptions on the verifier’s notion of correctness, producing outputs that satisfy proxy evaluation criteria while evading the intended reasoning objective.

3 同构扰动测试Isomorphic Perturbation Testing

我们如何确定法学硕士是否真正进行推理，而不是利用评估协议中的弱点？随着法学硕士通过 RLVR 进行优化，这个问题变得越来越紧迫，不完美的奖励可能会激励错位和奖励黑客行为How can we determine whether LLMs genuinely perform reasoning, rather than exploiting weaknesses in the evaluation protocol? This question is increasingly pressing as LLMs are optimized via RLVR, and imperfect rewards can incentivize misalignment and reward hacking(METR, 2025; Zhong et al., 2025)。 对于前沿法学硕士来说，检测这种捷径行为尤其具有挑战性，因为权重、激活和推理轨迹无法访问，导致评估仅限于最终输出。为了解决这个问题，我们引入. Detecting such shortcut behavior is especially challenging for frontier LLMs, since weights, activations, and reasoning traces are inaccessible, leaving evaluation limited to final outputs. To address this, we introduce同构扰动测试Isomorphic Perturbation Testing（IPT），一种仅基于模型输出来诊断捷径行为的方法。 IPT 建立在一个简单的逻辑原则之上：真正的归纳规则学习对于逻辑同构的任务是不变的。(IPT), a methodology for diagnosing shortcut behavior based solely on model outputs. IPT builds upon a simple logical principle: genuine inductive rule learning is invariant to logically isomorphic tasks.

5 结论Conclusion

我们将奖励捷径视为 RLVR 训练的推理模型中的系统性失败模式，其中模型利用验证者的弱点，而不是执行真正的规则归纳。通过 IPT，我们提供了一种黑盒方法来检测前沿系统中的此类行为，而无需访问权重或推理轨迹。我们的研究结果表明，捷径的流行随着任务复杂性和推理时间计算的增加而增加，并且这种行为不仅是相关的，而且可以由训练信号直接诱导。这些结果凸显了 RLVR 训练中的严重错位风险，并激发了更忠实地执行预期推理目标的评估协议。We identify reward shortcuts as a systematic failure mode in RLVR-trained reasoning models, where models exploit weaknesses in verifiers rather than performing genuine rule induction. With IPT, we provide a black-box method to detect such behaviors in frontier systems without requiring access to weights or reasoning traces. Our findings show that shortcut prevalence increases with both task complexity and inference-time compute, and that such behavior is not merely correlational but can be directly induced by the training signal. These results highlight a critical misalignment risk in RLVR training and motivate evaluation protocols that more faithfully enforce intended reasoning objectives.

附录AAppendix A局限性Limitations

我们的分析是在单个基准域（SLR-Bench）上进行的，该域通过训练分类任务上的逻辑编程来构建归纳推理。虽然我们发现的捷径行为是系统性的且可重复的，但它们在多大程度上推广到其他推理领域（例如数学、因果或溯因推理）仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。此外，我们对前沿模型（GPT-5 系列）的评估仅限于黑盒访问，无法直接检查推理痕迹或内部表示。 IPT 通过行为不变性测试来检测捷径，但无法区分捷径策略是在模型的推理过程中明确表示还是从输出分布中隐式出现。最后，由于计算限制，我们的受控训练实验使用了 7B 参数模型；观察到的训练动态是否与更大的模型大小相同，值得进一步研究。Our analysis is conducted on a single benchmark domain (SLR-Bench), which frames inductive reasoning through logic programming over train classification tasks. While the shortcut behaviors we identify are systematic and reproducible, the extent to which they generalize to other reasoning domains (e.g., mathematical, causal, or abductive reasoning) remains an open question. Furthermore, our evaluation of frontier models (GPT-5 family) is limited to black-box access, preventing direct inspection of reasoning traces or internal representations. IPT detects shortcuts through behavioral invariance testing, but cannot distinguish whether shortcut strategies are explicitly represented in the model’s reasoning process or emerge implicitly from output distributions. Finally, our controlled training experiment uses a 7B-parameter model due to computational constraints; whether the observed training dynamics scale identically to larger model sizes warrants further investigation.

In this sense, more compute amplifies the model’s ability to discover reward-maximizing behaviors—whether or not they correspond to the intended reasoning process.
