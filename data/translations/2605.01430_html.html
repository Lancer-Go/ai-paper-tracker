摘要。Abstract.我们如何衡量人工认知系统的真正理解？当前的方法面临着测量差距：概率系统逐渐完善信心，基于实践的系统通过重复执行来编译知识，神经系统在不透明的嵌入空间中分配理解。How do we measure genuine understanding in artificial cognitive systems? Current approaches face a measurement gap: probabilistic systems refine confidence gradually, practice-based systems compile knowledge through repeated execution, and neural systems distribute understanding across opaque embedding spaces.

我们建议，要使理解可测量，需要一种架构，其中理解形成可以产生离散的、可检查的结构特征。本文提出了由表示模式的有限状态机和表示组合的高阶自动机构建的分层自动机。约束推理根据单个观察构建自动机。相似性检测对相关自动机进行聚类，使概念的鲁棒性可量化。图存储器使组合知识可以直接检查。元认知机制可以实现可观察的重新配置。We propose that making understanding measurable requires architectures where understanding formation produces discrete, inspectable structural signatures. This paper presents hierarchical automata built from finite state machines representing patterns and higher-order automata representing compositions. Constraint inference constructs automata from single observations. Similarity detection clusters related automata, making concept robustness quantifiable. Graph memory makes compositional knowledge directly inspectable. Metacognitive mechanisms enable observable reconfiguration.

我们展示了对简单几何域中测量的理解。图演化跟踪揭示了五个可测量的特征：即时表示形成、结构知识、泛化能力、组成意识和元认知访问。这些测量将结构理解与统计相关性区分开来。We demonstrate understanding measurement in a simple geometric domain. Graph evolution tracking reveals five measurable signatures: immediate representation formation, structural knowledge, generalization capacity, compositional awareness, and metacognitive access. These measurements distinguish structural understanding from statistical correlation.

我们的贡献是一个框架，可以通过离散的组合知识结构来衡量理解。这种测量能力补充了神经系统中的感知学习和神经符号架构中的任务执行。Our contribution is a framework for making understanding measurable through discrete compositional knowledge structures. This measurement capability complements perceptual learning in neural systems and task execution in neurosymbolic architectures.

3 理论框架：什么使理解变得可衡量？3 Theoretical Framework: What Makes Understanding Measurable?

问题“我们如何衡量理解？”首先需要回答“什么是理解？”本节建立了来自心灵哲学和认知科学的五个标准，然后展示每个标准如何映射到分层自动机中的可测量计算属性。The question "how do we measure understanding?" requires first answering "what is understanding?" This section establishes five criteria drawn from philosophy of mind and cognitive science, then shows how each criterion maps to a measurable computational property in hierarchical automata.

结构知识：Structural Knowledge:一个真正理解的系统不仅知道存在什么模式，而且知道它们为什么存在。统计相关性表明屋顶和房屋经常同时出现；结构理解表明房屋需要屋顶，因为组成关系是强制性的，而不是可选的。这种区别贯穿于整个心灵哲学：塞尔的中文房间论证（Searle，1980）挑战了无意义的符号操纵，而哈纳德的符号基础问题（哈纳德，1990）则询问符号如何获得超越关联的语义内容。理解需要了解结构必然性而不是统计规律。A system that genuinely understands knows not just what patterns exist but why they exist. Statistical correlation shows roofs and houses cooccur frequently; structural understanding shows houses require roofs because the compositional relationship is mandatory, not optional. This distinction appears throughout philosophy of mind: Searle's Chinese Room argument (Searle, 1980) challenges symbol manipulation without meaning, while Harnad's symbol grounding problem (Harnad, 1990) asks how symbols acquire semantic content beyond associations. Understanding requires knowing structural necessity rather than statistical regularity.

衡量这一点需要了解因果关系或组合关系。当我们可以检查系统的表示并提取依赖关系时，系统就展示了结构知识：“X 需要 Y”而不是“X 与 Y 相关（概率为 p）”。表示必须明确必要性，而不仅仅是共现频率。Measuring this requires access to causal or compositional relationships. A system demonstrates structural knowledge when we can inspect its representation and extract dependencies: 'X requires Y' rather than 'X correlates with Y (with probability p)'. The representation must make necessity explicit, not just co-occurrence frequency.

构图意识：Compositional Awareness:人类的理解是组合的。我们从组件（墙壁、屋顶、门、窗）的角度来理解“房子”，并了解这些组件如何组成一个整体。这种组合结构既可以概括（小屋和豪宅尽管存在差异，但都是房屋），也可以分解（我们可以推理各个组件）。福多的思维语言假说（Fodor，1975）假设概念具有组合结构，发展心理学表明儿童通过理解部分与整体的关系而不仅仅是表面特征来学习类别（Mandler，2004）。Human understanding is compositional. We understand "house" in terms of components (walls, roof, doors, windows) and understand how these components compose into the whole. This compositional structure enables both generalization (a cottage and a mansion are both houses despite differences) and decomposition (we can reason about individual components). Fodor's language of thought hypothesis (Fodor, 1975) posits that concepts have combinatorial structure, and developmental psychology demonstrates that children learn categories by understanding part-whole relationships, not just surface features (Mandler, 2004).

衡量构图意识需要检查知识架构。当系统的表示揭示层次结构时，系统就证明了这一属性：什么包含什么，什么是由什么构建的。Measuring compositional awareness requires inspecting knowledge architecture. A system demonstrates this property when its representation reveals hierarchical structure: what contains what, what is built from what.

Seeing one house allows humans to recognize other instances, even with significant variation. This differs from rote memorization, which stores instances without abstracting commonalities. 元认知访问：Metacognitive Access:理解包括知道你所理解的内容。元认知是对自己知识状态的认识，包括认识差距和监控理解。 Flavell 在元认知监控方面的工作（Flavell，1979）表明，有效的学习者会跟踪他们的理解并在出现差距时调整策略。这种自我意识将真正的理解与盲目的模式匹配区分开来。Understanding includes knowing what you understand. Metacognition is awareness of one's own knowledge state, including recognizing gaps and monitoring comprehension. Flavell's work on metacognitive monitoring (Flavell, 1979) demonstrates that effective learners track their understanding and adjust strategies when gaps appear. This self-awareness distinguishes genuine understanding from blind pattern matching.

测量元认知需要对知识扰动的可观察反应。当结构变化（组件删除或发现更深层次的原语）触发反映依赖性意识的重组时，系统展示了元认知访问。Measuring metacognition requires observable responses to knowledge perturbations. A system demonstrates metacognitive access when structural changes (component deletion or discovery of deeper primitives) trigger reorganization that reflects awareness of dependencies.

样品效率：Sample Efficiency:人类的理解通常需要最少的接触。我们可以看到一种新动物类别的一个例子，并识别其他实例，即使有变化。这种样本效率将人类学习与需要数千个样本来提取统计规律的系统区分开来。 Carey 和 Bartlett 在快速绘图方面的工作（Carey & Bartlett，1978）表明，儿童从单次接触中获取单词含义，并通过经验完善这些初始表征。因此，理解的形成很快，而概念的稳健性则逐渐发展。Human understanding often requires minimal exposure. We can see one example of a new animal category and recognize other instances, even with variation. This sample efficiency distinguishes human learning from systems requiring thousands of examples to extract statistical regularities. Carey and Bartlett's work on fast mapping (Carey & Bartlett, 1978) shows that children acquire word meanings from single exposures, refining these initial representations through experience. So, understanding formation happens quickly while concept robustness develops gradually.

测量样本效率需要跟踪相对于暴露计数的表征形成。当我们可以观察到从单个实例立即创建表示时，系统就证明了这一属性，这与通过多个实例逐步完善概念不同。Measuring sample efficiency requires tracking representation formation relative to exposure count. A system demonstrates this property when we can observe immediate representation creation from single instances, distinct from gradual concept refinement through multiple instances.

4 架构4 Architecture

我们的架构通过图内存结构中组织的三种自动机类型来实现概述的理论框架。有限状态机 (FSM) 表示基本模式，高阶自动机 (HOA) 表示组合结构，学习引擎通过观察的约束推断来构建两者。本节描述每个组件并展示它们如何产生可测量的理解签名。Our architecture implements the outlined theoretical framework through three automata types organized in a graph memory structure. Finite state machines (FSMs) represent basic patterns, higher-order automata (HOAs) represent compositional structures, and the learning engine constructs both through constraint inference from observation. This section describes each component and shows how they produce measurable understanding signatures.

有限状态机 (FSM)：Finite State Machines (FSM):FSM 将模式表示提供为有向图，其中状态为节点，转换为边。每个转换都带有一个约束，指定触发状态更改的输入条件以及必须满足的任何空间或时间要求。学习引擎通过观察模式并推断捕获其结构所需的状态和转换来构建 FSM。An FSM provides pattern representation as a directed graph with states as nodes and transitions as edges. Each transition carries a constraint specifying the input condition that triggers the state change and any spatial or temporal requirements that must hold. The learning engine constructs an FSM by observing a pattern and inferring what states and transitions are necessary to capture its structure.

例如，如果显示一个正方形，学习引擎会观察具有特定空间约束的四个连续线段：每个线段以 90 度角连接到下一个线段，第四个线段连接回第一个线段。生成的 FSM 包含四种状态（每侧一个），并带有由几何约束标记的转换。For instance, if a square is displayed, the learning engine observes four sequential line segments with specific spatial constraints: each segment connects to the next at 90degree angles, and the fourth segment connects back to the first. The resulting FSM contains four states (one per side) with transitions labeled by geometric constraints.

这种表示可以直接检查什么输入触发它以及必须保持什么时空关系。Such representation enables direct inspection of what inputs trigger it and what spatio-temporal relationships must hold.

高阶自动机 (HOA)：Higher-Order Automata (HOA):HOA 通过在分层图中组织组件自动机来表示组合。 HOA 图包含 FSM 和其他 HOA 作为子图，通过指定包含和依赖关系的组合边连接。An HOA represents composition by organizing component automata in a hierarchical graph. The HOA graph contains FSMs and other HOAs as subgraphs, connected by compositional edges that specify containment and dependency relationships.

例如，房屋 HOA 包含墙壁（方形 FSM）和屋顶（三角形 FSM）的 FSM 作为组件子图。组合边缘指定了结构约束：墙壁是基础，屋顶位于墙壁之上。For instance, a house HOA contains FSMs for walls (square FSM) and roof (triangle FSM) as component subgraphs. Compositional edges specify structural constraints: walls are the foundation, and the roof sits atop walls.

这种分层组织使得成分检查成为可能。从房屋节点穿过组合边缘，可以看到墙壁和屋顶，每个都有自己的内部结构。Such hierarchical organization enables compositional inspection. Traversing from the house node through compositional edges reveals wall and roof, each with its own internal structure.

学习引擎：Learning Engine:学习引擎通过模式观察的约束推断来构造自动机。当出现新模式时，它会分析结构以识别必要的状态、所需的转换和强制约束。推理机制通过结构分解来运行。模式被分析为组成元素、时间顺序（如果适用）以及组合层次结构。对于每个元素，引擎确定必须满足哪些约束。例如，一个正方形需要四段等长、垂直连接和闭合。施工是立即进行的。如果观察到的模式包含与图形内存中现有自动机匹配的子模式，则引擎将构造引用这些组件的 HOA。否则，它会构建一个将模式视为整体的 FSM。这创建了顺序相关的学习：在房屋之前观察矩形会产生房屋 HOA，而观察房屋首先会产生房屋 FSM。The learning engine constructs automata through constraint inference from pattern observation. When presented with a novel pattern, it analyzes the structure to identify necessary states, required transitions, and mandatory constraints. The inference mechanism operates through structural decomposition. A pattern is analyzed into constituent elements, temporal ordering if applicable, and compositional hierarchy. For each element, the engine determines what constraints must hold. For instance, a square requires four segments of equal length, perpendicular connections, and closure. The construction is immediate. If the observed pattern contains subpatterns matching existing automata in graph memory, the engine constructs an HOA referencing these components. Otherwise, it constructs an FSM treating the pattern as monolithic. This creates order-dependent learning: observing rectangles before houses produces house HOAs, while observing houses first produces house FSMs.

在分析类似模式的后续观察结果时，引擎会区分尺寸变化和结构变化。仅参数值（尺寸、比例）不同的模式与现有的 FSM 结构相匹配，不需要新的表示。具有结构差异的模式（改变的状态序列、不同的约束类型）创建通过相似性检测进行聚类的新 FSM。When analyzing subsequent observations of similar patterns, the engine distinguishes dimensional variations from structural variations. Patterns differing only in parametric values (dimensions, proportions) match existing FSM structure and require no new representation. Patterns with structural differences (altered state sequences, different constraint types) create new FSMs that cluster through similarity detection.

相似度检测：Similarity Detection:相似性检测机制通过比较自动机来识别结构重叠。当两个自动机共享显着的约束结构但细节不同时，该机制会创建按重叠程度加权的相似边缘。相似度是通过比较约束图来计算的。考虑两个方形 FSM：一个轴对齐，一个旋转 45 度。它们共享核心约束结构，但方向不同。相似性检测器创建一条连接它们的边缘，并通过结构重叠进行加权。当我们的系统遇到变化时，相似性边缘就会积累。多个旋转的正方形、拉伸的矩形和梯形连接到具有不同边权重的原始正方形 FSM。由此产生的集群结构支持概念聚类，通过平均路径长度、连接度分布和模块化度量提供可测量的概念稳健性。The similarity detection mechanism compares automata to identify structural overlap. When two automata share significant constraint structure but differ in details, the mechanism creates a similarity edge weighted by the degree of overlap. Similarity is computed by comparing constraint graphs. Consider two square FSMs: one axis-aligned, one rotated 45 degrees. These share core constraint structure but differ in orientation. The similarity detector creates an edge connecting them, weighted by structural overlap. As our system encounters variations, similarity edges accumulate. Multiple rotated squares, stretched rectangles, and trapezoids connect to the original square FSM with varying edge weights. The resulting cluster structure enables concept clustering, providing measurable concept robustness through average path length, connection degree distribution, and modularity metrics.

Current approaches verify understanding indirectly through extensive testing. But adversarial examples demonstrate that high performance does not guarantee robust understanding.

7.4 局限性和未来方向7.4 Limitations and Future Directions

目前的工作展示了对简单几何领域的可测量的理解。四个重要的局限性为未来的研究指明了方向。The current work demonstrates measurable understanding in a simple geometric domain. Four significant limitations suggest directions for future research.

Scaling to complex domains introduces challenges: constraints become less crisp, composition becomes less hierarchical as concepts interconnect in networks, and variation becomes harder to characterize across multiple dimensions.

持续学习Continuous Learning：演示显示了离散的学习片段。现实世界的学习通常是连续的：新模式不断出现，概念逐渐演变，系统必须整合新知识，而不会发生灾难性遗忘。图结构支持连续添加而不破坏现有结构，但随着图的增长，长期内存管理、概念漂移和保持一致性等问题仍然存在。: The demonstration shows discrete learning episodes. Realworld learning is often continuous: new patterns arrive constantly, concepts evolve gradually, and the system must integrate new knowledge without catastrophic forgetting. The graph structure supports continuous addition without disrupting existing structure, but questions remain about long-term memory management, concept drift, and maintaining coherence as the graph grows.

与其他机制的整合Integration with Other Mechanisms：当前的架构侧重于从观察中进行结构学习。它没有涉及智力的其他方面：规划需要时间推理，语言需要句法和语用，社会认知需要心智理论。一个完整的AGI系统需要多种机制协同工作。: The current architecture focuses on structural learning from observation. It does not address other aspects of intelligence: planning requires temporal reasoning, language requires syntax and pragmatics, social cognition requires theory of mind. A complete AGI system needs multiple mechanisms working together.

可扩展性Scalability：演示涉及小图（数十个节点）。现实世界的知识库包含数千或数百万个概念。图操作必须有效扩展。随着图变大，关于计算复杂性的问题仍然存在。: The demonstration involves small graphs (tens of nodes). 这些限制并没有削弱核心贡献：分层自动机使理解可以通过结构属性来衡量。将此框架应用于完整的 AGI 系统将需要扩展该方法来处理复杂性、持续学习、机制集成和规模。These limitations do not undermine the central contribution: hierarchical automata make understanding measurable through structural properties. Applying this framework to full AGI systems will require extending the approach to handle complexity, continuous learning, mechanism integration, and scale.

那么，“我们如何衡量真正的理解呢？”这项工作提出了一个答案：构建系统，在该系统中理解形成会产生我们可以检查的离散结构特征，组成知识在层次图中明确表示，概念稳健性可以通过拓扑度量进行量化，元认知意识可以通过重新配置行为来观察。这与当前通过表现间接衡量理解或通过置信度分数估计理解的方法不同。直接测量需要具有代表性的基础，使理解能够是结构性的而不是统计性的、离散性的而不是连续性的、可检查性的而不是分布式的。So, "how do we measure genuine understanding?" This work proposes an answer: build systems where understanding formation produces discrete structural signatures we can inspect, compositional knowledge is explicitly represented in hierarchical graphs, concept robustness is quantifiable through topology metrics, and metacognitive awareness is observable through reconfiguration behavior. This differs from current approaches that measure understanding indirectly through performance or estimate it through confidence scores. Direct measurement requires representational substrates that make understanding structural rather than statistical, discrete rather than continuous, and inspectable rather than distributed.

从测量几何领域的理解到测量完整 AGI 系统的理解的道路并非微不足道。但我们的框架表明，如果我们专门设计架构来产生可测量的签名，那么理解是可以测量的。这提出了一个新的研究方向，我们会问“我们如何构建理解本质上可测量的人工智能系统？”而不是“我们如何衡量现有人工智能系统的理解？The path from measuring understanding in geometric domains to measuring understanding in full AGI systems is not trivial. But our framework establishes that understanding can be measurable if we design architectures specifically to produce measurable signatures. This suggests a new research direction in which we ask "how do we build AI systems where understanding is inherently measurable?" instead of "how do we measure understanding in existing AI systems?
