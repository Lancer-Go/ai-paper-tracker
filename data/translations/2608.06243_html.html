Weizhen Wang2,3, Yunyun Han2,3, Gengsheng Li1, Xiangzhao Hao1,

摘要Abstract

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）使用自动可验证的结果信号来提高大型语言模型的推理能力，但这些信号通常是稀疏的并且处于序列级别。按策略自蒸馏（OPSD）通过在学生访问的前缀上查询特权教师并提供密集的令牌级分布监督来减轻这种稀疏性。尽管这种密集的监督减轻了信号稀疏性，但我们发现标准 OPSD 仍然没有充分利用推出的时间结构。它为每个局部散度分配相同的系数，无论其位置或发生的散度顺序如何。在同策略自回归生成中，相同的分歧幅度可以遵循不同的差异历史，反映了教师和学生之间不匹配的不同演变。由于仅局部标量无法区分这些时间上下文，因此标准 OPSD 无法调整其标记级别权重以适应实现的差异序列。为了解决这个限制，我们建议Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) improves the reasoning capabilities of large language models using automatically verifiable outcome signals, but these signals are typically sparse and at the sequence-level. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) mitigates this sparsity by querying a privileged teacher at student-visited prefixes and providing dense token-level distributional supervision. Although this dense supervision alleviates signal sparsity, we find that standard OPSD still underexploits the temporal structure of the rollout. It assigns every local divergence the same coefficient, regardless of its position or the divergence sequence in which it occurs. In on-policy autoregressive generation, the same divergence magnitude can follow different discrepancy histories, reflecting different evolutions of the mismatch between the teacher and student. Since the local scalar alone cannot distinguish these temporal contexts, standard OPSD cannot adapt its token-level weights to the realized discrepancy sequence. To address this limitation, we propose分歧自适应监管视野Divergence-Adaptive Supervision Horizons（破折号）。 DASH 将每个局部蒸馏信号与序列级均值之间的差距映射到自适应传播门，然后使用这些门来控制向后多步聚合。通过这样做，DASH 根据生成过程中局部分歧的演变来调整代币级别的监督权重。对三个模型规模的三个数学推理基准的实验表明，DASH 比我们在所有三个规模的每个基准上重新运行的匹配的普通 OPSD 有所改进。 DASH 重复使用 OPSD 已计算的教师和学生分布，因此增益不需要额外的教师或学生前向传递。(DASH). DASH maps the gap between each local distillation signal and the sequence-level mean to an adaptive propagation gate and then uses these gates to control backward multi-step aggregation. By doing so, DASH adjusts token-level supervision weights according to how local divergences evolve during generation. Experiments on three mathematical reasoning benchmarks across three model scales show that DASH improves over our matched vanilla OPSD reruns on every benchmark at all three scales. DASH reuses the teacher and student distributions that OPSD already computes, so the gains require no additional teacher or student forward pass.

1 简介Introduction

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为提高大型语言模型的数学推理和代码生成能力的重要后训练范式Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has become an important post-training paradigm for improving the mathematical reasoning and code generation capabilities of large language models(Lambert et al. 2024; Shao et al. 2024; Guo et al. 2025)。它使用自动可验证信号优化模型，例如答案正确性和程序执行结果. It optimizes models using automatically verifiable signals, such as answer correctness and program execution results(Cobbe et al. 2021; Hendrycks et al. 2021; Chen et al. 2021)，避免昂贵的人类偏好注释, avoiding costly human preference annotations(Ouyang et al. 2022; Rafailov et al. 2023)并有效扩展and scales effectively(Yu et al. 2025)。 然而，标准 RLVR 通常将相同的稀疏序列级结果奖励应用于响应中的所有令牌级决策，从而很难区分它们对最终结果的各自贡献. However, standard RLVR typically applies the same sparse sequence-level outcome reward to all token-level decisions in a response, making it difficult to distinguish their individual contributions to the final outcome(Uesato et al. 2022; Lightman et al. 2024)这种时间信用分配挑战限制了长视野推理中的样本效率和优化稳定性.This temporal credit assignment challenge limits both sample efficiency and optimization stability in long-horizon reasoning(Sutton 1988; Kazemnejad et al. 2024; Parthasarathi et al. 2025).

为了减轻这种粗略的信用分配，最近的工作引入了同策略蒸馏（OPD）和同策略自蒸馏（OPSD）To mitigate this coarse credit assignment, recent work has introduced on-policy distillation (OPD) and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD)(Agarwal et al. 2024; Gu et al. 2024; Zhao et al. 2026)，对学生生成的轨迹提供密集的令牌级监督, which provide dense token-level supervision on student-generated trajectories(Hinton et al. 2015; Zelikman et al. 2022)。在 OPSD 中，学生生成一个响应，而同一模型以参考答案或其他特权信息为条件. In OPSD, the student generates a response, while the same model, conditioned on a reference answer or other privileged information(Vapnik and Vashist 2009; Lopez-Paz et al. 2016)，充当教师并在学生访问的前缀上提供分配监督。, acts as the teacher and provides distributional supervision at the student-visited prefixes.

然而，密集的代币级监督主要缓解了信号稀疏性，同时使推出的时间结构未得到充分利用。这是因为普通 OPSD 使用独立于令牌位置和沿推出时间差异曲线的统一系数来聚合位置分布差异。但在同政策自回归生成中However, dense token-level supervision primarily alleviates signal sparsity, while leaving the temporal structure of the rollout underexploited. This is because vanilla OPSD aggregates position-wise distributional discrepancies using uniform coefficients that are independent of both token position and the temporal discrepancy profile along the rollout. But in on-policy autoregressive generation(Ross et al. 2011; Bengio et al. 2015)，在不同的差异历史之后可能会出现相同的局部差异值，这反映了教师和学生之间的不匹配是如何随着学生访问的前缀而演变的。仅局部标量无法区分这些时间上下文，但普通 OPSD 在每种情况下都会分配相同的显式聚合系数。因此，普通 OPSD 无法使令牌级蒸馏权重适应差异序列的时间演变。, the same local discrepancy value can arise after different discrepancy histories that reflect how the mismatch between teacher and student has evolved over student-visited prefixes. The local scalar alone cannot distinguish these temporal contexts, yet vanilla OPSD assigns the same explicit aggregation coefficient in every case. Consequently, vanilla OPSD cannot adapt token-level distillation weights to the temporal evolution of the discrepancy sequence.

为了使系数分配对时间差异分布敏感，我们建议To make coefficient allocation sensitive to the temporal discrepancy profile, we propose分歧自适应监管视野Divergence-Adaptive Supervision Horizons（DASH），一种针对 OPSD 的散度自适应多步聚合方法。在每个位置，DASH 将局部蒸馏信号与其序列级均值之间的差距转换为自适应传播门(DASH), a divergence-adaptive multi-step aggregation method for OPSD. At each position, DASH converts the gap between the local distillation signal and its sequence-level mean into an adaptive propagation gateλt\lambda_{t}。这些门控制向后多步递归，为每个局部损失产生一个动态系数，该系数由其前面的门路径确定。因此，DASH 用路径相关的序列级目标取代了普通 OPSD 的统一系数分布，其有效监督范围适应实现的差异序列。. These gates control a backward multi-step recursion, yielding a dynamic coefficient for each local loss that is determined by its preceding gate path. DASH thus replaces the uniform coefficient profile of vanilla OPSD with a path-dependent sequence-level objective whose effective supervision horizon adapts to the realized discrepancy sequence.

2.2 符合政策的自蒸馏On-Policy Self-Distillation

按策略自我蒸馏（OPSD）通过在不同背景下作为非特权学生和特权教师使用相同的模型来训练模型自己的推出On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) trains a model on its own rollouts by using the same model under different contexts as an unprivileged student and a privileged teacher(Agarwal et al. 2024; Gu et al. 2024; Zelikman et al. 2022)。教师以参考解决方案或其他特权信息为条件，并在学生访问的前缀上提供令牌级分布监督. The teacher conditions on reference solutions or other privileged information and provides token-level distributional supervision at student-visited prefixes(Zhao et al. 2026; Vapnik and Vashist 2009; Lopez-Paz et al. 2016; Pinto et al. 2018; Weihs et al. 2021)。最近的 OPSD 方法改进了特权监督或令牌权重. Recent OPSD methods refine either privileged supervision or token weighting(Jin et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026; Xie et al. 2026; Lin et al. 2026; Shen et al. 2026; Liu et al. 2026b)。 AVSD 将跨视图共识与来自多个特权教师环境的特定视图信号相结合. These methods improve the supervision source or impose predefined positional weighting. DASH instead focuses on how local distillation signals are aggregated across the rollout. It uses sequence-relative divergence gaps to construct adaptive propagation gates and temporally conditioned token-level coefficients through multi-step aggregation, allowing dense supervision to exploit the temporal evolution of local discrepancies.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们重新审视了在策略自蒸馏中应该如何分配代币级监管的密集度。 Vanilla OPSD 为局部蒸馏信号分配统一的权重，忽略了沿着每个轨迹的师生差异的时间演变。为了解决这个限制，我们提出了分歧自适应监督视野（DASH），一种用于自适应监督分配的序列感知方法。 DASH 将序列相关的散度间隙转换为自适应传播门，并执行后向多步聚合以构建时间条件令牌级权重和自适应监督视野。跨三个数学推理基准和三个模型规模的实验表明，DASH 提高了我们匹配的普通 OPSD 重新运行的四种子平均值，并在比较结果中获得了最高分数。 DASH 重用了普通 OPSD 已经计算出的本地教师和学生分布，并且不需要额外的教师或学生前向传递。我们希望我们的工作能够为改善策略学习和长视野推理中的密集监督分配提供新的见解。In this work, we revisit how dense token-level supervision should be allocated in on-policy self-distillation. Vanilla OPSD assigns uniform weights to local distillation signals, overlooking the temporal evolution of teacher–student discrepancies along each trajectory. To address this limitation, we propose Divergence-Adaptive Supervision Horizons (DASH), a sequence-aware method for adaptive supervision allocation. DASH transforms sequence-relative divergence gaps into adaptive propagation gates and performs backward multi-step aggregation to construct temporally conditioned token-level weights and adaptive supervision horizons. Experiments across three mathematical reasoning benchmarks and three model scales show that DASH improves the four-seed mean of our matched vanilla OPSD reruns and obtains the highest scores among the compared results. DASH reuses the local teacher and student distributions already computed by vanilla OPSD and requires no additional teacher or student forward passes. We hope our work can offer new insights for improving dense supervision allocation in on-policy learning and long-horizon reasoning.
