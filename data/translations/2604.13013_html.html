摘要Abstract

本文通过双层优化框架解决电动电容车路由问题（E-CVRP），该框架根据搜索阶段单独或联合处理路由和充电决策。通过分析它们的相互作用，我们在上层引入了一个替代目标来指导搜索并加速收敛。引入了双层后期接受爬山算法 (b-LAHC)，该算法通过三个阶段进行操作：贪婪下降、邻域探索和最终解决方案细化。 b-LAHC 以固定参数运行，无需复杂的调整，同时保持轻量和有效。 IEEE WCCI-2020 基准的大量实验表明，b-LAHC 相对于八种最先进的算法实现了卓越或有竞争力的性能。在固定的评估预算下，它在小规模实例上获得了接近最优的解决方案，并在大规模基准上创下了 9/10 的新的最著名结果，将现有记录平均提高了 1.07%。此外，代理目标和完整成本之间观察到的强相关性（尽管不是普遍的）证明了代理目标的使用是合理的，同时仍然需要两个级别的联合解决方案，从而验证了所提出的双层框架的有效性，并突出了其有效解决具有层次结构的大规模路由问题的潜力。This paper tackles the Electric Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (E-CVRP) through a bilevel optimization framework that handles routing and charging decisions separately or jointly depending on the search stage. By analyzing their interaction, we introduce a surrogate objective at the upper level to guide the search and accelerate convergence. A bilevel Late Acceptance Hill Climbing algorithm (b-LAHC) is introduced that operates through three phases: greedy descent, neighborhood exploration, and final solution refinement. b-LAHC operates with fixed parameters, eliminating the need for complex adaptation while remaining lightweight and effective. Extensive experiments on the IEEE WCCI-2020 benchmark show that b-LAHC achieves superior or competitive performance against eight state-of-the-art algorithms. Under a fixed evaluation budget, it attains near-optimal solutions on small-scale instances and sets 9/10 new best-known results on large-scale benchmarks, improving existing records by an average of 1.07%. Moreover, the strong correlation (though not universal) observed between the surrogate objective and the complete cost justifies the use of the surrogate objective while still necessitating a joint solution of both levels, thereby validating the effectiveness of the proposed bilevel framework and highlighting its potential for efficiently solving large-scale routing problems with a hierarchical structure.

I 简介Introduction

随着全球向可持续交通的转变加速，电动汽车 (EV) 已成为道路物流脱碳的核心。在此背景下，电动汽车路径问题（EVRP）成为一个关键的研究前沿。其核心变体，电动电容车辆路径问题（E-CVRP）As the global shift toward sustainable transportation accelerates, electric vehicles (EVs) have become central to decarbonizing road-based logistics. In this context, the Electric Vehicle Routing Problem (EVRP) has emerged as a key research frontier. Its core variant, the Electric Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (E-CVRP)[20]，构成电动汽车物流的基础。扩展包含非线性充电、异构车队和时间窗口约束等功能。因此，解决 E-CVRP 的方法论进步至关重要，它支撑着从城市最后一英里交付到长途货运的应用。, forms the foundation of EV logistics. Extensions incorporate features such as non-linear charging, heterogeneous fleets, and time-window constraints. Methodological advances in solving E-CVRP are therefore crucial, underpinning applications from urban last-mile delivery to long-haul freight.

给定一支同质电动汽车车队，E-CVRP 的目标是在考虑电池充电和车辆货运能力限制的情况下，确定每辆车的最有效路线。每辆车在为一组客户提供服务时必须在一个仓库开始和结束。图。Given a fleet of homogeneous EVs, the objective of E-CVRP is to determine the most efficient route for each vehicle that respects battery charge and vehicle cargo capacity constraints. E-CVRP 可以被视为经典的容量车辆路径问题 (CVRP) 的组合The E-CVRP can be viewed as a combination of the classical Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP)[32]和绿色车辆路径问题（GVRP）and the Green Vehicle Routing Problem (GVRP)[5]。前者考虑载货能力限制，而后者则在载货能力无限的假设下考虑有限的行驶里程。 E-CVRP 同时考虑车辆容量和行驶里程限制。它也是具有中间停靠点的车辆路径问题 (VRPIS) 系列的成员. The former considers cargo capacity constraints, whereas the latter incorporates limited driving range under the assumption of unlimited cargo capacity. The E-CVRP simultaneously accounts for both vehicle capacity and driving range constraints. It is also a member of the family of Vehicle Routing Problems with Intermediate Stops (VRPIS)[29]，其中引入了可选的途中停靠点以支持服务运营。 VRPIS 通常出现在三种应用环境中：补给和处置操作、休息或闲置期以及加油。 E-CVRP属于加油类别。, where optional en-route stops are introduced to support service operations. VRPIS generally arise in three application contexts: replenishment and disposal operations, rest or idling periods, and refueling. The E-CVRP belongs to the refueling category.

First, at the modeling level, existing formulations either produce intractably large search spaces[20]或依靠积极的修剪策略or rely on aggressive pruning strategies[8]冒着放弃高质量解决方案的风险。它们通常无法有效地扩展到大规模实例。其次，从算法的角度来看，大多数现有方法依赖于高度定制的、特定于问题的启发式方法，通常嵌入模糊的概念框架中that risk discarding high‑quality solutions. They often fail to scale effectively to large‑scale instances. Second, from an algorithmic perspective, most existing approaches rely on highly customized, problem‑specific heuristics, often embedded within vague conceptual frameworks[12, 13, 28, 4]。虽然有效，但这种启发式方法难以复制、调整和适应。这些限制催生了一种可降低计算复杂性的建模方法，以及一种能够扩展到大型实例的透明通用解决方案。. While effective, such heuristics are difficult to reproduce, tune, and adapt. These limitations motivate a modeling approach that reduces computational complexity, together with a transparent, general-purpose solution approach capable of scaling to large instances.

从根本上来说，这些困难源于电动车队特定的领域特征。与传统的 CVRP 相比，E-CVRP 的主要额外挑战来自于电动汽车电池容量所带来的有限续航里程。与可以在几分钟内加满燃料的内燃机汽车不同，电动汽车需要更长的充电时间，使得绕道充电在运营时间和调度方面成本高昂。在现实场景中，电动汽车甚至可能需要中断其路线，以便在单次行程中进行多次充电。从充电基础设施的角度来看，有限的充电站数量意味着每个充电站可能会被相同或不同的车辆访问一次、多次或根本不访问。车辆路由、电池管理和充电基础设施之间的这些相互依赖性带来了独特的建模挑战，并显着增加了大规模求解 E-CVRP 的计算复杂性。At the root, these difficulties stem from domain characteristics specific to electric fleets. Compared to the classical CVRP, the main additional challenge in E-CVRP arises from the limited cruising range imposed by EV battery capacity. Unlike internal combustion engine vehicles, which can be refueled within minutes, EVs require significantly longer charging times, making detours for recharging costly in terms of operational time and scheduling. In real-world scenarios, EVs may even need to interrupt their routes for multiple recharges within a single trip. From the charging infrastructure perspective, the limited number of stations implies that each of them may be visited once, multiple times, or not at all, possibly by the same or different vehicles. These interdependencies between vehicle routing, battery management, and charging infrastructure create unique modeling challenges and significantly increase the computational complexity in solving E-CVRP at a large scale.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A EVRP 的解决方案Solution Approaches for EVRPs

根据现代 EVRP 的规模和现实性，解决方案方法大致分为两类：精确法和启发式。每个都提供互补的优势和权衡。Depending on the scale and realism of modern EVRPs, solution approaches broadly fall into two families: exact and heuristic. Each offers complementary strengths and trade-offs.

精确方法对于小规模实例非常有效，但由于指数复杂性而扩展性较差。大多数依赖 MILP 公式和商业求解器或专门的分支定界框架。左Exact methods are highly effective for small-scale instances but scale poorly due to exponential complexity. Most rely on MILP formulations and commercial solvers or specialized branch-and-bound frameworks. Zuo等人。et al. [38]采用基于割线的分段线性化来近似凹非线性充电，使用 CPLEX 求解最多 25 个客户和 4 个站点的实例。坎查拉和拉马杜赖employed secant-based piecewise linearization to approximate the concave nonlinear charging, solving instances with up to 25 customers and 4 stations using CPLEX. Kancharla and Ramadurai[14]通过非线性充电和负载相关放电对此进行了扩展，利用 Gurobi 解决了多达 30 个客户和 4 个充电站的实例。塔哈米extended this with nonlinear charging and load-dependent discharging, solving instances with up to 30 customers and 4 stations with Gurobi. Tahami等人。et al. [31]将分支剪切法应用于最多有 30 个客户和 21 个充电站的 E-CVRP 实例。最近，林applied branch-and-cut to E-CVRP instances with up to 30 customers and 21 charging stations. More recently, Lam等人。et al. [16]提出了针对带时间窗的电动汽车路径问题 (EVRPTW) 的分支切割定价方案，该问题结合了分段线性充电和容量充电站，并在最多 100 个客户的实例上进行了测试。纳夫斯塔德proposed a branch-and-cut-and-price for an Electric Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (EVRPTW) that incorporates piecewise-linear recharging and capacitated charging stations, tested on instances with up to 100 customers. VI 结论和未来的工作Conclusions and Future Work

本文介绍了一种用于 E-CVRP 双层公式化的双层后期接受爬山算法（b-LAHC）。通过利用上层的替代目标和下层的高效充电优化器，b-LAHC 是轻量级且可解释的。尽管使用固定参数，它仍可提供跨实例的高效搜索。This paper introduced a bilevel late acceptance hill climbing algorithm (b-LAHC) for the bilevel formulation of the E-CVRP. By leveraging a surrogate objective at the upper level with an efficient charging optimizer at the lower level, b-LAHC is lightweight and interpretable. Despite using fixed parameters, it delivers efficient search across instances.

与最先进的方法相比，b-LAHC 遵循独特的设计理念。 HHASA-TSCompared with state-of-the-art methods, b-LAHC follows a distinct design philosophy. HHASA-TS[28]将 SA 与强化学习相结合，动态调整搜索，提高适应性，但会带来计算开销和大型实例上更高的可变性。 CBMAintegrates SA with reinforcement learning to dynamically adjust the search, improving adaptiveness but incurring computational overhead and higher variability on large instances. CBMA[27]和 CBACO-Iand CBACO-I[13]在基于群体的框架中采用自适应多样性控制，可以对小型/中型实例进行有效探索，但在较大基准上表现出收敛速度较慢且鲁棒性降低。值得注意的是，b-LAHC 作为一种轻量级单点方法获得了可比或更好的结果，而不依赖于复杂的自适应机制。这些发现提出了两个方向：将适应性学习纳入 b-LAHC 中，或将 b-LAHC 中的有前途的功能纳入基于人群的框架中。employ adaptive diversity control in population-based frameworks, enabling effective exploration on small/medium instances but showing slower convergence and reduced robustness on larger benchmarks. Notably, b-LAHC attains comparable or superior results as a lightweight single-point method without relying on complex adaptive mechanisms. These findings suggest two directions: incorporating adaptive learning into b-LAHC or incorporating promising features in b-LAHC into a population-based framework.

有趣的是，M8算子扮演的角色比预想的更关键。在某些情况下（例如 E30），由于充电绕道和电池限制，最佳解决方案需要更多车辆。如果没有 M8，搜索会过早收敛到车辆较少的次优解决方案。这凸显了路线创建举措对于处理复杂的电动汽车充电行为的重要性。Interestingly, M8 operator plays a more critical role than expected. In some instances (e.g., E30), optimal solutions require more vehicles due to charging detours and battery limits. Without M8, the search converges prematurely to suboptimal solutions featuring fewer vehicles. This highlights the importance of route-creation moves for handling complex EV charging behaviors.

这些实验为所提出的双层框架提供了强有力的支持，该框架利用了 E-CVRP 中上层路由决策的主导地位，同时提供了一种原则性机制来确定何时引入联合优化，从而确保在不同问题实例中具有更大的通用性和适应性。The experiments provide strong support for the proposed bilevel framework, which leverages the dominance of upper-level routing decisions in the E-CVRP, while offering a principled mechanism to determine when to introduce joint optimization, thereby ensuring greater generality and adaptability across diverse problem instances.

未来的研究可以在三个主要方向上进行。首先，我们计划通过合并学习机制来扩展当前的 b-LAHC 算法，以在搜索过程中实现自适应行为。其次，我们的目标是将单点框架中的有用特征纳入能够进行多模式搜索的基于群体的元启发式算法中，从而允许对单点策略和基于群体的策略进行系统比较。第三，所提出的双层优化模型可以应用于具有分层决策结构的其他路由问题，为解决 E-CVRP 之外的双层路由挑战提供统一的视角。Future research can proceed in three main directions. First, we plan to extend the current b-LAHC algorithm by incorporating learning mechanisms to enable adaptive behavior during the search. Second, we aim to incorporate useful features in the single-point framework into a population-based metaheuristic capable of multimodal search, allowing a systematic comparison between single-point and population-based strategies. Third, the proposed bilevel optimization model can be applied to other routing problems with hierarchical decision structures, offering a unified perspective for tackling bilevel routing challenges beyond the E-CVRP.
