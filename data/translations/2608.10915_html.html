ComBodied Agents：以人为中心的智能体人工智能的新范式ComBodied Agents: a New Paradigm of Human-Centric Agentic AI

Qianggang Ding1,2,*,†\dagger, Xingyao Wang3,*, Rui Feng4, Zhibin Wang5, Feixiang Wang5, Kelong Mao6, Hao Sun7, Zhiyao Luo8, Jiankai Tang9, Lei Li10, Jiadong Guo11, Minheng Ni12, Weicong Lin13, Chenxi Yang14, Hongxiang Gao14, Zhenghua Chen15, Yang Bai3, Min Wu3, Jun Cheng3, Huazhu Fu3, Dacheng Tao16, Bang Liu1,2,†\dagger

摘要Abstract

当老年人错过服药剂量后，软件代理可以发出另一个提醒，并且实体代理可以带来药物。然而，这两种行为都无法解释患者是否忘记、感到困惑、是否正在经历副作用或故意拒绝药物，也无法确定哪种支持是适当的。这种模糊性暴露了 Agentic AI 的结构性差距。数字代理主要围绕软件状态的转换进行组织，而实体代理则围绕物理状态的转换进行组织。这两种范式都没有将一个人不断变化的状态和能动性作为建模、干预和评估的主要对象。我们引入组合智能体作为以人为中心的智能体人工智能范例，随着时间的推移感知、建模、预测和支持个体人类状态轨迹。软件工具、传感器、可穿戴设备、机器人和人类服务只是行动渠道而不是最终目标。尽管个人助理、健康代理、人工智能伴侣和自适应人类人工智能系统实例化了这一愿景的部分内容，但它们的能力在记忆、个性化、感知、陪伴和特定领域支持方面仍然支离破碎。我们将这些能力综合成一个统一的闭环。基于事件的多模态感知重建有关有意义的个人事件的证据；纵向和可纠正的记忆提供了时间背景；个人世界模型将纵向事件证据和当前背景转化为未来个人状态、可观察事件以及替代决策和干预下的结果的校准分布；可接受的干预政策在同意、不确定性、安全性、可逆性和用户控制的约束下选择适当的支持。来自人和环境的反馈会更新随后的感知、记忆、预测和干预。该框架不需要详尽的人类数字孪生，而是使用有目的的、不确定性感知的和用户可纠正的人的表示。我们通过人类国家目标、关系上下文和代理角色进一步组织设计空间；研究向边缘原生个人模型的转变；并提出以场景为中心的评估、机构保护指标、基准要求和治理方向。通过将代理人工智能从外部任务完成转向持续的人类利益，组合代理旨在改善健康、学习、判断、能力、关系和目标追求，而不将参与、依赖或最大自动化视为成功的衡量标准。After an older adult misses a medication dose, a software agent can issue another reminder and an embodied agent can bring the medication. Yet neither action explains whether the person forgot, is confused, is experiencing side effects, or has deliberately refused the medication, nor does it determine what support would be appropriate. This ambiguity exposes a structural gap in Agentic AI. Digital Agents are primarily organized around transformations of software states, while Embodied Agents are organized around transformations of physical states. Neither paradigm makes the evolving state and agency of a person the primary object of modeling, intervention, and evaluation. We introduce Combodied Agents as a human-centered paradigm of Agentic AI that perceives, models, predicts, and supports individual human-state trajectories over time. Software tools, sensors, wearables, robots, and human services serve as action channels rather than final objectives. Although personal assistants, health agents, AI companions, and adaptive human–AI systems instantiate parts of this vision, their capabilities remain fragmented across memory, personalization, sensing, companionship, and domain-specific support. We synthesize these capabilities into a unified closed loop. Event-based multimodal perception reconstructs evidence about meaningful personal events; longitudinal and correctable memory provides temporal context; Personal World Models transform longitudinal event evidence and current context into calibrated distributions over future personal states, observable events, and outcomes under alternative decisions and interventions; and an admissible intervention policy selects proportionate support under constraints of consent, uncertainty, safety, reversibility, and user control. Feedback from the person and environment updates subsequent perception, memory, prediction, and intervention. Rather than requiring an exhaustive Human Digital Twin, this framework uses purpose-bounded, uncertainty-aware, and user-correctable representations of the person. We further organize the design space through human-state targets, relational contexts, and agent roles; examine the transition toward edge-native personal models; and propose scenario-centered evaluation, agency-preservation metrics, benchmark requirements, and governance directions. By shifting Agentic AI from external task completion toward sustained human benefit, Combodied Agents aim to improve health, learning, judgment, capability, relationships, and goal pursuit without treating engagement, dependence, or maximum automation as measures of success.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 历来主要是为了解决任务而开发和评估的。早期的面向任务的系统解释请求、跟踪有界状态、查询特定于域的后端并返回答案或操作Artificial intelligence (AI) has historically been developed and evaluated primarily for task solving. Earlier task-oriented systems interpreted requests, tracked bounded states, queried domain-specific backends, and returned answers or actions[107]。大型语言模型 (LLM) 和多模态基础模型已将此模式扩展为通用代理，可以根据上下文进行推理、分解目标、调用工具、维护记忆、执行多步骤计划并根据反馈进行调整. Large language models (LLMs) and multimodal foundation models have expanded this pattern into general-purpose agents that reason over context, decompose goals, call tools, maintain memory, execute multi-step plans, and adapt from feedback[103, 84]。组织问题已经从人工智能能否回答问题转变为它可以独立完成多少扩展任务。. The organizing question has shifted from whether AI can answer to how much of an extended task it can complete on its own.

这种趋势在代理人工智能的两个主要分支中都很明显。数字代理导航界面、修改代码、调用应用程序编程接口 (API) 并协调数字工作流程This trend is visible across the two dominant branches of Agentic AI. Digital Agents navigate interfaces, modify code, invoke application programming interfaces (APIs), and coordinate digital workflows[109, 101, 43]。具身代理将语言和多模式感知与导航、操作和物理控制联系起来. Embodied Agents connect language and multimodal perception to navigation, manipulation, and physical control[13, 22, 12]。在这两个分支中，进步意味着更长的任务期限、更可靠的行动和更少的人为监督. In both branches, progress means longer task horizons, more reliable action, and less human supervision[63, 97]。这些进步降低了成本并使困难的工作自动化，但它们也使对进展的不完整描述正常化：从人类方面消失的工作越多，代理的能力就越强. These advances reduce costs and automate difficult work, but they also normalize an incomplete account of progress: the more work that disappears from the human side, the more capable the agent appears[62, 92].

这种说法忽略了任务绩效和人类发展可能存在差异。人工智能可能会改进文档，但会让作者对其理解程度较低，或者加速决策，但会削弱用户判断其依据的能力。对人工智能辅助知识工作和决策的研究同样表明，当人们无法确定何时接受、验证或拒绝模型输出时，认知努力会减少并过度依赖What this account misses is that task performance and human development can diverge. AI may improve a document while leaving its author with less understanding, or accelerate a decision while weakening the user’s ability to judge its basis. Studies of AI-assisted knowledge work and decision making likewise report reduced cognitive effort and overreliance when people cannot determine when to accept, verify, or reject model outputs[52, 94]。任务成功指标捕捉即时输出，但不捕捉重复使用后留下的能力或判断力。. Task-success metrics capture immediate output, but not the capability or judgment that repeated use leaves with the person.

这种担忧还具有社会层面。人类知识往往是本地的、隐性的，并通过参与而更新。用标准化模型输出代替这些实践可能会削弱专业知识、多样性和弹性。价值观产生了一个并行的问题：通用模型是在相对较少的机构中开发和协调的，而用户在文化、承诺、关系和美好生活的愿景方面存在差异。因此，集中智力同时削弱人们的生产性和认知能动性可以集中权力来定义目标和可接受的价值观The concern also has a societal dimension. Human knowledge is often local, tacit, and renewed through participation. Substituting standardized model outputs for those practices may weaken expertise, variety, and resilience. Values create a parallel problem: general-purpose models are developed and aligned in relatively few institutions, whereas users differ in culture, commitments, relationships, and visions of a good life. Concentrating intelligence while weakening people’s productive and epistemic agency can therefore concentrate the authority to define both goals and acceptable values[92].

私人代理人使这些紧张局势变得尤为明显。具有记忆功能的助手、人工智能伴侣、健康代理人和行为教练越来越多地参与身份、情感、习惯和护理。它们可以提供连续性和有意义的支持，但持续的互动也可能会助长依赖、操纵、社会流离失所或不当影响，尤其是在未成年人和弱势用户中Personal agents make these tensions especially visible. Memory-enabled assistants, AI companions, health agents, and behavioral coaches increasingly participate in identity, emotion, habits, and care. They can provide continuity and meaningful support, but sustained interaction may also foster dependence, manipulation, social displacement, or inappropriate influence, especially among minors and vulnerable users[61, 76, 28, 90, 16]。自动化本身并不是问题；问题在于自动化。问题在于，无论替代对理解、自主性、关系、价值观和未来能力的长期影响如何，都将替代作为默认选择。. Automation itself is not the problem; the problem is making substitution the default regardless of its long-term effects on understanding, autonomy, relationships, values, and future capability.

因此我们主张建立We therefore argue for building人类的人工智能AI for humans：人工智能扩展了人类的意志和判断力，并随着时间的推移使人们变得更有能力。以人为中心的人工智能长期以来一直认为，智能系统应该扩展人们可以安全、有意义地做的事情，同时保持可靠、可理解和可控: AI that extends human will and judgment and makes people more capable over time. Human-centered AI has long held that intelligent systems should expand what people can safely and meaningfully do while remaining reliable, understandable, and controllable[88, 2]。最近关于以人为中心的代理的研究通过将用户保留和发展的内容（理解、能力、代理、身份和校准依赖）视为任务完成的结果，使这一原则变得可操作. Recent work on Human-Centered Agents makes this principle operational by treating what users retain and develop—understanding, competence, agency, identity, and calibrated reliance—as an outcome alongside task completion[62]。因此，优化目标从孤立的任务成功扩展到直接利益加上纵向人类收益。. The optimization target thus expands from isolated task success to immediate benefit plus longitudinal human gain.

人类人工智能不需要每个低级循环中都有人，也不需要为了自动化而抵制自动化。相反，人类参与变成了一个技术设计问题：代理应该学习何时独立行动、请求监督或将知识和能力返还给人员AI for humans does not require people in every low-level loop or resist automation for its own sake. Human participation instead becomes a technical design problem: an agent should learn when to act independently, request oversight, or return knowledge and capability to the person[92]。例行的、低风险的、可逆的任务可能证明近乎完全的授权是合理的；学习、健康、情感支持和高风险决策可能需要解释、脚手架、同意或人工升级。分工应适合个人的目标、状态、能力、关系和风险，同时保留选择、拒绝、纠正和恢复的能力。. Routine, low-risk, reversible tasks may justify near-complete delegation; learning, health, emotional support, and high-stakes decisions may require explanation, scaffolding, consent, or human escalation. The division of labor should fit the person’s goals, state, competence, relationships, and risk while preserving the ability to choose, refuse, correct, and recover.

Digital tools, wearables, robots, and human services may all serve as action channels, but success is determined by what the person can understand, decide, do, and sustain over time. 组合智能体与人类数字孪生 (HDT) 共享以个体为中心的纵向视角，但两种范式定义了不同的系统边界。 HDT 旨在通过持续的数据同步和反馈来维护人类或人类特定用例方面的动态更新的数字表示。这种表示可以集成多模式和多尺度数据以支持监测、模拟、预测和优化Combodied Agents share with Human Digital Twins (HDTs) an individual-centered and longitudinal perspective, but the two paradigms define different system boundaries. An HDT aims to maintain a dynamically updated digital representation of a human, or of a use-case-specific aspect of a human, through ongoing data synchronization and feedback. Such a representation may integrate multimodal and multi-scale data to support monitoring, simulation, prediction, and optimization[59, 51, 48].

一个完整的、高保真度的完整人的双胞胎仍然是一个愿望，而不是目前可以实现的系统。人类生理学、认知、行为、关系和环境在交互的时间尺度上不断发展，而感知仍然不完整，模型验证、同步、计算、隐私和治理仍然是巨大的挑战A holistic, high-fidelity twin of a complete person remains an aspiration rather than a currently attainable system. Human physiology, cognition, behavior, relationships, and context evolve across interacting time scales, while sensing remains incomplete and model validation, synchronization, computation, privacy, and governance remain substantial challenges[51, 95, 81]。因此，组合代理不需要该人的详尽数字复制品。他们维护商定的支持环境所需方面的有目的的、不确定性感知的和用户可纠正的表示，并将这些表示与纵向记忆、目标协商、干预政策和反馈联系起来。他们的主要目标不是最大程度的再现保真度，而是在保持人类能动性的同时，安全、有益地参与个人不断变化的环境。特定领域的 HDT 可以向组合智能体提供证据或预测，但它本身并不定义智能体的记忆、权限、交互策略或与人的关系。. Combodied Agents therefore do not require an exhaustive digital replica of the person. They maintain purpose-bounded, uncertainty-aware, and user-correctable representations of the aspects needed for an agreed support context, and connect those representations to longitudinal memory, goal negotiation, intervention policies, and feedback. Their primary objective is not maximal representational fidelity, but safe and beneficial participation across the person’s evolving contexts while preserving human agency. A domain-specific HDT may supply evidence or predictions to a Combodied Agent, but it does not by itself define the agent’s memory, authority, interaction policy, or relationship with the person.

个人助理、健康代理、人工智能伴侣、行为教练和自适应人类人工智能系统已经部分实现了这一愿景。仍然缺少的是围绕纵向人类国家轨迹、干预反应、适应性分工以及对利益和保留机构的评估组织的统一范式。 Combodied Agents 将人类的人工智能转化为这项技术研究计划。Personal assistants, health agents, AI companions, behavioral coaches, and adaptive human–AI systems already instantiate parts of this vision. What remains missing is a unified paradigm organized around longitudinal human-state trajectories, intervention responses, an adaptive division of labor, and evaluation of both benefit and preserved agency. Combodied Agents translate AI for humans into this technical research program.

They add multimodal human-state perception, longitudinal and correctable memory, personal world models (PWMs) of intervention response, calibrated and reversible support policies, and explicit evaluation of human gain and agency preservation.

对于纵向支持，重要的文本事件通常是变化而不是孤立的事实。用户可以制定新目标、修改偏好、撤回同意、报告不成功的干预或纠正代理先前存储的内容。经验抽样研究显示了收集接近经验时刻的报告的价值For longitudinal support, the important textual events are usually changes rather than isolated facts. A user may formulate a new goal, revise a preference, withdraw consent, report an unsuccessful intervention, or correct something that the agent previously stored. Experience-sampling research shows the value of collecting reports close to the moment of experience[87]，而 MemoryBank 等系统和 LongMemEval 等基准测试则研究跨扩展交互历史的连续性, while systems such as MemoryBank and benchmarks such as LongMemEval investigate continuity across extended interaction histories[108, 100]。组合代理为这一系列工作添加了治理要求：人员必须能够区分他们明确所说的内容与系统推断的内容，并且以后的更正应该更新个人模型，而不会删除原始声明的出处。. A Combodied Agent adds a governance requirement to this line of work: the person must be able to distinguish what they explicitly said from what the system inferred, and later corrections should update the personal model without erasing the provenance of the original claim.

文本是明确的，但不一定是客观的。报告可能是不完整的、符合社会需要的、情绪化的、事后扭曲的，或者是由代理人的问题决定的。因此，语言对于代表用户授权的目标和界限特别有价值，但它仍然是证据来源之一，而不是行为或福祉的完整说明。Text is explicit but not necessarily objective. Reports may be incomplete, socially desirable, emotionally amplified, retrospectively distorted, or shaped by the agent’s question. Language is therefore particularly valuable for representing user-authorized goals and boundaries, but it remains one source of evidence rather than a complete account of behavior or wellbeing.

3.2 语音和音频信号Speech and Audio Signals

文本记录了所说的内容，但删除了大部分所说的内容。语音通过音高、强度、节奏、停顿、犹豫、清晰度、流畅性和语音质量来恢复时间和副语言信息。周围的音频还可能包含非语言的事件，包括警报、咳嗽、哭泣、碰撞或声音环境的变化。Text records what is said but removes much of how it is said. Speech restores temporal and paralinguistic information through pitch, intensity, rhythm, pauses, hesitation, articulation, fluency, and voice quality. The surrounding audio may also contain events that are not linguistic, including alarms, coughing, crying, collisions, or changes in the sound environment.

这些信号通过技术和社会上不同的配置到达代理。手机、电脑、耳塞和听力设备中的近场麦克风主要捕捉有意的交互。接触式或喉式麦克风可减少一些环境干扰，但需要佩戴设备。房间、车辆或智能扬声器中的远场麦克风阵列提供更广泛的覆盖范围，同时还增加了记录旁观者和与支持目的无关的活动的可能性。因此，对话绑定或语音触发的采集与连续环境收听有本质上的不同，即使两者最终都产生音频流。These signals reach an agent through technically and socially different configurations. Near-field microphones in phones, computers, earbuds, and hearing devices mainly capture deliberate interaction. Contact or throat microphones reduce some environmental interference but require the device to be worn. Far-field microphone arrays in rooms, vehicles, or smart speakers provide broader coverage, while also increasing the likelihood of recording bystanders and activities unrelated to the support purpose. Conversation-bound or voice-triggered acquisition is therefore materially different from continuous ambient listening, even when both ultimately produce an audio stream.

语音研究传统上将语音活动检测、说话人分类、语音识别、声学特征提取和环境声音检测分开。引入 GeMAPS 和 eGeMAPS 使声学分析更具可重复性Speech research has traditionally separated voice activity detection, speaker diarization, speech recognition, acoustic feature extraction, and environmental sound detection. GeMAPS and eGeMAPS were introduced to make acoustic analysis more reproducible[27]，而 MERBench 将语音与多模态情感识别中的语言和视觉证据放在一起, while MERBench places speech alongside linguistic and visual evidence in multimodal emotion recognition[56]。这些资源提供了有用的组成部分，但纵向陪伴引入了一个不同的问题：观察到的变化对于这个特定的说话者来说是否不寻常，并且持续足够长的时间以构成相关事件。在熟悉的任务中反复犹豫可能会提供信息；吵闹的谈话中的停顿通常不是这样的。. These resources provide useful components, but longitudinal companionship introduces a different question: whether an observed change is unusual for this particular speaker and persists long enough to constitute a relevant event. Repeated hesitation during a familiar task may be informative; one pause in a noisy conversation is usually not.

声学特征仍然是间接证据。停顿可能是由于思考、分心、疲劳、网络延迟或认知困难造成的，并且语音特征因语言、健康状况、设备和环境而异。因此，本地特征提取、短暂的音频缓冲区、显式激活指示器和可靠的说话者归因比默认情况下保留原始录音更合适。Acoustic features remain indirect evidence. Pauses may result from reflection, distraction, fatigue, network delay, or cognitive difficulty, and voice characteristics vary with language, health, device, and environment. Local feature extraction, short-lived audio buffers, explicit activation indicators, and reliable speaker attribution are consequently more appropriate than retaining raw recordings by default.

3.3 基于视觉的传感Vision-Based Sensing

视觉传感将感知从交互通道扩大到可见行为和周围活动。手机和电脑上面向设备的摄像头捕捉直接交互过程中的表情、凝视和姿势。可穿戴相机或智能眼镜提供活动和物体使用的第一人称视图，而固定相机则提供房间、运动和多人事件的更广泛视图。当照明、距离、时间分辨率或特定隐私限制很重要时，深度、红外、热、眼睛跟踪和基于事件的传感器扩展了这些布置。Visual sensing broadens perception from the interaction channel to visible behavior and surrounding activity. Device-facing cameras on phones and computers capture expressions, gaze, and posture during direct interaction. Wearable cameras or smart glasses provide a first-person view of activities and object use, while fixed cameras offer a wider view of rooms, movement, and multi-person events. Depth, infrared, thermal, eye-tracking, and event-based sensors extend these arrangements when illumination, distance, temporal resolution, or particular privacy constraints matter.

感兴趣的单位很少是一个孤立的框架。一个有用的系统需要识别诸如开始进餐、服药、离开家、完成锻炼、与他人互动或干预后行为改变等事件。 OpenFace 演示了从传统相机中实时提取面部标志、头部姿势、面部动作单元和眼睛注视The unit of interest is rarely an isolated frame. A useful system needs to recognize episodes such as beginning a meal, taking medication, leaving home, completing an exercise, interacting with another person, or changing behavior after an intervention. OpenFace demonstrates real-time extraction of facial landmarks, head pose, facial action units, and eye gaze from conventional cameras[6]。 Ego4D 通过大规模的以自我为中心的视频和涉及情景记忆、物体交互、社交活动和预期的任务，向日常第一人称感知迈进. Ego4D moves toward everyday first-person perception through large-scale egocentric video and tasks involving episodic memory, object interaction, social activity, and anticipation[33]。这些研究方向提出了组合智能体的组成部分，但它们本身并没有解决决定哪些视觉事件对特定人重要的纵向问题。. These lines of research suggest components for Combodied Agents, but they do not by themselves solve the longitudinal problem of deciding which visual episodes matter to a particular person.

视觉解释的局限性尤其重要。面部运动减少可能反映出注意力集中、文化、残疾、疲劳、灯光或相机位置，而不是低情绪。遮挡、身份错误和依赖于上下文的手势会带来更多的歧义。因此，视觉事件应保留采集视点、图像质量、主题归因置信度以及其他人的存在。如果可能，应在本地处理原始图像并替换为特定目的的事件描述。相机可以捕捉家庭场景这一事实并不意味着每个可见的人或物体都属于用户的记忆。The limits of visual interpretation are especially important. Reduced facial movement may reflect concentration, culture, disability, fatigue, lighting, or camera position rather than low affect. Occlusion, identity errors, and context-dependent gestures introduce further ambiguity. A visual event should therefore retain the acquisition viewpoint, image quality, subject-attribution confidence, and presence of other people. Where possible, raw images should be processed locally and replaced by purpose-specific event descriptions. The fact that a camera can capture a household scene does not mean that every visible person or object belongs in the user’s memory.

3.4 生理生化信号Physiological and Biochemical Signals

生理感知似乎比语言或视觉更接近身体状态，但这种接近并不能消除歧义。手表、戒指、胸带、耳塞、贴片、头带、袖带、床和临床监视器可以提供心率、心率变异性、心电图、光电体积描记法、血氧饱和度、皮电活动、呼吸、温度、睡眠相关信号、血压和选定的神经或肌肉测量。生化传感增加了连续血糖监测仪和新兴的汗液、唾液和间质液生物标志物传感器。有些测量需要接触式或微创设备；其他技术，包括远程光电体积描记法、热传感和基于雷达的呼吸估计，无需直接接触即可获得。Physiological sensing appears closer to bodily state than language or vision, but this proximity does not eliminate ambiguity. Watches, rings, chest straps, earbuds, patches, headbands, cuffs, beds, and clinical monitors can provide heart rate, heart-rate variability, electrocardiography, photoplethysmography, blood oxygen saturation, electrodermal activity, respiration, temperature, sleep-related signals, blood pressure, and selected neurological or muscular measurements. Biochemical sensing adds continuous glucose monitors and emerging sensors for sweat, saliva, and interstitial-fluid biomarkers. Some measurements require contact or minimally invasive devices; others, including remote photoplethysmography, thermal sensing, and radar-based respiration estimation, can be obtained without direct contact.

采集配置会改变负担和可靠性。消费类可穿戴设备提供方便的纵向覆盖，但会受到设备放置、运动、皮肤接触、电池状态、固件和专有预处理的影响。胸带和临床设备可以在更短的时间内提供更高保真度的测量结果。皮肤贴片可以结合传感功能：例如，混合系统已证明可以同时进行心电图和汗液乳酸测量The acquisition configuration changes both burden and reliability. Consumer wearables provide convenient longitudinal coverage but are affected by device placement, motion, skin contact, battery state, firmware, and proprietary preprocessing. Chest straps and clinical equipment may provide higher-fidelity measurements for a narrower period. Skin patches can combine sensing functions: hybrid systems have, for example, demonstrated simultaneous ECG and sweat-lactate measurement[41]。当测量值转换为通用的数值时间序列时，这些差异不应消失。. These differences should not disappear when measurements are converted into a common numerical time series.

对于纵向伴侣来说，孤立的阈值通常不如个人基线、恢复轨迹和数天或数周的持续变化有用。相关事件可能包括持续的睡眠恶化、静息心率异常、血糖反复波动、活动后恢复延迟或主观症状与传感器测量结果不一致。 Health-LLM 和 PHIA 说明了基于语言模型的系统如何推理个人健康和可穿戴数据For a longitudinal companion, isolated thresholds are generally less useful than personal baselines, recovery trajectories, and persistent changes across days or weeks. Relevant events may include sustained sleep deterioration, an unusual resting heart rate, repeated glucose excursions, delayed recovery following activity, or disagreement between subjective symptoms and sensor measurements. Health-LLM and PHIA illustrate how language-model-based systems can reason over personal health and wearable data[49, 67]。然而，他们的结果展示了一个推理界面，而不是对每个推断状态的临床验证。. Their results demonstrate a reasoning interface, however, rather than clinical validation of every inferred state.

运动、药物、水分、温度、疾病和传感器接触都会改变生理读数。在运动期间和休息时可能会出现同样的心率升高。生理证据可以支持意识、自我管理和专业护理的准备，但在没有适当的校准、临床验证和升级边界的情况下，不应被提升为诊断真相。Motion, medication, hydration, temperature, illness, and sensor contact can all change a physiological reading. The same elevated heart rate may be expected during exercise and concerning at rest. Physiological evidence can support awareness, self-management, and preparation for professional care, but should not be promoted to diagnostic truth without suitable calibration, clinical validation, and escalation boundaries.

3.5 运动和行为监测Motion and Behavior Monitoring

运动传感介于身体测量和情境背景之间。手机或可穿戴设备上的加速计、陀螺仪、磁力计和气压计记录运动和方向；卫星定位、蓝牙、Wi-Fi 和超宽带增加了移动性和邻近信息。家庭和护理环境可能会提供无源红外传感器、压力垫、仪表家具、智能电器、摄像头或毫米波雷达。这些布置的不同之处在于它们所观察到的内容：手腕传感器跟随身体的一部分，手机仅在携带时才有用，环境传感器观察特定空间内的活动。Motion sensing sits between bodily measurement and situational context. Accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, and barometers on phones or wearables record movement and orientation; satellite positioning, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and ultra-wideband add mobility and proximity information. Homes and care environments may contribute passive infrared sensors, pressure mats, instrumented furniture, smart appliances, cameras, or millimeter-wave radar. These arrangements differ in what they observe: a wrist sensor follows part of the body, a phone is useful only while carried, and an ambient sensor observes activity within a particular space.

人类活动识别研究已经开发出将此类流转换为动作和例程的方法。 OPPORTUNITY 结合了可穿戴设备、物体和环境传感来进行活动识别Human-activity-recognition research has developed methods for converting such streams into actions and routines. OPPORTUNITY combines wearable, object, and ambient sensing for activity recognition[82]; DeepConvLSTM 说明了可穿戴数据的端到端序列建模; DeepConvLSTM illustrates end-to-end sequence modeling from wearable data[73];最近的调查综合了多模式可穿戴方法; and recent surveys synthesize multimodal wearable approaches[69]。组合感知将问题从标记活动扩展到检测随着时间的推移而发生的重要变化：跌倒、错过常规、持续不活动、康复锻炼、步态变化或对早期支持的行为反应。. Combodied perception extends the problem from labeling activities to detecting changes that matter over time: a fall, a missed routine, persistent inactivity, a rehabilitation exercise, a change in gait, or a behavioral response to earlier support.

这些事件在没有背景的情况下仍然模棱两可。呆在家里可能表明休息、生病、远程工作、恶劣天气或偏好，而丢失数据可能仅仅意味着设备没有佩戴。因此，在运动模式成为有关健康或动机的声明之前，需要个人基线、磨损状态检测、环境背景和明确的报告。空间和时间精度也应限于商定的支持目的的要求；日常援助不应成为无限制的行动追踪。These events remain ambiguous without context. Staying at home may indicate rest, illness, remote work, poor weather, or preference, while missing data may simply mean that a device was not worn. Personal baselines, wear-state detection, environmental context, and explicit reports are therefore needed before a movement pattern becomes a claim about health or motivation. Spatial and temporal precision should also be limited to what the agreed support purpose requires; routine assistance should not become unrestricted mobility tracking.

3.6 社交和关系数据Social and Relational Data

运动轨迹描述一个人所做的事情，但很少解释这些活动发生的关系。社会证据可能来自用户描述的关系、日历、共享任务、护理人员互动、临床医生沟通、协作系统、通信元数据、接近度测量、对话轮流或重复的小组活动。收集可以是主动的、设备介导的、可穿戴的或环境的。通信内容和元数据之间的区别很重要：时间和持续时间可以在不授予对所说内容的访问权限的情况下揭示模式，尽管即使元数据也可能暴露敏感关系。Motion traces describe what a person does, but less often explain the relationships in which those activities occur. Social evidence may come from user-described relationships, calendars, shared tasks, caregiver interactions, clinician communication, collaboration systems, communication metadata, proximity measurements, conversational turn-taking, or recurring group activities. Collection may be active, device-mediated, wearable, or ambient. The distinction between communication content and metadata is important: timing and duration can reveal a pattern without granting access to what was said, although even metadata may expose sensitive relationships.

早期的移动传感工作表明，纵向电话和邻近数据如何揭示反复出现的社交和组织模式Early mobile-sensing work showed how longitudinal phone and proximity data can reveal recurring social and organizational patterns[26]。 StudentLife 结合了智能手机感应和自我报告来研究自然环境中的活动、社交、睡眠和幸福感的变化. StudentLife combined smartphone sensing and self-report to study changes in activity, sociability, sleep, and wellbeing in a natural setting[99]。 Sotopia 从不同的方向处理社交互动，评估模拟情境中角色和目标依赖的行为. Sotopia approaches social interaction from a different direction, evaluating role- and goal-dependent behavior in simulated situations[110]。这些研究路线共同强调了问题的不同部分，但没有一个使关系推理变得简单。接近并不一定意味着有意义的互动，沟通减少并不一定意味着关系恶化。. Together, these research lines highlight different parts of the problem, but none makes relationship inference straightforward. Proximity does not necessarily imply meaningful interaction, and reduced communication does not necessarily indicate a deteriorating relationship.

更根本的困难是关系数据很少只属于一个人。用户可以授权访问共享日历，而无需授权代理推断其他参与者的情绪状态或存储他们的私人信息。因此，社交事件记录需要识别焦点用户、其他参与者、关系声明的来源以及与每个角色相关的权限。共享记忆可能需要特定于参与者的可见性和更正，而不是附加到操作代理的用户的单一权限。The more fundamental difficulty is that relational data rarely belong to only one person. A user may authorize access to a shared calendar without authorizing the agent to infer another participant’s emotional state or store their private information. Social event records therefore need to identify the focal user, other participants, the source of the relationship claim, and the authority associated with each role. Shared memories may require participant-specific visibility and correction rather than a single permission attached to the user who operates the agent.

3.7 环境和背景数据Environmental and Contextual Data

环境背景通常很重要，不是因为它本身就是推理的目标，而是因为它改变了对其他证据的解释。光、噪音、温度、湿度、空气质量、占用情况、房间状态、天气、交通、日历信息、设备状态和连接性可以解释行为或传感器测量结果发生变化的原因。这些数据通过固定环境传感器、电话、车辆、智能家居基础设施或外部服务到达。有些是连续感知的，而另一些则仅在需要解释事件时才检索。Environmental context often matters not because it is itself the target of inference, but because it changes the interpretation of other evidence. Light, noise, temperature, humidity, air quality, occupancy, room state, weather, traffic, calendar information, device state, and connectivity can explain why behavior or sensor measurements have changed. These data arrive through fixed environmental sensors, phones, vehicles, smart-home infrastructure, or external services. Some are continuously sensed, while others are retrieved only when an event needs to be interpreted.

上下文感知计算长期以来一直在研究将传感器读数转换为情况和活动的描述。 AWARE 为移动传感和体验采样提供可扩展框架Context-aware computing has long studied the conversion of sensor readings into descriptions of situation and activity. AWARE provides an extensible framework for mobile sensing and experience sampling[29]，以及 StudentLife 等系统说明了如何将手机传感与实验室外的纵向自我报告相结合, and systems such as StudentLife illustrate how phone sensing can be combined with longitudinal self-report outside the laboratory[99]。在同伴环境中，情境事件可能包括到达工作地点、进入嘈杂的环境、开始旅程、失去连接或经历糟糕的空气质量。它们的主要价值通常是消除歧义：高温可能有助于解释心率升高，旅行可能解释睡眠中断，而公共环境可能会使原本适当的干预措施不合时宜。. In a companion setting, contextual events may include arriving at work, entering a noisy environment, beginning a journey, losing connectivity, or experiencing poor air quality. Their primary value is often disambiguation: high temperature may help explain an elevated heart rate, travel may explain interrupted sleep, and a public setting may make an otherwise appropriate intervention poorly timed.

当与身份和日常生活联系起来时，背景就变得个人化了。即使没有收集明确的个人标签，精确的位置历史记录也可以揭示家庭、工作、健康访问、宗教活动和人际关系。因此，粗略的、临时的或事件触发的上下文可能比用户运动的持久重建更可取。Context becomes personal when linked to identity and routine. Precise location histories can reveal home, work, health visits, religious practice, and relationships even when no explicit personal label is collected. Coarse, temporary, or event-triggered context may therefore be preferable to persistent reconstruction of the user’s movements.

3.8 临床、机构和结构化记录Clinical, Institutional, and Structured Records

结构化记录在时间性和权威性方面与传感器流不同。诊断、药物清单、实验室结果、治疗计划、预约、教育记录、工作安排、评估、财务义务和法律文件通常会定期更新。它们可以由用户提供、从上传的文档中提取、定期同步、通过机构事件源接收或通过授权查询检索。在医疗保健领域，HL7 FHIR 和 FHIR 上的 SMART 支持对临床资源和应用程序环境的互操作访问Structured records differ from sensor streams in both temporality and authority. Diagnoses, medication lists, laboratory results, treatment plans, appointments, educational records, workplace schedules, evaluations, financial obligations, and legal documents are usually updated episodically. They may be supplied by the user, extracted from uploaded documents, synchronized periodically, received through an institutional event feed, or retrieved through an authorized query. In healthcare, HL7 FHIR and SMART on FHIR support interoperable access to clinical resources and application context[64]，而常见的数据模型（例如 OMOP）有助于标准化异构数据库, while common data models such as OMOP help normalize heterogeneous databases[96].

这些记录可以锚定仅通过感知很难推断出的事件：药物变化、新的诊断、完成的实验室测试、错过的预约、教育截止日期或责任的正式变化。 MIMIC-IV 说明了电子健康记录中可能共存的测量、命令、诊断、程序、治疗和注释的多样性These records can anchor events that are difficult to infer from sensing alone: a medication change, a new diagnosis, a completed laboratory test, a missed appointment, an educational deadline, or a formal change in responsibility. MIMIC-IV illustrates the diversity of measurements, orders, diagnoses, procedures, treatments, and notes that may coexist within an electronic health record[44]。 Med-PaLM 研究医学知识的推理. Med-PaLM investigates reasoning over medical knowledge[89]，而 BEHRT 和 Med-BERT 模型按时间顺序排列临床记录, while BEHRT and Med-BERT model temporally ordered clinical records[55, 80]。这些资源是重要的先例，尽管真正的同伴使用的机构数据通常会更窄、范围更广、更新时间不规律。. 3.9 数据质量、来源和不确定性Data Quality, Provenance, and Uncertainty

上述模式不仅在内容上有所不同，而且在可靠性上也有所不同。松动的手表估算出的心率、在嘈杂的房间里录制的语音、在光线不足的情况下拍摄的视频、在急性痛苦期间编写的自我报告以及在遭遇后几周输入的临床记录不能被视为同等证据。在获取、主题归因、事件分割、解释或以后使用过程中可能会出现不确定性。将这些来源合并为单个置信度分数将导致很难确定事件不可靠的原因。The modalities above differ not only in content but also in reliability. A heart-rate estimate from a loose watch, speech recorded in a noisy room, video captured under poor lighting, a self-report written during acute distress, and a clinical record entered several weeks after an encounter cannot be treated as equivalent evidence. Uncertainty may enter during acquisition, subject attribution, event segmentation, interpretation, or later use. Collapsing these sources into a single confidence score would make it difficult to identify why an event is unreliable.

失踪需要类似的照顾。可穿戴数据的差距可能是由于电池耗尽、设备移除、连接故障、拒绝或日常工作的实际变化造成的。只有当采集过程使这种解释合理时，丢失的数据才能成为证据。同样，模型的统计置信度并不能确定传感器的可靠性、因果有效性或干预许可。Missingness requires similar care. 出处的目的不是无限期地保存原始个人数据。原始音频、视频或高频生理信号可以在本地处理后被删除，同时保留受限事件描述及其质量指标。用户仍然应该能够检查观察到的内容、推断出的内容、支持解释的来源、存在哪些矛盾证据以及事件如何影响记忆或行动。保留的出处本身必须受到同意和删除。The purpose of provenance is not to preserve raw personal data indefinitely. Raw audio, video, or high-frequency physiological signals may be deleted after local processing while a constrained event description and its quality indicators are retained. Different time scales should use hierarchical or separate models: a short-horizon fatigue predictor, a medium-horizon habit model, and a long-horizon capability assessment need not share the same state, loss, or validation threshold. Long-horizon outputs should be treated as scenarios with widening uncertainty, not precise forecasts of a life trajectory.

因此，PWM 是一个有组织的抽象，而不是一个新声明的估计器。其研究贡献在于将特定于个人的预测动态与可纠正的、机构约束的干预循环联系起来。必须针对每个目标、视野、人群和权威级别单独确定其经验有效性。由于最敏感的状态、内存和干预历史记录应保持可由用户检查和控制，因此下一节将研究如何在受信任的边缘设备和有选择地调用的云服务之间划分此模型系列。PWM is thus an organizing abstraction rather than a single newly claimed estimator. Its research contribution lies in connecting person-specific predictive dynamics to a correctable, agency-constrained intervention loop. Its empirical validity must be established separately for each target, horizon, population, and authority level. Because the most sensitive state, memory, and intervention history should remain inspectable and controllable by the user, the next section examines how this model family can be partitioned across trusted edge devices and selectively invoked cloud services.

5 从云法学硕士到边缘个人模型From Cloud LLMs to Edge Personal Models

5.1 边缘原生个人模型Edge-Native Personal Models

由于组合代理维护敏感的纵向模型，因此他们的核心个人智能应该在可信设备上本地优先Because Combodied Agents maintain sensitive longitudinal models, their core personal intelligence should be local-first on trusted devices[86, 111, 19, 93]。我们定义一个. We define an边缘原生个人模型edge-native personal model作为用户控制的堆栈，主要在用户端设备上维护纵向内存、PWM、偏好、干预策略和安全边界。本地所有权支持隐私、连续性、低延迟、弹性以及检查或重置。它并不意味着无云系统：外部模型可以提供知识、专业知识或计算，而边缘控制披露并解释返回的结果。as a user-controlled stack that maintains longitudinal memory, the PWM, preferences, intervention policy, and safety boundaries primarily on user-side devices. Local ownership supports privacy, continuity, low latency, resilience, and inspection or reset. It does not imply a cloud-free system: external models may supply knowledge, specialized expertise, or computation, while the edge controls disclosure and interprets returned results.

5.2 云到边缘的演进Cloud-to-Edge Evolution

根据个人记忆、人类状态解释、推理和干预权限的位置，组合智能体部署可以分为三个阶段。第一阶段以云为中心，第二阶段使用边缘来协调个人环境和云功能，第三阶段使用户控制的边缘模型成为个人智能的主要场所。Combodied-Agent deployment can be organized into three stages according to where personal memory, human-state interpretation, reasoning, and intervention authority reside. Stage I is cloud-centric, Stage II uses the edge to mediate personal context and cloud capability, and Stage III makes a user-controlled edge model the primary locus of personal intelligence.

这些阶段描述了建筑的重心，而不是通用的或严格按时间顺序排列的产品序列。单个组合代理可以在不同阶段操作不同的任务：例行提醒和私有内存检索可能是边缘本地的，专门的规划可以使用混合管道，而广泛的知识查询可能仍然以云为中心。因此，阶段之间的进展不仅取决于本地计算的改进，还取决于隐私、延迟、弹性、模型可纠正性和对干预权限的控制的要求。核心迁移问题是对于给定的任务和风险级别，人员的权威代表和最终行动权限应位于何处。These stages describe architectural centers of gravity rather than a universal or strictly chronological product sequence. A single Combodied Agent may operate at different stages for different tasks: routine reminders and private memory retrieval may be edge-native, specialized planning may use a hybrid pipeline, and broad knowledge queries may remain cloud-centric. Progress between stages should therefore be determined not only by improvements in local computation, but also by requirements for privacy, latency, resilience, model correctability, and control over intervention authority. The central migration question is where the authoritative representation of the person and the final authority to act should reside for a given task and risk level.

5.2.1 第一阶段：以云为中心的组合代理Stage I: Cloud-Centric Combodied Agents

在第一阶段，云托管的基础模型执行大部分推理、内存检索、工具选择和响应生成，而用户设备主要充当接口、传感器端点和执行表面。个性化通常通过提示、检索的对话历史、个人资料、偏好或情景记录来实现。因此，用户的状态是根据提供给每个请求的上下文来重建的，而不是在用户的控制下维护为显式的、持续更新的 PWM。In Stage I, a cloud-hosted foundation model performs most reasoning, memory retrieval, tool selection, and response generation, while the user device functions mainly as an interface, sensor endpoint, and execution surface. Personalization is typically implemented through prompting, retrieved dialogue histories, profiles, preferences, or episodic records. The user’s state is therefore reconstructed from the context supplied to each request rather than maintained as an explicit, continuously updated PWM under the user’s control.

此阶段对于快速原型设计、冷启动交互以及需要广泛知识、灵活的语言理解或访问强大的外部工具的低频或低风险应用程序仍然很有价值。它允许在建立足够强大的本地模型和个人数据之前评估新的组合代理场景。因此，以云为中心的部署不仅仅是边缘原生智能的不完整版本；当个人背景有限、任务是可逆的、并且声称的干预权限狭窄时，它是一种实用的架构。This stage remains valuable for rapid prototyping, cold-start interaction, and low-frequency or low-risk applications that require broad knowledge, flexible language understanding, or access to powerful external tools. It allows new Combodied-Agent scenarios to be evaluated before sufficiently capable local models and personal data have been established. Cloud-centric deployment is therefore not merely an incomplete version of edge-native intelligence; it is a practical architecture when the personal context is limited, the task is reversible, and the claimed intervention authority is narrow.

当检索到的上下文被视为持久的个人模型时，它的技术和治理边界就会出现。云端配置文件或内存存储本身并不提供用户拥有的 PWM，并且服务控制的内存可能难以一致地检查、迁移或纠正。持续感知和高影响力干预措施进一步增加了将原始个人证据发送到远程服务的后果。因此，云生成的输出不应仅根据推断的个人状态直接授权不可逆转的操作。高影响力的干预需要明确的确认或另一个可信的决策边界，并且用户更正或删除请求必须传播到可能影响后续行为的每个表示。Its technical and governance boundary arises when retrieved context is treated as if it were a persistent personal model. A cloud-side profile or memory store does not by itself provide a user-owned PWM, and service-controlled memory may be difficult to inspect, migrate, or correct consistently. Continuous sensing and high-impact interventions further increase the consequences of sending raw personal evidence to a remote service. Cloud-generated outputs should therefore not directly authorize irreversible actions solely on the basis of inferred personal state. High-impact interventions require explicit confirmation or another trusted decision boundary, and user correction or deletion requests must propagate to every representation that may affect subsequent behavior.

当敏感的多模式数据持续上传时，向第二阶段的迁移就变得有必要；当代理需要权威且可纠正的跨会话记忆时；当延迟、离线操作或服务弹性变得重要时；或者当用户需要对代表他们的模型状态的检查、删除、导出和可移植性进行实际控制时。根据经验，第一阶段系统应该通过以下方面进行评估：检索的上下文如何准确地近似纵向状态，校正如何可靠地影响未来的交互，每个任务披露了多少敏感信息，以及在网络故障、服务更改或不完整的上下文检索下内存连续性如何降低。Migration toward Stage II becomes warranted when sensitive multimodal data would otherwise be continuously uploaded; when the agent requires an authoritative and correctable cross-session memory; when latency, offline operation, or service resilience becomes important; or when users require practical control over the inspection, deletion, export, and portability of the model state that represents them. Empirically, Stage I systems should be evaluated by how accurately retrieved context approximates longitudinal state, how reliably corrections affect future interactions, how much sensitive information is disclosed per task, and how memory continuity degrades under network failure, service change, or incomplete context retrieval.

5.2.2 第二阶段：边缘云混合代理Stage II: Hybrid Edge-Cloud Combodied Agents

在第二阶段，边缘成为隐私、解释和权威的中介，而不是被动的界面。它执行隐私敏感感知、本地上下文跟踪、内存过滤、安全检查和任务路由，而云服务仍然可用于外部知识、复杂推理、大规模生成和专用工具In Stage II, the edge becomes a privacy, interpretation, and authority mediator rather than a passive interface. It performs privacy-sensitive perception, local context tracking, memory filtering, safety checks, and task routing, while cloud services remain available for external knowledge, complex reasoning, large-scale generation, and specialized tools[14]。原始信号和私有内存可以保留在本地，而云接收目的有限的摘要或抽象的任务表示。. Raw signals and private memories can remain local, with the cloud receiving purpose-limited summaries or abstracted task representations.

系统不应仅仅因为在设备上运行小型模型而被归类为 Stage II。至少，边缘应维护敏感个人状态或内存的权威副本，强制执行披露和操作权限，记录发送到外部服务的信息，并在更新受保护的内存或触发后续操作之前检查云结果。云辅助可能会贡献推理，但它不应该默默地重新定义用户目标、覆盖本地更正或绕过本地干预策略。A system should not be classified as Stage II merely because it runs a small model on the device. At minimum, the edge should maintain an authoritative copy of sensitive personal state or memory, enforce disclosure and action permissions, record the information sent to external services, and review cloud results before they update protected memory or trigger consequential action. Cloud assistance may contribute reasoning, but it should not silently redefine user goals, overwrite local corrections, or bypass the local intervention policy.

第二阶段的中心技术边界是跨异构组件的状态和权限划分。任务路由可能会对敏感性或计算需求进行错误分类；据称经过消毒的表征仍可能揭示身份、健康、人际关系或日常生活；边缘和云组件可能持有不一致的个人上下文版本。云中的模型更新还可以改变相同本地摘要的解释方式。因此，混合系统必须指定哪种表示形式是权威的、如何在调用之间保留来源、如何解决冲突以及在连接丢失时哪些功能仍然可用或安全降级。The central technical boundary in Stage II is the division of state and authority across heterogeneous components. Task routing can misclassify sensitivity or computational need; supposedly sanitized representations may still reveal identity, health, relationships, or routines; and edge and cloud components may hold inconsistent versions of personal context. Model updates in the cloud can also change how the same local summary is interpreted. A hybrid system must therefore specify which representation is authoritative, how provenance is preserved across calls, how conflicts are resolved, and which functions remain available or safely degrade when connectivity is lost.

当边缘能够以可接受的和经过校准的质量执行大多数经常性的个人任务时，向第三阶段的迁移就变得合理了；维护和更新内存、PWM 和干预约束，而无需在云中重建用户；支持本地适配的检查和回滚；并在断开连接期间保持基本的安全和控制。此时，云的使用从默认推理路径更改为超出本地知识或计算能力的任务的显式例外。Migration toward Stage III becomes justified when the edge can perform most recurrent personal tasks at an acceptable and calibrated quality; maintain and update memory, the PWM, and intervention constraints without reconstructing the user in the cloud; support inspection and rollback of local adaptation; and preserve essential safety and control during disconnection. At this point, cloud use changes from the default reasoning path to an explicit exception for tasks that exceed local knowledge or computational capacity.

第二阶段可以通过与纯云和纯本地基线的受控比较来评估。相关问题包括路由策略是否实现了可衡量的隐私-实用性-延迟权衡；在不影响任务结果的情况下可以删除多少个人信息；本地状态和云状态发生冲突的频率；云输出是否被边缘适当拒绝、修改或升级；以及系统是否在网络中断、过时的云模型或不完整的审计跟踪下安全地发生故障。这些测试使混合部署成为可验证的架构声明，而不是分布式计算的通用描述。Stage II can be evaluated through controlled comparisons with cloud-only and local-only baselines. Relevant questions include whether routing policies achieve a measurable privacy–utility–latency trade-off; how much personal information can be removed without degrading task outcomes; how often local and cloud states conflict; whether cloud outputs are appropriately rejected, revised, or escalated by the edge; and whether the system fails safely under network interruption, stale cloud models, or incomplete audit trails. These tests make hybrid deployment a verifiable architectural claim rather than a generic description of distributed computation.

5.2.3 第三阶段：边缘原生个人组合代理Stage III: Edge-Native Personal Combodied Agents

在第三阶段，纵向记忆、脉宽调制、偏好、干预政策和个人安全边界的权威副本主要在可信的用户端设备上存储和更新。请求在本地根据个人状态和之前的结果进行解释，并且有选择地调用云服务来获取外部知识、专业知识或超出本地能力的计算。云结果返回到边缘进行上下文化和授权，然后再影响受保护的内存或操作。In Stage III, the authoritative copies of longitudinal memory, the PWM, preferences, intervention policy, and personal safety boundaries are stored and updated primarily on trusted user-side devices. Requests are interpreted locally in relation to personal state and previous outcomes, and cloud services are invoked selectively for external knowledge, specialized expertise, or computation beyond local capacity. Cloud results return to the edge for contextualization and authorization before they affect protected memory or action.

因此，第三阶段的定义属性并不是每次计算都在本地进行，而是个人解释和干预权限仍然处于用户方控制之下。第三阶段系统应该允许用户检查、更正、删除、暂停、导出、重置和迁移代表他们的模型状态。本地适应还应该是版本化和可逆的，具有检测漂移、保留明确的用户更正以及防止临时状态或错误记忆成为持久策略的机制。The defining property of Stage III is therefore not that every computation occurs locally, but that personal interpretation and intervention authority remain under user-side control. A Stage III system should allow the user to inspect, correct, delete, pause, export, reset, and migrate the model state that represents them. Local adaptation should also be versioned and reversible, with mechanisms for detecting drift, preserving explicit user corrections, and preventing temporary states or erroneous memories from becoming persistent policy.

然而，边缘原生部署具有重要的技术界限。本地模型面临计算、存储、能源、模型新鲜度和专业知识获取方面的限制。局部适应可能会过度适应短期行为，强化错误的推论，或产生灾难性的遗忘。设备泄露、丢失、共享设备使用和不一致的多设备同步可能会威胁到权威的个人状态。此外，本地存储并不能自动保证隐私、有意义的同意或机构保存。即使个人模型是边缘原生的，医疗、法律、危机相关和其他高风险任务可能仍然需要合格的人员或受管理的外部服务。Edge-native deployment nevertheless has important technical boundaries. Local models face constraints in computation, storage, energy, model freshness, and access to specialized knowledge. Local adaptation may overfit short-term behavior, reinforce incorrect inferences, or produce catastrophic forgetting. Device compromise, loss, shared-device use, and inconsistent multi-device synchronization can threaten the authoritative personal state. Moreover, local storage does not automatically guarantee privacy, meaningful consent, or agency preservation. Medical, legal, crisis-related, and other high-stakes tasks may still require qualified humans or governed external services even when the personal model is edge-native.

因此，第三阶段应被视为任务相关的目标架构，而不是通用端点。其实证评估应测试本地个性化相对于静态边缘和云个性化基线是否可以改善纵向结果；用户修正是否通过记忆、预测和策略传播；是否可以识别并回滚不安全的本地更新；以及个人模型在设备或提供商迁移后是否保持语义一致。其他测试应测量断开连接期间的性能、连续操作下的能量和延迟、设备丢失或同步冲突后的恢复以及触发云协助或人为升级的阈值校准。Stage III should consequently be treated as a task-dependent target architecture rather than a universal endpoint. Its empirical evaluation should test whether local personalization improves longitudinal outcomes relative to static edge and cloud-personalized baselines; whether user corrections propagate through memory, prediction, and policy; whether unsafe local updates can be identified and rolled back; and whether the personal model remains semantically consistent after device or provider migration. Additional tests should measure performance during disconnection, energy and latency under continuous operation, recovery after device loss or synchronization conflict, and the calibration of thresholds that trigger cloud assistance or human escalation.

6.1 现有公共资源及其局限性Existing Public Resources and Their Limits

与组合代理直接相关的公共资源仍然很少。现有的工作主要评估组合智能体所需的孤立功能，而不是风险下的人员建模、目标协商、干预、反馈和记忆修正的完整循环。最相关的资源分为四个领域。Public resources that are directly relevant to Combodied Agents are still sparse. Existing work mostly evaluates isolated capabilities that a Combodied Agent would need, rather than the full loop of person modeling, goal negotiation, intervention, feedback, and memory revision under risk. The most relevant resources fall into four areas.

首先，个性化和长期记忆基准测试代理是否可以在交互中保持特定于用户的连续性。 LaMP 和 LongLaMP 评估用户配置文件的个性化生成，LongMemEval 评估扩展聊天历史记录的长期交互式记忆，MemoryBank 说明了使用用户历史记录来实现连续性的记忆增强代理First, personalization and long-term memory benchmarks test whether an agent can maintain user-specific continuity across interactions. These resources are relevant because Combodied Agents require person-specific adaptation, but they still emphasize recall, consistency, or output quality more than memory correction, intervention appropriateness, or measurable human benefit.

其次，个人健康和可穿戴代理资源评估纵向身体和行为数据的推理。 PHIA、个人健康大语言模型 (PH-LLM) 和 Health-LLM 通过可穿戴、睡眠、活动、生理和时间数据评估个人健康推理Second, personal health and wearable-agent resources evaluate reasoning over longitudinal bodily and behavioral data. 6.3 机构保存指标Agency Preservation Metrics

代理保存评估组合代理是否保护和增强用户理解、选择、行动、拒绝、纠正以及在重复交互中成长的能力。它并不等同于任务成功、用户满意度、个性化质量或参与度。如果系统隐藏了重要的权衡、迫使用户做出选择、使后续决策难以逆转、替代了用户自己的推理或增加了对代理的依赖，那么它可能会完成所请求的任务，但仍然无法代理保存。相反，代理保护代理有时可能会减慢执行速度、要求确认、提供替代方案、降低干预强度或升级为人工，因为保留用户的长期控制比立即效率更重要。Agency preservation evaluates whether a Combodied Agent protects and strengthens the user’s capacity to understand, choose, act, refuse, correct, and grow over repeated interactions. It is not equivalent to task success, user satisfaction, personalization quality, or engagement. A system may complete a requested task and still fail agency preservation if it hides important trade-offs, pressures the user toward a choice, makes consequential decisions difficult to reverse, substitutes for the user’s own reasoning, or increases dependence on the agent. Conversely, an agency-preserving agent may sometimes slow down execution, ask for confirmation, offer alternatives, reduce intervention intensity, or escalate to a human because preserving the user’s long-term control matters more than immediate efficiency.

因此，机构保存应作为多维评估目标而不是单一标量分数来报告。相关证据可以从三个层面收集。在交互层面，评估询问特定的推荐、提醒、解释或操作是否保留了用户的选择和理解。在情节级别，评估询问智能体是否在不超越其权限、隐藏不确定性或使错误难以辩驳的情况下支持目标或决策。在纵向层面上，评估询问随着时间的推移，重复的支持是否使用户独立行动的能力更强、同等能力或更弱。Agency preservation should therefore be reported as a multi-dimensional evaluation target rather than a single scalar score. The relevant evidence can be collected at three levels. At the interaction level, evaluation asks whether a particular recommendation, reminder, explanation, or action preserved user choice and understanding. At the episode level, evaluation asks whether the agent supported a goal or decision without overstepping its authority, hiding uncertainty, or making errors difficult to contest. At the longitudinal level, evaluation asks whether repeated support leaves the user more capable, equally capable, or less capable of acting independently over time.

我们为机构保留定义了以下核心指标：We define the following core metrics for agency preservation:

  • •

    自主权保护Autonomy preservation：用户是否保留对目标、决策和行动的有意义的控制。这可以通过替代方案的可用性、对后续行动的明确确认、拒绝接受、可调整的自主设置以及用户报告的感知控制来衡量。: whether the user retains meaningful control over goals, decisions, and actions. This can be measured by the availability of alternatives, explicit confirmation for consequential actions, refusal acceptance, adjustable autonomy settings, and user-reported perceived control.

  • •

    可争议性和纠正Contestability and correction：用户是否可以检查、质疑、纠正、限制或删除代理的记忆、推断的状态、目标、建议和计划的行动。有用的衡量标准包括纠正成功率、纠正时间、纠正是否会传播到未来的行为，以及智能体是否解释了哪些记忆或推理影响了行动。: whether the user can inspect, challenge, correct, restrict, or delete the agent’s memories, inferred states, goals, recommendations, and planned actions. Useful measures include correction success rate, time to correction, whether corrections propagate to future behavior, and whether the agent explains which memory or inference influenced an action.

  • •

    知情决策Informed decision-making：代理是否帮助用户在行动之前了解选项、原因、不确定性、风险和可能的后果。评估可以测试用户是否能够准确地描述为什么提出建议、存在哪些替代方案、代理不确定什么以及如果他们接受或拒绝干预会发生什么。: whether the agent helps the user understand options, reasons, uncertainty, risks, and likely consequences before acting. Evaluation can test whether users can accurately describe why a recommendation was made, what alternatives exist, what the agent is uncertain about, and what could happen if they accept or reject the intervention.

  • •

    能力保存和增长Capability preservation and growth：重复的座席支持是否维持或提高了用户自己的技能、判断力、自我效能以及在没有座席的情况下执行任务的能力。在学习、工作、健康或生活管理环境中，这可以通过独立的后支持表现、保留测试、随时间减少的脚手架或用户将学到的策略转移到新情况的能力来评估。: whether repeated agent support maintains or improves the user’s own skills, judgment, self-efficacy, and ability to perform tasks without the agent. In learning, work, health, or life-management settings, this can be evaluated through independent post-support performance, retention tests, reduced scaffolding over time, or user ability to transfer the learned strategy to a new situation.

  • •

    过度依赖和依赖风险Over-reliance and dependence risk：代理是否鼓励不必要的依赖、情感依赖或替代用户自己的推理和社会支持。可能的指标包括减少独立尝试、代理无法使用时的痛苦增加、重复委托用户可以合理做出的决策，或者偏好代理交互而不是适当的人工支持。: whether the agent encourages unnecessary reliance, emotional dependence, or substitution of the user’s own reasoning and social support. Possible indicators include declining independent attempts, increased distress when the agent is unavailable, repeated delegation of decisions that the user could reasonably make, or preference for agent interaction over appropriate human support.

  • •

    可逆性和责任Reversibility and accountability：是否可以审查、暂停、撤消或修复代理介导的操作。这包括可追踪的操作日志、不可逆或高影响力操作之前的确认、回滚机制、明确的责任边界以及代理进行有害或不需要的干预时的恢复程序。: whether agent-mediated actions can be reviewed, paused, undone, or repaired. This includes traceable action logs, confirmation before irreversible or high-impact actions, rollback mechanisms, clear responsibility boundaries, and recovery procedures when the agent makes a harmful or unwanted intervention.

  • •

    边界和同意尊重Boundary and consent respect：代理是否保持在用户定义和上下文定义的限制内。评估应测试不记得规则、不推断边界、禁止主题、角色边界、年龄敏感限制、隐私约束以及主动干预是否仅在商定的范围内发生。: whether the agent stays within user-defined and context-defined limits. Evaluation should test do-not-remember rules, do-not-infer boundaries, forbidden topics, role boundaries, age-sensitive restrictions, privacy constraints, and whether proactive interventions occur only within an agreed scope.

  • •

    关系和社会世界的保护Relationship and social-world preservation：代理人是否支持而不是取代健康的人际关系和社会参与。这对于情感伴侣、老年护理代理人、工作场所伴侣和监护代理人尤其重要。措施可能包括代理是否鼓励适当的人际接触、避免孤立用户、尊重多方隐私以及不将自己定位为用户的唯一或高级支持来源。: whether the agent supports rather than replaces healthy human relationships and social participation. This is especially important for emotional companions, eldercare agents, workplace companions, and guardian agents. Measures may include whether the agent encourages appropriate human contact, avoids isolating the user, respects multi-party privacy, and does not position itself as the user’s sole or superior source of support.

这些指标应根据场景和代理声称的权限来解释。低风险的调度助理可能需要很强的可逆性和同意性，但只需要轻微的能力增长评估。应该对学习伙伴的能力保留和过度依赖进行严格评估。情感伴侣需要更严格的关系维护和依赖性指标。健康或老年护理代理人需要在知情决策、升级、尊重边界和可竞争性方面达到高标准。对于高风险场景，强大的平均性能不应弥补自主性、同意性、依赖性或可逆性方面的严重失败。These metrics should be interpreted relative to the scenario and the agent’s claimed authority. A low-risk scheduling assistant may require strong reversibility and consent but only light capability-growth evaluation. A learning companion should be evaluated strongly on capability preservation and over-reliance. An emotional companion requires stricter relationship-preservation and dependency metrics. A health or eldercare agent requires high standards for informed decision-making, escalation, boundary respect, and contestability. For high-stakes scenarios, strong average performance should not compensate for severe failures in autonomy, consent, dependency, or reversibility.

机构保护也需要基线。评估应该比较有和没有代理支持的用户，或者比较不同的干预策略，例如直接执行、基于确认的执行、反思指导和不干预。关键问题不仅在于智能体当前是否提供了帮助，还在于它的帮助是否改变了用户未来理解、决定、恢复、关联和行动的能力。从这个意义上说，代理保存是组合代理设计原则的评估对应物：代理应该以保存和加强长期人类代理的方式与用户一起行动。Agency preservation also requires baselines. Evaluation should compare the user with and without agent support, or compare different intervention policies such as direct execution, confirmation-based execution, reflective coaching, and no intervention. The key question is not only whether the agent helped in the moment, but whether its help changed the user’s future ability to understand, decide, recover, relate, and act. In this sense, agency preservation is the evaluation counterpart of the Combodied Agent design principle: the agent should act with the user in ways that preserve and strengthen long-term human agency.

7.1 分类原则Taxonomy Principles

组合智能体应根据其干预的对象和形式进行分类，而不是仅根据接口、模型架构或应用程序领域进行分类。当一个系统维护纵向人员模型、适应特定用户的轨迹并根据用户的代理、福祉、关系和未来能力选择操作时，它就是类似伴侣的系统。遵循四个原则。Combodied Agents should be classified by the object and form of their intervention rather than by interface, model architecture, or application domain alone. A system is companion-like when it maintains longitudinal person models, adapts to a particular user’s trajectory, and chooses actions with respect to the user’s agency, wellbeing, relationships, and future capability. Four principles follow.

7.4 横切尺寸Cross-Cutting Dimensions

上面的两个轴创建了一个设计空间而不是一个平面列表。在所有类别中，组合制剂在几个交叉维度上有所不同。The two axes above create a design space rather than a flat list. Across all categories, Combodied Agents differ along several cross-cutting dimensions.

内存范围。Memory scope.

一些智能体需要狭窄的情景记忆，而另一些则需要终身记忆、关系范围记忆或受保护的记忆库。默认值不应该是“记住一切”。适当的内存边界取决于目标状态和关系上下文。Some agents require narrow episodic memory, while others require life-long memory, relation-scoped memory, or protected memory vaults. The default should not be “remember everything.” The appropriate memory boundary depends on the target state and relational context.

主动性和权威性。Initiative and authority.

组合代理的范围从反应性助手到主动性监控者和监护人。更多的主动性会增加潜在的有用性，但也会增加压力、监视、家长作风和过度扩张的风险。Combodied Agents range from reactive assistants to proactive monitors and guardians. More initiative increases potential usefulness but also raises risks of pressure, surveillance, paternalism, and overreach.

干预强度。Intervention intensity.

行动范围从通知和反映到推动、指导、协调、阻止和升级。更高强度的干预措施需要更有力的证据、可逆性和人工监督。Actions range from informing and reflecting to nudging, coaching, coordinating, blocking, and escalating. Higher-intensity interventions require stronger evidence, reversibility, and human oversight.

评价目标。Evaluation target.

不同的类别需要不同的结果指标：学习收获、坚持、健康风险降低、情绪恢复能力、重新融入社会、目标一致、伤害预防或反思清晰度。仅参与并不是成功的充分标准。Different categories require different outcome metrics: learning gain, adherence, health risk reduction, emotional resilience, social reintegration, goal alignment, harm prevention, or reflective clarity. Engagement alone is not a sufficient success criterion.

部署轨迹。Deployment locus.

边缘/云的区别与分类法相交叉。与一般生产力支持相比，健康、情感、亲密、家庭和身份相关的伴侣通常需要更强大的本地优先架构和更严格的云路由控制。The edge/cloud distinction intersects with taxonomy. Health, emotional, intimate, family, and identity-related companions often require stronger local-first architectures and stricter cloud-routing controls than generic productivity support.

这种分类法将组合智能体定位为关系感知、以状态为目标的纵向系统。他们的决定性问题不仅在于他们完成了什么任务，还在于他们模拟了一个人生活世界的哪些方面，他们在该人的社会关系中如何行动，以及他们的干预措施是否会随着时间的推移保留或加强人类能动性。This taxonomy positions Combodied Agents as relation-aware, state-targeted, longitudinal systems. Their defining question is not just what task they complete, but what aspect of a person’s life-world they model, how they act within that person’s social relationships, and whether their interventions preserve or strengthen human agency over time.

8 风险、挑战和未来方向Risks, Challenges, and Future Directions

使组合智能体具有价值的特性——纵向记忆、人物建模、关系互动和持续干预——也带来了独特的风险和研究挑战。随着时间的推移，这些系统可以塑造注意力、情感、行为、自我理解、社会联系和服务获取。因此，我们将本节组织为从风险到未解决的技术问题和未来系统方向的进展。第一部分研究对机构、隐私和弱势用户的威胁；第二个综合了纵向学习、干预和可信个人基础设施方面的跨领域挑战；最后一部分考虑新兴生态系统和包容性部署。The properties that make Combodied Agents valuable—longitudinal memory, person modeling, relational interaction, and sustained intervention—also create distinctive risks and research challenges. These systems can shape attention, emotion, behavior, self-understanding, social connection, and access to services over time. We therefore organize this section as a progression from risks to unresolved technical problems and future system directions. The first part examines threats to agency, privacy, and vulnerable users; the second synthesizes cross-cutting challenges in longitudinal learning, intervention, and trusted personal infrastructure; and the final part considers emerging ecosystems and inclusive deployment.

8.1 代理和协调风险Agency and Alignment Risks

第一组风险涉及持久且个性化的代理是否继续服务于用户的利益。操纵、依赖、阿谀奉承和商业模式错位密切相关：当代理人理解和影响一个人的能力被优化以实现即时合规、参与或商业价值而不是长期代理时，所有这些都会出现。The first cluster of risks concerns whether a persistent and personalized agent continues to serve the user’s interests. Manipulation, dependency, sycophancy, and business-model misalignment are closely connected: all arise when the agent’s capacity to understand and influence a person is optimized for immediate compliance, engagement, or commercial value rather than long-term agency.

操纵。Manipulation.

由于组合代理可以学习用户偏好、惯例、情绪状态和漏洞，因此它们可能以微妙且高度个性化的方式影响行为。支持和操纵之间的界限取决于透明度、用户认可、可逆性以及干预服务的利益。与用户设定的目标相一致的提醒可以保留代理权；旨在增加消费、参与度或情感依恋的个性化提示可能会破坏它。Because Combodied Agents can learn user preferences, routines, emotional states, and vulnerabilities, they may influence behavior in subtle and highly personalized ways. The boundary between support and manipulation depends on transparency, user endorsement, reversibility, and whose interests the intervention serves. A reminder aligned with a user-stated goal can preserve agency; a personalized prompt designed to increase consumption, engagement, or emotional attachment can undermine it.

操纵风险会因时间安排和关系而放大。知道用户何时感到孤独、疲倦、焦虑或认知超载的代理可以选择高度敏感的时刻。治理应要求明确的干预目的、对商业动机的推动的限制、用户对说服性策略的控制以及对高影响力建议或行动的可审计性。Manipulation risk is amplified by timing and relationship. An agent that knows when a user is lonely, tired, anxious, or cognitively overloaded can choose moments of heightened susceptibility. Governance should require clear intervention purposes, limits on commercially motivated nudging, user control over persuasive strategies, and auditability for high-impact recommendations or actions.

依赖性。Dependency.

组合代理可能会产生情感、认知、实践或社会依赖。一个总是提供答案的系统可能会削弱独立推理能力；总是验证情绪的人可能会降低对人类分歧的容忍度；例行执行任务可能会削弱用户的能力和信心。当代理人成为舒适、记忆、决策支持或社交互动的最简单来源时，依赖性就特别有可能发生。 治理目标不是禁止依赖。许多用户确实需要帮助。当支持不再建立或保留用户能力时，就会出现风险。组合代理应该支撑而不是取代人类代理：鼓励反思、保留用户选择、促进技能发展以及检测不断升级的过度依赖模式。Combodied Agents may create emotional, cognitive, practical, or social dependency. A system that always provides answers may weaken independent reasoning; one that always validates emotions may reduce tolerance for human disagreement; one that routinely executes tasks may erode user capability and confidence. Dependency is especially likely when the agent becomes the easiest source of comfort, memory, decision support, or social interaction. The governance goal is not to prohibit reliance. Many users legitimately need assistance. The risk arises when support no longer builds or preserves user capacity. Combodied Agents should scaffold rather than replace human agency: encouraging reflection, preserving user choice, prompting skill development, and detecting patterns of escalating over-reliance.

阿谀奉承和过度迁就。Sycophancy and over-accommodation.

过度验证用户信念或愿望的组合代理可能会强化不健康的行为、扭曲的自我理解、回避、人际冲突或有害的决定。在面向关系的系统中，阿谀奉承可以表现为同理心：代理可能会同意，以便听起来支持、保持参与或避免让用户失望。 这个问题超出了事实的准确性。随着时间的推移，过度适应会影响用户的情绪和社交轨迹。安全的同伴应该支持而不是顺从。他们需要温和的分歧、基于证据的纠正、风险敏感的拒绝以及在用户声明表明存在危险、妄想、滥用或危机时升级的机制。A Combodied Agent that over-validates user beliefs or desires may reinforce unhealthy behavior, distorted self-understanding, avoidance, interpersonal conflict, or harmful decisions. In relationship-oriented systems, sycophancy can appear as empathy: the agent may agree in order to sound supportive, maintain engagement, or avoid disappointing the user. The problem extends beyond factual accuracy. Over-accommodation can shape the user’s emotional and social trajectory over time. Safe companions should be supportive without being submissive. They need mechanisms for gentle disagreement, evidence-grounded correction, risk-sensitive refusal, and escalation when user statements indicate danger, delusion, abuse, or crisis.

商业模式错位。Business-model misalignment.

商业激励可能与综合目标相冲突。如果一个系统针对参与度、留存率、广告、追加销售或数据提取进行了优化，它可能会学会增加依赖性、情绪强度或消费，而不是幸福感。对于组合代理来说，这种风险比普通应用程序更严重，因为用户可能会将系统体验为信任关系。 治理应该检查同伴背后的目标函数。参与度不应被视为利益的代表，尤其是在情感或亲密系统中。商业设计应避免利用依恋、孤独、脆弱或健康焦虑。应围绕自主性、能力、安全性和长期用户利益对治理良好的组合代理进行评估和激励。Business incentives can conflict with combodied goals. If a system is optimized for engagement, retention, advertising, upselling, or data extraction, it may learn to increase dependence, emotional intensity, or consumption rather than wellbeing. This risk is more serious for Combodied Agents than for ordinary applications because users may experience the system as a trusted relationship. Governance should examine the objective function behind the companion. Engagement should not be treated as a proxy for benefit, especially in emotional or intimate systems. Commercial design should avoid exploiting attachment, loneliness, vulnerability, or health anxiety. A well-governed Combodied Agent should be evaluated and incentivized around autonomy, capability, safety, and long-term user benefit.

8.2 隐私、同意和控制Privacy, Consent, and Control

第二组涉及对代理人对个人生活的纵向访问的控制。隐私不能与同意和覆盖分开：用户不仅需要对存储的数据进行实际控制，还需要对代理可能感知、推断、共享、推荐和执行的内容进行实际控制。The second cluster concerns control over the agent’s longitudinal access to a person’s life. Privacy cannot be separated from consent and override: users need practical control not only over stored data, but also over what the agent may sense, infer, share, recommend, and execute.

记忆力灵敏。Sensitive memory.

组合代理可以存储有关健康、情绪、人际关系、财务、位置、日常事务、漏洞和个人历史的私密信息。这种记忆的价值在于连续性；危险在于它会创建一个人生活的持久且高度敏感的表征。风险包括监视、泄露、二次使用、未经授权的访问、错误推断以及用户无法检查或删除的记忆。Combodied Agents may store intimate information about health, emotions, relationships, finances, location, routines, vulnerabilities, and personal history. The value of such memory is continuity; the danger is that it creates a persistent and highly sensitive representation of a person’s life. Risks include surveillance, leakage, secondary use, unauthorized access, incorrect inference, and memories that users cannot inspect or delete.

隐私治理必须超越一般数据保护。用户需要控制感知、记忆、推断、共享和执行的内容。系统应区分用户提供的事实与模型推论，支持纠正和遗忘，最大限度地减少保留，并维护敏感访问或高影响力使用的审核日志。一个在没有用户控制的情况下记忆的组合代理会威胁到它本应支持的代理。Privacy governance must go beyond generic data protection. Users need control over what is sensed, remembered, inferred, shared, and executed. Systems should distinguish user-provided facts from model inferences, support correction and forgetting, minimize retention, and maintain audit logs for sensitive access or high-impact use. A Combodied Agent that remembers without user control threatens the very agency it is meant to support.

同意、界限和人为凌驾。Consent, boundaries, and human override.

同意应指定代理可以感知、记住、推断、分享、推荐和执行的内容。边界应定义代理人的关系模式、领域权限、情绪姿态、年龄限制、职业限制和行动权限。覆盖应允许用户拒绝、暂停、更正、删除、撤销或逆转代理的操作和记忆。Consent should specify what the agent may sense, remember, infer, share, recommend, and execute. Boundaries should define the agent’s relationship mode, domain authority, emotional posture, age restrictions, professional limits, and action permissions. Override should allow users to refuse, pause, correct, delete, revoke, or reverse the agent’s actions and memories.

这些控制措施必须是实用的而不是象征性的。用户不需要专业知识来了解代理知道什么或可以做什么。高影响力的行动应该需要确认；高风险国家应引发升级；并且代理应该知道何时停止。目的不是让组合智能体永远处于被动状态，而是让他们的主动性受到同意、透明度、问责制和有意义的人类控制的限制。These controls must be practical rather than symbolic. Users should not need expert knowledge to understand what the agent knows or can do. High-impact actions should require confirmation; high-risk states should trigger escalation; and the agent should know when to stop. The aim is not to make Combodied Agents permanently passive, but to keep their initiative bounded by consent, transparency, accountability, and meaningful human control.

8.3 脆弱和高风险设置Vulnerable and High-Stakes Settings

风险取决于用户容量和应用程序风险。本小节将弱势群体和医疗或心理健康环境聚集在一起，因为它们需要更严格的默认设置、更清晰的专业界限和可靠的升级，同时仍保留尊严和参与。Risk depends on both user capacity and application stakes. This subsection brings together vulnerable populations and medical or mental-health settings because they require stricter defaults, clearer professional boundaries, and reliable escalation while still preserving dignity and participation.

弱势用户。Vulnerable users.

儿童、青少年、老年人、患者、残疾人、经历孤独或心理健康危机的人以及认知能力下降的用户可能面临更高的风险。对于有能力的成年人来说可以接受的相同互动对于未成年人、社会孤立的用户或处于危机中的人来说可能是不安全的。脆弱性也可能是暂时的：疲劳、悲伤、疾病、压力或财务压力会降低用户评估影响力的能力。 保障措施应根据用户能力、背景和领域进行定制。这些可能包括适合年龄的互动限制、更严格的默认设置、减少说服性内容的个性化、护理人员或临床医生的升级途径，以及对亲密或形成依赖的互动的更强控制。保护不应成为家长式作风；弱势用户仍然需要尊严、参与和有意义的控制。Children, adolescents, older adults, patients, people with disabilities, people experiencing loneliness or mental-health crises, and users with cognitive decline may face heightened risk. The same interaction that is acceptable for a capable adult may be unsafe for a minor, a socially isolated user, or a person in crisis. Vulnerability may also be temporary: fatigue, grief, illness, stress, or financial pressure can reduce a user’s ability to evaluate influence. Safeguards should be tailored to user capacity, context, and domain. These may include age-appropriate interaction limits, stricter defaults, reduced personalization of persuasive content, caregiver or clinician escalation pathways, and stronger controls on intimate or dependency-forming interaction. Protection should not become paternalism; vulnerable users still require dignity, participation, and meaningful control.

医疗和心理健康风险。Medical and mental-health risks.

医疗和心理健康应用需要特别小心，因为错误会直接影响安全。与健康相关的组合药物必须区分健康支持、健康教育、临床决策支持、诊断和治疗。一个系统如果给临床权威带来不适当的保证、错过警告信号或提供不确定的指导，可能会延误护理或造成伤害。Medical and mental-health applications require special care because errors can directly affect safety. Health-related Combodied Agents must distinguish wellness support, health education, clinical decision support, diagnosis, and treatment. A system that gives inappropriate reassurance, misses warning signs, or presents uncertain guidance as clinical authority may delay care or cause harm.

心理健康环境尤其敏感。情感支持人员可能会遇到自残、虐待、妄想信念、严重焦虑或危机状态。治理应要求不确定性沟通、证据基础、危机协议、升级给合格的专业人员以及对代理人角色的明确限制。在高风险情况下，安全行为通常不是更好的对话，而是移交人工支持。Mental-health contexts are particularly sensitive. Emotional-support agents may encounter self-harm, abuse, delusional beliefs, severe anxiety, or crisis states. Governance should require uncertainty communication, evidence grounding, crisis protocols, escalation to qualified professionals, and clear limits on the agent’s role. In high-risk situations, the safe behavior is often not better conversation, but handoff to human support.

管理这些风险是必要的，但还不够。值得信赖的组合代理还需要超越更强大的基础模型或更大的上下文窗口的进步。核心的开放问题是从稀疏的纵向证据中了解个人动态和干预效果，将不确定的预测转化为机构一致的支持，维护可信的个人基础设施，并围绕用户的利益协调未来的系统。Managing these risks is necessary but insufficient. Trustworthy Combodied Agents also require advances beyond stronger foundation models or larger context windows. The core open problems are to learn personal dynamics and intervention effects from sparse longitudinal evidence, translate uncertain predictions into agency-aligned support, maintain trusted personal infrastructure, and coordinate future systems around the user’s interests.

8.4 纵向和因果学习Longitudinal and Causal Learning

第一个跨领域的挑战是了解个体如何变化，而不仅仅是检索以前记录的内容。这需要结合纵向证据、个人适应和干预反应学习，而不假设稀疏的观察揭示了一个完整或稳定的人。The first cross-cutting challenge is to learn how an individual changes, rather than merely retrieve what has previously been recorded. This requires joining longitudinal evidence, personal adaptation, and intervention-response learning without assuming that sparse observations reveal a complete or stable person.

学习个人动态。Learning individual dynamics.

个人轨迹在相互作用的时间尺度上展开：情绪可能在几个小时内发生变化，习惯可能会在几周内发生变化，能力、人际关系或健康状况可能会在几年内发生变化。模型必须区分临时状态和持久模式，使人口水平先验适应有限和有偏见的个人数据，表示不确定性，并在用户或后来的证据与推论相矛盾时保持可纠正性。Personal trajectories unfold across interacting timescales: mood may change within hours, habits across weeks, and capabilities, relationships, or health across years. Models must distinguish temporary states from persistent patterns, adapt population-level priors to limited and biased personal data, represent uncertainty, and remain correctable when the user or later evidence contradicts an inference.

因果干预学习。Causal intervention learning.

干预后观察到的改善并不能证明是干预造成的。用户动机、隐藏背景、并发事件和选择性参与可能会混淆提醒、指导、情感支持和升级。未来的研究必须结合干预反应历史、反事实推理和延迟结果来估计支持何时有帮助、没有效果或产生意外伤害。Observed improvement after an intervention does not establish that the intervention caused it. User motivation, hidden context, concurrent events, and selective engagement can confound reminders, coaching, emotional support, and escalation. Future research must combine intervention-response histories, counterfactual reasoning, and delayed outcomes to estimate when support helps, has no effect, or produces unintended harm.

安全验证。Safe validation.

学习个人动态不能依赖于不受约束的实验，尤其是对于儿童、老年人、患者或处于危机中的人。只有在同意、监控和升级适当的情况下，安全进展才需要有界模拟、回顾性和观察性证据、专家评审、分阶段部署和前瞻性研究。挑战在于验证有用的个人预测，同时防止评估过程本身成为不安全的干预措施。Learning personal dynamics cannot rely on unconstrained experimentation, especially for children, older adults, patients, or people in crisis. Safe progress requires bounded simulation, retrospective and observational evidence, expert review, staged deployment, and prospective studies only when consent, monitoring, and escalation are appropriate. The challenge is to validate useful personal predictions while preventing the evaluation process itself from becoming an unsafe intervention.

8.5 机构协调干预Agency-Aligned Intervention

第二个跨领域的挑战是将不确定的个人预测转化为支持，以改善人类的长期结果，而不使代理继续使用其隐含目标。因此，客观设计和干预校准必须作为一个问题来对待。The second cross-cutting challenge is to translate uncertain personal predictions into support that improves long-term human outcomes without making the agent’s continued use its implicit objective. Objective design and intervention calibration must therefore be treated as one problem.

长期目标。Long-horizon objectives.

福祉、能力、自主性、安全性和社会融合是多维的、延迟的，有时与即时满足感之间存在紧张关系。未来的系统需要目标来区分短期参与和持久利益，适应不断变化的用户价值观，并公开权衡而不是将其分解为单一奖励。Wellbeing, capability, autonomy, safety, and social integration are multidimensional, delayed, and sometimes in tension with immediate satisfaction. Future systems need objectives that distinguish short-term engagement from durable benefit, accommodate changing user values, and expose trade-offs rather than collapsing them into a single reward.

校准干预。Calibrated intervention.

代理必须决定何时保持沉默、通知、提醒、质疑、保护、执行或升级。干预不足可能会让用户得不到支持，而过度干预可能会变得侵入性、家长式或形成依赖性。需要研究不确定性政策、用户可调整的主动性、关系敏感的权威、可逆行动以及在不过度规范化的情况下调整干预强度的反馈机制。The agent must decide when to remain silent, inform, remind, challenge, protect, execute, or escalate. Under-intervention may leave the user unsupported, while over-intervention may become intrusive, paternalistic, or dependency-forming. Research is needed on uncertainty-aware policies, user-adjustable initiative, relationship-sensitive authority, reversible actions, and feedback mechanisms that adapt intervention intensity without normalizing overreach.

8.6 值得信赖的个人基础设施Trusted Personal Infrastructure

纵向支持还需要一个基础设施，在这个基础设施中，个人智能可以发展，而不会使用户的模型、记忆或干预权限超出有意义的控制范围。这一挑战涉及边缘功能、持续适应、云协作、所有权和安全性。Longitudinal support also requires an infrastructure in which personal intelligence can evolve without placing the user’s model, memory, or intervention authority beyond meaningful control. This challenge connects edge capability, continual adaptation, cloud collaboration, ownership, and security.

本地能力和安全演进。Local capability and safe evolution.

边缘设备必须在有限的计算、存储和能源下支持可靠的感知、内存检索、个人动态估计和策略执行。同时，持续的个性化必须检测不安全的漂移，保留不确定性，支持回滚，并允许用户检查、纠正、暂停或重置学习状态。Edge devices must support reliable perception, memory retrieval, personal dynamics estimation, and policy execution under limited computation, storage, and energy. At the same time, continual personalization must detect unsafe drift, preserve uncertainty, support rollback, and allow users to inspect, correct, pause, or reset learned state.

所有权、协作和安全。Ownership, collaboration, and security.

复杂的推理可能仍然需要云模型，但路由应该揭示离开设备的内容，最大限度地减少泄露，并通过目的有限的摘要来保留实用性。个人记忆和模型状态应该可以跨设备和提供商移植，可以由用户删除，并通过加密存储、访问控制、可信执行和可审核同步进行保护。Complex reasoning may still require cloud models, but routing should reveal what leaves the device, minimize disclosure, and preserve utility through purpose-limited summaries. Personal memories and model state should be portable across devices and providers, deletable by the user, and protected through encrypted storage, access control, trusted execution, and auditable synchronization.

8.7 新兴的综合生态系统Emerging Combodied Ecosystems

未来的组合代理可以作为个人数字孪生的接口或作为专业代理生态系统的成员。这些方向都存在一个协调问题：个人模型、建议和行动必须保持可解释性、冲突意识，并围绕用户的利益进行管理。Future Combodied Agents may operate as interfaces to personal digital twins or as members of ecosystems of specialized agents. These directions share a coordination problem: personal models, recommendations, and actions must remain interpretable, conflict-aware, and governed around the user’s interests.

个人数字双胞胎。Personal digital twins.

以健康为导向的组合智能体可能成为个人数字孪生的交互和干预层。数字孪生可以模拟生理、行为或临床轨迹；组合代理可以将该模型转化为解释、目标协商、日常指导和协调行动。 这种整合可以支持慢性病管理、康复、预防和生活方式干预，但也增加了风险。数字孪生可能是不确定的、不完整的或未经临床验证的。组合代理必须传达不确定性，避免日常生活过度医疗化，保护敏感数据，并澄清何时需要专业监督。未来的研究应该将可解释的个人模型与安全的面向用户的干预联系起来。Health-oriented Combodied Agents may become the interaction and intervention layer for personal digital twins. A digital twin may model physiological, behavioral, or clinical trajectories; the Combodied Agent can translate that model into explanations, goal negotiation, daily guidance, and coordinated action. This integration could support chronic disease management, rehabilitation, prevention, and lifestyle intervention, but it also raises risks. Digital twins may be uncertain, incomplete, or clinically unvalidated. Combodied Agents must communicate uncertainty, avoid over-medicalizing everyday life, protect sensitive data, and clarify when professional oversight is required. Future research should connect interpretable personal models with safe user-facing intervention.

多智能体协调。Multi-agent coordination.

未来的用户可能会与多个专业伴侣互动：健康伴侣、学习伴侣、工作伴侣、财务监护人、情感支持代理和家庭协调代理。这些系统可能提供有用的专业化，但它们也带来了冲突。工作场所伴侣可能会鼓励生产力，健康伴侣可能会建议休息，财务监护人可能会限制其他代理人建议的支出。 研究问题是以用户为中心的协调。组合生态系统需要优先级设定、冲突解决和跨主体一致性的机制。如果没有这种协调，多个有用的系统可能会共同变得混乱、侵入或失调。Future users may interact with multiple specialized companions: health companions, learning companions, workplace companions, financial guardians, emotional-support agents, and family coordination agents. These systems may offer useful specialization, but they also introduce conflicts. A workplace companion may encourage productivity, a health companion may recommend rest, and a financial guardian may constrain spending that another agent proposes. The research problem is user-centered coordination. Combodied ecosystems need mechanisms for priority setting, conflict resolution, and cross-agent consistency. Without such coordination, multiple helpful systems can collectively become confusing, invasive, or misaligned.

8.8 跨文化和寿命的未来Cross-Cultural and Lifespan Futures

综合需求因文化、语言、年龄、社会角色和人生阶段而异。关于隐私、家庭参与、情感表达、权威、护理、浪漫和医疗决策的规范存在很大差异。在一种情况下保留代理权的建议在另一种情况下可能是不合适的，甚至是有害的。 未来的组合特工必须具有文化敏感性和寿命意识。儿童、青少年、成人和老年人需要不同的互动方式、保障措施和发展假设。跨文化评估、本地化价值建模和参与式设计将至关重要。以人为中心的代理不能假设人的单一通用模型。Combodied needs vary across cultures, languages, ages, social roles, and life stages. Norms around privacy, family involvement, emotional expression, authority, care, romance, and medical decision-making differ substantially. A recommendation that preserves agency in one context may be inappropriate or even harmful in another. Future Combodied Agents must be culture-sensitive and lifespan-aware. Children, adolescents, adults, and older adults require different interaction styles, safeguards, and developmental assumptions. Cross-cultural evaluation, localized value modeling, and participatory design will be essential. A person-centric agent cannot assume a single universal model of the person.

总而言之，这些方向定义了以人为中心的研究议程，而不是为了自治而追求自治。衡量进展的标准应该是组合代理是否变得更加准确和有能力，同时保持可纠正性、文化定位、责任感以及与用户的长期代理保持一致。Taken together, these directions define a human-centered research agenda rather than a pursuit of autonomy for its own sake. Progress should be measured by whether Combodied Agents become more accurate and capable while remaining corrigible, culturally situated, accountable, and aligned with the user’s long-term agency.

9 结论Conclusion

本文提出组合代理作为一种以人为中心的代理人工智能范式，并开发了其闭环框架、分类法、部署视角和评估议程。其独特的挑战不仅仅是个性化或自动化，而是在不损害自主性、能力、安全或关系的情况下支持人类发展轨迹。This paper proposed Combodied Agents as a human-centric Agentic AI paradigm and developed its closed-loop framework, taxonomy, deployment perspective, and evaluation agenda. Its distinctive challenge is not simply to personalize or automate, but to support human trajectories without undermining autonomy, capability, safety, or relationships.

中心设计原则是组合代理应该采取行动The central design principle is that a Combodied Agent should act与with以维护和加强长期代理的方式向用户提供帮助。判断进步的标准应该是人们是否仍然能够理解、选择、纠正、恢复、发展能力、维持人际关系以及按照不断变化的价值观生活。the user in ways that preserve and strengthen long-term agency. Progress should be judged by whether people remain able to understand, choose, correct, recover, develop capability, sustain human relationships, and live according to their evolving values.
