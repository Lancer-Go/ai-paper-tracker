文化遗产联合持续学习的包容性轻量级方法An Inclusive and Lightweight Approach to Federated Continual Learning for Cultural Heritage

摘要Abstract

人工智能可以通过对数字化馆藏的大规模检索和分析来支持文化遗产和数字人文。然而，文化遗产数据通常分布在各个机构中，受到所有权和访问限制的限制，并且随着时间的推移不断发展。联合持续学习 (FCL) 非常适合这种设置，因为它使模型能够从分布式和顺序数据中学习，而无需共享原始集合。在本文中，我们提出了 FedCurv-DR，一种轻量级、基于正则化的 FCL 策略。该方法累积跨客户和经验的参数重要性估计，以保护学到的知识，同时仅以固定的时间间隔更新它们，以最大限度地减少通信和计算开销。我们使用 WikiArt 图像数据集在持续学习场景中评估 FedCurv-DR，以进行具有不断发展的风格、报告性能、能量和公平性指标的流派分类。我们的结果表明，FedCurv-DR 可以减少遗忘，并平衡性能、公平性和能源效率，实现文化遗产中的可持续人工智能。Artificial intelligence can support cultural heritage and digital humanities through large-scale retrieval and analysis of digitized collections. However, cultural heritage data are often distributed across institutions, constrained by ownership and access restrictions, and continuously evolving over time. Federated Continual Learning (FCL) is well suited to this setting, as it enables models to learn from distributed and sequential data without sharing raw collections. In this paper, we propose FedCurv-DR, a lightweight, regularisation-based FCL strategy. The method accumulates parameter-importance estimates across clients and experiences to protect learned knowledge, while updating them only at fixed intervals to minimize communication and computation overhead. We evaluate FedCurv-DR in a continual learning scenario using the WikiArt image dataset for genre classification with evolving styles, reporting performance, energy, and fairness metrics. Our results show that FedCurv-DR reduces forgetting and balances performance, fairness, and energy efficiency for sustainable AI in cultural heritage.

I 简介Introduction

人工智能越来越多地应用于包括文化遗产在内的许多领域AI is increasingly adopted across many domains, including cultural heritage[13]。然而，文化遗产数据提出了具体的伦理和技术挑战。藏品通常分布在各个机构，并通过数字化不断发展。数字化过程还可能重现现有的社会和文化偏见，包括少数群体的代表性不足或歪曲。然后，这些偏差可以被在此类集合上训练的人工智能管道继承或放大. However, cultural heritage data raise specific ethical and technical challenges. Collections are often distributed across institutions and continuously evolve through digitisation. Digitisation processes may also reproduce existing societal and cultural biases, including the under-representation or misrepresentation of minority groups. These biases can then be inherited or amplified by AI pipelines trained on such collections[14, 38]。这种偏见可能源于对文化遗产数据的不平等获取，因为所有权、版权、许可和隐私限制可能会限制用于计算研究和人工智能开发的馆藏的可用性. Such bias can derive from unequal access to cultural heritage data, since ownership, copyright, licensing, and privacy constraints can limit the availability of collections for computational research and AI development[9]。与此同时，机构参与数字化和人工智能采用的能力不平等可能会进一步加剧这种偏见，因为许多文化遗产组织面临资金有限、技术专业知识不足和资源密集型数字化要求. At the same time, the unequal capacity of institutions to participate in digitisation and AI adoption can further reinforce this bias, as many cultural heritage organisations face limited funding, insufficient technical expertise, and resource-intensive digitisation requirements[11].

这些挑战凸显了对绿色、值得信赖和包容的人工智能解决方案的需求——网络人文愿景的核心原则，它呼吁将技术创新与道德治理和公平参与相结合的文化遗产技术These challenges highlight the need for AI solutions that are green, trustworthy and inclusive - core principles of the Cyber Humanities vision, which calls for cultural heritage technologies that combine technical innovation with ethical governance and equitable participation[2, 5]。 联邦学习 (FL) 无需共享原始数据即可实现协作模型训练. Federated Learning (FL) enables collaborative model training without sharing raw data[23]，而持续学习（CL）使模型能够适应顺序到达的数据，而无需从头开始重新训练。它们的组合——联合持续学习（FCL）——可以支持绿色人工智能目标，并且非常适合文化遗产环境, while Continual Learning (CL) enables models to adapt to sequentially arriving data without retraining from scratch. Their combination, Federated Continual Learning (FCL), can support green AI objectives and is well suited to cultural heritage settings[29]。如图所示。. As shown in Fig.1，世界各地的艺术机构可以在其私人和不断发展的收藏上协作训练人工智能模型，同时尊重数据敏感性。然而，为了确保平等参与这一过程，部署此类系统时必须考虑能源效率和公平性。, art institutions across the world can collaboratively train AI models on their private and evolving collections while respecting data sensitivity. However, to ensure equal participation in this process, energy efficiency and fairness must be considered when deploying such systems.

We introduce FedCurv-DR, a regularisation-based strategy that periodically aggregates parameter-importance information across clients to adapt to new data, or experiences, while preserving performance on past data without relying on replay buffers or computationally intensive methods such as synthetic data generation. We evaluate FedCurv-DR on WikiArt, a large-scale public art dataset relevant to cultural heritage and digital humanities. By treating genre classification as the fixed objective and introducing artistic styles sequentially as new experiences, we model a realistic domain-incremental scenario where digitised cultural collections evolve over time.

II 相关作品Related Works

Ⅱ-AII-A 联邦学习Federated Learning

在联邦学习（FL）中，多个客户端在中央服务器的协调下协作训练共享的全局模型，同时保持数据本地和私有。客户端不交换原始数据，而是在本地训练模型并定期将模型更新发送到服务器进行聚合。用于组合这些更新的服务器端聚合算法通常称为 FL 策略。代表性的基准 FL 策略是 FedAvgIn Federated Learning (FL), multiple clients collaboratively train a shared global model under the coordination of a central server, while keeping their data local and private. Instead of exchanging raw data, clients train models locally and periodically send model updates to the server for aggregation. The server-side aggregation algorithms used to combine these updates are commonly referred to as FL strategies. A representative baseline FL strategy is FedAvg[23]，它通过根据本地数据集的大小加权的平均客户端参数来构建全局模型。, which constructs the global model by averaging client parameters weighted according to the sizes of the local datasets.

尽管 FL 在隐私保护和可扩展性方面具有优势，但它也带来了几个关键挑战。在现实环境中，客户端数据通常不是独立且同分布的（非 IID）。例如，在文化遗产中，国家博物馆和当地画廊之间的文物数量可能有很大差异，而语义分布可能因风格、时期或艺术运动而异。这种异质性可能会导致局部模型在训练过程中出现分歧，因为每个客户端都会针对自己的数据分布进行优化，从而阻碍全局模型的收敛。这种现象通常称为客户端漂移Despite its advantages for privacy preservation and scalability, FL introduces several key challenges. In real-world settings, client data are typically not independent and identically distributed (non-IID). In cultural heritage, for example, the number of artifacts may vary substantially between national museums and local galleries, while semantic distributions may differ across styles, periods, or art movements. This heterogeneity can cause local models to diverge during training, as each client optimizes toward its own data distribution, thereby hindering the convergence of the global model. This phenomenon is commonly referred to as inter-client drift[25]。为了应对异构数据条件下的这一挑战，人们提出了几种 FL 策略. Several FL strategies have been proposed to address this challenge under heterogeneous data conditions[16, 18, 27, 32, 4].

然而，虽然这些方法中的许多方法旨在提高非独立同分布条件下的性能，但它们可能仍然有利于更大或更具代表性的客户，从而导致模型在主导数据分布上表现更好，同时忽略代表性不足的参与者。这可能会引起或强化公平性问题，特别是在未明确报告客户之间的绩效差异的情况下。因此，有几项工作研究了 FL 的公平性，并提出了公平意识策略来减轻客户层面的偏见However, while many of these methods aim to improve performance under non-IID conditions, they may still favor larger or more representative clients, leading to models that perform better for dominant data distributions while neglecting under-represented participants. This can introduce or reinforce fairness concerns, particularly when performance disparities across clients are not explicitly reported. Several works have therefore investigated fairness in FL and proposed fairness-aware strategies to mitigate client-level bias[17, 12, 35, 20, 36].

Ⅱ-BII-B 持续学习Continual Learning

持续学习（CL）解决了随着时间的推移从任务流或数据分布中学习的问题，而无需从头开始重新训练。这种情况下的一个主要挑战是灾难性遗忘Continual Learning (CL) addresses the problem of learning from a stream of tasks or data distributions over time, without retraining from scratch. A central challenge in this setting is catastrophic forgetting[15]，模型在接受新任务训练时会迅速丢失先前获得的知识。为了减轻这种情况，已经提出了几种方法。基于正则化的方法，例如弹性权重合并 (EWC), where the model rapidly loses previously acquired knowledge when trained on new tasks. To mitigate this, several families of methods have been proposed. Regularization-based approaches such as Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC)[15]，记忆感知突触（MAS）, Memory Aware Synapses (MAS)[3]和突触智能 (SI), and Synaptic Intelligence (SI)[34]估计模型参数对于过去任务的重要性，并在后续训练期间对这些参数的更改进行惩罚。或者，基于重放的方法estimate the importance of model parameters for past tasks and penalize changes to those parameters during subsequent training. Alternatively, replay-based methods[22, 24]维护过去示例的缓冲区，允许模型在学习新信息的同时演练以前的知识。在各种持续学习场景中，重播方法已被证明优于基于正则化的方法maintain a buffer of past examples, allowing the model to rehearse previous knowledge while learning new information. Replay methods have been shown to outperform regularization-based methods in various continual learning scenarios[31]，但它们提出了可扩展性和隐私相关的问题，因为过去的数据需要存储。, but they raise scalability and privacy-related issues, as past data need to be stored.

Ⅱ-CII-C 联邦持续学习Federated Continual Learning

联邦持续学习（FCL）结合了联邦学习和持续学习的挑战，要求模型同时应对客户端之间的数据异构性以及新数据或体验的顺序到达。在这种情况下，必须减轻客户端之间的漂移和灾难性遗忘，从而使学习过程变得更加复杂。已经提出了几种方法来应对这些挑战Federated Continual Learning (FCL) combines the challenges of Federated Learning and Continual Learning, requiring models to simultaneously cope with data heterogeneity across clients and the sequential arrival of new data or experiences. In this setting, both inter-client drift and catastrophic forgetting must be mitigated, making the learning process significantly more complex. Several approaches have been proposed to address these challenges[33, 30, 37].

尽管它们很有效，但许多 FCL 方法引入了额外的计算和通信开销，例如通过合成数据生成Despite their effectiveness, many FCL methods introduce additional computational and communication overhead, for example through synthetic data generation[37]。这些因素会显着增加能源消耗，特别是在大规模或资源有限的部署中，甚至可能使此类方法在实践中不可行。这是包容性文化遗产人工智能生态系统的一个重要考虑因素。与基于蒸馏或生成的 FCL 方法相比，我们的方法不需要重放数据、内存缓冲区或计算密集型机制。相反，它依赖于参数重要性正则化，从而减少通信和计算开销。. These factors can significantly increase energy consumption, particularly in large-scale or resource-constrained deployments, and may even make such methods infeasible in practice. This is a critical consideration for an inclusive cultural heritage AI ecosystem. In contrast to distillation- or generation-based FCL methods, our approach does not require replay data, memory buffers, or computationally intensive mechanisms. Instead, it relies on parameter-importance regularization with reduced communication and computational overhead.

VI 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future work

在本文中，我们提出了一种用于包容性和轻量级联合持续学习的轻量级方法。结果表明，所提出的方法减轻了灾难性遗忘，同时通过基于间隔的重要性更新减少了能量足迹。我们还观察到，异构环境中客户层面的公平性得到了改善，这是文化遗产场景中减轻偏见的一个重要属性，在这些场景中，机构可能在馆藏规模、数据质量和风格分布方面存在差异。这项研究的局限性在于，实验使用了跨三个模拟客户端综合划分的公共数据集。这种受控设置使我们能够隔离遗忘、客户差异和能源消耗的影响，但并不能完全捕获真正的机构部署限制。 FedCurv-DR 直接兼容部署级隐私机制；实际系统应将其与安全聚合相结合In this paper, we proposed a lightweight approach for inclusive and lightweight Federated Continual Learning. The results show that the proposed method mitigates catastrophic forgetting while reducing the energy footprint through interval-based importance updates. We also observed improved client-level fairness in heterogeneous settings, an important property for bias mitigation in cultural heritage scenarios where institutions may differ in collection size, data quality, and style distribution. A limitation of this study is that the experiments use a public dataset synthetically partitioned across three simulated clients. This controlled setup allow us to isolate the effects of forgetting, client disparity, and energy consumption, but does not fully capture real institutional deployment constraints. FedCurv-DR is directly compatible with deployment-level privacy mechanisms; practical systems should combine it with secure aggregation[7]和差异隐私and differential privacy[1]限制模型更新中的信息泄漏。在实际的机构部署中，FL 服务器需要由受信任的中立方（例如国家遗产机构或研究基础设施）进行操作，并具有明确的治理程序，涵盖泄漏审计、知识产权归属和贡献馆藏的许可，与欧洲人工智能和文化遗产数据政策保持一致to bound information leakage from model updates. In real institutional deployments, the FL server would require operation by a trusted neutral party, such as a national heritage agency or research infrastructure, with defined governance procedures covering leakage auditing, intellectual property attribution, and licensing of contributed collections, in alignment with European AI and cultural heritage data policies[10]。 未来的工作将评估更大规模和真实的分布式环境，以及消融研究. Future work will evaluate larger-scale and real distributed settings, together with ablation studies onII, β\beta, 和, andλ\lambda.
