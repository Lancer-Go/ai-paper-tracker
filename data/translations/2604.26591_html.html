1. 简介Introduction

逻辑综合在电路设计过程中起着至关重要的作用，主要包括两个子过程：逻辑优化Logic synthesis plays a pivotal role in the circuit design process, primarily comprising two sub-processes: logic optimization(lee2023peephole; dc_rewriting; fu2026chop; fu2026dclog; fu2026elogic)和技术映射and technology mapping(ABC; calvino2022mapping)。在这些阶段中，技术映射尤其重要，因为它弥合了逻辑设计和物理设计之间的差距。. Among these stages, technology mapping is particularly crucial as it bridges the gap between logical design and physical design.

现有的技术映射优化方法主要集中在三种方法： i) 剪切选择以过滤逻辑级或物理级优越剪切，从而获得更好的门级网表，例如优先级剪切Existing optimization methods for technology mapping primarily focus on three approaches: i) cut selection to filter for logic-level or physical-level superior cuts, thereby obtaining better gate-level netlists, such as Priority Cuts(alan2007priority), 拍打, SLAP(neto2021slap), 飞跃, LEAP(chigarapally2025LEAP)和 PigMAP, and PigMAP(pan2025pigmap); ii) 多输出或特定于应用程序的单元利用，例如 emap; ii) multi-output or application-specific cell exploitation, such as emap(calvino2023emap)和双输出 LUTand dual-output LUT(shang2025dualoutput); iii) 标准细胞库过滤和扩展以缩小搜索范围或提高细胞质量，例如MapTune; iii) standard cell library filtering and extension to shrink the search range or improve cell quality, such as MapTune(liu2025maptune)和TeMACLEand TeMACLE(fu2025temacle)。 虽然这些进步提高了映射质量，但它们主要改进了启发式参数和搜索策略，而不是映射器本身的核心算法逻辑。. While these advances improve mapping quality, they predominantly refine heuristic parameters and search strategies rather than the core algorithmic logic within the mapper itself.
