摘要Abstract

强化学习已成为训练大型语言模型以执行搜索增强推理的有效范例。 然而，现有的方法依赖于轨迹级奖励，无法将精确的搜索查询与推出组中模糊或冗余的查询区分开来，并且每当每个采样轨迹失败时都会崩溃到接近零的梯度信号。 在本文中，我们建议Reinforcement learning has emerged as an effective paradigm for training large language models to perform search-augmented reasoning. However, existing approaches rely on trajectory-level rewards that cannot distinguish precise search queries from vague or redundant ones within a rollout group, and collapse to a near-zero gradient signal whenever every sampled trajectory fails. In this paper, we proposeIG-搜索IG-Search，一个强化学习框架，引入了基于信息增益（IG）的阶梯级奖励。 对于每个搜索步骤，IG 都会测量检索到的文档相对于随机文档的反事实基线提高了模型对黄金答案的置信度，从而反映了基础搜索查询的有效性。 该信号通过 GRPO 中的每个令牌优势调制反馈到相应的搜索查询令牌，从而在部署过程中实现细粒度、步骤级的信用分配。 与之前需要外部注释的中间监督或跨轨迹共享环境状态的步骤级方法不同，IG-Search 从策略自身的生成概率中获取信号，不需要标准问答对之外的中间注释。 对七个单跳和多跳 QA 基准的实验表明，IG-Search 使用 Qwen2.5-3B 实现了 0.430 的平均 EM，在整个基准测试中比最强的轨迹级基线 (MR-Search) 平均高出 1.6 分，比步骤级方法 GiGPO 平均高出 0.9 分，在多跳推理任务上的增益尤其明显。 尽管引入了密集的阶梯级信号，IG-Search 仅添加了, a reinforcement learning framework that introduces a step-level reward based on Information Gain (IG). For each search step, IG measures how much the retrieved documents improve the model’s confidence in the gold answer relative to a counterfactual baseline of random documents, thereby reflecting the effectiveness of the underlying search query. This signal is fed back to the corresponding search-query tokens via per-token advantage modulation in GRPO, enabling fine-grained, step-level credit assignment within a rollout. Unlike prior step-level methods that require either externally annotated intermediate supervision or shared environment states across trajectories, IG-Search derives its signals from the policy’s own generation probabilities, requiring no intermediate annotations beyond standard question-answer pairs. Experiments on seven single-hop and multi-hop QA benchmarks demonstrate that IG-Search achieves an average EM of 0.430 with Qwen2.5-3B, outperforming the strongest trajectory-level baseline (MR-Search) by 1.6 points and the step-level method GiGPO by 0.9 points on average across benchmarks, with particularly pronounced gains on multi-hop reasoning tasks. Recent advances demonstrate that reinforcement learning (RL) further enhances LLMs’ reasoning capabilities[6, 19]，尤其是在解决数学问题等复杂任务中, especially in complex tasks such as mathematical problem solving[26]和代码生成and code generation[5]。 然而，法学硕士中编码的知识本质上受到其训练语料库的限制，限制了其在需要最新或专门事实信息的任务上的推理性能. However, the knowledge encoded in LLMs is inherently bounded by their training corpora, limiting their reasoning performance on tasks that require up-to-date or specialized factual information[9, 25].

The core idea is simple: given a fixed retriever, the quality of a search query is directly reflected in the documents it retrieves, so we can evaluate each query by measuring the informational value of those documents. Concretely, for each search step, IG quantifies how much the actual retrieved documents improve the model’s confidence in the gold answer relative to a counterfactual baseline of random documents. This signal is then propagated back to the corresponding query tokens through per-token advantage modulation within GRPO, so that different search steps within the same trajectory receive gradients proportional to their informational contribution, rather than sharing a single trajectory-level advantage. Crucially, because IG is computed independently of whether the final answer is correct, the step-level signal remains informative even when all trajectories fail. We further develop stabilization mechanisms to ensure robust training, detailed in §2.4.

5 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

我们提出了 IG-Search，这是一个强化学习框架，它为每个单独的搜索步骤分配不同的奖励。 该奖励衡量检索到的文档相对于随机反事实基线提高了策略对黄金答案的信心的程度。 这种重新表述将稀疏的轨迹级信号转变为密集的、基于查询的信号，从而能够在部署过程中跨各个搜索步骤进行每个令牌的信用分配。 对七个 QA 基准的实验表明，IG-Search 在 3B 和 7B 规模的同等规模的开源模型中取得了最强的结果，在轨迹级信用分配更脆弱的多跳基准上获得了更大的收益。We have presented IG-Search, a reinforcement learning framework that assigns a distinct reward to each individual search step. This reward measures how much the retrieved documents improve the policy’s confidence in the gold answer relative to a random counterfactual baseline. This reformulation turns a sparse, trajectory-level signal into a dense, query-grounded one, enabling per-token credit assignment across individual search steps within a rollout. Experiments on seven QA benchmarks show that IG-Search achieves the strongest results among open-source models of comparable size at both the 3B and 7B scales, with larger gains on multi-hop benchmarks where trajectory-level credit assignment is more brittle.
