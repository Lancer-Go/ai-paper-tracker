摘要Abstract

过程奖励模型可以对法学硕士进行细粒度、步骤级的评估，但为代理环境构建它们仍然非常困难：长期交互、不可逆行为和随机环境反馈使得人工注释和蒙特卡罗估计在规模上都不可行。 在这项工作中，我们表明强化学习（RL）训练后已经提供了有效的步骤级评分的要素，完全消除了专门奖励模型训练的需要。具体来说，我们在一般随机马尔可夫决策过程下得出隐式优势，我们将其称为Process reward models enable fine-grained, step-level evaluation of LLMs, yet building them for agentic settings remains prohibitively difficult: long-horizon interactions, irreversible actions, and stochastic environment feedback make both human annotation and Monte Carlo estimation infeasible at scale. 6 结论Conclusion

配备代理工具的法学硕士在随机性下的多轮、交互式环境中运行，在这种环境中，衡量目标导向轨迹上的中间进度带来了巨大的机会；与此同时，真正的瓶颈是使用过程注释对 PRM 进行昂贵的训练。 我们通过进步优势为随机MDP中的隐性奖励技术奠定了理论基础；提供了一种新颖的角度和方法，可以从经过 RL 微调的 LLM 检查点对及其基础构建 PRM，绕过收集过程标签和专用奖励模型训练。 在五个代理基准、四个模型系列和三个下游应用程序中，进步优势始终优于基于置信度的基线，并匹配或击败基于竞争性培训的奖励模型以及专有的法学硕士法官。 我们希望这些有希望的结果能够激发未来研究的新范式，以实现可扩展且实用的方法来对现实世界的代理系统进行过程级指导和监控。LLMs equipped with the agentic harness operate on multi-turn, interactive environments under stochasticity, where measuring the intermediate progress over a goal-oriented trajectory brings huge opportunities; at the same time, the real bottleneck—expensive training of PRMs with process annotations. We establish the theoretical foundation through progress advantage for implicit reward technology in stochastic MDP; offer a novel angle and recipe to build PRMs from LLM checkpoint pairs of RL fine-tuned and its base, bypassing collecting the process labels and dedicated reward model training. Across five agentic benchmarks, four model families, and three downstream applications, progress advantage consistently outperforms confidence-based baselines and matches or beats competitive training-based reward models as well as a proprietary LLM judge. We hope these promising results stimulate a new paradigm of future research towards a scalable and practical approach to process-level guidance and monitoring of real-world agentic systems.
