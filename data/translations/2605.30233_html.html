7 结论Conclusion

实体跟踪是许多推理问题所必需的基本技能。我们对具有多个状态改变操作的自然 ET 的研究为理解 Transformer LM 提供了一些发现。我们发现模型不会跨令牌或层增量跟踪状态，而是在最终查询短语的先前上下文中动态聚合信息。机理研究表明Entity tracking is a fundamental skill necessary for many reasoning problems. Our investigation with naturalistic ET with multiple state-changing operations contributes several findings towards understanding transformer LMs. We find models do not track states incrementally across tokens or layers, but rather, dynamically aggregate information in prior contexts at the final query phrase. Based on this prediction, we design new diagnostic examples that surface the predicted degeneracies. Our work illustrates how behavioral and mechanistic analyses can interact productively.
