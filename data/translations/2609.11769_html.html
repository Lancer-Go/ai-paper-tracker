认识不是逆转：保留事实的新闻框架的受控倒置测试Recognizing Is Not Reversing: A Controlled Inversion Test of Fact-Preserving News Framing

刘毅Yi Liu

中国科学技术大学 scnuliuyi@mail.ustc.edu.cnUniversity of Science and Technology of China scnuliuyi@mail.ustc.edu.cn

摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地用于分析和重写新闻，但当前的框架研究主要评估生成、检测或重写的文本是否显得更中性。它们没有直接表明模型是否可以在保持事实固定的情况下撤消已知的框架转换。我们对三种已建立的框架文本实现引入了受控反转测试：评估词汇、代理实现和信息显着性。通过 60 篇新闻文章和三种干预优势，这产生了 540 个配对变体，并保留了原子事实和记录的编辑。在 Qwen、DeepSeek 和 Kimi 中，事实保留率仍接近 0.84，而干预逆转率为 0.044-0.068。即使正确识别了框架类型和方向，合并反转也达到 0.071。这些结果揭示了事实保真度、框架识别和框架倒置之间的明显区别：识别文章的框架并不意味着框架可以被撤销。Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used to analyze and rewrite news, yet current framing studies mainly evaluate generation, detection, or whether rewritten text appears more neutral. They do not directly show whether a model can undo a known framing transformation while keeping the facts fixed. We introduce a controlled inversion test over three established textual realizations of framing: evaluative lexis, agency realization, and information salience. Across 60 news articles and three intervention strengths, this yields 540 paired variants with preserved atomic facts and recorded edits. Across Qwen, DeepSeek, and Kimi, factual preservation remains near 0.84, whereas intervention reversal is 0.044–0.068. Even when both framing type and direction are recognized correctly, pooled reversal reaches 0.071. These results reveal a clear separation between factual fidelity, framing recognition, and framing inversion: recognizing how an article is framed does not imply that the framing can be undone.

1 简介1 Introduction

法学硕士正在成为计算社会科学的实用工具，支持社会文本的注释、解释和大规模分析[1-3]。新闻是一个重要的背景，因为框架可以改变同一事件的解释方式，而无需改变其核心事实。传播理论通过选择和显着性来描述框架，包括信息的放置[4]，而新闻话语分析则通过句法、主题和修辞组织来定位框架线索[5]。语言学工作提供了相应的实现机制：评价理论将评价性措辞视为立场的系统资源[6]，新闻的及物性分析表明有关行动者和目标的语法选择塑造了责任的表征[7]。这激发了三个实现层次：评价词汇、代理实现和信息显着性，对应于新闻框架中的词汇、从句和话语资源。LLMs are becoming practical instruments for computational social science, supporting annotation, explanation, and large scale analysis of social text [1–3]. News is a consequential setting because framing can change how the same event is interpreted without requiring a change in its core facts. Communication theory characterizes framing through selection and salience, including the placement of information [4], while news discourse analysis locates framing cues across syntactic, thematic, and rhetorical organization [5]. Linguistic work supplies corresponding realization mechanisms: appraisal theory treats evaluative wording as a systematic resource for stance [6], and transitivity analyses of news show that grammatical choices about actors and goals shape representations of responsibility [7]. This motivates three realization levels: evaluative lexis, agency realization, and information salience, corresponding to lexical, clause, and discourse resources in news framing.

最近的法学硕士工作衡量有偏见的新闻生成、框架检测、摘要偏见和中和[8-12]。这些任务揭示了框架是否被产生、识别或减少，但不揭示模型是否可以逆转已知的呈现变化。我们使正向变换变得可观察。来自参考文章Recent LLM work measures biased news generation, framing detection, summary bias, and neutralization [8–12]. These tasks reveal whether framing is produced, recognized, or reduced, but not whether a model can reverse a known presentation change. We make the forward transformation observable. 2 相关工作2 Related Work

新闻框架和计算媒体偏见。News framing and computational media bias.经典的框架工作将新闻解释与选择、显着性、责任和重复出现的问题框架联系起来[4,13,14]。 Pan 和 Kosicki 通过句法、脚本、主题和修辞结构来操作框架 [5]；语言学解释通过评价立场[6]、通过及物性进行的演员表征[7]以及将新闻故事组织为话语[15]来补充这一观点。然后，计算资源使框架和偏见可以大规模测量，包括媒体框架语料库、BASIL、MBIC 和更广泛的媒体偏见调查 [16-19]。Classic framing work links news interpretation to selection, salience, responsibility, and recurring issue frames [4,13,14]. Pan and Kosicki operationalize framing through syntactic, script, thematic, and rhetorical structures [5]; linguistic accounts complement this view with evaluative stance [6], actor representation through transitivity [7], and the organization of news stories as discourse [15]. Computational resources then make framing and bias measurable at scale, including the Media Frames Corpus, BASIL, MBIC, and broader surveys of media bias [16–19].

LLM测量、框架和重写。LLM measurement, framing, and rewriting.法学硕士现在被用作注释者和社会测量工具[1-3]。以新闻为中心的工作涵盖生成偏见、框架检测、摘要公平性和中和性[8-10,12,20,21]；相关的重构工作研究了跨度层面的偏见重写和积极重构[11,22]。 HELM 和更广泛的调查激发了多维评估 [23,24]，而 BBQ、CrowS-Pairs 和 BOLD 则提供互补的偏差测试 [25-27]。提示格式和示例顺序会影响模型行为[28,29]，而显式推理可以改变性能或忠实度[30-34]。受控反转增加了一个不同的目标：因为源、事实库存和前向干预是已知的，所以可以根据产生输入的表示变化对重建进行评分。LLMs are now used as annotators and social measurement tools [1–3]. News-focused work covers generated bias, framing detection, summary fairness, and neutralization [8–10,12,20,21]; related reframing work studies span-level bias rewriting and positive reframing [11,22]. HELM and broader surveys motivate multi-dimensional evaluation [23,24], while BBQ, CrowS-Pairs, and BOLD provide complementary bias tests [25–27]. Prompt formatting and example order affect model behavior [28,29], and explicit reasoning can alter performance or faithfulness [30–34]. Controlled inversion adds a different target: because the source, factual inventory, and forward intervention are known, reconstruction can be scored against the presentation change that produced the input.

3.2 构建与评估任务3.2 Construction and evaluation tasks

该基准包含 60 个英文新闻源，每个都规范化为 180-260 个单词，并由 6-10 个原子事实表示。三个操作员具有三种优势，每个源产生 9 个框架变体，总共 540 个变体；每个源和操作员对方向分配一次，有 90 个有利分配和 90 个不利分配。不相交的 10 源开发集在评估之前修复了提示模板和干预预算。The benchmark contains 60 English news sources, each canonicalized to 180–260 words and represented by 6–10 atomic facts. Three operators at three strengths yield nine framed variants per source, for 540 variants in total; direction is assigned once per source and operator pair with 90 favorable and 90 unfavorable assignments. A disjoint 10-source development set fixes prompt templates and intervention budgets before evaluation.

受控变体是在受限生成和验证程序下使用 GLM-5.2 生成的。生成器接收参考文章、不可变事实、目标参与者、操作员、方向和强度，并返回转换后的文章及其编辑图。验证强制执行事实保留、不存在无支持的添加、所请求的框架条件、专业合理性和操作员隔离。所有 540 种构建的变体均满足这些检查，为 540 种经验证的框架制品和 60 种清洁对照提供了基准。Controlled variants are produced with GLM-5.2 under a constrained generation and validation procedure. The generator receives the reference article, immutable facts, target actor, operator, direction, and strength, and returns the transformed article together with its edit map. Validation enforces factual preservation, absence of unsupported additions, the requested framing condition, professional plausibility, and operator isolation. All 540 constructed variants satisfy these checks, giving a benchmark of 540 validated framed articles plus 60 clean controls.

4.2 事实得以生存，而框架仍然存在4.2 Facts survive while frames persist

在所有三个模型系列中，重建保留事实内容的可靠性远远高于反转注入框架的可靠性：FactF1 保持在 0.84 附近，而 IRR 为 0.044-0.068（表 1）。这是本文的第一次分离：事实保真度和框架恢复衡量不同的能力。模型配置文件在各个阶段之间进一步分离：Kimi 领先于 Macro-F1 和精确识别，Qwen 获得最高的总体 IRR，而 DeepSeek 的干净误报率最低。因此，受控反演将灵敏度、校准和回收率暴露为不同的轴，而不是单个聚合等级。Across all three model families, reconstruction preserves factual content far more reliably than it reverses the injected framing: FactF1 remains near 0.84, whereas IRR lies at 0.044–0.068 (Table 1). This is the paper’s first separation: factual fidelity and framing recovery measure different capabilities. The model profiles separate further across stages: Kimi leads Macro-F1 and exact recognition, Qwen attains the highest overall IRR, and DeepSeek the lowest clean false positive rate. Controlled inversion therefore exposes sensitivity, calibration, and recovery as distinct axes rather than a single aggregate rank.

4.3 识别不是逆转4.3 Recognizing is not reversing

18.9% 的对齐重建出现准确识别。以精确识别为条件，将合并 IRR 从 0.049 提高到 0.071（表 2），相对增加 45%；各模型的增益范围为 0.018 至 0.030。然而，在该子集中 1,052 个注入编辑中，有 75 个被逆转，因此尽管类型和方向正确，但 92.9% 的记录干预仍然存在。图 2(c) 中的 S011 案例使这种分离具体化：DeepSeek 识别显着机制和有利方向，而重构则保持框架排序不变，产生 IRR = 0。因此，条件计数将反转隔离为附加操作，而不是识别的自动结果。Exact recognition occurs in 18.9% of aligned reconstructions. Conditioning on exact recognition raises pooled IRR from 0.049 to 0.071 (Table 2), a 45% relative increase; gains across models range from 0.018 to 0.030. Yet 75 of 1,052 injected edits are reversed in this subset, so 92.9% of logged interventions remain despite correct type and direction. The S011 case in Figure 2(c) makes this separation concrete: DeepSeek identifies the salience mechanism and favorable direction, while reconstruction leaves the framed ordering unchanged, yielding IRR = 0. The conditional counts therefore isolate inversion as an additional operation rather than an automatic consequence of recognition.

4.4 帧在何处编码很重要4.4 Where framing is encoded matters

机制模式与干预的语言轨迹相一致。评价词汇是局部明确的，并且每个模型都具有最高类别的 F1。显着性分布在位置和文档结构上，并且在所有三个系列中具有最低的逆转率。跨阶段的排名尤其提供了丰富的信息：词汇框架是所有三个模型中最容易检测的机制，但代理机构对于 DeepSeek 和 Kimi 具有更高的 IRR，而对于 Qwen 来说，词汇框架具有较高的 IRR。因此提示可见性并不决定编辑恢复。 FactF1 在各个操作员中保持在 0.83-0.84 的狭窄范围内，将机制效应集中在框架恢复而不是事实保留方面。The mechanism pattern aligns with the linguistic locus of the intervention. Evaluative lexis is locally explicit and has the highest class F1 for every model. Salience is distributed over placement and document structure and has the lowest reversal rate in all three families. The ranking across stages is especially informative: lexical framing is the easiest mechanism to detect in all three models, yet agency has higher IRR for DeepSeek and Kimi and ties lexical for Qwen. Thus cue visibility does not determine edit recovery. FactF1 remains in the narrow 0.83–0.84 range across operators, concentrating the mechanism effect in framing recovery rather than factual retention.

强度揭示了可见性梯度。从低强度到高强度，DeepSeek、Qwen 和 Kimi 的方向精度分别提高了 0.16、0.13 和 0.09，而 IRR 分别提高了 0.043、0.047 和 0.038。 FactF1 的变化要小得多。因此，相同的顺序出现在所有三个模型系列以及识别和反演中：较弱的框架保留了事实基础，同时使表示变化不那么明显。重要的是，每个模型（0.059、0.085 和 0.069）在高强度下的 IRR 都保持在 0.09 以下，因此即使在受控尺度的最明显端，反演分离仍然存在。Strength reveals a visibility gradient. From low to high strength, direction accuracy rises by .16, .13, and .09 for DeepSeek, Qwen, and Kimi, while IRR rises by .043, .047, and .038. FactF1 changes much less. The same ordering therefore appears across all three model families and both recognition and inversion: weaker framing preserves the factual substrate while making the presentation change less visible. Importantly, IRR at high strength remains below .09 for every model (.059, .085, and .069), so the inversion separation persists even at the most visible end of the controlled scale.

4.5 本土思维下的分离依然存在4.5 The separation persists under native thinking

本土思维使中心分离完好无损。 IRR 跨模型变化不均匀（DeepSeek 为 +.025，Qwen 为 -021，Kimi 为 +.000），而每个模型的 FactF1 变化均小于 0.01。因此，在第二个推理配置下，识别和反演之间的分离仍然可见。Native thinking leaves the central separation intact. IRR shifts heterogeneously across models (+.025 for DeepSeek, -.021 for Qwen, and +.000 for Kimi), while FactF1 changes by less than .01 for every model. The separation between recognition and inversion therefore remains visible under a second inference configuration.

5 讨论5 Discussion

受控反演将 LLM 新闻分析转变为多轴测量问题。干净的控件暴露了校准，框架变体暴露了敏感性，源文章与记录的编辑图一起将事实保真度与实际校正分开。由此产生的概况显示了为什么语料库编码、编辑审核和数据管理受益于单独报告这些数量，而不是将它们压缩成一个质量分数[23,24]。它们还激励分阶段进行模型选择，因为不同模型系列中的识别、干净校准和反演峰值。Controlled inversion turns LLM news analysis into a measurement problem with multiple axes. Clean controls expose calibration, framed variants expose sensitivity, and the source article together with the logged edit map separates factual fidelity from actual correction. The resulting profiles show why corpus coding, editorial auditing, and data curation benefit from reporting these quantities separately rather than compressing them into one quality score [23,24]. They also motivate model selection by stage because recognition, clean calibration, and inversion peak in different model families.

排名本身就说明了为什么各个阶段应该保持分开。 Kimi 的准确识别度 (.312) 超过了 Qwen 的 (.130)，而 Qwen 的 IRR 更高（0.068 比 0.054）。单个总分将隐藏模型顺序中的这种逆转。相反，受控反转使得从检测器到重建器的切换可以在具有固定事实的一个逆目标下进行测量，从而允许可审计的管道为每个阶段实际需要的功能选择检测和恢复组件。The ranking itself demonstrates why the stages should remain separate. Kimi’s exact recognition (.312) exceeds Qwen’s (.130), while Qwen has higher IRR (.068 versus .054). A single aggregate score would hide this reversal in model order. Controlled inversion instead makes the handoff from detector to reconstructor measurable under one inverse objective with fixed facts, allowing an auditable pipeline to choose detection and recovery components for the capability each stage actually requires.

对于使用 LLM 进行编辑，相同的分解还提供了可验证的工作流程：检测识别呈现机制，重建执行编辑，IRR 根据已知的干预而不是仅根据表面流畅性检查结果。这使得受控反转不仅可以作为基准，而且可以作为一种设计模式，用于审核自动编辑更正是否实际上改变了预期的表示选择，同时保留了内容。For editing with LLMs, the same decomposition also provides a verifiable workflow: detection identifies the presentation mechanism, reconstruction performs the edit, and IRR checks the result against the known intervention rather than against surface fluency alone. This makes controlled inversion useful not only as a benchmark, but also as a design pattern for auditing whether an automated editorial correction actually changed the intended representational choice while preserving content.

语言基础还产生了直接的稳健性目标。词汇评估相对可见，而代理尤其是显着性则需要跟踪谁处于前台以及其他有效事实的放置位置。由于配对文章共享相同的事实库存，因此监督可以针对记录的呈现变化本身：词汇框架的评估标记、代理的参与者和归因实现以及显着性的信息顺序。因此，受控倒置将广泛的去偏差目标转变为可验证的、基于语言的更正，同时保持内容固定。The linguistic grounding also yields a direct robustness target. Lexical evaluation is comparatively visible, whereas agency and especially salience require tracking who is foregrounded and where otherwise valid facts are placed. Because paired articles share the same factual inventory, supervision can target the logged presentation change itself: evaluative markers for lexical framing, actor and attribution realization for agency, and information order for salience. Controlled inversion thus turns a broad debiasing goal into verifiable, linguistically grounded corrections while keeping content fixed.

6 结论6 Conclusion

我们引入了受控倒置，以保护事实的新闻框架、评价性词汇、代理实现和话语显着性的基础干预。在三个法学硕士系列中，该基准将事实保真度、框架识别和框架反转分开，使框架恢复可直接测量。准确的认识始终会引发逆转，但并不能决定逆转；显着性是最不可逆的算子，在事实保真度保持稳定的情况下，更强的干预更容易恢复。通过根据已知的前向干预对重建进行评分，受控反转使恢复成为构建目标，而不是印象中的中性。因此，它为法学硕士提供了一个基于记录编辑的目标，即在更改其演示文稿的同时保留新闻事实。We introduced controlled inversion for fact-preserving news framing, grounding interventions in evaluative lexis, agency realization, and discourse salience. Across three LLM families, the benchmark separates factual fidelity, framing recognition, and framing inversion, making framing recovery directly measurable. Exact recognition consistently raises reversal but does not determine it; salience is the least reversible operator, and stronger interventions are easier to recover while factual fidelity remains stable. By scoring reconstruction against the known forward intervention, controlled inversion makes recovery the target by construction rather than neutrality by impression. It therefore provides an objective based on recorded edits for LLMs that preserve news facts while changing their presentation.
