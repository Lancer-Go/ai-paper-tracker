摘要Abstract 由于部分可观测性、感知不确定性以及需要权衡探索和导航效率，自主目标搜索对于在室内环境中运行的移动机器人来说具有挑战性。经典的概率方法明确地表示不确定性，但通常依赖于手工设计的行动选择启发式，而深度强化学习可以实现自适应策略，但通常会受到收敛缓慢和可解释性有限的影响。本文提出了一种混合对象搜索框架，将贝叶斯推理与深度强化学习相结合。该方法维护目标位置的空间置信图，通过校准对象检测的贝叶斯推理在线更新，并训练强化学习策略以直接从该概率表示中选择导航动作。使用 Habitat 3.0 在真实的室内模拟中对该方法进行评估，并与开发的基线策略进行比较。在两个室内环境中，所提出的方法提高了成功率，同时减少了搜索工作量。总体而言，结果支持将贝叶斯信念估计与学习动作选择相结合的价值，以在部分可观察性下实现更高效、更可靠的对象搜索行为。—Autonomous object search is challenging for mobile robots operating in indoor environments due to partial observability, perceptual uncertainty, and the need to trade off exploration and navigation efficiency. Classical probabilistic approaches explicitly represent uncertainty but typically rely on handcrafted action-selection heuristics, while deep reinforcement learning enables adaptive policies but often suffers from slow convergence and limited interpretability. This paper proposes a hybrid object-search framework that integrates Bayesian inference with deep reinforcement learning. The method maintains a spatial belief map over target locations, updated online through Bayesian inference from calibrated object detections, and trains a reinforcement learning policy to select navigation actions directly from this probabilistic representation. The approach is evaluated in realistic indoor simulation using Habitat 3.0 and compared against developed baseline strategies. Across two indoor environments, the proposed method improves success rate while reducing search effort. Overall, the results support the value of combining Bayesian belief estimation with learned action selection to achieve more efficient and reliable objectsearch behavior under partial observability.

一、简介I. INTRODUCTION

对象搜索是在复杂、非结构化环境中运行的自主移动机器人的一项基本功能[1]。由于部分可观测性、高维状态空间以及有限的传感器覆盖范围、遮挡和噪声检测引起的感知不确定性，该问题具有挑战性[2]。图 1 说明了部分可观测性下的代表性对象搜索事件，突出显示了从探索到置信目标检测的进展。Object search is a fundamental capability for autonomous mobile robots operating in complex, unstructured environments [1]. The problem is challenging due to partial observability, high-dimensional state spaces, and perceptual uncertainty arising from limited sensor coverage, occlusions, and noisy detections [2]. Fig. 1 illustrates a representative objectsearch episode under partial observability, highlighting the progression from exploration to confident target detection.

现有的机器人目标搜索研究主要遵循两个互补的方向。经典概率方法通过维持潜在目标位置的明确信念分布并在新的感知证据可用时更新它来解决不确定性[3]。这使得在部分可观测性下可以进行可解释的不确定性建模和有原则的决策。然而，动作选择通常是由手工启发式或预定义的实用函数驱动的，这可能会限制跨不同环境和传感条件的可扩展性和适应性。Existing research on robotic object search has largely followed two complementary directions. Classical probabilistic approaches address uncertainty by maintaining an explicit belief distribution over potential target locations and updating it as new perceptual evidence becomes available [3]. This enables interpretable uncertainty modeling and principled decision-making under partial observability. However, action selection is typically driven by handcrafted heuristics or predefined utility functions, which can limit scalability and adaptability across diverse environments and sensing conditions.

相比之下，深度强化学习（DRL）方法直接从经验中学习策略，使智能体无需手动特征即可获得有效的探索行为In contrast, deep reinforcement learning (DRL) methods learn policies directly from experience, enabling agents to acquire effective exploration behaviors without manual feature

工程[4]。尽管有强有力的实证结果，但标准的端到端 DRL 方法经常会遇到收敛速度慢和探索效率低下的问题，而且它们通常缺乏明确的不确定性推理和可解释性 [5]。engineering [4]. Despite strong empirical results, standard endto-end DRL approaches often suffer from slow convergence and inefficient exploration, and they commonly lack explicit uncertainty reasoning and interpretability [5].

为了解决这些限制，本文提出了一种混合框架，将贝叶斯推理与 DRL 相结合，以在部分可观测性下实现高效的对象搜索。该方法维护一个表示目标位置概率分布的空间置信图，并使用校准的对象检测通过贝叶斯推理进行在线更新。 DRL 策略经过训练，可以根据当前的信念状态选择平衡探索和利用的操作。通过将显式不确定性建模与数据驱动的政策学习相结合，该框架提供了一个统一的决策架构，将可解释性与适应性结合起来。To address these limitations, this paper proposes a hybrid framework that integrates Bayesian inference with DRL for efficient object search under partial observability. The method maintains a spatial belief map representing the probabilistic distribution of target locations, updated online through Bayesian inference using calibrated object detections. A DRL policy is trained to select actions that balance exploration and exploitation based on the current belief state. By combining explicit uncertainty modeling with data-driven policy learning, the framework provides a unified decision-making architecture that couples interpretability with adaptability.

使用配备 RGB-D 传感器和几何映射、导航和定位技能的移动机器人，在多个室内环境的 Habitat 3.0 模拟器 [6] 中对所提出的框架进行评估。The proposed framework is evaluated in the Habitat 3.0 simulator [6] across multiple indoor environments using a mobile robot equipped with an RGB-D sensor and geometric mapping, navigation and localization skills.

二.相关工作II. RELATED WORK

机器人物体搜索与主动感知密切相关，智能体必须决定如何移动以获得信息丰富的观察结果，同时最小化搜索成本[1]。该领域的早期工作依赖于几何推理、符号表示和手工制作的感知管道 [7]。随着用于检测和语义标记的深度神经网络的兴起，对象搜索日益转向数据驱动的感知和基于学习的决策。因此，现有的方法可以大致分为两种方法论范式：Robotic object search is closely related to active perception, where an agent must decide how to move in order to acquire informative observations while minimizing search cost [1]. Earlier work in this area relied on geometric reasoning, symbolic representations, and handcrafted perception pipelines [7]. With the rise of deep neural networks for detection and semantic labeling, object search has increasingly shifted toward data-driven perception and learning-based decision making. As a result, existing approaches can be broadly grouped into two methodological paradigms:基于概率信念的方法probabilistic belief-based methods和and基于学习的方法learning-based methods。这些范式在很大程度上是并行发展的，并解决了问题的互补方面：概率方法强调明确的不确定性建模和结构化决策，而强化学习方法则侧重于通过交互学习适应性策略。. These paradigms have evolved largely in parallel and address complementary aspects of the problem: probabilistic approaches emphasize explicit uncertainty modeling and structured decision making, while reinforcement learning approaches focus on learning adaptive policies through interaction.

概率方法Probabilistic approaches将目标位置表示为置信分布，该分布通常通过贝叶斯过滤 [3] 在获取新观测值时迭代更新。这些方法提供可解释的不确定性表示，并通过选择最大化预期效用或信息增益的行动来支持部分可观察性下的决策。一些工作结合了语义或结构先验来提高室内环境的可扩展性，例如通过空间关系、层次图或度量拓扑抽象的推理[8]-[11]。尽管有原则性的不确定性处理，但动作选择通常是由主观启发法或手动设计的效用函数驱动的，这可能需要针对特定​​环境的调整，并且可能会限制不同设置中的适应性。represent the target location as a belief distribution that is iteratively updated as new observations are acquired, typically through Bayesian filtering [3]. These methods provide interpretable uncertainty representations and support decision-making under partial observability by selecting actions that maximize expected utility or information gain. Several works incorporate semantic or structural priors to improve scalability in indoor environments, for example by reasoning over spatial relations, hierarchical maps, or metric–topological abstractions [8]–[11]. Despite their principled uncertainty handling, action selection is often driven by subjective heuristics or manually designed utility functions, which may require environment-specific tuning and can limit adaptability in diverse settings.

基于学习的方法Learning-based approaches主要基于 DRL，直接从与环境的交互中学习搜索和导航策略，并以客观奖励信号（例如，任务成功时）为指导，使代理能够获得有效的探索行为，而无需手动特征工程 [4]，[12]。许多方法通过塑造奖励、利用语义映射或将学习到的策略与经典规划组件相结合来指导探索来提高性能[5]。其他策略包含辅助学习目标，以提高样本效率和稳健性[13]。虽然这些方法可以取得强有力的实证结果，但它们通常缺乏感知不确定性的明确表示，降低了可解释性，并可能导致在噪声检测下的低效探索。此外，训练需要大量的交互数据和计算资源。, primarily based on DRL, learn search and navigation policies directly from interaction with the environment and guided by an objective reward signal (e.g., upon task success), enabling agents to acquire effective exploration behaviors without manual feature engineering [4], [12]. Many methods improve performance by shaping rewards, leveraging semantic mapping, or combining learned policies with classical planning components to guide exploration [5]. Additional strategies incorporate auxiliary learning objectives to enhance sample efficiency and robustness [13]. While these approaches can achieve strong empirical results, they typically lack explicit representation of perceptual uncertainty, reducing interpretability and potentially leading to inefficient exploration under noisy detections. Moreover, training requires substantial interaction data and computational resources.

总之，概率模型提供了明确的不确定性表示，但通常依赖于手工制定的决策规则，而基于 DRL 的方法学习自适应行为，但在很大程度上省略了原则信念建模。这项工作通过将贝叶斯信念更新与基于 DRL 的动作选择相结合以实现不确定性感知对象搜索来解决这一差距。此外，我们还制定基线策略，包括纯粹的概率信念驱动方法和手工设计的效用最大化策略，从而实现客观评估In summary, probabilistic models offer explicit uncertainty representation but commonly rely on handcrafted decision rules, whereas DRL-based methods learn adaptive behaviors but largely omit principled belief modeling. This work addresses this gap by integrating Bayesian belief updates with DRL-based action selection for uncertainty-aware object search. 三．提议的方法III. PROPOSED APPROACH

The key idea is to combine explicit probabilistic belief estimation with learning-based decision making: the robot maintains a spatial belief map over the target location, updated online from perceptual evidence, and uses a DRL policy to select navigation actions conditioned on this belief representation. The robot is provided with a geometric map of the environment先验的a priori以二维占用网格地图的形式，其中对象只能占用占用的单元格，即不可导航的空间。必须推断这些单元格的语义内容。in the form of a 2D occupancy grid map, where objects can only occupy occupied cells, i.e., non-navigable space. 在每个时间步，机器人都会获取 RGB-D 观察结果，并应用对象检测管道来获取目标类别的候选检测。这些检测通过将其投影到地图上而转化为空间证据，产生与机器人姿态和场景几何形状一致的观察信号。然后通过贝叶斯推理更新信念图，将新的证据与先前的信念相结合，以维持对目标可能所在位置的概率估计。At each time step, the robot acquires an RGB-D observation and applies an object detection pipeline to obtain candidate detections for the target category. These detections are transformed into spatial evidence by projecting them into the map, producing an observation signal that is consistent with the robot pose and the scene geometry. The belief map is then updated through Bayesian inference, integrating the new evidence with the prior belief to maintain a probabilistic estimate of where the target is likely to be located.

为了支持大型环境中的有效探索，已知的二元占用网格的自由空间也被划分为一组空间簇。该聚类子系统提供候选视点的紧凑表示并构建导航空间，允许代理在有意义的区域而不是单个网格单元上进行推理和操作。因此，信念表示和动作选择是相对于该聚类图结构来定义的。To support efficient exploration in large environments, the free space of the known binary occupancy grid is also partitioned into a set of spatial clusters. This clustering subsystem provides a compact representation of candidate viewpoints and structures the navigation space, allowing the agent to reason and act over meaningful regions rather than individual grid cells. The belief representation and action selection are therefore defined with respect to this clustered map structure.

由此产生的信念状态作为搜索问题的紧凑且不确定性感知的表示。 DRL 策略不是依靠手工设计的效用函数来决定下一步移动，而是训练 DRL 策略将当前的信念表示映射到离散运动空间中的动作。这使得代理能够学习平衡探索和利用的搜索行为，同时利用贝叶斯不确定性模型提供的结构。The resulting belief state serves as a compact and uncertainty-aware representation of the search problem. Rather than relying on a handcrafted utility function to decide where to move next, a DRL policy is trained to map the current belief representation to an action in a discrete motion space. This allows the agent to learn search behaviors that balance exploration and exploitation while leveraging the structure provided by Bayesian uncertainty modeling.

以下小节描述了该框架的主要构建块：（i）感知和观察模型，（ii）信念表示和贝叶斯更新规则，（iii）聚类和导航抽象，以及（iv）从信念状态选择动作的基于 DRL 的策略。The following subsections describe the main building blocks of the framework: (i) the perception and observation model, (ii) the belief representation and Bayesian update rule, (iii) the clustering and navigation abstraction, and (iv) the DRL-based policy that selects actions from the belief state.

e) 失效模式。e) Failure modes.其余的失败通常是感知受限的：如果目标长时间保持被遮挡或检测很弱/模糊，则信念可能会保持分散并延迟对正确区域的承诺。在这种情况下，渐进式细化可确保最终覆盖，但范围限制可能会阻碍成功。Remaining failures are typically perception-limited: if the target remains occluded for long periods or detections are weak/ambiguous, the belief can remain diffuse and delay commitment to the correct region. In such cases, progressive refinement ensures eventual coverage, but the horizon constraint can prevent success.

这项工作解决了部分可观察性和感知不确定性下室内环境中的自主对象搜索。我们提出了一个混合框架，将贝叶斯推理与 DRL 相结合，以统一不确定性感知信念估计和自适应决策。该方法维护对象类的空间狄利克雷置信图，根据校准的检测和背景证据在线更新，并学习在基于分层聚类的抽象上选择导航目标的 DQN 策略。This work addressed autonomous object search in indoor environments under partial observability and perceptual uncertainty. We proposed a hybrid framework that combines Bayesian inference with DRL to unify uncertainty-aware belief estimation and adaptive decision making. The method maintains a spatial Dirichlet belief map over object classes, updated online from calibrated detections and background evidence, and learns a DQN policy that selects navigation goals over a hierarchical clustering-based abstraction.

在两个真实的 Habitat 3.0 室内环境中的实验结果表明，信念驱动的策略比无知探索的成功率大大提高。此外，所提出的 BBDPS 方法总体上实现了最高的成功率，并在更大的环境中产生了最强的效率增益，与纯粹的概率效用最大化基线相比，减少了搜索工作量，同时保持了可靠性。这些结果支持了这项工作的中心结论：明确地建模不确定性并学习如何对其采取行动可以提高长期搜索性能，超越手工启发式或单独的置信估计。Experimental results in two realistic Habitat 3.0 indoor environments show that belief-driven strategies substantially improve success rate over uninformed exploration. Moreover, the proposed BBDPS method achieves the highest success rates overall and yields the strongest efficiency gains in the larger environment, reducing search effort compared to a purely probabilistic utility-maximization baseline while preserving reliability. These results support the central conclusion of this work: explicitly modeling uncertainty and learning how to act on it can improve long-horizon search performance beyond either handcrafted heuristics or belief estimation alone.

尽管取得了这些进展，但仍然存在一些局限性。该方法假设已知的占用网格，并且不解决联合映射和语义发现问题，而性能受到对象检测器质量的限制，因为误报或漏检会减慢信念收敛并降低探索效率。此外，基于网格的状态表示需要为每个环境训练单独的策略，限制跨场景泛化而无需进一步适应。未来的工作将侧重于通过更具表现力的状态表示来改善跨环境的传输，结合语义先验来指导信念细化，并将框架扩展到未知或动态地图。我们还计划将主动视点选择与更丰富的感知模型相结合，并评估模拟到真实的部署。Despite these gains, several limitations remain. The approach assumes a known occupancy grid and does not address joint mapping and semantic discovery, while performance is constrained by object detector quality, as false positives or missed detections can slow belief convergence and reduce exploration efficiency. In addition, the grid-based state representation requires training a separate policy per environment, limiting cross-scene generalization without further adaptation. Future work will focus on improving transfer across environments through more expressive state representations, incorporating semantic priors to guide belief refinement, and extending the framework to unknown or dynamic maps. We also plan to integrate active viewpoint selection with richer perception models and evaluate sim-to-real deployment.
