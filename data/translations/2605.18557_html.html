自监督局部学习规则学习高维数据隐藏的层次结构Self-supervised local learning rules learn the hidden hierarchical structure of high-dimensional data

Ariane Delrocq ariane.delrocq@epfl.ch    Wu S. Zihan zihan.wu@epfl.ch    Guillaume Bellec    Wulfram Gerstner [ [
摘要Abstract

大脑学习高维感觉输入的抽象表示，但实现这种学习的可塑性规则尚不清楚。我们在随机层次模型（RHM）上研究生物学上合理的算法，这是一个人工数据集，旨在研究深度神经网络如何学习高维数据的内在层次结构。我们专注于两种类型的局部学习规则，它们避免了较长的收敛时间和对称误差网络的使用。第一种类型使用直接反馈信号来近似来自输出层的误差传播。第二种类型使用分层自监督对比或非对比损失函数，这些函数不会明确近似输出层的误差。我们表明，第一类规则的所有规则都无法解决 RHM 的任务，并将这种失败追溯到输入特定的非线性（“掩蔽”），这些非线性在完全反向传播中实现，对于学习复杂任务至关重要。 然而，第二种类型的算法能够学习 RHM 任务的分层隐藏结构，并且与监督反向传播训练一样具有数据效率，同时与皮层突触可塑性的已知规则兼容。The brain learns abstract representations of high-dimensional sensory input, but the plasticity rules that enable such learning are unknown. We study biologically plausible algorithms on the Random Hierarchy Model (RHM), an artificial dataset designed to investigate how deep neural networks learn the intrinsic hierarchical structure of high-dimensional data. We focus on two types of local learning rules that avoid both a long convergence time and the use of a symmetric error network. The first type uses direct feedback signals to approximate error propagation from the output layer. The second type uses layerwise self-supervised contrastive or non-contrastive loss functions that do not explicitly approximate errors at the output layer. We show that all rules of the first type fail to solve the tasks of the RHM and trace this failure back to input-specific nonlinearities (‘masking’) that are implemented in full backpropagation and are essential for learning complex tasks. However, algorithms of the second type are able to learn the hierarchical hidden structure of the RHM tasks and are as data-efficient as supervised backpropagation training, while being compatible with known rules of synaptic plasticity in cortex.

2.2 生物学上合理的学习规则概述Overview of biologically plausible learning rules

4 讨论Discussion

我们的结果揭示了通过远程反馈近似反向传播的方法（例如 DFA）的基本局限性：缺乏特定于输入的掩蔽会阻止它们学习 RHM 任务的分层表示，即使反馈权重已充分初始化。这种限制在需要多层非线性变换的更深网络中变得至关重要。Our results reveal a fundamental limitation of methods that approximate backpropagation through long-range feedback, such as DFA: the lack of input-specific masking prevents them from learning hierarchical representations of RHM tasks, even when feedback weights are well-initialized. This limitation becomes critical in deeper networks where multiple layers of non-linear transformations are needed.

相比之下，我们已经证明 SimCLR、LPL 和 CLAPP，无论是分层版本还是端到端 BP 训练，都能够提取 RHM 的内在结构，而 ICA 则不能。这个结果很可能适用于更广泛的自监督算法，只要它们（i）利用编码字符串对之间输入特征的相关性，并且 (ii) 应用于足够深的神经网络来提取层次表示。In contrast, we have shown that SimCLR, LPL and CLAPP, both in a layer-wise version and with end-to-end BP training, are capable of extracting the intrinsic structure of the RHM, while ICA is not. It is likely that this result applies to a broader class of self-supervised algorithms, as long as they (i) leverage the correlations of input features across pairs of encoding strings and (ii) are applied to neural networks that are sufficiently deep to extract the hierarchical representations.

尽管深度对于构建 RHM 数据的有用表示是必要的，但局部学习规则足以利用可用深度并构建分层表示。 值得注意的是，CLAPP 和 LPL 的设计都是为了与大脑学习机制的生理限制兼容，并且仍然足够强大以实现数据高效（从某种意义上说，它们从相对较小的数据部分中学习分层数据）。 重要的是， 我们研究的所有自监督算法中显式或隐式使用的“相同性”信号似乎对于 分层的、数据高效的学习，因为 ICA（一种没有预测成分的赫布规则）需要大量数据，或者完全失败。 SFA等算法Although depth is necessary to build useful representations of the RHM data, local learning rules are sufficient to leverage the available depth and build hierarchical representations. Notably, both CLAPP and LPL were designed to be compatible with physiological constraints on learning mechanisms in the brain and are nonetheless powerful enough to be data-efficient (in the sense that they learn hierarchical data from a relatively small fraction of the data). Importantly, the "sameness" signal that is explicitly or implicitly used in all the self-supervised algorithms that we investigated seems to be crucial for hierarchical, data-efficient learning, since ICA, a Hebbian rule without a predictive component, is data-hungry – or fails altogether. Algorithms such as SFA(Sprekeler07)在概念上介于 ICA 和 LPL 之间，但尚未对此任务进行测试。 从生物学角度来看，在视觉等感觉处理路径中，我们的研究结果意味着，一个简单的自我监督学习信号，具有局部权重更新规则，足以让每个大脑区域建立有用的输入分层表示，而不需要来自更高区域的详细反馈。are conceptually midway between ICA and LPL, but have not been tested on this task.
