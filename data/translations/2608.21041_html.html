1. 简介Introduction

地理空间表示学习旨在将多源数据投射到统一的潜在空间中，有效捕获内在的空间相关性和时间动态，以促进强大的城市理解Geospatial representation learning aims to project multi-source data into a unified latent space, effectively capturing intrinsic spatial correlations and temporal dynamics to facilitate robust urban understanding(35)。如图所示. As illustrated in Figure1(a)，有意义的地理空间表示不仅应该捕捉当地的视觉模式，还应该捕捉塑造城市结构及其随时间演变的更高层次的地理规律(a), a meaningful geospatial representation should capture not only local visual patterns but also the higher-level geographic regularities that shape urban structure and its evolution over time(14)。 学习到的地理空间表示可以支持广泛的下游应用，例如城市感知和社会经济评估. The learned geospatial representations can support a wide range of downstream applications, such as urban perception and socio-economic assessment(31)。此外，这些表示的质量从根本上决定了它们在不同地理区域和任务之间泛化和迁移的能力。因此，学习可迁移且具有语义意义的地理空间表示已成为数据驱动的城市系统理解的核心问题. Moreover, the quality of these representations fundamentally determines their ability to generalize and transfer across diverse geographic regions and tasks. Therefore, learning transferable and semantically meaningful geospatial representations has become a core problem in data-driven urban system understanding(44).

Existing research on satellite-based representation learning generally follows two paradigms: (1)多模态语义对齐Multimodal Semantic Alignment，通过将卫星图像与 POI 等辅助数据对齐来整合社会经济语义, which incorporates socio-economic semantics by aligning satellite imagery with auxiliary data, such as POIs(28; 16)或移动数据or mobility data(43; 41)。虽然这些方法对于特定区域的分析和现实世界的社会经济预测有效，但在很大程度上依赖于外部数据源，其可用性、质量和分布在不同城市和国家之间存在很大差异，这限制了可扩展性并削弱了可转移性。 (2). While effective for region-specific profiling and real-world socio-economic prediction, these methods depend heavily on external data sources whose availability, quality, and distribution vary substantially across cities and countries, which limits scalability and weakens transferability. (2)自监督视觉预训练Self-supervised Visual Pre-training，其重点是通过直接从原始图像中挖掘不变的纹理模式来提取视觉特征。尽管此类方法为低级任务提供了强大的视觉编码器，但它们主要强调外观一致性和纹理辨别，而在高级地理语义方面的明确基础有限。因此，学习到的特征通常作为视觉描述符是有效的，但对于语义要求较高的城市分析来说信息量较少。, which focuses on extracting visual features by mining invariant textural patterns directly from raw imagery. Although such methods produce strong visual encoders for low-level tasks, they primarily emphasize appearance consistency and texture discrimination, with limited explicit grounding in high-level geo-semantics. As a result, the learned features are often effective as visual descriptors but less informative for semantically demanding urban analysis.

这些限制并不是独立的，指出了当前地理空间表示学习中更根本的差距：表示应该泛化具有不同视觉风格、开发模式和功能构成的区域，同时还保持足够的语义可解释性，以反映有意义的城市转型。这导致学习通用地理空间表示面临两个主要挑战：（i）跨区域分布变化下的泛化，以及（ii）弱监督下的语义可解释性。These limitations are not independent, pointing to a more fundamental gap in current geospatial representation learning: a representation should generalize across regions with different visual styles, development patterns, and functional compositions, while also remaining semantically interpretable enough to reflect meaningful urban transitions. This leads to two central challenges in learning universal geospatial representations: (i) generalization under cross-region distribution shifts, and (ii) semantic interpretability under weak supervision.
