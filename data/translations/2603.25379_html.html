摘要Abstract

Our prior work introduced PPS (Prompt Protocol Specification), a 5W3H-based structured intent framework, and demonstrated its effectiveness in Chinese. This paper extends that work along three dimensions: (1) two additional languages (English and Japanese), (2) a fourth experimental condition in which a user's simple prompt is automatically expanded into a full 5W3H specification via an AIassisted authoring interface (Condition D), and (3) a new research question on cross-model output consistency as a measure of protocol-level reliability.

在 2,160 个模型输出（3 种语言 × 4 种条件 × 3 个法学硕士 × 60 个任务）中，我们发现：(a) AI 扩展的 5W3H 提示（条件 D）与所有三种语言中手动制作的 5W3H 提示（条件 C）在目标一致性方面没有显着差异，同时只需要用户输入单句； (b) 结构化 PPS 条件通常会减少或重塑跨模型输出方差，尽管这种效果在所有语言和指标中并不统一——最有力的证据来自于无约束基线中虚假低方差的减少； (c) 非结构化提示（条件 A）表现出系统性的双重通货膨胀偏差——人为地提高综合分数和人为地降低跨模型方差——这挑战了它们作为评估基线的有效性。这些发现表明，结构化 5W3H 表示可以改善跨语言和模型的意图一致性和可访问性，特别是当人工智能辅助创作降低非专家用户的障碍时。Across 2,160 model outputs (3 languages × 4 conditions × 3 LLMs × 60 tasks), we find that: (a) AI-expanded 5W3H prompts (Condition D) show no statistically significant difference in goal alignment from manually crafted 5W3H prompts (Condition C) across all three languages, while requiring only a single-sentence input from the user; (b) structured PPS conditions often reduce or reshape cross-model output variance, though this effect is not uniform across all languages and metrics — the strongest evidence comes from the reduction of spurious low variance in unconstrained baselines; and (c) unstructured prompts (Condition A) exhibit a systematic dualinflation bias — artificially high composite scores and artificially low cross-model variance — that challenges their validity as an evaluation baseline. These findings suggest that structured 5W3H representations can improve intent alignment and accessibility across languages and models, particularly when AI-assisted authoring lowers the barrier for non-expert users.

一、简介1. Introduction

1.1 意图传输问题1.1 The Intent Transmission Problem

大型语言模型（LLM）的快速部署在人类与人工智能的交流中造成了根本性的不对称：虽然模型具有广泛的生成能力，但用户通常通过简短的、非结构化的自然语言提示来表达他们的意图，这些提示不完全指定所需的输出。这种差距——我们称之为The rapid deployment of large language models (LLMs) has created a fundamental asymmetry in human-AI communication: while models possess broad generative capability, users typically express their intent through brief, unstructured natural language prompts that incompletely specify the desired output. This gap — which we term意图传输损失intent transmission loss- 表现为不可预测的输出质量、会话之间的不一致以及对用户即时工程技能的高度依赖。— manifests as unpredictable output quality, inconsistency across sessions, and high dependence on users' prompt engineering skill.

实证研究证实了这个问题的严重性。提示措辞的微小变化可能会从同一模型产生截然不同的输出 [5, 25]，并且相同的任务描述在不同的法学硕士中会产生截然不同的结果 [12, 13]。对于实际应用——专业写作、研究协助、业务分析——这种不可预测性是采用的关键障碍。Empirical studies confirm the severity of this problem. Small changes in prompt wording can produce dramatically different outputs from the same model [5, 25], and the same task description yields substantially different results across different LLMs [12, 13]. For practical applications — professional writing, research assistance, business analysis — this unpredictability is a critical barrier to adoption.

1.2 PPS：结构化意图表示1.2 PPS: Structured Intent Representation

在我们之前的工作 (arXiv:2603.18976) 中，我们提出了 PPS（提示协议规范），这是一个围绕新闻 5W3H 框架中的八个维度组织的结构化意图表示框架：In our prior work (arXiv:2603.18976), we proposed PPS (Prompt Protocol Specification), a structured intent representation framework organized around eight dimensions drawn from the journalistic 5W3H framework:什么What（任务和 KPI），(task and KPIs),为什么Why（目标和限制），(goals and constraints),谁Who（角色和观众），(persona and audience),当When（时间），(timing),哪里Where（背景和平台），(context and platform),操作方法How-to-do（方法和步骤），(method and steps),多少钱How-much（定量要求），以及(quantitative requirements), and感觉如何How-feel（语气和风格）。(tone and style).

与优化单个提示的提示工程技术不同，PPS 将意图表示构建为人机交互的结构化通信层：目标是定义一个标准表示，无论接收它的特定模型如何，都可以实现更可靠的意图传输。这个框架激发了本文使用的评估标准：不仅是输出质量，还包括跨模型一致性和跨语言泛化性。Unlike prompt engineering techniques that optimize individual prompts, PPS frames intent representation as a structured communication layer for human-AI interaction: the goal is to define a standard representation that enables more reliable intent transmission regardless of the specific model receiving it. This framing motivates the evaluation criteria used in this paper: not only output quality, but also cross-model consistency and cross-language generalizability.

我们之前的工作 [27] 在三个实验条件（A：非结构化，B：原始 JSON，C：PPS 自然语言渲染）和三个法学硕士中确定了 PPS 在中文中的有效性。仍然存在两个局限性：该研究是单语的，条件 C 要求用户手动指定所有八个维度——这对非专家用户来说是一个重大障碍。Our prior work [27] established the effectiveness of PPS in Chinese across three experimental conditions (A: unstructured, B: raw JSON, C: PPS natural language rendering) and three LLMs. Two limitations remained: the study was monolingual, and condition C required users to manually specify all eight dimensions — a significant barrier for non-expert users.

七、结论7. Conclusion

本文将 PPS（提示协议规范）的实证基础从单一语言研究扩展到涵盖 2,160 个模型输出的三语言、四条件实验。我们的主要发现是：This paper extends the empirical foundation of PPS (Prompt Protocol Specification) from a single-language study to a three-language, four-condition experiment covering 2,160 model outputs. Our principal findings are:

  1. 总之，这些发现表明，结构化 5W3H 意图表示可以作为有用的人机人工智能界面层，尤其是与人工智能辅助创作相结合，使非专家用户可以访问结构化提示。最实际的重要结果是 C 和 D 之间不存在显着差异：单句输入在通过 AI 创作界面扩展时，可以实现与跨三种语言的专家级手动结构在统计上无法区分的意图对齐。结构化意图表示可能尚未构成通用协议，但它们提供了一个具体的、基于经验的步骤，以缩小用户意图与人工智能模型产生的结果之间的差距。Together, these findings suggest that structured 5W3H intent representations can serve as a useful human-AI interface layer — especially when combined with AI-assisted authoring that makes structured prompting accessible to non-expert users. The most practically significant result is the absence of a significant difference between C and D: a single-sentence input, when expanded by an AI authoring interface, achieves intent alignment statistically indistinguishable from expert-level manual structuring across three languages. Structured intent representations may not yet constitute a universal protocol, but they offer a concrete, empirically grounded step toward reducing the gap between what users intend and what AI models produce.
