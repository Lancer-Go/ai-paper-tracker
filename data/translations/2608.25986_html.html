摘要。Abstract.

检索增强生成（RAG）广泛用于减轻大语言模型（LLM）和多模态大语言模型（MLLM）中的幻觉问题。特别是，基于知识图（KG）的RAG利用结构化知识为（M）LLM提供高质量的外部信息。在这些工作的基础上，最近的研究探索了多模态知识图（MMKG）作为 GraphRAG 的知识库。这使得 Graph RAG 能够跨多种模式整合知识，从而进一步提高其性能。然而，现有的基于 MMKG 的 RAG 方法通常遵循共同的流程，其中不同的模态在融合之前很大程度上是独立处理的。因此，在视觉信息提取和随后的多模态知识融合过程中，文本上下文仅被有限程度地使用。这带来了图像和文本之间的语义差距，限制了多模态 GraphRAG 的性能。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一种新的框架来构建上下文增强型 MMKG (CEMMKG)，以更好地支持多模态 GraphRAG。所提出的 CEMMKG 在局部和全局范围内用互补的文本上下文丰富了每个图像。局部上下文通过合并与图像语义相关的句子超越了周围的文本，而全局上下文提供了整个段落的摘要。我们进一步引入了针对本地上下文的多粒度设计，使其能够捕获不同细节级别的语义相关信息。对选定的以视觉为中心的数据集进行的大量实验验证了 CEMMKG 在利用上下文信息来提高基于 MMKG 的 RAG 性能方面是有效的。此外，它在不同基于 MMKG 的 RAG 方法中的有效性证明了其广泛的适用性。Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is widely used to mitigate hallucination issues in large language models (LLMs) and multimodal large language models (MLLMs). In particular, knowledge graph (KG)-based RAG leverages structured knowledge to provide (M)LLMs with high-quality external information. Building on these works, recent studies have explored multimodal knowledge graphs (MMKGs) as knowledge bases for GraphRAG. This enables Graph RAG to integrate knowledge across multiple modalities, thereby further enhancing its performance. However, existing MMKG-based RAG methods generally follow a common pipeline in which different modalities are largely processed independently before being fusion. As a result, textual context is only used to a limited extent during visual information extraction and subsequent multimodal knowledge fusion. This brings a semantic gap between images and text which limits the multimodal GraphRAG performance. To address this issue, we propose a novel framework for constructing a Context-Enhanced MMKG (CEMMKG) to better support multimodal GraphRAG. The proposed CEMMKG enriches each image with complementary textual context at both local and global scopes. Local context goes beyond the surrounding text by incorporating sentences that are semantically related to the image, while global context provides a summary of the entire passage. We further introduce a multi-granularity design for the local context, allowing it to capture semantically relevant information at different levels of detail. Extensive experiments on the selected vision-centric dataset validate that CEMMKG is effective in leveraging contextual information to improve MMKG-based RAG performance. Moreover, its effectiveness across different MMKG-based RAG methods demonstrates its broad applicability.

2.1. 多模态大语言模型Multimodal Large Language Models

大型语言模型 (LLM)Large Language Models (LLMs)(OpenAI, 2023; Zhao et al., 2026)受益于大规模预训练等技术，展示了令人印象深刻的能力have demonstrated impressive capabilities, benefiting from techniques such as large-scale pretraining(Brown et al., 2020)和强化学习and reinforcement learning(Schulman et al., 2017; Shao et al., 2024)。多模态大语言模型 (MLLM). Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)(Yin et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024a)通过调整视觉编码器将法学硕士的能力扩展到视觉领域extend the capabilities of LLMs to the visual domain by aligning a vision encoder(Radford et al., 2021)拥有LLM骨干with an LLM backbone(Touvron et al., 2023)，或者通过轻量级投影层, either through lightweight projection layers(Liu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024a; Bai et al., 2023; Bai et al., 2025)或者通过交叉注意力和学习查询模块or through cross-attention and learned query modules(Li et al., 2023a; Chen et al., 2024b)。由于它们可以对交错的图像和文本进行联合推理，因此 MLLM 已成为将视觉信息转换为文本内容的常用工具，并广泛用于用视觉实体和关系填充多模态知识图。然而，MLLM继承了LLM的幻觉问题. Because they can reason jointly over interleaved images and texts, MLLMs have become a common tool for translating visual information into textual content, and are widely used to populates multimodal knowledge graphs with visual entities and relations. However, MLLMs inherit the hallucination problem of LLMs(Huang et al., 2025)并且可以描述输入图像中不存在或不一致的对象、属性和关系and may describe objects, attributes, and relations that are absent from or inconsistent with the input image(Bai et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023b)。简单地提供更多文本信息可能无法有效解决这些错误，因为这些模型可能在长输入中没有充分利用证据. Simply providing more textual information may not effectively address these errors, since these models may under-utilize evidence in the middle of long inputs(Liu et al., 2024b)并且可能会被不相关的段落分散注意力and could be distracted by irrelevant passages(Cuconasu et al., 2024).

2.2. 多模式RAGMultimodal RAG

多模态 RAG 将检索增强生成扩展到文本之外，以便图像和图表等其他模态的信息也可以作为生成的基础Multimodal RAG extends retrieval-augmented generation beyond text, so that the information in other modalities such as images and charts can also ground generation(Abootorabi et al., 2025)。早期工作使用联合训练的编码器检索图像文本对，并根据检索到的信息条件生成器. For documents containing rich visual information, two main strategies have emerged. One directly operates on visual content by encoding rendered document pages with vision–language retrievers, thereby preserving layout and visual information during retrieval(Yu et al., 2024; Faysse et al., 2025; Cho et al., 2024; Suri et al., 2024)。另一个使用解析器将文档转换为文本. The other converts documents into text using a parser(Wang et al., 2024a)并应用标准文本检索，以细粒度视觉信息为代价提供更高效且与模态无关的解决方案。除了这两种策略之外，最近的工作还探索了根据信息需求将查询路由到不同的检索源和粒度and applies standard text retrieval, offering a more efficient and modality-agnostic solution at the cost of fine-grained visual information. Beyond these two strategies, recent work also explores routing queries to different retrieval sources and granularities based on their information needs(Yeo et al., 2025)。然而，这些方法依赖于独立评分单元的平面检索，而没有明确地对检索到的信息之间的关系进行建模。此外，模型可能无法充分利用串联的证据. Nevertheless, these approaches rely on flat retrieval of independently scored units, without explicitly modeling the relations among the retrieved information. Moreover, the concatenated evidence may not be fully utilized by the model(Liu et al., 2026).

6. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们系统地研究了文本上下文在面向 GraphRAG 的 MMKG 构建中的作用。我们引入了 CEMMKG，一个在视觉信息处理和模态融合中系统地定义和利用文本上下文的框架。具体来说，CEMMKG从互补的角度设计视觉元素的文本上下文，包括不同粒度的局部上下文和基于不同文档级表示的全局上下文。我们进一步探讨如何将设计的环境有效地融入 MMKG 建设的不同阶段。基于不同上下文配置的大量实验表明，适当设计的文本上下文可以有效提高基于 MMKG 的 RAG 的性能。此外，CEMMKG可以有效地应用于不同的基于MMKG的RAG方法，展示了其更广泛的适用性。总的来说，我们的研究结果强调了在为多模态图 RAG 构建 MMKG 时明确考虑和利用上下文信息的重要性。未来的工作可以将我们的框架扩展到更广泛的模式，例如视频和音频，并研究如何针对不同的模式组合定义和利用上下文信息。In this work, we systematically investigate the role of textual context in GraphRAG-oriented MMKG construction. We introduce CEMMKG, a framework that systematically defines and utilizes textual context in both visual information processing and modality fusion. Specifically, CEMMKG designs textual context for visual elements from complementary perspectives, including local context at different granularities and global context based on different document-level representations. We further explore how the designed context can be effectively incorporated across different stages of MMKG construction. Extensive experiments based on different context configurations demonstrate that appropriately designed textual context can effectively improve the performance of MMKG-based RAG. Moreover, CEMMKG can be effectively applied to different MMKG-based RAG methods, demonstrating its broader applicability. Overall, our findings highlight the importance of explicitly considering and leveraging contextual information when constructing MMKGs for multimodal Graph RAG. Future work could extend our framework to a broader range of modalities, such as video and audio, and investigate how contextual information should be defined and utilized for different modality combinations.
