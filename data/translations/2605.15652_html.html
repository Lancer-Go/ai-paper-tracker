摘要Abstract

矢量海马支架异联想记忆（Vector-HaSH）和托尔曼-艾兴鲍姆机（TEM）提出，海马-内嗅回路分解来自预结构化网格细胞支架的内容存储，并通过纹波介导的重放支持组合记忆。与此同时，人类颅内电生理学也表明，海马尖波波纹（SWR）控制情景回忆，波纹锁定皮层重新激活概括了编码时间模式，并且多跳重放保真度沿着序列长度大约成倍地衰减。两种文献并行发展，但没有共享的代数对象。Vector Hippocampal Scaffolded Heteroassociative Memory (Vector-HaSH) and the Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine (TEM) propose that the hippocampal–entorhinal circuit factorizes content storage from a prestructured grid-cell scaffold and supports compositional memory through ripple-mediated replay. Human intracranial electrophysiology, in parallel, has shown that hippocampal sharp-wave ripples (SWRs) gate episodic recall, that ripple-locked cortical reactivation recapitulates encoding-time patterns, and that multi-hop replay fidelity decays approximately multiplicatively along sequence length. The two literatures have advanced in parallel without a shared algebraic object.

1 简介Introduction

在过去的五年里，三个基本上独立的研究方向汇聚在同一个海马-内嗅记忆的结构图上。Three largely independent research strands have converged, over the past five years, on the same architectural picture of hippocampal–entorhinal memory.

规范计算神经科学Normative computational neuroscience提出了两个有影响力的模型来解释内侧颞叶如何存储组合的、序列组织的记忆：矢量海马支架异联想记忆（Vector-HaSH）has produced two influential models of how the medial temporal lobe stores compositional, sequence-organized memory: Vector Hippocampal Scaffolded Heteroassociative Memory (Vector-HaSH)[2]和托尔曼-艾兴鲍姆机 (TEM)and the Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine (TEM)[3]。两者都认为，用内嗅网格细胞模块识别的预结构化低维支架通过固定投影映射到高维海马层，该内容通过联想学习绑定到支架状态，并且这种因式分解是跨空间和非空间域的泛化的基础。 Vector-HaSH 特别要求支架到海马体的投影是随机且固定的，并将关联内容学习附加到所得的近正交代码上。. Both posit that a prestructured low-dimensional scaffold—identified with entorhinal grid-cell modules—is mapped into a high-dimensional hippocampal layer through fixed projections, that content is bound to scaffold states by associative learning, and that this factorization underlies generalization across spatial and non-spatial domains. Vector-HaSH in particular requires the scaffold-to-hippocampus projection to be random and fixed, with associative content learning attached to the resulting near-orthogonal codes.

经验电生理学Empirical electrophysiology同时，还证明海马尖波波纹（SWR）在清醒人类的情景回忆中起着因果作用。在发声免费召回之前波纹率上升has, in parallel, demonstrated that hippocampal sharp-wave ripples (SWRs) play a causal role in episodic recall in awake humans. Ripple rate rises before vocalized free recall[4, 5];皮层中的纹波锁定重新激活概括了编码时间模式; ripple-locked reactivation in cortex recapitulates encoding-time patterns[6, 7];多跳、非本地重播支持推理和决策; multi-hop, non-local replay supports inference and decision[8, 9];多步重放保真度在序列长度上近似成倍衰减; and multi-step replay fidelity decays approximately multiplicatively in sequence length[10]。最近的一项工作将波纹介导的重新激活与创造性重组联系起来，人类 iEEG 表明，波纹在创造性构思过程中会暂时放松内侧前额叶图式限制. A recent line of work links ripple-mediated reactivation to creative recombination, with human iEEG showing that ripples transiently relax medial-prefrontal schema constraints during creative ideation[11].

代数超维计算Algebraic hyperdimensional computing已经独立生产了一个工程基质，我们将展示它在数学而不是类比层面上实例化这些生物学建议。 VaCoAl 架构has independently produced an engineering substrate that, we will show, instantiates these biological proposals at the level of mathematics rather than analogy. The VaCoAl architecture[1]取代稀疏分布式内存（SDM）的随机投影replaces the random projection of Sparse Distributed Memory (SDM)[12, 13]通过线性反馈移位寄存器 (LFSR) 实现确定性伽罗瓦场扩散；用逐块代数多数投票取代详尽的相似性搜索；并通过有限域位操作支持可逆组合绑定/绑定/解除绑定with deterministic Galois-field diffusion implemented by linear-feedback shift registers (LFSRs); replaces exhaustive similarity search with block-wise algebraic majority voting; and supports reversible compositional Binding/Bundling/Unbinding through finite-field bit operations[14, 15, 16]。在包含大约 470,000 条记录和超过 2550 万条路径的维基数据导师-学生有向无环图 (DAG) 上，VaCoAl 已被证明可以生成路径积分置信比 CR2，该置信比 CR2 在推理跳跃中呈乘法衰减，从而产生类似尖峰时序相关可塑性 (STDP) 的路径相关选择函数. On a Wikidata mentor–student directed acyclic graph (DAG) of approximately 470,000 records and more than 25.5 million paths, VaCoAl has been shown to produce a path-integral Confidence Ratio CR2 that decays multiplicatively across reasoning hops, yielding a Spike-Timing-Dependent-Plasticity (STDP)-like path-dependent selection function[1].

We argue that VaCoAl supplies both.

1.1 贡献Contributions

本文做出了四个交叉的贡献，所有的框架都是架构承诺对应关系，而不是底层级别的标识。This paper makes four intersecting contributions, all framed as architectural-commitment correspondences rather than substrate-level identifications.

  1. 1.

    伽罗瓦场扩散是 Vector-HaSH 随机支架投影的确定性实现（第 1 节）Galois-field diffusion is a deterministic realization of Vector-HaSH’s random scaffold projection (Section3).在 LFSR 生成多项式的标准原性假设下，确定性伽罗瓦场扩散满足 Vector-HaSH 的 Johnson–Lindenstrauss 式准正交性要求，具有相对于随机稀疏二元投影的匹配二阶矩统计量、在最小权重扰动下严格更强的最坏情况雪崩行为以及完全再现性。因此，随机投影中的“随机”对于 Vector-HaSH 提案来说并不重要：重要的是扩散准正交性，而有限域代数确定性地提供了它。Under standard primitivity assumptions on the LFSR generator polynomial, deterministic Galois-field diffusion satisfies the Johnson–Lindenstrauss-style quasi-orthogonality requirement of Vector-HaSH with matched second-moment statistics relative to a random sparse binary projection, strictly stronger worst-case avalanche behavior at minimum-weight perturbations, and full reproducibility.
