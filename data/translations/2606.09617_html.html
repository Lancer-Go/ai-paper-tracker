摘要Abstract

人工智能在全球范围内的快速扩张导致能源密集型超大规模数据中心（DC）的激增，使其成为电力系统规划和运营中结构上具有挑战性的组成部分。我们使用欧洲在 21 个人工智能增长场景中的空间显式优化模型，系统地量化了数据中心的额外需求、容量要求、排放和运营影响。结果表明，到 2050 年，人工智能可能会带来 73-723 太瓦时的额外需求，从而导致 2030 年至 2050 年间累计排放量超标 67-181 吨二氧化碳。我们的分析表明，2030 年之后，人工智能基础设施的布局将更多地受到公司实力和系统灵活性的影响，而不仅仅是丰富的清洁能源。在温和的情况下，人工智能需要额外 200 小时的固定发电时间，这会使关键枢纽的 LCOE 增加 35 欧元/兆瓦时。我们表明，即使在悲观的情况下，现有基础设施也将需要 70 吉瓦以上的额外容量，而在可控增长路径下，这一扩张可能达到 226 吉瓦以上。我们进一步发现，数据中心的工作负载动态强烈影响能源调度、系统灵活性和排放，而效率的提高则显着降低了容量需求和系统峰值。虽然我们的研究结果表明，2050 年的净零目标可能会实现，但关键的排放风险可能会在中间几年出现，除非政策适应这种加速的数字化转型，否则欧盟可能会损害其碳中和目标。The rapid expansion of AI globally has led to the proliferation of energy-intensive hyperscale data centres (DCs), making them as a structurally challenging component in power system planning and operation. Using a spatially explicit optimisation model of Europe across 21 AI growth scenarios, we systematically quantify additional demand, capacity requirements, emissions, and operational impacts of DCs. Results indicate that AI could drive 73–723 TWh of extra demand by 2050, risking cumulative emissions overshoots of 67–181 MtCO₂ between 2030 and 2050. Our analysis indicates that after 2030, the geography of AI infrastructure will be shaped more by firm power and system flexibility than by the mere abundance of clean energy. In moderate scenarios, AI requires an additional of 200 hours of firm generation, which increases LCOE by €35/MWh in key hubs. We show that even under the pessimistic scenarios, existing infrastructure would require +70 GW additional capacity, while under managed growth pathways, this expansion could reach +226 GW. We further find DCs workload dynamics strongly shape energy dispatch, system flexibility, and emissions, while improved efficiency significantly reduces capacity needs, and system peaks. While our findings suggest that net-zero targets for 2050 may be achieved, critical emission risks may appear in the intermediate years, and the EU may compromise its carbon-neutral goals unless policies adapt to this accelerating digital transformation.

到 2024 年，全球平均气温将比工业化前水平上升 1.55°C，超过《巴黎协定》1.5°C 的临界极限，阻止全球变暖的努力变得刻不容缓，因此能源系统的弹性脱碳变得至关重要[1,2]。 2019 年，欧洲发布了《绿色协议》，制定了到 2050 年实现碳中和的雄心勃勃的路线图，其中中期目标包括到 2030 年将温室气体 (GHG) 排放量减少 55%[3]。在实现净零排放的竞赛中，技术组合、脱碳步伐和需求管理等关键因素已证明会严重影响即将到来的电力系统转型的最终成本[4,5]。着眼于后者，最近广泛采用的生成式人工智能（GenAI）和云计算服务促进了持续的数字化工作，引入了一个新的重要需求组成部分，由于计算工作量的空前增长，应在脱碳路径中考虑到这一需求组成部分[6-8]。广泛With the global average temperature reaching 1.55[o] C above pre-industrial levels in 2024, overshooting the Paris Agreement’s critical limit of 1.5[o] C, the pursuit to halt global warming becomes urgent rendering the resilient decarbonisation of energy systems crucial[1,2] . In 2019, Europe issued its Green Deal, which sets out an ambitious roadmap to carbon neutrality by 2050 with intermediate targets including a 55% reduction of greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions by 2030[3] . In the race towards net-zero, key factors including technology mix, pace of decarbonisation and demand management have shown to influence heavily the end cost of the forthcoming power systems transformation[4,5] . Focusing on the latter, the recent widespread adoption of Generative AI (GenAI) and cloud computing services that facilitate ongoing digitalisation efforts, has introduced a new significant demand component that should be accounted for in decarbonisation pathways owing to unprecedented growth in computational workloads[6-8] . In Europe, this sector currently consumes roughly 70 TWh of electricity, but the trajectory is steepening[15] . While the International Energy Agency (IEA) forecasts a rise to 115 TWh by 2030[15,16] , other industry leaders suggest a much steeper climb; McKinsey and Independent Commodity Intelligence Services (ICIS) project totals reaching between 150-168 TWh[17-19] , with more aggressive estimates suggesting annual demand could soar as high as 287 TWh[20,21] .

除了这些中期预测之外，这种加速的速度已经给电网带来了切实的挑战，电网需要协调部门运营强度与现有基础设施。在全球最大的直流市场北弗吉尼亚州，计划天然气容量在2021年至2024年期间保持稳定，但在2025年猛增20%以满足飙升的需求，迫使一些公用事业公司延长燃煤电厂的运营以维持电网稳定[22,23]。如果这些趋势在欧洲得到体现，人工智能基础设施的能源密集型扩张可能会直接破坏其脱碳努力。然而，还必须从相反的角度来看待搁浅资产风险的挑战。如果人工智能部署或其相关电力需求低于当前不断增长的预测，电网升级的大量过度投资可能会导致最终消费者的财务效率显着低下，电价上涨[24,25]。Beyond these midterm forecasts, the sheer speed of this acceleration is already creating tangible challenges for power grid that need to reconcile the sector operational intensity with existing infrastructure. In Northern Virginia, the largest DC market in the world, planned gas capacity remained stable between 2021 and 2024, but surged by 20% in 2025 to satisfy skyrocketing demand, forcing some utilities to extend the operation of coal-fired plants to maintain grid stability[22,23] . If these trends are mirrored in Europe, the energy-intensive expansion of AI infrastructure could directly undermine its decarbonisation efforts. However, the challenge must also be viewed through an inverse lens regarding the risk of stranded assets. Should AI deployment or its associated electricity demand fall short of current ever-increasing projections significant over-investment in grid upgrades could lead to significant financial inefficiencies and higher electricity prices for end-consumers[24,25] .

在现有文献的基础上，我们的研究通过使用覆盖 33 个欧洲国家的空间显式优化模型来研究大规模人工智能部署如何影响规划的电网基础设施规划和净零排放战略，从而推进人工智能与能源之间的联系。该模型采用混合整数线性规划（MILP）公式来联合优化长期容量扩展和每小时系统调度。这种方法结合了现有的发展计划以及运营限制和气候政策目标。利用历史数据，我们得出超大规模数据中心的每小时负载概况，并优化 21 个情景（从悲观增长到乐观增长）到 2050 年人工智能相关容量的空间和时间演变。Building on this existing literature our study advances the AI-energy nexus by using a spatially explicit optimisation model covering 33 European countries to examine how large-scale AI deployment affects planned grid infrastructure planning and net-zero strategies. The model employs a mixed integer linear programming (MILP) formulation to jointly optimise long term capacity expansion and hourly system dispatch. This approach incorporates existing development plans along with operational constraints and climate policy targets. Using historical data, we derive hourly load profiles of hyperscale DCs and optimise the spatial and temporal evolution of AI related capacity across 21 scenarios ranging from pessimistic to optimistic growth through 2050.

这项工作围绕四个核心问题展开：This work is structured around four central questions:1.现有的欧洲电力发展计划是否足以满足直流需求的增长？Are existing European power development plans adequate for DC demand growth?2.哪些基础设施技术和能源战略对于协调快速人工智能至关重要，哪些因素促进或限制数据中心跨区域部署？Which infrastructure technologies and energy strategies are essential to reconcile rapid AI and what factors enable or constrain DCs deployment across regions?3.如果 2030 年后人工智能增长停滞或崩溃怎么办？电网扩张会面临哪些结构性风险？What if AI growth stalls or collapses after 2030, and what are the structural risks to power grid expansion?4.人工智能的增长和效率提升将如何重塑欧洲发电模式、跨境流动、电价和排放路径？How will AI growth and efficiency gains reshape European generation patterns, cross-border flows, electricity price and emission pathways?

通过解决这些问题，本文首次对大规模人工智能驱动的需求如何与欧洲脱碳计划相互作用进行了综合、空间明确和基于场景的分析，为政策制定者、系统运营商和投资者提供了新的见解。By addressing these questions, this paper provides the first integrated, spatially explicit, and scenariobased analysis of how large-scale AI-driven demand may interact with Europe’s decarbonisation plans, offering new insights for policymakers, system operators, and investors.

为了系统地探索人工智能能源足迹的不确定性，我们开发了 21 种不同的增长情景。这些情景基于 IEA[15]、ICIS y[18]、麦肯锡[19] 和 ICIS 的预测，为每个情景定义了七种情景：基础、起飞、逆风（Head）、高效率（High-efficiency）和三种悲观情景：通货紧缩、中度和结束。这种多场景框架使我们能够首先可视化能源影响、基础设施要求和排放轨迹，同时回答有关欧洲所有 21 种场景的需求地点和需求量的问题。为了说明超大规模数据中心的工作负载动态及其运营灵活性，我们还使用从历史数据派生的两个不同的负载配置文件来运行优化模型，每个负载配置文件在波动和负载系数方面表现出完全不同的模式，称为英国配置文件和美国配置文件。用于构建所有场景的详细假设参数记录在方法部分和补充材料 S.2 部分中。To systematically explore the uncertainties surrounding the energy footprint of AI, we develop 21 distinct growth scenarios. These scenarios are grounded in projections from the IEA[15] , ICIS y[18] , and McKinsey[19] , and ICIS, with seven scenarios defined for each: Base, Lift-off, Headwinds (Head), Highefficiency (High-eff), and three pessimistic scenarios: Deflation, Medium, and End. To illustrate the workload dynamics of hyperscale data centers and their operational flexibility, we also run the optimization model using two distinct load profiles derived from historical data, each exhibiting completely different patterns in terms of fluctuations and load factor, referred to as the UK profile and the US profile. PUE 对系统结果的影响：敏感性分析Impact of PUE on system outcomes: Sensitivity analysis

本节研究直流工作负载和能源效率如何影响未来的欧洲电力系统。电力使用效率（PUE）是数据中心用电量的关键驱动因素。 IEA 预测表明，到 2030 年，欧洲的平均 PUE 可能从目前的 1.45 降至 1.29。我们的基本情景假设 2030 年后保守的 1.25，但技术或基础设施升级的不确定性需要进行敏感性分析。 PUE 从 1.4 变化到 1.1 会影响总成本、排放和电网峰值需求（图 7）。如果与预期相反，PUE 仅提高至 1.4，即较当前水平小幅降低 5%，则在美国负荷情况下，到 2030 年、2040 年和 2050 年，总成本将分别增加约 8%、7% 和 6%。相反，到 2050 年，按照美国和英国的情况，实现 1.1 的 PUE 可以分别降低 6% 和 4% 的成本。This section examines how DC workloads and energy efficiency may affect the future European power system. The power usage effectiveness (PUE) is the key driver of electricity consumption in DCs. IEA projections indicate Europe’s average PUE could fall from 1.45 today to 1.29 by 2030. Our Base Case assumes a conservative 1.25 post-2030, but uncertainties in technology or infrastructure upgrades require sensitivity analysis. Varying PUE from 1.4 to 1.1 impacts total cost, emissions, and grid peak demand (Fig. 7). If, contrary to expectations, PUE improves only to 1.4, representing a modest 5% reduction from current levels, total costs increase by approximately 8%, 7%, and 6% by 2030, 2040, and 2050, respectively, under the U.S. load profile. Conversely, achieving a PUE of 1.1 yields a 6%, and 4% cost reduction following the U.S., and UK profile, respectively by 2050.

PUE 为 1.4 会使碳排放量在 2030 年增加高达 8%，在 2040 年增加 6%。效率的影响随工作负载波动而变化：在美国情景下将 PUE 提高到 1.1 可减少 5% 的排放，而在英国情景下同样改善仅减少 3%。这表明对于不稳定的 AI 工作负载，效率提升大约是 1.6 倍。A PUE of 1.4 raises carbon emissions by up to 8% in 2030 and 6% in 2040. The effect of efficiency varies with workload volatility: improving PUE to 1.1 under the U.S. profile reduces emissions by 5%, while the same improvement under the UK scenario cuts only 3%. This shows efficiency gains are roughly 1.6 times more effective for volatile AI workloads.

2050 年峰值需求分析显示，PUE 每提高 0.05，美国峰值电力需求将减少约 5 GWh，英国峰值电力需求将减少 3 GWh。如果 PUE 改进不超过 1.4，则需要额外 67 GW（美国）和 60 GW（英国）的发电容量来维持可靠性。这些国家层面的结果更为明显，到 2050 年可能会减少多达 50 吉瓦的新增产能需求（补充材料图 S.12 和 S.13）。The 2050 peak-demand analysis shows that each 0.05 improvement in PUE lowers peak electricity demand by roughly 5 GWh for the U.S. profile, and 3 GWh for the UK profile. Without PUE improvements beyond 1.4, an additional 67 GW (U.S.) and 60 GW (UK) of generation capacity would be needed to maintain reliability. These results at the national level are even more pronounced potentially reducing the need for up to 50 GW of new capacity by 2050 (Supplementary Material Figure S.12 and S.13).

讨论Discussion

这项研究表明，快速的直流扩张改变了欧洲电力系统转型的结构。主要限制不仅是年度能源供应，还有公司的产能充足性。在当前的基础设施发展轨迹下，这种结构性转变会导致化石能源淘汰动态暂时逆转，并放大 2030 年代的转型风险。This study shows that rapid DC expansion alters the structure of Europe’s power system transition. The primary constraint is not only annual energy supply, but firm capacity adequacy. Under current infrastructure trajectories, this structural shift induces a temporary reversal in fossil phase-out dynamics and amplifies transition risks during the 2030s.

20

在 21 种增长情景中，发展中国家的扩张系统性地增加了对固定热资源的依赖。从中期来看，天然气将成为主要的稳定技术，而煤炭和石油将持续超出预期的淘汰窗口。在起飞情景中，煤炭将一直保留在发电结构中直到 2045 年，而不是像非人工智能基线那样到 2035 年退出。 2030 年至 2045 年间，有 17 种情景出现排放超调。人工智能驱动的电力需求并不妨碍实现长期净零目标。然而，它大大增加了在 2030 年代超过欧洲中期排放目标的可能性。这种偏差反映了直流电需求扩张的速度与维持系统充足性所需的稳定、低碳可调度容量的部署之间存在暂时的差距。Across 21 growth scenarios, DCs expansion systematically increases reliance on firm thermal resources. In the medium term, gas emerges as the dominant stabilizing technology, while coal and oil persist beyond their expected phase-out window. In lift-off scenarios, coal remains in the generation mix until 2045 rather than exiting by 2035 as in non-AI baselines. Seventeen scenarios exhibit emission overshoot between 2030 and 2045. AI-driven power demand does not prevent the achievement of longterm net-zero targets. However, it substantially increases the likelihood of exceeding Europe’s interim emission targets during the 2030s. This deviation reflects a temporary gap between the pace of DC demand expansion and the deployment of firm, low-carbon dispatchable capacity required to maintain system adequacy.

我们的结果表明，如果不进行结构性扩张，欧洲的基准基础设施就无法适应人工智能的增长，在悲观到起飞的场景中需要额外的 73-723 GW 容量，其中约 70% 由可再生能源和电网级存储组成，约 30% 为公司可调度资源。根据现有容量假设，该系统必须要么扩大固定资源，主要是 CCGT、H2CCGT 和核能，要么接受推迟化石能源淘汰。 2030 年代，天然气将从边际平衡角色转变为结构性充足锚。随着 2040 年代碳约束的收紧，其功能变得越来越以备用为导向，但早期容量增加会产生惰性和潜在的锁定。Our results indicate that Europe’s baseline infrastructure cannot accommodate AI growth without structural expansion, requiring an additional 73–723 GW of capacity across pessimistic to lift-off scenarios, of which roughly 70% consists of renewables and grid-level storage and about 30% of firm dispatchable resources. Under existing capacity assumptions, the system must either expand firm resources, primarily CCGT, H2CCGT, and nuclear, or accept a delayed fossil phase-out. Gas shifts from a marginal balancing role to a structural adequacy anchor during the 2030s. As carbon constraints tighten in the 2040s, its function becomes increasingly backup-oriented, yet early capacity additions generate inertia and potential lock-in.

直流容量的地理分配进一步强调，每年的可再生能源丰度并不是充分的选址标准。由于可再生能源发电过剩，通常被认为是最佳地区，特别是北欧系统，面临结构性充足性限制。尽管年度能源平衡较高，但有限的可调度能力使这些系统在关键时刻面临短缺风险。因此，超大规模直流并网的约束因素不是能源数量，而是稳定的低碳调度能力。因此，将它们转变为未来的直流枢纽将在很大程度上依赖于灵活的发电。相反，拥有可再生能源、核基本负荷和灵活天然气资产的多元化投资组合的地区在最恶劣的条件下表现出更大的弹性。在此背景下，H2CCGT 技术在维持系统稳定性同时保持长期脱碳选择性方面发挥着关键的桥梁作用。The geographic allocation of DC capacity further underscores that annual renewable abundance is not a sufficient siting criterion. Regions commonly perceived as optimal due to surplus renewable generation, particularly Nordic systems, face structural adequacy constraints. Despite high yearly energy balances, limited dispatchable capacity exposes these systems to shortfall risk during critical hours. The binding constraint for hyperscale DC integration is therefore not energy volume but firm, low-carbon dispatchability. Hence, transforming them into future DC hubs will strongly depend on flexible generation. Conversely, regions with diversified portfolios combining renewables, nuclear baseload, and flexible gas assets demonstrate greater resilience under worst-day conditions. In this context, H2CCGT technologies play a pivotal bridging role in maintaining system stability while preserving long-term decarbonisation optionality.

我们的结果还发现，在发展中国家需求增长崩溃的情况下，存在明显的风险。在2035年后人工智能需求减弱的情况下，早期对企业产能的投资会导致产能僵局。即使在需求增长放缓的情况下，超大火电机组也能提高系统级平准化度电成本并延长化石燃料备用利用率。这创造了一种新的转型风险：数字化过度建设锁定，预期的基础设施扩张限制了未来的灵活性。Our results also identify a distinct risk in the case of DCs demand growth collapse. In scenarios where AI demand weakens after 2035, early investments in firm capacity result in capacity deadlocks. Oversized thermal fleets elevate system-level LCOE and prolong fossil backup utilisation even under slower demand growth. This creates a new category of transition risk: digital overbuild lock-in, whereby anticipatory infrastructure expansion limits future flexibility.

提高效率和增强工作负载灵活性可能成为数据中心未来的两个关键参数，将其重新定位为灵活性的来源，而不是电网的额外负担。在此过程中，数据中心可以支持可再生能源并网、减少峰值需求、减少总装机容量并降低总体排放量。Improving efficiency and enhancing workload flexibility could become two key parameters in the future of DCs, repositioning them not as an additional burden on the grid but as a source of flexibility. In doing so, DCs could support renewable integration, reduce peak demand, reduce total installed capacity and lower overall emissions.

我们证明，直流负载的时间行为强烈影响能源调度结构，从而影响系统的灵活性要求、排放轨迹以及对不同发电技术的依赖：更恒定的负载曲线会增加系统对核能和天然气发电的依赖，同时也鼓励更大的风电容量扩张和更少的太阳能部署。与此同时，对灵活平衡资源的需求减少导致 H2-CCGT 发电在电力结构中的作用降低。We demonstrated that the temporal behaviour of DC workloads strongly influences the energy dispatch structure and, consequently, the system’s flexibility requirements, emissions trajectory, and dependence on different generation technologies: A more constant load profile increases the system’s reliance on nuclear and natural gas generation, while also encouraging greater wind capacity expansion and lower solar deployment. At the same time, the reduced need for flexible balancing resources leads to a lower role for H2-CCGT generation in the electricity mix.

一些限制值得进一步调查。超大规模设施的土地使用限制、本地分配瓶颈和连接队列没有明确建模，可能会对可行的部署率产生重大影响。虽然敏感性分析考虑了 PUE 的改进，但硬件效率和冷却技术的快速进步可能会改变未来的负载强度和选址模式。最后，电动汽车（EV）和需求响应计划动态作为灵活的选择并未纳入我们的模型中。电动汽车被视为固定存储，而不是动态的车辆到电网参与者，这可能低估了系统的灵活性。此外，在本研究中，网络层面和数据中心角度的需求响应计划可能有助于提高网络灵活性，但尚未得到审查，留待未来工作。另外，需要注意的是，在本研究中，人工智能仅从电力消耗及其可能给电力系统带来的额外压力的角度来考虑。然而，众所周知，人工智能在提高效率和减少各个部门的电力消耗方面具有重要而广泛的应用，例如预测方法、降低成本和众多经济优化。Several limitations warrant further investigation. Land-use constraints for hyperscale facilities, local distribution bottlenecks, and connection queues are not explicitly modelled and could materially affect feasible deployment rates. While sensitivity analyses account for improvements in PUE, rapid advances in hardware efficiency and cooling technologies may alter future load intensity and siting patterns. Finally, electric vehicles (EVs) and demand response program dynamics as flexible options are not incorporated in our model. EVs are represented as stationary storage rather than dynamic vehicle-to-grid actors, potentially underestimating system flexibility. Additionally, in this study, demand response programs, both at the network level and from the perspective of DCs, which could contribute to improving network flexibility, have not been examined and are left for future work. Also, it is important to note that, in this study, AI has been considered only from the perspective of electricity consumption and the additional pressure it may impose on the power system. However, it is well recognized that AI has significant and widespread applications in improving efficiency and reducing electricity consumption across various sectors, such as forecasting methods, cost reduction, and numerous economic optimisations.

总体而言，即使在最乐观的情况下，即到 2050 年人工智能驱动的数据中心将占欧洲总电力消耗的 9% 左右，挑战也不仅仅是能源总需求之一；相反，它重塑了欧洲能源转型的充足架构。应对这一转变需要将数字化扩张与加速部署坚定的低碳产能相结合，以避免暂时的化石依赖和长期的结构性锁定。Overall, even in the most optimistic scenarios where AI-driven data centers account for around 9% of Europe’s total electricity consumption by 2050, the challenge is not merely one of aggregate energy demand; rather, it reshapes the adequacy architecture of Europe’s energy transition. Managing this shift requires aligning digital expansion with accelerated deployment of firm low-carbon capacity to avoid temporary fossil reliance and long-lived structural lock-in.

欧洲综合电力系统的长期发电和输电扩展规划问题被表述为多周期、空间明确的 MILP 模型，可同时优化投资和运营决策。目标函数最小化总系统成本，包括资本支出和运营支出。资本成本包括对新一代发电能力、能源存储系统和跨境传输基础设施的投资。运营成本包括可变发电和存储成本、燃料消耗、固定运营和维护成本、碳成本、负载损失惩罚（负载损失价值，VOLL）以及可再生能源削减惩罚。补充材料第 S.3 节提供了问题表述的完整细节，包括目标函数和约束。The long-term generation and transmission expansion planning problem for the integrated European power system is formulated as a multi-period, spatially explicit MILP model that simultaneously optimises investment and operational decisions. The objective function minimises total system cost, comprising both capital and operational expenditures. Capital costs include investments in new generation capacity, energy storage systems, and cross-border transmission infrastructure. Operational costs account for variable generation and storage costs, fuel consumption, fixed operation and maintenance costs, carbon cost, penalties for loss of load (value of lost load, VOLL), and penalties for renewable energy curtailment. The full details of problem formulation including objective function and constraints are provided in Supplementary Material, Section S. 3.
