摘要Abstract

Rubrics provide a natural interface for this fine-grained supervision, but their effectiveness depends on the execution accuracy during online RL. 1 简介Introduction

具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 已成为一种实用的训练后方法，因为确定性结果检查提供了明确的奖励信号Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has become a practical post-training recipe because deterministic outcome checks provide unambiguous reward signals[8]。它在数学和代码中很有效，正确性可以由精确的验证者来决定. It has been effective in math and code, where correctness can be decided by exact verifiers[22, 26, 6]，最近已扩展到视觉语言任务，例如 OCR、计数和接地, and has recently been extended to vision-language tasks such as OCR, counting, and grounding[23, 29, 27]。然而，这种范式受到任务级假设的限制：正在优化的行为必须是可验证的. However, this paradigm is bounded by a task-level assumption: the behavior being optimized must be verifiable[10].

许多视觉语言任务都属于Many vision-language tasks fall into a部分可验证partially verifiable政权。最终答案可能是完全可检查的，而中间的感知事实、推理步骤和遵循指令的细节也受益于额外的监督。从单一可验证性角度对整个任务进行评分将这些区别分解为粗略的奖励。这激发了特定于实例的评价标准，将模糊的响应质量评估转化为具体的标准regime. A final answer may be exactly checkable, while intermediate perceptual facts, reasoning steps, and instruction-following details also benefit from extra supervision. Scoring the whole task from a single verifiability perspective collapses these distinctions into a coarse reward. This motivates instance-specific rubrics, which turn ambiguous response quality assessment into concrete criteria[7, 28, 11, 38, 19, 35].

仅当可以在在线 RL 中准确评分时，评分标准才有用。 在离线评估中，不完美的评分标准执行只会给固定响应集增加噪音。 在在线强化学习中，任何系统性的错误执行都会成为政策的激励因素。 直观的优化是将每个标准与适当的执行路径相匹配：可验证的标准可以通过预测提取和确定性检查来处理，而其他标准可以通过语义判断来处理。Rubrics are useful only if their criteria can be accurately scored in online RL. In offline evaluation, imperfect rubric execution merely adds noise to a fixed response set. In online RL, any systematic rubric mis-execution becomes an incentive for the policy. An intuitive optimization is to match each criterion with an appropriate execution path: verifiable criteria can be handled by prediction extraction followed by deterministic checking, while the others can be handled by semantic judgment.

Each criterion is routed either to a text-only LLM-as-an-extractor followed by a deterministic verifier or to a text-only LLM-as-a-Judge when deterministic checking is unavailable. Verifier targets are hidden from the extractor, and source images are hidden from both execution paths to prevent shortcuts. 4 方法论Methodology

在线强化学习的一个基本挑战是，该策略往往会在持续优化过程中破坏奖励。 为了保持奖励的准确性和稳健性，A fundamental challenge in online RL is that the policy tends to hack the reward during continuous optimization. To keep rubric rewards accurate and robust,RLR3\text{RLR}^{3}遵循三个原则。 首先，我们优先考虑可验证性，以限制利用空间。 其次，我们限制证据暴露，以防止出现意外的捷径。 第三，我们在聚合过程中保留了多重奖励的区别。follows three principles. First, we prioritize verifiability to limit the space for exploitation. Second, we restrict evidence exposure to prevent unintended shortcuts. Third, we preserve the multi-reward distinctions during aggregation.

我们的方法通过三个组件实现这些原则。 部分Our method implements these principles across three components. 6 结论和局限性Conclusion and Limitations

我们提出了We presentedRLR3\text{RLR}^{3}，一个在线强化学习框架，在视觉语言模型中具有基于规则的奖励。 通过将可验证性视为标准级别的属性，, a framework for online reinforcement learning with rubric-based rewards in vision-language models. By treating verifiability as a criterion-level property,RLR3\text{RLR}^{3}将 RLVR 从任务级结果检查扩展到可能需要确定性验证或语义判断的标题标准。 其执行路由、最小暴露策略、分数重新映射、分层聚合和 RLVR 训练的 GenRM 使标准级评分在在线优化下更加忠实和信息丰富。 在三种开源培训混合物中，extends RLVR from task-level outcome checking to rubric criteria that may require either deterministic verification or semantic judgment. Its execution routing, minimal exposure strategy, score remapping, hierarchical aggregation, and RLVR-trained GenRM make criterion-level scoring more faithful and informative under online optimization. Across three open-source training mixtures,RLR3\text{RLR}^{3}相对 RLVR 提高了宏观平均值。 受控 GenRM 审计进一步表明，确定性验证和最小暴露可以减少可利用的误报，而不会牺牲评分准确性。improves the macro average over RLVR. Controlled GenRM audits further show that deterministic verification and minimal exposure reduce exploitable false positives without sacrificing scoring accuracy.

尽管取得了这些进展，当前的研究仍存在一些局限性。 首先，Despite these gains, the current study has several limitations. First,RLR3\text{RLR}^{3}仍然取决于前沿模型生成的评估标准。 尽管这些准则在实践中是有效的，但准则生成器位于策略优化循环之外，并且本身并未通过在线培训进行改进。 因此，标题质量可能成为瓶颈，特别是当生成器未指定中间感知证据或推理要求时。 其次，训练轨迹在检查点之间仍然表现出不小的度量波动。 这表明在线强化学习期间学到的成果尚未完全稳定或整合为单一的最终策略。 下一步重要的一步是引入在线策略蒸馏，以便可以更一致地积累在不同训练阶段发现的强行为。still depends on rubrics generated by frontier models. Although these rubrics are effective in practice, the rubric generator is external to the policy optimization loop and is not itself improved through online training. As a result, rubric quality can become a bottleneck, especially when the generator under-specifies intermediate perceptual evidence or reasoning requirements. Second, the training trajectories still exhibit non-trivial metric fluctuations across checkpoints. This suggests that the gains learned during online RL are not yet fully stabilized or consolidated into a single final policy. An important next step is to introduce online policy distillation so that strong behaviors discovered at different stages of training can be accumulated more consistently.
