1 简介Introduction

However, they still suffer from object hallucination[22, 4, 25]，即生成图像中不存在的对象，从而破坏其可靠性和实际用途。, i.e., generating objects that are not present in the image, undermining their reliability and practical use.

Accordingly, many methods attempt to amplify or redistribute attention[28, 11, 43, 15, 17]，或在训练/推理过程中注入额外的视觉指导, or inject extra visual guidance during training/inference[9, 34, 18, 45]。然而，我们的实验表明这种解释是不完整的：. However, our experiments reveal that this explanation is incomplete:关键问题可能不在于模型对图像的关注程度，而在于它关注什么以及为什么the key issue may not be how much the model attends to the image, but what it attends to and why。为了支持这一说法，我们首先检查. 近年来，大型视觉语言模型（LVLM）取得了显着进展，它将语言模型与视觉编码器集成以执行统一的多模态能力。 早期作品如 CLIPRecent years have witnessed remarkable progress in Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs), which integrate language models with visual encoders to perform unified multimodal capacity. Early work such as CLIP[32]通过对比图像-文本对齐建立大规模视觉-语言预训练。后续型号，包括 BLIP-2established large-scale vision–language pretraining via contrastive image–text alignment. More recently, instruction-tuned LVLMs such as LLaVA[27, 26], 什克拉, Shikra[6], mPLUG-Owl2 [42],Qwen-VL, Qwen-VL[3]和实习生VLand InternVL[8]展示了令人印象深刻的开放式生成和推理能力。尽管取得了这些进步，LVLM 仍然经常产生幻觉对象，即描述图像中不存在的对象，这损害了它们在实际应用中的可靠性。have demonstrated impressive open-ended generation and reasoning capabilities. 6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们通过注意力透镜和 Logit Lens 分析重新审视 LVLM 中的物体幻觉。通过分析对象标记生成过程，我们揭示了一种“相同的注意力，不同的事实”现象：真实和幻觉的对象在图像注意阶段表现出相似的注意模式，但只有前者可以通过 Logits-Lens 从关注区域进行语义解码。 在此基础上，我们提出了一个免训练的检测-缓解框架，该框架首先通过 Logit-Lens 一致性检查检测幻觉的对象标记，然后应用原因感知的高注意力区域屏蔽来检测视觉不确定性幻觉，并应用视觉证据增强解码来检测上下文先前的幻觉。 大量的实验证明了我们方法的有效性和通用性。In this work, we revisit object hallucination in LVLMs via the lens of attention and Logit Lens analysis. By analyzing object token generation process, we reveal a “same attention, different truths” phenomenon: both real and hallucinated objects exhibit similar attention patterns in the image-attention stage, yet only the former are semantically decodable from the attended regions via Logits-Lens. Building on this, we propose a training-free Detect–Mitigate framework that first detects hallucinated object tokens via Logit-Lens Consistency Check and then applies cause-aware High Attention Regions Masking for visual uncertainty hallucination and Visual Evidence Enhanced Decoding for contextual prior hallucination. Extensive experiments demonstrate the effectiveness and generality of our method.
