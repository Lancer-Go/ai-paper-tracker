提高实用性约束下综合临床基准的真实性Improving the Realism of Synthetic Clinical Benchmarks Under Utility Constraints

Omid Bazgir * , Md Nasir, Jacob Hoffman, Yang Yang, Manu Agrawal, Anusua Trivedi, Vinay Rao Dandin, Chris Gibbons, Christine Swisher Oracle Health and Life SciencesUnited States omid.bazgir@oracle.com
摘要。Abstract.

企业人工智能代理的综合临床基准可以通过现有的实用程序检查，但在结构上仍然不切实际，特别是在难以访问操作数据的隐私敏感的医疗保健环境中。我们研究如何在不破坏实践中已使用的下游实用程序检查的情况下改进此类基准。Synthetic clinical benchmarks for enterprise AI agents can pass existing utility checks and still remain structurally unrealistic, especially in privacy-sensitive healthcare settings where operational data are hard to access. We study how to improve such benchmarks without breaking the downstream utility checks already used in practice.

我们将基准修订制定为We formulate benchmark revision as效用限制的现实性改进utility-constrained realism improvement：数据集的变化应该增加真实性，同时保持在可操作的实用程序基础之上。我们在护理差距基准上实例化了这一想法，该基准源自 Synthea 生成的患者，通过演示电子健康记录工作流程进行锻炼，然后由与操作数据相同的下游管道进行处理。现实性是通过缺失结构、简单性、结构合理性和总体一致性来衡量的。: dataset changes should increase realism while staying above an operational utility floor. We instantiate this idea on a care-gap benchmark derived from Synthea-generated patients exercised through demonstration electronic health record workflows and then processed by the same downstream pipeline as operational data. Realism is measured through missingness structure, simplicity, structural plausibility, and population alignment.

1. 简介Introduction

当前的实用程序检查可能无法检测到不切实际的综合基准。在我们的设置中，即使基准极其稀疏、高度模板化并且以证据背景薄弱的患者行为主，评估者得出的效用指标仍然继续通过。对于企业医疗保健代理来说，这是一个严重的基准测试失败模式：如果效用指标对于现实缺陷部分不变，那么数据集在操作上看起来可以接受，但仍然无法代表部署数据的难度和模糊性。Current utility checks can fail to detect unrealistic synthetic benchmarks. In our setting, evaluator-derived utility metrics continued to pass even when the benchmark was extremely sparse, heavily templated, and dominated by patient rows with weak evidence context. That is a serious benchmarking failure mode for enterprise healthcare agents: if utility metrics are partially invariant to realism defects, then a dataset can look operationally acceptable while still failing to represent the difficulty and ambiguity of deployment data.

人们越来越期望医疗保健领域的企业人工智能代理能够对纵向患者记录进行推理，应用资格逻辑，并在操作数据高度敏感的环境中生成面向患者或操作员的建议。因此，合成数据和演示环境很有吸引力，因为它们可以减少治理摩擦并实现快速迭代。然而，这些优点带来了基准质量问题：数据集可能易于使用、操作兼容，甚至对下游有用，但仍然不切实际地稀疏、模板化或临床狭窄。Enterprise AI agents in healthcare are increasingly expected to reason over longitudinal patient records, apply eligibility logic, and generate patient- or operator-facing recommendations in environments where operational data are highly sensitive. Synthetic data and demonstration environments are therefore attractive because they reduce governance friction and enable rapid iteration. However, these advantages create a benchmark-quality problem: a dataset can be easy to use, operationally compatible, and even downstream-useful while still being unrealistically sparse, templated, or clinically narrow.

这种故障模式在代理级别很重要。在企业医疗保健环境中，许多代理管道依赖于检索到的证据片段和有界上下文窗口来支持多步骤推理That failure mode matters at the agent level. In enterprise healthcare settings, many agent pipelines rely on retrieved evidence snippets and bounded context windows to support multi-step reasoning(Lewis et al., 2020; Liu et al., 2024)。如果许多行不可操作，则检索返回的患者特定证据较少；如果时间字段缺失，新近度和到期日推理就变得更难确定；如果描述过度模板化，检索信号的辨别力就会降低。即使粗略的评估分数仍然可以接受，这些条件也可能会增加通用或弱基础的响应。. If many rows are non-actionable, retrieval returns less patient-specific evidence; if temporal fields are missing, recency and due-date reasoning become harder to ground; and if descriptions are over-templated, retrieval signal becomes less discriminative. These conditions can plausibly increase generic or weakly grounded responses, even when coarse evaluator scores remain acceptable.

本文研究了护理差距基准的问题，该基准支持企业代理任务，例如患者级别差距解释、证据总结和外展生成。该基准源自 Synthea 生成的合成患者This paper studies that problem for a care-gap benchmark supporting enterprise agent tasks such as patient-level gap interpretation, evidence summarization, and outreach generation. The benchmark originates from Synthea-generated synthetic patients(Walonoski et al., 2018)这些是通过演示电子健康记录工作流程来执行的，然后由与运营客户数据相同的下游管道进行处理。这种起源保留了现实的管道语义，但它仍然可以抑制自然发生的临床群体中出现的异质性、缺失和模糊性。that were exercised through demonstration electronic health record workflows and then processed by the same downstream pipeline as operational client data. That provenance preserves realistic pipeline semantics, but it can still suppress the heterogeneity, missingness, and ambiguity seen in naturally occurring clinical populations.

我们的贡献主要是方法论上的。我们建议Our contribution is mainly methodological. We propose效用限制的现实性改进utility-constrained realism improvement：基准修订应提高现实性，同时保持在系统已使用的操作实用程序基础之上。关键思想是将效用视为护栏，而不是作为基准质量的充分证据。本文中的医疗保健基准可作为该改进方案的具体测试平台。: benchmark revisions should improve realism while staying above the operational utility floor already used by the system. The key idea is to treat utility as a guardrail rather than as sufficient evidence of benchmark quality. The healthcare benchmark in this paper serves as a concrete testbed for that refinement recipe.

该框架建立在先前的工作基础上，表明合成健康数据质量本质上是多维的This framing builds on prior work showing that synthetic-health-data quality is inherently multidimensional(Giuffrè and Shung, 2023; Kaabachi et al., 2025);合成 EHR 生成范围来自 Synthea 等场景引擎; that synthetic EHR generation ranges from scenario engines such as Synthea(Walonoski et al., 2018)学习记录生成器to learned record generators(Choi et al., 2017; Li et al., 2023; Yoon et al., 2023);基准测试、可复制性和漂移分析揭示了不同的故障模式; and that benchmarking, replicability, and drift analyses expose different failure modes(Yan et al., 2022; Tucker et al., 2020; El Emam et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2022)。我们利用这些观察结果来激发实用的改进方法：在明确的效用约束下提高真实感，然后将源保真度与内部真实感分开评估。. We use those observations to motivate a practical refinement recipe: improve realism under an explicit utility constraint, then evaluate source fidelity separately from internal realism.

2. 综合数据改进的相关工作Related Work on Synthetic Data Improvement

先前关于合成健康数据的工作主要强调隐私、统计相似性和下游任务转移，而不是这里研究的更狭隘的问题：如何改进已经可操作但结构过于简单的基准。评论认为，必须通过多种标准来评估保真度Prior work on synthetic health data has largely emphasized privacy, statistical resemblance, and downstream-task transfer rather than the narrower question studied here: how to improve a benchmark that is already operationally usable but structurally too simple. Reviews argue that fidelity must be assessed through multiple criteria(Giuffrè and Shung, 2023; Kaabachi et al., 2025);生成论文跨越 Synthea 等场景引擎; generation papers span scenario engines such as Synthea(Walonoski et al., 2018)并学习了 EHR 生成器and learned EHR generators(Choi et al., 2017; Li et al., 2023; Yoon et al., 2023);评估研究表明，基准测试、软件测试、可复制性和偏差控制暴露出不同的弱点; and evaluation studies show that benchmarking, software testing, replicability, and drift control expose different weaknesses(Yan et al., 2022; Tucker et al., 2020; El Emam et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2022)。我们的设置更接近于数据集细化而不是端到端生成：基准保留了管道语义，但其场景驱动的出处仍然可以抑制异质性、模糊性和现实缺失。因此，我们将现实主义视为明确的目标，而不是事后的相似性得分。. Our setting is closer to dataset refinement than to end-to-end generation: the benchmark preserves pipeline semantics, but its scenario-driven provenance can still suppress heterogeneity, ambiguity, and realistic missingness. We therefore treat realism as an explicit objective rather than as a post hoc similarity score.

Refinement-B 保留 Refinement-A 的患者级别结构，并且仅更改测量描述的目标子集。它重点关注 Refinement-A 失去推荐可用性的措施，以及小型密集切片恢复集。这是 Refinement-A 和 Refinement-B 之间唯一有意的区别。Refinement-B preserves the patient-level structure of Refinement-A and changes only a targeted subset of measure descriptions. 密集控制是一种阴性对照，旨在表明仅减少缺失是不够的。它应用了积极的结构致密化，但保持原始基线度量描述不变，因此它可以提高密度，同时保留不切实际的模板。附录中提供了确切的阈值、节奏规则和目标逻辑Dense Control is a negative control designed to show that reducing missingness alone is not enough. It applies aggressive structural densification but leaves the original baseline measure descriptions unchanged, so it can improve density while preserving unrealistic templating. Exact thresholds, cadence rules, and targeting logic are provided in AppendixA.1.

6.6. 对度量设计的影响Implications for Metric Design

这些观察结果表明，稀疏临床基准的现实指标应该扩展到已经实施的面板之外。年龄范围覆盖范围和与参考队列的年龄范围差异应该是明确的指标，因为年龄限制的预防措施是护理差距工作流程的核心。性别、种族、民族和语言也应该报告，不仅是为了完整性，而且是为了过度清洁，因为示范输入的数据集可能会不切实际地抑制实践中经常出现的未知和模糊的值。These observations suggest that realism metrics for sparse clinical benchmarks should be expanded beyond the panels already implemented. Age-band coverage and age-band divergence from a reference cohort should be explicit metrics because age-constrained preventive measures are central to care-gap workflows. Gender, race, ethnicity, and language should also be reported not only for completeness but for over-cleanliness, since demonstration-entered datasets can unrealistically suppress the unknown and ambiguous values that occur routinely in practice.

更广泛地说，基准应该跟踪条件现实主义而不仅仅是边际现实主义。例子包括可采取行动的患者的年龄分布、人口统计亚组中适当措施的比例，以及矛盾证据模式的比率，例如带有令人难以置信的密集支持文件的应有标志。这些可以直接从聚合缓存中计算出来，并且会使真实感分数对表面致密化不那么敏感。More broadly, the benchmark should track conditional realism rather than only marginal realism. Examples include the age distribution among actionable patients, the proportion of due measures within demographic subgroups, and the rate of contradictory evidence patterns such as due flags with implausibly dense supporting documentation. These are straightforward to compute from aggregate caches and would make the realism score less sensitive to superficial densification.

7. 讨论和未来的工作Discussion and Future Work

一个实际的收获是，源自合成患者和演示工作流程的临床基准在操作上是有用的，但在成为强大的机器学习基准之前仍需要大量的现实性改进。在我们的设置中，下游处理是现实的，但上游创建是场景驱动的，因此模式有效性和管道兼容性是必要的，但还不够。更广泛地说，该方法应该超越目标设计和可审计的确定性修订层面的关注差距，而特定领域的节奏规则、评估器语义和黄金验证仍然是特定于应用程序的。当前最大的限制是方法论：下游证据仍然基于评估者，而不是由本地黄金注释集支持。这使得黄金 GC 注释、更广泛的参考群体拉动以及转移到其他企业代理基准成为最重要的后续步骤。A practical takeaway is that a clinical benchmark derived from synthetic patients and demonstration workflows can be operationally useful while still requiring substantial realism improvement before it becomes a strong machine learning benchmark. In our setting, downstream processing is realistic but upstream creation is scenario-driven, so schema validity and pipeline compatibility are necessary but insufficient. More broadly, the method should transfer beyond care gaps at the level of objective design and auditable deterministic revision, while domain-specific cadence rules, evaluator semantics, and gold validation remain application-specific. The largest current limitation is methodological: downstream evidence is still evaluator-based rather than backed by a local gold annotation set. That makes gold GC annotation, broader reference-cohort pulls, and transfer to additional enterprise-agent benchmarks the most important next steps.

8. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一项匿名研究，对源自 Synthea 生成的患者的综合护理差距数据集进行基准细化，并演示电子健康记录工作流程。基线数据集高度稀疏且简单，但当前的效用指标并不能可靠地暴露这一弱点。实用性约束的现实主义提供了一个实用的改进标准：修改数据集以改进现实主义面板，而不违反当前的实用性阈值。在这种情况下，两个确定性修订改进了几个现实主义面板，同时也暴露了下游行为和源保真度方面的重要权衡。仅聚合的操作参考进一步表明，源保真度和内部基准真实性应被视为单独的轴，而不是分解为单一的质量概念。We presented an anonymized study of benchmark refinement for a synthetic care-gap dataset derived from Synthea-generated patients and demonstration electronic health record workflows. The baseline dataset is highly sparse and simple, yet current utility metrics do not reliably expose that weakness. Utility-constrained realism provides a practical improvement criterion: revise the dataset to improve realism panels without violating the current utility threshold. In this setting, two deterministic revisions improve several realism panels, while also exposing nontrivial tradeoffs in downstream behavior and source fidelity. An aggregate-only operational reference further shows that source fidelity and internal benchmark realism should be treated as separate axes rather than collapsed into a single notion of quality.

A.3. 泛化边界Generalization Boundary

本文中的方法应该超越目标设计层面的关注差距：效用约束的现实主义、内部现实主义与源保真度的分离、多面板评估和可审计的确定性修订是普遍的。切片感知致密化、死区患者回填、时间恢复和阴性对照应通过轻微适应转移到其他企业基准。相比之下，节奏规则、特定于主题的支持事实合成、描述重写模板、推荐恢复触发器和评估器语义仍然是特定于领域的。黄金注释集和操作参考队列同样是特定于基准的验证资产，而不是可重用的方法组件。The method in this paper should transfer beyond care gaps at the level of objective design: utility-constrained realism, separation of internal realism from source fidelity, multi-panel evaluation, and auditable deterministic revisions are general. Slice-aware densification, dead-zone patient backfilling, temporal restoration, and negative controls should transfer with light adaptation to other enterprise benchmarks. By contrast, cadence rules, topic-specific supporting-fact synthesis, description-rewrite templates, recommendation-recovery triggers, and evaluator semantics remain domain-specific. Gold annotation sets and operational reference cohorts are likewise benchmark-specific validation assets rather than reusable method components.
