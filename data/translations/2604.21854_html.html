I 简介Introduction

人工智能系统现在可以跨领域运行，这些领域曾经是人类判断的专属领域，从医疗诊断和司法决策到就业筛选和关键基础设施等领域。随着部署的扩大，监管野心也在扩大。世界各地的司法管辖区已经趋同于一个共同的模式：基于风险的监管，根据人工智能系统的潜在危害对人工智能系统进行分类，并对其开发者和部署者施加相应的义务。Artificial intelligence systems now operate across domains that were once the exclusive province of human judgment, in areas from medical diagnostics and judicial decision-making to employment screening and critical infrastructure. As deployment has expanded, so has regulatory ambition. Jurisdictions worldwide have converged on a common model: risk-based regulation that classifies AI systems by their potential for harm and imposes proportionate obligations on their developers and deployers.

这种趋同意义重大。欧盟人工智能法案This convergence is significant. The regulatory architecture is in place; the measuring instrument is missing.

II 监管格局The Regulatory Landscape

基于风险的监管现在是全球人工智能治理的主要方法。各司法管辖区都遵循监管义务应与潜在危害相称的原则，但它们在规范基础和执行架构方面存在很大差异。理解这些差异很重要，有一个核心原因：目前还没有主要框架以可衡量的方式明确说明“可接受的风险”意味着什么，也没有从技术上验证对这一阈值的遵守情况。Risk-based regulation is now the dominant approach to AI governance worldwide. Jurisdictions have converged on the principle that regulatory obligations should be proportionate to potential harm, but they differ substantially in their normative foundations and enforcement architectures. Understanding these differences is important for one central reason: no major framework has yet specified what “acceptable risk” means in measurable terms, nor how compliance with such a threshold can be technically verified.

It classifies systems into four tiers: prohibited practices, high risk, limited/transparency risk, and minimal risk. High-risk systems, covering domains such as employment, law enforcement, and judicial administration, must pass conformity assessments, implement human oversight, and maintain extensive technical documentation before market entry. This structural prerequisite presents an insurmountable barrier for the evaluation of proprietary, commercial models, which are overwhelmingly deployed as opaque, black-box systems.

Instead of seeking deterministic proofs, this approach evaluates whether a neural network complies with a predefined risk or safety threshold within a calculable confidence interval. Statistical verification offers two critical advantages: (一)(i) 它可以在不透明的黑盒架构上有效运行；和It operates effectively on opaque, black-box architectures; and (二)(ii) 如果使用适当的抽样方法，它可以扩展以评估现代、高度参数化的模型provided an appropriate sampling methodology is utilized, it scales to evaluate modern, highly parameterized models[26]. 然而，主要缺点是其概率性质所固有的：它缺乏精确的确定性精度，并且其可靠性从根本上取决于基础采样方法的质量和分布The primary drawback, however, is inherent to its probabilistic nature: it lacks exact deterministic precision, and its reliability is fundamentally dependent on the quality and distribution of the underlying sampling method[26].

三、III评估系统安全性：RoMA 和 gRoMA 的机制Evaluating Systemic Safety: The Mechanics of RoMA and gRoMA

为了实施监管合规性的统计验证，认证框架需要能够大规模评估模型而不需要白盒访问的工具。稳健性测量和评估 (RoMA) 方法及其全球扩展 gRoMA 提供了计算这些精确概率保证的具体机制To operationalize statistical verification for regulatory compliance, certification frameworks require tools capable of assessing models at scale without demanding white-box access. The Robustness Measurement and Assessment (RoMA) method, alongside its global extension gRoMA, provides a concrete mechanism for calculating these precise probabilistic assurances[26].

Ⅲ-AIII-A 通过 RoMA 实现本地稳健性Local Robustness via RoMA

RoMA 算法通过计算随机输入扰动导致明显错误分类的概率来评估深度神经网络的局部鲁棒性。这种方法与航空领域使用的容错范例直接一致，该范例将故障建模为自然、随机事件，而不是敌对恶意的产物The RoMA algorithm evaluates the local robustness of a deep neural network by calculating the probability that a random input perturbation will result in a distinct misclassification. This approach aligns directly with the fault-tolerance paradigms used in aviation, which model failures as natural, random occurrences rather than the product of adversarial malice[23].

RoMA 完全在黑盒架构上运行，不需要事先假设网络拓扑或内部激活函数。该方法通过以下结构化步骤进行：RoMA operates entirely on black-box architectures, requiring no prior assumptions regarding the network’s topology or internal activation functions. The methodology proceeds through the following structured steps:

  • •

    采样：算法在固定输入点周围生成指定数量的随机扰动样本，确保扰动不超过最大定义大小，表示为Sampling: The algorithm generates a specified number of randomly perturbed samples around a fixed input point, ensuring the perturbations do not exceed a maximum defined size, denoted asϵ\epsilon.

  • •

    数据提取：每个样本都通过神经网络检索其输出置信度向量，RoMA 从中提取“最高错误置信度”分数。Data Extraction: Each sample is passed through the neural network to retrieve its output confidence vector, from which RoMA extracts the “highest incorrect confidence” score.

  • •

    分布归一化：RoMA 使用拟合优度程序（例如 Anderson-Darling 检验）测试提取的分数Distribution Normalization: RoMA tests the extracted scores using a goodness-of-fit procedure, such as the Anderson-Darling test[1]，评估它们是否服从正态分布。如果数据分布异常，算法将应用 Box-Cox 幂变换, to evaluate if they follow a normal distribution. If the data is abnormally distributed, the algorithm applies a Box-Cox power transformation[2]标准化测量尺度。to normalize the measurement scale.

  • •

    概率评估：Probabilistic Assessment:归一化后，该算法使用标准 Z 分数计算和高斯累积分布函数来评估随机扰动穿过指定置信度阈值的确切概率，Once normalized, the algorithm uses standard Z-score calculations and the Gaussian cumulative distribution function to evaluate the exact probability that a random perturbation will cross a specified confidence threshold,δ\delta，导致对抗性失败, resulting in an adversarial failure[26].

值得注意的是，RoMA 的原始作者将该算法严格概念化为安全认证工具，与 DO-178 等航空标准直接相似。因此，该方法专门用于评估内部故障模式 - 源自模型自身架构和统计表示的错误。该框架并未声称可以量化外部伤害造成的漏洞，例如恶意网络安全漏洞或协调的对抗性攻击。这种经过深思熟虑的边界可确保生成的证书衡量系统的基线运行可靠性，而不是针对敌对外部参与者的弹性。It is important to note that the original authors of RoMA conceptualize the algorithm strictly as a safety certification tool, drawing direct parallels to aviation standards like DO-178. Consequently, the methodology is exclusively calibrated to evaluate internal failure modes-errors originating from the model’s own architecture and statistical representations. The framework makes no claim to quantify vulnerabilities caused by external harm, such as malicious cybersecurity exploits or coordinated adversarial attacks. This deliberate boundary ensures that the resulting certificate measures the system’s baseline operational reliability, rather than its resilience against hostile external actors.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 通过 gRoMA 扩展到全局类别稳健性Scaling to Global Categorial Robustness via gRoMA

虽然 RoMA 为各个数据点提供精确的统计保证，但系统级认证需要了解模型在整个操作域中的行为方式。 gRoMA（全局 RoMA）工具扩展了 RoMA 方法来测量神经网络的概率全局分类稳健性While RoMA provides exact statistical guarantees for individual data points, system-level certification requires an understanding of how a model behaves across its entire operational domain. The gRoMA (global RoMA) tool scales the RoMA methodology to measure the probabilistic global categorial robustness of a neural network[27].

gRoMA 没有假设整个模型具有统一的鲁棒性，而是将分析隔离到特定的输出类别，并承认网络可能会根据分类任务表现出不同级别的可靠性。该工具按如下方式执行此全局评估：Rather than assuming uniform robustness across a model, gRoMA isolates the analysis to specific output categories, acknowledging that a network may exhibit varying levels of reliability depending on the classification task. The tool executes this global assessment as follows:

  • •

    代表性选择：gRoMA 随机抽取一组随机有限输入，这些输入代表输入空间中感兴趣的特定输出类别。Representative Selection: gRoMA randomly draws a random finite set of inputs that represent a specific output category of interest from the input space.

  • •

    迭代局部评估：对于该抽取集中的每个正确分类的样本，该工具都会调用底层 RoMA 算法来计算单个概率局部鲁棒性得分。Iterative Local Assessment: For every correctly classified sample within this drawn set, the tool invokes the underlying RoMA algorithm to calculate an individual probabilistic local robustness score.

  • •

    统计聚合：各个分数通常通过数值平均值进行数学聚合，以建立该特定类别的综合全局稳健性分数。Statistical Aggregation: The individual scores are mathematically aggregated, commonly through a numerical average, to establish a comprehensive global robustness score for that specific category.

  • •

    错误界限：为了满足安全关键型部署的严格保证要求，gRoMA 利用 Hoeffding 不等式来计算形式错误界限Error Bounding: To meet the strict assurance requirements of safety-critical deployments, gRoMA utilizes Hoeffding’s inequality to compute a formal error bound[17]。这保证了估计的全局鲁棒性得分与真实总体值的偏差不超过严格控制的预定义边际。. This guarantees that the estimated global robustness score deviates from the true population value by no more than a tightly controlled, predefined margin.

通过量化局部扰动风险和全球特定类别的漏洞，这些工具提供了目前自上而下的人工智能治理框架中缺少的可测量阈值。By quantifying both local perturbation risks and global category-specific vulnerabilities, these tools provide the measurable thresholds currently missing from top-down AI governance frameworks.

Ⅲ-CIII-C RoMA 的实证验证Empirical Validation of RoMA

形式验证方法通过确定指定域内对抗性扰动的精确频率来提供最终的数学保证Formal verification methods offer the ultimate mathematical guarantee by determining the precise frequency of adversarial perturbations within a specified domain[21]。为了确定关键系统集成统计近似的可靠性，最近的实证工作直接对照这些数学上精确的基线验证了 RoMA 框架. To establish the reliability of statistical approximation for critical system integration, recent empirical work validated the RoMA framework directly against these mathematically exact baselines[25]。在这项比较研究中，研究人员使用精确计数算法建立了真实的鲁棒性概率。这种形式方法通过系统地、递归地将输入空间划分为确定的子区域来计算真实的违规率，从而允许精确计算遇到对抗性扰动的概率。. In this comparative study, researchers established ground-truth robustness probabilities using the Exact Count algorithm. This formal methodology computes the true violation rate by systematically and recursively partitioning the input space into definitive sub-regions, allowing for an exact calculation of the probability of encountering an adversarial perturbation.

由于这种详尽的空间划分会遭受指数级计算复杂性的影响，因此计算最终的地面实况严格限于小规模神经网络，例如航空防撞中使用的 300 神经元 ACAS Xu 模型。然而，统计方法不受这种架构瓶颈的限制，并且可以有效地扩展Because this exhaustive space-partitioning suffers from exponential computational complexity, calculating the definitive ground truth is strictly constrained to small-scale neural networks, such as the 300-neuron ACAS Xu models utilized in aviation collision avoidance. Statistical methodologies, however, are not constrained by this architectural bottleneck and can scale effectively[21]。当评估这些网络时，精确的形式计算仍然易于处理，结果证明了异常的一致性：RoMA 生成的统计概率与真实形式概率的偏差不到 1%。此外，虽然 Exact Count 算法需要数小时的计算，并且在稍大的基准测试中总是在 24 小时后超时，但统计框架在 16 分钟内提供了这些高度精确的估计。这种低于 1% 的误差范围证实统计验证可以提供关键任务领域所需的高度准确、定量的安全保证，有效地弥合了精确的形式证明与实际部署所需的实际可扩展性之间的差距。. When evaluating these networks where exact formal computation remains tractable, the results demonstrated exceptional alignment: the statistical probabilities generated by RoMA deviated from the true formal probabilities by less than 1%. Furthermore, while the Exact Count algorithm required hours of computation-and consistently timed out after 24 hours on slightly larger benchmarks — the statistical framework delivered these highly precise estimates in under 16 minutes. This sub-1% error margin confirms that statistical verification can provide the highly accurate, quantitative safety assurances demanded by mission-critical domains, effectively bridging the gap between exact formal proofs and the practical scalability required for real-world deployment.

Ⅲ-DIII-D 方法论局限性和分布假设Methodological Limitations and Distributional Assumptions

RoMA 方法的主要局限性源于其基本依赖于神经网络的亚军置信度分数在扰动下呈正态分布的假设。为了执行和验证这一关键的统计特性，该框架利用 Anderson-Darling 拟合优度检验，偶尔结合 Box-Cox 幂变换来标准化偏斜数据。然而，这种分布假设并未在所有操作域或输入模式中得到普遍满足。The primary limitation of the RoMA methodology stems from its foundational reliance on the assumption that a neural network’s runner-up confidence scores distribute normally under perturbation. To enforce and verify this critical statistical property, the framework utilizes the Anderson-Darling goodness-of-fit test, occasionally coupled with a Box-Cox power transformation to normalize skewed data. However, this distributional assumption is not universally satisfied across all operational domains or input modalities.

例如，在评估拼写扰动下的大型语言模型时（例如在句子中随机替换单个字符以模拟人类印刷错误），所得的置信分布经常无法通过 Anderson-Darling 测试，甚至在转换后也是如此。在数据分布不正常的情况下，该方法的正式统计保证就会受到损害For example, when evaluating large language models under orthographic perturbations-such as substituting single characters randomly within a sentence to simulate human typographical errors, the resulting confidence distributions frequently fail the Anderson-Darling test, even post-transformation. In instances where the data does not distribute normally, the formal statistical guarantees of the method are compromised[25]。有趣的是，实证评估表明，即使违反正态性假设，该框架仍然可以产生高度准确的估计。在一项详尽的蛮力评估中，将 RoMA 对字符级噪声的估计与真实、准确的分布进行比较，统计鲁棒性概率与地面实况分数的偏差仅为 0.17%（相对于地面实况的 94.61% 和 93.84%，分别产生 94.44% 和 93.94% 的估计）. Interestingly, empirical evaluations demonstrate that the framework can still yield highly accurate estimates even when normality assumptions are violated. In an exhaustive brute-force evaluation comparing RoMA’s estimations of character-level noise against the true, exact distribution, the statistical robustness probabilities deviated from the ground-truth scores by merely 0.17% (yielding estimates of 94.44% and 93.94% against ground truths of 94.61% and 93.84%, respectively)[25].

尽管这证明了经验的准确性，但缺乏经过验证的正态分布意味着正式的安全保证无法得到明确的监管合规性认证。为了减轻安全关键环境中的这种限制，另一种策略是重新定义扰动边界，以找到输出成功标准化的紧密相邻或“封闭”用例。通过改变对抗性输入空间的定义——例如，从字符级噪声过渡到在绝大多数情况下保持正态性的语义嵌入替换——实践者可以恢复所需的分布属性，并重新建立系统认证所需的正式统计界限。Despite this demonstrated empirical accuracy, the absence of a verified normal distribution means that formal safety assurances cannot be definitively certified for regulatory compliance. To mitigate this limitation in safety-critical contexts, an alternative strategy is to redefine the perturbation boundaries to find a closely adjacent, or “closed” use case where the outputs do successfully normalize. By shifting the definition of the adversarial input space-for example, transitioning from character-level noise to semantic embedding substitutions where normality is maintained in the vast majority of cases-practitioners can recover the required distributional properties and re-establish the formal statistical bounds necessary for system certification.

IV-B 第一阶段：规范性确定Stage One: The Normative Determination

在进行任何技术验证之前，主管当局必须确定两个参数。首先是可接受的故障概率Before any technical verification can proceed, the competent authority must fix two parameters.
