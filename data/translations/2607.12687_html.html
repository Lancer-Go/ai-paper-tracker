1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展表明，它们不仅是强大的语言生成器，而且在医疗保健等跨领域的定量预测任务中也表现出色Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have shown that they are not only powerful language generators, but also excel in quantitative prediction tasks across domains such as healthcare(Hua et al., 2024), 金融, finance(Vedula et al., 2025)，以及科学分析, and scientific analysis(Lewkowycz et al., 2022)。然而，法学硕士仍然容易出现幻觉和过度自信的错误. However, LLMs remain susceptible to hallucinations and overconfident errors(Achiam et al., 2023)，这一限制在医疗保健和法律等高风险领域尤其令人担忧, a limitation that is especially concerning in high-stakes domains such as healthcare and law(Dahl et al., 2024; Omar et al., 2025)。例如，法学硕士有望根据膳食描述准确估计碳水化合物. For instance, LLMs show promise for accurate carbohydrate estimation from meal descriptions(Hua et al., 2024)，这是决定糖尿病患者胰岛素剂量的关键任务，过度自信但不正确的碳水化合物估计可能会导致严重的血糖后果。因此，在实践中部署此类模型需要的不仅仅是准确的点估计——安全关键环境中的用户不仅需要知道, a task critical for deciding insulin dosage in patients with diabetes, where overconfident but incorrect carbohydrate estimates can lead to severe glycemic consequences. Deploying such models in practice therefore requires more than accurate point estimates — users in safety-critical settings need to know not only什么what该模型预测，而且the model predicts, but also何时信任when to trust该预测以便人类在必要时进行干预that prediction in order for humans to intervene when necessary(Kalai et al., 2025; Tao et al., 2024).

虽然越来越多的工作研究法学硕士在分类和开放式生成任务中的置信度估计While a growing body of work studies confidence estimation for LLMs in classification and open-ended generation tasks(Geng et al., 2024)，对于基于语言的环境中的数值预测，它在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索，现有的工作主要集中于产生准确的点估计，而不考虑可靠的置信信号, it remains largely underexplored for numerical prediction in language-based settings, where existing work predominantly focuses on producing accurate point estimates without considering reliable confidence signals(Tang et al., 2024; Jacobs et al., 2024; Zausinger et al., 2024; Vacareanu et al., 2024)。为了解决这一差距，我们研究了基于语言的定量预测和置信度估计，其中法学硕士通过自然语言推理生成数值估计，并附有反映预测可靠性的置信度信号。. To address this gap, we study language-based quantitative prediction with confidence estimation, where an LLM generates numerical estimates through natural language reasoning, accompanied by confidence signals that reflect prediction reliability.

2 相关工作Related Work

基于语言的定量预测法学硕士。LLMs for language-based quantitative prediction.

先前将 LLM 应用于基于语言的定量预测的工作主要遵循三个方向：（i）使用 LLM 派生的表示作为下游模型的固定输入特征Prior work on applying LLMs to language-based quantitative prediction has primarily followed three directions: (i) using LLM-derived representations as fixed input features for downstream models(Imperial, 2021; Tang et al., 2024)，（ii）通过针对特定任务的微调来调整法学硕士，以直接产生数值预测, (ii) adapting LLMs through task-specific fine-tuning to directly produce numerical predictions(Jacobs et al., 2024; Zausinger et al., 2024)，以及（iii）采用上下文学习根据自然语言提示执行零样本或少样本数值估计, and (iii) employing in-context learning to perform zero-shot or few-shot numerical estimation from natural-language prompts(Vacareanu et al., 2024; Gopali et al., 2025)。虽然基于特征的方法可能很有效，但它们主要将法学硕士视为编码器，因此不会利用其生成界面进行交互使用或自然语言解释。同时，微调和上下文学习都强调点估计而不是丰富的语言生成。在这项研究中，我们重点关注对法学硕士进行基于语言的定量预测的微调，以保留其生成和对话能力以实现透明度和交互。. While feature-based approaches can be effective, they treat the LLM primarily as an encoder and thus do not leverage its generative interface for interactive use or natural-language explanations. Meanwhile, both fine-tuning and in-context learning emphasize point estimates rather than rich language generation. In this study, we instead focus on fine-tuning LLMs for language-based quantitative prediction in a way that preserves their generative and conversational capabilities for transparency and interaction.

5.2.3 CARE-PPO 保留任务感知行为CARE-PPO Preserves Task-Aware Behavior

在实践中，用户在开放式设置中与 LLM 进行交互，并且可能会发出部署用例之外的查询，因此即使在专门化为定量预测任务之后，保留通用行为也很重要。例如，针对营养估计进行微调的模型应该识别何时查询与食物无关并做出适当的响应，而不是尝试估计碳水化合物。因此，我们评估 CARE-PPO 是否在训练分布之外的通用查询上表现出更多的任务感知行为，特别是研究模型是否错误地对不相关的输入强制进行特定于任务的预测。In practice, users interact with LLMs in open-ended settings and may issue queries beyond the deployment use case, making it important to preserve general-purpose behavior even after specialization to a quantitative prediction task. 6 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced护理-PPOCARE-PPO，一种基于语言的定量预测的强化学习框架，它在不确定性估计的损失预测和行动者批评家强化学习微调之间建立了一种新颖的联系。通过将奖励定义为预测误差的函数，, a reinforcement learning framework for language-based quantitative prediction that draws a novel connection between loss prediction for uncertainty estimation and actor–critic RL fine-tuning. By defining rewards as functions of prediction error,护理-PPOCARE-PPO使批评者能够学习与预测质量一致的价值函数，从而使批评者能够在推理时充当有效的置信估计器。在医疗保健和金融领域的定量预测任务中，enables the critic to learn value functions aligned with prediction quality, allowing the critic to serve as an effective confidence estimator at inference time. Across quantitative prediction tasks in healthcare and finance,护理-PPOCARE-PPO相对于语言化和基于逻辑的置信度估计方法，实现了有竞争力的预测准确性，同时显着提高了置信度对齐。我们进一步表明，这些收益在现实的分布变化下仍然保持强劲，并扩展到任务外查询的任务感知行为的改进。achieves competitive prediction accuracy while substantially improving confidence alignment relative to verbalized and logit-based confidence estimation methods. We further showed that these gains remain robust under realistic distribution shifts and extend to improved task-aware behavior on out-of-task queries.

我们相信基于置信感知语言的定量预测代表了一个有前途的研究方向。同时We believe confidence-aware language-based quantitative prediction represents a promising research direction. While护理-PPOCARE-PPO依赖于 PPO 微调，置信度估计作为行动者批评家训练的副产品出现，而不需要单独的置信度学习阶段、校准程序或辅助模型。延迟分析表明，基于批评家的置信度估计仅引入很小的推理开销（附录relies on PPO fine-tuning, confidence estimation emerges as a byproduct of actor–critic training rather than requiring a separate confidence-learning stage, calibration procedure, or auxiliary model. Latency analysis shows that critic-based confidence estimation introduces only a small inference overhead (AppendixE），表明它可以以最小的额外成本纳入部署。由于计算限制，我们的实验目前仅限于最多 8B 个参数的模型和两个应用领域。未来的工作将探索将这些结果扩展到更大的语言模型和更广泛的定量预测设置，研究奖励设计和校准之间的理论联系，并将置信度估计更直接地集成到生成过程中，以更紧密地耦合预测和不确定性估计。), suggesting that it can be incorporated into deployment with minimal additional cost. Our experiments are currently limited to models up to 8B parameters and two application domains due to computational constraints. Future work will explore scaling these results to larger language models and broader quantitative prediction settings, investigating theoretical connections between reward design and calibration, and integrating confidence estimates more directly into the generation process to more tightly couple prediction and uncertainty estimation.

附录EAppendix E推理延迟分析Inference Latency Analysis

我们评估基于批评家的置信度估计引入的推理开销。使用从营养估计测试集中随机抽取的 100 个示例，我们测量了演员生成和评论家置信度估计的端到端延迟。平均而言，参与者每个样本需要 3108.2 毫秒，而批评者仅需要 74.0 毫秒，相当于总推理延迟增加了 2.4%。之所以会产生如此小的开销，是因为批评者仅对已完成的响应执行一次前向传递，而行动者则需要与输出长度成比例的多个自回归解码步骤。这些结果表明，基于批评家的置信度估计可以以最小的额外推理成本纳入部署中。We evaluate the inference overhead introduced by critic-based confidence estimation. Using 100 randomly sampled examples from the nutrition estimation test set, we measure end-to-end latency for both actor generation and critic confidence estimation. On average, the actor requires 3108.2 ms per sample, while the critic requires only 74.0 ms, corresponding to a 2.4% increase in total inference latency. This small overhead arises because the critic performs only a single forward pass on the completed response, whereas the actor requires multiple autoregressive decoding steps proportional to the output length. These results suggest that critic-based confidence estimation can be incorporated into deployment with minimal additional inference cost.
