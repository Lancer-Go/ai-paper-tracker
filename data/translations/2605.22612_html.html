2 需要明确的假设来缩小评估与部署之间的差距Explicit Assumptions are Needed to Close the Evaluation–Deployment Gap

机器学习中的评估用于量化单个模型的性能并比较模型之间的性能Evaluation in machine learning serves to quantify the performance of individual models and to compare performance across models(Hardt, 2025)。 虽然这种范式对于实现现代系统的功能发挥了重要作用，但它足以评估部署准备情况并不是不言而喻的。 在医疗保健等高风险领域，评估必须严格考虑不确定性和可重复性，以减轻意外的副作用。 如果没有此类保障措施，缺陷通常只会在部署期间或之后才会显现出来，并可能带来严重后果. While this paradigm has been instrumental in enabling the capabilities of modern systems, it is not self-evident that it suffices to assess deployment readiness. In high-stakes domains such as healthcare, evaluations must rigorously account for uncertainty and reproducibility to mitigate unintended side effects. Without such safeguards, shortcomings often surface only during or after deployment, with potentially severe consequences(Liao et al., 2021)。 评估不充分导致临床试验中产生有害结果，导致人工智能界尚未制定严格的评估标准. Inadequate evaluation has led to harmful outcomes in clinical trials, leading to the establishment of stringent evaluation standards that the AI community has yet to match(Lowe, 2020; Martin and Shenfield, 2016; Kaltenboeck et al., 2021; Vargesson, 2015).

至关重要的是，在医疗保健法学硕士文献中，越来越多的证据记录了法学硕士在基准评估中的表现与实践中的表现之间存在系统性差距Critically, in the healthcare LLM literature there is a growing body of evidence documenting a systematic gap between how LLMs perform in benchmark evaluation and how they perform in practice(Bean et al., 2026; Hager et al., 2024; Abaluck et al., 2026; Tiller et al., 2026)。 我们认为，这种差距不仅仅是模型能力不足或基准设计不当的结果，而是隐含在评估协议中并在部署时无意中违反的假设的结果。 这些假设涉及任务的结构、谁与模型交互以及模型的输出如何转化为现实世界的决策。 当此类假设被隐含时，强大的基准性能可能与较弱的部署性能共存，并且从业者没有系统的方法来预测或缓解这种情况。 从结构上讲，评估和部署之间的差距可能是由于各种因素造成的，包括现实世界和受控环境之间的分布变化、用户与工具交互方式的差异，以及基准评估通常针对代理结果进行优化时的目标差异。 为了使这一点具体化，我们研究了医疗保健文献中的三项代表性研究，每项研究都说明了隐含假设驱动评估部署差距的独特方式。. We argue that this gap is not merely a consequence of insufficient model capabilities or poorly designed benchmarks, but of assumptions that are implicitly embedded in evaluation protocols and inadvertently violated at deployment time. These assumptions concern how tasks are structured, who interacts with the model, and how its outputs translate into real-world decisions. When such assumptions are left implicit, strong benchmark performance can coexist with weak deployment performance, and practitioners have no systematic way to anticipate or mitigate this. Structurally, a gap between evaluation and deployment can arise due to various factors, including a distribution shift between real-world and controlled environments, differences in how users interact with the tool, and differences in objectives as benchmark evaluation typically optimizes for proxy outcomes. 另一种观点 1：Alternate Viewpoint 1:测试假设和进行敏感性分析的成本太高，无法实际执行。Testing assumptions and conducting sensitivity analyses is too costly of a procedure to be done practically.

反驳：Rebuttal:通过敏感性分析测试任务假设只需要部署上下文中的几个样本，而不可测试的假设可以根据领域知识进行推理。 因此，成本是有限的且是预先加载的。 经过验证的假设可以降低评估成本，因为如果假设成立，则更简单的协议就足够了，而未经验证的假设则精确地指出了评估所需的确切协议。Testing task assumptions via sensitivity analyses requires only a few samples from the deployment context, while non-testable assumptions can be reasoned based on domain knowledge. As a result, the cost is bounded and front-loaded. Verified assumptions reduce evaluation costs because if assumptions hold, simpler protocols suffice, while non-verified assumptions pinpoint the exact protocols necessary for evaluation.

另一种观点 2：Alternate Viewpoint 2:过去几十年来，机器学习的成功很大程度上得益于基准测试。为什么医疗保健应该有所不同？Benchmarks have been responsible for much of the success in machine learning in the past few decades; why should healthcare be any different?

反驳：Rebuttal:在编码等沙盒领域中，评估代表部署。 然而，评估不能在医疗保健中进行沙盒化，因为它依赖于患者的行为，而患者的行为即使不是不可能，也很难完全通过基准来捕获。 相反，基准代表某一时间点的静态快照，而医生和患者的互动在时间和地点上是不同的。 明确假设的框架使我们能够以静态基准无法捕获的原则性方式动态分析评估和部署之间的这种偏差。In sandboxable domains such as coding, evaluations are representative of deployment. However, evaluation cannot be sandboxed in healthcare because it relies on patient behavior which is difficult, if not impossible, to capture entirely through benchmarks. Instead, benchmarks represent a static snapshot at one point in time, while physician and patient interactions are heterogeneous in time and location. Frameworks that make assumptions explicit allow us to dynamically analyze this drift between evaluation and deployment in a principled manner that static benchmarks could not capture.

另一种观点 3：Alternate Viewpoint 3:明确假设只会将实践者已经显而易见的知识编码化。Making assumptions explicit only codifies knowledge that is already obvious to practitioners.

反驳：Rebuttal:由于现代法学硕士的先进能力，来自不同背景的从业者经常使用相同的模型来完成根本不同的任务。这些差异意味着不同的部署环境。鉴于这种多样性，假设从业者拥有相同的先入之见是不合理的，因为这些先入之见将导致解释基准和弥合培训部署差距所需的同一组隐含假设。 此外，敏感性分析正式量化了哪些假设是最重要的，这是从业者无法从隐性知识中确定的。 例如，虽然从业者可能知道患者查询与基准中的查询不同，但尚不清楚该假设是否比单轮和多轮交互之间的差异更重要。 了解假设的相对重要性可以围绕最重要的假设设计评估协议，以确保评估能够代表部署。Due to the advanced capabilities of modern LLMs, practitioners from diverse backgrounds often use the same model for fundamentally different tasks. These differences imply distinct deployment contexts. Given this diversity, it is not reasonable to assume that practitioners share the same preconceptions that will lead towards the same set of implicit assumptions required to interpret benchmarks and bridge the training–deployment gap. Further, sensitivity analysis formally quantifies which assumptions are most important, something which practitioners cannot determine from implicit knowledge. For example, while practitioners might know that patient queries are different from those in a benchmark, what is not clear is whether that assumption is more important than the difference between single-turn and multi-turn interactions. Understanding the relative importance of assumptions allows for the design of evaluation protocols around the most important assumptions to ensure that evaluations are representative of deployment.

另一种观点 4：Alternate Viewpoint 4:明确假设的评估会造成额外负担，而监管效益却微乎其微。Evaluations that make assumptions explicit create additional burden with little regulatory benefit.

反驳：Rebuttal:BenchmarkCards 等框架描述了何时应该使用基准、何时不应使用基准，以及基准在什么情况下仍然有效。 这为监管机构提供了支持，因为它可以诚实地评估责任所在。 此外，分阶段评估自然可以与临床试验文献相关，其中评估是按照复杂性和规模递增的顺序进行的连续试验。 该文献中也有类似使用代理和简化假设的方法，因为替代终点可以在经过验证且评估真实结果需要太长时间或成本太高的情况下使用Frameworks such as BenchmarkCards delineate when benchmarks should and should not be used, and the circumstances under which benchmarks remain valid. This supports regulatory bodies as it allows for an honest assessment of where liability should be. Moreover, staged evaluation can naturally be related to the clinical trials literature, where evaluation occurs with sequential trials in increasing order of complexity and size. There are also analogs to the use of proxies and simplifying assumptions in that literature, as surrogate endpoints can be used in situations where they are validated and assessing the true outcome would take too long or be too costly(Katz, 2004)。 当追求最大效率的试验设计效率太低时，监管机构在平衡效率和有效性方面表现出了灵活性。 同样，分阶段评估并不要求所有假设都得到充分考虑，而是未经验证的假设不会显着造成评估与部署之间的差距。. Regulatory bodies have shown flexibility in balancing efficiency and validity when pursuing a maximally efficient trial design is too inefficient. Similarly, staged evaluation does not require that all assumptions are fully addressed, but rather that non-verified assumptions do not significantly create an evaluation–deployment gap.

另一种观点 5：Alternate Viewpoint 5:LLM 能力最终将与医疗保健部署相匹配，从而呈现使假设明显不必要的框架。LLM capabilities will eventually match healthcare deployments, rendering frameworks that make assumptions explicit unnecessary.

反驳：Rebuttal:无论模型能力如何，在医疗保健等高风险领域都需要进行适当的评估。 目前的评估程序与现实相差太远，无法保证安全部署；基准性能并不等于实际性能。 相反，明确的假设框架打破了评估和部署之间的差距，因此可以迭代地测试模型，使其更接近真实的临床环境。Regardless of model capability, proper evaluation is necessary in high-stakes domains such as healthcare. Current evaluation procedures are too far removed from reality to guarantee safe deployment; performance on benchmarks does not equate with performance in reality. Instead, assumptions-explicit frameworks break down the gaps between evaluation and deployment so models can iteratively be tested closer to real clinical settings.

7 局限性和结论Limitations and Conclusion

局限性。Limitations.

我们的敏感性分析是通过一篇特定的论文完成的；这样做的目的不是要明确描述不同假设的重要性，而是说明敏感性分析可能是什么样子。 基准卡已被提议但未经验证； BenchmarkCards 对部署有何影响仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。 最后，我们的工作将任务与结果呈现为二元决策，而实际上不同的假设沿范围不断变化。 例如，某些假设可能需要结果和对话数据，而其他结果即使可以访问结果数据也可能无法测试。 此外，某些结果可能只能在很长的时间内进行测试，因此有必要找到合理的代理。Our sensitivity analysis is done with one particular paper; the purpose of this is not as a definitive characterization of the importance of different assumptions, but rather an illustration of what sensitivity analysis might look like. BenchmarkCards are proposed but not validated; it remains an open question to see what impact BenchmarkCards will have on deployment. Finally, our work presents task vs. outcome as a binary decision when in reality different assumptions vary continuously along a spectrum. For example, some assumptions might require both outcome and conversational data, and other outcomes might not be testable even with access to outcome data. Moreover, some outcomes might only be testable over long timescales, making it necessary to find reasonable proxies.

医疗保健之外的扩展。Extensions Beyond Healthcare.

我们的立场侧重于设计医疗保健，但许多相同的想法适用于部署成本高昂且与评估无关的任何领域。 我们的假设很大程度上是由医疗保健推动的，但看看需要进行哪些类型的修改才能扩展到金融等领域，这将是很有趣的Our position focuses on healthcare by design, but many of the same ideas are applicable to any field where deployment is costly and detached from evaluation. Our assumptions are largely motivated by healthcare, but it would be of interest to see what types of modifications are needed for extensions to fields such as finance(Li et al., 2023)和法律and law(Dehghani et al., 2025)。 这些领域的相似之处在于，决策不能被沙箱化，并且必须与真实的人或市场互动。 因此，有必要使用类似的假设测试和分阶段评估框架来系统地了解绩效。. Such fields are similar in that decisions cannot be sandboxed and necessarily interact with real people or markets. Therefore, similar frameworks of assumption testing and staged evaluation are necessary to understand performance in a methodical manner.

结论。Conclusion.

对于在医疗保健领域部署法学硕士来说，更好的基准是必要的，但还不够。 我们的立场是，缩小评估和部署之间的差距需要明确区分两者的假设。 我们建议将假设分为两类：任务和结果，它们的不同之处在于是否可以纯粹通过对话进行测试。 对现实世界随机对照试验的案例研究表明，结果假设约占评估和部署之间差距的一半，这意味着精心设计的基准无法完全缩小差距。 相反，需要现实世界的行为测试和随机对照试验来了解结果假设是否成立。 为了改进评估，我们首先提出基准卡以更好地记录假设，然后我们提出分阶段评估作为迭代测试假设并相应更新评估的程序。 医疗保健领域的评估存在透明度问题，这一问题可以通过明确区分评估和部署的假设来解决。Better benchmarks are necessary but insufficient for deploying LLMs in healthcare. Our position is that closing the gap between evaluation and deployment requires making explicit the assumptions separating the two. We propose classifying assumptions into two categories: task and outcome, which differ based on whether they can be tested purely from conversations. A case study on a real-world RCT reveals that outcome assumptions account for around half the gap between evaluation and deployment, implying that well-designed benchmarks cannot fully close the gap. Instead, real-world behavioral tests and RCTs are needed to understand whether outcome assumptions hold. To improve evaluations, we first propose BenchmarkCards for better documenting assumptions, and we second propose staged evaluation as a procedure to iteratively test assumptions and update evaluations accordingly. Evaluation in healthcare suffers from a transparency problem that is solvable by making explicit the assumptions that separate evaluation and deployment.
