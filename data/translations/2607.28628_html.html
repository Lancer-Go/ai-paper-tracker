摘要Abstract

对偶性在建立广泛的物理系统中的微观现象和涌现现象方面发挥着重要作用。然而，在实践中，即使所有“游戏规则”都是众所周知的，确定两个系统何时是对偶的，在计算上通常也具有挑战性。换句话说，当面对两个系统时，如何有效地确定它们实际上是对偶的？在本文中，我们使用机器学习方法来解决超对称箭袋规范理论的塞伯格对偶性问题。从数学上讲，这涉及建立箭袋的突变，这又是“学习解开”主题的变体。一方面，这为我们提供了一种用于确定不同对偶性的计算复杂性的实用工具。另一方面，它也让我们能够研究不同的网络架构如何学习如何追踪塞伯格对偶性。我们发现，对于具有适度数量的箭袋节点（10 阶）的箭袋，由变压器和多层感知器组成的不同网络架构往往优于确定性算法。通过完善的探路算法（本质上是“箭袋谷歌地图”）补充网络可以进一步提高搜索策略的效率和准确性。我们预计此类问题可以作为应用于理论物理的前沿人工智能模型的有用基准。Dualities play an important role in establishing both microscopic and emergent phenomena in a wide range of physical systems. In practice, though, it can often be computationally challenging to establish when two systems are dual, even when all of the “rules of the game” are well-known. Said differently, when confronted with two systems, how can one efficiently establish that they are in fact dual? In this paper we use machine learning methods to address this question for Seiberg dualities of supersymmetric quiver gauge theories. Mathematically, this involves establishing mutations of quivers, which is in turn a variation on the theme of “learning to unknot”. On the one hand, this leads us to a practical tool for establishing the computational complexity of different dualities. On the other hand, it also allows us to study how different network architectures learn how to trace Seiberg dualities. We find that for quivers with a modest number of quiver nodes (of order 10), different network architectures consisting of transformers and multi-layer perceptrons tend to outperform deterministic algorithms. Supplementing the network by well-established pathfinder algorithms (essentially “Google Maps for quivers”) leads to an additional improvement in the efficiency and accuracy of the search strategy. We anticipate that this class of questions can serve as a useful benchmark for frontier AI models applied to theoretical physics.

1 简介1 Introduction

对偶性在广泛的物理系统中提供了重要的互补视角。著名的例子包括二维伊辛模型的克莱默-万尼尔对偶性、麦克斯韦理论的电磁对偶性，以及从相互作用量子场论到弦/M/F理论到全息术的许多现代例子。Dualities provide important complementary perspectives in a wide range of physical systems. Celebrated examples include the Kramers–Wannier duality of the 2D Ising model, the electricmagnetic duality of Maxwell theory, and many modern examples ranging from interacting quantum field theories to string / M- / F-theory to holography.1事实上，对偶性为了解许多强耦合物理系统的结构提供了一个窗口。Indeed, dualities provide a window into the structure of many physical systems at strong coupling.

更准确地说，“二元性”通常可以连接两个以上的理论表述。事实上，在许多众所周知的例子中，连续的二元性导致了不同可能描述的疯狂扩散。反过来看，如果一个人面对两个理论，如何确定这对理论通过一连串的对偶链相互连接，如果是这样，需要多少个简单的对偶运算才能连接这对理论？More precisely, a “duality” can often connect more than two presentations of a theory. Indeed, in many well-known examples, successive dualities lead to a wild proliferation of different possible descriptions. Turning the question around, if one is confronted with two theories, how does one establish that the pair is connected to one another via a chain of dualities, and if so, how many simple duality operations does it take to connect the pair?

在本文中，我们研究了应用于箭袋规范理论的塞伯格对偶性 [1] 的问题，即 [2, 3] 意义上的箭袋突变。在超对称 QCD 的原始例子中，这种对偶性将“电”非交换规范理论与“磁”对偶非交换规范理论联系起来。这可以扩展到具有多个简单规范组因子的各种超对称规范理论，其中对偶性的基本操作涉及一次作用于一个规范组因子。此类的一个著名例子是参考文献[4]的二元级联，其中有一对规范组在双基本表示中共享物质。这个例子在许多弦理论应用中也很突出，因为它表征了锥折指定的扭曲喉部附近的分数膜[4-6]。从物理上讲，这些示例中的仪表耦合流程决定了对偶性如何进行（即哪个仪表组正在运行强耦合）。In this paper, we study this question for Seiberg dualities [1] applied to quiver gauge theories, i.e., mutations of quivers in the sense of [2, 3]. In the original examples of supersymmetric QCD, this duality relates an “electric” non-abelian gauge theory with a “magnetic” dual non-abelian gauge theory. This can be extended to a wide range of supersymmetric gauge theories with multiple simple gauge group factors, where the basic operation of duality involves acting on one gauge group factor at a time. A celebrated example of precisely this sort is the duality cascade of reference [4], in which there is a pair of gauge groups that share matter in bifundamental representations. This example also prominently features in many string theory applications since it characterizes fractional branes near the warped throat spec– ified by the conifold [4 6]. Physically, the flow of gauge couplings in these examples dictates how duality proceeds (i.e., which gauge group is running to strong coupling).

然而，在许多情况下，二元性的结构要复杂得多，并且根据箭袋的参数，通常可能有多个规定的答案。例如，[7]表明，更普遍的情况是存在无限的对偶理论族。特定的轨迹还对规范耦合的初始条件表现出高度的敏感性，从而导致可能的对偶序列的巨大混沌范围。In many cases, however, the structure of dualities is far more involved, and there can often be more than one prescribed answer depending on the parameters of the quiver. For example, [7] showed that the far more generic situation is that there is an infinite family of dual theories. The specific trajectory also exhibits a high level of sensitivity to the initial conditions of gauge couplings, resulting in a huge chaotic range of possible duality sequences.

从这个角度来看，已经存在一个实际问题：给定两个超对称箭袋规范理论，它们实际上是否通过一系列塞伯格对偶性相关？如果是这样，可用于实现这些转换的最有效/最佳的简单对偶操作是什么？从物理角度来看，这有助于确定达到箭袋规范理论双阶段的计算复杂性水平。从全息的角度来看，这相当于了解给定的流在喉咙中进入主体多远。From this perspective, there is already a practical question: Given two supersymmetric quiver gauge theories, are they in fact related via a sequence of Seiberg dualities? If so, what are the most efficient/optimal simple duality operations available to realize these transformations? In physical terms, this helps to establish the level of computational complexity in reaching dual phases of quiver gauge theories. From a holographic standpoint, this amounts to understanding how far into the bulk a given flow proceeds down a throat.2

On the other hand, there is also the question of performance, namely how efficient and accurate different approaches are in establishing a path to dualization. Indeed, while we focus our analysis on Seiberg dualities in quiver gauge theories, the same sort of combinatorial methods shows up in a broad class of other computational problems, ranging, e.g., from the mathematics of cluster algebras [2, 3] to scattering amplitudes [17]. As such, we anticipate that the analysis we provide will have far broader applications.
