对随机机进行分组Grouping the Stochastic Machine
 人工智能系统的前沿指标是精度，而不是能力Precision, Not Capability, as the Frontier Metric for AI Systems

George Andrikopoulos Affiliation: Independent researcher, London, United Kingdom Affiliation: https://github.com/george-andrikopoulos
2026 年 8 月August 2026
摘要Abstract

前沿语言模型的能力进行比较、营销和基准测试——它们的最佳或平均输出可以达到什么水平。我认为这测量了错误的轴。模型已经饱和Frontier language models are compared, marketed, and benchmarked on capability — what their best or average output can achieve. I argue this measures the wrong axis. The models have saturated准确度accuracy：他们的平均输出达到目标。现在，在实践中将一个系统与另一个系统区分开来的因素以及决定系统是否可用于工程工作的因素是: their mean output lands on the target. What now separates one system from another in practice — and what determines whether a system is usable for engineering work — is精度precision：在重复、相同的请求中，他们的产出围绕该目标的集中程度如何。借用射手的特点，能力就是平均射击的命中率。可靠性是群体的规模。我提出三点主张。首先，精度，而不是能力，是系统（Devin、Claude、Grok 及其后继者）之间的前沿区分因素，而行业的基准文化系统性地无法衡量它，因为基准报告的是集中趋势，而不是扩散。其次，通过运行一套固定的系统，可以廉价且无循环地测量精度。: how tightly concentrated their outputs are around that target across repeated, identical requests. Borrowing the marksman’s distinction, capability is where the average shot lands; reliability is the size of the group. I make three claims. First, precision, not capability, is the frontier differentiator between systems (Devin, Claude, Grok, and their successors), and the industry’s benchmark culture systematically fails to measure it because benchmarks report central tendency, not spread. Second, precision is measurable, cheaply and without circularity, by running a fixed suite of确定性评分deterministically scored在固定温度下多次执行任务并计算每个任务结果的一致性 - 不需要模型在环分级机。第三，测量不仅是描述性的，而且是决策指导性的：它将tasks many times at fixed temperature and computing the per-task consistency of outcomes — no model-in-the-loop grader required. Third, the measurement is not merely descriptive but decision-guiding: it separates一致的consistent故障（紧密组偏离中心，可通过论文 1 的操作规程纠正——瞄准调整）failures (a tight group off-centre, correctable by the operating discipline of Paper 1 — a sight adjustment) from分散的scattered失败（一个广泛的群体，只能通过改变模型或其采样来纠正——步枪问题）。我定义了一个分组指标，指定了一个重用现有挑战试验基础设施的工具，并展示了随着时间的推移跟踪人类与人工智能对的分组如何产生论文 1 实地研究所需的复合信号。自复制以来的第一次实际运行说明了该方法及其最重要的限制：一个测量的差距完全由单个规则（0/5 → 5/5）关闭，而一组任务failures (a wide group, correctable only by changing the model or its sampling — a rifle problem). I define a grouping metric, specify a harness that reuses existing challenge-trial infrastructure, and show how tracking a human-AI pair’s grouping over time yields the compounding signal that Paper 1’s field study requires. A first real run, since replicated, illustrates both the method and its most important limit: one measured gap was closed completely by a single rule (0/5 → 5/5), while a suite of tasks根据规则本身编写authored from the rules themselves没有发现任何价值，因为前沿模型已经体现了明确的良好实践——确定一门学科的价值是通过对实际工作的衡量来发现的，而不是根据它自己的规则手册构建的。found no value, because a frontier model already embodies explicit good practice — establishing that a discipline’s worth is found by measurement on real work, not constructed from its own rulebook.

1  轴错误1  The wrong axis

如果问哪个模型是“最好的”，答案就会以能力的语言给出：基准分数、推理深度、上下文长度、任何人都从中得到的最佳输出的令人印象深刻的结果。这种语言描述了平均击球的落地位置。它对每个从业者实际上都面临的问题保持沉默：Ask which model is “best” and the answer arrives in the language of capability: benchmark scores, reasoning depth, context length, the impressiveness of the best output anyone has coaxed out of it. This language describes where the average shot lands. It is silent on the question every practitioner actually lives with:下次我问时，它会以同样的方式再次这样做吗？will it do this again, the same way, the next time I ask?

这两个问题是射手的两个轴，而且是独立的。The two questions are the marksman’s two axes, and they are independent.准确度Accuracy是均值与目标的接近程度——偏差。is closeness of the mean to the target — bias.精度Precision是分组的紧密程度——方差。步枪可以是精确的和不精确的（射击集中在靶心但广泛分散），也可以是精确的和不精确的（在角落里紧密聚集）。它们不是相同的财产，它们的衡量方式也不同，并且——本文的要点——它们在边界上以这种方式出现了分歧。领先的模型已经在准确性上趋于一致：对于明确指定的工程任务，is the tightness of the grouping — variance. A rifle can be accurate and imprecise (shots centred on the bullseye but widely scattered) or precise and inaccurate (a tight cluster in the corner). They are not the same property, they are not measured the same way, and — the point of this paper — they have diverged in exactly this manner across the frontier. The leading models have converged on accuracy: for a well-specified engineering task, the最好的best他们中任何一个的输出都非常出色，并且他们的output of any of them is excellent, and their平均average输出达到目标。他们的区别在于群体的宽度。十次中有九次返回正确、惯用的解决方案，第十次返回一些疯狂的答案；另一个只是好的，但几乎不狂野。对于生产工程来说，第二个更有价值，没有能力基准可以告诉你这一点。output is on target. What separates them is the width of the group. One returns a correct, idiomatic solution nine times in ten and something wild on the tenth; another is merely good but almost never wild. For production engineering the second is more valuable, and no capability benchmark will tell you so.

这与论文 1 判断尾部的规则直接相关，而不是平均值。在这里我提出更尖锐的主张：尾巴This connects directly to Paper 1’s rule to judge the tail, not the average. Here I make the sharper claim: the tailis群体、精准the group, precisionis前沿指标，而该领域的失败在于它宣传了最佳能力，而用户却承受着预期的能力。the frontier metric, and the failure of the field is that it advertises capability-at-best while its users suffer capability-in-expectation.

2  为什么技能以精度为前提：归零论证2  Why skills presuppose precision: the zeroing argument

对于一个系统来说，最重要的属性是精度，而不是准确度，这是有原因的。There is a reason precision, not accuracy, is the property that matters for a system that will be经营的operated而不仅仅是欣赏。在射击技巧中，你可以将一个紧密的群体归零：如果射击聚集但落在低处和左侧，你可以调整瞄准器，整个集群移动到中心。你不能将分散的群体归零——没有任何调整可以纠正落在不同地方的镜头，因为没有任何一致的东西可以纠正。rather than merely admired. In marksmanship you can zero a tight group: if the shots cluster but land low and left, you adjust the sights and the whole cluster moves onto centre. You cannot zero a scattered group — there is no adjustment that corrects shots landing in different places, because there is nothing consistent to correct.

论文 1 的操作规则——版本化技能、错误循环、“调整视线，而不是记忆”——是视线归零。它将分组移动到目标上。因此它Paper 1’s operating discipline — versioned skills, the error loop, “adjust the sights, not the memory” — is sight-zeroing. It moves a grouping onto the target. It therefore以精密地板为前提presupposes a precision floor。一项技能可以让精密的模型变得准确；它无法挽救分散的错误，因为针对分散的错误编写的更正永远不会概括 - 该错误不会在同一位置重复出现，因此该规则永远不会针对相同的错误两次触发。这就产生了清晰的分工和清晰的决策规则：. A skill can make a precise model accurate; it cannot rescue a scattered one, because a correction written against a scattered error never generalises — the error does not recur in the same place, so the rule never fires against the same miss twice. This yields a clean division of labour and a clean decision rule:

That is the strongest existing statement of the premise this paper begins from: the mean is not the quantity that matters. What I have not found stated plainly is the框架framing- 精度是前沿系统现在不同的轴，应该选择 - 耦合到— that precision is the axis on which frontier systems now differ and should be selected — coupled to an运营的operational结果：测量的一致性可以预测论文 1 的操作规程是否有帮助。我的主张是狭隘而实用的：并不是说方差在文献中无法测量，而是说它是实践中的决定性选择标准，不需要随机评分器就可以廉价地测量它，并且它的测量是人类-人工智能操作循环的决策程序，而不仅仅是排行榜列。consequence: that measured consistency predicts whether the operating discipline of Paper 1 can help at all. My claim is narrow and practical: not that variance is unmeasured in the literature, but that it is the decisive selection criterion in practice, that it is cheaply measurable without a stochastic grader, and that its measurement is a decision procedure for the human-AI operating loop, not merely a leaderboard column.

4  测量组4  Measuring the group

干净的举动：确定性得分。The clean move: score deterministically.精度不能用模型在环分级机来测量，因为标尺本身会分散，测量结果会继承圆形度，从而导致自我分级评估失败。逃避方法是仅测量具有以下特征的任务的分组：Precision cannot be measured with a model-in-the-loop grader, because the ruler would itself scatter and the measurement would inherit the circularity that dooms self-graded evaluation. The escape is to measure grouping only on tasks with a确定性的、二元的deterministic, binary结果：它是否编译，测试是否通过，linter 是否干净，是否进行类型检查。这些信号在评分循环中没有模型；它们便宜、可重复且无可争议。outcome: does it compile, do the tests pass, is the linter clean, does it typecheck. These signals have no model in the scoring loop; they are cheap, repeatable, and beyond dispute.

8  结论8  Conclusion

业界询问机器有多智能，并给出了答案：足够智能。现在决定他们的价值的问题是更古老、更安静的问题，每个射手都知道这一点——不是最好的射击落在哪里，而是团队的紧密程度。精度是前沿指标；它可以在没有随机标尺的情况下通过计算重复的确定性试验的决定性结果来测量；计数不仅仅对系统进行排名，还告诉操作员是否需要通过技能来修复特定的失误，或者通过更换型号来修复步枪的失误。第 1 篇论文给出了将组归零的规则。这篇论文提供了衡量它的工具——两者共同提出了排行榜无法做到的主张：性能的单位不是模型的智能，而是其分散性的紧密性和易处理性。第一次实际运行两次证明了这一点：如果存在差距，则用一条规则将其从 0/5 缩小到 5/5（测量）；以及差距在哪里The industry asks how smart the machines are and has its answer: smart enough. The question that now decides their worth is older and quieter, and every marksman knows it — not where the best shot lands, but how tight the group. Precision is the frontier metric; it is measurable without a stochastic ruler by counting decisive outcomes over repeated deterministic trials; and the count does not merely rank systems but tells the operator whether a given miss is his to fix with a skill or the rifle’s to fix with a change of model. Paper 1 gave the discipline for zeroing the group. This paper gives the instrument for measuring it — and the two together make a claim the leaderboards cannot: that the unit of performance is not the model’s intelligence but the tightness, and the tractability, of its scatter. The first real run bore this out twice over: where a gap existed, one rule closed it from 0/5 to 5/5, measured; and where gaps were撰写的authored前沿模型并没有发现它们，而是已经关闭了它们，因此图书馆的价值显示为零——教导一门学科的价值不是通过列出模型做得好的地方来证明的，而是通过发现并淘汰它可靠地做错的少数事情来证明。rather than found, the frontier model had already closed them, so the library’s value showed as zero — teaching that a discipline’s worth is proven not by cataloguing what a model does well, but by finding, and retiring, the few things it reliably does wrong.
