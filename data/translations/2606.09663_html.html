从 0 到 1 到 1 到 N：MetaAI 递归自我设计的可重复工程证据From 0-to-1 to 1-to-N: Reproducible Engineering Evidence for MetaAI Recursive Self-Design

Dun Li , Jiatao Li, Hongzhi Li Dun Li is with the Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong SAR, China. E-mail: dun.li@polyu.edu.hk Jiatao Li is with the Merchant Marine College, Shanghai Maritime University, Shanghai 201306, China. 摘要Abstract

递归自我设计是指人工智能辅助修改人工智能系统构建、评估和改进的机制。本文并未将 MetaAI 视为成熟的范式，而是将其视为人类种子、人工智能扩展的开发模式的工作术语，其中设计空间本身成为修改的目标。我们提出了一个具有四个标准的操作证据框架：可检查的目标系统、元级修改器、反馈导向的选择和递归延续。然后，我们根据这些标准绘制公共系统，包括 Darwin Gödel Machine (DGM)、STOP、Gödel Agent 和 ShinkaEvolve。 DGM 提供了目前报告的最直接的证据：其公布的结果显示，经过 80 次迭代后，在 SWE-bench Verified 上的性能从 20% 提高到 50%，在完整的 Polyglot 上从 14.2% 提高到 30.7%，并且消融表明开放式探索和自我改进都做出了贡献。最后，我们提供 MetaAI-Mini，一个可复制的基于 HumanEval 的协议和代码库。由于此构建中不包含完整的模型运行，因此 MetaAI-Mini 被报告为协议而不是实验结果。Recursive self-design refers to AI-assisted modification of the mechanisms by which an AI system is built, evaluated, and improved. This paper treats MetaAI not as a mature paradigm, but as a working term for a human-seeded, AI-expanded development pattern in which the design space itself becomes a target of modification. We propose an operational evidence framework with four criteria: inspectable target system, meta-level modifier, feedback-directed selection, and recursive continuation. We then map public systems, including Darwin Gödel Machine (DGM), STOP, Gödel Agent, and ShinkaEvolve, against these criteria. DGM provides the most direct currently reported evidence: its published results show improvement from 20% to 50% on SWE-bench Verified and from 14.2% to 30.7% on full Polyglot after 80 iterations, with ablations suggesting that both open-ended exploration and self-improvement contribute. Finally, we provide MetaAI-Mini, a reproducible HumanEval-based protocol and codebase. Because no completed model run is included in this build, MetaAI-Mini is reported as a protocol rather than as an experimental result.

I 简介Introduction

我们使用 MetaAI 一词来表示一种由人类播种、人工智能扩展的开发模式，其中人类研究人员指定初始系统、约束和评估协议，而人工智能驱动的流程则提出并测试后续设计。该术语在这里用作分析标签，而不是声称已经存在固定的领域或完整的架构。因此，实证问题是狭窄的：公共系统是否提供有限的证据表明人工智能可以修改决定后续人工智能性能的部分设计空间？We use the term MetaAI to denote a human-seeded, AI-expanded development pattern in which human researchers specify an initial system, constraints, and evaluation protocol, while AI-driven processes propose and test successor designs. The term is used here as an analytical label rather than as a claim that a settled field or complete architecture already exists. The empirical question is therefore narrow: do public systems provide bounded evidence that AI can modify parts of the design space that determine later AI performance?

这个想法有着悠久的思想历史。古德的“超级智能机器”论点提出，一台足够强大的机器可以设计出更好的机器，从而产生智能的递归放大This idea has a long intellectual history. Good’s “ultraintelligent machine” argument proposed that a sufficiently capable machine could design still better machines, producing a recursive amplification of intelligence[6]。施米德胡贝尔的哥德尔机将自我修改形式化为一个证明管理的过程，其中系统只有在能够证明重写增加了预期效用时才会重写自身. Schmidhuber’s Gödel machine formalized self-modification as a proof-governed process in which a system rewrites itself only when it can prove the rewrite increases expected utility[17]。后来的工作讨论了有界递归自我改进和基于软件的种子人工智能，强调承诺和风险. Later work discussed bounded recursive self-improvement and software-based seed AI, emphasizing both promise and risk[11, 19]。尽管哥德尔不完备定理不应被解释为对人工智能架构的直接限制，但哥德尔机器文献引发了更广泛的问题：系统是否可以在明确的标准下修改自己的改进程序. Although Gödel’s incompleteness theorem should not be interpreted as a direct limitation on AI architectures, the Gödel-machine literature motivates the broader question of whether systems can modify their own improvement procedures under explicit criteria[5].

当代人工智能研究已经实现了许多设计步骤的自动化。 AutoML、神经架构搜索和元学习可以优化人类指定空间内的模型选择、架构和适应程序Contemporary AI research has automated many design steps. AutoML, neural architecture search, and meta-learning can optimize model choices, architectures, and adaptation procedures inside human-specified spaces[8, 25, 3]。大型语言模型 (LLM) 代理通过规划、内存、工具和代码执行扩展固定模型. Large language model (LLM) agents extend fixed models with planning, memory, tools, and code execution[18]。最近的公共系统还表明，递归自我改进正在成为一个有组织的研究方向，而不是一个单一的孤立项目：Sakana AI 的 RSI 实验室明确将 DGM、ShinkaEvolve 和相关系统构建为迈向自主进化优化循环的步骤. Recent public systems also suggest that recursive self-improvement is becoming an organized research direction rather than a single isolated project: Sakana AI’s RSI Lab explicitly frames DGM, ShinkaEvolve, and related systems as steps toward autonomous evolutionary optimization loops[16]。当前的方法主要在固定的、人类指定的设计空间内执行优化，而本文研究的模式明确将该空间的部分视为可变和可改进的对象。. Current methods predominantly perform optimization within a fixed, human-specified design space, whereas the pattern studied in this paper explicitly treats parts of that space as mutable and improvable objects.

许多关于递归自我完善的讨论的弱点是经验性的。他们要么保持哲学性，要么直接跳到投机性超级智能。本文提出了一个更狭隘的问题：我们能否确定一个真实的、公开的、可重复的实验，其中一个人工智能系统改进另一个人工智能系统的代码级架构或算法支架，使用性能反馈来选择改进，并在多代中迭代这个过程？如果是这样，这样的实验将无法证明完整的通用人工智能，但它将提供工程证据，证明递归自我设计可以在当今的系统中运行。The weakness of many discussions of recursive self-improvement is empirical. They either remain philosophical or jump directly to speculative superintelligence. This paper asks a narrower question: can we identify a real, public, reproducible experiment in which an AI system improves another AI system’s code-level architecture or algorithmic scaffold, uses performance feedback to select improvements, and iterates this process across multiple generations? If so, such an experiment would not prove full AGI, but it would provide engineering evidence that recursive self-design can be operationalized in present-day systems.

我们通过对达尔文哥德尔机（DGM）的二次实证分析来回答这个问题We answer this question through a secondary empirical analysis of the Darwin Gödel Machine (DGM)[24]。 DGM 特别合适，因为它使用一个编码代理来修改自己的代码存储库，在 SWE-bench Verified 和 Polyglot 上评估后代，并维护一个代理档案，这些代理可以成为父代以供以后自我修改。我们还讨论了 STOP、Gödel Agent、ADAS、SWE-agent 和 ShinkaEvolve 作为相关证据，并在下面的相应分析中给出了引用。该贡献不是运行新的 DGM 基准测试；而是运行新的 DGM 基准测试。它是对报告的公共系统的证据映射，以及未来独立运行的可重复的迷你协议。. DGM is especially suitable because it uses a coding agent that modifies its own code repository, evaluates descendants on SWE-bench Verified and Polyglot, and maintains an archive of agents that can become parents for later self-modification. We also discuss STOP, Gödel Agent, ADAS, SWE-agent, and ShinkaEvolve as related evidence, with citations given in the corresponding analysis below. The contribution is not a new DGM benchmark run; it is an evidence mapping over reported public systems plus a reproducible mini-protocol for future independent runs.

II 递归自我设计的操作标准Operational Criteria for Recursive Self-Design

我们首先区分几个经常互换使用的术语。We first separate several terms that are often used interchangeably.递归自我设计Recursive self-design表示对代理、支架、工具链、提示策略、评估工作流程或塑造未来代理行为的代码级机制的修改。denotes modification of the agent, scaffold, toolchain, prompt policy, evaluation workflow, or code-level mechanisms that shape future agent behavior.递归自我完善Recursive self-improvement更广泛，可能包括系统改进其自身未来性能的任何迭代过程，包括但不限于设计更改。is broader and may include any iterative process in which a system improves its own future performance, including but not limited to design changes.自我进化代理Self-evolving agents强调跨任务和环境的终身适应，但不一定要求代理自己的改进程序是可编辑的。emphasizes lifelong adaptation across tasks and environments, but does not necessarily require the agent’s own improvement procedure to be editable.LLM驱动的项目演变LLM-driven program evolution使用 LLM 作为程序的突变或搜索运算符，并且可以发展任务解决方案，而不使代理支架本身成为主要目标。uses LLMs as mutation or search operators over programs and may evolve task solutions without making the agent scaffold itself the primary target.

我们将递归自设计定义为满足四个操作条件的迭代过程：We define recursive self-design as an iterative process satisfying four operational conditions:

  1. 1.

    可检查目标系统Inspectable target system：有一个目标人工智能系统，其架构、工具、工作流程、提示、内存或代码级策略都可以检查和修改。: there is a target AI system whose architecture, tools, workflow, prompts, memory, or code-level policy can be inspected and modified.

  2. 2.

    元级别修饰符Meta-level modifier：人工智能驱动的流程建议对该目标系统进行更改，而不仅仅是随机扰动或人工编写的补丁。: an AI-driven process proposes changes to that target system, not merely random perturbations or human-authored patches.

  3. 3.

    反馈导向选择Feedback-directed selection：建议的更改是根据外部任务或效用函数进行评估的，反馈会影响保留哪些后代。: proposed changes are evaluated on an external task or utility function, and the feedback influences which descendants are retained.

  4. 4.

    递归延续Recursive continuation：保留的后代可以作为后续几轮改进的起点。: retained descendants can serve as the starting point for later rounds of improvement.

根据这个定义，不需要完全的神经权重自重写。如果编码代理支架实质性地改变了底层基础模型的使用方式，则它可以成为合法的目标。这很重要，因为当今的基础模型经常被冻结，而代理性能很大程度上取决于代码级工具的使用、上下文管理、搜索、验证和工作流设计。Under this definition, full neural-weight self-rewriting is not required. A coding-agent scaffold can be a legitimate target if the scaffold materially changes how the underlying foundation model is used. This is important because present-day foundation models are often frozen, while much of agent performance depends on code-level tool use, context management, search, verification, and workflow design.

Ⅱ-AII-A 与普通优化的区别Distinction from Ordinary Optimization

递归自设计比超参数调优更严格。在普通优化中，固定搜索过程会在预定义空间内调整参数。在递归自我设计中，系统可能会改变解决后续任务的表示和过程。单独的温度计划或重试计数将是无力的证据。新的编辑原语、上下文摘要机制或补丁排名工作流程是更有力的证据，因为它改变了代理与任务交互的结构。Recursive self-design is stricter than hyperparameter tuning. In ordinary optimization, a fixed search process adjusts parameters within a predefined space. In recursive self-design, the system may change the representation and procedure by which later tasks are solved. A temperature schedule or retry count alone would be weak evidence. A new editing primitive, a context summarization mechanism, or a patch-ranking workflow is stronger evidence because it changes the structure of the agent’s interaction with tasks.

其区别可表述如下。边界内部优化保持设计空间固定：The distinction can be stated as follows. Boundary-internal optimization keeps the design space fixed:

Dt+1=Dt,xt+1=Ω​(xt;Dt).D_{t+1}=D_{t},\qquad x_{t+1}=\Omega(x_{t};D_{t}). (1)

St+1=Ψ​(St,Rt,Ct),S_{t+1}=\Psi(S_{t},R_{t},C_{t}), (2)

三、III实验设计Experimental Design

Ⅲ-AIII-A 实验选择Experiment Selection

主要实验底物是 DGM，因为它最符合上述四种操作条件。它从一个编码代理开始，让存档的代理自我修改自己的代码库，根据编码基准评估生成的代理，并使用存档来保留不同的垫脚石。作者通过公共存储库发布了代码和实验工件The main experimental substrate is DGM because it most closely matches the four operational conditions above. It begins with one coding agent, lets archived agents self-modify their own codebases, evaluates the resulting agents on coding benchmarks, and uses an archive to preserve diverse stepping stones. The authors released code and experiment artifacts through a public repository[23]。 STOP 和 Gödel Agent 被列为次要证据，因为它们报告了递归支架改进和自引用代理更新的相关形式，但 DGM 是主要实验，因为它报告了标准化编码基准上的多迭代代码级代理演化。 ShinkaEvolve 被包含作为样本高效程序演化的外部基线和资源，而不是作为主要的递归自设计实验：它使用 LLM 突变、新颖性过滤、自适应父采样和基于老虎机的模型选择来演化目标程序，但报告的框架并没有主要使完整的改进引擎本身成为递归修改的目标。. STOP and Gödel Agent are included as secondary evidence because they report related forms of recursive scaffold improvement and self-referential agent update, but DGM is the primary experiment because it reports multi-iteration code-level agent evolution on standardized coding benchmarks. ShinkaEvolve is included as an external baseline and resource for sample-efficient program evolution rather than as the primary recursive self-design experiment: it evolves target programs using LLM mutation, novelty filtering, adaptive parent sampling, and bandit-based model selection, but the reported framework does not primarily make the full improvement engine itself the target of recursive modification.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 基准和数据Benchmarks and Data

DGM 根据两个基准评估编码代理。 SWE-bench 要求代理通过编辑存储库和通过隐藏测试来解决现实世界中的 GitHub 问题DGM evaluates coding agents on two benchmarks. SWE-bench asks agents to resolve real-world GitHub issues by editing repositories and passing hidden tests[9]。 DGM 使用 SWE-bench Verified，这是一个人工过滤的子集，旨在删除无法解决的任务. DGM uses SWE-bench Verified, a human-filtered subset intended to remove unsolvable tasks[13]。 Polyglot 评估跨多种编程语言的编码能力，并与 Aider 排行榜相关联. Polyglot evaluates coding ability across multiple programming languages and is associated with the Aider leaderboard[4]。 DGM 报告使用通过率作为主要指标，即座席解决任务的百分比。. The DGM report uses pass rates as the primary metric, i.e., the percentage of tasks solved by the agent.

这两个基准对于递归自设计分析非常有用，因为它们测试的不仅仅是单一答案预测。编码代理必须检查文件、编辑代码、管理上下文、运行测试并生成补丁。因此，即使基础模型被冻结，工具和工作流程的改进也可以改变代理的有效架构。The two benchmarks are useful for recursive self-design analysis because they test more than single-answer prediction. A coding agent must inspect files, edit code, manage context, run tests, and produce patches. Therefore, improvements to tools and workflow can change the agent’s effective architecture even if the foundation model is frozen.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 初始目标AI和元AI设置Initial Target AI and Meta-AI Setting

在 DGM 中，最初的目标 AI 是围绕冻结基础模型构建的编码代理，可以访问 Bash 工具和编辑工具。为了进行自我修改，编码代理在 SWE-bench 和 Polyglot 实验中都使用 Claude 3.5 Sonnet（新）。在基准评估期间，SWE-bench 代理使用 Claude 3.5 Sonnet（新），而 Polyglot 评估使用 o3-miniIn DGM, the initial target AI is a coding agent built around a frozen foundation model with access to a Bash tool and an edit tool. For self-modification, the coding agents use Claude 3.5 Sonnet (New) in both the SWE-bench and Polyglot experiments. During benchmark evaluation, the SWE-bench agents use Claude 3.5 Sonnet (New), while Polyglot evaluation uses o3-mini[1, 14].

元级改进过程的实施如下。 DGM 存档存储生成的代理。在每次迭代中，从档案中采样一个或多个父代理。父代理分析自己的基准日志，提出功能或工作流程更改，编辑自己的代码库以实施建议，并生成子代理。对孩子进行评估。如果它编译并保留基本的代码编辑功能，则会将其添加到存档中。 DGM 实验运行 80 次迭代，每次迭代生成一个新代理，并且父代选择具有并行性。The meta-level improvement process is implemented as follows. The DGM archive stores generated agents. At each iteration, one or more parent agents are sampled from the archive. A parent analyzes its own benchmark logs, proposes a feature or workflow change, edits its own codebase to implement the proposal, and produces a child agent. The child is evaluated. If it compiles and retains basic code-editing functionality, it is added to the archive. The DGM experiments run for 80 iterations, generating one new agent per iteration, with parallelism in parent selection.

IV 证据映射和报告结果Evidence Mapping and Reported Results

VI 分析Analysis

证据与有限的 MetaAI 风格模式一致。在DGM中，人类定义种子、基准、安全边界和归档过程；人工智能修改了代码级代理设计，成功的后代成为未来的父母。图The evidence is consistent with a limited MetaAI-style pattern. In DGM, humans define the seed, benchmark, safety boundary, and archive process; the AI modifies code-level agent design, and successful descendants become future parents. Figure1显示完整的 DGM 循环优于消融，而图shows that the full DGM loop outperforms ablations, while Figure2将协议结构与测量分开。 STOP和Gödel Agent通过递归改进器更新和自参考策略修改提供相邻支持； ShinkaEvolve 阐明了当进化对象是目标程序而不是代理支架时，评估器驱动的程序进化可以实现什么。separates protocol structure from measurement. STOP and Gödel Agent provide adjacent support through recursive improver updates and self-referential policy modification; ShinkaEvolve clarifies what evaluator-driven program evolution can achieve when the evolving object is a target program rather than the agent scaffold.

最重要的推论是结构性的，而不仅仅是定量的。 DGM 的收益来自对代理编辑工具、重试逻辑、上下文处理和候选排名工作流程的更改。这些不是最终的 AGI 机制，但它们是 AI 系统修改操作基础的具体示例，未来的任务尝试将通过该操作基础进行。The most important inference is structural rather than merely quantitative. DGM’s gains arise from changes to the agent’s editing tools, retry logic, context handling, and candidate-ranking workflow. These are not final AGI mechanisms, but they are concrete examples of an AI system modifying the operational substrate through which future task attempts are made.

档案馆显得尤为重要。贪婪的过程可能会丢弃低分变体，这些变体后来会成为有用的垫脚石，而 DGM 基于存档的过程会保留多个谱系。这表明未来的 MetaAI 风格的系统应该将单链自我编辑与进化档案、谱系审计、回滚机制和保留多样性的父代选择进行比较。The archive appears central. A greedy process may discard low-scoring variants that later become useful stepping stones, whereas DGM’s archive-based process preserves multiple lineages. This suggests that future MetaAI-style systems should compare single-chain self-edits with evolutionary archives, lineage audits, rollback mechanisms, and diversity-preserving parent selection.

证据是有限的。它没有建立完整的 RSI 或 AGI：基础模型保持固定，基准套件和保留规则是人为编写的，并且 DGM 不会修改其外部开放式探索循环。这些实验也很昂贵、随机且以编码为中心。因此，结果应该被解读为递归自我设计的可行性证据，而不是即将到来的智能爆炸的证据。更强大的下一步将报告机器可读的谱系数据、每代差异、评估跟踪和失败的修改，以便可以审核自我设计动态，而不是仅从端点分数推断。The evidence is bounded. It does not establish full RSI or AGI: the foundation models remain fixed, the benchmark suite and retention rules are human-authored, and DGM does not modify its outer open-ended exploration loop. The experiments are also expensive, stochastic, and coding-centric. The results should therefore be read as feasibility evidence for recursive self-design, not as evidence for an imminent intelligence explosion. A stronger next step would report machine-readable lineage data, per-generation diffs, evaluation traces, and failed modifications so that self-design dynamics can be audited rather than inferred from endpoint scores alone.

安全是不可或缺的，而不是次要的。自修改系统可以改变工具、评估器、日志记录、上下文处理和执行路径。所引用的 STOP 和 DGM 工件都表明未来的系统需要沙箱、绊线、独立评估、不可变的审计日志和人为管理的部署门。Safety is integral rather than secondary. A self-modifying system can alter tools, evaluators, logging, context handling, and execution pathways. The cited STOP and DGM artifacts both indicate that future systems need sandboxing, tripwires, independent evaluation, immutable audit logs, and human-governed deployment gates.

七VII结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种递归自我设计的操作证据框架，并将其应用于公共系统。 DGM 是我们发现的最清晰的报告示例：经过 80 次迭代后，种子编码代理在 SWE-bench Verified 上从 20% 提高到 50%，在完整的 Polyglot 上从 14.2% 提高到 30.7%，消融表明自我改进和开放式探索都很重要。这些系统与 STOP、Gödel Agent、ShinkaEvolve 和 MetaAI-Mini 一起提供了有限的证据，证明递归自我设计和相邻的 LLM 驱动的程序演化是合理的工程模式。This paper proposed an operational evidence framework for recursive self-design and applied it to public systems. DGM is the clearest reported example we identify: after 80 iterations, a seed coding agent improves from 20% to 50% on SWE-bench Verified and from 14.2% to 30.7% on full Polyglot, with ablations indicating that self-improvement and open-ended exploration both matter. Together with STOP, Gödel Agent, ShinkaEvolve, and MetaAI-Mini, these systems provide bounded evidence that recursive self-design and adjacent LLM-driven program evolution are plausible engineering patterns.

其贡献是有意限制的：该论文没有声称任意未来智能的自主设计，并且没有报告没有 API 支持运行的 MetaAI-Mini 性能。未来的工作应该发布每次迭代日志，超越编码进行测试，使外部改进循环可以在保障措施下进行修改，并将安全性评估为一流的结果。The contribution is deliberately limited: the paper does not claim autonomous design of arbitrary future intelligence and reports no MetaAI-Mini performance without an API-backed run. Future work should publish per-iteration logs, test beyond coding, make outer improvement loops modifiable under safeguards, and evaluate safety as a first-class outcome.
