1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 已展现出非凡的能力，利用其在预训练期间获得的广泛参数知识来执行各种基于语言的任务。促成他们成功的一个关键因素是情境学习的能力，这使得法学硕士能够动态地合并和推理输入提示中提供的信息。这催生了强大的范式，例如检索增强生成（RAG）Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities, leveraging their extensive parametric knowledge acquired during pre-training to perform a wide array of language-based tasks. A key factor contributing to their success is the ability for in-context learning, which allows LLMs to dynamically incorporate and reason over information provided within the input prompt. This has given rise to powerful paradigms such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)[13]，其中外部知识被检索并作为上下文提供，以增强模型的响应，赋予其更广泛的知识范围并提高其输出的事实准确性。, where external knowledge is retrieved and supplied as context to enhance the model’s responses, granting it a broader knowledge scope and improving the factual accuracy of its outputs.

然而，外部知识的整合带来了严峻的挑战：知识冲突的出现However, the integration of external knowledge introduces a critical challenge: the emergence of knowledge conflicts[26]。当检索到的上下文中包含的信息与法学硕士的内部参数知识相矛盾时，就会出现此类冲突. Such conflicts arise when information contained within the retrieved context contradicts the LLM’s internal, parametric knowledge[30]。 这些差异的根源是多方面的。他们通常来自拥有过时知识的法学硕士. The root of these discrepancies is multifaceted; they typically stem from the LLM possessing outdated knowledge[18, 6]，或者来自外部上下文本身存在不一致的信息, or from the presence of inconsistent information within the external context itself[20]。 当这些冲突发生时，无论错误来源如何，模型的性能都会显着下降，导致生成事实上不正确或误导性的答案. When these conflicts occur, regardless of the source of the error, the model’s performance can be significantly degraded, leading to the generation of factually incorrect or misleading answers[22]。 因此，通过辨别可靠信息（无论是来自上下文还是模型的内部信念）来解决这些知识冲突对于确保法学硕士生成内容的可信度至关重要。 本文要解决的中心问题是. 2.1 大型语言模型中的知识冲突解决Knowledge Conflict Resolution in Large Language Models

将外部信息整合到 LLM 提示中是提高回复质量的标准做法。然而，这经常导致模型的预训练参数知识与提供的非参数上下文知识之间发生冲突。为了应对这一挑战，已经出现了大量的工作，主要分为两种对立的哲学立场。The integration of external information into LLM prompts is a standard practice for enhancing response quality. However, this frequently leads to conflicts between the model’s pre-trained parametric knowledge and the provided non-parametric, contextual knowledge. A significant body of work has emerged to address this challenge, largely bifurcating into two opposing philosophical stances.

第一线研究的假设是所提供的背景是事实的权威来源。此类方法旨在引导模型的输出与上下文信息更紧密地结合，在检测到差异时有效抑制其内部知识The first line of research operates under the assumption that the provided context is the authoritative source of truth. Methods in this category aim to steer the model’s output to align more closely with the contextual information, effectively suppressing its internal knowledge when a discrepancy is detected[12, 9, 35, 31]。例如，卡夫特. For example, KAFT[14]涉及构建训练数据和微调模型，以确保其遵守相关文档，同时忽略不相关的文档。对比解码 (CAD)involves constructing training data and fine-tuning the model to ensure it adheres to relevant documents while disregarding irrelevant ones. Contrastive Decoding (CAD)[24]与上下文无关基线相比，放大了上下文感知模型更可能出现的标记的概率。同样，IRCANamplifies the probability of tokens that are more likely given the context-aware model compared to a context-free baseline. Similarly, IRCAN[23]提出了一种推理时间方法，可以校准模型输出以提高对所提供上下文的忠实度。当外部知识可靠时，这些方法是有效的，但它们有迫使模型重复上下文中存在的事实错误的风险。proposes an inference-time method that calibrates model outputs to increase faithfulness to the provided context. These approaches are effective when the external knowledge is reliable, but they risk forcing the model to repeat factual errors present in the context.

相反，第二条研究认为法学硕士的内在参数知识通常比潜在的嘈杂的外部环境更可靠。因此，这些方法优先考虑对上下文扰动的恢复能力，利用训练或提示技术鼓励模型忽略错误的外部信息而支持其内部记忆Conversely, the second line of research posits that an LLM’s intrinsic parametric knowledge is often more reliable than potentially noisy external contexts. Accordingly, these methods prioritize resilience against contextual perturbations, utilizing training or prompting techniques that encourage the model to disregard erroneous external information in favor of its internal memory[28, 29]。例如，潘等人。. For instance, Pan et al.[21]提出了三种防御策略——错误信息检测、提示和分而投票机制——以减轻噪声外部数据的影响。同样，徐等人。propose three defense strategies—misinformation detection, prompting, and a divide-and-vote mechanism—to mitigate the influence of noisy external data. Similarly, Xu et al.[29]提出一种基于提示的方法，插入一个专用的系统提示，以警告法学硕士潜在的错误信息，并指示其在生成响应之前验证其记忆的知识。 Hong 等人采用基于检测的方法。propose a prompt-based approach that inserts a dedicated system prompt to caution the LLM about potential misinformation and to instruct it to verify its memorized knowledge before generating a response. Adopting a detection-based approach, Hong et al.[10]在反事实噪声数据集上训练鉴别器；在识别出嘈杂的文档后，系统会在提示中插入特定的标识符，以明确警告模型存在错误信息。train a discriminator on a dataset of counterfactual noise; upon identifying noisy documents, the system inserts a specific identifier into the prompt to explicitly alert the model to the presence of misinformation.

认识到固定立场方法的局限性，最近的研究探索了动态或适应性策略。例如，李等人。Recognizing the limitations of a fixed-stance approach, more recent studies have explored dynamic or adaptive strategies. For instance, Li et al.[15]引入了一个能够在上下文跟踪和知识回忆模式之间切换的框架，尽管它需要手动的、预先指定的设置。提出了一种更复杂的动态方法introduced a framework capable of switching between context-following and knowledge-recalling modes, though it requires a manual, pre-specified setting. A more sophisticated dynamic approach was proposed by[2]和and[11]，它使用模型的输出置信度分数作为启发式来决定是否信任内部参数知识或基于每个查询的外部上下文。如果模型对自己的答案表现出高度的信心，那么它依赖于其内部知识；否则，它默认为提供的上下文。虽然这些方法代表了向前迈出的一步，但它们的核心逻辑仍然是二元选择：它们动态地选择一个值得信任的来源，从而避免冲突而不是解决冲突。一个关键的限制是它们无法处理普遍且复杂的场景，其中内部知识和外部环境都容易出错。我们的工作通过提出一种同时评估两个来源并通过其差异进行推理的机制来直接解决这一差距。, which use the model’s output confidence score as a heuristic to decide whether to trust the internal parametric knowledge or the external context on a per-query basis. If the model exhibits high confidence in its own answer, it relies on its internal knowledge; otherwise, it defaults to the provided context. While these methods represent a step forward, their core logic remains a binary choice: they dynamically select one source to trust, thereby avoiding the conflict rather than resolving it. A crucial limitation is their inability to handle the prevalent and complex scenario where both the internal knowledge and the external context are fallible. Our work directly addresses this gap by proposing a mechanism to evaluate both sources concurrently and reason through their discrepancies.

2.2 检索增强生成的鲁棒性Robustness in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

检索增强生成已成为构建知识密集型 LLM 应用程序的基石。标准 RAG 管道涉及从外部语料库检索相关文档，并将它们作为 LLM 的上下文添加到用户的查询中。检索到的文档的质量至关重要；然而，现实世界的检索系统并不完美，并且经常返回包含噪声的文档Retrieval-Augmented Generation has become a cornerstone for building knowledge-intensive LLM applications. The standard RAG pipeline involves retrieving relevant documents from an external corpus and prepending them to the user’s query as context for the LLM. The quality of the retrieved documents is paramount; however, real-world retrieval systems are imperfect and often return documents containing noise[5, 4].

在 RAG 的背景下，“噪声”传统上被定义为与用户查询无关或无关的信息In the context of RAG, ”noise” has traditionally been defined as information that is irrelevant or tangential to the user’s query[33, 17]。重要的工作重点是识别和减轻这种形式的噪音。例如，纠正 RAG (CRAG). A prominent body of work focuses on identifying and mitigating this form of noise. For example, Corrective RAG (CRAG)[32]引入了一个轻量级检索评估器来评估给定查询的检索文档的整体质量。如果文档被认为是相关的，则知识细化步骤会提取最重要的信息。如果它们不相关或不明确，CRAG 会通过网络搜索来补充该过程，以找到更好的信息。同样，指令RAGintroduces a lightweight retrieval evaluator to assess the overall quality of retrieved documents for a given query. If the documents are deemed relevant, a knowledge refinement step extracts the most salient information. If they are irrelevant or ambiguous, CRAG supplements the process with web search to find better information. Similarly, InstructRAG[27]对法学硕士进行微调，以主动检测上下文中的噪音，并在综合最终答案之前对所提供的文档进行“批判性分析”。真实的RAGfine-tunes an LLM to proactively detect noise within the context and generate a ”critical analysis” of the provided documents before synthesizing a final answer. TruthfulRAG[16]此外，还根据提供的上下文构建知识图（KG），并利用该结构来促进推理和解决冲突信息。additionally constructs a knowledge graph (KG) from the provided contexts and leverages this structure to facilitate reasoning and resolve conflicting information.

观察者报（The Observer (𝒜o​b​s\mathcal{A}_{obs})观察者主要在离线训练阶段运行。其作用是从数据集中识别知识冲突的普遍模式（例如时间差异、来源可靠性问题）。它负责生成候选规则库，最重要的是，根据保留的规则集验证这些规则，以确保部署前的高质量。The Observer operates primarily during the offline training phase. 分析仪（The Analyzer (𝒜a​n​a\mathcal{A}_{ana})分析器在在线推理阶段运行。它的任务是进行分层冲突检测过程。它不是直接比较原始文本，而是首先综合每个上下文的单独答案，以识别语义层面的矛盾。检测到冲突后，它会深入到源文本中以提取精确的冲突片段并对冲突类型进行分类（例如“时间冲突”）。The Analyzer functions during the online inference phase. It is tasked with a hierarchical conflict detection process. Instead of comparing raw texts directly, it first synthesizes individual answers for each context to identify contradictions at the semantic level. Upon detecting a conflict, it drills down into the source texts to extract the precise conflicting snippets and categorizes the conflict type (e.g., “Temporal Conflict”).

推理者（The Reasoner (𝒜r​e​a​s\mathcal{A}_{reas})Reasoner 充当最终决策者。它通过将观察者的验证规则应用于分析器的具体发现来综合最终输出。它检索与识别的冲突类型相关的规则，验证是否满足文本条件，并应用规则的解决逻辑来仲裁正确的信息，最终生成最终答案和解释。The Reasoner acts as the final decision-maker. It synthesizes the final output by applying the Observer’s validated rules to the Analyzer’s specific findings. It retrieves rules relevant to the identified conflict type, verifies if the textual conditions are met, and applies the rule’s resolution logic to arbitrate the correct information, ultimately generating the final answer and an explanation.

5 局限性Limitations

同时WhileMACR尽管显示了准确性的显着提高，但它存在两个主要限制。首先，效率受到多智能体架构的制约；分析器和推理器之间的迭代交互以及反馈循环需要频繁的 LLM 调用，与单遍方法相比，增加了延迟和计算成本。其次，规则归纳过程的稳定性很大程度上依赖于底层LLM的能力。归纳规则的质量和普遍性可能会根据模型从演示数据中抽象模式的能力而波动，从而导致高度模糊的规则应用中潜在的不可预测性。一个有前途的方向是集成符号推理组件并优化代理间通信，从而提高效率和稳定性，同时保留整体框架的可解释性。demonstrates significant improvements in accuracy, it presents two primary limitations. First, the efficiency is constrained by the multi-agent architecture; the iterative interactions between the Analyzer and Reasoner, along with the feedback loops, necessitate frequent LLM calls, increasing both latency and computational cost compared to single-pass methods. Second, the stability of the rule induction process relies heavily on the capabilities of the underlying LLM. The quality and generalizability of the induced rules can fluctuate depending on the model’s ability to abstract patterns from demonstration data, leading to potential unpredictability in rule application for highly ambiguous. One promising direction is to integrate symbolic reasoning components and optimize inter‑agent communication, thereby improving efficiency and stability while preserving the interpretability of the overall framework.

6 结论Conclusion

本文解决了大型语言模型中知识冲突的关键挑战，其中模型的参数记忆和检索到的上下文之间或上下文本身之间的差异可能会导致事实错误或幻觉。现有方法通常假设至少一个来源是可靠的，直接采用所选来源作为最终答案，而不暴露推理过程，从而限制了可解释性。This paper addresses the critical challenge of knowledge conflict in large language models, where discrepancies between a model’s parametric memory and retrieved contexts, or among the contexts themselves, can result in factual errors or hallucinations. Existing approaches typically assume that at least one source is reliable, directly adopt the chosen source as the final answer without exposing the reasoning process, thereby limiting interpretability.

为了克服这些限制，我们提出了一种新颖的、可解释的框架，它将自适应知识评估和检索方法与归纳多主体推理范式相结合。我们的方法明确地提取和评估模型的内部知识以及外部上下文，从而允许解决冲突，而无需预先假设哪个来源是值得信赖的。通过三个智能体之间的协调交互，系统归纳学习并应用逻辑规则来解决冲突，产生全面的理由。 对三个基准的广泛实验表明，我们的框架显着优于最先进的基准。To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel, interpretable framework that combines an adaptive knowledge assessment and retrieval approach with an inductive multi-agent reasoning paradigm. Our method explicitly extracts and evaluates the model’s internal knowledge alongside external contexts, allowing conflict resolution without presupposing which source is trustworthy. Through coordinated interaction among three agents, the system inductively learns and applies logical rules for resolving conflicts, producing comprehensive justifications. Extensive experiments on the three benchmarks demonstrate that our framework significantly outperforms state-of-the-art baselines.

未来的工作将集中于优化多智能体交互的计算效率以支持实时应用程序。此外，我们的目标是促进结构化规则和参数模型的结合，进一步提高大型语言模型在复杂信息环境中的可靠性和可信度。Future work will focus on optimizing the computational efficiency of the multi-agent interaction to support real-time applications. Additionally, we aim to promote the combination of structured rules and parametric models, further advancing the reliability and trustworthiness of Large Language Models in complex information environments.
