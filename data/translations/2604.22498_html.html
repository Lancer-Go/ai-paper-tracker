1. 简介Introduction

多模态大语言模型（MLLM）在单图像感知和推理任务上取得了强大的性能Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have achieved strong performance on single-image perception and reasoning tasks(Liu et al., 2023; Achiam et al., 2023)，但它们在多图像任务中仍然较弱, yet they remain much weaker in multi-image tasks(Wang et al., 2024a; Meng et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024)。细粒度的多图像理解要求模型不仅能够识别和定位目标，而且能够在跨图像干扰下将该目标归因于正确的源图像。如图所示。. Fine-grained multi-image understanding requires the model not only to recognize and localize a target, but also to attribute that target to the correct source image under cross-image interference. As shown in Fig.1、Qwen3-VL等领先基础型号, leading foundation models such as Qwen3-VL(Yang et al., 2025)和实习生VL3and InternVL3(Zhu et al., 2025)通常无法在异构图像序列中保持严格的图像文本属性边界。often fail to maintain strict image-text attribution boundaries across heterogeneous image sequences.

这种失败表现在几个方面。首先，图像之间的特征纠缠导致This failure manifests in several ways. First, feature entanglement across images leads to跨图像注意力泄漏cross-image attention leakage (Park et al., 2025b; Qiao et al., )，其中一幅图像的视觉证据污染了另一幅图像的解释（图 1）。, where visual evidence from one image contaminates the interpretation of another (Fig.1(b))。其次，如果没有明确的坐标水平基础，模型很容易出现(b)). Second, without explicit coordinate-level grounding, models are prone to空间幻觉spatial hallucination (Guan et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a)，产生看似合理但不正确的本地化（图 1）。, producing plausible but incorrect localizations (Fig.1(一))。第三，在观点或语境变化的情况下，他们常常无法保留(a)). Third, under viewpoint or contextual variation, they often fail to preserve物体恒常性object constancy (Zhou et al., 2025; Assran et al., 2025)，在图像或转换后的视图中失去对同一实体的跟踪（图 1）。, losing track of the same entity across images or transformed views (Fig.1(c))。这些问题共同表明，薄弱的低层基础仍然是细粒度多图像理解的主要瓶颈。(c)). Together, these issues reveal that weak low-level grounding remains a major bottleneck for fine-grained multi-image understanding.

为了解决这些瓶颈，最近的方法越来越多地转向强化学习（RL）来增强 MLLM 的视觉能力。具体来说，通过 GRPO 等算法进行具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR)To address these bottlenecks, recent methods have increasingly turned to Reinforcement Learning (RL) to enhance the visual capabilities of MLLMs. Specifically, Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) via algorithms like GRPO(Shao et al., 2024)已展现出巨大的希望has shown great promise(Liu et al., 2025b; Shen et al., 2025)。例如，优化边界框 IoU 等客观指标的面向基础的方法在单图像场景中取得了显着的成果. For instance, grounding-oriented methods that optimize objective metrics such as bounding-box IoU have achieved notable gains in single-image scenarios(Cao et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2026)。然而，将这些可验证的强化学习范式扩展到细粒度的多图像理解在很大程度上仍未得到探索。现有方法通常在单图像上下文中运行，并且不能直接解决跨图像引用模糊性，其中模型必须首先将目标归因于正确的源图像，然后才能将其接地。. However, extending these verifiable RL paradigms to fine-grained multi-image understanding remains largely unexplored. Existing approaches typically operate within single-image contexts and do not directly resolve cross-image referential ambiguity, where the model must first attribute the target to the correct source image before grounding it.

除了方法上的限制之外，现有的细粒度多图像方法还受到数据管理成本和弱监督的严重限制。 首先，许多方法依赖于昂贵的冷启动监督微调（SFT）和手动注释（例如，MigicianIn addition to methodological limitations, existing fine-grained multi-image methods are heavily constrained by data curation costs and weak supervision. First, many approaches rely on costly cold-start supervised fine-tuning (SFT) with manual annotations (e.g., Migician(Li et al., 2025)）或大规模思想链（CoT）数据生成。这不仅增加了管理开销，还可能引入不稳定或事实上不一致的训练信号。其次，最近的自监督或对比方法（例如 MiCo）主要捕获全局图像级别差异，但缺乏对源图像归因和对象位置的明确空间监督。因此，它们改善了粗略的跨图像辨别能力，但没有完全解决细粒度任务中的指称模糊性。) or large-scale chain-of-thought (CoT) data generation. This not only increases curation overhead but may also introduce unstable or factually inconsistent training signals. Second, recent self-supervised or contrastive approaches (e.g., MiCo) mainly capture global image-level differences but lack explicit spatial supervision over source-image attribution and object location. As a result, they improve coarse cross-image discrimination without fully resolving referential ambiguity in fine-grained tasks.

The key idea of CGC is to use low-level spatial grounding as a verifiable stepping stone for high-level multi-image reasoning.

2. 相关工作Related Work

2.1. MLLM 中的视觉基础Visual Grounding in MLLMs

最近的多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM)，例如 ShikraRecent Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), such as Shikra(Chen et al., 2023), 狮鹫, Griffon(Zhan et al., 2024)，和雪貂, and Ferret(You et al., 2023)，通过将空间坐标序列化为文本标记，将视觉基础表述为语言生成问题。这一工作线将基础建立为语言生成和对象本地化之间的实用接口。为了进一步提高接地保真度并减少幻觉，后续方法采用了更强的监督或优化策略，包括偏好学习, formulate visual grounding as a language generation problem by serializing spatial coordinates into text tokens. This line of work establishes grounding as a practical interface between language generation and object localization. To further improve grounding fidelity and reduce hallucination, subsequent approaches incorporate stronger supervision or optimization strategies, including preference learning(Yu et al., 2024)和单图像感知的强化学习and reinforcement learning for single-image perception(Ma et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025b)。最近，一些工作已经开始将基础扩展到多图像任务，包括基准构建和后训练方法，例如 Migician. More recently, several works have begun to extend grounding to multi-image tasks, including benchmark construction and post-training methods such as Migician(Li et al., 2025), MIRG-RL (Zheng et al., 2025)、GeM-VG, and GeM-VG(Zheng et al., 2026)。虽然这些努力证明了多图像场景基础的重要性，但它们通常依赖于手动策划的多图像注释、跨图像问答对或教师生成的推理数据。相比之下，我们的方法重用现有的单图像基础注释并将其重新组合为可验证的多图像训练实例。. While these efforts demonstrate the importance of grounding in multi-image scenarios, they typically rely on manually curated multi-image annotations, cross-image question-answer pairs, or teacher-generated reasoning data. By contrast, our approach reuses existing single-image grounding annotations and recomposes them into verifiable multi-image training instances.

2.2. 细粒度多图像理解Fine-Grained Multi-Image Understanding

随着 MLLM 在单图像任务上的能力越来越强，在 BLINK 等基准的推动下，最近的研究已转向多图像理解As MLLMs become increasingly capable on single-image tasks, recent research has shifted toward multi-image understanding, driven by benchmarks such as BLINK(Fu et al., 2024), MuirBench (Wang et al., 2024a)和MMIU, and MMIU(Meng et al., 2024)。这些基准凸显了跨多个图像、面板或空间分离的视觉区域聚合证据的挑战。然而，大多数现有方法仍然侧重于粗粒度语义比较或高级推理，没有明确的源图像归因和细粒度空间定位机制。基于 SFT 的多图像方法，包括 Mantis 等模型. These benchmarks highlight the challenge of aggregating evidence across multiple images, panels, or spatially separated visual regions. However, most existing approaches still focus on coarse-grained semantic comparison or high-level reasoning, without explicit mechanisms for source-image attribution and fine-grained spatial localization. SFT-based multi-image methods, including models such as Mantis(Jiang et al., 2024)以及 OBELICS 等数据资源and data resources such as OBELICS(Laurençon et al., 2023)，提高多图像适应性，但通常依赖昂贵的手动管理，并且在严重的交叉图像干扰下提供微弱的校正信号。相比之下，最近的自我监督或对比方法，例如 MiCo, improve multi-image adaptability but often rely on expensive manual curation and provide weak corrective signals under severe cross-image interference. In contrast, recent self-supervised or contrastive approaches such as MiCo(Chen et al., 2025c)通过对全局图像级相似性进行建模来改善跨图像辨别力，但不对源图像归因和定位施加明确的空间基础约束。更广泛地说，最近在多模式能力增强方面所做的努力，包括工具集成推理improve cross-image discrimination by modeling global image-level similarities, but do not impose explicit spatial grounding constraints on source-image attribution and localization. More broadly, recent efforts on multimodal capability enhancement, including tool-integrated reasoning(Chen et al., 2025b)、免培训经验复用, training-free experience reuse(Shen et al., 2026), 搜索代理评估, search-agent evaluation(Chen et al., 2026b)，多阶段后训练, multi-stage post-training(MindGPT-ov-Team, 2025)，以及大规模多式联运系统开发, and large-scale multimodal system development(Chen et al., 2026a)，进一步表明人们对提高复杂的多模式理解日益增长的兴趣。然而，这些方法主要针对一般推理、工具使用或整体模型能力，而不是具有可验证源属性的明确跨图像基础。受这一差距的启发，我们将细粒度多图像理解视为图像索引和空间位置的结构化基础问题。, further demonstrate the growing interest in improving complex multimodal understanding. However, these approaches primarily target general reasoning, tool use, or overall model capability, rather than explicit cross-image grounding with verifiable source attribution. Motivated by this gap, we treat fine-grained multi-image understanding as a structured grounding problem over image index and spatial location.

2.3. MLLM 的强化学习Reinforcement Learning for MLLMs

基于规则的强化学习，包括 GRPO 式优化Rule-based reinforcement learning, including GRPO-style optimization(Shao et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025)，最近已适应越来越多的视觉语言任务。现有的视觉强化学习方法广泛针对高级多模态推理, has recently been adapted to a growing range of vision-language tasks. Existing visual RL methods broadly target either high-level multimodal reasoning(Huang et al., 2025; Deng et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025b)或细粒度的视觉感知or fine-grained visual perception(Shen et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025)。与自由形式推理任务相比，基于基础的任务特别适合基于规则的强化学习，因为它们提供客观可验证的奖励信号。最近的面向基础的 RL 作品利用了这一特性，使用免训练或基于规则的奖励，包括 DIVA-GRPO 中的难度自适应奖励设计. Compared with free-form reasoning tasks, grounding-oriented tasks are particularly suitable for rule-based RL because they provide objectively verifiable reward signals. Recent grounding-oriented RL works exploit this property using training-free or rule-based rewards, including difficulty-adaptive reward design in DIVA-GRPO(Gao et al., 2026)，Ground-R1 中的尺度偏差缓解, scale-bias mitigation in Ground-R1(Cao et al., 2025)，以及 DIP-R1 中明确的视觉推理痕迹, and explicit visual reasoning traces in DIP-R1(Park et al., 2025a)。 然而，这些方法仍然主要局限于单图像任务，其中优化目标不是跨图像干扰下的源图像归因。相比之下，我们的框架通过源感知空间奖励和从重构的单图像基础注释派生的训练信号，将基于规则的视觉强化学习扩展到细粒度的多图像理解。. However, these methods remain largely confined to single-image tasks, where the optimization target is not source-image attribution under cross-image interference. In contrast, our framework extends rule-based visual RL to fine-grained multi-image understanding through source-aware spatial rewards and training signals derived from recomposed single-image grounding annotations.

3.4. 数据混合Data Mixing

CGC 以固定的 1:1 比例混合图像间对比度和图像内对比度样本。该设计结合了两种互补机制来构建合成的多图像。图像间对比度通过引入语义解耦的干扰上下文来加强源图像归因和跨图像辨别，而图像内对比度通过将模型暴露于同一目标的相关视图来提高对象的稳定性。这两种机制共同涵盖了细粒度多图像基础的互补方面，包括源图像归因、跨视图身份一致性和精确定位。CGC mixes Inter-Image Contrast and Intra-Image Contrast samples with a fixed 1:1 ratio.
