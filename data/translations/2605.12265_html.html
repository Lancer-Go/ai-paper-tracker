跨域泛化有多大用处How Useful Is Cross-Domain Generalization
用于培训 LLM 监视器？for Training LLM Monitors?

Sam Martin
Anthropic Fellows Program
&Fabien Roger
Anthropic
摘要Abstract

使用提示语言模型作为分类器可以在训练数据有限的领域中进行分类，但会错过微调可以带来的一些鲁棒性和性能优势。 我们研究了对多个分类任务（每个任务都有自己的提示）进行训练是否可以通过新的分类提示提高新领域的性能。 我们表明，这种训练可以部分推广到相邻领域，从而提高训练期间未见的任务的分类性能。 然而，我们发现了微调模型无法遵循提示的特定边缘情况，例如当分类提示完全改变而数据域保持与训练期间相同时。 我们表明，分类训练可以与训练后的一般指令混合，并且（如果做得好）这种训练保留了分类训练的优点并减轻了其泛化失败。 令人惊讶的是，我们看到这种无思考的监督分类训练可以推广到有思考的分类和总结，这表明无思考的分类训练可能有助于构建其他类型的分类器和监控系统。Using prompted language models as classifiers enables classification in domains with limited training data, but misses some of the robustness and performance benefits that fine-tuning can bring. We study whether training on multiple classification tasks, each with its own prompt, improves performance on new domains with new classification prompts. We show that such training partially generalizes to adjacent domains, improving classification performance on tasks that are unseen during training. However, we identify specific edge cases where the fine-tuned models fail to follow prompts, such as when the classification prompt changes completely while the data domain remains the same as during training. We show that classification training can be mixed with general instruction following training, and that (when done well) such training keeps the benefits of classification training and mitigates its generalization failures. Surprisingly, we see that this no-thinking supervised classification training can generalize to with-thinking classification and summarization, suggesting that no-thinking classification training might be instrumentally useful in building other kinds of classifiers and monitoring systems.

1 简介Introduction

语言模型越来越多地用作分类器，例如标记用户试图造成伤害的会话Language models are increasingly used as classifiers, for example to flag sessions where the user is trying to cause harm(Markov et al., 2023; Sharma et al., 2025)或人工智能本身正在执行危险或失调操作的会话or sessions where the AI itself is performing dangerous or misaligned actions(Anthropic, 2026a; OpenAI, 2026; Anthropic, 2026b).

分类器有时是专门的微调分类器，这被证明有助于提高鲁棒性Classifiers are sometimes specialized fine-tuned classifiers, which was shown to help improve robustness(Sharma et al., 2025; Cunningham et al., 2026)，但简单地使用提示语言模型也很常见, but it is also common to simply use prompted language models(Anthropic, 2026a; OpenAI, 2026; Anthropic, 2026b; Sumers et al., 2025)它依赖于模型的一般指令跟随能力，不需要创建专门的训练数据。中间方法是使用针对多个不同分类任务进行微调的模型（每个任务都有单独的分类提示）which rely on the general instruction following capabilities of the models and don’t require creating specialized training data. An intermediate approach is using models fine-tuned on multiple different classification tasks (with a separate classification prompt for each task)(Inan et al., 2023; OpenAI, 2025b)。在这项工作中，我们试图更好地理解这种通用分类器训练的泛化属性，目标是改进人工智能控制监视器： 这种培训转移到新任务的效果如何？与训练运行后在常规指令中包含相同的数据相比，训练专门的分类器模型能带来多少好处？. In this work, we attempt to better understand the generalization properties of such general classifier training, with the goal of improving AI control monitors: How well does such training transfer to new tasks? How much benefit comes from training a specialized classifier model, compared to including the same data in a regular instruction following training run?

我们证明分类训练可以推广到新的相邻领域。We show that classification training can generalize to new adjacent domains.具体来说，一个经过训练可以回答“这是网络伤害吗？”的模型。稍后可以提示回答“人工智能是否做了任何危险的事情？”并恢复直接针对目标任务进行训练所获得的改进的 75%。如果我们的可用域内数据数量有限，那么即使数据集非常不平衡，添加来自相邻域的附加数据也可以提高性能。Concretely, a model trained to answer "is this cyber harm?" can later be prompted to answer "does the AI do anything dangerous?" and recover 75% of the improvement obtained by training directly on the target task. If we have a limited amount of in-domain data available, then adding additional data from an adjacent domain improves performance even when the dataset is very imbalanced.

然而，However,我们观察到一些泛化失败we observe some generalization failures模型从训练数据中推断分类规则，而不是查看提示。这可能会导致某些情况下训练使模型变得更糟。例如，单任务训练可能会导致模型坚持训练任务：当给出新提示但与训练中使用的记录类似的记录时，模型有时会应用训练分类任务而不是遵循新提示。where the model infers the classification rule from the training data rather than looking at the prompt. This can lead to some cases where training makes the model worse. For example, single task training can cause the model to stick to the training task: when given a new prompt but a transcript similar to that used in training, the model sometimes applies the training classification task rather than following the new prompt.

我们能够通过对一般指令跟踪数据进行训练来减轻这些失败We are able to mitigate these failures by training on general instruction-following data与分类数据一起。我们发现，简单地混合两个数据集并不是很有效，因为分类样本包含的未屏蔽训练标记比指令跟随样本少得多。进行分类训练alongside the classification data. We find that simply mixing the two datasets is not very effective since classification samples contain many fewer unmasked training tokens than the instruction following samples. Doing classification training之前before指导培训可以减轻稀释带来的负面影响。instruction training mitigates the downsides of dilution.

我们发现单标记分类训练的好处不仅仅限于单标记分类We find that single-token classification training benefits extend beyond single-token classification。我们评估思维分类模型，要求模型在给出答案之前生成推理。仅在单令牌网络危害分类任务上训练 Qwen3-8B，思考 AUC 从 0.84 增加到 0.94。 我们还发现，单标记分类训练可以提高模型用作摘要器时识别危险操作的速度。. We evaluate models on thinking classification, where the model is asked to generate reasoning before giving an answer. Training Qwen3-8B only on a single-token cyber harm classification task increases with-thinking AUC from 0.84 to 0.94. We also find that single-token classification training can increase the rate at which models identify dangerous actions when used as summarizers.

最后，我们表明，通过在单标记分类训练中迭代使用思维分类预测，这种从单标记分类到思维分类的转变可以用作无监督启发技术。Finally, we show that this transfer from single-token classification to thinking classification can be used as an unsupervised elicitation technique by iteratively using thinking classification predictions in single-token classification training.

4.5 分类培训的好处在培训后的后续指导中持续存在Classification training benefits persist through subsequent instruction following training

监督训练通常将损失计算为批次中未屏蔽标记的平均损失。如果我们将分类数据与大量的指导数据混合，那么每个分类样本仅对损失贡献一个标记这一事实意味着我们面临分类损失被所有其他损失项稀释的问题。Supervised training typically computes the loss as the mean loss of unmasked tokens over a batch. If we mix our classification data in with a much larger quantity of instruct data, then the fact that each classification sample contributes only one token to the loss means that we face the issue that the classification loss gets diluted by all the other loss terms.

作为缓解这种情况的方法，我们考虑采用分组的两阶段训练过程。我们在一组中进行所有分类训练，然后进行指导训练。通过这个过程，我们没有混合批次的分类和指令数据，其中未屏蔽的指令和分类标记之间存在很大的不平衡，但我们依赖于在第二阶段持续进行分类数据训练的好处。As a mitigation to this we consider a grouped two stage training process. We do all of the classification training in one group, followed by the instruct training. With this process, we do not have mixed batches of classification and instruct data where there is a large imbalance between unmasked instruction and classification tokens, but we rely on the benefits from training on classification data persisting through the second stage.

数据生成。Data Generation.

我们使用 Dolci-Instruct-SFTWe use the Dolci-Instruct-SFT(Team OLMo, 2025)数据集作为指导数据与我们的分类数据混合的来源。这些补全是从各种 GPT 模型生成的，这意味着它们非常不符合我们使用的 Qwen3 模型的策略。为了更清晰地隔离分类数据和指导数据之间的交互，我们丢弃补全并使用我们正在使用的 Qwen3 模型重新生成。这意味着完工符合起始模型的政策。如果我们不重新生成补全，那么指导训练阶段也将教授模型一种新的说话风格，这可能会对分类能力产生进一步的影响。这也有点模仿强化学习后训练阶段，其中完成情况更接近政策。dataset as a source of instruct data to mix with our classification data. 6 局限性Limitations

我们研究了一小部分分类任务，并在单个模型系列上进行了大部分实验。虽然我们在 Qwen3 家族中的结果的一致性证明我们的结果对所研究的确切设置不是很敏感，但更广泛的任务和模型多样性将加强我们的结论。We studied a small set of classification tasks and we ran most of our experiments on a single family of models. While the consistency of our results within the Qwen3 family is evidence that our results are not very sensitive to the exact setting studied, a wider diversity of tasks and models would strengthen our conclusions.

我们的强化学习设置很小。对算法和超参数进行更广泛的扫描可能会出现 1 令牌 SFT 初始化提供较小性能提升的情况。Our reinforcement learning setup is minimal. A wider sweep over algorithms and hyperparameters may exhibit situations where 1-token SFT initialization provides a smaller performance boost.

7 结论Conclusion

我们研究了一种简单的分类训练方法，发现其益处可以推广到相邻领域，训练也与训练后的一般指令兼容，并且益处转移到了思维分类和总结的生成能力的提高。这表明这种训练方式可能有助于构建监控能力更强的模型，无论是作为分类器还是在其他分类相关任务中使用。We have studied a simple classification training method and seen that benefits can generalize to adjacent domains, that the training is compatible with general instruction following training as well, and that the benefits transfer to improved generation abilities on thinking classification and summarization. This suggests that this style of training may be useful for building models that are stronger at monitoring, both as classifiers and when used in other classification-adjacent tasks.

A.4 合成对话生成管道Synthetic dialog generation pipeline

我们使用四阶段管道生成综合网络安全和化学危害分类数据集。对于前三个阶段，我们使用仅有用的模型来避免拒绝。We generate the synthetic cybersecurity and chemical harm classification datasets using a four-stage pipeline. For the first three stages we use a helpful-only model to avoid refusals.

第一阶段：种子生成。Stage 1: Seed generation.

我们通过提示语言模型从内容审核构成中生成种子角色和场景来引导管道。宪法规定了阻止/允许标准：阻止旨在攻击、欺骗或伤害的请求，而允许防御、教育或授权的安全请求。该模型生成 10 个单句用户角色，设计得既现实又模糊——在阻止和允许上下文中都是合理的——而不是刻板印象中的恶意或良性。它单独生成 200 个场景种子想法（100 个区块，100 个允许），每个想法都是基于宪法标准的特定情况。提示指示模型最大限度地增加多样性并避免灰色区域。We bootstrap the pipeline by prompting a language model to produce seed personas and scenarios from a content moderation constitution. The constitution specifies block/allow criteria: requests aiming to attack, deceive, or harm are blocked, while defensive, educational, or authorized security requests are allowed. The model generates 10 single-sentence user personas designed to be realistic and ambiguous—plausible in both block and allow contexts—rather than stereotypically malicious or benign. Separately, it generates 200 scenario seed ideas (100 block, 100 allow), each a specific situation grounded in the constitution’s criteria. The prompts instruct the model to maximise variety and avoid grey areas.

第 2 阶段：对话生成。Stage 2: Dialog generation.

对于角色、场景和类别（阻止/允许）的每种组合，我们提示模型生成 15 个请求-响应对话框，理论上最多可生成 60,000 个对话框For each combination of persona, scenario, and category (block/allow), we prompt the model to generate 15 request–response dialogs, yielding a theoretical maximum of 60,000 dialogs across the10×200×210\times 200\times 2生成矩阵。提示指示模型使用自然语言工件（例如，拼写错误、歧义）生成现实的、多样化的请求。块类别响应是有意遵守（而不是拒绝），提供所请求的有害内容，以便下游分类器可以学习检测不安全交互的双方。生成的数据集包含 53,333 个对话。generation matrix. The prompt instructs the model to produce realistic, varied requests with natural language artifacts (e.g., typos, ambiguity). Block-category responses are intentionally compliant (not refusals), providing the harmful content requested, so that downstream classifiers can learn to detect both sides of an unsafe interaction. The resulting dataset contains 53,333 dialogs.

第三阶段：质量分级。Stage 3: Quality grading.

更强大的模型（Claude Opus 4.5）根据六个二元标准对每个对话进行评分：违反宪法、未经授权的访问、欺骗/欺诈、破坏性工具、侵犯隐私和合法意图。将这些标签与预期的阻止/允许类别进行比较，以衡量生成质量。A stronger model (Claude Opus 4.5) grades each dialog on six binary criteria: constitution violation, unauthorized access, deception/fraud, disruptive tools, privacy violation, and legitimate intent. These labels are compared against the intended block/allow category to measure generation quality.

第四阶段：过滤。Stage 4: Filtering.

根据评分标签对对话进行过滤，生成包含 46,623 个对话的最终数据集。Dialogs are filtered based on the grading labels, producing a final dataset of 46,623 dialogs.
