长视野的以自我为中心的视频是可穿戴人工智能助手的丰富基础，但以对象为中心的问题，例如项目被移动到哪里、最后一次改变状态是什么时候，或者为什么被重新定位，仍然很困难，因为基于字幕和转录的记忆很少保留持久的对象身份或结构化空间变化。现有的长视频 QA 方法主要强调时间基础和剪辑检索，而先前的 3D 场景图方法通常假设比自由运动可穿戴 RGB 视频提供更强的几何形状，包括点云、RGB-D 输入、姿势视图、稀疏重建或重建场景。 R4DSG 推出了Long-horizon egocentric video is a rich substrate for wearable AI assistants, but object-centric questions such as where an item was moved, when it last changed state, or why it was relocated remain difficult because caption- and transcript-based memories rarely preserve persistent object identity or structured spatial change. Existing long-video QA methods mainly emphasize temporal grounding and clip retrieval, while prior 3D scene-graph methods typically assume stronger geometry than free-motion wearable RGB video provides, including point clouds, RGB-D input, posed views, sparse reconstruction, or reconstructed scenes. R4DSG introduces a相对4D场景图形内存relative 4D scene graph memory以自我为中心的长视频。 R4DSG 不是存储原始图形序列，而是将视频转换为按时间、地点、持久对象、锚点相对变化和本地交互上下文索引的紧凑可查询内存条目。主要思想是将稳定锚点与动态对象分开，跨帧维护持久的对象身份，并通过锚点相对转换而不是全局对齐的世界模型来表示对象状态。该方法基于最近在快速视频分割、时间传播和相对 3D 提升方面仅 RGB 的进展，生成可直接用于长视野问答的检索就绪存储器。对来自 EgoLifeQA 的 255 个与对象相关的问题子集的评估显示，在仅问题检索下，整体比 EgoRAG-Text 提高了 6.7 分，比 EgoRAG-Text 提高了 12.5 分。for long egocentric video. Instead of storing raw graph sequences, R4DSG converts video into compact queryable memory entries indexed by time, place, persistent objects, anchor-relative change, and local interaction context. The main idea is to separate stable anchors from dynamic objects, maintain persistent object identity across frames, and represent object state through anchor-relative transitions rather than a globally aligned world model. 主要挑战是需要一个The central challenge is the need for a可查询内存queryable memory。以对象为中心的问题取决于保留对象身份、对象周围的稳定引用以及解释其当前状态的转换。简单的摘要、说明文字或检索到的剪辑通常足以进行粗略的事件回忆，但它们在保留对象状态变化的结构化证据方面却很弱。因此，对于以自我为中心的帮助来说，有用的记忆应该满足四个属性。它应该跨时间保留持久的对象身份，编码空间关系而不仅仅是局部外观，将长流压缩为紧凑的可检索单元，并以下游 QA 直接使用的形式公开这些单元。. Object-centric questions depend on preserving object identity, the stable references surrounding the object, and the transitions that explain its current state. Flat summaries, captions, or retrieved clips are often enough for coarse event recall, but they are weak at preserving structured evidence for object-state change. A useful memory for egocentric assistance should therefore satisfy four properties. It should preserve persistent object identity across time, encode spatial relations rather than only local appearance, compress long streams into compact retrievable units, and expose those units in a form directly usable by downstream QA.

最近在以自我为中心的长视频理解方面取得的进展加剧了这一差距，但并没有缩小它。扎根的多跳视频质量检查表明，许多问题取决于多个分散的证据时刻，而不是一个连续的片段Recent progress in long egocentric video understanding sharpens this gap but does not close it. Grounded Multi-Hop VideoQA shows that many questions depend on multiple scattered evidential moments rather than one contiguous segment(3)。 AMEGO 认为，很长的以自我为中心的流应该转换为显式的可重用内存，而不是每次都进行端到端处理. AMEGO argues that very long egocentric streams should be converted into an explicit reusable memory instead of being processed end to end each time(11)。 EgoLife 将这一议程扩展到生活助理环境，并强调记忆检索、事件回忆、习惯理解和关系推理是未来以自我为中心的助理的核心能力. EgoLife extends this agenda to a life-assistant setting and highlights memory retrieval, event recall, habit understanding, and relation reasoning as core capabilities for future egocentric assistants(38)。然而，占主导地位的抽象仍然以标题为中心或以摘要为中心。它们用语言记录事件，但它们不维护对象位置、其与稳定引用的关系、这些关系何时发生变化以及解释此类变化的本地上下文的持久对象级帐户。. Yet the dominant abstractions remain caption-centric or summary-centric. They record events in language, but they do not maintain a persistent object-level account of an object’s location, its relations to stable references, when those relations changed, and the local context that explains such changes.

一个自然的答案是使用场景图。在表示层面，场景图很有吸引力，因为它们将实体、关系和证据绑定到可以更新、搜索和重用的紧凑结构单元中A natural answer is to use scene graphs. At the representation level, scene graphs are appealing because they bind entities, relations, and evidence into compact structured units that can be updated, searched, and reused(37; 43; 6)。先前的工作表明，3D 场景图提供了对象和关系的紧凑且语义丰富的表示. Prior work has shown that 3D scene graphs provide compact and semantically rich representations of objects and relations(36; 35; 39)。 Open3DSG、VL-SAT、OpenFunGraph 和 3DGraphQA 进一步证明开放词汇语义、语言指导和图形推理可以使结构化 3D 表示在下游更具表现力. Open3DSG, VL-SAT, OpenFunGraph, and 3DGraphQA further demonstrate that open-vocabulary semantics, language guidance, and graph reasoning can make structured 3D representations more expressive downstream(34; 15; 40; 37)。这些结果高度相关，但它们仍然假设比此处提供的目标设置更强的几何形状，包括点云、RGB-D 输入、姿势视图、稀疏重建或重建的室内场景。在日常以自我为中心的拍摄中，相机随佩戴者移动，观察结果是局部的，并且全局对齐的世界坐标系通常不可用、不可靠或根本没有必要. These results are highly relevant, but they still assume stronger geometry than the target setting here provides, including point clouds, RGB-D input, posed views, sparse reconstruction, or reconstructed indoor scenes. In daily egocentric capture, the camera moves with the wearer, observations are partial, and a globally aligned world coordinate system is often unavailable, unreliable, or simply unnecessary(9; 10; 39).

最近用于开放式视频分割和跟踪、时间传播和单目 3D 提升的纯 RGB 基础模型使得这条路线在较弱的传感条件下在技术上是可行的Recent RGB-only foundation models for open-set video segmentation and tracking, temporal propagation, and monocular 3D lifting make this route technically plausible under weaker sensing conditions(27; 2; 14; 32; 17; 4)。特别是，SAM 3 通过概念感知时间传播增强了及时的视频分割，而 SAM 3D、DUSt3R 和 MASt3R 提供到相关 3D 线索的仅 RGB 路径，无需深度输​​入或预先存在的点云。总之，这些进步使得构建可查询的存储器成为可能，该存储器可以捕获长的以自我为中心的视频中的相对空间变化。相反，重要的是一个对象是否从一个稳定的参考移向另一个稳定的参考，这种转变是否持续，何时发生，以及什么本地交互上下文最好地解释了它。. In particular, SAM 3 strengthens promptable video segmentation with concept-aware temporal propagation, while SAM 3D, DUSt3R, and MASt3R provide RGB-only routes to relative 3D cues without requiring depth input or pre-existing point clouds. Together, these advances make it feasible to build a queryable memory that captures relative spatial change in long egocentric video. What matters instead is whether an object moved away from one stable reference and toward another, whether that transition persisted, when it occurred, and what local interaction context best explains it.

受此观点的启发，我们推出了 R4DSG，Motivated by this view, we introduce R4DSG, a相对4D场景图形内存relative 4D scene graph memory，它将长的以自我为中心的视频转换为按时间、地点、持久对象、锚点相对变化和本地交互上下文索引的可查询内存条目。 R4DSG 的关键见解是长范围对象内存可以围绕, which converts long egocentric video into queryable memory entries indexed by time, place, persistent objects, anchor-relative change, and local interaction context. The key insight in R4DSG is that long-horizon object memory can be organized around静态锚点static anchors和and动态对象dynamic objects。桌子、架子、柜台、抽屉、沙发或冰箱等静态锚点提供稳定的关系参考。动态对象随着时间的推移通过与不同锚点形成新的空间关系来积累状态变化。该方法不是将所有观察结果强制放入一个全局框架中，而是跨帧关联对象实例，仅保留随着时间的推移保持稳定的锚相对转换，并将这些转换提升到内存条目中。结果是内存足够紧凑以供检索，但结构适合以对象为中心的推理。. Static anchors such as tables, shelves, counters, drawers, sofas, or fridges provide stable relational references. Dynamic objects accumulate state changes by forming new spatial relations to different anchors over time. Instead of forcing all observations into one global frame, the method associates object instances across frames, retains only anchor-relative transitions that remain stable over time, and promotes those transitions into memory entries. The result is a memory that is compact enough for retrieval yet structured for object-centric reasoning.

2.1. 长时间以自我为中心的视频理解、记忆和回答问题Long egocentric video understanding, memory, and question answering

以自我为中心的视频理解已经从动作识别发展到长视记忆和问题回答。 EMQA 是情景记忆 QA 的早期表述，其中以自我为中心的代理使用显式场景记忆回答基础问题Egocentric video understanding has evolved from action recognition toward long-horizon memory and question answering. EMQA is an early formulation of episodic memory QA, where an egocentric agent answers grounded questions using an explicit scene memory(7)。 EgoSchema 后来建立了非常长的多项选择 QA 作为自我中心视频中时间理解的诊断基准. EgoSchema later established very long-form multiple-choice QA as a diagnostic benchmark for temporal understanding in egocentric video(23)。建立在 Ego4D 生态系统之上. Building on the Ego4D ecosystem(12)，GroundVQA 在以自我为中心的长视频中形式化了扎根的 QA，并将答案生成与相关证据的时间本地化相结合, GroundVQA formalized grounded QA in long egocentric videos and coupled answer generation with temporal localization of relevant evidence(8)。扎根多跳视频QA 通过要求单个问题多个不连续的证据片段进一步增加了推理负担. Grounded Multi-Hop VideoQA further increased the reasoning burden by requiring multiple discontiguous evidence segments for a single question(3)。 AMEGO 提出了一种用于很长的以自我为中心的视频的主动记忆表示，可以捕获重复出现的位置和对象交互，而无需重复处理整个流. AMEGO proposed an active-memory representation for very long egocentric video that captures recurring locations and object interactions without repeatedly processing the full stream(11)。 EgoLife 将任务扩展为以自我为中心的生活助手，并引入了围绕事件回忆、关系推理、习惯理解和记忆密集型帮助的长上下文任务. EgoLife broadened the task to an egocentric life assistant and introduced long-context tasks around event recall, relation reasoning, habit understanding, and memory-intensive assistance(38).

这些作品确立了长期记忆在以自我为中心的视频中的重要性，但它们的主要抽象仍然是剪辑、摘要、字幕或高级语义记忆。它们对于一般回忆和时间定位是有效的，但它们并没有明确针对基于相对空间关系的持久对象状态记忆。这个方向也与图感知检索工作相一致，其中 G-Retriever、KGThese works establish the importance of long-term memory in egocentric video, but their dominant abstractions are still clips, summaries, captions, or high-level semantic memories. They are effective for generic recall and temporal localization, yet they do not explicitly target persistent object-state memory grounded in relative spatial relations. This direction also aligns with graph-aware retrieval work, where G-Retriever, KG2RAG 和 GNN-RAG 表明，一旦知识被表达为图形，检索就可以在紧凑的结构化证据上进行操作，而不是在平面文本块上进行操作RAG, and GNN-RAG show that once knowledge is expressed as a graph, retrieval can operate over compact structured evidence rather than flat text chunks(13; 44; 24)。 R4DSG 专注于早期的多媒体问题：直接从以自我为中心的长视频构建这样一个可查询的图形内存。. R4DSG focuses on the earlier multimedia problem: constructing such a queryable graph memory directly from long egocentric video.

2.2. 3D 场景图和开放词汇结构化场景理解3D scene graphs and open-vocabulary structured scene understanding

场景图提供了实体及其关系的明确表示，这使得它们对于推理繁重的视觉任务很有吸引力。在 3D 感知方面，3DSSG 引入了室内 3D 重建语义场景图的基准和学习框架Scene graphs provide an explicit representation of entities and their relations, which makes them appealing for reasoning-heavy visual tasks. In 3D perception, 3DSSG introduced a benchmark and a learning framework for semantic scene graphs from indoor 3D reconstructions(31)。 SceneGraphFusion 随后表明，可以从 RGB-D 序列增量预测 3D 场景图，从而实现部分观察下的在线图构建. SceneGraphFusion then showed that 3D scene graphs can be predicted incrementally from RGB-D sequences, which enables online graph construction under partial observations(36)。吴等人。后来证明，通过将稀疏映射与图预测相结合，也可以从 RGB 序列增量构建一致的 3D 语义场景图. Wu et al. later demonstrated that consistent 3D semantic scene graphs can also be incrementally constructed from RGB sequences by coupling sparse mapping with graph prediction(35)。 VL-SAT 将视觉语言监督注入点云的 3D 语义场景图预测中. VL-SAT injected visual-linguistic supervision into 3D semantic scene graph prediction from point clouds(34)。 Open3DSG 从点云转向开放词汇 3D 场景图. Open3DSG moved toward open-vocabulary 3D scene graphs from point clouds(15; 25)。 EgoSG 通过从未设定的 RGB-D 序列中学习，而不依赖于重建算法或相机姿势，使 3D 场景图更接近以自我为中心的设置. EgoSG brought 3D scene graphs closer to the egocentric setting by learning from unposed RGB-D sequences without relying on reconstruction algorithms or camera poses(39; 41)。 OpenFunGraph 将结构化 3D 感知扩展到现实室内场景中的功能关系和下游面向交互的推理.
