摘要Abstract

RAM 将运动感知语义跟踪器与自适应卡尔曼滤波相结合，以在严重遮挡和动态交互下实现稳健的身份关联。 内存增强的时间 HMR 模块通过注入时空先验以实现一致且平滑的运动估计，进一步增强人体运动重建。 此外，轻量级预测器模块预测未来姿势以保持重建连续性，而门控组合器自适应地融合重建和预测的特征以确保一致性和鲁棒性。 在 PoseTrack 和 3DPW 等野外多人基准测试中进行的实验表明，RAM 在零样本跟踪稳定性和 3D 精度方面均远远优于之前的最先进技术，为野外无标记 3D 人体动作捕捉提供了通用范例。RAM incorporates a motion-aware semantic tracker with adaptive Kalman filtering to achieve robust identity association under severe occlusions and dynamic interactions. A memory-augmented Temporal HMR module further enhances human motion reconstruction by injecting spatio-temporal priors for consistent and smooth motion estimation. Moreover, a lightweight Predictor module forecasts future poses to maintain reconstruction continuity, while a gated combiner adaptively fuses reconstructed and predicted features to ensure coherence and robustness. Experiments on in-the-wild multi-person benchmarks such as PoseTrack and 3DPW, demonstrate that RAM substantially outperforms previous state-of-the-art in both Zero-shot tracking stability and 3D accuracy, offering a generalizable paradigm for markerless 3D human motion capture in-the-wild.

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 RAM，这是一种从单目视频中进行实时、鲁棒的多人 3D 运动重建的统一框架。通过将语义跟踪与运动感知建模相结合，RAM 有效地减轻了遮挡和视点变化下的 ID 切换和跟踪丢失，从而实现稳定的身份关联并减少冗余计算。利用时间先验和运动预测，即使在严重遮挡的情况下，RAM 也能实现平滑且准确的重建。大量实验表明，RAM 始终优于先前的工作，尤其是在复杂的现实运动场景中，在跟踪稳定性、重建质量和计算效率方面取得了显着的进步。 RAM 为未来以人为中心的运动理解研究提供了坚实的基础，并将扩展到人体物体恢复。We propose RAM, a unified framework for real-time and robust multi-person 3D motion reconstruction from monocular videos. By integrating semantic tracking with motion-aware modeling, RAM effectively mitigates ID switches and tracking loss under occlusion and viewpoint changes, enabling stable identity association and reducing redundant computation. Leveraging temporal priors and motion prediction, RAM achieves smooth and accurate reconstruction even under severe occlusions. Extensive experiments demonstrate that RAM consistently outperforms prior works, especially in complex real-world sports scenes, achieving substantial gains in tracking stability, reconstruction quality, and computational efficiency. RAM provides a solid foundation for future research in human-centric motion understanding and will be extended to human-object recovery.
