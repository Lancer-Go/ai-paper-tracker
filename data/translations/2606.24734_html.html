任务分解以实现高效注释Task Decomposition for Efficient Annotation

努普尔·甘地，艾玛·斯特鲁贝尔Nupoor Gandhi, Emma Strubell

卡内基梅隆大学Carnegie Mellon University

摘要Abstract

在大型语料库上收集结构化表示的高质量注释的成本很高。结构的手动注释非常费力，而基于模型的注释虽然生成成本较低，但需要昂贵的验证和潜在的重要监督，以确保注释质量足够强以在下游有用。在传统的注释工作流程中，每个完整示例的注释都是由单个注释器端到端执行的。然而，结构化注释很复杂，并且任务的每个方面都代表着给定注释器的相关推理负载的独特挑战。现代注释项目可以包含异构的注释者群体，包括具有不同领域和语言专业知识的模型和人类注释者。然而，目前尚不清楚如何在这种情况下重新设计注释任务，针对不同的注释挑战，在异构注释者之间有区别地分配工作。High-quality annotations of structured representations are expensive to collect over large corpora. Manual annotation of structure is laborious, and model-based annotation, although cheaper to generate, requires expensive validation and potentially significant supervision to ensure that the annotation quality is strong enough to be useful downstream. In traditional annotation workflows, annotation of each complete example is performed end-to-end by a single annotator. However, structured annotation is complex, and each aspect of the task represents a unique challenge with an associated inferential load for a given annotator. Modern annotation projects can incorporate heterogeneous groups of annotators, including both models and human annotators with varying domain and linguistic expertise. It remains unclear, however, how to redesign annotation tasks in this setting, where efforts are discriminately allocated across heterogeneous annotators with respect to distinct annotation challenges.

我们建议将注释任务分解为子任务，以减少注释项目的总推理负载。受居中理论中的中心概念的启发，我们引入了基于有效注释空间中的自由度的推理负载的形式模型。使用该模型，我们表明识别这些中心（即通过注释子任务实现的显着锚实体）限制了输出空间复杂性，并且隔离和推进中心识别的分解减少了总体推理负载。我们提供了分解复杂结构化注释任务的指南，并通过示例证明了我们之前的工作提高了成本效率。最后，我们提出了一种在注释者之间分配子任务的程序，以在固定预算下最大限度地提高质量。We propose to decompose annotation tasks into sub-tasks in order to reduce the aggregate inferential load of annotation projects. Inspired by the notion of centers from centering theory, we introduce a formal model of inferential load based on the degrees of freedom in the space of valid annotations. Using this model, we show that identifying these centers (i.e. salient anchor entities realized by annotation sub-tasks) constrains the output space complexity, and decompositions which isolate and advance center identification reduce the aggregate inferential load. We provide guidelines for decomposing complex structured annotation tasks, supported by examples demonstrating improved cost-efficiency from our prior work. Finally, we present a procedure for allocating subtasks across annotators to maximize quality under a fixed budget.

2 多注释器设置中注释项目的设计目标2 Design Objectives for Annotation Projects in Multi-Annotator Settings

我们首先回顾在具有一组异构注释器的设置中注释项目的核心目标。We begin by reviewing the core goals of annotation projects in a setting with a heterogeneous set of annotators.

低成本Low Cost注释项目的成本取决于需要注释的示例数量以及每个示例所需的推理负载。注释的推理负载取决于注释结构的复杂性，而注释结构与效用相关，突出了推理负载和下游效用之间的权衡。例如，一组大的、细粒度的、模糊的、高阶的标签可能比一组小的、表达能力较差的简单标签更有用，但同时会给注释者带来更高的认知负担（Ma et al., 2023）。The cost of an annotation project depends both on the number of examples that need to be annotated and the inferential load required for each individual example. The inferential load of annotation depends on the complexity of an annotation structure, which is tied to utility, highlighting a trade-off between inferential load and downstream utility. For example, a large, granular set of ambiguous, higher order tags may be more useful than a small, less expressive set of simple tags, but at the same time exert a higher cognitive load on the annotator (Ma et al., 2023).

对于给定的任务，人类或模型注释者的推理负载在绝对值或相对于其他任务方面不一定相同。对于给定的任务，我们还可以预期模型推理和训练的成本（如果执行）与人类专家注释的成本不同。我们可以预期，对于包括人类和模型在内的异构注释者集，任务的相对成本将会有所不同。为了有效地使用注释资源，应该分配给定的注释者，以便他们的工作集中在相对于其他注释者而言便宜的任务方面。For a given task, the inferential load for a human or model annotator is not necessarily the same, in absolute terms or relative to other tasks. For a given task, we can also expect the cost of model inference and training, if it is performed, to differ from the cost of human expert annotation. We can expect that for heterogeneous sets of annotators including humans and models, the relative costs of tasks will differ. To spend annotation resources efficiently, a given annotator should be assigned such that their efforts are concentrated on the aspects of a task that are cheap relative to other annotators.

高品质High Quality除了最小化成本之外，还必须建立可靠性和有效性，因为任务是明确定义的并且可以一致地注释，而有效性是任务捕获了我们感兴趣的现象的真相（Artstein 和 Poesio，2008）。由于注释者可能有相同的偏见，强烈的一致并不一定意味着有效性，因此一致是必要但不充分的条件。例如，Bayerl 和 Paul (2011) 发现，专家注释者和非专家注释者在组内往往具有相当的一致性，而专家和非专家混合组的一致性较差。然而，在实践中，与专家或共识注释的高度一致性是广泛使用的注释质量启发法。In addition to minimizing cost, it is necessary to establish both reliability, in that the task is well-defined and can be annotated consistently, and validity, in that that the task captures the truth about the phenomena we are interested in (Artstein and Poesio, 2008). Since annotators can share the same prejudices, strong agreement does not necessarily imply validity, so agreement is a necessary but insufficient condition. For example, Bayerl and Paul (2011) finds that expert annotators and non-expert annotators tend to have comparable agreement within-group, and poorer agreement for a mixed group of experts and non-experts. In practice, however, high agreement with an expert or consensus annotation is a widely-used heuristic for annotation quality.

为了表明任务是明确定义的，可以在数据样本的注释者之间建立一致性。一致性是注释者之间对指南的共同理解的启发式方法，这对于证明任务是明确定义的以及所提出的注释程序捕获注释指南中记录的所谓现象是必要的。To show that the task is well-defined, consistency can be established between annotators over a sample of data. Consistency is a heuristic for shared understanding of guidelines across annotators, which is necessary to demonstrate that the task is well-defined, and that the proposed annotation procedure captures the purported phenomena documented in the annotation guidelines.

Consistency between a pair of model annotators is not necessarily meaningful. For example, model annotators may share a similar training distribution, resulting in common biases and artificially high agreement (Szymanski et al., 2025; Bavaresco et al., 2025; Lu et al., 2025) with collapsed diversity in judgment (Messeri and Crockett, 2024). Agreement between a machine and human annotator is also not without limitations, as high agreement can suggest that the task is shallow or surface-driven and not necessarily well-defined. Historically, agreement between human experts is the standard approach to establish the clarity of annotation guidelines (Pustejovsky and Stubbs, 2012). Regardless of how agreement is measured, more informative signals may come from component-level consistency, since different parts of a task vary in their importance for downstream utility and in the extent to which they introduce disagreement with respect to the complete task, as seen in CRAFT concept annotation where unitization is a major bottleneck in annotator training (Bada et al., 2012).

任务分解在多大程度上可以减少验证劳动仍然是一个悬而未决的问题，我们留待未来研究。在这项工作中，我们建议分解主要作为减少推理负载和有效分配注释工作的工具。The extent to which task decomposition can reduce validation labor remains an open question that we leave for future investigation. In this work, we propose decomposition primarily as a vehicle for reducing inferential load and efficient distribution of annotation efforts.

Wei et al. (2018) investigate factors associated with NER annotation time for clinical text, finding that a larger atomic space is associated with higher annotation time (e.g. number of words, dependency distance, number of entities). Fort et al. (2012) establish several dimensions of annotation complexity, many of which underscore the high inferential load induced by a large atomic space. For example, they put forth the dimension of discrimination of markable units from the complete input document, which is a function of the number of input atoms and output atoms. The tag set dimension is a function of the number of map atoms. The weight of the context for performing the annotation task is a function of the number of input atoms. We note that some dimensions of complexity proposed in Fort et al. (2012) are not captured by the size of the atomic space, namely the expressiveness and the degree of ambiguity of map or input atoms.

5.2 何时避免分解5.2 When to Abstain from Decomposition

同时，即使包含中心识别的任务根据这两个条件进行分解，也可能存在一些设置，其中降低输出复杂性的潜在好处被处理成本或可能困扰分解设置的级联错误所抵消。At the same time, even if a task containing center identification is decomposed according to these two conditions, there may be settings where the potential benefits in reduced output complexity are outweighed by processing costs or cascading errors that can plague decomposed settings.

信息处理成本高High Cost of Information Processing在重复处理输入空间的成本很高的情况下，分解会导致更大的推理负载。例如，GLiNER2 的开发者就考虑了这样的设置。 GLiNER2 是一个小型多任务信息提取模型，参数少于 5 亿个，设计为在 CPU 硬件上运行，并经过训练可在单次传递中执行 NER 组合、层次结构提取和文本分类。扎拉蒂安娜等人。 (2025) 检查了一项富集任务，发现使用 435M DeBERTa 模型将 0-shot 多类分类任务分解为每个标签前向传递的计算成本更高，而且性能低于 GLiNER2。这个例子在一定程度上强调了这样一种情况：训练注释器联合执行多个相关的子任务可能比为单个子任务开发专门的注释器更有效。In cases where the cost of repeatedly processing input space is high, decomposition results in a larger inferential load. The developers of GLiNER2, for example, consider such a setting. GLiNER2 is a small multi-task Information Extraction model with fewer than 500 million parameters designed to run on CPU-hardware and trained to perform, in a single pass, compositions of NER, hierarchical structure extraction, and text classification. Zaratiana et al. (2025) inspect an enrichment task, finding that decomposing a 0-shot multi-class classification task to perlabel forward passes with a 435M DeBERTa model is both more computationally expensive and underperforms GLiNER2. This example, in part, highlights the case where training annotators to perform multiple related subtasks jointly may be more efficient than developing specialized annotators for individual sub-tasks.

然而，对于复杂结构的注释，在实践中，分解设置中的信息处理成本不太可能比完整任务设置中的输出复杂性的成本重要。对于任何文本绑定结构的注释，都会有至少一个中心识别任务。随着构成结构的依赖实体数量的增加，输出空间复杂度随着实体数量呈现迭代指数增长。另一方面，在分解下，处理映射和输入空间的成本对于每个任务细分呈多项式增长。考虑到先前工作（§3.3.2）中引用的原子空间复杂性和推理负载之间的紧密关系，我们认为分解任务设置中的信息处理成本通常会低于注释器完整任务设置中的潜在输出复杂性，特别是在具有多个相互依赖的显着实体的复杂结构的情况下。For annotation of complex structure, however, it is unlikely in practice that the cost of information processing in the decomposed setting is less significant than that of the output complexity in the complete task setting. For annotation of any text-bound structure, there will be at least one center identification task. As the number of dependent entities that make up the structure increase, the output space complexity exhibits iterated exponential growth with the number of entities. Under a decomposition, on the other hand, the cost of processing the map and input space grows polynomially for each task subdivision. Considering the strong relationship between atomic space complexity and inferential load cited in prior work (§3.3.2), we argue that the cost of information processing in the decomposed task setting will generally be lower than the potential output complexity in the complete task setting for annotators, especially in cases of complex structure with multiple interdependent salient entities.

5.3 注释器分配：最强注释器识别中心5.3 Annotator Allocation: The Strongest Annotator Identifies Centers

在注释资源受限的设置中，将资源集中在中心识别子任务上可能会产生更高质量的数据。一方面，中心识别对完整任务的整体注释质量有着不成比例的巨大影响。中心识别代表分解图的源组件，因此中心识别子任务的稳健注释将避免相关子任务中的级联错误。其次，我们应该预料到中心识别任务在认知上是困难的，因此成本高昂。这是根据我们的推理载荷模型和居中理论得出的（Grosz 等人，1995a）。在我们的推理负载模型中，我们计算每个任务引入的新中心的数量，使得中心识别任务至少有一个新中心，而丰富任务必然没有新中心。中心识别代表了焦点的明显转变，其中显着实体是在注释期间实现的。此类任务比中心具有连续性的丰富任务代表更高的认知负荷。In annotation resource constrained settings, concentrating resources on center identification sub-tasks will likely result in higher quality data. For one, center identification has a disproportionately large impact on the overall annotation quality of the complete task. Center identification represents the source component of the decomposition graph, so robust annotation of center identification sub-tasks would evade cascading errors in dependent sub-tasks. Second, we should expect that center identification tasks are cognitively hard and consequently expensive. This follows from our model of inferential load and centering theory (Grosz et al., 1995a). In our model of inferential load, we count the number of new centers introduced for each task, such that center identification tasks have at least one new center while enrichment tasks necessarily have no new centers. Center identification represents a clear shift in focus, where the salient entity is realized during annotation. Such tasks represent a higher cognitive load than enrichment tasks where there is continuity in the center.

5.4 定心理论与原子空间复杂度之间的相互作用5.4 Interaction between Centering Theory and Atomic Space Complexity

我们的推理负载模型与中心理论（Grosz 等人，1995b）一致，因为引入中心或转移注释者注意力的任务具有很大的原子输出空间。另一方面，丰富或表征中心的任务（例如，类型位置、统一位置、链接提及现有中心）必然具有较小的原子输出空间。这些任务代表了注释者的一系列连续性，其中中心被重复重用。Our model of inferential load is consistent with centering theory (Grosz et al., 1995b) in that tasks that introduce centers, or shift the attention of annotators, have a large atomic output space. On the other hand, tasks that enrich or characterize centers (e.g. type position, unitize position, link mention to an existing center) necessarily have a small atomic output space. Such tasks represent a sequence of continuity for annotators, where centers are repeatedly reused.

减少原子空间可以减少推理负载的概念也以中心理论为基础。对于较小的输入原子空间，在执行注释时需要处理的候选实体或概念较少，因此我们可以预期注释者会经历较少的焦点转移。类似地，对于输出空间复杂性，如果可供选择的潜在有效输出较少，则注释者在权衡候选注释时会经历较少的焦点转移。The notion that reducing the atomic space can reduce the inferential load is also grounded in centering theory. For smaller input atomic spaces, there are fewer candidate entities or concepts to process while performing annotation, so we can expect annotators to experience fewer shifts in focus. Similarly, with respect to output space complexity, if there are fewer potential valid outputs to select between, the annotator experiences fewer shifts in focus when weighing candidate annotations.

Model-based approaches characterize complexity in terms of the number of reasoning units required (Tang et al., 2025), descriptive capability classes (Wang et al., 2025), or the size of the smallest model capable of performing the task (Bae et al., 2023). In parallel, human annotation studies use proxies such as annotation time

任务分解作为资源效率的原则Task Decomposition as a Principle for Resource Efficiency高效注释的任务分解与部分注释密切相关，其中工作重点是信息丰富的子结构而不是完整的结构注释。这种方法在命名实体识别、依存解析、事件提取和关系提取等任务中非常有效（Zhang 等人，2023 年；Marcheggiani 和 Artières，2014 年）。我们的工作重点是在子任务之间分配注释工作，但是通过对具有部分注释的信息性子任务实例进行粒度区分可以进一步提高效率。Task decomposition for efficient annotation is closely related to partial annotation, where effort is focused on informative sub-structures rather than full structure annotation. This approach has been effective across tasks such as named entity recognition, dependency parsing, event extraction, and relation extraction (Zhang et al., 2023; Marcheggiani and Artières, 2014). Our work is focused on distributing annotation efforts across sub-tasks, but efficiency could be further improved by granular discrimination of informative sub-task instances with partial annotation.

这种观点与输出受限的注释和交互式校正相一致，其中固定部分结构减少了剩余决策的搜索空间。例如，Culotta 和 McCallum (2005) 表明，部分校正可以限制模型预测，有效地将分割转换为更简单的分类问题，强调分割复杂性随着序列长度呈指数增长。同样，Michael（2023）主张通过从简单的、范围狭窄的表示中引导来构建复杂的注释。这些方法反映了我们的中心概念，即限制下游注释并降低有效复杂性的核心子结构。This perspective aligns with output-constrained annotation and interactive correction, where fixing parts of the structure reduces the search space of remaining decisions. For example, Culotta and McCallum (2005) show that partial corrections can constrain model predictions, effectively converting segmentation into simpler classification problems, highlighting that segmentation complexity grows exponentially with sequence length. Similarly, Michael (2023) advocate constructing complex annotations by bootstrapping from simple, narrowly scoped representations. These approaches mirror our notion of centers as core sub-structures that constrain downstream annotation and reduce effective complexity.

For example, span extraction is often split into head identification followed by boundary detection, improving robustness in nested NER and mention detection (Lin et al., 2019; Peng et al., 2015). 8 结论8 Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们建议分解结构化注释任务，将中心识别隔离为子任务，以便更有效地分配注释器工作，特别是在分解产生的聚合推理负载低于使用单个注释器执行完整任务的情况下。我们将推理负载形式化为多个复杂维度的加权和（§3）。这包括在注释过程中处理和实现的单元，特别是原子输入空间、映射空间和输出复杂性。推理负载包括中心的引入，或在执行注释时实现的显着话语实体，我们借用了中心理论（Grosz 等人，1995a）。通过 NER 和共指消解中的示例，我们说明了结构化注释任务的分解（其中中心识别子任务被隔离并首先执行）如何通过限制注释的焦点来降低后续子任务的输出复杂性（§4）。考虑到确实减少了总推理负载的分解，我们提出了一种为每个子任务选择注释器的算法，其中我们要么贪婪地将注释器分配给子任务，要么使用每个子任务的蒸馏来开发满足预算约束的注释器（§6）。In this work, we propose to decompose structured annotation tasks, isolating center identification as a sub-task for more efficient allocation of annotator effort, specifically in cases where aggregate inferential load resulting from decomposition is lower than performing a complete task with a single annotator. We formalize inferential load as a weighted sum of several dimensions of complexity (§3). This includes the units that are processed and realized over the course of annotation, specifically the atomic input space, map space, and output complexity. The inferential load incorporates the introduction of centers, or salient discourse entities that are realized in performing annotation, which we borrow from centering theory (Grosz et al., 1995a). Through examples in NER and coreference resolution, we illustrate how decomposition of structured annotation tasks, where center identification sub-tasks are isolated and performed first, can reduce the output complexity of subsequent sub-tasks by constraining the focus of annotation (§4). Given a decomposition that does reduce aggregate inferential load, we present an algorithm for selecting annotators for each sub-task, where we either greedily assign annotators to sub-tasks or develop annotators that meet budget constraints using distillation for each sub-task (§6).

我们强调了这项工作的几个重要局限性。我们的推理负载概念没有考虑到单个原子的模糊性，也没有考虑到原子是否在输入或映射空间中隐式组合，这在实践中可能会产生影响。为了简单起见，这项工作也不区分有效和合理注释的空间。We highlight several important limitations of this work. Our notion of inferential load does not account for the ambiguity of individual atoms or whether the atoms are implicitly compositional in the input or map space which would likely have an effect in practice. For simplicity, this work also does not distinguish between the space of valid and reasonable annotations.

27

分解必然导致来自为每个子任务实例重复处理的输入空间的上下文，并且可能存在信息处理特别昂贵的注释者。最后，这项工作提出了高效分解的理论模型，虽然我们已经证明了 NER 和共指消解的两种特定分解的价值（Gandhi 等人，2023、2026），但我们还没有探索这两个任务的替代分解，更不用说其他结构化表示了。尽管存在这些限制，任务分解广泛适用于异构注释器设置下的结构化注释，并且我们提出了一个用于有效设计此类分解的框架。Decomposition necessarily results in context from the input space processed repeatedly for each sub-task instance, and there may be annotators for whom information processing is especially costly. Finally, this work presents a theoretical model of efficient decompositions, and while we have demonstrated the value of two particular decompositions for NER and coreference resolution (Gandhi et al., 2023, 2026), we have not explored alternate decompositions for these two tasks, let alone other structured representations. Despite these limitations, task decomposition is widely applicable for structured annotation under heterogeneous annotator settings, and we present a framework for the efficient design of such decompositions.
