摘要Abstract

预测编码（PC）为人工神经网络训练中的反向传播提供了一种本地且基于生物学的替代方案，但迄今为止，它仍然较慢，并且随着网络深度的增加，性能急剧下降。我们将这两个问题追溯到一个单一的简化：当前的 PC 网络将精度矩阵固定为恒等式，丢弃变分推导要求快速、局部和贝叶斯的精度加权预测误差。我们通过将预测编码网络表示为深层分层高斯滤波器（HGF）并恢复精确加权消息传递来缩小这一差距，从而在每一层产生动态不确定性估计和赫布兼容的更新规则。由此产生的网络可以在单个自由能目标下同时学习激活、权重和精度，没有全局误差信号，并且无需迭代或自动微分即可解决推理问题。在 FashionMNIST 上，我们的解决方案在纪元级挂钟成本中实现反向传播，同时在更少的纪元内收敛，并且在在线、数据效率和概念漂移任务方面优于它。因此，我们确定，在线精确学习的封闭式变分推理为深度预测编码网络提供了易于处理的基础，保留了生物学和解释性优势，而不需要迭代松弛或全局误差信号。Predictive coding (PC) offers a local and biologically grounded alternative to backpropagation in the training of artificial neural networks, yet to date, it remains slower, and performance degrades sharply as network depth increases. We trace both problems to a single simplification: current PC networks fix the precision matrix to the identity, discarding precision-weighted prediction errors that the variational derivation requires to be fast, local, and Bayesian. We close this gap by expressing predictive coding networks as deep hierarchical Gaussian filters (HGFs) and restore precision-weighted message passing, yielding dynamic uncertainty estimates and Hebbian-compatible update rules at every layer. The resulting networks can simultaneously learn activations, weights, and precisions under a single free-energy objective, with no global error signal, and resolve inference without requiring iterations or automatic differentiation. On FashionMNIST, our solution approaches backpropagation in epoch-level wall-clock cost while converging in fewer epochs, and outperforms it on online, data efficiency, and concept-drift tasks. We thus establish that closed-form variational inference with online precision learning provides a tractable foundation for deep predictive coding networks, retaining biological and interpretative advantages, without requiring iterative relaxation or global error signals.

2.1 预测编码网络2.1 Predictive coding networks

2.3 预测编码中的精度和波动性学习2.3 Precision and volatility learning in predictive coding

精度（即预测误差的逆协方差）在预测编码中占据理论上的中心位置。变分自由能分解为逐层精度加权平方预测误差的总和，并且调制精度相当于动态加权每个误差信号对信念更新和学习的影响[Friston，2005，Bogacz，2017]。在线性情况下，这种加权在形式上相当于自然梯度下降，其精度扮演 Fisher 信息矩阵的角色，并产生自适应的、曲率感知的学习率（[Millidge et al., 2021]，附录 B）。如果没有它，层就无法自适应地缩放其误差的影响，跨层能量分布会变得指数不平衡 Pinchetti 等人。 [2025]，齐等人。 [2025]，并且网络丧失了对不确定性的任何原则性处理。Precision (i.e., the inverse covariance of prediction errors) occupies a theoretically central position in predictive coding. The variational free energy decomposes into a sum of layer-wise, precisionweighted squared prediction errors, and modulating precisions amounts to dynamically weighting the influence of each error signal on belief updates and learning [Friston, 2005, Bogacz, 2017]. In the linear case, this weighting is formally equivalent to natural-gradient descent, with precisions playing the role of the Fisher information matrix and yielding adaptive, curvature-aware learning rates ([Millidge et al., 2021], Appendix B). Without it, layers cannot adaptively scale the influence of their errors, the cross-layer energy distribution becomes exponentially imbalanced Pinchetti et al. [2025], Qi et al. [2025], and the network forfeits any principled handle on uncertainty.

尽管如此，精度在深度计算机中的实际作用仍然有限。绝大多数为机器学习任务开发的 PCN [Whittington and Bogacz, 2017, Salvatori et al., 2021, 2024, Millidge et al., 2022, Pinchetti et al., 2025, Oliviers et al., 2025, Song et al., 2024] 在整个训练过程中固定恒等精度，有效地假设同方差噪声并将自由能减少为未加权平方误差之和。当精度被视为可学习时，它通常被估计为缓慢变化的参数，而不是快速变化的信念状态，而信念状态本身就是分层推理的目标。众所周知，精确加权的赫布塑性是一个有前途的新研究方向[Salvatori et al., 2026]。据我们所知，现有的深度PC网络没有将精度在线内生地推断为与层次结构的其余部分耦合的后验信念，并且没有在具有固定身份精度的网络和其精度本身就是层次推理方案的输出的网络之间进行系统的经验比较。The practical role of precision in deep PC has nonetheless remained limited. The large majority of PCNs developed for machine-learning tasks [Whittington and Bogacz, 2017, Salvatori et al., 2021, 2024, Millidge et al., 2022, Pinchetti et al., 2025, Oliviers et al., 2025, Song et al., 2024] fix precisions to the identity throughout training, effectively assuming homoscedastic noise and reducing the free energy to a sum of unweighted squared errors. Where precision is treated as learnable, it is typically estimated as a slowly varying parameter rather than as a fast-changing belief state that is itself the target of hierarchical inference. It is acknowledged that precision-weighted Hebbian plasticity is a promising new research direction [Salvatori et al., 2026]. To our knowledge, no existing deep PC network endogenously infers precisions online as posterior beliefs coupled to the rest of the hierarchy, and no systematic empirical comparison has been made between networks with fixed identity precisions and networks whose precisions are themselves the output of a hierarchical inference scheme.

5 结论和局限性5 Conclusion and limitations

我们专注于沿着预测编码的最中心轴建立算法的性能：更新的局部性、全局误差信号的缺失以及支持动态精确推理的贝叶斯机制。我们的比较仅限于保留这些承诺的基线。最近的深度扩展提案，例如 ePC [Goemaere 等人，2025] 和We have focused on establishing the algorithm’s performance along the most central axes to predictive coding: locality of updates, absence of a global error signal, and the Bayesian machinery that enables dynamic precision inference. Our comparison is restricted to baselines preserving these commitments. Recent depth-scaling proposals such as ePC [Goemaere et al., 2025] andµPC [Innocenti et al., 2025] 通过补充机制解决相关问题，其设计选择不在该制度范围内。受控比较将澄清变分推导的保真度与实现缩放的工程选择之间的权衡，但这超出了当前的范围。PC [Innocenti et al., 2025] address related concerns through complementary mechanisms whose design choices lie outside this regime. A controlled comparison would clarify the trade-off between fidelity to the variational derivation and the engineering choices that enable scaling, but this lies beyond the present scope.
