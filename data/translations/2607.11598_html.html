10  结论10  Conclusion

接地是承载变量，它必须保持在循环的两侧：驱动修订的反馈和对其进行评分的指标。推理和采样是内部的，并且达到了信息上限；相互作用逃脱了它，因为接地仪器在每个周期都会导入真实的观察结果，仅受该仪器可以观察到的内容限制。实际教训不是“添加更多循环”Grounding is the load-bearing variable, and it must hold on both sides of the loop: the feedback that drives revision and the metric that scores it. Reasoning and sampling are internal and hit an information ceiling; interaction escapes it because a grounded instrument imports a real observation each cycle, bounded only by what that instrument can observe. The practical lesson is not “add more loops”(swyx and Latent Space, 2026; Runkle, 2026)但是“将你添加的循环接地”：将冻结的模型包装在提议者-审阅者工具中，其工具覆盖了重要的缺陷，并且对于视觉伪影，在审阅者和评分者上测量渲染的 DOM，而不是屏幕截图。最近在自我改进的视觉生成方面取得的许多进展都是由 VLM 评委来衡量的，他们看不到有争议的缺陷；使指标接地是一种廉价的、可部署的修正。接下来有两个方向：超越布局和执行（音频、3D、表格、UI 交互工件）的模式的确定性工具（其中模型判断者同样是盲目的），以及收紧从信息绑定到覆盖率和可实现质量之间的定量关系的内部上限。交互缩放是真实的，不同于推理和采样，并且可以从覆盖范围中预测；一旦你确定了这个指标，它就显而易见了。but “ground the loop you add”: wrap a frozen model in a proposer–reviewer harness whose instrument covers the defects that matter, and for visual artifacts measure the rendered DOM, never a screenshot, on both the reviewer and the scorer. Much recent progress on self-improving visual generation is measured with VLM judges that cannot see the defects at issue; grounding the metric is a cheap, deployable correction. Two directions follow: deterministic instruments for modalities beyond layout and execution (audio, 3D, tabular, UI-interaction artifacts), where model judges are likewise blind, and tightening the internal ceiling from an information bound into a quantitative relationship between coverage and achievable quality. Interaction scaling is real, distinct from reasoning and sampling, and predictable from coverage; once you ground the metric, it is plainly visible.
