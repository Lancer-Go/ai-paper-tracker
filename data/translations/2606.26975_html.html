XMSE 感知自适应经验贝叶斯估计XMSE-Aware Adaptive Empirical Bayes Estimation

Minghao Chen and Jiale Zheng M. Chen is with Tencent Technology, Shenzhen, China (e-mail: monychen@tencent.com).J. Zheng is with Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab, Shenzhen, China (e-mail: zhengjiale2@huawei.com).
摘要Abstract

经验贝叶斯 (EB) 估计量可以匹配一阶渐近风险 最大似然 (ML)，但在二阶时表现非常不同： 最近的超额均方误差 (XMSE) 分析表明，基于内核的 EB 当内核与模型对齐不佳时，估计可能比 ML 更差 真参数。本文将这种诊断转化为设计原则。 我们提出了一种 XMSE 感知的混合估计器，可以在 ML 和 EB 之间进行插值 收缩。其固定权重 XMSE 是一个标量二次方程，产生 封闭式预言机混合权重，不比 ML 和基础差 XMSE 尺度的 EB 估计器。一个基于插件的实现 有限样本 XMSE 近似被证明是一致的，具有二阶 内部预言机权重的预言机后悔率。我们进一步建立一个 将遗憾绑定到评估的固定权重风险曲线上 选定的权重、阈值边界规则以及紧凑内核的扩展 族以及具有高概率的有限且不断增长的内核字典 预言机边界。 有限脉冲响应模拟，具有 SURE 调谐、硬选择和 跟踪校正基线，以及公共 Silverbox 和 Cascaded 坦克基准测试表明，所提出的估计器保留了大部分 当正则化有帮助时，它会带来好处，并且在以下情况下会退回到机器学习 内核错误指定，具有已识别的有限样本校准 根据基准分析故障模式。Empirical Bayes (EB) estimators can match the first-order asymptotic risk of maximum likelihood (ML) while behaving very differently at second order: recent excess mean squared error (XMSE) analysis shows that kernel-based EB estimation may be worse than ML when the kernel is poorly aligned with the true parameter. This paper turns that diagnostic into a design principle. We propose an XMSE-aware mixed estimator that interpolates between ML and EB shrinkage. Its fixed-weight XMSE is a scalar quadratic, yielding a closed-form oracle mixing weight that is no worse than both ML and the base EB estimator at the XMSE scale. A plug-in implementation based on finite-sample XMSE approximations is proved consistent, with a second-order oracle regret rate for an interior oracle weight. We further establish a transfer of the regret bound to the fixed-weight risk curve evaluated at the selected weight, a thresholded boundary rule, and extensions to compact kernel families and to finite and growing kernel dictionaries with high-probability oracle bounds. Finite impulse response simulations with SURE-tuned, hard-selection, and trace-corrected baselines, together with the public Silverbox and Cascaded Tanks benchmarks, show that the proposed estimator retains most of the benefit of regularization when it is helpful and retreats toward ML under kernel misspecification, with an identified finite-sample calibration failure mode analyzed on the benchmarks.

I 简介Introduction

经验贝叶斯 (EB) 和基于核的正则化估计器广泛应用于 线性回归和系统辨识Empirical Bayes (EB) and kernel-based regularized estimators are widely used in linear regression and system identification[1, 2, 3]。他们的一阶渐近 MSE 经常与此一致 最大似然 (ML) 估计量，因此一阶理论无法解释 当正规化有帮助或有害时。 XMSE 框架解决了这个问题 通过研究二阶差分来限制. Their first-order asymptotic MSE often coincides with that of the maximum likelihood (ML) estimator, so first-order theory cannot explain when regularization helps or hurts. The XMSE framework addresses this limitation by studying the second-order difference

XMSE​(θ^)=limN→∞N2​[MSE​(θ^)−MSE​(θ^ML)].\mathrm{XMSE}(\widehat{\theta})=\lim_{N\to\infty}N^{2}\left[\mathrm{MSE}(\widehat{\theta})-\mathrm{MSE}(\widehat{\theta}_{\mathrm{ML}})\right].

现有的 XMSE 分析主要是诊断性的：它分解了 EB 风险缺口 并将内核参数错位识别为正 XMSE 的来源。 本文将诊断转化为设计原则。Existing XMSE analysis is primarily diagnostic: it decomposes the EB risk gap and identifies kernel-parameter misalignment as a source of positive XMSE. This paper turns the diagnostic into a design principle.

X 结论Conclusion

XMSE 不仅是解释经验贝叶斯失败的诊断工具。它 也可以作为自适应估计器的建设性设计标准。的 这里研究的混合估计器具有二阶标量预言规则 在 XMSE 尺度上优于 ML 和基本 EB 估计器。其 插件版本由一致性支持，边界鲁棒的预言机 不平等，预言后悔率，以及将该比率转移到 以选定权重评估的固定权重风险曲线，以及 原始插件路线扩展到紧凑的多参数内核系列。 同样的标准也自然地适用于有限且不断增长的候选人 具有高概率和亚高斯预言保证的字典。的 结果是一个简单的保护措施：当 当正则化器有害时，内核是合适的，并且会退回到 ML。XMSE is not only a diagnostic tool for explaining empirical Bayes failures. It can also serve as a constructive design criterion for adaptive estimators. The mixed estimator studied here has a scalar oracle rule with second-order dominance over both ML and the base EB estimator at the XMSE scale. Its plug-in version is supported by consistency, a boundary-robust oracle inequality, an oracle-regret rate, and a transfer of that rate to the fixed-weight risk curve evaluated at the selected weight, and the primitive plug-in route extends to compact multi-parameter kernel families. The same criterion also extends naturally to finite and growing candidate dictionaries with high-probability and sub-Gaussian oracle guarantees. The result is a simple safeguard: it preserves useful regularization when the kernel is appropriate and retreats toward ML when the regularizer is harmful.
