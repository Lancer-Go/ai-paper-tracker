1  简介1  Introduction

合成后训练管道过滤生成的样本 法学硕士作为微调之前的质量关口。法官在这样的环境下 通常遵循包括帮助、指导在内的偏好标准 遵循和诚实Synthetic post-training pipelines filter generated samples with LLM-as-judge quality gates before fine-tuning. Judges in this setting typically follow preference rubrics covering helpfulness, instruction following, and truthfulness[3]，但是这些 量规不验证生成的响应是否受支持 引发它的源通道。源头扎根的评估 G-Eval 等框架, but these rubrics do not verify whether a generated response is supported by the source passage that induced it. Source-grounded evaluation frameworks such as G-Eval[13]和检查评估and CheckEval[12]直接从来源得出他们的标准 块并提供更有针对性的忠诚信号，但没有一个 主要的综合数据管理库，包括 distilabelderive their criteria directly from a source chunk and offer a more targeted faithfulness signal, yet none of the major synthetic data curation libraries, including distilabel[1]和代理指示and AgentInstruct[15], 保留 将每个生成的样本链接回的明确来源记录 其生成源。如果没有这个记录，忠诚度门控必须 对事后检索到的证据进行操作，这近似于 比观察原始证据关系。, preserve an explicit provenance record linking each generated sample back to its generation source. Without this record, faithfulness gating must operate on post-hoc retrieved evidence, which approximates rather than observes the original evidence relation.

第二个差距涉及被拒绝的样品。现有管道处理 拒绝作为终点，丢弃失败并花费一代 新鲜样品的预算。拒绝抽样文献，包括 RAFTA second gap concerns rejected samples. Existing pipelines treat rejection as terminal, discarding failures and spending generation budget on fresh samples. The rejection sampling literature, including RAFT[4]和休息and ReSTEM [19]，表明从相同的提示重新生成 在质量过滤器下大大提高了产量。这些方法 单一全局下的目标迭代模型自我完善 然而，验收标准以及是否存在结构性故障 诊断和有针对性的修复比单纯的治疗提供了有意义的收益 以源头为基础的策展环境中的再生是一种开放的 问题。, shows that regenerating from the same prompt under a quality filter substantially improves yield. These methods target iterative model self-improvement under a single global acceptance criterion, however, and whether structured failure diagnosis and targeted repair offers meaningful gains over naive regeneration in a source-grounded curation setting is an open question.

我们通过受控消融、变化门来研究这两个问题 配置、恢复策略和保持时的发电机规模 语料库和法官家族已修复。我们发现精确的出处门控 优于仅奖励过滤和事后检索证据 关于忠诚度检测，幻觉和奖励门拒绝 很大程度上是不相交的失败群体，因此都是必要的，并且 自适应诊断和修复优于幼稚再生 产率、回收率和注射召回率。下游微调 质量主要由发电机规模、过滤和 恢复条件的贡献是有意义的，但是次要的。We study both problems in a controlled ablation, varying gate configuration, recovery strategy, and generator scale while holding corpus and judge family fixed. We find that exact-provenance gating outperforms both reward-only filtering and post-hoc retrieved evidence on faithfulness detection, that hallucination and reward gates reject largely disjoint failure populations and are thus both necessary, and that adaptive diagnose-and-repair outperforms naive regeneration on yield, recovery rate, and injection recall. Downstream fine-tuning quality is driven primarily by generator scale, with filtration and recovery conditions contributing meaningfully but secondarily.
