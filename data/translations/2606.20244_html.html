SPOT-E：针对冻结 VLM 的视觉聚光灯测试时熵整形SPOT-E: Test-Time Entropy Shaping with Visual Spotlights for Frozen VLMs

摘要Abstract

视觉语言模型（VLM）通常在证据密集型任务中表现不佳，因为决定性的视觉证据很小、局部化且容易被忽视，即使高级推理完好无损，也会导致证据读出失败。 先前的推理时间视觉干预可以在无需重新训练的情况下改善基础，但它们在很大程度上是开环的，并且缺乏验证突出证据是否实际使用的机制。 我们研究答案跨度预测熵作为模型内部反馈信号，并表明朴素熵最小化是不明确的，因为低熵可能来自基于证据的置信度或捷径崩溃。 为了解决这种模糊性，我们引入了低熵锚和熵整形目标，可以减少答案的不确定性，同时保留基线高置信度标记。 我们在 SPOT-E 中实例化了这一原理，这是一种即插即用的测试时间方法，可生成问题条件聚光灯，并通过基于组相对策略优化 (GRPO) 的轻量级调整对每个实例进行优化。 在所有基准测试和不同的 VLM 系列中，SPOT-E 都能产生一致的增益并提高视觉损坏情况下的鲁棒性。 代码公开于：Vision-language models (VLMs) often underperform on evidence intensive tasks because decisive visual evidence are small, localized, and easy to overlook, leading to failures in evidence readout even when high-level reasoning is intact. Prior inference-time visual interventions can improve grounding without retraining, but they are largely open-loop and lack a mechanism to verify whether highlighted evidence is actually used. We study answer-span prediction entropy as a model-internal feedback signal and show that naive entropy minimization is ambiguous, since low entropy may arise from evidence-grounded confidence or shortcut collapse. To resolve this ambiguity, we introduce low-entropy anchors and an entropy-shaping objective that reduces answer uncertainty while preserving baseline high-confidence tokens. We instantiate this principle in SPOT-E, a plug-and-play test-time method that produces question-conditioned spotlights, optimized per instance via light-weight tuning based on Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). Across all benchmarks and different VLM families, SPOT-E yields consistent gains and improved robustness under visual corruptions. Code is publicly available at:https://github.com/YinBo0927/SPOT-E

1 简介Introduction

视觉语言模型在多模态理解方面取得了快速进展，但它们在图表阅读和文档解析等证据密集型任务上仍然不可靠Vision-language models have made rapid progress in multimodal understanding, yet they remain unreliable on evidence-intensive tasks such as chart reading and document parsing[liu2023visual, masry2022chartqa, mathew2021docvqa]。 在这些情况下，决定性的证据往往很小并且是局部的。 模型可能描述了正确的推理计划，例如它可能会说“读取 y 轴值，然后比较两个条形”，但仍然误读了底层数字，导致最终答案失败. In these settings, the decisive evidence are often small and localized. A model may describe a correct reasoning plan, for example it may say “read the y-axis value and then compare the two bars”, but still misread the underlying number, causing the final answer to fail[lu2023mathvista, zhang2023multimodal, guan2024hallusionbench]。 这种模式暴露了一个瓶颈，我们称之为证据利用差距：模型可以推理需要什么证据，但无法可靠地提取和关注决定答案的细粒度视觉证据. This pattern exposes a bottleneck that we call the evidence utilization gap: the model can reason about what evidence is needed, but cannot reliably extract and focus on the fine-grained visual evidence that determines the answer[tong2024eyes]。 在不进行昂贵的再培训或针对特定任务的注释的情况下，缩小已冻结、已部署的 VLM 的这一差距实际上是紧迫的，并且在方法上具有挑战性. Closing this gap for frozen, already-deployed VLMs, without costly retraining or task-specific annotation, is practically urgent and methodologically challenging[yin2024woodpecker]. 我们如何在保持主干网络冻结的同时提高推理时的证据利用率？How can we improve evidence utilization at inference time while keeping the backbone frozen?

一个自然的方向是在测试时干预视觉输入，以便模型更容易访问决定性区域A natural direction is to intervene on the visual input at test time so that decisive regions become more accessible to the model[yang2023set, zhang2023visual, yin2025fera, snell2024scaling, brown2024large]。 FGVP 等方法实例化了这一想法并在不修改模型权重的情况下报告增益. Methods such as FGVP instantiate this idea and report gains without modifying model weights[yang2023fine, yu2026dual]。 然而，这些方法是开环的。 他们应用了固定的干预措施，但没有提供一种机制来验证模型是否确实依赖于所强调的证据。 当选定的区域错过了决定性的证据时，或者当干预降低了证据本身时，该方法仍然看不到失败，因此无法纠正。 这就提出了一个关键问题：. However, these approaches are open-loop. They apply a fixed intervention but do not provide a mechanism to verify whether the model actually relied on the emphasized evidence. When the selected region misses the decisive evidence, or when the intervention degrades the evidence itself, the failure remains invisible to the method and therefore cannot be corrected. This raises a key question:我们如何设计一个既能适应实例又能自我验证的测试时干预？how can we design a test-time intervention that is both instance-adaptive and self-verifying?

2.1 推理时视觉干预和视觉提示Inference-Time Visual Interventions and Visual Prompting

推理时视觉干预通过操纵测试时呈现给冻结 VLM 的视觉证据来改善基础Inference-time visual interventions improve grounding by manipulating the visual evidence presented to a frozen VLM at test time[bar2022visual, chen2023understanding]。 常见的方法包括覆盖标记或蒙版以突出显示区域（如 FGVP 和标记集提示）、有选择地裁剪或放大候选区域以保留小细节（如 ViCrop）以及将分辨率重新分配给查询相关证据的空间转换（如 AttWarp）. Common approaches include overlaying marks or masks to highlight regions, as in FGVP and Set-of-Mark prompting, selectively cropping or zooming into candidate areas to preserve small details, as in ViCrop, and spatial transformations that reallocate resolution toward query-relevant evidence, as in AttWarp[yang2023fine, yang2023set, zhang2023visual, dalal2025constructive]。 另一个实用方向使用注意力或分数引导的提示策略，例如 API 式提示，将模型引导至信息丰富的区域，而无需重新训练. Another practical direction uses attention- or score-guided prompting strategies such as API-style prompting to steer the model toward informative regions without retraining[yu2024attention]。 这些方法很有吸引力，因为它们是轻量级的且与模型无关，但它们通常依赖于固定启发法、外部区域建议或可能在实例之间脆弱的离散选择。 我们的方法适合这种范式，并使用问题条件聚光灯在冻结的骨干下进行自适应证据强调。. These methods are appealing because they are lightweight and model-agnostic, but they often depend on fixed heuristics, external region proposals, or discrete choices that may be brittle across instances. Our method fits this paradigm and uses a question-conditioned spotlight for adaptive evidence emphasis under a frozen backbone.

2.2 证据定位的熵和不确定性Entropy and Uncertainty for Evidence Localization

不确定性信号长期以来一直被用来在推理时诊断和引导模型行为Uncertainty signals have long been used to diagnose and steer model behavior at inference time[kadavath2022language, hendrycks2016baseline]。 输出分布的熵是置信度的常见代理，并已用于校准和选择性预测、自洽和重新排序，以及熵和置信驱动的解码启发式方法. Entropy over output distributions is a common proxy for confidence and has been used for calibration and selective prediction, self-consistency and re-ranking, as well as entropy- and confidence-driven decoding heuristics[guo2017calibration, wang2022self, kuhn2023semantic]。 在视觉和多模态推理中，不确定性也与证据本地化密切相关，因为细粒度基础的失败通常表现为集中在一小部分答案标记上的高度不确定性. In vision and multimodal reasoning, uncertainty is also closely tied to evidence localization, since failures in fine-grained grounding often appear as high uncertainty concentrated on a small set of answer tokens[li2023evaluating, huang2024opera]。 最近的多模式工作进一步利用熵或置信度来触发额外的感知、指导区域选择并过滤视觉上不受支持的世代. Recent multimodal work further leverages entropy or confidence to trigger additional perception, guide region selection, and filter visually unsupported generations[yin2024woodpecker, yin2023large]。 我们的方法将熵塑造奖励与问题条件视觉聚光相结合，以鼓励果断的、视觉支持的答案，而无需更新冻结的 VLM。. Our method combines an entropy-shaping reward with a question-conditioned visual spotlight to encourage decisive, visually supported answers without updating the frozen VLM.

3 动机Motivation

在本节中，我们将提出 VLM 中视觉适应的以熵为中心的观点。 当决定性的视觉证据可用时，模型会更加一致地致力于单个最终答案，使答案跨度熵成为证据使用的代理。 我们表明，这种效应是空间基础的，并且可以随着决定性区域的可见性而急剧变化。 然而，熵减少是不明确的，也可能反映了压制确凿证据的捷径行为，产生自信但错误的预测。 为了解决这个问题，我们引入了低熵锚点和熵整形原理，可以在保持锚点稳定性的同时降低答案熵。In this section, we motivate an entropy-centric view of visual adaptation in VLMs. When decisive visual evidence is usable, the model commits more consistently to a single final answer, making answer-span entropy a proxy for evidence use. We show that this effect is spatially grounded and can change sharply with the visibility of the decisive region. However, entropy reduction is ambiguous and may also reflect shortcut behaviors that suppress hard evidence, yielding confident-but-wrong predictions. To resolve this, we introduce low-entropy anchors and an entropy-shaping principle that reduces answer entropy while preserving anchor stability.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 SPOT-E，一种即插即用的测试时间方法，通过对问题条件视觉聚光灯模块进行轻量级的按实例调整，增强了冻结 VLM 中细粒度视觉证据的利用。 在不同的主干和基准中，SPOT-E 在视觉损坏下提供一致的增益和更强的鲁棒性，而无需重新训练基本模型，并分析突出极小或本质上模糊的证据上剩余的失败。We present SPOT-E, a plug-and-play test-time method that strengthens fine-grained visual evidence utilization in frozen VLMs via lightweight per-instance adaptation of a question-conditioned visual spotlight module. Across diverse backbones and benchmarks, SPOT-E delivers consistent gains and stronger robustness under visual corruptions without retraining the base model, and analyses highlight remaining failures on extremely small or inherently ambiguous evidence.
