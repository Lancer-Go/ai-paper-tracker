摘要Abstract

基于大语言模型（LLM）的代理在组合泛化方面表现出系统性失败，限制了它们在交互环境中的鲁棒性。这项工作引入了 AGEL-Comp，这是一种神经符号 AI 代理架构，旨在通过代理的基础操作来应对这一挑战。 AGEL-Comp 集成了三个核心创新：（1）动态因果程序图（CPG）作为世界模型，将程序和因果知识表示为有向超图； (2) 归纳逻辑编程 (ILP) 引擎，可根据经验反馈合成新的 Horn 子句，通过交互奠定符号知识的基础； (3) 混合推理核心，其中法学硕士提出一组候选子目标，并由神经定理证明器 (NTP) 验证这些子目标的逻辑一致性。这些组件共同实现演绎-溯因学习循环：使智能体能够演绎计划并溯因扩展其符号世界模型，而神经适应阶段使其推理引擎与新知识保持一致。 我们提出了一个评估协议Large Language Model (LLM)-based agents exhibit systemic failures in compositional generalization, limiting their robustness in interactive environments. This work introduces AGEL-Comp, a neuro-symbolic AI agent architecture designed to address this challenge by grounding actions of the agent. AGEL-Comp integrates three core innovations: (1) a dynamic Causal Program Graph (CPG) as a world model, representing procedural and causal knowledge as a directed hypergraph; (2) an Inductive Logic Programming (ILP) engine that synthesizes new Horn clauses from experiential feedback, grounding symbolic knowledge through interaction; and (3) a hybrid reasoning core where an LLM proposes a set of candidate sub-goals that are verified for logical consistency by a Neural Theorem Prover (NTP). Together, these components operationalize a deduction–abduction learning cycle: enabling the agent to deduce plans and abductively expand its symbolic world model, while a neural adaptation phase keeps its reasoning engine aligned with new knowledge. We propose an evaluation protocol within the复古探索Retro Quest模拟环境来探测组合泛化场景以评估我们的 AGEL 代理。我们的研究结果清楚地表明，我们的 AGEL 模型比纯粹基于 LLM 的模型具有更好的性能。我们的框架为智能体提供了一条原则性的路径，可以对其世界建立明确的、可解释的、组合结构的理解。simulation environment to probe for compositional generalization scenarios to evaluate our AGEL agent. Our findings clearly indicate the better performance of our AGEL model over pure LLM-based models. Our framework presents a principled path toward agents that build an explicit, interpretable, and compositionally structured understanding of their world.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在广泛的自然语言任务中表现出了卓越的能力Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities across a wide range of natural language tasks[3, 26]。然而，随着这些模型越来越多地被部署为交互式代理的认知核心，一个基本的限制变得明显：组合泛化的失败——从已知的原始组件理解和产生新组合的能力. However, as these models are increasingly deployed as the cognitive core for interactive agents, a fundamental limitation becomes apparent: a failure in compositional generalization—the ability to understand and produce novel combinations from known, primitive components[11, 21]。 这种“组合性危机”是实现类人智能的一个关键障碍，因为它揭示了对统计模式匹配的依赖，而不是对世界的结构化、系统性的理解. This “compositionality crisis” is a critical barrier to achieving human-like intelligence, as it reveals a reliance on statistical pattern matching rather than a structured, systematic understanding of the world[1, 5, 8]。这种脆弱性的出现是因为模型的知识本质上是脱离实体的，并且脱离了结构化的因果世界。他们的理解缺乏经验基础，缺乏抽象符号与其现实世界所指对象之间的联系、相互作用以及控制概念如何系统组合的后果。 当前的模型，包括最先进的法学硕士，一直在应对这些挑战，限制了它们在开放式交互式环境中的稳健性和适应性. This brittleness arises because the models’ knowledge is inherently disembodied and detached from the structured, causal world. Their understanding lacks empirical grounding, the connection between abstract symbols and their real-world referents, interactions, and consequences that govern how concepts systematically combine. Current models, including state-of-the-art LLMs, consistently struggle with these challenges, limiting their robustness and adaptability in open-ended, interactive environments[7, 9].

为了弥补这一差距，我们建议To address this gap, we proposeAGEL-Comp（以行动为基础的组合性体验学习），一种新型的神经符号框架，用于 LLM 驱动的代理，明确设计用于促进组合推理。 我们认为，交互环境中的真正泛化不能仅通过神经缩放来实现，而是需要一种能够学习、表示和推理明确的结构化知识的架构。通过使代理能够在交互式环境中学习其行为的后果，我们可以通过捕获其环境的组成“语法”的显式世界模型来增强其预先存在的知识。(Action-Grounded Experiential Learning for Compositionality), a novel neuro-symbolic framework for LLM-powered agents designed explicitly to foster compositional reasoning. We argue that true generalization in interactive settings cannot be achieved through neural scaling alone but requires an architecture that can learn, represent, and reason over explicit, structured knowledge. By enabling an agent to learn from the consequences of its actions in an interactive environment, we can augment its pre-existing knowledge with an explicit world model that captures the compositional “grammar” of its environment.

AGEL-Comp 通过三个核心组件将神经模型的灵活性与符号系统的严谨性相结合：（1）动态因果程序图（CPG）作为其世界模型AGEL-Comp integrates the flexibility of neural models with the rigor of symbolic systems through three core components: (1) a dynamic Causal Program Graph (CPG) as its world modelWW，以模块化、分层结构表示程序和因果知识，有利于复杂的规划, representing procedural and causal knowledge in a modular, hierarchical structure conducive to complex planning[4, 27, 28]; (2) 归纳逻辑编程（ILP）引擎，; (2) an Inductive Logic Programming (ILP) engine,𝒢\mathcal{G}，从稀疏的经验反馈中合成新的、可概括的子程序（Horn 子句），为通过交互奠定符号知识提供了正式的机制, that synthesizes new, generalizable sub-programs (Horn clauses) from sparse experiential feedback, providing a formal mechanism for grounding symbolic knowledge through interaction[6, 16]; (3) 混合推理核心，其中法学硕士充当高级规划者，其建议由神经定理证明器 (NTP) 验证逻辑一致性; (3) a hybrid reasoning core where an LLM acts as a high-level planner, whose proposals are verified for logical consistency by a Neural Theorem Prover (NTP)[15, 14, 20].

通过根据扎实的经验构建这个模型，智能体可以超越浅薄的统计模式匹配，转向系统的、基于规则的推理。虽然之前的工作重点是将法学硕士集成到模拟环境中以完成任务，但我们的主要贡献是设计和评估明确针对组合泛化的协同架构。By building this model from grounded experience, the agent can move beyond shallow statistical pattern matching to systematic, rule-based reasoning. While prior work has focused on integrating LLMs into simulated environments for task completion, our primary contribution is the design and evaluation of a synergistic architecture that explicitly targets compositional generalization.

实体主体面临的主要挑战是超越统计相关性来理解因果关系。因果图模型通常表示为有向无环图 (DAG)，是用于此目的的正式工具A primary challenge for embodied agents is moving beyond statistical correlation to understand cause-and-effect. Causal graphical models, often represented as directed acyclic graphs (DAGs), are a formal tool for this purpose[4, 18, 27, 28]。这种结构超越了单纯的相关性，能够进行更深入的推理，例如预测干预措施的结果和考虑反事实. This structure moves beyond mere correlation to enable deeper reasoning, such as predicting the outcomes of interventions and contemplating counterfactuals[10, 19]。我们的工作通过引入因果程序图（CPG）从这一范式中汲取灵感，该图是专门为表示代理的程序和操作知识而设计的。. Our work draws inspiration from this paradigm by introducing the Causal Program Graph (CPG), which is specifically designed to represent an agent’s procedural and operational knowledge.

2.3 神经定理证明器 (NTP)Neural Theorem Provers (NTP)

NTPs are valued for their ability to bridge symbolic reasoning with neural representation learning, providing interpretability and strong systematic generalization properties[25]。虽然计算量很大，但它们的可扩展性是一个活跃的研究领域. An LLM planner is prone to generating plausible but logically unsound steps. We employ an NTP as a formal verifier to enforce compositional discipline. This is a more rigorous approach than simply prompting an LLM to “check its own work,” as the NTP provides a provable guarantee of a plan’s validity with respect to the agent’s learned world model.
