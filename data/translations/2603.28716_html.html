摘要Abstract

代理强化学习（RL）可以从可重用的经验中受益匪浅，但现有的基于技能的方法主要提取轨迹级指导，并且通常缺乏维持不断发展的技能记忆的原则性机制。我们建议Agentic reinforcement learning (RL) can benefit substantially from reusable experience, yet existing skill-based methods mainly extract trajectory-level guidance and often lack principled mechanisms for maintaining an evolving skill memory. We proposeD2技能D2Skill, 一个, a动态双粒度技能库dynamic dual-granularity skill bank用于将可重用经验组织成的代理强化学习for agentic RL that organizes reusable experience into任务技能task skills以获得高层指导和for high-level guidance and脚步技巧step skills用于细粒度的决策支持和错误纠正。 D2Skill 通过在同一策略下配对基线和技能注入部署来联合训练策略和技能库，利用它们的性能差距来得出用于技能更新和策略优化的事后效用信号。技能库完全根据培训时的经验构建，通过反思不断扩展，并通过实用感知检索和修剪进行维护。实验for fine-grained decision support and error correction. D2Skill jointly trains the policy and skill bank through paired baseline and skill-injected rollouts under the same policy, using their performance gap to derive hindsight utility signals for both skill updating and policy optimization. Built entirely from training-time experience, the skill bank is continuously expanded through reflection and maintained with utility-aware retrieval and pruning. 1 简介Introduction

代理强化学习（RL）最近成为一种有前景的范式，用于训练基于语言的代理来解决长期决策任务，包括交互式环境Agentic reinforcement learning (RL) has recently emerged as a promising paradigm for training language-based agents to solve long-horizon decision-making tasks, including interactive environments(Jiang et al., 2026), 网络搜索, web search(Zhang et al., 2025c)，以及研究场景, and research scenarios(Tu et al., 2026)。 在这些设置中，策略通过文本界面与环境交互，并根据任务描述以及过去观察和操作的有限历史来选择操作。 然而，这种基于历史的背景通常不足以对基础状态进行统计，导致严重的部分可观察性，并且随着决策范围的增长，信用分配变得越来越困难. In these settings, the policy interacts with the environment through a textual interface and selects actions based on the task description together with a limited history of past observations and actions. However, such history-based context is generally not a sufficient statistic of the underlying state, resulting in severe partial observability and making credit assignment increasingly difficult as the decision horizon grows(Zhang et al., 2025b)。 在奖励稀疏和行动空间大的情况下，孤立地学习每个任务的效率非常低. Under sparse rewards and large action spaces, learning each task in isolation is highly inefficient(Feng et al., 2025)。 因此，有效的政策需要积累可跨任务转移的可重用知识的机制。. Effective policies therefore require mechanisms for accumulating reusable knowledge that can be transferred across tasks.

最近的研究通过为代理强化学习引入额外的监督信号来缓解这些挑战。 一些方法采用基于结果的信用分配来提供过程奖励Recent studies alleviate these challenges by introducing additional supervision signals for agentic RL. Some methods employ outcome-based credit assignment to provide process rewards(Feng et al., 2025)，而其他人则从完成的轨迹中获得事后监督, while others derive hindsight supervision from completed trajectories(Yu et al., 2025)。 最近的工作重点是使智能体能够积累跨任务的经验，并在训练期间迭代地完善它. More recent work focuses on enabling agents to accumulate experience across tasks and iteratively refine it during training(Zhai et al., 2025; Cai et al., 2025b; a)。 在此范围内，可重用技能已成为过去经验的有效形式，并在代理强化学习中显示出强大的经验收益. Within this line, reusable skills have emerged as an effective form of past experience and shown strong empirical gains in agentic RL(Wang et al., 2026)。 例如，技能RL. For instance, SkillRL(Xia et al., 2026)从过去的轨迹中建立技能库，并检索相关技能来指导政策互动，提高长期任务的探索效率。builds a skill bank from past trajectories and retrieves relevant skills to guide policy interaction, improving exploration efficiency in long-horizon tasks.

然而，现有的基于技能和反思驱动的框架在两个方面仍然受到限制。 大多数方法从完整的轨迹中获得技能，并强调任务级反思，它捕获高级指导，但对于纠正单个交互步骤中的细粒度错误不太有效However, existing skill-based and reflection-driven frameworks remain limited in two respects. Most methods derive skills from complete trajectories and emphasize task-level reflection, which captures high-level guidance but is less effective for correcting fine-grained errors at individual interaction steps(Xia et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026a)。 此外，随着培训的进展，技能库不断扩大，使得检索和管理变得越来越具有挑战性。 如果没有原则性的技能评估和修剪机制，多余或无效的技能可能会降低检索到的指导并阻碍政策优化. In addition, as training progresses, the skill bank expands continuously, making retrieval and management increasingly challenging. Without principled mechanisms for skill evaluation and pruning, redundant or ineffective skills can degrade retrieved guidance and hinder policy optimization(Zhou et al., 2025; 2026).

3.1 总体框架Overall Framework

通过技能注入进行 RL 训练。 （节RL training with skill injection. (Section3.2)

在训练期间，轨迹按组进行采样，其中包括 在同一政策下进行基线部署和技能注入部署。 将检索到的技能注入政策环境中以指导决策， 两组之间的绩效差距用于构建事后诸葛亮 GRPO 下政策优化和技能实用程序更新的信号 客观。During training, trajectories are sampled in groups that include both baseline rollouts and skill-injected rollouts under the same policy. Retrieved skills are injected into the policy context to guide decision making, and the performance gap between the two groups is used to construct hindsight signals for policy optimization and skill utility updates under the GRPO objective.

反思驱动的技能生成。 （节Reflection-driven skill generation. (Section3.3)

当任务组的性能低于阈值时，反射模块 分析代表性轨迹以产生新的可重用技能。 生成的技能与检索键相关联并插入到 标准化和重复数据删除后的技能库，使座席能够 在培训期间积累跨任务的经验。When performance on a task group falls below a threshold, a reflection module analyzes representative trajectories to produce new reusable skills. Generated skills are associated with retrieval keys and inserted into the skill bank after normalization and deduplication, allowing the agent to accumulate experience across tasks during training.

技能检索和银行管理。 （节Skill retrieval and bank management. (Section3.3)

Skill utilities are updated online according to rollout outcomes, and the skill bank is periodically pruned using utility-based criteria to maintain a bounded memory while preserving effective skills.

5.1 代理进化和内存管理Agent Evolution and Memory Management

最近的工作越来越多地研究代理进化，以解决法学硕士训练后适应性有限的问题。该系列的核心机制是外部存储器，它支持参数更新之外的持续适应Recent work has increasingly studied agent evolution to address the limited post-training adaptability of LLMs. A central mechanism in this line is external memory, which supports continual adaptation beyond parameter updates(Zhang et al., 2024; Gao et al., 2025; Du, 2026)。现有研究从多个角度探索长期记忆的演变，包括保留和遗忘. Existing studies explore evolving long-term memory from multiple perspectives, including retention and forgetting(Chhikara et al., 2025)、结构化更新和组织, structured updating and organization(Xu et al., 2025; Yan et al., 2025), 检索感知优化, retrieval-aware optimization(Zhou et al., 2025)，以及分层或生成记忆构建, and hierarchical or generative memory construction(Zhang et al., 2025a)。除了存储交互历史之外，另一项工作是将经验抽象为可重用的知识，例如推理策略. Beyond storing interaction history, another line of work abstracts experience into reusable knowledge, such as reasoning strategies(Zhao et al., 2024; Ouyang et al., 2025), 可重复使用的工作流程, reusable workflows(Wang et al., 2024)、分层经验库, hierarchical experience libraries(Yang et al., 2025b)，并不断完善经验, and continual experience refinement(Cai et al., 2025b)。总的来说，这些研究表明智能体的进化越来越依赖于结构化、可重用的记忆以及有效的检索和管理。. Overall, these studies suggest that agent evolution increasingly relies on structured, reusable memory with effective retrieval and management.

5.2 记忆增强代理强化学习Memory-augmented Agentic RL

内存作为强化学习的非参数补充，通过在外部内存中存储有用的成功或失败经验并将其检索到策略上下文中以提高代理性能Memory serves as a non-parametric complement to RL by storing useful successful or failed experiences in external memory and retrieving them into the policy context to improve agent performance(Liu et al., 2026b; Zhou et al., 2026)。通过将参数化 RL 更新与不断发展的经验存储库相结合，最新的方法使 LLM 代理能够积累模型权重之外的可重用知识，从而改善推理.
