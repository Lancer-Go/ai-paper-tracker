摘要Abstract

讽刺是一种通过夸张、讽刺或比较来表达批评或强调某些个人或情况特征的修辞手段。现有的中文讽刺检测方法受到数据集有限和建设成本高的限制。他们主要关注文本特征，忽略了影响意见和情感表达方式的用户特定的语言模式。本文提出了一种生成对抗网络（GAN）和大型语言模型（LLM）驱动的数据增强框架，以动态建模用户的语言模式，以增强中文讽刺检测。首先，我们收集了新浪微博上各个主题的原始数据。然后，我们在这些数据上训练 GAN，并应用基于 GPT-3.5 的数据增强技术来合成扩展的讽刺评论数据集，名为 SinaSarc。该数据集包含目标评论、上下文信息和用户历史行为。最后，我们扩展了 BERT 架构，以纳入多维信息，特别是用户历史行为，使模型能够捕获动态语言模式并发现评论中隐含的讽刺线索。实验结果证明了我们提出的方法的有效性。具体来说，我们的模型在非讽刺和讽刺类别上都获得了最高的 F1 分数，分别为 0.9138 和 0.9151，这优于所有现有的最先进 (SOTA) 方法。这项研究提出了一种新颖的框架，用于对中文讽刺检测中用户的长期语言模式进行动态建模，有助于该领域的数据集构建和方法论进步。Sarcasm is a rhetorical device that expresses criticism or emphasizes characteristics of certain individuals or situations through exaggeration, irony, or comparison. Existing methods for Chinese sarcasm detection are constrained by limited datasets and high construction costs. And they mainly focus on the textual features, overlooking user-specific linguistic patterns that shape how opinions and emotions are expressed. This paper proposes a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) and Large Language Model (LLM)-driven data augmentation framework to dynamically model users’ linguistic patterns for enhanced Chinese sarcasm detection. First, we collect raw data from various topics on Sina Weibo. Then, we train a GAN on these data and apply a GPT-3.5 based data augmentation technique to synthesize an extended sarcastic comment dataset, named SinaSarc. This dataset contains target comments, contextual information, and user historical behavior. Finally, we extend the BERT architecture to incorporate multi-dimensional information, particularly user historical behavior, enabling the model to capture dynamic linguistic patterns and uncover implicit sarcastic cues in comments. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed method. Specifically, our model achieves the highest F1-scores on both the non-sarcastic and sarcastic categories, with values of 0.9138 and 0.9151 respectively, which outperforms all existing state-of-the-art (SOTA) approaches. This study presents a novel framework for dynamically modeling users’ long-term linguistic patterns in Chinese sarcasm detection, contributing to both dataset construction and methodological advancement in this field.

These methods reduce reliance on manual feature design and achieve substantial success in handling sarcasm’s complex contextual nature.

Due to individual differences in expressive style, the same words may convey different meanings when produced by different users. Therefore, it is necessary to develop methods that can capture and model such long-term linguistic patterns for more accurate sarcasm detection.

Moreover, most datasets focus on target texts and their immediate context, while overlooking user-level information derived from their historical behavior. Such information reflects users’ long-term linguistic patterns and behavioral tendencies, which provide implicit cues for sarcasm detection.

II 相关工作Related Work

近年来，随着情感分析技术的进步和社交媒体的日益普及，讽刺检测的研究越来越受到关注。现有的讽刺检测方法可以分为三种类型：基于规则的方法、机器学习方法和深度学习方法。In recent years, with the advancement of sentiment analysis techniques and the growing popularity of social media, research on sarcasm detection has received increasing attention. Existing sarcasm detection approaches can be categorized into three types: rule-based approaches, machine learning approaches, and deep learning approaches.

V 结论Conclusion

本研究提出了一种由 GAN 和 LLM 驱动的数据增强框架，用于对中文讽刺检测中的用户语言模式进行动态建模，并构建了一个新颖的数据集This study proposes a GAN and LLM-driven data augmentation framework to dynamically model users’ linguistic patterns in Chinese sarcasm detection, and constructs a novel dataset新浪萨尔克SinaSarc。实验结果表明，根据用户的历史行为对用户的语言模式进行建模可以显着提高讽刺检测的准确性和鲁棒性。尽管如此，当前的数据集受到领域限制，生成的样本可能无法完全捕捉自然讽刺表达的微妙之处。未来的工作将侧重于将数据集扩展到更广泛的领域，并结合更丰富的用户和多模式特征。此外，它还可以结合蒙面情感建模的想法. Experimental results demonstrate that modeling users’ linguistic patterns from their historical behavior significantly enhances the accuracy and robustness of sarcasm detection. Nonetheless, the current dataset is domain-constrained, and generated samples may not fully capture the subtlety of natural sarcastic expressions. Future work will focus on extending the dataset to broader domains and incorporating richer user and multimodal features.
