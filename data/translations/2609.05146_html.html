用于早期心血管疾病风险评估的机器学习和深度神经网络的混合预测集成A Hybrid Predictive Ensemble of Machine Learning and Deep Neural Networks for Early Cardiovascular Disease Risk Assessment

Balaji Venkateswaran Affiliation: Independent Researcher, Chennai, India Email: Balaji.Venkateswaran@gmail.com
摘要Abstract

本研究引入了一种集成机器学习和深度神经网络集成技术的智能框架，用于心血管疾病的早期检测和预后。该系统利用从医疗物联网 (IoMT) 设备收集的实时生理数据，包括心电图传感器、心率监测器和血压追踪器。为了确保输入数据的准确性和可靠性，采用了降噪、归一化和缺失值插补等预处理步骤。通过有效的特征选择方法识别最重要的健康指标，然后使用支持向量机 (SVM)、随机森林和极限梯度提升 (XGBoost) 等优化分类器进行处理，这些分类器结合在集成架构中以提高诊断精度。与传统方法相比，该框架在预测心血管疾病风险、实现更高的准确性、减少误报和增强一致性方面表现出卓越的性能。它是在基于云的基础设施上设计的，可确保连续患者监测的可扩展性和实时处理。对现实世界心血管数据集的实验评估证实了该框架在早期风险评估和临床决策支持方面的效率。结果凸显了将传统机器学习和深度学习范式结合起来实现主动医疗管理和改善患者治疗效果的潜力。This study introduces an intelligent framework that integrates machine learning and deep neural network ensemble techniques for early detection and prognosis of cardiovascular diseases. The system utilizes real-time physiological data collected from Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) devices, including ECG sensors, heart rate monitors, and blood pressure trackers. To ensure the accuracy and reliability of input data, preprocessing steps such as noise reduction, normalization, and missing value imputation are employed. The most significant health indicators are identified through effective feature selection methods and then processed using optimized classifiers such as Support Vector Machines (SVM), Random Forests, and eXtreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost), which are combined in an ensemble architecture to improve diagnostic precision. The framework demonstrates remarkable performance in predicting cardiovascular disease risk, achieving higher accuracy, reduced false positives, and enhanced consistency compared to conventional methods. It is designed on a cloud-based infrastructure that ensures scalability and real-time processing for continuous patient monitoring. Experimental evaluation on real-world cardiovascular datasets confirms the framework’s efficiency in early-stage risk assessment and clinical decision support. The results highlight the potential of combining traditional machine learning and deep learning paradigms to achieve proactive healthcare management and improve patient outcomes.

1 简介Introduction

心血管疾病（CVD）已成为全球主要健康问题，每年造成死亡和残疾的比例很大Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) have emerged as a major global health concern, accounting for a significant proportion of deaths and disabilities each year[11]。早期诊断和及时干预对于预防危及生命的并发症至关重要，但传统的诊断程序通常依赖于定期临床评估，而无法捕获实时的生理波动。技术的快速发展，特别是人工智能 (AI). Early diagnosis and timely intervention are critical to preventing life-threatening complications, yet traditional diagnostic procedures often rely on periodic clinical assessments that fail to capture real-time physiological fluctuations. The rapid evolution of technology, particularly in artificial intelligence (AI)[12, 13, 14], 机器学习 (ML), machine learning (ML)[15, 16]、以及医疗物联网 (IoMT), and the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT)[17]，通过实现持续监控、数据驱动决策和预测分析，彻底改变了现代医疗保健的格局。通过可穿戴和植入式传感器，可以无缝收集心率、血压、心电图（ECG）信号等大量生理数据，为智能疾病预测和预后模型提供基础, has revolutionized the landscape of modern healthcare by enabling continuous monitoring, data-driven decision-making, and predictive analysis. Through wearable and implantable sensors, vast amounts of physiological data such as heart rate, blood pressure, and electrocardiogram (ECG) signals can be collected seamlessly, providing a foundation for intelligent disease prediction and prognosis models[18].

这项研究的背景在于数据科学和生物医学工程的融合，传统的统计技术已经发展成为能够处理复杂健康数据的复杂的机器学习和深度学习算法。早期的心脏病检测方法通常依赖于逻辑回归或简单的决策树，这些方法虽然在一定程度上有效，但在处理非线性和高维数据方面受到限制。集成支持向量机 (SVM)、随机森林 (RF) 等先进算法The background of this study lies in the convergence of data science and biomedical engineering, where traditional statistical techniques have evolved into sophisticated machine learning and deep learning algorithms capable of processing complex health data. Earlier approaches to heart disease detection often relied on logistic regression or simple decision trees, which, though effective to a degree, were limited in handling nonlinear and high-dimensional data. With the integration of advanced algorithms such as Support Vector Machines (SVM), Random Forests (RF)[19]，和极限梯度提升（XGBoost），以及深度神经网络，预测准确性显着提高。此外，集成学习已成为一种强大的方法，可以通过结合各个模型的优势来克服各个模型的局限性，从而产生更稳定、更可靠的预测机制, and eXtreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost), along with deep neural networks, predictive accuracy has significantly improved. Furthermore, ensemble learning has emerged as a powerful approach to overcome the limitations of individual models by combining their strengths, resulting in a more stable and reliable prediction mechanism[20]。这些进步不仅提高了诊断精度，而且最大限度地减少了误报，为高效、自动化的心血管风险评估系统铺平了道路。. These advancements have not only enhanced diagnostic precision but also minimized false positives, paving the way for efficient and automated cardiovascular risk assessment systems.

在当今时代，医疗保健生态系统正在从反应性治疗模式转变为人工智能驱动的预测和预防框架。将混合预测模型纳入基于 IoMT 的系统中，甚至可以在临床环境之外持续、实时监测患者的心血管状况。此类系统可以在出现异常情况时向医生或护理人员发出警报，以便立即就医In the present era, the healthcare ecosystem is transitioning from reactive treatment models to predictive and preventive frameworks powered by AI. The incorporation of hybrid predictive models in IoMT-based systems allows continuous, real-time monitoring of patients’ cardiovascular conditions, even outside clinical environments. Such systems can alert physicians or caregivers in case of abnormalities, enabling immediate medical attention[21]。基于云的架构确保了这些系统的可扩展性和互操作性，从而允许安全的数据存储、快速计算以及跨多个设备和医疗保健网络的集成。目前的研究表明，与传统的诊断方法相比，集成框架内的机器学习和深度学习的协同作用实现了卓越的性能，增强了智能系统在医疗决策支持方面的潜力。. Cloud-based architectures ensure the scalability and interoperability of these systems, allowing secure data storage, fast computation, and integration across multiple devices and healthcare networks. Current research has demonstrated that the synergy of machine learning and deep learning within ensemble frameworks achieves superior performance compared to traditional diagnostic methods, reinforcing the potential of intelligent systems in medical decision support.

展望未来，随着可解释人工智能 (XAI)、联邦学习和边缘计算的加入，人工智能驱动的预测模型与主流医疗保健的集成预计将变得更加复杂Looking toward the future, the integration of AI-driven predictive models into mainstream healthcare is expected to become even more sophisticated with the inclusion of explainable AI (XAI), federated learning, and edge computing[22, 23]。这些技术将增强数据隐私、可解释性和计算效率，从而实现针对个体患者量身定制的个性化医疗保健解决方案。此外，生物医学传感器技术的进步与大数据分析相结合，将能够在临床症状出现之前及早识别心脏功能的微小偏差。最终，这种未来的混合模型有望将心血管护理从以治疗为导向的方法转变为真正的预防性和以患者为中心的模式，确保全球数百万人获得更好的健康结果和生活质量。. These technologies will enhance data privacy, interpretability, and computational efficiency, enabling personalized healthcare solutions tailored to individual patients. Moreover, advances in biomedical sensor technology, combined with big data analytics, will enable the early identification of even minor deviations in cardiac function before symptoms manifest clinically. Ultimately, such futuristic hybrid models promise to transform cardiovascular care from a treatment-oriented approach to a truly preventive and patient-centered paradigm, ensuring better health outcomes and quality of life for millions worldwide.

4 拟议的研究方法Proposed Research Methodology

所提出的方法引入了一个集成的、受机器启发的医疗物联网 (IoMT) 框架，旨在通过先进的深度学习技术和智能数据处理进行实时心脏病预测。该框架的整体架构强调IoMT数据采集之间的无缝协同The proposed methodology introduces an integrated, machine-inspired Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) framework designed for real-time heart disease prediction through advanced deep learning techniques and intelligent data processing. The overall architecture of the framework emphasizes the seamless synergy between IoMT-enabled data acquisition[15]和复杂的机器学习模型。该框架的核心是使用双向长短期记忆 (Bi-LSTM) 网络，这是一种特殊类型的循环神经网络 (RNN)，能够捕获顺序数据中复杂的时间依赖性。此功能对于处理心电图、血压和心率等生理信号特别有益，其中时间顺序和上下文对于准确诊断至关重要。 Bi-LSTM 的双向结构允许模型向前和向后分析输入序列，从而有效地同时捕获过去和未来的上下文。这种双重视角增强了框架检测细微心脏异常和预测早期心脏病的能力，并提高了精度和可靠性（图and sophisticated machine learning models. Central to this framework is the use of Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) networks, a specialized type of recurrent neural network (RNN) capable of capturing complex temporal dependencies in sequential data. This capability is particularly beneficial for processing physiological signals such as ECG, blood pressure, and heart rate, where the temporal order and context are critical for accurate diagnosis. The bidirectional structure of Bi-LSTM allows the model to analyze input sequences in both forward and backward directions, effectively capturing past and future contexts simultaneously. This dual perspective enhances the framework’s ability to detect subtle cardiac anomalies and predict early-stage heart disease with improved precision and reliability (Figure2).

6.1 数据集分割Dataset Splitting

数据集分为三个子集，以方便模型训练、验证和测试。通常，70% 的数据用于训练，15% 用于验证，15% 用于测试。训练集用于拟合模型，验证集用于调整超参数并防止过度拟合。测试集严格保留用于评估未见过的数据的最终模型性能。对于 ECG 等时间序列信号，在保持时间连续性的同时执行分割，以保留顺序依赖性，这对于 Bi-LSTM 学习至关重要。The datasets are divided into three subsets to facilitate model training, validation, and testing. Typically, 70% of the data is allocated for training, 15% for validation, and 15% for testing. The training set is used to fit the models, while the validation set is utilized to tune hyperparameters and prevent overfitting. The test set is strictly reserved for evaluating final model performance on unseen data. For time-series signals like ECG, splitting is performed while maintaining temporal continuity to preserve sequential dependencies, which is critical for Bi-LSTM learning.

对经典机器学习模型（SVM、随机森林、XGBoost）和 Bi-LSTM 网络进行超参数调优。对于SVM，核类型、正则化系数等参数（Hyperparameter tuning is conducted for both the classical machine learning models (SVM, Random Forest, XGBoost) and the Bi-LSTM network. For SVM, parameters such as kernel type, regularization coefficient (CC) 和 gamma 均得到优化。随机森林和 XGBoost 模型针对树的数量、最大深度、学习率和子采样比率进行了调整。对于 Bi-LSTM 网络，使用网格搜索或贝叶斯优化系统地优化隐藏单元数量、层数、dropout 率、学习率和批量大小等超参数。在训练过程中采用早期停止来防止过度拟合并确保收敛到最佳模型性能。), and gamma are optimized. Random Forest and XGBoost models are tuned for the number of trees, maximum depth, learning rate, and subsampling ratios. For the Bi-LSTM network, hyperparameters such as the number of hidden units, number of layers, dropout rate, learning rate, and batch size are systematically optimized using grid search or Bayesian optimization. Early stopping is employed during training to prevent overfitting and to ensure convergence to optimal model performance.

    召回率（灵敏度）：Recall (Sensitivity):评估模型识别所有实际阳性案例（即真阳性/实际阳性）的能力。Assesses the ability of the model to identify all actual positive cases (i.e., true positives / actual positives).

  • •

    F1-分数：F1-Score:精确率和召回率的调和平均值，提供两者之间的平衡。The harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a balance between the two.

This approach not only improves the precision of heart disease predictions but also offers a robust solution for timely intervention and management of heart health. Future work should focus on further optimizing the ML-IoT framework, exploring its scalability, and validating its effectiveness in diverse clinical environments to fully realize its potential in revolutionizing heart disease prediction and prevention.
