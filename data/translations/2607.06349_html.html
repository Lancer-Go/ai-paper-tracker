摘要。Abstract.

建筑传感器嵌入在物理拓扑、空间层次结构和操作环境中，但现有的预测者通常将它们视为孤立的时间序列或依赖于固定的协变量集。我们呈现Building sensors are embedded in physical topology, spatial hierarchy, and operational context, yet existing forecasters often treat them as isolated time series or rely on fixed covariate sets. We present拓扑砖TopoBrick，一个用于零样本构建物联网（IoT）预测的免培训框架。 TopoBrick 使用构建知识图来构建紧凑的结构骨架，并采用代理拓扑采样器来选择特定于目标的外生变量。所选变量按部署时间可用性进行组织，将过去已知的传感器状态与未来已知的日历、时间表和气象外生变量分开。在三座真实世界的建筑中，TopoBrick 的性能优于强大的零样本基础模型基线，并且与经过充分训练的特定建筑模型保持竞争力。消融表明，拓扑感知采样比随机、仅本体或固定跳选择更可靠，特别是对于物理耦合的 HVAC 和天气驱动的传感变量。这项工作的代码可以在, a training-free framework for zero-shot building IoT (Internet-of-Things) forecasting. TopoBrick uses building knowledge graphs to construct a compact structural skeleton and employs an agentic topology sampler to select target-specific exogenous variables. The selected variables are organized by deployment-time availability, separating past-known sensor states from future-known calendar, schedule, and meteorological exogenous variables. Across three real-world buildings, TopoBrick outperforms strong zero-shot foundation-model baselines and remains competitive with fully trained building-specific models. Ablations show that topology-aware sampling is more reliable than random, ontology-only, or fixed-hop selection, especially for physically coupled HVAC and weather-driven sensing variables. The code of this work is available inhttps://github.com/Dawnlxc/TopoBrick.git

1. 简介Introduction

现代建筑是密集的网络物理系统，配备了数百到数千个传感、控制和操作点。这些点测量室内温度、湿度、压力、气流、电力需求、设备模式、设定点、阀门和风门命令以及室外条件。这些建筑传感器时间序列的准确预测对于需求灵活性、故障检测、预测控制和电网交互操作至关重要。 与通用的多元时间序列基准不同，构建传感器数据不是独立通道的平面集合。每个传感器都嵌入在物理和空间结构中：它连接到位于房间或区域的设备上，由空气或水回路提供服务，并且通常通过控制回路与设定点、执行器命令和测量响应相结合。因此，辅助信号的相关性不仅取决于其数值历史，还取决于它在建筑物中的位置以及它如何物理或操作地连接到目标传感器。 现有的预测方法很大程度上忽略了这种结构。通道无关模型分别预测每个传感器，而多变量模型则从固定变量集中学习依赖性。两种设置均假设输入变量已知且已对齐。时间序列基础模型进一步实现了零样本预测，无需特定任务的训练，但它们仍然将外生变量视为预定义的平面集。它们没有提供原则性的机制来决定建筑物的许多可用点中的哪一个应该用作给定目标的外生变量。相反，训练特定于建筑物的模型可以利用本地模式，但它需要为每个新部署的建筑物提供历史数据、调整和集成工作，从而限制了跨异构建筑组合的可扩展性。 结构先验的一个自然来源是构建知识图。诸如 Brick 和 Project Haystack 之类的语义建筑模式将建筑表示为设备、位置、系统和点的图形，并具有描述包容性、点附件和物理流的关系。原则上，该图提供了预测所缺少的物理和空间背景。然而，在实践中，直接使用建筑 KG 并不简单。构建图是异构和分层的：设备或位置等节点不携带时间序列读数，而只有点节点传输数据。简单的图距离启发法，例如固定距离Modern buildings are dense cyber-physical systems instrumented with hundreds to thousands of sensing, control, and operational points. These points measure indoor temperature, humidity, pressure, airflow, electrical demand, equipment modes, setpoints, valve and damper commands, and outdoor conditions. Accurate forecasting of these building sensor time series is fundamental to demand flexibility, fault detection, predictive control, and grid-interactive operation. Unlike generic multivariate time-series benchmarks, building sensor data is not a flat collection of independent channels. Each sensor is embedded in a physical and spatial structure: it is attached to equipment, located in a room or zone, served by air or water loops, and often coupled with setpoints, actuator commands, and measured responses through control loops. Therefore, the relevance of an auxiliary signal depends not only on its numerical history, but also on where it is located in the building and how it is physically or operationally connected to the target sensor. This structure is largely ignored by existing forecasting methods. Channel-independent models forecast each sensor separately, while multivariate models learn dependencies from a fixed variable set. Both settings assume that the input variables are already known and aligned. Time-series foundation models further enable zero-shot forecasting without task-specific training, but they still treat exogenous variables as a pre-defined flat set. They do not provide a principled mechanism for deciding which of a building’s many available points should be used as exogenous variables for a given target. Conversely, training a building-specific model may exploit local patterns, but it requires historical data, tuning, and integration effort for every newly deployed building, limiting scalability across heterogeneous building portfolios. A natural source of structural prior is the building knowledge graph. Semantic building schemas such as Brick and Project Haystack represent a building as a graph of equipment, locations, systems, and Points, with relations describing containment, point attachment, and physical flow. In principle, this graph provides the missing physical and spatial context for forecasting. In practice, however, directly using a building KG is non-trivial. Building graphs are heterogeneous and hierarchical: nodes such as equipment or location do not carry time-series readings, while only Point nodes stream data. Simple graph-distance heuristics such as fixedkk-hop 邻域可能会产生误导，因为物理相关点可能距离几个结构跳远，而高级设备或建筑物级节点可以扩展到许多不相关的点。学习图编码器引入了额外的训练和模式对齐要求，这与零样本部署设置相冲突。 因此，挑战不仅在于预测传感器值，还在于在不进行特定于建筑物训练的情况下从异构建筑物 KG 中识别特定于目标的外生变量。本文将零样本建筑传感器预测表述为拓扑感知的外生变量选择问题。关键思想是使用建筑物知识图谱作为路由层：模型不应选择所有可用点或仅通过本体类或图距离检索传感器，而应选择物理上接地、具有操作意义且在实际部署约束下可用的外生变量。-hop neighborhoods can be misleading since a physically relevant point may be several structural hops away, while a high-degree equipment or building-level node can expand to many unrelated points. Learned graph encoders introduce additional training and schema-alignment requirements, which conflict with the zero-shot deployment setting. The challenge is therefore not only to forecast sensor values, but also to identify target-specific exogenous variables from a heterogeneous building KG without building-specific training. This paper formulates zero-shot building sensor forecasting as a topology-aware exogenous-variable selection problem. The key idea is to use the building KG as a routing layer: rather than conditioning on all available points or retrieving sensors only by ontology class or graph distance, the model should select exogenous variables that are physically grounded, operationally meaningful, and available under realistic deployment constraints.

我们推出了 TopoBrick，这是一种用于零样本建筑传感器预测的免训练框架。 TopoBrick 首先将原始建筑 KG 提炼成紧凑的建筑骨架，保留设备流、空间包含和结构关系，同时将点叶与拓扑遍历分开。给定目标点，代理拓扑采样器对以目标为中心的骨架上下文进行推理，并生成结构化采样动作以选择相关的外生变量。基于知识图谱的验证者会在确定性具体化为时间序列输入之前审核这些操作。然后，所选变量按部署时间可用性进行组织：仅在历史窗口中提供过去已知的传感器和设备状态，而在历史和预测范围内提供未来已知的日历、时间表和气象变量。这使得冻结的时间序列基础模型能够使用特定于建筑物的上下文，而无需更新模型参数或泄漏未来的观察结果。We present TopoBrick, a training-free framework for zero-shot building sensor forecasting. TopoBrick first distills the raw building KG into a compact building skeleton that preserves equipment-flow, spatial-containment, and structural relations while separating Point leaves from topology traversal. Given a target Point, an agentic topology sampler reasons over the target-centric skeleton context and generates structured sampling actions for selecting relevant exogenous variables. A KG-grounded verifier audits these actions before deterministic materialization into time-series inputs. The selected variables are then organized by deployment-time availability: past-known sensor and equipment states are provided only over the historical window, while future-known calendar, schedule, and meteorological variables are provided over both the history and prediction horizon. This allows a frozen time-series foundation model to use building-specific context without updating model parameters or leaking future observations.

2.1. 时间序列预测Time Series Forecasting

PatchTST(Nie et al., 2022)证明了修补输入序列和独立处理通道可以显着提高 Transformer 性能，而 iTransformerdemonstrated that patching the input series and treating channels independently substantially improves Transformer performance, while iTransformer(Liu et al., )反转注意力机制以跨变量而不是时间步进行操作。时间混合器inverts the attention mechanism to operate across variables rather than time steps. While effective, these methods generally require task dataset specific training.

一个互补的工作是开发时间序列基础模型，支持跨域零样本预测，而无需按任务进行训练。克罗诺斯A complementary line of work develops time series foundation models that support cross-domain zero-shot forecasting without per-task training. Chronos(Ansari et al., 2024, 2025)对时间序列进行标记，并在大规模语料库上进行训练，以实现零样本传输。时代调频tokenizes time series and trains on large-scale corpora to enable zero-shot transfer. Despite this progress, these methods are largely developed and evaluated on curated benchmarks where the variable set is fixed and pre-aligned, and they leave two assumptions unaddressed in the building IoT setting. First, channel-independent and implicitly multivariate models offer no principled way to decide其中which建筑物的数百到数千个点应作为给定目标的外生变量，要么忽略跨传感器结构，要么在所有通道上不加区别地进行调节，从而注入噪声和成本。其次，经过训练的模型会产生每个建筑物或每个数据集的训练成本，这些成本不会在部署中遇到的异构建筑物的长尾中摊销。基础模型消除了训练成本，但仍然将外生变量视为给定的平面集，没有使外生变量相关的空间、物理或控制关系的概念。我们的工作建立在零样本基础模型范式的基础上，但恰恰提供了这个缺失的成分：一种免训练、与建筑无关的机制，它使用建筑拓扑来选择物理相关的外生变量，并在实际操作环境中将过去已知的与未来已知的外生输入分开。of a building’s hundreds to thousands of points should serve as exogenous variables for a given target, either ignoring cross-sensor structure or conditioning indiscriminately on all channels, injecting noise and cost. Second, the trained models incur per-building or per-dataset training cost that does not amortize across the long tail of heterogeneous buildings encountered in deployment. Foundation models remove the training cost but still treat exogenous variables as a given flat set, with no notion of the spatial, physical, or control relationships that make an exogenous variable relevant. Our work builds on the zero-shot foundation model paradigm but supplies exactly this missing ingredient: a training-free, building-agnostic mechanism that uses building topology to select physically relevant exogenous variables and to separate past-known from future-known exogenous inputs in real operational settings.

2.2. 构建时间序列分析Building Time Series Analytics

越来越多的工作通过语义元数据的视角来研究构建操作数据，特别是 Brick 本体，它将建筑物表示为设备、位置、点及其物理和空间关系的知识图。这项研究的大部分目标是A growing body of work studies building operational data through the lens of semantic metadata, in particular the Brick ontology, which represents a building as a knowledge graph of equipment, locations, points, and their physical and spatial relations. Much of this research targets理解understanding元数据本身：将点和实体自动分类为 Brick 类the metadata itself: automatic classification of points and entities into Brick classes(Prabowo et al., 2025)解决了实际部署中发现的嘈杂、不一致的标签问题，同时系统工作演示了基于砖结构数据构建的端到端管道addresses the noisy, inconsistent labeling found in real deployments, while systems work demonstrates end-to-end pipelines built on Brick-structured data(Lin et al., 2024)。构建知识图也被用作高级推理的支柱，包括构建知识图谱的自然语言问答. Building knowledge graphs have also been used as a backbone for higher-level reasoning, including natural-language question answering over building KGs(Mulayim et al., 2025a, b)，其中该图提供了解决有关建筑物结构和操作的查询所需的语义上下文。 在分析方面，已经针对电力负荷预测等任务研究了构建时间序列，包括隐私保护公式, where the graph supplies the semantic context needed to resolve queries about a building’s structure and operation. On the analytics side, building time series have been studied for tasks such as electricity load forecasting, including privacy-preserving formulations(Zaman et al., 2025)，并了解在大流行封锁等特殊条件下运营模式如何变化, and for understanding how operational patterns shift under exceptional conditions such as pandemic lock-downs(Prabowo et al., 2023)。这些研究既确立了建筑预测的实际重要性，也确立了语义元数据的价值，但它们通常将知识图谱视为单个建筑的标签或上下文来源，而不是用于可转移、跨建筑预测的结构。 关键的是，这些产品线都没有结合我们的设置要求的两种功能。使用建筑元数据的预测方法仍然依赖于特定于建筑的训练或手工制作的协变量规则，而以 KG 为中心的方法则侧重于检索或问题回答，而不是驱动预测器。如何对具有大量非时间序列节点和不同本体完整性的异构分层建筑知识图谱进行推理，以选择外生变量并以免训练、建筑不可知的方式进行预测的问题仍然悬而未决。 TopoBrick 旨在填补这一空白，将零样本基础预测范式与构建知识图谱时编码的丰富但异构的结构连接起来。. These studies establish both the practical importance of building forecasting and the value of semantic metadata, but they typically treat the knowledge graph as a source of labels or context for a single building, rather than as a structure to be exploited for transferable, cross-building forecasting. Critically, none of these lines combine the two capabilities our setting demands. Forecasting methods that use building metadata still rely on building-specific training or handcrafted covariate rules, while KG-centric methods focus on retrieval or question answering rather than driving a forecaster. The question of how to reason over a heterogeneous, hierarchical building KG with a large amount of non-time-series nodes and varying ontology completeness to select exogenous variables and forecast in a training-free, building-agnostic manner remains open. TopoBrick is designed to fill this gap, connecting the zero-shot foundation-forecasting paradigm to the rich but heterogeneous structure encoded in building knowledge graphs.

概述。Overview.

3.1. 代理拓扑采样器Agentic Topology Sampler

代理拓扑采样器的目标是识别与给定目标点节点相关的一组紧凑的外生变量。建筑系统包含数百或数千个异构点，其中许多与特定目标无关。因此，直接调节所有可用点的效率很低，并且会注入噪声。相反，TopoBrick 使用建筑 KG 通过物理、空间和操作结构来路由外生变量选择。采样器对元数据和图形拓扑进行操作。它在选择过程中不使用未来的观察或预测误差。给定一个目标点The goal of the agentic topology sampler is to identify a compact set of exogenous variables that are relevant to a given target Point node. Building systems contain hundreds or thousands of heterogeneous points, many of which are unrelated to a particular target. Directly conditioning on all available Points is therefore inefficient and can inject noise. TopoBrick instead uses the building KG to route exogenous variable selection through physical, spatial, and operational structure. The sampler operates on metadata and graph topology. It does not use future observations or forecasting errors during selection. Given a target Pointpp，它返回一个外生变量集, it returns an exogenous variable setℰ​(p)\mathcal{E}(p)，稍后与预测窗口对齐。, which is later aligned to the forecasting window.

3.1.1. 建筑骨架施工Building Skeleton Construction

原始建筑 KG 是异构且密集的。点节点通常作为叶子附加到设备、房间、区域或系统，而有意义的物理结构则由设备流、部分整体层次结构和空间包含等关系承载。在全KG上直接遍历可能不稳定：高度结构节点可能扩展到许多不相关的Point，而物理相关的Point可能通过中间设备或位置节点相隔数跳。为了获得易于处理的结构表示，我们构建了一个建筑骨架Raw building KGs are heterogeneous and dense.
