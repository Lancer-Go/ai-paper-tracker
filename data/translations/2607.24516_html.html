摘要Abstract

We formulate data construction as a系统的混合优化systematic mixture-optimization问题，并通过将混合物解耦为两个正交子问题，将其转变为可重现的工程学科：problem and turn it into a reproducible engineering discipline by decoupling the mixture into two orthogonal sub-problems:类间inter-class各种能力的比率和ratios across capabilities and类内intra-class类别内的比率。 对于类间分配，我们使用单变量迭代搜索；对于类内组成，我们应用多维、数据集级评估评分质量和难度，并将选择制定为具有多样性目标的约束凸优化。 的ratios within a category. For inter-class allocation, we use a single-variable iterative search; for intra-class composition, we apply a multidimensional, dataset-level assessment scoring Quality and Difficulty, and formulate selection as a constrained convex optimization with a diversity objective. 5 相关作品Related Works

多模式数据管理。Multimodal data curation.

Large-scale VLM pipelines commonly improve data efficiency through similarity-based filtering [21]，基于模型的质量管理, model-based quality curation[22]，以及联合质量-多样性选择, and joint quality–diversity selection[11]。这些方法主要是对单个示例进行评分或保留。 De CoupleMix 是互补的：它将数据集视为管理后的分配单元，并解决保留的源应如何共享有限的训练预算。. These approaches primarily score or retain individual examples. DecoupleMix is complementary: it treats datasets as allocation units after curation and addresses how the retained sources should share a finite training budget.

数据混合优化。Data-mixture optimization.

数据混合优化在语言模型预训练中得到了广泛的研究。 DoReMi 通过代理模型和分组分布鲁棒优化来学习域权重Data-mixture optimization has been studied extensively in language-model pretraining. DoReMi learns domain weights with a proxy model and group distributionally robust optimization[23];数据混合定律和 RegMix 使用小规模训练运行来预测未见过的混合物的性能; Data Mixing Laws and RegMix use small-scale training runs to predict the performance of unseen mixtures[24, 25]; RegMix-D 将基于回归的混合优化扩展到动态调度; and RegMix-D extends regression-based mixture optimization to dynamic schedules[26]。最近的工作还研究了跨语言、视觉和本地多模式设置的最佳混合的缩放法则. Recent work also studies scaling laws for optimal mixtures across language, vision, and native multimodal settings[27]。这些研究确立了混合物优化的更广泛价值，并激发了我们特定于 VLM 的分层公式。. These studies establish the broader value of mixture optimization and motivate our VLM-specific hierarchical formulation.

7 结论Conclusion

我们呈现We present解耦混合DecoupleMix，一个系统的数据混合优化框架，将 VLM 预训练从试错法转变为原理工程。通过引入多维数据集级评估并将数据组合解耦到类间和类内优化中，我们建立了一种用于构建高性能配方的可扩展方法。我们的方法经过 2.5B 到 10B 代币的验证，无需重新调整到更大的数据和模型规模即可进行转移，其性能始终优于启发式基线，这表明系统数据管理是高效 VLM 开发的关键杠杆。, a systematic data mixture optimization framework that transforms VLM pretraining from trial-and-error into principled engineering. By introducing a multi-dimensional dataset-level assessment and decoupling data composition into inter-class and intra-class optimization, we establish a scalable methodology for constructing high-performance recipes. Validated across 2.5B to 10B tokens and transferring without retuning to larger data and model scales, our approach consistently outperforms heuristic baselines, demonstrating that systematic data curation is the critical lever for efficient VLM development.
