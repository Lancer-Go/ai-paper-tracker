摘要Abstract

检索增强生成（RAG）显着增强了大型语言模型（LLM），但通过外部知识访问引入了新的安全风险。虽然现有的研究涵盖了各种 RAG 漏洞，但它们经常将固有的 LLM 风险与 RAG 特别引入的风险混为一谈。在本文中，我们提出安全 RAG 从根本上讲是关于外部知识访问管道的安全性。我们建立了一个操作边界，将固有的 LLM 缺陷与 RAG 引入或 RAG 放大的威胁分开。在这个视角的指导下，我们将 RAG 工作流程抽象为六个阶段，并围绕三个信任边界和四个主要安全面组织文献，包括检索前知识损坏、检索时访问操纵、下游上下文利用和知识渗漏。通过系统地回顾相应的攻击、防御、修复机制和评估基准，我们发现当前的防御在很大程度上仍然是被动的和碎片化的。最后，我们讨论这些差距，并强调未来在整个知识访问生命周期中实现分层、边界感知保护的方向。Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) significantly enhances large language models (LLMs) but introduces novel security risks through external knowledge access. While existing studies cover various RAG vulnerabilities, they often conflate inherent LLM risks with those specifically introduced by RAG. In this paper, we propose that secure RAG is fundamentally about the security of the external knowledge-access pipeline. We establish an operational boundary to separate inherent LLM flaws from RAG-introduced or RAG-amplified threats. Guided by this perspective, we abstract the RAG workflow into six stages and organize the literature around three trust boundaries and four primary security surfaces, including pre-retrieval knowledge corruption, retrieval-time access manipulation, downstream context exploitation, and knowledge exfiltration. By systematically reviewing the corresponding attacks, defenses, remediation mechanisms, and evaluation benchmarks, we reveal that current defenses remain largely reactive and fragmented. Finally, we discuss these gaps and highlight future directions toward layered, boundary-aware protection across the entire knowledge-access lifecycle.

3.5 总结和观察Summary and Observations

总体而言，当前的攻击文献表明，通过外部知识访问管道视图可以最好地分析安全 RAG 风险。 本节中的四个攻击面在机制上有所不同，但它们具有相同的结构特性，即不可信的外部知识在进入系统后可以被注入、重定向、利用或泄露。Overall, the current attack literature shows that secure RAG risks are best analyzed through the external knowledge-access pipeline view. The four attack surfaces in this section differ in mechanism, but they share the same structural property that untrusted external knowledge can be injected, redirected, exploited, or disclosed after it enters the system.

其中，上游中毒尤其重要，因为它可以持续存在于共享知识基础中，并反复影响后续查询、用户和系统输出。 检索时间操纵和下游检索上下文利用凸显了另一个风险，即攻击者如果能够控制跨越检索边界的内容或检索的证据如何与生成交互，则可以更秘密地误导。 知识泄露和隐私攻击进一步表明，知识访问接口可以被反向滥用，以推断或提取敏感的外部内容。Among them, upstream poisoning is especially important because it can persist inside a shared knowledge substrate and repeatedly affect later queries, users, and system outputs. Retrieval-time manipulation and downstream retrieved-context exploitation highlight another risk that attackers can mislead more secretly if they can steer what crosses the retrieval boundary or how retrieved evidence interacts with generation. Knowledge exfiltration and privacy attacks further show that the knowledge-access interface can be abused in the reverse direction to infer or extract sensitive external content.

总而言之，安全 RAG 攻击并不是应答时的孤立故障。 它们是外部知识如何被接纳、访问、暴露给生成器以及通过响应接口披露的管道级故障。Taken together, secure RAG attacks are not isolated failures at answer time. They are pipeline-level failures in how external knowledge is admitted, accessed, exposed to the generator, and disclosed through the response interface.

4.5 总结和观察Summary and Observations

总体而言，当前的国防文献在 RAG 知识获取渠道中分布不均。 最成熟的工作线集中在第二和第三信任边界周围。 这些主要涉及证据成为模型可见上下文之前的检索时间强化，以及跨越该边界之后的检索后隔离。 相比之下，第一个上游边界受到的保护要少得多。 知识库完整性、出处和妥协后补救仍然相对稀疏，尽管它们对于长期共享语料库至关重要。 与此同时，访问控制、隐私和机密性作为系统级防线迅速发展，特别是通过差分隐私、加密检索和机密架构。Overall, the current defense literature is unevenly distributed across the RAG knowledge-access pipeline. The most mature lines of work concentrate around the second and third trust boundaries. These primarily involve retrieval-time hardening before evidence becomes model-visible context, and post-retrieval isolation after that boundary is crossed. By contrast, the first upstream boundary remains much less protected. Knowledge-base integrity, provenance, and post-compromise remediation are still relatively sparse despite their critical importance for long-lived shared corpora. In parallel, access control, privacy, and confidentiality have grown rapidly as a system-level line of defense, particularly through differential privacy, encrypted retrieval, and confidential architectures.

通过绘制图中的攻击分类法By mapping the attack taxonomy in Figure3到图中的防御机制to the defense mechanisms in Figure4，我们可以观察到几种结构模式。 首先，当前的防御仍然主要是反应性的。 许多研究仅在有害证据已经进入检索流程后才尝试对其进行过滤、重新排序或包含， 而在底层级别管理准入、回滚或语料库恢复的方法则少得多。 其次，威胁检测机制和高级攻击构建之间存在结构性不匹配。 尽管许多检索时防御隐含地假设中毒证据将显示为语义异常值，但现代攻击越来越多地优化可检索性、流畅性和上下文连贯性，以与良性证据无缝融合。 第三，隐私和保密机制是补充而非替代。 尽管它们对于防止未经授权的泄露和提取至关重要，但它们无法独立解决完整性损坏或检索上下文利用问题。, we can observe several structural patterns. First, current defenses remain predominantly reactive. Many studies attempt to filter, rerank, or contain harmful evidence only after it has already entered the retrieval flow, while significantly fewer methods govern admission, rollback, or corpus recovery at the substrate level. Second, there is a structural mismatch between threat detection mechanisms and advanced attack construction. Although many retrieval-time defenses implicitly assume that poisoned evidence will appear as a semantic outlier, modern attacks increasingly optimize for retrievability, fluency, and contextual coherence to seamlessly blend with benign evidence. Third, privacy and confidentiality mechanisms are complementary rather than substitutive. Although they are essential for preventing unauthorized disclosure and extraction, they cannot independently resolve integrity corruption or retrieved-context exploitation.

总而言之，这些观察结果表明，安全 RAG 不应被视为孤立的过滤任务，而应更多地被视为跨多个信任边界的分层控制问题。 强大的部署必须协同结合知识基础的上游治理、检索时证据强化、检索后遏制和机密性控制，而不是孤立地依赖任何单一防御系列。Taken together, these observations suggest that secure RAG should be viewed less as an isolated filtering task and more as a layered control problem across multiple trust boundaries. A robust deployment must synergistically combine upstream governance of the knowledge substrate, retrieval-time evidence hardening, post-retrieval containment, and confidentiality controls, rather than relying on any single defense family in isolation.

5.3 总结和观察Summary and Observations

总体而言，最近的基准测试和评估研究使安全 RAG 评估更加系统化且与部署更加相关。 与孤立的攻击演示相比，它们提供了更清晰的协议、更广泛的组件覆盖范围和更现实的端到端设置，用于研究安全故障在实践中如何出现。Overall, recent benchmarks and evaluation studies have made secure-RAG assessment more systematic and more deployment-relevant. Compared with isolated attack demonstrations, they provide clearer protocols, broader component coverage, and more realistic end-to-end settings for studying how security failures appear in practice.

同时，目前的评估文献仍然反映了前两节讨论的攻防格局的结构。 不同的研究通常关注不同的威胁面，例如中毒、即时注入、隐私泄露或提取，并且许多报告的指标仍然集中在最终输出而不是中间管道行为。 因此，当前的评估对于比较威胁环境中的方法越来越有用，而跨表面比较仍然不太统一。At the same time, the current evaluation literature still reflects the structure of the attack and defense landscape discussed in the previous two sections. Different studies often focus on different threat surfaces, such as poisoning, prompt injection, privacy leakage, or extraction, and many reported metrics still concentrate on final outputs rather than on intermediate pipeline behavior. As a result, current evaluation is increasingly useful for comparing methods within a threat setting, while cross-surface comparison remains less unified.

总而言之，这些研究表明 secure-RAG 评估正在成为该领域的核心部分，而不是次要的事后想法。 它使跨系统的管道级威胁更加可见和可比，从而在攻击分析和防御设计之间架起了一座重要的桥梁。Taken together, these studies show that secure-RAG evaluation is becoming a central part of the field rather than a secondary afterthought. It provides an important bridge between attack analysis and defense design by making pipeline-level threats more visible and comparable across systems.

7 结论Conclusion

受外部知识从根本上改变推理路径这一事实的启发，我们提出必须将安全 RAG 作为知识访问管道的安全性进行独立分析，该管道涵盖数据摄取、检索、组装和披露。 从这个角度来看，我们明确区分了固有的 LLM 漏洞与 RAG 引入或 RAG 放大的风险。 通过这个操作边界，我们将文献系统地构建为四个不同的安全表面和三个信任边界，详细介绍了当前攻击的机制、防御层和评估协议。Motivated by the fact that external knowledge fundamentally alters the inference path, we proposed that secure RAG must be independently analyzed as the security of the knowledge-access pipeline, which spans data ingestion, retrieval, assembly, and disclosure. Under this perspective, we explicitly distinguished inherent LLM vulnerabilities from RAG-introduced or RAG-amplified risks. Through this operational boundary, we systematically structured the literature into four distinct security surfaces and three trust boundaries, detailing the mechanics of current attacks, defense layers, and evaluation protocols.

我们的审查揭示了当前安全 RAG 格局中的结构性不匹配。 虽然攻击策略正在迅速发展以生成高度一致且可检索的有效负载， 大多数防御仍然严格被动，集中于中游过滤而不是上游语料库完整性或数据来源。 此外，针对孤立的威胁模型的实证评估仍然支离破碎。 展望未来，未来的研究应该从局部补丁过渡到分层的、边界感知的治理。 这需要开发统一的跨表面基准，加强预检索数据验证，并确保防御设计包含针对自适应威胁的实用回滚和修复功能。Our review reveals a structural mismatch across the current landscape of secure RAG. While attack strategies are rapidly evolving to generate highly coherent and retrievable payloads, most defenses remain strictly reactive, concentrating on mid-stream filtering rather than upstream corpus integrity or data provenance. Furthermore, empirical evaluations remain fragmented across isolated threat models. Moving forward, future research should transition from localized patches to layered, boundary-aware governance. This requires developing unified, cross-surface benchmarks, strengthening pre-retrieval data validation, and ensuring that defense designs incorporate practical rollback and remediation capabilities against adaptive threats.
