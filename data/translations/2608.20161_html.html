摘要。Abstract.

基于指令的图像编辑使用规划器渲染器管道：视觉语言模型 (VLM) 首先将指令转换为编辑计划，然后扩散模型执行该计划。仅使用最终图像奖励来训练此类系统效率低下，因为糟糕的编辑无法揭示额外的优化是否应该更加重视规划器或渲染器，甚至规划器主导的情况仍然难以在自由形式的推理跟踪中定位。我们呈现Instruction-based image editing uses a planner-renderer pipeline: a vision-language model (VLM) first converts the instruction into an edit plan, and a diffusion model then executes that plan. Training such systems with only final-image rewards is inefficient because a poor edit does not reveal whether additional optimization should place more emphasis on the planner or the renderer, and even planner-dominant cases remain difficult to localize within a free-form reasoning trace. We presentDARS，这是一个用于两阶段设置中的双层学分分配的强化学习框架。跨模块，多计划多渲染部署可估计软模块路由的计划间和计划内奖励可变性，而跨部署的平均奖励则为自适应课程提供硬度估计。在规划器中，四字段结构化推理输出可以实现前缀门控奖励和代币级优势重新加权，将结果级反馈转化为本地化监督。五个基准测试的实验表明, a reinforcement learning framework for dual-level credit assignment in this two-stage setting. Across modules, multi-plan multi-render rollouts estimate between-plan and within-plan reward variability for soft module routing, while mean rewards across rollouts provide hardness estimates for an adaptive curriculum. Within the planner, a four-field structured reasoning output enables a prefix-gated reward and token-level advantage reweighting, turning outcome-level feedback into localized supervision. Experiments on five benchmarks show thatDARS在相同主干、数据、奖励模型和部署预算的情况下，其性能优于联合强化学习基线，在推理密集型编辑方面收益最大。outperforms a Joint RL baseline with the same backbone, data, reward model, and rollout budget, with the largest gains on reasoning-intensive edits.

基于指令的图像编辑Instruction-based image editing(33; 21; 14; 13; 22; 8; 3)旨在根据自然语言指令修改源图像，同时保留不相关的内容。最近的工作越来越多地通过明确的推理或计划来增强编辑。重新计划aims to modify a source image according to a natural-language instruction while preserving irrelevant content. Taken together, these methods establish the value of introducing a planner-like stage before generation. Their main emphasis, however, is on designing stronger intermediate reasoning or refinement procedures. Our focus is different: we study how to optimize an already existing planner-renderer pipeline once a structured intermediate stage has been introduced.

强化学习最近被用来提高图像生成中的指令忠实度和多模态对齐Reinforcement learning has recently been used to improve instruction faithfulness and multimodal alignment in image generation(17; 42; 41; 36)和编辑。提示RLand editing. PromptRL(31)研究基于流量的发电的基于奖励的优化。在两阶段编辑流程中，现有的 RL 方法的主要区别在于优化了哪个模块：ThinkRL-Editstudies reward-based optimization for flow-based generation. In two-stage editing pipelines, existing RL methods differ mainly in which module is optimized: ThinkRL-Edit(15)通过在反射计划下调整扩散模型和文本编码器来关注生成方面，EditThinkerfocuses on the generation side by tuning the diffusion model and text encoder under reflective plans, EditThinker(16)重点优化VLM推理模块，ThinkGenconcentrates optimization on the VLM reasoning module, and ThinkGen(11)在推理模型和生成器之间交替更新。一旦编辑被组织为明确的提示重写、规划或推理阶段，然后是视觉执行，优化问题本质上就变成了两个阶段：一个模块生成中间文本计划，另一个模块在图像空间中实现它。在这种情况下，现有的强化学习策略主要遵循三种模式：(i)alternates updates between the reasoning model and the generator. Once editing is organized as an explicit prompt-rewriting, planning, or reasoning stage followed by visual execution, the optimization problem becomes inherently two-stage: one module produces an intermediate textual plan, and another realizes it in the image space. In this setting, existing RL strategies largely follow three patterns: (i)分阶段或交替stage-wise or alternating优化，将规划器和渲染器更新分开； (二)optimization, which separates planner and renderer updates; (ii)单模块single-module优化，冻结一个组件并调整另一个组件； (三)optimization, which freezes one component and tunes the other; and (iii)均匀接头uniform joint优化，它从相同的结果级别奖励更新两个模块。前两个减少了协调共同改进的机会，而第三个则使学习信号未指定，因为较低的最终分数并不能揭示哪个模块应该受到更多的优化重点。我们的方法与第三条工作关系最密切，但不同之处在于通过推出衍生路由同时保留联合训练来明确解决此模块分配问题。optimization, which updates both modules from the same outcome-level reward. The first two reduce the opportunity for coordinated co-improvement, while the third leaves the learning signal underspecified because a low final score does not reveal which module should receive more optimization emphasis. Our method is most closely related to the third line of work, but differs in explicitly addressing this module-allocation problem through rollout-derived routing while retaining joint training.

课程学习和结构化推理。Curriculum learning and structured reasoning.

课程学习Curriculum learning(4; 5)通过根据难度对训练样本进行排序或加权来改进优化。然而，标准课程设计将硬度视为每个样本的一维属性。在两阶段图像编辑中，这种抽象是不完整的：具有相似难度的两个样本在其不确定性主要是计划主导还是渲染主导方面可能仍然不同，这表明如果没有模块感知权重，仅样本调度是不够的。在推理方面，PromptEnhancerimproves optimization by ordering or weighting training samples according to difficulty. Standard curriculum designs, however, treat hardness as a one-dimensional property of each sample. In two-stage image editing, this abstraction is incomplete: two samples with similar difficulty may still differ in whether their uncertainty is primarily plan-dominant or render-dominant, suggesting that sample scheduling alone is insufficient without module-aware weighting. On the reasoning side, PromptEnhancer(32), 思创, ThinkGen(11)和编辑思考者, and EditThinker(16)显示了更丰富的中间文本的价值，但自由形式的推理痕迹在渲染后仍然难以在本地评估。它们通常依赖于设计的冷启动格式或专家设计的提示配方show the value of richer intermediate text, but free-form reasoning traces remain difficult to evaluate locally after rendering. They often depend on engineered cold-start formats or expert-designed prompting recipes(12)，这使得它们更难在 RL 下持续扩展和优化。我们的方法在这个设计空间中占据了不同的位置：我们不依赖长的自由形式跟踪，而是使用紧凑的四字段模式，该模式保持接近规划器的本机生成格式，同时公开语义对齐的插槽以进行渲染后奖励分解和令牌级信用分配。, which makes them harder to scale and optimize consistently under RL. Our method occupies a different point in this design space: instead of relying on long free-form traces, we use a compact four-field schema that remains close to the planner’s native generation format while exposing semantically aligned slots for post-render reward decomposition and token-level credit assignment.

6. 局限性Limitations

DARS需要requiresM×KM\times K每个训练示例都有一次部署，因此总成本仍然高于单路径更新，即使这些部署与策略优化共享。基于方差的路由和渲染后规划器分数取决于奖励模型，并且当两个模块同时失败时，规划器-渲染器交互可能更难理清。使框架适应视频编辑或多轮设置留待将来的工作。rollouts per training example, so the total cost remains higher than single-path updates even though these rollouts are shared with policy optimization. The variance-based routing and post-render planner scores depend on the reward model, and planner-renderer interactions can be harder to disentangle when both modules fail simultaneously. Adapting the framework to video editing or multi-turn settings is left for future work.

7. 结论Conclusion

我们呈现We presentDARS，它将两阶段规划器渲染器图像编辑管道中的联合强化学习表述为双层信用分配问题。 Rollout方差分解为跨模块更新提供路由信号；硬度衍生的分数驱动适应性课程安排；带有前缀门控的结构化四字段规划器可实现按时隙诊断。, which formulates joint RL in two-stage planner-renderer image editing pipelines as a dual-level credit-assignment problem. Rollout variance decomposition provides routing signals for cross-module updates; hardness-derived scores drive adaptive curriculum scheduling; and a structured four-field planner with prefix gating enables slot-wise diagnostics.DARS在五个基准测试中取得了最佳结果，在推理密集型任务上收益最大。attains the best results across five benchmarks, with the largest gains on reasoning-intensive tasks.

7. 局限性Limitations

尽管主论文和上面的补充可视化中报告了这些成果，但当前框架仍然存在一些明显的局限性。当所需的输出很难用自然语言紧凑地指定并且可以通过结构化视觉目标更自然地描述时，就会出现一个反复出现的困难。这包括迷宫求解、谜题完成或其他图表编辑等任务，其中模型必须推断并呈现精确的最终配置，而不是仅应用局部语义修改。Despite the gains reported in the main paper and the supplementary visualizations above, the current framework still has several clear limitations. One recurring difficulty arises when the desired output is hard to specify compactly in natural language and is more naturally described by a structured visual target. This includes tasks such as maze solving, puzzle completion, or other diagrammatic edits where the model must infer and render a precise final configuration rather than only apply a local semantic modification.

第二个限制出现在需要非常密集的链式推理或非常精确的几何控制的情况下。如图所示。A second limitation appears in cases that require either very dense chained reasoning or very precise geometric control. As shown in Fig.S3，代表性的故障模式包括不完整的谜题重建、力学风格图表中不正确的最终状态推理、跨多个对象的不准确的相对大小归一化以及迷宫中不稳定的路径绘制。这些例子表明，当编辑依赖于长推理链、图级状态转换或精确的多对象比例关系时，当前的规划器-渲染器分解仍然不太可靠。未来的进步可能需要更强大的结构化中间表示、更丰富的图解推理监督以及更明确的几何控制机制。, representative failure modes include incomplete puzzle reconstruction, incorrect final-state reasoning in mechanics-style diagrams, inaccurate relative-size normalization across multiple objects, and unstable path drawing in mazes. These examples suggest that the current planner–renderer decomposition is still less reliable when the edit depends on long reasoning chains, diagram-level state transitions, or exact multi-object scale relationships. Future progress will likely require stronger, structured intermediate representations, richer supervision for diagrammatic reasoning, and more explicit mechanisms for geometric control.
