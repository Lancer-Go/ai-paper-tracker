摘要。Abstract.

跨域推荐 (CDR) 跨相关域传输偏好知识，但联合部署使跨域对齐变得困难，因为对齐项目空间的行为锚点（例如重叠用户和共享交互信号）在客户端之间通常是稀疏的、不可用的或隐私敏感的。 为了解决这种紧张关系，我们重新审视联邦 CDR，将其作为稳定语义项目语言的生成。 通过将项目表示为从公共项目端元数据派生的离散语义 ID (SID) 序列，跨域项目对齐是由共享词汇表引发的，而不是通过交换私有交互或对齐特定于域的嵌入来实现。 然而，直接联合基于 SID 的生成器引入了两个设计约束：SID 标记器必须保持固定以保持跨客户端标记一致性，这会产生纯语义瓶颈，因为本地协同过滤 (CF) 信号无法全局共享或对齐；同时，标准联合平均会在域异质性下导致负迁移。 为了克服这些限制，我们提出了 FedCGR，这是一个联邦生成 CDR 框架，可以保持项目语言稳定并使适应明确。 FedCGR 通过可靠性感知语义接口注入本地 CF 证据，并训练原型个性化生成器，该生成器根据领域相关性有选择地聚合共享参数，同时保持领域特定数量本地化。 对六个 Amazon 跨域场景的实验表明，FedCGR 始终优于联邦生成基线，并在全排名和抽样评估协议下实现了与强大的顺序和联邦 CDR 方法相比的竞争性能。 我们的代码公开于Cross-domain recommendation (CDR) transfers preference knowledge across related domains, but federated deployment makes cross-domain alignment difficult because the behavioral anchors that align item spaces, such as overlapping users and shared interaction signals, are often sparse, unavailable, or privacy-sensitive across clients. To address this tension, we revisit federated CDR as generation over a stable semantic item language. By representing items as discrete semantic ID (SID) sequences derived from public item-side metadata, cross-domain item alignment is induced by a shared vocabulary rather than by exchanging private interactions or aligning domain-specific embeddings. Directly federating SID-based generators, however, introduces two design constraints: the SID tokenizer must remain fixed to preserve cross-client token consistency, which creates a semantic-only bottleneck because local collaborative filtering (CF) signals cannot be globally shared or aligned; meanwhile, standard federated averaging can cause negative transfer under domain heterogeneity. To overcome these constraints, we propose FedCGR, a federated generative CDR framework that keeps the item language stable and makes adaptation explicit. FedCGR injects local CF evidence through a reliability-aware semantic interface and trains a prototype-personalized generator that selectively aggregates shared parameters according to domain relatedness while keeping domain-specific quantities local. Experiments on six Amazon cross-domain scenarios show that FedCGR consistently outperforms federated generative baselines and achieves competitive performance against strong sequential and federated CDR methods under both full-ranking and sampled evaluation protocols. 1. 简介Introduction

跨域推荐 (CDR) 跨相关领域传输知识，以缓解稀疏性和冷启动问题Cross-domain recommendation (CDR) transfers knowledge across related domains to alleviate sparsity and cold-start issues(Zang et al., 2022)。 现有的 CDR 方法通过嵌入映射、跨域特征交互和可转移用户表示学习取得了实质性进展，包括 CoNet. Existing CDR methods have made substantial progress through embedding mapping, cross-domain feature interaction, and transferable user representation learning, including CoNet(Hu et al., 2018), DDTCDR (Li and Tuzhilin, 2020), CATN (Zhao et al., 2020), RecGuru, RecGURU(Li et al., 2022)、和迪森CDR, and DisenCDR(Liu et al., 2022)。 然而，这些方法中许多都假设跨域交互的集中访问，当用户行为由不同平台或业务部门掌握时，这很难满足。 联邦学习（佛罗里达州）. However, many of these methods assume centralized access to cross-domain interactions, which is difficult to satisfy when user behavior is held by different platforms or business units. Federated Learning (FL)(McMahan et al., 2017)提供了一种自然的替代方案，允许客户协作训练模型，同时将原始交互序列保留在本地。 然而，联邦 CDR 面临着一个根本性的张力：使跨域项目空间可对齐的行为锚点（重叠用户、共现图或共享交互信号）恰恰是在联邦隔离下变得稀疏、不可用或隐私敏感的信号。offers a natural alternative by allowing clients to collaboratively train models while keeping raw interaction sequences local. Yet federated CDR faces a fundamental tension: the behavioral anchors that make cross-domain item spaces alignable—overlapping users, co-occurrence graphs, or shared interaction signals—are precisely the signals that become sparse, unavailable, or privacy-sensitive under federated isolation.

联合 CDR 方法探索了点对点协作Federated CDR methods have explored peer-to-peer collaboration(Chen et al., 2023)，解缠结序列建模, disentangled sequence modeling(Zhang et al., 2024a)、图或超图建模, graph or hypergraph modeling(Tian et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024b)，以及基于原型的对比学习, and prototype-based contrastive learning(Wang et al., 2025a)。 尽管取得了这些进步，大多数现有的联合 CDR 模型仍然具有歧视性：它们在通常特定于领域的连续表示空间中对候选项目进行评分。 如图. Despite these advances, most existing federated CDR models are still discriminative: they score candidate items in continuous representation spaces that are often domain-specific. Centralized generative CDR has already demonstrated the promise of unified semantic tokenization(Hu et al., 2026a)，但集中式交互访问与联合设置不兼容。, but centralized interaction access is incompatible with the federated setting.

然而，共享 SID 词汇表并不是联合 CDR 的即插即用解决方案。 直接联合基于 SID 的生成器引入了两个设计约束。 首先，SID tokenizer 必须保持固定以保持跨客户端令牌一致性；否则，相同的标记可能指代跨域的不一致的语义区域。 然而，修复分词器会产生一个However, a shared SID vocabulary is not a plug-and-play solution for federated CDR. Directly federating SID-based generators introduces two design constraints. First, the SID tokenizer must remain fixed to preserve cross-client token consistency; otherwise, the same token may refer to inconsistent semantic regions across domains. Fixing the tokenizer, however, creates a纯语义瓶颈semantic-only bottleneck：特定领域的协同过滤（CF）信号无法通过分词器本身合并，并且在不同客户端上独立训练的本地 CF 嵌入存在噪声、质量不均匀，并且跨域坐标不对齐。 其次，标准联合平均不足以在域异质性下生成自回归 SID。 由于 SID 预测将一个项目分解为多个连续的令牌决策，因此早期令牌分布中的扰动可能会通过前缀特里树级联并导致负转移。 因此，联合生成推荐不仅需要共享的令牌空间，还需要可靠性感知的行为注入和相似性感知的参数共享。: domain-specific collaborative filtering (CF) signals cannot be incorporated through the tokenizer itself, and local CF embeddings trained independently on different clients are noisy, non-uniform in quality, and not coordinate-aligned across domains. Second, standard federated averaging is inadequate for autoregressive SID generation under domain heterogeneity. Since SID prediction decomposes an item into multiple sequential token decisions, perturbations in early token distributions can cascade through the prefix trie and cause negative transfer. Therefore, federated generative recommendation requires not only a shared token space, but also reliability-aware behavior injection and similarity-aware parameter sharing.

我们提出 FedCGR，这是一个围绕一个原则组织的联合跨域生成推荐框架：We propose FedCGR, a federated cross-domain generative recommendation framework organized around one principle:保持项目语言稳定并使适应明确keep the item language stable and make adaptation explicit。 稳定部分是根据项目端元数据训练的固定 SID 标记器，为所有客户端提供共享的离散词汇表。 自适应部分在两个层面上实现。 首先，FedCGR 构建了. The stable part is a fixed SID tokenizer trained from item-side metadata, providing a shared discrete vocabulary across all clients. The adaptive part is implemented at two levels. First, FedCGR builds a可靠性感知语义接口reliability-aware semantic interface：每个交互的项目由其 SID 令牌嵌入加上残余 CF 信号表示，该信号的强度由项目级可靠性和客户端本地门控制。 其次，FedCGR 训练: each interacted item is represented by its SID token embedding plus a residual CF signal whose strength is controlled by item-level reliability and a client-local gate. Second, FedCGR trains a原型个性化生成器prototype-personalized generator：共享生成器参数从行为相关的域中更强大地聚合，而域嵌入、门、私人专家和本地 CF 统计数据保留在每个客户端上。 这种设计将尽可能多的组件合并到两种机制中，每种机制都解决上述两个约束之一：稳定语义接口上的可靠本地证据，以及该接口上的个性化联合生成。: shared generator parameters are aggregated more strongly from behaviorally related domains, while domain embeddings, gates, private experts, and local CF statistics stay on each client. This design merges what could appear as many components into two mechanisms that each address one of the two constraints above: reliable local evidence on a stable semantic interface, and personalized federated generation over that interface.

3.1. 概述Overview

图Figure2举例说明了 FedCGR。 中心思想是使用固定的SID空间作为域之间的通信接口，并使构建在该接口之上的所有内容都适应可靠性和域相关性。 项目端元数据映射到 SID，它在客户端之间提供共享的离散词汇表，而无需使用私有交互。 由于此词汇表在联合训练期间必须保持稳定，因此 FedCGR 在项目端预训练后不会更新 SID 标记器。 相反，每个客户端都使用从其自己的交互序列中提取的本地 CF 证据来丰富 SID 令牌表示。 由于CF嵌入是在不同客户端上独立训练的，因此它们的坐标系不具有可比性；因此，FedCGR 将每个客户端的 CF 适配器和密集辅助头保留在本地，而不是聚合它们。illustrates FedCGR. The central idea is to use a fixed SID space as the communication interface among domains, and to make everything built on top of this interface adaptive to reliability and domain relatedness. Item-side metadata is mapped to SIDs, which provide a shared discrete vocabulary across clients without using private interactions. Because this vocabulary must remain stable during federated training, FedCGR does not update the SID tokenizer after item-side pre-training. Instead, each client enriches SID token representations with local CF evidence extracted from its own interaction sequences. Since CF embeddings are trained independently on different clients, their coordinate systems are not comparable; therefore FedCGR keeps each client’s CF adapter and dense auxiliary head local rather than aggregating them.

6. 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 FedCGR，它将联邦 CDR 制定为稳定语义项目语言的生成。 可靠性感知语义接口通过置信门控残差将本地 CF 证据注入固定的 SID 表示；原型个性化生成器根据领域相关性有选择地聚合共享参数。 对六个 Amazon 场景的实验证实了联合生成基线的持续改进以及与强大的顺序和联合 CDR 方法相比的竞争性能。 消融揭示了一种交叉模式：CF 富集在高亲和力场景中占主导地位，而个性化聚合成为高异质性下的绑定约束。 尽管每轮通信量提高了 18%，但更快的收敛速度使总预算减少了 34-45%。 未来的工作可能会集成安全聚合，学习端到端的置信度估计，并将框架扩展到新域或项目在联合期间增量到达的场景。This paper proposed FedCGR, which formulates federated CDR as generation over a stable semantic item language. A reliability-aware semantic interface injects local CF evidence into fixed SID representations through confidence-gated residuals; a prototype-personalized generator selectively aggregates shared parameters according to domain relatedness. Experiments on six Amazon scenarios confirm consistent improvements over federated generative baselines and competitive performance against strong sequential and federated CDR methods. Ablations reveal a cross-over pattern: CF enrichment dominates in high-affinity scenarios while personalized aggregation becomes the binding constraint under high heterogeneity. Despite an 18% higher per-round communication, faster convergence reduces the total budget by 34–45%. Future work may integrate secure aggregation, learn confidence estimates end-to-end, and extend the framework to scenarios where new domains or items arrive incrementally during federation.
