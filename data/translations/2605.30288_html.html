摘要Abstract

中期培训已成为现代法学硕士发展的重要阶段，在最终培训后使用大规模策划的混合物来增强能力。 其数据选择问题很明显：数据在接近预训练规模的预训练式目标下进行优化，但针对下游功能进行整理，并从具有不同格式和训练角色的异构源中提取。 因此，有效的选择需要可扩展性和源自适应语义标准。 现有的基于模型的方法可以很好地扩展，但仅提供隐式质量信号。语义选择方法提供了更强的判断力，但通常假设固定的标题或标准化的数据格式。 为了解决这种不匹配问题，我们建议Mid-training has become an important stage in modern LLM development, using large-scale curated mixtures to strengthen capabilities before final post-training. Its data selection problem is distinct: the data are optimized under a pretraining-style objective at near-pretraining scale, but are curated toward downstream capabilities and drawn from heterogeneous sources with different formats and training roles. As a result, effective selection requires both scalability and source-adaptive semantic criteria. Existing model-based methods scale well, but provide only implicit quality signals. Semantic selection methods offer stronger judgments, but usually assume fixed rubrics or standardized data formats. To address this mismatch, we propose米拉MIRA，一个基于源感知的过滤框架, a source-aware filtering framework based on自锚定标题发现self-anchored rubric discovery。 关键思想是使标题构建成为数据选择的一部分：MIRA 首先发现每个源组应该评估什么，然后将这些判断提炼到可扩展的学生评分器中以进行全语料库过滤。 在具有 21 个源和 5 个源组的面向代码的中期训练中，MIRA 在 9 个代码基准测试中的表现优于选择基线，并且在仅使用一半标记的情况下与完整语料库的运行相匹配。. The key idea is to make rubric construction part of data selection: MIRA first discovers what should be evaluated for each source group, then distills those judgments into scalable student scorers for full-corpus filtering. On code-oriented mid-training with 21 sources and 5 source groups, MIRA outperforms selection baselines across nine code benchmarks and matches the full-corpus run while using only half the tokens.

1 简介Introduction

中期训练已成为现代大语言模型（LLM）的关键阶段Mid-training has become a key stage in modern large language model (LLM)(Touvron et al., 2023; OpenAI, 2023; Yang et al., 2024, 2025a, 2025b, 2026b)开发，但其数据分布与广泛的预训练和最终训练后不同development, but its data distribution differs from both broad pretraining and final post-trainingLiu et al. (2026)。它保持预训练式的目标和接近预训练的规模，同时使用精选数据来增强推理、编码、长上下文理解和工具使用等下游能力. It keeps a pretraining-style objective and near-pretraining scale, while using curated data to strengthen downstream capabilities such as reasoning, coding, long-context understanding, and tool useteam et al. (2025)。这创建了一个混合体：它包括类似预训练的源，例如过滤后的网络文档、代码、数学和长技术文本，以及更结构化的源，例如指令式示例、推理跟踪、工具使用日志和多轮智能体轨迹. This creates a hybrid mixture: it includes pretraining-like sources such as filtered web documents, code, mathematics, and long technical text, as well as more structured sources such as instruction-style examples, reasoning traces, tool-use logs, and multi-turn agent trajectories(Grattafiori et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025c; Tu et al., 2025)。 因此，中期训练数据形成了大规模、面向能力、高度异构的、不同格式和训练角色的来源混合体。. As a result, mid-training data form a large-scale, capability-oriented, and highly heterogeneous mixture of sources with different formats and training roles.

这种组合使得很难重用为训练前或训练后设计的数据选择方法。 面向预训练的方法提供可扩展的信号，例如困惑度、梯度信息、影响估计或分布匹配This combination makes it difficult to reuse data selection methods designed for either pretraining or post-training. Pretraining-oriented methods provide scalable signals such as perplexity, gradient information, influence estimates, or distributional matching(Marion et al., 2023; Penedo et al., 2024; Soldaini et al., 2024; Xie et al., 2023; Xia et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025a)。 然而，这些方法依赖于隐式模型或分布级信号，并且没有明确定义使示例在其源格式中有用的语义标准。 对于结构化的中期训练源，困难或有影响力的示例可能反映有用的信号，但它也可能反映格式化伪影、不一致的轨迹或不正确的推理。 面向培训后的方法，包括人类定义的标准、偏好信号、LLM 判断或学习质量评分器. However, these methods rely on implicit model- or distribution-level signals, and do not explicitly define the semantic criteria that make an example useful within its source format. For structured mid-training sources, a difficult or influential example may reflect useful signal, but it may also reflect formatting artifacts, inconsistent trajectories, or incorrect reasoning. Post-training-oriented methods, including human-defined criteria, preference signals, LLM judgments, or learned quality scorers(Du et al., 2023; Ivison et al., 2025; Li et al., 2023b; Wettig et al., 2024; Peng et al., 2025)，提供更强的语义监督，但通常假设固定标准或更标准化的数据格式。 这使得它们很难直接应用于训练中期的混合物，其中来源在格式、训练作用和表明质量的证据方面有所不同。 因此，现有的方法存在不匹配的问题：预训练过滤器进行缩放，但不定义特定于源的语义质量，而后训练过滤器提供语义监督，但假设标准已知。, offer stronger semantic supervision, but typically assume fixed criteria or more standardized data formats. This makes them difficult to apply directly to mid-training mixtures, where sources differ in format, training role, and the evidence that indicates quality. Thus, existing methods leave a mismatch: pretraining filters scale but do not define source-specific semantic quality, while post-training filters provide semantic supervision but assume the criteria are already known.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 训练中期Mid-training

中期培训已成为大规模预培训和特定任务后培训之间的一个专门阶段，旨在加强一般网络语料库中代表性不足或组织薄弱的能力Mid-training has emerged as a dedicated stage between large-scale pretraining and task-specific post-training, designed to strengthen capabilities that are underrepresented or weakly organized in general web corpora(Tu et al., 2025)。 与特定领域的持续预训练不同，后者主要使模型适应狭窄的目标分布. Unlike domain-specific continued pretraining, which primarily adapts a model toward a narrow target distribution(Zhang et al., 2025)中期培训在更广泛的能力水平上进行，提高编码、数学推理和长上下文理解等技能，同时保留一般语言能力。 实证研究表明，当训练前和训练后分布出现分歧时（例如在代码和数学方面），训练中期尤其有效, mid-training works at a broader capability level, improving skills such as coding, mathematical reasoning, and long-context understanding while preserving general language competence. Empirical studies show that mid-training is especially effective when the pretraining and post-training distributions diverge, such as in code and mathematics(Liu et al., 2026)，并且可以进一步放大后续强化学习的收益, and can further amplify the gains of subsequent reinforcement learning(Wang et al., 2025c)。 互补的工作研究如何在持续训练期间混合和安排领域，包括基于学习速度的动态采样. A complementary line of work studies how to mix and schedule domains during continued training, including dynamic sampling based on learning velocity(Luo et al., 2024)和域混合比例的缩放定律and scaling laws for domain mixture ratios(Gu et al., 2024)。 这些工作确立了中期训练数据组合的重要性，但它们主要关注阶段、域、混合或时间表，而 MIRA 则关注如何从异构中期训练源中选择单个记录的更细粒度的问题。. These works establish the importance of mid-training data composition, but they mainly focus on stages, domains, mixtures, or schedules, whereas MIRA focuses on the finer-grained question of how to select individual records from heterogeneous mid-training sources.

2.2 训练数据选择Training Data Selection

训练数据选择方法可以大致分为三类。 (1) 启发式和语料库级过滤方法使用重复数据删除、困惑度、毒性过滤、教育价值评分、领域分类或轻量级分类器来管理大型网络语料库Training data selection methods can be broadly grouped into three categories. (1) Heuristic and corpus-level filtering methods use deduplication, perplexity, toxicity filtering, educational-value scoring, domain classification, or lightweight classifiers to curate large web corpora(Thrush et al., 2025; Soldaini et al., 2024; Penedo et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025b)。 这些方法是可扩展的，但提供的关于结构化示例是否教导预期功能的语义证据有限。 (2) 优化和目标感知选择方法使用代理损失、梯度、影响估计或分布匹配来选择目标分布或评估目标的数据. These methods are scalable, but provide limited semantic evidence about whether a structured example teaches the intended capability. (2) Optimization- and target-aware selection methods use proxy losses, gradients, influence estimates, or distribution matching to select data for a target distribution or evaluation objective(Xie et al., 2023; Shum et al., 2025; Engstrom et al., 2024; Xia et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025a; Pan et al., 2024; Du et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2024)。 它们与下游效用更直接相关，但它们的信号仍然是隐含的。 (3) 基于语义和评分标准的选择方法使用人类定义的标准、偏好信号、法学硕士作为评判注释或学习评分器来评估教学质量、多样性、教育价值或通用实用性. They are more directly tied to downstream utility, but their signals remain implicit. (3) Semantic and rubric-based selection methods use human-defined criteria, preference signals, LLM-as-judge annotations, or learned scorers to evaluate instruction quality, diversity, educational value, or generic usefulness(Du et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023b; Ivison et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024; Wettig et al., 2024; Peng et al., 2025)。 这些方法更容易解释，但通常依赖于固定或全球共享的标准。 MIRA 的不同之处在于使评分标准构建源自适应：它从采样记录中引入特定于组的锚评分标准，将其提炼为学生评分者，并在整个语料库中应用源感知过滤。. These methods are more interpretable, but typically rely on fixed or globally shared criteria. MIRA differs by making rubric construction source-adaptive: it induces group-specific anchor rubrics from sampled records, distills them into student scorers, and applies source-aware filtering across the full corpus.

3 方法Method

3.1 概述Overview

MIRA 通过将标题构建作为过滤管道的一部分来解决异构中间训练数据选择问题。关键的挑战是不同的来源暴露出不同的能力信号，因此不能假设质量标准是固定的或全球共享的。 因此，MIRA 首先从采样记录中发现源组特定的评分标准，然后将判断提炼成可应用于整个语料库的高效评分器。MIRA addresses heterogeneous mid-training data selection by making rubric construction part of the filtering pipeline. The key challenge is that different sources expose different capability signals, so the quality criteria cannot be assumed to be fixed or globally shared. MIRA therefore first discovers source-group-specific rubrics from sampled records, then distills the judgments into efficient scorers that can be applied to the full corpus.

The third aggregates student scores using source-conditioned reliability estimates. The fourth converts aggregated scores into a filtered corpus through source-aware retention. This decomposition separates the semantic part of selection from the scalable part: the frontier judge is used to identify what should be scored for each source group, while student scorers and source-aware aggregation perform full-corpus filtering.

3.2 自锚定量规发现Self-Anchored Rubric Discovery

每个中间训练来源都具有不同的能力概况，因此没有一个全球标准可以忠实地评估所有这些来源。然而，在现代训练中期语料库的规模下，为每个来源手动编写单独的标题是不切实际的。因此，MIRA 的运行粒度为Mid-training sources each carry a distinct capability profile, so no single global rubric can faithfully evaluate all of them. 3.3 锚定法官蒸馏Anchored Judge Distillation

自由形式的判断非常适合标题发现，但由于每个采样记录都收到一组不同的维度，因此它们不能直接充当学生评分员的训练标签，因为学生评分员必须在所有记录中产生一致的输出。因此，我们修复了发现的锚并使用它们构建一个稳定的、可蒸馏的标签空间。Free-form judgments are well-suited to rubric discovery, but because each sampled record receives a different set of dimensions, they cannot directly serve as training labels for a student scorer that must produce consistent output across all records. 3.5 源保留选择Source-Preserving Selection

不同训练来源的分数分布各不相同：简洁的来源 数学推导和长智能体轨迹的来源将具有不同的平均分数和不同的分布，反映了能力差异而不是统一的质量。因此，对所有来源应用单一全局截止将首先耗尽平均水平较低的来源，并且由于每个来源对应于一个能力区域，因此来源损耗直接转化为能力损耗。Score distributions vary across mid-training sources: a source of concise mathematical derivations and a source of long agent trajectories will have different mean scores and different spreads, reflecting capability differences rather than uniform quality. Applying a single global cutoff to all sources will therefore drain the lower-mean sources first, and because each source corresponds to a capability region, source attrition translates directly into capability attrition.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presented米拉MIRA，一种用于异构中间训练数据的源感知过滤框架。 MIRA 从采样记录中发现特定于组的锚定规则，将其提炼为高效的学生评分器，并应用可靠性感知聚合和源感知保留阈值来进行全语料库选择。在分为 5 组的 21 个来源中，MIRA-Group 在 9 个代码相关基准中实现了最佳 25B 代币宏观平均值，优于 PPL、DSIR、DataMan 和随机选择，同时以代币预算的一半匹配未过滤的 50B 代币语料库。这些结果表明，训练中期数据选择受益于源自适应质量标准，而不仅仅是可扩展的评分。, a source-aware filtering framework for heterogeneous mid-training data. MIRA discovers group-specific anchor rubrics from sampled records, distills them into efficient student scorers, and applies reliability-aware aggregation with source-aware retention thresholds for full-corpus selection. On 21 sources organized into 5 groups, MIRA-Group achieves the best 25B-token macro average across nine code-related benchmarks, outperforming PPL, DSIR, DataMan, and Random selection while matching the unfiltered 50B-token corpus at half the token budget. These results show that mid-training data selection benefits from source-adaptive quality criteria, not only scalable scoring.

局限性Limitations

MIRA 通过以下方式解决异构中间训练数据的源感知过滤问题： 得出超出单一全局标准的语义质量分数。然而， 过滤只是训练中期数据管理的一部分。更广泛的决策 例如资源发现、混合比设计、课程安排、 重复数据删除和污染控制仍然不属于这项工作的范围。未来的工作可能包括将 MIRA 集成到更广泛的数据混合优化管道中，并研究源感知质量分数如何与课程设计和培训时间采样策略相互作用。MIRA addresses source-aware filtering for heterogeneous mid-training data by deriving semantic quality scores beyond a single global criterion. However, filtering is only one part of mid-training data management. Broader decisions such as source discovery, mixture-ratio design, curriculum scheduling, deduplication, and contamination control remain outside the scope of this work. Future work could include both integrating MIRA into broader data-mixture optimization pipelines and studying how source-aware quality scores interact with curriculum design and training-time sampling strategies.
