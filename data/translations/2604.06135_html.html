摘要Abstract

高效的数据加载仍然是近期量子机器学习的瓶颈。现有方案（角度、幅度和基础编码）要么未充分利用指数希尔伯特空间容量，要么需要超出噪声中等规模量子硬件的相干预算的电路深度。我们引入了基于镜头的量子编码（SBQE），这是一种数据嵌入策略，根据多个初始量子态上的数据相关经典分布来分配硬件的本机资源镜头。通过将射击计数视为可学习的自由度，SBQE 产生了一种混合状态表示，其期望值在经典概率中是线性的，因此可以由非线性激活函数组成。我们证明了 SBQE 在结构上相当于一个多层感知器，其权重由量子电路实现，并且我们描述了一个硬件兼容的实现协议。 Fashion MNIST 和 Semeion 手写数字的基准测试，每个模型有 10 个独立的初始化，表明 SBQE 实现了Efficient data loading remains a bottleneck for near‑term quantum machine‑learning. Existing schemes (angle, amplitude, and basis encoding) either underuse the exponential Hilbert‑space capacity or require circuit depths that exceed the coherence budgets of noisy intermediate‑scale quantum hardware. We introduce Shot‑Based Quantum Encoding (SBQE), a data embedding strategy that distributes the hardware’s native resource, shots, according to a data‑dependent classical distribution over multiple initial quantum states. By treating the shot counts as a learnable degree of freedom, SBQE produces a mixed‑state representation whose expectation values are linear in the classical probabilities and can therefore be composed with non‑linear activation functions. We show that SBQE is structurally equivalent to a multilayer perceptron whose weights are realised by quantum circuits, and we describe a hardware‑compatible implementation protocol. The present work addresses the first challenge.

当前的数据加载范例分为两个主要系列。Current data‑loading paradigms fall into two main families.角度编码Angle encoding通过将特征值映射到单量子位旋转上，将一个特征分配给一个量子位assigns one feature to one qubit by mapping the feature value onto a single‑qubit rotation[27, 32]。 该策略受益于浅层电路和简单的硬件实现，但其表示能力仅与量子位的数量线性扩展，使得指数希尔伯特空间维度在很大程度上尚未开发。 数据重新上传技术通过在整个变分电路中插入额外的编码层来部分缓解这一限制，但代价是更大的深度和噪声敏感性. This strategy benefits from shallow circuits and straightforward hardware implementation, but its representational capacity scales only linearly with the number of qubits, leaving the exponential Hilbert‑space dimension largely untapped. Data‑reuploading techniques partially mitigate this limitation by inserting additional encoding layers throughout the variational circuit, at the cost of greater depth and noise susceptibility[26].

V 讨论Discussion

SBQE 使用了一个被忽视的资源，SBQE uses an overlooked resource,经典分配的镜头classically allocated shots，以避免数据加载瓶颈。 通过消除编码门，我们减少了深度和相干误差累积，使该方案与当今的兼容, to sidestep the data‑loading bottleneck. By eliminating encoding gates, we reduce both depth and coherent error accumulation, rendering the scheme compatible with today’s∼100\sim\!100－门错误预算。 此外，SBQE 与基于测量和经典阴影后处理自然地结合在一起；将框架扩展到误差估计器是一个有前途的方向。噪声引起的正则化技术‑gate error budgets. Further, SBQE meshes naturally with measurement‑based and classical‑shadow post‑processing; extending the framework to error‑mitigated estimators is a promising direction. Noise‑induced regularisation techniques[16]可以应用于 SBQE 之上，以进一步提高噪声硬件上的泛化能力。could be applied on top of SBQE to further improve generalisation on noisy hardware.

Because SBQE encodes data through classical probabilities rather than through rotation angles, the resulting model class is not restricted to periodic functions, and preliminary experiments on synthetic two‑dimensional tasks confirm that SBQE can separate linear data with ease.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 SBQE，这是一种数据加载方法，用概率镜头分配代替门重编码。 由此产生的混合状态形式在结构上相当于经典的多层感知器，其权重矩阵位于量子电路内部，并且它自然地与变分层组合。 在两个图像分类基准上，SBQE 匹配或优于幅度编码和宽度匹配的经典网络，同时需要零编码门并保持在 NISQ 深度预算内。 这些结果表明，每个量子实验中都存在的射击自由度是当前硬件上数据编码的实用通道。We have introduced SBQE, a data‑loading method that replaces gate‑heavy encodings with probabilistic shot allocations. The resulting mixed‑state formalism is structurally equivalent to a classical multilayer perceptron whose weight matrices live inside a quantum circuit, and it composes naturally with variational layers. On two image‑classification benchmarks, SBQE matches or outperforms both amplitude encoding and a width‑matched classical network while requiring zero encoding gates and staying within NISQ depth budgets. These results suggest that the shot degree of freedom, already present in every quantum experiment, is a practical channel for data encoding on current hardware.
