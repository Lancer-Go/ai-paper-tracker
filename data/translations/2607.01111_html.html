Carnegie Mellon University
摘要Abstract

机器人策略在实际环境中部署时不可避免地会遇到失败。 天真的重试经常会重复相同的错误，而许多现有的恢复方法依赖于人为干预。在本文中，我们建议Robot policies inevitably encounter failures when deployed in real environments. Naive retries often repeat the same mistakes, while many existing recovery methods rely on human intervention. In this paper, we proposeF艾鲁尔-ailure-A洁具wareRetry（FAR），一个框架，使机器人能够在测试时从以前的失败中学习，相应地调整它们的行为，并最终自主完成任务。 FAR 结合了失败对比偏好适应（Failure-Contrastive Preference Adaptation），它从失败中构建偏好学习数据，以引导策略远离以前不成功的行为，并在重试期间进行轻量级的动作扰动，以鼓励局部探索。 我们进一步将成功的恢复轨迹纳入培训循环中，以不断改进政策。模拟和现实世界操纵任务中的实验表明，FAR 显着提高了成功率和鲁棒性，在模拟中比标准扩散策略平均增益为 17.6%，在现实世界中平均增益为 11.7%。此外，在持续策略改进过程中，FAR 通过利用信息丰富的失败案例，显着提高了重置和时间步预算下的数据效率。etry (FAR), a framework that enables robots to learn from previous failures at test time, adapt their behavior accordingly, and eventually complete the task autonomously. FAR combines Failure-Contrastive Preference Adaptation, which constructs preference learning data from failures to steer the policy away from previously unsuccessful behaviors, with lightweight action perturbations during retries to encourage local exploration. We further incorporate successful recovery trajectories into a training loop for continual policy improvement. Experiments in both simulation and real-world manipulation tasks show that FAR substantially improves success rates and robustness, with average gains of 17.6% over the standard diffusion policy in simulation and 11.7% in the real world. In addition, FAR significantly improves data efficiency under both reset and timestep budgets during continual policy improvement by exploiting informative failure cases.

1 简介Introduction

经过离线专家演示训练的机器人策略在广泛的操作任务中取得了出色的性能Robot policies trained on offline expert demonstrations have achieved strong performance on a wide range of manipulation tasks[8, 33, 3, 89]。然而，当部署在真实环境中时，这些策略不可避免地会遇到失败，例如在执行过程中抓取时丢失目标或掉落物体。从此类故障中恢复具有挑战性，因为这些状态很少被离线演示所覆盖，并且通常超出预训练策略的分布范围。因此，简单地重新执行相同的策略常常会重复相同的错误。. However, when deployed in real environments, these policies inevitably encounter failures, such as missing the target during grasping or dropping an object during execution. Recovering from such failures is challenging because these states are rarely covered by offline demonstrations and are often out-of-distribution for the pretrained policy. As a result, simply re-executing the same policy often repeats the same mistake.

现有方法通常依赖人工干预来纠正机器人行为并收集额外数据以供未来训练Existing methods often rely on human intervention to correct robot behavior and collect additional data for future training[30, 29, 52]，包括DAgger和相关的人机交互方法, including DAgger and related human-in-the-loop methods[54, 26, 25, 24]。 这些方法虽然有效，但需要大量的人力。 这就提出了一个问题：机器人是否能够从失败中学习、在测试时调整其行为并自主恢复。. While effective, these approaches require substantial human effort. This raises the question of whether a robot can learn from failures, adapt its behavior at test time, and recover autonomously.

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个从失败中学习并在测试时调整机器人行为的框架，以改进基于重试的恢复In this work, we propose a framework for learning from failure and adapting robot behavior at test time to improve retry-based recovery[11, 9]。有效的重试面临两个关键挑战：策略在失败后往往会重复同样的错误，其动作分布来自离线演示，限制了对分布外恢复状态的探索。我们的方法通过失败对比偏好适应（FCPA）来解决这些挑战，它根据失败经验构建偏好学习信号，以引导策略远离以前不成功的行为，并在重试期间进行行动扰动，以鼓励局部探索。它们共同提高重试成功率并生成多样化的恢复轨迹以持续改进政策。. Effective retries face two key challenges: the policy tends to repeat the same mistake after failure, and its action distribution comes from offline demonstrations, limiting exploration in out-of-distribution recovery states. Our approach addresses these challenges with Failure-Contrastive Preference Adaptation (FCPA), which constructs preference learning signals from failure experience to steer the policy away from previously unsuccessful behaviors, and action perturbations during retries to encourage local exploration. Together, they improve retry success and generate diverse recovery trajectories for continual policy improvement.

这些恢复轨迹很有价值，因为它们揭示了当前策略的局限性和失败边界，提供了离线训练所缺乏的监督。因此，我们利用它们来进一步改进政策。 从离线演示中预先训练的策略开始，机器人 与环境交互并使用 FAR 对每个任务进行多次尝试。 由此产生的轨迹被纳入在线微调中，使策略能够从具有挑战性的故障案例中学习，提高恢复性能并实现更高的数据效率。These recovery trajectories are valuable because they reveal the limitations and failure boundaries of the current policy, providing supervision that is absent from offline training. We therefore use them to further improve the policy. Starting from a policy pretrained on offline demonstrations, the robot interacts with the environment and makes multiple attempts on each task using FAR. The resulting trajectories are incorporated into online finetuning, allowing the policy to learn from challenging failure cases, improve recovery performance, and achieve greater data efficiency.

我们根据涵盖九个机器人操作任务的三个模拟基准以及 xArm 平台上的三个实际任务来评估 FAR。 我们的贡献如下： (1)我们介绍We evaluate FAR on three simulation benchmarks spanning nine robot manipulation tasks, as well as three real-world tasks on an xArm platform. Our contributions are as follows: (1) We introduceF艾鲁尔-ailure-A洁具wareRetry (FAR)，一种基于重试的测试时恢复框架，它将故障感知适应与轻量级探索相结合，以实现更有效的故障恢复。 (2) 我们表明，FAR 生成信息丰富的恢复轨迹，为持续的政策改进提供有用的监督。 (3) 我们证明 FAR 提高了模拟和现实世界中的任务成功率，同时减少了在线学习期间昂贵的环境重置的需要。etry (FAR), a retry-based test-time recovery framework that combines failure-aware adaptation with lightweight exploration for more effective failure recovery. (2) We show that FAR generates informative recovery trajectories that provide useful supervision for continual policy improvement. (3) We demonstrate that FAR improves task success rates in both simulation and the real world, while reducing the need for costly environment resets during online learning.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 FAR，一种使策略能够从失败中学习并在测试时进行改进的方法。我们进一步表明，收集的恢复经验可以纳入持续的政策改进中，帮助政策解决硬故障案例并随着时间的推移而改进。我们在三个模拟环境中的九个操作任务以及三个现实世界任务中评估了 FAR。结果表明，FAR 提高了预训练策略的性能，并且无需重置环境即可实现恢复。我们关于持续政策改进的结果进一步表明，FAR 提高了学习效率并减少了人为干预的需要，展示了其在部署过程中强大且数据高效的自我改进的潜力。We propose FAR, a method that enables a policy to learn from failures and improve at test time. We further show that the collected recovery experience can be incorporated into continual policy improvement, helping the policy address hard failure cases and improve over time. We evaluate FAR in three simulation environments across nine manipulation tasks, as well as on three real-world tasks. The results show that FAR improves the performance of pretrained policies and enables recovery without environment resets. Our results on continual policy improvement further show that FAR increases learning efficiency and reduces the need for human intervention, demonstrating its potential for robust and data-efficient self-improvement during deployment.

局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and Future Work.首先，我们的实验重点是在单任务设置中从头开始训练的扩散策略。未来的一个重要方向是评估所提出的预训练策略方法，例如 VLA 模型，它可以提供更丰富的先验知识和更广泛的泛化能力。其次，我们当前的框架不包括原则性的故障检测机制，而是依赖于环境反馈和时间限制。在未来的工作中，我们计划结合预训练模型（例如 VLM）来进行任务跟踪和故障检测。通过启用阶段级重试和恢复，这可以进一步提高复杂的长期任务的性能。First, our experiments focus on diffusion policies trained from scratch in single-task settings. An important future direction is to evaluate the proposed method on pretrained policies, such as VLA models, which may offer richer prior knowledge and broader generalization. Second, our current framework does not include a principled failure detection mechanism, and instead relies on environment feedback and time limits. In future work, we plan to incorporate pretrained models, such as VLMs, for task tracking and failure detection. This could further improve performance on complex long-horizon tasks by enabling stage-level retry and recovery.
