6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了文本参数图优化（TPGO），这是一个通过结构化图演化而不是平面提示调整来改进代理系统的框架。通过将代理配置分解为模块化文本组件，使用基于执行的文本梯度信号对其进行细化，并通过 GRAO 利用优化内存，TPGO 可以实现更稳定、更有效的代理优化。 MCP-Universe 和 GAIA 上的实验显示了有效性和效率的持续提升，这表明结构化的、基于经验的优化是减少代理工程的手动负担的一种有前景的方法。We introduced Textual Parameter Graph Optimization (TPGO), a framework that improves agent systems through structured graph evolution rather than flat prompt tuning. By decomposing agent configurations into modular textual components, refining them with execution-grounded textual-gradient signals, and leveraging optimization memory through GRAO, TPGO enables more stable and effective agent optimization. Experiments on MCP-Universe and GAIA show consistent gains in both effectiveness and efficiency, suggesting that structured, experience-informed optimization is a promising approach for reducing the manual burden of agent engineering.

7 局限性Limitations

TPGO 有一些局限性。这是一个没有正式优化保证的经验框架，因为“文本梯度”是启发式反馈信号而不是分析梯度。它的优化循环也可能成本高昂，因为每个提案都必须在任务子集上进行验证，这可能会限制更大基准或更复杂代理系统的可扩展性。此外，TPGO 依赖于用于解析、诊断和提案生成的底层 LLM 的能力；较弱的模型可能会产生更嘈杂的反馈或效率较低的更新。最后，我们当前的实现仅限于使用一组预定义的图编辑操作进行文本参数优化，并且尚未考虑主干模型选择、数值超参数或更丰富的结构变化（例如节点合并和子图生成）。TPGO has several limitations. It is an empirical framework without formal optimization guarantees, since “textual gradients” are heuristic feedback signals rather than analytical gradients. Its optimization loop can also be costly, as each proposal must be validated on task subsets, which may limit scalability on larger benchmarks or more complex agent systems. In addition, TPGO depends on the capabilities of the underlying LLMs used for parsing, diagnosis, and proposal generation; weaker models may yield noisier feedback or less effective updates. Finally, our current implementation is limited to textual parameter optimization with a predefined set of graph edit operations, and does not yet consider backbone model selection, numerical hyperparameters, or richer structural changes such as node merging and subgraph generation.
