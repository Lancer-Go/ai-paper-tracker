摘要Abstract

在异构人群和机器人车队中安全高效的形状感知导航仍然具有挑战性。传统方法通常假设同质机器人、稀疏工作空间、简化几何结构、离线计算或手工设计参数来使问题易于处理，这限制了它们在密集人群场景中的部署。为此，我们提出了形状感知强化学习模型预测控制（SRL-MPC），这是一种在形状异构的人群中安全、高效、自适应导航的方法，无需进行几何简化。为了编码形状感知安全性，我们根据支持函数变换从几何分离特征（GSF）制定高阶控制屏障函数（HOCBF）约束。然后，强化学习 (RL) 框架学习读取 GSF 并输出实时 MPC 参数更新的神经策略，使 MPC 求解器能够适应邻近的人群几何形状。 SRL-MPC的主要优点是它保留了MPC的安全结构和泛化性，同时集成了RL的适应性和智能性。使用任意形状的机器人车队在随机人群场景中进行的实验证明了 SRL-MPC 的有效性、可扩展性和鲁棒性。结果表明，SRL-MPC 在安全性和适应性方面大大优于代表性基线。Safe and efficient shape-aware navigation in heterogeneous crowds and robot fleets remains challenging. Traditional approaches often assume homogeneous robots, sparse workspaces, simplified geometry, offline computation, or handcrafted parameters to make the problem tractable, which limits their deployment in dense crowd scenarios. Toward this end, we propose Shape-Aware Reinforcement Learned Model Predictive Control (SRL-MPC), a method for safe, efficient, and adaptive navigation in crowds with heterogeneous shapes without geometry simplification. To encode shape-aware safety, we formulate high-order control barrier function (HOCBF) constraints from geometric separation features (GSFs) based on support function transformation. A reinforcement learning (RL) framework then learns a neural policy that reads GSFs and outputs real-time MPC parameter updates, enabling the MPC solver to adapt to neighboring crowd geometries. The key advantage of SRL-MPC is that it preserves the safety structure and generalizability of MPC while integrating the adaptability and intelligence of RL. Experiments in randomized crowd scenarios with arbitrary shaped robot fleets demonstrate the effectiveness, scalability, and robustness of SRL-MPC. The results show that SRL-MPC substantially outperforms representative baselines in safety and adaptability.项目网站：Project website: https://hanruihua.github.io/srl_mpc_project/

1 简介Introduction

在具有异质形状的人群中安全导航是与其他机器人和人类共享空间中操作的机器人的核心能力Safe navigation in crowds with heterogeneous shapes is a central capability for robots operating in shared spaces with other robots and humans[9]。当大量机器人必须同时满足安全性、效率和运动学约束时，这个问题就变得很困难。现有方法，包括基于速度障碍 (VO) 的方法. The problem becomes difficult when a large number of robots must satisfy safety, efficiency, and kinematic constraints simultaneously. Existing approaches, including velocity obstacle (VO)-based methods[32, 11, 25]，基于优化的方法, optimization-based methods[40, 24, 21, 22]，以及基于强化学习（RL）的方法, and reinforcement learning (RL)-based methods[4, 13, 33, 37]，已经显示出有希望的结果，但它们通常依赖于同质机器人、稀疏工作空间或简化的圆形表示等假设, have shown promising results, but they often rely on assumptions such as homogeneous robots, sparse workspaces, or simplified circular shape representations[32, 4, 14, 31]，以及针对特定场景进行离线计算或反复调整参数, as well as offline computation or repeatedly tuned parameters for specific scenarios[40, 22, 20]。这些假设使问题易于处理，但它们也限制了在密集和动态人群中的部署，如果不明确考虑几何信息，机器人可能会被卡住或碰撞。. These assumptions make the problem tractable, but they also limit deployment in dense and dynamic crowds, where robots may get stuck or collide if the geometric information is not considered explicitly.

核心难点是实时避免与密集、动态、任意形状的物体发生碰撞。基于强化学习的方法可以针对特定场景学习强大的神经策略，但它们很容易过度拟合，并且可能无法推广到任意形状或扩展到大量人群The core difficulty is to avoid collisions with dense, dynamic, and arbitrarily shaped objects in real time. RL-based methods can learn strong neural policies for specific scenarios, but they are prone to overfitting and may not generalize to arbitrary shapes or scale to large crowds[31]。基于优化的方法能够通过紧凑集明确制定约束来处理静态形状的障碍物. Optimization-based approaches have the ability to handle static shaped obstacles by explicitly formulating constraints by compact sets[40, 17]或构建先前的网格地图or building a prior grid map[28]。最近的工作强调了将学习与优化相结合以整合这些优势的前景，例如将可微模型预测控制（MPC）嵌入到行为批评学习中. Recent works highlight the promise of combining learning with optimization to integrate these advantages, such as embedding differentiable model predictive control (MPC) into actor-critic learning[27]，并使用基于采样的 MPC 内部学习动态进行敏捷自适应控制, and using learned dynamics inside sampling based MPC for agile adaptive control[36]。然而，这些方法主要关注单个机器人敏捷控制，其安全特性通常取决于学习成本或动力学的准确性，而不是明确的形状感知安全项。. However, these methods mainly focus on single robot agile control, and their safety properties typically depend on the accuracy of learned costs or dynamics rather than explicit shape-aware safety terms.

为此，本文提出To this end, this paper proposesSRL-MPC，一种分布式形状感知强化学习模型预测控制（MPC）框架，用于在具有任意形状障碍物或其他机器人的人群中进行机器人导航。其关键思想是在原始优化问题中制定基于几何分离特征（GSF）的安全集作为高阶控制障碍函数（HOCBF）约束，同时使用强化学习根据邻近代理 GSF 调整 MPC 参数。问题分解后，局部 MPC 子问题通过软形状感知残差惩罚来处理 HOCBF 条件。这样，强化学习不会取代基于模型的规划器，而是调整显式形状感知 HOCBF-MPC 问题的参数。这种方法有几个优点。首先，与基于强化学习的方法不同，基于强化学习的方法对精心设计的奖励函数很敏感，并且可能会受到有限泛化的影响, a distributed shape-aware reinforcement learned Model Predictive Control (MPC) framework for robot navigation in crowds with arbitrary shaped obstacles or other robots. The key idea is to formulate a safe set based on geometric separation features (GSFs) as high-order control barrier function (HOCBF) constraints in the primal optimization problem, while using RL to adjust MPC parameters based on the neighboring agent GSFs. After problem decomposition, the local MPC subproblem handles the HOCBF condition through a soft shape-aware residual penalty. In this way, RL does not replace the model-based planner, instead, it adapts the parameters of an explicit shape-aware HOCBF-MPC problem. This approach has several advantages. First, unlike RL-based approaches that are sensitive to carefully designed reward functions and may suffer from limited generalization[14, 37]，所提出的方法学习显式形状感知 HOCBF-MPC 问题的参数自适应，而不是无约束的端到端策略。其次，HOCBF-MPC 参数根据相邻 GSF 进行调整，避免了许多基于优化的方法所需的针对不同场景的重复手动权重调整, the proposed method learns parameter adaptation for an explicit shape-aware HOCBF-MPC problem rather than an unconstrained end-to-end policy. Second, the HOCBF-MPC parameters are adapted from neighboring GSFs, avoiding repeated manual weight tuning for different scenarios as required by many optimization-based approaches[22]。第三，所提出的方法直接处理显式凸几何形状表示，并且非凸对象可以表示为凸组件的并集。这种表示比近似圆形模型更准确且更通用。最后，遵循问题分解技术. Third, the proposed method directly handles explicit convex geometric shape representations, and nonconvex objects can be represented as unions of convex components. This representation is more accurate and generalizable than approximated circular shape models. Finally, following the problem decomposition technique in[16]，部署的本地 HOCBF-MPC 子问题足够简单，可以为每个机器人实时求解，从而形成密集动态人群导航的实用解决方案。, the deployed local HOCBF-MPC subproblem is simple enough to solve in real time for each robot, resulting in a practical solution for dense dynamic crowd navigation.

为凸显成效To highlight the effectiveness ofSRL-MPC，我们在由多个差分驱动机器人组成的高度随机场景中对其进行评估，其中位置、目标和形状都是随机生成的。这对于现有方法来说是相当具有挑战性的，而结果表明, we evaluate it in highly randomized scenarios consisting of multiple differential-driven robots, where the positions, goals, and shapes are all randomly generated. This is quite challenging for existing methods, while results show thatSRL-MPC在任务完成、安全性和稳健性方面优于基线，尤其是随着人群密度的增加。outperforms the baselines in terms of task completion, safety, and robustness, especially as crowd density increases.

6 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了This paper presentsSRL-MPC，一种形状感知强化学习 MPC 框架，用于在具有多个机器人和障碍物的拥挤动态环境中避免碰撞。它通过基于支持函数变换的 GSF 表示几何约束，在耦合原始问题中制定 2 度 HOCBF 约束，并将在线规划分解为 GSF 更新和局部 HOCBF-MPC 子问题，其中分解的 HOCBF 残差受到软惩罚。强化学习根据邻近的形状感知几何特征调整 MPC 参数，而执行的控制仍然是显式 MPC 优化问题的解决方案。 在具有密度扫描的随机多边形场景中进行的实验表明, a shape-aware reinforcement learned MPC framework for collision avoidance in crowded dynamic environments with multiple robots and obstacles. It represents geometric constraints through GSFs based on support function transformation, formulates degree-2 HOCBF constraints in the coupled primal problem, and decomposes online planning into a GSFs update and a local HOCBF-MPC subproblem where the decomposed HOCBF residual is softly penalized. Reinforcement learning adapts MPC parameters from neighboring shape-aware geometric features, while the executed control remains the solution of an explicit MPC optimization problem. Experiments in random polygon scenarios with density sweeps show thatSRL-MPC在代表性基线中实现了最高的成功率，特别是在基线急剧下降的密集场景中。消融表明，分解的软 HOCBF 惩罚比静态距离裕度惩罚更可靠，并且学习的参数自适应提高了固定手工设置的鲁棒性。未来的工作包括不确定性感知的邻居预测、更丰富的非凸体分解以及具有机载传感和计算的现实世界部署。achieves the highest success rate among representative baselines, especially in dense scenes where the baselines degrade sharply. Ablations show that the decomposed soft HOCBF penalty is more reliable than a static distance-margin penalty and that learned parameter adaptation improves robustness over fixed handcrafted settings. Future work includes uncertainty-aware neighbor prediction, richer nonconvex body decompositions, and real-world deployment with onboard sensing and computation.

A.3 广泛的影响Broad Impact

这项工作旨在提高共享空间（包括仓库、服务机器人环境和异构机器人车队）中自主机器人导航的安全性和可靠性。通过将执行的控制保持在显式 MPC 优化问题内，并使用 RL 来调整可解释的参数，该框架可以降低密集导航任务中不透明的端到端策略行为的风险。潜在的负面影响主要来自过早部署：不准确的状态估计、模型不匹配、计算延迟或未建模的行人行为仍然可能导致不安全的运动。This work aims to improve the safety and reliability of autonomous robot navigation in shared spaces, including warehouses, service-robot environments, and heterogeneous robot fleets. By keeping the executed control inside an explicit MPC optimization problem and using RL to adapt interpretable parameters, the framework may reduce the risk of opaque end-to-end policy behavior in dense navigation tasks. Potential negative impacts mainly come from premature deployment: inaccurate state estimation, model mismatch, computation delays, or unmodeled pedestrian behavior could still lead to unsafe motion.
