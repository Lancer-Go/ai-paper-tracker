摘要。Abstract.

大型语言模型（LLM）在推理方面取得了重大进展，特别是在演绎推理方面，这对于高风险决策至关重要。随着模型的改进，评估基准也应该不断发展以跟上步伐。然而，现有的基准测试缺乏对逻辑复杂性的细粒度控制，并且难以平衡语义多样性和逻辑一致性。Large Language Models (LLMs) have made significant progress in reasoning, particularly in deductive reasoning, which is crucial for high-stakes decision-making. As models improve, evaluation benchmarks should evolve to keep pace. However, existing benchmarks lack fine-grained control over logical complexity and struggle to balance semantic diversity with logical consistency.

为了解决这些问题，我们提出了 QMFOL，一种自动化框架，用于生成具有可量化和可控复杂性的一元一阶逻辑推理任务。它使用合取和析取模式构建形式逻辑结构，从而能够精确控制推理深度、宽度、标签类型和干扰因素。然后，这些结构通过法学硕士翻译成自然语言，并通过使用外部证明者的往返验证确保逻辑一致性。 基于我们的框架，我们构建了 QMFOLBench，这是一个包含 2880 个实例、跨不同逻辑和语义维度的 960 个配置的基准测试。对六个大型推理模型 (LRM) 和两个 LLM 的评估表明，随着逻辑复杂性的增加，性能会下降，计算开销也会增加。模型在 True 标记的任务上比在 False 或 Unknown 任务上表现更好，并且对语义变化表现出敏感性。 总体而言，QMFOL 提供了一种可扩展且可靠的方法，用于构建复杂度可控的演绎推理基准，从而能够更精确地评​​估现代语言模型的推理能力。To address these issues, we propose QMFOL, an automated framework for generating monadic first-order logic reasoning tasks with quantifiable and controllable complexity. It constructs formal logical structures using conjunction and disjunction patterns, enabling precise control over reasoning depth, width, label types, and distractors. These structures are then translated into natural language via LLMs, with logical consistency ensured through round-trip verification using an external prover. Based on our framework, we build QMFOLBench, a benchmark comprising 2880 instances with 960 configurations across diverse logical and semantic dimensions. Evaluations on six large reasoning models (LRMs) and two LLMs show that performance degrades and computational overhead increases with rising logical complexity. Models perform better on True-labeled tasks than on False or Unknown ones, and exhibit sensitivity to semantic variation. Overall, QMFOL offers a scalable and reliable approach for constructing deductive reasoning benchmarks with controllable complexity, enabling more precise evaluation of reasoning capabilities in modern language models.

1. 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在自然语言理解、生成和推理方面取得了重大进展。在这些能力中，推理能力进步最快。 最近的大型推理模型（LRM）以前所未有的速度发布和更新Large Language Models (LLMs) have made significant progress in natural language understanding, generation, and reasoning. Among these abilities, reasoning advances most rapidly. Recent Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) are released and updated at an unprecedented pace(DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025; OpenAI, 2026; Google, 2026; Anthropic, 2026)。 随着模型能力的提高，评估方法也必须进步，以确保新版本实现真正的收益，而不是回归或边际改进。这一趋势凸显了严格评估的必要性. As model capabilities improve, evaluation methods must also advance to ensure that new versions achieve real gains rather than regressions or marginal improvements. This trend highlights the need for rigorous evaluation(Rostam et al., 2025; Mohammadi et al., 2025)。 先前的工作开发了跨越数学的动态基准. Prior work develops dynamic benchmarks spanning mathematics(Huang et al., 2025; Mirzadeh et al., 2024), 常识推理, commonsense reasoning(Li et al., 2024), 时间推理, temporal reasoning(Islakoglu and Kalo, 2025; Chu et al., 2024)，以及逻辑推理, and logical reasoning(Qi et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025)。这些基准评估跨不同任务的推理，并帮助减轻现有数据集中的数据污染. These benchmarks evaluate reasoning across diverse tasks and help mitigate data contamination in existing datasets(Zhao et al., 2025; Choi et al., 2025)。 特别是其中，演绎推理. In particular among them all, deductive reasoning(Pan et al., 2023; Nguyen et al., 2025)是决策的核心is central to decision making(Eigner and Händler, 2024; Yang et al., 2023)。它需要从明确的前提中得出结论，并支持系统性和可解释的推理。 这种能力在高风险和知识密集型推理场景中至关重要，例如法律. It requires deriving conclusions from explicit premises and supports systematic and interpretable reasoning. This capability is critical in high-stakes and knowledge-intensive reasoning scenarios, such as law(Lai et al., 2024)和医疗保健and healthcare(Clusmann et al., 2023)。因此，准确评估语言模型的演绎推理能力至关重要。. Therefore, accurately evaluating the deductive reasoning ability of language models is essential.
