摘要Abstract

目标条件视觉导航要求机器人在部分可观察性下行动，预测其运动将如何改变未来以自我为中心的观点，以及这种变化是否使其更接近目标。 导航世界模型提供了这种视觉远见，但它们仍然是预测模块，需要外部规划器将预测的未来转换为闭环控制。 我们提出了导航世界行动模型（NavWAM），这是一种扩散变压器策略，通过在共享潜在序列中表示未来观察、目标进度值和行动块，将导航世界模型预测转化为可执行行动。 通过联合学习未来预测以及确定闭环行为的行动和价值目标，NavWAM 使视觉预见可直接用于机器人控制。 我们通过模拟预训练和真实机器人适应构建 NavWAM，并根据基于规划的世界模型和代表性的直接导航策略对图像目标导航进行评估。 在离线基准测试和闭环真实机器人部署中，NavWAM 在我们的评估中改进了基于规划的世界模型基线，同时使用默认策略模式，无需 CEM 式动作搜索。Goal-conditioned visual navigation requires a robot to act under partial observability by anticipating how its motion will change the future egocentric view and whether that change brings it closer to the goal. Navigation world models provide such visual foresight, but they remain prediction modules that require an external planner to convert predicted futures into closed-loop control. We propose Navigation World Action Model (NavWAM), a diffusion-transformer policy that turns navigation world-model prediction into executable action by representing future observations, goal-progress values, and action chunks in a shared latent sequence. By learning future prediction jointly with the action and value targets that determine closed-loop behavior, NavWAM makes visual foresight directly usable for robot control. We build NavWAM through simulation pretraining and real-robot adaptation, and evaluate it on image-goal navigation against planning-based world models and a representative direct navigation policy. Across offline benchmarks and closed-loop real-robot deployment, NavWAM improves over planning-based world-model baselines in our evaluations while using the default policy mode without CEM-style action search.

1 简介Introduction

最近的目标条件导航政策通过学习将以自我为中心的观察和目标直接映射到来自不同导航数据的行动，取得了实质性进展Recent goal-conditioned navigation policies have made substantial progress by learning to map egocentric observations and goals directly to actions from diverse navigation data[34, 35, 37, 17, 16]。 这些方法是强大而有效的闭环动作预测器，但部分可观察性下的导航通常需要的不仅仅是对当前视图做出反应。 机器人必须预测它的运动将如何改变未来以自我为中心的观察，以及这种变化是否会让它更接近目标。 直接政策可能会从行动监督中隐含地获得这种远见，但它们没有经过明确的训练来预测其行动的视觉后果。 这就留下了一个机器人学习的核心问题：如何使显式视觉预见对于可执行的闭环控制有用？. These methods are strong and efficient closed-loop action predictors, but navigation under partial observability often requires more than reacting to the current view. A robot must anticipate how its motion will change the future egocentric observation and whether that change will bring it closer to the goal. Direct policies may acquire such foresight implicitly from action supervision, but they are not explicitly trained to predict the visual consequences of their actions. This leaves a central robot-learning question: how can explicit visual foresight be made useful for executable closed-loop control?

用于导航的视觉预测方法通过沿着可能的路径综合未来以自我为中心的观察，使预见变得明确Visual prediction approaches for navigation make foresight explicit by synthesizing future egocentric observations along possible paths[23, 20, 15]。 导航世界模型 (NWM) 特别相关，因为它们学习以行动为条件的预测模型，这些模型生成的未来可以根据目标进行评分并用于规划. Navigation World Models (NWMs) are particularly relevant because they learn action-conditioned predictive models whose generated futures can be scored against the goal and used for planning[2]。 然而，NWM 仍然是预测模块，而不是产生行动的政策。 在推理时，他们依赖外部规划器，在选择执行之前根据预测的未来结果搜索候选操作。 这种分离给机器人部署带来了瓶颈：闭环行为取决于外部规划选择，而不是仅取决于学习的世界模型。. However, NWMs remain prediction modules rather than action-producing policies. At inference time, they rely on an external planner that searches over candidate actions based on predicted future outcomes before selecting one for execution. This separation creates a bottleneck for robot deployment: closed-loop behavior depends on external planning choices rather than on the learned world model alone.

Unlike prior video policies developed primarily for manipulation, NavWAM targets goal-conditioned navigation, where the model must couple viewpoint-changing egocentric prediction with goal-progress estimation and local-frame action generation under partial observability. This distinction is central to our setting: NWM-style methods can predict plausible future views, but action selection is still delegated to a separate planner. NavWAM instead learns future prediction, value estimation, and action generation in one policy representation.

Across these settings, NavWAM achieves better navigation performance than planning-based world-model baselines while avoiding CEM-style test-time trajectory optimization. It also remains competitive with the larger direct-policy baseline using a 2B-parameter video backbone, while additionally producing future-view and value predictions.

2 相关工作Related Work

目标条件视觉导航。Goal-Conditioned Visual Navigation.

目标条件视觉导航已经在各种目标规范下进行了研究，包括对象类别Goal-conditioned visual navigation has been studied under a variety of goal specifications, including object categories[3, 5, 11, 26, 13]，目标图像, target images[34, 35, 37, 19]，以及自然语言指令或问题, and natural-language instructions or questions[9, 39, 31, 32, 17, 16]。 在这些环境中，核心挑战是将目标理解与行动选择联系起来。 先前的工作通常通过构建显式的几何、拓扑或语义表示并对其进行规划来解决这个问题. Across these settings, the central challenge is to connect goal understanding with action selection. Prior work has often addressed this by constructing explicit geometric, topological, or semantic representations and planning over them[14, 4, 6, 41]。 最近学习的方法通过使用扩散策略直接根据观察预测行动来减少这种模块化. More recent learned approaches reduce this modularity by directly predicting actions from observations, using diffusion policies[37]、大规模强化学习, large-scale reinforcement learning[42]，或视觉-语言-动作模型, or vision-language-action models[16, 7, 45, 44]。 这些方法在直接动作预测方面很强大，但未来的视觉预测、目标进度估计和动作生成通常不是在单个策略表示中学习的。 NavWAM 通过将未来预测集成到用于闭环导航的策略表示中来解决这一差距。. These methods are strong at direct action prediction, but future visual prediction, goal-progress estimation, and action generation are usually not learned within a single policy representation. NavWAM addresses this gap by integrating future prediction into the policy representation used for closed-loop navigation.

导航视觉预见。Visual Foresight for Navigation.

未来预测为部分可观测性下的导航提供了有用的先验。 基于地图的方法可以预测当前视野之外的占用情况、语义或可能的目标位置Future prediction provides a useful prior for navigation under partial observability. Map-based approaches predict occupancy, semantics, or likely target locations beyond the current field of view[29, 12, 46, 36, 43]，但不要对机器人在移动后收到的未来以自我为中心的观察进行建模。 像素空间方法而是沿着可能的轨迹合成未来的观察结果，如 PathDreamer 中那样, but do not model the future egocentric observations that the robot would receive after moving. Pixel-space methods instead synthesize future observations along possible trajectories, as in PathDreamer[23], 薛定谔的导航仪, Schrödinger’s Navigator[15], 净水器, NWM[2]和 NavDreamer, and NavDreamer[20]。 这些方法使视觉预见变得明确，但仍然需要单独的规划器、价值图或评分函数来将预测的未来转换为行动。 NavWAM 通过在一个策略表示中联合预测行动块、未来以自我为中心的观察和目标条件值来避免这种分离。. These methods make visual foresight explicit, but still require a separate planner, value map, or scoring function to convert predicted futures into actions. NavWAM avoids this separation by jointly predicting action chunks, future egocentric observations, and goal-conditioned values within one policy representation.

基于视频的机器人政策。Video-Based Robot Policies.

最近的机器人学习方法已经开始使用生成建模作为策略表示。 扩散政策将动作扩散确立为视觉运动控制的有力表述Recent robot learning methods have begun to use generative modeling as a policy representation. Diffusion Policy established action diffusion as a strong formulation for visuomotor control[8]，NoMaD 将动作序列扩散适应目标条件导航, and NoMaD adapted action-sequence diffusion to goal-conditioned navigation[37]。 更密切相关的方向使用策略内的视频预测或生成。 UniPi 和 Dreamitate 生成视频作为中间计划，然后从中提取或跟踪操作. A more closely related direction uses video prediction or generation inside the policy. UniPi and Dreamitate generate videos as intermediate plans and then extract or track actions from them[10, 24]，而 GR-1 联合预测未来图像和机器人动作以进行操纵, while GR-1 jointly predicts future images and robot actions for manipulation[40]。 与模型设计最密切相关的是，Cosmos 策略将动作、未来状态和值编码为预训练视频模型中的潜在帧，用于视觉运动控制和可选规划. Most closely related in model design, Cosmos Policy encodes actions, future states, and values as latent frames in a pretrained video model for visuomotor control and optional planning[22]。 NavWAM 遵循这个更广泛的方向，但目标是在部分可观测性下进行目标条件导航。 与面向操作的设置不同，导航需要将改变视角的自我中心预测与目标进度估计和局部帧动作生成结合起来。 NavWAM 将这些目标集成到一个策略表示中，以便视觉预见直接支持闭环导航，而不是保留一个单独的规划模块。. NavWAM follows this broader direction, but targets goal-conditioned navigation under partial observability. Unlike manipulation-oriented settings, navigation requires coupling viewpoint-changing egocentric prediction with goal-progress estimation and local-frame action generation. NavWAM integrates these targets into one policy representation so that visual foresight directly supports closed-loop navigation rather than remaining a separate planning module.

4 方法Method

概述。Overview.

NavWAM 使用预训练的视频世界模型参数化闭环导航策略。 NavWAM 不是仅使用模型来预测外部计划操作的未来视图，而是将导航控制所需的变量表示为共享潜在画布。 当前观察、图像目标、机器人状态、动作块、未来自我中心观察和目标进度值被分配给同一扩散变换器序列中的不同潜在帧。 这将目标条件导航变成了未来感知、进度估计和可执行运动的联合去噪问题。NavWAM parameterizes a closed-loop navigation policy using a pretrained video world model. Rather than using the model only to predict future views for externally planned actions, NavWAM represents the variables needed for navigation control as a shared latent canvas. The current observation, image goal, robot state, action chunk, future egocentric observations, and goal-progress value are assigned to different latent frames in the same diffusion-transformer sequence. This turns goal-conditioned navigation into a joint denoising problem over future perception, progress estimation, and executable motion.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 NavWAM，一种导航世界动作模型，它将视觉预见转变为目标条件视觉导航的闭环策略。 通过在共享潜在序列中表示未来观察、目标进度值和可执行动作块，NavWAM 将未来感知、进度估计和动作生成作为一个耦合的预测问题进行学习。 在我们的评估中，这种联合公式提高了基于规划的世界模型基线的导航性能，在默认策略模式下无需 CEM 式动作搜索，与更大的直接导航策略保持竞争力，并转移到闭环真实机器人图像目标导航。 这些结果表明，当未来预测与确定闭环行为的动作和价值目标一起学习时，而不是被视为单独的规划模块，对机器人导航最有用。We presented NavWAM, a navigation world action model that turns visual foresight into a closed-loop policy for goal-conditioned visual navigation. By representing future observations, goal-progress values, and executable action chunks in a shared latent sequence, NavWAM learns future perception, progress estimation, and action generation as one coupled prediction problem. In our evaluations, this joint formulation improves navigation performance over planning-based world-model baselines without CEM-style action search in the default policy mode, remains competitive with a larger direct navigation policy, and transfers to closed-loop real-robot image-goal navigation. These results suggest that future prediction is most useful for robot navigation when it is learned together with the action and value targets that determine closed-loop behavior, rather than treated as a separate planning module.
