用于多模态车辆轨迹预测的自我发现意图感知变压器Self-Discovered Intention-aware Transformer for Multi-modal Vehicle Trajectory Prediction

Diyi Liu, Zihan Niu, Tu Xu, and Lishan Sun D. Liu, Z. Niu, X. Tu was with the School of Metropolitan Transportation, Beijing Univeristy of Technology, Beijing, China e-mail: (see diyi.liu@bjut.edu.cn).Manuscript received xxxx xx, 2026; revised xxxx xx, 2026.
摘要Abstract

预测车辆轨迹在自动驾驶和智能交通系统应用中发挥着重要作用。尽管设计了多种深度学习算法来预测车辆轨迹，但它们对特定图结构（例如图神经网络）或明确意图标记的依赖限制了它们的灵活性。在这项研究中，我们提出了一个纯粹的基于 Transformer 的网络，具有考虑相邻车辆的多种模式。采用两个独立的轨道。一个轨道专注于预测轨迹，而另一个轨道则专注于预测考虑到邻近车辆的每个意图的可能性。研究发现，双轨迹设计可以通过将空间模块与轨迹生成模块分离来提高性能。此外，我们发现模型可以通过预测 K 个轨迹之间的残差偏移来学习一组有序的轨迹。Predicting vehicle trajectories plays an important role in autonomous driving and ITS applications. Although multiple deep learning algorithms are devised to predict vehicle trajectories, their reliant on specific graph structure (e.g., Graph Neural Network) or explicit intention labeling limit their flexibilities. In this study, we propose a pure Transformer-based network with multiple modals considering their neighboring vehicles. Two separate tracks are employed. One track focuses on predicting the trajectories while the other focuses on predicting the likelihood of each intention considering neighboring vehicles. Study finds that the two track design can increase the performance by separating spatial module from the trajectory generating module. Also, we find the the model can learn an ordered group of trajectories by predicting residual offsets among K trajectories.

I 简介Introduction

车辆轨迹预测是开发自动驾驶车辆、车辆到车辆/车辆到基础设施应用等的基石技术。然而，车辆轨迹预测任务也是一项具有挑战性的任务，因为有多种影响因素（例如，驾驶员的意图、其他车辆的运动、道路几何形状和环境）同时影响行为。Vehicle trajectory prediction is a cornerstone technology for developing autonomous driving vehicles, Vehicle-to-Vehicle/Vehicle-to-Infrastructure applications, etc. However, the vehicle trajectory prediction task is also a challenging task because there are multiple influential factors (e.g., driver’s intention, other vehicle’s movements, road geometries and contexts) influencing behaviors at the same time.

在过去的十年中，深度学习模型的快速发展提供了通过数据驱动的方法预测轨迹的机会。循环神经网络 (RNN)、长短期记忆 (LSTM)、门控循环单元 (GRU) 等模型可以将个体轨迹解释为时间序列来预测未来轨迹。为了更好地考虑车辆之间的交互，设计了多种方法来对车辆之间的交互进行建模。社交 LSTM 是预测车辆/行人轨迹的早期成功工作之一。随着图神经网络（GNN）的发展，许多研究正在使用 GNN 来进一步建模车辆之间的交互。最近，模型正在转向 Transformer 架构，以取代编码器和解码器过程中的传统 RNN/LSTM，以获得更好的建模能力。During the last decade, the fast development of deep learning models provide a chance to predict trajectories by a data driven approach. Models like Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN), Long-short Term Memory (LSTM), Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) can interpret individual trajectories as a time series to predict future trajectories. To better consider the interactions between vehicles, multiple approaches are designed to model the interactions among vehicles. Social LSTM is one of the early successful work predicting vehicle/pedestrian trajectories. With the development of the Graph Neural Network (GNN), many studies are using GNN to further model the interactions among vehicles. Recently, models are shifting to the Transformer architecture to replace traditional RNN/LSTM for both the encoder and decoder processes for better modeling capabilities.

尽管取得了一些进展，但仍有几个方向需要改进。首先，考虑到决策过程的复杂性，应该同时具备多种意图或多模态能力。由于不同的驾驶员在同一场景下可能有不同的目标和行动，因此该问题最好归类为概率问题而不是确定性问题。生成对抗网络（GAN）、变分自动编码器（VAE）被广泛用于生成多个预测。Although developments have made, there are still several directions to improve. First, considering the complexity of decision-making process, multiple intentions, or multi-modal capabilities, should be available at the same time. Since different drivers can have different objectives and actions under the same scenario, the problem is better classified as a probabilistic problem rather than deterministic. Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN), Variational Autoencoders (VAE) are widely used for generating multiple predictions.

解决预测问题的其他一些方面也很重要。首先，与模型架构设计相比，之前基于 Transformer 的模型的互操作性讨论较少。其次，在没有手动意图标签（例如“左转”）的情况下，很难指导基于 Transformer 的模型学习多模态轨迹。Some other aspects of solving the prediction problem are also important. First, the interoperability of previous Transformer-based models are less discussed compared to the model architecture design. Second, it is hard to guide Transformer-based model to learn multi-modal trajectories without manual intention labels (e.g., ’turn left’).

在本研究中，我们设计了一个基于 Transformer 的多模态轨迹预测模型，其贡献如下：In this study, we designed a Transformer-based, multi-modal trajectory prediction model with the following contributions:

  • •

    我们提出了一个新的关注模块，考虑边缘关系并考虑车辆速度。We propose a new attention Module considering edge relationships considering vehicle velocities.

  • •

    我们总结了“建议然后选择”的理念。我们发现两个解码器分别预测意图和概率的多模态性能更好。We summarize the ”propose then select” philosophy. We discovered the multi-modal performance is better by two decoders to separately predict intentions and probabilities.

  • •

    我们发现了一种多模态残差预测技巧，可以获取高速公路交通的自正则化轨迹。We discovered a multi-modality residual prediction trick to get self-regularized trajectories for highway traffic.

  • •

    我们通过应用反事实分析和时间序列分析，提供更具体的解释车辆决策的方法。We provide more specific way of interpreting vehicle decisions by applying counterfactual analysis and time series analysis.

II 相关作品Related Works

Ⅱ-AII-A 早期研究Early Studies

车辆轨迹预测一直是一个长期讨论的研究课题。传统上，受计算能力的限制，采用物理方法或数学拟合曲线进行轨迹预测。卡尔曼滤波器 (KF)、恒定静脉 (CV)、恒定加速度等方法被视为朴素的地面实况模型。然而，这些模型无法纳入驾驶员的意图或车辆之间的交互，因此它们不适合长期预测。为了考虑车辆意图，使用隐马尔可夫模型 (HMM) 和贝叶斯网络等模型来预测操作（例如，变道、右转）。建议像社会力量这样的早期尝试来预测考虑多个轨迹的轨迹。Vehicle trajectory prediction has been a long discussed research topic. Traditionally, limited by the computation abilities, physical methods or mathematical fitting curves are applied for trajectory prediction. Methods like Kalman Filters (KF), Constant Venosity (CV), Constant Acceleration methods are treated as naive ground-truth models. However, these model cannot incorporate driver’s intention or interactions between vehicles, thus they are unsuitable for long horizon prediction. To consider vehicle intentions, models like Hidden Markov Models (HMM) and Bayesian Networks are applied to predict maneuvers (e.g., lane change, right turn). Very early attempts like Social Forces are suggested to predict trajectories considering multiple trajectories.

Ⅱ-BII-B 信息编码Information Encoding

在过去的十年中，深度学习模型的发展提供了使用面向数据的方法对考虑操作的行为进行建模的机会。为了学习历史轨迹和动态，使用编码器对历史轨迹进行编码。 RNN/LSTM/GRU 等自回归架构经常用于编码信息。一些应用时间卷积网络（TCN）和卷积神经网络（CNN）的研究在循环模型结构上实现了并行化。然而，大多数现代研究都使用 Transformer 作为编码器，因为它在捕获长范围依赖性、通过自注意力、并行性等处理可变长度序列方面具有优势。典型的模型包括 Wayformer、Scene Transformer 等。During the last decade, the evolution of deep learning models have provided an opportunity to model behaviors considering maneuvers using a data oriented approach. To learn the history trajectories and dynamics, an encoder is used to encode the historical trajectories. Auto-regressive architecture like RNNs/LSTMs/GRUs are frequently used to encode information. Some studies applied Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) and Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) are achieve parallelization over recurrent model structure. However, most modern studies use the Transformer for encoders due to its benefits in capturing long range dependencies, handle variable length sequences via self-attention, parallelism, etc. Typical models include Wayformer, Scene Transformer, etc.

Ⅱ-CII-C 车辆交互Vehicular Interaction

除了对车辆的动力学进行建模之外，不同车辆之间的相互作用也很重要。社交 LSTM 作为语义方法之一，应用池化网格在附近的旅行者之间共享信息Besides modeling vehicle’s dynamics, the interactions between different vehicles are also important. Social LSTM, as one of the semental method, applied pooling grids to share information between nearby travelers[1]。图神经网络已成为掌握空间关系的主导范式之一。通过将每个旅行者修改为节点，将每个成对关系修改为链接，以动态方式对空间关系进行建模。具体来说，图卷积网络（GCN）、图注意力网络（GAT）等架构都经常使用。一些有影响力的工作包括 GRIP. Graph Neural Network has become one of the dominant paradigm to grasp the spatial relationship. By modifying every traveler as a node each pairwise relationship as a link, the spatial relationships are modeled in a dynamic way. Specifically, architectures like Graph Convolutional Network (GCN), Graph Attention Network (GATs) are both frequently used. Some influential work includes GRIP[6],向量网, VectorNet[3]等。然而，图结构可能是限制之一，因为它可能忽略距离车辆的交互。此外，预定义的图结构可能无法实现现场的异构潜在模式。, etc. However, the graph structure could be one of limitations as it may ignore distance vehicle interactions. Also, predefined graph structure may not capable of heterogeneous potential patterns in field.

Ⅱ-DII-D 多模态预测Multi-modality Prediction

本质上，旅行轨迹是一系列具有不同意图的未来轨迹的随机过程。因此，在给定相同历史条件的情况下生成多个轨迹非常重要。 Social-GAN 只是使用生成对抗训练来训练生成器以获得真实输出的早期工作之一Travel trajectories, by nature, are a stochastic process over a range of future trajectories with different intentions. Thus, it is important to generate multiple trajectory given the same historical condition. Social-GAN is only of the early work using generating adversarial training to train a generator for realistic outputs[4]。轨迹的分布也可以解释为贝叶斯过程，其中代表不同选择的超参数用于生成特定的轨迹。变分自动编码器（VAE）通过在可能未来的潜在空间上绘制参数，可以生成不同的轨迹（参见 Trajectron++）. The distribution of trajectories can also be interpreted as a Bayesian process where hyper-parameters representing different choices are used to generate specific trajectories. Variational Autoencoder (VAE) by drawing parameter over a latent space of possible futures, can generate different trajectories (see Trajectron++)[8]。最近，像（Leapfrog Diffusion、MotionDiffuser）这样的模型正在将轨迹生成建模为扩散过程. Recently, models like (Leapfrog Diffusion, MotionDiffuser) are modeling the trajectory generation as a diffusion process[5, 7]。许多工作仍然关注 Transformer 范式，以针对不同的意图使用不同的解码器头。据作者所知，所有基于 Transformer 的模型都需要手动标签来训练不同的行为（例如，左变道）。. Many works still focus on the Transformer paradigm to use different decoder head for different intentions. To the best knowledge of the authors, all Transformer-based model would require manual label to train different behavior (e.g., left lane change).

Ⅱ-EII-E 模型解读Model Interpretation

除了考虑车辆之间的相互作用来预测多模态轨迹外，最终轨迹的解释也很重要。对于基于 Transformer 的模型，可视化车辆之间的注意力权重是分析不同车辆之间交互程度的重要方法。然而，这些分析是基本的，没有明确解释他们的方法。现在一些研究开始结合其他因果推理机制来寻找因果关系。Besides predicting multi-modal trajectories considering interactions between vehicles, the interpretation of final trajectories are also important. For Transformer-based model, visualizing attention weights between vehicles are an important way to analyze the level of interactions between different vehicles. However, those analysis is basic without explicitly explaining their approach. Some studies now move to incorporate other causal inference mechanism to find causal relationships.

在本研究中，使用名为“Ubiquitous Traffic Eye”的新开源车辆轨迹数据集来评估模型的性能In this study, a new open source vehicle trajectory dataset named “Ubiquitous traffic eyes” is used to evaluate the model’s performance[2]。具体而言，由于该区域交通车道较多且冲突区域复杂，因此采用特殊的合流/分流区域进行模型训练和模型评估。选择三个数据集（即“SQM-W”、“SQM-W”和“SQM-N_up”）进行训练。选择“SQM-N_down”数据集进行测试。. Specifically, a special merging/splitting zone is used for model training and model evaluation since the zone has a lot of traffic lanes with complex conflict zones. Three datasets (i.e., “SQM-W”, “SQM-W”, and “SQM-N_up”) are selected for training. The “SQM-N_down” dataset is selected for testing.

IV-A 数据处理和模型配置Data Processing and Model Configuration

每个数据集包含数十分钟的车辆轨迹。我们首先将数据集分成独立的 10 秒卡盘。对于每个卡盘，前 5 秒用于历史信息，其余 5 秒用于预测的地面实况数据。为 10 秒阶段期间提前进入或离开的车辆提供布尔掩码。因此，模型输入和输出的所有未知车辆坐标都被过滤掉。Each dataset contains vehicle trajectories for tens of minutes. We first chunk the dataset into isolated 10-second chucks. For each chuck, the first 5 seconds are used for historical information and the remaining 5 seconds are used for ground-truth data for prediction. A boolean mask is provided for vehicles entered or left early during the 10-second phase. Thus, all unknown vehicle coordinates are filtered out for both model inputs and outputs.

默认情况下，模型预测 8 种意图模式。应用四个并行的 Transformer 头，每个头都有一个维度大小为 16 的向量。所有模型都在数据集上训练 100 个时期。By default, the model predicts 8 modes of intentions. Four parallel Transformer heads are applied, and each head has a vector with dimension size of 16. All models are trained over the dataset for 100 epoches.

V 结论Conclusion

在本节中，我们介绍了一个基于 Transformer 的模型来预测具有多种意图的旅行轨迹。应用了几个重要的技巧来提高性能。其次，我们设计了一种特殊的有序残差方法来预测不同的轨迹。其次，我们通过建模结合车辆之间相对运动权重的自适应偏差来建模车辆之间的注意力权重。第三，我们发现通过将预测轨迹及其概率的过程分开，可以提高模型性能。换句话说，对车辆之间的交互进行建模只是为了改变每个轨迹的概率，而不是车辆可能的意图/轨迹。结合这些技巧，我们确定了一种轻量但有效的结构来预测具有不同意图的多个车辆轨迹。我们还发现，对于预测高速公路交通的情况，8 种意图模式可以在轨迹多样性和模型简单性之间取得平衡。In this section, we introduced a Transformer-based model to predict travel trajectories with multiple intentions. Several important tricks are applied to enhance performance. Secondly, we designed a special ordered residual way of predicting different trajectories. Secondly, we modeled attention weights between vehicles by modeling an adaptive bias incorporating relative motion weights between vehicles. Thirdly, we found that by separating the process of predicting trajectories and their probabilities, the model performance can be enhanced. In other words, the interactions between vehicles are modeled just to change the probability of each trajectory, not the vehicle’s possible intentions/trajectories. Combining these tricks, we identify a light but efficient structure to predict multiple vehicle trajectories with different intentions. We also find for the case of predicting highway traffic, 8 intention modes can make a balance between trajectory diversity and model simplicity.

这项研究有一些局限性。首先，由于数据源的覆盖范围有限，多模态残差预测技巧可能存在一些局限性。例如，在当前高速公路轨迹数据集下不太可能精确预测交叉路口的掉头或左转车辆轨迹。其次，本研究没有考虑周围环境的影响。例如，道路边界的信息不会直接输入到模型中。换句话说，考虑到前方数百米的匝道末端，模型不太可能了解到入口匝道上的车辆会并入相邻车道。最后，有必要与考虑车辆相互作用的类似模型进行比较。对于未来的研究，我们会将这种架构应用到更多的数据集上，以覆盖更普遍的日常情况，包括十字路口情况、行人情况等。The study has some limitations. First, the multi-modal residual prediction trick may have some limitations due to the limited coverage of data sources. For example, it is not likely to predict U-turn or left turn vehicle trajectory precisely for intersections under the current highway trajectory dataset. Second, the impact of surrounding environments are not considered in this study. For example, the information of road borders are not directly fed into the model. In other words, it is not likely for the model to learn vehicles from on-ramps will merge to the neighboring lane considering the end of the ramp hundreds of meters ahead. Finally, it is necessary to compare against similar models considering vehicle interactions. For future study, we would apply this architecture on more datasets to cover a more generalized daily cases, including intersection cases, pedestrian cases, etc.
