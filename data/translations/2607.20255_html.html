摘要Abstract

法学硕士驱动的自主代理正在重塑进攻性安全。与传统的渗透测试工具（确定性、范围狭窄且由训练有素的从业人员操作）不同，代理安全工具表现出LLM-driven autonomous agents are reshaping offensive security. Unlike traditional penetration-testing tooling — deterministic, narrowly scoped, and operated by trained practitioners — agentic security tools exhibit不确定性indeterminacy沿着三个独立的维度。首先，他们的along three independent dimensions. First, their行动actions来自非确定性政策，其产出无法进行事前和事后解释，令人沮丧的事件归因和部署前安全审查。其次，他们的are drawn from a non-deterministic policy whose outputs resist both ex-ante and ex-post explanation, frustrating incident attribution and pre-deployment safety review. Second, their影响impact由于不确定性的行为、所使用模型的代理以及不透明的法学硕士供应链，该问题是开放式的。第三，他们的is open-ended due to the non-deterministic actions, agency of utilized models, and opaque LLM supply-chains. Third, their用户群体user population规模和所需技能都是不确定的：使用或发展进攻能力的操作技能底线急剧下降。这三个属性在主题上相互关联，但不能相互推导。结合攻防之间的结构性成本不对称，它们使进攻能力产业化。短期净效应有利于攻击者，即使从长远来看，相同的技术可能使防御实践民主化。现有的军民两用网络安全和人工智能伦理框架并不是为这种组合而设计的。我们的工作分析了当使用自主人工智能代理进行攻击性安全时，道德归因如何在用户、工具制造商和第三方之间扩散。我们还研究了该技术对利益相关者的影响并提供分层建议。is indeterminate in both size and required skill: the operating skill floor for using or developing offensive capabilities has dropped sharply. These three properties are linked thematically, but are not derivable from one another. Combined with the structural cost asymmetry between offense and defense, they enable the industrialization of offensive capability. The net short-term effect favors attackers, even if the same technology may, in the long run, democratize access to defensive practice. Existing dual-use cybersecurity and AI-ethics frameworks were not designed for this combination. Our work analyzes how moral attribution becomes diffuse between users, tool-makers, and third parties when employing autonomous AI agents for offensive security. We also examine the stakeholder impact of this technology and provide stratified recommendations.

9 结论Conclusion

从确定性的、人为操作的攻击工具到法学硕士驱动的自主渗透测试代理的转变混淆了道德代理的界限，造成了复杂的供应链依赖性，并挑战了网络安全中现有的道德框架The shift from deterministic, human-operated offensive tools to autonomous LLM-driven penetration-testing agents obfuscates the boundaries of moral agency, creates complex supply-chain dependencies, and challenges existing ethical frameworks in cybersecurity[17]。与此同时，我们经历了攻击者与防御者的不平衡，以及闭源模型制作者与其用户之间的权力不对称。在这种紧张局势中，我们呼吁采取方法论上的谦逊态度。三十年来，进攻性安全一直在解决监管无法解决的双重用途困境。随着我们在能力轨迹高度不确定的情况下发展得更快，并且利益相关者的利益在全球范围内日益不对称，我们必须重新致力于人类监督和道德代理。. At the same time, we experience the attacker-defender imbalance, and the power-asymmetry between closed-source model-makers and their users. In this tension, we call for a posture of methodological humility. Offensive security has, for thirty years, navigated dual-use dilemmas that regulation could not solve. As we evolve faster under conditions of high uncertainty about capability trajectories, and with stakeholders whose interests are increasingly globally asymmetric, we must recommit to human oversight and moral agency.
