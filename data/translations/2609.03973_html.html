9 范围、信息边界和限制Scope, information boundary, and limitations

我们首先澄清结果的范围。无限制的像素级 反事实通常无法从不成对的政权边缘中识别出来。 因此，该框架的目标是预先指定的尖锐界限 声明的支持限制下的有限维图像特征。这个 是分析的确定对象，而不是近似值 以其他方式识别的像素级反事实。We first clarify the scope of the results. Unrestricted pixel-level counterfactuals are generally not identified from unpaired regime marginals. Accordingly, the framework targets sharp bounds for prespecified finite-dimensional image features under a declared support restriction. This is the identified object of the analysis rather than an approximation to an otherwise identified pixel-level counterfactual.

目击者图谱和跨世界关系更加科学 输入，而不是观察到的制度边际的后果。证人图集 确定要审核的连贯性问题，而关系 确定用于部分识别的允许特征耦合。 如果其中任何一个指定错误，审核可能会拒绝相干对或 确定的区间可能会排除真正的目标。样本分割可以评估 经验表现，但本身不能确定因果有效性 这些输入。同样，违规预算是敏感参数，除非 它们是独立校准的。The witness atlas and the cross-world relation are additional scientific inputs, not consequences of the observed regime marginals. The witness atlas determines the coherence question being audited, whereas the relation determines the admissible feature couplings used for partial identification. If either is misspecified, the audit may reject coherent pairs or the identified interval may exclude the true target. Sample splitting can assess empirical performance but cannot by itself establish the causal validity of these inputs. Likewise, violation budgets are sensitivity parameters unless they are independently calibrated.

进一步的信息边界由特征投影产生。投影一个 粗糙特征空间上的图像级关系可以丢弃图像级 限制。特征级和图像级分析仅在以下情况下一致 特征饱和度或条件光纤条件中所述 补充材料。A further information boundary arises from feature projection. Projecting an image-level relation onto a coarse feature space can discard image-level restrictions. The feature-level and image-level analyses coincide only under the feature-saturation or conditional-fiber conditions stated in the Supplementary Material.

当前的实验提供了对 MNIST 的受控评估， Morpho-MNIST、smallNORB，以及合成和计算 研究。他们验证了预测的局部-全局分离，锐界 计算、有限样本覆盖和结构化计算缩放 这些设置。对更广泛的自然图像领域和实证的评估 多机制扩展的验证留待未来工作。The current experiments provide controlled evaluations on MNIST, Morpho-MNIST, and smallNORB, together with synthetic and computational studies. They validate the predicted local–global separation, sharp-bound calculations, finite-sample coverage, and structured computational scaling in these settings. Evaluation on broader natural-image domains and empirical validation of the multi-regime extension are left for future work.

10 结论Conclusion

局部区域合理性并不意味着全球一致 反事实解释。普通证人等级记录了这种区别 作为一个可行性综合体。有限且行动分层的证人结构 生成简短的准确证书和维修计数；外部合理的 然后特征关系变窄，但不选择其中的耦合 政权边缘。对所有兼容的联轴器进行优化可提供清晰的 所述仅支持模型中的特征边界，以及同时边际 置信区域给出了完整预言机的有限样本外部覆盖范围 间隔。由此产生的管道在数学上是明确的 信息进入以及模糊性仍然存在的地方。其在新的情况下的有效性 申请取决于预先指定、证人的外部验证 关系，以及在正确的独立单位可重复的证据。Local regional plausibility need not imply one globally coherent counterfactual explanation. The common-witness grade records this distinction as a feasibility complex. Finite and action-stratified witness structures yield short exact certificates and repair counts; an externally justified feature relation then narrows, but does not select within, the coupling of the regime marginals. Optimizing over all compatible couplings gives sharp feature bounds in the stated support-only model, and simultaneous marginal confidence regions give finite-sample outer coverage of the complete oracle interval. The resulting pipeline is mathematically explicit about where information enters and where ambiguity remains. Its validity in a new application rests on prespecification, external validation of the witness relation, and reproducible evidence at the correct independent unit.
