摘要Abstract

临床诊断评价不应仅评估模型是否 既能提供正确的诊断，又能反映现实情况 临床实践，包括多模式的逐步披露 信息、诊断假设的动态更新以及持续 完善临床推理。然而，现有的评价 多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 通常依赖于单轮 或孤立的任务，使得难以完全捕捉复杂性 真实世界的临床诊断。为了弥补这一差距，我们开发了 ClinMM-Bench，最大的多轮多模式临床诊断 迄今为止的评估基准。 ClinMM-Bench 包含 1,089 个具有挑战性的项目 真实世界的临床案例和 8 个领域的 3,760 张医学图像 特产。我们使用以下方法系统地评估了 15 个具有代表性的 MLLM： 评估诊断准确性的两级评估框架 和诊断推理质量。结果表明，专有 模型实现了最高的总体诊断准确性，但 完全正确诊断的比例仍然有限 模型。在诊断推理质量方面，当前模型可以 确定合理的诊断方向，但仍存在相当大的困难 产生可靠的诊断推理的局限性。误差分析 进一步确定了五种有代表性的失效模式：信息 综合失败、知识图谱错误、感知错误、过早 封闭和幻视。Clinical diagnostic evaluation should not only assess whether models can provide correct diagnoses, but also reflect the realities of clinical practice, including progressive disclosure of multimodal information, dynamic updating of diagnostic hypotheses, and continuous refinement of clinical reasoning. However, existing evaluations of multimodal large language models (MLLMs) typically rely on single-turn or isolated tasks, making it difficult to fully capture the complexity of real-world clinical diagnosis. To bridge this gap, we developed ClinMM-Bench, the largest multi-turn multimodal clinical diagnostic evaluation benchmark to date. ClinMM-Bench contains 1,089 challenging real-world clinical cases and 3,760 medical images across eight specialties. We systematically evaluated 15 representative MLLMs using a two-level evaluation framework that assessed both diagnostic accuracy and diagnostic reasoning quality. Results showed that proprietary models achieved the highest overall diagnostic accuracy, but the proportion of completely correct diagnoses remained limited across all models. In terms of diagnostic reasoning quality, current models can identify plausible diagnostic directions but still have considerable limitations in generating reliable diagnostic reasoning. Error analysis further identified five representative failure modes: information synthesis failure, knowledge mapping error, perception error, premature closure, and visual hallucination.

ClinMM-Bench 包含 1,089 个具有挑战性的现实世界临床诊断 从 PubMed 中央开放获取 (PMCOA) 病例报告中精选的病例， 拥有 3,760 张医学图像，涵盖八个专业。放射学是 最大的专科，有 679 例（62.35%），其次是肿瘤科，有 114例（10.47%），神经内科98例（9.00%），皮肤科 63例（5.79%），眼科60例（5.51%）， 肾内科29例（2.66%），急诊科23例 (2.11%)，内科23例(2.11%)。每个案例都是 结构为多轮多模式诊断对话， 每个案例平均 5.45 轮对话。在每种情况下，临床 信息和图像逐渐公开，要求模型 随着时间的推移更新他们的诊断。除了真实的情况之外 诊断中，每个病例都包含一个参考诊断推理过程。 由于 ClinMM-Bench 是根据已发表的病例报告整理而成，因此丰富了 诊断上具有挑战性的病例涉及相对不常见的临床病例 常规临床记录中代表性不足的表现。ClinMM-Bench comprises 1,089 challenging real-world clinical diagnostic cases curated from PubMed Central Open Access (PMCOA) case reports, with 3,760 medical images, spanning eight specialties. Radiology is the largest specialty, with 679 cases (62.35%), followed by Oncology with 114 cases (10.47%), Neurology with 98 cases (9.00%), Dermatology with 63 cases (5.79%), Ophthalmology with 60 cases (5.51%), Nephrology with 29 cases (2.66%), Emergency Medicine with 23 cases (2.11%), and Internal Medicine with 23 cases (2.11%). In addition to the ground-truth diagnosis, each case includes a reference diagnostic reasoning process. As ClinMM-Bench was curated from published case reports, it is enriched for diagnostically challenging cases involving relatively uncommon clinical presentations that are underrepresented in routine clinical records.

2.4 多轮多模态临床诊断误差分析 推理Error Analysis of Multi-Turn Multimodal Clinical Diagnostic Reasoning

为了研究 MLLM 的局限性，我们分析了诊断失败 案例并将其分为五种有代表性的错误类型： 信息合成失败、知识图谱错误、感知 错误、过早关闭和幻视。To investigate the limitations of MLLMs, we analyzed diagnostic failure cases and categorized them into five representative error types: information synthesis failure, knowledge mapping error, perception error, premature closure, and visual hallucination.

首先，当模型无法进行信息合成时，就会发生信息合成失败。 跨回合和方式综合信息。例如，在一个 在与吸烟相关的机化性肺炎病例中，该模型过度依赖 早期影像学和细胞学检查结果提示恶性肿瘤， 忽视了随后的病变消退和活检结果，并且 误诊为浸润性粘液腺癌。第二， 当模型识别出关键事实但未识别出知识图谱时，就会发生知识图谱错误 将它们映射到错误的疾病机制。如果是蜱传病例 脑炎，模型识别发热，脑脊液 细胞增多、MRI 异常和鞘内抗体阳性，但 误诊为急性播散性脑脊髓炎。三、 当模型错误地解释密钥时会出现感知错误 影像学发现。在 Chilaiditi 综合征的病例中，模型 将肝脏和膈肌之间的结肠气体误解为气体 胆囊内，诊断为气肿性胆囊炎。 第四，当模型过早承诺时，就会发生过早关闭 最初的假设，并在额外后未能对其进行修改 信息出现了。在甲母瘤病例中，模型继续 尽管随后进行了皮肤镜、MRI 和 支持良性诊断的病理证据。最后，视觉 当模型引入不存在的成像时会出现幻觉 调查结果。在脑海绵状血管瘤伴血肿的病例中，模型 幻觉的囊性病变、偏心头节和点征， 导致神经囊尾蚴病的错误诊断。总体而言，这些 错误表明 MLLM 中的诊断失败是由能力引起的 多个层面的赤字。First, information synthesis failures occurred when models could not synthesize information across turns and modalities. Second, knowledge mapping errors occurred when models recognized key facts but mapped them to an incorrect disease mechanism. In a case of tickborne encephalitis, the model recognized fever, cerebrospinal fluid pleocytosis, MRI abnormalities, and positive intrathecal antibodies but incorrectly diagnosed acute disseminated encephalomyelitis. Third, perception errors occurred when models incorrectly interpreted the key imaging findings. Fourth, premature closure occurred when models committed too early to an initial hypothesis and failed to revise it after additional information emerged. Finally, visual hallucinations occurred when models introduced nonexistent imaging findings. Overall, these errors indicate that diagnostic failures in MLLMs arise from capability deficits at multiple levels.

Premature closure and information synthesis failures reflect limitations in models’ ability to dynamically update diagnostic reasoning across multiple turns: models may over-rely on early clues or initial hypotheses and fail to sufficiently integrate subsequently disclosed information. These findings highlight the need for benchmarks that can decompose performance into clinically meaningful capabilities. ClinMM-Bench provides such a framework and can support future work on model training, evaluation, and safety monitoring.

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，ClinMM-Bench主要包括 挑战已发表的病例报告，这意味着其诊断 困难可能与遇到的更常见情况不同 常规临床实践This study has several limitations. First, ClinMM-Bench primarily consists of challenging published case reports, meaning that its diagnostic difficulty may differ from that of more common cases encountered in routine clinical practiceVandenbroucke2001-et。因此，这 基准不应被视为对性能的直接估计 常见的临床诊断。二、ClinMM-Bench采用被动多圈 信息披露格式，而在真实的临床环境中， 医生主动询问病史，选择进一步检查， 根据现有情况动态确定下一步 假设. Therefore, this benchmark should not be taken as a direct estimate of performance on common clinical diagnoses. Second, ClinMM-Bench adopts a passive multi-turn information disclosure format, whereas in real clinical settings, physicians actively elicit history, select further investigations, and dynamically determine the next steps based on existing hypothesesJohri2025-vn。第三，虽然我们采用了多阶段 数据管理、质量控制、专家验证、双法学硕士共识 评估和原子事实分解，潜在的偏差可能仍然存在 在数据管理和自动评估期间引入。. Third, although we employed multi-stage data curation, quality control, expert validation, dual-LLM consensus evaluation, and atomic fact decomposition, potential biases may still be introduced during data curation and automated evaluation.

总体而言，ClinMM-Bench 建立了大规模、多回合、多模式 评估临床诊断推理的基准。我们的发现 表明当前模型可以识别部分诊断模式，但是 仍然无法可靠地获得完全正确的诊断或 保持推理的保真度。Overall, ClinMM-Bench establishes a large-scale, multi-turn, multimodal benchmark for evaluating clinical diagnostic reasoning. Our findings show that current models can recognize partial diagnostic patterns but remain unable to reliably achieve completely correct diagnoses or maintain reasoning fidelity.

4.3.1 诊断准确性评估Diagnostic Accuracy Evaluation

4.3.2 诊断推理质量评估Diagnostic Reasoning Quality Evaluation

使用原子事实分解来评估推理的质量。 MLLM 生成推理和参考推理均被分解 转化为一系列原子临床事实，每个事实代表一个 最小的、可独立验证的临床声明。然后我们定义了 三个指标：(1) 事实回忆 (Fact Recall) 衡量推理完整性， 正确计算参考原子事实的比例 由模型在所有参考原子事实中识别。更高的事实 召回率表明模型成功捕获了关键点 诊断所需的临床信息； (2) 幻觉对策 推理可靠性，定义为模型生成的比例 参考文献不支持的原子事实。较低的 幻觉评分反映了对实际临床的更强依从性 证据并减少捏造无依据信息的倾向； (3) 事实密度衡量推理效率，量化为 有效原子事实在所有原子事实中所占的比例 模型。较高的事实密度表明模型传达了 以简洁且提供临床信息的方式进行诊断推理， 从而减轻过长输出造成的度量稀释。的 详细提示在The quality of reasoning was evaluated using atomic fact decomposition. Both MLLM-generated reasoning and reference reasoning were decomposed into a series of atomic clinical facts, with each fact representing a minimal, independently verifiable clinical statement. We then defined three metrics: (1) Fact Recall measures reasoning completeness and is calculated as the proportion of reference atomic facts correctly identified by the model among all reference atomic facts. A higher fact recall indicates that the model successfully captures the critical clinical information required for diagnosis; (2) Hallucination measures reasoning reliability and is defined as the proportion of model-generated atomic facts that are unsupported by the reference. A lower hallucination score reflects stronger adherence to actual clinical evidence and a reduced tendency to fabricate unsupported information; and (3) Fact Density measures reasoning efficiency and is quantified as the proportion of valid atomic facts among all atomic facts produced by the model. A higher fact density indicates that the model conveys diagnostic reasoning in a concise and clinically informative manner, thereby mitigating metric dilution from excessively long outputs.
