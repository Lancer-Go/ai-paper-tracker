1 简介Introduction

随着现代图像和视频生成模型的能力迅速提高，他们对数据的胃口也随之增加。尽管前沿视觉生成模型的训练细节很少公开，但最先进的开放权重模型是在包含数亿到数十亿个示例的图像和视频语料库上进行训练的As the capabilities of modern generative models for images and videos have rapidly advanced, so has their appetite for data. Although the training details of frontier visual generative models are seldom made public, state-of-the-art open-weight models are trained on image and video corpora containing hundreds of millions to billions of examples[31, 21, 22, 35]。专有模型可能在可比或更大的数据规模上运行. Proprietary models presumably operate at comparable or greater data scales[4, 9]。此外，视觉生成已经从类别调节信号转向密集调节信号，例如富文本字幕. In addition, visual generation has shifted away from class-conditioning signals toward dense conditioning signals such as rich text captions[22, 4].

类条件 ImageNet-1K 基准测试推动了视觉生成研究的实质性进展，成为 BigGAN 等方法的测试平台The class-conditional ImageNet-1K benchmark has driven substantial progress in visual generation research, serving as a testbed for methods such as BigGAN[3], VQVAE [30],VQGAN, VQGAN[7]，和迪特, and DiT[19]。 然而，在关注同一视觉生成基准十多年之后，两个关键问题变得显而易见。 首先，现代视觉生成模型依赖于更大、更多样化的训练语料库以及丰富的条件信号（例如自由格式文本）。因此，ImageNet-1K 基准测试越来越偏离当代视觉生成建模实践，其结论不太可能转移到现代实际环境中。 其次，十多年来在 ImageNet-1K 上的爬山已经使 FID 分数饱和并推动了该指标的“Goodharting”. However, after more than a decade of focus on the same visual generation benchmark, two critical issues have become apparent. First, modern visual generative models rely on much larger and more diverse training corpora together with rich conditioning signals such as free-form text. As a result, the ImageNet-1K benchmark has increasingly drifted away from contemporary visual generative modeling practice, with conclusions less likely to transfer to modern practical settings. Second, over a decade of hillclimbing on ImageNet-1K has saturated FID scores and driven “Goodharting” of the metric111古德哈特定律：Goodhart’s law:当一项措施成为目标时，它就不再是一个好的措施。When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.。 值得注意的是，最近的几种方法在 ImageNet-1K 基准上获得的 FID 分数低于保留的真实图像. Notably, several recent methods achieve lower FID scores on the ImageNet-1K benchmark than held-out real images[36, 11, 33, 29].

另一方面，许多工业实验室报告文本条件生成图像和视频的结果，但使用专有或不稳定的数据集，阻碍了再现性和开放的科学比较。这促使人们重新思考视觉生成建模研究的基准数据集。具体来说，我们确定了用于视觉生成的现代基准数据集的四个关键属性。On the other hand, many industrial labs report results on text-conditioned generation of images and video, but use proprietary or unstable datasets, hindering reproducibility and open scientific comparisons. This motivates rethinking benchmark datasets for visual generative modeling research. Concretely, we identify four key properties of a modern benchmark dataset for visual generation.

  • •

    允许：Permissive:数据集中的每个图像都应该有一个已知的许可证，允许研究和商业使用，而不对派生的工件施加限制。此外，数据集本身（包括元数据和注释）应在许可下发布。Every image in the dataset should have a known license permitting both research and commercial use, without imposing restrictions on derived artifacts. Moreover, the dataset itself, including metadata and annotations, should be released under a permissive license.

  • •

    稳定：Stable:为了确保有效的科学比较，基准数据集不能随时间变化。许多现代图像数据集以 URL 索引的形式分发，由于链接失效，这使得比较变得困难To ensure valid scientific comparisons, the benchmark dataset cannot change over time. Many modern image datasets are distributed as URL indices, which makes comparisons difficult due to link rot[8, 28].

  • •

    大：Large:基准数据集应该足够大，并具有丰富的文本标题，以训练和评估现代视觉生成模型。The benchmark dataset should be large enough, with rich text captions, to train and evaluate modern visual generative models.

  • •

    无障碍：Accessible:数据集必须能够以分片格式轻松下载，无需爬行基础设施The dataset must be easily downloadable in a sharded format without requiring crawling infrastructure[24, 8, 2]或内存密集型重新分片or memory-intensive resharding[28].

2.4 字幕 GPICCaptioning GPIC

GPIC 使用由视觉语言模型生成的高质量合成字幕，而不是源元数据或替代文本，这些文本通常不可用、存在噪声或与图像内容的一致性较差。GPIC uses high-quality synthetic captions generated by a vision-language model rather than source metadata or alt text, which are often unavailable, noisy, or weakly aligned with image content.

3 基准测试协议Benchmarking Protocol

严格的评估协议对于推动视觉生成的进步至关重要。 一个好的评估者应该区分真实的图像和生成的图像，同时与人类的感知保持一致。 GPIC 旨在为现代视觉生成模型提供更加人性化且饱和度较低的评估设置。Rigorous evaluation protocols are imperative to drive progress in visual generation. A good evaluator should distinguish real and generated images while remaining aligned with human perception. GPIC is designed to provide a more human-aligned and less saturated evaluation setting for modern visual generative models.

保留测试统计数据。Held-out test statistics.

GPIC 与标准 ImageNet-1K 评估的不同之处还在于计算参考统计数据的方式。 标准 ImageNet-1K FID 将生成的样本与训练集统计数据进行比较。 相反，GPIC 将生成的样本与保留的 100 万图像测试集的统计数据进行比较。 这是更好的科学实践，因为与训练集统计数据进行比较可能无法检测记忆或过度拟合。GPIC also differs from standard ImageNet-1K evaluation in how reference statistics are computed. Standard ImageNet-1K FID compares generated samples against training-set statistics. GPIC instead compares generated samples against statistics from a held-out 1M-image test set. This is better scientific practice because comparing against train-set statistics can fail to detect memorization or overfitting.

5 结论Conclusion

GPIC（巨型许可图像语料库）是一个用于视觉生成建模的大型许可基准数据集。在本文中，我们描述了使 GPIC 宽松、稳定、大型且可访问所需的设计选择，包括其构建管道、发布格式、评估协议、合规性指南和参考基线。随着视觉生成模型的不断发展，基准数据集和指标也必须随之发展。除了文本到图像的生成之外，GPIC 还为更广泛的多模态研究提供大规模、高质量的图像文本资源。我们希望 GPIC 支持大规模视觉生成建模的开放、可访问和可重复的研究。 GPIC 可在GPIC (Giant Permissive Image Corpus) is a large, permissive benchmark dataset for visual generative modeling. In this paper, we described the design choices needed to make GPIC permissive, stable, large, and accessible, including its construction pipeline, release format, evaluation protocol, compliance guidelines, and reference baseline. As visual generative models continue to evolve, benchmark datasets and metrics must evolve with them. Beyond text-to-image generation, GPIC provides a large-scale, high-quality image-text resource for broader multimodal research. We hope GPIC supports open, accessible, and reproducible research on large-scale visual generative modeling. 更广泛的影响和限制。Broader Impact and Limitations.GPIC 是一个完全允许的 1 亿图像数据集，用于视觉生成建模，支持透明且可合法验证的基准测试和模型训练。同时，GPIC 也具有与现有大规模图像语料库相同的社会风险GPIC is a fully permissive 100M-image dataset for visual generative modeling, supporting transparent and legally verifiable benchmarking and model training. At the same time, GPIC carries societal risks shared with prior large-scale image corpora[28, 24, 8]，包括潜在的滥用有害生成、记忆培训内容以及放大源平台偏差。我们采取多项措施来降低这些风险。 GPIC 中的每个图像都有明确的重新分发和使用的法律依据，并且许可证名称、许可证 URL 和归属字符串都作为每个样本的元数据保留。字幕由 Qwen3-VL-4B 生成, including potential misuse for harmful generation, memorization of training content, and amplification of source-platform biases. We take several steps to mitigate these risks. Every image in GPIC has a clear legal basis for redistribution and use, and license names, license URLs, and attribution strings are retained as metadata for every sample. Captions are generated by Qwen3-VL-4B[1]而不是从替代文本中删除，避免直接发布可能包含有毒或个人识别语言的源文本。我们还将 GPIC 作为冻结的 tar 碎片而不是 URL 索引发布，从而消除了静默数据集漂移、暴露于 URL 级数据中毒以及在过滤管道之外重新抓取源图像的​​需要。最后，尽管我们进行了重复数据删除工作，但 GPIC 中仍可能保留一些接近重复的数据，尽管它们的流行程度估计很小。rather than scraped from alt text, avoiding direct release of source text that may contain toxic or personally identifying language. We also release GPIC as frozen tar shards rather than a URL index, eliminating silent dataset drift, exposure to URL-level data poisoning, and the need to re-scrape source images outside our filtering pipeline. Finally, despite our deduplication efforts, some near-duplicates may remain in GPIC, although their prevalence is estimated to be small.

B.3 DINOv2 主干大小和寄存器令牌对 FD 的影响Effect of DINOv2 Backbone Size and Register Tokens on FD

使用 FD-DINOv2 时的一个实际问题是如何解释其数值范围。 与像素空间距离不同，FD-DINOv2 取决于学习神经网络产生的特征值。 特别是，这些特征值可能会随着 DINOv2 主干尺寸以及模型是否使用寄存器而变化。 因此，我们在四种 DINOv2 主干尺寸中消除了带和不带寄存器的变体：ViT-S/14、ViT-B/14、ViT-L/14 和 ViT-g/14。A practical question when using FD-DINOv2 is how to interpret its numerical scale. Unlike pixel-space distances, FD-DINOv2 depends on feature values produced by a learned neural network. In particular, these feature values can change with the DINOv2 backbone size and whether the model uses registers. We therefore ablate variants with and without registers across four DINOv2 backbone sizes: ViT-S/14, ViT-B/14, ViT-L/14, and ViT-g/14.

引入视觉变换器中的寄存器是为了减少 DINOv2 特征图中的高范数伪影。 由于 FD-DINOv2 是直接在 DINOv2 特征空间中计算的，因此特征分布的变化会影响绝对度量值和生成模型之间的比较。Registers in vision transformers were introduced to reduce high-norm artifacts in DINOv2 feature maps. Since FD-DINOv2 is computed directly in DINOv2 feature space, changes to the feature distribution can affect both the absolute metric value and comparisons between generative models.
