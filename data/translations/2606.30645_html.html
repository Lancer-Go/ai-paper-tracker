摘要Abstract

基于感知的人形机器人控制需要将自我中心的观察和任务指令与全身运动联系起来。学习这种映射需要同步的以自我为中心的图像、语言命令和机器人兼容的运动轨迹，但现有的数据源没有大规模地提供这种完整的元组。我们通过在重建场景中综合生成视觉语言运动学（VLK）监督来解决这个瓶颈。我们的管道利用 3D 高斯分布来重建公制尺度的室内环境，使用特权场景信息合成导航和对象交互轨迹，并在事后渲染成对的以自我为中心的观察结果。我们在没有人工干预的情况下生成了 48,000 条配对轨迹，并训练了预测短视界全身运动轨迹的 VLK 策略。全身追踪器将这些预测转化为对物理人形机器人的操作。我们对执行导航和单对象传输的物理 Unitree G1 进行了评估，证明重建场景中的综合交互为基于模拟到真实感知的人形机器人操作提供了有效的监督。Perception-based humanoid loco-manipulation requires connecting egocentric observations and task instructions to whole-body motion. Learning this mapping requires synchronized egocentric images, language commands, and robot-compatible kinematic trajectories, yet no existing data source provides this complete tuple at scale. We address this bottleneck by generating vision-language-kinematics (VLK) supervision synthetically in reconstructed scenes. Our pipeline leverages 3D Gaussian Splatting to reconstruct metric-scale indoor environments, synthesizes navigation and object-interaction trajectories using privileged scene information, and renders paired egocentric observations after the fact. We produce 48,000 paired trajectories with no human intervention and train a VLK policy that predicts short-horizon whole-body kinematic trajectories. A whole-body tracker converts these predictions into actions on the physical humanoid. We evaluate on the physical Unitree G1 performing navigation and single-object transport, demonstrating that synthesized interactions in reconstructed scenes provide effective supervision for sim-to-real perception-based humanoid loco-manipulation.

1 简介Introduction

将自我中心的观察与全身行动联系起来是在以人为中心的环境中运行的人形机器人面临的基本挑战。类人机器人必须从自己的角度感知世界，识别与任务相关的物体，导航到它们，并通过协调的全身运动与它们进行物理交互。人类在日常环境中毫不费力地执行这种从感知到行动的循环，需要一种将高维视觉输入和语言指令映射到全身运动行为的策略。Connecting egocentric observations to whole-body action is a fundamental challenge for humanoid robots operating in human-centered environments. A humanoid must perceive the world from its own viewpoint, identify task-relevant objects, navigate toward them, and physically interact with them through coordinated full-body motion. This perception-to-action loop, which humans execute effortlessly in everyday settings, requires a policy that maps high-dimensional visual input and language instructions to whole-body kinematic behavior.

学习这样的策略需要配对数据：同步的自我中心观察、任务指令和与机器人兼容的全身轨迹。之前的工作已经探索了几种获得这种监督的途径，但每种途径都有很大的局限性。现实世界的远程操作系统Learning such a policy requires paired data: synchronized egocentric observations, task instructions, and robot-compatible whole-body trajectories. Prior work has explored several paths to obtain this supervision, each with significant limitations. Real-world teleoperation systems[ze2025twist2, li2025clone]可以产生高质量的配对演示，但收集全身数据仍然昂贵且难以跨不同场景、对象和交互类型进行扩展。人体动作捕捉数据集提供了丰富的全身参考，可以重新定位到人形形态，但它们缺乏相应的机器人自我中心观察。以自我为中心的视频数据集提供丰富的视觉体验，但不与机器人兼容的运动学或动作配对。没有任何一个现有来源可以提供可扩展的政策学习所需的完整元组——视觉、语言和全身运动学。can produce high-quality paired demonstrations, but collecting full-body data remains expensive and difficult to scale across diverse scenes, objects, and interaction types. Human motion-capture datasets offer rich whole-body references that can be retargeted to humanoid morphologies, but they lack the corresponding robot egocentric observations. Egocentric video datasets provide abundant visual experience, but are not paired with robot-compatible kinematics or actions. No single existing source provides the complete tuple — vision, language, and whole-body kinematics — needed for scalable policy learning.

3 方法Method

我们的目标是使 Unitree G1 类人机器人能够根据以自我为中心的观察和任务指令执行基于感知的局部操作，包括对象导向导航和盒子交互。我们将问题表述为运动学预测和全身跟踪：给定当前以自我为中心的视图、指令和机器人运动学状态，高级策略预测短视界 G1 全身运动学轨迹，全身跟踪器将预测轨迹转换为可执行的机器人动作。这种分解使全身运动学成为感知策略的学习目标，并将中心数据需求转移到配对的自我中心观察、任务指令和 G1 运动学。我们在重建场景中综合获得这种监督，避免了为目标任务收集全身遥操作数据的需要。图Our goal is to enable a Unitree G1 humanoid to perform perception-based loco-manipulation, including object-directed navigation and box interaction, from egocentric observations and task instructions. We formulate the problem as kinematic prediction followed by whole-body tracking: given the current egocentric view, instruction, and robot kinematic state, a high-level policy predicts a short-horizon G1 whole-body kinematic trajectory, and a whole-body tracker converts the predicted trajectory into executable robot actions. This decomposition makes whole-body kinematics the learning target for the perception policy, and shifts the central data requirement to paired egocentric observations, task instructions, and G1 kinematics. We obtain this supervision synthetically in reconstructed scenes, avoiding the need to collect full-body teleoperation data for the target tasks. 5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一种基于感知的人形局部操纵系统，该系统通过重建 3D 场景中的综合交互进行训练。我们的管道生成与 Unitree G1 兼容的导航和盒子操纵轨迹，呈现配对的以自我为中心的观察结果，并训练 VLK 策略来预测基于跟踪器的执行的全身运动轨迹。模拟实验和物理 Unitree G1 演示了对象导向导航和框操作，表明基于场景的综合监督可以支持模拟到真实的人形机器人操作。这些结果表明，在重建场景中合成与机器人兼容的交互是实现可扩展的感知条件人形控制的有前途的途径。We presented a perception-based humanoid loco-manipulation system trained from synthesized interactions in reconstructed 3D scenes. Our pipeline generates Unitree G1-compatible navigation and box-manipulation trajectories, renders paired egocentric observations, and trains a VLK policy to predict whole-body kinematic trajectories for tracker-based execution. Experiments in simulation and on the physical Unitree G1 demonstrate object-directed navigation and box manipulation, showing that scene-grounded synthetic supervision can support sim-to-real humanoid loco-manipulation. These results suggest that synthesizing robot-compatible interactions in reconstructed scenes is a promising path toward scalable perception-conditioned humanoid control.

局限性。Limitations.

我们当前的交互合成受到 OMOMO 覆盖范围的限制，其中包含与有限的一组大型对象的交互。因此，生成的行为更适合双手运输盒状物体，而不是抓取杯子或工具等小物体。类似地，接触感知跟踪器通过手腕与物体接触来稳定大物体的操纵，但不能解决精确的物体抓取问题。将 VLK 扩展到小物体操作需要更丰富的交互数据和专为精确物体抓取而设计的低级控制器。Our current interaction synthesis is limited by the coverage of OMOMO, which contains interactions with a limited set of large objects. As a result, the generated behaviors are better suited to bimanual transport of box-like objects than to grasping small objects such as cups or tools. Similarly, the contact-aware tracker stabilizes large-object manipulation through wrist-object contact, but does not address precise object grasping. Extending VLK to small-object manipulation would require richer interaction data and a low-level controller designed for precise object grasping.

B.2 仿真评估指标Metric for Evaluation in Simulation

During evaluation, the simulator is paused while the VLK policy performs trajectory inference at the end of each motion chunk, avoiding the need for real-time chunked execution. For each task mode, we hold out 10% of the synthesized trajectories (1000 trajectories per mode) for evaluation. The humanoid is initialized from the initial state of each held-out trajectory and evaluated on whether it can successfully complete the corresponding language-conditioned task.

对于“步行到”任务，如果人形机器人到达指定的目标对象或框，停止在目标的 0.5 米范围内，并避免碰撞或穿透目标几何体，则认为卷展成功。For “Walk To” tasks, a rollout is considered successful if the humanoid reaches the specified target object or box, stops within 0.5 m of the target, and avoids colliding with or penetrating the target geometry.

对于“转身”任务，成功需要人形机器人朝着指示的方向执行清晰的转身动作，而不是保持静止或继续向前运动。For “Turn Around” tasks, success requires the humanoid to perform a clear turning motion toward the instructed direction, rather than remaining stationary or continuing forward motion.

对于“拾取”任务，人形机器人必须成功抓住放置在地板上或物体顶部的盒子，并保持至少 20 个连续帧的稳定握持。For “Pick Up” tasks, the humanoid must successfully grasp a box placed either on the floor or on top of an object and maintain stable holding for at least 20 consecutive frames.

对于“放下”任务，成功的定义是人形机器人到达指定的目标物体并成功地将携带的盒子放置到目标表面或地板上。For “Put Down” tasks, success is defined as the humanoid reaching the specified target object and successfully placing the carried box onto the target surface or floor.
