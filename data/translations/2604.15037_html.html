从被动到主动：通过 ProVoice-Bench 评估语音代理的主动性From Reactive to Proactive: Assessing the Proactivity of Voice Agents via ProVoice-Bench

Ke    Yuhao    Yu 1 Shanghai Jiao Tong University {overji1, colane, yuwangsjtu}@sjtu.edu.cn
摘要Abstract

LLM 代理的最新进展正在逐渐从反应式、基于文本的范式转向主动式、多模式交互。然而，现有的基准主要侧重于反应性响应，忽视了主动干预和监控的复杂性。为了弥补这一差距，我们推出了 ProVoice-Bench，这是第一个专为主动语音代理设计的评估框架，具有四个新颖的​​任务。通过利用多阶段数据合成管道，我们精选了 1,182 个高质量样本进行严格测试。我们对最先进的多模式法学硕士的评估揭示了显着的性能差距，特别是在过度触发和推理能力方面。这些发现凸显了当前模型的局限性，并为开发更自然、上下文感知的主动代理提供了路线图。Recent advancements in LLM agents are gradually shifting from reactive, text-based paradigms toward proactive, multimodal interaction. However, existing benchmarks primarily focus on reactive responses, overlooking the complexities of proactive intervention and monitoring. To bridge this gap, we introduce ProVoice-Bench, the first evaluation framework specifically designed for proactive voice agents, featuring four novel tasks. By leveraging a multi-stage data synthesis pipeline, we curate 1,182 high-quality samples for rigorous testing. Our evaluation of state-of-the-art Multimodal LLMs reveals a significant performance gap, particularly regarding over-triggering and reasoning capabilities. These findings highlight the limitations of current models and offer a roadmap for developing more natural, context-aware proactive agents.

1 简介Introduction

随着多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的快速发展With the rapid development of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)[comanici2025gemini, wu2025omnigen2explorationadvancedmultimodal, Qwen3-Omni, kimiteam2025kimiaudiotechnicalreport]，我们见证了先进语音代理的出现，能够通过端到端模型感知音频并生成自然语音响应, we have witnessed the emergence of advanced voice agents capable of perceiving audio and generating natural speech responses via end-to-end models[zou2026ltsvoiceagentlistenthinkspeakframeworkefficient, jain2025voiceagentbenchvoiceassistantsready]。这些语音代理还可以利用其强大的文本功能执行复杂的推理、规划和工具使用. These voice agents can also execute complex reasoning, planning, and tool use by leveraging their robust textual capabilities[chen2024voicebenchbenchmarkingllmbasedvoice, chiang2025shankssimultaneoushearingthinking, maben2025auraagentunderstandingreasoning]。虽然这些代理提供了更自然和智能的交互体验，但它们主要以反应范式运行，仅在明确指示时才做出响应。这种限制使他们无法推断隐含需求或检测需要干预的特定触发点. While these agents offer a more natural and intelligent interaction experience, they operate primarily in a reactive paradigm, responding only when explicitly instructed. This limitation prevents them from inferring implicit needs or detecting specific trigger points that necessitate intervention[10.1145/3539618.3594250]。相比之下，人类沟通本质上是主动的。人类拥有基于对上下文和环境的持续评估灵活干预对话的能力. In contrast, human communication is inherently proactive; humans possess the ability to flexibly intervene in a conversation based on their continuous assessment of the context and environment[lu2024proactiveagentshiftingllm].

与反应性模型相比，我们主张主动代理的必要性In contrast to reactive models, we advocate for the necessity of proactive agents[wang2025proactivevideoqacomprehensivebenchmarkevaluating, wang2025roboomni]。这些代理不应该仅仅等待用户指示他们；他们还应该意识到何时提供援助会有帮助。此外，他们应该有能力保持休眠状态，等待特定的触发点（例如预定义的主题或特定的环境声音），然后再进行干预。例如，主动代理可能会在检测到用户语音中的犹豫时提供帮助，或者在出现先前指定的警报声音时触发提醒。最近，一些研究探索了主动多模式代理，例如 ContextAgent. Such agents should not merely wait for users to instruct them; they should also sense when providing assistance would be helpful. Furthermore, they should have the ability to remain dormant, waiting for specific trigger points—such as a pre-defined topic or a particular environmental sound—before intervening. For instance, a proactive agent might offer assistance upon detecting hesitation in a user's speech, or trigger a reminder when a previously specified alarm sound occurs. Recently, some studies have explored proactive multimodal agents, like ContextAgent[yang2025contextagentcontextawareproactivellm]和 ProAgentand ProAgent[yang2025proagentharnessingondemandsensory]，但他们主要关注视觉提示，而忽略音频模式中的丰富信息。此外，他们通常将主动交互限制为隐式提示，而没有探索用户定义的触发点的范例。, but they focus primarily on visual cues and ignore the rich information within the audio modality. Moreover, they often limit proactive interaction to implicit cues, without exploring the paradigm of user-defined trigger points.

为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了 ProVoice-Bench，这是第一个专门用于评估语音代理主动性的评估框架。该基准包括 1,182 个精心策划的样本，涵盖四项新颖的主动任务，如图所示To bridge this gap, we introduce ProVoice-Bench, the first evaluation framework specifically designed to assess proactivity in voice agents. The benchmark comprises 1,182 meticulously curated samples across four novel proactive tasks as shown in Figure1: (1) 主动意图捕获 (PIC)Proactive Intent Capture (PIC)，要求模型掌握对话中隐含的意图并发起工具调用； (2), requiring the model to grasp implicit intent within the conversation and initiate tool calls; (2)潜在主题监视器 (LTM)Latent Topic Monitor (LTM)，其中模型监控对话并仅在检测到用户定义的语义触发器时触发帮助； (3), where the model monitors dialogues and triggers assistance only upon detecting a user-defined semantic trigger; (3)上下文事实检查(CFC)Context Fact Checking(CFC)，当口头陈述与数字上下文记录相矛盾时，需要打断用户； (4), which necessitates interrupting the user when verbal statements contradict digital context records; (4)环境声音传感(ESS)Environment Sound Sensing(ESS)，代理将用户定义的声音事件识别为干预提示。, where the agent recognizes user-defined acoustic events as cues for intervention.

总的来说，这四项任务封装了强大的主动式语音代理所需的核心功能。虽然 PIC 解决了从隐含语言线索推断意图的传统挑战，但其余任务为更复杂的主动行为引入了开创性基准：LTM 和 ESS 专注于跨对话和声学环境的基于条件的触发，而 CFC 通过识别口头陈述和数字上下文之间的矛盾来探索知识驱动的干预。为了促进严格的评估，我们开发了专门的数据合成管道来生成高保真、自然的样本。我们对最先进的 MLLM 的后续评估发现了巨大的性能差距，这表明当前模型尚未完全具备可靠的主动交互能力。这项工作的贡献总结如下：Collectively, these four tasks encapsulate the core capabilities required for a robust proactive voice agent. While PIC addresses the traditional challenge of inferring intent from implicit linguistic cues, the remaining tasks introduce pioneering benchmarks for more complex proactive behaviors: LTM and ESS focus on condition-based triggering across dialogues and acoustic environments, and CFC explores knowledge-driven intervention by identifying contradictions between verbal statements and digital contexts. To facilitate rigorous evaluation, we developed a specialized data synthesis pipeline to produce high-fidelity, naturalistic samples. Our subsequent evaluation of state-of-the-art MLLMs uncovers a substantial performance gap, indicating that current models are not yet fully equipped for reliable proactive interaction. The contributions of this work are summarized as follows:

  • •

    我们为主动代理提出了新的任务和范例，将音频和数字环境集成在一起，为与语音代理进行更自然的通信提供了愿景。We propose new tasks and a paradigm for proactive agents that integrate audio and digital context, offering a vision for more natural communication with voice agents.

  • •

    我们推出了 ProVoice-Bench，其中包含四项全新主动任务中精心挑选的 1,182 个样本。We present the ProVoice-Bench, containing 1,182 carefully selected samples across the four brand-new proactive tasks.

  • •

    我们在 ProVoice-Bench 上评估了多个开源 MLLM，发现了显着的性能差距，并确定了 MLLM 当前在主动交互方面的缺点。We evaluate several open-source MLLMs on ProVoice-Bench, uncovering a significant performance gap and identifying the current shortcomings of MLLMs in proactive interaction.

2 ProVoice-工作台结构ProVoice-Bench Construction

2.1 任务概述Task Overview

与现有的反应代理相比，我们提出了 ProVoice-Bench，这是一个综合评估套件，包含专为主动交互任务而设计的大规模语音语料库。在此基准中，交互的必要性是通过监视多模式信号来确定的，包括数字上下文（例如移动应用程序状态）以及对话中的隐式提示和显式触发。我们提出四项综合性主动任务：In contrast to existing reactive agents, we propose ProVoice-Bench, a comprehensive evaluation suite comprising a large-scale speech corpus designed for proactive interaction tasks. Within this benchmark, the necessity for interaction is determined by monitoring multimodal signals, including digital context (e.g., mobile application states), as well as implicit cues and explicit triggers within the conversation. We propose four comprehensive proactive tasks as below:

  • •

    主动意图捕获 (PIC)：Proactive Intent Capture (PIC):该模型从微妙的语言线索（例如，对话中讨论的犹豫或预期行动项目）推断出隐含的用户意图，并在寻求确认时主动发起工具调用请求。某些请求需要了解用户的数字环境以确保操作准确性。The model infers implicit user intentions from nuanced linguistic cues (e.g., hesitation or prospective action items discussed in dialogue) and proactively initiates tool-call requests while seeking confirmation. Some requests require understanding of the user's digital context for operational accuracy.

  • •

    潜在主题监视器 (LTM)：Latent Topic Monitor (LTM):用户指示模型监视周围的对话。该模型保持沉默，仅在对话者的语音中检测到用户指定的触发器时进行干预。The user instructs the model to monitor ambient conversations. The model remains silent, intervening only when a trigger designated by the user is detected in the speech of interlocutors.

  • •

    环境声音传感（ESS）：Environment Sound Sensing (ESS):该模型在检测特定声音事件（例如警报）时提供帮助，保持安静，直到识别出用户预定义的触发器。The model provides assistance upon detecting specific acoustic events (e.g., alarms), remaining silent until the trigger predefined by the user is recognized.

  • •

    上下文事实检查 (CFC)：Context Fact Checking (CFC):当口头陈述与用户手机上的数字环境相矛盾时，模型会主动中断以提供事实更正，确保与用户数字环境的一致性。When verbal statements contradict digital context on users' mobile phones, the model proactively interrupts to provide factual corrections, ensuring consistency with the user's digital context.

2.2.1 数字国家建设Digital State Construction

为了模拟真实且语义丰富的数字环境，以奠定用户角色并告知未来潜在的操作，我们采用 OB2 中的应用程序状态格式To simulate realistic and semantically rich digital contexts, which serve to ground user personas and inform potential future actions, we adopt the application state format from OB2[veerabadran2025benchmarkingegocentricmultimodalgoal]。生成过程首先从对话框主题数据集中随机选择一个主题. The generation process begins by randomly selecting a theme from the dialog-topics dataset[thatsgroes2025dialogtopics]。随后，我们采用Qwen3-Max. Subsequently, we employ Qwen3-Max[qwen3max]合成以这些主题为条件的细粒度数字状态。to synthesize fine-grained digital states conditioned on these themes.

这些状态包含隐式提示，例如预定的约会（例如，特定的会议时间）或个人限制（例如，饮食限制或医疗条件）。通过合并这些潜在变量，管道为模型提供了必要的上下文基础，以评估主动干预在后期阶段是否在上下文中合理。These states incorporate implicit cues, such as scheduled appointments (e.g., specific meeting times) or personal constraints (e.g., dietary restrictions or medical conditions). By incorporating these underlying variables, the pipeline provides the necessary contextual foundation for the model to evaluate whether proactive intervention is contextually justified in later stages.

2.2.2 场景合成Scene Synthesis

在此阶段，法学硕士分析数字状态中的隐含信息，以构建自然且明确的面向任务的场景。每个场景都是根据数字环境、指定的任务类别和一组可用工具进行合成的。生成的输出包括：触发线索（引发主动模型响应的特定用户操作或环境输入）、对话上下文的描述以及旨在确保模型能够准确破译相对时间和事件序列的显式时间元数据。During this phase, the LLM analyzes the implicit information within the digital states to construct a natural and unambiguous task-oriented scenario. Each scene is synthesized based on the digital context, a designated task category, and a set of available tools. The resulting output comprises: triggering cues (specific user actions or environmental inputs that elicit proactive model response), a description of the conversational context, and explicit temporal metadata engineered to ensure models can accurately decipher relative timing and event sequences.

3.2 指标Metrics

过度触发的倾向。Propensity for Over-triggering.结果揭示了过度触发的普遍趋势。这在 LTM 任务中尤其明显，无论对话中是否存在实际触发点，大多数模型都倾向于做出响应。同样，在 CFC 任务中，模型经常无法识别对话何时在不违反数字环境的情况下进行。The results reveal a widespread tendency toward over-triggering. This is particularly evident in LTM tasks, where most models tend to respond regardless of whether actual trigger points exist in the conversation. Similarly, in CFC tasks, models frequently fail to recognize when a conversation proceeds without any violation of the digital context.

思想链 (CoT) 增强分析密集型任务。Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Enhances Analysis-Intensive Tasks.实验证据表明 CoTExperimental evidence suggests that CoT[shao2024deepseekmathpushinglimitsmathematical]显着提高 CFC、LTM 和 PIC 任务的性能。这些任务需要模型来分析嵌入数字环境或用户语音中的隐式线索。此外，CoT 有助于弥合交互决策与执行适当响应之间的差距。significantly improves performance in CFC, LTM, and PIC tasks. Furthermore, CoT helps bridge the gap between the decision to interact and the execution of an appropriate response.

发言决策与任务执行之间的差异。Discrepancy Between Decision-to-Speak and Task Execution.结果凸显了“知道何时说话”和“知道做什么”之间的根本差距。当前的模型在交互过程中经常遭受语义漂移或生成幻觉的工具调用。这强调了未来研究迫切需要优先考虑 MLLM 中的情境感知和环境感知。The results highlight a fundamental gap between ``knowing when to speak'' and ``knowing what to do.'' Current models frequently suffer from semantic drift during interactions or generate hallucinated tool calls. This underscores the urgent need for future research to prioritize context awareness and environment sensing in MLLMs.

4 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 ProVoice-Bench，这是第一个针对主动式音频代理的评估套件，具有跨四项新颖任务的 1,182 个高质量样本。通过将数字上下文与音频输入相集成，我们的基准测试将代理范例从被动响应转变为上下文感知、主动交互。最先进的 MLLM 的实验结果揭示了显着的性能差距，特别是在过度触发和弥合“决策与执行”鸿沟方面。这些发现强调了未来多模式代理增强情境意识的必要性。我们希望 ProVoice-Bench 成为开发更自然、更自主的主动语音助手的催化剂。We presented ProVoice-Bench, the first evaluation suite for proactive audio agents, featuring 1,182 high-quality samples across four novel tasks. By integrating digital context with audio input, our benchmark shifts the agent paradigm from reactive responses to context-aware, proactive interaction. Experimental results on state-of-the-art MLLMs reveal a significant performance gap, particularly in over-triggering and bridging the ``decision-to-execution'' divide. These findings underscore the necessity of enhanced context awareness for future multimodal agents. We hope ProVoice-Bench serves as a catalyst for developing more natural and autonomous proactive voice assistants.
