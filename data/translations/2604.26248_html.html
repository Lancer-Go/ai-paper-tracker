摘要Abstract

最初的 SCR 理论提出，通货膨胀有两种不同的表达方式：The original SCR theory proposed that inflation has two distinct expressions:流通通货膨胀circulation inflation，通过商品和服务交易价格的上涨来衡量，以及, measured by rising transaction prices of goods and services, and预留通货膨胀reservation inflation[Huang 2018]，以货币符号、债务合同、储备债权和其他名义价值储存手段相对于实物商品的实际权重上升来衡量。日本的一篇配套论文 [Huang 2026] 测试了这一理论的一个方面，表明在货币进入储备主导阶段后，货币基础扩张不再强烈转化为消费者价格通胀。本文测试了SCR的另一面：在货币发行受到限制、流通通胀不存在的情况下，是否会出现储备通胀。 1873 年至 1896 年的经典金本位通货紧缩提供了干净的历史背景。使用英国长期零售价格数据和明尼阿波利斯联储历史美国消费者物价指数系列，我们发现两个经济体的价格水平均下降而不是上涨。 1873年至1896年间，英国的物价指数从18.0下降到14.7，而美国的历史CPI从36.0下降到25.0。然而，这种流通通货紧缩机械地增加了固定名义债权的实际价值。英国的固定索赔保留指数上升了 22.4%，美国上升了 44.0%。因此，这一事件将负流通通胀与正储备通胀结合起来。结果表明，硬通货并不能消除 SCR 意义上的通胀压力；它改变了它的表达域。在发行受到限制的情况下，通货膨胀可能不会表现为消费者价格上涨，而是表现为耐用名义债权相对于不断贬值的消费品和日益丰富的实物商品的实际扩张。因此，本文完成了 SCR 计划的经验对称性：日本显示了没有流通通胀的储备吸收，而金本位情况则显示了没有流通通胀的储备通胀。[Huang 2018], measured by the rising real weight of monetary symbols, debt contracts, reserve claims, and other nominal stores of value relative to physical goods. A companion Japan paper [Huang 2026] tested one side of this theory by showing that, after money entered a reserve-dominant phase, monetary-base expansion no longer translated strongly into consumer-price inflation. The present paper tests the other side of SCR: whether reservation inflation can arise even when monetary issuance is constrained and circulation inflation is absent. The classical gold-standard deflation of 1873–1896 provides a clean historical setting. Using long-run British retail price data and the Minneapolis Fed historical U.S. CPI series, we show that the price level declined rather than inflated in both economies. Between 1873 and 1896, Britain’s price index fell from 18.0 to 14.7, while the U.S. historical CPI fell from 36.0 to 25.0. Yet this circulation deflation mechanically increased the real value of fixed nominal claims. A fixedclaim reservation index rose by 22.4% in Britain and 44.0% in the United States. Thus, the episode combines negative circulation inflation with positive reservation inflation. The result demonstrates that hard money does not abolish inflationary pressure in the SCR sense; it changes its domain of expression. Under constrained issuance, inflation may appear not as rising consumer prices but as the real expansion of durable nominal claims over depreciating, consumable, and increasingly abundant physical goods. The paper therefore completes the empirical symmetry of the SCR program: Japan shows reserve absorption without circulation inflation, while the gold-standard case shows reservation inflation without circulation inflation.

一、简介1. Introduction

最初的SCR理论认为，通货膨胀并不是局限于消费者价格水平的单一现象，而是由贬值的实物商品和不贬值的货币符号之间的结构性不对称引起的双重过程。 [Huang 2018] 在这个框架下，通货膨胀有两个宏观表现。第一个是The original SCR theory proposed that inflation is not a single phenomenon confined to the consumer-price level, but a dual process arising from the structural asymmetry between depreciating physical goods and non-depreciating monetary symbols. [Huang 2018] In that framework, inflation has two macroscopic expressions. The first is流通通货膨胀circulation inflation，其中货币扩张传导到商品和服务的交易价格中。第二个是, in which monetary expansion is transmitted into the transaction prices of goods and services. The second is预留通货膨胀reservation inflation，其中货币符号、储备余额、债务合同、金融债权或其他名义价值储存手段相对于实物商品的实际权重不断扩大，即使消费者价格通胀不存在。日本报纸检验了这一命题的一个方面：它表明，在货币进入储备主导阶段后，货币基础扩张不再转化为强劲的流通价格通胀。 [Huang 2026]本文旨在测试SCR的另一面。我们问，在相反的货币条件下，储备通货膨胀是否会出现：当货币发行受到限制时，流通通货膨胀不存在，物价水平下降。, in which monetary symbols, reserve balances, debt contracts, financial claims, or other nominal stores of value expand in real weight relative to physical goods, even when consumer-price inflation is absent. The Japan paper tested one side of this proposition: it showed that, after money entered a reserve-dominant phase, monetary-base expansion no longer translated into strong circulation-price inflation. [Huang 2026] The present paper is designed to test the other side of SCR. We ask whether reservation inflation can arise under the opposite monetary condition: when money issuance is restrained, circulation inflation is absent, and the price level falls.

1873 年至 1896 年的经典金本位通货紧缩为这一测试提供了一个特别干净的历史背景。在金本位制下，货币扩张在制度上受到可兑换性、黄金供应、国际收支纪律和央行储备管理的限制。这并不意味着货币存量实际上是固定的，但它确实意味着货币创造比现代法定信贷体系受到更多机械限制。同期，工业生产率、国际贸易、运输能力和商品供应大幅增长。其结果不是消费价格的通货膨胀性上涨，而是总体物价水平的长期下降。在英国，零售价格指数从 1873 年的 18.0 下降到 1896 年的 14.7。 [ONS 2026] 在美国，明尼阿波利斯联储历史 CPI 系列从 1873 年的 36.0 下降到 1896 年的 25.0；同一消息来源指出，其 1913 年之前的值是历史估计值，并使用 1967 年=100 作为指数基础。 [美联储明尼阿波利斯，2026]The classical gold-standard deflation of 1873–1896 provides a particularly clean historical setting for this test. Under the gold standard, monetary expansion was institutionally constrained by convertibility, gold supply, balance-of-payments discipline, and central-bank reserve management. This does not mean that the money stock was literally fixed, but it does mean that money creation was far more mechanically constrained than in modern fiat-credit systems. During the same period, industrial productivity, international trade, transport capacity, and commodity supply expanded substantially. The result was not an inflationary rise in consumer prices, but a long decline in the general price level. In Britain, the retail price index declined from 18.0 in 1873 to 14.7 in 1896. [ONS 2026] In the United States, the Minneapolis Fed historical CPI series declined from 36.0 in 1873 to 25.0 in 1896; the same source notes that its pre-1913 values are historical estimates and uses 1967=100 as the index base. [FED Minneapolis, 2026]

用传统术语来说，这一事件通常被描述为通货紧缩。然而，在 SCR 框架中，通货紧缩并不只是通货膨胀的对立面。如果货币债权、债券、债务合同、租金债权或现金余额的名义价值保持不变，而价格水平下降，则该名义债权的实际购买力会自动上升。持有固定债权的债权人不需要名义升值即可实现真正的扩张；流通价格水平的下降本身就增加了对商品索取权的实际控制力。这是本文研究的中心机制。这种说法并不是说金本位经济经历了消费者价格通胀。显然没有。该观点认为，流通通胀的缺失暴露了更深层次的货币通胀：非贬值名义债权相对于贬值、消费品和日益丰富的实物商品的实际通胀。In conventional terminology, this episode is usually described as deflation. In the SCR framework, however, deflation is not simply the opposite of inflation. If the nominal value of a monetary claim, bond, debt contract, rent claim, or cash balance remains fixed while the price level falls, then the real purchasing power of that nominal claim rises automatically. A creditor holding a fixed claim does not need nominal appreciation in order to experience real expansion; the decline of the circulation price level itself increases the real command of the claim over goods. This is the central mechanism examined in the present paper. The claim is not that the gold-standard economy experienced consumer-price inflation. It plainly did not. The claim is that the absence of circulation inflation exposed a deeper form of monetary inflation: the real inflation of non-depreciating nominal claims relative to depreciating, consumable, and increasingly abundant physical goods.

这种区别对于 SCR 理论至关重要。如果通货膨胀仅被定义为消费者价格的上涨，那么1873年至1896年的金本位通货紧缩似乎是一个反通货膨胀的时期。如果通货膨胀更普遍地被定义为耐用货币符号与实物商品之间交换比率的变化，那么同一事件揭示了通货膨胀机制的相反面。流通价格水平下降，但固定名义债权的实际价值上升。因此，在硬通货条件下，储备通胀并不表现为资产价格繁荣或广泛的消费者价格通胀；而是表现为资产价格繁荣。它表现为名义债权的实际负担和实际购买力的机械增加。This distinction is essential for the SCR theory. If inflation is defined only as a rise in consumer prices, then the gold-standard deflation of 1873–1896 appears to be an anti-inflationary episode. If inflation is defined more generally as a change in the exchange ratio between durable monetary symbols and physical goods, then the same episode reveals the opposite side of inflationary mechanics. The circulation price level fell, but the real value of fixed nominal claims rose. Thus, under hard money, reservation inflation does not appear as asset-price exuberance or broad consumer-price inflation; it appears as a mechanical increase in the real burden and real purchasing power of nominal claims.

本文的经验逻辑故意被最小化。我们首先记录了 1873 年至 1896 年间英国和美国价格水平的下降。然后，我们构建了一个固定债权保留通货膨胀指数，定义为一单位名义债权相对于其 1873 年价值的实际价值。如果 𝑃𝑡 表示价格水平，并且名义债权 𝑁𝑡 固定为其初始值，则该指数将降至 𝑃1873/𝑃𝑡。每当价格水平下降时，这个数量就会增加。 1873 年至 1896 年间，英国价格指数下降了 18.3%，这意味着固定名义债权的实际价值增加了​​ 22.4%。美国价格指数下降 30.6%，意味着固定债权实际价值增长 44.0%。这些幅度并不是理论的产物；而是真实存在的。它们是固定名义索赔下价格水平下降的直接算术结果。The empirical logic of this paper is deliberately minimal. We first document the fall in price levels in Britain and the United States during 1873–1896. We then construct a fixed-claim reservation inflation index, defined as the real value of one unit of nominal claim relative to its 1873 value. If 𝑃𝑡 denotes the price level, and a nominal claim 𝑁𝑡 is fixed at its initial value, the index reduces to 𝑃1873/𝑃𝑡. This quantity rises whenever the price level falls. Over 1873–1896, the British price index declined by 18.3%, implying a 22.4% increase in the real value of a fixed nominal claim. The U.S. price index declined by 30.6%, implying a 44.0% increase in fixed-claim real value. These magnitudes are not artifacts of theory; they are the direct arithmetic consequence of a falling price level under fixed nominal claims.

因此，本文的贡献不是将金本位重新解释为普通的通货膨胀制度。它的贡献在于表明，在 SCR 内，硬通货通货紧缩和保留通胀并不矛盾。它们是从不同领域观察到的同一结构过程的两种描述。在流通领域，商品价格下降。在保留领域，名义债权的实际权重上升。因此，金本位事件为日本案例提供了缺失的实证对应物。这两种情况共同表明，通胀压力是阶段性的：在法定储备制度下，货币扩张可能会被吸收到储备结构中，而不会产生 CPI 通胀；在硬通货制度下，限制发行可能会抑制消费者物价指数通胀，同时仍会增加名义债权的实际主导地位。The contribution of this paper is therefore not to reinterpret the gold standard as an ordinary inflationary regime. Its contribution is to show that, within SCR, hardmoney deflation and reservation inflation are not contradictory. They are two descriptions of the same structural process observed from different domains. In the circulation domain, the price of goods falls. In the reservation domain, the real weight of nominal claims rises. The gold-standard episode thereby provides the missing empirical counterpart to the Japan case. Together, the two cases imply that inflationary pressure is phase-dependent: in a fiat reserve regime, money expansion may be absorbed into reserve structures without producing CPI inflation; in a hardmoney regime, constrained issuance may suppress CPI inflation while still increasing the real dominance of nominal claims.

2.理论：流通通胀和储备通胀2. Theory: Circulation Inflation and Reservation Inflation

SCR 框架始于物理不对称。 [Huang 2018] 商品、服务、机器、农作物、库存、建筑物和生物体都嵌入在物质时间中。它们会腐烂、被消耗、贬值、过时或需要维护。相比之下，货币符号不会以同样的方式经历物理贬值。纸币、准备金余额、政府债券、债务合同或账本条目可能会因制度程序、违约、通货膨胀或政策而失去价值，但它自然不会以与货币相同的速度衰减。The SCR framework begins from a physical asymmetry. [Huang 2018] Goods, services, machines, crops, inventories, buildings, and biological bodies are embedded in material time. 金本位情况特别有用，因为它接近该理论的硬通货极限。在法定信贷制度中，𝑁𝑡和𝑃𝑡都可能迅速变化，并且可能很难将流通通胀与储备通胀分开。相比之下，在古典金本位制下，货币扩张受到制度上的限制。相关的实证问题变得更简单：如果流通价格下跌，而名义债权在制度上保持持久，那么这些债权的实际价值会上升吗？机械地回答是肯定的。历史问题是这种影响有多大。The gold-standard case is especially useful because it approximates the hardmoney limit of this theory. In a fiat-credit regime, both 𝑁𝑡 and 𝑃𝑡 can change rapidly, and it may be difficult to separate circulation inflation from reservation inflation. Under the classical gold standard, by contrast, monetary expansion was institutionally constrained. The relevant empirical question becomes simpler: if circulation prices fall while nominal claims remain institutionally durable, does the real value of those claims rise? The answer is mechanically yes. The historical question is how large this effect was.

1873年至1896年时期给出了强有力的肯定答案。英国的价格指数从 18.0 下降至 14.7。相应的固定索赔保留指数从1.00上升至1.224。美国历史CPI从36.0下降至25.0。相应的固定索赔保留指数从1.00上升至1.440。因此，尽管流通价格水平正在下降，但固定名义债权的持有者的实际购买力却大幅增加。用 SCR 语言来说，系统经历了负流通通胀但正储备通胀。The 1873–1896 period gives a strong positive answer. In Britain, the price index fell from 18.0 to 14.7. The corresponding fixed-claim reservation index rose from 1.00 to 1.224. In the United States, the historical CPI fell from 36.0 to 25.0. The corresponding fixed-claim reservation index rose from 1.00 to 1.440. Thus, a holder of a fixed nominal claim experienced a substantial increase in real purchasing power, even though the circulation price level was falling. In SCR language, the system experienced negative circulation inflation but positive reservation inflation.

这种二元性可以通过以下阶段分类来概括。在流通通货膨胀体制中，𝐶𝐼𝑡 > 0，货币扰动在消费者价格中可见。在日本这样的储备吸收制度中，货币扩张可能会提高储备余额和央行资产负债表，而𝐶𝐼𝑡仍然疲软。在硬通货预留通胀制度中，𝐶𝐼𝑡 < 0 但 𝑅𝐼𝑡 > 0，因为固定名义债权获得实际权重。最后一个案例是本文的理论重点。This duality can be summarized by the following phase classification. 本文的实证背景是 1873 年至 1896 年的经典金本位通货紧缩。选择这一时期并不是因为它代表了完全没有货币变化，也不是因为金本位创造了完全固定的货币存量。之所以选择它，是因为它接近历史上重要的硬通货制度，其中货币发行受到可兑换性、储备纪律、国际黄金流动和央行信誉的限制。 [Capie and Webber 1985] 这使得它成为 SCR 预留通货膨胀方面的合适试验场：如果流通价格在受约束的货币体系下下降，那么即使没有普通的消费者价格通胀，固定名义债权的实际价值也应该上升。The empirical setting of this paper is the classical gold-standard deflation of 1873– 1896. This period is not selected because it represents a complete absence of monetary change, nor because the gold standard created a perfectly fixed money stock. It is selected because it approximates a historically important hard-money regime in which monetary issuance was constrained by convertibility, reserve discipline, international gold flows, and central-bank credibility. [Capie and Webber 1985] This makes it a suitable testing ground for the reservation-inflation side of SCR: if circulation prices fall under a constrained monetary system, then fixed nominal claims should rise in real value even without ordinary consumer-price inflation.

古典金本位在货币和金属储备之间强加了特定的制度关系。 [Eichengreen 2019] 国内货币负债在形式上或实际上都与黄金可兑换性挂钩。中央银行和银行系统可以扩大信贷，但扩张受到维持可兑换性信心的需要以及黄金流动的外部纪律的限制。失去黄金的国家面临着收紧货币条件、提高利率或减少国内信贷扩张的压力。 [Bordo 1981] 获得黄金的国家可以更容易地扩张，但整个体系仍然以货币黄金的供应和分配为基础。本文的重点并不是金本位制使货币供应量保持不变。但事实并非如此。关键在于，它在制度上使货币供应比现代法定制度更加困难。The classical gold standard imposed a specific institutional relation between money and metallic reserves. [Eichengreen 2019] Domestic monetary liabilities were linked, formally or effectively, to gold convertibility. Central banks and banking systems could expand credit, but expansion was constrained by the need to maintain confidence in convertibility and by the external discipline of gold flows. Countries losing gold faced pressure to tighten monetary conditions, raise interest rates, or reduce domestic credit expansion. [Bordo 1981] Countries gaining gold could expand more easily, but the system as a whole remained anchored by the supply and distribution of monetary gold. The important point for the present paper is not that the gold standard made money supply constant. It did not. The point is that it made money supply institutionally harder than in a modern fiat regime.

这种区别很重要，因为该论文不需要字面上固定数量的货币。 SCR 预留通货膨胀仅要求名义债权相对于流通价格水平具有耐久性。在硬通货制度下，许多名义债权——现金余额、债务本金、债券面值、租金合同和其他固定货币义务——在商品价格下跌时可以保持名义不变。一旦发生这种情况，这些索赔的实际价值就会机械地上升。因此，金本位在这里并不被视为一个完全封闭的物理系统，而是作为受限制的符号发行的历史基础近似值。This distinction matters because the paper does not require a literal fixed quantity of money. SCR reservation inflation requires only that nominal claims possess durability relative to the circulation price level. Under a hard-money regime, many nominal claims—cash balances, debt principal, bond face values, rent contracts, and other fixed monetary obligations—could remain fixed in nominal terms while goods prices declined. Once that happens, the real value of those claims rises mechanically. Therefore, the gold standard is not treated here as a perfectly closed physical system, but as a historically grounded approximation of constrained symbolic issuance.

在传统货币史上，这一时期通常被称为“大通货紧缩”。如果计量对象是流通商品和服务的价格水平，则该术语是准确的。从 1870 年代初到 1890 年代中期，英国零售价格大幅下降。美国历史消费者物价指数系列在同一窗口内也显示出类似的下降。因此，在流通价格层面上的经验事实是明确的：这一时期没有产生流通通胀。它产生了流通通货紧缩。In conventional monetary history, this episode is usually called the “Great Deflation.” The term is accurate if the object of measurement is the price level of circulating goods and services. 然而，对于 SCR 来说，这正是这一事件具有理论上价值的原因。如果价格下跌，而名义索赔仍然持久，那么这些索赔的实际负担就会上升。一单位货币可以购买更多商品。固定债务需要更多的实物来偿还。债券面值代表更大的购买力。固定租金要求需要更多的实际消费。货币符号相对于实物产出变得更重。因此，流通领域记录的是通货紧缩，而保留领域记录的是名义债权实际权重的增加。For SCR, however, this is precisely why the episode is theoretically valuable. If prices fall while nominal claims remain durable, then the real burden of those claims rises. A unit of money buys more goods. A fixed debt requires more real goods to repay. The circulation domain therefore records deflation, while the reservation domain records an increase in the real weight of nominal claims.

第一个实证结果很简单：在1873年至1896年的金本位窗口期间，流通价格水平没有上升。它掉下来了。在英国，零售价格指数从1873年的18.0下降到1896年的14.7，对应的累计流通价格变化为-18.3%。在美国，历史CPI在同一窗口内从36.0下降至25.0，对应的累计流通价格变化为-30.6%。明尼阿波利斯联储消息来源报告了 1967 年=100 的美国序列，并指出 1913 年之前的值应被视为历史估计；然而，十九世纪广泛的通货紧缩运动的规模足以支持目前的领域层面的比较。 [美联储明尼阿波利斯，2026]The first empirical result is straightforward: during the 1873–1896 gold-standard window, the circulation price level did not rise. It fell. In Britain, the retail price index declined from 18.0 in 1873 to 14.7 in 1896, corresponding to a cumulative circulation price change of -18.3%. In the United States, the historical CPI declined from 36.0 to 25.0 over the same window, corresponding to a cumulative circulation price change of -30.6%. The Minneapolis Fed source reports the U.S. series with 1967=100 and notes that pre-1913 values should be considered historical estimates; nevertheless, the broad nineteenth-century deflationary movement is large enough to support the present domain-level comparison. [FED Minneapolis, 2026]

这一结果证实了所选的历史窗口不是流通-通货膨胀事件。事实恰恰相反。如果通货膨胀仅被定义为消费者或零售价格水平的上涨，那么该时期应被描述为通货紧缩。这种传统的描述在流通商品和服务的层面上是正确的。然而，SCR 并不仅仅通过流通商品的价格变动来定义通货膨胀。它区分流通域和保留域。因此，流通价格水平的下降并不是分析的终点；这是保留机制变得可见的条件。This result confirms that the selected historical window is not a circulation-inflation episode. It is the opposite. If inflation is defined only as an increase in the consumer or retail price level, then the period should be described as deflation. That conventional description is correct at the level of circulating goods and services. However, SCR does not define inflation solely by the price movement of circulating commodities. It distinguishes between the circulation domain and the reservation domain. The fall in the circulation price level is therefore not the endpoint of the analysis; it is the condition under which the reservation mechanism becomes visible.

The British and U.S. series have different origins, constructions, and institutional contexts, yet both point to the same domain-level result. Circulation inflation was absent; circulation deflation was present. The next question is therefore not whether prices rose, but what this price decline did to the real value of nominal claims.

It does not require broad asset-price appreciation, speculative bubbles, or expansionary central-bank balance sheets. It arises from the durability of nominal symbols under a falling circulation price level. The monetary claim does not need to increase in nominal size. It inflates in real terms because the goods against which it is measured become cheaper.

5.6.结果证明了什么，没有证明什么5.6. What the result proves, and what it does not prove

实证结果证明了一个狭隘但重要的观点。他们表明，在 1873 年至 1896 年的硬通货环境中，流通通胀的缺失并不意味着 SCR 术语中通胀再分配的缺失。由于流通价格水平下降，固定名义债权的实际价值升值。这正是 SCR 的预留通货膨胀方面。The empirical results prove a narrow but important point. They show that, in the 1873–1896 hard-money environment, the absence of circulation inflation did not imply the absence of inflationary redistribution in SCR terms. Fixed nominal claims appreciated in real value because the circulation price level fell. This is exactly the reservation-inflation side of SCR.

结果并不能证明所有资产持有者都获益，所有债务人均等损失，或者每种形式的财富都表现为固定的名义债权。他们也不声称金本位制是纯粹的固定货币体系。信贷条件发生了变化，黄金供应发生了变化，银行体系也发生了演变。这个论点更精确：每当名义债权保持持久而𝑃𝑡下降时，这些债权就会获得实际价值。历史上的金本位通缩大规模地提供了这样的背景。The results do not prove that all asset holders gained, that all debtors lost equally, or that every form of wealth behaved like a fixed nominal claim. They also do not claim that the gold standard was a purely fixed-money system. Credit conditions changed, gold supply changed, and banking systems evolved. The argument is more precise: whenever nominal claims remain durable while 𝑃𝑡 declines, those claims gain real value. The historical gold-standard deflation supplied such a setting at large scale.

这种区别可以防止论文容易被误解。该说法并不是“金本位导致CPI通胀”。但事实并非如此。这种说法并不是“通货紧缩实际上是CPI通胀”。它不是。其主张是消费者物价指数通货紧缩可以与储备通货膨胀共存，并自动产生。就SCR而言，金本位并没有消除通胀压力。它将相关的通货膨胀表达从流通领域移至保留领域。This distinction protects the paper from an easy misunderstanding. The claim is not “the gold standard caused CPI inflation.” It did not. The claim is not “deflation is secretly CPI inflation.” It is not. The claim is that CPI deflation can coexist with, and mechanically generate, reservation inflation. In SCR terms, the gold standard did not abolish inflationary pressure. It moved the relevant inflationary expression from the circulation domain into the reservation domain.

6. 讨论6. Discussion

6.1.通货紧缩不一定是通货膨胀的对立面6.1. Deflation is not necessarily the opposite of inflation

本文的中心含义是通货紧缩不一定是通货膨胀的对立面。它只是流通通胀的反面。在传统用法中，通货膨胀和通货紧缩是通过消费者价格变化的符号来定义的。物价水平上升就是通货膨胀；价格水平下降就是通货紧缩。这种语言对于衡量家庭购买成本很有用，但作为货币象征权力的理论并不完整。 SCR增加了第二个域：保留域，其中相关对象不是商品的价格，而是名义索赔的实际重量。The central implication of this paper is that deflation is not necessarily the opposite of inflation. It is only the opposite of circulation inflation. In conventional usage, inflation and deflation are defined by the sign of consumer-price change. A rising price level is inflation; a falling price level is deflation. That language is useful for measuring household purchasing costs, but it is incomplete as a theory of monetary-symbolic power. SCR adds a second domain: the reservation domain, where the relevant object is not the price of goods, but the real weight of nominal claims.

在这个两个领域的框架下，通货紧缩在保留领域可能会导致通货膨胀。当价格水平下降时，一单位的固定名义债权会购买更多的商品。该索赔实际上不会生产更多的商品，也不需要增加名义数量。它的实际命令会因为分母 𝑃𝑡 下降而扩大。因此，对于流通商品来说表现为通货紧缩的价格变动对于货币债权来说表现为通货膨胀。Under this two-domain framework, deflation can be inflationary in the reservation domain. When the price level falls, one unit of fixed nominal claim purchases more goods. The claim does not physically produce more goods, and it does not need to increase in nominal magnitude. Its real command expands because the denominator, 𝑃𝑡 , declines. Thus, the same price movement that appears as deflation for circulating goods appears as inflation for monetary claims.

这一结果对于硬通货系统尤其重要。硬通货经常被认为可以抗通胀，因为它可以限制超额发行并保护货币的购买力。该描述部分正确。硬通货可以抑制流通通胀。但从SCR的角度来看，同样的机制可以加强货币的储备力。通过防止货币符号与实物商品一起贬值，随着生产力和供应扩张推动价格下跌，硬通货使得固定名义债权获得实际权重。This result is especially important for hard-money systems. Hard money is often defended as anti-inflationary because it constrains overissue and protects the purchasing power of money. That description is partly correct. Hard money can suppress circulation inflation. But from an SCR perspective, the same mechanism can strengthen the reservation power of money. By preventing monetary symbols from depreciating alongside physical goods, hard money allows fixed nominal claims to gain real weight as productivity and supply expansion push prices downward.

因此，这个悖论只是显而易见的。硬通货在流通领域是抗通胀的，在储备领域是抑制通胀的。它限制了商品价格的上涨，但它允许货币符号的实际价值上升。它保护货币持有者和名义债权人，但增加了欠钱或必须通过出售实物产出获得货币的人的负担。The paradox is therefore only apparent. Hard money is anti-inflationary in the circulation domain and inflationary in the reservation domain. It constrains the price of goods from rising, but it permits the real value of monetary symbols to rise. It protects the holder of money and nominal claims, but it increases the burden on those who owe money or must obtain money through the sale of physical output.

6.2.预留通货膨胀的物理意义6.2. The physical meaning of reservation inflation

更深层次的 SCR 论点是物理性的，而不仅仅是基于会计的。实物商品存在于物质时间中。它们被消耗、磨损、更换、按成本存储或变得过时。货币符号不同。名义债权可以作为账本条目、债券面值、债务合同或准备金余额持续存在，而不会经历相同的实质性衰减。这种不对称造成了物质生产和象征保留之间的结构性失衡。The deeper SCR argument is physical rather than merely accounting-based. Physical goods exist in material time. They are consumed, worn out, replaced, stored at cost, or rendered obsolete. Monetary symbols are different. A nominal claim can persist as a ledger entry, bond face value, debt contract, or reserve balance without undergoing the same material decay. This asymmetry produces a structural imbalance between physical production and symbolic reservation.

在不断扩大的生产性经济中，商品可能会变得更加丰富和便宜。如果货币符号不会同时贬值，那么每个符号单位都会控制更多的物质世界。这是硬通货储备通货膨胀的物理核心。货币符号相对于它所声称的商品变得越来越强大，不是因为它变得更有生产力，而是因为实体经济降低了商品的流通价格，而符号名义上却保持不变。In an expanding productive economy, goods may become more abundant and cheaper. If monetary symbols do not depreciate in parallel, then each unit of symbol commands more of the physical world. This is the physical core of hardmoney reservation inflation. The monetary sign becomes increasingly powerful relative to the goods it claims, not because it has become more productive, but because the physical economy has lowered the circulation price of goods while the sign remains nominally intact.

这就是为什么 SCR 将通货膨胀视为比消费者价格上涨更广泛的概念。根本问题不仅仅是价格是否上涨。问题在于非折旧符号与折旧商品之间的关系是否发生变化。在法币超发的情况下，该符号可能相对于商品贬值，从而产生流通通胀。在硬通货通货紧缩的情况下，商品价格相对于符号贬值，从而产生储备通货膨胀。从最深的意义上来说，这些并不是对立的。它们是同一符号材料不对称性的不同相表达。This is why SCR treats inflation as a broader concept than consumer-price increase. The fundamental issue is not only whether prices rise. The issue is whether the relation between non-depreciating symbols and depreciating goods shifts. Under fiat overissue, the symbol may depreciate relative to goods, producing circulation inflation. Under hard-money deflation, goods depreciate in price relative to the symbol, producing reservation inflation. These are not opposites in the deepest sense. They are different phase expressions of the same symbolic-material asymmetry.

In hard-money regimes, it may appear as the real expansion of fixed nominal claims.

这一论点与传统的债务通货紧缩机制相关，但并不完全相同。 [Fisher 1933] 在债务通货紧缩理论中，价格下跌会增加实际债务负担，削弱借款人的实力，并可能引发清算、困境和金融收缩。 SCR 接受这种机制，但将其置于更广泛的预留通胀理论中。债务是名义债权的一种，但不是唯一的一种。当价格水平下跌时，货币余额、债券、租金索赔、准备金索赔和其他固定名义工具也会获得实际权重。The argument is related to, but not identical with, the traditional debt-deflation mechanism. 因此，SCR 的重点不仅仅是金融不稳定。它是象征性主张相对于实物商品的结构性扩展。债务通货紧缩理论强调当实际债务负担上升时可能随之而来的危机动态。 SCR强调事前领域转型：在危机发生之前，名义债权的实际价值已经膨胀。债务-通货紧缩螺旋式上升是储备通货膨胀可能产生的病理结果，而不是整个概念。The focus of SCR is therefore not only financial instability. 6.6.硬通货储备通胀的政治经济学6.6. The political economy of hard-money reservation inflation

硬通货储备通胀具有明显的分配影响。它有利于货币和固定名义债权的持有者，而不是收入依赖于以下跌价格出售商品的债务人和生产者。当价格下跌时，债务人必须提供更多的实际商品或劳动力来满足相同的名义债务。债权人从相同的名义债权中获得更大的实际价值。因此，硬通货通货紧缩并不是中性的。它将实体经济的实际控制权重新分配给保留方。Hard-money reservation inflation has a clear distributional implication. It favors holders of money and fixed nominal claims over debtors and producers whose income depends on selling goods at falling prices. When prices decline, a debtor must deliver more real goods or labor to satisfy the same nominal obligation. A creditor receives greater real value from the same nominal claim. Thus, hard-money deflation is not neutral. It redistributes real command over the physical economy toward the reservation side.

1873 年至 1896 年的经典金本位通货紧缩提供了清晰的历史检验。在英国，物价指数从1873年的18.0下降到1896年的14.7。在美国，历史CPI同期从36.0下降到25.0。这些变动代表负流通通胀。然而，同样的价格下跌却机械地增加了固定名义债权的实际价值。名义价值不变的固定债权的实际价值在英国增长了 22.4%，在美国增长了 44.0%。因此，消费者价格通胀的缺失并不意味着通胀性再分配的缺失。这意味着通胀表现从流通领域转向保留领域。The classical gold-standard deflation of 1873–1896 provides a clean historical test. In Britain, the price index declined from 18.0 in 1873 to 14.7 in 1896. In the United States, the historical CPI declined from 36.0 to 25.0 over the same period. These movements represent negative circulation inflation. Yet the same price decline mechanically increased the real value of fixed nominal claims. A fixed claim unchanged in nominal terms gained 22.4% in real value in Britain and 44.0% in the United States. Thus, the absence of consumer-price inflation did not imply the absence of inflationary redistribution. It implied a shift of inflationary expression from the circulation domain to the reservation domain.

这一结果改变了 SCR 中硬通货的解释。硬通货体系可以抑制流通通胀，但不一定能消除通胀压力。通过允许流通价格水平下降，同时名义债权保持持久，它增加了这些债权对实物商品的实际控制力。从这个意义上说，金本位通缩不仅仅是反通货膨胀。它对于流通商品来说是反通货膨胀的，但对于固定名义债权的实际重量来说却是通货膨胀的。This result changes the interpretation of hard money within SCR. A hard-money system may suppress circulation inflation, but it does not necessarily abolish inflationary pressure. By allowing the circulation price level to fall while nominal claims remain durable, it increases the real command of those claims over physical goods. In this sense, gold-standard deflation was not merely anti-inflationary. It was anti-inflationary for circulating goods, but inflationary for the real weight of fixed nominal claims.

结合日本的案例，本文给出了 SCR 的对称经验结构。日本的情况表明，当货币进入储备主导阶段时，货币扩张可能会失去与消费者价格指数的耦合。金本位案例表明，当物价下跌时，受限制的货币仍然会产生预留通胀。因此，这两种情况都支持同一个更深层次的原则：通货膨胀是阶段相关和领域相关的。 CPI只是货币物质不对称性的一种可观察的表现；在其他制度中，同样的不对称现象表现为储备吸收、资产负债表扩张或固定名义债权的实际升值。Together with the Japan case, the present paper gives SCR a symmetric empirical structure. Japan shows that monetary expansion can lose its coupling to CPI when money enters a reserve-dominant phase. The gold-standard case shows that constrained money can still generate reservation inflation when prices fall. The two cases therefore support the same deeper principle: inflation is phase-dependent and domain-dependent. CPI is only one observable expression of the monetarymaterial asymmetry; in other regimes, the same asymmetry appears as reserve absorption, balance-sheet expansion, or the real appreciation of fixed nominal claims.
