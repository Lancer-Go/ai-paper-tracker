空间和空间的自然语言接口 时态数据库：全面概述 方法、分类和未来方向Natural Language Interfaces for Spatial and Temporal Databases: A Comprehensive Overview of Methods, Taxonomy, and Future Directions

SAMYA ACHARJA    KANCHAN CHOWDHURY Department of Computer Science, Marquette University, Milwaukee, Wisconsin, USA
摘要Abstract

为数据库构建自然语言接口（称为 NLIDB）的任务最近引起了数据库和自然语言处理 (NLP) 社区的极大关注。随着位置感知传感器的迅速出现，地理空间数据集激增，地理空间数据库在支持地理空间应用方面发挥着至关重要的作用。然而，由于地理空间拓扑运算符和时间运算符的存在，查询地理空间和时间数据库与查询传统关系数据库有很大不同。为了弥合地理空间查询语言和非专家用户之间的差距，地理空间研究界越来越关注为地理空间数据库开发 NLIDB。然而，现有的研究在系统、数据集和方法选择方面仍然支离破碎，因此很难清楚地了解现有方法的概况、它们的优点和缺点以及未来研究的机会。现有的 NLIDB 调查侧重于通用数据库系统，并未将地理空间和时间数据库作为分析的主要焦点。为了解决这一差距，本文对地理空间和时间数据库的 NLIDB 研究进行了全面的调查。具体来说，我们提供了地理空间和时间 NLIDB 的数据集、评估指标和方法分类的详细概述，以及对现有方法的比较分析。我们的调查揭示了现有方法中反复出现的趋势、数据集和评估实践的巨大变化以及继续阻碍该领域进展的几个开放挑战。基于这些发现，我们确定了未来研究的有希望的方向，以推进地理空间和时间数据库的自然语言接口。The task of building a natural language interface to a database, known as NLIDB, has recently gained significant attention from both the database and Natural Language Processing (NLP) communities. With the proliferation of geospatial datasets driven by the rapid emergence of location-aware sensors, geospatial databases play a vital role in supporting geospatial applications. However, querying geospatial and temporal databases differs substantially from querying traditional relational databases due to the presence of geospatial topological operators and temporal operators. To bridge the gap between geospatial query languages and non-expert users, the geospatial research community has increasingly focused on developing NLIDBs for geospatial databases. Yet, existing research remains fragmented across systems, datasets, and methodological choices, making it difficult to clearly understand the landscape of existing methods, their strengths and weaknesses, and opportunities for future research. Existing surveys on NLIDBs focus on general-purpose database systems and do not treat geospatial and temporal databases as primary focus for analysis. To address this gap, this paper presents a comprehensive survey of studies on NLIDBs for geospatial and temporal databases. Specifically, we provide a detailed overview of datasets, evaluation metrics, and the taxonomy of the methods for geospatial and temporal NLIDBs, as well as a comparative analysis of the existing methods. Our survey reveals recurring trends in existing methods, substantial variation in datasets and evaluation practices, and several open challenges that continue to hinder progress in this area. Based on these findings, we identify promising directions for future research to advance natural language interfaces to geospatial and temporal databases.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

ST-NLIDB 的评估本质上比标准 NL 到 SQL (NL2SQL) 设置更加异构。一个关键原因是多个形式查询可以表达相同的用户意图，特别是当涉及空间谓词（距离缓冲区、包含关系、空间连接）或时间结构（时间窗口、间隔、轨迹段）时。因此，许多研究较少关注再现精确的参考查询，而更多地关注系统是否产生语义上正确的结果。Evaluation of ST-NLIDBs is inherently more heterogeneous than in standard NL to SQL (NL2SQL) settings. A key reason is that multiple formal queries can express the same user intent, particularly when spatial predicates (distance buffers, containment relations, spatial joins) or temporal constructs (time windows, intervals, trajectory segments) are involved. As a result, many studies focus less on reproducing an exact reference query and more on whether a system produces a semantically correct outcome.

第三个方法组成部分涉及识别查询的类型。许多系统首先将 NL 查询分类为一小组支持的类别之一，例如范围查询、最近邻搜索、空间连接、轨迹相似性查询或时间间隔查询。早期的方法通常依赖于基于规则的启发式方法来执行此步骤，而较新的系统通常使用轻量级神经分类器，例如基于 LSTM 的神经分类器，在精选的 NL 查询集合上进行训练以推断预期的查询类型（Wang 等人，2017）。The third methodological component involves identifying the type of query. Many systems begin by classifying an NL query into one of a small set of supported categories, such as range queries, nearest-neighbor searches, spatial joins, trajectory similarity queries, or temporal interval queries. Earlier approaches typically rely on rule-based heuristics for this step, whereas more recent systems often use lightweight neural classifiers, such as LSTM-based, trained on curated collections of NL queries to infer the intended query type (Wang et al.[wang2023nalmo]，刘等人。, Liu et al.[liu2023nalsd]).

系统识别出用户的意图后，下一步就是构建结构化查询。在大多数空间和时空 NLIDB 系统中，这是使用预定义的模板或运算符映射来完成的，而不是完全基于端到端神经模型的生成。典型的方法是槽填充，其中先前提取的元素（例如空间关系、距离、时间间隔或对象标识符）被插入到固定的查询模板中。这些槽对应于具体的数据库运算符，例如相交、内、k 最近邻、通过或 atperiod。使用这种规则引导的构造可确保生成的查询在语法上有效且可执行，这对于无约束神经模型通常难以可靠生成的复杂空间和时间操作非常重要。After the system identifies the user’s intent, the next step is to build a structured query. In most spatial and spatiotemporal NLIDB systems, this is done using predefined templates or operator mappings rather than fully end-to-end neural model-based generation. The typical approach is slot filling, where previously extracted elements such as spatial relations, distances, time intervals, or object identifiers are inserted into fixed query templates. These slots correspond to concrete database operators such as intersects, within, k-nearest neighbors, passes, or atperiods. Using this rule-guided construction ensures that the resulting query is both syntactically valid and executable, which is important for complex spatial and temporal operations that unconstrained neural models often struggle to generate reliably.

最近的工作通过合并代理或检索增强推理组件来扩展这种基于模板的管道。在这些系统中，复杂的问题被分解为更小的子任务，根据需要检索相关的模式信息或文档，并迭代地细化候选查询。一些系统还在产生最终答案之前执行中间查询来检查结果（Redd 等人，2017）。More recent works extend this template-based pipeline by incorporating agentic or retrieval-augmented reasoning components. In these systems, complex questions are broken down into smaller sub-tasks, relevant schema information or documentation is retrieved as needed, and candidate queries are refined iteratively. Some systems also execute intermediate queries to check results before producing a final answer (Redd et al.[ito2024clasp];于等人。; Yu et al.[yu2025monkuu];卡扎齐等人。; Kazazi et al.[khosravi2025questions]）。即使引入了法学硕士，整体流程仍然基本保持不变，通常包括实体提取、知识库的使用、规划、结构化查询生成、执行和结果解释。). Even with the introduction of LLMs, the overall pipeline remains largely unchanged, typically consisting of entity extraction, use of knowledge-bases, planning, structured query generation, execution, and result interpretation.

大多数研究还包括基于执行的验证步骤，其中针对空间或时空数据库运行生成的查询以确认正确性。输出通常采用结果集、时空轨迹或空间特征的形式，并且通常与地图或绘图等可视化效果配对。因此，评估的重点是查询是否正确执行并返回预期结果，而不是生成的查询文本与参考查询的匹配程度。Most studies also include an execution-based validation step, where generated queries are run against spatial or spatiotemporal databases to confirm correctness. The outputs usually take the form of result sets, spatiotemporal trajectories, or spatial features, and are often paired with visualizations such as maps or plots. As a result, evaluation focuses on whether the query executes correctly and returns the intended results, rather than on how closely the generated query text matches a reference query.

总而言之，文献表明 ST-NLIDB 系统始终青睐混合架构，这种架构结合了基于规则的逻辑、轻量级神经模型以及越来越多的基于 LLM 的组件，而不是依赖于纯粹的端到端学习。To summarize, the literature shows that ST-NLIDB systems consistently favor hybrid architectures that combine rule-based logic, lightweight neural models, and, increasingly, LLM-based components, rather than relying on purely end-to-end learning.

V-A 基于规则和语法驱动的 NLIDBRule-Based and Grammar-Driven NLIDBs

Once the input sentence matches a known grammatical structure, it is deterministically translated into a structured spatial query or analytical workflow. While this approach offers transparency and control over query interpretation, it also requires substantial manual effort to design and maintain the grammars, and it typically struggles to handle linguistic variation or unexpected query formulations.

基于规则和语法驱动的自然语言界面代表了空间 NLIDB 研究的一些最早的尝试。空间数据库的 NL 接口的早期研究很大程度上依赖于基于规则的推理和语法驱动的解析，其中用户查询的结构是通过语言规则明确定义的。这些方法的动机是大型训练数据集的可用性有限以及空间查询翻译中对可预测、可解释行为的需求。Rule-based and grammar-driven natural language interfaces represent some of the earliest attempts in spatial NLIDB research. Early research on NL interfaces for spatial databases relied heavily on rule-based reasoning and grammar-driven parsing, where the structure of a user query was explicitly defined through linguistic rules. These approaches were motivated by the limited availability of large training datasets and the need for predictable, interpretable behavior in spatial query translation.

最近的工作已经从单模型管道转向多代理架构，其中解释和执行空间或时空查询的任务在多个协作组件之间显式分解。在这些系统中，不同的代理被分配专门的角色，例如实体提取、规划、模式推理、查询生成、验证和解释。多代理方法不依赖单次查询生成，而是强调迭代推理、验证和纠正，旨在提高复杂查询的鲁棒性、可解释性和执行可靠性。在非时空域，NLIDB 采用多代理方法More recent work has moved beyond single-model pipelines toward multi-agent architectures, where the task of interpreting and executing spatial or spatiotemporal queries is explicitly decomposed across multiple cooperating components. In these systems, different agents are assigned specialized roles such as entity extraction, planning, schema reasoning, query generation, validation, and explanation. Rather than relying on a single pass of query generation, multi-agent approaches emphasize iterative reasoning, verification, and correction, aiming to improve robustness, interpretability, and execution reliability for complex queries. VI 讨论Discussion

空间、时空和时间序列数据库的自然语言接口的研究已经在几个不同的方向上发展，但仍然存在局限性。 本节重点介绍我们的观察结果以及我们在现有研究中注意到的局限性。这些局限性可以从数据集和方法论的角度来看，而且它们经常以影响这些系统在实际环境中使用的方式一起出现。The research in natural language interfaces for spatial, spatiotemporal, and time series databases has grown in several varying directions, but there are still limitations. This section focuses on our observations and the limitations we noticed across the existing studies. The limitations can be seen on both the datasets and the methodological perspectives, and they often appear together in ways that affect how well these systems can be used in real settings.

六一AVI-A 现有数据集的局限性Limitations of Existing Datasets

一个主要限制是该领域缺乏标准的、广泛使用的基准，这与广泛采用 WikiSQL 等数据集的一般 NL2SQL 研究不同One major limitation is the lack of a standard, widely used benchmark for this domain, unlike general NL2SQL research, which has widely adopted datasets such as WikiSQL[hwang2019comprehensive]和蜘蛛and Spider[yu2018spider]。 ST-NLIDB 中使用的大多数数据集都很小、是合成的或与特定数据库环境相关，因此很难在不同系统之间进行公平比较。最近的作品，例如 GeoSQL Bench 和 GeoSQL Eval. Most datasets used in ST-NLIDBs are small, synthetic, or tied to particular database environments, making it difficult to conduct fair comparisons across different systems. Recent works such as GeoSQL Bench and GeoSQL Eval[houanl2sql, hou2025geosql]提供了大量的综合任务并帮助操作员覆盖，但它们仍然无法捕获真实的地理不规则性或真实的用户查询。其他数据集，例如 SpatialQueryQAprovide a large set of synthetic tasks and help with operator coverage, but they still do not capture real geographic irregularities or authentic user queries. Other datasets, such as SpatialQueryQA[khosravi2025questions]和 GeoQueryJPand GeoQueryJP[yu2025monkuu]，对于测试特定情况很有用，但它们相对较小，并且缺乏代表真实世界条件的评估所需的空间和时间运算符的范围。, are useful for testing specific situations, but they are relatively small and lack the range of spatial and temporal operators needed for an evaluation representing real-world conditions.

BerlinMOD 等时空数据集Spatiotemporal datasets like BerlinMOD[duntgen2009berlinmod]以及 NLMO 中使用的 SECONDO 移动对象集合and the SECONDO moving-object collections used in NLMO[wang2020nlmo]和纳尔莫and NALMO[wang2021nalmo, wang2023nalmo]仅涵盖时空推理的一小部分，主要关注运动模式或时间窗口。时间序列数据集，例如 TRUCE、SUSHI 和基于 TACO 的对cover only a narrow slice of spatiotemporal reasoning, mostly focusing on movement patterns or time windows. Time-series datasets such as TRUCE, SUSHI, and the TACO-based pairs[ito2024clasp, dohi2025retrieving]支持对时间模式的自然语言监督，但它们不与任何空间几何相关联。因此，他们无法测试系统如何将时间与位置结合起来。在所有这些类别中，实际应用中常见的嘈杂地点描述和多步骤推理大多仍未经过测试。support natural-language supervision for temporal patterns, but they are not linked to any spatial geometry. Therefore, they cannot test how systems combine time with location. Across all these categories, noisy place descriptions and multi-step reasoning, which are common in real applications, remain mostly untested.

另一个限制是现有的 ST-NLIDB 数据集很少包含不明确或未指定的内容Another limitation is that existing ST-NLIDB datasets rarely include ambiguous or underspecified[wang2023know, wu2024need, zhang2020did]用户查询。现实世界的问题通常包含模糊的空间或时间表达，例如“附近”、“周围”或“最近”，其中预期的距离或时间范围没有明确定义。然而，ST-NLIDB 基准测试中仍然很大程度上缺少此类评估设置。最后，当前的 ST-NLIDB 基准测试不会在模式匿名化或混淆下测试系统user queries. Real-world questions often contain vague spatial or temporal expressions such as “near”, “around”, or “recent”, where the intended distance or time range is not clearly defined. However, such evaluation settings are still largely missing in ST-NLIDB benchmarks. Finally, current ST-NLIDB benchmarks do not test systems under schema anonymization or obfuscation[fieschi2025characterising, templ2022systematic]。为了处理敏感信息，ST-NLIDB 基准可以用中性标识符替换表名和列名。然而，ST-NLIDB 基准测试中仍然缺少这种类型的评估。. To handle sensitive information, ST-NLIDB benchmarks can replace table and column names with neutral identifiers. However, this type of evaluation is still missing in ST-NLIDB benchmarks.

总而言之，当前的 ST-NLIDB 缺乏通用的评估和基准测试系统。许多系统在它们所训练或设计的数据集上表现良好，但它们对新城市或更自然的用户查询的泛化仍然不确定。因此，缺乏涵盖空间和/或时间推理的大型且广泛采用的基准是当前工作的主要限制之一。Putting everything together, current ST-NLIDBs lack a common evaluation and benchmarking system. Many systems perform well on the datasets they were trained or designed around, but their generalization to new cities or more natural user queries remains uncertain. The absence of a large, widely adopted benchmark that covers spatial and/or temporal reasoning is therefore one of the central limitations of current work.

六-CVI-C 未来的研究方向Future Research Directions

我们的研究结果表明，ST-NLIDB 的研究取得了良好进展，但仍有重大改进的空间。受控实验和实际使用之间的主要差距意味着未来的系统将需要更多地关注鲁棒性，而不仅仅是小基准的准确性。Our findings suggest that research on ST-NLIDBs have made good progress, but there are still room for major improvements. The main gap between controlled experiments and real-world use means that future systems will need to focus more on robustness rather than only accuracy on a small benchmark.

A benchmark that combines spatial and/or temporal reasoning, noisy geographic descriptions, and realistic schema variation would better represent the real-world scenarios. Another useful direction is the design of benchmarks that include ambiguous or underspecified queries, as discussed in SectionVI-A。 还可以设计新的基准来评估模式匿名化或混淆下的系统。此类设置将有助于评估 ST-NLIDB 系统是否依赖架构名称或实际解释查询。 此外，地理空间表示学习技术和一般 NL2SQL 研究的进展，例如模式链接、文本和表格之间的交叉注意力或 SQL 日志的预训练，可以集成到 ST-NLIDB 系统中。. New benchmarks can also be designed to evaluate systems under schema anonymization or obfuscation. Such settings would help assess whether ST-NLIDB systems rely on schema names or actually interpret the query. In addition, geospatial representation learning techniques and advances from general NL2SQL research, such as schema linking, cross-attention between text and tables, or pretraining on SQL logs, may be integrated into ST-NLIDB systems.

总体而言，ST-NLIDB 的未来工作应侧重于构建不仅在简化设置下准确，而且更具通用性并能够处理现实世界地理数据的混乱且有时不可预测的性质的系统。Overall, the future work in ST-NLIDBs should focus on building systems that are not just accurate in simplified settings but are also more generalizable and able to deal with the messy and sometimes unpredictable nature of real-world geographic data.
