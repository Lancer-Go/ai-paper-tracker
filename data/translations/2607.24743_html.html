摘要Abstract

In this paper, we introduce ClinFusion, a vision-centric MLLM designed for holistic medical understanding that systematically addresses these limitations. We propose a compositional and cascaded vision encoder architecture featuring a Cascade Spatial-Aware Locality Fusion operator that unifies diverse 2D and native 3D medical image understanding within a fused encoder. 3 讨论Discussion

将 MLLM 融入临床实践具有重大前景，但这种转变受到了根本性障碍的阻碍： 处理异构医疗数据的挑战、学术评估与现实临床需求之间的差距以及模型知识的静态性质。 在这项工作中，我们引入了 ClinFusion，这是一种以视觉为中心的 MLLM，它提供了一个系统且整体的框架来解决这些关键限制。 我们的工作提出了一个系统框架，可以解决这些限制并展示强大的基准性能，提出了在临床环境中部署 MLLM 的可行途径。The integration of MLLMs into clinical practice holds significant promise, yet this transition has been impeded by fundamental obstacles: the challenge of processing heterogeneous medical data, the gap between academic evaluation and real-world clinical needs, and the static nature of model knowledge. In this work, we introduced ClinFusion, a vision-centric MLLM that provides a systematic and holistic framework to address these critical limitations. Our work presents a systematic framework that addresses these limitations and demonstrates strong benchmark performance, suggesting a viable path toward deploying MLLMs in clinical environments.

从本质上讲，ClinFusion 的进步建立在两个主要贡献之上。 在架构上，我们提出了一种以 CaSL Fusion 算子为特色的组合级联架构，超越了单片视觉编码器。 正如我们的消融研究所证实的那样，该设计通过级联和 2D 锚定的相互作用机制统一了不同的 2D 和原生 3D 数据处理，创建了丰富且协同的视觉表示，始终有利于细致入微的诊断任务。 为了弥合评估与实践之间的差距，我们引入了一个新的基于愿景的评估框架。 这包括强制遵循可靠指令的 MedIF-Bench，以及用于临床一致和事实驱动的报告生成评估的新颖的基于 RoI 的报告生成方法。 为了进一步增强实际部署，我们开发了一种代理工具使用扩展，为 ClinFusion 配备了检索增强生成和使用外部专家模型作为工具的能力，在纯文本和多模式临床场景中展示了一致的改进。At its core, ClinFusion’s advancements are built on two principal contributions. Architecturally, we moved beyond monolithic vision encoders by proposing a compositional and cascaded architecture featuring the CaSL Fusion operator. This design unifies diverse 2D and native 3D data processing through a cascaded and 2D-anchored interplay mechanism, creating a rich and synergistic visual representation that consistently benefits nuanced diagnostic tasks, as confirmed by our ablation studies. To bridge the evaluation-practice gap, we introduced a new vision-grounded evaluation framework. This includes the MedIF-Bench to enforce reliable instruction-following, and a novel RoI-grounded report generation methodology for clinically aligned and factualness-driven report generation evaluation. To further enhance practical deployment, we developed an agentic tool use extension that equips ClinFusion with retrieval-augmented generation and the ability to use external specialist models as tools, demonstrating consistent improvements in both text-only and multimodal clinical scenarios.

Independently, the strong correlation between our RoI-Grounded evaluation and expert radiologist judgment confirms that vision-grounded, factualness-driven metrics better reflect real clinical needs than conventional text-matching approaches. Using a large corpus of 2D medical image-text pairs, we exclusively train the projection layers, the CaSL Fusion modules and all the encoders. The LLM and all base vision encoders remain frozen, allowing for an efficient and targeted alignment of the perception components, specifically in the medical domain.

    第 2 阶段：深度视觉语言对齐（2D）Stage 2: Deep vision-language alignment (2D)。 一旦视觉特征得到协调，此阶段的目标是在融合的 2D 视觉表示和整个法学硕士之间建立深度语义对齐。 我们解冻 LLM 并继续对相同的 2D 图像文本对齐数据进行训练。 此步骤对完整模型进行微调，以理解和描述丰富的多编码器视觉信息。. Once the visual features are harmonized, this stage aims to establish a deep semantic alignment between the fused 2D visual representation and the entire LLM. We unfreeze the LLM and continue training on the same 2D image-text alignment data. This step fine-tunes the full model to understand and describe the rich, multi-encoder visual information.

    第 3 阶段：2D 指令调整Stage 3: 2D instruction tuning。 该模型现在能够进行二维感知，我们教它遵循复杂的指令并执行不同的医疗任务。 我们在高质量、多任务 2D 视觉指令数据集上微调整个模型。 此阶段为模型注入了针对 2D 图像的高级推理和任务执行功能。. With the model now capable of grounded 2D perception, we teach it to follow complex instructions and perform diverse medical tasks. We fine-tune the entire model on a high-quality, multi-task 2D visual instruction dataset. This stage imbues the model with advanced reasoning and task-execution capabilities for 2D imagery.

    第 4 阶段：快速 3D 编码器对齐Stage 4: Rapid 3D encoder alignment。 此阶段引入了 3D 模式。 利用我们的 2D 锚定策略，目标是有效地将新的 3D 编码器与模型的其余部分对齐，并保留 2D 图像的任务执行能力。 使用 3D 体积文本对齐数据，我们冻结 LLM 和所有 2D 感知组件（编码器和融合模块），并仅训练 3D 编码器和 2D 锚定深度感知融合模块。 这将训练隔离到新的 3D 组件，从而实现快速稳定的收敛。. This stage introduces the 3D modality. Leveraging our 2D-anchored strategy, the goal is to efficiently align the new 3D encoder with the rest of the model and to retain the task-execution capabilities for 2D imagery. Using 3D volume-text alignment data, we freeze the LLM and all 2D perception components (encoders and fusion modules) and train only the 3D encoder and the 2D-anchored depth-aware fusion modules. This isolates the training to the new 3D components, enabling rapid and stable convergence.

    第 5 阶段：整体 2D/3D 指令调整Stage 5: Holistic 2D/3D instruction tuning。 在最后阶段，我们通过在结合 2D 和 3D 医学图像的综合视觉指令调整数据集上微调所有组件来释放模型的全部潜力。 此步骤统一了模型的功能，使其能够处理需要深入了解 2D 和 3D 医疗数据的复杂指令。. In the final stage, we unlock the model’s full potential by fine-tuning all components on a comprehensive visual instruction tuning dataset combining 2D and 3D medical imagery. This step unifies the model’s capabilities, enabling it to handle complex instructions that require a deep understanding of both 2D and 3D medical data.

这种分阶段的培训确保每项新功能都建立在稳定的基础上，最终形成强大而强大的多模式医疗模型。This staged training ensures that each new capability is built upon a stable foundation, culminating in a robust and powerful multimodal medical model.

S2.1 知识工具：混合检索和跨源聚合Knowledge Tool: Hybrid Retrieval and Cross-Source Aggregation

具有概念标准化的混合检索Hybrid retrieval with concept normalization。 医学查询和文档普遍存在歧义（同义词、缩写、品牌通用药物名称、变体拼写）。 为了减少检索阶段的这种歧义，我们在查询之前使用 UMLS 执行概念规范化：查询中提到的医学实体被映射到标准化的医学概念。 然后通过两个互补的渠道进行检索。 首先，矢量搜索引擎从大规模语料库中检索语义匹配的证据段落，提高语言变化下的召回率。 其次，基于 KG 的召回利用了两个开源医学知识图：UMLS. Medical queries and documents contain pervasive ambiguity (synonyms, abbreviations, brand–generic drug names, variant spellings). To reduce such ambiguity at the retrieval stage, we perform concept normalization using UMLS before querying: medical entities mentioned in the query are mapped to standardized medical concepts. Retrieval then proceeds through two complementary channels. First, a vector search engine retrieves semantically matched evidence passages from large-scale corpora, improving recall under linguistic variation. Second, KG-based recall leverages two open-source medical knowledge graphs: UMLS[73]，它提供标准化的医学概念标识符，以实现同义词和缩写之间的稳健标准化，以及 PrimeKG, which provides standardized medical concept identifiers for robust normalization across synonyms and abbreviations, and PrimeKG[74]，它提供了丰富的生物医学关系（, which offers rich biomedical relations (例如e.g.、药物-疾病、症状-疾病、禁忌症），支持基于链接的扩展到临床相关的上游/下游概念。 这种结构化回忆可以实现从概念到证据的路由，并减少因表面形式不匹配而导致的遗漏。 这两个通道通过规范化概念和语义相似性进行整合，产生基于显式医疗实体和关系的候选证据集。, drug–disease, symptom–disease, contraindication) that support link-based expansion to clinically relevant upstream/downstream concepts. This structured recall enables concept-to-evidence routing and reduces misses caused by surface-form mismatch. The two channels are integrated by normalized concepts and semantic similarity, producing a candidate evidence set grounded in explicit medical entities and relations.

跨源证据聚合Cross-source evidence aggregation。 医学 QA 通常受益于结合互补的证据视图：矢量检索通常返回高度查询对齐的段落，而基于知识图谱的召回提供更丰富的锚定到规范化实体的上游/下游上下文。 由于这两个渠道在粒度和表面形式上有所不同，我们通过使用规范化医疗实体的语义相似性和重叠对跨两个渠道检索到的块进行聚类来聚合证据，对陈述相同临床主张或提供来自不同来源的相互支持的内容进行分组。 然后，ClinFusion 总结每个聚类，以保留共享的临床结论以及关键限定符（. Medical QA often benefits from combining complementary evidence views: vector retrieval typically returns highly query-aligned passages, while KG-based recall provides richer upstream/downstream context anchored to normalized entities. Since these two channels differ in granularity and surface form, we aggregate evidence by clustering retrieved chunks across both channels using semantic similarity and overlap of normalized medical entities, grouping content that states the same clinical claim or offers mutual support from different sources. ClinFusion then summarizes each cluster to preserve the shared clinical conclusion together with key qualifiers (例如e.g.、适应症、禁忌症、剂量限制）并在支持部分附上引文。 这种先聚类后总结的聚合联合利用了基于嵌入的检索的精度和 KG 召回的关系广度，为忠实、可追踪的临床答案生成紧凑的、基于证据的上下文。, indication, contraindication, dose constraints) and attaches citations to the supporting chunks. This cluster-then-summarize aggregation jointly leverages the precision of embedding-based retrieval and the relational breadth of KG recall, producing a compact, evidence-grounded context for faithful, traceable clinical answers.
