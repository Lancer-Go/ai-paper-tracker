I 简介Introduction

自动驾驶汽车预计将在开放且不断发展的环境中运行，其中道路几何形状、交通交互和代理行为的罕见组合不可避免地会产生长尾情况Autonomous vehicles are expected to operate in open and continually evolving environments, where rare combinations of road geometry, traffic interactions, and agent behaviors inevitably give rise to long-tail situations[17, 10]。面向规划和端到端自动驾驶的最新进展显着提高了闭环性能. Recent advances in planning-oriented and end-to-end autonomous driving have substantially improved closed-loop performance[14, 8, 4]通过日益强大的政策架构through increasingly powerful policy architectures[6, 38, 22], 生成模型, generative models[26, 55, 42], 和大型基础模型, and large-scale foundation models[59, 60, 50]。 尽管取得了这些进步，错误仍然不可避免。即使能力很强的驾驶策略也可能会遇到学习行为不足的情况，从而导致决策不理想或闭环失败. Despite these advances, mistakes remain inevitable. Even highly capable driving policies may encounter situations in which their learned behaviors are insufficient, leading to suboptimal decisions or closed-loop failures[33, 12]。这些失败可能源于罕见的交互、不完善的概括或闭环执行过程中策略引起的偏差的累积。这就提出了一个基本问题：如果错误是不可避免的，部署的驾驶策略能否从中吸取教训并随着时间的推移不断改进自己？. These failures may stem from rare interactions, imperfect generalization, or the accumulation of policy-induced deviations over closed-loop execution. This raises a fundamental question: if mistakes are inevitable, can a deployed driving policy learn from them and continually improve itself over time?

大多数现有的基于学习的规划政策通过利用预先收集的专家数据中更丰富的信息来应对这一挑战。基于模仿的规划器改进了场景表示、模型架构、交互推理和训练策略，以便从专家演示中获得更强的泛化能力Most existing learning-based planning policies address this challenge by exploiting richer information from pre-collected expert data. Imitation-based planners improve scene representations, model architectures, interaction reasoning, and training strategies to obtain stronger generalization from expert demonstrations[15, 7, 6, 5, 22]。生成规划器使用基于扩散或流的公式进一步对多模态轨迹分布进行建模. Generative planners further model multimodal trajectory distributions using diffusion- or flow-based formulations[55, 39, 53]。视觉-语言-动作和语言增强驾驶模型引入高级推理和更广泛的语义先验，以提高复杂场景的适应性. Vision-language-action and language-augmented driving models introduce high-level reasoning and broader semantic priors to improve adaptability in complex scenarios[59, 60, 50, 57]。 这些方法通过日益强大的政策模型和预先收集的专家数据更强的概括性，显着推进了基于学习的规划。然而，它们主要侧重于更好地利用现有知识，提供有限的机制来整合政策失败和困难情况所揭示的新知识。. These approaches have significantly advanced learning-based planning through increasingly powerful policy models and stronger generalization from pre-collected expert data. However, they primarily focus on better utilizing existing knowledge, offering limited mechanisms to incorporate new knowledge revealed by policy failures and difficult scenarios.

一个有前途的方向是不断从政策互动中获取新知识，并利用由此产生的反馈来进一步改进政策行为。 强化学习和基于偏好的优化可以改善专家演示之外的闭环行为，但奖励设计、稀疏的安全反馈和探索风险仍然是安全关键型驾驶的主要挑战A promising direction is to continuously acquire new knowledge from policy interactions and exploit the resulting feedback to further improve policy behavior. Reinforcement learning and preference-based optimization can improve closed-loop behavior beyond expert demonstration, but reward design, sparse safety feedback, and exploration risk remain major challenges for safety-critical driving[46, 51, 35, 40, 11]。 DAgger等数据集聚合方法. Dataset aggregation methods such as DAgger[33]通过推出当前的学习者并询问访问过的州的专家来改进政策。然而，在大规模驾驶场景中，获得对任意政策诱导状态的专家监督通常成本高昂。最近的闭环监督微调方法，例如 CAT-Kimprove policies by rolling out the current learner and querying an expert on visited states. However, obtaining expert supervision for arbitrary policy-induced states is often prohibitively expensive in large-scale driving scenarios. Recent closed-loop supervised fine-tuning methods, such as CAT-K[54]和道路and RoaD[12]，通过使用记录的地面实况轨迹来指导闭环数据生成，然后根据恢复的推出体验微调驾驶策略来减轻这种负担。 然而，从闭环推广中恢复的知识在质量和信息量上往往是参差不齐的。由于政策在推出时偏离专家状态，一些恢复的知识提供了有价值的纠正信息，而其他知识可能会引入有偏见或误导性的更新。因此，从闭环部署中有效提取有用的纠正知识仍然是一个开放的挑战。, alleviate this burden by using logged ground-truth trajectories to guide closed-loop data generation and then fine-tuning driving policies on the recovered rollout experiences. Nevertheless, the knowledge recovered from closed-loop rollout is often heterogeneous in quality and informativeness. As policies deviate from expert states in rollout, some recovered knowledge provide valuable corrective information, whereas others may introduce biased or misleading updates. Consequently, effectively extracting useful corrective knowledge from closed-loop rollout remains an open challenge.

Ⅱ-CII-C 终身政策学习Lifelong Policy Learning

终身学习，通常称为持续学习，研究模型如何从顺序或增量数据中获取知识，同时减少灾难性遗忘Lifelong learning, often referred to as continual learning, studies how a model can acquire knowledge from sequential or incremental data while mitigating catastrophic forgetting[41, 43]。代表性策略包括基于正则化的方法. Representative strategies include regularization-based methods[19], 基于蒸馏的方法, distillation-based methods[23], 基于重放的方法, replay-based methods[32, 28, 1]，以及架构或参数隔离方法, and architecture- or parameter-isolation methods[49, 29]。通过大型预训练模型，最近的研究进一步探索了即时高效和参数高效的持续学习. With large pretrained models, recent studies further explore prompt- and parameter-efficient continual learning[44, 45, 25, 58]。这些方法主要解决如何在适应给定任务或数据分布的新序列的同时保留过去的知识。策略学习带来了额外的困难：数据分布是由策略本身引起的，每次策略更新都可能改变未来推出时遇到的状态。此外，政策诱导的国家通常不会采取明确的地面实况行动，因此必须首先从闭环经验中构建有用的监督。因此，终身的政策学习不仅需要非遗忘优化，还需要一种从政策的演化行为中获取可靠的纠正知识的机制。. These methods mainly address how to preserve past knowledge while adapting to a new sequence of given tasks or data distributions. Policy learning poses an additional difficulty: the data distribution is induced by the policy itself, and each policy update can change the states that will be encountered in future rollouts. Moreover, policy-induced states usually do not come with explicit ground-truth actions, so useful supervision must first be constructed from closed-loop experience. Therefore, lifelong policy learning requires not only non-forgetting optimization, but also a mechanism for acquiring reliable corrective knowledge from the evolving behavior of the policy.

自动驾驶中也探索了持续学习，包括感知、预测、决策模块Continual learning has also been explored in autonomous driving, including perception, prediction, and decision-making modules[24]。面向预测的研究调查驾驶场景的案例级遗忘、动态扩展和无任务识别. These works extend continual learning to driving-specific data streams, but still largely follow the conventional setting where supervision from incremental data or task changes are given. Lifelong policy learning differs from conventional continual learning because the policy is updated through data that is partly induced by its own closed-loop behavior: its decisions affect future state visitation, and the resulting states may not come with explicit supervision. Existing studies have explored continual policy learning from different angles, including dynamic confidence-aware reinforcement learning for autonomous driving[2]、机器人操纵中的终身技能保存和重组, lifelong skill preservation and recombination in robot manipulation[31]，路径跟踪控制的终身策略学习, life-long policy learning for path tracking control[13]，以及人工引导的持续学习以实现个性化驾驶决策, and human-guided continual learning for personalized driving decisions[48]。 这些工作表明，随着时间的推移，可以通过互动、积累的知识或外部反馈来改进政策。然而，它们主要关注政策更新或适应，而错误驱动的纠正性知识获取的探索仍然较少。在闭环规划中，故障不会直接揭示负责任的决策、可恢复性或纠正目标。因此，规划的终身政策学习不仅需要保留以前的知识，还需要将与错误相关的经验转化为可靠的监督，以避免重复失败。. These works show that policies can be improved over time through interaction, accumulated knowledge, or external feedback. However, they mainly focus on policy update or adaptation, while mistake-driven acquisition of corrective knowledge remains less explored. In closed-loop planning, failures do not directly reveal responsible decisions, recoverability, or corrective targets. Therefore, lifelong policy learning for planning requires not only preserving previous knowledge, but also converting mistake-related experience into reliable supervision for avoiding repeated failures.

Each ROCL round follows a Rollout–Retrieval–Lifelong Policy Learning cycle. The current policy is first executed in a closed-loop simulator or world model to induce states under its own decisions and expose failure, risk, or conflict evidence. The retrieval stage then searches the rollout experience for recoverable mistake-related states and converts them into corrective targets. Finally, lifelong policy learning updates the planner with the newly retrieved knowledge while replaying previous corrections. After the update, the improved policy induces a new closed-loop state distribution, which starts the next ROCL round.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们提出了RWe presented R2LPL，自动驾驶的推出-检索终身策略学习范例。 R 不是仅将闭环故障视为评估结果LPL, a rollout-retrieval lifelong policy learning paradigm for autonomous driving. Instead of treating closed-loop failures only as evaluation outcomes, R2LPL 使用策略诱导的推出来识别与错误相关的状态，从结构化的行动空间中检索可恢复的纠正目标，并通过终身策略学习合并检索到的知识。核心观点是，对于持续改进政策最有用的知识不是失败本身，而是帮助政策避免重蹈覆辙的纠正知识。 nuPlan 上的实验表明 RLPL uses policy-induced rollouts to identify mistake-related states, retrieve recoverable corrective targets from a structured action space, and incorporate the retrieved knowledge through lifelong policy learning. The central insight is that the most useful knowledge for continual policy improvement is not the failure itself, but the corrective knowledge that helps the policy avoid repeating the same mistakes. Experiments on nuPlan show that R2LPL 可以显着改进具有中等性能的基于锚的规划器，并将其提升到跨多个闭环基准的基于学习的规划器中最先进的性能。收益在硬尾场景和长尾场景中最为明显，而更新后的策略在更广泛的验证和随机测试分割上也保持了强劲的性能。此外，尽管纠正知识是从非反应性推出中获得的，但改进的策略可推广到反应性模拟，这表明检索到的目标编码可重用的决策偏好，而不是过度拟合非反应性代理模式。这些结果表明，可以通过 R 的紧凑 ROCL 轮次来实现大规模闭环策略改进LPL can substantially improve an anchor-based planner with moderate performance and elevate it to state-of-the-art performance among learning-based planners across multiple closed-loop benchmarks. The gains are most pronounced on hard and long-tailed scenarios, while the updated policy also maintains strong performance on broader validation and random test splits. Moreover, although the corrective knowledge is acquired from non-reactive rollouts, the improved policy generalizes to reactive simulation, suggesting that the retrieved targets encode reusable decision preferences rather than overfitting to non-reactive agent patterns. These results demonstrate that large-scale closed-loop policy improvement can be achieved with compact ROCL rounds through R2LPL，而不是仅仅依赖于更大的专家日志模仿、奖励驱动的探索或部署时规则细化。LPL, without relying solely on larger expert-log imitation, reward-driven exploration, or deployment-time rule refinement.

R的当前实例化The current instantiation of R2LPL 专注于具有明确或可采样操作候选的决策策略，其中可通过候选评估来实现可恢复性目标检索。因此，其有效性取决于行动空间的覆盖范围以及用于选择纠正目标的评估器的一致性。将这一想法扩展到连续生成规划器可能需要在潜在轨迹空间中进行检索或投影，以及用于过滤噪声目标的更强大的机制。另一个重要方向是用类人代理学习的物理世界模型来取代或补充基于模拟的推出，使政策能够从更大、更多样化的闭环交互流中获取正确的知识。LPL focuses on decision policies with explicit or sampleable action candidates, where recoverability-aware target retrieval can be implemented through candidate evaluation. Its effectiveness therefore depends on the coverage of the action space and the alignment of the evaluator used to select corrective targets. Extending this idea to continuous generative planners may require retrieval or projection in latent trajectory space, together with stronger mechanisms for filtering noisy targets. Another important direction is to replace or complement simulation-based rollouts with learned physical world models with human-like agents, enabling policies to acquire corrective knowledge from a much larger and more diverse stream of closed-loop interactions.
