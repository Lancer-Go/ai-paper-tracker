摘要Abstract

深度学习模型在各个领域都取得了令人印象深刻的表现，但仍然容易受到对抗性输入的影响，特别是在 NLP 领域，此类攻击可能会在现实世界中产生重大后果。对抗性攻击通常涉及小的、语义相似的标记替换来欺骗 NLP 模型，而最近的方法通过针对特定的易受攻击的单词（通常是利用对模型内部结构的某种级别的访问）变得更加精确。本文提出Deep learning models have achieved impressive performance across various fields but remain vulnerable to adversarial inputs, particularly in NLP, where such attacks can have significant real-world consequences. Adversarial attacks often involve small, semantically similar token replacements to fool NLP models, and recent methods have become more precise by targeting specific vulnerable words, often by exploiting some level of access to the model’s internal structure. This paper proposesGAversary，一种混合遗传算法（GA），用于对自然语言模型产生对抗性攻击。遗传算法能够将目标模型视为黑匣子，只需要模型输出的logit值来指导搜索。, a hybrid Genetic Algorithm (GA) to generate adversarial attacks on natural language models. The GA is able to treat the target model as a black box, requiring only the logit value output by the model to guide the search.GAversary与之前针对该问题提出的 GA 不同，它通过使用 GloVe 嵌入来提出单词替换（变异算子），以提高对抗性示例的语义相似性。differs from GAs previously proposed for this problem, by using GloVe embeddings to propose word replacements (the mutation operator) to improve the semantic similarity of the adversarial examples.GAversary应用于多个基准数据集和众所周知的目标模型。is applied to several benchmark data sets and well-known target models.GAversary与 BAE 和 A2T 攻击相比，能够大幅降低目标模型在测试数据上的准确性（在最好的情况下，将 76.8% 的准确性降低到 5.8%，而 BAE 的准确性为 27.6%）。权衡是is able to substantially reduce the target model’s accuracy on test data compared to the BAE and A2T attacks compared against (in the best case, reducing a 76.8% accuracy to 5.8%, compared to BAE’s 27.6%). The trade-off is thatGAversary扰动的单词数量略低于其他两种方法的两倍，与原始文本的语义相似度略低，运行时间增加了约 5%。perturbs just under twice as many words as the other two methods, with a slightly lower semantic similarity to the original text, with around a 5% increase in run-time.

1 简介Introduction

深度神经网络（DNN）等深度学习模型已应用于语音识别、自然语言处理（NLP）和计算机视觉等广泛领域。在许多领域，他们的表现即使不比人类专家更好，也同样出色Deep learning models such as deep neural networks (DNNs) have been applied to a vast range of fields including speech recognition, natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision. In many areas they have performed as well if not better than human experts[12]。然而，他们很容易受到对抗性投入的影响；人类可能无法察觉但可能导致 DNN 对输出进行错误分类的扰动. However, they are vulnerable to adversarial inputs; perturbations that may be imperceptible to humans but can cause DNNs to misclassify the output[9].

通过 NLP 模型进行自动文本分析越来越多地用于自动生成摘要，这些摘要可驱动新闻推荐网站等，对声誉、可靠性和品牌塑造具有直接影响Automated text analysis through NLP models is increasingly used to auto-generate summaries which drive, for example, news recommender websites, with direct implications for reputation, reliability, and branding[36]。例如，NLP 模型可用于根据这些评论中的普遍情绪告知投资者购买/出售决策. For example, NLP models can be used to inform investors on buy/sell decisions based on the prevailing sentiment within these reviews[3, 28]。因此，对 NLP 模型的对抗性攻击可能会产生重大影响，例如引发糟糕的投资决策以及相应的当地经济破坏。. Thus, adversarial attacks on NLP models could have major impact, such as triggering poor investment decisions and the corresponding disruption of local economies.

对抗系统的总体目标是以对原始文本进行更改的形式生成攻击，使原始文本被错误分类，对原始文本进行最少数量的修改，并在这些修改后保持语义相似性。 文献中已经论证了多种方法。一般来说，这些依赖于替换示例中的标记The overall goal for an adversarial system is to generate an attack, in the form of changes to an original text such that the original text is misclassified, having the least number of modifications to the original text, and maintaining semantic similarity after those modifications. A variety of approaches have been demonstrated in the literature. Generally these rely on replacing tokens in the examples[37]。 通常，此类方法依赖于生成一些同义词标记替换（通常使用某种形式的语义相似性），并选择对目标模型的分类概率影响最大的一个替换. Typically such approaches rely on generating a few synonym token replacements (usually using some form of semantic similarity), and choosing the one replacement that has the biggest influence on the classification probability for the targeted model[24, 10]。 最近，这些方法变得更有针对性，识别最脆弱的单词，并使用上下文来选择合适的替换词. Recently these approaches have become more targeted, identifying the most vulnerable words, and using context to select suitable replacements[25, 22]。 然而，这些方法中的每一种所遵循的搜索过程通常相当于局部搜索或某些其他单点搜索的变体。 遗传算法 (GA) 在具有较大搜索空间的组合优化问题上表现良好. However, the search process each of these methods follows generally amounts to a variation of local or some other single-point search. Genetic Algorithms (GAs) perform well on combinatorial optimization problems where there is a large search space[2, 4]。即使只有同义词组合（忽略句子结构），自然语言也有如此广阔的探索空间，但应用遗传算法生成对抗性例子的例子却很少。. Even with only the combination of synonyms (ignoring sentence structure), Natural Language has such a vast space to explore, yet there are few examples of the application of GAs to generating adversarial examples.

As GAs follow a search process that simply seeks inferences from the target model and can be terminated at any arbitrary point, they also have the potential to generate attacks more quickly than GANs. 3 方法论Methodology

目前的工作旨在研究针对 NLP 模型的对抗性示例的创建。与图像模型的类似工作相比，该研究领域相对较新，因此该项目的目标是将几个类似领域已经完成的工作结合起来The present work seeks to investigate the creation of adversarial examples against an NLP model. This research area, compared to similar work with image models, is relatively new and so this project will aim to combine work already done in several similar areas[13, 32, 10, 26, 49]。 总的来说，生成对抗性示例有两个目标：（1）示例应该被错误分类； (2)示例应尽可能与原文相似。. Overall there are two aims when generating the adversarial examples: (1) the examples should be incorrectly classified; and (2) the examples should be as similar to the original text as possible.

该提案是使用 GA 来演化一组对抗性示例。 GA 是一种基于搜索的优化框架，具有多个组件，可以限制这些组件来指导搜索以满足上述目标。 GA 基于以下概念找到解决方案The proposal is to use a GA to evolve a population of adversarial examples. GAs are a search-based framework for optimisation and have several components that can be constrained to guide the search to meet the aims noted above. 3.3 健身与选择Fitness and Selection

适应度测量的目标是第一个目标：错误分类，并且遵循默认的 TextAttack Untargeted Classification 实现。 文本只有两种可能的分类：否定和肯定。 NLP 模型为每个类别分配一个分数，分数较高的类别作为预测。如果真实类别仍然具有更高的分数，则错误分类的接近程度就是这两个分数之间的差异。该算法力求最小化这种差异，直到正确的类别不再具有更高的分数。The fitness measure targets the first aim: incorrect classification, and is follows the default TextAttack Untargeted Classification implementation. There are only two possible classifications for the text: negative, and positive. The NLP model assigns a score to each class, with the class having the higher score being the prediction. The proximity to misclassification is simply the difference between these two scores, if the true class still has the higher score. The algorithm seeks to minimise this difference until the correct class no longer has the higher score.

健身比例选择A fitness proportionate selection[29]操作使用这种适应度的概念来为剩余的 GA 算子选择父母。通过将其适应度除以群体中所有解决方案的总适应度，为每个解决方案分配一个选择概率。然后根据该概率选择解决方案，以便大致按照其适应度比例选择解决方案。使用 TextAttack 的适应度比例选择实现，它使用 softmax 函数对父母进行采样时的概率进行归一化。operation uses this concept of fitness to choose parents for the remaining GA operators. Each solution is allocated a probability of selection, by taking its fitness divided by the total fitness of all solutions in the population. Solutions are then selected according to this probability so that they are chosen roughly in proportion with their fitness. TextAttack’s implementation of fitness proportionate selection is used, which uses a softmax function to normalise the probability when sampling parents.

3.6 停止标准Stopping Criteria

该算法有两种可能的退出条件。第一个是在查询情感模型后发现一个群体成员，该成员等于标签，因此是一个对抗性示例。 第二个条件是算法已经运行了一定数量的代数。 最大代数是在实验后确定的。 最初，代数设置为 100。经过多次试验，发现 20 代后生成的对抗性示例没有任何改进。There are two possible exit conditions for the algorithm. The first is the discovery of a population member that, after querying the sentiment model, is equal to the label and so is an adversarial example. The second condition is that the algorithm has run for a set number of generations. The maximum number of generations was determined after experimentation. Initially, the number of generations was set at 100. After multiple trials it was noted that after 20 generations there was no improvement in the adversarial examples generated.

一般来说，所有方法的对抗性示例都替换了专有名词和缩写，例如ag-news“T N”被替换为随机的其他首字母缩写，“墨尔本”被替换为其他澳大利亚城市。这是仅使用嵌入空间中的相似性可能存在的缺陷。显然，诸如此类的专有名词在语料库中以类似的方式使用，因此将具有类似的嵌入，但不可互换。命名实体识别In general, the adversarial examples from all methods replaced proper nouns and abbrevations, e.g. ag-news “T N” being replaced with random other initials and “Melbourne” being replaced with other Australian cities. This is a possible flaw in only using similarity in the embedding space. Clearly proper nouns such as these are used in similar ways across a corpus so will have similar embeddings, but are not interchangeable. 5 结论Conclusion

本文提出了This paper has proposedGAversary，一种生成对抗性文本的混合方法，寻求先前工作的最佳成果：一种基于搜索的方法，使用遗传算法，只需要模型的 logit 值输出，由 GloVe 引导以实现语义相似性。在实践中, a hybrid approach to generating adversarial text seeking the best of previous work: a search based approach using a genetic algorithm that only needs the logit value output from the model, guided by GloVe for semantic similarity. In practiceGAversary准确率的降低甚至比 BAE 和 A2T 方法还要多，但代价是需要对目标模型进行 2 到 8 倍的查询，并且平均扰动大约两倍的单词。然而，生成的示例显示，与其他方法生成的示例相比，语义相似性仅略有下降，尽管模型查询数量较多，但运行时间仅增加了 5% 左右。reduces accuracy even more than the BAE and A2T approaches , but at the cost of needing 2x-8x as many queries of the target model and perturbing around twice as many words on average. However, the resulting examples show only a slight reduction in semantic similarity over those generated by the other approaches, and only around 5% increase in run time despite the greater number of model queries.

未来有几条潜在的工作方向。该算法可以进一步借鉴 BERT，利用被扰动的单词的上下文来进一步提高替换文本的语义相似度。在每次替换期间可以应用简单的本地搜索，根据给定单词上下文的 BERT 获取前 n 个最有可能的单词，在决定保留哪个之前测试每个替换的结果文本（这称为模因算法）There are several lines of potential future work. The algorithm could draw further on BERT to use the context of words being perturbed to further improve the semantic similarity of the replacement text. A simple local search could be applied during each replacement, taking the top-n most likely words according to BERT given the word’s context, testing the resulting text for each replacement before deciding which one to keep (this is known as a memetic algorithm[31]）。修改 GA 的目标函数以包含对替换的惩罚也可以提高对抗性示例与原始文本的相似性。 尝试确定专有名词和缩写在何处出现以防止其被替换将是很有趣的。显然，还可以借鉴最近的大型语言模型，即 LLaMA). Amending the objective function for the GA to include a penalty for replacements could also improve the similarity of the adversarial examples to the original text. It would be interesting to try to identify where proper nouns and abbreviations appear to prevent their replacement. Clearly there is also scope for drawing from more recent large language models i.e. LLaMA[15], 混合, Mixtral[19]），但计算成本可能会增加。), but at a potential increase in computational cost.
