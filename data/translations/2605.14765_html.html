Persian MusicGen：波斯音乐的大规模数据集和文化意识生成模型Persian MusicGen: A Large-Scale Dataset and Culturally-Aware Generative Model for Persian Music

Mohammad Hossein Sameti1, Diba Hadi Esfangereh1, Sepehr Harfi Moridani1, Leili Javidpour2,
Mahdieh Soleymani Baghshah1
1Sharif University of Technology, 2Independent Researcher,
Correspondence: soleymani@sharif.edu
摘要Abstract

波斯音乐以其独特的音调、调式系统（Dastgah）和节奏结构，给主要以西方音乐训练的音乐生成模型提出了重大挑战。我们通过策划第一个大规模波斯歌曲数据集来解决这一差距，该数据集包含超过 900 小时的高质量音频样本，涵盖不同的子流派，包括流行、传统和现代风格。该数据集捕捉了波斯音乐丰富的旋律和文化多样性，并作为微调 MusicGen（一种最先进的生成音乐模型）的基础。我们使 MusicGen 适应该领域，并利用主观和客观指标评估其性能。为了评估生成的音乐和预期风格标签之间的语义一致性，我们报告了生成的输出中准确反映的相关标签的比例。我们的结果表明，经过微调的模型产生的作品更符合波斯风格惯例。这项工作为生成音乐研究引入了一种新资源，并说明了音乐生成模型对代表性不足的文化和语言环境的适应性。Persian music, with its unique tonalities, modal systems (Dastgah), and rhythmic structures, presents significant challenges for music generation models trained primarily on Western music. We address this gap by curating the first large-scale dataset of Persian songs, comprising over 900 hours high-quality audio samples across diverse sub-genres, including pop, traditional, and contemporary styles. This dataset captures the rich melodic and cultural diversity of Persian music and serves as the foundation for fine-tuning MusicGen, a state-of-the-art generative music model. We adapt MusicGen to this domain and evaluate its performance by utilizing subjective and objective metrics. To assess the semantic alignment between generated music and intended style tags, we report the proportion of relevant tags accurately reflected in the generated outputs. Our results demonstrate that the fine-tuned model produces compositions that more align with Persian stylistic conventions. This work introduces a new resource for generative music research and illustrates the adaptability of music generation models to underrepresented cultural and linguistic contexts.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 音乐生成数据集Music Generation Datasets

音乐生成的最新进展是由大规模符号和音频数据集的可用性推动的，这些数据集主要集中在西方音乐传统。例如，MAESTRO 是一个广泛使用的古典钢琴演奏音频和 MIDI 录音对齐数据集，可以对精确的音符时序和动态进行监督训练Recent advances in music generation have been driven by the availability of large-scale symbolic and audio datasets, primarily focused on Western music traditions. For instance, MAESTRO is a widely used dataset of aligned audio and MIDI recordings of classical piano performances, enabling supervised training on precise note timings and dynamicsHawthorne et al. (2019)。 Lakh MIDI 数据集是最大的符号音乐语料库之一，包含超过 170,000 个源自西方流行音乐的 MIDI 文件，涵盖摇滚、爵士和流行等流派. The Lakh MIDI Dataset, one of the largest symbolic music corpora, comprises over 170,000 MIDI files derived from Western popular music, spanning genres like rock, jazz, and popRaffel (2016)。 Slakh2100 对此进行了扩展，通过使用高质量乐器样本从数十万个 MIDI 文件中合成多轨音频再现，从而促进了具有多乐器结构的音频域音乐生成的研究. Slakh2100 expands on this by synthesizing multitrack audio renditions from Lakh MIDI files using high-quality instrument samples, thereby facilitating research in audio-domain music generation with multi-instrumental structureManilow et al. (2019).

MusicNet 和 Free Music Archive (FMA) 等其他数据集分别为古典音乐和知识共享许可音乐提供对齐的音频和元数据，进一步拓宽了深度生成模型的训练资源Other datasets such as MusicNet and the Free Music Archive (FMA) provide aligned audio and metadata for classical and Creative Commons-licensed music, respectively, further broadening the training resources for deep generative modelsThickstun et al. (2016); Defferrard et al. (2017)。同样，MTG-Jamendo 数据集提供了超过 55,000 首完整的、高质量的曲目，具有丰富的流派、乐器和情绪标签，成为音频领域任务的关键公共基准. Similarly, the MTG-Jamendo dataset provides over 55,000 full-length, high-quality tracks with rich genre, instrument, and mood tags, becoming a key public benchmark for audio-domain tasksBogdanov et al. (2019)。最近，XMIDI 已成为一个大规模数据集，包含超过 100,000 个 MIDI 文件的细粒度情感和流派注释，增强了音乐生成任务中的控制和调节. Recently, XMIDI has emerged as a large-scale dataset with fine-grained emotion and genre annotations across over 100,000 MIDI files, enhancing control and conditioning in music generation tasksTian et al. (2025)。尽管它们很实用，但这些数据集有一个共同的局限性：它们绝大多数代表了西方音乐结构、流派和乐器。因此，对其进行训练的模型往往会复制西方的音调和节奏模式，这限制了它们对代表性不足的音乐传统的适用性。. Despite their utility, these datasets share a common limitation: they overwhelmingly represent Western musical structures, genres, and instrumentation. Consequently, models trained on them tend to replicate Western tonalities and rhythmic patterns, which limits their applicability to underrepresented musical traditions.

2.2 音乐数据集和模型中的偏差Bias in Music Datasets and Models

第 2.1 节中提到的数据集的一个关键限制是它们压倒性的西方偏见。一项研究对这个问题进行了量化，该研究分析了超过一百万小时的音乐语料库。研究发现，近 94% 的数据代表西方音乐，只有约 5.7% 来自所有其他非西方流派的总和。至关重要的是，印度斯坦、中东和非洲音乐等传统只占数据的一小部分（例如，中东传统不到 1%）。这种严重的数据不平衡对模型性能产生直接的负面影响：在此类数据上训练的最先进的生成模型往往默认为西方音调和节奏惯例，即使在明确提示使用其他风格时也是如此A critical limitation of the mentioned datasets in Section 2.1 is their overwhelming Western bias. This issue was quantified in a study, which analyzed over one million hours of music corpora. The study found that nearly 94% of the data represents Western music, with only about 5.7% coming from all other non-Western genres combined. Crucially, traditions like Hindustani, Middle Eastern, and African music accounted for a tiny fraction of the data (e.g., Middle Eastern traditions at under 1%). This profound data imbalance has a direct, negative impact on model performance: state-of-the-art generative models trained on such data tend to default to Western tonal and rhythmic conventions, even when explicitly prompted for other stylesMehta et al. (2025b).

2.3 特定文化和波斯音乐数据Culturally-Specific and Persian Music Data

只有少数数据集关注非西方或资源匮乏的音乐传统。特别是对于波斯（伊朗）音乐，最值得注意的资源集中在古典和传统形式。例如，Nava 数据集包含 7 个主要 Dastgah 系统中 40 名艺术家演奏的 5 种传统乐器（kamancheh、tar、setar 等）的 1,786 个独奏录音（55 小时）Only a few datasets focus on non-Western or low-resource musical traditions. For Persian (Iranian) music specifically, the most notable resources focus on classical and traditional forms. The Nava dataset, for instance, contains 1,786 solo recordings (55 hours) of five traditional instruments (kamancheh, tar, setar, etc.) played by 40 artists across the seven main Dastgah systemsEbrat et al. (2022)。另一个收藏是波斯钢琴语料库 (PPC)，其中包括波斯作曲家的 2,022 首钢琴作品，并附有 Dastgah 调式注释. Another collection is the Persian Piano Corpus (PPC), which includes 2,022 piano pieces from Persian composers, annotated with their Dastgah modesRasouli and Bastanfard (2023)。这些数据集支持对 Dastgah 分类和乐器识别等任务的研究，但规模不大且高度特定于流派。. These datasets have enabled research on tasks like Dastgah classification and instrument recognition but are modest in size and highly genre-specific.

这种对古典形式的关注与最近的其他努力是一致的。例如，IRMA 数据集提供了 radif 表演的音频 MIDI 语料库This focus on classical forms is consistent across other recent efforts. For example, the IRMA dataset provides an audio-MIDI corpus of radif performancesShafiei and Hakam (2025)，象征性的 Radif Corpus 提供了非度量伊朗古典音乐的数据集, and the symbolic Radif Corpus offers a dataset for non-metric Iranian classical musicKanani et al. (2025)。较小的专业语料库也遵循这种模式，例如为乐器识别研究收集的七种波斯古典乐器的 700 个短片的数据集. Smaller, specialized corpora also follow this pattern, such as a dataset of  700 short clips of seven Persian classical instruments collected for an instrument recognition studyMousavi et al. (2019)。除此之外，仅存在非常小的或未出版的波斯音乐语料库（例如，包含 500 首歌曲的流派标签集）。重要的是，据我们所知，还没有当代波斯音乐（现代流行、摇滚、说唱等）的大规模公共数据集。这与大量的西方数据形成鲜明对比，并凸显了一个重大差距：现代波斯音乐在当前生成模型的训练数据中基本上仍然缺失。. Beyond these, only very small or unpublished corpora of Persian music exist (e.g., a 500-song genre-labeled set). Importantly, no large-scale, public dataset of contemporary Persian music (modern pop, rock, rap, etc.) is available to our knowledge. This stands in stark contrast to the plentiful Western data, and it highlights a major gap: modern Persian musics remain essentially absent from current generative models’ training data.

3.1 爬行的波斯音乐曲目Crawling Persian Music Tracks

为了构建大规模的波斯音乐数据集，我们从一个著名的波斯音乐网站上抓取了音轨，该网站拥有全面的当代和古典作品收藏。该平台包含丰富的歌曲档案，涵盖各种流派、情绪和艺术家。我们总共收集了大约 16,000 首波斯音乐曲目，涵盖了广泛的时间和风格范围。这个大型语料库是我们数据集管道中进一步处理、分割和注释步骤的基础。To build a large-scale dataset of Persian music, we crawled audio tracks from a prominent Persian music website that hosts a comprehensive collection of contemporary and classical works. The platform contains a rich archive of songs across various genres, moods, and artists. In total, we collected approximately 16k tracks of Persian music, covering a wide temporal and stylistic spectrum. This large corpus served as the foundation for further processing, segmentation, and annotation steps in our dataset pipeline.

3.2 将曲目分割成音乐剪辑Segmenting Tracks into Musical Clips

为了准备模型训练的音乐数据，我们将每个波斯语曲目分割成持续时间从 10 到 30 秒不等的较短剪辑。我们没有使用固定长度的切片，而是使用了基于信号能量的自适应分割策略。对短重叠帧上的信号能量进行分析，以识别显着动态变化的点，例如音乐短语的开始或偏移。选择分段边界，该特征表明结构变化，有助于保持每个剪辑内的音乐连贯性。这种方法使我们能够捕捉与波斯音乐的自然措辞一致的有意义的音乐单元，同时保持剪辑长度的一致性。To prepare the music data for model training, we segmented each Persian track into shorter clips ranging from 10 to 30 seconds in duration. Instead of fixed-length slicing, we used an adaptive segmentation strategy based on signal energy. Signal energy was analyzed over short overlapping frames to identify points of significant dynamic change, such as the onset or offset of a musical phrase. Segmentation boundaries were chosen where this features indicated structural changes, helping preserve musical coherence within each clip. This method allowed us to capture meaningful musical units aligned with the natural phrasing of Persian music while maintaining consistency in clip length.

3.4 声乐分离Vocal-Instrumental Separation

为了更好地控制音乐属性并改善生成条件，我们对所有剪辑进行了声乐与乐器的分离。我们利用了 Mel-band Roformer 模型，这是一种最新的音乐源分离方法To enable finer control over the musical attributes and improve conditioning for generation, we performed vocal-instrumental separation on all clips. We utilized the Mel-band Roformer model, a recent state-of-the-art method for music source separationWang et al. (2023)。与启发式地将频谱划分为不重叠子带的传统频带分割方法不同，梅尔频带 Roformer 使用基于感知的梅尔尺度投影。该模型将音频转换为复值梅尔频谱图，其中频率仓被映射到重叠的梅尔频带，反映了人耳对音调的非线性感知。然后，使用具有旋转位置嵌入 (RoPE) 的分层 Transformer 对带内和带间随时间的依赖关系进行建模，捕获对音乐至关重要的长程结构。该模型估计每个梅尔带的掩模，然后将其投影回全频域以进行波形重建。与 BSRNN 或 BS-RoFormer 等先前模型相比，这种方法在分离人声、鼓声和其他声干方面表现出卓越的性能，特别是在信号失真比 (SDR) 方面. Unlike traditional band-split approaches that divide the frequency spectrum into non-overlapping subbands heuristically, Mel-band Roformer uses a perceptually grounded mel-scale projection. The model converts the audio into a complex-valued mel-spectrogram, where frequency bins are mapped into overlapping mel bands, reflecting the human ear’s nonlinear perception of pitch. A hierarchical Transformer with Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) is then used to model both intra- and inter-band dependencies across time, capturing the long-range structure crucial for music. The model estimates a mask for each mel band, which is then projected back to the full frequency domain for waveform reconstruction. This approach has shown superior performance in separating vocals, drums, and other stems compared to prior models like BSRNN or BS-RoFormer, especially in terms of signal-to-distortion ratio (SDR)Luo and Yu (2022); Lu et al. (2023)。在我们的管道中，我们利用此功能从波斯音乐剪辑中提取干净的人声和器乐，随后用于标签预测和条件生成。. In our pipeline, we leveraged this capability to extract clean vocal and instrumental stems from Persian music clips, which were later used for tag prediction and conditional generation.

4 培训流程Training Procedure

我们的方法基于We base our approach on音乐生成器MusicGen，一个强大且公开的生成模型，专为文本条件音乐合成而设计, a powerful and publicly available generative model designed for text-conditioned music synthesisCopet et al. (2024)。 MusicGen 采用单级 Transformer 解码器，可自回归生成与可选文本调节标记交错的离散音频标记。它利用高质量音频标记器（例如 EnCodec）将立体声音频表示为离散标记流，从而允许模型在语言建模框架中运行。由此产生的系统能够根据自由格式的文本提示或旋律调节生成连贯的音乐音频。. MusicGen employs a single-stage Transformer decoder that autoregressively generates discrete audio tokens interleaved with optional text-conditioning tokens. It leverages a high-quality audio tokenizer (e.g., EnCodec) to represent stereo audio as discrete token streams, allowing the model to operate in a language-modeling framework. The resulting system is capable of generating coherent musical audio from either free-form text prompts or melody conditioning.

为了有效地使 MusicGen 适应波斯音乐领域，不仅要以忠实反映波斯音乐作品特征的方式控制离散音频标记，而且还要确保与其文本描述一致。为此，我们引入了一个三阶段的训练管道，引导模型从一般音频生成转向以细粒度文本细节为条件的文化特定、乐器感知的波斯作曲。该训练过程的概述如图所示To effectively adapt MusicGen to the Persian music domain, it is essential not only to control the discrete audio tokens in a manner that faithfully reflects the characteristics of Persian musical pieces, but also to ensure coherent alignment with their textual descriptions. To this end, we introduce a three-stage training pipeline that guides the model from general audio generation toward culturally specific, instrument-aware Persian composition conditioned on fine-grained textual details. 6 结论Conclusion

这项研究展示了第一个大规模、高质量的波斯音乐数据集的管理，并展示了 MusicGen 模型成功适应这个文化丰富的领域。通过对这个综合语料库（包含流行、传统和当代流派的 900 多个小时的音频）对 MusicGen 进行微调，我们已经证明，可以将最先进的生成模型应用于文化独特且历史上代表性不足的音乐传统。This study presents the curation of the first large-scale, high-quality Persian music dataset and demonstrates the successful adaptation of the MusicGen model to this culturally rich domain. By fine-tuning MusicGen on this comprehensive corpus which comprises over 900 hours of audio across pop, traditional, and contemporary genres, we have shown that it is possible to adapt state-of-the-art generative models to culturally distinct and historically underrepresented musical traditions.

我们的实验结果证实，经过微调的 MusicGen 模型产生的音乐明显更符合波斯音乐结构。与基线相比，较低的 KLD 值和较高的色度余弦相似度证明了这一点，表明该模型成功捕获了 Dastgah 系统独特的情态和节奏习语。这些结果为未来研究文化意识音乐的产生提供了一个有希望的途径。Our experimental results confirm that the fine-tuned MusicGen model produces music significantly more aligned with Persian musical structures. This is evidenced by lower KLD values and higher Chroma Cosine Similarity compared to the baseline, indicating that the model successfully captured the unique modal and rhythmic idioms of the Dastgah system. These results provide a promising avenue for future research into culturally-aware music generation.

在未来的工作中，我们计划通过合并额外的音乐特征并扩展数据集以包含更多流派来进一步增强模型的功能。我们还致力于探索更先进的微调技术，以提高模型生成具有更高风格准确性的音乐的能力In future work, we plan to further enhance the model’s capabilities by incorporating additional musical features and extending the dataset to include even more genres. We also aim to explore more advanced fine-tuning techniques to improve the model’s ability to generate music with even greater stylistic accuracy

7 局限性Limitations

数据集范围和平衡。Dataset Scope and Balance.

尽管我们的数据集包含超过 900 小时的波斯音乐，但其流派分布严重偏向波斯流行音乐，约占所有曲目的 93.7%。传统音乐、摇滚音乐和新传统音乐等流派的代表性严重不足。这种不平衡可能会使模型偏向流行风格的生成，并限制其忠实再现古典和传统波斯音乐（Dastgah 系统在这些音乐中表现最突出）的旋律和节奏细微差别的能力。Although our dataset comprises over 900 hours of Persian music, its genre distribution is heavily skewed toward Persian pop, which accounts for approximately 93.7% of all tracks. Genres such as traditional, rock, and neo-traditional music are substantially underrepresented. This imbalance may bias the model toward pop-style generation and limit its ability to faithfully reproduce the melodic and rhythmic nuances of classical and traditional Persian music, where the Dastgah system is most prominently expressed.

自动标记和字幕质量。Automatic Tagging and Caption Quality.

用于调节的元数据是使用自动化管道而不是专家的人工注释来提取的。这些自动预测不可避免地会引入噪音；例如，乐器分类器可能会混淆波斯乐器（例如 tar 和 setar）之间的音色相似性，并且为西方音调设计的关键估计工具可能无法可靠地捕获波斯微音程。同样，LLaMA 3.2 3B 生成的字幕虽然流畅，但并未经过领域专家的系统验证，可能会在文本调节中引入语义不准确。The metadata used for conditioning were extracted using automated pipelines rather than expert human annotation. These automatic predictions inevitably introduce noise; for example, instrument classifiers may confuse timbral similarities between Persian instruments (e.g., tar and setar), and key estimation tools designed for Western tonalities may not reliably capture Persian microtonal intervals. Similarly, the captions generated by LLaMA 3.2 3B, while fluent, were not systematically validated by domain experts, potentially introducing semantic inaccuracies in the text conditioning.

评估方法。Evaluation Methodology.

我们的评估主要依赖于 KL 散度和色度余弦相似度，这两者都测量分布和和声对齐，但不直接评估波斯音乐的感知显着品质，例如微音准确性、装饰保真度或对 Dastgah 调式进行的遵守。此外，虽然我们采用混合人类分类器评估标签的正确性，但并未对训练有素的波斯音乐家进行全面的主观听力研究，以评估文化真实性、情感表达力和风格连贯性。这种专家评估将为模型的文化保真度提供更有意义的评估。Our evaluation relies primarily on KL Divergence and Chroma Cosine Similarity, both of which measure distributional and harmonic alignment but do not directly assess perceptually salient qualities of Persian music such as microtonal accuracy, ornamentation fidelity, or adherence to Dastgah modal progressions. Furthermore, while we employ a hybrid human–classifier evaluation for tag correctness, a comprehensive subjective listening study with trained Persian musicians, assessing cultural authenticity, emotional expressiveness, and stylistic coherence, was not conducted. Such expert evaluation would provide a more meaningful assessment of the model’s cultural fidelity.

模型规模。Model Scale.

由于计算限制，我们仅对 MusicGen 的小变体进行了微调。较大的模型变体可以提高捕捉波斯音乐复杂旋律结构和长距离依赖性特征的能力，特别是在扩展即兴段落（avaz）常见的传统形式中。我们的研究结果对更大架构的普遍适用性仍有待探索。We fine-tuned only the small variant of MusicGen due to computational constraints. Larger model variants may offer improved capacity for capturing the complex melodic structures and long-range dependencies characteristic of Persian music, particularly in traditional forms where extended improvisatory passages (avaz) are common. The generalizability of our findings to larger architectures remains unexplored.

微音调和模态表示。Microtonal and Modal Representation.

底层音频分词器 (EnCodec) 和 MusicGen 架构最初是为西方音乐设计的，在 12 音平均律系统上运行。波斯音乐依赖于西方半音之间的微音程，并且在不同的 Dastgah 模式中根据上下文而变化。目前尚不清楚当前的标记化方案可以在多大程度上忠实地编码和重建这些微妙的音高差异，并且没有对微音保真度进行专门的分析。The underlying audio tokenizer (EnCodec) and the MusicGen architecture were originally designed for Western music, which operates on a 12-tone equal temperament system. Persian music relies on microtonal intervals that fall between Western semitones and vary contextually across different Dastgah modes. It is unclear to what extent the current tokenization scheme can faithfully encode and reconstruct these subtle pitch distinctions, and no dedicated analysis of microtonal fidelity was performed.
