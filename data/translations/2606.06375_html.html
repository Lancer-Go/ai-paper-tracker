摘要Abstract

数字孪生（DT）允许道路基础设施检查的数字化，尽管这受到有限的注释数据的阻碍。这项工作利用连续资产状况监控的关系性质，将基于图像的缺陷检测重新表述为图像差异分类 (IDC)，以减少数据依赖。这是在使用新策划的高质量数据集使用不同 IDC 分类器进行低资源交通标志检查的案例研究中对此进行了评估。结果表明，基于指令的分类器优于基于编码器的分类器，并且通过与参考图像的比较获得了收益。这表明IDC可以成为解决基础设施检查和DT资产状况更新中的数据约束的有效任务建模。Digital twins (DTs) allow the digitalization of road infrastructure inspection, though this is hindered by limited annotated data. This work exploits the relational nature of continuous asset condition monitoring to reformulate image-based defect detection as image difference classification (IDC) to reduce data reliance. This was evaluated in a case study on low-resource traffic sign inspection with different IDC classifiers using a newly-curated, high quality dataset. Results indicate that the instruction-based classifier outperforms encoder-based ones and gains from comparison with reference images. This shows that IDC can be an effective task modeling for tackling data constraints in infrastructure inspection and DT asset condition updating.

1 简介Introduction

维护道路基础设施需要手动目视状况检查。道路数字孪生（DT）的提议Maintaining road infrastructure requires manual visual condition inspection. The proposal of a Road Digital Twin (DT)[10]与智能车辆捕获的道路图像数量不断增加相一致，为这一过程的数字化提供了机会。特别是，Road DT 框架通过连续的更新步骤来跟踪道路资产不断变化的状况，每个更新步骤都涉及将图像分类为由资产特定数据要求指定的不同状况标签, coinciding with increasing road imagery capturing from smart vehicles, presents an opportunity to digitalize this process. Particularly, the Road DT framework tracks the evolving condition of road assets through successive updating steps each involving classifying an image into different condition labels specified by asset-specific data requirements[15].

与许多建筑、工程和施工 (AEC) 领域一样，道路维护的注释数据很少。为了减少数据依赖性，这项工作通过将缺陷识别重新考虑为图像差异分类 (IDC) 任务，利用 DT 更新步骤的关系性质，其中将检查图像与先前拍摄的同一资产的参考图像进行比较，以产生接地输出。随着时间的推移，资产图像已经存在于真实的道路资产管理系统 (AMS) 中，并且此任务制定将它们用作参考，而不需要额外的注释。由于资产范围广泛，这项工作重点关注交通标志，因为它们对道路安全至关重要，并且包含大量故障模式。Annotated data for road maintenance is scarce like in many Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) fields. To reduce data dependency, this work exploits the relational nature of the DT updating step by rethinking defect identification as an image difference classification (IDC) task where an inspection image is compared against a reference image of the same asset taken previously to produce a grounded output. Asset images over time already exist in real road asset management systems (AMSs), and this task formulation uses them as references without needing additional annotation. Due to the wide range of assets, this work focuses on traffic signs as they are both critical to road safety and contain a large number of failure modes.

本文的贡献是：（1）构建具有条件注释的高质量、多功能的交通标志图像数据集。 (2) 在交通标志检查任务中试验 IDC 管道，并量化添加参考图像在减少资源匮乏环境中数据依赖性方面的效果。The contributions of this paper are: (1) Curating a high quality and versatile traffic sign imagery dataset with condition annotation. (2) Experimenting with IDC pipelines on traffic sign inspection tasks and quantifying the effects of adding a reference image in reducing data-dependency in low-resource settings.

据作者所知，现有的工作与真实的标志检查需求不一致，并且研究仍然存在很大差距。首先对于数据集，许多只包含标志脸部的特写（没有帖子或背景）To the best of the authors’ knowledge, existing work misaligns with real sign inspection needs and there remain large gaps in research. Firstly for datasets, many only contain close-ups of the sign face (without post or background)[17][11]或非常粗略的条件标记（二元或一个缺陷）or very coarse condition labeling (binary or one defect)[13][2][5]，并且没有一个允许随着时间的推移进行状态跟踪。其次，对于任务建模，没有一个将缺陷识别视为多类多标签任务（不同的缺陷通常同时发生），没有一个利用多个图像进行检测（模拟道路DT更新和状态跟踪），所有这些都仅使用单图像并且大多数仅依靠增强来解决数据稀缺问题, and none allows for condition tracking over time. Secondly for task modeling, none treats defect identification as a multi-class multi-label task (different defects often occur simultaneously), none leverages multiple images for detection (simulating Road DT updating and condition tracking), with all using only single-images and most relying solely on augmentation to tackle data scarcity[17][13].

3 方法论Methodology

为了弥补这些差距，本节介绍了一个新的数据集并讨论了所执行的目标和实验。To cover the gaps, this section introduces a new dataset and discusses the objectives and experiments performed.

3.1 数据集Dataset

交通标志图像是从英国高速公路管理局国家公路局使用的真实道路 AMS 中收集的。该数据集通过提供以下功能填补了空白：(1) 显示标志所有部分（标志面、柱子和背景）的真实检查图像，以捕获更多故障模式。 (2) 每个独特标志两张图像（未损坏的参考 + 检查）以跟踪状况。 (3)细粒度多标签状态标注，匹配真实维护需求。Traffic sign images were collected from a real road AMS used by National Highways, the UK highway authority. This dataset fills the gap by providing: (1) Real inspection imagery showing all parts of a sign (sign face, post, and background) to capture more failure modes. (2) Two images per unique sign (undamaged reference + inspection) to track conditions. (3) Fine-grained multi-label condition annotation to match real maintenance needs.

3.2 目的与实验Objective and experiments

这项工作的目的是研究参考图像是否可以通过图像-图像比较来提高交通标志缺陷分类性能，并减少资源匮乏环境中的数据依赖性。作者假设随着时间的推移，资产图像可以用作补充训练数据的参考。The objective of this work is to investigate whether reference images can improve traffic sign defect classification performance through image-image comparison and reduce data-dependency in low-resource settings. The authors hypothesize that asset images over time can be used as references to supplement training data.

研究了两个任务：二元缺陷存在检测和多类多标签缺陷分类。前者与用于对图像进行优先级排序以进行手动审核的过滤系统相关，而后者可以直接应用于道路 DT 资产状况更新。这两个任务都被建模为一个组合的多类多标签 IDC 问题，并带有一个用于缺陷存在的附加标志。通过使用每个类（镜头）不同数量的示例微调分类器来模拟数据约束。然后将 IDC 管道的性能与不使用参考图像的最佳性能等效单图像管道进行比较。Two tasks were studied: binary defect presence detection and multi-class multi-label defect classification. The former is relevant to filtering systems for prioritizing images for manual review, while the latter can be directly applicable to Road DT asset condition updating. Both tasks were modeled as a combined multi-class multi-label IDC problem with an additional flag for defect presence. Data constraints were simulated by fine-tuning classifiers using different numbers of examples per class (shot). The performance of IDC pipelines was then compared with the best performing equivalent single-image pipelines that do not use reference images.

实验使用具有 16GB VRAM 的 RTX 4080，使用包括 Transformers、Pytorch 和 Unsloth 在内的库。在微调过程中，所有训练集参考图像都被用作伪“无缺陷”示例，以确保模型看到相同的图像，无论分类是否使用参考。每个实验都用五个不同的数据分割重复，以减轻少数样本学习的不稳定性（类似于Experiments used an RTX 4080 with 16GB VRAM, using libraries including Transformers, Pytorch, and Unsloth. All training-set reference images were used during fine-tuning as pseudo “no defect” examples to ensure models see the same images irrespective of whether classification uses references. Every experiment was repeated with five different data splits to mitigate few-shot learning instability (similarly to[21]），并报告平均结果。), with averaged results reported.

分类器管道分为基于编码器的和基于指令的。下面详细描述它们。Classifier pipelines are divided into encoder-based and instruction-based. They are described in detail below.

3.2.1 基于编码器的管道Encoder Based Pipelines

提出了四种基于编码器的管道。两个 IDC 分类器纯粹基于视觉：一个简单地使用线性分类器来融合两个串联的图像嵌入，而另一个则在线性层之前添加一个图像-图像交叉注意层（在原始转换器中的交叉注意块之后建模）以允许显式交互。另外两个 IDC 分类器也使用文本类描述：一个在线性分类器之前添加图像-图像-文本交叉注意层，而另一个使用图像嵌入差异和类文本之间的相似性作为分类逻辑（类似于Four encoder-based pipelines were proposed. Two IDC classifiers are purely vision-based: one simply uses a linear classifier to fuse the two concatenated image embeddings while the other adds an image-image cross attention layer (modeled after the cross attention block in the original transformer) prior to the linear layer to allow explicit interaction. Two more IDC classifiers also use textual class descriptions: one adds an image-image-text cross attention layer prior to the linear classifier, while the other uses similarity between image embedding difference and class texts as classification logits (similarly to[16]）。纯基于视觉的 IDC 分类器的等效单图像管道被选为单图像嵌入的线性分类器，而文本增强分类器的等效单图像管道被选为线性分类器之前的图像文本交叉注意和单图像嵌入与类文本的相似性。 MetaCLIP2 被选为骨干，因为它的性能优于其他测试模型（例如 SigLIP2）。). The equivalent single-image pipeline for purely vision-based IDC classifiers was chosen as a linear classifier on the single-image embedding, while those for the text-enhanced classifiers were chosen as image-text cross attention prior to the linear classifier and single-image embedding similarity with class texts. MetaCLIP2 was chosen as the backbone as it outperformed other models tested (e.g. SigLIP2).

3.2.2 基于指令的流水线Instruction Based Pipeline

基于指令的 VLM 在比较推理方面表现出了强大的性能Instruction-based VLMs have shown strong performance in comparative reasoning[16]。检查图像和参考图像被插入到用户提示中，指示用户使用后者作为分类指南。系统提示中提供了文本类描述。结构化分类输出是从 json 格式的模型响应中收集的。初步研究的重点是根据示例交互对模型进行微调。选择 Qwen3 8B 作为模型，因为它的性能优于其他测试模型（例如 Gemma 3）。. The inspection and reference images are inserted into the user prompt where it is instructed to use the latter as a classification guide. Textual class descriptions are provided in the system prompt. Structured classification outputs are collected from model response in json. Primary investigations focused on fine-tuning the model based on example interactions. Qwen3 8B was chosen as the model as it outperformed other models tested (e.g. Gemma 3).

5 讨论Discussion

值得注意的是，所有管道（IDC 和单图像）在微调期间都可以访问相同的图像，因为参考图像仍然用作伪“无缺陷”示例，无论它们是否用于分类。因此，任何改进都是参考图像提供的最佳资产状态的附加背景的结果，而不是数据可用性有效增加的结果。It is important to note that all pipelines (IDC and single-image) have access to the same images during fine-tuning, as reference images are still used as pseudo “no defect” examples irrespective of whether they are used for classification. Therefore, any improvement is a result of the additional context of optimal asset states provided by reference images and not of an effective increase in data availability.

话虽如此，我们可以得出两个主要结论：首先， 基于指令的分类器优于所有基于编码器的分类器，并且能够利用参考图像来提高性能。造成这种差距的一个潜在原因可能是模型参数的数量不同，这将在消融中进行探讨That being said, two main observations can be made: Firstly, the instruction-based classifier outperforms all encoder-based ones and is able to leverage reference images for performance gains. A potential reason for this gap can be the different number of model parameters, which will be explored in the Ablations inSec. 6。其次，模型在能够使用额外的参考输入之前需要“校准”。这可以通过每类只需一个示例进行微调来完成，这提高了. Secondly, models require “calibration” before being able to make use of the additional reference input. This can be done by fine-tuning with as few as one example per class, which raisedf​1f1用于 0.07 至 0.905 的二元缺陷存在检测。for binary defect presence detection from 0.07 to 0.905.

通过这些观察，IDC 可以成为持续基础设施检查和 DT 条件更新的有效任务建模。参考图像可以改善检测结果并补充资源匮乏环境中的训练数据。然而，这仅限于基于指令的分类器，并且需要一个小的“校准”微调集。这样做的一个缺点是，几次学习的不稳定性可能会产生波动的输出，从而影响可靠性。With these observations, IDC can be an effective task modeling for continuous infrastructure inspection and DT condition updating. Reference images can improve detection results and supplement training data in low-resource settings. However, this is limited to only instruction-based classifiers and requires a small “calibrating” fine-tuning set. A drawback of this is that instability from few-shot learning can create fluctuating outputs which can affect reliability.

6 消融Ablations

进行了以下消融研究。The following ablation studies were carried out.

6.1 更大的编码器骨干Bigger encoder backbone

为了研究使用与基于指令的骨干网大小相当的更大视觉编码器是否可以弥补基于编码器和基于指令的管道之间的性能差距，除了 2B MetaCLIP2 之外，还对 7B DINOv3 进行了测试。To investigate whether using a bigger vision-encoder comparable to the size of the instruction-based backbone can bridge the gap in performance between the encoder-based and instruction-based pipelines, the 7B DINOv3 was tested in addition to the 2B MetaCLIP2.Tab. 4节目showsm​a​c​r​omacro f​1f1具有两个主干的两个仅视觉管道的分数。尽管 MetaCLIP2 尺寸较小，但在每个训练镜头中，MetaCLIP2 的性能均明显优于 DINOv3。模型参数的纯粹数量似乎并不是基于编码器和基于指令的管道之间性能差异的唯一决定因素。scores of the two vision-only pipelines with the two backbones. MetaCLIP2 significantly outperforms DINOv3 in every training shot despite being smaller in size. Pure number of model parameters does not seem to be the sole determinant of the difference in performance between encoder-based and instruction-based pipelines.

6.2 伪缺陷Pseudo defects

在主要设置中，参考图像被用作“无缺陷”伪缺陷图像，以保持管道之间的训练数据相同，以避免在使用参考图像时因有效增加数据可用性而获得意外收益。对基于指令的管道进行了没有伪缺陷图像的实验，以了解它们对性能的影响。结果表明，它们的使用提高了所有训练场景的性能（例如，8 镜头 IDC 的性能提高了 +0.016），并且即使没有伪缺陷，IDC 管道也保持了相对于单图像管道的领先优势（例如 8 镜头的 +0.053）。In the main setup, reference images were used as “no defect” pseudo defect images to keep training data identical between pipelines to avoid unintended gains from an effective increase in data availability when using reference images. Experiments without pseudo defect images were performed for the instruction-based pipeline to understand their effects on performance. Results show that their use improves performance across all training scenarios (e.g. +0.016 for 8-shot IDC), and that the IDC pipeline maintains its lead over the single-image pipeline even without pseudo defects (e.g. +0.053 for 8-shot).

7 结论Conclusion

Road DT 旨在实现道路资产状况监测的数字化。这项工作利用连续资产跟踪的关系性质，通过重新思考 IDC 的缺陷识别来减少数据依赖性。随着时间的推移，它使用现有的资产图像作为通过图像比较进行基础分类的参考。使用新整理的真实检查图像数据集，在低资源交通标志检查的案例研究中测试了该任务制定。结果表明，基于指令的分类器始终优于基于编码器的分类器，并且通过每个类只有一个示例的小型微调数据集，它能够利用额外的参考输入来提高性能。因此，IDC可以成为一种有效的任务建模，以减少持续基础设施检查和DT条件更新中的数据依赖。The Road DT aims to digitalize road asset condition monitoring. This work exploits the relational nature of continuous asset tracking to reduce data dependency by rethinking defect identification as IDC. This uses already-existing asset images over time as references for grounded classification through image comparison. Using a newly curated dataset of real inspection images, this task formulation was tested in a case study on low-resource traffic sign inspection. Results show that the instruction-based classifier consistently outperforms encoder-based ones, and that with a small fine-tuning dataset of as few as one example per class, it is able to leverage the additional reference inputs for performance gains. Therefore, IDC can be an effective task modeling for reducing data reliance in continuous infrastructure inspection and DT condition updating.

未来的工作包括测试更大的数据集以消除少数镜头波动，并尝试每个标志使用多个参考图像。Future work includes testing larger datasets to eliminate few-shot fluctuations and experimenting with using more than one reference images per sign.
