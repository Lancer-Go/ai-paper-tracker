摘要Abstract

生成式人工智能 (GenAI) 方法能够以逼真的方式改变相机图像，这提高了人们对在线共享图像真实性的认识。有趣的是，我们的相机直接拍摄的图像被认为是真实可信的。然而，随着深度学习模块越来越多地集成到相机的捕获时间硬件（即图像信号处理器（ISP））中，现在我们的相机直接输出的图像中可能会出现幻觉内容。幻觉捕获时图像内容通常是良性的，例如增强的边缘或纹理，但在某些操作中，例如基于人工智能的数字变焦或低光图像增强，幻觉可能会改变图像内容的语义和解释。因此，用户可能没有意识到他们的相机图像中的内容是不真实的。本文通过使用户能够恢复相机图像的“未幻觉”版本来解决这个问题，以避免对图像内容的误解。我们的方法通过优化特定于图像的多层感知器（MLP）解码器和特定于模态的编码器来工作，这样，在给定相机图像的情况下，我们可以在添加幻觉内容之前恢复图像。编码器和 MLP 是独立的，可以在捕获后应用于图像，无需访问相机 ISP。此外，编码器和 MLP 解码器仅需要 180 KB 存储空间，并且可以轻松保存为 JPEG 和 HEIC 等标准图像格式的元数据。The ability of generative AI (GenAI) methods to photorealistically alter camera images has raised awareness about the authenticity of images shared online. Interestingly, images captured directly by our cameras are considered authentic and faithful. However, with the increasing integration of deep-learning modules into cameras’ capture-time hardware—namely, the image signal processor (ISP)—there is now a potential for hallucinated content in images directly output by our cameras. Hallucinated capture-time image content is typically benign, such as enhanced edges or texture, but in certain operations, such as AI-based digital zoom or low-light image enhancement, hallucinations can potentially alter the semantics and interpretation of the image content. As a result, users may not realize that the content in their camera images is not authentic. This paper addresses this issue by enabling users to recover the “unhallucinated” version of the camera image to avoid misinterpretation of the image content. Our approach works by optimizing an image-specific multi-layer perceptron (MLP) decoder together with a modality-specific encoder so that, given the camera image, we can recover the image before hallucinated content was added. The encoder and MLP are self-contained and can be applied post-capture to the image without requiring access to the camera ISP. Moreover, the encoder and MLP decoder require only 180 KB of storage and can be readily saved as metadata within standard image formats such as JPEG and HEIC.

The implications of such GenAI to alter and create images are profound. Fakes and altered images circulated at scale can, for example, sway political sentiment, influence election outcomes, or create confusion and spread misinformation about serious issues[2, 9, 20, 51]。这促使各国政府和监管机构出台立法来遏制 GenAI 的滥用. This has prompted governments and regulatory bodies to introduce legislation to curb GenAI misuse[12]。正在引入规则，要求开发人员和部署人员确保最终用户意识到他们正在与人工智能图像进行交互。主要社交媒体平台已经开始对使用生成人工智能方法编辑或数字化的照片进行标记和显着划分. Rules are being introduced requiring developers and deployers to ensure end-users are aware that they are interacting with AI imagery. Major social media platforms have already begun tagging and prominently demarcating photos that were edited or born digital using generative AI methods[4, 17]。对深度伪造和 GenAI 修改图像的认识现已成为主流。. Awareness of deepfakes and GenAI-altered images is now mainstream.

关于更改相机图像的讨论仍然集中在Discussions about altered camera images remain focused on捕获后post-capture操纵，假设摄像机直接输出的图像是物理场景的真实且可信的再现。这不再是真的manipulation, assuming images directly output by cameras as authentic and trusted renditions of the physical scene. This is no longer true[47]。相机采用称为图像信号处理器 (ISP) 的专用板载硬件，它将各种处理步骤应用于传感器响应，例如去噪、去马赛克、自动白平衡、颜色和色调调整、锐化、数字变焦等，以生成向用户显示的照片. Cameras employ dedicated onboard hardware called an image signal processor (ISP), which applies various processing steps to the sensor response—such as denoising, demosaicing, auto white balance, color and tonal adjustments, sharpening, digital zoom, and more—to produce the photo displayed to the user[8, 5]。传统上，信号处理算法一直是 ISP 处理模块的基础。虽然传统的基于信号处理的方法经常操纵图像的颜色和色调，但它们并不是为了生成“幻觉”图像内容而设计的。然而，现代 ISP 越来越多地使用基于人工智能的模块，特别是对于数字变焦和夜间摄影等具有挑战性的成像场景。当在 ISP 中使用经过生成或感知损失训练的 AI 模型时，它们很容易产生幻觉内容，从而可能改变图像含义. Traditionally, signal-processing algorithms have served as the underpinning of ISP processing blocks. While conventional signal-processing-based methods often manipulate the colors and tones of an image, they are not designed to generate “hallucinated” image content. However, modern ISPs increasingly use AI-based modules, especially for challenging imaging scenarios such as digital zoom and night photography. When AI models trained with generative or perceptual losses are used in ISPs, they are prone to hallucinate content, potentially altering image meaning[47]。这意味着从相机直接输出的图像现在可能包含“虚假”内容，尤其是在智能手机相机中，AI-ISP 模块的采用越来越多。 GenAI 在相机硬件中的使用标志着我们查看相机图像的方式发生了范式转变，并挑战了相机捕获图像的传统取证观点，认为其本质上是值得信赖的。. The implication is that images directly output from the camera may now contain “fake” content, especially in smartphone cameras where AI-ISP modules are seeing increasing adoption. The use of GenAI in camera hardware marks a paradigm shift in how we view camera images and challenges the traditional forensic view of camera-captured images as inherently trustworthy.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们强调了相机捕获图像的真实性问题。由于 GenAI 模块越来越多地集成到现代相机硬件 ISP 中，尤其是在智能手机中，甚至在相机直接输出的图像中也可能出现幻觉内容。我们提倡建立一个框架，允许用户在捕获后访问未出现幻觉的图像。In this work, we highlight the issue of authenticity in camera-captured images. Hallucinated content can appear even in images directly output by cameras due to the increasing integration of GenAI modules into modern camera hardware ISPs, particularly in smartphones. We advocate for a framework that allows users to access the unhallucinated image after capture.

为此，我们建议将基于 MLP 的轻量级神经网络的参数作为元数据与每个相机捕获的图像一起存储。虽然我们的主要目标是引起人们对相机图像中被广泛忽视的幻觉问题的关注，但我们还通过针对超分辨率和低光图像增强等任务的大量实验证明，我们的元数据辅助方法在存储占用、执行时间和准确性方面比其他方法有所改进。To this end, we propose storing the parameters of a lightweight MLP-based neural network as metadata alongside each camera-captured image. While our primary goal is to draw attention to the widely overlooked problem of hallucination in camera images, we also demonstrate through extensive experiments—on tasks such as super-resolution and low-light image enhancement—that our metadata-assisted approach offers improvements over alternative methods in terms of storage footprint, execution time, and accuracy.

随着捕获后人工智能编辑的法律和标准不断发展，相机图像的真实性将面临新的法律和行业要求。我们希望我们的工作为这一关键领域的未来研究奠定基础。As laws and standards evolve around post-capture AI editing, camera image authenticity will face new legal and industry requirements. We hope our work provides a foundation for future research in this critical area.
