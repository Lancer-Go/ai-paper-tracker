摘要Abstract

工业视频异常检测（IVAD）旨在识别工业过程中的异常物体和事件，这对于现代制造和质量控制系统至关重要。 现有的基于 VLM 的异常推理方法能够检测一般领域中的开放式异常。然而，在以复杂的对象转换、严格的物理和程序限制为特征的工业环境中，它们的性能会下降。 为了解决这种交互密集型检测的复杂性，我们引入了一种免训练的代理框架Industrial Video Anomaly Detection (IVAD) aims to identify anomalous objects and events in an industrial process, which is crucial for modern manufacturing and quality control systems. Existing VLM-based anomaly reasoning methods are capable of detecting open-ended anomalies in general domains. However, their performance declines in industrial settings characterized by intricate object transformations, strict physics, and procedural constraints. To tackle the complexity of such interaction-intensive detection, we introduce a training-free agentic frameworkVADO-VAD用于无需特定领域知识的异常检测，强调像人类检查员一样的对象状态演化。 它旨在跟踪检测到的对象随时间的时空动态和潜在变换，然后对对象的时间状态轨迹进行推理，以识别接地框架中的异常对象。 我们的方法克服了先前方法的局限性，这些方法依赖于对正常剪辑的再训练或注入领域知识作为测试时推理的上下文。 对三个 IVAD 数据集的大量实验表明，我们的方法优于在各自数据集上微调的前沿 VLM、代理框架和传统 VAD 方法，同时提供有关异常过程和类型的可解释报告。for anomaly detection free of domain-specific knowledge, emphasizing object state evolution like humans inspectors. It is designed to track spatial-temporal dynamics and underlying transformations of detected objects over time, and then reason over the object-wise temporal state trajectories to identify abnormal objects in grounded frames. Our method overcomes limitations of prior approaches that rely on retraining on normal clips or injecting domain knowledge as context for test-time inference. Extensive experiments on three IVAD datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms frontier VLMs, agentic frameworks, and traditional VAD methods fine-tuned on the respective datasets, while providing interpretable reports over anomaly processes and types.

传统视频异常检测。Traditional Video Anomaly Detection.

传统的视频异常检测方法主要采用两种范式：基于重建的方法Traditional video anomaly detection methods primarily adopt two paradigms: reconstruction-based approaches[12, 8, 42, 44, 43, 9]通过重构误差升高来标记异常，而基于嵌入的方法flag anomalies by their elevated reconstruction error, while embedding-based methods[17, 15, 32, 6, 11]对正态样本的特征分布进行建模并测量与其的偏差。尽管基准测试性能强劲，但两者都存在三个局限性。首先，他们遵循“一类一模型”范式model the feature distribution of normal samples and measure deviations from it. Despite strong benchmark performance, both share three limitations. First, they follow a “one-class-one-model” paradigm[10]，需要大量的正常样本和每个对象类的单独模型，并且必须针对每个未见的域或异常类型进行重新训练, requiring abundant normal samples and a separate model per object class, and must be re-trained for every unseen domain or anomaly type[22]，使得它们对于新颖的类别和动态的生产线来说不切实际。其次，它们很大程度上是黑匣子，在没有语义基础的情况下发出异常分数, making them impractical for novel categories and dynamic production lines. Second, they are largely black-box, emitting an anomaly score without semantic rationale[39]，这激发了人们对可解释的、基于语言的异常理解的兴趣日益增长, which has motivated growing interest in explainable, language-grounded anomaly understanding[22, 46]。第三，他们将整个帧压缩为整体特征，忽略区域级线索并过度依赖主导背景上下文. Our O-VAD addresses these limitations with a training-free, object-centric framework that requires no per-class training, produces semantic explanations for its decisions, and reasons over region-level evidence.

5 结论Conclusion

无需劳动密集型注释，我们的代理框架可以在任何级别的异常检测上提供最先进的性能，在定量和定性评估方面超越前沿 VLM。这展示了工业视频异常检测的新范例，弥合了低级模式识别和高级认知推理之间的差距，为更可靠、可解释和通用的工业检测系统铺平了道路。Without labor-intensive annotations, our agentic framework delivers state-of-the-art performance on any level anomaly detection, surpassing frontier VLMs on quantitative and qualitative evaluations. This demonstrates a new paradigm for industrial video anomaly detection that bridges the gap between low-level pattern recognition and high-level cognitive reasoning, paving the way for more reliable, explainable, and generalizable industrial inspection systems.

局限性。Limitations.

首先，多级代理管道比单前向传递 VAD 模型产生的延迟要高得多，并随着每个视频跟踪对象的数量而扩展。 其次，O-VAD 依赖于 VLM 的内在常识，而不是特定领域的专家先验，因此其性能会因不可见的物理属性或精确规范定义的异常而下降 这不能仅从视觉外观中推断出来——带有规范示例的少量镜头上下文学习是弥合这一差距的一个有希望的方向。 第三，静态或感知模糊的缺陷（First, the multi-stage agentic pipeline incurs substantially higher latency than single-forward-pass VAD models, scaling with the number of tracked objects per video. Second, O-VAD relies on the VLM’s internalized commonsense rather than domain-specific expert priors, so its performance declines on anomalies defined by invisible physical properties or precise specifications that cannot be inferred from visual appearance alone—few-shot in-context learning with specification examples is a promising direction to bridge this gap. Third, static or perception-ambiguous defects (例如e.g.、卡住的按钮、消磁的磁铁）产生视觉上合理的行为仍然是感知驱动推理的基本边界，推动轻量级领域先验和流程规范参考的未来集成。, stuck buttons, degaussed magnets) that produce visually plausible behavior remain a fundamental boundary of perception-driven reasoning, motivating future integration of lightweight domain priors and process specification references.

O-VAD’s three-stage pipeline, consisting of object discovery, state tracking, and chain-of-thought reasoning, is principally designed to address the first two settings, where anomalies are rooted in physical commonsense and object-level state evolution. Notably, the temporal reasoning capacity inherent in state tracking, which models how object states evolve across frames, generalizes naturally to periodic structures, making O-VAD’s training-free design readily extensible to the reference-based evaluation protocol of IPAD.
