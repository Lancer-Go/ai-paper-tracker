TRACE：二元语音中的时间关系感知会话夹带检测TRACE: Temporal Relationship-Aware Conversational Entrainment Detection in Dyadic Speech

Anonymized submission to IEEE SLT 2026    Sathvik Manikantan Napa Ugandhar1,*,†\dagger, Hao Zhang1,*,†\dagger, Alison Gunzler1, Yuzhe Wang2 Thomas Thebaud2, Georgi Tinchev3, Venkatesh Ravichandran4, Laureano Moro-Velázquez2,†\dagger * Equal contribution.  †\dagger Corresponding author.
摘要Abstract

随着语音人工智能代理的激增，理解对话交互中的情感夹带变得越来越重要。情绪夹带是由社会关系和对话环境塑造的，随着时间的推移影响情感协调。我们介绍了 DyadEE，这是一个用于二元语音交互中情绪夹带检测的数据集，包含情感夹带对话和合成交互，其中夹带通过伙伴交换和情绪重新合成而被破坏。我们进一步提出了 TRACE，一个窗口级框架，它将二元交互建模为源自情感微调 Whisper 表示的声学嵌入的有序序列，将每个样本视为交互轨迹而不是汇集的话语。 DyadEE 上的实验结果表明，结合对话上下文和关系信息可以改善情绪夹带检测，TRACE 的最佳准确率达到 97.01%。With the proliferation of speech AI agents, understanding emotional entrainment in conversational interaction has become increasingly important. Emotional entrainment is shaped by social relationships and conversational context, influencing affective coordination over time. We introduce DyadEE, a dataset for emotional entrainment detection in dyadic speech interactions, containing both emotionally entrained conversations and synthetic interactions where entrainment is disrupted through partner swapping and emotion resynthesis. We further propose TRACE, a window-level framework that models dyadic interaction as ordered sequences of acoustic embeddings derived from emotion fine-tuned Whisper representations, treating each sample as an interaction trace rather than pooled utterances. Experimental results on DyadEE show that incorporating conversational context and relationship information improves emotional entrainment detection, with TRACE achieving the best accuracy of 97.01%.

I 简介Introduction

夹带是对话中的两个参与者在交互过程中相互协调的自然倾向。 二元语音交互体现在多个维度，包括语速、词汇选择、韵律和情感适应Entrainment is the natural tendency of two participants in a conversation to align with each other over the course of an interaction. Dyadic speech interaction manifests across multiple dimensions, including speech rate, lexical choice, prosody, and affective adaptation[21, 10]。 其中，. Among these,情感夹带emotional entrainment，在互动过程中说话者之间情感状态的相互适应，很大程度上受到说话者之间和对话环境的关系的影响, the mutual adaptation of affective states between speakers during interaction, is strongly shaped by the relationship between speakers and the conversational context[8]。 在自然对话中，情感协调不仅限于镜像： 合作伙伴在亲和环境中情感上趋于一致，而在冲突、谈判或寻求支持的情况下，互补或监管反应则更为自然. In natural conversations, emotional alignment is not limited to mirroring: partners converge affectively in affiliative contexts, while complementary or regulatory responses are more natural in conflict, negotiation, or support-seeking scenarios[23]。 因此，最好将情绪夹带理解为关系条件和情境条件的过程，而不是与情境无关的单一全局相似性得分. Emotional entrainment is therefore best understood as a relationship-conditioned and context-conditioned process rather than a context-free single global similarity score[2].

理解情绪夹带对于对话式语音人工智能具有直接的实用价值。在语音到语音代理系统中，适合同伴交互的响应方式在临床医生-患者环境中可能不适合甚至不安全。因此，部署在情感敏感领域（例如陪伴、心理健康支持或专业援助）的模型必须使其韵律和情感行为适应其社会角色和不断变化的对话环境Understanding emotional entrainment has direct practical value for conversational speech AI. In speech-to-speech agent systems, a response style that is appropriate in peer interaction may be inappropriate or even unsafe in a clinician-patient setting. Models deployed in emotionally sensitive domains such as companionship, mental health support, or professional assistance must therefore adapt their prosodic and affective behavior to both their social role and the evolving conversational context[15, 18]。忽视这些因素的评估可能会产生误导性的结论，奖励违反特定角色规范的全球愉快的反应，并掩盖仅在特定交互环境下出现的失败. Evaluations that ignore these factors can produce misleading conclusions, rewarding globally pleasant responses that violate role-specific norms and masking failures that emerge only under particular interaction settings[17]。这显然需要在明确的情境和关系约束下评估情绪夹带的基准。. This creates a clear need for benchmarks that assess emotional entrainment under explicit contextual and relational constraints.

先前的工作探索了对话中情感协调的相关方面，包括 NLP 中的移情对齐、二元交互中的情感同步以及言语中的声韵律夹带Prior work has explored related aspects of affective coordination in conversation, including empathetic alignment in NLP, emotional synchrony in dyadic interaction, and acoustic–prosodic entrainment in speech[11],[22]。这些工作表明，对齐不仅取决于词汇内容，还取决于时间交互结构和更广泛的对话条件. These lines of work suggest that alignment is shaped not only by lexical content, but also by temporal interaction structure and broader conversational conditions[13, 6]。 在建模层面，先前的方法包括轻量级语音情感识别基线，例如 Emotion MLP. At the modeling level, prior approaches range from lightweight speech emotion recognition baselines such as Emotion MLP[7]远程二元交互模型，例如 DyadFormerto long-range dyadic interaction models such as DyadFormer[3]，但两者都不是为了评估明确的对话背景和社会关系约束下的情绪夹带而设计的。然而，据我们所知，先前的工作很少评估在对话情境和社会关系的共同约束下二元言语中的情感夹带。这种差距激发了我们的研究。, but neither is designed to evaluate emotional entrainment under explicit conversational context and social relationship constraints. However, to the best of our knowledge, prior work has rarely evaluated emotional entrainment in dyadic speech under the joint constraints of conversational context and social relationship. This gap motivates our study.

我们将情感夹带概述为交互的合理性：二元体是否表现出连贯的交互动态和情感流，而不仅仅是声学现实主义。我们将情绪夹带检测制定为双扬声器音频流上的二元分类任务，区分连贯的夹带和中断的交互。基于无缝交互语料库We outline emotional entrainment as interaction plausibility: whether a dyad exhibits coherent interaction dynamics and affective flow rather than mere acoustic realism. We formulate emotional entrainment detection as a binary classification task over two-speaker audio streams, distinguishing coherent entrainment from disrupted interaction. Built on the Seamless Interaction corpus[1]，我们的数据集包括关系标签、情感信息上下文、来自伙伴交换和情感矛盾的 TTS 再合成的中断二元组，以及用于工件鲁棒性训练的语音转换夹带二元组。我们将每个二元组建模为来自情感微调 Whisper 编码器的窗口级嵌入的时间序列，并表明关系和对话上下文对于捕获情感夹带都至关重要。, our dataset includes relationship labels, emotionally informative contexts, disrupted dyads from partner swapping and emotion-contradicting TTS resynthesis, and voice-converted entrained dyads for artifact-robust training. We model each dyad as a temporal sequence of window-level embeddings from an emotion-finetuned Whisper encoder, and show that relationship and conversational context are both crucial for capturing emotional entrainment.

我们的贡献是：(i) TRACE，一种鉴别器，将二元交互建模为以关系和上下文信号为条件的窗口级声学嵌入的时间序列；(ii) DyadEE，一个用于情感夹带检测的关系和上下文条件数据集，具有破坏夹带和语音转换增强的补充策略，以减少声学捷径；我们发布数据集和代码库以支持未来的研究。Our contributions are: (i) TRACE, a discriminator that models dyadic interaction as temporal sequences of window-level acoustic embeddings conditioned on relationship and context signals and (ii) DyadEE, a relationship- and context-conditioned dataset for emotional entrainment detection, with complementary strategies for disrupting entrainment and voice conversion augmentation to reduce acoustic shortcuts; We release both the dataset and codebase to support future research.111https://github.com/anonymoususer276/TRACE

Voice conversion is applied to one randomly selected speaker in each dyad, while the other speaker is left unchanged. This one-sided augmentation preserves a more partially original interaction while introducing speaker-level acoustic variation without altering the underlying conversational structure. Denoising is applied to recover cleaner speech from noisy recordings while retaining the original interaction pattern. The two augmentations are applied independently and may overlap. By introducing acoustic variation without changing the interaction itself, these augmented examples encourage the model to attend to emotional entrainment rather than speaker identity, channel conditions, or synthetic artifacts.

声学情感嵌入：Acoustic Emotion Embeddings:对于每个语音窗口，我们从情感微调的 Whisper-large-v3 编码器的最终隐藏层中提取密集的声学嵌入For each speech window, we extract a dense acoustic embedding from the final hidden layer of an emotion-finetuned whisper-large-v3 encoder[19]由 VoxProfile 套件提供provided by the VoxProfile suite[5]222预训练模型于 2026 年 1 月 8 日访问Pretrained model accessed on January 8th 2026。编码器经过效价、唤醒和优势预测的训练，使其能够捕获与情感相关的韵律和声学线索，例如短时间段上的语调、强度和频谱变化。 生成的窗口级嵌入根据时间二元序列排列，并用作 TRACE 的主要声学输入。这使得模型能够跟踪局部情感模式如何在交替的说话者窗口中演变，而不是依赖于单个话语级别的摘要。. The encoder is trained for valence, arousal, and dominance prediction, enabling it to capture affect-relevant prosodic and acoustic cues such as intonation, intensity, and spectral variation over short temporal segments. The resulting window-level embeddings are arranged according to the temporal dyadic sequence and serve as the primary acoustic input to TRACE. This allows the model to track how local affective patterns evolve across alternating speaker windows rather than relying on a single utterance-level summary.

背景和关系特征：Context and Relationship Features:除了声学线索之外，TRACE 还以两个二元级信号为条件：对话上下文和说话者关系。我们从说话者提示中导出会话上下文提示，并使用句子转换器 SBERT 将它们编码为固定维度的语义嵌入In addition to acoustic cues, TRACE conditions on two dyad-level signals: conversational context and speaker relationship. We derive the conversational context prompt from speaker prompts and encode them as fixed-dimensional semantic embeddings using a sentence-transformers SBERT[9]，它捕获有关交互设置的高级信息（例如道歉、分歧、寻求支持）。我们将关系类别（例如朋友、同事、浪漫伴侣）编码为一组关系标签上的单热向量，然后是可学习的嵌入层以获得密集的分类表示。与窗口级声学序列不同，这两个信号对于给定的二元组保持固定，并提供有关应解释情绪夹带的社会和情境约束的全局调节信息。, which captures high-level information about the interaction setting (e.g., apology, disagreement, support-seeking). We encode the relationship category (e.g., friends, coworkers, romantic partners) as a one-hot vector over the set of relationship labels, followed by a learnable embedding layer to obtain a dense categorical representation. Unlike the window-level acoustic sequence, these two signals remain fixed for a given dyad and provide global conditioning information about the social and situational constraints under which emotional entrainment should be interpreted.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 情绪诱导建模Emotional Entrainment Modeling

情感交互建模：Emotional Interaction Modeling:给定窗口级声学情感嵌入的时间序列，TRACE 使用 6 个 LLaMA 风格块的轻量级堆栈对二元交互进行建模Given the temporal sequence of window-level acoustic emotion embeddings, TRACE models dyadic interaction with a lightweight stack of 6 LLaMA-style blocks[4]。每个块都采用 RoPE 位置编码、RMSNorm 和具有 SwiGLU 激活的前馈层，同时用双向自注意力替换原始因果注意力，以捕获前后说话者窗口之间的依赖关系。这种设计更适合二元序列分类，其中情感夹带取决于分布在整个对话中的交互模式，而不仅仅是从左到右的生成。通过这些堆叠的块，TRACE 将局部声学情感线索转换为更高层次的表示，对整个二元体的交互动态和情感协调进行编码。. Each block adopts RoPE positional encoding, RMSNorm, and feed-forward layers with SwiGLU activation, while replacing the original causal attention with bidirectional self-attention to capture dependencies across both preceding and following speaker windows. This design is better suited to dyadic sequence classification, where emotional entrainment depends on interaction patterns distributed across the full conversation rather than left-to-right generation alone. Through these stacked blocks, TRACE transforms local acoustic emotion cues into higher-level representations that encode interaction dynamics and emotional coordination across the dyad.

特征整合与分类：Feature Integration and Classification:为了合并更高级别的社交和情境信息以改进情绪夹带检测，我们将序列级声学表示与上下文嵌入和关系嵌入连接起来。然后将所得特征向量输入多层感知器 (MLP) 分类头，该分类头会生成二进制预测（夹带与非夹带）以及校准概率。通过融合这些信号，TRACE 不仅可以根据交互驱动的声学动力学来评估情绪夹带，还可以在形成适当情绪协调的上下文和关系约束下评估情绪夹带。To incorporate higher-level social and situational information for improved emotional entrainment detection, we concatenate the sequence-level acoustic representation with the context embedding and the relationship embedding. The resulting feature vector is then fed into a multilayer perceptron (MLP) classification head, which produces a binary prediction (entrained vs. non-entrained) together with a calibrated probability. By fusing these signals, TRACE evaluates emotional entrainment not only from interaction-driven acoustic dynamics, but also under the contextual and relational constraints that shape appropriate emotional coordination.

V 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

在这项工作中，我们引入了 DyadEE（一个用于二元语音交互中的情感夹带检测的数据集）和 TRACE（一个将窗口级声学情感表示与上下文和关系信息相结合的强大时间框架）。 我们的结果表明，情绪夹带的建模不仅仅取决于语音信号，因为对话上下文和社会关系对于建模适当的情感协调都很重要。 尽管我们检测情绪夹带的方法已经产生了出色的性能，但非夹带条件是通过一组有限的合成转换来实例化的，这可能无法完全反映非夹带会话语音的多样性。 未来的工作应该用更自然和多样化的非夹带语音来扩展 DyadEE，并对二元分类之外的情感夹带进行建模，例如作为分级或随时间变化的现象。将 DyadEE 扩展到多模式环境、更广泛的社会背景以及跨文化或多语言场景，以及探索夹带感知模型如何改进会话语音代理也很有价值。In this work, we introduced DyadEE, a dataset for emotional entrainment detection in dyadic speech interaction, and TRACE, a strong temporal framework that combines window-level acoustic emotion representations with contextual and relationship information. Our results show that modeling emotional entrainment does not depend on the speech signal alone, as both conversational context and social relationship are important for modeling appropriate affective coordination. Although our approach detecting emotional entrainment has yielded outstanding performance, the non-entrained condition is instantiated via a limited set of synthetic transformations, which may not fully reflect the diversity of non-entrained conversational speech. Future work should expand DyadEE with more naturalistic and diverse non-entrained speech, and model emotional entrainment beyond binary classification, for example as a graded or time-varying phenomenon. It would also be valuable to extend DyadEE to multimodal settings, broader social contexts, and cross-cultural or multilingual scenarios, as well as to explore how entrainment-aware modeling can improve conversational speech agents.
