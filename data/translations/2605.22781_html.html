8. 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 DeltaBox，这是一个操作系统级可回滚沙箱，旨在加速 AI 代理工作负载，例如测试时树搜索和强化学习。 认识到后续代理状态高度相似，DeltaBox 避免完全状态复制，转而支持基于差异的检查点和恢复。为了实现这一目标，DeltaFS 为文件系统 C/R 启用动态、免卸载的 Overlayfs 层切换，而 DeltaCR 利用增量转储和热模板分叉来进行内存 C/R。We present DeltaBox, an OS-level rollbackable sandbox designed to accelerate AI agent workloads such as test-time tree search and reinforcement learning. Recognizing that subsequent agent states are highly similar, DeltaBox eschews full state duplication in favor of diff-based checkpoint and restore. To achieve this, DeltaFS enables dynamic, unmount-free overlayfs layer switching for filesystem C/R, while DeltaCR utilizes incremental dumps and warm-template forking for memory C/R.
