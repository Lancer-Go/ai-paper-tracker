1 简介Introduction

深入研究具有挑战性，不仅因为相关证据难以找到，而且因为有效的答案通常必须同时满足多个耦合约束。 代理必须发现可行的候选者，整合分布式和潜在冲突的证据，并验证每个所需条件是否得到充分支持Deep research is challenging not only because relevant evidence is difficult to locate, but because a valid answer must often satisfy multiple coupled constraints simultaneously. An agent must discover viable candidates, integrate distributed and potentially conflicting evidence, and verify that each required condition is adequately supported(react; webgpt; deepresearch_survey)。 许多现有系统通过附加推理、工具交互或上下文扩展单个搜索轨迹来解决这一挑战. Many existing systems address this challenge by extending a single search trajectory with additional reasoning, tool interactions, or context(deepresearch; searchr1; webdancer; beyond_ten_turns)。尽管更大的推理时间计算可以拓宽探索范围，但它并不能保证系统性进展：早期错误可能会持续存在，可能会重新审视用尽的方向，并且可能会过早地接受部分有效的候选者。因此，关键的挑战不仅仅是更长的搜索时间，而是确定哪些约束仍未解决，并利用该诊断来制定更有针对性的下一个研究问题。. Although greater inference-time computation can broaden exploration, it does not guarantee systematic progress: early errors may persist, exhausted directions may be revisited, and partially valid candidates may be accepted prematurely. The key challenge is therefore not merely to search longer, but to identify which constraints remain unresolved and use that diagnosis to formulate a more targeted next research problem.

我们观察到深入的研究任务表现出一个基本的We observe that deep research tasks exhibit a fundamental发现-验证不对称discovery–verification asymmetry。发现共同满足所有约束的答案的成本很高，因为它需要导航大型且信息稀疏的搜索空间，而评估提议的候选者通常可以分解为更简单的约束检查。 这种验证不仅可以揭示当前答案是否正确，还可以揭示哪些主张得到支持，哪些主张尚未解决，以及现有证据在哪些地方存在冲突。 现有工作利用验证来对已完成的候选轨迹进行排名或在正在进行的轨迹中完善决策. Discovering an answer that jointly satisfies all constraints is costly because it requires navigating a large and sparsely informative search space, whereas evaluating a proposed candidate can often be decomposed into substantially simpler constraint-wise checks. Such verification reveals not only whether the current answer is correct, but also which claims are supported, which remain unresolved, and where the available evidence conflicts. Existing work leverages verification either to rank completed candidate trajectories or to refine decisions within an ongoing trajectory(zeng2025pushing; team2026mirothinker)。 我们的主要见解是验证还可以定义研究轮次之间的过渡。通过将临时答案转换为部分验证的状态，智能体可以保留既定的进展，隔离剩余的不确定性，并制定更有针对性的下一个研究问题。. Our key insight is that verification can also define the transition between research rounds. By converting a provisional answer into a partially verified state, the agent can preserve established progress, isolate the remaining uncertainty, and formulate a more targeted next research problem.

4.1 多阶段代理中期训练Multi-stage Agentic Mid-training

长期深度研究需要异构能力的组合，包括工具调用、网络导航、证据获取、专家级推理、上下文跟踪和答案合成。 直接在单个混合分布上训练模型可能会导致不稳定的学习动态和不同能力类型之间的干扰。 因此，我们采用多阶段代理中期训练方法，逐步提高模型的研究能力。Long-horizon deep research requires a combination of heterogeneous capabilities, including tool invocation, web navigation, evidence acquisition, expert-level reasoning, context tracking, and answer synthesis. Constraint-wise auditing converts a provisional answer into a partially verified research state: supported claims are preserved, unresolved conditions are isolated, and contradictory evidence is surfaced. The next round of research is therefore driven by the structure of remaining uncertainty, not by undirected continuation of search.

研究状态管理和长期培训。Research-State Management and Long-Horizon Training.

持续的研究要求代理人在包含证据、失败的搜索、推测性假设和不断发展的计划的长轨迹中维护有用的信息。之前的工作研究了分层记忆、虚拟上下文管理、学习记忆状态、定期总结和显式记忆编辑操作Sustained research requires agents to maintain useful information across long trajectories containing evidence, failed searches, speculative hypotheses, and evolving plans. Prior work studies hierarchical memory, virtual context management, learned memory states, periodic summarization, and explicit memory-editing actions(memgpt; hiagent; mem1; resum; supo; memory_as_action). 阿雷克斯AREX在此方向的基础上，通过学习调用上下文更新工具，该工具产生围绕当前研究目标组织的改进状态，包括经过验证的证据、引用、约束状态、未解决的差距和下一个计划。训练此类代理也会带来稀疏的信用分配挑战，因为最终的成功很少揭示哪些中间步骤是决定性的。受到流程监督和轮级信用分配的启发builds on this direction by learning to invoke a context-update tool that produces an improvement state organized around the current research objective, including verified evidence, citations, constraint status, unresolved gaps, and the next plan. Training such agents also raises sparse credit-assignment challenges, since final success rarely reveals which intermediate steps were decisive.
