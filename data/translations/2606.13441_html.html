为什么采样不是选择：大型语言模型中的意向性、能动性和道德责任Why Sampling Is Not Choosing: Intentionality, Agency, and Moral Responsibility in Large Language Models

Joseph Keshet
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）的最新进展促使人们声称此类系统表现出代理作用或有资格成为道德代理。本文认为这些归因是错误的。我们认为，道德责任需要基于内在意向和自我归因行动的承担承诺的能动性，并且这种能动性构成了与责任相关的自由意志的形式。尽管法学硕士生成连贯且规范可评估的输出，但其操作完全由从数据中学习的概率输入输出映射来表征。他们表面上的意向是衍生出来的，而不是内在的，他们的产出既不属于承诺，也不受理由指导。随机抽样带来的变异并不等于选择或作者身份。我们解决了来自意向立场、功能主义、相容主义以及模型输出中道德推理的存在的反对意见，认为这些都不足以建立真正的能动性。Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have prompted claims that such systems exhibit agency or qualify as moral agents. This paper argues that these attributions are misguided. We maintain that moral responsibility requires commitment-bearing agency grounded in intrinsic intentionality and self-attributed action, and that such agency constitutes the form of free will relevant to responsibility. Although LLMs generate coherent and normatively evaluable outputs, their operation is fully characterized by probabilistic input-output mappings learned from data. Their apparent intentionality is derived rather than intrinsic, and their outputs are neither owned as commitments nor guided by reasons. Variability introduced by stochastic sampling does not amount to choice or authorship. We address objections from the intentional stance, functionalism, compatibilism, and the presence of moral reasoning in model outputs, arguing that none suffice to establish genuine agency.

2 概念框架Conceptual Framework

我们首先定义本文中使用的关键术语。关于意向性及其在奠定心理状态中的作用的定义遵循由We begin by defining the key terms used in this paper. The definitions concerning intentionality and its role in grounding mental states follow the general framework developed bySearle (1983).

意向性。Intentionality.意向性是指某些心理状态的特征，这些心理状态通过这些特征指向或关于对象、事态或命题。这个概念起源于布伦塔诺对心理现象“有意不存在”的表述Intentionality refers to the characteristic feature of certain mental states whereby they are directed toward, or are about, objects, states of affairs, or propositions. This notion, originating in Brentano’s formulation of the “intentional inexistence” of mental phenomena(Brentano, 1995)，并在现象学中进一步发展, and further developed in phenomenology(Husserl, 1983)，抓住了信念、欲望、感知和判断总是, captures the idea that beliefs, desires, perceptions, and judgments are alwaysof or 关于about某事。因此，意向性不仅仅是表象结构，而且是主体与呈现给该主体的有意义的客体之间的关系。something. Intentionality is thus not merely representational structure, but a relation between a subject and a meaningful object as presented to that subject.

关于性Aboutness是意向性的核心特征，指的是一种状态对某一对象或内容的指向性。这就是意向状态与纯粹物理过程的区别。在典型案例中，关于性不仅涉及参考，还涉及有意义的呈现，其中对象被指定为某种东西（例如，威胁性的、令人向往的或真实的）。这一方面是现象学叙述的核心，其中关于性与向主体揭示对象的方式密不可分。is the core feature of intentionality, referring to the directedness of a state toward an object or content. It is what distinguishes intentional states from purely physical processes. In paradigmatic cases, aboutness involves not only reference but also meaningful presentation, where the object is given as something (e.g., as threatening, desirable, or true). This aspect is central in phenomenological accounts, where aboutness is inseparable from the way objects are disclosed to a subject.

在这里，我们根据意义的来源来区分两种形式的意向性。Here we distinguish between two forms of intentionality based on the source of their meaning.内在意向性Intrinsic intentionality指的是系统本身固有的、不依赖于外部解释的一种有关性形式。在这种情况下，意向状态对于接受它的主体来说是有意义的。相比之下，refers to a form of aboutness that is inherent to a system itself and does not depend on external interpretation. In such cases, the intentional state has meaning for the subject that entertains it. By contrast,派生意向性derived intentionality指的是一种并非系统固有的、而是由外部代理、用户或解释实践归因于系统的有关性形式。在这些情况下，与系统状态相关的任何含义都是外部强加的，而不是源自系统本身。refers to a form of aboutness that is not inherent to the system but is attributed to it by external agents, users, or interpretive practices. In these cases, any meaning associated with the system’s states is imposed from outside rather than originating within the system itself.

内在意向性和派生意向性之间的区别可以通过对比例子来阐明。首先考虑人类的感知判断：当主体看到院子里有一只狗时，就会形成这样的想法：“院子里有一只狗。”这种思想是针对一个对象的，具有确定的内容，并且可以评估真假。如果没有狗在场，则主题是错误的。至关重要的是，思想的内容对于主题本身是有意义的；相关性是认知状态的内在特征。这构成了内在意向性的一个范例。The distinction between intrinsic and derived intentionality can be clarified through contrastive examples. Consider first a human perceptual judgment: upon seeing a dog in a yard, a subject forms the thought, “There is a dog in the yard.” This thought is directed toward an object, has determinate content, and is assessable for truth or falsity. If no dog is present, the subject is mistaken. Crucially, the content of the thought is meaningful for the subject itself; the aboutness is internal to the cognitive state. This constitutes a paradigm case of intrinsic intentionality.

相比之下，考虑显示“22°C”的温度计。虽然读数似乎与环境温度有关，但设备无法体验或理解温度，也不会接受对其有意义的表示。如果读数不正确，则设备不是错误而是发生故障。温度计输出的明显变化取决于用户和设计者的外部解释。在这种情况下，意向性并不是系统固有的，而是源自人类的解释实践By contrast, consider a thermometer displaying “22°C.” While the reading appears to be about the ambient temperature, the device does not experience or understand temperature, nor does it entertain a representation that is meaningful for it. If the reading is incorrect, the device is not mistaken but malfunctioning. The apparent aboutness of the thermometer’s output depends on external interpretation by users and designers. In this case, intentionality is not intrinsic to the system but derived from human interpretive practices(Searle, 1980; Harnad, 1990).

规范性和道德责任。Normativity and Moral Responsibility. 规范性Normativity涉及正确性、适当性或义务方面的评估领域。一个系统或行动是规范性的，因为它可以根据标准或规则进行评估concerns the domain of evaluation in terms of correctness, appropriateness, or obligation. A system or action is normative insofar as it can be assessed relative to standards or rules(Brandom, 1994)。区分薄弱的规范性和较强的道德规范性很重要。弱规范性可以适用于任何可以评估其输出的系统（例如，预测的正确性），而更强的形式涉及问责制和理由。. It is important to distinguish between thin normativity and stronger moral normativity. Thin normativity may apply to any system whose outputs can be evaluated (e.g., correctness of a prediction), whereas stronger forms involve accountability and justification.

道德责任Moral responsibility是一种强有力的规范性形式，涉及对代理人的赞扬、责备和责任。它预设代理可以被理解为有意义的行为的作者，能够认可、修改行为并对行为负责is a robust form of normativity involving the attribution of praise, blame, and accountability to an agent. It presupposes that the agent can be understood as the author of its actions in a meaningful sense, capable of endorsing, revising, and being answerable for them(Strawson, 1963; Williams, 1985)。因此，道德责任需要的不仅仅是因果参与；它涉及由理由、承诺和自我归因能力构成的能动性。. Moral responsibility thus requires more than mere causal participation; it involves agency structured by reasons, commitments, and the capacity for self-attribution.

自由意志。Free Will.自由意志，在与道德责任相关的意义上，是指主体以不能仅归结为外部因果关系的方式决定其行为的能力。在存在主义的解释中，这包括超越给定条件、在可能性中进行选择以及致力于采取行动的能力Free will, in the sense relevant to moral responsibility, refers to the capacity of an agent to determine its actions in a manner that is not reducible to external causation alone. In existentialist accounts, this includes the ability to transcend given conditions, to select among possibilities, and to commit to a course of action(Sartre, 1943).

3 论点The Argument

本文的核心论点可以表述如下：The core argument of this paper can be stated as follows:

  1. 1.

    道德责任需要承担承诺的机构。Moral responsibility requires commitment-bearing agency.

  2. 2.

    承担承诺的机构需要内在的意向性和自我归因行动的能力。Commitment-bearing agency requires intrinsic intentionality and the capacity for self-attributed action.

  3. 3.

    这种能动性构成了与道德责任相关的自由意志的一种形式。Such agency constitutes a form of free will in the sense relevant to moral responsibility.

  4. 4.

    基于变压器的系统缺乏内在的意向性和自我归因行动的能力，因此缺乏相关意义上的自由意志。Transformer-based systems lack intrinsic intentionality and the capacity for self-attributed action, and thus lack free will in the relevant sense.

  5. 5.

    因此，基于变压器的系统不适合承担道德责任。Therefore, transformer-based systems are not appropriate subjects of moral responsibility.

第一个前提指出，道德责任需要承担承诺的机构。这反映了当代行动哲学中广泛认同的观点：责任不适当地归因于单纯的事件或因果过程，而是归因于其行为表达承诺、评估立场和对规范的响应的代理人The first premise states that moral responsibility requires commitment-bearing agency. This reflects a broadly shared view in contemporary philosophy of action: responsibility is not appropriately attributed to mere events or causal processes, but to agents whose actions express commitments, evaluative stances, and responsiveness to norms(Fischer and Ravizza, 1998; Watson, 2004)。让代理人承担责任就是将其行为视为可以负责、证明或否认的事情。例如，当一个人故意欺骗另一个人时，该行为可归因于代理人表达了对行动方针的承诺，无论多么有缺陷，因此可以受到指责。相比之下，如果一个人在癫痫发作期间造成伤害，这种行为并不表达这种承担责任的机构，并且通常不会以同样的方式受到道德评价。. To hold an agent responsible is to regard its action as something it can answer for, justify, or repudiate. For example, when an individual deliberately deceives another, the act is attributable to the agent as expressing a commitment, however defective, to a course of action and is thus open to blame. By contrast, if a person causes harm during an epileptic seizure, the behavior does not express such commitment-bearing agency and is not typically subject to moral appraisal in the same way.

第二个前提认为承担承诺的机构需要The second premise maintains that commitment-bearing agency requires内在意向性intrinsic intentionality和and the自我归因行动的能力capacity for self-attributed action。从相关意义上讲，承诺并不会因外在行为而耗尽；它涉及主体将其行为视为自己的行为并将其理解为具有规范意义的能力. Commitment, in the relevant sense, is not exhausted by outward behavior; it involves the agent’s capacity to take its actions as its own and to understand them as bearing normative significance(Velleman, 2000; Bratman, 2007)。这需要对代理本身有意义的有意状态，而不仅仅是观察者可以解释的状态。换句话说，承担承诺的能动性取决于内在的意向性，而不仅仅是派生的意向性：主体必须获得相关的相关性，而不仅仅是从外部解释者的角度来看。. This requires intentional states that are meaningful for the agent itself, rather than merely interpretable by observers. Put differently, commitment-bearing agency depends on intrinsic rather than merely derived intentionality: the relevant aboutness must obtain for the agent, not only from the standpoint of an external interpreter.

例如，当一个人形成遵守承诺的意图时，该意图就会以代理人可以理解的方式指向未来的行动，作为其致力于做的事情。代理人随后可以识别与该承诺相关的成功或失败，并可以承认或否认它作为自己的承诺。相比之下，仅仅导出明显意向性的系统可能会表现出可解释为类似承诺的行为，但缺乏做出或认可此类承诺的任何立场。For instance, when a person forms an intention to keep a promise, the intention is directed toward a future action in a way that is intelligible to the agent as something it is committed to doing. The agent can subsequently recognize success or failure relative to that commitment, and can acknowledge or repudiate it as its own. By contrast, systems whose apparent intentionality is merely derived may exhibit behavior that is interpretable as commitment-like, yet lack any standpoint from which such commitments are undertaken or recognized.

第三个前提的特点是The third premise characterizes自由意志free will扎根于这样的机构。这种解释没有将自由意志视为一种独立的形而上学能力，而是将其理解为由责任所需的代理构成，即有理由行动的能力，将一个人的行为视为自己的行为，并对它们实行某种形式的控制的能力as grounded in such agency. Rather than treating free will as an independent metaphysical capacity, this account understands it as constituted by the kind of agency required for responsibility, namely, the ability to act for reasons, to regard one’s actions as one’s own, and to exercise a form of control over them(Kane, 2005; Fischer, 2006)。根据这种观点，自由意志并不依赖于非决定论本身，而是依赖于理性——反应性和行动的所有权。例如，一个在相互竞争的原因之间进行深思熟虑并按照他们认为是最佳行动方案采取行动的人，在与责任相关的意义上行使了自由意志。因此，重点是作者身份和理性控制，而不仅仅是不可预测性或缺乏因果决定性。. On this view, free will does not depend on indeterminism per se, but on reasons-responsiveness and ownership of action. For example, a person who deliberates between competing reasons and acts on what they judge to be the best course of action exercises free will in the sense relevant to responsibility. The emphasis is thus on authorship and rational control, rather than mere unpredictability or absence of causal determination.

第四个前提认为，基于变压器的系统缺乏内在的意向性和自我归因行动的能力，因此缺乏相关意义上的自由意志。这一主张是基于此类系统的架构和功能特征。The fourth premise argues that transformer-based systems lack intrinsic intentionality and the capacity for self-attributed action, and thus lack free will in the relevant sense. This claim is grounded in the architectural and functional characteristics of such systems.

基于 Transformer 的模型实现了高度结构化的输入输出映射，其中通过一系列学习的参数操作将输入标记序列转换为输出标记序列Transformer-based models implement a highly structured input–output mapping in which a sequence of input tokens is transformed into a sequence of output tokens through a series of learned parametric operations(Vaswani et al., 2017)。在生成的每个步骤中，模型使用应用于内部表示的 softmax 函数来计算可能的下一个标记的概率分布。最终输出是通过从此分布中采样而产生的。实践中使用了各种采样策略，包括贪婪解码（选择最高概率的标记）、温度缩放（调制分布锐度）、top-. At each step of generation, the model computes a probability distribution over possible next tokens using a softmax function applied to internal representations. The final output is produced by sampling from this distribution. Various sampling strategies are used in practice, including greedy decoding (selecting the highest-probability token), temperature scaling (modulating distribution sharpness), top-kk抽样（限制选择sampling (restricting selection to thekk最可能的标记）和核心（top-most probable tokens), and nucleus (top-pp）采样（从累积概率超过阈值的最小标记集中进行选择）) sampling (selecting from the smallest set of tokens whose cumulative probability exceeds a threshold)(Holtzman et al., 2020)。这些机制引入了可变性，使得相同的提示可能在运行中产生不同的输出。然而，这种可变性反映了从学习分布中的随机选择，而不是系统审议、评估替代方案或致力于特定结果的过程。. These mechanisms introduce variability such that identical prompts may yield different outputs across runs. However, this variability reflects stochastic selection from a learned distribution rather than a process in which the system deliberates, evaluates alternatives, or commits to a particular outcome.

在训练过程中，基于 Transformer 的模型经过优化，可以在给定先前上下文的情况下预测下一个标记，通常使用大量人工生成文本的语料库。该过程采用During training, transformer-based models are optimized to predict the next token given preceding context, typically using large corpora of human-generated text. This process employs老师强迫teacher forcing，模型以之前的真实标记而不是其自己的输出为条件。结果，它学会了近似令牌序列的条件概率分布，而无需自主生成或迭代自校正。在训练期间不使用采样，仅在推理时应用。, whereby the model is conditioned on ground-truth previous tokens rather than its own outputs. As a result, it learns to approximate the conditional probability distribution of token sequences without autonomous generation or iterative self-correction.Sampling is not used during training and is applied only at inference.目标函数不编码语义理解或真值条件，而是编码语言使用的统计规律The objective function does not encode semantic understanding or truth conditions, but statistical regularities in language use。因此，模型的能力来自于模式识别，而不是对意义的理解。. Consequently, the model’s competence arises from pattern recognition rather than any grasp of meaning.

索绪尔Saussure(2011)强调能指和所指之间的关系是任意的，并且是通过符号系统内的差异而不是通过与外部现实的内在联系来维持的。 Transformer 模型在这种差异结构的层面上精确运行，捕获标记之间的统计关系，而无需访问系统之外的指称或含义。从这个意义上说，他们的输出体现了符号操纵的纯粹关系形式，与派生但不内在的意向性一致。emphasizes that the relation between signifier and signified is arbitrary and sustained by differences within a system of signs rather than by intrinsic connections to external reality. Transformer models operate precisely at the level of such differential structures, capturing statistical relations among tokens without access to referents or meanings beyond the system. In this sense, their outputs exemplify a purely relational form of sign manipulation, consistent with derived but not intrinsic intentionality.

Searle (1980)认为根据句法规则操纵形式符号不足以实现真正的理解或内在意向性。 Transformer 系统体现了这种区别：它们根据学习的统计规则处理和生成符号序列，但没有证据表明这些过程伴随着语义理解或系统本身有意义的意图状态。argues that the manipulation of formal symbols according to syntactic rules is insufficient for genuine understanding or intrinsic intentionality. Transformer systems instantiate this distinction: they process and generate symbol sequences according to learned statistical rules, yet there is no evidence that these processes are accompanied by semantic comprehension or meaningful intentional states for the system itself.

最后，Finally,Harnad (1990)提出了符号基础问题，以强调解释纯粹的符号系统如何获得不依赖于外部解释的意义的困难。 Transformer 模型专门针对文本数据进行训练，在封闭的符号域中运行，其中令牌仅根据与其他令牌的关系进行定义。如果没有感觉运动或与世界的体验交互的基础，就没有任何机制可以使意义成为系统的内在特征。formulates the symbol grounding problem to highlight the difficulty of explaining how purely symbolic systems can acquire meaning that is not parasitic on external interpretation. Transformer models, trained exclusively on textual data, operate within a closed symbolic domain where tokens are defined only in relation to other tokens. Without grounding in sensorimotor or experiential interaction with the world, there is no mechanism by which meanings could become intrinsic to the system.

这种关注延伸到了多模态法学硕士（尽管形式有所修改），它将文本处理与视觉或听觉编码器集成在一起，以产生基于多种输入模态的上下文敏感输出。实证研究表明，它们的基础仍然有限，并且经常表现出由于模态整合不平衡而产生的幻觉，其中文本表示主导生成过程，而视觉或听觉输入未得到充分利用This concern extends, albeit in a modified form, to multimodal LLMs, which integrate textual processing with visual or auditory encoders to produce context-sensitive outputs grounded in multiple input modalities. Empirical studies suggest that their grounding remains limited, and often exhibit hallucinations arising from imbalances in modality integration, where textual representations dominate the generation process while visual or auditory inputs are underutilized(Liu et al., 2024; Zou et al., 2024; Hsu et al., 2025; Asadi et al., 2026)。因此，即使存在非文本输入，模型的输出也主要反映内部统计关联，而不是真正扎根的语义内容，这强化了这样的说法：其明显的意向性仍然是派生的，而不是内在的。. As a result, even when non-textual inputs are present, the model’s outputs reflect predominantly internal statistical associations rather than genuinely grounded semantic content, reinforcing the claim that their apparent intentionality remains derived rather than intrinsic.

与内在意向性的缺失密切相关的是自我归因行动的缺失。承担承诺的代理机构要求代理人能够将其行为视为自己的行为，将其视为其承诺的表达并对其负责。基于变压器的系统缺乏这种能力。他们不具备统一的视角来生成认可或否认的输出。相反，每个输出都是不涉及自引用或所有权的计算结果。Closely related to the absence of intrinsic intentionality is the absence of self-attributed action. Commitment-bearing agency requires that an agent be able to take its actions as its own, recognizing them as expressions of its commitments and being answerable for them. Transformer-based systems lack this capacity. They do not possess a unified perspective from which outputs are generated as endorsed or disavowed. Instead, each output is the result of a computation that does not involve self-reference or ownership.

概率抽样的作用进一步强调了这一点。 Transformer 系统采用随机机制，因此相同的提示可能会在运行过程中产生不同的输出。虽然这种可变性表面上可能类似于替代方案中的选择，但它并不等于代理或自由意志。随机生成应与代理区别开来：随机性引入了不确定性，而不是作者身份；产出的产生不涉及认可或承诺；并且没有证据表明第一人称视角或行为的自我归因。至关重要的是，The role of probabilistic sampling further underscores this point. Transformer systems employ stochastic mechanisms such that identical prompts may yield different outputs across runs. While this variability might superficially resemble choice among alternatives, it does not amount to agency or free will. Stochastic generation should be distinguished from agency: randomness introduces indeterminacy, not authorship; output generation does not involve endorsement or commitment; and there is no evidence of a first-person perspective or self-attribution of action. Crucially,自由意志并不等于随机性或不确定性；它涉及作者身份和责任free will is not equivalent to randomness or indeterminacy; it involves authorship and responsibility。从这个意义上说，一个行动是自由的，不是因为它是不可预测的，而是因为它可以归因于主体本身。. An action is free in this sense not because it is unpredictable, but because it is attributable to the agent as its own.

这种特征延伸到This characterization extends to推理模型reasoning models，通过中间步骤增强标准生成，例如思想链提示、自我一致性或对候选输出的基于树的搜索, which augment standard generation with intermediate steps such as chain-of-thought prompting, self-consistency, or tree-based search over candidate outputs(Wei et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023)。虽然此类方法可能通过生成多个候选延续、探索替代解决方案路径或根据启发式标准在其中进行选择来产生类似于结构化推理的输出，但它们并没有因此实例化相关意义上的审议。中间步骤的生成仍然由相同的底层机制控制：根据学习的统计模式生成令牌序列。同样，替代方案的选择通常基于可能性、一致性或外部定义的评分程序，而不是基于对原因的识别和评估。例如，一个模型可能会为一个问题产生多个候选解释，并选择最常见或内部一致的解释，但它不会因此将这些替代方案理解为相互竞争的原因或将其中一个解释为自己的结论。由此产生的行为可能会模拟推理的各个方面，但它不涉及作者身份、承诺或原因响应控制。因此，即使在推理增强模型中，输出生成的过程仍然是统计选择的形式，而不是真正的深思熟虑。. While such methods may give rise to outputs that resemble structured reasoning by generating multiple candidate continuations, exploring alternative solution paths, or selecting among them according to heuristic criteria, they do not thereby instantiate deliberation in the relevant sense. The generation of intermediate steps remains governed by the same underlying mechanism: the production of token sequences according to learned statistical patterns. Likewise, the selection among alternatives is typically based on likelihood, consistency, or externally defined scoring procedures, rather than on the recognition and evaluation of reasons as reasons. For example, a model may produce several candidate explanations for a problem and select the most frequent or internally consistent one, yet it does not thereby understand those alternatives as competing reasons or endorse one as its own conclusion. The resulting behavior may simulate aspects of reasoning, but it does not involve authorship, commitment, or reasons-responsive control. Accordingly, even in reasoning-augmented models, the processes underlying output generation remain forms of statistical selection rather than genuine deliberation.

这些特征共同表明，基于变压器的系统缺乏与道德责任相关的自由意志所需的结构条件。如果缺乏内在的意向性和自我归因的行动，就没有作者身份、承诺或原因响应控制的基础。因此，虽然此类系统生成可接受规范评估的输出，但它们不具备作为能够自由行动的主体的资格。These features jointly indicate that transformer-based systems lack the structural conditions required for free will in the sense relevant to moral responsibility. In the absence of intrinsic intentionality and self-attributed action, there is no basis for authorship, commitment, or reasons-responsive control. Accordingly, while such systems generate outputs that are open to normative evaluation, they do not qualify as agents capable of free action.

4 结论Conclusion

本文认为，尽管基于变压器的系统产生可以规范评估的输出，但它们不是道德责任的适当主体。其含义既有哲学意义，也有实践意义。这些系统的行为和部署的责任仍然由人类设计者、部署者、用户和机构框架承担，而不是系统本身。因此，调整或监管基于变压器的系统的努力不应被理解为将它们转变为道德主体，而应被理解为在人类规范实践中约束和指导其行为的机制。同样，虽然此类系统可能有助于人类决策，但它们不应被视为道德判断或责任的自主承担者。This paper has argued that, although transformer-based systems produce outputs that can be normatively evaluated, they are not appropriate subjects of moral responsibility. The implications are both philosophical and practical. Responsibility for the behavior and deployment of these systems remains with human designers, deployers, users, and institutional frameworks, rather than with the systems themselves. Accordingly, efforts to align or regulate transformer-based systems should not be understood as transforming them into moral agents, but as mechanisms for constraining and guiding their behavior within human normative practices. Likewise, while such systems may assist human decision-making, they should not be regarded as autonomous bearers of moral judgment or responsibility.

反对意见1：故意的立场。Objection 1: The Intentional Stance.可能会争论，如下It may be argued, followingDennett (1987)，如果一个系统可以被有效地视为具有信念和愿望，那么这就足以归因于代理。, that if a system can be fruitfully treated as having beliefs and desires, this suffices for attributing agency.

回复。Reply.这种归因是预测性和实用性的，而不是本体论的。意向立场解释了我们如何建模和预测行为，但它并没有证明系统具有内在意向性或承诺承担机构。处理系统Such attributions are predictive and pragmatic rather than ontological. The intentional stance explains how we model and anticipate behavior, but it does not establish that the system possesses intrinsic intentionality or commitment-bearing agency. Treating a system好像as if它有信念并不等于那些对系统本身有意义的信念，也不代表道德责任所需的作者身份。it has beliefs is not equivalent to those beliefs being meaningful for the system itself, nor does it ground the authorship required for moral responsibility.

反对意见2：功能主义。Objection 2: Functionalism.功能主义者可能会认为，心理状态是根据其因果作用而不是物理实现来个体化的，并且任何表现出适当功能组织的系统都会实例化相关的心理属性A functionalist might argue that mental states are individuated by their causal roles rather than by their physical realization, and that any system exhibiting the appropriate functional organization thereby instantiates the relevant mental properties(Putnam, 1967; Fodor, 1975).

回复。Reply.即使变压器系统实现了复杂的功能结构，目前的论点也针对缺乏内在意向性和自我归因行为。输入-输出行为层面的功能对等本身并不能证明系统的状态对其有意义或者其输出被视为承诺。如果没有这种内在的意义和作者身份，仅靠职能组织不足以奠定道德责任的基础。Even if transformer systems realize complex functional structures, the present argument targets the absence of intrinsic intentionality and self-attributed action. Functional equivalence at the level of input–output behavior does not by itself establish that the system’s states are meaningful for it or that its outputs are owned as commitments. Without such intrinsic significance and authorship, functional organization alone is insufficient to ground moral responsibility.

反对意见3：相容论。Objection 3: Compatibilism.相容论者可能会认为，自由意志并不需要非决定论，而是需要适当地引发行动，通常是通过对原因做出反应的机制A compatibilist might argue that free will does not require indeterminism, but rather that actions be appropriately caused, typically through mechanisms that are responsive to reasons(Fischer and Ravizza, 1998).

回复。Reply.目前的说法与这种观点是一致的。然而，即使在相容主义下，自由意志也要求主体对理性做出反应，并能够将其行为视为自己的行为。基于 Transformer 的系统不会评估原因、识别原因或将其输出认可为承诺。他们的行为受统计相关性而非原因响应控制的控制，因此甚至无法满足代理的相容性条件。The present account is compatible with this view. However, even under compatibilism, free will requires that an agent be responsive to reasons and capable of regarding its actions as its own. Transformer-based systems do not evaluate reasons, recognize them as such, or endorse their outputs as commitments. Their behavior is governed by statistical correlations rather than reasons-responsive control, and thus fails to satisfy even compatibilist conditions for agency.

反对意见 4：道德推理输出。Objection 4: Moral Reasoning Outputs.变压器系统能够产生复杂的道德论证，解决道德困境，并产生适合情境的规范判断。因此，可以说这样的系统表现出道德主体的推理特征。Transformer systems are capable of generating sophisticated moral arguments, engaging with ethical dilemmas, and producing contextually appropriate normative judgments. It may therefore be argued that such systems exhibit the kind of reasoning characteristic of moral agents.

回复。Reply.道德语言的产生并不意味着拥有道德能动性。虽然变压器系统可以通过再现学习的语言模式来模拟推理，但它们不会对它们生成的声明采取立场，也不承认这些声明具有约束力。例如，一个模型可能会断言在一种情况下“一个人应该信守承诺”，然后在另一种情况下为违背承诺提供理由，但没有任何能力协调这些输出或将其视为权威。因此，推理的出现是统计模式生成的产物，而不是判断的运用。推理模拟并不需要承诺，没有承诺就没有道德责任的基础。The production of moral language does not entail the possession of moral agency. While transformer systems can simulate reasoning by reproducing learned linguistic patterns, they do not take a stance toward the claims they generate, nor do they recognize those claims as binding. For example, a model may assert that “one ought to keep promises” in one context and later generate a justification for breaking promises in another, without any capacity to reconcile these outputs or to regard either as authoritative. The appearance of reasoning is thus a product of statistical pattern generation rather than the exercise of judgment. Simulation of reasoning does not entail commitment, and without commitment there is no basis for attributing moral responsibility.
