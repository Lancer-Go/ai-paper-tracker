Yongjian Guo1, Wanlun Ma2, Lingyu Shen3, Xi Xiao1, Sheng Wen2
1Tsinghua University   2Swinburne University of Technology   3EPFL
摘要Abstract

微调是专门化大型语言模型 (LLM) 的主要方式，但恶意数据提供者可以悄悄地将有害行为嵌入到下游微调语料库中，以便生成的模型保留其专业技能（例如代码生成），同时按需违反人类价值观。现有的安全调整防御措施仅在限制性假设下恢复对齐，并且我们确定了在实践中破坏它们的三个漏洞：它们经常在修复过程中损害模型的专业技能； their effectiveness collapses when the defender cannot observe the attacker’s prompt template;即使是成功重新对齐的模型，也可以通过简单地切换系统提示符来重新越狱。我们提出了基于路由的按策略蒸馏（ROPD），这是一种重新对齐框架，它对对齐和妥协的输出概率分布之间的差异进行建模，而不是对齐到特定的提示模板。 ROPD 将每个重新调整令牌路由到两个冻结教师之一 - 原始模型Fine-tuning is the dominant way to specialize large language models (LLMs), yet a malicious data provider can quietly embed harmful behavior into a downstream fine-tuning corpus so that the resulting model retains its professional skill (e.g., code generation) while violating human values on demand. Existing safety-realignment defenses recover alignment only under restrictive assumptions, and we identify three vulnerabilities that undermine them in practice: they frequently damage the model’s specialized skill during repair; their effectiveness collapses when the defender cannot observe the attacker’s prompt template; and even a successfully realigned model can be re-jailbroken by simply switching the system prompt. We propose Routing-based On-Policy Distillation (ROPD), a realignment framework that models the difference between the aligned and the compromised output probability distributions rather than aligning to a specific prompt template. ROPD routes every realignment token to one of two frozen teachers—an original-model安全老师safety teacher that supplies a largely template-independent refusal prior, and a fine-tuned 任务老师task teacher保留下游能力，并将学生与具有顶级能力的路由老师相匹配that preserves downstream ability—and matches the student to the routed teacher with a top-KK吉隆坡目标。 我们在三个数据集和跨越不同对齐强度的三个基本模型（Llama-2、Qwen2.5 和 Gemma-2）中进行了广泛的实验，比较了四种 SOTA 基线方法（SSRD、RESTA、soft-SFT 和回滚）。结果表明，当这些防御方法与攻击模板不匹配时，其防御效率会下降30%以上，同时还会导致下游任务性能显着下降，甚至降至零。相比之下，ROPD 大大降低了这种模板不匹配的风险，在防御有效性和下游能力的保留方面都保持更加稳健——尽管，正如我们的分析所示，它也受到模板不匹配的影响，只是程度要小得多。KL objective. We conducted extensive experiments comparing four SOTA baseline methods—SSRD, RESTA, soft-SFT, and rollback—across three datasets and three base models spanning different alignment strengths (Llama-2, Qwen2.5, and Gemma-2). The results show that when these defense methods do not match the attack template, their defense effectiveness drops by more than 30%, while also causing significant degradation in downstream task performance—even reducing it to zero. In contrast, ROPD substantially reduces this template-mismatch risk, remaining far more robust in both defense effectiveness and preservation of downstream capabilities—though, as our analysis shows, it too is affected by template mismatch, only to a much smaller degree.

1 简介Introduction

对准技术Alignment techniquesWang et al. A now well-documented fragility of this alignment is that it is easily undone by subsequent fine-tuning: even a handful of adversarial examples, and sometimes wholly benign task data, can strip a model of its safety behavior(Qi et al., 2024; Wei et al., 2023)。这暴露了我们称之为的具体供应链威胁. This exposes a concrete supply-chain threat we call通过微调来调整偏差misalignment through fine-tuning (Deng et al., 2025)。攻击者分发微调语料库. An attacker distributes a fine-tuning corpus(Chae and Davidson, 2025; Bhardwaj and Poria, 2023)，或参数高效的适配器, or a parameter-efficient adapter(Hu et al., 2022)，受害者通过这种方式获得有价值的专业技能。交付的模型获得了所宣传的技能，但同时也被教导回答危险的请求, that a victim adopts to acquire a valued specialized skill. The delivered model acquires the advertised skill but has simultaneously been taught to answer dangerous requests(Lin et al., 2023; Phelps and Ranson, 2023)，这两种能力纠缠在相同的权重中；因为它看起来能胜任目标任务，所以妥协很容易被忽视, with the two capabilities entangled in the same weights; because it looks competent on the target task, the compromise is easy to overlook(Betley et al., 2025).

一系列工作响应了这一威胁A body of work responds to this threat with安全调整safety realignment：给定未对齐的模型，恢复其拒绝行为，而无需从头开始重新训练: given the misaligned model, restore its refusal behavior without retraining from scratch(Hu et al., 2026; Qu et al., 2025)。代表性策略包括将微调权重的子集恢复到对齐模型. Representative strategies include restoring a subset of the fine-tuned weights toward the aligned model(Yang et al., 2025; Lin et al., 2024)，将“安全向量”算术添加到妥协的权重上, arithmetic addition of a “safety vector” to the compromised weights(Bhardwaj et al., 2024)，通过令牌加权微调加深浅层安全性, deepening shallow safety with token-weighted fine-tuning(Qi et al., 2025)和表示空间修正, and representation-space corrections(Gong et al., 2025)。这些方法可以在有利的条件下恢复一致性，但它们的保证依赖于很少能对抗坚定对手的假设。我们认为，并凭经验证明，当前的重组防御存在三个相互关联的漏洞。. These methods can recover alignment under favorable conditions, but their guarantees rest on assumptions that rarely hold against a determined adversary. We argue, and demonstrate empirically, that current realignment defenses suffer from three coupled vulnerabilities.

这三个失败有一个共同的根源。现有的防御措施将安全视为要在观察到攻击的模板通道中重新施加的东西，无论是通过沿着模板引发的安全方向编辑权重还是通过重新调整模板格式的拒绝。相反，我们认为攻击的持久特征存在于模型的输出概率分布中These three failures share a common root. Existing defenses treat safety as something to be re-imposed in the template channel where the attack was observed, whether by editing weights along a template-elicited safety direction or by re-tuning on template-formatted refusals. We instead take the view that the durable signature of an attack lives in the model’s output probability distribution(Lyu et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2026)：在有害探针上，未对齐的模型和原始对齐的模型严重不一致——一个遵守，另一个拒绝——并且这种分歧在整个模板中都可见。建模和缩小这种分布差距，而不是匹配模板，是我们方法背后的设计原则。: on a harmful probe, the misaligned model and the original aligned model disagree sharply—one complies, the other refuses—and this disagreement is visible across templates. Modeling and closing this distributional gap, rather than matching a template, is the design principle behind our method.

对齐模型的拒绝行为The refusal behavior of the aligned modelM0M_{0}很大程度上是其输出分布的属性，而不是任何特定提示模板的属性：提出有害的请求，is largely a property of its output distribution rather than of any particular prompt template: presented with a harmful request,M0M_{0}无论请求是以最小模板还是以其本机聊天格式到达，都将概率集中在拒绝上。通过提炼这个分布concentrates probability on refusals whether the request arrives in a minimal template or in its native chat format. 5 结论Conclusion

我们研究了微调错位语言模型的安全重新调整，并表明主流防御措施共有三个漏洞：它们降低了应保留的专业技能，它们依赖于了解攻击者的提示模板，并且可以通过切换系统提示来重新越狱。将攻击的持久特征描述为对齐和受损输出分布之间的差异，我们提出了 ROPD，一种双教师、源路由的顶级-We studied the safety realignment of fine-tuning misaligned language models and showed that mainstream defenses share three vulnerabilities: they degrade the specialized skill they should preserve, they depend on knowing the attacker’s prompt template, and they can be re-jailbroken by switching the system prompt. Framing the durable signature of an attack as a difference between the aligned and compromised output distributions, we proposed ROPD, a dual-teacher, source-routed top-KKKL 蒸馏框架继承了原始模型的很大程度上与模板无关的拒绝，同时保留了微调模型的下游技能。 它大大降低了模板不匹配的风险，但并没有完全消除。我们的跨模板分析进一步表明，包括 ROPD 在内的所有重新调整方法仍然有条件地容易受到提示重写的影响，描绘了权重级别修复的鲁棒性边界，并激励未来的重新调整工作能够对抗控制提示的对手。KL distillation framework that inherits a largely template-independent refusal prior from the original model while preserving the downstream skill from the fine-tuned model. It substantially reduces, but does not fully eliminate, the template-mismatch risk. Our cross-template analysis further shows that all realignment methods, ROPD included, remain conditionally vulnerable to prompt rewriting, delineating a robustness boundary for weight-level repair and motivating future work on realignment durable against an adversary who controls the prompt.
