1 简介Introduction

现代人工智能系统的公共评估报告是有选择性的和时间索引的：排行榜、基准发布、竞技场切片或代理任务报告通常会公开排名或最高的Public evaluation reporting for modern AI systems is selective and time-indexed: a leaderboard, benchmark release, arena slice, or agentic task report usually exposes a ranked or top-kk子集，同时隐藏尝试的、未发布的、修订的、搭建的或低于阈值的系统。代理报告还可以聚合提示、工具、规划器、判断器和环境设置，而不暴露执行跟踪。因此，最终排名是重复的公共历史的有损端点，而不是历史本身。subset while hiding attempted, unpublished, revised, scaffolded, or under-threshold systems. Agentic reports may also aggregate over prompts, tools, planners, judges, and environment settings without exposing the execution trace. A terminal ranking is therefore a lossy endpoint of a repeated public history, not the history itself.

这段历史是这里的主要技术对象。重复快照保留报告时间、基准版本、可见性规则以及区分证据更改和报告工件所需的元数据。我们专注于可从源本地版本重建的公共人工智能评估历史，包括客观基准记录、偏好排行榜和汇总代理结果报告。存档合约、可观察性边界和裁决协议一起使重复的公共评估记录可以在选择和基准漂移下进行分析：合约指定快照必须携带哪些元数据，边界将可从重复跟踪中恢复的内容与需要完整执行日志的内容分开，并且协议根据保留的观察结果来裁决候选推理方法。That history is the primary technical object here. Repeated snapshots retain report timing, benchmark version, visibility rule, and the metadata needed to distinguish evidential change from reporting artifact. We focus on public AI evaluation histories reconstructable from source-native releases, including objective benchmark records, preference leaderboards, and aggregate agentic-result reports. An archive contract, an observability boundary, and an adjudication protocol together make repeated public evaluation records analyzable under selection and benchmark drift: the contract specifies what metadata a snapshot must carry, the boundary separates what is recoverable from repeated traces from what requires full execution logs, and the protocol adjudicates candidate inference methods against held-out observations.

边界问题位于选择性推理和赢家诅咒修正的交叉点The boundary question sits at the intersection of selective inference and winner’s-curse correction(Zrnic and Fithian, 2025), 操作边界估计, operational frontier estimation(Liu et al., 2022; Einmahl and He, 2025)，以及治疗风格设置中的高原识别, and plateau identification in cure-style settings(Jackson et al., 2026; Yuen and Musta, 2026)。区分这种设置的具体证据属性是，重复选择的记录是主要对象，档案建设是科学主张的一部分，更强大的模型陈述必须通过固定的审计门，而不是从终端等级借用可信度。. The specific evidential property that distinguishes this setting is that repeated selected records are the primary object, archive construction is part of the scientific claim, and stronger model statements must pass fixed audit gates rather than borrowing credibility from a terminal rank.

因此，我们将档案级证据与模型认可分开。重复的公共历史可以被重建、分级、跨观察体系进行比较，并用于裁决候选推理方法。本工作中应用的选择感知前沿模型根据恢复、预测、转移和校准标准进行评估；该协议旨在区分满足这些标准的情况和不满足这些标准的情况，而不是将不受支持的前沿推断主张吸收到汇总排行榜叙述中。当前的候选者不符合这四个标准，从而确定了该档案和协议目前可以证明的范围。We therefore separate archive-level evidence from model endorsement. Repeated public histories can be reconstructed, graded, compared across observation regimes, and used to adjudicate candidate inference methods. The selection-aware frontier model applied in this work is evaluated against recovery, predictive, transfer, and calibration criteria; the protocol is designed to distinguish cases where those criteria are met from cases where they are not, rather than absorbing unsupported frontier-inference claims into aggregate leaderboard narratives. The current candidate meets none of those four criteria, establishing the scope of what this archive and protocol can certify at present.

2 相关工作Related Work

AI评测工作多次暴露出终端分数和静态排行榜的局限性。先前的工作认为最终测试数字应附有搜索和验证痕迹AI evaluation work has repeatedly exposed the limits of terminal scores and static leaderboards. Prior work argues that final test numbers should be accompanied by search and validation traces(Dodge et al., 2019)，排行榜排名可能与用户效用不同, that leaderboard rank can diverge from user utility(Ethayarajh and Jurafsky, 2020)，并且小的基准差异在统计上通常是脆弱的, and that small benchmark differences are often statistically fragile(Card et al., 2020)。基准修复工作通过强调有效性、余量、动态数据收集、诊断排行榜、整体多指标报告、污染检查、敏感性审计和显式饱和度分析，朝着同一方向推进. Benchmark-repair efforts push in the same direction by emphasizing validity, headroom, dynamic data collection, diagnostic leaderboards, holistic multi-metric reporting, contamination checks, sensitivity audits, and explicit saturation analysis(Bowman and Dahl, 2021; Kiela et al., 2021; Liu et al., 2021; Liang et al., 2023; Sainz et al., 2023; Alzahrani et al., 2024; Akhtar et al., 2026)。持续更新LiveBench等基准测试. Continuously updated benchmarks such as LiveBench(White et al., 2025)和开放 LLM 排行榜 v2and Open LLM Leaderboard v2(Open LLM Leaderboard, 2024)解决一些污染和维护问题，但支持临时存档声明需要这些格式目前不提供的源本地历史记录。address some contamination and maintenance issues, but supporting temporal archive claims requires source-native histories that these formats do not currently provide.

统计问题还与自适应数据分析、可靠的排行榜机制、选择后推断和赢家诅咒校正重叠The statistical concern also overlaps with adaptive data analysis, reliable leaderboard mechanisms, post-selection inference, and winner’s-curse correction(Dwork et al., 2015; Blum and Hardt, 2015; Roelofs et al., 2019; Berk et al., 2013; Andrews et al., 2024; Zrnic and Fithian, 2025)。这些文献解释了为什么选定的边境记录不应被解释为普通的固定估计。解决这一差距需要重建源本地公共痕迹，标记缺失的观察结果，并根据未来的观察结果测试候选推理方法，而不是将观察到的获胜者视为公正的目标。应用于选定公共记录的具有固定审计门的档案合同提供了在实践中实施这些统计要求所需的结构。. Those literatures explain why selected frontier records should not be interpreted as ordinary fixed estimates. Addressing this gap requires reconstructing source-native public traces, marking missing observations, and testing candidate inference methods against future observations rather than treating the observed winner as an unbiased target. An archive contract with fixed audit gates applied to selected public records provides the structure needed to operationalize these statistical requirements in practice.

几个基线系列是此存档设置的自然比较器。用于评估排行榜的项目响应和潜在能力模型将示例视为信息不均等，并将项目难度与系统技能分开Several baseline families are natural comparators for this archive setting. Item-response and latent-ability models for evaluation leaderboards treat examples as unequally informative and separate item difficulty from system skill(Lalor et al., 2016; Rodriguez et al., 2021; Vania et al., 2021)。尺度法则和能力外推工作提供了不同的前沿基线，同时也警告聚合指标可能会导致或隐藏明显的不连续性. Scaling-law and capability-extrapolation work provides a different frontier baseline while also warning that aggregate metrics can induce or hide apparent discontinuities(Kaplan et al., 2020; Hoffmann et al., 2022; Srivastava et al., 2023; Schaeffer et al., 2023)。偏好排行榜继承了 Bradley-Terry 和 Elo 风格的假设. Preference leaderboards inherit Bradley-Terry and Elo-style assumptions(Bradley and Terry, 1952; Elo, 1978)，而现代竞技场风格的评估增加了提示、人口、判断和发布时间的混乱, and modern Arena-style evaluations add prompt, population, judge, and release-timing confounds(Zheng et al., 2023; Chiang et al., 2024)。总之，这些基线促使将潜在效应/因子、终末历史、滚动前沿和 Bradley-Terry / Elo 方法视为比较器，而不是假设选择感知模型应该获胜。. Together, these baselines motivate treating latent-effect/factor, terminal-history, rolling-frontier, and Bradley-Terry / Elo methods as comparators instead of assuming a selection-aware model should win.

代理基准测试将评估从基本模型扩展到涉及工具、浏览、代码执行、环境、模拟器和判断的系统配置。 GAIA 和 tau-bench 是一般助理和工具-代理-用户交互评估的核心示例Agentic benchmarks extend evaluation from base models to system configurations involving tools, browsing, code execution, environments, simulators, and judges. GAIA and tau-bench are central examples for general-assistant and tool-agent-user interaction evaluation(Mialon et al., 2024; Yao et al., 2025)，而 SWE-bench、WebArena、AgentBench 和 ToolLLM 则说明了向基于执行和工具介导的任务的更广泛的转变, while SWE-bench, WebArena, AgentBench, and ToolLLM illustrate the broader move toward execution-grounded and tool-mediated tasks(Jimenez et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Qin et al., 2024)。这些基准系列揭示了任何涵盖代理任务的临时存档必须满足的元数据要求； GAIA 和 tau-bench 试点代表了早期的档案范围，而不是前沿模型排名的主要证据。. These benchmark families reveal the metadata requirements that any temporal archive covering agentic tasks must satisfy; the GAIA and tau-bench pilots represent early archive scope rather than primary evidence for frontier model ranking.
