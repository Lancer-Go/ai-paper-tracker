摘要。Abstract.

尽管过去几十年取得了显着的进步，但由于视觉线索不足，准确识别小物体仍然具有挑战性。以前的工作通常尝试构建小物体的判别表示。然而，大范围的频域噪声和标签模糊性被极大地忽视，这极大地阻碍了准确定位。为了解决这些问题，我们提出了一种名为 DyFrDet 的新型小物体检测（SOD）检测器，它能够通过动态抑制频域中的背景干扰来精确定位小物体。具体来说，我们提出了动态频率感知特征金字塔网络（DyFrFPN）来自适应抑制低频冗余和过多的高频噪声。 DyFrFPN 将层次特征转换为频域表示，并引入动态频带预测器 (DBP) 来保留小物体识别的判别分量。随后，我们提出了一种新颖的标签消歧模块（LDM），它利用概率分布来显式建模并减轻目标标签固有的歧义，从而有效提高低分辨率小物体的定位精度。大量实验表明 DyFrDet 在多个基准测试中实现了最先进的性能，表明其在各种具有挑战性的场景中的有效性和稳健性。我们的代码位于Despite the remarkable progress over the past decades, accurately identifying small objects remains challenging because of their insufficient visual cues. Previous works typically attempt to construct discriminative representation of the small objects. However, the wide range frequency domain noises and label ambiguities have been greatly overlooked, which significantly hinders the accurate localization. To address these issues, we propose a novel small object detection (SOD) detector termed DyFrDet, which is able to precisely localize the small object by dynamically suppressing the background distractions in frequency domain. Specifically, we propose a Dynamic Frequency-aware Feature Pyramid Network (DyFrFPN) to adaptively suppress low-frequency redundancy and excessive high-frequency noises. The DyFrFPN transforms the hierarchical features into frequency domain representation, and introduces a Dynamic Band Predictor (DBP) to preserve the discriminative components for small object identification. Afterwards, we present a novel Label Disambiguation Module (LDM), which leverages probabilistic distributions to explicitly model and alleviate the inherent ambiguity of target labels, yielding efficient improvement in localization precision of the small objects with low-resolution. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DyFrDet achieves state-of-the-art performance across multiple benchmarks, indicating its effectiveness and robustness in various challenging scenarios. 1. 简介Introduction

SOD plays an essential role in a wide range of real-world applications, including unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) surveillance, remote sensing localization, scene monitoring, obstacle avoidance, and autonomous driving. However, due to the lack of visual cues and complex scenarios, directly applying generic object detectors(Girshick et al., 2014; Ren et al., 2016; Liu et al., 2016; Lin et al., 2017b; Carion et al., 2020; Zhu et al., 2021)小物体检测通常会导致性能显着下降。to small object detection often leads to significant performance degradation.

从技术角度来看，当代小物体检测框架From a technical viewpoint, contemporary small object detection frameworks(Tan et al., 2020; Yang et al., 2022; Yuan et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Sun et al., 2025)主要致力于构建语义判别特征，以缓解小对象固有的特征表示的缺陷。尽管这些方法在一般小物体检测方面取得了显着改进，但它们仍然难以在高度混乱的复杂场景中保持鲁棒性。首先，由于空间足迹较小，小物体非常容易受到复杂背景纹理和细粒度噪声的干扰，这通常会导致误报或漏检。一些先进的方法，如 HS-FPNpredominantly make efforts on constructing semantically discriminative features to alleviate the deficiency of feature representation inherent to small objects. Although these approaches yield significant improvements in general small object detection, they still struggle to maintain robustness in highly cluttered complex scenarios. Primarily, due to the diminutive spatial footprint, small objects are highly susceptible to interference from complex background textures and fine-grained noise, which often leads to false positives or missed detections. Some advanced approaches like HS-FPN(Shi et al., 2025)引入基于离散余弦变换（DCT）的过滤策略来去除低频分量，旨在鼓励模型关注注意力集中的小物体而不是大规模的干扰物。然而，高频干扰因素仍然可能妨碍定位能力。此外，小物体的低分辨率往往会导致边界模糊，这不可避免地会引入标签模糊性来监督模型训练，这使得实现准确的物体定位具有挑战性。introduce a Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) based filtering strategy to remove the low-frequency components, aiming to encourage the model to focus on the attentive small objects rather than large-scale distractors. However, the high-frequency distractors may still hamper the localization capability. Besides, the low resolution of small objects often leads to blurred boundaries, which inevitably introduces label ambiguity to supervise the model training, making it challenging to achieve accurate object localization.

2.3. 标签消歧Label Disambiguation

标签歧义通常是由人类注释偏差或低质量图像引起的。 这个问题在小物体检测中变得尤其严重，其中物体和背景之间的区别通常不清楚。 大多数现有作品Label ambiguity is often caused by human annotation bias or low-quality images. This issue becomes especially severe in small object detection, where the distinction between object and background is often unclear. Most existing works(Yao et al., 2021; Ye et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025)将边界框回归视为确定性预测问题。然而，当面对不精确或模糊的对象边界时，此类方法表现出很大的局限性。 最近，将边界框建模为概率分布而不是固定坐标已经引起了各个领域越来越多的关注，包括不确定或不利条件下的视觉跟踪。例如，UASTtreat bounding box regression as a deterministic prediction problem. However, such approaches exhibit significant limitations when faced with imprecise or ambiguous object boundaries. Recently, modeling bounding boxes as probability distributions rather than fixed coordinates has attracted increasing attention across various domains, including visual tracking under uncertain or adverse conditions. For instance, UAST(Zhang et al., 2022)将定位输出离散化为概率分布，而 UMDATrackdiscretizes localization outputs into probability distributions, while UMDATrack(Yao et al., 2025b)和 UncTrackand UncTrack(Yao et al., 2025a)引入不确定性感知跟踪框架，以提高挑战性条件下的定位可靠性。这种概率公式在解决标签模糊性和在复杂或不确定条件下增强定位鲁棒性方面表现出了强大的能力。然而，据我们所知，用于小目标检测的分布式边界框回归尚未得到仔细探索。在这项工作中，我们展示了我们的方法在减轻标签模糊性和更好地处理小目标检测（SOD）中的复杂场景方面的潜力。introduce uncertainty-aware tracking frameworks to improve localization reliability under challenging conditions. This probabilistic formulation has demonstrated strong capabilities in resolving label ambiguity and enhancing localization robustness under complex or uncertain conditions. However, to the best of our knowledge, the distributional bounding box regression for small object detection has not been carefully explored. In this work, we demonstrate the potential of our approach to mitigate label ambiguity and better handle complex scenarios in small object detection(SOD).

5. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们建议In this paper, we propose镝酰二胺DyFrDet，由动态频率感知特征金字塔网络（DyFrFPN）和标签消歧模块（LDM）组成的两级检测器。前者利用动态频带预测器 (DBP) 自适应地控制频率表示，使检测器能够抑制低频冗余和高频噪声。后者通过更加关注高置信度样本并抑制模糊样本来实现准确的边界框估计。实验结果表明，我们的方法在几个广泛使用的小物体检测基准上实现了最先进的性能。, a two-stage detector composed of a Dynamic Frequency-aware Feature Pyramid Network (DyFrFPN) and a Label Disambiguation Module (LDM).
