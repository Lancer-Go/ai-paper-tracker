InpaintSLat：修复结构化 3D 潜伏InpaintSLat: Inpainting Structured 3D Latents
通过初始噪声优化via Initial Noise Optimization

Jaeyoung Chung    Suyoung Lee    Kyoung Mu Lee
摘要Abstract

我们提出了一种基于初始噪声优化的免训练可控 3D 修复方法。在结构化 3D 潜在扩散框架中，我们观察到底层几何结构是在扩散过程的早期阶段建立的，并且对初始噪声表现出高度敏感性。 这些特征会损害修复和编辑等任务的稳定性，在这些任务中，模型必须确保在合成新结构的同时与现有上下文严格一致。 在本文中，我们介绍了一种在结构化 3D 潜在扩散框架内优化初始噪声的策略，确保高保真 3D 修复。 具体来说，我们通过利用基于整流流模型的反向传播近似来更新初始噪声，并使用专为稳健且高效的结构化 3D 潜在优化而设计的谱参数化。 实验表明，与代表性的免训练修复基线相比，上下文一致性和提示对齐得到了持续改进，将初始噪声控制建立为 3D 修复的独立维度，与传统采样轨迹操作正交。We present a training-free approach for controllable 3D inpainting based on initial noise optimization. In the structured 3D latent diffusion framework, we observe that the underlying geometric structure is established during the early stages of the diffusion process and exhibits high sensitivity to the initial noise. Such characteristics compromise stability in tasks like inpainting and editing, where the model must ensure strict alignment with the existing context while synthesizing a new structure. In this paper, we introduce a strategy to optimize the initial noise within the structured 3D latent diffusion framework, ensuring high-fidelity 3D inpainting. Specifically, we update the initial noise by leveraging a backpropagation approximation grounded in the rectified flow model, with the spectral parameterization specially designed for robust and efficient structured 3D latent optimization. Experiments demonstrate consistent improvements in contextual consistency and prompt alignment over representative training-free inpainting baselines, establishing initial noise control as an independent dimension for 3D inpainting, orthogonal to conventional sampling trajectory manipulation.

1 简介Introduction

3D 生成建模的最新突破，最引人注目的是 TRELLIS 等结构化潜在框架Recent breakthroughs in 3D generative modeling, most notably structured latent frameworks like TRELLIS[xiang2025structured]，显着提高了我们合成各种模式的高保真 3D 资产的能力。然而，将这些功能扩展到 3D 修复（即在保留现有结构的同时完成 3D 对象缺失区域的任务）仍然是一项艰巨的挑战。与 2D 图像修复不同，2D 图像修复的模型主要预测密集、固定网格上的像素强度，3D 修复必须同时解析几何形状和外观。这引入了一个根本性的困难：虽然 2D 像素根据定义占据了整个图像空间，但 3D 生成模型必须确定缺失区域的占用情况，本质上不仅决定要填充的颜色，还决定表面本身的放置位置。这种额外的结构要求要求生成先验和上下文约束之间有更紧密的耦合。, have significantly advanced our ability to synthesize high-fidelity 3D assets from various modalities. However, extending these capabilities to 3D inpainting—the task of completing missing regions of a 3D object while preserving existing structures—remains a formidable challenge. Unlike 2D image inpainting, where the model primarily predicts pixel intensities across a dense, fixed grid, 3D inpainting must simultaneously resolve both geometry and appearance. This introduces a fundamental difficulty: while 2D pixels occupy the entire image space by definition, 3D generative models must determine the occupancy of the missing regions, essentially deciding not only what color to fill but also where to place the surface itself. This additional structural requirement demands a much tighter coupling between the generative prior and the contextual constraints.

现有的免训练可控生成方法很大程度上依赖于控制采样轨迹，这是通过 RePaint 等方法在 2D 扩散模型中成功开创的策略Existing training-free approaches for controllable generation largely rely on steering the sampling trajectory, a strategy successfully pioneered in 2D diffusion models through methods like RePaint[lugmayr2022repaint]和SD编辑and SDEdit[meng2021sdedit]。然而，当直接应用于 3D 结构化潜在时，这些基于轨迹的操作通常表现出很大的局限性。在 3D 生成中，全局几何布局是在去噪的早期阶段建立的，并且在很大程度上由初始噪声种子决定。当随机初始种子与目标结构对齐不良时，迫使采样轨迹接近约束通常会导致缺乏鲁棒性。这通常会导致结构崩溃，模型难以将纹理合成到支离破碎或不连贯的几何基础上，从而导致预训练先验无法自然解决的伪影。. When directly applied to 3D structured latents, however, these trajectory-based manipulations often exhibit significant limitations. In 3D generation, the global geometric layout is established during the early stages of denoising and is heavily dictated by the initial noise seed. When the random initial seed is poorly aligned with the target structure, forcing the sampling trajectory toward the constraints often results in a lack of robustness. This often leads to structural collapses, where the model struggles to synthesize textures onto fragmented or incoherent geometric foundations, resulting in artifacts that the pretrained prior cannot naturally resolve.

为了了解这种敏感性的根源，我们研究了初始噪声与最终合成的 3D 结构之间的关系。如图所示To understand the root of this sensitivity, we investigate the relationship between the initial noise and the final synthesized 3D structure. As illustrated inFig.˜2，我们观察到从初始潜在空间到生成输出的映射表现出两种不同的行为：（1）初始噪声中的小扰动会导致输出的局部平滑变化，但是（2）种子中的大偏差会导致基本几何主干发生剧烈的非线性变化。这些观察结果表明，3D 修复的质量和可靠性本质上与起始噪声的适用性有关。因此，我们不只是纠正路径，而是探索一个更有原则的方向：识别与保留的上下文和目标提示本质上兼容的最佳初始噪声种子。, we observe that the mapping from the initial latent space to the generated output exhibits two distinct behaviors: (1) small perturbations in the initial noise result in locally smooth variations in the output, but (2) large deviations in the seed lead to drastic, non-linear shifts in the fundamental geometric backbone. These observations suggest that the quality and reliability of 3D inpainting are inherently bound to the suitability of the starting noise. Consequently, instead of merely correcting the path, we explore a more principled direction: identifying an optimal initial noise seed that is inherently compatible with both the preserved context and the target prompt.

在这项工作中，我们提出了一套技术创新，以实现 3D 修复稳定且高效的初始种子优化。为了管理通过 3D 扩散步骤进行反向传播的计算复杂性，我们引入了一种基于修正流建模的近似反向传播策略。为了更好地捕捉 3D 形状的多尺度性质，我们对结构化潜在的几何分量采用谱参数化（基于频率的更新），这确保了全局结构的更平滑优化。此外，我们结合了分布正则化损失，以在 i.i.d. 内保持优化的噪声。预训练先验的高斯流形，确保生成鲁棒性。至关重要的是，我们的种子优化框架与现有的轨迹引导方法正交，因此兼容。通过改进初始化，我们提供了一个连贯的结构锚点，它可以单独使用，也可以与每步指导配对，以实现增强的修复质量。In this work, we propose a suite of technical innovations to enable stable and efficient initial seed optimization for 3D inpainting. To manage the computational complexity of backpropagating through 3D diffusion steps, we introduce an approximate backpropagation strategy based on rectified flow modeling. To better capture the multi-scale nature of 3D shapes, we employ spectral parameterization (frequency-based updates) for the geometric components of the structured latent, which ensures smoother optimization of the global structure. Furthermore, we incorporate a distributional regularization loss to maintain the optimized noise within the i.i.d. Gaussian manifold of the pretrained prior, ensuring generative robustness. Crucially, our seed optimization framework is orthogonal to, and thus compatible with, existing trajectory-steering methods. By refining the initialization, we provide a coherent structural anchor that can either stand alone or be paired with per-step guidance to achieve enhanced inpainting quality.

我们的贡献总结如下：Our contributions are summarized as follows:

  • •

    我们提出了一个免训练的 3D 修复框架，该框架可以优化初始结构化潜在种子，以确保几何一致性和结构完整性。We propose a training-free 3D inpainting framework that optimizes the initial structured latent seed to ensure geometric consistency and structural integrity.

  • •

    我们引入了用于稳定 3D 潜在细化的技术套件，包括通过整流流进行近似反向传播、3D 几何的谱参数化和高斯正则化。We introduce a technical suite for stable 3D latent refinement, including approximate backpropagation via rectified flow, spectral parameterization for 3D geometry, and Gaussian regularization.

  • •

    我们证明种子优化与现有的基于轨迹的指导正交，提供了一种通用且稳健的方法，与传统的采样操作相比，可以显着减少几何故障。We demonstrate that seed optimization is orthogonal to existing trajectory-based guidance, offering a versatile and robust approach that significantly reduces geometric failures compared to traditional sampling manipulation.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 3D 生成模型和修复3D Generative Models and Inpainting

3D 生成模型。3D Generative Models.

随着 DreamFusion 等乐谱蒸馏方法的引入，文本到 3D 的生成进展迅速Text-to-3D generation has progressed rapidly with the introduction of score distillation methods such as DreamFusion[poole2022dreamfusion], 魔法3D, Magic3D[lin2023magic3d]和多产的梦想家, and ProlificDreamer[wang2023prolificdreamer]，利用预训练的 2D 扩散先验来优化神经辐射场或网格。最近，出现了直接对结构化 3D 表示进行建模的原生 3D 扩散模型。立方体, which leverage pretrained 2D diffusion priors to optimize neural radiance fields or meshes. More recently, native 3D diffusion models have emerged that directly model structured 3D representations. XCube[ren2024xcube]和格子and TRELLIS[xiang2025structured]将扩散与体素化或结构化潜在表示相集成，通过显式几何建模实现可扩展的 3D 生成。这些方法建立了结构化 3D 潜在扩散作为可控 3D 合成的实践基础。integrate diffusion with voxelized or structured latent representations, enabling scalable 3D generation with explicit geometric modeling. These approaches establish structured 3D latent diffusion as a practical foundation for controllable 3D synthesis.

3D 完成和编辑。3D Completion and Editing.

3D 补全传统上被研究为部分观测的形状补全，包括 PCN 等方法3D completion has traditionally been studied as shape completion from partial observations, including methods such as PCN[yuan2018pcn]以及后续的点云完成模型。神经辐射场编辑方法通过每个场景的优化进一步实现局部几何或外观修改and subsequent point cloud completion models. Neural radiance field editing approaches further enable localized geometry or appearance modification via per-scene optimization[yuan2022nerf]。然而，这些方法通常需要监督训练或特定于场景的优化，并且不适用于预训练的 3D 扩散先验下的免训练修复。迄今为止，还没有建立基于结构化 3D 潜在扩散模型（例如 TRELLIS 或 XCube）的免培训修复框架。. However, these methods typically require supervised training or scene-specific optimization and are not designed for training-free inpainting under a pretrained 3D diffusion prior. To date, there is no established training-free inpainting framework built upon structured 3D latent diffusion models such as TRELLIS or XCube.

2.2 免培训的 2D 修复方法Training-Free Approaches for 2D Inpainting

基于抽样的指导。Sampling-Based Guidance.

二维中的免训练扩散控制通常通过修改采样轨迹来实现。重画Training-free diffusion control in 2D is commonly achieved by modifying the sampling trajectory. RePaint[lugmayr2022repaint]引入掩模重采样以在扩散过程中强制区域一致性。 SD编辑introduces masked resampling to enforce region consistency during diffusion. SDEdit[meng2021sdedit]允许通过添加和删除噪音进行编辑，同时保留结构。扩散后采样 (DPS)enables editing by adding and removing noise while preserving structure. Diffusion Posterior Sampling (DPS)[chung2022diffusion]和ILVRand ILVR[choi2021ilvr]结合测量一致性或低频约束来指导采样过程中的生成。这些方法通过将约束注入中间去噪步骤来进行操作。incorporate measurement consistency or low-frequency constraints to guide generation during sampling. These methods operate by injecting constraints into intermediate denoising steps.

空间混合和聚合。Spatial Blending and Aggregation.

多重扩散MultiDiffusion[bar2023multidiffusion]执行补丁式聚合以强制跨空间域的区域一致性，而混合扩散performs patch-wise aggregation to enforce regional consistency across spatial domains, while Blended Diffusion[avrahami2022blended]通过空间混合将生成的内容和原始内容结合起来。这种方法通过操纵中间去噪预测而不是修改模型参数来控制生成。combines generated and original content through spatial blending. Such approaches control generation by manipulating intermediate denoised predictions rather than modifying model parameters.

初始噪声和潜在优化。Initial Noise and Latent Optimization.

最近的研究表明，扩散结果对初始噪声高度敏感。空文本反转Recent works demonstrate that diffusion outcomes are highly sensitive to the initial noise. Null-Text Inversion[mokady2023null]优化真实图像编辑的潜在变量，而无需重新训练模型。索尼克optimizes latent variables for real-image editing without retraining the model. SONIC[baek2025sonic]显式优化初始潜在变量以改善扩散模型中的条件对齐。这些结果表明，初始化为轨迹操纵之外的扩散控制提供了另一个轴。explicitly optimizes the initial latent to improve conditional alignment in diffusion models. These results suggest that initialization provides an alternative axis for diffusion control beyond trajectory manipulation.

尽管在 2D 扩散方面取得了这些进展，但在结构化 3D 潜在扩散模型中尚未系统地探索基于初始化的控制。我们的工作通过在几何约束下引入种子优化来实现免训练 3D 修复，从而弥补了这一差距。Despite these advances in 2D diffusion, initialization-based control has not been systematically explored in structured 3D latent diffusion models. Our work bridges this gap by introducing seed optimization for training-free 3D inpainting under geometric constraints.

5 结论和局限性Conclusion and Limitation

我们提出了一种无需训练的 3D 修复方法，该方法通过优化预训练 3D 扩散模型的初始结构化潜在种子来进行操作。我们的方法不是修改模型参数或操纵采样轨迹，而是在上下文重建约束下细化初始化，允许在合成屏蔽区域之前进行预训练生成，同时保持未屏蔽区域的几何一致性。通过结合内存高效的近似反向传播、结构化基础参数化和减少步长优化，我们实现了对 3D 生成的稳定有效的控制。We presented a training-free approach for 3D inpainting that operates by optimizing the initial structured latent seed of a pretrained 3D diffusion model. Rather than modifying model parameters or manipulating the sampling trajectory, our method refines the initialization under contextual reconstruction constraints, allowing the pretrained generative prior to synthesize masked regions while preserving geometric consistency in unmasked areas. By combining memory-efficient approximate backpropagation, structured basis parameterization, and reduced-step optimization, we achieve stable and efficient control over 3D generation.

然而，我们的方法继承了基于先验的生成模型的基本限制。因为我们在预训练模型的学习分布中搜索合适的初始化，所以成功的修复取决于保留的结构和目标提示之间的兼容性。当上下文几何和文本条件在训练数据中高度不一致或代表性不足时，模型可能无法合成合理的补全。可能通过更强的结构调节或混合引导机制来解决这些先前的限制，仍然是未来研究的重要方向。Our approach nevertheless inherits a fundamental limitation of prior-based generative modeling. Because we search for a suitable initialization within the learned distribution of the pretrained model, successful inpainting depends on the compatibility between the preserved structure and the target prompt. When the contextual geometry and textual condition are highly inconsistent or underrepresented in the training data, the model may fail to synthesize plausible completions. Addressing such prior limitations, potentially through stronger structural conditioning or hybrid guidance mechanisms, remains an important direction for future research.
