摘要Abstract

在医学教育中，医生通过住院医师培训将学术知识转化为临床专业知识：在数千次诊疗中接受多年的培训，反馈来源多样，自主权逐渐增强。 许多临床推理依赖于与患者的接触，即临床医生在对话中引出病史、完善诊断假设并在不确定的情况下决定治疗方案。 虽然大型语言模型 (LLM) 在静态医学基准方面表现出色，但优化临床决策全序列的方法仍然不发达。 我们提出了 ResidencyRL，这是一种强化学习 (RL) 方法，用于通过模拟多回合临床遭遇（每个轨迹最多 60 个对话回合和 8 个工具调用）来训练临床人工智能 (AI) 代理。 ResidencyRL 将政策代理与 LLM 模拟器配对，该模拟器能够执行复杂的对抗性行为，并根据与诊断准确性、管理质量、沟通、文档和安全性相一致的结构化奖励进行训练。 在保留评估中，ResidencyRL 代理在对抗条件下将诊断准确性提高了 7.0%（88.0% 与 81.0%），并将漏报危险信号率降低了 31%，这证明了对过早关闭的严格缓解。 盲法专家临床医生验证了这些成果，并在 87.6% 的并排比较中更倾向于接受过训练的代理。 程序能力转移到看不见的基准：代理在 AMIE 多次访问基准的所有六个临床轴上都优于基本模型，并且在 AgentClinic 和 CRAFT-MD 上显示出一致的方向性改进。 我们的研究结果表明，可以通过模拟中的多轮强化学习有效地学习顺序临床决策，从而产生强大的、可推广的能力，为临床掌握铺平道路。 为了建立临床实用性，仍然需要对现实工作流程进行前瞻性验证。In medical education, physicians convert academic knowledge into clinical expertise through residency: years of training across thousands of encounters, with diverse sources of feedback and progressively greater autonomy. Much of clinical reasoning relies on the patient encounter, a dialogue in which a clinician elicits history, refines diagnostic hypotheses, and decides management under uncertainty. While large language models (LLMs) excel on static medical benchmarks, methods to optimize the full sequence of clinical decisions remain underdeveloped. We present ResidencyRL, a reinforcement learning (RL) method for training clinical artificial intelligence (AI) agents through simulated multi-turn clinical encounters (up to 60 dialogue turns and 8 tool calls per trajectory). ResidencyRL pairs the policy agent with LLM simulators capable of complex, adversarial behaviors, training against a structured reward aligned to diagnostic accuracy, management quality, communication, documentation, and safety. On held-out evaluations, the ResidencyRL agent improves diagnostic accuracy by 7.0% under adversarial conditions (88.0% vs. 81.0%) and reduces missed red flag rates by 31%, demonstrating rigorous mitigation of premature closure. Blinded expert clinicians validated these gains, preferring the trained agent in 87.6% of side-by-side comparisons. The procedural competencies transfer to unseen benchmarks: the agent outperforms the base model across all six clinical axes of the AMIE multi-visit benchmark, and shows consistent directional improvements on AgentClinic and CRAFT-MD. Our findings demonstrate that sequential clinical decision-making can be effectively learned through multi-turn RL in simulation, yielding robust, generalizable capabilities, paving the way towards clinical mastery. Prospective validation with real-world workflows remains necessary to establish clinical utility.

1 简介Introduction

医学培训从教科书开始，但临床能力是通过实践形成的：多年监督下的临床接触，每次对话都由患者的恐惧、误解和沟通限制形成，将知识转化为能力Medical training begins in textbooks, but clinical competence is forged through practice: years of supervised clinical encounters, each conversation shaped by the patient’s fears, misconceptions, and communicative limitations, transform that knowledge into competence[barrows1993overview]。 在体检中取得完美成绩的学员在表现不典型时仍可能无法检测到心肌梗塞，或者在患者出现失眠时错过心理健康危机。 真实的患者是复杂的：他们可能会提出含糊的主诉、拒绝建议或隐瞒药物治疗. A trainee who scores perfectly on board examinations may still fail to detect a myocardial infarction when the presentation is atypical, or miss a mental health crisis when the patient presents with insomnia. Real patients are complex: they may present with vague complaints, resist recommendations, or conceal medications[levy2018prevalence, kemp2020patients]，健康素养和医疗不信任等系统性障碍阻碍了有效沟通, and systemic barriers such as health literacy and medical mistrust hinder effective communication[paasche2005prevalence, laveist2009mistrust].

2 相关工作Related Work

虽然早期的医疗人工智能展示了狭窄的、特定于任务的模型的潜力While early medical AI demonstrated the potential of narrow, task-specific models[topol2019high, rajpurkar2022ai]具有先进医学推理能力的通才基础模型有望为医学人工智能带来新的范式, generalist foundation models with advanced medical reasoning abilities promise a new paradigm for medical AI[moor2023foundation]。 这些模型通过对大型、多样化的数据集进行自我监督而构建，无需实践即可学习医学。 就像住院医生通过对真实患者进行监督实践来培养能力一样，ResidencyRL 也训练了一种代理策略，通过在线强化学习在模拟中引导完整的、多轮的临床遭遇。. 5 讨论Discussion

虽然语言模型展示了强大的医学知识，但掌握临床实践需要更多：顺序信息收集、假设细化和患者在场的不确定性管理。 ResidencyRL 通过模拟患者接触中的多轮强化学习来发展这些能力，从而在诊断准确性、管理质量和以患者为中心的沟通方面产生可衡量的改进。 这些改进概括为五个评估框架（AMIE Mx、肿瘤案例、AgentClinic、CRAFT-MD 和临床医生策划的远程医疗模拟），每个框架都有不同的临床场景、交互协议和评估细则。我们在下面详细检查这些结果。While language models demonstrate strong medical knowledge, mastering clinical practice demands more: sequential information gathering, hypothesis refinement, and uncertainty management in the presence of a patient. ResidencyRL develops these competencies through multi-turn reinforcement learning in simulated patient encounters, yielding measurable improvements in diagnostic accuracy, management quality, and patient-centered communication. These improvements generalize to five evaluation frameworks (AMIE Mx, oncology cases, AgentClinic, CRAFT-MD, and clinician-curated telehealth simulations), each with different clinical scenarios, interaction protocols, and evaluation rubrics. 局限性。Limitations.所有培训都仅限于基于文本的远程医疗咨询，这是临床实践的一小部分，而现实世界的医疗保健涵盖了系统的整个范围——从紧急分诊和手术决策到慢性病管理和临终关怀，每个培训都有不同的交互动态、安全要求和文化背景（这里只模拟了美国的英语患者）。 所有培训都是单次访问，​​管理质量由自动评估者判断，而不是根据长期患者结果进行验证。 真正的临床推理将感官输入（体格检查、声调、面部表情）与基于文本的模拟无法访问的渠道的多模态数据（成像、实验室结果、生理监测）相结合All training is confined to text-based telehealth consultations, a narrow slice of clinical practice, whereas real-world healthcare spans the full breadth of the system—from emergency triage and surgical decision-making to chronic disease management and end-of-life care, each with distinct interaction dynamics, safety requirements, and cultural contexts (here, only English-speaking, US-based patients were simulated). All training encounters are single-visit, with management quality judged by the autorater rather than validated against long-term patient outcomes. Real clinical reasoning integrates sensory inputs (physical examination, vocal tone, facial expressions) with multimodal data (imaging, laboratory results, physiological monitoring) across channels that text-based simulation cannot access[Saab2026-yj, Shah2026-mf]。 除了这种感觉差距之外，交互本身更窄：代理可以通过其管理计划订购测试并开出治疗处方，但永远不会收到结果、执行物理程序或与其他提供者实时协调。. Beyond this sensory gap, the interaction itself is narrower: the agent can order tests and prescribe treatments through its management plan, but never receives results, performs physical procedures, or coordinates in real time with other providers.

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入 ResidencyRL 的前提是，对于人工智能和医生来说，临床掌握都是通过实践来发展的。 该框架通过模拟患者接触来训练人工智能代理，并提供以准确性、管理质量、患者沟通和安全为目标的复合奖励。 由此产生的代理在临床医生盲法评估中始终优于其基本模型，特别是在病史采集的彻底性以及在对抗条件下对不安全诊断捷径的抵抗力方面。 由于这些成果转移到了域外评估框架和肿瘤学等看不见的专业，因此它们提供了多轮强化学习开发适应性临床能力的证据，而不是简单地过度拟合训练信号。 模拟与临床实践之间的差距仍然很大，沿着临床覆盖范围、模拟保真度和时间范围缩小差距是未来的主要挑战。 然而核心发现是：通过模拟中的强化学习，已经拥有广泛医学知识的前沿模型可以成为一个更有意义、更有能力、更有帮助的临床医生。We introduced ResidencyRL on the premise that clinical mastery, for AI as for physicians, is developed through practice. The framework trains AI agents through simulated patient encounters under a composite reward that targets accuracy, management quality, patient communication, and safety. The resulting agent consistently outperforms its base model in blinded clinician evaluation, particularly in the thoroughness of its history-taking and its resistance to unsafe diagnostic shortcuts under adversarial conditions. Because these gains transfer to out-of-domain evaluation frameworks and unseen specialties like oncology, they provide evidence that multi-turn RL develops adaptable clinical capabilities rather than simply overfitting to a training signal. The gap between simulation and clinical practice remains substantial, and narrowing it along the axes of clinical coverage, simulation fidelity, and temporal horizon is the central challenge ahead. Yet the core finding stands: a frontier model that already possesses broad medical knowledge can be made into a meaningfully more competent and helpful clinician through reinforcement learning in simulation.
