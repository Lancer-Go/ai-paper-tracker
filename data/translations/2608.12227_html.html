SGNet：域感知 HSI 鱼类新鲜度SGNet: Domain-Aware HSI Fish Freshness

用于高光谱鱼类新鲜度分类的领域感知轻量级光谱分组卷积Domain-Aware Lightweight Spectral-Grouped Convolutions for Hyperspectral Fish Freshness Classification

Kazi Nabiul Alam    Pooneh Bagheri Zadeh    Akbar Sheikh-Akbari
摘要Abstract

高光谱成像 (HSI) 通过检测跨光谱带的生化变化来对鱼的新鲜度进行无损评估。然而，传统的深度学习方法并不能完全解决 HSI 数据的特定特征，例如空间纹理的光谱优势、序数标签结构和少量训练样本。我们提出了 SGNet（光谱分组网络），这是一种轻量级架构，它使用分组卷积和深度空间路径来分离光谱和空间特征提取。将通道挤压和激励与空间门控相结合的双重注意机制自适应地突出信息特征。在我们新开发的冰箱保存 16 天的鲑鱼片数据集上进行测试时，SGNet 仅用 475 万个参数就实现了 97.8% 的分类准确率和 0.64 天的平均绝对误差 (MAE)。消融研究验证了每个组件的贡献，而比较表明相对于 ResNet-50 和 Vision Transformers，参数减少了五到十八倍。我们的研究结果表明，领域感知设计支持工业实施的精确、实时的新鲜度预测。Hyperspectral imaging (HSI) offers nondestructive assessment of fish freshness by detecting biochemical alterations across spectral bands. However, conventional deep learning approaches do not fully address the particular characteristics of HSI data, such as spectral dominance over spatial textures, ordinal label structure, and a small number of training samples. We propose SGNet (Spectral-Grouped Network), a lightweight architecture that separates spectral and spatial feature extraction using grouped convolutions and a depthwise spatial pathway. A dual attention mechanism that couples channel-wise squeeze-and-excitation with spatial gating adaptively highlights informative features. SGNet achieves 97.8% classification accuracy and 0.64 days mean absolute error (MAE) with just 4.75M parameters when tested on our newly developed 16-day refrigerator-stored salmon fillet dataset. Ablation studies validate the contribution of each component, while comparisons demonstrate a five- to eighteen-fold parameter reduction relative to ResNet-50 and Vision Transformers. Our findings indicate that domain-aware design supports precise, real-time freshness prediction for industrial implementation.

1 简介Introduction

鱼类新鲜度是全球海鲜行业营养成分、消费者安全和市场价值的重要决定因素Fish freshness is a significant determinant of nutritional content, consumer safety, and market value in the global seafood industry[1, 2]。传统的评估技术，如感官评估、微生物测试和化学分析，具有主观性、破坏性或耗时性，这使得它们不适合实时工业应用. Conventional assessment techniques, such as sensory evaluation, microbiological testing, and chemical analysis, are variously subjective, destructive, or time-intensive, which makes them inappropriate for real-time industrial use[3, 4]。越来越多的传感技术研究反映了对自动化、无损和快速质量检测的明确需求。. The growing body of research into sensing technologies reflects a clear demand for automated, non-destructive, and rapid quality inspection.

高光谱成像 (HSI) 已成为评估食品质量的有效工具，它将空间成像与在多个连续波段获取的广泛光谱数据相结合Hyperspectral imaging (HSI) has emerged as an effective tool for evaluating food quality by integrating spatial imaging with extensive spectral data acquired across numerous contiguous wavelength bands[5, 6]。 HSI 系统的运行方式与标准 RGB 成像不同，因为它能够通过分析特定光谱带来识别化学分解产物. An HSI system operates differently from standard RGB imaging because it is able to identify chemical breakdown products through the analysis of specific spectral bands[7, 8]。这些特征使 HSI 对于评估鱼的新鲜度特别有用，因为鱼的质量会随着时间的推移逐渐持续下降，在腐败的每个阶段都会留下可测量的光谱特征。. These traits make HSI especially useful for estimating fish freshness, since the quality of fish degrades gradually and continuously over time, leaving a measurable spectral signature at each stage of spoilage.

尽管偏最小二乘判别分析 (PLS-DA) 和支持向量机 (SVM) 等经典机器学习方法已经证明了基于 HSI 的新鲜度评估的潜力Although classical machine learning methods such as partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA) and support vector machines (SVM) have demonstrated potential for HSI-based freshness evaluation[9, 10]，它们很大程度上依赖于手动设计的特征和事先的波长选择, they depend heavily on manually designed features and prior wavelength selection[11]，这限制了它们捕获详细光谱空间相互作用的能力。根据最近的研究，深度学习技术在高光谱图像分类方面取得了优异的结果, which limits their capacity to capture detailed spectral-spatial interactions. Deep learning techniques have achieved superior results in hyperspectral image classification according to recent research[12]，但大多数已建立的模型都是为遥感任务设计的。此类模型不能充分适应食品质量数据的特征，食品质量数据将大光谱维数与有限的样本可用性和固有的有序标签结构相结合。另一个经常被忽视的困难是相邻光谱带之间存在大量冗余。因此，所有波段的不加区别的混合存在使相关和不相关的波长响应纠缠在一起的风险，这会放大噪声并鼓励在有限的数据范围下过度拟合。, yet most established models were designed for remote sensing tasks. Such models do not adequately accommodate the characteristics of food quality data, which combine large spectral dimensionality with restricted sample availability and an inherently ordinal label structure. A further difficulty, frequently overlooked, is the substantial redundancy between neighbouring spectral bands; indiscriminate mixing of all bands therefore risks entangling correlated and unrelated wavelength responses, which amplifies noise and encourages overfitting under limited data regimes.

这项研究引入了 SGNet（光谱分组卷积神经网络），这是一个围绕高光谱食品数据结构设计的框架，而不是改编自遥感或自然图像主干。我们的贡献不是提出新的原语，而是高效算子的原则性组合，这些算子共同尊重光谱优势、序数标签结构和严重的样本稀缺性，以及在这种情况下验证它所需的分析和数据集。我们的贡献如下：This study introduces SGNet (Spectral-Grouped Convolutional Neural Network), a framework designed around the structure of hyperspectral food data rather than adapted from a remote sensing or natural image backbone. Rather than proposing new primitives, our contribution is a principled composition of efficient operators that jointly respects spectral dominance, ordinal label structure, and severe sample scarcity, together with the analysis and dataset needed to validate it in this regime. 2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 高光谱成像确保鱼类新鲜度Hyperspectral Imaging for Fish Freshness

近年来，HSI已广泛应用于鱼类新鲜度评估。程HSI has been applied extensively to fish freshness assessment in recent years. Cheng等人et al [1]利用 HSI 和最小二乘 SVM 来预测草鱼中总挥发性碱氮 (TVB-N) 含量，而 Yuemployed HSI with least squares SVM to predict total volatile basic nitrogen (TVB-N) content in grass carp, while Yu等人et al [2]结合HSI与数据融合快速评价罗非鱼片新鲜度。法拉哈特内贾德combined HSI with data fusion for rapid evaluation of tilapia fillet freshness. Falahatnejad等人et al [3]使用 HSI 和计算机视觉进行鱼类质量评估调查，区分了该领域占主导地位的传统管道。哈代surveyed fish quality assessment using HSI and computer vision, distinguishing the conventional pipelines that dominate the field. Hardy等人et al [13]将 K 最近邻应用于高光谱数据来预测鲑鱼储存日，达到 77% 的准确率。卡沙尼·扎德applied K-nearest neighbours to hyperspectral data for salmon storage-day prediction, achieving 77% accuracy. Kashani Zadeh等人et al [14]开发了一种多模式光谱系统，结合了 VIS-NIR、SWIR 和荧光数据，用于鲑鱼新鲜度分类，以及 Khoshnoudi-Nia 和 Moosavi-Nasabdeveloped a multimode spectroscopic system that combined VIS-NIR, SWIR, and fluorescence data for salmon freshness classification, and Khoshnoudi-Nia and Moosavi-Nasab[4]通过单个多光谱成像系统预测鱼片的多个新鲜度指标。这些研究表明，HSI 数据携带丰富的新鲜度信息，但它们主要依赖于传统的机器学习管道，需要人工干预来设计特征并选择信息波长。predicted multiple freshness indicators in fish fillets from a single multispectral imaging system. These studies establish that HSI data carry rich freshness information, yet they predominantly rely on conventional machine learning pipelines that require human intervention to engineer features and select informative wavelengths.

2.2 高光谱分类深度学习Deep Learning for Hyperspectral Classification

深度学习通过其分层特征提取的能力改变了高光谱图像分析。早期基于 CNN 的方法Deep learning has transformed hyperspectral image analysis through its capacity for hierarchical feature extraction. Early CNN-based methods[15, 16]使用3D卷积层来联合融合光谱和空间信息，但其高计算要求限制了其实用性。罗伊used 3D convolutional layers to fuse spectral and spatial information jointly, but their high computational requirements limited their practicality. Roy等人et al [17]随后提出了 HybridSN，一种混合 3D-2D 卷积设计，可以降低纯 3D 模型的成本，同时保留强大的光谱空间辨别力。与此同时，注意力机制已被证明非常有效：Husubsequently proposed HybridSN, a hybrid 3D–2D convolutional design that reduces the cost of pure 3D models while retaining strong spectral-spatial discrimination. In parallel, attention mechanisms have proven highly effective: Hu等人et al [18]引入了用于通道重新校准的挤压和激励（SE）模块，以及 Woointroduced squeeze-and-excitation (SE) blocks for channel recalibration, and Woo等人et al [19]提出了CBAM，它统一了通道和空间注意力。特别是对于 HSI，频谱和循环注意力网络proposed CBAM, which unifies channel and spatial attention. For HSI specifically, spectral and recurrent attention networks[20, 12]在遥感基准上取得了最先进的结果。一个反复出现的限制是，这些系统是针对大规模数据收集进行校准的，并会产生相当大的计算成本，这与表征食品质量检测的小样本、高维制度不太匹配。have achieved state-of-the-art results on remote sensing benchmarks. A recurring limitation is that these systems are calibrated for large-scale data collections and incur considerable computational cost, which is poorly matched to the small-sample, high-dimensional regime that characterises food quality inspection.

3.2 设计原理Design Rationale

高光谱鱼类新鲜度分类任务存在三个主要障碍。首先，光谱优势意味着新鲜度信息主要由波长相关的反射率图案而不是空间纹理来承载。其次，时间平滑性要求相邻日期的表示在特征空间中保持接近，因此一天的错误被视为远没有几天的错误那么严重。第三，可用数据的数量有限，并且受到其他变化和噪音来源的影响。不考虑这些约束而将光谱和空间信息结合起来的通用 CNN 表现不佳，因为它们过早地缠结了相关频带并过度拟合虚假模式。因此，所提出的设计包含两个独立的处理路径，它们使用分组卷积和轻量级注意力来抑制不重要的变化，同时以受控方式执行光谱和空间交互。The hyperspectral fish freshness classification task presents three principal obstacles. First, spectral dominance means that freshness information is carried primarily by wavelength-dependent reflectance patterns rather than by spatial texture. Second, temporal smoothness requires that representations of neighbouring days remain close in feature space, so that an error of one day is treated as far less severe than an error of several days. Third, the available data are limited in number and are subject to additional sources of variation and noise. Generic CNNs that combine spectral and spatial information without regard for these constraints perform poorly, because they entangle correlated bands prematurely and overfit to spurious patterns. The proposed design therefore contains two independent processing paths that use grouped convolutions together with lightweight attention to suppress unimportant variation while performing spectral and spatial interaction in a controlled manner.

5 讨论Discussion

这些实验支持我们的中心论点，即领域感知分解，而不是更大的容量，是准确的高光谱新鲜度评估的更有效的途径。三个观察结果值得强调。首先，腐败后期（第 15-16 天）残留误差的集中与其说是一种失​​效模式，不如说是生物学的反映，因为晚期腐败中的降解标记重叠，并且相邻后期之间的光谱间隔确实很小。其次，SGNet 和 Transformer 基线之间的差距在小样本情况下精确地扩大，这表明当数据稀缺时，分组卷积和深度卷积编码的归纳偏差是有价值的。第三，双重注意机制以可忽略的参数成本提供了可测量的准确性增益，这表明选择性强调而不是密集的全局混合足以完成这项任务。The experiments support our central claim that domain-aware factorisation, rather than greater capacity, is the more productive route to accurate hyperspectral freshness assessment. Three observations are worth emphasising. First, the concentration of residual error in the late spoilage stages (days 15–16) is not a failure mode so much as a reflection of biology, since degradation markers in advanced spoilage overlap and the spectral separation between adjacent late days is genuinely small. Second, the gap between SGNet and the transformer baselines widens precisely in the small-sample regime, which indicates that the inductive biases encoded by grouped and depthwise convolutions are valuable when data are scarce. Third, the dual attention mechanism delivers a measurable accuracy gain for a negligible parameter cost, which suggests that selective emphasis, rather than dense global mixing, is sufficient for this task.

仍然存在一些限制。该数据集虽然经过仔细包装以防止泄漏，但却是从受控冷藏条件下的单个物种中提取的，因此尚未建立跨物种、存储条件和采集仪器的概括。当前模型使用交叉熵进行训练，并使用序数度量进行评估；纳入明确的排名一致的目标，例如 CORALSeveral limitations remain. The dataset, although carefully pack-separated to prevent leakage, is drawn from a single species under controlled refrigerated storage, so generalisation across species, storage conditions, and acquisition instruments is not yet established. The current model is trained with cross-entropy and evaluated with ordinal metrics; incorporating an explicit rank-consistent objective such as CORAL[27]或其后继者 CORNor its successor CORN[30]训练是一种自然的延伸，可以进一步减少大距离的错误。我们认为跨物种转移、显式序数训练和额外食物矩阵的扩展是未来工作最有希望的方向。into training is a natural extension that may further reduce large-distance errors. We regard cross-species transfer, explicit ordinal training, and an extension to additional food matrices as the most promising directions for future work.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 SGNet，这是一种用于高光谱鱼类新鲜度分类的轻量级域感知架构，可明确分解光谱和空间特征提取。在精心策划的 16 天鲑鱼存储数据集上进行评估，SGNet 仅使用 475 万个参数即可达到 97.8% 的准确率和 0.64 天的 MAE，同时在相同条件下匹配或超过更大的卷积和 Transformer 基线。大多数残余误差仅限于腐败后期（第 14-16 天，最明显的是第 15-16 天），这与鱼类退化的基本生物学一致。消融研究证实，每个组件都会对整体结果做出贡献，并且设计在各种配置中保持稳定。总而言之，这些发现表明，尊重高光谱食品数据的光谱优势、序数结构和有限的样本量，可以产生准确且可部署的模型，而成本只是通用骨干网的一小部分。We presented SGNet, a lightweight domain-aware architecture for hyperspectral fish freshness classification that explicitly factorises spectral and spatial feature extraction. Evaluated on a curated 16-day salmon storage dataset, SGNet reaches 97.8% accuracy and an MAE of 0.64 days with only 4.75M parameters, while matching or exceeding substantially larger convolutional and transformer baselines under identical conditions. Most residual error is confined to the late spoilage window (days 14–16, most pronounced at days 15–16), which is consistent with the underlying biology of fish degradation. Ablation studies confirm that every component contributes to the overall result and that the design remains stable across configurations. Taken together, these findings indicate that respecting the spectral dominance, ordinal structure, and limited sample size of hyperspectral food data yields an accurate and deployable model at a fraction of the cost of generic backbones.
