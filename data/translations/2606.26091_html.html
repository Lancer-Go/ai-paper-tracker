带有采样演示的合规自蒸馏减少了输出多样性On-Policy Self-Distillation with Sampled Demonstrations Reduces Output Diversity

Andrei Liviu Nicolicioiu[1,2,3,†] Mohammad Pezeshki[3,] 亚伦·考维尔[1,2,4,] Aaron Courville[1,2,4,]

摘要Abstract

策略自蒸馏通过使用单一模型作为教师和学生，实现了很高的 pass@1 准确性，教师以正确的演示为条件，以提供密集的令牌级反馈。我们表明，这可能会带来隐性成本：rollout 多样性下降且 pass@k 曲线变平（即，生成更多 rollout 无法提高准确性）。我们将其追溯到自蒸馏设计中的复合偏差和抽样演示。教师对每个学生的展示进行评分，同时以正确的展示样本为条件，通过模型自身的偏差来引导其反馈。我们从理论上分析了最佳自蒸馏策略，并表明它通过学生的推出和用作上下文的正确推出之间的逐点条件互信息分数来倾斜基础分布。与理想的最优同策略强化学习（RL）不同，自蒸馏可以扩大现有的概率差距，将质量集中在已经占主导地位的模式上。在受控图寻路任务和科学问答基准上，自蒸馏模型的平均性能匹配或超过 RL，但表现出明显较低的性能On-policy self-distillation achieves strong pass@1 accuracy by using a single model as both teacher and student, with the teacher conditioned on a correct demonstration to provide dense token-level feedback. We show that this could come at a hidden cost: rollout diversity decreases and pass@k curves flatten (i.e., generating more rollouts fails to improve accuracy). We trace this to compounding biases in the design of self-distillation with sampled demonstrations. The teacher scores each student rollout while conditioned on a sampled correct rollout, channeling its feedback through the model’s own biases. We theoretically analyze the optimal self-distillation policy and show that it tilts the base distribution by a pointwise conditional mutual information score between the student’s rollout and the correct rollout used as context. Unlike the ideal optimal on-policy reinforcement learning (RL), which preserves probability ratios among equally correct rollouts, selfdistillation can amplify existing probability gaps, concentrating mass on alreadydominant modes. On a controlled graph path-finding task and science questionanswering benchmarks, self-distilled models match or exceed RL on average performance but exhibit substantially lower功能性的functional和and语义semantic多样性，在需要多样化策略的分布外环境中失败。diversity, failing on out-of-distribution settings that require diverse strategies.

1 简介1 Introduction

当前向模型灌输能力的 LLM 后训练方法具有不同的权衡，有监督微调 (SFT) 学习初始行为，而策略 RL 方法则精炼和探索新方法（Zhang 等人，2025）。在此期间，在政策蒸馏（Agarwal et al., 2024；Lu and Thinking Machines, 2025）使用更强大的教师来指导学生使用学生生成的数据。自我蒸馏更进一步，完全消除了外部教师：相同的模型，以特权信息（例如正确的解决方案或环境反馈）为条件，为学生自己的世代提供了密集的令牌级反馈。最近的方法，包括 SDPO (Hübotter et al., 2026)、SDFT (Shenfeld et al., 2026)、OPSD (Zhao et al., 2026; Penaloza et al., 2026) 和 OPCD (Ye et al., 2026)，实例化了这种方法，在科学问答、持续学习任务和代理等多项任务中实现了强大的性能任务。Current LLM post-training approaches to instill capabilities in models have different tradeoffs, with supervised fine-tuning (SFT) learning initial behaviors and on-policy RL methods refining and exploring new approaches (Zhang et al., 2025). In between, on-policy distillation (Agarwal et al., 2024; Lu and Thinking Machines, 2025) uses a stronger teacher to guide a student using studentgenerated data. Self-distillation goes further by eliminating the external teacher entirely: the same model, conditioned on privileged information, such as a correct solution or environmental feedback, provides dense token-level feedback on the student’s own generations. Recent methods, including SDPO (Hübotter et al., 2026), SDFT (Shenfeld et al., 2026), OPSD (Zhao et al., 2026; Penaloza et al., 2026), and OPCD (Ye et al., 2026), instantiate this approach, achieving strong performance across several tasks such as scientific question-answering, continual learning tasks, and agentic tasks.

在本文中，我们研究了一个具体案例，即带有采样演示的自蒸馏（SDSD），其中学生在老师的指导下进行展示，并在其背景下进行演示。演示可以来自正确的学生展示（正是 Hübotter 等人（2026）与学生演示的设置）或来自外部模型（Zhao 等人，2026；Penaloza 等人，2026）。在这里，我们发现良好的准确性可能会带来隐性成本。具有采样演示的 SDSD 模型表现出斜率较小或几乎平坦的 pass@k 曲线（图 3、4）：生成更多的推出无法解决新问题。相比之下，使用同策略 RL（例如 GRPO）训练的模型显示出显着的 pass@k 改进，其中每个额外的样本都有意义地增加了问题覆盖率。因此，SDSD 可以用部署多样性换取平均准确度。In this paper, we investigate a specific case, Self-Distillation with Sampled Demonstrations (SDSD) where student rollouts are guided by a teacher with demonstrations in its context. The demonstrations could come from correct student rollouts (exactly the setup of Hübotter et al. (2026) with student demonstrations) or from external models (Zhao et al., 2026; Penaloza et al., 2026). Here, we find that good accuracy might come at a hidden cost. SDSD models with sampled demonstrations exhibit pass@k curves with small or nearly flat slopes (Figures 3, 4): generating more rollouts fails to solve new problems. By contrast, models trained with on-policy RL (e.g., GRPO) show steep pass@k improvement, where each additional sample meaningfully increases problem coverage. SDSD could thus trade rollout diversity for average accuracy.

Theoretically, the optimal selfdistillation policy tilts the base distribution by a pointwise conditional mutual information (PCMI) score between the student rollout and the demonstration. Unlike standard RL objectives, which preserve probability ratios among equally correct rollouts, this mechanism amplifies pre-existing imbalances and reinforces solutions that already align with the demonstrations and base policy. Empirically, we observed this diversity collapse on controlled graph path-finding, scientific QA, and synthetic tasks. Our results show that average accuracy alone is insufficient for evaluating self-distillation methods. Functional and semantic diversity are at risk of collapse and should be explicitly monitored during training and deployment.

范围和限制。Scope and limitations.我们特别关注自蒸馏的变体，它使用采样的正确卷展作为演示。我们不会分析教师以更丰富的特权信号为条件的设置，例如编码中的运行时错误（Hübotter 等人，2026）、环境反馈或外部验证者，其中学习动态可能存在很大差异。我们的理论分析假设教师对基本政策冻结，而大多数实际的 SDSD 实施（包括我们实验中使用的实施）都聘用来自学生本身的 EMA 教师。此外，我们的推导假设演示是从基本策略中采样的，与 Hübotter 等人完全自行生成的设置相比，它更类似于 OPSD（Zhao 等人，2026）和我们的外部演示图实验。 （2026）。在实践中，EMA 教师和自我生成的演示都引入了超出我们分析所捕获的其他形式的自我选择偏差。最后，我们的推导是在序列级别上呈现的；然而，类似的令牌级别推导在每个下一个令牌分布处产生相同的基于 PCMI 的倾斜，导致效果沿着轨迹自回归复合，如 B.3 节中所讨论的。We focus specifically on the variant of self-distillation that uses sampled correct rollouts as demonstrations. We do not analyze settings where the teacher is conditioned on richer privileged signals, such as runtime errors in coding (Hübotter et al., 2026), environmental feedback, or external verifiers, where the learning dynamics may differ substantially. Our theoretical analysis assumes a teacher frozen at the base policy, whereas most practical SDSD implementations, including those used in our experiments, employ an EMA teacher derived from the student itself. In addition, our derivation assumes demonstrations are sampled from the base policy, which more closely resembles OPSD (Zhao et al., 2026) and our external-demonstration graph experiments than the fully self-generated setup of Hübotter et al. (2026). In practice, both EMA teachers and self-generated demonstrations introduce additional forms of self-selection bias beyond those captured by our analysis. Finally, our derivation is presented at the sequence level; however, an analogous token-level derivation yields the same PCMI-based tilt at each next-token distribution, causing the effect to compound autoregressively along a trajectory, as discussed in section B.3.

代币级目标的含义。Implications of the token-level objective.在序列级目标的情况下，我们发现常见的推出存在偏差。这种偏见来自于展示和演示之间的一致性以及老师的偏好。类似的现象也发生在代币层面。有些标记更符合演示和学生展示的先前标记所确定的上下文。同样，将当前部署推向新颖或不常见方向的下一个令牌可能与上下文不太一致。此外，在相同的上下文中，教师更喜欢某些下一个标记。In the case of sequence-level objective, we have seen that there is a bias for common rollouts. That bias comes from the alignment between rollouts and demonstrations and the preference of the teacher. A similar phenomenon is happening at the token-level. Some tokens are more aligned to the context determined by the demonstration and the previous tokens of the student rollout. Again, next-tokens that will move the current rollout into a novel or uncommon direction might be less aligned to the context. Moreover, given the same context, some next-tokens are preferred over others by the teacher.

最重要的是，与序列级别目标不同，这里老师对学生的全面展示缺乏远见。它指导下一个令牌，而不考虑与未来令牌的关系。因此，学生展示和演示之间的一些共性可能只能在序列级别上看到，并且在中间标记位置上可能更难以建立。因此，中间标记位置的对齐可能较低，导致学习更加有偏差。这导致了对公共下一个令牌的偏见，这变成了我们之前讨论的公共整个分布。On top of this, differently from the sequence level objective, here the teacher is myopic to the full student rollout. It guides the next token without taking into account the relation to future tokens. Thus, some commonalities between student rollout and demonstrations might only be seen at a sequence level and could be harder to establish at intermediate token positions. Thus, the alignment at intermediate token positions might be low, causing learning to be even more biased. This leads to a bias for common next-tokens, which turns into common entire distributions as we discussed before.
