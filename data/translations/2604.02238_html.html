生成式人工智能聚焦数据科学的人类核心：对教育的影响Generative AI Spotlights the Human Core of Data Science: Implications for Education

内森·塔巴克[1]Nathan Taback[1]

1 多伦多大学统计科学系，加拿大安大略省多伦多1 Department of Statistical Sciences, University of Toronto, Toronto, Ontario, Canada

摘要Abstract

生成式人工智能（GAI）揭示了数据科学中心不可简化的人类核心，这是违反直觉的：GAI 的进步应该加强而不是削弱数据科学教育中对人类推理的关注。 GAI 现在能够执行许多常规数据科学工作流程，包括清理、总结、可视化、建模和起草报告，但最重要的能力仍然是不可简化的人类能力——人类核心：问题表述、测量和设计、因果识别、统计和计算推理、道德和责任以及意义建构。借鉴多诺霍的大数据科学框架、诺兰和坦普尔·朗的计算素养愿景，以及麦克卢汉-卡尔金的“我们塑造工具，然后我们的工具塑造我们”的见解，我通过三个融合的谱系追踪了数据科学的出现：图基将数据分析作为一门科学的知识愿景、为数据科学家创造了工业需求的监视资本主义的商业逻辑，以及随后的学术项目。将 GAI 的影响映射到 Donoho 的大数据科学的六个部门表明，数据计算 (GDS3) 已基本实现自动化，Donoho 认为最重要的部门，数据收集、准备和探索 (GDS1) 以及关于数据科学的科学 (GDS6) 需要人工投入。教育意义在于，数据科学课程应关注人类核心，同时了解人类输入在 GAI 中的重要性，作为迭代提示-输出-提示 (POP) 循环的一部分，并具有检索增强生成和学习成果，评估应包括和评估推理和判断。Generative AI (GAI) reveals an irreducible human core at the center of data science that is counterintuitive: advances in GAI should sharpen rather than diminish the focus on human reasoning in data science education. GAI is now capable of executing many routine data science workflows including cleaning, summarizing, visualizing, modeling, and drafting reports, yet the competencies that matter most remain irreducibly human—the human core: problem formulation, measurement and design, causal identification, statistical and computational reasoning, ethics and accountability, and sensemaking. Drawing on Donoho’s Greater Data Science framework, Nolan and Temple Lang’s vision of computational literacy, and the McLuhan-Culkin insight that we shape our tools and thereafter our tools shape us, I trace the emergence of data science through three converging lineages: Tukey’s intellectual vision of data analysis as a science, the commercial logic of surveillance capitalism that created industrial demand for data scientists, and the academic programs that followed. Mapping GAI’s impact onto Donoho’s six divisions of Greater Data Science reveals that computing with data (GDS3) has been substantially automated, the divisions Donoho identified as most important, data gathering, preparation, and exploration (GDS1) and science about data science (GDS6), require human input. The educational implications are that data science curriculum should focus on the human core while understanding how human input is essential in GAI as part of iterative prompt–output–prompt (POP) cycles with retrieval-augmented generation and learning outcomes and assessments should include and evaluate reasoning and judgment.

1.2.知识分子谱系：从图基到多诺霍1.2. The Intellectual Lineage: From Tukey to Donoho

六十多年前，约翰·图基 (John Tukey) 在《More than sixty years ago, John Tukey published a deeply unconventional article in数理统计年鉴The Annals of Mathematical Statistics。 《数据分析的未来》（Tukey，1962 年）发表在一本期刊上，该期刊的其他文章介绍了定义、定理和证明。相反，图基的贡献是一种专业的自白。他认为，他关心的数据分析不是数学的一个分支，而是一门经验科学，一门“由普遍存在的问题而不是由具体主题定义的科学”。他写道，数据分析至少和生物学一样复杂，而正式的统计理论在其进展中只能发挥部分作用。. “The Future of Data Analysis” (Tukey, 1962) appeared in a journal whose other articles presented definitions, theorems, and proofs. Tukey’s contribution was instead a kind of professional confession. He argued that what he cared about, data analysis, was not a branch of mathematics but an empirical science, one “defined by a ubiquitous problem rather than by a concrete subject.” Data analysis, he wrote, was at least as complex as biology, and formal statistical theory could play only a partial role in its progress.

图基确定了影响这门新科学的四个驱动力：统计的正式理论、计算的加速发展、越来越大的数据体的挑战，以及对更广泛学科的量化的强调。这份清单非常现代。每一篇宣布数据科学计划的当代新闻稿都会引用这些相同力量的某些版本。 1962 年的激进之处在于图基坚持认为形式理论只是四种理论之一，而且也许不是最重要的。Tukey identified four driving forces acting on this new science: the formal theories of statistics, accelerating developments in computing, the challenge of ever-larger bodies of data, and the emphasis on quantification across an ever-wider array of disciplines. This list is strikingly modern. Every contemporary press release announcing a data science initiative cites some version of these same forces. What was radical in 1962 was Tukey’s insistence that formal theory was only one among four, and perhaps not the most important.

图基还担心如何教授数据分析。他观察到，统计学往往作为数学的一个分支来教授，学生对实际数据分析的接触有限，而且统计学学生与专业人士接触的年限远少于物理科学学生。他提出，教授数据分析更像是教授生物化学——这是一个充满了教科书需要关联的详细知识的领域，而不是像教授数学一样，通过证明来取得进展。他对“避免食谱和增进理解只能通过数学处理并强调证明”的观点提出警告。Tukey also worried about how data analysis was taught. He observed that statistics tended to be taught as a branch of mathematics, that students had limited exposure to actual data analysis, and that the number of years of contact with practicing professionals was far less for statistics students than for students in the physical sciences. He proposed that teaching data analysis was more like teaching biochemistry — a field overflowing with detailed knowledge that textbooks needed to relate, than like teaching mathematics, where one progresses through proofs. He warned against the view that “avoidance of cookbookery and growth of understanding come only by mathematical treatment, with emphasis upon proofs.”

Donoho（2017）在《数据科学的 50 年》（50 Years of Data Science）一书中综合了三十多年来的此类劝告——来自钱伯斯（Chambers，1993）、克利夫兰（Cleveland，2001）、吴（1997）以及其他敦促学术统计学拓展其边界的人。Donoho (2017) synthesized over three decades of such exhortations — from Chambers (1993), Cleveland (2001), Wu (1997), and others who urged academic statistics to expand its boundaries, in “50 Years of Data Science,” published in the计算与图形统计杂志Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics。多诺霍在图基百年纪念之际撰文，区分了他所说的“小数据科学”和“大数据科学”（GDS），前者是新设立的硕士课程中教授的共识版本，相当于统计学加一些计算机科学，后者是一个更大的知识领域。他将GDS分为六个部分：数据收集、准备和探索（GDS1）；数据表示和转换（GDS2）；数据计算（GDS3）；数据建模（GDS4）；数据可视化和呈现（GDS5）；以及关于数据科学的科学 (GDS6)。多诺霍认为，GDS1——获取、清理和探索数据的混乱、充满判断力的工作——消耗了从业者的大部分精力，但在课程中却很少体现，而 GDS4（数据建模）在教学和研究中占据主导地位。他还认为，GDS6 是关于数据分析实际如何进行以及如何改进的循证研究，是最具前瞻性的部门，它可以使数据科学成为 Tukey 意义上的真正的科学。. Writing on the occasion of the Tukey centennial, Donoho distinguished between what he called “lesser data science,” the consensus version taught in newly minted master’s programs, amounting to statistics plus some computer science, and “greater data science” (GDS), a substantially larger intellectual field. He organized GDS into six divisions: data gathering, preparation, and exploration (GDS1); data representation and transformation (GDS2); computing with data (GDS3); data modeling (GDS4); data visualization and presentation (GDS5); and science about data science (GDS6). Donoho argued that GDS1 — the messy, judgment-laden work of acquiring, cleaning, and exploring data — consumed the majority of practitioner effort yet was poorly represented in curricula, while GDS4 (data modeling) dominated both teaching and research. He also argued that GDS6, the evidence-based study of how data analysis is actually done and how it might be improved, was the most forward-looking division — the one that could make data science a genuine science in Tukey’s sense.

从图基到多诺霍的知识分子血统，越来越明确地呼唤着 GAI 现在所强制的那种拓宽。 Tukey 强调数据分析“必须高度重视判断”（Tukey，1962）。当 GAI 吸收了数据科学项目在过去十年中教授的大部分数据科学堆栈时，剩下的正是这些思想家认为最重要的领域：正式理论和技术技能始终应该服务于充满判断力、依赖于上下文、面向人的工作。The intellectual lineage from Tukey through Donoho was calling, with increasing specificity, for exactly the kind of broadening that GAI now forces. Tukey emphasized that data analysis “must look to a heavy emphasis on judgement” (Tukey, 1962). When GAI absorbs much of the data science stack that data science programs have spent the last decade teaching, what remains is precisely the territory these thinkers identified as most important: the judgment-laden, contextdependent, human-facing work that formal theory and technical skill were always supposed to serve.

1.3.商业血统：监控资本主义和数据科学家1.3. The Commercial Lineage: Surveillance Capitalism and the Data Scientist

知识谱系告诉我们塑造数据科学的学术力量。平行的商业血统告诉我们什么The intellectual lineage tells us about the academic forces that have shaped data science. A parallel commercial lineage tells us what it成为became，以及为什么。, and why.

2000 年代初，Google 面临一个问题：如何通过迅速成为互联网上访问量最大的网站的搜索引擎获利。正如 Zuboff（2019）所记录的那样，答案在于关于数据的发现。谷歌的工程师发现，用户留下的行为痕迹（搜索日志、点击模式、停留时间）——构成了超出改进搜索产品本身所需的盈余。这种行为盈余可以转化为预测产品：预测用户会点击、查找或购买什么的计算。谷歌首席经济学家哈尔·瓦里安（Hal Varian）通过坦率的观察阐明了由此产生的商业模式：“突然间，我们意识到我们处于拍卖业务中”（Zuboff，2019）。广告商会付费将他们的信息展示在行为数据预测接受度的用户面前。In the early 2000s, Google confronted a problem: how to monetize a search engine that was rapidly becoming the most visited site on the internet. The answer, as Zuboff (2019) has documented, lay in a discovery about data. Google’s engineers found that the behavioral traces users left behind (search logs, click patterns, dwell times) — constituted a surplus that exceeded what was needed to improve the search product itself. This behavioral surplus could be translated into prediction products: computations that anticipated what a user would click on, look for, or buy. Hal Varian, Google’s chief economist, clarified the resulting business model with a candid observation: “All of a sudden, we realized we were in the auction business” (Zuboff, 2019). Advertisers would pay to place their messages in front of users whose behavioral data predicted receptivity.

所出现的机制可以用简单的术语来描述。数据科学使公司能够将私人和人类经验作为大部分免费的原材料转化为行为数据。这些数据作为计算过程（“机器智能”层）的输入，产生预测产品，预测个人现在、不久或以后会做什么。然后，预测产品在祖博夫所谓的行为期货市场上进行交易。这种经济逻辑迅速蔓延到科技领域之外。正如 Zuboff（2019）所观察到的那样，它现在的业务涵盖保险、汽车、健康、教育、金融以及几乎所有被描述为“智能”的产品或被描述为“个性化”的服务。The mechanism that emerged can be described in straightforward terms. Data science operationalizes the ability for companies to claim private and human experience as mostly free raw material for translation into behavioral data. These data serve as inputs into computational processes — the “machine intelligence” layer, that produce prediction products anticipating what individuals will do now, soon, or later. The prediction products are then traded in what Zuboff calls behavioral futures markets. This economic logic spread rapidly beyond the technology sector. As Zuboff (2019) observes, it now operates across insurance, automobiles, health, education, finance, and virtually every product described as “smart” or service described as “personalized.”

这种商业逻辑以特定的方式塑造了数据科学行业。 “数据科学家”被 Dorschel (2021) 称为混合专业人士，同时是通才和专家、技术人员和沟通者、数据开发者和数据伦理学家。 2009 年，瓦里安预测“获取数据的能力——能够理解数据、处理数据、从中提取价值、可视化数据、交流数据——将在未来几十年成为一项非常重要的技能”（Varian，2009）。他尖锐地补充说，稀缺且昂贵的是“分析数据并利用数据讲述故事的人才”。到 2012 年，“数​​据科学家”一词在流行话语中取代了“统计学家”，《哈佛商业评论》宣布其为 21 世纪最性感的工作（Davenport & Patil，2012）。This commercial logic shaped the data science profession in specific ways. The “data scientist” emerged as what Dorschel (2021) calls a hybrid professional, simultaneously generalist and specialist, technician and communicator, data exploiter and data ethicist. In 2009, Varian predicted that “the ability to take data — to be able to understand it, to process it, to extract value from it, to visualize it, to communicate it — is going to be a hugely important skill in the next decades” (Varian, 2009). He added, pointedly, that what was scarce and expensive was “the talent to analyze data and make it tell its story.” By 2012, the term “data scientist” had displaced “statistician” in popular discourse, and the Harvard Business Review had declared it the sexiest job of the 21st century (Davenport & Patil, 2012).

2.2.将 GAI 映射到大数据科学的六个部门2.2. Mapping GAI onto the Six Divisions of Greater Data Science

现在，我依次对多诺霍的六个部门进行评估，评估 GAI 在多大程度上改变了其中的活动。结果并不是一张统一的图片。有些部门基本上是自动化的；其他人则几乎没有被触及；这种模式揭示了一些始终是基础的东西。I now take each of Donoho’s six divisions in turn and assess the degree to which GAI transforms the activities within it. 2.2.1. GDS1：数据收集、准备和探索——最具弹性的部门2.2.1. GDS1: Data Gathering, Preparation, and Exploration — The Most Resilient Division

Donoho 认为，从从业者在 GDS1 上花费的时间来衡量，GDS1 比数据建模 (GDS4) 更重要——通常引用的估计是数据科学项目中 80% 的工作用于收集、清理和探索数据。至关重要的是，GDS1 不是由特定的编码或编程工具定义的。它是关于一般数据收集、准备和探索的活动，需要了解数据存在的原因以及生成数据的过程。 GAI 可以有效地支持这些活动。从业者可以提示语言模型建议探索策略、生成清理脚本、标记数据集中的潜在异常或提出健全性检查。这些都是加速工作流程的真正贡献。Donoho argued that GDS1 is more important than data modeling (GDS4), as measured by the time practitioners spend on it—the commonly cited estimate is that 80% of effort in a data science project goes to gathering, cleaning, and exploring data. Crucially, GDS1 is not defined by specific coding or programming tools. It is about general data collection, preparation, and exploration—activities that require understanding why data exists and what process generated it. GAI can support these activities effectively. A practitioner can prompt a language model to suggest exploration strategies, generate cleaning scripts, flag potential anomalies in a dataset, or propose sanity checks. These are genuine contributions that accelerate the workflow.

但 GAI 无法发起定义 GDS1 的判断。应该收集哪些数据？什么算作异常值与合法极值？相关人群是多少？数据生成过程中可能潜伏着哪些混杂因素？源域使用哪些测量约定，它们是否与研究问题一致？这些决策需要领域知识、情境理解和问题表述，正如我在第 3 节中所说，这是一种不可简化的人类能力。不了解该领域的学生将面临评估 GAI 关于数据探索的建议是否切中要害或离题的挑战。But GAI cannot originate the judgment calls that define GDS1. A student who does not understand the domain will be challenged to evaluate whether GAI’s suggestions for data exploration are on target or beside the point.

GDS1 是 GAI 下最具弹性的部门，因为它从来不主要关注技术执行。这始终是根据具体情况做出的判断，而这恰恰是 GAI 目前无法占据的领域。GDS1 is the most resilient division under GAI because it was never primarily about technical execution. It was always about judgment exercised in context, precisely the territory that GAI cannot currently occupy.

这就是麦克卢汉的见解：媒介就是信息。媒介决定了学生如何遇到计算思想（消息）。在 R/Python 介质中，编写 for 循环来模拟采样分布的学生同时学习了采样变异性和控制流。在 GAI 媒介中，学生提示系统产生相同的模拟，然后询问输出（测试边缘情况、修改假设、检查代码是否按照其声称的那样执行），从而通过不同的认知过程学习抽样可变性。教育挑战是确保 GAI 媒介能够发展真正的推理，而不是在不理解的情况下进行流畅的提示，这是第 1 节中提到的新手困境。This is McLuhan’s insight that the medium is the message. The medium shapes how students encounter computational ideas (messages). In the R/Python medium, students who wrote a for loop to simulate sampling distributions were simultaneously learning about sampling variability and control flow. In the GAI medium, students who prompt a system to produce the same simulation and then interrogate the output (testing edge cases, modifying assumptions, checking whether the code does what it claims) are learning about sampling variability through a different cognitive process. The educational challenge is to ensure that the GAI medium develops genuine reasoning rather than fluent prompting without understanding, which is the novice dilemma identified in Section 1.

2.2.4. GDS4：数据可视化和演示——混合案例2.2.4. GDS4: Data Visualization and Presentation — A Mixed Case

GAI 可以在最少的提示下生成标准统计图形，例如直方图、散点图、时间序列图、热图，并且在给出详细说明时可以生成更精细的可视化效果。仪表板构建曾经是一项专业技能，现在越来越成为 GAI 的能力范围。GAI can generate standard statistical graphics such as histograms, scatterplots, time series plots, heatmaps, with minimal prompting, and can produce more elaborate visualizations when given detailed instructions. Dashboard construction, once a specialized skill, is increasingly within GAI’s reach.

剩下的人类就是我所说的What remains human is what I will call意义建构sensemaking（Weick，1995）：将分析结果转化为特定受众可执行的理解的更广泛活动。在这种情况下，意义建构不仅仅包括狭义上的沟通，即制作清晰的图表和写得好的报告。它包括确定利益相关者是谁以及分析旨在为哪些决策提供信息；判断要显示什么和省略什么；用非技术受众可以推理的术语来描述不确定性；预测结果可能如何被误解；让合作者和决策者参与解释研究结果，而不是简单地传递结果。意义建构，如此定义，是一种关系和情境活动。它不仅需要了解数据，还需要了解使用数据的制度和人文环境。 GAI 可以起草摘要段落或建议图表类型，但它无法判断框架是否适合具有特定利害关系的特定受众。(Weick, 1995): the broader activity of translating analytical results into understanding that is actionable by specific audiences. Sensemaking in this context encompasses more than communication in the narrow sense of producing clear charts and well-written reports. It includes identifying who the stakeholders are and what decisions the analysis is meant to inform; judging what to show and what to leave out; framing uncertainty in terms that a non-technical audience can reason with; anticipating how results might be misinterpreted; and engaging collaborators and decision-makers in interpreting findings rather than simply delivering them. Sensemaking, so defined, is a relational and contextual activity. It requires understanding not just the data but the institutional and human environment in which the data will be used. GAI can draft a summary paragraph or suggest a chart type, but it cannot judge whether the framing is appropriate for a specific audience with specific stakes.

Cleveland (1993) 和 Wickham (2016) 将可视化的设计维度系统化为图形通信的原则方法，但也阻碍了完全自动化。选择正确的视觉编码、通过颜色或分面对附加变量进行分层，以及构建揭示结构而不是模糊结构的图形，这些都是需要理解数据和受众的判断行为。The design dimension of visualization, what Cleveland (1993) and Wickham (2016) have systematized as principled approaches to graphical communication, also resists full automation. Choosing the right visual encoding, layering additional variables through color or faceting, and composing graphics that reveal structure rather than obscure it are acts of judgment that require understanding both the data and the audience.

2.2.5。 GDS5：数据建模 - 自动化日常任务2.2.5. GDS5: Data Modeling — Automated for Routine Tasks

Breiman (2001) 确定的两种建模文化——生成建模（拟合随机模型并对数据生成机制进行推断）和预测建模——Both modeling cultures that Breiman (2001) identified—generative modeling (fitting stochastic models and making inferences about the data-generating mechanism) and predictive modeling—

完全符合 GAI 标准任务的能力范围。 GAI 系统可以拟合回归、训练随机森林、通过交叉验证调整超参数、评估预测性能以及解释系数，所有这些都来自自然语言提示。are well within GAI’s capability envelope for standard tasks. GAI systems can fit regressions, train random forests, tune hyperparameters via cross-validation, evaluate predictive performance, and interpret coefficients, all from natural-language prompts.

2.1 节中的收敛观察在这里特别适用。对于固定数据集和明确指定的建模任务，不同的 GAI 系统将收敛于本质上相同的拟合模型。线性回归就是线性回归；这些系数不取决于编写代码的系统。即使对于更复杂的模型，方法的收敛也意味着不同的系统将倾向于为标准问题选择相似的算法、相似的预处理步骤和相似的评估指标。The convergence observation from Section 2.1 applies with particular force here. For a fixed dataset and a well-specified modeling task, different GAI systems will converge on essentially the same fitted model. A linear regression is a linear regression; the coefficients do not depend on which system wrote the code. Even for more complex models, the convergence in approach means that different systems will tend to select similar algorithms, similar preprocessing steps, and similar evaluation metrics for standard problems.

抵制自动化的人类判断在于其他方面：在生成框架和预测框架（以及模型）之间进行选择，指定因果假设并评估它们是否站得住脚，识别模型的错误指定，理解模型输出的实质含义，以及在诊断发现问题时决定该怎么做。这些是构成统计和计算推理的活动，不是模型拟合管道的执行，而是根据科学问题和数据生成过程来设计和询问该管道。布雷曼的核心见解是，建模文化之间的选择本身就是一种判断，取决于目标是解释还是预测、数据可以支持什么以及利益相关者需要什么。这种选择是 GAI 无法做出的人类核心能力，因为它需要理解分析的目的，而不仅仅是其机制。The human judgment that resists automation lies elsewhere: in choosing between generative and predictive frameworks (and then which models), in specifying causal assumptions and assessing whether they are defensible, in recognizing model misspecification, in understanding what a model’s outputs mean substantively, and in deciding what to do when diagnostics reveal problems. These are the activities that constitute statistical and computational reasoning, not the execution of a model-fitting pipeline, but the design and interrogation of that pipeline in light of the scientific question and the data-generating process. Breiman’s central insight was that the choice between modeling cultures is itself a judgment call, one that depends on whether the goal is explanation or prediction, on what the data can support, and on what the stakeholders need. This choice is a human core competency that GAI cannot make, because it requires understanding the purpose of the analysis, not just its mechanics.

2.2.6。 GDS6：关于数据科学的科学 — 比以往任何时候都更重要2.2.6. GDS6: Science About Data Science — More Important Than Ever

多诺霍的第六个部门是他最具前瞻性的：对如何实践数据分析以及如何改进数据分析进行系统的、基于证据的研究。他设想了一个领域，该领域将研究常见的工作流程，衡量标准分析程序的有效性，并揭示已发表结果中的新兴模式和工件。他指出，科学文献的荟萃分析（Ioannidis，2005）、交叉研究验证练习（Bernau 等人，2014）和跨工作流程分析（Madigan 等人，2014）是这门科学在实践中的早期例子。 GAI 使 GDS6 变得更加重要，而不是变得更加重要。随着自动化分析变得普遍，通过促进语言模型而不是手动编码管道，对这些管道产生的内容进行系统评估的需求也相应增长。谁在检查 GAI 生成的分析？我们怎么知道它是正确的？我们如何评估在一系列研究中一个分析工作流程是否比另一个分析工作流程产生更有效的结论？Donoho’s sixth division is his most forward-looking: the systematic, evidence-based study of how data analysis is practiced and how it might be improved. He envisioned a field that would study commonly occurring workflows, measure the effectiveness of standard analytical procedures, and uncover emergent patterns and artifacts in published results. He pointed to meta-analysis of the scientific literature (Ioannidis, 2005), cross-study validation exercises (Bernau et al., 2014), and cross-workflow analyses (Madigan et al., 2014) as early examples of this science in action. GAI makes GDS6 more important, not less. As automated analysis becomes widespread, by prompting language models rather than by hand-coding pipelines, the need for systematic evaluation of what these pipelines produce grows correspondingly. Who is checking the GAIgenerated analysis? How do we know it is correct? How do we evaluate whether one analytical workflow produces more valid conclusions than another across a body of studies?

在这种情况下，再现性问题特别有趣。从某种意义上说，GAI 可能有助于再现性：可以记录生成的代码，可以记录提示，并且可以记录整个分析工作流程，比传统的手工编码分析更轻松。从另一种意义上说，GAI 引入了新的变异来源。语言模型输出可能因会话、模型版本和提供者而异。第 2.1 节中提到的 RLHF 收敛在一定程度上缓解了这种情况（不同的系统往往会为标准任务产生类似的输出），但版本更新、随机采样和即时敏感性意味着无法保证精确的再现性。理解和管理这种变化本身就是 GDS6 的问题。The question of reproducibility is particularly interesting in this context. In one sense, GAI may aid reproducibility: generated code can be logged, prompts can be recorded, and entire analytical workflows can be documented with less friction than traditional hand-coded analyses. In another sense, GAI introduces new sources of variation. Language model outputs can vary across sessions, across model versions, and across providers. The RLHF convergence noted in Section 2.1 mitigates this somewhat (different systems tend to produce similar outputs for standard tasks), but version updates, stochastic sampling, and prompt sensitivity mean that exact reproducibility is not guaranteed. Understanding and managing this variation is itself a GDS6 problem.

检查 GAI 生成的分析结果（代码、统计输出、解释）应遵循与人工审查他人工作相同的原则。代码审查是软件工程中一种行之有效的实践，其逻辑自然延伸到 GAI 输出：代码是否按照其声称的那样工作？边缘情况是否得到处理？诊断看起来合理吗？是否满足假设？Checking the results of GAI-generated analyses (code, statistical output, interpretations) should follow the same principles as human review of another human’s work. Code review is a wellestablished practice in software engineering, and its logic extends naturally to GAI outputs: does the code do what it claims? Are edge cases handled? Do diagnostics look reasonable? Are assumptions met?

展望未来，人们可以设想多代理审查工作流程，其中特定于任务的人工智能系统审查数据科学分析的不同组成部分，一个检查统计有效性，另一个审计公平性，另一个验证可重复性，然后由人类协调员综合评估并做出最终判断。这是 GDS6 的可操作性：一种结构化的、基于证据的方法来评估分析流程，并通过自动化辅助评估而不是取代评估。Looking further ahead, one can envision multi-agent review workflows in which task-specific AI systems review different components of a data science analysis, with one checking statistical validity, another auditing fairness, another verifying reproducibility, before a human orchestrator synthesizes the assessments and makes final judgments. This is GDS6 operationalized: a structured, evidence-based approach to evaluating analytical pipelines, with automation assisting the evaluation rather than replacing it.

3. Implications for Data Science Curriculum and Assessment

第 2 节指出，GAI 对大数据科学的六个部门的转变是不均衡的：技术执行基本上是自动化的，而充满判断力的活动本质上仍然是人类。实际问题是这对于设计数据科学教育的学习成果、课程和评估意味着什么。本节分四个部分介绍教育意义：关注人力核心、整合 GAI（POP 周期和结构化审查）、为什么持续参与应用领域至关重要，以及评估应如何改变。Section 2 established that GAI transforms the six divisions of Greater Data Science unevenly: technical execution is substantially automated, while judgment-laden activities remain essentially human. The practical question is what this means for designing learning outcomes, curricula, and assessments in data science education. This section presents the educational implications in four parts: focus on the human core, integrating GAI (POP cycles and structured review), why sustained engagement with an application area is essential, and how assessment should change.

3.1.人类核心：学生必须学习什么3.1. The Human Core: What Students Must Learn

GAI 剥离了许多数据科学堆栈，这些堆栈通常围绕（有时掩盖）需要判断的能力。这些能力与统计和数据科学教育的长期目标有很大重叠（ASA，2014；国家科学院，2018；De Veaux 等，2017；Tu 等，2024）。我将它们分为六个要素。GAI strips away much of the data science stack that often surrounds (and sometimes obscures) competencies requiring judgment. These competencies overlap substantially with longstanding goals in statistics and data science education (ASA, 2014; National Academies, 2018; De Veaux et al., 2017; Tu et al., 2024). 与 GAI 在数据科学领域的合作并不是单一的提示行为。 LLM 辅助数据分析的实证研究一致发现，单轮提示对于大多数实际任务来说是不够的：它们可能会生成可能运行但经常解决错误问题的代码，省略关键的预处理步骤，或生成听起来合理但不正确的解释（Liu 等人，2024）。数据分析本质上是多步骤且依赖于上下文的，其中每个决策（如何处理缺失值、要包含哪些变量、要运行哪些诊断）都取决于在先前步骤中学到的内容。这种顺序的、充满判断力的结构无法压缩成单个提示。Working with GAI in data science is not a single act of prompting. Empirical studies of LLMassisted data analysis consistently find that single-turn prompts are insufficient for most realistic tasks: they may produce code that might run but often addresses the wrong question, omit critical preprocessing steps, or generate plausible-sounding but incorrect interpretations (Liu et al., 2024). Data analysis is inherently multi-step and context-dependent where each decision (how to handle missing values, which variables to include, what diagnostics to run) depends on what was learned in prior steps. This sequential, judgment-laden structure resists compression into a single prompt.

因此，有效使用 GAI 进行数据分析是一个迭代过程，我称之为 POP 循环：提示、输出、提示。从业者发出提示，请求对数据集进行探索性分析，检查输出，然后根据输出显示的内容发出改进的提示：请求诊断、测试边缘情况、修改假设或探测意外结果。 （有关 POP 周期的说明，请参阅附录）每个周期都需要人为判断。输出是否正确？它是否解决了正确的问题？接下来我应该检查什么？重要的是，POP 周期通常涉及检索增强生成 (RAG)：从业者将数据集、现有代码、文档或先前的分析上传到对话中，以便模型在特定的分析上下文中运行，而不是仅根据一般知识生成。这种基于实际数据和项目工件的基础使得 POP 循环成为真正的分析实践，而不是即时工程中的抽象练习。Effective use of GAI for data analysis is therefore an iterative process I call the POP cycle: prompt, output, prompt. The practitioner issues a prompt requesting, say, an exploratory analysis of a dataset, examines the output, and then issues a refined prompt based on what the output reveals: asking for diagnostics, testing an edge case, modifying an assumption, or probing an unexpected result. (see Appendix for an illustration of the POP cycle) Each cycle requires human judgment. Is the output correct? Does it address the right question? What should I check next? Critically, POP cycles typically involve retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): the practitioner uploads the dataset, existing code, documentation, or prior analyses into the conversation so that the model operates on the specific analytical context rather than generating from general knowledge alone. This grounding in actual data and project artifacts is what makes the POP cycle a genuine analytical practice rather than an abstract exercise in prompt engineering.

POP 循环是在 GAI 媒介中锻炼人类核心能力的工具。问题的表述形成了最初的提示及其后续的改进。统计推理决定诊断的选择。因果思维决定了建议的分析是否合适。意义建构指导如何构建最终输出。如果没有这些能力，POP 循环就会退化为浅循环——接受第一个输出，或者在不知道要寻找什么的情况下进行提示——这是第 1 节中提到的新手困境。在 GAI 之前，学生通过编写和修改代码来学习推理数据。在 GAI 介质中，POP 循环是类似的载体。认知工作的要求并不低；The POP cycle is the vehicle through which the human core competencies are exercised in the GAI medium. Problem formulation shapes the initial prompt and its successive refinements. Statistical reasoning governs the choice of diagnostics. Causal thinking determines whether a suggested analysis is appropriate. Sensemaking guides how the final output is framed. Without these competencies, the POP cycle degenerates into a shallow loop—accepting the first output, or prompting without knowing what to look for—which is the novice dilemma identified in Section 1. Before GAI, students learned to reason about data by writing and revising code. In the GAI medium, the POP cycle is the analogous vehicle. The cognitive work is not less demanding;

if anything, it requires more conceptual understanding, because the student must evaluate outputs they did not construct line by line.

Code review is a well-established practice in software engineering: a second person reads the code, checks that it does what it claims, tests edge cases, and verifies that the logic is sound. 展望未来，专门的人工智能系统可能会在人类做出最终判断之前审查分析的不同组成部分：一个检查统计有效性，另一个审核公平性，另一个验证可重复性。在这个世界中，通过综合自动化评估、解决冲突并做出需要上下文理解的判断，人类的角色可以从唯一的审核者转变为协调者。这直接与 Donoho 的 GDS6 相关：一种评估分析流程的结构化、部分自动化的方法，由人工协调员询问分析是否有效以及结论是否有根据。Looking further ahead, specialized AI systems may review different components of an analysis before a human makes final judgments: one checking statistical validity, another auditing for fairness, another verifying reproducibility. In this world the human’s role could shift from sole reviewer to orchestrator by synthesizing automated assessments, resolving conflicts, and making the judgment calls that require contextual understanding. This connects directly to Donoho’s GDS6: a structured, partially automated approach to evaluating analytical pipelines, with the human orchestrator asking whether the analysis is valid and whether the conclusions are warranted.

4. 结论4. Conclusion

生成式人工智能依赖于通用方法，例如随着计算而扩展的搜索和统计学习（Sutton，2019），而不是透明的、特定领域的模型。这些方法足够强大，可以自动化过去十年开发的课程中嵌入的大部分常规数据科学工作流程。但从图基到多诺霍的知识传统长期以来一直认为，这种工作流程无论多么必要，都不是该领域最重要的贡献。最重要的工作始终是充满判断力、依赖情境、面向人的活动，包括提出问题、设计研究、推理证据以及为需要的人理解结果。 GAI 创造了这个层次结构Generative AI rests on general-purpose methods such as search and statistical learning that scale with computation (Sutton, 2019) rather than on transparent, domain-specific models. These methods are powerful enough to automate much of the routine data science workflow embedded in curricula developed in the last decade. But the intellectual tradition from Tukey through Donoho has long argued that this workflow, however necessary, was never the field’s most important contribution. The most important work was always the judgment-laden, contextdependent, human-facing activity of formulating questions, designing studies, reasoning about evidence, and making sense of results for the people who need them. Future data scientists will need to pose questions, design studies, interrogate models, and take responsibility for the consequences of automated pipelines. Understanding the commercial and institutional contexts that produce data, working effectively within a medium capable of generating plausible but potentially misleading outputs at scale, and exercising judgment about when conventional approaches fail are not skills that automation renders obsolete.

数据科学家在 GAI 重塑的职业中处于独特的地位。这些系统的统计和计算基础——梯度下降、正则化、交叉验证、强化学习、偏差方差权衡——是该学科自己的工具包的一部分。当律师、医生或教育工作者将人工智能视为外部干扰时，数据科学界的许多人对这些系统的工作原理有内部了解，而不仅仅是它们产生的结果。正如林等人。 （2025）认为，统计学家和数据科学家可以成为人工智能研究的领导者，而不仅仅是合作者，正是因为人工智能工具本质上是算法，其行为可以使用学科自己的方法进行研究。其教育意义在于，数据科学课程不仅应该让学生能够使用 GAI，还应该让学生从内部理解其行为，并推理输出为何收敛、幻觉从何而来，以及如何评估模型的可靠性。这是其他领域无法比拟的优势，应该刻意培养。Data scientists are uniquely positioned among the professions being reshaped by GAI. The statistical and computational foundations of these systems—gradient descent, regularization, cross-validation, reinforcement learning, bias-variance tradeoff—are part of the discipline’s own toolkit. Where lawyers, physicians, or educators encounter AI as an external disruption, many in the data science community have an insider’s understanding of how these systems work, not just what they produce. As Lin et al. (2025) have argued, statisticians and data scientists can be leaders rather than merely collaborators in AI research, precisely because AI tools are essentially algorithms whose behavior can be studied using the discipline’s own methods. The educational implication is that data science curricula should equip students not only to use GAI but to understand its behavior from the inside and reason about why outputs converge, where hallucination comes from, and how to evaluate model reliability. This is an advantage no other field can claim, and it should be cultivated deliberately.

无论人类的核心能力是永久不可还原的，反映了从世界数据中学习的内在结构，还是仅仅超出了当前人工智能系统的能力范围，课程的含义都是一样的：对数据、证据和后果进行真正推理的学生将为任何未来做好准备，包括人工智能系统最终共享这些能力的未来。媒介已经改变，但人的核心却一直存在。围绕这些能力定义学习成果和课程，使教学与学生练习的新媒介保持一致，并评估推理而不是答案，这是生成人工智能时代数据科学教育的工作。Whether the human core competencies are permanently irreducible, reflecting the inherent structure of learning from data about the world, or merely beyond the reach of current AI systems, the curricular implications are the same: students who develop genuine reasoning about data, evidence, and consequences will be prepared for any future, including one in which AI systems eventually share these capacities. The medium has changed but the human core endures. Defining learning outcomes and curriculum around these competencies, aligning instruction with the new medium through which students will practice, and assessing reasoning rather than answers is the work of data science education in the generative AI era.
