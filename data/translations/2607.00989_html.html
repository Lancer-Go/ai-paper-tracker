1. 简介Introduction

想象一下，一位博物馆专业人士试图了解不同类型的游客如何浏览和参与展览。 例如，初次游客可能会遵循现场标牌并主要关注标志性艺术品，而经常访问的当地艺术学生可能会采取自我引导的路线，有选择地重新访问喜欢的部分。 分析这些不同的模式为个性化路线推荐带来了巨大的希望Imagine a museum professional seeking to understand how different types of visitors navigate and engage with exhibitions. For instance, a first-time tourist may follow on-site signage and focus primarily on iconic artworks, whereas a local art student who frequently visits might adopt a self-directed route, selectively revisiting favored sections. Analyzing these distinct patterns holds substantial promise for personalized route recommendation(recommend_2021; target_2019)、适应性展览设计, adaptive exhibition design(design_2024)，以及细致入微的参与度评估, and nuanced engagement evaluation(experiment_2024)，对于文化机构和公共场所来说越来越重要, which are increasingly important for cultural institutions and public venues(semantic_museum_2021; generator2024he; recommend_2021; hybrid2025zuo).

仅关注原始空间路径或总客流量统计数据不足以解释游客的移动。尽管两位访客可能穿越重叠的区域，但他们的参与方式却有所不同。 为了捕捉这些不断演变的模式，先前的工作引入了语义轨迹的概念Focusing solely on raw spatial paths or aggregate footfall statistics is insufficient to explain visitors’ movement. Although the two visitors may traverse overlapping areas, their engagement unfolds differently. To capture these evolving patterns, prior work introduced the concept of semantic trajectories(Semantic_model_2013; semantic_prediction_2011; mining2010ying)，它通过描述整个轨迹的语义注释来增强空间和时间坐标。 语义注释不受限制，但可以大致分为两大类：行为和目标, which augment spatial and temporal coordinates with semantic annotations describing the trajectory in its entirety. Semantic annotations are not limited but can be broadly categorized into two main types: behaviors and goals(semantic_museum_2021; warehouse2019michael)。 行为注释反映了运动的现实（. Behavior annotations reflect the actuality of movement (例如e.g.，在展览中闲逛），而目标注释表明了运动的潜力（, wandering around an exhibition), whereas goal annotations indicate the potentiality of movement (例如e.g.，目的是参观蒙娜丽莎），即使目标最终没有实现。, aiming to visit the Mona Lisa), even if the goal is not ultimately achieved.

然而，收集语义轨迹数据在操作上要求很高。首先，高质量的时空跟踪通常需要昂贵且复杂的传感基础设施（However, collecting semantic trajectory data is operationally demanding. First, high-quality spatiotemporal tracking often requires costly and complex sensing infrastructure (例如e.g.、传感器、可穿戴设备或多摄像头系统），但仍然受到噪声和覆盖范围差距的影响, sensors, wearables, or multi-camera systems) and still suffers from noise and coverage gaps(position2024hailu; track2025auro)。 其次，语义无法直接观察。 将原始轨迹丰富为语义表示通常依赖于事后注释技术，这需要额外的上下文源和领域专业知识. Second, semantics can not be directly observed. Enriching raw trajectories into semantic representations typically relies on post-hoc annotation techniques, which require additional contextual sources and domain expertise(enrichment2023lettich; semitri2011yan)。 第三，与轨迹相关的访客档案很难获得。年龄、兴趣或时间预算等属性很少能大规模获得，并且收集这些属性通常依赖于侵入性调查，这引发了隐私和同意问题。 最后，许多现实世界的环境面临着冷启动场景，几乎没有或没有历史访客轨迹数据。例如，当一个新的展览推出时，无法获得之前的参观轨迹，但仍然必须进行路线引导。. Third, visitor profiles that associate with trajectories are difficult to obtain. Attributes such as age, interests, or time budget are rarely available at scale, and collecting them often relies on intrusive surveys, which raises privacy and consent concerns. Finally, many real-world settings face a cold-start scenario with little or no historical visitor trajectory data. For instance, when a new exhibition is launched, prior visitor trajectories are unavailable, yet route guidance must still be made.

受这些限制的启发，我们的目标是为轨迹感知从业者开发一种模拟工具，他们根据访客的移动做出决策，以生成适合他们需求的语义轨迹。 我们的工作重点是分区环境Motivated by the limitations, we aim to develop a simulation tool for trajectory-aware practitioners who make decisions based on visitor movements to generate semantic trajectories tailored to their needs. Our work focuses on zoned environments(pena2012problem; syntax2021yamu) (例如e.g.、博物馆、展览馆和购物中心），它们是有意划分为语义不同区域的空间边界区域。 需要克服三个主要挑战。 首先，模拟语义轨迹需要平衡物理合理性和语义连贯性。 模拟必须尊重空间限制，例如可达性和移动成本，同时还要反映游客的特征和访问的语义逻辑。 其次，实现有效和灵活的创作仍然具有挑战性，因为许多目标用户缺乏技术或建模专业知识。 在从业者的分析工作流程中，他们需要反复调整假设并探索替代方案以收敛于期望的结果。 现有的商业工具，如 Anylogic, museum, exhibition, and shopping mall), which are spatially bounded areas intentionally divided into semantically distinct zones. There are three major challenges to overcome. First, simulating semantic trajectories requires balancing physical plausibility and semantic coherence. Simulations must respect spatial constraints, such as accessibility and movement costs, while also reflecting visitors’ characteristics and the semantic logic of the visit. Second, enabling effective and flexible authoring remains challenging, as many target users lack technical or modeling expertise. During practitioners’ analytic workflow, they need to repeatedly adjust assumptions and explore alternative scenarios to converge on desired outcomes. Existing commercial tools like Anylogic111https://www.anylogic.com/和 PTV Viswalkand PTV Viswalk222https://www.ptvgroup.com/en/products/pedestrian-simulation-software-ptv-viswalk主要需要陡峭的学习曲线，需要熟悉参数校准和工程工作流程。 复杂性限制了此类工具在实际应用中的实际可用性。 第三，解释生成的轨迹并非易事。在实践中，从业者不仅需要了解访客移动的位置，还需要了解为什么会发生特定决策，例如访问或跳过特定区域，以建立信任并将模拟输出转化为可行的见解。primarily entail a steep learning curve, requiring familiarity with parameter calibration and engineering workflows. The complexity limits the practical usability of such tools in real-world applications. Third, interpreting generated trajectories is nontrivial. In practice, practitioners need to understand not only where visitors move, but also why specific decisions occur, such as visiting or skipping particular zones, to build trust and translate simulation outputs into actionable insights.

2.1. 语义轨迹建模与分析Semantic Trajectory Modeling and Analysis

由于需要超越原始运动点而转向可解释的表示，语义轨迹在移动社区中得到了广泛的研究Motivated by the need to move beyond raw movement points toward interpretable representations, the semantic trajectory has been extensively studied in the mobility communities(giannotti_unveiling_2011; mining2007gian; engine2008gko)。 轨迹由运动轨迹的两个特定时空位置来识别. Semantic trajectory enriches these coordinates with contextual annotations to enhance the analysis of movement data and facilitate the discovery of semantically implicit behavior patterns(Semantic_model_2013; survey2015albanna)。 一种有前途的丰富方法是利用本体和链接数据在语义上将轨迹模式与更广泛的行为和上下文知识联系起来. A promising enrichment approach is to leverage ontologies and linked data to semantically connect trajectory patterns with broader behavioral and contextual knowledge(recommend_2021)。 语义轨迹分析具有广泛的实际应用。 例如，康塔里尼斯. 由于数据短缺和隐私限制，研究人员将注意力集中在轨迹模拟方法上，这些方法可以分为基于规则的方法Due to data shortage and privacy restrictions, researchers have focused on trajectory simulation methods, which can be categorized into rule-based(LAMMEL2015950)和数据驱动and data-driven(rasouli2022pedformerpedestrianbehaviorprediction; hybrid2025zuo)。 基于规则的模型通过预先确定的机制（例如数学方程）对行走轨迹进行编码. Rule-based models encode the walking trajectories through predetermined mechanisms, such as mathematical equations(integrateXi2011)或元胞自动机or cellular automata(seitz2016superposition)。 数据驱动的仿真模型使用深度神经网络通过学习训练数据来生成轨迹. Data-driven simulation models use deep neural networks to generate trajectories by learning from the training data(Zhang_2020_CVPR; graph2022fang; cao2025holisticsemanticrepresentationnavigational)。 然而，现有的方法很难捕捉运动背后更深层次的语义逻辑，导致注释标记了运动发生的位置，但对运动发生原因的理解却很有限。 我们利用法学硕士作为语义注释引擎，连接低级轨迹和高级解释。 上下文感知使其能够将注释与上下文输入对齐，例如区域描述、访客资料、时间约束和环境语义。 法学硕士还可以提供超出预定义的基于规则的注释类型的灵活性，并动态调整粒度以满足不同从业者的需求。. However, existing methods struggle to capture the deeper semantic logic underlying movement, resulting in annotations that label where movement occurs but offer limited understanding of why it occurs. We utilize LLMs to serve as a semantic annotation engine that bridges low-level trajectories and high-level interpretations. The context-awareness enables it to align annotations with contextual inputs, such as zone descriptions, visitor profiles, temporal constraints, and environmental semantics. LLMs can also provide flexibility beyond predefined rule-based annotation types and dynamically adapt the granularity to suit diverse practitioner needs.

8.4. 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

我们了解我们系统的一些局限性并提出未来可能的方向。 首先，虽然我们当前的设计主要依赖于文本输入，但结合多模态信号（例如视觉观察和声音交互）可以进一步增强模拟工作流程的真实性。 其次，LLM 集成带来了大量的计算开销和延迟。 虽然我们实现并行计算来帮助减轻延迟，但它并没有从根本上解决基于LLM的组件的高计算成本。 未来的工作可以探索选择性调用和混合设计以提高效率。 第三，由于LLM生成的输出固有的不确定性和可变性，用户可能会发现很难完全信任系统的决策。 缺乏可预测性可能会影响用户信心，特别是在需要可靠且负责任的决策的情况下。未来的工作可以探索交互机制，例如不确定性估计，以增强信任。We comprehend some limitations of our system and suggest possible future directions. First, while our current design primarily relies on textual inputs, incorporating multimodal signals, such as visual observations and vocal interactions, could further enhance the realism of the simulation workflow. Second, LLM integration introduces significant computational overhead and latency. Although we implement parallel computation to help mitigate latency, it does not fundamentally resolve the high computational cost of LLM-based components. Future work could explore selective invocation and hybrid designs to improve efficiency. Third, due to the inherent uncertainty and variability of LLM-generated outputs, users may find it difficult to fully trust the system’s decisions. The lack of predictability may affect user confidence, particularly in scenarios that require reliable and accountable decision-making.
