1.1 医疗人工智能的临床部署差距The Clinical Deployment Gap in Medical AI

用于医学图像分析的深度学习模型已经取得了一些成果 超越领域，专家级判别性能Deep learning models for medical image analysis have achieved, and in some domains exceeded, expert-level discriminative performance[Gulshan et al., 2016, Esteva et al., 2017]。 在胸部 X 射线数据集上训练的卷积神经网络现在可以实现 肺炎检测AUC值超过0.99. The “Clever Hans” phenomenon — where models exploit dataset-specific shortcuts rather than genuine pathological features — is now well-documented across medical imaging domains[DeGrave et al., 2021, Zech et al., 2018, Oakden-Rayner et al., 2020]。胸部 X 射线分类器已被证明可以利用设备标记， 患者定位伪影和起搏器的存在作为代理鉴别器. A critical reconceptualisation follows from the reliability failures documented above. Clinical ROI alignment is desirable, but is not the fundamental requirement for trust. What is fundamental is whether the model consistently applies its learned decision strategy across similar cases.

1.5 C-Score：贡献和定位The C-Score: Contribution and Positioning

我们建议We propose theC-分数C-Score（一致性分数）作为衡量标准 直接解决类内一致性差距。核心论文是 可解释性本身无法被证明，因为没有客观的 “正确”解释的基本事实，但一致性 可解释性是可以量化的。始终如一地关注的模型 具有相同诊断的患者具有相同的解剖区域 至少表明其解释是可重复的。这个 可重复性是临床可解释性的必要前提。(Consistency Score) as a metric that directly addresses the intra-class consistency gap. The core thesis is that explainability itself cannot be proven because there is no objective ground truth for what a “correct” explanation looks like, but consistency in explainability can be quantified. A model that consistently attends to the same anatomical regions across patients with the same diagnosis demonstrates at minimum that its explanations are reproducible. This reproducibility is a necessary precondition for clinical interpretability.

C 分数被制定为置信度加权、强度强调的 正确分类的相同实例的平均成对软 IoU 给定模型检查点的类。该指标专门设计用于 使用图像级标签训练的图像分类任务，没有 像素级注释，其中空间解释是事后得出的 使用基于 CAM 的归因方法。在此设置下，C-Score 提供 空间解释一致性的无注释测量，可以 在每个训练时期进行计算并应用于不同的 CAM 技术和 CNN 架构。The C-Score is formulated as a confidence-weighted, intensity-emphasised mean pairwise soft IoU across correctly classified instances of the same class at a given model checkpoint. The metric is designed specifically for image classification tasks trained with image-level labels and no pixel-level annotations, where spatial explanations are derived post hoc using CAM-based attribution methods. Under this setting, C-Score provides an annotation-free measure of spatial explanation consistency that can be computed at every training epoch and applied across different CAM techniques and CNN architectures.

7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 C-Score（一致性分数），这是一种置信度加权的、 用于量化类内解释的无注释度量 医学图像分类中基于 CAM 的方法的再现性。 对六种 CAM 技术、三种 CNN 架构和三十种技术进行了评估 在 Kermany 胸部 X 射线数据集上的训练时期，C-Score 揭示了三个 AUC 的独特机制——标准不可见的一致性分离 分类指标。 DenseNet201展示了最有利的 跨两个维度的临床部署概况。 C-Score 提供 实用的、可连续计算的质量保证信号 补充现有的评估框架并满足一致性 临床人工智能监管指南中出现的要求。We proposed the C-Score (Consistency Score), a confidence-weighted, annotation-free metric for quantifying intra-class explanation reproducibility of CAM-based methods in medical image classification. Evaluated across six CAM techniques, three CNN architectures, and thirty training epochs on the Kermany chest X-ray dataset, C-Score revealed three distinct mechanisms of AUC–consistency dissociation invisible to standard classification metrics. DenseNet201 demonstrated the most favourable profile for clinical deployment across both dimensions. C-Score provides a practical, continuously computable quality assurance signal that complements existing evaluation frameworks and satisfies the consistency requirements emerging from regulatory guidance on clinical AI.
