摘要Abstract

移动操纵需要机器人预测运动和手臂运动如何共同改变未来的观察和控制。现有的世界动作模型主要是为固定底座平台开发的，没有明确区分相机自我运动与底座和手臂动作。在这里，我们介绍 DECOWAM，一种全身世界动作模型，它通过专用的条件接口分离这些因素。 DECOWAM 冻结了适应的 FastWAM 主干并训练残差适配器，这是从特权观察中提取的相当于动作的未来瓶颈、对抗性分离的基础和臂潜伏以及用于视频预测的基础速度调节。我们进一步介绍Mobile manipulation requires a robot to predict how locomotion and arm motion jointly alter future observations and control. Existing world–action models, developed largely for fixed-base platforms, do not explicitly distinguish camera ego-motion from base and arm actions. Here we introduce DECOWAM, a whole-body world–action model that separates these factors through dedicated conditional interfaces. DECOWAM freezes an adapted FastWAM backbone and trains residual adapters, an action-equivalent future bottleneck distilled from privileged observations, adversarially separated base and arm latents, and base-velocity conditioning for video prediction. We further introduce手臂狗ARMDOG，一个同步视频、全身状态和动作以及语言的真实机器人数据集。在固定重放协议上，DECOWAM 比 FastWAM 改进了未来视频和动作预测，通过 2595 万个可训练的自适应参数将动作 MSE 降低了 21.7%。在每种方法的 79 次闭环试验中，它在比较系统中实现了观察到的最高的全身协调性和基础位移稳健性，同时任务完成情况仍与最强基线相当。这些结果表明，实施例感知分解可以支持移动视点下的参数高效联合视觉预测和全身控制。, a real-robot dataset that synchronizes video, whole-body state and action, and language. On a fixed replay protocol, DECOWAM improved both future-video and action prediction over FastWAM, reducing action MSE by 21.7% with 25.95M trainable adaptation parameters. Across 79 closed-loop trials per method, it achieved the highest observed whole-body coordination and base-displacement robustness among the compared systems, while task completion remained comparable to the strongest baseline. These results show that embodiment-aware factorization can support parameter-efficient joint visual prediction and whole-body control under moving viewpoints.

七VII结论Conclusion

我们提出了 DECOWAM，一种全身世界动作模型，它明确区分了基础运动、手臂操作和相机自我运动。已同步We presented DECOWAM, a whole-body world–action model that explicitly separates base motion, arm manipulation, and camera ego-motion. On synchronized手臂狗ARMDOG数据、分阶段冻结适应和未来等效瓶颈改进了每个报告的 FastWAM 视频/动作诊断，同时将 Stage-2 可训练参数从 6.021B 减少到 25.95M。 DECOWAM 在预测未来 8 个 RGB 帧的同时仍然接近强大的 VLA Action 性能，并且在每种方法 79 次试验中领先于真实机器人接近、传输、协调和位移鲁棒性率。综合结果建立了一个联合模型，将竞争控制与全身运动下场景演化的显式预测相结合。data, staged frozen adaptation and the future-equivalent bottleneck improve every reported FastWAM video/action diagnostic while reducing Stage-2 trainable parameters from 6.021B to 25.95M. DECOWAM remains close to strong VLA Action performance while predicting eight future RGB frames, and leads the real-robot approach, transport, coordination, and displacement-robustness rates over 79 trials per method. The combined results establish a joint model that couples competitive control with explicit prediction of scene evolution under whole-body motion.
