1 简介Introduction

现代人工智能能力与计算基础设施密不可分。占主导地位的 训练模式假设密集的加速器集群、高带宽 互连、集中调度的作业以及可以 财务、运营和管理整个培训体系。该模型已生产 非凡的系统，但它也创造了一条狭窄的参与路径： 谁控制了最大的集群，谁就控制了边境。Modern AI capability is inseparable from compute infrastructure. The dominant training pattern assumes dense accelerator clusters, high-bandwidth interconnects, centrally scheduled jobs, and a single organization that can finance, operate, and govern the full training stack. This model has produced remarkable systems, but it also creates a narrowing path for participation: whoever controls the largest clusters controls the frontier.

Spheroid Labs 的动机很简单：更多的人工智能工作负载应该是可训练的 不依赖于超大规模。去中心化的人工智能协议应该允许 许多较小的工作人员，包括消费者和专业消费者 GPU，为 无需任何一名工作人员持有完整模型、完整优化器的训练 状态，或整个网络的同步视图。困难的部分不仅在于 系统工程。通过深度网络的标准反向传播是 本质上是全局的：早期层的误差信号取决于前向层 以及后面每一层的向后计算。如果我们只是对标准进行分片 跨不可靠的边缘设备、通信和同步进行培训 变相恢复中心化集群。Spheroid Labs’ motivation is simple: more AI workloads should be trainable without depending on a hyperscaler. A decentralized AI protocol should allow many smaller workers, including consumer and prosumer GPUs, to contribute to training without any one worker holding the full model, the full optimizer state, or a synchronous view of the whole network. The hard part is not only systems engineering. Standard backpropagation through deep networks is inherently global: the error signal for an early layer depends on the forward and backward computation of every later layer. If we merely shard standard training across unreliable edge devices, communication and synchronization recover the centralized cluster in disguise.

当前分散培训的情况。Where current decentralized training stands.

现有的去中心化人工智能系统大多保留了标准的学习问题， 改变它周围的系统包装。联合平均、本地 SGD、DiLoCo、 OpenDiLoCo 和 INTELLECT-1 减少了全模型副本之间的通信。 Gossip 和去中心化 SGD 通过平均去除中央参数服务器 对等图。 志愿者计算系统，例如 Hivemind、Learning@home、Petals 和 协作变压器训练表明异构互联网机器可以 参与大型模型执行或训练。 较新的去中心化人工智能努力做出了额外的赌注：管道并行广域网 培训、无需许可的强化学习参与、验证和激励 层。 这些都是重要的系统进步，但大多数仍然分布传统的 全模型目标、传统目标的管道阶段或 培训后的工作量。 Spheroid 的赌注不同：制作学习单元 本身就是本地的。而不是要求边缘工人持有整个模型或 模拟数据中心培训循环，公开工作人员的块本地目标 可以直接优化并组合成一个模型。Existing decentralized AI systems mostly keep the standard learning problem and change the systems wrapper around it. Federated averaging, local SGD, DiLoCo, OpenDiLoCo, and INTELLECT-1 reduce communication between full-model replicas. Gossip and decentralized SGD remove a central parameter server by averaging over peer graphs. Volunteer-compute systems such as Hivemind, Learning@home, Petals, and collaborative transformer training show that heterogeneous internet machines can participate in large-model execution or training. Newer decentralized AI efforts make additional bets: pipeline-parallel WAN training, permissionless RL participation, verification, and incentive layers. These are important systems advances, but most still distribute a conventional full-model objective, a pipeline stage of a conventional objective, or a post-training workload. Spheroid’s bet is different: make the learning unit itself local. Instead of asking edge workers to hold the whole model or to simulate a data-center training loop, expose block-local objectives that workers can optimize directly and compose into one model.

论文。Thesis.

当训练目标达到时，去中心化人工智能在技术上变得可行 以与硬件网络相同的粒度进行本地化。我们称之为 原则Decentralized AI becomes technically plausible when the training objective is made local at the same granularity as the hardware network. We call this principle目标-硬件协调objective-hardware alignment。在 BlockTrain 中，自然 硬件单元是一个拥有一个块副本的工作者。自然的学习 目标是块局部损失。自然同步单位为 块内平均，而不是全模型全归约。. In BlockTrain, the natural hardware unit is a worker that owns one block replica. The natural learning objective is a block-local loss. The natural synchronization unit is within-block averaging, not full-model all-reduce.

为什么这与联邦学习不同。Why this is different from federated learning.

联合平均Federated averaging(McMahan et al., 2017)和本地 SGD 方法减少 通过在平均之前让全模型副本在本地进行训练来进行通信。 他们很强大，但每个参与者仍然需要保持和训练充分 模型。这与最强大的去中心化人工智能目标不相容：训练 其完整优化器状态大于消费者 GPU 的模型，而每个模型 工人只拥有一块易于处理的切片。我们的协议是块本地的：完整模型 作为组装对象存在，但训练内存随活动块扩展。and local SGD methods reduce communication by letting full-model replicas train locally before averaging. They are powerful, but each participant still needs to hold and train the full model. That is incompatible with the strongest decentralized AI goal: training models whose full optimizer state is larger than a consumer GPU while each worker only owns a tractable slice. Our protocol is block-local: the full model exists as an assembled object, but training memory scales with the active block.

6 结论Conclusion

本文提出 Spheroid BlockTrain 作为去中心化的技术路线 通过改变学习单元进行预训练。而不是普通的分发 全模型反向传播，BlockTrain 公开了块局部去噪目标 可以通过仅持有一个块和共享嵌入的工作人员来优化。This paper presents Spheroid BlockTrain as a technical route to decentralized pretraining by changing the unit of learning. Instead of distributing ordinary full-model backpropagation, BlockTrain exposes block-local denoising objectives that can be optimized by workers holding only one block and the shared embedding.
