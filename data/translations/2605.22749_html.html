摘要Abstract

现代智能电网依赖于密集的测量基础设施、通信链路和智能现场设备。虽然这改善了监督和控制，但也增加了网络物理破坏的脆弱性。操作员必须区分物理事件（例如故障或线路干扰）与恶意行为（例如虚假数据注入或未经授权的命令执行）。本章使用著名的 MSU/ORNL 电力系统攻击数据集研究此问题。所提出的方法将机器学习与基于遗传算法的特征选择相结合。目标是双重的：准确分类攻击和自然事件，并确定一组精简的物理信息 PMU/IED 测量是否可以支持可靠的检测。评估了多种基线模型，包括逻辑回归、RBF-SVM、XGBoost、随机森林和额外树。结果表明，基于树的集成模型对于所考虑的数据集是最有效的，而额外树提供了最强的全功能基线。特征选择后，GA + Extra Trees 模型在五次运行中将干净的 PMU 特征空间从 112 个属性减少到平均 27.4 个属性，同时将 Macro-F1 从 0.9118 增加到 0.9212，将 ROC-AUC 从 0.9791 增加到 0.9837。这些结果表明许多同步电气测量是多余的。基于相量的特征的紧凑子集仍然可以在智能电网中提供准确且可解释的异常检测。Modern smart grids rely on dense measurement infrastructures, communication links, and intelligent field devices. Although this improves supervision and control, it also increases vulnerability to cyber-physical disruptions. Operators must distinguish physical incidents, such as faults or line disturbances, from malicious actions, such as false data injection or unauthorized command execution. This chapter investigates this problem using the well-known MSU/ORNL Power System Attack Dataset. The proposed method combines machine learning with genetic-algorithm-based feature selection. The objective is twofold: to classify attack and natural events accurately, and to determine whether a reduced set of physically informative PMU/IED measurements can support reliable detection. Several baseline models are evaluated, including logistic regression, RBF-SVM, XGBoost, Random Forest, and Extra Trees. The results show that tree-based ensemble models are the most effective for the considered dataset, with Extra Trees providing the strongest full-feature baseline. After feature selection, the GA + Extra Trees model reduces the clean PMU feature space from 112 attributes to an average of 27.4 attributes over five runs, while increasing macro-F1 from 0.9118 to 0.9212 and ROC-AUC from 0.9791 to 0.9837. These results indicate that many synchronized electrical measurements are redundant. A compact subset of phasor-based features can still provide accurate and interpretable anomaly detection in smart grids.

1 简介Introduction

现代电力系统不再局限于传统的电力基础设施。他们现在将物理网格组件与通信网络、测量设备和基于软件的控制系统相结合。这种转变很大程度上受到智能电表、相量测量单元 (PMU)、智能电子设备 (IED)、分布式发电、可再生能源、储能系统、电动汽车和物联网层等技术的影响Modern power systems are no longer limited to traditional electrical infrastructure. They now combine physical grid components with communication networks, measurement devices, and software-based control systems. This transition has been largely shaped by technologies such as smart meters, phasor measurement units (PMUs), intelligent electronic devices (IEDs), distributed generation, renewable energy sources, energy storage systems, electric vehicles, and IoT-based layers[15, 16, 17]。这些发展使网格更加灵活、可观察和响应迅速，但也带来了新的网络物理安全挑战. These developments have made the grid more flexible, observable, and responsive, but they have also introduced new cyber-physical security challenges[18, 2]。传感器和通信通道的使用不断增加，提高了态势感知能力，并支持负载预测、需求响应、故障诊断和更快的系统响应等应用. The growing use of sensors and communication channels improves situational awareness and enables applications such as load forecasting, demand response, fault diagnosis, and faster system response[18, 19]。与此同时，这些技术也带来了额外的漏洞。错误的测量、受损的传感器、恶意的控制命令或错误的警报可能会影响控制中心的决策. At the same time, these technologies create additional points of vulnerability. Faulty measurements, compromised sensors, malicious control commands, or false alarms can influence decisions made at the control center[18, 23]。换句话说，电网的可靠性现在与其网络组件的安全性和可靠性密切相关. In other words, the reliability of the electrical grid is now closely connected to the security and dependability of its cyber components[18, 2]。人工智能（AI）已成为分析智能电网生成的数据的重要工具。机器学习和深度学习技术广泛应用于异常检测、入侵检测、电压稳定性评估、故障分类和能源管理. Artificial intelligence (AI) has become an important tool for analyzing data generated by smart grids. Machine learning and deep learning techniques are widely used for anomaly detection, intrusion detection, voltage stability assessment, fault classification, and energy management[19, 1, 5]。在这些应用中，基于人工智能的异常检测受到特别关注，因为现代能源系统需要快速、自动化的方法来识别异常行为. Among these applications, AI-based anomaly detection has gained particular attention because modern energy systems require fast and automated methods for identifying abnormal behavior[1, 5]。人工智能驱动的网络安全也变得越来越重要，因为传统的被动防御机制通常不足以应对自适应和不断演变的威胁. AI-driven cybersecurity is also becoming increasingly important, since traditional passive defense mechanisms are often not sufficient against adaptive and evolving threats[2, 21]。智能电网运行最严重的威胁之一是虚假数据注入攻击. One of the most serious threats to smart grid operation is the false data injection attack[20, 23]。在这种类型的攻击中，对手会操纵测量数据，以便控制中心收到看似有效但实际上不正确的信息。此类攻击特别难以检测，因为它们可以绕过传统的不良数据检测技术. In this type of attack, an adversary manipulates measurement data so that the control center receives information that appears valid but is actually incorrect. Such attacks are especially difficult to detect because they can bypass conventional bad-data detection techniques[20, 10]。它们的影响可以扩展到状态估计、最佳功率流、继电器操作和能源管理. Their impact can extend to state estimation, optimal power flow, relay operation, and energy management[20, 23]。因此，空间、时间和基于图形的模型特别有用，因为电力系统测量是相互关联的并受到物理定律的约束. For this reason, spatial, temporal, and graph-based models are particularly useful, as power system measurements are interconnected and constrained by physical laws[10, 11, 3]。尽管基于人工智能的方法显示出强大的潜力，但仍然存在一些实际挑战。许多现有模型使用所有可用的测量值，即使某些特征是多余的或提供的有用信息很少。这导致了更大的功能集，从而增加了计算和通信要求。对于边缘设备、变电站和物联网网关来说，这种开销尤其成问题. Although AI-based methods show strong potential, several practical challenges remain. Many existing models use all available measurements, even when some features are redundant or provide little useful information. This leads to larger feature sets, which increase computational and communication requirements. Such overhead is especially problematic for edge devices, substations, and IoT gateways[22, 8]。另一个重要问题是可解释性。高精度模型往往难以解释. Another important issue is interpretability. Models with high accuracy are often difficult to explain[13, 7]。这是关键基础设施中的一个主要问题，操作员需要了解为什么特定事件被归类为攻击或自然干扰. This is a major concern in critical infrastructure, where operators need to understand why a certain event has been classified as an attack or as a natural disturbance[7]。此外，智能电网数据的集中处理可能会引起隐私和安全问题，特别是当数据是从分布式设备和智能电表收集时. In addition, centralized processing of smart grid data may raise privacy and security concerns, particularly when the data is collected from distributed devices and smart meters[8, 21]。由于这些问题，联邦学习和边缘学习与未来智能电网安全解决方案变得越来越相关. Because of these concerns, federated learning and edge learning are becoming increasingly relevant for future smart grid security solutions[8, 9]。在本章中，我们提出了一个紧凑的机器学习框架，用于物联网智能电网中的异常检测。所提出的方法不依赖所有可用的测量，而是使用遗传算法来识别更小、信息更丰富的 PMU/IED 特征子集。然后使用基于树的集成分类器评估这些选定的特征。主要目标是保持强大的检测性能，同时降低维度并提高在实际环境中部署的可行性。使用 MSU/ORNL 电力系统攻击数据集评估该框架. In this chapter, we propose a compact machine learning framework for anomaly detection in IoT-enabled smart grids. Instead of relying on all available measurements, the proposed approach uses a genetic algorithm to identify a smaller and more informative subset of PMU/IED features. These selected features are then evaluated using tree-based ensemble classifiers. The main goal is to preserve strong detection performance while reducing dimensionality and improving the feasibility of deployment in practical environments. The framework is evaluated using the MSU/ORNL Power System Attack Dataset[24]。该问题被表述为二元分类任务，其目标是区分网络攻击和自然事件。比较了多种模型，包括逻辑回归、径向基函数支持向量机 (RBF-SVM)、XGBoost、随机森林和额外树。分析了三种功能配置：所有属性、仅 PMU 属性、以及不带中继状态标志的仅 PMU 属性。这种比较有助于确定分类器主要依赖于电气测量还是日志和状态指示器。本章的主要贡献总结如下：. The problem is formulated as a binary classification task, where the objective is to distinguish between cyber-attacks and natural events. Several models are compared, including logistic regression, radial basis function support vector machines (RBF-SVM), XGBoost, Random Forest, and Extra Trees. Three feature configurations are analyzed: all attributes, PMU-only attributes, and PMU-only attributes without relay status flags. This comparison helps determine whether the classifiers primarily depend on electrical measurements or on log and status indicators. The main contributions of this chapter are summarized as follows:

2 相关工作Related Work

智能电网异常检测的最新研究总体上遵循三个主要研究方向。第一组方法依赖于经典的机器学习方法，例如逻辑回归、决策树、随机森林、支持向量机、梯度提升和浅层神经网络Recent studies on anomaly detection in smart grids generally follow three main research directions. The first group of approaches relies on classical machine learning methods, such as logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, support vector machines, gradient boosting, and shallow neural networks[24, 1, 25, 7]。这些技术通常很有吸引力，因为它们相对容易解释并且不需要过多的计算资源。然而，它们的性能通常取决于所选特征的质量，这意味着仔细的特征工程仍然是建模过程的重要组成部分. These techniques are often attractive because they are relatively easy to interpret and do not require excessive computational resources. However, their performance usually depends on the quality of the selected features, which means that careful feature engineering is still an important part of the modeling process[1, 13]。第二个研究方向是深度学习。 CNN、LSTM、GRU、时间卷积网络、自动编码器和基于 Transformer 的架构等模型能够直接从网格数据学习复杂的非线性和时间关系. The second research direction focuses on deep learning. Models such as CNNs, LSTMs, GRUs, temporal convolutional networks, autoencoders, and transformer-based architectures are able to learn complex nonlinear and temporal relationships directly from grid data[26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 3]。混合深度学习模型在智能电网网络安全任务中取得了很好的成果，特别是在处理大型复杂数据集时。然而，它们的实际使用需要额外关注计算成本、模型可解释性和实际操作条件下的鲁棒性. Hybrid deep learning models have achieved strong results in smart grid cybersecurity tasks, especially when dealing with large and complex datasets. Nevertheless, their practical use requires additional attention to computational cost, model interpretability, and robustness under real operating conditions[2]。第三个方向是基于图学习。由于电力系统具有自然的图结构，其中总线、线路和变电站物理连接，因此图神经网络可以使用这种拓扑来捕获难以用传统方法建模的依赖关系。基于 GNN 的方法已应用于 FDIA 检测，包括基准电力系统中的隐形攻击检测、攻击定位和可扩展实时检测. The third direction is based on graph learning. Since power systems have a natural graph structure, where buses, lines, and substations are physically connected, graph neural networks can use this topology to capture dependencies that are difficult to model with conventional methods. GNN-based approaches have been applied to FDIA detection, including stealth attack detection, attack localization, and scalable real-time detection in benchmark power systems[10]。最近的时空模型通过将图卷积与时间卷积相结合来扩展这一想法，使模型能够捕获总线之间的空间关系和系统状态的时间演化. More recent spatio-temporal models extend this idea by combining graph convolution with temporal convolution, allowing the model to capture both spatial relationships among buses and the temporal evolution of system states[3]。此外，超分辨率感知辅助图深度学习已被提出作为提高 FDIA 场景中检测性能的一种方法. In addition, super-resolution perception-assisted graph deep learning has been proposed as a way to improve detection performance in FDIA scenarios[11]。可解释性也成为该领域的一个重要话题。在关键基础设施中，模型仅仅将事件分类为恶意或异常是不够的；运营商还需要了解该决定背后的原因。因此，基于 SHAP 的方法和其他可解释的人工智能技术越来越多地用于识别对模型预测贡献最大的测量值，并提高对自动检测系统的信任度. Explainability has also become an important topic in this field. In critical infrastructure, it is not enough for a model to simply classify an event as malicious or abnormal; operators also need to understand the reason behind that decision. For this reason, SHAP-based methods and other explainable AI techniques are increasingly used to identify the measurements that contribute most strongly to model predictions and to improve trust in automated detection systems[7, 6]。最近对智能电网网络安全的评论也强调了对人工智能模型的需求，这些模型不仅要准确，而且要可解释、紧凑且足够强大，适合实际部署. Recent reviews on smart grid cybersecurity also highlight the need for AI models that are not only accurate, but also explainable, compact, and robust enough for practical deployment[2, 4].

3 智能电网架构和威胁模型Smart Grid Architecture and Threat Model

智能电网可以被视为一个网络物理系统，其中电力和通信基础设施一起运行。电气层包括发电机、输电线路、变压器、负载、储能单元、可再生能源、断路器和保护装置。网络层由传感器、智能电表、PMU、SCADA系统、通信网络、数据集中器、控制中心、云服务和决策支持软件组成。这两层紧密相连，因为从物理电网收集的测量数据通过通信网络传输，由计算工具处理，然后用于生成直接影响电力系统的控制或保护动作。这种结构改进了监控、控制和能源管理，但也带来了新的安全风险。不正确的测量、延迟的消息、受损的传感器或恶意命令可能会影响状态估计、继电器操作、潮流分析和控制中心决策。因此，智能电网安全不能仅作为传统电力系统问题或仅作为通信安全问题来解决。它必须被视为一个网络物理问题，因为一个层中的事件可以直接影响另一层的行为。A smart grid can be viewed as a cyber-physical system in which the electrical and communication infrastructures operate together. The electrical layer includes generators, transmission lines, transformers, loads, energy storage units, renewable energy sources, breakers, and protection devices. The cyber layer consists of sensors, smart meters, PMUs, SCADA systems, communication networks, data concentrators, control centers, cloud services, and decision-support software. These two layers are closely connected, since measurements collected from the physical grid are transmitted through communication networks, processed by computational tools, and then used to generate control or protection actions that directly affect the power system. This structure improves monitoring, control, and energy management, but it also introduces new security risks. Incorrect measurements, delayed messages, compromised sensors, or malicious commands can affect state estimation, relay operation, power flow analysis, and control-center decisions. Therefore, smart grid security cannot be addressed only as a traditional power-system issue or only as a communication-security issue. It must be treated as a cyber-physical problem, because an event in one layer can directly influence the behavior of the other.

本章提出了一个基于人工智能的框架，用于物联网智能电网中的网络物理异常检测，重点是紧凑且具有物理意义的特征选择。实验评估是在 MSU/ORNL 电力系统攻击数据集上进行的。评估了几种基线模型，包括逻辑回归、RBF-SVM、XGBoost、调整的 XGBoost、随机森林和额外树。结果表明，对于所考虑的二元攻击自然事件分类任务，基于树的装袋集成比线性的、基于内核的、经过测试的提升基线要有效得多。通过将基于遗传算法的特征选择与额外树相结合，获得了最强的结果。使用没有日志属性和中继状态标志的干净 PMU 特征集，所提出的 GA + Extra Trees 框架将特征空间从 112 个测量值减少到五个独立运行中的平均 27.4 个测量值。这相当于减少了约 75.5%。尽管有所减少，该方法将平均测试宏 F1 分数提高到 0.9212，平均 ROC-AUC 提高到 0.9837，优于全功能 Extra Trees 基线。这些发现表明，许多同步电测量对于所考虑的攻击自然事件辨别任务来说是多余的。基于相量的测量的紧凑子集可以提供强大的检测性能，同时降低通信开销、特征提取成本和推理复杂性。这使得所提出的方法适合未来安全、智能和边缘部署的能源基础设施。未来的工作应该将该框架扩展到三类和多类设置、具有显式电力系统拓扑的基于图的模型、在线学习、对抗鲁棒性以及用于保护隐私的智能电网安全的联合学习。This chapter presented an AI-based framework for cyber-physical anomaly detection in IoT-enabled smart grids, with emphasis on compact and physically meaningful feature selection. The experimental evaluation was conducted on the MSU/ORNL Power System Attack Dataset. Several baseline models were evaluated, including logistic regression, RBF-SVM, XGBoost, tuned XGBoost, Random Forest, and Extra Trees. The results show that tree-based bagging ensembles are much more effective than the linear, kernel-based, and tested boosting baselines for the considered binary attack–natural event classification task. The strongest result was obtained by combining genetic-algorithm-based feature selection with Extra Trees. Using the clean PMU feature set without log attributes and relay status flags, the proposed GA + Extra Trees framework reduced the feature space from 112 measurements to an average of 27.4 measurements across five independent runs. This corresponds to a reduction of approximately 75.5%. Despite this reduction, the approach improved the average test macro-F1 score to 0.9212 and the average ROC-AUC to 0.9837, outperforming the full-feature Extra Trees baseline. These findings indicate that many synchronized electrical measurements are redundant for the considered attack–natural event discrimination task. A compact subset of phasor-based measurements can provide strong detection performance while reducing communication overhead, feature extraction cost, and inference complexity. This makes the proposed approach suitable for future secure, intelligent, and edge-deployable energy infrastructures. Future work should extend the framework to three-class and multi-class settings, graph-based models with explicit power-system topology, online learning, adversarial robustness, and federated learning for privacy-preserving smart-grid security.
