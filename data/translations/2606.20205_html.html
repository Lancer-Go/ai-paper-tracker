大型语言模型的明显心理特征很大程度上是一种测量假象Apparent Psychological Profiles of Large Language Models are Largely a Measurement Artifact

\fnmJelena \surMeyer    \fnmDavid \surGarcia    wulff@mpib-berlin.mpg.de [ [ [ [
摘要Abstract

为人类设计的心理工具越来越多地用于分配大语言模型（LLM）稳定的心理特征，这些特征会影响其可用性、安全性评估以及作为人类研究参与者的代理。使用正式的心理测量框架，我们表明这些配置文件很大程度上是测量工件。通过管理一系列性格和风险偏好工具，涵盖自我报告和行为任务，以及 56 名经指令调整的法学硕士以及大量人类参考样本，我们报告了四项发现。首先，模型之间的差异不是由工具目标特征驱动的，而是由定向响应偏差、对量表一端响应的倾向或一个标记选项驱动的，无论项目内容如何；方差分解将 81-90% 的模型间变异归因于这种偏差，而人类中这一比例为 9-16%。其次，偏差会随着模型能力的增强而减弱，但不会因此而消除。第三，由于偏见而非特质驱动响应，因此工具的表观可靠性几乎完全由其响应正交性预测，正交性是我们为特质和偏见指向相反方向的项目比例创造的术语。第四，模型的轮廓似乎随着所使用的物品而变化，并且可以通过物品选择来制造。这些结果表明，法学硕士的明显心理特征是用于测量他们的工具的产物，而不是模型本身的属性。由于从人类心理学借用的工具很少是完全正交的，并且可能本质上缺乏对法学硕士的有效性，我们呼吁以反应正交性为中心进行专门的评估。Psychological instruments designed for humans are increasingly used to assign large language models (LLMs) stable psychological profiles that affect their usability, safety assessment, and use as proxies for human participants in research. Using a formal psychometric framework, we show that these profiles are largely a measurement artifact. Administering a battery of personality and risk-preference instruments spanning self-reports and behavioral tasks to 56 instruction-tuned LLMs alongside large human reference samples, we report four findings. First, differences between models are driven not by the traits an instrument targets but by a directional response bias, a tendency to respond toward one end of the scale, or one labeled option, regardless of item content; a variance decomposition attributes 81–90% of between-model variation to this bias, against 9–16% in humans. Second, the bias declines with model capability but is not eliminated by it. Third, because bias rather than trait drives responding, an instrument’s apparent reliability is almost entirely predicted by its response orthogonality, a term we coin for the proportion of items for which trait and bias point in opposite directions. Fourth, the profile a model appears to have shifts with the items used and can be manufactured through item selection. These results demonstrate that the apparent psychological profiles of LLMs are artifacts of the instrument used to measure them, not properties of the models themselves. As instruments borrowed from human psychology are rarely fully orthogonal and may inherently lack validity for LLMs, we call for dedicated assessments centered on response orthogonality.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地介导日常人际互动，从休闲对话到建议和决策支持，涉及数亿日常交流Large language models (LLMs) increasingly mediate everyday human interaction, from casual conversation to advice and decision support, across hundreds of millions of daily exchanges[openai2026scaling]。由于这些系统承担了曾经为人类保留的角色，开发人员和用户都将它们视为具有稳定的角色：开发人员通过后期训练刻意培养模型的行为倾向. As these systems assume roles once reserved for people, developers and users alike treat them as having stable characters: developers deliberately cultivate a model’s behavioral disposition through post-training[ouyang2022training]并公布其预期价值和个性的详细规范and publish detailed specifications of its intended values and personality[askell2026constitution, openai2025modelspec]，而用户对给定模型的行为形成期望。因此，法学硕士实际上是否拥有稳定的行为倾向以及这种倾向是否可以被测量是至关重要的：对于这些系统的日常可用性, while users form expectations about how a given model will behave. Whether LLMs in fact possess stable behavioral dispositions and whether such dispositions can be measured is therefore consequential: for the everyday usability of these systems[kadambi2026anthropomorphism, sun2026friendly]，对他们进行危险或有害选择的倾向进行安全相关评估, for safety-relevant assessment of their propensity for risky or harmful choices[hartley2025personality, fitz2025psychometric]，以及越来越多的研究机构招募法学硕士作为人类参与者的替身, and for the growing body of research that recruits LLMs as stand-ins for human participants[argyle_out_2023, horton2023large]。这些法学硕士的配置越来越多地被总结为. Increasingly, these LLM dispositions are being summarized as心理概况psychological profiles并被视为模型的稳定描述。但这些个人资料是否真实是我们要考虑的问题。and treated as stable descriptions of the models. But whether these profiles actually are real is the question we take up.

表征法学硕士倾向的主要方法是借用人类差异心理学的测量工具，例如自我报告特质清单或行为任务The dominant approach to characterizing LLM dispositions is to borrow the measurement instruments of human differential psychology, such as self-report trait inventories or behavioral tasks[pellert2024ai, serapio-garcia_psychometric_2025]。利用这些，研究人员基本上从人类特征的每个维度对法学硕士进行了分析。据报道，人格清单可以在法学硕士中产生可靠且有效的测量结果，从而影响下游生成模型的人格. Using these, researchers have profiled LLMs along essentially every dimension along which humans are characterized. Personality inventories have been reported to yield reliable and valid measurements in LLMs, affecting a model’s personality in downstream generation[serapio-garcia_psychometric_2025]。决策任务已被用来将模型描述为风险规避、风险寻求或期望值理性，并认为对齐训练系统地改变了这些倾向. Decision-making tasks have been used to characterize models as risk-averse, risk-seeking, or expected-value rational, and to argue that alignment training systematically shifts these dispositions[jia_decision-making_2024, hartley2025personality]。价值、道德和政治调查问卷被视为模型具有稳定、自由主义倾向的道德和政治形象以及以西方为中心的价值取向的证据. Value, moral, and political questionnaires have been read as evidence that models hold stable, liberal-leaning moral and political profiles and Western-centric value orientations[abdulhai2024moral, rozado2024political]。除此之外，人类仪器已应用于法学硕士，以评估从幸福感和精神病理学到文化取向和认知偏见的各种结构，现在涵盖了大量文献. Beyond these, human instruments have been applied to LLMs to assess constructs ranging from well-being and psychopathology to cultural orientation and cognitive bias, now spanning a sizeable literature[ye2025large, xie2026aipsychobench]。因此，目前已知的关于法学硕士心理特征的大部分信息都依赖于为人类设计的仪器的分数。然而，调查结果已经开始削弱人们对这家企业的信心，特别是对于个性自我报告评级：法学硕士的反应在轻微的即时扰动下发生变化. Much of what is currently claimed to be known about the psychological character of LLMs thus rests on scores from instruments designed for humans. Yet findings have begun to undermine confidence in this enterprise, particularly for personality self-report ratings: LLM responses shift under minor prompt perturbations[gupta-etal-2024-self, shu2024you]，对改写的项目的反应与人类不同, react to reworded items differently than humans[tjuatja2024llms]，从它们身上恢复的因子结构与在人类中发现的因子结构不同, the factor structures recovered from them diverge from those found in humans[peereboom_cognitive_2025, suhr-challenging-valid]，自我报告的特征与实际行为的相关性较弱, self-reported traits correlate weakly with actual behavior[jung-etal-2026-psychometric]，并且人类特质结构对法学硕士的适用性在概念上受到质疑, and the applicability of human trait constructs to LLMs has been questioned on conceptual grounds[suhr2025stop]。因此，目前还不清楚如何解释法学硕士行为的心理特征，或者模型反应的明显一致性是否反映了工具要捕捉的特征。这项调查通过分析数十名法学硕士在自我报告和基于任务的措施中的处置情况来解决这一差距。. It is consequently unclear how to interpret psychological profiles of LLM behavior, or whether the apparent consistency in a model’s responses reflects the trait an instrument is meant to capture. This investigation addresses that gap by analyzing the dispositions of dozens of LLMs across both self-report and task-based measures.

测量的倾向或概况只是对基于特征的模型的有意义的描述：特征驱动的概况在测量上下文（例如项目、框架或任务）中自我复制，而不受特征驱动的概况可以随着工具提出问题的方式而变化。至关重要的是，系统的响应可以是系统性的，不受特征驱动：它们也可能源于响应偏差，即基于响应格式而不是项目内容进行响应的倾向A measured disposition or profile is only a meaningful description of a model to the extent that it is trait-based: a trait-driven profile reproduces itself across measurement contexts (e.g., items, framings, or tasks), whereas one not driven by trait can shift with how the instrument poses its questions. Crucially, the responses of a system can be systematic without being driven by trait: they can also arise from a response bias, a tendency to respond based on the format of the response rather than the content of the item[couch_yeasayers_1960, cronbach_further_1950]。人类心理测量学的典型案例是默许，即无论他们提出什么要求，都会倾向于向天平的一极点认可项目。特质和偏差都可以产生强大的个体差异和更高的可靠性估计，表明系统性而非噪音的响应变异所占比例很高. The canonical case in human psychometrics is acquiescence, the tendency to endorse items toward one pole of the scale regardless of what they ask. We call the presence of items or tasks that place trait and bias in opposition响应正交性response orthogonality。尽管响应正交性对于测量至关重要，但在法学硕士处置的研究中尚未考虑到响应正交性，并且许多广泛使用的工具几乎不包含反向键控项目（如果有的话）。在这样的工具上，特征和反应偏差是混杂的，因此从它们导出的概况的稳定性和相关的可靠性估计可能反映了定向反应偏差，而不是对测量结构的任何稳定的类似特征的倾向。. Despite its centrality to measurement, response orthogonality has not been considered in research on LLM dispositions, and many widely used instruments include few reverse-keyed items, if any. On such instruments, trait and response bias are confounded, so that the stability of the profile derived from them and associated estimates of reliability may reflect a directional response bias rather than any stable trait-like disposition toward the measured construct.

结果Results

结果分为四个部分。第一个测试使用直接来自正式框架的诊断测试来测试标准人格量表的模型间变异是否反映了特质差异或反应偏差。第二个将每个模型的响应分解为特征和响应偏差分量，并检查响应偏差如何随模型能力、模型大小和类型（开放权重与专有权重）的两个代理而变化。第三个将分析扩展到涵盖自我报告量表以及跨性格和风险的行为任务的 29 种工具的全部分析，测试 LLM 响应的表观可靠性是否由工具的响应正交性决定，这意味着偏见驱动而不是特质驱动的响应。第四个结果表明，偏见驱动的响应使心理特征不稳定，并且可以在正向和反向键控工具中进行控制。The results are organized in four parts. The first tests whether between-model variation on a standard personality inventory reflects trait differences or a response bias, using a diagnostic test that follows directly from the formal framework. The second decomposes each model’s responses into a trait and a response-bias component, and examines how response bias varies with two proxies of model capability, model size and type (open-weight versus proprietary). The third extends the analysis to the full battery of 29 instruments spanning self-report scales and behavioral tasks across personality and risk, testing whether the apparent reliability of LLM responding is determined by an instrument’s response orthogonality, implying bias-driven rather than trait-driven responding. The fourth shows that bias-driven responding makes psychological profiles unstable and steerable across forward- and reverse-keyed instruments.

讨论Discussion

法学硕士是否拥有稳定的行为倾向，以及是否可以用人类差异心理学的工具来测量这种倾向，变得越来越重要，因为这些模型塑造了日常互动，进行分析和安全评估，并代表研究中的人类受访者。我们通过大量的自我报告和行为工具、由 56 名经过指导调整的法学硕士组成的小组（被视为个体受访者）以及将系统响应的两个组成部分分开的心理测量框架来解决这个问题。出现了四项发现。首先，法学硕士（而非人类）之间的差异主要是由反应偏差而非心理特征驱动的。其次，这种偏差会随着模型能力的增强而缩小，但并不会因此而消除。第三，在缺乏响应正交性的情况下，响应偏差会夸大表观可靠性，因此内部一致性几乎完全可以从工具的反向键控项目比例中预测出来。第四，相同的偏见会根据是否使用正向或反向键控项目而显着改变心理特征，并且可以通过项目选择来引导这些特征。总之，这些发现重塑了法学硕士的表面心理特征实际上反映的内容。Whether LLMs possess stable behavioral dispositions, and whether such dispositions can be measured with the instruments of human differential psychology, is increasingly consequential as these models shape everyday interaction, undergo profiling and safety assessment, and stand in for human respondents in research. We approached this question with a large battery of self-report and behavioral instruments, a panel of 56 instruction-tuned LLMs treated as individual respondents, and a psychometric framework that separates the two components of systematic responding. Four findings emerge. First, differences between LLMs but not humans are driven primarily by response bias rather than psychological traits. Second, this bias shrinks with greater model capability but is not eliminated by it. Third, in the absence of response orthogonality, response bias inflates apparent reliability, so that internal consistency is almost entirely predictable from an instrument’s proportion of reverse-keyed items. Fourth, the same bias substantially shifts psychological profiles depending on whether forward- or reverse-keyed items are used, and these profiles can be steered through item selection. Together, these findings recast what apparent psychological profiles in LLMs actually reflect.

如果没有完全的反应正交性，特质和反应偏差就会混淆Without full response orthogonality, trait and response bias are confounded[couch_yeasayers_1960, cronbach_further_1950]，并且仪器的表观可靠性与真实特征、纯粹反应偏差或两者的任意混合一致。这是法学硕士文献在很大程度上没有意识到的情况：它从人类心理学借用的工具大多不是完全正交的，因此从它们中读出的概况不能被解释为特质。揭示这一点不仅需要借用人类心理学的工具，还需要借用其形式框架。在明确的测量模型中铸造法学硕士的响应使得特征和响应偏差之间的混淆变得可见和可测量，显示出仪器的表观可靠性在多大程度上反映了响应偏差而不是特征。因此，对心理特征的有效评估需要完全正交的仪器或在正式的心理测量框架内进行明确的建模，这与人类心理测量学中长期确立的考虑因素一致。这些建议不仅影响法学硕士的工作分析，还影响使用它们代表人类参与者的工作, and an instrument’s apparent reliability is consistent with a genuine trait, a pure response bias, or any mixture of the two. This is the condition the LLM literature has worked under largely without recognizing it: the instruments it borrowed from human psychology are mostly not fully orthogonal, so the profiles read off them cannot be interpreted as trait. Revealing this requires borrowing not only the instruments of human psychology but also its formal frameworks. Casting LLM responding in an explicit measurement model makes the confound between trait and response bias visible and measurable, showing how much of an instrument’s apparent reliability reflects response bias rather than trait. Valid assessment of psychological profiles, therefore, requires either fully orthogonal instruments or explicit modeling within a formal psychometric framework, consistent with long-established considerations in human psychometrics. These recommendations affect not only work profiling LLMs, but also work using them to stand in for human participants[e.g., argyle_out_2023, horton2023large]或作为评估者or as raters[e.g., eberhardt_development_2025].

除了使用反应正交工具和正式的心理测量框架之外，我们的工作至少还有三个进一步的实际意义。首先，需要根据反应正交性重新评估法学硕士的心理概况及其可靠性系数In addition to using response-orthogonal instruments and formal psychometric frameworks, our work has at least three further practical implications. First, the psychological profiles already reported for LLMs, along with their reliability coefficients, need to be re-evaluated against response orthogonality[serapio-garcia_psychometric_2025, jiang2023evaluating]：缺乏完全正交性，轮廓会扭曲，可以通过正向或反向重项目选择来制造，并且不需要在仪器之间进行比较。其次，行为任务并不能解决这个问题。正交性一致性梯度对它们来说就像自我报告一样，并且在平衡键控下可靠性崩溃到接近零或更低；如果说有什么不同的话，那就是他们的情况更糟，对反应偏差表现出更大的敏感性，这反映了最近的研究结果，即倾向于自我报告而不是人类的行为评估: absent full orthogonality, profiles are distorted, can be manufactured through forward- or reverse-heavy item selection, and need not be comparable across instruments. Second, behavioral tasks are no refuge from this problem. 一个关键问题是是什么导致了法学硕士的反应偏差。虽然我们没有提供结论性的答案，但响应偏差似乎与训练后引起的方向偏差的特征不匹配。这包括有关阿谀奉承的解释A critical question is what drives the response bias in LLMs. While we do not provide a conclusive answer, the response bias does not seem to match the signature of directional biases induced during post-training. This includes explanations around sycophancy[perez-etal-2023-discovering, sharma2024towards]，人们可能期望表现为默许（同意）或社会期望。默认会产生同质的、单方面的偏差，而我们观察到的响应偏差是双向的，模型分布在无偏差基线的上方和下方（图, which one might expect to manifest as either acquiescence (yea-saying) or social desirability. Acquiescence would produce a homogeneous, one-sided bias, whereas the response bias we observe is two-sided, with models distributed above and below the bias-free baseline (Figure3）。另一方面，社会期望表现为特质方差，而不是主导我们分解的反应偏差。这是因为人类数据证实，高宜人性或低神经质是社会所希望的). Social desirability, on the other hand, surfaces as trait variance rather than the response bias that dominates our decomposition. This is because it is socially desirable to be, for instance, high in agreeableness or low in neuroticism, as confirmed by human data[li2006using];这种可取性将反映在特质方差上，而不是反应偏差上。这些偏差也不能归因于噪声：补充信息中的分析证实模型内的响应偏差在方向和幅度上都是稳定的（SI 附录，B.3 节，表 S9 和图 S3）。这种稳定性受仪器类别限制，在库存（例如 IPIP-NEO-300、BARRATT、SSSV）和仪器类型（自我报告与任务）内最高，而在它们之间较低，这种模式也可能对有效性评估产生不利影响。总而言之，响应偏差很难用大型语言模型的已知趋势来解释，并且需要将其视为一种潜在的独特现象，而不是训练后或噪声解码的副产品。; such desirability would be reflected in trait variance, not response bias. The biases are also not attributable to noise: analyses in the Supplementary Information confirm that response biases are stable within models in both direction and magnitude (SI Appendix, section B.3, Table S9 and Fig. S3). This stability is bounded by instrument category, being highest within inventories (e.g., IPIP-NEO-300, BARRATT, SSSV) and within instrument types (self-report versus task) and lower between them, a pattern that may also adversely affect assessments of validity. Taken together, the response biases are not readily explained by known tendencies of large language models and warrant treatment as a potentially distinct phenomenon rather than a byproduct of post-training or noisy decoding.

一个更深层次的问题是，我们观察到的是工具问题还是模型问题：法学硕士是否需要以不同的方式进行评估，或者他们是否完全缺乏稳定的倾向？这里使用的工具是为人类开发的，尽管它们被设计为涵盖广泛的后果性行为，但它们的许多项目都以体现或意志为前提，例如“我喜欢参加聚会”，这些系统不存在或至少存在争议A deeper question is whether what we observe is an instrument problem or a model problem: do LLMs need to be assessed differently, or do they lack stable dispositions altogether? The instruments used here were developed for humans, and although they are designed to span a broad range of consequential behaviors, many of their items presuppose embodiment or volition, for example “I like to go to parties”, which is absent or at least contested for these systems[hussainhuman, xu2025large]。因此，它们可能缺乏法学硕士的有效性，并且稳定的概况可能只出现在对法学硕士有效的文书中。但法学硕士也可能首先缺乏像人类一样的性格。他们的行为在设计上是强烈依赖于环境的. They may therefore lack validity for LLMs, and stable profiles may only emerge in instruments valid for LLMs. But LLMs may also lack human-like dispositions in the first place. Their behavior is, by design, strongly context-dependent[kumar2026failure, gupta-etal-2024-self]，除了训练后明确设计的倾向之外，尚不清楚他们的行为是否有任何稳定的因素，无论是故意灌输的，例如乐于助人，还是作为副作用出现，例如阿谀奉承, leaving it unclear whether anything stable underlies their behavior, aside from tendencies explicitly engineered during post-training, whether deliberately instilled, such as helpfulness, or emerging as side effects, such as sycophancy[sharma2024towards]。然而，我们希望避免过早地得出法学硕士完全缺乏性格的结论. However, we would like to guard against prematurely concluding that LLMs lack dispositions altogether[hussain2025rebuttal]。区分这些帐户可能需要针对法学硕士特定属性进行测量，而不是借鉴人类差异心理学. Distinguishing these accounts may require measurements tailored to LLM-specific properties rather than borrowed from human differential psychology[suhr2025stop]。这个方向的第一步正在出现，重点是在大型、受控刺激集上引发行为并从统计上恢复结构。一种方法用超过 100,000 个单词的判断代替问卷项目，并使用交叉随机效应模型将方差划分为共识、方向偏差和构成真实行为指纹的特定刺激交互. First steps in this direction are emerging, focusing on eliciting behavior across large, controlled stimulus sets and recovering structure statistically. One approach replaces questionnaire items with judgments over more than 100,000 words and uses crossed random-effects models to partition variance into consensus, directional bias, and the stimulus-specific interaction that constitutes a genuine behavioral fingerprint[kriegmair_machine_2026];另一种方法将其工具建立在经过验证的调查理论之上，但对许多转述的命题引发开放式反应，对潜在立场进行分类，同时明确地建模即时敏感性; another grounds its instrument in validated survey theory but elicits open-ended responses to many paraphrased propositions, classifying the latent stance while explicitly modeling prompt sensitivity[faulborn-etal-2025-little]。它们的共同之处在于，倾向是从工程刺激的行为分布中推断出来的，响应伪影是建模的而不是假设的。. What unites them is that disposition is inferred from the distribution of behavior across engineered stimuli, with response artifacts modeled rather than assumed away.

我们的研究结果对人类评估也有影响。首先，我们的方法以响应正交性的形式将反向键控项的逻辑扩展到行为任务，其中类似的设计特征、特征偏好选项占据不同位置的问题很少被视为可靠性问题。其次，还发现响应正交性会影响人类响应的可靠性。这种效应通常被解读为正向项目和反向项目之间有效性的差异Our findings also have implications for human assessment. First, our approach extends the logic of reverse-keyed items, in the form of response orthogonality, to behavioral tasks, where the analogous design feature, problems on which the trait-favored option occupies different positions, has rarely been treated as a reliability concern. Second, response orthogonality has also been found to affect reliability in human responses. Such effects have often been read as differences in validity between forward and reverse items[weijters_misresponse_2012, sonderen_ineffectiveness_2013]，但我们认为响应偏差的作用，包括项目内容和响应风格之间可能存在的相互作用，也值得考虑；如果没有反应正交性，人类的反应也可能看起来比底层构造所保证的更可靠。考虑到全面的自我报告量表和行为任务仍然被广泛部署, but we think the role of response bias, including a possible interaction between item content and response style, also deserves consideration; without response orthogonality, human responses too may appear more reliable than the underlying construct warrants. Considering that all-forward self-report scales and behavioral tasks are still widely deployed[e.g., DOSPERT_scale, lejuez_evaluation_2002]，这值得直接研究，并且我们应用于法学硕士的分解可以移植到人类数据中，正是为了这个目的。, this is worth examining directly, and the decomposition we apply to LLMs is portable to human data for exactly that purpose.

有几个限制验证了我们的结论。首先，我们将每个模型视为默认状态下的单个受访者，而其他工作则通过引导或角色提示来引发模型内的变化。在我们看来，已部署模型的默认行为与其实际使用最相关，但模型个体的构成是什么，个体差异赖以存在的单位，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题Several limitations qualify our conclusions. First, we treat each model as a single respondent in its default state, whereas other work elicits within-model variation through steering or persona prompting. In our view, the default behavior of a deployed model is most relevant to its actual use, but what constitutes a model individual, the unit on which individual differences would rest, remains an open question[lohn2024machine, suhr-challenging-valid]。其次，我们在零温度下生成响应以消除采样噪声。据报道，在相关环境中采样可以产生稍高的人类对齐度. Second, we generated responses at temperature zero to eliminate sampling noise. Sampling has been reported to yield slightly higher human alignment in related settings[kriegmair_machine_2026]，但差异很小，可能无法证明再现性损失是合理的, but the differences are small and may not justify the loss in reproducibility[wulff2024behavioral];考虑到所报告的影响的严重程度，概率解码下的重大变化似乎令人难以置信。第三，我们仅评估训练后的模型。即使训练后不能轻易解释我们的发现，它也会驱动模型的“个性”，并且被发现会减少与人类行为的一致性; given the magnitude of the reported effects, a substantial change under probabilistic decoding seems implausible. Third, we assessed only post-trained models. Even if post-training cannot readily explain our findings, it drives model ”personality” and has been found to reduce alignment with human behavior[binz2026post, kuribayashi2024psychometric]。未来的工作应该评估基本模型并研究响应偏差作为架构和设计的函数，超越大小和类型。第四，我们的评估涵盖两个领域：个性和风险，选择它们是因为它们与可用性和安全性相关；结果是否扩展到其他领域还有待测试。第五，我们的正式框架故意简约，重点关注特质和反应偏差；未来的工作可以添加诸如项目难度、极端响应或中点趋势等组件. Future work should evaluate base models and study response bias as a function of architecture and design beyond size and type. Fourth, our assessment spans two domains, personality and risk, chosen for their relevance to usability and safety; whether the results extend to other domains remains to be tested. Fifth, our formal framework is deliberately parsimonious, focusing on trait and response bias; future work could add components such as item difficulty, extreme-response, or midpoint tendencies[baumgartner-steenkamp]，尽管使这些可识别会对仪器设计提出进一步的要求。最后，我们的 56 个法学硕士样本按照当前标准来说很大，但对于评估个体差异来说仍然很小，特别是专有子集 (N = 10)，尽管涵盖了大多数当前可用的模型，但对于标准推理测试来说仍然太小；开源小组涵盖 1B-70B，其偏见模式主要体现在规模、架构和启发方面。, though rendering these identifiable would impose further demands on instrument design. Finally, our sample of 56 LLMs is large by current standards but still small for assessing individual differences, especially the proprietary subset (N = 10), which is too small for standard inferential tests despite covering most currently available models; the open-source panel spans 1B–70B with the bias pattern largely holding across size, architecture, and elicitation.

结论Conclusion

评估法学硕士的稳定性格特征是一项关键任务，但仅仅借用人类心理学的范式是不够的。法学硕士的明显心理特征在很大程度上是反应偏差的产物，当前的工具无法将其与真实特征分开，而不是所测量系统的可靠属性。法学硕士是否拥有任何可以被视为真正的倾向，以及如何有效地衡量它，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。研究应该利用为人类评估而开发的丰富的心理测量技术，这些技术既可以暴露此处记录的问题，又可以告知法学硕士特定的工具，以进行有效的测量。Assessing stable dispositional profiles in LLMs is a critical undertaking, but simply borrowing the paradigms of human psychology is not enough. Apparent psychological profiles in LLMs are, in large part, a product of response bias that current instruments cannot separate from genuine trait, rather than reliable properties of the systems measured. Whether LLMs possess anything that would count as genuine dispositions, and how it might be measured validly, remain open. Research should draw on the wealth of psychometric technology developed for human assessment, which can both expose problems like the one documented here and inform the LLM-specific instruments that valid measurement will require.
