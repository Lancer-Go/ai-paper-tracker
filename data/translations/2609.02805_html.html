摘要Abstract

根本原因分析 (RCA) 是电信网络运营中的一项关键任务，但由于复杂的跨层依赖性，诊断现代 5G 和新兴 6G 网络的性能下降仍然具有挑战性。虽然大型语言模型 (LLM) 为推理和知识集成提供了有前途的功能，但直接将普通的 LLM 应用于电信 RCA 通常会导致幻觉、推理不稳定以及与结构化网络证据的一致性不佳。 这项工作首先回顾了电信 RCA 从基于规则和机器学习 (ML) 方法到新兴的法学硕士支持技术的演变，并概述了最新的范式，包括结构化推理、检索增强知识基础、代理编排和可验证推理。 基于这些见解，我们为支持 LLM 的电信 RCA 提出了一个结构化推理框架，该框架将诊断推理与电信特定证据和领域知识结合起来。 所提出的方法首先将异构网络遥测数据组织成规范上下文，然后在诊断过程中强制执行决策路径推理，最后生成基于证据的解释以进行可靠的故障识别。 两个 5G RCA 数据集 TeleLogs 和 TelecomTS 上的实验结果表明，与基线技术相比，所提出的框架持续提高了诊断准确性和决策一致性。 这些跨数据集结果凸显了结构化推理设计对于下一代电信网络中基于 LLM 的实用 RCA 系统的重要性。Root cause analysis (RCA) is a critical task in telecom network operations, but diagnosing performance degradations in modern 5G and emerging 6G networks remains challenging due to complex cross-layer dependencies. While large language models (LLMs) offer promising capabilities for reasoning and knowledge integration, directly applying vanilla LLMs to telecom RCA often leads to hallucination, unstable reasoning, and poor alignment with structured network evidence. This work first reviews the evolution of telecom RCA from rule-based and machine learning (ML) approaches to emerging LLM-enabled techniques, and provides an overview of recent paradigms, including structured reasoning, retrieval-augmented knowledge grounding, agentic orchestration, and verifiable reasoning. Building upon these insights, we propose a structured reasoning framework for LLM-enabled telecom RCA that aligns diagnostic reasoning with telecom-specific evidence and domain knowledge. The proposed approach first organizes heterogeneous network telemetry into canonical contexts, and then enforces decision-path reasoning during diagnosis, and finally generates evidence-grounded explanations for reliable fault identification. Experimental results on two 5G RCA datasets, TeleLogs and TelecomTS, demonstrate that the proposed framework consistently improves diagnostic accuracy and decision consistency compared with baseline techniques. These cross-dataset results highlight the importance of structured reasoning design for practical LLM-based RCA systems in next-generation telecom networks.

I 简介Introduction

随着5G-Advanced和新兴6G网络的部署，电信系统正在演变成高度复杂和大规模的基础设施。 这些网络集成了无线电、传输和核心层的异构组件，使可靠运行变得越来越具有挑战性。 因此，网络故障和服务质量下降可能会导致严重的运营后果。例如，加拿大的 Rogers 断电（2022 年）、美国的 AT&T 断电（2024 年）和澳大利亚的 Optus 断电（2023 年）中断了超过 1 亿台设备的连接，阻止了超过 25,000 个紧急呼叫，并导致全国范围内的支付系统、交通网络和医疗服务中断，损失超过 1 亿美元With the deployment of 5G-Advanced and the emerging 6G networks, telecom systems are evolving into highly complex and large-scale infrastructures. These networks integrate heterogeneous components across radio, transport, and core layers, making reliable operation increasingly challenging. As a result, network failures and service degradations may lead to significant operational consequences. For example, the Rogers outage in Canada (2022), the AT&T outage in the United States (2024), and the Optus outage in Australia (2023) disrupted connectivity for over 100 million devices, blocked more than 25,000 emergency calls, and caused nationwide disruptions to payment systems, transportation networks, and healthcare services, with more than $100 million loss[1].

传统上，电信 RCA 依赖于基于规则的专家系统，最近又依赖于机器学习技术Traditionally, telecom RCA has relied on rule-based expert systems and, more recently, ML techniques[2]。特别是，早期的 RCA 解决方案依赖于预定义的警报模式或 KPI 阈值来触发相应的故障排除程序。 为了提高可扩展性，引入了数据驱动的机器学习模型来检测和分类潜在的故障。这些方法可以处理大量遥测数据，但它们主要执行统计模式识别而不是显式诊断推理。因此，基于 ML 的 RCA 系统通常很难在不进行大量再训练的情况下适应看不见的故障模式. In particular, early RCA solutions rely on predefined alarm patterns or KPI thresholds to trigger corresponding troubleshooting procedures. To improve scalability, data-driven ML models have been introduced to detect and classify potential faults. These approaches can process large volumes of telemetry data, but they primarily perform statistical pattern recognition rather than explicit diagnostic reasoning. As a result, ML-based RCA systems often struggle to adapt to unseen failure modes without extensive retraining[3].

近年来，大语言模型（LLM）已成功应用于许多领域，它们也为电信领域提供了有前景的机会，例如知识理解Recently, large language models (LLMs) have been successfully applied to many fields, and they also offer promising opportunities for telecom, e.g., knowledge understanding[4], 电源控制, power control[5]、交通预测, traffic prediction[6]、网络管理, network management[7]等 与传统的基于规则的电信 RCA 和机器学习方法相比，法学硕士具有几个关键优势。首先，法学硕士拥有强大的推理能力，可以进行多步骤诊断分析。与遵循预定义逻辑的规则引擎和主要学习统计相关性的 ML 模型不同，LLM 可以跨多个系统指标执行逐步推理，以推断潜在的根本原因。 其次，法学硕士可以整合跨异构领域的知识，例如无线电接入、传输和核心网络。对于通常在孤立的特征集上运行的传统方法来说，这种跨域推理是很困难的。 第三，法学硕士可以对其诊断过程生成人类可读的解释。此功能提高了 RCA 结果的可解释性，并帮助网络工程师了解特定证据如何导致特定的 RCA 诊断。, etc. LLMs have several critical advantages over traditional rule-based and ML approaches for telecom RCA. Firstly, LLMs possess strong reasoning capabilities that enable multi-step diagnostic analysis. Unlike rule engines that follow predefined logic and ML models that primarily learn statistical correlations, LLMs can perform step-by-step reasoning across multiple system indicators to infer potential root causes. Secondly, LLMs can integrate knowledge across heterogeneous domains, e.g., radio access, transport, and core networks. Such cross-domain reasoning is difficult for traditional methods that typically operate on isolated feature sets. Thirdly, LLMs can generate human-readable explanations of their diagnostic process. This capability improves the interpretability of RCA outcomes and helps network engineers understand how specific evidence leads to a particular RCA diagnosis.

然而，尽管有这些优势，直接将普通法学硕士应用于电信 RCA 仍然具有挑战性。首先，法学硕士的推理过程可能不稳定。具体来说，电信 RCA 通常需要对多个诊断因素进行系统验证，例如移动行为、资源调度和覆盖几何。普通法学硕士在解释相似的网络测量集时可能会产生不一致的推理路径However, despite these advantages, directly applying vanilla LLMs to telecom RCA remains challenging. Firstly, the reasoning process of LLMs can be unstable. Specifically, telecom RCA often requires systematic verification of multiple diagnostic factors, such as mobility behavior, resource scheduling, and coverage geometry. Vanilla LLMs may produce inconsistent reasoning paths when interpreting similar sets of network measurements[8]。 其次，电信 RCA 涉及来自多个系统层的异构和结构化证据。如果没有明确的机制将结构化遥测与诊断推理相结合，法学硕士可能依赖于表面相关性，而不是网络事件和性能下降之间的因果关系. Secondly, telecom RCA involves heterogeneous and structured evidence from multiple system layers. Without explicit mechanisms to align structured telemetry with diagnostic reasoning, LLMs may rely on superficial correlations rather than causal relationships between network events and performance degradations[9]。 第三，法学硕士容易产生幻觉。例如，在电信 RCA 场景中，当模型推测潜在原因而没有将其推理基于 KPI、拓扑关系或配置参数等具体指标时，可能会得出错误的结论. Thirdly, LLMs are prone to hallucination. For instance, in telecom RCA scenarios, incorrect conclusions may arise when the model speculates about potential causes without grounding its reasoning in concrete indicators such as KPIs, topology relationships, or configuration parameters[10].

为了应对这些挑战，这项工作提出了一个结构化的证据和知识一致的微调框架（SEKA-FT），用于基于证据的电信 RCA。 我们没有将 RCA 视为输入到标签的预测任务，而是将其表述为结构化推理问题，其中必须逐步检查异构网络证据，然后才能做出最终的根本原因决策。 根据这种表述，诊断过程明确由证据到路径到决策结构指导。 首先，异构故障排除输入被重新组织成规范的上下文块，以便可以一致地解释用户平面 KPI、移动性统计数据、相邻小区关系和工程参数。 其次，引入决策路径推理将 RCA 分解为阶段性诊断检查，使模型能够在产生最终答案之前排除不受支持的假设。 第三，将循证解释纳入监管目标，使生成的诊断能够追溯到具体的电信指标和领域知识。 因此，SEKA-FT 在统一的微调过程中联合调整结构化证据、中间诊断路径、最终 RCA 标签和可追溯的解释。 通过这种设计，电信 RCA 从基于预测的故障排除转变为决策路径控制推理。这使得模型不仅可以基于最终标签监督，还可以基于结构化诊断证据和中间推理一致性来做出 RCA 决策。 SEKA-FT 提高了根本原因识别的准确性和生成的诊断的可信度。To address these challenges, this work proposes a structured evidence- and knowledge-aligned fine-tuning framework (SEKA-FT) for evidence-grounded telecom RCA. Rather than treating RCA as an input-to-label prediction task, we formulate it as a structured reasoning problem, where heterogeneous network evidence must be progressively examined before a final root-cause decision is made. Under this formulation, the diagnostic process is explicitly guided by an evidence-to-path-to-decision structure. First, heterogeneous troubleshooting inputs are reorganized into canonical context blocks so that user-plane KPIs, mobility statistics, neighbor-cell relations, and engineering parameters can be interpreted consistently. Second, decision-path reasoning is introduced to decompose RCA into staged diagnostic checks, enabling the model to rule out unsupported hypotheses before producing the final answer. Third, evidence-grounded explanations are incorporated into the supervision target so that the generated diagnoses remain traceable to concrete telecom indicators and domain knowledge. Therefore, SEKA-FT jointly aligns structured evidence, intermediate diagnostic paths, final RCA labels, and traceable explanations within a unified fine-tuning process. Through this design, telecom RCA is transformed from prediction-based troubleshooting into decision-path controlled reasoning. This enables the model to make RCA decisions based not only on final label supervision, but also on structured diagnostic evidence and intermediate reasoning consistency. SEKA-FT improves both the accuracy of root-cause identification and the trustworthiness of generated diagnosis.

这项工作的贡献是双重的。 首先，我们系统地概述了 RCA 在电信网络中的演变，并分析了法学硕士在下一代 RCA 系统中的新兴作用。 我们特别讨论了支持 LLM 的范式如何通过结构化推理、检索增强生成 (RAG)、代理编排和可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 来增强 RCA 能力。 其次，我们开发 SEKA-FT 作为电信 RCA 特定的微调框架The contributions of this work are two-fold. First, we provide a systematic overview of the evolution of RCA in telecom networks and analyze the emerging role of LLMs in next-generation RCA systems. In particular, we discuss how LLM-enabled paradigms can enhance RCA capabilities through structured reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agentic orchestration, and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). Second, we develop SEKA-FT as a telecom-RCA-specific fine-tuning framework that将异构证据、中间诊断检查、根本原因决策和基于证据的解释整合到统一的证据到路径到决策的监督结构中integrates heterogeneous evidence, intermediate diagnostic checks, root-cause decisions, and evidence-grounded explanations into a unified evidence-to-path-to-decision supervision structure. 技术贡献在于根据电信 RCA 的诊断依赖性对这些元素进行联合编码，使得模型不仅学习最终标签，而且还学习与电信证据和领域约束一致的结构化推理路径。The technical contribution lies in jointly encoding these elements according to the diagnostic dependencies of telecom RCA, such that the model learns not only the final label but also a structured reasoning path consistent with telecom evidence and domain constraints.对两个 5G RCA 数据集 TeleLogs 和 TelecomTS 的广泛案例研究表明，与 ICL、非 LLM 和基于 SFT 的基线相比，有一致的改进。Extensive case studies on two 5G RCA datasets, TeleLogs and TelecomTS, demonstrate consistent improvements over ICL, non-LLM, and SFT-based baselines.这些结果表明，所提出的框架不仅限于单个数据集模板，而且在具有不同证据表示和诊断特征的不同 5G RCA 和可观测性设置中仍然有效。These results show that the proposed framework is not limited to a single dataset template, but remains effective across distinct 5G RCA and observability settings with different evidence representations and diagnostic characteristics.

II RCA：从基于规则和机器学习到法学硕士RCA: From Rule-based and ML to LLMs

如图所示。As shown in Fig.1，RCA的演变可以被视为从确定性规则匹配到统计标签预测，并进一步转向基于证据的推理的转变。 首先，基于规则的专家系统提供透明和确定性的行为，但它们依赖于预定义的警报模式、KPI 阈值和手动维护的故障排除逻辑。随着网络场景变得更加多样化，这种静态规则难以扩展和更新。, the evolution of RCA can be viewed as a shift from deterministic rule matching, to statistical label prediction, and further toward evidence-grounded reasoning. First, rule-based expert systems provide transparent and deterministic behavior, but they rely on predefined alarm patterns, KPI thresholds, and manually maintained troubleshooting logic. As network scenarios become more diverse, such static rules are difficult to scale and update.

数据驱动的机器学习方法通过从历史遥测中学习统计模式来提高可扩展性。它们可以从大量 KPI、警报和日志中检测异常、对故障进行分类并对可能的根本原因进行排序。 然而，大多数基于 ML 的 RCA 方法仍然作为输入到标签映射系统运行。它们通常输出概率分布或预测类别，但没有明确描述不同的证据如何支持候选根本原因。它们的性能还很大程度上取决于训练数据覆盖范围和分布稳定性。Data-driven ML methods improve scalability by learning statistical patterns from historical telemetry. They can detect anomalies, classify faults, and rank possible root causes from large volumes of KPIs, alarms, and logs. However, most ML-based RCA methods still operate as input-to-label mapping systems. They usually output a probability distribution or a predicted class, but do not explicitly describe how different pieces of evidence support candidate root causes. Their performance also depends strongly on training-data coverage and distribution stability.

法学硕士为电信 RCA 带来了新的机会，因为它们可以在统一的推理界面中结合结构化遥测、文本描述、工程知识和操作指令。 与传统分类器相比，法学硕士可以生成人类可读的诊断轨迹，并支持跨异构证据源的多步骤分析。 然而，语言级推理能力的存在并不能自动使法学硕士成为可靠的 RCA 引擎。如果没有结构化的证据对齐，法学硕士可能会产生看似合理但不受支持的解释，遵循不稳定的推理路径，或者依赖于输入片段和根本原因标签之间的表面相关性。LLMs introduce a new opportunity for telecom RCA because they can combine structured telemetry, textual descriptions, engineering knowledge, and operational instructions within a unified reasoning interface. Compared with conventional classifiers, LLMs can generate human-readable diagnostic traces and support multi-step analysis across heterogeneous evidence sources. However, the presence of language-level reasoning ability does not automatically make LLMs reliable RCA engines. Without structured evidence alignment, an LLM may produce plausible but unsupported explanations, follow unstable reasoning paths, or rely on superficial correlations between input fragments and root-cause labels.

因此，关键的挑战不仅仅是将法学硕士应用于电信 RCA，而是控制模型如何将异构证据与诊断决策联系起来。 这激发了本工作中采用的结构化推理设计：RCA 应该被表述为一种基于证据的推理过程，其中中间诊断检查、最终标签和解释共同对齐。Therefore, the key challenge is not merely to apply an LLM to telecom RCA, but to control how the model connects heterogeneous evidence to diagnostic decisions. This motivates the structured reasoning design adopted in this work: RCA should be formulated as an evidence-grounded reasoning process in which intermediate diagnostic checks, final labels, and explanations are jointly aligned.

三、III用于电信根本原因分析的法学硕士技术LLM-enabled Techniques for Telecom Root Cause Analysis

本节介绍了电信 RCA 支持 LLM 范式的发展路径。This section presents a progression path of LLM-enabled paradigms for telecom RCA.

Ⅲ-AIII-A 思路链：构建诊断逻辑Chain-of-Thought: Structuring Diagnostic Logic

CoT 是一种提示技术，鼓励法学硕士在得出最终答案之前生成一系列中间推理步骤CoT is a prompting technique that encourages LLMs to generate a series of intermediate reasoning steps before reaching a final answer[11]。 在电信 RCA 中，这一点很重要，因为一次性推理通常无法捕获跨层依赖关系。例如，当 gNodeB 报告下行链路吞吐量下降时，普通 LLM 可能会直接将问题归因于拥塞，而不检查控制平面事件。 相反，结构化 CoT 可以通过分阶段验证来指导模型：首先评估物理层指标，然后检查 MAC/RLC 层证据，最后将观察结果与 RRC 或切换相关的信令日志相关联。 这说明了为什么 RCA 应被组织为显式诊断路径，而不是直接的标签预测过程。. In telecom RCA, this is important because one-shot inference often fails to capture cross-layer dependencies. For example, when a gNodeB reports downlink throughput degradation, a vanilla LLM may directly attribute the issue to congestion without checking control-plane events. A structured CoT can instead guide the model through staged verification: first evaluating physical-layer indicators, then checking MAC/RLC-layer evidence, and finally correlating the observations with RRC or handover-related signalling logs. This illustrates why RCA should be organized as an explicit diagnostic path rather than a direct label prediction process.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 检索增强生成：知识基础Retrieval-Augmented Generation: Knowledge Grounding

标准的法学硕士经常会产生看似合理但技术上不正确的幻觉。 为此，RAG 是一个框架，允许法学硕士在生成响应之前从外部权威知识库检索相关信息Standard LLMs often produce plausible but technically incorrect hallucinations. To this end, RAG is a framework that allows an LLM to retrieve relevant information from an external, authoritative knowledge base before generating a response[12]。 对于电信 RCA，RAG 系统可以检索相关材料，例如 3GPP 规范、供应商手册、历史事件报告或基于当前遥测或警报上下文的经过验证的故障排除规则。 检索到的证据可以限制法学硕士的诊断并减少幻觉。 例如，当在服务小区中观察到吞吐量下降时，系统可以跟踪从本地 KPI 中无法直接看到的相关传输、核心网络或相邻小区依赖性。 这表明可靠的基于LLM的RCA不仅需要语言推理，还需要领域知识和网络结构的外部基础。. For telecom RCA, a RAG system can retrieve relevant materials such as 3GPP specifications, vendor manuals, historical incident reports, or verified troubleshooting rules based on the current telemetry or alarm context. The retrieved evidence can then constrain the LLM’s diagnosis and reduce hallucination. For instance, when throughput degradation is observed in a serving cell, the system may trace related transport, core-network, or neighboring-cell dependencies that are not directly visible from local KPIs. This shows that reliable LLM-based RCA requires not only language reasoning, but also external grounding in domain knowledge and network structure.

Ⅲ-CIII-C 代理编排：环境交互规划Agentic Orchestration: Environment-interactive Planning

代理法学硕士系统与标准聊天机器人的不同之处在于，它能够使用工具并自主执行多步骤计划以实现目标。 虽然直接的 LLM 接口仅限于提示中提供的信息，但 LLM 代理可以通过 API 调用与外部系统交互An agentic LLM system differs from a standard chatbot in its ability to use tools and execute multi-step plans autonomously to achieve a goal. While a direct LLM interface is limited to the information provided in the prompt, an LLM agent can interact with external systems through API calls[13]。 在电信 RCA 中，代理框架可以将法学硕士视为使用专门工具验证诊断假设的协调员。例如，代理可以触发 ns-3 模拟来估计配置更改的影响，或查询自组织网络模块以获得天线倾斜或功率控制建议。 此外，代理范式可以自然地扩展到多代理系统，其中复杂的RCA工作流程被分解为专门的角色，形成协作的“专家团队”。 不同的代理可能专注于解决方案规划、日志分析、配置验证等。这种编排对于没有单一证据源足够的多域故障非常有用。. In telecom RCA, an agentic framework can treat the LLM as a coordinator that verifies diagnostic hypotheses using specialized tools. For example, the agent may trigger an ns-3 simulation to estimate the impact of a configuration change, or query a self-organizing network module for antenna-tilt or power-control recommendations. Furthermore, the agentic paradigm can naturally extend to a multi-agent system, where the complex RCA workflow is decomposed into specialized roles, forming a collaborative “expert team”. Different agents may focus on solution planning, log analysis, configuration validation, etc. Such orchestration is useful for multi-domain failures where no single evidence source is sufficient.

Ⅲ-DIII-D RLVR：物理可验证的推理和对齐RLVR: Physically Verifiable Reasoning and Alignment

可验证推理是指一种范式，其中模型的输出不仅通过其语言流畅性来判断，还通过其满足可检查条件或奖励的能力来判断。标准法学硕士通常根据人类偏好进行调整，这是主观的，可能无法反映技术事实Verifiable reasoning refers to a paradigm where the model’s output is not just judged by its linguistic fluency, but by its ability to satisfy checkable conditions or rewards. Standard LLMs are typically aligned using human preference, which is subjective and may not reflect technical truth[14]。 在 RLVR 范式中，高保真模拟器或数字孪生等网络环境可以作为反馈源。 LLM 提出诊断路径和缓解措施后，系统会评估吞吐量、丢包率或切换失败率等目标 KPI 是否有所改善。 成功的推理轨迹可以得到强化，而无效的推理轨迹则可以受到惩罚。这将调整目标从单纯的人类偏好转变为网络约束下的运营有效性。. In the RLVR paradigm, a network environment such as a high-fidelity simulator or digital twin can serve as a feedback source. After the LLM proposes a diagnostic path and mitigation action, the system evaluates whether target KPIs such as throughput, packet loss, or handover failure rate improve. Successful reasoning trajectories can be reinforced, while ineffective ones can be penalized. This shifts the alignment objective from human preference alone to operational effectiveness under network constraints.

总之，支持 LLM 的 RCA 并不依赖于单一技术，而是通过逐渐更强的接地和环境耦合形式不断发展。 CoT 强调诊断路径组织，RAG 提供知识基础，代理编排实现基于工具的假设验证，RLVR 引入基于结果的对齐。 这些见解激发了拟议的 SEKA-FT 框架，该框架侧重于结构化证据对齐和决策路径控制，作为迈向可靠的基于 LLM 的 RCA 的实际步骤。In summary, LLM-enabled RCA does not rely on a single technique, but evolves through progressively stronger forms of grounding and environmental coupling. CoT emphasizes diagnostic path organization, RAG provides knowledge grounding, agentic orchestration enables tool-based hypothesis verification, and RLVR introduces outcome-based alignment. These insights motivate the proposed SEKA-FT framework, which focuses on structured evidence alignment and decision-path control as a practical step toward reliable LLM-based RCA.

SEKA-FT 不是独立监督这些要素，而是将它们耦合到一个统一的证据到路径到决策的学习结构中，允许每个诊断结论逐步基于先前的证据和领域约束。Rather than supervising these elements independently, SEKA-FT couples them within a unified evidence-to-path-to-decision learning structure, allowing each diagnostic conclusion to be progressively grounded in preceding evidence and domain constraints. 因此，通过这种联合对齐，SEKA-FT将微调从直接标签监督转变为结构化诊断推理监督，提高监督密度、抗幻觉性、推理稳定性以及RCA输出的可追溯性。Therefore, through this joint alignment, SEKA-FT transforms fine-tuning from direct label supervision into structured diagnostic reasoning supervision, improving supervision density, hallucination resistance, reasoning stability, and the traceability of RCA outputs.

VI 结论Conclusion

RCA在现代电信网络中发挥着至关重要的作用，本文研究了法学硕士在电信RCA中的应用。我们提出了一个结构化推理框架，将诊断推理与电信特定的证据和决策路径结合起来。实验结果表明，与传统的 SFT 和 ICL 基线相比，所提出的方法显着提高了诊断准确性和决策一致性。这些发现凸显了结构化推理设计对于下一代电信网络中可靠的支持 LLM 的 RCA 的重要性。RCA plays a critical role in modern telecom networks, and this paper studied the application of LLMs for telecom RCA. We proposed a structured reasoning framework that aligns diagnostic reasoning with telecom-specific evidence and decision paths. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach significantly improves diagnostic accuracy and decision consistency compared with conventional SFT and ICL baselines. These findings highlight the importance of structured reasoning design for reliable LLM-enabled RCA in next-generation telecom networks.作为未来的工作，我们将在更多样化和更现实的电信 RCA 场景中进一步评估和扩展所提出的框架，包括核心网络和传输网络故障以及以前未见过的和真实的运营商事件。As future work, we will further evaluate and extend the proposed framework in more diverse and realistic telecom RCA scenarios, including core- and transport-network faults as well as previously unseen and real operator incidents.
