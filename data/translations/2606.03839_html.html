摘要。Abstract.

文本属性图（TAG）是一种重要的图结构化数据，其中每个节点都有文本描述。 TAG模型通常联合训练图神经网络（GNN）和语言模型，这会导致较高的空间和时间消耗，特别是在大型数据集上。为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了 TAGSAM，这是一种压缩方法，可以在保持训练准确性的同时压缩 TAG。 TAGSAM 有两个关键设计，即Text-Attributed Graph (TAG) is an important type of graph structured data, where each node has a text description. TAG models usually train a Graph Neural Network (GNN) and language model jointly, which leads to high space and time consumption, especially on large datasets. To mitigate this, we propose TAGSAM, a condensation method that compresses TAGs while preserving training accuracy. TAGSAM comes with two key designs, i.e.,子图文本选择subgraph text Selection和and属性相似度匹配Attribute similarity Matching，分别压缩TAG的文本描述和图拓扑。对于文本，子图文本选择通过最大化互信息从多个相关文本描述中选择并合并代表性文本块。对于图拓扑，基于匹配训练轨迹（MTT）的流行压缩方法存在高方差，这阻碍了准确性。我们的属性相似性匹配通过对齐稳定的相似性矩阵来缓解这个问题。我们根据六个最先进的基准评估 TAGSAM，它展示了卓越的性能。对于相同的压缩大小，TAGSAM 的准确率比性能最佳的基线平均提高了 4.9%。此外，即使 TAG 压缩到只有 1% 大小，它也能保持有竞争力的训练准确性。我们的代码可在 https://github.com/SundayVHan/TAGSAM 获取, which compress the text description and graph topology of TAG, respectively. For the texts, subgraph text selection selects and merges representative text chunks from multiple related text descriptions by maximizing mutual information. For the graph topology, popular condensation methods based on Matching Training Trajectories (MTT) suffer from high variance, which hinders accuracy. Our attribute similarity matching mitigates this issue by aligning stable similarity matrices. We evaluate TAGSAM against six state-of-the-art baselines, where it showcases superior performance. For the same compressed size, TAGSAM improves upon the best-performing baseline by an average of 4.9% in accuracy. Furthermore, it maintains competitive training accuracy even when the TAG is condensed to just 1% size. Our code is available at https://github.com/SundayVHan/TAGSAM

1. 简介Introduction

文本属性图 (TAG)Text-Attributed Graphs (TAGs)(Chang and Blei, 2009; Liu et al., 2025)将图拓扑与文本节点描述集成。当前的方法联合训练图神经网络（GNN）和语言模型；例如，G2P2integrate graph topology with textual node descriptions. Current methods jointly train a Graph Neural Network (GNN) and a language model; for example, G2P2(Wen and Fang, 2023)通过对比学习对齐图形和文本编码器表示，而 GraphCLIPaligns graph and text encoder representations via contrastive learning, while GraphCLIP(Zhu et al., 2024)利用法学硕士来总结当地社区以获得更丰富的背景。应用于文章分类和社交网络分析等任务utilizes an LLM to summarize local neighborhoods for richer context. Applied to tasks like article classification and social network analysis(Li et al., 2023; Brannon et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025b, a; Huang et al., 2024)与传统图模型相比，TAG 模型利用文本作为额外的监督信号来降低标签依赖性并增强零样本能力。然而，这种双模式训练在大型数据集上仍然是高度内存和时间密集型的。, TAG models leverage text as an extra supervisory signal to lower label dependency and boost zero-shot capabilities compared to traditional graph models. However, this dual-modal training remains highly memory- and time-intensive on large datasets.

数据集压缩Dataset condensation(Wang et al., 2018; Zhao and Bilen, 2023)通过将大型数据集压缩为较小的、具有高度代表性的数据集来降低训练成本。当前的方法分为两类。 选择性方法reduces training costs by compressing a large dataset into a smaller, highly representative one. Current approaches fall into two categories. Selective methods(Har-Peled et al., 2006; Welling, 2009; Farahani and Hekmatfar, 2009; Braverman et al., 2022)从原始数据中整理出一个有代表性的子集；例如，放牧curate a representative subset from the original data; for instance, Herding(Welling, 2009)最小化子集和完整数据集之间的特征差异。 生成方法minimizes the feature discrepancy between the subset and the full dataset. Generative methods(Wang et al., 2018; Zhao et al., 2020; Zhao and Bilen, 2023)通过双层优化合成压缩数据，将学生模型（在压缩数据上训练）与教师模型（在原始数据上训练）对齐。这种范例最近已通过 GCond 等方法扩展到图形领域synthesize compressed data via bi-level optimization, aligning student models (trained on compressed data) with teacher models (trained on original data). This paradigm has recently been extended to graph by methods like GCond(Jin et al., 2021)，它匹配学生和教师模型之间的梯度以生成合成图。, which matches gradients between student and teacher models to generate synthetic graphs.

As Figure1表明，这种范式在这种情况下面临两个关键限制：首先，它们仅浓缩了图模态，忽略了丰富的高维文本。其次，它们对标签监督的依赖与常见的 TAG 场景不兼容，在常见的 TAG 场景中，由于注释成本高昂，真实标签往往稀缺或完全不可用demonstrates, such paradigm face two key limitations in this context: First, they condense only the graph modality, ignoring the rich, high-dimensional text. Second, their reliance on label supervision is incompatible with common TAG scenarios, where ground-truth labels are often scarce or entirely unavailable due to high annotation costs(Wen and Fang, 2023)。因此，这些传统方法并不是为了保存联合信息而设计的，无法满足 TAG 模型对对比学习和零样本分类等任务的需求。. Consequently, these traditional methods, not being designed to preserve joint information, fail to meet the needs of TAG models for tasks like contrastive learning and zero-shot classification.

当前冷凝方法面临的挑战。Challenges for current condensation methods.在最近的进展中，与 TAG 压缩最相关的工作是使用匹配训练轨迹（MTT）来压缩图像文本数据集的多模态数据集压缩方法。Among recent advances, the work most relevant to TAG condensation is the line of multi-modal dataset condensation methods that employ Matching Training Trajectories (MTT) to compress image-text datasets(Wu et al., 2023; Xu et al., 2024)，其中涉及对齐原始数据集和压缩数据集训练之间产生的模型参数更新轨迹。然而，尽管他们在其他方面取得了成功，但我们发现他们在 TAG 上表现不佳，原因如下：, which involves aligning the model parameter update trajectories produced between training on original dataset and compressed one. Yet, despite their success elsewhere, we find they perform poorly on TAGs due to the following two reasons:

  • ∙\bullet

    无法阅读的文字。Unreadable text.MTT 采用梯度下降进行压缩，其优于连续特征，但在处理离散文本时遇到困难，导致文本被压缩成不可读的嵌入向量。因此，这些嵌入与标记器等下游模型不兼容。虽然尝试在嵌入空间中检索最近邻的原始文本作为替代，但它会导致运行时间长和模型性能较低。MTT employs gradient descent for condensation, which excels continuous features but struggles with discrete text, resulting in text being compressed into unreadable embedding vectors. Consequently, these embeddings are incompatible with downstream models like tokenizers. While retrieving nearest neighbor original texts in embedding space as substitutes is attempted, it incurs long runtime and lower model performance.

这些分析表明，现有的缩合方法不能很好地应用于 TAG。因此，我们提出以下研究问题：These analysis shows that existing condensation methods cannot work well on TAGs. Thus, we ask the following research question:

是否可以设计一种在无标签场景下的TAG压缩方法，能够生成离散的压缩文本并实现稳定收敛？Is it possible to design a TAG condensation method in no-label scenarios, which can generate discrete compressed text and achieves stable convergence?

Instead of updating compressed text embeddings via SGD, subgraph text selection directly selects representative text from the original space. Besides, compared to condensing TAGs via matching training trajectories, which suffers from high variance due to random batching, we propose to match attribute similarity, which is inherently more stable. This approach yields greater training stability and improves model accuracy, as shown in Figure2（二）。 此外，我们从理论上分析了我们提出的方法。首先，对于子图文本选择，我们引入了一种实用的贪心方法，并从理论上证明它可以有效地逼近最优解。其次，我们证明属性相似性可以作为属性之间逐点互信息的有效估计，因此本质上是稳定的，从而导致更稳健的训练。(b). Furthermore, we theoretically analyze our proposed method. First, for subgraph text selection, we introduce a practical greedy method and theoretically demonstrate that it effectively approximates the optimal solution. Second, we prove that attribute similarity serves as an effective estimate for point-wise mutual information between attributes, and is thereby inherently stable, leading to more robust training.

为了评估 TAGSAM，我们进行了广泛的实验，并将其与 6 种最先进的数据集压缩方法进行了比较。结果表明，TAGSAM 始终比所有基线产生更高的模型训练准确性。与性能最佳的基线相比，TAGSAM 的平均准确率提高了 4.9%。此外，TAGSAM 仅以原始 TAG 1% 的压缩比就实现了有竞争力的训练精度。此外，消融研究表明我们的模型设计是有效的。此外，效率研究表明 TAGSAM 在运行时间上是高效的。To evaluate TAGSAM, we conduct extensive experiments and compare it with 6 state-of-the-art dataset condensation methods. The results show that TAGSAM consistently yields higher model training accuracy than all baselines. Compared with the best performing baseline, the average accuracy improvement of TAGSAM is 4.9%. Moreover, TAGSAM achieves competitive training accuracy with only a 1% condensation ratio of original TAGs. Besides, ablation studies indicate our model designs are effective. Furthermore efficiency studies suggest that TAGSAM is efficient in running time.

GCond* 表现不佳，因为其基于梯度的匹配策略旨在匹配具有相同类别标签的样本的梯度。然而，在无法使用类别标签的TAG凝聚中，这种方法是无效的，因为不同类别样本之间的匹配梯度会导致不稳定。此外，这种传统的图压缩方法被设计为仅压缩图模态，这意味着它们受到无法压缩文本模态的限制。GCond* performs poorly because its gradient-based matching strategy is designed to match the gradients of samples with the same class label. However, in TAG condensation, where no class label can be used, this approach is ineffective because matching gradients between different class samples will lead to instability. Furthermore, such conventional graph condensation methods are designed to compress graph modality only, which means they are limited by their inability to compress text modality.

现有的多模态凝结方法（例如 MTT-KNN 和 MTT-TAG）通常平均表现最好。值得注意的是，MTT-KNN 的准确率低于 MTT-TAG。这是因为 MTT-KNN 将文本压缩为嵌入向量，为此它必须找到最近邻的原始文本描述。这样的操作会破坏压缩节点特征和学习过程产生的文本之间的对齐。 尽管它们的性能更高，但它们也受到无法压缩离散文本描述的限制，并且受到不稳定的匹配目标的影响。我们提出的 TAGSAM 克服了这些限制，在所有数据集和凝结尺寸上实现了卓越的性能。与最强的基线 MTT-TAG 相比，它的准确性提高了 14.1%。对于较大的凝结尺寸，尽管与整个数据集上的训练相比，TAGSAM 的准确性表现出更大的可变性，但达到了与之高度可比的平均值。Existing multi-modal condensation methods like MTT-KNN and MTT-TAG generally perform best on average. Such an operation disrupts the alignment between the compressed node features and texts produced by the learning process. Despite their higher performance, they are also limited by their inability to compress discrete text descriptions and suffer from unstable matching objectives. 6. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了TAGSAM，一种新颖的文本属性图（TAG）数据集压缩方法，解决了现有方法无法有效压缩TAG的问题。我们的方法利用子图文本选择将多个文本压缩为有代表性的离散文本，并利用属性相似性匹配来分配鲁棒的合成属性。实验表明，在 TAGSAM 压缩到原始大小 1% 的图上训练的模型仍然保持有竞争力的性能，从而使得大规模 TAG 的高效预训练成为可能。In this work, we introduce TAGSAM, a novel dataset condensation method for Text-Attributed Graphs (TAGs) that addresses the issue that existing methods cannot effectively compress TAGs. Our method utilizes subgraph text selection to compress multiple texts into a representative and discrete one and attribute similarity matching to assign robust synthetic attributes. Experiments show that models trained on graphs condensed by TAGSAM to just 1% of their original size still maintain competitive performance, thus making efficient pre-training on large-scale TAGs feasible.

附录CAppendix C零样本分类Zero-shot Classification

遵循G2P2Following the G2P2(Wen and Fang, 2023; Wang et al., 2024b)，在本文中，零样本分类使用对比学习在统一语义空间内对齐图形和文本表示。该架构采用图形编码器为每个节点生成嵌入，并使用文本编码器将描述潜在类别（包括训练期间未见的类别）的文本提示转换为相应的类别嵌入。为了进行推理，模型通过计算节点嵌入与所有候选类嵌入之间的余弦相似度来对节点进行分类。然后，将该节点分配给相似度得分最高的类，从而能够有效分类为新类别，而无需为其提供任何特定的训练示例。, in this paper, zero-shot classification aligns graph and text representations within a unified semantic space using contrastive learning. The architecture employs a graph encoder to generate an embedding for each node and a text encoder to transform text prompts describing potential classes—including those unseen during training—into corresponding class embeddings. For inference, the model classifies a node by calculating the cosine similarity between its embedding and all candidate class embeddings. The node is then assigned to the class with the highest similarity score, enabling effective classification into novel categories without any specific training examples for them.
