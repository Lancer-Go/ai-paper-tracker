摘要Abstract

非高斯统计是数据同化的一个挑战。线性方法过度简化了问题，但完全非线性方法在实践中使用通常过于昂贵。最佳解决方案通常介于这两个极端之间。三角测量传输为非线性数据同化提供了灵活的框架。然而，它的成功取决于地图的参数化方式。太多的灵活性会导致过度拟合；太少会错过重要的结构。为了解决这种平衡，我们开发了一种自适应算法，可以自动选择简约的参数化。我们的方法使用 P 样条基函数和信息标准作为模型复杂性的连续度量。该公式支持梯度下降，并允许在高维设置中进行高效、精细的适应。生成的算法不需要超参数调整。它根据系统统计数据和集合大小将传输图调整到适当的复杂程度。我们展示了其在非线性、非高斯问题（包括高维分布式地下水模型）中的性能。Non-Gaussian statistics are a challenge for data assimilation. Linear methods oversimplify the problem, yet fully nonlinear methods are often too expensive to use in practice. The best solution usually lies between these extremes. Triangular measure transport offers a flexible framework for nonlinear data assimilation. Its success, however, depends on how the map is parametrized. Too much flexibility leads to overfitting; too little misses important structure. To address this balance, we develop an adaptation algorithm that selects a parsimonious parametrization automatically. Our method uses P-spline basis functions and an information criterion as a continuous measure of model complexity. This formulation enables gradient descent and allows efficient, fine-scale adaptation in high-dimensional settings. The resulting algorithm requires no hyperparameter tuning. It adjusts the transport map to the appropriate level of complexity based on the system statistics and ensemble size. We demonstrate its performance in nonlinear, non-Gaussian problems, including a high-dimensional distributed groundwater model.

1 简介Introduction

集合数据同化是高维动力系统统计推断的重要工具。它将模型预测与观测相结合来估计不确定状态和参数，广泛应用于地球科学、工程和环境建模Ensemble data assimilation is an important tool for statistical inference in high-dimensional dynamical systems. It combines model predictions with observations to estimate uncertain states and parameters, and it is widely used in geoscience, engineering, and environmental modeling(e.g., Carrassi et al., 2018; Fletcher, 2017).

在许多应用中，前向模型是非线性的，先验分布是非高斯的。因此，后验分布可能表现出偏态、多模态和非线性依赖结构。 经典的集成方法，包括集成滤波器和平滑器In many applications, forward models are nonlinear and prior distributions are non-Gaussian. Consequently, posterior distributions may exhibit skewness, multimodality, and nonlinear dependence structure. Classical ensemble methods, including ensemble filters and smoothers(Evensen, 2003; Evensen and Van Leeuwen, 2000)和迭代集成平滑器and iterative ensemble smoothers(Emerick and Reynolds, 2013; White, 2018)，通常依赖于线性更新和重复的前向模型评估。 虽然在近线性高斯设置中有效，但这些方法通常不会产生可靠的后验样本，并且可能需要对前向模型进行多次顺序模拟。, usually rely on linear updates and repeated forward model evaluations. While effective in near linear-Gaussian settings, these methods do not in general produce reliable posterior samples and may require multiple sequential simulations of the forward model.

三角传输图为基于非线性采样的推理提供了一个有前途的框架Triangular transport maps provide a promising framework for nonlinear sampling-based inference(Spantini et al., 2022)。三角形传输图在感兴趣的目标分布和简单的用户定义的参考测量之间构建双射。一旦学习，它就可以实现目标密度评估、无条件采样和条件采样，而无需额外的正向模型运行。在非线性设置中，传输图可以表征复杂的后验结构，同时保持计算的易处理性。. A triangular transport map constructs a bijection between a target distribution of interest and a simple user-defined reference measure. Once learned, it enables target density evaluations, unconditional sampling, and conditional sampling without additional forward model runs. In nonlinear settings, transport maps can characterize complex posterior structure while retaining computational tractability.

三角运输的成功关键取决于控制地图的复杂性。过于严格的映射再现了线性高斯近似的局限性，并且无法捕获非高斯结构。相比之下，过度非线性的更新可能会过度拟合采样噪声并导致不利的偏差-方差权衡。这一挑战推动了平衡近似精度和统计稳定性的适应策略的发展。The success of triangular transport depends critically on controlling map complexity. Overly restrictive maps reproduce the limitations of linear–Gaussian approximations and fail to capture non-Gaussian structure. By contrast, overly nonlinear updates may overfit to sampling noise and incur unfavourable bias-variance tradeoffs. This challenge has motivated the development of adaptation strategies balancing approximation accuracy and statistical stability.

在这项工作中，我们为三角传输地图开发了一个连续复杂性适应框架。我们对由平滑参数索引的一系列函数内的映射组件进行参数化，并将复杂性选择公式化为该平滑参数在信息标准上的优化。由此产生的适应目标允许对平滑参数进行基于梯度的有效探索，从而实现高维集成设置中的实际应用。In this work, we develop a continuous complexity adaptation framework for triangular transport maps. We parametrise map components within a family of functions indexed by a smoothness parameter and formulate complexity selection as the optimisation of this smoothing parameter over an information criterion. The resulting adaptation objective admits efficient gradient-based exploration of the smoothing parameter, enabling practical application in high-dimensional ensemble settings.

5 结论Conclusion

在这份手稿中，我们开发了一种用于非线性三角传输图的连续复杂度自适应算法。通过平滑惩罚系数，我们可以不断调整通过 P 样条参数化的三角形图的自由度。利用信息标准作为连续复杂性的度量，我们通过梯度下降有效地自动识别地图复杂性的最佳水平。In this manuscript, we developed a continuous complexity adaptation algorithm for nonlinear triangular transport maps. Through a smoothing penalty coefficient, we can continuously adapt the degrees of freedom of a triangular map parametrized through P-splines. Leveraging an information criterion as a measure of continuous complexity, we efficiently and automatically identify an optimal level of map complexity through gradient descent.

我们在三个数值实验中展示了所得算法的性能。首先，我们说明了二元测试分布中拟合不足和过度拟合的后果。其次，我们展示了 Lorenz-63 系统中非线性滤波算法的性能，并将结果与​​之前的研究进行了比较。最后，我们说明了该方法在高维分布式地下水模型中历史匹配的可扩展性。We demonstrated the performance of the resulting algorithm in three numerical experiments. First, we illustrated the consequences of under- and overfitting in a bivariate test distribution. Second, we demonstrated the performance of the algorithm for nonlinear filtering in a Lorenz-63 system and compared the results to a previous study. Finally, we illustrated the method’s scalability for history matching in a high-dimensional, distributed groundwater model.

局限性包括计算需求：虽然适应策略本身是有效的，但它是基于每个地图组件函数的完整 P 样条基础的构建和评估Limitations include computational demand: while the adaptation strategy itself is efficient, it is based on the construction and evaluation of a full P-spline basis for each map component functionSiS_{i}。因此，地图复杂性的降低不会导致计算需求的减少。为了提高效率，我们还假设可能没有必要调整每个映射分量函数的单调部分，因为单调重新参数化已经发挥了正则化影响。. In consequence, reductions in map complexity do not cause a reduction in computational demand. To increase efficiency, we also hypothesize that it may not be necessary to adapt the monotone part of each map component function, since the monotone reparametrisation already exerts a regularising influence.

为了确保三角映射的可扩展性，必须有效地检测和利用高维系统中的条件独立性。在本研究中，我们利用稀疏 Cholesky 算法To ensure scalability in triangular maps, it is imperative to efficiently detect and exploit conditional independence in high-dimensional systems. In this study, we leverage the sparse Cholesky algorithm bySchäfer et al. (2021)，它提出了空间变量的有效排序。然而，在许多系统中，可能有必要利用图检测策略，该策略也可以有效地处理没有空间或时间坐标的变量。为此，与位置无关的策略（例如坐标梯度增强或 LASSO）似乎是未来研究的有希望的方向。, which proposes an efficient ordering for spatial variables. However, in many systems, it may be necessary to leverage graph detection strategies that can also deal efficiently with variables that do not have spatial or temporal coordinates. To this end, location-agnostic strategies such as coordinate gradient boosting or LASSO seem promising directions for future research.

本研究开发的算法允许高效的即插即用非线性数据同化，从而自动搜索简约的地图参数化，而不需要手动超参数调整。我们观察到该算法可以匹配甚至优于手动调整的非线性三角图。我们还表明，其简约的非线性更新使后验样本能够比线性高斯替代方案更好地遵循复杂的地质先验。The algorithm developed in this study permits efficient plug-and-play nonlinear data assimilation, which automates the search for a parsimonious map parametrisation without requiring manual hyperparameter tuning. We observe that this algorithm can match and even outperform manually tuned nonlinear triangular maps. We also show that its parsimonious nonlinear updates allow the posterior samples to honor complex geological priors significantly better than linear-Gaussian alternatives.
