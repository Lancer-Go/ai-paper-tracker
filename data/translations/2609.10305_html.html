RiLM 将小语言建模重新构建为几何：上下文是流形上的路径，解码读取到词汇点的距离，而不是应用单独的输出矩阵。使用共享 MLPRiLM reframes small language modeling as geometry: context is a path on a manifold, and decoding reads distances to vocabulary points rather than applying a separate output matrix. Stable Möbius recurrence proved essential—without it, hyperbolic RiLM does not train.

在我们研究的受控范围内，测地线解码是当参数稀缺时耦合表示和预测的具体方法。它并不是十亿参数预训练的直接替代品，但它提供了一种原则上的替代方案Within the controlled regime we study, geodesic decoding is a concrete way to couple representation and prediction when parameters are scarce.
