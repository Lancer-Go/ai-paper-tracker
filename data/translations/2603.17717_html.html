用于网络攻击的机器学习 用于合成数据生成的对抗性学习方法的分类和统计评估Machine Learning for Network Attacks Classification and Statistical Evaluation of Adversarial Learning Methodologies for Synthetic Data Generation

Iakovos-Christos Zarkadis    Christos Douligeris
摘要Abstract

网络攻击的监督检测一直是网络入侵检测系统（NIDS）的关键部分。如今，在人工智能 (AI) 的关键时期，利用生成人工智能 (GenAI) 和强化学习等先进技术进行更复杂的攻击，如果我们希望保护分散在网络上的个人数据，它已成为至关重要的组成部分。在本文中，我们在第一个统一的多模式 NIDS 数据集中解决了两个任务，该数据集包含来自重新处理的 CIC-IDS-2017、CIC-IoT-2023、UNSW-NB15 和 CIC-DDoS-2019 的流级数据、数据包有效负载信息和时间上下文特征，具有相同的特征空间。在第一个任务中，我们使用机器学习（ML）算法，通过分层交叉验证，以防止网络攻击，稳定可靠。在第二个任务中，我们使用对抗性学习算法生成合成数据，将它们与真实数据进行比较，并使用 SDV 框架评估它们的保真度、实用性和隐私性，Supervised detection of network attacks has always been a critical part of network intrusion detection systems (NIDS). Nowadays, in a pivotal time for artificial intelligence (AI), with even more sophisticated attacks that utilize advanced techniques, such as generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) and reinforcement learning, it has become a vital component if we wish to protect our personal data, which are scattered across the web. In this paper, we address two tasks, in the first unified multi-modal NIDS dataset, which incorporates flow-level data, packet payload information and temporal contextual features, from the reprocessed CIC-IDS-2017, CIC-IoT-2023, UNSW-NB15 and CIC-DDoS-2019, with the same feature space. In the first task we use machine learning (ML) algorithms, with stratified cross validation, in order to prevent network attacks, with stability and reliability. In the second task we use adversarial learning algorithms to generate synthetic data, compare them with the real ones and evaluate their fidelity, utility and privacy using the SDV framework,ff-分歧、可区分性和非参数统计检验。通过将 Synthetic Data Vault 框架、TRTS 和 TSTR 测试与非参数统计测试相结合，研究结果为入侵检测和生成模型提供了稳定的 ML 模型，具有高保真度和实用性。-divergences, distinguishability and non-parametric statistical tests. The findings provide stable ML models for intrusion detection and generative models with high fidelity and utility, by combining the Synthetic Data Vault framework, the TRTS and TSTR tests, with non-parametric statistical tests andff- 分歧措施。-divergence measures.

I 简介Introduction

在人工智能时代，检测攻击类型变得更加困难。机器学习算法已在许多研究论文和应用领域中得到实现In the era of AI, detecting the type of the attack becomes even more difficult. Machine learning algorithms have been implemented in many research papers and application areas[10]显示了跨各种知名入侵检测系统 (IDS) 数据集应对攻击的效率showing efficiency in tackling attacks across a variety of well-known intrusion detection system (IDS) datasets[38], [24], [29], [30], [32]。然而，有必要不仅仅关注在分类指标上取得高结果，还需要为入侵检测系统提供可靠性和稳定性。. Nevertheless, it is necessary to not simply focus on achieving high results in classification metrics, but offering reliability and stability to an intrusion detection system.

在过去的几年中，对抗性攻击因其复杂的攻击而变得流行，这些攻击利用对抗性机器学习算法在数据中注入扰动，以使强分类器无法正确分类某些攻击，从而误导 IDS。In the last few years adversarial attacks have gained popularity due to their sophisticated attacks, that utilize adversarial machine learning algorithms to inject perturbations in the data, in order to make a strong classifier unable to classify certain attacks correctly, thus misleading the IDS.

许多类型的算法，从线性模型和支持向量机到神经网络和强化学习已经被提出Many types of algorithms, from linear models and SVM to neural networks and reinforcement learning have been proposed in[5]。物联网表格数据中常用的算法是作为多类分类基线的逻辑回归，以及深度神经网络和集成模型等更高级的模型. Commonly used algorithms in IoT tabular data are logistic regression as a baseline for multi-class classification as well as more advanced models like deep neural networks and ensemble models[9]。更详细的研究检查了 LDA、Xgboost 和决策树的跨数据集验证. More detailed studies have examined cross-dataset validation with LDA, Xgboost and decision trees[24]。不同的框架已广泛用于数据生成目的，例如 Synthcity. Different frameworks have been widely used for data generation purposes like Synthcity[40]和SDVand SDV[26], [8]提供高质量的生成器和评估指标。研究人员的注意力最近集中在许多用于 GAN 和 VAE 的合成网络数据生成的条件架构上offering high quality generators and evaluating metrics. The attention of researchers has recently been directed in many conditional architectures for synthetic network data generation for GANs and VAEs[20], [21], [22], [25], [27], [31]，以及在新兴的, as well as in the emergingff-GAN 和扩散模型-GANs and diffusion models[6], [12], [39]法学硕士是顶级模型with the LLMs being top-tier models[37]。此外，一些考虑网络数据的论文中已经使用了针对均值、协方差矩阵和多元分布比较的特定非参数统计检验. Furthermore, the specific non-parametric statistical tests, for means, covariance matrices and multivariate distribution comparison, have been used in some papers considering network data[14], [17], [19]，但可能从未被合并以提供完整的视图。在本文中，我们的重点不是如何执行此类攻击，而是如何演变生成过程并测试不同的对抗算法、扩散森林和 LLM 以创建合成数据, but may have never been combined to offer a complete view. Our focus, in this paper is not how to perform such attacks but on how the generation process evolves and test different adversarial algorithms, diffusion forests and LLM to create synthetic data[27], [31]并评估哪些是在保真度、实用性和隐私方面最有效的。在本文中我们使用 TRTS、TSTR 测试、监督区分度测试、and to evaluate which are the most efficient ones, in terms of fidelity, utility and privacy. In this paper we use TRTS, TSTR tests, supervised distinguishability tests,ff- 分歧、隐私测量-divergences, privacy measurement[11]并首次结合SDV框架and combine for the first time the SDV framework[34]，通过稳健的非参数统计测试来比较多元均值、协方差矩阵和特征的联合多元分布, with robust non-parametric statistical tests for comparing multivariate means, covariance matrices and the feature’s joint multivariate distributions[14], [17], [19].

这项工作分为两个部分。首先，我们介绍攻击的分类，讨论数据收集、预处理步骤和用于入侵检测的 ML 模型。在第二部分中，我们展示了合成数据的生成过程，包括对数学文献的简短回顾、对抗性、扩散和 LLM 算法的训练以及最后的评估程序。This work is organized in two sections. In the first we present the classification of the attacks, where we discuss data collection, pre-processing steps and the ML models used for intrusion detection. In the second section we show the generation process of synthetic data, including a short review of the mathematical literature, the training of adversarial, diffusion and LLM algorithms and finally the evaluation procedure.

预处理过程首先省略重复值，然后对标签变量进行编码，测试不同的方法，例如对具有少量唯一值的某些数值变量进行分箱。我们进行了深入的统计分析，包括发现线性和非线性关系、通过 QQ 图和拟合优度检验（如 Kolmogorov-Smirnov 和 Anderson Darling）对正态性是否存在进行假设检验，以及对一些数值特征进行回归分析。我们还使用 IQR、Z 分数、DBSCAN 和局部异常值因子检查了异常值的存在，并且由于异常值数量较多，我们更愿意保留它们并使用鲁棒缩放器作为缩放方法，在我们执行了The pre-processing procedure started by omitting the duplicate values, then encoding the label variable, testing different methods like binning for certain numeric variables with a small number of unique values. We performed an in-depth statistical analysis that included the discovering of linear and non-linear relationships, hypothesis testing for existence of normality through QQ-Plots and goodness of fit tests like Kolmogorov-Smirnov and Anderson Darling, as well as regression analysis for some numeric features. We also examined the existence of outliers with IQR, Z-score, DBSCAN and Local-Outlier Factor and due the great number of outliers we preferred to keep them and use as a scaling method the Robust scaler, after we performed an80/2080/20相对于攻击类型变量，训练/测试分层分割。train/test stratified split, with respect to the attack type variable.

此外，大量的特征工程技术被用来提高机器学习算法的效率，分别用于训练和测试数据。为了解决数据集不平衡的问题，我们仅针对训练集研究了不同的过采样和欠采样技术，以避免数据泄漏，例如合成少数过采样（SMOTE）、编辑最近邻（ENN）和自适应合成采样（ADASYN），这使得我们选择过采样方法来减少信息丢失的影响。最后，对于特征选择过程，我们测试了多种选择技术，例如递归特征消除、逐步（向前-向后）消除和 boruta。Furthermore a great deal of feature engineering techniques were used to increase the efficiency of the ML algorithms, separately for the training and testing data. To tackle the problem of the unbalanced dataset, we investigated different over-sampling and under-sampling techniques only for the training set to avoid data leakage, like synthetic minority oversampling (SMOTE), edited nearest neighbor (ENN) and adaptive synthetic sampling (ADASYN) that lead us to select the over-sampling approach to reduce the impact of information loss. Finally for the feature selection procedure we tested multiple selection techniques like recursive feature elimination, stepwise (forward-backward) elimination and boruta.

三、III综合数据的生成Generation of Synthetic Data

如今，合成数据的生成越来越受欢迎，尤其是随着 GenAI 的炒作。非常有趣的是，假数据的生成如何欺骗基于机器学习的入侵检测系统，但更有趣的是我们如何使用这种生成技术来构建与真实数据共享统计属性的合成数据集。The generation of synthetic data has gained popularity now-days, especially with the hype of GenAI. It is very interesting how the generation of fake data can trick a machine learning based intrusion detection system, but even more interesting how we can use such generation techniques to construct synthetic datasets, that share statistical properties with the real ones.

在本文中，我们阐明了当网络数据以表格格式处理时，与其他基线模型相比，树模型和集成模型在速度、准确性和稳定性方面的效率如何。与基本条件架构相比，条件架构与混合模型结合时，在捕获条件变量的概率分布函数方面具有出色的性能。我们还重点介绍了扩散和 LLM 模型在可区分性测试中的表现以及捕获潜在分布和质量隐私权衡的情况。使用稳健的非参数统计测试来分析从非差分或差分架构生成的合成数据的多变量性质，以及散度度量，使我们能够对网络中攻击的单变量分布和特征的联合分布有一个坚实的了解。未来的工作包括与其他众所周知的 IDS 数据集进行跨数据集验证，对这个多模式数据集使用对抗性攻击，对生成模型使用条件隐私保护架构，对 UMNIDS 数据集的不平衡版本使用贝叶斯混合，利用无监督的可区分性测试并应用参数测试来测量真实数据和合成数据的多元分布之间的差异。有必要对不同的差分隐私架构及其效果以及它们对隐私分数与保真度的影响进行进一步评估。In this paper we clarified how efficient, in terms of speed, accuracy and stability, the tree and ensemble models are in classifying network attacks compared to other baseline models, when the network data are processed in tabular format. The conditional architectures, when combined with mixture models have outstanding performance in capturing the probability distribution function of the conditional variable, contrast to basic conditional architectures. We also highlight the diffusion and LLM models for their performance in the distinguishability tests and for capturing the underlying distributions and the quality-privacy tradeoff. The use of robust and non-parametric statistical tests for analyzing the multivariate nature of the synthetic data, generated either from non-differential or differential architectures, along with the divergence measures give us a solid perspective of the univariate distribution of the attacks in the network and the joint distribution of the features. Future work includes cross-dataset validation with other well known IDS datasets, using adversarial attacks for this multi-modal dataset, using conditional, privacy-preserving architectures for generative models, with Bayesian mixtures, for the unbalanced version of the UMNIDS dataset, utilizing unsupervised distinguishability tests and applying parametric tests to measure the difference between the multivariate distribution of the real and synthetic data. Further evaluation is necessary with different differential-privacy architectures and their effect and their affect in privacy scores in combination with fidelity.
