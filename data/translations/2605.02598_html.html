摘要Abstract

人工智能可以学习哪些工作？我们对美国经济中的每一个职业都进行了研究。现有的指数衡量的是人工智能能力和职业任务之间的重叠，而不是人工智能系统可以学习执行哪些任务，从而对现有能力和可学习性之间差距较大的职业进行错误分类。培训后的强化学习现在是前沿的主导范式，它是围绕任务完成而构建的，并且比以前的方法更直接地映射到基于任务的职业分类架构。使用以 RL 专家开发的标题为指导并根据已确认的部署案例进行验证的 LLM 注释器，我们对所有 17,951 个 ONET 任务进行培训可行性评分，并汇总到职业级别，生成 RL 可行性指数。该指数与针对特定职业群体的现有人工智能暴露衡量标准存在很大差异：发电厂操作员、铁路售票员和飞机货物装卸主管在强化学习可行性方面得分较高，但在一般人工智能暴露方面得分较低，而创造性和人际角色（音乐家、医生、自然科学经理）则表现出相反的情况。这些分歧对政策干预有直接影响。Which jobs can AI learn to do? We examine this for every occupation in the US economy. Existing indices measure the overlap between AI capabilities and occupational tasks rather than which tasks AI systems can learn to perform, and as a result misclassify occupations where the gap between present capability and learnability is large. Reinforcement learning in post-training, now the dominant paradigm at the frontier, is structured around task completion and maps more directly onto the task-based architecture of occupational classifications than prior approaches. Using LLM annotators guided by a rubric developed with RL experts and validated against confirmed deployment cases, we score all 17,951 ONET tasks for training feasibility and aggregate to the occupation level, producing an RL Feasibility Index. The index diverges sharply from existing AI exposure measures for specific occupation groups: power plant operators, railroad conductors, and aircraft cargo handling supervisors score high on RL feasibility but low on general AI exposure, while creative and interpersonal roles (musicians, physicians, natural sciences managers) show the reverse. These divergences carry direct implications for policy interventions.

1 方法论Methodology

我们在任务级别对 RL 可行性进行评分，汇总到职业，并与现有的 AI 暴露指标进行比较。我们使用 O*NET 30.0，其中包含 8 位 SOC 级别的 894 个职业的 17,951 个独特任务语句。对于每项任务，我们都会询问构建一个让人工智能可以学习执行任务的强化学习环境的可行性如何。答案取决于任务是否允许可验证的奖励信号、可模拟的环境和易于处理的决策空间等属性。We score RL feasibility at the task level, aggregate to occupations, and compare with existing AI exposure measures. We use O*NET 30.0, which contains 17,951 unique task statements across 894 occupations at the 8-digit SOC level. For each task, we ask how feasible is it to construct an RL environment in which AI could learn to perform it. The answer depends on properties such as whether the task admits a verifiable reward signal, a simulable environment, and a tractable decision space.

在对任何维度进行评分之前，我们会施加一个二元物理可行性门，以确定任务是否需要与物质世界进行大量的物理交互。不可避免地需要物理身体的任务（体力劳动、精细运动灵活性、运动）无法通过，并且 RL 指数为 0，因为我们关注的是软件而不是物理 AI 中存在的 RL 环境。主要通过数字方式执行的任务通过大门并进行维度评分。Before scoring any dimensions, we impose a binary physical feasibility gate that determines whether the task requires substantial physical interaction with the material world. Tasks that irreducibly require a physical body (manual labour, fine motor dexterity, locomotion) fail the gate and receive an RL index of 0, since we focus on RL environments that exist in software rather than physical AI. Tasks that can be performed primarily through digital means pass the gate and proceed to dimensional scoring.

对于通过门的任务，我们将 RL 可行性分解为八个维度，每个维度按照 1-10 Likert 量表进行评分（1 = RL 不可行，10 = RL 理想）。评分假设通过 RL 方法对预训练的基础模型进行微调，例如来自人类反馈的强化学习 (RLHF)、来自人工智能反馈的强化学习 (RLAIF) 或具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR)。有关这些方法的基础治疗，请参见For tasks that pass the gate, we decompose RL feasibility into eight dimensions, each scored on a 1–10 Likert scale (1 = RL infeasible, 10 = ideal for RL). The scoring assumes fine-tuning a pre-trained foundation model via RL methods such as reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), reinforcement learning from AI feedback (RLAIF), or reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). For foundational treatments of these methods, seeChristiano et al. (2017); Ouyang et al. (2022); Bai et al. (2022); Rafailov et al. (2023).

D2 (Environment Simulability) asks whether the task setting can be cheaply replicated digitally. The three MDP dimensions capture structural properties of the decision problem: whether task-relevant information is observable (D3), how varied task instances are and how broad the required expertise (D4), and how deep the sequential decision chain is (D5). 3 结论Conclusion

大多数现有的人工智能暴露指数都会询问当前语言模型目前可以做什么。我们构建了一个新的指数，通过强化学习（最近人工智能能力提升背后的训练范式）来衡量每个职业对自动化的暴露程度。将工作分解为任务，工作的强化学习暴露取决于任务级属性，例如任务具有可验证奖励的程度、可模拟的环境和易于处理的决策空间。我们为所有美国职业创建该指数。我们的 RL 可行性在工资和资历上都是驼峰形的：它在中上工资、职业中期工人中达到顶峰，在分布的两个极端中最低。Most existing AI exposure indices ask what current language models can currently do. We construct a new index that measures how exposed each occupation is to automation through reinforcement learning, the training paradigm behind recent AI capability gains. Decomposing jobs into tasks, the RL exposure of a job depends on task-level properties such as the degree to which the task has verifiable rewards, a simulable environment, and tractable decision spaces. We create this index for all US occupations. Our RL feasibility is hump-shaped in both wages and seniority: it peaks among upper-middle-wage, mid-career workers and is lowest at both extremes of the distribution.
