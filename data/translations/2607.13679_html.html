摘要Abstract

人工智能代理加入人类团队，提出了一个基本问题：当自动化代理成为常规参与者时，团队组织会加强还是削弱？我们在开源软件中研究这个问题，其中机器人打开拉取请求，审查代码，并与人们一起合并更改，留下每次交互的公共记录。我们将机器人视为参与者而不是工具，在每个项目采用第一个机器人之前和之后的两年内检查了 2,991 个 GitHub 项目。我们衡量制度理论与持久协调相关的三种能力——重复参与、社会记忆和角色分化——以及两种结果：冲突级联和产出独特性。采用机器人后，会出现更多的重复协作、对讨论中的特定机器人的更多认可、更少的冲突级联以及更独特的输出。这些变化集中在采用上，而不是逐渐积累。因为我们缺乏未经治疗的对照组，所以我们将结果解释为精确定时的关联，而不是因果效应。有两种模式很难用其他解释来解释：能力根据其功能（协调与分化）预测结果，而不是人类或机器人提供它们，而人方能力解释机器人与冲突的关联，但不能解释机器人与独特性的关联。研究结果与一个具体的解释是一致的：可预测的、基于规则的代理可以成为社区社会基础设施的一部分。机器人就是契机；社会组织是机制。AI agents are joining human teams, raising a basic question: when an automated agent becomes a regular participant, does group organization strengthen or weaken? We study this question in open-source software, where bots open pull requests, review code, and merge changes alongside people, leaving a public record of every interaction. Treating bots as participants rather than tools, we examine 2,991 GitHub projects for two years before and after each adopted its first bot. We measure three capabilities that institutional theory links to durable coordination—repeated engagement, social memory, and role differentiation—and two outcomes: conflict cascades and output distinctiveness. Bot adoption is followed by more repeated collaboration, greater recognition of specific bots in discussion, fewer conflict cascades, and more distinctive outputs. These changes cluster around adoption rather than accumulating gradually. Because we lack an untreated comparison group, we interpret the results as precisely timed associations, not causal effects. Two patterns are difficult for alternative explanations to account for: capabilities predict outcomes according to their function—coordination versus differentiation—rather than whether humans or bots provide them, and human-side capabilities account for the bot–conflict association but not the bot–distinctiveness association. The findings are consistent with a specific interpretation: predictable, rule-based agents can become part of a community's social infrastructure. The bot is the occasion; social organization is the mechanism.

一、简介1. Introduction

人工智能（AI）代理——能够感知环境、做出决策和采取自主行动的系统——越来越多地嵌入到人类协作工作中。 AI 代理现在建议对开源软件进行代码更改，协助医生进行临床诊断，并为科学研究的实验设计做出贡献（Rahwan 等人，2019 年），而自主编码代理现在可以在数十万个 GitHub 存储库中编写拉取请求（Hassan 等人，2025 年；AIDev 2026）。社会学家呼吁制定一个足以应对这一转变的研究计划：研究人类和机器如何共同产生社会成果的群体层面的科学，超越人机二元关系（Cerulo 2009；Tsvetkova 等人 2024）。实证问题不再是自动化代理是否会加入人类团队，而是它们的存在与组织结构中的什么相一致，从而使协调成为可能。Artificial-intelligence (AI) agents — systems capable of perceiving their environment, making decisions, and taking autonomous action — are increasingly embedded in human collaborative work. AI agents now propose code changes in open-source software, assist physicians in clinical diagnosis, and contribute to experimental design in scientific research (Rahwan et al. 2019), and autonomous coding agents now author pull requests across hundreds of thousands of GitHub repositories (Hassan et al. 2025; AIDev 2026). Sociologists have called for a research program adequate to this shift: a group-level science of how humans and machines jointly produce social outcomes, beyond the human–machine dyad (Cerulo 2009; Tsvetkova et al. 2024). The empirical question is no longer whether automated agents will join human teams but what their presence coincides with in the organizational fabric that makes coordination possible.

Contributors who remember who reviewed what, who keep encountering the same collaborators month after month, and who differentiate into distinct evaluative roles sustain an institutional pattern that prevents conflict and enables non-redundant exploration of the design space. The pattern persists not because it is mandated but because participants' capabilities reproduce it.

目前构成的人工智能代理仅部分具备这些能力。他们缺乏能够相互预期的可靠心理理论。他们不能像人类那样受到制裁。他们的记忆是配置文件的产物，而不是生活经验。然而，尽管存在这些限制，它们仍然可靠且可预测地以可能改变制度结构的方式存在。当机器人持续参与拉取请求、被视为可识别的参与者并处理特定的工作领域时，它们就会以类似于人类贡献者的方式嵌入到项目的交互结构中。这些痕迹是否重要——可预测的机器人存在是否与强化的制度结构相一致，还是仅仅增加噪音——是我们要考虑的问题。AI agents, as currently constituted, possess these capabilities only partially. They lack the reliable theory of mind that enables mutual anticipation. They cannot be sanctioned in the way humans can. Their memory is a product of configuration files rather than lived experience. Yet they are present, reliably and predictably, in ways that may alter the institutional fabric despite these limitations. When bots consistently participate in pull requests, are treated as recognizable actors, and handle specific domains of work, they become embedded in the project's interaction structure in ways that resemble human contributors. Whether such traces matter — whether predictable bot presence coincides with a reinforced institutional fabric or merely adds noise — is the question we take up.

本文提出并测试了一个主张：自动化代理可以成为社区制度基础设施的一部分，因为它们的参与改变了社区的共享互动记录——公开可观察的记录，记录了谁做了什么、对谁做了什么以及产生了什么后果——从中复制了制度。该记录包含机构能力的行为指标，进而预测集体结果。该链条中的每个环节都会产生可测试的影响，我们使用在机器人采用过程中观察到的 2,991 个开源项目来检查这些影响。This paper develops and tests a single claim: automated agents can become part of a community's institutional infrastructure because their participation alters the community's shared record of interaction — the publicly observable trace of who did what, toward whom, and with what consequences — from which institutions are reproduced. This record carries behavioral indicators of institutional capabilities, which in turn predict collective outcomes. Each link in this chain yields testable implications, which we examine using 2,991 open-source projects observed across bot adoption.

三个范围条件定义了我们的主张。首先，我们的衡量标准是行为指标，而不是机构本身。贡献者重叠、机器人引用语言和行动混合熵是广泛结构的狭隘可观察痕迹，因此我们的发现应该被解读为与制度强化一致的行为特征，而不是整体观察到的制度复制。其次，我们研究制度的协调维度，而不是它们在分配权力、赋予合法性或构建身份方面的作用（DiMaggio and Powell 1983；Meyer and Jepperson 2000）。第三，我们的分析涉及开源社区中基于规则的、可预测的、特定领域的、非会话机器人；对于更强大的人工智能代理的影响仍然是推测性的。Three scope conditions define our claims. First, our measures are behavioral indicators, not institutions themselves. Contributor overlap, bot-referencing language, and action-mix entropy are narrow observable traces of broad constructs, so our findings should be read as behavioral signatures consistent with institutional reinforcement rather than as institutional reproduction observed in its entirety. Second, we study the coordinative dimension of institutions, not their roles in allocating power, conferring legitimacy, or constructing identities (DiMaggio and Powell 1983; Meyer and Jepperson 2000). Third, our analysis concerns rule-based, predictable, domainspecific, non-conversational bots in open-source communities; implications for more capable AI agents remain conjectural.

同样的谨慎也适用于因果解释。我们的项目内事件研究设计以每个项目的机器人采用月份为中心，缺乏未经处理的比较组，并且采用似乎是内生于项目条件的。因此，我们将结果解释为精确定时的关联：机器人的采用与能力指标和结果的预测方向的变化相一致。我们陈述设计的推理限制，评估替代解释——直接自动化、项目成熟、平台变更和短暂冲击的选择——并为未来的工作确定更强的识别策略。机构解释不依赖于任何单一的估计，而是依赖于证据的融合：变化与采用的时间一致性、将能力指标与预测结果联系起来的特异性，以及机器人强度关联的不对称衰减。The same caution applies to causal interpretation. Our within-project event-study design, centered on each project's bot-adoption month, lacks an untreated comparison group, and adoption is plausibly endogenous to project conditions. We therefore interpret the results as precisely timed associations: bot adoption coincides with shifts in the predicted directions across the capability indicators and outcomes. We state the design's inferential limits, evaluate alternative explanations — direct automation, project maturation, platform change, and selection on transitory shocks — and identify stronger identification strategies for future work. The institutional interpretation rests not on any single estimate but on the convergence of evidence: the temporal alignment of changes with adoption, the specificity linking capability indicators to predicted outcomes, and the asymmetric attenuation of bot-intensity associations.

之前对开源软件中的机器人的研究测量了它们与输出（活动量、合并和拒绝时间、贡献者数量）的关联，结果好坏参半（Wessel 等人，2018 年、2022 年；Wessel 和 Steinmacher 2020 年；Rebatchi 等人，2024 年）。相关文献将自动化系统视为实验团队依赖和信任的对象（O'Neill et al. 2022）或作为对工人进行管理控制的工具（Kellogg、Valentine 和 Christin 2020）。我们提出了一个不同的问题，一个需要系统级视图的问题（Anthony、Bechky 和 ​​Fayard 2023）：机器人的采用与维持工作的社会组织相一致。当关系结构加强时，沟通量可能会下降；随着社会组织的发展，吞吐量可以保持不变。结构不同，因此我们将展示答案。Prior work on bots in open-source software has measured their association with output — activity volume, merge and rejection times, contributor counts — with mixed results (Wessel et al. 2018, 2022; Wessel and Steinmacher 2020; Rebatchi et al. 2024). Adjacent literatures frame automated systems as objects of reliance and trust in experimental teams (O'Neill et al. 2022) or as instruments of managerial control over workers (Kellogg, Valentine, and Christin 2020). We ask a different question, one that requires a system-level view (Anthony, Bechky, and Fayard 2023): what bot adoption coincides with in the social organization that sustains the work. Communication volume can fall while relational structure strengthens; throughput can remain unchanged while the social organization develops. The constructs differ, and so, we will show, do the answers.

2. 理论框架：机器人、共享记录和制度复制2. Theoretic Framework: Bots, the Shared Record, and Institutional Reproduction

该框架是一个具有三个环节的链条：机器人输入交互的共享记录；该记录载有机构能力的行为指标；能力可以预测集体结果。第 2.1 节到第 2.3 节依次展开链接； 2.4 节陈述了假设。The framework is a chain with three links: bots enter the shared record of interaction; the record carries behavioral indicators of institutional capabilities; and the capabilities predict collective outcomes. Sections 2.1 through 2.3 develop the links in turn; Section 2.4 states the hypotheses.

2.1 AI智能体作为协同生产的参与者2.1 AI Agents as Participants in Collaborative Production

我们所说的人工智能代理是指在社会环境中感知环境、做出决策并采取自主行动的系统。开源软件项目中的机器人满足每个标准：它感知存储库的事件流，根据配置的策略对其进行评估，并在人类参与者占据的同一交互空间内进行操作（打开拉取请求、发布评论、合并更改、关闭不活跃的讨论）。这些操作以名称进行，留下时间戳痕迹，可由其他参与者寻址，并触发社区人类成员的响应。By an AI agent we mean a system that perceives its environment, makes decisions, and takes autonomous action within a social setting. A bot in an open-source software project meets each criterion: it perceives the repository's event stream, evaluates it against a configured policy, and acts — opening pull requests, posting reviews, merging changes, closing inactive discussions — within the same interactional space that human participants occupy. The actions are taken under a name, leave timestamped traces, are addressable by other participants, and trigger responses from the human members of the community.

代理与工具的区别有两个特征（Lebeuf、Storey 和 Zagalsky 2018）。首先，代理在项目的交互空间中可见且自主地运行，而不是在单个用户的工作流程中隐形运行。区别很重要：人们发现，隐形人工智能助手在提高个人产出的同时也会提高项目的协调成本（Song、Agarwal 和 Wen 2024），这与我们的前提是一致的，即参与共享公共记录而不是自动化本身带来了我们研究的组织后果。其次，代理具有可寻址的身份：其他参与者在后续交互中使用和引用的稳定名称。可寻址身份使代理成为社区的一部分，而不是技术环境的特征。这种区别具有可观察到的行为后果：实验研究表明，人们将社会规范和期望应用于显示哪怕是最小的社会线索的机器，例如姓名、轮流或一致的行为（Nass and Moon 2000）。可识别的机器人通过社区的公共交互记录不断提供这些线索。Two features distinguish an agent from a tool (Lebeuf, Storey, and Zagalsky 2018). First, an agent operates visibly and autonomously in the project's interaction space, rather than invisibly within a single user's workflow. The distinction matters: invisible AI assistants have been found to raise coordination costs in projects even as they raise individual output (Song, Agarwal, and Wen 2024), which is consistent with our premise that participation in the shared public record, not automation itself, carries the organizational consequences we study. Second, an agent has addressable identity: a stable name that other participants use and reference in subsequent interactions. Addressable identity makes agents part of the community rather than features of the technological environment. The distinction has observable behavioral consequences: experimental research shows that people apply social norms and expectations to machines that display even minimal social cues, such as a name, turn-taking, or consistent behavior (Nass and Moon 2000). An identifiable bot provides these cues continuously through the community's public record of interaction.

我们从一开始就强调它们是什么类型的代理：基于规则的、模板驱动的、特定领域的和非会话的。他们是认知能力最差的代理人，符合上述定义的参与者资格，这使得他们在理论上很有用。如果如此简单的参与者留下了在制度上很重要的痕迹，那么该机制就不能依赖于丰富的认知。We emphasize at the outset what kind of agents these are: rule-based, template-driven, domainspecific, and non-conversational. They are the least cognitively sophisticated agents that qualify as participants under the definition above, which is what makes them theoretically useful. If participants this simple leave traces that matter institutionally, the mechanism cannot depend on rich cognition.

2.2 机构协调能力2.2 Institutional Capabilities for Coordination

机构履行许多职能：它们分配权力，定义合法性，塑造身份，并构成有权采取行动的行动者（DiMaggio 和 Powell 1983；Meyer 和 Rowan 1977；Meyer 和 Jepperson 2000）。本文关注制度生活的一个维度：协调维度——循环交互模式的再现，集体通过这种模式调整行为、遏制冲突并维持非冗余的探索（Berger and Luckmann 1966；Giddens 1984；Jepperson 1991；Zucker 1977）。我们把权力、合法性和身份问题放在一边，不是因为它们不重要，而是因为我们的档案措施无法触及它们。在这个维度中，我们的方法是微观社会学的：我们遵循制度主义的思路，将再生产定位于情境互动、Institutions perform many functions: they allocate power, define legitimacy, shape identities, and constitute the actors entitled to act (DiMaggio and Powell 1983; Meyer and Rowan 1977; Meyer and Jepperson 2000). This paper concerns one dimension of institutional life: the coordinative dimension — the reproduction of the recurrent interaction patterns through which a collective aligns behavior, contains conflict, and sustains non-redundant exploration (Berger and Luckmann 1966; Giddens 1984; Jepperson 1991; Zucker 1977). We set aside questions of power, legitimacy, and identity not because they are unimportant but because our archival measures cannot reach them. 正如扎克（Zucker，1977）所表明的那样，制度化是一个变量而不是二元的。这带有方法论的含义：我们不是问一个项目是否有机构，而是问维持其协调模式的能力有多强，以及能力强度是否可以预测这些模式应该产生的结果。原则上任何参与者都可以使用的三种能力是核心。Institutionalization, as Zucker (1977) showed, is a variable rather than a binary. This carries a methodological implication: rather than asking whether a project has institutions, we ask how strong the capabilities sustaining its coordinative patterns are, and whether capability strength predicts the outcomes those patterns are supposed to produce. Three capabilities, available in principle to any participant, are central.

社会记忆。Social memory.协调要求参与者保留过去互动的知识——不仅包括发生的事件，还包括谁对谁做了什么。社会学家长期以来一直将这种记忆定位于个人之外的集体记忆实践中（Halbwachs 1992 [1925]；Olick and Robbins 1998）以及在真实环境中进行认知工作的外部制品和记录中（Hutchins 1995）。相反，认知账户强调分布在个体之间的记忆以及他们彼此之间的知识（Wegner 1987；Lewis 2003）。我们不会在这些观点之间做出裁决。我们关注可观察的记录，通过这些记录，记忆可以用于协调，并且低认知智能体可以通过它参与。尽管机器人缺乏情景记忆，但它们的重复贡献会在共享记录中留下痕迹，参与者可以根据这些痕迹形成期望（Esposito 2022）。Coordination requires participants to retain knowledge of past interactions — not only that events occurred, but who did what to whom. Sociologists have long located such memory beyond the individual, in collective mnemonic practices (Halbwachs 1992 [1925]; Olick and Robbins 1998) and in the external artifacts and records that carry cognitive work in real settings (Hutchins 1995). Cognitive accounts instead emphasize memory distributed across individuals and their knowledge of one another (Wegner 1987; Lewis 2003). We do not adjudicate between these perspectives. We focus on the observable record through which memory becomes available for coordination, and through which low-cognition agents can participate. Although bots lack episodic memory, their repeated contributions leave traces in the shared record from which participants form expectations (Esposito 2022).

反复订婚。Repeated engagement.协调需要参与者之间持续的互动。对未来接触的期望鼓励对协调的投资，并建立信任基础上的熟悉度（Axelrod 1984；Kollock 1994；Uzzi 1997），而反复的集中互动会产生合作所依赖的团结和共同期望（Collins 2004）。在软件团队中，一起工作的经验预测的协调超出了个人经验的预测（Reagans、Argote 和 Brooks 2005），而熟悉度则减少了分布式工作中对显式协调的依赖（Espinosa 等人 2007）。我们的框架为重复参与分配了一项特定功能：防止冲突级联，即贡献者反复覆盖彼此的工作。这种级联标志着开源项目中协调的失败，与维基百科恢复冲突直接相似（Yasseri 等人，2012 年；Halfaker 等人，2013 年）。Coordination requires sustained interaction among participants. The expectation of future contact encourages investment in coordination and builds the familiarity underlying trust (Axelrod 1984; Kollock 1994; Uzzi 1997), and repeated focused interaction generates the solidarity and shared expectations on which cooperation rests (Collins 2004). In software teams, experience working together predicts coordination beyond what individual experience predicts (Reagans, Argote, and Brooks 2005), and familiarity reduces reliance on explicit coordination in distributed work (Espinosa et al. 2007). Our framework assigns repeated engagement one specific function: preventing conflict cascades, in which contributors repeatedly overwrite one another's work. Such cascades mark failures of coordination in open-source projects, with direct parallels in Wikipedia revert conflicts (Yasseri et al. 2012; Halfaker et al. 2013).

角色分化。Role differentiation.协调模式不仅可以通过重复的互动来维持，还可以通过参与者之间的专业化来维持（Stinchcombe 1965；Padgett and Powell 2012）。专业化在开源社区中很常见（von Krogh、Spaeth 和 Lakhani 2003）。角色分化有两个功能：它跨领域划分任务，并扩大了集体探索的视角范围（1991 年 3 月；2007 年页）。Coordinative patterns are sustained not only through repeated interaction but also through specialization among participants (Stinchcombe 1965; Padgett and Powell 2012). Specialization is common in open-source communities (von Krogh, Spaeth, and Lakhani 2003). Role differentiation serves two functions: it divides tasks across domains, and it expands the range of perspectives available for collective exploration (March 1991; Page 2007).

2.3 机器人作为共享记录的贡献者2.3 Bots as Contributors to the Shared Record

如果协调依赖于共享的交互记录，那么即使是认知有限的智能体也可以做出贡献，只要其行为留下可公开观察、可解释的痕迹。可预测地处理特定贡献者的机器人将成为此记录的一部分，尤其是当其他人在后续交互中响应或引用其操作时。到那时，它不再是一种工具，而是作为协调的参与者（Seeber et al. 2020）。If coordination depends on the shared record of interaction, then even an agent with limited cognition can contribute, provided its actions leave publicly observable, interpretable traces. A bot that predictably addresses specific contributors becomes part of this record, especially when others respond to or reference its actions in subsequent interactions. At that point it functions less as a tool than as a participant in coordination (Seeber et al. 2020).

我们并不认为机器人和人类是可以互换的。机器人缺乏心理理论，不能在传统意义上得到认可，并且依赖于编程规则而不是经验（Premack 和 Woodruff 1978；Mead 1934；Heckathorn 1990；参见 Strachan 等人 2024）。我们的主张是共享记录层面的功能等效性：如果机器人的重复跟踪支持与人类贡献者相同的期望形成，那么无论代理内部的机制如何，它们都可以加强协调。这种观点与赋予非人类行为者社会意义的理论相关（Latour 1992；Nass and Moon 2000；Cerulo 2009），但它提出了一种关于社区层面组织的更狭隘且可检验的主张。We do not argue that bots and humans are interchangeable. Bots lack theory of mind, cannot be sanctioned in the conventional sense, and rely on programmed rules rather than experience (Premack and Woodruff 1978; Mead 1934; Heckathorn 1990; cf. Strachan et al. 2024). Our claim is one of functional equivalence at the level of the shared record: if a bot's recurring traces support the same expectation formation as those of a human contributor, they can reinforce coordination regardless of the mechanism inside the agent. This view is related to theories that attribute social significance to nonhuman actors (Latour 1992; Nass and Moon 2000; Cerulo 2009), but it advances a narrower and testable claim about community-level organization.

有两个功能使机器人成为共享记录的重要贡献者。首先，他们是异常稳定的参与者。与人类贡献者的参与随着时间的推移而变化不同，机器人以机械规律重复出现，为重复参与提供了可靠的基础。其次，机器人是可寻址的：它们具有持久的身份，由贡献者命名和引用，并与特定的工作领域相关联。可寻址性为参与者提供了一个稳定的对象来形成对未来行为的期望。Two features make bots consequential contributors to the shared record. First, they are unusually stable participants. Unlike human contributors, whose participation varies over time, bots recur with mechanical regularity, providing a reliable basis for repeated engagement. Second, bots are addressable: they have persistent identities, are named and referenced by contributors, and become associated with specific domains of work. Addressability gives participants a stable object for forming expectations about future behavior.

这些功能激发了两个机器人端能力指标，由机器人留下的痕迹而不是其内部认知来定义。机器人对人类的寻址捕获了机器人针对特定贡献者而不是广播的直接行动的程度；解决问题就是分配责任，因此应该减少冲突。机器人间交互捕获机器人对彼此输出的作用程度，组成专门的工作流程，扩展项目的协调行动库；因此，链接应该增加独特性。我们的结构预测是，这些指标与它们的痕迹执行的功能（协调或分化）相一致，而不是与贡献者是人类还是自动化相一致。These features motivate two bot-side capability indicators, defined by the traces bots leave rather than by their internal cognition. Bot-to-human addressing captures the extent to which bots direct actions toward specific contributors rather than broadcasting; addressing assigns responsibility and should therefore reduce conflict. Inter-bot interaction captures the extent to which bots act on one another's outputs, composing specialized workflows that expand the project's repertoire of coordinated action; chaining should therefore increase distinctiveness. Our structural prediction is that these indicators align with the functions their traces perform — coordination or differentiation — rather than with whether the contributor is human or automated.

The construct has direct peer-production precedents: revert chains are the behavioral signature of conflict on Wikipedia (Yasseri et al. 2012; Halfaker et al. 2013), and cascades are their version-control analogue.

表 7 的两个面板之间的不对称性值得强调，因为它是与其他解释最不兼容的单一结果。一个通用的混杂因素——成熟度、生存、平台变化、项目健康——必须解释为什么机器人强度与一个结果的关联被两个人类行为指标完全吸收，而它与另一个结果的关联在相同的控制下却没有改变。机构账户准确地预测了这种不对称性：冲突遏制是通过机器人的存在所对应的人方协调能力来运作的，而独特性则利用了能力指标无法衡量的操作新颖性。The asymmetry between the two panels of Table 7 deserves emphasis, because it is the single result least compatible with the alternative explanations. A generic confound — maturation, survival, platform change, project health — would have to explain why the bot-intensity association with one outcome is fully absorbed by two human-side behavioral indicators while its association with the other outcome is unchanged by the same controls. The institutional account predicts exactly this asymmetry: conflict containment operates through the human-side coordination capabilities that the bot's presence coincides with, while distinctiveness draws on operational novelty that the capability indicators do not measure.

8.2 机构作为机制，机器人作为场合8.2 Institution as Mechanism, Bot as Occasion

衰减不对称性（表7）是机构账户与机器人是高效工具的替代方案之间最直接的比较。如果机器人在不改变社会组织的情况下提高了吞吐量，那么它们与级联减少的关联应该是直接的，并且应该在能力控制后保留，并且仅测量吞吐量的研究将捕获它们的全部效果。此类研究几乎没有发现什么（Wessel et al. 2018），或者发现沟通减少（Wessel et al. 2022），这与组织性而非机械性的影响是一致的。在我们的数据中，机器人强度与级联的关联完全由两个人为指标来解释——如果机器人通过社会工作组织的变化与冲突相关，则预期的模式。The attenuation asymmetry (Table 7) is the most direct comparison between the institutional account and the alternative that bots are efficient tools. If bots raised throughput without altering social organization, their association with cascade reduction should be direct and should remain after capability controls, and studies measuring only throughput would have captured their full effect. That such studies found little (Wessel et al. 2018), or found reduced communication (Wessel et al. 2022), is consistent with the effects being organizational rather than mechanical. In our data, the bot-intensity association with cascades is fully accounted for by two human-side indicators — the pattern expected if bots relate to conflict through changes in the social organization of work.

这种阅读将这项研究置于人工智能之前的社会学谱系中。 Barley（1986）表明，相同的技术引入不同的组织会产生不同的结构结果，因为该技术是重组互动的机会，而不是单独起作用的原因；技术的组织效应是通过实践实现的（Orlikowski 2000）。我们的结果将这种洞察力扩展到人口规模的自动化参与者：机器人是场合；社会组织是机制。原则上，一个项目可以通过任何加强重复参与和角色区分的方式来实现类似的冲突减少，例如稳定的招聘或指导。机器人提供的是可靠性：它们不会离开、减少参与或变得不可用。This reading places the study in a sociological lineage that predates AI. Barley (1986) showed that identical technology introduced into different organizations produced different structural outcomes, because the technology was an occasion for restructuring interaction rather than a cause acting alone; technology's organizational effects are realized through practice (Orlikowski 2000). Our results extend this insight to automated participants at population scale: the bot is the occasion; the social organization is the mechanism. A project could in principle achieve similar conflict reduction by any means that strengthens repeated engagement and role differentiation, such as stable recruitment or mentorship. What bots offer is reliability: they do not leave, reduce their involvement, or become unavailable.

8.3 其他解释8.3 Alternative Explanations

我们考虑四种替代解释，并根据三种观察到的模式对每种解释进行评估：集中在每个项目自己的采用月份的变化（图 1-2）、特定功能的能力-结果图（表 6）以及机器人强度关联的不对称衰减（表 7）。We consider four alternative explanations and evaluate each against three observed patterns: shifts concentrated at each project's own adoption month (Figures 1–2), the function-specific capability–outcome map (Table 6), and the asymmetric attenuation of bot-intensity associations (Table 7).

直接自动化。Direct automation.冲突可能会减少，因为机器人删除了容易发生冲突的工作：格式变得标准化，依赖项更新随着机器人拉取请求而到达，合并冲突会自动解决。该解释预测了在能力控制后仍然存在的与级联的直接机器人强度关联，并且它没有给出人类贡献者重叠增加的理由（表 3）或能力-结果图是特定于功能的（表 6）。衰减结果（表7）为直接测试，自动化账户未通过。该帐户保留了组合版本：自动化可能会改变人类执行的任务，从而留下不易发生冲突的工作，我们的数据并不排除这一点。机械变体适用于独特性：其四个组成部分（评论模式距离）之一包含机器人活动通过构造进入的动作混合分布（第 5.5 节），因此 et = 0 处的跳跃部分可以是定义性的。其他三个组件不受这种耦合的影响，组件级分解是适当的检查。Conflict might fall because bots remove conflict-prone work: formatting becomes standardized, dependency updates arrive as bot pull requests, merge conflicts are resolved automatically. This account predicts a direct bot-intensity association with cascades that remains after capability controls, and it gives no reason for human contributor overlap to rise (Table 3) or for the capability–outcome map to be function-specific (Table 6). The attenuation result (Table 7) is the direct test, and the automation account fails it. The account retains a compositional version: automation may change which tasks humans perform, leaving work that is less prone to collision, and our data do not exclude this. A mechanical variant applies to distinctiveness: one of its four components (review-pattern distance) incorporates action-mix distributions that bot activity enters by construction (Section 5.5), so part of the jump at et = 0 could be definitional. The other three components are free of this coupling, and a componentlevel decomposition is the appropriate check.

成熟和生存。Maturation and survival.随着时间的推移，项目可能会趋于稳定。项目固定效应消除了稳定的差异，横截面模型控制了规模和活动，但成熟是项目内的过程，固定效应无法吸收。时机是反对它的主要证据：成熟预示着平稳的轨迹，而不是因项目而异的采用月份的中断（图 1）。一个相关的问题是生存率：该设计需要在收养后进行 24 个月的观察，因此生存率样本条件以及不断增加的重复参与度可以部分反映这一点。即使在活动低迷的月份也定义了这些指标，这限制了机械通胀，但需要进行匹配比较才能解决这一问题。Projects may stabilize as they age. Project fixed effects remove stable differences, and the cross-sectional models control for size and activity, but maturation is a within-project process that fixed effects do not absorb. Timing is the main evidence against it: maturation predicts smooth trajectories, not breaks at adoption months that vary across projects (Figure 1). A related concern is survivorship: the design requires 24 post-adoption months of observation, so the sample conditions on survival, and rising repeated engagement could partly reflect this. The indicators are defined even in low-activity months, which limits mechanical inflation, but matched comparisons are needed to resolve the concern.

平台和日历发生变化。Platform and calendar change.GitHub 的基础设施在整个窗口期内发生了变化：Dependabot 于 2019 年成为原生，而 2022-2024 年队列的采用后窗口与生成人工智能时期重叠。然而，平台变更是日历时间事件，采用月份分散在十年间（第 4 节），因此没有共同的日历事件可以产生与每个项目自己的采用月份一致的中断。剩下的问题是针对具体队列的——2020 年的大队列可能会主导汇总估计——而队列分层估计就是检验（第 8.5 节）。GitHub's infrastructure changed throughout the window: Dependabot became native in 2019, and the 2022–2024 cohort's post-adoption windows overlap the generative-AI period. Platform changes are calendar-time events, however, and adoption months are dispersed across a decade (Section 4), so no common calendar event can produce breaks aligned to each project's own adoption month. The remaining concern is cohort-specific — the large 2020 cohort could dominate pooled estimates — and cohort-stratified estimation is the check (Section 8.5).

每个选项都解释了部分证据。没有一个考虑到每个项目自己的采用月份的休息时间、特定功能的能力-结果图和不对称衰减的组合。制度解释是对这种组合最简洁的解释。它仍然是一种解释，我们的主张也相应地陈述。Each alternative accounts for part of the evidence. None accounts for the combination of breaks timed to each project's own adoption month, a function-specific capability–outcome map, and asymmetric attenuation. The institutional interpretation is the most parsimonious account of that combination. It remains an interpretation, and our claims are stated accordingly.

8.4 能力-成果图及其范围8.4 The Capability–Outcome Map and Its Scope

五指标矩阵（表 6）是该框架最有力的确认。每个指标占据一个不同的单元格：重复的参与预示着级联的减少；社会记忆预示着更大的独特性；角色分化预测了两者，因为它分离了任务领域并增加了评估视角。机器人端指标遵循其痕迹的功能，而不是其作者的性质：寻址行为类似于协调指标，链接类似于差异指标。The five-indicator matrix (Table 6) is the framework's strongest confirmation. Each indicator occupies a distinct cell: repeated engagement predicts fewer cascades; social memory predicts greater distinctiveness; role differentiation predicts both, because it separates task domains and multiplies evaluative perspectives. The bot-side indicators follow the function of their traces, not the nature of their author: addressing behaves like the coordination indicators, chaining like the differentiation indicators.

在这个群体中，重要的是参与者的痕迹做了什么，而不是参与者是什么。这个结论的范围必须准确地陈述，因为它是本文最有力的主张，也是最容易被过度扩展的。研究的代理是基于规则的、模板驱动的、特定领域的和非会话的；该设置是开源软件，具有异常完整的公共记录；所显示的等价性是在跟踪功能的水平上，而不是在行为水平上——智能体群体的行为方式明显非人类（Goyal et al. 2026）。泛化到整个人工智能代理是一个猜想，该框架具体说明了它所依赖的内容：自动化参与者的制度贡献应该随着其痕迹的形式（可寻址性、稳定性、链接）而不是其智能而增加。更强大的智能体使这个猜想变得可测试（第 8.6 节）。Within this population, what mattered was what a participant's traces do, not what the participant is. The scope of this conclusion must be stated precisely, because it is the paper's strongest claim and the most easily overextended. The agents studied are rule-based, template-driven, domainspecific, and non-conversational; the setting is open-source software, with its unusually complete public record; and the equivalence shown is at the level of trace function, not behavior — agent populations left to themselves behave in distinctly non-human ways (Goyal et al. 2026). Generalization to AI agents at large is a conjecture, and the framework specifies what it depends on: the institutional contribution of an automated participant should increase with the form of its traces — addressability, stability, chaining — rather than with its intelligence. More capable agents make this conjecture testable (Section 8.6).

理论上的结果仍然是真实的。团队协调的认知解释（Wegner 1987；Lewis 2003）不会预测如此简单的参与者可能与加强协调有关。我们的结果表明，它们可以通过与人机功能相同的功能渠道来实现。这并不表明记录取代了思想；而是记录取代了思想。最有可能的是，机器人追踪物质是因为人类认知处理它们。它确实表明，可观察记录是一个可以在不解决认知问题的情况下进行制度分析的水平（Esposito 2022；Knorr Cetina 1997），并且在这个水平上，没有头脑的参与者可以用与有头脑的参与者相同的条件来衡量。The theoretical consequence is nonetheless real. Cognitive accounts of team coordination (Wegner 1987; Lewis 2003) would not predict that participants this simple could be associated with strengthened coordination. Our results show that they can be, through the same functional channels as human-side capabilities. This does not show that records replace minds; most plausibly, bot traces matter because human cognition processes them. It does show that the observable record is a level at which institutional analysis can proceed without settling the cognitive question (Esposito 2022; Knorr Cetina 1997), and at which participants without minds can be measured on the same terms as participants with them.

第一，身份识别。First, identification.该设计缺乏未经处理的对照组；采用似乎是内生于维护负担、贡献者超载、治理变化和成熟度的；与采用捆绑在一起的任何内容都会被吸收到采用月份的估计中。优先分析是交错的双重差异，以后来处理的项目作为对照（Callaway 和 Sant'Anna 2021；Sun 和 Abraham 2021；Goodman-Bacon 2021）、匹配的非采用者比较、安慰剂采用日期、综合对照和队列分层估计。在那之前，我们的主张被表述为精确定时的关联。The design lacks an untreated comparison group; adoption is plausibly endogenous to maintenance burden, contributor overload, governance change, and maturity; and anything bundled with adoption is absorbed into the adoption-month estimate. 第二，测量。Second, measurement.每个结构都是通过一个狭窄的行为指标来衡量的。对于社会记忆（设计参考机器人；第 5.2 节）和输出独特性（不是未经外部验证的创新；第 5.5 节），指标和构造之间的差距最大。针对分叉、星型和下游依赖项的验证是下一步的具体步骤。Every construct is measured by a narrow behavioral indicator. The gap between indicator and construct is largest for social memory (bot-referential by design; Section 5.2) and output distinctiveness (not innovation without external validation; Section 5.5). Validation against forks, stars, and downstream dependents is the concrete next step.

三是机构范围。Third, institutional scope.我们的证据涉及制度再生产的协调维度。机构还分配权力、赋予合法性并构建身份，机器人似乎也在那里很重要——参与治理的机器人做出分配权力的决策——但我们的指标并不衡量权力、合法性或制裁。相互预期和规范执行同样不会留下档案痕迹，只能用于实验工作。关于制度结构的主张应该被理解为关于其协调维度的主张。Our evidence concerns the coordinative dimension of institutional reproduction. Institutions also allocate power, confer legitimacy, and construct identity, and bots plausibly matter there as well — governance-participating bots make decisions that allocate authority — but our indicators do not measure authority, legitimacy, or sanctioning. Mutual anticipation and norm enforcement likewise leave no archival trace and are reserved for experimental work. Claims about the institutional fabric should be read as claims about its coordinative dimension.

第四，供应商的确定。Fourth, vendor determination.机器人端指标反映了所采用的机器人组合，而不是在项目学习中：一个项目在解决问题上得分很高，很大程度上是因为它采用了 Mergify，其模板提到了审阅者的名字。跟踪级别的解释不受影响，但因果轨迹是采用决策，而不是项目内的适应。The bot-side indicators reflect the adopted bot mix, not withinproject learning: a project scores high on addressing largely because it adopted Mergify, whose templates mention reviewers by name. The trace-level interpretation is unaffected, but the causal locus is the adoption decision, not within-project adaptation.

9. 结论9. Conclusion

我们着手对当自动化代理成为人类社区的常规参与者时所发生的情况进行裁决：流离失所，其中自动化消除了社会组织所依赖的互动；以及基础设施，其中稳定的、可寻址的参与者加强了这种互动。在 2,991 个开源项目中，证据有利于基础设施。在采用机器人的当月，重复参与和社会记忆指标不断上升，冲突级联下降，产出独特性不断上升；指标根据其痕迹的功能而不是提供痕迹的参与者来预测结果；机器人强度与冲突的关联完全由人为指标来解释，而与独特性的关联则不然。这些都是精确定时的关联，而不是确定的因果效应，而替代方案——自动化、成熟、平台变更、选择——每一个都保持残余形式。他们都没有解释这种组合。We set out to adjudicate between two accounts of what happens when an automated agent becomes a regular participant in a human community: displacement, in which automation removes the interaction on which social organization depends, and infrastructure, in which a stable, addressable participant reinforces it. Across 2,991 open-source projects, the evidence favors infrastructure. Bot adoption is followed, at the month of adoption, by rising indicators of repeated engagement and social memory, falling conflict cascades, and rising output distinctiveness; the indicators predict outcomes according to the function of their traces rather than the actor supplying them; and the bot-intensity association with conflict is fully accounted for by human-side indicators while its association with distinctiveness is not. These are precisely timed associations rather than identified causal effects, and the alternatives — automation, maturation, platform change, selection — each remain in residual form. None of them explains the combination.

这一贡献是一个主张，现在已经有了大规模的证据：自动化代理可以成为社区制度基础设施的一部分，因为它们的参与改变了协调复制所依赖的交互的共享记录。机器人就是契机；社会组织是机制。对于制度社会学来说，这意味着经典解释的协调机制在可观察的互动痕迹的水平上运作，在这个水平上，具有最低认知的参与者可以参与并以与人类参与者相同的条件进行衡量。对于人类与人工智能协作的研究来说，这意味着自动化队友最重要的属性可能不是其技术输出，而是其痕迹的形式：它是否足够稳定以供重新遇到，是否足够可寻址以供记忆，以及是否足够可预测以进行组织。The contribution is one claim, now with large-scale evidence: automated agents can become part of a community's institutional infrastructure because their participation alters the shared record of interaction on which coordinative reproduction draws. The bot is the occasion; the social organization is the mechanism. For the sociology of institutions, the implication is that the coordinative mechanisms of the classical account operate at the level of observable interactional traces, a level at which participants with minimal cognition can take part and be measured on the same terms as human participants. For the study of human–AI collaboration, the implication is that the most consequential property of an automated teammate may not be its technical output but the form of its traces: whether it is stable enough to be re-encountered, addressable enough to be remembered, and predictable enough to be organized around.
