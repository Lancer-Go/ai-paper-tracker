总结Summary

MCP 服务器协议-OKN (MCP Server Proto-OKN (mcp-proto-okn）是一个基于Python的模型上下文协议服务器，使AI助手能够通过自然语言发现、检查、查询和集成科学知识图谱。该服务器提供图路由、模式检查、SPARQL执行、本体扩展、多图查询和转录本生成，降低了生物医学和科学用户跨领域知识图谱分析的障碍。) is a Python-based Model Context Protocol server that enables AI assistants to discover, inspect, query and integrate scientific knowledge graphs through natural language. The server provides graph routing, schema inspection, SPARQL execution, ontology expansion, multi-graph querying, and transcript generation, lowering the barrier to cross-domain knowledge graph analysis for biomedical and scientific users.

简介INTRODUCTION

生物医学和科学数据的快速增长推动了知识图谱 (KG) 的发展，成为表示、集成和查询跨生物、临床和环境的异构信息的强大范例The rapid proliferation of biomedical and scientific data has driven the development of knowledge graphs (KGs) as a powerful paradigm for representing, integrating, and querying heterogeneous information across biological, clinical, and environmental

域。通过编码语义上相关的实体和关系，知识图谱能够跨学科边界进行推理，将基因与疾病、化学物质与毒理学结果、基因组扰动与临床表型联系起来。认识到这一潜力，美国国家科学基金会 (NSF) 在“构建原型开放知识网络 (Proto-OKN)”计划 (https://www.proto-okn.net/) 中投资了 2670 万美元，资助多学科项目，以构建一个开放、可公开访问的知识图谱互联网络，涵盖从生物医学和环境科学到刑事司法和供应链物流等领域。这些图通过 OKN Fabric (https://registry.okn.us/) 进行托管和互连，该结构提供了将许多单独的图链接到一个连贯的、可查询的网络中的技术基础设施。domains. By encoding semantically linked entities and relationships, KGs enable reasoning across disciplinary boundaries, connecting genes to diseases, chemicals to toxicological outcomes, and genomic perturbations to clinical phenotypes. Recognizing this potential, the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) invested $26.7 million in the “Building the Prototype Open Knowledge Network (Proto-OKN)” program (https://www.proto-okn.net/), funding multidisciplinary projects to construct an interconnected network of open, publicly accessible knowledge graphs spanning domains from biomedicine and environmental science to criminal justice and supply chain logistics. These graphs are hosted and interconnected through the OKN Fabric (https://registry.okn.us/), which provides the technical infrastructure to link many individual graphs into a coherent, queryable network.

尽管有这样的基础设施，对完整 Proto-OKN 生态系统的编程访问对于最终用户来说仍然是一个重大挑战。 30 多个组成知识图跨越不同的本体领域，共享很少的实体，并公开需要特定领域专业知识的异构 SPARQL 端点。这种碎片化限制了跨图的综合分析，特别是对于没有 SPARQL 专业知识的研究人员。Despite this infrastructure, programmatic access to the full Proto-OKN ecosystem remains a significant challenge for end users. The 30+ constituent knowledge graphs span different ontological domains, share few entities, and expose heterogeneous SPARQL endpoints requiring domain-specific expertise. This fragmentation limits integrative analyses across graphs, particularly for researchers without SPARQL expertise.

模型上下文协议 (MCP) (https://modelcontextprotocol.io/) 的出现是一种使基于大语言模型 (LLM) 的 AI 助手能够与外部工具和数据服务交互的标准，现在为解决这一访问障碍提供了令人信服的解决方案。通过将结构化查询功能作为 MCP 工具公开，知识图谱可以通过自然语言进行查询，从而使 AI 助手能够代表用户构建和执行 SPARQL 查询、解释结果并在单个对话会话中跨多个端点链接查询。The emergence of the Model Context Protocol (MCP) (https://modelcontextprotocol.io/), a standard for enabling large language model (LLM)-based AI assistants to interact with external tools and data services, now offers a compelling solution to this access barrier. By exposing structured query capabilities as MCP tools, knowledge graphs become queryable through natural language, enabling AI assistants to construct and execute SPARQL queries on behalf of users, interpret results, and chain queries across multiple endpoints in a single conversational session.

案例研究2：跨本体数据集发现的自动本体扩展Case study 2: Automatic ontology expansion for cross-ontology dataset discovery

第二个用例说明了查询生物医学知识图时反复出现的挑战：用户问题中的术语通常是高级概念（例如“心血管疾病”），而底层图中的数据集和记录则用从大型参考本体中提取的特定子类型标识符进行注释。对单个根 URI 的查询过滤会错过最相关的记录。A second use case illustrates a recurring challenge in querying biomedical knowledge graphs: terms in users’ questions are typically high-level concepts (e.g., "cardiovascular disease"), whereas datasets and records in the underlying graphs are annotated with specific subtype identifiers drawn from large reference ontologies. A query filtering on a single root URI misses most relevant records.

我们通过 NIAID 数据生态系统 (NDE) 演示了这一点，这是一个生物医学数据集的发现目录，其中每个数据集都标记有一个或多个 MONDO 疾病 URI（聊天记录：https://github.com/sbl-sdsc/mcp-protookn/blob/main/docs/examples/cardioangio-disease-ontology-expansion.md）。响应自然语言提示We demonstrate this with the NIAID Data Ecosystem (NDE), a discovery catalog of biomedical datasets in which each dataset is tagged with one or more MONDO disease URIs (Chat transcript: https://github.com/sbl-sdsc/mcp-protookn/blob/main/docs/examples/cardiovascular-disease-ontology-expansion.md). In response to the natural-language prompt“@NDE 对于心血管疾病，列出所研究的疾病以及涵盖每种疾病的数据集数量；按数据集数量显示前 20 名，”"@NDE For cardiovascular disease, list the diseases studied and the number of datasets covering each; show the top 20 by dataset count,"助手首先将术语“心血管疾病”映射到规范的 URI（MONDO:0004995，对应于“心血管疾病”）（图 1）。the assistant first mapped the term "cardiovascular disease" to a canonical URI (MONDO:0004995, corresponding to “cardiovascular disorder”) (Figure 1).

然后，一种简单的方法会针对 NDE 图发出单 URI SPARQL 查询。这种方法产生了 447 个数据集，直接用根级“心血管疾病”术语进行注释。较低的数字强调了大部分相关记录都没有用这个高级疾病术语进行注释。A naïve approach would then issue a single-URI SPARQL query against the NDE graph. This approach yielded 447 datasets annotated directly with the root-level “cardiovascular disorder” term. The low number underscores that the bulk of relevant records are not annotated with this high-level disease term.

相比之下，我们的本体感知助手首先自动从 UberGraph 获取 MONDO:0004995 的后代，并重写 SPARQL FILTER 子句以接受其中的任何一个 - 将一个输入 URI 扩展到心血管子树上的 1,592 个后代 URI。分 80 批查询扩展的 URI 集以遵守端点限制，并将扩展元数据返回给助手。In contrast, our ontology-aware assistant first automatically fetched descendants of MONDO:0004995 from UberGraph and rewrote the SPARQL FILTER clause to accept any of them — expanding one input URI into 1,592 descendant URIs across the cardiovascular subtree. 结论CONCLUSIONS

mcp-proto-okn演示模型上下文协议如何连接 OKN Fabric 上托管的碎片化科学知识图，并使研究人员无需查询语言专业知识即可访问它们。基于 OKN Fabric 构建，用于图形发现和 SPARQL 查询，demonstrates how the Model Context Protocol can connect fragmented scientific knowledge graphs hosted on the OKN Fabric and make them accessible to researchers without query-language expertise. Built on the OKN Fabric for graph discovery and SPARQL querying,mcp-proto-okn使 AI 助手能够将自然语言问题路由到相关的 Proto-OKN 知识图、检查模式、执行查询并综合结果。这里介绍的用例展示了它在跨图查询、本体扩展搜索以及与 PubMed 等补充 MCP 服务集成方面的实用性。这些功能共同降低了生物医学及其他领域综合发现的障碍。enables AI assistants to route natural-language questions to relevant Proto-OKN knowledge graphs, inspect schemas, execute queries, and synthesize results. The use cases presented here showcase its utility for cross-graph queries, ontology-expanded search, and integration with complementary MCP services such as PubMed. Together, these capabilities lower the barrier for integrative discovery across biomedicine and beyond.
