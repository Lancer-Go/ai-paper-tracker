摘要Abstract

由于对未修剪视频中隐藏时间动态的捕获有限，导致长序列中的基础不精确，文本驱动的视频时刻检索（VMR）仍然具有挑战性。传统方法依赖于自然语言查询 (NLQ) 或静态图像增强，忽略了运动序列，并且在基于 Transformer 的架构中面临着高昂的计算成本。现有的方法无法有效地整合字幕上下文并生成时间先验，因此我们提出了一种新颖的两阶段框架来增强时间基础。在第一阶段，LLM引导的字幕匹配从视频字幕中识别相关文本线索，与查询融合以通过文本到视频模型生成辅助短视频，捕获隐式运动信息作为时间先验。在第二阶段，通过多模态控制的 Mamba 网络处理增强查询，通过视频引导门控扩展文本控制选择，以有效融合生成的先验和长序列，同时过滤噪声。我们的框架与基础检索模型无关，并且广泛适用于多模式 VMR。 TVR 基准的实验评估表明，它比最先进的方法有显着改进，包括减少计算开销和提高长序列接地的召回率。Text-driven video moment retrieval (VMR) remains challenging due to limited capture of hidden temporal dynamics in untrimmed videos, leading to imprecise grounding in long sequences. Traditional methods rely on natural language queries (NLQs) or static image augmentations, overlooking motion sequences and suffering from high computational costs in Transformer-based architectures. Existing approaches fail to integrate subtitle contexts and generated temporal priors effectively, we therefore propose a novel two-stage framework for enhanced temporal grounding. In the first stage, LLM-guided subtitle matching identifies relevant textual cues from video subtitles, fused with the query to generate auxiliary short videos via text-to-video models, capturing implicit motion information as temporal priors. In the second stage, augmented queries are processed through a multi-modal controlled Mamba network, extending text-controlled selection with video-guided gating for efficient fusion of generated priors and long sequences while filtering noise. Our framework is agnostic to base retrieval models and widely applicable for multimodal VMR. Experimental evaluations on the TVR benchmark demonstrate significant improvements over state-of-the-art methods, including reduced computational overhead and higher recall in long-sequence grounding.
