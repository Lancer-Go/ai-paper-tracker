摘要Abstract

随着智能恶意软件检测系统部署在不断发展的移动生态系统中，良性和恶意应用程序的结构和行为都会发生变化，从而导致非平稳数据分布，从而挑战长期模型的可靠性。与此同时，对抗性扰动暴露了基于机器学习的检测器的其他漏洞。尽管独立地对概念漂移和对抗鲁棒性进行了大量工作，但它们在自适应智能系统中的相互作用仍然没有得到充分的表征。As intelligent malware detection systems are deployed in evolving mobile ecosystems, both benign and malicious applications change in structure and behavior, inducing non-stationary data distributions that challenge long-term model reliability. In parallel, adversarial perturbations expose additional vulnerabilities in machine learning–based detectors. Despite extensive work on concept drift and adversarial robustness independently, their interaction in adaptive intelligent systems remains insufficiently characterized.

1 简介Introduction

在现实环境中部署基于机器学习的恶意软件检测器使它们暴露于不断变化的数据分布，违反了传统离线监督学习的平稳性假设The deployment of machine learning–based malware detectors in real-world environments exposes them to evolving data distributions, violating the stationarity assumption underlying conventional offline supervised learning[1, 2, 3, 4, 5]。在安全数据流上运行的智能决策系统必须应对固有的非平稳性，包括概念漂移，其中输入和标签之间的条件关系随着新威胁、平台和用户行为的出现而变化. Intelligent decision systems operating on security data streams must contend with inherent non-stationarity, including concept drift, where the conditional relationship between inputs and labels changes as new threats, platforms, and user behaviors emerge[6, 7]。当这种漂移没有得到明确的监控和调节时，最初实现强大性能的模型可能会随着时间的推移而退化，从而增加漏检率并损害长期智能系统的可靠性。. When such drift is not explicitly monitored and accommodated, models that initially achieve strong performance may degrade over time, increasing missed detections and undermining the reliability of long-lived intelligent systems.

Android 为研究分布转移下的学习提供了具有代表性和操作相关性的设置。作为占主导地位的移动操作系统和主要恶意软件目标Android provides a representative and operationally relevant setting for studying learning under distribution shift. As the dominant mobile operating system and a primary malware target[8, 9, 8, 10, 11, 12]，它推动了对传统和深度学习架构中使用静态、动态和混合特征表示的数据驱动检测的广泛研究, it has motivated extensive research on data-driven detection using static, dynamic, and hybrid feature representations across both traditional and deep learning architectures[13, 14, 15]。尽管许多研究报告了策划基准的高精度，但大多数评估隐含地假设训练和测试样本是从相同的时间分布中抽取的。这种假设限制了对长期系统行为的洞察以及在不断变化的环境中部署的智能探测器的鲁棒性。. Although many studies report high accuracy on curated benchmarks, most evaluations implicitly assume that training and test samples are drawn from the same temporal distribution. This assumption limits insight into long-term system behavior and the robustness of intelligent detectors deployed in evolving environments.

最近的工作已经开始通过检查 Android 恶意软件检测和相关领域的时间效应来重新审视这种平稳性假设。使用时间索引集合（例如 KronoDroid 和其他纵向数据集）在数据集和行为级别上表征概念漂移Recent work has begun to revisit this stationarity assumption by examining temporal effects in Android malware detection and related domains. Concept drift has been characterized at both dataset and behavioral levels using time-indexed collections such as KronoDroid and other longitudinal datasets[16, 17, 18]。这些研究表明，当在未来的时间窗口或不断变化的环境中评估模型时，性能可能会下降，并且不正确的时间分区可能会引入泄漏和乐观偏差. These studies demonstrate that performance can decline when models are evaluated on future time windows or across changing environments, and that improper temporal partitioning may introduce leakage and optimistic bias[19, 20]。然而，先前的分析主要强调清洁数据性能和缓解策略，而没有从鲁棒智能系统的角度充分探讨时间分布变化与对抗性脆弱性之间的关系。. However, prior analyses largely emphasize clean-data performance and mitigation strategies, leaving the relationship between temporal distribution shift and adversarial vulnerability insufficiently explored from the perspective of robust intelligent systems.

与此同时，对抗性机器学习表明，精心设计的小扰动可能会导致各种现代模型的错误分类In parallel, adversarial machine learning has shown that small, carefully crafted perturbations can induce misclassification across a wide range of modern models[21, 22, 23]。在恶意软件检测中，可以通过特征空间和问题空间操作来逃避分类器. Within malware detection, classifiers can be evaded through both feature-space and problem-space manipulations[24, 25]。然而，大多数对抗性评估都假设固定的基础数据分布，并且没有考虑随着数据生成过程随着时间的推移而变化，漏洞概况如何演变。. Yet most adversarial evaluations assume a fixed underlying data distribution and do not account for how vulnerability profiles may evolve as the data-generating process shifts over time.

因此，两条研究方向在很大程度上独立进展：①概念漂移下恶意软件检测器时间退化的研究；②静态训练条件下对抗性规避的分析。它们在随时间演变的部署场景中的交互仍然没有得到充分的表征。特别是，目前尚不清楚增加时间间隔是否会系统地放大对抗性漏洞，超出其对干净性能的影响，以及部分解决漂移的增量再训练策略是否也能提高鲁棒性或重塑有效攻击面。Consequently, two lines of research have progressed largely independently: ① the study of temporal degradation in malware detectors under concept drift and ② the analysis of adversarial evasion under static training conditions. Their interaction in time-evolving deployment scenarios remains insufficiently characterized. In particular, it is unclear whether increasing temporal separation systematically amplifies adversarial vulnerability beyond its impact on clean performance, and whether incremental retraining strategies that partially address drift also improve robustness or reshape the effective attack surface.

在本研究中，我们使用从跨越多年的时间顺序 Android 数据集中提取的静态和动态特征表示来研究这种交互，包括在模拟器和真实设备上的执行。我们评估了两种模拟现实学习场景的部署协议：（i）跨年度设置，其中在一年中训练的模型在随后的几年中进行评估而无需更新；（ii）扩展窗口设置，其中训练集随着时间的推移累积增长以合并新观察到的数据。我们的贡献如下。In this study, we investigate this interaction using static and dynamic feature representations extracted from a temporally ordered Android dataset spanning multiple years, including executions on both emulators and real devices. We evaluate two deployment protocols that emulate realistic learning scenarios: (i) a cross-year setting, where a model trained on a single year is evaluated on subsequent years without updates, and (ii) an expanding-window setting, where the training set grows cumulatively over time to incorporate newly observed data. 3 概念漂移Concept Drift

概念漂移是指联合分布的时间变化Concept drift refers to temporal changes in the joint distributionP​(x,y)P(x,y)特征数of featuresxx和标签and labelsyy [26]。在非平稳学习环境中，当在历史数据上训练的模型部署在未来的观察中时，这种分布演化可能会大大降低预测性能。从智能系统的角度来看，概念漂移挑战了学习决策边界的稳定性，并且需要考虑依赖时间的数据生成过程的评估框架。. In non-stationary learning environments, such distributional evolution can substantially degrade predictive performance when models trained on historical data are deployed on future observations. From the perspective of intelligent systems, concept drift challenges the stability of learned decision boundaries and necessitates evaluation frameworks that account for time-dependent data generation processes.

在 Android 恶意软件检测中，偏差可能来自多种来源，包括 API 演变、应用程序开发实践的变化、良性应用程序的行为变化以及恶意软件家族的出现、突变或消失。随着时间的推移，这些因素会引起特征分布的结构和行为变化，从而影响分类性能和稳健性。In Android malware detection, drift may arise from multiple sources, including API evolution, changes in application development practices, behavioral shifts in benign applications, and the emergence, mutation, or disappearance of malware families. These factors induce structural and behavioral changes in feature distributions over time, affecting both classification performance and robustness properties.

连接到对抗性设置。Connection to adversarial settings.在这项工作中，我们区分了自然时间漂移和测试时间对抗性扰动。自然漂移源于生态系统随时间的变化，例如平台演变、良性应用程序行为和恶意软件系列变化。对抗性扰动不会在我们的纵向数据集中产生漂移，相反，它们测试在早期分布上训练的模型在对未来数据进行评估时是否对受约束的特征变化更加敏感。因此，我们的研究研究了对抗性脆弱性在自然演变的时间分布下如何变化，而不是声称单个对抗性例子会导致真正的漂移。In this work, we distinguish natural temporal drift from test-time adversarial perturbations. Natural drift arises from ecosystem changes over time, such as platform evolution, benign application behavior, and malware-family changes. Adversarial perturbations do not create drift in our longitudinal dataset, instead, they test whether models trained on earlier distributions become more sensitive to constrained feature changes when evaluated on future data. Thus, our study examines how adversarial vulnerability changes under naturally evolving temporal distributions, rather than claiming that individual adversarial examples cause real drift.

8 结论Conclusion

我们使用 KronoDroid 数据集（2008-2020）对 Android 恶意软件检测中的时间漂移和对抗鲁棒性进行了纵向评估，目的是在基于传输的特征空间对抗评估下表征它们的关联。 在跨年度和扩展窗口设置中，训练和评估数据之间时间间隔的增加始终与相对于同年基线的干净预测性能下降和对抗准确性降低相关。在选定的配置中，早年训练的模型的攻击成功率也会增加，这表明在持续的分布式演化下脆弱性被放大。We presented a longitudinal evaluation of temporal drift and adversarial robustness in Android malware detection using the KronoDroid dataset (2008–2020), with the objective of characterizing their association under transfer-based feature-space adversarial evaluation. Across cross-year and expanding-window settings, increasing temporal separation between training and evaluation data is consistently associated with declines in clean predictive performance and reductions in adversarial accuracy relative to same-year baselines. In selected configurations, attack success rates also increase for models trained on earlier years, indicating amplified vulnerability under sustained distributional evolution.

扩展窗口再培训通过增加时间覆盖范围来减轻与跨年度部署相关的这些影响。然而，在持续的时间分离下，鲁棒性并没有完全恢复，可能是因为累积训练保留了旧的分布，而新出现的恶意软件和良性行为可能无法完全由历史静态和动态特征表示。因此，累积再训练会缩小但不能消除所评估的特征空间威胁模型内的鲁棒性损失。Expanding-window retraining mitigates these effects relative to cross-year deployment by increasing temporal coverage. However, robustness does not fully recover under continued temporal separation, likely because cumulative training retains older distributions, whereas newly emerging malware and benign behaviors may not be fully represented by historical static and dynamic features. Thus, cumulative retraining narrows, but does not eliminate, robustness loss within the evaluated feature-space threat model.

结果表明，在评估的 Android 恶意软件检测设置中，时间间隔与对抗准确性降低之间存在可测量的关联。 更广泛地说，他们强调在评估智能检测系统的稳健性时明确考虑非平稳数据分布的重要性。这些发现激发了未来在漂移监控机制、自适应再训练策略和针对不断变化的对抗性环境而设计的鲁棒性感知时间学习框架方面的工作。The results indicate a measurable association between temporal separation and reduced adversarial accuracy in the evaluated Android malware detection setting. More broadly, they underscore the importance of explicitly accounting for non-stationary data distributions when evaluating the robustness of intelligent detection systems. These findings motivate future work on drift monitoring mechanisms, adaptive retraining strategies, and robustness-aware temporal learning frameworks designed for evolving, adversarial environments.

除了 Android 设置之外，这些发现还强调了更广泛的系统级含义：智能检测系统的鲁棒性本质上是依赖于时间的。当模型部署在不断变化的环境中时，在固定假设下进行的评估可能会高估长期可靠性。因此，对抗鲁棒性不仅应根据扰动强度进行评估，还应根据训练和部署数据之间的时间分离进行评估。因此，为了对非平稳、对抗性领域中运行的智能安全系统进行实际评估，将漂移感知评估协议纳入鲁棒性评估框架可能是必要的。这些结论仅限于评估的 Android 数据集系列、静态和动态 KronoDroid 特征表示、选定的分类器系列以及基于约束传输的特征空间攻击。Beyond the Android setting, these findings highlight a broader systems-level implication: robustness in intelligent detection systems is inherently time-dependent. Evaluations conducted under stationary assumptions may overestimate long-term reliability when models are deployed in evolving environments. As a result, adversarial robustness should be assessed not only against perturbation strength but also against temporal separation between training and deployment data. Incorporating drift-aware evaluation protocols into robustness assessment frameworks may therefore be necessary for realistic appraisal of intelligent security systems operating in non-stationary, adversarial domains. These conclusions are limited to the evaluated Android dataset family, static and dynamic KronoDroid feature representations, selected classifier families, and constrained transfer-based feature-space attacks.
