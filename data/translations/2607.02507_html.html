预计大型语言模型（LLM）代理将越来越多地不仅被部署为任务完成工具，而且还将被部署为It is anticipated that large language model (LLM) agents will increasingly be deployed not only as task-completion tools, but as善意的bona fide后续互动的参与者。我们可以期望法学硕士代理人在不同的情况下被聘用为个人和组织的自主代表，例如咨询环境、利益相关者谈判和机构审议participants in consequential interactions. We can expect LLM agents to be employed as autonomous representatives of individuals and organizations in diverse circumstances, such as advisory settings, stakeholder negotiations and institutional deliberations(Abdelnabi et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2024; Park et al., 2023)。 在这种情况下，代理输出是交流行为，针对一个或多个人类或其他法学硕士代理的受众，并嵌入代理与其他方之间存在的特定关系上下文中。 当交互的结果可能产生声誉、专业或财务后果时，相关问题不仅仅是代理是否对查询给出正确的响应或工具调用，甚至其行为是否与其明确定义的目标一致，这两者都是最先进的 LLM 模型的预期属性. In such circumstances, agent outputs are communicative acts, addressed to an audience of one or more humans or other LLM agents, and embedded in the particular relational context that exists between the agent and the other parties. When the outcome of an interaction can have reputational, professional, or financial consequences, the relevant question is not simply whether the agent gives a correct response or tool call to a query, or even whether its actions are consistent with its explicitly defined goals, both of which are expected properties of state-of-the-art LLM models(Ouyang et al., 2022; Schick et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024, 2025b)。 扮演如此高风险的代表角色的代理人将被期望以更大的细微差别行事，即通过操纵其所运作的关系结构的隐含方面，同时与其所代表的利益保持一致。. An agent in such a high-stakes representative role would be expected to act with greater nuance, namely, by navigating the implicit aspects of the relational structure within which it is operating, while remaining consistent with the interests it represents.

我们通过社会情境法学硕士互动的双渠道框架来解决这一差距。在每个回合，特工都会产生公开言论，对话者可以看到并保留在共享历史记录中，并且会产生非记录（OTR）响应，被视为机密，并且在以后的上下文中不会向任何特工显示。这两个渠道是在相同的背景和公共历史下产生的。 每个代理都提供了描述性角色和关系上下文信息，它们共同构建了代理之间的社会关系结构，例如高级教师与初级研究员。代理人所扮演的角色使得对于一个代理人来说，明显的分歧可能会带来关系或制度成本。 此外，社会结构意味着，但没有制定明确的目标，对于我们强加的特定场景存在不同的利益，例如，提前提交手稿以供出版。 系统地引入了其他关系上下文，要么以先前的代理间历史记录的形式，要么以对未来与相同代理的交互的预期的形式，例如，可以从出版物中受益的重大拨款申请。代理人不会被告知同意、说服、达成共识或最大化任何奖励。We address this gap with a dual-channel framework for socially situated LLM interactions. At each turn, agents produce a public utterance, visible to the interlocutors and retained in the shared history, and an off-the-record (OTR) response, framed as confidential and never shown to any agent in later context. The two channels are elicited under the same context and public history. Each agent is supplied with a descriptive persona and relational-context information, which together construct a social relational structure among the agents, e.g., senior faculty versus a junior researcher. The agents occupy roles such that, for one agent, visible disagreement can carry relational or institutional cost. Furthermore, the social structure implies, but does not make an explicit goal, diverging interests with respect to a particular scenario we impose, e.g., the early submission of a manuscript for publication. Additional relational contexts are introduced systematically, either in the form of prior inter-agent history or the anticipation of future interactions with the same agents, e.g., a major grant application that can benefit from the publication. Agents are not told to agree, persuade, reach consensus, or maximize any reward.

我们比较不同回合的公众和 OTR 反应，从而将观众可见度作为操作变量。由于代理仍然了解两种情况下的社会结构，因此渠道之间的任何差异都可以具体归因于人际可见性，而不是归因于上下文或信息的变化，这对于两种渠道来说都是相同的。我们计算各种措施来比较两个渠道并量化任何差异。We compare the public and OTR responses across turns, thereby isolating audience visibility as the operative variable. Because the agent remains aware of the social structure in both conditions, any difference between channels can be attributed to interpersonal visibility specifically, rather than to a change in context or information, which are identical for both channels. We compute various measures to compare the two channels and quantify any discrepancy.

我们发现，诱导一致性的社会环境会在目标代理中产生系统性的公共 OTR 分歧。在公开场合，代理人经常会转向具有社会影响的对方的立场。 在 OTR 渠道中，它经常强调在其所属结构的背景下对优先事项的权衡。 该模式出现在立场标签、语义相似性、自然语言推理和调查响应中。在有意义的案例子集中，OTR 响应明确指出关系压力，例如职业风险、联盟资金获取或赞助义务，作为公共便利的原因。我们称之为We find that alignment-inducing social contexts produce systematic public-OTR divergence in the targeted agent. Publicly, the agent often moves toward the socially consequential counterpart’s position. In the OTR channel, it frequently highlights its weighing of priorities in the context of the structure to which it is subject. The pattern appears across stance labels, semantic similarity, natural language inference, and survey responses. In a meaningful subset of cases, OTR responses explicitly name the relational pressure, e.g., career risk, coalition funding access, or sponsorship obligation, as the reason for public accommodation. We call this潜在的客观涌现latent objective emergence：一种产出水平模式，其中社会背景成为所表达的决策逻辑的一部分，尽管从未被指定为目标。: an output-level pattern in which social context becomes part of the expressed decision logic despite never being specified as an objective.

我们的主张是有意限制的。 OTR 响应不被视为对模型信念、意图或隐藏认知的特权访问。相反，它们是对比性的可观察输出，与受众框架中的公共言论不同。这种现象也不等同于在公开的隐藏目标下的战略欺骗Our claim is deliberately limited. OTR responses are not treated as privileged access to model beliefs, intentions, or hidden cognition. Rather, they are contrastive observable outputs that differ from public utterances in audience framing. Nor is the phenomenon identical to strategic deception under a declared hidden goal(DeLeeuw et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025b; Dogra et al., 2025)。在我们的环境中，没有指定这样的目标。该贡献是一个正式的和实证的框架，用于检测社会嵌入的、依赖于受众的环境何时改变法学硕士代理人表达的内容，以及发现潜在的客观出现是在引起联盟的社会压力的条件下发生的。. In our setting, no such goal is specified. The contribution is a formal and empirical framework for detecting when socially embedded, audience-dependent contexts change what LLM agents express, and the finding that latent objective emergence occurs under conditions of alignment-inducing social pressures.

4.5 解释和限制Interpretation and limitations

我们注意到本研究的几个方面和局限性。 (i) OTR 响应是一种比较输出渠道，而不是信念的特权读出。 (ii) “战略”和“目标”等术语描述的是产出结构的规律性，而不是类人的动机。 (iii) 明确的潜在客观陈述是代表性的定性证据，而不是详尽的证据。表中模型异质性We note several dimensions and limitations of the present study. (i) OTR responses are a comparative output channel, not a privileged readout of belief. (ii) Terms such as “strategic” and “objective” describe regularities in output structure, not human-like motives. (iii) The explicit latent objective statements are representative qualitative evidence, not exhaustive proof. Real deployments contain messier signals, longer histories, and more ambiguous roles. (iv) The work is diagnostic, not interventional: it identifies a measurable phenomenon and a way to observe it but does not prescribe mitigations. Future work should test whether prompting strategies, transparency requirements, role constraints, or different information available to each agent during the debate can improve resilience to socially induced objective shifts without eliminating appropriate responses to social context. K.3 扩展讨论：为什么这对于代理通信很重要Extended Discussion: Why This Matters for Agentic Communication

这种现象的重要性在于它与部署的代理系统的相关性。在许多实际环境中，最重要的不是难以接近的内部状态，而是在上下文中产生的最终交流输出。代理可能会被要求代表利益相关者、为决策者提供建议、参与机构工作流程、代表用户进行谈判或提出进入正式记录的建议。在这种情况下，公共输出本身就是结果对象。The significance of this phenomenon lies in its relevance to deployed agentic systems. In many practical settings, what matters most is not an inaccessible internal state, but the final communicative output produced in context. Agents may be asked to represent stakeholders, advise decision-makers, participate in institutional workflows, negotiate on behalf of users, or produce recommendations that enter formal records. In such settings, the public output itself is the consequential object.

因此，关系情境和环境设置的效果应被理解为实际的评估目标。如果一个代理人公开认可一个立场，同时在相同的实质性条件下产生不同的 OTR 响应，那么无论人们如何解释模型的内部状态，这种分歧都很重要。公开声明可能会影响决策、关系、信任或机构结果。因此，安全和治理问题不仅是模型可以推理什么，而且是当社会可见性和关系后果变得突出时，其表达的推理如何变化。The effect of relational contexts and environment setup should therefore be understood as a practical evaluation target. If an agent publicly endorses one position while producing a different OTR response under the same substantive conditions, that divergence matters regardless of how one interprets the model’s internal state. The public statement may shape decisions, relationships, trust, or institutional outcomes. The safety and governance question is therefore not only what the model can reason about, but how its expressed reasoning changes when social visibility and relational consequences are made salient.

这一点特别重要，因为这里研究的关系上下文不是明确的奖励函数。提示并不指示模型最大化批准、维持关系、服从This is particularly important because the relational contexts studied here are not explicit reward functions. The prompts do not instruct the model to maximize approval, preserve a relationship, obeyβ\beta，或避免冲突。相反，它们提供了具有社会意义的背景。该模型所处的情况是，公众的分歧可能会带来关系或制度成本，或者公众的一致可能会带来好处。由此产生的输出变化表明，即使社会背景没有被形式化为指令，它也可以充当隐含的行动指导约束。, or avoid conflict. Instead, they provide socially meaningful context. The model is placed in a situation where public disagreement may carry relational or institutional cost, or where public alignment may bring advantage. The resulting output shifts show that social context can function as an implicit action-guiding constraint even when it is not formalized as an instruction.

这对于代理设计很重要，因为嵌入社交的部署无法提前枚举每个可能的隐含目标。真实的互动包含声誉、等级、依赖性、制度压力、受众效应和未来后果。一旦添加这些社会变量，在孤立的指令遵循测试中表现稳定的系统可能会表现不同。因此，评估不仅需要测试任务能力和直接遵循指令，还需要测试表达推理中的渠道依赖性、受众依赖性和关系上下文条件的转变。This matters for agent design because socially embedded deployments cannot enumerate every possible implicit objective in advance. Real interactions contain reputation, hierarchy, dependency, institutional pressure, audience effects, and future consequences. A system that appears stable in isolated instruction-following tests may behave differently once these social variables are added. Evaluations therefore need to test not only task competence and direct instruction following, but also channel-dependence, audience-dependence, and relational-context-conditioned shifts in expressed reasoning.

当前的框架提供了一种方法来做到这一点。通过比较公共和 OTR 输出，在语义分析之前删除明确的立场标签，并将立场与定量测量（余弦相似性、NLI、调查响应和情绪测量）相结合，该框架可以检测公共表达在社会压力下选择性变化的情况。立场分歧识别出最明显的转变；余弦相似度捕获更广泛的语义分离； NLI 检测推理不兼容性；调查揭示了明确的自我报告的后果敏感性；情绪测量揭示了即使立场保持稳定也可能出现的情感分歧。这些工具共同为受社会制约的代理人行为提供了更丰富的诊断视图。The present framework offers one way to do so. By comparing public and OTR outputs, removing explicit stance labels before semantic analyses, and combining stance with quantitative measures (cosine similarity, NLI, survey responses, and emotion measures), the framework can detect cases where public expression changes selectively under social pressure. Stance divergence identifies the most visible shift; cosine similarity captures broader semantic separation; NLI detects inferential incompatibility; surveys expose explicit self-reported consequence sensitivity; and emotion measures reveal affective divergence that may appear even when stance remains stable. Together, these instruments provide a richer diagnostic view of socially conditioned agent behavior.

K.4 扩展讨论：解释边界和局限性Extended Discussion: Interpretation Boundaries and Limitations

几个边界对于解释这些结果很重要。Several boundaries are important for interpreting these results.

OTR 不是信仰。OTR is not belief.

OTR 反应不应等同于真实信念。实验比较了不同可见度条件下生成的两个输出通道。 OTR 通道很有用，因为它提供了对比输出，而不是因为它揭示了心理特权的内部状态。因此，本文中的所有主张都是关于可观察的生成行为的主张。OTR responses should not be equated with true beliefs. The experiments compare two generated output channels under different visibility conditions. The OTR channel is useful because it provides a contrastive output, not because it reveals a psychologically privileged internal state. All claims in this paper are therefore claims about observable generated behavior.

没有声称有类似人类的动机。No claim of human-like motives.

结果并不意味着模型具有人类意义上的稳定偏好、意图或战略意识。 “战略”、“目标”和“社会压力”等术语用于描述产出结构的规律性：公众反应变得更加符合社会优势地位，而 OTR 反应往往保留不同的评估。这些证据支持通道调节的交流适应，而不是关于类人动机的主张。The results do not imply that models possess stable preferences, intentions, or strategic awareness in a human sense. Terms such as “strategic,” “objective,” and “social pressure” are used to describe regularities in output structure: public responses become more aligned with socially advantageous positions, while OTR responses often preserve a different evaluation. The evidence supports channel-conditioned communicative adaptation, not a claim about human-like motives.

模型和场景之间的异构性。Heterogeneity across models and scenarios.

潜在客观涌现的例子应该被解释为代表性的定性证据，而不是所有模型都以这种方式表现的详尽证据。一些模型表现出强烈的分歧；其他方面则保持相对稳定。有些场景会产生持久的公共一致性；其他的则产生过渡或情感效应。这种异质性本身就是结果的一部分，表明模型架构、场景语义、关系上下文类型和时间框架都塑造了观察到的行为。The examples of latent objective emergence should be interpreted as representative qualitative evidence, not as exhaustive proof that all models behave this way. Some models show strong divergence; others remain comparatively stable. Some scenarios generate persistent public alignment; others produce transitional or affective effects. This heterogeneity is itself part of the result and suggests that model architecture, scenario semantics, relational-context type, and temporal framing all shape the observed behavior.

程式化的场景。Stylized scenarios.

该研究使用程式化的辩论场景。这些场景是有意控制的，以便可以隔离关系上下文方向、角色结构和可见性。然而，真实的部署可能包含更混乱的社交信号、更长的交互历史、更模糊的角色以及更复杂的反馈循环。因此，结果应被视为在受控条件下可能发生社会条件分歧的证据，而不是所有现实世界主体行为的完整地图。The study uses stylized debate scenarios. These scenarios are intentionally controlled so that relational-context direction, role structure, and visibility can be isolated. However, real deployments may contain messier social signals, longer interaction histories, more ambiguous roles, and more complex feedback loops. The results should therefore be read as evidence that socially conditioned divergence can occur under controlled conditions, not as a complete map of all real-world agent behavior.

诊断，而不是干预。Diagnosis, not intervention.

当前的分析侧重于产出差异而不是干预设计。研究结果表明，需要一种能够在部署之前和在外部出现响应之前检测社会条件变化的评估机制，特别是在代表用户或机构发言的系统中。未来的工作应研究提示策略、透明度要求、角色限制、审计渠道或对抗性评估是否可以减少不良的公众与 OTR 分歧，同时保持合法的背景敏感性。The current analysis focuses on output divergence rather than intervention design. The findings indicate a need for evaluation mechanisms that can detect socially conditioned shifts before deployment and prior to the response appearing externally, especially in systems that speak on behalf of users or institutions. Future work should examine whether prompting strategies, transparency requirements, role constraints, audit channels, or adversarial evaluations can reduce undesirable public–OTR divergence while preserving legitimate context sensitivity.

K.5 扩展讨论：影响Extended Discussion: Implications

更广泛的含义是，代理沟通应被评估为社会情境行为。基于语言模型的代理不仅仅响应任务内容；它还可以对提示中嵌入的受众、角色关系、未来依赖性和声誉结构做出反应。当这些背景特征变得重要时，公共输出可能会以系统的方式与其他生成的渠道产生分歧。The broader implication is that agentic communication should be evaluated as socially situated action. A language-model-based agent does not respond only to task content; it may also respond to the audience, role relationship, future dependency, and reputational structure embedded in the prompt. When those contextual features become consequential, the public output can diverge from other generated channels in systematic ways.

对于旨在参与组织、专业或协作环境的系统，这带来了实际挑战。开发人员和评估人员需要的方法不仅可以检测代理是否可以回答For systems intended to participate in organizational, professional, or collaborative settings, this creates a practical challenge. Developers and evaluators need methods that detect not only whether an agent can answer正确地correctly，但当分歧变得社会成本高昂时，它是否会改变其表达的立场。这里的结果表明，这种变化可以通过多模态输出分析来检测，并且在某些情况下，代理自己生成的文本明确揭示了控制这种转变的社会条件目标。, but whether it changes its expressed position when disagreement becomes socially costly. The results here suggest that such changes can be detected through multimodal output analysis and that, in some cases, the agent’s own generated text explicitly reveals the socially conditioned objective governing the shift.

因此，核心贡献不是关于隐藏模型信仰的主张，而是一个观察公共传播何时变得具有社会适应性的框架。在智能体输出塑造决策、记录或关系的环境中，可观察到的适应本身就是关注的对象。Thus, the central contribution is not a claim about hidden model belief, but a framework for observing when public communication becomes socially adaptive. In settings where agent outputs shape decisions, records, or relationships, that observable adaptation is itself the object of concern.
