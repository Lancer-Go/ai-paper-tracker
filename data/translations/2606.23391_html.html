摘要Abstract

时间序列预测是一项基本的机器学习任务。最近的工作为此目的探索了大型语言模型（LLM），因为它们具有强大的泛化性、模式识别和零样本或少样本能力。尽管法学硕士适合长上下文学习，但它们在多模式环境中面临挑战：它们缺乏针对非文本数据的校准概率模型，并且很难对齐异构表示。为了解决这些问题，我们提出了一个新的框架Time series forecasting is a fundamental machine learning task. Recent work has explored Large Language Models (LLMs) for this purpose due to their strong generalization, pattern recognition, and zero-shot or few‑shot capabilities. Despite their suitability for long‑context learning, LLMs face challenges in multimodal settings: they lack calibrated probabilistic modeling for non‑text data and struggle to align heterogeneous representations. To address these issues, we propose a new framework扩散法学硕士Diffusion‑LLM它将条件扩散模型集成到基于 LLM 的预测管道中。这种联合设计能够学习未来数据的条件分布，同时改善共享潜在空间中的语义对齐。我们评估that integrates a conditional diffusion model into an LLM-based forecasting pipeline. This joint design enables learning the conditional distribution of future data while improving semantic alignment in a shared latent space. We evaluate扩散法学硕士Diffusion‑LLM基于六个长期预测基准，包括 ETT、Weather 和 ECL。我们的方法始终优于现有的基于 LLM 的基线，在超长期和小样本预测方面取得了显着的成果，并证明了分布感知正则化对于增强时间序列 LLM 的鲁棒性和泛化性的价值。on six long-term forecasting benchmarks, including ETT, Weather, and ECL. Our method consistently outperforms existing LLM-based baseline, achieving notable gains in ultra‑long‑term and few‑shot forecasting and demonstrating the value of distribution-aware regularization for enhancing robustness and generalization in time series LLMs.

1 简介Introduction

时间序列预测在能源系统等领域至关重要Time series forecasting is essential in domains such as energy systems[29, 3], 医疗保健, healthcare[16], 气候科学, climate science[11]和供应链管理, and supply chain management[18]。许多应用，包括能源需求规划、气候建模和电池寿命预测. Many applications, including energy demand planning, climate modeling, and battery lifetime prediction[13, 30]需要超长期预测，通常根据有限的历史数据，提前数千或更多步骤。require ultra-long-term forecasts extending thousands or more steps ahead, often from limited historical data.

法学硕士最近因其强大的泛化能力、模式识别能力和零/少样本能力而成为有前途的预测者LLMs have recently emerged as promising forecasters due to their strong generalization, pattern recognition, and zero‑/few‑shot abilities[6]。然而，将预训练的法学硕士应用于时间序列仍然具有挑战性。它们的表示是针对语言中的语义结构而不是时间动态进行调整的，这使得跨模态对齐变得困难，并导致性能下降和潜在的多模态幻觉. However, applying pretrained LLMs to time series remains challenging. Their representations are tuned for semantic structure in language, not temporal dynamics, making cross‑modal alignment difficult and leading to degraded performance and potential multimodal hallucinations[24]。此外，受过 MSE 训练的 LLM 预测者往往会回归均值，无法捕获可能未来的完整分布，尤其是对于不规则或噪声序列. Moreover, MSE‑trained LLM forecasters tend to regress toward the mean and fail to capture the full distribution of possible futures, especially for irregular or noisy series[27]。随着世代的进步，人们的注意力越来越集中在最近的预测上，从而降低了全球背景意识并放大了不确定性的低估. As generation progresses, attention increasingly concentrates on recent predictions, reducing global context awareness and amplifying uncertainty underestimation[23, 8].

2.2 时间序列预测中的 DDPM：DDPM in Time Series Forecasting:

基于 DDPM 的预测器通常将扩散模型与自回归主干配对。时间梯度DDPM-based forecasters generally pair diffusion models with autoregressive backbones. TimeGrad[20]用噪声破坏未来值，并以 RNN 编码的回溯窗口为条件对它们进行去噪。分数等级corrupts future values with noise and denoises them conditioned on RNN‑encoded lookback windows. ScoreGrad[37]遵循类似的特征提取流程，但采用基于 SDE 的条件分数匹配。与这些模型不同，我们不使用 DDPM 作为独立的生成预测器。相反，我们利用 DDPM 作为辅助学习器，规范基于 LLM 的预测器，提高鲁棒性和不确定性建模，而无需取代 LLM 的预测作用。follows a similar feature extraction pipeline but employs conditional SDE-based score matching. Unlike these models, we do not use DDPMs as stand‑alone generative forecasters. Instead, we leverage DDPMs as auxiliary learners that regularize LLM-based predictors, improving robustness and uncertainty modeling without replacing the LLM’s forecasting role.

0.A.2 评估指标Evaluation Metrics

为了评估模型在时间序列预测方面的性能，我们采用两个标准回归指标：To evaluate model performance on time series forecasting, we adopt two standard regression metrics:

  • •

    均方误差 (MSE)Mean Squared Error (MSE)：该指标计算预测值和真实值之间的平方差的平均值：: This metric computes the average of the squared differences between the predicted values and the ground truth:

    MSE=1N​∑i=1N(yi−y^i)2\text{MSE}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(y_{i}-\hat{y}_{i})^{2}

    较低的 MSE 表示更好的性能，并且由于平方项而对较大的错误进行更严厉的惩罚。A lower MSE indicates better performance and penalizes larger errors more heavily due to the squared term.

  • •

    平均绝对误差 (MAE)Mean Absolute Error (MAE)：MAE 衡量预测值与实际值之间的平均绝对差：: MAE measures the average absolute difference between predictions and actual values:

    MAE=1N​∑i=1N|yi−y^i|\text{MAE}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}|y_{i}-\hat{y}_{i}|

    与 MSE 相比，MAE 对异常值更稳健，并提供了预测准确性的直观度量。MAE is more robust to outliers compared to MSE and provides an intuitive measure of forecast accuracy.
