摘要Abstract

评估法学硕士生成并基于时间序列数据的自然语言解释的事实正确性仍然是一个开放的挑战。尽管现代模型可以生成数字信号的文本解释，但现有的自动评估方法是有限的：基于参考的相似性度量和一致性检查模型需要真实的解释，而传统的时间序列分析技术纯粹对数值进行操作，无法评估自由形式的文本推理。因此，目前还没有通用的方法可以在没有预定义参考或特定于任务的规则的情况下直接验证解释是否忠实于底层时间序列数据。在这项工作中，我们研究大型语言模型，作为无参考环境中时间序列解释的生成器和评估器。给定时间序列、问题和候选解释，评估者会根据模式识别、数字准确性和答案忠实度分配三元正确性标签，从而实现有原则的评分和解释比较。为了研究这一设置，我们构建了涵盖 7 种查询类型的 350 个时间序列案例的综合基准，每个案例都配有正确、部分正确和不正确的候选解释。我们评估四个任务的模型：解释生成、相对排名、独立评分和多异常检测。我们的结果显示生成和评估之间存在明显的不对称性：虽然解释生成高度依赖于模式，并且在某些查询类型上表现出系统性故障，季节性下降和波动性变化的准确度范围为 0.00 到 0.12，结构断裂的准确度为 0.94 到 0.96，但评估性能要稳定得多，即使模型自己生成的响应不正确，模型通常也能够正确对解释进行排名和评分。这些发现证明了基于数据的 LLM 评估对时间序列解释的可行性，并强调了此类模型作为时间序列域中基于数据的推理的可靠评估器的潜力。代码和数据可在以下位置获取：Evaluating the factual correctness of natural language explanations generated by LLMs and grounded in time-series data remains an open challenge. Although modern models can generate textual interpretations of numerical signals, existing automatic evaluation methods are limited: reference-based similarity metrics and consistency-checking models require ground-truth explanations, and traditional time-series analysis techniques operate purely on numerical values and cannot assess free-form textual reasoning. As a result, there is currently no general-purpose method for directly verifying whether an explanation is faithful to the underlying time-series data without predefined references or task-specific rules. In this work, we study large language models as both generators and evaluators of time-series explanations in a reference-free setting. Given a time series, a question, and a candidate explanation, the evaluator assigns a ternary correctness label based on pattern identification, numeric accuracy, and answer faithfulness, enabling principled scoring and comparison of explanations. To study this setting, we construct a synthetic benchmark of 350 time-series cases spanning seven query types, each paired with correct, partially correct, and incorrect candidate explanations. We evaluate models across four tasks: explanation generation, relative ranking, independent scoring, and multi-anomaly detection. Our results show a clear asymmetry between generation and evaluation: while explanation generation is highly pattern-dependent and exhibits systematic failures on certain query types, with accuracies ranging from 0.00 to 0.12 for Seasonal Drop and Volatility Shift, to 0.94 to 0.96 for Structural Break, evaluation performance is substantially more stable, with models often able to correctly rank and score explanations even when their own generated responses are incorrect. These findings demonstrate the feasibility of data-grounded LLM-based evaluation for time-series explanations and highlight the potential of such models as reliable evaluators of data-grounded reasoning in time series domain. Code and data are available at:https://github.com/Prxxthxm/LLM-Timeseries-Evaluation

1 简介Introduction

大语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地用于为结构化数据和数值数据生成自然语言解释，包括金融、医疗保健、气候科学和工业监测等领域的时间序列信号Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used to generate natural language explanations for structured and numerical data, including time series signals in domains such as finance, healthcare, climate science, and industrial monitoring(Zhang et al., 2024)。随着这些系统变得更有能力对数字模式产生流畅的文本解释，确保此类解释的事实正确性变得至关重要。在高风险环境中，即使是细微的数字误差或不受支持的统计声明也可能导致误导性结论和下游决策错误。因此，对基于数据的解释的可靠评估已成为一个重要的研究问题。. As these systems become more capable of producing fluent textual interpretations of numerical patterns, ensuring the factual correctness of such explanations becomes critical. In high stakes settings, even subtle numerical inaccuracies or unsupported statistical claims can lead to misleading conclusions and downstream decision errors. Consequently, reliable evaluation of data grounded explanations has emerged as an important research problem.

在这项工作中，我们研究评估时间序列解释的任务。输入包含时间序列、有关该序列的自然语言问题以及候选解释。目的是确定解释是否忠实于底层的数值数据、数值准确并正确回答问题。与标准文本生成评估不同，此设置的正确性取决于与结构化数字证据的一致性，而不是与参考答案的相似性。这使得这项任务本质上具有挑战性，因为解释可能在措辞和结构上有所不同，数字错误可能很微妙，并且必须联合评估多个推理方面（In this work, we study the task of evaluating time series explanations. The input consists of a time series, a natural language question about the series, and a candidate explanation. The objective is to determine whether the explanation is faithful to the underlying numerical data, numerically accurate, and correctly answers the question. Unlike standard text generation evaluation, correctness in this setting depends on alignment with structured numerical evidence rather than similarity to a reference answer. This makes the task inherently challenging since explanations may vary in wording and structure, numeric errors may be subtle, and multiple reasoning aspects must be jointly assessed (Bowman et al. (2015); Box et al. (2015)).

现有的评估方法仅限于此设置。基于参考的度量（例如 BLEU、ROUGE 和基于嵌入的相似性度量）评估生成的文本和真实参考之间的词汇或语义重叠（Existing evaluation approaches are limited for this setting. Reference based metrics such as BLEU, ROUGE, and embedding based similarity measures assess lexical or semantic overlap between generated text and ground truth references (Papineni et al. (2002); Lin (2004); Zhang et al. (2020)）。虽然对机器翻译和摘要等任务有效，但这些方法假设参考解释的可用性，并且纯粹基于文本相似性进行操作，而无需访问底层数字数据。自然语言推理模型类似地评估文本之间的蕴涵，但需要参考语句并且不直接验证数字基础（). While effective for tasks such as machine translation and summarization, these methods assume the availability of reference explanations and operate purely on textual similarity without access to the underlying numerical data. Natural language inference models similarly evaluate entailment between texts but require reference statements and do not directly verify numerical grounding (Bowman et al. (2015)）。相反，经典的时间序列分析方法直接对数值信号进行操作并支持趋势检测和异常识别等任务，但无法评估自由形式的文本推理（). Conversely, classical time series analysis methods operate directly on numerical signals and support tasks such as trend detection and anomaly identification, but cannot assess free form textual reasoning (Hamilton (1994); Box et al. (2015)）。最近基于法学硕士的评估者的工作表明，模型可以在结构化提示下近似人类的判断（). Recent work on LLM based evaluators shows that models can approximate human judgments under structured prompting (Fu et al. (2023); Liu et al. (2023); Zheng et al. (2023); Chiang et al. (2024)），但这些方法主要在文本到文本设置中运行，并且不解决基于结构化数字数据的评估。因此，目前还没有通用的、免费的参考框架来验证时间序列解释的事实正确性。), but these approaches primarily operate in text to text settings and do not address evaluation grounded in structured numerical data. As a result, there is currently no general purpose, reference free framework for verifying the factual correctness of time series explanations.

为了解决这一差距，我们采用了一个标题引导的、无参考的评估框架，并将其扩展到多实验设置，从而能够对模型行为进行系统分析。我们构建了涵盖七种查询类型的 350 个时间序列实例的综合基准，每个实例都与正确、部分正确和不正确的候选解释配对。我们评估四个任务的模型：解释生成、候选解释的相对排名、独立正确性评分和多异常检测。这种设计使我们能够研究模型如何在结构化提示下充当解释生成器和正确性评估器。To address this gap, we adopt a rubric guided, reference free evaluation framework and extend it to a multi experiment setting that enables systematic analysis of model behavior. We construct a synthetic benchmark of 350 time series instances spanning seven query types, each paired with correct, partially correct, and incorrect candidate explanations. We evaluate models across four tasks: explanation generation, relative ranking of candidate explanations, independent correctness scoring, and multi anomaly detection. This design allows us to study how models perform both as generators of explanations and as evaluators of correctness under structured prompting.

我们的实验表明，模型性能高度依赖于所提出的查询类型。在解释生成中，模型在多种查询类型上取得了很好的结果，但在其他查询类型上表现出系统性弱点，其准确度范围从“结构断裂”的 0.94-0.96 到“季节性下降和波动性转变”的 0.00-0.12，这表明它们对时间序列数据的推理能力参差不齐。然而，即使在生成中断的情况下，相同的模型通常也能够在结构化提示和明确的评估标准指导下评估正确性、对竞争解释进行排名并识别错误，在这些任务中实现高达 0.96 的准确度。这些发现表明，虽然开放式生成对于特定查询类型仍然很脆弱，但结构化提示可以显着提高评估性能，凸显了法学硕士作为数据基础推理环境中可靠评估者的潜力。Our experiments show that model performance is highly dependent on the type of query being asked. In explanation generation, models achieve strong results on several query types but exhibit systematic weaknesses on others, with accuracy ranging from 0.94–0.96 for Structural Break to 0.00–0.12 for Seasonal Drop and Volatility Shift, indicating that their ability to reason over time-series data is uneven. However, even in cases where generation breaks down, the same models are often able to assess correctness, rank competing explanations, and identify errors when guided by structured prompts and explicit evaluation criteria, achieving up to 0.96 accuracy in these tasks. These findings suggest that while open ended generation remains brittle for specific query types, structured prompting can substantially improve evaluative performance, highlighting the potential of LLMs as reliable evaluators in data grounded reasoning settings.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 基于参考的文本评估Reference-Based Text Evaluation

生成文本的自动评估传统上依赖于基于参考的度量，例如 BLEU、ROUGE 和基于嵌入的相似性度量Automatic evaluation of generated text has traditionally relied on reference-based metrics such as BLEU, ROUGE, and embedding-based similarity measures(Papineni et al., 2002; Lin, 2004; Zhang et al., 2020)。这些指标量化了生成的输出和黄金参考之间的词汇或语义重叠，并已广泛应用于机器翻译、摘要和问答领域。自然语言推理 (NLI) 模型也已用于评估候选输出和参考语句之间的蕴涵. These metrics quantify lexical or semantic overlap between a generated output and a gold reference, and have been widely adopted in machine translation, summarization, and question answering. Natural language inference (NLI) models have also been used to assess entailment between candidate outputs and reference statements(Bowman et al., 2015).

虽然这些方法在许多 NLP 任务中有效，但它们假设有真实的解释，并且纯粹在文本到文本的环境中运行。它们不包含结构化的数字证据，因此无法直接验证自由形式的解释是否忠实于基础时间序列数据。在参考解释无法对所有数字约束或边缘情况进行编码的设置中，这些方法可能无法检测细微的不一致或幻觉的统计数据。While effective in many NLP tasks, these approaches assume the availability of ground-truth explanations and operate purely in a text-to-text setting. They do not incorporate structured numerical evidence and therefore cannot directly verify whether a free-form explanation is faithful to underlying time-series data. In settings where reference explanations cannot encode all numeric constraints or edge cases, these methods may fail to detect subtle inconsistencies or hallucinated statistics.

2.2 时间序列分析和语言集成Time-Series Analysis and Language Integration

经典时间序列分析提供了建模时间动态的原则方法，包括趋势检测、异常识别和预测Classical time-series analysis provides principled methods for modeling temporal dynamics, including trend detection, anomaly identification, and forecasting(Hamilton, 1994; Box et al., 2015)。最近，大型语言模型已被集成到时间序列管道中，方法是对预先训练的 LLM 进行重新编程以进行预测，或者使用它们生成数字信号的自然语言解释. More recently, large language models have been integrated into time-series pipelines, either by reprogramming pretrained LLMs for forecasting or by using them to generate natural-language interpretations of numerical signals(Jin et al., 2023; Zhou & Yu, 2024)。这些工作表明法学硕士可以处理时间信息并根据时间序列输入产生流畅的解释。. These works demonstrate that LLMs can process temporal information and produce fluent explanations grounded in time-series inputs.

然而，此类系统中的评估通常侧重于预测准确性（例如 MAE、MSE）或依赖于表面级别的文本相似性来保证解释质量。在系统地评估生成的解释在数字上是否忠实于基础数据方面的工作仍然有限。However, evaluation in such systems typically focuses on forecasting accuracy (e.g., MAE, MSE) or relies on surface-level textual similarity for explanation quality. There remains limited work on systematically evaluating whether generated explanations are numerically faithful to the underlying data.

2.3 时间序列问答和解释基准Time-Series Question Answering and Explanation Benchmarks

最近的数据集（例如 TSAQA）引入了时间序列分析的问答基准，要求模型产生基于时间信号的自由形式的解释Recent datasets such as TSAQA introduce question-answering benchmarks for time-series analysis, requiring models to produce free-form explanations grounded in temporal signals(Jing et al., 2026)。这些基准凸显了解释级推理在时间序列系统中日益增长的重要性。然而，评估协议通常依赖于人工注释或基于参考的文本指标，从而留下了如何对解释忠实度进行自动、无参考验证的问题。. These benchmarks highlight the growing importance of explanation-level reasoning in time-series systems. However, evaluation protocols often depend on human annotation or reference-based text metrics, leaving open the question of how to perform automated, reference-free verification of explanation faithfulness.

2.4 法学硕士作为评估者LLMs as Evaluators

人们越来越有兴趣使用大型语言模型本身作为生成输出的评估器。调查和实证研究调查了法学硕士在对话、总结和一般语言生成方面的框架，表明法学硕士可以在精心设计的提示和评分标准下近似人类的判断There is increasing interest in using large language models themselves as evaluators of generated outputs. Survey and empirical studies have investigated LLM-as-a-Judge frameworks in dialogue, summarization, and general language generation, demonstrating that LLMs can approximate human judgments under carefully designed prompts and scoring rubrics(Fu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2023)。 MT-Bench和Chatbot Arena等基准进一步研究比较环境中LLM法官的可靠性和校准. Benchmarks such as MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena further study the reliability and calibration of LLM judges in comparative settings(Chiang et al., 2024)。此外，诸如 RAGAS 之类的无参考评估方法强调了基于模型的评估在不依赖真实参考的情况下的可行性. Additionally, reference-free evaluation approaches such as RAGAS highlight the feasibility of model-based evaluation without reliance on ground-truth references(Akin et al., 2023).

尽管取得了这些进步，大多数现有的法学硕士法官工作都是在文本到文本的评估环境中进行的。基于法学硕士的法官对数字领域的适用性仍有待探索，在数字领域，正确性取决于对结构化时间序列数据的推理，而不是文本相似性。特别是，目前尚不清楚法学硕士法官是否捕捉到实质性的基于数据的推理，或者只是复制在非法学硕士基线中观察到的启发式相似模式。Despite these advances, most existing LLM-judge work operates in text-to-text evaluation settings. The applicability of LLM-based judges to numerically grounded domains, where correctness depends on reasoning over structured time-series data rather than textual similarity, remains underexplored. In particular, it is unclear whether LLM judges capture substantive data-grounded reasoning or simply replicate heuristic similarity patterns observed in non-LLM baselines.

我们工作的定位。Positioning of Our Work.

我们的工作通过将法学硕士作为法官范式扩展到以数据为基础、无参考文献的评估环境，在这些研究领域之间架起桥梁。与基于参考的指标不同，我们的方法直接以原始时间序列输入为条件。与经典数值方法不同，它评估自由形式的文本解释。与之前专注于文本一致性的法学硕士法官研究不同，我们明确调查了时间序列解释中的数字忠实度、幻觉统计数据和部分正确性。此外，我们引入了一个多实验框架，可以对不同推理任务中的生成和评估行为进行系统分析。Our work bridges these lines of research by extending the LLM-as-a-Judge paradigm to a data-grounded, reference-free evaluation setting. Unlike reference-based metrics, our approach directly conditions on raw time-series inputs. Unlike classical numerical methods, it evaluates free-form textual explanations. Unlike prior LLM-judge studies focused on textual consistency, we explicitly investigate numeric faithfulness, hallucinated statistics, and partial correctness in time-series explanations. Further, we introduce a multi-experiment framework that enables systematic analysis of both generation and evaluation behavior across diverse reasoning tasks.

3 方法论Methodology

3.1 概述Overview

我们使用标题引导的无参考框架来研究时间序列解释的评估。给定一个时间序列、一个问题和一个或多个候选解释，目标是评估这些解释是否与基础数值数据实际上一致。We study the evaluation of time-series explanations using a rubric-guided, reference-free framework. Given a time series, a question, and one or more candidate explanations, the goal is to assess whether the explanations are factually consistent with the underlying numerical data.

我们的方法将结构化提示与多任务评估设置相结合，探索模型行为的不同方面。评估不是依赖参考解释，而是通过验证模式、数值声明和逻辑一致性，直接针对时间序列进行评估。该框架旨在在统一的环境下评估生成能力和评估能力。Our approach combines structured prompting with a multi-task evaluation setup that probes different aspects of model behavior. Rather than relying on reference explanations, evaluation is performed directly against the time series by verifying patterns, numerical claims, and logical consistency. The framework is designed to evaluate both generative and evaluative capabilities under a unified setting.

3.2 输入表示和评估设置Input Representation and Evaluation Setting

3.3 评分标准引导的评估Rubric-Guided Evaluation

尽管最终输出是单个标签，但评估是由一个隐含的准则指导的，该准则捕获了正确性的多个维度。该模型被指示对以下方面进行推理：Although the final output is a single label, evaluation is guided by an implicit rubric that captures multiple dimensions of correctness. The model is instructed to reason over the following aspects:

  1. 1.

    数据真实性：Data Faithfulness:解释是否正确识别时间序列中存在的模式、异常或趋势。Whether the explanation correctly identifies patterns, anomalies, or trends present in the time series.

  2. 2.

    数字准确度：Numeric Accuracy:数值声明（例如幅度、指数和百分比变化）是否计算正确。Whether numerical claims such as magnitudes, indices, and percentage changes are computed correctly.

  3. 3.

    问题相关性：Question Relevance:解释是否直接回答了问题。Whether the explanation directly answers the question.

  4. 4.

    逻辑连贯性：Logical Coherence:推理是否内部一致。Whether the reasoning is internally consistent.

  5. 5.

    不支持的主张：Unsupported Claims:解释是否避免了没有数据依据的陈述。Whether the explanation avoids statements not grounded in the data.

明确指示模型根据原始时间序列验证所有数字声明，而不是依赖于解释中所述的值。这些维度没有明确评分，而是通过结构化提示内化，以指导最终的分类决策。The model is explicitly instructed to verify all numerical claims against the raw time series and not rely on values stated in the explanation. These dimensions are not scored explicitly but are internalized through structured prompting to guide the final classification decision.

3.5 设计选择和理由Design Choices and Justification

为什么要采用标题引导的方法？Why a rubric-guided approach?

标题为评估正确性的多个方面提供了结构化指导，包括数字准确性和逻辑一致性。这使得模型能够超越表面水平的文本相似性并执行基于数据的推理。A rubric provides structured guidance for evaluating multiple aspects of correctness, including numerical accuracy and logical consistency. This enables the model to move beyond surface-level textual similarity and perform data-grounded reasoning.

为什么要进行无参考评估？Why reference-free evaluation?

基于参考的指标需要真实的解释，并且通常无法捕获数字不一致或推理错误。无参考设置允许直接基于基础数据进行评估。Reference-based metrics require ground-truth explanations and often fail to capture numeric inconsistencies or reasoning errors. A reference-free setup allows evaluation to be grounded directly in the underlying data.

为什么要执行多项评估任务？Why multiple evaluation tasks?

不同的任务探测不同的能力。生成评估产生解释的能力，而排名和评分则评估评估正确性的能力。多重异常检测测试对数值数据的直接推理。这些任务共同提供了模型行为的更全面的视图。Different tasks probe different capabilities. Generation evaluates the ability to produce explanations, while ranking and scoring evaluate the ability to assess correctness. Multi-anomaly detection tests direct reasoning over numerical data. Together, these tasks provide a more comprehensive view of model behavior.

4 实验装置Experimental Setup

4.1 型号Models

我们评估了三种开放权重大型语言模型：Qwen-3 8B、LLaMA-3 8B 和 Gemma-2 9B IT。选择这些模型来表示可比较的参数尺度，但训练数据、架构和指令调整策略有所不同。We evaluate three open-weight large language models: Qwen-3 8B, LLaMA-3 8B, and Gemma-2 9B IT. These models are selected to represent comparable parameter scales while differing in training data, architecture, and instruction tuning strategies.

每个模型在我们的框架中都以两种角色进行评估：作为生成任务中的解释生成器，以及作为排名和评分任务中的评估者。这使得能够在统一的环境下直接比较生成能力和评估能力。Each model is evaluated in both roles within our framework: as a generator of explanations in the generation task, and as an evaluator in the ranking and scoring tasks. This enables direct comparison between generative and evaluative capabilities under a unified setting.

4.2 数据集Datasets

我们评估我们的方法We evaluate our approach onTS查询基准TSQueryBench，涵盖七种查询类型的 350 个时间序列实例的综合基准：线性峰值、季节性下降、结构性断裂、多指标一致性、相对极值、均值漂移和波动性漂移。每个查询类型包含 50 个实例，时间序列长度分布在 100、200、300 和 500 个时间步长中。尽管所有实例和评估任务都是独立构建的，但查询类型的整体结构松散地受到先前时间序列 QA 公式的启发。, a synthetic benchmark of 350 time-series instances spanning seven query types: linear spike, seasonal drop, structural break, multi-metric consistency, relative extremum, mean shift, and volatility shift. Each query type contains 50 instances, with time-series lengths distributed across 100, 200, 300, and 500 time steps. The overall structure of query types is loosely inspired by prior time-series QA formulations, though all instances and evaluation tasks are constructed independently.

对于每个实例，我们使用基于规则的构造管道生成与正确、部分正确和不正确推理相对应的三个候选解释。忠实的解释直接来自真实的统计数据（例如，异常位置、百分比变化、z 分数或分布属性），部分正确的解释引入受控的数值扰动，不正确的解释由看似合理但事实上不一致的推理模式组成，例如移动的指数、不正确的趋势或否认异常。这导致独立评分任务中总共有 1050 个用于评估的解释实例。For each instance, we generate three candidate explanations corresponding to correct, partially correct, and incorrect reasoning using a rule-based construction pipeline. Faithful explanations are derived directly from ground-truth statistics (e.g., anomaly location, percentage change, z-score, or distributional properties), partially correct explanations introduce controlled numerical perturbations, and incorrect explanations consist of plausible but factually inconsistent reasoning patterns such as shifted indices, incorrect trends, or denial of anomalies. This results in 1050 total explanation instances for evaluation in the independent scoring task.

多异常检测任务在包含 100 个时间序列实例的单独数据集上进行，每个实例包含 1 到 10 个异常，均匀分布在 100、200、300 和 500 的序列长度上。附录中提供了两个数据集的代表性样本以及每个任务的完整评估轨迹The multi-anomaly detection task is conducted on a separate dataset of 100 time-series instances containing between 1 and 10 anomalies each, evenly distributed across sequence lengths of 100, 200, 300, and 500. Representative samples from both datasets, along with full evaluation traces for each task, are provided in AppendixA.

4.3 评估协议Evaluation Protocol

所有任务均使用结构化提示执行，这些提示指示模型直接对基础时间序列进行推理，而不是依赖于解释中所述的值。All tasks are performed using structured prompts that instruct the model to reason directly over the underlying time series rather than relying on values stated in the explanation.

为了评估解释生成任务中生成的解释，我们使用人类专家的注释来确保标签质量和一致性。For evaluation of generated explanations in the explanation generation task, we use annotations from human experts to ensure label quality and consistency.

为了生成解释，模型输出使用第 3 节中描述的三元标题进行评估For explanation generation, model outputs are evaluated using the ternary rubric described in Section3。对于相对排名，准确性是通过选择正确解释的实例的比例来衡量的。对于独立评分，准确性是根据所有候选解释的真实标签来计算的。对于多异常检测，使用两个指标来评估性能：计数精度（衡量预测的异常数量及其索引是否与真实值匹配）和 F1 分数（评估预测异常指数与真实异常指数之间的重叠）。预测索引与地面实况索引的匹配误差在 ±2 个时间步长内。. For relative ranking, accuracy is measured as the proportion of instances where the correct explanation is selected. For independent scoring, accuracy is computed against ground-truth labels across all candidate explanations. For multi-anomaly detection, performance is evaluated using two metrics: count accuracy, which measures whether the predicted number of anomalies and their indices matches the ground truth, and F1 score, which evaluates the overlap between predicted and true anomaly indices. Predicted indices are matched to ground truth indices within a tolerance of ±2 time steps.

4.4 实施细节Implementation Details

所有实验都是在零样本设置中进行的，没有针对特定任务的微调。跨模型的生成和评估提示是标准化的，以确保可比性。输出被限制为结构化 JSON 格式，以实现确定性解析。All experiments are conducted in a zero-shot setting without task-specific fine-tuning. Generation and evaluation prompts are standardized across models to ensure comparability. Outputs are constrained to structured JSON formats to enable deterministic parsing.

为了减少随机变异性，我们使用跨模型的固定解码设置进行实验。所有评估均在同一组实例上进行，确保实验条件的一致性。To reduce stochastic variability, experiments are conducted with fixed decoding settings across models. All evaluations are performed on the same set of instances, ensuring consistency across experimental conditions.

跨序列长度的性能保持稳定，这表明与生成相比，排序对输入规模的敏感度较低。Performance remains stable across sequence lengths, suggesting that ranking is less sensitive to input scale compared to generation.

5.5 讨论Discussion

结果揭示了生成能力和评估能力之间的明显区别。虽然生成性能高度依赖于查询类型并表现出系统故障，但评估性能在排名和评分任务中保持稳定。The results reveal a clear distinction between generation and evaluation capabilities. While generation performance is highly dependent on query type and exhibits systematic failures, evaluation performance remains stable across both ranking and scoring tasks.

一个关键的观察结果是，即使模型无法产生正确的解释，它们通常也能够识别出正确的解释。这表明结构化提示比开放式生成能够实现更可靠的验证。A key observation is that models are often able to identify correct explanations even when they fail to produce them. This suggests that structured prompting enables more reliable verification than open-ended generation.

此外，模型在局部模式上表现良好，但在处理波动性等高阶统计特性时表现不佳。异常检测结果进一步凸显了精确定量推理的局限性，特别是在阈值校准方面。Additionally, models perform well on localized patterns but struggle with higher-order statistical properties such as volatility. The anomaly detection results further highlight limitations in precise quantitative reasoning, particularly in threshold calibration.

6 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了一个基于标题的法学硕士法官框架，用于在无参考设置中评估时间序列解释。通过直接根据原始数值数据进行评估，我们的方法可以对解释正确性进行结构化评估，而无需依赖真实的文本参考。In this work, we introduced a rubric-based LLM-as-a-Judge framework for evaluating time-series explanations in a reference-free setting. By conditioning evaluation directly on raw numerical data, our approach enables structured assessment of explanation correctness without relying on ground-truth textual references.

通过一系列受控实验，我们表明生成任务和评估任务之间的模型性能存在显着差异。虽然解释生成高度依赖于查询类型，并且对于某些模式（例如波动性变化）表现出系统性故障，但排名和独立评分等评估任务在不同设置中保持相对稳定。值得注意的是，即使模型无法生成准确的解释，它们通常也能够正确识别或评分解释。Through a series of controlled experiments, we show that model performance differs significantly between generation and evaluation tasks. While explanation generation is highly dependent on query type and exhibits systematic failures for certain patterns such as volatility shifts, evaluation tasks such as ranking and independent scoring remain comparatively stable across settings. Notably, models are often able to correctly identify or score explanations even when they fail to generate accurate ones.

我们的结果进一步强调，模型在局部和显式模式上表现良好，但在高阶统计特性和精确定量推理方面表现不佳。在多异常检测设置中，模型表现出中等的定位异常的能力，但无法准确估计其计数，这表明存在过度敏感检测的趋势。Our results further highlight that models perform well on localized and explicit patterns, but struggle with higher-order statistical properties and precise quantitative reasoning. In the multi-anomaly detection setting, models demonstrate moderate ability to localize anomalies but fail to accurately estimate their count, suggesting a tendency toward over-sensitive detection.

总的来说，这些发现表明，在评估基于数据的推理能力时，分离生成和评估至关重要。在这种情况下，法学硕士作为评估者似乎比作为主要生成者更可靠，特别是在结构化提示和明确的标准指导下。Overall, these findings suggest that separating generation and evaluation is critical when assessing data-grounded reasoning capabilities. LLMs appear to be more reliable as evaluators than as primary generators in such settings, particularly when guided by structured prompts and explicit rubrics.

未来的工作包括将该框架扩展到现实世界的数据集，提高统计复杂模式的稳健性，以及探索结合基于模型和符号验证方法的混合评估系统。Future work includes extending the framework to real-world datasets, improving robustness on statistically complex patterns, and exploring hybrid evaluation systems that combine model-based and symbolic verification methods.
