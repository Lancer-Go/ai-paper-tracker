摘要Abstract

人形运动跟踪是远程操作和全身模仿的核心，但评估往往与人们在视频中的感知不一致。 每帧平均运动误差会造成差异，但会忽略最重要的物理伪影，特别是不稳定的支撑和不正确的接触，例如脚滑行和不合时宜的触地得分。 与此同时，广泛使用的测试套件都很小，并且缺乏强调接触丰富、长期行为所需的多样性。 我们引入 HumanTracker 来使人形跟踪评估在感知上一致且可扩展。 HumanTracker 基准测试包含来自多个专业表演者的约 153 小时的光学运动轨迹，分为四个运动系列，并带有用于细粒度诊断的文本标签。 我们进一步提出了 HumanScore，这是一种在包含 24K 运动的 12K 运动对上训练的偏好对齐度量。 在具有代表性的最先进跟踪器中，HumanScore 可以更好地预测人类偏好，并揭示运动学指标经常忽略的接触和稳定性故障。Humanoid motion tracking is central to teleoperation and whole-body imitation, yet evaluation often disagrees with what people perceive in videos. Kinematic errors average per-frame pose differences but miss the physical artifacts that matter most, particularly unstable support and incorrect contacts such as foot skating and mistimed touch-downs. Meanwhile, widely used test suites are small and lack the diversity needed to stress contact-rich, long-horizon behaviors. We introduce HumanTracker to make humanoid tracking evaluation both perceptually aligned and scalable. The HumanTracker benchmark contains approximately 153 hours of optical motion trajectories from multiple professional performers, organized into four motion families with text labels for fine-grained diagnosis. We further propose HumanScore, a preference-aligned metric trained on 12K motion pairs containing 24K motions. Across representative state-of-the-art trackers, HumanScore better predicts human preferences and reveals contact and stability failures that kinematic metrics often miss.

1 简介Introduction

运动跟踪正在成为人形机器人控制的基石Motion tracking is becoming the cornerstone of humanoid robot control[23, 4, 24, 42, 19, 30, 37]。这种能力推动了远程操作和全身模仿的应用. This ability drives applications in teleoperation and whole-body imitation[43, 44, 9, 7, 16, 10, 6]。大多数系统使用物理模拟中学习的反馈策略来跟踪参考运动. Most systems track a reference motion using a learned feedback policy in physics simulation[23, 4, 24, 42]。然而，衡量这种跟踪的真实质量仍然非常困难。目前有两个主要问题限制了该领域的进展。. Yet, measuring the true quality of this tracking remains surprisingly difficult. Two major issues currently limit progress in this field.

第一个主要问题是我们如何衡量成功。运动跟踪看起来很容易测量：可以将推出姿势与参考姿势进行比较并报告运动学误差，例如平均关节误差和关键点误差The first major issue is how we measure success. Motion tracking looks easy to measure: one can compare the rollout pose to the reference pose and report kinematic errors such as mean joint error and key point error[4, 24, 42, 48, 29]。然而，我们发现这些数字与人们在视频中看到的数字之间存在明显差距。推出可能会实现较低的运动学误差，但看起来仍然很糟糕，尤其是在接触情况下. However, we find a clear gap between these numbers and what people see in videos. A rollout may achieve low kinematic error but still look bad, especially under contacts[12, 41, 1, 40]。脚可能会在地面上滑动，触点可能会在错误的时间断开并重新连接；这些伪影正是基于视频的设置中接触感知全局运动重建和细化方法的目标. Feet may slide on the ground, and contacts may break and reattach at the wrong time; these artifacts are exactly what contact-aware global motion reconstruction and refinement methods target in video-based settings as well[32, 47, 45]。这些伪像是决定运动是否稳定、流畅和是否像人类的关键因素。. These artifacts are the key aspect to decide whether the motion looks stable, smooth and human-like.

这种差距并不是一个极端情况，而是运动学指标测量结果的结果。通过将跟踪视为每帧姿势匹配问题并对关节和时间上的误差进行平均，这些指标无法捕获闭环控制中的接触、支撑、平衡和误差累积。因此，两个卷展栏可能具有相似的姿态误差，但稳定性却有很大差异。观察者确实更喜欢干净的接触和稳定的支持，即使 MPJPE 是相似的。图This gap is not a corner case but a consequence of what kinematic metrics measure. By treating tracking as a per-frame pose matching problem and averaging errors over joints and time, these metrics fail to capture contact, support, balance, and the accumulation of errors in closed-loop control. As a result, two rollouts can have similar pose errors yet differ substantially in stability. Observers reliably prefer the rollout with clean contacts and steady support, even when MPJPE is similar. 第二个主要问题是评估数据的范围。尽管有大型运动存储库（例如 PHUMA）The second major issue is the scope of evaluation data. Despite the availability of large motion repositories such as PHUMA[12]和索尼克and SONIC[24]，最常用的人形跟踪评估套件仍然是只有 140 个序列的 AMASS 测试集, the most commonly used evaluation suite for humanoid tracking is still an AMASS test set with only 140 sequences[26]。这个小集合缺乏多样性，并且不足以代表人类运动的长尾，包括具有挑战性的接触过渡、不对称平衡和复杂的恢复。此外，结果通常被总结为单个总分，而没有按运动类别进行详细细分，因此很难准确查明跟踪器失败的位置和原因。表. This small set lacks diversity and under-represents the long tail of human movement, including challenging contact transitions, asymmetric balancing, and complex recoveries. Moreover, results are often summarized as a single aggregate score, without a detailed breakdown by motion category, making it difficult to pinpoint exactly where and why a tracker fails. 我们引入 HumanTracker 来解决这些问题。为了解决指标不一致的问题，我们开发了一种偏好一致的评估：我们收集同步跟踪视频上的人类比较，并训练奖励模型来预测这些偏好We introduce HumanTracker to address these problems. To address the metric misalignment, we develop a preference-aligned evaluation: we collect human comparisons on synchronized tracking videos and train a reward model to predict these preferences[5, 27, 38]。我们将此指标称为 HumanScore。与关节误差不同，HumanScore 明确针对人类偏好、感知稳定性和现实的身体接触。它会惩罚那些在人类观察者看来错误的物理制品。重要的是，我们证明 HumanScore 捕获了微妙的感知质量，而这些感知质量不能简单地简化为基于规则的诊断，例如脚滑或根漂移。大量的实验和视觉分析证明了 HumanScore 的准确性和零样本泛化能力。. We call this metric HumanScore. Unlike joint error, HumanScore explicitly targets human preferences, perceptual stability, and realistic physical contacts. It penalizes the exact physical artifacts that look wrong to human observers. Importantly, we demonstrate that HumanScore captures nuanced perceptual qualities that cannot be simply reduced to rule-based diagnostics such as foot slip or root drift. Extensive experiments and visual analyses demonstrate the accuracy and zero-shot generalization of HumanScore.

为了解决现有测试套件缺乏多样性的问题，我们提供了大约 153 小时的光学运动轨迹，并配有类别和文本标签，补充了现有的大规模运动源To address the lack of diversity in existing test suites, we provide approximately 153 hours of optical motion trajectories paired with category and text labels, complementing existing large-scale motion sources[26, 20, 46, 12]。所有源运动均由 24 名专业表演者（包括舞蹈老师和健身教练）在配有多摄像头光学系统的受控工作室中记录，为接触丰富的跟踪评估提供高保真参考。我们明确地将数据集组织成四个不同的系列，涵盖日常任务、高动态运动、交互运动和地面运动，从而实现每个系列的指标和故障模式的细粒度诊断。 HumanTracker 大大超过了之前用于人形跟踪的动作捕捉数据集。我们根据我们的基准全面评估了几种最先进的跟踪器，包括 GMT、TWIST2、SONIC 和 Humanoid-GPT. All source motions are recorded in a controlled studio with a multi-camera optical system by 24 professional performers, including dance teachers and fitness coaches, yielding high-fidelity references for contact-rich tracking evaluation. We explicitly organize the dataset into four distinct families covering daily tasks, highly dynamic movements, interaction motions, and ground-level motions, enabling per-family metrics and fine-grained diagnosis of failure modes. HumanTracker substantially exceeds prior mocap datasets used for humanoid tracking. We comprehensively evaluate several state-of-the-art trackers, including GMT, TWIST2, SONIC, and Humanoid-GPT, under our benchmark[4, 44, 24, 29].

总之，我们的主要贡献有三个方面。首先，我们强调传统运动学指标的失败，并引入 HumanScore 来使评估与人类保持一致。其次，我们提供了一个大规模的运动跟踪基准，其中包含大约 153 小时的多样化、分类的光学轨迹。第三，我们建立严格且标准化的评估协议，以确保人形跟踪的未来进展既可衡量又有意义。In summary, our main contributions are threefold. First, we highlight the failure of traditional kinematic metrics and introduce HumanScore to align evaluation with humans. Second, we provide a massive motion tracking benchmark featuring approximately 153 hours of diverse, categorized optical trajectories. Third, we establish a rigorous and standardized evaluation protocol to ensure future progress in humanoid tracking is both measurable and meaningful.

深度模仿DeepMimic[28]为基于物理的角色控制建立了参考条件策略学习。 PHC 和 UHMestablished reference conditioned policy learning for physics-based character control. PHC and UHM[23, 22]将这种范例扩展到各种人形运动。最近的系统通过多种机制扩大了运动覆盖范围并控制鲁棒性。 GMT、SONIC、Humanoid-GPT 和 UniTrackerextended this paradigm to diverse humanoid motion. Recent systems broaden motion coverage and control robustness through several mechanisms. GMT, SONIC, Humanoid-GPT and UniTracker[4, 24, 29, 42]强调规模和一般跟踪。 ResMimic、MHC 和 iCTRLemphasize scale and general tracking. ResMimic, MHC and iCTRL[52, 6, 40]探索残差校正、多模式命令和约束控制。任意2轨explore residual correction, multimodal commands and constrained control. Any2Track[48]使跟踪策略适应地形、外力和物理特性的变化。人形PFadapts tracking policies to changes in terrain, external forces and physical properties. HumanoidPF[39]使用人形势场来学习杂乱的室内场景的防撞技能。对抗性差分判别器uses a humanoid potential field to learn collision avoidance skills for cluttered indoor scenes. Adversarial Differential Discriminators[51]提供学习的运动模仿目标。全方位追踪provide a learned motion imitation objective. OmniTrack[17]构建物理上一致的参考，而 LIMMTconstructs physically consistent references, while LIMMT[8]研究精心选择的训练动作如何改善跟踪。同样的功能支持全身远程操作和模仿系统，包括 TWIST、OmniH2O、HumanPlus、CLONE 和 H2Ostudies how carefully selected training motions can improve tracking. The same capability underpins whole-body teleoperation and imitation systems, including TWIST, OmniH2O, HumanPlus, CLONE and H2O[43, 9, 7, 16, 10]。这些进步产生了越来越强大的跟踪器，但它们报告的性能很难比较，因为结果不仅取决于学习的策略，还取决于参考集、模拟器、动作表示、初始化和终止规则。因此，HumanTracker 将评估视为对照实验。跟踪器特定的策略接口被保留，而参考表示、推出记帐和报告的指标被标准化。. These advances have produced increasingly capable trackers, but their reported performance is difficult to compare because results depend not only on the learned policy, but also on the reference set, simulator, action representation, initialization, and termination rule. HumanTracker therefore treats evaluation as a controlled experiment. Tracker specific policy interfaces are preserved, whereas the reference representation, rollout accounting, and reported metrics are standardized.

2.2 运动数据评估Motion data evaluation

大型运动存储库，例如 AMASS、Motion-X 和 Motion-X++Large motion repositories such as AMASS, Motion-X and Motion-X++[26, 20, 46]大大扩展了可用于学习的人体运动的多样性。近期作品have substantially expanded the diversity of human motion available for learning. Recent works[2, 49, 14, 50]进一步支持语义检索和条件生成。然而，对于人形跟踪，数据集大小本身并不能定义有用的基准。人体运动还必须重新定位到机器人形态，在接触周围保持物理合理性，并包含足够困难的机制来暴露控制器故障。 PHUMA、OmniRetarget 和 GMRfurther support semantic retrieval and conditional generation. For humanoid tracking, however, dataset size alone does not define a useful benchmark. Human motion must also be retargeted to the robot morphology, remain physically plausible around contacts, and contain sufficiently difficult regimes to expose controller failures. PHUMA, OmniRetarget and GMR[12, 41, 1]通过物理接地数据或机器人重定向来满足这些要求。 WHAM、ProxyCap 和 RoHMaddress these requirements through physically grounded data or robot retargeting. WHAM, ProxyCap and RoHM[32, 47, 45]解决全局人体运动重建中的相关错误。 Switch-舞力全开address related errors in global human motion reconstruction. Switch-JustDance[11]提供基于商业动作游戏的全身技能的补充基准。然而，现有的跟踪评估仍然集中在相对较小的测试集上，并且经常将异构运动分解为一个聚合值。 HumanTracker 通过一个大型、明确分类的测试台补充了先前的运动源，该测试台的四个系列将稳定运动、快速冲击力丰富的运动、交互运动和具有多个接触的地面过渡分开。provides a complementary benchmark based on whole-body skills from a commercial motion game. Existing tracking evaluations nevertheless remain concentrated on comparatively small test sets and often collapse heterogeneous motions into one aggregate value. HumanTracker complements prior motion sources with a large, explicitly categorized test bed whose four families separate steady locomotion, rapid impact rich motion, interaction motions, and ground level transitions with multiple contacts.

3.2 标准化跟踪器评估Standardized tracker evaluation

对于每种方法，我们将参考转换为相同的 29-DoF 人形机器人For every method, we convert the reference to the same 29-DoF humanoid队列位置qpos通过通用 MuJoCo 评估入口点表示并执行策略。每个跟踪器保留其本机策略观察和动作解码器，而评估器则标准化运动列表、机器人模型、参考索引、推出会计和度量实施。控制轨迹以 50Hz 记录。在每一步，评估器都会存储模拟的广义位置和速度、策略动作和运动目标、足部接触和接触力、足部和骨盆速度以及 14 个关键点姿势和空间速度。相同的状态历史记录用于传统诊断和 HumanScore，这可以防止后处理中的差异被误认为跟踪器之间的差异。representation and execute the policy through a common MuJoCo evaluation entry point. Each tracker retains its native policy observations and action decoder, and the evaluator instead standardizes the motion list, robot model, reference indexing, rollout accounting and metric implementation. 5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了一个大规模基准测试和 HumanScore（一种与人类对齐的指标），用于评估人形运动跟踪。发布的基准测试基于 24 名专业表演者记录的光学运动，包含四个运动系列的大约 153 小时。它揭示了高动态和地面接触运动中持续存在的弱点。 HumanTracker 为跟踪器比较和故障分析提供了一个可重复的框架，未来的工作将扩展到跨实施例设置、现实世界硬件和奖励优化。We introduce a large-scale benchmark and HumanScore, a human-aligned metric, for evaluating humanoid motion tracking. Built from optical motion recorded from 24 professional performers, the released benchmark contains approximately 153 hours across four motion families. It reveals persistent weaknesses in highly dynamic and ground-contact motions. HumanTracker provides a reproducible framework for tracker comparison and failure analysis, with future work extending to cross-embodiment settings, real-world hardware, and reward optimization.

附录GAppendix G讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

基准和偏好结果从不同的方向得出了相同的结论。类别级评估表明，接触方式改变了跟踪器的相对行为：在日常直立运动上可靠的方法在地面过渡期间仍然可能几乎完全失败。偏好实验解释了为什么这种区别不能完全用联合误差来表示。当 HumanScore 可以在几秒钟内观察与接触相关的状态时，它就获得了大部分优势，这表明失败的感知单元通常是滑动、撞击、支撑切换或恢复等事件，而不是孤立的姿势。因此，HumanScore 应与成功和运动学误差一起阅读，而不是作为所有分析诊断的替代品：前者总结了感知的轨迹质量，而后者对于定位特定的误差源仍然很有价值。The benchmark and preference results point to the same conclusion from different directions. Category-level evaluation shows that contact regime changes the relative behaviour of trackers: a method that is reliable on upright daily motion can still fail almost completely during ground-level transitions. The preference experiment explains why this distinction is not fully represented by joint error. HumanScore gains most of its advantage when it can observe contact-related state over several seconds, suggesting that the perceptual unit of failure is often an event such as a slide, impact, support switch or recovery, rather than an isolated pose. HumanScore should therefore be read alongside success and kinematic error, not as a replacement for all analytic diagnostics: the former summarizes perceived trajectory quality, whereas the latter remain valuable for locating a specific source of error.

仍然存在一些界限。 HumanScore 是根据四个跟踪器产生的 HumanTracker 训练动作和展示进行训练的，因此它的运动不相交测试测量对不可见运动的泛化，而不是对完全不可见的机器人、模拟器或控制器系列的泛化。其 539 维输入还包括可用于基准测试但可能无法在硬件上观察到的特权模拟器状态和接触量；将度量应用于现实世界的轨迹将需要可观察的特征集或单独验证的状态估计器。偏好池为每一对分配一个主要判断，这提供了广泛的覆盖范围，但没有通过重复的独立标签来量化不确定性。最后，本指标比较涵盖了代表性的个体诊断；针对拟合的线性和非线性诊断复合材料进行更广泛的测试将进一步界定必须从轨迹级偏好数据中了解的内容。Several boundaries remain. HumanScore is trained from HumanTracker training motions and rollouts produced by four trackers, so its motion-disjoint test measures generalization to unseen motions, not to an entirely unseen robot, simulator or controller family. Its 539-dimensional input also includes privileged simulator state and contact quantities that are available for benchmarking but may not be observable on hardware; applying the metric to real-world trajectories will require an observable feature set or a separately validated state estimator. The preference pool assigns one primary judgment to each pair, which provides broad coverage but does not quantify uncertainty through repeated independent labels. Finally, the present metric comparison covers representative individual diagnostics; broader tests against fitted linear and nonlinear diagnostic composites would further delimit what must be learned from trajectory-level preference data.

HumanTracker 也继承了其数据分布的限制。这四个系列是故意诊断的，但不平衡，地面剪辑比每日或交互剪辑少得多，并且评估使用 MuJoCo 中的一个 29-DoF 人形实体。结果不应被解释为对所有人类活动的人口估计或硬件稳健性的证据。因此，将基准扩展到其他实施例、真实机器人的推出和罕见的接触状态是自然而然的下一步。在这项工作中，我们还将 HumanScore 限制为评估。直接优化学习分数可以创建利用模型缺陷的行为；将其用作强化学习奖励将需要明确的正则化和独立的人类评估，而不是假设基准对齐自动转移到策略优化。HumanTracker also inherits the limits of its data distribution. The four families are deliberately diagnostic but imbalanced, with far fewer Ground clips than Daily or Interaction clips, and the evaluation uses one 29-DoF humanoid embodiment in MuJoCo. Results should not be interpreted as a population estimate over all human activity or as evidence of hardware robustness. Extending the benchmark to additional embodiments, real-robot rollouts and rare contact regimes is therefore a natural next step. We also restrict HumanScore to evaluation in this work. Directly optimizing a learned score can create behaviours that exploit model imperfections; using it as a reinforcement-learning reward will require explicit regularization and an independent human evaluation rather than assuming that benchmark alignment transfers automatically to policy optimization.
