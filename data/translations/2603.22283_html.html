1 简介Introduction

现代基础模型Modern foundation models(Brown et al., 2020; Koroteev, 2021; Radford et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2023; Esser et al., 2024; Polyak et al., 2024; Wan et al., 2025)从语言模型到视频生成器、视觉语言系统和科学生成模型，都是围绕两个核心操作构建的：标记化和生成。标记化将高维观察结果映射到紧凑的潜在空间中，从而实现忠实的重建和有效的区分；一代学习该空间的分布以合成合理的新样本。这种划分自然暗示了一个顺序的秘诀：首先学习一个易于重构且对下游计算有用的表示空间；然后学习从该空间采样的生成过程。因此，大多数系统将标记化和生成视为—from language models to video generators, vision–language systems, and scientific generative models—are built around two core operations: tokenization and generation. Tokenization maps high-dimensional observations into a compact latent space that enables both faithful reconstruction and efficient discrimination; generation learns a distribution over this space to synthesize plausible new samples. This division naturally suggests a sequential recipe: first learn a representation space that is easy to reconstruct from and useful for downstream computation; then learn a generative process that samples from that space. As a result, most systems treat tokenization and generation as分开separate设计问题并分阶段训练它们——学习分词器，冻结它，然后在诱导的潜在分布上拟合生成器。design problems & train them in stages—learning a tokenizer, freezing it & only then fitting a generator on the induced latent distribution.

这种分离虽然方便，但背离了端到端学习的原则，留下了一个基本问题没有解决：This separation is convenient, but it departs from the principle of end-to-end learning and leaves a basic question unresolved:是否应该联合训练标记化和生成，以便每个目标都可以塑造学习的潜在空间should tokenization and generation be trained jointly so that each objective can shape the learned latent space？在联合环境中，生成压力可以将潜在空间塑造为更容易建模的区域，而重建和推理压力可以保留特定于实例的信息和语义结构。了解当这些目标一起训练时会出现什么，以及它们的相互作用是有益还是有害，是这项工作的起点。? In a joint setting, generative pressure could sculpt the latent space toward regions that are easier to model, while reconstruction and inference pressure could preserve instance-specific information and semantic structure. Understanding what emerges when these objectives are trained together—and whether their interaction helps or hurts—is the starting point of this work.

3.3 了解 UNITE 的培训动力Understanding UNITE’s Training Dynamics

联合训练的对抗性。The adversarial nature of joint training.

在权重共享下联合训练标记化和生成会产生不平凡的动态。在标准 LDM 中，潜在空间是由预训练（通常是冻结的）分词器生成的，因此生成目标不会塑造潜在接口。在UNITE中，重建和生成目标在相同的参数上联合优化，因此每个目标都可以影响另一个目标使用的表示。Jointly training tokenization and generation under weight sharing induces non-trivial dynamics. In a standard LDM, the latent space is produced by a pretrained (and typically frozen) tokenizer, so the generative objective does not shape the latent interface. In UNITE, reconstruction and generative objectives are optimized jointly over the same parameters, so each objective can influence the representations used by the other.

这种动态最好理解为寻找一个潜在空间，以满足塑造其结构的两种不同压力。 重建目标驱动编码器最大化信息内容，防止潜在表示变得太粗糙而无法捕获特定于实例的细节。 同时，生成目标限制了这些信息的编码方式：它会惩罚学习脆弱的表示，这些表示的语义内容很容易被噪声破坏，因为这种不稳定性使得去噪变得更加困难。 因此，联合优化平衡了这些压力，找到了一个足够丰富的潜在空间来进行重建，同时又足够鲁棒以抵抗扰动。 通过迫使编码器采用这种鲁棒的几何结构，生成损失有效地将潜在空间塑造成本质上更容易去噪的空间，从而促进高保真生成。This dynamic is best understood as the search for a latent space that satisfies two distinct pressures shaping its structure. The reconstruction objective drives the encoder to maximize information content, preventing the latent representation from becoming too coarse to capture instance-specific detail. Simultaneously, the generative objective constrains how this information is encoded: it penalizes learning fragile representations whose semantic content can be easily destroyed by noise, since such instability makes denoising harder. Consequently, joint optimization balances these pressures, finding a latent space that is rich enough for reconstruction yet robust enough against perturbations. By forcing the encoder to adopt this robust geometry, the generative loss effectively molds the latent space into one that is intrinsically easier to denoise—facilitating high-fidelity generation.

根据经验，这种相互作用可以类似于“对抗性”游戏：两种损失不一定同时单调减少。 生成保真度的提高甚至可能与去噪损失的增加同时发生，如图 2 所示。Empirically, this interaction can resemble an “adversarial” game: the two losses do not necessarily decrease monotonically together. Crucially, a rising denoising loss does not imply worse generation. Instead, it often signals that the latent space is becoming richer and more informative to satisfy the reconstruction objective, making the denoising task harder but the resulting samples more realistic. During training, we often observe generation metrics (e.g., FID/IS) improving even as the denoising loss increases, until the system reaches a stable equilibrium. 6 结论Conclusion

我们推出 UNITE，一种联合代币化和生成的统一方法。我们的编码器称为生成编码器，既充当分词器又充当潜在降噪器，两个目标共享权重。这种共享参数化允许重建和生成梯度共同塑造表示空间，从而鼓励支持这两项任务的共同潜在“语言”。 UNITE 在单个阶段进行端到端训练，每次迭代都会通过同一生成编码器执行两次前向传递：一次用于标记化/重建，一次用于潜在去噪。在 ImageNet 和分子生成过程中，UNITE 实现了近乎最先进的保真度：基本模型在 ImageNet 256 上达到 2.12 gFIDWe present UNITE, a unified approach to joint tokenization and generation. Our encoder, termed the Generative Encoder, serves as both tokenizer and latent denoiser, with weights shared across the two objectives. This shared parameterization allows reconstruction and generation gradients to jointly shape the representation space, encouraging a common latent “language” that supports both tasks. UNITE is trained end-to-end in a single stage, with each iteration performing two forward passes through the same Generative Encoder: one for tokenization/reconstruction and one for latent denoising. Across ImageNet and molecule generation, UNITE achieves near-state-of-the-art fidelity: the base model reaches 2.12 gFID on ImageNet 256×\times256，缩放至 XL 将其提高至 1.75 gFID。我们通过表示对齐和压缩的镜头进一步分析生成编码器。256, and scaling to XL improves this to 1.75 gFID. We further analyze the Generative Encoder through the lenses of representation alignment and compression.

更广泛地说，我们的结果提出了两个实际意义。首先，它消除了对 DINO 等预训练编码器进行生成建模的依赖，为在此类编码器不可用的领域中进行潜在生成建模打开了大门。其次，我们的统一架构比传统的两级管道更简单、更高效，降低了实现复杂性和总体计算要求。More broadly, our results suggest two practical implications. First, it removes the reliance on pretrained encoders such as DINO for generative modeling, opening the door to latent generative modeling in domains where such encoders are unavailable. Second, our unified architecture is simpler and more efficient than conventional two-stage pipelines, reducing both implementation complexity and overall computational requirements.
