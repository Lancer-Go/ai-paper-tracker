摘要Abstract

人工智能系统越来越多地参与自身改进：修订 他们的输出，在期间调整和发展自己的工具 部署、对其生成的数据进行培训，以及——在不断增长的情况下 研究线索——进行人工智能研究本身。文献 描述这种参与已经爆炸式增长，但在一个词汇下 （“自我完善”、“自我奖励”、“自我游戏”、“自我进化”） 融合了根本不同的野心。我们调查了 1,250 个 arXiv 论文（2024-2026）并沿两个轴组织它们：AI systems increasingly participate in their own improvement: revising their outputs, adapting and evolving their own harnesses during deployment, training on data they generate, and — in a growing research thread — conducting AI research itself. 9. 结论9. Conclusion

我们通过一项调查调查了 1,250 篇最近关于人工智能自我完善的论文 两轴分类法——系统改进了什么（产出、政策、 脚手架、研究过程）以及由谁验证改进 - 并认为轴结构解决了“自我X”的问题 词汇模糊：有界自我完善和开放式递归 自我完善是具有不同证据基础的不同现象， 不同的理论和不同的风险状况。We surveyed 1,250 recent papers on AI self-improvement through a two-axis taxonomy — what the system improves (outputs, policy, scaffolding, the research process) and who validates the improvement — and argued that the axis structure resolves what the “self-X” vocabulary obscures: bounded self-refinement and open-ended recursive self-improvement are different phenomena with different evidence bases, different theory, and different risk profiles.

证据整理得很清楚。有界的自我完善是一门工程 成功：推理时间循环在接地时可靠地提高输出 外部信号；训练时间循环持续这些收益并且是 工业实践；代理商积累技能和经验 剧集；和进化发现系统产生文物 - 算法、内核、数学结构——反馈到 人工智能发展本身。相比之下，开放式 RSI 仍受限制 我们可以测量的每一面：理论上通过接地要求和 通过塌陷动力学凭经验计算弹性，并在实践中 由于判断的不可验证性（做什么工作、做什么） 算作更好），这将使循环自给自足。The evidence sorts cleanly. Bounded self-refinement is an engineering success: inference-time loops reliably improve outputs when grounded in external signals; training-time loops persist those gains and are industrial practice; agents accumulate skills and experience across episodes; and evolutionary discovery systems produce artifacts — algorithms, kernels, mathematical constructions — that feed back into AI development itself. Open-ended RSI, by contrast, remains bounded on every side we can measure: theoretically by grounding requirements and compute elasticities, empirically by collapse dynamics, and practically by the non-verifiability of exactly the judgments (what to work on, what counts as better) that would make the loop self-sufficient.

连接每个类别的唯一线索是自我完善 真实性取决于其验证。该框架改变了起飞 猜测测量程序的问题：看不 基准分数但验证层次结构 - 特别是， 系统是否成为开放式研究的可靠评估者 判断。到那时，人类在循环中的角色不再是感性的 保留；它是该领域最后的验证层。的 这里调查的文献表明该层将承重 需要一段时间——并小心翼翼地构建其最终的替代品 可以衡量的是，这十年的人工智能安全研究项目可能是最重要的 依赖。The single thread connecting every category is that self-improvement is only as real as its verification. That framing converts the takeoff question from speculation into a measurement program: watch not benchmark scores but the verification hierarchy — in particular, whether systems become reliable evaluators of open-ended research judgment. Until then, the human role in the loop is not a sentimental holdover; it is the field’s verification layer of last resort. The literature surveyed here suggests that layer will be load-bearing for some time — and that building its eventual replacement, carefully and measurably, is the research program this decade’s AI safety may most depend on.
