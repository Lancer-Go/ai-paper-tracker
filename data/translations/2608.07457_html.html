Although the two AIs share the same well-defined (decoding) temperature, the subordinate neither copies its boss nor returns to how it behaves on its own; instead, it adopts an entirely different behavior. This suggests more broadly that in networks of interacting AIs, who communicates with whom, which models interact, and their decoding temperatures will become control parameters in a new arena of out-of-equilibrium physics.
