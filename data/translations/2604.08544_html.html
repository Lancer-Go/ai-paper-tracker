摘要Abstract

对可变形物体的机器人操纵代表了体现学习中的数据密集型机制，其中形状、接触和拓扑以远远超过刚体可变性的方式共同演化。尽管模拟有望减轻现实世界数据采集的成本，但流行的模拟到真实管道仍然植根于刚体抽象，产生不匹配的几何形状、脆弱的软动力学和不适合布料交互的运动基元。 我们认为模拟之所以失败，不是因为它是综合性的，而是因为它没有根据。 为了解决这个问题，我们引入Robotic manipulation with deformable objects represents a data-intensive regime in embodied learning, where shape, contact, and topology co-evolve in ways that far exceed the variability of rigids. Although simulation promises relief from the cost of real-world data acquisition, prevailing sim-to-real pipelines remain rooted in rigid-body abstractions, producing mismatched geometry, fragile soft dynamics, and motion primitives poorly suited for cloth interaction. We posit that simulation fails not for being synthetic, but for being ungrounded. To address this, we introduce卡1SIM1，一个物理一致的真实到模拟到真实的数据引擎，将模拟建立在物理世界中。在有限的演示下，该系统将场景数字化为度量一致的双胞胎，通过弹性建模校准可变形动力学，并通过基于扩散的轨迹生成和质量过滤来扩展行为。 该管道将​​稀疏观察结果转化为具有接近演示保真度的规模化综合监督。 实验表明，基于纯合成数据训练的策略以 1:15 的等比实现了与真实数据基线的同等水平，同时在实际部署中实现了 90% 的零样本成功率和 50% 的泛化收益。 这些结果验证了物理对齐模拟作为可变形操纵的可扩展监督和数据高效政策学习的实用途径。, a physics-aligned real-to-sim-to-real data engine that grounds simulation in the physical world. Given limited demonstrations, the system digitizes scenes into metric-consistent twins, calibrates deformable dynamics through elastic modeling, and expands behaviors via diffusion-based trajectory generation with quality filtering. This pipeline transforms sparse observations into scaled synthetic supervision with near-demonstration fidelity. Experiments show that policies trained on purely synthetic data achieve parity with real-data baselines at a 1:15 equivalence ratio, while delivering 90% zero-shot success and 50% generalization gains in real-world deployment. These results validate physics-aligned simulation as scalable supervision for deformable manipulation and a practical pathway for data-efficient policy learning.

1 简介Introduction

对可变形物体（尤其是服装）的灵巧操纵支撑着人类的广泛日常活动，并且仍然是机器人技术的前沿领域。 最新进展Dexterous manipulation of deformable objects, notably garments, underpins a wide spectrum of daily human activities and remains a frontier for robotics. Recent advances[Black et al., 2024, Cai et al., 2026, Intelligence et al., 2025, Bjorck et al., 2025]揭示了一个明显的扩展趋势，即扩展真实机器人数据集不断提高性能，强化了数据扩展作为体现学习的主要驱动力的日益增长的共识。 同时 这个范例显示出希望 在刚性物体环境中，可变形操纵加剧了对数据的渴望，因为其不断变化的几何形状和接触丰富的动力学需要更广泛的状态和视觉覆盖范围。 然而，大规模获取现实世界的操纵数据仍然昂贵得令人望而却步。 这样的努力 需要熟练的操作员、专业的硬件和大量的人力，这对数据集规模造成了实际限制。 因此，在有限的可变形交互数据上训练的视觉语言动作模型表现出有限的泛化能力。reveal a clear scaling trend whereby expanding real-robot datasets consistently improves performance, reinforcing the growing consensus on data scaling as a primary driver of embodied learning. While this paradigm shows promise in rigid-object settings, deformable manipulation intensifies the hunger for data, as its evolving geometry and contact-rich dynamics demand substantially broader state and visual coverage. Yet, acquiring real-world manipulation data at scale remains prohibitively expensive. Such efforts demands skilled operators, specialized hardware, and extensive human labor, placing practical limits on dataset scale. Consequently, vision-language-action models trained on limited deformable-interaction data exhibit constrained generalization.

在该领域，模拟到真实 (S2R) 合成数据生成已成为扩展操作数据的引人注目的策略In the field, sim-to-real (S2R) synthetic data generation has emerged as a compelling strategy for scaling manipulation data[Gu et al., 2023, Ye et al., , He et al., 2025, Xue et al., 2025, Deng et al., 2025]。 在刚性对象领域，在丰富的资产库和自动生成管道的支持下，不断增加的合成多样性通常会转化为可衡量的现实世界收益. In rigid-object domains, increasing synthetic diversity often translates into measurable real-world gains, supported by rich asset libraries and automated generation pipelines[Tian et al., 2025, Chen et al., 2025a, Nasiriany et al., ]。 然而，这种范式在变形操纵中失效了。. 5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一种物理对齐的真实到模拟到真实的管道，将有限的真实演示转换为可扩展的合成数据，以进行可变形操作。通过共同解决几何精度、动态保真度和运动合成，我们的方法实现了服装折叠中的零次模拟到真实的转换。实验表明，纯粹基于合成数据训练的策略可以实现与真实数据基线相当的性能，并且随着数据规模的扩大而持续改进。这些结果表明，高保真模拟是可变形操纵的可行且可扩展的监督来源，可以补充真实数据收集并以更低的成本实现更广泛的政策学习。We present a physics-aligned real-to-sim-to-real pipeline that transforms limited real demonstrations into scalable synthetic data for deformable manipulation. By jointly addressing geometric accuracy, dynamic fidelity, and motion synthesis, our approach achieves zero-shot sim-to-real transfer in garment folding. Experiments demonstrate that policies trained purely on synthetic data achieve comparable performance to real-data baselines, with consistent improvement as data scales. These results show that high-fidelity simulation is a viable and scalable source of supervision for deformable manipulation, complementing real-data collection and enabling broader policy learning at reduced cost.

局限性和更广泛的影响。Limitations and broader impact.当前的限制是材料校准需要专家指导对每种资产进行参数调整，这限制了任意布料类型的完全自动化。 通过证明高保真模拟可以补充真实的机器人数据并实现可扩展的策略学习，这项工作为数据高效的机器人开发提供了实用的基础。A current limitation is that material calibration requires expert-guided parameter tuning for each asset, which constrains full automation across arbitrary cloth types. By demonstrating that high-fidelity simulation can complement real-robot data and enable scalable policy learning, this work provides a practical foundation for data-efficient robotic development.

D.5 失败案例Failure Cases

我们的实验表明，政策绩效对生成数据的质量高度敏感。当低质量或无效样本进入训练集时，由于判别器错误，它们可能会毒害模型，导致系统故障。具体来说，损坏的样品通常会导致不合理的行为，例如过度伸手、接触前夹具过早关闭或抓取不对齐。相比之下，真正的分布外场景主要由于有限的泛化而产生失败，例如缺少服装边缘或相对于对象的错误定位。在我们的管道中，鉴别器在过滤无效轨迹方面取得了超过 99% 的成功，并且可以通过简单的基于规则的检查进一步提高其性能，确保合成数据集保持高度可靠且损坏程度最低。Our experiments indicate that policy performance is highly sensitive to the quality of generated data. When low-quality or invalid samples enter the training set, due to discriminator errors, they can poison the model, leading to systematic failures. Specifically, corrupted samples often result in unreasonable behaviors such as overreaching, premature gripper closure before contact, or misaligned grasps. In contrast, genuine out-of-distribution scenarios primarily produce failures due to limited generalization, such as missing the garment edge or mispositioning relative to the object. In our pipeline, the discriminator achieves over 99% success in filtering invalid trajectories, and its performance can be further improved with simple rule-based checks, ensuring that the synthetic dataset remains highly reliable and minimally corrupted.
