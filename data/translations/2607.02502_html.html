摘要Abstract

按策略自蒸馏 (OPSD) 已成为训练大型语言模型 (LLM) 进行推理的实用方法，其中单个模型既充当教师又充当学生，具有不同级别的信息访问权限。 然而，最近的研究发现，教师以特权信息为条件的密集令牌级监督可能会导致对域内模式的过度拟合，抑制探索并损害跨域泛化，同时还引入了一个更根本的问题：On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) has emerged as a practical method for training large language models (LLMs) to reason, where a single model acts as both the teacher and the student with different levels of information access. However, recent studies have found that the teacher’s dense token-level supervision, conditioned on privileged information, can lead to overfitting to in-domain patterns, suppress exploration, and hurt cross-domain generalization, while also introducing a more fundamental issue:特权信息泄露privileged information leakage，其中学生对测试时不可用的依赖于答案的快捷方式进行编码。 我们介绍, where the student encodes answer-dependent shortcuts that are unavailable at test time. We introduce演示PSDDemoPSD，一个新颖的框架，通过以下思想解决此类问题, a novel framework that resolves such problems through the idea of有选择地采用教师指导selective adoption of teacher guidance：当学生的分布保持相当一致时，学生会采用教师的指导；而当学生的分布明显偏离时，学生会更多地依赖自己的推理，这表明教师的输出过度受到特权信息的影响。 DemoPSD 不是拟合完整的教师分布，而是引导学生走向: the student adopts the teacher’s guidance when their distributions remain reasonably consistent, and relies more on its own reasoning when their distributions substantially diverge, indicating that the teacher’s output is overly influenced by privileged information. Instead of fitting the full teacher distribution, DemoPSD steers the student toward a反向KL重心目标reverse-KL barycenter target，教师和学生分布的加权几何组合，自然地平衡了教师的学习与保留学生自己的推理能力。我们测量它们分布之间的差异，并使用这种差异来自适应控制每个令牌位置的混合。 我们证明 DemoPSD 实现了, a weighted geometric combination of the teacher and student distributions, that naturally balances learning from the teacher with preserving the student’s own reasoning capacity. We measure the difference between their distributions and use such a discrepancy to adaptively control the blending at each token position. We provably show that DemoPSD achieves(1) 泄漏衰减leakage attenuation，即有效缓解特权信息泄露；和, i.e., effective mitigation of privileged information leakage; and(2) 探索保存exploration preservation，即在密集代币级蒸馏下保留探索能力。对 SciKnowEval 跨越四个科学领域的广泛实验表明，DemoPSD 的性能优于 GRPO 和 SDPO，同时保持较高的训练熵并稳健地推广到分布外 GPQA 基准。, i.e., preservation of exploration capacity under dense token-level distillation. Extensive experiments on SciKnowEval across four scientific fields show that DemoPSD outperforms both GRPO and SDPO while maintaining higher training entropy and robustly generalizing to out-of-distribution GPQA benchmarks.

1 简介Introduction

具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）已成为推理任务后训练大型语言模型的中心范例Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has become a central paradigm for post-training large language models on reasoning tasks(Shao et al., 2024, DeepSeek-AI, 2025, Yu et al., 2026a)。组相对策略优化 (GRPO) 等方法通过对每个问题的多次部署进行采样并使用结果正确性作为奖励信号来训练模型。 RLVR 虽然有效，但存在一个根本问题：. Methods such as Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) train models by sampling multiple rollouts per question and using outcome correctness as a reward signal. While effective, RLVR suffers from a fundamental信用分配瓶颈credit assignment bottleneck：标准 RLVR 方法在部署中的所有代币之间均匀分配部署级别奖励，提供粗略的代币级别信用信号，无法区分各个代币的贡献: standard RLVR methods distribute a rollout-level reward uniformly among all tokens in a rollout, offering coarse token-level credit signals that fail to distinguish individual token contributions(Hübotter et al., 2026).

在策略蒸馏（OPD）通过从教师模型中引入对学生自我生成轨迹的密集、代币级监督来解决这一瓶颈On-policy distillation (OPD) addresses this bottleneck by introducing dense, token-level supervision from a teacher model on the student’s self-generated trajectories(Agarwal et al., 2024, Gu et al., 2024, Lu and Thinking Machines Lab, 2025)。与离策略蒸馏不同，离策略蒸馏是在教师生成的文本上进行训练并遭受复合曝光偏差的影响. Unlike off-policy distillation, which trains on teacher-generated texts and suffers from compounding exposure bias(Ross et al., 2011)，OPD 允许学生从自己的分布中学习，同时获得丰富的反馈。这种范式已被业界广泛采用，包括 Qwen3, OPD allows the student to learn from its own distribution while receiving rich feedback. This paradigm has been widely adopted in industry, including Qwen3(Qwen Team, 2025)和 DeepSeek-V4and DeepSeek-V4(DeepSeek-AI, 2026)，建立 OPD 作为 RLVR 的实际补充。, establishing OPD as a practical complement to RLVR.

一个特别有吸引力的变体是按策略自蒸馏（OPSD）A particularly appealing variant is on-policy self-distillation (OPSD)(e.g., Zhao et al., 2026, Hübotter et al., 2026)，其中单个模型既充当教师又充当学生。教师是同样的模型，以特权信息为条件，例如经过验证的推理轨迹或真实答案，而学生只收到问题。 OPSD 消除了对外部教师的需求，并且已证明代币效率比 GRPO 提高了数倍, where a single model serves as both teacher and student. The teacher is the same model conditioned on privileged information, such as a verified reasoning trace or ground-truth answer, while the student receives only the question. 7 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 DemoPSD，一个基于选择性采用教师指导的自我蒸馏框架：DemoPSD 构建了一个反向 KL 重心目标，自适应地平衡教师指导与学生自己的推理能力，而不是强迫学生在每个标记上模仿特权教师。我们的分析表明，依赖分歧的重心权重直接调节训练梯度中教师诱导的信号：低分歧标记保留密集的教师监督，而高分歧标记接受减弱的特权指导。我们通过泄漏衰减和探索保留将这种行为形式化，展示了所提出的学习目标如何减少模仿特权信息的压力，同时保持更高的熵监督。根据经验，DemoPSD 在四个科学领域比 SDPO 和 GRPO 有所改进，保持了 35-98% 的较高训练熵，并且可以稳健地推广到分布外基准。We introduced DemoPSD, a self-distillation framework based on selective adoption of teacher guidance: instead of forcing the student to imitate the privileged teacher at every token, DemoPSD constructs a reverse-KL barycenter target that adaptively balances teacher guidance with the student’s own reasoning capacity. Our analysis shows that the disagreement-dependent barycenter weight directly modulates the teacher-induced signal in the training gradient: low-disagreement tokens retain dense teacher supervision, whereas high-disagreement tokens receive attenuated privileged guidance. We formalized this behavior through leakage attenuation and exploration preservation, showing how the proposed learning target reduces pressure to imitate privileged information while maintaining higher-entropy supervision. Empirically, DemoPSD improves over SDPO and GRPO across four scientific domains, maintains 35–98% higher training entropy, and generalizes robustly to out-of-distribution benchmarks.
