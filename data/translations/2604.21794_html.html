学习沟通：走向多代理语言系统的端到端优化Learning to Communicate: Toward End-to-End Optimization of Multi-Agent Language Systems

Ye Yu1, Heming Liu1, Haibo Jin1, Xiaopeng Yuan1, Peng Kuang1, Haohan Wang1
1 University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, IL, USA Corresponding author: haohanw@illinois.edu
摘要Abstract

基于大型语言模型构建的多代理系统在复杂的推理任务上表现出了强大的性能，但大多数工作都集中在代理角色和编排上，同时将代理间通信视为固定接口。通过键值缓存等内部表示进行的潜在通信为基于文本的协议提供了一种有前景的替代方案，但现有方法并未与多代理推理联合优化通信。因此，我们提出了 DiffMAS，这是一种将潜在通信视为多智能体系统的可学习组件的训练框架。 DiffMAS 对多智能体潜在轨迹执行参数高效的监督训练，使智能体能够共同学习如何跨交互编码和解释信息。数学推理、科学 QA、代码生成和常识基准测试表明，与单智能体推理、基于文本的多智能体系统和先前的潜在通信相比，DiffMAS 持续提高了推理准确性和解码稳定性 方法，在 AIME24 上实现 +26.7%， GPQA-Diamond 增加 20.2%，并且在推理基准测试中持续获得收益。Multi-agent systems built on large language models have shown strong performance on complex reasoning tasks, yet most work focuses on agent roles and orchestration while treating inter-agent communication as a fixed interface. Latent communication through internal representations such as key–value caches offers a promising alternative to text-based protocols, but existing approaches do not jointly optimize communication with multi-agent reasoning. Therefore we propose DiffMAS, a training framework that treats latent communication as a learnable component of multi-agent systems. DiffMAS performs parameter-efficient supervised training over multi-agent latent trajectories, enabling agents to jointly learn how information should be encoded and interpreted across interactions. Experiments on mathematical reasoning, scientific QA, code generation, and commonsense benchmarks show that DiffMAS consistently improves reasoning accuracy and decoding stability over single-agent inference, text-based multi-agent systems, and prior latent communication methods, achieving +26.7% on AIME24, +20.2% on GPQA-Diamond, and consistent gains across reasoning benchmarks.

1 简介Introduction

基于大型语言模型构建的多智能体系统 (MAS) 已成为解决复杂推理任务的强大范例，包括数学、编程和常识性问题解决Multi-agent systems (MAS) built on large language models have emerged as a powerful paradigm for solving complex reasoning tasks, including mathematics, programming, and commonsense problem solving(Zhang et al., 2025a; b)。它们的有效性通常归因于两个互补因素：. Their effectiveness is commonly attributed to two complementary factors:(i) 系统结构和代理角色分配(i) system structure and agent role assignment (Chen et al., 2024)，它决定了代理的组织方式和专业化方式，以及, which determine how agents are organized and specialized, and(ii) 代理间通信(ii) inter-agent communication (Chen et al., 2025; Shen et al., 2025)，它控制中间推理状态和信息的交换方式。通过分解复杂的任务, which governs how intermediate reasoning states and information are exchanged. By decomposing complex tasks(Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023)，多智能体系统始终优于单智能体基线。, multi-agent systems consistently outperform single-agent baselines.

随着多智能体语言系统的不断成熟，最近的研究越来越多地探索如何通过优化来系统地提高其性能As multi-agent language systems continue to mature, recent research has increasingly explored how to systematically improve their performance through optimization(Wang et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025a; b; Chen et al., 2024)。现有的工作主要集中在通过微调或提示来提高代理能力，以及通过更好的角色分配、规划策略和执行结构来完善工作流程设计. Existing efforts have primarily focused on improving agentic capabilities through fine-tuning or prompting, as well as refining workflow design through better role assignment, planning strategies, and execution structures(Shinn et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2025b; Chen et al., 2024)。这些进步带来了更强大的代理和更有效的系统结构。. These advances have led to more capable agents and more effective system structures.

然而，此类改进很大程度上将多智能体系统视为优化组件的模块化组合，而不是其相互作用本身可以学习的系统However, such improvements largely treat multi-agent systems as modular compositions of optimized components, rather than systems whose interactions can themselves be learned(Zhuge et al., 2024; Shen et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025)。因此，下一步自然是探索多智能体系统是否可以更全面地优化，不仅允许智能体行为和工作流程，还允许它们的交互通过学习得到改善. A natural next step is therefore to explore whether multi-agent systems can be optimized more holistically, allowing not only agent behaviors and workflows, but also their interactions to improve through learning(Motwani et al., 2025; Ma et al., 2025)。实现这一愿景需要超越固定的系统界面，转向可以与代理推理一起优化的交互机制。由于不同的任务可能对如何表示和保留中间推理状态提出不同的要求，因此有效的通信策略需要适应任务，而不是依赖固定的协议. Achieving this vision requires moving beyond fixed system interfaces toward interaction mechanisms that can be optimized together with agentic reasoning. As different tasks can place different demands on how intermediate reasoning states should be represented and preserved, effective communication strategies need to adapt to the task rather than relying on fixed protocols(Chen et al., 2025; Shen et al., 2025)。在这种情况下，使通信成为可学习的组件代表着迈向更全面优化的多智能体推理系统的关键一步。. In this context, enabling communication to become a learnable component represents a critical step towards more fully optimizable multi-agent reasoning systems.

使通信变得可学习的一个关键障碍在于当前代理如何交换信息的离散性。在大多数现有的多智能体系统中，中间推理结果必须先解码为自然语言，然后才能传输给下游智能体A key obstacle to making communication learnable lies in the discrete nature of how agents currently exchange information. In most existing multi-agent systems, intermediate reasoning results must be decoded into natural language before being transmitted to downstream agents(Chen et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2023)。虽然这种设计自然地与基于标记的语言模型接口保持一致，但它引入了一个基本限制：离散消息传递在代理之间创建了优化边界. While this design aligns naturally with the token-based interfaces of language models, it introduces a fundamental limitation: discrete message passing creates optimization boundaries between agents(Sukhbaatar et al., 2016; Foerster et al., 2016; Mordatch and Abbeel, 2018; Havrylov and Titov, 2017)。这种限制尤其具有限制性，因为系统的性能关键取决于中间推理结果在代理之间的保存和解释程度. This limitation is particularly restrictive as the performance of the system depends critically on how well intermediate reasoning results are preserved and interpreted across agents(Deng et al., 2023; Wei et al., 2023)。更根本的是，这反映了推理期间使用的连续内部表示与用于代理间交换的离散消息之间的不匹配. More fundamentally, this reflects a mismatch between the continuous internal representations used during reasoning and the discrete messages used for inter-agent exchange(Zelikman et al., 2024; Hao et al., 2025; Fu et al., 2025; Zou et al., 2025)。为了使通信真正可学习并实现多智能体系统的更多端到端优化，智能体间信息交换必须在可以支持可微分信息流的连续表示上进行操作。. To make communication truly learnable and enable more end-to-end optimization of multi-agent systems, inter-agent information exchange must therefore operate over continuous representations that can support differentiable information flow.

键值 (KV) 缓存提供了一种实现这种可微分通信的自然机制Key–Value (KV) caches provide a natural mechanism for enabling such differentiable communication(Vaswani et al., 2023)。作为由 Transformer 注意层产生的连续内部表示，KV 状态可以直接被下游代理重用，而不需要离散解码或重新编码. As continuous internal representations produced by transformer attention layers, KV states can be directly reused by downstream agents without requiring discrete decoding or re-encoding(Kwon et al., 2023)。这允许中间推理表示通过连续通道传输，该通道与基于梯度的学习保持兼容。通过将 KV 缓存视为潜在的通信接口，梯度不仅可以在各个代理内传播，还可以跨代理边界传播，从而允许与底层模型一起优化通信行为. This allows intermediate reasoning representations to be transmitted through a continuous channel that remains compatible with gradient-based learning. By treating KV caches as a latent communication interface, gradients can propagate not only within individual agents but also across agent boundaries, allowing communication behaviors to be optimized together with the underlying model(Motwani et al., 2025; Zhuge et al., 2024; Ma et al., 2025).

2.1 多代理法学硕士系统Multi-Agent LLM Systems

基于 LLM 的多智能体系统通过跨专业智能体和结构化交互工作流程分解问题来改进复杂推理。之前的工作探索了通过角色工程、自动化工作流程构建和架构搜索来提高代理能力和系统设计LLM-based multi-agent systems improve complex reasoning by decomposing problems across specialized agents and structured interaction workflows. Prior work has explored improving agent capabilities and system design through role engineering, automated workflow construction, and architecture search(Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023; Qian et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025b; a)。其他研究侧重于通过执行策略或自我配置来改善协调. 尽管取得了这些进步，大多数多智能体系统仍依赖自然语言作为通用通信接口Despite these advances, most multi-agent systems rely on natural language as a universal communication interface(Zhang et al., 2025b; Chen et al., 2023)。虽然灵活的基于文本的通信需要将内部推理状态序列化为离散标记. While flexible, text-based communication requires internal reasoning states to be serialized into discrete tokens(Wei et al., 2023)，这会限制信息保真度并阻止与代理推理联合优化通信, which can limit information fidelity and prevent communication from being optimized jointly with agent reasoning(Zelikman et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024; Ma et al., 2025)。这些限制提出了一个问题：更丰富的内部表示是否可以提供更好的沟通机制。. 3.1 问题设置和操作员视图Problem Setting and Operator View

我们提出了 DiffMAS，一个用于学习多智能体语言系统中智能体间通信的框架。我们考虑一个由以下组成的系统：We propose DiffMAS, a framework for learning inter-agent communication in multi-agent language systems.We consider a system composed ofKK通过中间推理步骤进行协作的顺序代理。与将通信视为固定协议的传统 MAS 不同，DiffMAS 将通信本身视为可学习计算的一部分。sequential agents that collaborate through intermediate reasoning steps. Unlike conventional MAS that treats communication as a fixed protocol, DiffMAS treats communication itself as part of the learnable computation.

直观上，这种设计有几个优点。首先，连续的潜在通信允许梯度在代理之间传播，从而使通信策略能够端到端优化。其次，保留中间表示可以避免过早压缩为离散令牌，从而允许传输更丰富的推理信号。第三，通过将通信构建为计算图的一部分，DiffMAS 使系统能够学习如何跨代理编码和解释信息。Intuitively, this design provides several advantages. First, continuous latent communication allows gradients to propagate across agents, enabling communication strategies to be optimized end-to-end. Second, preserving intermediate representations avoids premature compression into discrete tokens, allowing richer reasoning signals to be transmitted. Third, by constructing communication as part of the computation graph, DiffMAS enables the system to learn how information should be encoded and interpreted across agents.

7 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 DiffMAS，这是一种监督训练框架，它将潜在通信视为多智能体交互中的可学习算子。通过优化完整的潜在轨迹，DiffMAS 通过代理推理学习通信，从而提高了免训练潜在通信的稳定性。在推理、编码和常识基准测试中，DiffMAS 持续提高了单代理、基于文本和潜在基线的准确性和解码稳定性。更广泛地说，我们的结果强调潜在通信是多智能体系统的重要优化目标，并激励未来在具有端到端优化交互的完全可微 MAS 方面的工作。In this paper, we introduce DiffMAS, a supervised training framework that treats latent communication as a learnable operator within multi-agent interactions. By optimizing full latent trajectories, DiffMAS learns communication with agentic reasoning, improving stability over training-free latent communication. Across reasoning, coding, and commonsense benchmarks, DiffMAS consistently improves accuracy and decoding stability over single-agent, text-based, and latent baselines. More broadly, our results highlight latent communication as an important optimization target for multi-agent systems and motivate future work on fully differentiable MAS with end-to-end optimized interactions.

附录CAppendix C训练设置Training Setup

DiffMAS 直接在多智能体交互循环中进行训练，其中智能体间的潜在通信被视为模型调节上下文的一部分。在训练期间，骨干语言模型保持冻结状态，并且仅优化轻量级 LoRA 适配器，从而无需修改预训练的基础模型即可实现参数高效的自适应。DiffMAS is trained directly within the multi-agent interaction loop, where inter-agent latent communication is treated as part of the model’s conditioning context. During training, the backbone language model remains frozen, and only lightweight LoRA adapters are optimized, enabling parameter-efficient adaptation without modifying the pretrained base model.

对于每个训练实例，中间代理首先执行潜在推理步骤并生成对其内部计算进行编码的连续 KV 表示。这些潜在的 KV 状态在智能体之间顺序累积，并作为共享的潜在上下文传递给下游智能体，形成完整的多智能体推理轨迹。最终的输出代理以累积的潜在上下文及其特定于任务的提示为条件，并自回归生成目标输出。训练是在教师强制目标序列的情况下进行的，并且损失被计算为以完整潜在上下文和最终提示为条件的目标标记的负对数似然。提示标记和填充位置从损失中被屏蔽，确保梯度仅由最终输出的正确性驱动。通过这种方式，梯度通过潜在通信路径传播，允许模型共同适应上游代理如何将信息编码为 KV 状态以及下游代理如何解释它们。For each training instance, intermediate agents first perform latent reasoning steps and produce continuous KV representations that encode their internal computation. 附录EAppendix E案例研究Case Study

为了更好地理解代理间通信机制如何影响推理行为和解码稳定性，我们提出了一个定性案例研究，比较了三种代表性范式：自然语言（TextMAS）、不带自适应的原始 KV 缓存（LatentMAS）和我们学习的潜在 KV 通信（DiffMAS）。我们分析了 AIME 2024 的代表性示例，在所有设置中使用相同的代理角色、提示和解码配置。 该分析补充了第 5 节中的定量结果To better understand how inter-agent communication mechanisms influence reasoning behavior and decoding stability, we present a qualitative case study comparing three representative paradigms: natural language (TextMAS), raw KV caches without adaptation (LatentMAS), and our learned latent KV communication (DiffMAS). We analyze a representative example from AIME 2024, using identical agent roles, prompts, and decoding configurations across all settings. This analysis complements the quantitative results in Section4.2通过检查通信设计如何影响实例级别的推理轨迹。by examining how communication design affects reasoning trajectories at the instance level.

基于文本的通信。Text-based Communication.

TextMAS 在三种范式中表现出最稳定的推理行为。由于预训练语言模型针对自然语言的下一个标记预测进行了优化，因此通过文本交换中间推理与模型的训练分布密切匹配。因此，解码往往保持良好的结构和逻辑组织。TextMAS exhibits the most stable inference behavior among the three paradigms. Since pretrained language models are optimized for next-token prediction over natural language, exchanging intermediate reasoning through text closely matches the model’s training distribution. As a result, decoding tends to remain well-structured and logically organized.

然而，这种稳健性是以代表性能力为代价的。在传递给下游代理之前，中间推理必须被压缩为离散的令牌。这种序列化过程不可避免地会引入信息丢失，特别是对于微妙的中间计算或隐式上下文信号。在复杂的数学推理问题中，我们观察到下游代理有时需要重建缺失的细节，这可能导致推理链不完整或解决方案精度降低。However, this robustness comes at the cost of representational capacity. Intermediate reasoning must be compressed into discrete tokens before being passed to downstream agents. This serialization process inevitably introduces information loss, particularly for subtle intermediate computations or implicit contextual signals. In complex mathematical reasoning problems, we observe that downstream agents sometimes need to reconstruct missing details, which can lead to incomplete reasoning chains or reduced solution accuracy.

无需培训的潜在沟通。Training-free Latent Communication.

LatentMAS 通过允许代理直接共享 KV 缓存来消除这个文本瓶颈。这使得下游代理能够处理更丰富的内部表示，从而保留几乎所有上游推理信息。在某些情况下，这种额外的表达能力允许代理捕获有用的中间结构，否则这些结构会在文本摘要中丢失。LatentMAS removes this textual bottleneck by allowing agents to directly share KV caches. This enables downstream agents to attend to richer internal representations that preserve nearly all upstream reasoning information. In some cases, this additional expressivity allows agents to capture useful intermediate structure that would otherwise be lost in textual summaries.

然而，我们观察到推理过程中存在很大的不稳定。中间推理步骤经常偏离主题、引入不相关的关联或不一致地跟踪变量。这些行为似乎源于分布不匹配：预训练的 Transformer 没有经过训练将外部注入的 KV 状态解释为语义通信接口。因此，潜在表征可以充当结构化但未经校准的信号，从而扰乱注意力模式。However, we observe substantial instability in the reasoning process. Intermediate reasoning steps frequently drift off-topic, introduce irrelevant associations, or inconsistently track variables. These behaviors appear to stem from a distribution mismatch: pretrained transformers are not trained to interpret externally injected KV states as a semantic communication interface. Consequently, latent representations can function as structured but uncalibrated signals that disrupt attention patterns.

在分析的例子中，这表现为混乱的推理轨迹，其中出现了正确的中间想法，但后来被放弃或误用。尽管某些推理片段在局部仍然可信，但全局推理过程常常变得不连贯，最终导致错误的最终答案。In the analyzed example, this manifests as chaotic reasoning trajectories in which correct intermediate ideas appear but are later abandoned or misused. Although some reasoning segments remain locally plausible, the global reasoning process often becomes incoherent, ultimately leading to incorrect final answers.

DiffMAS：学习的潜在沟通。DiffMAS: Learned Latent Communication.

DiffMAS 通过明确学习代理应如何在潜在空间中进行通信来解决这种稳定性与表达性的权衡。通过对完整的多智能体潜在推理轨迹进行监督微调，该模型共同学习中间智能体应如何将有用信息编码为 KV 状态以及下游智能体应如何解释这些表示。DiffMAS addresses this stability–expressivity tradeoff by explicitly learning how agents should communicate in latent space. Through supervised fine-tuning over full multi-agent latent reasoning trajectories, the model jointly learns both how intermediate agents should encode useful information into KV states and how downstream agents should interpret these representations.

在同一个 AIME 示例中，DiffMAS 产生更加连贯的推理行为。中间步骤仍然关注相关约束，变量定义在代理之间保持一致，并且推理轨迹遵循解决方案的清晰进展。与 LatentMAS 相比，我们观察到注意力失败和推理漂移更少。In the same AIME example, DiffMAS produces substantially more coherent reasoning behavior. Intermediate steps remain focused on relevant constraints, variable definitions remain consistent across agents, and the reasoning trajectory follows a clear progression toward the solution. Compared to LatentMAS, we observe fewer attention failures and less reasoning drift.

重要的是，DiffMAS 生成的最终答案是正确的，这表明监督训练使潜在通信与任务目标保持一致，而不是将其保留为隐式且脆弱的界面。这表明 DiffMAS 的主要优势不仅在于更丰富的表示，还在于能够构建跨代理生成和使用这些表示的方式。Importantly, the final answer produced by DiffMAS is correct, demonstrating that supervised training aligns latent communication with task objectives rather than leaving it as an implicit and brittle interface. This suggests that the primary advantage of DiffMAS is not merely richer representations, but the ability to structure how those representations are generated and consumed across agents.

观察总结。Summary of Observations.

本案例研究强调了多智能体通信设计中稳定性和表现力之间的基本权衡。基于文本的通信由于与预训练分布一致而提供了很强的稳定性，但会受到信息压缩的影响。免训练的潜在通信提高了表达能力，但由于分布不匹配而引入了不稳定。 DiffMAS 通过学习与任务一致的潜在通信协议来解决这种紧张关系，该协议在保持稳定的解码行为的同时保留表达能力。This case study highlights a fundamental tradeoff between stability and expressivity in multi-agent communication design. Text-based communication provides strong stability due to alignment with pretraining distributions but suffers from information compression. Training-free latent communication improves expressivity but introduces instability due to distribution mismatch. DiffMAS resolves this tension by learning a task-aligned latent communication protocol that preserves expressivity while maintaining stable decoding behavior.

这些定性观察结果与我们在解码稳定性、困惑度和自我一致性方面的定量发现一致，这表明 DiffMAS 实现了更高的推理准确性和更可靠的多智能体协调。These qualitative observations are consistent with our quantitative findings on decoding stability, perplexity, and self-consistency, which show that DiffMAS achieves both improved reasoning accuracy and more reliable multi-agent coordination.
