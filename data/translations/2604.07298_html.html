摘要Abstract

多实例学习 (MIL) 是十亿像素的主导框架 计算病理学中的全幻灯片图像（WSI）分类。然而，当前的 MIL 聚合器通过共享路径路由所有实例，限制了它们专门针对每张幻灯片固有的病理异质性的能力。专家混合 (MoE) 方法通过跨专门的专家子网络划分实例提供了一种自然的补救措施；然而，不受约束的 softmax 路由可能会产生高度不平衡的利用，其中一个或几个专家吸收了大部分路由质量，将混合物压缩回接近单路径的解决方案。 为了解决这些限制，我们提出 ROAM (Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) is the dominant framework for gigapixel whole-slide image (WSI) classification in computational pathology. However, current MIL aggregators route all instances through a shared pathway, constraining their capacity to specialise across the pathological heterogeneity inherent in each slide. Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) methods offer a natural remedy by partitioning instances across specialised expert subnetworks; yet unconstrained softmax routing may yield highly imbalanced utilisation, where one or a few experts absorb most routing mass, collapsing the mixture back to a near-single-pathway solution. To address these limitations, we propose ROAM (R区域图egion-graphO普蒂姆ptimAL-运输l-transportMixture-of-experts），一个空间感知的 MoE-MIL 聚合器，通过容量受限的熵最优传输将区域代币路由到专家池，通过构建促进平衡的专家利用率。 ROAM 在空间区域令牌上运行——通过将密集补丁包压缩成与局部组织邻域对齐的空间分箱单元来获得——并引入两个关键机制：（i）区域到专家分配，制定为熵最优传输（Sinkhorn），具有明确的每幻灯片容量边际，强制平衡专家利用率，而无需辅助负载平衡损失； (ii) 图正则化 Sinkhorn 迭代，将路由分配分散到空间区域图上，鼓励相邻区域一致地路由到相同的专家。 通过使用冻结的基础模型补丁嵌入对四个 WSI 基准进行评估，ROAM 的性能可与强大的 MIL 和 MoE 基准以及 NSCLC 泛化 (TCGA) 相媲美ixture-of-experts), a spatially aware MoE-MIL aggregator that routes region tokens to expert poolers via capacity-constrained entropic optimal transport, promoting balanced expert utilisation by construction. 1 简介Introduction

全幻灯片图像 (WSI) 包含具有极端形态和空间异质性的十亿像素级组织，其中诊断相关证据通常稀疏且局部聚集。随着病理学基础模型的兴起，将 WSI 表示为一袋冷冻补丁嵌入已成为标准Whole-slide images (WSIs) contain gigapixel-scale tissue with extreme morphological and spatial heterogeneity, where diagnostically relevant evidence is often sparse and locally clustered. With the rise of pathology foundation models, it has become standard to represent a WSI as a bag of frozen patch embeddings[3, 23]并在弱监督下学习滑动级预测器。 多实例学习 (MIL) 是此设置的主导框架and learn a slide-level predictor under weak supervision. Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) is the dominant framework for this setting[9, 2]：将每个补丁嵌入视为一个实例，跨包聚合（一个包 每张幻灯片），并仅在袋级监督的情况下预测幻灯片级标签。通常，门控注意力（ABMIL: treat each patch embedding as an instance, aggregate across the bag (one bag per slide), and predict a slide-level label with only bag-level supervision. Typically, gated attention (ABMIL[9]）学习实例级重要性权重。实例级集群（CLAM) learns instance-level importance weights. Instance-level clustering (CLAM[13]）在池化之前将实例划分为子组。双流架构（DSMIL) partitions instances into subgroups before pooling. Dual-stream architectures (DSMIL[12]）结合最大实例和注意力路径。基于 Transformer 的聚合器 (TransMIL) combine max-instance and attention pathways. Transformer-based aggregators (TransMIL[17]）模型成对实例相关性，而低秩近似（ILRA) model pairwise instance correlations, while low-rank approximations (ILRA[22]) 提高可扩展性。然而，所有这些方法都有一个结构限制：每个实例都通过单个共享路径进行处理，迫使形态和语义上不同的群体进入一个公共参数空间。这种合并限制了专业化和可解释性。) improve scalability. However, all these methods share a structural limitation: every instance is processed through a single shared pathway, forcing morphologically and semantically distinct populations into a common parameter space. This conflation limits both specialisation and interpretability.

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presented漫游ROAM，一个空间感知的 MoE 聚合器，通过容量受限的熵最优传输将区域令牌路由给专家，并在展开的 Sinkhorn 更新中使用基于图形的平滑正则化器。在具有冻结基础模型嵌入的四个 WSI 基准中，ROAM 实现了与强大的 MIL 和 MoE 基准相比的竞争性能； NSCLC 外部概括（TCGA, a spatially aware MoE aggregator that routes region tokens to experts via capacity-constrained entropic optimal transport with a graph-based smoothing regulariser within unrolled Sinkhorn updates. Across four WSI benchmarks with frozen foundation-model embeddings, ROAM achieves competitive performance against strong MIL and MoE baselines; on NSCLC external generalisation (TCGA→\rightarrowCPTAC）它实现了 AUCCPTAC) it achieves AUC0.845±0.0190.845{\pm}0.019并且在我们的设置中表现出比 softmax 路由的 MoE 基线更低的折叠方差，这与显式容量边际在域转移下稳定路由的假设一致。局限性包括依赖冻结编码器和固定的基于网格的区域标记化；自适应区域化和端到端微调是未来工作的有希望的方向。and exhibits lower fold-to-fold variance than softmax-routed MoE baselines in our setting, consistent with the hypothesis that explicit capacity marginals stabilise routing under domain shift. Limitations include reliance on frozen encoders and fixed grid-based region tokenisation; adaptive regionisation and end-to-end fine-tuning are promising directions for future work.
