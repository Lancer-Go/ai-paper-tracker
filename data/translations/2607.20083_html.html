摘要Abstract

训练后评估者对政策诱导样本的反馈是改进大型语言模型的主要机制。 随着政策的改进，这些抽样响应的质量变得接近。 这些接近的候选人给政策优化带来了瓶颈：相对评估者分数差距的缩小导致政策监督薄弱或误导。 我们从理论上描述了为什么这些差距很重要，通过概率分配视图，表明将概率质量从一种响应转移到另一种响应的方向增益正是它们之间的评估者分数差距。 这将相对分数差距识别为指导更新的策略优化信号。 受此观点的启发，我们建议Post-training with evaluator feedback on policy-induced samples serves as a major mechanism for improving large language models. As policies improve, these sampled responses become close in quality. These close candidates create a bottleneck for policy optimization: collapsed relative evaluator score gaps yield weak or misleading policy supervision. We theoretically characterize why these gaps matter through a probability allocation view, showing that the directional gain of shifting probability mass from one response to another is exactly the evaluator score gap between them. This identifies relative score gaps as the policy optimization signals that guide updates. Motivated by this view, we propose动态量规DynamicRubric，一个以响应集为条件的评估器-策略共同进化框架，为每个候选集生成加权二元标题项，并将结果判断聚合为响应级别分数。 在我们使用 8B 主干的实验中，DynamicRubric 提高了评估器的性能，并提供比使用 70B 奖励模型或 235B 静态 rubric 生成器的基线更强的策略监督。 DynamicRubric 优化策略还显示出在可验证推理和编码任务方面的收益。, a response-set-conditioned evaluator–policy co-evolution framework that generates weighted binary rubric items for each candidate set and aggregates the resulting judgments into response-level scores. In our experiments with 8B backbones, DynamicRubric improves evaluator performance and provides stronger policy supervision than baselines using a 70B reward model or a 235B static rubric generator. DynamicRubric-optimized policies also show gains on verifiable reasoning and coding tasks.DynamicRubric优化模型全面部署在微信搜索的AI应答场景中，服务于每天数千万个请求的所有在线流量，并改善了关键的在线指标。A DynamicRubric-optimized model is fully deployed in WeChat Search’s AI answering scenario, where it serves all online traffic across tens of millions of requests per day and improves key online metrics.这些结果提出了评估者引导的培训后原则：评估者应该随着他们监督的政策而发展。These results suggest a principle for evaluator-guided post-training: evaluators should evolve with the policies they supervise.

1 简介Introduction

带有评估者反馈的培训后已成为改进大型语言模型 (LLM) 的核心机制Post-training with feedback from evaluators has become a central mechanism for improving large language models (LLMs)(Ouyang et al., 2022; Rafailov et al., 2023)。 在这些管道中，使用从当前策略中采样的响应反馈来优化策略。 随着优化改进策略，其采样响应的质量通常会变得接近，从而使剩余的有用差异变得微妙且特定于实例。 这为评估者引导的优化造成了一个中心瓶颈：策略更新取决于相近候选者之间的相对分数差距，这决定了概率质量应如何重新分配。 对于这种细粒度的比较，现有的奖励模型和法学硕士评估者通常不可靠. In these pipelines, policies are optimized using feedback on responses sampled from the current policy. As optimization improves a policy, its sampled responses often become close in quality, making the remaining useful differences subtle and instance-specific. This creates a central bottleneck for evaluator-guided optimization: policy updates depend on relative score gaps among close candidates, which determine how probability mass should be reallocated. Existing reward models and LLM-as-Judge evaluators are often unreliable for such fine-grained comparisons(Lambert et al., 2024; Tan et al., 2025)。 当这些差距消失或指向错误的方向时，政策优化就会收到微弱或误导性的反馈。. When these gaps collapse or point in the wrong direction, policy optimization receives weak or misleading feedback.

基于评分标准的评估提供了一种自然的方式，通过将整体判断分解为标准来明确细粒度差异Rubric-based evaluation offers a natural way to make fine-grained differences explicit by decomposing holistic judgments into criteria(Liu et al., 2023; Ye et al., 2023; Kim et al., 2024; Song et al., 2024)。 然而，只有当其标准将当前政策实际产生的反应分开时，标题才有助于政策优化。 随着政策的改进，这些候选人的回答通常满足广泛的标准，例如有用性和清晰度。 一旦这些广泛的标准变得饱和，对于策略优化来说重要的区别就变得更加特定于实例：错过的约束、有缺陷的推理步骤或无法匹配用户意图的选择。 在观察候选人反应之前编写的标题很容易忽视决定下一次政策更新的区别。. Yet a rubric helps policy optimization only when its criteria separate the responses that the current policy actually produces. As policies improve, these candidate responses often satisfy broad criteria such as helpfulness and clarity. Once these broad criteria become saturated, the distinctions that matter for policy optimization become more instance-specific: a missed constraint, a flawed reasoning step, or a choice that fails to match the user’s intent. Rubrics written before observing the candidate responses can easily overlook the distinctions that determine the next policy update.

总而言之，这种保留间隙的要求激励了Taken together, this gap-preservation requirement motivates动态dynamic，响应集条件评估。 给定提示和从当前政策中采样的响应，评估器会生成针对集合中重要差异的二进制标题项。 验证者将这些项目应用于每个响应，并将是/否判断汇总为响应级别分数。 共享的评分标准使所得的分数差距在集合内直接进行比较，并可追溯到明确的判断，从而产生与推动政策优化的比较相一致的反馈。, response-set-conditioned rubric evaluation. Given a prompt and responses sampled from the current policy, the evaluator generates binary rubric items aimed at the differences that matter within the set. A verifier applies these items to every response and aggregates the yes/no judgments into response-level scores. The shared rubric makes the resulting score gaps directly comparable within the set and traceable to explicit judgments, yielding feedback aligned with the comparisons that drive policy optimization.

我们从理论上通过政策优化的概率分配观点来支持这一设计。 在这种观点下，以提示和采样响应集为条件，将概率质量从一个候选者转移到另一个候选者的定向增益正是他们之间的评估者分数差距。 这种恒等式将评估者的可辨别性转化为优化量：相对分数差距是指导响应集中策略更新的信号。 我们进一步确定，响应集条件评估器包括仅提示评估器作为特殊情况，而响应集输入允许评估器适应正在比较的候选者。 由于策略更新不断改变响应分布，因此评估器必须适应其用于监督的响应集。 总之，这些结果产生了评估器-策略共同进化原则：使评估器适应当前策略响应集，然后使用适应的评估器优化策略。We theoretically ground this design through a probability allocation view of policy optimization. Under this view, conditioned on a prompt and a sampled response set, the directional gain of shifting probability mass from one candidate to another is exactly the evaluator score gap between them. This identity turns evaluator discriminability into an optimization quantity: relative score gaps are the signals that guide policy updates within the response set. We further establish that response-set-conditioned evaluators include prompt-only evaluators as a special case, while the response-set input allows the evaluator to adapt to the candidates being compared. Since policy updates continually change the response distribution, the evaluator must adapt to the response sets it is used to supervise. Together, these results yield an evaluator–policy co-evolution principle: adapt the evaluator to current-policy response sets, then optimize the policy using the adapted evaluator.

Using Qwen3-8B as the backbone, the trained DR-Generator outperforms larger evaluator baselines on preference evaluation, including a Qwen3-32B zero-shot dynamic rubric generator. DynamicRubric meets this requirement by letting the rubric evaluator evolve with the response sets encountered during optimization, keeping evaluation focused on the comparisons that determine the next policy update.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 基于评分标准的评估和反馈Rubric-Based Evaluation and Feedback

基于法学硕士的评估器被广泛用于评估开放式生成并大规模地近似人类偏好LLM-based evaluators are widely used to assess open-ended generation and approximate human preferences at scale(Zheng et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023)。 基于评分标准的评估通过将响应质量分解为明确的标准、结构化形式或细粒度维度来提高可解释性. Rubric-based evaluation improves interpretability by decomposing response quality into explicit criteria, structured forms, or fine-grained dimensions(Liu et al., 2023)。 G-Eval 使用思维链和表格填写提示进行自然语言生成评估，而 FLASK 将指令分解为细粒度的对齐技能. G-Eval uses chain-of-thought and form-filling prompts for natural language generation evaluation, while FLASK decomposes instruction following into fine-grained alignment skills(Liu et al., 2023; Ye et al., 2023)。 Prometheus 训练开源评估器模型以遵循定制的评分标准，FineSurE 设计多维细粒度评估进行总结. Prometheus trains open-source evaluator models to follow customized grading rubrics and FineSurE designs multi-dimensional fine-grained evaluation for summarization(Kim et al., 2024; Song et al., 2024)。 评分细则也被探索作为具有确定性验证者的域外策略强化学习的奖励信号. Rubrics have also been explored as reward signals for on-policy reinforcement learning beyond domains with deterministic verifiers(Gunjal et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025).

2.2 法学硕士的政策优化和反馈Policy Optimization with Feedback for LLMs

培训后反馈是改进法学硕士政策的标准方法。 RLHF 通过人类偏好比较来训练奖励模型以进行策略优化，而 RLAIF 使用人工智能生成的反馈来降低人类注释成本Post-training with feedback is a standard approach for improving LLM policies. RLHF trains reward models from human preference comparisons for policy optimization, while RLAIF uses AI-generated feedback to reduce human annotation cost(Ouyang et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022)。 直接偏好优化通过直接从偏好对优化策略，无需显式训练奖励模型，进一步简化了偏好学习. Direct preference optimization further simplifies preference learning by optimizing policies directly from preference pairs without explicitly training a reward model(Rafailov et al., 2023)。 最近的推理导向系统通过可验证的、基于结果的奖励扩展了基于强化学习的后期训练，以提高数学、编码和一般推理能力. Recent reasoning-oriented systems extend RL-based post-training with verifiable, outcome-based rewards to improve mathematical, coding, and general reasoning abilities(Shao et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025b)。 这些方法的有效性取决于策略优化时反馈信号的可靠性，不完善的奖励模型可能导致过度优化. The effectiveness of these methods depends on the reliability of the feedback signal during policy optimization, and imperfect reward models can lead to overoptimization(Gao et al., 2023)。 这些观察激发了我们评估者的观点：训练后应该在优化的响应分布上保留有区别的分数差距。. These observations motivate our evaluator-side perspective: post-training should preserve discriminative score gaps on the response distribution being optimized.

6 结论Conclusion

我们确定了评估者引导的政策优化的一个中心瓶颈：随着当前政策响应的质量变得接近，有效的更新要求评估者保持可区分性。 我们通过概率分配视图形式化这一要求，其中在两个响应之间移动概率质量会产生等于其评估者分数差距的增益。 这就产生了一个简单的原则：评估者应该随着他们监督的政策而发展，在下一次政策更新之前适应当前的政策响应集。 我们建议We identify a central bottleneck in evaluator-guided policy optimization: as current-policy responses become close in quality, effective updates require evaluators to maintain discriminability. We formalize this requirement through a probability allocation view, where moving probability mass between two responses yields a gain equal to their evaluator score gap. This yields a simple principle: evaluators should evolve with the policies they supervise, adapting to current-policy response sets before the next policy update. We propose动态量规DynamicRubric通过将响应集条件的评分表聚合成响应级别分数以进行监督来实例化这一原则。 实验显示了评估、开放式生成和可验证任务方面的进展，强调了政策引起的响应分布的相对可辨别性是有效的培训后评估者的核心。to instantiate this principle with response-set-conditioned rubrics aggregated into response-level scores for supervision. Experiments show gains in evaluation, open-ended generation, and verifiable tasks, highlighting relative discriminability on the policy-induced response distribution as central to effective post-training evaluators.

局限性Limitations

训练时的计算成本。Training-time computational cost.

DynamicRubric 引入了额外的训练时间成本，因为策略优化需要生成器和验证器调用。 虽然附录DynamicRubric introduces additional training-time cost because policy optimization requires generator and verifier calls. Although AppendixE报告称，这种开销仍然低于我们设置中较大奖励模型或标题生成器基线的监督，串行生成器验证器调用仍然会增加策略训练期间的挂钟延迟。 随着生成器更大、验证器更强或响应集更大，这种成本可能会变得更加显着。 未来的工作可以通过并行验证、异步评估器更新和更高效的验证器调度来减少这种开销。reports that this overhead remains lower than supervision from larger reward model or rubric generator baselines in our setup, serial generator–verifier calls still increase wall-clock latency during policy training. This cost may become more significant with larger generators, stronger verifiers, or larger response sets. Future work can reduce this overhead through parallel verification, asynchronous evaluator updates, and more efficient verifier scheduling.

验证者范围。Verifier scope.

DynamicRubric 依赖验证者的反馈来对生成的评分标准下的响应进行评分，因此验证者的选择定义了训练期间可用的监督。 虽然附录DynamicRubric relies on verifier feedback to score responses under generated rubrics, so the verifier choice defines the supervision available during training. Although AppendixF.1评估不同规模和不同模型系列的验证器，由于评估成本，我们的研究在训练期间不包括更强的黑盒验证器，例如 GPT-4.1。 我们还冻结 DR-Verifier，而不是训练专门的验证者。 未来的工作可以研究更强大的验证者家族、自适应验证者训练以及基于评分标准的校准策略。evaluates verifiers of different scales and from different model families, our study does not include stronger black-box verifiers such as GPT-4.1 during training because of evaluation cost. We also keep DR-Verifier frozen rather than training a dedicated verifier. Future work can study stronger verifier families, adaptive verifier training, and calibration strategies for rubric-based scoring.

对于 DR-Generator 优化，每个训练实例都包含一个用户提示和一个候选响应集。 我们应用轻量级过滤来确保生成器观察到足够的信息输入，同时避免由退化模型输出主导的响应集。For DR-Generator optimization, each training instance contains a user prompt and a candidate response set. We apply lightweight filtering to ensure that the generator observes sufficiently informative inputs while avoiding response sets dominated by degenerate model outputs.

我们首先应用基于长度的过滤。 每个示例必须至少包含七个候选响应。 用户提示和排名靠前的响应都必须包含超过 50 个字符。 然后，我们对前七个响应应用响应集级别的过滤。 如果响应集包含任何重复生成，如果至少四个响应是退化的，如果少于四个响应有用，或者如果至少四个响应主要由样板组成，则响应集将被丢弃。 在这里，退化的回复包括破损的文本、简单的拒绝、仅问候的回复和仅澄清的回复，而有用的回复包括实质性答案、拒绝有帮助的替代方案以及其他重要的回复。 经过过滤，我们获得了 24K 训练样本用于 DR-Generator 优化。We first apply length-based filtering. Each example must contain at least seven candidate responses. The user prompt and the top-ranked response must each contain more than 50 characters. Appendix I Potential Risks

DynamicRubric optimizes policies with rubric-based automatic supervision, so policies may learn to satisfy verifier-specific preferences or rubric-compliance patterns that do not fully match broader human judgments. Generated rubrics may also inherit biases or blind spots from the generator and verifier, and repeated optimization can amplify such patterns. Although our robustness checks and qualitative inspection provide partial safeguards, they do not replace large-scale human validation. Future work can study verifier ensembles, human calibration, and auditing procedures for bias and reward-hacking behavior before deployment.
