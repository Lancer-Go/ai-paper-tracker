代理引导的关系概念发现：走向可解释的手术边缘评估Agent-Guided Relational Concept Discovery: Toward Interpretable Surgical Margin Assessment

Nooshin Maghsoodi Corresponding author.    Amoon Jamzad    Robert Policelli    Mohammad Farahmand    Dilakshan Srikanthan    Martin Kaufmann    Kevin Y. M. Ren    Shaila Merchant    Sonal Varma    Ross Walker    Doug McKay    John Rudan    Gabor Fichtinger    Parvin Mousavi
摘要Abstract

深度学习模型可以有效地利用快速蒸发电离质谱（REIMS）数据进行手术切缘评估。然而，由于对手术室条件的推广有限，它们的临床应用仍然具有挑战性。之所以出现这种困难，是因为模型通常是根据从切除的组织样本收集的标记光谱进行训练的，而它们必须对手术期间直接获取的嘈杂、未标记的数据进行操作。此外，深度学习模型的黑盒性质使得理解和系统地改进其行为变得困难。基于概念的学习通过将原始测量结果映射到人类可理解的概念，提供了一种解决这些挑战的有前途的方法。然而，基于概念的监督方法依赖于概念注释，这在复杂的质谱工作流程中很难获得。我们提出了代理引导的概念发现，这是一个直接从数据中学习有意义的概念而不需要预定义概念标签的框架。在训练过程中，推理代理会完善所学概念的语义描述，并根据诊断相关性自适应调整其权重。这些概念进一步基于生化知识图，以确保与已知代谢关系的一致性。在皮肤癌和乳腺癌数据集中，我们的模型在基线上提高了平衡的准确性和灵敏度。在代表性的术中病例中，它显示出较少的假阳性，表明对手术条件有更好的概括。Deep learning models can effectively use Rapid Evaporative Ionization Mass Spectrometry (REIMS) data for surgical margin assessment. However, their clinical adoption remains challenging due to limited generalization to operating room conditions. This difficulty arises because models are typically trained on labeled spectra collected from resected tissue samples, while they must operate on noisy, unlabeled data acquired directly during surgery. In addition, the black-box nature of deep learning models makes it difficult to understand and systematically improve their behavior. Concept-based learning offers a promising way to address these challenges by mapping raw measurements to human-understandable concepts. However, supervised concept-based approaches rely on concept annotations, which are difficult to obtain in complex mass spectrometry workflows. We propose Agent-Guided Concept Discovery, a framework that learns meaningful concepts directly from data without requiring predefined concept labels. During training, a reasoning agent refines semantic descriptions of the learned concepts and adaptively adjusts their weight based on diagnostic relevance. These concepts are further grounded using a biochemical knowledge graph to ensure consistency with known metabolic relationships. Across Skin and Breast Cancer datasets, our model improves balanced accuracy and sensitivity over the baseline. In a representative intraoperative case, it shows fewer false positives, indicating better generalization to surgical conditions.

1 简介Introduction

实现清晰的手术切缘对癌症结果具有重大影响。负切缘表明切除组织的边界处没有残留恶性细胞，表明肿瘤已被完全切除并降低了残留疾病的风险。如今，切缘状态通常是在术后通过组织病理学确定的，这不提供术中调整的机会。相比之下，实时边缘评估允许外科医生在切除过程中纠正路线，既避免切穿肿瘤，又最大限度地减少不必要的健康组织切除Achieving clear surgical margins has a significant impact on cancer outcomes. Negative margins indicate that no malignant cells remain at the boundary of the excised tissue, suggesting that the tumor has been fully removed and reducing the risk of residual disease. Today, margin status is typically determined postoperatively through histopathology, which provides no opportunity for intraoperative adjustment. In contrast, real-time margin assessment allows surgeons to course-correct during resection, both avoiding cutting through the tumor and minimizing unnecessary removal of healthy tissue[10]。快速蒸发电离质谱 (REIMS) 通过提供烧灼组织的瞬时代谢组学特征来实现术中反馈，可以使用机器学习对这些组织进行分类，以识别切口处的癌症. Rapid Evaporative Ionization Mass Spectrometry (REIMS) enables intraoperative feedback by providing instantaneous metabolomic profiles from cauterized tissue, which can be classified using machine learning to identify cancer at the point of incision[1].

先前的工作已经探索了多种策略来改进基于 REIMS 的裕度评估，包括用于不确定性估计的贝叶斯神经网络和基于图像的质谱表示Prior works have explored multiple strategies to improve REIMS-based margin assessment, including Bayesian neural networks for uncertainty estimation and image-based representations of mass spectra[8, 6]。最近，在大规模串联质谱数据上预训练的基础模型，深度表示赋能质谱注释 (DreaMS). More recently, a foundation model pretrained on large-scale tandem mass spectrometry data, Deep Representations Empowering the Annotation of Mass Spectra (DreaMS)[5]，已被提出并随后适用于 REIMS 应用, has been proposed and later adapted for REIMS applications[7]。然而，在小型、标记的离体 REIMS 数据集上微调该基础模型存在过度拟合的风险，并限制了对嘈杂的术中数据的泛化。此外，尽管性能强劲，这些模型在很大程度上仍然是黑匣子，对其预测的生化驱动因素的了解有限。. However, fine-tuning this foundation model on small, labeled ex vivo REIMS datasets risks overfitting and limits generalization to noisy intraoperative data. Moreover, despite strong performance, these models largely remain black boxes, offering limited insight into the biochemical drivers of their predictions.

基于概念的学习为纯粹的端到端分类提供了一种有前途的替代方案。这些模型不是将原始光谱直接映射到诊断标签，而是引入了由人类可理解的变量组成的中间表示Concept-based learning offers a promising alternative to purely end-to-end classification. Rather than mapping raw spectra directly to diagnostic labels, these models introduce an intermediate representation composed of human-understandable variables[13]。通过对这些潜在因素进行显式建模，可以用有意义的证据来解释预测，并且通过依赖稳定的高级抽象而不是低级特征，决策对分布变化变得更加稳健. By explicitly modeling such latent factors, predictions can be explained in terms of meaningful evidence, and decision-making becomes more robust to distribution shifts by relying on stable, high-level abstractions instead of low-level features[12, 17]。然而，现有的监督框架依赖于手动定义的概念注释，这在复杂的质谱工作流程中是不切实际的。. However, existing supervised frameworks depend on manually defined concept annotations, which are impractical in complex mass spectrometry workflows.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了代理引导的关系概念发现，这是一个可解释的手术切缘评估的框架。我们引入了一种推理代理循环训练框架，可以自动发现判别性概念，而无需概念注释。代理分析共享的频谱模式，分配语义描述，并自适应地调整概念相关性。这些概念使用知识图进一步奠定基础，以确保与已建立的代谢途径的一致性。我们的主要贡献总结如下：To address these challenges, we propose Agent-Guided Relational Concept Discovery, a framework for interpretable surgical margin assessment. We introduce a reasoning-agent-in-the-loop training framework that enables automatic discovery of discriminative concepts without requiring concept annotations. The agent analyzes shared spectral patterns, assigns semantic descriptions, and adaptively adjusts concept relevance. These concepts are further grounded using a knowledge graph to ensure consistency with established metabolic pathways. Our main contributions are summarized as follows:

  1. 1.

    无需监督的代理引导概念发现。Agent-Guided Concept Discovery without Supervision.推理代理被纳入训练循环中，以帮助模型从嵌入中发现和细化有意义的潜在表示，而无需手动注释。这是通过以下两个关键贡献实现的。A reasoning agent is incorporated into the training loop to help the model discover and refine meaningful latent representations from embeddings without requiring manual annotations. This is achieved through two key contributions below.

  2. 2.

    通过知识图构建进行关系概念建模Relational Concept Modeling via Knowledge Graph Construction代理接收与每个学习概念相关的信息最丰富的光谱模式，然后查询代谢数据库以将这些概念扎根于生物知识中。这些链接生成一个知识图，将概念与代谢物、途径和组织状态连接起来，该知识图在训练过程中不断发展，以捕获关系并支持更一致的代理反馈。The agent receives the most informative spectral patterns associated with each learned concept and then queries metabolic databases to ground these concepts in biological knowledge. These links generate a knowledge graph connecting concepts to metabolites, pathways, and tissue states, which evolves during training to capture relationships and support more consistent agent feedback.

  3. 3.

    推理驱动的概念权重优化。Reasoning-Driven Concept Weight Optimization.在训练期间，智能体会汇总支持证据，以提供有关所学概念相关性的反馈。该反馈被纳入辅助对齐损失中，指导概念权重的学习。During training, the agent aggregates supporting evidence to provide feedback on the relevance of the learned concepts. This feedback is incorporated into an auxiliary alignment loss, which guides the learning of the concept weights.

2 材料和方法Materials and Methods

2.1 数据Data

离体数据：Ex vivo data:

离体 REIMS 光谱是在机构道德批准下从两个外科肿瘤学队列收集的。基底细胞癌 (BCC) 数据集包括来自 91 名患者的 693 个带注释的烧伤（252 个肿瘤，441 个良性），这些烧伤是从新鲜切除的标本中获取的。乳腺癌数据集包含来自 11 名患者的 144 例离体烧伤（41 例肿瘤，103 例良性），使用相同的 REIMS 协议从恶性组织和邻近良性实质组织中取样。Ex vivo REIMS spectra were collected from two surgical oncology cohorts under institutional ethics approval. The basal cell carcinoma (BCC) dataset includes 693 annotated burns from 91 patients (252 tumors, 441 benign), acquired from freshly excised specimens. The breast cancer dataset comprises 144 ex vivo burns from 11 patients (41 tumors, 103 benign), sampled from malignant tissue and adjacent benign parenchyma using the same REIMS protocol.

术中数据：Intraoperative data:

术中兰斯数据是在保乳手术期间收集的。在 27 分钟的过程中，在 1 处记录了 1,616 个光谱Intraoperative reims data were collected during a breast-conserving surgery. Over a 27-minute procedure, 1,616 spectra were recorded at 1Hz并使用术中手术注释进行标记。and labeled using intraoperative surgical annotations.

3.0.1 分类性能比较Classification performance comparison

分类结果见表The classification results are shown in Table1。总体而言，所提出的模型在两个数据集上均优于 DreaMS 基线。这表明，结合智能体引导的概念嵌入，以提高泛化性和可解释性为目标，不会牺牲分类性能，甚至可以提高分类性能。在皮肤癌数据集上，我们观察到平衡准确度相对提高了约 7%，同时 AUROC 更高，灵敏度显着提高，这表明肿瘤组织的检测得到了改善。 同样，在乳腺癌数据集上，我们的方法实现了更高的平衡准确性 (0.87)，同时还提高了灵敏度和特异性。. Overall, the proposed model outperforms the DreaMS baseline across both datasets. This indicates that incorporating agent-guided concept embeddings, with the goal of improving generalization and interpretability, does not sacrifice classification performance and can even improve it. On the Skin Cancer dataset, we observe an approximate 7% relative improvement in balanced accuracy, along with higher AUROC and a noticeable increase in sensitivity, suggesting improved detection of tumor tissue. Similarly, on the Breast Cancer dataset, our method achieves higher balanced accuracy (0.87) while also improving both sensitivity and specificity.

4 结论Conclusion

在我们提出的框架中，代理在训练循环中运行，帮助模型发现有意义的概念，而不需要明确的概念注释，目的是提高可解释性和更强的泛化性。这种方法将基于离体数据训练的模型扩展到具有挑战性的术中设置，同时使他们的决策更容易理解。该模型学习高级代谢概念，并将其直接整合到预测过程中，以在术中噪声下保持稳定的模式为决策基础。此外，动态构建的特定于数据的知识图揭示了潜在的生化结构，并支持超出基于固定规则的系统所能提供的迭代解释和概念细化。当前的限制是对一般代谢数据库查询的依赖；未来的工作将探索更有针对性的查询策略和基因水平信息的整合，以进一步增强生物学特异性和可解释性。In our proposed framework, an agent operates within the training loop to help the model discover meaningful concepts without requiring explicit concept annotations, with the goals of improved interpretability and stronger generalization. This approach extends models trained on ex vivo data to challenging intraoperative settings while making their decisions easier to understand. The model learns high-level metabolic concepts and directly integrates them into the prediction process, grounding decisions in patterns that remain stable under intraoperative noise. In addition, a dynamically constructed, data-specific knowledge graph reveals underlying biochemical structure and supports iterative interpretation and concept refinement beyond what fixed rule-based systems can provide. A current limitation is the reliance on general metabolic database queries; future work will explore more targeted querying strategies and the integration of gene-level information to further enhance biological specificity and interpretability.
