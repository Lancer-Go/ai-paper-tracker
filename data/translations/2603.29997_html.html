1 简介Introduction

类比推理是识别两种情况或领域之间的共同结构并使用该结构得出进一步的结论或见解的过程Analogical reasoning is the process of identifying a common structure between two situations or domains and using that structure to draw further conclusions or insights(Gentner and Smith, 2012)。 类比是人类泛化任务的关键驱动力，从简单的比较到高度复杂的推理。类比使人类能够通过将不熟悉的概念映射到熟悉的概念来解释或解释它们. Analogy is a key driver of human generalization across tasks ranging from simple comparisons to highly complex reasoning. Analogy enables humans to interpret or explain unfamiliar concepts by mapping them onto familiar ones(Holyoak and Thagard, 1996)。因此，它在复杂的领域和任务中发挥着核心作用：法律论证、商业决策、因果解释、创造性解决问题. As such, it plays a central role in complex domains and tasks: legal argumentation, business decision-making, causal explanation, creative problem-solving(Penn et al., 2008)，甚至非传统形式的推理，例如解释诗意隐喻, and even non-traditional forms of reasoning, such as interpreting poetic metaphors(Penn et al., 2008)。由于其广泛的用途，从学习到创造力，类比技能被认为是人类认知的核心过程. Due to its wide usage, from learning to creativity, analogical skills have been considered a core process of human cognition(Gentner and Smith, 2012; Penn et al., 2008; Hofstadter and others, 2001).

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 类比的认知理论和计算框架Cognitive theories and computational frameworks for analogy

类比的范围很广，从简单的基于属性的比较到比例映射，再到跨领域的关系系统的完全结构类比Analogies span a wide spectrum, ranging from simple attribute-based comparisons to proportional mappings, and to fully structural analogies that align systems of relations across domains(Turney et al., 2003; Holyoak and Thagard, 1996)。尽管叙述已被用来研究类比推理. Although narratives have been used to investigate analogical reasoning(Gentner and Toupin, 1986; Gentner, 1983)，此类研究主要集中在简化的环境或人类参与者, such studies have focused mainly on simplified settings or human participants(Lu et al., 2022)。文献中的一个基本区别是将表面映射（匹配对象属性）与结构映射（对齐深层关系结构）进行对比. A fundamental distinction in the literature contrasts surface mappings, which match object attributes, with structural mappings, which align deep relational structures(Gentner, 1983)。这种区别支撑了近类比和远类比之间的差异：近类比共享表面和结构特征，使它们更容易识别，而远类比尽管表面重叠极小，但仍保留关系结构，需要更高的认知要求的关系推理. This distinction underpins the difference between near and far analogies: near analogies share both surface and structural features, making them easier to recognize, while far analogies preserve relational structure despite minimal surface overlap, requiring more cognitively demanding relational reasoning(Alexieva and Hristova, 2017; Gentner, 1983).

对人类类比推理的洞察激发了一系列理论和计算框架。 Gentner 的 SMT 认为类比推理涉及通过对齐关系结构将基本域映射到目标域Insights into human analogical reasoning have inspired a range of theoretical and computational frameworks. Gentner’s SMT posits that analogical reasoning involves mapping a base domain to a target domain by aligning their relational structures(Gentner, 1983)。该理论为 SME 提供了基础，SME 是一种计算系统，可识别结构化表示之间的一对一映射. This theory underpins the SME, a computational system that identifies one-to-one mappings between structured representations(Falkenhainer et al., 1989)，最近对贪婪合并和增量处理进行了扩展，以有效地处理新知识, with recent extensions for greedy merging and incremental processing to handle new knowledge efficiently(Forbus et al., 2017)。现代衍生品 FAME（灵活、可扩展的类比映射引擎）通过将基于 LLM 的关系提取与贪婪波束搜索映射算法相结合，使 SME 适合实体映射. A modern derivative, FAME (Flexible, Scalable Analogy Mappings Engine), adapts SME for entity mapping by combining LLM-based relation extraction with a greedy beam-search mapping algorithm(Jacob et al., 2023)。 替代框架提供了相互竞争的观点。 ACME 将类比框架视为由结构一致性、语义相似性和语用约束控制的并行约束满足过程. Alternative frameworks offer competing perspectives. ACME frames analogy as a parallel constraint satisfaction process governed by structural consistency, semantic similarity, and pragmatic constraints(Holyoak and Thagard, 1989)。模仿者将高级类比建模为从小的低级计算动作中产生的. Copycat models high-level analogies as emerging from small, low-level computational actions(Hofstadter and Mitchell, 1995)，而 LISA 将命题表示为语义单元上的分布式模式，以动态绑定结构化表示, while LISA represents propositions as distributed patterns over semantic units to bind structured representations dynamically(Hummel and Holyoak, 1997).

这些认知引擎的一个常见限制是它们无法直接处理非结构化文本，例如叙述。我们通过一些抽象将非结构化文本提炼成结构化的、以事件为中心的叙述表示来弥合这一差距；这些随后被输入到结构映射过程中，该过程通过成对比对来估计类比拟合。A common limitation of these cognitive engines is their inability to process unstructured texts, such as narratives, directly. We bridge this gap by distilling unstructured text into structured, event-centric representations of narratives through a number of abstractions; these are subsequently fed into a structural mapping process that estimates the analogical fit via pairwise alignment.

2.2 用于类比推理的 NLP 模型NLP models for analogical reasoning

NLP 中类比推理的处理已经从静态嵌入演变为生成模型。早期的工作依赖于这样的假设：Word2Vec 和 GloVe 等模型中的向量偏移可以代表语义关系The treatment of analogical reasoning in NLP has evolved from static embeddings to generative models. Early work relied on the assumption that vector offsets in models like Word2Vec and GloVe could proxy for semantic relations(Mikolov et al., 2013; Pennington et al., 2014)。后来，这种线性假设被证明不足以进行稳健推理. Later, this linear assumption was proven to be insufficient for robust reasoning(Rogers et al., 2017)。上下文嵌入也发现了类似的稳健性挑战. Similar robustness challenges were discovered for contextualized embeddings(Ushio et al., 2021).

法学硕士的兴起重新引入了计算类比的可能性，一些人声称其具有超人的表现The rise of LLMs reintroduced the possibility of computational analogy, with some claims of superhuman performance(Webb et al., 2023)。然而，这些能力似乎很脆弱：当测试数据偏离其训练分布时，法学硕士表现出急剧的性能下降，无法像人类那样转移抽象关系. However, these capabilities appear to be brittle: LLMs exhibit sharp performance drops when testing data shifts from their training distribution, failing to transfer abstract relations as humans do(Lewis and Mitchell, 2024; Stevenson et al., 2024)。这种缺陷在需要深度结构调整的叙事类比背景下至关重要。 ARN 和 StoryAnalogy 等当前基准表明法学硕士未能掌握远类比. This deficiency is critical in the context of narrative analogies, where deep structural alignment is required. Current benchmarks such as ARN and StoryAnalogy show that LLMs failing to grasp far analogies(Jiayang et al., 2023; Sourati et al., 2024)，这可能是由于他们在因果结构的抽象和提取方面遇到困难, which may be due to their struggles with abstraction and extraction of causal structures(Opiełka et al., 2025; Lee et al., 2025).

现有的研究在很大程度上将法学硕士的评估限制在比例类比的情境学习中。相比之下，我们的神经符号方法将 LLM 的生成能力与结构映射相结合：LLM 生成表示，而映射组件发现有效类比映射所需的一对一对应关系。Existing research has largely restricted LLM evaluation to in-context learning on proportional analogies. In contrast, our neurosymbolic approach combines the generative power of LLMs with structural mapping: LLMs generate the representations, while the mapping component discovers the one-to-one correspondences required for valid analogical mapping.

事件提取错误。Event extraction errors.虽然法学硕士通常可以有效地提取事件短语，但我们观察到四个反复出现的问题：（i）While LLMs can generally extract event phrases effectively, we observe four recurring issues: (i)失踪事件Missing events：有些事件被完全省略，例如，从“Jucy 应该对 Maria 给予她的东西感到满意，至少 Maria 正在想着她”，模型只提取了“Maria 想着 Jucy”，缺少“Jucy 应该感到满足”。尽管将“Jucy should be content”视为一个事件可能存在争议，但我们根据我们在中的定义将其视为一个事件。: Some events are omitted entirely, e.g., from “Jucy should be content of what Maria gives to her at least Maria is thinking of her,” the model extracted only “Maria thinks of Jucy,” missing “Jucy should be content.” Although treating the phrase “Jucy should be content” as an event may be debatable, we consider it an event based on our definition insubsection 3.1和andsection 4。 (二). (ii)改变的意思：Altered meaning:提取的事件有时会更改关键细节，例如生成“工人刚刚从学校毕业”而不是“邻居刚刚从学校毕业”。 (三)The extracted event sometimes changes key details, such as generating “Worker had just graduated from school” instead of “Neighbor had just graduated from school.” (iii)冗余事件：Redundant events:同一事件可能会以略有不同的形式出现，因为模型会重复它，例如“花比其他花枯萎得更快”和“花比早晨授粉的花枯萎得更快”，它们传达的含义基本相同。 （四）The same event may appear in slightly different forms because the model duplicates it, e.g., “Flower wilted faster than others” and “Flower wilted faster than morning pollinated flowers,” which convey essentially the same meaning. (iv)幻觉事件：Hallucinated events:该模型可能会引入故事中未提及的事件。例如，从“丈夫意识到她的妻子更好，是一个很好的妻子”，它产生了“丈夫决定和他的妻子在一起”，增加了一个未说明的决定。尽管不常见，但这些错误表明即使是基本的事件提取也很重要，并且可能会将噪声引入管道，而其余组件必须考虑到这一点。先前有关提取事件触发器、类型和参数的工作中也强调了类似的限制The model may introduce events not stated in the story. For instance, from “Husband realized that her wife is better and a good wife to be with,” it generated “Husband decided to be with his wife,” adding an unstated decision. Although infrequent, these errors demonstrate that even basic event extraction is non-trivial and can introduce noise into the pipeline, which the remaining components must account for. Similar limitations are highlighted in prior work on extracting event triggers, types, and arguments(Li et al., 2023).

事件抽象错误。Event abstraction errors.事件抽象是一项新颖的任务，可以说比提取困难得多，因为它要求法学硕士了解事件如何关联、它们的一般含义是什么以及它们在叙述中扮演什么角色。我们的分析表明，法学硕士在抽象方面遇到了困难，特别是当因果关系处于核心地位时。我们观察到三个主要问题：（i）Event abstraction is a novel task and is arguably much harder than extraction, as it requires LLMs to understand how events relate, what their general meaning is, and what role they play in the narrative. Our analysis shows that LLMs struggle with abstraction, particularly when causality is central. We observe three main issues: (i)保持靠近表面：Staying close to the surface:概念抽象有时没有得到充分概括。例如，从“明天是他们的两周年纪念日”开始，模型生成了“关系周年纪念日”。虽然没有错，但它很大程度上重复了表面术语，未能捕捉到更高层次的含义。 (二)conceptual abstractions are sometimes not sufficiently generalized. For example, from “Their second anniversary is tomorrow,” the model produced “relationship-anniversary.” While not wrong, it largely repeats the surface term and fails to capture a higher-level meaning. (ii)缺少活动的务实作用：Missing the pragmatic role of an event:在其他情况下，抽象是笼统的，但无法捕捉故事中事件的核心功能。例如，从“莉莉讲了一个笑话”中，模型生成了“消息语音”。虽然这是一个公平的抽象，但它忽略了给定故事的意图，即使用幽默来克服困难或维持参与。 (三)in other cases, abstractions are general but fail to capture the core function of the event within the story. For example, from “Lily told a joke,” the model generated “message speech.” While this is a fair abstraction, it misses the intent in the given story, namely, using humor to overcome difficulty or sustain engagement. 7 结论Conclusions

我们提议We proposed纱线YARN，一个叙事中的类比推理框架，利用法学硕士从原始故事中提取信息，并对结果表示应用结构映射。为了支持这一过程，我们引入了基于框架理论的四个抽象级别，它们在实现超越表面相似性的稳健映射方面发挥着核心作用。我们对两个叙述类比数据集的方法进行的评估表明，虽然法学硕士在需要结构映射时难以进行类比推理，但将抽象与结构映射相结合会产生有竞争力的、在某些情况下甚至更优越的性能。此外，通过分析不同的抽象设置，我们发现它们的有效性取决于表面相似性的水平，并且没有单一的抽象在所有设置中都效果最好。我们观察到法学硕士在不同的单元粒度和指令上是不一致的，这使得它们对于类比推理来说不可靠。我们的错误分析确定了故障发生的位置和原因, a framework for analogical reasoning in narratives that leverages LLMs to extract information from raw stories and applies structural mapping over the resulting representations. To support this process, we introduced four levels of abstraction, grounded in framing theories, that play a central role in enabling robust mapping beyond surface similarity. The evaluation of our approach on two narrative analogy datasets demonstrated that, while LLMs struggle with analogical reasoning when structural mapping is required, combining abstraction with structural mapping yields competitive, and in some cases superior, performance. Additionally, by analyzing different abstraction settings, we find that their effectiveness depends on the level of surface similarity, and no single abstraction works best across all settings. We observed that LLMs are inconsistent across different unit granularities and instructions, making them unreliable for analogical reasoning. Our error analysis identifies where and why failures occur in the纱线YARN管道，包括事件提取、抽象（例如因果误解）、映射（表面级相似性）和数据集设计（ARN 中的多个类比模式）。pipeline, including in event extraction, abstraction (e.g., causal misinterpretation), mapping (surface-level similarity), and dataset design (multiple analogy patterns in ARN).

我们认为这项工作是实现原始自然语言类比推理认知理论的第一步，并系统地探索它们与法学硕士的整合。我们的结果表明，虽然单独的法学硕士在抽象和类比推理方面并不可靠，但它们与认知启发的映射机制的结合是有前途的。与此同时，远距离类比仍然很困难。人工智能系统中推进类比推理的未来方向包括探索结构化单元表示、整合替代认知框架、利用道德和情感理论丰富抽象层次，以及开发具有细粒度映射注释的类比数据集。We view this work as a first step toward operationalizing cognitive theories of analogical reasoning over raw natural language and systematically exploring their integration with LLMs. Our results suggest that while LLMs alone are unreliable for abstraction and analogical reasoning, their combination with cognitively inspired mapping mechanisms is promising. At the same time, far analogies remain difficult. Future directions for advancing analogical reasoning in AI systems include exploring structured unit representations, integrating alternative cognitive frameworks, enriching the abstraction levels with theories of morality and emotion, and developing analogy datasets with fine-grained mapping annotations.

B.2 实施错误Implementation Errors

我们进行了详细的分析，以确保法学硕士基线和我们的管道的输出遵循预期的格式并识别潜在的错误。对于执行事件映射的 LLM 基线，我们要求模型为 CoT 返回 JSON 格式的答案，为 ZS 返回一个数字。在少数情况下，模型不遵循请求的格式，导致无法提取最终答案。在每个模型的四个实验设置（两个数据集上的零样本和 CoT）中，这种情况很少见。对于 Qwen，我们在 MCQ 零样本中观察到两个格式错误，在 MCQ CoT 中观察到一个格式错误，在 ARN 中观察到零问题。对于 Llama，我们在 ARN 零样本中观察到 5 个案例，在 MCQ CoT 中观察到 7 个案例，在其他案例中观察到零问题。在这些情况下，答案是随机选择的。由于这些案例很少，因此对最终结果的影响可以忽略不计，并且不会改变方法的排名。We conducted a detailed analysis to ensure that the outputs of both the LLM baselines and our pipeline followed the expected formats and to identify potential errors. For the LLM baselines performing event mapping, we asked the models to return answers in JSON format for CoT and just one number for ZS. In a small number of cases, the models did not follow the requested format, making it impossible to extract the final answer. Across four experimental settings for each model (zero-shot and CoT on two datasets), such cases were rare. For Qwen, we observed two formatting errors in MCQ zero-shot and one in MCQ CoT, and zero problems in ARN. For Llama, we observed five cases in ARN zero-shot and seven in MCQ CoT, and zero problems in other cases. In these situations, the answer was selected randomly. Because these cases are very few, they have a negligible impact on the final results and do not change the ranking of methods.

对于我们的管道，可能会出现两个实施问题。首先，FAME 框架采用的结构映射组件将映射构建为四元组，这需要每个故事至少两个单元。如果故事仅包含单个单元，则无法形成映射。其次，多个目标有时可能会获得相同的最高分数，从而导致随机选择。在实践中，这些案例也并不多见。当使用事件短语或概念抽象时，故事通常包含足够的单元，并且在大多数情况下我们不会遇到任何问题；而在某些情况下，我们只有一两个有问题的情况，主要是因为两个目标获得相同的最高分数。这个问题在阶段抽象中更频繁地出现，其中多个事件被合并到一个阶段中。这主要发生在 MCQ 数据集中，该数据集的故事很短；合并事件后，有些故事只包含一个阶段。这也解释了我们在主要结果中观察到的 MCQ 阶段抽象性能较低的原因。由于叙述较长，该问题在 ARN 中出现的频率较低。我们的实验表明，替代评分策略可以减轻这种限制。例如，使用没有局部和全局映射的阶段抽象，并使用它们的弧形成映射并使用基于 NLI 的相似性对其进行评分，产生了有希望的结果，特别是对于远类比，没有任何问题的情况。For our pipeline, two implementation issues may arise. First, the structural mapping component adopted from the FAME framework constructs mappings as quadruples, which requires at least two units per story. If a story contains only a single unit, no mapping can be formed. Second, multiple targets may occasionally receive the same maximum score, resulting in a random selection. In practice, these cases are also rare. When using event phrases or conceptual abstractions, stories typically contain enough units, and we don’t have any problems in most cases; while in some cases, we have just one or two problematic cases, mostly because of getting the equal max score for two targets. The problem arises more frequently with stage abstraction, where several events are merged into a single stage. This occurs mainly in the MCQ dataset, which stories are short; after merging events, some stories contain only one stage. This also explains the lower performance observed with stage abstraction on MCQ in our main results. The issue appears less frequently in ARN due to its longer narratives. Our experiments suggest that alternative scoring strategies can mitigate this limitation. For example, using stage abstractions without local and global mapping and forming the mapping using their arc and scoring them with NLI-based similarity produced promising results, particularly for far analogies, without any problematic cases.
