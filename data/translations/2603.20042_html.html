摘要Abstract

However, existing benchmarks predominantly focus on high-resource languages, leaving the ASR behavior of SpeechLMs in low-resource languages insufficiently understood. This gap is critical, as practical ASR systems must reliably support low-resource languages and generalize across diverse language families, and it directly hinders the deployment of SpeechLM-based ASR in real-world multilingual scenarios. As a result, it is essential to evaluate SpeechLMs on low-resource languages to ensure their generalizability across different language families. I 简介Introduction

最近出现的大型语言模型（LLM）极大地推动了语音语言模型（SpeechLM）的发展The recent emergence of large language models (LLMs) has significantly propelled the development of speech language models (SpeechLMs)[1, 2, 3]。这些 SpeechLM 通常利用大规模多模式和多语言预训练来在各种语音相关任务中实现出色的性能。随着这一快速进展，基准在系统评估和比较这些 SpeechLM 方面发挥着至关重要的作用. These SpeechLMs typically leverage large-scale multimodal and multilingual pre-training to achieve strong performance across a wide range of speech-related tasks. With this rapid progress, benchmarks play a crucial role in systematically evaluating and comparing these SpeechLMs[4, 6, 5]。现有基准主要评估模型在语言识别、自动语音识别（ASR）和语音翻译等任务上的性能。. Existing benchmarks primarily assess model performance on tasks such as language identification, automatic speech recognition (ASR), and speech translation.

将法学硕士应用于语音任务的常见方法是构建级联系统，其中首先使用 ASR 模型转录语音。然后将成绩单传递给法学硕士进行文本到文本的生成A common approach to applying LLMs to speech tasks is to construct cascaded systems, where speech is first transcribed with ASR models. The transcripts are then passed into LLMs for text-to-text generation[8, 9, 10]。在这项工作中，我们专注于 ASR，旨在在这项基础且广泛部署的任务中对 SpeechLM 进行重点和深入的评估。. In this work, we concentrate on ASR, aiming to provide a focused and in-depth evaluation of SpeechLMs in this foundational and widely deployed task.

尽管级联方法对于高资源语言来说很实用，但由于错误传播和有限的训练数据，性能在低资源设置中通常会下降Although cascaded approaches are practical for high-resource languages, performance often degrades in low-resource settings due to error propagation and limited training data[12, 11, 13]。为了解决这些限制，最近的 SpeechLM 越来越多地采用端到端 (E2E) 架构，在统一框架内对语音和文本进行联合训练. To address these limitations, recent SpeechLMs have increasingly adopted end-to-end (E2E) architectures that are jointly trained on speech and text within a unified framework[14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 19, 18]。 此外，自我监督学习方法如 XLSR-53. In addition, self-supervised learning approaches such as XLSR-53[8, 21]在学习跨语言的通用语音表示方面表现出了强大的潜力。have shown strong potential in learning universal speech representations across languages.

SpeechLM 越来越多地接受多语言环境培训，以促进跨语言知识转移SpeechLMs are increasingly trained in multilingual settings to facilitate cross-lingual knowledge transfer[22, 23, 24]。这种传输对于语音和文本数据都有限的低资源语言特别有利。通过利用跨语言共享的语音和语义特征，SpeechLM 可以提高资源匮乏场景中的 ASR 性能. Such transfer is particularly beneficial for low-resource languages, where both speech and text data are limited. By leveraging shared phonetic and semantic features across languages, SpeechLMs can improve ASR performance in low-resource scenarios[27, 26, 25]。这种多语言训练策略使模型能够学习更通用的表示。. This multilingual training strategy enables models to learn more universal representations.

人们已经开发了许多基准来从不同角度评估 ASR 系统。早期的基准测试主要关注英语和其他高资源语言Numerous benchmarks have been developed to evaluate ASR systems from different perspectives. Early benchmarks focused primarily on English and other high-resource languages[32, 29, 31, 28, 33, 36, 30, 34, 35]。最近的努力已扩展到多语言环境，并采用 CommonVoice 等基准. More recent efforts have expanded to multilingual settings, with benchmarks such as CommonVoice[37]和花and FLEURS[38]。然而，这些基准通常独立地对待语言，并且缺乏对不同语言家族特征（即德拉威语、乌拉尔语和突厥语）或文字多样性（拉丁语与非拉丁语）的系统分析。因此，它们对 ASR 模型如何跨语言泛化的了解有限。. However, these benchmarks typically treat languages independently and lack systematic analysis of typologically diverse language-family characteristics (i.e., Dravidian, Uralic, and Turkic) or script diversity (Latin vs. non-Latin). As a result, they provide limited insight into how ASR models generalize across languages.

Ⅱ-AII-A 语系Language Families

由于缺乏评估类型多样的语言家族中的 SpeechLM 的系统基准，Motivated by the absence of systematic benchmarks that assess SpeechLM in typologically diverse language families,LoASR 工作台LoASR-Bench结合跨家庭评估来评估类型多样性下模型的泛化性和稳健性。评估不同语系的语言至关重要，因为音韵、形态、句法和书写系统的变化会极大地影响 ASR 性能，特别是在资源匮乏的环境中。incorporates cross-family evaluations to assess model generalization and robustness under typological diversity. Evaluating languages from diverse linguistic families is essential, as variations in phonology, morphology, syntax, and writing systems can substantially influence ASR performance, particularly in low-resource settings.

我们的OurLoASR 工作台LoASR-Bench包括九个主要语系，涵盖德拉威语系（泰米尔语、泰卢固语、马拉雅拉姆语）、罗曼语系（葡萄牙语、西班牙语、法语、意大利语、加泰罗尼亚语、加利西亚语、罗马尼亚语）、乌拉尔语系（爱沙尼亚语、芬兰语、匈牙利语）、隔离语系（巴斯克语）、日本语（日语）、汉藏语系（粤语和普通话）、印度-雅利安语系（印地语、乌尔都语、阿萨姆语、马拉地语、孟加拉语、旁遮普语）、突厥语（鞑靼语）和韩语（朝鲜语）。这种广泛的覆盖范围可以对不同语言家族和脚本中的 SpeechLM 行为进行细粒度分析，揭示在单一语言或特定区域基准中无法观察到的优势和局限性。includes nine major language families, covering the Dravidian family (Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam), Romance (Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, Catalan, Galician, Romanian), Uralic (Estonian, Finnish, Hungarian), Isolate (Basque), Japonic (Japanese), Sino-Tibetan (Cantonese and Mandarin Chinese), Indo-Aryan (Hindi, Urdu, Assamese, Marathi, Bengali, Punjabi), Turkic (Tatar), and Koreanic (Korean). This broad coverage enables fine-grained analysis of SpeechLM behavior across diverse linguistic families and scripts, revealing strengths and limitations that are not observable in single-language or region-specific benchmarks.

Ⅱ-BII-B SpeechLM 和低资源语言微调SpeechLMs and Low-resource Language Fine-Tuning

我们评估了一系列广泛采用且最先进的 SpeechLM，它们在架构、规模和训练范式方面有所不同。其中包括 XLSR-53，这是一种基于 wav2vec 2.0 的自监督编码器模型，针对 53 种语言进行了预训练We evaluate a diverse set of widely adopted and state-of-the-art SpeechLMs that differ in architecture, scale, and training paradigm. These include XLSR-53, a self-supervised encoder model pre-trained on 53 languages based on wav2vec 2.0[8]; Whisper-Medium 和 Whisper-Large-v3，编码器-解码器 ASR 模型在 68 万小时的弱监督多语言数据上进行训练; Whisper-Medium and Whisper-Large-v3, encoder–decoder ASR models trained on 680k hours of weakly supervised multilingual data[21];以及 Qwen 系列的多模态 SpeechLM，包括 Qwen2-Audio-7B 和 Qwen3-Omni-30B; and multimodal SpeechLMs from the Qwen family, including Qwen2-Audio-7B and Qwen3-Omni-30B[39, 40, 41]。值得注意的是，Qwen3-Omni 进一步扩展了多语言能力，数据覆盖 100 多种语言。. Notably, Qwen3-Omni further extends multilingual capability with data covering over 100 languages.

为了支持在资源匮乏的环境中进行系统且公平的评估，我们建议使用 Common Voice 16.1 对 25 种目标语言中所有评估的 SpeechLM 进行微调To support systematic and fair evaluation in low-resource settings, we propose to fine-tune all evaluated SpeechLMs across 25 target languages using Common Voice 16.1[37]在统一且可扩展的培训协议下。 每个模型都训练 10 个 epoch，批量大小为 8，学习率为 5e-5，混合精度训练 (fp16)。 模型选择采用脚本感知验证，对拉丁文字使用 CER，对非拉丁文字使用 WER，并保留性能最好的检查点进行评估。文本规范化在所有模型和语言中一致应用，以确保可比性。这种提出的大规模微调策略建立了一个受控且可重复的基准设置，可以实现跨语言、脚本和语言家族的直接比较。所有实验均使用官方 Hugging Face Transformers 框架实现。under a unified and scalable training protocol. Each model is trained for 10 epochs with a batch size of 8, a learning rate of 5e-5, and mixed-precision training (fp16). Model selection adopts script-aware validation, using CER for Latin-script languages and WER for non-Latin scripts, with the best-performing checkpoint retained for evaluation. Text normalization is applied consistently across all models and languages to ensure comparability. This proposed large-scale fine-tuning strategy establishes a controlled and reproducible benchmark setting, enabling direct comparison across languages, scripts, and language families. All experiments are implemented using the official Hugging Face Transformers framework.

同时，尽管较小的模型与较大的模型相比表现出性能下降，但差距仍然相对较小，这表明对于计算资源受限的场景，较小的模型在低资源语言的计算成本和 ASR 准确性之间提供了合理的平衡。Meanwhile, despite the smaller models showing performance degradation compared to the bigger models, the gap remains relatively modest, suggesting that for scenarios where computing resources are constrained, smaller models offer a reasonable balance between computational costs and ASR accuracy for low-resource languages.

IV 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 LoASR-Bench，这是一个评估低资源 ASR 语音语言模型的基准，涵盖 9 个类型不同的语系的 25 种语言。我们的评估表明，Qwen3-Omni 比早期模型和 Whisper 基线有了显着改进，证实了广泛的多语言预训练的价值。此外，目标语言微调也发挥着重要作用，与未经微调的基本模型相比，经过微调的 Qwen2-Audio 显示出显着的错误减少。We introduce LoASR-Bench, a benchmark evaluating speech language models on low-resource ASR across 25 languages from 9 typologically diverse language families. Our evaluation reveals that Qwen3-Omni demonstrates substantial improvements over earlier models and Whisper baselines, confirming the value of extensive multilingual pretraining. Furthermore, target-language fine-tuning plays an important role, with fine-tuned Qwen2-Audio showing dramatic error reductions compared to the base model without fine-tuning.

我们发现，尽管当前的 SpeechLM 取得了成功，但仍然存在重大挑战。首先，拉丁文字和非拉丁文字之间的性能差距仍然存在，经常出现文字混淆和语言错误识别的情况。数据极其有限的语言（We find that despite the success of the current SpeechLMs, significant challenges remain. We also find that identifying the language is crucial in the inference of ASR.
