自动形式化（AF）系统将自然语言推理步骤转化为形式化的数学陈述，然后可由 Lean（de Moura 和 Ullrich 2021）、Rocq（Coquand 和 Huet 1988）和 Isabelle（Paulson 1990）等证明助手进行验证。人们越来越有兴趣使用 AF 来验证大型语言模型 (LLM) 的思想链 (CoT) 推理，其想法是单独形式化每个步骤并使用证明助手检查其正确性（Quan et al. 2025；Zhou and Zhuang 2025；Liu et al. 2025a）。对于这些用例，自动对焦系统至关重要Autoformalisation (AF) systems translate natural-language reasoning steps into formal mathematical statements that can then be verified by proof assistants like Lean (de Moura and Ullrich 2021), Rocq (Coquand and Huet 1988), and Isabelle (Paulson 1990). 这些方法涉及对比权衡。一方面，虽然通常非常可靠，但人工注释的数据集获取起来非常缓慢且昂贵。另一方面，神经方法虽然快速且可扩展，但无法保证准确性，并且无法识别形式化中的细微错误。此外，这两种方法通常都假设输入的自然语言语句是正确的，因此不会测试 AF 系统是否可以保留不正确语句中的错误，这对于 CoT 验证等应用程序至关重要，因为在这些应用程序中，重点是识别不正确的推理步骤。These approaches involve contrasting tradeoffs. On the one hand, while usually very reliable, human-annotated datasets are very slow and expensive to obtain. In addition, both approaches typically assume that the input natural language statements are correct, and so do not test whether the AF system can preserve errors in incorrect statements, which is crucial for applications such as CoT verification where the entire point is to identify reasoning steps that are incorrect.

7 结论7 Conclusion

我们的结果揭示了当前 AF 训练中的紧张局势：最能保持正确输入有效性的系统也最容易对错误输入进行无声纠正。这表明 AF 管道隐含地将忠实翻译与生成可证明的声明混为一谈。这在 CoT 验证设置中造成了一个陷阱，其中不正确的输入正是我们感兴趣的情况。Our results reveal a tension in current AF training: the systems best at preserving validity on correct inputs are also the most prone to silent correction on incorrect ones. This suggests that AF pipelines implicitly conflate faithful translation with producing a provable statement. This poses a pitfall in the CoT verification setting, where incorrect inputs are precisely the cases of interest.

我们认为，解决这种紧张关系给用于验证的自动对焦系统带来了一个重要的开放性问题。一个自然的方向是训练自动对焦系统，故意暴露于无效输入，这样它们就会学会保留错误而不是修复错误。另一个是将我们提出的基于扰动的方法扩展到其他自然到形式的翻译任务（例如，形式规范、规范代码），其中静默校正具有类似的成本。In our view, addressing this tension poses an important open problem for AF systems intended for verification. One natural direction is to train AF systems with deliberate exposure to invalid inputs, so they learn to preserve errors rather than repair them. Another is to extend the perturbationbased methodology we propose to other natural-to-formal translation tasks (e.g., formal specifications, code-from-spec) where silent correction has similar costs.

局限性Limitations

我们的方法并没有完全普遍地评估语义漂移，因此不是Our method does not assess semantic drift in full generality, and is therefore not完成complete：即使 UFLB 度量非常小，这并不意味着 AF 一定是忠实的。例如，(6) 中的 AF 将返回完美的 UFLB 分数，但显然是不忠实的。此外，我们的方法依赖于对 Prove 组件的强大证明者的访问。如果这不够强大，那么即使应用于明显会引发错误或默默纠正的 AF，该方法也可能返回不确定的结果。总的来说，因此我们建议在评估自动形式化器时将 FaithformBench 用作一种诊断措施，而不是单独作为完整的可靠性证书。: even if the UFLB metric is very small, this does not imply that the AF is necessarily faithful. For example, the AF in (6) would return a perfect UFLB score, but is clearly unfaithful. In addition, our method relies on access to a strong prover for the component Prove. If this is not powerful enough, then the method may return inconclusive results even when applied to an AF that is clearly error inducing or silently correcting. Overall, we therefore recommend that FaithformBench be used as one diagnostic measure among others when evaluating autoformalisers, rather than as a complete certificate of reliability on its own.

正文（以及附录 B）中的结果是通过 BrokenMath 风格的基于 LLM 的扰动函数获得的（Petrov、Dekoninck 和 Vechev 2025）。一个自然的问题是我们的结论是否取决于珀特的这一特定选择。为了评估这一点，我们在 FaithformBench-Regex 上重复了对四个微调自动形式化器的全面评估（参见附录 A），其中每个可验证的步骤都与基于规则的正则表达式扰动函数生成的对应步骤配对。The results in the main text (and in Appendix B) are obtained with the BrokenMath-style LLM-based perturbation function (Petrov, Dekoninck, and Vechev 2025). A natural question is whether our conclusions depend on this particular choice of Pert. To assess this, we repeated the full evaluation of the four fine-tuned autoformalisers on FaithformBench-Regex (see Appendix A), in which each verifiable step is instead paired with a counterpart produced by a rule-based, regex perturbation function.

基于正则表达式的扰动函数。Regex-based perturbation function.正则表达式干扰器应用基于正则表达式的转换的层次结构。它首先尝试两次数字编辑：更改最右边的数字文字（通过最多其大小一半的随机偏移量）并交换算术或比较运算符（偏向最右边的出现）；两种编辑都可以应用于同一步骤，并且由于大多数步骤都包含方程，因此它们成功地扰乱了大部分数据集。仅当两种编辑均不适用时，干扰者才会回退到否定：如果语句已经包含“not”，则将其删除，否则它会在第一个情态动词或系动词之后插入“not”（例如，将“大于”转换为“不大于”）；作为最后的后备措施，它将字符串“It is false that”添加到整个语句前面。与基于 LLM 的扰动器不同，这个过程是无 LLM 的，并且在我们发布的配置的固定随机种子的情况下完全可重现，但它也更加模板化，并且偶尔可以生成仍然正确的语句（例如，将“17 是奇数”变成“13 是奇数”），这促使我们选择基于 LLM 的策略作为主要扰动方法。The regex perturber applies a hierarchy of regular-expression-based transformations. It first attempts two numeric edits: changing the rightmost numeric literal (by a random offset of up to half its magnitude) and swapping an arithmetic or comparison operator (biased towards the rightmost occurrence); both edits can apply to the same step and, as most steps contain equations, they successfully perturb the majority of the dataset. Only when neither edit applies does the perturber fall back to negation: if the statement already contains a “not” it removes it, otherwise it inserts “not” after the first modal or copular verb (e.g., transforming “is larger than” into “is not larger than”); as a final fallback, it prepends the string “It is false that” to the entire statement. Unlike the LLM-based perturber, this procedure is LLM-free and fully reproducible given the fixed random seed of our released configuration, but it is also more templated and can occasionally produce a statement that is still true (e.g., turning “17 is odd” into “13 is odd”), which motivated our choice of the LLM-based strategy as the primary perturbation method.
