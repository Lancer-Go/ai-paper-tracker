远程工作拓展了职业晋升的途径Remote work expands pathways to upward career mobility

Yunhan Zheng1,2∗, Jinhua Zhao3    1College of Urban and Environmental Sciences, Peking University, Beijing 100871, PR China.    2Laboratory for Earth Surface Processes of the Ministry of Education, Peking University, Beijing 100871, PR China.    3Department of Urban Studies and Planning, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge 02139, MA, USA.    ∗Corresponding author. Email: yunhan@pku.edu.cn
摘要Abstract

长期以来，地理限制一直限制着人们获得高增长职业机会的机会，向上流动性集中在有限的城市和组织内。远程工作的扩展可能会通过将工作匹配与物理距离脱钩来改变这种机会结构，但其对职业流动性的影响仍不清楚。利用 2020 年至 2024 年间美国 4800 万个与雇主层面的远程资格衡量标准相关的工作转换，我们估计了进入符合远程资格的工作如何影响工作转换时的职业成果。与完全在现场工作的同类工人相比，进入符合远程资格的工作岗位的工人的工资增长率和资历向上流动率要高得多。这些转变还与更大的跨城市工作流动性以及向规模较小、声誉较差的雇主的流动有关。重要的是，对低收入工人和来自高技能机会密度有限地区的工人的影响最大。总之，研究结果表明，远程工作放松了工作匹配的地理限制，重塑了不同地点和工人之间向上流动的分布。Geographic constraints have long structured access to high-growth career opportunities, concentrating upward mobility within a limited set of cities and organizations. The expansion of remote work potentially alters this opportunity structure by decoupling job matching from physical proximity, yet its implications for career mobility remain unclear. Using 48 million U.S. job transitions between 2020 and 2024 linked to employer-level measures of remote eligibility, we estimate how entering remote-eligible jobs shapes career outcomes at job transitions. Workers entering remote-eligible jobs experience significantly higher wage growth and higher rates of upward seniority mobility than comparable workers entering fully on-site roles. These transitions are also associated with greater cross-metropolitan job mobility and moves toward smaller, less prestigious employers. Importantly, effects are largest among lower-income workers and those originating from regions with limited high-skill opportunity density. Together, the findings indicate that remote work relaxes geographic constraints in job matching, reshaping the distribution of upward mobility across places and workers.

美国的高薪就业日益集中在一小部分高生产力的大都市区和主导企业，文献中经常将其描述为“超级明星城市”和“超级明星组织”（High-wage employment in the United States has become increasingly concentrated in a small set of highly productive metropolitan areas and dominant firms, often described in the literature as “superstar cities” and “superstar organizations”  (?, ?, ?）。这种空间和组织聚集被认为限制了职业向上流动的机会。为了进入高增长的职业道路，工人必须经常搬到昂贵的城市中心，并在少数精选雇主中竞争职位（). This spatial and organizational clustering has been argued to restrict access to upward career mobility. To enter high-growth career paths, workers must often relocate to expensive urban centers and compete for positions within a narrow set of selective employers  (?, ?）。结果，许多合格的工人仍然与劳动力市场最具活力的机会脱节，仅仅是因为他们在地理或制度上遥不可及（). As a result, many qualified workers remain disconnected from the labor market’s most dynamic opportunities simply because they are geographically or institutionally out of reach  (?, ?, ?).

COVID-19 大流行后远程工作的迅速扩张已经开始放松这些长期存在的限制。远程和混合安排现在在美国受过大学教育的工人中很常见（The rapid expansion of remote work after the COVID-19 pandemic has begun to loosen these long-standing constraints. Remote and hybrid arrangements, now common among college-educated workers in the United States  (?, ?），降低了职业发展对经济中心地理位置的依赖程度。在此过程中，远程工作可能会重塑职业机会的地理分布，因为它允许工人在当地劳动力市场以外的地方找到雇主，而无需搬迁或每天通勤到主要经济中心。这种转变向研究人员和政策制定者提出了一个核心问题：远程工作是否真正扩大了获得高增长就业的机会，还是会给采用远程工作的人带来新形式的不利条件？), have reduced the extent to which career advancement depends on geographic proximity to economic hubs. In doing so, remote work potentially reshapes the geography of career opportunity by allowing workers to access employers beyond their local labor markets without requiring relocation or daily commuting to major economic hubs. This shift raises a central question for researchers and policymakers: does remote work genuinely broaden access to high-growth employment, or does it introduce new forms of disadvantage for those who adopt it?

现有研究强调了与远程灵活性相关的潜在权衡。 补偿差异理论强调，工人可能会接受较低的工资，以换取灵活性等有价值的便利设施（Existing research highlights potential trade-offs associated with remote flexibility. The theory of compensating differentials emphasizes that workers may accept lower wages in exchange for valued amenities such as flexibility  (?, ?, ?）。城市和组织经济学的相关工作强调了空间邻近性和面对面互动在通过公司和当地劳动力市场塑造职业道路方面的作用（). Related work in urban and organizational economics underscores the role of spatial proximity and in-person interaction in shaping career pathways through firms and local labor markets (?, ?, ?）。然而，尽管有这些理论观点，关于远程工作如何重塑职业轨迹的经验证据仍然有限。现有的研究主要依赖于横断面调查或单一公司案例研究，主要关注短期结果，例如生产力或主观幸福感（). Yet despite these theoretical perspectives, empirical evidence on how remote work reshapes career trajectories remains limited. Existing studies—relying largely on cross-sectional surveys or single-firm case studies—have primarily focused on short-term outcomes, such as productivity or subjective well-being  (?, ?, ?）。我们缺乏系统的、全经济范围的证据来证明远程工作资格如何影响长期职业成果，包括工资成果和工作过渡时的资历向上流动，以及组织和地域流动模式。). We lack systematic, economy-wide evidence on how remote work eligibility shapes long-term career outcomes, including wage outcomes and upward seniority mobility at job transitions, as well as patterns of organizational and geographic mobility.

为了研究这个问题，我们收集了一个大型数据集，将工人的职业轨迹与他们所从事的工作的远程资格状态联系起来。我们将涵盖数千万个求职事件的纵向就业历史与数亿条职位发布记录相结合，这些记录表明特定职位在招聘时是否被指定为远程资格。重要的是，远程工作资格是根据工人工作转换之前的雇主职位发布来衡量的，这使我们能够将远程资格描述为由雇主而不是个人谈判或工人偏好决定的工作结构的一个特征。这种联系使我们能够在每次工作转换的层面上衡量远程工作的资格，作为雇主确定的工作的供给方特征。然后，我们将进入远程资格和完全在职职位的员工与进入前的相似特征进行比较，并估计远程资格如何影响随后的职业成果，包括工资增长、资历向上流动以及组织和地域流动模式。To examine this question, we assemble a large-scale dataset that links workers’ career trajectories to the remote-eligibility status of the jobs they enter. We combine longitudinal employment histories covering tens of millions of job-entry events with hundreds of millions of job-posting records that indicate whether specific roles were designated as remote-eligible at the time of hiring. Importantly, remote-work eligibility is measured from employer job postings prior to the worker’s job transition, allowing us to characterize remote eligibility as a feature of job structure determined by employers rather than by individual negotiation or worker preferences. This linkage allows us to measure remote-work eligibility at the level of each job transition as a supply-side feature of the job determined by employers. We then compare workers entering remote-eligible and fully in-office positions with similar pre-entry characteristics and estimate how remote eligibility shapes subsequent career outcomes, including wage growth, upward seniority mobility, and patterns of organizational and geographic mobility.

我们的研究结果表明，远程工作扩大了向上流动的途径，而不仅仅是精英组织。进入符合远程资格的工作的工人会经历更高的工资增长和更大的资历向上流动性，伴随着独特的组织流动性模式和扩大当地劳动力市场以外的工作机会。工人通常不是主要进入有声望的组织，而是经常转向规模较小、声望较低的雇主，包括当地劳动力市场之外的雇主。这些影响会随着时间的推移而持续存在，并根据工人之前的收入而系统性地变化，低收入工人的工资涨幅更大。Our findings suggest that remote work expands pathways to upward mobility that are not confined to elite organizations. Workers entering remote-eligible jobs experience higher wage growth and greater upward seniority mobility, accompanied by a distinctive pattern of organizational mobility and expanded access to jobs beyond local labor markets. Rather than primarily moving into prestigious organizations, workers frequently transition toward smaller and less prestigious employers, including those outside their local labor markets. These effects persist over time and vary systematically by workers’ prior earnings, with larger wage gains among lower-paid workers.

我们的研究对远程工作和劳动力市场机会的文献做出了三项贡献。首先，利用有关职业历史和职位发布的大规模观察数据，我们提供了系统性的、整个经济范围的证据，表明远程工作的资格与职业向上流动相关，而不是系统性的职业惩罚。这一证据证明了人们长期以来对工作场所灵活性是否会以牺牲职业发展或工资为代价的担忧（Our study makes three contributions to the literature on remote work and labor-market opportunity. First, using large-scale observational data on career histories and job postings, we provide systematic, economy-wide evidence that eligibility for remote work is associated with upward career mobility, rather than systematic career penalties. This evidence speaks to long-standing concerns about whether workplace flexibility comes at the expense of career advancement or wages (?, ?, ?).

其次，我们确定了一种独特的向上流动模式，其特征是符合远程资格的工作转换。 我们发现，进入符合远程资格的工作岗位的员工在进入规模较小、声望较低的组织的同时，实现了更高的工资增长和更高的资历晋升率。 这种模式背离了传统观点，即职业发展主要与大型、有声望的组织的就业有关，正如劳动力市场分类模型所强调的那样（Second, we identify a distinctive pattern of upward mobility characterizing remote-eligible job transitions. We find that workers entering remote-eligible jobs achieve higher wage growth and higher rates of upward seniority moves while sorting into organizations that are smaller and less prestigious. This pattern departs from the conventional view that career advancement is primarily tied to employment at large, prestigious organizations, as emphasized in models of labor-market sorting (?, ?），揭示了远程合格劳动力市场转型中职业发展的独特配置。), revealing a distinct configuration of career advancement in remote-eligible labor-market transitions.

第三，虽然之前的研究强调，获得远程工作的机会不成比例地集中在高技能、高收入的工人身上，但人们担心远程工作可能会加剧劳动力市场的不平等（Third, while prior work emphasizes that access to remote work is disproportionately concentrated among high-skilled, high-income workers—raising concerns that remote work may exacerbate labor market inequality (?, ?）——我们表明，在考虑了工人之前收入的差异后，符合远程资格的工作进入与收入分配中较高的工资增长和向上的资历流动性相关。 这些收益存在于所有收入三分位数中，但在先前收入最低群体的工人中增幅最大，这表明远程资格的回报并不局限于已经处于有利地位的工人。 这种模式凸显了远程工作以前被低估的分配维度，表明其相关的职业收益远远超出了收入分配的上限。)—we show that, after accounting for differences in workers’ prior earnings, remote-eligible job entry is associated with higher wage growth and upward seniority mobility across the earnings distribution. These gains are present in all income terciles but are largest among workers in the lowest prior-income groups, indicating that the returns to remote eligibility are not confined to already advantaged workers. This pattern highlights a previously underappreciated distributional dimension of remote work, showing that its associated career gains extend well beyond the top of the earnings distribution.

数据和识别策略Data and Identification Strategy

为了研究符合远程资格的工作结构如何重塑职业流动性，我们结合了两个大型数据集，这些数据集捕获了已实现的工作转换和远程工作机会的供应。第一个数据集来自 Revelio Labs，包含根据公共专业档案构建的纵向就业历史，使我们能够观察详细的工作进入事件，包括工资、资历水平、职业、雇主和大都市位置。第二个数据集 Cosmos 汇总了雇主发布的职位空缺，并提供有关工作特征的标准化信息，包括远程和混合工作安排。To examine how remote-eligible job structures reshape career mobility, we combine two large-scale datasets that capture both realized job transitions and the supply of remote work opportunities. The first dataset, from Revelio Labs, contains longitudinal employment histories constructed from public professional profiles, allowing us to observe detailed job-entry events including wages, seniority levels, occupations, employers, and metropolitan locations. The second dataset, Cosmos, aggregates employer-posted vacancies and provides standardized information on job characteristics, including remote and hybrid work arrangements.

通过将求职事件与招聘组织的同期职位空缺联系起来，我们在招聘时构建了远程资格的供应方衡量标准。对于每次过渡，我们都会在三个月的聘用前窗口内匹配目的地雇主的职位，并在大多数匹配的职位表明远程或混合安排时将工作分类为远程资格。该程序根据进入者面临的机会结构而不是个人谈判结果来分配远程资格。我们的主要分析样本包括 2020 年至 2024 年间美国发生的 4800 万起求职事件，其中约 100 万起转变为符合远程资格的职位。By linking job-entry events to contemporaneous vacancy postings at the hiring organization, we construct a supply-side measure of remote eligibility at the point of hire. For each transition, we match the destination employer’s postings within a three-month pre-hire window and classify a job as remote-eligible when the majority of matched postings indicate remote or hybrid arrangements. This procedure assigns remote eligibility based on the opportunity structure facing entrants rather than individual negotiation outcomes. Our primary analytic sample includes 48 million job-entry events in the United States between 2020 and 2024, including approximately one million transitions into remote-eligible roles.

由于进入远程工作和完全现场工作的工人的特征可能有所不同，因此我们实施了逆概率加权回归框架来近似可比较的工作转换。现场输入被重新加权，以匹配可观察的输入前特征（包括人口特征、先前工资、资历、职业、行业、先前雇主特征和入职年份指标）的远程资格转变，并在吸收大都市区固定效应的同时，在所得加权样本中估计处理效果。使用个体内比较的补充分析产生了相似的效应量，表明结果不太可能是由稳定的个体差异驱动的（补充部分S3）。其他技术细节在方法的统计分析部分中描述。Because workers entering remote-eligible and fully on-site jobs may differ in their characteristics, we implement an inverse-probability-weighted regression framework to approximate comparable job transitions. On-site entries are reweighted to match remote-eligible transitions on observable pre-entry characteristics—including demographic attributes, prior wages, seniority, occupation, industry, previous employer characteristics, and job-entry-year indicators—and treatment effects are estimated within the resulting weighted sample while absorbing metropolitan-area fixed effects. Supplementary analyses using within-individual comparisons yield similar effect magnitudes, suggesting that the results are unlikely to be driven by stable individual differences (Supplementary Section S3). Additional technical details are described in the Statistical analysis section of the Methods.

远程工作是工资和职业流动性的驱动力Remote Work as a Driver of Wage and Career Mobility

为了估计远程工作资格对职业成果的影响，我们将逆概率加权与固定效应回归相结合，以解决远程工作的非随机排序问题（详细信息请参阅方法）。与预测工作灵活性的工资惩罚的补偿差异框架相反，结果显示，在统计上，与进入远程合格工作相关的工资溢价是稳健的。To estimate the effects of remote work eligibility on career outcomes, we combine inverse probability weighting with fixed-effects regression to address non-random sorting into remote jobs (see Methods for details). In contrast to the compensating-differentials framework that predicts wage penalties for work flexibility, the results reveal a statistically robust wage premium associated with entering a remote-eligible job.

讨论Discussion

这项研究表明，远程工作的资格通过改变工作转换的结构而不是仅仅提供短期灵活性来重塑职业发展。在远程工作已经形成稳定和持久组织模式的劳动力市场中，我们的研究结果表明，远程资格与工资增长的持久收益相关，反映了工作转型期间的一系列机制，包括扩大有效机会集、职业升级和更高的资历晋升率。这些成果是随着组织和空间轨迹的系统性转变而产生的。具有远程资格的员工更有可能选择规模较小、声誉较差的组织，并参与跨城市的工作转换，这揭示了一种独特的向上流动模式，这种模式不依赖于与地理位置或组织地位相关的传统职业门户。This study shows that eligibility for remote work reshapes career advancement by altering the structure of job transitions rather than merely offering short-term flexibility. In a labor market where remote work has settled into a stable and persistent mode of organization, our findings indicate that remote eligibility is associated with durable gains in wage growth, reflecting a combination of mechanisms during job transitions, including expanded effective opportunity sets, occupation upgrading, and higher rates of upward seniority moves. These gains arise alongside systematic shifts in organizational and spatial trajectories. Workers in remote-eligible roles are more likely to sort toward smaller and less prestigious organizations and to engage in cross-metropolitan job transitions, revealing a distinct pattern of upward mobility that does not rely on traditional career gateways tied to physical proximity or organizational status.

这些结果的一个重要含义是，在远程工作已经普遍存在的劳动力市场（例如近年来的美国），职业发展不必与地理位置或组织声望紧密相关。劳动力市场流动性的传统解释强调集聚经济（A critical implication of these results is that, in labor markets where remote work has become widespread—such as the United States in recent years—career advancement need not remain tightly coupled to physical proximity or organizational prestige. In contrast, our evidence suggests that the expansion of remote-eligible work relaxes these constraints by decoupling access to employment opportunities from geographic proximity, allowing workers to compete for positions beyond their local labor markets without relocating. Workers who enter remote-eligible roles achieve faster wage growth and higher rates of upward seniority moves even as they sort away from established hubs and elite organizations. This pattern is difficult to reconcile with compensating-differentials models that predict wage penalties for flexibility and instead suggests that remote work is creating alternative pathways to upward mobility that are less tightly tied to traditional geographic and organizational hierarchies.

重要的是，远程资格的好处分布并不均匀。我们发现，对于收入最低的十分之一的工人以及来自高科技就业集中度相对有限的地区的工人来说，收益最大。这种模式表明，尽管远程工作为整个收入分配带来了好处，但其收益不成比例地集中在当地劳动力市场提供的高价值就业机会较少的工人身上。Importantly, the benefits of remote eligibility are not evenly distributed. We find that the gains are largest for workers in the lowest income terciles and for those originating from regions with relatively limited concentrations of high-tech employment. This pattern suggests that, although remote work delivers benefits across the income distribution, its gains are disproportionately concentrated among workers whose local labor markets offer fewer high-value employment opportunities.

这些发现有两个更广泛的含义。首先，主要从工人福祉、工作与生活平衡或短期生产力角度来构建远程工作的观点忽视了远程工作在塑造职业轨迹和劳动力市场效率方面的更深层次的结构性作用。我们的研究结果表明，远程资格是通过核心流动机制（职业升级、更高的资历晋升可能性以及扩大获得更高质量工作匹配的机会）来运作的，而不是通过补偿性权衡来实现。其次，持续努力将工作严格限制在实体办公室内，可能会低估限制远程选择的效率成本。通过扩大在少数雇主和地点之外获得高增长职位的机会，远程资格可以成为释放未充分利用的人力资本的杠杆，有可能提高国家范围内的配置效率，并缩小长期存在的经济机会的地区差距。These findings have two broader implications. First, perspectives that frame remote work primarily in terms of worker well-being, work–life balance, or short-run productivity overlook its deeper structural role in shaping career trajectories and labor-market efficiency. Our results show that remote eligibility operates through core mobility mechanisms—occupation upgrading, higher probabilities of upward seniority moves and expanded access to higher-quality job matches—rather than through compensating trade-offs. Second, ongoing efforts to re-anchor work strictly within physical offices risk underestimating the efficiency costs of constraining remote options. By expanding access to high-growth roles beyond a narrow set of employers and locations, remote eligibility acts as a lever for unlocking underutilized human capital, with the potential to improve allocative efficiency at the national scale and to mitigate long-standing regional disparities in economic opportunity.

我们认为这项研究是系统记录远程工作如何重塑现代劳动力市场机会结构的第一步。有几个限制值得注意。 首先，虽然我们的识别策略利用了个体内部差异和丰富的进入前控制，但我们不能完全排除在工作转型时因职业升级而产生的时变混杂，即工人进入薪水更高但也更遥远的职业。然而，数据中的几种模式表明，这种机制不太可能成为我们研究结果的主要驱动力：估计的影响并不集中在高工资、工程或传统远程职业中，也不集中在来自高科技大都市地区的工人中，并且在限制对个人内部、相同职业转换的关注或吸收逐年职业固定效应时仍然保持稳健（补充第7节）。We view this study as an initial step toward systematically documenting how remote work reshapes the structure of opportunity in modern labor markets. Several limitations are worth noting. First, while our identification strategy leverages within-individual variation and rich pre-entry controls, we cannot fully rule out time-varying confounding arising from occupational upgrading at the moment of job transition, whereby workers move into higher-paying occupations that are also more remote-feasible. However, several patterns in the data suggest that this mechanism is unlikely to be the primary driver of our findings: the estimated effects are not concentrated among high-wage, engineering, or traditionally remote occupations, nor among workers originating from high-tech metropolitan areas, and remain robust when restricting attention to within-individual, same-occupation switches or absorbing occupation-by-entry-year fixed effects (Supplementary Section 7).

其次，我们的分析重点是入职时远程工作的资格，捕捉工作结构如何影响职业转型，而不是聘用后远程工作的实际强度或持续时间。因此，我们的估计反映了与获得远程资格职位相关的职业影响，而不是远程工作本身的直接影响。 第三，虽然我们将职业升级、资历晋升和扩大机会集视为核心机制，但我们并没有直接观察可能受到远程工作影响的组织内部动态，例如指导、隐性知识转移或网络形成，这些可能会影响更长期的职业轨迹。 最后，我们的证据来自 2020 年后的美国劳动力市场，该市场的特点是远程工作采用率和劳动力流动性相对较高。类似的机制是否在其他国家或机构环境中运作仍然是未来研究的一个悬而未决的问题。Second, our analysis focuses on eligibility for remote work at the point of job entry, capturing how job structure shapes career transitions rather than the realized intensity or duration of remote work after hiring. As a result, our estimates reflect the career effects associated with access to remote-eligible positions rather than the direct impact of working remotely itself. Third, while we identify occupation upgrading, upward seniority mobility and expanded opportunity sets as central mechanisms, we do not directly observe within-organization dynamics that may also be affected by remote work, such as mentorship, tacit knowledge transfer, or network formation, which could influence career trajectories over longer horizons. Finally, our evidence is drawn from the post-2020 U.S. labor market, which features relatively high levels of remote work adoption and labor mobility. Whether similar mechanisms operate in other countries or institutional contexts remains an open question for future research.

附录S5Appendix S5对资历晋升的替代定义的稳健性Robustness to alternative definitions of upward seniority moves

职位名称和资历标签在不同规模的组织中并不具有统一的经济含义：在大型雇主和小型雇主中，相同的名义头衔可能对应于明显不同水平的薪酬、责任和组织范围。因此，基于职称的资历晋升指标可能会将职位级别的实质性变化与雇主之间职称惯例的差异混为一谈。Job titles and seniority labels do not have a uniform economic meaning across organizations of different sizes: the same nominal title can correspond to markedly different levels of compensation, responsibility, and organizational scope in large versus small employers. As a result, title-based indicators of upward seniority moves may conflate substantive changes in job rank with differences in title conventions across employers.

这种担忧在符合远程资格的工作转换的背景下尤其重要，这可能会扩大小型或年轻雇主获得机会的机会。为了评估主要分析中记录的资历上升效应是否反映了资历的实质性变化而不是名义头衔的重新分类，我们根据雇主规模调整的资历上升的替代定义进行了稳健性分析。This concern is particularly relevant in the context of remote-eligible job transitions, which may expand access to opportunities at smaller or younger employers. To assess whether the upward seniority effects documented in the main analysis reflect substantive changes in seniority rather than nominal title reclassifications, we conduct a robustness analysis based on alternative, employer-size-adjusted definitions of upward seniority moves.

For example, senior roles at mid-sized and large employers tend to occupy nearby, but not identical, positions in the wage-equivalent space, indicating horizontal shifts in wage levels rather than changes in the seniority hierarchy. We also find that at the upper end of the employer size distribution, larger employers do not always offer higher predicted wages than slightly smaller large employers.

On the one hand, the dominance of seniority gradients supports the interpretation of reported titles as informative signals of job level. On the other hand, the visible horizontal dispersion within each seniority band highlights why nominal title changes across employers of different sizes may not correspond to equivalent economic advancement. In particular, a move to a higher-sounding title at a smaller employer can leave a worker at a similar—or even lower—position in the wage-equivalent space.

因此，工资-资历热图激发了对雇主规模校准的需求。通过将资历转变锚定到其预测的工资等值内容，分析中使用的调整后的资历上升衡量标准过滤了纯粹的名义头衔膨胀，并重点关注经济方面的实质性向上流动性。The wage–seniority heatmap therefore motivates the need for employer-size calibration. By anchoring seniority transitions to their predicted wage-equivalent content, the adjusted upward seniority measures used in the analysis filter out purely nominal title inflation and focus on substantive upward mobility in economic terms.
