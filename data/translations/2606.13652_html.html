摘要Abstract

图像到 3D 方法通常会在忠实性和完整性之间进行权衡：深度估计器锚定到输入像素，但停留在可见表面，而图像到 3D 模型生成的完整形状通常与输入不对齐。我们引入了世界追踪，这是一种生成像素对齐的几何表示，可以预测与观察到的像素对齐的 3D 点，同时完成可见表面之外的几何。 对于每个输入像素，世界追踪预测相机空间 3D 点的有序堆栈，其中堆栈的第一层表示可见表面，后续层表示与遮挡表面的前后交叉点。 我们使用世界追踪扩散变换器（WT-DiT）实例化这种表示，它将多个几何层视为通过分解和全局注意力耦合的单独的去噪标记。 WT-DiT 使用像素空间流匹配和混合噪声调度进行训练，以平衡可见表面重建与遮挡几何生成。 World Tracing 在可见表面重建和跨对象、场景和动态基准的完整几何生成方面实现了强大的性能，优于深度预测器和图像到 3D 生成器。它还保留 2D 到 3D 的对应关系，从而实现文本驱动的 3D 场景编辑、几何条件新颖视图视频合成以及与纹理网格生成器的免训练集成。Image-to-3D methods often trade off faithfulness and completeness: depth estimators are anchored to input pixels but stop at the visible surface, while image-to-3D models generate complete shapes that are often misaligned to the input. We introduce World Tracing, a generative pixel-aligned geometry representation that predicts 3D points aligned with the observed pixels while completing geometry beyond the visible surface. For each input pixel, World Tracing predicts an ordered stack of camera-space 3D points, where the first layer of the stack represents the visible surface and subsequent layers represent front-to-back intersections with occluded surfaces. We instantiate this representation with a world-tracing diffusion transformer (WT-DiT), which treats multiple geometry layers as separate denoising tokens coupled through factorized and global attention. WT-DiT is trained with pixel-space flow matching and a mixed noise schedule that balances visible-surface reconstruction with occluded-geometry generation. World Tracing achieves strong performance on visible-surface reconstruction and complete geometry generation across object, scene, and dynamic benchmarks, outperforming both depth predictors and image-to-3D generators. It also preserves 2D-to-3D correspondence, enabling text-driven 3D scene editing, geometry-conditioned novel-view video synthesis, and training-free integration with textured-mesh generators.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了世界追踪，一种用于忠实生成的像素对齐多层几何表示，其中可见表面保真度和遮挡几何完成成为一个相机空间张量的连续层。 WT-DiT 使用冻结的 2D 基础编码器、流匹配和仅 XYZ 深度填充目标来学习这种表示，并应用于对象、场景和动态剪辑。由此产生的几何形状不仅在基准上是准确的，而且在基准上也是准确的。它还为场景编辑、视图合成和姿势对齐纹理网格生成提供了更强大的 2D 到 3D 接口。我们希望 WT 成为 3D 感知、生成、编辑和视频的通用基础。We presented World Tracing, a pixel-aligned multilayer geometry representation for faithful generation, where visible-surface fidelity and occluded-geometry completion become successive layers of one camera-space tensor. WT-DiT learns this representation with a frozen 2D foundation encoder, flow matching, and an XYZ-only depth-filling objective, and applies across objects, scenes, and dynamic clips. The resulting geometry is not only accurate in benchmarks; it also provides a stronger 2D-to-3D interface for scene editing, view synthesis, and pose-aligned textured mesh generation. We hope WT becomes a common substrate for 3D-aware perception, generation, editing, and video.

F.1 有几层？How many layers?

六层是一个实用的最佳点，而不是一个任意的常数。正如应用程序中报道的那样。Six layers are a practical sweet spot rather than an arbitrary constant. As reported in App.D，有效支撑在前两个面之后迅速下降；后面的层捕获薄结构和重复遮挡，但它们的边缘区域很小。增加, valid support falls rapidly after the first two surfaces; later layers capture thin structures and repeated occlusions, but their marginal area is small. IncreasingLL可以改善罕见的高度穿孔的形状，如树叶、笼子或格栅，但它也会增加记忆力，并使本来就稀疏的尾巴更难监督。这激发了未来的可变深度或自适应射线表示，同时保持would improve rare highly perforated shapes such as foliage, cages, or grates, but it also increases memory and makes the already sparse tail harder to supervise. F.2 XYZ 点图与深度加内在函数XYZ pointmaps versus depth plus intrinsics

我们还测试了使用相机内在函数而不是相机空间 XYZ 来预测深度。尽管深度+本征是紧凑且可解释的，但它通过全局校准预测耦合每条光线：焦距或主点的小误差会连贯地扭曲整个形状。直接 XYZ 预测将这种校准吸收到点图本身中，从而产生更合理的全局形状，尤其是在裁剪、掩模和相机元数据不确定的真实世界图像上。这与基于点图的重建器的动机相匹配：输出已经是 2D 到 3D 地图，并且可以在需要时从预测的 L0 几何体中恢复内在函数。We also tested predicting depth with camera intrinsics instead of camera-space XYZ. Although depth+intrinsics is compact and interpretable, it couples every ray through a global calibration prediction: small errors in focal length or principal point coherently warp the whole shape. Direct XYZ prediction absorbs this calibration into the pointmap itself, which leads to more plausible global shape, especially on real-world images whose crop, mask, and camera metadata are uncertain. This matches the motivation of pointmap-based reconstructors: the output is already a 2D-to-3D map, and intrinsics can be recovered from the predicted L0 geometry when needed.

F.4 超越训练制度的概括Generalization beyond the training regime

有两种经验行为尤其令人鼓舞。首先，即使没有场景训练，对象模型也会在场景图像上产生合理的几何形状。这表明像素对齐的多层预测比规范的图像到 3D 生成更自然地跨对象和场景体系进行传输，后者的输出帧和单个对象假设在杂乱的场景中会被打破。其次，尽管没有经过多对象增强的显式训练，但对象模型对于一张图像中的多个对象具有惊人的鲁棒性。由于 WT 预测每个输入光线一个堆栈，而不是每个裁剪一个规范资产，因此多个断开连接的对象可以共存于同一输出张量中。这些观察结果表明了明确的下一步：将对象和场景训练统一到一个更强大的模型中，而不是维护单独的专家。Two empirical behaviors are especially encouraging. First, the object model, even without scene training, produces reasonable geometry on scene images. This suggests that pixel-aligned multilayer prediction transfers across object and scene regimes more naturally than canonical image-to-3D generation, whose output frame and single-object assumptions break on cluttered scenes. Second, the object model is surprisingly robust to multiple objects in one image, despite not being explicitly trained with multi-object augmentation. Because WT predicts one stack per input ray rather than one canonical asset per crop, multiple disconnected objects can coexist in the same output tensor. These observations point to a clear next step: unify object and scene training into a single stronger model, rather than maintaining separate specialists.

F.5 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and future work

WT 仍然受到固定层数、渲染监督和迭代流采样的限制。高度穿孔的几何形状可能需要六个以上的表面；合成到真实的伪影保留在无纹理或反射区域；实时应用将需要蒸馏或更少的采样步骤。目前的动态模型还可以对短片进行操作，将长视频中的长程记忆和持久身份留给未来的工作。一个自然的扩展是使 WT-D 不仅可以预测每帧多层几何形状，还可以预测显式的像素/点对应关系和跨时间的 3D 轨迹。另一个方向是将 WT-S 从单帧场景提升扩展到多帧输入，其中多个观察结果可以融合到更大的相机对齐世界中，而不是单视图局部场景中。更广泛地说，未来最强大的方向是训练一种具有更强真实掩模增强和自适应层分配的统一对象场景动态模型。WT is still bounded by a fixed layer count, rendered supervision, and iterative flow sampling. Highly perforated geometry may require more than six surfaces; synthetic-to-real artifacts remain on textureless or reflective regions; and real-time applications will require distillation or fewer sampling steps. The current dynamic model also operates on short clips, leaving long-range memory and persistent identities across long videos to future work. A natural extension is to make WT-D predict not only per-frame multilayer geometry, but also explicit pixel/point correspondences and 3D trajectories across time. Another direction is to extend WT-S from single-frame scene lifting to multi-frame input, where multiple observations can be fused into a larger camera-aligned world rather than a single-view local scene. More broadly, the strongest future direction is to train one unified object-scene-dynamic model with stronger real-mask augmentation and adaptive layer allocation.
