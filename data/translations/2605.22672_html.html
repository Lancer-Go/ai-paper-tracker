摘要Abstract

我们记录了法学硕士中预测问题的逆标度，这些问题的基本时间序列表现出超线性增长和政权更迭的尾部风险，这是金融和流行病学中常见的结构。在这些任务中，能力更强的模型会产生更差的分布预测。该模式出现在 ForecastBench-Sim (FBSim) 上，这是我们发布的一个无污染的模拟世界基准，用于通过匹配的线性控制来预测合成 SIR 流行病，并在有关 COVID-19、麻疹、房地产市场和恶性通货膨胀的现实数据集中进行复制。按分位数分解显示，失败集中在上尾，更有能力的模型向上移动以跟踪增长的积极外推，而下尾保持不变。 Llama-3.1 的一项家庭内部研究表明，模型规模和训练后都独立地促成了这种效应。领域知识并不能可靠地挽救校准。这种反向缩放不会出现在 LLM 预测基准中常见的单阈值指标上，从而反转了相同输出上的能力-准确性关系的符号。传统截止点的单阈值评分忽略了上尾成本；包含尾部的评分反转了相同输出上的能力-准确度关系的符号。我们建议 LLM 预测评估使用连续（且无界）的准确性度量以及有界二元阈值指标。We document inverse scaling in LLMs on forecasting problems whose underlying time series exhibit superlinear growth and tail risk of regime change, a structure common in finance and epidemiology. On these tasks, more capable models produce worse distributional forecasts. The pattern appears on ForecastBench-Sim (FBSim), a contamination-free, simulated-world benchmark we release, in forecasting synthetic SIR epidemics with a matched linear control, and replicates in real-world datasets on COVID-19, measles, housing markets, and hyperinflation. A per-quantile decomposition shows the failure concentrates at the upper tail, which more capable models shift upward to track aggressive extrapolations of growth, while the lower tail stays put. A within-family study of Llama-3.1 shows that both model scale and post-training independently contribute to this effect. Domain knowledge does not reliably rescue calibration. This inverse scaling does not appear on single-threshold metrics common in LLM forecasting benchmarks, reversing the sign of the capability–accuracy relationship on identical outputs. Single-threshold scoring at conventional cutoffs misses the upper-tail cost; tail-inclusive scoring reverses the sign of the capability–accuracy relationship on the same outputs. We recommend that LLM forecasting evaluations use continuous (and unbounded) measures of accuracy alongside bounded binary threshold metrics.
