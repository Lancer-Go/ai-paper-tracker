TRUST-ESD：一个经过风险校准和治理感知的人工智能框架，用于不确定性下的企业战略决策支持TRUST-ESD: A Risk-Calibrated and Governance-Aware AI Framework for Enterprise Strategic Decision Support Under Uncertainty

Tian Qiu    Li Yan    Mahabubur Rahman Miraj (🖂)    Shanqin Yi    Md Intekhab Rahman Galib    Jahid Hasan
摘要Abstract

企业战略决策支持需要人工智能系统不仅准确，而且具有不确定性感知、风险校准、可解释和合规性。本文提出了 TRUST-ESD，一种用于不确定性下企业决策支持的风险校准和治理意识框架。 TRUST-ESD 通过预测效用估计、保形不确定性校准、基于 CVaR 的下行风险评分、风险记忆检索、策略即代码治理、可解释性和人工监督来评估可行的反事实策略。与通过最大预期效用选择行动的纯预测方法不同，TRUST-ESD 推荐平衡价值、可靠性、风险暴露和合规性的策略。实验结果表明，与强不确定性感知基线相比，TRUST-ESD 使风险调整效用提高 7.95%，风险暴露降低 23.22%，CVaR 降低 23.78%，校准误差降低 13.89%，解释保真度提高 10.90%，治理合规性提高 9.76%，同时保持有竞争力的预测准确性。消融和案例研究分析进一步证实，不确定性校准、下行风险评分、风险记忆、可解释性和治理验证共同提高了值得信赖的企业决策。Enterprise strategic decision support requires AI systems that are not only accurate, but also uncertainty-aware, risk-calibrated, explainable, and governance-compliant. This paper proposes TRUST-ESD, a risk-calibrated and governance-aware framework for enterprise decision support under uncertainty. TRUST-ESD evaluates feasible counterfactual strategies through predictive utility estimation, conformal uncertainty calibration, CVaR-based downside-risk scoring, risk-memory retrieval, policy-as-code governance, explainability, and human oversight. Unlike prediction-only methods that select actions by maximum expected utility, TRUST-ESD recommends strategies that balance value, reliability, risk exposure, and compliance. Experimental results show that TRUST-ESD improves risk-adjusted utility by 7.95%, reduces risk exposure by 23.22%, reduces CVaR by 23.78%, lowers calibration error by 13.89%, improves explanation fidelity by 10.90%, and increases governance compliance by 9.76% compared with strong uncertainty-aware baselines, while maintaining competitive predictive accuracy. Ablation and case-study analyses further confirm that uncertainty calibration, downside-risk scoring, risk memory, explainability, and governance validation jointly improve trustworthy enterprise decision-making.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能越来越多地用于企业战略决策，组织必须根据市场波动、运营不确定性、资源约束和治理要求来选择行动Artificial intelligence is increasingly used for enterprise strategic decision-making, where organizations must choose actions under market volatility, operational uncertainty, resource constraints, and governance requirements[3, 7]。然而，对于此类设置，仅预测准确性是不够的，因为传统的评估指标无法完全捕捉决策风险、政策可行性或下游业务后果. However, prediction accuracy alone is insufficient for such settings, since conventional evaluation metrics do not fully capture decision risk, policy feasibility, or downstream business consequences[1]。如果不确定且面临严重的下行风险，则具有高预期效用的策略可能仍然不合适. A strategy with high expected utility may still be unsuitable if it is uncertain, exposed to severe downside risk[24]，很难通过可解释的证据来证明, difficult to justify through explainable evidence[8]，或不符合企业政策约束, or inconsistent with enterprise policy constraints[3, 10]。因此，企业决策支持需要人工智能系统不仅具有预测性，而且能够进行风险校准. Thus, enterprise decision support requires AI systems that are not only predictive, but also risk-calibrated[24], 可解释的, explainable[8]、可审计、具有治理意识, auditable, and governance-aware[2, 6].

现有的基于人工智能的决策支持方法很大程度上以预测为中心：它们估计未来的结果并选择具有最高期望值的选项。这种方法在战略性企业场景中受到限制，其中定价调整、库存扩张、资源配置或市场进入等行动在点预测下可能看似有益，但在需求波动、财务风险、运营不稳定或监管限制下变得脆弱Existing AI-based decision-support methods are largely prediction-centered: they estimate future outcomes and select the option with the highest expected value. This approach is limited in strategic enterprise scenarios, where actions such as pricing adjustment, inventory expansion, resource allocation, or market entry may appear beneficial under point prediction but become fragile under demand fluctuation, financial exposure, operational instability, or regulatory constraints[3, 7]。尽管之前关于可信人工智能的工作强调人类监督. Although prior work on trustworthy AI emphasizes human oversight[2]、透明度和问责制, transparency and accountability[4, 6], 预测系统的公平性, fairness in predictive systems[5]，黑盒模型的可解释性, explainability for black-box models[8]和网络安全意识治理, and cybersecurity-aware governance[10]，这些方面很少被整合到统一的定量决策管道中。, these aspects are rarely integrated into a unified quantitative decision pipeline.

为了弥补这一差距，我们提出了 TRUST-ESD，这是一个经过风险校准和治理意识的框架，用于在不确定性下支持企业战略决策。 TRUST-ESD 通过估计预测效用、使用保形区间校准不确定性、使用 CVaR 测量下行风险、纳入历史风险记忆以及在人工监督下实施政策即代码治理来评估可行的反事实策略。 TRUST-ESD 不选择预测效用最高的行动，而是推荐最佳平衡价值、可靠性、风险控制、可解释性和合规性的策略。To address this gap, we propose TRUST-ESD, a risk-calibrated and governance-aware framework for enterprise strategic decision support under uncertainty. TRUST-ESD evaluates feasible counterfactual strategies by estimating predictive utility, calibrating uncertainty with conformal intervals, measuring downside risk using CVaR, incorporating historical risk memory, and enforcing policy-as-code governance with human oversight. Instead of selecting the action with the highest predicted utility, TRUST-ESD recommends the strategy that best balances value, reliability, risk control, explainability, and compliance.

6 结论Conclusion

本文提出了 TRUST-ESD，一种用于不确定性下企业战略决策支持的风险校准和治理意识框架。通过集成预测效用估计、共形不确定性校准、基于 CVaR 的下行风险评分、风险记忆检索、策略即代码治理、可解释性和人工监督，TRUST-ESD 超越了仅预测的建议，转向值得信赖的战略决策。实验结果表明，所提出的框架提高了风险调整效用，降低了风险暴露和CVaR，加强了校准，并提高了解释保真度和治理合规性，同时保持了竞争性预测准确性。消融和案例研究分析进一步证实，每个组成部分都有助于提供稳健、可解释且符合政策的决策支持。未来的工作将把 TRUST-ESD 扩展到动态企业环境、适应性治理政策和现实世界的人类反馈设置。This paper presented TRUST-ESD, a risk-calibrated and governance-aware framework for enterprise strategic decision support under uncertainty. By integrating predictive utility estimation, conformal uncertainty calibration, CVaR-based downside-risk scoring, risk-memory retrieval, policy-as-code governance, explainability, and human oversight, TRUST-ESD moves beyond prediction-only recommendation toward trustworthy strategic decision-making. Experimental results show that the proposed framework improves risk-adjusted utility, reduces risk exposure and CVaR, strengthens calibration, and improves explanation fidelity and governance compliance while maintaining competitive predictive accuracy. The ablation and case-study analyses further confirm that each component contributes to robust, interpretable, and policy-compliant decision support. Future work will extend TRUST-ESD to dynamic enterprise environments, adaptive governance policies, and real-world human feedback settings.
