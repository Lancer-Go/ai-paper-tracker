摘要Abstract

无线数字孪生需要在有限且随时间变化的通信资源下，在随时间变化的物理场景与其数字对应场景之间进行重复同步。对于以感知为中心的双胞胎，像素域传输或统一保护的比特流可能与双胞胎应用程序消耗的语义状态不匹配。本文提出了 TWIST，一种用于应用感知无线数字孪生的闭环令牌同步框架。 TWIST 将每个物理观察结果表示为一个令牌，并通过无线链路同步该状态，而不是优化视觉重建。令牌位置按任务相关性进行分组，并通过低、中、高同步模式下的模式条件不等错误保护进行保护。在孪生方面，解码置信度将不可靠的硬令牌决策转换为擦除，这些擦除在更新语义孪生状态之前由完成模型恢复。恢复的状态支持流量状态推断并生成紧凑的反馈统计数据，包括信道质量、接收器不确定性、语义漂移和应用程序优先级，用于后续模式自适应。动态道路场景数字孪生场景的实验表明，与固定模式和仅通道自适应策略相比，TWIST 改进了交通状态推断和语义双状态同步，同时相对于始终较高的传输降低了平均同步成本。Wireless digital twins require repeated synchronization between a time-evolving physical scene and its digital counterpart under limited and time-varying communication resources. For perception-centric twins, pixel-domain transmission or uniformly protected bitstreams can be mismatched to the semantic state consumed by twin-side applications. This paper proposes TWIST, a closed-loop token synchronization framework for application-aware wireless digital twins. TWIST represents each physical observation as a token and synchronizes this state over a wireless link, rather than optimizing visual reconstruction. Token positions are grouped by task relevance and protected through mode-conditioned unequal error protection under low-, medium-, and high-synchronization modes. At the twin side, decoding confidence converts unreliable hard token decisions into erasures, which are restored by a completion model before updating the semantic twin state. The recovered state supports traffic-state inference and generates compact feedback statistics, including channel quality, receiver uncertainty, semantic drift, and application priority, for subsequent mode adaptation. Experiments on a dynamic road-scene digital-twin scenario show that TWIST improves traffic-state inference and semantic twin-state synchronization compared with fixed-mode and channel-only adaptation strategies, while reducing the average synchronization cost relative to always-high transmission.

I 简介Introduction

数字孪生正在从静态数字副本发展成为闭环、应用感知系统，持续与物理世界同步并支持监控、预测和控制。在无线系统中，这种演变使通信成为中心瓶颈：数字对应物必须在衰落、带宽限制、延迟限制和时变应用要求的情况下进行更新。数字孪生网络被认为是通过传感、通信、计算和反馈耦合物理和虚拟系统的有前途的范例Digital twins are evolving from static digital replicas into closed-loop, application-aware systems that continuously synchronize with the physical world and support monitoring, prediction, and control. In wireless systems, this evolution makes communication a central bottleneck: the digital counterpart must be updated under fading, bandwidth limitations, latency constraints, and time-varying application requirements. Digital twin networks have been surveyed as a promising paradigm for coupling physical and virtual systems through sensing, communication, computation, and feedback[26]。无线数字孪生进一步强调通信系统如何实现这种物理-虚拟耦合并从中受益. Wireless digital twins further emphasize how communication systems can both enable and benefit from such physical–virtual coupling[11]。最近的研究调查了实时无线数字孪生、数字孪生通道和 DT 辅助波束或 CSI 预测. Recent studies have investigated real-time wireless digital twins, digital-twin channels, and DT-assisted beam or CSI prediction[1, 23]。然而，它们主要关注通道和网络状态或控制决策，而不是关注以感知为中心的孪生体应如何同步其下游应用程序消耗的语义表示。. However, they mainly focus on channel and network states, or control decisions, rather than on how a perception-centric twin should synchronize the semantic representation consumed by its downstream application.

随着现代感知和生成模型管道越来越多地在标记化表示上运行，这种区别变得很重要。学习的离散表示，例如 VQ 式标记器，将视觉观察结果映射到紧凑的码本索引中This distinction becomes important as modern perception and generative-model pipelines increasingly operate on tokenized representations. Learned discrete representations, such as VQ-style tokenizers, map visual observations into compact codebook indices[22]。标记化视觉表示也已广泛用于生成和屏蔽标记建模，其中下游模型在标记网格而不是原始像素上运行. Tokenized visual representations have also been widely used in generative and masked-token modeling, where downstream models operate on token grids rather than raw pixels[6, 5]。因此，对于以感知为中心的数字孪生，需要同步的对象不一定是像素级重建或统一保护的比特流。相反，它可以是一个令牌：一种携带双边应用程序所需信息的紧凑表示形式。. For a perception-centric digital twin, the object that needs to be synchronized is therefore not necessarily a pixel-level reconstruction or a uniformly protected bitstream. It can instead be a token: a compact representation that carries the information needed by the twin-side application.

这一观察提出了一个核心问题：无线数字孪生应该如何在有限且时变的通信资源下同步令牌？该问题在几个方面与传统图像传输不同。首先，代币位置对于双侧应用来说并不同等重要，因此统一保护所有代币可能会浪费稀缺的通道使用。其次，在令牌状态系统中，错误的硬令牌更新可能比显式擦除更有害，因为错误的令牌可能会污染孪生状态，而擦除可以通过完成先验来恢复。第三，同步本质上是动态的：适当的资源水平不仅取决于无线信道，还取决于恢复的孪生状态的不确定性、场景的语义漂移以及可能的应用优先级信号。最后，实用的无线 DT 架构应保持模块化和可解释性，而不是完全依赖于单片端到端学习收发器。This observation raises a central question: how should a wireless digital twin synchronize tokens under limited and time-varying communication resources? The problem differs from conventional image delivery in several aspects. First, token positions are not equally important for the twin-side application, so uniformly protecting all tokens can waste scarce channel uses. Second, in a token-state system, an erroneous hard token update can be more harmful than an explicit erasure, since a wrong token may contaminate the twin state while an erasure can be recovered by a completion prior. Third, synchronization is inherently dynamic: the appropriate resource level depends not only on the wireless channel, but also on the uncertainty of the recovered twin state, the semantic drift of the scene, and possible application-priority signals. Finally, a practical wireless-DT architecture should remain modular and interpretable, rather than relying entirely on a monolithic end-to-end learned transceiver.

语义和面向任务的通信已确定通信目标应与含义或下游效用保持一致，而不是精确的位恢复Semantic and task-oriented communication has established that communication objectives should be aligned with meaning or downstream utility rather than exact bit recovery[7, 27]。面向任务的边缘通信通过优化推理性能方面的通信，进一步形式化了这一原则. Task-oriented edge communication further formalizes this principle by optimizing communication with respect to inference performance[18]。同时，深度联合源通道编码表明，学习图像传输在带宽和通道限制下可以有效. In parallel, deep joint source–channel coding has shown that learned image transmission can be effective under bandwidth and channel constraints[4, 12]。最近，以令牌为中心的通信已成为大模型时代通信系统的自然方向。 UniToCom从信息瓶颈的角度将代币视为通信单位. More recently, token-centric communication has emerged as a natural direction for large-model-era communication systems. UniToCom treats tokens as communication units from an information-bottleneck perspective[24]，而令牌域多址则考虑如何在多用户语义通信中共享令牌化源和调制码本, while token-domain multiple access considers how tokenized source and modulation codebooks can be shared in multiuser semantic communication[16]。令牌感知语义信道编码和调制进一步证明令牌域可靠性和调制可以联合设计. Token-aware semantic-channel coding and modulation further demonstrate that token-domain reliability and modulation can be jointly designed[31]。尽管取得了这些进步，现有的工作并没有共同解决无线数字孪生中的令牌状态同步、接收器决策和闭环模式适应问题。. Despite these advances, existing works do not jointly address token-state synchronization, receiver decisions, and closed-loop mode adaptation in a wireless digital twin.

为了解决这一差距，我们提出了 TWIST（数字孪生的令牌化无线智能同步），这是一种用于应用程序感知的无线数字孪生的闭环令牌同步框架。 TWIST 不优化像素级重建。相反，它在孪生端维护一个同步令牌，并将该状态用于应用程序状态推断和后续同步控制。在物理方面，每个帧都被标记化，并且其标记位置根据任务相关性进行分组。然后，模式调节的不平等保护机制在低同步、中同步和高同步模式下选择分组通道保护。在孪生方面，软解码置信度用于将不可靠的硬令牌决策转换为擦除，并在更新语义孪生状态之前由完成模型恢复。双胞胎进一步计算紧凑的反馈统计数据，包括信道质量、擦除比不确定性、语义漂移和应用程序优先级，以选择下一个同步模式。 本文的主要贡献总结如下：To address the gap, we propose TWIST (Tokenized Wireless Intelligent Synchronization for digital Twins), a closed-loop token synchronization framework for application-aware wireless digital twins. TWIST does not optimize pixel-level reconstruction. Instead, it maintains a synchronized token at the twin side and uses this state both for application-state inference and for subsequent synchronization control. At the physical side, each frame is tokenized and its token positions are grouped according to task relevance. A mode-conditioned unequal protection mechanism then selects group-wise channel protection under low-, medium-, and high-synchronization modes. At the twin side, soft-decoding confidence is used to convert unreliable hard token decisions into erasures, which are restored by a completion model before updating the semantic twin state. The twin further computes compact feedback statistics, including channel quality, erasure-ratio uncertainty, semantic drift, and application priority, to select the next synchronization mode. 七VII结论Conclusion

本文从以代币为中心的角度研究数字孪生同步。 TWIST 不是同步像素级观测或统一保护的比特流，而是在孪生端维护恢复的令牌，并将其用于流量状态推断和后续同步控制。该框架结合了令牌效用感知分组、不平等保护、置信感知门控、完成辅助恢复和基于反馈的模式适应。效用加权损失界限激发了任务相关性感知保护，而贝叶斯风险解释解释了为什么低置信度硬令牌决策可以在完成之前转换为擦除。动态道路场景场景的实验表明，与固定模式和仅通道自适应策略相比，TWIST 在交通状态推断、语义双状态同步和通信成本之间提供了良好的平衡。这些结果表明，代币可以为数字孪生提供实用的同步接口。This paper studies digital twin synchronization from a token-centric perspective. Instead of synchronizing pixel-level observations or uniformly protected bitstreams, TWIST maintains a recovered token at the twin side and uses it for both traffic-state inference and subsequent synchronization control. The framework combines token-utility-aware grouping, unequal protection, confidence-aware gating, completion-assisted recovery, and feedback-based mode adaptation. A utility-weighted loss bound motivates task-relevance-aware protection, while a Bayes-risk interpretation explains why low-confidence hard token decisions can be converted into erasures before completion. Experiments on a dynamic road-scene scenario show that TWIST provides a favorable balance between traffic-state inference, semantic twin-state synchronization, and communication cost compared with fixed-mode and channel-only adaptation strategies. These results show that tokens can provide a practical synchronization interface for digital twins.
