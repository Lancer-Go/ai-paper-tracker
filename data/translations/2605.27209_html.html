摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展促进了 LLM 作为能够推理、规划和使用工具的交互式代理的广泛部署。 尽管在现有基准上表现强劲，但此类代理在部署到现实环境中时通常会表现出显着的性能下降，因为环境本质上是随机且不完美的。 我们认为，这种差异源于理想化的训练环境和现实世界的交互动态之间的根本不匹配，其中当前的范式依赖于精心策划的任务指令和稳定、良好控制的环境。 为了解决这一差距，我们提出了 NoisyAgent，这是一种代理训练框架，它明确地将环境缺陷纳入代理学习过程中。 我们确定了现实场景中交互噪声的两个主要来源：用户噪声（捕获用户交互中的模糊性和可变性）和工具噪声（反映工具执行中的故障和异常）。 我们通过修改用户交互模式并在训练环境中模拟工具执行结果，将此类扰动引入训练管道中。 为了稳定训练，同时鼓励代理处理日益具有挑战性的缺陷，噪声仅应用于一部分部署，并随着模型适应当前噪声水平而逐渐增加难度。 大量的实验表明，我们的方法持续提高了代理在嘈杂和动态环境下的鲁棒性。 我们的分析表明，噪声条件下的训练也会在理想化基准上产生性能提升，这表明受控的环境噪声暴露可以促进更普遍的推理和决策行为。 我们的研究结果强调了对交互缺陷进行建模对于缩小代理训练和实际部署之间的差距的重要性。Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have facilitated the widespread deployment of LLMs as interactive agents capable of reasoning, planning, and tool use. Despite strong performance on existing benchmarks, such agents often exhibit notable degradation when deployed in real-world settings, where environments are inherently stochastic and imperfect. We argue that this discrepancy arises from a fundamental mismatch between idealized training settings and real-world interaction dynamics, where current paradigms rely on carefully curated task instructions and stable, well-controlled environments. To address this gap, we propose NoisyAgent, an agentic training framework that explicitly incorporates environmental imperfections into the agent learning process. We identify two major sources of interaction noise in real-world scenarios: user noise, which captures ambiguity and variability in user interaction, and tool noise, which reflects failures and anomalies in tool execution. We introduce such perturbations into the training pipeline by modifying user interaction patterns and simulating tool execution results within the training environment. To stabilize training while encouraging agents to handle increasingly challenging imperfection, noise is applied to only a subset of rollouts and progressively increased in difficulty as the model adapts to the current noise level. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our approach consistently improves agent robustness under noisy and dynamic environments. Our analysis reveals that training under noise condition also yields performance gains on idealized benchmarks, suggesting that controlled exposure to environmental noise promotes more generalizable reasoning and decision-making behaviors. Our findings highlight the importance of modeling interaction imperfections for bridging the gap between agent training and real-world deployment.

1 简介Introduction

大语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展已将它们从被动文本生成器转变为能够推理、规划和使用工具的交互式代理Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have transformed them from passive text generators into interactive agents capable of reasoning, planning, and tool use[28, 10, 47]，使其能够在实际应用中广泛部署。 随着这些能力的不断提高, enabling their widespread deployment in real-world applications. As these capabilities continue to improve[46, 76, 48], LLM 代理在广泛的基准测试中取得了强劲的表现, LLM agents have achieved strong performance across a wide range of benchmarks[69, 3, 12]。 然而，这种成功并不总是转移到更现实的环境中：当面对复杂和动态的环境时，许多智能体表现出显着的性能下降. However, this success does not consistently transfer to more realistic settings: when confronted with complex and dynamic environments, many agents exhibit notable performance degradation[5, 84, 67].

我们认为，当前的代理学习范例在训练条件和现实世界部署之间存在根本差距。 现有智能体训练范例的一个共同特征是它们依赖于理想化假设，其中智能体接受精心策划的指令训练，并与稳定、控制良好的环境进行交互We argue that current agent learning paradigms exhibit a fundamental gap between training conditions and real-world deployment. A common characteristic shared by existing agent training paradigms is their reliance on idealized assumptions, where agents are trained with carefully curated instructions and interact with stable, well-controlled environments[75, 32, 17]。 相比之下，现实世界的环境本质上是随机且不完美的。 用户经常表现出多样化的交互风格和不可预测的行为. In contrast, real-world environments are inherently stochastic and imperfect. Users often exhibit diverse interaction styles and unpredictable behaviors[8, 51, 58]，而外部工具可能会由于各种不可控因素而返回有噪音、不完整甚至失败的输出, while external tools may return noisy, incomplete, or even failed outputs due to various uncontrollable factors[54, 65]。 训练条件和部署环境之间的这种差异限制了当前代理的鲁棒性，通常导致实际应用中的性能下降. This discrepancy between training conditions and deployment environments limits the robustness of current agents, often leading to degraded performance in practical applications[33, 45, 41].

受到强化学习中随机扰动成功的启发Inspired by the success of stochastic perturbations in reinforcement learning[50, 34, 82]，我们认为代理的鲁棒性来自于学习过程中的各种缺陷。 我们没有依赖理想化的训练环境并期望智能体事后适应，而是明确地将环境噪声和不确定性纳入智能体训练过程中。 然而，如何在代理训练中建模和引入此类噪声仍有待探索，并且天真地将噪声注入训练环境很容易破坏训练动态的稳定性，使其成为一个不小的挑战。, we argue that agent robustness emerges from exposure to diverse imperfections in learning process. Rather than relying on idealized training settings and expecting agents to adapt post hoc, we explicitly incorporate environmental noise and uncertainty into the agentic training process. However, how to model and introduce such noise in agentic training remains underexplored, and naively injecting noise into the training environment can easily destabilize training dynamics, making it a non-trivial challenge.

为了实现这一目标，我们提出了 NoisyAgent，一种用于在噪声环境下进行训练的代理强化学习方法。 我们首先确定现实世界噪声的代表性形式，并开发一个自动化管道，将这些缺陷纳入训练过程。 具体来说，我们考虑现实世界代理场景中交互噪声的两个主要来源：用户噪声（捕获用户交互中的模糊性和可变性）和工具噪声（模拟外部工具的执行异常）。 这些扰动是通过修改用户指令并在训练环境中模拟工具执行结果来引入的，扰动仅应用于每个任务的部署子集。 培训遵循课程表。从轻微的扰动开始，随着模型在每个阶段都表现出足够的鲁棒性，我们逐渐增加噪声的难度和比率。 鲁棒性是通过相同任务的理想环境和扰动环境之间的性能差距来量化的。 这种适应性过程确保训练保持信息丰富而不是压倒性的，同时避免对过于嘈杂的情况进行低效探索。Toward this goal, we propose NoisyAgent, an agentic RL method for training under noisy environments. We begin by identifying representative forms of real-world noise and developing an automated pipeline to incorporate such imperfections into the training process. Concretely, we consider two major sources of interaction noise in real-world agent scenarios: user noise, which captures ambiguity and variability in user interactions, and tool noise, which simulates execution anomalies from external tools. These perturbations are introduced by modifying user instructions and simulating tool execution results within the training environment, with perturbations applied to only a subset of rollouts for each task. Training follows a curriculum schedule. Starting from mild perturbations, we progressively increase the difficulty and ratio of noise as the model exhibits sufficient robustness at each stage. Robustness is quantified by the performance gap between idealized and perturbed environments on the same tasks. This adaptive process ensures that training remains informative rather than overwhelming, while avoiding inefficient exploration of excessively noisy regimes.

受益于我们的噪声感知训练，智能体在现实世界噪声增强的基准测试中实现了更高的性能，这表明在不完美和动态环境下增强了鲁棒性。 有趣的是，我们还观察到标准、理想化基准的持续增长。 我们假设适当设计的噪声将受控的不稳定性引入训练环境，并促进更普遍的推理和决策。特别是，暴露在嘈杂和不确定的交互中会鼓励代理从错误中恢复，解决歧义并适应意外结果。从这个角度来看，噪声作为隐式难度增强的一种形式，丰富了训练分布并提高了超出理想化设置的鲁棒性。Benefiting from our noise-aware training, agents achieve improved performance on benchmarks augmented with real-world noise, indicating enhanced robustness under imperfect and dynamic environments. Interestingly, we also observe consistent gains on standard, idealized benchmarks. We hypothesize that appropriately designed noise introduces controlled instability into the training environment and promotes more generalizable reasoning and decision-making. In particular, exposure to noisy and uncertain interactions encourages agents to recover from errors, resolve ambiguities, and adapt to unexpected outcomes. From this perspective, noise serves as a form of implicit difficulty augmentation, enriching the training distribution and improving robustness beyond idealized settings.

Despite these advances, existing work is largely conducted under idealized settings, leaving a gap between training conditions and real-world noisy environments.

On the执行方execution side，对外部工具的依赖会带来额外的不稳定来源，因为工具可能返回不完整、过时或错误的输出, reliance on external tools introduces an additional source of instability, as tools may return incomplete, outdated, or erroneous outputs[66, 80, 54, 19, 65, 77, 78]。 这种局部错误经常沿着交互轨迹传播，导致下游决策的级联失败. 6 局限性Limitation

虽然我们的框架显示出稳健性的持续改进，但我们注意到可以在未来工作中进一步探讨的几个方面。 首先，我们的主要目标是研究纳入现实世界的交互噪声是否可以提高代理策略的稳健性。 为此，我们关注两个有代表性的噪声源——用户端和工具端扰动——并对实践中观察到的一组常见故障模式进行建模。 虽然这种设计捕捉到了广泛的现实缺陷，但它并不旨在详尽地涵盖所有可能形式的不确定性。 在现实环境中，噪声可能更加复杂、更具组合性并且动态变化。 扩展框架以建模更丰富、更多样化的交互模式是未来工作的重要方向。 此外，我们的实验主要在接近现实世界交互动态的合成环境中进行。 原则上，所提出的框架是通用的，可以通过用结构化噪声对其进行增强来应用于任何代理环境。 然而，由于代理训练的成本高昂，并且需要系统地评估分布外条件下的鲁棒性，因此我们专注于受控设置，而不是跨多个域内训练和测试数据集进行广泛的基准测试。 我们相信，将我们的框架应用于更广泛的现实世界和领域内基准是未来工作的重要方向。 我们将这些方向留给未来的工作。While our framework demonstrates consistent improvements in robustness, we note several aspects that could be further explored in future work. First, our primary goal is to investigate whether incorporating real-world interaction noise can improve the robustness of agent policies. To this end, we focus on two representative sources of noise—user-side and tool-side perturbations—and model a set of common failure patterns observed in practice. While this design captures a broad range of realistic imperfections, it does not aim to exhaustively cover all possible forms of uncertainty. In real-world environments, noise can be more complex, compositional, and dynamically evolving. Extending the framework to model richer and more diverse interaction patterns is an important direction for future work. Also, our experiments are primarily conducted in synthesized environments that approximate real-world interaction dynamics. In principle, the proposed framework is general and can be applied to any agentic environment by augmenting it with structured noise. However, due to the high cost of agentic training and the need to systematically evaluate robustness under out-of-distribution conditions, we focus on controlled settings rather than extensively benchmarking across multiple in-domain training and testing datasets. We believe that applying our framework to broader real-world and in-domain benchmarks is an important direction for future work. We leave these directions for future work.

7 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们研究了理想化代理训练与现实世界部署之间的根本差距，并确定训练期间缺乏环境缺陷是限制代理鲁棒性的关键因素。 为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一种噪声感知训练框架，该框架明确地将随机性和缺陷纳入代理学习过程中。 通过系统地建模指令噪声和工具噪声，并通过自动噪声注入管道引入它们，我们的方法使代理能够接触到更真实的交互动态。 为了确保稳定的优化，我们进一步设计了一种自适应训练策略，结合了干净和扰动的推出，同时根据模型的鲁棒性逐步增加噪声难度。 大量的实验表明，我们的方法在噪声和动态环境下持续提高代理性能，验证了其在增强鲁棒性方面的有效性。 值得注意的是，我们还观察到标准、理想化基准的持续增长，这表明受控的环境噪声暴露可以促进更普遍的推理和决策行为。 总体而言，这项工作强调了将训练条件与现实世界交互特征相结合的重要性，并提供了一个实用的框架，用于提高基于 LLM 的代理在实际部署环境中的稳健性。In this work, we investigate the fundamental gap between idealized agentic training and real-world deployment, and identify the lack of environmental imperfections during training as a key factor limiting agent robustness. To address this issue, we propose a noise-aware training framework that explicitly incorporates stochasticity and imperfections into the agent learning process. By systematically modeling instruction noise and tool noise, and introducing them through an automatic noise injection pipeline, our approach exposes agents to more realistic interaction dynamics. To ensure stable optimization, we further design an adaptive training strategy that combines clean and perturbed rollouts while progressively increasing noise difficulty based on the model’s robustness. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our method consistently improves agent performance under noisy and dynamic environments, validating its effectiveness in enhancing robustness. Notably, we also observe consistent gains on standard, idealized benchmarks, suggesting that controlled exposure to environmental noise promotes more generalizable reasoning and decision-making behaviors. Overall, this work highlights the importance of aligning training conditions with real-world interaction characteristics, and provides a practical framework for improving the robustness of LLM-based agents in realistic deployment settings.
