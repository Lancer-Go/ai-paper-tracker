摘要Abstract

由于复杂的多车辆交互以及平衡安全性和效率的需要，无信号交叉口的自动驾驶具有挑战性。模型预测控制 (MPC) 通过优化提供结构化约束处理，但依赖于手工制定的规则，这些规则通常会产生过于保守的行为。深度强化学习（RL）从经验中学习适应性行为，但常常在安全保证和对未见过的环境的泛化方面遇到困难。在本研究中，我们提出了一个集成的 MPC-RL 框架，以提高多智能体场景中的导航性能。实验表明，MPC-RL 在三个流量密度级别上的性能优于独立 MPC 和端到端 RL。总的来说，与纯MPC相比，MPC-RL将碰撞率降低了21%，成功率提高了6.5%。我们进一步评估了无需重新训练的高速公路合并场景的零镜头转移。两种基于 MPC 的方法的传输性能都比端到端 PPO 好得多，这凸显了 MPC 主干在跨场景鲁棒性中的作用。该框架在训练过程中还表现出比端到端强化学习更快的损失稳定性，这表明学习负担减轻了。这些结果表明，集成方法可以改善多智能体交叉路口场景中安全性能和效率之间的平衡，而 MPC 组件为跨驾驶环境的泛化提供了坚实的基础。实现代码是开源的。Automated driving at unsignalized intersections is challenging due to complex multi-vehicle interactions and the need to balance safety and efficiency. Model Predictive Control (MPC) offers structured constraint handling through optimization but relies on hand-crafted rules that often produce overly conservative behavior. Deep Reinforcement Learning (RL) learns adaptive behaviors from experience but often struggles with safety assurance and generalization to unseen environments. In this study, we present an integrated MPC-RL framework to improve navigation performance in multi-agent scenarios. Experiments show that MPC-RL outperforms standalone MPC and end-to-end RL across three traffic-density levels. Collectively, MPC-RL reduces the collision rate by 21% and improves the success rate by 6.5% compared to pure MPC. We further evaluate zero-shot transfer to a highway merging scenario without retraining. Both MPC-based methods transfer substantially better than end-to-end PPO, which highlights the role of the MPC backbone in cross-scenario robustness. The framework also shows faster loss stabilization than end-to-end RL during training, which indicates a reduced learning burden. These results suggest that the integrated approach can improve the balance between safety performance and efficiency in multi-agent intersection scenarios, while the MPC component provides a strong foundation for generalization across driving environments. The implementation code is available open-source.

I 简介Introduction

自动驾驶技术的快速发展有望通过提高安全性、效率和乘客舒适度来彻底改变交通运输。该技术的一个关键组成部分是开发稳健且适应性强的运动规划算法，特别是在无信号交叉口等复杂的驾驶场景中。这些场景不仅需要复杂的环境理解，还需要基于隐性规则和社会惯例与人类驾驶员进行隐性合作或谈判的能力The rapid advancement of automated driving technology promises to revolutionize transportation by offering increased safety, efficiency, and passenger comfort. A critical component of this technology is the development of robust and adaptable motion planning algorithms, particularly in complex driving scenarios such as unsignalized intersections. These scenarios demand not only sophisticated environmental understanding but also the ability to engage in implicit cooperation or negotiation with human drivers based on implicit rules and social conventions[1, 2]。驾驶的社会动态要求在自信和合作行为之间保持谨慎的平衡，自信不足会导致拥堵，而过度的攻击性则会损害安全。. The social dynamics of driving demand a careful balance between assertive and cooperative behaviors, where insufficient assertiveness causes congestion while excessive aggression compromises safety.

人们提出了各种方法来解决这种紧张局势。模型预测控制 (MPC) 因其处理系统约束的能力而提供了一种有前途的方法Various approaches have been proposed to address this tension. Model Predictive Control (MPC) offers a promising approach due to its ability to handle system constraints[3]。然而，MPC 方法通常依赖于预定义的、手工制定的规则和成本函数，这导致在复杂场景中的操作过于保守. However, MPC approaches often rely on pre-defined, hand-crafted rules and cost functions, which leads to overly conservative maneuvers in complex scenarios[4, 5]。深度强化学习（Deep RL）方法在通过与环境的试错交互来学习复杂的驾驶行为方面显示出了希望，但它们经常在安全保证和推广到新场景方面遇到困难. Deep reinforcement learning (Deep RL) methods have shown promise in learning complex driving behaviors through trial-and-error interactions with the environment, but they often struggle with safety guarantees and generalization to new scenarios[6].

最近的混合方法尝试将 MPC 和 RL 结合起来，以利用它们的互补优势。这些方法大多数采用切换或监督机制，其中 RL 和 MPC 独立运行；例如，根据安全标准交替控制权限Recent hybrid approaches have attempted to combine MPC and RL to leverage their complementary strengths. Most of these methods employ switching or supervisory mechanisms in which RL and MPC operate independently; for example, alternating control authority based on safety criteria[7, 8]，在并行 RL 和 MPC 规划器之间进行选择, selecting between parallel RL and MPC planners[9]，或仅使用 RL 来决定 MPC 何时应重新计算, or using RL solely to decide when MPC should recompute[10]。采用更集成架构的少数研究之一. Among the few studies that adopt a more integrated architecture[11, 12]，评估仅限于仅涉及一两辆周围车辆的简化场景，并且尚未研究所学习的策略对结构不同的驾驶环境的普遍性。此外，他们的训练过程会禁用规划器的防撞约束，使 RL 代理面临碰撞，然后在部署时重新启用它们, the evaluation has been limited to simplified scenarios involving only one or two surrounding vehicles, and the generalizability of the learned policies to structurally different driving environments has not been investigated. Moreover, their training procedure disables the planner’s collision avoidance constraints to expose the RL agent to collisions, then re-enables them at deployment[4]。这会导致训练和部署动态之间不匹配，从而导致操作期间两个组件之间的交互不理想。. This introduces a mismatch between training and deployment dynamics, which can lead to suboptimal interaction between the two components during operation.

为了解决这些差距，我们提出了一个集成的 MPC-RL 框架，以便在多智能体场景中更安全、更高效地导航。在我们的架构中，RL 提供高级速度指导，MPC 处理低级轨迹优化，这两个组件在每个控制时间步长耦合，而不是独立运行或在不同时间尺度运行。这与基于交换的方法形成鲜明对比，在基于交换的方法中，MPC 和 RL 交替控制权限或监督方案，其中一个组件监视另一个组件，而无需持续的双向交互。此外，我们的框架在整个训练和评估过程中维护了 MPC 的碰撞避免机制。这确保了 RL 代理学习速度参考来解释和补充 MPC 的反应性安全行为，从而避免在学习过程中缺少安全层时出现的训练部署不匹配。我们在三个交通密度级别的多车辆、多方向交叉路口场景中训练和评估所提出的框架，并评估无需重新训练即可从零镜头转移到高速公路合并的能力。结果显示，相对于独立 MPC 和端到端 RL 都有一致的改进，具有强大的零样本传输到合并的能力，尤其是在不太拥塞的场景中。To address these gaps, we propose an integrated MPC-RL framework for safer and more efficient navigation in multi-agent scenarios. In our architecture, RL provides high-level speed guidance, and MPC handles low-level trajectory optimization, with both components coupled at every control timestep rather than operating independently or at different time scales. This stands in contrast to switching-based approaches, where MPC and RL alternate control authority, or supervisory schemes, where one component monitors the other without continuous bidirectional interaction. Furthermore, our framework maintains the MPC’s collision avoidance mechanism throughout both training and evaluation. This ensures that the RL agent learns speed references that account for and complement the MPC’s reactive safety behavior, thereby avoiding the train-deployment mismatch that arises when the safety layer is absent during learning. We train and evaluate the proposed framework in multi-vehicle, multi-directional intersection scenarios across three traffic-density levels and assess zero-shot transferability to highway merging without retraining. The results show consistent improvements over both standalone MPC and end-to-end RL, with robust zero-shot transfer to merging, especially in less congested scenarios.

我们的框架提供了几个实际优势。首先，MPC 组件内的车辆动力学显式建模可确保生成的轨迹遵守建模的物理约束，从而支持运动学可行性和安全导向的行为。其次，通过限制 RL 代理学习高级速度参考而不是完全控制策略，该框架显着减轻了学习负担，从而在训练过程中实现更快的损失稳定。此外，MPC 组件充当一致的控制层，在整个操作过程中强制执行运动学可行性和输入约束，因此即使 RL 代理提出激进的速度参考，执行的轨迹仍然受到 MPC 优化和防撞逻辑的限制。实施代码省略匿名评审，发布后将发布。Our framework offers several practical advantages. First, the explicit modeling of vehicle dynamics within the MPC component ensures that generated trajectories respect the modeled physical constraints, which supports kinematic feasibility and safety-oriented behavior. Second, by restricting the RL agent to learning high-level speed references rather than the full control policy, the framework significantly reduces the learning burden, which leads to faster loss stabilization during training. Furthermore, the MPC component acts as a consistent control layer that enforces kinematic feasibility and input constraints throughout operation, so that even if the RL agent proposes aggressive speed references, the executed trajectory remains bounded by the MPC optimization and the collision-avoidance logic. The implementation code is omitted for anonymous review and will be released upon publication.

V 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

实验结果表明，MPC-RL 框架为平衡自动交叉路口导航的安全性和效率提供了一个有前途的方向。在所有难度级别中，MPC-RL 始终实现最高的成功率和最低的碰撞率，这表明 RL 引导的速度自适应比仅手工制作的基于 TTC 的碰撞避免具有明显的优势。这种优势在交通密度较低的情况下最为明显。 零样本传输结果呈现出一幅微妙的画面。在较低的流量密度下，两种基于 MPC 的方法都实现了完美的合并性能，而在最高密度下，纯 MPC 的性能优于 MPC-RL。然而，端到端的 PPO 在传输中完全失败，这凸显了 MPC 主干网是跨场景鲁棒性的主要推动者。分层架构似乎可以减轻灾难性的传输失败：即使 RL 组件在新域中提供次优速度参考，MPC 层也能保留比端到端 PPO 更好的行为。此外，事实证明，从多车辆交互中学习自适应速度参考比以端到端方式学习原始控制命令更具可转移性。 我们的框架所展示的平衡行为也对自动驾驶汽车的社会接受度产生潜在影响，因为之前的研究表明，过于保守的驾驶方式可能会导致不安全或低效的情况The experimental results suggest that the MPC-RL framework offers a promising direction for balancing safety and efficiency in automated intersection navigation. Across all difficulty levels, MPC-RL consistently achieves the highest success rate and lowest collision rate, indicating that RL-guided speed adaptation provides clear benefits beyond hand-crafted TTC-based collision avoidance alone. The advantage is most pronounced in lower-density traffic. The zero-shot transfer results present a nuanced picture. At lower traffic densities, both MPC-based methods achieve perfect merging performance, while at the highest density, pure MPC outperforms MPC-RL. However, end-to-end PPO fails entirely in transfer, highlighting that the MPC backbone is the primary enabler of cross-scenario robustness. The hierarchical architecture appears to mitigate catastrophic transfer failure: even when the RL component provides suboptimal speed references in a new domain, the MPC layer preserves substantially better behavior than end-to-end PPO. Moreover, learning adaptive speed references from multi-vehicle interactions proves far more transferable than learning raw control commands in an end-to-end fashion. The balanced behavior demonstrated by our framework also has potential implications for social acceptance of automated vehicles, as previous research has shown that overly conservative driving styles can lead to unsafe or inefficient situations[21, 22]。尽管有这些发现，在对实际部署做出更强有力的声明之前，还需要进行广泛的现实验证。 应该承认一些限制。首先，我们的基线仅限于独立 MPC 和端到端 PPO；与其他混合方法或安全约束强化学习方法的比较将提供更完整的情况。其次，Highway-Env 使用简化的车辆动力学和理想化的道路几何形状，因此仍然可以转移到现实世界条件。第三，需要进行消融研究来隔离 RL 组件相对于基于 TTC 的防撞的贡献。最后，硬合并中的性能逆转表明特定领域的适应策略值得进一步研究。. Despite these findings, extensive real-world validation would be needed before making stronger claims about practical deployment. Several limitations should be acknowledged. First, our baselines are limited to standalone MPC and end-to-end PPO; comparisons against other hybrid approaches or safety-constrained RL methods would provide a more complete picture. Second, Highway-Env uses simplified vehicle dynamics and idealized road geometries, so transferability to real-world conditions remains open. Third, ablation studies are needed to isolate the RL component’s contribution relative to the TTC-based collision avoidance. Finally, the performance reversal in hard merging suggests that domain-specific adaptation strategies merit further investigation.

VI 结论Conclusion

本研究提出了一个用于无信号交叉口自动导航的耦合 MPC-RL 框架，其中 RL 代理从多车辆交互中学习自适应速度参考，而 MPC 处理轨迹优化和约束执行。三个流量密度级别的实验结果表明，MPC-RL 在成功率和冲突率方面实现了统计上显着的改进，始终优于独立 MPC 和端到端 PPO。尽管在冲突最密集的情况下优势会变小，但在不同难度级别上优势都是一致的。高速公路合并的零样本传输实验表明，两种基于 MPC 的方法在未见过的场景中仍然有效，而端到端 PPO 则失败。这表明 MPC 主干可以促进跨域泛化。重要的是，即使 RL 组件在分布式情况下运行，混合架构也有助于减轻灾难性故障。该框架在训练过程中还表现出比端到端 RL 更快的损失稳定，这与限制 RL 代理的角色减少学习负担的想法是一致的。未来的工作应侧重于更广泛的基线比较，包括安全约束的强化学习方法、改进传输的领域适应策略、隔离单个组件贡献的消融研究以及在更高保真度模拟器中的评估。This study presents a coupled MPC-RL framework for automated navigation at unsignalized intersections, in which the RL agent learns adaptive speed references from multi-vehicle interactions while MPC handles trajectory optimization and constraint enforcement. Experimental results across three traffic-density levels show that MPC-RL consistently outperforms both standalone MPC and end-to-end PPO by achieving statistically significant improvements in both success rate and collision rate. The advantage is consistent across difficulty levels, though it becomes smaller in the most conflict-dense scenarios. Zero-shot transfer experiments to highway merging reveal that both MPC-based methods remain effective in the unseen scenario, whereas end-to-end PPO fails. This suggests that the MPC backbone can facilitate cross-domain generalization. Importantly, the hybrid architecture helps mitigate catastrophic failure even when the RL component operates out of distribution. The framework also shows faster loss stabilization during training than end-to-end RL, which is consistent with the idea that restricting the RL agent’s role reduces the learning burden. Future work should focus on broader baseline comparisons, including safety-constrained RL methods, domain adaptation strategies for improved transfer, ablation studies to isolate individual component contributions, and evaluation in higher-fidelity simulators.
