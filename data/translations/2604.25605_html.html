摘要Abstract

简介：Introduction:语义搜索基于概念相似性而不是关键字匹配来检索文档，为临床信息的检索提供了巨大的优势。然而，在整个卫生系统（包括数亿条临床记录）中部署语义搜索会带来巨大的工程、成本和治理挑战，阻碍了机构的大规模采用。Semantic search, which retrieves documents based on conceptual similarity rather than keyword matching, offers substantial advantages for retrieval of clinical information. However, deploying semantic search across entire health systems, comprising hundreds of millions of clinical notes, presents formidable engineering, cost, and governance challenges that have prevented institutional adoption at scale.

方法：Methods:我们在一家大型儿童医院部署了语义搜索系统，索引了来自 168 万名患者的 1.66 亿条临床记录（4.84 亿个嵌入向量）。该系统使用指令调整的 qwen3-embedding-0.6B 嵌入，将向量存储在具有存储优化索引的托管数据库中，在低延迟键值存储中维护全文元数据，并在符合 HIPAA 的治理框架内运行。我们通过三个实验评估了系统：（1）使用医生编写的基准数据集优化嵌入模型和分块策略，（2）全面性能（成本、延迟、检索质量）的表征，以及（3）通过比较三个任务的图表抽象效率来评估临床效用。We deployed a semantic search system at a large children’s hospital indexing 166 million clinical notes (484 million embedding vectors) from 1.68 million patients. The system uses instruction-tuned qwen3-embedding-0.6B embeddings, stores vectors in a managed database with storage-optimized indexing, maintains full-text metadata in a low-latency key-value store, and operates within a HIPAA-compliant governance framework. We evaluated the system through three experiments: (1) optimization of embedding model and chunking strategy using a physicianauthored benchmark dataset, (2) characterization of full-scale performance (cost, latency, retrieval quality), and (3) clinical utility assessment via comparison of chart abstraction efficiency across three tasks.

结果：Results:该系统提供亚秒级查询延迟（单用户并发时的中位数为 237 毫秒，20 用户并发时的中位数为 451 毫秒），每月运营成本约为 4,000 美元。具有 300 个令牌块大小的 Qwen3 嵌入在临床问答基准上实现了 94.6% 的准确率。在三个抽象任务的临床效用评估中，与临床医生执行的基于关键词的图表审查相比，语义搜索将完成时间缩短了 24% 至 89%，同时保持了可比的评估者间一致性。The system delivers sub-second query latency (median 237 ms at single-user concurrency, 451 ms at 20-user concurrency) with monthly operational costs of approximately USD 4,000. Qwen3 embeddings with 300-token chunk size achieved 94.6% accuracy on a clinical question-answering benchmark. In clinical utility evaluation across three abstraction tasks, semantic search reduced time-to-completion by 24 to 89% compared to clinician-performed keyword-based chart review while maintaining comparable inter-rater agreement.

简介Introduction

患者病历中的许多重要内容，包括临床推理、鉴别诊断、家族史和影像学印象，都是以自由文本书写的。虽然结构化电子健康记录 (EHR) 字段捕获离散数据点，但非结构化笔记仍然是临床医生记录的主要媒介。大规模访问这些信息一直是一个持续的挑战：关键字搜索在同义词、缩写、否定和释义上失败，直接数据库查询通常需要专门的信息学支持，这使得非专业临床研究人员难以大规模访问[1]。Much of what matters in a patient's medical record, including clinical reasoning, differential diagnoses, family history, and imaging impressions, is written in free text. While structured electronic health records (EHR) fields capture discrete data points, unstructured notes remain the primary medium through which clinicians document. Accessing this information at scale has been a persistent challenge: keyword search fails on synonyms, abbreviations, negations, and paraphrases, and direct database queries typically require specialized informatics support, making large-scale access difficult for nonexpert clinical researchers[1] .

语义向量搜索提供了一种根本不同的方法。它不是匹配精确的术语，而是将文本编码为捕获含义的密集向量表示，从而能够基于概念相似性而不是词汇重叠进行检索。当与检索增强生成 (RAG)[2] 相结合时，语义搜索可以提供大型语言模型 (LLM)[3] 以及他们无法访问的患者特定上下文，从而改善生成输出的事实基础并减少幻觉。Semantic vector search offers a fundamentally different approach. Instead of matching exact terms, it encodes text into dense vector representations that capture meaning, enabling retrieval based on conceptual similarity rather than lexical overlap. When combined with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)[2] , semantic search can provide large language models (LLMs)[3] with patientspecific context they otherwise cannot access, improving the factual grounding of generated outputs and reducing hallucinations.

尽管人们的兴趣日益浓厚，但之前的医疗保健语义搜索工作主要集中在较窄的用例上，例如文献检索或特定任务队列，而不是跨所有临床记录的健康系统范围的搜索[4,5]。先前的努力目标是放射学报告、临床指南或特定专业应用等设置[6-12]。这些努力并没有解决核心工程挑战：Despite growing interest, prior healthcare semantic search efforts have largely concentrated on narrower use cases such as literature retrieval or task-specific cohorts rather than health-systemwide search across all clinical notes[4,5] . Prior efforts target settings such as radiology reports, clinical guidelines, or specialty-specific applications[6–12] . These efforts do not address the central engineering challenge:语义搜索能否在整个卫生系统的临床记录中运行，并具有持续机构部署所需的成本、延迟和治理属性？can semantic search operate across an entire health system’s clinical notes, with the cost, latency, and governance properties required for sustained institutional deployment?

我们试图直接解决这个问题。本手稿描述了费城儿童医院 (CHOP) 部署的语义搜索系统的设计、实现和评估，该系统将 168 万名患者的 1.66 亿份临床记录索引为 4.84 亿个嵌入向量。该系统以较低的每月运营成本提供亚秒级查询延迟，支持细粒度元数据过滤，并在符合 HIPAA 的治理框架内运行，并具有项目级访问控制和审核日志记录。We sought to address this question directly. This manuscript describes the design, implementation, and evaluation of a semantic search system deployed at the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP) that indexes 166 million clinical notes for 1.68 million patients as 484 million embedding vectors. The system delivers sub-second query latency at a low monthly operational cost, supports fine-grained metadata filtering, and operates within a HIPAA-compliant governance framework with project-level access controls and audit logging.

该系统通过三个实验进行评估：使用医生编写的临床问答基准来评估检索质量、并发工作负载下的系统性能以及通过图表抽象效率的受控比较来评估临床实用性。该系统不是作为单点解决方案设计的，而是作为机构基础设施设计的：一个共享语义索引，可以在该索引上构建交互式搜索、队列生成和下游法学硕士支持的应用程序。The system was evaluated across three experiments: retrieval quality using a physician-authored clinical question-answering benchmark, system performance under concurrent workload, and clinical utility through a controlled comparison of chart abstraction efficiency. The system is designed not as a point solution but as institutional infrastructure: a shared semantic index over which interactive search, cohort generation, and downstream LLM-powered applications can be built.

方法Methods

我们对语义搜索系统的前瞻性评估是在多专业儿科医疗保健环境中进行的。我们的研究分为三个阶段，具体如下：（1）嵌入模型和分块策略的评估和选择； (2)系统性能和成本； (3) 系统在临床图表审查任务中的准确性和效率。Our prospective evaluation of a semantic search system was conducted in the context of a multispecialty pediatric healthcare setting. Our study had 3 phases as detailed below: (1) evaluation and selection of embedding model and chunking strategy; (2) system performance and cost; and (3) accuracy and efficiency of the system in the context of a clinical chart review task.

设置Setting

费城儿童医院 (CHOP) 是一个多专业四级儿科医疗保健系统，每年在两家医院照顾 400,000 名患者。这项研究包括 2000 年 1 月至 2025 年 9 月在 CHOP 接受护理的患者，并得到了 CHOP 机构审查委员会 (IRB #22-020587) 以及 CHOP AI 治理委员会的批准。The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP) is a multi-specialty quaternary pediatric healthcare system caring for 400,000 patients annually across two hospitals. This study included patients cared for at CHOP from January 2000 through September 2025 and was approved by the CHOP Institutional Review Board (IRB #22-020587) as well as the CHOP AI Governance Committee.

数据Data

CHOP EHR 包含超过 2.28 亿条临床记录。在排除没有文本内容的笔记和包含受特殊保护的健康信息的笔记后，168 万名患者的 1.66 亿条笔记被索引。这些注释涵盖 80 多个专业，由超过 44,000 名提供者撰写。每个注释的相关元数据包括患者人口统计数据（姓名、出生日期、性别）、注释属性（类型、科室、专业）、作者信息（姓名、角色）和时间戳。The CHOP EHR contained over 228 million clinical notes. After excluding notes without text content and those containing specially protected health information, 166 million notes for 1.68 million patients were indexed. The notes span over 80 specialties and were authored by over 44,000 providers. Associated metadata for each note includes patient demographics (name, date of birth, sex), note attributes (type, department, specialty), author information (name, role), and timestamps.

系统架构System architecture

该语义系统包含四个主要组件：文本处理管道、向量数据库、键值元数据存储和基于 Web 的用户界面（图 1）。从 CHOP EHR 中提取临床记录，分成重叠的块，并使用嵌入模型编码成密集向量。这些向量在托管向量数据库中建立索引，该数据库支持结构化元数据字段的近似最近邻检索和过滤。完整的注释文本和详细的元数据单独存储在低延迟键值存储中，以减少存储开销。基于 Web 的用户界面将自然语言查询与用于交互式搜索的元数据过滤器相结合（图 2）。所有组件都在符合 HIPAA 的环境中运行，并具有访问控制和审核日志记录。The semantic system comprises four main components: a text processing pipeline, a vector database, a key-value metadata store, and a web-based user interface (Figure 1). Clinical notes are extracted from the CHOP EHR, split into overlapping chunks, and encoded into dense vectors using an embedding model. The vectors are indexed in a managed vector database that supports approximate nearest-neighbor retrieval and filtering on structured metadata fields. Full note text and detailed metadata are stored separately in a low-latency key-value store to reduce storage overhead. A web-based user interface combines natural-language querying with metadata filters for interactive search (Figure 2). All components operate within a HIPAA-compliant environment with access controls and audit logging.

分块和嵌入Chunking & embedding

临床记录通常在一份文档中包含多个不同的概念（主诉、病史、药物、结果、评估和计划）。整个音符的单一嵌入会淡化任何单个概念的表示。分块创建语义连贯的片段，使检索系统能够呈现最相关的上下文。我们评估了 200 到 600 个令牌的块大小，其中有 50 个令牌重叠，并选择了 300 个令牌的块大小作为生产配置（参见结果）。令牌重叠保留了跨越块边界的概念。此外，分块管道优先考虑自然语言边界（分段、换行），以保持连贯性并避免跨块的临床概念支离破碎。Clinical notes frequently contain multiple distinct concepts (chief complaint, history, medications, results, assessment and plan) within a single document. A single embedding for an entire note dilutes the representation of any individual concept. Chunking creates semantically coherent segments that allow the retrieval system to surface the most relevant context. We evaluated chunk sizes ranging from 200 to 600 tokens with a 50-token overlap and selected a 300-token chunk size as the production configuration (see Results). The token overlap preserves concepts that span chunk boundaries. Additionally, the chunking pipeline prioritizes natural linguistic boundaries (paragraph breaks, line breaks) to maintain coherence and avoid fragmenting clinical concepts across chunks.

为了将生成的块编码到密集向量空间中以进行语义检索，我们评估了四种嵌入模型： qwen3-embedding-0.6B[13] 、 nomic embed[14] 、 E5 large[15] 和 BioClinical BERT[16] 。优先考虑输出维度等于或低于 1024 的模型，以限制云存储成本。根据基准测试结果（参见结果），选择 qwen3-embedding-0.6B 作为生产嵌入模型。 Qwen3-embedding-0.6B 是一种指令调整的嵌入模型，可生成 1024 维向量并接受根据检索上下文定制嵌入的任务指令；在查询时，每个查询都以检索指令为前缀。嵌入是使用最后一个令牌池化[17]和归一化为单位长度的 L2 来计算的，以便点积相似性反映方向对齐而不是大小。To encode the resulting chunks into a dense vector space for semantic retrieval, we evaluated four embedding models: qwen3-embedding-0.6B[13] , nomic embed[14] , E5 large[15] , and BioClinical BERT[16] . Models with output dimensionality at or below 1024 were prioritized to limit cloud storage costs. Based on benchmarking results (see Results), qwen3-embedding-0.6B was selected as the production embedding model. Qwen3-embedding-0.6B is an instruction-tuned embedding model that produces 1024-dimensional vectors and accepts task instructions that tailor the embedding to the retrieval context; at query time, each query is prefixed with a retrieval instruction. Embeddings were computed using last-token pooling[17] and L2 normalized to unit length so that dot-product similarity reflects directional alignment rather than magnitude.

使用 PyTorch/XLA 在 Google Cloud Trillium TPU 上执行嵌入计算。语料库按年和月进行分区，每个分区独立处理。对 1.66 亿张纸币进行分块后得到了 4.84 亿个块，这些块是使用 Google 的 TPU 研究云 (TRC) 计划提供的 TPU 分配在大约 11 个日历日内嵌入的。Embedding computations were performed on Google Cloud Trillium TPUs using PyTorch/XLA. The corpus was partitioned by year and month, with each partition processed independently. Chunking the 166 million notes resulted in 484 million chunks, which were embedded over approximately 11 calendar days using TPU allocations provided through Google's TPU Research Cloud (TRC) program.

矢量数据库索引Vector database indexing

矢量存储在 Google Cloud Vertex AI Vector Search 中，这是一个托管矢量数据库，支持内存（基于 RAM）和存储优化（基于磁盘）部署层。我们选择了存储优化层，该层以较高的检索延迟来换取大幅降低的托管成本[18]。检索通过 ScaNN（可扩展最近邻）和 SOAR[19] 使用点积相似性。 ScaNN 采用分层聚类和非对称哈希来有效缩小搜索空间，然后以全精度重新评分近似候选者[19]。Vectors were stored in Google Cloud Vertex AI Vector Search, a managed vector database that supports both in-memory (RAM-based) and storage-optimized (disk-based) deployment tiers. We selected the storage-optimized tier, which trades modestly higher retrieval latency for substantially lower hosting costs[18] . Retrieval uses dot-product similarity via ScaNN (Scalable Nearest Neighbors) and SOAR[19] . ScaNN employs hierarchical clustering and asymmetric hashing to narrow the search space efficiently, then rescores approximate candidates at full precision[19] .

该索引支持对分类属性（患者标识符、注释类型、遭遇类型、部门、专业、作者类型、作者姓名）和数字范围（日期、年龄）进行查询时过滤。这些过滤器既提高了检索精度又减少了噪声。这一点尤其重要，因为密集的相似性评分往往有利于简短的、焦点集中的文档，其嵌入与查询紧密对齐。按注释类别过滤允许用户优先考虑临床实质性文档。The index supports query-time filtering on categorical attributes (patient identifier, note type, encounter type, department, specialty, author type, author name) and numeric ranges (date, age). These filters both improve retrieval precision and reduce noise. This is particularly important because dense similarity scoring tends to favor short, narrowly focused documents whose embeddings are tightly aligned with a query. Filtering by note category allows users to prioritize clinically substantive documents.

矢量数据库支持增量插入，无需重建完整索引。我们设计了一个管道来定期提取、分块、嵌入和插入新笔记。该管道的全面自动化（包括调度和监控）正在进行中。The vector database supports incremental insertion without requiring a full index rebuild. We have designed a pipeline to extract, chunk, embed, and insert new notes on a regular schedule. Full automation of this pipeline, including scheduling and monitoring, is ongoing work.

元数据和注释文本服务Metadata & note text serving

矢量数据库仅存储注释标识符、嵌入和可过滤元数据。由于矢量数据库定价随存储数据的总大小而变化，因此排除完整注释文本和详细元数据可大大降低托管成本。完整的注释文本和元数据保存在低延迟键值存储 (Bigtable) 中，从而显着降低存储成本。行键使用加盐和反转方案来防止连续注释 ID 上出现热点。在查询时，向量搜索返回的前 k 个音符标识符用于以最小的增加延迟从键值存储中获取相应的完整记录。The vector database stores only note identifiers, embeddings, and filterable metadata. Because vector database pricing scales with the total size of stored data, excluding full note text and detailed metadata substantially reduces hosting costs. Full note text and metadata are instead maintained in a low-latency key-value store (Bigtable), which offers significantly lower storage costs. Row keys use a salt-and-reverse scheme to prevent hot spotting on sequential note IDs. At query time, the top-k note identifiers returned by vector search are used to fetch the corresponding full records from the key-value store with minimal added latency.

标杆管理Benchmarking

为了评估检索质量，我们创建了特定于患者的多项选择问答基准数据集 (CHOP_MCQA_v0.5)。三位医生（I.R.R、A.B.R 和 I.M.C）撰写了与 322 名独特患者相关的 334 个问题，涉及多个专业，包括遗传学、心脏病学、风湿病学、社会工作和新生儿学。每个问题都有一个正确答案和四个看似合理的干扰项，旨在模拟真实临床信息需求的模糊性（补充表 1）。To evaluate retrieval quality, we created a patient-specific multiple-choice question-answering benchmarking dataset (CHOP_MCQA_v0.5). Three physicians (I.R.R, A.B.R, and I.M.C) authored 334 questions tied to 322 unique patients, spanning multiple specialties including genetics, cardiology, rheumatology, social work, and neonatology. Each question has one correct answer and four plausible distractors designed to simulate the ambiguity of real clinical information needs (Supplementary Table 1).

所有嵌入模型和分块配置都是在仅包含基准患者注释的简化索引上进行评估的，因为对整个语料库的评估将产生高昂的成本。每个问题都被嵌入，检索到前 20 个最相关的块（过滤到目标患者），并且法学硕士 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B[20] 评估是否可以使用检索到的上下文选择正确的答案。每个问题都通过随机解码的五次独立运行进行评估，最终答案由多数票决定。All the embedding models and chunking configurations were evaluated on a reduced index containing only the notes for benchmark patients, as evaluations over the full corpus would have incurred prohibitive cost. Each question was embedded, the top-20 most relevant chunks retrieved (filtered to the target patient), and an LLM, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B[20] , assessed whether it could select the correct answer using the retrieved context. Each question was evaluated across five independent runs with stochastic decoding, and the final answer was determined by majority vote.

由于真实注释级相关性标签不可用，并且需要昂贵且耗时的注释，因此我们使用端到端准确性作为检索质量的实用代理。正确的答案意味着检索出现了足够的证据，并且法学硕士对其进行了正确的解释；错误的答案可能反映出任一阶段的失败。由于 LLM 在所有条件下都保持不变，因此准确度的差异隔离了嵌入模型和块大小对检索的影响。Because ground-truth note-level relevance labels are unavailable and would require costly and time-intensive annotation, we used end-to-end accuracy as a pragmatic proxy for retrieval quality. A correct answer implies that retrieval surfaced sufficient evidence, and the LLM interpreted it correctly; an incorrect answer may reflect failure in either stage. Because the LLM was held constant across all conditions, differences in accuracy isolate the effect of embedding model and chunk size on retrieval.

讨论Discussion

我们已经证明，对整个卫生系统的临床记录进行语义搜索在技术上是可行的，并且在操作上是可持续的。该系统将 1.66 亿条笔记索引为 4.84 亿个向量，在一秒内返回结果，无论查询量如何，每月的运行成本约为 4,000 美元。我们在医生编写的临床问答基准上取得了很高的准确性，并且语义搜索和传统 EHR 抽象之间的评估者间一致性具有可比性，这表明该系统保持了较高的检索质量。据我们所知，这是第一份在整个卫生系统 EHR 中部署语义检索的报告。We have demonstrated that semantic search over the entirety of a health system's clinical notes is technically feasible and operationally sustainable. The system indexes 166 million notes as 484 million vectors, returns results in under one second, and costs approximately USD 4,000 per month to operate regardless of query volume. We achieved a high accuracy on a physician-authored clinical question-answering benchmark, and inter-rater agreement between semantic search and traditional EHR abstraction was comparable, indicating that the system maintains high retrieval quality. To our knowledge, this is the first report of semantic retrieval deployed across an entire health system EHR.

嵌入模型、向量数据库和近似最近邻算法都是成熟的技术。我们的贡献是演示它们以这种规模和成本组成一个工作系统，并支持现实世界的图表抽象工作流程。有几个设计决策至关重要。将向量存储（在索引中）与全文存储（在键值存储中）分开，避免了将笔记内容与高维向量一起存储的费用，从而降低了成本。选择存储优化索引而不是内存配置可以降低每月的托管成本，子集比较显示内存中 8% 的语料库的成本超过存储优化磁盘上的 100%。延迟权衡保持在交互式使用可接受的亚秒范围内。Embedding models, vector databases, and approximate nearest neighbor algorithms are established technologies. Our contribution is the demonstration that they compose into a working system at this scale and cost, and support real-world chart abstraction workflows. Several design decisions were critical. Separating vector storage (in the index) from full-text storage (in the key-value store) reduced costs by avoiding the expense of storing note content alongside high-dimensional vectors. Selecting a storage-optimized index over an in-memory configuration reduced monthly hosting costs, with a subset comparison showing that 8% of the corpus in memory costs more than 100% on storage-optimized disk. The latency trade-off remained within sub-second bounds acceptable for interactive use.

对可用性和检索质量都有影响的进一步设计决策是元数据过滤器的集成，它具有双重目的。它们使用户能够按患者、时间、专业、注释类型和其他属性进行查询，这是临床医生如何看待患者信息的自然延伸。它们还减轻了密集检索的一个已知特性：相似性评分有利于简短、焦点集中的文档，其嵌入与查询紧密结合。按注释类别过滤允许用户优先考虑临床实质性文档，例如进度注释和出院摘要，而不是路由注释或患者教育文档。A further design decision with implications for both usability and retrieval quality was the integration of metadata filters, which serve a dual purpose. They enable users to scope queries by patient, time, specialty, note type, and other attributes, a natural extension of how clinicians think about patient information. They also mitigate a known property of dense retrieval: similarity scoring favors short, narrowly focused documents whose embeddings are tightly aligned with queries. Filtering by note category allows users to prioritize clinically substantive documents such as progress notes and discharge summaries over routing notes or patient education documentation.

我们对临床实用性的评估表明，该系统减少了图表审查任务所需的时间，同时保持了抽象数据的高精度，这与先前的工作一致，表明传统的图表审查耗时且难以扩展[27,28]。该系统已被几位主要研究人员及其研究团队采用，并且正在继续被 CHOP 的其他研究人员更广泛地采用。最初的用户交互和反馈揭示了两个观察结果。首先，用户看重同时查询整个患者群体的能力，亚秒级的响应时间与传统的一次处理一个患者的图表审查形成鲜明对比。对于罕见疾病研究或关键字搜索产生许多误报并且否定和同义词失败的情况，语义搜索显示出改善队列识别的希望，特别是考虑到基于关键字的方法已表现出较差的特异性和缺乏语义理解[29-31]。其次，用户指出，相关信息是从被忽视的注释类型中浮现出来的，例如护士分类和电话咨询，这些信息在传统图表审查中经常被跳过，但包含关键的临床细节。通过纵向用户研究将这些观察结果形式化并衡量其对研究结果的影响对于未来的工作仍然很重要。Our evaluation of clinical utility demonstrated that the system reduced the time necessary for chart review tasks while maintaining a high level of accuracy in the abstracted data, consistent with prior work showing that traditional chart review is time-intensive and difficult to scale[27,28] . The system has already been adopted by several principal investigators and their research teams and is continuing to see broader uptake by additional researchers across CHOP. Initial user interactions and feedback revealed two observations. First, users valued the ability to query the entire patient population simultaneously, with sub-second response times contrasting sharply with conventional one-patient-at-a-time chart review. For rare disease research or cases where keyword search produces many false positives and fails on negations and synonyms, semantic search showed promise for improving cohort identification, particularly given that keyword-based approaches have demonstrated poor specificity and lack of semantic understanding[29–31] . Second, users noted that relevant information was surfaced from overlooked note types, such as nurse triage and telephone encounters, that are often skipped in traditional chart review but contain critical clinical details. Formalizing these observations through longitudinal user studies and measuring their impact on research outcomes remains important for future work.

该系统被设计为基础设施而不是独立的应用程序。检索到的内容充当下游任务（包括检索增强生成）的基础证据，并且索引可以支持自动化工作流程的编程访问。治理框架（包括每个项目的容器化部署、基于白名单的访问控制、查询审核日志记录和标准化 IRB 协议附件）提供了广泛采用所需的机构框架。The system is designed as an infrastructure rather than a standalone application. The retrieved content serves as grounding evidence for downstream tasks including retrieval-augmented generation, and the index can support programmatic access for automated workflows. The governance framework, including per-project containerized deployments, allowlist-based access control, query audit logging, and a standardized IRB protocol attachment provides the institutional scaffolding needed for broad adoption.

局限性和未来的工作Limitations and future work

这项研究的主要局限性是评估仅限于部署在谷歌云平台上的单个儿科学术医疗中心；文档实践、最佳系统配置和基础设施选择可能因机构和云提供商而异。此外，系统仅索引文本，不支持图像或表格等多模态数据；扩展系统以支持多模式输入将为复杂的临床任务提供更丰富的背景。此外，分块策略依赖于具有语言边界启发式的固定大小窗口，并且不考虑语义连贯性；开发基于临床概念边界对笔记进行内容感知的语义分块方法是未来工作的一个重要方向。The primary limitation of this study is that the evaluation is limited to a single pediatric academic medical center deployed on Google Cloud Platform; documentation practices, optimal system configurations, and infrastructure choices may differ across institutions and cloud providers. Furthermore, the system indexes only text and does not support multimodal data such as images or tables; extending the system to support multimodal inputs would provide richer context for complex clinical tasks. Additionally, the chunking strategy relies on fixed-size windows with linguistic boundary heuristics and does not account for semantic coherence; developing contentaware semantic chunking methods that segment notes based on clinical concept boundaries is an important direction for future work.

结论Conclusion

对非结构化临床记录进行卫生系统规模的语义搜索在技术上是可行的，具有亚秒级延迟和低运营成本。通过结合高性能开源嵌入模型、存储优化的向量索引和低延迟键值存储，我们Health-system scale semantic search over unstructured clinical notes is technically feasible with sub-second latency and low operational cost. By combining a high-performance open-source embedding model, a storage-optimized vector index, and a low-latency key-value store, we

Importantly, this work moves semantic search from a proof-of-concept task to shared institutional infrastructure. Through a researcher-facing interface that supports natural-language queries and fine-grained filters, investigators can perform chart review and cohort discovery with accuracy comparable to traditional EHR review methods. The system provides institutional infrastructure for interactive search, automated abstraction, and LLM-powered clinical reasoning across the health system.
