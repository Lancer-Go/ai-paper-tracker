心血管疾病仍然是全球死亡的主要原因。进展受到一个关键瓶颈的阻碍：人类对复杂心脏测试的解释。人工智能 (AI) 已成为一种潜在的解决方案，但当前的视觉语言模型仅限于单一模态输入，并且是非交互式的。在这里，我们推出了 MARCUS（超声和信号多模态自主推理和聊天），这是一种代理视觉语言系统，设计用于独立地和作为多模态输入对原始心电图 (ECG)、超声心动图和心脏磁共振成像 (CMR) 进行端到端解释。 MARCUS 采用分层代理架构，其中包含特定于模态的视觉语言专家模型，每个模型都将经过领域训练的视觉编码器与多阶段语言模型优化相集成，并由多模态编排器进行协调。 MARCUS 经过 1350 万张图像（25 万张心电图、130 万张超声心动图图像和 1200 万张 CMR 图像）以及我们新颖的、专家策划的问答和涵盖 160 万个问题的自由文本数据集的训练，实现了超越前沿模型（GPT-5 Thinking 和 Gemini 2.5 Pro Deep Think）的最先进性能。在内部（斯坦福大学）和外部（UCSF）测试队列中，MARCUS 的心电图准确率达到 87-91%，超声心动图准确率达到 67-86%，CMR 判读准确率达到 85-88%，显着优于前沿模型 34-45%（Cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of global mortality. This limitation necessitates a manual human-text step and introduces the risk of upstream errors or omissions.

随后的进步试图通过开发直接解释原始诊断信号的模型来缩小这一差距Subsequent advancements have sought to close this gap by developing models that interpret raw diagnostic signals directly[6, 11, 24]。 EchoNext 就是一个例子，它是一种卷积神经网络模型，经过训练可以根据 12 导联心电图波形预测心脏病的概率。 EchoNext 改进了纯文本方法，因为它直接解释原始 12 导联形式的心电图，并在检测心脏病方面实现了高精度。同样，EchoPrime 是一种基于视频的视觉语言基础模型，利用对比学习对超声心动图视频进行综合评估。 EchoPrime 可以自动消化超声心动图视频，并通过从先前解释的语料库中检索临床上最相关的文本来生成报告. One example is EchoNext, a convolutional neural network model trained to predict probabilities of cardiac diseases from 12-lead ECG waveforms. EchoNext advances text-only approaches as it interprets the ECG directly in their raw, 12-lead form and achieved high accuracy in detecting cardiac disease. Similarly, EchoPrime is a video-based vision-language foundation model that utilizes contrastive learning to perform comprehensive assessments of echocardiogram videos. EchoPrime can autonomously digest echocardiogram videos and generate reports by retrieving the most clinically relevant text from a corpus of prior interpretations[29].

尽管有这些技术飞跃，当前的视觉模型仍存在单一模式的上限。 EchoNext 在架构上仅限于心电图，而 EchoPrime 仅限于超声心动图。跨模式合成的缺乏代表了临床实用性的一个关键限制。这些单一模态工具可帮助医生完成离散任务（“根据心电图，患者是否需要超声心动图”），但临床心脏病专家使用多模态数据来诊断、分类和管理患者。为了进一步推进临床实施，医生和政策制定者建议临床人工智能模型的下一个前沿是多模式Despite these technical leaps, current vision models have a single-modality ceiling. EchoNext is architecturally restricted to ECGs, and EchoPrime to echocardiograms. This lack of cross-modal synthesis represents a critical limitation in clinical utility. These single modality tools help physicians with discrete tasks (‘Based on this ECG does a patient need an echocardiogram’), but clinical cardiologists use multimodal data to diagnose, triage and manage a patient. To further advance clinical implementation, physicians and policy makers have recommended the next frontier of clinical AI models be multimodal[22, 18, 3, 27, 20].

在我们的配套论文中In our companion paper[1]，我们描述了“海市蜃楼推理”，即视觉语言模型提供从未提供过的图像的详细描述的现象，包括复杂推理痕迹的生成。我们发现海市蜃楼推理在所有视觉语言模型中普遍存在，从根本上挑战了当前关于这些模型中视觉理解的假设。在这里，我们使用方法中描述的反事实探测协议独立评估 MARCUS 在每个特定模态专家模型中对这种影响的敏感性。当在没有代理协调器验证的情况下进行单独测试时，各个专家模型表现出非零的幻影率。当出现无图像查询时，ECG、超声心动图和 CMR 专家模型平均分别在 33.0%、38.5% 和 36.4% 的病例中产生幻觉。这些比率与前沿 VLM 报告的海市蜃楼效应一致, we describe ‘mirage reasoning’, the phenomenon in which vision-language models provide detailed descriptions of images never provided, including the generation of complex reasoning traces. We find that mirage reasoning is widespread amongst all vision-language models, fundamentally challenging current assumptions about visual understanding in these models. Here, we assess MARCUS’s susceptibility to this effect across each modality-specific expert model independently using the counterfactual probing protocol described in Methods. When tested in isolation without the agentic orchestrator’s verification, individual expert models exhibited non-zero mirage rates. When presented with image-absent queries, the ECG, echocardiography, and CMR expert models generated mirages in 33.0%, 38.5%, and 36.4% of cases on average, respectively. However, when MARCUS was used to process multimodal data and thus had the full agentic pipeline engaged, the orchestrator’s counterfactual probes successfully identified all occurrences of mirages. After confidence-weighted aggregation, MARCUS achieved a mirage rate of 0% across all modalities and test cohorts, with no correctly grounded responses inadvertently suppressed. These results demonstrate that mirage susceptibility is not an intrinsic limitation of vision-language architectures and can be mitigated through inference-time verification, provided the system architecture supports counterfactual probing of its own components.

2 讨论Discussion

在这项工作中，我们提出了 MARCUS，一种代理的多模态基础模型，专为心电图 (ECG)、超声心动图和心脏磁共振成像 (CMR) 的端到端解释而设计。 MARCUS 从根本上代表了当前人工智能模型的进步。首先，与现有的单模态模型相比，MARCUS 能够进行多模态理解。其次，它是第一个能够进行交互式临床推理的心血管视觉语言系统。第三，MARCUS 中采用的多阶段训练管道（下一个令牌预测、监督微调和组相对策略优化）与其代理协调器相结合，促进了单模态和多模态诊断准确性、抗海市蜃楼推理的最先进性能，并产生新颖的、生成的、非对比性的输出。第四，我们通过 MARCUS 引入了由心脏病专家策划的大型、严格的问答基准，并结合从医生报告中提取的基本事实。最后，MARCUS 接受了迄今为止最大的多模态心脏数据集（1350 万张图像）的训练。为了加速研究和临床转化，我们公开发布了经过训练的模型、代码、多模态数据集和视觉问题基准测试集，作为科学界的开源资源。In this work, we present MARCUS, an agentic, multimodal foundation model designed for the end-to-end interpretation of electrocardiograms (ECGs), echocardiograms, and cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (CMR). MARCUS represents an advance on current AI models in fundamental ways. First, MARCUS is capable of multimodal understanding, compared with existing, single modality models. Second, it is the first cardiovascular vision-language system capable of interactive clinical reasoning. Third, the multistage training pipeline employed in MARCUS (next-token prediction, supervised fine-tuning, and group relative policy optimization) coupled with its agentic orchestrator facilitates state-of-the-art performance in single and multimodality diagnostic accuracy, resistance to mirage reasoning, and produces novel, generative, non-contrastive output. Fourth, with MARCUS we introduce a large, rigorous Q&A benchmark curated by expert cardiologists and coupled with ground truths extracted from physician reports. Finally, MARCUS is trained on the largest corpus of multimodal cardiac data (13.5 million images) to date. To accelerate research and clinical translation, we have publicly released our trained models, code, multimodal dataset, and visual questions benchmarking set as an open-source resource for the scientific community.

我们的工作有局限性。我们的训练数据来自一个中心。虽然我们在外部验证了性能，但对不同社区人群、成像协议和设备供应商的普遍性需要进一步研究。其次，我们的评估集中在精心策划的多项选择和结构化临床推理开放式问题上。这种格式可以进行严格的基准测试，但可能无法完全捕捉现实世界临床查询的异质性或许多成像结果固有的模糊性。第三，本研究是回顾性的。目前尚不清楚 MARCUS 是否可以改善结果、减少诊断错误或缩短治疗时间。这些仍有待在前瞻性试验中确定。第四，虽然我们模型的超声心动图准确性超过了大型前沿模型，但低于 ECG 和 CMR。这可能反映了超声成像更大的异质性和操作员依赖性。未来的工作也应该包括这些数据。值得注意的是，这些限制并不是我们工作所独有的。单中心开发、精心策划的评估数据集和回顾性设计是大多数已发布的人工智能模型的特点，强调了整个领域对标准化、多机构基准和前瞻性验证框架的需求。Our work has limitations. Our training data was derived from a single centre. While we validated performance externally, generalizability to diverse community populations, imaging protocols, and equipment vendors requires further study. This may reflect the greater heterogeneity and operator-dependence of ultrasound imaging. Future work should also include this data. Notably, these limitations are not unique to our work; single-centre development, curated evaluation datasets, and retrospective design characterize most published AI models, underscoring the need for standardized, multi-institutional benchmarks and prospective validation frameworks across the field.

总之，MARCUS 代表了人工智能系统迈出的一步，该系统可以对现代心血管护理的核心模式进行推理。通过超越单任务分类到交互式、多模式解释，这项工作为下一代临床决策支持奠定了基础；这是一种人工智能增强临床护理并将专家级评估扩展到近乎即时的患者和那些原本无法获得的患者的范例。In conclusion, MARCUS represents a step towards AI systems that reason across modalities central to modern cardiovascular care. By moving beyond single-task classification to interactive, multimodal interpretation, this work offers a foundation for the next generation of clinical decision support; a paradigm where AI augments clinical care and extends specialist-level assessments to patients near-instantly and to those who might not otherwise have access.

3.1 概述Overview

我们分别为三种心脏模式（心电图、超声心动图和 CMR）创建了交互式视觉到语言模型，并创建了一个综合所有三种数据类型的多模式编排代理。超声心动图和 CMR 个体编码器模型遵循类似的架构，由四个主要组件组成：(1) 提取成像特征的多视图视觉编码器，(2) 模拟心脏运动的时间聚合模块，(3) 集成跨视图信息的跨视图融合机制，以及 (4) 使用组相对策略优化 (GRPO) 生成文本输出的语言模型（补充图 1）。 ECG 模型由三个主要组件组成：(1) 从基于块的图像表示中提取特征的 SigLIP 视觉编码器，(2) 将视觉嵌入与语言模型的表示空间对齐的两层 MLP 投影模块，以及 (3) 生成文本输出的 Qwen2 语言模型。该研究得到了斯坦福大学机构审查委员会的批准（IRB：67663, 41045）。We created interactive, vision-to-language models for each of our three cardiac modalities (ECGs, echocardiograms, and CMRs) separately and a multimodality orchestrating agent that synthesizes all three data types. The echocardiogram and CMR individual encoder models followed a similar architecture and comprised four main components: (1) a multi-view visual encoder that extracts imaging features, (2) a temporal aggregation module that models cardiac motion, (3) a cross-view fusion mechanism that integrates information across views, and (4) a language model that generates textual outputs with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) (Supplementary Figure 1). The ECG model consisted of three main components: (1) a SigLIP vision encoder that extracts features from patch-based image representations, (2) a two-layer MLP projection module that aligns visual embeddings with the language model’s representational space, and (3) a Qwen2 language model that generates textual outputs. The study was approved by Stanford’s Institutional Review Board (IRB: 67663, 41045).

3.7 模型架构：多模态Model architecture: Multimodality

我们采用模块化代理方法进行多模态集成，其中特定模态模型通过自然语言而不是共享嵌入空间进行通信。这一架构决策是出于两个考虑因素。首先，大型语言模型开发的快速步伐（大约每三个月发布一次最先进的新模型）需要面向未来的架构，该架构可以融合先进技术而无需进行大量的再培训。其次，虽然嵌入空间方法（例如 NExT-GPT、LLaVA-NeXT）训练投影层将每种模态的表示映射到统一的标记空间中，但每个新语言模型引入了不同的嵌入空间和标记器，需要重新训练投影层以实现兼容性。通过使用自然语言作为特定模态模型之间的接口，代理框架可以无缝集成更新或替代语言模型，而无需重新训练视觉编码器或投影模块。第三，基于文本的编排器减少了我们在其他视觉语言模型中观察到的海市蜃楼，如我们的配套论文（Mirage）中所述We adopted a modular agentic approach for multimodal integration, wherein modality-specific models communicate through natural language rather than shared embedding spaces. This architectural decision was motivated by two considerations. First, the rapid pace of large language model development (with new state-of-the-art models released approximately every three months) necessitates future-proof architectures that can incorporate advances without extensive retraining. Second, while embedding-space approaches (e.g., NExT-GPT, LLaVA-NeXT) train projection layers to map each modality’s representations into a unified token space, each new language model introduces a different embedding space and tokenizer, requiring projection layers to be retrained for compatibility. By using natural language as the interface between modality-specific models, the agentic framework enables seamless integration of updated or alternative language models without retraining visual encoders or projection modules. Third, the text-based orchestrator reduces mirages we have observed in other vision language models, as described in our companion paper (Mirage)[1].

协调器还实现了置信度评估协议，旨在检测未基于所提供的成像数据的输出。为了防止海市蜃楼推理，协调器在推理时让每个模态专家接受三步验证程序。首先，对于每个临床子查询，协调器生成三个语义上等效但语法上不同的改写，并将所有变体路由到相关的专家模型。其次，编排器发出相同查询的无图像版本（海市蜃楼模式）作为反事实探针，在不访问任何视觉输入的情况下先建立专家模型的语言。第三，每个模态的置信度得分是根据两个信号计算的：专家在三个改写的查询中的响应的一致性，以及图像存在响应和图像不存在基线之间的差异。重述间的高度一致性加上与反事实基线的高度背离表明视觉基础推理；相反，与无图像输出非常相似的响应，无论其明显的置信度如何，都被标记为可能受海市蜃楼影响。编排器根据每种模态的置信度分数对最终评估的贡献进行加权，并在标记一种或多种模态时向用户传达剩余的不确定性。The orchestrator additionally implements a confidence-assessment protocol designed to detect outputs not grounded in the provided imaging data. To guard against mirage reasoning, the orchestrator subjects each modality expert to a three-step verification procedure at inference time. First, for each clinical sub-query, the orchestrator generates three semantically equivalent but syntactically distinct rephrasings and routes all variants to the relevant expert model. Second, the orchestrator issues an image-absent version (mirage-mode) of the same query as a counterfactual probe that establishes the expert model’s language prior without access to any visual input. Third, a per-modality confidence score is computed from two signals: the consistency of the expert’s responses across the three rephrased queries, and the divergence between the image-present responses and the image-absent baseline. High inter-rephrase consistency coupled with high divergence from the counterfactual baseline indicates visually grounded reasoning; conversely, responses that closely resemble the image-absent output, regardless of their apparent confidence, are flagged as potentially mirage-affected. The orchestrator weights each modality’s contribution to the final assessment proportionally to its confidence score, and communicates residual uncertainty to the user when one or more modalities are flagged.

3.3 GRPO奖励功能GRPO Reward Function

组相对策略优化（GRPO）是使用带有二进制正确性奖励的 verl 框架实现的。对于每个训练提示，该策略都会生成一组Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) was implemented using the verl framework with a binary correctness reward. For each training prompt, the policy generates a group ofnn=4个候选人的回应。每个响应都通过确定性奖励函数进行评分：如果提取的 MCQ 答案字母与真实答案匹配，则为 1.0，否则为 0.0。答案提取使用一系列与常见响应格式匹配的正则表达式模式（例如，“答案是 B”、“B. 轻度 LV 扩张”、独立字母）。组基线被估计为 4 个响应的平均奖励，并且使用类似于 PPO 但没有学习价值函数的剪裁比率目标相对于该基线计算策略梯度。低方差 KL 散度惩罚（系数 0.01）针对冻结的参考模型规范更新的策略，以防止奖励黑客和模式崩溃。与标准 PPO 不同，GRPO 不需要单独的批评者网络，从而降低了计算成本，同时保持稳定的训练动态。=4 candidate responses. Each response is scored by a deterministic reward function: 1.0 if the extracted MCQ answer letter matches the ground-truth answer, 0.0 otherwise. Answer extraction uses a cascade of regular expression patterns that match common response formats (e.g., “The answer is B”, “B. Mild LV dilation”, standalone letter). The group baseline is estimated as the mean reward across the 4 responses, and the policy gradient is computed relative to this baseline using a clipped ratio objective analogous to PPO but without a learned value function. A low-variance KL divergence penalty (coefficient 0.01) regularises the updated policy against the frozen reference model to prevent reward hacking and mode collapse. Unlike standard PPO, GRPO eliminates the need for a separate critic network, reducing computational cost while maintaining stable training dynamics.

反事实海市蜃楼探测协议的操作如下。对于每个临床子查询，协调器使用预定义模板生成三个语义上等效但语法上不同的改写（例如，直接问题形式：“{问题}”；描述性形式：“请描述所提供的{模态}数据中所见的{主题}。”；命令式形式：“根据提供的{模态}，回答以下问题：{问题}”）。所有三个改写都与图像或视频一起发送到相关的专家模型。然后，协调器发送没有任何视觉输入的原始查询作为反事实探测。The counterfactual mirage probing protocol operates as follows. For each clinical sub-query, the orchestrator generates three semantically equivalent but syntactically distinct rephrasings using predefined templates (e.g., direct question form: “{question}”; descriptive form: “Please describe {subject} as seen in the provided {modality} data.”; imperative form: “Based on the {modality} provided, answer the following: {question}”). All three rephrasings are sent to the relevant expert model together with the image or video. The orchestrator then sends the original query without any visual input as a counterfactual probe.

A response is flagged as a potential mirage when the image-absent similarity exceeds a threshold of 0.85 (i.e., divergence<<0.15），表明当视觉输入被移除时，模型的输出没有发生有意义的变化。综合置信度计算为一致性分数和散度分数的平均值，且加权相同。0.15), indicating that the model’s output did not meaningfully change when visual input was removed.
