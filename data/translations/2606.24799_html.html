OrbitForge：通过重建锚定视频合成生成文本到 3D 场景OrbitForge: Text-to-3D Scene Generation via Reconstruction-Anchored Video Synthesis

Chenrui Fan  Paolo Favaro
Computer Vision Group, Institute of Computer Science
University of Bern, Switzerland
{chenrui.fan,paolo.favaro}@unibe.ch
摘要Abstract

通用文本到视频模型可以用作丰富的开放世界场景先验。尽管当今生成的视频质量很高，但它们并不能直接生成可靠的 3D 资产：摄像机运动难以控制、视图覆盖不完整，而且帧通常包含随时间不一致的内容。我们介绍了 OrbitForge，这是一个由冻结视频先验和按提示高斯泼溅重建优化构建的适配器，可将单个文本生成的视频转换为规范的闭轨 3D 高斯泼溅场景。我们使用 3D 重建作为锚点来提高生成视频的 3D 一致性。我们使用强大的 MedianGS 代理通过可变形高斯泼溅从第一个生成的视频中获得初步 3D 重建。我们从规定的轨道渲染视图以检测缺失的视点。 OrbitForge 使用文本到视频模型仅完成缺失的视图，并将完成的轨道重建为最终的高斯溅射场景。这种设计不需要针对特定​​任务的视频或多视图微调，避免按提示进行分数蒸馏优化，并且不会一次一步地逐步生成视图。我们进一步认为，这种设置需要覆盖感知评估：仅局部平滑度就奖励从不尝试完整轨道的方法。在冻结的 300 提示 T 上Generic text-to-video models can be used as rich open-world scene priors. Despite the high quality of today’s generated videos, they do not directly yield reliable 3D assets: camera motion is difficult to control, view coverage is partial, and frames often contain inconsistencies across time. We introduce OrbitForge, an adapter built from frozen video priors and per-prompt Gaussian Splatting reconstruction optimization that converts a single text-generated video into a canonical closed-orbit 3D Gaussian Splatting scene. We use 3D reconstruction as an anchor to improve the 3D consistency of the generated video. We obtain a preliminary 3D reconstruction from a first generated video via Deformable Gaussian Splatting with a robust MedianGS proxy. We render views from a prescribed orbit to detect missing viewpoints. OrbitForge uses the text-to-video model to complete only the missing views, and reconstructs the completed orbit into a final Gaussian Splatting scene. This design requires no task-specific video or multiview fine-tuning, avoids per-prompt score-distillation optimization, and does not progressively generate views one step at a time. We further argue that this setting demands coverage-aware evaluation: local smoothness alone rewards methods that never attempt a full orbit. On a frozen 300-prompt T3基于基准导出的审核，OrbitForge 重建获得了 359.0 度的测量中值跨度，相对于仅使用 MedianGS 的重建，将最初不受支持的 bin Q10 ImageReward 从 8.07 提高到 16.36，同时在覆盖质量上与 VideoMV 保持竞争力。Bench-derived audit, OrbitForge reconstruction attains a 359.0-degree measured median span, raises originally unsupported-bin Q10 ImageReward from 8.07 to 16.36 relative to MedianGS-only reconstruction, while remaining competitive with VideoMV on the coverage-quality.

1 简介Introduction

通过借用大型 2D 和视频扩散模型的视觉知识，文本驱动的 3D 生成取得了显着的进展。然而，生成精美的图像或视频与可用的 3D 场景之间仍然存在巨大差距。 3D 场景不仅仅是合理视图的集合：它必须存在于坐标系中，保持可从请求的摄像机渲染，保持所描绘的对象和场景在轨道周围可识别，并暴露背面、侧面、地面和周围环境，而不仅仅是最上镜的前视图。Text-driven 3D generation has made remarkable progress by borrowing the visual knowledge of large 2D and video diffusion models. Yet there remains a sharp gap between a beautifully generated image or video and a usable 3D scene. A 3D scene is not merely a collection of plausible views: it must live in a coordinate system, remain renderable from requested cameras, keep the depicted object and scene recognizable around the orbit, and expose the back, sides, ground, and surrounding environment rather than only the most photogenic front view.

现有方法从三个方向之一应对这一挑战。分数蒸馏方法使用事先冻结的图像来优化每个提示的 3D 表示，通常会生成可渲染的以对象为中心的资产，但需要长时间优化并遭受过饱和、Janus 伪影或弱场景上下文的影响Existing approaches address this challenge from one of three directions. Score-distillation methods optimize a 3D representation per prompt using a frozen image prior, often producing renderable object-centric assets but requiring long optimization and suffering from over-saturation, Janus artifacts, or weak scene context[13, 17, 8, 18]。专门的多视图生成器学习相机条件先验并生成一组固定的视图，方便重建，但相机格式、高程和以对象为中心的假设通常内置于模型中. Specialized multiview generators learn a camera-conditioned prior and produce a fixed set of views that is convenient for reconstruction, but the camera format, elevation, and object-centric assumptions are usually built into the model[14, 16, 21, 7]。局部新颖视图方法可以从一张或多张图像合成高质量的短轨迹，但它们的优势是局部视图完成，不一定从无约束的文本生成视频生成封闭的 360 度场景. Local novel-view methods can synthesize high-quality short trajectories from one or a few images, but their strength is local view completion, not necessarily closed 360-degree scene generation from an unconstrained text-generated video[3, 20, 9].

我们研究一个补充问题：We study a complementary question:是否可以将冻结的通用文本到视频模型用作闭轨 3D 场景生成器，而无需事先训练新的多视图或运行按提示分数蒸馏采样 (SDS) 优化？can a frozen generic text-to-video model be used as a closed-orbit 3D scene generator without training a new multiview prior or running per-prompt Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) optimization?通用的文本到视频模型很有吸引力，因为它们已经产生了场景级的真实感：物体放在地板上，反射光线，与背景交互，并从语言提示继承开放世界的语义细节。然而，他们的视频不是经过校准的多视图捕获。相机运动是隐式的，并且通常仅覆盖部分弧线。物体形状可以变形，纹理可以重新绘制，背景可以漂移。直接将 3D 高斯拟合到此类帧会将这些矛盾转化为漂浮物、模糊的几何形状、拉伸的表面和依赖于视图的伪影。Generic text-to-video models are attractive because they already produce scene-level realism: objects rest on floors, reflect light, interact with backgrounds, and inherit open-world semantic detail from language prompts. However, their videos are not calibrated multiview captures. Camera motion is implicit and often covers only a partial arc. Object shape can deform, textures can be repainted, and backgrounds can drift. Directly fitting 3D Gaussians to such frames turns these contradictions into floaters, blurred geometry, stretched surfaces, and view-dependent artifacts.

OrbitForge 背后的关键观察是，失败的第一次重建仍然有用。即使它不完美，它也发挥了关键作用：它将生成的帧组织到共享的 3D 坐标系中。一旦沿着规定的规范轨道进行了初步重建，问题就变得结构化了。有些观点得到了源轨迹的支持；其他观点显然没有得到支持。然后该方法可以通过再次查询文本转视频模型来完成不支持的轨道间隔，同时保持支持的弧线固定，并在已知相机下再次重建（图The key observation behind OrbitForge is that a failed first reconstruction is still useful. Even when it is imperfect, it has performed a crucial act: it has organized the generated frames into a shared 3D coordinate system. Once this preliminary reconstruction is rendered along a prescribed canonical orbit, the problem becomes structured. Some views are supported by the source trajectory; other views are visibly unsupported. The method can then complete the unsupported orbit interval by querying the text-to-video model again, while keeping the supported arc fixed, and reconstruct again under known cameras (Figure1显示了 OrbitForge 生成的几个输出）。shows several outputs generated by OrbitForge).

因此，本文提出了精确的主张。 OrbitForge 的设计目的并不是要主导每个局部美学指标，也不应该将其视为狭窄的视图完成轨迹和完整的场景轨道是同一任务。其目标是从通用文本到视频先验进行提示可控、场景级、闭轨 3D 生成，无需特定于任务的生成模型训练或按提示 SDS 优化，同时仍使用按提示高斯 Splatting 重建优化。公平的评估必须在奖励局部平滑度之前衡量覆盖范围。Therefore, this paper makes a precise claim. OrbitForge is not designed to dominate every local aesthetic metric, and it should not be evaluated as if a narrow view-completion trajectory and a complete scene orbit were the same task. Its target is prompt-controllable, scene-level, closed-orbit 3D generation from generic text-to-video priors without task-specific generative-model training or per-prompt SDS optimization, while still using per-prompt Gaussian Splatting reconstruction optimization. A fair evaluation must measure coverage before rewarding local smoothness.

我们的贡献是四重的。首先，我们从通用文本生成的视频中制定闭轨 3D 场景生成，这种设置利用冻结的文本到视频先验，同时明确处理未校准的摄像机、部分覆盖和生成的视频不一致。其次，我们提出了 OrbitForge，一种重建-渲染-完成适配器，它不训练新的生成先验，并使用不完美的第一次重建作为锚点而不是最终输出。第三，我们引入了 MedianGS 静态场景整合和覆盖感知端点窗口轨道完成，它将部分生成的视频轨迹转换为完整的规范轨道重建信号，而无需渐进视图扩展。第四，我们引入了覆盖感知评估协议——测量角跨度、单调进度、闭轨成功、低尾质量和诊断外观一致性——以便对方法进行实际解决的任务进行比较，而不仅仅是相邻帧的平滑度。Our contributions are fourfold. First, we formulate closed-orbit 3D scene generation from generic text-generated videos, a setting that leverages frozen text-to-video priors while explicitly handling uncalibrated cameras, partial coverage, and generated-video inconsistency. Second, we propose OrbitForge, a reconstruction-render-completion adapter that does not train a new generative prior and uses an imperfect first reconstruction as an anchor rather than as a final output. Third, we introduce MedianGS static scene consolidation and coverage-aware endpoint-window orbit completion, which convert a partial generated-video trajectory into a complete canonical-orbit reconstruction signal without progressive view expansion. Fourth, we introduce a coverage-aware evaluation protocol–measured angular span, monotone progress, closed-orbit success, lower-tail quality, and diagnostic appearance consistency–so that methods are compared on the task they actually solve rather than only on adjacent-frame smoothness.

分数蒸馏文本到 3D。Score-distillation text-to-3D.

DreamFusion 使用预先训练的文本到图像扩散通过概率密度蒸馏优化 NeRFDreamFusion optimizes a NeRF using a pretrained text-to-image diffusion prior through probability density distillation[13]; SJC 通过可微渲染器反向传播扩散分数; SJC back-propagates diffusion scores through a differentiable renderer[17]; Magic3D 通过从粗到细的网格优化管道提高分辨率和效率; Magic3D improves resolution and efficiency through a coarse-to-fine mesh optimization pipeline[8];后来的方法如 ProlificDreamer 和 JSDDreamer 重新审视蒸馏目标以提高多样性和稳定性; and later methods such as ProlificDreamer and JSDDreamer revisit the distillation objective to improve diversity and stability[18, 1]。这些方法通常会事先冻结扩散，但它们仍然执行按提示的 SDS 式 3D 优化。 OrbitForge 而是先对通用视频进行采样，并根据生成的观察结果进行重建，避免了按提示进行 SDS 优化，同时保留了现代视频模型的场景级外观。. These methods usually keep the diffusion prior frozen, but they still perform per-prompt SDS-style 3D optimization. OrbitForge instead samples a generic video prior and reconstructs from generated observations, avoiding per-prompt SDS optimization while retaining the scene-level appearance of modern video models.

专门的多视图生成。Specialized multiview generation.

MVDream、SV3D、VideoMV 和 CoSER 表明扩散和视频架构可以适应强大的多视图生成器MVDream, SV3D, VideoMV, and CoSER show that diffusion and video architectures can be adapted into strong multiview generators[14, 16, 21, 7]。它们的输出对于对象重建很有价值，因为相机网格是已知的并且视图被设计为相互一致。权衡是专业化：此类系统通常采用固定的多视图格式、以物体为中心的轨道、规范高度或特定于任务的多视图训练。 OrbitForge 则采取相反的路线。它不会将视频模型微调为多视图生成器；它使用通用的文本到视频模型作为先验开放世界场景，然后在生成后构建相机系统。. Their outputs are valuable for object reconstruction because the camera grid is known and the views are designed to be mutually consistent. The trade-off is specialization: such systems typically assume a fixed multiview format, an object-centered orbit, a canonical elevation, or task-specific multiview training. OrbitForge takes the opposite route. It does not fine-tune the video model into a multiview generator; it uses a generic text-to-video model as an open-world scene prior, then constructs the camera system after generation.

作为 3D 或轨道生成器的视频先验。Video priors as 3D or orbit generators.

越来越多的工作更直接地使用视频扩散来生成 3D 或相机轨道。 ViVid-1-to-3 将新颖视图合成重新表述为生成朝向目标视图的扫描视频A growing line of work uses video diffusion more directly for 3D or camera-orbit generation. ViVid-1-to-3 reformulates novel-view synthesis as generating a scanning video toward a target view[6]; V3D 微调视频扩散模型，以便在重建之前从单个图像生成 360 度物体轨道帧; V3D fine-tunes a video diffusion model to generate 360-degree object-orbit frames from a single image before reconstruction[10];生成高斯分布将高斯分布特征表示集成到潜在视频扩散模型中，以生成 3D 场景; Generative Gaussian Splatting integrates a Gaussian Splatting feature representation inside a latent video diffusion model for 3D scene generation[15]; G4Splat 使用几何引导的生成完成来进行稀疏视图或无姿势视频重建; and G4Splat uses geometry-guided generative completion for sparse-view or unposed-video reconstruction[12]。 OrbitForge 是互补的：它不训练新的视频到 3D 生成器或假设真实的稀疏视图。相反，它通过重建、规范轨道拟合、覆盖感知完成和第二次重建来包装冻结的通用文本到视频。. OrbitForge is complementary: it does not train a new video-to-3D generator or assume real sparse views. Instead, it wraps a frozen generic text-to-video prior with reconstruction, canonical-orbit fitting, coverage-aware completion, and a second reconstruction.

用于新颖视图合成的视频扩散。Video diffusion for novel view synthesis.

CAT3D、ViewCrafter 和 ReconX 使用生成先验从稀疏的可靠观察中合成新颖的视图CAT3D, ViewCrafter, and ReconX use generative priors to synthesize novel views from sparse reliable observations[3, 20, 9]。 ViewCrafter 尤为重要，因为它可以通过逐步扩展 3D 线索来生成高质量的局部轨迹。我们的问题在起点和输出协议上有所不同。我们不是从真实的校准图像集开始；而是从真实的校准图像集开始。我们从生成的视频开始，该视频本身的帧可能不一致。我们不仅需要一个局部合理的轨迹；还需要一个局部合理的轨迹。我们需要一个适合 3D 重建和重复渲染的闭合规范轨道。. ViewCrafter is especially important because it can produce high-quality local trajectories by progressively extending 3D clues. Our problem differs in the starting point and the output protocol. We do not start from a real calibrated image set; we start from a generated video whose own frames may be inconsistent. We do not only require a locally plausible trajectory; we require a closed canonical orbit suitable for 3D reconstruction and repeated rendering.

生成视频作为 3D 先验。Generated videos as 3D priors.

WonderVerse 等最近的工作表明视频生成先验可以支持沉浸式 3D 场景生成和扩展Recent work such as WonderVerse demonstrates that video generation priors can support immersive 3D scene generation and extension[2]。这个方向激发了我们的中心设计原则：文本到视频模型不应仅仅被视为视频渲染器，而应被视为可扩展的场景先验。 OrbitForge 在此类先验和 3D 重建之间提供了一个简单的接口：第一次重建构建坐标，规范渲染暴露覆盖范围，视频补全填充缺失的间隔，第二次重建关闭轨道。. This direction motivates our central design principle: text-to-video models should not be treated merely as video renderers, but as scalable scene priors. OrbitForge contributes a simple interface between such priors and 3D reconstruction: a first reconstruction builds coordinates, canonical rendering exposes coverage, video completion fills the missing interval, and a second reconstruction closes the orbit.

高斯泼溅和变形重建。Gaussian Splatting and deformable reconstruction.

3D 高斯泼溅提供了具有高效可微渲染的显式表示3D Gaussian Splatting provides an explicit representation with efficient differentiable rendering[5]。可变形 3D 高斯通过学习规范高斯 Splatting 状态和时间相关变形场，将此表示扩展到单眼动态场景. We use DeformableGS in a nonstandard way. The goal is not to preserve a real dynamic scene, but to absorb the pseudo-dynamics of a generated video–shape drift, texture repainting, and background inconsistency–and then compress the result into a static structural proxy.
