不完整的观测提高了海洋建模中的进化性能Incomplete Observations Boost Evolutionary Performance in Ocean Modeling

摘要Abstract

数据驱动的方法彻底改变了海洋建模，但当前的方法严重依赖完整的再分析数据集，施加计算约束并将模型性能限制为训练数据的性能。在这里，我们提出了一个生成状态空间模型和一个优化框架，可以直接从稀疏和嘈杂的观察中学习。该模型本质上是一个具有连续状态空间的隐马尔可夫模型，其中海洋物理量被视为隐藏状态，测量被视为观测，从而实现了海洋场和观测数据的统一表示。初始状态和状态转换模块均以神经网络的形式实现，以捕获海洋状态的复杂性和时间演化，而发射模块则被公式化为掩蔽高斯分布。为了从稀疏观测中训练模型，我们得出了一个基于期望最大化（EM）算法的优化框架。该框架通过朗之万动力学交替重建高保真海洋场，并优化深度神经网络以捕获时间演化。理论分析表明，该框架最大化了生成模型下观察的可能性。为了提高效率，我们假设海洋状态演化遵循平稳、遍历和马尔可夫随机过程，并在优化过程中仅采用长度二状态序列。 CMIP6模拟数据和FY-3D卫星数据的实验证明了高保真重建和准确预测，表明稀疏观测可以直接提高模型对海洋状态动力学的表征。这项工作为下一代地球系统模型提供了一条可扩展的途径，可以直接从稀疏、不完整的现实世界观测中学习。Data-driven methods have revolutionized ocean modeling, yet current approaches rely heavily on complete reanalysis datasets, imposing computational constraints and limiting model performance to that of the training data. Here, we present a generative state-space model and an optimization framework that enable learning directly from sparse and noisy observations. The model is essentially a hidden Markov model with a continuous state space, where oceanic physical quantities are treated as hidden states and measurements as observations, enabling a unified representation of ocean fields and observational data. Both the initial-state and state-transition modules are implemented as neural networks to capture the complexity and temporal evolution of ocean states, while the emission module is formulated as a masked Gaussian distribution. To train the model from sparse observations, we derive an optimization framework based on the expectation–maximization (EM) algorithm. The framework alternately reconstructs high-fidelity ocean fields via Langevin dynamics and optimizes deep neural networks to capture temporal evolution. Theoretical analysis shows that the framework maximizes the likelihood of observations under the generative model. For efficiency, we assume that ocean-state evolution follows a stationary, ergodic, and Markovian stochastic process and adopt only length-two state sequences during optimization. Experiments on CMIP6 simulation data and FY-3D satellite data demonstrate high-fidelity reconstruction and accurate prediction, showing that sparse observations can directly improve the model’s representation of ocean-state dynamics. This work offers a scalable pathway for next-generation Earth system models to learn directly from sparse, incomplete real-world observations.

1 简介Introduction

近年来，以深度学习为引领的人工智能（AI）推动了科学发现的重大转变，在“AI for Science”的旗帜下对地球科学的影响越来越大In recent years, artificial intelligence (AI), spearheaded by deep learning, has driven a substantial shift in scientific discovery, with growing implications for the Earth sciences under the banner of “AI for Science”(Reichstein2019Nature)。 以盘古天气为代表的下一代数据驱动基础模型的出现. The emergence of next-generation data-driven foundation models, exemplified by Pangu-Weather(Bi2023Nature), GraphCast (Lam2023Science), 凤舞, FengWu(Chen2023FengWu)和 FourCastNet, and FourCastNet(Pathak2022FourCastNet)，正在重塑地球系统建模。 这些模型以低得多的推理成本实现了与传统数值天气预报 (NWP) 相当的精度，这是一项显着的进步, is reshaping Earth system modeling. These models achieve accuracies comparable to traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP) at substantially lower inference cost, representing a notable advance(Nguyen2023ClimaX)。 这些发展使得构建能够以高分辨率和保真度模拟和预测复杂地球系统的“数字孪生”的目标比以前更容易实现。. These developments have made the goal of constructing a “Digital Twin” capable of simulating and predicting complex Earth systems at high resolution and fidelity more attainable than before.

然而，这些最先进的人工智能模型的成功几乎无一例外地依赖于大规模、高质量的再分析数据集，例如大气的 ERA5However, the success of these state-of-the-art AI models rests almost invariably on large-scale, high-quality reanalysis datasets, such as ERA5 for the atmosphere(Hersbach2020QJRMS)以及全球海洋状态估计，包括 GLORYS 和 ECCOand global ocean state estimates including GLORYS and ECCO(Forget2015ECCO)。 再分析场是通过数据同化 (DA) 将稀疏、多模态观测与传统物理数值模型的模拟相融合而产生的。 尽管此类产品目前是主要的训练数据，但对这种完整领域范式的依赖对进一步的进展施加了两个限制。 首先，海洋数据同化的计算成本限制了再分析产品的时空分辨率，从而导致相对于人工智能模型的能力，更精细、动态重要的亚介尺度过程无法得到解决. Reanalysis fields are produced by fusing sparse, multi-modal observations with simulations from traditional physical numerical models via data assimilation (DA). Although such products are currently the principal training data, dependence on this complete-field paradigm imposes two constraints on further progress. First, the computational cost of ocean data assimilation limits the spatiotemporal resolution of reanalysis products, thereby leaving finer, dynamically important sub-mesoscale processes under-resolved relative to the capacity of AI models(Brajard2020JCS)。 其次，再分析的准确性受到观测覆盖范围和数值模型保真度的限制，从而创建了与内在不确定性相关的有效性能上限。 当人工智能模型将再分析数据视为基本事实时，预测能力在很大程度上受到再分析保真度本身的限制，这阻碍了它们发展成为原则上可以补充传统数值方法的独立物理模拟器。 因此，一个核心科学问题是是否可以减少对再分析的依赖，是否可以建立一种训练范式，允许人工智能模型直接从不完整、稀疏和嘈杂的海洋观测中学习——例如具有较大空间间隙的云污染卫星测绘带测量. Second, reanalysis accuracy is bounded by observational coverage and numerical model fidelity, creating an effective performance ceiling associated with intrinsic uncertainties. When AI models treat reanalysis data as ground truth, predictive skill is largely limited by reanalysis fidelity itself, which hinders their development into independent physical simulators that could, in principle, complement traditional numerical methods. Consequently, a central scientific question is whether dependence on reanalysis can be reduced and whether a training paradigm can be established that allows AI models to learn directly from incomplete, sparse, and noisy ocean observations—such as cloud-contaminated satellite swath measurements with large spatial gaps(Zhang2025JGRMLC)。 应对这一挑战可能会使人工智能模型超越模拟同化产品，转向学习观察一致的动态. Meeting this challenge may move AI models beyond emulating assimilated products toward learning observation-consistent dynamics(Runge2019NatComms)。 然而现有的生成范式也面临着困难。 是否基于生成对抗网络（GAN）. Yet existing generative paradigms also face difficulties. Whether based on generative adversarial networks (GANs)(Ravuri2021Nature), 扩散模型, diffusion models(Mardani2023CorrDiff)或最近的稀疏重建方法，这些方法在直接应用于此类科学任务时遇到基本限制。 标准生成模型主要为自然图像合成而设计，通常不具有明确的物理约束。 尽管它们可以产生视觉上合理的静态场，但它们常常难以在复杂的时间演化中保持物理一致性——地球系统机器学习中越来越注意到这一差距, or recent sparse reconstruction methods, these approaches encounter fundamental limitations when applied directly to such scientific tasks. Designed primarily for natural image synthesis, standard generative models are typically not constructed with explicit physical constraints. Although they can produce visually plausible static fields, they often struggle to maintain physical consistency over complex temporal evolution—a gap increasingly noted in Earth system machine learning(Sonnewald2019; Feng2025JGRMLC)。 特别是，它们缺乏明确的机制来保持近似地转平衡、质量守恒或海洋动力学特征的大规模环流特征。. In particular, they lack explicit mechanisms to preserve approximate geostrophic balance, mass conservation, or large-scale circulation features characteristic of ocean dynamics.

为了解决这一差距，我们提出了一种专为不完整的观测数据设计的生成状态空间建模框架。 通过结合时间动态的状态空间表示，该框架在生成过程中保持时空一致性。 完整的海洋场被视为潜在状态，异构测量通过掩蔽高斯观测模型链接，深度生成网络参数化初始状态分布和随机状态转换。 使用期望最大化 (EM) 策略来估计参数To address this gap, we propose a generative state-space modeling framework designed for incomplete observational data. By incorporating a state-space representation of temporal dynamics, the framework maintains spatiotemporal coherence within the generative process. The complete ocean field is treated as a latent state, heterogeneous measurements are linked through a masked Gaussian observation model, and deep generative networks parameterize the initial-state distribution and stochastic state transitions. Parameters are estimated using an expectation–maximization (EM) strategy(Dempster1977EM)。 在期望（E）步骤中，我们使用基于朗之万动力学的蒙特卡罗采样；以稀疏观测为条件，此步骤重建高保真、时空连续的海洋场。 在最大化 (M) 步骤中，重建字段监督初始状态生成器（修改后的 StyleGAN2-ADA 主干）和状态转换模型（随机 U-Net）的更新. In the expectation (E) step, we use Monte Carlo sampling based on Langevin dynamics; conditioned on sparse observations, this step reconstructs high-fidelity, spatiotemporally continuous oceanographic fields. In the maximization (M) step, the reconstructed fields supervise updates to the initial-state generator (a modified StyleGAN2-ADA backbone) and the state-transition model (a stochastic U-Net)(Ross2023JAMES)。 E 步和 M 步之间的迭代允许模型从噪声和缺失数据中细化物理模式，并重建和预测物理系统的时空演化。. Iterating between the E- and M-steps allows the model to refine physical patterns from noisy and missing data and to reconstruct and predict spatiotemporal evolution of the physical system.
