1 简介Introduction

在服务、医疗保健和辅助场景中与人类交互的机器人不仅需要估计外部行为，还需要估计内部生理状态。 心率 (HR) 是了解人类状况、工作量、压力和安全性的有用生理线索，但可穿戴或接触式传感器需要与机器人共享数据，而这是不太可能的。 机器人已经配备了 RGB 摄像头。 因此，基于摄像头的生理传感为实现不显眼的人类感知机器人感知提供了一条有吸引力的途径Robots that interact with humans in service, healthcare, and assistive scenarios need to estimate not only external behavior but also internal physiological state. Heart rate (HR) is a useful physiological cue for understanding human condition, workload, stress, and safety, but wearable or contact sensors would require data sharing with robots, which is unlikely. Robots already have RGB cameras. Camera-based physiological sensing therefore provides an attractive path toward unobtrusive human-aware robot perception[7].

远程光电体积描记法 (rPPG) 旨在通过分析皮下血量变化引起的细微时间颜色变化，从普通面部视频中恢复与脉搏相关的生理信号Remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) aims to recover pulse-related physiological signals from ordinary facial videos by analyzing subtle temporal color variations caused by blood volume changes beneath the skin[9]。 由于 rPPG 仅需要 RGB 摄像头，因此它与已经使用摄像头进行感知和交互的人脸机器人非常吻合. Because rPPG only requires an RGB camera, it is well aligned with human-facing robots that already use cameras for perception and interaction[8]。 然而，与环境和采集噪声相比，有用的 rPPG 分量较弱。 在真实的人机交互环境中，摄像机观察可能会在不同的房间照明、视角和用户位置下捕获。 跨视频照明变化可以主导微弱的脉冲相关颜色信号，使得基于相机的稳健生理感知变得困难。. However, the useful rPPG component is weak compared with environmental and acquisition noise. In real human-robot interaction environments, camera observations may be captured under different room lighting, viewing angles, and user positions. Cross-video illumination variation can dominate the weak pulse-related color signal, making robust camera-based physiological perception difficult.

心率信息出现在 rPPG 信号的时间波形和频谱峰值中。 因此，之前的 rPPG 研究越来越多地结合时域和频域监督。 AutoHR 将 Pearson 式时间波形约束与频域 HR 监控相结合Heart-rate information appears in both the temporal waveform and the spectral peak of the rPPG signal. Prior rPPG studies therefore increasingly combine temporal and frequency-domain supervision. AutoHR combines Pearson-style temporal waveform constraints with frequency-domain HR supervision[15]。 PhysFormer集成了时间波形监控、频率交叉熵和标签分布学习，并进一步研究频率加权和动态加权效果. PhysFormer integrates temporal waveform supervision, frequency cross-entropy, and label-distribution learning, and further studies frequency weighting and dynamic weighting effects[16]。 LSTC-rPPG 使用具有尺度不变功率谱密度 (PSD) 损失的时域波形回归. LSTC-rPPG uses time-domain waveform regression with a scale-invariant power spectral density (PSD) loss[4]，而最近强大的 rPPG 框架使用时间、频率和 HR 分布监督或动态混合损失来提高复杂和跨数据集场景中的泛化能力, while recent robust rPPG frameworks use temporal, frequency, and HR-distribution supervision or dynamic hybrid losses to improve generalization in complex and cross-dataset scenarios[1]。 FreqPhys 进一步强调生理光谱先验作为稳健 rPPG 的频率感知线索. FreqPhys further highlights physiological spectral priors as frequency-aware cues for robust rPPG[6]。 总之，这些研究表明时间波形约束和频谱指导为 rPPG 学习提供了补充监督。 然而，这些研究主要集中在标准基准、跨数据集泛化或一般频率感知鲁棒性；受控照明变化下适当的时频损耗平衡仍未得到充分研究。. Together, these studies suggest that temporal waveform constraints and spectral guidance provide complementary supervision for rPPG learning. However, these studies mainly focus on standard benchmarks, cross-dataset generalization, or general frequency-aware robustness; the appropriate temporal-frequency loss balance under controlled illumination variation remains insufficiently examined.

这项工作的贡献是三重的。 首先，我们将基于 rPPG 的心率估计制定为光照变化下面向人的机器人系统的基于相机的生理感知问题。 其次，我们的估计器结合了基于 PRNet 的面部对齐、剪辑级照明增强和残留时间标准化模块 (RTSM)，以减少照明引起的时间特征统计变化。 第三，我们研究了对独立于受试者的多照明协议的时间频率监督的调整，作为实现稳健的生理传感的第一步。 我们比较静态全级混合协议中的仅时间、仅频率和多个加权混合设置，以确定受控照明变化下时间波形一致性和光谱 HR 指导之间的实际平衡，同时保持运动干扰最小。The contributions of this work are threefold. First, we formulate rPPG-based heart-rate estimation as a camera-based physiological perception problem for human-facing robotic systems under illumination variation. Second, our estimator combines PRNet-based facial alignment, clip-level illumination augmentation, and Residual Temporal Standardization Module (RTSM) to reduce illumination-induced temporal feature-statistic shifts. Third, we study tuning of temporal-frequency supervision on a subject-independent, multi-illumination protocol as a first step toward robust physiological sensing. We compare time-only, frequency-only, and multiple weighted hybrid settings in a static all-level mix protocol to identify a practical balance between temporal waveform consistency and spectral HR guidance under controlled illumination variation while keeping motion disturbance minimal.

2 相关工作Related Work

生理传感已被研究为一种通过估计外部可见行为之外的人类状态来支持人机交互和协作的方法。 人机协作中生理计算的先前工作强调了心脏活动、皮肤电导、呼吸和大脑活动等信号作为工作负载、压力、情感和安全的线索Physiological sensing has been studied as a way to support human-robot interaction and collaboration by estimating human state beyond externally visible behavior. Prior work on physiological computing in human-robot collaboration has highlighted signals such as heart activity, skin conductance, respiration, and brain activity as cues for workload, stress, affect, and safety[7]。 非接触式脉搏测量也在移动服务机器人上进行了演示，显示了基于摄像头的心率传感与机器人平台的相关性. Non-contact pulse measurement has also been demonstrated on a mobile service robot, showing the relevance of camera-based heart-rate sensing for robotic platforms[8].

早期的 rPPG 研究首先证明，可以在环境光下从普通视频远程测量与脉冲相关的颜色变化Early rPPG studies first demonstrated that pulse-related color variations can be measured remotely from ordinary videos under ambient light[9]。 随后的手工方法通过盲源分离来分离 RGB 迹线，从而改进了脉冲提取. Subsequent handcrafted methods improved pulse extraction by separating RGB traces with blind source separation[5]或者通过在色度和皮肤反射空间中投影颜色痕迹，如 CHROM 和 POSor by projecting color traces in chrominance and skin-reflection spaces, as in CHROM and POS[2, 12]。 这些方法建立了 rPPG 的光学和信号处理基础，但它们对颜色投影和皮肤反射假设的依赖使照明变化成为一个挑战。 王 (2020). These methods established the optical and signal-processing basis of rPPG, but their reliance on color-projection and skin-reflection assumptions makes illumination variation a challenge. Wang (2020)[10]建立了我们实验室使用的数据集协议，包括受控的照明水平、静态和动态任务以及同步的 ECG/PPG 参考。 该协议为研究受控采集条件下的跨视频照明变化提供了实验基础。established the dataset protocol used in our lab with controlled illumination levels, static and dynamic tasks, and synchronized ECG/PPG references. This protocol provides the experimental basis for studying cross-video illumination variation under controlled acquisition conditions.

最近的深度 rPPG 方法越来越多地直接从视频中学习时空表示。 PhysFormer 引入了时差多头自注意力 (TD-MHSA) 和时空前馈 (ST-FF) 模块，用于基于面部视频的生理测量，使其成为微妙的脉冲相关时空变化的合适预测主干Recent deep rPPG methods increasingly learn spatiotemporal representations directly from video. PhysFormer introduced temporal-difference multi-head self-attention (TD-MHSA) and spatio-temporal feed-forward (ST-FF) blocks for facial video-based physiological measurement, making it a suitable predictor backbone for subtle pulse-related spatio-temporal variations[16]。 以照明为重点的评估进一步表明，照明变化会强烈影响传统和深度 rPPG 方法. Illumination-focused assessments further show that lighting variation can strongly affect both traditional and deep rPPG methods[14]。 混合时频监督在 AutoHR、LSTC-rPPG、最近强大的 rPPG 框架和 FreqPhys 中也变得很常见. Hybrid temporal-frequency supervision has also become common in AutoHR, LSTC-rPPG, recent robust rPPG frameworks, and FreqPhys[15, 4, 1, 6]。 这些研究激发了本工作中使用的时频混合损失，但它们并没有直接确定受控照明变化下的最佳时频平衡。. These studies motivate the temporal-frequency hybrid loss used in this work, but they do not directly determine the best temporal-frequency balance under controlled illumination variation.

面部对齐是另一个重要因素，因为不稳定的面部区域直接影响恢复的 rPPG 信号的质量。 PRNet 通过 UV 位置图回归估计密集的 3D 面部几何形状Face alignment is another important factor because unstable facial regions directly affect the quality of the recovered rPPG signal. PRNet estimates dense 3D face geometry through UV position map regression[3]。 王等人。. Wang et al.[13]在动态 rPPG 条件下采用基于 PRNet 的 3D 对齐后，报告了更好的 HR 估计性能。 因此，本文使用 PRNet 作为预处理主干，并将主要贡献集中在光照感知训练和损失设计上。reported better HR-estimation performance after adopting PRNet-based 3D alignment in dynamic rPPG conditions. Therefore, this paper uses PRNet as the preprocessing backbone and focuses the main contribution on illumination-aware training and loss design.

受到稳健 rPPG 时间归一化的启发Inspired by temporal normalization for robust rPPG[11]，我们设计了残差时间标准化模块。 跨视频照明变化可能会引起中间时间特征统计的分布变化，这可能会掩盖弱脉冲相关的模式。 RTSM 使时间标准化成为残差校正，而不是原始特征流的替代，从而使我们的估计器能够抑制与照明相关的时间偏差，同时保留微妙的生理模式。, we design the Residual Temporal Standardization Module. Cross-video illumination changes can induce distribution shifts in intermediate temporal feature statistics, which may obscure weak pulse-related patterns. RTSM makes temporal standardization a residual correction rather than a replacement of the original feature stream, allowing our estimator to suppress illumination-related temporal bias while preserving subtle physiological patterns.

5 讨论Discussion

静态全级混合结果表明，照明鲁棒性取决于数据侧和目标侧设计。 PRNet 预处理提供稳定对齐的面部输入，这有助于减少学习前的空间不一致。 剪辑级照明增强扩展了表观亮度和对比度分布，同时保持时间连续性。 然后，RTSM 遵循时间归一化的鲁棒性动机，在我们的估计器内稳定时间特征统计数据The static all-level mix results show that illumination robustness depends on both data-side and objective-side design. PRNet preprocessing provides stable aligned facial inputs, which helps reduce spatial inconsistency before learning. Clip-level illumination augmentation expands the apparent brightness and contrast distribution while preserving temporal continuity. RTSM then stabilizes temporal feature statistics inside our estimator, following the robustness motivation of temporal normalization[11]同时使结构适应当前的端到端框架。while adapting the structure to the current end-to-end framework.

Prior work has shown that temporal waveform constraints and spectral HR guidance are complementary, but the balance under controlled illumination variation has not been examined. 这项研究尚未评估移动机器人平台、在线适应或闭环 HRI 行为。 相反，当前的静态全级混合协议将照明变化隔离为面向机器人的生理传感的受控第一步。 未来的工作将研究照明、运动和生理状态变化如何在说话、头部旋转、自行车运动和机器人安装相机条件下相互作用。This study does not yet evaluate moving robot platforms, online adaptation, or closed-loop HRI behavior. Instead, the current static all-level mix protocol isolates illumination variation as a controlled first step toward robot-facing physiological sensing. Future work will study how illumination, motion, and physiological-state changes interact in speaking, head-rotation, bicycle-exercise, and robot-mounted-camera conditions.

6 结论Conclusion

本文演示了一种在受控照明变化下通过基于 rPPG 的心率估计来实现基于摄像头的生理感知的估计器。 我们的估计器集成了基于 PRNet 的 3D 人脸对齐、剪辑级照明增强、RTSM 和混合时频监督。 在我们的数据集上，静态所有级别混合实验表明This paper demonstrates an estimator for camera-based physiological perception through rPPG-based heart-rate estimation under controlled illumination variation. Our estimator integrates PRNet-based 3D face alignment, clip-level illumination augmentation, RTSM, and hybrid temporal-frequency supervision. On our dataset, the static all-level mix experiment shows thatβ=5\beta=5给出了测试权重中最强的性能，达到 0.79 bpm HR MAE、2.40 bpm HR RMSE 和 0.982 HR 相关性。 与相同协议的 PhysFormer 基线相比，我们的估计器将 HR MAE 降低了 93.6%，HR RMSE 降低了 84.5%。 这些结果支持了将目标级混合监督、特征级残差时间标准化和数据侧照明增强相结合以实现稳健的 rPPG 学习的价值。 这些发现支持照明鲁棒性 rPPG 作为未来面向人类的机器人系统中不引人注目的生理感知的有前途的组件。gives the strongest performance among the tested weights, achieving 0.79 bpm HR MAE, 2.40 bpm HR RMSE, and 0.982 HR correlation. Compared with the PhysFormer baseline on the same protocol, our estimator reduces HR MAE by 93.6% and HR RMSE by 84.5%. These results support the value of combining objective-level hybrid supervision, feature-level residual temporal standardization, and data-side illumination augmentation for robust rPPG learning. These findings support illumination-robust rPPG as a promising component for unobtrusive physiological perception in future human-facing robotic systems.
