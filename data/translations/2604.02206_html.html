摘要Abstract

动态物体的准确形状和轨迹估计对于可靠的自动驾驶至关重要。经典贝叶斯扩展对象模型提供了理论上的稳健性和效率，但依赖于先验和更新似然函数的完整性，而深度学习方法以密集注释和高计算为代价带来了适应性。我们通过 LEO（对象的学习扩展）来桥接这些优势，LEO 是一种时空图注意力网络，它融合多模式生产级传感器轨迹来学习自适应融合权重，确保时间一致性并表示多尺度形状。使用特定于任务的平行四边形地面实况公式，LEO 可以对复杂的几何形状（例如铰接式卡车和拖车）进行建模，并概括传感器类型、配置、对象类别和区域，从而对具有挑战性的远程目标保持鲁棒性。对 Mercedes-Benz DRIVE PILOT SAE L3 数据集的评估证明了适合生产系统的实时计算效率；对公共数据集（例如 View of Delft (VoD)）的额外验证进一步证实了跨数据集泛化。Accurate shape and trajectory estimation of dynamic objects is essential for reliable automated driving. Classical Bayesian extended-object models offer theoretical robustness and efficiency but depend on completeness of a-priori and update-likelihood functions, while deep learning methods bring adaptability at the cost of dense annotations and high compute. We bridge these strengths with LEO (Learned Extension of Objects), a spatio-temporal Graph Attention Network that fuses multi-modal production-grade sensor tracks to learn adaptive fusion weights, ensure temporal consistency, and represent multi-scale shapes. Using a task-specific parallelogram ground-truth formulation, LEO models complex geometries (e.g. articulated trucks and trailers) and generalizes across sensor types, configurations, object classes, and regions, remaining robust for challenging and long-range targets. Evaluations on the Mercedes‑Benz DRIVE PILOT SAE L3 dataset demonstrate real-time computational efficiency suitable for production systems; additional validation on public datasets such as View of Delft (VoD) further confirms cross-dataset generalization.

VI 结论和未来的工作Conclusion And Future Work

这部作品呈现了This work presented低地轨道LEO，一种基于 GAT 的时空框架，用于跟踪级传感器输入的扩展对象跟踪中的自适应形状估计。 LEO 利用基于平行四边形的地面实况，对矩形和铰接式目标几何形状进行建模，同时其双重注意力机制能够对时间动态和空间依赖性进行联合推理，以实现鲁棒的多传感器融合。对大规模现实世界数据集的广泛评估证实，LEO 在不同场景中提供准确、稳定且计算高效的形状表示，验证了其部署的适用性。未来的工作将探索嵌入式平台的不确定性感知估计、领域适应、持续学习和轻量级变体，以及与规划模块的集成，以量化形状感知感知对自动驾驶安全性和效率的影响。, a spatio-temporal GAT-based framework for adaptive shape estimation in extended object tracking on track-level sensor inputs. Future work will explore uncertainty-aware estimation, domain adaptation, continual learning, and lightweight variants for embedded platforms, as well as integration with planning modules to quantify the impact of shape-aware perception on automated driving safety and efficiency.
