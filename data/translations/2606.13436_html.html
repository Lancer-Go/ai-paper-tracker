摘要Abstract

机器学习中的评估通常被视为中立的测量过程。然而，在操作信息系统中，评估结果通常取决于用于生成标签的过程。本文并不旨在提高分类性能。相反，它检查不同标签权威制度下绩效衡量的有效性。这个问题在大规模元数据驱动的系统中尤其明显，其中标签不完整、不一致或监督薄弱。Evaluation in machine learning is typically treated as a neutral measurement process. However, in operational information systems, evaluation outcomes are often conditioned by the processes used to generate labels. This paper does not aim to improve classification performance. Instead, it examines the validity of performance measurement under differing label authority regimes. This issue is particularly pronounced in large-scale metadata-driven systems, where labels are incomplete, inconsistent, or weakly supervised.

我们引入了评估主权，定义为绩效指标独立于标签权威和监督制度的程度，并提出了一个多轨评估框架，系统地改变培训和评估标签来源。使用大规模科学元数据的分层多标签分类，我们表明，在操作（“银”）评估下表现出强大性能的模型在独立（“金”）评估下大幅下降，特别是对于细粒度分类（例如，Micro-F1 从 ~0.54 降至 ~0.03）。值得注意的是，基于排名的指标仍然很重要，揭示了潜在模型信号和分类有效性之间的差异。We introduce evaluation sovereignty, defined as the degree to which performance metrics are independent of label authority and supervision regime, and propose a multitrack evaluation framework that systematically varies training and evaluation label sources. Using hierarchical multi-label classification on large-scale scientific metadata, we show that models exhibiting strong performance under operational (“silver”) evaluation degrade substantially under independent (“gold”) evaluation, particularly for fine-grained classification (e.g., Micro-F1 decreases from ~0.54 to ~0.03). Notably, ranking-based metrics remain non-trivial, revealing a divergence between latent model signal and classification validity.

这些发现表明，常见报告的指标可能反映了与标签流程的一致性，而不是真正的预测能力。因此，我们将评估有效性重新定义为由标签治理形成的系统级属性，并为弱监督下的智能系统审计提供了实用的方法。These findings demonstrate that commonly reported metrics may reflect alignment with labeling processes rather than true predictive capability. We therefore reframe evaluation validity as a system-level property shaped by label governance and provide a practical methodology for auditing intelligent systems under weak supervision.

In these settings, evaluation metrics guide system selection and deployment. Misalignment between evaluation and true predictive validity therefore represents a system-level risk, not merely a modeling issue.

评估时的假设与机器学习系统运行环境的现实之间存在重大脱节。训练和评估数据通常来自同一个地方，即操作系统，因此由于与训练和评估期间使用的标记过程保持一致，模型有很大的潜力表现良好。这创建了一种循环评估形式，即根据与训练期间使用的假设类似的假设来测试模型，这种现象与标签噪声和弱监督效应有关（Frénay & Verleysen，2014）。通过通用指标衡量的高水平性能不一定转化为可靠的预测或广义的预测。相反，它们倾向于反映模型输出与可能存在缺陷或不完整的监督输入的一致性。There is a major disconnect between what is assumed at the time of evaluation versus the realities of the environment in which ML systems operate. The training and evaluation data are typically sourced from the same place, i.e. the operational system, so there is a great deal of potential for models to appear to perform well due to alignment with the labeling process used during training and evaluation. This creates a form of circular evaluation, whereby models are tested against assumptions similar to those used during training, a phenomenon related to label noise and weak supervision effects (Frénay & Verleysen, 2014). High levels of performance measured by common metrics do not necessarily translate into reliable predictions or generalized prediction. Rather, they tend to reflect the agreement of the model's output with potentially deficient or incomplete supervisory input.

我们将评估结果对现行标签制度的依赖称为评估主权。虽然此处在分层多标签分类中进行了演示，但评估主权广泛适用于任何训练和评估标签源自依赖或部分对齐流程的系统，包括弱监督、人工注释管道和法学硕士辅助标签。评估主权代表模型的性能指标独立于管理其标签的制度的程度。实际上，它描述了评估结果如何受到用于评估模型的标签制度的权威、组织结构和独立性的影响。低水平的评估主权导致模型通过由负责创建模型训练数据的类似流程生成的标签进行评估；因此，产生潜在的循环和人为提高的评估。另一方面，高水平的评估主权意味着评估将使用独立验证的标签或来自上级机构的标签；从而对模型能力进行更现实的衡量。We call this dependency of evaluation outcome on the prevailing label regime, evaluation sovereignty. While demonstrated here in hierarchical multi-label classification, evaluation sovereignty applies broadly to any system in which training and evaluation labels are derived from dependent or partially aligned processes, including weak supervision, human annotation pipelines, and LLM-assisted labeling. Evaluation sovereignty represents the extent to which a model's performance metric(s) are independent of the regime governing its labels. Practically speaking, it describes how the outcomes of evaluation are influenced by the authority, organizational structure, and independence of the labeling regime(s) utilized for assessing a model. Low levels of evaluation sovereignty lead to models being assessed via labels produced from similar processes that were responsible for creating the model's training data; thus, producing potential circularity and artificially elevated assessments. On the other hand, high levels of evaluation sovereignty imply that assessment will utilize labels either independently verified or from higher authorities; thereby, resulting in a more realistic measure of model capacity.

它在严重依赖分类系统和大规模元数据（例如科学信息数据库和生物医学索引系统）的领域尤其相关，因为分类输出促进搜索、发现和基于主题的路由，而主题标签充当训练数据以及访问/检索数据的主要机制。因此，依赖于训练期间使用的操作标记流程的特定于应用程序的评估实践会带来巩固元数据工作流程可变性的风险。因此，报告的性能可能反映与索引实践的一致性，而不是分类系统准确捕获文档内容的能力。这就提出了一个基本问题：当标签是动态变化的治理过程的产物而不是事实的固定表示时，如何评估分类系统？It is particularly relevant in fields relying heavily upon classification systems and large-scale metadata (e.g. scientific information databases and biomedical indexing systems), since classification outputs facilitate search, discovery, and subject-based routing, and subject labels function as training data as well as the primary mechanisms for accessing/retrieving data. Therefore, application-specific evaluation practices reliant upon operational labeling processes used during training pose risks of entrenching metadata workflow variability. Consequently, reported performance may reflect alignment with indexing practices rather than the ability of the classification system to accurately capture document content. This raises a foundational question: How can one evaluate classification systems when labels are products of dynamically changing governance processes rather than fixed representations of truth?

Even highly precise data can produce incorrect conclusions when interpreted within flawed frameworks. 操作分类系统中也出现了类似的问题，其中评估依赖于与训练相同的过程生成的标签。评估主权的概念通过强调评估与训练标签源的独立性来形式化这种区别，从而能够评估观察到的性能是否反映了基础模型能力或数据生成过程的工件。An analogous issue arises in operational classification systems, where evaluation relies on labels generated by the same processes used for training. The concept of evaluation sovereignty formalizes this distinction by emphasizing the independence of evaluation from training label sources, enabling assessment of whether observed performance reflects underlying model capability or artifacts of the data-generating process.

标签通常被认为是权威的，无论它们是否代表了可变的人类判断、不断变化的标准或操作限制。先前对元数据驱动系统（包括 OSTI 等大型存储库）的观察表明，经常使用从相同操作管道开发的标签来训练和评估模型，从而人为地提高性能指标。Labels are typically accepted as authoritative regardless of whether they represent variable human judgments, evolving standards or operational limitations. Prior observations in metadata-driven systems, including large-scale repositories such as OSTI, suggest that models are frequently trained and evaluated utilizing labels developed from identical operational pipelines leading to artificially elevated performance metrics.

为了帮助弥合这一现有差距，我们提出了一个多轨评估框架，旨在通过明确区分监管制度和标签机构来实施评估主权。我们提出的框架将区分操作衍生的（“银”）标签和独立验证的（“金”）标签，同时定义各种评估条件，消除或减少由于不同的监管质量而导致的潜在循环的影响。通过我们提出的框架，我们可以明确衡量标签权威之间的关系如何影响评估结果。To help bridge this existing gap we propose a multi-track evaluation framework designed to operationalize evaluation sovereignty by explicitly distinguishing among supervision regimes and label authorities. Our proposed framework will distinguish between operationally-derived ("silver") labels and independently verified ("gold") labels while defining various evaluation conditions that remove or reduce the influence of potential circularity due to differing supervision qualities. Through our proposed framework, we enable explicit measurement of how the relationship between label authority influences evaluation outcomes.

使用来自科学元数据和生物医学领域的大规模数据集，我们应用多轨框架来评估传统机器学习模型和基于 Transformer 的模型。结果表明，模型性能会随着标签权威的变化而发生显着变化。例如，在使用操作标签的条件下表现良好的模型在使用独立验证的标签进行评估时始终会大幅下降。此外，即使分类精度下降，表明模型的固有功能与观察到的评估结果之间存在脱节，基于排名的指标也可以保留信号。Using large-scale datasets from both scientific metadata and biomedical domains we apply our multi-track framework to evaluate both traditional machine learning models and transformer-based models. Results show that model performance can significantly change as a function of label authority. For example, models performing highly under conditions employing operational labels consistently degrade substantially when evaluated using independently verified labels. Moreover, rank-based metrics can retain signal even after classification accuracy has declined indicating a disconnect between the inherent capabilities of a model and observed evaluation outcomes.

我们的结果表明，现实世界系统中评估的验证不能被视为模型本身的固有属性。相反，它需要被视为依赖于模型、数据和治理流程之间的交互的系统属性。如果不考虑评估中的标签权威，通常报告的指标可能会对系统可靠性产生误导性的表述。Our results illustrate that validation of evaluations in real-world systems cannot be viewed as an intrinsic property of models themselves. Instead, it needs to be recognized as a systemic attribute dependent on interactions between models, data, and governance processes. Without consideration of label authority in assessments, commonly reported metrics may represent misleading representations of system reliability.

因此，本文的三个主要贡献是：（i）我们将评估主权正式化为评估独立性的属性，（ii）我们凭经验证明，绩效指标受到标签权威的强烈制约，并且可能无法可靠地反映弱监督下的模型能力，以及（iii）我们提供了一个可推广的审计框架，用于评估弱监督下的智能分类系统。本研究中使用的模型不是主要贡献。它们作为受控工具来分析评估行为在不同监管制度下如何变化。这对智能系统中的模型选择、基准测试有效性和部署决策有直接影响。Therefore, the three main contributions of this paper are: (i) We formalize evaluation sovereignty as a property of evaluation independence, (ii) We show empirically that performance metrics are strongly conditioned by label authority and may not reliably reflect model capability under weak supervision, and (iii) We provide a generalizable audit framework for evaluating intelligent classification systems under weak supervision. The models used in this study are not the primary contribution. They serve as controlled instruments to analyze how evaluation behavior changes under different supervision regimes. This has direct implications for model selection, benchmarking validity, and deployment decisions in intelligent systems.

2.1 信息系统中元数据驱动的分类2.1 Metadata-Driven Classification in Information Systems

信息系统，特别是基于大规模科技档案的信息系统，严重依赖元数据来提供搜索功能、帮助用户发现材料并执行管理工作流操作。美国能源部 OSTI（科学技术信息办公室）就是此类操作的一个例子。他们创建和维护大量档案材料，并根据分配的主题标签对每个项目进行分类和路由。随着 OSTI 中存储的项目数量持续增长，手动分配主题标签对于继续对档案进行手动分类变得不切实际，因此，自动化机器学习分类已成为自动化分类的潜在解决方案。此外，基于文本的自动文档分类在信息科学文献中得到了广泛的研究（Sebastiani，2002）。Information systems, especially those based on large-scale scientific and technological archives, rely heavily on metadata to provide search functions, help users discover materials, and perform administrative workflow operations. OSTI (Office of Scientific and Technical Information), U.S. Department of Energy, is an example of such an operation. They create and maintain large amounts of archival material and they classify and route each item based on assigned subject labels. As the number of items stored in OSTI continues to grow, assigning subject labels manually is becoming impractical to continue with manual classification of the archive and thus, automated machine learning classification has become a potential solution for automating classification. In addition, textbased automatic document classification has been extensively researched in information science literature (Sebastiani, 2002).

大多数系统的分类模型必须依赖于容易获得的描述性元数据元素，例如标题和摘要。尽管使用有限数量的元数据元素可以实现可扩展的实现，但它提出了几个问题，包括有关分类文档的上下文信息的限制以及作者描述内容的可变性。当使用多标签层次分类时，这些问题最为明显，其中文档可以分为多个抽象级别，例如大类别和细粒度子类别。该问题的许多解决方案包括多标签分类框架（Tsoumakas & Katakis，2008；Silla & Freitas，2011）。The majority of systems have classification models that have to rely upon readily available descriptive metadata elements such as title and abstract. Although using the limited amount of metadata elements allows for a scalable implementation, it presents several issues including the limitation of contextual information about the classified document and the variability in which the authors describe the contents. These problems are most apparent when using multi-label hierarchical classification where a document can be categorized into multiple levels of abstraction such as broad categories and fine grain sub-categories. Many solutions to this problem include multi-label classification frameworks (Tsoumakas & Katakis, 2008; Silla & Freitas, 2011).

2.2 监管薄弱和标签噪音2.2 Weak Supervision and Label Noise

在所有现实世界的分类系统中发现的一个常见问题是使用不完美标记的分类。在大多数情况下，操作标签是由执行索引过程的索引器、启发式规则或创建标签的半自动工具创建的。由于这些标签通常是自动生成的，因此它们可能会包含错误、丢失标签或其他形式的标签噪声。先前的研究表明，不完美的标签会对模型的训练和测试方式产生负面影响，并最终降低模型性能或产生不正确的结果（Frénay & Verleysen，2014）。One common issue found in all real world classification systems is the use of imperfectly labeled classifications. In most cases, operational labels are created by either indexers performing indexing processes, heuristics rules or semi-automatic tools creating labels. Since these labels are typically produced automatically they will likely include errors, missing labels or other forms of label noise. Previous studies have demonstrated that imperfect labels can negatively affect the way models are trained and tested and ultimately decrease model performance or produce incorrect results (Frénay & Verleysen, 2014).

弱监督学习方法和噪声容忍学习方法（Frénay & Verleysen，2014）的开发是为了提高使用噪声标签训练的模型的稳定性。之前的大多数研究都侧重于提供一种提高模型鲁棒性的方法，而不是研究噪声标签如何影响我们对测试模型后提供的评估结果的信心。因此，尽管我们知道模型是在嘈杂的标签条件下训练的，但我们仍然无法确认我们报告的性能指标是否有效。Weak supervised learning methods and noise tolerant learning methods (Frénay & Verleysen, 2014) were developed to improve the stability of models being trained with noisy labels. Most previous studies focused on providing a method to increase model robustness instead of studying how noisy labels affect our confidence in the evaluation results provided after testing the model. Therefore, although we know a model was trained under noisy label conditions we still cannot confirm whether the performance metrics we report are valid.

Despite increasing interests in developing data centric methodologies, little emphasis has been placed on understanding how different label authorities influence evaluation outcomes.

2.4 评估、标签权威性和基准有效性2.4 Evaluation, Label Authority, and Benchmark Validity

机器学习中的评估通常被视为客观、中立的过程；然而，越来越多的研究挑战了这一假设。最近的研究表明，评估结果对基准的设计、基础数据的质量以及用于生成和验证标签的协议高度敏感。特别是，基于基准的评估因产生的结果不能推广到现实世界条件而受到批评，导致所谓的“幻影进步”，即基准数据集的改进不一定对应于现实世界性能或实际效用的改进（Koohestani 等人，2026 年；Alampara 等人，2025 年）。对广泛使用的基准的分析发现了一些问题，包括基准饱和、数据污染、有缺陷的测试用例和维护不足，所有这些都可能夸大性能估计并掩盖底层模型的局限性。事实证明，纠正此类缺陷可以显着改变报告的模型性能，这表明基准质量本身是评估结果的关键决定因素，而不是中立的测量工具（Koohestani 等人，2026）。Evaluation in machine learning is often treated as an objective and neutral process; however, a growing body of research challenges this assumption. Recent studies demonstrate that evaluation outcomes are highly sensitive to the design of benchmarks, the quality of underlying data, and the protocols used to generate and validate labels. In particular, benchmark-based evaluation has been criticized for producing results that do not generalize to real-world conditions, leading to what has been described as “phantom progress,” where improvements on benchmark datasets do not necessarily correspond to improvements in real-world performance or practical utility (Koohestani et al., 2026; Alampara et al., 2025). Analyses of widely used benchmarks have identified issues including benchmark saturation, data contamination, flawed test cases, and inadequate maintenance, all of which can inflate performance estimates and obscure underlying model limitations. Correcting such deficiencies has been shown to substantially alter reported model performance, suggesting that benchmark quality itself is a critical determinant of evaluation outcomes rather than a neutral measurement instrument (Koohestani et al., 2026).

这种批评的一个中心问题是基准条件与实际部署环境之间的不匹配。许多基准依赖于静态数据集、简化的任务公式和狭窄的评估指标，这些指标无法捕获操作系统中存在的复杂性、可变性和上下文依赖性（McIntosh 等人，2026 年；Ashqar，2025 年）。此外，基准测试可能会无意中鼓励对基准性能的优化，而不是真正的推理、稳健的泛化或现实世界的适用性，从而导致对模型有效性的评估夸大（McIntosh 等人，2026 年；Orr & Kang，2024 年）。数据污染进一步加剧了这些担忧，其中训练和评估数据之间的重叠可能会人为地夸大性能指标并掩盖真实的模型行为，从而削弱基于基准的评估的有效性（McIntosh 等人，2026 年；Koohestani 等人，2026 年）。A central issue in this critique is the mismatch between benchmark conditions and real-world deployment environments. Many benchmarks rely on static datasets, simplified task formulations, and narrow evaluation metrics that fail to capture the complexity, variability, and contextual dependencies present in operational systems (McIntosh et al., 2026; Ashqar, 2025). Furthermore, benchmarks may inadvertently encourage optimization for benchmark performance rather than genuine reasoning, robust generalization, or realworld applicability, leading to inflated assessments of model effectiveness (McIntosh et al., 2026; Orr & Kang, 2024). These concerns are further compounded by data contamination, where overlap between training and evaluation data can artificially inflate performance metrics and obscure true model behavior, thereby weakening the validity of benchmarkbased assessments (McIntosh et al., 2026; Koohestani et al., 2026).

与基准限制密切相关的是标签质量和数据集构建在形成评估结果中的作用。对标签噪声和数据质量的研究表明，噪声、不一致或弱监督标签会显着降低模型性能、降低泛化性并改变模型行为（Gharawi 等人，2022）。评估数据集中的标签错误尤其成问题，因为它们直接损害绩效评估的有效性，影响模型选择，并且可能Closely related to benchmark limitations is the role of label quality and dataset construction in shaping evaluation outcomes. Research on label noise and data quality has shown that noisy, inconsistent, or weakly supervised labels can significantly degrade model performance, reduce generalization, and alter model behavior (Gharawi et al., 2022). Labeling errors in evaluation datasets are particularly problematic because they directly compromise the validity of performance assessment, influence model selection, and can

导致关于模型能力的错误结论（Goswami 等人，2023）。在大型信息系统中，标签通常是通过操作流程、索引工作流程或启发式程序生成的，标签实践的可变性会给评估带来额外的不确定性，从而加强了性能指标对底层标签生成流程的依赖，而不仅仅是对模型能力的依赖。lead to incorrect conclusions about model capability (Goswami et al., 2023). In large-scale information systems, where labels are often generated through operational processes, indexing workflows, or heuristic procedures, variability in labeling practices introduces additional uncertainty into evaluation, reinforcing the dependence of performance metrics on the underlying label-generation process rather than solely on model capability.

在弱监督学习环境中，这一挑战会进一步放大，其中真实标签可能不完整、有噪音或完全不可用。最近的工作将弱监督下的评估框架视为部分识别问题，其中如果没有对弱标签和真实标签之间关系的强有力假设，就无法唯一确定准确度、精确度、召回率和 F1 分数等标准性能指标（Polo 等人，2024）。基于概率性能界限和部分识别框架的替代方法强调了评估在弱监督下训练的模型的固有不确定性，这表明报告的指标可能反映监督制度和标签生成过程的属性，而不是内在模型性能（Polo 等人，2024）。This challenge is further amplified in weakly supervised learning settings, where ground truth labels may be incomplete, noisy, or entirely unavailable. Recent work has framed evaluation under weak supervision as a partial identification problem, in which standard performance metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score cannot be uniquely determined without strong assumptions about the relationship between weak and true labels (Polo et al., 2024). Alternative approaches based on probabilistic performance bounds and partial identification frameworks highlight the inherent uncertainty in evaluating models trained under weak supervision, suggesting that reported metrics may reflect properties of the supervision regime and label-generation process rather than intrinsic model performance (Polo et al., 2024).

除了数据集构建之外，评估可靠性还受到训练和部署环境之间的分布变化的影响。当数据的统计属性随时间或跨上下文而变化时，模型通常会表现出显着的性能下降，并且在传统评估设置下测量的性能可能无法准确预测现实部署条件下的模型行为（Koh 等人，2021；Yao 等人，2022）。独立同分布 (i.i.d.) 假设的这种分解强调了传统评估框架的局限性，并强调需要考虑动态、现实世界条件和不断变化的数据分布的评估方法。Beyond dataset construction, evaluation reliability is also affected by distribution shift between training and deployment environments. When the statistical properties of data change over time or across contexts, models often exhibit significant degradation in performance, and performance measured under conventional evaluation settings may fail to accurately predict model behavior under real-world deployment conditions (Koh et al., 2021; Yao et al., 2022). This breakdown of the independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) assumption underscores the limitations of conventional evaluation frameworks and highlights the need for evaluation approaches that account for dynamic, real-world conditions and evolving data distributions.

在多标签和分层分类系统中，评估指标本身的选择会带来额外的复杂性。基于排名的指标（例如平均精度）捕获预测标签的相对顺序，而基于分类的指标（例如 Micro-F1 和汉明损失）评估二进制正确性。先前的工作表明，基于排名和基于分类的指标可以对多标签分类中的模型性能产生显着不同的评估，而竞争方法的相对性能在很大程度上取决于所选的评估指标（García-Pedrajas 等人，2024）。这种分歧在弱监督环境中尤其重要，在这种环境中，模型可能保留有意义的排名信号，但无法产生准确的离散预测。In multi-label and hierarchical classification systems, additional complexity arises from the choice of evaluation metrics themselves. Ranking-based metrics, such as average precision, capture the relative ordering of predicted labels, while classification-based metrics, such as Micro-F1 and Hamming loss, assess binary correctness. Prior work demonstrates that ranking-based and classification-based metrics can produce substantially different assessments of model performance in multilabel classification, with the relative performance of competing methods depending strongly on the selected evaluation metric (García-Pedrajas et al., 2024). This divergence is especially relevant in weakly supervised environments, where models may retain meaningful ranking signal while failing to produce accurate discrete predictions.

最后，这些限制促使人们做出更广泛的努力，从可信度、问责制和系统级可靠性方面重新概念化评估。当代框架强调，评估必须超越基于准确性的指标，纳入可靠性、稳健性、透明度、公平性、问责制、人工监督以及与组织治理流程的一致性等维度（Siavvas 等，2026；Seralidou 等，2025）。在这种观点中，评估不仅仅是模型的属性，而且是模型、数据、人类参与者和模型之间的相互作用的结果。Finally, these limitations have motivated broader efforts to reconceptualize evaluation in terms of trustworthiness, accountability, and system-level reliability. Contemporary frameworks emphasize that evaluation must extend beyond accuracy-based metrics to incorporate dimensions such as reliability, robustness, transparency, fairness, accountability, human oversight, and alignment with organizational governance processes (Siavvas et al., 2026; Seralidou et al., 2025). In this view, evaluation is not solely a property of the model, but emerges from the interaction among models, data, human actors, and

塑造人工智能系统设计、部署、监控和解释方式的治理结构。governance structures that shape how AI systems are designed, deployed, monitored, and interpreted.

总而言之，这项工作表明评估结果并不是模型能力的内在衡量标准，而是受到基准设计、标签质量、监督制度和部署环境的制约。这种观点激发了对明确考虑评估对标签权威和数据治理的依赖性的框架的需求，为更可靠和可解释的智能系统评估提供基础。Taken together, this body of work suggests that evaluation outcomes are not intrinsic measures of model capability, but are conditioned by benchmark design, label quality, supervision regime, and deployment context. This perspective motivates the need for frameworks that explicitly account for the dependence of evaluation on label authority and data governance, providing a foundation for more reliable and interpretable assessment of intelligent systems.

2.5 差距：不同标签权威下的评估2.5 Gap: Evaluation Under Varying Label Authority

尽管以前的工作探讨了标签噪声、数据质量和模型性能，但不存在系统的方法来分析不同级别的标签权威如何影响模型评估。特别是，当前的方法都没有明确区分用于训练模型的操作标签和用于评估模型的独立验证标签。Although previous works explored label noise, data quality, and model performance, no systematic methodologies exist for analyzing how differing levels of label authority affect model evaluations. In particular, none of the current methodologies clearly differentiate between operational labels used for training models and independent verification labels used for evaluating models.

鉴于标签既是信息系统中的操作工件又是评估目标（Frénay & Verleysen，2014），这一差距为开发区分操作标签和评估标签的明确方法提供了重要动力。此外，如果不考虑不同程度的标签权威，评估结果可能会歪曲模型性能并掩盖固有的局限性。此外，由于分层分类通常包括细粒度标签的长尾分布，因此评估指标对不完整标记过程的敏感性使得这种差距在分层分类场景中特别相关。Given that labels are both operational artifacts and evaluation targets in information systems (Frénay & Verleysen, 2014), this gap provides significant motivation for developing an explicit methodology for distinguishing between operational and evaluative labels. Furthermore, without considering varying degrees of label authority, evaluation results may misrepresent model performance and mask inherent limitations. Additionally, since hierarchical classification often includes a long tail distribution of fine grain labels, evaluation metric sensitivity to incomplete labeling processes make this gap particularly relevant in hierarchical classification scenarios.

虽然之前的工作广泛研究了标签噪声、弱监督和以数据为中心的方法来提高模型的稳健性，但这些工作主要将标签质量视为训练数据的属性，并侧重于减轻其对模型学习的影响。相比之下，这项工作解决了一个独特但相关的问题：不同标签权威下评估结果的有效性。具体来说，我们研究训练和评估标签源之间的关系如何影响模型性能的解释。通过明确分离监管制度并引入评估主权的概念，本研究将评估重新定义为以标签独立性和权威为条件的因变量，而不是模型能力的客观衡量。While prior work has extensively examined label noise, weak supervision, and datacentric approaches to improving model robustness, these efforts primarily treat label quality as a property of the training data and focus on mitigating its effects on model learning. In contrast, this work addresses a distinct but related problem: the validity of evaluation outcomes under varying label authorities. Specifically, we examine how the relationship between training and evaluation label sources influences the interpretation of model performance. By explicitly separating supervision regimes and introducing the concept of evaluation sovereignty, this study reframes evaluation as a dependent variable conditioned on label independence and authority, rather than as an objective measurement of model capability.

为了弥补这一差距，本研究开发了一种多轨评估方法，该方法分别考虑监管制度并相对于各自标签来源的权威级别评估模型。因此，它提出了一种实用方法，通过将标签治理视为评估模型可靠性的变量来识别现实世界信息系统评估中的偏差。与侧重于数据集设计的基准有效性研究不同，评估主权侧重于培训标签权威和评估标签权威之间的关系，作为观察到的绩效的决定因素。To bridge this gap, this study develops a multi-track evaluation methodology that separately considers supervisory regimes and evaluates models relative to the authority level of their respective label sources. Consequently, it advances a practical method for identifying biases in real-world information system evaluations by treating label governance as a variable to assess model reliability. Unlike benchmark validity studies that focus on dataset design, evaluation sovereignty focuses on the relationship between training-label authority and evaluation-label authority as a determinant of observed performance.

在大多数情况下，在机器学习分类系统的训练和评估期间使用真实标签假设这些标签始终可靠且准确地表示数据中的真实关系。然而，在In most cases, the use of ground-truth labels during training and evaluation of machine-learning classification systems assumes that these labels are consistently reliable and accurate representations of the true relationships within the data. Within this framework, we separately identify training and evaluation labels so that we can examine the influence of training label conditions on performance measured during the evaluation phase.

3.1 标签权威3.1 Label Authority

标签权威表示一组特定标签准确捕获数据中存在的实际关系的程度。在操作系统中，生成标签主要是为了提高操作效率，而不是为了确保一致的语义准确性。因此，这些标签在捕捉数据真实性质方面可能会存在差异。例如，通过使用不断发展的分类法、机构政策/实践和/或人类解释生成的标签并不总是充分捕获数据真实结构性质的所有方面。Label authority represents the extent to which a particular set of labels accurately captures the actual relationships present in the data. In operational systems, labels are produced primarily for operational efficiency purposes rather than to ensure consistent semantic accuracy. Therefore, there will likely be differences in terms of how well these labels capture the true nature of the data. For example, labels generated through the use of evolving taxonomies, institutional policies/practices, and/or human interpretation, do not always adequately capture all aspects of the true structural nature of the data.

根据我们对标签权威的概念化，我们将所有标签分为两类：Based upon our conceptualization of label authority, we categorize all labels into two types:

  • 银牌/操作标签Silver/Operational Labels：通过现有操作系统或工作流程创建的标签（例如元数据索引、自动标记、启发式分类），可以轻松大规模获得；然而，它们可能包含错误、不一致或噪音。: Labels created through existing operational systems or workflow processes (e.g., metadata indexing, automated tagging, heuristic-based classifications), which can be easily obtained at large-scales; however, they may include errors, inconsistencies, or noise.

  • 金牌/验证标签Gold/Verified Labels：通过独立的人工审查、专家注释或符合特定受控词汇进行验证的标签，提供更准确的地面事实近似值。然而，由于这些验证要求，它们的范围或规模通常受到限制。: Labels verified through independent manual reviews, expert annotations, or conformity to specific controlled vocabularies, providing higher accuracy approximations to ground truth. 上述区别使我们能够更清楚地了解评估结果如何根据所使用的标签提供的权威级别而变化。The distinctions outlined above allow us to gain a clearer picture of how evaluation results vary depending upon the level of authority provided by the labels utilized.

3.2 监督制度和评价主权3.2 Supervision Regimes and Evaluation Sovereignty

ML 模型可以在不同的监督机制下进行训练和评估，具体取决于训练和评估的标签是否来自单独的或相同的标签源。在许多操作环境中，用于培训和评估的标签是从操作标签源获取的。尽管易于实施，但这种方法存在根据训练数据中固有的完全相同的假设来验证模型的风险。我们在下面正式阐述了这个概念。ML models can be trained and evaluated under varying supervision regimes contingent upon whether the labels for training and evaluation were sourced from separate or identical label sources. In many operational contexts, the labels utilized for both training and evaluation are acquired from operational label sources. Although easy to implement, this approach risks validating models against the exact same assumptions inherent within their training data. We formalized this concept below.

评估主权是指用于培训的给定标签流程与评估期间使用的标签流程之间的独立程度。当使用拥有不同权威的不同来源（即存在独立性）开发培训和评估标签时，可以减少对反映标签过程属性的验证性能的依赖（相对于真实的预测能力）。相反，当训练和评估标签被标记时，评估主权就会降低。Evaluation sovereignty refers to the amount of independence between a given labeling process used for training and the labeling process employed during evaluation. When training and evaluation labels are developed using distinct sources possessing different authorities (i.e., independence exists), it is possible to reduce reliance upon validation performance reflecting properties of a labeling process (versus true predictive ability). Conversely, low evaluation sovereignty occurs when training and evaluation labels

源自相同的标签源或过程，从而创建循环验证，其中模型根据自己对训练数据的假设进行验证。与其他概念（例如主权）不同，评估主权并不作为二元属性存在。相反，它沿着连续的范围存在，取决于各个标签生成过程之间的独立性水平以及每个标签源包含的相对权限。引入了多轨框架，该框架提供了一种通过改变训练和评估标签源之间的关系来系统地探索该范围的方法（表 1）。originate from the same label sources or processes, thereby creating circular validation where models are validated against their own assumptions about training data. Unlike other concepts (such as sovereign rights), evaluation sovereignty does not exist as a binary attribute. Instead, it exists along a continuous spectrum dependent upon both the level of independence between respective label generating processes and the relative authority each label source contains. 框架影响Framework Implications

多轨评估设计将评估从基于单一指标的模型性能测量扩展到提供一种系统方法来评估现实条件下分类系统的可靠性。该框架并没有假设评估标签代表真正的权威，而是系统地测试了这一假设并量化了其效果。The multi-track evaluation design expands evaluation from a single metric-based measurement of model performance to provide a systematic methodology for assessing the reliability of classification systems under real-world conditions. Rather than assume that evaluation labels represent true authority, the framework systematically tests this assumption and quantifies its effects.

此外，通过提供各种监督制度，该框架允许从业者区分在操作约束下有效执行的模型和在更权威的评估条件下继续保持其性能的模型。在考虑信息系统中的模型行为时，理解这些区别至关重要，其中标签既是操作工件又是评估目标。Additionally, by providing various supervision regimes, the framework allows practitioners to differentiate between models that perform effectively within operational constraints and models that continue to maintain their performance when subjected to more authoritative evaluation conditions. Understanding these distinctions are critical when considering model behavior in information systems where labels are both operational artifacts and evaluation targets.

4.6 多轨实验设计4.6 Experimental Design Across Multiple Tracks

该实验的方法设计基于第 3 节中描述的多轨框架。对于每个模型，在以下条件下评估性能：This experiment’s methodological design is based on the multiple track framework described in Section 3. For each model, performance is evaluated under the following conditions:

  • F 轨道（基线运行）Track F (Baseline-operational)：使用源自 OSTI 数据集的操作（银）标签进行培训和评估。: Training and evaluation are conducted using operational (silver) labels derived from the OSTI dataset.

  • Track R（评估权威差距）Track R (Evaluation authority gap)：在此类别中，使用操作标签训练的模型将通过与独立的黄金标准进行比较来衡量其准确性。因此，我们可以衡量由标签权威引起的差异（即，当模型的数据使用“金”而不是“银”进行标记时，模型的表现会好多少）。: in this category, models that were trained using operational labels will have their accuracy measured by comparing them to an independent gold standard. Thus, we can measure the differences caused by label authority (i.e., how much better does a model perform when its data has been labeled using “gold” instead of “silver”).

  • 轨道 P（使用外部管理标签进行验证）Track P (Validation using externally governed labels)：在此类别中，将使用 PubMed 数据库中的精选标签数据集来训练和测试模型。我们使用这些结果作为在外部管理实体监督下衡量绩效的比较。: in this category, the models will be trained and tested using a dataset of curated labels found in the PubMed database. We use these results as a comparison for measuring performance while being supervised by external governing entities.

使用类似的数据库进行训练和测试，以及不同类别的相同模型和评估标准，使我们能够隔离标签权威对模型性能的影响。Using similar databases for both training and testing, along with identical models and evaluation criteria for the different categories allows us to isolate the effects of label authority on model performance.

5. 结果5. Results

我们在多轨框架的各个方面测量了模型性能，以便我们可以确定标签权威对观察结果的影响。我们在所有轨道级别上使用相同的指标和模型设置，并提供 1 级 (L1) 和 2 级 (L2) 标签空间的结果。We measured model performance under all aspects of the multi-track framework so we could determine what influence(s) label authority has upon observed outcomes. We used identical metrics and model settings across all track levels and provide results for both Level-1 (L1) and Level-2 (L2) label space.

5.1 操作基线下的模型性能（F 轨）5.1 Model Performance Under Operational Baseline (Track F)

使用训练集和测试集都包含“银”标签的操作基线（Track F），我们的模型在应用通用评估指标时在操作评估条件下表现出强大的性能。基于 Transformer 的模型在微平均 F1 和 AUPRC 方面显着优于传统模型。传统的 TF-IDF + Logistic 回归模型在 L1 标签空间中仍然具有竞争力，但由于细粒度标签的稀疏性和长尾分布更大，其性能在 L2 标签空间中大大降低。Using an operational baseline (Track F) where both the train set and test set contain "silver" labels, our models exhibited strong performance under operational evaluation conditions when applying common evaluation metrics. Transformer-based models significantly outperformed traditional models in terms of micro-averaged F1 and AUPRC. Traditional TF-IDF + Logistic Regression models remained competitive in L1 label space, however their performance was greatly diminished in L2 label space due to the much greater sparseness and long tail distribution of fine grained labels.

在 L2 中，应用 Top-N 排名约束（即前 100 名）有助于稳定评估并防止模型产生过多的不良置信度标签。这种通过排名评估模型的方法也得到了先前有关多标签分类中涉及阈值和排名的评估方法的研究的支持（Tsoumakas & Katakis，2008；Lipton 等人，2014）。In L2, the application of a Top-N ranking constraint (i.e., top 100) helped stabilize the evaluation and prevented the models from producing excessive numbers of poor confidence labels. This method of evaluating models via ranking is also supported by previous studies regarding evaluation methods that involve thresholding and ranking within multi-label classification (Tsoumakas & Katakis, 2008; Lipton et al., 2014).

重要的是，Top-N 约束的使用改变了 Level-2 分类指标的解释。在此设置下，模型根据其在有界候选集中恢复相关标签的能力进行评估，有效地将任务构建为受约束的排名问题，而不是不受约束的多标签分类。因此，Track F 中报告的 Micro-F1 分数反映了排名约束评估制度下的表现，并且不能直接与没有此类约束的严格分类结果进行比较。Importantly, the use of a Top-N constraint alters the interpretation of classification metrics at Level-2. Under this setting, models are evaluated based on their ability to recover relevant labels within a bounded candidate set, effectively framing the task as a constrained ranking problem rather than unconstrained multi-label classification. As a result, Micro-F1 scores reported in Track F reflect performance under a ranking-constrained evaluation regime and are not directly comparable to strict classification outcomes without such constraints.

在 Top-N 约束下进行评估时，传统模型和基于 Transformer 的模型都产生了中等至较强的性能（1 级时 Micro-F1 ≈ 0.61–0.64），其中基于 Transformer 的模型在 micro-F1 和 AUPRC 方面表现最佳。这些结果支持以这种方式训练的模型的典型期望，表明模型已经学习了操作标签结构中存在的可识别模式。请注意，Top-N 约束并不是为了提高分类性能，而是通过将任务重新构建为有界候选恢复来稳定极端标签稀疏下的评估。When evaluated under the Top-N constraints, both traditional and transformerbased models produced moderate to strong performance (Micro-F1 ≈ 0.61–0.64 at Level-1), with the transformer-based models being the best performers in terms of micro-F1 and AUPRC. These results support typical expectations for models trained in this manner, indicating that models have learned identifiable patterns present in the operational labeling structure. Note that the Top-N constraint is not intended to improve classification performance, but to stabilize evaluation under extreme label sparsity by reframing the task as bounded candidate recovery.

5.2 权威差距：银到金评估（R轨）5.2 Authority Gap: Silver-to-Gold Evaluation (Track R)

在 Track R 评估条件下，所有模型类型的性能都会显着下降，其中最初使用操作（“银”）标签数据开发的模型与独立验证的（“金”）标签进行比较。这种下降应该被解释为评估制度的影响，而不仅仅是模型能力的限制。Performance for all model types declines significantly under Track R evaluation conditions, where models initially developed with operational (“silver”) label data are compared with independently validated (“gold”) labels. This decline should be interpreted as an effect of the evaluation regime rather than solely as a limitation of model capability.

L1 性能从 Track F 下的中等高 Micro-F1 分数下降到 Track R 下的较低值（例如，从约 0.64 降至 0.37），表明操作标签集和验证标签集之间的重叠有限。在 Level-2，分类性能在严格的评估标准下接近于零水平（Micro-F1 从大约 0.54 下降到 0.03）。尽管模型仍然可能产生重要的标签排名（即排名指标保持在基线之上），但与经过验证的标签集相比，这些排名通常是不一致的。L1 performance drops from moderately high Micro-F1 scores under Track F to lower values under Track R (e.g., from approximately 0.64 to 0.37), indicating limited overlap between the operational and validated label sets. At Level-2, classification performance approaches near-zero levels under strict evaluation criteria (Micro-F1 decreases from approximately 0.54 to 0.03). Although models may still produce non-trivial rankings of labels (i.e., ranking metrics remain above baseline), these rankings are generally misaligned when compared to the verified label set.

观察到的 2 级性能下降的幅度必须根据标签权威和评估协议的差异来解释。在 Track F 中，Level-2 评估是在 Top-N 排名约束下进行的，它通过将输出限制为得分最高的候选标签来稳定预测。这有效地评估了模型在有界集合内对相关标签进行排名的能力，而不是要求在整个标签空间中进行精确分类。The magnitude of the observed performance decline at Level-2 must be interpreted in light of both label authority and evaluation protocol differences. In Track F, Level-2 evaluation is conducted under a Top-N ranking constraint, which stabilizes predictions by limiting outputs to the highest-scoring candidate labels. This effectively evaluates the model’s ability to rank relevant labels within a bounded set rather than requiring precise classification across the full label space.

相比之下，Track R 消除了这种排名限制，并使用严格的分类标准根据独立验证的黄金标签评估预测。这种转变在稀疏的高维标签空间中引入了更加严格的评估条件，其中正确的分类需要与独立验证的标签进行精确对齐。因此，观察到的下降（Micro-F1从大约0.54到0.03）不仅反映了运营标签和验证标签之间的权威差距，还反映了从排名受限评估到无约束评估的转变In contrast, Track R removes this ranking constraint and evaluates predictions against independently verified gold labels using strict classification criteria. This transition introduces a substantially more stringent evaluation condition in a sparse, highdimensional label space, where correct classification requires precise alignment with independently verified labels. Consequently, the observed decline (Micro-F1 from approximately 0.54 to 0.03) reflects not only the authority gap between operational and verified labels, but also the shift from ranking-constrained evaluation to unconstrained

二元分类。这种综合效应凸显了观察到的跨轨道性能差异既是语义上的（标签权威）又是方法上的（评估协议）。binary classification. This combined effect highlights that observed performance differences across tracks are both semantic (label authority) and methodological (evaluation protocol).

尽管 Track F（Top-N 约束）和 Track R（严格分类）之间的评估协议有所不同，但性能崩溃的程度超出了仅根据协议差异所预期的程度，这表明标签权威的主导效应。Although evaluation protocols differ between Track F (Top-N constrained) and Track R (strict classification), the magnitude of performance collapse exceeds what would be expected from protocol differences alone, indicating a dominant effect of label authority.

5.3 策划标签下的外部验证（Track P）5.3 External Validation Under Curated Labels (Track P)

Track P 下的模型所表现出的一致性和强度表明模型可以在标签权威性高且一致的条件下有效学习。在精心策划的监督下，性能得到恢复，在 1 级达到约 0.95 Micro-F1，在 2 级达到 0.83，在经典模型和基于变压器的模型中都具有同样强大的 AUPRC 值。The consistency and strength demonstrated by models under Track P indicate that models can learn effectively under conditions where label authority is high and consistent. Performance recovers under curated supervision, reaching approximately 0.95 Micro-F1 at Level-1 and 0.83 at Level-2, with similarly strong AUPRC values across both classical and transformer-based models.

这些结果表明，当培训和评估都受到高权威、一致策划的标签的管理时，就可以实现出色的绩效。然而，这并不意味着这种绩效会转移到运营环境中，其中标签流程在结构和治理方面都存在很大差异。These results demonstrate that strong performance is achievable when both training and evaluation are governed by high-authority, consistently curated labels. However, this does not imply that such performance transfers to operational environments, where labeling processes differ substantially in both structure and governance.

重要的是，Track P 和 Track R 之间的对比凸显了 Track R 中观察到的退化不仅仅是由于模型限制。相反，它反映了培训和评估条件之间标签权威不匹配的影响。当这种不匹配被消除时，如在 Track P 中，模型性能仍然很强，这表明 Track R 中的主要约束是评估条件而不是模型的学习能力。Importantly, the contrast between Track P and Track R highlights that the degradation observed in Track R is not solely due to model limitations. Rather, it reflects the effect of mismatched label authority between training and evaluation conditions. When this mismatch is removed, as in Track P, model performance remains strong, indicating that the primary constraint in Track R is the evaluation condition rather than the model’s ability to learn.

5.4 排名与分类行为5.4 Ranking vs. Classification Behavior

首先对所有赛道类型的基于排名和基于分类的评估进行分析，我们观察到两种类型指标的趋势非常相似。例如，在 Track R 中，尽管 Level-2 的二元分类性能接近机会水平，但精确率-召回率曲线下面积 (AUPRC) 和标签排名平均精度 (LRAP) 值仍高于基线。Beginning with an analysis of ranking-based vs. classification-based evaluations across all track types, we observed very similar trends across both types of metrics. For example, in Track R, although binary classification performance at Level-2 approaches chance levels, the Area Under the Precision–Recall Curve (AUPRC) and Label Ranking Average Precision (LRAP) values remain above baseline.

这与之前强调基于排名的指标在多标签分类中的有用性的工作是一致的（Tsoumakas & Katakis，2008）。事实上，模型保留了一些有关标签的排序信息，即使它们在严格的评估标准下不会产生强有力的预测，这进一步支持了此类指标的实用性。This is consistent with prior work highlighting the usefulness of ranking-based metrics in multi-label classification (Tsoumakas & Katakis, 2008). The fact that models retain some rank-ordering information concerning labels, even though they do not produce strong predictions under strict evaluation criteria, further supports the utility of such metrics.

这些发现的直接结果是，在弱标记数据上训练的模型似乎能够推断项目之间的部分语义关系；然而，当相对于更高权威的标签进行评估时，这些模型无法将这种关系信号转化为正确的分类决策。As a direct result of these findings, it appears that models trained on weakly labeled data are able to infer partial semantic relationships between items; however, these models fail to translate this relational signal into correct classification decisions when evaluated relative to higher-authority labels.

因此，在弱标记环境中评估模型性能时需要谨慎。特别是，区分基于排名的模型质量度量和基于分类的预测准确性度量非常重要。这种区别在解释 Track F 结果时尤其重要，其中 Top-N 约束允许模型满足基于排名的性能标准，但缺乏在较高权威条件下进行准确分类所需的精度。Thus, caution is required when assessing model performance in weakly labeled environments. In particular, it is important to distinguish between measures of model quality based on ranking and measures of predictive accuracy based on classification. This distinction is especially relevant when interpreting Track F results, where Top-N constraints allow models to satisfy ranking-based performance criteria while lacking the precision required for accurate classification under higher-authority conditions.

6. 讨论6. Discussion

重要的是，本研究的结果不应被解释为对 OSTI 系统内分类性能的验证。相反，结果构成了对智能分类系统和标签权威中评估制度的实证审计，说明了评估结果如何取决于培训和评估标签来源之间的关系。观察到的跨轨道性能差异反映了评估条件的属性，而不是模型能力的内在差异。对于所有测试的模型和数据集，我们证明模型性能根据用于评估的标签的权威性而有很大差异；即，训练期间提供的“操作”（银）标签或每次测试运行后创建的“独立验证”（金）标签。我们的研究结果挑战了这样一种观点，即分类模型性能的完善衡量标准将为此类模型的性能提供一致且客观的观点。Importantly, the findings of this study should not be interpreted as a validation of classification performance within the OSTI system. Rather, the results constitute an empirical audit of evaluation regime in intelligent classification systems and label authority, illustrating how evaluation outcomes depend on the relationship between training and evaluation label sources. The observed performance differences across tracks reflect properties of the evaluation conditions rather than intrinsic differences in model capability. For all tested models and data sets, we demonstrate that model performance varies substantially depending on the authority of the labels used for evaluation; i.e., either the "operational" (silver) labels provided during training or the "independently validated" (gold) labels that were created after each test was run. Our research results challenge the idea that well-established measures of classification model performance will provide a consistent and objective view of the performance of such models.

一个关键的观察结果是在运营评估条件下存在夸大的绩效。这种效应与之前关于标签噪音和弱监督的研究结果一致（Frénay & Verleysen，2014）。在 F 轨道中，训练和评估依赖于相同的操作标签源，模型在多个指标上取得了出色的结果。然而，这些结果并不一定表明真正的预测有效性。相反，它们反映了与标签系统本身的一致性。由于训练和评估标签都源自同一流程，因此模型经过有效优化，可以重现该流程中嵌入的模式，包括其中可能包含的任何偏差或不一致之处。A key observation is the presence of inflated performance under operational evaluation conditions. This effect is consistent with prior findings on label noise and weak supervision (Frénay & Verleysen, 2014). In Track F, where training and evaluation rely on the same operational label source, models achieve strong results across multiple metrics. However, these results are not necessarily indicative of true predictive validity. Instead, they reflect consistency with the labeling system itself. Because both training and evaluation labels originate from the same process, models are effectively optimized to reproduce the patterns embedded in that process, including any biases or inconsistencies it may contain.

当转向 Track R 时，这种效果变得明显，在 Track R 中，根据独立验证的标签对在操作标签上训练的模型进行评估。在这种情况下观察到的绩效大幅下降凸显了运营监管和验证监管之间的权威差距。在 2 级，这种差距尤其明显，尽管排名性能不平凡，但二元分类指标接近于零值。这些结果表明，在弱监督下训练的模型可能会捕获部分信号，但在评估时无法产生可靠的预测This effect becomes evident when moving to Track R, where models trained on operational labels are evaluated against independently verified labels. The substantial drop in performance observed in this setting highlights the authority gap between operational and verified supervision. At Level-2, this gap is particularly pronounced, with binary classification metrics approaching near-zero values despite non-trivial ranking performance. These results indicate that models trained under weak supervision may capture partial signal but fail to produce reliable predictions when evaluated against

更高权威的标签。虽然评估协议的差异会导致观察到的差异，但主要影响来自标签权威higher-authority labels. While evaluation protocol differences contribute to observed variation, the dominant effect arises from label authority

基于排名和基于分类的指标之间的差异提供了对弱监督下模型行为的进一步洞察。虽然模型保留了一定的对相关标签进行排名的能力，如 AUPRC 和相关度量所反映的那样，但它们无法将此信号转化为准确的离散预测。这表明模型学习的底层表示包含有用的信息，但可靠分类所需的决策边界与真实的标签结构不一致。实际上，这意味着模型可能对候选生成或优先级任务有用，但在没有额外验证的情况下不适用于明确的分类。The divergence between ranking-based and classification-based metrics provides further insight into model behavior under weak supervision. While models retain some ability to rank relevant labels, as reflected in AUPRC and related measures, they are unable to translate this signal into accurate discrete predictions. This suggests that the underlying representation learned by the model contains useful information, but that the decision boundaries required for reliable classification are misaligned with the true label structure. In practical terms, this means that models may be useful for candidate generation or prioritization tasks, but not for definitive classification without additional validation.

Track P 中观察到的强劲性能证实了模型本身并没有本质上的限制。当使用权威的、外部策划​​的标签进行训练和评估时，模型可以获得一致且可靠的结果。这表明Track R的主要约束不是模型容量，而是训练和评估标签权威之间的不匹配。这意味着仅改进模型架构不足以解决弱监督系统中的性能问题；还必须关注标签过程的质量和管理。The strong performance observed in Track P confirms that the models themselves are not inherently limited. When both training and evaluation are conducted using highauthority, externally curated labels, models achieve consistent and reliable results. This indicates that the primary constraint in Track R is not model capacity, but rather the mismatch between training and evaluation label authority. The implication is that improving model architecture alone is insufficient to address performance issues in weakly supervised systems; attention must also be directed toward the quality and governance of the labeling process.

这些发现支持了所提出框架的中心前提：评估有效性是系统级属性，而不是纯粹的模型级特征。模型性能不仅取决于算法和输入数据，还取决于标签的生成、管理和评估方式。在标签同时发挥操作和评估作用的系统中，这种双重功能可能会掩盖模型的真实行为，并导致对性能的过度自信的解释。These findings support the central premise of the proposed framework: evaluation validity is a system-level property, not a purely model-level characteristic. Model performance depends not only on the algorithm and input data, but also on how labels are generated, curated, and used for evaluation. In systems where labels serve both operational and evaluative roles, this dual function can obscure the true behavior of the model and lead to overconfident interpretations of performance.

从实践的角度来看，多轨框架提供了一种诊断这些问题的结构化方法。通过明确区分监督制度并比较不同轨道的绩效，从业者可以确定评估结果何时可能被夸大以及何时反映更可靠的预测行为。这种方法可以使有关模型部署的决策更加明智，特别是在错误预测可能会产生重大后果的高风险环境中。From a practical perspective, the multi-track framework provides a structured methodology for diagnosing these issues. By explicitly separating supervision regimes and comparing performance across tracks, practitioners can identify when evaluation results are likely to be inflated and when they reflect more reliable predictive behavior. This approach enables more informed decisions about model deployment, particularly in highstakes environments where incorrect predictions may have significant consequences.

更广泛地说，结果凸显了将数据治理考虑因素整合到机器学习系统设计中的重要性。该框架没有将标签视为静态输入，而是强调它们作为由组织流程塑造的动态工件的作用。这种观点与以数据为中心的人工智能的新兴工作相一致，但通过专门关注评估的影响来扩展它。More broadly, the results highlight the importance of integrating data governance considerations into machine learning system design. Rather than treating labels as static inputs, the framework emphasizes their role as dynamic artifacts shaped by organizational processes. This perspective aligns with emerging work in data-centric artificial intelligence, but extends it by focusing specifically on the implications for evaluation.

最后，概念Finally, the concept of评价主权evaluation sovereignty为理解这些动态提供了一个有用的视角。通过将评估框架构建为依赖于标签来源的独立性，该框架提供了一种原则性的方法来评估绩效指标何时可信。缺乏评估主权的系统更容易受到循环和夸大性能的影响，而那些保持训练和评估标签之间分离的系统可以提供对模型能力更可靠的估计。这种观点与新兴的以数据为中心的方法相一致，这些方法强调数据质量和标签流程的重要性（Ng，2021）。这项工作将模型评估本身重新定义为研究对象，而不是将评估视为中立的测量过程。provides a useful lens for understanding these dynamics. By framing evaluation as dependent on the independence of label sources, the framework offers a principled way to assess when performance metrics can be trusted. Systems that lack evaluation sovereignty are more susceptible to circularity and inflated performance, while those that maintain separation between training and evaluation labels provide more reliable estimates of model capability. This perspective aligns with emerging data-centric approaches that emphasize the importance of data quality and labeling processes (Ng, 2021). This work reframes model evaluation itself as the object of study, rather than treating evaluation as a neutral measurement process.

7. 对现实世界系统的影响7. Implications for Real-World Systems

这项研究的结果对现实世界信息环境中机器学习系统的设计、评估和部署有直接影响。在许多操作环境中，标签充当训练数据和评估目标的双重角色。当使用相同的标签源进行评估时，这种双重使用可能会掩盖模型的真实行为，并导致对性能的高估。The results of this study have direct implications for the design, evaluation, and deployment of machine learning systems in real-world information environments. In many operational settings, labels serve a dual role as both training data and evaluation targets. This dual use can obscure the true behavior of models and lead to overestimation of performance when evaluation is conducted using the same labeling source.

研究结果表明，从业者在评估模型质量时不应仅仅依赖于从操作标签得出的标准性能指标。相反，评估应尽可能纳入独立或更高权威的标签来源。即使经过验证的标签的有限子集也可以提供有价值的见解，了解模型性能在多大程度上反映了真实的预测能力，而不是与特定的标签系统保持一致。The findings suggest that practitioners should not rely solely on standard performance metrics derived from operational labels when assessing model quality. Instead, evaluation should incorporate independent or higher-authority label sources whenever possible. Even limited subsets of verified labels can provide valuable insight into the extent to which model performance reflects true predictive capability rather than alignment with a specific labeling system.

基于排名和基于分类的指标之间观察到的差异进一步凸显了仔细解释模型输出的必要性。在弱监督环境中，即使分类性能不可靠，模型也可能保留用于排名或优先级任务的有用信号。这表明部署策略应考虑模型输出的适当使用，区分需要精确分类的任务和可以容忍近似排名的任务。The divergence observed between ranking-based and classification-based metrics further highlights the need for careful interpretation of model outputs. In weakly supervised environments, models may retain useful signal for ranking or prioritization tasks, even when classification performance is unreliable. This suggests that deployment strategies should consider the appropriate use of model outputs, distinguishing between tasks that require precise classification and those that can tolerate approximate ranking.

该框架还强调了数据治理在机器学习系统设计中的重要性。标签质量不是静态属性，而是由组织流程、不断发展的标准和人类决策决定的。因此，提高系统性能不仅需要模型架构的进步，还需要关注标签的生成、维护和验证方式。The framework also underscores the importance of data governance in machine learning system design. Label quality is not a static property but is shaped by organizational processes, evolving standards, and human decision-making. As such, improving system performance requires not only advances in model architecture, but also attention to how labels are generated, maintained, and validated.

最后，评价主权的概念为制度设计提供了实践指导。在可行的情况下，保持培训和评估标签源之间的分离可以降低循环评估的风险并提高绩效评估的可靠性。在无法完全分离的情况下，多轨评估框架提供了一种结构化方法来识别和量化潜在偏差。Finally, the concept of evaluation sovereignty provides a practical guideline for system design. Maintaining separation between training and evaluation label sources, where feasible, can reduce the risk of circular evaluation and improve the reliability of performance estimates. In cases where full separation is not possible, the multi-track evaluation framework offers a structured approach for identifying and quantifying potential bias.

8. 局限性8. Limitations

应该承认这项研究的一些局限性。首先，Track R 中使用的黄金评估子集由 1,000 个 OSTI 记录组成，这些记录由单个审阅者根据源自官方 OSTI L1 标签列表和既定规范化规则的记录标签指南进行注释。虽然黄金子集并非旨在作为普遍的基本事实，但其目的是强制独立于操作标签制度，从而实现对监督效果的受控评估。由于未计算注释者间的一致性，Track R 结果应被解释为旨在强制监督分裂并减少循环评估的结构化审计条件，而不是传统意义上独立裁决的基本事实。此约束反映了注释过程的实际限制，并且与多轨框架内分配给 Track R 的探索角色一致。Several limitations of this study should be acknowledged. First, the gold evaluation subset used in Track R consists of 1,000 OSTI records annotated by a single reviewer under documented labeling guidelines derived from the official OSTI L1 label list and established normalization rules. While the gold subset is not intended as a universal ground truth, its purpose is to enforce independence from the operational labeling regime, enabling controlled evaluation of supervision effects. Because inter-annotator agreement was not computed, Track R results should be interpreted as a structured audit condition designed to enforce the supervisory split and reduce circular evaluation, rather than as independently adjudicated ground truth in the traditional sense. This constraint reflects practical limitations of the annotation process and is consistent with the exploratory role assigned to Track R within the multi-track framework.

其次，这里报告的实证研究结果来自两个领域——科学元数据 (OSTI) 和生物医学文献 (PubMed/MeSH)。虽然这些数据集提供了关于操作和策划标签环境的补充视角，但该框架及其研究结果对其他信息系统、分类法和机构标签实践的普遍性仍有待确定。未来的工作Second, the empirical findings reported here are drawn from two domains — scientific metadata (OSTI) and biomedical literature (PubMed/MeSH). While these datasets provide complementary perspectives on operational and curated labeling environments, the generalizability of the framework and its findings to other information systems, taxonomies, and institutional labeling practices remains to be established. Future work

第三，本研究中没有对 Track X 条件进行实证评估，其中根据可操作的银标准对在金标签上训练的模型进行评估。正如第 3 节中所讨论的，这种倒置权威条件在理论上很重要，因为它预测按照较高标签标准训练的模型在针对较低权威标签进行评估时可能会表现不佳。这种效应的实证表征代表了未来工作的优先方向，并将完善此处介绍的四轨框架。Third, the Track X condition, in which models trained on gold labels are evaluated against operational silver standards, was not empirically evaluated in this study. As discussed in Section 3, this inverted authority condition is theoretically significant because it predicts that models trained to a higher labeling standard may appear to underperform when evaluated against lower-authority labels. Empirical characterization of this effect represents a priority direction for future work and would complete the four-track framework introduced here.

此外，规范化程序提高了运营（白银）监督下的稳定性，但没有解决与标签权威和 2 级长尾稀疏性相关的潜在结构性限制。Furthermore, normalization procedures improved stability under operational (silver) supervision but did not resolve the underlying structural limitations associated with label authority and long-tail sparsity at Level-2.

最后，模型输入仅限于所有轨道的元数据字段（标题和摘要）。虽然这反映了大规模存储库中的实际部署限制，但它排除了可能携带与细粒度分类相关的附加信号的全文内容。全文输入会在多大程度上改变在 Track R 中观察到的权威差距仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。Finally, model inputs were restricted to metadata fields (title and abstract) throughout all tracks. While this reflects realistic deployment constraints in large-scale repositories, it excludes full-text content that may carry additional signal relevant to finegrained classification. The extent to which full-text inputs would alter the authority gap observed in Track R remains an open question.

9. 结论9. Conclusion

本文引入评估主权作为分析标签权威和监督制度如何影响观察到的模型性能的框架。通过在多个评估轨道上明确区分可操作（“银”）和独立验证（“金”）标签，拟议的框架表明标签来源的权威性和独立性是实际衡量绩效指标的主要决定因素。This paper introduced evaluation sovereignty as a framework for analyzing how label authority and supervision regimes influence observed model performance. By explicitly distinguishing between operational (“silver”) and independently verified (“gold”) labels across multiple evaluation tracks, the proposed framework demonstrates that the authority and independence of label sources are primary determinants of what performance metrics actually measure.

在所有实验中，模型性能在不同的监督制度下有很大差异。在操作评估中表现强劲的模型在针对独立标签进行评估时显着退化，在细粒度分类中观察到最明显的影响。在 2 级，性能从大约 0.54 Micro-F1 下降到 0.03 Micro-F1，而基于排名的指标仍然很重要，揭示了潜在模型信号和分类有效性之间的差异。相比之下，在策划监督下评估的模型强劲恢复，表明观察到的退化反映了治理差距，而不是模型能力的限制。Across all experiments, model performance varied substantially under different supervision regimes. Models that performed strongly under operational evaluation degraded significantly when assessed against independent labels, with the most pronounced effects observed in fine-grained classification. At Level-2, performance collapsed from approximately 0.54 to 0.03 Micro-F1, while ranking-based metrics remained non-trivial, revealing a divergence between latent model signal and classification validity. In contrast, models evaluated under curated supervision recovered strongly, indicating that the observed degradation reflects a governance gap rather than a limitation of model capability.

这些发现表明，评估有效性是由模型、数据和标签治理流程之间的交互形成的系统级属性。如果不明确考虑标签权威，通常报告的指标可能反映与标签流程的一致性，而不是真正的预测行为。这对模型选择和部署有直接影响，因为针对操作指标优化的系统可能会强化标签过程的属性，而不是感兴趣的潜在现象。These findings demonstrate that evaluation validity is a system-level property shaped by the interaction between models, data, and label governance processes. Without explicit consideration of label authority, commonly reported metrics may reflect alignment with labeling processes rather than true predictive behavior. This has direct implications for model selection and deployment, as systems optimized against operational metrics may reinforce properties of the labeling process rather than the underlying phenomenon of interest.

未来的工作将侧重于将该框架扩展到其他领域，并凭经验描述倒置权威条件（Track X），其中根据低权威标准评估在高权威标签上训练的模型。该方向将进一步阐明标签权威如何塑造评估结果并完善本研究中引入的框架。Future work will focus on extending this framework to additional domains and empirically characterizing the inverted authority condition (Track X), in which models trained on high-authority labels are evaluated against lower-authority standards.
