IRWOZ 2.0：用于工业机器人对话的大型语言模型驱动的对话数据集IRWOZ 2.0: A Large Language Model-driven Dialogue Dataset for Industrial Robot Conversations

Chen Li Email: cl@mp.aau.dk Affiliation: Department of Materials and Production, Aalborg University, Fibigerstraede 16, Aalborg, 9220, Denmark    Dimitrios Chrysostomou Email: dimi@mp.aau.dk Affiliation: Department of Materials and Production, Aalborg University, Fibigerstraede 16, Aalborg, 9220, Denmark
摘要Abstract

IRWOZ 通过特定领域的注释改进了工业人机交互 (HRI) 对话系统。然而，其初始版本在对话状态和话语中包含大量噪音，限制了状态跟踪的准确性。我们推出了 IRWOZ 2.0，它通过大型语言模型 (LLM) 增强生成 (Mistral/Claude-3.5) 和质量改进来解决这些限制。我们改进的数据集扩展到 4 个工业领域（装配、交付、定位、搬迁）的 390 个对话，具有手动更正和自动拼写错误删除功能。对话状态跟踪的基准实验证明了显着的改进，与原始 IRWOZ 相比，GPT-2 的 BLEU-4 分数从 0.1651 增加到 0.5604。为了支持工业 HRI 研究，我们在 https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/irwoz-20-large-language-model-driven-dialogue-dataset-industrial-robot-conversations 公开发布了 IRWOZ 2.0 数据集IRWOZ has improved industrial human-robot interaction (HRI) dialogue systems through domain-specific annotations. However, its initial version contains substantial noise in dialogue states and utterances, limiting state-tracking accuracy. We introduce IRWOZ 2.0, which addresses these limitations through large language model (LLM) enhanced generation (Mistral/Claude-3.5) and quality refinements. Our improved dataset expands to 390 dialogues across 4 industrial domains (Assembly, Delivery, Position, Relocation), featuring manual corrections and automated typo removal. Benchmark experiments on dialogue state tracking demonstrate significant improvements, with GPT-2’s BLEU-4 score increasing from 0.1651 to 0.5604 compared to original IRWOZ. To support industrial HRI research, we publicly released IRWOZ 2.0 dataset at https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/irwoz-20-large-language-model-driven-dialogue-dataset-industrial-robot-conversations

2 相关工作Related Work

在本节中，我们回顾与我们工作相关的三个关键领域：面向任务的对话数据集、使用 LLM 进行数据集校正以及 LLM 用于数据集生成。这些领域构成了我们创建 IRWOZ 2.0（工业机器人对话的新颖数据集）的方法的基础。In this section, we review three key areas relevant to our work: task-oriented dialogue datasets, the use of LLMs for dataset correction, and LLMs for dataset generation. These areas form the foundation for our approach in creating IRWOZ 2.0, a novel dataset for industrial robot conversations.

2.1 面向任务的对话数据集Task-Oriented Dialogue Datasets

面向任务的对话数据集在推进对话系统研究方面发挥着至关重要的作用Task-oriented dialogue datasets play a crucial role in advancing research in dialogue systemsReimann et al. (2024)。面向任务的对话系统的传统管道模型通常需要对对话状态和手工制作的动作空间进行显式建模，以与特定领域的知识库进行交互. Traditional pipeline models for task-oriented dialogue systems often require explicit modeling of dialogue states and hand-crafted action spaces to interact with domain-specific knowledge basesWen et al. (2018)。面向任务的对话系统旨在帮助用户完成特定任务，例如预订航班、预订餐厅或提供信息。此类系统的传统数据集通常是通过手动注释或收集现实世界的交互来创建的。例如，ATIS（航空旅行信息系统）数据集. Task-oriented dialogue systems aim to assist users in completing specific tasks such as booking flights, making restaurant reservations, or providing information. Traditional datasets for such systems are often created through manual annotation or by collecting real-world interactions. For instance, the ATIS (Airline Travel Information System) datasetHemphill et al. (1990)一直是口语理解系统的基准，包含航班信息查询。has been a benchmark for spoken language understanding systems, containing flight information queries.

另一个重要的数据集是 MultiWOZ 数据集Another significant dataset is the MultiWOZ datasetBudzianowski et al. (2018)，这是一个大规模的多域对话数据集，包含跨越多个域和服务的交互。这些数据集为训练和评估对话系统提供结构化和注释数据，有助于推动该领域的发展。最近，模式引导对话（SGD）数据集, which is a large-scale multi-domain dialogue dataset containing interactions spanning over multiple domains and services. These datasets have been instrumental in advancing the field by providing structured and annotated data for training and evaluating dialogue systems. More recently, the Schema-Guided Dialogue (SGD) datasetRastogi et al. (2020)已推出，其中包括跨越 16 个领域的 20,000 多个对话。 SGD 旨在通过合并描述每个域内的实体和操作的模式信息，为面向任务的对话系统提供更全面、更灵活的框架。框架数据集has been introduced, which includes over 20,000 dialogues spanning 16 domains. SGD aims to provide a more comprehensive and flexible framework for task-oriented dialogue systems by incorporating schema information that describes the entities and actions within each domain. The Frames datasetEl Asri et al. (2017)另一个例子，重点关注处理面向任务的对话中隐含信息的挑战，为系统推断此类信息并采取行动提供测试平台。is another example, which focuses on the challenge of handling implicit information in task-oriented dialogues, providing a testbed for systems to infer and act upon such information.

然而，尽管这些数据集具有显着先进的对话系统研究，但它们主要关注旅行和酒店等一般领域。工业机器人领域以其独特的词汇和特定任务的交互，仍然代表性不足。这一差距凸显了对 IRWOZ 2.0 等专业数据集的需求，这些数据集可以捕捉工业环境中 HRI 的细微差别。However, even though these datasets have significantly advanced dialogue systems research, they primarily focus on general domains such as travel and hospitality. The industrial robotics domain, with its unique vocabulary and task-specific interactions, remains underrepresented. This gap underscores the need for specialized datasets like IRWOZ 2.0 that can capture the nuances of HRI in industrial settings.

CoachLM represents a significant approach, automatically enhancing the quality of instruction pairs in datasetsLiu et al. The model’s effectiveness is demonstrated by its real-world application at Huawei, where it increased dataset cleaning efficiency by 20%. (2024)。此外，GrammarGPT 在使用混合数据集纠正中文原生文本中的语法错误方面表现出了希望. Additionally, GrammarGPT has shown promise in correcting grammatical errors in native Chinese text using a hybrid datasetFan et al. However, applying LLMs to correct specialized datasets such as those for industrial robotics presents unique challenges, including the need to preserve domain-specific terminology and task-relevant information while improving overall quality.

显然，传统数据集提供了特定领域知识和结构化注释的优势，但是，它们通常需要大量的手动工作，并且在大小和多样性方面受到限制。另一方面，LLM 生成的数据集提供了可扩展性和更广泛的对话上下文，但可能缺乏特定领域知识的深度。我们与 IRWOZ 2.0 的合作代表了一种混合方法，利用法学硕士生成可扩展、多样化且与行业相关的对话数据集，同时保持对特定行业领域的关注。这种方法对于先进的对话系统尤其重要，该系统能够处理现实世界工业应用的复杂需求，而现有的法学硕士应用在这一领域受到限制。It is evident, that the traditional datasets offer the advantage of domain-specific knowledge and structured annotations, however, they often require significant manual effort and are limited in size and diversity. Our work with IRWOZ 2.0 represents a hybrid approach, leveraging LLMs to generate scalable, diverse, and industry-relevant dialogue datasets while maintaining focus on specific industrial domains. This method is particularly important for advancing dialogue systems capable of handling the complex demands of real-world industrial applications, an area where existing LLM applications have been limited.

3 数据集修正Dataset Corrections

为了创建 IRWOZ 2.0，我们首先解决了原始 IRWOZ 数据集中存在的限制和错误。原始 IRWOZ 数据集是使用 Wizard-of-OZ 设置在 18 名参与者中收集的，其中包括学生、学者和具有从机器人到计算机科学背景的车间工人。参与者被随机配对为两人来构建对话。一名参与者扮演向导的角色，另一名参与者扮演车间工人的角色。车间工人被要求选择一项任务并在其中一个领域发起对话，To create IRWOZ 2.0, we first addressed the limitations and errors present in the original IRWOZ dataset. The original IRWOZ dataset was collected using a Wizard-of-OZ setup among 18 participants, including students, academics, and shop floor workers with backgrounds ranging from robotics to computer science. The participants were randomly paired as two to construct dialogues. One participant played the role of the Wizard and the other plays the shop floor worker. The shop floor worker is asked to choose a task and initiate a dialogue in one of domains,发货Delivery, 职位Position, 组装Assembly和and搬迁Relocation。向导需要根据所需的任务对车间工作人员做出响应。 Web 应用程序是用单独的界面构建的，一个用于用户，一个用于向导。该向导有权访问后端数据库和机器人控制 API 列表，以验证用户请求的资源。. The wizard needed to respond to the shop floor worker according to the required task. The web application was constructed with separate interfaces, one for the user and one for the Wizard. The wizard was given access to the back-end database and a list of the robot-controlling APIs to verify the resource requested by user.

尽管付出了巨大的努力来开发此类用于对话数据集收集和注释的 Web 应用程序，并实施人工验证过程，但 IRWOZ 中仍然存在许多错误，例如不完整的标记和错误注释。这些错误源于误解、注释者之间的不一致以及对话中上下文细微差别的复杂性。这种不准确性会严重影响数据集的质量和对话系统的性能。Regardless the great effort to develop such web application for dialogue dataset collection and annotation, along with implementing human validation processes, there are many mistakes in IRWOZ such as incomplete markups, and mis-annotations. These errors arise from misinterpretations, inconsistencies among annotators, and the complexities of contextual nuances in dialogues. Such inaccuracies can significantly affect the quality of the dataset and the performance of dialogue systems.

4 数据集生成Dataset Generation

尽管 IRWOZ 是通过 WoZ 方法收集的，该方法可以通过模拟与人类操作员的系统响应来实现丰富、真实的交互，但工业领域中 HRI 的此类对话数据集的收集和注释是一项劳动密集型且耗时的任务。因此，我们利用法学硕士的能力来生成现实且与上下文相关的对话，以反映四个工业领域中支持语言的 HRI。Even though the IRWOZ was collected through WoZ method, which allows for rich, realistic interactions by simulating system responses with human operators, the collection and annotation of such dialogue datasets for HRI in industrial domains is a labor-intensive and time-consuming task. Therefore, we leverage the LLMs’ capabilities to generate realistic and contextually relevant dialogues that reflect the language-enabled HRIs in four industrial domains.

4.2 及时设计Prompt Design

虽然法学硕士在处理对话方面非常有效，但他们回复的质量和风格在很大程度上取决于他们从用户那里收到的提示。良好设置和清晰的提示对于法学硕士展示所需的行为至关重要。While LLMs are highly effective in handling conversations, the quality and style of their responses are largely dependent on the prompts they receive from users. Well-set up and articulate prompts are imperative for LLMs to exhibit the desired behavior.

主要目标是确保生成的对话数据集与上下文相关且技术准确。这需要对提示试图解决的工业任务有一个清晰的定义——任务通常如何执行的详细背景，以及人类和机器人在相应场景中的具体角色。例如，设计用于生成搬迁任务对话的提示需要指定：要搬迁的项目、将它们搬迁到哪里以及可能遇到的任何障碍。提示还应鼓励生成包含常见工业术语和命令的对话，确保机器人的响应精确且与行业领域保持一致。The primary objective is to ensure the generated dialogue dataset is contextually relevant and technically accurate. This requires a clear definition of the industrial task which the prompt is trying to address — a detailed background of how the task is usually carried out as well as the specific roles of humans and robots in the corresponding scenario. As an example, a prompt designed to generate a dialogue for a relocation task would need to specify: the items to be relocated, where to relocate them to, and any barriers that could be run into. The prompt should also encourage the generation of dialogues that incorporate common industrial terminologies and commands, ensuring that the robot’s responses are precise and aligned with the industry domain.

此外，该提示旨在通过结合消歧、确认和错误处理等自然语言元素来模拟现实的 HRI。这需要创建场景，其中人类操作员可以要求机器人报告任务状态、验证正确的操作或修复任务期间的任何问题。例如，为搬迁任务生成的响应，如“PCB 是否已正确放置在传送带上？”或“如果在所需位置找不到 PCB 该怎么办。”通过将这种交互嵌入到对话中，生成的对话能够解决任务的操作方面，增强机器人有效沟通的能力，并对意外情况做出反应。此外，由于法学硕士接受了各种上下文中大量多样的文本数据的训练，因此他们可以有效地模仿人类的语音风格并生成类似人类的反应。此功能符合 IRWOZ 数据集的关键目标之一：提供与任务相关的响应和闲聊响应，从而增强 HRI 期间的用户体验。Furthermore, the prompt is designed to simulate realistic HRI by incorporating natural language elements such as disambiguation, confirmation, and error-handling. This requires creating scenarios in which the human operator may ask the robot to report the status of the task, verify a correct action, or fix any problem during the task. For example, the generated response for the relocation task like “Has the PCB been correctly put on the conveyor belt?” or ”What to do if the PCB cannot be found in the desired location.” By embedding such interactions in the conversations, the generated dialogues are able to address the operational aspects of the tasks, enhance the robot’s ability to communicate effectively, and respond to unexpected situations. Moreover, due to the fact that LLMs are trained on vast and diverse text data across various contexts, they can effectively mimic human speech styles and generate human-like responses. This capability aligns with one of the key objectives of the IRWOZ dataset: to provide both task-related and small talk responses, thereby enhancing the user experience during HRI.

7 结论和未来的工作Conclusions & Future Work

在本文中，我们提出了 IRWOZ 2.0，这是一种用于工业 HRI 的高级对话数据集。它将基于法学硕士的生成与手动和自动校正流程相结合，产生了更全面、更准确的数据集，涵盖四个关键工业领域：装配、交付、定位和搬迁。基准测试结果显示，IRWOZ 2.0 的性能优于其前身，在各种模型架构中的 BLEU 分数、联合目标准确性和其他关键指标方面都有显着改进。 IRWOZ 2.0 的公开发布将为研究界提供宝贵的资源，用于为工业 HRI 开发更复杂、响应更快的对话系统。未来的工作可能侧重于扩展到其他工业领域、集成多模态数据以及探索迁移学习技术，以进一步提高跨各种工业场景的模型性能。In this paper, we present IRWOZ 2.0, an advanced dialogue dataset for industrial HRI. It combines LLM-based generation with manual and automated correction processes, resulted in a more comprehensive and accurate dataset covering four key industrial domains: Assembly, Delivery, Position, and Relocation. Benchmarking results show that IRWOZ 2.0 outperforms its predecessor, with notable improvements in BLEU scores, Joint Goal Accuracy, and other key metrics across various model architectures. The public release of IRWOZ 2.0 will provide the research community with a valuable resource for developing more sophisticated and responsive dialogue systems for industrial HRI. Future work may focus on expanding to additional industrial domains, integrating multimodal data, and exploring transfer learning techniques to further improve model performance across various industrial scenarios.
