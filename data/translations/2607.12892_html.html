UR-VC：时间派生进度代理的无监督机器人值校正UR-VC: Unsupervised Robotic Value Correction for Time-Derived Progress Proxies

Lirui Zhao, Modi Shi, Li Chen, Qi Liu, Ping Luo, Hongyang Li
摘要Abstract

现代机器人学习系统越来越依赖密集的进度或价值信号来评估中间状态、指导策略学习和检测任务完成情况，这使得这些信号的质量至关重要。由于如此密集的标签很少能大规模使用，因此演示中的标准化时间通常被用作可扩展的替代品：后面的帧被视为更高的进度。然而，这个时间派生的标签只是物理任务进度的嘈杂代理。在接触丰富的操作中，机器人可能会取得进展，然后由于滑倒、抓握失败或部分撤销而失去进展，而时间衍生标签继续单调增加。 我们引入了无监督机器人值校正（UR-VC），这是一种离线、免训练的方法，用于校正时间派生的进度标签。 UR-VC 利用了演示数据中的一个简单规律：相似的状态经常在不同的事件中重复出现，但在不同的时间戳上。 UR-VC 不是信任来自单个轨迹的时间戳，而是从其他事件中检索类似的状态，并聚合它们的时间派生标签以获得正确的进度估计。 UR-VC 不需要手动进度标签、奖励注释或附加价值模型。 我们在真实的双手布料压平和折叠数据上评估 UR-VC，这是一项具有可见中间进度的长视野可变形对象操作任务。校正后的标签捕获归一化时间无法表示的局部回归和不均匀进度，同时保留总体任务趋势。在最近的优势条件策略学习之后，我们进一步使用校正后的信号来构建 VLA 训练的优势标签。在匹配的数据、模型和训练设置下，UR-VC 显示出真实机器人任务成功的积极趋势。Modern robot learning systems increasingly rely on dense progress or value signals to evaluate intermediate states, guide policy learning, and detect task completion, making the quality of these signals critical. Since such dense labels are rarely available at scale, normalized time within a demonstration is often used as a scalable substitute: later frames are treated as higher progress. However, this time-derived label is only a noisy proxy for physical task progress. In contact-rich manipulation, a robot may make progress and then lose it through slips, failed grasps, or partial undoing, while the time-derived label continues to increase monotonically. We introduce Unsupervised Robotic Value Correction (UR-VC), an offline, training-free method for correcting time-derived progress labels. UR-VC exploits a simple regularity in demonstration data: similar states often recur across different episodes, but at different timestamps. Instead of trusting the timestamp from a single trajectory, UR-VC retrieves similar states from other episodes and aggregates their time-derived labels to obtain a corrected progress estimate. UR-VC requires no manual progress labels, reward annotations, or additional value model. We evaluate UR-VC on real bimanual cloth flatten-and-fold data, a long-horizon deformable-object manipulation task with visible intermediate progress. The corrected labels capture local regressions and non-uniform progress that normalized time cannot represent, while preserving the overall task trend. We further use the corrected signal to construct advantage labels for VLA training, following recent advantage-conditioned policy learning. UR-VC shows a positive trend in real-robot task success under matched data, model, and training settings.

I 简介Introduction

除了动作监督之外，现代机器人学习系统越来越多地使用中间任务信号。 在长期操作中，此类信号可以估计当前状态距离完成任务有多远，以及最近的行动是否使任务向前推进。 它们通常采用任务进度、价值或优势的形式，并用于完成检测、价值函数学习和优势条件策略学习Modern robot learning systems increasingly use intermediate task signals in addition to action supervision. In long-horizon manipulation, such signals estimate how far the current state has advanced toward task completion and whether recent actions have moved the task forward. They commonly take the form of task progress, value, or advantage, and are used for completion detection, value-function learning, and advantage-conditioned policy learning[1, 25, 18, 22, 6, 9]。 随着视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型在更大、更多样化的机器人数据集上进行训练. As vision–language–action (VLA) models are trained on larger and more diverse robot datasets[5, 15, 4, 3]，这些密集信号的质量变得越来越关键。, the quality of these dense signals becomes increasingly critical.

困难在于密集的进展或价值标签很少能大规模获得。常见的可扩展替代方案是成功演示中的标准化时间The difficulty is that dense progress or value labels are rarely available at scale. A common scalable substitute is normalized time within a successful demonstration[1, 25]，它将后面的帧视为更高的进度，因为它们通常对应于更接近任务完成的状态。然而，生成的时间派生标签只是物理任务进度的嘈杂代理。它假设进度随时间单调且均匀地增加，而实际操作，尤其是接触丰富和可变形的操作, which treats later frames as higher progress since they usually correspond to states closer to task completion. However, the resulting time-derived label is only a noisy proxy for physical task progress. It assumes that progress increases monotonically and uniformly with time, while real manipulation, especially contact-rich and deformable manipulation[16, 8]，可以以不同的速率后退、停滞或前进。例如，在真实的布料压平和折叠事件中（图 1）。, can regress, stall, or advance at different rates. For example, in the real cloth flatten-and-fold episode (Fig.1），机器人首先抚平衣服，但随后的抓取滑动会重新引入皱纹并使任务状态向后移动，因此即使时间派生标签继续线性增加，物理进度也会倒退。), the robot first smooths the garment, but a later grasp slip reintroduces wrinkles and moves the task state backward, so physical progress regresses even as the time-derived label continues to increase linearly.

这种不匹配对下游学习有两个影响。 在一个情节中，这直接影响优势式监督：在固定水平进度差异下，线性时间代理为每一帧分配相同的增量，因此它无法区分改善物理状态的动作与停滞或撤消进度的动作。 在各个剧集中，这个问题很微妙，但更持久：相似的身体状态可能会被贴上不同的标签，仅仅是因为演示以不同的速度进行或采取不同的恢复动作。 然后，在这些噪声标签上训练的模型可以使用外观、纹理或执行速度等偶然线索而不是物理任务进度来解释标签变化。This mismatch matters for downstream learning in two ways. Within an episode, this directly affects advantage-style supervision: under a fixed-horizon progress difference, a linear time proxy assigns the same increase to every frame, so it cannot distinguish actions that improve the physical state from actions that stall or undo progress. Across episodes, the problem is subtle but more persistent: similar physical states can receive different labels simply because demonstrations proceed at different speeds or take different recovery motions. A model trained on these noisy labels may then explain label variation using incidental cues such as appearance, texture, or execution speed, rather than physical task progress.

常见的反应是从数据中学习该信号，例如通过使用时间对比、语言图像目标、价值预训练、最佳传输或生成建模来训练进度估计器或价值模型A common response is to learn this signal from data, for example by training progress estimators or value models using temporal contrast, language–image objectives, value pretraining, optimal transport, or generative modeling[23, 19, 20, 2, 11, 13]，或者通过从预训练模型中得出价值估计, or by eliciting value estimates from pretrained models[18, 7, 17]。 然而，当监督目标是系统偏差的时间派生代理时，学习的估计器可以继承相同的偏差：它可以平滑局部回归，并将执行速度或恢复行为的差异编码为进度差异。 这引发了一个补充问题：. However, when the supervision target is a systematically biased time-derived proxy, a learned estimator can inherit the same bias: it may smooth over local regressions and encode differences in execution speed or recovery behavior as differences in progress. This motivates a complementary question:在充当估计器或策略的监督之前，代理标签本身是否可以被纠正？can the proxy labels themselves be corrected before they serve as supervision for estimators or policies?

UR-VC is based on a simple data-driven premise: time-derived labels are noisy mainly because each demonstration has its own temporal distortion. Cross-episode state matches provide anchors for reducing this distortion: when similar physical states are found in independently collected episodes, the state is approximately held fixed while the timestamp varies across trajectories. Aggregating the time-derived labels of these matched states therefore estimates where that state typically lies in the task, rather than where it happened to occur in one particular episode. 我们首先将标准化时间形式化为潜在任务进展的噪声代理，并解释为什么其固定范围差异提供了退化的优势信号。然后，我们引入 UR-VC，它通过聚合来自跨情节的语义匹配状态的时间派生标签来纠正此代理。最后，我们描述了如何将校正后的进度估计转换为政策培训的优势标签。We first formalize normalized time as a noisy proxy for latent task progress and explain why its fixed-horizon difference provides a degenerate advantage signal. IV 实验Experiments

我们在真实的双手布料展平和折叠数据上评估 UR-VC。 由于在实际演示中无法直接观察到真正的物理进展，因此我们不会声称可以访问地面真实进展标签。 相反，我们的评估遵循该方法隐含的要求：跨情节状态重现应足够密集，检索到的帧应对应于相同的语义任务状态而不是表面外观，校正后的标签应表示非单调进展，并且生成的信号应可在下游机器人学习管道中使用。We evaluate UR-VC on real bimanual cloth flatten-and-fold data. Since true physical progress is not directly observed in real demonstrations, we do not claim access to ground-truth progress labels. Instead, our evaluation follows the requirements implied by the method: cross-episode state recurrences should be sufficiently dense, retrieved frames should correspond to the same semantic task state rather than superficial appearance, the corrected labels should express non-monotone progress, and the resulting signal should be usable in a downstream robot-learning pipeline.

V 局限性Limitations

UR-VC 假设视觉上相似的观察结果表明相似的任务进度。 这个假设与进度估计器和价值模型相同：当视觉上相似的状态发生在不同的任务阶段或不同的行动历史之后，它们的潜在进度可能是不明确的。 片段平衡平均减少了轨迹特定的定时噪声，但它不能纠正系统检索错误。UR-VC assumes that visually similar observations indicate similar task progress. This assumption is shared with progress estimator and value models: when visually similar states occur at different task stages or after different action histories, their latent progress can be ambiguous. Episode-balanced averaging reduces trajectory-specific timing noise, but it cannot correct systematic retrieval errors.

我们的下游评估重点关注一个代表性用例：使用 UR-VC 派生的监督在真实的双手布料操作中进行优势条件 VLA 训练。在匹配的实验设置下，校正后的标签会带来更高的平均成功率，这表明它们作为直接监督信号的实用性。未来的工作可能会进一步检查更广泛的任务族、训练规模和超参数设置的方法。Our downstream evaluation focuses on one representative use case: using UR-VC-derived supervision for advantage-conditioned VLA training in real bimanual cloth manipulation. Under matched experimental settings, the corrected labels lead to higher average success, suggesting their practical utility as a drop-in supervision signal. Future work may further examine the method across broader task families, training scales, and hyperparameter settings.

VI 结论Conclusion

UR-VC 通过平均独立机器人事件中的语义状态重现来纠正时间派生的进度代理。 它利用数据集冗余来更好地将可扩展代理标签与潜在的物理进展保持一致。 在真实的双手布料数据上，跨集匹配丰富且语义一致，校正后的估计恢复了单调时间代理无法表达的回归，并且所得信号可用于优势条件 VLA 训练。 时间提供了有用且可扩展的监督，但不应将其与身体进步混为一谈。 作为与模型无关的预处理步骤，UR-VC 在下游模型训练之前细化时间戳派生标签，无需额外的价值模型或对模型架构或训练目标进行任何更改。UR-VC corrects time-derived progress proxies by averaging semantic state recurrences across independent robot episodes. It exploits dataset redundancy to better align scalable proxy labels with latent physical progress. On real bimanual cloth data, cross-episode matches are abundant and semantic, corrected estimates recover regressions that a monotone time proxy cannot express, and the resulting signal can be used for advantage-conditioned VLA training. Time provides useful and scalable supervision, but it should not be conflated with physical progress. As a model-agnostic preprocessing step, UR-VC refines timestamp-derived labels before downstream model training, without requiring an additional value model or any changes to the model architecture or training objective.
