摘要Abstract

现在，数百万人向人工智能 (AI) 系统寻求个人建议、指导和支持。这样的系统可能是阿谀奉承的，经常肯定用户的观点和信仰。在五项预先注册的研究（N = 3,075 名参与者，12,766 次人类与人工智能对话）中，包括一项针对美国人口普查代表样本的为期三周的研究，我们提供了纵向实验证据，表明阿谀奉承的人工智能改变了用户处理最亲密关系的方式。我们表明，阿谀奉承的人工智能会立即为用户提供通常与亲密朋友和家人联系在一起的情感和尊重支持。经过三周的此类互动，用户从阿谀奉承的人工智能那里寻求个人建议的可能性几乎与从亲密的朋友和家人那里寻求个人建议一样，并且对现实世界的社交互动的满意度较低。当在人工智能响应方式中进行选择时，大多数人更喜欢谄媚的人工智能——不是因为它的建议质量，而是因为这让他们感觉最被理解。总之，这些发现提供了人工智能阿谀奉承的关系说明：通过提供无摩擦的理解，它可能会悄悄提高判断人际关系的标准。Millions of people now turn to artificial intelligence (AI) systems for personal advice, guidance, and support. Such systems can be sycophantic, frequently affirming users’ views and beliefs. Across five preregistered studies (N = 3,075 participants, 12,766 human-AI conversations), including a three-week study with a census-representative U.S. sample, we provide longitudinal experimental evidence that sycophantic AI shifts how users approach their closest relationships. We show that sycophantic AI immediately delivers the emotional and esteem support users typically associate with close friends and family. Over three weeks of such interactions, users became nearly as likely to seek personal advice from sycophantic AI as from close friends and family, and reported lower satisfaction with their real-world social interactions. When given a choice among AI response styles, a majority preferred sycophantic AI—not for the quality of its advice, but because it made them feel most understood. Together, these findings offer a relational account of AI sycophancy: by providing frictionless understanding, it may quietly raise the bar against which human relationships are judged.

简介Introduction

当面临艰难的工作决定、陷入困境的人际关系或日常忧虑时，人们长期以来都会向身边的人寻求安慰和建议。如今，人工智能系统正在迅速扮演这一角色，12% 的美国青少年和超过 20% 的年轻人已经转向聊天机器人寻求个人支持When facing a difficult job decision, a struggling relationship, or an everyday worry, people have long turned to those close to them for comfort and advice. Today, AI systems are rapidly taking on this role, with 12% of U.S. teens and over 20% of young adults already turning to chatbots for personal support[mcclain2026teens, Zao-Sanders_2025, mcbain2025genai]。越来越多的证据表明，在此类对话中，人工智能系统往往会阿谀奉承，经常肯定用户的观点和信仰. A growing body of evidence shows that, in such conversations, AI systems tend to be sycophantic, frequently affirming users’ views and beliefs[cheng2026elephant, sharma2023towards]。事实证明，即使是与阿谀奉承的人工智能的一次对话也会影响用户的判断，导致他们持有更加意识形态极端的政治观点. Even a single conversation with sycophantic AI has been shown to influence users’ judgment, leading them to hold more ideologically extreme political views[rathje2025sycophantic]，不太愿意考虑自己在冲突中的角色, to be less willing to consider their own role in conflicts[cheng2026sycophantic]并且，在严重的情况下，更有可能进行自残and, in severe cases, to be more likely to engage in self-harm[hill2025teen].

之前的工作研究了单次对话中的这些影响，但实际上，人们会在数周甚至数月内反复向人工智能系统吐露心声，就像他们与生活中的人一样。在这种规模上，阿谀奉承的人工智能的实际负面影响并不取决于任何孤立的对话，而是取决于这种影响如何随着用户与朋友、家人和合作伙伴的关系而展开。即使阿谀奉承的人工智能扭曲了用户的判断，这些人际关系仍然可以提供诚实的反思、阻力和观点，缓冲其影响。与此同时，反复接触无条件的人工智能肯定本身可能会改变用户与周围人交谈的体验，改变那些感觉充分理解、满足或值得寻求的东西Previous work has studied these effects in single conversations, but in reality, people confide in AI systems repeatedly over weeks and months, just as they do with the people in their lives. At this scale, the actual negative influence of sycophantic AI depends not on any isolated conversation, but on how that influence unfolds alongside users’ relationships with friends, family, and partners. Even if sycophantic AI distorts a user’s judgment, these human relationships can still offer honest reflection, pushback, and perspective, buffering against its effects. At the same time, repeated exposure to unconditional AI affirmation may itself alter how users experience conversations with the people around them, shifting what feels sufficiently understanding, satisfying, or worth seeking out[perry2026defense]。因此，阿谀奉承的人工智能的主要长期风险可能不是它在任何一次对话中扭曲的判断，而是它如何逐渐重塑原本会限制其影响力的关系。. Thus, the central long-term risk of sycophantic AI may not be the judgments it distorts in any single conversation, but how it gradually reshapes the very relationships that would otherwise constrain its influence.

As the AI industry shifts toward greater personalization and user choice, our findings underscore the importance of both user-side and model-level mitigations of AI sycophancy.

1 定义和实施阿谀奉承Defining and operationalizing sycophancy

奉承被广泛定义为人工智能系统与用户达成一致，即使该协议没有得到保证。然而，它在各个领域的运作方式却截然不同：从同意事实上不正确的主张Sycophancy has been broadly defined as AI systems agreeing with users even when that agreement is not warranted. However, it has been operationalized very differently across domains: from agreeing with factually incorrect claims[sharma2023towards]，支持用户在个人冲突中的行为, to endorsing users’ actions in personal conflicts[cheng2026sycophantic]，支持用户先前存在的政治观点, to supporting users’ preexisting political views viewpoints[rathje2025sycophantic]，未能阻止用户在寻求建议的询问中对情况的框架, to failing to push back on users’ framing of situations in advice-seeking queries[cheng2026elephant]。在这里，我们将阿谀奉承操作为对用户观点和推理的积极肯定。由于主动肯定和主动挑战可能各自对用户产生影响，而我们的主要兴趣是具体隔离主动肯定的影响，因此我们比较了. Here, we operationalize sycophancy as active affirmation of user views and reasoning. As active affirmation and active challenge may each have their own effects on users, and our primary interest is in isolating the effects of active affirmation specifically, we compare a阿谀奉承的sycophantic人工智能，旨在同意并支持用户的观点，以及AI, designed to agree with and support a user’s views, and a中立的neutral人工智能旨在保持公正并呈现多种观点，而无需明确同意或不同意。在研究 4 和 5 中，我们还包括AI, designed to remain impartial and present multiple perspectives without explicitly agreeing or disagreeing. In Studies 4 and 5, we additionally include a具有挑战性的challenging人工智能旨在质疑用户的观点并提供反驳，以更全面地了解不同参与风格的比较。所有三种条件在语气、格式和对话风格方面都有相同的说明，唯一不同的是它们如何回应参与者的观点（完整提示请参见补充方法，第 1.1 节）。我们通过计算分析（即测量模型响应中特定阿谀奉承行为的普遍程度）和参与者感知（参见补充信息，第 2.1 节）验证了每个模型都按预期参与。AI, designed to question users’ views and offer counterarguments, to provide a more comprehensive view of how different engagement styles compare. All three conditions shared identical instructions on tone, formatting, and conversational style, differing only in how they responded to participants’ views (see Supplementary Methods, Sections 1.1 for full prompts). We validated that each model engaged as intended through both computational analyses (i.e., measuring the prevalence of specific sycophantic behaviors in model responses) and participant perceptions (see Supplementary Information, Section 2.1).

Prior work has focused on measuring sycophancy in LLMs, understanding why it may emerge, and assessing its influence on users in single interactions[ibrahim2025training, sharma2023towards, rathje2025sycophantic, cheng2026sycophantic, dubois2026ask, cheng2026verbalizing, shen2026guidance, sun2025friendly]。在这里，我们超越了孤立的互动，提供了第一个纵向实验证据，证明阿谀奉承的人工智能对用户的影响。我们还将阿谀奉承的人工智能与中立的人工智能进行比较，而不是与默认或具有挑战性的基线进行比较，将主动肯定的效果与缺乏挑战的效果区分开来。着眼于个人建议领域，我们发现，与阿谀奉承的人工智能的互动会导致人们期望在最亲密的关系中付出更大的努力来被理解，让他们对现实世界的社交互动不太满意，并且随着时间的推移，他们会转向将阿谀奉承的人工智能视为与朋友和家人类似的建议来源。. When feeling understood becomes the default of every interaction, the human relationships that still require the work may, over time, come to feel like insufficient versions of what AI systems readily provide. As AI takes on a growing role in everyday life, whether it strengthens or damages human relationships will depend on designing these systems to help users without quietly leading them to expect from one another what only AI can deliver.
