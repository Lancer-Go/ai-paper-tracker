将 JEPA 式预测学习应用于 JA4 派生的网络指纹Applying JEPA-Style Predictive Learning to JA4-Derived Network Fingerprints

Aygul Zagidullina
Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts (HSLU)
aygul.zagidullina@hslu.ch    Javier Izquierdo
Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts (HSLU)
javier.izquierdo@stud.hslu.ch
摘要Abstract

I-JEPA 和 V-JEPA 通过将潜在预测与目标编码器输出匹配来学习，而不是重新生成原始输入，这对于图像和视频非常有效。我们探索相同的目标是否适用于紧凑的网络指纹。我们构建了 JA4-JEPA，这是一个基于 Transformer 的模型，在来自 JA4DB 和 CIC-IDS-2017 的 JA4、JA4H、JA4S 和 JA4X 子字段上进行训练。训练数据结合了来自两个来源的大约 397K 样本，但没有一个样本包含所有四个视图系列。我们使用冻结的 kNN 探针对跨 TLS、DNS 和 SSH 的协议族分类评估了学习到的表示。在 39,416 个保留样本上，该模型实现了 0.9899 的余弦相似度和 0.9220 的 kNN 精度。这些结果表明，JEPA 式的预测学习可以从 JA4 派生的指纹中生成有用的嵌入，即使源之间的视图重叠不完整。I-JEPA and V-JEPA learn by matching latent predictions to target encoder outputs rather than regenerating the original input, and this has worked well for images and video. We explore whether the same objective works for compact network fingerprints. We built JA4-JEPA, a Transformer-based model trained on JA4, JA4H, JA4S, and JA4X subfields drawn from JA4DB and CIC-IDS-2017. The training data combines roughly 397K samples from both sources, though no single sample contains all four view families. We evaluated the learned representations with a frozen kNN probe on protocol-family classification across TLS, DNS, and SSH. On 39,416 held-out samples the model achieved a cosine similarity of 0.9899 and a kNN accuracy of 0.9220. These results indicate that JEPA-style predictive learning can produce useful embeddings from JA4-derived fingerprints, even with incomplete view overlap across sources.

关键词：Keywords:JA4、网络指纹、JEPA、预测表示学习、自监督学习JA4, network fingerprinting, JEPA, predictive representation learning, self-supervised learning

1 简介Introduction

JA3 和 JA4 等网络指纹将协议握手详细信息压缩为短标识符，存储成本低且匹配速度快。然而，这些指纹主要用作静态查找键。它们本身并没有为我们提供可以概括不同分析任务的学习表示。Network fingerprints like JA3 and JA4 compress protocol handshake details into short identifiers that are cheap to store and fast to match. However, these fingerprints are mostly used as static lookup keys. They do not, on their own, give us a learned representation that could generalize across different analysis tasks.

I-JEPA 和 V-JEPA 表明，预测潜在空间中的目标表示，而不是重建原始输入，可以从图像和视频中学习强大的特征I-JEPA and V-JEPA showed that predicting target representations in latent space, instead of reconstructing the raw input, can learn strong features from images and video(Assran et al., 2023; Bardes et al., 2024)。我们想知道相同的预测方法是否可以适用于完全不同类型的数据：源自 JA4+ 的紧凑网络指纹。. We wanted to know if the same predictive approach could work on a very different kind of data: compact network fingerprints derived from JA4+.

为了测试这一点，我们构建了 JA4-JEPA，这是一个基于 Transformer 的 JEPA 模型，在来自两个来源的 JA4、JA4H、JA4S 和 JA4X 子字段上进行训练：JA4DB 和 CIC-IDS-2017。组合数据集大约有 397K 个标记化样本，但模态重叠不完整——没有一个样本包含所有四个视图系列，而且 JA4 通常是跨源共享的唯一视图。To test this, we built JA4-JEPA, a Transformer-based JEPA model trained on JA4, JA4H, JA4S, and JA4X subfields from two sources: JA4DB and CIC-IDS-2017. The combined dataset has roughly 397K tokenized samples, but modality overlap is incomplete — no single sample contains all four view families, and JA4 is often the only view shared across sources.

我们通过冻结 kNN 探针对 TLS、DNS 和 SSH 上的协议族分类评估了学习到的表示。尽管训练数据中的视图重叠不完整，但该模型在此任务中生成了有用的嵌入。主要贡献是：We evaluated the learned representations through a frozen kNN probe on protocol-family classification over TLS, DNS, and SSH. The model produces useful embeddings on this task despite the incomplete view overlap in the training data. The main contributions are:

  • •

    JEPA 风格的预测学习对 JA4 派生的网络指纹的改编。An adaptation of JEPA-style predictive learning to JA4-derived network fingerprints.

  • •

    JA4、JA4H、JA4S 和 JA4X 上的混合源训练设置，其中模态覆盖范围因样本而异，并且没有样本具有完全视图重叠。A mixed-source training setup over JA4, JA4H, JA4S, and JA4X where modality coverage varies across samples and no sample has complete view overlap.

  • •

    评估表明，该模型的冻结嵌入支持对保留的 TLS、DNS 和 SSH 数据进行协议族分类。Evaluation showing that frozen embeddings from this model support protocol-family classification on held-out TLS, DNS, and SSH data.

  • •

    210 万个网关指纹对的生产试验语料库上的匹配基线异常基准，在一个无泄漏协议下将预测能量信号与频率、最近邻、自动编码器、重建和聚类基线进行比较，包括训练集大小扫描（第 1 节）A matched-baseline anomaly benchmark on a production pilot corpus of 2.1M gateway fingerprint pairs, comparing the prediction-energy signal against frequency, nearest-neighbour, autoencoder, reconstruction, and clustering baselines under one leakage-free protocol, including a training-set size sweep (Section4.5).

这项工作的动机是实用的。标准指纹识别将每个签名视为查找密钥，而流级或数据包级模型需要大量输入。从多个紧凑的指纹视图中学习的模型可以处于有用的中间地带——轻量级输入，但比单一签名匹配更丰富。The motivation for this work is practical. Standard fingerprinting treats each signature as a lookup key, while flow-level or packet-level models require much heavier input. A model that learns from several compact fingerprint views could sit in a useful middle ground — lightweight inputs, but richer than single-signature matching.

2 相关工作Related Work

基于 TLS 的指纹识别有着悠久的实践历史。 JA3 表明 TLS 握手结构可以识别客户端，但现实世界 TLS 实现的多样性使得单一指纹变得脆弱，客户端可以故意且令人信服地模仿其他实现的 TLS 行为TLS-based fingerprinting has a long practical history. JA3 showed that TLS handshake structure can identify clients, but the diversity of real-world TLS implementations makes single fingerprints brittle, and clients can deliberately and convincingly mimic the TLS behavior of other implementations(Frolov and Wustrow, 2019)。此外，像 QUIC 这样的协议完全绕过经典的基于 TLS 的指纹识别。这些问题推动了对更丰富的指纹家族的研究。. In addition, protocols like QUIC bypass classic TLS-based fingerprinting entirely. These problems motivated work on richer fingerprint families.

JA4+ 就是这样一个家族。它定义了一组模块化指纹，涵盖客户端 TLS、服务器 TLS、HTTP 行为和证书属性JA4+ is one such family. It defines a modular set of fingerprints covering client TLS, server TLS, HTTP behavior, and certificate properties(Althouse, 2023)。就我们的目的而言，JA4+ 很有趣，因为它提供了同一连接的多个相关但结构不同的视图。这使得它成为多视图表示学习的自然输入。. For our purposes, JA4+ is interesting because it provides several related but structurally different views of the same connection. That makes it a natural input for multi-view representation learning.

最近的网络流量分析工作已经开始将网络数据视为多视图问题。焦阿基尼等人。表明自监督多模态嵌入可以在参数较少的网络分析任务上匹配或优于特征串联Recent work in network traffic analysis has started treating network data as a multi-view problem. Gioacchini et al. showed that self-supervised multimodal embeddings can match or outperform feature concatenation on network-analysis tasks with fewer parameters(Gioacchini et al., 2024)，和胡伊迪等人。主张将网络数据视为多模态表示问题而不是单个特征空间, and Houidi et al. argued for treating network data as a multimodal representation problem instead of a single feature space(Houidi et al., 2022)。我们的工作具有这种多视角动机。主要区别在于训练目标：我们使用预测潜在损失而不是重建或融合，并且我们的输入是紧凑的 JA4 派生子字段，在样本之间具有不完全共现。. Our work shares that multi-view motivation. The main difference is the training objective: we use a predictive latent loss instead of reconstruction or fusion, and our inputs are compact JA4-derived subfields with incomplete co-occurrence across samples.

我们的目标来自 JEPA 系列。在 I-JEPA 中，Assran 等人。训练一个图像模型，其中一个编码器看到上下文补丁，而预测器尝试匹配看到其他补丁的 EMA 目标编码器的输出Our objective comes from the JEPA family. In I-JEPA, Assran et al. trained an image model where one encoder sees a context patch and a predictor tries to match the output of an EMA target encoder that sees other patches(Assran et al., 2023)。巴德斯等人。使用 V-JEPA 将相同的原理应用于视频. Bardes et al. applied the same principle to video with V-JEPA(Bardes et al., 2024)。这两个模型都不会尝试重新生成原始输入。图像补丁和网络指纹显然是非常不同的数据，因此不能保证这会传输。但潜在的目标——将预测与学习空间中的目标进行匹配——足够通用，似乎值得在结构化指纹视图上尝试。. Neither model tries to regenerate the raw input. Image patches and network fingerprints are obviously very different data, so there was no guarantee this would transfer. But the underlying objective — match predictions to targets in a learned space — is generic enough that it seemed worth trying on structured fingerprint views.

在网络方面，ET-BERT 证明了对加密流量字节序列进行自监督预训练可以改善下游分类On the network side, ET-BERT demonstrated that self-supervised pre-training over encrypted traffic byte sequences can improve downstream classification(Lin et al., 2022)。我们的设置的不同之处在于我们不对原始数据包或流序列进行建模。相反，我们使用短的标记化指纹视图，这些视图在源之间重叠不均匀。. Our setup differs in that we do not model raw packet or flow sequences. Instead, we work with short tokenized fingerprint views that have uneven overlap across sources.

据我们所知，之前没有任何工作将 JEPA 式的潜在预测应用于紧凑协议指纹。最接近的工作——字节序列预训练To our knowledge, no prior work applies JEPA-style latent prediction to compact protocol fingerprints. The closest lines of work — byte-sequence pre-training(Lin et al., 2022)和多视图流量嵌入and multi-view traffic embeddings(Gioacchini et al., 2024; Houidi et al., 2022)- 基于重建或分类。这就是本文所解决的差距。— are reconstruction- or classification-based. That is the gap this paper addresses.

3 方法Method

该模型使用 JEPA 训练目标：预测器网络尝试在共享潜在空间中匹配 EMA 更新的目标编码器的输出，而无需重建原始输入。与之前的 JEPA 工作的不同之处在于：使用短标记化的 JA4 派生指纹子字段，而不是图像补丁或视频帧。The model uses the JEPA training objective: a predictor network tries to match the output of an EMA-updated target encoder in a shared latent space, without ever reconstructing the raw input. The difference from prior JEPA work is what goes in: short tokenized JA4-derived fingerprint subfields instead of image patches or video frames.

3.2 预测架构Predictive Architecture

该架构由三个部分组成：处理可见视图的上下文编码器、生成预测目标的 EMA 更新目标编码器以及从上下文表示映射到目标空间的预测器。 13 个子字段中的每一个都有自己的标记嵌入表。这些嵌入在馈送到编码器之前与学习的模态类型和位置嵌入相结合。的The architecture has three parts: a context encoder that processes visible views, an EMA-updated target encoder that produces the prediction targets, and a predictor that maps from the context representation to the target space. Each of the 13 subfields has its own token embedding table. These embeddings are combined with learned modality-type and position embeddings before being fed to the encoder. 3.3 训练设置Training Setup

损失函数是 JEPA MSE，在预测器输出和目标编码器输出之间计算。我们使用批量大小 256 进行了 30 个 epoch 的训练，学习率为The loss function is JEPA MSE, computed between the predictor output and the target encoder output. We trained with batch size 256 for 30 epochs, using a learning rate of2×10−32\times 10^{-3}，权重衰减为 0.04，3 个预热阶段，EMA 衰减为 0.997。在训练期间，各个子字段以 0.15 的概率被屏蔽，整个模态组以 0.30 的概率被屏蔽。测试分割是数据集的 0.1。训练后，编码器权重被冻结并使用，无需任何监督微调。, weight decay of 0.04, 3 warmup epochs, and EMA decay of 0.997. During training, individual subfields are masked with probability 0.15 and entire modality groups with probability 0.30. The test split is 0.1 of the dataset. After training, the encoder weights are frozen and used without any supervised fine-tuning.

4.3 掩模视图消融Masked-View Ablation

隐藏任何单一视图系列都会降低性能，但效果各不相同（表Hiding any single view family degrades performance, but the effect varies (Table3）。与掩蔽 JA4 或 JA4X 相比，掩蔽 JA4H 或 JA4S 会导致余弦一致性更大的下降以及能量距离的更大增加。这与数据集结构一致：JA4H 和 JA4S 涵盖不同的协议级信息（分别是 HTTP 和服务器 TLS），而 JA4 已经是最广泛共享的视图。我们注意到这些消融在这个特定的设置中显示出敏感性；他们没有建立一个总体排名来确定哪种观点家庭最重要。). We note that these ablations show sensitivity in this particular setup; they do not establish a general ranking of which view family is most important.

4.5 试点语料库的匹配基线异常基准Matched-Baseline Anomaly Benchmark on a Pilot Corpus

经过上述研究后，该模型在不同的语料库上重建为生产试点：来自企业网关遥测的 210 万个真实（JA4、JA4H）指纹对，仅两个视图。在该语料库上，我们进行了仅探针评估无法提供的匹配基线比较。所有方法在 400K 样本上共享一个协议：训练/验证/保留分割以完整对的内容哈希为键（训练 320,105，保留 39,990），因此在训练中从未见过保留组合，并且所有频率表和候选池仅根据训练分割构建。After the study above, the model was rebuilt as a production pilot on a different corpus: 2.1 million real (JA4, JA4H) fingerprint pairs from enterprise gateway telemetry, two views only. On that corpus we carried out the matched-baseline comparison that the probe evaluation alone cannot provide. All methods share one protocol on a 400K sample: a train/validation/held-out split keyed on a content hash of the full pair (train 320,105, held-out 39,990), so a held-out combination was never seen in training, and all frequency tables and candidate pools are built from the training split only.

三个突出的发现（表Three findings stand out (Table4, 图, Figure3）。首先，JEPA 能量是两个异常类别上唯一的强信号（最差类别 AUC 0.922；次佳 0.870）：重建式方法在混洗的单例重流量上倾向于偶然，而频率楼层因构造而盲目。其次，具有记忆能力的方法在全参考范围内主导着硬正类，这意味着检测新的重组很大程度上是一个记忆问题——该类本身无法区分学习的表示，这是未来基准的负集设计观察。第三，成本结构在性质上有所不同：最近邻搜索为每次查询支付与语料库一起增长的参考，而模型以恒定的两次编码器传递和一次预测器传递进行评分。). First, the JEPA energy is the only signal strong on both anomaly classes (worst-class AUC 0.922; next best 0.870): reconstruction-style methods fall toward chance on shuffled singleton-heavy traffic, while frequency floors are blind by construction. Second, memory-capable methods dominate the hard-positive class at full reference scale, which means detecting novel recombinations is largely a memory problem — that class alone cannot differentiate learned representations, a negative-set design observation for future benchmarks. Third, the cost structures differ qualitatively: nearest-neighbour search pays per query for a reference that grows with the corpus, while the model scores at a constant two encoder passes plus one predictor pass.

The model, a single checkpoint at every size, scores about 8,000 pairs per second on CPU with no reference database.

有两个警告限制了这些主张。异常类别是合成代理，而不是标记的入侵，因此没有可操作的检测声明。阈值行为是该模型的弱点：在 5% 的假阳性预算下，它捕获了 53% 的硬阳性异常——排名很强，校准是开放工作。Two caveats bound these claims. The anomaly classes are synthetic proxies, not labeled intrusions, so no operational detection claim follows. And the threshold behavior is the model’s weak point: at a 5 % false-positive budget it catches 53 % of hard-positive anomalies — ranking is strong, calibration is open work.

5 讨论Discussion

混合源模型使用冻结嵌入在三类协议族探针上实现了很高的 kNN 精度，并且预测器和目标输出之间的余弦一致性很高。这表明，首先在视觉领域提出的 JEPA 式预测目标也可以从紧凑的网络指纹中学习有用的结构——即使训练数据具有不完全的视图重叠。The mixed-source model achieved strong kNN accuracy on the three-class protocol-family probe using frozen embeddings, with high cosine agreement between predictor and target outputs. This suggests that a JEPA-style predictive objective, first proposed in the vision domain, can also learn useful structure from compact network fingerprints — even when the training data has incomplete view overlap.

不过，协议族分类是一项粗略的任务。将 TLS 与 DNS 和 SSH 区分开来是很有用的，但它并没有说明是什么应用程序生成了流量或者流量是否是恶意的。测试更细粒度的区别需要我们没有的标签。Protocol-family classification is a coarse task, though. Telling TLS apart from DNS and SSH is useful, but it does not say anything about what application generated the traffic or whether it is malicious. Testing finer-grained distinctions would require labels we did not have.

数据本身也限制了我们所能得出的结论。没有样本包含所有四个视图系列，并且 JA4 充当跨源的主要共享视图。因此，该模型在训练期间永远不会看到完全对齐的多视图示例。事实上，该探测器在这些条件下仍然运行良好，这是有希望的，但这意味着我们不能对跨模式理解做出强有力的声明——该模型可能严重依赖 JA4 作为桥梁，而不是学习所有视图系列之间的深层关系。The data itself also limits what we can conclude. No sample contains all four view families, and JA4 serves as the main shared view across sources. The model therefore never sees a fully aligned multi-view example during training. The fact that the probe still works well under these conditions is promising, but it means we cannot make strong claims about cross-modal understanding — the model may be relying heavily on JA4 as a bridge rather than learning deep relationships between all view families.

掩模视图消融支持了这种解释。隐藏 JA4H 或 JA4S 导致的性能下降比隐藏 JA4 或 JA4X 更大。 JA4H和JA4​​S分别携带HTTP端和服务器端TLS信息，它们在结构上与客户端JA4视图不同。当它们被删除时，模型将无法访问无法从剩余视图中轻松恢复的信息。然而，我们无法从一个数据集的单一消融研究中得出视图重要性的明确排名。The masked-view ablations support this interpretation. Hiding JA4H or JA4S caused larger performance drops than hiding JA4 or JA4X. JA4H and JA4S carry HTTP-side and server-side TLS information respectively, which are structurally distinct from the client-side JA4 view. When they are removed, the model loses access to information that cannot be easily recovered from the remaining views. However, we cannot draw a definitive ranking of view importance from a single ablation study on one dataset.

在几何方面，轮廓得分略为负（On the geometry side, the silhouette score is slightly negative (−0.0397-0.0397），这意味着嵌入空间没有形成分离良好的全局簇。 kNN 探针之所以有效，是因为它依赖于局部邻域结构，而不是全局簇边界。因此，该表示对于最近邻分类很有用，但不应将其描述为产生完全分离的簇。), which means the embedding space does not form well-separated global clusters. The kNN probe works because it relies on local neighborhood structure, not on global cluster boundaries. So the representation is useful for nearest-neighbor classification, but it should not be described as producing cleanly separated clusters.

即便如此，该方法还是填补了一个空白。标准指纹识别将每个散列视为固定密钥。数据包级或流级模型需要更重的输入管道。从几个紧凑的指纹视图中学习比第二个更轻，比第一个更丰富。我们尚未测试流量分组或检索等应用程序，但这些自然是下一个目标。Even so, the approach fills a gap. Standard fingerprinting treats each hash as a fixed key. Packet-level or flow-level models need much heavier input pipelines. Learning from several compact fingerprint views is lighter than the second and richer than the first. We have not yet tested applications like traffic grouping or retrieval, but those are natural next targets.

匹配基线问题现在得到了部分解答。在双视图试点语料库上，Section 中的基准The matched-baseline question is now partly answered. On the two-view pilot corpus, the benchmark in Section4.5在一种无泄漏协议下将预测能量信号与频率、最近邻、自动编码器、重建和聚类基线进行头对头比较：预测目标是在两个异常类别上唯一强大的目标，并且其评分成本不会随着语料库的增加而增长。这些结果是针对不同数据的异常评分，因此它们补充而不是取代混合源语料库上的探测任务比较，该比较仍然开放。compares the prediction-energy signal head-to-head against frequency, nearest-neighbour, autoencoder, reconstruction, and clustering baselines under one leakage-free protocol: the predictive objective is the only one strong on both anomaly classes, and its scoring cost does not grow with the corpus. Those results are for anomaly scoring on different data, so they complement rather than replace the probe-task comparison on the mixed-source corpus, which remains open.

除此之外，接下来的两个步骤也很突出。更丰富的标签可以让我们评估在操作上更重要的任务，比如应用程序识别——在试点语料库上，这也是将强大的排名转变为校准操作点所需要的。在更多样本具有重叠视图的数据集上，跨视图学习故事会更加强大，因为目前 JA4 是大多数视图系列之间的唯一桥梁。Beyond that, two next steps stand out. Richer labels would let us evaluate on tasks that matter more operationally, like application identification — on the pilot corpus this is also what turning the strong ranking into a calibrated operating point requires. And the cross-view learning story would be much stronger on a dataset where more samples have overlapping views, since currently JA4 is the only bridge between most view families.

6 结论Conclusion

我们着手测试 JEPA 式的预测学习是否可以从紧凑的 JA4 派生的网络指纹中产生有用的表示。在混合源协议族探测中，冻结嵌入在 TLS、DNS 和 SSH 上实现了强大的 kNN 准确性，并且具有高潜在余弦一致性。基于这些结果，答案是谨慎肯定的：预测潜在目标确实转移到这种结构化网络数据，至少对于我们评估的任务来说是这样。We set out to test whether JEPA-style predictive learning can produce useful representations from compact JA4-derived network fingerprints. On the mixed-source protocol-family probe, the frozen embeddings achieved strong kNN accuracy across TLS, DNS, and SSH with high latent cosine agreement. Based on these results, the answer is cautiously positive: the predictive latent objective does transfer to this kind of structured network data, at least for the task we evaluated.

剩下的差距是探测任务的粗糙度和训练数据中不完整的视图重叠。在生产试点语料库上，Section的基线比较The remaining gaps are the coarseness of the probe task and the incomplete view overlap in the training data. On the production pilot corpus, the baseline comparison of Section4.5解决了匹配基线问题：预测能量是两个合成异常类别上唯一强的信号，并且其得分与语料库大小无关。settled the matched-baseline question: the prediction energy was the only signal strong on both synthetic anomaly classes, and it scores at a cost independent of corpus size.

剩下的就是将相同的比较带回混合源探测任务，一旦存在更丰富的标签，就对更细粒度的任务进行评估，并将强大的排名转化为校准的操作点——后续论文工作的重点。What remains is to carry the same comparison back to the mixed-source probe task, to evaluate on finer-grained tasks once richer labels exist, and to turn the strong ranking into a calibrated operating point — the focus of the follow-up thesis work.
