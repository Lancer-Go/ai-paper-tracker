摘要Abstract

联合预测使客户（例如公司、工厂和生产线）能够协作开发故障时间预测模型，同时保持每个客户的数据本地化和机密性。然而，传统的联合模型通常假设跨客户端的退化过程具有同质性，这一假设在许多工业环境中可能不成立。为了克服这个问题，本文提出了一种个性化的联合预测模型，旨在适应具有异构退化过程的客户，使他们能够构建定制的预测模型。预测模型迭代地促进具有相似退化模式的客户端之间的底层成对协作，从而增强个性化联合学习的性能。为了使用分散数据集联合估计参数，我们开发了一种基于近端梯度下降的联合参数估计算法。所提出的方法通过同时实现模型个性化、保护数据隐私和提供全面的故障时间分布来解决现有联合预测模型的局限性。通过广泛的模拟研究和使用 NASA 存储库中的涡轮风扇发动机退化数据集的案例研究，验证了所提出模型的优越性。Federated prognostics enable clients (e.g., companies, factories, and production lines) to collaboratively develop a failure time prediction model while keeping each client's data local and confidential. However, traditional federated models often assume homogeneity in the degradation processes across clients, an assumption that may not hold in many industrial settings. To overcome this, this paper proposes a personalized federated prognostic model designed to accommodate clients with heterogeneous degradation processes, allowing them to build tailored prognostic models. The prognostic model iteratively facilitates the underlying pairwise collaborations between clients with similar degradation patterns, which enhances the performance of personalized federated learning. To estimate parameters jointly using decentralized datasets, we develop a federated parameter estimation algorithm based on proximal gradient descent. The proposed approach addresses the limitations of existing federated prognostic models by simultaneously achieving model personalization, preserving data privacy, and providing comprehensive failure time distributions. The superiority of the proposed model is validated through extensive simulation studies and a case study using the turbofan engine degradation dataset from the NASA repository.

一、简介1. Introduction

剩余使用寿命 (RUL) 预测是估计组件在发生故障之前可以正常运行的时间量，这在工业中至关重要，因为它在防止计划外停机和优化维护计划方面发挥着重要作用 [1]。 RUL预测方法可以分为模型驱动方法和数据驱动方法[2]。模型驱动方法采用基于物理的模型，需要专业领域知识来构建解析或近似方程，例如描述退化动力学的微分方程或基于应力应变关系的疲劳寿命方程。相比之下，数据驱动方法利用直接从数据中学习的机器学习算法。由于数据驱动方法对潜在故障机制的先验知识要求较少，因此它们可以揭示人类分析师可能难以检测的复杂模式，例如传感器相关性。因此，数据驱动的方法通常优于传统的模型驱动的方法，特别是当退化过程复杂且数据是高维时[3]。在系统预测中，数据驱动的方法通常是通过建立机器学习和统计模型来实现的，这些模型将状态监测传感器信号映射到故障时间 (TTF) [4-6]。Remaining useful life (RUL) prediction is to estimate the amount of time that a component can function properly before failure, which is essential in industry due to its role in preventing unscheduled downtime and optimizing maintenance schedules [1]. Approaches of RUL prediction can be categorized into modeldriven methods and data-driven methods [2]. Model-driven methods employ physics-based models that require expert domain knowledge to build analytical or approximation equations, such as differential equations describing degradation kinetics or fatigue life equations based on stress–strain relationships. In contrast, data-driven methods utilize machine learning algorithms that are learned directly from data. Since data-driven approaches impose fewer requirements on prior knowledge of the underlying failure mechanisms, they can uncover complex patterns, such as sensor correlations, that might be difficult for human analysts to detect. As a result, data-driven methods usually outperform traditional model-driven ones, particularly when degradation processes are complex, and data is high-dimensional [3]. In system prognostics, data-driven approaches are typically achieved by establishing machine learning and statistical models that map condition monitoring sensor signals to their time-to-failure (TTFs) [4-6].

大多数现有的数据驱动方法都假设有大量的历史数据，即退化信号及其相应的故障时间，用于模型训练[7-11]。不幸的是，单个客户或组织拥有的历史故障数据通常非常有限，因此很难独立开发有效的预测模型。一种允许多个分布式客户端联合训练预测模型的简单解决方案是基于云计算的预测。它的工作原理是首先将多个客户端的数据上传到集中式云服务器，然后根据云上的聚合数据训练预测模型。训练后的模型最终分发回客户端进行实时状态监测。然而，它给工业系统和最终客户带来了两个主要挑战。第一个挑战是数据隐私和安全法规[12-15]。大多数数据本质上是高度敏感的，并且由于隐私保护政策和监管合规性而不能简单地转移。许多组织甚至不愿意与云共享他们的数据，因为它可能包含一些专有信息和运营指标。第二个问题是计算和通信资源限制。虽然每个客户端可能只有很少的故障记录，但每条记录都包含在各种运行条件下收集的大量多传感器数据。因此，集中此类数据需要将大量数据传输到服务器，从而导致高昂的通信成本。Most existing data-driven approaches assume there is a large amount of historical data, i.e., degradation signals and their corresponding failure times, for model training [7-11]. Unfortunately, the historical failure data owned by an individual client or organization are often very limited, making it difficult to develop an effective prognostic model independently. One naïve solution that allows multiple distributed clients to jointly train a prognostic model is cloud computing-based prognostics. It works by first uploading the data from multiple clients to a centralized cloud server, then training a prognostic model based on the aggregated data on the cloud. The trained model is finally distributed back to the clients for real-time condition monitoring. However, it introduces two primary challenges for industrial systems and end clients. The first challenge is data privacy and security regulations [12-15]. Most data are highly sensitive in nature, and cannot be simply transferred due to privacy protection policies and regulatory compliance. Many organizations are even not willing to share their data with the cloud since it may contain some proprietary information and operational metrics. The second concern is computational and communication resource constraints. Although each client may have only a few failure records, each record contains a large amount of multi-sensor data collected under various operating conditions. Consequently, centralizing such data involves transmitting substantial data volumes to the server, leading to high communication costs.

为了克服这些限制，基于 FL 的预测越来越受欢迎。 FL 是一种分布式学习范例，允许多个数据所有者（从个人移动设备到整个组织）以保护隐私的方式集体训练他们的模型 [16]。当前的许多 FL 训练方法都源于[17]中引入的联合平均（FedAvg）算法。To overcome these limitations, FL-based prognostics has gained increasing popularity. FL is a distributed learning paradigm that allows multiple data owners, ranging from individual mobile devices to entire organizations, to collectively train their models in a privacy-preserving manner [16]. Numerous current FL training methods originate from the federated averaging (FedAvg) algorithm introduced in [17].

它包括两个步骤，包括本地训练和全局聚合。首先，客户可以利用本地的隔离数据来训练机器学习模型。然后，不传输原始数据，而是仅将模型更新发送回服务器以合并为单个全局模型。由于数据在训练过程中不直接共享，因此有效解决了数据隐私和安全方面的常见挑战。因此，对于由于监管限制而无法集中数据的场景，基于 FL 的预测是一个有价值的选择。它允许数据有限的多个组织在不共享原始数据的情况下联合训练 RUL 预测模型，广泛应用于航空、汽车和半导体领域。此外，FL 的通信效率也很高，因为仅传输模型参数，其大小比原始数据小得多。It consists of two steps, including local training and global aggregation. First, clients are facilitated to train machine-learning models with their isolated data locally. Then, instead of transferring the raw data, only the model updates are sent back to the server to be combined as a single global model. Since data is not directly shared during the training process, it effectively addresses common challenges in data privacy and security. Thus, FL-based prognostics is a valuable choice for scenarios where data cannot be centralized due to regulatory constraints. It allows multiple organizations with limited data to jointly train a model for RUL prediction without sharing their raw data, which is widely applied in aviation, automotive, and semiconductors. Moreover, FL is also communication efficient as only the model parameters are transmitted, which are considerably smaller in size than raw data.

最近的研究在开发基于 FL 的预测模型方面做出了一些努力，然而，大多数现有模型假设客户端之间的数据同质性，这意味着所有客户端都被认为在相同的条件下操作相同类型的设备和组件 [5,6,18-22]。在实际应用中，这种假设通常不成立，因为客户端数据通常是异构的。例如，两个客户可能使用不同但功能相似类型的轴承。在另一种情况下，客户可能采用相同的轴承类型，但运行条件有所不同，例如，一个客户的设备可能以 1600 rpm 运行，而另一个客户的设备可能以 2000 rpm 运行。在这种异构场景中，底层降级过程可能具有相似的特征，但在客户端之间并不严格相同。因此，现有预后模型的性能由于违反同质性假设而受到损害。Recent studies have made some efforts on developing FL-based prognostic models, however, most existing models assume data homogeneity across clients, meaning that all clients are considered to operate the same type of equipment and components under identical conditions [5, 6, 18-22]. In real-world applications, this assumption often does not hold, as client data are frequently heterogeneous. For example, two clients may utilize different but functionally similar types of bearings. In another case, clients may employ the same bearing type, but the operational conditions vary, for instance, one client's equipment may operate at 1600 rpm, while another's runs at 2000 rpm. In such heterogeneous scenarios, the underlying degradation processes may share similar characteristics but are not strictly identical across clients. Thus, the performance of existing prognostic models is compromised due to the violation of the homogeneity assumption.

一些研究试图通过开发在联合预后模型中明确解释客户之间差异的方法来解决客户级异质性的挑战[23-29]。例如，陈等人。 [23] 在 FL 框架中采用深度神经网络来学习共享的全局健康退化表示以及特定于客户的模型头​​，以进行个性化 RUL 预测。卢等人。 [29]提出了一种用于锂离子电池 RUL 预测的具有知识蒸馏的异构联合迁移学习方法，其中服务器在公共数据上训练设备定制的网络，设备通过交换蒸馏的见解来协作完善它们。然而，这些方法仍然存在一些常见的局限性。首先，几乎所有这些都是基于深度学习，而统计学习模型却被忽视了。基于深度学习的预测模型涉及大量需要训练的参数，这对于计算能力有限的边缘客户端来说可能非常耗时且不可行。此外，基于深度学习的方法需要大量的训练数据，而收集足够的故障数据很困难，即使汇集多个组织的数据也是如此。相比之下，统计学习模型通常更高效、可解释，更适合数据有限和计算能力有限的场景。其次，当前考虑异质性的基于 FL 的预测模型只能提供 RUL 预测的点估计，而不是故障时间分布。Several studies have attempted to address the challenges of client-level heterogeneity by developing methods that explicitly account for variations across clients in federated prognostic modeling [23-29]. For example, Chen et al. [23] employed deep neural networks in an FL framework to learn a shared global health degradation representation alongside client-specific model heads for a personalized RUL prediction. Lu et al. [29] proposed a heterogeneous federated transfer learning method with knowledge distillation for lithium‐ion battery RUL prediction, where the server trains device‐tailored networks on public data, and devices collaboratively refine them by exchanging distilled insights. However, these methods still suffer from some common limitations. First, almost all of them are based on deep learning, while statistical learning models are being ignored. Deep learning-based prognostic models involve a large number of parameters to be trained, which can be time-consuming and infeasible for edge clients with limited computing power. Besides, deep learning-based methods require a large volume of training data, while collecting sufficient failure data is difficult, even when pooling data from multiple organizations. In contrast, statistical learning models are often more efficient, interpretable, and better suited for scenarios with limited data and restricted computation capacity. Second, current FL-based prognostic models considering heterogeneity can only provide a point estimate of the RUL prediction instead of a failure time distribution.

然而，许多决策任务，例如维护计划和库存优化，需要预测故障时间的分布，而不是单点估计。如果没有这一点，将提议的模型集成到操作流程中仍然具有挑战性。However, many decision-making tasks, such as maintenance scheduling and inventory optimization, require a distribution of predicted failure times rather than a single point estimate. Without this, integrating proposed models into operational processes remains challenging.

为了应对这些挑战，本研究提出了一种专为具有异构数据的客户设计的个性化联合预后模型。所提出的方法可以实现结构相似但针对个别客户量身定制的特定于客户的预测模型。每个客户端都根据自己的数据训练其本地模型，并在训练过程中从其他类似模型中借用有用的信息，同时保留其独特的特征。这是通过为每个客户训练（对数）位置尺度（LLS）回归模型并鼓励客户之间的回归系数相似但不相同来实现的。 LLS回归是一种回归模型，它扩展了传统的线性回归模型，适应响应变量可能表现出偏度或异方差的场景[30]。它假设响应变量遵循（对数）位置尺度系列的分布，例如正态、对数正态、物流、对数物流、小极值 (SEV) 和威布尔。当响应变量是从正态分布、逻辑分布或 SEV 分布中采样时，该模型称为位置尺度回归。当响应变量服从对数正态分布、对数逻辑分布或威布尔分布时，称为对数位置尺度回归。由于它涵盖了工业应用中工程资产的大部分故障时间分布，因此这种灵活性使其在预测中特别有用。另一个关键优势是它能够提供预测故障时间的分布而不是点估计。此功能对于预测性维护和库存控制等应用中的明智决策特别有价值。 LLS回归中的参数可以使用最大似然估计（MLE）来估计，相当于通过最小化负对数似然函数来求解。为了实现跨客户端的回归模型相似但不相同的目标，我们制定了一个优化问题，该问题有两个组成部分：第一个是每个客户端的损失函数的总和，第二个是正则化项，用于惩罚模型参数与其他客户端差异太大的客户端。通过一起解决这两个组件，组合目标最终与最佳解决方案一致，并在客户之间具有更好的泛化能力。To address these challenges, this study proposes a personalized federated prognostic model designed for clients with heterogeneous data. The proposed approach enables client-specific prognostic models that are structurally similar yet tailored to individual clients. Each client trains its local model on its own data, and borrows useful information from other similar models during training while preserving its unique characteristics. This is achieved by training a (log)-location-scale (LLS) regression model for each client and encouraging the regression coefficients to be similar but not identical across clients. LLS regression is a type of regression model that extends the conventional linear regression model, accommodating the scenarios where the response variable may exhibit skewness or heteroscedasticity [30]. It assumes the response variable follows a distribution from the (log)-location-scale family, such as normal, log-normal, logistics, log-logistics, small extreme value (SEV), and Weibull. When the response variable is sampled from a normal, logistic, or SEV distribution, the model is termed as location-scale regression. When the response variable follows a log-normal, log-logistic, or Weibull distribution, it is termed as log-locationscale regression. Since it covers most of the failure time distributions of engineering assets in industrial applications, this flexibility makes it particularly useful in prognostics. Another key advantage is its ability to provide a distribution of the predicted failure time rather than a point estimate. This feature is particularly valuable for informed decision-making in applications such as predictive maintenance and inventory control. The parameters in LLS regression can be estimated using maximum likelihood estimation (MLE), equivalently, it is solved by minimizing the negative log-likelihood function. To realize the goal that the regression models are similar but not identical across clients, we formulate an optimization problem that has two components: the first is the summation of the loss function of each client, and the second is a regularization term that penalizes the clients whose model parameters are diverse too much from other clients. By solving the two components together, the combined objective eventually coincides with the optimal solution with better generalization across clients.

2. 联合预测模型开发2. Federated Prognostic Model Development

在本节中，我们将介绍所提出的联合预测模型的开发，该模型旨在解决分布式客户端之间的数据异构性。在实际工业场景中，每个客户在不同的环境和负载条件下操作机器，导致性质相似但不相同的降解过程。依靠单个共享的全局模型来代表所有客户端通常无法捕获其局部变化，从而导致预测性能不佳。另一方面，训练独立的本地模型保留了客户特定的特征，但受到数据可用性有限的影响，因此很难在不同的操作条件下实现可靠​​的 RUL 预测。为了克服这些问题，本文提出了一种个性化的联合预测模型，该模型可以由多个异构客户端协作构建，允许每个客户端通过利用来自相似客户端的信息来开发定制的预测模型，同时保留数据隐私。在接下来的段落中，我们将首先介绍每个客户端本地预测模型的制定，然后引入一个优化问题，允许所有客户端以保护隐私的方式共同估计其模型参数。In this section, we will introduce the development of the proposed federated prognostic model, which is designed to address data heterogeneity across distributed clients. In practical industrial scenarios, each client operates machines under distinct environmental and loading conditions, leading to degradation processes that are similar in nature but not identical. Relying on a single shared global model to represent all clients often fails to capture their local variations, which results in suboptimal prediction performance. On the other hand, training independent local models preserves client-specific characteristics but suffers from limited data availability, making it difficult to achieve reliable RUL predictions under diverse operating conditions. To overcome these, this article proposes a personalized federated prognostic model that can be collaboratively constructed by multiple heterogeneous clients, allowing each client to develop a tailored prognostic model by leveraging information from similar clients while preserving data privacy. In the following paragraphs, we will first introduce the formulation of each client’s local prognostic model, and then introduce an optimization problem that allows all clients to jointly estimate their model parameters in a privacy-preserving manner.

The TTFs are then mapped to the features extracted by splines using the statistical learning model. 七、结论7. Conclusions

本文介绍了一种个性化联合预测框架，专为客户端表现出异构退化行为的环境而定制。与假定客户之间的一致性的传统 FL 模型不同，我们的方法允许每个客户构建定制的预后模型，同时仍然受益于This paper introduces a personalized federated prognostic framework tailored for settings where clients exhibit heterogeneous degradation behaviors. Unlike traditional FL models that assume uniformity across clients, our approach allows each client to build a customized prognostic model while still benefiting from

协作学习。通过促进具有相似退化模式的客户端之间的选择性信息共享，该模型促进了成对协作，从而提高了模型性能。为了在保持数据机密性的同时估计参数，我们进一步提出了一种基于近端梯度下降的联合参数估计算法。该算法在两个协调步骤之间交替：鼓励相似客户端之间参数对齐的协作梯度更新，以及使用基于私有客户端数据的近端映射的局部细化步骤。我们已经证明，这些步骤可以以联合方式应用，从而允许跨多个客户端进行分散优化。collaborative learning. By promoting selective information sharing among clients with similar degradation patterns, the model facilitates pairwise collaborations that enhance the model performance. To estimate parameters while keeping the data confidential, we further propose a federated parameter estimation algorithm based on proximal gradient descent. The algorithm alternates between two coordinated steps: a collaborative gradient update that encourages parameter alignment among similar clients, and a local refinement step using proximal mapping based on private client data. We have proved that these steps can be applied in a federated manner, allowing for decentralized optimization across multiple clients.

我们的方法通过同时实现模型个性化、保护数据隐私和提供全面的故障时间分布，提供了优于其他替代方案的几个关键优势，特别是在数据有限和资源受限的场景中。对模拟数据集和 NASA 涡扇发动机退化数据集的综合验证证明了所提出方法的有效性。结果表明，在低异质性和高异质性下，其性能均优于本地训练模型和传统 FL 方法。值得注意的是，训练样本较少的客户可以从我们模型的个性化结构中受益匪浅。通过利用更丰富的数据实现客户的知识转移，该方法有助于减轻由于数据不平衡而通常出现的性能差异，最终导致整个客户网络的预测性能更加公平和可靠。Our approach offers several key advantages over other alternatives by simultaneously realizing model personalization, preserving data privacy, and providing comprehensive failure time distributions, especially in scenarios with limited data and constrained resources. Comprehensive validation on both simulated datasets and the NASA turbofan engine degradation dataset demonstrates the effectiveness of the proposed approach. The results have shown an improved performance over local training models and traditional FL methods, under both low and high heterogeneity. Notably, clients with fewer training samples benefit significantly from the personalized structure of our model. By enabling knowledge transfer from clients with richer data, the approach helps mitigate performance disparities that commonly arise due to data imbalance, ultimately leading to more equitable and reliable prognostic performance across the client network.
