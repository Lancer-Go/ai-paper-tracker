摘要Abstract

标准自动化投资回报率忽略了影响长期组织绩效的四类系统性风险——隐性知识侵蚀、弹性降低、监管风险和社会制度资本退化。 PHP-AIO（人工智能优化组织中的人类保护协议）是一种五门顺序决策协议，具有最终的综合检查，可在角色级别量化这些未定价的系统性风险并生成可审计的自动化决策。封闭式自动化债务措施（Standard automation ROI misses four categories of systemic risk—tacit knowledge erosion, resilience reduction, regulatory exposure, and socio-institutional capital degradation—that affect long-term organizational performance. PHP-AIO (Protocol for Human Preservation in AI-Optimized Organizations) is a five-gate sequential decision protocol with a final composite check that quantifies these unpriced systemic risks at the role level and produces auditable automation decisions. 1 问题：自动化的盲点The Problem: Automation’s Blind Spot

PHP-AIO 是一种五门决策协议，可量化自动化决策中四类未定价的系统性风险，并产生可审计的结果（自动化、保留、增强或混合），每个结果都具有规定的治理操作。PHP-AIO is a five-gate decision protocol that quantifies four categories of unpriced systemic risk in automation decisions and produces auditable outcomes—automate, preserve, augment, or hybrid—each with prescribed governance actions.

该协议解决了组织如何决定自动化的结构性盲点。标准 ROI 模型针对每笔交易成本、处理时间和错误率进行优化The protocol addresses a structural blind spot in how organizations decide to automate. Standard ROI models optimize for cost per transaction, processing time, and error rates[4, 1]。他们忽略了四个长期成本：几十年来积累的隐性知识的不可逆转的损失、缓冲人工智能故障的组织冗余的减少、司法管辖区强制人类监督而增加的监管风险，以及金融机构所依赖的公共信任资本的侵蚀. They miss four long-term costs: the irreversible loss of tacit knowledge accumulated over decades, the reduction of organizational redundancy that buffers AI failures, the rising regulatory exposure as jurisdictions mandate human oversight, and the erosion of the public trust capital that financial institutions depend on[5].

我们将这四种成本纳入一个单一的组织原则：We frame these four costs under a single organising principle:自动化主权automation sovereignty——随着人工智能能力的进步，组织对其后续决策保留战略自主权的能力。我们在本文中引入自动化主权一词作为四个风险维度的组织框架；它在之前的工作中没有出现在这个标签下，但它巩固了 Mittelstadt 的原则与实践差距中已经存在的担忧—an organisation’s capacity to retain strategic autonomy over its consequential decisions as AI capabilities advance. We introduce the term automation sovereignty in this paper as an organising frame for the four risk dimensions; it does not appear under this label in prior work, but it consolidates concerns already present in Mittelstadt’s principles-to-practice gap[21]以及瓦格纳对自动化决策系统下责任转变的分析and Wagner’s analysis of accountability shifts under automated decision systems[39]。部分的自动化债务结构. 我们将这四类称为系统性风险。学者们记录了隐性知识的侵蚀We call these four categories systemic risks. Scholars have documented tacit-knowledge erosion[2, 7]、自动化导致的自满和对算法建议的过度依赖, automation-induced complacency and overreliance on algorithmic recommendations[27, 6, 8]、组织脆性, organizational brittleness[28, 37]，以及人工智能伦理中的原则与实践差距, and the principles-to-practice gap in AI ethics[14, 21]。最近的调查巩固了人机交互设计空间. Recent surveys consolidate the human-in-the-loop design space[22]。差距不是意识，而是操作：没有正式的协议将这些风险纳入自动化决策本身。现有治理框架——美国NIST人工智能风险管理框架. The gap is not awareness, it is operational: no formal protocol incorporates these risks into the automation decision itself. Existing governance frameworks—the US NIST AI Risk Management Framework[24]、欧盟人工智能法案, the EU AI Act[13, 38]和人机交互 (HITL) 监督分类法——在部署承诺决策后参与。没有人决定是否自动化。此外，社会技术学术警告称，当系统设计嵌入错误的抽象时，仅靠程序性的人类监督和技术合规性并不能保证有意义的控制, and human-in-the-loop (HITL) oversight taxonomies—engage after the deployment-commitment decision. None decide whether to automate. Sociotechnical scholarship moreover warns that procedural human oversight and technical compliance alone do not guarantee meaningful control when system design embeds the wrong abstractions[33, 39, 15, 35].

2 未定价风险的四个维度Four Dimensions of Unpriced Risk

四个风险维度中的每一个都反映了自动化可能损害组织绩效的独特机制：TKE 捕获了不可逆转的判断力损失，一旦角色被删除，就无法重新编码； RR 捕获自动化系统共享故障模式时引入的相关脆弱性；如果后来监管要求人工监督，RE 可以捕获逆转自动化的期权价值成本； SCD 捕获信托资本的消耗，从而使后续决策得以执行。Each of the four risk dimensions captures a distinct mechanism through which automation can damage organizational performance: TKE captures the irreversible loss of judgment that cannot be re-encoded once a role is removed; RR captures the correlated fragility introduced when automated systems share failure modes; RE captures the option-value cost of reversing automation if regulation later mandates human oversight; and SCD captures the depletion of the trust capital that makes consequential decisions enforceable.

7 结论Conclusion

PHP-AIO 将四维风险概况（隐性知识侵蚀、弹性降低、监管暴露和社会机构资本退化）正式化为确定性五门协议，在组织承诺部署之前产生可审计的自动化/保留/增强/混合决策。它不是 NIST AI RMF、欧盟人工智能法案合格评估、FAccT 式影响评估或部署前风险概况披露标准的竞争对手PHP-AIO formalises a four-dimension risk profile—tacit-knowledge erosion, resilience reduction, regulatory exposure, and socio-institutional capital degradation—as a deterministic five-gate protocol that yields an auditable automate / preserve / augment / hybrid decision before an organisation commits to deployment. It is not a competitor to NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act conformity assessment, FAccT-style impact assessments, or pre-deployment risk-profile disclosure standards[34]——一旦存在部署候选者，所有这些都会起作用——但它们是上游的补充：预决策过滤器，其输出是版本化配置加上逐个门的分数，而不是叙述。—all of which act once a deployment candidate exists—but a complement upstream of them: a pre-decision filter whose output is a versioned configuration plus a gate-by-gate score, not a narrative.

确定性评分和可选的法学硕士协助之间的分离使得协议可以根据《欧盟人工智能法案》可追溯性要求进行审核（第 12 条记录保存）The separation between deterministic scoring and optional LLM assistance is what makes the protocol auditable under EU AI Act traceability requirements (Art. 12 record-keeping)[13]和 NIST AI RMF 可追溯性指南（测量功能）and NIST AI RMF traceability guidance (Measure function)[24]：无论模型、提示或运行时间如何，相同的输入都会产生相同的决策，并且每个配置更改都是带有时间戳、批准者签名的工件。每个领域的权重、阈值和国家乘数的可配置性使每个机构能够根据自己的风险偏好进行重新调整，本文中的金融服务配置作为 v1 专家引出的基线而不是通用默认值提供。: identical inputs yield identical decisions regardless of model, prompt, or runtime, and every configuration change is a timestamped, approver-signed artefact. Per-domain configurability of weights, thresholds, and country multipliers lets each institution recalibrate to its own risk appetite, with the financial-services configuration in this paper offered as a v1 expert-elicited baseline rather than a universal default.

目前正在对记录在案的自动化决策进行回顾性验证；加上医疗保健和公共管理的跨行业校准以及从累积评估中学习漂移率，这三项工作将使 PHP-AIO 从 v1 治理协议转向特定于机构的、经证实的风险模型。自动化决策影响重大，不能仅考虑节省成本； PHP-AIO 是作为确保这种情况的一种方法而提供的。A retrospective validation against documented automation decisions is currently underway; together with cross-industry calibration for healthcare and public administration and learning drift rates from accumulated assessments, these are the three lines of work that would move PHP-AIO from a v1 governance protocol toward an institution-specific, evidenced risk model.
