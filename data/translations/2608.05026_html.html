该框架还引发了对人类与人工智能共同进化的更广泛讨论This framework also raises a broader discussion on Human-AI coevolution(coevolution; evoAgents; coevolution2)。 虽然当前的研究通常侧重于人工智能向通用智能的自主进化，但我们的工作提出了另一种轨迹：人类专业知识和机器智能协同发展的相互进化过程。 我们没有将人工智能视为一个独立的进化实体，而是主张一种范式，其中交互本身充当集体智慧的引擎。 例如，在教育环境中，人工智能可以充当讲师，通过提供解释、反馈和练习来加速学习者的技能习得，同时可以捕获学习者解决问题的过程和反馈，以改进人工智能的教学策略和知识组织。 这样，人类的学习和人工智能能力的提升就可以形成一个相辅相成的循环。. While current research often focuses on autonomous AI evolution toward general intelligence, our work suggests an alternative trajectory: a reciprocal evolutionary process where human expertise and machine intelligence grow in tandem. Instead of viewing AI as a self-contained evolving entity, we argue for a paradigm where the interaction itself serves as the engine for collective intelligence. For example, in educational settings, AI can act as an instructor, accelerating learners’ skill acquisition by providing explanations, feedback, and exercises, while learners’ problem-solving processes and feedback can be captured to improve the AI’s teaching strategies and knowledge organization. In this way, human learning and AI capability improvement can form a mutually reinforcing loop.

6.3. 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

虽然所提出的系统为协作式人类人工智能注释任务提供了一种强大的方法，但存在一些限制，应在未来的迭代中解决这些限制，以增强其适应性、效率和性能。While the proposed system offers a robust approach for collaborative Human-AI annotation tasks, several limitations exist that should be addressed in future iterations to enhance its adaptability, efficiency, and performance.

用户交互缺乏个性化和灵活性。Lack of Personalization and Flexibility in User Interaction.当前的系统采用主动推荐方法，该方法构建图像的顺序和分组。虽然这种设计有利于一组用户，但其他人可能更喜欢根据自己的节奏和工作流程浏览图像。 这将使系统更能适应个人用户的需求和使用习惯，通过提供更加个性化和响应迅速的交互模型来增强整体用户体验。The current system adopts a proactive recommendation approach, which structures the order and grouping of images. While this design benefits a group of users, others may prefer to browse images according to their own pace and workflow. This would make the system more adaptive to individual user needs and usage habits, enhancing the overall user experience by providing a more personalized and responsive interaction model.

技能可靠性和注释者偏差。Skill Reliability and Annotator Bias.该系统依赖于预设的算法（The system relies on preset algorithms (algorithm 1）和 代理更新和合并技能的自主决策，但确实 尚未验证注释质量。 由于交互序列没有正确性标记，因此存在噪声 诸如贴错标签之类的行为可能会传播到学到的技能中，而 故意保留用户之间相互冲突的解释 保留解释多样性。未来的版本应该增加质量 保证，例如对生成的技能进行专家审查。 ArtAnno 中的技能是从人类注释中提炼出来的，因此 系统继承了注释者的偏见。在墨西哥海报案中， 系统默认为西方“万圣节”读数，已更正 仅仅因为用户注意到异常；如果它被忽视的话， 有偏见的技能将被保留和重用，从而传播 错误。 因此，未来的工作应该增加偏差检测， 每项技能的置信度估计以及记录的来源跟踪 每项技能的起源地和通过专家控制其重用的地方 审查。) and the agent’s autonomous decisions to update and merge skills, but does not yet validate annotation quality. Since interaction sequences carry no correctness markers, noisy actions such as mislabeling may propagate into learned skills, while conflicting interpretations across users are intentionally retained to preserve interpretive diversity. Future versions should add quality assurance, e.g., expert review of generated skills. Skills in ArtAnno are distilled from human annotations, so the system inherits its annotators’ biases. In the Mexican-poster case, the system defaulted to a Western “Halloween” reading, corrected only because the user noticed the anomaly; had it gone unnoticed, the biased skill would have been retained and reused, propagating the error. Future work should therefore add bias detection, per-skill confidence estimation, and provenance tracking that records where each skill originates and gates its reuse through expert review.

多个标签的注释框架的灵活性有限。Limited Flexibility in Annotation Framework for Multiple Labels.目前的系统使用相对固定的标注框架，无法处理单个对象需要多个标签的情况。 例如，在中国画中，符号和元素可能有多种解释。 该系统的未来版本应该允许对对象进行更灵活和适应性更强的标记，例如多标签注释，从而更好地适应不同的艺术形式和背景。The current system uses a relatively fixed annotation framework, which cannot handle situations where a single object requires multiple labels. For example, in Traditional Chinese Painting, symbols and elements may have multiple interpretations. Future versions of the system should allow for more flexible and adaptable tagging of objects, such as multi-label annotation, thus better accommodating diverse art forms and contexts.

缺乏大规模的纵向测试。Lack of Large-Scale, Longitudinal Testing.当前系统仅在受控的实验室环境中进行了评估。 未来的工作应该对不同的用户和更大的数据集进行纵向评估，以评估可扩展性、可用性和长期可靠性，特别是技能和知识库如何随着扩展使用而发展和适应。The current system has been evaluated only in controlled in-lab environments. Future work should conduct longitudinal evaluations with diverse users and larger datasets to assess scalability, usability, and long-term reliability, particularly how the skill and knowledge base evolves and adapts over extended use.

7. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了解决高效且知识密集型艺术品注释的持续挑战In this paper, we address the persistent challenge of efficient and knowledge-intensive artwork annotation by proposingBiHAA，一个双向人类人工智能辅助框架，将人工智能支持的任务完成与人类交互驱动的系统进化结合起来。 在形成性发现的指导下，我们设计并实施了 ArtAnno，这是一个多代理系统，它提供主动帮助，同时通过技能库和知识库不断捕获和重用人类专业知识。 我们的用户研究和案例研究结果表明，ArtAnno 不仅提高了标注效率 还可以实现两个领域知识的积累和重用 以及跨交互回合的程序策略 会议。 更广泛地说，这项工作表明艺术品注释系统应该超越单向协助，转向人类和人工智能不断相互增强的协作范式。, a bidirectional Human-AI assistance framework that unifies AI-supported task completion with human-interaction-driven system evolution. Guided by formative findings, we designed and implemented ArtAnno, a multi-agent system that provides proactive assistance while continuously capturing and reusing human expertise through a Skill Library and Knowledge Base. Our results from user study and case study show that ArtAnno not only improves annotation efficiency but also enables the accumulation and reuse of both domain knowledge and procedural strategies across interaction rounds within a session. More broadly, this work suggests that artwork annotation systems should move beyond one-way assistance toward a collaborative paradigm in which humans and AI continuously enhance one another.
