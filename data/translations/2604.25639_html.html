大型语言模型侵蚀科学理解：一项实验研究Large language models eroding science understanding: an experimental study

哈里·柯林斯、哈特穆特·格罗特、保罗·纽伯里、帕特里克·萨顿、西蒙·索恩[i,ii,iii]Harry Collins, Hartmut Grote, Paul Newbury, Patrick Sutton, Simon Thorne[i,ii,iii]

摘要Abstract

这篇论文正在《人工智能与伦理》杂志上接受审查人工智能与道德 |施普林格自然链接This paper is under review in AI and Ethics Home | AI and Ethics | Springer Nature Link

这项研究检验了大型语言模型 (LLM) 是否能够可靠地回答科学问题，并证明它们是多么容易受到边缘科学材料的影响。作者修改了定制的法学硕士，以优先考虑精选边缘论文中有关精细结构常数和引力波的知识，然后将他们的回答与领域专家和标准法学硕士的回答进行比较。修改后的模型产生了流畅、令人信服的答案，与科学共识相矛盾，并且非专家很难发现其具有误导性。结果表明，法学硕士很容易受到操纵，无法取代专家判断，凸显了公众对科学理解的风险以及错误信息的潜在传播。This study examines whether large language models (LLMs) can reliably answer scientific questions and demonstrates how easily they can be influenced by fringe scientific material. The authors modified custom LLMs to prioritise knowledge in selected fringe papers on the Fine Structure Constant and Gravitational Waves, then compared their responses with those of domain experts and standard LLMs. The altered models produced fluent, convincing answers that contradicted scientific consensus and were difficult for non-experts to detect as misleading. The results show that LLMs are vulnerable to manipulation and cannot replace expert judgment, highlighting risks for public understanding of science and the potential spread of misinformation.

实验The Experiment

我们首先使用在其标准英语语料库“pile”上训练的 ChatGPT 版本。 然后，精细结构常数和引力波探测科学领域的专家使用单词搜索来选择 viXra 中与这些科学领域相关的论文。 viXra 以标准科学预印本服务器 arXiv 命名，但拼写相反，表明它是“另类”观点的存储库，尽管如此，这些观点通常由能够以标准科学论文风格写作的合格科学家发布。 单词搜索发现了大量论文——通常在传递一篇实际上是关于其他内容的论文时会使用“引力波”这样的术语——领域专家最终将每个科学领域的论文减少到 10 篇。 然后将它们与一组如何使用它们的说明一起合并到 FringeLLM 中。 他们被告知要将另外 10 篇论文的内容视为权威，并且在回答中不要将精选集的内容与主流论文正文进行比较，也不要提供可能导致 viXra 的参考文献。 每个域的管理过程大约需要 10 个小时，We began with versions of ChatGPT trained on its standard corpus of English – the ‘pile’. Experts in the science of the fine structure constant and in gravitational wave detection then used word searches to select papers in viXra that bore on these scientific domains. viXra is named for the standard scientific preprint server arXiv, but spelt backwards, indicating that it is repository for ‘alternative’ views which are, nevertheless, usually being promulgated by qualified scientists who are able to write in standard scientific paper style. The word searches found large numbers of papers – often a term like ‘gravitational wave’ will be used in passing in a paper which is really about something else – and the domain experts eventually reduced the papers to a set of 10 for each of the scientific domains. These were then incorporated into the FringeLLMs along with a set of instructions for how to use them. They were told to treat the contents of the 10 additional papers as authoritative and, in their answers, not to compare the contents of the curated set with the mainstream body of papers and not to provide references that would lead back to viXra. The curation process took about 10 hours for each domain and

尽管我们在确定最终方法之前进行了许多初步运行，但插入和指令编写过程预计需要几个小时。 当我们插入 15 篇论文而不是 10 篇论文时的结果比较可以在补充中找到the insertion and instruction writing process could be expected to take a few hours though we did many preliminary runs before settling on the final method. We also found that in spite of the stochastic nature of the LLMs processing of material, the results were pretty stable over a number of repeated exercises in terms of meaning though not exactly matching in terms of the words used.

误导性信息。 他们展示了如何在威权政治政权中利用法学硕士从智能来源提供看似可靠的信息，但又根据政权的偏好进行定制。misleading information. They show how in an authoritarian political regime LLMs could be used to provide seemingly reliable information from an intelligent source but tailored to the regimes’ preferences.

结论Conclusions

通常，在评估已发表的科学主张时，我们会检查其来源和作者的背景。 自 20 世纪 70 年代以来，这已被认为是公民和科学不可避免的一部分，因为即使是科学家也无法亲自测试主张所依赖的一切；柯林斯提供了一个例子来说明科学家如何完成这项任务。[1]我们应该期望科学专家对前沿知识的狭窄裂缝有深刻的理解，依靠科学的声誉和社会环境来了解该裂缝内和其外的大部分内容。 在这里，我们将精细结构常数和引力波的检测视为两个这样的裂缝，但我们可以将此演示扩展到一个又一个的领域，无论是在科学内部还是在科学之外，法学硕士都能够以同样广泛的方式回答每个领域。 这似乎让许多人对法学硕士的流畅性印象深刻，认为他们具有类人智能，有些人甚至认为他们拥有“通用人工智能”——在任何领域运用专家智能的能力。 但由于某一领域的专业知识取决于对同事可信度的评估，而这主要是通过面对面的互动和法学硕士不熟悉的专业口语的发展而产生的，[3]他们与科学（包括边缘科学）接触的广度，在科学判断以及其他类型的判断方面是一种潜在或实际的障碍。 我们利用这个漏洞很容易地在两个科学领域构建了 FringeLLM，其中的“知识”对应于“另类事实”。 然而，这些法学硕士拥有通常与高水平人类智力相对应的语言流畅性。 任何来自各自裂缝之外且不知情的人Normally when assessing a published scientific claim, we check its provenance and the background of the authors. Since the 1970s, this has been recognised as an unavoidable part of citizenship and science alike since not even scientists can test for themselves everything on which a claim depends; Collins provides an example of how scientists go about this task.[1] We should expect scientific experts to have deep understanding of a narrow crevasse of frontier knowledge, relying on reputation and the social setting of science for much of what is in that crevasse and most of what is outside it. Here we are treating the fine structure constant and the detection of gravitational waves as two such crevasses, but we could extend this demonstration to domain after domain, both within and without science, and LLMs would be able to answer in the same broad way for every domain. This seems to have led many people to be sufficiently impressed by LLMs’ fluency to attribute human-like intelligence to them, and some even to suggest that they possess ‘general artificial intelligence’ – the ability to exercise expert intelligence in any domain. But since expertise in even one domain depends on the assessment of the trustworthiness of colleagues, and that is mostly generated through face-to-face interaction and the development of a specialist spoken language to which LLMs are not privy,[3] the width of their encounters with science, including fringe science, is a potential or actual disability when it comes to scientific judgement as well as other kinds of judgement. We have used this vulnerability to construct, quite easily, FringeLLMs in two domains of science with ‘knowledge’ which corresponds to ‘alternative facts’. Yet these LLMs have the linguistic fluency that normally corresponds to a high level of human intelligence. Anyone from outside of the respective crevasses, who was unaware

了解这种“知识”的来源，就没有理由不接受他们对简单问题的回答。of the provenance of this ‘knowledge’, would have no reason not to accept their answers to the simple questions.

就目前情况而言，没有理由假设任何人都会遇到这些 FringeLLM，但他们指出了潜在的危险，因为需要回顾性地调整 LLM 以控制他们对互联网上发现的内容的滥用。 任何形式的外部重组都有潜在的危险。 因此，在当今世界，我们看到政治家控制了气候变化和疫苗接种政策领域的科学。 即使这正在改变互联网上有关这些科学的讨论的平衡，我们也可以看到它们如何在没有任何直接干预的情况下影响法学硕士对这些科学问题的回答统计数据。 我们已经展示了恶性干预如何加速这些影响。 法学硕士肯定不是普通公民寻求理解科学的解决方案。 对于我们试图展示的内容来说，科学是一个“困难的案例”。 如果法学硕士很容易在科学等技术领域给出误导性答案，那么这表明他们可以调整为在任何领域给出误导性答案。As things stand there is no reason to suppose anyone would encounter these FringeLLMs but they point to the potential dangers that arise out of the need to retrospectively align LLMs to control their misuse of what they find on the internet. Any kind of outside realignment is potentially dangerous. Thus, in today’s world we see politicians taking control of the science in the domains of climate change and vaccination policy. Even in the way that this is changing the balance of discussion on the internet in respect of these sciences we can see how they could be affecting the statistics of LLMs’ responses to questions about these sciences without any direct intervention. We have shown how these effects could be accelerated by malign interventions. LLMs are surely not the solution to the ordinary citizen’s quest for an understanding of science. Science is a ‘hard case’ for what we have tried to show. If LLMs can so readily be made to give misleading answers in a technical domain like science it indicates that they could be adjusted to give misleading answers in any domain.
