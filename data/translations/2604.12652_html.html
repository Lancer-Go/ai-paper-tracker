We propose PromptEcho, a reward construction method that requiresno注释和annotation andno奖励模型训练。给定生成的图像和引导查询，PromptEcho 计算以原始提示作为标签的冻结 VLM 的标记级交叉熵损失，直接提取在 VLM 预训练期间编码的图像文本对齐知识。奖励是确定性的、计算效率高的，并且随着更强大的开源 VLM 的出现而自动提高。为了进行评估，我们开发了 DenseAlignBench，这是一个概念丰富的密集字幕基准，用于严格测试提示跟随能力。两个最先进的 T2I 模型（Z-Image 和 QwenImage-2512）的实验结果表明，PromptEcho 在 DenseAlignBench 上实现了实质性改进（+26.8pp / +16.2pp 净胜率），并且在 GenEval、DPG-Bench 和 TIIFBench 上获得了一致的增益，而无需任何特定于任务的训练。消融研究证实，在相同的 VLM 下，PromptEcho 的性能全面优于基于推理的评分，并且奖励质量与 VLM 大小相关。我们将开源经过训练的模型和 DenseAlignBench。reward model training. Given a generated image and a guiding query, PromptEcho computes the token-level cross-entropy loss of a frozen VLM with the original prompt as the label, directly extracting the image-text alignment knowledge encoded during VLM pretraining. The reward is deterministic, computationally efficient, and improves automatically as stronger open-source VLMs become available. For evaluation, we develop DenseAlignBench, a benchmark of concept-rich dense captions for rigorously testing prompt following capability. Experimental results on two state-of-the-art T2I models (Z-Image and QwenImage-2512) demonstrate that PromptEcho achieves substantial improvements on DenseAlignBench (+26.8pp / +16.2pp net win rate), along with consistent gains on GenEval, DPG-Bench, and TIIFBench without any task-specific training. Ablation studies confirm that PromptEcho comprehensively outperforms inference-based scoring with the same VLM, and that reward quality scales with VLM size. We will open-source the trained models and the DenseAlignBench.

1 简介Introduction

文本到图像 (T2I) 生成已从 GAN 快速发展到扩散模型和流匹配架构，但提示跟踪（生成的图像和输入文本之间的精确语义对齐）仍然是一个核心挑战。涉及多个对象、属性绑定、空间关系或精确计数的复杂描述仍然会导致最先进的模型经常出现遗漏和错误。Text-to-image (T2I) generation has advanced rapidly from GANs to Diffusion Models and Flow Matching architectures, yet prompt following—the precise semantic alignment between generated images and input text—remains a core challenge. Complex descriptions involving multiple objects, attribute binding, spatial relationships, or precise counting still cause frequent omissions and errors in state-of-the-art models.

强化学习 (RL) 已被证明可以有效提高即时跟随能力。 DDPOReinforcement learning (RL) has proven effective for improving prompt following. DDPO(Black et al., 2024)和DPOKand DPOK(Fan et al., 2023)将扩散去噪建模为马尔可夫决策过程，并通过策略梯度优化外部奖励信号；流-GRPOmodel diffusion denoising as a Markov Decision Process and optimize external reward signals via policy gradients; Flow-GRPO(Liu et al., 2025)和AWMand AWM(Xue et al., 2025)将此框架扩展到流量匹配模型。然而，奖励信号的质量仍然是瓶颈。剪辑分数extend this framework to Flow Matching models. However, the quality of the reward signal remains the bottleneck. CLIP Score(Hessel et al., 2021)粒度太粗，无法捕获属性绑定、空间关系或计数。根据人类偏好训练的奖励模型——PickScoreis too coarse-grained to capture attribute binding, spatial relations, or counting. Reward models trained on human preferences—PickScore(Kirstain et al., 2023), 图片奖励, ImageReward(Xu et al., 2023), 高压钠灯, HPS(Wu et al., 2023)—比 CLIP 有所改进，但受到模型尺寸小和注释覆盖范围有限的限制。最近基于 VLM 的奖励模型—improve over CLIP but are constrained by small model sizes and limited annotation coverage. Recent VLM-based reward models(Xu et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a)通过对带注释的数据微调 VLM 来提高能力上限，但它们仍然需要昂贵的注释和额外的培训。另一种方法是直接提示 VLM 进行零样本推理评分（我们称之为 InferScore），但这会受到幻觉和采样随机性的影响（第 1 节）raise the capability ceiling by fine-tuning VLMs on annotated data, yet they still require costly annotation and additional training. 2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 文本到图像模型的强化学习Reinforcement Learning for Text-to-Image Models

近年来，应用强化学习优化扩散模型的研究进展迅速。 DDPO（去噪扩散策略优化）Research on applying reinforcement learning to optimize diffusion models has progressed rapidly in recent years. DDPO (Denoising Diffusion Policy Optimization)(Black et al., 2024)是第一个将扩散模型的去噪过程形式化为多步骤马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）的人，将每个去噪步骤视为一个动作，并通过策略梯度方法优化外部奖励。 DPOKwas the first to formalize the denoising process of diffusion models as a multi-step Markov Decision Process (MDP), treating each denoising step as an action and optimizing external rewards via policy gradient methods. DPOK(Fan et al., 2023)建立在 DDPO 的基础上，通过引入 KL 散度正则化，限制优化策略偏离原始预训练分布太远，从而在提高目标属性的同时保持图像质量和多样性。builds upon DDPO by introducing KL divergence regularization, constraining the optimized policy from deviating too far from the original pretrained distribution, thereby maintaining image quality and diversity while improving target attributes.

随着流匹配模型的兴起，强化学习优化方法也得到了相应的扩展。流-GRPOWith the rise of Flow Matching models, RL optimization methods have been extended accordingly. Flow-GRPO(Liu et al., 2025)将组相对策略优化（GRPO）算法适应连续流匹配框架，通过组相对优势估计来执行策略更新。然而，Flow-GRPO训练过程中引入的探索噪声会导致采样轨迹偏离正态分布，导致图像质量下降。 AWM（优势加权匹配）adapts the Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) algorithm to the continuous Flow Matching framework, performing policy updates through group-relative advantage estimation. However, the exploration noise introduced during Flow-GRPO training can cause sampled trajectories to deviate from the normal distribution, leading to image quality degradation. AWM (Advantage Weighted Matching)(Xue et al., 2025)提出了一种更温和的优化策略：通过优势加权 MSE 损失直接在 ODE 预测空间中进行优化，避免 SDE 探索导致的质量下降。我们的实验采用 AWM 作为 RL 训练框架。proposes a gentler optimization strategy: optimizing directly in the ODE prediction space via advantage-weighted MSE loss, avoiding the quality degradation caused by SDE exploration. Our experiments adopt AWM as the RL training framework.

2.2 奖励信号源Reward Signal Sources

T2I RL 优化中的奖励信号可以按其来源和构建方法进行分类。Reward signals in T2I RL optimization can be categorized by their source and construction method.

传统的奖励指标。Traditional reward metrics.

剪辑分数CLIP Score(Hessel et al., 2021)依赖于粗粒度的向量相似性，无法捕获复杂的语义对齐。挑选分数relies on coarse-grained vector similarity and cannot capture complex semantic alignment. PickScore(Kirstain et al., 2023), 图片奖励, ImageReward(Xu et al., 2023), 高压钠灯, HPS(Wu et al., 2023)等人在人工注释的偏好数据上训练专用的奖励模型，但受到模型尺寸小和训练数据有限的限制，难以为前沿 T2I 模型提供有效的优化信号。扩散-DPO 等方法, and others train dedicated reward models on human-annotated preference data, but are limited by small model sizes and finite training data, struggling to provide effective optimization signals for frontier T2I models. Methods such as Diffusion-DPO(Wallace et al., 2024)和扩散-RPOand Diffusion-RPO(Gu et al., 2024)绕过显式奖励模型，但仍然依赖于人类注释的偏好对。bypass explicit reward models but still depend on human-annotated preference pairs.

基于VLM的奖励构建。VLM-based reward construction.

最近的工作将奖励模型升级到 VLM 级别，遵循两个主要范式：（1）基于回归的 VisionRewardRecent works upgrade reward models to the VLM level, following two main paradigms: (1) Regression-based—VisionReward(Xu et al., 2024)微调 VLM 以回答有关图像质量的细粒度二元（是/否）问题，然后通过学习的线性权重将每个问题的概率聚合为标量奖励； (2) 生成式——RewardDancefine-tunes a VLM to answer fine-grained binary (yes/no) questions about image quality, then aggregates the per-question probabilities into a scalar reward via learned linear weights; (2) Generative—RewardDance(Wu et al., 2025a)并统一奖励and UnifiedReward(Wang et al., 2025)将评分作为 VLM 下一个标记预测任务，根据人工注释的偏好或指令数据微调 VLM，以直接生成评估分数。虽然这些方法提高了奖励模型的能力上限，但都需要人工注释数据和额外的 VLM 微调。另一种方法是直接使用 VLM 进行零样本推理评分（InferScore），但这会受到幻觉和采样随机性的影响，产生不稳定的奖励信号（我们在第 1 节中通过实验验证了这一点）cast scoring as a VLM next-token prediction task, fine-tuning the VLM on human-annotated preference or instruction data to directly generate evaluation scores. While these methods raise the capability ceiling of reward models, all require human-annotated data and additional VLM fine-tuning. An alternative is to directly use VLMs for zero-shot inference scoring (InferScore), but this suffers from hallucinations and sampling randomness, producing unstable reward signals (we experimentally validate this in Section4.3).

PromptEcho 的定位。Positioning of PromptEcho.

与上述所有基于 VLM 的奖励方法不同，PromptEcho 不需要注释数据，也不需要奖励模型训练，通过确定性前向传递直接从冻结的 VLM 中提取奖励信号，奖励质量随着开源 VLM 的进步而提高。Unlike all the above VLM-based reward methods, PromptEcho requires no annotation data and no reward model training, directly extracting reward signals from a frozen VLM through deterministic forward passes, with reward quality that improves as open-source VLMs advance.

2.3 视觉语言模型Vision-Language Models

开源VLM的快速发展为PromptEcho提供了坚实的技术基础。代表性的 VLM 系列包括 LLaVAThe rapid development of open-source VLMs provides a solid technical foundation for PromptEcho. Representative VLM families include LLaVA(Liu et al., 2023)，开创了视觉指令调整范式；实习生VL系列, which pioneered the visual instruction tuning paradigm; the InternVL series(Chen et al., 2023)，扩大了视觉编码器的规模并实现了强大的多模态推理；和 Qwen-VL 系列, which scales up vision encoders and achieves strong multimodal reasoning; and the Qwen-VL series(Bai et al., 2023)从Qwen-VL经过Qwen2-VL、Qwen2.5-VL到Qwen3-VL不断演进，在模型规模、训练数据、多模态理解能力等方面迭代改进。本文采用 Qwen3-VL-32B 作为奖励信号的源 VLM，因为它在 32B 参数尺度上具有强大的图文对齐能力。重要的是，PromptEcho 不与任何特定的 VLM 绑定——当发布更强大的开源 VLM 时，只需替换 VLM 即可获得更高质量的奖励信号，而无需重新收集注释数据或重新训练奖励模型。, which has continuously evolved from Qwen-VL through Qwen2-VL and Qwen2.5-VL to Qwen3-VL, with iterative improvements in model scale, training data, and multimodal understanding capabilities. This paper adopts Qwen3-VL-32B as the source VLM for reward signals, owing to its strong image-text alignment capability at the 32B parameter scale. Importantly, PromptEcho is not tied to any specific VLM—when a more powerful open-source VLM is released, one simply replaces the VLM to obtain higher-quality reward signals, without re-collecting annotation data or retraining a reward model.

训练数据构建过程简单高效。我们构建了一个包含大约 100K 个概念丰富的高质量图像的内部数据集，并使用 Qwen3-VL-32B 为这些图像生成详细的说明文字，并使用 VLM 提示“详细描述该图像”。 Qwen3-VL-32B通常生成200-400个英文单词的详细字幕，涵盖图像中的物体、属性、空间关系、颜色、纹理和其他多维信息。The training data construction process is simple and efficient. We construct an internal dataset of approximately 100K concept-rich, high-quality images, and use Qwen3-VL-32B to generate detailed captions for these images, using the VLM prompt “Describe this image in detail.” Qwen3-VL-32B typically generates detailed captions of 200–400 English words, covering objects, attributes, spatial relationships, colors, textures, and other multi-dimensional information in the images.

值得注意的是，数据准备阶段使用的 VLM (Qwen3-VL-32B) 和提示（“详细描述该图像”。）与后续 RL 训练期间用于计算 PromptEcho 奖励的保持一致。这种设计选择的动机是不同的 VLM 和不同的查询/提示会导致生成的字幕的先验分布不同；保持一致性可确保训练数据分布与用于奖励计算的条件分布相匹配，从而使 PromptEcho 奖励能够更准确地测量图像文本对齐。Notably, the VLM (Qwen3-VL-32B) and prompt (“Describe this image in detail.”) used during the data preparation stage are kept consistent with those used for computing PromptEcho rewards during subsequent RL training. This design choice is motivated by the fact that different VLMs and different queries/prompts lead to different prior distributions of generated captions; maintaining consistency ensures that the training data distribution matches the conditional distribution used for reward computation, enabling PromptEcho rewards to more accurately measure image-text alignment.

构建完成后，后续的RL训练过程只需要字幕文本，不再需要原始图像。这一特性使得数据准备成本极低，并且字幕数据集可以方便地扩展和更新。After construction, the subsequent RL training process only requires the caption text and no longer needs the original images. This property makes the data preparation cost extremely low, and the caption dataset can be conveniently expanded and updated.

5 讨论Discussion

5.1 理解与推理Understanding vs. Reasoning

InferScore 依赖于 VLMInferScore relies on the VLM’s推理能力reasoning ability-通过自回归生成明确判断和评分图像文本对齐，这是一个受到推理时间幻觉和采样随机性限制的过程。 PromptEcho 相反提取 VLM—explicitly judging and scoring image-text alignment through autoregressive generation, a process limited by inference-time hallucinations and sampling randomness. PromptEcho instead extracts the VLM’s理解能力understanding ability：在预训练权重中编码的隐式图像文本对齐知识，通过预训练期间使用的相同交叉熵损失来访问。由于下一个标记的预测准确性通常比显式推理链更稳健，因此 PromptEcho 提供了更稳定、更可靠的奖励信号——正如我们消融中巨大的性能差距所证实的那样（第 1 节）: the implicit image-text alignment knowledge encoded in the pretrained weights, accessed through the same cross-entropy loss used during pretraining. Because next-token prediction accuracy is typically more robust than explicit reasoning chains, PromptEcho provides more stable and reliable reward signals—as confirmed by the large performance gap in our ablation (Section4.3.1).

5.2 作为免费奖励注释器的开源 VLMOpen-Source VLMs as Free Reward Annotators

PromptEcho 将开源 VLM 社区的集体进步转化为 T2I 优化的免费奖励信号。传统的奖励模型需要专门的注释和专门的培训，而 PromptEcho 直接利用现有的 VLM。随着这些 VLM 的改进，PromptEcho 的奖励质量也随之提高——我们的 32B 与 8B 消融（第 1 节）PromptEcho transforms the collective progress of the open-source VLM community into free reward signals for T2I optimization. Traditional reward models require dedicated annotation and specialized training, while PromptEcho directly leverages existing VLMs. As these VLMs improve, PromptEcho’s reward quality improves in tandem—our 32B vs. 8B ablation (Section4.3.2）提供了这种缩放特性的直接证据。) provides direct evidence of this scaling property.

5.3 局限性Limitations

我们的方法有一定的局限性。首先，在 VLM 缺乏特定视觉专业知识的领域（例如，细粒度的摄影美学、细微文本错误的识别），PromptEcho 可能无法提供有效的奖励信号。其次，由于PromptEcho的奖励计算是基于VLM的前向传递而不是自回归推理，因此它没有利用最先进的大型模型更强大的推理和思想链能力，这可能会限制需要复杂推理和判断的场景中奖励信号的精度。Our method has certain limitations. First, in domains where the VLM lacks specific visual expertise (e.g., fine-grained photographic aesthetics, recognition of subtle text errors), PromptEcho may not provide effective reward signals. Second, since PromptEcho’s reward computation is based on the VLM’s forward pass rather than autoregressive reasoning, it does not leverage the more powerful reasoning and chain-of-thought capabilities of state-of-the-art large models, which may limit the precision of reward signals in scenarios requiring complex reasoning and judgment.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 PromptEcho，它直接使用冻结 VLM 的交叉熵损失作为 T2I 强化学习的无注释、无训练奖励信号。关键的见解是，VLM 的预训练损失已经编码了丰富的图像文本对齐知识，这些知识可以通过单个确定性前向传递来提取。 Z-Image 和 QwenImage-2512 上的实验证明了 DenseAlignBench (+26.8pp / +16.2pp) 的显着改进以及 GenEval、DPG-Bench 和 TIIFBench 的一致增益。消融研究证实 PromptEcho 的性能远远优于基于推理的评分，并且奖励质量与 VLM 大小相关。文本渲染的成功应用进一步验证了 PromptEcho 作为通用奖励范例的有效性。We presented PromptEcho, which directly uses the cross-entropy loss of a frozen VLM as an annotation-free, training-free reward signal for T2I reinforcement learning. The key insight is that the VLM’s pretraining loss already encodes rich image-text alignment knowledge that can be extracted through a single deterministic forward pass. Experiments on Z-Image and QwenImage-2512 demonstrate substantial improvements on DenseAlignBench (+26.8pp / +16.2pp) and consistent gains across GenEval, DPG-Bench, and TIIFBench. Ablation studies confirm that PromptEcho far outperforms inference-based scoring and that reward quality scales with VLM size. Successful application to text rendering further validates PromptEcho as a general-purpose reward paradigm.

当前的局限性包括 VLM 缺乏视觉专业知识的领域的有效性降低，以及无法利用思想链推理。未来的方向包括扩展到更大的 VLM 和训练数据、特定领域的 VLM 适应以及扩展到文本到视频生成。Current limitations include reduced effectiveness in domains where the VLM lacks visual expertise, and the inability to leverage chain-of-thought reasoning. Future directions include scaling to larger VLMs and training data, domain-specific VLM adaptation, and extension to text-to-video generation.
