CAMO：多目标多旅行商问题的条件神经求解器CAMO: A Conditional Neural Solver for the Multi-objective Multiple Traveling Salesman Problem

Tanishq Duhan1, and Guillaume Sartoretti1 *Equal contribution. †Corresponding author: ming.fan@nus.edu.sg1National University of Singapore, Singapore.2China Unicom Shenzhen Branch, Shenzhen, China.3Central South University, Changsha, China.
摘要Abstract

机器人系统通常需要一组机器人共同访问多个目标，同时优化竞争目标，例如总行程成本和完工时间。该设置可以表述为多目标多旅行商问题 (MOMTSP)。尽管基于学习的方法在单智能体 TSP 和多目标 TSP 变体上表现出了强大的性能，但它们很少解决多智能体协调和多目标权衡的综合挑战，这引入了复杂性的双重来源。 为了弥补这一差距，我们提出了 CAMO，这是一种 MOMTSP 的条件神经求解器，可泛化不同数量的目标、代理和偏好向量，并产生帕累托前沿 (PF) 的高质量近似值。 具体来说，CAMO 由一个条件编码器和一个协作解码器组成，条件编码器将偏好融合到实例表示中，从而实现对多目标权衡的显式控制，协作解码器通过交替代理选择和节点选择来协调所有代理，以自回归构建多代理旅行。 为了进一步提高泛化能力，我们在问题规模的混合分布上使用基于 REINFORCE 的目标来训练 CAMO。大量实验表明，CAMO 的性能优于神经启发法和传统启发法，实现了更接近的 PF。此外，消融结果验证了CAMO关键部件的贡献，移动机器人平台上的实际测试证明了其实际适用性。Robotic systems often require a team of robots to collectively visit multiple targets while optimizing competing objectives, such as total travel cost and makespan. This setting can be formulated as the Multi-Objective Multiple Traveling Salesman Problem (MOMTSP). Although learning-based methods have shown strong performance on the single-agent TSP and multi-objective TSP variants, they rarely address the combined challenges of multi-agent coordination and multi-objective trade-offs, which introduce dual sources of complexity. To bridge this gap, we propose CAMO, a conditional neural solver for MOMTSP that generalizes across varying numbers of targets, agents, and preference vectors, and yields high-quality approximations to the Pareto front (PF). Specifically, CAMO consists of a conditional encoder to fuse preferences into instance representations, enabling explicit control over multi-objective trade-offs, and a collaborative decoder that coordinates all agents by alternating agent selection and node selection to construct multi-agent tours autoregressively. To further improve generalization, we train CAMO with a REINFORCE-based objective over a mixed distribution of problem sizes. Extensive experiments show that CAMO outperforms both neural and conventional heuristics, achieving a closer approximation of PFs. In addition, ablation results validate the contributions of CAMO’s key components, and real-world tests on a mobile robot platform demonstrate its practical applicability.

I 简介Introduction

多重旅行推销员问题 (MTSP) 旨在为多个代理规划路线，使每个目标位置仅访问一次，同时优化最终行程的预定义目标。 MTSP自然出现在需要在实际约束下协调任务分配和路线规划的多机器人系统中。代表性应用包括协作双臂重排The Multiple Traveling Salesman Problem (MTSP) aims to plan routes for multiple agents such that each target location is visited exactly once, while optimizing a predefined objective over the resulting tours. MTSP arises naturally in multi-robot systems that require coordinated task allocation and route planning under practical constraints. Representative applications include cooperative dual-arm rearrangement[18]，约束多站点路由, constrained multiple-depot routing[32]、多平台农业运营, multi-platform agricultural operations[3]，以及动态环境中的协作多智能体导航, and cooperative multi-agent navigation in dynamic environments[19]。 根据目标定义，MTSP 通常分为 Min-Sum MTSP（最小化总行程长度）和 Min-Max MTSP（最小化最长行程长度（即完工时间））。两种变体都是 NP 难最优求解的. Based on the objective definition, MTSP is commonly categorized into Min-Sum MTSP, which minimizes the total tour length, and Min-Max MTSP, which minimizes the longest tour length (i.e., the makespan). Both variants are NP-hard to solve optimally[12]属于单目标组合优化问题（SOCOP）类别。and belong to the class of single-objective combinatorial optimization problems (SOCOPs).

VI 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 CAMO，一种 MOMTSP 的条件神经求解器。 CAMO 通过条件编码器融合偏好和实例信息，并通过协作解码器协调多智能体决策。我们在问题规模的混合分布上进一步使用基于 REINFORCE 的目标来训练 CAMO，以提高对未见过的代理和节点配置的鲁棒性。根据经验，CAMO 具有强大的性能，可以很好地推广到分布式实例和更大的规模，并通过移动机器人平台上的实际部署展示了实际适用性。在未来的工作中，我们将研究如何解决多智能体 MOCOP 中常见的不平衡目标，并探索通过先进的生成模型或分而治之原则解决大规模 MOMTSP 实例。In this paper, we propose CAMO, a conditional neural solver for MOMTSP. CAMO fuses preference and instance information via a conditional encoder and coordinates multi-agent decision making through a collaborative decoder. We further train CAMO with a REINFORCE-based objective over a mixed distribution of problem sizes to improve robustness to unseen agent and node configurations. Empirically, CAMO delivers strong performance, generalizes well to out-of-distribution instances and larger scales, and demonstrates practical applicability through real-world deployment on a mobile robot platform. In future work, we will investigate how to address the imbalanced objectives commonly encountered in multi-agent MOCOPs and explore solving large-scale MOMTSP instances through advanced generative models or divide-and-conquer principles.
