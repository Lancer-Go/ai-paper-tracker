简介Introduction

组合概括是理解已知成分并生成新组合的能力Compositional Generalization is the ability to understand and generate novel combinations from known components(Frankland and Greene 2020; Lin et al. 2023; Hupkes et al. 2020)。人类可以毫不费力地理解熟悉概念的新颖组合. Humans effortlessly understand novel combinations of familiar concepts(Lake et al. 2017)。然而，这种组合能力是现代神经网络的基本弱点. However, this capacity for compositionality is a foundational weakness of modern neural networks(Fodor and Pylyshyn 1988)。 即使是最先进的模型在面对这种成分变化时仍然表现出严重的脆弱性. Even state-of-the-art models still exhibit profound brittleness when confronted with such compositional shifts(Woydt et al. 2025)。这一差距阻碍了它们在需要强大的、非分布推理的领域的应用. This gap hinders their application in domains requiring robust, out-of-distribution reasoning(Lake and Baroni 2023)。神经符号人工智能已经成为一种有前途的范式，通过将神经网络的感知优势与符号逻辑的结构化推理能力相结合来解决这种脆弱性. Neuro-symbolic AI has emerged as a promising paradigm to address this brittleness by integrating the perceptual strengths of neural networks with the structured reasoning capabilities of symbolic logic(Bhuyan et al. 2024; Feldstein et al. 2024; Sheth et al. 2023)。目标是建立可以将高维感知输入转化为符号词汇的模型，并随后对结果表示进行结构化推理。. The objective is to build models that can ground high-dimensional perceptual input into a symbolic vocabulary and subsequently perform structured reasoning over the resulting representations.

在这个领域中，经常有一个隐含的假设，即一旦实现了基础，组合性就会默认地从符号组件中自然地或内在地出现Within this field, there is often an implicit assumption that compositionality will emerge naturally or intrinsically by default from symbolic components once grounding is achieved(Garcez and Lamb 2023; Marcus 2003; Pavlick 2023; Wu and Zhao 2024)。直觉是，如果模型能够可靠地将像素映射到符号（例如“3”、“蓝色”、“立方体”），那么它本质上就能够在新的逻辑推论中使用这些符号. The intuition is that if a model can reliably map pixels to symbols (e.g., ‘3’, ‘blue’, ‘cube’), it will inherently be able to use those symbols in new logical deductions(Andreas et al. 2016b; Mao et al. 2019a)。然而，这一假设在很大程度上仍未得到证实。 实证研究倾向于关注丰富的感知环境中的符号基础，但没有系统的泛化测试. However, this hypothesis remains largely unverified. Empirical studies tend to focus on either symbol grounding in rich perceptual settings but without systematic generalization tests(Antol et al. 2015; Topan et al. 2021; Li et al. 2024; Umili et al. 2023)，或关于抽象感知的纯粹符号领域的组合概括, or on compositional generalization in purely symbolic domains that abstract away perception(Conklin et al. 2021; Lake and Baroni 2018; Lightman et al. 2023)，很少在统一的、受控的环境中评估它们。这在我们对这两种能力是否具有内在联系或代表不同的、非互补的挑战的理解上造成了重大差距。, rarely evaluating them in a unified, controlled setting. 问题定义Problem Definition

这项研究在统一的神经符号框架内纳入了三种不同且日益具有挑战性的成分概括（CompGen）形式。我们将我们的研究形式化为一系列神经符号约束满足问题（NS-CSP），旨在测试模型泛化到其训练分布之外的能力。我们的中心假设是，稳健的泛化需要的不仅仅是简单的符号基础；它需要明确的架构支持来推理新颖的实体、关系和规则组合。This study incorporates three distinct and increasingly challenging forms of compositional generalization (CompGen) within a unified neuro-symbolic framework. We formalize our investigation as a series of Neuro-Symbolic Constraint Satisfaction Problems (NS-CSPs) designed to test a model’s ability to generalize beyond its training distribution. Our central hypothesis is that robust generalization requires more than simple symbol grounding; it requires explicit architectural support for reasoning about novel entities, relations, and rule compositions.

讨论Discussion

The stark performance gap between the fulliiLTN 和仅接地基线表明，虽然接地是必要的先决条件，但不足以实现稳健的推理。LTN and the Grounding-Only baseline demonstrates that while grounding is a necessary prerequisite, it is insufficient for enabling robust reasoning.

基线的失败凸显出，如果架构缺乏复杂问题所需的顺序、多步骤推导的能力，成功地将像素映射到符号并不会自动为模型配备管理这些符号如何交互的原则。相比之下，The baseline’s failure highlights that successfully mapping pixels to symbols does not automatically equip a model with the principles governing how those symbols interact if the architecture lacks the capacity for the sequential, multi-step deduction required for complex problems. In contrast, theiiLTN 的成功直接归功于其迭代细化循环，它为组合推理提供了明确的学习目标。通过对整个推理轨迹的训练，LTN’s success is directly attributable to its iterative refinement loop, which provides an explicit learning objective for compositional reasoning. By training on the entire reasoning trace, theiiLTN 学习了一个可推广的演绎过程，使其能够为困难的、看不见的问题组成更长的推理链。LTN learns a generalizable deduction process, allowing it to compose longer chains of reasoning for difficult, unseen problems.

这些发现意味着神经符号架构必须将推理过程视为一等公民，除了训练有素的感知前端之外还需要专门的架构支持。未来的工作应该在机器人或自然语言处理等更多样化的领域测试这一原理，并探索迭代推理的替代架构。These findings imply that neuro-symbolic architectures must treat the reasoning process as a first-class citizen, requiring dedicated architectural support beyond a well-trained perception front-end. Future work should test this principle in more diverse domains like robotics or NLP and explore alternative architectures for iterative reasoning.

The counter-intuitive finding that our fulliiLTN 的表现优于仅推理版本（该版本接收预先接地的符号输入），这表明接地不仅仅是一个先决条件。我们假设联合训练感知和推理模块可以充当强大的正则化器。通过迫使推理模块对感知前端产生的噪声和不确定的表示进行操作，它可以学习更强大和更通用的演绎策略。这表明持久的现实世界感知不确定性可能是开发灵活推理系统的关键要素，也是未来神经符号研究的一个有希望的方向。LTN outperformed the Reasoning-Only version—which received pre-grounded symbolic inputs—suggests that grounding is more than just a prerequisite. We hypothesize that jointly training the perceptual and reasoning modules acts as a powerful regularizer. By forcing the reasoning module to operate on the noisy and uncertain representations produced by the perception front-end, it learns a more robust and generalizable deductive policy. This suggests that enduring real-world perceptual uncertainty may be a critical element for developing flexible reasoning systems, a promising direction for future neuro-symbolic research.

局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

尽管我们的研究结果为基础和推理的非互补性提供了强有力的证据，但我们承认某些局限性为未来的研究开辟了道路。我们的实验是在受控的合成视觉域中进行的。这是一种深思熟虑的方法论选择，旨在将基础和推理之间的关系与现实世界中感知不确定性的混杂变量隔离开来。未来的工作应该在更复杂和嘈杂的领域（例如机器人和自然语言理解）中测试此处建立的原则。另外，Although our findings provide strong evidence for the non-complementarity of grounding and reasoning, we acknowledge certain limitations that open avenues for future research. Our experiments were conducted in a controlled, synthetic visual domain. This was a deliberate methodological choice to isolate the relationship between grounding and reasoning from the confounding variable of perceptual uncertainty in real-world. Future work should test the principles established here in more complex and noisy domains, such as robotics and natural language understanding. Plus, the scalability of theiiLTN 针对更大问题空间的迭代过程仍然是一个有待探索的悬而未决的问题。LTN’s iterative process to vastly larger problem spaces remains an open question for exploration.

结论Conclusion

本文测试了解决符号基础足以实现神经符号系统中的组合泛化的假设，并表明事实并非如此。 实验表明，仅在基础上训练的模型无法推广到新的组合，即使具有正确的感知。相比之下，所提出的迭代逻辑张量网络（iLTN）通过明确的多步推理目标进行训练，实现了强大的零样本性能。它的成功来自于迭代细化，能够实现复杂的演绎推理。 贡献包括：（1）证据表明基础和组合概括必须一起优化，而不是单独优化； (2) 实现这一目标的新颖架构； (3) 结果显示联合训练优于单独基于基础或推理训练的模型。This paper tests the assumption that solving symbol grounding is enough for compositional generalization in neuro-symbolic systems and shows that it is not. Experiments demonstrate that models trained only on grounding fail to generalize to new combinations, even with correct perception. In contrast, the proposed Iterative Logic Tensor Network (iLTN), trained with an explicit multi-step reasoning objective, achieves strong zero-shot performance. Its success comes from iterative refinement that enables complex deductive reasoning. The contributions are: (1) evidence that grounding and compositional generalization must be optimized together, not separately; (2) a novel architecture that achieves this; and (3) results showing joint training outperforms models trained on grounding or reasoning alone.
