摘要Abstract

约束规划是解决调度、规划、配置和验证等复杂组合问题的核心技术。因此，信任其结果需要在两个层面上保证：预先应用的重新表述是保留语义的，并且求解器会产生正确的答案。在这项工作中，我们引入了一个解决精益定理证明器中两个验证级别的框架：它可用于证明整个问题族的参数化公式级属性，例如等价性、等可满足性和对称破缺约束的正确性；并通过将后端转换为外部格式（例如 MiniZinc、SMT-LIB 和 OPB）来检查各个实例的求解器生成的证书。将这两个级别结合起来会产生一个端到端的工作流程，该工作流程无需信任外部求解器即可确定约束问题的可满足性或不可满足性。实验结果表明，我们的框架经过验证的对称性破缺在实践中也得到了回报：每个问题族一个参数证明，在所有实例大小中重复使用，可以将求解器搜索工作量减少高达一个因子Constraint programming is a core technology for solving complex combinatorial problems in scheduling, planning, configuration, and verification. Trusting its results therefore demands guarantees at two levels: that reformulations applied beforehand are semantics-preserving, and that solvers produce correct answers. In this work, we introduce a framework that addresses both verification levels in the Lean theorem prover: it can be used to prove formulation-level properties, such as equivalence, equisatisfiability, and the correctness of symmetry-breaking constraints, parametrically for entire problem families; and to check solver-produced certificates for individual instances via translation backends to external formats such as MiniZinc, SMT-LIB, and OPB. Combining both levels yields an end-to-end workflow that establishes the satisfiability or unsatisfiability of a constraint problem without trusting the external solver. Experimental results show that our framework’s verified symmetry breaking also pays off in practice: a single parametric proof per problem family, reused across all instance sizes, reduces solver search effort by a factor of up to2×1072\times 10^{7}，而整个精益认证仍然经济实惠，对于我们最大的实例来说最多只需要几分钟。, while the entire in-Lean certification stays affordable, taking at most a few minutes for our largest instances.

5 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 LeanCSP，一个用于在精益定理证明器内验证约束重构和约束求解的框架。它允许人们为整个参数化的 CSP 系列证明一次的等价性、等可满足性和对称性破缺健全性，并通过转换到外部求解器来证明单个实例的（不）可满足性，从而产生一个在精益内部验证这些结果的端到端管道。 作为警示性的例子We presented LeanCSP, a framework for certifying both constraint reformulation and constraint solving inside the Lean theorem prover. It allows one to prove equivalence, equisatisfiability, and symmetry-breaking soundness once for entire parameterized families of CSPs, and to certify the (un)satisfiability of individual instances through translation to external solvers, yielding an end-to-end pipeline that verifies these results inside Lean. As the cautionary example ofSection˜3表明，我们的框架拒绝其正确性未经证明的重新表述。 我们希望研究人员能够在 LeanCSP 的基础上制定和解决具有端到端形式保证的开放验证和组合问题。shows, our framework rejects reformulations whose correctness is not proved. We hope that researchers will build on LeanCSP to formulate and settle open verification and combinatorial problems with end-to-end formal guarantees.
