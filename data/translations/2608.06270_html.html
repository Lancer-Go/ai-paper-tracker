5 讨论Discussion

视觉工具使用的错觉。The illusion of visual tool-use.

可视化工具的使用所带来的政策层面的总体准确性提升是真实的，但它可能会产生一种误导性的印象，即工具的使用在整个部署过程中具有广泛且因果关系的有效性。相反，我们的因果审计表明，收益集中在经过校准的少数人身上，而许多其他工具使用轨迹要么未能使用返回的视觉证据，要么调用时间表不连贯的工具。我们称这种现象为The aggregate policy-level accuracy gain from visual tool-use is real, but it can create the misleading impression that tool-use is broadly and causally effective across rollouts. Our causal audit shows instead that the gain is concentrated in a calibrated minority, while many other tool-using trajectories either fail to use the returned visual evidence or invoke tools with an incoherent schedule. We call this phenomenon视觉工具使用的错觉the illusion of visual tool-use: 拥有Having工具并不一定意味着它是a tool does not necessarily mean it is使用过的used正确地properly。问题“模型是否用图像思考？”因此比“模型是否调用可视化工具？”更重要。现行政策更能满足后者的需求，而不是满足前者的需求。因此，提高工具使用能力不仅应该关注更丰富的界面和更长的推出时间，还应该关注行动之间的因果耦合、它们引发的证据和最终答案。. The question “does the model think with images?” is therefore more important than “does the model call visual tools?”. Current policies satisfy the latter far more often than the former. Improving tool-use capability should thus focus not only on richer interfaces and longer rollouts, but also on the causal coupling between actions, the evidence they elicit, and the final answer.

局限性Limitations

我们的研究有一些局限性，限制了其结论的范围，并为未来的工作指明了方向。Our study has several limitations that constrain the scope of its conclusions and point to directions for future work.

所有实验均在开源的图像思维上进行 模型。我们的干预级别有不同的访问要求：政策级别和轨迹级别的干预只需要运行模型并控制返回的观察结果，因此它们可以应用于具有可观察工具接口的黑盒模型，而步骤级别的 VEG 使用令牌级别的分数，因此需要白盒访问。 OpenAI o3/o4-mini 等闭源模型All experiments are conducted on open-source thinking-with-images models. Our intervention levels have different access requirements: the policy-level and trajectory-level interventions only require running the model and controlling the returned observations, so they can apply to black-box models with an observable tool interface, whereas the step-level VEG uses token-level scores and thus needs white-box access. Closed-source models such as OpenAI o3/o4-mini(OpenAI, 2025a)既不暴露它们的代币级概率，也不暴露干预中间观察的能力，因此相同的结论是否扩展到闭源模型是一个我们无法在这里解决的悬而未决的问题。expose neither their token-level probabilities nor the ability to intervene on intermediate observations, so whether the same conclusions extend to the closed-source models is an open question that we cannot resolve here.

我们只专注于We only focus on the裁剪和缩放crop-and-zoom操作，是当前图像思维模型中的主导工具。其他工具（例如图像分割、OCR、视频中的帧选择、基于代码的图像处理或外部搜索）可能会表现出本质上不同的校准行为，但我们的因果框架通过构建特定于工具的反事实观察来转移。对于 OCR，可以用来自另一区域的文本或空结果替换返回的文本；对于本地化，可以用移位的或随机的框替换返回的框；对于视频帧选择，可以用时间偏移或不相关的帧替换所选帧。我们并不声称裁剪和缩放模式一定适用于这些工具。operation, which is the dominant tool in current thinking-with-images models. Other tools such as image segmentation, OCR, frame selection in video, code-based image manipulation, or external search may exhibit qualitatively different calibration behaviors, but our causal framework transfers by constructing a tool-specific counterfactual observation. For OCR one can replace the returned text with text from another region or an empty result; for localization one can replace the returned box with a shifted or random box; for video frame selection one can replace the chosen frame with a temporally shifted or irrelevant frame.
