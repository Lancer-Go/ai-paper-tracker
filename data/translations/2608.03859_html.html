简介Introduction

随着法学硕士从写作助理演变为研究代理人，他们正在成为 嵌入学术知识生产中。他们可以检索来源， 用有限的人力综合证据、组织论点并起草报告 干预。这些功能可以实现更深层次的重写，其中 中心思想、证据、推理模式和组织逻辑 原始材料被重新组合成流畅的新散文，几乎无法辨认 词汇重叠。因此，文档可能严重依赖于特定的 源，但表面相似度有限。检测到这种形式As LLMs evolve from writing assistants into research agents, they are becoming embedded in scholarly knowledge production. They can retrieve sources, synthesize evidence, organize arguments, and draft reports with limited human intervention. These capabilities enable deeper forms of rewriting, in which the central ideas, evidence, reasoning patterns, and organizational logic of source materials are recombined into fluent new prose with little recognizable lexical overlap. A document may therefore depend heavily on a particular source while bearing limited surface resemblance to it. Detecting this form of生成剽窃generative plagiarism需要在广泛的情况下识别源重用 转变。requires identifying source reuse under extensive transformation.

LLM 生成的文本检测不能解决同样的问题。现有 分类器和基于可能性的检测器估计法学硕士是否有贡献 到一段文字LLM-generated-text detection does not address the same question. Existing classifiers and likelihood-based detectors estimate whether an LLM contributed to a text(Yang et al. 2024; Mitchell et al. 2023; Bao et al. 2024)，而 生成剽窃检测会询问文本是否重复使用特定来源的内容。人工智能的参与既不意味着也不需要这种重用：机器生成的文本可能是独立生成的，而来源衍生的内容可能在经过大量人工修改后仍然保留。, whereas generative plagiarism detection asks whether the text reuses content from a particular source. AI involvement neither implies nor is required for such reuse: machine-generated text may be independently produced, while source-derived content may remain after substantial human revision.

经典的外部抄袭检测通常结合来源检索 与通道对齐Classical external plagiarism detection typically combines source retrieval with passage alignment(Potthast et al. 2010; foltynek2019academic)。当重复使用的段落保留可识别的措辞时，词汇匹配和 BM25 效果很好，而 基于嵌入的方法将比较扩展到语义相似性。然而，这两种信号在广泛的释义、重组和多源合成下都会减弱. Lexical matching and BM25 work well when reused passages retain recognizable wording, while embedding-based methods extend the comparison to semantic similarity. Both signals, however, weaken under extensive paraphrasing, reorganization, and multi-source synthesis(Wahle et al. 2021, 2022; Greiner-Petter et al. 2025)。提示LLM也可以直接判断文档对. Prompted LLMs can also judge document pairs directly(Lee et al. 2025)，但他们的决定可能取决于提示，并且不能对每个候选来源所做的贡献进行稳定的衡量。, but their decisions may depend on prompting and do not yield a stable measure of the contribution made by each candidate source.

剩下的困难是相似性并不能可靠地表明源依赖性。关于同一主题的独立编写的文档可能高度相似，而深度重写的文档可能与其所依赖的源仅微弱相似。因此，我们寻求一种定向的、特定于源的信号来测量候选源的程度The remaining difficulty is that resemblance does not reliably indicate source dependence. Independently written documents about the same topic may be highly similar, whereas a deeply rewritten document may be only weakly similar to the source on which it relies. We therefore seek a directional, source-specific signal that measures how much a candidate sourceSS促成可疑文件contributes to a suspicious documentPP，同时控制基线可预测性, while controlling for the baseline predictability ofPP。这引发了基于描述长度对源证据的不同看法。在固定概率模型下，序列的负对数概率对应于其模型相对码长. This motivates a different view of source evidence based on description length. Under a fixed probabilistic model, the negative log-probability of a sequence corresponds to its model-relative codelength(Shannon 1948; Rissanen 1978; MacKay 2003)。我们比较分配给同一可疑文档的代码长度，在模型上下文中是否有候选源。当文档重用来自该源的信息时，对其进行调节应该使重用的内容更容易预测，从而缩短文档的代码长度。由此产生的减少衡量候选源的增量预测贡献。. 结论Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们引入了 SCDG，这是一种定向且免训练的框架，可通过描述长度对比来衡量候选源提供的增量预测证据。受控干预支持其对保留源证据的敏感性，而实验证明在冻结语言模型后端的 PAN25 成对检测和 PAN26 候选源排名方面具有出色的性能。多新闻测试进一步表明了对同一主题和同一事件混杂的稳健性。然而，SCDG 的计算成本比传统的相似性度量更高，并且主要根据英文科学文献和构建的新闻领域压力集进行评估。未来的工作应该提高评分效率，扩大多语言和跨领域评估，本地化段落级证据，并研究更复杂的源重用，同时利用其令牌级分解进行可解释的证据追踪。与高召回率检索相结合，SCDG 无需更新特定任务的模型即可实现可扩展的来源归因，从而支持学术写作中更透明的学术诚信筛选和来源验证。In this work, we introduced SCDG, a directional and training-free framework that measures the incremental predictive evidence supplied by a candidate source through description-length contrast. Controlled interventions support its sensitivity to retained source evidence, while experiments demonstrate strong performance on PAN25 pairwise detection and PAN26 candidate-source ranking across frozen language-model backends. The Multi-News test further indicates robustness to same-topic and same-event confounding. Nevertheless, SCDG is more computationally expensive than conventional similarity measures and has been evaluated mainly on English scientific documents and a constructed news-domain stress set. Future work should improve scoring efficiency, broaden multilingual and cross-domain evaluation, localize passage-level evidence, and study more complex source reuse, while leveraging its token-level decomposition for interpretable evidence tracing. Combined with high-recall retrieval, SCDG enables scalable source attribution without task-specific model updates, supporting more transparent academic-integrity screening and source verification in scholarly writing.
