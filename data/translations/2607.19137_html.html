Andrea Borghesi

摘要Abstract

从一张对比前的乳房 MRI 切片推断对比增强是不确定的：对比后的外观包含在基线解剖学中未唯一编码的生理信息。仅优化配对像素保真度可以抑制不确定的病变增强，而对抗性或随机生成目标可以有利于真实的对比后外观，而不能保证患者特定的病变保真度。我们引入了 MIRAGE，一种残差 2D U-Net，它将全局重建和感知损失与仅在训练期间可用的三种形式的病变感知监督相结合：对错过的肿瘤增强的不对称惩罚、多尺度辅助肿瘤分割以及通过冻结后对比肿瘤分割 nnU-Net 进行指导。我们使用八个互补的基于图像、区域、放射组学和分割的指标，对来自多中心 MAMA-SYNTH 数据的 301 个病例评估该方法。 MIRAGE 在六个指标上排名第一，并且与经过调整的 pix2pix、条件扩散和潜在桥匹配基线相比，显着改善了下游病变定位。生成替代方案保留了 LPIPS 或对比分类方面的优势，揭示了清晰的保真度与效用的权衡。留一进和留一出消融表明，损失对于病变定位来说是部分多余的，但对外观、放射组学和边界精度产生明显影响。这些结果支持任务感知综合，同时还表明其明显的最优性取决于用于定义效用的下游模型和指标。Inferring contrast enhancement from one pre-contrast breast MRI slice is underdetermined: post-contrast appearance contains physiological information that is not uniquely encoded in baseline anatomy. Optimizing only paired pixel fidelity can suppress uncertain lesion enhancement, whereas adversarial or stochastic generative objectives can favor realistic post-contrast appearance without guaranteeing patient-specific lesion fidelity. We introduce MIRAGE, a residual 2D U-Net that combines global reconstruction and perceptual losses with three forms of lesion-aware supervision available only during training: an asymmetric penalty for missed tumor enhancement, multi-scale auxiliary tumor segmentation, and guidance through a frozen post-contrast tumor segmentation nnU-Net. We evaluate the method on 301 cases from the multi-centre MAMA-SYNTH data using eight complementary image-, region-, radiomics-, and segmentation-based metrics. MIRAGE ranks first on six metrics and markedly improves downstream lesion localization over tuned pix2pix, conditional diffusion, and latent bridge-matching baselines. The generative alternatives retain advantages in LPIPS or contrast classification, revealing a clear fidelity-utility trade-off. Leave-one-in and leave-one-out ablations show that the losses are partly redundant for lesion localization but exert distinct effects on appearance, radiomics, and boundary accuracy. These results support task-aware synthesis while also showing that its apparent optimality is conditional on the downstream models and metrics used to define utility.

1 简介Introduction

动态对比增强 (DCE) MRI 是最灵敏的乳腺成像方式，支持病变检测、疾病程度评估、治疗计划和反应监测Dynamic contrast-enhanced (DCE) MRI is the most sensitive breast-imaging modality and supports lesion detection, disease-extent assessment, treatment planning, and response monitoring[14]。其值源自使用钆基造影剂 (GBCA) 后的信号变化。尽管当代 GBCA 具有良好的安全记录，但造影剂给药仍然需要静脉注射，增加了时间和复杂性，仅限于选定的患者，并导致环境污染. Its value derives from signal changes after administration of a gadolinium-based contrast agent (GBCA). Although contemporary GBCAs have a strong safety record, contrast administration still requires intravenous access, adds time and complexity, is restricted in selected patients, and contributes to environmental contamination[22, 5]。这些考虑因素促进了降低对比度或无对比度方案，前提是可以可靠地恢复临床相关的增强信息。. These considerations motivate contrast-reduced or contrast-free protocols, provided that clinically relevant enhancement information can be recovered reliably.

虚拟对比度增强预测对比度后图像Virtual contrast enhancement predicts a post-contrast imageyy来自非对比输入from non-contrast inputxx。由于增强取决于未完全由造影前解剖结构决定的生理因素，因此多个目标可能与相同的输入兼容，特别是当只有单个 T1 加权切片可用时。因此，方法必须平衡患者特定的保真度与真实的对比后外观，反映感知与失真的权衡. Because enhancement depends on physiological factors not fully determined by pre-contrast anatomy, multiple targets may be compatible with the same input, particularly when only a single T1-weighted slice is available. Methods must therefore balance patient-specific fidelity with realistic post-contrast appearance, reflecting the perception–distortion trade-off[1]。然而，在医学成像中，合理的合成仍可能增加、改变或抑制临床相关特征. In medical imaging, however, plausible synthesis may still add, shift, or suppress clinically relevant features[4].

乳房虚拟对比研究使用了多序列输入、模拟低剂量采集、扩散加权 MRI、病变加权对抗目标和下游分割指导Breast virtual-contrast studies have used multi-sequence inputs, simulated low-dose acquisitions, diffusion-weighted MRI, lesion-weighted adversarial objectives, and downstream segmentation guidance[3, 15, 25, 11, 18]。条件扩散还表明，减法目标和肿瘤感知损失提高了病变保真度，而掩模调节进一步有所帮助，但需要推理时的病变定位. Conditional diffusion has also shown that subtraction targets and tumor-aware losses improve lesion fidelity, while mask conditioning further helps but requires lesion localization at inference[8]。这些结果表明，仅全局像素或感知标准是不够的。现有的综合系列做出了不同的妥协：pix2pix 使用对抗性学习来鼓励清晰的目标域外观. These results suggest that global pixel or perceptual criteria alone are insufficient. Existing synthesis families make different compromises: pix2pix uses adversarial learning to encourage sharp target-domain appearance[10];去噪扩散模型通过迭代采样表示灵活的条件分布; denoising diffusion models represent flexible conditional distributions through iterative sampling[7];潜在桥匹配（LBM）可实现潜在空间中的一步翻译; and latent bridge matching (LBM) enables one-step translation in latent space[2]。直接配对回归更紧密地锚定于观察到的解剖结构，但可能会平均不确定的细节。. Direct paired regression is more tightly anchored to the observed anatomy but may average uncertain detail.

2 材料和方法Materials and Methods

2.1 数据与任务Data and task

我们使用源自 MAMA-MIA 的 MAMA-SYNTH 版本We use the MAMA-SYNTH release derived from MAMA-MIA[6, 19]。它包含来自四个公共队列（杜克大学）的 1,506 项治疗前乳腺 DCE-MRI 检查。. It contains 1,506 pretreatment breast DCE-MRI examinations from four public cohorts (Duke[21],ISPY1, ISPY1[16],ISPY2, ISPY2[13], 国家行动计划, NACT[17]），在多个供应商处以 1.5 或 3.0 T 的价格获取。对于每次检查，预处理都会选择带注释的肿瘤内平均信号最高的对比后相位以及具有最大肿瘤面积的 2D 切片。每个病例均包含脂肪抑制前对比图像), acquired at 1.5 or 3.0 T across multiple vendors. For each examination, preprocessing selects the post-contrast phase with the highest mean signal inside the annotated tumor and the 2D slice with the largest tumor area. Each case comprises a fat-suppressed pre-contrast imagexx，其峰值增强目标, its peak-enhancement targetyy，和二元肿瘤掩模, and a binary tumor maskmm。根据训练预对比图像估计的全局平均值和标准差应用于两者. A global mean and standard deviation estimated from training pre-contrast images are applied to bothxx和andyy，保持它们的相对强度变化。我们使用 1,205 个案例进行训练，并使用固定的 301 个案例内部验证队列。在推理时永远不会向合成器提供掩码。, preserving their relative intensity change. We use 1,205 cases for training and a fixed 301-case internal validation cohort. Masks are never provided to the synthesizer at inference.

这些差异与各自的目标是一致的。对抗性和生成分布学习方法鼓励输出类似于对比后域，但仅真实外观并不能保证正确的局部增强。 结果涉及评估的实现，不应被解释为表明对抗性、扩散或桥接匹配方法本质上是不合适的。These differences are consistent with the respective objectives. Adversarial and generative distribution-learning methods encourage outputs that resemble the post-contrast domain, but realistic appearance alone does not guarantee correctly localised enhancement. 然而，所选择的工作点是有条件的。损失权重是通过对一个内部验证队列进行粗略开发扫描来选择的，而不是详尽的多目标优化，并且不同的应用程序可能会优先考虑清晰度、定位和下游效用之间的不同折衷方案。此外，下游模型的变化The selected operating point is nevertheless conditional. Loss weights were chosen through coarse development sweeps on one internal validation cohort rather than exhaustive multi-objective optimisation, and different applications may prioritise different compromises between sharpness, localization, and downstream utility. 最后，所报告的指标都没有建立生理正确性。像素指标与观察到的对比后采集进行比较，放射组学指标捕获选定的图像属性，固定分类器和分割器继承它们自己的偏差。因此，需要外部队列、多种采集协议和下游模型以及读者研究来确定合成增强是否保留诊断相关的发现而不引入、取代或抑制病变。Finally, none of the reported metrics establishes physiological correctness. Pixelwise metrics compare against one observed post-contrast acquisition, radiomic metrics capture selected image properties, and fixed classifiers and segmenters inherit their own biases. External cohorts, multiple acquisition protocols and downstream models, and reader studies are therefore needed to determine whether synthetic enhancement preserves diagnostically relevant findings without introducing, displacing, or suppressing lesions.

总之，MIRAGE 使用仅在训练期间可用的肿瘤知情监督从单个造影前切片合成峰值造影后乳腺 MRI。与评估的生成基线相比，它提供了患者特异性保真度、肿瘤区域放射组学一致性和下游分割的更强的整体平衡，同时承认选定的外观导向指标。更广泛地说，这些发现支持任务感知的医学图像合成，其中预先训练的下游模型指导输出保留临床相关信息，而不仅仅是看似合理的外观。In conclusion, MIRAGE synthesizes peak post-contrast breast MRI from a single pre-contrast slice using tumor-informed supervision available only during training. It provides a stronger overall balance of patient-specific fidelity, tumor-region radiomic agreement, and downstream segmentation than the evaluated generative baselines, while conceding selected appearance-oriented metrics. More broadly, the findings support task-aware medical image synthesis in which pretrained downstream models guide outputs towards preserving clinically relevant information, rather than merely plausible appearance.
