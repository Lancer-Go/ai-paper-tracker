工业化预测驱动的推理：用于可靠 GenAI 和代理系统评估的 GLIDE 库Industrializing Prediction-Powered Inference: The GLIDE Library for Reliable GenAI and Agentic Systems Evaluation

Grégoire Martinon    Ibrahim Merad    Mohamed Raki
摘要Abstract

对代理系统的可靠评估需要具有有效不确定性的无偏估计，但标准实践在昂贵的人工注释和有偏见的法学硕士作为法官代理之间进行导航。预测驱动推理（PPI）将两者结合成具有有效置信区间的去偏估计，但其各种方法仍然分散在部分实现的论文中。我们介绍 GLIDE，一个开源 Python 库，它在专门用于均值估计的 scipy 风格 API 下统一了最先进的 PPI 估计器（PPI++、分层 PPI、Predict-Then-Debias 及其分层变体、主动统计推断）和采样器（均匀、分层、主动、成本最优）。 GLIDE 附带了可重复的蒙特卡罗验证套件、用于方法选择的基于经验的决策树，以及在同等精度下显示大量注释节省的代理评估案例研究。 GLIDE 包可通过以下 URL 获取：Reliable evaluation of agentic systems requires unbiased estimates with valid uncertainty, but standard practice navigates between costly human annotation and biased LLM-as-judge proxies. Prediction-powered inference (PPI) combines both into debiased estimates with valid confidence intervals, yet its various methods remain scattered across papers under partial implementations. We introduce GLIDE, an open-source Python library that unifies state-of-the-art PPI estimators (PPI++, Stratified PPI, Predict-Then-Debias and its stratified variants, Active Statistical Inference) and samplers (uniform, stratified, active, cost-optimal) under a scipy-style API specialized to mean estimation. GLIDE ships with a reproducible Monte Carlo validation suite, an empirically grounded decision tree for method selection, and an agentic evaluation case study showing substantial annotation savings at equivalent precision. The GLIDE package is available at this URL:https://github.com/EmertonData/glide

1 简介Introduction

考虑一个临床决策支持代理，它可以检索指南、患者记录的原因并提出治疗建议。衡量这种智能体的行为是否正确需要临床专家对多步骤轨迹进行审查，而当今的两种主要做法都在这一过程中大规模失败。完整的人工注释可靠，但速度慢且成本高；法学硕士作为法官快速且廉价，但存在偏见，特别是对于知识密集型、特定领域的任务。Consider a clinical decision-support agent that retrieves guidelines, reasons over a patient record, and proposes a treatment. Measuring whether such an agent behaves correctly requires expert clinician review of multi-step trajectories, a process where today’s two dominant practices both fail at scale. Full human annotation is reliable but slow and costly; LLM-as-judge is fast and cheap but biased, especially for knowledge-intensive, domain-specific tasks.

不是在人类可靠性和代理规模之间进行选择，Rather than choosing between human reliability and proxy scale,预测驱动的推理prediction-powered inference（生产者价格指数）(PPI)(Angelopoulos et al., 2023a, b)将它们结合起来以获得两者的优势：一小组人工注释和大量廉价的代理预测，以产生具有有效置信区间的无偏估计。覆盖范围无条件地保留在代理上：更差的代理会产生更宽的间隔，而不是无效的间隔。combines them to obtain the strengths of both: a small set of human annotations with a large set of cheap proxy predictions to produce unbiased estimates with valid confidence intervals. Coverage holds unconditionally on the proxy: a worse proxy yields wider intervals, not invalid ones.

从几个方面来看，代理评估非常适合 PPI。代理和真实情况之间的成本不对称是极端的。代理管道还表现出自然的异质性：性能和代理质量在调用的工具、处理的查询类型以及涉及的子代理之间存在很大差异，从而激发了分层估计。现代法官越来越多地产生校准的每个样本的不确定性，从而解锁主动采样。现有的库涵盖了这一领域的部分内容，但没有一个界面能够统一估计器和采样器的匹配系列，从而使从业者可以将学术存储库中的方法缝合在一起。Agentic evaluation is a natural fit for PPI on several counts. The cost asymmetry between proxy and ground truth is extreme. Agentic pipelines also exhibit natural heterogeneity: performance and proxy quality vary substantially across the tools invoked, the types of queries handled, and the sub-agents involved, motivating stratified estimation. Modern judges increasingly produce calibrated per-sample uncertainty, unlocking active sampling. Existing libraries cover parts of this landscape but no single interface unifies the matching family of estimators and samplers, leaving practitioners to stitch methods together across academic repositories.

3 为什么代理评估需要 PPIWhy Agentic Evaluation Needs PPI

部分Section2修复了抽象设置；我们现在认为，代理系统同时表现出四种属性，并且达到了不同寻常的程度，在这四种属性下，PPI 是最有价值的。每个属性都直接映射到最近文献中的方法，以及相应的 GLIDE 组件。fixed the abstract setting; we now argue that agentic systems exhibit four properties, simultaneously and to an unusual degree, under which PPI is most valuable. Each property maps directly to a method in the recent literature, and to a corresponding GLIDE component.

1、成本极度不对称。1. Extreme cost asymmetry.

从质量上讲，评估代理轨迹比评估单个法学硕士输出更昂贵。轨迹可能涉及检索、工具调用、中间推理步骤和最终响应，领域专家必须检查所有这些以证明正确性。在医疗保健、法律和金融领域，每个轨迹的注释成本为几美元，而法学硕士作为法官的费用为几美分。这种两到三个数量级的差距正是成本意识抽样的制度Evaluating an agentic trajectory is qualitatively more expensive than evaluating a single LLM output. A trajectory may involve retrieval, tool calls, intermediate reasoning steps, and a final response, all of which a domain expert must inspect to certify correctness. Annotation costs of several dollars per trajectory are common in healthcare, legal, and financial settings, while an LLM-as-judge call costs cents. This two-to-three-orders-of-magnitude gap is precisely the regime where cost-aware sampling(Angelopoulos et al., 2025)相关，将标记率设置为代理/注释成本比和可用信号的函数。is relevant, setting the labeling rate as a function of the proxy/annotation cost ratio, and the available signal.

2、自然分层。2. Natural stratification.

代理管道沿着评估时通常已知的几个轴划分观察结果：调用的工具、处理的查询类型、涉及的子代理以及咨询的检索源。这些阶层的代理质量以重要的方式存在差异：可靠地评估算术推理的法学硕士法官可能会在代码执行跟踪或特定领域的临床指南方面遇到困难。分层生产者价格指数Agentic pipelines partition observations along several axes that are typically known at evaluation time: the tools invoked, the type of query handled, the sub-agents involved, and the retrieval source consulted. Proxy quality varies across these strata in ways that matter: an LLM-as-judge that reliably evaluates arithmetic reasoning may struggle with code-execution traces or with domain-specific clinical guidelines. Stratified PPI(Fisch et al., 2024; Fogliato et al., 2024)在每个层内运行 PPI，并将结果与每个层的权重相结合，隔离代理质量差异并产生比未分层估计器更严格的间隔。runs PPI within each stratum and combines the results with per-stratum weights, isolating proxy-quality differences and yielding tighter intervals than the unstratified estimator.

3. 可用代理的不确定性。3. Available proxy uncertainty.

现代法官可以产生每个样本的不确定性信号：言语置信度、对数概率、多代一致性或法官集合。这些信号识别代理最不可靠的轨迹，因此识别人工注释信息最丰富的轨迹。主动统计推断Modern judges can produce per-sample uncertainty signals: verbalized confidence, log-probabilities, multiple generation consistency, or ensembles of judges. These signals identify the trajectories on which the proxy is least reliable, and therefore the trajectories where human annotation is most informative. Active Statistical Inference(Zrnic and Candès, 2024; Gligorić et al., 2025)通过对与代理不确定性成比例的标记概率进行采样，将昂贵的专家工作集中在具有最大统计价值的地方来利用这一点。exploits this by sampling labeling probabilities proportional to proxy uncertainty, concentrating expensive expert effort where it carries the most statistical value.

4. 相应的部署制度。4. Consequential deployment regimes.

关键系统（例如临床决策支持、财务咨询、受监管的自动化、安全关键型自主）也是那些没有有效不确定性的有偏差的点估计会产生直接后果的系统。免分发覆盖保证是这些环境中的部署要求。 PPI 的无条件代理有效性使其非常适合必须可审计的代理管道。Criticial systems (e.g., clinical decision support, financial advisory, regulated automation, safety-critical autonomy) are also those where biased point estimates without valid uncertainty have direct consequences. Distribution-free coverage guarantees are a deployment requirement in these settings. PPI’s unconditional-on-the-proxy validity makes it well suited to agentic pipelines that must be auditable.

4 滑翔框架The GLIDE Framework

4.1 三步分解Three-step decomposition

GLIDE 围绕继承自经典调查理论的三个连续步骤组织评估：GLIDE organizes evaluation around three sequential steps inherited from classical survey theory:抽样sampling, 注释annotation, 和, and估计estimation。从大量代理标记的观测值（或轨迹）中选择少量预算（抽样）；领域专家在选定的子集上生成真实标签（注释）；标记子集和代理预测被组合成具有置信区间（估计）的去偏点估计。抽样和估计是统计和算法的；而注释是不可简化的特定于任务的并且超出了 GLIDE 的范围。该库仅要求专家以机器可读的形式（类别或数字）返回标签，其业务含义取决于上下文，但其统计处理则不然。. A small budget of observations (or trajectories) is selected from a large pool of proxy-labeled ones (sampling); domain experts produce ground-truth labels on the selected subset (annotation); the labeled subset and the proxy predictions are combined into a debiased point estimate with a confidence interval (estimation). Sampling and estimation are statistical and algorithmic; while annotation is irreducibly task-specific and out of scope for GLIDE. The library requires only that experts return labels in a machine-readable form (categories or numbers), whose business meaning is context-dependent but whose statistical treatment is not.

这种因式分解不仅仅是一种表示选择。它允许独立开发、测试和贡献采样器和估计器：提出新采样器的贡献者不需要接触任何估计器，反之亦然。它还允许从业者混合和匹配第 5 节中回顾的方法系列This factorization is more than a presentation choice. It allows samplers and estimators to be developed, tested, and contributed independently: a contributor proposing a new sampler need not touch any estimator, and conversely. It also lets practitioners mix and match across the families of methods recalled in Section2，选择与其评估设置相匹配的采样器以及与其统计制度相匹配的估计器。, choosing the sampler that matches their evaluation setup and the estimator that matches their statistical regime.

局限性。Limitations.

GLIDE 专门从事均值估计。这是一个经过深思熟虑的范围选择：部署设置中的绝大多数性能和风险控制指标（准确率、相关率、幻觉率、毒性率、精确度、召回率）都是平均值或比例，并且限制到这种制度简化了代码库，因为当目标是平均值时，在一般 M 估计设置中发散的几种方法会崩溃到相同的估计器。其他具有实际意义的估计值，特别是分位数和 GLM 系数，不在范围内；GLIDE specializes in mean estimation. This is a deliberate scope choice: the vast majority of performance and risk-control metrics in deployed settings (accuracy, relevance rate, hallucination rate, toxicity rate, precision, recall) are means or proportions, and restricting to this regime simplifies the codebase, since several methods that diverge in the general M-estimation setting collapse to the same estimator when the target is a mean. Other estimands of practical interest, in particular quantiles, and GLM coefficients, are out of scope;ppi_py (Angelopoulos et al., 2023a)仍然是更广泛的家庭的适当参考。根据经验，GLIDE 中基于 CLT 的估计器（PPI++、分层 PPI++、ASI）要求每个层至少有 50 个标记样本，以使渐近区间可靠；低于该阈值，基于引导程序的 PTD 变体是记录的后备。该库目前假设每个观察都有一个代理，并且独立同分布。来自同一群体的标记和未标记集：它不提供任何时候有效的构造，不聚合来自多个代理的预测，并且尚未处理标记集和未标记集之间的协变量或标签转移。最后，注释步骤是故意不受管理的；从业者必须集成自己的标签管道，在特定领域的部署中，该管道通常是工作流程中劳动力最密集的部分。remains the appropriate reference for the broader family. The CLT-based estimators in GLIDE (PPI++, Stratified PPI++, ASI) require, as a rule of thumb, at least fifty labeled samples per stratum for the asymptotic intervals to be reliable; below that threshold, the bootstrap-based PTD variants are the documented fallback. The library currently assumes a single proxy per observation and i.i.d. labeled and unlabeled sets drawn from the same population: it does not provide anytime-valid constructions, does not aggregate predictions from multiple proxies, and does not yet handle covariate or label shift between the labeled and unlabeled sets. Finally, the annotation step is intentionally unmanaged; practitioners must integrate their own labeling pipeline, which in domain-specific deployments is often the most labor-intensive component of the workflow.

9 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 GLIDE，这是一个开源 Python 库，用于对 GenAI 和代理系统进行预测驱动的评估。 GLIDE 专门从事均值估​​计，并在单个 scipy 风格的 API 下集成了现代估计器（PPI++、分层 PPI++、PTD、分层 PTD、ASI）和采样器（均匀、分层、主动、成本最优）。可重复的蒙特卡罗验证套件建立了估计器的名义覆盖范围，并将其效率增益描述为代理质量的函数。We presented GLIDE, an open-source Python library for prediction-powered evaluation of GenAI and agentic systems. GLIDE specializes in mean estimation, and integrates modern estimators (PPI++, Stratified PPI++, PTD, Stratified PTD, ASI) and samplers (uniform, stratified, active, cost-optimal) under a single scipy-style API. A reproducible Monte Carlo validation suite establishes nominal coverage across estimators and characterizes their efficiency gains as a function of proxy quality.

该图书馆在统计观点的同时提出了一个实用的观点：投资于更高质量的法学硕士法官可以直接转化为更大的有效注释预算，并且收益在代理/真实相关性中单调增长。更好的代理并不能替代人工注释；他们是它的乘数。The library makes a practical point alongside its statistical one: investing in a higher-quality LLM-as-judge translates directly into a larger effective annotation budget, with the gain growing monotonically in the proxy/true correlation. Better proxies are not a substitute for human annotation; they are a multiplier on it.
