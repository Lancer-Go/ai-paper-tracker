机器人动作表示的语义锚定Semantic Anchoring for Robotic Action Representations

Yuan Xu∗,1,  Youheng Shi∗,1,  Chengyang Li2,3,  Wentao Zhu2,  Yizhou Wang1
1Peking University 2Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo 3Shanghai Jiao Tong University
摘要Abstract

视觉语言动作（VLA）模型继承了预训练视觉语言模型的丰富语义表示，但对有限的机器人演示进行微调会降低这种结构并破坏泛化。因此出现了一个基本问题：Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models inherit rich semantic representations from pretrained Vision-Language Models, yet fine-tuning on limited robot demonstrations degrades this structure and undermines generalization. A fundamental question therefore arises:什么构成了良好的行动表现？what constitutes a good action representation?受镜像神经元理论的启发，即观察和执行共享意图级编码，我们检查机器人的动作表示是否保留了预训练编码器捕获的语义结构。系统探索证实，这种结构在微调过程中会受到侵蚀，并且其质量与任务成功和分布外泛化同步。我们进一步引入了一种即插即用的方法，它将动作表示锚定到语义流形，同时将表示分解为共享语义通道和私有通道，所有通道在推理时都被丢弃，使部署的模型保持不变。我们的方法在模拟和现实世界基准的不同 VLA 主干上进行了验证，得出的结果高达Inspired by the mirror neuron theory’s insight that observation and execution share an intention-level encoding, we examine whether a robot’s action representations preserve the semantic structure captured by pretrained encoders. Systematic probing confirms that this structure erodes during fine-tuning, and that its quality synchronizes with both task success and out-of-distribution generalization. We further introduce a plug-and-play method that anchors action representations to a semantic manifold while decomposing representations into a shared semantic channel and a private channel, all discarded at inference, leaving the deployed model unchanged. 1 简介Introduction

视觉语言动作 (VLA) 模型通过在机器人演示上微调预训练的视觉语言模型 (VLM) 来构建通用机器人策略，继承丰富的结构化表示空间，原则上可以实现跨新颖对象、场景和指令的泛化Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models build generalist robot policies by fine-tuning pretrained Vision-Language Models (VLM) on robot demonstrations, inheriting a richly structured representation space that, in principle, enables generalization across novel objects, scenes, and instructions[2, 3, 4]。 然而，在实践中，演示数据比 VLM 的预训练语料库小和窄几个数量级，并且仅动作监督会驱动模型走向快捷映射，从而降低继承的语义结构. In practice, however, demonstration data are orders of magnitude smaller and narrower than the VLM’s pretraining corpus, and action-only supervision drives the model toward shortcut mappings that degrade the inherited semantic structure[5, 6, 7]：图。: Fig.1a 显示预训练表示中存在的清晰任务级聚类在微调后崩溃了。 现有的努力通过扩大数据规模来缓解这一问题a shows that the clear task-level clustering present in pretrained representations collapses after fine-tuning. Existing efforts mitigate this by scaling up data[8, 9]或重新设计架构or redesigning architectures[10, 11, 12]，但他们这样做并没有清楚地了解正在失去什么。这两种策略都面临着一个更根本的问题：, but they do so without a clear picture of what is being lost. A more fundamental question precedes both strategies:什么构成了良好的行动表现，我们如何判断它何时有辱人格？what constitutes a good action representation, and how can we tell when it is degrading?

认知神经科学对运动表征支持灵活泛化的原因进行了广泛研究。 镜像神经元理论What makes a motor representation support flexible generalization has been studied extensively in cognitive neuroscience. The mirror neuron theory[13, 14, 15]研究表明，在灵长类动物中，当观察和执行目标导向的动作时，相同的神经元会放电reveals that in primates, the same neurons fire when observing a goal-directed action and when executing it[16, 17]。 至关重要的是，这些神经元是围绕. Crucially, these neurons are organized around意图intentions：他们对嵌入不同目标的相同运动行为有不同的反应，形成意图级的“运动词汇”: they respond differently to identical motor acts embedded in different goals, forming an intention-level “motor vocabulary”[18, 19]。 这意味着生物运动表征不仅编码运动模式，还编码. This implies that biological motor representations encode not only movement patterns but also代理打算完成什么任务what task the agent intends to accomplish，正是这种意图级别的组织支持对新情况的灵活泛化。, and it is this intention-level organization that supports flexible generalization to novel situations.

我们将其与机器人学习进行类比。 VLA 的动作表征不仅仅编码运动命令；它还编码运动命令。它们还承载了模型对当前任务的理解，例如机器人是否正在拿起勺子、堆放盘子或关闭柜子。 如果这种理解保持良好的组织性，模型就可以在类似的任务之间转移知识；如果性能下降，模型就会依赖于表面线索并失去泛化能力。 在镜像神经元的类比下，预训练的视觉语言编码器可以被视为“观察端”的具体实例：在大规模多模态数据上进行训练，它们将不同的视觉和语言体验压缩成语义流形，其中任务相关的概念得到了很好的组织。 柏拉图表示假说We draw an analogy to robot learning. A VLA’s action representations do not merely encode motor commands; they also carry the model’s understanding of the current task, e.g., whether the robot is picking up a spoon, stacking a dish, or closing a cabinet. If this understanding remains well-organized, the model can transfer knowledge across similar tasks; if it degrades, the model falls back on superficial cues and loses generalization. Under the mirror neuron analogy, pretrained vision-language encoders can be viewed as a concrete instance of the “observation side”: trained on large-scale multimodal data, they compress diverse visual and linguistic experience into semantic manifolds in which task-relevant concepts are well organized. 我们表明，VLA 的仅动作微调会侵蚀从预训练继承的意图级语义结构，并且该结构的质量与泛化同步。在这一发现和镜像神经元理论的指导下，我们提出了一种即插即用的方法，通过共享/私有分解将动作表示锚定到语义流形，并在推理时全部丢弃。在模拟和现实环境中对各种 VLA 主干进行的实验验证了分布内和分布外设置的一致改进。尽管取得了这些有希望的结果，但仍然存在两个局限性。首先，性能的上限取决于冻结对齐目标的质量；更强大的以操纵为中心的编码器可能会产生进一步的收益。其次，我们仅在特定任务演示的培训后应用该目标。未来的工作将探索将语义对齐扩展到预训练阶段，并扩展到更广泛的数据源，以实现更通用的机器人操作。We show that action-only fine-tuning of VLAs erodes the intention-level semantic structure inherited from pretraining, and that this structure’s quality synchronizes with generalization. Guided by this finding and the mirror neuron theory, we propose a plug-and-play method that anchors action representations to a semantic manifold via shared/private decomposition, all discarded at inference. Experiments on various VLA backbones across simulation and real-world settings validate consistent improvements in both in-distribution and out-of-distribution settings. Despite these promising results, two limitations remain. First, performance is upper-bounded by the quality of the frozen alignment target; a stronger manipulation-centric encoder would likely yield further gains. Second, we apply the objective only during post-training on task-specific demonstrations. Future work will explore extending semantic alignment to the pre-training stage and scaling to broader data sources toward more generalizable robotic manipulation.
