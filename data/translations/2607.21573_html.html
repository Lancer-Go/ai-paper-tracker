超越充分性：具有反事实必要性的时间序列解释Beyond Sufficiency: Time Series Explanation with Counterfactual Necessity

1School of Computer Science, University of Bristol
2School of Engineering Mathematics and Technology, University of Bristol
hongnan.ma@bristol.ac.uk

摘要Abstract

对时间序列分类器的忠实解释应该识别出不仅足以保留黑盒模型的预测，而且对于维护它也是必要的子序列。然而，现有的面向充分性的方法可以高度重视支持预测的虚假子序列，但对模型的决策来说并不重要。我们介绍Faithful explanations of time-series classifiers should identify subsequences that are not only sufficient to preserve a black-box model’s prediction, but also necessary for maintaining it. However, existing sufficiency-oriented methods can assign high importance to spurious subsequences that support the prediction without being essential to the model’s decision. We introduce时间PNSTimePNS，一个用于时间序列解释的必要性感知框架。受到 Pearl 的反事实必要性概念的启发，TimePNS 通过干预时间因素并测量原始预测是否被破坏来评估时间因素是否必要。该框架采用两阶段设计。第一阶段学习可识别的因果生成过程以及面向充分性的解释面具。第二阶段对时间因素进行反事实干预，以得出必要性信号，这些信号监督时间门，通过抑制非必要成分并强调反事实必要成分来完善最初的解释。对合成和现实世界时间序列基准的实验表明，TimePNS 可以更准确地识别决策关键子序列，并持续改进强基准上的充分性与必要性权衡。, a necessity-aware framework for time-series explanation. Inspired by Pearl’s counterfactual notion of necessity, TimePNS assesses whether a temporal factor is necessary by intervening on it and measuring whether the original prediction is disrupted. The framework adopts a two-stage design. Stage I learns an identifiable causal generative process together with a sufficiency-oriented explanation mask. Stage II performs counterfactual interventions on temporal factors to derive necessity signals, which supervise a temporal gate that refines the initial explanation by suppressing non-essential components and emphasizing counterfactually necessary ones. Experiments on synthetic and real-world time-series benchmarks show that TimePNS more accurately identifies decision-critical subsequences and consistently improves sufficiency-necessity trade-offs over strong baselines.

1 简介Introduction

解释多元时间序列黑盒模型的预测是医疗保健、金融和自治系统等高风险应用的基本要求Explaining predictions made by black-box models for multivariate time-series is a fundamental requirement in high-stakes applications such as healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems(Morid et al., 2023; Kim et al., 2025)。在这些设置中，从业者必须确定哪些变量和时间步骤真正对模型的输出负责，以支持及时干预并建立对自动化决策系统的信任. In these settings, practitioners must identify which variables and time steps are truly responsible for a model’s output in order to support timely intervention and build trust in automated decision systems(Küsters et al., 2020)。大多数现有的解释方法通过对输入序列应用掩蔽和扰动操作并测量预测结果的变化来解决这个问题，以推断不同变量或时间段的重要性得分. Most existing explanation methods tackle this problem by applying masking and perturbation operations to the input sequence and measuring the resulting change in prediction to infer the importance score of different variables or temporal segments(Crabbé and Van Der Schaar, 2021)。这些方法旨在提取输入序列的子集，保留原始预测，同时保持简洁和可解释。. These methods aim to extract a subset of the input sequence that preserves the original prediction while remaining concise and interpretable.

然而，从必要性和充分性概率 (PNS) 的角度来看However, when viewed through the lens of the probability of necessity and sufficiency (PNS)(Pearl, 2009)，现有方法主要是可操作的, existing methods primarily operationalize充分性sufficiency：他们寻求在保留时足以保留模型原始预测的子序列: they seek subsequences that are sufficient to preserve the model’s original prediction when retained(Queen et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024)。这种限制在现实世界的时间序列中变得尤其成问题，其中不同的时间段或通道可能高度相关，从而产生虚假关联，使多个子序列似乎为模型的预测提供信息. This limitation becomes particularly problematic in real-world time series, where different temporal segments or channels may be highly correlated, giving rise to spurious associations that make multiple subsequences appear informative for the model’s prediction(Zhang et al., 2023)。在这种情况下，基于充分性的解释方法可能会高度重视相关但非必要的子序列，从而导致解释不能忠实地反映模型实际依赖的内容。当应用于新的时间序列数据时，这种脆弱的相关性可能不再持续存在，使得这些解释在分布变化下不可靠. In such cases, a sufficiency-based explanation method may assign high importance to correlated but non-essential subsequences, leading to explanations that do not faithfully reflect what the model actually relies on. When applied to new time series data, such fragile correlations may no longer persist, making these explanations unreliable under distribution shift(Yang et al., 2023; Schölkopf et al., 2021)。相反，互补的概念. In contrast, the complementary notion of必要性necessity提供了过滤掉相关但非必要子序列的附加标准。provides an additional criterion for filtering out correlated but non-essential subsequences.

在珀尔的框架中，必然性是通过反事实的“假设”问题来捕捉的：如果不存在感兴趣的因素，结果是否不会发生In Pearl’s framework, necessity is captured through a counterfactual ‘what-if’ question: would the outcome fail to occur if the factor of interest were absent(Pearl, 2009)？这种反事实的观点强化了时间序列解释的概念：一个因素之所以具有解释性，并不是因为它可以保留黑盒模型的原始预测，而是因为删除它会改变该预测。图。? This counterfactual view sharpens the notion of time series explanation: a factor is not explanatory simply because it can preserve the black-box model’s original prediction, but because removing it changes that prediction. To address this challenge, TimePNS introduces a two-stage training procedure built upon a learned causal latent representation. In Stage I, a mask-based explainer is trained to select compact subsequences that preserve the black-box model’s original prediction, while a causal representation branch maps the input into a structured latent space and learns both instantaneous and lagged causal mechanisms among latent factors. 8 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 TimePNS，这是一个用于时间序列分类器事后解释的必要性感知框架。与主要寻求能够保留黑盒预测的子序列的面向充分性的方法不同，它通过使用从潜在反事实干预中得出的必要性信号来完善充分性保留掩模，从而进一步结合了反事实必要性。合成基准和现实基准的实验表明，TimePNS 改进了决策关键子序列的识别，并实现了比竞争基准更强的充分性-必要性行为。消融分析进一步验证了必要性引导的细化在过滤掉可预测但冗余的区域中的作用。一个限制是重复反事实干预的计算成本，我们将其留给未来的工作以及更广泛的时间架构的扩展。We introduced TimePNS, a necessity-aware framework for post-hoc explanation of time-series classifiers. Unlike sufficiency-oriented methods that mainly seek subsequences capable of preserving the black-box prediction, it further incorporates counterfactual necessity by refining a sufficiency-preserving mask with necessity signals derived from latent counterfactual interventions. Experiments on synthetic and real-world benchmarks show that TimePNS improves the identification of decision-critical subsequences and achieves stronger sufficiency–necessity behavior than competitive baselines. Ablation analyses further validate the role of necessity-guided refinement in filtering out predictive but redundant regions. A limitation is the computational cost of repeated counterfactual interventions, which we leave to future work together with extensions to broader temporal architectures.
