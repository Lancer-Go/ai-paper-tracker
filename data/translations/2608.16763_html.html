LAVA：用于大规模财务文档审计的逻辑感知验证和增强框架LAVA: Logic-Aware Validation and Augmentation Framework for Large-Scale Financial Document Auditing

摘要Abstract

生产中的财务文件验证（例如工资审计、税务合规和贷款承销）需要在严格的企业约束下具有极高的准确性、一致性和可重复性。在实践中，文档以异构布局和格式、语义丰富、上下文相关的内容以及当前管道难以可靠处理的嵌入式业务规则到达。我们介绍 LAVA（逻辑感知验证和增强）——一种基于多模式大语言模型构建的模块化、与主干无关的管道——集成了四阶段设计：文档规则检索、布局保留信息提取、辅助元数据丰富和可审计的符号/算术验证。 LAVA 支持强大的规则基础、细粒度的错误归因以及一致、可追踪的端到端执行，这些功能对于高风险部署至关重要。在具有不同财务文档和数十个专家策划的验证规则的大型现实世界基准上进行评估，LAVA 在幻觉控制和边缘情况处理方面优于基线，同时保持高效的代币使用，展示了大容量、时间关键型验证的实用性。Financial document validation in production—such as payroll auditing, tax compliance, and loan underwriting—demands exceptional accuracy, consistency, and reproducibility under strict enterprise constraints. In practice, documents arrive with heterogeneous layouts and formats, semantically rich, context-dependent content, and embedded business rules that current pipelines struggle to process reliably. We introduce LAVA (Logic-Aware Validation and Augmentation)—a modular, backbone-agnostic pipeline built on multimodal large language models—that integrates a four-stage design: document–rule retrieval, layout-preserving information extraction, auxiliary metadata enrichment, and auditable symbolic/arithmetic verification. LAVA supports robust rule grounding, fine-grained error attribution, and consistent, traceable end-to-end execution—capabilities essential for high-stakes deployment. Evaluated on a large real-world benchmark with diverse financial documents and dozens of expert-curated validation rules, LAVA outperforms baselines in hallucination control and edge-case handling while maintaining efficient token usage, demonstrating practicality for high-volume, time-critical validation.

1 简介Introduction

监管处罚、财务损失和声誉损害都可能是由财务文件验证中的一个错误造成的，这使得准确性、一致性和可审计性变得不可协商。金融机构每天在贷款承销、工资审计、税务合规和欺诈检测等工作流程中处理数百万份文档。对于报表、发票和税单等半结构化文档来说，挑战非常严峻，这些文档跨越多个页面，呈现不规则的布局，对特定领域的业务逻辑进行编码，并且通常以嘈杂的扫描件或非标准 PDF 形式到达Regulatory penalties, financial losses, and reputational damage can all result from a single error in financial document validation, making accuracy, consistency, and auditability non-negotiable. Financial institutions process millions of documents daily across workflows such as loan underwriting, payroll auditing, tax compliance, and fraud detection. The challenge is acute for semi-structured documents like statements, invoices, and tax slips, which span multiple pages, exhibit irregular layouts, encode domain-specific business logic, and often arrive as noisy scans or non-standard PDFs3; 10; 41; 8.

近年来，通过多模式大语言模型 (MLLM) 的布局和结构感知预训练，视觉丰富的文档理解取得了快速进展。布局LMRecent years have witnessed rapid progress in visually rich document understanding through layout- and structure-aware pretraining of multimodal large language models (MLLMs). LayoutLM41率先提出了空间文本联合编码，随后是 DocFormerpioneered spatial–textual joint encoding, followed by DocFormer2, 法学硕士, DocLLM35, mPLUG-DocOwl2 15，和ROP, and ROP42，推进结构表示的多模式架构。 这种转变从纯文本建模转向多模式文档智能。然而，评估仍然以感知和问答（QA）任务为主，以狭隘的、特定于任务的方式探索推理，而很少进行验证——在结构化和跨领域约束下测试解释和可靠性的推理。, advancing multimodal architectures for structural representation. This shift moves beyond pure text modelling to multimodal document intelligence. However, evaluations remain dominated by perceptual and question answering (QA) tasks, probing reasoning in a narrow, task-specific manner while rarely accessing validation—reasoning that tests both interpretation and reliability under structured and cross-field constraints.

企业级文档验证提出的要求超出了仅感知或推理的要求。它要求符号规则执行、跨领域一致性和多步骤逻辑连贯性——这些能力仅部分反映在现有基准中Enterprise-grade document validation presents requirements beyond those of perception or reasoning alone. It calls for symbolic rule enforcement, cross-field consistency, and multi-step logical coherence—capabilities only partially reflected in existing benchmarks37; 20; 4。最近的数据集已将评估扩展到布局感知和理解. Recent datasets have expanded evaluation to layout-aware perception and understanding45; 39; 23; 31; 29，但合规性关键验证逻辑仍未得到充分探索。在实践中，企业通常通过将通用模型与严格的基于规则的模块配对来弥补差距, but compliance-critical validation logic remains underexplored. In practice, enterprises often patch the gap by pairing general-purpose models with rigid rule-based modules28。然而，即使是擅长提取或理解的最先进模型，在直接应用于验证时也表现出持续的局限性：幻觉仍然很常见，并且推理可追溯性有限。跨监管模式的适应是脆弱的，并且计算成本随着企业规模代币的使用而上升。这些缺点使得验证不仅是文档智能的一个延伸，而且是一个独特的新兴前沿——它需要以准确性、效率、审计就绪性和稳健性为核心要求的框架。. Yet even state-of-the-art models that excel at extraction or understanding exhibit ongoing limitations when directly applied to validation: hallucinations remain common, and reasoning traceability is limited. Adaptation across regulatory schemas is fragile, and computational costs escalate with token usage at enterprise scale. These shortcomings make validation not merely an extension but a distinct and emerging frontier of document intelligence—one that demands frameworks where accuracy, efficiency, audit readiness, and robustness are central requirements.

Results show competitive gains in factual accuracy and symbolic correctness, together with lower computational overhead than baseline MLLM pipelines, demonstrating robustness and cost-effectiveness in realistic financial validation scenarios.

5 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 LAVA，这是一个模块化、可解释、与主干网和领域无关的系统，用于企业级财务文档验证。 LAVA 结合了布局保留提取、领域感知增强以及符号和算术验证，在大型现实世界基准上实现了高度的事实对齐和数值可靠性，并且比整体提示的计算开销更低。对事实幻觉率、数字不忠率和边缘情况处理的评估表明，结构化的多阶段推理可以实现细粒度的错误归因，在合规性敏感的工作流程中具有更强的鲁棒性，并可以传输到结构相似的数据。展望未来，我们的目标是处理噪音更大、结构性较差的文档，并学习跨格式和领域的规则泛化。We presented LAVA, a modular, interpretable and backbone- and domain-agnostic system for enterprise-grade financial document validation. Combining layout-preserving extraction, domain-aware augmentation, and symbolic and arithmetic verification, LAVA achieves high factual alignment and numerical reliability on a large real-world benchmark, with lower computational overhead than monolithic prompting. Evaluation with factual hallucination rate, numerical infidelity rate, and edge case handling shows that structured, multi-stage reasoning enables fine-grained error attribution, stronger robustness in compliance-sensitive workflows, and transfer to structurally similar data. Looking ahead, we aim to handle noisier, less-structured documents and to learn rule generalization across formats and domains.

6 道德考虑Ethical Considerations

这项研究是使用专有的财务文件数据集进行的，所有数据都是完全匿名的，并根据严格的机构隐私协议进行处理。我们承认金融决策中算法偏差的潜在风险。 LAVA 的设计是一个准确、可扩展和可审计的系统，是通过提高透明度和确保人的责任来降低这种风险的有意步骤。然而，任何生产部署都需要对人口偏见进行严格、持续的审计，以确保公平和公正的结果。This research was conducted using a proprietary dataset of financial documents, with all data fully anonymized and handled under strict institutional privacy protocols. We acknowledge the potential risk of algorithmic bias in financial decision-making. LAVA’s design as an accurate, scalable and auditable system is a deliberate step to mitigate this risk by enhancing transparency and ensuring human accountability. Nevertheless, any production deployment would necessitate rigorous, ongoing audits for demographic bias to ensure fair and equitable outcomes.
