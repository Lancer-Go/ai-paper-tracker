2.1.重新思考长上下文建模2.1. Rethinking Long-Context Modeling

LLM长上下文训练和推理面临两个主要挑战：KV缓存增长和注意力计算成本。回想起来，Full Attention 及其变体 GQA/MLA 保留了完整的历史记录，但 KV 缓存随序列长度线性增长，这成为长上下文推理的主要瓶颈。相比之下，纯线性或局部注意力通过固定大小的循环状态或窗口大小来实现线性缩放，但有限的状态容量通常无法有效地保留长上下文中的细粒度语义信息回调。LLM long-context training and inference faces two main challenges: KV cache growth and attention computation cost. In retrospect, Full attention and its variants GQA/MLA preserves the complete history, but has KV cache grows linearly with sequence length, which becomes the primary bottleneck for long-context inference. In contrast, pure linear or local attention attains linear scaling through fixed-size recurrent states or window size, yet the limited state capacity often fails to retain finegrained semantic information callback over long contexts effectively.

KSA 在这两个极端之间进行了权衡：它不断将长上下文信息压缩为越来越多的摘要状态，从而能够对远程依赖关系进行表达建模，而无需显式缓存所有历史标记。与纯线性注意力不同，摘要状态不是固定大小的，而是作为摘要标记逐渐增长；与稀疏注意力不同，远程建模不再依赖于稀疏的令牌到令牌的连接，而是通过充当压缩中继的摘要令牌来路由远程信息。KSA strikes a trade-off between these two extremes: it continuously compresses long-context information into a growing set of summary states, enabling expressive modeling of distant dependencies without explicitly caching all historical tokens. Unlike pure linear attention, the summary state is not fixed-size but grows progressively as summary tokens; unlike sparse attention, long-range modeling no longer hinges on sparse token-to-token connections but routes distant information through summary tokens that act as compressed relays.

总而言之，KSA 可以被视为一种混合机制，它将局部全局注意力（滑动窗口内精确的局部令牌级建模）与压缩全局远程注意力（通过按特定比例线性增长的一组摘要令牌）相结合，在建模能力、计算效率和内存占用之间提供更好的平衡。Taken together, KSA can be regarded as a mixture mechanism that integrates local-global attention (exact local token-level modeling within a sliding window) with compressed global long-range attention (via a linearly growing set of summary tokens through a specific ratio), offering a better balance among modeling capacity, computational efficiency, and memory footprint.

Instead of simply interleaving full and efficient layers, KSA performs hybridization inside the attention mechanism itself: local tokens preserve fine-grained short-range dependencies, while summary tokens provide compact access to distant contexts. This intrinsic mixing mechanism enables KSA to reduce long-context attention and KV-cache overhead while maintaining strong modeling capacity for both local details and global information.
