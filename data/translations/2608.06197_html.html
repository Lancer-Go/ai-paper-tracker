摘要Abstract

训练用于长期工具使用的大型语言模型代理通常依赖于与真实或合成的可执行环境的交互，其构建和验证成本高昂，或者依赖于难以落地的外部模拟器。 我们介绍Training large language model agents for long-horizon tool use typically relies on interactions with real or synthesized executable environments, whose construction and verification are costly, or on external simulators that are difficult to ground. We introduceEnvACE，一种代理强化学习方法，在训练过程中替代外部环境交互EnvACE, an agentic reinforcement learning method that replaces external environment interaction during training with世界排练world rehearsal。 该策略在表演和演练之间交替：它首先生成工具调用，然后扮演环境的角色来产生由该操作引起的响应，并根据演练的响应制定后续决策。 这两个角色都使用任务成功奖励进行端到端联合优化。 通过世界演练，该策略将行为与其环境响应之间的关系内化到其参数中，从而产生直接支持决策的代理世界模型。 在 BFCL-v4 中，. The policy alternates between acting and rehearsal: it first generates a tool call, then plays the role of the environment to produce the response induced by that action, and conditions subsequent decisions on the rehearsed response. Both roles are jointly optimized end-to-end using task-success rewards. Through world rehearsal, the policy internalizes the relationship between actions and their environment responses in its parameters, yielding an agent world model that directly supports decision making. Across BFCL-v4,τ2\tau^{2}-Bench、VitaBench 和 FinMCP-Bench，EnvACE 实现了强大且可转移的性能，在整体评估中优于环境扩展基线。 对照研究进一步表明，世界演练持续改善跨模型尺度的政策学习。 在测试时，内部化的世界模型可以在承诺执行之前进行私人排练，在适度的排练预算下产生进一步的收益，而无需额外的外部交互。 我们的研究结果表明，世界预演是一种超越外部环境限制的 LLM 代理人培训的新途径。 我们的代码公开于-Bench, VitaBench, and FinMCP-Bench, EnvACE achieves strong and transferable performance, outperforming environment-scaling baselines in the overall evaluation. Controlled studies further show that world rehearsal consistently improves policy learning across model scales. At test time, the internalized world model enables private rehearsal before committed execution, yielding further gains under a moderate rehearsal budget without additional external interaction. Our findings establish world rehearsal as a new path toward scaling LLM agent training beyond the constraints of external environments. Our code is publicly available athttps://github.com/Within-yao/EnvACE.

1 简介Introduction

人们越来越期望大型语言模型 (LLM) 在现实应用程序中充当代理，与用户交互以收集信息并调用工具来查询或更新特定环境的状态Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly expected to serve as agents in real-world applications, interacting with users to gather information and invoking tools to query or update the state of a specific environment(Z.ai, 2026; OpenAI, 2025; Team et al., 2026)。 在此类环境中取得成功需要有效地将对话和工具使用结合起来，根据环境反馈调整行动，并在长期互动范围内进行操作。 为了发展这种能力，我们希望让法学硕士接触足够多样化的工具和用户请求，同时提供有效的优化信号。. Success in such settings requires effectively combining dialogue and tool use, adapting actions based on environment feedback, and operating over long interaction horizons. To develop such ability, we expect to expose LLMs to a sufficiently diverse range of tools and user requests while providing effective optimization signals.

为了让法学硕士接触丰富多样的工具交互环境，早期工作收集了工具使用和用户交互的轨迹，以进行监督微调To expose LLMs to rich and diverse tool-interactive environments, early work collected trajectories of tool use and user interaction for supervised fine-tuning(Zeng et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025; Qin et al., 2023)。 随着强化学习的成功和可验证的奖励（RLVR）. Following the success of reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR)(Lambert et al., 2024; Guo and others, 2025)，最近的工作自动合成了可执行环境，其中代理通过交互生成符合策略的轨迹，并从可验证的任务奖励中学习, recent work has automatically synthesized executable environments in which agents generate on-policy trajectories through interaction and learn from verifiable task rewards(Dong et al., 2026; Tu et al., 2026), 为了提供更多信息的训练信号，一些方法还训练策略来预测其自身行为所产生的观察结果作为辅助目标, To provide more informative training signals, some methods additionally train the policy to predict the observations resulting from its own actions as an auxiliary objective(Lu et al., 2026a; Guo et al., 2025)。 然而，扩展此类环境依赖于昂贵且复杂的合成管道，而随着复杂性的增加，验证其正确性变得更加困难。 基于 LLM 的模拟器通过直接生成环境响应（包括工具输出和用户反馈）提供成本更低、更灵活的替代方案，而不需要完全可执行的环境. However, scaling such environments relies on costly and complex synthesis pipelines, while their correctness becomes harder to verify as complexity grows. LLM-based simulators provide a less costly and more flexible alternative by directly generating environment responses, including tool outputs and user feedback, without requiring a fully executable environment(Li et al., 2025b; Ruan et al., 2024)。 然而，他们的反应可能不准确或不一致，将他们接地仍然需要来自真实环境的监督。 因此，现有方法仍然从根本上依赖于对真实环境的访问，无论是为了生成可执行的交互还是为了奠定和验证模拟的交互。. However, their responses may be inaccurate or inconsistent, and grounding them still requires supervision from real environments. Consequently, existing approaches remain fundamentally dependent on access to real environments, either for generating executable interactions or for grounding and validating simulated ones.

如图所示As illustrated in Figure1，现有的训练范例从外部环境或单独的模拟器获得继续推出所需的响应。 在这两种情况下，策略都会学习对外部提供的响应采取行动，从而将环境建模置于执行策略之外。 我们认为，有效的代理策略不仅应该采取行动来完成任务，还应该模拟环境如何响应其行动。 受到学习世界模型成功的启发, existing training paradigms obtain the responses needed to continue a rollout from either an external environment or a separate simulator. In both cases, the policy learns to act on externally supplied responses, leaving environment modeling outside the acting policy. We argue that an effective agent policy should not only act to accomplish the task but also model how the environment responds to its actions. Inspired by the success of learned world models(Zuo et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2025)，我们通过为策略分配两个角色来实现这个想法：与环境交互的参与者和向参与者提供反馈的环境，无需外部真实环境交互。 策略本身不只是消耗或预测外部提供的观察结果，而是制定环境的响应，从而将行为及其引发的响应之间的关系内化为自己的参数。, we realize this idea by assigning the policy two roles: an actor that interacts with the environment and an environment that provides feedback to the actor, without external real-environment interaction. Rather than merely consuming or predicting externally supplied observations, the policy itself enacts the environment’s responses, thereby internalizing the relationship between actions and their induced responses into its own parameters.

为此，我们提出了 EnvACE，这是一种代理强化学习方法，可以训练单个策略来执行和演练其环境。 在每个回合中，策略首先发出工具调用，然后演练工具调用将引发的环境响应，并在此演练中确定下一步行动。 因此，轨迹不再是与外部环境的对话，而是政策本身展开的过程。使用任务成功奖励来端到端联合优化表演和排练行为。 我们将这个过程称为世界演练：之前的工作分配给单独模拟器的世界建模功能被吸收到策略中并内联执行，使策略能够内化其行为如何塑造环境响应，同时行动和世界建模作为一个统一的过程展开。Toward this end, we propose EnvACE, an agentic RL method that trains a single policy to both act and rehearse its environment. At each turn, the policy first issues a tool call, then rehearses the environment response that the tool call would induce, and conditions its next action on this rehearsal. A trajectory is therefore no longer a dialogue with an external environment, but a process unfolded by the policy itself. The acting and rehearsal behaviors are jointly optimized end-to-end using a task-success reward. We call this process world rehearsal: the world modeling capability that prior work assigns to a separate simulator is absorbed into the policy and executed inline, enabling the policy to internalize how its actions shape environment responses while acting and world modeling unfold as a unified process.

跨广泛代理基准的广泛实验，包括 BFCL-v4、Extensive experiments across a wide range of agentic benchmarks, including BFCL-v4,τ2\tau^{2}-Bench、VitaBench 和 FinMCP-Bench 表明 EnvACE 的性能始终优于强大的基线。 通过表演和排练的联合优化，EnvACE 捕获动作与其引起的环境响应之间的关系，将这些动态内化到政策中以支持更普遍的决策。 在测试时，这种内在的世界模型允许政策在实际执行之前排练候选行动，预测其结果，避免代价高昂的错误，并收集有用的经验，而无需与外部环境进行额外的交互。 我们的实验进一步研究了性能如何随排练尝试次数的变化而变化，并表明适度的排练预算可以提高测试时的性能。-Bench, VitaBench, and FinMCP-Bench, show that EnvACE consistently outperforms strong baselines. Through the joint optimization of acting and rehearsal, EnvACE captures the relationship between actions and the environment responses they induce, internalizing these dynamics within the policy to support more generalizable decision making. At test time, this internalized world model allows the policy to rehearse candidate actions before real execution, anticipate their outcomes, avoid costly mistakes, and gather useful experience without additional interaction with the external environment. Our experiments further examine how performance varies with the number of rehearsal attempts and show that a moderate rehearsal budget improves test-time performance.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 代理强化学习Agentic Reinforcement Learning

已经开发出越来越现实的基准来通过与现实世界环境的长期交互来评估语言代理Increasingly realistic benchmarks have been developed to evaluate language agents through long-horizon interactions with real-world environments(Xie et al., 2024; Jimenez et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2026b)。强化学习极大地改进了语言模型推理，最近的工作进一步提高了其效率并将其扩展到搜索和工具增强推理. Reinforcement learning has substantially improved language-model reasoning, with recent work further advancing its efficiency and extending it to search and tool-augmented reasoning(Guo and others, 2025; Yao et al., 2026; Jin et al., 2025; Feng et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025a)。构建于交错推理、行动和观察的界面之上. Building on interfaces that interleave reasoning, actions, and observations(Yao et al., 2022)，代理强化学习将结果优化扩展到网络导航、多轮工具使用、GUI 交互和软件工程, agentic RL extends outcome optimization across web navigation, multi-turn tool use, GUI interaction, and software engineering(Qi et al., 2025; Singh et al., 2025; Lu et al., 2026b; Wei et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2026a)。由此产生的长期轨迹也激发了训练稳定性、学分分配和特定角色优势估计方面的工作. The resulting long-horizon trajectories have also motivated work on training stability, credit assignment, and role-specific advantage estimation(Feng et al., 2026a; b; Lu et al., 2026c).

2.2 LLM 代理的环境建模Environment Modeling for LLM Agents

交互式代理训练需要一个将操作映射到响应和任务结果的环境。固定的可执行环境提供了基础转换，但构建和扩展成本高昂Interactive agent training requires an environment that maps actions to responses and task outcomes. Fixed executable environments provide grounded transitions but are costly to construct and scale(Lu et al., 2025; Yao et al., 2024)。最近的工作综合了可执行工具、数据库、任务和评估器. Recent work instead synthesizes executable tools, databases, tasks, and evaluators(Song et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026b; Tu et al., 2026; Dong et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2026)，或者使用语言模型来模拟环境反馈, or uses language models to simulate environment feedback(Li et al., 2025b; Xiao et al., 2026)。这些方法扩展了可用的交互体验，同时将环境动态保持在政策之外。学习的世界模型通过前瞻和规划进一步支持代理. These approaches expand the available interaction experience while keeping environment dynamics external to the policy. Learned world models further support agents through lookahead and planning(Chae et al., 2025; Zeng et al., 2026; Liu et al., 2026)，或者将环境建模目标纳入代理培训中, or by incorporating environment-modeling objectives into agent training(Yu et al., 2026; Lu et al., 2026a; Wang et al., 2026a; Cai et al., 2026)。虽然这些信号通常用作辅助或规划信号，但 EnvACE 直接优化排练的环境响应，作为其政策轨迹的一部分。. Whereas these signals generally serve as auxiliary or planning signals, EnvACE directly optimizes rehearsed environment responses as part of its on-policy trajectories.

4 方法Method

图Figure2介绍了 EnvACE 的概述，其中包括世界排练、角色明智的 GRPO 优化和测试时间扩展。在训练期间，策略在表演和排练之间交替，将每个生成的环境响应附加到交互历史中，以便在没有外部环境的情况下展开轨迹。角色明智的 GRPO 对两个角色使用单独的基线，同时联合更新策略，使其能够内部化行动如何塑造环境响应。在测试时，该策略并行或顺序执行私有排练，将它们汇总到排练内存中，并使用该内存来指导外部环境中的已提交执行。presents an overview of EnvACE, which comprises world rehearsal, role-wise GRPO optimization, and test-time scaling. During training, the policy alternates between acting and rehearsal, appending each generated environment response to the interaction history so that the trajectory unfolds without an external environment. Role-wise GRPO uses separate baselines for the two roles while jointly updating the policy, enabling it to internalize how actions shape environment responses. At test time, the policy performs private rehearsals in parallel or sequentially, summarizes them into a rehearsal memory, and uses this memory to guide a committed execution in the external environment.

4.1 世界排练World Rehearsal

仅当外部环境或模拟器在每个操作后提供响应时，传统的代理推出才能继续。因此，政策学习的可扩展性和质量仍然与外部响应提供者相关，而对环境响应进行建模的能力仍然在执行政策之外。世界演练通过使环境响应生成成为政策本身的明确作用，改变了这种劳动分工。这使得策略能够展开自己的训练轨迹，而无需查询外部环境。A conventional agent rollout can proceed only when an external environment or simulator provides a response after each action. Consequently, the scalability and quality of policy learning remain tied to an external response provider, while the ability to model environment responses remains outside the acting policy. World rehearsal changes this division of labor by making environment response generation an explicit role of the policy itself. This allows the policy to unfold its own training trajectories without querying an external environment.
