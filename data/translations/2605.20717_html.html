电子侦察：E-ReCON:用于传统和尖峰神经边缘推理的节能和资源高效的精确可配置稀疏 nvCIM 宏An Energy- and Resource-Efficient Precision-Configurable Sparse nvCIM Macro for Conventional and Spiking Neural Edge Inference

Ankit Kumar Tenwar, Mukul Lokhande, Santosh Kumar Vishvakarma
This work was supported partially by the Dept of Science and Technology (DST), Govt of India, for the INSPIRE PhD fellowship, and MeitY/SMDP-C2S for ASIC design tools.
摘要Abstract

这项工作提出了 E-ReCON，这是一种基于紧凑型 3T1R ReRAM 位单元的 16 Kb 能源和资源高效型数字内存计算 (DCIM) 宏，用于边缘 AI 推理。所提出的位单元仅占用 0.85This work presents E-ReCON, a 16 Kb energy- and resource-efficient digital compute-in-memory (DCIM) macro based on a compact 3T1R ReRAM bitcell for edge-AI inference. The proposed bitcell occupies only 0.85μ\mum2并支持传统卷积神经网络 (CNN) 和尖峰神经网络 (SNN) 工作负载的可靠的基于 AND 的内存乘法。为了减少累加开销，引入了一种新颖的交错式 10T/28T 加法器树，与传统的基于 28T RCA 的设计相比，晶体管数量和功耗分别减少了 37% 和 28%。该宏在 1.2 V 电压下采用 65 nm CMOS 实现，可实现 0.48 ns 的最小延迟、2.31-3.1 TOPS 的吞吐量以及高达 419 TOPS/W 的能效。在 LeNet-5、AlexNet 和 CNN-8 模型上进行评估时，该宏在 MNIST/A-Z、CIFAR-10 和 SVHN 数据集上分别实现了 97.81%、93.23% 和 96.51% 的准确率。此外，40%的剪枝保留了近99.8%的原始精度，同时减少了MAC运算和计算周期。对于面向 SNN 的工作负载，所提出的 AND 型位单元有效地支持低切换活动的峰值权重乘法，其中 2A2W 配置在 CIFAR-10、CIFAR-100 和 ImageNet-1K 数据集上的 VGG-8、VGG-16 和 ResNet-18 网络上实现了接近 FP32 基线的精度。与之前基于 ADC 的 ReRAM-CIM 设计相比，所提出的架构将延迟和能源效率提高了近 30-40%，同时在完全 PVT 和 ReRAM 可变性下保持稳健运行。总体而言，E-ReCON 为下一代边缘人工智能、物联网、生物医学传感和神经形态应用提供了一个可扩展、低延迟且节能的 nvCIM 平台。and supports reliable AND-based in-memory multiplication for both conventional convolutional neural network (CNN) and spiking neural network (SNN) workloads. To reduce accumulation overhead, a novel interleaved 10T/28T adder tree is introduced, reducing transistor count and power consumption by 37% and 28%, respectively, compared to a conventional 28T RCA-based design. Implemented in 65 nm CMOS at 1.2 V, the proposed macro achieves a minimum latency of 0.48 ns, throughput of 2.31-3.1 TOPS, and energy efficiency of up to 419 TOPS/W. When evaluated on LeNet-5, AlexNet, and CNN-8 models, the macro achieves 97.81%, 93.23%, and 96.51% accuracy on MNIST/A-Z, CIFAR-10, and SVHN datasets, respectively. In addition, 40% pruning preserves nearly 99.8% of the original accuracy while reducing MAC operations and computation cycles. For SNN-oriented workloads, the proposed AND-type bitcell efficiently supports spike-weight multiplication with low switching activity, where the 2A2W configuration achieves accuracy close to the FP32 baseline across VGG-8, VGG-16, and ResNet-18 networks on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet-1K datasets. Compared to prior ADC-based ReRAM-CIM designs, the proposed architecture improves latency and energy efficiency by nearly 30-40% while maintaining robust operation under full PVT and ReRAM variability. Overall, E-ReCON provides a scalable, low-latency, and energy-efficient nvCIM platform for next-generation edge-AI, IoT, biomedical sensing, and neuromorphic applications.

I 简介Introduction

人工智能 (AI) 工作负载越来越多地部署在边缘设备上，用于图像分类、语音识别、生物医学传感和自主监控等应用。这些工作负载严重依赖卷积神经网络 (CNN) 和其他需要数十亿次乘法累加 (MAC) 运算的神经模型。然而，由于内存和处理单元之间频繁的数据移动，传统的冯诺依曼架构面临着显着的延迟和能源开销，这使得它们对于资源有限的边缘平台来说效率低下Artificial Intelligence (AI) workloads are increasingly being deployed on edge devices for applications such as image classification, speech recognition, biomedical sensing, and autonomous monitoring. These workloads rely heavily on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and other neural models that require billions of multiply-and-accumulate (MAC) operations. However, conventional Von-Neumann architectures suffer from significant latency and energy overhead due to frequent data movement between memory and processing units, making them inefficient for resource-constrained edge platforms[13]。为了应对这一挑战，内存计算 (CIM) 架构已成为一种有前途的替代方案，它可以直接在内存阵列内执行计算，从而减少数据传输、提高吞吐量并提高能源效率. To address this challenge, compute-in-memory (CIM) architectures have emerged as a promising alternative by performing computation directly inside the memory array, thereby reducing data transfer, improving throughput, and enhancing energy efficiency[8, 27, 23].

在MRAM、FERAM、PCRAM和ReRAM等各种非易失性存储器（NVM）技术中，ReRAM因其低工作电压、快速开关速度、紧凑的占用空间和CMOS兼容性而在CIM系统中引起了相当大的关注Among various non-volatile memory (NVM) technologies, such as MRAM, FERAM, PCRAM, and ReRAM, ReRAM has attracted considerable attention for CIM systems because of its low operating voltage, fast switching speed, compact footprint, and CMOS compatibility[17, 15, 18]。此外，ReRAM 的非易失性可实现低待机功耗，并减少外部 NVM、DRAM 和基于 SRAM 的计算单元之间昂贵的数据传输需求。通过直接将神经权重存储在 ReRAM 阵列内，可以显着减少具有严格能量和延迟限制的边缘 AI 设备的片外存储器访问。. Furthermore, the non-volatility of ReRAM enables low standby power and reduces the need for costly data transfers between external NVM, DRAM, and SRAM-based compute units. By directly storing neural weights inside the ReRAM array, off-chip memory accesses can be significantly reduced for edge-AI devices with strict energy and latency constraints.

典型的基于 ReRAM 的 CIM 架构依赖于位线上的模拟电流累积来执行 MAC 操作。尽管此类方法可提供高密度，但它们通常会遇到信噪比 (SNR) 低、过程电压温度 (PVT) 变化、ADC 开销和精度有限的问题Typical ReRAM-based CIM architectures rely on analog current accumulation at the bitline to perform MAC operations. Although such approaches provide high density, they often suffer from low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), process-voltage-temperature (PVT) variations, ADC overhead, and limited precision[10, 14, 24]。电流域 CIM 提供高并行性，但在较大阵列尺寸下存在读取电流大和能源效率下降的问题. Current-domain CIM offers high parallelism but suffers from large read currents and degraded energy efficiency at larger array sizes[30]。电荷域 CIM 通过电容积累或 2T2R 结构减少静态电流，但会带来面积开销和额外的外围电路. Charge-domain CIM reduces static current through capacitive accumulation or 2T2R structures, but introduces area overhead and additional peripheral circuitry[6]。时域 CIM 提高了针对电流求和误差的鲁棒性，但仍然需要高分辨率 ADC，从而增加面积和延迟. Time-domain CIM improves robustness against current summation errors, but still requires high-resolution ADCs, increasing area and latency[22].

基于数字 SRAM 的 DCIM 架构通过提供确定性计算克服了模拟方法的许多精度和噪声限制Digital SRAM-based DCIM architectures overcome many of the precision and noise limitations of analog approaches by providing deterministic computation[7, 28]。然而，模拟 ReRAM-CIM 架构仍然严重依赖 ADC 或时分复用转换方案，这会降低吞吐量并增加外设开销. However, analog ReRAM-CIM architectures still rely heavily on ADCs or time-multiplexed conversion schemes, which reduce throughput and increase peripheral overhead[33]。为了解决这些限制，无 ADC、基于 ReRAM 的数字 CIM 宏变得越来越有吸引力。除了传统的 CNN 工作负载之外，所提出的基于 AND 的 3T1R nvCIM 位单元还适用于尖峰神经网络 (SNN) 推理。由于基于尖峰的计算依赖于二进制激活和事件驱动的累积，因此所提出的位单元可以使用相同的基于 AND 的操作直接支持尖峰权重乘法。与之前基于 AND 的面向 SNN 的 CIM 工作类似. To address these limitations, ADC-less digital ReRAM-based CIM macros are becoming increasingly attractive. In addition to conventional CNN workloads, the proposed AND-based 3T1R nvCIM bitcell is also suitable for spiking neural network (SNN) inference. Since spike-based computation relies on binary activations and event-driven accumulation, the proposed bitcell can directly support spike-weight multiplication using the same AND-based operation. Similar to prior AND-based SNN-oriented CIM works[34]，所提出的架构可实现低功耗尖峰域累积，同时减少开关活动和内存访问开销。, the proposed architecture enables low-power spike-domain accumulation with reduced switching activity and memory access overhead.

这项工作提出了采用 65 nm CMOS 技术的基于 16 Kb ReRAM 的数字内存计算 (DCIM) 宏，以实现高效的边缘 AI 推理。所提出的架构通过紧凑的 3T1R 数字 ReRAM 位单元和用于低面积累加的交错 10T/28T 加法器树消除了 ADC/DAC 开销。这项工作的主要贡献总结如下：This work proposes a 16 Kb ReRAM-based digital compute-in-memory (DCIM) macro in 65 nm CMOS technology for efficient edge-AI inference. The proposed architecture eliminates ADC/DAC overhead through a compact 3T1R digital ReRAM bitcell and an interleaved 10T/28T adder tree for low-area accumulation. Ⅱ-CII-C 交错 10T/28T 加法器树Interleaved 10T/28T Adder Tree

加法器树的面积开销仍然是 DCIM 架构中的主要挑战之一。传统的纹波进位加法器每个全加器需要 28 个晶体管，这增加了面积和功耗。为了解决这个问题，这项工作使用了一个紧凑的 10T 全加器The area overhead of the adder tree remains one of the major challenges in DCIM architectures. Conventional ripple-carry adders require 28 transistors per full adder, which increases both area and power. To address this issue, this work uses a compact 10T full adder from[23]以交错的方式与传统的28T全加器结合。combined with conventional 28T full adders in an interleaved manner.

与传统的基于 28T RCA 的结构相比，交错加法器树减少了近 37% 的晶体管数量，并将功耗降低了 28%The interleaved adder tree reduces transistor count by nearly 37% and lowers power consumption by 28% compared to a conventional 28T RCA-based structure[26, 21]。由于传输晶体管逻辑导致 10T 全加器引入电压降级，因此可以在连续的 RCA 级上交替使用 10T 和 28T 全加器以恢复信号完整性。在第一级中，RCA 首先是 28T 全加器，然后是 10T 全加器。在后续阶段中，交替布置，使得电压降级不会传播到加法器树上。. In the first stage, the RCA begins with a 28T full adder followed by a 10T full adder. In subsequent stages, the arrangement is alternated such that voltage degradation does not propagate across the adder tree.

Ⅱ-DII-D 基于 ReRAM 的 16KB 数字 CIM 宏ReRAM-Based 16KB Digital CIM Macro

拟议的基于 16 Kb ReRAM 的 DCIM 宏被组织成 64 个独立的存储体，每个存储体由 64 行和 4 列组成。每个Bank支持4位重量精度，多个Bank组合可以达到更高的精度。该架构支持 1 至 8 位的位串行输入精度，允许灵活映射卷积层和全连接层。操作期间，输入位从最高有效位 (MSB) 到最低有效位 (LSB) 按顺序应用。在每个周期中，ReRAM 阵列计算部分积，这些部分积使用所提出的低区域交错加法器树进行累加，并存储在本地 10 位累加寄存器中。这种位串行方法可以支持任意输入精度，对于 N 位输入只需 N 个计算周期。The proposed 16 Kb ReRAM-based DCIM macro is organised into 64 independent banks, each consisting of 64 rows and 4 columns. Each bank supports 4-bit weight precision, while higher precision can be achieved by combining multiple banks. The architecture supports bit-serial input precision from 1 to 8 bits, allowing flexible mapping of convolutional and fully connected layers. During operation, input bits are applied sequentially from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). In each cycle, the ReRAM array computes partial products, which are accumulated using the proposed low-area interleaved adder tree and stored in local 10-bit accumulation registers. This bit-serial approach enables support for arbitrary input precision with only N computation cycles for N-bit inputs.

所提出的架构还通过消除 ADC/DAC 模块来减少外设开销。因此，功耗主要分布在累加器、激活函数、池化、批量归一化和控制逻辑上。去除 ADC/DAC 电路可显着提高吞吐量和能源效率，同时保持全数字操作。除了传统的 CNN 推理之外，所提出的宏还可以支持事件驱动的 SNN 执行。由于 SNN 激活是二进制尖峰事件，因此所提出的 3T1R-AND 位单元可以直接执行尖峰权重乘法，无需额外的转换电路。这使得尖峰域部分和的有效累积成为可能，同时保留了所提出架构的低功耗和无 ADC 特性。The proposed architecture also reduces peripheral overhead by eliminating ADC/DAC blocks. As a result, power consumption is distributed mainly across the accumulator, activation functions, pooling, batch normalisation, and control logic. The removal of ADC/DAC circuitry significantly improves throughput and energy efficiency while maintaining fully digital operation. Beyond conventional CNN inference, the proposed macro can also support event-driven SNN execution. Since SNN activations are binary spike events, the proposed 3T1R-AND bitcell can directly perform spike-weight multiplication without additional conversion circuitry. This enables efficient accumulation of spike-domain partial sums while preserving the low-power and ADC-less characteristics of the proposed architecture.

IV 结论Conclusion

这项工作提出了 E-ReCON，这是一种紧凑且节能的基于 ReRAM 的 DCIM 宏，用于边缘 AI 推理。所提出的架构采用 3T1R AND 型 ReRAM 位单元来消除 ADC/DAC 开销，同时为 CNN 和 SNN 工作负载提供可靠的内存乘法。此外，与传统的基于 28T RCA 的结构相比，新颖的交错式 10T/28T 加法器树将晶体管数量和功耗分别减少了 37% 和 28%。This work presents E-ReCON, a compact and energy-efficient ReRAM-based DCIM macro for edge-AI inference. The proposed architecture employs a 3T1R AND-type ReRAM bitcell to eliminate ADC/DAC overhead while enabling reliable in-memory multiplication for both CNN and SNN workloads. In addition, a novel interleaved 10T/28T adder tree reduces transistor count and power consumption by 37% and 28%, respectively, compared to a conventional 28T RCA-based structure.

该宏在 65 nm CMOS 中实现，可实现 0.48 ns 的最小延迟、2.31-3.1 TOPS 的吞吐量以及 419 TOPS/W 的峰值能效。在低精度配置下，该架构在 LeNet-5 上实现了 97.81% 的准确率，在 CIFAR-10 的 AlexNet 上实现了 93.23% 的准确率，在 SVHN 的 CNN-8 上实现了 96.51% 的准确率。此外，40% 的剪枝保留了近 99.8% 的原始精度，同时减少了 MAC 运算、内存使用和计算周期。Implemented in 65 nm CMOS, the proposed macro achieves a minimum latency of 0.48 ns, throughput of 2.31-3.1 TOPS, and peak energy efficiency of 419 TOPS/W. The architecture achieves 97.81% accuracy on LeNet-5, 93.23% on AlexNet for CIFAR-10, and 96.51% on CNN-8 for SVHN under low-precision configurations. In addition, 40% pruning preserves nearly 99.8% of the original accuracy while reducing MAC operations, memory usage, and computation cycles.

对于 SNN 工作负载，所提出的位单元支持尖峰域乘法和事件驱动累加，无需额外的转换电路。 2A2W 精度设置在 VGG-8、VGG-16 和 ResNet-18 模型中实现了接近 FP32 的精度，而事件驱动推理则减少了内存访问、切换活动和累积开销。总体而言，E-ReCON 在支持 1-8 位精度的同时，展示了针对流程和 ReRAM 变化的强大鲁棒性。这些特性使其适用于电池供电的边缘人工智能、物联网和神经形态应用。For SNN workloads, the proposed bitcell supports spike-domain multiplication and event-driven accumulation without additional conversion circuitry. The 2A2W precision setting achieves accuracy close to FP32 across VGG-8, VGG-16, and ResNet-18 models, while event-driven inference reduces memory accesses, switching activity, and accumulation overhead. Overall, E-ReCON demonstrates strong robustness against process and ReRAM variability while supporting 1-8 bit precision. These characteristics make it suitable for battery-powered edge-AI, IoT, and neuromorphic applications.
