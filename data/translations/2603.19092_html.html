摘要Abstract

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 越来越多地部署在现实世界和具体环境中，其中安全决策取决于视觉上下文。然而，目前尚不清楚哪些视觉证据推动了这些判断。我们研究 VLM 中的多模式安全行为是否可以通过简单的语义线索来引导。我们引入了一个语义引导框架，该框架应用受控的文本、视觉和认知干预，而不改变底层场景内容。为了评估这些影响，我们提出了 SAVeS（语义线索下的情境安全基准），以及区分行为拒绝、扎根安全推理和错误拒绝的评估协议。跨多个 VLM 的实验和额外的最先进基准表明，安全决策对语义线索高度敏感，表明依赖于习得的视觉语言关联，而不是基于基础的视觉理解。我们进一步证明自动转向管道可以利用这些机制，突出多模式安全系统中的潜在漏洞。Vision-language models (VLMs) are increasingly deployed in real-world and embodied settings where safety decisions depend on visual context. However, it remains unclear which visual evidence drives these judgments. We study whether multimodal safety behavior in VLMs can be steered by simple semantic cues. We introduce a semantic steering framework that applies controlled textual, visual, and cognitive interventions without changing the underlying scene content. To evaluate these effects, we propose SAVeS, a benchmark for situational safety under semantic cues, together with an evaluation protocol that separates behavioral refusal, grounded safety reasoning, and false refusals. Experiments across multiple VLMs and an additional state-of-the-art benchmark show that safety decisions are highly sensitive to semantic cues, indicating reliance on learned visual–linguistic associations rather than grounded visual understanding. We further demonstrate that automated steering pipelines can exploit these mechanisms, highlighting a potential vulnerability in multimodal safety systems.

5 结论Conclusions

本文研究了具体 VLM 中多模式情景安全决策的语义引导。在 MSSBench-Embodied 和 SAVeS 中，我们发现安全行为对语义线索高度敏感。视觉标记通常比仅坐标文本提示更强烈地引导行为，并且添加明确的焦点指令通常会进一步增加拒绝行为。与此同时，这些收益并不都是有益的。较高的拒绝率可能会导致更多的误报，这表明存在明显的校准权衡。我们的消融表明，效果取决于标记语义、提示指令兼容性和场景上下文，而不仅仅是空间突出显示。最后，自动化管道确认转向是双向的：辅助管道仅产生适度的、依赖于模型的改进，而对抗性覆盖可以可靠地利用相同的机制并引起虚假拒绝。总体而言，当前的安全行为是高度可操纵的，但只是部分接地，这激发了更强大、具有接地意识的安全调整。This paper investigated semantic steering for multimodal situational safety decisions in embodied VLMs. Across MSSBench-Embodied and SAVeS, we found that safety behavior is highly sensitive to semantic cues. Visual markers generally steer behavior more strongly than coordinate-only text prompts, and adding explicit focus instructions often further increases refusal behavior. At the same time, these gains are not uniformly beneficial. Higher refusal rates can lead to more false alarms, indicating a clear calibration trade-off. Our ablations show that the effect depends on marker semantics, cue–instruction compatibility, and scene context, rather than spatial highlighting alone. Finally, automated pipelines confirm that steering is bidirectional: assistive pipelines yield only modest, model-dependent improvements, whereas adversarial overlays can reliably exploit the same mechanism and induce spurious refusals. Overall, current safety behavior is highly steerable but only partially grounded, motivating more robust, grounding-aware safety alignment.

我们将 SAVeS 构建为用于情景安全评估的合成配对数据集，如图所示We construct SAVeS as a synthetic paired dataset for situational safety evaluation, as illustrated inFigure 6。该设计受到 MSSBench 任务类别的启发，但明确针对固定指令下的视觉基础。每个场景都遵循成对的安全-不安全结构，其中相同的指令同时呈现安全和不安全的视觉上下文。. The design is inspired by the task categories of MSSBench but explicitly targets visual grounding under fixed instructions. Each scenario follows a paired safe–unsafe structure in which the same instruction is presented with both a Safe and an Unsafe visual context.

我们首先手动创建了一小组参考场景，每个场景都包含上下文中立的指令、安全和不安全的场景描述、真实的危险原理以及相应的图像生成提示。在几次设置中使用这些示例，我们提示 Gemini 生成其他候选场景。然后对生成的场景进行手动审查和编辑，以确保逻辑一致性、清晰的危险识别以及指令和视觉场景之间的一致性。We first manually created a small set of reference scenarios, each consisting of a context-neutral instruction, safe and unsafe scene descriptions, a ground-truth hazard rationale, and corresponding image-generation prompts. Using these examples in a few-shot setup, we prompted Gemini to generate additional candidate scenarios. The generated scenarios were then manually reviewed and edited to ensure logical consistency, clear hazard identification, and alignment between the instruction and the visual scene.

对于每个最终的场景，使用 Gemini-2.5-flash-image 的条件特定提示分别生成安全和不安全图像。低质量或语义错位的样本被迭代地重新生成，直到它们满足可靠的安全判断的视觉保真度和场景一致性要求。最终的 SAVeS 数据集包含 60 个场景，每个场景由一条指令与一张安全图像和一张不安全图像配对组成，以及真实的危险注释和场景元数据。For each finalized scenario, safe and unsafe images were generated separately using condition-specific prompts with Gemini-2.5-flash-image. Low-quality or semantically misaligned samples were iteratively regenerated until they satisfied visual fidelity and scenario consistency requirements for reliable safety judgment. The final SAVeS dataset contains 60 scenarios, each consisting of one instruction paired with a safe image and an unsafe image, along with ground-truth hazard annotations and scenario metadata.

除了基本的安全/不安全图像对之外，我们还从相同的 60 个场景中生成一系列特定于干预的视图，以支持受控转向分析。使用确定性转换脚本，我们得出颜色标记变体（红/白/绿/黄/橙）、诱饵和对抗性覆盖、语义文本贴纸变体（安全/危险）和上下文视图变体（仅裁剪和屏蔽）。我们还维护边界框注释，并在所有变体（每个场景的安全/不安全实例）中保留一致的配对索引方案，从而在受控视觉干预下实现匹配比较。Beyond the base safe/unsafe image pairs, we generate a family of intervention-specific views from the same 60 scenarios to support controlled steering analyses. Using deterministic transformation scripts, we derive color-marker variants (red/white/green/yellow/orange), decoy and adversarial overlays, semantic text-sticker variants (safe/danger), and context-view variants (crop-only and masked). We also maintain bounding-box annotations and preserve a consistent paired indexing scheme across all variants (safe/unsafe instances per scenario), enabling matched comparisons under controlled visual interventions.

10 局限性和附加讨论Limitations and Additional Discussion

我们的研究有几个局限性。首先，语义引导对提示措辞和提示设计很敏感，微小的变化可以产生明显的行为变化。其次，观察到的效果强烈依赖于模型，这表明一个模型系列或规模的改进不一定会转移到其他模型或保证更好的校准。第三，虽然 MSSBench 和 SAVeS 可以实现受控配对分析，但它们并未涵盖现实世界视觉条件或长视野体现交互的全部多样性。第四，我们的指标依赖于法学硕士的评估者；尽管我们应用了一致性检查和手动验证，但分级噪声或偏差仍然可能会影响困难的情况。最后，当前的设置主要是单图像和单轮，使得时间鲁棒性和闭环校正成为悬而未决的问题。更广泛地说，更强的拒绝行为和增加的错误拒绝之间的权衡仍未解决。Our study has several limitations. First, semantic steering is sensitive to prompt wording and cue design, and small changes can produce noticeable behavioral shifts. Second, the observed effects are strongly model-dependent, indicating that improvements in one model family or scale do not necessarily transfer to others or guarantee better calibration. Third, while MSSBench and SAVeS enable controlled paired analysis, they do not cover the full diversity of real-world visual conditions or long-horizon embodied interactions. Fourth, our metrics rely on an LLM-based evaluator; although we applied consistency checks and manual verification, grading noise or bias may still affect difficult cases. Finally, the current setup is primarily single-image and single-turn, leaving temporal robustness and closed-loop correction as open problems. More broadly, the trade-off between stronger refusal behavior and increased false refusals remains unresolved.
