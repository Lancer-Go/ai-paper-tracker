1 简介Introduction

随着大型语言模型的快速发展，基于 LLM 的系统（例如客户服务助理、编码代理、虚拟医生和聊天机器人）现已被全球数十亿人使用。 几乎每个部署的 LLM 支持的产品的基础都是With the rapid advancement of Large Language Models, LLM-based systems like customer service assistants, coding agents, virtual doctors, and companion chatbots are now used by billions of people around the world. Underlying nearly every deployed LLM-powered product is a系统提示system prompt：一组由开发人员编写的指令，用于塑造模型之前的行为方式: a set of developer-authored instructions that shapes how the model behaves before任何any用户交互开始。 系统提示定义模型的角色、范围和操作边界。 它们指定模型应该说什么、应该拒绝什么、应该优先考虑谁的利益以及应该如何处理敏感或模糊的情况。 系统提示构成开发人员配置的主要杠杆 将通用基础模型集成到特定产品中，并在用户与产品的交互中持续存在。user interaction begins. System prompts define the model’s persona, scope, and operational boundaries. They specify what the model should say, what it should refuse, whose interests it should prioritize, and how it should handle sensitive or ambiguous situations. System prompts constitute the primary lever through which developers configure a general-purpose foundation model into a specific product, and persist across user interactions with the product.

尽管系统提示在塑造人工智能系统的行为方面发挥着重要作用，但它们很少被披露，也不受系统的独立审查。 虽然模型提供商在构建安全且一致的基础模型上花费了巨大的努力，但系统提示的不透明性成为一个严重问题，因为如果系统提示指示其将参与优先于诚实、忽略安全指导或向用户隐藏其 AI 身份，则一致的模型仍然可能具有欺骗或操纵行为。 例如，提示模型“永远不要说你是人工智能语言模型或助手”。或者“在没有用户询问的情况下巧妙地将对话引向新的方向”直接违背用户的利益，无论底层模型训练得多么仔细Despite their role in shaping AI systems’ behavior, system prompts are rarely disclosed and are not subject to systematic independent review. While model providers have spent enormous efforts on building safe and aligned foundation models, the opacity of system prompts becomes a serious concern because an aligned model can still have deceptive or manipulative behaviors if its system prompt instructs it to prioritize engagement over honesty, omit safety guidance, or conceal its AI identity from users. For instance, a prompt instructing the model to “NEVER say you are an AI language model or an assistant.” or to “cleverly steer the conversation in a new direction without the user asking” directly works against user interests, regardless of how carefully the underlying model was trained111这两个例子都来自我们收集的真实系统提示Both examples come from real system prompts in our collection.

随着基础模型在现实世界应用中的广泛采用，系统提示已悄然获得了影响数十亿用户与人工智能交互的能力。然而，作为日益复杂的人工智能系统中的新产物，系统提示却很少受到关注。政府、学者和从业者制定了人工智能系统的治理标准With the wide adoption of foundation models in real-world applications, system prompts have quietly gained the power to shape billions of users’ interactions with AI. Yet as a new artifact within increasingly complex AI systems, system prompts have received little attention. Governments, scholars, and practitioners have developed governance standards for AI systems(nist_ai_600_1, eu_ai_act, farley2023ai, manheim2025necessity, brundage2026frontier)和防御对抗性攻击的安全工具and security tools to defend against adversarial attacks(tedeschi2024alert, zhu2025harmtransform)，但是这些努力都没有提供有关系统提示中应该或不应该包含哪些内容的具体指导。与此同时，即时安全方面的技术工作，包括即时注入防御和即时强化技术，几乎完全集中在保护系统免受恶意用户输入的影响。这种框架将系统提示视为需要保护的可信工件，而不是作为独立的审查对象。这两项工作都没有询问指令本身是否服务于用户利益，是否包含操纵性或欺骗性指令，或者是否满足用户保护充分性的任何标准。最近的一项调查发现，近 90% 的用户要求系统提示具有更高的透明度，超过 70% 的用户认为信任是希望获得透明度的主要原因, but none of these efforts provide concrete guidance on what should or should not be included in a system prompt. Meanwhile, technical work on prompt security, including prompt injection defenses and prompt hardening techniques, has focused almost exclusively on protecting systems against malicious user inputs. This framing treats the system prompt as a trusted artifact to be defended, rather than as an independent object of scrutiny. Neither strand of work asks whether the instructions themselves serve user interests, whether they contain manipulative or deceptive directives, or whether they meet any standard of user-protective adequacy. A recent survey found that nearly 90% of users demand greater transparency about system prompts, and over 70% identified trust as the central reason for wanting it(neumann2026controls)。然而现实远未达到这一预期：系统提示仍然不透明且实际上不受监管，尽管它是人工智能系统的关键组成部分，确实有可能伤害用户和公众。. Yet the reality falls far short of this expectation: system prompts remain opaque and effectively unregulated, despite being a critical component of AI systems with real potential to harm users and the public.

From 2024 to 2026, prompts have grown substantially longer and more user-protective, with the average number of protective instructions more than doubling over the period, and 98.9% of products contain at least one protective instruction. Despite this overall positive trend, problematic instructions are common and comprehensive protections are rare: roughly 40% of commercial AI products contain at least one instruction that works against user interests and only 23.9% cover all eight auditing dimensions. Beyond clear-cut problematic instructions, our audit further surfaces a recurring class of gray area instructions that occupy the boundary between legitimate design choices and potential harms, including parasocial dependency cues, identity concealment tactics, and politically motivated content policy relaxations. Taken together, our results highlight the importance of third-party auditing for AI system prompts and could inspire new research and industry standards on building trustworthy and transparent AI systems.

Using this framework, we conduct the first audit of 3,249 instructions from 88 system prompts of real-world AI products. 10 局限性Limitations

我们的工作有以下局限性： (1) 我们的系统提示语料库来自公开可用的 GitHub 存储库，其中包含泄露或社区公开的提示。由于这些提示不是通过官方渠道获得的，因此我们无法完美验证它们是否代表当前生产中部署的确切版本。自披露以来，提示可能已被更新、修改或替换。因此，我们的发现反映了泄漏时系统提示实践的快照，而不是当前部署的保证代表。为了部分缓解这种担忧，我们通过比较多个独立存储库中同一产品的提示来执行跨存储库验证，并确认了高度重叠，这证明了数据集中系统提示的可靠性。 （2）对泄露提示的依赖引入了潜在的选择偏差：可用提示集可能过多地代表了提示更容易提取或用户技术性更强的产品，而可能低估了具有更强提示保护机制的产品。因此，我们的语料库可能无法构成所有已部署人工智能系统的代表性样本。然而，我们的数据集确实涵盖了领先人工智能公司创建的流行人工智能产品，研究结果仍然可以反映人工智能系统提示的重要趋势。Our work has the following limitations: (1) Our corpus of system prompts was sourced from publicly available GitHub repositories containing leaked or community-disclosed prompts. Because these prompts were not obtained through official channels, we cannot perfectly verify whether they represent the exact versions currently deployed in production. Prompts may have been updated, modified, or replaced since their disclosure. Consequently, our findings reflect a snapshot of system prompt practices at the time of leakage rather than a guaranteed representation of current deployments. To partially mitigate this concern, we performed cross-repository verification by comparing prompts for the same product across multiple independent repositories and confirmed high overlap, which demonstrate the reliability of the system prompts in our dataset. (2) The reliance on leaked prompts introduces a potential selection bias: the set of available prompts may over-represent products whose prompts are easier to extract or whose users are more technically engaged, and may under-represent products with stronger prompt protection mechanisms. Therefore, our corpus may not constitute a representative sample of all deployed AI systems. However, our dataset does cover popular AI products created by leading AI companies and the findings could still reflect important trends of AI system prompts.
