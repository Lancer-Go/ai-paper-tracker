ReflectRL：通过反思到直接推理从黄金负轨迹中学习ReflectRL: Learning from Golden Negative Trajectories via Reflective-to-Direct Reasoning

摘要Abstract

在策略训练已成为一种强大的后训练范例，用于提高大型语言模型的推理能力，并且通常通过更强大的专家模型的黄金轨迹来增强。然而，当专家在更困难的问题上失败时，现有的轨迹引导方法就失去了主要的监督来源，并且这些失败的轨迹通常作为负样本被丢弃。我们认为，这种被我们称为“黄金负轨”的失败，当不被视为可供模仿的示范，而是被视为可供反思的有缺陷的轨线时，仍然可以提供有价值的推理信号。 我们确定了反思优势：对于难题，反思有缺陷的轨迹比直接从头开始解决问题更容易、更有效。受此启发，我们提出了 ReflectRL，这是一个轻量级的即插即用框架，可以在策略训练期间从黄金负轨迹中学习。 ReflectRL 首先使用这些轨迹来引发反射推理，然后应用反射到直接策略转换将获得的推理行为转移回直接推理。跨 9 个基准、4 个 LLM 主干和 4 个策略训练方法的实验表明，ReflectRL 以最小的开销持续提高推理性能。On-policy training has emerged as a powerful post-training paradigm for improving the reasoning capabilities of large language models, and is often enhanced by golden trajectories from stronger expert models. However, when the expert fails on harder problems, existing trajectory-guided methods lose their main source of supervision, and these failed trajectories are typically discarded as negative samples. We argue that such failures, which we call Golden Negative Trajectories, can still provide valuable reasoning signals when treated not as demonstrations to imitate, but as flawed trajectories to reflect upon. We identify a Reflection Advantage: for hard problems, reflecting on a flawed trajectory can be easier and more effective than solving the problem directly from scratch. Motivated by this, we propose ReflectRL, a lightweight plug-and-play framework that learns from Golden Negative Trajectories during on-policy training. ReflectRL first uses these trajectories to elicit Reflective Reasoning, then applies Reflective-to-Direct Policy Transition to transfer the acquired reasoning behavior back to Direct Reasoning. Experiments across 9 benchmarks, 4 LLM backbones, and 4 on-policy training methods show that ReflectRL consistently improves reasoning performance with minimal overhead.

1 简介Introduction

在策略训练已成为提高大型语言模型推理能力的核心秘诀On-policy training has become a central recipe for improving the reasoning capabilities of large language models[DeepSeekAI2025DeepSeekR1IR, yu2026dapo, Bi2025CoTKineticsAT, huang2025loongsynthesizelongchainofthoughts, mid, ma-etal-2026-self]。在这种范式中，模型对训练问题的推理进行采样，并使用来自验证者、奖励模型或分配目标的反馈来更新其策略。最近的方法，例如具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）和策略蒸馏（OPD）表明，当采样的推出提供信息丰富的学习信号时，这种训练范例可以显着提高推理性能. In this paradigm, a model samples reasoning rollouts on training problems and updates its policy using feedback from verifiers, reward models, or distributional objectives. Recent methods such as Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) and On-Policy Distillation (OPD) show that this training paradigm can substantially improve reasoning performance when the sampled rollouts provide informative learning signals[lu2025onpolicydistillation, DeepSeekAI2025DeepSeekR1IR]。这种对推出质量的依赖在解决难题时变得更加关键，在这些难题中，直接抽样通常会产生稀疏的积极奖励和薄弱的政策更新. This dependence on rollout quality becomes more critical on hard problems, where direct sampling often produces sparse positive rewards and weak policy updates[bi2026echorl].

改进在策略训练的常见策略是结合来自更强大的专家模型（例如 DeepSeek-R1）的黄金推理轨迹A common strategy for improving on-policy training is to incorporate golden reasoning trajectories from stronger expert models, such as DeepSeek-R1[DeepSeekAI2025DeepSeekR1IR]。在 RLVR 中，正确的专家黄金轨迹可以引导 rollout 一代走向更有希望的推理路径. 具有可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)

最近，大型语言模型和多模态 LLM（MLLM）在各种复杂的推理任务中表现出了卓越的能力Recently, large language models and multi-modal LLMs (MLLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities across various complex reasoning tasks[bi-etal-2025-llava, Bi2025PRISMSI, zhao2026nl2codestructuredsurveymultimodal, zhang2023spot, Zhang2025TheLO, yang2026drdocbenchcomprehensivebenchmark, yang2026alignsaeconceptalignedsparseautoencoders, jiang2025koreenhancingknowledgeinjection, Wang_Bi_Pirk_Ma_2026, wan2025magicwordssharpnessawareprompt, wan2025hyperion, rong2026backdoor, peng2025visualinputcompressedvisual, jiang2025minedprobingupdatingmultimodal]。为了进一步增强这些能力，具有可验证奖励的强化学习已成为推理任务后培训的主要范例. To further enhance these capabilities, Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards has emerged as a predominant paradigm for post-training on reasoning tasks[lightman2024let, DeepSeekAI2025DeepSeekR1IR]。与依赖于根据人类偏好训练的奖励模型的传统 RLHF 不同，RLVR 利用基于规则的验证器或确定性结果信号（例如，匹配数学答案或通过单元测试）来提供明确的奖励信号. Unlike traditional RLHF which relies on reward models trained on human preferences, RLVR leverages rule-based verifiers or deterministic outcome signals (e.g., matching a mathematical answer or passing unit tests) to provide unambiguous reward signals[zelikman2022star, wang2024mathcoder]。该领域的最新进展，例如组相对策略优化 (GRPO)，通过计算相对于一组抽样部署的优势，消除了对外部批评模型的需求. Recent advancements in this space, such as Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), eliminate the need for an external critic model by computing advantages relative to a sampled group of rollouts[DeepSeekAI2025DeepSeekR1IR]。虽然 RLVR 在探索和强化正确推理路径方面非常有效，但它在很大程度上依赖于模型偶尔采样正确答案的内在能力。在模型无法生成任何正确轨迹的高度复杂问题上，奖励信号变得稀疏，导致探索效率低下和策略更新退化. While RLVR is highly effective at exploring and reinforcing correct reasoning paths, it heavily relies on the model’s intrinsic capability to occasionally sample the correct answer. On highly complex problems where the model fails to generate any correct trajectory, the reward signal becomes sparse, leading to inefficient exploration and degraded policy updates[bi2026echorl]。 ReflectRL 建立在这个范式的基础上，将反射推理注入 RLVR 过程中，确保即使直接探索失败，模型仍然可以通过反射黄金负轨迹来接收密集且有建设性的信号。. ReflectRL builds upon this paradigm by injecting Reflective Reasoning into the RLVR process, ensuring that even when direct exploration fails, the model can still receive dense and constructive signals by reflecting on Golden Negative Trajectories.

按策略蒸馏旨在使用学生自己生成的轨迹将知识从更强大的专家模型（教师）转移到目标模型（学生）On-Policy Distillation aims to transfer knowledge from a stronger expert model (teacher) to a target model (student) using the student’s own generated trajectories[gu2024minillm, zhao2026selfdistilledreasoneronpolicyselfdistillation, lu2025onpolicydistillation]。通过计算在政策状态下教师和学生分布之间的 Kullback-Leibler (KL) 差异，OPD 减轻了离线监督微调 (SFT) 中通常遇到的分布偏移和暴露偏差问题. In standard OPD, the teacher model evaluates the student’s reasoning path and provides a token-level distribution to guide the student. However, when the problem is challenging, the teacher might also lack sufficient context to provide optimal step-by-step guidance. ReflectRL extends the OPD framework by providing the Golden Negative Trajectory as privileged contextual information to the teacher model. This allows the teacher to perform Reflective Reasoning and generate higher-quality target distributions, while the student model distills this error-correction capability directly into its Direct Reasoning pathway without relying on the external trajectory during inference.
