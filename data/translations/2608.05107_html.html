CoPlan：通过基于角色的可争议论证图进行护理计划的值得信赖的协同智能界面CoPlan: A Trustworthy Co-Intelligence Interface for Care Planning through Role-Based Contestable Argument Graphs

Hung Truong Thanh Nguyen    Hélène Fournier    Piper Jackson    Makoto Itoh    Shannon Freeman    Rene Richard    Hung Cao
摘要Abstract

人工智能支持的护理规划可以帮助临床医生、患者、护理人员和护理团队协调临床、功能、社会心理和环境需求方面的复杂决策。然而，许多人工智能系统将建议作为固定输出，限制了利益相关者在与临床判断、患者价值观或现实世界可行性相冲突时检查、质疑和修改计划的能力。 我们呈现AI-supported care planning can help clinicians, patients, caregivers, and care teams coordinate complex decisions across clinical, functional, psychosocial, and environmental needs. However, many AI systems present recommendations as fixed outputs, limiting stakeholders’ ability to inspect, challenge, and revise plans when they conflict with clinical judgment, patient values, or real-world feasibility. We presentCoPlan – 用于人类人工智能护理规划的联合智能且可竞争的界面CoPlan – a Co-Intelligent and Contestable Interface for Human-AI Care Planning。 CoPlan 使用多代理工作流程，其中专门的 AI 代理生成候选干预措施以及支持或质疑的论点，而人类护理规划人员可以在最终计划生成之前接受、拒绝、修改或添加论点。通过这种设计，CoPlan 将协同智能与可竞争性相结合，其中人类和人工智能代理贡献互补的专业知识，其中建议仍然可以接受检查、修订和论证。 我们在居家养老护理规划场景中演示了 CoPlan。该系统支持适应性护理团队招募、基于角色的论证审查、最终护理计划生成以及通过调度代理的实际后续行动。这项工作提供了一个有争议的护理规划界面和一个可信赖的人类人工智能护理规划的设计框架，保留了人类机构和临床责任。. CoPlan uses a multi-agent workflow in which specialized AI agents generate candidate interventions and supporting or challenging arguments, while human care planners can accept, reject, modify, or add arguments before final plan generation. Through this design, CoPlan combines co-intelligence, in which humans and AI agents contribute complementary expertise, with contestability, where recommendations remain open to inspection, revision, and justification. We demonstrate CoPlan in an aging-in-place care planning scenario. The system supports adaptive care team recruitment, role-based argument review, final care plan generation, and practical follow-up through scheduling agents. This work contributes a contestable care planning interface and a design framing for trustworthy human-AI care planning that preserves human agency and clinical accountability.

1 简介Introduction

护理计划是一个持续协作的过程，临床医生、患者、护理人员和护理团队在此过程中定义目标、协调干预措施并根据情况变化修改决策Care planning is a collaborative and ongoing process in which clinicians, patients, caregivers, and care teams define goals, coordinate interventions, and revise decisions as conditions change[3]。护理计划不是固定的文件，而是一个共享对象，它将临床证据与患者偏好、社会背景、实际约束和专业责任联系起来。这对于慢性和复杂的疾病尤其重要，因为护理通常跨越多个提供者和环境，并且规划质量取决于利益相关者是否能够共同理解、协商和更新计划. Rather than a fixed document, a care plan is a shared object that connects clinical evidence with patient preferences, social context, practical constraints, and professional responsibilities. This is especially important for chronic and complex conditions, where care often spans multiple providers and settings, and where planning quality depends on whether stakeholders can understand, negotiate, and update the plan together[26].

人工智能 (AI) 系统通过总结患者信息、识别风险、比较干预措施和生成建议，为支持护理计划提供了新的机会。多代理系统 (MAS) 提供了一种很有前途的组织算法护理计划的方法，因为它们可以在不同形式的专业知识中分配推理，包括诊断、药物审查、康复、社会支持和患者偏好诱导Artificial intelligence (AI) systems offer new opportunities to support care planning by summarizing patient information, identifying risks, comparing interventions, and generating recommendations. Multi-agent systems (MAS) provide a promising way to organize algorithmic care planning because they can distribute reasoning across different forms of expertise, including diagnosis, medication review, rehabilitation, social support, and patient preference elicitation[27, 15, 3, 14]。然而，护理计划不能简化为产生优化建议，因为当临床上合理的计划忽略了本地工作流程、患者价值、护理人员能力或数据中缺少的上下文信息时，它可能仍然不合适。. Yet care planning cannot be reduced to the production of an optimized recommendation, since a plan that is clinically reasonable may still be unsuitable when it overlooks local workflows, patient values, caregiver capacity, or contextual information absent from the data.

这对设计值得信赖的人工智能支持的护理计划界面提出了一个关键挑战。可信度不能仅仅依赖于准确性、模型性能或解释，因为当人工智能建议不完整、不安全、有偏见或与临床判断和患者目标不一致时，用户还需要有意义的方式来响应This raises a key challenge for designing trustworthy AI-supported care planning interfaces. Trustworthiness cannot rely only on accuracy, model performance, or explanation, because users also need meaningful ways to respond when AI recommendations are incomplete, unsafe, biased, or misaligned with clinical judgment and patient goals[10, 9]。虽然可解释的人工智能 (XAI) 可以帮助用户理解为什么会产生建议，但仅靠解释并不能确保用户能够纠正系统、挑战其假设或重塑提议的计划。因此，界面设计成为值得信赖的护理计划的关键部分，因为它决定了用户如何遇到人工智能建议，如何检查其背后的推理，以及当建议不适合护理环境时他们如何采取行动. While explainable AI (XAI) can help users understand why a recommendation was produced, explanation alone does not ensure that users can correct the system, challenge its assumptions, or reshape the proposed plan. Therefore, interface design becomes a critical part of trustworthy care planning, because it shapes how users encounter AI recommendations, how they examine the reasoning behind them, and how they act when the recommendation does not fit the care context[19, 22, 1].

2.1 以人为本且值得信赖的人工智能护理规划Human-Centered and Trustworthy AI for Care Planning

护理计划是一项协作医疗活动，需要临床医生、患者、护理人员和护理团队将临床证据与患者目标、社会背景、可用资源和实际限制相结合。医疗保健领域人工智能的先前研究表明，计算系统在支持临床推理、风险评估、诊断和治疗计划方面具有潜力Care planning is a collaborative healthcare activity that requires clinicians, patients, caregivers, and care teams to align clinical evidence with patient goals, social context, available resources, and practical constraints. Prior work on AI in healthcare has shown the potential of computational systems to support clinical reasoning, risk assessment, diagnosis, and treatment planning[15, 6]，同时也强调人工智能应该增强而不是取代人类的判断, while also emphasizing that AI should augment rather than replace human judgment[28, 21]。这种观点在护理计划中尤其重要，其中的决策不仅是技术或临床的，而且是关系性的、情境性的和价值敏感的。. This perspective is especially important in care planning, where decisions are not only technical or clinical, but also relational, contextual, and value-sensitive.

以人为中心的人工智能研究认为，人工智能系统应该围绕人类的目标、能力、责任和使用环境进行设计Human-centered AI research argues that AI systems should be designed around human goals, capabilities, responsibilities, and contexts of use[25, 24, 19]。在医疗保健领域，这意味着人工智能支持的系统必须适应临床工作流程、传达不确定性、支持用户控制并保持问责制。人机交互指南同样强调系统应使其能力可见，支持纠正，并使用户能够理解和控制人工智能行为. In healthcare, this means that AI-supported systems must fit clinical workflows, communicate uncertainty, support user control, and preserve accountability. Guidelines for human-AI interaction similarly emphasize that systems should make their capabilities visible, support correction, and enable users to understand and control AI behavior[2, 20]。这些原则与护理计划直接相关，因为不同的利益相关者贡献不同形式的知识：临床医生带来临床专业知识，患者带来生活经验和偏好，护理人员带来有关日常可行性的实用知识。. These principles are directly relevant to care planning because different stakeholders contribute different forms of knowledge: clinicians bring clinical expertise, patients bring lived experience and preferences, and caregivers bring practical knowledge about daily feasibility.

最近关于多智能体系统（MAS）的工作扩展了这个方向，展示了多个人工智能智能体如何协调不同形式的专业知识，例如诊断、药物审查、康复和患者偏好诱导Recent work on multi-agent systems (MAS) extends this direction by showing how multiple AI agents can coordinate different forms of expertise, such as diagnosis, medication review, rehabilitation, and patient preference elicitation[17, 15, 6, 27]。这对于护理计划很有用，因为护理工作已经跨角色和专业分配。然而，现有的人工智能和多智能体医疗保健系统通常关注推荐质量、智能体协调或推理性能，而很少关注人类用户检查、协商和修改人工智能生成的计划的界面。. This is useful for care planning because care work is already distributed across roles and specialties. However, existing AI and multi-agent healthcare systems often focus on recommendation quality, agent coordination, or reasoning performance, while giving less attention to the interface through which human users inspect, negotiate, and revise AI-generated plans.

CoPlan 通过将护理计划视为CoPlan addresses this gap by framing care planning as a人类与人工智能的协同智能流程co-intelligent human-AI process，人类利益相关者和人工智能代理共同参与规划，同时界面保留了人类的代理和责任。, where human stakeholders and AI agents jointly contribute to planning while the interface preserves human agency and accountability.

2.2 从可解释性到有争议的人机交互界面From Explainability to Contestable Human-AI Interfaces

可解释的人工智能（XAI）已成为提高人工智能支持的决策信任度的主要方法，特别是在医疗保健等高风险领域。解释可以帮助用户了解哪些因素影响了推荐、系统产生特定输出的原因以及输出何时可能需要进一步审核Explainable AI (XAI) has become a major approach for improving trust in AI-supported decision-making, especially in high-stakes domains such as healthcare. Explanations can help users understand what factors shaped a recommendation, why a system produced a certain output, and when the output may require further review[13, 18]。然而，仅仅解释是有限的，因为理解人工智能建议并不一定能为用户提供有意义的方式来挑战或改变它。在护理计划中，这种限制至关重要，如果建议与患者偏好、护理人员能力、成本、访问或临床判断相冲突，则建议可能是可以解释的，但仍然不合适. However, explanation alone is limited because understanding an AI recommendation does not necessarily give users a meaningful way to challenge or change it. In care planning, this limitation is critical, where a recommendation may be explainable but still inappropriate if it conflicts with patient preferences, caregiver capacity, cost, access, or clinical judgment[23].

值得信赖的人工智能框架强调透明度、公平性、稳健性、问责制和人工监督Trustworthy AI frameworks emphasize transparency, fairness, robustness, accountability, and human oversight[10, 9]。然而，除非将这些原则转化为具体的交互机制，否则它们可能仍然是抽象的。可争议的人工智能（CAI）对此问题做出了回应，认为受自动化决策影响的人应该能够质疑系统输出、提供反证、寻求修正并获得有意义的回应. Yet these principles can remain abstract unless they are translated into concrete interaction mechanisms. Contestable AI (CAI) responds to this problem by arguing that people affected by automated decisions should be able to question system outputs, provide counterevidence, seek revision, and receive a meaningful response[16, 11, 7]。最近的工作进一步表明，应将可争议性设计到人工智能系统中，而不是仅作为事后上诉过程添加，因为有意义的争议要求用户了解建议的基础，确定分歧的理由，并了解他们的挑战如何改变结果. Recent work further suggests that contestability should be designed into AI systems rather than added only as a post-hoc appeal process, because meaningful contestation requires users to see the basis of a recommendation, identify grounds for disagreement, and understand how their challenge changes the outcome[22, 19]。 图。. Fig.1说明了从单向解释（从人工智能到人类）到交互式争论循环（人工智能和人类之间）的进展，其中人类可以请求理由、注入更正并引导人工智能走向更可接受的结果。illustrates this progress of moving from one-way explanations (from AI to human) to an interactive loop of contestations (between AI and human), wherein the human can request justification, inject corrections, and guide the AI towards more acceptable outcomes.

CoPlan 建立在从可解释性到可竞争性的转变之上。 CoPlan 没有将人工智能生成的护理计​​划作为最终建议，而是将其视为可以检查、质疑、修改、论证或升级的建议。这种界面级框架很重要，因为护理计划经常涉及分歧、不确定性和利益相关者谈判。通过将协同智能与可竞争性相结合，CoPlan 旨在支持人工智能辅助规划，同时确保临床医生、患者和护理人员对护理计划保留有意义的权力。这样，设计目标就从自动生成计划转变为负责任的计划协商。CoPlan builds on this shift from explainability to contestability. Instead of presenting AI-generated care plans as final recommendations, CoPlan treats them as proposals that can be inspected, challenged, revised, justified, or escalated. This interface-level framing is important because care planning often involves disagreement, uncertainty, and stakeholder negotiation. By combining co-intelligence with contestability, CoPlan aims to support AI-assisted planning while ensuring that clinicians, patients, and caregivers retain meaningful authority over the care plan. In this way, the design goal shifts from automatic plan generation to accountable plan negotiation.

3.1 问题环境表述Problem Environment Formulation

我们考虑一个决策环境，其目标是为社区中的老年人制定安全且个性化的护理计划。这项任务需要综合临床条件、功能能力、环境风险和个人偏好。在实践中，高质量的护理规划需要多个专业角色的贡献，因为没有任何一个学科可以完全捕捉到居家养老的复杂性。我们的系统旨在通过创建一个结构化的多主体环境来支持这一过程，在该环境中揭示、比较和验证不同的专业观点。 正式地，我们将环境建模为一个元组：We consider a decision environment in which the goal is to produce a safe and personalized care plan for an older adult living in the community. This task requires synthesizing clinical conditions, functional abilities, environmental risks, and personal preferences. In practice, high-quality care planning requires contributions from several professional roles, as no single discipline can fully capture the complexity of aging-in-place. After Stage 4, the human care planner remains responsible for reviewing the proposed plan, identifying infeasible, contradictory, unsafe, or resource-incompatible recommendations, and requesting revision before implementation. If a recommendation cannot be realized because required services, personnel, funding, caregiver capacity, or local resources are unavailable, the care planner may reopen the relevant arguments, add feasibility constraints, revise the recommendation, or reject the plan component.

这一结果还凸显了人工监督在工作流程中的作用。在第三阶段，人类护理规划师审查代理生成的论点，并可以根据专业判断接受、修改或添加要点。这个争论过程确保最终的护理计划不仅仅是自动推理的输出，而是人类利益相关者和人工智能代理之间共同智能、可竞争的协作的结果。This result also highlights the role of human oversight in the workflow. In Stage 3, the human care planner reviews the agent-generated arguments and can accept, revise, or add points based on professional judgment. This contestation process ensures that the final care plan is not simply the output of automated reasoning but the result of a co-intelligent, contestable collaboration between human stakeholders and AI agents.

最后阶段展示了如何将经过验证的论据综合到结构化护理计划中。在图示的案例中，系统优先考虑家庭安全评估和修改、社会参与和支持小组以及定期健康监测和护理协调作为最高有效性的建议。最终输出还包括实施步骤、论证有效性摘要和基于证据的论证，展示 CoPlan 如何支持透明、可操作和以人为本的护理计划。The final stage demonstrates how the validated arguments are synthesized into a structured care plan. In the illustrated case, the system prioritizes home safety assessment and modification, social engagement and support groups, and regular health monitoring and care coordination as the highest-validity recommendations. The final output also includes implementation steps, argument validity summaries, and evidence-based justification, showing how CoPlan supports transparent, actionable, and human-centered care planning.

5 结论Conclusion

本文提出This paper presentedCoPlan——用于人类人工智能护理规划的联合智能且可竞争的界面CoPlan—a Co-Intelligent and Contestable Interface for Human-AI Care Planning。 CoPlan 将人工智能建议视为可审查的建议，而不是固定的决策。它分析患者信息，招募相关的护理角色，产生干预措施和支持或反对的论点，并允许护理规划​​者在制定最终计划之前接受、拒绝、修改或添加论点。 就地养老场景说明了 CoPlan 如何将患者背景、基于角色的专业知识、结构化争论和基于证据的建议结合起来。其可见、可编辑和可审计的推理支持共同智能，同时保留人类对护理决策的权威。 当前的限制是有效性和置信度分数支持审查而不是自主决策。未来的工作应该评估替代聚合方法，并检查它们对透明度、公平性、合法性和过度依赖的影响。研究还应该评估临床医生、患者和护理人员是否可以通过争论有效表达基于价值的担忧。 总体而言，值得信赖的人工智能支持的护理计划不仅需要准确的建议或解释，还需要负责任的谈判。 CoPlan 提供了一个保留人类机构、临床责任和有意义的监督的设计方向。. CoPlan treats AI recommendations as reviewable proposals rather than fixed decisions. It analyzes patient information, recruits relevant care roles, generates interventions and supporting or opposing arguments, and allows care planners to accept, reject, revise, or add arguments before producing the final plan. The aging-in-place scenario illustrates how CoPlan combines patient context, role-based expertise, structured contestation, and evidence-based recommendations. Its visible, editable, and auditable reasoning supports co-intelligence while preserving human authority over care decisions. A current limitation is that validity and confidence scores support review rather than autonomous decision-making. Future work should evaluate alternative aggregation methods and examine their effects on transparency, fairness, legitimacy, and overreliance. Studies should also assess whether clinicians, patients, and caregivers can effectively express value-based concerns through contestation. Overall, trustworthy AI-supported care planning requires not only accurate recommendations or explanations, but also accountable negotiation. CoPlan provides a design direction that preserves human agency, clinical responsibility, and meaningful oversight.
