1 简介Introduction

Fundamentally, they allow researchers to structure unstructured language. Embeddings have long been ubiquitous(Wu et al., 2018)他们的影响力只增不减and their reach has only grown(Brunila, 2025)。来自聚类法律文件. From clustering legal documents(Vianna et al., 2024)分析公共卫生讨论to analysing public health discussions(Xu et al., 2022), 音乐行为, music behaviour(Hansen et al., 2020)，或遗传密码, or genetic code(Ji et al., 2021)嵌入有助于使不规则的数据变得清晰易读embeddings help make unruly data legible(Scott, 2020)。通过将语言翻译成向量，嵌入还提供了新的方法来为复杂政策问题的决策提供信息。通过使研究人员能够对大量定性数据进行聚类和可视化，他们可以展现不同且相互矛盾的来源的模式，使许多社会问题所占据的混乱、有争议的领域变得清晰可见. By translating language into vectors, embeddings also offer new ways to inform decision-making on complex policy issues. By enabling researchers to cluster and visualise large volumes of qualitative data, they can surface patterns across diverse and contradictory sources, rendering legible the kind of messy, contested terrain that many societal problems occupy(Madsen et al., 2025; Winther Nielsen, 2025).

5 结论Conclusion

我们引入了利益相关者接地练习，这是一种使专家关联可测量的方法，在人类分析师的构造空间和神经文本嵌入的特征空间之间架起了一座桥梁。我们对丹麦政策问题的初步研究产生了三个关键结果，尽管语言、协议和专家存在差异，但每个结果都在我们的 Gov-AI 研究中得到了重复。We introduced the Stakeholder Grounding Exercise, a method that makes expert associations measurable, providing a bridge between the construct space of human analysts and the feature space of neural text embeddings. Our primary study on Danish policy issues yields three key results, each of which is replicated in our Gov-AI study despite differences in language, protocol, and experts.
