学习、潜力和保留：一种评估自适应人工智能医疗设备的方法Learning, Potential, and Retention: An Approach for Evaluating Adaptive AI-Enabled Medical Devices

Alexis Burgon Berkman Sahiner Nicholas A Petrick Gene Pennello Ravi K Samala Ravi.Samala@fda.hhs.gov.
摘要Abstract

这项工作解决了评估方面的挑战This work addresses challenges in evaluating适应性adaptive用于医疗设备的人工智能（AI）模型，其中模型和评估数据集的迭代更新使性能评估变得复杂。我们引入了一种具有三种互补测量的新颖方法：artificial intelligence (AI) models for medical devices, where iterative updates to both models and evaluation datasets complicate performance assessment. We introduce a novel approach with three complementary measurements:学习learning（当前数据的模型改进），(model improvement on current data),潜力potential（数据集驱动的性能变化），以及(dataset-driven performance shifts), and保留retention（跨修改步骤的知识保存），以理清由模型适应与动态环境引起的性能变化。使用模拟人口变化的案例研究证明了该方法的实用性：逐步过渡可以实现稳定的学习和保留，而快速变化则揭示了可塑性和稳定性之间的权衡。这些测量为监管科学提供了实用的见解，能够对自适应人工智能系统在连续修改过程中的安全性和有效性进行严格评估。(knowledge preservation across modification steps), to disentangle performance changes caused by model adaptations versus dynamic environments. Case studies using simulated population shifts demonstrate the approach’s utility: gradual transitions enable stable learning and retention, while rapid shifts reveal trade-offs between plasticity and stability. These measurements provide practical insights for regulatory science, enabling rigorous assessment of the safety and effectiveness of adaptive AI systems over sequential modifications.

1 简介Introduction

在临床环境中部署的支持人工智能 (AI) 的设备传统上使用“锁定”模型，定义为“每次应用相同输入时都提供相同结果且不会随着使用而改变的算法”Artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled devices deployed in clinical settings traditionally use a “locked” model, defined as “an algorithm that provides the same result each time the same input is applied to it and does not change with use”[FDApccpRF], [FDAglossary]。使用锁定模型通常是为了防止由于系统参数的变化而出现未评估的行为。然而，使用锁定模型可能无法保证持续的可预测性能。当应用于动态环境（例如临床环境中）时，锁定模型可能会随着时间的推移而出现性能下降. The use of locked models is typically intended to prevent the emergence of unevaluated behaviors due to changes in system parameters. However, utilization of a locked model may not guarantee ongoing predictable performance. When applied within dynamic environments, such as those in clinical settings, locked models may experience performance decay over time[davis2017calibration, chen2017decaying, nestor2019feature, sahiner2023data]。相比之下，持续学习. In contrast, continual learning[de2021continual]人工智能模型或许能够适应不断变化的环境，但代价是可能会出现意想不到的行为AI models may be able to adapt to changing environments, though at the cost of potentially developing unexpected behaviors[babic2019algorithms].

自适应人工智能模型，一种随着时间的推移而版本化的持续学习人工智能模型Adaptive AI models, a type of continual learning AI model that is versioned over time[FDAglossary, IMDRFmlmdDefs]，在完全静态的“锁定”模型和持续学习的模型之间提出了一个潜在的中间立场。与持续学习的人工智能模型不同，自适应人工智能模型将提供的数据视为持续的训练流，并根据遇到的每个样本或任务进行更新，自适应人工智能是在不同的阶段（修改步骤）进行训练的，如图所示, present a potential middle ground between entirely static “locked” models and those that continuously learn. In contrast to continually learning AI models, which view provided data as a constant stream for training and update with each sample or task encountered, adaptive AI is trained in distinct stages (modification steps), as shown in Figure1。这些修改步骤通常是由已知的输入变化、观察到的性能下降、大量新训练数据的积累或向新技术进步的过渡触发的。此外，虽然修改步骤表明模型发生了变化，但总体而言. These modification steps are typically triggered by a known change in the input, an observed decrease in performance, the accumulation of a large amount of new training data, or the transition to new technological advances. Furthermore, while modification steps indicate model changes, the overall任务task（例如，胸部 X 光检查的疾病分类）在所有修改步骤中保持相同，这与一些学习不同任务序列的持续学习模型形成鲜明对比(e.g., disease classification from chest X-rays) remains the same across all modification steps, in contrast to some continual learning models that learn sequences of different tasks[lopez2017gradient]。这种训练方法可以使模型随着时间的推移而适应，同时也为评估提供明确的变化点。. This training approach enables model adaptation over time while also providing clear change points for evaluation.

然而，自适应模型的评估比锁定模型的评估更复杂，这主要是由于模型和评估数据的潜在可变性，它们提供了评估变化的两个轴。如果同步创建模型和数据集的新修改步骤，则面临的挑战是确定从一个修改步骤到下一个修改步骤所观察到的变化的来源。However, the evaluation of adaptive models is more complicated than that of their locked counterparts, largely due to the potentially variable nature of both the model and the evaluation data, which provide two axes for assessing change. If new modification steps of the model and dataset are created synchronously, the challenge is to determine the source of the change observed from one modification step to the next.观察到的性能改进实际上是由于模型的更改所致，还是新的测试数据集只是不同？Is an observed improvement in performance actually due to a change in the model, or is the new test dataset simply different?

过渡到更简单的测试数据集可能会导致过于乐观的性能评估，这可能会促进对模型的不必要的信任。此外，如果测试数据集的这种变化伴随着模型训练数据集的类似变化，则可能会过度拟合新的训练数据。尽管在评估过程中测量到性能有所提高，但在临床环境中应用时，这可能会导致模型性能下降。A transition to an easier test dataset can result in overly optimistic performance evaluation, which may promote unwarranted trust in the model. Furthermore, if this change in the test dataset is accompanied by a similar change in the model’s training dataset, there is the potential for overfitting to the new training data. This may result in a decrease in model performance when applied in a clinical setting, despite the measured increase in performance during evaluation.

由于模型的固有权衡，自适应人工智能的评估变得更加复杂。Evaluation of adaptive AI is further complicated by the inherent trade-off between a model’s可塑性plasticity（学习新信息的能力）和(ability to learn new information) and稳定性stability（保留先验知识的能力）(capacity to retain prior knowledge)[mermillod2013stability]。可塑性和稳定性的相对重要性取决于人工智能模型开发和部署的背景。对于使用大型、多样化数据集不经常修改的模型，可塑性可能优先于稳定性，因为每个新数据集都足以代表该时间点的预期人群。相反，在频繁更新的模型中优先考虑可塑性而不是稳定性可能是有害的，因为过度适应不断变化的群体可能会导致不可预测的模型行为。. The relative importance of plasticity and stability depends on the context in which an AI model is developed and deployed. For models that are modified infrequently using large, diverse datasets, plasticity may be prioritized over stability, as each new dataset is sufficiently representative of the intended population at that point in time. In contrast, prioritizing plasticity over stability in frequently updated models could be detrimental, since over-adaptation to changing populations may result in unpredictable model behavior.

为了深入了解自适应人工智能评估的复杂性，我们提出并评估了一种新颖的方法，该方法由三种互补的测量组成，旨在跨顺序修改步骤对人工智能模型进行精细且富有洞察力的分析。该方法通过测量以下内容提供诊断见解：(1) 模型的To provide insight into the complexities of adaptive AI evaluation, we propose and assess a novel approach consisting of three complementary measurements designed to provide a granular and insightful analysis of AI models across sequential modification steps. This approach provides diagnostic insight by measuring: (1) the model’s学习learning– 无论评估数据如何变化，(2)– irrespective of changes to the evaluation data, (2) the潜力potential通过适应数据集变化可以获得改进，以及（3）模型的improvement available through adaptation to dataset changes, and (3) the model’s保留retention在之前的修改步骤中学到的知识。总之，这些测量可以提供对模型可塑性和稳定性的深入了解，超越传统的性能评估。of knowledge learned in previous modification steps. Together, these measurements may provide insights into model plasticity and stability that extend beyond traditional performance assessment.

4 讨论与结论Discussion & Conclusion

传统的性能评估旨在通过在锁定人工智能模型的假设下考虑设备输入和输出的预期变化来衡量整体性能。然而，当应用于内部运作不断演变的自适应人工智能系统时，它们可能还不够。自适应人工智能范式提供了模型改进的机会，这可以谨慎地应对诸如提高与输入采集设备的兼容性、观察到的性能下降、目标人群的已知变化或适应新技术进步等情况。在这种范式中，对人工智能模型和评估数据集的同时顺序修改会引入无法通过单个测量捕获的多个变化轴。正如我们的案例研究所示Traditional performance assessments are designed to measure overall performance by accounting for the expected variability in device inputs and outputs under the assumption of a locked AI model. However, they may be insufficient when applied to adaptive AI systems whose inner workings continually evolve. The adaptive AI paradigm presents opportunities for model improvement, which may be prudent in response to circumstances such as improving compatibility with input acquisition devices, an observed decrease in performance, a known change in the intended population, or adaptation to new technological advances. In this paradigm, simultaneous sequential modifications to the AI model and evaluation datasets introduce multiple axes of change that cannot be captured by a single measurement. As demonstrated in our case study in Section2.2以及节中的相应结果and corresponding results in Section3，我们提出的测量可能能够区分由于模型适应和数据变化的动态环境导致的性能变化。, our proposed measurements may be able to distinguish performance changes due to model adaptations versus dynamic environments with changing data.

自适应人工智能为设备提供了在初始部署后继续学习的机会，有可能提高设备性能并适应不断变化的环境。然而，对变化模型的评估所带来的挑战超出了评估锁定模型时通常遇到的挑战。在这项工作中，我们尝试通过引入三种测量来解决这样的限制 -Adaptive AI presents an opportunity for devices to continue to learn after initial deployment, potentially improving device performance and allowing for adaptation to changing environments. However, the evaluation of changing models presents challenges beyond those typically encountered in the evaluation of locked models. In this work, we try to address one such limitation by introducing three measurements –学习learning, 潜力potential和and保留retention– 这可能有助于解决由于模型适应与数据集变化而导致的性能变化。未来的工作可能会探索这些指标在其他医疗设备人工智能应用中的应用及其集成到自动化人工智能监控系统中的潜力。– which potentially assist with disentangling performance changes attributable to model adaptations versus dataset shifts.
