QFCQT：用于波动时间序列预测的混沌门控量化框架QFCQT: A Chaotically Gated Quantformer Framework for Volatile Time-Series Forecasting

Junkai Lin†, Siqi Hou†, Raymond Lee∗
摘要Abstract

由于长程依赖性、局部波动爆发、结构变化和非线性振荡行为，预测非平稳时间序列仍然很困难。尽管基于 Transformer 的预测器对于长期时间依赖性建模非常有效，但它们的前馈块通常依赖于对突然的状态变化不够敏感的平滑静态激活。受定量 Transformer 设计和基于振荡器的非线性激活的启发，我们提出了 QFCQT（量子分形启发的混沌门控量化器的缩写），用于在复杂的波动动力学下进行稳健的预测。这里，“量子分形启发”表示基于软振子叠加和多尺度非线性响应的计算类比，而不是正式的量子力学或分形理论推导。Forecasting non-stationary time series remains difficult due to long-range dependencies, local volatility bursts, structural shifts, and nonlinear oscillatory behaviors. Although Transformer-based forecasters are effective for modeling long-term temporal dependencies, their feed-forward blocks typically rely on smooth static activations that are insufficiently sensitive to abrupt regime changes. Motivated by quantitative Transformer designs and oscillator-based nonlinear activations, we propose QFCQT, short for Quantum-Fractal-inspired Chaotically Gated Quantformer, for robust forecasting under complex volatile dynamics. Here, “quantum-fractal-inspired” denotes a computational analogy based on soft oscillator superposition and multi-scale nonlinear responses, rather than a formal quantum-mechanical or fractal-theoretic derivation.

QFCQT 由三个主要组件组成：1）Quantformer 式数值编码器，通过线性嵌入直接处理多元输入； 2）可学习的 Lee 振荡器激活模块，将标量预激活映射到动态振荡响应，并通过最大时间池化对它们进行汇总； 3）平滑混沌门控融合机制，自适应地平衡传统平滑激活和混沌敏感响应。此外，QFCQT 不使用单个固定振荡器，而是采用八个参数化 Lee 振荡器系列的软叠加来自适应捕获跨状态的不同非线性响应模式。QFCQT consists of three main components: 1) a Quantformer-style numerical encoder that directly processes multivariate inputs via linear embedding; 2) a learnable Lee-oscillator activation module that maps scalar pre-activations to dynamic oscillatory responses and summarizes them through Max-over-Time pooling; and 3) a smooth-chaotic gated fusion mechanism that adaptively balances conventional smooth activations and chaos-sensitive responses. Furthermore, instead of using a single fixed oscillator, QFCQT employs a soft superposition of eight parameterized Lee oscillator families to adaptively capture different nonlinear response patterns across regimes.

ETTh 实验Experiments on ETTh1, ETTh2和 A 股股指基准显示，QFCQT 的表现始终优于强基准，包括 Informer、LogTrans、LSTMa、HAT 和 COTN。在高度波动的环境中，所提出的方法在 ETTh 上比 HAT 提高了高达 43.9% 的 MSE，比 COTN 提高了 41.3%, and A-share Stock Index benchmarks show that QFCQT consistently outperforms strong baselines, including Informer, LogTrans, LSTMa, HAT, and COTN. On highly volatile settings, the proposed method achieves up to 43.9% MSE improvement over HAT and 41.3% over COTN on ETTh2预测范围为 24。这些结果表明，集成到 Quantformer 式数值主干中的混沌感知激活动力学为非平稳和高度不稳定的环境中的预测提供了有效且稳健的解决方案。with prediction horizon 24. These results demonstrate that chaos-aware activation dynamics integrated into a Quantformer-style numerical backbone provide an effective and robust solution for forecasting in non-stationary and highly volatile environments.

I 简介Introduction

时间序列预测是定量金融、电力系统、交通分析和工业监测中的一个重要问题。在实践中，时间序列经常表现出长程依赖性、局部波动、波动聚集、结构突变和非平稳行为，这使得准确预测变得困难。Time-series forecasting is an important problem in quantitative finance, power systems, traffic analysis, and industrial monitoring. In practice, time series often exhibit long-range dependency, local fluctuation, volatility clustering, abrupt structural changes, and non-stationary behavior, which make accurate prediction difficult.

LSTM 和 GRU 等循环模型可以对顺序依赖进行建模Recurrent models such as LSTM and GRU can model sequential dependence[5]，但它们的顺序计算限制了并行性，并且常常使长期优化变得困难。基于 Transformer 的模型通过自注意力解决了这个问题，并已广泛应用于长期预测。然而，大多数现有的 Transformer 预测器主要改进注意力设计或计算效率，而前馈块中的非线性变换基本保持不变。在高度不稳定的条件下，这种平滑的静态激活可能不足以捕获突然的局部转变。, but their sequential computation limits parallelism and often makes long-horizon optimization difficult. Transformer-based models address this issue through self-attention and have become widely used in long-term forecasting. However, most existing Transformer forecasters mainly improve attention design or computational efficiency, while the nonlinear transformation in the feed-forward block remains largely unchanged. Under highly volatile conditions, such smooth static activations may be insufficient to capture abrupt local transitions.

所提出的方法保留了全局依赖建模的时间自注意力，同时用基于多个 Lee 振荡器系列的混沌门控激活取代了前馈块中的传统点式激活。具体来说，QFCQT采用多元数值序列的线性嵌入，引入八个参数化Lee振子族与Max-over-Time池化的软叠加，并使用平滑混沌门控融合和代理梯度训练来实现稳定优化。The proposed method retains temporal self-attention for global dependency modeling, while replacing the conventional pointwise activation in the feed-forward block with a chaotically gated activation based on multiple Lee oscillator families. Specifically, QFCQT adopts linear embedding for multivariate numerical sequences, introduces a soft superposition of eight parameterized Lee oscillator families with Max-over-Time pooling, and uses smooth-chaotic gated fusion with surrogate-gradient training for stable optimization.

因此，我们的贡献被定位为现有思想的实际整合和扩展，而不是量子或分形动力学的新形式理论。具体来说，QFCQT 将 Quantformer 式数值编码器与 Lee 振荡器激活族的可学习混合和平滑混沌门控融合机制相结合。该设计旨在提高 Transformer 前馈模块对局部波动、突变状态变化和非线性振荡模式的敏感性，同时保持稳定的优化。Our contribution is therefore positioned as a practical integration and extension of existing ideas rather than a new formal theory of quantum or fractal dynamics. Specifically, QFCQT combines a Quantformer-style numerical encoder with a learnable mixture of Lee-oscillator activation families and a smooth-chaotic gated fusion mechanism. This design aims to improve the sensitivity of Transformer feed-forward blocks to local volatility, abrupt regime changes, and nonlinear oscillatory patterns while preserving stable optimization.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于变压器的时间序列预测Transformer-Based Time-Series Forecasting

Transformer 变体已广泛用于时间序列预测，因为自注意力可以有效地建模长期时间依赖性。现有方法通过稀疏注意力、分解机制、频域建模、补丁标记化或架构效率来提高预测性能。尽管取得了这些进步，但其中许多仍然在前馈网络内采用传统的平滑激活，当本地系统动态快速变化时，这可能会降低响应能力。Transformer variants have been widely used in time-series forecasting because self-attention can effectively model long-term temporal dependencies. Existing methods improve forecasting performance through sparse attention, decomposition mechanisms, frequency-domain modeling, patch tokenization, or architectural efficiency. Despite these advances, many of them still employ conventional smooth activations inside the feed-forward network, which may reduce responsiveness when local system dynamics change rapidly.

Ⅱ-BII-B 数值序列的 QuantformerQuantformer for Numerical Sequences

Quantformer 证明标准 NLP 风格的 Transformer 设计并不总是适合数值时间序列建模。特别是，它用可学习的线性投影代替了量化输入的词嵌入，并通过删除自回归解码和掩码操作来简化输出过程，使该框架更适合与股票相关的数字序列。Quantformer demonstrates that standard NLP-style Transformer design is not always ideal for numerical time-series modeling. In particular, it replaces word embedding with a learnable linear projection for quantitative inputs and simplifies the output process by removing autoregressive decoding and masking operations, making the framework better suited to stock-related numerical sequences.

该原理与预测任务直接相关，其中输入标记已经是按时间顺序排列的数值观察值而不是符号语言单元。我们的工作继承了这个想法，并将其从面向股票的因子学习扩展到更广泛的多元预测设置。This principle is directly relevant to forecasting tasks where the input tokens are already temporally ordered numerical observations rather than symbolic language units. Our work inherits this idea and extends it from stock-oriented factor learning to a broader multivariate forecasting setting.

Ⅱ-CII-C 基于混沌振荡器的神经激活Chaotic Oscillator-Based Neural Activation

基于 Lee 振荡器的神经动力学为具有强非线性、波动性和突变的系统建模提供了一种有前景的机制。 COTNLee Oscillator-based neural dynamics provide a promising mechanism for modeling systems with strong nonlinearity, volatility, and abrupt transitions. COTN[4]表明，通过使用最大时间池化压缩振荡器轨迹并通过可学习门将所得响应与 GELU 自适应融合，混沌激活可以在 Transformer 预测中变得实用。shows that chaotic activation can be made practical in Transformer forecasting by compressing oscillator trajectories using Max-over-Time pooling and adaptively fusing the resulting response with GELU through a learnable gate.

此外，Lee 振荡器特别有吸引力，因为它的离散时间公式在计算上易于处理，并且其渐进混沌增长适合易失性系统。Moreover, the Lee Oscillator is particularly attractive because its discrete-time formulation is computationally tractable and its progressive chaotic growth is suitable for volatile systems.

我们的方法建立在这个基础上，但我们不是依赖于单一选定的振荡器类型，而是引入了超过八个振荡器系列的可学习软混合，并将其集成到 Quantformer 风格的主干中。Our method builds on this foundation, but instead of relying on a single chosen oscillator type, we introduce a learnable soft mixture over eight oscillator families and integrate it into a Quantformer-style backbone.

维-DV-D 为什么所提出的方法有效Why the Proposed Method Works

QFCQT 的性能提升可归因于三个互补因素。The performance gains of QFCQT can be attributed to three complementary factors.

首先，Quantformer 式的数值嵌入避免了不必要的 NLP 式的假设，并且更好地与有序的定量观察保持一致。这为数值序列预测提供了更清晰、更直接的表示。First, the Quantformer-style numerical embedding avoids unnecessary NLP-style assumptions and is better aligned with ordered quantitative observations. This provides a cleaner and more direct representation for numerical sequence forecasting.

其次，李氏振荡器激活引入了比传统的逐点激活更丰富的非线性响应景观。通过内部时间演化和最大随时间池化，该模型可以将每个标量预激活转换为对急剧局部变化更敏感的动态元激活。Second, the Lee Oscillator activation introduces a richer nonlinear response landscape than conventional pointwise activations. Through internal temporal evolution and Max-over-Time pooling, the model can transform each scalar pre-activation into a dynamic meta-activation that is more sensitive to sharp local variations.

第三，平滑混沌门控融合稳定了训练。可学习门不会让混沌响应主导所有隐藏变换，而是自适应地平衡振荡器响应和平滑 GELU 分量。这种受控融合使模型能够从增强的响应能力中受益，而无需牺牲收敛行为。Third, the smooth-chaotic gated fusion stabilizes training. Instead of letting chaotic responses dominate all hidden transformations, the learnable gate adaptively balances the oscillator response and the smooth GELU component. This controlled fusion allows the model to benefit from enhanced responsiveness without sacrificing convergence behavior.

VI 讨论Discussion

这些结果表明，前馈激活设计是 Transformer 预测中一个重要但经常未被充分探索的因素。 QFCQT 并不主张正式的量子或分形理论；相反，术语“量子分形启发”是指基于软振荡器族叠加和多尺度非线性响应组织的计算类比。通过最大随时间池化和平滑混沌门控融合来约束混沌响应，该模型提高了对局部突然变化的敏感性，同时保持稳定的优化。These results suggest that feed-forward activation design is an important but often underexplored factor in Transformer forecasting. QFCQT does not claim a formal quantum or fractal theory; rather, the term “quantum-fractal-inspired” refers to a computational analogy based on soft oscillator-family superposition and multi-scale nonlinear response organization. By constraining chaotic responses through Max-over-Time pooling and smooth-chaotic gated fusion, the model improves sensitivity to abrupt local changes while maintaining stable optimization.

七VII结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 QFCQT，这是一种用于复杂波动动态下时间序列预测的混沌门控 Quantformer 框架。与依赖固定平滑激活的传统基于 Transformer 的预测模型不同，QFCQT 将基于 Lee 振荡器的动态激活、Max-over-Time 池化和平滑混沌门控融合策略集成到 Quantformer 式编码器中，从而能够对长程依赖性和非线性时间动态进行有效建模。In this paper, we proposed QFCQT, a chaotically gated Quantformer framework for time-series forecasting under complex volatile dynamics. Unlike conventional Transformer-based forecasting models relying on fixed smooth activations, QFCQT integrates a Lee-oscillator-based dynamic activation, Max-over-Time pooling, and a smooth-chaotic gated fusion strategy into a Quantformer-style encoder, enabling effective modeling of long-range dependencies and nonlinear temporal dynamics.

所提出的框架在 ETTh 上进行了评估The proposed framework was evaluated on ETTh1, ETTh2、A股股指基准。实验结果表明，QFCQT 在多个预测范围内始终优于代表性基线，包括 Informer、LogTrans、LSTMa、HAT、COTN 和 TimesNet，在高波动性环境下的改进尤其显着。, and A-share Stock Index benchmarks. Experimental results showed that QFCQT consistently outperformed representative baselines, including Informer, LogTrans, LSTMa, HAT, COTN, and TimesNet, across multiple forecasting horizons, with particularly notable improvements under highly volatile settings.

总的来说，结果表明前馈非线性变换在 Transformer 预测中起着至关重要的作用。通过引入自适应混沌动力学，QFCQT 为非平稳时间序列预测提供了有效的解决方案，未来的工作将探索其扩展到更广泛的序列建模任务。Overall, the results demonstrate that the feed-forward nonlinear transformation plays a crucial role in Transformer forecasting. By introducing adaptive chaotic dynamics, QFCQT provides an effective solution for non-stationary time-series prediction, and future work will explore its extension to broader sequence modeling tasks.
