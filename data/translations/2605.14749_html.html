Sangwoo Kim

However, existing intervention methods are confined to linear interventions grounded in the Linear Representation Hypothesis, leaving features encoded along non-linear manifolds beyond their reach. In this work, we introduce a general formulation of intervention that extends naturally to non-linearly represented features, together with a learning procedure that further enables intervention on隐含的implicit缺乏直接输出签名的功能。我们验证了拒绝旁路引导的框架，通过干预管理拒绝的非线性特征，它比线性基线更精确地引导模型。features lacking a direct output signature. 1 简介Introduction

干预是理解大语言模型（LLM）内部表征的核心工具。通过在推理过程中修改模型的内部激活并观察由此产生的输出变化，干预措施可以产生将特定组件与模型行为联系起来的因果证据Intervention is a central tool in understanding inner representations of large language models (LLMs). By modifying a model’s internal activations during inference and observing the resulting change in output, interventions yield causal evidence linking specific components to model behavior(Vig et al., 2020; Meng et al., 2022; Geiger et al., 2024)。通过干预，我们可以了解可解释的特征是如何在模型的隐藏状态中表示的. Through intervention, we can understand how interpretable features are represented in the model’s hidden states(Huang et al., 2024)，并通过这样做，通过指导 LLM 行为进行有效控制，这些行为很难通过单独提示来解决，例如反应风格, and by doing so, effectively control via steering LLM behaviors that are hard to address through prompting alone, such as response style(Turner et al., 2025), 幻觉, hallucination(Li et al., 2023)，并拒绝, and refusal(Arditi et al., 2024).

大多数现有的干预此类特征的方法都是线性的：它们通过在激活空间中添加或消除固定方向来修改隐藏状态Most existing methods for intervening on such features are linear: they modify hidden states by adding or ablating a fixed direction in activation space(Li et al., 2023; Arditi et al., 2024)。该设计基于线性表示假设 (LRH)，该假设假设可解释的概念被编码为模型表示空间中的方向. This design rests on the Linear Representation Hypothesis (LRH), which posits that interpretable concepts are encoded as directions in the model’s representation space(Mikolov et al., 2013; Park et al., 2023)。然而，最近的工作表明，一些概念是沿着非线性流形表示的，例如，一周中的几天组织为圆形结构. Recent work, however, has shown that some concepts are instead represented along non-linear manifolds—for example, days of the week organized as a circular structure(Engels et al., 2025)，以及用于执行计数的几何结构, and the geometric structures used to perform counting(Gurnee et al., 2025)。由于线性干预只能通过构造来操纵编码为方向的特征，因此它们无法达到此类非线性表示的结构。. Since linear interventions can by construction only manipulate features encoded as directions, they cannot reach this class of non-linearly represented structure.

为了克服这个限制，我们引入了非线性干预的一般公式。我们通过用可逆非线性特征图替换其底层线性变换来概括线性干预。此外，我们提出了一个通过以下方式学习该地图的过程：To overcome this limitation, we introduce a general formulation of non-linear intervention. We generalize linear interventions by replacing their underlying linear transformation with an invertible non-linear feature map. In addition, we propose a procedure for learning this map via交流干预interchange interventions (Geiger et al., 2024)，以及将程序扩展到, together with a loss design that extends the procedure to隐含的implicit特点。features.

我们实例化拒绝绕行转向的框架We instantiate the framework on refusal bypass steering(Arditi et al., 2024; Wollschläger et al., 2025)，一个代表性的隐式特征干预任务。我们的非线性干预获得了与强线性基线相当的转向有效性，同时以少几个数量级的隐藏状态位置编辑激活。进一步的分析表明，这种优势源于主要位于模型中间层的控制拒绝的真正非线性特征图。, a representative implicit-feature intervention task. Our non-linear intervention attains steering effectiveness comparable to strong linear baselines while editing activations at orders of magnitude fewer hidden-state locations. Further analysis indicates that this advantage stems from a genuinely non-linear feature map governing refusal that resides primarily at the model’s middle layers.

我们的工作做出了三个贡献。首先，我们提出了非线性干预的一般公式，它包含现有的线性干预，并自然地扩展到沿非线性流形编码的特征。其次，我们引入了基于交换干预的非线性特征图的学习过程，以及进一步实现学习的损失设计Our work makes three contributions. First, we propose a general formulation of non-linear intervention that subsumes existing linear interventions and naturally extends to features encoded along non-linear manifolds. Second, we introduce a learning procedure for the non-linear feature map based on interchange interventions, together with a loss design that further enables learning隐含的implicit模型输出中未出现的特征。第三，我们凭经验将我们的框架应用于拒绝旁路转向，并表明对控制拒绝的非线性特征进行干预比线性基线更精确地引导模型。代码可在features that do not surface in the model’s output. Third, we empirically apply our framework to refusal bypass steering and show that intervening on a non-linear feature governing refusal steers the model more precisely than linear baselines. 6 结论Conclusion

我们通过提供非线性干预的一般公式来克服现有干预的线性限制。除了公式本身之外，我们还提出了一种通过交换干预来学习非线性特征图的程序，以及监督该程序的想法We overcome the linearity restriction of existing interventions by providing a general formulation of non-linear intervention. Beyond the formulation itself, we propose a procedure for learning the non-linear feature map via interchange interventions, together with the idea of supervising this procedure for隐含的implicit通过与目标特征相关的模型的隐藏状态来特征。我们在旁路拒绝转向上实例化该框架，这是一种代表性的隐式特征转向任务；我们的实验和分析表明，非线性干预可以有效地引导拒绝行为，并且在此过程中，它揭示了模型内部的非线性特征。features through hidden states of the model that are correlated with the target feature. We instantiate this framework on bypass refusal steering, a representative implicit-feature steering task; our experiments and analysis show that non-linear intervention effectively steers refusal behavior and that, in doing so, it uncovers a non-linear feature inside the model.
