OmniFood-Bench：评估 VLM 的营养推理和个性化健康建议OmniFood-Bench: Evaluating VLMs for Nutrient Reasoning and Personalized Health Advice

Qian Jiang1, Zhecheng Shi2, Jingpu Yang3, Zirui Song4, Miao Fang1* *Corresponding author: Miao Fang.
摘要Abstract

大视觉语言模型 (VLM) 快速集成到关键基础设施中有望彻底改变个性化医疗保健和饮食管理。然而，在食品系统领域，自主代理面临着独特且持续的挑战：视觉外观和内在营养成分之间的“系统信息不对称”。现有的基准主要关注粗粒度的分类任务，例如食品类别识别，无法评估现实世界饮食管理所需的复杂推理链，特别是从识别隐藏成分到估计物理质量，最后综合安全关键医疗建议的能力。在本文中，我们介绍The rapid integration of Large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) into critical infrastructure promises to revolutionize personalized healthcare and dietary management. However, in the domain of food systems, autonomous agents face a unique and persistent challenge: the “Systemic Information Asymmetry” between visual appearance and intrinsic nutritional composition. Existing benchmarks primarily focus on coarse-grained classification tasks, such as food category recognition, which fail to evaluate the intricate reasoning chain required for real-world dietary management—specifically, the ability to traverse from identifying hidden ingredients to estimating physical mass, and finally synthesizing safety-critical medical advice. In this paper, we introduceOmniFood-长凳OmniFood-Bench，根据 MM-Food-100K 数据集构建的综合基准。与之前的工作不同，OmniFood-Bench 通过三个渐进功能评估 VLM：基本感知（成分和烹饪方法）、定量推理（份量和营养分析）和安全关键咨询（特定疾病建议）。我们评估了六种最先进的 VLM，包括 gpt-5.1、gemini-3-flash 和 qwen3-vl-8B。我们的大量实验揭示了惊人的“语义-物理差距”：虽然模型在命名菜肴方面达到了接近人类的准确性，但它们在质量估计方面表现出灾难性的失败，并且经常对高风险糖尿病概况产生幻觉良性建议。这项工作为公共卫生部署的自主代理的可信度建立了严格的标准。 代码和数据集可在：https://anonymous.4open.science/r/OmniFood-Bench-7D0B, a comprehensive benchmark constructed from the MM-Food-100K dataset. Unlike previous works, OmniFood-Bench evaluates VLMs across three progressive capabilities: Basic Perception (Ingredients & Cooking Methods), Quantitative Reasoning (Portion Size & Nutritional Profiling), and Safety-Critical Advisory (Disease-Specific Recommendations). We evaluate six state-of-the-art VLMs, including gpt-5.1, gemini-3-flash, and qwen3-vl-8B. Our extensive experiments reveal a startling “Semantic-Physical Gap”: while models achieve near-human accuracy in naming dishes, they exhibit catastrophic failure in mass estimation and frequently hallucinate benign advice for high-risk diabetic profiles. This work establishes a rigorous standard for trustworthiness in autonomous agents deployed for public health. The code and datasets are available in:https://anonymous.4open.science/r/OmniFood-Bench-7D0B

I 简介Introduction

在智能媒体时代，自主代理越来越多地承担解释高维多模态数据以协助复杂的日常决策过程的任务In the era of intelligent media, autonomous agents are increasingly tasked with interpreting high-dimensional multimodal data to assist in complex daily decision-making processes[24]。在这些药物的众多应用中，人工智能驱动的饮食管理在应对肥胖、糖尿病和高血压等慢性病全球负担日益沉重方面具有巨大潜力。. Among the myriad applications of these agents, AI-driven dietary management holds immense potential for combating the rising global burden of chronic diseases such as obesity, diabetes, and hypertension.[11, 32]理论上的“人工智能营养师”代理应该具备通过摄像头感知膳食的能力，以临床精度分析其营养成分，并根据用户的特定生理状态提供个性化建议。这样一个系统的实现将使个性化营养的获取民主化，超越一般的卡路里计数，转向具有风险意识的健康监测。A theoretical ”AI Dietitian” agent should possess the capability to perceive a meal via a camera, analyze its nutritional composition with clinical precision, and offer personalized advice tailored to the user’s specific physiological state. The realization of such a system would democratize access to personalized nutrition, moving beyond generic calorie counting to risk-aware health surveillance.

然而，此类自主代理的部署目前受到鲁棒性、数据信任和安全性方面的重大挑战的阻碍However, the deployment of such autonomous agents is currently hindered by significant challenges regarding robustness, data trust, and safety[9, 31]。与识别边界框就足够的一般对象检测任务不同，. Unlike general object detection tasks where identifying a bounding box suffices,[26]理解食物需要一个复杂的过程food understanding requires a complex process of视觉到物理推理Visual-to-Physical Inference。代理必须从可见的事物中推断出不可见的事物：确定菜肴是油炸的还是蒸的（这会极大地改变热量密度），从 2D 像素估计牛排的物理质量（解决规模和深度模糊性），并检索特定领域的医学知识，以警告糖尿病用户釉料中潜在隐藏的糖分。这就造成了“系统信息不对称”，如果没有强大的推理和世界知识，仅靠视觉信号往往是不够的。. An agent must deduce the invisible from the visible: determining whether a dish is deep-fried or steamed (which drastically alters caloric density), estimating the physical mass of a steak from 2D pixels (resolving scale and depth ambiguity), and retrieving domain-specific medical knowledge to warn a diabetic user about potential hidden sugars in a glaze. This creates a ”Systemic Information Asymmetry” where the visual signal alone is often insufficient without robust reasoning and world knowledge.

这个领域的风险非常高。在典型的视觉任务中，错误分类可能会给用户带来轻微的不便。在慢性病患者的饮食管理中，对糖含量或份量的“幻觉”可能会导致不良的健康结果，例如糖尿病患者的高血糖事件或心脏病患者的高血压危象。The stakes in this domain are exceptionally high. In typical vision tasks, a misclassification might result in a minor user inconvenience. In dietary management for chronic disease patients, a ”hallucination” regarding sugar content or portion size can lead to adverse health outcomes, such as hyperglycemic events for diabetics or hypertensive crises for heart patients.[23]因此，这些代理的“安全一致性”不仅仅是质量指标，而且是部署的基本要求。当前的大型视觉语言模型 (VLM) 尽管在一般字幕方面具有令人印象深刻的功能，但尚未在这个特定的高风险领域进行严格的压力测试。Therefore, the ”Safety Alignment” of these agents is not merely a metric of quality but a fundamental requirement for deployment. Current Large Vision-Language Models (VLMs), despite their impressive capabilities in general captioning, have not been rigorously stress-tested in this specific, high-risk domain.[2, 3]他们经常表现出“语义-物理差距”，他们可以雄辩地描述一道菜，但无法理解其物理特性或健康影响。They often exhibit a ”Semantic-Physical Gap,” where they can eloquently describe a dish but fail to understand its physical properties or health implications.

食品计算的现有基准，例如 Food-101Existing benchmarks in food computing, such as Food-101[1], VireoFood-172[4]，以及其他人, and others[29]，主要关注视觉分类或食谱检索的问题。虽然这些数据集推动了细粒度识别的进步，但它们主要将食物视为语义标签，而不是具有质量、体积和化学属性的物理对象。最近的努力，例如 Nutrition5k, have predominantly focused on the problem of visual classification or recipe retrieval. While these datasets have driven progress in fine-grained recognition, they treat food largely as semantic labels rather than physical objects with mass, volume, and chemical properties. More recent efforts like Nutrition5k[25]引入了深度和大量数据，但它们通常是在受控实验室环境中收集的，缺乏现实世界就餐场景的视觉复杂性和“野外”可变性。此外，没有现有的基准明确评估have introduced depth and mass data, but they are often collected in controlled laboratory settings that lack the visual complexity and ”in-the-wild” variability of real-world dining scenarios. Furthermore, no existing benchmark explicitly evaluates the端到端推理链end-to-end reasoning chain——从像素到处方——专门针对安全关键的健康场景。—from pixels to prescription—specifically targeting safety-critical health scenarios.

为了弥合这一关键差距，我们建议To bridge this critical gap, we proposeOmniFood-长凳OmniFood-Bench，一个分层评估框架，旨在对当前最先进的 VLM 的极限进行压力测试。我们将评估分类法分为三个渐进层，以模仿人类营养师的认知过程。首先，, a hierarchical evaluation framework designed to stress-test the limits of current state-of-the-art VLMs. We structure our evaluation taxonomy into three progressive layers to mimic the cognitive process of a human dietitian. First,基本认知Basic Perception测试模型识别食材和烹饪方法的能力，建立语义理解的基线。第二，tests the model’s ability to identify ingredients and cooking methods, establishing a baseline for semantic understanding. Second,定量估算Quantitative Estimation探索模型的“物理世界模型”，要求它从 2D 图像中估计份量大小（以克为单位）和营养成分（宏观）。三、probes the model’s ”Physical World Model,” requiring it to estimate portion sizes (in grams) and nutritional profiles (macros) from 2D images. Third,高级推理Advanced Reasoning评估代理将这些信息合成为具有特定医疗状况的用户的安全、个性化饮食建议的能力。evaluates the agent’s ability to synthesize this information into safe, personalized dietary advice for users with specific medical conditions.

我们的贡献是三重的。首先，我们建立了第一个统一基准，将视觉感知直接与医疗安全结果联系起来，超越简单的准确性指标，转向“风险意识”评估。其次，我们对六种领先的专有和开源模型进行了全面分析，揭示了强大的通用能力并不会自动转化为安全饮食推理。第三，我们诊断特定的故障模式，确定当前代理的主要瓶颈在于“视觉到质量”估计步骤，该步骤将错误传播到下游健康建议。这项工作是为全球食品系统建立值得信赖、安全的自主代理的基础性步骤。Our contributions are threefold. First, we establish the first unified benchmark that links visual perception directly to medical safety outcomes, moving beyond simple accuracy metrics to ”Risk-Aware” evaluation. Second, we provide a comprehensive analysis of six leading proprietary and open-source models, revealing that strong general capabilities do not automatically translate to safe dietary reasoning. Third, we diagnose specific failure modes, identifying that the primary bottleneck for current agents lies in the ”Visual-to-Mass” estimation step, which propagates errors downstream to health advice. This work serves as a foundational step toward building trustworthy, safety-aligned autonomous agents for the global food system.[19]

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 食品计算的演变Evolution of Food Computing

食品计算领域在过去十年中经历了重大转变，从简单的图像分类发展到复杂的多模态分析。早期的基础数据集，例如 Food-101The field of food computing has undergone a significant transformation over the past decade, evolving from simple image classification to complex multimodal analysis. Early foundational datasets, such as Food-101[1]和 UEC-食品and UEC-Food[14]，主要侧重于将菜肴分类为固定分类法。这些基准推动了卷积神经网络 (CNN) 的发展，该网络能够区分视觉上相似的菜肴, focused primarily on categorizing dishes into fixed taxonomies. These benchmarks drove the development of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) capable of distinguishing between visually similar dishes[22, 16]后续工作通过引入成分级注释和配方检索任务扩展了这一范围.Subsequent works expanded this scope by introducing ingredient-level annotations and recipe retrieval tasks[10]，如 VireoFood-172 中所示, as seen in VireoFood-172[13]和食谱1Mand Recipe1M[17]。这些数据集使模型能够学习视觉外观和文本成分之间的相关性。然而，这些早期作品的一个主要限制是将食物视为语义标签而不是物理对象。它们缺乏精确营养分析所需的定量元数据，例如重量、体积和热量密度。虽然最近的数据集如 Nutrition5k. These datasets enabled models to learn the correlation between visual appearance and textual ingredients. However, a major limitation of these earlier works is their treatment of food as semantic labels rather than physical objects. They lack the quantitative metadata—such as weight, volume, and caloric density—necessary for precise nutritional analysis. While recent datasets like Nutrition5k[25]试图通过结合深度数据和尺度测量来解决这个问题，但它们在很大程度上受限于实验室环境，限制了它们对现实世界餐饮中复杂、杂乱和闭塞环境的推广。have attempted to address this by incorporating depth data and scale measurements, they are largely constrained to laboratory settings, limiting their generalizability to the complex, cluttered, and occluded environments found in real-world dining.

Ⅱ-BII-B 医疗保健领域的大型多式联运模型Large Multimodal Models in Healthcare

多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 的最新进展，例如 Med-GeminiRecent advancements in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), such as Med-Gemini[21]和专门的临床 VLM，在解释放射扫描和生成诊断报告方面表现出了令人印象深刻的能力。and specialized clinical VLMs, have demonstrated impressive capabilities in interpreting radiological scans and generating diagnostic reports.[15, 20, 27]然而，在以下领域仍存在显着差距：However, a significant gap remains in the domain of预防性的preventive健康和饮食监测。与标准化医学成像不同，食物图像高度非结构化，并且存在严重的遮挡和尺度模糊。目前的通用型号，如 GPT-5.1health and dietary monitoring. Unlike standardized medical imaging, food imagery is highly unstructured and suffers from severe occlusion and scale ambiguity. Current general-purpose models like GPT-5.1[8]虽然功能强大，但通常缺乏区分视觉上相似但营养上不同的食品制剂所需的特定领域对齐（例如，区分无糖与釉面甜点），从而导致潜在危险的咨询幻觉。, while powerful, often lack the specific domain alignment required to distinguish between visually similar but nutritionally distinct food preparations (e.g., distinguishing sugar-free vs. glazed desserts), leading to potentially dangerous advisory hallucinations.

Ⅱ-CII-C 自主代理的安全协调Safety Alignment in Autonomous Agents

对于在高风险环境中运行的自主代理来说，可信度至关重要。在医疗保健和食品系统的背景下，“幻觉”不仅仅是一个事实错误Trustworthiness is paramount for autonomous agents operating in high-stakes environments. In the context of healthcare and food systems, a ”hallucination” is not merely a factual error[12, 5];这是一个安全隐患。人工智能安全的最新研究主要集中在防止毒性、偏见和有害内容的生成; it is a potential safety hazard. Recent research in AI safety has focused heavily on preventing toxicity, bias, and harmful content generation[33, 18]。然而，在生物医学背景下，关于“事实安全”的研究相对缺乏——具体来说，确保代理人不会基于有缺陷的视觉感知而推荐禁忌行为。 OmniFood-Bench 通过在饮食建议中明确量化“安全幻觉”来解决这个问题。我们超越标准毒性检测来评估“生物医学事实一致性”，创建一个新标准来确定自主代理是否足够安全，可以部署为个人健康助理。. However, there is a relative scarcity of research on ”Factual Safety” in biomedical contexts—specifically, ensuring that an agent does not recommend contraindicated actions based on flawed visual perception. OmniFood-Bench addresses this by explicitly quantifying ”Safety Hallucinations” in the context of dietary advice. We move beyond standard toxicity detection to evaluate ”Biomedical Factual Alignment,” creating a new standard for determining whether an autonomous agent is safe enough to be deployed as a personal health assistant.

三、IIIOmniFood-Bench 框架The OmniFood-Bench Framework

为了确保对食品领域的自主代理进行严格评估，我们设计了 OmniFood-Bench 框架。本节详细介绍了数据管理过程、严格的注释管道和分层任务定义。To ensure a rigorous evaluation of autonomous agents in the food domain, we designed the OmniFood-Bench framework. This section details the data curation process, the rigorous annotation pipeline, and the hierarchical task definitions.

Ⅲ-AIII-A 数据构建和管理Data Construction and Curation

我们使用精心策划的 MM-Food-100K 子集构建了 OmniFood-BenchWe constructed OmniFood-Bench using a carefully curated subset of the MM-Food-100K[6]数据集，专门选择以最大限度地提高多样性和挑战。该数据集包含 1,208 个高质量样本，分布在四个主要类别：dataset, specifically selected to maximize diversity and challenge. The dataset comprises 1,208 high-quality samples distributed across four major categories:自制食品Homemade Food, 餐厅食物Restaurant Food, 包装食品Packaged Food, 和, and原料Raw Ingredients.

通过人工抽查验证了这一高质量子集的完整性，以确保数据的真实性和可靠性。此外，根据权威的健康和卫生标准，我们根据原始数据集中蛋白质、脂肪和碳水化合物的具体重量（以克为单位）建立了标签系统，分为正常摄入量、控制摄入量和不推荐摄入量。这些标签通过定义必需营养素摄入量的精确数值范围，针对各种临床状况（例如糖尿病和慢性肾病）量身定制。The integrity of this high-quality subset was verified through manual spot checks to ensure data authenticity and reliability. Furthermore, in accordance with authoritative health and hygiene standards, we established a labeling system—categorized as Normal Intake, Controlled Intake, and Not Recommended—based on the specific weights (in grams) of proteins, fats, and carbohydrates found in the original dataset. These labels are tailored to various clinical conditions, such as diabetes and chronic kidney disease, by defining precise numerical ranges for essential nutrient intake.

我们在 1,208 个样本上评估了开源模型（例如 qwen3-vl-8B），而在 496 个样本的代表性子集上评估了闭源模型（例如 gpt-5.1）。We evaluated open-source models (e.g., qwen3-vl-8B) on 1,208 samples, while closed-source models (e.g., gpt-5.1) were evaluated on a representative subset of 496 samples.

IV 实验与分析Experiments and Analysis

IV-A 实验装置Experimental Setup

V 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了In this paper, we introducedOmniFood-长凳OmniFood-Bench，一个开创性的基准，旨在评估食品系统中自主代理的可信度和稳健性。通过对 6 个最先进的 VLM 进行严格的 3 阶段评估，我们展示了明显的“语义-物理差距”。当前的模型擅长识别，但在物理量化方面失败，更关键的是，它们缺乏安全关键的健康咨询所需的强大推理能力。开源模型开始缩小视觉基础方面的差距，但复杂的推理仍然是所有架构的挑战。今后的工作必须重点, a pioneering benchmark designed to evaluate the trustworthiness and robustness of autonomous agents in food systems. Through a rigorous 3-stage evaluation of 6 state-of-the-art VLMs, we have demonstrated a clear ”Semantic-Physical Gap.” Current models excel at recognition but fail at physical quantification, and more critically, they lack the robust reasoning capabilities required for safety-critical health advisory. Open-source models are beginning to close the gap in visual grounding, but complex reasoning remains a challenge for all architectures. Future work must focus on神经符号整合Neuro-Symbolic Integration，将 VLM 的感知优势与结构化营养知识库和检索增强生成 (RAG) 相结合，以确保安全、准确且有依据的饮食建议。, combining the perceptual strengths of VLMs with structured nutritional knowledge bases and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ensure safe, accurate, and physically grounded dietary advice.
