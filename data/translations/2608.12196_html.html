1 简介Introduction

医学图像分割是计算医学的基石任务，是解剖结构和病理区域定量分析的先决条件。通过计算机断层扫描 (CT) 和磁共振成像 (MRI) 准确描绘器官、肿瘤和组织，使临床医生能够评估疾病进展、计划手术干预并定量精确地监测治疗反应Medical image segmentation is a cornerstone task in computational medicine, serving as the prerequisite for quantitative analysis of anatomical structures and pathological regions. Accurate delineation of organs, tumors, and tissues from computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) enables clinicians to assess disease progression, plan surgical interventions, and monitor treatment responses with quantitative precision1。在最具临床意义的应用中，对比增强 CT 扫描的肝脏分割对于肝细胞癌（全球第六大常见癌症）的诊断和治疗至关重要. Among the most clinically consequential applications, liver segmentation from contrast-enhanced CT scans is essential for the diagnosis and management of hepatocellular carcinoma, the sixth most common cancer worldwide2。同样，肾脏分割和脑肿瘤分割分别代表腹部和神经肿瘤成像的基本任务. Similarly, kidney segmentation and brain tumor segmentation represent fundamental tasks in abdominal and neuro-oncological imaging, respectively3, 4.

1.1 背景和动机Background and Motivation

自从 Ronneberger 等人的开创性工作以来。Since the seminal work of Ronneberger et al.52015年，基于卷积神经网络（CNN）的方法已经在医学图像分割领域占据主导地位。事实证明，U-Net 架构具有对称的编码器-解码器结构和跳跃连接，对于训练数据稀缺但空间精度至关重要的生物医学应用非常有效。 U-Net 的成功源于其将编码器的高级语义特征与解码器的细粒度空间细节相结合的能力，即使在注释数据有限的情况下也能实现精确定位。in 2015, convolutional neural network (CNN)-based approaches have come to dominate the field of medical image segmentation. The U-Net architecture, with its symmetric encoder-decoder structure and skip connections, has proven remarkably effective for biomedical applications where training data is scarce but spatial precision is paramount. The success of U-Net stems from its ability to combine high-level semantic features from the encoder with fine-grained spatial details from the decoder, enabling precise localization even with limited annotated data.

在过去的十年中，人们提出了许多扩展来解决原始 U-Net 的特定限制。切切克等人。Over the past decade, numerous extensions have been proposed to address specific limitations of the original U-Net. 1.2 挑战和限制Challenges and Limitations

  1. 1.

    界限模糊：Ambiguous boundaries:肿瘤-组织和器官-组织界面通常表现出低对比度和逐渐的强度转变，使得边界描绘本身就困难。从像素级模式中学习的标准 CNN 特征可能缺乏捕捉这些界面上细微纹理不连续性的灵敏度。Tumor-tissue and organ-tissue interfaces often exhibit low contrast and gradual intensity transitions, making boundary delineation inherently difficult. Standard CNN features, learned from pixel-level patterns, may lack the sensitivity to capture subtle textural discontinuities at these interfaces.

  2. 2.

    不规则形态：Irregular morphologies:病理区域和解剖结构表现出高度不规则的形状和异质纹理，与许多分割算法假设的平滑、紧凑的结构显着偏离。这种不规则性在侵袭性肿瘤边缘尤其明显，其中组织浸润形成复杂的几何图案。Pathological regions and anatomical structures exhibit highly irregular shapes and heterogeneous textures that deviate significantly from the smooth, compact structures assumed by many segmentation algorithms.   3. 3.

6.3 现场操作员的角色Role of Physical Field Operators

发散算子对于检测具有不同强度分布的局灶性病变最为有效，例如在较暗的肝实质中显示为明亮区域的血管丰富的肝脏肿瘤。正散度表示强度源（明亮的肿瘤核心），而负散度表示强度汇（暗坏死区域）。The divergence operator is most effective for detecting focal lesions with distinct intensity profiles—such as hypervascular liver tumors that appear as bright regions against darker liver parenchyma.
