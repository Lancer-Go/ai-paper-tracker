1 简介Introduction

高质量文本到图像 (T2I) 模型的迅速崛起已将生成式 AI 推向实际的现实应用The rapid rise of high-quality text-to-image (T2I) models has moved generative AI into practical, real-world applications(Google, 2026a, 2025; AI, 2025, 2026; Labs, 2025; Black Forest Labs, 2024; Esser et al., 2024; Cao et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025; Wan Team, 2026; Recraft, 2026)。在这些设置中，忠实度（图像遵循用户提示的准确程度）是一项关键要求。虽然建立了 DSG 等基准. In these settings, faithfulness—how accurately an image follows a user’s prompt—is a critical requirement. While established benchmarks like DSG(Cho et al., 2023), TIFA (Hu et al., 2023), 和 DPG, and DPG(Hu et al., 2024)是为了衡量这一点而创建的，它们主要依赖于易于验证的简单指令。因此，大多数最先进的模型现在在这些现有测试中取得了近乎完美的分数（超过 95%）。然而，随着这些模型集成到日常工作流程中，用户请求变得越来越复杂和现实。当前的基准很难衡量这些复杂的、多方面的指令的忠实度。如图were created to measure this, they predominantly rely on simple instructions that can be easily verified. Consequently, most state-of-the-art models now achieve near-perfect scores (over 95%) on these existing tests. However, as these models integrate into daily workflows, user requests are becoming increasingly complex and realistic. Current benchmarks struggle to measure faithfulness for these sophisticated, multi-faceted instructions. In these scenarios, existing benchmarks are no longer sufficient to capture the nuances of where a model succeeds or fails.

6 结论Conclusion

我们研究如何确保现代文本到图像生成模型的忠实度。我们引入了 Arena-T2I Hard，这是顶级 T2I 模型的压力测试基准，以及依赖项感知检查表分数，可通过结构化、可验证的提示约束来衡量忠诚度。然后，我们使用这个清单分数作为训练后的 RL 奖励。通过大量的实验，我们发现有效的 T2I RL 需要将奖励结构与正在优化的维度相匹配。及时的忠诚度最好通过针对提示的、具有依赖意识的清单奖励来实现，而美学则可以通过基于人类比较训练的 BT 偏好奖励来更好地体现。将这两种互补的奖励结构结合起来可以提高忠诚度和美观性，同时减少它们之间的权衡。We study how to secure faithfulness in modern text-to-image generative models. We introduce Arena-T2I Hard, a stress-test benchmark for top T2I models, and a dependency-aware checklist score that measures faithfulness through structured, verifiable prompt constraints. We then use this checklist score as an RL reward for post-training. Across extensive experiments, we find that effective T2I RL requires matching the reward structure to the dimension being optimized. Prompt faithfulness is best served by prompt-specific, dependency-aware checklist rewards, whereas aesthetics is better captured by BT preference rewards trained on human comparisons. Combining these two complementary reward structures improves both faithfulness and aesthetics, while reducing the trade-off between them.
