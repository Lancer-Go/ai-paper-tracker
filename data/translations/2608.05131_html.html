OPD-V：具有模态平衡的视觉策略自蒸馏OPD-V: Visual On-Policy Self-Distillation with Modality Balance

Aniri    Jinhe Bi    Peng Liao    Zengjie Jin    Volker Tresp    Fei Shen    Yunpu Ma     Tat-Seng Chua [ [ [ [ bijinhe@outlook.com cognitive.yunpu@gmail.com

摘要Abstract

按策略自蒸馏 (OPSD) 已成为一种标准的训练后方法，用于改进多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 中的视觉推理。现有方法从不同的输入源中提取特权信息来指导自蒸馏。然而，这些设计忽视了模态不平衡，这是 MLLM 推理固有的挑战。当文本信息主导生成时，模型无法完全集成其多模态输入。因此，精心设计的特权信息仍未得到充分利用，限制了 OPSD 的有效性。为了检查这一局限性，我们构建了一个带有放大图像的正教师和一个带有掩模图像的负教师，它们表现出不同程度的模态不平衡。推理正确性和令牌逻辑的变化表明模态平衡本身可以充当特权信息。受这一发现的启发，我们引入了 OPD-V，这是一种视觉 OPSD 范式，它通过积极教师和消极教师实例化此类信息。正模态平衡逻辑裕度定义模态平衡信任区域，该区域选择用于自蒸馏的在策略令牌。 6 个基准、4 个 MLLM 主干和 5 种训练后方法的实验表明，OPD-V 持续提高推理性能，同时降低训练成本。On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) has become a standard post-training approach for improving visual reasoning in multimodal large language models (MLLMs). Existing methods draw privileged information from diverse input sources to guide self-distillation. Yet these designs overlook Modality Imbalance, a challenge inherent to MLLM reasoning. When textual information dominates generation, the model cannot fully integrate its multimodal input. Consequently, carefully designed privileged information remains underused, limiting the effectiveness of OPSD. To examine this limitation, we construct a Positive Teacher with the Zoom-In Image and a Negative Teacher with the Mask Image, which exhibit different degrees of Modality Imbalance. Changes in their reasoning correctness and token logits reveal that Modality Balance can itself serve as privileged information. Motivated by this finding, we introduce OPD-V, a visual OPSD paradigm that instantiates such information through the Positive Teacher and Negative Teacher. Positive Modality-Balance Logits Margins define a Modality-Balance Trust Region that selects the on-policy tokens used for self-distillation. Experiments across 6 benchmarks, 4 MLLM backbones, and 5 post-training methods show that OPD-V consistently improves reasoning performance while reducing training cost.

1 简介Introduction

多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 支持广泛的视觉理解和推理任务。按策略自蒸馏（OPSD）最近已成为进一步提高视觉推理能力的标准训练后方法。 OPSD 对当前学生的轨迹进行采样，并使用同一模型的副本在学生访问过的前缀处提供密集目标Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) support a broad range of visual understanding and reasoning tasks. On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) has recently become a standard post-training approach for further improving their visual reasoning. OPSD samples trajectories from the current student and uses a copy of the same model to provide dense targets at student-visited prefixes[agarwal2024policy, lu2025onpolicydistillation, zhao2026self]。该模型副本接收仅在培训期间可用的特权信息，从而无需单独培训的外部教师。. This model copy receives privileged information available only during training, removing the need for a separately trained external teacher.

这些目标的质量取决于特权信息的构建方式。通用策略提供经过验证的文本解决方案或参考答案The quality of these targets depends on how the privileged information is constructed. A common strategy provides verified textual solutions or reference answers[zhao2026self]。最近的视觉 OPSD 方法反而从附加或转换的视觉输入中获取特权信息。 Vision-OPD 使教师以证据为中心的作物，而 Visual-OPSD 使用特权视觉思维. Recent visual OPSD methods instead derive privileged information from additional or transformed visual inputs. Vision-OPD conditions the teacher on an evidence-centered crop, whereas Visual-OPSD uses privileged visual thoughts[yuan2026visionopd, li2026visualopsd]。 VA-OPD 和 VCSD 通过改变视觉内容的访问方式形成教师对比. VA-OPD and VCSD create teacher contrasts by changing access to visual content[liu2026visualadvantage, liang2026visualcontrastive]。尽管这些方法以不同的方式构造特权信号，但它们没有考虑模态不平衡，这是 MLLM 推理固有的挑战。. Although these methods construct the privileged signal in different ways, they do not account for Modality Imbalance, a challenge inherent to MLLM reasoning.

MLLM 根据视觉输入、文本指令和自回归上下文生成每个响应。这种联合条件作用会产生持久的不对称性，因为即使任务依赖于图像，强文本先验也可以主导生成MLLMs generate each response from visual inputs, textual instructions, and autoregressive context. This joint conditioning creates a persistent asymmetry because strong textual priors can dominate generation even when the task depends on the image[bi-etal-2025-llava, park2025generalizing, zhang2025evaluating, zhang2026instruction]。我们将这种趋势定义为. We define this tendency as模态失衡Modality Imbalance：该模型过度依赖文本上下文，而不是集成多模式输入。精心设计的特权信息可以丰富教师的输入，而无需透露视觉和文本模式如何对每个预测做出贡献。因此，模型可能无法充分利用这些信息，从而限制了 OPSD 的有效性。 这种限制提出了我们工作的核心问题：: the model relies disproportionately on textual context instead of integrating the multimodal input. Carefully designed privileged information can enrich the teacher input without revealing how the visual and textual modalities contribute to each prediction. Consequently, the model can underuse this information, limiting the effectiveness of OPSD. This limitation raises the central question of our work:

如何设计特权信息以在模态不平衡的情况下对 MLLM OPSD 保持有效？How can privileged information be designed to remain effective for MLLM OPSD under Modality Imbalance?

目前的工作：The Present Work:我们通过处理来解决这个问题We address this question by treating情态平衡本身Modality Balance itself作为 OPSD 的特权信息并介绍as privileged information for OPSD and introducingOPD-V。与文本解决方案或视觉输入等传统特权信息不同，模态平衡不是可以直接附加到教师上下文的显式输入。相反，它描述了模型在生成过程中视觉和文本信息的内部分配。因此，核心挑战是通过明确的教师输入来表达这种内部条件，并将其转换为 OPSD 的代币级监督。. Unlike conventional privileged information like textual solutions or visual inputs, Modality Balance is not an explicit input that can be directly appended to the teacher context. It instead describes the model’s internal allocation of visual and textual information during generation. The central challenge is therefore to express this internal condition through explicit teacher inputs and convert it into token-level supervision for OPSD.

我们通过 6 个基准、4 个 MLLM 主干和 5 种训练后方法来评估 OPD-V。这些基准跨越视觉要求较高的设置和更广泛的跨图像比例、分辨率、语言和现实世界上下文的多模式推理任务。 OPD-V 在评估的主干网和设置中持续提高推理性能。与标准 OPSD 相比，它还减少了 4B 和 9B 主干上的步进延迟。这些结果表明，OPD-V 使用模态平衡作为特权信息来提高推理准确性，同时降低训练成本。We evaluate OPD-V across 6 benchmarks, 4 MLLM backbones, and 5 post-training methods. These benchmarks span visually demanding settings and broader multimodal reasoning tasks across image scale, resolution, language, and real-world context. OPD-V consistently improves reasoning performance across the evaluated backbones and settings. Compared with standard OPSD, it also reduces step latency on both 4B and 9B backbones. These results show that OPD-V uses Modality Balance as privileged information to improve reasoning accuracy while reducing training cost.

与标准 OPSD 相比，OPD-V 增加了一个 Negative Teacher 前向传递，以在 Mask Image 下获得相同的 on-policy 标记。 Modality-Balance Logits Margin 和 Modality-Balance Trust Region 是按 token 方式计算的，而两位教师共享相同的 EMA 参数；因此，OPD-V 没有引入额外的模型参数或学生更新。在实践中，更短的响应和更有效的教师投入抵消了这一额外的通过，如第 1 节中所测量的那样Compared with standard OPSD, OPD-V adds one Negative Teacher forward pass to score the same on-policy tokens under the Mask Image. The Modality-Balance Logits Margin and Modality-Balance Trust Region are computed tokenwise, while both teachers share the same EMA parameters; OPD-V therefore introduces no additional model parameters or student updates. In practice, shorter responses and more efficient teacher inputs offset this additional pass, as measured in Section4.3.
