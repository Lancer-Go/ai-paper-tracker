局限性Limitations

潜在的限制A potential limitation ofRED是由于解码过程中的额外操作而引入额外的延迟。然而，我们通过严格的延迟压力测试评估了这种额外的延迟（▶ 附录 J）。具体来说，我们通过禁用提前退出机制（即即使满足退出标准也继续生成）来隔离操作开销，同时完全保持探测提示的周期性注入并基于熵和熵方差进行推理干预。最坏情况分析表明，额外的延迟开销平均仅为 4.6%。由于这种额外的延迟很小，因此提前退出机制节省的时间明显超过它。结果，is the introduction of additional latency due to extra operations within the decoding process. However, we evaluated this additional latency through a rigorous latency stress test (▶ Appendix J). Specifically, we isolated the operational overhead by disabling the early-exit mechanism (i.e., continuing generation even if exit criteria are satisfied) while fully maintaining the periodic injection of probe prompts and conducting inference intervention based on entropy and entropy variance. This worst-case profiling reveals that the additional latency overhead averages only 4.6%. Since this additional latency is minimal, the time saved by the early-exit mechanism significantly outweighs it. As a result,RED与基线相比实现了净加速。achieves a net speedup compared to the baseline.
