长度偏差下的准确度和归一化准确度：分析、指南和贝叶斯替代方案Accuracy and Normalized Accuracy under Length Bias: Analysis, Guidelines, and a Bayesian Alternative

Koen Oostermeijer
摘要Abstract

通过条件对数概率对候选完成情况进行排名的多项选择基准会受到长度偏差的影响：因为对数概率对标记求和，所以在实践中，相对于较短的答案，较长的答案往往会受到惩罚。一种常见的缓解方法是通过完成长度来标准化分数，但我们根据经验表明，这种启发式经常会过度纠正，从而引入对较长答案的偏见。我们首先分析这些评分规则，描述标准准确度和长度归一化准确度何时合适，以及它们的长度偏差如何取决于完成长度的分布。受此分析的启发，我们介绍Multiple-choice benchmarks that rank candidate completions by conditional log-probability suffer from a length bias: because log-probabilities sum over tokens, longer answers tend to be penalized relative to shorter ones in practice. A common mitigation is to normalize scores by completion length, but we show empirically that this heuristic frequently over-corrects, introducing a bias toward longer answers instead. We first analyze these scoring rules, characterizing when standard and length-normalized accuracy are appropriate and how their length biases depend on the distribution of completion lengths. Motivated by this analysis, we introduce贝叶斯准确性Bayesian accuracy，一种评分规则，计算每个候选者在答案长度的显式先验下的后验概率，从而消除线性长度效应。贝叶斯准确性是基于可能性的多项选择评估的直接替代品，不需要额外的前向传递，并且在基准和少样本设置中始终表现出比标准和长度归一化准确性更低的经验长度偏差。, a scoring rule that computes the posterior probability of each candidate under an explicit prior over answer length, thereby removing linear length effects. Bayesian accuracy is a drop-in replacement for likelihood-based multiple-choice evaluation, requires no additional forward passes, and consistently exhibits lower empirical length bias than both standard and length-normalized accuracy across benchmarks and few-shot settings.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在从事实问答到多步推理的各种自然语言处理任务上都取得了出色的性能Large language models (LLMs) have achieved strong performance on a wide range of natural language processing tasks, from factual question answering to multi-step reasoning(Brown et al., 2020)。评估此类模型的常见方法是通过多项选择基准：为模型提供提示以及一小组候选完成情况，这些完成情况根据模型分配给每个候选的条件对数概率进行排名。得分最高的候选者被视为模型的答案，准确度计算为得分最高的候选者正确的提示分数。这种基于可能性的多项选择评估的突出例子包括 MMLU. A common way to evaluate such models is via multiple-choice benchmarks: the model is given a prompt together with a small set of candidate completions, which are ranked according to the conditional log-probability the model assigns to each candidate. The candidate with the highest score is taken as the model’s answer, and accuracy is computed as the fraction of prompts for which this highest-scoring candidate is correct. Prominent examples of this likelihood-based multiple-choice evaluation include MMLU(Hendrycks et al., 2020), 海拉斯瓦格, HellaSwag(Zellers et al., 2019)，和威诺格兰德, and WinoGrande(Sakaguchi et al., 2021).

这种评估模型的方法有一个关键优势：通过将可能输出的空间限制为一组固定的基准提供的完成，它将开放式生成变成了一个简单的答案检查问题。评估者不再需要解析或解释任意文本，它只需要比较一个小的、已知的候选集的分数。这对于预先训练的基础模型特别有用 尚未经过指令调整，可能无法可靠地遵循自由格式生成设置中的模板或格式化指令。因为多项选择评估只需要模型为给定的候选者分配概率并选择最有可能的一个，所以它回避了这些格式问题并产生一个对输出格式不敏感的简单、可扩展的指标。This way of evaluating models has a key advantage: by restricting the space of possible outputs to a fixed set of benchmark-provided completions, it turns open-ended generation into a simple answer-checking problem. The evaluator no longer has to parse or interpret arbitrary text, it only needs to compare scores over a small, known candidate set. This is particularly useful for pretrained base models that have not been instruction-tuned and may not reliably follow templates or formatting instructions in free-form generation settings. Because multiple-choice evaluation only requires the model to assign probabilities to the given candidates and select the most likely one, it sidesteps these formatting issues and yields a simple, scalable metric that is insensitive to output format.

这些基准的一个关键属性是完成的总对数似然通常由其按标记对数概率的总和给出。由于此分数是相对于令牌而言的累加性，因此任何每个令牌的不确定性或建模误差都会随着长度而累积：即使模型“知道”正确答案，它仍然可能会在每个令牌上产生非零损失，因此较长的答案有更多机会累积负对数概率。根据经验，如本文所示，总对数似然通常随完成长度近似线性减小，从而产生系统A key property of these benchmarks is that the total log-likelihood of a completion is typically given by the sum of its token-wise log-probabilities. Because this score is additive over tokens, any per-token uncertainty or modeling error is accumulated with length: even when the model “knows” the correct answer, it is likely to still incur a non-zero loss on each token, so longer answers have more opportunities to accumulate negative log-probability. Empirically, as will be shown in this paper, the total log-likelihoods often decrease approximately linearly with completion length, inducing a systematic长度偏差length bias倾向于身材矮小的候选人。towards shorter candidates.

减轻这种偏差的一种广泛使用的启发式方法是将总对数似然除以完成的长度，例如除以标记或字节的数量A widely used heuristic to mitigate this bias is to normalize the total log-likelihood by the length of the completion, for example by dividing by the number of tokens or bytes(Brown et al., 2020; Gao et al., 2024)。长度归一化分数可以解释为平均每个标记交叉熵（的负数），并且与困惑度单调相关。然而，在实践中，我们发现这种标准化经常. Length-normalized scores can be interpreted as (the negative of) an average per-token cross-entropy and are monotonically related to perplexity. In practice, however, we find that such normalization frequently过度纠正over-corrects：分数变得偏向相反的方向，有利于更长的完成时间。此外，这些影响在基准或任务格式中并不统一，引发了两个问题：（I）何时适合使用非标准化精度与长度标准化精度，以及（II）是否有一种在任务中表现稳健的替代方案？: scores become biased in the opposite direction, favoring longer completions instead. Moreover, these effects are not uniform across benchmarks or task formats, raising two questions: (I) when is it appropriate to use unnormalized versus length-normalized accuracy, and (II) is there an alternative that behaves robustly across tasks?

除了长度归一化之外，还提出了几种替代评分规则来调整无条件先验或重新调整分数，最显着的是逐点互信息（PMI）和非对称归一化 PMI（ANPMI）；我们在第 1 节中回顾了这些指标及其局限性作为基线Beyond length normalization, several alternative scoring rules have been proposed that adjust for unconditional priors or rescale scores, most notably pointwise mutual information (PMI) and asymmetric normalized PMI (ANPMI); we review these metrics and their limitations as baselines in Section2.2.

A.4 基准详情Benchmark Details

弧。ARC.

AI2 推理挑战赛 (ARC) 是一个多项选择题回答数据集，包含来自美国小学考试的 7,787 道科学问题，分为“简单”子集和更难的“挑战”子集。它被设计为需要非平凡的科学和常识推理，而不是简单的模式匹配或检索。The AI2 Reasoning Challenge (ARC) is a multiple-choice question answering dataset of 7,787 science questions from U.S. grade-school exams, split into an “Easy” and a more difficult “Challenge” subset. It is designed to require non-trivial scientific and commonsense reasoning rather than simple pattern matching or retrieval.

海拉斯瓦格。HellaSwag.

HellaSwag 是一个对抗性常识基准，其中模型必须从四个选项中选择最合理的简短叙述或教学背景的延续。大约 70,000 个示例是根据视频字幕和操作文章构建的，其令人分心的结局经过精心设计，看起来很流畅，但在语义上对人类来说却难以置信。HellaSwag is an adversarial commonsense benchmark in which models must choose the most plausible continuation of a short narrative or instructional context from four options. The roughly 70,000 examples are built from video captions and how-to articles, with distractor endings crafted to look fluent yet be semantically implausible to humans.

MMLU。MMLU.

大规模多任务语言理解 (MMLU) 基准测试汇总了 57 个学术科目的 15,908 个四向多项选择题，涵盖人文、社会和自然科学以及专业领域。它被广泛用作语言模型中广泛的世界知识和特定领域推理的代理。The Massive Multitask Language Understanding (MMLU) benchmark aggregates 15,908 four-way multiple-choice questions across 57 academic subjects, spanning humanities, social and natural sciences, and professional domains. It is widely used as a proxy for broad world knowledge and domain-specific reasoning in language models.

MMLU（全文答案）。MMLU (full-text answer).

在我们的In ourMMLU 全文MMLU Full-Text在变体中，基本问题和答案选项不变，但要求模型输出完整的文本答案（例如“巴黎”）而不是选项标签（“C”）。准确性是通过生成的答案与相应的黄金选项字符串之间的精确匹配比较来计算的。variant, the underlying questions and answer options are unchanged, but the model is asked to output the full textual answer (e.g., “Paris”) rather than the option label (“C”). Accuracy is computed by exact-match comparison between the generated answer and the corresponding gold option string.

MMLU（完型填空）。MMLU (cloze).

在In theMMLU完型填空MMLU cloze变体中，多项选择选项从提示中删除，问题被表述为简答题或填空题。模型必须直接产生答案；在评估时，我们将完成映射回原始 MMLU 答案键（在次要标准化之后）以确定正确性。variant, the multiple-choice options are removed from the prompt and the problem is phrased as a short-answer or fill-in-the-blank question. The model must produce the answer directly; at evaluation time we map the completion back onto the original MMLU answer key (after minor normalization) to determine correctness.

科学。SciQ.

SciQ 是一个四选项多项选择基准测试，包含约 14,000 个以小学和初中考试为模型的普通科学问题。问题通常与相关的源段落一起出现，并且数据集通常用作介于简单事实回忆和 ARC 等要求更高的基准之间的中等难度的科学 QA 任务。SciQ is a four-option multiple-choice benchmark of about 14,000 general science questions modeled on elementary and middle-school exams. Questions often come with associated source passages, and the dataset is commonly used as a mid-difficulty science QA task between simple fact recall and more demanding benchmarks such as ARC.

威诺格兰德。WinoGrande.

WinoGrande 是一个大型代词解析基准，灵感来自 Winograd Schema Challenge。每个例子都是一个短句，带有一个歧义代词和两个候选先行词；模型必须决定哪个选项使句子连贯，探索细粒度的常识推理，同时控制数据集伪影。WinoGrande is a large-scale pronoun resolution benchmark inspired by the Winograd Schema Challenge. Each example is a short sentence with an ambiguous pronoun and two candidate antecedents; the model must decide which option makes the sentence coherent, probing fine-grained commonsense reasoning while controlling for dataset artifacts.
