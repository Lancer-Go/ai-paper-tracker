摘要Abstract

了解学习者的认知和情感内部状态对于适应性教育系统和有效教学至关重要。尽管许多研究已经检验了可观察的非语言线索与内部状态之间的关系，但没有全面的框架来通过适当的证据校准来系统地组织这些见解。本文提出了非语言句法框架，这是一个用于将行为线索映射到学习者状态的循证系统，该框架建立在对 908 项实证研究的系统文献综述的基础上，这些研究产生了 17,043 个线索状态映射（Turaev 等人，2026）。Understanding learners’ internal states, both cognitive and affective, is essential for adaptive educational systems and effective teaching. Although much research has examined relationships between observable nonverbal cues and internal states, there is no comprehensive framework to systematically organize these insights with proper evidence calibration. This paper presents the Nonverbal Syntax Framework, an evidence-based system for mapping behavioral cues to learner states, built on a systematic literature review of 908 empirical studies that yielded 17,043 cue-state mappings (Turaev et al., 2026).

1. 简介1. INTRODUCTION

有效的教学和学习关键取决于识别和响应学习者内部状态的能力。当学生遇到困惑时，及时干预可以将挣扎的时刻转化为富有成效的学习（D’Mello & Graesser，2012）。相反，未被发现的无聊或沮丧可能会导致脱离和学习失败（Baker et al., 2010）。随着教育技术变得越来越复杂，对能够自动检测和响应学习者状态的系统的需求大幅增长（Calvo & D’Mello，2010）。Effective teaching and learning depend critically on the ability to recognize and respond to learners’ internal states. When students experience confusion, timely intervention can transform a moment of struggle into productive learning (D’Mello & Graesser, 2012). Conversely, undetected boredom or frustration can lead to disengagement and learning failure (Baker et al., 2010). As educational technologies become increasingly sophisticated, the demand for systems capable of automatically detecting and responding to learner states has grown substantially (Calvo & D’Mello, 2010).

学习者的内部状态包括认知维度（例如注意力、参与度和认知负荷）和情感维度（包括困惑、沮丧、无聊和快乐）（Pekrun，2006）。这些状态不仅是学习的附带条件，而且也是学习的附带条件。它们在塑造学习成果方面发挥着重要作用。研究表明，情感状态会影响记忆巩固（Tyng et al., 2017）、解决问题的策略（Isen, 2001）以及面对困难时的坚持（Dweck, 2006）。注意力和参与度等认知状态直接决定哪些信息被处理和保留（Fredricks et al., 2004）。Learner internal states encompass both cognitive dimensions, such as attention, engagement, and cognitive load, and affective dimensions, including confusion, frustration, boredom, and delight (Pekrun, 2006). These states are not merely incidental to learning; they play a fundamental role in shaping learning outcomes. Research has demonstrated that affective states influence memory consolidation (Tyng et al., 2017), problem-solving strategies (Isen, 2001), and persistence in the face of difficulty (Dweck, 2006). Cognitive states such as attention and engagement directly determine which information is processed and retained (Fredricks et al., 2004).

我们自然地通过观察他人的行为线索来推断他人的内部状态。面部表情传达情绪效价和唤醒程度（Ekman，1992）；眼球运动揭示注意力分配和认知处理（Just & Carpenter，1980）；身体姿势表明参与或退出（Harrigan，2005）；声音特征表明压力、自信或不确定性（Scherer，2003）。这种“解读”非语言行为的自然能力激发了数十年旨在通过计算检测情感和认知状态的研究（Picard，1997）。We naturally infer others’ internal states through their observable behavioral cues. Facial expressions convey emotional valence and arousal (Ekman, 1992); eye movements reveal attention allocation and cognitive processing (Just & Carpenter, 1980); body posture signals engagement or withdrawal (Harrigan, 2005); and vocal characteristics indicate stress, confidence, or uncertainty (Scherer, 2003). This natural capacity for “reading” nonverbal behavior has inspired decades of research aimed at computational detection of affective and cognitive states (Picard, 1997).

经济实惠的传感器和计算机视觉技术的进步使得在教育环境中捕获多模式行为数据变得越来越可行（Woolf 等人，2009）。深度学习的最新进展显着提高了自动情感识别的准确性，在基准数据集上实现了接近人类的性能（Li & Deng，2022）。这种技术能力推动了将可观察线索映射到底层状态的广泛研究，特别是在在线和远程学习环境中，这种研究在 COVID-19 大流行后显着扩展（Dewan 等人，2019 年；Sharma 等人，2020 年）。大流行后向混合教学的转变以及生成式人工智能工具快速集成到学习环境中Advances in affordable sensors and computer vision technologies have made it increasingly feasible to capture multimodal behavioral data in educational settings (Woolf et al., 2009). Recent progress in deep learning has significantly improved the accuracy of automatic affect recognition, achieving near-human performance on benchmark datasets (Li & Deng, 2022). This technological capability has driven extensive research on mapping observable cues to underlying states, particularly in online and remote learning contexts, which expanded significantly following the COVID-19 pandemic (Dewan et al., 2019; Sharma et al., 2020). The post-pandemic shift toward hybrid instruction and the rapid integration of generative AI tools into learning environments have

进一步重塑了必须观察学习者状态的环境，提出了早期基于课堂的研究中建立的提示状态关系在当代数字环境中是否仍然有效的问题。有两个考虑因素可以缓解这种担忧。首先，非语言交流的基础渠道，包括面部动作、凝视、姿势和韵律，植根于人类生理和情感表达，因此在这种综合所涵盖的时期内不太可能发生重大变化。其次，经验证据基础本身已明显转向近期研究，基于当前框架的 908 项研究中有 56.8% 是在 2020 年至 2025 年间发表的，确保当代学习环境得到充分体现。与此同时，某些与情境相关的线索，例如面向屏幕的姿势或在线学习特有的互动日志行为，直到最近几年才出现或受到重视，而传统的课堂指标，例如举手，在某些情况下可能正在减少；这些时间变化在第 4 节中作为限制重新审视。further reshaped the contexts in which learner states must be observed, raising the question of whether cue-state relationships established in earlier classroom-based studies remain valid in contemporary digital settings. Two considerations mitigate this concern. First, the channels underlying nonverbal communication, including facial action, gaze, posture, and prosody, are anchored in human physiology and emotional expression and are therefore unlikely to have changed materially over the period covered by this synthesis. Second, the empirical evidence base itself has shifted markedly toward recent research, with 56.8% of the 908 studies underlying the present framework published between 2020 and 2025, ensuring that contemporary learning contexts are well represented. At the same time, certain context-bound cues, such as screenoriented postures or interaction-log behaviors characteristic of online learning, have emerged or gained prominence only in recent years, while traditional classroom indicators such as hand-raising may be diminishing in some settings; these temporal shifts are revisited as a limitation in Section 4.

尽管进行了数十年的研究，该领域仍缺乏理解提示状态关系的统一框架。三个关键问题阻碍了研究成果转化为实际应用：Despite decades of research, the field lacks a unified framework for understanding cue-state relationships. Three critical problems impede the translation of research findings into practical application:

首先，术语碎片化。First, terminological fragmentation.同一行为现象在不同的​​研究中可能有不同的描述：“前倾”、“向屏幕倾斜”和“身体朝向任务”可能都指的是类似的姿势配置。同样，内部状态也受到标签不一致的影响：“全神贯注”、“心流”、“深度参与”和“全神贯注”通常描述重叠的结构。这种不一致使得很难综合研究结果或建立累积知识（Bosch et al., 2015）。The same behavioral phenomenon may be described differently across studies: “forward lean,” “leaning toward the screen,” and “body orientation toward the task” may all refer to similar postural configurations. Similarly, internal states suffer from inconsistent labeling: “engaged concentration,” “flow,” “deep engagement,” and “absorbed attention” often describe overlapping constructs. This inconsistency makes it difficult to synthesize findings across studies or build cumulative knowledge (Bosch et al., 2015).

第二，证据异质性。Second, evidence heterogeneity.线索和状态之间的关系在其经验支持方面差异很大。一些关联，例如皱眉和困惑之间的联系，已在采用不同方法的众多独立研究中得到记录（Grafsgaard 等，2013）。其他人依赖于单一观察或小样本研究。如果没有评估证据强度的系统方法，从业者就无法在不同的提示状态映射中校准他们的置信度。Relationships between cues and states vary dramatically in their empirical support. Some associations, such as the link between frowning and confusion, have been documented across numerous independent studies employing diverse methodologies (Grafsgaard et al., 2013). Others rely on single observations or small-sample studies. Without a systematic method for assessing evidence strength, practitioners cannot calibrate their confidence across different cue-state mappings.

第三，表述模糊。Third, state ambiguity.许多非语言线索与多种有时相互矛盾的内部状态相关。皱起眉头可能表示困惑、专注或沮丧（Craig et al., 2004）。减少目光接触可能意味着无聊、认知负担或社交焦虑。线索和状态之间的这种多对多映射给自动检测系统和人类观察者带来了巨大的挑战。Many nonverbal cues are associated with multiple, sometimes contradictory, internal states. Furrowed brows may indicate confusion, concentration, or frustration (Craig et al., 2004). Reduced eye contact might signal boredom, cognitive load, or social anxiety. This many-to-many mapping between cues and states poses substantial challenges for both automatic detection systems and human observers.

此前应对这些挑战的努力采取了多种形式。情绪识别系统通常关注源自 Ekman 工作的六种基本情绪（Ekman & Friesen，1971），但这些类别很难捕捉与学习相关的状态，如困惑或参与（D’Mello & Graesser，2012）。参与度检测系统通常针对特定上下文狭隘地定义状态，从而限制了普遍性（Whitehill et al., 2014）。深度学习方法在基准数据集上取得了令人印象深刻的准确性，但通常充当“黑匣子”，无法深入了解哪些行为特征驱动预测（Zeng 等人，2009），并且经常无法跨上下文、人群或记录条件进行泛化（Kołakowska 等人，2017）。Previous efforts to address these challenges have taken several forms. Emotion recognition systems typically focus on the six basic emotions derived from Ekman’s work (Ekman & Friesen, 1971), but these categories poorly capture learning-relevant states like confusion or engagement (D’Mello & Graesser, 2012). Engagement detection systems often define states narrowly for specific contexts, limiting generalizability (Whitehill et al., 2014). Deep learning approaches have achieved impressive accuracy on benchmark datasets but often function as “black boxes” that provide little insight into which behavioral features drive predictions (Zeng et al., 2009), and frequently fail to generalize across contexts, populations, or recording conditions (Kołakowska et al., 2017).

术语The term非语言语法Nonverbal Syntax反映了我们对行为线索作为语法元素的概念化：正如单词根据句法规则组合以传达含义一样，非语言线索以模式化方式组合以表示内部状态。单个提示就像单个单词一样，携带有限的信息并允许多种解释。然而，当多个线索同时出现在行为通道、面部表情与身体姿势、注视模式和声音特征相结合时，它们会形成“行为句子”，限制解释并实现更可靠的状态推断。了解这种“语法”使从业者和自动化系统能够超越单线索检测，转向基于模式的识别。reflects our conceptualization of behavioral cues as elements of a grammar: just as words combine according to syntactic rules to convey meaning, nonverbal cues combine in patterned ways to signal internal states. A single cue, like a single word, carries limited information and admits multiple interpretations. However, when multiple cues co-occur across behavioral channels, facial expression combined with body posture, gaze pattern, and vocal characteristics, they form “behavioral sentences” that constrain interpretation and enable more reliable state inference. Understanding this “syntax” enables practitioners and automated systems alike to move beyond single-cue detection toward pattern-based recognition.

2.2 标准化程序2.2 Normalization Procedures

综合这些文献的研究需要解决相当大的术语变异性。根据纪律惯例和测量方法，相同的行为可以被描述为“皱眉”、“低眉”、“皱眉肌激活”或“AU4”。类似地，内部状态表现出标签不一致：“参与”、“参与”、“参与注意力”、“行为参与”、“认知参与”和“任务参与”可能指的是相关或相同的结构。如果没有标准化，这些变异将被视为不同的现象，从而破坏证据基础并模糊复制模式。Synthesizing research across this literature required addressing considerable terminological variability. 2.3 双证据评估体系2.3 Dual-Evidence Assessment System

一个关键的方法论贡献是双证据评估系统，它解决了现有证据合成的基本限制：成分级和关系级证据的合并。本小节的其余部分将通过五个步骤来阐述这个想法。我们首先阐明激发双重证据方法的核心问题，然后介绍两个互补层（成分证据和关系证据）以及用于组合它们的规则，最后检查两个指标之间的经验分歧及其对如何在框架中解释可操作关系的影响。A critical methodological contribution is the dual-evidence assessment system, which addresses a fundamental limitation in existing evidence synthesis: the conflation of component-level and relationship-level evidence. The remainder of this subsection unpacks this idea in five steps. We first articulate the core problem that motivates the dual-evidence approach, then introduce the two complementary tiers (Component Evidence and Relationship Evidence) along with the rules used to combine them, and finally examine the empirical divergence between the two metrics and its implications for how actionable relationships are interpreted in the framework.

问题：组件与关系证据。The Problem: Component vs. Relationship Evidence.传统的置信度评估文献中各个组成部分（线索和状态）的研究程度。然而，高成分级评级必然意味着良好复制关系的假设并不总是有道理的：分别被单独研究多次的提示和状态可能仅在一篇论文中链接在一起。因此，将组件级证据聚合成单个置信度分数可能会夸大任何特定提示状态组合的复制强度，特别是当目标是识别足够强大的关系以指导实践或为自动检测系统提供种子时。Traditional confidence ratings assess how well-studied the individual components (cues and states) are across the literature. However, the assumption that a high component-level rating necessarily implies a well-replicated relationship is not always warranted: a cue and a state that have each been studied many times in isolation may have been linked together in only a single paper. Aggregating component-level evidence into a single confidence score therefore risks overstating the replication strength of any specific cue-state combination, particularly when the goal is to identify relationships robust enough to inform practice or to seed automated detection systems.

组件证据层。Component Evidence Tiers.成分证据层表明了所有关联中各个线索和状态所受到的总体研究关注。选择层级阈值是为了反映证据合成中常用的截止点：单个观察标记了探索性前沿（第5层），两项研究表明需要进一步验证的新兴支持（第4层），三到四项研究提供了合理的经验支持（第3层），五项或更多研究将构建移至适度或广泛重复的范围（第2层和第1层）。由于州和线索的典型论文数量不同，因此第 1 层的截止值针对两个维度分别进行校准：州≥20 篇论文，线索≥10 篇论文。表 4 和表 5 展示了最终的层次分布，揭示了严重倾斜的证据基础，其中少数核心结构占据了大部分研究注意力，而状态和线索的长尾仅在一项研究中得到了检验。Component Evidence Tiers indicate the overall research attention received by individual cues and states across all their associations. 2.4 可操作性分类2.4 Actionability Classification

为了弥合研究成果和实际应用之间的差距，我们根据线索对线索进行了分类To bridge the gap between research findings and practical application, we classified cues by their可操作性actionability，他们指定从业者可以直接检测和记录的可观察行为的程度。, the degree to which they specify observable behaviors that practitioners can directly detect and record.

可操作性在概念上与证据不同：线索可能在文献中被广泛记录，但过于笼统而无法直接观察到，而研究较少的线索可能在课堂或基于屏幕的环境中完全可操作。文献中经常报道诸如“面部表情”或“身体动作”之类的通用类别，但对于观察者实际应该寻找什么提供了很少的指导。为了明确这种区别，我们将每个线索分为四个级别的可操作性范围，反映了行为描述的特异性和直接观察的可行性。高度可操作的线索描述了特定的、可立即观察到的行为，例如“抓头”或“打哈欠”；适度可操作的线索描述了观察者在给定简短指导的情况下可以识别的相当具体的行为，例如“向前倾”或“坐立不安”；弱可操作线索描述了广泛的行为类别，需要进一步规范才能实施；不可操作的线索要么是通用描述符，如“面部表情”，要么是仪器信号，如“脑电图”，如果没有专门的设备就无法观察到。表 10 总结了四个级别的定义、示例及其预期实际用途。Actionability is conceptually distinct from evidence: a cue may be extensively documented in the literature yet too generic to be directly observable, while a less-studied cue may be entirely actionable in classroom or screen-based contexts. Generic categories such as “facial expressions” or “body movement” are reported frequently in the literature but provide little guidance on what an observer should actually look for. To make this distinction explicit, we classified each cue along a four-level actionability spectrum that reflects the specificity of the behavioral description and the feasibility of direct observation. Highly actionable cues describe a specific, immediately observable behavior such as “scratching head” or “yawning”; moderately actionable cues describe a reasonably specific behavior that an observer can recognize given brief guidelines, such as “leaning forward” or “fidgeting”; weakly actionable cues describe broad behavioral categories that require further specification before they can be operationalized; and non-actionable cues are either generic descriptors such as “facial expressions” or instrumental signals such as “EEG” that cannot be observed without specialized equipment. 3.5.3 诊断应用3.5.3 Diagnostic Application

当从业者观察到与多种状态一致的行为时，区分性线索可以解决歧义。两个说明性场景展示了其在实践中的工作原理。When a practitioner observes behaviors consistent with multiple states, discriminative cues resolve ambiguity. Two illustrative scenarios show how this works in practice.

Both confusion and frustration include these cues. Discriminative analysis suggests: if scratching head is also present, the most likely state is confusion; if sighing is present, the most likely state is frustration; if banging on the keyboard is present, the most likely state is also frustration; and if “Why didn’t it work?” is verbalized, the most likely state is confusion.

场景2。Scenario 2.一名学生的活动减少，偶尔打哈欠。无聊和疲劳都包含这些暗示。判别分析表明：如果同时存在无精打采和下巴托在手掌上的情况，则最可能的状态是无聊；如果出现揉眼和伸展眼睛的情况，最可能的状态是疲劳。A student shows reduced activity and occasional yawning. Both boredom and fatigue include these cues. Discriminative analysis suggests: if slouching and resting chin on palm are also present, the most likely state is boredom; if eye rubbing and stretching are present, the most likely state is fatigue.

3.6 框架集成3.6 Framework Integration

这四个层次共同支持推理。用户观察提示并查询 1 级相关状态；识别候选状态并查询 2 级以获取其他预期线索；将观察到的模式与 3 级配置文件进行比较；并通过在第 4 级查询候选者之间的区分线索来解决任何剩余的歧义。The four levels work together to support inference. The user observes a cue and queries Level 1 for associated states; identifies candidate states and queries Level 2 for additional expected cues; compares the observed pattern against Level 3 profiles; and resolves any remaining ambiguity by querying Level 4 for discriminative cues between candidates.

4.1.1 术语整合4.1.1 Terminological Consolidation

对于研究人员来说，该框架可以识别知识差距（未研究的状态、渠道和关系），提供标准化术语，揭示只有 4.5% 的记录关系具有足够的复制性 (R1–R4) 以便可靠地应用，并通过结构化证据组织支持理论测试。For researchers, the framework identifies knowledge gaps (understudied states, channels, and relationships), provides standardized terminology, reveals that only 4.5% of documented relationships have sufficient replication (R1–R4) for confident application, and supports theory testing through structured evidence organization.

对于从业者（教育工作者、培训师、临床医生）来说，该框架为观察评估提供了经过证据校准的指导。关键学习状态的详细行为签名准确指定了要监控的可观察行为，并具有明确的论文计数和证据层。言语指示（“我很困惑”、“这很无聊”）在出现时提供明确的状态识别。For practitioners (educators, trainers, clinicians), the framework offers evidence-calibrated guidance for observational assessment. The detailed behavioral signatures for key learning states specify exactly which observable behaviors to monitor, with explicit paper counts and evidence tiers. 对于开发情感感知系统的技术人员来说，该框架提供了经过验证的状态检测功能（优先考虑 R1-R3 关系证据）、基于关系层而不是单独的组件层的置信度校准指南、用于解决分类歧义的判别性线索，以及关于哪些线索需要仪器检测以及哪些线索需要通过标准摄像头或麦克风检测到的明确指导。For technologists developing affect-aware systems, the framework provides validated features for state detection (prioritizing R1–R3 relationship evidence), confidence calibration guidelines based on relationship tiers rather than component tiers alone, discriminative cues for resolving classification ambiguities, and clear guidance on which cues require instrumentation versus those detectable through standard cameras or microphones.

该框架综合了来自异质性研究的相关证据，但无法确定因果关系。个体差异、文化因素和背景影响会产生人口水平模式中未捕获的变异。判别线索代表了需要实证验证的分析得出的假设。对限制的详细讨论出现在第 4.4 节和 Turaev 等人中。 （2026）。The framework synthesizes correlational evidence from heterogeneous studies and cannot establish causation. Individual differences, cultural factors, and contextual influences produce variation not captured in population-level patterns. The discriminative cues represent analytically derived hypotheses that require empirical validation. A detailed discussion of limitations appears in Section 4.4 and in Turaev et al. (2026).

未来工作的优先方向包括直接比较研究，测试易混淆状态之间的区分线索；检查跨通道模式共现的多模态传感研究；通过预测准确性研究验证关系证据等级系统；扩展到时间动态和强度等级；文化验证研究；以及开发用于观察和自动检测的实用工具。Priority directions for future work include direct comparison studies testing discriminative cues between confusable states; multimodal sensing studies examining cross-channel pattern cooccurrence; validation of the relationship evidence tier system through predictive accuracy studies; extension to temporal dynamics and intensity gradations; cultural validation studies; and the development of practical tools for observation and automated detection.

非语言语法框架代表着使数十年的情感计算和教育研究变得更容易理解和更可操作的一步。通过组织我们共同了解的内部状态如何在行为上和批判性地表现出来，通过区分良好复制的关系和初步观察，该框架支持更有原则的研究、更自信的实践和更明智的技术开发。The Nonverbal Syntax Framework represents a step toward making decades of affective computing and educational research more accessible and actionable. By organizing what we collectively know about how internal states manifest behaviorally, and critically, by distinguishing between well-replicated relationships and preliminary observations, the framework supports more principled research, more confident practice, and more informed technology development.
