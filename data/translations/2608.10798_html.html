当应用于历史摄影时，这个问题变得更加严重。早期的正色胶片对红色波长不敏感，产生的灰度图像的亮度分布与人类视觉感知不相符。现代着色模型在全色数据上进行训练并以保留的亮度为条件，继承了结构域不匹配。他们被迫将色度分配给亮度值，而这些值在感知上从来不准确，从而导致红色被抑制。从这个意义上说，当前的系统在设计上就失败了：当输入灰度图像比应有的暗时，模型无法使这些区域变亮（图 3）。The issue becomes more severe when applied to historical photography. Early orthochromatic film stock was insensitive to red wavelengths, producing grayscale images whose luminance distribution does not correspond to human visual perception. Modern colorization models, trained on panchromatic data and conditioned on preserved luminance, inherit a structural domain mismatch. They are forced to assign chroma to brightness values that were never perceptually accurate, leading to suppressed reds. In this sense, current systems fail by design: when the input grayscale image is darker than it should be, the model is incapable of brightening those regions (Figure 3).

大多数采用预训练文本到图像扩散模型的当代方法都是通过改变架构以将其调节为灰度[2, 5,27–29,42]。虽然有些有效，但这些方法继承了一个根本性的不匹配：文本到图像模型没有针对图像条件任务进行预训练。因此，需要大量的建筑工程将灰度结构“强制”到最初设计用于仅从文本合成的生成管道中。最近在图像编辑上进行本地训练的基础模型为着色提供了一种优越的替代方案，无需架构黑客攻击[24,25,37]。Most contemporary approaches that adapt pretrained text-to-image diffusion models do so by changing the architecture to condition them on grayscale [2, 5,27–29,42]. While somewhat effective, these approaches inherit a fundamental mismatch: text-to-image models are not pretrained for image-conditioned tasks. As a result, significant architectural engineering is required to “force” grayscale structure into a generative pipeline originally designed for synthesis from text alone. Recent foundation models natively trained on image editing provide a superior alternative for colorization without architectural hacking [24,25,37].

我们发现，当输入灰度图像不能保留人类感知的亮度时，现有的着色方法会大幅退化，如在正色图像中。相比之下，我们的方法在这种转变下更加稳健，同时在标准灰度输入上保持竞争力。这一趋势在 ImageNet、COCO 和多实例基准（表 2、1 和 3）上得到了定量的体现，在定性上也得到了反映，我们的输出呈现出更少的局部伪影和更一致的颜色分配（图 3 和 2）。我们还对取自 [31] 的真实历史正色相机图像进行评估（图 4）。更多示例在补充中。We find that existing colorization methods degrade substantially when the input grayscale image does not preserve human-perceived luminance, as in orthochromatic imagery. In contrast, our method is substantially more robust under this shift while remaining competitive on standard grayscale inputs. This trend is reflected both quantitatively across ImageNet, COCO, and the Multi-Instance benchmark (Tables 2, 1, and 3) and qualitatively, where our outputs exhibit fewer local artifacts and more consistent color assignments (Figures 3 and 2). 5 结论5 Conclusion

我们通过亮度不匹配的角度重新审视图像着色。大多数现有系统在预测色度的同时保留输入衍生的亮度通道，这在灰度强度对齐时效果很好，但在域移位（例如历史正色图像）下可能会崩溃。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一种与亮度无关的着色框架，该框架可以使用基础图像编辑模型直接预测完整的 RGB 输出，而不是依赖于亮度交换。We revisited image colorization through the lens of luminance mismatch. Most existing systems predict chroma while preserving an input-derived luminance channel, which works well when grayscale intensities align but can break under domain shifts such as historical orthochromatic imagery. To address this, we proposed a luminance-agnostic colorization framework that predicts full RGB outputs directly using a foundation image-editing model, rather than relying on luminance swapping.

我们方法的核心是混合灰度目标，它在标准全色和模拟正色条件下训练模型，使其能够在灰度形成偏离人类感知的亮度时重新解释亮度。在 COCO、ImageNet 和多实例基准测试中，我们发现，虽然我们的方法在标准灰度输入上仍然具有竞争力，但它在正色输入上更加稳健，而之前的方法在正色输入上性能退化更严重。定性比较进一步表明输出更清晰，局部色彩伪影更少。Central to our approach is a mixed grayscale objective that trains the model under both standard panchromatic and simulated orthochromatic conditioning, allowing it to reinterpret brightness when the grayscale formation deviates from human-perceived luminance. Across COCO, ImageNet, and a multi-instance benchmark, we find that while our method remains competitive on standard grayscale inputs, it is substantially more robust on orthochromatic inputs, where prior methods degrade more strongly. Qualitative comparisons further indicate cleaner outputs with fewer local color artifacts.
