6. 结论6. Conclusion

本文将 LLM 代理的技能选择视为硬代币预算下的正则化子模块最大化：子模块能力优势使互补性和冗余性变得明确，并且线性惩罚对每个注入的代币收费。 我们的多项式时间选择规则 BPS 具有双标准This paper casts skill selection for LLM agents as regularized submodular maximization under a hard token budget: a submodular capability benefit makes complementarity and redundancy explicit, and a linear penalty charges every injected token. Our polynomial-time selection rule BPS carries a bicriteria(1−1/e, 1)(1-1/e,\,1)保证，通过预算调整插值和严格的效益系数证明。 在具有实际执行的污染控制基准上，拟合的目标是最准确的价值模型，并且 BPS 在衡量成功方面优于每个部署的技能选择器，同时注入的代币比任何已发布的系统都少。 未来的工作包括流式查询的在线选择和超出线性每个代币收费的降级模型。guarantee, proved via budget-aligned interpolation and tight in the benefit coefficient. On a contamination-controlled benchmark with real executions, the fitted objective was the most accurate value model, and BPS outperformed every deployed skill selector in measured success while injecting fewer tokens than any released system. Future work includes online selection over streaming queries and degradation models beyond a linear per-token charge.
