Ⅱ-AII-A 变化检测：从代数到深度学习Change Detection: From Algebraic to Deep Learning

变化检测是遥感的一项基本任务，旨在通过在不同时间观察现象来识别现象状态的明显差异。从历史上看，该领域主要由应用于均匀光学图像的代数方法主导，例如图像差分、比率和变化矢量分析Change detection constitutes a fundamental task in remote sensing, aiming to identify distinct differences in the state of a phenomenon by observing it at different times. Historically, this field was dominated by algebraic methods applied to homogeneous optical imagery, such as image differencing, ratioing, and Change Vector Analysis[32, 5]。随着深度学习的快速发展，范式已经转向全卷积连体网络. With the rapid advancement of deep learning, the paradigm has shifted towards fully convolutional Siamese networks[7, 58]。最近，基于 Transformer 的架构已成为标准，利用自注意力机制来模拟远程时空依赖性，从而提高针对季节变化和传感器噪声的鲁棒性. Recently, Transformer-based architectures have become the standard, utilizing self-attention mechanisms to model long-range spatio-temporal dependencies, thereby improving robustness against seasonal variations and sensor noise[10, 3].

然而，在快速灾难响应的背景下，同质数据可用性的假设常常被违反。光学传感器However, in the context of rapid disaster response, the assumption of homogeneous data availability is often violated. Optical sensors[38, 57, 37]云层或烟雾经常导致失效。因此，异构变化检测，特别是事件前光学图像与事件后 SAR 数据的融合，已成为一个关键方向。 SAR 提供全天候成像功能，但与光学数据相比，引入了显着的几何和辐射失真are frequently rendered ineffective by cloud cover or smoke. Consequently, heterogeneous change detection, particularly the fusion of pre-event optical imagery with post-event SAR data, has emerged as a critical direction. SAR offers all weather imaging capabilities but introduces significant geometric and radiometric distortions compared to optical data[6]。为了弥补这一领域差距，最近的方法采用了循环一致的对抗网络来进行图像到图像的转换. To bridge this domain gap, recent methodologies have employed cycle-consistent adversarial networks for image-to-image translation[44, 29]或图形神经网络以跨模态调整结构特征or graph neural networks to align structural features across modalities[49, 12]。还开发了专注于特征空间对齐的技术，将这些不同的模式关联起来，以识别自然灾害和人为冲突中的损害模式. Techniques focusing on feature space alignment have also been developed to correlate these distinct modalities for identifying damage patterns in both natural disasters and anthropogenic conflicts[1, 55]。 尽管取得了这些进步，现有的变化检测方法主要将任务表述为语义分割问题. Despite these advancements, existing change detection methods predominantly formulate the task as a semantic segmentation problem[60, 50]。最终输出通常是二进制或分类掩码。虽然定位精确，但这些掩码缺乏高级语义解释性，并且无法以有助于战略决策的结构化方式明确描述损害的性质或量化严重程度。. The final output is typically a binary or categorical mask. While precise in localization, these masks lack high-level semantic interpretability and cannot explicitly describe the nature of damage or quantify severity in a structured manner useful for strategic decision making.

Ⅱ-BII-B 改变解释：从面具到语言Change Interpretation: From Mask to Language

为了解决二值掩模的语义限制，该领域逐渐扩展到遥感图像字幕To address the semantic limitations of binary masks, the field has gradually expanded towards remote sensing image captioning[40]。早期的作品利用编码器-解码器架构来生成单时间场景的静态描述。随着双时态分析的出现，研究已经转向变化字幕，其目的是用自然语言描述两个图像之间的差异。 RSICCformer 等开创性作品. Early works utilized encoder-decoder architectures to generate static descriptions of single-temporal scenes. With the advent of bi-temporal analysis, research has shifted towards Change Captioning, which aims to describe differences between two images in natural language. Pioneering works like the RSICCformer[34]和细心的融合网络and attentive fusion networks[14, 46, 48, 47]证明了从双时态对生成句子的可行性。have demonstrated the feasibility of generating sentences from bi-temporal pairs.

然而，这些方法通常充当“图像到文本翻译器”而不是“智能分析师”。它们在很大程度上仅限于同质光学数据，并产生缺乏交互性的固定字幕。当前的模型很难执行基础任务，例如计算损坏结构、估计损坏比率或根据用户请求分析特定子区域。这造成了一个瓶颈，下游专家仍然必须手动解释生成的文本以提取操作指标。However, these approaches typically function as “image to text translators” rather than “intelligent analysts”. They are largely limited to homogeneous optical data and produce fixed captions that lack interactivity. Current models struggle to perform grounded tasks such as counting damaged structures, estimating damage ratios, or analyzing specific subregions upon user request. This creates a bottleneck where downstream experts must still manually interpret the generated text to extract operational metrics.

Ⅱ-CII-C 复杂场景下的多模态学习Multimodal Learning in Complex Scenarios

通用领域多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM) 的出现，例如 LLaVAThe emergence of General-domain Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), such as LLaVA[36]和 MiniGPT-4and MiniGPT-4[61]，彻底改变了视觉推理。通过将视觉编码器与大型语言模型 (LLM) 结合起来，这些系统展示了令人印象深刻的零样本泛化能力。然而，由于鸟瞰视角、小物体尺度和多光谱特征，将它们直接应用于遥感面临着独特的挑战, has revolutionized visual reasoning. By aligning visual encoders with Large Language Models (LLMs), these systems demonstrate impressive zero-shot generalization. However, applying them directly to remote sensing presents unique challenges due to the bird’s-eye view perspective, small object scales, and multispectral characteristics[35, 52, 24].

当前文献中的一个关键差距是对复杂的非自然灾害场景的处理。虽然存在关注自然灾害的数据集，但它们经常忽略冲突地区的独特特征，这些地区的损害是离散的且结构复杂。此外，现有的遥感多模态融合技术通常将SAR视为次要辅助通道，而不是结构信息的主要来源A critical gap in current literature is the handling of complex, non-natural disaster scenarios. While datasets focusing on natural disasters exist, they often neglect the unique signatures of conflict zones, where damage is discrete and structurally complex. Furthermore, existing multimodal fusion techniques in remote sensing often treat SAR as a secondary auxiliary channel rather than a primary source of structural information[25]。我们的工作与此不同，将光学和 SAR 视为同样重要：光学用于事件前语义上下文，SAR 用于事件后结构验证. Our work departs from this by treating optical and SAR as equally critical: optical for pre-event semantic context and SAR for post-event structural verification[41] [56]，从而实现全天候、接地灾害分析的统一框架。, thereby enabling a unified framework for all weather, grounded disaster analysis.

IV-B 两阶段训练策略Two-Stage Training Strategy

在异构遥感数据上训练多模态大语言模型提出了重大的优化挑战，这主要是由于光学和 SAR 模态的独特特征分布以及预训练主干中灾难性遗忘的风险。为了确保稳定的收敛和强大的指令遵循，我们采用渐进式训练范式，如图 1 所示。Training a Multimodal Large Language Model on heterogeneous remote sensing data presents significant optimization challenges, primarily due to the distinct feature distributions of optical and SAR modalities and the risk of catastrophic forgetting in the pre-trained backbone. To ensure stable convergence and robust instruction following, we adopt a progressive training paradigm, as illustrated in Fig.8，它将特征对齐与语义推理分离。, which decouples feature alignment from semantic reasoning.

IV-B1 第一阶段：模态对齐和特征初始化Stage 1: Modality Alignment and Feature Initialization

在初始阶段，主要目标是弥合视觉编码器和法学硕士之间的语义差距，同时初始化变化检测能力。在此阶段，我们保持双流视觉编码器和 LLM Backbone 的大量参数冻结。优化仅限于差分模块和 MLP 投影仪。该策略的动机是需要在给模型增加复杂的推理任务负担之前构建稳定的视觉语言界面。由于差异模块是从头开始初始化的，这种集中训练使其能够学习光学纹理和 SAR 反向散射之间的基本映射相关性，而不会干扰预训练主干中固有的广义知识。实际上，这个阶段迫使连接器层充当翻译器，将原始异构特征差异转换为连贯的语言嵌入。In the initial phase, the primary objective is to bridge the semantic gap between the visual encoders and the LLM while initializing the change detection capability. During this stage, we keep the massive parameters of both the dual stream Vision Encoders and the LLM Backbone frozen. Optimization is restricted exclusively to the Difference Module and the MLP Projector. This strategy is motivated by the need to construct a stable visual language interface before burdening the model with complex reasoning tasks. Since the Difference Module is initialized from scratch, this focused training allows it to learn the fundamental mapping correlations between optical texture and SAR backscatter without perturbing the generalized knowledge inherent in the pre-trained backbones. Effectively, this stage forces the connector layers to act as a translator, converting raw heterogeneous feature disparities into coherent linguistic embeddings.

IV-B2 第 2 阶段：端到端指令调整Stage 2: End-to-End Instruction Tuning

随后，培训过渡到全面的指令调整阶段，旨在掌握灾难报告的具体语法。在此阶段，我们解冻视觉编码器以允许域适应，使 CLIP 模型能够调整到遥感图像的最低点视角。同时，LLM Backbone 使用 LoRA 进行微调。 至关重要的是，这个阶段是由我们的管道生成的结构化多轮对话驱动的。训练目标不仅仅是静态字幕，而是需要分层推理的交互式对话。该模型受到明确监督，可以执行从全局损坏分级（例如“0 级”）到细粒度区域描述的任务。这种严格的监督确保模型学会将其文本输出基于从异构视觉输入中提取的特定定量证据，从而有效地减少幻觉。Subsequently, the training transitions to a full scale instruction tuning phase designed to master the specific syntax of disaster reporting. In this stage, we unfreeze the Vision Encoders to allow for domain adaptation, enabling the CLIP model to adjust to the nadir perspective of remote sensing imagery. Simultaneously, the LLM Backbone is finetuned using LoRA. Crucially, this stage is driven by the structured multi-turn conversations generated by our pipeline. The training targets are not merely static captions but interactive dialogues requiring hierarchical reasoning. The model is explicitly supervised to perform tasks ranging from global damage grading (e.g., “Level 0”) to fine-grained regional descriptions. This rigorous supervision ensures the model learns to ground its textual outputs in the specific quantitative evidence extracted from the heterogeneous visual inputs, effectively minimizing hallucinations.

在本文中，我们介绍了 ChangeQuery，这是一个统一的多模式框架，它通过将范式从低级像素分类转变为高级语义推理来重新定义灾害损失评估。为了解决现有方法的关键局限性，我们构建了 DICQ 数据集。这一大规模基准将事件前的光学语义与事件后的 SAR 结构特征独特地结合起来，涵盖从自然灾害到人为冲突的各种场景。通过采用新颖的自动语义注释管道，我们将原始分割掩码转换为基础的分层指令集，使模型能够在无需人工干预的情况下学习空间推理和精确量化。In this paper, we introduced ChangeQuery, a unified multimodal framework that redefines disaster damage assessment by shifting the paradigm from low level pixel classification to high-level semantic reasoning. Addressing the critical limitations of existing methodologies, we constructed the DICQ dataset. This large scale benchmark uniquely couples pre-event optical semantics with post-event SAR structural features, covering a diverse spectrum of scenarios ranging from natural catastrophes to anthropogenic conflicts. By employing a novel automated semantic annotation pipeline, we transformed raw segmentation masks into grounded, hierarchical instruction sets, enabling the model to learn spatial reasoning and precise quantification without human intervention.

从方法上讲，ChangeQuery 框架结合了变化感知差异模块，并利用渐进的两阶段训练策略来有效地弥合异构模式之间的重大领域差距。大量实验表明，我们的方法在多个指标上实现了 SOTA 性能，显着优于通用视觉语言模型和专业遥感基线。该模型在生成空间精确的逻辑驱动的灾难报告方面表现出卓越的能力，同时成功缓解了灾难偏差和幻觉等常见问题。最终，这项工作缩小了原始遥感数据和可操作决策支持之间的语义差距，为下一代全天候、交互式人道主义援助系统提供了坚实的基础。Methodologically, the ChangeQuery framework incorporates a Change-Aware Difference Module and utilizes a progressive two-stage training strategy to effectively bridge the significant domain gap between heterogeneous modalities. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our approach achieves SOTA performance across multiple metrics, significantly outperforming both generalist vision language models and specialized remote sensing baselines. The model exhibits remarkable capabilities in generating spatially precise logic-driven disaster reports while successfully mitigating common issues such as disaster bias and hallucinations. Ultimately, this work narrows the semantic gap between raw remote sensing data and actionable decision support, providing a robust foundation for the next generation of all weather, interactive humanitarian assistance systems.
