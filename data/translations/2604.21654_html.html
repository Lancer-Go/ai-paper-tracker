摘要Abstract

现有的基于深度网络的全参考图像质量评估（FR-IQA）模型通常通过对参考图像和失真图像的深度特征进行成对比较来工作。在本文中，我们从不同的角度解决这个问题，并提出了一种基于因果推理和解耦表示学习的新颖的 FR-IQA 范式。与典型的基于特征比较的 FR-IQA 模型不同，我们的方法将退化估计制定为以潜在表示干预为指导的因果解开过程。我们首先通过利用参考图像和扭曲图像之间的内容不变性来解耦退化和内容表示。其次，受人类视觉掩蔽效应的启发，我们设计了一个掩蔽模块来模拟图像内容与退化特征之间的因果关系，从而从扭曲图像中提取受内容影响的退化特征。最后，使用监督回归或无标签降维从这些退化特征中预测质量分数。大量实验表明，我们的方法在完全监督、少标签和无标签设置的标准 IQA 基准上实现了极具竞争力的性能。此外，我们还评估了具有稀缺数据的各种非标准自然图像领域的方法，包括水下、射线照相、医学、中子和屏幕内容图像。得益于无需标记 IQA 数据即可执行特定场景训练和预测的能力，与现有的免训练 FR-IQA 模型相比，我们的方法表现出卓越的跨域泛化能力。Existing deep network-based full-reference image quality assessment (FR-IQA) models typically work by performing pairwise comparisons of deep features from the reference and distorted images. In this paper, we approach this problem from a different perspective and propose a novel FR-IQA paradigm based on causal inference and decoupled representation learning. Unlike typical feature comparison-based FR-IQA models, our approach formulates degradation estimation as a causal disentanglement process guided by intervention on latent representations. We first decouple degradation and content representations by exploiting the content invariance between the reference and distorted images. Second, inspired by the human visual masking effect, we design a masking module to model the causal relationship between image content and degradation features, thereby extracting content-influenced degradation features from distorted images. Finally, quality scores are predicted from these degradation features using either supervised regression or label-free dimensionality reduction. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our method achieves highly competitive performance on standard IQA benchmarks across fully supervised, few-label, and label-free settings. Furthermore, we evaluate the approach on diverse non-standard natural image domains with scarce data, including underwater, radiographic, medical, neutron, and screen-content images. Benefiting from its ability to perform scenario-specific training and prediction without labeled IQA data, our method exhibits superior cross-domain generalization compared to existing training-free FR-IQA models.

要实现这种无标签适应性，关键在于建立训练和预测的无监督范式。对于训练阶段，我们的方法建立在失真图像基本上由两个组成部分组成的前提之上：图像内容和图像退化。在 FR-IQA 中，失真图像通常是通过对参考图像应用降级来合成的。这个生成过程不仅验证了这个前提，而且保证了参考图像和失真图像之间的图像内容保持严格相同。通过利用这种图像内容不变性，我们可以以无监督的方式将退化特征与图像内容解耦。然而，仅仅解耦图像退化不足以完全模拟人类主观感知。根据视觉掩蔽效应 (VME)，人类视觉系统 (HVS) 对相同的失真表现出不同的敏感度，具体取决于底层图像内容。例如，噪点在平滑区域（例如晴朗的天空）中非常明显，但在纹理区域（例如茂密的草地）中几乎难以察觉。这种现象揭示了人类对图像劣化的感知受到图像内容和失真之间相互作用的影响。由于图像内容影响退化的可见性，因此必须明确地建模图像内容如何影响退化表示。为此，我们提出了一种基于因果推理和解耦表示学习的方法来提取内容影响的退化特征。在预测阶段，采用监督回归或少样本回归允许模型将这些因果特征明确地映射到主观分数，从而实现与人类视觉感知的更精细的对齐。当 MOS 注释不可用时，如果没有额外的监督，绝对质量预测是无法识别的。因此，我们将零样本质量预测表述为流形学习问题。具体来说，基于上述分析，假设学习到的内容影响的退化特征主要由潜在感知质量变量控制，因此其质量相关变化位于高维特征空间中的低维流形附近。根据这种观点，特征流形的内在几何形状反映了扭曲的相对感知严重程度。因此，我们使用 UMAP 来恢复一维嵌入，从而保留所学习的特征空间的局部邻域结构。生成的坐标用作相对质量描述符，无需依赖 MOS 监督即可实现零样本质量排名和区分。To achieve this label-free adaptability, the key lies in establishing unsupervised paradigms for both training and prediction. For the training phase, our approach is built upon the premise that a distorted image fundamentally comprises two components: image content and image degradation. In FR-IQA, distorted images are typically synthesized by applying degradations to reference images. This generation process not only validates this premise but also guarantees that the image content remains strictly identical between the reference and the distorted images. By exploiting this image content invariance, we can decouple the degradation features from the image content in an unsupervised manner. However, merely decoupling image degradation is insufficient to fully simulate human subjective perception. According to the visual masking effect (VME), the Human Visual System (HVS) exhibits varying sensitivities to the same distortion depending on the underlying image content. For example, noise is highly visible in smooth areas (e.g., a clear sky) but nearly imperceptible in textured ones (e.g., dense grass). This phenomenon reveals that human perception of image degradation is affected by the interaction between image content and distortion. Because image content influences the visibility of degradation, it is imperative to explicitly model how image content influences the degradation representation. To this end, we propose a method based on causal inference and decoupled representation learning to extract content-influenced degradation features. In the prediction phase, employing supervised or few-shot regression allows the model to explicitly map these causal features to subjective scores, thereby achieving a finer alignment with human visual perception. When MOS annotations are unavailable, absolute quality prediction is not identifiable without additional supervision. We therefore formulate zero-shot quality prediction as a manifold learning problem. Specifically, based on the analysis above, the learned content-influenced degradation feature is assumed to be primarily governed by a latent perceptual quality variable, so that its quality-relevant variation lies near a low-dimensional manifold in the high-dimensional feature space. Under this view, the intrinsic geometry of the feature manifold reflects the relative perceptual severity of distortions. We thus employ UMAP to recover a one-dimensional embedding that preserves the local neighborhood structure of the learned feature space. The resulting coordinate serves as a relative quality descriptor, enabling zero-shot quality ranking and discrimination without relying on MOS supervision.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 多种质量评估场景Various Quality Assessment Scenarios

FR-IQA模型的发展从来没有脱离实际的质量评估场景。早期的基准数据集，例如 TID2013、LIVE、CSIQ 和 KADID-10k，主要是为了研究人类对受控设置下合成失真引起的保真度损失的感知而开发的。最近的数据集，包括 PIPAL、QADS、SynTEX 和 TQD，进一步将 FR-IQA 扩展到由现代恢复和合成算法生成的图像，其中感知质量通常更难以确定，因为算法引起的变化并不总是对应于质量下降。同时，FR-IQA还在更具体的场景中进行了探索，例如屏幕内容、红外、医学和中子图像。然而，这些领域的进展仍然有限，很大程度上是因为 IQA 注释成本高昂、主观，并且通常需要领域专业知识，使得标记数据集难以获得。此外，现有的免训练 FR-IQA 方法在跨域场景中表现出性能下降，因为它们通常依赖于主要针对自然图像优化的 ImageNet 预训练特征表示。当应用于特定领域的数据时，图像统计和结构信息的差异会导致领域转移并损害预测可靠性。这些限制要求 FR-IQA 框架能够适应特定领域的功能，而不依赖于主观质量标签。The development of FR-IQA models has never been detached from practical quality assessment scenarios. Early benchmark datasets, such as TID2013, LIVE, CSIQ, and KADID-10k, were mainly developed to study human perception of fidelity loss caused by synthetic distortions under controlled settings. More recent datasets, including PIPAL, QADS, SynTEX, and TQD, further extend FR-IQA to images generated by modern restoration and synthesis algorithms, where perceptual quality is often harder to determine because algorithm-induced changes do not always correspond to quality degradation. Meanwhile, FR-IQA has also been explored in more specific scenarios, such as screen-content, infrared, medical, and neutron images. However, progress in these areas remains limited, largely because IQA annotation is costly, subjective, and often requires domain expertise, making labeled datasets difficult to obtain. Moreover, existing training-free FR-IQA methods show performance degradation in cross-domain scenarios, since they usually rely on ImageNet-pretrained feature representations that are primarily optimized for natural images. When applied to domain-specific data, the discrepancy in image statistics and structural information leads to domain shift and compromises prediction reliability. These limitations call for an FR-IQA framework that can be adapted to domain-specific features without relying on subjective quality labels.

VI 局限性和失败案例Limitations and failure cases

限制：Limitations:尽管我们的方法通过特定领域的预训练实现了强大的性能，但这种依赖性也限制了其适用性。与完全免训练的 FR-IQA 方法不同，它仍然需要干净的参考数据和显式的退化模拟来构建预训练集。因此，它无法提供与完全免培训方法相同的即插即用便利性。Although our method achieves strong performance with domain-specific pre-training, this dependence also limits its applicability. 七VII结论Conclusions

受解耦表示学习概念的启发，我们提出了一种基于因果推理和解耦表示学习的FR-IQA方法。具体来说，该方法构建了一个模型，可以解耦受内容影响的退化特征。随后，通过学习退化特征的近似流形来预测质量得分。实验结果验证了我们方法的有效性。此外，与现有的免训练 FR-IQA 模型相比，该方法表现出优越的跨域泛化能力，因为它能够在没有标记的 IQA 数据的情况下进行场景特定的训练。Inspired by the concept of decoupled representation learning, we propose an FR-IQA method based on causal inference and decoupled representation learning. Specifically, the method constructs a model that decouples content-influenced degradation features. Subsequently, the quality score is predicted by learning the approximate manifold of the degradation features. Experimental results verify the effectiveness of our method. Moreover, the method demonstrates superior cross-domain generalization compared with existing training-free FR-IQA models, owing to its capability for scenario-specific training without labeled IQA data.
