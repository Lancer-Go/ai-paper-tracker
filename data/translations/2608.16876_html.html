AutoSR：通过搜索研究状态自动符号回归AutoSR: Automatic Symbolic Regression by Searching Research States

Kejia Zhang    Youran Sun    Xinyu Ren Affiliation: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong SAR, China    Chugang Yi       Haizhao Yang    [0.5em] Department of Mathematics    University of Maryland    College Park    MD    USA
摘要Abstract

我们引入自动符号回归（AutoSR），这是一个完全自动化的系统，它通过搜索持续的科学研究而不是孤立的方程来实例化研究空间符号回归。 有限的、嘈杂的数据通常会产生数值上有竞争力的表达式，这意味着在观察范围之外有非常不同的行为，使得数值拟合和句法复杂性不足以衡量科学可信度。 现有的方法主要集中在改进表达上，但搜索通常除了结果公式和分数之外几乎不保留什么，从而丢失了科学记录，例如动机和探索，这些记录告诉下一步要尝试什么。 AutoSR 将此记录保存在We introduce Automatic Symbolic Regression (AutoSR), a fully automated system that instantiates Research-Space Symbolic Regression by searching persistent scientific investigations rather than isolated equations. Finite, noisy data often yield numerically competitive expressions that imply very different behavior outside the observed regime, making numerical fit and syntactic complexity insufficient measures of scientific credibility. Existing approaches largely focus on improving expressions, yet the search typically retains little beyond the resulting formula and score, losing the scientific record, such as motivations and probes, that inform what to try next. AutoSR preserves this record in a研究状态Research State，将每个候选方程与沿着其分支发展的推理、计算证据和独立审查结合起来。 提案者-评审者代理在渐进式扩大蒙特卡罗树搜索（PW-MCTS）下开发这些状态，该搜索在竞争性调查之间分配计算，而积累的研究记录最终被合成为最终报告，解释主导关系及其选择的基础。 在两个基准套件中选定的九个挑战中，AutoSR 在每种情况下都恢复了代数等效关系，包括三个未发布系统恢复的 cp3 基准问题和六个结构不同的 LSR 变换问题。 总体而言，AutoSR 将符号回归从方程级搜索扩展到自动化科学研究，使科学知识和积累的证据能够塑造探索的内容以及如何证明所得方程的合理性。, coupling each candidate equation with the reasoning, computational evidence, and independent review developed along its branch. Proposer–reviewer agents develop these states under progressive-widening Monte Carlo tree search (PW-MCTS), which allocates computation across competing investigations, while the accumulated research record is ultimately synthesized into a final report that explains the leading relation and the basis for its selection. Across nine selected challenges from two benchmark suites, AutoSR recovers algebraically equivalent relations in every case, including three cp3-bench problems that no published system recovers and six structurally diverse LSR-Transform problems. Overall, AutoSR extends symbolic regression from equation-level search toward automated scientific investigation, allowing scientific knowledge and accumulated evidence to shape both what is explored and how the resulting equation is justified.

1 简介Introduction

来自数据的科学发现寻求一种解释现象的关系，并且在观察到的样本之外仍然有用，而不仅仅是重现观察结果的公式。 有限数据不确定生成函数：无限多个不同的函数可以对相同的有限数据集进行插值Scientific discovery from data seeks a relation that accounts for the phenomenon and remains useful beyond the observed sample rather than merely a formula that reproduces the observations. Finite data underdetermine the generating function: infinitely many distinct functions can interpolate the same finite dataset[5, 8]。 在存在噪声的情况下，结构上不同的表达式可能会对观察到的数据产生几乎无法区分的误差，同时在科学含义（例如外推法、维度、单调性、限制行为和机制）上存在巨大差异. In the presence of noise, structurally different expressions can attain nearly indistinguishable errors over the observed data while differing sharply in scientific implications such as extrapolation, dimensions, monotonicity, limiting behavior, and mechanism[26, 31]。 传统的符号回归（SR）通过搜索显式数学表达式并通过数值拟合和句法复杂性的通用度量对它们进行排序，使得从有限观测中发现方程的不确定问题变得易于处理. Conventional symbolic regression (SR) makes this underdetermined problem of equation discovery from finite observations tractable by searching for explicit mathematical expressions and ranking them by numerical fit and generic measures of syntactic complexity[23, 41, 12]。 这些标准有利于简洁的候选定律，但它们本身并不能建立科学的可信度。 科学家利用. These criteria favor concise candidate laws, but they do not by themselves establish scientific credibility. Scientists use科学先验scientific priors——包括物理世界的常识、领域理论和特定问题的要求——来判断哪些假设是合理的。 一些要求，例如维度一致性、单调性或边界值，可以编译成专门的约束或搜索空间—including general knowledge of the physical world, domain theory, and problem-specific requirements—to judge which hypotheses are plausible. Some requirements, such as dimensional consistency, monotonicity, or a boundary value, can be compiled into specialized constraints or search spaces[24, 50, 8]，而其他形式的科学知识，包括因果期望、本体论和机械合理性，很难用固定的语法或标量目标来表达。 此外，科学知识不仅指导接受哪个方程，而且指导下一步研究什么。例如，可疑的渐近线可能需要进行数值极限测试，而意外的函数形式可能会激发与理论模型的比较。 最近基于 LLM 和代理 SR 方法使用上下文知识、计算工具、诊断和可重用存储器来改进方程建议, whereas other forms of scientific knowledge, including causal expectations, ontology, and mechanistic plausibility, are difficult to express in a fixed grammar or scalar objective. Moreover, scientific knowledge guides not only which equation to accept but also what to investigate next. For example, a suspicious asymptote may call for a numerical limit test, whereas an unexpected functional form may motivate comparison with a theoretical model. Recent LLM-based and agentic SR methods use contextual knowledge, computational tools, diagnostics, and reusable memories to improve equation proposals[43, 55, 28, 35, 62]。 他们的进步引发了一个互补的问题：与每个候选人相关的完整动机、实验、失败的替代方案、诊断和批评如何成为全局搜索的持久对象，而不是产生下一个方程的短暂背景？ 为了解决这个差距，我们引入. Their advances motivate a complementary question: how can the complete motivation, experiments, failed alternatives, diagnostics, and criticism associated with each candidate become persistent objects of global search rather than transient context for producing the next equation? To address this gap, we introduce自动符号回归 (AutoSR)Automatic Symbolic Regression (AutoSR)，一个完全自动化的代理系统，将调查结果合成为可审计的报告，该报告在整个假设提出、测试和批评过程中使用科学先验提供推理和调查的主要方程，同时搜索持久的研究记录而不是孤立的表达。, a fully automated agentic system that synthesizes the resulting investigation into an auditable report that presents the leading equation with reasoning and investigation using scientific priors throughout hypothesis proposal, testing, and criticism while searching over persistent research records rather than isolated expressions.

AutoSR 的目标是在给定的问题规范和计算预算下发现科学上可信的数学关系。 问题规范将观察到的数据与科学先验相结合，用于判断哪些关系是合理的，包括可接受的关系应满足的任何明确要求。 为了保持更广泛的研究，AutoSR 将每项研究尝试表示为AutoSR targets the discovery of a scientifically credible mathematical relation under a given problem specification and computation budget. The problem specification combines the observed data with the scientific priors used to judge which relations are plausible, including any explicit requirements that an acceptable relation should satisfy. To preserve the broader investigation, AutoSR represents each research attempt as a研究状态Research State，一个持久搜索单元，包含候选方程和产生该方程的尝试的更广泛的研究记录。 研究状态形成了一个分支搜索空间，其中新状态可以发起调查或通过继承其积累的发现和评论来扩展现有的调查，而兄弟分支则独立地寻求相互竞争的解释。 以研究状态为搜索单元，AutoSR 必须根据积累的科学证据和剩余的计算预算反复确定下一步要启动或扩展的调查。 我们将这个顺序决策问题表述为, a persistent search unit comprising a candidate equation and the broader research record of the attempt that produced it. Research States form a branching search space in which a new state can either initiate an investigation or extend an existing one by inheriting its accumulated findings and reviews, while sibling branches pursue competing explanations independently. With the Research State as the search unit, AutoSR must repeatedly determine which investigation to initiate or extend next from the accumulated scientific evidence and remaining computation budget. We formulate this sequential decision problem as研究状态搜索Research-State Search。 AutoSR 使用蒙特卡罗树搜索 (MCTS) 执行研究状态搜索. AutoSR performs Research-State Search using Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS)[11, 22, 6]，它通过平衡有前途的调查的扩展与替代分支的探索来在分支搜索空间中分配计算预算。 搜索完整的科学研究而不是孤立的表达定义了更广泛的范式, which allocates the computation budget across the branching search space by balancing the extension of promising investigations with the exploration of alternative branches. Searching over complete scientific investigations rather than isolated expressions defines the broader paradigm of研究空间符号回归Research-Space Symbolic Regression，AutoSR 通过研究状态搜索实例化。 该设计提供了五个实际好处：, which AutoSR instantiates through Research-State Search. This design provides five practical benefits:

  • •

    知识引导的调查。Knowledge-guided investigation.通过问题规范提供的科学先验不仅指导接受哪些候选方程，还指导在整个搜索过程中如何提出假设、如何进行计算测试和批评。Scientific priors supplied through the problem specification guide not only which candidate equations are accepted, but also how hypotheses are proposed, computationally tested, and criticized throughout the search.

  • •

    积累研究记忆。Accumulated research memory.每个研究状态都保留其发现、失败的尝试和审阅者反馈，允许同一分支的后代状态以积累的证据为基础，而不是仅从方程式及其分数重新开始。Each Research State preserves its findings, failed attempts, and reviewer feedback, allowing descendant states along the same branch to build on accumulated evidence instead of restarting from only an equation and its score.

  • •

    独立的替代方案。Independent alternatives.通过将研究状态组织成单独的分支，AutoSR 允许独立发展替代解释，而不会过早地简化为单一表达。By organizing Research States into separate branches, AutoSR allows alternative explanations to develop independently without being prematurely reduced to a single expression.

  • •

    完全自主的调查。Fully autonomous investigation.通过将研究状态搜索与自动假设生成、计算测试和独立审查相结合，AutoSR 可以重复选择并开展下一次调查。一旦提供了问题规范和计算预算，系统就可以维持这个完整的过程，而无需持续的人工管理。By combining Research-State Search with automated hypothesis generation, computational testing, and independent review, AutoSR can repeatedly select and carry out the next investigation. Once the problem specification and computation budget are provided, the system can sustain this complete process without continuous human management.

  • •

    可审计的科学结果。Auditable scientific results.由于每个候选者背后的调查都被保留，最终报告可以将主要方程与其支持证据、局限性、研究历史和可信替代方案联系起来。Because the investigation behind each candidate is preserved, the final report can connect the leading equation to its supporting evidence, limitations, research history, and credible alternatives.

通过这种方式，AutoSR 可以积累科学证据来确定系统接下来要调查的内容，而不是仅允许数字拟合来控制搜索。In this way, AutoSR allows accumulated scientific evidence to determine what the system investigates next, rather than allowing numerical fit alone to control the search.

AutoSR 的设计前提是，当系统运行人类科学家使用的科学探究功能循环时，科学方程发现可以变得更加可靠。 科学家不会仅通过数值拟合来判断候选方程；而是通过其数值拟合来判断候选方程。他们利用科学先验提出假设，设计实验或计算来测试其含义，批评由此产生的证据，并利用所学到的知识来确定下一步的调查。 例如，可靠的黑体辐射定律必须考虑测量的光谱，同时与位移和总辐射定律以及当时可用的极限形式保持一致AutoSR is designed around the premise that scientific equation discovery can become more reliable when a system operationalizes the functional cycle of scientific inquiry used by human scientists. Scientists do not judge a candidate equation by its numerical fit alone; they use scientific priors to propose hypotheses, design experiments or computations to test their implications, criticize the resulting evidence, and allow what they learn to determine the next investigation. For example, a credible black-body radiation law had to account for the measured spectrum while remaining consistent with displacement and total-radiation laws and the limiting forms then available[53, 54, 46, 4, 38]。 研究并没有以一个合适的公式结束：对物理解释的探索导致普朗克引入了与频率成比例的能量元素. The investigation did not end with a fitted formula: the search for a physical account led Planck to introduce energy elements proportional to frequency[37]，这个想法随后支持了爱因斯坦对光量子的解释, an idea that subsequently supported Einstein’s interpretation of light quanta[14]。 科学探究可以在仅靠数字拟合无法成功的情况下取得成功，因为每个额外的经验或理论测试都可以消除在数字上仍然具有竞争力但违反科学证据或要求的表达方式，而积累的发现可以指导下一步应该研究哪个假设。 AutoSR 在计算规模上实现了这一因果过程：科学先验限制了合理的方程，研究状态保留了特定于分支的研究记录，独立审查挑战了不受支持的结论，研究状态搜索指导了进一步的调查。 从这个功能意义上来说，AutoSR 模仿了人类科学家的探索周期，而不试图重现任何单个科学家的推理。 通过这个循环，AutoSR 旨在将科学上可信的方程与仅针对观测数据准确的表达式区分开来。. Scientific inquiry can succeed where numerical fit alone cannot because each additional empirical or theoretical test can eliminate expressions that remain numerically competitive but violate scientific evidence or requirements, while the accumulated findings guide which hypothesis should be investigated next. AutoSR operationalizes this causal process at a computational scale: scientific priors constrain plausible equations, Research States preserve branch-specific research records, independent review challenges unsupported conclusions, and Research-State Search directs further investigation. In this functional sense, AutoSR mimics the exploratory cycle of human scientists without attempting to reproduce the reasoning of any individual scientist. Through this cycle, AutoSR is designed to distinguish scientifically credible equations from expressions that are merely accurate over the observed data.

我们通过对两个基准套件中选定的挑战问题进行精确方程恢复来评估 AutoSR。 对于所有九个选定的问题，AutoSR 都能在代数上恢复与基本事实等价的关系。 其中包括 12 个已发布的 SR 系统中的任何一个均未恢复的 3 个 cp3 基准方程We evaluate AutoSR through exact equation recovery on selected challenge problems from two benchmark suites. On all nine selected problems, AutoSR recovers relations algebraically equivalent to the ground truth. These include three cp3-bench equations that were not recovered by any of twelve published SR systems[51]和六个结构不同的 LSR 变换方程and six structurally diverse LSR-Transform equations[44]，全部使用相同的核心搜索程序和代理角色进行恢复。 目前的证据证明了这些选定的质疑案例的可行性；重复运行可靠性、组件去除、调度效率和领域验证仍然是未来评估的主题。, all recovered using the same core search procedure and agent roles. The present evidence establishes feasibility on these selected challenge cases; repeated-run reliability, component ablations, scheduling efficiency, and domain validation remain subjects for future evaluation.

我们的贡献是三重的。 首先，我们介绍研究空间符号回归，据我们所知，研究状态搜索的第一个表述是该范式背后的顺序决策问题。 其次，我们将 AutoSR 开发为一种完全自动化的实现，结合了持久的研究状态、独立的科学审查、自主计算调查和基于 MCTS 的搜索。 第三，我们展示了跨越两个基准套件的九个选定挑战方程的精确恢复。 本文的其余部分提供了相关工作回顾，定义了 AutoSR 方法，提出了选定的基准评估，并讨论了将符号回归视为自动化科学探究的含义、局限性和未来评估。Our contributions are threefold. First, we introduce Research-Space Symbolic Regression and, to our knowledge, the first formulation of Research-State Search as the sequential decision problem underlying this paradigm. Second, we develop AutoSR as a fully automated implementation that combines persistent Research States, independent scientific review, autonomous computational investigation, and MCTS-based search. Third, we demonstrate exact recovery on nine selected challenge equations spanning two benchmark suites. The remainder of the paper provides the related work review, defines the AutoSR method, presents the selected benchmark evaluation, and discusses the implications, limitations, and future evaluation of treating symbolic regression as automated scientific inquiry.

2.1 传统的和先验的符号回归Conventional and Prior-Informed Symbolic Regression

符号回归联合搜索显式数学表达式的结构和参数。 遗传编程建立了表达树、进化变异和基于适应度的选择作为该搜索的一般表述Symbolic regression jointly searches for the structure and parameters of an explicit mathematical expression. Genetic programming established expression trees, evolutionary variation, and fitness-based selection as a general formulation of this search[23]。 科学发现的早期系统通过动态系统的主动探测扩展表达搜索. Early systems for scientific discovery extended expression search with active probing of dynamical systems[5]并在实验数据中寻找不变关系and searches for invariant relations in experimental data[41]。 后续方法涵盖确定性模型构建，例如 FFX. Subsequent approaches span deterministic model building such as FFX[32], SINDy 中指定函数库的稀疏识别, sparse identification over a prescribed function library in SINDy[7]，SISSO中的压缩感知特征构建, compressed-sensing feature construction in SISSO[34]，贝叶斯 SR 中表达式树的概率搜索, probabilistic search over expression trees in Bayesian SR[19]，以及 AI Feynman 中通过对称性、可分离性和维度分析进行物理启发的分解, and physics-inspired decomposition by symmetry, separability, and dimensional analysis in AI Feynman[52]。 PySR 和 FEX 等现代系统将富有表现力的搜索空间与专门的优化相结合. Modern systems such as PySR and FEX combine expressive search spaces with specialized optimization[12, 18]，而并行符号枚举共享子树计算以有效地评估非常大的表达式集合, while parallel symbolic enumeration shares subtree computations to evaluate very large collections of expressions efficiently[39].

在这些算法系列中，候选算法通常是通过预测误差以及简约性、稀疏性、结构先验或相关模型选择标准来选择的。 SRBench 比较了已知地面实况和黑盒任务上的当代方法，并表明预测准确性、符号恢复和表达复杂性揭示了性能的不同方面Across these algorithmic families, candidates are ordinarily selected by predictive error together with parsimony, sparsity, a structural prior, or a related model-selection criterion. SRBench compares contemporary methods on both known-ground-truth and black-box tasks and shows that predictive accuracy, symbolic recovery, and expression complexity expose different aspects of performance[26]。 SRSD 进一步强调科学发现评估的现实采样范围、不相关变量和归一化树编辑距离. SRSD further emphasizes realistic sampling ranges, irrelevant variables, and normalized tree-edit distance for scientific-discovery evaluation[31]，而 LLM-SRBench 添加了转换和综合的科学方程以及防止方程记忆的保护措施, while LLM-SRBench adds transformed and synthetic scientific equations together with safeguards against equation memorization[44]。 这些基准清楚地表明，句法大小是一个有用的操作代理，但无论是低错误还是简短的表达，都不能单独证明恢复的关系具有预期的科学意义。. These benchmarks make clear that syntactic size is a useful operational proxy, but neither low error nor a short expression alone establishes that a recovered relation has the intended scientific meaning.

5 讨论Discussion

所选回收率的重要性在于产生它们的共同研究工作流程。 在选定的问题中，AutoSR 保留了相同的核心搜索过程和代理角色。 这些案例表明，共同的持续研究工作流程可以恢复结构多样的方程。The significance of the selected recoveries lies in the common research workflow that produced them. Across the selected problems, AutoSR retained the same core search procedure and agent roles. These cases show that a common sustained research workflow can recover structurally diverse equations.

核心区别在于系统搜索的单位。 传统的表达水平搜索只能保留公式及其分数，而研究状态则需要保留与一次尝试相关的动机、计算、失败的测试和批评。 这种持久的记录允许证据影响接下来的调查内容，而特定于分支的记忆允许相互竞争的解释独立发展。 在这种结构中，科学先验不仅可以指导哪种关系被接受，还可以指导提出哪种假设、测试哪种含义以及哪个分支接受进一步的计算。The central distinction is the unit over which the system searches. A conventional expression-level search can retain only a formula and its score, whereas a Research State is required to preserve the motivation, computations, failed tests, and criticism associated with one attempt. This persistent record allows evidence to influence what is investigated next, while branch-specific memory permits competing explanations to develop independently. Within this structure, scientific priors can guide not only which relation is accepted, but also which hypothesis is proposed, which implication is tested, and which branch receives further computation.

持续研究国家还支持最终报告，将主要关系与其证据、局限性、研究历史和可信替代方案联系起来。 在这个设定下，Persistent Research States also support a final report that connects the leading relation to its evidence, limitations, research history, and credible alternatives.
