PAL：个人自适应学习器PAL: Personal Adaptive Learner

Megha Chakraborty, Darssan L. Eswaramoorthi, Madhur Thareja, Het Riteshkumar Shah, Finlay Palmer, Aryaman Bahl, Michelle A Ihetu, Amit Sheth
摘要Abstract

人工智能驱动的教育平台在个性化方面取得了一些进展，但大多数仍然局限于静态适应——预定义的测验、统一的节奏或通用的反馈——限制了它们对学习者不断变化的理解做出反应的能力。这一不足凸显了对环境感知和实时自适应系统的需求。 我们推出 PAL（个人自适应学习者），这是一个人工智能驱动的平台，可将讲座视频转化为交互式学习体验。 PAL 不断分析多模式讲座内容，并通过不同难度的问题动态地吸引学习者，并随着课程的展开调整他们的反应。在课程结束时，PAL 会生成个性化摘要，强化关键概念，同时根据学习者的兴趣定制示例。通过将多模式内容分析与自适应决策相结合，PAL 为响应式数字学习提供了一个新颖的框架。我们的工作展示了人工智能如何超越静态个性化，转向实时、个性化支持，解决人工智能教育的核心挑战。AI-driven education platforms have made some progress in personalisation, yet most remain constrained to static adaptation—predefined quizzes, uniform pacing, or generic feedback—limiting their ability to respond to learners’ evolving understanding. This shortfall highlights the need for systems that are both context-aware and adaptive in real time. We introduce PAL (Personal Adaptive Learner), an AI-powered platform that transforms lecture videos into interactive learning experiences. PAL continuously analyzes multimodal lecture content and dynamically engages learners through questions of varying difficulty, adjusting to their responses as the lesson unfolds. At the end of a session, PAL generates a personalized summary that reinforces key concepts while tailoring examples to the learner’s interests. By uniting multimodal content analysis with adaptive decision-making, PAL contributes a novel framework for responsive digital learning. Our work demonstrates how AI can move beyond static personalization toward real-time, individualized support, addressing a core challenge in AI-enabled education.

1 简介Introduction

人工智能越来越多地应用于数字教育，以提高个性化和参与度。然而，大多数基于讲座的平台仍然是静态的：学习者被动地观看视频，接受统一的测验，收到的反馈有限。这样的系统无法实时适应个体学习者，常常让学生要么缺乏挑战，要么不知所措，这两者都会降低动机和保留率。解决这一差距是实现可扩展、公平和有吸引力的学习环境的关键。Artificial intelligence is increasingly used in digital education to improve personalization and engagement. However,ver, most lecture-based platforms remain static: learners watch videos passively, receive uniform quizzes, receive receive limited feedback. Such systems fail to adapt to individual learners in real time, often leaving students either under-challenged or overwhelmed, both of which reduce motivation and retention. Addressing this gap is key for scalable, equitable, and engaging learning environments.

现有的自适应学习方法，例如预测试或严格的基于规则的调整，仅捕获粗略的能力测量Existing adaptive learning approaches, such as pretests or rigid rule-based adjustments, capture only coarse measures of ability(Claned 2025; Hyperspace 2025)。他们努力让学习者始终处于最佳“学习区域”，即材料既不太简单也不太困难。. They struggle to keep learners consistently within their optimal “learning zone” where the material is neither too easy nor too difficult.

我们提出了We present the个人自适应学习器 (PAL)Personal Adaptive Learner (PAL)，一个将讲座视频转换为交互式学习课程的系统。 PAL 将多模态视频分析与自适应问题引擎集成在一起，该引擎在播放期间定期插入多项选择题。混合强化学习 (RL) 算法根据响应的准确性、连续性和时间动态调整问题的难度，确保学习者在逐步接受挑战的同时保持参与度。在每节课结束时，PAL 都会生成个性化的摘要，强化关键概念并将其与学习者的具体兴趣联系起来。, a system that transforms lecture videos into interactive learning sessions. PAL integrates multimodal video analysis with an adaptive question engine that periodically inserts multiple-choice questions during playback. A Hybrid Reinforcement Learning (RL) algorithm dynamically adjusts the difficulty of the question based on the accuracy, streaks, and timing of the responses, ensuring that learners remain engaged while progressively challenged. At the end of each lesson, PAL produces a personalized summary that reinforces key concepts and connects them to the specific interests of the learner.

4 个性化的课后总结Personalized Post-Lesson Summary

讲座结束时，PAL 根据完整的学习者资料生成个性化摘要At the end of a lecture, PAL generates a personalized summary based on the full learner profile(Essa, Celik, and Human-Hendricks 2023)。总结要点. The summary highlights领地掌握Territory Mastered和and探索区Discovery Zone.

第 1 阶段 - 通过语义搜索进行智能提取。Stage 1 - Smart extraction with semantic search.首先，后端对整个讲座进行预处理：它将成绩单分成句子，并使用 SentenceTransformer 将每个句子转换为数字向量First, the backend preprocesses the whole lecture: it breaks the transcript into sentences and turns each sentence into a numeric vector with a SentenceTransformer(Ruas and Grosky 2017)。这创建了讲座的“语义地图”。 当学生询问一个概念（例如，“相关性与因果关系”）时，系统也会将该概念转换为向量，并在语义图上进行快速相似性搜索。这样它就能找到最相关的句子，即使它们使用不同的单词或分散开来。该过程忽略对话噪音，并收集紧凑、上下文丰富的原始文本块以在下一步中使用。. This creates a “semantic map” of the lecture. When a student asks about a concept (for example, “Correlation vs Causation”), the system converts that concept into a vector too and does a fast similarity search on the semantic map. That way it finds the most relevant sentences even if they use different words or are spread out. The process ignores conversational noise and collects a compact, context-rich block of raw text to use in the next step.

第 2 阶段 - 使用 Llama 3.2 进行上下文感知综合。Stage 2 - Context-aware synthesis with Llama 3.2.收集的文本（可能是碎片化的）被发送到我们的 Llama 3.2 1B Instruct 模型。该模型不是简单的“总结”命令，而是收到精心设计的提示，使其像专家助教一样。提示告诉模型使用讲座文本作为主要来源，但也允许它使用自己的知识来填补空白并澄清尴尬的措辞。提示还可以根据学生的需求进行调整（例如，要求提供有关学生遇到的主题的更多详细信息）。其结果是一个清晰、结构良好的解释，忠实于讲座，同时提高了可读性和完整性。The collected text (which may be fragmented) is sent to our Llama 3.2 1B Instruct model. Instead of a simple “summarize” command, the model receives a carefully designed prompt that makes it act like an expert teaching assistant. The prompt tells the model to use the lecture text as the main source but also allows it to fill gaps and clarify awkward phrasing using its own knowledge. The prompt can also adapt based on the student’s needs (e.g., request more detail for topics the student struggled with). The result is a clear, well-structured explanation that stays faithful to the lecture while improving readability and completeness.

5 讨论Discussion

PAL（个人自适应学习器）通过多模式、代理管道从讲座视频中生成带时间戳的难度等级问题来调整教学。与静态 AI 教育工具不同，PAL 集成了成绩单分析、视觉上下文验证、问题生成和难度评级，使内容与学习者的表现实时保持一致。 PAL 的评估将通过交互式界面在线提供，允许读者探索示例记录、生成的问题和难度评级。未来的工作将扩大反馈循环、整合协作​​学习并评估课堂影响。PAL (Personal Adaptive Learner) adapts instruction by generating timestamped, difficulty-rated questions from lecture videos through a multimodal, agentic pipeline. Unlike static AI education tools, PAL integrates transcript analysis, visual context validation, question generation, and difficulty rating to align content with learner performance in real time. The evaluation of PAL will be made available online through an interactive interface, allowing readers to explore example transcripts, generated questions, and difficulty ratings. Future work will expand feedback loops, integrate collaborative learning, and evaluate classroom impact.
