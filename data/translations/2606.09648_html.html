ArtiFact：大规模多模式文化遗产数据集ArtiFact: A Large-Scale Multi-Modal Cultural Heritage Dataset

Luciano Duarte BIFOLD & TU Berlin duarte.castineira@tu-berlin.de , Olga Ovcharenko BIFOLD & TU Berlin ovcharenko@tu-berlin.de and Sebastian Schelter 0000-0003-4722-5840 BIFOLD & TU Berlin schelter@tu-berlin.de
摘要。Abstract.

多模式数据管理已成为数据库社区的中心研究主题，涵盖数据集成、语义查询处理和数据质量评估。尽管人们的兴趣日益浓厚，但社区缺乏结合表格、文本和图像的大规模真实数据集。我们呈现Multi-modal data management has emerged as a central research topic in the database community, spanning data integration, semantic query processing, and data quality assessment. Despite this growing interest, the community lacks large-scale, real-world datasets combining tables, text, and images. We present神器ArtiFact，多模式文化遗产数据集, a multi-modal cultural heritage dataset of651,045博物馆记录来自大都会艺术博物馆、芝加哥艺术博物馆和国立博物馆。我们展示了实用性museum records collected from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago, and the Rijksmuseum. We demonstrate the utility of神器ArtiFact通过两个下游任务。对于跨模式错误检测，我们引入了注入到的七个错误类别的精心分类法through two downstream tasks. For cross-modal error detection, we introduce a curated taxonomy of seven error categories injected into130,209记录并表明，可靠地检测微妙的特定领域错误（例如材料不合时宜和时间变化）仍然是一个开放的挑战。对于语义查询处理，我们表明当前的系统很难处理涉及文化邻近性、模糊对象类型和历史偶然术语的查询。我们的结果定位records and show that reliably detecting subtle domain-specific errors such as material anachronisms and temporal shifts remain an open challenge. For semantic query processing, we show that current systems struggle with queries involving cultural proximity, ambiguous object types, and historically contingent terminology. Our results position神器ArtiFact作为多模式数据管理研究的具有挑战性的基准。as a challenging benchmark for multi-modal data management research.

1. 简介Introduction

多模式数据管理已成为数据库社区的中心研究前沿，涵盖数据集成Multi-modal data management has emerged as a central research frontier in the database community, spanning data integration(Urban and Binnig, 2024)、语义查询处理, semantic query processing(Patel et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025; Jo and Trummer, 2024; Ovcharenko et al., 2026; Lao et al., 2025)、实体解析, entity resolution(Wilke and Rahm, 2021)，以及数据质量评估, and data quality assessment(Ovcharenko and Schelter, 2025)。随着集合越来越多地将结构化记录与非结构化文本和图像配对，出现了如何跨模式存储、索引、查询和清理数据的基本问题。 然而，尽管人们对以数据为中心的人工智能和全模态模型越来越感兴趣. As collections increasingly pair structured records with unstructured text and images, fundamental questions arise about how to store, index, query, and clean data across modalities. However, despite growing interest in data-centric AI and omni-modal models(Jiang et al., 2025)，很少有人关注跨越表格、文本和图像数据的大规模且精心策划的多模态数据集的构建。许多现有的基准隐含地假设关联的元数据和图像是正确且一致的, little attention has been paid to the construction of large-scale and well-curated multi-modal datasets spanning tabular, textual, and image data. Many existing benchmarks implicitly assume that associated metadata and images are correct and consistent(Northcutt et al., 2021b)，尽管现实世界的馆藏经常包含嘈杂的注释、含糊的术语、缺失的信息和历史的不一致。, even though real-world collections often contain noisy annotations, ambiguous terminology, missing information, and historical inconsistencies.

缺乏文化遗产领域的数据集。Lack of datasets for the cultural heritage domain.这种差距在文化遗产领域尤为明显。世界各地的博物馆和档案馆正在迅速数字化藏品，并发布包含艺术品图像的开放获取记录和包含元数据、材料、艺术家归属和地理信息的目录描述。这些馆藏代表了跨世纪文化遗产的领域专家收集的独特丰富的多模式知识来源。同时，这些收集的记录源自异构元数据标准、不断发展的机构实践和手动文档工作流程，使得大规模规范化、互操作性和验证本身就具有挑战性This gap is particularly pronounced in the cultural heritage domain. Museums and archives worldwide are rapidly digitizing collections and publishing open-access records containing artwork images and catalog descriptions containing metadata, materials, artist attributions, and geographic information. These collections represent a uniquely rich source of multi-modal knowledge collected by domain experts spanning centuries of cultural heritage. At the same time, these collected records originate from heterogeneous metadata standards, evolving institutional practices, and manual documentation workflows, making large-scale normalization, interoperability, and validation inherently challenging(Whang et al., 2023)。 现有大型馆藏如OmniArt. Existing large-scale collections such as OmniArt(Strezoski and Worring, 2017), 语义艺术, SemArt(Garcia and Vogiatzis, 2018)（私人），SILKNOW(private), SILKNOW(Rei et al., 2022)，和时间旅行, and TimeTravel(Ghaboura et al., 2025)为检索、分类和多模态推理任务提供宝贵的资源，而无需检查数据质量和元数据的正确性。 同样，跨模式错误检测基准，例如 MERITprovide valuable resources for retrieval, classification, and multi-modal reasoning tasks, leaving data quality and metadata correctness unexamined. Similarly, cross-modal error detection benchmarks such as MERIT(Ovcharenko and Schelter, 2025)和MMIRand MMIR(Yan et al., 2025)证明了多模式数据质量评估的重要性，但仍仅限于电子商务和网络数据等领域。 用于多模式数据管理的开源大规模数据集仍然稀缺，特别是在文化领域demonstrate the importance of multi-modal data quality evaluation, yet remain restricted to domains such as e-Commerce and web data. Open source large-scale datasets for multi-modal data management remain scarce, especially in the cultural domain(Georgakopoulos et al., 2026).

4.2. 语义查询处理Semantic Query Processing

语义查询处理——对多模式数据执行由 LLM 支持的 SQL 查询——已成为数据库社区的一个核心挑战Semantic query processing — the execution of LLM-powered SQL queries over multi-modal data — has emerged as a central challenge for the database community(Lao et al., 2025)。 评估此类系统需要结合结构化和非结构化数据、涵盖不同语义运算符并提供可靠的基本事实的数据集。 现有基准测试，例如 SemBench. Evaluating such systems requires datasets that combine structured and unstructured data, cover diverse semantic operators, and provide reliable ground truth. Existing benchmarks such as SemBench(Lao et al., 2025)提供了坚实的基础，但仍然仅限于少数领域和受 LLM 污染的数据集。provide a strong foundation, but remain limited to a small number of domains and LLM-contaminated datasets.神器ArtiFact通过提供大规模、特定领域和视觉丰富的集合来补充这些努力，其中语义查询涵盖表格、具有特定领域术语的文本描述以及具有文化背景的图像。complements these efforts by offering a large-scale, domain-specific, and visually rich collection where semantic queries cover tables, textual descriptions with domain-specific terminology, and images with cultural context.

讨论。Discussion. Table 2显示了文化遗产数据上的性能语义查询处理系统。 物体外观模糊性是一个一致的错误来源：具有象征形状的历史花瓶在 Q1 中导致许多误报，而 Q3 将乌克兰服饰装饰品错误分类为奖章，可能是由于其历史形式与 LLM 训练数据中普遍存在的现代表示形式之间的视觉差距。 地理和文化上的接近加剧了这些错误：Q2 很难将英国雪花石膏物品与视觉上相似的荷兰和德国文物区分开来，而 Q4 经常将中国剑与日本、西藏和赫梯物品混淆。 最后，Q5 显示了区分不同技术的困难，其中染色、编织和水洗与蛋白银印花混为一谈。 我们的结果表明，如果没有特定领域的知识，语义查询系统就无法可靠地区分相邻文化，解析历史上偶然的材料和技术 术语，或解释物体外观如何随着时间和地理的变化而演变。shows the performance semantic query processing systems on cultural heritage data. Object appearance ambiguity is a consistent source of error: historical vases with figurative shapes cause many false positives in Q1, and Q3 missclassifies Ukrainian dress ornaments as medallions, likely due to the visual gap between their historical forms and the modern representations prevalent in LLM training data. Geographic and cultural proximity compounds these errors: Q2 struggles to separate British alabaster objects from visually similar Dutch and German artifacts, and Q4 frequently confuses Chinese swords with Japanese, Tibetan, and Hittite objects. Finally, Q5 shows the difficulty of differentiating between different techniques, where dyeing, weaving, and washing are conflated with albumen silver printing. Our results suggest that without domain-specific knowledge, semantic query systems cannot reliably distinguish between neighboring cultures, resolve historically contingent material and technique terminology, or account for how object appearances evolve across time and geography.

5. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presented神器ArtiFact，一个大规模多模态数据集, a large-scale multi-modal dataset of651,045文化遗产记录结合了来自三个主要博物馆的表格、数据、文本和图像数据，以及用于跨机构标准化的统一 ETL 管道。 我们对跨模式错误检测和语义查询处理的评估表明，当前的系统可以很好地处理视觉上显着的不一致，但无法处理需要文化和历史知识的微妙的特定领域的细微差别。 我们希望cultural heritage records combining tabular, data, textual, and image data from three major museums, together with a unified ETL pipeline for cross-institutional normalization. Our evaluation on cross-modal error detection and semantic query processing reveals that current systems handle visually salient inconsistencies well but fail on subtle domain-specific nuances requiring cultural and historical knowledge. We hope that神器ArtiFact将激发进一步的研究 文化遗产领域的多模式数据管理，特别是文化和历史偏见。will stimulate further research on multi-modal data management in cultural heritage domain, and in particular on the cultural and historical biases.
