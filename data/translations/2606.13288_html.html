This limitation arises not only from the reliance on global, single-vector representations for optimization, but also from the insufficient exploitation and modeling of the rich compositional information inherently present in paired image text data. 5 结论Conclusion

我们的工作引入了 MACCO，这是一个可以提高对 CLIP 等 VLM 的构图理解的框架。通过屏蔽一种模态中的构图概念并从另一种模态中重建它们，MACCO 更好地利用成对的图像文本数据中对齐的构图信号。我们进一步提出了两个辅助目标，MCA 和 MIR，以增强跨模态对齐和模内正则化。大量实验和深入分析表明，MACCO 有效提高了组合推理，增强了模型对句法结构和语义细微差别的编码，并有利于其他多模态任务。Our work introduced MACCO, a framework that improves compositional understanding in VLMs like CLIP. By masking compositional concepts in one modality and reconstructing them from the other, MACCO better exploits the aligned compositional signals in paired image-text data. We further proposed two auxiliary objectives, MCA and MIR, to enhance cross-modal alignment and intra-modal regularization. Extensive experiments and in-depth analyses show that MACCO effectively improves compositional reasoning, enhances the model’s encoding of syntactic structure and semantic nuance, and benefits other multimodal tasks.

6 局限性Limitations

虽然 MACCO 引入了一种新颖且有效的框架来增强视觉语言模型的组合理解，而不依赖于明确的硬否定构造，但仍然存在一些局限性，每个局限性都为未来的研究指明了有希望的途径。While MACCO introduces a novel and effective framework for enhancing the compositional understanding of vision-language models without relying on explicit hard negative construction, several limitations remain, each pointing to promising avenues for future research.第一First，虽然 MACCO 利用自然对齐的图像文本对进行掩码跨模态重建，但它需要轻量级预处理来提取组合概念（, although MACCO leverages naturally aligned image-text pairs for masked cross-modal reconstruction, it requires lightweight pre-processing to extract compositional concepts (e.g.、短语或区域）来自两种形式。此步骤虽然最少且与标准工具兼容，但引入了可能限制完全端到端管道的灵活性的依赖性。., phrases or regions) from both modalities.
