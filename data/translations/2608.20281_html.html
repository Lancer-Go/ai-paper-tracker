注入、对齐、恢复：无检索文档知识内化的分阶段后培训Inject, Align, Recover: Staged Post-Training for Retrieval-Free Document Knowledge Internalization

Qian Kou Thanks: Corresponding authors: Qian Kou (kouqian@baai.ac.cn) and Xiaofeng Shi (xfshi@baai.ac.cn).    Xiaofeng Shi22footnotemark: 2    Xiaosong Qiu    Hua Zhou Thanks: Project leader.
摘要Abstract

当推理时未检索源文档时，大型语言模型通常无法回答有关有界文档集合的问题。我们将这种设置研究为文档知识内化：将固定语料库转换为可用的参数化知识，以进行免检索问答。我们建议Large language models often fail to answer questions about a bounded document collection when the source documents are not retrieved at inference time. We study this setting as document knowledge internalization: converting a fixed corpus into usable parametric knowledge for retrieval-free question answering. We proposeIAR（注入、对齐和恢复），一个三阶段的训练后框架，将结构化文档知识注入、QA行为对齐和通用能力恢复分开。与传统的持续预训练不同，Inject 将源文档转换为延续、重写和指令条件重建目标。然后，Align 通过仅回答 QA 监督来调整注入模型，而 Recover 将域适应模型与基本指令模型合并以恢复一般功能。跨 Common Corpus (CC) 和 CCI，以及跨 Llama、Phi、Qwen 和 SmolLM 模型系列，(Inject, Align, and Recover), a three-stage post-training framework that separates structured document knowledge injection, QA behavior alignment, and general ability recovery. Unlike conventional continued pretraining, Inject converts source documents into continuation, rewrite, and instruction-conditioned reconstruction objectives. Align then adapts the injected model with answer-only QA supervision, while Recover merges the domain-adapted model with the base instruction model to recover general capabilities. Across Common Corpus (CC) and CCI, and across Llama, Phi, Qwen, and SmolLM model families,IAR改进了领域-主领域-通用边界，以实现免检索文档内化。主要比较中，improves the domain-primary domain-general frontier for retrieval-free document internalization. In the main comparison,IAR在 8 个数据集模型设置中的 7 个中，与 Vanilla SFT 相比，在所有四个报告指标上都有所改进，在 IFEval、MMLU 和 MSBench 中，域 QA 准确性平均提高了 3.6 个百分点，平均总体性能提高了 12.1 个百分点。扩展的 CC 基线表明 LoRA 和 FAPM 可以赢得单独的一般指标，但在也达到领先或接近领先领域内部化的方法中，improves over Vanilla SFT on all four reported metrics in 7 of 8 dataset-model settings, with average gains of 3.6 percentage points in domain QA accuracy and 12.1 percentage points in mean general performance across IFEval, MMLU, and MSBench. 简介Introduction

检索增强生成是基于文档的问答的标准工程答案：从语料库中检索段落，将它们附加到提示中，并要求模型用该证据进行回答Retrieval-augmented generation is the standard engineering answer to document-grounded question answering: retrieve passages from a corpus, append them to the prompt, and ask a model to answer with that evidence(14)。本文研究了不同的设置。在许多部署中，检索可能不可用，由于延迟或隐私原因而不受欢迎，或者故意删除以测试训练后是否改变了模型的参数知识。我们称这个设置为. This paper studies a different setting. In many deployments, retrieval may be unavailable, undesirable for latency or privacy reasons, or deliberately removed to test whether post-training has changed the model’s parametric knowledge. We call this setting文档知识内化document knowledge internalization。给定有限的文档集合和文档派生的问题，模型必须回答保留的问题，而无需在推理时接收源文档。我们在通用语料库（CC）上评估这个问题. Given a bounded document collection and document-derived questions, the model must answer held-out questions without receiving the source documents at inference time. We evaluate this problem on Common Corpus (CC)(24)和CCIand CCI(1)，并在整篇论文中使用缩写 CC 和 CCI。, and use the abbreviations CC and CCI throughout the paper.

最简单的解决方案是对源自文档的问答对进行监督微调 (SFT)。这种方法为模型提供了与测试时相同的输入输出格式，但学习信号很稀疏：只有 QA 生成器选择的事实才会造成损失。持续预训练 (CPT) 提供更密集的文档文本暴露The simplest solution is supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on document-derived question-answer pairs. This approach gives the model the same input-output format that it will see at test time, but the learning signal is sparse: only the facts selected by the QA generator contribute to the loss. Continued pretraining (CPT) provides denser exposure to document text(5)，但它并不直接教模型如何回答问题。两条路线还面临一般能力权衡：领域适应可以改善目标 QA 行为，同时降低指令跟踪或广泛的基准性能，这是一种灾难性遗忘的形式, but it does not directly teach the model how to answer questions. Both routes also face a general-capability trade-off: domain adaptation can improve target QA behavior while degrading instruction following or broad benchmark performance, a form of catastrophic forgetting(13)。因此，文档内化需要域获取和受控恢复。. Document internalization therefore requires both domain acquisition and controlled recovery.

为什么要分三个阶段？Why Three Stages?

这三个阶段的动机是关于文档公开、QA 可访问性和能力保留的三个可测试假设。首先，仅进行质量检查的监督可能会暴露太少的语料库。其次，文档级培训可能无法使所获取的信息可以在问答界面下访问。第三，目标域适应可能会损害一般指令跟踪能力。将这些假设分解为一个微调方案，使得很难确定哪种干预措施可以解释收益或失败。The three stages are motivated by three testable hypotheses concerning document exposure, QA accessibility, and capability retention. First, QA-only supervision may expose too little of the corpus. Second, document-level training may not make the acquired information accessible under a question-answering interface. Third, target-domain adaptation may damage general instruction-following ability. Collapsing these hypotheses into one fine-tuning recipe makes it hard to identify which intervention explains a gain or failure.
