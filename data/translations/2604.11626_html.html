摘要Abstract

大多数视觉生成奖励模型将丰富的人类判断减少到一个无法解释的分数，丢弃了偏好背后的推理。我们表明，在评分之前教授奖励模型产生明确的、多维的批评，可以将它们从被动评估器转变为主动优化工具——以两种互补的方式改进生成器：在训练时，结构化原理为强化学习提供可解释的、细粒度的奖励；在测试时，“生成-批评-改进”循环将批评转变为有针对性的提示修订，从而在不更新任何参数的情况下改进输出。为了在没有昂贵的基本原理注释的情况下训练这样的模型，我们引入了偏好锚定合理化（PARROT），这是一个原则框架，可以通过锚定生成、一致性过滤和蒸馏从现成的偏好数据中恢复高质量的基本原理。由此产生的模型，Most reward models for visual generation reduce rich human judgments to a single unexplained score, discarding the reasoning that underlies preference. We show that teaching reward models to produce explicit, multi-dimensional critiques before scoring transforms them from passive evaluators into active optimization tools—improving generators in two complementary ways: at training time, structured rationales provide interpretable, fine-grained rewards for reinforcement learning; at test time, a Generate–Critique–Refine loop turns critiques into targeted prompt revisions that improve outputs without any parameter updates. To train such a model without costly rationale annotations, we introduce Preference-Anchored Rationalization (PARROT), a principled framework that recovers high-quality rationales from readily available preference data through anchored generation, consistency filtering, and distillation. 5 结论Conclusions

我们提出了We presented理性奖励RationalRewards，一种基于推理的奖励模型，用结构化的、多维的思想链批评取代不透明的标量评分，而 PARROT 是一种变分框架，通过将基本原理视为可从现成的偏好数据中恢复的潜在变量，使这一过程变得易于处理。我们的工作产生了三个主要发现。首先，结构化合理化充当强大的归纳偏差：通过要求模型阐明为什么首选一张图像，8B 参数模型实现了与 Gemini-2.5-Pro 竞争并接近 GPT-5 的偏好预测精度，同时消耗的训练数据比标量基线少 10-20 倍。二、产生的多维理由, a reasoning-based reward model that replaces opaque scalar scoring with structured, multi-dimensional chain-of-thought critiques, and PARROT, a variational framework that makes this tractable by treating rationales as latent variables recoverable from readily available preference data. Our work yields three principal findings. First, structured rationalization acts as a powerful inductive bias: by requiring the model to articulate why one image is preferred, an 8B-parameter model achieves preference-prediction accuracy competitive with Gemini-2.5-Pro and approaching GPT-5, while consuming 10–20× less training data than scalar baselines. Second, the multi-dimensional rationales produced by理性奖励RationalRewards作为基于语义的 RL 奖励，在文本到图像和图像编辑基准测试中，其表现始终优于标量奖励模型和更大规模的通用 VLM 判断。第三，也是最值得注意的，生成-批判-优化循环（一种纯粹的测试时干预，不需要参数更新）在多个基准上匹配或超过基于强化学习的微调，为当前生成器拥有次优提示无法引发的潜在能力的假设提供了经验支持。serve as semantically grounded RL rewards that consistently outperform both scalar reward models and generic VLM judges of larger scale across text-to-image and image-editing benchmarks. Third, and most notably, the Generate–Critique–Refine loop – a purely test-time intervention requiring no parameter updates – matches or exceeds RL-based fine-tuning on several benchmarks, lending empirical support to the hypothesis that current generators harbor latent capabilities that suboptimal prompts fail to elicit.

G.1 局限性Limitations

我们承认这项工作存在以下局限性：We acknowledge the following limitations of this work:

  1. 1.
