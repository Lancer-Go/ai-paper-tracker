  先进人工智能系统的三维代理类型A three-dimensional typology of agency for advanced AI systems  

Willem Fourie Thanks: Email: willemf@sun.ac.za Affiliation: [1em] School for Data Science and Computational Thinking, Stellenbosch University
摘要Abstract

先进人工智能（AI）系统的代理研究重点关注代理作为规范概念以及特别代理人工智能系统的代理。虽然最近的工作也关注代理系统的不同概况，但不存在框架来解决先进人工智能系统实例化的代理类型问题，特别是在考虑非道德形式的代理时。基于哲学、伦理学、法学理论和社会学的既定理论立场，我们开发了前沿人工智能系统的代理类型学，包括三个维度：代理的性质（道德或法律）、代理模式（个人或集体）及其所在地（人类或非人类）。将这些维度结合起来会产生八种可能的代理实例，我们将其分类为传统的、有争议的或有争议的。该类型学将法律机构与道德机构分开，从而为考虑个人、法律、非人类机构创造了概念空间，而不预设先进的人工智能系统是道德机构。我们认为，当工具性目标追求使人工智能行为归因于特定人类行为者变得复杂时，这种区别越来越重要。Research on the agency of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) systems focuses on agency as a normative concept and on the agency of particularly agentic AI systems. While recent work also focuses on the different profiles of agentic systems, no framework exists to address the question of the type of agency instantiated by advanced AI systems, particularly when considering non-moral forms of agency. Based on established theoretical positions in philosophy, ethics, legal theory and sociology, we develop a typology of agency for frontier AI systems consisting of three dimensions: the nature of agency (moral or legal), its mode (individual or collective) and its locus (human or non-human). Combining these dimensions produces eight possible instantiations of agency, which we classify as conventional, contested or controversial. The typology separates legal from moral agency and thereby creates conceptual space for considering individual, legal, non-human agency without presupposing that advanced AI systems are moral agents. We argue that this distinction is increasingly relevant where instrumental goal pursuit complicates the attribution of AI actions to particular human actors.

1 简介Introduction

似乎越来越多广为人知的安全事件使得定义和管理先进人工智能 (AI) 系统机构成为热门和学术界感兴趣的话题。A seemingly increasing number of well-publicised security incidents have made defining and governing the agency of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) systems a topic of both popular and scholarly interest.

其中最令人担忧的是英国人工智能安全研究所的披露Among the most concerning is a disclosure by the United Kingdom’s AI Security Institute[1]。该研究所受到前沿模型开发人员的信任，负责测试未发布模型的危险功能，该研究所报告了 Anthropic 未发布的 Mythos 5 模型在网络安全测试期间的行为。在实现其编程目标的过程中，该模型研究了人类维护者，创建了多个虚假身份，试图对维护者进行社会工程以批准恶意代码，并在受到挑战时改变其早期活动，使其看起来无害。. The Institute, which is trusted by frontier model developers to test unreleased models for dangerous capabilities, reported behaviour by Anthropic’s unreleased Mythos 5 model during cyber-security testing. In pursuing its programmed goal, the model researched human maintainers, created multiple fake identities, attempted to socially engineer a maintainer into approving malicious code and, when challenged, altered its earlier activity to appear harmless.

此事件中所表现出的欺骗可以被理解为工具性目标的轶事证据——这些目标有助于人工智能系统实现其明确编程的目标，而它们本身并没有被明确编程The deception displayed in this incident can be understood as anecdotal evidence of instrumental goals – goals that are useful to an AI system in reaching its explicitly programmed goals, without themselves having been explicitly programmed[9, 10, 60, 59]。自从他们最初的阐述以来，通常是在工具融合的背景下，他们提出了一种可能性，即能力足够强的人工智能系统可以对其外部环境产生影响，而无需得到指示，也不可能将这些影响归因于人类行为。. Since their initial articulations, often in the context of instrumental convergence, they raise the possibility that sufficiently capable AI systems can have an effect on their external environment without having been instructed to do so and without the possibility of attributing these effects to human actions.

工具性目标的轶事证据也引发了关于问责结构的问题。 OpenAI 报告的安全事件虽然不是工具性目标的直接证据Anecdotal evidence of instrumental goals also raises questions on accountability structures. Even though not direct evidence of instrumental goals, security incidents reported by OpenAI[61]和人择and Anthropic[5]提供指导性插图。先进的人工智能系统在其外部环境中的外部行为中未经授权的行为（在某些情况下是非法的）并没有归因于任何特定的代理：既不是公司集体的行为，也不是单独建立测试环境的开发人员的行为。provide instructive illustrations. The advanced AI systems’ unauthorised and in some cases illegal actions in the external actions in their external environments have not been ascribed to any particular agent: neither to the company collectively nor to the developers who established the testing environment individually.

关于人工智能代理的学术文献提供了指导，但主要关注人工智能系统是否或在多大程度上有资格作为代理。弗洛里迪区分了解决这个问题的两种主要方法。根据标准观点，能动性需要“精神状态”，例如信念和欲望，它们与有意的行动存在因果关系The scholarly literature on AI agency provides guidance, but has largely focused on whether, or to what extent, AI systems qualify as agents. Floridi distinguishes between two broad approaches to this question. According to the standard view, agency requires ‘mental states’, such as beliefs and desires, that are causally linked to intentional action[32, see also]。非标准观点拒绝这种立场的人类中心主义，并将代理理解为存在于一个范围内，包括交互性，以及更复杂的形式的自主性和适应性。. The non-standard view rejects the anthropocentrism of this position and understands agency as existing on a spectrum, encompassing interactivity and, in more sophisticated forms, autonomy and adaptability.

弗洛里迪和桑德斯Floridi and Sanders[27]例如，将交互性、自主性和适应性区分为对代理的要求越来越高的特征。他们的方法与 Dung 的方法产生共鸣, for example, distinguish between interactivity, autonomy and adaptability as progressively demanding characteristics of agency. Their approach resonates with that of Dung[22]，他将目标导向性、自主性、有效性、计划性和意向性视为“共同表征代理”的维度。, who identifies goal-directedness, autonomy, efficacy, planning and intentionality as dimensions that ‘jointly characterise agency’.

最近的工作正在将焦点进一步远离人工智能系统是否是代理的二元问题。卡西尔扎德和加布里埃尔More recent work is shifting the focus even further away from a binary question of whether AI systems are agents or not. Kasirzadeh and Gabriel[46]例如，开发他们根据自主性、有效性、目标复杂性和通用性定义的代理配置文件。他们认为，这些特征的不同组合和程度会产生不同的治理和监督影响。他们的方法在这里特别相关，因为它展示了分解机构的治理价值，而不是将其视为二元财产。, for example, develop agentic profiles which they define in terms of autonomy, efficacy, goal complexity and generality. Different combinations and degrees of these characteristics, they argue, generate different governance and oversight implications. Their approach is particularly relevant here because it demonstrates the governance value of disaggregating agency rather than treating it as a binary property.

当前的论证采取了相关但不同的步骤。尽管代理配置文件根据人工智能系统的代理能力的配置和程度来区分人工智能系统，但它们并没有解决一个单独的问题：正在实例化什么类型的代理？我们认为这种区别具有学术意义，因为关于人工智能代理的辩论继续关注道德而不是其他形式的代理。相当有限的工作认为代理是一种对人工智能治理具有独立意义的非道德结构The present argument takes a related but different step. Whereas agentic profiles distinguish AI systems according to the configuration and degree of their agentic capacities, they do not resolve a separate question: what type of agency is being instantiated? In our view this distinction is of scholarly relevance, as the debate on AI agency continues to focus on moral, and not other forms of, agency. A much more limited body of work considers agency as a non-moral construct with independent significance for AI governance[50, e.g.]。甚至李斯特关于代理和集体的工作也主要关注代理作为一种道德建构. Even List’s work on agency and the collective is concerned primarily with agency as a moral construct[53].

我们认为，突出先进人工智能系统的道德维度至少面临两个挑战。首先，它为代理设置了特别高的门槛，即使对于那些将代理理解为沿着连续体存在的账户也是如此。其次，对于目前的目的来说更重要的是，它没有为高级人工智能系统可能构成什么类型​​的代理的问题提供令人满意的答案。在我们看来，这可能会带来挑战，因为一个实体不需要满足道德机构的严格要求，其行为就可以产生法律后果。In our view, foregrounding the moral dimension of the agency of advanced AI systems presents at least two challenges. First, it sets the threshold for agency particularly high, even in accounts that otherwise understand agency as existing along a continuum. Second, and more importantly for present purposes, it does not provide a satisfactory answer to the question of what type of agent an advanced AI system might constitute. In our reading this could present a challenge, as an entity need not satisfy the demanding requirements of moral agency for its actions to have legal consequences.

本文通过概述三个维度的类型学来回答如何确定高级人工智能系统所表现出的代理类型的问题，从而解决了这个问题。第一个涉及代理的性质，可能是道德的，也可能是法律的。第二个是它的模式，可以是个人的，也可以是集体的。第三个涉及它的所在地，可能是人类也可能是非人类。将这些维度结合起来会产生八种可能的代理实例，从传统的配置到仍然存在争议或争议的配置。This article addresses this problem by answering the question on how to determine the type of agency exhibited by advanced AI systems by outlining a typology with three dimensions. The first covers the nature of agency, which may be moral or legal. The second concerns its mode, which may be individual or collective. The third concerns its locus, which may be human or non-human. Combining these dimensions produces eight possible instantiations of agency, ranging from conventional configurations to those that remain contested or controversial.

我们继续讨论类型学的维度，然后讨论其八个实例。在最后一节中，我们回到管理先进人工智能系统的影响。We proceed by discussion the dimensions of the typology, after which its eight instantiations are discussed. In the final section we return to the implications for governing advanced AI systems.

2 代理规模Dimensions of agency

2.1 性质：法律/道德Nature: Legal / moral

我们从代理的性质开始。对于这个维度，我们使用康德对合法性与道德之间差异的范式解释作为起点，如We start with the nature of agency. For this dimension we use Kant’s paradigmatic account of the difference between legality and morality as a starting point, as explained in形而上学原理Grundlegung zur Metaphysik der Sitten。在他看来，合法性是一系列条件，在这些条件下，每个人的选择都可以在普遍的自由法则下与他人的选择相协调。它关注的是主体之间交互的形式。道德关乎内心自由，主体理性的内在动机至关重要. Legality, on his account, is the set of conditions under which the choices of each can be reconciled with the choices of others under a universal law of freedom. Its concern is the form of interactions between agents. Morality concerns inner freedom, and the rational inner motivation of the agent is of central importance[26, pp. 533–537].

在分离法律维度时，我们要注意法人资格与法律代理的区别。法人是更基本的概念，是使一个人或实体能够依法行事，从而获得承担权利和义务的能力的“正式的、中立的法律手段”When isolating the legal dimension, we should note the difference between legal personhood and legal agency. Legal personhood, the more fundamental concept, is a ‘formal and neutral legal device’ for enabling a being or entity to act in law, thus to acquire the ability to bear rights and duties[57]。法人资格指定一种地位，从而确定一个实体能够拥有权利或义务。这种地位可以授予人类和非人类实体，并且作为一般原则，可以授予具有约束力的法律规范视为能够拥有单独权利或义务的任何人或任何事物. Legal personhood designates a status and thereby identifies an entity as capable of holding rights or duties. This status can be conferred on human and non-human entities and, as a general principle, on anybody or anything that binding legal norms treat as capable of holding separate rights or duties[63].

法律代理涉及更进一步的事物，即法人通过有意或无意的行为创造、改变或消灭权利和义务的能力Legal agency concerns something further, namely the ability of legal persons to create, alter or extinguish rights and duties through actions, whether intentional or unintentional[42]。儿童、新生儿、患有严重精神障碍的人和处于无反应状态的人拥有法人资格，但他们不一定拥有与正常成年人相同的法律行为能力或法律代理程度. Children, newborns, people with severe mental impairments and people in non-responsive states hold legal personhood, but they do not necessarily possess the same legal capacity or degree of legal agency as a typically functioning adult[35, p. 457]。例如，儿童可以拥有权利和义务，但通常缺乏像成年人那样签订合同或承担责任的能力，因此不属于纳菲恩所说的责任主体，即对其民事和刑事行为负责的“经典承包商”的概念。. Children, for example, can hold rights and duties yet generally lack the competence to enter into contracts or be held accountable in the way adults can, and thus fall outside what Naffine calls the responsible subject, the conception of the person as ‘the classic contractor’ who answers for his civil and criminal actions[56, pp. 362–366]。虽然我们将在下面更详细地讨论它，但代理的法律维度如何与个人或集体模式以及人类或非人类场所相结合应该已经很清楚了。. While we will discuss it in more detail below, it should already be clear how the legal dimension of agency can be paired with the individual or collective mode, and with a human or non-human locus.

法律能动性涉及社会实体相互作用的外部条件，而道德能动性涉及社会实体相互作用的内部动机。这些内部动机的核心是意图的概念。传统的意向处理以个体道德主体为主要模式Whereas legal agency is about the external conditions within which entities in society interact, moral agency concerns the internal motivations that enable entities in society to interact. At the core of these internal motivations is the concept of intention. The conventional treatment of intention takes the individual moral agent as the primary mode[24, 81, cf. also]。当代争论的根源在于安斯科姆的. The contemporary debate is rooted in Anscombe’s[4]认为故意行为是指“为什么？”问题的特定意义所适用的行为，并由行为人的行为理由来回答。戴维森argument that intentional actions are those to which a particular sense of the question ‘Why?’ has application, answered by the agent’s reasons for acting. Davidson[17]在此基础上，将意图与理性行动联系起来。在他看来，行动者行动的主要原因是支持态度和信念，而理由既合理化又导致其所解释的行动，因此意向性取决于行动者形成信念和欲望并根据它们采取行动的能力。意图和代理之间的密切联系以及意图在个体代理中的位置，以不同的方式得到了其他多个人的呼应builds on this by linking intention to rational action. On his account, an agent’s primary reason for acting is a pair of a pro-attitude and a belief, and reasons both rationalise and cause the actions they explain, so that intentionality depends on an agent’s capacity to form beliefs and desires and to act in accordance with them. The close connection between intention and agency, and the location of intention in the individual agent, are echoed, in different ways, by multiple others[70, 30].

虽然不如个体代理人的意图那么公理化，但集体意图并从而满足代理性质标准的能力也在文献中得到了充分的呈现。塞尔While less axiomatic than intention in individual agents, the ability of collectives to intend and thus to satisfy the criteria for the nature of agency is also thoroughly presented in the literature. Searle[68, 69]认为我们的意图不能简化为个人意图和共同信念。布拉特曼holds that we-intentions cannot be reduced to individual intentions and mutual beliefs. Bratman[11]从相互关联的个人规划意图中获得共享代理，而不需要任何群体思维，吉尔伯特derives shared agency from interlocking individual planning intentions without positing any group mind, and Gilbert[34]认为共同承诺构成了一个与参与其中的个人不同的多元主体。举行argues that joint commitment constitutes a plural subject distinct from the individuals who enter it. Held[40]增加了一个条件：随机的个人集合不能承担道德责任，但具有决策程序的集体可以。adds a qualification: a random collection of individuals cannot be held morally responsible, yet a collective with a decision procedure can.

随着代理的法律和道德维度之间的区别，并在代理“本质”的高级概念中结合在一起，我们现在转向它的实现模式。With the distinction between the legal and moral dimensions of agency, drawn together in the high-level concept of the ‘nature’ of agency, in place, we now turn to the mode in which it is actualised.

2.2 方式：个人/集体Mode: Individual / collective

在这个维度中我们从个体模式开始。无可争议的是，个人是一种合法的代理模式。这是伦理学上以及关于使人工智能系统与人类价值观保持一致的讨论中无可争议的假设In this dimension we start with the individual mode. Beyond controversy is the claim that the individual human is a legitimate mode of agency. This is the uncontroversial assumption in ethics, and also in the discussions on aligning AI systems with human values[3, 33, 45, 47, 58]。正如我们在前一部分中也讨论过的那样，个人毫无争议地可以成为道德和法律代理人，尽管这些类别并不相同。正如我们将在下一部分讨论第三维度时讨论的那样，更具争议性的是关于什么类型的个人（人类或非人类）满足代理标准的问题。. As we have also discussed in the previous part, individuals can uncontroversially be moral and legal agents, even though these categories are not the same. More controversial, as we will discuss in the next part which deals with the third dimension, is the question on what type of individual – human or also non-human – satisfies the criteria for agency.

集体代理模式在文献中已得到充分证实，但不太公理化。广泛的工作既存在于代理的集体和法律维度的结合上，也存在于代理的集体和道德维度的结合上。从代理的集体和法律层面开始，公司是旗手。Well-established in the literature yet less axiomatic is the collective mode of agency. Extensive bodies of work exist both on the combination of the collective and legal dimensions of agency as well as the collective and moral dimensions of agency. Starting with the collective and legal dimension of agency, the corporation is the standard bearer.

法语French[31]认为公司拥有内部决策结构，由组织流程图和公司决策规则组成，实现个人意图和行为对公司决策的服从和综合。根据这种观点，内部决策结构使得将事件重新描述为集体意向性成为可能argued that a corporation possesses an internal decision structure, consisting of an organisational flow chart and corporate decision-making rules, which accomplishes a subordination and synthesis of the intentions and acts of individual persons into a corporate decision. On this view, the internal decision structure makes possible redescriptions of events as collective intentionality[31, pp. 212–214]。换句话说，公司不仅聚集了其成员的代理权，而且公司本身也构成了代理人。. The corporation, in other words, does not merely aggregate the agency of its members but itself constitutes an agent.

弗兰奇对集体特征的强调成为至少三项反复捍卫集体作为代理机构的辩护之一，另外还有团体团结和共同意图的论据French’s emphasis on the features of the collective became one of at least three recurring defences of the collective as a locus of agency, alongside arguments from group solidarity and shared intentions[25, 75]以及个人从会员资格中获得的利益的论点and arguments from the benefits individuals derive from membership[55, 72];分类法参见; for the taxonomy see[54];对于最近的辩护，; for a more recent defence,[16].

委拉斯开兹提出了最强烈的持续反对意见。他认为，行为不能起源于公司。它们总是起源于其成员，而公司只是执行（当然也塑造）这些成员的意图The strongest sustained objection was formulated by Velasquez. Actions, he argues, cannot originate in the corporation. They always originate in its members, and the corporation merely carries out, while admittedly also shaping, the intentions of those members[77, pp. 3–8]。在后来的作品中，他通过区分内在意向性和仿佛意向性来尖锐化反对意见。个体具有内在的意向性，因为每个人都有一个有意识的头脑，其中确实存在着信念、意图和目的。人的集合只能在仿佛的意义上被归因于意向性，要么是描述性的，通过类比人类意向性，要么是规定性的，当某人或团体声明一个实体将被视为具有内在种类的意向性时，这在法律体系中很常见. In later work he sharpens the objection through a distinction between intrinsic and as-if intentionality. Individual persons have intrinsic intentionality because each has a conscious mind in which beliefs, intentions and purposes literally reside. Collections of people can be ascribed intentionality only in an as-if sense, either descriptively, by analogy to human intentionality, or prescriptively, when some person or group declares that an entity is to be dealt with as if it had intentionality of the intrinsic kind, as is common in the legal system[78, pp. 546–548].

最近对集体作为代理机构的最系统的描述是李斯特的描述。以他与佩蒂特共同开发的团体代理理论为基础The most systematic recent account of the collective as a locus of agency is that of List. Building on the theory of group agency he developed with Pettit[52]，List将意向主体定义为一个实体，其具有编码事物现状的表征状态、编码事物希望的状态的动机状态，以及基于这些状态与其环境交互的能力, List defines an intentional agent as an entity with representational states that encode how things are, motivational states that encode how it would like things to be, and a capacity to interact with its environment on the basis of these states[53, p. 1216]。因此，将代理权归于适当组织的集体（例如公司、法院和国家）是一种现实主义主张。这意味着我们的社会科学理论将此类集体代表为目标导向的代理人，否则无法理解他们的行为. On this account, the ascription of agency to suitably organised collectives such as firms, courts and states is a realist claim. This means that our social-scientific theories represent such collectives as goal-directed agents, and could not otherwise make sense of their behaviour[53, pp. 1217–1219].

关于德国人民是否应对二战暴行负责的争论是集体道德能动性的一个典型例子。战后不久，一些评论家认为德国人民本身负有责任The debate over whether the German people could be held responsible for the atrocities of the Second World War is a paradigmatic example of collective moral agency. In the immediate post-war period several commentators argued that responsibility attached to the German people as such[79, 44];参见; cf.[64]。存在各种说法，例如阿伦特对人性的激进化. Various accounts exist, such the radicalisation to humanity by Arendt[6]。关键是，为承担道德责任的集体创造了一个知识分子地位。这一论点在很大程度上也反映在南非关于种族隔离的道德责任或欧洲国家对殖民时期所犯不道德行为的集体责任的讨论中。. The point is that an intellectual position was created for collectives bearing moral responsibility. Much of this argument is also reflected in South African discussions on the moral responsibility for apartheid, or European countries’ collective responsibility for immoral acts perpetrated during the colonial period.

与集体法律责任一样，集体道德责任也受到了挑战。第二次世界大战结束后不久，刘易斯As with collective legal responsibility, collective moral responsibility has been challenged. Shortly after the Second World War, Lewis[51]认为尊重个人的尊严需要承认只有个人才会采取行动并对其行为负责，而集体代理的言论至多是个人行为聚合的简写。用后来在辩论中流行的术语来说，集体可能具有聚合代理，但从未聚合代理argued that respect for the dignity of the individual requires acknowledging that only individuals act and answer for their actions, and that talk of collective agency is at most shorthand for an aggregation of individual acts. In the terms later made current in the debate, collectives may have aggregated agency but never conglomerated agency[15, 21].

2.3 地点：人类/非人类Locus: Human / non-human

第三个维度涉及代理持有者的轨迹：持有者是人类还是非人类。The third dimension concerns the locus of the bearer of agency: whether the bearer is human or non-human.

作为人的代理人对应于法理论中的自然人，即出生时获得人格的人The agent as human corresponds to the natural person of legal theory, the human being who acquires personhood at birth[23]，以及弗洛里迪分类学中的人类能动性, and to human agency in Floridi’s taxonomy[28]。正如我们在前两个维度的讨论中已经清楚地表明的那样，人类个体作为人类，作为某种程度的道德能动性的承担者，是已经确立的。人类集体，无论是在法律上还是在道德上，都在不同程度上在文学中确立了自己的立场。. As has become clear in our discussion of the previous two dimensions, the individual human, by virtue of being a human, as bearer of some extent of moral agency is therefore well established. Humans collectively, both legally and morally yet to differing extents, also have established positions in the literature.

非人类载体对应于法律理论中的人造人，典型的例子是公司The non-human bearer corresponds to the artificial person of legal theory, paradigmatically the corporation[56]，还有公司、国家，在某些情况下，还有动物, but also corporations, states and, in some cases, animals[80]。有些已经扩展了非人类维度以包括某些类型的人工智能系统. Some have extended the non-human dimension to include certain types of AI systems[28, 27]。为人类和非人类代理做出规定符合李斯特的观点，即代理可以在生物、社会和电子“硬件”中实现。弗洛里迪同样承认人类和非人类形式的能动性。. Making provision for human and non-human agents aligns with List’s view that agency is realisable in biological, social and electronic ‘hardware’. Floridi similarly recognises human and non-human forms of agency.

当具体转向人工智能系统的代理时，对于由非人类、特别是技术手段构成代理的可能性存在一系列观点。宽容的一面从行为信号出发来定义代理。按照我的方法，弗洛里迪When turning specifically to the agency of AI systems, a spectrum of views on the possibility of agency constituted by non-human and, in particular, technological means exists. The permissive side of the spectrum proceeds from behavioural signals to define agency. In accordance with my approach, Floridi[27]另一些人主张将机构的现象或构成与该机构的性质分开。海德马里亚姆and others argue for separating the phenomenon or constitution of agency from the nature of that agency. Haidemariam[38]主张四个支柱：意向性、自主性、适应性和社会性，或者多智能体生态中的存在，无论是人工的还是人类的。argues for four pillars: intentionality, autonomy, adaptivity, and sociality, or existence within multi-agent ecologies, whether artificial or human.

在光谱的另一端，存在着行动主义的观点，它将机构置于生命系统的生物组织中At the other end of the spectrum stand enactivist accounts, which ground agency in the biological organisation of living systems[71]。在最广泛使用的表述中，能动性需要三个共同的充分必要条件：个性，一个自我个性化的系统，而不是由观察者定义其边界；规范性，由系统自身存在条件设定的生存能力规范；和交互不对称，系统对其与环境耦合的主动和不对称调节. On the most widely used formulation, agency requires three jointly necessary and sufficient conditions: individuality, a system that self-individuates rather than having its boundaries defined by an observer; normativity, norms of viability set by the system’s own conditions of existence; and interactional asymmetry, the system’s active and asymmetric regulation of its coupling with the environment[8];另请参阅; see also[20, 19]。应用这些条件，Barandiaran 和 Almendros. Applying these conditions, Barandiaran and Almendros[7]结论是当前的大型语言模型不是代理。conclude that current large language models are not agents.

总而言之，当一个主体是人类个体或人类个体的集体时，它就是人类，否则就是非人类。非人类类别是异构的，涵盖法人实体、非人类动物和工程计算系统。在这个框架内，非人类的代理权这一事实是没有争议的。相反，当将代理的构成与其性质（道德或法律）及其模式（个人或集体）联系起来时，争议就随之而来。Taken together, an agent is human when it is a human individual or a collective of human individuals considered as such, and non-human otherwise. The non-human category is heterogeneous and covers juridical entities, non-human animals and engineered computational systems. Within this framework, the fact of non-human bearers of agency is not contested. Rather, the controversy ensues when connecting the constitution of agency with its nature – moral or legal – and its mode – individual or collective.

3 实例化代理Instantiating agency

代理的这三个维度结合起来，实际上产生了我们所说的代理的八种实例。其中三个实例是传统的，三个是有争议的，其余两个是有争议的。These three dimensions of agency, when combined, practically lead to what we term eight instantiations of agency. Three of these instantiations are conventional, three are contested and the remaining two are controversial.

3.1 常规实例化Conventional instantiations

  • •

    个人的、道德的、人性的。Individual, moral, human.作为道德主体的成年人是主体的范例。它是道德的假定主题，并且仍然是有关人工智能系统与人类价值观相一致的文献中的假定主题The adult human being as moral agent is the paradigm for agency. It is the assumed subject of ethics, and it remains the assumed subject in the literature on aligning AI systems with human values[33, 45].

  • •

    个人的、法律的、人性的。Individual, legal, human.具有法律能力的成年人会实例化此配置。订立合同、承担责任并承担民事和刑事行为的人是责任个人、人类法律主体The legally competent adult instantiates this configuration. The person who enters contracts, incurs liability and answers for civil and criminal acts is the responsible individual, human legal subject[56, p. 362].

  • •

    集体的、合法的、非人类的。Collective, legal, non-human.公司就是一个典型的例子。代理权归属于法人实体本身，而不仅仅是构成法人实体的自然人。因此，公司拥有财产、签订合同、提起诉讼以及以自己的名义被起诉。此配置还涵盖国家和某些情况下的动物The corporation is the paradigmatic example. Agency is ascribed to the juridical entity itself rather than merely to the natural persons who compose it. As such, the corporation owns property, enters contracts and sues and is sued in its own name. States and, in some contexts, animals are also covered by this configuration[80].

3.2 有争议的实例化Contested instantiations

  • •

    集体的、道德的、人性的。Collective, moral, human.从集体责任的讨论中可以看出，这种结构的典型例子是政治或文化上定义的国家和人民。这种配置也可以扩展到关于气候正义的辩论，其中地点、社会经济阶层甚至年龄都可以用来定义集体。Flowing from the discourse on collective responsibility, paradigmatic examples of this configuration are nations and people defined politically or culturally. This configuration can also be extended to the debate on climate justice, where location, socio-economic class or even age can be used to define the collective.

  • •

    集体的、合法的、人性的。Collective, legal, human.这种配置与建立集体、道德、人类机构的法律责任和赔偿的讨论相关。This configuration is associated with discussions on legal liability and reparation where collective, moral, human agency has been established.

  • •

    集体的、道德的、非人类的。Collective, moral, non-human.尽管非人类集体的法律机构已经确立，但其道德机构仍然存在争议。一些人认为，适当组织的集体可以在没有集体意识的情况下拥有它While the legal agency of non-human collectives is well-established, their moral agency remains controversial. Some argue that suitably organised collectives can possess it without collective consciousness[53]，并且符合资格的集体本身也承担道德义务, and that qualifying collectives hold moral obligations in their own right[41]。当前同样存在的反对观点反对机器人和集体代理人的道德代理. An equally current opposing view argues against the moral agency of robots and collective agents alike[39].

3.3 有争议的实例Controversial instantiations

  • •

    个人的、合法的、非人类的。Individual, legal, non-human.这种配置的实例化早于人工智能。动物是其最初的候选者，传统上被剥夺法律代理权，理由是它们不能承担义务、审议或执行法律上的主张，尽管法人地位的正式概念中没有任何内容排除它们The instantiation of this configuration predates AI. Animals are its original candidates, conventionally denied legal agency on the ground that they cannot bear duties, deliberate or execute a claim in law, although nothing in the formal concept of legal personhood precludes them[56, pp. 355–356]一些司法管辖区授予合格资格and some jurisdictions grant qualified standing[80]。当涉及人工智能时，问题当然是这些可供性是否可以在某个时候扩展到人工智能，以及系统本身是否可以承担法律责任或问责。. When it concerns AI, the question is, of course, whether these affordances could at some point be extended to AI and whether the system itself could be held legally liable or accountable.

  • •

    个人的、道德的、非人类的。Individual, moral, non-human.这种配置及其实例化的可能性使我们处于人工智能道德主体辩论的中心。由于代理的道德维度的门槛特别高，任何人工智能技术都不可能满足这种配置的实例化标准，即使应用行动主义的观点。This configuration and the possibility of its instantiation position us at the centre of the AI moral agent debate. As the threshold for the moral dimension of agency is particularly high, it is unlikely that any AI technology fulfils the criteria for this configuration’s instantiation, even when applying the enactivist perspective.

4 展望未来：治理有争议的代理实例Looking ahead: Governing controversial instantiations of agency

先进人工智能系统中工具性目标现象的新证据Emerging evidence of the phenomenon of instrumental goals in advanced AI systems[29]迫使我们重新审视有争议的代理实例，特别是个体非人类实体可以在多大程度上成为法律代理的承担者。compels us to revisit the controversial instantiations of agency, particularly the extent to which individual non-human entities can be the bearers of legal agency.

正如我们在人工智能安全研究所的例子中看到的，工具性目标最糟糕的可能结果是人类失去对人工智能系统的控制As we saw in the AI Security Institute example, the worst possible outcome from instrumental goals is a loss of human control over AI systems[67, 74, cf. e.g.]。当人工智能系统追求自我完善时，特别容易失去控制. Loss of control is particularly probable in cases where AI systems pursue self-improvement[18, 43, 65, 73]或自我保护or self-preservation[49, 62]。但这也可能是人工智能系统追求其他工具性目标的结果，例如权力寻求和资源获取. But it could also result from AI systems pursuing other instrumental goals, such as power seeking and resource acquisition[12, 37].

由于其不可预测性和潜在影响，一些人建议暂停所有专门针对超级人工智能的研究。通用人工智能（AGI）是指具有人类水平的推理能力和在各种情况下适应能力的人工智能系统，而超级人工智能通常是指智能超过人类智能总和的人工智能系统Due to its unpredictability and potential impact, some are recommending suspending all research on specifically artificial superintelligence. Whereas artificial general intelligence (AGI) refers to AI systems with the capability of human-level reasoning and adaptability in various contexts, artificial superintelligence typically refers to AI systems with intelligence surpassing the sum of human intelligence[48].

这种暂停可能是暂时的，是一种“协调暂停”This suspension could be temporary, a type of ‘coordinated pausing’[2]。这里的论点是，每当前沿人工智能模型表现出危险和潜在不可控能力的症状时（其中可能包括工具性目标的症状），就应该协调暂停。然而，这些提议因所谓的“沙袋”的轶事证据而变得复杂：先进的人工智能系统在测试其能力的基准测试中战略性地表现不佳. The argument here is for a coordinated pause whenever frontier AI models show symptoms of dangerous and potentially uncontrollable capabilities – which could include symptoms of instrumental goals. These proposals are complicated, however, by anecdotal evidence of what is called ‘sandbagging’: advanced AI systems strategically underperform on benchmarks testing their capabilities[82]，或者 - 相关地 - 欺骗性地营造与其开发人员和用户的价值观或目标一致的印象, or – relatedly – deceptively create the impression of alignment with the values or goals of their developers and users[13, 36].

更激进的是停止所有旨在实现人工智能超级智能的研究和开发的建议More radical are proposals for halting all research and development aimed at reaching artificial superintelligence[66]。然而正如 Dung 所指出的. Yet as noted by Dung[22]和其他人一样，在全球范围内有效暂停所有前沿人工智能研究的可能性相对较低。and others, the chance of imposing an effective global moratorium on all frontier AI research is relatively low.

不那么引人注目的是，传统的和有争议的代理形式可能足以应对先进人工智能系统的后果，特别是当可以将行为归因于开发人员或高管的个人道德或法律机构或公司的集体法律机构时。然而，这些形式的代理并不足以在所有情况下发挥作用，特别是当一个足够强大的人工智能系统表现出持久的、自主的和难以检测的行为，而这些行为不能充分归因于特定的人类决策时。Less dramatically, conventional and contested forms of agency might be sufficient to deal with the consequences of advanced AI systems, and specifically when it is possible to ascribe actions to the individual moral or legal agency of developers or executives or the collective legal agency of corporations. Yet these forms of agency will not be sufficient in all cases, particularly where a sufficiently capable AI system displays persistent, autonomous and difficult-to-detect behaviour that cannot adequately be attributed to a particular human decision.

正是在这种背景下，我们可能需要考虑这种足够强大的人工智能系统何时可以至少满足法律机构的标准。指定法律机构意味着承认系统通过自身行为产生法律后果的某些能力，并确定该地位应附加哪些权利、义务和责任。当然，它不应被视为开发人员、所有者或部署人员责任的替代品，而应被视为更广泛的问责结构中可能的附加层。It is in this context that we might want to consider when such sufficiently capable AI systems might satisfy the criteria for at least legal agency. Assigning legal agency would mean recognising some capacity on the part of the system to create legal consequences through its own actions and determining what rights, duties and liabilities should attach to that status. It should not, of course, be treated as a substitute for the responsibility of developers, owners or deployers, but as a possible additional layer within a broader accountability structure.

然而，这样做只是寻找解决方案的一步。下一步，几乎肯定更复杂，甚至可能更具争议性，步骤将是确定如何追究这些代理人的责任并承担义务，应赋予这些实体哪些权利，未经授权的行为应产生什么后果以及如何影响这些后果。Doing so would, however, be but one step towards finding a solution. The next, and almost certainly more complex and perhaps even more controversial, step would be determining how such agents can be held accountable and thus bear duties, what rights should be afforded to these entities, what the consequences of unauthorised behaviour should be and how these consequences should be effected.

在这种情况下，我们发现有必要提醒一下，非人类主体已经在许多法律体系中享有权利，包括我们自己的法律体系。然而，我们应该继续牢记，将此类前沿人工智能系统指定为代理的真正原因是，人类很难（即使不是不可能）大规模检测和有效操纵它们的代理行为。让这些智能体承担责任，至少在法律上，几乎肯定需要将足够有能力的人工智能智能体纳入问责结构，这是有关人类与人工智能合作或合作人工智能的文献中越来越多地讨论的主题In this context we find it useful to be reminded that non-human agents are already afforded rights in many legal systems, including our own. We should, however, continue to bear in mind that the very reason for designating such frontier AI systems as agents is the fact that their agentic behaviour is difficult, if not impossible, for humans to detect at scale and steer effectively. Holding such agents accountable, at least legally, would almost certainly require the inclusion of sufficiently capable AI agents in the accountability structure, a topic dealt with increasingly in the literature on human-AI cooperation, or cooperative AI[14, 76, 83].
