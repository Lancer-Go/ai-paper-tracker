摘要Abstract

高分辨率数值天气预报（NWP）系统中的预报误差通常与未解决的行星边界层（PBL）过程、对流、地形引起的环流和其他垂直结构的大气现象有关。先前的工作表明，长短期记忆（LSTM）网络可以使用介观网观测成功预测高分辨率快速刷新（HRRR）模型中的预报误差，但我们认为性能下降与复杂的垂直大气演化时期有关。为了解决这一限制，我们开发了一种混合 LSTM-Vision Transformer (LSTM-ViT) 框架，该框架将表面观测的时间序列学习与纽约州 Mesonet 剖面仪网络的大气剖面相结合。 LSTM-ViT 框架经过训练，可以预测各个 mesonet 站的 HRRR 每小时降水量、10 米风速和 2 米温度预报误差。在所有三个预测器中，相对于基线 LSTM 架构，结合剖面仪导出的大气结构提高了预测误差预测技能，最大的收益发生在较短的预测提前期和增强的 PBL 活动期间。降水预报误差的改进尤其明显，其中 LSTM-ViT 框架的预测能力相对于基线 LSTM 大约提高了一倍，同时更好地捕获对流驱动的误差演变并减少与 PBL 过程相关的退化。这些结果表明，将时间序列学习与垂直通知注意机制相结合，为改进业务 NWP 系统中的预报误差预测提供了一条具有物理意义的途径。我们的研究为预测​​者提供了有关模型偏差和预测置信度的增强指导。Forecast errors in high-resolution numerical weather prediction (NWP) systems are often linked to unresolved planetary boundary layer (PBL) processes, convection, terrain-induced circulations, and other vertically structured atmospheric phenomena. Previous work demonstrated that Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks can successfully predict forecast errors in the High-Resolution Rapid Refresh (HRRR) model using mesonet observations, but we believe performance degradation is linked to periods of complex vertical atmospheric evolution. To address this limitation, we develop a hybrid LSTM–Vision Transformer (LSTM–ViT) framework that combines temporal sequence learning from surface observations with atmospheric profiles from the New York State Mesonet profiler network. The LSTM–ViT framework is trained to predict HRRR hourly precipitation, 10-m wind speed, and 2-m temperature forecast errors at individual mesonet stations. Across all three predictors, incorporation of profiler-derived atmospheric structure improves forecast-error prediction skill relative to the baseline LSTM architecture, with the largest gains occurring at shorter forecast lead times and during periods of enhanced PBL activity. Improvements are particularly pronounced for precipitation forecast error, where the LSTM-ViT framework achieves approximately a twofold increase in predictive skill relative to the baseline LSTM while better capturing convectively driven error evolution and reducing degradation associated with PBL processes. These results demonstrate that combining temporal sequence learning with vertically informed attention mechanisms provides a physically meaningful pathway for improving forecast-error prediction in operational NWP systems. 3 型号Model

LSTM-ViT 架构结合了两种互补的建模范式，可解决预测误差预测的不同方面，并且，尽管两种架构都对时间顺序数据进行操作，但它们具有不同的目的。 LSTM 对地面观测和 NWP 预报的时间演变进行建模，保留预报误差预测的顺序和因果结构。相比之下，ViT 分析垂直解析的 MWR 剖面，使用注意机制来表示 PBL 内不同高度和时间观测值之间的关系。然后，该模型将所得的潜在表示组合成大气状态的统一描述。最后，LSTM 解码器使用这种融合表示来生成整个预测范围内的预测误差预测。这种设计保留了原始 LSTM 框架的时间预测能力，同时融入了仅靠表面观测无法提供的低层大气的垂直结构和演化信息。The LSTM-ViT architecture combines two complementary modeling paradigms that address distinct aspects of forecast-error prediction, and, although both architectures operate on temporally ordered data, they serve different purposes. The LSTM models the temporal evolution of surface observations and NWP forecasts, preserving the sequential and causal structure of forecast error prediction. In contrast, the ViT analyzes vertically resolved MWR profiles, using attention mechanisms to represent relationships among observations at different altitudes and times within the PBL. The model then combines the resulting latent representations into a unified description of the atmospheric state. Finally, an LSTM decoder uses this fused representation to generate forecast-error predictions across the forecast horizon. This design preserves the temporal forecasting capabilities of the original LSTM framework while incorporating information about the vertical structure and evolution of the lower atmosphere that surface observations alone cannot provide.

Unlike the broader regional structures evident in the LSTM framework, the LSTM-ViT architecture appears more sensitive to localized PBL evolution, terrain interactions, and mesoscale variability. Overall, these findings highlight both the promise and complexity of vertically-informed ML frameworks for wind forecast-error prediction in geographically and dynamically complex environments.

5 总结和结论Summary and Conclusions

我们使用 NYSM Profiler 和 Standard 网络的观测数据为每个 mesonet 站和目标变量训练独立的混合 LSTM-ViT 模型，以预测 HRRR 每小时降水量、10 米风速和 2 米温度预报误差。模型根据 2018-2023 年的数据进行训练，并根据 2024-2025 年的数据进行评估。我们采用以异常值为中心的损失函数来改善罕见、高影响事件的表示，并使用 RMSE、MAE 和平均误差评估性能，并根据地理位置、一天中的时间、季节和预测提前时间进行额外分析。We trained independent hybrid LSTM-ViT models for each mesonet station and target variable using observations from the NYSM Profiler and Standard networks to predict HRRR hourly precipitation, 10-m wind speed, and 2-m temperature forecast errors. Models were trained on data from 2018–2023 and evaluated on data from 2024–2025. We employed an outlier-focused loss function to improve representation of rare, high-impact events and evaluated performance using RMSE, MAE, and mean error, with additional analyses by geography, time of day, season, and forecast lead time.

(2025)，预测误差预测技能表现出连贯的空间和时间模式，在与复杂的中尺度和 PBL 过程相关的时期通常会出现性能下降。尽管这些关系没有建立因果关系，但它们与地形、对流和 PBL 演化导致预测误差复杂性的解释是一致的。相对于独立的 LSTM，LSTM-ViT 框架在受复杂垂直大气过程影响的环境中表现出的退化要少得多。最大的改进发生在更短的预测提前期，此时观测到的垂直大气结构仍然最能代表未来的预测环境。随着前置时间的增加，大气层与观测到的剖面仪状态的距离越来越远，从而降低了 MWR 观测值的预测价值，并削弱了 LSTM-ViT 架构的相对优势。与这种行为一致，站级统计分析表明，LSTM-ViT 框架在短到中范围的交付时间内比独立 LSTM 提供了统计上显着的改进，而在最长的交付时间内差异变得更小并且通常不具有统计显着性。, forecast-error prediction skill exhibits coherent spatial and temporal patterns, with reduced performance often occurring during periods associated with complex mesoscale and PBL processes. Although these relationships do not establish causality, they are consistent with the interpretation that topography, convection, and PBL evolution contribute to forecast-error complexity. Relative to the standalone LSTM, the LSTM-ViT framework exhibits substantially less degradation in environments influenced by complex vertical atmospheric processes. The largest improvements occur at shorter forecast lead times, where the observed vertical atmospheric structure remains most representative of the future forecast environment. As lead time increases, the atmosphere evolves farther from the observed profiler state, reducing the predictive value of the MWR observations and diminishing the relative advantage of the LSTM-ViT architecture. Consistent with this behavior, station-level statistical analyses indicate that the LSTM-ViT framework provides a statistically significant improvement over the standalone LSTM at short- to-intermediate-range lead times, whereas differences become smaller and are generally not statistically significant at the longest lead times.

LSTM-ViT 降水误差预测在大多数性能指标上始终优于 LSTM 基线。特别是，LSTM-ViT 模型可以产生更真实的负误差预测，并在对流事件期间表现出较低的性能下降。然而，该框架在错误预测方面保留了不对称性，正错误通常比负错误更准确地捕获。第二个限制是时间位移。尽管 LSTM-ViT 模型经常识别预测错误事件的发生，但预测经常会发生一到两个预测小时的偏差。这种偏差会导致 RMSE 和 MAE 增大，特别是在交货时间较长的情况下，而输出变异性的增加会进一步导致性能下降。减少时间偏移和提高预测一致性是未来工作的重要方向。LSTM-ViT precipitation-error prediction consistently improves upon the LSTM baseline across most performance metrics. In particular, the LSTM-ViT model produces more realistic negative-error forecasts and exhibits reduced performance degradation during convective events. However, the framework retains an asymmetry in error prediction, with positive errors generally captured more accurately than negative errors. A second limitation is temporal displacement. Although the LSTM-ViT model often identifies the occurrence of forecast-error events, predictions are frequently shifted by one to two forecast hours. This misalignment inflates RMSE and MAE, particularly at longer lead times, while increasing output variability further contributes to performance degradation. Reducing temporal offsets and improving forecast consistency represent important directions for future work.

LSTM-ViT 风误差预测与基线 LSTM 框架相比表现出适度但一致的改进，特别是在较短的预测周期内。 LSTM-ViT 模型保留了一些与每日 PBL 演化和过渡期相关的性能下降；然而，与 LSTM 框架中观察到的特征相比，这些特征更加局部化，并且表现出不同的幅度和偏差结构。这种空间异质性可能反映了预测近地表风强度的固有困难，其中地形效应、湍流混合和中尺度变化强烈影响预测误差的演变。尽管合并垂直解析的热力学信息可以提高预测能力，但与降水和温度域相比，风预报误差似乎不太受可用的MWR观测结果的严格限制。包含垂直解析的风廓线和空间上更一致的热力学观测可能会进一步提高性能。尽管如此，LSTM-ViT 框架持续改进了 HRRR 和基线 LSTM，特别是在较短的交付时间内，这表明它捕获了导致近地表风预报误差的垂直大气过程。LSTM-ViT wind-error predictions demonstrate modest but consistent improvements over the baseline LSTM framework, particularly at shorter forecast lead times. The LSTM-ViT model retains some of the performance degradation associated with diurnal PBL evolution and transition periods; however, these signatures are more localized and exhibit different magnitudes and bias structures than those observed in the LSTM framework. This spatial heterogeneity likely reflects the inherent difficulty of predicting near-surface wind magnitude, where terrain effects, turbulent mixing, and mesoscale variability strongly influence forecast-error evolution. Although incorporating vertically resolved thermodynamic information improves predictive skill, wind forecast error appears less strongly constrained by the available MWR observations than the precipitation and temperature domains. Inclusion of vertically resolved wind profiles and more spatially consistent thermodynamic observations would likely further improve performance. Nevertheless, the LSTM-ViT framework consistently improves upon both the HRRR and baseline LSTM, particularly at shorter lead times, suggesting that it captures vertical atmospheric processes that contribute to near-surface wind forecast error.

LSTM-ViT 温度误差预测表现出比 LSTM 基线在空间和时间上更加一致的聚合模式。尽管两个框架之间的整体性能大致相当，但 LSTM-ViT 模型似乎对白天 PBL 翻转和太阳辐射驱动的变异性不太敏感，而在夜间条件下表现出更大的退化。这种夜间技能的减少可能反映了日落后近地表热力学的复杂性增加，此时局部热源和热汇、稳定边界层和逆温变得更加重要。这些过程仍然难以通过 NYSM 剖面仪观测来解决，并且可能会引入偏差，导致夜间模式的模型准确性降低。LSTM-ViT temperature-error predictions exhibit more spatially and temporally coherent aggregate patterns than the LSTM baseline. Although overall performance between the two frameworks is broadly comparable, the LSTM-ViT model appears less sensitive to daytime PBL overturning and solar-radiation-driven variability, while exhibiting greater degradation under nocturnal conditions. This reduced nighttime skill likely reflects the increased complexity of near-surface thermodynamics after sunset, when local heat sources and sinks, stable boundary layers, and temperature inversions become more important. These processes remain difficult to resolve with NYSM profiler observations and may introduce biases that contribute to reduced model accuracy in nocturnal regimes.

这些结果表明，所提出的 LSTM-ViT 框架在较短的预测周期中最为有效，在预测误差预测的幅度和方向精度方面始终优于基线 LSTM。这一优势在快速变化的大气状况中尤其明显，其中垂直解析的 PBL 信息改善了近期预报误差增长和变化的表示。These results suggest that the proposed LSTM-ViT framework is most effective at shorter forecast lead times, where it consistently outperforms the baseline LSTM in both the magnitude and directional accuracy of forecast-error prediction. This advantage is particularly evident in rapidly evolving atmospheric regimes, where vertically resolved PBL information improves representation of near-term forecast-error growth and variability.

在较长的交付时间内，E25 中探索的 LSTM 框架通常在纠正系统 HRRR 偏差方面表现出更稳定的技能，特别是对于持久性和空间相干的错误结构。因此，这两种架构表现出互补的优势：LSTM-ViT 框架可以在更短的交付时间内更好地解决局部和动态演变的过程，而 LSTM 在延长的交付时间内更有效地捕获更广泛的和暂时持续的预测偏差。这些发现表明，垂直信息架构和序列学习方法在用作统一预测误差预测框架的补充组件而不是作为竞争方法时，可以提供最大的操作价值。At longer lead times, the LSTM framework explored in E25 often demonstrates more stable skill in correcting systematic HRRR biases, particularly for persistent and spatially coherent error structures. Consequently, the two architectures exhibit complementary strengths: the LSTM-ViT framework better resolves localized and dynamically evolving processes at shorter lead times, whereas the LSTM more effectively captures broader-scale and temporally persistent forecast biases at extended lead times. These findings suggest that vertically informed architectures and sequence-learning approaches may provide the greatest operational value when used as complementary components of a unified forecast-error prediction framework rather than as competing methodologies.

尽管 LSTM-ViT 框架超越了 LSTM 基线，但仍然存在重要的局限性。未来的工作应侧重于为两种架构开发基于贝叶斯神经网络 (BNN) 的不确定性校准和可靠性 (UCR) 框架，实现预测误差预测的概率特征，并为预测者提供可操作的置信度测量和确定性指导Although the LSTM-ViT framework advances beyond the LSTM baseline, important limitations remain. Future work should focus on developing a Bayesian Neural Network (BNN)-based Uncertainty Calibration and Reliability (UCR) framework for both architectures, enabling probabilistic characterization of forecast-error predictions and providing forecasters with actionable measures of confidence alongside deterministic guidance(Mansfield and Christensen, 2026; Clare et al., 2022).

本研究的另一个局限性是需要提高整个建模框架的训练稳定性。由于模型输出错误或不稳定，一些站点被排除在分析之外，这表明数据质量、观测代表性或模型稳健性存在潜在问题。分析器数据不一致、校准问题和大量数据缺失可能会降低某些地点的培训效果。此外，许多受影响的站并不与辐射计位于同一地点，而是依赖于 30 公里半径内站点的观测，这表明观测覆盖范围和仪器放置的限制可能会限制模型准确描述当地垂直大气结构的能力。这些发现强调了在未来的工作中需要改进数据质量控制、扩大观测覆盖范围和更强大的培训策略。Another limitation of the present study is the need for improved training stability across the modeling framework. Several stations were excluded from the analysis due to erroneous or unstable model output, indicating potential issues with data quality, observational representativeness, or model robustness. Profiler-data inconsistencies, calibration issues, and substantial missing data may reduce training effectiveness at some locations. In addition, many affected stations are not co-located with radiometers but instead rely on observations from sites within a 30-km radius, suggesting that limitations in observational coverage and instrument placement may restrict the model’s ability to accurately characterize local vertical atmospheric structure. These findings highlight the need for improved data quality control, enhanced observational coverage, and more robust training strategies in future work.

LSTM-ViT 框架通过联合编码表面观测和垂直解析的 PBL 结构，为 HRRR 预测误差的预期幅度和方向提供特定于站点的实时指导。通过将 LSTM 的时间序列学习与 ViT 中 MWR 衍生的大气剖面的基于注意力的表示相结合，该模型捕获了与 PBL 过程相关的预报误差特征，而这些特征在基于地面的方法或纯序列方法中并未完全表示。此功能可以对预测不确定性进行更明智的评估，特别是在与已知模型缺陷和复杂垂直变化相关的环境中。The LSTM–ViT framework provides station-specific, real-time guidance on the expected magnitude and direction of HRRR forecast error by jointly encoding surface observations and vertically resolved PBL structure. By combining temporal sequence learning from the LSTM with attention-based representations of MWR-derived atmospheric profiles from the ViT, the model captures forecast-error characteristics associated with PBL processes that are not fully represented in surface-based or purely sequential approaches. This capability enables more informed assessment of forecast uncertainty, particularly in environments associated with known model deficiencies and complex vertical variability.

与 LSTM 基线相比，LSTM-ViT 的性能得到了改善，这证明了将垂直大气背景与时间序列学习相结合以进行预测误差预测的价值。结果凸显了混合架构在增强 HRRR 等高分辨率 NWP 系统中的预报误差预测方面的潜力，特别是在较短的交付时间内。该框架为预报员提供了在使用时对预报不确定性进行更实际的评估，并且可以轻松扩展到其他介网-剖面仪网络和数值天气预报系统。The improved performance of the LSTM-ViT compared to the LSTM baseline demonstrates the value of combining vertical atmospheric context with temporal sequence learning for forecast-error prediction. The results highlight the potential of hybrid architectures to enhance forecast-error prediction in high-resolution NWP systems such as the HRRR, particularly at shorter lead times. This framework provides forecasters with a more physically informed assessment of forecast uncertainty at the point of use and can be readily extended to other mesonet–profiler networks and NWP systems.
