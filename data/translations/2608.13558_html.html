摘要Abstract

基础模型的最新进展使人工智能科学家能够自动化日益完整的研究工作流程，从假设生成和代码执行到手稿准备。然而，仅工作流程覆盖并不能提供科学发现所依赖的完整证据。现有系统通常对文本、代码、标签或预先计算的摘要进行推理，从而使智能体无法获得科学决定性的空间、时间、跨渠道和程序关系。我们介绍Recent advances in foundation models have enabled AI scientists to automate increasingly complete research workflows, from hypothesis generation and code execution to manuscript preparation. Yet workflow coverage alone does not provide access to the full evidence on which scientific discovery depends. Existing systems typically reason over text, code, labels, or precomputed summaries, leaving scientifically decisive spatial, temporal, cross-channel, and procedural relations unavailable to the agent. By running idea, rigour, and claim checks in code, the system enforces novelty screening, statistical validity, execution provenance, and numerical traceability. 1 简介Introduction

基础模型的最新进展扩大了自动化科学发现的范围。 推理、代码生成和工具使用方面的改进Recent advances in foundation models have expanded the scope of automated scientific discovery. Improvements in reasoning, code generation, and tool use(OpenAI 2023; Anthropic 2024; DeepSeek-AI 2025)已经将科学人工智能从特定任务系统中转移出来，蛋白质结构预测就是例证have moved scientific AI from task-specific systems, exemplified by protein structure prediction(Jumper et al. 2021)，面向协调假设制定、实验执行和科学写作的代理, toward agents that coordinate hypothesis formulation, experiment execution, and scientific writing(Schmidgall et al. 2025; Yuan et al. 2025; Tang et al. 2025)。 在当前前沿，端到端系统可以从一个研究方向开始，自主产生候选想法、可运行代码、实验图、完整手稿和自我评审. At the current frontier, end-to-end systems can start from a research direction and autonomously produce candidate ideas, runnable code, experimental figures, complete manuscripts, and self-reviews(Lu et al. 2026; Yamada et al. 2025; InternAgent Team et al. 2025; Intology AI 2025; Gottweis et al. 2026)。 因此，这些系统几乎涵盖了整个可见的研究工作流程。 另一项能力尚未解决：人工智能科学家还必须从科学所依赖的异质观察中得出可辩护的问题和主张。. These systems therefore cover almost the entire visible research workflow. A separate capability remains unresolved: an AI scientist must also derive defensible questions and claims from the heterogeneous observations on which science depends.

科学证据以内部结构明显不同的形式到达研究人员手中。 文本、公式、序列和知识图编码符号关系，而显微镜、光谱、波形、音频、视频、3D结构、分布和轨迹则承载空间、时间、跨通道、统计和程序关系Scientific evidence reaches researchers in forms with markedly different internal structure. Text, formulae, sequences, and knowledge graphs encode symbolic relations, while microscopy, spectra, waveforms, audio, video, 3-D structures, distributions, and trajectories carry spatial, temporal, cross-channel, statistical, and procedural relations(Wang et al. 2023; Baltrušaitis et al. 2019; Yue et al. 2024; Li et al. 2024)。 所有这些工件都可以序列化为令牌或通过代码访问。 决定性的问题是哪些关系能够在界面中幸存下来。 标题可以省略局部形态，无序特征向量可以消除时间顺序，一小组标量可以隐藏跨通道的不一致。 然而，当前大多数人工智能科学家管道都是通过文本、代码、标签或预先准备的摘要来公开数据. All of these artifacts can be serialised into tokens or accessed through code. The decisive issue is which relations survive the interface. A caption can omit local morphology, an unordered feature vector can erase temporal order, and a small set of scalars can hide cross-channel inconsistency. Yet most current AI-scientist pipelines expose data through text, code, labels, or summaries prepared in advance(Lu et al. 2026; Yamada et al. 2025; Jiang et al. 2025; Schmidgall et al. 2025; Yuan et al. 2025; InternAgent Team et al. 2025)。 因此，在查询开始之前，代理会继承人类选择的表示。 该界面限制了它可以注意到的异常、它可以提出的假设以及它可以支持的声明。 现有系统越来越. The agent therefore inherits a human-chosen representation before inquiry begins. This interface constrains the anomalies it can notice, the hypotheses it can formulate, and the claims it can support. Existing systems are increasingly工作流程完整workflow-complete，同时剩余, while remaining证据不完整evidence-incomplete.

强大的多式联运模型本身并不能弥补这一差距。 科学的多模式基准通常会提前确定观察结果和问题Strong multimodal models do not by themselves close this gap. Scientific multimodal benchmarks usually fix the observation and the question in advance(Wang et al. 2024; Roberts et al. 2024; Pramanick et al. 2024; Li et al. 2024)，而使用感知的科学代理通常在本地阶段调用它，例如检查生成的图形、评分图、读取界面或监控设备, while scientific agents that use perception generally invoke it at a local stage, such as inspecting generated figures, scoring plots, reading an interface, or monitoring an apparatus(Yamada et al. 2025; Gandhi et al. 2025; Sun et al. 2026; Darvish et al. 2025; Mandal et al. 2025)。 这种用途可以改善孤立的决策，但它们并没有建立一个研究循环，在这个循环中观察可以改变研究的方向。 对于感知驱动的人工智能科学家来说，原始证据应该影响选择哪个问题、如何设计实验、执行后检查什么以及哪些陈述被纳入最终手稿。 更广泛的观察和搜索空间也扩大了数据泄露、重复测试、已知结果后的假设（HARKing）以及不受支持的报告的空间。 因此，全生命周期的感知需要一个控制结构来保留来源并强制执行统计和事实约束。. Such uses can improve an isolated decision, yet they do not establish a research loop in which an observation can redirect the study. In a perception-driven AI scientist, raw evidence should influence which question is selected, how the experiment is designed, what is examined after execution, and which statements are admitted to the final manuscript. A broader observation and search space also enlarges the room for data leakage, repeated testing, hypothesising after results are known (HARKing), and unsupported reporting. Lifecycle-wide perception therefore needs a control structure that preserves provenance and enforces statistical and factual constraints.

2.1 科学发现的自主主体Autonomous agents for scientific discovery

自主主体越来越多地参与科学发现。 早期的框架针对一个领域和一台仪器，执行实验室程序或从衍射图案中选择下一个合成Autonomous agents are increasingly participating in scientific discovery. Early frameworks targeted one domain and one instrument, executing laboratory procedures or choosing the next synthesis from diffraction patterns(Boiko et al. 2023; Szymanski et al. 2023)。 后来的系统扩展到跨学科的真实科学数据，提出的假设随后在实验室得到验证. Later systems widened to real scientific data across disciplines, proposing hypotheses that were subsequently validated at the bench(Mitchener et al. 2025; Villaescusa-Navarro et al. 2025; Swanson et al. 2025; Gottweis et al. 2026; Ghareeb et al. 2026)。 并行浪潮自动化了软件存储库的整个研究生命周期，从想法生成到起草和自我审查的手稿，并以改进的数字指标作为成功的衡量标准. A parallel wave automated the entire research lifecycle on software repositories, from idea generation to a drafted and self-reviewed manuscript, with an improved numerical metric as the measure of success(Lu et al. 2026; Yamada et al. 2025; Jiang et al. 2025; Schmidgall et al. 2025; Yuan et al. 2025; InternAgent Team et al. 2025)，最近将该管道嵌入到协作生态系统中, most recently embedding that pipeline in a collaborative ecosystem(Shao et al. 2025)。 尽管取得了这些进步，但当前的特工只关注一个人已经转化为文本、代码或数字的内容，而忽略了原始证据本身。 因此，几乎每个基准测试都会对系统产生的工件进行评分，而不是对它可以感知的内容进行评分. Despite these advances, current agents attend only to what a person has already turned into text, code, or a number, and overlook the raw evidence itself. As a result, almost every benchmark grades the artifacts a system produces and never what it can perceive(Chan et al. 2025; Starace et al. 2025; Chen et al. 2025; Wei et al. 2025)。 因此，我们建立了一位人工智能科学家，可以直接感知原始观察结果，涵盖 4 个科学证据系列. We therefore build an AI scientist that perceives the raw observation directly, across the 4 families of scientific evidence inTable 2.

Yet they treat perception as a standalone question-answering skill, with a human selecting the observation, posing the question, and judging the response. Even MicroVQA scores hypothesis generation and experiment proposal against fixed choices, and instrument-specific and pathology models remain predictors for tasks defined in advance(Chen et al. 2024)。 科学代理人更直接地应用感知，尽管只是在孤立的阶段，检查他们自己的数据. A few systems let observations inform claim formation(Yao et al. 2025; Zhao et al. 2026)，尽管感知在研究过程中仍然发挥着局部和碎片化的作用。, though perception still plays a local and fragmented role in the research process.Omni科学家Scientist使感知在每个阶段都活跃起来，从提出问题到在完成的论文中奠定主张的基础。makes perception active at every stage, from forming the question to grounding the claims in the finished paper.

2.3 代理推理和编排Agentic reasoning and orchestration

代理推理为自主科学发现提供了控制基础。 早期的方法让单个代理将推理与工具使用交织在一起Agentic reasoning provides the control foundation for autonomous scientific discovery.
