走向分布转移下的校准专家组合Toward Calibrated Mixture-of-Experts Under Distribution Shift

Gina Wong    Drew Prinster    Suchi Saria    Rama Chellappa    Anqi Liu
摘要Abstract

校准将模型的预测不确定性与其经验结果的频率保持一致，对于理解和信任报告的概率非常重要。最近的工作表明，在单个预测变量的层面上实施校准可以提高整体精度和校准，特别是专家混合（MoE）模型显示出强大的经验改进；然而，校准对教育部有帮助的条件尚不清楚。在这项工作中，我们研究 MoE 模型在分布转移下的表现，重点关注路由机制如何与专家级校准相互作用。我们表明，专家校准足以确保在硬路由模型的广泛分布变化下校准整个模型，但不足以校准软路由模型。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一种对抗性重新加权，它可以惩罚分布偏移下路由聚合的校准误差，并且我们证明它可以改善模型类、预测任务和分布偏移的平均和困难数据子集上的精度校准权衡。Calibration aligns a model’s predictive uncertainty with the frequencies of its empirical outcomes and is important for understanding and trusting reported probabilities. Recent work shows that enforcing calibration at the level of individual predictors can improve ensemble accuracy and calibration, with mixture-of-experts (MoE) models showing strong empirical improvements in particular; however, the conditions under which calibration helps MoE are not well understood. In this work, we study how MoE models behave under distribution shift, focusing on how routing mechanisms interact with expert-level calibration. We show that expert calibration is sufficient to ensure calibration of the overall model under a broad class of distribution shifts in hard-routed models, but is insufficient for calibrating soft-routed models. To address this, we propose an adversarial reweighting that penalizes calibration errors of the routed aggregate under distribution shift, and we demonstrate that it improves the accuracy-calibration tradeoff both on average and on difficult subsets of the data, across model classes, prediction tasks, and distribution shifts.

专家混合 (MoE) 是一种广泛使用的架构，它将困难的学习问题分解为更简单、专门的子问题。在 MoE 中，路由模型将输入定向到硬路由下的单个专家，或软路由下的专家的加权组合。这个设计自然支持Mixture-of-experts (MoE) is a widely used architecture that decomposes a difficult learning problem into simpler, specialized subproblems. In an MoE, a routing model directs inputs to a single expert under hard routing, or to a weighted combination of experts under soft routing. This design naturally supports条件计算conditional computation，其中每个输入仅激活一部分参数，允许模型容量增长而无需按比例增加计算量, where only a subset of parameters is activated for each input, allowing model capacity to grow without a proportionate increase in compute[7, 5, 27]。由于不同的专家接受不同输入子集的培训，教育部还鼓励专家专门研究异构数据的不同部分. Because different experts are trained on different subsets of inputs, MoEs also encourage experts to specialize on different parts of heterogeneous data[13, 2, 10]。高效的扩展和专家的专业化共同使教育部成为许多现代大规模学习系统的标准组成部分. Together, efficient scaling and expert specialization have made MoEs a standard component in many modern large-scale learning systems[20, 2, 14].

由于 MoE 将预测分解为共享输入空间的专家，因此人们普遍认为它们对于空间填充方式的变化具有鲁棒性。例如，如果 MoE 包含一位可靠的数学专家和一位可靠的历史专家，那么即使训练数据强调历史问题而测试数据强调数学问题，也很自然地期望整体预测器保持可靠。在这部作品中，Because MoEs decompose prediction across experts that share the input space, there is a common intuition that they would be robust to shifts in how that space is populated. For example, if an MoE contains a reliable math expert and a reliable history expert, it would be natural to expect the overall predictor to remain reliable even if the training data emphasizes history questions while the test data emphasizes math questions. In this work,我们证明这种直觉具有误导性we show that this intuition is misleading：即使每个专家单独可靠，软路由 MoE 的聚合预测器在分布转移下也可能变得系统不可靠。: even when each expert is individually reliable, the aggregate predictor of a soft-routed MoE can become systematically unreliable under distribution shift.

更准确地说，我们表明，当训练和测试之间的路由模式不同时，软路由 MoE 可能会出现校准错误，即使每个专家都根据自己的路由引起的数据视图保持完美校准。原因是软路由将路由权重和专家预测的完整配置折叠为单个聚合置信度值，并且许多不同的配置可以报告相同的值。训练分布的校准仅需要共享置信度的配置More precisely, we show that when routing patterns differ between training and test, a soft-routed MoE can become miscalibrated, even if every expert remains perfectly calibrated on its own routing-induced view of the data. The reason is that soft routing collapses a full configuration of routing weights and expert predictions into a single aggregate confidence value, and many distinct configurations can report the same value. Under such a shift, the same calibrated experts would combine into an aggregate predictor whose confidence no longer matches its accuracy.

For samples that lead to configurations where one expert dominates, the aggregate confidence is essentially governed by a single expert-confidence pair rather than by a soft mixture. MoE、校准和分布偏移。MoEs, calibration, and distribution shift.

大多数关于分布转移下的 MoE 的工作都使用专家专业化来减轻转移，因为多样化或特定领域的专家支持目标域适应、长尾识别和开放集域适应Most work on MoEs under distribution shift uses expert specialization to mitigate shift, as diverse or domain-specific experts support target domain adaptation, long-tailed recognition, and open-set domain adaptation[39, 36, 6]。单独的工作线在组合之前对每位专家进行校准，以便混合物由单独可靠的组件构建而成. A separate line of work calibrates each expert before combining them, so that the mixture is built from individually reliable components[29, 32]。这两条线的工作重点都是使专家可靠，从而使整体混合物可靠；然而，我们发现可靠的专家并不能保证可靠的混合，这促使我们研究路由引起的偏移下聚合预测器的校准。. Both lines of work focus on making the experts reliable so that the overall mixture is reliable; however, we find that reliable experts do not guarantee a reliable mixture, and this motivates our study of the aggregate predictor’s calibration under routing-induced shift.

8 讨论与结论Discussion and conclusion

经过校准的专家的混合整体是否可靠取决于路由器如何组合专家。在硬路由下，混合很大程度上继承了专家的可靠性：每个输入都发送给单个专家，因此校准仅取决于所选专家及其置信度，并且改变每个区域的访问频率不会影响模型的校准。然而，在软路由下，这种情况就会崩溃：聚合将许多路由配置折叠成一个置信值，因此模型看起来可以在训练分布上进行校准，因为配置级错误被取消，但重新加权这些配置的转变会破坏这种平衡。我们的实验表明，对于软路由 MoE，校准每个专家并不能防止混合物的错误校准。相比之下，我们的 Robust MoE 和 Robust Filtered 物镜通过将训练集中在导致校准错误的路由重叠配置上，大大减少了 CIFAR-10H、PACS 和 CivilComments 的移动和模糊子集上的校准误差。Whether a mixture of calibrated experts is reliable as a whole depends on how the router combines the experts. Under hard routing, the mixture largely inherits the reliability of its experts: each input goes to a single expert, so calibration depends only on the chosen expert and its confidence, and changing how often each region is visited cannot impact the model’s calibration. Under soft routing, however, this breaks down: the aggregate folds many routing configurations into one confidence value, so a model can look calibrated on the training distribution because configuration-level errors cancel, but a shift that reweights those configurations disrupts this balance. Our experiments show that for soft-routed MoEs, calibrating each expert does not prevent miscalibration of the mixture. In contrast, our Robust MoE and Robust Filtered objectives substantially reduce calibration error on the shifted and ambiguous subsets of CIFAR-10H, PACS, and CivilComments by concentrating training on the routing-overlap configurations that drive the miscalibration.

实际意义是，教育部校准应在总体水平上并在有意义的子群体或路由变化下进行评估。在标准评估中，平均精度和每位专家校准看起来都是可以接受的，而最终概率在路由器更改配置组合的子集上失败。事后温度调节本身无法纠正布线引起的校准错误，因为单个标量无法修复训练和测试时间配置之间的不匹配。A practical implication is that MoE calibration should be evaluated at the aggregate level and under meaningful subpopulation or routing shifts. Both average accuracy and per-expert calibration can look acceptable on standard evaluations, while the final probabilities fail on subsets where the router changes the configuration mix. Post-hoc temperature scaling cannot correct routing-induced miscalibration on its own, since a single scalar cannot repair a mismatch between training- and test-time configurations.

我们提出的对抗性重新加权是故意保守的。高固有损耗只是路由引起的校准误差的替代，并且一些高损耗示例由于与路由无关的原因而很难实现。这就是鲁棒过滤解决的差距：它将压力集中在路由看起来很重要的地方，这就是它比完全重新加权更好地平衡精度和校准的原因。The adversarial reweighting we propose is deliberately conservative. High proper loss is only a surrogate for routing-induced calibration error, and some high-loss examples are hard for reasons unrelated to routing. This is the gap Robust Filtered addresses: it concentrates pressure where routing looks consequential, and this is what allows it to balance accuracy and calibration better than the full reweighting.

仍然存在一些限制。我们的实验使用由四位专家组成的紧凑型 MoE。我们特意选择了此设置，以将聚合作为错误校准的根源隔离，并排除规模或架构的混淆。我们确定的机制是软聚合本身的属性，而不是任何特定模型大小的属性 - 较大的稀疏 MoE 拥有更多专家，相应地更丰富的路由重叠，这意味着转向重新加权的配置更多，而不是更少。因此，我们预计这种失败将持续存在，并且可能会大规模加剧，并且在大型稀疏和生成性 MoE 上确认这一点是自然的下一步。另外，我们测量校准的两种选择值得注意。我们总结了 ECE 的校准，它对置信区间进行平均，并且可能会错过一个小子组，其误差被其区间的其余部分抵消；我们通过高损耗代理子集来识别脆弱的示例，因为无法直接观察到失败的配置。更直接的方法可以在每个输入处使用路由权重和专家分歧，尽管这需要额外的步骤来估计检查预测的结果频率。Several limitations remain. Our experiments use compact MoEs with four experts. We chose this setting deliberately, to isolate aggregation as the source of miscalibration and rule out confounds from scale or architecture. The mechanism we identify is a property of soft aggregation itself rather than of any particular model size—larger sparse MoEs have more experts and correspondingly richer routing overlap, which means more configurations for a shift to reweight, not fewer. We therefore expect the failure to persist, and plausibly intensify, at scale, and confirming this on large sparse and generative MoEs is a natural next step. Separately, two choices in how we measure calibration are worth noting. We summarize calibration with ECE, which averages over confidence bins and can miss a small subgroup whose errors are offset by the rest of its bin; and we identify fragile examples through high-loss proxy subsets, since the failing configurations cannot be observed directly. A more direct approach could use the routing weights and expert disagreement at each input, though this would require an additional step to estimate the outcome frequency the prediction is checked against.

FGR 是一种训练程序，旨在改进单个模型协变量偏移下的校准。每个训练步骤计算两个梯度：一个来自批次的频率扰动版本的主要分类损失，另一个来自可微校准损失（软 ECEFGR is a training procedure designed to improve calibration under covariate shift for single models.
