1 简介1 Introduction

综合问答（QA）监督已成为教授语言模型的默认方式。模型读取源文本，生成有关源文本的问题或请求，并根据同一文本编写答案。由此产生的交互可以训练适配器、微调模型、提取较小的模型或压缩长上下文Synthetic question–answer (QA) supervision has become a default way to teach language models. A model reads source text, generates questions or requests about it, and writes answers grounded in the same text. The resulting interactions train an adapter, fine-tune a model, distill a smaller one, or compress a long context[self_instruct, cartridges, parametric_rag, active_reading].

这方面的进展很快。自我指导将一些种子任务转化为数据集，以调整有能力的助手Progress along this line has been fast. Self-instruction turns a handful of seed tasks into datasets that tune capable assistants[self_instruct, alpaca, unnatural_instructions]。更强大的生成器和更好的提示提高了合成数据的质量. Stronger generators and better prompts raise the quality of the synthetic data[wizardlm, magpie, synthetic_data_survey]。基于文档的变体现在构建紧凑的工件，可以实现长上下文提示，同时以更低的成本提供服务. Document-grounded variants now build compact artifacts that approach long-context prompting while serving at lower cost[cartridges, cartridges_at_scale]。相同的两阶段循环驱动所有这些：一个模型决定要问什么，另一个模型回答，答案成为下一个模型学习的信号。. The same two-stage loop drives all of them: one model decides what to ask, another answers, and the answer becomes the signal the next model learns from.

我们直接研究这个选择政策，我们发现它是We study this selection policy directly, and we find it is既不统一也不稳定neither uniform nor stable。问题生成首先涵盖每个文档的很大一部分，然后饱和：后来的交互重新访问相同的显着跨度，而不是达到新的跨度。即时多样性并不能完全解决这个问题，因为不同的种子聚集在相同的热点上。 回答阶段并不安全。生成的请求由读取相同不受信任块的模型应答，并且该答案被提取到工件中。当块包含类似指令的段落（拒绝模板、策略块、欺骗的系统令牌）时，应答模型往往会遵循它。. Question generation covers a large fraction of each document at first, then saturates: later interactions revisit the same salient spans instead of reaching new ones. Prompt diversity does not fully fix this, because different seeds converge on the same hotspots. The answering stage is no safer. The generated request is answered by a model that reads the same untrusted chunk, and that answer is what gets distilled into the artifact. When the chunk contains an instruction-like passage – a refusal template, a policy block, a spoofed system token – the answering model tends to obey it.

这些失败是范例的属性，而不是一种实现的属性，因此修复应该针对过程起作用。 我们评估这两个阶段的轻量级程序保障措施。 对于问题生成，多样化的多问题提示和固定的句子目标减少了对最显着跨度的依赖。 为了回答，在读取块之前过滤类似指令的段落可以减少劫持，同时在我们的评估中保留几乎所有干净的文本。 总之，这些结果表明，可以在不改变下游训练循环的情况下降低一些 QA 生成风险。These failures are properties of the paradigm, not of one implementation, so the fixes should act on the procedure. We evaluate lightweight procedural safeguards for both stages. For question generation, diverse multi-question prompting and fixed sentence targets reduce reliance on the most salient span. For answering, filtering instruction-like passages before the chunk is read reduces hijacking while retaining nearly all clean text in our evaluation. Together, these results suggest that some QA-generation risks can be reduced without changing the downstream training loop.

We instead study a passive setting, where the instruction already sits in the untrusted source chunk and the fragile step is generation itself.

3 问题生成作为证据选择3 Question Generation as Evidence Selection

上面讨论的系统在架构和训练目标上有所不同，但共享相同的中间步骤：在一个模型可以从文档中学习之前，另一个模型决定要询问什么内容The systems discussed above differ in architecture and training objective, but share the same intermediate step: before a model can learn from a document, another model decides what to ask about it[parametric_rag, prompt_distillation, automatic_qa_generation, active_reading, synthetic_mixed_training, cartridges]。我们将此步骤视为隐含的证据选择政策。生成器选择的内容成为通过生成的问答进行监督，而未选择的内容可能永远不会被练习。因此，合成数据集可以是流畅的并且对于训练有用，同时仍然过度代表一组狭窄的局部显着跨度。. We treat this step as an implicit evidence-selection policy. Content selected by the generator becomes supervision through the resulting question and answer, while unselected content may never be practiced. Thus, a synthetic dataset can be fluent and useful for training while still over-representing a narrow set of locally salient spans.

这使得问题生成成为一种设计选择，而不是一个中性的预处理步骤。管道可能会改变块采样器、提示、生成的交互数量或多样性约束，但这些选择会影响哪些文档区域进入训练信号。我们使用 Cartridges 的自学设置来研究这种效应This makes question generation a design choice rather than a neutral preprocessing step. A pipeline may vary the chunk sampler, prompts, number of generated interactions, or diversity constraints, but these choices affect which document regions enter the training signal. We study this effect using the self-study setup from Cartridges[cartridges]：采样的块与提示种子配对，引发问题、摘要、结构化请求、用例和其他综合交互，然后是基于文档的响应。此设置为研究合成交互生成提供了一个方便的测试平台，同时还允许我们检查不同的提示公式如何影响文档的哪些部分被选择作为证据。: sampled chunks are paired with prompt seeds that elicit questions, summaries, structuring requests, use cases, and other synthetic interactions, followed by document-grounded responses. This setup provides a convenient testbed for studying synthetic-interaction generation while also allowing us to examine how different prompt formulations affect which parts of the document are selected as evidence.

6 迈向更安全的 QA 生成实践6 Toward Safer QA Generation Practices

前面的部分展示了 QA 式综合监督中的两种失败模式：问题生成器过度选择局部显着跨度，而答案生成器可以遵循嵌入在块中的类似指令的段落（第 1 节）The previous sections show two failure modes in QA-style synthetic supervision: question generators over-select locally salient spans, while answer generators can follow instruction-like passages embedded in the chunk (Section5）。因此，我们测试对生成过程的简单更改是否可以减少这些故障。我们从问题生成阶段开始，其中关键问题是不受约束的锚选择：模型可以自由决定块的哪一部分值得询问。). 7 结论7 Conclusion

我们研究了当语言模型从它生成的有关其自身文档的问答对中学习时会发生什么，并且我们表明此步骤不是中立的预处理。在不可信文本下选择证据并形成答案是一种隐性策略，在这两个阶段都是脆弱的。当选择要询问的内容时，生成器不会均匀扫描。覆盖范围很早就饱和，集中在种子内部和种子之间的一些显着跨度，并遵循本地呈现，因此标题、标记和注入的工件重定向了监督内容。在回答时，编写监督的模型遵循类似指令的段落作为约束性规则，由意图和表面形式而不是严格性驱动，并且在任务冲突下最糟糕，因为更大的能力会受到损害。We study what happens when a language model learns from question-answer pairs it generates about its own documents, and we show this step is not neutral preprocessing. It is an implicit policy that selects evidence and forms answers under untrusted text, and it is fragile at both stages. When choosing what to ask, the generator does not scan uniformly. Coverage saturates early, concentrates on a few salient spans within and across seeds, and follows local presentation, so headings, markup, and injected artifacts redirect what becomes supervision. When answering, the model that writes the supervision obeys instruction-like passages as binding rules, driven by intent and surface form rather than strictness, and worst under task conflict, where greater capability hurts.

这些失败在模型系列和规模中重复出现，因此它们属于 QA 范式，而不是某个实现。它们还屈服于简单的程序修复。外部化锚点可以抑制有偏见的选择，而清理类似指令的跨度意味着注入合规性These failures recur across model families and scales, so they belong to the QA paradigm, not to one implementation. They also yield to simple procedural fixes. Externalizing the anchor curbs biased selection, and sanitizing instruction-like spans cuts mean injection compliance from88%88\% to 13%13\%同时保留几乎所有干净的文本。修复仍然是部分的，并且训练循环保持不变。虽然不受信任的文档驱动综合 QA，但模型学习的内容是由其监督来源决定的，而不是其流畅性。控制该源而不是将其留给发电机。while keeping nearly all clean text. The fixes remain partial and leave the training loop untouched. While untrusted documents drive synthetic QA, what a model learns is set by the source of its supervision, not its fluency. Control that source rather than leaving it to the generator.
