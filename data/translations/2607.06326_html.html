DT-Guard：无推理LLM安全护栏的意图驱动推理主动训练DT-Guard: Intent-Driven Reasoning-Active Training for Reasoning-Free LLM Safety Guardrail

Ant Digital Technologies Ant Group
摘要Abstract

部署在开放世界应用程序中的大型语言模型需要安全护栏，这些安全护栏既能抵御复杂风险，又能足够高效地进行低延迟运行时审核。现有的护栏面临着基于轻量级分类的模型和基于推理的防护之间的实际权衡，基于轻量级的分类模型很有效，但经常与隐藏的意图、模糊的语义和边界安全决策作斗争，而基于推理的防护则提高了判断质量，但引入了额外的令牌生成和推理延迟。 我们提出了 DT-Guard，一种基于主动推理训练、无推理推理范式的内容安全护栏模型。关键思想是在训练期间使用推理监督，同时在推理时仅发出结构化安全标签。 DT-Guard 将安全判断制定为渐进式决策过程，意图→类别→安全，并构建具有意图标签、风险类别、安全标签和结构化推理轨迹的意图驱动数据集。为了进一步提高硬案例的鲁棒性，我们提出了 Rollout-Guided Progressive Hard-Case Optimization (RG-PHO)，它使用多次 rollout 一致性来识别稳定掌握、持续失败和偏好不稳定的样本，并相应地应用有针对性的监督和偏好优化。 在推理时，DT-Guard 直接生成结构化标签，无需显式推理痕迹，保持部署效率。提示侧和响应侧安全基准实验表明，DT-Guard 的平均 F1 分数分别为 0.886 和 0.870。仅使用 4B 骨干网，其双侧平均 F1 就达到 0.878，优于强大的 8B 护栏基线。这些结果表明推理监督可以有效地内化为低延迟安全判别。Large language models deployed in open-world applications require safety guardrails that are both robust to complex risks and efficient enough for low-latency runtime moderation. Existing guardrails face a practical trade-off between lightweight classification-based models, which are efficient but often struggle with concealed intent, ambiguous semantics, and borderline safety decisions, and reasoning-based guards, which improve judgment quality but introduce additional token generation and inference latency. We present DT-Guard, a content safety guardrail model based on a Reasoning-Active Training, Reasoning-Free Inference paradigm. The key idea is to use reasoning supervision during training while emitting only structured safety labels at inference time. DT-Guard formulates safety judgment as a progressive decision process, Intent → Category → Safety, and constructs an intent-driven dataset with intent labels, risk categories, safety labels, and structured reasoning trajectories. To further improve hard-case robustness, we propose Rollout-Guided Progressive Hard-Case Optimization (RG-PHO), which uses multi-rollout consistency to identify stably mastered, persistently failed, and preference-unstable samples, and applies targeted supervised and preference optimization accordingly. At inference time, DT-Guard directly generates structured labels without explicit reasoning traces, preserving deployment efficiency. Experiments on prompt-side and response-side safety benchmarks show that DT-Guard achieves average F1 scores of 0.886 and 0.870, respectively. With only a 4B backbone, it reaches a dual-side average F1 of 0.878, outperforming strong 8B guardrail baselines. These results demonstrate that reasoning supervision can be effectively internalized into low-latency safety discrimination.

RG-PHO converts CoT samples, hard-case SFT, and rollout-contrastive DPO into label-level discrimination capability without adding reasoning-chain generation overhead.

我们呈现We presentDT-卫士DT-Guard，专为低延迟部署而设计的内容安全护栏模型。与在推理过程中依赖于显式思维链或解释生成的护栏方法不同，DT-Guard 遵循, a content safety guardrail model designed for low-latency deployment. Unlike guardrail methods that rely on explicit chain-of-thought or explanation generation during inference, DT-Guard follows a推理主动训练，推理自由推理Reasoning-Active Training, Reasoning-Free Inference范式：训练时使用显式推理监督，而推理仅输出结构化安全标签。这使得模型能够内化复杂安全判断所需的意图识别、风险归因和安全判断，同时保留有效的推理格式。 为了实现这一目标，我们构建了一个意图驱动的安全数据框架，将安全歧视组织为paradigm: explicit reasoning supervision is used during training, while inference outputs only structured safety labels. This enables the model to internalize intent recognition, risk attribution, and safety adjudication required for complex safety judgment while preserving an efficient inference format. To achieve this, we construct an intent-driven safety data framework that organizes safety discrimination as意图Intent →\rightarrow 类别Category →\rightarrow 安全Safety，并介绍, and introduce推出引导的渐进式硬案例优化Rollout-Guided Progressive Hard-Case Optimization。该训练策略使用多转出一致性来识别不同类型的困难案例，并分别通过Hard-Case SFT和Hard-Case DPO提高模型对持续失败和偏好不稳定样本的判断。实验表明，DT-Guard 在提示侧和响应侧安全基准上均取得了一致的增益，优于多个仅具有 4B 参数的 8B 护栏模型。这些结果验证了训练期间的推理监督可以有效地转化为推理时的低延迟判别能力。. This training strategy uses multi-rollout consistency to identify different types of hard cases, and improves the model’s judgment on persistently failed and preference-unstable samples through Hard-Case SFT and Hard-Case DPO, respectively. Experiments show that DT-Guard achieves consistent gains on both prompt-side and response-side safety benchmarks, outperforming several 8B guardrail models with only 4B parameters. These results validate that reasoning supervision during training can be effectively converted into low-latency discrimination capability at inference time.
