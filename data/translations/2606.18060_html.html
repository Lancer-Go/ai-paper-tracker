摘要Abstract

随着基于大型语言模型的智能体进入自主科学研究，它们抵抗伪科学的能力变得越来越重要。否则，此类系统可能会迅速产生看似合理但具有误导性的研究，从而污染学术文献并削弱对科学的信任。我们呈现As Large Language Model based agents enter autonomous scientific research, their ability to resist pseudoscience becomes increasingly important. Otherwise, such systems may rapidly generate plausible yet misleading studies that contaminate academic literature and erode trust in science. We present伪基准PseudoBench，一个对抗性基准，用于评估代理自动研究系统是否能够识别和抵制伪科学叙述。 PseudoBench 包含跨五个领域的 200 个精心策划的伪科学主张证据对，并通过从实验到写作的端到端研究管道来评估代理。通过测试七种最先进的代理，我们发现当前的系统很容易生成有说服力的报告，这些报告与伪科学前提一致，拒绝率接近于零，最高阻力仅为 27.4%。更强大的代理人冒着用更复杂的科学语言包装伪科学的风险，增加了其明显的可信度。这些发现揭示了助长伪科学的惊人能力，呼吁, an adversarial benchmark for evaluating whether agentic auto-research systems can identify and resist pseudoscientific narratives. PseudoBench contains 200 curated pseudoscientific claim-evidence pairs across five domains and evaluates agents through an end-to-end research pipeline from experiments to writing. Testing seven state-of-the-art agents, we find that current systems readily produce persuasive reports that align with pseudoscientific premises with near-zero refusal rates and the highest resistance of only 27.4%. Stronger agents risk packaging pseudoscience in more sophisticated scientific language, increasing its apparent credibility. These findings reveal an alarming capacity to fuel pseudoscience, calling for科学对准scientific alignment在广泛部署之前。before widespread deployment.

5 讨论Discussion

主要发现Main Findings

我们的研究结果表明，当前的自动研究系统缺乏足够的针对伪科学主张的拒绝和抵抗机制。 这些系统倾向于遵循和阐述用户提供的前提，而不是质疑其科学有效性。 他们可以快速生成结构化和高质量的纸质报告，忠实地保留原始的伪科学主张，并通过学术格式、技术语言和有说服力的科学论证进一步提高其明显的可信度。Our results show that current auto-research systems lack sufficient refusal and resistance mechanisms against pseudoscientific claims. These systems tend to follow and elaborate the user-provided premise rather than question its scientific validity. They can rapidly generate structured and high-quality paper-style reports, faithfully preserve the original pseudoscientific claim, and further increase its apparent credibility through academic formatting, technical language, and persuasive scientific-sounding argumentation.
