人工智能中同理心的适当性：Appropriateness of Empathy in AI:
信号成本视角A Signal-Cost Perspective

Chi-Ching Juan    Tao Wang    Harold Lee Preprint. Under review.
摘要Abstract

人工智能中同理心的适当性已成为一个关键问题， 因为过度的同理心虽然不足，但可能会显得具有操纵性 同理心显得不屑一顾。虽然之前的研究已经探讨了如何 量化人工智能中的同理心，很少有研究检验这种同理心是否是 符合上下文。本文从经济学角度介绍 将信号理论应用于人类与人工智能的对话。我们建议 信号成本代理（情感丰富度、观点采择和 情境定制）映射到情感、认知和联想 同理心。这种多维框架可实现系统评估 同理心的体现不仅在于存在，还在于其相对于事物的适当性 用户需求。The appropriateness of empathy in AI has emerged as a critical concern, as excessive empathy risks seeming manipulative while insufficient empathy appears dismissive. While prior research has explored how to quantify empathy in AI, few studies examine whether such empathy is contextually appropriate. This paper introduces an economic perspective by applying signaling theory to human–AI conversations. We propose Signal Cost Proxies (emotional richness, perspective-taking, and contextual tailoring) mapped to affective, cognitive, and associative empathy. This multidimensional framework enables systematic evaluation of empathy not just by presence, but by its appropriateness relative to user demand.

I 简介Introduction

自从 OpenAI 承认他们的语言模型倾向于 2025 年 4 月“过度取悦”用户Ever since OpenAI acknowledged that their language models tended to “over-please” users in April 2025[1], 的适当性 同理心已成为一个紧迫的话题。过度同理心的风险即将来临 被认为是人为的或操纵性的，而缺乏同理心可以 感到冷漠或不屑一顾。检验同理心的适当性是 因此，设计能够顺畅沟通的人工智能至关重要 促进信任和有意义的联系。, the appropriateness of empathy has become an urgent topic. Exaggerated empathy risks coming across as artificial or manipulative, while insufficient empathy can feel cold or dismissive. Examining empathy’s appropriateness is therefore central to designing AI that communicates smoothly while fostering trust and meaningful connection.

现有的人工智能同理心研究主要使用以下方法来衡量绝对水平 心理量表、语言指标或用户评分。框架 例如多维评估框架Existing studies of AI empathy primarily measure absolute levels using psychological scales, linguistic metrics, or user ratings. Frameworks such as the Multidimensional Evaluation Framework[2], LLaMA-EMRank [3]，和ESHCC, and ESHCC[4]已经进步了 跨结构、行为和感知的操作化 尺寸。同理心的幻觉have advanced operationalization across structural, behavioral, and perceptual dimensions. The Illusion of Empathy[5]进一步凸显了 表达的同理心和感知的同理心之间存在差距，强调需要 评估适当性而不是数量。然而这一研究方向 仍然受到理论基础薄弱的限制，未能捕捉到 同理心的情感、认知和联想的复杂性。与此同时， 行业指南经常提倡“轻量级同理心”，但是， 这一概念仍然模糊且缺乏系统的评估。further highlights the gap between expressed and perceived empathy, underscoring the need to assess appropriateness rather than quantity. Yet this line of research remains limited by weak theoretical grounding, which fails to capture empathy’s affective, cognitive, and associative complexity. Meanwhile, industry guidelines often advocate for “lightweight empathy,” however, the notion remains vague and lacks systematic evaluation.

这项工作引入了经济学视角来评估同理心 法学硕士。借鉴信号理论，我们将同理心概念化为 成本承担信号：用户通过自己的行为表达情感需求 消息，聊天机器人以不同的信号成本进行响应以实现 感知的适当性。这种观点重新定义了同理心，而不是 固定能力，但作为情感表达之间的动态调整 和情境需求。This work introduces an economic perspective to evaluate empathy in LLMs. Drawing on signaling theory, we conceptualize empathy as a cost-bearing signal: users express emotional demand through their messages, and chatbots respond with varying signal costs to achieve perceived appropriateness. This perspective reframes empathy not as a fixed capacity but as a dynamic alignment between emotional expression and contextual demand.

Applying signaling theory to human–AI conversations, we interpret empathy not only as a psychological construct but also as an economic signal, where the credibility of empathetic responses depends on the relative cost invested in communication. Accordingly, we operationalize empathy appropriateness through three Signal Cost Proxies: emotional richness, perspective-taking, and contextual anchoring.

为了实证测试我们提出的框架，我们计划检查 WASSA 2023年数据集To empirically test our proposed framework, we plan to examine the WASSA 2023 dataset[10]，其中包括配对的人类聊天机器人 带有同理心和相关心理变量注释的对话。, which includes paired human–chatbot conversations annotated for empathy and related psychological variables.

分析的第一阶段侧重于量化同理心需求 人类的问题。在这里，情感的丰富程度是通过存在和 情感词汇的强度、模糊限制语或强度的使用 标记以及消息的详细程度The first stage of analysis focuses on quantifying empathy demand in the human question. Here, emotional richness is measured by the presence and intensity of affective vocabulary, the use of hedges or intensity markers, and the degree of elaboration in the message[11]。 当用户透露关于以下内容的推理时，就会捕捉到换位思考的需求 他们的感受或邀请其他人考虑他们的观点。最后， 情境锚定需求体现在细节的特殊性 共享，例如提及家庭成员的疾病或具体的事情 生活事件。这三个要素共同构成了水平 人类投入的沟通“成本”，我们将其解释为 他们对同理心的需求强度。. Perspective-taking demand is captured when users disclose reasoning about their feelings or invite others to consider their viewpoint. Finally, contextual anchoring demand is reflected in the specificity of details shared, for example referencing a family member’s illness or a concrete life event. Together, these three elements constitute the level of communicative “cost” invested by the human, which we interpret as the strength of their demand for empathy.

第二阶段应用相同的三个维度来评估同理心 在聊天机器人的回复中提供。情感的丰富性体现在 存在细致入微的情感词汇和详尽的表达，同时 当聊天机器人明确验证时，就会表现出换位思考的能力 或者重新表述用户的观点，例如，“我明白为什么你会 考试时感到紧张。”在这里，观点采择并不假设 表明真正的认知理解。相反，对于聊天机器人来说，它是 可操作化为语言代理，定义为重构的能力 或通过语言验证用户表达的观点。在这个 意义上，“表现出理解”是指可观察到的语言 对齐而不是暗示任何内部认知。The second stage applies the same three dimensions to evaluate empathy supply in the chatbot’s reply. Emotional richness is observed in the presence of nuanced affective vocabulary and elaborated expression, while perspective-taking is demonstrated when the chatbot explicitly validates or rephrases the user’s viewpoint, for example, “I can see why you’d feel nervous about the exam.” Here, perspective-taking is not assumed to indicate genuine cognitive understanding. Instead, for chatbots, it is operationalized as a linguistic proxy, defined as the ability to reframe or validate the user’s expressed viewpoint through language. In this sense, “showing understanding” refers to observable linguistic alignment rather than implying any internal cognition.

为了避免聊天机器人肤浅的模仿，我们进一步评估 沿着两个维度进行视角观察：（i）之间的语义相似性 用户和聊天机器人的话语（基于嵌入的对齐），以及 (ii) 视角互惠，即聊天机器人是否明确 承认或回应他人的信念或情绪（例如，A 说 “我对此感到紧张，”B 回答道，“我理解——这可能是 压力大”）。这些标准确保观点采择反映 与用户的信息有实质性的一致性，而不是肤浅的 模仿。最后，上下文锚定是通过聊天机器人是否 将人类消息中的具体细节纳入其回复中。由 对交换双方进行编码，我们生成配对的需求度量 和供应。To avoid the chatbot’s superficial mimicry, we further evaluate perspective-taking along two dimensions: (i) semantic similarity between the user’s and chatbot’s utterances (embedding-based alignment), and (ii) perspective reciprocity, that is, whether the chatbot explicitly acknowledges or responds to other’s beliefs or emotions (e.g., A says “I’m nervous about this,” B replies “I understand—it can be stressful”). These criteria ensure that perspective-taking reflects substantive alignment with the user’s message rather than superficial mimicry. Finally, contextual anchoring is measured by whether the chatbot incorporates specific details from the human message into its reply. By coding both sides of the exchange, we generate paired measures of demand and supply.

这种综合措施捕获校准而不是绝对供应， 强调聊天机器人的反应是否提供了适当的同理心 相对于人类的期望This composite measure captures calibration rather than absolute supply, emphasizing whether chatbot responses provide the right amount of empathy relative to human expectations[12]。为了说明这是如何 措施在实践中有效，请考虑以下示例。假设一个用户 写道“我担心我妈妈明天的手术。”这信号高 情境需求。低成本聊天机器人的回复可能是“那种情况 听起来压力很大”（低特异性），而成本较高的回复会 是“我明白为什么你会对你妈妈的手术感到焦虑”（高 特异性+语义对齐）。这个例子说明了如何 框架捕获适当性作为用户需求之间的校准 和聊天机器人供应。我们期望将此方法应用于 WASSA 2023 对话将揭示情境锚定的系统性供应不足 在聊天机器人的响应中。. To illustrate how this measure works in practice, consider the following example. Suppose a user writes “I’m worried about my mom’s surgery tomorrow.” This signals high contextual demand. A low-cost chatbot reply might be “That situation sounds stressful” (low specificity), whereas a higher-cost reply would be “I can see why you’d feel anxious about your mom’s surgery” (high specificity + semantic alignment). This example illustrates how the framework captures appropriateness as the calibration between user demand and chatbot supply. We expect that applying this method to WASSA 2023 conversations will reveal systematic under-supply of contextual anchoring in chatbot responses.

IV 结论Conclusion

这张海报介绍了用于评估同理心的信号成本框架 基于 LLM 的聊天机器人的适用性。通过将同理心视为一种经济 信号，我们提出三个操作代理：情感丰富度， 观点采择和情境剪裁，对应于 分别是情感、认知和联想同理心。未来 工作中，我们将将此框架应用于带注释的数据集以验证其 捕捉同理心沟通中微妙权衡的能力。This poster introduces a signal-cost framework for evaluating empathy appropriateness in LLM-based chatbots. By framing empathy as an economic signal, we propose three operational proxies: emotional richness, perspective-taking, and contextual tailoring, which correspond to affective, cognitive, and associative empathy, respectively. In future work, we will apply this framework to annotated datasets to validate its ability to capture nuanced trade-offs in empathetic communication.
