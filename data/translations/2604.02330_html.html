摘要Abstract

视频传播的最新进展使得能够模拟交互环境的“世界模型”得以发展。 然而，这些模型很大程度上仅限于单代理设置，无法在场景中同时控制多个代理。 在这项工作中，我们解决了现有视频传播模型中动作绑定的基本问题，该模型很难将特定动作与其相应的主题相关联。 为此，我们提出了 ActionParty，一种用于生成视频游戏的动作可控多主体世界模型。 它介绍了Recent advances in video diffusion have enabled the development of “world models” capable of simulating interactive environments. However, these models are largely restricted to single-agent settings, failing to control multiple agents simultaneously in a scene. In this work, we tackle a fundamental issue of action binding in existing video diffusion models, which struggle to associate specific actions with their corresponding subjects. For this purpose, we propose ActionParty, an action controllable multi-subject world model for generative video games. It introduces主体状态subject state代币，tokens,即i.e.持续捕捉场景中每个主体状态的潜在变量。 通过使用空间偏置机制对状态标记和视频潜伏进行联合建模，我们将全局视频帧渲染与单个动作控制的主题更新分开。 我们根据 Melting Pot 基准评估 ActionParty，展示了第一个能够在 46 个不同环境中同时控制最多 7 名玩家的视频世界模型。 我们的结果表明，行动跟踪准确性和身份一致性有了显着提高，同时通过复杂的交互实现了对受试者的稳健自回归跟踪。latent variables that persistently capture the state of each subject in the scene. We evaluate ActionParty on the Melting Pot benchmark, demonstrating the first video world model capable of controlling up to seven players simultaneously across 46 diverse environments. Our results show significant improvements in action-following accuracy and identity consistency, while enabling robust autoregressive tracking of subjects through complex interactions.

1 简介Introduction

视频传播模型的出现The emergence of video diffusion models[DDPM, VideoDiffusionModels, Wan21, ImagenVideo, SVD, Sora]已将生成式人工智能的重点从静态内容创建转移到“世界模型”的开发，模拟器能够根据交互动作预测未来的观察结果has shifted the focus of generative AI from static content creation to the development of “world models”, simulators capable of predicting future observations conditioned on interactive actions[bruce2024geniegenerativeinteractiveenvironments, RELIC, ha2018worldmodels]。 通过直接从像素学习环境的潜在动态，Genie 等最近的突破. By learning the underlying dynamics of environments directly from pixels, recent breakthroughs such as Genie[genie3]和GameNGenand GameNGen[GameNGen]已经展示了生成高保真交互式视频游戏的潜力。 尽管取得了这些进步，现有的世界模型通常仅限于具有以自我为中心的输出视图的单代理设置have demonstrated the potential for generating high-fidelity, interactive video games. Despite these advances, existing world models are often restricted to single-agent settings with an egocentric output view[genie3]。 它们接受单个控制信号流作为输入，并且只能控制场景中的单个主体。 因此，这些生成的“世界”无法模拟定义复杂社会环境（例如自动驾驶）的多智能体动态. 5 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 ActionParty，一个用于解决多人游戏模拟中的动作绑定挑战的框架。 通过引入与视频潜伏联合去噪的主体状态标记，我们成功地将独特的、可控的标识符与 46 个不同环境中的各个主体关联起来。 通过注意力掩蔽和空间 RoPE 偏置，ActionParty 解开了主题更新和帧渲染，从而能够同时精确控制最多七个主题。 我们的结果表明，明确的主题基础显着提高了自回归视频扩散设置中的动作跟踪准确性和身份保留。 我们相信我们的研究解决了动作条件视频生成的基本瓶颈，我们希望它能够激发未来对稳健的多主题视频世界模型的研究。In this paper, we present ActionParty, a framework for resolving the action binding challenge in multi-player game simulations. By introducing subject state tokens that are jointly denoised with video latents, we successfully associate unique, controllable identifiers with individual subjects across 46 diverse environments. Through attention masking and spatial RoPE biasing, ActionParty disentangles subject update and frame rendering, enabling precise control of up to seven subjects simultaneously. Our results demonstrate that explicit subject grounding significantly improves action-following accuracy and identity preservation in the autoregressive video diffusion setup. We believe our study addresses a fundamental bottleneck in action-conditioned video generation, and we hope it can inspire future research on robust, multi-subject video world models.
