为了确保 Agentic RL 过程的高保真度，我们实施了两种不同的数据管理策略：自采样策略和先验过滤策略。 前者采用多轮共识机制，其中候选人的资格由目标模型多次推出的 Pass@k 指标决定。 或者，先验过滤策略充当补充的外在质量门，利用高容量教师模型的强大评估能力进行严格验证。 这种双轨方法有效地减轻了训练分布中的噪声，为复杂的策略优化提供了稳定的基础。To ensure the high fidelity of the Agentic RL process, we implement two distinct data curation strategies: Self-Sampled and Prior-Filtered Strategy. Alternatively, the Prior-Filtered Strategy functions as a supplementary extrinsic quality gate, leveraging the robust evaluative capacity of high-capacity teacher models for rigorous verification. This dual-track approach effectively mitigates noise in the training distribution, providing a stable foundation for complex policy optimization.

我们的方法由两个训练阶段组成：冷启动监督微调（SFT）和交错推理强化学习。 为了支持这些阶段，我们设计了两个专用数据管道：SFT 数据构建管道和 RL 数据过滤管道。Our approach consists of two training stages: cold-start Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and interleaved reasoning Reinforcement Learning. 3.3 自适应交错推理的强化学习Reinforcement Learning for Adaptive Interleaved Reasoning

自适应交错推理要求模型在推理过程中自主做出两个关键决策：一是是否采用交错推理范式本身；二是是否采用交错推理范式。其次，何时在所选范式内执行文本推理或调用工具。这种双重自主能力可以通过强化学习得到有效增强。 本章详细阐述了提高模型自适应交错推理性能的强化学习方法。 为了增强这种能力，我们详细介绍了训练数据过滤和奖励策略设计。Adaptive interleaved reasoning requires the model to autonomously make two key decisions during the reasoning process: first, whether to adopt the interleaved reasoning paradigm itself; and second, when to perform textual reasoning or invoke tools within the chosen paradigm. This chapter elaborates on the reinforcement learning methods for improving the model’s adaptive interleaved reasoning performance. To boost this capability, we provide a detailed introduction to training data filtering and reward strategy design.

5 结论和局限性Conclusion and Limitations

在本文中，我们提出了 AIR，这是一个成功解决多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM) 中自主交错推理挑战的框架。 为此，我们设计了由SFT冷启动和RL增强组成的两阶段训练范式来完成模型训练。 与现有的多模式工具使用范例相比，AIR 通过群体约束的奖励机制确保自主执行和长期训练稳定性。 我们在不同数学基准上的实验结果表明，所提出的方法产生了显着的性能提升，为复杂的多模态推理任务提供了稳健的解决方案。In this paper, we presented AIR, a framework that successfully addresses the challenges of autonomous interleaved reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). To this end, we designed a two-stage training paradigm consisting of SFT cold-start and RL enhancement to complete the model training. Compared to existing multimodal tool-use paradigms, AIR  ensures both autonomous execution and long-horizon training stability through a group-constrained reward mechanism. Our experimental results on diverse mathematical benchmarks demonstrate that the proposed method yields substantial performance gains, providing a robust solution for complex multimodal reasoning tasks.

虽然 AIR 通过代码调用实现自主交错推理，并在基准评估中取得了良好的结果，但我们的工作仍然存在一定的局限性，值得在未来的研究中进一步探索。While AIR  realizes autonomous interleaved reasoning via code invocation and attains strong results across benchmark evaluations, our work still bears certain limitations worthy of further exploration in future studies.

    模型和编码能力。Model and Coding Capabilities.AIR 的性能本质上受到其基本模型的固有容量的限制。我们的实验结果验证了代码调用可以作为复杂数值计算任务的有效补救措施。然而，当前的代码工具包在功能上仍然很初级，大型基础模型的代码生成能力仍然有很大的改进空间。同时，基础模型本身的感知和推理能力同样不可或缺。其基础的感知基础为后续的逻辑推演奠定了基础，模型只有具备严密的逻辑推理能力并辅以代码执行工具，才能获得优越的整体性能。The performance of AIR is inherently bounded by the inherent capacity of its base model. Our experimental results verify that code invocation serves as an effective remedy for intricate numerical computation tasks. Nevertheless, current code toolkits remain functionally rudimentary, and the code generation proficiency of large base models still leaves substantial room for improvement. Meanwhile, the perceptual and reasoning prowess of the base model itself is equally indispensable. Its fundamental perception foundation lays the groundwork for subsequent logical deduction, and only when the model is equipped with rigorous logical reasoning and complemented by code-execution tools can it attain superior holistic performance.
