MIFair：交叉性和多类公平性的互信息框架MIFair: A Mutual-Information Framework for Intersectionality and Multiclass Fairness

摘要Abstract

由于其道德复杂性、缺乏通用定义以及需要针对具体情况的偏差指标，机器学习的公平性仍然具有挑战性。现有方法在处理交叉性、多类设置以及更广泛的灵活性和通用性方面也仍然有限。为了解决这些限制，我们引入Fairness in machine learning remains challenging due to its ethical complexity, the lack of a universal definition, and the need for context-specific bias metrics. Existing methods also remain limited in handling intersectionality, multiclass settings, and broader flexibility and generality. To address these limitations, we introduce米兰国际博览会MIFair，基于互信息的偏差评估和缓解的统一框架。 MIFair 提供了灵活的度量模板和处理中的缓解方法，其灵感来自 Prejudice Remover，将群体公平定义为预测派生变量和敏感属性之间的统计独立性，同时与广泛使用的概念（例如独立性和分离）建立等价关系。 MIFair 天然支持交叉性、复杂子组结构和多类分类，并采用基于正则化的训练来根据所选指标减少偏差。其主要优势在于其多功能性：它将不同的公平性要求整合到一个统一的框架中，从而实现一致的基准测试并促进实际采用。 对现实世界表格和图像数据集的实验表明，MIFair 有效减少了偏差，包括以前未解决的多属性场景，同时在所有评估的设置中保持强大的预测性能。, a unified framework for bias assessment and mitigation based on mutual information. MIFair provides a flexible metric template and an in-processing mitigation method inspired by the Prejudice Remover, defining group fairness as statistical independence between prediction-derived variables and sensitive attributes, while establishing equivalences with widely used notions such as independence and separation. MIFair naturally supports intersectionality, complex subgroup structures, and multiclass classification and employs regularization-based training to reduce bias according to the selected metric. Its key advantage is its versatility: it consolidates diverse fairness requirements into a single coherent framework, enabling consistent benchmarking and facilitating practical adoption. Experiments on real-world tabular and image datasets show that MIFair effectively reduces bias, including in previously unaddressed multi-attribute scenarios, while maintaining strong predictive performance across all evaluated settings.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 MIFair，这是一个基于互信息的统一框架，用于评估和减轻先前在 AI/ML 文献中提出并最初在二进制设置中定义的各种公平概念的偏差，从而将其适用性扩展到交叉和多类场景。由此产生的基于正则化的处理中方法支持多种敏感属性配置和预测设置。通过对表格数据的神经网络模型和图像分类深度模型的广泛实验以及对结果的详细分析，我们证明了 MIFair 提供了强大且通用的公平机制。它通过减少偏差极大地提高了公平性，同时仅造成有限的准确性损失。未来的工作包括探索替代的互信息估计方法，并通过连续互信息公式将 MIFair 扩展到连续特征和输出。We introduced MIFair, a unified mutual-information-based framework for assessing and mitigating bias across various fairness notions previously proposed in the AI/ML literature and originally defined in binary settings, thereby extending their applicability to intersectional and multiclass scenarios. The resulting regularization-based in-processing method supports multiple sensitive-attribute configurations and prediction settings. Through extensive experiments on neural-network models for tabular data and on deep models for image classification and a detailed analysis of results, we demonstrated that MIFair provides a robust and versatile fairness mechanism. It substantially improves fairness by reducing bias while incurring only limited accuracy loss. Future work includes exploring alternative mutual-information estimation methods and extending MIFair to continuous features and outputs through continuous mutual-information formulations.
