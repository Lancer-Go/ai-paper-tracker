RayMamba：用于远距离 3D 物体检测的光线对齐序列化RayMamba: Ray-Aligned Serialization for Long-Range 3D Object Detection

Cheng Lu , Mingqian Ji, Shanshan Zhang , Zhihao Li , and Jian Yang  Cheng Lu, Mingqian Ji, Shanshan Zhang, Zhihao Li, and Jian Yang are with the School of Computer Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Science and Technology, Nanjing 210094, China.Shanshan Zhang is the corresponding author.E-mail: lucheng@njust.edu.cn; mingqianji1299@gmail.com; shanshan.zhang@njust.edu.cn; zhihaoli828@njust.edu.cn; csjyang@njust.edu.cn.
摘要Abstract

远距离 3D 物体检测仍然具有挑战性，因为 LiDAR 观测结果在远场变得高度稀疏和分散，使得现有检测器难以进行可靠的上下文建模。为了解决这个问题，最近基于状态空间模型（SSM）的方法提高了远程建模效率。然而，它们的有效性仍然受到通用序列化策略的限制，这些策略无法在稀疏场景中保留有意义的上下文邻域。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 RayMamba，这是一种针对基于体素的 3D 探测器的几何感知即插即用增强功能。 RayMamba 通过光线对齐序列化策略将稀疏体素组织成扇区有序序列，这为后续基于 Mamba 的建模保留了方向连续性和遮挡相关上下文。它与仅 LiDAR 探测器和多模态探测器兼容，同时仅引入适度的开销。 nuScenes 和 Argoverse 2 上的大量实验表明，在强大的基线上取得了一致的改进。特别是，RayMamba 在 nuScenes 上具有挑战性的 40-50 m 范围内实现了高达 2.49 mAP 和 1.59 NDS 增益，并进一步将 Argoverse 2 上的 VoxelNeXt 从 30.3 mAP 提高到 31.2 mAP。Long-range 3D object detection remains challenging because LiDAR observations become highly sparse and fragmented in the far field, making reliable context modeling difficult for existing detectors. To address this issue, recent state space model (SSM)-based methods have improved long-range modeling efficiency. However, their effectiveness is still limited by generic serialization strategies that fail to preserve meaningful contextual neighborhoods in sparse scenes. To address this issue, we propose RayMamba, a geometry-aware plug-and-play enhancement for voxel-based 3D detectors. RayMamba organizes sparse voxels into sector-wise ordered sequences through a ray-aligned serialization strategy, which preserves directional continuity and occlusion-related context for subsequent Mamba-based modeling. It is compatible with both LiDAR-only and multimodal detectors, while introducing only modest overhead. Extensive experiments on nuScenes and Argoverse 2 demonstrate consistent improvements across strong baselines. In particular, RayMamba achieves up to 2.49 mAP and 1.59 NDS gain in the challenging 40–50 m range on nuScenes, and further improves VoxelNeXt on Argoverse 2 from 30.3 to 31.2 mAP.

I 简介Introduction

3D 物体检测在自动驾驶中发挥着核心作用，因为它提供了定位和分类周围物体所需的空间理解。在实际驾驶场景中，特别是在高速或复杂的交通环境中，可靠地检测远处物体对于安全运动规划至关重要。然而，远距离感知仍然是基于激光雷达的探测器的主要挑战。在 nuScenes 数据集上3D object detection plays a central role in autonomous driving, as it provides the spatial understanding needed to localize and classify surrounding objects. In practical driving scenarios, especially at high speeds or in complex traffic environments, reliable detection of distant objects is crucial for safe motion planning. However, long-range perception remains a major challenge for LiDAR-based detectors. On the nuScenes dataset[1]使用 32 束 LiDAR 传感器，由于距离引起的稀疏性和前景遮挡，超过大约 40 米的物体通常用不到 10 个返回来表示，如图 1 所示。, which uses a 32-beam LiDAR sensor, objects beyond roughly 40 meters are typically represented by fewer than ten returns due to distance-induced sparsity and foreground occlusion, as illustrated in Fig.1。阿尔戈宇宙2. Argoverse 2[2]使用双 32 光束 LiDAR，在大约 50 米之外也表现出类似的高稀疏性问题。这种严重的退化使得精确的远程 3D 检测比近距离和中距离感知更加困难。, which uses dual 32-beam LiDARs, exhibits a similar high-sparsity issue beyond roughly 50 meters. This severe degradation makes accurate long-range 3D detection substantially more difficult than near-range and mid-range perception.

这一困难也对现有探测器架构提出了挑战。早期的方法，例如基于稀疏卷积的检测器This difficulty also poses a challenge to existing detector architectures. Earlier methods, such as sparse convolution-based detectors[3, 4]和基于窗口的 Transformer 变体and window-based Transformer variants[5, 6, 7, 8]，很大程度上受到本地交互的限制，因此很难在稀疏的远场场景中捕获可靠的上下文。尽管最近的稀疏注意力设计, are largely constrained by local interactions and therefore struggle to capture reliable context in sparse far-field scenes. Although recent sparse attention designs[9, 10]和基于曼巴的and Mamba-based[11, 12, 13]主干网可以实现更高效的远程建模，但当底层几何结构已经严重稀疏和碎片化时，其有效性仍然有限。在这种情况下，关键的挑战不再是简单地如何扩大感受野，而是模型是否能够从不完整的观察中建立有用的上下文关系。backbones enable more efficient long-range modeling, their effectiveness remains limited when the underlying geometric structure is already severely sparse and fragmented. In such cases, the key challenge is no longer simply how to enlarge the receptive field, but whether the model can establish useful contextual relationships from incomplete observations.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于 LiDAR 的 3D 物体检测LiDAR-based 3D Object Detection

基于LiDAR的3D物体检测器主要包括基于点的LiDAR-based 3D object detectors mainly include point-based[16, 17, 18, 19, 20]和基于体素的方法and voxel-based methods[21, 22, 4, 23, 14, 24, 25, 26]。在大规模驾驶场景中，基于体素的方法由于使用体素编码器和稀疏卷积主干进行有效的特征提取而成为主导范例。. In large-scale driving scenes, voxel-based methods have become the dominant paradigm due to their efficient feature extraction with voxel encoders and sparse convolutional backbones.

然而，远距离检测仍然具有挑战性。许多探测器依赖于密集的 BEV 预测，当需要更高的空间分辨率来保留远处物体的细节时，其成本会迅速增加。完全稀疏的管道，例如 VoxelNeXtHowever, long-range detection remains challenging. Many detectors rely on dense BEV prediction, whose cost increases rapidly when higher spatial resolution is needed to preserve distant object details. Fully sparse pipelines such as VoxelNeXt[4]和FSDand FSD[27]因此，人们一直在探索避免昂贵的密集 BEV 表示。与此同时，由于采样密度降低和前景遮挡，LiDAR 观测结果随着距离的增加而变得越来越稀疏和不完整。之前的作品，例如 FocalConvhave therefore been explored to avoid expensive dense BEV representations. Meanwhile, LiDAR observations become increasingly sparse and incomplete with distance due to reduced sampling density and foreground occlusion. Prior works, such as FocalConv[24], 大型内核转换, Large Kernel Conv[25]，和链接, and LINK[26]，尝试通过扩大稀疏交互或感受野来减轻由此产生的上下文损失。然而，当远处的观测已经高度稀疏和分散时，仅扩大感受野通常是不够的，这就激发了基于序列的替代方案来进行远程上下文建模。, attempt to alleviate the resulting context loss by enlarging sparse interactions or receptive fields. Nevertheless, when distant observations are already highly sparse and fragmented, enlarging the receptive field alone is often insufficient, motivating sequence-based alternatives for long-range context modeling.

Ⅱ-BII-B 3D 感知中的状态空间模型和序列化State Space Models and Serialization in 3D Perception

状态空间模型（SSM），特别是曼巴式架构，由于其线性复杂性和可扩展的远程序列建模能力，最近在计算机视觉领域引起了越来越多的关注。曼巴角State space models (SSMs), especially Mamba-style architectures, have recently attracted growing attention in computer vision because of their linear complexity and scalable long-range sequence modeling ability. PointMamba[28]将 Mamba 扩展到 3D 点云分析。在 3D 检测中，VoxelMambaextends Mamba to 3D point cloud analysis. In 3D detection, VoxelMamba[12]采用无组体素序列化和双尺度 SSM 进行全局上下文建模，LIONemploys group-free voxel serialization with dual-scale SSMs for global context modeling, LION[29]引入了基于窗口和轴的体素序列化的线性循环建模，以及 UniMambaintroduces linear recurrent modeling with window- and axis-based voxel serialization, and UniMamba[11]使用互补的 Z 顺序序列化和局部全局顺序聚合将稀疏 3D 卷积与双向 SSM 相结合。combines sparse 3D convolution with bidirectional SSMs using complementary Z-order serialization and local-global sequential aggregation.

更一般地说，点云序列化将无序 3D 点或体素转换为 1D 序列以进行序列建模。现有策略包括 PointMamba 中采用的空间填充曲线，例如 Z 顺序和希尔伯特曲线More generally, point cloud serialization converts unordered 3D points or voxels into 1D sequences for sequence modeling. Existing strategies include space-filling curves such as Z-order and Hilbert, as adopted in PointMamba[28], 曼巴大学, UniMamba[11], 曼巴融合, MambaFusion[13]和体素曼巴, and VoxelMamba[12]，以及窗口分区和坐标轴排序，如 SWFormer 中所示, as well as window partitioning and coordinate-axis sorting, as in SWFormer[30], 深静脉血栓形成, DSVT[31], 相变材料, PCM[32]，和狮子, and LION[29]。然而，这些通用序列化策略通常不会明确考虑 LiDAR 采集几何结构。. However, these generic serialization strategies usually do not explicitly consider LiDAR acquisition geometry.

这种限制在稀疏的远场场景中变得更加明显，从而激发了用于远距离 3D 检测的传感器几何感知序列化。This limitation becomes more pronounced in sparse far-field scenes, motivating sensor-geometry-aware serialization for long-range 3D detection.

It includes two parts: a sector-wise ordering rule defined on the 3D voxel grid, and a runtime sequence construction process that maps active sparse voxels into ordered 1D sequences. Ⅲ-DIII-D 扇区曼巴3DSectorMamba3D

SectorMamba3D 充当 RayMamba 的序列建模组件。给定来自前一个主干阶段的稀疏体素特征，它首先应用轻量级稀疏卷积编码以原始分辨率和较低分辨率产生两个特征分支。此阶段还引入了本地聚合，这有助于缓解扇区边界附近的不连续性。SectorMamba3D serves as the sequence modeling component of RayMamba. Given sparse voxel features from the preceding backbone stage, it first applies lightweight sparse convolutional encoding to produce two feature branches at the original and lower resolutions. This stage also introduces local aggregation, which helps alleviate discontinuities near sector boundaries.

基于光线对齐序列化构建的扇区有序序列，SectorMamba3D 使用可学习的位置嵌入来增强每个序列，并为每个批扇区对独立执行基于 Mamba 的序列建模；跳过空扇区。扇区序列建模公式为Based on the sector-wise ordered sequences constructed by Ray-Aligned Serialization, SectorMamba3D augments each sequence with learnable positional embeddings and performs Mamba-based sequence modeling independently for each batch-sector pair; empty sectors are skipped. The sector-wise sequence modeling is formulated as

𝐇b,k(l)=Mamba(l)⁡(𝐅~b,k(l)),\mathbf{H}_{b,k}^{(l)}=\operatorname{Mamba}^{(l)}\!\left(\tilde{\mathbf{F}}_{b,k}^{(l)}\right), (6)

哪里where𝐅~b,k(l)\tilde{\mathbf{F}}_{b,k}^{(l)}表示位置编码后的序列化特征。denotes the serialized features after positional encoding.

序列建模后，增强的特征通过逆索引分散回其原始稀疏体素位置。低分辨率分支通过稀疏逆卷积进一步上采样，并通过逐元素加法与更高分辨率分支融合。通过这种方式，SectorMamba3D 通过扇区长范围上下文丰富了稀疏体素特征，同时保持与标准稀疏卷积管道完全兼容。After sequence modeling, the enhanced features are scattered back to their original sparse voxel locations through the inverse indices. The low-resolution branch is further upsampled by sparse inverse convolution and fused with the higher-resolution branch through element-wise addition. In this way, SectorMamba3D enriches sparse voxel features with sector-wise long-range context while remaining fully compatible with standard sparse convolution pipelines.

Ⅲ-EIII-E 效率和实施Efficiency and Implementation

RayMamba 仅引入适度的开销。对于固定体素网格配置，密集扇区模板是离线生成的，存储每个离散体素位置的扇区分配和排序分数。在训练和推理过程中，活跃的稀疏体素在运行时查询该模板，并通过RayMamba introduces only modest overhead. For a fixed voxel grid configuration, the dense sector template is generated offline, storing the sector assignment and ordering score of each discrete voxel location. During training and inference, active sparse voxels query this template at runtime and are reordered through a空间到序列Spatial-to-Sequence操作以形成扇区有序序列。由于此过程仅需要模板查找和对活动体素进行基于 CUDA 的并行排序，因此运行时开销仍然很小。operation to form sector-wise ordered sequences. Since this process only requires template lookup and CUDA-based parallel sorting over active voxels, the runtime overhead remains small.

随后的序列建模阶段继承了状态空间模型的线性复杂性，使得 RayMamba 对于密集全局注意力不切实际的稀疏 3D 特征图非常有效。第 2 节中提供了特定于探测器的放置和配置详细信息。The subsequent sequence modeling stage inherits the linear complexity of state space models, making RayMamba efficient for sparse 3D feature maps where dense global attention is impractical. V 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 RayMamba，一种用于远距离 3D 物体检测的光线对齐序列建模模块。通过用传感器几何感知的扇区排序策略替换通用空间序列化，RayMamba 为稀疏远场场景中基于 Mamba 的建模提供了更多信息丰富的上下文转换。所提出的设计是轻量级的，与现有的稀疏卷积管道兼容，并且适用于仅激光雷达和多模态检测器。 nuScenes 和 AV2 上的大量实验表明，RayMamba 持续提高了整体检测性能，尤其是在远距离区域中的增益尤其明显。这些结果表明，几何感知序列化是增强远程 3D 感知序列建模的有效方向。In this paper, we presented RayMamba, a ray-aligned sequence modeling module for long-range 3D object detection. By replacing generic spatial serialization with a sensor-geometry-aware sector-wise ordering strategy, RayMamba provides more informative context transitions for Mamba-based modeling in sparse far-field scenes. The proposed design is lightweight, compatible with existing sparse convolutional pipelines, and applicable to both LiDAR-only and multimodal detectors. Extensive experiments on nuScenes and AV2show that RayMamba consistently improves overall detection performance, with especially clear gains in long-range regions. These results demonstrate that geometry-aware serialization is an effective direction for enhancing sequence modeling in long-range 3D perception.

局限性和未来的工作Limitation and future works当前设计的一个限制是扇区级 Mamba 模块是跨扇区串行执行的。随着扇区角度减小，扇区数量增加，导致推理效率明显下降。这种权衡在 Argoverse 2 等大型数据集上变得更加明显，其中扇形角度较小（例如，One limitation of the current design is that the sector-wise Mamba module is executed serially across sectors. As the sector angle decreases, the number of sectors increases, leading to a noticeable drop in inference efficiency. This trade-off becomes more evident on large-scale datasets such as Argoverse 2, where smaller sector angles (e.g.,15∘15^{\circ}）可以带来额外的性能提升，但运行时成本却要高得多。在未来的工作中，我们计划研究并行的扇区序列建模，以更好地支持更大范围和更密集的场景感知设置。) can bring additional performance gains, but at a substantially higher runtime cost. In future work, we plan to investigate parallelized sector-wise sequence modeling to better support larger-range and denser-scene perception settings.
