摘要Abstract

随机动量方法，例如重球 (HB)、Nesterov 动量和加速 SGD (ASGD) 的变体（Kidambi 等人，2018）在现代训练中广泛使用，但它们的随机收益取决于两个不同的量：串行运行时间、达到目标精度所需的迭代次数和计算效率 (CE)、逆总梯度查询或 FLOP 成本。仅当收缩间隙随批次大小线性增长时，较大批次可减少串行运行时间而不损害 CE。Stochastic momentum methods such as heavy ball (HB), Nesterov momentum, and variants of Accelerated SGD (ASGD) (Kidambi et al., 2018) are widely used in modern training, but their stochastic benefits depend on two distinct quantities: serial runtime, the number of iterations needed to reach a target accuracy, and compute efficiency (CE), the inverse total gradient-query or FLOP cost. Larger batches reduce serial runtime without hurting CE only when the contraction gap grows linearly with batch size.

6 讨论与结论6 Discussion and Conclusions

在这项工作中，我们建立了重球动量和加速 SGD 的计算效率下限，作为问题实例和批量大小的函数。将问题实例专门化为幂律谱，我们直接比较了两种算法，表明串行运行时的性能存在权衡：可以使用 ASGD 以较小的批量大小进行训练，以获得更长的串行运行时间，从而实现更好的最终损失。从理论角度来看，我们的 ASGD 下限比（Kidambi 等人，2018）的下限有所改善，最显着的是由于该下限适用于任何幂律谱，而不是专门构建的问题。我们相信，本工作中用于推导下界的技术也可以应用于推导上限，我们将这种推导留给未来的工作。In this work, we have established compute efficiency lower bounds for heavy ball momentum and accelerated SGD, as a function of the problem instance and the batch size. Specializing the problem instances to power law spectra, we have directly compared the 2 algorithms showing that there is a performance to serial runtime tradeoff: one can train with ASGD at a smaller batch size for a longer serial runtime, achieving better final loss. From a theoretical perspective, our lower bound for ASGD improves over that of (Kidambi et al., 2018), most notably due to the bound applying for any power law spectrum and not a specifically constructed problem. We believe that the techniques used in this work to derive the lower bounds can also be applied to deriving upper bounds, and we leave this derivation to future work.
