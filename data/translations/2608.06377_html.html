摘要Abstract

The obvious remedy, training models to resist such signals, hides a failure mode: a model that ignores all context looks robust yet is useless when the context is worth trusting. Across a comprehensive benchmark study, we observe that such a susceptibility is universal. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型现在充当推理引擎：它们回答科学问题并通过长链思维证明其决策的合理性Large language models now serve as reasoning engines: they answer scientific questions and justify their decisions through long chains of thought(Wei et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022)，并且广泛的基准使得这一进步可以通过知识、推理和整体度量套件来衡量, and broad benchmarks have made this progress measurable across knowledge, reasoning, and holistic metric suites(Hendrycks et al., 2021a; Srivastava et al., 2023; Suzgun et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024)。但这些基准测试是在单一背景下对模型进行评分，最终的答案对于问题周围的内容来说是脆弱的。当提示带有看似合理但错误的建议时，干净地解决问题的模型通常会放弃其正确答案。这些线索引导预测，但没有忠实地反映在模型陈述的推理中. But those benchmarks score a model under a single context, and a final answer is fragile to what surrounds the question. A model that solves a problem cleanly will often abandon its correct answer the moment the prompt carries a plausible but wrong suggestion. Such cues steer predictions without being faithfully reflected in the model’s stated reasoning(Turpin et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2025)，并且基于偏好的一致性可能会因为奖励与用户的同意而不是事实而使事情变得更糟, and preference-based alignment can make matters worse by rewarding agreement with the user over truth(Sharma et al., 2024)。图。. Fig.1显示了跨模型的影响：看似官方但错误的提示甚至将边界从正确翻转到错误。shows the effect across models: an official-looking but wrong hint flips even frontiers from right to wrong.

本能的解决方法是训练模型以抵抗误导性的环境，但抵抗隐藏了故障模式。当评估仅将干净的提示与带有错误提示的提示进行对比时，简单地忽略所有外部上下文的模型看起来很稳健。然而，一个真正有用的推理模型必须同时做三件事：在不牺牲清晰推理的情况下拒绝误导性信号，从实际上正确的上下文中受益，并不受不相关的上下文影响。单一条件评估无法区分这些行为。The instinctive fix is to train models to resist misleading context, but resistance hides a failure mode. A model that simply ignores all external context looks robust whenever evaluation only contrasts clean prompts with prompts carrying wrong hints. A genuinely useful reasoning model, however, must do three things at once: reject a misleading signal without sacrificing clean reasoning, benefit from context that is actually correct, and stay unmoved by irrelevant context. Single-condition evaluation cannot tell these behaviors apart.

Our benchmark turns each reasoning item into four matched versions that differ only in the context wrapped around the same question: a clean version with no added context, a misleading version whose context points to a plausible wrong answer, a correct-context version whose context supports the gold answer, and an irrelevant-context version with related but non-answer-bearing text. Because the question, answer space, and gold answer are held fixed across all four versions, any change in accuracy is attributable to the added signal rather than to task difficulty, and the four conditions together separate the three behaviors a trustworthy model must combine.

然而，仅聚合准确性就模糊了模型的潜在能力及其对信号的敏感性：模型可以取得高分，只是因为它在干净的问题上很强，即使误导性的背景悄悄地推翻了它本来会得到的正确答案。因此我们报告Aggregate accuracy alone, however, blurs a model’s underlying ability with its susceptibility to signals: a model can post a high score simply because it is strong on the clean questions, even as misleading context quietly overturns answers it would otherwise get right. It conditions on the items a model already solves when clean and measures how often a misleading signal flips them to wrong, separating signal-induced failures from questions the model cannot answer.

选择性信任是仅抵抗方法无法实现的训练目标，我们通过以下方法来实现它：Selective trust is a training target that resistance-only methods miss, and we meet it withSCOPE (S选修课electiveCo文本ntextP参考reference优化）Optimization)，一个信号反事实偏好框架，可以挖掘重要的失败：基本模型本身可以正确回答的项目，但一旦添加误导性信号就会出错。对于每个信号，它更喜欢模型的真实一致响应而不是信号跟踪响应，并在所有四个上下文条件中重复使用同一对信号，将学习信号与上下文所扮演的角色联系起来，而不是与任何单个提示的表面形式联系起来。关键思想是平衡：我们不是单独对误导性的对进行训练，这只会让人们产生全面的不信任，而是将它们与干净、正确的上下文和不相关的上下文控制进行同等权重，而标准 DPO 目标本身不受影响。, a signal-counterfactual preference framework that mines the failures that matter: items a base model answers correctly on its own but gets wrong once a misleading signal is added. For each, it prefers the model’s truth-consistent response over its signal-following response and reuses the same pair across all four context conditions, tying the learning signal to the role the context plays rather than to any single prompt’s surface form. The key idea is balance: rather than train on the misleading pairs alone, which merely teaches blanket distrust, we weight them equally against the clean, correct-context, and irrelevant-context controls, leaving the standard DPO objective itself untouched.
