2.1 自我监督学习Self-supervised Learning

SSL 是无监督学习的一个重要分支，旨在直接从大规模未标记数据中学习判别性特征表示。近年来，SSL 引起了广泛的关注，导致了多种 SSL 方法的发展。其中，基于判别对比的 SSL 和基于自蒸馏的 SSL 方法是最有影响力和最成功的两种SSL, a prominent branch of unsupervised learning, aims to learn discriminative feature representations directly from large-scale unlabeled data. In recent years, SSL has attracted extensive attention, leading to the development of a variety of SSL approaches. Among these, the discriminative contrastive-based SSL and self-distillation-based SSL approaches are two of the most influential and successful[14, 2].

基于区分对比的 SSL。Discriminative contrastive-based SSL.早期的 CL 方法主要建立在使用负例的基础上，负例指的是来自不同类别或实例的样本，这些样本与目标样本不同，用于教导编码器区分相似和不相似的输入。经典的 SimCLREarly CL methods were predominantly founded on the use of negative examples, which refer to samples from different classes or instances that are distinct from the target sample and are used to teach the encoder to differentiate between similar and dissimilar inputs. A classical SimCLR[1]采用对比损失来最大化目标样本的不同增强视图（即正样本）之间的相似性，同时最小化与负示例（即其他不相关样本的视图）的相似性。受到 SimCLR、MoCo v2 的启发employs a contrastive loss to maximize the similarity between differently augmented views (i.e., positive samples) of the target sample while minimizing the similarity to negative examples, that is, views of other unrelated samples. Inspired by SimCLR, MoCo v2[15]通过集成模糊增强策略来增强动量对比框架。 MoCo v2 的一个关键创新是它使用动量编码器来维护负例的动态队列，这使得它即使在更小的批量大小和更少的迭代次数下也能比 SimCLR 获得更高的表示精度和更好的收敛稳定性。enhances the momentum contrast framework by integrating a blur augmentation strategy. A key innovation of MoCo v2 is its use of a momentum encoder to maintain a dynamic queue of negative examples, which allows it to achieve higher representation accuracy and better convergence stability than SimCLR even with a smaller batch size and fewer epochs.

2.2 深度学习模型水印Deep Learning Model Watermarking

模型水印是一种侵入性技术，涉及将秘密水印嵌入到模型中。之后，可以提取水印作为所有权证据。根据验证手段，这些技术分为两种类型：白盒和黑盒。Model watermarking is an invasive technique that involves embedding a secret watermark into model. Later, the watermark can be extracted as the ownership evidence. Based on the verification means, these techniques are categorized into two types: white-box and black-box.

1）白盒：1) White-box:白盒模型水印涉及将秘密水印直接嵌入深度学习 (DL) 模型的内部参数中。White-box model watermarking involves embedding a secret watermark directly into a deep learning (DL) model’s internal parameters.

内田Uchida等人。et al. [19]首创了通过参数正则化将水印嵌入模型参数的首批方法之一。同样，DeepMarkspioneered one of the first methods to embed watermarks into model parameters via parameter regularization. Similarly, DeepMarks[20]计算目标模型的参数和保留的水印签名之间的相关性分数，以说明所有权验证的准确性。除了静态参数之外，动态参数也可以作为水印载体。刘computes a correlation score between the target model’s parameters and preserved watermark signature to illustrate the accuracy of ownership verification. Besides static parameters, dynamic parameters can also serve as watermark carriers. Liu等人。et al. [21]提出了贪婪残差，选择性地将水印嵌入到较小的显着模型权重集中，残差信息定义为提取的权重值的总和。难波以不同的方式proposed greedy residuals, which selectively embeds watermarks into a smaller set of salient model weights, with the residual information defined as the sum of the extracted weight values. In a different approach, Namba等人。et al. [22]确定了对预测有显着贡献的模型权重，并以指数方式增加其权重值以嵌入水印。identified model weights that significantly contribute to predictions and exponentially increase their weight values to embed the watermark.

尽管取得了这些进步，白盒水印方案仍然非常容易受到水印检测和删除攻击Despite these advances, white-box watermarking schemes remain highly vulnerable to watermark detection and removal attacks[23]。例如，通过分析模型参数中的分布异常，攻击者可以识别嵌入的水印并将其删除以逃避所有权验证。更重要的是，在验证过程中白盒访问可​​疑模型的内部参数的要求在实际部署中造成了重大的实际限制。. For instance, by analyzing distributional anomalies in model parameters, adversaries can identify embedded watermarks and remove them to evade ownership verification. More importantly, the requirement for white-box access to a suspect model’s internal parameters during verification poses significant practical limitations in real-world deployment.

2）黑匣子：2) Black-box:可以通过将特定的触发样本与预定的模型输出模式或行为相关联来嵌入黑盒水印，以便通过查询带有触发携带样本的可疑模型以表现出特殊的响应来推断水印的存在。Black-box watermarks can be embedded by associating specific trigger samples with predetermined model output patterns or behaviors, such that the presence of the watermark is inferred by querying the suspect model with trigger-carrying samples to exhibit special responses.

MOVE[25]修改触发器携带样本的图像风格并将其嵌入到受害者模型中，而不改变水印样本的标签。与上述方法不同，EaaWmodifies the trigger-carrying samples’ image style and embeds them into the victim model, without changing the label of watermark samples. Unlike the methods described above, EaaW[26]将可验证的后门模式植入到特征归因的解释中，而不是模型预测中。此外，杨implants verifiable backdoor patterns into the explanation of feature attribution instead of model predictions. Further, Yang等人。et al. 黑盒水印比白盒方法具有更优越的实用性；然而，由于嵌入访问要求存在根本冲突，将这些受监督的黑盒水印技术应用于 SSL 具有挑战性。它们对标签相关信息（例如决策边界）和完整模型控制的依赖直接违背了 SSL 的原则，其中水印必须通过无注释的预训练/微调来嵌入，通常只能通过白盒访问编码器。更具挑战性的是，在对 SSL 编码器加水印时，没有下游分类器和数据集的先验知识。Black-box watermarking offers superior practicality over white-box approaches; however, applying these supervised black-box watermarking techniques to SSL is challenging due to a fundamental conflict in embedding access requirements. Their reliance on label-dependent information (e.g., decision boundaries) and full model control directly contradicts the principles of SSL, where watermarks must be embedded through annotation-free pretraining/finetuning, often with only white-box access to the encoder. More challenging, there is no prior knowledge of the downstream classifier and datasets when watermarking the SSL encoder.

3.1 威胁模型Threat Model

我们的威胁模型由两方组成：模型所有者（充当 IP 防御者）和侵权者（充当 IP 攻击者）。模型所有者开发并拥有受害编码器的 IP，其目标是在 EaaS 或 MLaaS 环境中使用编码器时保护编码器的 IP。 相反，侵权者试图非法窃取或复制受害编码器，意图绕过为受害编码器实施的 IP 保护。Our threat model consists of two parties: the model owner, who acts as the IP defender and the infringer, who is the IP attacker. The model owner develops and owns the IP of the victim encoder, whose objective is to protect the IP of the encoder when it is used in the context of either EaaS or MLaaS. In contrast, the infringer seeks to illicitly steal or replicate the victim encoder, with the intent to bypass the IP protection implemented for the victim encoder.

模特拥有者Model Owner完全了解训练过程，包括访问训练数据、编码器架构和参数。然而，受害者编码器最终部署的具体下游任务仍然未知。因此，只能保留预训练数据的一小部分来构建用于水印嵌入和所有权验证的影子数据集。此外，嵌入的水印必须在 EaaS 和 MLaaS 场景下保持可提取性。在这些场景中，所有者只能通过查询部署为 EaaS 的可疑编码器或部署为 MLaaS 的模型并观察其输出来确定其是否源自受保护的编码器，以黑盒方式执行 IP 验证。has full knowledge of the training process, including access to the training data, encoder architecture, and parameters. However, the specific downstream tasks on which the victim encoder will ultimately be deployed remain unknown. Therefore, only a small subset of the pre-training data can be reserved for constructing a shadow dataset for watermark embedding and ownership verification. Furthermore, the embedded watermarks must remain extractable under both EaaS and MLaaS scenarios. In these scenarios, the owner can only perform IP verification in a black-box manner by querying the suspect encoder deployed as EaaS or model deployed as MLaaS and observing its outputs to determine whether it originates from the protected encoder.

侵权人Infringer试图非法窃取或复制训练有素的编码器并将其部署在自己的平台上（例如EaaS和MLaaS），从而以极低的成本获取巨额利润。攻击者通常精通深度学习技术，并且意识到水印机制的潜在存在。因此，攻击者采用各种水印去除技术，例如微调和模型剪枝攻击，试图消除受害者编码器中的水印。此外，攻击者可以获得水印技术的部分知识并进行自适应攻击以去除水印。attempts to illegally steal or replicate well-trained encoders and deploy them on their own platforms (e.g., EaaS and MLaaS), thereby reaping significant profits at a very low cost.
