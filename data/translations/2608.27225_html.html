摘要Abstract

工业环境中有效的人机协作需要机器人理解人类意图并协助任务规划，减少工作量。 最近的工作探索了在此类数据稀缺场景中使用多模态大语言模型（MM-LLM）进行任务规划，利用上下文学习来解释用户操作并以自然语言生成长期行动计划。 然而，MM-LLM 本质上缺乏对系统状态的理解，并且不跟踪状态转换，常常导致偏离预期目标的幻觉行为。此外，以自然语言生成行动计划往往会将生成的计划限制在较高的水平，从而在行动执行中引入歧义。 为了解决这些限制，我们提出了状态感知任务估计器和规划器（STEP），它提示 MM-LLM 显式估计系统状态并预测执行操作导致的状态转换。通过预测未来状态和行动，STEP 确保任务聚合规划，同时还提供执行预测行动所需的额外辅助参数。 我们使用机器人装配任务在模拟环境中评估 STEP。我们的方法优于最先进的方法Effective human-robot collaboration in industrial settings requires robots to understand human intentions and assist with task planning, reducing workload. Recent works have explored the use of Multi-modal Large Language Models (MM-LLMs) for task planning in such data-scarce scenarios, leveraging in-context learning to interpret user actions and generate long-horizon action plans in natural language. However, MM-LLMs inherently lack an understanding of system states and do not track state transitions, often leading to hallucinated actions that deviate from the intended goal. Additionally, generating action plans in natural language tends to limit the generated plans to a high level, introducing ambiguity in action execution. To address these limitations, we propose the State-aware Task Estimator and Planner (STEP), which prompts a MM-LLM to explicitly estimate the state of the system and predict the state transitions resulting from executed actions. By forecasting future states alongside actions, STEP ensures task-convergent planning while also providing additional assistance parameters necessary for executing the predicted actions. We evaluate STEP in a simulated environment using a robot assembly task. I 简介Introduction

随着机器人变得更加经济实惠和用途广泛，它们的应用范围也不断扩大，特别是在需要更多人际互动和通用化的行业中。 人机协作通过利用各自的优势来提高效率。 然而，有效的团队合作需要共同控制和相互理解。 为了实现无缝协作，机器人必须能够推断人类意图并接管部分工作量。 解除对机器人的控制可以让一个人监督多个机器人，或者通过制定愿景并将执行委托给机器人来发挥创造性作用As robots have become more affordable and versatile, their applications have expanded, particularly in industries requiring greater human interaction and generalization. Human-robot collaboration improves efficiency by drawing on their respective strengths. However, effective teamwork requires shared control and mutual understanding. For seamless collaboration, robots must be able to infer human intentions and take over some of the workload. Relieving control over to a robot can enable a person to supervise multiple robots or take on a creative role by developing a vision and delegating execution to the robot[1]。 然而，机器人协助的一些任务本质上是人类的，并且是为以人为中心的环境而设计的，训练机器人推断人类目标并生成可行的计划需要大量的数据收集，这仍然是一个重大挑战. However, several tasks in which robot assistance would help are intrinsically human and designed for human-centric environments, and training robots to infer human goals and generate actionable plans requires extensive data collection, which remains a significant challenge[2, 3, 4]。 最近流行的另一种方法是使用 MM-LLM 根据观察进行长期行动预期 (LTA). An alternative approach that has recently gained popularity is the use of MM-LLMs for long-term action anticipation (LTA) from observations[5, 6, 7, 8]。 先前的工作已经探索了 MM-LLM 的上下文性能，以根据人类执行的操作序列推断人类的目标并预测实现该目标所需的未来操作. Prior work has explored the in-context performance of MM-LLMs to infer a human’s goal and predict the future actions needed to achieve it, based on the sequence of actions performed by the human[9]。 然而，直接查询 MM-LLM 来规划机器人动作有几个限制。. However, directly querying an MM-LLM for planning robot actions has several limitations.

由于 MM-LLM 在预训练期间缺乏现实世界的互动，因此他们很难跟踪计划行动的效果Since MM-LLMs lack real-world interaction during pre-training, they struggle to track the effects of their planned actions[10, 11, 12]。 因此，执行生成的动作的代理通常会偏离预期的目标状态。 为了解决这个问题，最近的工作提出明确查询 MM-LLM 来跟踪系统的状态，从而使他们能够衡量实现目标的进度. As a result, an agent that executes the generated actions often deviates from the intended goal state. To address this, recent work has proposed explicitly querying MM-LLMs to track the state of the system, allowing them to measure progress toward the goal[13, 14, 15, 16, 17]。 然而，这些方法要么依赖于诸如以自然语言全面描述系统状态的能力之类的假设，要么依赖于经济地查询环境以获取状态信息的能力；或者他们通过将状态跟踪与任务规划分开来限制他们的注意力。 在本文中，我们通过放宽一些假设和约束来扩展 LTA 的先前研究，以更好地匹配现实世界的工业环境。. However, these approaches either rely on assumptions such as the ability to comprehensively describe system states in natural language or affordably query the environment for state information; or they constrain their focus by treating state tracking separately from task planning. In this paper, we extend prior research on LTA by relaxing some of the assumptions and constraints to better match a real-world industrial setting.

将 MM-LLM 应用于机器人技术的另一个关键挑战是处理动作空间的高维性。 由于 MM-LLM 未能考虑现实世界执行的细微差别，因此之前的几项工作将其输出限制为“动词，名词”对的粗略行动计划（例如“pick，cup”，“pour from，cup”，“place on，table”），同时依赖预定义函数来抽象其他参数Another key challenge in applying MM-LLMs to robotics is handling the high dimensionality of the action space. Since MM-LLMs fail to consider the nuances of real-world execution, several previous works limit their output to a cursory action plan of “verb, noun” pairs (e.g., “pick, cup”, “pour from, cup”, “place on, table”), while relying on predefined functions to abstract away the other parameters[11, 14, 18, 19, 20]。 然而，这通常会导致操作执行过程中出现歧义。 例如，用杯子执行“放置在桌子上”需要确定桌子上的放置位置，同时考虑其他物体以及放置后杯子的方向（直立、倒置等）。 一些工作通过纳入反馈（来自循环中的人类或通过查询环境）并重新规划任何故障来缓解这种情况。. However, this often leads to ambiguity during action execution. For instance, executing “place on, table” with a cup requires determining a placement location on the table, while accounting for other objects, and the orientation of the cup after placement (upright, upside down, etc.). Some works mitigate this by incorporating feedback, either from humans in the loop or by querying the environment, and re-planning for any failures.

II 相关工作Related Work

STEP 的灵感来自关于使用法学硕士的共享自治和 LTA 的论文。我们通过扩展之前跟踪 LTA 环境的状态表示的工作来解决它们的一些局限性。STEP is inspired by papers on shared autonomy and LTA using LLMs. We address some of their limitations by extending prior work on tracking a state representation of the environment for LTA.

Ⅱ-AII-A 远程操作中的共享自主权Shared autonomy in teleoperation

共享远程操作的想法在工业界越来越受欢迎，作为一种提高产量的方式，同时赋予人类承担监督角色的能力The idea of shared teleoperation has gained popularity in industry as a way to increase production while empowering humans to assume a supervisory role[1]。 之前的工作已经通过让半自主机器人为操作员提供帮助来实现共享远程操作. Previous work has approached shared teleoperation by having a semi-autonomous robot provide assistance to an operator[22, 23, 24]。 通常，机器人一开始是非自主的，并尝试推断用户的目标. Usually, the robot starts as non-autonomous and attempts to infer the user’s goal[25, 26, 27, 28]，或对其进行合理的估计, or form a reasonable estimate of it[29]，通过观察他们的行为。 然后机器人通过控制来提供帮助以完成任务, by observing their actions. The robot then provides assistance to complete the task by taking control[30]或提供视觉、触觉或多模式提示or providing visual, haptic, or multi-modal cues[31, 32, 33, 24]。 在本文中，我们遵循类似的流程，将用户执行的操作作为自然语言操作序列（例如“选择块 1”、“站立块 1”、“撤回”）作为输入，并预测用户的目标，然后是完成目标所需的操作。. In this paper, we follow a similar pipeline, by taking as input the actions performed by the user as a sequence of natural language actions (e.g. “Pick block 1”, “Stand block 1”, “Withdraw”) and predicting the user’s goal, followed by the actions required to complete the goal.

Ⅱ-BII-B 大型语言模型和长期行动预期Large language models and long-term action anticipation

大型语言模型 (LLM) 及其图像+文本对应物 MM-LLM 在各种任务上都表现出了出色的性能Large Language Models (LLMs), and their images + text counterparts, MM-LLMs, have shown great performance on diverse tasks[34, 35, 36, 37, 38, 39, 40]。 特别有用的是它们的上下文学习能力，当推理过程中提供的特定领域示例很少时，用作冻结模型的法学硕士可以跨领域进行泛化. Particularly useful is their in-context learning ability, where LLMs used as frozen models can generalize across domains when few domain-specific examples are provided during inference[41, 42]。 多项研究建议使用情境学习来将 LLM 输出落实到机器人可操作的步骤中. Several studies have proposed using in-context learning to ground LLM output in robot-actionable steps[11, 18, 19, 20]。 情境学习在工业机器人任务规划中特别有用，因为法学硕士通常没有接受与这些场景相关的数据的培训。 与共享自治规划一样，LTA 涉及根据观察到的行为的初始序列来预测代理的未来行为. In-context learning is especially useful in robot task planning for industry, as LLMs are often not trained on data that is relevant for these scenarios. Like shared autonomy planning, LTA involves forecasting an agent’s future actions from an initial sequence of observed behavior[5, 6, 7, 8]。 之前的研究探索了将视频转换为一系列自然语言动作，并将其用作法学硕士的输入来预测未来的动作。 蚂蚁GPT. Inspired by these approaches, our prediction pipeline further extends this framework by also predicting and propagating the state of the environment.

三、III方法Approach

STEP 将操作员执行的操作和工作站图像以及一组提示 MM-LLM 的示例交互作为输入。如图所示STEP takes as input the actions performed by an operator and an image of the workstation, along with a set of example interactions to prompt an MM-LLM. As illustrated in Fig.2，我们的管道预测操作员试图完成的总体任务。同时，它还根据图像估计当前工作空间的结构化状态表示。然后，它使用估计的任务和操作历史记录来预测完成任务所需的未来操作。然后传播当前状态以估计工作站的未来状态。使用结构化的未来状态表示，它计算它们与估计任务的目标状态的距离，并截断距离最小的状态下的未来动作。 STEP 还重复动作预测和状态传播步骤进行多次部署，以迭代方式更接近完成任务。, our pipeline predicts the overall task the operator is trying to accomplish. Simultaneously, it also estimates a structured state representation of the current workspace from its image. It then uses the estimated task and action history to predict future actions required to complete the task. The current state is then propagated to estimate the future states of the workstation. Using the structured future state representations, it calculates their distance from the goal state of the estimated task and truncates the future actions at the state with the least distance. STEP also repeats the action prediction and state propagation steps for multiple rollouts to iteratively get closer to completing the task.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 STEP，这是一种通过显式建模状态转换来增强 MM-LLM 长期动作预期的方法，从而在协作工业任务中产生更准确和可执行的机器人动作计划。我们构建了一个多阶段管道，首先估计机器人操作员尝试执行的总体任务，以及机器人工作站当前状态的结构化表示。然后，我们将此信息传递到后续阶段，以预测完成任务所需的操作，并传播状态表示以获得成功执行所需任务的预测操作所需的辅助参数。我们还跟踪传播状态和目标状态之间的距离，并指导预测的行动实现目标。We introduce STEP, a method to enhance long-term action anticipation with MM-LLMs by explicitly modeling state transitions, leading to more accurate and executable robot action plans in collaborative industrial tasks. We construct a multi-staged pipeline composed of first estimating the overall task a robot operator is trying to perform, along with a structured representation of the current state of the robot’s workstation. We then pass this information on to later stages to predict the actions required to complete the task and propagate the state representation to obtain the assistance parameters required to successfully execute the predicted actions for the desired task. We also track the distance between the propagated states and the goal state and guide predicted actions towards the goal.

我们在遥控机器人装配场景的数据集上广泛评估了我们的方法。我们的结果强调了我们通过展示相对于现有最先进技术在可执行性和最终错误方面的改进性能，为弥合现有文献中的一些差距做出的贡献。然而，我们看到了一些需要改进的地方。 STEP 的一个限制是其当前的实现特定于块组装场景。业界可以通过使用与新场景相关的信息，通过修改提示、状态表示结构和功能实现，将STEP扩展到其他场景。针对新场景修改 STEP 的这种投资在开始时只需要一次。然而，未来的工作可以探索如何以最小的修改将 STEP 推广到各种场景。一个潜在的研究途径可能是使用 MM-LLM 来分析场景并自动制作所需的提示、状态表示结构和函数实现。We extensively evaluated our method on a dataset of an assembly scenario with teleoperated robots. Our results highlight our contribution towards bridging some of the gaps in the existing literature by demonstrating improved performance in executability and final error over the existing state-of-the-art. However, we see some areas for improvement. One limitation of STEP is that its current implementation is specific to the block assembly scenario. Industry may extend STEP to other scenarios by using information relevant to the new scenarios, by modifying the prompts, state representation structure, and function implementations. This investment in modifying STEP for a new scenario would only be required once at the start. However, future work can explore ways to generalize STEP to various scenarios with minimal modifications. A potential avenue of research could be using an MM-LLM to analyze a scenario and automatically craft the required prompts, state representation structure, and function implementations.

在实际使用之前，还需要进行额外的工作才能使 STEP 实时化。为了传播状态并指导行动计划的生成以实现目标，STEP 比基线查询 MM-LLM 多次。然而，这导致 STEP 需要牺牲速度来换取性能准确性。在我们的实验中，我们观察到基线的运行时间（M = 18.24，SD = 4.23 秒）比 STEP（M = 148.07，SD = 55.03 秒）更短。未来的研究可以研究在本地托管 MM-LLM 或微调较小的模型以减少每个查询的延迟。我们希望我们的工作能够鼓励进一步探究 MM-LLM 在人机协作的长期推理中的潜力。Additional work is also required to make STEP real-time before it can be adopted for practical use. In order to propagate states and guide the generation of action plans towards the goal, STEP queries an MM-LLM several more times than the baseline. However, this causes STEP to trade off speed for performance accuracy. In our experiments, we observed that the baseline had a shorter run time (M = 18.24, SD = 4.23 sec) than STEP (M = 148.07, SD = 55.03 sec). Future research could investigate hosting an MM-LLM locally or finetuning a smaller model to reduce the latency of each query. We hope that our work encourages further inquiries into the potential of MM-LLMs in long-horizon reasoning for human-robot collaboration.
