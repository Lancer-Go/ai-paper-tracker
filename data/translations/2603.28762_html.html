摘要。Abstract.

现代文本到图像（T2I）扩散模型已经实现了显着的语义对齐，但它们常常严重缺乏多样性，对于任何给定的提示都集中在一组狭窄的视觉解决方案上。 这种典型性偏差 对需要广泛生成结果的创意应用提出了挑战。我们确定一个基本的 权衡 当前 多样性方法：修改模型输入需要昂贵的优化才能纳入来自生成路径的反馈。相反，作用于空间上承诺的中间潜伏往往会破坏正在形成的视觉结构，导致伪影。 在这项工作中，我们建议将上下文空间中的排斥力应用为一种新颖的框架，以实现扩散变压器的丰富多样性。通过干预多模态注意通道，我们应用 变压器前向传递过程中的动态排斥，在块之间注入干预，其中文本调节通过新兴图像结构得到丰富。这允许 在确定结构后但在确定构图之前重新定向引导轨迹。 我们的结果表明，上下文空间中的排斥会产生更丰富的多样性，而不会牺牲视觉保真度或语义依从性。 此外，我们的方法 具有独特的高效性， 施加一个小 计算开销 即使在现代“涡轮”和蒸馏模型中，传统的基于轨迹的干预措施通常会失败，但仍保持有效。 项目页面：Modern Text-to-Image (T2I) diffusion models have achieved remarkable semantic alignment, yet they often suffer from a significant lack of variety, converging on a narrow set of visual solutions for any given prompt. This typicality bias presents a challenge for creative applications that require a wide range of generative outcomes. We identify a fundamental trade-off in current approaches to diversity: modifying model inputs requires costly optimization to incorporate feedback from the generative path. In contrast, acting on spatially-committed intermediate latents tends to disrupt the forming visual structure, leading to artifacts. In this work, we propose to apply repulsion in the Contextual Space as a novel framework for achieving rich diversity in Diffusion Transformers. By intervening in the multimodal attention channels, we apply on-the-fly repulsion during the transformer’s forward pass, injecting the intervention between blocks where text conditioning is enriched with emergent image structure. This allows for redirecting the guidance trajectory after it is structurally informed but before the composition is fixed. Our results demonstrate that repulsion in the Contextual Space produces significantly richer diversity without sacrificing visual fidelity or semantic adherence. Furthermore, our method is uniquely efficient, imposing a small computational overhead while remaining effective even in modern “Turbo” and distilled models where traditional trajectory-based interventions typically fail. 1. 简介Introduction

文本到图像 (T2I) 生成模型的快速发展开创了高保真视觉合成的新时代，其中模型现在与复杂的文本提示表现出前所未有的一致性The rapid evolution of Text-to-Image (T2I) generative models has ushered in a new era of high-fidelity visual synthesis, where models now exhibit unprecedented alignment with complex textual prompts(Rombach et al., 2022; Podell et al., 2023; Esser et al., 2024)。然而，这一进步付出了巨大的代价：生成多样性的减少。随着先进的生成模型越来越针对精度和人类偏好进行优化，它们往往会收敛于一组狭窄的“典型”视觉解决方案，这种现象通常被描述为典型性偏差. However, this progress has come at a significant cost: the reduction of generative diversity. As advanced generative models are increasingly optimized for precision and human preference, they tend to converge on a narrow set of “typical” visual solutions, a phenomenon often described as typicality bias(Teotia et al., 2025)。多样性不再是次要指标；它已成为一个中心研究问题，并有越来越多的工作来解决. Diversity is no longer a secondary metric; it has become a central research problem addressed by a growing body of work(Um and Ye, 2025; Morshed and Boddeti, 2025; Jalali et al., 2025)。这是因为生成式人工智能的效用取决于它作为创造性合作伙伴探索人类丰富想象力的能力。它应该充当生成引擎而不仅仅是复杂的检索机制。. This is because the utility of generative AI depends on its ability to act as a creative partner that explores the vast manifold of human imagination. It should function as a generative engine rather than merely a sophisticated retrieval mechanism.

由于质量和多样性之间的结构性紧张，多样性问题从根本上来说是困难的。目前高质量的生成依赖于强大的调节信号，最显着的是无分类器引导（CFG）The diversity problem is fundamentally difficult due to the structural tension between quality and variety. High-quality generation currently relies on strong conditioning signals, most notably Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG)(Ho and Salimans, 2022)，它通过抑制附近语义上有效的替代方案，有效地锐化单个模式周围的概率分布。因此，恢复多样性需要一种有效的机制来克服这种偏见，而不降低图像的结构完整性或失去语义依从性。, which effectively sharpens the probability distribution around a single mode by suppressing nearby semantically valid alternatives. Consequently, restoring diversity requires an efficient mechanism to overcome this bias without degrading the structural integrity of the image or losing semantic adherence.

A key distinction lies in the conditioning mechanism: whereas UNets typically incorporate text via cross-attention layers, DiTs process text and image tokens concurrently within the transformer. This architecture employs multimodal attention blocks to facilitate bidirectional interaction, ensuring a unified integration of visual and textual information throughout the generation process. A growing body of research has successfully employed this architecture across diverse downstream tasks(Avrahami et al., 2025; Tan et al., 2025; Garibi et al., 2025; Labs et al., 2025; Dalva et al., 2024; Kamenetsky et al., 2025; Zarei et al., 2025)

6. 结论Conclusions

在较高的层面上，这项工作强调了扩散变压器中的语境空间是在实现多样性时特别有效的干预场所。上下文空间位于文本和图像之间：这些表示已经编码了由新兴图像形成的丰富语义意图，但它们并未在空间上锁定。与图像潜伏不同，该空间不依赖于空间网格，因此可以在语义上将样本分开，而不会撕裂几何形状或引入视觉伪影。同时，与早期的文本嵌入不同，它具有结构信息，这意味着干预措施会有意义地影响模型实际生成的内容。At a high level, this work highlights the Contextual Space in Diffusion Transformers as a particularly effective place to intervene when aiming for diversity. The Contextual Space sits between text and image: the representations already encode rich semantic intent shaped by the emerging image, yet they are not spatially locked in. Unlike image latents, this space is not tied to a spatial grid, so samples can be pushed apart semantically without tearing geometry or introducing visual artifacts. At the same time, unlike early text embeddings, it is structurally informed, meaning that interventions meaningfully influence what the model actually generates.

在这个空间中应用动态排斥可以以受控的方式增加多样性，而不会牺牲视觉质量或依赖于具有大量计算成本的大量优化。更广泛地说，这指出了在正确的代表性层面进行干预的重要性，在这种层面上，决策仍然灵活，但已经扎根于正在形成的形象。Applying on-the-fly repulsion in this space allows diversity to be increased in a controlled way, without sacrificing visual quality or relying on heavy optimization with significant computational cost. More broadly, this points to the importance of intervening at the right representational level, where decisions are still flexible, but already grounded in the image being formed.

局限性Limitations。上下文排斥增加了多样性，但无法直接控制哪些属性会发生变化，并且有时可能有利于粗略的语义变化而不是精细的、用户指定的语义变化。此外，干预的重点是世代的早期到中期；如何最好地与后续阶段协调，或将其与其他控制机制结合起来，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。. Contextual repulsion increases diversity but does not provide direct control over which attributes will vary, and may sometimes favor coarse semantic changes over fine, user-specified ones. In addition, the intervention is focused on early to mid stages of generation; how to best coordinate it with later stages, or combine it with other control mechanisms, remains an open question.

未来方向Future directions。未来工作的一个有趣方向是研究用户提供的文本提示（例如“颜色”或“大小”）是否可用于引导上下文空间中沿着特定语义方向的排斥。这个想法不是以不受约束的方式鼓励多样性，而是偏置排斥力，以便样本主要沿着与给定单词相关的属性传播。这可以实现更加受控和可解释的多样性形式，其中变化集中在选定的语义方面，而一代的其他部分保持稳定。. An interesting direction for future work is to investigate whether a user provided textual cue, such as “color” or “size”, can be used to guide the repulsion along a specific semantic direction in the Contextual Space. Instead of encouraging diversity in an unconstrained manner, the idea would be to bias the repulsive forces so that samples spread primarily along attributes associated with the given word. This could enable a more controlled and interpretable form of diversity, where variation is focused on selected semantic aspects while other parts of the generation remain stable.

时间步消融Timestep ablation

我们通过在特定时间步间隔内应用干预，同时保持所有其他超参数恒定，来分析排斥窗口对扩散轨迹的影响。表We analyze the impact of the repulsion window across the diffusion trajectory by applying the intervention within specific timestep intervals while keeping all other hyperparameters constant.
