AI 天气模型的物理原理The physics of AI weather models

摘要Abstract

人工智能天气模型是否正在求解物理方程，尽管它们可能不是传统 NWP 模型使用的方程？我们计算预测技能和中心核对齐的相关性，提供证据表明不同的人工智能天气模型以相似的方式表示大气，尽管架构和容量存在差异。我们认为人工智能模型的架构和训练限制了它们可能模拟的物理定律的形式。特别是，我们建议模型实现大气的粒子描述，其中每个网格点的潜在变量对应于高维潜在空间中粒子的位置。我们假设粒子的运动遵循潜在空间中的梯度流，朝向学习的自由能泛函的最小值。对 GraphCast 和 Aurora 模型的分析表明，它们在早期处理器层中在大空间尺度上进行变化，并随着层深度的增加而转向更小的尺度，这与梯度流假设一致。Could it be that AI weather models are solving physical equations, although they may not be the equations used by conventional NWP models? We compute correlations of forecast skill and Centered Kernel Alignment, providing evidence that different AI weather models represent the atmosphere in similar ways, despite differences in architecture and capacity. We argue that the architecture and training of the AI models constrains the form of the physical laws that they might simulate. In particular, we propose that the models implement a particle description of the atmosphere, where the latent variables at each mesh point correspond to the position of a particle in the high dimensional latent space. We hypothesize that the movement of the particles follows a gradient flow in the latent space towards a minimum of a learned free energy functional. Analysis of the GraphCast and Aurora models show that they make changes on large spatial scales in the early processor layers and move to smaller scale with increasing layer depth, consistent with the gradient flow hypothesis.

1 AI天气模型和物理定律AI weather models and physical laws

人工智能（AI）模型最近在多个方面的预测能力已达到甚至超越了传统数值天气预报（NWP）模型Artificial intelligence (AI) models have recently matched and even surpassed the forecast skill of conventional Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) models on a variety of scores(Bi et al., 2023; Lam et al., 2023; Pathak et al., 2022; Bodnar et al., 2025)。人工智能模型正在提供每日天气预报. AI models are providing daily weather forecasts(ECMWF, 2025)、运营气象服务以及科技公司正在大力投资开发新的人工智能模型。, and operational weather services, as well as technology companies, are investing heavily in developing new AI models.

虽然新人工智能模型的技能得到了大气科学家的认可，但这些模型是数据驱动的，经常被批评为“黑匣子”。它们产生预测，但无法实现物理理解。在内部，人工智能模型使用没有直接解释为物理量的潜在变量，经历许多阶段的非线性计算，并依赖于许多（在某些情况下超过十亿）学习参数。人工智能模型中可以包含物理定律，例如物理信息神经网络While the skill of the new AI models is acknowledged by atmospheric scientists, these models are data-driven and frequently criticized as “black boxes”. They produce predictions, but do not enable physical understanding. Internally AI models use latent variables with no direct interpretation as physical quantities, go through many stages of nonlinear calculations, and rely on many (in some cases over a billion) learned parameters. It is possible to include physical laws in AI models, e.g. Physics Informed Neural Networks(Cuomo et al., 2022)，但这不是最近几代人工智能天气模型所做的。这一哲学让人想起大卫·默明（David Mermin）对量子力学哥本哈根解释的著名漫画, but this is not what the recent generations of AI weather models do. The philosophy is reminiscent of David Mermin’s famous caricature of the Copenhagen interpretation of quantum mechanics(Mermin, 1990)：“闭嘴，算算！”: “Shut up and calculate!”

Indeed, the very next sentence in Mermin’s essay is “But I won’t shut up.” We are curious; we use models to help us explore and understand the complexity of the physical world. Physical understanding is the basis for determining where and how models can be applied. This is particularly critical in the case of data-driven models, where it is not先验的a priori显然，他们可以对不严格位于训练数据范围内的情况做出有效的预测。最近的模型在预测日常天气模式方面表现出了良好的性能，尽管天气从未完全重复，但对于它们所训练的数量而言。然而，他们经常因未包含在培训中的数量而失败。obvious that they can produce valid predictions of situations that are not strictly within the bounds of their training data. Recent models have shown good performance in predicting daily weather patterns, for quantities that they have been trained on, even though the weather never exactly repeats. However, they often fail for quantities that have not been included in the training.

其中许多问题已通过模型或其训练制度的进一步发展得到改善，但也存在其他缺陷，例如无法响应较小的初始扰动而产生快速的误差增长Many of these issues have been improved by further developments to the models or their training regimes, but other deficiencies such as the inability to produce rapid error growth in response to small initial perturbations(Lorenz, 1969; Palmer et al., 2014; Selz and Craig, 2023)可能表明数据驱动方法的更根本的局限性。这种担忧对于气候变化的应用至关重要：数据驱动的模型能否准确预测从未发生过的气候？如果我们无法将预测的关键方面追溯到负责的输入和假设，我们如何评估这一点？此外，这些预测是决策者使用的场景和故事情节的基础may indicate more fundamental limitations of the data-driven approach. This concern is crucial for the application to climate change: can a data-driven model make accurate projections of a climate that has never occurred? And how can we assess this if we cannot trace critical aspects of the projection back to the inputs and assumptions that are responsible? Furthermore, these projections are the basis for scenarios and storylines that are used by decision-makers(Shepherd et al., 2018)，并要求模型输出的各个方面之间保持一致，包括未观察到且无法直接学习或评估的数量, and require consistency between all aspects of the model output, including quantities that are not observed and cannot be directly learned or evaluated(Olivetti and Messori, 2024; Sun et al., 2025).

显然，对数据驱动的大气模型的物理理解是可取的，但尚不清楚这种理解将采取什么形式。悲观的观点是，模型进行了复杂形式的曲线拟合和插值，但没有学习物理知识，也没有期望泛化到性能尚未直接验证的问题。更乐观的观点是，这些模型实现了与当前 NWP 模型相同的基本物理定律，但可能采用模糊的形式，并额外调整输出以更接近匹配观测结果。如果这一观点能够得到证实，那么当前有关大气和大气建模的许多知识就可以应用于新模型。第三种更有趣的可能性是，新模型对大气如何运作产生了一种新的、不同的物理理解，在这种情况下，它们可能成为新见解的来源。It is clear that physical understanding of data-driven atmospheric models would be desirable, but it is not clear what form this understanding would take. The pessimistic view is that the models do a sophisticated form of curve fitting and interpolation, but there is no physics learned and no expectation of generalization to problems where the performance has not been directly verified. A more optimistic view is that the models implement the same underlying physical laws as current NWP models but perhaps in an obfuscated form, and with extra tuning of the outputs to closer match observations. If this view could be demonstrated, then much current knowledge of the atmosphere and atmospheric modeling could be applied to the new models. A third, more interesting, possibility is that the new models encode a new and different physical understanding of how the atmosphere works, in which case they could be a source of new insights.

我们的任务是采用以代码和训练参数实现的人工智能天气模型，并提取物理原理。这并不容易——想象一下，给定一个现代数值天气预报或气候模型的代码，并要求写下它实现的方程。凭借一些先见之明，人们可能可以识别动力核心并找到数值近似的流体方程，但人们也会找到基于附加物理（例如辐射传递）或更抽象的底层动力学表示（例如湍流理论或质量通量对流）的参数化。该模型包含通过拟合观察或实验室实验获得的参数，最后是一些经过简单调整以产生所需结果的值。Our task is then to take an AI weather model, as implemented in code and trained parameters, and to extract physical principles. This is not easy – imagine being given the code of a modern NWP or climate model and being asked to write down the equations that it implements. With some fore-knowledge, one could probably identify the dynamical core and find the numerically approximated fluid equations, but one would also find parameterizations, that are based on additional physics (e.g. radiative transfer) or more abstract representations of the underlying dynamics (e.g. turbulence theory or mass flux convection). The model contains parameters obtained by fit to observations or laboratory experiments, and finally, some values that are simply tuned to produce the desired results.

那么，当基本物理定律以不熟悉的近似形式表达时，我们将如何识别它们呢？在大气建模中，基本表示通常被认为是流体运动方程、纳维-斯托克斯方程或某种近似方程，但这不是唯一的选择。如图所示So how will we recognize fundamental physical laws when they are expressed in an unfamiliar and approximated form? In atmospheric modeling, the fundamental representation is usually considered to be the equations of fluid motion, Navier-Stokes or some approximation, but this is not the only option. As illustrated in Fig.1，存在物理理论的层次结构。流体方程是分子运动的近似，但人们可以对自然进行更基本的描述，并最终一直到粒子物理学的标准模型。, there is a hierarchy of physical theories. The fluid equations are an approximation to molecular motions, but one could go to even more fundamental descriptions of nature, and eventually all the way to the standard model of particle physics.

一般来说，当我们转向更基本的描述水平时，我们获得了准确性和通用性，但失去了计算效率。另一方面，当我们向上移动层次结构时，物理模型基于实体，这些实体是较低级别解决方案中的新兴现象。例如，在适当的条件下，分子运动将组织成流体动力流，可以用连续近似有效地表示。自由度的数量减少了，解的计算变得更加有效，但是更高层次的方程的有效性范围只是更基本的表示所涵盖的情况的一个子集。 随着我们转向更抽象的流体运动理论，例如湍流的壳模型，甚至均匀和各向同性湍流的动能谱的柯尔莫哥洛夫标度，这些趋势仍在继续。这些简化的动机不是计算效率，而是简化模型中可能的更全面的理解。In general, as we move to a more fundamental level of description, we gain accuracy and generality but lose computational efficiency. On the other hand, as we move up the hierarchy, the physical models are based on entities that are emergent phenomena in lower level solutions. For example, under the right conditions, molecular motions will organize into hydrodynamic streaming flows that can be represented efficiently in a continuum approximation. The number of degrees of freedom is reduced and the computation of solutions becomes more efficient, but the range of validity of the higher level equations is only a subset of the situations covered by the more fundamental representation. These trends continue as we move to more abstract theories of fluid motions, such as shell models of turbulence, or even the Kolmogorov scaling for the kinetic energy spectrum of homogeneous and isotropic turbulence. The motivation for these simplifications is not computational efficiency but rather the more comprehensive understanding that is possible in a simplified model.

本文的目的是提出这样一个假设：人工智能天气模型确实代表了物理学，但不是作为流体方程，而是作为一种更抽象的新型理论。如果这是真的，那么模型不仅有可能运行得更快，而且还可以为大气流动提供新的物理见解。The aim of this paper is to advance the hypothesis that AI weather models are indeed representing physics, but not as fluid equations, rather as a new type of theory that is more abstract. If this is true, there is potential not only for models that run faster, but that also give new physical insights into the atmospheric flow.

2 所有人工智能天气模型都一样吗？Are all AI weather models alike?

如果人工智能天气模型是物理定律的新表示，那么它们在某种意义上应该表现相似，尽管架构和规模（参数数量）存在差异。在本节中，我们考虑两种相似性度量，一种基于预测技能（相似的行为），一种基于内部表示（相似的学习结构）。If AI weather models are a new representation of physical laws, they should in some sense behave similarly, despite differences in architecture and scale (number of parameters). In this section, we consider two measures of similarity, one based on forecast skill (similar behavior) and one based on internal representation (similar learned structure).

2.2 潜在空间表示的普遍性Universality of the latent space representation

不同人工智能天气模型之间预测技能的相关性增加了模型本身在某种意义上相似的可能性。现在我们将考虑不同模型学习到的大气状态的内部表示是否也相似。比较人工智能模型的内部结构并不直接，因为气象信息被编码成没有直接物理解释的潜在空间变量。甚至潜在空间的尺寸也因模型而异。The correlation of forecast skill between different AI weather models raises the possibility that the models themselves are in some sense similar. Now we will consider whether the internal representations of atmospheric states learned by different models is also similar. It is not straight-forward to compare the internal structure of AI models since the meteorological information is encoded into latent space variables that have no direct physical interpretation. Even the dimension of the latent space varies between models.

为了比较不同模型的潜在空间表示，我们将利用这样一个事实：我们可以单独比较每个模型内的不同网格点。后续机器学习其他应用的工作To compare the latent space representations of different models, we will take advantage of the fact that we can compare different mesh points within each model individually. Following work in other applications of machine learning(Huh et al., 2024; Edamadaka et al., 2025)，我们将问题改写为询问两个模型是否认为相同的网格点对相似。特别是，对于每个模型，我们计算对应于不同时间的成对网格点的潜在空间向量之间的内积。对于对应于相同位置和时间的网格点对，可以将一个模型计算出的内积与另一模型的内积进行比较。两个模型计算的值之间的归一化相关性提供了两个模型通过相似向量表示相似状态的程度的度量。正式地，该方法称为中心核分析 (CKA)，是希尔伯特-施密特独立准则的归一化版本, we will rephrase the question to ask whether the two models consider the same pairs of mesh points to be similar. In particular, for each model, we compute the inner product between latent space vectors of pairs of mesh points corresponding to different times. The inner product computed from one model can be compared to that of the other model for pairs of mesh points corresponding to the same locations and times. The normalized correlation between the values computed by the two models provides a measure of the extent to which the two models are representing similar states by similar vectors. Formally, this method is referred to as Centered Kernel Analysis (CKA), and is the normalized version of the Hilbert-Schmidt independence criterion(Kornblith et al., 2019; Huh et al., 2024).

CKA 的一个潜在问题是，内积相似性的变化可能由一年中不同时间的点对主导，因此不同模型之间的高度相关性可能仅仅由季节周期产生。为了提供更具体的测试，我们还将考虑 CKA 的变体，即居中核最近邻对齐 (CKNNA)，它将相关性限制为最相似的网格点对子集。A potential problem with CKA is that the variation in inner-product similarity may be dominated by pairs of points at different times of year, so that a high correlation between different models could arise simply from the seasonal cycle. To provide a more specific test, we will also consider a variant of CKA, Centered Kernel Nearest Neighbor Alignment (CKNNA), which restricts the correlation to the subset of mesh point pairs that are most similar.

6.1 从梯度流到连续时间演化From gradient flow to continuous time evolution

为了完整地了解人工智能天气模型可以解决哪些微分方程，我们还必须考虑到该模型是自回归应用的，因此模型的每次应用都会以有限的时间步长推进预测To complete the picture of what differential equations might be solved by an AI weather model, we must also consider that the model is applied autoregressively, so that each application of the model advances the forecast by finite timestepΔ​t\Delta t。对于全球模型，通常选择 6 小时。以时间步长为极限，得到时间微分方程. Different AI weather models will find the same minima. There is no requirement that the flow must reach a minimum, so this must be regarded as an additional hypothesis that must be tested empirically.

6.3 梯度流的证据Evidence for gradient flow

尽管梯度流假设无法从模型的结构中严格推导出来，但我们可以在计算预测时寻找证据来证明它描述了观察到的行为。 潜在空间坐标流向静止状态的一个指示是速度（即每个处理器层添加的增量的大小）随着接近最终状态而减小。不幸的是，不可能直接比较各层的幅度，因为潜在空间向量仅定义为任意仿射变换，每个层可能不同Although the gradient flow hypothesis cannot be derived rigorously from the structure of the models, we can look for evidence that it describes their observed behavior while computing a forecast. One indication of a flow of the latent space coordinates towards stationary state would be that the velocity, i.e. the size of the increments added by each processor layer, decreases as the final state is approached. Unfortunately, it is not possible to directly compare the magnitudes across layers because the latent space vector is only defined up to an arbitrary affine transform that may be different for each layer(Elhage et al., 2021)。在本小节中，我们将分两个阶段解决这个问题。. In this subsection, we will address this problem in two stages.

为了避免比较不同层的增量幅度（其中表示可能因仿射变换而不同），我们考虑潜在空间通道的空间方差谱，它与绝对幅度无关。这样做的动机是，大气中最大的变化发生在大空间尺度上，预计状态的大增量应该在许多网格点上相关。在这种情况下，增量的幅度随着处理器深度而减小的趋势将与增量的空间尺度的减小相关联。尽管潜在空间向量的绝对大小是任意的，但同一层深度的网格点之间的相对大小是有意义的。这使我们能够计算由光谱质心定义的增量的特征波数：方差加权平均波数。To avoid comparing the magnitude of the increments across different layers where the representations my differ by an affine transform, we consider the spatial variance spectra of the latent space channels, which is independent of the absolute magnitude. The motivation for this is the fact that the largest variability in the atmosphere occurs on large spatial scales, it would be expected that large increments to the state should be correlated over many mesh points. In this case, a tendency for the magnitude of the increments to decrease with processor depth would be associated with decrease in the spatial scale of the increments. Although the absolute magnitude of the latent space vector is arbitrary, the relative size between mesh points at the same layer depth is meaningful. This allows us to compute a characteristic wavenumber of the increment, defined by the spectral centroid: the variance-weighted average wavenumber.

要克服的第二个问题是，我们希望对不同潜在空间通道上的光谱进行平均，以获得代表整体增量的光谱。但同样，这在层之间是不可比较的，因为潜在空间向量的分量可能相对于彼此重新缩放。为了纠正这个问题，我们尝试使用翻译器将不同层的表示与公共基础对齐。 翻译器学习映射中间潜在状态的仿射变换A second issue to be overcome is that we would like to average the spectra over the different latent space channels to get a spectrum that is representative of the increment as a whole. But again this will not be comparable between layers since the component of the latent space vector may be rescaled relative to one another. To correct for this we attempt to align the representation at different layers to a common basis using translators. Translators are learned affine transformations that map intermediate latent statesX~i\tilde{X}_{i}到最终的潜伏状态to the final latent stateX~F\tilde{X}_{F} (Belrose et al., 2023)。翻译器由单个学习的线性变换组成.
