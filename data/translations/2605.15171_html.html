1.1 目前的限制Current Limitations

我们确定了开发可解释的现实世界疾病筛查方法的三个主要局限性：We identify three primary limitations in developing interpretable real-world disease screening methods:

缺乏面向临床的评估框架。Lack of a clinically oriented evaluation framework.虽然疾病筛查通常被视为异常检测问题，但现有的评估方案无法满足现实世界的临床需求。 首先，标准性能指标，例如 ROC 曲线下面积 (AUROC)，与临床要求不符。 其次，当前的基准通常无法评估模型的通用性，因为它们通常缺乏对外部测试集的实际测试。While disease screening is often framed as an anomaly detection problem, existing evaluation protocols fall short of real-world clinical needs. Firstly, standard performance metrics, such as the area under the ROC curve (AUROC), are not aligned with clinical requirements. Secondly, current benchmarks often fail to assess model generalizability, as they typically lack real-world testing on external test sets.

主流筛查渠道中的证据意识有限。Limited evidence awareness in mainstream screening pipelines.许多模型的可解释性依赖于事后显着图。 虽然这些地图突出显示了被认为重要的区域，但它们缺乏基于案例的证据来解释为什么某个区域看起来病态，从而限制了它们与专家推理的一致性。 基于原型的可解释方法通过将图像区域与学习的原型或历史示例进行比较来部分解决这个问题。 然而，它们的表征能力受到训练前指定的固定数量的原型的限制，这可能不足以涵盖现实世界筛查中遇到的各种病理表现。The interpretability of many models relies on post-hoc saliency maps. While these maps highlight regions deemed important, they lack case-based evidence explaining why a region appears pathological, limiting their alignment with expert reasoning. Prototype-based interpretable methods partially address this issue by comparing image regions with learned prototypes or historical examples. However, their representational capacity is bounded by a fixed number of prototypes specified before training, which may be insufficient to cover the diverse pathological appearances encountered in real-world screening.

从病理病例中挖掘颗粒证据不足。Insufficient mining of granular evidence from pathological cases.基于偏差的异常检测方法可以有效地识别偏离正常的情况，但无法利用病理病例中包含的丰富、精细的信息。 核心挑战在于病理图像包括Deviation-based anomaly detection methods can effectively identify what deviates from normal, but cannot leverage the rich, granular information contained within pathological cases.
