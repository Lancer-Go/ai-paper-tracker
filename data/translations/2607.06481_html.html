用于参数高效的多镜头长视频外推的提示适配器上下文路由Prompt-Adapter Context Routing for Parameter-Efficient Multi-Shot Long Video Extrapolation

Anna Córdoba  Adam Puente Tercero  Nerea Angulo Hijo  Mar Linares Tercero
Julia Barrientos  Ainhoa Miranda  Jesús Olivera
Instituto de Investigación en Visión Artificial
contact@iiva.tibeu
摘要Abstract

我们提出了 PACR-Video，这是一种用于多镜头长视频外推的参数高效框架，可以保留重复实体、场景结构、视觉风格和因果进程，而无需对生成器进行全面微调。 PACR-Video 保持文本到视频的扩散转换器冻结，并通过学习的镜头角色提示令牌调节的低阶时间适配器对其进行增强。为了保持长期连贯性，它构建了一个递归提示库，存储先前镜头中的紧凑实体、位置、动作和风格提示，然后根据预测的叙事依赖性将它们路由到适配器门。镜头局部/故事全局调整目标结合了下一个镜头重建、跨镜头身份对比和即时稀疏正则化，而适配器组合时间表平衡了早期镜头视觉一致性与后期镜头事件进展和视点变化。在六个多镜头和长视频基准测试中，PACR-Video 在分布质量、语义对齐、身份一致性、时间平滑性、运动稳定性、转换连贯性和人类偏好方面优于文本到视频、基于调整、内存增强、流媒体和递归上下文基线。这些结果表明，紧凑的即时路由和轻量级的时间自适应为稳定的长视频外推提供了足够的可控能力。We present PACR-Video, a parameter-efficient framework for multi-shot long video extrapolation that preserves recurring entities, scene structure, visual style, and causal progression without full generator fine-tuning. PACR-Video keeps a text-to-video diffusion transformer frozen and augments it with low-rank temporal adapters conditioned by learned shot-role prompt tokens. To maintain long-horizon coherence, it builds a recursive prompt bank that stores compact entity, location, action, and style prompts from previous shots, then routes them through adapter gates according to predicted narrative dependencies. A Shot-Local/Story-Global tuning objective combines next-shot reconstruction, cross-shot identity contrast, and prompt sparsity regularization, while an adapter composition schedule balances early-shot visual consistency with later-shot event progression and viewpoint change. Across six multi-shot and long-video benchmarks, PACR-Video outperforms text-to-video, tuning-based, memory-augmented, streaming, and recursive-context baselines on distributional quality, semantic alignment, identity consistency, temporal smoothness, motion stability, transition coherence, and human preference. These results show that compact prompt routing and lightweight temporal adaptation provide sufficient controllable capacity for stable long video extrapolation.

1 简介Introduction

长视频生成需要的不仅仅是延长剪辑持续时间。连贯的多镜头序列必须保留重复出现的角色、空间布局、视觉风格、对象可供性和因果事件进展，同时允许每个镜头引入新的动作、观点和叙事信息。这种平衡在文本调节生成中尤其困难：调节信号通常位于下一个剪辑的本地，而最重要的错误会在镜头中累积。因此，长视频系统经常会出现身份漂移、重复或忘记对象、扁平化事件结构或过度适应早期视觉环境。Long-form video generation requires more than extending clip duration. A coherent multi-shot sequence must preserve recurring characters, spatial layout, visual style, object affordances, and causal event progression while allowing each shot to introduce new motion, viewpoint, and narrative information. This balance is especially difficult in text-conditioned generation: conditioning signals are often local to the next clip, whereas the errors that matter most accumulate across shots. As a result, long video systems frequently drift in identity, duplicate or forget objects, flatten event structure, or overfit to early visual context.

最近的工作通过明确的故事记忆、镜头规划和递归上下文分配取得了进展Recent work has made progress through explicit story memories, shot planning, and recursive context allocationZhang et al. (2025b); Luo et al. (2026); Zheng et al. (2025); Liu et al. (2026b)。 ReCA 特别相关：它将多镜头外推作为一个递归分配问题，决定哪个先前的上下文应该调节每个未来的镜头. ReCA is particularly relevant: it frames multi-shot extrapolation as a recursive allocation problem, deciding which previous context should condition each future shotLiu et al. (2026b)。然而，许多现有方法要么对生成器的大部分进行微调，要么依赖外部存储器模块，其容量和检索行为在长期内可能会变得脆弱。这就提出了一个自然的问题：仅使用轻量级、参数高效的模块能否以足够的精度控制冻结的视频扩散骨干网？. However, many existing approaches either fine-tune large portions of the generator or rely on external memory modules whose capacity and retrieval behavior can become brittle over long horizons. This raises a natural question: can a frozen video diffusion backbone be steered with enough precision using only lightweight, parameter-efficient modules?

我们提出了 PACR-Video，一种用于参数高效的多镜头长视频外推的提示适配器上下文路由框架。 PACR-Video 将低等级时间适配器插入冻结的文本到视频扩散转换器，并使用学习的镜头角色提示标记对其进行调节。该模型没有存储密集的视频内存，而是维护一个递归提示库，其中包含从以前的镜头中提取的紧凑实体、位置、动作和风格提示。在生成时，这些提示根据预测的叙述依赖性强度通过适配器门进行路由，从而允许模型重用相关上下文，同时抑制陈旧或分散注意力的历史记录。We propose PACR-Video, a Prompt-Adapter Context Routing framework for parameter-efficient multi-shot long video extrapolation. PACR-Video inserts low-rank temporal adapters into a frozen text-to-video diffusion transformer and conditions them with learned shot-role prompt tokens. Instead of storing dense video memories, the model maintains a recursive prompt bank containing compact entity, location, action, and style prompts extracted from previous shots. At generation time, these prompts are routed through adapter gates according to predicted narrative dependency strength, allowing the model to reuse relevant context while suppressing stale or distracting history.

我们的贡献是四重的。首先，我们介绍 Prompt-Adapter Context Routing，这是一种参数高效的替代方案，可以替代多镜头外推的完整生成器微调。其次，我们提出了一个递归提示库，用于存储结构化的镜头级提示并通过门控时间适配器路由它们。第三，我们设计了一个镜头局部/故事全局调整目标，结合了下一镜头重建、跨镜头身份对比和提示稀疏正则化以减少长范围漂移。第四，我们引入了一个适配器组合计划，该计划重用早期镜头适配器以实现视觉一致性，同时激活后期适配器以实现事件进展和视点变化。Our contributions are fourfold. First, we introduce Prompt-Adapter Context Routing, a parameter-efficient alternative to full generator fine-tuning for multi-shot extrapolation. Second, we propose a recursive prompt bank that stores structured shot-level prompts and routes them through gated temporal adapters. Third, we design a Shot-Local/Story-Global tuning objective combining next-shot reconstruction, cross-shot identity contrast, and prompt sparsity regularization to reduce long-horizon drift. Fourth, we introduce an adapter composition schedule that reuses early-shot adapters for visual consistency while activating later adapters for event progression and viewpoint change.

我们根据不同的故事和教学视频基准评估 PACR-Video，包括 FlintstonesSV、Pororo-SV、ActivityNet Captions、YouCook2、Shot2Story 和 MovieNetWe evaluate PACR-Video on diverse story and instructional video benchmarks, including FlintstonesSV, Pororo-SV, ActivityNet Captions, YouCook2, Shot2Story, and MovieNetHan et al. Across comparisons with text-to-video, tuning-based, streaming, memory-augmented, and recursive-context baselines such as VideoCrafter2, ModelScopeT2V, AnimateDiff, Tune-A-Video, Text2Video-Zero, SEINE, StoryMem, ShotStream, and ReCAZhang et al. (2025b); Luo et al. (2026); Liu et al. (2026b)，PACR-Video 在分布质量、语义对齐、身份保存、时间一致性、转换连贯性和人类偏好方面取得了巨大的进步，同时保持主干网冻结。, PACR-Video yields substantial gains in distributional quality, semantic alignment, identity preservation, temporal consistency, transition coherence, and human preference while keeping the backbone frozen.

(2026)。流式和代理公式通过在线更新上下文进一步解决交互式或视野扩展生成问题. Streaming and agentic formulations further address interactive or horizon-extending generation by updating context onlineLuo et al. (2026); Long et al. PACR-Video shares the goal of long-horizon coherence, but differs by replacing large external memories and full-generator adaptation with compact prompt banks routed through lightweight temporal adapters.

PACR-Video builds directly on this allocation view, but moves the controllable capacity into parameter-efficient adapter gates and learned shot-role prompt tokens. This makes context routing differentiable, sparse, and compatible with a frozen diffusion transformer backbone.

(2026); Chen et al. (2025)。 PACR-Video 在操作上使用这些见解：其递归提示库存储实体、位置、动作和风格提示，其镜头本地/故事全局目标明确地将下一个镜头重建与跨镜头身份保留保持一致。. PACR-Video uses these insights operationally: its recursive prompt bank stores entity, location, action, and style prompts, and its Shot-Local/Story-Global objective explicitly aligns next-shot reconstruction with cross-shot identity preservation.

In contrast to methods that tune the full generator or attach high-capacity memory modules, PACR-Video concentrates adaptation in low-rank temporal adapters, regularizes prompt sparsity, and composes early- and late-shot adapters to balance visual consistency with event progression.

推论。Inference.

在推理时，PACR-Video 以自回归方式进行。对于每个新镜头， 路由器读取当前的提示库，预测叙述依赖性，激活 相应的时间适配器，并用冻结生成下一个镜头 扩散骨干。然后生成的镜头被概括为实体、位置、 操作和样式提示并附加到银行。因为银行商店 紧凑的提示和骨干网被冻结，推理成本随着 镜头数量，不需要对每个故事进行全模型微调。At inference time, PACR-Video proceeds autoregressively. For each new shot, the router reads the current prompt bank, predicts narrative dependencies, activates the corresponding temporal adapters, and generates the next shot with the frozen diffusion backbone. The generated shot is then summarized into entity, location, action, and style prompts and appended to the bank. Because the bank stores compact prompts and the backbone is frozen, inference cost grows slowly with the number of shots and does not require full-model fine-tuning for each story.

效率。Efficiency.

PACR-Video 调整了 3.8% 的主干参数计数，并且不更新文本编码器或扩散变压器权重。相比之下，Tune-A-Video 和长上下文基线的完全调整变体需要更大的可训练参数预算。在推理时，递归提示库仅存储紧凑的实体、位置、动作和风格提示向量，因此内存随着镜头数量而不是生成帧的数量而增长。这使得 PACR-Video 对于多镜头外推变得实用，同时保持冻结生成器的稳定性。PACR-Video tunes 3.8% of the backbone parameter count and does not update the text encoder or diffusion transformer weights. In contrast, full tuning variants of Tune-A-Video and long-context baselines require substantially larger trainable parameter budgets. At inference time, the recursive prompt bank stores only compact entity, location, action, and style prompt vectors, so memory grows with the number of shots rather than the number of generated frames. This makes PACR-Video practical for multi-shot extrapolation while retaining the stability of the frozen generator.

Adapter gates are also critical: ungated adapters provide extra capacity, but they cannot suppress irrelevant prompt-bank entries, leading to worse identity consistency and higher temporal error.

时间适配器占据了大部分参数有效的适应优势。当它们被删除并且只保留提示调整时，性能会下降到接近基于内存的基线，特别是在 DINO 身份一致性和 RAFT 扭曲错误方面。客观条件起到互补作用。交叉镜头身份对比度损失改善了重复字符的保留，而即时稀疏性则防止路由器对陈旧历史记录进行过度调节。最后，删除适配器组成时间表会损害过渡连贯性和运动稳定性，这证实了重复使用早期镜头适配器的外观，同时激活后来的适配器以进行事件进展对于长视野外推是有用的。Temporal adapters account for most of the parameter-efficient adaptation benefit. The objective terms contribute complementary effects. The cross-shot identity contrast loss improves recurring character preservation, while prompt sparsity prevents the router from over-conditioning on stale history. Finally, removing the adapter composition schedule hurts transition coherence and motion stability, confirming that reusing early-shot adapters for appearance while activating later adapters for event progression is useful for long-horizon extrapolation.

6 结论Conclusion

PACR-Video 为多镜头长视频外推提供了一个参数高效的框架，该框架使文本到视频的扩散主干保持冻结，同时通过轻量级时间适配器路由紧凑的历史提示。通过结合学习的镜头角色提示标记、递归提示库、门控适配器路由和镜头本地/故事全局调整目标，PACR-Video 可以分配跨镜头上下文，而无需依赖完整的生成器微调或大型外部内存模块。这种设计扩展了 ReCA 的递归分配视角PACR-Video presents a parameter-efficient framework for multi-shot long video extrapolation that keeps the text-to-video diffusion backbone frozen while routing compact historical prompts through lightweight temporal adapters. By combining learned shot-role prompt tokens, a recursive prompt bank, gated adapter routing, and a Shot-Local/Story-Global tuning objective, PACR-Video allocates cross-shot context without relying on full generator fine-tuning or large external memory modules. This design extends the recursive allocation perspective of ReCALiu et al. (2026b)同时使可控内存稀疏、可微且具有适配器条件。while making the controllable memory sparse, differentiable, and adapter-conditioned.

7 未来的工作Future Work

PACR-Video 留下了几个开放的方向。首先，当前的提示库存储紧凑的实体、位置、操作和风格提示，但它没有明确对这些摘要中的不确定性进行建模。未来的工作可以使即时条目成为概率性的，从而允许路由器在推断很长的范围时将持久的事实与模糊或短暂的证据区分开来。PACR-Video leaves several directions open. First, the current prompt bank stores compact entity, location, action, and style prompts, but it does not explicitly model uncertainty in these summaries. Future work could make prompt entries probabilistic, allowing the router to distinguish persistent facts from ambiguous or transient evidence when extrapolating over very long horizons.

其次，PACR-Video 假设镜头级提示可用。一个自然的扩展是将提示适配器路由与自动镜头规划和修订结合起来，以便共同优化生成、依赖性预测和叙事分解。这可以将参数有效的外推与基于规划的长视频系统连接起来Second, PACR-Video assumes shot-level prompts are available. A natural extension is to couple prompt-adapter routing with automatic shot planning and revision, so that generation, dependency prediction, and narrative decomposition are optimized jointly.
