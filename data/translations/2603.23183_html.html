语义 ID 推理增强生成推荐Reasoning over Semantic IDs Enhances Generative Recommendation

摘要。Abstract.

生成推荐领域的最新进展利用了预训练的 LLM，将顺序推荐制定为在包含语言标记和项目标识符的统一标记空间上的自回归生成，其中每个项目由离散标记的紧凑序列（即语义 ID (SID)）表示。Recent advances in generative recommendation have leveraged pretrained LLMs by formulating sequential recommendation as autoregressive generation over a unified token space comprising language tokens and itemic identifiers, where each item is represented by a compact sequence of discrete tokens, namely Semantic IDs (SIDs). 这种基于 SID 的公式可以对大规模项目语料库进行高效解码，并为基于 LLM 的推荐器提供一个自然的接口，以利用丰富的世界知识。This SID-based formulation enables efficient decoding over large-scale item corpora and provides a natural interface for LLM-based recommenders to leverage rich world knowledge. 与此同时，法学硕士推理的突破激发了推理增强推荐，但对 SID 的有效推理仍然没有得到充分探索和挑战。Meanwhile, breakthroughs in LLM reasoning motivate reasoning-enhanced recommendation, yet effective reasoning over SIDs remains underexplored and challenging. 项目代币对于法学硕士来说本身并没有意义；此外，面向推荐的SID推理难以评估，导致高质量的监督稀缺。Itemic tokens are not natively meaningful to LLMs; moreover, recommendation-oriented SID reasoning is hard to evaluate, making high-quality supervision scarce.

为了应对这些挑战，我们建议To address these challenges, we proposeSID推理机SIDReasoner，一个两阶段框架，通过加强 SID 语言对齐来解锁可转移的 LLM 推理，而不是依赖于大量特定于推荐的推理轨迹，从而引发对 SID 的推理。, a two-stage framework that elicits reasoning over SIDs by strengthening SID–language alignment to unlock transferable LLM reasoning, rather than relying on large amounts of recommendation-specific reasoning traces. 具体来说，SIDReasoner 首先通过对由更强大的教师模型合成的丰富的以 SID 为中心的语料库进行多任务训练来增强 SID 语言对齐，将项目标记扎根于不同的语义和行为上下文中。 在此增强的对齐基础上，SIDReasoner 通过结果驱动的强化优化进一步改进了推荐推理，从而引导模型走向有效的推理轨迹，而无需明确的推理注释。Concretely, SIDReasoner first enhances SID-language alignment via multi-task training on an enriched SID-centered corpus synthesized by a stronger teacher model, grounding itemic tokens in diverse semantic and behavioral contexts. Building on this enhanced alignment, SIDReasoner further improves recommendation reasoning through outcome-driven reinforced optimization, which guides the model toward effective reasoning trajectories without requiring explicit reasoning annotations. 对三个真实世界数据集的大量实验证明了我们基于 SID 的推理增强生成推荐的有效性。 除了准确性之外，结果还凸显了大型推理模型在生成推荐方面的更广泛潜力，包括改进的可解释性和跨领域泛化。Extensive experiments on three real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our reasoning-augmented SID-based generative recommendation. Beyond accuracy, the results highlight the broader potential of large reasoning models for generative recommendation, including improved interpretability and cross-domain generalization.我们的代码可在Our codes are available athttps://github.com/HappyPointer/SIDReasoner.

1. 简介Introduction

生成式推荐最近已成为顺序推荐的一种有前景的范式，并且越来越多地关注基于语义 ID (SID) 的方法Generative recommendation has recently emerged as a promising paradigm for sequential recommendation with an increasing attention on Semantic ID (SID)-based methods(Rajput et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024a; Zhou et al., 2025b, c; Kong et al., 2025b)。 通过将项目的语义嵌入量化为离散标识符的紧凑序列（称为语义 ID (SID)）. By quantizing an item’s semantic embedding into a compact sequence of discrete identifiers called Semantic IDs (SID)(Hou et al., 2023; Rajput et al., 2023)，项目推荐可以表示为 SID 序列上的自回归生成任务。 这种序列预测范式自然地与 Transformer 架构保持一致， 促进生成方法与预训练的大语言模型（LLM）的无缝集成，其丰富的语义知识可以提高推荐质量和跨领域泛化, item recommendation can be formulated as autoregressive generation task over SID sequences. This sequence prediction paradigm naturally aligns with the Transformer architecture, facilitating seamless integration of generative methods with pretrained large language models (LLMs), whose rich semantic knowledge can facilitate recommendation quality and cross-domain generalization(Zheng et al., 2024; He et al., 2025)。 尽管取得了这些进步，推理仍然是法学硕士近期突破背后的核心能力之一. Despite these advances, reasoning, one of the central capabilities behind recent breakthroughs of LLMs(Wei et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2025; Hou et al., 2025b; Guo et al., 2025a)，仍处于生成推荐探索的早期阶段，其全部潜力尚未被发掘。, remains at an early stage of exploration in generative recommendation, with its full potential yet to be uncovered.

现有的推荐推理方法主要用文本描述来表示项目，利用法学硕士丰富的世界知识来理解项目语义并推断交互背后的用户意图Existing approaches to reasoning in recommendation predominantly represent items with textual descriptions, leveraging the rich world knowledge of LLMs to comprehend item semantics and infer user intentions behind interactions(Tsai et al., 2024; You et al., 2025; Fang et al., 2025; Tang et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025d; Yu et al., 2025)。 这些方法可以大致分为两种范式：显式推理和潜在推理。 显式推理生成自然语言原理来明确表达用户偏好. These methods can be broadly categorized into two paradigms: explicit reasoning and latent reasoning. Explicit reasoning generates natural language rationales to explicitly articulate user preferences(Tsai et al., 2024; Fang et al., 2025; Zeng et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025; You et al., 2025; Kong et al., 2025a)。潜在推理通过跳过显式思维链生成来提高效率，但牺牲了可解释性和可控性. The latent reasoning improves efficiency by skipping explicit chain-of-thought generation, but sacrifices interpretability and controllability(Tang et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025d; Lin et al., 2025; Dai et al., 2025)。 然而，纯粹用文本描述来表示项目会遇到严重的解码效率低下和项目接地问题，限制了其在现实世界系统中的实用性. However, representing items purely with textual descriptions suffers from severe decoding inefficiency and item grounding issues, limiting its practicality in real-world systems(Li et al., 2025)。 最近的一个有希望的方向通过用 SID 表示项目并将 SID 令牌扩展到 LLM 词汇表来避免此类问题。经过 SID 语言对齐训练后，模型可以在统一表示空间内用语言进行推理并在 SID 中进行推荐. A recent promising direction avoids such issues by representing items with SIDs and expanding SID tokens into the LLM vocabulary. After the SID-language alignment training, the model can reason in language and recommend in SIDs within a unified representation space(He et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025)。 虽然这种范式为推荐推理提供了新的潜力，但其有效性关键取决于 SID 语言对齐的质量，并且现有的成功很大程度上依赖于具有大量计算成本的工业规模预训练。. While this paradigm offers new potential for reasoning in recommendation, its effectiveness critically depends on the quality of SID–language alignment, and existing successes largely rely on industrial-scale pretraining with substantial computational cost.

在这项工作中，我们研究了如何通过 SID 实现生成推荐的有效且数据高效的推理。 推荐中的推理本质上是具有挑战性的，因为它对无法直接观察到的隐式用户偏好进行建模。 这种隐含性带来了两个根本性的挑战：首先，高质量的推理监督是稀缺且昂贵的。 其次，鉴于用户偏好的隐含性质，推荐推理的质量很难评估。In this work, we investigate how to achieve effective and data-efficient reasoning for generative recommendation over SIDs. Reasoning in recommendation is inherently challenging, as it models implicit user preferences that are not directly observable. This implicitness gives rise to two fundamental challenges: first, high-quality reasoning supervision is scarce and expensive to obtain. Second, the quality of recommendation reasoning is hard to evaluate given the implicit nature of user preferences.

Concretely, we establish SID–language grounding through multi-task alignment, where the model comprehend semantics of SID tokens through a wide range of recommendation-related data. We further leverage a large teacher model with rich world knowledge to perform semantic expansion based on item titles, synthesizing more diverse and informative SID-centered descriptive texts. This enriched corpus exposes the model to broader and more fine-grained semantic associations for SID tokens, strengthening their alignment with natural language. Second, once the model attains a comprehensive understanding of SIDs and can transfer general knowledge to recommendation tasks, we enable it to self-explore reasoning patterns that are effective for recommendation. In the absence of a direct evaluation criterion for reasoning quality, we adopt Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)(Shao et al., 2024)提供基于结果的反馈并引导模型走向有效的推理轨迹。 对三个真实世界数据集的广泛实验表明，通过丰富的 SID 语言对齐，即使在学术规模的数据集上也可以实现有效的 SID 对齐和推理，使法学硕士能够以数据高效的方式执行有效的基于推理的生成推荐。 除了有效性之外，我们的实证结果还进一步揭示了推荐推理的更广泛潜力，包括强大的跨领域泛化能力和通过显式推理过程提高可解释性。to provide outcome-based feedback and steer the model toward effective reasoning trajectories. Extensive experiments on three real-world datasets demonstrate that, with enriched SID–language alignment, effective SID alignment and reasoning can be achieved even at academic-scale dataset, enabling LLMs to perform effective reasoning-based generative recommendation in a data-efficient manner. Beyond effectiveness, our empirical results further reveal the broader potential of reasoning in recommendation, including strong cross-domain generalization and improved interpretability through explicit reasoning processes.

2. 相关工作Related Work

在本节中，我们从两个主要类别简要回顾与本文相关的工作：1）生成推荐，2）大型推理模型。In this section, we briefly review the works related to this paper from two main categories: 1) generative recommendation, and 2) large reasoning models.

2.1. 生成推荐Generative Recommendation

随着法学硕士和 Transformer 架构的日益成功，生成推荐已成为推荐系统中一个有前途的研究方向。现有的生成推荐研究可大致分为三类：基于稀疏 ID 的方法、基于文本的方法和基于语义 ID 的方法。With the growing success of LLMs and the transformer architecture, generative recommendation has emerged as a promising research direction in the recommender systems. Existing studies in generative recommendation can be broadly classified into three categories: sparse ID-based methods, text-based methods, and semantic ID-based methods.

基于稀疏 ID 的方法Sparse ID-based methods将每个项目表示为唯一标识符，并将用户交互序列化为令牌序列，将推荐制定为下一个令牌预测任务represent each item as a unique identifier and serialize user interactions into token sequences, formulating recommendation as next-token prediction tasks(Zhai et al., 2024; Geng et al., 2022; Agarwal et al., 2025)。现有的探索沿着两个主要方向发展：结合额外的功能或上下文以实现可控生成. Existing explorationss has evolved along two major directions: incorporating additional features or contexts for controllable generation(Han et al., 2025; Yan et al., 2025; Agarwal et al., 2025)，并优化代币结构以提高效率, and optimizing token structures to improve efficiency(Huang et al., 2025; Guo et al., 2025b)。 然而，基于稀疏ID的方法由于输出空间大而存在可扩展性问题，并且在冷启动和长尾场景中无效. However, sparse ID-based methods suffer from scalability issues due to the large output space and are ineffective in cold-start and long-tail scenarios(Li et al., 2025).

基于文本的方法Text-based methods使用自然语言描述来表示每个项目，并生成目标项目的文本描述作为推荐输出represent each item using its natural language description and generate the textual description of the target item as the recommendation output(Cui et al., 2022; Chu et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2025a)。 预训练的语言模型保留了强大的语义理解能力，并在推荐任务上进一步进行了微调. The pretrained language models, which preserve strong semantic understanding capabilities, are further fine-tuned on recommendation tasks(Bao et al., 2023, 2025; Liao et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024)，对冷启动项目表现出很强的泛化能力，并提供更多可解释的建议, exhibiting strong generalization to cold-start items and enabling more interpretable recommendations(Yang et al., 2023; Lei et al., 2024)。 然而，生成长文本序列也会引入大量的计算开销，并且在将输出可靠地接地到现实世界项目方面面临固有的挑战. However, generating long textual sequences also introduces substantial computational overhead and faces inherent challenges in reliably grounding the output to real-world items(Zhang et al., 2025c; Bao et al., 2024)，使得此类方法难以在实际推荐系统中部署。, making such approaches difficult to deploy in practical recommendation systems.

基于语义ID的方法Semantic ID-based methods用通过量化连续项目嵌入获得的短离散代码序列来表示项目represent items with short sequences of discrete codes obtained by quantizing continuous item embeddings(Hou et al., 2023; Rajput et al., 2023; Kong et al., 2025b)。语义ID（SID）提供了语义表达和解码效率之间的权衡，在紧凑的解码空间下保留丰富的语义信息。 随后的研究通过将协作信号集成到 SID 构建中进一步扩展了这一范式. Semantic IDs (SIDs) offer a trade-off between semantic expressiveness and decoding efficiency, preserving rich semantic information under a compact decoding space. Subsequent studies further extend this paradigm by integrating collaborative signals into SID construction(Wang et al., 2024a, b; Xiao et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025b, c)并推进量化方案以提高表示质量或生成效率and advancing quantization schemes to improve representation quality or generation efficiency(Hou et al., 2025a; Wei et al., 2025; Zhong et al., 2025)。 与此同时，最近的工作是通过将 SID 标记与 LLM 的语义空间对齐来探索将语义 ID 集成到预训练的语言模型中. Meanwhile, recent efforts explore the integration of Semantic IDs into pretrained language models by aligning SID tokens with the semantic space of LLMs(Zheng et al., 2024; He et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025a; Hong et al., 2025)。这使得模型能够用自然语言说话，并通过生成 SID 进行推荐。这种集成释放了生成推荐的新功能，包括交互式推荐、可解释的决策以及对用户-项目交互的显式推理. This enables models to speak in natural language as well as recommend by generating SIDs. Such integration unlocks new capabilities of generative recommendation, including interactive recommendation, explainable decision-making, and explicit reasoning over user–item interactions(Liu et al., 2025).

2.2. 大型推理模型Large Reasonging Models

在大型语言模型中，推理将复杂的问题分解为一系列更简单的步骤，并允许模型通过额外的推理过程以更高的置信度进行回答In large language models, reasoning decomposes complex problems into a series of simpler steps and allows the model to answer with higher confidence through an additional reasoning process(Kojima et al., 2022; Zelikman et al., 2022; Snell et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025)。 这最常见地反映在思想链（CoT）中. This is most commonly reflected in Chain-of-Thought (CoT)(Wei et al., 2022)，它引入了显式的令牌级中间步骤，并通过在最终答案之前生成这些步骤来训练或提示模型解决复杂问题。 通过分配额外的推理时间计算，例如通过并行采样，可以进一步提高推理质量, which introduces explicit token-level intermediate steps and trains or prompts the model to solve complex problems by generating these steps prior to the final answer. The quality of reasoning can be further improved by allocating additional inference-time computation, for example through parallel sampling(Wang et al., 2022; Lightman et al., 2023; Xie et al., 2023)，以及迭代细化, and iterative refinement(Kamoi et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2023; Welleck et al., 2022)。此外，除了推理时的启发式控制之外，具有可验证奖励的强化学习提供了一种更直接的方法来完善推理过程，提高中间推理的可靠性和最终的正确性. Moreover, beyond heuristic control at inference time, reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards provides a more direct way to refine the reasoning process, improving both the reliability of intermediate inference and final correctness(Guo et al., 2025a; Hou et al., 2025b; Arora and Zanette, 2025; Aggarwal and Welleck, 2025)。 总体而言，大型推理模型可以理解为显式控制和分配额外测试时间计算以实现更强的问题解决性能的系统. Overall, large reasoning models can be understood as systems that explicitly control and allocate additional test-time computation to achieve stronger problem-solving performance(Wu et al., 2025; Sheng et al., 2025b; Zhang et al., 2025b).

在推荐系统中，推理也被类似地引入，用多步推理过程取代一次性评分，逐步细化用户意图和推荐决策。现有将推理纳入推荐任务的探索可大致分为两个方向：显式推理和潜在推理。 第一个方向，显式推理，依赖显式自然语言原理作为中间推理步骤，实现可解释、可控的多步骤推荐In recommendation systems, reasoning is similarly introduced to replace one-shot scoring with multi-step inference processes that progressively refine user intent and recommendation decisions. Existing explorations of incorporating reasoning into recommendation tasks can be broadly categorized into two directions: explicit reasoning and latent reasoning. The first direction, explicit reasoning, relies on explicit natural language rationales as intermediate inference steps, enabling interpretable and controllable multi-step recommendation(Tsai et al., 2024; Fang et al., 2025; Zeng et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025; You et al., 2025; Kong et al., 2025a; Liu et al., 2025)。 相比之下，第二个方向，即潜在推理，通过迭代地细化连续表示空间中模型的潜在状态来执行多步推理，而不依赖于显式的自然语言原理. In contrast, the second direction, latent reasoning, performs multi-step inference by iteratively refining the model’s latent states in the continuous representation space, without relying on explicit natural language rationales(Zhang et al., 2025d; Tang et al., 2025; Lin et al., 2025; Dai et al., 2025)。 尽管最近取得了进展，但这两个方向仍然面临着巨大的挑战。显式推理依赖于自然无法获得的高质量的面向推荐的推理数据，而潜在推理在决策过程中缺乏可解释性和可控性。. Despite recent progress, both directions still face substantial challenges. Explicit reasoning relies on high-quality recommendation-oriented reasoning data that is not naturally available, whereas latent reasoning lacks interpretability and controllability in the decision process.

5. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们研究了如何在基于 SID 的生成推荐中实现有效推理。 我们提出了 SIDReasoner，这是一个两阶段框架，可以有效地允许大型语言模型对项目标记进行推理。 更强的 SID-语言一致性可以帮助一般推理能力更好地推广到推荐任务。 受这种洞察力的启发，我们的方法利用多任务对齐和教师辅助的语义扩展在 SID 和语言标记之间建立强大的语义基础。 在此基础上，结果驱动强化进一步引导模型自我探索推荐中的有效推理模式。 对现实世界数据集的大量实验表明，将推理纳入生成推荐可以产生具有竞争力的性能，并提高跨域泛化性和可解释性。In this work, we investigate how to enable effective reasoning in SID-based generative recommendation. We propose SIDReasoner, a two-stage framework that effectively allows large language models to reason over itemic tokens. Stronger SID–language alignment can help general reasoning abilities better generalize to recommendation tasks. Motivated by this insight, our approach leverages multi-task alignment and teacher-assisted semantic expansion to establish strong semantic grounding between SID and language tokens. Building on this foundation, outcome-driven reinforcement further guides the model to self-explore effective reasoning patterns in recommendation. Extensive experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that incorporating reasoning into generative recommendation yields competitive performance, together with improved cross-domain generalization and interpretability.

对于未来的工作，我们计划探索 SID 推理是否可以通过更大的骨干模型产生进一步的收益，并研究在更大规模的训练数据下丰富的 SID 语言对齐的有效性。由于时间和计算资源有限，我们将规模扩大到更大的模型和数据集以供将来探索。For future work, we plan to explore whether reasoning over SIDs yields further gains with larger backbone models, and to investigate the effectiveness of enriched SID–language alignment under substantially larger-scale training data.
