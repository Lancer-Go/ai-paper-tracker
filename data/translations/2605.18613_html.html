摘要Abstract

潜在表征是大多数现代生成模型的核心。在音频领域，它们通常由神经音频编解码器自动编码器生成。在这项工作中，我们引入了 SAME（语义对齐音乐自动编码器），这是一种用于立体声音乐和一般音频的自动编码器，达到 4096Latent representations are at the heart of the majority of modern generative models. In the audio domain they are typically produced by a neural-audio-codec autoencoder. In this work we introduce SAME (Semantically-Aligned Music autoEncoder), an autoencoder for stereo music and general audio that reaches a 4096×\times时间压缩比，同时保持重建质量和下游生成性能。我们通过将基于变压器的主干与一组语义正则化方法、相位感知重建损失和改进的鉴别器设计相结合来实现这一目标。该架构通过其高压缩比和对优化良好的变压器原语的依赖，提供了巨大的计算成本优势。两种变体（大型 SAME-L 和 CPU 可部署 SAME-S）以开放重量形式发布。temporal compression ratio while maintaining reconstruction quality and downstream generative performance. We achieve this by combining a tranformer-based backbone with set of semantic regularisation approaches, phase-aware reconstruction losses and improved discriminator designs. The architecture delivers substantial computational cost benefits, through both its high compression ratio and its reliance on well-optimised transformer primitives. Two variants (a large SAME-L and a CPU-deployable SAME-S) are released in open-weights form.

2.3 软标准化瓶颈Soft-Normalisation Bottleneck

在编码器和解码器之间，我们使用轻微约束的瓶颈而不是 VAE 公式。编码器输出经过可学习的每通道仿射变换（比例和偏差），然后除以指数移动平均值跟踪的运行标准差，在训练期间适应数据统计并将潜在幅度标准化到一致的范围。Between encoder and decoder we use a lightly constrained bottleneck rather than the VAE formulation. The encoder output passes through a learnable per-channel affine transform (scale and bias), then is divided by a running standard deviation tracked by exponential moving average, adapting to data statistics during training and normalising latent magnitudes to a consistent range.

6 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 SAME，一种立体声音乐和通用音频自动编码器，达到 4096We introduced SAME, a stereo music and general-audio autoencoder that reaches a 4096×\times时间压缩比，同时保持音质（通过客观和主观评估来判断）、生成易处理性和快速推理速度。该架构将变压器重采样主干和瓶颈与三个辅助损失（流匹配对齐、语义回归和跨模态对比对齐）配对，共同塑造下游使用的潜在空间。我们证明，这些辅助损失是一种机制，通过这种机制，更简单的瓶颈可以在高压缩下胜过 VAE。 SAME-L 和 SAME-S 的模型重量发布于temporal compression ratio while maintaining sound quality (as judged by objective and subjective evaluation), generative tractability and fast inference speed. The architecture pairs a transformer resampling backbone and a bottleneck with three auxiliary losses (flow-matching alignment, semantic regression, and cross-modal contrastive alignment) that jointly shape the latent space for downstream use. We demonstrate that these auxiliary losses are a mechanism by which a simpler bottleneck can outperform a VAE at high compression. Model weights for SAME-L and SAME-S are released athttps://stability-ai.github.io/SAME.
