LISA：无信号自主交叉口管理的认知仲裁LISA: Cognitive Arbitration for Signal-Free Autonomous Intersection Management

Abderrahmane Lakas13, Mohamed Amine Ferrag1, Merouane Debbah2
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM）在智能交通系统（ITS）方面显示出强大的潜力，特别是对于需要情景推理和多智能体协调的任务。这使得它们非常适合协同驾驶，在这种情况下，基于规则的方法常常难以应对复杂和动态的交通条件。 交叉口管理是一个特别具有挑战性的环境，因为它必须实时解决相互冲突的通行权需求、异构车辆优先级以及特定于车辆的运动学约束。然而，现有方法通常使用 LLM 作为传统信号控制系统之上的辅助推理模块，而不是作为直接交叉点级决策的自主代理。基于信号的控制器仍然与车辆无关，基于预订的方法缺乏意图意识，并且最近基于 LLM 的系统仍然依赖于信号基础设施。此外，LLM 推理延迟进一步限制了它们的使用，因为亚秒级控制周期无法等待多秒推理调用。在本文中，我们提出了 LISA（基于 LLM 的意图驱动速度咨询），一种用于自主交叉口管理的无信号认知仲裁方法。 LISA 使用 LLM 通过推理优先级、队列压力和能源偏好来仲裁声明的车辆意图。我们根据传统的交叉口管理方法（包括固定周期控制、SCATS、AIM 和 GLOSA）在三个交通负载级别上评估 LISA。我们表明，与固定周期控制相比，我们的方法将平均控制延迟减少了高达 89.1%，维持了服务水平 C，而所有非 LLM 基线均降级至服务水平 F，并且在接近饱和的需求下，将平均等待时间减少了 93%，峰值队列长度减少了 60.6%。相对于固定循环，LISA 进一步降低油耗高达 48.8% 控制并实现了 86.2% 的总体意向满意度 最好的非 LLM 成绩为 61.2%。这些结果表明，LLM 推理与轻量级延迟缓解相结合，可以在没有信号基础设施的情况下支持实时协作交叉口仲裁。Large language models (LLM) show strong potential for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), especially for tasks requiring situational reasoning and multi-agent coordination. This makes them well suited to cooperative driving, where rule-based methods often struggle with complex and dynamic traffic conditions. Intersection management is a particularly challenging setting, as it must resolve conflicting right-of-way demands, heterogeneous vehicle priorities, and vehicle-specific kinematic constraints in real time. However, existing approaches typically use LLMs as auxiliary reasoning modules on top of conventional signal-control systems, rather than as autonomous agents for direct intersection-level decision-making. Signal-based controllers remain vehicle-agnostic, reservation-based methods lack intent awareness, and recent LLM-based systems still depend on signal infrastructure. Moreover, LLM inference latency further limits their use, since sub-second control cycles cannot wait for multi-second reasoning calls. In this paper, we propose LISA (LLM-Based Intent-Driven Speed Advisory), a signal-free cognitive arbitration method for autonomous intersection management. LISA uses an LLM to arbitrate declared vehicle intents by reasoning over priority classes, queue pressure, and energy preferences. We evaluate LISA against conventional intersection management methods, including fixed-cycle control, SCATS, AIM, and GLOSA, across three traffic load levels. We show that our approach reduces mean control delay by up to 89.1%, maintains Level of Service C while all non-LLM baselines degrade to Level of Service F, and, under near-saturated demand, reduces mean waiting time by 93% and peak queue length by 60.6% relative to fixed-cycle control. LISA further lowers fuel consumption by up to 48.8% relative to fixed-cycle control and achieves an overall intent satisfaction of 86.2%, compared with the best non-LLM result of 61.2%. These results demonstrate that LLM reasoning, combined with lightweight latency mitigation, can support real-time cooperative intersection arbitration without signal infrastructure.

I 简介Introduction

城市交通系统历来都是围绕人工操作的车辆设计的，这些车辆需要外部物理提示（交通灯、标志和信号相位控制器）来协商共享道路 安全空间Urban transportation systems have historically been designed around human-operated vehicles that require external physical cues (traffic lights, signs, and signal-phase controllers) to negotiate shared road space safely[38]。这个 几十年来，以基础设施为中心的范式已经塑造了智能交通系统（ITS），但它也带来了结构性限制：交通流是通过聚合的、反应性的命令来调节的，而不是通过 而不是通过直接推理个别车辆的意图和能力. This infrastructure-centric paradigm has shaped Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) for decades, but it also imposes a structural limitation: traffic flow is regulated through aggregate, reactive commands rather than through direct reasoning over the intentions and capabilities of individual vehicles[40]。配备车载传感、协作感知和车对万物 (V2X) 通信的联网自动驾驶汽车 (CAV) 的出现挑战了这一假设. The emergence of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs), equipped with onboard sensing, cooperative perception, and Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication, challenges this assumption[2]。如果车辆能够感知周围环境、交换信息并遵循协调一致的建议，那么十字路口管理就不再需要严格依赖于最初为人类驾驶员设计的物理信号相位。. If vehicles are able to perceive their surroundings, exchange information, and follow coordinated advisories, then intersection management no longer needs to be strictly tied to physical signal phases originally designed for human drivers.

这种转变在城市十字路口尤其重要，来自竞争途径的车辆必须共享同一冲突区域，同时避免碰撞、僵局和不必要的延误。传统上，这一责任被分配给交通灯基础设施This shift is especially important at urban intersections, where vehicles from competing approaches must share the same conflict zone while avoiding collisions, deadlock, and unnecessary delay. Traditionally, this responsibility has been assigned to traffic light infrastructure[6, 25]。然而，在 CAV 密集的环境中，车辆可以提前传达其预期运动、优先级和操作偏好。我们需要的是一种能够解释这些意图并将其实时转化为安全、无冲突的过境决策的协调机制。从这个角度来看，交叉口管理可以重新表述为. In a CAV-populated environment, however, vehicles can communicate their intended movements, priority classes, and operating preferences in advance. What is needed is a coordination mechanism capable of interpreting these intents and converting them into safe, conflict-free crossing decisions in real time. From this perspective, intersection management can be reformulated as a认知仲裁cognitive arbitration问题中，推理代理共同解释所有主动接近的交叉意图，并在车辆进入冲突区域之前分配合作通行权角色。problem, in which a reasoning agent jointly interprets the crossing intents of all active approaches and assigns cooperative right-of-way roles before vehicles enter the conflict zone.

Signal-based control, including fixed-cycle systems[4]和自适应控制器，例如最大压力变体and adaptive controllers such as Max Pressure variants[29]，基本上仍然与车辆无关： 阶段是从总流量或占用测量得出的，而不是 而非车辆级意图、优先级或路线信息, remains fundamentally vehicle-agnostic: phases are derived from aggregate flow or occupancy measurements rather than from vehicle-level intent, priority, or route information[40, 11, 28]。 绿灯最佳速度咨询 (GLOSA) 提高运动效率 通过将信号相位和定时 (SPaT) 信息转换为 进场速度建议. Green Light Optimal Speed Advisory (GLOSA) improves kinematic efficiency by translating Signal Phase and Timing (SPaT) information into approach-speed recommendations[32, 10]，但它优化了个人 车辆在现有阶段计划内运动，并且不提供 多车辆通行权决议机制, yet it optimizes individual vehicle motion within an existing phase schedule and provides no mechanism for multi-vehicle right-of-way resolution[5]。 自主交叉口管理 (AIM) 完全消除了信号 通过分配预留槽或冲突区域图块. Autonomous Intersection Management (AIM) eliminates the signal entirely by assigning reservation slots or conflict-zone tiles[8], 但协调仍然是车辆运动学上的确定性调度 状态，没有能力推理穿越意图、优先级， 队列压力或上下文权衡, but coordination remains deterministic scheduling over vehicle kinematic state, with no capacity to reason over crossing intent, priority class, queue pressure, or contextual trade-offs[25, 19, 14, 36]。什么是 所有三个范例中都缺少一个可以读取 情况，不仅了解车辆在哪里，而且 他们的意图是什么，并可以据此进行仲裁。. What is absent across all three paradigms is a controller that can read the situation, one that understands not just where vehicles are, but what they intend, and can arbitrate accordingly.

大型语言模型 (LLM) 提供了质的不同基础 对于这个问题Large Language Models (LLMs) offer a qualitatively different foundation for this problem[13]。他们的开放词汇推理能力， 上下文推理和多方商议自然映射到 交叉谈判的结构，其中相互竞争的主张 必须在不同的优先事项下解决共同的冲突区 和实时约束. Their capacity for open-vocabulary reasoning, contextual inference, and multi-party deliberation maps naturally onto the structure of intersection negotiation, where competing claims over a shared conflict zone must be resolved under heterogeneous priorities and real-time constraints[34, 18]。最近 工作已经开始探索这种一致性：基于法学硕士的代理人已经 显示通过思想链推理来选择信号相位 队列和延迟状态. Recent work has begun to explore this alignment: LLM-based agents have been shown to select signal phases through chain-of-thought reasoning over queue and delay state[24, 20], 审核并纠正通信质量下降情况下的 RL 决策，或者 紧急情况, to audit and correct RL decisions under degraded communication or emergency conditions[27]，并协调 通过向本地提供预测上下文来实现多个交叉路口 代理人, and to coordinate across multiple intersections by supplying predictive context to local agents[41]。其中最具语义表达能力的 表述处理空间、时间、情境和自然 语言意图同时产生冲突意识 决定. The most semantically expressive of these formulations processes spatial, temporal, contextual, and natural language intent simultaneously to generate conflict-aware decisions[23]。然而，在每种情况下，法学硕士都在运作 在信号相位驱动层内或旁边：它的原因是 流量，但它不会取代流量所依赖的基础设施 围绕设计。仍然悬而未决的问题是LLM是否 单独推理，没有信号阶段，没有保留 时间表，并且无需针对特定任务的培训，就足以 仲裁穿越意图并提供合作运动学建议 接近的车辆。. Yet in each case the LLM operates within or alongside a signal-phase actuation layer: it reasons about traffic, but it does not replace the infrastructure that traffic was designed around. The question that remains open is whether LLM reasoning alone, without signal phases, without reservation schedules, and without task-specific training, is sufficient to arbitrate crossing intent and deliver cooperative kinematic advisories to approaching vehicles.

为了解决这一差距，我们引入了 LISA（基于 LLM 的意图驱动速度咨询），这是一种用于无信号自主交叉口管理的认知仲裁框架。与现有方法不同，LISA 通过基于意图的仲裁来解决通行权问题，而不是聚合流量测量或 确定性的时间表。它通过以下方式克服了 LLM 延迟限制 两种互补机制：用于重复性的记忆仲裁表 (MAT) 之前触发的冲突签名和预期仲裁 车辆抵达冲突地区。大语言模型函数 不是作为信号优化器，而是作为明确的仲裁代理，对车辆意图和冲突区域周围的交通状况进行推理。然后，每个仲裁结果都会传递给确定性运动执行器，该执行器将决策转换为特定于车辆的速度建议。该设计保留了法学硕士推理的可解释性，同时提供车辆级控制所需的时间精度。To address this gap, we introduce LISA (LLM-Based Intent-Driven Speed Advisory), an cognitive arbitration framework for signal-free autonomous intersection management. Unlike existing methods, LISA resolves right-of-way through intent-based arbitration rather than aggregate flow measurements or deterministic schedules. It overcomes LLM latency constraints through two complementary mechanisms: a Memoized Arbitration Table (MAT) for recurring conflict signatures and anticipatory arbitration triggered before vehicles reach the conflict zone. The large language model functions not as a signal optimizer but as an explicit arbitration agent, reasoning over both vehicle intent and the traffic situation around the conflict zone. Each arbitration outcome is then passed to a deterministic kinematic executor, which converts the decision into vehicle-specific speed advisories. This design preserves the interpretability of the LLM’s reasoning while providing the temporal precision required for vehicle-level control.

II 相关工作Related Work

交叉口管理已经历了四大范式的演变： 固定时间和驱动信号控制、自适应信号控制、 自主交叉口管理（AIM），以及最近， 基于LLM的流量推理。 GLOSA 和 V2I 速度咨询系统 占据中间位置，提高整车级效率 信号基础设施范式之内而不消除它。 本节回顾每个类别的代表性作品，并 将 LISA 置于由此产生的景观中。Intersection management has evolved through four broad paradigms: fixed-time and actuated signal control, adaptive signal control, autonomous intersection management (AIM), and, most recently, LLM-based traffic reasoning. GLOSA and V2I speed advisory systems occupy an intermediate position, improving vehicle-level efficiency within the signal-infrastructure paradigm without eliminating it. This section reviews representative work in each category and situates LISA within the resulting landscape.

Ⅱ-AII-A 基于信号的自适应控制Signal-Based and Adaptive Control

交通信号控制仍然是城市十字路口的主导机制。固定时间、驱动和自适应控制器通过调整阶段持续时间、周期长度或相序来响应交通观测，从而改善队列长度、行程时间和吞吐量Traffic signal control remains the dominant mechanism for urban intersections. Fixed-time, actuated, and adaptive controllers improve queue length, travel time, and throughput by adjusting phase duration, cycle length, or phase sequence in response to traffic observations[11, 28, 1, 16]。然而，这些方法仍然是基于阶段的，并且主要基于总流量或占用测量而不是车辆级意图、优先级或路线信息。. However, these methods remain phase-based and primarily operate on aggregate flow or occupancy measurements rather than vehicle-level intent, priority, or route information.

Ⅱ-BII-B GLOSA 和 V2I 速度咨询系统GLOSA and V2I Speed Advisory Systems

绿灯最佳速度咨询 (GLOSA) 系统Green Light Optimal Speed Advisory (GLOSA) systems[10]标志着从纯粹以基础设施为中心的信号控制向车辆感知引导迈出了重要的一步。 GLOSA 并没有改变信号计划本身，而是使用信号相位和定时 (SPaT) 信息来推荐接近速度，使车辆能够更顺利地通过十字路口。这些建议可以减少不必要的停车、适度加速和减速，并提高燃料或能源效率mark an important step from purely infrastructure-centered signal control toward vehicle-aware guidance. Rather than changing the signal plan itself, GLOSA uses Signal Phase and Timing (SPaT) information to recommend approach speeds that allow vehicles to pass intersections more smoothly. These advisories can reduce unnecessary stops, moderate acceleration and deceleration, and improve fuel or energy efficiency[32, 10]。实证研究表明，只要信号配时信息可靠并且驾驶员或自动驾驶车辆适当遵循建议的速度，GLOSA 就可以改善交通性能和环境结果. VII-A 为什么 LISA 优于基线方法Why LISA Outperforms Baseline Methods

LISA 的优势在于将语义仲裁与 低级运动执行。法学硕士收到的结构化描述 进场队列、车辆优先级、运动冲突和能量 偏好，并返回象征性的通行权角色。这些角色是 然后通过运动学转换为确定性速度建议 执行人。这种分离使 LISA 能够共同推理冲突 回避、优先服务、排队压力、轨迹平滑度 不需要为每个目标单独制定规则。LISA’s advantage comes from separating semantic arbitration from low-level motion execution. The LLM receives a structured description of approach queues, vehicle priorities, movement conflicts, and energy preferences, and returns symbolic right-of-way roles. These roles are then converted into deterministic speed advisories by the kinematic executor. This separation allows LISA to reason jointly about conflict avoidance, priority service, queue pressure, and trajectory smoothness without requiring a separate hand-crafted rule for each objective.

主要的实际挑战是 LLM 推理延迟。 LISA 缓解 这是通过记忆的仲裁表进行的，该表仍然有效 LLM 查询之间的通行权分配。这解耦了较慢的 认知推理间隔来自更快的车辆咨询间隔。 实验中观察到的高缓存命中率表明，许多 流量状态保持足够稳定，可以重用缓存的分配， 允许摊销推理成本，而不需要连续的法学硕士 来电。The main practical challenge is LLM inference latency. LISA mitigates this through the memoized arbitration table, which persists valid right-of-way assignments between LLM queries. This decouples the slower cognitive reasoning interval from the faster vehicle-advisory interval. The high cache hit rates observed in the experiments indicate that many traffic states remain stable enough for cached assignments to be reused, allowing inference cost to be amortized without requiring continuous LLM calls.

八VIII结论Conclusion

本文提出了 LISA，一种无信号交叉口管理系统 使用法学硕士进行基于意图的通行权仲裁的框架。 LISA 将高级语义推理与低级运动分开 控制：法学硕士分配符号角色，而确定性运动学 执行器将这些角色转换为每辆车的速度建议。一个 记忆仲裁表减少了冗余的推理调用，并且 独立的基于瓦片的看门狗在外部提供安全支持 LLM推理路径。This paper presented LISA, a signal-free intersection management framework that uses an LLM for intent-based right-of-way arbitration. LISA separates high-level semantic reasoning from low-level motion control: the LLM assigns symbolic roles, while a deterministic kinematic executor converts those roles into per-vehicle speed advisories. A memoized arbitration table reduces redundant inference calls, and an independent tile-based watchdog provides a safety backstop outside the LLM inference path.

在评估的场景中，LISA 提高了大部分流量效率， 相对于固定时间的排队、意图满意度和能量指标， 自适应、基于预订和基于咨询的基线。其最强 过渡制度会带来好处，其中仲裁质量 决定车辆是否顺利通过路口或进入 持续排队。未来的工作将把 LISA 扩展到多路口 走廊，评估部分 V2I 渗透率，并研究稳健性 迅速变化和更广泛的型号系列。Across the evaluated scenarios, LISA improves most traffic-efficiency, queueing, intent-satisfaction, and energy metrics relative to fixed-time, adaptive, reservation-based, and advisory-based baselines. Its strongest gains occur in the transition regime, where arbitration quality determines whether vehicles clear the intersection smoothly or enter persistent queueing. Future work will extend LISA to multi-intersection corridors, evaluate partial V2I penetration, and study robustness to prompt variation and broader model families.
