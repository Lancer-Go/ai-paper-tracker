1 简介Introduction

代理人工智能的出现使大型语言模型（LLM）能够访问不同的用户数据，以实现灵活且可扩展的任务执行The emergence of agentic AI enables Large language models (LLMs) to access diverse user data for flexible and scalable task execution(20; 17; 13)。 然而，这种增强的功能引起了严重的隐私问题，因为敏感的用户数据可能在交互过程中被访问和暴露。 为了解决这些问题，之前的工作引入了上下文隐私的概念，强调在给定上下文下规范用户数据的披露. However, this increased capability raises critical privacy concerns, as sensitive user data may be accessed and exposed during interactions. To address these concerns, prior work has introduced the notion of contextual privacy, which emphasizes regulating the disclosure of user data under a given context(12; 9)。 基于这个观点，最近的研究通过评估模型在处理敏感信息时如何适应不同的上下文来研究法学硕士的上下文隐私. Building on this perspective, recent studies have examined contextual privacy in LLMs by evaluating how models adapt to different contexts when handling sensitive information(11; 4)。 在这些设置中，隐私策略通常被定义为每个上下文中的固定规则，并且评估模型是否遵守这些预定义的规范。. In these settings, privacy policies are typically defined as fixed rules within each context, and models are evaluated for their adherence to these predefined norms.

然而，上下文隐私政策并不普遍适用，因为可接受的披露程度可能因用户而异。 例如，在预订酒店时，代理可能会分享用户的身体残疾信息，以帮助提供更舒适的住宿体验。 虽然此类披露可能看起来与上下文相关，但一些用户可能仍然不愿意共享此敏感信息。 这种差异强调了对个性化隐私的需求，其中信息披露进一步受到超出上下文相关性的用户特定偏好的限制。 图However, contextual privacy policies are not universally applicable, as the acceptable level of disclosure may vary across users. For instance, when making a hotel reservation, an agent may share a user’s information about physical disability to help provide a more comfortable stay. While such disclosure may appear contextually relevant, some users may still prefer not to share this sensitive information. This discrepancy underscores the need for personalized privacy, in which information disclosure is further constrained by user-specific preferences beyond contextual relevance.
