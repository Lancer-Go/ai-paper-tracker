摘要。Abstract.

虽然多模态检索增强生成（MM-RAG）已经显示出有希望的结果，但它仍然难以处理复杂的多跳推理任务。 现有方法主要关注独立的实例级匹配，这通常无法捕获跨模式和文档的显式关系。 尽管图增强方法引入了结构建模，但它们在多模态场景中面临着根本性挑战：合并细粒度的视觉特征会导致快速的图扩展和检索噪声，而粗粒度的表示会导致关键局部证据的丢弃。 为了解决这个困境，我们建议While Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (MM-RAG) has shown promising results, it still struggles with complex multi-hop reasoning tasks. Existing methods primarily focus on independent instance-level matching, which often fails to capture explicit relationships across modalities and documents. Although Graph-enhanced methods introduce structural modeling, they face a fundamental challenge in multimodal scenarios: incorporating fine-grained visual features leads to rapid graph expansion and retrieval noise, whereas coarse-grained representations cause the discarding of critical local evidence. To address this dilemma, we propose双G-MRAGDualG-MRAG, 一个, a双Dual-层框架引入了由宏观推理和微观匹配组成的解耦架构-tier framework that introduces a decoupled architecture comprising Macro-reasoning and Micro-matchingG拉夫斯raphs forM多式联运ultimodalRAG。 具体来说，为了通过将全局结构推理与细粒度证据匹配隔离来抑制检索噪声， 我们构建了用于全局拓扑路由的宏观图和用于精确局部验证的微观图。 随后，为了实现跨异构证据源的动态相关性传播，我们通过 GNN 检索器将检索制定为查询驱动的消息传递过程。 此外，为了为生成模型提供连贯的结构指导，我们引入了一种动态编程解码机制，该机制直接从 GNN 的前向传递中提取显式推理路径，替换孤立文档块的标准输入。大量实验表明，DualG-MRAG 在证据召回率和复杂 QA 准确性方面均优于基线。. Specifically, to suppress retrieval noise by isolating global structural reasoning from fine-grained evidence matching, we construct a Macro Graph for global topological routing and a Micro Graph for precise local verification. Subsequently, to enable dynamic relevance propagation across heterogeneous evidence sources, we formulate retrieval as a query-driven message passing process via a GNN Retriever. Furthermore, to provide the generative model with coherent structural guidance, we introduce a dynamic programming decoding mechanism that extracts explicit reasoning paths directly from the GNN’s forward pass, replacing the standard input of isolated document chunks. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DualG-MRAG outperforms baselines in both evidence recall and complex QA accuracy.

1. 简介Introduction

多模态大语言模型（MLLM）在各种任务中取得了显着的成功；然而，他们仍然难以进行长上下文推理，并且在处理知识密集型查询时容易产生幻觉。为了增强其能力，MM-RAG将传统的RAG框架扩展到异构数据源，旨在以统一的方式检索和利用多模态知识Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have achieved remarkable success across various tasks; however, they still struggle with long-context reasoning and are prone to hallucinations when handling knowledge-intensive queries. To enhance their capabilities, MM-RAG extends the traditional RAG framework to heterogeneous data sources, aiming to retrieve and utilize multimodal knowledge in a unified manner(Mei et al., 2025; Abootorabi et al., 2025)。尽管取得了这些进展，但现有方法. Despite this progress, existing methods(Radford et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2022; Yu et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024)主要依赖于粗粒度对齐范例，范围从双编码器到基于 LLM 的嵌入框架。虽然对一般检索有效，但这些方法通常将复杂的多模态证据压缩到静态嵌入中，从而难以显式捕获模态和文档之间的细粒度依赖关系。因此，这些方法在处理需要多跳推理的复杂问答（QA）任务时表现出明显的局限性。mainly rely on coarse-grained alignment paradigms, ranging from dual-encoders to LLM-based embedding frameworks. Consequently, these methods exhibit clear limitations when handling complex Question Answering (QA) tasks that require multi-hop reasoning.

为了解决这一限制，最近的研究探索了图增强方法，通过结构建模提高跨源信息融合和推理能力To address this limitation, recent studies have explored graph-enhanced methods, which improve cross-source information fusion and reasoning capabilities through structural modeling(Wan and Yu, 2025; Park et al., 2025)。然而，将此类方法扩展到多模式环境仍然具有挑战性。首先，在多模态知识图谱中，天真地结合细粒度的特征很容易导致图的快速扩展和相当大的检索噪声，使得宏观推理和微观匹配之间难以取得平衡. Nevertheless, extending such methods to multimodal settings remains challenging. First, in Multimodal Knowledge Graphs, naively incorporating fine-grained features easily leads to a rapid graph expansion and considerable retrieval noise, making it difficult to strike a balance between macro-level reasoning and micro-level matching(Liu et al., 2025a)。其次，虽然现有的基于图的检索器在复杂关系建模方面很有效，但它们的图结构和信息传播规则通常仅针对文本数据量身定制. Second, while existing graph-based retrievers are effective at complex relation modeling, their graph structures and information propagation rule are often tailored for textual data only(He et al., 2024; Luo et al., 2025a; Gutiérrez et al., 2025)或依赖静态拓扑or rely on static topologies(Wan and Yu, 2025; Ling et al., 2025).

为了构建一个处理多模态异构数据、同时具有深度推理能力和最小化检索噪声的图增强检索系统，必须克服三个相互关联的核心挑战。To build a graph-enhanced retrieval system that handles multimodal heterogeneous data with both deep reasoning capability and minimized retrieval noise, it is essential to overcome three interconnected core challenges.

2. 相关工作Related Work

2.1. 多模态检索-增强生成Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation

主流多模态 RAG 框架通过将跨模态检索集成到生成管道中而发展起来。跨模态对比学习和联合表示空间等技术已被广泛采用来弥合跨模态的语义差距Mainstream Multimodal RAG frameworks have evolved by integrating cross-modal retrieval into the generative pipeline. Techniques such as cross-modal contrastive learning and joint representation spaces have been widely adopted to bridge the semantic gap across modalities(Radford et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2024; Faysse et al., 2024)。这些方法在将多模态信息映射到共享连续空间以促进密集检索方面表现出了强大的有效性。然而，尽管实现了高质量的表示对齐，这些向量匹配范例仍然难以捕获显式的关系依赖性。对潜在空间中基于邻近度的匹配的依赖意味着，虽然检索到语义相关的项目，但它们之间的结构连接（例如，视觉实体如何精确地基于表格中的事实主张）仍然是隐式的。这种结构限制限制了需要多跳推理和跨源证据集成的任务的效率。. These methods have shown strong effectiveness in mapping multimodal information into a shared continuous space to facilitate dense retrieval. However, despite achieving high-quality representation alignment, these vector-matching paradigms still struggle to capture explicit relational dependencies. The reliance on proximity-based matching in latent spaces means that while semantically related items are retrieved, the structural connections between them (e.g., how a visual entity precisely grounds a factual claim in a table) remain implicit. This structural limit limits the efficacy of tasks requiring multi-hop reasoning and cross-source evidence integration.

2.2. 知识图谱增强的多模态检索Knowledge Graph-Enhanced Multimodal Retrieval

为了提供潜在嵌入所缺乏的结构基础，研究人员引入了知识图（KG）来表示显式实体和关系To provide the structural grounding that latent embeddings lack, researchers have introduced Knowledge Graphs (KGs) to represent explicit entities and relations(Edge et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024; Peng et al., 2025)。当扩展到多模式设置时，最近的框架如 RAG-Anything. When extended to multimodal settings, recent frameworks like RAG-Anything(Guo et al., 2025)和 MMGraphRAGand MMGraphRAG(Wan and Yu, 2025)尝试通过构建多模态图来架起视觉和语言的桥梁。 然而，这些系统在平衡表示粒度和检索效率方面遇到了挑战。首先，映射细粒度图像块可能会导致图规模和计算复杂性大幅增加，而依赖粗粒度实体会导致严重的多模态信息丢失attempt to bridge vision and language by constructing multimodal graphs. However, these systems encounter challenges in balancing representation granularity with retrieval efficiency. First, mapping fine-grained image patches can lead to a substantial increase in graph scale and computational complexity, whereas relying on coarse-grained entities results in critical multimodal information loss(Yang et al., 2025)。其次，它们的检索机制主要依赖于预定义的图拓扑，其中信息传播规则不适应不同用户查询的特定推理要求。这表明架构具有将宏观推理与微观证据定位分离的潜力。. Second, their retrieval mechanisms predominantly rely on predefined graph topologies where the information propagation rules do not adapt to the specific reasoning requirements of different user queries. This suggests a potential for architectures that can decouple macro-reasoning from micro-evidence localization.

2.3. 用于检索的图神经网络Graph Neural Networks for Retrieval

事实证明，GNN 可以有效地从复杂的拓扑依赖性中提取显着的证据。最近的研究已经从仅仅使用 GNN 进行节点分类转变为将它们用作密集神经检索器。例如，GNN-RAGGNNs have proven effective in extracting salient evidence from complex topological dependencies. Recent studies have transitioned from using GNNs merely for node classification to employing them as dense neural retrievers. For instance, GNN-RAG(Mavromatis and Karypis, 2024)使用 GNN 对候选节点进行推理，而 GFM-RAGemploys GNNs to reason over candidate nodes, while GFM-RAG(Luo et al., 2025a)探索图基础模型以捕获全局关系模式。尽管如此，大多数现有的基于 GNN 的 RAGexplores graph foundation models to capture global relationship patterns. Nevertheless, most existing GNN-based RAG(Mavromatis and Karypis, 2024; Luo et al., 2025a; Gutiérrez et al., 2025; Luo et al., 2025b; Yuan et al., 2026)方法主要关注文本数据，对图像和表格的异构结构的支持有限。 此外，尽管基于 GNN 的 RAG 取得了进步，但这些模型的输出 通常被视为孤立的节点分数，从而隐含了复杂的多跳推理路径。他们缺乏系统的机制来提取异质证据并将其整合到明确的、可读的推理路径中。 因此，建立一种系统机制将异构证据整合到显式推理路径中仍然是一个挑战。methods primarily focus on textual data, with limited support for the heterogeneous structures of images and tables. Furthermore, despite advancements in GNN-based RAG, the outputs of these models are typically treated as isolated node scores, leaving the intricate multi-hop reasoning paths implicit. They lack a systematic mechanism to extract and integrate heterogeneous evidence into explicit, readable reasoning paths. Consequently, establishing a systematic mechanism to integrate heterogeneous evidence into explicit reasoning paths remains a challenge.
