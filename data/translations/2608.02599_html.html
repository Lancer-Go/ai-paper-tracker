I 简介Introduction

Ⅰ-AI-A 动机Motivation

人工智能（AI）作为最具代表性的先进技术之一，目前正在各领域得到广泛讨论和采用。在电力能源系统领域，人工智能在建模、预测、优化和控制方面展现出强大潜力Artificial intelligence (AI), as one of the most representative advanced technologies, is now being widely discussed and adopted across many sectors. In the field of power and energy systems, AI has shown strong potential in modeling, forecasting, optimization, and control[3, 5]。这些功能不仅加速计算，还有助于通过可再生能源集成和新兴技术解决现代电网日益复杂的问题. These capabilities not only accelerate computation but also help address the increasing complexity of modern grids with renewable integration and emerging technologies[7]。在日常生活中，人工智能已经给我们带来了极大的便利，从智能推荐到语音助手，可见当提供合适的工具时，人工智能是多么强大和平易近人。. In daily life, AI has already brought us great convenience, from smart recommendations to voice assistants, which shows how powerful and approachable it can be when proper tools are provided.

然而，现有的大部分学术工作仍然强调高水平的研究贡献或专业应用。虽然这些结果对专家来说很有价值，但初学者、学生或其他学科的从业者并不总是能够理解它们。对于普通大众来说，开始使用人工智能仍然并不简单：工作流程可能看起来很抽象，学习曲线很陡峭，而且许多开源代码要么过于碎片化，要么缺乏上下文。However, most of the existing academic work still emphasizes high-level research contributions or specialized applications. While these results are valuable to experts, they are not always accessible to beginners, students, or practitioners from other disciplines. For the general public, getting started with AI is still not straightforward: the workflow may seem abstract, the learning curve is steep, and many open-source codes are either too fragmented or lack context.

因此，显然需要简单通用的实践代码来降低进入门槛。通过提供从基本函数近似开始的渐进示例，然后转向噪声条件下的数据集拟合，最后到受现实世界启发的电力系统案例，我们的目标是让人工智能变得更加平易近人。这些例子可以作为学生、工程师和跨学科学习者的踏脚石，使他们能够快速掌握基本思想并使方法适应自己的问题，无论是在电力系统、能源预测还是其他应用领域。Therefore, there is a clear need for simple and generic hands-on codes that can lower the entry barrier. By providing progressive examples which start from basic function approximation, then moving to dataset fitting under noisy conditions, and finally to a real-world inspired power system case, we aim to make AI more approachable. Such examples can serve as stepping stones for students, engineers, and interdisciplinary learners, enabling them to quickly grasp fundamental ideas and adapt the methods to their own problems, whether in power systems, energy forecasting, or other application domains.

Ⅰ-BI-B 相关工作Related Work

最近的文献表明，将人工智能融入多个阶段和学科的教育中的趋势日益明显。人工智能相关课程和学习活动已从中学阶段引入高等教育，展示了早期接触人工智能以及先进的人工智能支持的学习环境的可行性Recent literature indicates a growing trend of integrating AI into education across multiple stages and disciplines. AI-related curricula and learning activities have been introduced from the secondary school level to higher education, demonstrating the feasibility of early AI exposure as well as advanced AI-supported learning environments[4, 18]。在 STEM 和工程教育中，先前的研究已经探索了将人工智能基础知识系统地纳入课程和课程模块中，以及在教学和学习中采用生成式人工智能的更广泛的制度框架. Within STEM and engineering education, prior studies have explored systematic incorporation of AI fundamentals into curricula and course modules, alongside broader institutional frameworks for adopting generative AI in teaching and learning[9, 21]。与此同时，我们还努力提高教育工作者人工智能工具的可访问性和可解释性，以促进课堂部署. In parallel, efforts have been made to improve the accessibility and interpretability of AI tools for educators to facilitate classroom deployment[25]。总体而言，这些作品共同反映了人工智能在教育阶段和学科背景中广泛且日益融合的情况. Overall, these works collectively reflect the widespread and increasing integration of AI across educational stages and disciplinary contexts[8].

人工智能与电力和能源系统之间的相互作用已从多个角度得到广泛研究。现有审查涵盖电力系统中的人工智能操作、控制和规划The interplay between AI and power & energy systems has been extensively surveyed from multiple angles. Existing reviews cover AI-enabled operation, control, and planning in power systems[19, 23]以及贯穿电力电子系统设计、控制和维护生命周期的人工智能技术, as well as AI techniques across the design, control, and maintenance life-cycle of power-electronics systems[29]。社区观点进一步凸显了人工智能工作负载与电力电子基础设施之间的相互耦合. Community perspectives further highlight the mutual coupling between AI workloads and power-electronics infrastructures[2]，而面向领域的审查和愿景工作总结了电力系统社区内的新兴应用和开放挑战, while domain-facing review and vision efforts summarize emerging applications and open challenges within the power-systems community[12]。与此同时，最近的研究提倡开源生态系统以提高可重复性、透明度和长期软件可持续性. In parallel, recent studies advocate open-source ecosystems to improve reproducibility, transparency, and long-term software sustainability[1]，并推广基于Python的模拟器和类似数字孪生的工作流程，以实现快速原型设计以及与面向数据和人工智能的工具链的集成, and promote Python-based simulators and digital-twin-like workflows for rapid prototyping and integration with data- and AI-oriented toolchains[10].

尽管调查和立场文件的数量不断增加，但许多公开的学习材料仍然围绕单个任务或狭义定义的用例进行组织。实践教程通常侧重于特定问题，例如最佳功率流 (OPF)Despite the growing body of surveys and position papers, many publicly available learning materials remain organized around a single task or narrowly defined use case. Hands-on tutorials often focus on specific problems such as optimal power flow (OPF)[17]，而技术范例通常强调特定的人工智能或控制目标，例如，在不同的电网条件下对转换器接口资源进行基于强化学习的自适应控制，而不是涵盖数据处理、模型构建、训练和评估的通用工作流程, while technical exemplars typically emphasize a particular AI or control objective—for example, reinforcement-learning-based adaptive control of converter-interfaced resources under varying grid conditions—rather than a generalizable workflow spanning data handling, model construction, training, and evaluation[6]。经典基准测试系统继续在经济和以 OPF 为中心的演示中发挥重要作用. Classical benchmark test systems continue to play an important role in economic and OPF-centered demonstrations[11]但此类演示并不一定转化为可重复使用的端到端编码框架，可以轻松适应更广泛的人工智能电力系统任务。, but such demonstrations do not necessarily translate into reusable, end-to-end coding frameworks that can be readily adapted to a broader range of AI-enabled power-system tasks.

总体而言，尽管现有文献和社区资源强调可重复的软件实践和可访问的工具Overall, although existing literature and community resources emphasize reproducible software practices and accessible tooling[1, 10]很少有资源提供初学者可以直接重用和扩展的分步的、与任务无关的、实用的 AI 程序。在人工智能方法快速扩展及其在电力系统和电力电子领域的广泛适用性的背景下，这种差距尤为明显，新手通常主要依靠大型语言模型（LLM）（例如 ChatGPT、Claude 或 Gemini）来获取答案, few resources provide step-by-step, task-agnostic, hands-on AI programs that beginners can directly reuse and extend. This gap is particularly evident in the context of the rapid expansion of AI methods and their broad applicability across power systems and power electronics, where newcomers often rely primarily on large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to obtain answers[26]，而不是开发自己的模型和实验工作流程。为了弥补这一差距，这项工作提供了一套用于函数拟合、数据集学习和潮流预测的教育性、实践性人工智能模块，使初学者能够主动修改参数、函数和更高级别的模型架构，并直接观察它们对学习结果的影响。所提出的模块旨在提供最小但可扩展的编码基线，连接基本概念和更先进的人工智能电力系统分析。, rather than developing their own models and experimentation workflows. To address this gap, this work provides a set of educational, hands-on AI modules for function fitting, dataset learning, and power-flow prediction, enabling beginners to actively modify parameters, functions, and higher-level model architectures, and to directly observe their impact on learning outcomes. The proposed modules aim to offer a minimal yet extensible coding baseline that bridges foundational concepts and more advanced AI-enabled power-system analyses.

Making these engineering constraints visible and executable, rather than hiding them behind a conversational interface, is what turns a generic AI recipe into a dependable power-system tool, and it is the principle we encourage learners to carry from these examples into their own work.

目前的模块是故意轻量化的，并且它们的小型演示设置是为了速度而不是真实性而选择的；将它们扩展到更大的系统和更大的数据集是自然的下一步，笔记本中已经标明了这一点。进一步的方向包括添加基于循环和长期短期记忆网络的时间序列模块进行预测，结合可解释性工具以便学习者可以询问模型行为，并将前沿层扩展到数字孪生和闭环网格操作工作流程。按照此处报告的调查驱动设计，对学习成果进行正式的课堂评估，将进一步量化教育影响。The present modules are intentionally lightweight, and their small demonstration settings are chosen for speed rather than realism; scaling them to larger systems and larger datasets is a natural next step already signposted in the notebooks. Further directions include adding time-series modules based on recurrent and long short-term memory networks for forecasting, incorporating explainability tools so that learners can interrogate model behavior, and extending the frontier tier toward digital-twin and closed-loop grid-operation workflows. A formal classroom evaluation of learning outcomes, following the survey-driven design reported here, would further quantify the educational impact.

八VIII结论Conclusion

本文提出了一个用于电力系统人工智能教学的教育框架和一个开放的、可执行的模块库。该框架以对研究人员和从业者的社区调查为基础，沿着渐进的难度阶梯组织了六个实践模块，将核心人工智能概念映射到代表性的电力系统任务。在这三层中，一个可配置的笔记本模板将学习者从基础 DNN 函数和负载曲线拟合，通过域耦合 CNN 功率流代理，带到 DNN 辅助优化、用于存储控制的 DRL 以及基于物理的摆动方程学习的前沿模块。This paper presented an educational framework and an open, executable module library for teaching AI in power systems. Grounded in a community survey of researchers and practitioners, the framework organizes six hands-on modules along a progressive difficulty ladder that maps core AI concepts onto representative power-system tasks. Across the three tiers, a single configurable notebook template carries the learner from foundational DNN function and load-curve fitting, through a domain-coupled CNN power-flow surrogate, to frontier modules on DNN-assisted optimization, DRL for storage control, and physics-informed learning of the swing equation.

开发的示例和代码降低了设置障碍，并将每种方法固定在熟悉的电力系统问题中。 它们作为可复制、Colab 就绪的笔记本通过 IEEE 在线课程和 IEEE PES 网络研讨会发布，早期参与度很高。 拟议的框架将概念理解和实际实施联系起来，并鼓励积极实验而不是被动依赖法学硕士。The developed samples and codes lower setup barriers and anchor each method in a familiar power-system problem. They are released as a reproducible, Colab-ready notebook delivered through an IEEE online course and an IEEE PES webinar with strong early engagement. The proposed framework bridges conceptual understanding and practical implementation and encourages active experimentation over passive reliance on LLMs.
