摘要Abstract

大型语言模型（LLM），特别是那些采用专家混合（MoE）架构的模型，已经在不同的自然语言处理任务中取得了卓越的能力。然而，这些模型经常产生幻觉——生成看似合理但实际上不正确的内容——并表现出系统偏差，而推理过程中专家激活的不均匀会放大这些偏差。在本文中，我们提出Large Language Models (LLMs), particularly those employing Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures, have achieved remarkable capabilities across diverse natural language processing tasks. However, these models frequently suffer from hallucinations—generating plausible but factually incorrect content—and exhibit systematic biases that are amplified by uneven expert activation during inference. In this paper, we propose the理事会模式Council Mode，一种新颖的多代理共识框架，通过并行地将查询分派到多个异构前沿 LLM 并通过专用共识模型综合其输出来解决这些限制。委员会管道分三个阶段运行：(1) 智能分类器，根据复杂性路由查询；(2) 跨不同架构的模型并行生成专家；(3) 结构化共识综合，在生成最终响应之前明确识别同意、分歧和独特发现。我们在开源人工智能工作区中实施和评估该架构。我们对多个基准的综合评估表明，与表现最佳的单个模型相比，Council 模式在 HaluEval 基准上实现了幻觉率相对降低 35.9%，在 TruthfulQA 上提高了 7.8 个百分点，同时保持了显着较低的跨领域偏差方差。我们提供了共识机制的数学公式，详细介绍了系统架构，并通过消融研究提供了广泛的实证结果。, a novel multi-agent consensus framework that addresses these limitations by dispatching queries to multiple heterogeneous frontier LLMs in parallel and synthesizing their outputs through a dedicated consensus model. The Council pipeline operates in three phases: (1) an intelligent triage classifier that routes queries based on complexity, (2) parallel expert generation across architecturally diverse models, and (3) a structured consensus synthesis that explicitly identifies agreement, disagreement, and unique findings before producing the final response. We implement and evaluate this architecture within an open-source AI workspace. Our comprehensive evaluation across multiple benchmarks demonstrates that the Council Mode achieves a 35.9% relative reduction in hallucination rates on the HaluEval benchmark and a 7.8-point improvement on TruthfulQA compared to the best-performing individual model, while maintaining significantly lower bias variance across domains. We provide the mathematical formulation of the consensus mechanism, detail the system architecture, and present extensive empirical results with ablation studies.

1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型（LLM）的快速发展从根本上改变了自然语言处理和人工智能的格局The rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) has fundamentally transformed the landscape of natural language processing and artificial intelligence(Vaswani et al., 2017)。 GPT-5.4、Claude Opus 4.6 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro 等模型在推理、生成和理解任务中展现了前所未有的能力。为了在推理过程中不按比例增加计算成本的情况下扩展模型容量，专家混合 (MoE) 架构已成为主导范例. Models such as GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro have demonstrated unprecedented capabilities in reasoning, generation, and comprehension tasks. To scale model capacity without proportionally increasing computational costs during inference, the Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture has emerged as a dominant paradigm(Shazeer et al., 2017; Fedus et al., 2022)。通过为每个输入令牌仅激活专家模块的子集，MoE 模型实现了显着的效率，同时保持或超过了密集模型的性能。. By activating only a subset of expert modules for each input token, MoE models achieve remarkable efficiency while maintaining or exceeding the performance of dense counterparts.

尽管取得了这些进步，基于教育部的法学硕士特别容易受到两种关键故障模式的影响：Despite these advances, MoE-based LLMs are particularly susceptible to two critical failure modes:幻觉hallucination和and系统性偏差systematic bias。幻觉是指生成流畅且可信但实际上不正确或不受输入上下文支持的文本. Hallucination refers to the generation of text that is fluent and plausible but factually incorrect or unsupported by the input context(Ji et al., 2023)。在 MoE 架构中，稀疏路由机制可能通过多种途径加剧这个问题。首先，门控网络可能会一致地将某些类型的查询路由给在训练期间内化了不正确相关性的专家，这种现象称为. In MoE architectures, the sparse routing mechanism can exacerbate this problem through several pathways. First, the gating network may consistently route certain types of queries to experts that have internalized incorrect correlations during training, a phenomenon known as专家崩溃expert collapse (Zoph et al., 2022)。其次，专家的激活有限意味着模型对给定查询的响应仅由其总参数知识的一小部分决定，从而增加了认知差距的可能性。第三，路由决策本身可能会引入系统偏差，因为门函数可能会产生与表面特征而不是语义内容相关的偏好. Second, the limited activation of experts means that the model’s response to a given query is determined by only a fraction of its total parametric knowledge, increasing the likelihood of epistemic gaps. Third, the routing decisions themselves can introduce systematic biases, as the gating function may develop preferences that correlate with surface-level features rather than semantic content(Riquelme et al., 2021).

减轻幻觉的传统方法包括检索增强生成（RAG）和人类反馈强化学习（RLHF），前者将模型输出建立在外部知识源中，后者使模型行为与人类偏好保持一致。虽然在不同程度上有效，但这些方法从根本上来说是在单个模型的参数记忆和推理能力的范围内运行的。最近的研究表明，多智能体辩论和共识机制可以显着提高推理质量和事实准确性Traditional approaches to mitigating hallucinations include Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), which grounds model outputs in external knowledge sources, and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), which aligns model behavior with human preferences. While effective to varying degrees, these methods fundamentally operate within the confines of a single model’s parametric memory and reasoning capabilities. Recent research has demonstrated that multi-agent debate and consensus mechanisms can significantly enhance reasoning quality and factual accuracy(Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023)。关键的见解是，不同的模型在不同架构的不同数据上进行训练，可以开发互补的知识表示和推理策略。当独立查询这些模型并系统地比较它们的输出时，往往会通过交叉验证来识别和纠正特殊错误。. The key insight is that different models, trained on different data with different architectures, develop complementary knowledge representations and reasoning strategies. When these models are queried independently and their outputs are systematically compared, idiosyncratic errors tend to be identified and corrected through cross-verification.

6 讨论Discussion

6.1 为什么异质专家的表现优于同质集成Why Heterogeneous Experts Outperform Homogeneous Ensembles

消融研究表明，相同模型的集合（The ablation study reveals that the same-model ensemble (3×3\times与单一 GPT-5.4 模型相比，GPT-5.4）的幻觉相对减少了 18.3%，而异构理事会则实现了 35.9%。出现这种差异的原因是来自同一提供商的模型共享训练数据、架构偏差和故障模式。当 GPT-5.4 对某个主张产生幻觉时，GPT-5.4 的另一个实例可能会产生相同的幻觉。相比之下，Claude Opus 4.6 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro 在不同架构的不同语料库上进行训练，更有可能提供正确的信息或至少提供不同的错误，从而使综合模型能够识别和纠正幻觉。GPT-5.4) achieves an 18.3% relative reduction in hallucination compared to the single GPT-5.4 model, while the heterogeneous Council achieves 35.9%. This disparity arises because models from the same provider share training data, architectural biases, and failure modes. When GPT-5.4 hallucinates on a claim, another instance of GPT-5.4 is likely to produce the same hallucination. In contrast, Claude Opus 4.6 and Gemini 3.1 Pro, trained on different corpora with different architectures, are more likely to provide correct information or at least different errors, enabling the synthesis model to identify and correct the hallucination.

6.2 结构化合成的作用The Role of Structured Synthesis

简单多数投票将所有主张视为二元（正确/不正确）并丢弃有价值的推理链。结构化综合方法保留了每位专家响应的完整背景，使综合模型能够：(1) 识别Simple majority voting treats all claims as binary (correct/incorrect) and discards valuable reasoning chains. The structured synthesis approach preserves the full context of each expert’s response, enabling the synthesis model to: (1) identify为什么why专家们不同意，不仅仅是experts disagree, not just那个that他们不同意； (2) 评估支持每项主张的证据强度； (3) 保留可能在投票方案中丢失的独特见解。这对于复杂、多方面的问题尤其重要，因为“正确”答案是微妙的。they disagree; (2) assess the strength of evidence supporting each claim; and (3) preserve unique insights that might be lost in a voting scheme. This is particularly important for complex, multi-faceted questions where the “correct” answer is nuanced.

6.3 局限性Limitations

理事会模式有几个限制。首先，延迟增加（大约 2-3The Council Mode has several limitations. First, the increased latency (approximately 2–3×\times与单个模型相比）对于延迟敏感的应用程序可能是不可接受的。其次，该方法依赖于多个前沿模型 API 的可用性和成本，这可能不适用于所有部署。第三，共识机制虽然减少了幻觉，但并不能完全消除幻觉——如果所有专家都有同样的误解，理事会就会重现它。最后，当前的实现使用一组固定的三名专家；基于查询特征的自适应专家选择可以进一步提高性能。compared to individual models) may be unacceptable for latency-sensitive applications. Second, the approach relies on the availability and cost of multiple frontier model APIs, which may not be feasible for all deployments. Third, while the consensus mechanism reduces hallucination, it cannot eliminate it entirely—if all experts share the same misconception, the Council will reproduce it. Finally, the current implementation uses a fixed set of three experts; adaptive expert selection based on query characteristics could further improve performance.

7 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了理事会模式，这是一种多代理共识架构，旨在减轻传统法学硕士和教育部模型中固有的幻觉和偏见。通过利用多个异构前沿模型的认知多样性，并采用明确识别共识、分歧和独特发现的结构化综合管道，理事会模式提供了高度可靠且基于事实的响应。我们的综合评估显示了多个基准的显着改进：HaluEval 的幻觉率相对降低了 35.9%，TruthfulQA 提高了 7.8%，跨领域的偏差方差降低了 85-89%。In this paper, we presented the Council Mode, a multi-agent consensus architecture designed to mitigate hallucinations and biases inherent in traditional LLMs and MoE models. By leveraging the cognitive diversity of multiple heterogeneous frontier models and employing a structured synthesis pipeline that explicitly identifies consensus, disagreement, and unique findings, the Council Mode delivers highly reliable and factually grounded responses. Our comprehensive evaluation demonstrates significant improvements across multiple benchmarks: a 35.9% relative reduction in hallucination rates on HaluEval, a 7.8-point improvement on TruthfulQA, and an 85–89% reduction in bias variance across domains.

开源实现为未来的多模型编排研究提供了坚实的基础。我们相信，理事会范式代表了构建值得信赖的人工智能系统的一个有前途的方向，补充而不是取代了模型级一致性和安全性方面正在进行的努力。The open-source implementation provides a robust foundation for future research into multi-model orchestration. We believe that the Council paradigm represents a promising direction for building trustworthy AI systems, complementing rather than replacing ongoing efforts in model-level alignment and safety.

附录AAppendix A综合提示模板Synthesis Prompt Template

综合模型收到包含 16 条强制规则的系统提示。关键规则包括：The synthesis model receives a system prompt containing 16 mandatory rules. The key rules include:

  1. 1.

    输出必须包含四个部分：模型共识、模型分歧、独特发现和综合分析。The output must contain exactly four sections: Model Consensus, Model Disagreement, Unique Findings, and Comprehensive Analysis.

  2. 2.

    每个共识点都必须得到所有三位专家的证据的支持。Each consensus point must be supported by evidence from all three experts.

  3. 3.

    分歧必须包括每位专家立场背后的理由。Disagreements must include the reasoning behind each expert’s position.

  4. 4.

    综合分析必须直接回答用户的问题，整合所有证据。The Comprehensive Analysis must directly answer the user’s question, integrating all evidence.

  5. 5.

    必须保存并注明来自专家答复的引用。Citations from expert responses must be preserved and attributed.

  6. 6.

    综合不得引入任何专家回应中未出现的新主张。The synthesis must not introduce new claims not present in any expert response.

  7. 7.

    当专家一致同意时，综合报告应具有高度可信度。When experts unanimously agree, the synthesis should present the claim with high confidence.

  8. 8.

    当专家意见不一致时，综合报告应呈现所有观点并表明证据的重要性。When experts disagree, the synthesis should present all perspectives and indicate the weight of evidence.
