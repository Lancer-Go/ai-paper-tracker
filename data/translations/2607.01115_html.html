为大学利益相关者开发多模式聊天助手：基于 RAG 的方法Towards Developing a Multimodal Chat Assistant for University Stakeholders: RAG-based Approach

Md Abu Hanif Shaikh, and Abdullah Al Shafi
摘要Abstract

大学利益相关者在获取及时、可靠的信息方面常常面临困难，特别是在智能支持系统很少的发展中国家。现有的基于规则的聊天机器人无法处理复杂的、特定领域的查询，并且没有能力适应不断变化的机构政策。作为一种填补空白的解决方案，我们提出了具有检索增强生成功能的多模式大学聊天机器人。该系统将大型语言模型与语义检索相结合，从以机构为中心的资源（例如大学手册）生成基于上下文的响应。该系统通过视觉语言模型接受文本和图像查询，并应用量化推理以在受限硬件上快速部署。使用 FastAPI 构建的可扩展后端与使用 Next.js 开发的响应式前端相连，确保了实时可用性。我们的多模式评估表明，尽管视觉输入的响应时间增加，但系统在文本和图像查询方面都保持了很高的满意度分数。此外，定量评估表明，在我们提出的基于 RAG 的系统中，幻觉从 31.7% 减少到 6.6%，证实了检索接地的有效性。University stakeholders often face difficulties in accessing timely and reliable information, especially in developing countries, where there are very few intelligent support systems. Existing rule-based chatbots are unable to handle complex, domain-specific queries and are not well-equipped to adapt to evolving institutional policies. As a fill-in-the-gap solution, we present the multimodal university chatbot with retrieval-augmented generation. The system combines the large language model with semantic retrieval to produce context-based responses from institution-centric resources, such as the university handbook. The system accepts text and image queries through the vision-language model and applies quantized inference for rapid deployment on constrained hardware. A scalable backend built with FastAPI, adjoined with a responsive frontend developed with Next.js, ensures real-time usability. Our multimodal evaluation demonstrates that the system maintains strong satisfaction scores across both text and image queries, despite increased response time for visual inputs. Furthermore, quantitative evaluation shows that hallucination is reduced from 31.7% to 6.6% in our proposed RAG-based system, confirming the effectiveness of retrieval grounding.

I 简介Introduction

人们通常更喜欢收到即时的总结性答复，而不是搜索冗长的文档Instead of searching through lengthy documents, people generally prefer to receive an instant and summarized response[mcgrath2025generative]。这就是为什么聊天机器人在教育、临床决策支持系统、客户服务等各个领域日益流行的原因. That is why chatbots are getting popular day by day in various sectors like education, clinical decision support system, customer service, etc[peyton2025review]。这同样适用于大学及其利益相关者，如教师、行政官员、学生、工作人员和潜在申请人。大学聊天机器人在美国、英国、加拿大等发达国家非常常见，但在孟加拉国等发展中国家几乎闻所未闻. The same holds for universities and their stakeholders like teachers, administrative officers, students, staff, and potential applicants. University chatbots are very common in developed countries like the USA, UK, Canada, but almost unheard of in developing countries like Bangladesh[peyton2025review]。因此，构建这样的聊天机器人确实是迈向学术支持基础设施现代化、弥合数字鸿沟以及增强学生在资源有限的教育环境中获取信息的重要一步。. Hence, building such a chatbot is indeed an important step toward the modernization of academic support infrastructure, bridging the digital divide, and enhancing student access to information in resource-constrained educational environments.

此外，传统的基于规则的聊天机器人系统和静态知识库通常无法处理复杂的查询，涉及机构政策中的细微差别、课程设置的动态变化或与用户上下文相关的个性化响应Furthermore, traditional rule-based chatbot systems and static knowledge bases frequently cannot handle complex queries involving subtle nuances in institutional policies, dynamic changes in course offerings, or personalized responses tied to user context[peyton2025review]。这一差距迫切需要开发能够推理、检索和自主行动的智能系统。. This gap necessitates the urgent development of intelligent systems capable of reasoning, retrieval, and autonomous action.

我们提出的系统通过利用检索增强生成（RAG）来解决这一挑战Our proposed system addresses this challenge by leveraging retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)[li2025retrieval]，一种先进的人工智能范式，将大型语言模型 (LLM) 与检索机制集成在一起。这种方法使聊天机器人能够检索相关的大学特定文档（例如，学术政策、课程目录、常见问题解答），这些文档可能会动态变化，而无需计算成本高昂的微调过程。我们系统的核心使用 RAG 来查找和组合文档中的信息，根据上下文给出准确的答案。后端使用 FastAPI 一次性处理多个请求，前端使用 Next.js 和 React 确保流畅的用户体验。该工作的主要贡献如下：, an advanced paradigm in AI that integrates large language models (LLMs) with retrieval mechanisms. This approach enables the chatbot to retrieve relevant university-specific documents (e.g., academic policies, course catalogs, FAQs), which may change dynamically, without a computationally expensive fine-tuning process. The core of our system uses RAG to find and combine information from documents, giving accurate answers based on context. The backend uses FastAPI for handling many requests at once, and the frontend ensures a smooth user experience with Next.js and React. The main contributions of the work are listed below:

  1. i.

    开发由 RAG 驱动的大学聊天助手，利用视觉语言模型 (VLM) 处理文本和图像查询。Development of a RAG-powered university chat assistant that processes both text and image queries utilizing a vision language model (VLM).

  2. 二.ii.

    为了支持这个聊天助手，构建了一个利用语义嵌入和 chromaDB 的检索管道，以提供来自机构文档的上下文感知的准确响应。To support this chat assistant, a retrieval pipeline utilizing semantic embeddings and chromaDB was constructed to provide context-aware, accurate responses from institutional documents.

  3. 三.iii.

    实现量化推理和异步 FastAPI 后端，以实现实时、资源高效的操作。Implementation of quantized inference and asynchronous FastAPI backend for real-time, resource-efficient operation.

  4. 四.iv.

    将我们提议的系统部署为可公开访问的聊天助手：Deployment of our proposed system as a publicly accessible chat assistant at\ttfamilychat.kuet.ac.bd具有交互式界面、反馈系统，并具有处理幻觉问题的能力。with an interactive interface, feedback system, and demonstrated ability to handle hallucination issues.

Neupane et al.[neupane2024questions]推出 BARKPLUG V.2，这是一个基于 RAG 的聊天机器人，它通过提供准确的、针对特定上下文的答案来改善对大学资源的访问，实现高性能和令人满意的可用性。论文present BARKPLUG V.2, a RAG-based chatbot that improves access to university resources by delivering accurate, context-specific answers, achieving high performance and satisfactory usability. 三、III提议的基于 RAG 的系统Proposed RAG-based System

如图所示。As shown in Fig.1，人工智能系统被设计为接受多模式输入（文本和图像）以生成连贯且上下文敏感的响应。该系统利用尖端的视觉语言模型、嵌入技术和文档检索技术。其首要目的是理解以自然语言或图像形式提出的查询，然后通过从预定义的知识源（大学手册）查询或实时图像分析来提供描述性响应。, an AI system was designed to accept multimodal input (text and image) to generate coherent and context-sensitive responses. The system utilizes cutting-edge vision language models, embedding techniques, and document retrieval techniques. Its foremost purpose is the understanding of queries posed in natural language or image form, and then providing a descriptive response by either querying from pre-defined knowledge sources (University handbook) or image analysis on a real-time basis.

Ⅲ-AIII-A 系统概览System Overview

该系统被配置为支持 RESTful API 的 Web 应用程序。它以自然语言文本或带有文本的图像的形式接收用户的输入，并根据输入的内容动态选择适当的管道。该架构主要有三个模块：（1）推理引擎； (2) 知识检索和增强； (3) 反馈收集和监测。The system is configured as a web application with RESTful APIs support. It receives input from the user in the form of natural-language text or an image with text and dynamically selects the appropriate pipeline depending on the content of the input. The architecture has three main modules: (1) inference engine; (2) knowledge retrieval and augmentation; and (3) feedback collection and monitoring.

Ⅲ-AIII-A1 多模态推理引擎Multimodal Inference Engine

用户输入由大型 VLM llava-1.5-7b 处理The user inputs are processed by a large-scale VLM llava-1.5-7b111https://huggingface.co/liuhaotian/llava-v1.5-7b，能够进行多模式处理。该模型在量化级别上加载，以最大限度地减少内存消耗并使推理速度更快，同时为描述和解释任务保持足够的准确性。用户可以发送图像或文本查询，或同时发送两者。如果两种模式都存在，系统会构建一个包含用户查询和图像的结构化提示，以进行上下文感知视觉推理。纯文本查询仅进行语言处理，并有一个可选步骤，从特定领域的知识库补充上下文信息。无论情况如何，该模型都擅长指令跟踪和自然语言生成。, which is capable of multimodal processing. The model is loaded on a quantized level to minimize memory consumption and to make the inference faster, while maintaining sufficient accuracy for the description and interpretation tasks. A user may send either an image or a textual query, or both at the same time. The system, in case both modalities exist, builds a structured prompt incorporating the user’s query and the image for context-aware visual reasoning. Text-only queries undergo language-only processing, with an optional step of supplementing in-context information from a domain-specific knowledge base. Whatever the scenario might be, the model excels at instruction following and natural language generation.

Ⅲ-AIII-A2 知识库建设Knowledge Base Construction

为了增强模型对特定领域知识的响应，对静态文档语料库进行了预处理和索引。该管道包括：To enhance the model’s responses with domain-specific knowledge, a static document corpus is preprocessed and indexed. The pipeline includes:

文件加载：Document loading:系统的第一步涉及文档的摄取，其中从知识源中提取原始文本数据。在考虑本研究时，以 PDF 格式的 KUET 大学官方手册作为主要来源。解析文档的每一页以获得行级文本。为了减少噪音，应用正则表达式来删除不相关的元素，例如罗马数字标题、数字索引、页面标记等，这些元素根本没有语义信息。通过这种方式，只保留有意义的内容，从而最大限度地提高 RAG 管道其他阶段的相关性和准确性。The first step of the system concerns the ingestion of documents, where raw textual data is extracted from the knowledge source. In the consideration of this study, the official KUET university handbook, in PDF format, was taken as the primary source. Each page of the document was parsed to obtain line-level text. To reduce noise, regular expressions were applied to remove irrelevant elements such as roman-numeral headers, numeric indices, page markers, etc., none of which have semantic information at all. In this way, only meaningful content was retained, thus maximizing the relevance and accuracy of the other stages of the RAG pipeline.

文本分割：Text splitting:由于法学硕士无法处理任意长的文本序列，因此加载的文档被分成更小的、语义连贯的单元。首先，在考虑基于字符的分割之前，会递归地尝试在逻辑边界（段落、句子）处分割文本。块大小为 512 个字符，重叠为 64 个字符，非常适合相邻块之间的上下文连续性；确保边界附近的重要短语在多个块中共享，从而最大限度地减少检索过程中的信息丢失。粒度和语义完整性之间的这种平衡保证了块在上下文上足够丰富，并且在计算上足够小，以便由系统处理嵌入生成。Since LLMs are unable to process arbitrarily long sequences of text, the loaded document was thus divided into smaller, semantically coherent units. At first, the text is recursively attempted to be split at logical boundaries (paragraphs, sentences) before considering character-based splits. A chunk size of 512 characters, with an overlap of 64, is ideal for contextual continuity between adjacent chunks; ensuring that important phrases near the boundaries are shared in multiple chunks and therefore minimize information loss during retrieval. This balance between granularity and semantic integrity guarantees that chunks are rich enough contextually and small enough computationally to be handled for embedding generation by the system.

语义索引：Semantic Indexing:文本分割完成后，每个文本块都被转换为密集向量嵌入来表达语义。嵌入是使用 all-miniLM-L6-v2 创建的Once text splitting was done, each chunk of text was converted into a dense vector embedding to express semantic meaning. Embeddings were created using the all-miniLM-L6-v2222https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2生成针对语义相似性任务优化的 384 维向量的模型。然后，由于 Chroma 向量存储极其轻便、保存数据的能力以及快速的相似性搜索机制，这些向量被放入 Chroma 向量存储中进行存储。保留嵌入可以使系统免于跨会话重新计算嵌入，从而减少延迟。嵌入将语义相似的文本块在向量空间中聚集在一起，从而允许在查询时检索相关知识。model that yields 384-dimensional vectors optimized for semantic similarity tasks. The vectors were then put into a Chroma vector store for storage due to its extreme lightness, ability to persist data, and fast similarity search mechanisms. Persisting the embeddings saved the system from recomputing the embeddings across sessions, reducing latency. The embeddings clustered semantically similar text chunks together in vector space, thereby allowing the retrieval of pertinent knowledge at query time.

Ⅲ-AIII-A3 检索Retrieval

检索器充当用户查询和知识库本身之间的桥梁。当提交查询时，必须使用相同的嵌入模型将其嵌入到相同的语义空间中，以保持表示的一致性。然后，通过查询嵌入与存储的文档嵌入的余弦相似度进行对比，以选择高度相关的块。这里，考虑到相关性和效率之间的良好平衡，检索了前 3 名。The retriever acts as a bridge between user queries and the knowledge base itself. When the query is submitted, it must be embedded into the same semantic space using the same embedding model to maintain uniformity in representation. Then, the query embedding is contrasted with the stored document embeddings by their cosine similarity to select the highly relevant chunks. Here, the top-3 were retrieved, as a good balance between relevance and efficiency was considered.

Ⅲ-AIII-A4 通过上下文查询融合进行增强Augmentation via Context-Query Fusion

根据用户的查询检索最相关的前 3 个块后，将这些片段连接起来以创建一个上下文块。然后，将该上下文块与原始查询结合起来，创建增强提示，然后将其传递到语言模型。最终的提示模板如下：After retrieving the most relevant top-3 chunks based on the user’s query, concatenates these segments to create one contextual block. This contextual block is then joined with the original query to create an augmented prompt, which is then passed to the language model. The final prompt template is as follows:

“使用以下经过大学验证的信息来回答。如果信息不充分，请说‘我无法回答这个问题。’“Use the following university-verified information to answer. If the information is insufficient, say ‘I cannot answer the question.’
检索到的上下文 1：...Retrieved Context 1: …
检索到的上下文 2：……Retrieved Context 2: …
检索到的上下文 3：……Retrieved Context 3: …
用户查询：……”User Query: …”

这种即时构建迫使模型了解从底层语料库中检索到的特定领域知识，从而增强其生成扎根的、上下文感知的响应，并减少幻觉。Such prompt construction forces the model to be aware of domain-specific knowledge retrieved from the underlying corpus, thus sharpening its generation of grounded, context-aware responses, and reducing hallucinations.

Ⅲ-AIII-A5 绩效监控和反馈收集Performance Monitoring and Feedback Collection

反馈可以通过表单提供，该表单接受姓名、电子邮件和对所生成输出的评论等详细信息。该反馈被记录到 CSV 文件中，随后用于评估和增强模型。Feedback can be provided via a form that accepts details such as name, email, and comments on the outputs produced. This feedback is logged into a CSV file and later used for assessment and enhancement of the model.

IV-A 实验装置Experimental setup

建议的系统是使用 Next.js 前端和 FastAPI 后端构建的。对于视觉语言处理，它采用LLaVA-1.5-7B模型，而ChromaDB作为矢量数据库。生成式 AI 组件是使用 PyTorch 实现的。文档通过 PyPDFLoader 模块摄取并使用 RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter 进行分段。使用语义相似性搜索作为检索机制，并由全 MiniLM-L6-v2 嵌入模型支持。整个检索增强生成（RAG）工作流程是通过 LangChain 框架协调的。The proposed system is built using a Next.js front end and a FastAPI back end. For visual–language processing, it employs the LLaVA-1.5-7B model, while ChromaDB serves as the vector database. The generative AI components are implemented using PyTorch. Documents are ingested through the PyPDFLoader module and segmented using the RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter. Semantic similarity search is used as the retrieval mechanism, supported by the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embedding model. The overall retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflow is orchestrated through the LangChain framework.

IV-F 多模式绩效评估Multimodal Performance Evaluation

52 名试验参与者填写的反馈表的平均满意度为 4.26/5，表明获得学术资源的机会有所改善。除此之外，我们还分析了用户对不同查询方式的满意度。该系统的纯文本查询平均得分为 4.56/5，基于图像查询的平均得分为 3.95/5。图像查询的平均响应时间为 16.4 秒，比纯文本查询长约 4.6 秒，这是由多模式处理的开销造成的。The feedback form filled out by 52 trial participants yielded a satisfaction score of 4.26/5 on average, indicating improved access to academic resources. Besides that, we also analyzed user satisfaction with the different query modalities. The system managed to reach the average score of 4.56/5 for text-only queries and 3.95/5 for image-based queries. The average response time for image queries was 16.4 seconds, about 4.6 seconds longer than for text-only ones, which was caused by the overhead of multimodal processing.

V 数据隐私、安全访问控制和合规性Data Privacy, Secure Access Control, and Compliance

机构文件和用户查询均在非常严格的安全措施下处理。检索功能仅涉及基于JWT的授权，并且客户端和服务器的整个通信均受到HTTPS保护。电子邮件或 ID 等敏感数据在记录前已被屏蔽，上传的图像仅在内存中处理，除非获得许可。此外，还制定了非常严格的 CORS 策略，仅允许来自受信任的大学域的请求。这些行为不仅可以确保隐私，而且还符合机构数据治理政策。Institutional documents and user queries are dealt with under very strict security measures. Retrieval functions involve only JWT-based authorization, and the entire communication of the client and server is protected with HTTPS. Sensitive data like emails or IDs are already masked before logging, and uploaded images are processed only in memory unless permission is granted. Furthermore, very strict CORS policies are in place that allow requests from only the trusted university domains. Not only do these actions assure privacy, but they also comply with the institutional data governance policies.

VI 结论Conclusions

该研究展示了将 RAG 与多模态推理相结合来创建可以处理文本和图像查询的智能大学聊天助手的有效性。通过使用语义嵌入、向量存储和检索管道，系统提供基于上下文的响应。这减少了对静态知识库的依赖，并有助于避免大型语言模型中常见的幻觉。聊天助手被部署为公开可用的 Web 应用程序，向各个大学利益相关者展示了其实际用途。这种方法强调特定领域的检索增强型聊天助手如何使信息更易于访问、简化管理任务并帮助缩小教育机构（尤其是发展中地区）的数字差距。尽管我们的系统正确回答了大多数特定领域的问题，但仍然存在一些限制，例如多跳推理、延迟问题等。有时我们还面临上下文集成问题，从而导致非常通用的响应。将来，我们希望使用代理 RAG、多跳 RAG 等高级 RAG 变体来扩展我们的系统，使其更加可靠。The study shows the effectiveness of combining RAG with multimodal inference to create a smart university chat assistant that can handle both text and image queries. By using semantic embeddings, vector storage, and retrieval pipelines, the system provides responses based on context. This reduces the reliance on static knowledge bases and helps avoid the hallucinations often seen in large language models. The chat assistant is deployed as a publicly available web application, demonstrating its practical use for various university stakeholders. This method emphasizes how domain-specific, retrieval-augmented chat assistants can make information more accessible, streamline administrative tasks, and help close digital gaps in educational institutions, especially in developing areas. Though our system answers most of the domain-specific questions correctly, there are still several limitations, like multihop reasoning, latency issues, etc. Sometimes we also face the contextual integration problem, thus resulting in a very generic response. In the future, we would like to extend our system to be more reliable using advanced RAG variants like agentic RAG, multi-hop RAG, etc.
