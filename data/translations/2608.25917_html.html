明智地选择你的游戏：测量现实世界车辆交互中的博弈论结构Choose Your Game Wisely: Measuring Game-Theoretic Structures in Real-World Vehicle Interactions

Yueyuan Li    Rongcheng Nie    Weijie Xi    Mingyang Jiang    Songan Zhang    Hanyang Zhuang    and Ming Yang ††thanks: This work is supported in part by the National Natural Science Foundation of China under Grant 62573289, 52402504, and U22A20100 (Corresponding author: Ming Yang, email: MingYANG@sjtu.edu.cn).††thanks: Yueyuan˜Li, Weijie˜Xi, Mingyang˜Jiang, Ming˜Yang are with the School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Key Laboratory of System Control and Information Processing, Ministry of Education of China, Shanghai, 200240, CN.††thanks: Rongcheng Nie is with the School of Airspace Science and Engineering, Shandong University, Weihai, Shandong, 264209, CN.††thanks: Songan˜Zhang is with the Global Institute of Future Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, 200240, CN.††thanks: Hanyang˜Zhuang is with Global College, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, 200240, CN.
摘要Abstract

博弈论模型为车辆交互建模提供了原则框架，但其潜在的时间假设尚未针对现实世界的驾驶行为进行系统检验。 特别是，目前尚不清楚如何从车辆轨迹测量同时、顺序和不对称的相互作用结构。 本文开发了一种基于轨迹的交互测量框架，以识别交互事件并量化行为变化的发生、时间组织、发生后反应动态和秩序稳定性。 该框架使用行为偏差来验证候选人的互动。 我们在六个真实世界轨迹数据集上评估该框架，包括 INTERACTION、highD、inD、roundD、Waymo Open Motion 和 nuPlan，涵盖不同的道路几何形状、交通环境和交互类型。 结果表明，同时发生和连续发生的行为变化在观察到的跟随、合并和冲突互动中都占很大比例。 在顺序交互中，稳定排序比交替排序更普遍，这表明持续的不对称角色是一种常见的交互结构。 重要的是，时间优先性不一定与可测量的行为反应一致，这表明单独的时间顺序可能不足以表征行为依赖性。 这些发现表明，现实世界的交互表现出并发、顺序和持续有序的时间结构。 因此，不同的博弈论公式最好被视为不同交互机制的补充建模抽象，而不是作为管理所有车辆交互的通用结构。Game-theoretic models provide principled frameworks for modeling vehicle interactions, but their underlying temporal assumptions have not been systematically examined against real-world driving behavior. In particular, it remains unclear how simultaneous, sequential, and asymmetric interaction structures can be measured from vehicle trajectories. This paper develops a trajectory-based interaction measurement framework to identify interaction events and quantify behavioral change onset, temporal organization, post-onset response dynamics, and ordering stability. The framework uses behavioral deviations to verify candidate interactions. We evaluate the framework on six real-world trajectory datasets, including INTERACTION, highD, inD, rounD, Waymo Open Motion, and nuPlan, covering diverse road geometries, traffic environments, and interaction types. The results show that concurrent and sequential behavioral changes both constitute substantial proportions of observed following, merging, and conflicting interactions. Among sequential interactions, stable ordering is more prevalent than alternating ordering, indicating that persistent asymmetric roles are a common interaction structure. Importantly, temporal precedence does not necessarily coincide with a measurable behavioral response, indicating that temporal ordering alone may not be sufficient to characterize behavioral dependence. These findings show that real-world interactions exhibit concurrent, sequential, and persistently ordered temporal structures. Different game-theoretic formulations are therefore better regarded as complementary modeling abstractions for different interaction regimes rather than as a universal structure governing all vehicle interactions.

I 简介Introduction

复杂交通中的车辆行为通常受到周围车辆的影响，而不是独立确定Vehicle behavior in complex traffic is often shaped by surrounding vehicles rather than determined independently[1]。 这种相互作用在高速公路、坡道、十字路口和环岛中尤其明显. Such interactions are particularly evident in highways, ramps, intersections, and roundabouts[2, 3]，其中一辆车的行为变化可能会引起另一辆车的后续响应。 真实世界的轨迹数据提供车辆运动的直接测量，并已广泛用于识别交互车辆, where a behavioral change by one vehicle may induce a subsequent response from another. Real-world trajectory data provide direct measurements of vehicle motion and have been widely used to identify interacting vehicles[4]，表征交互模式, characterize interaction patterns[3, 5]，并对车辆行为进行建模或预测, and model or predict vehicle behavior[6]。 然而，现有的研究主要检测或模拟观察到的行为，而行为反应的时间和方向组织仍然缺乏系统的测量。 特别是，仅靠接近度或时间优先级并不能确定观察到的变化是否构成对另一辆车的可测量的行为反应。. However, existing studies primarily detect or model observed behavior, while the temporal and directional organization of behavioral responses remains less systematically measured. In particular, proximity or temporal precedence alone does not establish whether an observed change constitutes a measurable behavioral response to another vehicle.

博弈论通过指定交互车辆如何做出决策，提供了交互决策的明确模型。 在驱动应用中已考虑合作和非合作公式Game theory provides explicit models of interactive decision making by specifying how interacting vehicles make decisions. Both cooperative and non-cooperative formulations have been considered in driving applications[7, 8]，其中常用的方法包括纳什和斯塔克尔伯格公式, with Nash and Stackelberg formulations among the commonly used approaches[9, 10]。 纳什公式通常假设同时决策，而斯塔克尔伯格公式则强加不对称的领导者-追随者角色. Nash formulations typically assume simultaneous decisions, whereas Stackelberg formulations impose asymmetric leader–follower roles[11, 12]。 因此，这些表述意味着可观察行为中不同的时间和方向结构。 然而，现实世界的相互作用是否表现出这些结构尚未得到系统测量。. These formulations therefore imply distinct temporal and directional structures in observable behavior. Whether real-world interactions exhibit these structures, however, has not been systematically measured.

为了解决这一差距，我们开发了一个基于轨迹的交互测量框架，该框架可以识别交互事件并量化行为变化的发生、时间组织、响应动态和排序稳定性。 交互方向性捕获了哪个车辆首先改变其行为以及是否发生后续可测量的响应，而时间组织和顺序稳定性则表征了这些角色的时间安排和持久性。 然后，我们使用这些测量来评估哪些博弈论时间结构最能表征不同观察到的相互作用机制。To address this gap, we develop a trajectory-based interaction measurement framework that identifies interaction events and quantifies behavioral change onset, temporal organization, response dynamics, and ordering stability. Interaction directionality captures which vehicle changes its behavior first and whether a subsequent measurable response occurs, while temporal organization and ordering stability characterize the timing and persistence of these roles. We then use these measurements to assess which game-theoretic temporal structures best characterize different observed interaction regimes.

本研究不是提出另一种交互模型，而是为在模拟现实世界驾驶时在替代时间交互结构之间进行选择建立了经验基础。 本文的贡献如下：Rather than proposing another interaction model, this study establishes an empirical basis for choosing between alternative temporal interaction structures when modeling real-world driving. The contributions of this paper are as follows:

  • •

    一种交互测量框架，将行为起始、时间组织、响应动态和有序稳定性分开，使交互结构能够直接从现实世界的轨迹进行量化。An interaction measurement framework that separates behavioral onset, temporal organization, response dynamics, and ordering stability, enabling interaction structures to be quantified directly from real-world trajectories.

  • •

    跨数据集表征表明，并发和顺序行为变化在跟随、合并和冲突交互中共存，而顺序交互的主要特征是稳定而不是交替排序。A cross-dataset characterization showing that concurrent and sequential behavioral changes coexist across following, merging, and conflicting interactions, while sequential interactions are predominantly characterized by stable rather than alternating ordering.

  • •

    实证评估表明，并发、顺序和持续有序的交互结构在现实世界的驾驶中共存，支持跨交互机制的同时移动、顺序移动和领导者-跟随者公式的互补观点。An empirical assessment showing that concurrent, sequential, and persistently ordered interaction structures coexist in real-world driving, supporting a complementary view of simultaneous-move, sequential-move, and leader–follower formulations across interaction regimes.

II 相关作品Related Works

Ⅱ-AII-A 交互行为分析Interaction Behavior Analysis

大规模现实世界轨迹数据集的可用性使得可以直接根据观察到的驾驶行为来研究车辆交互。 INTERACTION、nuPlan、Waymo Open Motion Dataset (WOMD)等数据集包含大量交互交通场景，已广泛用于交互分析和运动预测The availability of large-scale real-world trajectory datasets has enabled vehicle interactions to be studied directly from observed driving behavior. Datasets such as INTERACTION, nuPlan, and the Waymo Open Motion Dataset (WOMD) contain numerous interactive traffic scenarios and have been widely used for interaction analysis and motion prediction[13, 14, 15]。 车辆交互也从行为、认知、基于优化、基于学习和博弈论的角度进行了研究. Vehicle interaction has also been studied from behavioral, cognitive, optimization-based, learning-based, and game-theoretic perspectives[1, 16]。 对于本研究来说，基于轨迹的分析尤其相关，因为它可以直接观察交互车辆如何随时间调整其行为。. For this study, trajectory-based analysis is especially relevant because it provides direct observations of how interacting vehicles adjust their behavior over time.

总体而言，基于轨迹的研究在描述车辆是否相互作用、涉及哪些车辆以及它们的行为相关程度方面变得越来越详细。 然而，在大多数情况下，引入这些描述是为了支持交互识别或轨迹预测。 很少直接从观察到的轨迹来检查两个车辆之间的行为依赖性在交互事件中如何发展。Overall, trajectory-based studies have become increasingly detailed in describing whether vehicles interact, which vehicles are involved, and how strongly their behaviors are related. In most cases, however, these descriptions are introduced to support interaction identification or trajectory prediction. How the behavioral dependence between two vehicles develops within an interaction episode is less often examined directly from observed trajectories.

Ⅱ-BII-B 基于博弈论的交互建模Interaction Modeling Based on Game Theory

博弈论已被广泛用于模拟交互式驾驶，因为它明确地解释了车辆决策之间的相互依赖性。 在博弈论公式中，每辆车通常都与反映安全、效率和舒适度等因素的目标相关联Game theory has been widely used to model interactive driving because it explicitly accounts for the interdependence between vehicle decisions. In a game-theoretic formulation, each vehicle is typically associated with an objective that reflects factors such as safety, efficiency, and comfort[10]。 该目标还可以纳入其他道路使用者的结果，允许不同的社会偏好，从主要自利行为到更合作的行为. The objective may also incorporate the outcomes of other road users, allowing different social preferences, ranging from predominantly self-interested behavior to more cooperative behavior[25].

博弈论模型不仅在车辆目标方面有所不同，而且在车辆之间的交互结构方面也有所不同。 纳什公式通常对同一决策阶段内相互依赖的决策进行建模Game-theoretic models differ not only in vehicle objectives but also in how the interaction between vehicles is structured. Nash formulations generally model mutually dependent decisions within the same decision stage[10, 26]。 Stackelberg 公式引入了一种不对称和顺序关系，其中一辆车被视为领导者，另一辆车被视为跟随者. Stackelberg formulations instead introduce an asymmetric and sequential relationship, in which one vehicle is treated as the leader and another responds as the follower[27, 28]。 分层公式引入了额外的推理级别或决策阶段来描述更多涉及的交互. Hierarchical formulations introduce additional reasoning levels or decision stages to describe more involved interactions[29, 30]。 纳什和斯塔克尔伯格公式也被考虑在同一驾驶决策框架内，以研究不同的博弈结构如何影响决策. Nash and Stackelberg formulations have also been considered within the same driving decision framework to examine how different game structures affect decision making[28, 31].

一些基于游戏的方法允许部分交互随着更多观察的出现而改变。 张等人。Some game-based methods allow parts of the interaction to change as more observations become available. Zhang et al.[32]例如，在基于 Stackelberg 的控制器中在线估计驾驶员的攻击性，以进行强制变道。 李等人。, for example, estimated driver aggressiveness online within a Stackelberg-based controller for mandatory lane changing. Li et al.[33]使用贝叶斯推理和认知层次理论，根据另一位驾驶员的推断行为来更新自我车辆的策略。 刘等人。used Bayesian inference together with cognitive hierarchy theory to update the ego vehicle’s strategy according to the inferred behavior of another driver. Liu et al.[34]在部分可观察的游戏中，将成对的领导者-追随者关系视为不确定，并根据强制合并期间观察到的轨迹来估计它们。 在这些模型中，驾驶员类型、意图或角色不必在整个交互过程中保持固定，尽管这种适应仍然在指定的游戏公式中执行。treated pairwise leader–follower relationships as uncertain in a partially observable game and estimated them from observed trajectories during forced merging. In these models, driver type, intention, or role does not have to remain fixed throughout the interaction, although such adaptation is still performed within a specified game formulation.

这些公式已应用于各种驾驶场景，包括变道、并道、十字路口、环岛和赛车These formulations have been applied to a variety of driving scenarios, including lane changing, merging, intersections, roundabouts, and racing[35]。 尽管具体假设不同，但交互结构通常被指定为模型公式的一部分. Although the specific assumptions differ, the interaction structure is generally specified as part of the model formulation[36]，而真实世界或模拟驾驶数据主要用于校准模型参数、重现观察到的行为或评估模型性能, while real-world or simulated driving data are mainly used to calibrate model parameters, reproduce observed behavior, or evaluate model performance[27, 28]。 因此，经验证据通常用于评估在预定义的交互结构下生成的行为，而不是直接从观察到的交互中识别该结构。. Consequently, empirical evidence is often used to assess behavior generated under a predefined interaction structure rather than to identify that structure directly from observed interactions.

总的来说，现有的研究要么代表行为分析和预测的相互作用，要么在行为模型中规定其结构。 这使得在实际驾驶行为中观察到的结构不太容易被理解。Taken together, existing studies either represent interaction for behavioral analysis and prediction or prescribe its structure within a behavioral model. This leaves the structure observed in real driving behavior less well understood.

三、III方法Method

Ⅲ-AIII-A 初步Preliminary

在介绍交互测量框架之前，我们回顾了车辆交互常用博弈论模型假设的可观察的时间影响。Before introducing the interaction measurement framework, we review the observable temporal implications of assumptions underlying commonly used game-theoretic models for vehicle interaction.

Ⅲ-AIII-A1 同时移动假设Simultaneous-move assumption

在同步移动游戏中，玩家在同一决策阶段选择自己的行动，而无需观察其他玩家当前的行动。 纳什均衡为此类博弈提供了经典的解决方案概念，其中每个玩家的策略在给定其他玩家选择的策略的情况下都是最优的In simultaneous-move games, players select their actions within the same decision stage without observing the current actions of other players. The Nash equilibrium provides a classical solution concept for such games, where each player’s strategy is optimal given the strategies selected by others[11]。 在车辆交互中，这对应于车辆之间没有可测量的时间优先级的并发行为变化。. In vehicle interactions, this corresponds to concurrent behavioral changes without a measurable temporal precedence between the vehicles.

Ⅲ-AIII-A2 顺序移动假设Sequential-move assumption

顺序移动游戏描述了玩家在不同阶段做出决策的交互，创建了一个有序的决策过程，其中一个玩家的行动先于另一玩家的后续决策，并且可以在另一玩家的后续决策之前观察到Sequential-move games describe interactions in which players make decisions at different stages, creating an ordered decision process where the action of one player precedes and can be observed before the subsequent decision of another player[37]。 在车辆交互中，这对应于可观察行为变化的按时间顺序排列的序列。. In vehicle interactions, this corresponds to a temporally ordered sequence of observable behavioral changes.

Ⅲ-AIII-A3 领导者-追随者结构Leader–follower structure

Stackelberg 博弈代表了不对称领导者-追随者角色的顺序交互，追随者在观察领导者的行为后做出反应Stackelberg games represent a sequential interaction with asymmetric leader–follower roles, where the follower responds after observing the leader’s action[27]。 在车辆交互中，这种结构意味着持续的不对称排序，这可以通过观察到的行为变化进行实证检验。. In vehicle interactions, this structure implies persistent asymmetric ordering, which can be empirically examined from observed behavioral changes.

Ⅲ-BIII-B 交互事件提取Interaction Event Extraction

遵循互动的概念Following the concept of interaction in[38]，我们将交互视为两辆车打算在不久的将来同时占据同一空间区域的情况。 这种交互可以通过车辆运动的变化以及非运动学线索（例如车辆灯光、凝视或手势）来表达。 在这项研究中，我们重点关注从车辆轨迹中可观察到的相互作用。, we consider an interaction as a situation in which two vehicles intend to occupy the same spatial region at the same time in the near future. Such interactions may be expressed through changes in vehicle motion as well as non-kinematic cues such as vehicle lights, gaze, or gestures. In this study, we focus on interactions observable from vehicle trajectories.

IV-E 对博弈论交互模型的影响Implications for Game-Theoretic Interaction Models

实证研究结果为第 2 节中介绍的博弈论假设提供了以下启示。The empirical findings provide the following implications for the game-theoretic assumptions introduced in Sec.III-A.

IV-E1 同时移动博弈假设Simultaneous-move game assumption

并发行为变化在数据集和交互类型中很常见，这表明纳什公式背后的同时移动结构代表了一类有意义的车辆交互。Concurrent behavioral changes are common across datasets and interaction types, indicating that the simultaneous-move structure underlying Nash formulations represents a meaningful class of vehicle interactions.

IV-E2 顺序移动博弈假设Sequential-move game assumption

顺序行为变化也很普遍，其比例有时超过同时互动的比例。 这一发现表明，顺序移动公式同样与观察到的车辆相互作用相关。Sequential behavioral changes are also prevalent, with their proportion sometimes exceeding that of concurrent interactions. This finding shows that sequential-move formulations are likewise relevant to observed vehicle interactions.

IV-E3 领导者-追随者结构Leader–follower structure

在顺序交互中，稳定排序比数据集中的交替排序更为常见。 这表明持久不对称排序是顺序交互中的一种有意义的结构，支持领导者-追随者公式的使用。Among sequential interactions, stable ordering is substantially more common than alternating ordering across datasets. This suggests that persistent asymmetric ordering is a meaningful structure within sequential interactions, supporting the use of leader–follower formulations.

总体而言，结果表明Overall, the results suggest a相互作用结构的混合mixture of interaction structures而不是单一的普遍时间组织。 在不同的交互环境和数据集中都观察到了并发、顺序和稳定有序的行为模式，这表明不同的博弈论公式提供了对现实世界车辆交互的补充描述。rather than a single universal temporal organization. Concurrent, sequential, and stably ordered behavioral patterns are all observed across different interaction contexts and datasets, indicating that different game-theoretic formulations provide complementary descriptions of real-world vehicle interactions.

V 结论Conclusion

这项工作提出了一个测量框架，用于使用真实世界的车辆轨迹检查与博弈论公式相关的时间交互结构。 在六个现实世界的轨迹数据集中，车辆交互在时间组织上显示出巨大的变化。 并发和顺序的行为变化都发生在跟随、合并和冲突场景中，而顺序交互的特征通常是稳定的而不是交替的顺序。 这些结果表明，现实世界的驾驶并不遵循单一的时间交互模式。 并发行为变化支持同时移动时间公式，而稳定排序的顺序交互则支持领导者-追随者时间公式。 相同的几何关系可以在数据集中表现出不同的时间组织，这表明仅交互几何不足以确定适当的交互结构。 反应分析还表明，时间优先性并不一定意味着可测量的行为反应。 因此，在解释顺序交互时应单独考虑时间顺序和行为依赖性。 该证据涉及可观察行为的时间组织。它本身并不建立车辆的决策过程、信息集、效用函数、策略或均衡机制。 未来的交互模型应该考虑时间组织和响应行为的这种变化，而不是假设单一的交互机制。This work presents a measurement framework for examining the temporal interaction structures associated with game-theoretic formulations using real-world vehicle trajectories. Across six real-world trajectory datasets, vehicle interactions show substantial variation in temporal organization. Concurrent and sequential behavioral changes both occur across following, merging, and conflicting scenarios, while sequential interactions are more often characterized by stable than alternating ordering. These results indicate that real-world driving does not follow a single temporal interaction pattern. Concurrent behavioral changes support simultaneous-move temporal formulations, whereas sequential interactions with stable ordering support leader–follower temporal formulations. The same geometric relation can exhibit different temporal organizations across datasets, suggesting that interaction geometry alone is not sufficient to determine the appropriate interaction structure. The response analysis also shows that temporal precedence does not necessarily imply a measurable behavioral response. Temporal ordering and behavioral dependence should therefore be considered separately when interpreting sequential interactions. This evidence concerns the temporal organization of observable behavior. It does not by itself establish vehicles’ decision-making processes, information sets, utility functions, strategies, or equilibrium mechanisms. Future interaction models should account for such variation in temporal organization and response behavior rather than assuming a single interaction mechanism.
