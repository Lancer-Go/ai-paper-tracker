具有有限内存门的稳定自调制量子快速重量编程器Stable Self-Modulating Quantum Fast-Weight Programmers with Bounded Memory Gates

Kuo-Chung Peng    Jiun-Cheng Jiang    Chun-Hua Lin    Yifeng Peng    Junghoon Justin Park    Huan-Hsin Tseng    Hsin-Yi Lin    Kuan-Cheng Chen    Chen-Yu Liu    Shinjae Yoo    Samuel Yen-Chi Chen
摘要Abstract

量子快速权重编程器 (QFWP) 将时间信息存储在动态编程的变分电路参数中，而不是非线性循环隐藏状态中，从而为量子序列建模提供了一条实用的途径。自调制 QFWP 通过使用依赖于输入的门来进行新的快速权重更新和累积的快速权重状态，从而改进了该框架，但其无界旧状态乘数在长序列机制中可能会发散。我们提出了一种有界旧态调制规则，该规则应用符号保留Quantum Fast-Weight Programmers (QFWPs) store temporal information in dynamically programmed variational-circuit parameters rather than in nonlinear recurrent hidden states, offering a practical route to quantum sequence modeling. Self-Modulating QFWP improves this framework by using input-dependent gates for both new fast-weight updates and the accumulated fast-weight state, but its unbounded old-state multiplier can diverge in long-sequence regimes. We propose a bounded old-state modulation rule that applies a sign-preservingtanh\tanh仅对循环内存分支进行门控，同时保持附加更新和新更新调制不变。我们在两个 CUDA-Q 量子动力学预测任务和米兰 SMS 电信活动预测上评估标准 QFWP、完整自调制 QFWP、Only-New 和 Only-Old 变体。量子动力学结果表明，旧状态调制是相对于标准 QFWP 最一致的改进来源，并且限制旧状态门消除了长序列发散，同时提高了总体鲁棒性。在 Milan SMS 预测中，原始的无界自调节 QFWP 在测试的网格中收敛，并在较长的输入窗口中显示出最明显的增益，其行为接近仅旧消融。这些发现将累积记忆调制确定为自调节 QFWP 的关键机制，并将有界旧态门控确定为有针对性的稳定策略。gate only to the recurrent memory branch while leaving the additive update and new-update modulation unchanged. We evaluate standard QFWP, full Self-Modulating QFWP, Only-New, and Only-Old variants on two CUDA-Q quantum-dynamics forecasting tasks and on Milan SMS telecommunication activity prediction. The quantum-dynamics results show that old-state modulation is the most consistent source of improvement over Standard QFWP, and that bounding the old-state gate removes long-sequence divergence while improving aggregate robustness. On Milan SMS forecasting, the original unbounded Self-Modulating QFWP converges across the tested grid and shows its clearest gains at longer input windows, with behavior close to the Only-Old ablation. These findings identify accumulated-memory modulation as the key mechanism of Self-Modulating QFWP and bounded old-state gating as a targeted stabilization strategy.

1 简介Introduction

量子序列模型已成为利用混合量子经典架构学习时间依赖性的一个有前途的方向Quantum sequence models have become a promising direction for learning temporal dependencies with hybrid quantum–classical architectures[6, 1, 3]。一个有代表性的起点是量子长短期记忆（QLSTM）模型，它用变分量子电路取代了部分经典的 LSTM 单元，并在时间学习任务上表现出了有希望的经验行为. A representative starting point is the Quantum Long Short-Term Memory (QLSTM) model, which replaces parts of the classical LSTM cell with variational quantum circuits and has shown promising empirical behavior on temporal learning tasks[9, 7, 31, 10]。这些研究表明，变分量子电路可以充当时态数据的紧凑非线性处理器，但循环量子模型在训练过程中仍然继承了一个中心限制。具有非线性递归的模型必须通过时间有序的非线性状态演化来传播梯度。. These studies suggest that variational quantum circuits can act as compact nonlinear processors for temporal data, but recurrent quantum models still inherit a central limitation during training. Models with nonlinear recurrence must propagate gradients through a time-ordered nonlinear state evolution.

引入量子快速权重编程器 (QFWP) 来解决这一瓶颈，方法是将内存从循环隐藏状态移动到动态编程的电路参数Quantum Fast Weight Programmers (QFWPs) were introduced to address this bottleneck by moving memory from a recurrent hidden state into dynamically programmed circuit parameters[8]。在 QFWP 中，经典的慢速程序员读取每个输入并生成对变分量子电路参数的更新，该电路充当快速程序员。由于时间状态由累积的快速权重而不是非线性循环隐藏状态表示，因此该模型避免了量子循环单元上的时间反向传播 (BPTT)，并允许更简单、更可并行的梯度路径. In QFWP, a classical slow programmer reads each input and generates an update to the parameters of a variational quantum circuit, which acts as the fast programmer. Because the temporal state is represented by accumulated fast weights rather than by a nonlinear recurrent hidden state, the model avoids backpropagation through time (BPTT) across a quantum recurrent cell and admits a simpler, more parallelizable gradient path[24]。这使得 QFWP 成为量子时间序列预测和顺序控制的一个有吸引力的框架，其中电路评估成本和梯度深度是主要的实际限制。. This makes QFWP an attractive framework for quantum time-series prediction and sequential control, where circuit-evaluation cost and gradient depth are major practical constraints.

最近的自调制 QFWP 通过在新生成的快速权重更新和先前累积的快速权重状态上引入依赖于输入的乘法调制来扩展此框架The recent Self-Modulating QFWP extends this framework by introducing input-dependent multiplicative modulation over both the newly generated fast-weight update and the previously accumulated fast-weight state[11]。此更新规则使模型能够直接控制如何注入新信息以及如何保留、抑制、放大或反转过去的快速权重记忆。先前的结果表明，这种自调制提高了收敛性和预测精度，而旧状态分支通常提供了主要的改进来源. This update rule gives the model direct control over how new information is injected and how past fast-weight memory is retained, suppressed, amplified, or sign-reversed. Prior results indicate that such self-modulation improves convergence and prediction accuracy, with the old-state branch often providing the dominant source of improvement[11]。然而，由于原始的旧状态乘法器不受约束，重复的乘法更新在长序列状态下可能会变得不稳定。 在这项工作中，我们在两个互补的下一步预测设置上重新审视自调制 QFWP：电信流量预测和量子动力学预测。前者在实际时间信号上测试模型，而后者评估自适应快速权重存储器是否可以学习模拟量子系统生成的可观测值。我们在隐藏尺寸和输入窗口长度上比较标准 QFWP、全自调制 QFWP 以及仅新和仅旧的自调制消融，并且我们使用相对改进、旧状态强度和协同诊断来识别哪个调制分支驱动观察到的增益。. However, because the original old-state multiplier is unconstrained, repeated multiplicative updates can become unstable in long-sequence regimes. In this work, we revisit Self-Modulating QFWP on two complementary next-step prediction settings: telecommunication traffic prediction and quantum-dynamics prediction. The former tests the model on practical temporal signals, while the latter evaluates whether adaptive fast-weight memory can learn observables generated by simulated quantum systems. We compare Standard QFWP, full Self-Modulating QFWP, and the Only-New and Only-Old Self-Modulating ablations across hidden sizes and input-window lengths, and we use relative-improvement, old-state strength, and synergy diagnostics to identify which modulation branch drives the observed gains.

我们的主要架构贡献是有界的旧状态调制规则。具体来说，我们应用了符号保留Our main architectural contribution is a bounded old-state modulation rule. Specifically, we apply a sign-preservingtanh\tanh绑定到旧状态乘法门，同时保持加法更新和新更新调制不变。这将稳定机制与循环快速权重内存分支隔离。根据经验，有界门保留了旧状态调制的低误差行为，同时解决了在无界乘法变体中观察到的长序列发散。由此产生的分析支持一个重点结论：累积快速权重的输入相关控制是自调制 QFWP 优势的核心，而限制该控制为稳定的量子快速权重编程提供了一种简单有效的途径。bound to the old-state multiplicative gate while leaving the additive update and new-update modulation unchanged. This isolates the stabilization mechanism to the recurrent fast-weight memory branch. Empirically, the bounded gate preserves the low-error behavior of old-state modulation while curing the long-sequence divergence observed in unbounded multiplicative variants. The resulting analysis supports a focused conclusion: input-dependent control of accumulated fast weights is central to the benefit of Self-Modulating QFWP, and bounding this control provides a simple and effective route to stable quantum fast-weight programming.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 量子和量子启发的序列模型Quantum and quantum-inspired sequential models

早期的 QRNN 和 QLSTM 研究表明 变分量子电路（VQC）可以处理时间数据和序列识别任务Early QRNN and QLSTM studies showed that variational quantum circuits (VQCs) can process temporal data and sequence-recognition tasks[3, 9]。 最近的工作还研究了基于 VQC 的基于量子启发的柯尔莫哥洛夫-阿诺德网络 (QKAN) 的 LSTM 模型和通过单量子位数据重新上传电路实现的变压器模型，以实现可扩展且高效的量子启发序列学习. Recent works also investigate VQC-instantiated quantum-inspired Kolmogorov–Arnold network (QKAN)-based LSTM models and transformer models implemented by single-qubit data re-uploading circuits for scalable yet efficient quantum-inspired sequence learning[16, 14, 22]。 此后，应用范围已扩展到环境和能源预测，包括气候预测. Applications have since expanded to environmental and energy forecasting, including climate[12], 空气质量, air-quality[20], 太阳能, solar-power[17]、洪水预报, and flood prediction[21];经济和基础设施预测，包括碳价格; economic and infrastructure forecasting, including carbon prices[4], 股票指数, stock indices[28]，以及城市电信流量, and urban telecommunication traffic[6, 14];以及科学或生物医学序列建模，包括药物发现; and scientific or biomedical sequence modeling, including drug discovery[31], 预测性维护, predictive maintenance[30]、人类活动识别, human activity recognition[13]和可穿戴设备健康评估, and wearable-health estimation[29]。这些研究证明了量子顺序建模的广度，但循环量子单元仍然跨时间耦合计算，因此保留了随时间顺序反向传播的成本。. These studies demonstrate the breadth of quantum sequential modeling, but recurrent quantum cells still couple computation across time and therefore retain the cost of sequential backpropagation through time.

2.2 快速体重程序员Fast weight programmers

快速权重程序员 (FWP) 对内存采取不同的看法：慢速网络将上下文相关参数写入快速网络，因此时间信息存储在快速权重动态中，而不是非线性隐藏状态循环中Fast Weight Programmers (FWPs) take a different view of memory: a slow network writes context-dependent parameters into a fast network, so temporal information is stored in fast-weight dynamics rather than in a nonlinear hidden-state recurrence[27]。这个经典的想法后来与线性自注意力相关，并通过循环快速权重变体进行扩展. This classical idea was later connected to linear self-attention and extended through recurrent fast-weight variants[26, 15]。 QFWP 通过使用经典的慢速编程器来更新变分量子电路的参数，将 FWP 原理转移到混合量子学习中，并演示了时间序列预测和强化学习. QFWP transfers the FWP principle to hybrid quantum learning by using a classical slow programmer to update the parameters of a variational quantum circuit, with demonstrations on time-series prediction and reinforcement learning[8, 5]。最近的扩展包括用于参数高效电路编程的 Quantum-Train QFWP. Recent extensions include Quantum-Train QFWP for parameter-efficient circuit programming[23]和受量子启发的门控 QKAN-FWP，用于可扩展序列学习、长视太阳周期预测、MiniGrid 强化学习and quantum-inspired Gated QKAN-FWP for scalable sequence learning, long-horizon solar-cycle prediction, MiniGrid reinforcement learning[24]，以及交通矩阵预测, and traffic matrix forecasting[25]。先前的自调制 QFWP 在新的快速权重更新和累积的快速权重状态上进一步引入了输入相关门. The prior Self-Modulating QFWP further introduces input-dependent gates on both the new fast-weight update and the accumulated fast-weight state[11];我们的工作保留了这种内存控制思想，但限制了旧状态乘法器以消除长序列分歧。; our work preserves this memory-control idea but bounds the old-state multiplier to remove long-sequence divergence.

4 量子动力学预测任务Quantum-Dynamics Prediction Tasks

我们评估了使用 CUDA-Q Dynamics（CUDA-Q 的动力学模拟后端）生成的两个量子动力学基准测试We evaluate two quantum-dynamics benchmarks generated with CUDA-Q Dynamics, the dynamics-simulation backend of CUDA-Q[18]。在这两种情况下，我们从模拟的量子轨迹中提取单个标量可观测值，并将其视为单变量下一步预测任务。每个轨迹包含 3000 个均匀采样的时间步长，最小-最大归一化为. Although this benchmark is derived from a telecommunication forecasting task, the SMS activity sequences are real-world urban time series with noise, irregular fluctuations, and heterogeneous temporal patterns, making them useful for evaluating the potential applicability of self-modulating sequence models to other noisy real-world forecasting scenarios.

This ablation result is consistent with our previous observations: the main advantage of self-modulation comes from modulating the memory-bearing existing QFWP parameters, rather than merely adding a new modulated parameter branch. These numerical results therefore support the view that retaining and modulating internal memory is critical for exploiting long-range temporal structure in the Milan SMS forecasting task.

最后，我们注意到米兰 SMS 实验使用了原始的无界自调节 QFWP 及其消融，而不是有界的旧修改。这一选择反映了该基准在我们研究中的作用。 SMS 任务包含大量独立训练的时间序列，并且所有测试的配置都完成了训练，而不需要有界的旧状态门。因此，我们使用这个基准主要是为了测试在量子动力学实验中观察到的自调节记忆机制是否也转移到嘈杂的现实世界城市时间序列。结果支持了这种解释：全自调节 QFWP 的增益成为最大 在较长的输入窗口中保持一致，并且其行为仍然接近“仅旧”消融，而“仅新”在长序列范围内较弱。因此，SMS 结果提供了实际证据，表明调制累积的快速权重状态除了模拟量子动力学之外还有用。同时，由于没有在这个更大的电信网格上详尽地评估有界变体，因此我们并不认为有界门对于所有现实世界的预测设置都是不必要的。相反，有界操作应被视为无界旧状态乘法表现出发散的制度的稳定机制，而 SMS 基准表明原始自调节机制在当前电信预测协议下已经可以有效且稳定。Finally, we note that the Milan SMS experiment uses the original unbounded Self-Modulating QFWP and its ablations, rather than the bounded-old modification. This choice reflects the role of this benchmark in our study. The SMS task contains a much larger number of independently trained time series, and all tested configurations completed training without requiring the bounded old-state gate. Therefore, we use this benchmark primarily to test whether the self-modulating memory mechanism observed in the quantum-dynamics experiments also transfers to noisy real-world urban time series. The results support this interpretation: the gains of full Self-Modulating QFWP become most consistent at longer input windows, and its behavior remains close to the Only-Old ablation, whereas Only-New is weaker in the long-sequence regime. Thus, the SMS results provide practical evidence that modulating the accumulated fast-weight state is useful beyond simulated quantum dynamics. At the same time, because bounded variants were not exhaustively evaluated on this larger telecommunication grid, we do not claim that the bounded gate is unnecessary for all real-world forecasting settings. Rather, the bounded operation should be viewed as a stability mechanism for regimes where unbounded old-state multiplication exhibits divergence, while the SMS benchmark demonstrates that the original self-modulating mechanism can already be effective and stable under the present telecommunication forecasting protocol.
