我们注意到这些结果的一个潜在局限性：我们的排除标准排除了无法解决基本分数问题的参与者，但没有考虑到在人工智能条件下同样无法通过人工智能提交正确答案的参与者。这带来了一个潜在的混乱，因为人工智能条件可能会选择性地保留能力较低的参与者，从而扩大绩效差距。我们在实验 2 中直接解决了这个困惑。We note one potential limitation of these results: our exclusion criteria removed participants unable to solve basic fraction problems but didn’t account for participants in the AI condition who were similarly unable yet submitted correct answers via AI. This introduced a potential confound in that the AI condition could have selectively retained lower-ability participants, inflating the performance gap. We address this confound directly in Experiment 2.

在实验 2 中，我们复制了实验 1，并进行了两项关键的方法改进。首先，我们添加了简单的一步分数问题的预测试，并使用预测试性能来排除，而不是实验中的性能，解决了上述的技能水平混淆问题。其次，我们为控制参与者配备了一个显示预测试解决方案的侧边栏——信息已经看到，因为在两种情况下每个预测试问题后都会显示解决方案——以消除人工智能侧边栏出现然后突然删除而引入的界面不对称性（见图 1）。In Experiment 2, we conducted a replication of Experiment 1 with two key methodological improvements. 认知卸载。Cognitive offloading.认知卸载Cognitive offloading(Risko & Gilbert, 2016)是使用身体动作（例如写笔记、输入计算器、使用搜索引擎）来改变任务的信息处理要求并减少认知需求is the use of physical action (e.g. writing notes, typing in a calculator, using a search engine) to alter the information processing requirements of a task and reduce cognitive demand(Goldin-Meadow et al., 2001; Martin & Schwartz, 2005; Gilbert et al., 2020)。然而，当认知辅助不可用时，这种任务绩效的提高也可能导致绩效下降. However, this improved task performance also risks decline in performance when the cognitive aid is not available(Sparrow et al., 2011; Grinschgl et al., 2021; Richmond & Taylor, 2025)。 人工智能几乎在每个推理领域都加速了这种动态. AI accelerates this dynamic across virtually every reasoning domain(Kosmyna et al., 2025; Shen & Tamkin, 2026)，引起过度依赖的担忧, raising concerns of overreliance(Ibrahim et al., 2025; Kim et al., 2025)和去技能化and deskilling(Budzyń et al., 2025; Shen & Tamkin, 2026)。然而，现有证据在很大程度上是通过调查或访谈得出的相关性. Existing evidence, however, is largely correlational through surveys or interviews(Gerlich, 2025; Lee et al., 2025)。我们通过一系列大规模随机对照试验提供因果证据来解决这一局限性。. We address this limitation by providing causal evidence through a series of large-scale RCTs.

人机协作。Human-AI collaboration.越来越多的工作考虑如何设计优化长期结果而不是短期偏好满足的人类人工智能系统A growing line of work considers how to design human-AI systems that optimize for long-term outcomes rather than short-term preference satisfaction(Sumers et al., 2022; Collins et al., 2024; Sucholutsky et al., 2025; Steyvers & Mayer, 2025; Zhao & Hawkins, 2025; Cheng et al., 2026)。短期优化通常会导致人工智能系统的奖励与用户的需求不一致. Short-term optimization often produces misalignment between what AI systems are rewarded for and what users need(Christian et al., 2026)，引起阿谀奉承, giving rise to sycophancy(Cheng et al., 2025a; b; Rathje et al., 2025), 操纵, manipulation(Williams et al., 2024)，以及欺骗, and deception(Zhou et al., 2025)。我们认为，认知去技能化代表了这种失调的一个未被充分认识的例子：为立即提供帮助而优化的人工智能系统可能会损害他们旨在帮助的人的长期能力。. We argue that cognitive deskilling represents an underappreciated instance of this misalignment: AI systems optimized for immediate helpfulness may undermine the long-term capabilities of the people they aim to assist.

渐进的人工智能风险。Gradual AI risks.人工智能安全研究传统上侧重于先进人工智能系统引起的突发、可怕事件AI safety research has traditionally focused on abrupt, dire events caused by advanced AI systems(Bucknall & Dori-Hacohen, 2022; Carlsmith, 2022; Bengio et al., 2024)。相反，越来越多的文献考虑人工智能风险显现的可能性. A growing literature instead considers the possibility of AI risks manifesting渐渐地gradually通过人类偏好和依赖人工智能的社会系统之间一系列不断增加的目标不一致through an incremental series of misalignment in objectives between human preferences and AI-dependent societal systems(Kasirzadeh, 2025; Kulveit et al., 2025)。然而，关于人工智能风险增量的“温水煮青蛙”场景将如何发挥作用，特别是在个人层面，经验证据有限. However, there is limited empirical evidence on how this “boiling frog” scenario of incremental AI risks will play out, especially at the individual level(Ibrahim et al., 2025)。尽管相关工作已经探索了人工智能的使用来减少人际关系中人类代理的潜力. Although related work has explored the potential of AI usage to reduce human agency in interpersonal relationships(Kirk et al., 2025a; Sharma et al., 2026)、创意输出, creative output(Padmakumar & He, 2023; Doshi & Hauser, 2024)，以及个人信仰, and personal beliefs(Jakesch et al., 2023; Williams et al., 2024; Guingrich et al., 2026)，之前没有研究研究人工智能的使用如何侵蚀认知过程并破坏自主思维。我们的工作直接解决了这一差距，为“逐渐剥夺权力”的假设提供了认知证据。持续的人工智能使用可能会侵蚀基本的认知和动机能力，从而随着时间的推移取代人类的参与。, no prior work has examined how AI use erodes cognitive processes and undermines autonomous thought. Our work addresses this gap directly, providing cognitive evidence for a “gradual disempowerment” hypothesis. Sustained AI use risks eroding essential cognitive and motivational capacities, and as a consequence, displacing human participation over time.

6 结论Conclusion

人类认知始终受到外部工具的影响，从计算器到互联网再到 GPS 导航。然而，当前的人工智能系统代表了一种新型的认知支架：它可以解决任何问题，很少拒绝提供帮助，并立即提供答案。在这里，我们表明，仅仅 10-15 分钟的人工智能交互就会导致独立表现和持久性的严重损害，而这些能力是终身学习的基础。如果短暂的接触会产生可测量的侵蚀，那么日常人工智能使用数月或数年的累积影响可能是深远的且难以逆转。Human cognition has always been shaped by external tools, from calculators to internet to GPS navigation. Current AI systems, however, represent a new kind of cognitive scaffold: one that solves anything, rarely refuses to help, and delivers answers instantly. Here, we show that just 10–15 minutes of AI interaction can result in significant impairments in independent performance and persistence – capacities that are foundational to life-long learning. If brief exposure produces measurable erosion, the cumulative effects of daily AI use over months or years may be profound and difficult to reverse.

有两种机制可以解释观察到的持久性下降。首先，当人工智能通常在几秒钟内完成任务时，任务多长时间的参考点Two mechanisms may explain the observed decline in persistence. First, when AI routinely completes tasks in seconds, the reference point for how long a task应该should采取可以改变——因此，独立工作开始感觉反事实地更加费力，这个过程在结构上类似于享乐适应take can shift – and as a consequence, unaided work starts to feel counterfactually more effortful, a process structurally analogous to hedonic adaptation(Brickman, 1971; Brickman et al., 1978; Frederick & Loewenstein, 1999)。至关重要的是，这种机制是自我强化的：每次卸载行为都会改变参考点，增加独立努力的主观成本，并使未来的卸载更具吸引力。其次，人工智能消除了人们的生产性斗争，通过这种斗争，人们不仅发展准确的知识，而且发展准确的知识。. Crucially, this mechanism is self-reinforcing: each act of offloading shifts the reference point, increases the subjective cost of unaided effort, and makes future offloading more attractive. Second, AI removes the productive struggle through which people develop not only accurate knowledge but accurate自我self-知识。如果没有独立工作的机会，人们永远不会了解自己的能力，从而破坏了维持毅力的元认知校准-knowledge. Without opportunities to work independently, people never learn what they are capable of, undermining the metacognitive calibration that sustains persistence(Yeung & Summerfield, 2012; Fleming & Daw, 2017; Dubey et al., 2021; Elizondo et al., 2024)。这些机制共同预测了更广泛的元认知衰退，而不仅仅是持久性——未来的工作应该在自然主义、纵向环境中研究这一方向。. Together, these mechanisms predict broader metacognitive decay beyond persistence alone – a direction future work should examine in naturalistic, longitudinal settings.

我们的结果具有重要的政策意义。这里研究的任务，例如分数算术和阅读理解，似乎可以委托给计算器等工具，但对这些技能的概念性掌握是一项艰巨的任务。Our results carry important policy implications. The tasks investigated here, such as fraction arithmetic and reading comprehension, may seem delegable to tools like calculators, but conceptual mastery of these skills is a发展性的developmental前提条件。如果没有这些技能，代数或批判性推理等高阶能力仍然无法获得。如果持续的人工智能使用削弱了推动长期学习的动力和毅力，这些影响将随着时间的推移不断积累，当它们显现出来时，它们将很难逆转。这类似于“温水煮青蛙”效应，每次增量行动都感觉没有成本，直到累积效应变得难以解决prerequisite. Without these skills, higher-order competencies like algebra or critical reasoning remain inaccessible. If sustained AI use erodes the motivation and persistence that drive long-term learning, these effects will accumulate over years, and by the time they are visible, they will be difficult to reverse. This is analogous to the “boiling frog” effect, where each incremental act feels costless, until the cumulative effect becomes overwhelming to address(Moore et al., 2019; Kasirzadeh, 2025; Liu et al., 2025)。这些风险的分布也并不均匀：学术资源较少的学生可能最容易受到影响。虽然面向用户的干预措施（例如，苏格拉底式人工智能、减少使用时间等）可能会有所帮助，但我们认为此类解决方案只能起到“创可贴”的作用，而无法解决更深层次的问题，因为人工智能提供了大规模卸载的诱惑。减轻这些风险需要重新思考人工智能系统如何与人们长期合作，并扩大短期用户满意度之外的目标. These risks are also not equally distributed: students with fewer academic resources may be most vulnerable. While user-facing interventions (e.g., Socratic AI, reduced use time, etc.), might help at the margins, we believe that such solutions will only serve as “band-aids” and will not resolve the deeper issue, since AI offers a temptation to offload at scale. Mitigating these risks requires rethinking how AI systems collaborate with people long-term and broadening objectives beyond short-term user satisfaction(Zhi-Xuan et al., 2025; Kirk et al., 2025b)追求赋权和关怀的精神toward an ethos of empowerment and care(Kleiman-Weiner, 2024; Christian, 2025)。我们希望我们的工作能够激发该领域思考优化而不仅仅是人们可以做的事情. We hope that our work inspires the field to think about optimizing not just what people can do与with人工智能，但他们能做什么AI, but what they can do没有without它。it.
