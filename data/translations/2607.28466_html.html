来自 280,000 份常规报告的基于报告的结肠镜检查视觉语言基础模型A report-grounded vision–language foundation model for colonoscopy from 280,000 routine reports

摘要Abstract

尽管常规报告中记录了丰富的专家描述，但视觉语言模型在结肠镜检查中仍未得到充分利用。这些报告记录了病变的外观、大小和位置，但总结了整个过程，而不是为各个帧添加标题，使得临床发现与相应图像的联系很弱。在这里，我们开发了 EndoCLIP，这是一种结肠镜检查视觉语言基础模型，经过从 280,476 条常规结肠镜检查记录中逐步恢复的 125,756 个病变级图像-文本对进行训练。在病变级图像文本检索、结构化报告生成和六个多中心临床分类任务中，EndoCLIP 在零样本和线性探针设置中均优于通用和生物医学视觉语言编码器。在良性与恶性分类方面，其线性探针在一项涉及 12 名内窥镜医师的双盲研究中接近专家读者的表现。这些结果表明，恢复发现与框架的对应关系可以将常规文档转化为可扩展的监督，从而使临床目标能够用语言指定，而不是为每个任务单独注释。Vision–language models remain underused in colonoscopy despite the rich expert descriptions recorded in routine reports. These reports document lesion appearance, size and location but summarise entire procedures rather than caption individual frames, leaving clinical findings only weakly linked to the corresponding images. Here we develop EndoCLIP, a colonoscopy vision–language foundation model trained on 125,756 lesion-level image–text pairs progressively recovered from 280,476 routine colonoscopy records. Across lesion-level image–text retrieval, structured report generation and six multi-centre clinical classification tasks, EndoCLIP outperforms general-purpose and biomedical vision–language encoders in both zero-shot and linear-probe settings. On benign-versus-malignant classification, its linear probe approaches the performance of expert readers in a blinded study involving 12 endoscopists. These results suggest that recovering finding-to-frame correspondence can transform routine documentation into scalable supervision, enabling clinical targets to be specified in language rather than separately annotated for each task.

1 简介Introduction

结肠镜检查对于预防结直肠癌至关重要，结直肠癌是全球癌症相关死亡的主要原因Colonoscopy is central to the prevention of colorectal cancer, a leading cause of cancer-related mortality worldwide[bray2024global, winawer1993prevention, zauber2012colonoscopic, bretthauer2022nordicc]。人工智能辅助系统改进了腺瘤检测并支持结直肠病变的自动检测和分类. AI-assisted systems have improved adenoma detection and supported the automated detection and classification of colorectal lesions[urban2018deep, ahmad2019artificial, wang2019real, repici2020efficacy]。然而，大多数此类系统依赖于使用密集注释和精心策划的图像进行训练的特定任务模型，这很难在常规结肠镜检查中遇到的各种发现中进行扩展。它们在患者群体、成像系统和临床中心中的稳健性和普遍性也仍然令人担忧. However, most such systems rely on task-specific models trained with densely annotated and carefully curated images, which is difficult to scale across the diverse findings encountered in routine colonoscopy. Their robustness and generalisability across patient populations, imaging systems and clinical centres also remain concerns[parasa2023framework, boers2024foundation, 11080481].

自监督视觉预训练可以减少对手动注释的依赖Self-supervised visual pretraining can reduce this dependence on manual annotation[zhou2023retfound]。视觉基础模型，例如 GastroNet. Visual foundation models such as GastroNet[boers2024foundation]和EndoFMand EndoFM[wang2023endofm]从大量未标记的内窥镜框架和视频中学习可转移的视觉特征。这些功能与临床语言无关，因此不直接支持文本引导检索或基于提示的识别。视觉-语言预训练将图像与文本对齐，从而允许用自然语言指定临床概念learn transferable visual features from large collections of unlabelled endoscopy frames and videos. These features are not linked to clinical language and therefore do not directly support text-guided retrieval or prompt-based recognition. Vision–language pretraining instead aligns images with text, allowing clinical concepts to be specified in natural language[radford2021learning, jia2021scaling, cherti2023reproducible]。在放射学和病理学中，这种方法在零样本和标签高效识别方面取得了成功. In radiology and pathology, this approach has been successful in zero-shot and label-efficient recognition[zhang2022convirt, huang2021gloria, tiu2022chexzero, huang2023plip, lu2024conch, hamamci2026ctclip]，而在结肠镜检查中，它仍然较少被探索, while in colonoscopy it remains less explored[wang2025endoked]。常规结肠镜检查报告描述病变形态、表面外观、大小和解剖位置，但总结检查结果而不是为各个帧添加标题。一个程序可能包含数十个静止帧，其中许多没有清晰的病变视图，而一份报告可能描述了多个病变，但没有将结果与帧联系起来。因此，报告和视觉记录仅粗略对齐，尤其是在多病灶手术中（图 1）。. Routine colonoscopy reports describe lesion morphology, surface appearance, size and anatomical location but summarise the examination rather than caption individual frames. A procedure may contain dozens of still frames, many without a clear lesion view, and one report may describe several lesions without linking findings to frames. The report and visual record are therefore only coarsely aligned, especially in multi-lesion procedures (Fig.1).

在这里，我们介绍 EndoCLIP，这是一种视觉语言基础模型，可通过病例级证据定位、单病灶锚点选择和多病灶消歧来恢复发现与框架的对应关系。该方法应用于 280,476 份常规报告，生成 125,756 个病变级图像文本对，用于对比预训练（图 1）。Here we introduce EndoCLIP, a vision–language foundation model that recovers finding-to-frame correspondence through case-level evidence localisation, single-lesion anchor selection and multi-lesion disambiguation. Applied to 280,476 routine reports, this approach yields 125,756 lesion-level image–text pairs for contrastive pretraining (Fig.1）。我们还推出了EndoReport100). We also introduce EndoReport100[li2026endoreport100]，临床医生策划的基准，包含 100 个多病变病例和 7,002 个帧级注释，用于评估病变级检索。 EndoCLIP 实现了 14.3% 的双向全局 Recall@1，而最强的比较器为 2.8%。此外，我们还在多中心环境中验证了 EndoCLIP 在六项临床分类任务和结构化报告生成上的性能, a clinician-curated benchmark of 100 multi-lesion cases and 7,002 frame-level annotations for evaluating lesion-level retrieval. EndoCLIP achieves a bidirectional global Recall@1 of 14.3%, compared with 2.8% for the strongest comparator. Furthermore, we validated the performance of EndoCLIP on six clinical classification tasks and structured report generation in a multi-centre setting[fu2026endovl]。在比较的视觉语言编码器中，其冻结功能实现了最高的零样本和标签效率分类性能，并产生最准确的结构化输出。这些结果表明，恢复发现与框架的对应关系可以将常规报告转变为临床相关内窥镜任务的可扩展监督，而很少或没有特定于任务的注释。. Its frozen features achieve the highest zero-shot and label-efficient classification performance among the compared vision–language encoders and yield the most accurate structured outputs. These results suggest that recovering finding-to-frame correspondence can turn routine reports into scalable supervision for clinically relevant endoscopy tasks with little or no task-specific annotation.

2.1 EndoCLIP 概述Overview of EndoCLIP

EndoCLIP 从结肠镜检查期间生成的常规自由文本报告中学习。由于每份报告都记录了整个检查而不是单个帧，因此其发现语句没有明确链接到显示相应病变的帧（图 1）。EndoCLIP learns from routine free-text reports generated during colonoscopy. Because each report documents an entire examination rather than individual frames, its finding sentences are not explicitly linked to the frames that show the corresponding lesions (Fig.1）。为了恢复这种对应关系，我们使用了一个三阶段渐进训练程序，该程序定位病例级视觉证据，从单病变病例中选择锚点，并消除多病变报告中发现与帧匹配的歧义（图 1）。). To recover this correspondence, we used a three-stage progressive training procedure that localises case-level visual evidence, selects anchors from single-lesion cases and disambiguates finding-to-frame matches in multi-lesion reports (Fig.2;方法，第二节。; Methods, Sec.4.4.2）。该过程应用于 280,476 份报告及其静止帧，生成 125,756 个病变级图像-文本对，用于对比预训练（补充表 S1）。使用粗略病例级提示（“存在息肉”或“未发现”）的对比训练产生 EndoCLIP-I，它可以定位包含息肉的帧。对保留的高分帧进行再训练会产生 EndoCLIP-II，它可以过滤无信息的背景。使用 EndoCLIP-II 将单个病变病例中的查找句子与框架进行匹配，生成 EndoCLIP-III，这是编码描述性病变语义的第一个检查点。使用 EndoCLIP-III 消除多病变病例的歧义并组合所有恢复的对，然后生成 EndoCLIP，下面评估了完整的模型；中间检查点仅用于分阶段分析。). Applied to 280,476 reports and their still frames, this procedure yields 125,756 lesion-level image–text pairs for contrastive pretraining (Supplementary Table S1). Contrastive training with coarse case-level prompts (“polyp present” or “no finding”) produces EndoCLIP-I, which localises polyp-containing frames. Retraining on the retained high-scoring frames yields EndoCLIP-II, which filters uninformative background. Matching finding sentences to frames in single-lesion cases with EndoCLIP-II produces EndoCLIP-III, the first checkpoint to encode descriptive lesion semantics. Disambiguating multi-lesion cases with EndoCLIP-III and combining all recovered pairs then produces EndoCLIP, the completed model evaluated below; the intermediate checkpoints are used only in stage-wise analyses.

2.3 多中心零样本和标签高效识别Multi-centre zero-shot and label-efficient recognition

我们通过六项临床任务评估 EndoCLIP，这些任务结合了多中心外部基准和独立病理学队列。五项任务来自 EndoVLWe evaluate EndoCLIP across six clinical tasks that combine a multi-centre external benchmark with an independent pathology cohort. Five tasks come from EndoVL[fu2026endovl]，涵盖九个公共源数据集：四级巴黎形态、粘膜充血、分叶表面、绒毛表面和息肉大小。第六是中山医院队列的良性与恶性分类。这些目标具有直接的临床意义：巴黎类型和表面图案告知侵袭风险和切除计划, which spans nine public source datasets: four-class Paris morphology, mucosal congestion, lobulated surface, villous surface and polyp size. 3 讨论Discussion

视觉语言预训练在胃肠内窥镜检查中仍然不常见。大多数内窥镜基础模型从图像或视频中学习，并根据预定义的视觉任务进行评估Vision–language pretraining remains uncommon in gastrointestinal endoscopy. Most endoscopy foundation models learn from images or videos and are evaluated on predefined visual tasks[wang2023endofm, boers2024foundation]。相比之下，在放射学和病理学中，配对临床文本支持检索、零样本识别和语言生成. In radiology and pathology, by contrast, paired clinical text supports retrieval, zero-shot recognition and language generation[lu2024conch, hamamci2026ctclip]。 EndoCLIP 通过共同学习常规报告和程序框架，将这种范例扩展到结肠镜检查。生成的表示支持从临床描述中检索、提示定义的识别、适应本地标签和结构化报告。因此，临床语言可以提供跨任务的通用界面，否则需要单独的注释和输出头。. EndoCLIP extends this paradigm to colonoscopy by learning jointly from routine reports and procedure frames. The resulting representation supports retrieval from clinical descriptions, prompt-defined recognition, adaptation to local labels and structured reporting. Clinical language can therefore provide a common interface across tasks that otherwise need separate annotations and output heads.

然而，常规报告不提供标准 CLIP 培训中使用的图像标题对Routine reports, however, do not provide the image–caption pairs used in standard CLIP training[radford2021learning, zhang2025biomedclip, lin2023pmcclip]。文档的单位是过程，而跨模式学习所需的单位是病变级别的框架-句子对。一份报告可以总结许多帧和多个病变，因此直接病例级配对存在将发现句子与另一个病变或正常粘膜相关联的风险。 EndoCLIP 从本地化相关证据的案例级提示开始，逐步缩小这种多对多的模糊性。然后，单病变报告为多病变病例中的句子到框架消歧提供低歧义的描述性锚点。该过程将案例级弱监督转换为跨模式训练的查找到框架对应关系。. The unit of documentation is the procedure, whereas the desired unit of cross-modal learning is a lesion-level frame–sentence pair. A report can summarise many frames and several lesions, so direct case-level pairing risks associating a finding sentence with another lesion or with normal mucosa. EndoCLIP narrows this many-to-many ambiguity progressively, beginning with case-level prompts that localise relevant evidence. Single-lesion reports then provide low-ambiguity descriptive anchors for sentence-to-frame disambiguation in multi-lesion cases. This procedure converts case-level weak supervision into finding-to-frame correspondences for cross-modal training.

检查点序列将粗息肉检测与基于报告的病变检索分开。 EndoCLIP-I 和 EndoCLIP-II 的帧级 AUROC 分别为 0.843 和 0.889。尽管如此，他们的全局描述性 Recall@1 仍然是 1.2% 和 0.9%，并且他们的案例内 Recall@1 仍然处于 46.3% 的机会水平。对单病灶锚点的描述性训练将全球范围内的这些值提高到 9.2%，在 EndoCLIP-III 的病例中提高到 75.3%。添加消除歧义的多病灶对进一步将其提高至 14.3% 和 81.2%，分别提高 5.1 和 5.8 个百分点。阶段式分类显示，当 5% 的标签可用时，形态丰富的任务的后期收益最大（补充表 S11）。这些结果将粗略的帧级病变检测与描述性病变检索分开，描述性病变检索是在病变级句子帧对训练后出现的。The checkpoint sequence separates coarse polyp detection from report-grounded lesion retrieval. EndoCLIP-I and EndoCLIP-II reach frame-level AUROCs of 0.843 and 0.889. Despite this, their global descriptive Recall@1 remains 1.2% and 0.9%, and their intra-case Recall@1 remains at the 46.3% chance level. Descriptive training on single-lesion anchors raises these values to 9.2% globally and 75.3% within cases for EndoCLIP-III. Adding disambiguated multi-lesion pairs further raises them to 14.3% and 81.2%, gains of 5.1 and 5.8 percentage points, respectively. Stage-wise classification shows the largest later-stage gains on morphology-rich tasks when 5% of labels are available (Supplementary Table S11). These results separate coarse frame-level lesion detection from descriptive lesion retrieval, which emerges after training on lesion-level sentence–frame pairs.

共享图像-文本空间还改变了下游任务的指定方式。仅图像编码器通常需要为每个新目标标记示例和特定于任务的分类器。 EndoCLIP 可以直接对临床提示进行评分，同时在本地标签可用时保留线性探针或端到端适应的选项。它在所有六个分类任务上的比较编码器中实现了最高的零样本 AUC-ROC，并且在四个外部形态和表面任务上超过最强比较器 0.176-0.230。这些目标包括巴黎形态、表面外观和病变大小，为侵袭风险、切除策略和监测提供信息The shared image–text space also changes how a downstream task is specified. An image-only encoder generally requires labelled examples and a task-specific classifier for each new target. EndoCLIP can instead score clinical prompts directly, while retaining the option of a linear probe or end-to-end adaptation when local labels are available. It achieves the highest zero-shot AUC-ROC among the compared encoders on all six classification tasks and exceeds the strongest comparator by 0.176–0.230 across the four external morphology and surface tasks. These targets include Paris morphology, surface appearance and lesion size, which inform invasion risk, resection strategy and surveillance[paris2003endoscopic, hayashi2013nice, gupta2020recommendations]。这种能力可能与罕见或新定义的表型有关，这些表型很难组装大型标记群体. This capability may be relevant to rare or newly defined phenotypes for which large labelled cohorts are difficult to assemble[lu2024conch, hamamci2026ctclip].

结构化报告的生成提出了更严格的测试，因为临床有用的报告必须反映图像，而不仅仅是听起来合理。先前的多中心研究已经开发了基于图像的结构化报告，并前瞻性评估了用于上消化道诊断和报告的特定领域多模态模型Structured report generation poses a stricter test because a clinically useful report must reflect the image rather than merely sound plausible. Previous multi-centre studies have developed image-based structured reporting and prospectively evaluated domain-specific multimodal models for upper-gastrointestinal diagnosis and reporting[qu2020reportgeneration, jiang2026reportangel]。 GI-Bench 表明，通用多模态语言模型的可读输出可能仍包含本地化错误、事实不准确和幻觉视觉结果. GI-Bench shows that readable outputs from general-purpose multimodal language models may still contain localisation errors, factual inaccuracies and hallucinated visual findings[zhu2026gibench]。在解码器冻结的情况下，EndoCLIP 会在比较的编码器中产生最准确的描述符集、Paris 类型和病变大小估计。这些结果突出了内窥镜特定图像文本数据和视觉基础编码器的价值，超出了语言模型流畅性的范围，并支持内窥镜审查的结构化草案作为内窥镜特定视觉语言模型的临床相关用途。. With the decoder frozen, EndoCLIP yields the most accurate descriptor sets, Paris types and lesion-size estimates among the encoders compared. These results highlight the value of endoscopy-specific image–text data and visually grounded encoders beyond language-model fluency alone, and support structured drafts for endoscopist review as a clinically relevant use of endoscopy-specific vision–language models.

Language-based retrieval could support case review and teaching by locating lesions from textual descriptions, extending the image-query paradigm used in EndoFinder[Yan_EndoFinder_MICCAI2024]。当注释有限时，基于提示的识别和轻量级本地适应可以支持质量保证，而结构化输出可以减少重复的文档。 EndoReport100 为在多病变程序中开发帧级检测和基于报告的检索提供了基准。对 EndoVL 和独立中山医院病理队列的评估提供了跨预训练语料库之外的异构数据源转移的证据.
