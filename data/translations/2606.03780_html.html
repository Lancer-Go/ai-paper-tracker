摘要Abstract

事实回忆的因果追踪主要在密集变压器语言模型中进行研究，其中干预措施将信息流定位到层或前馈模块。 稀疏专家混合 (MoE) 语言模型引入了一个更尖锐的问题：当事实预测由路由 MoE 块调节时，哪个路由专家贡献很重要？ 我们为稀疏 MoE 语言模型制定了专家感知的因果追踪。使用Causal tracing of factual recall has been studied predominantly in dense transformer language models, where interventions localize information flow to layers or feed-forward modules. Sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE) language models introduce a sharper question: when a factual prediction is mediated by a routed MoE block, which routed expert contributions matter? We formulate expert-aware causal tracing for sparse MoE language models. Using反事实CounterFact事实上，我们首先通过向主题标记嵌入添加噪声来破坏模型的事实偏好，然后测试干净的 MoE 块输出或干净的专家级更新是否恢复了 true-vs-foil logit 对比。对于facts, we first corrupt the model’s factual preference by adding noise to subject-token embeddings, and then test whether clean MoE-block outputs or clean expert-level updates restore the true-vs-foil logit contrast. ForQwen3-30B-A3B-底座Qwen3-30B-A3B-Base，层扫描选择并验证第 44 层，专家级跟踪将 L44E069 识别为在干净运行中重复选择的专家，其保留的补丁优于其他活动的同层专家补丁。对于, a layer sweep selects and validates layer 44, and expert-level tracing identifies L44E069 as an expert repeatedly selected in the clean run whose held-out patch outperforms other active same-layer expert patches. ForMixtral-8x7B-v0.1，层级跟踪验证了中间层信号，但该信号未本地化到所选的单例专家；相反，联盟检查通过路由多专家更新来恢复它。这些结果表明，MoE 事实追踪可以具有专家意识，同时也表明专家级本地化是模型和协议相关的，而不是通用的。, layer-level tracing validates a mid-layer signal, but the signal is not localized to the selected singleton expert; a coalition check instead recovers it with routed multi-expert updates. These results suggest that MoE factual tracing can be made expert-aware, while also showing that expert-level localization is model- and protocol-dependent rather than universal.

1 简介Introduction

事实回忆是语言模型因果分析的常见设置：给出如下提示：Factual recall is a common setting for causal analysis of language models: given a prompt such as法国的首都是The capital of France is，模型应该为正确的对象分配高概率，, the model should assign high probability to the correct object,巴黎Paris。因果追踪和激活修补通过破坏预测来研究这种行为，然后从干净运行中恢复内部组件以测试哪些组件介导恢复. Causal tracing and activation patching study this behavior by corrupting a prediction and then restoring internal components from a clean run to test which components mediate the recovery(Meng et al., 2022; Geva et al., 2023; Vig et al., 2020; Wang et al., 2023)。在密集变压器中，这些干预措施通常针对残差流激活、注意力输出或前馈模块。. In dense transformers, these interventions often target residual-stream activations, attention outputs, or feed-forward modules.

稀疏专家混合 (MoE) 语言模型使本地化问题更加细粒度。令牌不是由单个密集前馈模块处理，而是由一小部分专家路由处理Sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE) language models make this localization problem more fine-grained. A token is not processed by a single dense feed-forward module, but by a small routed subset of experts(Shazeer et al., 2017; Fedus et al., 2022; Jiang et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2025)。因此，层级补丁可以表明 MoE 块很重要，但修补的块是路由专家更新的聚合。它没有回答哪些选定的专家贡献支持事实预测。本文提出：. Thus a layer-level patch can show that an MoE block matters, but the patched block is an aggregate over routed expert updates. It does not answer which selected expert contributions support the factual prediction. This paper asks:事实回忆因果追踪能否在稀疏 MoE 语言模型中实现专家感知？Can factual-recall causal tracing be made expert-aware in sparse MoE language models?

我们使用过滤的单标记在受控诊断环境中研究这个问题We study this question in a controlled diagnostic setting using filtered single-token反事实CounterFact Meng et al. (2022)对比。对于每个事实，输入是主语关系前缀，并且宾语不包含在提示中。我们将事实偏好作为真实与箔片下一个标记的逻辑差异来衡量，通过向主题标记嵌入添加噪声来破坏运行，并询问干净的内部组件是否可以恢复偏好。我们的协议有两个阶段：首先，我们将干净的最终位置 MoE 块输出修补到噪声运行中，并在发现案例上扫描 MoE 层；其次，在选定的层，我们通过消融差异来估计路由专家贡献，并将专家级干净减噪声更新修补到噪声运行中。我们将选定的专家与同一层的其他清洁活跃专家进行比较。contrasts. For each fact, the input is a subject–relation prefix and the object is not included in the prompt. We measure factual preference as a true-vs-foil next-token logit difference, corrupt the run by adding noise to subject-token embeddings, and ask whether clean internal components restore the preference. Our protocol has two stages: first, we patch clean final-position MoE-block outputs into the noised run and sweep MoE layers on discovery cases; second, at the selected layer, we estimate routed expert contributions by ablation difference and patch an expert-level clean-minus-noised update into the noised run. We compare selected experts against other clean-active experts from the same layer.

主要结果是两个 MoE 主干网不对称。开The main result is asymmetric across two MoE backbones. OnQwen3-30B-A3B-底座Qwen3-30B-A3B-Base，层扫描选择层 44 并在保留时验证它, the layer sweep selects layer 44 and validates it on held-out反事实CounterFact案例；然后，专家级跟踪选择 L44E069，它对保留的案例进行验证，并优于其他活跃的同层专家。开cases; expert-level tracing then selects L44E069, which validates on held-out cases and outperforms other active same-layer experts. OnMixtral-8x7B-v0.1，层级跟踪选择了中间层站点，第 19 层，这也验证了，但所选的单例专家 L19E006 的性能低于主动控件。联盟检查（修补干净路由的前 2 位专家或清洁路由和噪声路由专家的联合）恢复 Mixtral 层级信号，这表明层级事实恢复可以集中在循环专家中，也可以保持分布在路由专家联盟中。, layer-level tracing selects a mid-layer site, layer 19, which also validates, but the selected singleton expert L19E006 underperforms active controls. A coalition check (patching either the clean-routed top-2 experts or the union of clean- and noised-routed experts) recovers the Mixtral layer-level signal, suggesting that layer-level factual recovery can either concentrate in a recurrent expert or remain distributed across a routed expert coalition.

总之，我们贡献了一个两阶段的 MoE 因果追踪方案和一项受控的双模型研究。主要的实证见解是，层级事实恢复和单专家定位可以分开：Qwen3 承认强大的单专家干预，而 Mixtral 经验证的层级信号可以通过路由专家联盟更好地解释。In summary, we contribute a two-stage MoE causal-tracing protocol and a controlled two-model study. The main empirical insight is that layer-level factual recovery and single-expert localization can come apart: Qwen3 admits a robust single-expert intervention, whereas Mixtral’s validated layer-level signal is better explained by routed expert coalitions.

2 相关工作Related Work

事实回忆和因果追踪。Factual recall and causal tracing.

语言模型可以通过完形填空式的提示来表达事实关联Language models can express factual associations through cloze-style prompts(Petroni et al., 2019)。先前的工作将事实知识与前馈记忆、知识神经元和因果追踪干预联系起来. Prior work links factual knowledge to feed-forward memories, knowledge neurons, and causal tracing interventions(Geva et al., 2021; Dai et al., 2022; Meng et al., 2022; Geva et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025)。 在因果追踪和激活修补的基础上，我们使用前向传递干预来测试干净的内部组件是否可以恢复损坏的真实与箔纸事实偏好。. Building on causal tracing and activation patching, we use forward-pass interventions to test whether clean internal components can restore a corrupted true-vs-foil factual preference.

稀疏 MoE 模型和可解释性。Sparse MoE models and interpretability.

稀疏 MoE 语言模型将每个标记路由到一小部分专家，增加参数数量，同时保持活动计算稀疏Sparse MoE language models route each token to a small subset of experts, increasing parameter count while keeping active computation sparse(Shazeer et al., 2017; Lepikhin et al., 2021; Fedus et al., 2022; Du et al., 2022; Jiang et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2025)。最近的工作研究路由模式、专家专业化和教育部知识归因. Recent work studies routing patterns, expert specialization, and MoE knowledge attribution(Fayyaz et al., 2026; Bandarkar et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026; Li et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2026)。由于所选专家和路由权重取决于输入，因此我们将 MoE 块输出修补与每个专家贡献修补区分开来。. Because the selected experts and routing weights are input-dependent, we distinguish MoE-block output patching from per-expert contribution patching.

6 讨论Discussion

区块级救援并非专家级证据。Block-level rescue is not expert-level evidence.

主要的方法论教训是，MoE 事实追踪不应停留在层级激活修补上。干净的 MoE 块输出可以挽救有噪声的事实提示，但这只能将信号定位到聚合路由块。由于块输出由选定的专家更新组成，因此专家感知跟踪会询问哪些路由贡献支持恢复。路由器选择可以识别候选者，但选择本身并不是因果关系；因果证据来自修补估计的专家更新并将其与主动控制进行比较。The main methodological lesson is that MoE factual tracing should not stop at layer-level activation patching. A clean MoE-block output can rescue a noised factual prompt, but this only localizes the signal to an aggregate routed block. Since the block output is composed of selected expert updates, expert-aware tracing asks which routed contributions support the recovery. Router selection identifies candidates, but selection alone is not a causal claim; causal evidence comes from patching the estimated expert update and comparing it with active controls.

Mixtral 体现了单一专家本地化的局限性。Mixtral exemplifies the limits of single-expert localization.

在Qwen3中，选定的层和选定的专家都进行验证，多个控件支持L44E069。在 Mixtral 中，层级跟踪转移到中间层带，但所选专家的性能低于主动控制。联盟检查表明了原因：干净的 top-2 和干净/噪音路由联盟专家联盟恢复了接近 L19 MoE 块效应的救援（附录In Qwen3, the selected layer and selected expert both validate, with multiple controls supporting L44E069. In Mixtral, layer-level tracing transfers to a mid-layer band, but the selected expert underperforms active controls. A coalition check suggests why: clean top-2 and clean/noised routing-union expert coalitions recover rescue close to the L19 MoE-block effect (AppendixG）。我们将这些发现视为特定于模型的结果，因为 Qwen3 和 Mixtral 在分词器、训练、路由设计和保留案例方面有所不同。). We treat these findings as model-specific, since Qwen3 and Mixtral differ in tokenizer, training, routing design, and retained cases.

范围是受控诊断。The scope is a controlled diagnostic.

我们并不声称存在通用事实专家或跨模型专家同源性。由于分词器和主题噪声滤波器是特定于模型的，因此我们将 Qwen3 和 Mixtral 结果视为两个受控案例，而不是体系结构级别的比较。宽松过滤器放大保留了相同的定性模式，但设置仍然是过滤的、单标记的最终位置诊断。We do not claim a universal factual expert or cross-model expert homology. Because tokenizer and subject-noise filters are model-specific, we treat the Qwen3 and Mixtral results as two controlled cases rather than an architecture-level comparison. The relaxed-filter scale-up preserves the same qualitative pattern, but the setting remains a filtered, single-token, final-position diagnostic.

7 结论Conclusion

我们为稀疏 MoE 事实回忆诊断引入了专家感知的因果追踪。 Qwen3 显示了一个分层站点，可以进一步本地化为强大的路由专家贡献 L44E069。 Mixtral 显示了不同的模式：层级救援传输，但通过路由专家联盟比通过选定的单个专家更好地恢复信号。这些结果表明，MoE 事实追踪应区分聚合 MoE 块救援、单专家定位和多专家路由恢复。We introduced expert-aware causal tracing for sparse MoE factual-recall diagnostics. Qwen3 shows a layer-level site that can be further localized to a robust routed expert contribution, L44E069. Mixtral shows a different pattern: layer-level rescue transfers, but the signal is better recovered by routed expert coalitions than by the selected single expert. These results suggest that MoE factual tracing should distinguish aggregate MoE-block rescue, single-expert localization, and multi-expert routed recovery.
