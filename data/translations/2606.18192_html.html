摘要Abstract

随着高质量的公共网络语料库日益枯竭，干净的长上下文文档已成为大型语言模型 (LLM) 的稀缺且昂贵的训练数据源。现有的长上下文语料库通常是专有的且获取成本高昂、综合生成或集中在编程等狭窄领域。我们引入了斯坦福 EDGAR 文件数据集 (SEFD)，它是 SEC 文件的开放式重构，可重构为布局忠实的 MultiMarkdown，用于金融语言建模和评估。 SEFD 使经过审计的财务报表、风险披露、所有权报告、会计附注和市场动态事件归档可用作长上下文预训练数据以及财务推理、预测、合规性和文档理解的基础。生成的语料库具有令牌效率、模型就绪性，并且与 Common Crawl 派生语料库的重叠率小于 0.1%。我们发布了 SEFD-v1，这是一个 152B 代币的初始公开快照，并提供了对估计为 550B 代币的更大的 1850 万个归档档案的语料库级别分析。我们进一步介绍了两个源自 SEFD 的基准：EDGAR-Forecast，它评估模型知识截止后基于归档的数值预测；EDGAR-OCR，它评估复杂财务表的转录。As high-quality public web corpora become increasingly exhausted, clean long-context documents have become a scarce and expensive source of training data for large language models (LLMs). Existing long-context corpora are often proprietary and costly to acquire, synthetically generated, or concentrated in narrow domains such as programming. We introduce the Stanford EDGAR Filings Dataset (SEFD), an open reconstruction of SEC filings into layout-faithful MultiMarkdown for financial language modeling and evaluation. SEFD makes audited financial statements, risk disclosures, ownership reports, accounting notes, and market-moving event filings usable as long-context pretraining data and as a basis for financial reasoning, forecasting, compliance, and document understanding. The resulting corpus is token-efficient, model-ready, and has less than 0.1% overlap with Common Crawl-derived corpora. We release SEFD-v1, a 152B-token initial public snapshot, and provide corpus-level analyses of a larger 18.5M-filing archive estimated at 550B tokens. We further introduce two SEFD-derived benchmarks: EDGAR-Forecast, which evaluates filing-grounded numerical forecasting after model knowledge cutoffs, and EDGAR-OCR, which evaluates transcription of complex financial tables.

2 相关工作Related Work

大规模预训练语料库。Large-scale pretraining corpora.

大多数大型开放式预训练语料库都是由 Common Crawl 等异构网络源组装而成，包括 C4、RefinedWeb 和 FineWeb 等变体Most large open pretraining corpora are assembled from heterogeneous web sources such as Common Crawl, including variants such as C4, RefinedWeb, and FineWeb[8, 9, 12, 13]。这些数据集提供了规模，但需要积极的过滤来删除垃圾邮件、样板文件、重复和不连贯的文本。它们通常还会扁平化文档结构，放弃表格、缩进和视觉层次结构等布局线索。尽管 SEC EDGAR 数据库完全公开，但其文件提出了不同类型的构建挑战 - 有价值的财务内容嵌入在嘈杂的 HTML、遗留格式约定和布局繁重的结构中，这些结构不太适合通过标准文本提取管道捕获。. These datasets provide scale, but require aggressive filtering to remove spam, boilerplate, duplication, and incoherent text. They also typically flatten document structure, discarding layout cues such as tables, indentation, and visual hierarchy. Although the SEC EDGAR Database is entirely public, its filings pose a different kind of construction challenge – valuable financial content is embedded in noisy HTML, legacy formatting conventions, and layout-heavy structures that are poorly suited to be captured by standard text extraction pipelines.

EDGAR 文件的很大一部分包含以 PDF 或类似图像的形式进入档案的表格，而不是干净的 HTML。因此，我们根据 SEFD 所需的特定任务评估 OCR 系统 - 将视觉呈现的 SEC 表转换为忠实的 HTML 样式表转录，保留单元格位置、行和列结构以及源表包含的内联格式。附录中显示了复杂 OCR 输入的示例A nontrivial fraction of EDGAR filings contain tables that enter the archive as PDFs or image-like exhibits rather than clean HTML. We therefore evaluate OCR systems on the specific task needed by SEFD – converting visually rendered SEC tables into faithful HTML-style table transcriptions, preserving cell placement, row and column structure, and inline formatting where the source table contains it. Example complex OCR inputs are shown in Appendix8.4.1.

基准建设。Benchmark Construction.

我们根据原始 SEC 文件中的 300 个手工选择的表格构建了 EDGAR-OCR 基准。不是在原始归档表上评估模型，而是将每个选定的表转换为综合基准实例，以减少训练测试污染并防止模型通过记忆或外部查找来恢复答案。该管道将​​源表解析为 MMD，去除实体名称和值的标识，并使用 GPT-5.4 (xhigh) 从表中提取行级变量和关系。然后，这些变量用于替换数字条目，同时保留本地算术。生成的合成表被渲染回归档样式的 HTML、屏幕截图并用作 OCR 输入，而目标转录是在渲染之前从同一合成表生成的。We constructed an EDGAR-OCR benchmark from 300 hand-selected tables from raw SEC filings. Rather than evaluating models on the original filing tables, each selected table is converted into a synthetic benchmark instance to reduce train-test contamination and prevent models from recovering answers through memorization or external lookup. The pipeline parses the source table into MMD, deidentifies entity names and values, and uses GPT-5.4 (xhigh) to extract row-level variables and relationships from the table. These variables are then used to replace numeric entries while preserving local arithmetic. The resulting synthetic table is rendered back into filing-style HTML, screenshotted, and used as the OCR input, while the target transcription is generated from the same synthetic table before rendering.

备用正确表。Alternate Correct Tables.

一些 SEC 表允许多个有效转录，特别是当单元格边界不直接可见或嵌套多行标题支持多个合理结构时。我们避免用多重答案真值集来惩罚正确的选择；在 300 个样本中，241 个样本至少有一个备用有效表，总共 782 个备用转录。每个模型输出都会根据主要转录和所有替代转录进行评分，保持最高分数。Some SEC tables admit multiple valid transcriptions, especially when cell boundaries are not directly visible or nested multi-row headers support more than one reasonable structure. We avoid penalizing correct alternatives with multiple-answer truth sets; across 300 samples, 241 have at least one alternate valid table, with 782 alternate transcriptions total. Each model output is scored against the primary transcription and all alternates, keeping the highest score.

得分。Scoring.

预测表被解析为单元格网格，并与地面实况网格进行比较。对于每个非空真实单元格，当单元格内容、内联格式签名和网格放置全部匹配时，预测将获得 1.0 分。当文本正确但内联格式不同时，它会获得 0.5 分；当正确的内容出现在预测表中但在错误的单元格中时，它会获得 0.25 分。我们将调整后的召回分数以及内联格式保存和中值延迟报告为主要基准指标。Predicted tables are parsed into cell grids and compared against the ground-truth grid. For each nonempty ground-truth cell, a prediction receives 1.0 point when the cell content, inline formatting signature, and grid placement all match. It receives 0.5 points when the text is correct but the inline formatting differs, and 0.25 points when the correct content appears in the predicted table but in the wrong cell. We report this adjusted-recall score as the main benchmark metric, along with inline formatting preservation and median latency.

6 EDGAR-预测EDGAR-Forecast

EDGAR-Forecast 评估模型是否可以使用历史 SEC 归档来预测稍后隐藏归档中出现的数值。每个基准实例都是围绕一家目标为 2026 年 10 季度申报的公司构建的。该模型可以访问公司之前的申请历史，但目标申请本身是隐藏的。任务是从隐藏的文件中预测五个数值，例如收入、费用、数量、百分比、每股数量或经常性财务和运营指标。EDGAR-Forecast evaluates whether models can use historical SEC filings to forecast numeric values that appear in a later hidden filing. Each benchmark instance is built around a company with a target 2026 10-Q filing. The model is given access to the company’s prior filing history, but the target filing itself is hidden. The task is to predict five numeric values from the hidden filing, such as revenues, expenses, volumes, percentages, per-share quantities, or recurring financial and operating metrics.

7 结论Conclusion

我们引入了 SEFD，这是一种将 SEC EDGAR 存档忠实布局地重建为令牌高效的 MultiMarkdown 的方法。 SEFD 将异构纯文本、HTML、XML、SGML 和 PDF 文件转换为标准化表示形式，保留在纯文本提取中经常丢失的财务表格、缩进、文档层次结构和视觉结构。由此产生的语料库提供了一个开源的长上下文财务文档，与 Common Crawl 派生的语料库重叠度较低，并且相当于大约 1.3B 页的重构归档内容。We introduce SEFD, a layout-faithful reconstruction of the SEC EDGAR archive into token-efficient MultiMarkdown. SEFD converts heterogeneous plaintext, HTML, XML, SGML, and PDF filings into a standardized representation that preserves financial tables, indentation, document hierarchy, and visual structure often lost in text-only extraction. The resulting corpus provides an open source of long-context financial documents with low overlap against Common Crawl-derived corpora and is comparable to roughly 1.3B pages of reconstructed filing content.

我们的分析表明，SEFD 的训练信号在文件类型、源格式和长度方面非常不均匀。高频形式不一定是高标记形式，长申请的一小部分贡献了大部分语料库质量。这种异质性使得档案对于语言建模很有价值，但也难以重建。 SEFD 的特定格式解析管道大规模解决了这一挑战，保留了机器可读的披露信息和可视化表达的财务结构。Our analysis shows that SEFD’s training signal is highly uneven across filing type, source format, and length. High-frequency forms are not necessarily high-token forms, and a small tail of long filings contributes most corpus mass. This heterogeneity makes the archive valuable for language modeling, but also difficult to reconstruct. SEFD’s format-specific parsing pipeline addresses this challenge at scale, preserving both machine-readable disclosures and visually expressed financial structure.

最后，EDGAR-OCR 和 EDGAR-Forecast 表明，高保真归档重建支持超越面向检索的搜索的评估。 EDGAR-OCR 测试根据视觉输入转录复杂的财务表格，而 EDGAR-Forecast 测试前沿模型是否可以使用长期历史归档上下文进行基于归档的数字预测。语料库和基准展示了 SEFD 作为金融语言建模、长上下文预训练、金融推理、文档理解和评估的实用资源。Finally, EDGAR-OCR and EDGAR-Forecast show that high-fidelity filing reconstruction supports evaluation beyond retrieval-oriented search. EDGAR-OCR tests transcription of complex financial tables from visual inputs, while EDGAR-Forecast tests whether frontier models can use long historical filing context for filing-grounded numerical forecasts. The corpus and benchmarks showcase SEFD as a practical resource for financial language modeling, long-context pretraining, financial reasoning, document understanding, and evaluation.

9 局限性Limitations

SEFD 目前不解析嵌入图像，PDF 派生内容取决于 OCR 质量。解析器覆盖范围可能会滞后于新引入或更改的 SEC 归档模式，特别是对于罕见的 XML 分支或非典型条件路径。与标准 HTML、XML、SGML 或纯文本文件相比，布局极其繁重的文件仍然会产生保真度较低的重建。SEFD does not currently parse embedded images, and PDF-derived content depends on OCR quality. Parser coverage may lag newly introduced or changed SEC filing schemas, especially for rare XML branches or atypical conditional paths. Extremely layout-heavy filings can still produce lower-fidelity reconstructions than standard HTML, XML, SGML, or plaintext filings.
