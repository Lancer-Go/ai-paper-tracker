可观察的性能并不能完全反映系统组织：Observable Performance Does Not Fully Reflect System Organization:
咬合约束下步态动力学的多层次分析A Multi-Level Analysis of Gait Dynamics Under Occlusal Constraint

Jacques Raynal1, Pierre Slangen2, Jacques Margerit3
1Laboratory of Bioengineering and Nanosciences (LBN), University of Montpellier, France
2EuroMov Digital Health in Motion, University of Montpellier, IMT Mines Ales, France
3Emeritus Professor, University of Montpellier, France
Corresponding author: raynal.cab@gmail.com
摘要Abstract

在生物力学系统中，可观察的性能通常用作底层系统组织的代理。然而，这种假设隐含地假定了输出度量和内部系统状态之间的对应关系，而这在自适应系统中可能不成立。 在本研究中，遮挡的垂直维度（VDO）被认为是应用于自适应神经力学系统的约束，从而能够探索受控变化下的系统级响应。帕金森病患者的单病例设计允许对重复条件进行个体内分析。 该分析跨三个互补的层次进行构建：（i）描述可观察性能的聚合线性指标，（ii）描述状态空间中的时间组织的动态系统框架，以及（iii）通过无监督嵌入获得的潜在空间表示。 结果表明，具有可比较的可观察性能的条件可能对应于状态空间和潜在空间表示中的不同组织。这种分离凸显了聚合指标的局限性，并表明相似的输出可能来自不等效的系统状态。 第四层被提议作为纯粹概念性的扩展，描述系统状态之间的潜在关系。该级别尚未实现，也不是从实验数据得出的。In biomechanical systems, observable performance is often used as a proxy for underlying system organization. However, this assumption implicitly presumes a correspondence between output metrics and internal system states that may not hold in adaptive systems. In this study, the vertical dimension of occlusion (VDO) is considered as a constraint applied to an adaptive neuromechanical system, enabling the exploration of system-level responses under controlled variations. A single-case design in a patient with Parkinson’s disease allows an intra-individual analysis across repeated conditions. The analysis is structured across three complementary levels: (i) aggregated linear metrics describing observable performance, (ii) a dynamical systems framework describing temporal organization in state space, and (iii) a latent space representation obtained through unsupervised embedding. The results show that conditions with comparable observable performance may correspond to different organizations in both state space and latent space representations. This dissociation highlights a limitation of aggregated metrics and suggests that similar outputs may arise from non-equivalent system states. A fourth level is proposed as a purely conceptual extension describing potential relationships between system states. This level is not implemented and is not derived from experimental data.

这些观察结果是严格的探索性和非因果性的。所提出的框架不建立机械的、预测的或方向的关系，而是提供一种结构化方法来分析跨多个表示级别的约束驱动系统。These observations are strictly exploratory and non-causal. The proposed framework does not establish mechanistic, predictive, or directional relationships, but provides a structured approach for analyzing constraint-driven systems across multiple levels of representation.

1 简介Introduction

在生物力学系统中，可观察的性能指标通常用于表征功能行为。这种方法隐含地假设可测量的输出提供了有关系统底层组织的足够信息。然而，在适应性生物系统中，这种对应关系可能不成立：不同的内部系统状态可能会产生类似的可观察性能。 在系统行为由约束和自适应机制之间的相互作用产生的情况下，这种限制变得尤其重要。在这种情况下，单个最佳参数的识别可能不足以描述系统组织。相反，系统行为必须被视为强加的约束与系统的适应能力之间相互作用的结果。 闭塞垂直尺寸 (VDO) 提供了此类约束的临床相关示例。传统上将其描述为需要恢复的可测量的解剖学价值，但通常在基于形态、美学或机械参考的静态范式中进行处理。虽然这些方法保留了临床实用性，但它们的局限性在复杂的康复中变得明显，尽管解剖结构重建明显可以接受，但在复杂的康复中可能会出现功能不稳定。 越来越多的观察结果表明，VDO 不能减少到特定于患者的固定内在值In biomechanical systems, observable performance metrics are commonly used to characterize functional behavior. This approach implicitly assumes that measurable outputs provide sufficient information about the underlying organization of the system. However, in adaptive biological systems, this correspondence may not hold: similar observable performance may arise from different internal system states. This limitation becomes particularly relevant in contexts where system behavior emerges from interactions between constraints and adaptive mechanisms. In such cases, the identification of a single optimal parameter may not adequately describe system organization. Instead, system behavior must be considered as the result of an interaction between imposed constraints and the adaptive capacity of the system. The vertical dimension of occlusion (VDO) provides a clinically relevant example of such a constraint. Traditionally described as a measurable anatomical value to be restored, it has often been approached within a static paradigm based on morphological, esthetic, or mechanical references. While these approaches retain clinical utility, their limitations become apparent in complex rehabilitations, where functional instability may occur despite apparently acceptable anatomical reconstruction. A growing body of observations suggests that VDO cannot be reduced to a fixed intrinsic value specific to the patient[1, 2, 3]。相反，它可以被理解为应用于自适应神经力学系统的约束，随着时间的推移参与感觉运动过程。从这个角度来看，临床问题从确定最佳值转向描述给定约束下的系统行为。 这种重新解释与动力系统理论的框架一致，其中全局行为是由系统组件和约束之间的相互作用产生的. Instead, it may be understood as a constraint applied to an adaptive neuromechanical system, engaging sensorimotor processes over time. Within this perspective, the clinical question shifts from identifying an optimal value to characterizing system behavior under a given constraint. This reinterpretation is consistent with frameworks from dynamical systems theory, in which global behavior emerges from interactions between system components and constraints[4, 5, 6]。在这个框架内，闭塞不被认为是纯粹的局部机械现象，而是作为集成到感觉运动调节中的神经感觉界面的一部分，特别是通过三叉神经传入. Within this framework, occlusion is not considered as a purely local mechanical phenomenon, but as part of a neurosensory interface integrated into sensorimotor regulation, notably through trigeminal afferents[7, 8, 9]。尽管咬合、姿势和运动之间的相互作用仍然存在争议，但现有证据表明咬合状况可能与整体神经肌肉组织的调节有关. Although interactions between occlusion, posture, and locomotion remain debated, available evidence suggests that occlusal conditions may be associated with modulations of global neuromuscular organization[10, 11, 12]。 可穿戴技术的最新进展使得能够在生态条件下获取步态数据。时空参数、压力动力学中心和姿势指数提供了系统行为的可测量描述符. Recent advances in wearable technologies enable the acquisition of gait data under ecological conditions. Spatiotemporal parameters, center of pressure dynamics, and postural indices provide measurable descriptors of system behavior[13, 14, 15]。然而，这些聚合指标并不能捕获系统的完整组织，特别是其时间结构和系统状态之间的关系。 为了解决这个限制，我们提出了一个多层次的分析框架。第一级由描述可观察性能的聚合指标组成。第二个层次引入了动态系统视角，其中步态被解释为状态空间内系统的演化。第三层依赖于通过无监督嵌入获得的潜在空间表示，从而能够识别高维数据中的结构. However, these aggregated metrics do not capture the full organization of the system, particularly its temporal structure and the relationships between system states. To address this limitation, we propose a multi-level analytical framework. The first level consists of aggregated metrics describing observable performance. The second level introduces a dynamical systems perspective, in which gait is interpreted as the evolution of a system within a state space. The third level relies on latent space representation obtained through unsupervised embedding, enabling the identification of structures within high-dimensional data[16, 17, 18]。 本研究的目的不是确定最佳 VDO，而是探索咬合约束如何与跨多个表示级别的系统组织变化相关联，并检查类似的可观察性能是否可能对应于不同的系统组织。 这项工作旨在作为一种方法和概念框架来构建约束驱动的生物系统的分析，而不是作为预测、验证或临床规范模型。. The objective of this study is not to determine an optimal VDO, but to explore how an occlusal constraint is associated with variations in system organization across multiple levels of representation, and to examine whether similar observable performance may correspond to distinct system organizations. This work is intended as a methodological and conceptual framework to structure the analysis of constraint-driven biological systems, rather than as a predictive, validated, or clinically prescriptive model.

2 材料与方法Materials and Methods

2.1 研究设计Study Design

本研究的主要目的是描述受约束的系统级组织的特征，而不是建立临床方案。它基于单例实验设计，可对 VDO 的受控变化对步态和姿势调节的影响进行个体内分析This study aims primarily to characterize system-level organization under constraint, rather than to establish clinical protocols. It is based on a single-case experimental design, enabling an intra-individual analysis of the effects of controlled variations in VDO on gait and postural regulation[19, 20]。 两次实验间隔十一周进行：基线条件 (M1) 和第二次实验 (M2)，在两个时间点测试相同的咬合条件。在 M1 和 M2 之间，参与者保持自然咬合，并且没有引入受控咬合干预。 该研究是描述性和探索性的。它的目的并不是将观察到的影响隔离或归因于单一因果因素。. Two experimental sessions were conducted eleven weeks apart: a baseline condition (M1) and a second session (M2), with identical occlusal conditions tested at both time points. Between M1 and M2, the participant remained under natural occlusion, and no controlled occlusal intervention was introduced. The study is descriptive and exploratory. It does not aim to isolate or attribute observed effects to a single causal factor.

2.2 参加者Participant

参与者是一名72岁男性，被诊断患有帕金森病13年。步态是自主的，无需使用辅助设备。据报告，神经系统状况描述了所研究系统的临床背景，不用作解释变量。 在 M1 和 M2 之间，参与者每周接受一次结构化的心理辅导干预，包括 45 分钟的课程，旨在改善感觉运动调节。这种干预被报告为实验背景的一部分，但在研究设计中并未受到控制或隔离The participant was a 72-year-old male diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease for 13 years. Gait was autonomous, without the use of assistive devices. The neurological condition is reported to describe the clinical context of the system under study and is not used as an explanatory variable. Between M1 and M2, the participant underwent a structured weekly sophrocoaching intervention consisting of 45-minute sessions aimed at improving sensorimotor regulation. This intervention is reported as part of the experimental context but was not controlled or isolated within the study design[21].

评估了六种咬合状况：Six occlusal conditions were evaluated:

The expression of VDO variation in degrees requires methodological clarification, including the definition of the reference axis, the measurement protocol, and its correspondence to anatomical displacement.

2.4 步态采集Gait Acquisition

使用结合压力传感器和惯性测量单元的仪器鞋垫记录步态数据。这种可穿戴系统已用于生态步态分析，并在神经学和生物力学背景下显示出相关性Gait data were recorded using instrumented insoles combining pressure sensors and inertial measurement units. Such wearable systems have been used for ecological gait analysis and have shown relevance in neurological and biomechanical contexts[24, 25, 26].

该协议由具有线性前进和返回阶段的标准化步行路径组成。仅保留线性行走阶段进行分析。The protocol consisted of a standardized walking path with linear forward and return phases. Only linear walking phases were retained for analysis.

2.5 数据处理Data Processing

从 CSV 文件中提取数据并在 Python 环境中进行处理。预处理包括使用四分位数范围 (IQR) 方法进行离群值检测、用中值替换离群值以及最小-最大归一化。标准化程序在所有条件下以一致的方式应用，尽管其确切范围（每个会话、每个条件或全局）未指定并且可能影响纵向可比性。 在潜在空间转换之前不应用特征选择或聚合，确保在没有事先聚合的情况下对完整数据集执行第 3 级分析。这保留了数据的内在结构以进行无监督表示。Data were extracted from CSV files and processed in a Python environment. Preprocessing included outlier detection using the interquartile range (IQR) method, replacement of outliers by median values, and min–max normalization. The normalization procedure was applied in a consistent manner across all conditions, although its exact scope (per session, per condition, or global) is not specified and may affect longitudinal comparability. No feature selection or aggregation was applied prior to latent-space transformation, ensuring that Level 3 analysis was performed on the full dataset without prior aggregation. This preserves the intrinsic structure of the data for unsupervised representation.

2.6 分析框架Analytical Framework

该分析分为三个互补的层次，描述了不同规模的系统：可观察的性能、动态组织和潜在组织。The analysis was structured across three complementary levels describing the system at different scales: observable performance, dynamical organization, and latent organization.

These changes cannot be attributed to a specific causal factor, as multiple contextual elements were not isolated within the study design.

4 讨论Discussion

4.1 主要发现Main Finding

这项研究的中心发现是可观察性能与系统组织之间的分离：The central finding of this study is the dissociation between observable performance and system organization:

G​P​P​Si≈G​P​P​Sjwithzi​ and ​zj​ belonging to different regions of the latent representationGPPS_{i}\approx GPPS_{j}\quad\text{with}\quad z_{i}\text{ and }z_{j}\text{ belonging to different regions of the latent representation}

这一结果凸显了仅基于聚合指标的方法的根本局限性。虽然这些指标捕捉了全球趋势，但它们不提供对系统内部组织的访问。This result highlights a fundamental limitation of approaches based solely on aggregated metrics. While such metrics capture global trends, they do not provide access to the internal organization of the system.

4.2 对生物力学的影响Implications for Biomechanics

这些发现表明，无法使用标量性能指标来充分表征生物力学系统。相反，系统行为应该被理解为在约束下发展的组织。These findings suggest that biomechanical systems cannot be fully characterized using scalar performance indicators. Instead, system behavior should be understood as an organization evolving under constraints.

所提出的框架提供了补充描述：第 1 级描述可观察的性能，第 2 级描述时间组织，第 3 级描述潜在结构。The proposed framework provides complementary descriptions: Level 1 describes observable performance, Level 2 describes temporal organization, and Level 3 describes latent structure.

4.3 将 VDO 解释为约束参数Interpretation of VDO as a Constraint Parameter

在此框架内，VDO 不被解释为因果决定因素，而是被解释为与系统组织变化相关的约束参数。这种解释与生物系统中基于约束的模型相一致，其中多种功能状态可能在类似的可观察性能条件下共存。Within this framework, VDO is not interpreted as a causal determinant but as a constraint parameter associated with variations in system organization. This interpretation aligns with constraint-based models in biological systems, where multiple functional states may coexist under similar observable performance conditions.

4.4 潜在表征的贡献Contribution of Latent Representation

潜在空间分析基于完整数据集而不是聚合指标。因此，它提供了对线性表示未捕获的组织结构的访问。 然而，观察到的结构取决于算法选择，不应被解释为系统组织的独特或内在表示。 这种转换提供了系统组织的模型相关的紧凑表示，而无需假设预定义的生理模型。Latent space analysis is based on the full dataset rather than on aggregated metrics. It therefore provides access to organizational structures not captured by linear representations. However, the observed structures depend on algorithmic choices and should not be interpreted as unique or intrinsic representations of system organization. This transformation provides a model-dependent compact representation of system organization without assuming a predefined physiological model.

4.5 临床和概念意义Clinical and Conceptual Implications

从临床角度来看，结果表明具有相似可观察性能的条件可能对应于不同的系统组织。这意味着决策不能仅仅依赖于汇总指标。 本研究没有提供选择特定咬合配置的决策规则或阈值。相反，它凸显了单级评估的局限性。From a clinical perspective, the results suggest that conditions with similar observable performance may correspond to different system organizations. This implies that decision-making cannot rely solely on aggregated metrics. The present study does not provide a decision rule or threshold for selecting a specific occlusal configuration. Instead, it highlights the limits of single-level evaluation.

4.6 走向概念层面的国家关系Toward a Conceptual Level of State Relationships

在潜在空间中观察到的组织表明系统状态可能嵌入在结构化配置空间中。 因此，第四个分析级别可以被概念化为不同约束下系统状态之间潜在关系的表示。 然而，该水平并未在本研究中实施，不应被解释为转变或因果关系的模型。 相反，它代表了该框架的理论扩展，旨在构建对状态转换和系统动力学的未来研究。 目前的工作仍然是严格的描述性的，并且没有为状态之间的方向或机械转变提供证据。The organization observed in latent space suggests that system states may be embedded within a structured configuration space. A fourth analytical level can therefore be conceptualized as the representation of potential relationships between system states under varying constraints. However, this level is not implemented in the present study and should not be interpreted as a model of transitions or causal relationships. Instead, it represents a theoretical extension of the framework, aimed at structuring future investigations into state transitions and system dynamics. The present work remains strictly descriptive and does not provide evidence for directional or mechanistic transitions between states.

4.7 局限性Limitations

这项研究有几个局限性。首先，它基于单例设计，这排除了超出观察到的个体配置之外的泛化。其次，帕金森病的存在表征了特定的功能背景，但不用作解释变量。第三，潜在表示取决于投影方法，并且不提供系统的唯一描述。第四，不受控制的心理辅导干预的存在代表了一个可能有助于时间演变但不能孤立的背景因素。第五，概念性的 4 级表示与实验验证的转变并不对应，不应被解释为预测或机械模型。 最后，分析框架是探索性的，不支持因果推理。This study has several limitations. First, it is based on a single-case design, which precludes generalization beyond the observed individual configuration. Second, the presence of Parkinson’s disease characterizes the specific functional context but is not used as an explanatory variable. Third, latent representations depend on the projection method and do not provide a unique description of the system. Fourth, the presence of an uncontrolled sophrocoaching intervention represents a contextual factor that may contribute to temporal evolution but cannot be isolated. Fifth, the conceptual Level 4 representation does not correspond to experimentally validated transitions and should not be interpreted as a predictive or mechanistic model. Finally, the analytical framework is exploratory and does not support causal inference.

此外，缺乏对潜在结构的定量验证限制了嵌入空间中空间组织的可解释性。In addition, the absence of quantitative validation of latent structure limits the interpretability of spatial organization in the embedded space.

4.8 观点Perspectives

未来的工作应该将该框架扩展到更大的群体，并调查可观察的绩效和潜在组织之间是否可以在个体之间观察到类似的分离。 定量动态测量、纵向记录和替代嵌入方法的整合将进一步加强对系统状态之间转换的分析。 更广泛地说，该框架可能适用于其他约束驱动的生物系统，其中类似的输出可能来自不同的内部组织。Future work should extend this framework to larger cohorts and investigate whether similar dissociations between observable performance and latent organization can be observed across individuals. The integration of quantitative dynamical measures, longitudinal recordings, and alternative embedding methods would further strengthen the analysis of transitions between system states. More broadly, this framework may be applicable to other constraint-driven biological systems in which similar outputs may arise from distinct internal organizations.

5 结论Conclusion

这项研究表明，相似的可观察性能可能对应于不同的系统组织。因此，仅聚合指标不足以表征约束下的系统行为。 在此框架内，VDO 不应被视为固定的最优值，而应被视为与可访问系统状态的组织相关的约束。 所提出的多层次方法提供了一个结构化框架，用于分析复杂的生物力学系统，将可观察的性能、时间动态和潜在组织联系起来。This study shows that similar observable performance may correspond to distinct system organizations. Aggregated metrics alone are therefore insufficient to characterize system behavior under constraint. Within this framework, VDO should not be considered as a fixed optimal value, but as a constraint associated with the organization of accessible system states. The proposed multi-level approach provides a structured framework for analyzing complex biomechanical systems, linking observable performance, temporal dynamics, and latent organization.
