现实世界的财务分析需要对两个互补的信息源进行推理：公司基本面和市场动态。Real-world financial analysis requires reasoning on two complementary information sources: company fundamentals and market dynamics.公司基本面Company fundamentals是源自公司资产负债表或证券交易委员会 (SEC) 文件的基于会计的指标，例如盈利能力、杠杆率和估值比率，可反映公司的基本财务状况are accounting-based metrics derived from company balance sheets or Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filings, such as profitability, leverage, and valuation ratios, that capture a company’s underlying financial health(Fama and French, 1992; Harvey et al., 2016)。相比之下，. In contrast,交易信号，trading signals,根据历史价格和交易量数据计算，捕捉市场动态和投资者情绪，包括动量、波动性和趋势逆转computed from historical price and volume data, capture market dynamics and investor sentiment, including momentum, volatility, and trend reversals(Brock et al., 1992; Jegadeesh and Titman, 1993; Lo et al., 2000; Andersen et al., 2003; Park and Irwin, 2007; Choi, 2021)。因此，有效的财务分析和估值需要整合这两种观点，而不是孤立地依赖任一来源。此外，综合异构信息源、对数值指标进行推理以及解释不确定性下的市场行为，使得财务分析成为一项本质上具有挑战性的任务，即使对于专业的人类分析师来说也是如此。. Effective financial analysis and valuation, therefore, require integrating these two perspectives rather than relying on either source in isolation. Additionally, synthesizing heterogeneous information sources, reasoning over numerical indicators, and interpreting market behavior under uncertainty make financial analysis an inherently challenging task, even for expert human analysts.

随着人工智能（AI）的出现，法学硕士越来越多地被用来协助分析师完成财务分析任务，例如总结财报电话会议、解释监管文件以及回答有关公司业绩和风险的问题In the advent of artificial intelligence (AI), LLMs are increasingly used to assist analysts with financial analysis tasks such as summarizing earnings calls, interpreting regulatory filings, and answering questions about company performance and risk(Lee et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025, 2023; Djagba and Saley, 2025; Wu et al., 2023; Shah et al., 2022; Araci, 2019)。现有基准测试数据集，例如 FinQA. FinTradeBench is based on a decade of NASDAQ-100 data using a calibration-then-scaling framework that combines expert-authored seeds with scalable automated evaluation. We benchmark 14 LLMs under No-RAG and RAG settings and observe several key patterns. Quantitatively, we witness the highest performance gains across all models in hybrid reasoning-based questions (up to↑\uparrow大型 39.8%39.8% for large,↑\uparrow中期为 55.1%，55.1% for mid, and↑\uparrow小型法学硕士为 49.5%）。 RAG 对基本推理产生了统计上显着的改进，其中检索到的 SEC 文件激活了相关的先验知识。但 RAG 会降低交易信号问题的性能，在这些问题上，原始数值时间序列数据会混淆模型而不是帮助模型。它还引入了信息过载，提高了表面基础，同时减少了财务分析所需的精确指标提取和推理深度。此外，我们见证模型架构发挥着重要作用。潜在推理模型（例如 DeepSeek-R1 及其提炼）在混合问题上远远优于指令调整模型，这表明推理时间思想链计算对于异构金融信号的推理至关重要。模范家庭还表现出对环境的不同敏感性； Qwen 模型通常受益于 RAG，而 LLaMA 模型整体性能会下降，即使在 70B 参数下也是如此。我们期望 FinTradeBench 支持一致的财务推理基准测试，并为研究人员和从业人员提供可扩展的行业标准数据集。49.5% for small LLMs). RAG yields statistically significant improvements for fundamental reasoning, where retrieved SEC filings activate relevant prior knowledge. But RAG degrades performance on trading-signal questions, where raw numerical time-series data confuses models rather than assists them. Additionally, we witness that model architecture plays a significant role. Latent reasoning models (e.g., DeepSeek-R1 and its distillations) substantially outperform instruction-tuned models on hybrid questions, suggesting that inference-time chain-of-thought computation is crucial for reasoning over heterogeneous financial signals. Model families also exhibit different sensitivities to context; Qwen models generally benefit from RAG, while LLaMA models degrade overall, even at 70B parameters. We expect FinTradeBench to support consistent benchmarking for financial reasoning and provide a scalable, industry-standard dataset for researchers and practitioners.
