利用高光谱鱼类图像进行日间新鲜度估计的少样本序数学习Few-Shot Ordinal Learning for Day-Wise Freshness Estimation with Hyperspectral Fish Images

Kazi Nabiul Alam 1, Pooneh Bagheri-Zadeh 1, Akbar Sheikh-Akbari 1⋆ Affiliation: 1School of Built Environment, Engineering and Computing,
Leeds Beckett University, Leeds, United Kingdom.
Correspondence: A.Sheikh-Akbari@leedsbeckett.ac.uk
摘要Abstract

无损食品质量评估越来越受益于高光谱成像 (HSI)，它可以捕获与储存期间生化变化相关的光谱特征。然而，由于鱼片间的强烈变异性和每种产品的标签数据稀缺，估计每日新鲜度仍然具有挑战性。所有现有的基于 HSI 的新鲜度预测的深度学习方法都在完全监督下运行，需要密集注释的训练集，而在单个产品级别获得这些训练集的成本很高。据我们所知，我们介绍了第一个基于 HSI 的食品质量估计的几次学习框架。每个圆角定义了一个独特的情景任务，并且 CORAL 风格的序数预测头通过累积阈值建模捕获新鲜度进展的排名性质。基于生物学的单调性和嵌入平滑度约束进一步引导预测走向合理的轨迹。在严格的看不见的鱼片协议下的 16 天鲑鱼 HSI 数据集上，我们的方法实现了 1.58 天的平均绝对误差，Non-destructive food quality assessment has increasingly benefited from hyperspectral imaging (HSI), which captures spectral signatures linked to biochemical changes during storage. Estimating day-wise freshness, however, remains challenging owing to strong inter-fillet variability and scarce labelled data per product. All existing deep learning approaches for HSI-based freshness prediction operate under full supervision, requiring densely annotated training sets that are costly to obtain at the individual-product level. We introduce, to the best of our knowledge, the first few-shot learning framework for HSI-based food quality estimation. Each fillet defines a distinct episodic task, and a CORAL-style ordinal prediction head captures the ranked nature of freshness progression through cumulative threshold modelling. Biologically grounded monotonicity and embedding smoothness constraints further guide predictions toward plausible trajectories. On a 16-day salmon HSI dataset under a strict unseen-fillet protocol, our method achieves a mean absolute error of 1.58 days and±\pm每个鱼片仅标记三天，2 天准确度为 72.3%，在相同的看不见的鱼片协议下，大大优于标量回归和标签分布基线。2-day accuracy of 72.3% with only three labelled days per fillet, substantially outperforming scalar regression and label-distribution baselines under an identical unseen-fillet protocol.

I 简介Introduction

确保易腐食品保持新鲜对于消费者健康、减少浪费和高效的供应链管理至关重要。传统的新鲜度评估方法，包括感官小组、化学分析和微生物培养，往往具有破坏性、速度慢且成本高，限制了它们在工业环境中的可扩展性。高光谱成像 (HSI)Ensuring that perishable food products remain fresh is vital for consumer health, waste reduction, and efficient supply chain management. Conventional approaches to freshness evaluation, including sensory panels, chemical assays, and microbiological cultures, tend to be destructive, slow, and costly, restricting their scalability in industrial settings. Hyperspectral imaging (HSI)[1, 2]通过将光谱学与空间成像相结合，提供了一种引人注目的替代方案，从而产生丰富的光谱空间特征offers a compelling alternative by merging spectroscopy with spatial imaging, thereby producing rich spectral–spatial signatures[3]反映了储存和腐败过程中潜在的生化变化。that reflect underlying biochemical changes during storage and spoilage.

深度学习架构，特别是卷积神经网络 (CNN)，在从 HSI 数据中提取复杂的光谱空间模式以用于水果、蔬菜和肉类等一系列食品的质量和安全检测方面已展现出巨大的前景。Deep learning architectures, particularly convolutional neural networks (CNNs), have demonstrated considerable promise in extracting complex spectral–spatial patterns from HSI data for quality and safety inspection across a range of food products including fruits, vegetables, and meat[2, 4]。结合光谱和空间卷积的深层架构. Deep architectures incorporating spectral and spatial convolutions[4]和序数回归头and ordinal regression heads[11]已显示出高质量时间线预测的前景，而紧凑的少样本设计可以用更少的参数实现有竞争力的分类精度have shown promise for quality timeline prediction, while compact few-shot designs achieve competitive classification accuracy with substantially fewer parameters[5]。然而，一个反复出现的限制是，这些完全监督的模型需要大量带注释的数据集，当必须以精细的时间粒度为每个单独的鱼片分配新鲜度标签时，这种数据集很少可用。除了数据稀缺之外，大多数基于 HSI 的研究都框架新鲜度估计. A recurring limitation, however, is that these fully supervised models require large annotated datasets, something rarely available when freshness labels must be assigned per individual fillet at fine temporal granularity. Beyond data scarcity, most HSI-based studies frame freshness estimation[2]作为标准回归或名义分类，忽略了存储天数之间的固有顺序。as either standard regression or nominal classification, overlooking the inherent ordering among storage days.

为了解决数据稀缺问题，小样本学习重新制定了围绕通过情景协议采样的任务分布的训练，从而能够从很少的标记示例中进行泛化Addressing data scarcity, few-shot learning reformulates training around task distributions sampled through episodic protocols, enabling generalization from very few labelled examples[6, 7]。众所周知的深度少样本策略，包括原型、匹配、关系和基于梯度的元学习器，已被证明在低样本约束下是有效的. Well-known deep few-shot strategies, including prototypical, matching, relation, and gradient-based meta-learners, have proven effective under low-shot constraints[8, 9]。在 HSI 中，少样本方法最近已应用于标签有限的遥感和作物分类. Within HSI, few-shot approaches have recently been applied to remote sensing and crop classification with limited labels[5, 10].

然而，标准回归和分类目标无法保留存储天数之间的顺序关系，可能会产生时间上不一致的估计。序数回归通过显式建模有序标签空间来解决这个问题Standard regression and classification objectives, however, fail to preserve ordinal relationships between storage days, potentially producing temporally inconsistent estimates. Ordinal regression addresses this by explicitly modelling ordered label spaces[11, 12, 14]。一致性排序 Logits (CORAL). Consistent Rank Logits (CORAL)[13]将序数问题分解为具有权重共享的二元任务，保证排名一致性，但将此类方法与少样本范例相结合仍有待探索。decompose the ordinal problem into binary tasks with weight sharing, guaranteeing rank consistency, yet combining such methods with few-shot paradigms remains unexplored.

我们提出了一种标签高效的情景序数回归框架，用于从高光谱图像估计每日新鲜度。它不是依赖于支持条件的度量比较，而是通过情景任务采样与基于生物学的序数正则化器相结合来实现小样本泛化，这些正则化器使预测在训练期间从未见过的鱼片上保持时间一致。据我们所知，这是首次将小样本学习应用于基于 HSI 的食品质量或新鲜度预测，其中所有先前的深度学习解决方案都在完全监督下运行We propose a label-efficient episodic ordinal regression framework for day-wise freshness estimation from hyperspectral images. Rather than relying on support-conditioned metric comparison, it attains few-shot generalization through episodic task sampling combined with biologically grounded ordinal regularizers that keep predictions temporally consistent on fillets never seen during training. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first application of few-shot learning to HSI-based food quality or freshness prediction, where all prior deep learning solutions have operated under full supervision[1, 2, 3]。情景协议与 CORAL 风格的序数头和双重正则化配对：预测日期的单调性和学习表示的嵌入平滑性。实验表明，在严格的看不见的圆角评估下，这种组合的性能明显优于标量回归和基于分布的基线。. The episodic protocol is paired with CORAL-style ordinal heads and dual regularization: monotonicity on predicted days and embedding smoothness on learned representations. Experiments show this combination decisively outperforms scalar regression and distribution-based baselines under a strict unseen-fillet evaluation.

IV 结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种用于从高光谱图像估计每日新鲜度的少镜头序数学习范例。该方法结合了情景训练、基于 CORAL 的序数回归和时间正则化来预测以前未见过的鱼片的新鲜度，每个任务仅标记三个存储天。实验表明，在严格的看不见的圆角评估下，标签分布学习和传统回归得到了一致且显着的改进。一个限制是鲑鱼 HSI 数据集是专有的且尚未公开，这限制了可重复性；该框架本身与数据集无关，我们计划在公共 HSI 食品质量基准上对其进行验证，并在未来的工作中发布代码。该方法提供了一种标签高效且可配置的方法，可用于行业可扩展的、无损的食品质量评估。This paper proposes a few-shot ordinal learning paradigm for estimating day-wise freshness from hyperspectral images. The approach combines episodic training, CORAL-based ordinal regression, and temporal regularization to predict freshness across previously unseen fish fillets with only three labelled storage days per task. Experiments demonstrate consistent and significant improvements over label distribution learning and conventional regression under a rigorous unseen-fillet evaluation. A limitation is that the salmon HSI dataset is proprietary and not yet publicly available, which constrains reproducibility; the framework itself is dataset-agnostic, and we plan to validate it on public HSI food-quality benchmarks and release the code in future work. The method offers a label-efficient and configurable approach for scalable, non-destructive food quality assessment for industry.
