1 简介Introduction

变分量子本征解算器 (VQE) 估计分子基态 通过优化量子电路的连续参数来提高能量 安萨兹The variational quantum eigensolver (VQE) estimates molecular ground-state energies by optimizing the continuous parameters of a quantum circuit ansatz(Peruzzo et al., 2014; Kandala et al., 2017; Tilly et al., 2022)。 它的准确性和可训练性在很大程度上取决于 ansatz 架构，但是 最佳架构因分子、哈密顿量和硬件而异。 量子架构搜索（QAS）通过构建 使用可微的、离散的、来自指定门空间的电路 生成或学习的搜索过程. Its accuracy and trainability depend strongly on the ansatz architecture, yet the best architecture varies with the molecule, Hamiltonian, and hardware. Quantum architecture search (QAS) addresses this design problem by constructing circuits from a prescribed gate space using differentiable, discrete, generative, or learned search procedures(Zhang et al., 2022; Du et al., 2022; Lu et al., 2023; Nakaji et al., 2024; He et al., 2024).

RL-QAS 中的评估瓶颈尤其严重。一项政策附加一项 一次合法的门，环境通过以下方式评估每个新的电路前缀 使用 VQE 优化其参数。策略训练重复此操作 数千条轨迹。由此产生的成本在结构上是不对称的： 附加门和更新操作合法性是准确且廉价的，而 优化后的反馈是昂贵的并且最初是未知的。这表明 基于 QAS 特定模型的公式，保留已知的电路转换 并且仅学习否则需要另一次 VQE 调用的反馈。The evaluation bottleneck is especially severe in RL-QAS. A policy appends one legal gate at a time, and the environment evaluates each new circuit prefix by optimizing its parameters with VQE. Policy training repeats this operation over thousands of trajectories. The resulting cost is structurally asymmetric: appending a gate and updating action legality are exact and inexpensive, whereas post-optimization feedback is expensive and initially unknown. This suggests a QAS-specific model-based formulation that preserves the known circuit transition and learns only the feedback that would otherwise require another VQE call.

将这种不对称性转化为有用的综合经验并不等同于 拟合全局精确的能量回归器。首先，RL-QAS 决策是 有条件：模型必须区分同一电路的合法延续 前缀，即使它的错误在整个架构空间中不一致。 其次，可部署的培训反馈不能假设可以访问确切的信息 基态能量，其有用尺度随着经验搜索而变化 边境改善。第三，想象力将政策转向模型偏好 电路，因此系统误差可能会在连续决策中被放大。 因此，QAS 世界模型必须表示有序前缀，学习 无预言机决策信号，并公开控制的可靠性信息 何时以及如何使用想象的反馈。Turning this asymmetry into useful synthetic experience is not equivalent to fitting a globally accurate energy regressor. First, an RL-QAS decision is conditional: the model must distinguish legal continuations of the same circuit prefix, even when its errors are nonuniform across the full architecture space. Second, deployable training feedback cannot assume access to the exact ground-state energy, and its useful scale changes as the empirical search frontier improves. Third, imagination shifts the policy toward model-preferred circuits, so systematic errors can be amplified across consecutive decisions. A QAS world model must therefore represent ordered prefixes, learn an oracle-free decision signal, and expose reliability information that controls when and how imagined feedback is used.

7 结论Conclusion

DreamQAS 将分子 RL-QAS 视为已知的动态、昂贵的反馈 问题。它保留了精确的合法电路转换，学习无预言机 VQE 后反馈的循环集合，并将该反馈转换为 多步骤想象的政策梯度。通过五项任务，它取得了强有力的成果 最终质量配置文件，同时以更少的实际 VQE 达到匹配的错误水平 调用比非想象力的控制。反事实调查表明该模型 学习行动层面的效用；直接使用同型号不能替代 想象中的政策学习；整体分歧识别出有风险的反馈。 因此，QAS 的世界模型不需要重现完整的环境或行为 作为精确的能量神谕：他们应该保留已知的结构并学习 可靠的顺序决策所需的反馈。DreamQAS treats molecular RL-QAS as a known-dynamics, expensive-feedback problem. It preserves exact legal circuit transitions, learns an oracle-free recurrent ensemble of post-VQE feedback, and converts that feedback into multi-step imagined policy gradients. Across five tasks, it achieves a strong final-quality profile while reaching matched error levels with fewer real VQE calls than a non-imaginative control. Counterfactual probes show that the model learns action-level utility; direct use of the same model cannot replace imagined policy learning; and ensemble disagreement identifies risky feedback. World models for QAS therefore need not reproduce the full environment or act as exact energy oracles: they should preserve known structure and learn the feedback required for reliable sequential decisions.
