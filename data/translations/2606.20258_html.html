2. 背景Background

本节介绍了本文在三个工作领域的贡献：作为设计问题的一致性、人工智能开发的参与式方法，以及这些问题变得紧迫的制度环境。我们首先将人工智能对齐视为设计挑战而不是价值规范问题，并介绍可用于基于 LLM 的系统的对齐技术，其可供性和约束定义了可以作为对齐目标有意义地追求的内容。由此我们得出的方法论结论是，为制度化管理的环境设计一致的法学硕士系统需要参与式方法，这些方法可以体现专业嵌入的价值观并将其转化为一致的目标。我们将这项工作置于参与式设计和参与式人工智能的传统中。最后，我们转向当前面临这一挑战的机构环境，调查北欧公共知识机构如何应对法学硕士整合，并确定本文所解决的现有应对措施中的结构性差距。This section situates the paper’s contribution across three bodies of work: alignment as a design problem, participatory approaches to AI development, and the institutional landscape in which these concerns have become urgent. We begin by examining AI alignment as a design challenge rather than a problem of value specification, and introduce the alignment techniques available for LLM-based systems whose affordances and constraints define what can be meaningfully pursued as an alignment objective. From this we draw the methodological consequence that the design of aligned LLM systems for institutionally governed contexts requires participatory approaches that can surface professionally embedded values and translate them into alignment objectives. We situate this work in the traditions of participatory design and participatory AI. Finally, we turn to the institutional landscape in which this challenge is currently unfolding, surveying how Nordic public knowledge institutions have responded to LLM integration and identifying the structural gap in existing responses that this paper addresses.

2.1. 对齐设计Alignment as Design

人工智能对齐的概念作为一种文化和学术实践有着悠久的历史。作为一种文化理念，它至少可以追溯到 1940 年代的科幻小说和艾萨克·阿西莫夫的机器人三定律The concept of AI alignment has a long history as both a cultural and academic exercise. As a cultural idea, it goes back at least as far as 1940’s sci-fi and Isaac Asimov’s three laws of robotics(Asimov, 1942)。讨论通常与人工智能的概念联系在一起，人工智能为机器提供一定程度的不可预测的代理权和独立于人类参与的自主权. The discussion has often been tied to a conception of AI that affords machines a degree of unpredictable agency and autonomy independent of human involvement(Wiener, 1960; Russell, 2019)和价值调整，即将人工智能系统的价值和目标与其人类设计者、用户或所有者的价值和目标保持一致，通常被提议作为管理这种自主权的一种方式, and value alignment, that is, aligning the values and objectives of AI systems with those of their human designers, users, or owners, is commonly proposed as a way to govern this autonomy(Russell, 2019; Gabriel, 2020)。这一概念的前提是人工智能系统能够实现真正的自主性，并且它们的代理可以扎根于内在的价值观中。识别这些价值观通常被视为伦理和道德的实践，尽管人们普遍认为以足够的细节和明确的方式规定道德行为是极其困难的. This conception presupposes that AI systems are capable of genuine autonomy and that their agency can be anchored in internalized values. Identifying such values is often framed as an exercise in ethics and morality, though prescribing moral behavior with sufficient detail and unambiguity is widely recognized as extraordinarily difficult(Gabriel, 2020)。相反，最近的常见做法是选择一些广泛的道德原则作为联盟的基础（通常是乐于助人、无害和诚实的变化），并用从收集的人类反馈中提取的“人类偏好”的量化表达来补充这些原则. Instead, it has recently become common practice to select a few broad ethical principles as a foundation for alignment—typically variations on helpfulness, harmlessness, and honesty—and supplement these with quantified expressions of “human preference” extracted from collected human feedback(Christiano et al., 2017; Bai et al., 2022; Lindström et al., 2025)。在他们关于宪法人工智能的开创性论文中，. In their seminal paper on Constitutional AI,Bai et al.写道：“我们的目标不是定义或规定‘有用’和‘无害’的含义，而是评估我们训练技术的有效性，因此在大多数情况下，我们只是让我们的众包工作者按照他们认为合适的方式解释这些概念”write: “Our goal is not to define or prescribe what ‘helpful’ and ‘harmless’ mean but to evaluate the effectiveness of our training techniques, so for the most part we simply let our crowdworkers interpret these concepts as they see fit”(Bai et al., 2022, p. 4)。因此，在实践中，广泛的原则被委托给个别注释者的解释自由裁量权——一致性的基础不是道德推理，而是一种“聚合直觉”。. Broad principles, in practice, are thus delegated to the interpretative discretion of individual annotators—grounding alignment less in ethical reasoning than in a kind of “aggregated intuition.”

这种实践依赖于普遍主义和本质主义的价值观This practice relies on a universalist and essentialist conception of values(Arzberger et al., 2024)。普遍主义，因为所应用的价值观被假定为广泛适用或广泛代表普遍的人类或文化. Universalist, because the values applied are assumed to be either broadly applicable or broadly representative of universal human or cultural(Pawar et al., 2025)价值观。本质主义，认为人类反馈被认为揭示了基本的人类或文化价值观和偏好，可以用作通过机器学习（ML）进行调整的“基本事实”。尽管有人尝试通过广泛参与来民主化调整目标的制定，但在实践中，这通常相当于从一部分人群中“获取”价值观和原则，并应用数学技术来汇总结果values. Essentialist, in that human feedback is assumed to reveal essential human or cultural values and preferences that can be used as a “ground truth” for alignment through machine learning (ML). While there have been attempts at democratizing the formulation of alignment objectives through broad participation, in practice this often amounts to “sourcing” values and principles from a segment of a population and applying mathematical techniques to aggregate the results(Huang et al., 2024; Moats and Ganguly, 2025).

我们在本文中采用的人工智能对齐的另一种解释是将机器学习模型（现代人工智能系统的核心）视为固有地嵌入现有社会技术系统中的设计工件An alternative interpretation of AI alignment, which we employ in this paper, views ML models—the core of modern AI systems—as design artifacts inherently embedded in existing sociotechnical systems(Selbst et al., 2019; Enni and Assent, 2021; Arzberger et al., 2026; Elmqvist et al., 2025; Lindström et al., 2025)。在这种观点中，价值观被嵌入到模型中以及通过设计过程使用它们的更大系统中，并且模型本身的行为不能脱离其创建、实现和使用的社会技术背景。设计与概念密切相关. In this view, values are embedded in the models and in the larger systems in which they are used through a process of design, and the behavior of the models themselves cannot be disentangled from the sociotechnical context of their creation, implementation, and use. Design is strongly related to the notion of邪恶的问题wicked problems，即没有明确界定、单方面无法解决的问题, that is, problems that are not clearly defined and that cannot be unilaterally solved(Buchanan, 1992)。这意味着设计必然需要在多种可能的解决方案之间进行定制的优先级排序和深思熟虑。以设计为中心的方法不是假设基本价值观可以来自人类反馈或编纂为普遍的道德原则，而是将人工智能的一致性视为管理和实施对设计系统的承诺和潜力的多种可能相互矛盾的观点的练习。最近针对现代人工智能技术的巨大伦理影响和复杂性的务实转向得出了类似的结论——在人机交互领域. This means that design necessarily entails bespoke prioritization and deliberation between multiple possible solutions. Rather than assuming that essential values can be sourced from human feedback or codified as universal ethical principles, a design-centric approach views AI alignment as an exercise in managing and implementing multiple, potentially contradictory, perspectives on the promise and potential of the designed system. Recent pragmatic turns to address the immense ethical implications and complexity of modern AI technologies come to similar conclusions—in both HCI(Öz et al., 2026)和人工智能研究and AI research(Nyrup and Robinson, 2022)。此外，这一框架呼应了几十年来参与式设计处理一般技术开发和集成项目的方式. Further, this framing echoes the ways in which participatory design for decades has approached general technological development and integration projects(Bødker et al., 2021; Karasti, 2014)——我们将在下一节中回顾这一传统。—a tradition we return to in the next section.

在本文中，我们特别关注法学硕士：通过应用从大型文本语料库中学到的统计规律来生成输出的系统，而不是通过执行明确指定的符号逻辑。传统软件将所需行为表达为可以直接指定、验证和调整的确定性规则，而 LLM 行为是根据经验进行评估并通过一系列技术塑造的。其中包括操纵模型输入上下文的方法In this paper, we focus specifically on LLMs: systems that generate outputs by applying statistical regularities learned from large text corpora, rather than by executing explicitly specified symbolic logic. Whereas conventional software expresses desired behavior as deterministic rules that can be specified, verified, and adjusted directly, LLM behavior is evaluated empirically and shaped through a range of techniques. These include approaches that manipulate the model’s input context(Mei et al., 2025)，监视和过滤输出的模块, modules that monitor and filter outputs(Dong et al., 2025)以及训练后策略，例如根据人类反馈进行监督微调或强化学习, and post-training strategies such as supervised fine-tuning or reinforcement learning from human feedback(Ji et al., 2023)。这些技术通常结合应用，每种技术都具有特定的可供性和约束，决定了可以通过它们有意义地表达哪些类型的对齐目标。因此，为基于法学硕士的系统制定协调目标是一种在这些限制之内和跨越这些限制的工作——更接近于设计而不是规范，并且对参与性协调过程可以实际达到的目标提出了真正的要求：目标必须同时植根于特定的专业价值观，可以通过可用的技术来表达，并且服从于经验而不是正式的评估。. Typically applied in combination, these techniques each carry specific affordances and constraints that determine what kinds of alignment objectives can be meaningfully expressed through them. Formulating alignment objectives for an LLM-based system is accordingly an exercise in working within and across these constraints—closer to design than to specification, and one that places real demands on what a participatory alignment process can realistically aim to produce: objectives must be simultaneously grounded in situated professional values, expressible through available techniques, and amenable to empirical rather than formal evaluation.

2.2. 参与式人工智能Participatory AI

将对齐描述为一个棘手的设计问题具有直接的方法论含义。棘手的问题不能单方面解决，而是需要多方进行专门的审议，这些各方对系统应该做什么和应该为谁服务提出不同的、部分不可通约的观点。对于以LLM为媒介的知识传播，与此审议相关的各方不仅是技术性的，即了解对齐技术可以表达什么和不可以表达什么的开发人员和研究人员，而且是专业的和制度性的：体现系统旨在反映的价值观和标准的从业者，并对系统传播的知识的质量承担责任。制定同时作为制度价值的准确和情境描述以及技术上可操作的约束的协调目标需要各方之间的深思熟虑，而不是由任何一方单方面指定。在这些不同的观点（技术、编辑、机构）及其各自的判断之间进行调解意味着公共知识机构的基于法学硕士的界面设计非常适合参与式设计。The characterization of alignment as a wicked design problem carries a direct methodological implication. Wicked problems cannot be resolved unilaterally, but require bespoke deliberation among multiple parties who bring different and partially incommensurable perspectives on what a system should do and who it should serve. For LLM-mediated knowledge dissemination, the parties relevant to this deliberation are not only technical, that is, developers and researchers who understand what alignment techniques can and cannot express, but also professional and institutional: the practitioners who embody the values and standards the system is meant to reflect, and who bear accountability for the quality of the knowledge it disseminates. Formulating alignment objectives that function simultaneously as accurate and situated descriptions of institutional values and as technically operationalizable constraints requires deliberation between these parties rather than unilateral specification by any one of them. Mediating between these different perspectives—technical, editorial, institutional—and their respective judgments means that the design of LLM-based interfaces for public knowledge institutions is well-suited for participatory design.

因此，我们将这项工作置于参与式设计（PD）的传统中Thus, we situate this work in the traditions of participatory design (PD)(Bødker et al., 2021; Karasti, 2014)以及参与式人工智能（PAI）这一新兴领域and the emerging field of participatory AI (PAI)(Elmqvist et al., 2025)。 PD 研究开创了利益相关者和领域专家参与社会技术系统的开发和集成的先河，并展示了这如何能够创建深入的背景知识并为受数字技术影响的人提供支持. PD research pioneered the participatory involvement of stakeholders and domain experts in the development and integration of sociotechnical systems, and has shown how this can create deep contextual knowledge and empower those affected by digital technologies(Bødker et al., 2000)。在此背景下，. In this context,参与性的participatory意味着用户、设计者、管理人员、研究人员和其他人在整个设计过程中为了共同的目标而协作并协商系统设计。 PD 围绕一个means that users, designers, management, researchers, and others collaborate towards shared goals and negotiate system designs throughout the design process. The role of the researcher in such workshops is that of the facilitator: guiding the process, helping stakeholders negotiate the situation, and participating actively rather than observing(Sanders and Stappers, 2008)。 PAI 将这些承诺扩展到建立在基础模型之上的人工智能系统的特定环境——这些系统“经过微调、专门化或以其他方式适应特定领域和实践社区”. PAI extends these commitments to the specific context of AI systems built on top of foundation models—systems that are “fine-tuned, specialized, or otherwise adapted for specific domains and communities of practices”(Elmqvist et al., 2025)。 PAI 将核心 PD 原则应用于人工智能开发，旨在将此类系统视为“增强而不是削弱人类能动性、人类尊严和人类价值观的共享社会技术系统”. PAI applies central PD principles to AI development with the aim of treating such systems “as shared socio-technical systems that enhance rather than diminish human agency, human dignity, and human values”(Elmqvist et al., 2025)。因此，PAI 保证系统应该“与了解领域细微差别、边缘情况和‘良好’表现的社会意义的从业者共同设计和微调”，并且结果应该是“保留文化多样性和社区自决的本地控制的人工智能系统”. Accordingly, PAI warrants that systems should be “co-designed and fine-tuned with practitioners who understand domain-nuances, edge-cases and the social meaning of ’good’ performance,” and that the result should be “locally-controlled AI systems that preserve cultural diversity and community self-determination”(Elmqvist et al., 2025).

PAI 应用于基于 LLM 的系统（至少）提出了两个挑战。第一个问题涉及人工智能数据驱动开发实践背景下的参与意味着什么。作为The application of PAI to LLM-based systems raises (at least) two challenges. The first concerns what participation means in the context of AI’s data-driven development practices. AsBirhane et al.观察一下，参与人工智能开发的风险被简化为数据来源：收集人类输入，进行统计汇总，并用于引导模型行为——个人参与者充当数据生成者，而不是故意的设计决策共同作者observe, participation in AI development risks being reduced to the sourcing of data: human inputs are collected, aggregated statistically, and used to steer model behavior—with individual participants functioning as data generators rather than as deliberate co-authors of design decisions(Birhane et al., 2022)。上述训练后对齐技术（RLHF、DPO 和相关方法）的普遍应用加剧了这种风险，因为这些方法倾向于从人类反馈中提取偏好信号，并通过机器学习而不是通过 PD 设想的协商审议来操作它们，从而使参与者在可扩展性服务中扮演数据源的角色。这两种参与模式之间的区别对于能够产生什么结果很重要。如果参与以“采购”价值观和偏好结束，那么参与者的观点就会被扁平化为聚合模式，这些模式被操作和解释，而这些模式的产生者几乎没有代理权。然而，如果参与延伸到更广泛的系统设计和审议中，那么这些相同的数据——以及由集体专业判断产生的任何其他设计工件——就可以由产生这些数据的人进行检查、质疑和修改。在使法学硕士与机构的编辑标准保持一致的背景下，后者使编辑团队成为调整过程中真正的代理人，而不是一个需要挖掘的来源。第二个挑战是，现代法学硕士的社会技术复杂性使得真正的参与在实践中难以维持：当参与者对系统的作用及其行为如何受到管理缺乏足够的了解时，他们的反应往往倾向于反应性而不是评估性，这正是使数据源成为审议的诱人替代品的条件。. The pervasive application of post-training alignment techniques described above—RLHF, DPO, and related approaches—compounds this risk, as these methods tend to extract preference signals from human feedback and operationalize them through machine learning rather than through the kind of negotiated deliberation that PD envisions, thereby relegating participants to the role of data sources in the service of scalability. The distinction between these two modes of participation matters for what can be produced. If participation ends at “sourcing” values and preferences, participants’ perspectives are flattened into aggregate patterns that are operationalized and interpreted in ways that leave little agency for those who produced them. If participation extends into the broader design and deliberation of the system, however, these same data—and any other design artifact produced by collective professional judgment—can be examined, contested, and revised by those who produced it. In the context of aligning an LLM to an institution’s editorial standards, the latter makes the editorial team genuine agents in the alignment process rather than a source to be mined. The second challenge is that the sociotechnical complexity of modern LLMs makes genuine participation difficult to sustain in practice: when participants lack sufficient understanding of what a system does and how its behavior is governed, their responses tend toward the reactive rather than the evaluative, which is precisely the condition that makes data sourcing a tempting substitute for deliberation(Delgado et al., 2023; Corbett et al., 2023)。因此，培养参与者对系统可能性和局限性的理解是有意义地参与协调工作的先决条件，这直接呼应了传统的政策制定对参与利益相关者赋权的强调. Developing participants’ understanding of the system’s possibilities and limitations is accordingly a precondition for meaningful participation in alignment work, which directly echoes traditional PD emphasis on empowerment of the participating stakeholders(Ehn, 1988).

Workshop formats that ask practitioners to exercise their judgment on concrete materials make tacit knowledge available in the form in which it is most reliable.

2.3. 北欧公共知识机构中的人工智能整合AI Integration in Nordic Public Knowledge Institutions

北欧公共知识机构具有独特的机构形象：它们将自己定位为商业信息来源的值得信赖的替代品，让专业编辑人员对其产出的质量和完整性负责，并将其公共合法性建立在编辑权威之上。最近法学硕士驱动的信息服务的激增迫使这些机构在人工智能整合方面制定立场，而检查反应范围是有启发性的——主要不是作为对当前实践的调查，而是因为它揭示了该领域尚未解决的结构性差距。Nordic public knowledge institutions share a distinctive institutional profile: they position themselves as trustworthy alternatives to commercial information sources, maintain professional editorial staff accountable for the quality and integrity of their output, and ground their public legitimacy on this editorial authority. The recent proliferation of LLM-driven information services has forced these institutions to develop positions on AI integration, and examining the range of responses is instructive—not primarily as a survey of current practice, but because it reveals a structural gap that the field has not yet resolved.

The design challenge of editorial alignment is accordingly twofold: 1) to engage editors as professional experts in the formulation of alignment objectives, centering the accountability and expertise that are constitutive of the institution’s legitimacy; and 2) to do so in a way that accommodates the dynamic nature of editorial practice, establishing alignment not as a one-time specification but as an ongoing process subject to the same professional participation and revision as the editorial practice it reflects(Arzberger et al., 2026; Elmqvist et al., 2025; Birhane et al., 2022).

As one participant said, “this concerns what [the encyclopedia] is. Whereas the intended audience for the encyclopedia was previously academically-trained professionals, today the encyclopedia is intended to be, as one participant put it, “for everyone. It is for the broad, interested population.” However, the initial prototype chatbot’s outputs reflected the formal, encyclopedic tone of the underlying articles, which is poorly suited to the platform’s primary user base of young people and non-academic users.

It was clear that the editors had an idea about what kind of tone and style was fitting for the system, but it was less clear how to formalize that in a way that could be implemented into the system without needing editorial oversight of each individual output. 4.2. 研讨会 2：审议编辑标准Workshop 2: Deliberating an Editorial Standard

为了制定可操作的编辑标准来指导编辑协调过程，我们于 2026 年春季举办了第二次研讨会，旨在捕捉通过编辑工作出现的编辑决策和理由，而不是对事实和细微差别重要性的抽象描述。在本次研讨会上，我们再次邀请了四位高级编辑（与第一次研讨会重叠的三位），以及同一位项目经理和两位研究人员出席。未来研讨会的重点是对原型的构思和批评，而第二次研讨会则有一个更集中的目标：得出一套具体的编辑原则和价值观，这些原则和价值观可以构成法学硕士界面生成的人工智能生成文本的工作编辑标准。至关重要的是，这个标准不是外部强加的，而是通过编辑自己的专业实践得出的。这些标准可能已经部分编入法典，但也可能嵌入到机构的专业实践和工作文化中。由此产生的编辑标准理想地将编辑实践和价值观转化为技术实施的一致目标，尽管正如我们上面所描述的，它必然存在于不同类型知识的症结所在。在这个关键问题上，研讨会试图以反映或以其他方式类似于团队已建立的编辑工作流程的流程为基础，以便识别编辑标准的重要位置方面，这些方面仅在编辑遇到特定类型的文本并立即知道如何处理它时出现，尽管事先并不知道该知识。To arrive at an actionable editorial standard to guide the editorial alignment process, we conducted a second workshop in spring 2026 intended to capture editorial decisions and rationales as these emerged via editorial work, rather than abstract descriptions of the importance of factuality and nuance. For this workshop we again invited four senior editors (three overlapping from the first workshop), as well as the same project manager and two researchers present. Whereas the Future Workshop centered on ideation and critique of the prototype, the second workshop had a more focused aim: to derive a concrete set of editorial principles and values that could constitute a working editorial standard for the AI-generated text produced by the LLM interface. Crucially, this standard was not to be imposed externally, but elicited through the editors’ own professional practice. These standards might be partially codified already, but are likely also embedded in the professional practice and working culture of the institution. The resulting editorial standard would ideally translate editorial practice and values into alignment objectives for technical implementation, although it, as we describe above, necessarily exists at the crux of diverging kinds of knowledge. At this crux, the workshop sought to base itself on processes that mirror or otherwise resemble the established editorial workflows of the team in order to identify important situated aspects of the editorial standard that emerged only as an editor encountered a specific kind of text and immediately knew what to do with it, despite not being aware of that knowledge beforehand.

4.2.1. 编辑标准The Editorial Standard

研讨会总结了围绕五个核心价值观组织的编辑标准：The workshop concluded with the consolidation of an editorial standard, organized around five core values:

  • •

    尊重读者的时间和注意力Respect the reader’s time and attention

  • •

    提供适当的背景Provide appropriate context

  • •

    以教学方式呈现内容Present content pedagogically

  • •

    不要居高临下地对读者说话Do not talk down to the reader

  • •

    保持中立且有分寸的语气Maintain a neutral and measured tone

这些价值观被落实为十二项具体规则来指导实施：These values were operationalized into twelve concrete rules to guide implementation:

  • •

    以总结答案和要点的引导段落开头Open with a lead paragraph that summarizes the answer and key points

  • •

    表明用户的查询是如何被解释的Signal how the user’s query has been interpreted

  • •

    定义基本概念Define essential concepts

  • •

    尽量减少文本复杂性、学术语境和不必要的术语Minimize textual complexity, academic register, and unnecessary jargon

  • •

    将历史事实置于其地理和时间顺序背景中Place historical facts in their geographic and chronological context

  • •

    遵循[百科全书]关于正式标准（例如缩写）的现有风格指南Follow [the encyclopedia]’s existing style guidelines on formal criteria (e.g., on abbreviations)

  • •

    使用示例来说明关键概念，但仅来自源材料Use examples to illustrate key concepts, but only from the source material

  • •

    为引导段落后的正文保留高度细节和阐述Reserve high detail and elaboration for the body text following the lead paragraph

  • •

    避免规范或情绪化的判断Avoid normative or emotional judgments

  • •

    避免直接向读者讲话Avoid directly addressing the reader

  • •

    避免对读者做出假设Avoid making assumptions about the reader

  • •

    避免使用比喻或叙述性语言Avoid figurative or narrative language

研讨会最直接的结果是根据编辑标准重组了系统的输出格式。回复从对话风格转向更结构化的呈现方式：第三人称、客观的格式，以总结答案的引导段落开头，然后是对如何解释查询的可选阐述，以及为需要的读者提供更详细的正文部分。这些改变主要通过上下文工程和约束生成来实现，之所以选择这些改变是因为它们对参与者来说具有高优先级，并且与可用的对齐技术具有高度兼容性。The workshop’s most immediate consequence was a restructuring of the system’s output format on the basis of the editorial standard. Responses were reoriented away from a conversational style toward a more structured presentation: a third-person, impersonal format that opens with a lead paragraph summarizing the answer, followed by an optional elaboration of how the query was interpreted and a more detailed body section for readers who require it. These changes were implemented primarily through context engineering and constrained generation and were chosen due to their high priority for the participants and their high compatibility with available alignment techniques.

4.3. 研讨会2分析Analysis of Workshop 2

我们发现，研讨会结构在不同阶段维持了不同类型的编辑见解，具体取决于每个阶段利用的专业实践类型。在研讨会的第一部分（上述第 2-3 阶段），参与者关注与内容和精度相关的编辑决策，而在第二部分（第 4 阶段），重点转向语气和风格。我们在下面详细阐述了这种差异，我们认为它表明了不同形式的编辑知识，这些知识也强调了百科全书精神的两个不同方面，每个方面都因引入基于法学硕士的传播而受到压力。We found that the workshop structure sustained different kinds of editorial insights at different stages, depending on what kind of professional practice each stage harnessed. In the first part of the workshop (stages 2–3 described above), participants focused on editorial decisions related to content and precision, whereas in the second part (stage 4), focus shifted to tone and style. We elaborate on this difference below, which we take to indicate different forms of editorial knowledge that also underscore two different aspects of the encyclopedia’s ethos, each of which are put under pressure by the introduction of LLM-based dissemination.

4.3.1. 预编辑示例的呈现Presentation of Pre-edited Examples

参与者事先从与当前原型的 20 次真实用户交互中选择了 3 个响应，并对其进行了编辑评论。在研讨会的第一部分，参与者提出了他们的批评和他们产生的编辑反思。In advance, participants had selected and editorially critiqued three responses each from a set of 20 real user interactions with the current prototype. In the first part of the workshop, participants presented their critiques and the editorial reflections they had generated.

编辑们主要关注回复的内容以及它们如何反映源文本。一个例子涉及对一种特定方言的概述，这引发了关于法学硕士回复应包含多少信息的讨论。尽管普遍认为输出不应过长，但主编对答复缺乏对核心术语的解释感到不满意。在一个相关案例中，一位编辑注意到，与某种形式的神经分歧相关的回应缺乏重要信息，无法引入必要的细微差别，以避免强化与相关神经分歧相关的现有负面偏见。问题不在于所提供的信息不真实，而在于其不足以有效打击无端负面偏见的传播。当被问及法学硕士输出可能会以这种方式恶化负面偏见的后果时，编辑们一致认为，正如一位参与者所说，“这将是非常有问题的，因为我认为你必须将聊天机器人视为百科全书的一部分。”这位参与者补充说，在这种情况下，输出“必须更复杂。它必须更长。”Editors largely focused on the content of the responses and how they reflected the source texts. One example involved an overview of a specific dialect, which opened up a discussion of how much information the LLM response should include. Although there was general agreement that the output should not be excessively long, the presenting editor found it unsatisfactory that the response lacked explanation of central terms. In a related case, an editor noticed that a response related to a form of neurodivergence lacked important information that would introduce necessary nuance to avoid strengthening existing negative biases related to the neurodivergence in question. The issue was not that the information provided was untrue, but that it was insufficient to effectively combat the propagation of unwarranted negative bias. When asked about the consequence of an LLM output potentially worsening a negative bias in this way, the editors agreed that, as one participant put it, “that would be very problematic because I think you have to consider the chatbot to be part of the encyclopedia.” This same participant added that in such a case, the output “will have to be more complex. It will have to be longer.”

Instead, an optimal structure would present a brief overall conclusion first—including, if relevant, rejection or redirection to other services—and only then provide a more in-depth answer below. The leading conclusion should also, at least for sufficiently complex queries, contain a disambiguation and contextualization of the topics covered. As one editor stressed, “we contextualize anything we describe, whether it is a concept, a person, or an era, we always contextualize it chronologically and geographically.” Following such a structure, leading with a conclusion containing some contextualization, would correspond to existing guidelines used for the underlying encyclopedic entries.

此外，编辑们还评论了输出的相对较高的语言复杂性，这在很大程度上与底层百科全书条目的语气相对应。主要结论和情境化的建议很大程度上是为了吸引那些可能不习惯阅读具有更高细节水平和不太明确结论的较长输出的读者。然而，正如下面显而易见的那样，与据称更具吸引力的传播形式相比，编辑们在很大程度上赞成遵循正式且有点学术性的语气和风格。In addition, the editors commented on the relatively high linguistic complexity of the outputs, which largely corresponded to the tone of the underlying encyclopedic entries. The suggestion of a leading conclusion and contextualization was largely informed by a desire to appeal to readers who may not be comfortable reading longer outputs with higher levels of detail and less clear conclusions. However, as becomes evident below, the editors were largely in favor of conforming to a formal and somewhat academic tone and style, compared to more supposedly-engaging forms of dissemination.

4.3.2. 对制造的边缘情况做出反应Reacting to Manufactured Edge-cases

在研讨会的第二部分，参与者评估了两位参与研讨会的研究人员在研讨会之前做出的四项回应。这些人为的回复是使用 Claude Sonnet 4.6 生成的，并带有故意突破风格和交际语域界限的提示。这四个提示模仿了不同的角色：一个简单语言的事实助理，一个叙事科普传播者，一个针对中学生的同伴注册助理，以及一个使用表情符号和注意力经济修辞的社交媒体风格的传播者。这些回应并非以百科全书的源材料为基础，因此，重点较少关注输出与基础材料之间的对应关系，而更多关注语气和风格。人物角色提示的编写目的是打破现有的语言实践并引发边界设定。百科全书的语言复杂性是研讨会 1 和研讨会 2 第一部分的主题，角色提示有助于指定编辑认为合适的语言更改类型。In the second part of the workshop, participants evaluated four responses generated prior to the workshop by the two researchers participating. These manufactured responses were generated using Claude Sonnet 4.6 with prompts deliberately intended to push the boundaries of style and communicative register. The four prompts mimicked distinct personas: a plain-language factual assistant, a narrative popular-science communicator, a peer-register assistant aimed at middle-school students, and a social-media-style communicator using emojis and an attention-economy rhetoric. These responses were not grounded in the source material of the encyclopedia and, accordingly, focus was less on the correspondence between output and underlying material and more on tone and style. The persona prompts were written with the purpose of breaching existing linguistic practices and provoking boundary-setting. The linguistic complexity of the encyclopedia was a theme in both Workshop 1 and in the first part of Workshop 2, and the persona prompts were instrumental in specifying what kind of linguistic changes the editors would deem appropriate.

这项活动引起了强烈反响，并揭示了一些尚未阐明的隐含原则。参与者拒绝任何可能被视为居高临下或居高临下的回应，使用比喻或叙述性语言，对读者的背景或理解做出假设，或采用对话式的“闲聊”语气。尽管编辑们一致认为，编造的回复在传达哪些信息以及传达多少信息方面结构良好，但他们也同意，正如一位参与者所说，“语气不知何故有些不对劲”。具体来说，参与者对第一人称和第二人称代词的使用做出了反应，这些代词通常构成大多数基于法学硕士的产品中已知的聊天格式。正如一位参与者所说，“（……）一旦你开始直接向读者讲话，事情就会变得困难，对吧？（……）当你开始试图深入读者的头脑时，我认为你的立场相当不稳定。”这与基于 LLM 的接口的传统实现存在明显的风格差异。The exercise elicited strong reactions and surfaced several implicit principles that had not yet been articulated. Participants rejected any response that could be perceived as patronizing or condescending, employed figurative or narrative language, made assumptions about the reader’s background or understanding, or adopted a conversational, “chatty” tone. Although the editors agreed that the manufactured responses were well-constructed in terms of what information—and how much information—they relayed, they also agreed that, as one participant put it, “the tone is off somehow.” Specifically, participants reacted to the use of first- and second-person pronouns that typically structure the chat format known from most LLM-based products. As one participant argued, “(…) as soon as you start addressing the reader directly, it gets difficult, right? (…) when you start trying to crawl into the reader’s head, you’re on rather shaky ground, I think.” This is a clear stylistic divergence from conventional implementation of LLM-based interfaces.

这些反应反映了研讨会始终贯穿的专业态度：作为在制度上对传播质量负责的一方，编辑们对系统可以做的事情设定了限制，而其他利益相关者——管理层、设计师和最终用户——则有责任在这些限制内推动所做的事情。尽管内容可能仍然是首要关注的问题，但编辑们对边缘案例的反应几乎是发自内心的，就好像百科全书的身份受到威胁一样。这表明公共服务机构的作用不仅体现在风格上，也体现在内容上。尽管编辑们的目标是吸引那些无法直观地理解百科全书语气的新读者，但编辑们还是准备牺牲（一些）读者群，以保留他们认为合适的语气。一位参与者明确承认，负责任的传播可能会给读者带来更多的工作量：“我们These reactions reflect a professional disposition that ran consistently through the workshop: as the party institutionally accountable for the quality of the dissemination, editors set limits on what the system may do, while it falls to other stakeholders—management, designers, and ultimately users—to push what is done within those limits. Although content may remain the primary concern, for good reason, the editors reacted almost viscerally to the edge cases as though the very identity of the encyclopedia was at stake. This indicates that the role of public service institutions is as much carried through style as content. Despite having an ambition to reach new audiences who do not intuitively connect to the encyclopedic tone of voice, the editors were prepared to sacrifice (some) readership in favor of preserving a tone that they thought was fitting. The notion that responsible dissemination might cause more work for the reader was acknowledged explicitly by one of the participants: “We是are一本百科全书，我们可以要求读者付出一些努力。没关系。”当然，编辑的意见并不是管理性的，也可能不符合百科全书的整体战略，但从编辑角度来说，它们表明了公共服务机构是什么。从这个意义上说，风格和呈现问题成为公共服务道德以及这种道德对接受服务的公众提出的要求的指标。an encyclopedia, and it’s okay for us to require our reader put in some work. That’s okay.” Of course, the editors’ opinions are not managerial in kind and may not correspond to the overall strategy of the encyclopedia at large, but they are indicative of what a public service institution is, editorially speaking. In this sense, the question of style and presentation becomes an indicator of the ethic of public service and the demands that such an ethic puts on the public that receives the service.

使用角色提示来浮现边缘案例导致了重要的编辑边界的阐明，而编辑们在研讨会 1 或研讨会 2 的第一部分期间未能成功地用语言表达或什至认识到这些边界，尽管风格问题在这两种情况下都出现了。通过这种方式，引入故意错位的输出非常适合激活否则无法获得的隐性知识。The use of persona prompts to surface edge cases resulted in the articulation of nontrivial editorial boundaries that the editors had not succeeded in verbalizing, or even recognizing, during either Workshop 1 or during the first part of Workshop 2, even though the question of style had come up on both occasions. In this way, the introduction of purposefully misaligned output was well-suited for activating tacit knowledge that was not available otherwise.

5. 讨论Discussion

编辑协调研讨会最引人注目的成果之一是所出现的编辑标准的特征。当编辑直接处理法学硕士生成的文本并集体审议应该做什么和不应该做什么时，最终的标准比最初预期更接近管理百科全书文章的要求。没有直接的讲话，没有对读者背景或先验知识的假设，没有比喻或叙述性语言，没有情感语域，没有不受源材料支持的评价判断：总而言之，这些原则与项目中迄今为止所追求的聊天机器人格式的对话可供性存在明显的紧张关系。虽然这可以被解读为该方法的局限性，或者作为编辑未能富有想象力地利用该技术的可能性的证据，但我们认为这是一个核心发现：表明基于实际专业实践而不是抽象咨询的编辑协调过程表面上真正的制度价值而不是社会期望的普遍性。One of the more striking outcomes of the editorial alignment workshops was the character of the editorial standard that emerged. When editors worked directly with LLM-generated text and collectively deliberated on what it should and should not do, the resulting standard was closer to the requirements governing encyclopedia articles proper than initially expected. No direct address, no assumptions about the reader’s background or prior knowledge, no figurative or narrative language, no emotional register, no evaluative judgment unsupported by the source material: taken together, these principles stand in clear tension with the conversational affordances of the chatbot format that had been pursued up to that point in the project. While this could be read as a limitation of the method, or as evidence that editors failed to imaginatively engage the possibilities of the technology, we argue instead that it is a central finding: an indication that an editorial alignment process grounded in actual professional practice, rather than abstract consultation, surfaces genuine institutional values rather than socially desirable generalities.

这种保守主义并不是编辑实践的偶然现象，而是编辑实践的组成部分。参与的编辑对机构传播的知识的可信度负有专业责任，他们的标准反映了责任而非可及性的导向。正如一位参与者所说，百科全书是一个“你可以找到可靠知识的地方”，而法学硕士生成的文本如果偏离这些标准，无论多么容易理解或吸引人，都有可能破坏将百科全书与其他信息来源区分开来的机构权威。正如章节中提到的This conservatism is not incidental to editorial practice but constitutive of it. The editors who participated are professionally accountable for the trustworthiness of the knowledge the institution disseminates, and their standards reflect an orientation toward responsibility over accessibility. As one participant put it, the encyclopedia is a place “where you can go to find reliable knowledge”—and LLM-generated text that departs from these criteria, however accessible or engaging, risks undermining precisely the institutional authority that distinguishes the encyclopedia from other information sources. As mentioned in Section4.3.2正是出于这个原因，编辑们不愿意直接向读者讲话或试图“爬进读者的头脑”。与此同时，保守主义并不是唯一的价值。虽然管理层认为传播的可信度至关重要，但他们同样甚至可能更加重视可访问性，特别是对于年轻和受教育程度较低的受众，并且更愿意接受与正式百科全书式的文体背离，以实现这一目标。我们建议不要将这种紧张视为设计工作进行之前必须解决的问题，而是将其更准确地理解为设计工作本身。编辑一致性作为一种实践，是一种管理竞争性和部分不可通约价值的练习——正是将设计定义为与规范不同的条件, the editors were reluctant to address readers directly or attempt to “crawl into the reader’s head” for exactly this reason. At the same time, conservatism is not the only value in play. While management regards the trustworthiness of the dissemination as essential, they place equal or perhaps even greater emphasis on accessibility, particularly for younger and less educated audiences, and are more willing to accept stylistic departures from the formal encyclopedic register in service of this goal. Rather than viewing this tension as a problem that must be resolved before design work can proceed, we suggest it is more accurately understood as the design work itself. Editorial alignment, as a practice, is an exercise in managing competing and partially incommensurable values—precisely the condition that defines design as distinct from specification(Buchanan, 1992)。构建这一点的一种有效方法是将编辑实践理解为建立负责任传播的外部边界：系统可以在不损害机构可信度的情况下运行的限制。在这些界限内，还有进一步设计工作的空间——除其他外，涉及系统如何应对不同的受众以及如何处理百科全书式和对话式语域之间的紧张关系——这涉及编辑团队以外的利益相关者。这个框架将编辑的贡献放在最可靠的位置，同时承认它并没有穷尽设计问题。. One productive way to frame this is to understand editorial practice as establishing the outer boundaries of responsible dissemination: the limits within which the system may operate without jeopardizing institutional trustworthiness. Within those boundaries, there is latitude for further design work—concerning, among other things, how the system addresses different audiences and navigates the tension between encyclopedic and conversational registers—that involves stakeholders beyond the editorial team. This framing situates the editors’ contribution where it is most reliable, while acknowledging that it does not exhaust the design problem.

本案例研究中进行的编辑协调过程提出了一个严重的问题，即它对 PD 的承诺使其难以回避：它是否真的逃脱了它所批评的数据源动态？批评，在章节中展开The editorial alignment process as conducted in this case study raises a serious issue that its commitment to PD makes it difficult to sidestep: does it actually escape the data-sourcing dynamic it critiques? The critique, developed in Section2.2，认为参与人工智能开发的风险被简化为提取人类偏好，然后由开发人员以让原始参与者对结果没有真正代理权的方式进行操作, holds that participation in AI development risks being reduced to the extraction of human preferences, which are then operationalized by developers in ways that leave the original participants without genuine agency over the result(Birhane et al., 2022)。这里描述的研讨会代表了一种更实质性的参与形式：编辑们不仅生成偏好数据，而且集体审议原则，质疑彼此的判断，并制作出承载其专业推理分量的设计工件。编辑标准将对系统的实施产生真实、具体的影响。然而，该项目归管理层所有，而不是编辑团队，并且编辑标准可以被推翻或悄悄取消优先级，而无需任何正式的编辑批准要求。编辑在设计过程中被赋予了影响力——但他们并没有被赋予控制它的权力。这使得数据源安排的基本不对称性在结构上完好无损，即使该安排中的参与质量更高。这并不会使编辑一致性作为一种实践无效，但它确实确定了这种实践的实例在哪里未能达到其自身的愿望，以及需要进行哪些制度变革来缩小这一差距。. The workshops described here represent a more substantive form of engagement: editors did not merely generate preference data but collectively deliberated on principles, contested each other’s judgments, and produced a design artifact that carries the weight of their professional reasoning. The editorial standard will have real, concrete consequences for the system’s implementation. And yet the project is owned by management, not the editorial team, and the editorial standard can be overridden or quietly de-prioritized without any formal requirement for editorial ratification. Editors were given influence in the design process—they were not given power over it. This leaves the fundamental asymmetry of the data-sourcing arrangement structurally intact, even when the quality of participation within that arrangement is more substantial. This does not invalidate editorial alignment as a practice, but it does identify where this instantiation of the practice falls short of its own aspirations, and what institutional changes that would be required to close this gap.

鉴于这一差距，谁参与编辑协调以及编辑为何特别集中的问题值得进一步反思。 PAI 的承诺源自斯堪的纳维亚的 PD 传统，在其早期发展过程中提供了具有启发性的历史相似之处。在这里，重点是受技术变革最直接影响的工人——排字工，在典型的报纸案例中——因为他们的技能、生计和职业责任最直接受到威胁The question of who participates in editorial alignment, and why editors are centered specifically, warrants further reflection in light of this gap. The Scandinavian PD tradition, from which PAI draws its commitments, offers an instructive historical parallel in its early development. Here, focus was on the workers most directly affected by technological transformation—typesetters, in a paradigmatic newspaper case—on the grounds that their skills, their livelihoods, and their professional accountability were most immediately at stake(Bødker et al., 2021; Elmqvist et al., 2025)。从本质上讲，这是一种认知和道德选择，而不是务实选择：对工作质量承担责任的工人被理解为参与对最终系统的完整性最重要的各方。同样的逻辑也适用于编辑对齐。编辑是对机构知识传播的质量和责任负有专业责任的从业者——他们每天在实践中运用的判断构成了机构的知识权威，而他们的专业角色受到向法学硕士媒介传播的转变最直接的威胁。因此，以他们的参与为中心遵循编辑管理的公共知识机构的制度逻辑及其民主问责制。然而，值得注意的是，早期斯堪的纳维亚民主党对工人的关注也让其他政党——读者、作家和这些机构服务的更广泛的公众——在很大程度上处于参与过程之外。是否以及如何将编辑协调的参与范围扩大到编辑团队之外，扩展到该机构所服务的受众，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题，本案例研究尚未解决——尽管 PAI 领域似乎已经做好了应对这一问题的准备。. This was, at its core, an epistemic and ethical choice rather than a pragmatic one: the workers who bore the accountability for the quality of the work were understood to be the parties whose participation was most consequential for the integrity of the resulting system. The same logic applies to editorial alignment. Editors are the practitioners professionally accountable for the quality and responsibility of the institution’s knowledge dissemination—it is their judgment, exercised daily in practice, that constitutes the institution’s intellectual authority, and it is their professional role that is most directly threatened by the shift to LLM-mediated dissemination. Centering their participation thus follows from the institutional logic of editorially governed public knowledge institutions and their democratic accountability. It is worth noting, however, that early Scandinavian PD’s focus on workers also left other parties—readers, writers, and the broader public served by these institutions—largely outside the participatory process. Whether and how to extend participation in editorial alignment beyond the editorial team, toward the audiences the institution exists to serve, remains an open question that the present case study does not resolve—though one that the field of PAI seems well positioned to take up.

对于编辑联盟来说，缩小影响力和权力之间的差距，并为编辑提供 LLM 体系的真正所有权，而不是在其发展中发挥咨询作用，意味着什么？我们的分析表明了几个含义。最直接的是，它将要求编辑们获得该项目的正式共同所有权——这是一个结构性信号，表明他们的专业判断在系统方向决策中对管理和研究观点具有同等的机构影响力。更根本的是，它需要将编辑标准重新概念化，不仅仅是在研讨会上制作的设计制品，而是更接近宪法文件的东西：一组原则，如果没有正式的编辑过程就不能被推翻，类似于为百科全书内容的编写和编辑维护的现有标准，因此作为持续编辑参与的锚点，而不是对设计项目的一次性贡献。让编辑一致性研讨会具有重复性——编辑定期审查人工智能生成的输出，以评估对标准的遵守情况并发现新出现的不一致——可能是在编辑实践和系统一致性之间建立紧密联系的一种方法，这种联系有两个不同的目的：作为维护和改进系统一致性的技术机制，以及作为一种结构化的、经常性的专业参与形式，使编辑团队保持积极和反思性的形式，而不仅仅是与他们帮助塑造的系统保持历史关系。What would it mean for editorial alignment to close the gap between influence and power, and to provide editors with genuine ownership of the LLM system rather than a consultative role in its development? Our analysis suggests several implications. Most immediately, it would require that editors be given formal co-ownership of the project—a structural signal that their professional judgment carries equal institutional weight to management and research perspectives in decisions about the system’s direction. More fundamentally, it would require reconceptualizing the editorial standard not merely as a design artifact produced in a workshop, but as something closer to a constitutional document: a set of principles that cannot be overridden without a formal editorial process, analogous to the existing standard maintained for the writing and editing of encyclopedic content, and that therefore serves as an anchor for ongoing editorial participation rather than a one-time contribution to a design project. Giving the editorial alignment workshop a recurring character—where editors periodically review AI-generated outputs both to evaluate compliance with the standard and to surface emerging misalignments—could be a way to establish a tight link between editorial practice and system alignment that serves two distinct purposes: as a technical mechanism for maintaining and improving system alignment, and as a structured, recurring form of professional participation that keeps the editorial team in an active and reflexive rather than merely historical relationship with the system they helped shape(Arzberger et al., 2026)。总之，这些安排将更充分地实现 PAI 的建议以及编辑管理的知识机构的制度逻辑的要求：对知识传播质量负责的从业者也管理知识传播日益发生的系统。正如本案例研究中所实践的那样，编辑对齐是朝着这种安排迈出的一步。它是否能到达那里，与其说取决于设计实践本身，不如说取决于相应重组治理的机构意愿。. Together, these arrangements would more fully realize what PAI recommends and what the institutional logic of editorially governed knowledge institutions demands: that the practitioners accountable for the quality of knowledge dissemination also govern the systems through which that dissemination increasingly occurs. Editorial alignment, as practiced in this case study, is a step toward that arrangement. Whether it arrives there depends less on the design practice itself than on the institutional will to restructure governance accordingly.

6. 结论Conclusion

向以法学硕士为媒介的知识传播的转变给公共知识机构带来了结构性挑战，这些机构的权威取决于编辑责任：如何在不放弃作为机构可信度基础的编辑职能的情况下整合这些技术。本文提出编辑对齐作为参与式人工智能中的一种设计实践，通过将人工智能对齐视为一种协作的、以实践为中心的设计活动来应对这一挑战，而不是将人工智能对齐视为技术优化问题，而是与体现系统应反映的制度价值观的编辑一起进行的、以实践为中心的设计活动。通过北欧在线百科全书的案例研究，我们展示了基于具体编辑工作而不是抽象咨询的参与式研讨会流程如何能够浮现默契的专业审议并将其转化为编辑标准，该标准同时充当机构价值观的代表和技术实施的一组协调目标。该案还暴露了一个未解决的紧张局势：赋予编辑在设计过程中的影响力本身并不赋予他们对系统治理的权力，而且如果没有结构性变化——正式的共同所有权、定期的编辑审查流程以及具有宪法性而非仅仅咨询地位的编辑标准——编辑一致性不足以从根本上打破人工智能中构建大量参与性参与的数据源动态。因此，本文的贡献是一种设计实践，编辑管理机构可以利用该实践来建立负责任的法学硕士介导的传播边界，并认识到要实现这种方法的全部潜力，需要进行制度安排，在这种制度安排中，编辑不仅要接受咨询，而且要真正获得授权。The shift toward LLM-mediated knowledge dissemination poses a structural challenge for public knowledge institutions whose authority rests on editorial accountability: how to integrate these technologies without ceding the editorial function that grounds the institution’s trustworthiness. This paper has proposed editorial alignment as a design practice within participatory AI that addresses this challenge by treating AI alignment not as a technical optimization problem but as a collaborative, practice-centered design activity conducted with and by the editors who embody the institutional values the system should reflect. Through a case study with a Nordic online encyclopedia, we have shown how a participatory workshop process, grounded in concrete editorial work rather than abstract consultation, can surface tacit professional deliberation and translate it into an editorial standard that functions simultaneously as a representation of institutional values and as a set of alignment objectives for technical implementation. The case also surfaces an unresolved tension: giving editors influence in the design process does not, by itself, give them power over the system’s governance, and without structural changes—formal co-ownership, a recurring editorial review process, and an editorial standard that carries constitutional rather than merely advisory status—editorial alignment is insufficient to substantially break with the data-sourcing dynamic that structured much participatory involvement in AI. The contribution of this paper is accordingly a design practice that editorially governed institutions can use to establish the boundaries of responsible LLM-mediated dissemination, with the recognition that realizing the full potential of this approach demands institutional arrangements in which editors are not only consulted but genuinely empowered.
