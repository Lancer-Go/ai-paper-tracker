1 简介Introduction

LLM 取消学习要求模型抑制目标行为或知识集群，同时保留非目标能力。现有方法通常将本地更新应用于训练后模型，例如遗忘示例的梯度上升、保留感知变体、负面偏好目标或表示控制。这些工具通常在通过监督微调引入或强调目标行为的环境中进行评估。这种评估机制留下了一个实际问题：当 RLVR 诱发行为时，相同的遗忘目标是否仍然合适？LLM unlearning asks a model to suppress a target behavior or knowledge cluster while preserving non-target capabilities. Existing methods often apply local updates to a post-trained model, such as gradient ascent on forget examples, retain-aware variants, negative preference objectives, or representation control. These tools are commonly evaluated in settings where the target behavior was introduced or emphasized through supervised fine-tuning. That evaluation regime leaves open a practical question: do the same unlearning targets remain appropriate when the behavior was induced by RLVR?

这个问题很重要，因为 RLVR 不仅仅是具有不同损失的 SFT。最近的工作表明，RLVR 可以通过特定方向的概率变化或偏离原理的更新组件来改变模型行为，而不是通过 SFT 强调的相同的高方差方向This question matters because RLVR is not merely SFT with a different loss. Recent work suggests that RLVR can shift model behavior through direction-specific probability changes or off-principal update components, rather than through the same high-variance directions emphasized by SFT[16, 4, 13]。如果标准的忘却更新隐式地针对类似 SFT 的方向，那么它们可以移动准确度数字，同时仍然更新错误的行为子空间或造成可避免的附带损害。. If standard unlearning updates implicitly target SFT-like directions, then they can move accuracy numbers while still updating the wrong behavioral subspace or causing avoidable collateral damage.

我们在两个模型族的受控数学推理设置中研究了这个假设。从 Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B（主要）和 Qwen3-1.7B-Base（跨模型）开始，该协议在匹配的 GSM8K 数据上从相同的基础模型构建 SFT 和 RLVR 检查点。然后，它会评估 MATH 类别划分的遗忘情况，同时测量非目标 MATH 和 GSM8K 性能。我们在方向意义上使用“错误目标”：标准忘记更新与 RLVR 实际移动的方向不一致。We study this hypothesis in a controlled mathematical reasoning setting on two model families. Starting from Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B (primary) and Qwen3-1.7B-Base (cross-model), the protocol constructs SFT and RLVR checkpoints from the same base model on matched GSM8K data. It then evaluates forgetting on MATH category splits while measuring non-target MATH and GSM8K performance. We use “mis-targeting” in a directional sense: the standard forget update is poorly aligned with the directions RLVR actually moves.

该论文做出了四项贡献，在主要模型上进行了验证，并在第二个模型系列上得到了证实：The paper makes four contributions, validated on the primary model and corroborated on a second model family:

  1. 1.

    机制分离。Mechanism separation.在匹配的目标精度下，SFT 更新和 SFT 到 RLVR 增量可通过标记级别的增量对数概率来区分：SFT 是现有延续的正似然提升，而 RLVR 增量是平衡的且特定于方向。第二个模型上再现了相同的方向。Under matched target accuracy, the SFT update and the SFT-to-RLVR increment are distinguishable by token-level delta-log-probability: SFT is a positive likelihood boost on existing continuations, while the RLVR increment is balanced and direction-specific. The same direction reproduces on the second model.

  2. 2.

    没有用于全参数取消学习的干净操作点。No clean operating point for full-parameter unlearning.RLVR 检查点上的标准全参数梯度上升只会对保留 MATH 和 GSM8K 造成较大的附带损害，从而降低目标指标。该边界在两个模型上都重现，并表明仅忘记准确度并不足以作为忘记学习的信号。Standard full-parameter gradient ascent on the RLVR checkpoint reduces the target metric only by inflicting large collateral damage on both retain MATH and GSM8K. This boundary reproduces on both models and shows that forget accuracy alone is not a sufficient unlearning signal.

2 相关工作Related Work

LLM 忘却学习。LLM unlearning.

LLM 忘却方法包括梯度上升、保留感知目标、偏好型消极目标（例如 NPO 和 SimNPO）以及表示控制方法（例如 RMU）LLM unlearning methods include gradient ascent, retain-aware objectives, preference-style negative objectives such as NPO and SimNPO, and representation-control methods such as RMU[15, 1, 7]。 TOFU 和 WMDP 等基准强调必须结合遗忘和保留效用来评估遗忘. Benchmarks such as TOFU and WMDP emphasize that unlearning must be evaluated jointly on forgetting and retained utility[8, 7, 12]。本文的不同之处在于关注训练后源不匹配：目标行为由 SFT 或 RLVR 引起，而取消学习工具保持固定。. This paper differs by focusing on a post-training source mismatch: the target behavior is induced by either SFT or RLVR, while the unlearning tool is held fixed.

RLVR 和推理更新。RLVR and reasoning updates.

RLVR 的推理工作提出了两个相关点。首先，能力边界分析认为 RLVR 可能会改变解决方案的采样效率或概率质量，而不是创造全新的能力RLVR work on reasoning raises two relevant points. First, capability-boundary analyses argue that RLVR may change sampling efficiency or probability mass over solutions rather than create wholly new capabilities[13]。其次，几何和令牌方向研究表明 RLVR 更新可以存在于非原理或特定方向的组件中. Second, geometry and token-direction studies suggest that RLVR updates can live in off-principal or direction-specific components[16, 4, 14].

模型差异和副作用。Model diffing and side effects.

模型差异和稀疏差异工具为本地化微调效果和预测副作用提供了更广泛的背景Model diffing and sparse-difference tools provide a broader context for localizing fine-tuning effects and predicting side effects[5, 6]。我们对 delta 对数概率、更新几何和梯度对齐的使用具有类似的诊断作用，但实验目标是 SFT 引起的推理和 RLVR 引起的推理之间无法学习的不匹配。我们的目标张量排名遵循知识编辑方法的本地化和特异性范例. Our use of delta log-probability, update geometry, and gradient alignment is similarly diagnostic, but the experimental target is the unlearning mismatch between SFT-induced and RLVR-induced reasoning. Our target-tensor ranking follows the localization-and-specificity paradigm of knowledge-editing methods[9, 10, 2]，它验证了针对随机位置控制和反向消融的局部干预，而不是仅通过编辑幅度。, which validate a localized intervention against random-location controls and reverse ablations rather than by edit magnitude alone.

5 机理分析Mechanism Analysis

我们使用三种诊断来测试遗忘失配是否是定向的。We use three diagnostics to test whether the unlearning mismatch is directional.

更新几何图形。Update geometry.

对于每个检查点，我们计算检查点之间的分层能量增量，并将增量投影到基础权重顶部奇异子空间上。两个更新几乎完全偏离基本权重主方向：与基本张量的前导奇异方向正交的更新能量分数（偏离主方向）For each checkpoint, we compute layerwise delta energy between checkpoints and project deltas onto the base-weight top singular subspace. Both updates lie almost entirely off the base-weight principal directions: the fraction of update energy orthogonal to the base tensor’s leading singular directions (the off-principal fractionρt\rho_{t}) 两者均为 0.9998，确认增量是特定于方向的；该几何形状为选择器选择目标张量提供了来源。) is 0.9998 for both, confirming the increment is direction-specific; this geometry provides the selector’s provenance for choosing target tensors.

SFT produces a positive mean delta-log-probability of 0.4477 and a positive-delta fraction of 0.7296. The SFT-to-RLVR increment has mean delta-log-probability -0.0002 and positive-delta fraction 0.3995. This indicates that the RLVR increment is not simply another SFT-like likelihood boost on the same continuations; it redistributes probability in a more balanced and direction-specific way. 11 结论Conclusion

在两个模型系列中，匹配的 SFT/RLVR 控制表明，SFT 到 RLVR 的增量与令牌级概率结构中的 SFT 更新不同，并且标准全参数遗忘在 RLVR 检查点上没有干净的操作点 - 它只会通过造成较大的附带损害来遗忘。 MAST 将这种诊断转化为一种方法：通过机制得分对注意力投影张量进行排名，并将更新限制为排名最高的子集，它在主要模型上的接近零的抵押品上有意义地忘记了（McNemarAcross two model families, matched SFT/RLVR controls show that the SFT-to-RLVR increment differs from the SFT update in token-level probability structure, and that standard full-parameter unlearning has no clean operating point on the RLVR checkpoint—it forgets only by inflicting large collateral damage. MAST turns this diagnosis into a method: ranking attention-projection tensors by a mechanism score and restricting the update to the top-ranked subset, it forgets meaningfully at near-zero collateral on the primary model (McNemarp=0.0078p=0.0078）并且在两个模型上都比全参数遗忘产生了统计上显着的附带优势。我们的诊断还暴露了一个普遍的评估陷阱：更新较低的解决方案轨迹可能性，同时提高最终答案的可能性，因此仅忘记准确性和仅答案可能性的视图都是不完整的。) and yields a statistically significant collateral advantage over full-parameter unlearning on both models. Our diagnostics also expose a general evaluation pitfall: updates lower solution-trajectory likelihood while raising final-answer likelihood, so forget-accuracy-only and answer-likelihood-only views are each incomplete.
