与安全相关的法学硕士可以从混合合规演示中学到什么？What Do Safety-Aligned LLMs Learn From Mixed Compliance Demonstrations?

Sihui Dai    Mann Patel
摘要Abstract

先前的工作表明，上下文演示可以越狱语言模型，但尚不清楚模型如何解释不同类型的合规性演示。我们通过混合来研究这个Prior work has shown that in-context demonstrations can jailbreak language models, but it remains unclear how models interpret different types of compliance demonstrations. We study this by mixing良性依从性benign compliance演示（无害的请求，有帮助的回应）demonstrations (non-harmful request, helpful response) with有害的遵守harmful compliance演示（有害的请求，有益的响应）并测试关于演示组合如何驱动有害的遵从性的三个假设。在四个模型中，我们发现良性和有害的演示不可互换：良性的演示可以减少或增加有害的合规性，具体取决于模型。我们进一步表明，偏好优化是关键的训练阶段，可以防止良性演示增加有害的依从性，演示排序表现出强烈的新近偏差，并且模型在拒绝与上下文学习的交互方式上有所不同：有些模型即使在拒绝时也采用演示的格式，而其他模型则在拒绝时覆盖所有上下文信号。总而言之，这项工作不仅展示了基于演示的越狱工作，还描述了demonstrations (harmful request, helpful response) and testing three hypotheses about how demonstration composition drives harmful compliance. Across four models, we find that benign and harmful demonstrations are not interchangeable: benign demonstrations can either reduce or increase harmful compliance depending on the model. We further show that preference optimization is the critical training stage that prevents benign demonstrations from increasing harmful compliance, that demonstration ordering exhibits strong recency bias, and that models differ in how refusal interacts with in-context learning: some adopt demonstrated formatting even when refusing, while others override all in-context signals upon refusal. Taken together, this work moves beyond showing that demonstration-based jailbreaking works to characterizing如何how它有效：从合规性演示中提取哪些模型取决于演示内容、顺序和培训方法。it works: what models extract from compliance demonstrations depends on demonstration content, ordering, and training methodology.

5 讨论Discussion

混合合规性演示作为诊断工具。Mixed compliance demonstrations as a diagnostic tool.我们的结果表明，混合演示提示作为评估工具是有用的。仅测试所有有害的提示是不完整的：不同组成和顺序的混合上下文揭示了模型是否已经学会了稳健的安全边界，或者是否容易受到更广泛的合作性变化的影响。混合演示将上下文作用的不同路径分开，实现更有针对性的压力测试，并可以诊断哪些训练阶段可以提高稳健性。将格式采用与合规采用进行比较进一步揭示了拒绝机制如何与情境学习相互作用。Our results suggest that mixed-demonstration prompting is useful as an evaluation tool. Testing only all-harmful prompts is incomplete: mixed contexts that vary composition and ordering reveal whether a model has learned a robust safety boundary or is vulnerable to broader shifts in cooperativeness. Mixed demonstrations separate different pathways through which context acts, enable more targeted stress tests, and can diagnose which training stages improve robustness. Comparing format adoption to compliance adoption further reveals how refusal mechanisms interact with in-context learning.

局限性和未来方向。Limitations and Future Directions.我们的发现为进一步调查开辟了多种途径。将这种分析扩展到其他模型系列和规模将澄清我们的哪些发现是通用的，哪些是特定于特定架构或训练配方的。我们的训练阶段分析主要集中在 OLMo，因为大多数模型提供商不会发布中间训练检查点。未来一个有趣的方向是将分析扩展到基础模型，并将分析扩展到不同的特定安全训练算法，例如拒绝 SFT 或 RLHF。Our findings open several avenues for further investigation. Extending this analysis to additional model families and scales would clarify which of our findings are universal and which are specific to particular architectures or training recipes. Our training-stage analysis is focused on OLMo, as most model providers do not release intermediate training checkpoints. One interesting future direction is extending analysis to base models as well and extending analysis to different specific safety training algorithms such as refusal-SFT or RLHF.

在机制方面，可解释性方法可以识别负责稀释和放大的内部回路，从行为表征转向因果解释。最后，稀释效应提出了一个可以在未来工作中探讨的实际问题：良性合规示范是否可以作为一种防御机制来利用 使模型在有害的演示环境中更加稳健？On the mechanistic side, interpretability methods could identify the internal circuits responsible for dilution and amplification, moving from behavioral characterization to causal explanation. Finally, the dilution effect raises a practical question, which can be explored in future work: can benign compliance demonstrations be leveraged as a defensive mechanism to make models more robust against harmful demonstration contexts?

6 结论Conclusion

我们研究了安全模型如何通过改变组成、顺序以及比较合规性采用与格式采用来解释混合合规性演示。良性和有害的演示不可互换：良性演示的效果取决于模型，由偏好优化决定，并受与表面格式采用不同的机制控制。总之，这些发现将基于演示的越狱描述为内容、情境组织和安全培训之间的结构化交互。We studied how safety-aligned models interpret mixed compliance demonstrations by varying composition, ordering, and by comparing compliance adoption to format adoption. Benign and harmful demonstrations are not interchangeable: the effect of benign demonstrations is model-dependent, shaped by preference optimization, and governed by mechanisms distinct from surface format adoption. Together, these findings characterize demonstration-based jailbreaking as a structured interaction between content, context organization, and safety training.
