为了获得更好的性能而进行的配置调整对于质量保证至关重要。然而，由于可配置系统的黑匣子性质，调谐器的有效性长期以来一直是个谜。先前的工作主要采用静态域分析（例如静态污点分析），这通常缺乏通用性，或者动态数据分析（例如基准性能分析），限制了可解释性。在这项工作中，我们采用健身景观分析（FLA）作为领域知识和调整难度之间的桥梁。我们提出了 Domland，这是一种双管齐下的方法，可以将从 FLA 和领域驱动分析中获得的空间信息相结合，系统地捕获配置调整案例的隐藏特征，解释调整器如何以及为何会成功或失败。这有助于更好地解释调谐器的行为并将其置于上下文中，并为调谐器设计提供信息。为了评估 Domland，我们对 9 个软件系统和 93 个工作负载进行了案例研究，从中我们揭示了几个关键发现：（1）配置景观本质上是特定于系统的，没有单一领域因素（例如系统区域、编程语言或资源强度）一致地塑造其结构； （2）控制主要功能流程的核心选项（例如x264的pic-struct）与资源选项（例如x264的cpu-independent）相比，对景观坚固性（即调优难度）的影响更大； (3) 工作量对景观结构的影响并不完全与类型或规模相关。两者都会导致景观变化，但其影响取决于系统。Configuration tuning for better performance is crucial in quality assurance. Yet, there has long been a mystery on tuners’ effectiveness, due to the black-box nature of configurable systems. Prior efforts predominantly adopt static domain analysis (e.g., static taint analysis), which often lacks generalizability, or dynamic data analysis (e.g., benchmarking performance analysis), limiting explainability. In this work, we embrace Fitness Landscape Analysis (FLA) as a bridge between domain knowledge and difficulty of the tuning. We propose Domland, a two-pronged methodology that synergizes the spatial information obtained from FLA and domain-driven analysis to systematically capture the hidden characteristics of configuration tuning cases, explaining how and why a tuner might succeed or fail. This helps to better interpret and contextualize the behavior of tuners and inform tuner design. To evaluate Domland, we conduct a case study of nine software systems and 93 workloads, from which we reveal several key findings: (1) configuration landscapes are inherently system-specific, with no single domain factor (e.g., system area, programming language, or resource intensity) consistently shaping their structure; (2) the core options (e.g., pic-struct of x264), which control the main functional flows, exert a stronger influence on landscape ruggedness (i.e. the difficulty of tuning) compared to resource options (e.g., cpu-independent of x264); (3) Workload effects on landscape structure are not uniformly tied to type or scale. Both contribute to landscape variations, but their impact is system-dependent.

1 简介1 Introduction

软件系统具有高度可配置性，提供大量可调配置选项。其目标很简单：通过允许灵活的配置，软件系统可以在广泛的领域中实现更大的适用性，并满足不同的性能要求，例如运行时间和吞吐量[25,26,87]。然而，过度的可配置性也会带来其自身的成本：研究表明，全球 59% 的软件性能问题（严重违反性能要求）归因于配置选择不当，而不是代码 [43]。Software systems are highly configurable, offering numerous tunable configuration options. The goal thereof is straightforward: by allowing flexible configuration, software systems can achieve greater applicability across a wide range of domains and cater to varying performance requirements, e.g., runtime and throughput [25, 26, 87]. Yet, excessive configurability comes with its own costs: it has been shown that globally 59% of the software performance issues—where performance requirements were severely violated—are attributed to poorly chosen configurations rather than code [43].

为了缓解这些挑战，人们开发了各种依赖于贝叶斯优化（BO）[19,39,41,100]和遗传算法（GA）[16,74,97]等算法的自动配置调谐器来搜索最佳配置。然而，据报道，这些调谐器的有效性在不同的环境中存在显着差异[99]。例如，图 1 描述了两个调谐器、随机搜索 (RS) [73] 和爬山 (HC) [60, 95] 在 Batik 和 H2 上的调谐轨迹，这两个系统表现出不同的配置景观。显然，收敛和调整质量有所不同，在 Batik 上 HC 优于 RS，而在 H2 上则截然不同。调优算法的有效性在很大程度上取决于系统/工作负载的基本特征，例如，H2 的地形比 Batik 的地形更崎岖（参见图 1 的顶部面板），导致 HC 无效，但 RS 作为随机采样，不受崎岖程度的影响。然而，对于社区来说，仍然不清楚配置环境和特定领域因素（例如系统区域、结构和工作负载特征）如何To mitigate these challenges, various automated configuration tuners, relying on algorithms such as Bayesian optimization (BO) [19, 39, 41, 100] and genetic algorithms (GAs) [16, 74, 97], have been developed to search for optimal configurations. However, it has been reported that the effectiveness of these tuners varies significantly across different contexts [99]. For instance, Figure 1 depicts the tuning trajectories of two tuners, random search (RS) [73] and hill climbing (HC) [60, 95], on Batik and H2—two systems that exhibit distinct configuration landscapes.   • 优先HC：Priority HC:这种方法在突变过程中对敏感选项给予更高的优先级。具体来说，在每次迭代中，有 90% 的概率将突变应用于其中一个敏感选项，只有 10% 的概率选择非敏感选项。在我们的实验中，敏感选项通过耐用性分析或方差分析来识别。This approach gives higher priority to sensitive options during mutation. Specifically, at each iteration, there is a 90% probability that a mutation is applied to one of the sensitive options, and only a 10% chance that a non-sensitive option is selected. In our experiments, sensitive options are identified either by ruggedness analysis or by ANOVA.

  • 香草佐治亚州：Vanilla GA:此方法使用统一交叉，其中每个配置选项在父配置之间交换的概率相同，均为 50%，从而确保所有选项都得到平等对待。This approach uses uniform crossover, where each configuration option has an equal 50% probability of being exchanged between parent configurations, ensuring all options are treated equally.

  • 优先遗传算法：Priority GA:这种方法使交叉偏向于敏感选项。在每次交叉操作中，有 90% 的概率会交换敏感选项。与 HC 一样，敏感选项是通过耐用性分析或方差分析来确定的。This approach biases the crossover toward sensitive options. At each crossover operation, there is a 90% probability that a sensitive option is exchanged. As with HC, sensitive options are determined either through ruggedness analysis or ANOVA.

我们的研究结果表明，调整有效性与配置景观的结构属性密切相关，而配置景观的结构属性由系统的域特征决定。这些观察结果强调了不仅要了解调优器的作用，还要了解调优器在特定调优任务下成功或失败的原因的重要性。The results of our study demonstrate that tuning effectiveness is closely tied to the structural properties of the configuration landscape, which are shaped by the system’s domain characteristics. These observations reinforce the importance of understanding not only what a tuner does, but why it succeeds or fails under specific tuning tasks.

我们提出的方法 Domland 提供了一种系统的方法来缩小这一差距。通过协同领域知识和通过 FLA 挖掘的配置景观的空间信息，Domland 使研究人员和从业者能够推断调优难度、解释调优器行为并为设计更稳健的调优策略提供信息。因此，我们的方法对于调优研究人员和系统设计人员以及软件工程中的一些相关实践都具有重要意义，我们将在下面概述。Our proposed methodology, Domland, offers a systematic way to close this gap. While no dataset can be fully representative of all modern configurable systems, the breadth of this dataset provides a strong empirical foundation for the domain–spatiality analyses conducted in this study. In addition, while our analysis reveals some common patterns across systems or workloads, different types of configurable software systems may exhibit fundamentally distinct landscape features. Therefore, the findings reported here might not directly or fully generalize to all configurable systems. Nonetheless, the methodology is designed to be general-purpose and can be applied to a wide range of black-box configurable systems. We believe the FLA perspective presented in this work offers a systematic lens through which diverse configuration-performance spatial correlations can be analyzed, and it can be extended to support system-specific insights in other application domains.

9 结论9 Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 Domland，一种将空间适应性景观分析与领域知识联系起来以理解配置调整的方法。 Domland 能够对配置-性能相关性进行结构化解释，有助于评估调优难度并为算法设计提供信息。通过对 9 个可配置系统和 93 个工作负载进行的广泛实验，我们展示了如何遵循 Domland 并揭示了几个关键见解：In this paper, we propose Domland, a methodology that bridges spatial fitness landscape analysis with domain knowledge to understand configuration tuning. Domland enables a structured interpretation of configuration-performance correlation, helping to assess tuning difficulty and inform algorithm design. Through extensive experiments across nine configurable systems and 93 workloads, we show how to follow Domland and reveal several key insights:

  • 系统特定的景观：System-specific landscapes:不同系统的配置情况有所不同；没有哪个单一领域（例如区域、语言或资源强度）能够一致地塑造其结构。Configuration landscape vary across systems; no single domain (e.g., area, language, or resource intensity) consistently shapes their structure.

  • 核心选项的影响：Impact of core option:核心选项对景观崎岖度的影响比资源选项更大。Core options have a stronger influence on landscape ruggedness than resource options.

  • 工作负载引起的变化：Workload-induced variations:工作量对景观结构的影响并不完全与类型或规模相关，而是更多地依赖于系统。Workload effects on landscape structure are not uniformly tied to type or scale, but are more system-dependent.

我们希望这项工作中理解配置调整的领域空间协同效应为更好的自适应调谐器设计和可配置系统的发展铺平道路。通过将 Domland 集成到调谐器选择和设计过程中，未来的工作可以转向景观感知自动调谐，其中优化策略与系统和工作负载特征动态匹配，以获得更好的性能和适应性。此外，将因果推理集成到 Domland 中可能代表着一个有希望的未来方向，超越相关性分析，更深入地理解领域因素如何塑造配置景观。We hope that domain-spatiality synergies for understanding configuration tuning in this work pave the way for better adaptive tuner designs and the evolution of configurable systems. By integrating Domland into the tuner selection and design process, future work can move toward landscape-aware autotuning, where optimization strategies are dynamically matched to system and workload characteristics for better performance and adaptability. Additionally, integrating causal reasoning into Domland may represent a promising future direction to move beyond correlation analysis and toward a deeper understanding of how domain factors shape configuration landscapes.
