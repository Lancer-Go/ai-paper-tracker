摘要。Abstract.

随着深度学习和量子计算之间的协同作用不断增强，量子神经网络（QNN）利用量子并行性和纠缠性已成为一种有前途的范式。然而，由于 QNN 复杂的量子动力学和有限的可解释性，测试 QNN 的探索仍未充分。开发 QNN 突变测试技术前景广阔，但需要解决随机因素，包括突变算子和量子测量固有的随机性。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 QuanForge，一个专门为 QNN 设计的突变测试框架。我们首先引入统计突变杀死以提供更可靠的标准。 QuanForge 在门和参数级别包含 9 个训练后突变算子，能够模拟量子电路中的各种潜在错误。最后，突变体生成算法被形式化，可以系统地产生有效的突变体，从而实现稳健且可靠的突变分析。通过对基准数据集和 QNN 架构的大量实验，我们表明 QuanForge 可以有效区分不同的测试套件并定位易受攻击的电路区域，为 QNN 的数据增强和结构评估提供见解。我们还分析了不同算子的生成能力并评估模拟噪声条件下的性能，以评估 QuanForge 对于未来量子器件的实际可行性。With the growing synergy between deep learning and quantum computing, Quantum Neural Networks (QNNs) have emerged as a promising paradigm by leveraging quantum parallelism and entanglement. However, testing QNNs remains underexplored due to their complex quantum dynamics and limited interpretability. Developing a mutation testing technique for QNNs is promising while requires addressing stochastic factors, including the inherent randomness of mutation operators and quantum measurements. To tackle these challenges, we propose QuanForge, a mutation testing framework specifically designed for QNNs. We first introduce statistical mutation killing to provide a more reliable criterion. QuanForge incorporates nine post-training mutation operators at both gate and parameter levels, capable of simulating various potential errors in quantum circuits. Finally, a mutant generation algorithm is formalized that systematically produces effective mutants, thereby enabling a robust and reliable mutation analysis. Through extensive experiments on benchmark datasets and QNN architectures, we show that QuanForge can effectively distinguish different test suites and localize vulnerable circuit regions, providing insights for data enhancement and structural assessment of QNNs. We also analyze the generation capabilities of different operators and evaluate performance under simulated noisy conditions to assess the practical feasibility of QuanForge for future quantum devices.

1. 简介Introduction

近年来，量子计算无论是在硬件能力还是算法发展方面都取得了快速的进步。最有前途的领域之一是量子机器学习（QML）Quantum computing has made rapid progress in recent years, both in terms of hardware capabilities and algorithmic development. One of the most promising areas is quantum machine learning (QML)(Biamonte et al., 2017)，它将量子计算与机器学习相结合，以提高相互的性能。在 QML 中，量子神经网络 (QNN), which combines quantum computing with machine learning to improve mutual performance. In QML, quantum neural networks (QNNs)(Cong et al., 2018)因其将叠加和纠缠等量子特性集成到神经网络中的能力而引起了人们的关注。这些属性提供了潜在的优势，包括时间复杂度的加速have attracted attention for their ability to integrate quantum properties, such as superposition and entanglement, into neural networks. These properties offer potential advantages, including speedup in time complexity(Cerezo et al., 2022)。 QNN 在图像分类等任务中取得了初步成功. QNNs have achieved initial success in tasks such as image classification(Li et al., 2022), 生成模型, generative models(Dallaire-Demers and Killoran, 2018)和自然语言处理, and natural language processing(Pandey et al., 2023).

另一个关键挑战是量子上下文中涉及的突变测试的随机性，这源于两个方面：(1) 算子应用程序的随机性（例如，哪个门发生突变）和 (2) 测量过程的随机性，其中在同一数据集上重复执行 QNN 可能会产生不同的结果。后者是量子计算所独有的，直接影响突变杀死和突变分数。考虑到这些因素，迫切需要为 QNN 开发一个高效可靠的突变测试框架。Another key challenge is the stochasticity of mutation testing involved in the quantum context, which arises from two aspects: (1) randomness of operator application (e.g., which gates to mutate) and (2) randomness of the measurement process, where repeated executions of a QNN on the same dataset may yield different outcomes. Unlike DNNs composed of neurons connected by weights, QNNs are constructed from qubits and quantum gates, endowed with the properties of superposition and entanglement. The novel structure of quantum circuits renders the neuron- and weight-based mutation operators in DNNs inapplicable. Moreover, the inefficiency and resource overhead of retraining QNNs also prevent a direct adaptation of source-level mutation strategies from DNNs.
