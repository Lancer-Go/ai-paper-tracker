1 简介1 Introduction

梯度下降是连续优化中最古老、使用最广泛的方法之一。每次迭代仅需要一个梯度和一些向量运算，这使其在大规模问题中具有吸引力。然而，它的性能可能会在病态设置下恶化：精确的线搜索可能会产生之字形迭代，而良好的恒定步骤需要很少能提前获得的光谱信息。Gradient descent is one of the oldest and most widely used methods in continuous optimization. Each iteration requires only a gradient and a few vector operations, making it attractive in large-scale problems. Its performance, however, can deteriorate on ill-conditioned settings: exact line search may produce zigzag iterates, whereas a good constant step requires spectral information that is rarely available in advance.

Barzilai-Borwein (BB) 方法（Barzilai 和 Borwein，1988）在不改变负梯度方向的情况下解决了这个难题。相反，它从最近的两次迭代中提取标量曲率估计。这种步长规则的改变，而不是搜索方向的改变，可以显着改善基本梯度迭代的实际行为（Fletcher，2005；Birgin 等人，2014）。The Barzilai–Borwein (BB) method (Barzilai and Borwein, 1988) addresses this difficulty without changing the negative-gradient direction. Instead, it extracts a scalar curvature estimate from the two most recent iterates. This change of step-size rule, rather than search direction, can substantially improve the practical behavior of a basic gradient iteration (Fletcher, 2005; Birgin et al., 2014).
