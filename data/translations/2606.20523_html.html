摘要Abstract

得益于大型光学基准，多模态基础模型发展迅速，但合成孔径雷达 (SAR) 的类似资源仍然有限。现有的 SAR 光学数据集在很大程度上依赖于低分辨率、仅强度的地面范围检测 (GRD) 产品，并且不保留复值 SAR 测量或本机采集几何结构，这限制了物理接地的多模态学习。特别是，仍然缺乏结合超高分辨率（VHR）SAR SLC、对齐光学图像和自然语言描述的大规模公共数据集。 我们提出了一个 VHR SAR-光学-文本数据集，该数据集是根据开放访问的 Umbra 聚光灯采集构建的，作为传感器独立复杂数据 (SICD) 分发。来自Multimodal foundation models have advanced rapidly thanks to large optical benchmarks, but comparable resources for synthetic aperture radar (SAR) remain limited. Existing SAR–optical datasets largely rely on low-resolution, intensity-only Ground Range Detected (GRD) products and do not preserve complex-valued SAR measurements or native acquisition geometry, which restricts physically grounded multimodal learning. In particular, large-scale public datasets combining very-high-resolution (VHR) SAR SLC, aligned optical imagery, and natural-language descriptions are still lacking. We present a VHR SAR–optical–text dataset built from open-access Umbra spotlight acquisitions distributed as Sensor Independent Complex Data (SICD). From∼\sim2,500 个全球场景（VV/HH，20cm–2m 原始分辨率），我们通过带限 FFT 重采样将所有 SAR 数据标准化为 80cm 斜距网格，并将图像平铺为2,500 worldwide scenes (VV/HH, 20 cm–2 m native resolution), we standardize all SAR data to an 80 cm slant-range grid via band-limited FFT resampling and tile the imagery into1024×10241024\times 1024补丁。对于每个 SAR 补丁，我们检索高分辨率光学瓦片，并使用局部坐标对应将其扭曲到 SAR 网格中以进行局部像素级对齐。我们进一步为每个样本生成三个标题变体（短/中/长）以支持视觉语言训练和评估。 我们的数据集包含 119,566 个三元组（复杂和振幅倾斜范围 SAR 补丁、对齐光学补丁、自然语言描述），覆盖 72 个国家/地区的 257 个位置以及广泛的土地类型和基础设施。我们发布了固定的训练/验证/测试分割以及完整的预处理和基线代码，以便为原生 SAR 几何中的跨模态检索和条件生成的多模态对齐提供可重复的基准。该数据集可在 Hugging Face Hub 上公开获取：patches. For each SAR patch, we retrieve a high-resolution optical tile and warp it into the SAR grid using local coordinate correspondences for local pixel-level alignment. We further generate three caption variants (SHORT/MID/LONG) per sample to support vision–language training and evaluation. Our dataset contains 119,566 triplets (complex and amplitude slant-range SAR patch, aligned optical patch, natural-language description) covering 257 locations across 72 countries and a broad range of land types and infrastructures. We release fixed train/validation/test splits and the full preprocessing and baseline code to enable reproducible benchmarks for multimodal alignment on cross-modal retrieval and conditional generation in native SAR geometry. The dataset is publicly available on the Hugging Face Hub athttps://huggingface.co/datasets/ONERA/SARLO-80.

1 简介Introduction

合成孔径雷达 (SAR) 是对光学图像的补充，因为它通过雷达散射而不是使用可见光来测量场景结构。它昼夜工作，受云或烟雾的影响要小得多。此外，SAR 数据最初是作为传感器原生斜距几何结构中复杂的 SLC 测量（幅度 + 相位）提供的，其中可以看到诸如停留和阴影之类的影响。保留这些物理信息很有价值，但也使 SAR 更难以使用，并且通常需要特定的处理和领域知识。Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) complements optical imagery because it measures the scene structure through radar scattering instead of using visible light. It works day and night and is much less affected by clouds or smoke. Moreover, SAR data are originally provided as complex SLC measurements (amplitude + phase) in the sensor’s native slant-range geometry, where effects such as layover and shadow are visible. Keeping this physical information is valuable, but it also makes SAR harder to use and often requires specific processing and domain knowledge.

遥感中的多模态学习依赖于大型且精心策划的数据集，但大多数现有的 SAR 光学基准都是根据 Sentinel-1 地面范围检测 (GRD) 产品构建的。这些数据仅是强度、地面投影且分辨率相对较低（通常为Multimodal learning in remote sensing relies on large and well-curated datasets, yet most existing SAR–optical benchmarks are built from Sentinel-1 Ground Range Detected (GRD) products. These data are intensity-only, ground-projected, and relatively low resolution (typically∼\sim10-30 m），它丢弃了复杂 SAR 测量中包含的重要信息，例如相位和本地采集几何形状。因此，当前数据集对充分利用 SAR 特定物理特性的学习表示提供的支持有限。特别是，将超高分辨率（VHR）SAR SLC 图像与对齐的光学观测和自然语言描述相结合的大规模多模态资源仍然基本上不可用，并且目前没有公共视觉语言模型关注此类数据。与此同时，SAR 传感能力正在迅速发展：现代商业星座（例如 Umbra、ICEYE、Capella）可以获取 VHR SAR 图像，并且最近的开放获取计划开始向研究界提供这些数据。10–30 m), which discards important information contained in complex SAR measurements such as phase and native acquisition geometry. As a result, current datasets provide limited support for learning representations that fully exploit SAR-specific physical properties. In particular, large-scale multimodal resources combining very-high-resolution (VHR) SAR SLC imagery with aligned optical observations and natural-language descriptions remain largely unavailable, and no public vision–language models are currently focused on such data. At the same time, SAR sensing capabilities are rapidly evolving: modern commercial constellations (e.g., Umbra, ICEYE, Capella) can acquire VHR SAR imagery, and recent open-access initiatives are beginning to make these data available to the research community.

在这种情况下，我们以本地 SAR 几何结构（80 厘米处的 119,566 个样本）展示了 SAR 特定的多模态数据集，该数据集是通过处理 Umbra 卫星星座的开放访问聚光采集数据而构建的In this context, we present our SAR-specific multimodal dataset in native SAR geometry (119,566 samples at 80 cm) built from processing open-access spotlight acquisitions of the Umbra satellite constellation[7]。我们从全球采集的数千个复杂 SAR 场景 (SICD) 开始，通过在 80 厘米处重新聚焦和重新采样来处理它们。然后将每个场景平铺为. We start from thousands of complex SAR scenes (SICD) acquired worldwide and processed them by refocusing and resampling at 80 cm. Each scene is then tiled into1024×10241024\times 1024倾斜范围网格中的补丁。为了形成多模态对，我们将每个 SAR 块与通过一阶仿射近似映射到 SAR 斜距框架的高分辨率光学图像相关联，确保像素级对应。通过在原生斜距几何中保留复值 SAR 信号，该数据集保留了香农奈奎斯特理论下合理的事后重采样所需的带限相干场，同时避免了几何上重新扭曲停留、缩短和阴影效应的检测和重投影步骤。然后，从光学模态生成三个字幕并进行后处理以保持一致性。patches in slant-range grid. To form multimodal pairs, we associate each SAR patch with a high-resolution optical image mapped into the SAR slant-range frame via a first-order affine approximation, ensuring pixel-wise correspondence. By preserving the complex-valued SAR signal in native slant-range geometry, the dataset retains the band-limited coherent field required for theoretically sound post-hoc resampling under Shannon–Nyquist, while avoiding detection and reprojection steps that geometrically rewarp layover, foreshortening, and shadowing effects. Then, three captions are generated from the optical modality and post-processed for consistency.

我们基线实验的目标不是建立一个新的最先进的模型，而是提供一个简单的概念验证，说明所提出的数据集如何支持极高分辨率 SLC SAR 中的跨模式检索和条件生成。我们将发布完整的预处理管道、训练/验证/测试拆分和评估代码，以促进可重复的基准测试。一般来说，我们的数据集为训练多模态 SAR 感知模型提供了基础，包括视觉语言模型 (VLM)、生成模型以及用于超分辨率、语义理解和多模态推理等任务的其他基础模型。The goal of our baseline experiments is not to establish a new state-of-the-art model, but to provide a simple proof-of-concept illustrating how the proposed dataset can support cross-modal retrieval and conditional generation in very-high-resolution SLC SAR. We will release the full preprocessing pipeline, training/validation/testing splits, and evaluation code to facilitate reproducible benchmarking. In general, our dataset provides a foundation for training multimodal SAR-aware models, including vision–language models (VLMs), generative models, and other foundation models for tasks such as super-resolution, semantic understanding, and multimodal reasoning.

3.2 预处理步骤Preprocessing steps

频域重采样至 0.8 m 像素间距Frequency-domain resampling to 0.8 m pixel spacing

每个SICD场景转换为80厘米的固定目标采样Each SICD scene is converted into a fixed target sampling of 80 cm×\times80 厘米倾斜范围几何形状。为了一致地重新采样，同时保留复杂的信号结构，我们在 2D 频域中操作。我们计算中心频谱然后执行80 cm in slant-range geometry. To resample consistently while preserving the complex signal structure, we operate in the 2D frequency domain. We compute the centered spectrum then perform带限重采样band-limited resampling通过裁剪中心频带（下采样）或对频谱进行零填充（上采样），具体取决于与目标相比的原始采样。最后通过傅里叶逆变换得到标准化复图像。该过程保留了带限相干场，并确保香农-奈奎斯特一致的重采样，而不会引入几何失真。by either cropping the central band (downsampling) or zero-padding the spectrum (upsampling), depending on the original sampling compared to the target. Finally, the standardized complex image is obtained by the inverse Fourier Transform. This procedure preserves the band-limited coherent field and ensures Shannon–Nyquist-consistent resampling without introducing geometric distortions.

地理定位网格：从 SAR 像素到地球坐标Geolocation grid: from SAR pixels to Earth coordinates

SAR 图像以斜距几何形式提供，这意味着每个像素都在雷达采集坐标（方位角和距离）中进行索引，而不是在地图投影的地平面中进行索引，如图所示SAR images are delivered in slant-range geometry, meaning that each pixel is indexed in the radar acquisition coordinates (azimuth and range) rather than in a map-projected ground plane, as shown in Figurefig.˜1(b).

为了将每个标准化 SAR 像素与其在地球上的物理位置联系起来，我们使用 SICD 元数据计算密集的地理位置网格。对于每个场景，SICD 文件提供像素坐标中的场景中心点 (SCP)、方位角和范围中的采样间距以及将图像坐标投影到地球上所需的成像几何形状。我们首先在标准化 SAR 图像上构建一个规则的像素坐标网格，以 SCP 为中心，并以原始 SICD 采样表示。然后，我们将 SICD 图像到地面投影应用于每个像素，以获得其相应的地心地固 (ECEF) 坐标。最后，我们将这些 ECEF 坐标转换为 WGS84 大地坐标（纬度、经度、海拔）。这会为每个场景生成两个对齐的网格（ECEF 和纬度/经度/高度），这些网格会保存一次，然后进行裁剪以匹配每个网格To link each standardized SAR pixel to its physical location on Earth, we compute a dense geolocation grid using the SICD metadata. For each scene, the SICD file provides the scene center point (SCP) in pixel coordinates, the sampling spacings in azimuth and range, and the imaging geometry needed to project image coordinates onto the Earth. We first build a regular grid of pixel coordinates over the standardized SAR image, centered on the SCP and expressed in the original SICD sampling. We then apply the SICD image-to-ground projection to every pixel to obtain its corresponding Earth-Centered, Earth-Fixed (ECEF) coordinate. Finally, we convert these ECEF coordinates to WGS84 geodetic coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude). 5 实验与讨论Experiments and Discussion

视觉语言模型（VLM）和生成文本到图像管道的最新进展表明，大规模多模态对齐可以实现强大的跨模态检索、字幕和条件生成功能。然而，这些进步几乎完全是针对光学图像而开发的。相比之下，超高分辨率 (VHR) 合成孔径雷达 (SAR) 图像，特别是复值 SLC 格式的图像，在多模态学习基准中仍然很大程度上尚未得到探索。Recent progress in vision–language models (VLMs) and generative text-to-image pipelines has demonstrated that large-scale multimodal alignment enables strong cross-modal retrieval, captioning, and conditional generation capabilities. However, these advances have been almost exclusively developed for optical imagery. In contrast, very-high-resolution (VHR) synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imagery, particularly in complex-valued SLC format, remains largely unexplored in multimodal learning benchmarks.

现有的 SAR 数据集通常依赖于较低分辨率的 GRD 产品或不同的传感器配置，因此很难与当前的基础模型进行直接比较。因此，目前还没有公开的基于 VHR SAR SLC 图像训练的视觉语言模型。因此，我们的数据集为研究这种模式的多模式学习提供了第一个基准。为了说明数据集启用的功能，我们评估了两个代表性的多模态任务：文本到 SAR 生成和跨模态检索。Existing SAR datasets typically rely on lower-resolution GRD products or different sensor configurations, making direct comparison with current foundation models difficult. As a result, there are currently no publicly available vision–language models trained on VHR SAR SLC imagery. Our dataset therefore provides a first benchmark for studying multimodal learning in this modality. To illustrate the capabilities enabled by the dataset, we evaluate two representative multimodal tasks: text-to-SAR generation and cross-modal retrieval.

5.3 讨论Discussion

结果表明 SARLO-80 可以支持 SAR 之间有意义的对齐 图像和自然语言。同时，它们也表明SAR仍然是一个 难以建模，因为它的外观与 雷达成像物理学。一些容易用光学描述的物体 SAR 图像中的图像可能更难识别，尤其是当它们 雷达响应受几何形状、散斑或强散射效应的影响。 80 cm 的分辨率也很重要。它比典型的 Sentinel 级 GRD 产品精细得多，因此 SAR 特定效果变得更加明显。这些包括停留、阴影、强城市散射和非高斯散射统计，可以通过对数累积分析等进行研究。入射角元数据还可以帮助分析采集几何形状和地形如何影响 SAR 外观。The results show that SARLO-80 can support meaningful alignment between SAR images and natural language. At the same time, they also show that SAR remains a difficult modality to model, because its appearance is strongly linked to the physics of radar imaging. Some objects that are easy to describe in optical images can be much harder to recognize in SAR images, especially when their radar response is affected by geometry, speckle, or strong scattering effects. The 80 cm resolution is also important. It is much finer than typical Sentinel-scale GRD products, so SAR-specific effects become more visible. These include layover, shadowing, strong urban scattering, and non-Gaussian scattering statistics, which can be studied for example with log-cumulant analysis. The incidence-angle metadata can also help analyze how acquisition geometry and terrain affect the SAR appearance.

SAR-文本对使得研究视觉-语言基础和 跨模态检索。由于每个 SAR 图像都与多个标题相关联 级别，数据集还可以用于分析有多少语义细节 需要描述 SAR 场景。这对于理解模型是否有用 只学习广泛的场景类别，例如城市地区或水体，或者 他们是否还可以捕获更详细的结构。The SAR–text pairs make it possible to study vision–language grounding and cross-modal retrieval. Since each SAR image is associated with several caption levels, the dataset can also be used to analyze how much semantic detail is needed to describe a SAR scene. This is useful for understanding whether models only learn broad scene categories, such as urban areas or water bodies, or whether they can also capture more detailed structures.

This makes it possible to test different SAR representations, such as amplitude, log-intensity, phase-related information, or other derived features. Comparing these representations can help identify which parts of the SAR signal are most useful for multimodal alignment and retrieval. The complex SAR signal can be used even without the optical image or the text caption. It supports tasks such as despeckling, spectrum-domain super-resolution, and speckle or equivalent-number-of-looks analysis. It can also be decomposed into sub-apertures. These sub-apertures can be used to study scattering diversity and to create colorized SAR images, by assigning different sub-aperture views to different color channels. This SAR colorization can make some structures easier to interpret and can also provide another useful representation for learning models.

6 结论Conclusion

据我们所知，这是第一个提供如此大集合的大型数据集（>To the best of our knowledge, this is the first large-scale dataset providing such a large collection (>110,000110{,}000）以亚米级分辨率共同配准的 SLC SAR 光学贴片，同时保留复杂的 SAR 测量结果。总体而言，该数据集为下游分析和基准测试提供了语义和地理信号。我们希望我们的数据集能够支持 SAR 多模态表示学习、检索、分割和生成建模的研究，并作为训练和评估面向 SAR 的基础模型的基础。未来的工作包括使用其他模式（例如，高程数据或共同注册的多时相场景）扩展数据集，提高字幕多样性和基础，并引入针对超高分辨率斜距 SAR 定制的标准化下游基准和评估协议。) of co-registered SLC SAR-optical patches at sub-meter resolution while preserving the complex SAR measurements. Overall, this dataset provides both semantic and geographic signals for downstream analysis and benchmarking. We expect our dataset to support research on multimodal representation learning, retrieval, segmentation, and generative modeling for SAR, and to serve as a foundation for training and evaluating SAR-oriented foundation models. Future work includes extending the dataset with additional modalities (e.g., elevation data or co-registered multi-temporal scenes), improving caption diversity and grounding, and introducing standardized downstream benchmarks and evaluation protocols tailored to very-high-resolution slant-range SAR.
