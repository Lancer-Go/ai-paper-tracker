具有姿势、求解和记忆功能的自我进化搜索代理Self-Evolving Search Agents that Pose, Solve, and Remember

摘要Abstract

自我对弈代理可以在没有目标提出问题的情况下生成训练问题 基准，但他们的课程缺乏持久状态：失败影响梯度 但没有明确塑造未来的实践。保留外部技能记忆 程序经验，但通常是从固定的任务分配中学习的。 我们介绍Self-play agents can generate training problems without questions from target benchmarks, but their curricula lack persistent state: failures affect gradients yet do not explicitly shape future practice. External skill memories preserve procedural experience but are typically learned from fixed task distributions. We introduceSESA（自我进化技能增强代理），这使得 程序记忆是工具增强搜索自我博弈的不断发展的状态。一个 挑战者提出问题，而单独参数化的求解器则单独提出问题 检索技能。信息性的失败被提炼成可重用的技能 写回到内存中。更新的内存改变了求解器的行为和成功， 这改变了挑战者的奖励和未来问题的分配； 由此产生的边界会产生新的故障，从而重写内存。这个 双向循环使任务生成和技能记忆共同进化。因为 检索到的技能塑造了政策培训轨迹，它们的好处可以 输入模型参数并保留在外部银行中，从而使 无内存部署和可选的推理时间检索。跨越七 开放域和多跳问答基准，SESA 提高了平均水平 在多个骨干网中，准确率比 SSP 高 1.2-3.2 个百分点，并超越 统一评估下技能增强 SkillRL 基线提高 0.9 分 协议。在 Qwen3 型号上，SESA-Off 比 Qwen3 型号保留 1.8–2.2 个百分点的改进 SSP，而最终技能库又增加了 0.5-1.0 点。这些结果 表明不断发展的技能记忆不仅仅是一个推理时间插件：它 改变政策学习和未来的培训分布，同时保留 值作为可选的外部存储器。我们的代码位于(Self-Evolving Skill-Augmented Agent), which makes procedural memory an evolving state of tool-augmented search self-play. A challenger poses problems, while a separately parameterized solver alone retrieves skills. Informative failures are distilled into reusable skills and written back to memory. The updated memory changes solver behavior and success, which changes the challenger’s reward and the distribution of future problems; the resulting frontier produces new failures that rewrite memory. This bidirectional loop makes task generation and skill memory co-evolve. Because retrieved skills shape on-policy training trajectories, their benefits can enter the model parameters as well as remain in the external bank, enabling memory-free deployment and optional inference-time retrieval. Across seven open-domain and multi-hop question-answering benchmarks, SESA improves average accuracy over SSP by 1.2–3.2 points across multiple backbones and surpasses the skill-augmented SkillRL baseline by 0.9 points under a unified evaluation protocol. On Qwen3 models, SESA-Off retains 1.8–2.2 points of improvement over SSP, while the final skill bank adds a further 0.5–1.0 points. These results show that evolving skill memory is not merely an inference-time plug-in: it changes policy learning and the future training distribution while retaining value as optional external memory. Our code is available athttps://github.com/Zenghuang-Fu/SESA-Self-Evolving-Search-Agents.

简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 代理越来越多地从自己的模型中进行训练 经验而不是固定的论证语料库。一条路线是Large language model (LLM) agents are increasingly trained from their own experience rather than a fixed corpus of demonstrations. One route is零数据自博弈zero-data self-play，其中代理使用以下方式提出并解决问题 无需外部问题集即可验证的奖励, where an agent poses and solves problems using verifiable rewards without an external question set(Lu et al. 2026; Chen et al. 2025; Xia et al. 2025; Acikgoz et al. 2026)。因为任务生成是 内生的，自我对弈可以随着求解器的改进而适应难度。然而它的 经验通常是短暂的：轨迹会产生政策梯度 但没有留下明确的、可重复使用的从中学到的策略的说明。. Because task generation is endogenous, self-play can adapt difficulty as the solver improves. Yet its experience is usually transient: a trajectory contributes a policy gradient but leaves no explicit, reusable account of the strategy learned from it.

互补路线，A complementary route,技能增强强化学习skill-augmented reinforcement learning, 蒸馏 将经验转化为可检索的策略或结构化笔记, distills experience into retrievable strategies or structured notes(Xia et al. 2026; Wang et al. 2026; Shi et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026a, b; Zhang et al. 2026)。这样的记忆得以保留 程序性知识超出了产生它的更新，但它们通常是 根据固定数据集或手工设计的课程构建。这两个限制是 镜像：自我对弈决定练习什么，但忘记了教训， 而技能增强强化学习会记住它没有选择的任务中的经验教训。的 缺少设置是可以同时执行这两项操作的代理。. Such memories preserve procedural knowledge beyond the update that produced it, but they are typically built from fixed datasets or hand-designed curricula. These two limitations are mirror images: self-play decides what to practice but forgets its lessons, whereas skill-augmented RL remembers lessons from tasks it did not choose. The missing setting is an agent that can do both.

我们呈现We presentSESA（自我进化技能增强代理），将 工具增强搜索自我博弈中的在线技能进化。挑战者 提出一个具有可验证目标和单独参数化的问题 求解器使用搜索工具尝试它。提炼出信息丰富的求解器故障 转化为人类可读的技能、重复数据删除并写入有界的 非参数记忆。求解器在后面的回合中检索这些技能，因此 过去的失败改变了未来的政策轨迹和用于政策的数据 优化。记忆对挑战者来说仍然是隐藏的，从而防止直接 技能泄漏并保持问题生成和问题生成之间的不对称博弈 解决问题。(Self-Evolving Skill-Augmented Agent), which places online skill evolution inside tool-augmented search self-play. A challenger poses a question with a verifiable target, and a separately parameterized solver attempts it with a search tool. Informative solver failures are distilled into human-readable skills, deduplicated, and written to a bounded non-parametric memory. The solver retrieves these skills in later rounds, so past failures change future on-policy trajectories and the data used for policy optimization. Memory remains hidden from the challenger, preventing direct skill leakage and preserving an asymmetric game between problem generation and problem solving.

A closed loop must therefore decide which failures are learnable, who may access their distilled lessons, and how those lessons return to on-policy training.

非对称自我对弈Asymmetric Self-Play

SESA 使用单独的参数化提议者和求解器策略，如下 非对称自我博弈SESA uses separately parameterized proposer and solver policies, following asymmetric self-play(Xia et al. 2025)。这种分离使求解器成功 将稳定的解释评为提议者生成的问题的难度 而且，最重要的是，让 SESA 仅向求解器公开检索到的技能。的 因此，提议者通过奖励反馈进行调整，而无需直接观察 面向解决方案的记忆。其他动机和实施细节 这种信息不对称出现在补充材料中。.
