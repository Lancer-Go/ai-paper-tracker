一、简介1. Introduction

背景和动机。Background and motivation.随机镜像下降 (SMD) 及其变体是随机优化中研究最广泛的一阶方法之一。 SMD 最初由 Nemirovski [76] 和 Nemirovski & Yudin [77] 构想为解决约束凸最小化问题的通用方案，后来发展成为一个成熟的算法模板，应用于最小-最大问题和变分不等式 [78,80,81]，在线优化和老虎机 [1,7,22,53]，博弈和均衡问题 [24,42,74]，采样 [26, 37]、最佳传输 [88, 118] 以及许多其他机器学习核心领域。Stochastic mirror descent (SMD) and its variants comprise one of the most widely studied classes of first-order methods in stochastic optimization. Originally conceived by Nemirovski [76] and Nemirovski & Yudin [77] as a general scheme for solving constrained convex minimization problems, SMD has since grown into a mature algorithmic template with applications in min-max problems and variational inequalities [78, 80, 81], online optimization and bandits [1, 7, 22, 53], games and equilibrium problems [24, 42, 74], sampling [26, 37], optimal transport [88, 118], and many other fields that are central to machine learning.

在高层次上，镜像下降方法通过用非欧几里得“prox-type”步骤替换欧几里得投影来进行，或者，双重地，通过在无约束的对偶空间中聚合梯度步骤，并在每次迭代时执行“镜像步骤”以生成新的查询点并获得新的梯度样本。这种机制将底层问题的几何形状与定义方法的近似步长联系起来，如果选择得当，可以带来卓越的、几乎无维的收敛保证。At a high level, mirror descent methods proceed by replacing Euclidean projections with a non-Euclidean “prox-type” step—or, dually to this, by aggregating gradient steps in an unconstrained, dual space, and performing a “mirror step” at each iteration to generate a new query point and obtain a new gradient sample. This mechanism links the geometry of the underlying problem with the prox-step defining the method and which, if chosen appropriately, can lead to superior, almost-dimension-free convergence guarantees.
