摘要Abstract

化学文献综合通常需要汇集分散在许多出版物中的具体发现，但现有的文献检索系统主要返回排名的文档列表。 因此，科学家和人工智能代理需要找到相关信息，验证其来源，并手动组装跨论文答案。 我们呈现Chemistry literature synthesis often requires assembling specific findings scattered across many publications, yet existing literature-search systems primarily return ranked document lists. As a result, scientists and AI agents need to locate relevant information, verify their provenance, and assemble cross-paper answers manually. We present询问化学AskChem，一个以权利要求为中心的跨论文化学搜索基础设施。 AskChem 将检索单位从论文更改为带有出处的声明：每篇论文都转换为原子的、键入的声明，每个声明均以源 DOI 和逐字引用或明确的证据定位器为基础。通过这个共享的声明存储，AskChem 公开了用于搜索和综合的互补结构：用于分层检索和浏览的稳定的多面分类法、通过关系链接声明的证据图，以及将索引论文置于科学原理之下的探索性活分类法。 AskChem 目前对 147,000 篇论文中的 240 万条声明进行了索引，并为 AI 代理提供了 Web 界面以及 REST、SDK 和 MCP 访问权限。 在 AskChem-Bench 上，将 GPT-5.5 阅读器置于 AskChem 中可产生 100% 可解析的 DOI，而没有检索时则为 88.3%，并且是五个测试系统中最高的引用密度。 AskChem 的直播地址为, a claim-centered infrastructure for cross-paper chemistry search. AskChem changes the unit of retrieval from the paper to the provenance-carrying claim: each paper is converted into atomic, typed claims, each grounded by a source DOI and a verbatim quote or an explicit evidence locator. Over this shared claim store, AskChem exposes complementary structures for search and synthesis: a stabilized faceted taxonomy for hierarchical retrieval and browsing, an evidence graph linking claims through relations, and an exploratory living taxonomy that situates indexed papers under scientific principles. AskChem currently indexes 2.4M claims from 147K papers and provides a web interface, as well as REST, SDK, and MCP access for AI agents. On AskChem-Bench, grounding a GPT-5.5 reader in AskChem yields 100% resolvable DOIs, compared with 88.3% without retrieval, and the highest citation density among five tested systems. AskChem is live athttps://askchem.org.

1 简介Introduction

化学家经常提出一些问题，这些问题的答案必须从多篇论文的具体发现中汇总，而不是从单篇论文中检索。 例如，当化学家问“Chemists often ask questions whose answers must be assembled from specific findings across many papers, rather than retrieved from a single paper. For example, when a chemist asks “已报道哪些 CO 电催化剂What electrocatalysts have been reported for CO2还原成二氧化碳，法拉第效率是多少？reduction to CO, and at what Faradaic efficiency?”，答案作为关于催化剂、反应条件、测量和机制的单独声明分布在许多论文中。现有的文献搜索界面主要返回排序的文档列表，让科学家打开论文、查找相关证据、验证报告的值并手动组装答案。”, the answer is distributed across many papers as individual claims about catalysts, reaction conditions, measurements, and mechanisms. Existing literature-search interfaces primarily return ranked lists of documents, leaving the scientist to open papers, locate relevant evidence, verify reported values, and assemble the answer by hand.

这种以文档为中心的界面与科学家和人工智能代理使用文献的方式越来越不一致。 LLM 代理现在调查论文并计划实验This document-centered interface is increasingly misaligned with how scientists and AI agents use the literature. LLM agents now survey papers and plan experiments(Boiko et al., 2023)，但继承了其检索工具的局限性：文档检索并不直接暴露权利要求级别的证据、领域的组织或合成所需的文档之间的关系。当要求法学硕士从参数记忆中回答时，法学硕士可能会编造看似合理的引文, but inherit the limitations of their retrieval tools: document retrieval does not directly expose evidence at the claim level, the domain’s organization, or the relationships between documents needed for synthesis. When asked to answer from parametric memory instead, an LLM may fabricate plausible-looking citations(Agrawal et al., 2024).

A claim is an atomic, typed assertion extracted from a paper, grounded by a source DOI and a verbatim quote or an explicit evidence locator. As the Segment Anything Model decomposed images into reusable regions(Kirillov et al., 2023)，我们使用法学硕士将论文细分为权利要求。 通过直接索引声明，AskChem 允许用户检索特定的发现、检查其证据并组装跨论文答案，而无需首先阅读和过滤整个文档。, we use LLMs to segment papers into claims. By indexing claims directly, AskChem lets users retrieve specific findings, inspect their evidence, and assemble cross-paper answers without first reading and filtering whole documents.

AskChem 沿着稳定的、语料库引发的方面组织声明，例如反应类型、物质类别、应用、技术、机制主题、声明类型、数据和时间。这些方面不是科学结构本身，而是支持分层检索、浏览、分组和时间探索的操作视图。AskChem organizes claims along stabilized, corpus-induced facets such as reaction type, substance class, application, technique, mechanism topic, claim type, data, and time. 作为探索性的延伸，AskChem 将纸质叶子置于原理、理论、模型、机制和现象之下。这种以原则为中心的层次结构提供了索引语料库的不断发展的概述，补充了用于搜索的操作方面。As an exploratory extension, AskChem places paper-grounded leaves beneath principles, theories, models, mechanisms, and phenomena. This principle-centered hierarchy provides an evolving overview of the indexed corpus, complementing the operational facets used for search.

4 稳定的多面分类法Stabilized Faceted Taxonomy

诱导稳定的搜索方面。Inducing stable search facets.

AskChem 使用多面分类法根据索赔所关注的内容来组织索赔。 AskChem 不是强加完全预定义的化学本体，而是在消化论文和提取声明的同时引入类别路径。这些路径揭示了反应、物质、应用、技术和机制中反复出现的术语。然后，我们通过规范的顶级路由、同义词标准化和近重复子类别的模糊聚类来稳定诱导路径。结果是一组源自语料库但持久的 L1/L2/L3 路径，适合生产检索和浏览。AskChem uses a faceted taxonomy to organize claims by what they concern. Rather than imposing a fully predefined chemistry ontology, AskChem induces category paths while digesting papers and extracting claims. These paths reveal recurring terminology across reactions, substances, applications, techniques, and mechanisms. We then stabilize the induced paths through canonical top-level routing, synonym normalization, and fuzzy clustering of near-duplicate subcategories. The result is a corpus-derived but persistent set of L1/L2/L3 paths suitable for production retrieval and browsing.

9 结论Conclusion

AskChem 展示了一种以权利要求为中心的替代仅文档化学文献搜索的方法。通过将来源声明作为中央单元，AskChem 支持搜索结果，这些结果可以根据来源引用和 DOI 进行验证，通过稳定的方面进行组织，并通过论文之间的证据关系进行连接。部署的系统以语料库规模运行，并向人类用户和人工智能代理公开相同的声明对象。在跨论文化学问题上，AskChem 提高了引文基础，表明以权利要求为中心的基础设施是化学文献合成的实用基础。AskChem demonstrates a claim-centered alternative to document-only chemistry literature search. By making provenance-carrying claims the central unit, AskChem supports search results that can be verified against source quotes and DOIs, organized through stabilized facets, and connected through evidence relations across papers. The deployed system operates at corpus scale and exposes the same claim objects to both human users and AI agents. On cross-paper chemistry questions, AskChem improves citation groundedness, suggesting that claim-centered infrastructure is a practical foundation for chemistry literature synthesis.

局限性Limitations

该语料库仅涵盖化学的一小部分，并且摘要提取比全文提取浅。 LLM 生成的权利要求、关系和分类布局可能是错误的； AskChem-Bench 衡量 30 个问题的基础性，而不是完全的事实准确性或用户实用性。 基于字符串的分类规范化可以合并不同的类别或保留接近重复的类别，并且其检索增益并未被孤立。The corpus covers only a fraction of chemistry, and abstract extraction is shallower than full-text extraction. LLM-generated claims, relations, and taxonomy placements can be wrong; AskChem-Bench measures groundedness on 30 questions rather than full factual accuracy or user utility. String-based taxonomy normalization can merge distinct categories or retain near-duplicates, and its retrieval gain has not been isolated.

道德和更广泛的影响Ethics and Broader Impact

AskChem 使用基于 LLM 的提取，其声明可能包含错误。因此，每个声明都包含出处：源 DOI 和逐字引用，以便用户可以根据原始论文进行验证。该界面还支持社区标记。 AskChem 旨在协助文献检索和综合，而不是取代阅读主要资源以做出关键决策。通过将人工智能辅助化学工作流程建立在可验证的引文基础上，它的目的是减少引文伪造并使生成的答案更容易审核。该系统使用公共元数据、摘要和开放获取全文（如果有）对论​​文进行索引。公共索引公开声明文本、源元数据和出处，但不会重新分发付费全文。AskChem uses LLM-based extraction, and its claims may contain errors. Each claim therefore includes provenance: a source DOI and a verbatim quote, so users can verify it against the original paper. The interface also supports community flagging. AskChem is intended to assist literature search and synthesis, not to replace reading primary sources for critical decisions. By grounding AI-assisted chemistry workflows in verifiable citations, it aims to reduce citation fabrication and make generated answers easier to audit. The system indexes papers using public metadata, abstracts, and open-access full text where available. The public index exposes claim text, source metadata, and provenance, but does not redistribute paywalled full text.
