摘要Abstract

我们研究了 T 轮范围内 N 个交互模块之间的在线资源分配。与标准在线优化不同，为模块分配资源的成本不是外生的，而是We study online resource allocation among N interacting modules over a horizon of T rounds. Unlike standard online optimization, the cost of allocating resources to a module is not exogenous but内生的endogenous：它取决于通过交互矩阵 W 的整个分配向量，该矩阵 W 编码模块之间的成对合作和竞争。这种内生性抓住了最近关于竞争成本发现的研究中发现的一个现象，即与智能资源分配最相关的成本是在竞争过程本身中构成的，而不是提前给出的。我们分析了对应于三种成本处理范式的三种分配规则：（I）统一分配（成本无知），（II）门控分配（成本估计），以及（III）通过交互反馈的乘法权重更新进行竞争性分配（成本揭示）。我们的主要结果在三种范式之间建立了严格的分离。在具有有限变化的对抗性任务序列下，统一分配会导致遗憾 Ω(T)；门控分配实现 O(T²″³)；竞争性分配实现了 O(√(T log N))，差距归因于竞争性分配利用交互反馈揭示的内生成本信息的能力。我们进一步表明，W 的拓扑控制着计算与遗憾的权衡：完全交互 (|E| = O(N²)) 产生最紧的界限，但每步成本最高，而稀疏拓扑 (|E| = O(N)) 最多增加一个 O(√(log N)) 因子，同时将每步成本从 O(N²) 降低到 O(N)。我们确定了最小化计算×遗憾乘积的最佳稀疏性，并表明具有合作和竞争链接的环结构拓扑，其中: it depends on the entire allocation vector through an interaction matrix W that encodes pairwise cooperation and competition among modules. This endogeneity captures a phenomenon identified in recent work on competitive cost discovery—that the costs most relevant to intelligent resource allocation are constituted in the competitive process itself, not given in advance. We analyze three allocation rules that correspond to three paradigms of cost handling: (I) uniform allocation (cost-ignorant), (II) gated allocation (cost-estimating), and (III) competitive allocation via multiplicative weights update with interaction feedback (cost-revealing). Our main results establish a strict separation among the three paradigms. 1 简介1 Introduction

当分配成本事先未知但由分配过程本身构成时，模块化系统应如何在其组件之间分配有限的计算资源？这个问题在多个专用模块（每个模块负责不同的认知功能）共享公共资源预算并通过合作和竞争进行交互的架构中自然出现。How should a modular system allocate finite computational resources among its components when the costs of allocation are not known in advance but are constituted through the allocation process itself? This question arises naturally in architectures where multiple specialized modules— each responsible for a distinct cognitive function—share a common resource budget and interact through cooperation and competition.

在之前的工作中（Chai & Li，2025d），我们从哲学和信息论的角度论证了与智能分配最相关的成本——机会成本和干扰成本——是分布式的、默认的和动态构成的。没有集中控制器可以预先计算它们，因为它们依赖于所有模块的并发状态和策略。这一观察是定性的。本文将其定量化。In prior work (Chai & Li, 2025d), we argued on philosophical and information-theoretic grounds that the costs most relevant to intelligent allocation—opportunity costs and interference costs—are distributed, tacit, and dynamically constituted. No centralized controller can precompute them because they depend on the concurrent states and strategies of all modules. This observation was qualitative. The present paper makes it quantitative.

我们将分配问题形式化为We formalize the allocation problem as an instance of具有耦合损失的在线凸优化online convex optimization with coupled losses——每轮的损失函数不仅取决于学习者的行为，还取决于由交互矩阵 W 介导的行为组件之间的成对交互。我们分析了与 Chai & Li (2025d) 中确定的三种范式相对应的三种分配规则：成本无知（统一）、成本估计（门控）和成本揭示（竞争）。我们的主要贡献是在遗憾（在线学习绩效的标准衡量标准）方面对这些范式进行了正式分离，证明竞争性分配的优势不仅是启发式的，而且是信息论的。—a setting in which the loss function at each round depends not only on the learner’s action but on pairwise interactions among the action’s components, mediated by an interaction matrix W. We analyze three allocation rules that correspond to the three paradigms identified in Chai & Li (2025d): cost-ignorant (uniform), cost-estimating (gated), and cost-revealing (competitive). Our main contribution is a formal separation among these paradigms in terms of regret—the standard measure of online learning performance—establishing that competitive allocation’s advantage is not merely heuristic but information-theoretic.

7.1 成本内生性的本质7.1 The Nature of Cost Endogeneity

我们的结果形式化了一个易于表述但具有深远影响的区别：Our results formalize a distinction that is easy to state but has far-reaching consequences: the difference between不知道not knowing成本（部分可观察性）和成本the costs (partial observability) and the costs不存在not existing分配之前（内生性）。标准在线学习假设前者：有一个学习者不知道的固定损失函数，必须通过反馈来学习。我们的模型假设后者：损失函数由学习者自己的动作通过交互矩阵W构成。prior to the allocation (endogeneity). Standard online learning assumes the former: there is a fixed loss function that the learner does not know and must learn through feedback. Our model assumes the latter: the loss function is constituted by the learner’s own action through the interaction matrix W.

这种区别很重要，因为它决定了哪种分配模式是合适的。如果成本只是未知且是外生的，则门控分配（范式 II）就足够了：足够强大的估计器可以学习成本函数并预测最佳分配前馈。但是，如果成本是内生的（如果它们通过交互效应依赖于分配本身），那么任何前馈估计器都无法避免 O(T^{2/3}) 惩罚，因为它尝试估计的成本会随着每次分配而变化。只有使用分配过程本身作为信息源的范式（范式III）才能实现最优的O(√T)率。This distinction matters because it determines which allocation paradigm is appropriate. If costs are merely unknown but exogenous, gated allocation (Paradigm II) is adequate: a sufficiently powerful estimator can learn the cost function and predict optimal allocations feedforward. But if costs are endogenous—if they depend on the allocation itself through interaction effects—then no feedforward estimator can avoid the O(T^{2/3}) penalty, because the cost it is trying to estimate changes with every allocation it makes. Only a paradigm that uses the allocation process itself as an information source (Paradigm III) can achieve the optimal O(√T) rate.

A gated allocator is a price-taker: it observes prices (costs) and responds optimally. A competitive allocator is a price-maker: its actions constitute the prices through the competitive process. The regret separation between Paradigms II and III is the formal expression of a deep insight from institutional economics: in markets with endogenous prices, decentralized competition outperforms centralized estimation not because it has better algorithms but because it has access to information that centralized estimation cannot, in principle, obtain.
