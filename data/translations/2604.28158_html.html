摘要Abstract

现有的研究基础设施基本上是以文档为中心的，提供论文之间的引用链接，但缺乏方法论演变的明确表示。特别是，它没有捕捉到解释研究方法如何以及为何出现、适应和相互构建的结构化关系。随着人工智能驱动的研究代理作为科学知识的新消费者阶层的崛起，这种限制变得越来越严重，因为此类代理无法从非结构化文本可靠地重建方法演化拓扑。我们引入了 Intern-Atlas，这是一种方法论演化图，它可以自动识别方法级实体，推断方法论之间的沿袭关系，并捕获推动连续创新之间过渡的瓶颈。建自Existing research infrastructure is fundamentally document-centric, providing citation links between papers but lacking explicit representations of methodological evolution. In particular, it does not capture the structured relationships that explain how and why research methods emerge, adapt, and build upon one another. With the rise of AI-driven research agents as a new class of consumers of scientific knowledge, this limitation becomes increasingly consequential, as such agents cannot reliably reconstruct method evolution topologies from unstructured text. We introduce Intern-Atlas, a methodological evolution graph that automatically identifies method-level entities, infers lineage relationships among methodologies, and captures the bottlenecks that drive transitions between successive innovations. 1 简介Introduction

传统上，人工智能的科学进展是通过论文记录的，而为组织这一知识体系而开发的基础设施反映了以文档为中心的范式。谷歌学术等平台Scientific progress in artificial intelligence has traditionally been documented through papers, and the infrastructure developed to organize this body of knowledge reflects a document-centric paradigm. Platforms such as Google Scholar[google_scholar], 语义学者, Semantic Scholar[ammar2018construction]和 OpenAlex, and OpenAlex[priem2022openalex]将论文视为原子单元：它们索引标题、摘要和引用计数，并通过引用链接将论文彼此连接起来。这种范式已经为人类研究人员服务了数十年。例如，为了了解视觉变换器的出现，研究人员可以从这些系统中检索一组相关论文，阅读它们，并通过自注意力机制重建从卷积神经网络到现代架构的谱系。该工作流程中的关键步骤是从叙述文本中提取方法之间的结构关系并将其组装成连贯的进化图景，这完全是在研究人员的头脑中进行的（图treat the paper as the atomic unit: they index titles, abstracts, and citation counts, and they connect papers to one another through citation links. This paradigm has served human researchers for decades. To understand the emergence of vision transformers, for example, a researcher can retrieve a set of relevant papers from these systems, read them, and reconstruct the lineage from convolutional neural networks through self-attention mechanisms to modern architectures. The critical step in this workflow, extracting the structural relationships among methods from narrative text and assembling them into a coherent evolutionary picture, is performed entirely inside the researcher’s head (Figure1，左）。, left).

一个新的知识消费者阶层正在出现。人工智能驱动的研究代理可以自动化从假设生成到实验的科学流程A new class of knowledge consumer is now emerging. AI-driven research agents that automate the scientific pipeline from hypothesis generation to experimentation[lu2024ai, sanyal2025spark, fan2026deepinnovator]代表了一种完全不同的知识消费者。与人类不同，这些代理无法从非结构化文本可靠地重建方法进化拓扑。它们的参数记忆是一种有损压缩，不足以代表低频或长尾方法知识。它们的自回归推理作为固定深度的前向计算而不是显式的图遍历来运行，限制了它们枚举分支方法空间的能力。最关键的是，他们缺乏区分研究领域的真正差距和自身内部表征的差距的能力，因为两者都表现为缺乏相关激活。因此，它们最持久的局限性在于创意的生成，其中产出质量取决于对方法论景观的结构理解：不仅存在哪些方法，而且还包括它们如何演变、它们解决了哪些限制以及哪些方向尚未探索。represent a fundamentally different kind of knowledge consumer. Unlike humans, these agents cannot reliably reconstruct method evolution topologies from unstructured text. Their parametric memory is a lossy compression that underrepresents low-frequency or long-tail methodological knowledge.Their autoregressive inference operates as fixed-depth forward computation rather than explicit graph traversal, limiting their ability to enumerate branching method spaces. Most critically, they lack the capacity to distinguish between genuine gaps in the research landscape and gaps in their own internal representations, since both manifest as an absence of relevant activations. As a result, their most persistent limitation lies in idea generation, where output quality depends on structural understanding of the methodological landscape: not only which methods exist, but how they evolved, what constraints they addressed, and which directions remain unexplored.

这种差距反映了一种反复出现的历史模式。结构化知识基础设施变得至关重要，不是当人类需要它时，而是当自动化系统出现而无法有效地处理非结构化数据时。蛋白质数据库 (PDB)This gap reflects a recurring historical pattern. Structured knowledge infrastructure becomes essential not when humans require it, but when automated systems emerge that cannot operate effectively over unstructured data. The Protein Data Bank (PDB)[berman2000protein]标准化蛋白质结构比 AlphaFold 早几十年standardized protein structures decades before AlphaFold[jumper2021highly]揭示了它们作为机器消耗训练数据的全部价值。图像网revealed their full value as machine-consumable training data. ImageNet[deng2009imagenet]在广泛采用依赖于大规模结构化注释的卷积神经网络之前，将视觉数据组织成分层标签。在每种情况下，新的计算消费者的出现将潜在结构转化为明确的需求。科学方法论现在也出现了类似的拐点：organized visual data into hierarchical labels prior to the widespread adoption of convolutional neural networks that depended on large-scale structured annotations. In each case, the emergence of a new computational consumer transformed latent structure into an explicit requirement. A similar inflection point is now occurring in scientific methodology:人工智能研究代理已经出现，但支持它们所需的结构化数据层仍然缺失AI research agents have emerged, yet the structured data layer required to support them remains absent.

Atlas processes papers from top-tier AI venues, extracts method entities with alias resolution, classifies every citation edge into semantic types, and grounds each non-background edge in a verbatim quote with structured bottleneck and mechanism annotations. The resulting graph forms a queryable causal topology of methodological evolution, providing a persistent data layer that can be directly consumed by downstream systems (Figure1，对）。除了图构建之外，识别有意义的进化链还带来了额外的挑战。方法论进展形成有向无环图：单一方法如 Transformer, right). Beyond graph construction, identifying meaningful evolution chains introduces an additional challenge. Methodological progress forms a directed acyclic graph: a single method such as the Transformer[vaswani2017attention]产生多个分支，包括 BERTgives rise to multiple branches, including BERT[devlin2019bert], GPT [radford2018improving, radford2019language, brown2020language], 视觉变压器, Vision Transformer[dosovitskiy2020image, khan2022transformers, han2022survey]和 DETR, and DETR[carion2020end]。在每个分支点提交的贪婪遍历策略会丢弃替代轨迹。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了自引导时间蒙特卡罗树搜索（SGT-MCTS），它平衡了高置信度路径的利用与对访问不足的分支的探索，同时加强了时间一致性，从而能够更忠实地重建方法论的演变。. Greedy traversal strategies that commit at each branching point discard alternative trajectories. To address this, we propose Self-Guided Temporal Monte Carlo Tree Search (SGT-MCTS), which balances exploitation of high-confidence paths with exploration of under-visited branches while enforcing temporal coherence, enabling more faithful reconstruction of methodological evolution.

我们从三个互补的维度评估 Intern-Atlas。我们首先通过将构建的进化链与专家策划的链进行定量比较来评估内在图质量。然后，我们检查其在想法评估中的实用性，测试基于图形的评分信号是否表现出与人类层级的单调对齐。最后，我们通过比较使用和不使用图派生进化上下文产生的输出来研究其对于想法生成的价值。总而言之，我们的结果表明，方法论演化图可以作为新兴的自动化科学发现生态系统的基础数据层。We evaluate Intern-Atlas along three complementary dimensions. We first assess intrinsic graph quality by quantitatively comparing constructed evolution chains against expert-curated chains. We then examine its utility for idea evaluation, testing whether graph-grounded scoring signals exhibit monotonic alignment with human tiers. Finally, we investigate its value for idea generation by comparing outputs produced with and without access to graph-derived evolutionary context. Taken together, our results suggest that methodological evolution graphs can serve as a foundational data layer for the emerging ecosystem of automated scientific discovery.

D.1 方法评估基准和指标Method-evaluation Benchmark and Metrics

目的和选择。Purpose and selection.

我们根据 30 篇高影响力的调查论文构建了方法演化基准，并使用调查作为方法演化专家共识的代表。 候选调查根据五个标准进行选择：权威性、影响力、经典地位、子领域多样性和以方法为中心。在实践中，我们 优先考虑在公认的场所发布、被社区广泛引用、通常用作领域级分类法、涵盖不同人工智能子领域的调查， 并包含对方法演变的明确讨论，而不仅仅是应用摘要。这些调查为静态和静态分析提供了外部参考 图覆盖率和谱系重建评估。We construct the method-evolution benchmark from 30 high-impact survey papers, using surveys as proxies for expert consensus on methodological evolution. Candidate surveys are selected according to five criteria: authority, impact, classic status, subfield diversity, and method-centricity. In practice, we prioritize surveys published in recognized venues, widely cited by the community, commonly used as field-level taxonomies, covering diverse AI subfields, and containing explicit discussions of method evolution rather than only application summaries. These surveys provide external references for both static graph coverage and lineage reconstruction evaluation.

每项调查均分两个阶段进行。首先，法学硕士从论文中提取讨论的方法、定向进化关系和基于叙述的方法链。 调查文本和分类数字。其次，领域研究人员通过删除幻觉方法、纠正关系来手动审核提取的结果 方向，合并重复的方法名称，并根据原始调查文本或图形检查每个保留的关系。最终基准包含 30 由调查得出的方法演化图，具有 2,268 个节点、1,462 个有向演化边和 133 个参考演化链。Each survey is processed in two stages.
