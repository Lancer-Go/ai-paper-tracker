BiST：用于句子结构和时态分类的黄金标准孟加拉英语双语语料库，具有注释者间协议BiST: A Gold Standard Bangla-English Bilingual Corpus for Sentence Structure and Tense Classification with Inter-Annotator Agreement

摘要Abstract

高质量的双语资源仍然是在资源匮乏的环境中推进多语言 NLP 的关键瓶颈，尤其是孟加拉语。为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了 BiST，这是一个经过严格策划的孟加拉英语语料库，用于句子级语法分类，在两个基本维度上进行注释：句法结构（简单、复杂、复合、复合复合）和时态（现在、过去、将来）。该语料库由开放许可的百科全书资源和自然组成的会话文本编译而成，经过系统预处理和自动语言识别，生成了 30,534 个句子，其中包括 17,465 个英语和 13,069 个孟加拉语实例。通过具有三个独立注释器和维度方面的 Fleiss’ Kappa 的多级框架确保注释质量（High-quality bilingual resources remain a critical bottleneck for advancing multilingual NLP in low-resource settings, particularly for Bangla. To mitigate this gap, we introduce BiST, a rigorously curated Bangla-English corpus for sentence-level grammatical classification, annotated across two fundamental dimensions: syntactic structure (Simple, Complex, Compound, Complex-Compound) and tense (Present, Past, Future). The corpus is compiled from open-licensed encyclopedic sources and naturally composed conversational text, followed by systematic preprocessing and automated language identification, resulting in 30,534 sentences, including 17,465 English and 13,069 Bangla instances. Annotation quality is ensured through a multi-stage framework with three independent annotators and dimension-wise Fleiss’ Kappa (κ\kappa）协议，产生可靠且可重复的标签) agreement, yielding reliable and reproducible labels withκ\kappa结构和时间注释的值分别为 0.82 和 0.88。统计分析证明了真实的结构和时间分布，而基线评估表明，利用互补的特定语言表示的双编码器架构始终优于强大的多语言编码器。除了基准测试之外，BiST 还提供显式语言监督，支持语法建模任务，包括受控文本生成、自动反馈生成和跨语言表示学习。该语料库为双语语法建模建立了统一的资源，并促进了以语言为基础的多语言研究。values of 0.82 and 0.88 for structural and temporal annotation, respectively. Statistical analyses demonstrate realistic structural and temporal distributions, while baseline evaluations show that dual-encoder architectures leveraging complementary language-specific representations consistently outperform strong multilingual encoders. Beyond benchmarking, BiST provides explicit linguistic supervision that supports grammatical modeling tasks, including controlled text generation, automated feedback generation, and cross-lingual representation learning. The corpus establishes a unified resource for bilingual grammatical modeling and facilitates linguistically grounded multilingual research.

本文提出了 BiST，这是一个严格构建的孟加拉英语双语语料库，用于跨结构和时间维度的句子级语法分类。该语料库旨在解决资源匮乏环境中可靠双语资源稀缺的问题，集成了系统数据收集、多阶段注释和注释者间协议，以确保一致性和可重复性。语料库级分析证实了现实的语言分布和结构多样性，而基线实验表明双编码器架构始终优于一般的多语言编码器，凸显了特定于语言的表示的重要性。 BiST 因此为双语语法建模和跨语言研究建立了一个统一的、经过实验验证的基准。This paper presented BiST, a rigorously constructed Bangla-English bilingual corpus for sentence-level grammatical classification across structural and temporal dimensions. Designed to address the scarcity of reliable bilingual resources for low-resource settings, the corpus integrates systematic data collection, and multi-stage annotation with inter-annotator agreement to ensure consistency and reproducibility. Corpus-level analyses confirm realistic linguistic distributions and structural diversity, while baseline experiments demonstrate that dual-encoder architectures consistently outperform general multilingual encoders, highlighting the importance of language-specific representations. BiST thus establishes a unified and experimentally validated benchmark for bilingual grammatical modeling and cross-lingual research.

尽管做出了贡献，拟议的 BiST 语料库仍存在一些局限性。注释方案是粗粒度的，仅关注结构和时态，并且不捕获更精细的语言现象，例如体、情态或话语关系。该语料库不包括代码转换实例，这可能会限制现实世界的可变性。此外，虽然语料库规模较大，但规模和领域多样性的进一步扩大将提高其普遍性。 在未来的工作中，我们计划通过额外的语法维度和语言现象来扩展语料库，并探索孟加拉语-英语混合代码场景，以更好地反映现实世界的语言使用。我们还旨在在基准上评估新兴的多语言基础模型，以进一步推进低资源语法理解。Despite its contributions, the proposed BiST corpus has several limitations. The annotation scheme is coarse-grained, focusing only on structure and tense, and does not capture finer linguistic phenomena such as aspect, modality, or discourse relations. The corpus excludes code-switched instances, which may limit real-world variability. Additionally, while the corpus is moderately large, further expansion in size and domain diversity would improve its generalizability. In future work, we plan to expand the corpus with additional grammatical dimensions and linguistic phenomena, as well as explore code-mixed Bangla-English scenarios to better reflect real-world language use. We also aim to evaluate emerging multilingual foundation models on the benchmark to further advance low-resource grammatical understanding.
