人机协作将群体监管从社会共享重新配置为混合共同监管Human–AI Collaboration Reconfigures Group Regulation from Socially Shared to Hybrid Co-Regulation

Yujing Zhang    Xianghui Meng    Shihui Feng    Jionghao Lin
摘要Abstract

生成式人工智能（GenAI）越来越多地用于协作学习，但其对群体如何调节协作的影响仍不清楚。有效的协作不仅取决于团体讨论的内容，还取决于他们如何通过共同监管和社会共享监管来共同管理目标、参与、策略使用、监控和修复。我们在一项平行组随机实验中比较了人类与人工智能和人类与人类之间的协作调节，其中 71 名大学生使用可用或不可用的 GenAI 完成相同的协作任务。我们以人类话语为重点，利用统计分析来检验协作监管在监管模式、监管流程和参与焦点之间分布的差异。结果表明，GenAI 的可用性将监管从主要的社会共享形式转变为更加混合的共同监管形式，并选择性地增加了指令性、障碍导向性和情感性监管过程。然而，不同条件下的参与焦点分布大致相似。这些发现表明，GenAI 重塑了协作中监管责任的分配，并为人工智能支持的协作学习的以人为本的设计提供了启示。Generative AI (GenAI) is increasingly used in collaborative learning, yet its effects on how groups regulate collaboration remain unclear. Effective collaboration depends not only on what groups discuss, but on how they jointly manage goals, participation, strategy use, monitoring, and repair through co-regulation and socially shared regulation. We compared collaborative regulation between Human–AI and Human–Human groups in a parallel-group randomised experiment with 71 university students completing the same collaborative tasks with GenAI either available or unavailable. Focusing on human discourse, we used statistical analyses to examine differences in the distribution of collaborative regulation across regulatory modes, regulatory processes, and participatory focuses. Results showed that GenAI availability shifted regulation away from predominantly socially shared forms towards more hybrid co-regulatory forms, with selective increases in directive, obstacle-oriented, and affective regulatory processes. Participatory-focus distributions, however, were broadly similar across conditions. These findings suggest that GenAI reshapes the distribution of regulatory responsibility in collaboration and offer implications for the human-centred design of AI-supported collaborative learning.

1 简介Introduction

计算机支持的协作学习 (CSCL) 中的生成人工智能 (GenAI) 越来越被概念化，不仅作为一种工具，而且作为一种交互式贡献者，可以在小组工作期间产生想法、挑战假设并扩展集体推理Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) in computer-supported collaborative learning (CSCL) is increasingly conceptualised not only as a tool, but also as an interactive contributor that can generate ideas, challenge assumptions, and extend collective reasoning during group work[14]。然而，有效的合作不仅仅取决于参与，还取决于如何通过共同规划、监测、评估和长期适应来组织联合活动. However, effective collaboration depends not simply on participation, but on how joint activity is organised through shared planning, monitoring, evaluation, and adaptation over time[12]。在协作环境中，监管超出了个人自我监管学习的范围，包括共同监管（CoRL）和社会共享监管（SSRL），这两者都是有效协作的核心，因为它们依赖于团队成员之间的协调和分担责任. In collaborative contexts, regulation extends beyond individual self-regulated learning to include co-regulation (CoRL) and socially shared regulation (SSRL), both of which are central to effective collaboration because they rely on coordination and shared responsibility among group members[6].

尽管它们很重要，但关于 GenAI 如何与 CoRL 和 SSRL 相关的经验证据仍然有限Despite their importance, empirical evidence on how GenAI relates to CoRL and SSRL remains limited[9]。随着 GenAI 被整合到协作学习环境中而无需仔细检查群体监管，这种差距变得越来越重要。最近的研究表明 GenAI 可以重塑协作解决问题的参与和互动. This gap is increasingly important as GenAI is integrated into collaborative learning settings without closely examining group regulation. Recent studies suggest that GenAI can reshape participation and interaction in collaborative problem solving[5]，引发了人们对 GenAI 进入团队工作后监管责任如何重新分配的担忧。因此，解决这一差距对于推进人机协作理论并为教育领域的以人为本的人工智能设计提供信息非常重要。, raising concerns about how regulatory responsibility is redistributed when GenAI enters group work. Addressing this gap is therefore important for advancing Human–AI collaboration theory and informing human-centred AI design in education.

为了检验 GenAI 的可用性是否会重新配置协作监管，本研究设计并部署了一个明确面向支持协作监管的对话式 GenAI 代理。代理人没有提供直接的解决方案，而是通过对话进行干预，以塑造团体如何协调和规范其联合活动。通过比较从事相同任务的人类-人工智能和人类-人类群体，我们研究了协作监管在监管模式、监管流程和参与重点方面的分布差异。具体来说，我们问：To examine whether GenAI availability reconfigures collaborative regulation, this study designed and deployed a conversational GenAI agent explicitly oriented towards supporting collaborative regulation. Rather than providing direct solutions, the agent intervened through dialogue to shape how groups coordinated and regulated their joint activity. By comparing Human–AI and Human–Human groups working on the same tasks, we examined differences in the distribution of collaborative regulation across regulatory modes, regulatory processes, and participatory focuses. Specifically, we asked:人类与人工智能和人类与人类群体之间的协作监管分布有何差异？What differences are observed in the distribution of collaborative regulation between Human–AI and Human–Human groups?

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 中海集运的共同监管和社会共享监管Co-Regulation and Socially Shared Regulation in CSCL

当协作任务变得复杂且开放时，团体必须通过共同调节学习（CoRL）和社会共享学习调节（SSRL）来调节他们的活动When collaborative tasks become complex and open-ended, groups must regulate their activity through co-regulated learning (CoRL), and socially shared regulation of learning (SSRL)[6]。 CoRL 和 SSRL 是互动的，并通过对话制定。 CoRL 描述了同伴支持的监管（例如，提示、指导、反馈），帮助个人管理他们的学习，而 SSRL 涉及团体对目标、策略、监控和反思的深思熟虑的集体监管。 CoRL 通常充当桥梁，使 SSRL 得以出现并稳定下来. CoRL and SSRL are interactional and enacted through dialogue. CoRL describes peer-supported regulation (e.g., prompts, guidance, feedback) that helps individuals manage their learning, while SSRL involves the group’s deliberate, collective regulation of goals, strategies, monitoring, and reflection. CoRL often serves as a bridge that enables SSRL to emerge and stabilise[6]。因此，SSRL 被广泛视为有效协作的标志，捕捉团队共同制定任务、协商方法、跟踪进度和适应挑战的时刻. As such, SSRL is widely treated as a hallmark of effective collaboration, capturing moments when groups jointly frame the task, negotiate approaches, track progress, and adapt to challenges[6, 8].

然而，在中海集运中，如果没有支持，这些形式的监管很少能可靠地出现。学习者带来不同的目标、知识和情感状态，因此简单地将它们放在一起并不能确保 CoRL 或 SSRL 的发展In CSCL, however, these forms of regulation rarely arise reliably without support. Learners bring differing goals, knowledge, and affective states, so simply placing them together does not ensure the development of CoRL or SSRL[10]。富有成效的协作需要持续的协调，包括共享任务理解、角色协调、进度监控和期望管理，并且这些监管要求随着交互的展开而变化. Productive collaboration requires ongoing alignment, including shared task understanding, role coordination, progress monitoring, and expectation management, and these regulatory demands shift as interaction unfolds[7]。这使得脚手架 CoRL 和 SSRL 成为 CSCL 的核心设计问题，也是旨在改善协作过程和结果的干预措施的关键目标. This makes scaffolding CoRL and SSRL a central design problem in CSCL and a key target for interventions intended to improve collaborative process and outcomes[3].

2.2 CSCL 中的 GenAI 代理GenAI Agent in CSCL

GenAI 的最新进展为 CSCL 干预开辟了新的可能性：除了信息传递之外，GenAI 系统还可以产生想法、提出问题和支架推理，这表明在塑造协作学习过程中的潜在作用Recent advances in GenAI have opened new possibilities for CSCL interventions: beyond information delivery, GenAI systems can generate ideas, pose questions, and scaffold reasoning, suggesting potential roles in shaping collaborative learning processes[1]。然而，尽管共同监管和社会共享监管在有效的 CSCL 中发挥着核心作用，但很少有研究探讨 GenAI 是否能够在小组学习中有意义地塑造协作监管。一项著名的早期研究引入了 GenAI 代理来促进元认知反射以支持 SSRL，但在通过语言对齐进行评估时发现改善调节的证据有限. Yet, despite the central role of co-regulation and socially shared regulation in effective CSCL, few studies have examined whether GenAI can meaningfully shape collaborative regulation in group learning. A notable early study introduced a GenAI agent to prompt metacognitive reflection in support of SSRL, but found limited evidence of improved regulation when assessed through linguistic alignment[3]。至关重要的是，我们认为这反映了分析的局限性，而不是人工智能支持的失败：将监管视为语言使用的静态转变不足以捕捉其分布式和突发性的本质。基于这一见解，我们将 CoRL 和 SSRL 视为交互现象，可以通过群体交互中监管模式、监管过程和参与性话语动作的分布来观察。. Crucially, we argue that this reflects an analytic limitation rather than a failure of AI support: treating regulation as a static shift in language use is insufficient to capture its distributed and emergent nature. Building on this insight, we treat CoRL and SSRL as interactional phenomena that can be observed through the distribution of regulatory modes, regulatory processes, and participatory discourse moves in group interaction.

3.2 小组任务设计Group Task Design

小组任务以集体智慧范式为基础，该范式认为小组绩效反映了稳定的集体智慧因素，而不仅仅是特定于任务的技能The group tasks were grounded in the collective intelligence paradigm, which posits that group performance reflects a stable collective intelligence factor rather than task-specific skills alone[13]。为了捕捉不同的协调和换位思考的需求，我们使用了两个互补的任务：一个关于学术不端行为的道德推理案例，需要基于价值的讨论并在多项选择选项中达成合理的共识；以及一个购物计划任务，需要整合约束并制定具体的路线计划。. To capture different coordination and perspective-taking demands, we used two complementary tasks: a moral-reasoning case on academic misconduct that required value-based discussion and justified consensus among multiple-choice options, and a shopping-planning task that required integrating constraints and producing a concrete route plan.

3.4 测量Measure

3.4.1 集团监管动态Group Regulatory Dynamics

使用两级编码框架对群体监管动态进行了分析，该框架将扩展的监管活动与即时的话语行为区分开来。Group regulatory dynamics were analysed using a two-level coding framework that distinguishes extended regulatory activity from moment-to-moment discourse actions.

在情节级别，监管情节被定义为专注于单个监管问题的连续片段，通常跨越多个回合。当监管问题出现时，事件就开始了，当问题得到解决或团队转移焦点时，事件就结束了。每个情节都针对监管模式进行编码：CoRL、SSRL 或混合 CoRL&SSRL（即，同一情节中同时发生单独指导和共享监管）。然后针对特定的监管流程对情节进行编码（改编自 Nguyen 等人，2017）。At the episode level, a regulatory episode was defined as a continuous segment focused on a single regulatory issue, typically spanning multiple turns. Episodes began when a regulatory concern was introduced and ended when it was resolved or the group shifted focus. Each episode was coded for regulatory mode: CoRL, SSRL, or hybrid CoRL&SSRL (i.e., individual guidance and shared regulation co-occurring within the same episode). Episodes were then coded for specific regulatory processes (adapted from Nguyen et al.[11];见表; see Table2).

在话语层面，每个对话回合都针对参与焦点进行编码（改编自 Dang 等人，2017）。At the utterance level, each conversational turn was coded for participatory focus (adapted from Dang et al.[2];见表; see Table3）。这捕捉了演讲者的立即监管行动（例如，规划后续步骤、推理选项、报告结果）). This captures the speaker’s immediate regulatory action(e.g., planning next steps, reasoning about options, reporting outcomes)[4].

两名训练有素的编码员独立注释数据。对于话语层面的参与焦点，评估者间的一致性近乎完美（Two trained coders independently annotated the data. This span-level evaluation was adopted because regulatory episodes vary in length, and reliable coding requires both consistent category assignment and accurate identification of episode boundaries.

4 数据分析Data Analysis

所有分析均在群体层面进行；在人类-人工智能条件下，GenAI 生成的转向被排除在外，因此研究结果反映了 GenAI 可用性如何重塑人类监管组织，而不是 GenAI 话语量或内容驱动的影响。All analyses were conducted at the group level; in the Human–AI condition, GenAI-generated turns were excluded so that findings reflected how GenAI availability reshaped human regulatory organisation rather than effects driven by GenAI utterance volume or content.

我们使用混合设计方差分析，因为类别（即监管模式、监管过程或参与焦点）是重复测量的组内因素，而条件（即人类-人工智能与人类-人类）是组间因素。然后使用事后霍尔姆调整的成对对比来比较每个类别内的条件，同时控制多个测试中的家庭错误率。We used mixed-design ANOVAs because category (i.e., regulatory mode, regulatory process, or participatory focus) was a within-group factor measured repeatedly, whereas condition (i.e., Human–AI vs Human–Human) was a between-group factor. Post hoc Holm-adjusted pairwise contrasts were then used to compare conditions within each category while controlling the family-wise error rate across multiple tests.

6 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

这项研究提供了经验证据，证明 GenAI 的可用性与 CSCL 中协同监管分布的变化相关。与人类-人类群体相比，人类-人工智能群体表现出较少的社会共享监管和更多的混合共同监管形式，以及更高比例的指令性、障碍导向性和情感性监管过程。然而，不同条件下的参与焦点分布大致相似，这表明 GenAI 更多地与监管责任的重新分配相关，而不是与即时话语的广泛变化相关。这些发现表明，GenAI 可能通过改变群体协调支持、监控和共享控制的方式来重新配置协作监管。在实践中，他们暗示教育 GenAI 的设计应旨在支撑而不是取代协作期间的共享监管。This study provides empirical evidence that GenAI availability is associated with changes in the distribution of collaborative regulation in CSCL. Compared with Human–Human groups, Human–AI groups showed less predominantly socially shared regulation and more hybrid co-regulatory forms, together with higher proportions of directive, obstacle-oriented, and affective regulatory processes. Participatory-focus distributions, however, were broadly similar across conditions, suggesting that GenAI was associated more with a redistribution of regulatory responsibility than with broad changes in moment-to-moment discourse. These findings suggest that GenAI may reconfigure collaborative regulation by changing how groups coordinate support, monitoring, and shared control. For practice, they imply that educational GenAI should be designed to scaffold rather than replace shared regulation during collaboration.

这项研究有几个局限性。该样本相对较小且人口统计上存在偏差，许多参与者使用英语作为第二语言，并且该研究侧重于纯文本交互。未来的研究应该检验类似的模式是否出现在更加多样化和多模式的协作环境中。This study has several limitations. The sample was relatively small and demographically skewed, many participants used English as a second language, and the study focused on text-only interaction. Future research should examine whether similar patterns emerge in more diverse and multimodal collaborative settings.
