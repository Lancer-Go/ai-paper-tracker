VGenST-Bench：通过主动视频合成进行时空推理的基准VGenST-Bench: A Benchmark for Spatio-Temporal Reasoning via Active Video Synthesis

摘要Abstract

时空推理是现实世界中运行的多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM) 的核心功能。因此，对其进行精确评估已成为一项重要挑战。然而，现有的时空推理基准数据集主要依赖于静态图像集或被动策划的视频数据，这限制了细粒度推理能力的评估。在本文中，我们介绍Spatio-temporal reasoning is a core capability for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) operating in the real world. As such, evaluating it precisely has become an essential challenge. However, existing spatio-temporal reasoning benchmark datasets primarily rely on static image sets or passively curated video data, which limits the evaluation of fine-grained reasoning capabilities. In this paper, we introduceVGenST 工作台VGenST-Bench，一种视频基准测试，采用生成模型主动合成高度受控且多样化的评估场景。为了构建 VGenST-Bench，我们提出了一个包含人工质量控制阶段的多智能体管道，确保所有生成的视频和 QA 对的质量。我们建立了全面的, a video benchmark that employs generative models to actively synthesize highly controlled and diverse evaluation scenarios. To construct VGenST-Bench, we propose a multi-agent pipeline incorporating a human quality control stage, ensuring the quality of all generated videos and QA pairs. We establish a comprehensive3×2×23\times 2\times 2视频分类，包括video taxonomy, encompassing空间尺度Spatial Scale, 视角Perspective, 和, and场景动态Scene Dynamics跨越不同的场景。此外，我们设计了一个分层任务套件，将低级视觉感知与高级时空推理分离。通过将范式从被动管理转变为主动合成，VGenST-Bench 能够对 MLLM 中的时空理解进行细粒度诊断。to span diverse scenarios. Furthermore, we design a hierarchical task suite that decouples low-level visual perception from high-level spatio-temporal reasoning. By shifting the paradigm from passive curation to active synthesis, VGenST-Bench enables fine-grained diagnosis of spatio-temporal understanding in MLLMs.

1 简介Introduction

多模态大型语言模型 (MLLM) 已迅速超越图像识别和字幕等基本感知任务，目前正在部署在机器人等物理应用中Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have rapidly advanced beyond basic perceptual tasks such as image recognition and captioning, and are now being deployed in physically grounded applications, including robotics[13, 83, 31]和自动驾驶and autonomous driving[72, 61]。这些部署将 MLLM 定位为可以理解和预测物理环境动态的世界模型的基础. These deployments position MLLMs as a foundation toward world models that can understand and predict the dynamics of physical environments[25, 27]。 然而，尽管取得了这些进展，当前的 MLLM 在理解对象和场景如何随时间和跨视点演变方面仍然面临着显着的挑战。特别是，. However, despite this progress, current MLLMs still exhibit notable challenges in understanding how objects and scenes evolve over time and across viewpoints. In particular,时空推理spatio-temporal reasoning跨时间和不断变化的视角感知和推断物体的位置、方向和属性的能力仍然是一个重大挑战, the ability to perceive and infer the positions, orientations, and attributes of objects across time and changing perspectives, remains a major challenge[45, 44, 41].

为了评估这些能力，已经提出了许多基准To evaluate these capabilities, numerous benchmarks have been proposed[6, 80, 48]。然而，现有的工作主要集中在基于静态图像的空间推理，无法捕捉动态的时空关系. However, existing efforts predominantly focus on static image-based spatial reasoning, which cannot capture dynamic spatio-temporal relationships[30, 78, 68, 28]。最近基于视频的基准测试已经开始解决这一差距，但它们都有一个共同的依赖. Recent video-based benchmarks have begun to address this gap, but they share a common reliance on被动策展passive curation，从网络收集剪辑或使用现有数据集，这会产生三个反复出现的限制。, collecting clips from the web or using existing datasets, which gives rise to three recurring limitations.

(i) 容易受到数据污染的影响。(i) Susceptibility to data contamination.现代 MLLM 在预训练期间会摄取大量公开可用的视频和图像数据，从而对被动策划的基准进行评估，很容易受到训练测试重叠的影响。这种污染在多模式环境中普遍存在，并系统性地夸大了报告的绩效，使当前 MLLM 评估的可靠性受到质疑Modern MLLMs ingest vast volumes of publicly available video and image data during pretraining, making evaluations on passively curated benchmarks vulnerable to train-test overlap. Such contamination is pervasive in multimodal settings and systematically inflates reported performance, leaving the reliability of current MLLM evaluations questionable[58, 8, 54]. (ii) 捷径利用。(ii) Shortcut exploitation.除了污染之外，被动策划的基准还继承了源数据的分布规律，允许模型替换语言先验、单帧线索或静态场景上下文来进行真正的时空推理Beyond contamination, passively curated benchmarks inherit distributional regularities from their source data that allow models to substitute linguistic priors, single-frame cues, or static scene context for genuine spatio-temporal reasoning[11, 33]。最近的研究表明，标准视频语言基准无法隔离时间理解. Recent studies show that standard video-language benchmarks fail to isolate temporal understanding[3, 35, 5]，表明所报告的时空推理方面的大部分进展可能反映了对捷径的利用，而不是这些基准旨在衡量的能力。, suggesting that much of the reported progress on spatio-temporal reasoning may reflect exploitation of shortcuts rather than the capability these benchmarks purport to measure.(iii) 可扩展性有限且覆盖范围窄。(iii) Limited scalability and narrow coverage.从网络源构建视频基准需要大量的手动工作来收集、过滤和注释包含所需推理场景的剪辑Constructing video benchmarks from web sources requires extensive manual effort to collect, filter, and annotate clips that contain the desired reasoning scenarios[81, 76, 36]。作为替代方案，最近的基准测试重新利用了现有的 3D 场景数据集. As an alternative, recent benchmarks repurpose existing 3D scene datasets[12, 2]作为他们的数据源as their data source[75, 73, 19, 46]，但这些通常仅覆盖狭窄范围的 3D 环境，因此很难将评估扩展到不同的空间尺度、视角或场景动态。, but these usually cover only a narrow range of 3D environments, making it difficult to extend evaluation to diverse spatial scales, perspectives, or scene dynamics.

This motivates our question:主动合成的视频能否作为 MLLM 中时空推理的可靠测试平台？Can actively synthesized videos serve as a reliable testbed for spatio-temporal reasoning in MLLMs?
