1 简介Introduction

强化学习（RL）可以直接从交通状态观测中学习信号配时策略，在仿真中通常优于传统的交通信号控制方法Reinforcement learning (RL) can directly learn signal timing strategies from traffic state observations, generally outperforming traditional traffic signal control methods in simulation(Wei et al., 2019)。然而，大多数研究评估单一需求设置下的性能，而没有检查不同控制器在需求变化时如何相互比较. However, most studies evaluate performance under a single demand setting, without checking how different controllers compare to each other as demand is varied(Zhao et al., 2024)。在交通网络中，信号控制器维持稳定交通状况的能力取决于信号控制器能否有效满足交通需求。当需求超过控制器的服务能力时，队列持续增长，系统变得不稳定，导致溢出和网络拥塞。在这种情况下，直接比较平均行程时间 (ATT) 没有意义。相反，我们认为性能比较只能在所有控制器稳定的流量需求条件下进行。为了解决这个限制，我们采用了. In traffic networks, the ability of a signal controller to maintain stable traffic conditions depends on whether the traffic demand can be effectively served by the controller. When demand exceeds the service capacity of the controller, queues grow persistently and the system becomes unstable, leading to spillback and network congestion. In this case, direct comparison of average travel times (ATTs) is not meaningful. Rather, we argue that the performance comparison should only be conducted under traffic demand conditions where all controllers are stable. To address this limitation, we have adopted the concept of the容量区域capacity region在我们之前的工作中in our previous work(Song et al., 2026)，定义为控制器维持稳定队列动态的交通需求组合集。, defined as the set of traffic demand combinations under which a controller maintains stable queue dynamics.

在这项工作中，我们将系统容量区域视角扩展到多路口交通网络，重点关注城市走廊网络的特殊情况。特别是，我们训练和评估集中式、完全分散式和参数共享分散式 RL 控制器，并将它们的容量区域和 ATT 与经典基线 MaxPressure 控制器进行比较In this work, we extend our systematic capacity region perspective to multi-junction traffic networks, focussing on the special case of an urban corridor network. In particular, we train and evaluate centralized, fully decentralized, and parameter-sharing decentralized RL controllers, and compare their capacity regions and ATTs together with a classical baseline MaxPressure controller(Varaiya, 2013)。此外，我们还展示了如何将参数共享控制器推广到部署在比最初训练的网络更大的网络上。在这种情况下，我们展示了一些初步发现，表明即使路口没有正式协调，交通也可能会自行组织成“绿波”。. Further, we show how the parameter-sharing controller may be generalised to be deployed on a larger network than it was originally trained on. In this setting, we show some initial findings that suggest that even though the junctions are not formally coordinated, traffic may self-organise into ‘green waves’.

每个枢纽有两个阶段，交替服务于 NS/SN 和 WE/EW 运动。 节点每 17 秒同时执行（可能不同的）信号控制决策，此时，节点切换相位（在这种情况下，有 2 秒的琥珀色间隔，然后是 15 秒的新相位），或者当前相位再保持 17 秒，直到下一个决策。这种离散时间决策结构被采用作为实现 RL 控制器的最简单方法。Each junction has two phases, alternatively serving the NS/SN and WE/EW movements. The junctions execute (potentially different) signal control decisions simultaneously every 17 s, when, either a junction switches phase — in which case there is a 2 s amber interval followed by 15 s of the new phase — or the current phase is maintained for a further 17 s, until the next decision. This discrete-time decision structure is adopted as the simplest way to implement RL controllers.

我们使用 MaxPressure 控制器作为基准， 它根据队列长度不平衡来选择信号相位。对于 RL 控制，我们应用近端策略优化 (PPO)We use the MaxPressure controller as a baseline, which selects signal phases based on queue length imbalances. For RL control, we apply Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)(Schulman et al., 2017)，一种广泛使用的策略梯度方法，以其稳定的训练性能而闻名。对于 PPO，我们训练和评估了三种架构：（i）集中式 PPO：单个控制器观察整个交通状态并输出所有路口的信号决策, a widely used policy-gradient method known for its stable training performance. For PPO, we trained and evaluated three architectures: (i) Centralized PPO: a single controller observes the entire traffic state and outputs signal decisions for all junctions(Liu et al., 2025)。 (ii) 完全去中心化 (FD) PPO：每个路口均由独立控制器控制，该控制器观察其路口本地的交通状态并学习自己的策略。 (iii) 参数共享（PS）去中心化 PPO：完全去中心化 PPO 的特例，其中我们要求每个路口经过训练的控制策略都是相同的，从而通过共享学习实现去中心化执行. (ii) Fully decentralized (FD) PPO: Each junction is controlled by an independent controller that observes the traffic state local to its junction and learns its own policy. (iii) Parameter-sharing (PS) decentralized PPO: a special case of fully decentralized PPO, in which we require that the trained control policy is identical for each junction, enabling decentralized execution with shared learning(Yu et al., 2022).

对于 RL 情况，交通信号控制被表述为马尔可夫决策过程。状态定义为传入车道上的交通密度。动作空间是二元的：控制器要么维持当前阶段，要么切换到竞争阶段。奖励被定义为负队列长度。对于分散控制，每个路口收到的本地奖励等于该路口队列长度的负和。相反，集中控制器接收定义为整个网络的负总队列长度的全局奖励。For the RL cases, traffic signal control is formulated as a Markov decision process. The state is defined as the traffic densities on incoming lanes. The action space is binary: the controller either maintains the current phase or switches to the competing phase. The reward is defined as the negative queue length. For decentralized control, each junction receives a local reward equal to the negative sum of queue lengths at that junction. In contrast, the centralized controller receives a global reward defined as the negative total queue length across the network.

4 讨论与展望Discussion and Outlook

在现实世界的交通网络上实际部署强化学习控制器仍然面临着关键挑战。首先，控制器在遇到超出其训练集的意外需求条件时必须表现良好 - 我们相信我们对容量区域的应用是理解这种鲁棒性的第一个系统尝试。其次，控制器应该推广到其训练集之外的网络，或者至少不应该要求对每个新网络部署进行基础训练。参数共享分散控制器似乎是这个问题的自然解决方案组件，我们对它的早期实验表明它可以促进绿波动力学，即使连接点之间没有直接协调。在正在进行的工作中，我们正在研究泛化Key challenges remain for the practical deployment of RL controllers on real-world traffic networks. Firstly, controllers must perform well when exposed to unexpected demand conditions that are outside their training set — and we believe our application of capacity regions is the first systematic attempt to understand this kind of robustness. Secondly, controllers should generalize to networks outside their training set, or at least, should not require ground-up training for each new network deployment. The parameter-sharing decentralized controller appears to be a natural solution component for this problem, and our early experiments with it suggest it can facilitate green-wave dynamics, even though there is no direct coordination between junctions.
