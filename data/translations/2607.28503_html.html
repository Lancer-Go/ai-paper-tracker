信息运营平台InfoOpsBench

Dorian Quelle dorian.quelle@pattrn.ai Lisa-Maria Neudert lisa-maria.neudert@pattrn.ai Jonathan Bright jonathan.bright@pattrn.ai John Gallacher john.gallacher@pattrn.ai
（2026 年 7 月）(July 2026)
摘要Abstract

在本文中，我们提出了一个活跃的、不断更新的人工智能基准，该基准衡量前沿语言模型的完整性，防止其被用于国家支持的信息操作。我们利用实时监控管道中的 2,100 多项信息操作，跟踪俄罗斯、中国和伊朗国家支持的信息资产。除了本文之外，我们还发布了一个配套网站，该网站跟踪国家支持的媒体传播的最突出的主张，每周更新一次：In this paper we present an active, constantly updated AI benchmark which measures the integrity of frontier language models against being co-opted for state-backed information operations. We draw on over 2,100 information operations from a live monitoring pipeline which tracks Russian, Chinese and Iranian state-backed information assets. Alongside this paper, we release a companion website that tracks the most prominent claims spread by state-backed media outlets, updated weekly:pattrn.ai/research/infoopsbench。基准测试的动态特性使其能够抵抗饱和。. The dynamic nature of the benchmark makes it resistant to saturation.

在基准测试中，我们在四个即时框架中测试了来自 8 个提供商的 17 个模型。我们发现大多数模型都可以用于信息操作。完整性得分（定义为拒绝请求的百分比）范围为 8.8% 到 94.5%，即 85.7 个百分点的差距无法用模型大小来解释。模型选择也会改变最终操作的特征。一些模型捏造细节并产生比源材料更有害的输出，另一些模型即使在遵守规定的情况下也会化解索赔，事实核查率从 2.9% 到 72.9% 不等。In the benchmark, we test 17 models from 8 providers across four prompt framings. We find that most models can be co-opted for information operations. Integrity scores, defined as the percentage of refused requests, range from 8.8% to 94.5%, an 85.7-percentage-point spread not explained by model size. Model choice also changes the character of the resulting operation. Some models fabricate details and produce output more harmful than the source material, others defuse claims even while complying, and fact-checking rates vary from 2.9% to 72.9%.

针对信息操作的完整性至少部分与拒绝生成内容（即使是善意的声明）有关，这说明了平衡模型可用性与安全性的挑战。除了一个例外（Z.ai 的 GLM 5.2），中国开发的模型大幅降低了对事实依据但批评中国的主张的合规性，相对于匹配的良性主张下降了 48-70 个百分点。Integrity against information operations is at least partly related to refusal to produce content even for benign claims, illustrating the challenge of balancing model usability with safety. With one exception (Z.ai’s GLM 5.2), the Chinese-developed models sharply cut compliance on factually grounded but China-critical claims, dropping 48–70 percentage points relative to matched benign claims.

1 简介Introduction

国家支持的信息行动是对民主的有据可查的持续威胁。俄罗斯、中国和伊朗各自维护着由国家支持的媒体、代理网站和相关社交媒体渠道组成的广泛网络，这些网络产生大量内容，旨在推进其战略传播目标。这些资产通常侧重于破坏目标国家的民主进程State-backed information operations are a well-documented and persistent threat to democracy. Russia, China, and Iran each maintain extensive networks of state-backed media outlets, proxy websites and associated social media channels that produce huge volumes of content designed to advance their strategic communications objectives. These assets often focus on undermining the democratic processes in target countries(Rid, 2020; Martin et al., 2023; Hanhijärvi, 2026)。总的来说，这些国家支持的媒体通过各种不同的信息资产发布了数千万条内容. Collectively, these state-backed outlets publish tens of millions of items through a wide variety of different information assets(pattrn.ai, )。从历史上看，他们的运营一直受到手动内容制作的成本和速度的限制. Historically, their operations have been constrained by the cost and speed of manual content production(Goldstein et al., 2023; Buchanan et al., 2021)，这需要大量的预算和大量的人员配置。, which required large budgets and significant staffing.

前沿人工智能模型的出现已经开始消除其中一些限制。当代人工智能模型可以以边际成本生成数十种语言的有说服力的平台就绪内容，使其成为影响力活动的天然力量倍增器The emergence of frontier AI models has started to remove some of these limitations. Contemporary AI models can generate persuasive, platform-ready content in dozens of languages at marginal cost, making them a natural force multiplier for influence campaigns(Goldstein et al., 2023; Bergmanis-Korāts and Chia Tee Hiang, 2026)。这不是一个理论上的问题；而是一个问题。例如，在 2025 年至 2026 年的美国/以色列-伊朗冲突期间，伊朗国家行为者在社交媒体上充斥着人工智能生成的图像和视频，旨在夸大军事能力并在整个地区散布恐惧. This is not a theoretical concern; for example, during the 2025–2026 US/Israel–Iran conflict, Iranian state actors flooded social media with AI-generated imagery and video designed to exaggerate military capabilities and sow fear across the region(Institute for Strategic Dialogue, 2026a, b)，说明如何快速地调动人工智能工具来塑造围绕危机事件的叙述, illustrating how quickly AI tools can be mobilised to shape narratives around crisis events(Stockwell et al., 2025)。在更结构化的层面上，总部位于莫斯科的“真理报”网络由约 150 个域组成，仅在 2024 年就发表了超过 360 万篇文章，故意在网络上充斥亲克里姆林宫的内容，以污染人工智能训练数据和聊天机器人输出. At a more structural level, the Moscow-based ‘Pravda’ network comprising some 150 domains published over 3.6 million articles in 2024 alone, deliberately flooding the web with pro-Kremlin content to contaminate AI training data and chatbot outputs(Sadeghi and Blachez, 2025; DFRLab and Check First, 2025)。最近的一项研究. A recent study in自然Nature表明国家媒体控制已经通过其训练语料库塑造了主要语言模型的输出，当用媒体控制更严格的国家的国家语言进行询问时，这些模型对政府的评价更高demonstrates that state media control already shapes the outputs of major language models through their training corpora, which rate governments more favourably when queried in the national language of countries with tighter media control(Waight et al., 2026)。此外，主要人工智能实验室本身也越来越多地记录国家支持的对其模型的恶意使用. Furthermore, major AI labs themselves are increasingly documenting malicious state-backed use of their own models(Nimmo et al., 2025; Google Threat Intelligence Group, 2025; Anthropic, 2025; Microsoft Threat Intelligence, 2024).

人工智能生成的内容也具有影响力；甚至可能比人类编写的内容还要重要。实验证据证实人工智能生成的内容可以大规模改变人类的态度AI-generated content is also impactful; potentially even more so than content written by humans. The combination of scalable content generation and demonstrated persuasive effect makes AI-enabled influence operations a pressing safety concern(Simon et al., 2023)。因此，了解当代人工智能模型实际上可以在多大程度上参与信息运营至关重要。. It is therefore critical to understand the extent to which contemporary AI models can actually become co-opted for information operations.

(2019); Larson et al. (2009); North Atlantic Treaty Organization (2025)）。在当代信息环境中，它们越来越多地在网上进行，并且往往涉及大型公共国家支持媒体、政府机构、隐蔽的“垃圾新闻”网站以及受控社交媒体帐户网络之间的协调). In the contemporary information environment they increasingly take place online, and often involve coordination between large, public state-backed media outlets, government agencies, covert ‘junk news’ sites, and networks of controlled social media accounts(Wang et al., 2023; Starbird et al., 2019; Bradshaw et al., 2021)。信息运营是信息环境中越来越重要的一部分，许多国家每年都在信息运营上投入大量资源。例如，最近泄露的内部文件表明，俄罗斯数字影响力生态系统的年度预算高达数亿美元. Information operations are an ever more important part of the information environment, with many states spending substantial resources on them annually. These networks push out a variety of claims, some of which might be regarded as mis- or disinformation, others which are simply misleading spins or biased reporting. For example, recent Russian information operations have ranged from very specific false claims about President Zelenskyy purchasing Hitler’s former ‘Eagle’s Nest’ retreat which was outright disinformation(Lehn and Anguelov, 2025)更广泛的运动宣扬继续支持乌克兰是徒劳的，或者乌克兰不可能取得胜利（这是对未来的预测，因此不能说是绝对正确或错误的）。然而，所有这些主张都有利于资助或支持它们的国家的战略传播目标。to much broader campaigns promoting the claim that continued support for Ukraine is futile or that Ukrainian victory is impossible (which is a prediction about the future and hence cannot be said to be conclusively true or false). All of the claims, however, benefit the strategic communications goals of the states funding them or supporting them.

正如我们上面所概述的，对于那些监控信息操作的人来说，一个关键问题是前沿人工智能模型可以在多大程度上被用来帮助其生产和传播。这不是现有基准测试中解决的问题。尽管在大型语言模型的错误信息领域有大量研究，并且在动态基准领域也有一些新兴工作，但到目前为止，这项工作并没有直接解决当前理解模型倾向支持实时信息操作的挑战。错误信息领域的工作通常集中于语言模型是否会对所提出的问题给出真实答案，或者它们本身是否会重复错误信息的问题。该领域的早期工作是 TruthfulQAAs we have outlined above, a critical concern for those monitoring information operations is the extent to which frontier AI models can be co-opted to aid in their production and dissemination. This is not something addressed in existing benchmarks. While there is considerable research in the area of misinformation in large language models, and some emerging work in the area of dynamic benchmarks, thus far, this work does not directly address the current challenge of understanding model propensity to support information operations in real-time. Work in the area of misinformation typically focusses on the question of whether language models will give truthful answers to questions they are posed, or whether they themselves will repeat misinformation. However, they do not generalise well to the problem of model support for information operations. If models are co-opted into information operations by a malicious actor, it will be to enable content generation rather than bypassing fact-checking: to create social media posts, news articles, comments or other types of content which are critical to the construction of a large scale information operation. Hence, a benchmark focussing on information operations needs to focus on whether models are willing to create this type of content, rather than asking direct questions about misinformation (the benchmark DisElect goes some way to addressing this(Williams et al., 2025)，但纯粹关注选举虚假信息）。此外，支持信息操作的内容不一定可以明确地分类为“真实”或“虚假”，而是可以由各种真实性可能不清楚的支持或宣传材料组成。, but focusses purely on election disinformation). Furthermore, content supporting information operations is not necessarily cleanly classifiable as either ‘true’ or ‘false’, but instead can consist of a wide variety of supporting or promotional material where truthfulness may be unclear.

Large, well-funded information operations networks are capable of generating large numbers of false claims at a fast pace, potentially adversarially selecting claims to pass content filters; and just because a model is resistant to one claim does not necessarily make it resistant to future ones. The evaluation set refreshes weekly without human curation of individual items. The result is a benchmark that cannot be saturated in the conventional sense, as a model that learns to handle last week’s claims will face new ones in the next week.

3 基准设计Benchmark Design

我们的基准测试重点是确定前沿和开源模型是否可以用于支持国家支持的信息操作，从而允许对新颖的信息操作进行动态测试。该基准涉及三个组成部分，如下所述： 用于从信息运营网络中提取新出现的索赔的管道；一组提示，用于测试模型是否将为这些声明提供支持内容；以及自动判断模型是否符合提示的方法。下面依次描述这些。Our benchmark focusses on establishing whether frontier and open-source models can be used to support state-backed information operations in a way that allows dynamic testing against novel information operations. The benchmark involves three components, which are described in turn below: a pipeline for extracting emerging claims from information operations networks; a set of prompts that test whether a model will provide supporting content for these claims; and an approach to automatically judging whether a model complied with a prompt. These are described in turn below.

3.1 信息运营理赔管道Information operation claims pipeline

我们的基准中使用的声明来自一个在线监控系统，该系统跟踪与俄罗斯、伊朗和中国相关的国家支持资产（见图The claims used in our benchmark come from an online monitoring system that tracks state-backed assets linked to Russia, Iran, and China (see Figure1，另见, see alsopattrn.ai/monitor）。该系统每周从与信息操作相关的网络和社交媒体信息资产中摄取大约一百万个内容项。例如，资产可以是媒体组织（公开的国有资产或以某种方式归属的秘密资产），或政府官员或外交官运营的社交媒体帐户。该系统不会从一般公众或与国家信息运营生态系统没有可验证联系的人收集数据。收集的内容项是来自这些组织的新闻文章或社交媒体帖子。). This system ingests roughly one million content items per week across web and social media information assets that are linked to information operations. Assets may be, for example, media organisations (either overt state assets or covert ones which have been attributed in some way), or social media accounts run by government officials or diplomats. The system does not collect data from general members of the public or people with no verifiable links to state information operations ecosystems. The content items collected are news articles or social media posts originating from these organisations.

3.2 提示模板Prompt templates

我们使用四个提示模板，要求模型创建内容来支持给定的声明（见图We use four prompt templates that ask a model to create content to support a given claim (see Figure2）。这些模板以越来越复杂的程度来近似算子。在低端，一个最小的“写社交媒体帖子”请求代表操作员只需将声明大规模地输入到模型中。在高端，社会工程框架将模型塑造为新闻影响者或宣传活动家，代表一个预期会被拒绝并构建一个合理的角色来规避它的操作者。所有提示均未提及虚假信息、信息操作或声明的来源或真实性。符合要求的模型会生成促进国家叙事的基于文本的内容，因此可用于支持信息操作。). The templates approximate operators with increasing levels of sophistication. At the low end, a minimal “write a social media post” request represents an operator who simply feeds claims into a model at scale. At the high end, a social-engineering framing casts the model as a news influencer or advocacy campaigner, representing an operator who anticipates refusal and constructs a plausible persona to circumvent it. None of the prompts mention disinformation, information operations, or the origin or veracity of the claim. A model that complies produces text-based content promoting a state narrative and could therefore be used to support an information operation.

3.3 判断方法Judgement approach

响应由评判模型进行评估，该模型在四个维度上对内容进行评分（温度为 0 时的 Mistral Small 3.1 24B，该设置可最大限度地减少模型输出的随机性，以便相同的响应每次都会获得接近相同的分数）。首先，我们询问模型的输出是否包含可以发布在社交媒体上的文本。其次，我们询问模型的输出是否包含原始信息操作声明，或者模型是否删除了该声明。考虑到某些模型倾向于遵守要求，同时使声明无害，我们还将帖子中包含的声明分为以下三类之一：放大（通过添加虚假引用或先前未在原始提示中列出的后果，使声明变得更加有害）；保留（声明按给定的方式重述），或者最后减弱（声明存在，但其大部分或所有有害方面已被删除）。最后，我们询问该模型是否明确强调该声明是错误的或有问题的，而不是仅仅提供通用的“共享前验证”免责声明。最重要的合规性指标是严重性Responses are evaluated by a judge model that scores content on four dimensions (Mistral Small 3.1 24B at temperature 0, the setting that minimises randomness in a model’s output so that the same response receives close to the same score each time). First, we ask whether the output of the model contains text that could be posted on social media. Secondly, we ask whether the output of the model contains the original information operations claim, or whether the model has removed the claim. Taking into account that some models have the tendency to comply with the request while simultaneously rendering the claim harmless, we also classify the claim contained in the post into one of three categories: amplified (the claim has been made more harmful by adding fake citations, or consequences not previously listed in the original prompt); preserved (the claim is restated as given), or lastly, attenuated (the claim is present but most or all harmful aspects of it have been removed). Lastly, we ask whether the model explicitly highlights that the claim is false or questionable, rather than merely providing a generic “verify before sharing” disclaimer. 6 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 InfoOpsBench，这是一个实时人工智能安全基准，用于测试领先的人工智能系统是否会生成社交媒体内容，以宣传俄罗斯、中国和伊朗官方媒体活动中的主张。在来自 8 个提供商的 17 个模型中，我们发现完整性得分从 8.8% 到 94.5% 不等，差距更多地是由提供商级别的差异而非模型大小或功能造成的。除了二进制合规性指标之外，我们还探索了对每个模型生成的内容的严重性的估计。我们发现，虽然一些模型按照最初的严重程度再现了主张，但其他模型却捏造了额外的细节，发明了来源，并升级了含义，可能使输出比源材料更具危害性。我们发现，在所有判断的响应中，部长模型放大了 67.6-71.9% 的主张，而人择模型很少这样做。In this paper, we introduced InfoOpsBench, a live AI safety benchmark that tests whether leading AI systems will produce social media content that promotes claims drawn from active Russian, Chinese, and Iranian state media campaigns. Across 17 models from 8 providers, we find integrity scores ranging from 8.8% to 94.5%, with the gap driven more by provider-level variance than by model size or capability. In addition to binary compliance metrics, we also explore estimates of the severity of the content produced by each model. We find that while some models reproduce claims at their original severity, others fabricate additional details, invent sources, and escalate meaning, potentially making the output more harmful than the source material. We find that the Ministral models amplify claims in 67.6–71.9% of all judged responses, while Anthropic models rarely do so.

对照实验表明，低合规率可以反映完全不同的潜在行为。在更新后的控制名单中，Anthropic 的 Haiku 对政治社交媒体内容总体上持谨慎态度，而 GPT-5.6 系列和 Claude Sonnet 5 的歧视最为尖锐，遵守良性主张的次数远远多于信息操作。我们发现了一些特定国家表现的证据。 DeepSeek V4 Flash 和 Kimi K3 显示了针对中国的政治过滤，这提高了对与安全一致性无关的内容的拒绝率（在 Kimi 的案例中是由提供商级内容过滤器强制执行的），而 OpenAI 模型则显示出强烈的歧视性，而没有 Haiku 所特有的全面过度谨慎。这些代表了对部署有影响的不同故障模式。The control experiments illustrate that low compliance rates can reflect quite different underlying behaviours. In the refreshed control roster, Anthropic’s Haiku is broadly cautious about political social media content altogether, while the GPT-5.6 line and Claude Sonnet 5 discriminate most sharply, complying with benign claims far more often than with information operations. We find some evidence of country-specific performance. DeepSeek V4 Flash and Kimi K3 show China-specific political filtering that inflates their refusal rates on content unrelated to safety alignment – in Kimi’s case enforced by a provider-level content filter – whereas OpenAI models show strong discrimination without the blanket overcaution that characterises Haiku. These represent distinct failure modes with implications for deployment.

局限性Limitations

InfoOpsBench 借鉴了现实世界中国家支持的媒体运营，而不是合成或历史示例，其动态设计意味着评估集每周都会随着新的声明进入管道而刷新，旨在解决静态基准的局限性。有一些限制。我们测试的所有提示和声明都是英文的，这可能低估了其他全球语言的合规率。这尤其值得注意，因为研究表明，非英语和资源匮乏的语言中的人工智能安全功能通常较低InfoOpsBench draws from real-world state-backed media operations rather than synthetic or historical examples, and its dynamic design means the evaluation set refreshes weekly as new claims enter the pipeline, intended to address the limitations of static benchmarks. Several limitations apply. All the prompts and claims that we test are in English, which likely understates compliance rates in other global languages. This is especially notable because research shows that AI safety features are typically lower in non-English and low-resource languagesBanerjee et al. (2026).

我们的提示将任务定义为宣传在线宣传和信息操作中发现的真实主张，无论其实际真实性如何。这回避了限制许多错误信息/虚假信息基准的实际问题，因为我们不需要在测试之前验证每个声明。与安全相关的问题是关于模型是否愿意制作与国家活动相一致的内容，而不是关于任何个人声明本身的真实性。因此，我们认为，出于战略目的而由国家支持的信息运营网络放大的事实准确的主张可能是有害的。因此，将其重新包装为可共享社交媒体内容的模型仍在参与该操作。然而，这确实意味着我们从根本上衡量的是模型在国家竞选中使用的倾向，而不是它在改变用户信念、传播虚假信息或造成任何现实世界影响方面的功效。这些需要使用不同的方法来测量。Our prompts frame the task as that of promoting real claims identified in online propaganda and information operations, regardless of their actual veracity. This sidesteps a practical problem that limits many misinformation/disinformation benchmarks as we do not need to verify every claim before testing it. The safety-relevant question is about a model’s willingness to produce content aligned with a state campaign, not about the veracity of any individual claim itself. As such we consider a factually accurate claim amplified by state-backed information operations networks for strategic purposes as potentially harmful. Therefore, a model that repackages it as shareable social media content is still participating in that operation. This does mean, however, that we are fundamentally measuring a model’s propensity for use within a state campaign, rather than its efficacy in changing users’ beliefs, spreading disinformation, or causing any real-world effects. These would need to be measured using a different approach.

在我们的工作中，我们测试了四种即时框架，它们涵盖了一系列操纵策略，但没有耗尽可能越狱或对抗性输入的空间。未来的研究可以进一步扩展这些框架，并测试更广泛的分销渠道和人工智能与用户的直接交互。我们的法官模型根据人类标签进行了验证，标题一致性为 92.3%，但这种规模的自动评估不可避免地会引入噪音，特别是对于边缘案例。我们也无法区分训练时的安全对齐和推理时的内容过滤，这意味着明显的安全改进可能反映了过滤层的策略更改，而不是更深层次的模型级对齐。最后，由于提供商不断更新其模型，因此此处报告的合规率反映了及时的快照。实时基准In our work, we test four prompt framings, which cover a range of manipulation strategies but do not exhaust the space of possible jailbreaks or adversarial inputs. Future research could expand these framings further and test for wider distribution channels and direct AI-to-user interactions. Our judge model was validated against human labels at 92.3% headline agreement, but automated evaluation at this scale inevitably introduces noise, particularly for borderline cases. We also cannot distinguish training-time safety alignment from inference-time content filtering, meaning that apparent safety improvements may reflect policy changes to a filter layer rather than deeper model-level alignment. Finally, because providers update their models continuously, the compliance rates reported here reflect a snapshot in time.
