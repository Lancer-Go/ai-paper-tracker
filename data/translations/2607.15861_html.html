用于多语言和代码混合滥用检测的毒性信号的条件可靠性Conditional Reliability of Toxicity Signals for Multilingual and Code-Mixed Abuse Detection

Indraveni Chebolu All authors contributed equally    Rohan Singh    Arnab Mallick Corresponding Author    Harmesh Rana
摘要Abstract

审核系统越来越依赖外部毒性工具，但这些工具在代码混合、音译、俚语和语言不匹配的情况下并不可靠。我们研究的是Moderation systems increasingly rely on external toxicity tools, but those tools are unreliable under code-mixing, transliteration, slang, and language mismatch. We study the条件可靠性conditional reliability印度多语言和代码混合短文本中的毒性先验：英语毒性、印度滥用和基于规则的严重性线索可能是有用的证据，但仅限于某些语言和滥用严重性背景。我们提出了 ToxGate，一种信任融合头，在将其添加到预测状态之前，在编码器表示上调节每个辅助信号。在三个短文本滥用数据集、四个转换器编码器和每个设置五个种子中，ToxGate 在 12 个域内设置中的 10 个和 8 个传输设置中的 7 个中改进了匹配的普通编码器。最大且最容易解释的收益出现在高风险审核部分，包括明确的诽谤、暴力威胁和跨数据集传输。更广泛的教训是，审核系统应将外部毒性工具和先验视为条件证据，而不是固定特征或基本事实，在集中消融中，特定源门控在转移、严重滥用切片和高风险分类方面给出了最强的结果。of toxicity priors in Indian multilingual and code-mixed short text: English toxicity, Indic abuse, and rule-based severity cues can be useful evidence, but only in some linguistic and abuse-severity contexts. We propose ToxGate, a trust-fusion head that conditions each auxiliary signal on the encoder representation before adding it to the prediction state. Across three short-text abuse datasets, four transformer encoders, and five seeds per setting, ToxGate improves over matched plain encoders in 10 of 12 in-domain settings and 7 of 8 transfer settings. The largest and most interpretable gains occur in high-risk moderation slices, including explicit slurs, violent threats, and cross-dataset transfer. The broader lesson is that moderation systems should treat external toxicity tools and priors as conditional evidence rather than fixed features or ground truth, in focused ablations, source-specific gating gives the strongest results in transfer, severe-abuse slices, and high-risk triage.

1 简介Introduction

在线平台托管多语言和混合代码的帖子，这些帖子结合了脚本、拼写变体、俚语、讽刺和音译。印度英语滥用检测是一个典型案例：印地语-英语帖子可以混合英语脏话、罗马化的印地语诽谤和社区特定的嘲讽，从而对单语编码器和现成的毒性工具造成压力Online platforms host multilingual and code-mixed posts that combine scripts, spelling variants, slang, sarcasm, and transliteration. Hinglish abuse detection is a representative case: Hindi–English posts can mix English profanity, Romanized Hindi slurs, and community-specific mockery in ways that strain both monolingual encoders and off-the-shelf toxicity tools[21, 17, 2]。常见的工程响应是将外部毒性分数（例如 Detoxify 或 Perspective API 输出）附加到上下文编码器. A common engineering response is to append external toxicity scores, such as Detoxify or Perspective API outputs, to a contextual encoder[9, 14, 4]。这会有所帮助，但它假设外部分数都是一致可信的。. This can help, but it assumes that external scores are uniformly trustworthy.

我们认为核心应用问题是We argue that the central applied problem is条件可靠性conditional reliability。毒性先验对于明确的英语脏话可能是可靠的，对于罗马化印地语滥用是不完整的，对于严重威胁有用，对于良性俚语具有误导性。朴素串联将每个分数在每种情况下都同等有用，这对于必须处理相互冲突的证据并将不确定的案例送交人工审查的审核管道来说不太适合。因此，我们的问题是狭窄且可测试的：当辅助毒性信号的贡献取决于文本上下文时，它们是否可以提供更多帮助？. A toxicity prior can be reliable for explicit English profanity, incomplete for Romanized Hindi abuse, useful for severe threats, and misleading for benign slang. Naive concatenation treats each score as equally useful in every context, which is a weak fit for moderation pipelines that must handle conflicting evidence and route uncertain cases to human review. Our question is therefore narrow and testable: can auxiliary toxicity signals help more when their contribution is conditioned on the text context?

我们通过 ToxGate 来回答这个问题，ToxGate 是一个源感知门控融合头。 ToxGate 分别投影 Detoxify、印度辱骂语言和基于规则的严重性源，然后为每个源学习上下文条件门，然后将其与编码器表示融合。该贡献不是一个新的门控原语，而是何时应该信任外部毒性工具的有针对性的形式化——这是之前的静态融合工作尚未解决的问题。We answer this through ToxGate, a source-aware gated fusion head. ToxGate separately projects Detoxify, Indic abusive-language, and rule-based severity sources, then learns a context-conditioned gate for each source before fusing it with the encoder representation. The contribution is not a new gating primitive but a targeted formalization of when external toxicity tools should be trusted-a question that prior static-fusion work has left unaddressed.

我们的贡献是：Our contributions are:

  • •

    我们制定外部毒性工具作为多语言调节的条件证据，而不是作为基本事实或一致可靠的特征。We formulate external toxicity tools as conditional evidence for multilingual moderation, not as ground truth or uniformly reliable features.

  • •

    我们引入了 ToxGate，一个针对英语毒性、印度语滥用和基于规则的严重性先验的特定源信任融合头。We introduce ToxGate, a source-specific trust-fusion head for English toxicity, Indic abuse, and rule-based severity priors.

  • •

    我们评估三个数据集、四个编码器、五个种子、域内测试、跨数据集传输、损坏分析、切片、引导置信区间和轻量级审核分类模拟。We evaluate three datasets, four encoders, five seeds, in-domain tests, cross-dataset transfer, corruption analyses, slices, bootstrap confidence intervals, and a lightweight moderation triage simulation.

  • •

    我们表明，最强的收益集中在审核风险最高的地方：明确的诽谤、暴力威胁以及相关代码混合数据集之间的传输。We show that the strongest gains concentrate where moderation risk is highest: explicit slurs, violent threats, and transfer between related code-mixed datasets.

2 相关工作Related Work

滥用语言检测长期以来一直将词汇、社交和神经信号结合起来。代码混合设置使问题变得更加困难，因为发言者在单个帖子中混合了脚本、音译、俚语和社区特定的侮辱性内容。早期的印度英语作品使用手工制作的特征和经典分类器Abusive-language detection has long combined lexical, social, and neural signals. Code-mixed settings make the problem harder because speakers mix scripts, transliteration, slang, and community-specific insults within a single post. Early Hinglish work used handcrafted features and classical classifiers[2, 17]，后来的系统转向多语言转换器和共享任务式基准测试, later systems moved toward multilingual transformers and shared-task-style benchmarks[16]。我们的工作建立在这一点上，但提出了一个更狭窄的可靠性问题：不是代码混合滥用是否可以分类，而是何时应该信任外部毒性先验。. Our work builds on this line but asks a narrower reliability question: not whether code-mixed abuse can be classified, but when external toxicity priors should be trusted.

辅助信号在仲裁系统中很常见。社交语境特征已与语境嵌入相结合，用于滥用语言检测Auxiliary signals are common in moderation systems. Social context features have been concatenated with contextual embeddings for abusive-language detection[22]，而 Detoxify 和 Perspective API 提供毒性、侮辱、威胁和身份攻击分数，可以作为密集的词汇先验, while Detoxify and Perspective API provide toxicity, insult, threat, and identity-attack scores that can serve as dense lexical priors[9, 14]。 QBERTOX 同样通过 Detoxify 衍生的功能增强了基于 BERT 的印度英语分类器. QBERTOX similarly augments a BERT-based Hinglish classifier with Detoxify-derived features[4]。这项工作中占主导地位的集成策略是静态特征串联。该策略很简单，但它假设辅助源对于英语脏话、罗马化印地语滥用、暴力威胁和良性俚语以同样的方式有用。先前关于毒性模型偏差和校准的工作表明了为什么这种假设在源模型的训练分布之外是有风险的. The dominant integration strategy in this work is static feature concatenation. That strategy is simple, but it assumes the auxiliary source is useful in the same way for English profanity, Romanized Hindi abuse, violent threats, and benign slang. Prior work on toxicity-model bias and calibration shows why this assumption is risky outside the source model’s training distribution[3, 13].

门控融合在多模式和多语言学习中得到了很好的应用Gated fusion is well established in multimodal and multilingual learning[1, 12, 18]。这些方法学习何时组合模式、特征或适配器，而不是总是平等地使用每个信号。源感知门控在这里被用作现有融合架构尚未直接解决的信任和安全问题的原则机制：当外部毒性工具信息丰富但在语言不匹配的情况下不可靠时，调节模型能否学习以文本上下文为条件的每个源的信任？这种区别很重要，因为我们的辅助来源是冻结的、异质的和不完善的：英国毒性模型、印度滥用先验和基于规则的严重性启发式。. These methods learn when to combine modalities, features, or adapters, rather than always using every signal equally. Source-aware gating is applied here as a principled mechanism for a trust-and-safety question that existing fusion architectures have not directly addressed: when external toxicity tools are informative but unreliable under language mismatch, can a moderation model learn per-source trust conditioned on textual context? This distinction matters because our auxiliary sources are frozen, heterogeneous, and imperfect: an English toxicity model, an Indic abuse prior, and a rule-based severity heuristic.

因此，最接近的现有系统激发了本文的基线。普通编码器测试单独的文本表示是否足够。 Concat 测试标准静态融合假设，MLP 测试通用非线性融合是否足够，SharedGate 和 ScalarGate 测试门控是否单独解释增益。 ToxGate 旨在测试剩余的假设：当每个毒性先验在语言和严重性上下文中具有不同的可靠性特征时，源特定条件融合非常有用。The closest prior systems therefore motivate the baselines in this paper. Plain encoders test whether text representations alone are sufficient. Concat tests the standard static-fusion assumption, MLP tests whether generic nonlinear fusion is enough, SharedGate and ScalarGate test whether gating alone explains the gains. ToxGate is designed to test the remaining hypothesis: source-specific conditional fusion is useful when each toxicity prior has a different reliability profile across linguistic and severity contexts.

4 实验设计Experimental Design

我们评估了三个短文本滥用语言数据集。恶霸解释We evaluate three short-text abusive-language datasets. BullyExplain[15]是我们主要的印度英语网络欺凌基准。印度英语头条新闻is our primary Hinglish cyberbullying benchmark. Our empirical scope is Indian multilingual and code-mixed moderation, not universal language coverage. We map all datasets to binary abusive/non-abusive labels and use fixed stratified 70/10/20 train/validation/test splits.

In addition to clean tests, we run cross-dataset transfer between the two Hinglish datasets, auxiliary-source corruptions, slice analysis, qualitative diagnostics, paired bootstrap confidence intervals over saved predictions, and a high-risk triage simulation.

5.4 稳健性和诊断Robustness and Diagnostics

我们在测试时通过改组、删除或扰乱 Detoxify、Indic、规则或所有辅助功能来破坏辅助源。该测试将有用的依赖与脆弱的依赖分开。 Concat 在腐败情况下变化很小，这本身并不是鲁棒性的有力证据。加上其较弱的平均结果，这表明静态串联通常会学会忽略辅助分数。 MLP 更脆弱，尤其是当 Detoxify 或所有辅助功能被删除时。 ToxGate 介于这两个极端之间：它使用足够的辅助证据来改善传输和严重切片，但其在腐败情况下的下降是有限的。它的门均值很窄，通常在 sigmoid 范围的中间附近，因此我们不会将门解释为硬性解释。最好将它们理解为软残差调制：对来源依赖的诊断，而不是对每个决定的忠实因果解释。We corrupt auxiliary sources at test time by shuffling, dropping, or perturbing Detoxify, Indic, rule, or all auxiliary features. This test separates useful dependence from brittle dependence. Concat changes very little under corruption, which is not strong evidence of robustness by itself. Together with its weaker average results, this suggests that static concatenation often learns to ignore the auxiliary scores. MLP is much more brittle, especially when Detoxify or all auxiliary features are removed. ToxGate sits between these extremes: it uses auxiliary evidence enough to improve transfer and severe slices, but its drops under corruption are bounded. Its gate means are narrow, usually around the middle of the sigmoid range, so we do not interpret gates as hard explanations. They are better read as soft residual modulation: a diagnostic of source reliance, not a faithful causal explanation of every decision.

The practical diagnostic is therefore not “the gate explains the label,” but “external priors are useful only when the linguistic context makes them trustworthy.”

7 局限性和道德规范Limitations and Ethics

该研究有五个主要局限性。首先，它使用三个二进制标签数据集，因此它不涵盖所有语言、脚本、目标群体或细粒度的滥用分类法。其次，印度语辅助分数来自公共外部检查点，我们冻结它并且从不使用它来定义标签或分割，但我们无法完全审核其上游训练数据是否重叠。第三，重点消融行是聚合诊断、SharedGate、ScalarGate 的每个数据集置信区间和源过滤变体将进一步锐化因果分解。第四，一个固定分割上的五个种子测量优化方差，而不是完全重采样不确定性；预测上的配对引导间隔有帮助，但不能取代重复的分割。第五，切片标签和规则启发法是操作诊断而不是社会语言学分析。The study has five main limitations. First, it uses three binary-label datasets, so it does not cover all languages, scripts, target groups, or fine-grained abuse taxonomies. Second, the Indic auxiliary score comes from a public external checkpoint, we freeze it and never use it to define labels or splits, but we cannot fully audit its upstream training data for overlap. Third, the focused ablation rows are aggregate diagnostics, per-dataset confidence intervals for SharedGate, ScalarGate, and source-filtered variants would further sharpen the causal decomposition. Fourth, five seeds on one fixed split measure optimization variance, not full resampling uncertainty; paired bootstrap intervals over predictions help but do not replace repeated splits. Fifth, slice labels and rule heuristics are operational diagnostics rather than sociolinguistic analysis.

自动滥用检测也会带来道德风险。毒性工具可以编码针对方言、边缘化社区、回收语言或身份术语的偏见Automated abuse detection also raises ethical risks. Toxicity tools can encode biases against dialects, marginalized communities, reclaimed language, or identity terms[3]。在某些情况下，信任感知融合可以减少对不可靠信号的依赖，但它并不能消除偏见。任何实际使用都应该让人类参与进来，暴露不确定性，并在部署之前评估不同的错误率。. Trust-aware fusion can reduce reliance on unreliable signals in some cases, but it does not eliminate bias. Any practical use should keep humans in the loop, expose uncertainty, and evaluate disparate error rates before deployment.

8 结论Conclusion

本文证明印度的代码混合审核是一个条件可靠性问题。外部毒性工具和先验是有用的，但在代码混合、音译、俚语和语言不匹配的情况下不应盲目信任它们。 ToxGate 提供了一个简单的源感知机制，用于学习何时使用英语毒性、印度语滥用和基于规则的严重性先验。重点诊断中的证据最为有力：与普通编码器相比的匹配增益、印度英语数据集之间的传输、严重滥用切片以及高风险分类精度。最后的教训是实用的：审核系统应该将毒性先验视为条件证据，而不是固定特征或基本事实。This paper demonstrate Indian code-mixed moderation as a problem of conditional reliability. External toxicity tools and priors are useful, but they should not be trusted blindly under code-mixing, transliteration, slang, and language mismatch. ToxGate provides a simple source-aware mechanism for learning when to use English toxicity, Indic abuse, and rule-based severity priors. The evidence is strongest in the focused diagnostics: matched gains over plain encoders, transfer between Hinglish datasets, severe-abuse slices, and high-risk triage precision. The final lesson is practical: moderation systems should treat toxicity priors as conditional evidence, not as fixed features or ground truth.
