摘要Abstract

多实例学习（MIL）解决了在实例包级别上进行监督的问题，并已成功应用于从计算病理学到卫星图像等领域。然而，现有的算法在低标签机制中举步维艰，而低标签机制是许多现实世界应用的特征。灵活的模型过度拟合，而刚性的模型无法适应手头的任务。我们表明，使用感知器式架构对合成数据进行预训练，可以生成一个模型，该模型可以解决少量标记包中的新任务。在推理时，分类发生在单次前向传递中，不需要梯度更新。我们提出并研究了用于袋结构数据的不同合成数据生成器，发现它们捕获了互补的归纳偏差。在这些生成器的混合物上进行预训练的模型继承了它们每个任务的优势，并在 12 个 MIL 基准中实现了最佳平均性能，优于需要特定任务训练的监督基线。Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) addresses problems where supervision is available at the level of bags of instances and has been successfully applied in fields ranging from computational pathology to satellite imagery. Nevertheless, existing algorithms struggle in the low-label regime that characterizes many real-world applications. Flexible models overfit and rigid ones fail to adapt to the task at hand. We show that pretraining an in-context learner with a Perceiver-style architecture on synthetic data yields a model that can solve new tasks from a handful of labeled bags. At inference time, classification happens in a single forward pass and requires no gradient updates. We propose and investigate different synthetic data generators for bag-structured data and find that they capture complementary inductive biases. A model pretrained on a mixture of these generators inherits their per-task strengths and achieves the best average performance across twelve MIL benchmarks, outperforming supervised baselines that require task-specific training.

Intuitively, by training across many simulated MIL tasks, the model learns what plausible bag classifiers look like, so at test time it can infer the labeling rule and use it to predict labels for unseen bags. This shifts the problem from fitting an aggregator on a handful of bags to designing good data generators (priors) over bag-structured tasks that span the relevant hypothesis space. At inference time, the model is given a real MIL dataset as context and classifies the test bags in a single forward pass, without gradient updates or task-specific training. In this paper, we propose to address these with a Perceiver-style architecture in which learnable bag tokens iteratively gather information from their instances and exchange it with representations and labels of other bags.

Standard MIL architectures assume that bag labels factorize through a permutation-invariant decomposition over instances, and the natural question is whether this assumption should also be reflected in the prior. We design two families of generators that take opposite stances. Factorized priors encode the classical decomposition, while joint priors drop it in favor of a single generator over the full bag that permits richer dependence structures among instances. We study whether either family suffices on its own or whether a mixture yields a single model that performs robustly across MIL regimes.

5 局限性Limitations

我们设置的一些方面留有进一步调查的空间。我们的培训课程目前使用的包大小最多为 20 个实例。虽然该模型可以很好地推广到较大的包袋 推理时间，正如其在 Musk 数据集上的强劲表现所证明的那样，包括 训练期间使用更大的包是自然而然的下一步。此外，我们的基准测试侧重于二元分类，通过 PCA 将特征维度减少到 25，留下多类目标 和高维基础模型嵌入是有前途的方向。这两个问题都应该可以通过对高维合成数据进行培训以及扩展模型容量和培训持续时间来解决A few aspects of our setup leave room for further investigation. Our training curriculum currently uses bag sizes of up to 20 instances. While the model generalizes well to larger bags at inference time, as demonstrated by its strong performance on the Musk datasets, including larger bags during training is a natural next step. Furthermore, our benchmarks focus on binary classification with feature dimensionality reduced to 25 via PCA, leaving multi-class targets and higher-dimensional foundation-model embeddings as promising directions. Both should be addressable by training on higher-dimensional synthetic data and scaling model capacity and training duration[26]。与此同时，我们的扩展实验表明，增加容量和训练时间所带来的收益并不会增加。 跨基准完全一致，并暗示了先前的混合物、模型大小和训练持续时间之间的相互作用，值得进一步研究。. At the same time, our scaling experiment suggests that gains from added capacity and training time are not fully uniform across benchmarks and hint at interactions between prior mixture, model size and training duration that warrant closer study.

6 结论Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced情境中的多实例学习In-Context Multiple Instance Learning(ICMIL)，一个通过袋结构数据的上下文学习解决 MIL 问题的框架。为了支持这一点，我们提出了一种架构，可以解决分层集输入带来的可扩展性、任务相关压缩和排列不变性挑战，并设计了一系列合成先验，对 MIL 任务上的互补归纳偏差进行编码。混合这些先验训练的模型在低标签制度下的 12 个 MIL 基准中实现了最佳平均 AUROC 和最佳平均排名。(ICMIL), a framework for solving MIL problems via in-context learning over bag-structured data. To support this, we proposed an architecture that addresses the scalability, task-dependent compression, and permutation-invariance challenges that arise with hierarchical set inputs, and designed a family of synthetic priors that encode complementary inductive biases over MIL tasks. A model trained on a mixture of these priors achieves the best average AUROC and the best average rank across twelve MIL benchmarks in the low-label regime.

除了上一节概述的扩展方向之外，几种正交途径可能会产生进一步的收益。在先验方面，我们的结果表明，合成数据生成器的选择非常重要，并且我们可以有效地结合编码互补归纳偏差的先验。因此，设计先验与特定应用领域相一致Beyond the scaling directions outlined in the previous section, several orthogonal avenues are likely to yield further gains. On the prior side, our results show that the choice of synthetic data generator matters considerably and that we can fruitfully combine priors that encode complementary inductive biases. Therefore, designing priors aligned with specific application domains[41]，例如计算病理学的空间先验或时间解析任务的序列感知先验, such as spatial priors for computational pathology or sequence-aware priors for time-resolved tasks[20]，是另一个有前途的方向。, is another promising direction.
