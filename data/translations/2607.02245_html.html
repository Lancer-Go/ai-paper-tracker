Copewell：用于公平心理健康支持的多代理群体架构Copewell: A Multi-Agent Swarm Architecture for Equitable Mental Wellness Support

摘要Abstract

精神健康障碍影响着全球近 10 亿人，但由于劳动力短缺、成本障碍和耻辱，低收入和中等收入国家 75% 的人得不到治疗。当前人工智能驱动的健康解决方案主要依赖于单一模式的对话界面，这种界面的放弃率很高，并且无法根据用户的动态情绪状态提供可测量的、即时的缓解。Mental health disorders affect nearly one billion people globally, yet 75% of individuals in low- and middle-income countries receive no treatment due to workforce shortages, cost barriers, and stigma. Current AI-powered wellness solutions predominantly rely on single-mode conversational interfaces that suffer high abandonment rates and fail to provide measurable, immediate relief calibrated to users’ dynamic emotional states.

本文介绍了 Copewell，这是一种新颖的多智能体群系统，旨在通过以人为本的人工智能原理扩大获得心理健康支持的机会。我们的架构引入了三项技术创新：（1）集成自我报告、生理和情境数据的多源评估框架，以减轻算法偏差； (2) 使用 Russell 的情感循环模型进行效价-唤醒情感映射，将用户路由到专门的人工智能代理； (3) 将对话支持与基于证据的感官健康协议相结合的双模式干预实施。This paper presents Copewell, a novel multi-agent swarm system designed to expand access to mental wellness support through human-centered AI principles. Our architecture introduces three technical innovations: (1) a multi-source assessment framework integrating self-reported, physiological, and contextual data to mitigate algorithmic bias; (2) valence-arousal emotion mapping using Russell’s Circumplex Model of Affect to route users to specialized AI agents; and (3) dual-mode intervention delivery combining conversational support with evidence-based sensory wellness protocols.

我们研究了 Copewell 开发背后的社会技术设计考虑因素，包括隐私优先的架构、通过专门的道德监督代理人进行的嵌入式道德监督，以及由心理健康从业者提供信息的参与式设计。早期从业者的参与和测试版部署为设计决策提供了信息，并确定了未来实证评估的方向。这项工作通过展示技术架构如何从一开始就实现公平和安全原则，为负责任的人工智能话语做出了贡献。We examine the sociotechnical design considerations underlying Copewell’s development, including a privacy-first architecture, embedded ethical oversight through a dedicated Ethics Supervisor agent, and participatory design informed by mental health practitioners. Early practitioner engagement and beta deployment inform design decisions and identify directions for future empirical evaluation. This work contributes to responsible AI discourse by demonstrating how technical architecture can operationalize equity and safety principles from inception.

1 简介Introduction

精神健康障碍是全球公共卫生领域最重大但尚未得到充分解决的挑战之一。世界卫生组织 (WHO) 估计，全球约有 10 亿人患有精神健康问题，但大多数人没有接受治疗或支持Mental health disorders represent one of the most significant yet underaddressed challenges in global public health. The World Health Organization (WHO) estimates that approximately one billion people worldwide live with a mental health condition, yet the majority receive no treatment or support[1]。这种治疗差距在低收入和中等收入国家 (LMIC) 最为严重，由于专业劳动力短缺、经济障碍和持续的社会耻辱，估计有 75% 的患有精神健康问题的人无法获得护理. This treatment gap is most acute in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), where an estimated 75% of individuals experiencing mental health difficulties have no access to care, owing to a combination of professional workforce shortages, economic barriers, and persistent social stigma[2, 3].

心理健康专业人员的稀缺是一个结构性问题，而不是偶然问题。全球心理健康工作者中位数为每 10 万人 13.5 人，其中 43% 是护士，22% 是心理学家，16% 是精神科医生，这意味着结构化治疗护理仍然集中在本已稀缺的劳动力中的一小部分。The scarcity of mental health professionals is a structural rather than incidental problem. The global median number of mental health workers is 13.5 per 100,000 population, of whom 43% are nurses, 22% psychologists, and 16% psychiatrists, meaning structured therapeutic care remains concentrated in a small fraction of an already scarce workforce[1]。中低收入国家远低于这一阈值，比率在每 100,000 人 1.1 至 2.4 之间。在高收入国家，虽然专业机会相对较好（每 10 万人中有 67.2 人），但需求始终超过供应，给卫生系统带来严重的经济压力，并延长了护理等待时间. LMICs fall well below this threshold with ratios between 1.1 and 2.4 per 100,000. In high-income countries, while professional access is comparatively better (67.2 per 100,000), demand consistently outpaces supply, placing severe economic strain on health systems and prolonging wait times for care[4, 5]。实际后果是，全球大多数患有精神健康问题的人要么得不到支持，要么需要很长时间才能获得支持。. The practical consequence is that the majority of the global population experiencing mental health difficulties faces either no support or a long delay before receiving it.

数字技术，特别是移动应用，已被提议作为弥补这一差距的可扩展机制。心理健康应用程序的激增，估计主要应用程序平台上有超过 10,000 个，反映了公众的真实需求Digital technologies, and mobile applications in particular, have been proposed as scalable mechanisms for bridging this gap. The proliferation of mental wellness applications, estimated at over 10,000 on major app platforms, reflects genuine public demand[6]。然而，支持大多数这些工具的证据基础很薄弱。对 278 个心理健康应用程序的系统分析发现，只有 16% 发表了可行性或功效研究. However, the evidence base underpinning most of these tools is thin. A systematic analysis of 278 mental health apps has found that only 16% had published feasibility or efficacy studies[7]。许多依赖于单一模式的对话界面，这些界面提供脚本化或狭隘响应的交互，无法适应用户不断变化的情绪状态或提供有效的干预. Many rely on single-mode conversational interfaces that provide scripted or narrowly responsive interactions, failing to adapt to users’ changing emotional states or to offer effective intervention[8, 9]。这可能会导致高放弃率；与移动健康工具一样，心理健康应用程序的保留率往往特别低，很少有用户在第一个月后继续参与. This arguably results in high abandonment rates; as among mobile health tools, mental health apps tend to have especially low retention, with very few users continuing to engage beyond the first month[10].

这些限制指出了一个结构性问题，即大多数数字健康工具旨在复制而不是重新思考传统的支持模式。人工智能（AI）原生补充护理是一种完全不同的护理；其定义是整个护理路径的可用性、即时性和连续性，而不是临床接触的深度These limitations point to a structural problem that most digital wellness tools have been designed to replicate, rather than rethink, traditional models of support. Artificial Intelligence (AI)-native supplementary care is a different kind of care entirely; one defined by availability, immediacy, and continuity across the care pathway rather than the depth of clinical encounter[11]。因此，此类工具的负责任设计要求的不是近似治疗，而是响应性、实时参与，承认情绪状态，提供即时缓解，并保持通往专业护理的清晰途径。. The responsible design of such tools, therefore, demands not an approximation of therapy, but responsive, real-time engagement that acknowledges emotional state, provides immediate relief, and maintains clear pathways to professional care.

2 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

2.1 数字心理健康景观Digital Mental Health Landscape

过去十年，在智能手机普及、自然语言处理 (NLP) 进步以及公众日益认识到心理健康作为优先健康领域的推动下，数字心理健康干预措施取得了显着增长。 Headspace、Wysa 和 Woebot 等应用程序已获得大量用户采用，表明对可访问的健康支持的真正需求The past decade has seen remarkable growth in digital mental health interventions, driven by smartphone proliferation, advances in natural language processing (NLP), and growing public recognition of mental health as a priority health domain. Applications such as Headspace, Wysa and Woebot have achieved significant user adoption, demonstrating genuine demand for accessible wellness support[12, 13, 14]。证据基础为这一势头提供了实质性依据。. The evidence base offers substantive grounds for this momentum.

数字心理健康应用程序在多个方面都展现了真正且有据可查的价值。作为可访问、低门槛、可扩展的工具，它们解决了传统临床环境无法解决的结构性限制，例如，绕过看医生的漫长等待时间、全天候运行，并覆盖那些在经济或系统上仍然无法获得专业精神卫生保健的人群Digital mental health applications have demonstrated genuine and well-documented value across several dimensions. As accessible, low-barrier, scalable tools, they address structural constraints that traditional clinical settings cannot, e.g., bypassing long waiting times to see a clinician, operating around the clock, and reaching populations for whom professional mental health care remains financially or systemically inaccessible[15, 16, 17]。收费应用程序的平均费用仅为单次治疗费用的一小部分，使不同经济能力的学生、年轻专业人士和来自低收入国家的社区等能够在经济上获得心理健康支持. Fee-based apps average a fraction of the cost of a single therapy session, making mental health support economically accessible to varying financial abilities such as students, young professionals and communities from low-income countries[16, 18]。除了改善可及性之外，应用程序还展示了通过降低个人（例如，内在羞耻、自我耻辱）和文化（例如，集体主义背景下的家庭声誉问题）层面寻求帮助的障碍来减少耻辱感的有意义的能力，这通常会使面对面的治疗感到不舒服. Beyond improving access, apps have demonstrated a meaningful capacity to reduce stigma by lowering barriers to help-seeking both on personal (e.g., internalized shame, self-stigma) and cultural (e.g., family reputation concerns in collectivist contexts) levels, that often make in-person treatment feel uncomfortable[19, 20]。临床上，随机对照试验和荟萃分析表明，心理健康应用程序始终如一. Clinically, randomized controlled trials and meta-analyses have shown that mental health apps consistentlydo帮助减少抑郁、焦虑和相关的痛苦。据报道，与对照条件相比，抑郁症（效应大小~0.28-0.38）和广泛性焦虑（~0.26-0.30）的症状显着减轻，证实了真实的但小到中等的益处help reduce depression, anxiety and related distress. Significant symptom reduction has been reported for depression (effect sizes ~0.28–0.38) and generalized anxiety (~0.26–0.30) compared with control conditions, confirming real, though small-to-moderate benefits[14, 21, 22, 23]。认知行为疗法 (CBT)、情绪跟踪、聊天机器人系统和专业指导与更大的效果和进一步持续的结果相关. Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), mood tracking, chatbot systems and professional guidance have been linked to larger effects and further sustained outcomes[23, 24]。最持久的是，应用程序已被证明作为心理教育工具是有效的，可以提供心理健康素养、应对技能指导和结构化的自我管理内容. Most durably, apps have proven effective as psychoeducation vehicles, delivering mental health literacy, coping skill instruction, and structured self-management content[25, 26, 27]。总体而言，数字心理健康应用程序不是作为专业护理的替代品，而是作为专业护理的补充层，在支持生态系统中占据着独特且合法的作用。. Overall, used not as replacements for professional care but as a complementary layer within it, digital mental health applications occupy a distinct and legitimate role in the support ecosystem.

尽管有这一证明的价值，但文献一致指出了一系列限制当前数字心理健康工具影响的结构性限制，其中最根本的是采用和证据之间的差距。大多数可用的应用程序都是在临床或学术框架之外开发的，市场需求比治疗证据更频繁地推动设计决策Despite this demonstrated value, the literature consistently identifies a set of structural limitations that constrain the impact of current digital mental health tools, the most fundamental being the gap between adoption and evidence. Most available apps are developed outside clinical or academic frameworks, with market imperatives driving design decisions more often than therapeutic evidence[28]。这导致工具依赖于未经验证的内容和通用对话脚本，而不是已建立的心理框架，从而使采用速度显着超过验证速度. This has resulted in tools that rely on unvalidated content and generic conversational scripts rather than established psychological frameworks, allowing adoption to significantly outpace validation[29, 30]。除了可信度之外，当前的工具还面临着重大的设计限制。大多数部署统一的、单模态的对话界面，无论用户的情绪状态、上下文或需求如何，都应用相同的方法；从而无法动态适应人类心理体验的多样性. Beyond credibility, current tools face significant design limitations. Most deploy uniform, single-modality conversational interfaces that apply the same approach regardless of the user’s emotional state, context, or need; thus fail to adapt dynamically to the diversity of human psychological experience[31, 32, 33]。安全、道德和隐私是另一个令人担忧的问题。大多数应用程序中的危机管理仍然处于初级阶段，通常仅限于显示静态热线号码，而没有结构化检测或升级协议. Safety, ethics, and privacy represent a further cluster of concerns. Crisis management in most apps remains rudimentary, often limited to displaying a static hotline number without structured detection or escalation protocols[34, 35]。此外，道德保障措施通常作为事后内容过滤器而不是架构功能来实现。整个应用程序领域的数据实践也引起了严格审查，隐私保护往往不足以满足心理健康信息的敏感性. Furthermore, ethical safeguards are typically implemented as post-hoc content filters rather than architectural features. Data practices across the app landscape have also attracted significant scrutiny, with privacy protections frequently inadequate for the sensitivity of mental health information[36]。最后，偏见和公平问题仍未得到充分解决. Finally, questions of bias and equity remain underaddressed[37, 38]。主要为西方、英语人群设计的工具可能会大规模重现现有的医疗保健不平等现象，其分类情感框架和特定文化的互动规范限制了向最受益的全球多元化人群的可转移性. Tools designed primarily for Western, English-speaking populations risk reproducing existing healthcare inequities at scale, with categorical emotion frameworks and culturally-specific interaction norms limiting transferability to the diverse global populations who stand to benefit most[39]。解决这些限制不需要对现有设计进行渐进式改进，而是需要对人工智能健康工具的构建方式进行根本性的重新思考——将证据、安全、道德和公平作为基本原则，而不是事后添加的功能. Addressing these limitations requires not incremental improvement to existing designs, but a fundamental rethinking of how AI wellness tools are architected - one that embeds evidence, safety, ethics, and equity as foundational principles rather than features added after the fact[40, 41].

2.2 情感计算和情感建模Affective Computing and Emotion Modeling

理解和应对情绪状态是有效心理健康支持的核心，并且越来越成为心理健康人工智能设计的核心。情感计算被定义为计算系统识别、解释和响应人类情绪状态的能力，已成为构建能够有意义地参与心理体验的人工智能工具的基础学科。Understanding and responding to emotional states lies at the heart of effective mental health support and increasingly, at the heart of mental health AI design. Affective computing, defined as the capacity of computational systems to recognize, interpret, and respond to human emotional states, has emerged as a foundational discipline for building AI tools that can engage meaningfully with psychological experience[42]。其技术涵盖情感识别、情感分析和多模态信号处理，利用文本、语音、生理信号和面部表情来构建用户情感状态的全面图景. Its techniques span emotion recognition, sentiment analysis, and multimodal signal processing, drawing on text, voice, physiological signals, and facial expressions to build a comprehensive picture of a user’s affective state[43, 44]。特别是在心理健康领域，情感计算使系统能够超越静态的、一刀切的交互，转向实时响应情绪状态的动态自适应支持，这是有效的个性化数字心理健康干预的基本途径. In mental health contexts specifically, affective computing enables systems to move beyond static, one-size-fits-all interactions toward dynamically adaptive support that responds to emotional state in real time, which is a foundational pathway to effective personalized digital mental health intervention[8, 43].

在数字心理健康环境中，情感模型的选择对系统设计具有重大影响。分类方法，例如艾克曼的基本情感框架In digital mental health contexts, the choice of emotion model carries significant consequences for system design. Categorical approaches, such as Ekman’s basic emotion framework[45]，将情绪分类为离散状态，例如恐惧、悲伤或愤怒，并提供可解释性，但牺牲了细粒度计算分析和旨在服务全球不同人群的系统的跨文化适用性所需的细微差别, classify emotion into discrete states such as fear, sadness, or anger, and offer interpretability but sacrifice the nuance required for fine-grained computational analysis and cross-cultural applicability for systems designed to serve diverse global populations[46, 47]。另一方面，维度模型将情绪状态表示为连续多维空间中的点（例如 Plutchik 的情绪轮）. The core advantage of this approach is specialization: rather than requiring one agent to approximate all support modalities, a swarm of specialized agents can each be optimized for a specific context, with coordination mechanisms determining which agent engages at any given moment.

在医疗保健人工智能领域，多智能体架构在临床决策支持、治疗计划和患者监测等领域展现了价值，在这些领域，人类健康状态的复杂性同样超过了单智能体的能力In healthcare AI, multi-agent architectures have demonstrated value across clinical decision support, treatment planning, and patient monitoring, domains where the complexity of human health states similarly exceeds single-agent capacity[54]。特别是在心理健康领域，之前的工作已经探索了治疗支持的代理框架，包括 RAG 增强系统，该系统将对话代理建立在经过验证的临床知识中. 2.4 以人为本的人工智能和负责任的设计Human-Centered AI and Responsible Design

心理健康人工智能占据着独特的高风险设计空间。与通用人工智能应用程序不同，旨在解决心理困扰的系统在用户脆弱性、敏感个人数据和相应支持决策的交叉点上运行。这使得负责任的设计不再是一种理想品质，而是一项基本要求Mental health AI occupies a uniquely high-stakes design space. Unlike general-purpose AI applications, systems designed to engage with psychological distress operate at the intersection of user vulnerability, sensitive personal data, and consequential support decisions. This makes responsible design not an aspirational quality but a foundational requirement[36, 41]。该领域的设计失败会给用户安全、尊严和自主带来直接风险，需要与环境敏感性相称的道德严格程度. Design failures in this domain carry direct risks to user safety, dignity, and autonomy that demand a level of ethical rigour commensurate with the sensitivity of the context[37].

已建立的负责任的人工智能框架，例如 IEEE 道德统一设计原则、欧盟人工智能法案和经合组织人工智能原则，都集中在一组核心要求上：透明度、问责制、公平性和安全性Established responsible AI frameworks such as the IEEE Ethically Aligned Design principles, the EU AI Act, and the OECD Principles on AI converge on a core set of requirements: transparency, accountability, fairness, and safety[59, 60, 61]。特别是在心理健康背景下，这些抽象原则转化为具体的架构需求：危机检测和升级机制、保护隐私的数据处理、文化包容性交互模型以及对系统范围和限制的诚实描述。心理健康人工智能工具评估框架（FAITA-MH）将这些要求具体化为六个评估维度：可信度、用户体验、用户代理、公平和包容性、透明度和危机管理，为人工智能驱动的健康工具提供最直接相关的评估视角. In mental health contexts specifically, these abstract principles translate into concrete architectural demands: crisis detection and escalation mechanisms, privacy-preserving data handling, culturally inclusive interaction models, and honest delineation of system scope and limitations. The Framework for AI Tool Assessment in Mental Health (FAITA-MH) operationalizes these requirements into six evaluative dimensions: Credibility, User Experience, User Agency, Equity and Inclusivity, Transparency, and Crisis Management, providing the most directly relevant evaluation lens for AI-powered wellness tools[62].

敏感领域中的负责任的人工智能进一步要求将道德承诺嵌入架构中，而不是作为事后合规机制应用，这被称为Responsible AI in sensitive domains further requires that ethical commitments be embedded architecturally rather than applied as post-hoc compliance mechanisms, which is referred to as设计伦理ethics by design [63]。对于心理健康人工智能来说，这种区别尤其重要，因为在生成响应后监控道德违规行为的系统所提供的保护从根本上来说比道德监督作为交互结构参与者的系统要弱。. For mental health AI, this distinction is particularly consequential since a system that monitors for ethical violations after generating a response offers fundamentally weaker protection than one in which ethical oversight is a structural participant in the interaction.

最后，心理健康人工智能中的负责任的设计需要在整个开发过程中与从业者和用户进行有意义的参与，不是作为验证主体，而是作为共同设计师，其领域专业知识决定架构决策Finally, responsible design in mental health AI requires meaningful engagement with practitioners and users throughout the development process, not as validation subjects but as co-designers whose domain expertise shapes architectural decisions[64]。第 5 节详细讨论了 Copewell 开发中这种参与的性质和影响。这些原则共同构成的不是应用于 Copewell 设计的清单，而是派生其架构的哲学基础 - 每个原则都可以追溯到特定的设计决策。. The nature and implications of this engagement in Copewell’s development are discussed in detail in Section 5. Together, these principles constitute not a checklist applied to Copewell’s design but the philosophical foundation from which its architecture was derived - each traceable to specific design decisions.

3.2 多源评估框架Multi-Source Assessment Framework

健康应用中单一模式评估的一个主要限制是它容易受到系统偏差的影响。自我报告的情绪状态受到社会期望效应、有限的内省准确性以及沟通主观体验的固有困难的影响。仅依赖自我报告可能会错误地分类用户的实际需求，从而可能将他们引导到不适当的支持方式。相反，仅依赖生理信号会忽略自我报告所捕捉到的情绪状态的主观、体验维度A central limitation of single-modality assessment in wellness applications is its susceptibility to systematic bias. Self-reported emotional states are subject to social desirability effects, limited introspective accuracy, and the inherent difficulty of communicating subjective experience. Reliance on self-report alone risks misclassifying a user’s actual need, potentially routing them to an inappropriate support modality. Conversely, reliance on physiological signals alone ignores the subjective, experiential dimension of emotional states that self-report captures[65, 66].

Copewell 通过集成三个数据流的多源评估框架解决了这个问题：自我报告状态（权重为 40%）、生理和生物识别数据（35%）以及环境和背景数据（25%）。相对权重反映了信号与用户体验状态的接近度的层次结构：自我报告提供了对主观体验的直接访问，生理学提供了唤醒的客观相关性，上下文提供了解释这些信号的情境框架。该加权方案是根据生态瞬时评估中既定原则制定的原始设计决策Copewell addresses this through a multi-source assessment framework integrating three data streams: self-reported state (weighted at 40%), physiological and biometric data (35%), and environmental and contextual data (25%). The relative weighting reflects the hierarchy of signal proximity to the user’s experienced state: self-report provides direct access to subjective experience, physiology provides objective correlates of arousal, and context provides the situational frame for interpreting these signals. This weighting schema is an original design decision informed by established principles in Ecological Momentary Assessment[67]和情境行为科学and contextual behavioral science[68].

自我报告的数据是通过结构化的每日情绪检查和聊天机器人对话的对话输入来收集的。除了明确的报告之外，系统还分析语言表达中的隐含模式，包括词汇广度、句子长度和情感语气，以显示用户可能没有有意识报告的信号Self-reported data is collected through structured daily mood check-ins and conversational input from chatbot dialogues. Beyond explicit reports, the system analyses implicit patterns in linguistic expression, including vocabulary breadth, sentence length, and affective tone, to surface signals that may not be consciously reported by the user[69].

通过设备健康应用程序编程接口提供的生理数据集成包括睡眠质量指标、心率变异性 (HRV) 和活动水平。这些生物识别信号旨在用作替代性唤醒和压力的客观关联，以补充和交叉验证自我报告。Physiological data integration, where available through device health application programming interfaces, includes sleep quality metrics, heart rate variability (HRV), and activity levels. These biometric signals are meant to be used for proxy as objective correlates of arousal and stress that complement and cross-validate self-report.

情境数据源自日历集成，使系统能够考虑情境因素，例如，高会议周、锻炼课程、定期就医，为当前状态评估提供解释性情境。这一层将情境行为科学的见解付诸实践，这表明情绪体验在很大程度上是由环境环境塑造的，而个人可能不会有意识地将其与当前状态联系起来Contextual data is derived from calendar integration and enables the system to account for situational factors, e.g., a high-meeting week, workout session, regular doctor visit, that provide interpretive context for current state assessment. This layer operationalizes insights from contextual behavioral science, which demonstrates that emotional experience is substantially shaped by environmental circumstances that individuals may not consciously connect to their current state[68, 70].

这三个数据流的交叉验证旨在防止系统将孤立信号作为确定信号时出现的算法错误。报告感觉良好但睡眠数据显示严重中断且日历显示持续超负荷的用户与所有三个信号一致的用户代表不同的风险状况。The cross-validation of these three data streams is designed to prevent the category of algorithmic error that arises when a system takes an isolated signal as definitive. A user who reports feeling well but whose sleep data indicates severe disruption and whose calendar shows sustained overload represents a different risk profile from one in whom all three signals are aligned.

3.3 效价-唤醒情绪图Valence-Arousal Emotion Mapping

效价维度捕捉当前情绪体验的愉快（例如，快乐、平静）或不愉快（例如，心烦意乱、昏昏欲睡）。唤醒维度捕获激活水平，范围从高能量状态（例如兴奋、紧张）到低能量状态（例如平静、疲劳）。这些维度共同定义了四个功能象限，每个象限都与有意义的不同支持需求相关联，并对应于专门的人工智能代理。The valence dimension captures the pleasantness (e.g., happy, serene) or unpleasantness (e.g., upset, lethargic) of the current emotional experience. The arousal dimension captures the activation level, ranging from high-energy states (e.g., excited, tense) to low-energy states (e.g., calm, fatigued). Together these dimensions define four functional quadrants, each associated with meaningfully distinct support needs and corresponding to a specialized AI agent.

这种连续表示的数学优势包括能够计算情绪距离（即用户的状态距中性或积极基线有多远）、质心（即整个会话或时期的典型情绪位置）和变异分数（即情绪波动程度，其本身可能具有解释意义）The mathematical advantages of this continuous representation include the ability to calculate emotional distances (i.e., how far a user’s state is from a neutral or positive baseline), centroids (i.e., the typical emotional position across a session or period), and variability scores (i.e., the degree of emotional fluctuation, which may itself carry interpretive significance)[50, 51]。这些指标旨在定量描述用户随时间的情绪模式，并为系统健康范围内的个性化和纵向模式分析提供基础。. These metrics aim to enable quantitative characterization of a user’s emotional pattern over time and provide a foundation for both personalization and longitudinal pattern analysis within the system’s wellness scope.

3.5 道德监督代理人The Ethics Supervisor Agent

Copewell 的一个显着架构特征是道德监督代理，它是一种专用监督机制，作为跨所有代理交互的横切层运行。与将道德保障措施作为静态系统检查点或事后内容过滤器实施的方法不同A distinctive architectural feature of Copewell is the Ethics Supervisor agent, which is a dedicated oversight mechanism that operates as a cross-cutting layer across all agent interactions. Unlike approaches that implement ethical safeguards as static system checkpoints or post-hoc content filters[40, 63]，科普韦尔的道德主管是代理群体的积极参与者，能够实时观察、评估和干预。, Copewell’s Ethics Supervisor is an active participant in the agent swarm, capable of observing, evaluating, and intervening in real time.

这种设计的概念基础与人类认知中的调节网络功能进行了类比：前额皮质系统通常被描述为支持更自动化、情感驱动的过程的评估和调节The conceptual basis for this design draws an analogy to regulatory network functioning in human cognition: prefrontal cortex systems are often described as supporting the evaluation and regulation of more automatic, affect-driven processes[71]。在科普韦尔的架构中，道德主管执行类似的功能，监控专门代理的输出，以符合道德准则、安全协议和范围边界，并在受到威胁时进行干预。. In Copewell’s architecture, the Ethics Supervisor performs an analogous function monitoring the outputs of specialized agents for alignment with ethical guidelines, safety protocols, and scope boundaries, and intervening when these are threatened.

道德主管的干预权限跨越两个不同的领域。在道德监督领域，它标记了超出科普韦尔健康范围的隐含临床主张的响应，重定向接近临床边界的对话（例如，从情感支持转向诊断框架的讨论），并确保数据处理符合隐私承诺。在安全领域，它监控所有对话互动中的急性风险指标（例如与自杀意念和自残相关的语言标记）。当检测到这些情况时，它会完全覆盖所有活跃的代理，提供经过验证的、富有同理心的危机响应，同时为紧急资源提供清晰的路标。这种双重功能在架构上统一在单个代理中，因为安全和道德在心理健康背景下不是可分离的问题，而且它们是对用户福祉的相同基本承诺的两种表达。The Ethics Supervisor’s intervention authorities span two distinct domains. In the domain of ethical oversight, it flags responses that make implicit clinical claims beyond Copewell’s wellness scope, redirects conversations approaching clinical boundaries (e.g., discussions that move from emotional support toward diagnostic framing), and ensures data handling aligns with privacy commitments. In the domain of safety, it monitors all conversational interactions for acute risk indicators (e.g., linguistic markers associated with suicidal ideation and self-harm). When these are detected, it overrides all active agents entirely, delivering a validated, empathetic crisis response alongside clear signposting to emergency resources. This dual function is architecturally unified in a single agent because safety and ethics are not separable concerns in a mental wellness context, and they are rather two expressions of the same foundational commitment to user wellbeing.

3.6 感官健康方式Sensory Wellness Modalities

科普韦尔的一个决定性架构选择是将基于证据的感官健康模式与对话支持相结合。现有的心理健康人工智能系统主要仅限于对话，不提供直接生理干预的机制A defining architectural choice in Copewell is the integration of evidence-based sensory wellness modalities alongside conversational support. Existing mental health AI systems are predominantly conversation-only, offering no mechanism for direct physiological intervention[40]。这种单通道设计限制了系统提供可测量和立即缓解的能力，特别是在仅靠认知参与可能不足或禁忌的高唤醒状态下。 Copewell 采用了一个针对生理和知觉调节机制的感官健康库。. This single-channel design limits the system’s capacity to deliver measurable and immediate relief, particularly in high-arousal states where cognitive engagement alone may be insufficient or contraindicated. Copewell employs a sensory wellness library targeting physiological and perceptual mechanisms of regulation.

感官库由音频、视觉和组合的多感官组件组成，通过适应用户偏好和环境背景的三层系统提供。仅音频层支持在视觉参与不切实际的环境中使用，例如闭眼练习、通勤、低带宽环境、利用双耳节拍、自主感觉经络反应 (ASMR) 内容和治疗频率传递。仅视觉层支持对声音敏感的用户、共享环境中的用户或对视觉处理反应更强烈的用户，使用分形图案、色彩疗法和无声脑电波夹带。完整的多感官层协调同步的音频和视觉传输，以在上下文允许的情况下实现最大程度的治疗参与。The sensory library is organized across audio, visual, and combined multi-sensory components, delivered through a three-tier system that accommodates user preferences and environmental contexts. The audio-only tier supports use in contexts where visual engagement is impractical, e.g., eyes-closed practice, commuting, low-bandwidth environments, drawing on binaural beats, Autonomous Sensory Meridian Response (ASMR) content, and therapeutic frequency delivery. The visual-only tier supports users with sound sensitivity, those in shared environments, or those who respond more strongly to visual processing, using fractal patterns, color therapy, and silent brainwave entrainment. The full multi-sensory tier coordinates synchronized audio and visual delivery for maximum therapeutic engagement when context permits.

每种方式都借鉴了不同的研究成果，将感官输入与可测量的生理和情感结果联系起来。在受控条件下，ASMR 与心率降低和情绪改善有关Each modality draws on a distinct body of research linking sensory input to measurable physiological and affective outcomes. ASMR has been associated with reductions in heart rate and improvements in mood across controlled conditions[72, 73]。视觉诱导技术，包括特定频率的脑电波同步闪烁模式，已被研究其在焦虑、情绪低落和聚焦演示中调节注意力和唤醒状态的能力. Visual entrainment techniques, including brainwave-synchronized flicker patterns at specific frequencies, have been investigated for their capacity to modulate attentional and arousal states across anxiety, low mood, and focus presentations[74, 75]。分形图案暴露，特别是在中范围分形维数下，与偏好和一些减轻压力或放松相关的效果相关，通常通过分形流畅性来解释. Fractal pattern exposure, especially at mid-range fractal dimensions, has been associated with preference and some stress-reduction or relaxation-related effects, often explained through fractal fluency[76, 77]。尽管不同颜色模式的证据基础强度各不相同，但根据特定波长的心理生理学关联进行校准的色彩疗法干预措施在对照研究中显示出对焦虑和情绪状态的影响. Color therapy interventions, calibrated to wavelength-specific psychophysiological associations, have shown effects on anxiety and mood states in controlled studies, though the evidence base across color modalities varies in strength[78]。使用双耳节拍的脑电波夹带针对特定频段：α (8–12 Hz) 用于减轻焦虑和压力，γ (40 Hz) 用于集中注意力和认知表现，δ (0.5–4 Hz) 用于睡眠开始，β (13–30 Hz) 用于情绪激活. Brainwave entrainment using binaural beats targets specific frequency bands: alpha (8–12 Hz) for anxiety and stress reduction, gamma (40 Hz) for focus and cognitive performance, delta (0.5–4 Hz) for sleep onset, and beta (13–30 Hz) for mood activation[79, 80, 81, 82]。这些模式的证据基础强度各不相同，科普韦尔对它们的整合被视为以证据为依据，而不是经过临床验证，并将持续的实证评估确定为优先事项。. The evidence base across these modalities is variable in strength, and Copewell’s integration of them is framed as evidence-informed rather than clinically validated, with ongoing empirical evaluation identified as a priority.

4 社会技术设计考虑因素Sociotechnical Design Considerations

本节记录了影响 Copewell 发展的关键社会技术设计考虑因素，涵盖数据隐私、道德监督和危机安全、公平和参与式设计。每一项都反映了在系统架构层面而不是治理文档层面上实施以人为本的人工智能承诺的刻意努力。This section documents the key sociotechnical design considerations that shaped Copewell’s development, spanning data privacy, ethical oversight and crisis safety, equity, and participatory design. Each reflects a deliberate effort to operationalise human-centered AI commitments at the level of system architecture rather than governance documentation.

4.1 隐私优先架构Privacy-First Architecture

心理健康数据在更广泛的个人信息领域中占据着独特的敏感地位。在情绪困扰背景下进行的披露涉及情绪、心理状态、个人历史和危机经历，并对就业、人际关系、保险和个人尊严产生影响，这将它们与其他类别的用户数据区分开来Mental health data occupies a uniquely sensitive position within the broader landscape of personal information. Disclosures made in the context of emotional distress concern mood, psychological state, personal history, and crisis experiences, and carry implications for employment, relationships, insurance, and personal dignity that distinguish them from other categories of user data[36, 40]。科普韦尔的隐私架构通过一系列结构性承诺反映了这种敏感性。. Copewell’s privacy architecture reflects this sensitivity through a set of structural commitments.

所有敏感数据类别，包括精神状态评估、对话交互以及可选的集成日历和健康数据，都是端到端加密的，并且只能由用户和人工智能系统访问。模型训练不使用任何数据，任何情况下都不发生第三方共享。用户保留对其数据的完全控制权，可以随时通过帐户删除进行删除。仅当用户保持活跃帐户时，保留期才会延长，超过该期限后不会积累任何残留数据。All sensitive data categories, including mental state assessments, conversational interactions, and optionally integrated calendar and health data, are end-to-end encrypted and accessible exclusively to the user and the AI system. No data is used for model training, and no third-party sharing occurs under any circumstances. Users retain full control over their data, with deletion available at any point through account removal. Retention extends only as long as the user maintains an active account, with no residual data accumulation beyond that period.

这些承诺反映了与适用的隐私监管框架一致的既定数据最小化原则These commitments reflect established data minimization principles consistent with applicable privacy regulatory frameworks[83]旨在确保有意义地参与心理健康工具所需的信任得到结构性支持。and are designed to ensure that the trust necessary for meaningful engagement with a mental wellness tool is structurally supported.

4.2 道德监督和危机安全Ethical Oversight and Crisis Safety

现有心理健康人工智能工具的一个反复出现的局限性是将道德保障措施视为事后添加，例如在响应生成后应用内容过滤器，或在系统本身外部运行的治理流程A recurring limitation of existing mental health AI tools is the treatment of ethical safeguards as post-hoc additions e.g., content filters applied after response generation, or governance processes operating external to the system itself[40, 63]。这种方法在结构上对于实时对话环境来说是不够的，在实时对话环境中，道德上重要的时刻是动态出现的，并且静态规则集无法预测。. This approach is structurally insufficient for real-time conversational contexts where ethically consequential moments arise dynamically and cannot be anticipated by static rule sets.

Copewell 通过道德监督代理（参见第 3.5 节）解决了这个问题，其架构定位为实时群体参与者，而不是事后过滤器，直接操作Copewell addresses this through the Ethics Supervisor agent (See Section 3.5) whose architectural positioning as a real-time swarm participant rather than a post-hoc filter directly operationalizes the设计伦理ethics by design原则：从一开始就将道德要求嵌入到系统架构中，而不是作为合规性的补充principle: the embedding of ethical requirements into system architecture from inception rather than as compliance additions[84]。至关重要的是，道德监督员的任务涵盖了单一统一机制内的道德监督和危机安全，不要将这些视为单独的问题，而是将其视为对用户福祉的相同基本承诺的两种表达。. Critically, the Ethics Supervisor’s mandate encompasses both ethical oversight and crisis safety within a single unified mechanism, treating these not as separate concerns but as two expressions of the same foundational commitment to user wellbeing.

在即时工程层面，道德约束通过明确的系统指令得到加强。该系统将自己定位为健康伴侣而不是有执照的专业人士，禁止使用暗示有执照的实践的临床术语，并且被指示永远不要将互动视为治疗课程。当用户需求超出系统的适当范围时，会嵌入重定向脚本，并在需要临床干预时路由到专业支持。定期的对话中免责声明不仅在入职时而且在整个参与过程中不断强化范围界限（见图 1）。At the prompt engineering level, ethical constraints are reinforced through explicit system instructions. The system identifies itself as a wellness companion rather than a licensed professional, is prohibited from using clinical terminology implying licensed practice, and is instructed never to frame interactions as therapy sessions. Redirection scripting is embedded for moments when user needs exceed the system’s appropriate scope, routing to professional support when clinical intervention is indicated. 危机应对设计反映了两个深思熟虑的选择。首先，推荐前验证原则确保用户在被引导至外部资源之前感受到被倾听The crisis response design reflects two deliberate choices. Second, the use of structurally constrained rather than LLM-generated crisis responses with mandatory elements, including empathetic acknowledgment, geolocalised resource provision, and metadata tagging for system-level safety tracking, prioritizes predictability and reliability over conversational fluency in high-stakes moments where response consistency is a safety requirement[34, 35]。科普韦尔的危机路径明确在健康伴侣的范围内运作：它提供即时的同理心支持和专业转介，但并不将自己定位为临床安全网或紧急专业干预的替代品。. Copewell’s crisis pathway operates explicitly within the scope of a wellness companion: it provides immediate empathetic support and professional referral but does not position itself as a clinical safety net or substitute for emergency professional intervention.

4.3 公平和可及性Equity and Accessibility

公平系统设计的核心要求是架构、成本结构和用户界面不能概括系统旨在解决的访问障碍。弱势群体表示，由于语言不匹配、数字素养差、信任薄弱、缺乏设备或强身份验证以及数字接触中的沟通质量差等障碍A core requirement of equitable system design is that the architecture, cost structure, and user interface do not recapitulate the very barriers to access that the system purports to address. Vulnerable groups report barriers from language mismatch, poor digital literacy, weak trust, lack of devices or strong authentication, and poor communication quality in digital encounters[86]。因此，公平设计需要将公平目标转化为具体的架构决策。. Thus, designing for equity requires translating equity goals into concrete architectural decisions.

一些具体的设计选择反映了这一承诺。 Copewell 坚持移动优先，反映出智能手机接入在其目标地区的许多宽带基础设施中占据主导地位Several specific design choices reflect this commitment. Copewell is mobile-first, reflecting the dominance of smartphone access over broadband infrastructure in many of the regions it targets[87]。该系统旨在以低数据速率有效运行，目前有 20 种语言版本。它的对话界面故意避免假设熟悉西方临床心理学的术语或规范，这可能无法跨文化背景翻译。. The system is designed to function effectively at low data rates, and currently available in 20 language versions. Its conversational interface deliberately avoids assuming familiarity with the terminology or norms of Western clinical psychology, which may not translate across cultural contexts.

选择罗素的Circumplex模型作为情绪映射的计算基础具有重大的公平意义The choice of Russell’s Circumplex Model as the computational substrate for emotion mapping has significant equity implications[49, 88]。源自临床传统的分类情感框架在跨文化可转移性方面具有明确的局限性，反映了情感表达、概念化和管理情感披露的社会规范的文化特异性. Categorical emotion frameworks derived from clinical traditions have well-documented limitations in cross-cultural transferability, reflecting cultural specificity in emotional expression, conceptualization, and the social norms governing emotional disclosure[89]。诸如效价-唤醒框架之类的维度模型似乎比分类情感框架提供相对更强的跨文化有效性，因为即使唤醒的显着性和效价-唤醒关系因文化而异，愉悦性和激活性也会在不同的文化背景下恢复。. Dimensional models such as the valence–arousal framework appear to offer comparatively stronger cross-cultural validity than categorical emotion frameworks, because pleasantness and activation are recovered across diverse cultural contexts even though the salience of arousal and the valence–arousal relationship vary by culture[88, 90].

4.4 参与式设计Participatory Design

敏感领域中负责任的人工智能开发要求受系统设计影响最大的人员对其架构进行有意义的输入，不是作为最终阶段的验证主体，而是作为共同设计师，其专业知识决定了基本决策Responsible AI development in sensitive domains requires that the people most affected by a system’s design have meaningful input into its architecture, not as end-stage validation subjects but as co-designers whose expertise shapes foundational decisions[64]。在心理健康人工智能中，这一原则尤其重要，因为该领域的心理、临床和文化维度无法仅靠技术专长来充分代表。. In mental health AI, this principle is particularly consequential as the psychological, clinical, and cultural dimensions of the domain cannot be adequately represented by technical expertise alone.

Copewell 的参与式设计过程得益于与心理健康专业人士和早期用户的持续接触，他们的反馈塑造了具体的架构决策。这种参与被视为连续的设计输入，符合参与式设计原则，强调在整个设计过程中持续的专家输入，而不是离散的验证阶段Copewell’s participatory design process has been informed by ongoing engagement with mental health professionals and early users whose feedback has shaped specific architectural decisions. This engagement is treated as a continuous design input, consistent with participatory design principles that emphasize continuous expert input throughout the design process rather than discrete validation phases[62, 91].

Feedback was gathered through informal consultative conversations, system walkthroughs, and discussion of specific architectural and product decisions. Practitioners reviewed conversational interaction samples, including crisis response appropriateness, therapeutic boundary maintenance, and safety protocol effectiveness, and evaluated the system’s suitability for different user populations. Their input combined clinical expertise with observations grounded in local market contexts.

这次合作中出现了几个反复出现的主题，这些主题有意义地塑造了科普韦尔的架构和定位。首先，从业者一致认为临床治疗之间的时间间隔是当前护理中的结构性差距，特别是对于那些有明确关注点的客户，他们可以从补充支持中受益，而无需充分的临床关注。这种洞察力直接将科普韦尔的价值主张导向弥合这一时间差距，而不是将该系统定位为独立的健康工具。其次，从业者强调了明确区分用例的重要性，区分治疗师指导的为有明确关注点的客户提供的补充支持，以及为用户在没有特定临床目标的情况下应对一般生活挑战的更广泛的自我发展支持。第三，从业者强调了危机应对校准的至关重要性，并一致认为过度检测和检测不足都会产生严重后果，并且必须在转介之前进行验证。第四，从业者指出，用户信任在很大程度上取决于诚实的范围沟通，当人工智能系统透明地代表其角色时，用户会受益。Several recurring themes emerged from this engagement that meaningfully shaped Copewell’s architecture and positioning. This insight directly oriented Copewell’s value proposition toward bridging this temporal gap rather than positioning the system as a standalone wellness tool. Second, practitioners emphasized the importance of clearly differentiating use cases distinguishing between therapist-guided supplementary support for clients with defined concerns, and broader self-development support for users navigating general life challenges without a specific clinical target. Fourth, practitioners noted that user trust depends substantially on honest scope communication where users benefit when an AI system represents its role transparently.

6.2 多智能体系统设计对心理健康的影响Implications for Multi-Agent System Design in Mental Wellness

本文描述的架构为心理健康和邻近敏感领域的多智能体系统的更广泛设计提供了一些启示。最基本的含义涉及专业化的情况。无论用户当前的情绪状态如何，单代理会话系统都应用统一的支持方向，这种架构限制限制了他们对心理健康呈现的心理多样性做出适当反应的能力。 Copewell 基于象限的路由表明，动态代理专业化在实时对话环境中在技术上是可行的，并且情感计算理论可以作为路由决策的计算基础，而不是依赖于关键字匹配或意图分类。专用路由是否比统一方法产生有意义的更好结果是一个经验问题，但该架构确定设计空间比当前单代理实现所建议的更广泛。The architecture described in this paper offers several implications for the broader design of multi-agent systems in mental wellness and adjacent sensitive domains. The most fundamental implication concerns the case for specialization. Single-agent conversational systems apply a uniform support orientation regardless of the user’s current emotional state, an architectural constraint that limits their capacity to respond appropriately to the psychological diversity of mental health presentations. Copewell’s quadrant-based routing demonstrates that dynamic agent specialization is technically feasible within a real-time conversational context, and that affective computing theory can serve as the computational substrate for routing decisions rather than relying on keyword matching or intent classification. Whether specialized routing produces meaningfully better outcomes than uniform approaches is an empirical question, but the architecture establishes that the design space is wider than current single-agent implementations suggest.

道德主管代理代表了除心理健康之外具有潜在普遍性的第二个含义。它展示了作为代理群结构参与者的道德监督，能够进行实时观察和干预，而不是回顾性审查。这种架构模式将道德监督和危机安全统一在一个专用机制中，可能与其他敏感的人工智能领域相关，在这些领域中，道德重大时刻是动态出现的，静态规则集无法完全预测。The Ethics Supervisor agent represents a second implication with potential generalizability beyond mental wellness. It demonstrates ethical oversight as a structural participant in the agent swarm, which can be capable of real-time observation and intervention rather than retrospective review. This architectural pattern, unifying ethical oversight and crisis safety within a single dedicated mechanism, may be relevant to other sensitive AI domains where ethically consequential moments arise dynamically and cannot be fully anticipated by static rule sets.

情感计算与多智能体协调的集成是第三个贡献。 MAS 之前在心理健康领域的工作探索了代理专业化和动态路由The integration of affective computing with multi-agent coordination represents a third contribution. Prior work in mental health MAS has explored agent specialization and dynamic routing[56, 57]，但是在连续维度情感模型中路由决策的明确基础引入了基于关键字或基于意图的方法所无法提供的一定程度的计算严谨性和理论论证。这种集成提出了更广泛的设计原则：情感计算框架可以充当为情感敏感环境设计的多智能体系统中的原则性协调机制，为路由决策提供数学上易于处理且理论上有依据的基础，否则将依赖于启发式分类。, but the explicit grounding of routing decisions in a continuous dimensional emotion model introduces a level of computational rigor and theoretical justification that keyword or intent-based approaches do not provide. This integration suggests a broader design principle: that affective computing frameworks can serve as principled coordination mechanisms in multi-agent systems designed for emotionally sensitive contexts, providing a mathematically tractable and theoretically grounded basis for routing decisions that would otherwise depend on heuristic classification.

最后，这里描述的群体架构除了心理健康之外，还具有潜在的适用性，适用于具有相似要求的相邻领域，例如情感敏感性、动态支持模式匹配的需要以及嵌入式道德监督的必要性。慢性病自我管理、教育福祉支持和老年人护理代表了候选领域，其中核心架构模式、多源评估、情感路由、专业代理交付和统一道德监督可以通过适当的特定领域校准进行调整。Finally, the swarm architecture described here has potential applicability beyond mental wellness to adjacent domains characterized by similar requirements such as emotional sensitivity, the need for dynamic support modality matching, and the imperative for embedded ethical oversight. Chronic illness self-management, educational wellbeing support, and elder care represent candidate domains where the core architectural pattern, multi-source assessment, affective routing, specialized agent delivery, and unified ethical oversight, could be adapted with appropriate domain-specific calibration.

6.3 局限性和未来方向Limitations and Future Directions

本文明确是一项设计研究。它的主要贡献是架构文档和设计原理，并且整个权利要求的范围也受到相应的限制。从这个框架直接得出一些限制，值得明确说明。This paper is explicitly a design study. Its primary contribution is architectural documentation and design rationale, and the scope of claims made throughout has been bounded accordingly. Several limitations follow directly from this framing and are worth stating clearly.

第 5 节中报告的从业者参与度和测试用户信号是形成性的，而不是系统性的。从业者咨询是作为产品开发过程的一部分在一小群专业人士中进行的，并不构成结构化的参与式设计研究。 Beta 部署涉及少数早期用户，他们的反馈虽然具有方向性信息，但无法支持普遍的结论。安全测试是在内部进行的，尚未经过独立的外部验证或针对标准化对抗性评估工具的基准测试。The practitioner engagement and beta user signals reported in Section 5 are formative rather than systematic. Practitioner consultations were conducted as part of the product development process across a small group of professionals, and do not constitute a structured participatory design study. Beta deployment involved a small number of early users whose feedback, while directionally informative, cannot support generalizable conclusions. Safety testing was conducted internally and has not yet been subject to independent external validation or benchmarking against standardized adversarial evaluation instruments.

在架构层面，一些核心设计选择仍然未经经验验证。多源评估框架中数据源的相对权重（40% 自我报告、35% 生理学、25% 情境）反映了理论上的设计决策，而不是经验上优化的参数。基于象限的路由逻辑虽然基于情感计算理论，但尚未根据专家临床判断进行验证，以确认计算分配与临床适当的支持方向一致。价唤醒象限和特定感觉健康模式之间的映射理论上是由每种模式的神经机制驱动的，但尚未在科普韦尔的特定路由环境中进行测试。其中每一个都代表了下一阶段发展必须解决的开放性经验问题。At the architectural level, several core design choices remain empirically unvalidated. The relative weighting of data sources in the multi-source assessment framework (40% self-report, 35% physiological, 25% contextual) reflects theoretically motivated design decisions rather than empirically optimized parameters. The quadrant-based routing logic, while grounded in affective computing theory, has not been validated against expert clinical judgment to confirm that computational assignments align with clinically appropriate support orientations. The mapping between valence-arousal quadrants and specific sensory wellness modalities is theoretically motivated by the neural mechanisms of each modality but has not been tested within Copewell’s specific routing context. Each of these represents an open empirical question that the next phase of development must address.

这些限制定义了本工作最直接遵循的研究议程。最紧迫的优先事项是与独立研究机构合作进行正式的实证评估研究，纳入标准化的心理健康测量、参与度和保留指标、定性用户访谈和正式的从业者评估。这样的研究对于确定此处记录的设计承诺是否转化为对用户有意义的结果是必要的。These limitations define the research agenda that follows most directly from this work. The most immediate priority is a formal empirical evaluation study conducted in collaboration with an independent research institution, incorporating standardized psychological wellbeing measures, engagement and retention metrics, qualitative user interviews, and formal practitioner assessment. Such a study is necessary to establish whether the design commitments documented here translate into meaningful outcomes for users.

跨文化验证是第二要务。相对于分类情感模型，效价唤醒框架提供了相对较强的跨文化有效性，但其作为 Copewell 目标不同人群的路由基础的性能尚未经过实证检验。有必要对不同文化的用户群体进行验证研究，以确认第 4.3 节中描述的以公平为导向的设计选择在实践中实现了预期结果。Cross-cultural validation represents a second priority. The valence-arousal framework offers comparatively strong cross-cultural validity relative to categorical emotion models, but its performance as a routing substrate across the diverse populations Copewell targets has not been empirically tested. Validation studies across culturally distinct user groups are necessary to confirm that the equity-oriented design choices described in Section 4.3 achieve their intended outcomes in practice.

对第 3.6 节中描述的感觉形态-状态映射框架的实证评估构成了第三个优先事项。对理论上激励的象限与模态配对是否产生预测的用户感知和生理结果进行比较评估，既可以验证核心架构主张，又可以为数字健康环境中的感官干预提供更广泛的证据基础。Empirical evaluation of the sensory modality-state mapping framework described in Section 3.6 constitutes a third priority. 7 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 Copewell，这是一种多智能体群体健康系统，旨在通过以人为本的人工智能原理扩大获得心理健康支持的机会。它的发展基于这样一种认识，即全球心理健康治疗差距不仅仅是一个资源问题，而且是一个设计问题，需要人工智能系统从一开始就围绕那些在结构上被排除在护理之外的人群的需求而构建。This paper has presented Copewell, a multi-agent swarm wellness system designed to expand access to mental health support through human-centered AI principles. Its development is grounded in a recognition that the global mental health treatment gap is not merely a resource problem but a design problem, one that demands AI systems built from inception around the needs of populations who have been structurally excluded from care.

这篇论文的贡献是建筑性和概念性的。它详细介绍了系统设计，试图从一开始就实现公平、安全和负责任的人工智能原则，并且诚实地对待设计承诺和演示结果之间的界限。对这里提出的主张进行实证验证仍然是下一研究阶段的工作。The paper’s contribution is architectural and conceptual. It offers a detailed account of a system design that attempts to operationalise equity, safety, and responsible AI principles from inception, and it does so with honesty about the boundary between design commitment and demonstrated outcome. Empirical validation of the claims made here remains the work of the next research phase.

我们认为，这一阶段的贡献，使塑造人工智能系统对用户和社会影响的设计决策变得可见，本身对人工智能和社会讨论就很有价值。在架构级别做出的选择决定了系统可以做什么和不能做什么、它编码什么值以及它旨在服务谁的需求。明确这些选择并接受严格审查是心理健康领域人工智能系统真正值得用户信任的必要前提。We argue that this phase of contribution, making visible the design decisions that shape an AI system’s implications for users and society, is itself valuable to the AI and Society discourse. The choices made at the architectural level determine what a system can and cannot do, what values it encodes, and whose needs it is designed to serve. Making those choices explicit and subjecting them to critical scrutiny is a necessary precondition for AI systems in mental wellness that are genuinely worthy of user trust.

随着人工智能系统不断进入与健康相关的敏感领域，该领域需要技术创新和严格的道德责任。科普韦尔的发展表明这些并不是相互竞争的需求。最终，负责任的设计是更好的设计。As AI systems continue to enter sensitive health-adjacent domains, the field requires both technical innovation and rigorous ethical accountability. Copewell’s development demonstrates that these are not competing demands. Responsible design is, in the end, better design.
