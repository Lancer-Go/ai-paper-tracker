一、简介1. Introduction

人们越来越期望自动驾驶系统能够在复杂、动态和安全关键的交通环境中运行（Che 等人，2026；Ye 等人，2026a,b）。在这种环境中，安全驾驶需要的不仅仅是检测周围物体或产生低级控制命令。一个可靠的自治系统应该能够了解哪个对象或代理产生了风险，风险如何随着时间的推移而演变，风险关键对象位于何处，以及为什么这种情况需要谨慎响应。这种能力在涉及微妙危险、远处物体、部分遮挡、脆弱道路使用者、不确定意图和快速发展交互的长尾驾驶场景中尤其重要（Chen et al., 2024）。在这些情况下，仅输出不透明驾驶决策或粗略场景描述的系统可能不足以进行安全验证、事后审核以及与人类用户的沟通。因此，可解释且基于视觉的风险理解已成为实际自动驾驶系统的关键能力（Malla 等，2023；Zhou 等，2024）。Autonomous driving systems are increasingly expected to operate in complex, dynamic, and safety-critical traffic environments (Che et al., 2026; Ye et al., 2026a,b). In such environments, safe driving requires more than detecting surrounding objects or producing low-level control commands. A reliable autonomous system should be able to understand which object or agent creates risk, how the risk evolves over time, where the risk-critical object is located, and why the situation requires a cautious response. This capability is particularly important in long-tail driving scenarios involving subtle hazards, distant objects, partial occlusions, vulnerable road users, uncertain intentions, and rapidly evolving interactions (Chen et al., 2024). In these cases, a system that only outputs an opaque driving decision or a coarse scene description may be insufficient for safety validation, post-hoc auditing, and communication with human users. Therefore, interpretable and visually grounded risk understanding has become a critical capability for practical autonomous driving systems (Malla et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024).

Motivated by these advances, driving-oriented MLLMs have extended language-enhanced perception to risk localization, scene captioning, visual question answering, highresolution understanding, and end-to-end driving assistance (Malla et al., 2023; Xu et al., 2024; Ding et al., 2023; Sima et al., 2024; Tian et al., 2024; Hwang et al., 2024). These studies suggest that language-enhanced visual understanding can improve the transparency, generalization, and human interpretability of autonomous driving systems.

然而，现有的面向驾驶的 MLLM 方法在理解安全关键风险方面仍然面临着重要的限制，因为它们常常难以共同捕获时间风险演化和细粒度的空间证据。基于单帧推理或相对低分辨率视觉输入的模型可以生成对交通场景的合理描述，但它们可能会错过对安全决策至关重要的小型、遥远或部分遮挡的风险对象（Ding et al., 2023；Xu et al., 2024）。基于视频的模型更适合捕获动态交互和时间上下文，但当风险对象仅占据图像的一小部分区域时，它们的空间基础可能仍然很粗糙（Zhang 等人，2023）。相反，强调高级语言交互、问答或驱动界面的方法可能会改善推理和沟通，但它们通常为风险关键目标的细粒度视觉基础提供有限的支持（Sima et al., 2024；Tian et al., 2024；Wang et al., 2023）。因此，当前的模型可能会在没有精确的视觉证据的情况下产生合理的语言解释，或者在没有充分解释其动态风险相关性的情况下定位对象。Nevertheless, existing driving-oriented MLLM methods still face an important limitation for safety-critical risk understanding as they often struggle to jointly capture temporal risk evolution and fine-grained spatial evidence. Models based on single-frame reasoning or relatively low-resolution visual inputs can generate plausible descriptions of traffic scenes, but they may miss small, distant, or partially occluded risk objects that are critical for safe decision-making (Ding et al., 2023; Xu et al., 2024). Video-based models are better suited to capturing dynamic interactions and temporal context, yet their spatial grounding can remain coarse when risk objects occupy only a small region of the image (Zhang et al., 2023). Conversely, approaches that emphasize high-level language interaction, question answering, or driving interfaces may improve reasoning and communication, but they often provide limited support for fine-grained visual grounding of risk-critical targets (Sima et al., 2024; Tian et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023). As a result, current models may produce reasonable language explanations without precise visual evidence, or localize objects without adequately explaining their dynamic risk relevance.

这种限制尤其成问题，因为推动风险理解本质上是暂时的和局部的。行人从停放的车辆后面走出来，骑自行车的人逐渐进入自我车道，或者具有微妙运动线索的远处交通参与者，都需要模型将时间语义与高分辨率空间细节相结合。对于以安全为导向的解释，这两种形式的信息不应被视为单独的输出。相反，该模型应该将动态上下文与明确的视觉证据结合起来，并生成语义上有意义且具有空间基础的解释。这一要求与 DRAMA 等以风险为中心的基准密切相关，其中风险解释和对象级基础对于理解交互式驾驶场景都至关重要（Malla 等人，2023）。This limitation is especially problematic because driving risk understanding is inherently both temporal and localized. A pedestrian stepping out from behind a parked vehicle, a cyclist gradually entering the ego lane, or a distant traffic participant with subtle motion cues requires the model to integrate temporal semantics with high-resolution spatial details. For safety-oriented interpretation, these two forms of information should not be treated as separate outputs. Instead, the model should align dynamic context with explicit visual evidence and generate explanations that are both semantically meaningful and spatially grounded. This requirement is closely aligned with risk-centric benchmarks such as DRAMA, where risk explanation and object-level grounding are both essential for understanding interactive driving scenarios (Malla et al., 2023).

Experiments show that UniDrive consistently improves both language-based risk explanation and grounded risk-object localization compared with representative imagebased and video-based baselines. 2.1.多模态推理和视觉基础2.1. Multimodal Reasoning and Visual Grounding

VLM 和 MLLM 的最新进展极大地提高了人工智能系统将视觉感知与基于语言的推理联系起来的能力。 Flamingo 等代表性模型证明，大型语言模型可以通过交叉注意力以视觉输入为条件，从而实现跨不同任务的少量视觉语言理解（Alayrac 等人，2022）。 LLaVA 及其改进变体进一步表明，视觉指令调整可以产生强大的多模态会话和推理能力（Liu et al., 2024）。除了一般的视觉对话之外，扎根的多模式生成也受到越来越多的关注。 Kosmos-2将语言输出与视觉区域联系起来以进行扎根生成（Peng等，2023），GroundHog加强了语言生成和密集视觉理解之间的耦合（Zhang等，2024），SpatialRGPT强调了区域级几何和空间关系对于空间推理的重要性（Cheng等，2024）。同时，Video-LLaMA 将多模态语言建模从静态图像扩展到时间结构化视频输入，显示了 MLLM 在动态场景理解方面的潜力（Zhang 等人，2023）。Recent advances in VLMs and MLLMs have substantially improved the ability of artificial intelligence systems to connect visual perception with language-based reasoning. Representative models such as Flamingo demonstrated that large language models can be conditioned on visual inputs through cross-attention, enabling few-shot visual-language understanding across diverse tasks (Alayrac et al., 2022). LLaVA and its improved variants further showed that visual instruction tuning can produce strong multimodal conversational and reasoning abilities (Liu et al., 2024). Beyond general visual dialogue, grounded multimodal generation has received increasing attention. Kosmos-2 links language outputs to visual regions for grounded generation (Peng et al., 2023), GroundHog strengthens the coupling between language generation and dense visual understanding (Zhang et al., 2024), and SpatialRGPT highlights the importance of region-level geometry and spatial relationships for spatial reasoning (Cheng et al., 2024). Meanwhile, Video-LLaMA extends multimodal language modeling from static images to temporally structured video inputs, showing the potential of MLLMs for dynamic scene understanding (Zhang et al., 2023).

这些研究为语言增强感知、视觉基础和时间推理提供了重要基础。然而，通用 MLLM 并不是专门为安全关键的自动驾驶场景而设计的。推动风险理解需要的不仅仅是通用对象识别、图像字幕或视觉对话。模型应该识别哪个对象会产生风险，解释为什么它是危险的，并将这种解释建立在视觉场景中。它还应该考虑时间线索，例如运动、交互和意图变化。因此，尽管现有的 MLLM 建立了多模态推理和基础的可行性，但如果没有明确地将时间风险演化与细粒度空间证据相结合的机制，它们在自动驾驶中的直接应用仍然受到限制。These studies provide important foundations for language-enhanced perception, visual grounding, and temporal reasoning. However, general-purpose MLLMs are not specifically designed for safety-critical autonomous driving scenarios. Driving risk understanding requires more than generic object recognition, image captioning, or visual dialogue. A model should identify which object creates risk, explain why it is hazardous, and ground this explanation in the visual scene. It should also account for temporal cues such as motion, interaction, and intention changes. Therefore, although existing MLLMs establish the feasibility of multimodal reasoning and grounding, their direct application to autonomous driving remains limited without mechanisms that explicitly integrate temporal risk evolution with finegrained spatial evidence.

This setting allows us to evaluate whether model outputs are not only linguistically plausible, but also grounded in the correct risk objects. Together with zero-shot transfer, robustness analysis, and human-centered evaluation, our experiments examine the reliability of UniDrive under safety-relevant conditions.

5. 讨论5. Discussion

5.1.扎根的风险推理5.1. Grounded Risk Reasoning

实验结果表明，UniDrive 改进了基于语言的风险解释和对象级基础，但主要含义超出了数值收益本身。字幕、mIoU、小物体定位、零样本传输和人类偏好方面的持续改进表明，以安全为导向的驾驶理解不应被视为单独的字幕问题或检测问题。在交互式驾驶场景中，只有当解释可以链接到正确的风险源时，它才与安全相关，并且只有当可以解释其动态风险相关性时，局部对象才是可解释的。这一发现与 DRAMA 的动机一致，DRAMA 强调风险定位和字幕在推动场景理解中的共同作用（Malla 等人，2023），但我们的结果进一步表明，可以通过时间语义和空间证据之间的明确架构对齐来加强这种联合表述。The experimental results show that UniDrive improves both language-based risk explanation and object-level grounding, but the main implication goes beyond the numerical gains themselves. The consistent improvements in captioning, mIoU, small-object localization, zero-shot transfer, and human preference suggest that safety-oriented driving understanding should not be treated as either a captioning problem or a detection problem alone. In interactive driving scenes, an explanation is only safety-relevant when it can be linked to the correct risk source, and a localized object is only interpretable when its dynamic risk relevance can be explained. This finding is consistent with the motivation of DRAMA, which emphasizes the joint role of risk localization and captioning in driving scene understanding (Malla et al., 2023), but our results further show that such a joint formulation can be strengthened through explicit architectural alignment between temporal semantics and spatial evidence.

与基于图像和基于视频的基线的比较提供了解释这一点的有用方法。基于图像的 MLLM 可以利用当前帧中的细粒度视觉细节，但它们对风险如何随时间演变的了解有限。相比之下，基于视频的模型更适合捕捉运动和交互线索，但它们的空间基础可能会因低分辨率输入、时间压缩或有限的视觉标记预算而被削弱。这有助于解释为什么 UniDrive 在字幕和检测方面都取得了改进，而不仅仅是任务的一方面。高分辨率感知分支提高了模型识别视觉上微妙或小风险对象的能力，而时间推理分支提供动态上下文来解释这些对象的重要性。因此，生成的输出不仅在语言上变得合理，而且在视觉上也更可验证。The comparison with image-based and video-based baselines provides a useful way to interpret this point. Imagebased MLLMs can exploit fine-grained visual details in the current frame, but they have limited access to how a risk evolves over time. Video-based models, in contrast, are better positioned to capture motion and interaction cues, yet their spatial grounding may be weakened by low-resolution inputs, temporal compression, or limited visual-token budgets. This helps explain why UniDrive obtains improvements in both captioning and detection rather than only one side of the task. The high-resolution perception branch improves the model’s ability to identify visually subtle or small risk objects, while the temporal reasoning branch provides dynamic context for explaining why these objects matter. As a result, the generated output becomes not only linguistically plausible, but also more visually verifiable.

5.2.时空融合的作用5.2. Role of Spatio-Temporal Fusion

消融结果让我们深入了解 UniDrive 性能背后的机制。去除时间推理分支会降低风险描述的质量，尽管高分辨率分支仍然可以保持相对较强的检测性能。这表明仅高空间保真度不足以解释动态驾驶风险。许多危险不仅取决于物体是什么，还取决于它的移动方式、它是否进入自我车道、自我车辆是否正在接近它以及周围环境是否需要做出谨慎的反应。从这个意义上说，时间推理贡献了语义和因果上下文，而不仅仅是额外的框架。The ablation results provide insight into the mechanism behind UniDrive’s performance. Removing the temporal reasoning branch reduces the quality of risk descriptions, even though the high-resolution branch can still maintain relatively strong detection performance. This suggests that high spatial fidelity alone is insufficient for interpreting dynamic driving risk. Many hazards are defined not only by what an object is, but also by how it moves, whether it is entering the ego lane, whether the ego vehicle is approaching it, and whether the surrounding context makes a cautious response necessary. In this sense, temporal reasoning contributes semantic and causal context rather than merely additional frames.

相反，去除高分辨率感知分支会导致检测性能大幅下降，尤其是对于小物体。这表明时间上下文无法弥补细粒度空间证据的缺失。在驾驶场景中，安全关键对象可能仅占据较小的图像区域、部分被遮挡或出现在远处。这种情况对于基于视频的 MLLM 来说尤其具有挑战性，因为可能会为了时间覆盖而牺牲空间精度（Zhang 等人，2023；Xu 等人，2024）。因此，结果支持了一种互补的观点：时间信息有助于确定风险如何出现，而高分辨率感知有助于确定责任对象所在的位置。Conversely, removing the high-resolution perception branch leads to a substantial drop in detection performance, especially for small objects. This indicates that temporal context cannot compensate for missing fine-grained spatial evidence. In driving scenes, safety-critical objects may occupy only a small image region, be partially occluded, or appear at a distance. Such cases are particularly challenging for video-based MLLMs, where spatial precision may be sacrificed for temporal coverage (Zhang et al., 2023; Xu et al., 2024). The results therefore support a complementary view: temporal information helps determine how a risk emerges, while high-resolution perception helps determine where the responsible object is located.

所提出的机制最有力的证据来自时空融合的消融。用朴素的特征串联替换门控交叉注意力会导致字幕和基础指标的性能明显崩溃。这表明仅仅让法学硕士同时接触时间和空间特征是不够的。该模型必须了解动态上下文应如何关注细粒度的空间区域。门控交叉注意力模块通过使用时间特征作为查询和高分辨率空间特征作为键和值来提供这种对齐机制。换句话说，它允许模型询问哪些视觉区域与不断变化的风险背景相关。The strongest evidence for the proposed mechanism comes from the ablation of spatio-temporal fusion. Replacing gated cross-attention with naive feature concatenation causes a clear performance collapse across both captioning and grounding metrics. This shows that simply exposing the LLM to both temporal and spatial features is not enough. The model must learn how dynamic context should attend to fine-grained spatial regions. The gated cross-attention module provides this alignment mechanism by using temporal features as queries and high-resolution spatial features as keys and values. In other words, it allows the model to ask which visual regions are relevant to the evolving risk context.

5.3.对安全驾驶 MLLM 的影响5.3. Implications for Safety-Oriented Driving MLLMs

在模型设计方面，结果表明，未来的驾驶 MLLM 应超越一般的场景理解，而更加重视扎根的风险推理。在通用 VLM 中，视觉基础通常被视为连接语言表达与图像区域的一种方式（Peng 等人，2023；Zhang 等人，2024；Cheng 等人，2024）。然而，在自动驾驶中，接地具有更直接的安全意义，因为它决定了解释是否涉及对自我车辆真正重要的物体。因此，接地不应被视为附属于语言生成的辅助输出。相反，它应该融入到核心推理过程中，使语言解释、风险对象定位和驾驶建议相互制约。In terms of model design, the results suggest that future driving MLLMs should move beyond general scene understanding and place greater emphasis on grounded risk reasoning. In general-purpose VLMs, visual grounding is often treated as a way to connect language expressions with image regions (Peng et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2024; Cheng et al., 2024). In autonomous driving, however, grounding carries a more direct safety meaning because it determines whether an explanation refers to the object that actually matters for the ego vehicle. Therefore, grounding should not be considered an auxiliary output attached to language generation. Instead, it should be integrated into the core reasoning process, so that language explanations, risk-object localization, and driving suggestions are mutually constrained.

这种观点对于评估也有影响。 BLEU-4、METEOR、CIDEr 和 SPICE 等标准字幕指标对于测量语言相似性非常有用，而基于 IoU 的指标对于测量定位准确性非常有用。然而，这两种指标都不能单独评估以安全为导向的解释。流畅的标题可能会引用错误的对象，而正确的边界框可能仍然无法解释为什么该对象有风险。本研究采用的综合评估，包括字幕、基础、对象尺度分析、零样本迁移、鲁棒性测试、定性案例和以人为中心的评估，提供了更全面的模型可靠性视图。同时，失败案例表明，即使这种评估仍然不完整，因为风险理解还需要对多种可能的危险进行优先排序。因此，未来的基准应该考虑风险排名、轨迹相关性、因果相关性和不确定性，而不是仅评估一个主要边界框或一个参考标题。This perspective also has implications for evaluation. Standard captioning metrics such as BLEU-4, METEOR, CIDEr, and SPICE are useful for measuring linguistic similarity, while IoU-based metrics are useful for measuring localization accuracy. However, neither type of metric alone can fully evaluate safety-oriented explanations. A fluent caption may refer to the wrong object, while a correct bounding box may still fail to explain why the object is risky. The combined evaluation adopted in this study, including captioning, grounding, object-scale analysis, zero-shot transfer, robustness testing, qualitative cases, and human-centered assessment, provides a more comprehensive view of model reliability. At the same time, the failure cases indicate that even this evaluation remains incomplete, because risk understanding also requires prioritizing among multiple plausible hazards. Future benchmarks should therefore consider risk ranking, trajectory relevance, causal relevance, and uncertainty, rather than evaluating only one primary bounding box or one reference caption.

以人为本的评估进一步表明了为什么有根据的解释对于值得信赖的自动驾驶很重要。参与者在实用性、准确性和可信度方面更倾向于 UniDrive 的解释。这一结果不应仅仅被解释为对更流畅文本的偏好。相反，它表明，当解释确定风险对象、描述关注原因并为建议的响应提供视觉上可检查的基础时，解释就会变得更有用。此类产出与以安全为导向的感知和事后审计相关，其中解释必须是可检查的，而不仅仅是合理的。从这个意义上说，UniDrive 可以被视为感知和下游决策之间的中间推理层。它不会取代规划或控制，但可以提供支持安全监控、人工审查和故障分析的扎实风险证据。The human-centered evaluation further shows why grounded explanations are important for trustworthy autonomous driving. Participants more frequently preferred UniDrive’s explanations in terms of usefulness, accuracy, and trustworthiness. This result should not be interpreted merely as a preference for more fluent text. Rather, it suggests that explanations become more useful when they identify the risk object, describe the reason for concern, and provide a visually checkable basis for the recommended response. Such outputs are relevant to safety-oriented perception and post-hoc auditing, where an explanation must be inspectable rather than only plausible. In this sense, UniDrive can be viewed as an intermediate reasoning layer between perception and downstream decision-making. It does not replace planning or control, but it can provide grounded risk evidence that supports safety monitoring, human review, and failure analysis.

稳健性结果进一步支持了这种以安全为导向的解释。在夜间和下雨条件下，UniDrive 表现出比视频语言基线更优雅的退化。这表明将时间背景与高分辨率空间证据相结合可以减少对任何单一脆弱线索的依赖。当视觉细节退化时，时间上下文仍然可以提供连续性和运动信息；当时间线索不明确时，高分辨率感知仍然可以保留物体级证据。这种互补性对于安全相关场景尤其重要，在这些场景中，罕见、不利或长尾条件通常决定系统的可靠性。The robustness results further support this safety-oriented interpretation. Under night and rainy conditions, UniDrive exhibits more graceful degradation than the video-language baseline. This suggests that combining temporal context with high-resolution spatial evidence can reduce dependence on any single fragile cue. When visual details are degraded, temporal context can still provide continuity and motion information; when temporal cues are ambiguous, high-resolution perception can still preserve object-level evidence. Such complementarity is particularly important for safety-relevant scenarios, where rare, adverse, or long-tail conditions often determine system reliability.

5.4.局限性和未来的研究5.4. Limitations and Future Studies

尽管取得了这些有希望的结果，但仍然存在一些局限性。首先，失败案例表明，当多个候选危险同时发生时，UniDrive 仍然可以选择一个看似合理但非主要的风险对象。这不是简单的对象检测失败。在这两种代表性案例中，模型都基于视觉上合理的对象，但不是被注释为最安全的对象。这表明存在风险优先级问题。该模型可能会偏向动态主体，例如行人，或者偏向大型、附近和视觉上显着的物体，即使另一个物体与自我车辆的轨迹具有更强的相关性。因此，未来的工作应该超越单对象基础，引入风险层次结构、多风险排名和轨迹感知相关性估计。明确的风险指标Despite these promising results, several limitations remain. In both representative cases, the model grounds an object that is visually reasonable, but not the one annotated as most safety-critical. This suggests a risk-prioritization problem. The model may be biased toward dynamic agents such as pedestrians, or toward large, nearby, and visually salient objects, even when another object has stronger relevance to the ego vehicle’s trajectory. 其次，当前的戏剧推理设置建立在主要风险对象注释和丰富的文本描述之上。这种设计可以实现推理和接地的联合评估，同时也简化了真实驾驶场景的复杂性。在实践中，多个对象可能同时造成不同级别的风险，并且不同的人类注释者可能不同意哪个对象应被视为主要对象。因此，未来的数据集应包括多个候选风险、风险严重程度、因果关系和注释者间协议。此类注释可以评估模型是否理解风险的结构，而不是仅匹配一个选定的目标。Second, the current DRAMA-Reasoning setting is built on primary risk-object annotations and enriched textual descriptions. Although the model can infer many risk cues from video and high-resolution images, it does not directly model ego-motion, vehicle dynamics, traffic rules, HD maps, LiDAR/radar measurements, or downstream planning feasibility. This limits its ability to determine which object will affect the ego vehicle’s future path under different maneuvers. Future studies should integrate grounded risk reasoning with BEV representations, map priors, trajectory prediction, and planning modules. Such integration would allow the model to reason not only about what is visible, but also about what is reachable, avoidable, and operationally relevant.

最后，部署效率和可靠性需要进一步研究。 UniDrive 在性能和计算成本之间实现了合理的平衡，但所报告的 A100 GPU 上的推理速度不应被解释为实时车载部署的充分证据。实际系统需要更低的延迟、更强的可靠性保证以及闭环条件下的广泛验证。未来的工作应该探索模型压缩、知识蒸馏、标记修剪和面向边缘的推理。更重要的是，应在闭环模拟、人机交互实验和现实世界回放研究中测试扎根风险解释的价值，以检验它们是否改善下游安全监控、决策支持和故障诊断。Finally, deployment efficiency and reliability require further investigation. UniDrive achieves a reasonable balance between performance and computational cost, but the reported inference speed on an A100 GPU should not be interpreted as sufficient evidence for real-time in-vehicle deployment. Practical systems require lower latency, stronger reliability guarantees, and extensive validation under closed-loop conditions. Future work should explore model compression, knowledge distillation, token pruning, and edge-oriented inference. More importantly, the value of grounded risk explanations should be tested in closed-loop simulation, human-in-the-loop experiments, and real-world replay studies to examine whether they improve downstream safety monitoring, decision support, and failure diagnosis.

六、结论6. Conclusion

本文介绍了 UniDrive，这是一种统一的视觉语言和基础框架，用于理解自动驾驶中的可解释风险。受现有面向驾驶的 MLLM 中时间理解和空间精度之间持续权衡的推动，UniDrive 集成了时间推理分支、高分辨率感知分支和门控交叉注意融合模块，以将动态场景上下文与细粒度空间证据对齐。基于这种融合表示，该模型联合生成自然语言风险描述和风险关键对象的接地边界框输出。This paper presented UniDrive, a unified visual-language and grounding framework for interpretable risk understanding in autonomous driving. Motivated by the persistent trade-off between temporal understanding and spatial precision in existing driving-oriented MLLMs, UniDrive integrates a temporal reasoning branch, a high-resolution perception branch, and a gated cross-attention fusion module to align dynamic scene context with fine-grained spatial evidence. Based on this fused representation, the model jointly generates natural-language risk descriptions and grounded bounding-box outputs for risk-critical objects.

DRAMA-Reasoning 基准测试表明，UniDrive 在驾驶场景字幕和风险对象定位方面均取得了出色的性能。与代表性的基于图像和基于视频的基线相比，UniDrive 提供了更准确的接地解释、更强的小目标定位以及更好的整体推理-接地一致性。其他评估进一步表明其对未见过的驾驶数据集的零样本泛化，在不利条件下提高了鲁棒性，以及更高的人类评价有用性、准确性和可信度。消融研究证实，时间推理、高分辨率感知和明确的时空融合对于最终性能都至关重要。Experiments on the DRAMA-Reasoning benchmark demonstrate that UniDrive achieves strong performance in both driving scene captioning and risk-object localization. Compared with representative image-based and videobased baselines, UniDrive provides more accurate grounded explanations, stronger small-object localization, and better overall reasoning-grounding consistency. Additional evaluations further show its zero-shot generalization to unseen driving datasets, improved robustness under adverse conditions, and higher human-rated usefulness, accuracy, and trustworthiness. Ablation studies confirm that temporal reasoning, high-resolution perception, and explicit spatiotemporal fusion are all essential to the final performance.

总的来说，研究结果表明，以安全为导向的驾驶 MLLM 不应仅通过其生成合理语言响应或孤立检测物体的能力来评估。相反，他们应该能够解释为什么某种情况是有风险的，并以正确的视觉证据来解释这一解释。通过将时间语义、高分辨率感知和对象级基础连接到统一的生成框架内，UniDrive 为可解释的自动驾驶智能提供了更加透明和可靠的基础。Overall, the findings show that safety-oriented driving MLLMs should not be evaluated only by their ability to generate plausible language responses or detect objects in isolation. Instead, they should be able to explain why a situation is risky and ground that explanation in the correct visual evidence. By linking temporal semantics, highresolution perception, and object-level grounding within a unified generative framework, UniDrive provides a more transparent and reliable foundation for interpretable autonomous driving intelligence.
