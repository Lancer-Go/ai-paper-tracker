摘要Abstract

可靠的概率估计在许多机器学习应用中至关重要，但现代分类器往往校准不佳。事后校准提供了一种简单且广泛使用的解决方案，但大量提出的方法，加上小规模和不一致的评估，使得很难确定哪些方法在实践中真正有效。 我们引入了用于事后校准的大规模标准化基准，涵盖了表格和计算机视觉任务的近 2000 个实验，包括二进制、多类和大规模分类设置。我们的基准聚合了来自各种经典模型、现代深度学习架构和基础模型的预测，并在通用评估框架内提供了数十种校准方法的统一、可重复的实现。 我们认为，适当评分规则中的事后改进（PHI）为传统校准误差估计器提供了原则上的替代方案，用于比较事后方法，捕获校准质量和模型预测性能的潜在退化。 利用这个框架，我们进行了迄今为止最全面的事后校准实证研究。 我们的结果揭示了跨领域的一致模式：平滑的校准函数优于基于分箱的方法，专用的多类方法在高维设置中至关重要，并且如果没有特定于校准的设计，通用机器学习模型就没有竞争力。为了促进未来的研究，我们发布了所有数据、代码和评估工具，为开发和比较校准方法提供了即插即用的基准。Reliable probability estimates are critical in many machine learning applications, yet modern classifiers are often poorly calibrated. Post-hoc calibration provides a simple and widely used solution, but the large number of proposed methods, combined with small-scale and inconsistent evaluations, makes it difficult to determine which approaches are truly effective in practice. We introduce a large-scale, standardized benchmark for post-hoc calibration, covering nearly 2000 experiments across tabular and computer vision tasks, including binary, multiclass, and large-scale classification settings. Our benchmark aggregates predictions from a diverse set of classical models, modern deep learning architectures, and foundation models, and provides unified, reproducible implementations of dozens of calibration methods within a common evaluation framework. We argue that Post-Hoc Improvement (PHI) in proper scoring rules offers a principled alternative to traditional calibration error estimators for comparing post-hoc methods, capturing both calibration quality and potential degradation to the model’s predictive performance. Using this framework, we conduct the most comprehensive empirical study of post-hoc calibration to date. Our results reveal consistent patterns across domains: smooth calibration functions outperform binning-based approaches, dedicated multiclass methods are essential in high-dimensional settings, and generic machine learning models are not competitive without calibration-specific design. To facilitate future research, we release all data, code, and evaluation tools, providing a plug-and-play benchmark for developing and comparing calibration methods.

1 简介Introduction

准确的分类是许多机器学习应用的核心，从医疗诊断和欺诈检测到自动驾驶和天气预报。 除了预测类标签之外，现代分类器还输出反映实例属于每个类的置信度的概率分布。 这些概率预测在下游决策中发挥着至关重要的作用，特别是在必须明确考虑不确定性的高风险环境中。Accurate classification is central to many machine learning applications, ranging from medical diagnosis and fraud detection to autonomous driving and weather forecasting. Beyond predicting class labels, modern classifiers output probability distributions that reflect their confidence that the instance belongs to each class. These probabilistic predictions play a critical role in downstream decision-making, especially in high-stake settings where uncertainty must be explicitly accounted for.

然而，在实践中，这些预测概率通常校准不佳：预测概率与观察到的类别频率不匹配。 这种不匹配破坏了机器学习系统的可靠性和可信度。 从支持向量机和提升树等经典方法来看，错误校准已在各种模型中得到广泛记录In practice, however, these predicted probabilities are often poorly calibrated: the predicted probabilities do not match observed class frequencies. This mismatch undermines the reliability and trustworthiness of machine learning systems. Miscalibration has been extensively documented across a wide range of models, from classical methods such as support vector machines and boosted trees[54, 73, 74, 52]到深度神经网络to deep neural networks[28, 48].

解决这个问题的一种广泛采用的方法是A widely adopted approach to address this problem is事后校准post-hoc calibration，它使用在保留的验证集上学习的“校准函数”在训练后调整预测概率。 概率和事后校准的正式背景提供于, which adjusts predicted probabilities after training using a “calibration function” learned on a held-out validation set. A formal background on probability and post-hoc calibration is provided inAppendix˜A。 多年来，人们针对二元分类和多类分类提出了大量事后校准方法。 尽管方法如此丰富，但仍不清楚哪些方法在实践中以及在什么条件下最有效。. Over the years, a large number of post-hoc calibration methods have been proposed for both binary and multiclass classification. Despite this abundance of methods, it remains unclear which approaches are most effective in practice, and under which conditions.

这种缺乏清晰度源于当前文献中的几个关键限制。 首先，现有的实证评估依赖于小规模或过时的基准，限制了它们对现代机器学习环境的代表性。 其次，对于如何正确评估校准尚未达成共识：众所周知，预期校准误差 (ECE) 等常用指标对设计选择很敏感，导致比较不可靠。 第三，许多提出的方法缺乏可访问的、最新的实现，阻碍了全面和公平的比较。 因此，两篇评估同一方法的论文可能会得出相互矛盾的结论，实践者在选择校准方法时没有可靠的依据。This lack of clarity stems from several key limitations in the current literature. First, existing empirical evaluations rely on small-scale or outdated benchmarks, limiting their representativeness of modern machine learning settings. Second, there is no consensus on how to properly evaluate calibration: commonly used metrics such as Expected Calibration Error (ECE) are known to be sensitive to design choices, making comparisons unreliable. Third, many proposed methods lack accessible, up-to-date implementations, preventing comprehensive and fair comparisons. As a result, two papers evaluating the same method can reach contradictory conclusions, and practitioners have no reliable basis for choosing a calibration method.

2 基准测试Benchmarks

我们引入了一套旨在严格评估事后校准方法的基准。 这些基准涵盖不同的数据模式（表格和计算机视觉）、任务类型（二进制、多类和大规模多类）和模型架构（经典模型、深度学习和基础模型）。 每个基准测试都包含多个实验，其中一个实验被定义为具有相关验证和测试集预测的数据集模型对。 验证集预测用于拟合事后校准方法，而测试集预测使我们能够评估和比较它们的性能。We introduce a suite of benchmarks designed to rigorously evaluate post-hoc calibration methods. These benchmarks cover diverse data modalities (tabular and computer vision), task types (binary, multiclass, and large-scale multiclass), and model architectures (classical models, deep learning, and foundation models). Each benchmark comprises multiple experiments, where an experiment is defined as a dataset-model pair with associated validation and test set predictions. Validation set predictions are used to fit post-hoc calibration methods, while test set predictions allow us to evaluate and compare their performance.

6 要点Takeaways

流畅度很重要。Smoothness matters.

在我们的基准测试中，平滑校准函数明显优于基于分箱的方法。 标准等渗回归和 CIR 之间观察到的巨大性能差距很好地说明了这一点，CIR 是对线性化 PAV 算法引入的跳跃的初始函数的简单修改。 在多类预测中也观察到了这种效应。Smooth calibration functions clearly outperform binning-based methods on our benchmarks. This is well illustrated by the large performance gap observed between standard isotonic regression and CIR, which is a simple modification of the initial function that linearizes the jumps introduced by the PAV algorithm. This effect is also observed on multiclass predictions.

7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一个用于事后校准的大规模标准化基准，涵盖了不同模型、数据集和预测设置的近 2000 个实验。 通过统一数据源、实施和评估协议，我们的基准提供了一个可靠且可重复的框架，用于比较校准方法，解决先前实证研究的关键局限性。We presented a large-scale, standardized benchmark for post-hoc calibration, covering nearly 2000 experiments across diverse models, datasets, and prediction settings. By unifying data sources, implementations, and evaluation protocols, our benchmark provides a reliable and reproducible framework for comparing calibration methods, addressing key limitations of prior empirical studies.

在适当的评分规则中使用事后改进为比较校准方法提供了原则性指标。这种观点避免了传统校准误差估计器的陷阱，并能够进行有意义的比较，既考虑了校准质量，又考虑了后处理引起的预测性能的潜在下降。The use of Post-Hoc Improvement in proper scoring rules provides a principled metric for comparing calibration methods. This perspective avoids the pitfalls of traditional calibration error estimators and enables meaningful comparisons that account for both calibration quality and potential decrease in predictive performance induced by post-processing.

我们的实证研究得出了一些见解。 尽管存在有前途的非参数替代方案，但参数逻辑事后校准模型在二元和多类设置中始终优于其他现有方法。 平滑校准方法也系统地优于基于分箱的方法，尽管改进了标准的、过于简单的校准误差指标，但后者通常会降低预测性能。 尽管在低维环境中很有前景，但一对一策略无法有效扩展，并且当类数量增加时，本地多类方法至关重要。 最后，通用机器学习模型对于事后校准来说不具有竞争力，这凸显了特定于校准的设计原则（例如适应正则化和单调性）的重要性。Our empirical study yields several insights. Although promising non-parametric alternatives exist, parametric logistic post-hoc calibration models consistently outperform other existing approaches in both binary and multiclass settings. Smooth calibration methods also systematically outperform binning-based approaches, which often degrade predictive performance despite improving standard, overly simple calibration error metrics. Despite being promising in low-dimensional settings, one-versus-rest strategies fail to scale effectively and native multiclass methods are essential when the number of classes grows. Finally, generic machine learning models are not competitive out of the box for post-hoc calibration, highlighting the importance of calibration-specific design principles such as adapted regularization and monotonicity.

除了这些发现之外，我们的基准测试还旨在作为社区的实用工具，推动对各种校准方法的相对优缺点的持续调查。 通过在即插即用框架中发布所有数据、代码和评估实用程序，我们的目标是促进未来的研究，并能够对新的校准方法以及我们的基准中缺少的现有校准方法进行公平、大规模的比较。 我们希望这项工作有助于建立更可靠的评估标准并加速事后校准的进展。Beyond these findings, our benchmark is intended as a practical tool for the community to drive ongoing investigation of the relative strengths and weaknesses of various calibration methods. By releasing all data, code, and evaluation utilities in a plug-and-play framework, we aim to facilitate future research and enable fair, large-scale comparisons of new calibration methods, as well as existing calibration methods that are missing in our benchmark. We hope this work will contribute to establishing more reliable evaluation standards and accelerate progress in post-hoc calibration.

附录CAppendix C局限性Limitations

在本节中，我们概述了当前基准测试的一些限制，这些限制可以在未来版本中解决。In this section, we outline some limitations of our current benchmark that could be addressed in a future version.

研究超参数的影响。Investigate the impact of hyperparameters.

为简单起见，我们的基准测试使用固定超参数评估事后校准方法。 然而，有几种方法具有可从交叉验证中受益的可调参数，例如 Dirichlet、SVS 和 SMS 中的正则化强度。 实现这种调整需要对每个任务多次拟合每个方法，目前对于我们的大规模基准框架中较慢的算法来说，这在计算上是令人望而却步的。 这凸显了事后校准方法良好（快速）实施的重要性。For simplicity, our benchmark evaluates post-hoc calibration methods using fixed hyperparameters. However, several methods feature tunable parameters that could benefit from cross-validation, such as the regularization strengths in Dirichlet, SVS, and SMS. Implementing such tuning requires fitting each method multiple times per task, which is currently computationally prohibitive for the slower algorithms within our large-scale benchmark framework. This highlights the importance of good (fast) implementations for post-hoc calibration methods.

研究其他形式的多类校准。Investigate other forms of multiclass calibration.

通过考虑适当分数的事后改进，我们明确地针对事后校准后的完整校准误差，无论考虑的类别数量如何。 多类校准的较弱概念存在并且也非常流行，例如顶级校准，它仅考虑分类器分配的最大概率的校准。 尽管在全面校准上表现不佳，某些校准方法可能在这些较窄的目标上表现出色，这意味着方法排名可能会在不同的评估指标下发生变化。By considering post-hoc improvement in proper scores, we explicitly target the full calibration error after post-hoc calibration, whatever the number of classes considered. Weaker notions of multiclass calibration exist and are very popular as well, like top-class calibration, which considers only the calibration of the largest probability assigned by the classifier. Certain calibration methods might excel at these narrower objectives despite performing poorly on full calibration, meaning method rankings could shift under different evaluation metrics.

更大规模的计算机视觉基准。Larger scale computer vision benchmarks.

虽然我们的表格基准涵盖了数十个数据集和模型，但我们的计算机视觉评估的多样性和规模可以进一步扩大。 未来的迭代可以结合更广泛的现代架构和多样化的图像数据集，以确保基准结论在视觉任务中的稳健性。While our tabular benchmarks cover dozens of datasets and models, the diversity and scale of our computer vision evaluations could be further expanded.
